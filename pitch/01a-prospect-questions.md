# Slide 01a — The questions on every retail marketer's whiteboard

## You don't need a research project. You need answers to the questions you're already asking.

Walk into any retail marketing standup and you'll hear some version of these:

### About your own brand
- *"How are real customers actually perceiving our last campaign?"*
- *"Which subject lines feel pushy to our best segment?"*
- *"Did the new welcome flow improve over last quarter, or did we kid ourselves?"*
- *"What does the homepage feel like to a 62-year-old shopper on her phone right now?"*
- *"Are we missing common sizes in our top styles — and where?"*

### About your competitors
- *"What promos is Macy's running this Memorial Day weekend?"*
- *"How aggressive is Nike's email cadence vs ours?"*
- *"Is DSW out-merchandising us on Slip-ins right now?"*
- *"What does Sephora's mobile signup flow look like end-to-end?"*

### About the gap between you and them
- *"How do my offers compare to the competitor's offers — today, not last quarter?"*
- *"If Walker received both our email and Skechers' on the same Tuesday, which one wins?"*
- *"Where is our shelf weak and theirs strong — by category, by size, by color?"*

These are the **questions retailers can't answer with Litmus, Klaviyo dashboards, or Contentsquare** — those tools measure aggregates *after* the customer has acted. They tell you what happened. They don't tell you why a specific shopper would or wouldn't.

**etell answers all of these — continuously, in first-person, with proof.**

---

*Presenter notes:* Read the questions one section at a time. Pause after each. The room will start nodding by the second bullet — that's when you've built the case for the rest of the deck. If they push back ("we already do this"), ask: "Today, who tells you what a 62-year-old comfort shopper thinks of your Mother's Day email — by name, with receipts?" Silence is the answer.
