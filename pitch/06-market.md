# Slide 06 — Market

## The math we're honest about.

```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  TAM — Global martech spend                                         │
   │  ~$540B/yr (Scott Brinker, Martech Map 2026 — 14,106 vendors)       │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
             ↓
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  SAM — Email + CX analytics + experience-testing spend              │
   │  ~$28B/yr (Litmus / Mutiny / Movable Ink / Contentsquare /          │
   │  Qualtrics / Persado / Phrasee categories combined)                 │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
             ↓
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  SOM (wedge) — Top 2,000 consumer brands with email revenue >$10M   │
   │  2,000 brands × $30K avg ACV = ~$60M ARR opportunity                │
   │  Expansion: site, app, ads, support, inventory.                     │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why the SOM is the real number

TAM charts lie. The honest question is: **how many brands have both the email volume and the team sophistication to buy this in year 1?**

The answer is a few thousand — brands doing >$10M/yr in email revenue, with a dedicated email team or agency of record. That's enough for a ~$60M ARR wedge at $30K ACV. Expand from email into site journeys + paid media + support + inventory audits, and the ACV goes to $80–120K — wedge becomes a **$160–240M ARR** opportunity without adding a customer.

### Why now, commercially

Retail marketing teams are **already paying for**:

- **Email QA / render testing** — Litmus ($5–15K), Email on Acid ($3–10K)
- **Email AI copy** — Persado ($60–150K), Phrasee ($30–75K), Movable Ink ($50–200K)
- **Site analytics** — Contentsquare ($50–250K), FullStory ($30–120K), Mutiny ($30–100K)
- **VOC + survey** — Qualtrics ($25–100K), Medallia (enterprise-only)

We don't need to create a new budget line; we replace a slice of existing QA / CX / personalization / VOC spend with a capability **none of those tools offer** — first-person persona critique with memory.

---

*Presenter notes:* Show the wedge math, not the TAM chart. Sophisticated investors roll their eyes at $500B TAMs. The wedge math (2,000 brands × $30K = $60M) is what gets believed. Have the existing-spend numbers memorized — they're the answer to "isn't this a vitamin?"
