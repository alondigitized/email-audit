# Slide 02a — The new reader

## Email isn't dying. The reader is changing.

Apple Intelligence, Gemini in Gmail, and Copilot in Outlook already summarize Promotions-tab email by default. The human never sees the hero image — the AI does, and shows them a one-liner.

That's the floor. The trajectory:

```
2024 ──────────► 2026-27 ──────────► 2028-29 ──────────► 2030+
Human reads      AI summarizes       AI filters &        AI buys on
the email        & notifies          recommends          their behalf
(legacy)         (default)           (agentic triage)    (agentic commerce)
```

### What breaks

Open rate. Click rate. Subject-line A/B tests. **Every aggregate metric brands run today is measuring something the AI did, not what the human felt.** When 70% of "opens" are an AI summarizing for triage, those numbers stop predicting anything.

### Why this is etell's wedge, not its risk

Today: *"Would Walker open this email?"* is a useful proxy for whether real Walker would.

2027: *"Would Walker's assistant surface this email favorably to him?"* becomes the actual question. The assistant filters using the same kind of persona-grounded inference etell already runs.

2030: etell is the measurement layer for a world where ESPs send to **agents, not inboxes**. Litmus tells you "did Outlook 2027 render this email." We tell you "did Walker's agent represent this email accurately, and would it have triggered a buy."

### The honest objection — and the answer

> *"Isn't email kind of dying?"*

Email-to-humans is dying. **Email-to-agents is the next measurement layer**, and AI personas are the only way to test for it. Every incumbent measures aggregates of human behavior — which evaporates as agents take over. We measure agent reaction, which is the only signal left when humans stop opening.

The wedge is the same in either world: a synthetic persona reviewing brand creative continuously. We just bet on which world is coming.
