# Slide 03a — Capabilities at a glance

## What etell does, mapped to the questions you just heard.

| Capability | What it does | Which question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Persona panel** | Standing roster of synthetic shoppers with persistent demographic + psychographic identity (Walker 62 comfort-shopper, Martha 34 mom-of-two, Calvin 25 streetwear, Ivy retail auditor, + custom). | *"Who is this even aimed at?"* |
| **Email audit** | Every inbound email scored 1–10 in the persona's first-person voice. Subject + preheader analysis, predicted open/click likelihood, technical QA, screenshot proof. | *"How do real customers perceive our last campaign?"* |
| **Chat with persona** | Ask a persona anything: *"Walker, what was the worst email Skechers sent you this month?"* — answer is grounded in every audit they've ever done. | *"Why did the BOGO bomb with Boomers?"* |
| **Site experience audit** | Daily Playwright-driven journey through a retailer's site (homepage → category → PDP → cart) with persona-perspective review + regression scoring. | *"Did the homepage redesign actually improve, or did we kid ourselves?"* |
| **Inventory audit** | Weekly walk of every PLP — top styles, every (color, width) variant, size availability. Heatmap + downloadable CSV. | *"Are we missing the most-popular sizes in our top styles? In Wide?"* |
| **Competitor tracking** | Same persona subscribes to your competitors' email programs and audits their content alongside yours. | *"What is Macy's running this weekend? Is their mobile signup smoother?"* |
| **Cross-brand benchmark** | One persona reviews your campaign + the competitor's — same lens, same scale. | *"How do my offers compare to the competitor's, today?"* |
| **Persistent persona brain** | Each persona accumulates an Obsidian-style markdown vault of every audit they've ever run; vector-indexed for retrieval. The longer they run, the smarter their critique. | *"Is our customer experience trending up or down over time?"* |
| **Auto-confirm + manual queue** | Brand newsletter signups (DOI / "I'M IN!") are detected, clicked, and logged automatically. Failed clicks fall back to a manual queue with one-click resolution. | *"How do I get this thing running across 5 brands without babysitting it?"* |

Every audit lands on a shareable URL with the persona's first-person review, the screenshot, and a downloadable spreadsheet of the underlying data. **No CSV export wrangling. No PowerPoint redraws. The artifacts are the deliverable.**

The capability matrix lets them see at a glance that etell isn't any of those — it's adjacent to all of them but synthesises across email + site + inventory + competitor in one persona-narrative shape no incumbent has.
