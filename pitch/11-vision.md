# Slide 11 — Vision

## In 3 years, every consumer brand operates a standing panel of synthetic personas.

### Today: email
One persona can read every email a brand sends and critique it in their voice. Useful, but narrow.

### Year 1: full brand surface
The same persona critiques every touchpoint:
- Marketing emails and preview text
- Landing pages and product detail pages
- Checkout flows and cart abandon journeys
- Paid social and display creative
- Customer support email + chat transcripts

One persona, one voice, one continuously growing brain that can answer *any* "what do you think?" question about the brand.

### Year 3: cross-brand benchmarks
With 20+ retailers running persona panels against the same shared brand universe, etell becomes the first platform that can say **"your welcome email underperforms your category average for Millennial moms by 22% — here are three specific creative choices that correlate with the delta."**

This is where persona × time × cross-brand data becomes a defensible business, not a feature.

### Year 5: the persona becomes the interface
Marketing teams stop asking "what's our open rate?" and start asking "what did Walker think of Tuesday's campaign?" The reply is a two-paragraph memo, grounded in 3 years of Walker's recorded reactions. Human QA shifts from subjective taste to curating persona panels that reflect real customer mix.

### The bigger arc: AI is the new reader

By year 5 the human inbox is largely mediated by AI assistants. Apple Intelligence, Gemini in Gmail, and Copilot already summarize Promotions-tab email by default; that line moves from "summarize" to "filter and recommend" to "buy on the user's behalf" over the next 36 months (see slide 02a).

When that happens, **every aggregate metric on the email-marketing P&L stops predicting anything** — because 70%+ of "opens" are an AI assistant's triage pass, not a human reaction. The category of "what Walker's assistant would surface" *replaces* the category of "what Walker opened."

That category needs a measurement layer. Litmus measures rendering. Klaviyo measures sends. **etell measures persona-perspective reaction** — which is the only signal left once the agent takes over the inbox.

We're not building a better email-marketing tool. We're building the measurement layer for the agent-mediated commerce era — wedging in through the email use case while it's still the most concrete pain.
