# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- I opened Temu on my phone and got a plain white "Sign in / Register" page, not the $1 finds and spinning wheel I came for. I can't see a single product or deal until I hand over my email or log in with Google, Facebook, Apple or my phone number. It looks like a clean, trustworthy checkout-style screen, but it has none of the slot-machine excitement that makes me open the app.

## What's working

- **The trust strip** ("Price adjustment within 30 days / Free shipping / Free returns up to 90 days / Delivery guarantee – Refund for any issue"). I return a lot of what I buy, so "Free returns up to 90 days" and "Refund for any issue" are what let me click without worrying.
- **The one-tap social logins** (Google, Apple, Facebook, phone number). They're fast, so I don't have to make up another password.
- **The orange "Continue" button.** It's big, easy to hit with a thumb, and impossible to miss.
- **"All data will be encrypted" and "All data is safeguarded"** in green, at the top and under the heading. They make a site that has had a sketchy reputation feel safer.

## What's weak

- **No deals before the sign-in wall.** I'm not giving my email to a site I can't browse. Nothing here tells me what I'm signing up for: no "$1 finds", no "spin to win", no coupon bundle.
- **No reason to join.** There is no "Get X% off your first order", no free gift and no wheel. The page asks for my email and gives nothing back.
- **"Free shipping – Excludes items shipped from sellers"** with no minimum shown. My $20 hauls are mostly seller items, so I can't tell if I'd actually get free shipping.
- **The page looks like a bank login, not a bargain app.** There's no color, no products and no countdown, so it kills the impulse-buy mood.
- **The footer "Start Selling to Millions of Buyers on Temu"** with a seller photo is aimed at vendors, not me. It's a distraction.

## Recommendations

- Put a first-order offer above the email field, for example an orange banner reading "New here? Get $X off + free gift – claim after sign-in". Sign-up then buys something concrete.
- Add a blurred or small strip of $1 to $5 product tiles with prices behind or beside the form, plus the spin wheel teaser ("Spin to win up to 90% off"). This is the reason I'm here.
- Put the free-shipping minimum in the strip (for example "Free shipping on orders $X+") and say which sellers are excluded.
- Add a "Skip, just browse" or "Continue as guest" link so I can see products first and sign up when I'm ready to check out.
