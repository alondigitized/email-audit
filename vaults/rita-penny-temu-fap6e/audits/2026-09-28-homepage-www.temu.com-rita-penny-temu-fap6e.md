# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- I open Temu expecting the $1 finds and that spinning wheel game, and instead I get slammed into a "Sign in / Register" wall before I've seen a single product. This doesn't look like a bargain-shopping homepage at all — it looks like a bank login screen. If I didn't already know Temu, I'd think I clicked the wrong link.

## What's working

- The four trust badges (Price adjustment, Free shipping, Free returns, Delivery guarantee) are easy to scan and reassuring even before I sign in.
- The "All data will be encrypted" / "All data is safeguarded" lines are front and center, which matters to me since I'm only medium-comfortable with tech and always a little wary of typing my email into a shopping app.
- Social sign-in options (Google, Facebook, Apple, phone) mean I don't have to remember another password — that's the kind of shortcut I actually use.
- The orange "Continue" button is impossible to miss.

## What's weak

- There's NO shopping here. No $1 finds, no spinning wheel, no deals, no products — the thing I downloaded this app for isn't even a scroll away, it's blocked entirely.
- Zero personality or urgency. No "today's deals," no countdown, nothing screaming "come play."
- The only imagery on the whole page is a stock photo of a guy in an apron in the footer pitching "Start Selling to Millions of Buyers" — that's a seller ad, not a shopper hook, and definitely not speaking to me.
- No sense this is even the Temu I know — it reads generic, could be any e-commerce login screen.

## Recommendations

- Let me browse products and see at least a taste of the deals/spinning wheel before forcing sign-in — gate checkout, not window shopping.
- Put a real offer front and center: a specific "$1 deals," a % off banner, or the wheel game teaser instead of a blank login card.
- Swap the seller-recruitment photo in the footer for a lifestyle shot of someone shopping/unboxing — that banner is pointed at the wrong audience for a homepage.
- Add a "New here? See what's on sale" preview link next to the sign-in box so impatient bargain hunters like me aren't stuck staring at an email field.
