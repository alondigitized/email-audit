# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open up Temu and the first big product I see is a power drill kit? That's... not for me. What I *do* clock immediately is that "Lightning Deals" banner in blue, and my brain goes *slot machine time* — so they've got my attention in that one sliver. But overall this landing feels like it got dressed in a hurry: scam-warning banners, no spinning wheels in sight, no personalized "welcome back, Rita" energy.

## What's working

- **"Lightning Deals" banner** — this is my catnip. I see that blue strip and I want to tap it immediately. This is pure slot machine energy and Temu knows it.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" nav tabs** — I can jump to crowd-vetted stuff in one tap, which is exactly how I browse when I'm doing a $20 haul.
- **Free shipping callout at the very top** — I don't even have to hunt for it. It's the first thing I read and it lowers my resistance to impulse adds.
- **Search bar with "trending squishies" placeholder** — cute, feels playful, matches the slightly chaotic bargain-bin vibe I expect from Temu.

## What's weak

- **Power tools as the hero product** — this is not my world. I'm here for $1 finds, random home goods, and cute stuff. A drill kit ad is the first thing I'm scrolling past.
- **The "Be wary of messages about delivery issues" warning banner** — this is alarming to see on a homepage. It tells me there's a scam problem big enough that Temu had to put it front and center. Makes me nervous, not confident.
- **No personalization / returning-shopper hook** — I use this app constantly. Where's my coin balance? My spin wheel? My "based on your last order" module? Cold homepage for a daily user is a miss.
- **"Why choose Temu?" trust copy feels defensive** — brands that are confident don't need to ask and answer their own value prop on the home screen. It reads like damage control.
- **No visible price drops, percentage off, or countdown** — "Lightning deals" is teased but I can't see what's actually on sale or how soon it expires. All sizzle, no steak.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with a spinning wheel or coin-earn CTA above the fold** — that's the whole hook for users like me. "Spin for a mystery deal" or "You have 3 coins — redeem now" would have me tapping before I even scroll.
- **Swap the power tools ad hero for a lifestyle grid of your top $5-and-under finds** — small items, bright colors, stuff you'd impulse-buy at a dollar store. That's the Temu visual identity for women my age.
- **Move the scam warning to a help center link in the footer** — having it on the homepage above the fold poisons the vibe. Handle it with a subtle info icon, not a green banner that screams "we have fraud problems."
- **Add a personalized welcome strip for returning users** — "Welcome back, Rita — your haul is waiting 🛒" with a coin count and recent-browsing shelf would make me feel seen instead of like a first-time visitor every time I open the app.
