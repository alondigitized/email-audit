# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I know Temu, I love Temu — it's basically my digital slot machine. But looking at this homepage right now, it's giving me more "random imports warehouse" than "ooh, what deal did I just unlock." The Lightning Deals banner caught my eye immediately, which is on brand, but then the first actual product they show me is a drying rack ad? Come on. I came here for chaos and $3 earrings, not laundry equipment.

## What's working

- **"Lightning Deals" banner** — that red strip with the lightning bolt is exactly why I'm here. Flashes bargain-brain immediately.
- **Free shipping callout** — it's the very first thing at the top. Even with the "excludes local items" caveat, it sets a positive expectation.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" nav chips** — quick shortcuts I'd actually tap. Both feel trustworthy and aligned with how I shop (social proof + popularity).
- **Search bar pre-populated with "womans clothes trending"** — tells me right away this page knows I'm here for clothes, not power tools.
- **Security warning banner** ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues") — weird flex, but I actually appreciate that Temu is proactively warning me. Feels like they're on my side.

## What's weak

- **The hero AD is a drying rack** — I came for deals, maybe a cute top or some kitchen gadget at 89% off. A clothes-drying stand with measurements labeled "THICKENED" is not the dopamine hit I need at the top of the page.
- **Lightning Deals banner is clipped** — "Limite" is not a word. The most important promo element on the page is cut off on the right side. That's a real miss.
- **No prices, no discounts visible** — I can't see a single "$3.49" or "72% off" above the fold. That's Temu's whole identity. Why is it hiding?
- **No gamification visible** — Where's the spin wheel? Where's the coins? The app has all of that. This web homepage feels like a stripped-down version of what makes Temu addictive.
- **No returning-user hook** — I've definitely ordered from Temu before. Show me my rewards. Show me what's new since I last visited. This feels like a cold-start page every time.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a price immediately** — Put one real deal number (like "Starts at $0.99" or "Up to 90% off") right inside the Lightning Deals banner, before it's even tapped. That's the hook.
- **Swap the drying rack ad for a product in women's fashion or beauty** — The search bar already signals I'm looking at women's clothing. The first featured product should match that intent, not undercut it.
- **Unclip the Lightning Deals banner** — Adjust the horizontal layout so the full text "Limited time" fits on screen without being cut off. It's literally the most important element and it's broken on mobile.
- **Add a returning-user strip** — Even something like "Your rewards: 🪙 340 coins" or "You've got credit waiting" above the fold would make me feel like Temu knows me and wants me back.
