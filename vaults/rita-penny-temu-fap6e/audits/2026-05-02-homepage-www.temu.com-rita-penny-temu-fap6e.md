# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is my people. Temu hits you immediately with free shipping, lightning deals, and a Best-Selling items tab — it's a dopamine machine dressed up as a shopping app, and I am not mad about it. Right now it's pushing whatever these recliner chairs are front and center, which feels random, but honestly that's part of the thrill. This is chaos capitalism and I love it.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **"Free shipping" banner at the very top** — that's the first thing I see, and it immediately removes my #1 objection. Good.
- **"Lightning deals ⚡" strip** — the red bar with the lightning bolt is pure slot machine energy. I want to scroll immediately to see what's in there.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" tabs** — these are exactly how my brain shops. Give me social proof, give me the hits.
- **The "Why choose Temu?" green trust bar** — for a newbie this matters. For me it's reassurance that I'm not getting scammed (again).
- **The search bar is front and center** — I'm usually hunting for something specific like phone cases or cable organizers, so having search right there is correct.

## What's weak

- **The hero product is weird** — "1MM/0.0394in Thickness Alloy Frame Pipe / Max. Weight Capacity: 500lb" zero-gravity chairs as the first product I see? I came here for a $3 hair clip, not patio furniture specs. And it's labeled "AD" which means it's a paid placement, not even Temu's own recommendation.
- **The warning message cuts off mid-sentence** — "Be wary of messages about delivery issues claimi..." I can't even read it. That green bell banner is important (sounds like a scam warning) but it's sliced off the right edge. That's sloppy.
- **"Secure pri..." is cut off** — the security reassurance text gets truncated. That's the one thing you don't want to look broken.
- **Search bar shows "clear bra"** — that's either a pre-filled placeholder or leftover session text, and either way it looks weird and unpolished.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the paid-placement chairs ad with actual best-sellers** — put something cheap and irresistible above the fold, like a $1.99 item with 50k reviews. That's the Temu magic, not a spec-sheet for pipe furniture.
- **Fix the truncated text on the trust/warning banners** — either make those banners scrollable horizontally or rewrite the copy shorter. Clipped security messaging backfires.
- **Make the Lightning Deals banner tappable with a preview** — show me one teaser deal price (like "$0.99 ends in 2:14:07") right on that red bar. Give me the hook before I even tap.
- **Clear or replace the "clear bra" search ghost text** — use a rotating placeholder that shows trending cheap items ("$1 finds", "flash sale picks") to prime me for impulse mode.
