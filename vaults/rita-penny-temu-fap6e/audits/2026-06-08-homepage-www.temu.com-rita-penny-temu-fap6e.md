## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" banner**: Blue background, lightning bolt, "Limited" — that's my slot machine. I will always tap it.

## What's weak

- **Fraud warning in the hero zone**: "Be wary of messages about delivery issues claiming..." is sitting above the shopping content — I get it, Temu has scammer problems, but putting that in my face while I'm trying to get excited about deals kills the vibe completely.
- **AD-labeled hero**: The main image is a sponsored ad, not editorial. That means Temu cares more about ad revenue than showing me relevant products right now.
- **Zero prices anywhere above the fold**: Not one dollar amount, not one percentage off. This is a site famous for $0.99 items. Why am I not seeing that immediately?

- **No gamification visible**: The daily spin wheels, coin games, scratch cards — that's the actual reason I keep coming back. None of it is in this viewport.
- **"Secure priv..." text cut off**: The trust signal is half-readable, which actually makes it less trustworthy.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the AD hero for an editorial "Lightning Deals Top Picks" unit** — show three items under $5 with the original price crossed out. That's the deal-brain dopamine hit I came for.
- **Move the fraud warning to a dismissible banner at the very top or to account settings** — not sandwiched between my nav and my deals.
- **Surface a "Daily Games" or "Spin & Win" shortcut above the fold** — that's Temu's biggest retention hook for habitual users like me and it's invisible here.
- **Add a price anchor in the hero zone** — even a strip that says "Items from $0.49" gets me oriented and excited before I start browsing.
