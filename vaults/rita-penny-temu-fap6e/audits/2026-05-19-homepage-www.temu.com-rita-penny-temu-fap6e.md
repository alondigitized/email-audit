# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Temu opens and my first thought is: where's the spinning wheel? I came here for chaos and discount dopamine — $1 finds, flash deals, all of it — and instead I'm getting a scam warning front and center like I'm being lectured before I even get to browse. The page feels half-loaded, text is getting chopped off on the right side, and the only product I can actually see is an AD for a solar bug zapper. A bug zapper. I didn't come here for that.

## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" strip** — that lightning bolt and "Limited" copy is exactly my language. I see that and my bargain-hunter brain lights up. Good hook if I can actually get to it.
- **"Free shipping" bar at the very top** — I notice this immediately and it lowers my resistance to browsing. Even with the exclusion footnote, it sets a positive frame.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" navigation pills** — as someone who doesn't know what she wants until she sees it, these are genuinely useful starting points for a scroll session.

## What's weak

- **The fraud/scam warning ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues claimi...")** — this is the worst possible welcome mat. I'm barely through the door and Temu is already telling me bad things happen here. It reads as defensive and erodes trust immediately.
- **"Why choose Temu?" banner** — nobody who just navigated to Temu.com needs to be sold on why Temu exists. This is wasted real estate and feels insecure, like a brand that doesn't believe in itself.
- **The AD-labeled bug zapper as the hero product** — a paid ad is literally the first product I see. That's not a curated bargain discovery, that's someone paying for my eyeballs. Feels like a flea market where the vendors bribed the front booth.
- **Clipped text on the right edge** — "Secure priv..." and "5-Star Rated" navigation items are getting cut off. On my phone this looks broken.
- **Search bar pre-filled with "squishy"** — I didn't search for that. Why is it there? That's just confusing.
- **No spinning wheel, no daily deals gamification, no "$1 finds" hook** — the whole reason I'm here as a persona is completely absent from this view.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the scam warning with a proactive trust signal** — swap "be wary of scams" for "Official Temu app only — download here" or a "Verified purchases" badge. Same message, but confident instead of alarming.
- **Put the Lightning Deals rail front and center with a real countdown** — show me three items, prices crossed out, a timer ticking. That's the slot machine pull. Right now "Lightning deals" is just a label with no payload visible.
- **Kill the "Why choose Temu?" banner** — I'm already here. Use that space for a loyalty balance or a "Your $1 finds this week" hook for returning users.
- **Clear the search bar default state** — no pre-filled terms I didn't type. Start with a punchy placeholder like "Search 100M+ items" to reinforce scale.
