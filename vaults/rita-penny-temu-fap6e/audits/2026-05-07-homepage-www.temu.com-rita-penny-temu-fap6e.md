# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Temu is my jam — I literally have it on my phone for the daily spin-the-wheel dopamine hit. But landing on this homepage right now? It's giving me "hardware store clearance aisle." They've got my search bar pre-filled with "womans clothes trending" yet the first thing they shove in my face is a zero-gravity lawn chair marketed as an "Alloy Frame Pipe" with a 500 lb weight capacity. The Lightning Deals banner gives me hope, but everything else above the fold is misfiring.

## What's working

- **Lightning Deals banner** — that red "⚡ Lightning deals" strip with the "Limite..." cutoff is exactly the urgency energy that makes me open the app. Speaks directly to my slot-machine brain.
- **Free shipping callout** — right at the very top, first thing I read. No threshold number visible, but the green truck makes it feel like a win before I've even scrolled.
- **"5-Star Rated" nav tab** — I love social proof. I'd tap that just to see what's hot.
- **"Why choose Temu?" green badge** — feels like reassurance for a skeptic. I've had friends tell me Temu is sketchy, so seeing them proactively address it is smart.
- **Search bar with "womans clothes trending"** — this is clearly reflecting an active or recent search, and at least tells me the site knows women's clothing is a thing here.

## What's weak

- **Hero product: outdoor recliner chairs** — this is the very first product I'm seeing and it has nothing to do with me or my shopping habits. I came for $1 earrings and they're showing me patio furniture with load-bearing specs.
- **"AD" label on the featured product** — the top product slot is an advertisement, not a curated pick. That erodes the "great deals" trust immediately.
- **Scam warning banner** — "Be wary of messages about delivery issues claimi..." is cut off but still alarming. Even if it's Temu trying to protect me, it reads as "something sketchy is happening here," and that kills my mood.
- **No price visible anywhere** — Lightning Deals promises bargains but I can't see a single dollar amount or discount percentage above the fold. That's the whole draw for me and they're making me work for it.
- **Right-side nav items cropped** — there's at least one more nav option after "5-Star Rated" that's completely cut off. Sloppy on mobile.
- **No spinning wheel / gamification hook** — this is Temu! The daily check-in, the coin rewards, the wheel spin — none of it is visible. That's their biggest retention weapon and it's buried somewhere off-screen.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the above-fold product slot with women's apparel or accessories under $5** — if the search history shows "womans clothes trending," surface that category first. A grid of $1.99 earrings or printed tees would make me stop scrolling immediately.
- **Move the gamification/rewards hook above the fold** — show my coin balance, a "spin the wheel" CTA, or a daily check-in counter. That's the hook that keeps Gen X bargain hunters coming back daily.
- **Add a concrete price to the Lightning Deals banner** — "Lightning deals starting at $0.99" or "Up to 80% off ends tonight" gives the urgency copy some actual teeth.
- **Relocate the scam-warning notice** — the "Be wary of..." banner is well-meaning but it's surfaced in the worst possible spot, right between the trust badge and the deals. Put it in a dismissible modal on first visit or in the footer, not wedged into the above-fold experience.
