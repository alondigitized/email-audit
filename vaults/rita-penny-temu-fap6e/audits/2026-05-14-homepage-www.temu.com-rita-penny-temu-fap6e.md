# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Temu and the first thing I see isn't a spinning wheel or a "$1 flash deal — grab it now!" — it's a garden hose and a warning about scam texts. That's... not the vibe. This looks more like a marketplace that's on the defensive than a dopamine machine trying to get me to blow my Tuesday lunch break clicking "Add to Cart." I know Temu can be chaotic-fun, but right now the homepage is giving me "please don't sue us" energy more than "here's your daily prize."

## What's working

- **"Free shipping" top bar** — it's the first thing I read and it's the one thing that makes me not immediately close the tab. Free shipping is my love language.
- **"Lightning deals" cyan strip** — that's the right energy for me. I'm here for deals that feel urgent and slightly unhinged. This strip is at least pointing in the right direction.
- **"Best-Selling Items" + "5-Star Rated" nav tabs** — quick-tap shortcuts that let me browse without thinking too hard. One tap to bestsellers is exactly how I shop.

## What's weak

- **The scam warning banner** ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues claimi...") — I get why it's there, but opening your homepage with a fraud alert is like a casino greeting you with "watch out for pickpockets." It immediately makes me feel unsafe, not excited.
- **"Why choose Temu?" defensive strip** — this reads like a brand that's rattled. I already chose you, I'm literally on your homepage. Sell me something.
- **The garden hose AD** — this is the hero real estate and it's a sponsored expandable hose. I'm not here for that. Where's the chaotic pile of $3 earrings and $6 kitchen gadgets I came for?
- **"needoh" in the search bar** — this looks like a stale or broken state. It feels glitchy, which is already medium-trust at best.
- **Text clipped on right edge** — "Secure priv..." and "claimi..." are cut off. The layout is breaking at mobile width.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the scam-warning banner with a gamification hook above the fold** — a spinning rewards wheel teaser or "Daily check-in bonus: claim your free coins" would immediately shift the mood from defensive to addictive. Move the fraud warning to the footer or a dedicated safety page.
- **Swap the AD garden hose hero for a curated "$5 and under finds" grid** — three or four small products with prices front and center. That's the Temu promise. Lead with it.
- **Surface the rewards/coins balance for logged-in users right in the nav** — "You have 340 coins" is the first thing that should catch my eye. That's my slot machine token count.
- **Fix the mobile layout so text doesn't clip** — the right-edge truncation on the "Secure privacy" and scam-warning banners needs an immediate fix; it looks broken.
