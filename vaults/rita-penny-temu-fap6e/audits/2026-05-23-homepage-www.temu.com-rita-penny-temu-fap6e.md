# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Temu opens and I'm immediately hit with like five things at once — free shipping banner, two nav tabs, a search bar, a green "Why choose Temu?" strip, THEN a green scam-warning banner, THEN a Lightning Deals header, and then I'm staring at a grid of brass garden hose connectors. This is the Temu I know — chaotic, loud, always something happening — but today's vibe is very "plumbing aisle at Home Depot" and that's not why I downloaded this app. I came for the spinning wheels and the $1 lip balm finds, not brass fittings.

## What's working

- **"Free shipping" top banner** — I clock that immediately. It's the first line and it's the thing that makes me feel like I'm winning before I even browse.
- **"Lightning deals" section header** — that word "Lightning" gets my dopamine going. Even if it's cut off, I know what that means and I want to tap it.
- **"5-Star Rated" nav tab** — that's a trust badge dressed up as a filter, and I actually appreciate that framing. It tells me the good stuff is one tap away.
- **"Why choose Temu?" + "Secure priv[acy]"** — the green credibility strip is reassuring, even if it's small. I've had friends warn me off Temu and seeing that makes me feel a little better.

## What's weak

- **The scam-warning banner** ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues claimi...") — this is genuinely alarming to see right on the homepage. I know Temu is trying to protect me but now I'm thinking about scams instead of shopping. Bad placement.
- **Brass garden hose connectors as the hero product grid** — this is the most visible product on the page and it's completely off-target for me. I didn't come here for plumbing hardware.
- **Search bar showing "needoh"** — that looks like a leftover search artifact and it's distracting. Feels unpolished.
- **No spinning wheel, no daily reward, no points balance** — this is literally why I open the app daily and it's invisible above the fold.
- **Four stacked information bars before any real content** — free shipping, nav tabs, search, green strip, warning banner, then deals header. That's five layers of chrome before I see a product.
- **Right side content cut off** — I can see the edge of what looks like a kitchen scene but it's cropped and I can't tell what it is. Feels broken even if it's just a carousel.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the daily reward / spin wheel above the fold.** That's the #1 reason I (and people like me) open Temu every single day. A returning-user hook — even just a "Spin your daily reward" chip — would be the first thing I tap.
- **Move the scam-warning banner to a dismissible toast or the account/help section.** Putting it between the trust strip and the deals header is jarring. It kills deal-hunting momentum right when I'm getting excited.
- **Replace the hose-connector product grid with a "trending under $5" or "women's picks" carousel.** The algorithm probably knows I don't buy plumbing hardware — show me what I actually buy.
- **Clean up the search bar state** — whatever "needoh" is doing there, clear it before the page loads. It looks like a bug.
