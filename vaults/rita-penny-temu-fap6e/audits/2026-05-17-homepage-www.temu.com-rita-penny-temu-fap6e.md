# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is what Temu decided to show me when I opened the app? No spinning wheel, no "you've got a freebie waiting" pop-up — just a green warning banner telling me to watch out for scammers and a garden hose ad. I shop Temu for the chaos and the deals, and this landing experience is giving me neither. It looks less like a treasure hunt and more like a cautious legal disclaimer.

## What's working

- **"Free shipping" banner at the very top** — that's the first thing I want to see, and it's there. Even with the "excludes local items" caveat, it sets the right tone.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" quick-filter tabs** — one tap and I'm in the good stuff. That's how I shop: just show me what other people already bought.
- **"Lightning deals" strip** — this is my language. Limited-time, fast-moving, feels like I might miss something. Even though it's cut off on the right, it's the most Temu-brain thing on this screen.

## What's weak

- **The scam warning banner** — a green bell icon telling me to "be wary of messages about delivery issues" is the last thing I want to see when I'm in deal-hunting mode. It puts me on edge before I've even tapped anything.
- **"Why choose Temu?" green banner** — feels like a pop-up ad from 2009. I already downloaded the app. I don't need to be sold on whether to trust Temu right now.
- **The hero "product" is a garden hose — marked "AD"** — I didn't come here for a garden hose. And knowing it's a paid placement, not an editorial pick, makes me trust it less.
- **No spinning wheel, no "you have credits," no game mechanic** — Temu's whole thing is the gamification. Where's the hook that made me download the app in the first place?
- **Search bar has "needoh" in it** — that's a leftover search term sitting in the field. Sloppy.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the legal warning banner with a returning-user rewards hook** — "You have $3.20 in rewards waiting" would make me tap in two seconds flat. Save the scam warning for a settings page.
- **Swap the garden-hose AD for a curated "Today's Top Deals" grid** — three or four items under $5, mix of categories, no AD label. That's the slot-machine pull I'm looking for.
- **Add a visible gamification CTA above the fold** — a "Spin to save" button or a "Daily check-in bonus: tap to claim" chip. That is Temu's differentiation and it's completely absent here.
- **Clean up the competing banner strips** — pick one: either the trust message OR the lightning deals header. Right now there are three stacked green/blue bars before I see a single product.
