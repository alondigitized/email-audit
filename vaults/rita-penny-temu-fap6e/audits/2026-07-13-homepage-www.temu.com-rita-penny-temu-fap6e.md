# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Temu is basically my guilty pleasure app and this homepage is doing exactly what Temu always does — hits you with the deal signals fast and doesn't waste time. "Lightning deals" right there near the top, free shipping banner front and center. It's not glamorous, it's not pretty, but I know what I'm here for and this page knows it too. That scam warning banner in the middle is... a choice, but I appreciate that they're flagging it even if it makes me pause for a second.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner at the very top** — I'm not spending a dime I don't have to on shipping, so this is the first thing I want to see and it's the first thing I see. Good.
- **Lightning deals section** — This is the slot machine moment I come here for. The ⚡ icon and the "Limited" cut-off already tells me there's urgency, even though I can't read the rest.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" tabs** — For a bargain hunter, both of these are decision shortcuts I actually use. I don't want to dig; I want to be told what's hot.
- **"Why choose Temu?" + "Secure priv[acy]" trust line** — I'm always a little nervous clicking on stuff from Temu's competitors, so seeing that security messaging right below the fold is reassuring even if it's cut off.
- **Scam warning notification** — Actually, yeah, I respect that they put this here. I've gotten those fake Temu delivery texts before. Points for protective honesty.

## What's weak

- **The search bar has "squishy" in it** — That's left over from a previous session, which means my personalized welcome-back experience starts with someone else's search term. Sloppy.
- **Featured products are completely off-target for me**: a tire inflator with centimeter specs in a tiny font and cargo shorts on a male model — neither of these is in my $20 haul vocabulary. No accessories, no home finds, no beauty dupes.
- **Critical text is cut off on the right edge**: "Secure priv[acy]", "Limited[...]" — I'm on a phone and key messaging is getting clipped. That's a layout problem.
- **No rewards or spin-wheel hook above the fold** — The gamification IS why I'm here. Where's my daily login bonus, my lucky draw, my credit balance? That's the slot machine that keeps me opening the app every morning and it's not visible.
- **Scam warning banner in the middle of the page** — I get why it's there but it's right between the trust signals and the deals. It disrupts the flow and makes me second-guess for a second whether I'm even on the real site.
- **"AD" badge on the first featured product** — I clicked on "Lightning deals" to see deals, and the first card is an ad for a tire inflator. That's not a lightning deal, that's a paid placement.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the first product card with a personalized "Your daily deals" module** — Show me the categories I actually buy (home, accessories, beauty) not a tire inflator ad. If they have my purchase history, use it.
- **Add the rewards/credit balance and spin-the-wheel CTA above the Lightning Deals section** — That gamification element is Temu's biggest retention hook for shoppers like me. It should be unmissable, not buried.
- **Fix the right-edge text clipping on mobile** — "Secure privacy" and "Limited [time]" are being cut off. Add proper overflow handling or shorten the copy so the full message lands.
- **Personalize the hero category based on browse history** — If I've been buying kitchen gadgets and earrings, lead with those. The tire inflator is alienating me in the first scroll.
