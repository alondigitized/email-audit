# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly the chaos I expect when I open Temu — deals stacked on deals on deals, red slashed-out prices everywhere, and a "sign in" popup shoving itself in my face before I even got a good look at the lightning deals. It reads like a bargain-bin flea market that got turned into an app, which honestly? That's the appeal. Right now it's pushing Lightning Deals and a "Fresh Deals" interest rail, heavy on cheap gadgets and summer stuff.

## What's working

- The **Lightning Deals** orange banner up top — that's my slot machine trigger, I want to tap that immediately
- Prices like **$4.92** and **$6.99** front and center with the strikethrough "was" price — that's the dopamine hit I'm here for
- "Sold" counts (13K+ sold, 64K+ sold) — makes cheap junk feel validated, like other bargain hunters already vetted it
- "Shop now, pay later" strip with Klarna/Afterpay/Affirm/PayPal — reassuring even if I don't usually need it
- "Fresh Deals - Explore Your Interests" category pills — easy one-tap browsing into stuff I'd actually dig through

## What's weak

- That **sign-in popup** ambushing me over the "Why choose Temu?" banner — I didn't ask to sign in, and it's blocking text I was trying to read
- No spinning wheel, no coin/points balance, nothing gamified visible on this screen — that's literally why I downloaded the app and it's not here
- No "welcome back" or recently-viewed anything — feels like it doesn't know I'm a regular
- The 85%-off foam pillow with only 2 reviews sitting next to 6,853-review items — feels like a placement mismatch, kind of undercuts trust
- No actual "Add to Cart" or "Shop" button anywhere — just price tags, so it's a bit passive for people who want a satisfying tap

## Recommendations

- Kill the sign-in modal on first load, or at least don't let it cover the "Why choose Temu?" trust copy — let me read that first
- Put a spin-the-wheel or coupon-game banner right under the top bar — that's my actual hook, not buried
- Add a visible points/coins balance near the cart icon so I feel like I'm "winning" something by being logged in
- Swap that low-review foam pillow out of the lead deals row for something with more social proof — it drags the whole row down
