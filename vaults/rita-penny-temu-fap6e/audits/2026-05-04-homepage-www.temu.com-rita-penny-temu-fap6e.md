## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" strip in hot pink** — that flashing bolt icon and "Limite..." cutoff is pure slot-machine bait. I am clicking that. This is literally why I have the app.
- **Free shipping callout at very top** — first thing I see. Removes my biggest hesitation before I even look at products.
- **"5-Star Rated" tab** — I trust social proof over brand copy, and it's right in the nav. Easy to tap.
- **Scam warning banner** — weirdly reassuring. Temu telling me "hey, watch out for fake delivery texts" makes me trust them *more*, not less.
- **"Why choose Temu?" trust strip** — quick credibility at a glance, even if it's cut off.

## What's weak

- **720LBS wagon as hero ad** — this is an *AD*, labeled right there. The hero slot on a homepage is premium real estate and they're burning it on a cargo wagon I have zero interest in. Feels like a TV commercial for truck tires during a cooking show.
- **No prices or % off visible above fold** — Lightning Deals is teased but I can't see any actual deal yet. The payoff is hidden.
- **"Squishy" in the search bar** — that placeholder is weirdly specific and a little jarring. Feels like someone's leftover search, not a curated prompt for me.
- **No spinning wheel / gamification visible** — Temu's whole hook for me is the spin-to-win mechanic and there's zero sign of it above the fold. Miss.
- **"Secure pri..." cut off** — the trust bar is clipped. If you're gonna say "secure privacy," finish the sentence.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the wagon AD out of the hero slot** — or at least rotate in a deal grid of $1–$5 items with visible prices. That's the dopamine hit that keeps me scrolling.
- **Add a visible countdown timer to the Lightning Deals banner** — "Ends in 2:14:33" turns a banner into a trigger. Right now "Limite..." is doing almost no work.
- **Surface the spin wheel or daily reward above the fold** — this is a known retention lever for Temu's daily-active crowd. I open the app for this. Put it on the homepage.
- **Show me a price** — literally one product with a crossed-out price and a new price anywhere above the fold. That's the signal that gets me to scroll.
