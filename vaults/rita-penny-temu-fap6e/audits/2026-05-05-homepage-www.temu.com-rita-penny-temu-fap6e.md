# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**

- **5/10**
- + Concrete offer visible above fold: "Free shipping / Excludes local items" banner is right at the top
- + Primary CTA unmistakably visible: "Sign in for the best experience" with Google button and "Sign in / Register" — hard to miss

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner** — the green truck icon + "Free shipping" is the very first thing I see at the top, and for someone doing $20 hauls weekly, that's the right message to lead with
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" tabs** in the navigation — I love a ranked list, it's basically Temu's version of a leaderboard for deals
- **Search bar with "womans clothes trending" placeholder** — at least they're signaling they know who shops here; women's stuff, trending = right language for me
- **Google sign-in option** — medium tech comfort means I appreciate the one-tap login instead of having to create yet another password

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal is killing the vibe** — I came here to browse deals, not to log in before I've seen a single item. This is the number-one thing that would make me hit the back button
- **The visible "ad" product (zero-gravity chairs) is completely random** — 500lb capacity patio chairs are not what pulls me into a Temu session; this doesn't match my bargain-hunting, fashion-adjacent shopping mode
- **"Why choose Te…" and "Be wary of me…" text partially visible behind the modal** — those are chopped off and look janky, even if it's just because the modal is overlapping
- **No urgency, no spinning wheel, no countdown** — the slot-machine dopamine loop that keeps me coming back isn't visible at all above the fold
- **No prices, no percentage-off badges, no "$1 finds" hook** — the whole reason I use Temu daily is invisible right now

## Recommendations

- **Delay the sign-in prompt by at least one scroll** — let me see one juicy deal first before you ask for my login; a cold sign-in wall is a bounce trigger
- **Lead with a "$1 Finds" or "Today's Drops" banner instead of the modal** — that's the slot-machine energy that makes Temu sticky; put it front and center
- **Swap the generic patio-chair ad for a curated "Trending in Women's" product strip** — if you already know I'm searching women's clothes, show me that content instead of a sponsored chair
- **Add a visible countdown or flash-sale strip above the fold** — even a simple "Deal ends in 2:47:30" would re-create that daily-push-notification urgency I already trained myself to respond to
