# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Oh okay, Temu, I see you. This looks like the app version of a dollar store that exploded — orange logo, free shipping banner, and a big ol' sign-in pop-up before I've even seen what's on sale. Right now it's pushing Best-Selling Items and 5-Star Rated stuff, though I can barely see any of it because this login modal is eating half my screen. Classic Temu.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner at the very top** — that's the first thing I see and it's the right opener for someone like me who checks shipping cost before anything else
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" tabs in the nav** — these are exactly how I browse Temu; I want social proof and volume, not editorial picks
- **Sign-in with Google** — one tap, no typing, I'll actually do this; it removes friction once I decide to log in
- **"Why choose Temu" and "Be wary of messages" blurbs** partially visible behind the modal — at least they're trying to address trust concerns upfront

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal fires immediately before I've seen a single product** — I came to browse deals, not to create an account; this is the number-one reason I'd tap out
- **Search bar is pre-filled with "squishy"** — that's someone else's search, which looks like a bug or a weird personalization miss; it's jarring
- **The hero product visible below the modal is zero-gravity patio chairs labeled "AD"** — feels like an ad unit masquerading as featured content, not the $1 impulse-buy energy I come here for
- **No countdown timers, flash-sale banners, or spinning wheel anywhere visible** — Temu's whole addiction loop is the urgency and gamification; none of that is above the fold here
- **"Excludes local items" immediately under the free shipping claim** — fine print right in the banner erodes the opening hook

## Recommendations

- **Delay the sign-in modal by at least one scroll** — let me see two or three deal rails before asking me to log in; give me the slot-machine dopamine hit first, then catch me mid-browse
- **Replace the patio-chair ad unit with a flash-deal countdown strip** — even a simple "Ends in 2h 14m" with a $0.99 find would triple the click-through for a bargain hunter like me
- **Clear the "squishy" pre-fill from the search bar** — either blank it or show my recent searches if I'm logged in; a stranger's search makes the site feel glitchy
- **Bring a loyalty/coins-balance badge into the top bar** — I know Temu has a rewards system; showing my coin balance or "spin the wheel" CTA above the fold is the hook that keeps me coming back daily
