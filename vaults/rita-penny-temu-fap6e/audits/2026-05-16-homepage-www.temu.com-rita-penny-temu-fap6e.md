# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Oh yeah, I know Temu — it's my guilty pleasure app. First thing I see is "Lightning deals" in that bright teal bar and I'm already reaching for my wallet. This homepage is leaning hard into the deal-first experience, which is basically designed for me. Though I'll be honest, a garden hose was not what I expected to see front and center — and what's that scam warning banner doing there?

## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" strip** — that bright teal bar with the lightning bolt is crack for me. That's literally why I open the app daily.
- **"Free shipping" top bar** — it's the first thing I see, and even with the "Excludes local items" caveat right underneath, at least they're upfront about it.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" nav tabs** — I tap "5-Star Rated" every single time. That's my filter for finding the actual keepers in my $20 hauls.
- **The scam warning banner** ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues...") — weirdly reassuring. It shows Temu knows their rep and is trying to protect me. That's something.
- **Search bar front and center** — "squishy" in there, which is either a past search or placeholder. Either way, I use that search bar constantly.

## What's weak

- **The hero product is a garden hose (and it's an AD)** — nothing says "we don't know you" like leading with a sponsored garden hose. I want a spin wheel or a "$1 finds" teaser, not a hose.
- **No spinning wheel / gamification hook visible** — that's Temu's #1 engagement mechanic for people like me and it's nowhere above the fold.
- **Loyalty or reward balance totally absent** — I have Temu credits. Why aren't they showing me what I have to spend?
- **"Lightning deals" banner is cut off on the right** — "Limited" trails into nothing. I can't see the deadline or the deal, which kills the urgency before it even starts.
- **No person in any imagery** — the garden hose and a partial product on the right edge, no lifestyle, no model, nothing I can relate to.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the sponsored garden hose hero with the gamification entry point** — show the spin wheel or a "$1 Flash Finds" carousel. That's the Rita bait that actually converts.
- **Surface the user's Temu credits/coupon balance in the top bar** — even a small "You have $3.20 in credits" line next to free shipping would make me shop instead of browse.
- **Expand the Lightning Deals banner to show the countdown AND one teaser item** — don't truncate the urgency. Give me a timer I can see, not a word that gets cut off.
- **Swap the "AD" garden hose for a curated "$20 haul" editorial block** — show four or five wildly varied items under $5 each. That's the Temu promise and it's not on screen.
