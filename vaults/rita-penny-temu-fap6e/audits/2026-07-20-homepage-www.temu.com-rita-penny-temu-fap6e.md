# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I open Temu and the first thing I see is a scam warning and a search bar that says "squishy." Like, this is very on-brand Temu energy — a little chaotic, a little suspicious of itself. It's clearly pushing Lightning deals and best-sellers, which is my whole reason for being here, but the homepage feels more like a clearinghouse than a curated experience.

## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" banner (orange, prominent):** That orange flash icon and the word "Limited" peeking out — that's the dopamine trigger I came for. I'll tap that every time.
- **"Free shipping" in the top sticky bar:** I see it before anything else. That's the hook that got me to open the app in the first place.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" nav tabs:** Love a curated filter. If I don't know what I want (and I never do), these are my starting points.
- **Product image visible (wooden garden path):** Tappable, clear image, I can see the product. Temu's always been good at making products look fancier than they are, and this is no exception.

## What's weak

- **Scam warning banner ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues claiming…"):** Temu putting a fraud alert on its own homepage is a red flag dressed up as a trust signal. It makes me nervous right when I'm about to spend money.
- **"Squishy" in the search bar:** I don't know if this is a saved/residual search or a suggested query, but it's disorienting. It makes the page feel like I'm in someone else's session.
- **No personalization whatsoever:** I've shopped here dozens of times — where's my rewards balance, my daily spin, my "based on what you browsed" rail? None of that is visible.
- **Product selection above fold is random:** A garden walkway and what looks like bedding — these aren't wrong, but they're not targeted. For someone who checks the app daily, I want to see what's new and hot, not just whatever product happened to load.
- **Truncated text everywhere:** "Secure priv–" and "claiming–" are both cut off. On mobile, this reads as broken.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic product image rail with a "Today's $5 and Under" or "Daily Finds" section above the fold** — that's literally why bargain hunters open this app every day.
- **Add a visible daily spin/reward balance chip near the top** — returning users want to see "You have 47 coins" immediately; that alone drives daily opens.
- **Move the scam warning to a dismissible modal or a help-center link** — a fraud alert on the homepage hurts conversion more than any scam protects against.
- **Pin Lightning Deals as a sticky tab in the nav** (not just a banner mid-page) — it's the biggest conversion lever they have and it should be one tap from anywhere.
