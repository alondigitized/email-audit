# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing Temu does is throw a sign-in pop-out at me before I can even see what's on sale — I came to browse, not to log in. The search bar already has "womans clothes trending" typed in which is kind of what I'm here for, but then the actual product I can see is a car jump starter? And below that looks like patio furniture? This feels like Temu being Temu: chaotic, with "Lightning deals" teasing me just out of reach like a scratch-off I can't finish scratching.

## What's working

- **"Free shipping" strip at the very top** — I see it instantly, that's the first thing I need to know
- **"Lightning deals" label** — even half-hidden behind the modal, that's my jam; I will scroll for it
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" nav tabs** — quick ways to find what's already proven without digging
- **Search bar pre-loaded with "womans clothes trending"** — at least the app knows why I'm here

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal fires immediately** — half the screen is a login sheet before I've seen a single deal; I did not come to register, I came to spin the wheel
- **Jump starter as the hero product** — it's an AD, it's not relevant to me, and it's taking up premium real estate
- **The fraud warning ("Be wary of messages about delivery issues claiming...")** — this is a red flag waving at me on the homepage; it makes me feel like something sketchy already happened or is happening
- **No visible countdown timer or specific deal number** — "Lightning deals" text is there but I can't see how long I have or what the discount is
- **No price anchoring visible** — not a single "was $29.99, now $4.99" moment above the fold

## Recommendations

- **Kill the immediate sign-in modal — or at least delay it** by one scroll or one tap; let me see two or three flash deals first so I have a reason to log in
- **Replace the jump starter AD with a women's fashion item or a trending category carousel** since the search bar is already hinting that's what I'm looking for
- **Make "Lightning deals" the actual hero** — show a countdown timer and two or three products with crossed-out prices; that's the slot machine pull that keeps me here
- **Move the fraud warning off the homepage** or reframe it as "Shop safe" trust messaging; "be wary" on the front door is not the vibe
