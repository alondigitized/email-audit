# Homepage snapshot · www.temu.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I know this site — it's my guilty pleasure and this homepage is giving me the usual Temu chaos, just on mobile. It's clearly running that "Lightning Deals" slot-machine energy I'm here for, but the first real product I see is a garden hose? That's not exactly pulling me in. The scam warning sitting right there in the trust banner is a weird vibe — like the store putting a sign on the door saying "don't trust anyone here."

## What's working

- **"Lightning deals" banner** — that cyan strip with the bolt icon hits exactly the right nerve for me. Limited-time, special-deal energy. That's the slot machine pull.
- **"Free shipping" at the very top** — I always check this first. It's front and center where I need it.
- **"Best-Selling Items" and "5-Star Rated" tabs** — two-tap access to the most-validated products. Good shortcut for someone like me who hauls fast.
- **Search bar prominent and pre-populated** — "squishies" in the bar tells me they're surfacing trending searches, which is fun to browse.

## What's weak

- **The trust banner is a mess** — "Be wary of messages about delivery issues claiming..." is cut off AND it's a fraud warning on their own site. That's the first thing below the nav and it immediately makes me nervous instead of excited.
- **The hero product is a garden hose** — labeled AD, not personalized, not remotely aimed at me. That's filler, not a hook.
- **No price visible anywhere above the fold** — I came here for the $1 finds. Where are the numbers? Show me the deal.
- **No rewards or points balance** — I have the app, I've been spinning. Show me my status or what I've earned. A returning user deserves a welcome back.
- **Right-side content is clipped** — there's clearly a third nav tab cut off, and both lines of the green banner are truncated. Feels unfinished.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the fraud-warning banner with a rewards/balance teaser** — "You have 340 points — redeem today!" turns a trust detractor into an engagement hook instantly.
- **Show prices on the above-fold product tiles** — even "From $0.99" or a crossed-out original price. I need to see the number to believe the deal.
- **Swap the garden hose AD for a personalized "based on your last haul" module** — Temu knows what I buy. Use it. Even a "Because you liked squishies" rail would land better.
- **Fix the clipped green banner text** — either shorten the copy to fit the mobile viewport or use a carousel. Cut-off text reads as broken, and broken reads as sketchy.
