# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- HelloFresh's mobile homepage immediately hits me with a gamified interstitial — three mystery boxes asking me to "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" before I can even see what's behind it. The brand clearly knows acquisition is the game right now, and the whole above-the-fold experience is subordinated to capturing me with a discount before I can actually evaluate the product. It's scrappy, aggressive, and a little exhausting.

## What's working

- **Modal renders cleanly** — no layout breakage, the HelloFresh branding is consistent on the boxes, and the cream/green palette is on-brand and crisp.
- **"Continue Without Offer" escape hatch** — I appreciate that I can skip it without closing a tiny X button. That's actually respectful UX for someone like me who hates being trapped.
- **Gamification concept** — In theory, a mystery-box mechanic is psychologically engaging. For a first-time visitor, the curiosity hook is real.

## What's weak

- **Offer is completely hidden** — I cross-shop Blue Apron, Home Chef, and Marley Spoon. I want to see "Get $120 off your first 5 boxes" before deciding to play along. Hiding the number behind a game is friction, not fun, for someone who's already comparison-shopping.
- **Zero product content visible** — I cannot see a single recipe, menu variety signal, or ingredient photo. The entire homepage is blocked. For me, recipe variety is the deciding factor — this modal erases it entirely.
- **"New subscribers only" exclusion** — visible in the fine print at the bottom. If I'm a lapsed subscriber thinking about coming back, I immediately feel penalized.
- **No loyalty or returning-customer hook whatsoever** — nothing for me to log in to, no "welcome back" surface, nothing.
- **The background hero is completely obscured** — I can see a partial "M" and what looks like a vegetable photo behind the modal, but I can't read or evaluate any of it.

## Recommendations

- **Show the offer number upfront** — "Get up to $120 off" with the mystery box as the mechanic to choose *which* deal, not to reveal *whether* there's a deal. That resolves my comparison-shopping anxiety immediately.
- **Add a "Returning customer? Log in" link inside the modal** — costs nothing, removes the alienation of "new subscribers only" for lapsed users.
- **Delay the modal by 3–5 seconds or one scroll** — let me see at least one recipe image and the menu variety pitch before gating me. I need to want the product before I'll play the game.
- **Swap the mystery-box CTA copy** — "Reveal Your Offer" is fine, but add a subline like "Pick a box — unlock your first-box deal" so it's clear I'm choosing between discount tiers, not just waiting for a random result.
