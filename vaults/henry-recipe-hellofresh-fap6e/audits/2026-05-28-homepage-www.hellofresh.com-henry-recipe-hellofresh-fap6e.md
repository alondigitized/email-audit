# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- I open HelloFresh and the entire viewport is swallowed by a gamified interstitial — three branded gift boxes and the headline "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" I can't see a single recipe photo, a meal plan, a price, or a navigation menu. HelloFresh is pushing a mystery-discount mechanic before I've even seen what I'm buying. As someone who cross-shops specifically on recipe variety and despises surprise charges, this landing experience is immediately off-putting.

## What's working

- **Modal renders cleanly on mobile** — no layout breakage, text is sharp, the three boxes are evenly spaced and tappable.
- **"Continue Without Offer" escape link** — at least there's a visible opt-out that doesn't bury itself in fine print. A user who just wants to browse can skip it.
- **Logo/nav bar is visible above the modal** — I can see the HelloFresh wordmark, so I know I'm in the right place.

## What's weak

- **Mystery-box mechanic is a direct conflict with my core preference**: I hate surprise charges. An opaque "reveal your offer" mechanic reads as bait-and-switch before I've even seen a recipe.
- **The entire homepage is inaccessible**: hero, meal plans, recipe previews, pricing tiers — all obscured. I can't evaluate what I'm even signing up for.
- **No recipe or food imagery above the fold**: there's a tiny sliver of what looks like a dish at the very bottom edge of the screen, but it's barely visible and cropped out by fine print.
- **The gamified offer is not time-bounded**: no countdown, no "ends today" — so the urgency play doesn't even land.
- **Fine print at the bottom** ("*Only... subscription. Free meals... new sub...") suggests the offer has meaningful restrictions, but I can't read it fully. That's exactly the kind of buried caveat that makes me distrust a brand.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the mystery-box modal or make the offer explicit**: show "Get 10 free meals + free shipping on your first box" directly — I'll respond to a clear value prop faster than a prize-reveal mechanic.
- **Let me see food before you ask for anything**: even a background hero of a plated weeknight meal would establish context; right now the page looks more like a scratch-card app than a meal kit service.
- **Surface the offer in a banner strip, not a full-screen takeover**: a persistent top banner with a clear discount code lets me browse while staying aware of the promo.
- **Add a recipe-variety proof point above the fold**: a "Choose from 40+ recipes a week" callout directly addresses the comparison shopper like me who's deciding between this and Blue Apron.
