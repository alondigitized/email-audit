# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- I landed on HelloFresh's homepage and the first thing I see is a gamified mystery-box interstitial blocking everything behind it. As someone who compares meal kit services on recipe variety and pricing, hiding the actual offer behind a reveal mechanic feels more like a casino slot pull than a brand I'm supposed to trust with my weekly grocery budget. HelloFresh is clearly pushing acquisition right now, but this execution tells me nothing about why I should pick them over Blue Apron this week.

## What's working

- **Single focus forced by the modal** — despite being annoying, there's zero visual clutter; you know exactly what the page wants you to do
- **"Continue Without Offer" escape link** — giving me an out prevents the hard-trapped feeling; I appreciate that it's there and not tiny
- **HelloFresh branding on the boxes** — consistent green brand color, logo on the boxes, clean typography at the top; at least it looks professional

## What's weak

- **The mystery-box mechanic itself** — I'm a high-tech, price-conscious 33-year-old who cross-shops three services. Hiding a discount behind a slot-machine reveal doesn't feel clever; it feels like they don't trust their offer to stand on its own
- **Zero recipe or food content above the fold** — the entire reason I'm here is to see what meals are on the menu this week. There's literally no food imagery in the active viewport (I can barely see some blurred food at the very bottom edge behind the modal)
- **No personalization or returning-user recognition** — I've been a customer before; nothing here greets me or references my account status
- **No offer specificity** — "Reveal Your Offer" could be 10% off or free shipping or a free add-on. Without a number, I have low motivation to click; I might just navigate away
- **Navigation is inaccessible** — the hamburger menu is visible in the top-left but the modal blocks any content exploration

## Recommendations

- **Show the actual offer upfront** — replace "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" with a banner that states the discount directly, e.g. "Get 18 Free Meals + Free Shipping" (whatever the real number is). Transparency beats gamification for this demographic.
- **Surface one food image or menu teaser inside the modal** — even a single recipe card thumbnail inside this interstitial would connect the promo to the reason I'm here (food), not just acquisition mechanics
- **Add a returning-customer path** — if a logged-in or cookied user lands here, swap the modal for a "Welcome back — skip weeks or pick new recipes" CTA that acknowledges prior relationship
- **Put a deadline on the offer** — if this is time-limited, say "This week only" with a date; credible urgency is a conversion lever that doesn't require hiding the number
