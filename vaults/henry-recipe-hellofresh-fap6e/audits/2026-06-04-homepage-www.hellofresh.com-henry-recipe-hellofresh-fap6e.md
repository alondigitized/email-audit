# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- HelloFresh is leading with a gamified mystery-box modal that swallows the entire viewport before I can see a single meal, recipe, or price. The brand feels like it's trying to manufacture intrigue where transparency would serve it better — I can't see the hero, the menu variety, or any reason to trust them yet. Right now it reads less like a meal-kit brand and more like a sweepstakes site.

## What's working

- **Modal headline clarity** — "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" is at least direct about the mechanic; I know what to tap
- **Brand recognition** — the HelloFresh green-and-white logo reads instantly; no confusion about where I landed
- **Escape hatch** — "Continue Without Offer" is prominently placed, which is more honest than a fake X button buried in a corner

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked** — I can't see a single recipe, price, or value proposition before being forced to engage with a gamified prompt
- **Mystery-box mechanic feels manipulative** — as someone who "hates surprise charges," hiding the offer behind a lottery-style reveal is the opposite of the transparency I need to trust a subscription brand
- **Fine print at the bottom** — partially visible text about "subscription" and "new subscribers only" undercuts the playful framing; I see the strings
- **No recipe imagery above the fold** — the food shot behind the modal is barely visible; I have no idea what the variety or quality looks like
- **No navigation accessible** — only a hamburger menu icon; I can't jump to meal plans, pricing, or dietary filters without first dismissing the modal

## Recommendations

- **Show the offer directly** — replace the mystery-box mechanic with a banner or hero strip that states the discount upfront ("Get 16 free meals on your first 6 boxes") — transparency converts better than gamification for subscription skeptics
- **Let the homepage breathe behind the offer** — if you must use a modal, make it smaller (bottom sheet or corner card) so the hero imagery and recipe variety are visible and building desire simultaneously
- **Add a "I'm already a subscriber" link** — returning visitors skipping weeks are a core audience; the current modal has no path for them and just creates friction
- **Surface the fine print in the modal body**, not below the fold — "for new subscribers only" should sit right under the CTA, not buried in light gray text I have to squint to read
