# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- I open HelloFresh and instead of a homepage I get a full-screen "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" game with three identical green boxes to click before I can see anything. This feels less like a meal-kit brand and more like a scratch-off lottery ticket — and given I already know my way around Blue Apron, Home Chef, and Marley Spoon, I don't need a gimmick, I need the actual deal.

## What's working

- The three HelloFresh-branded box icons are at least on-brand and instantly recognizable as "this is a meal kit."
- "Continue Without Offer" is present as an escape hatch — annoying, but at least there's a way out instead of a full lockout.
- "Pause or cancel anytime" is peeking through at the bottom, which is the one line that actually matters to me as someone who skips weeks when traveling.

## What's weak

- The entire above-the-fold is a gamified mystery-box modal — "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" — instead of just telling me the discount. I hate surprise charges, and a brand that won't show me the number up front makes me trust it less, not more.
- Real hero copy is visibly cut off behind the modal (a truncated headline fragment at top-left, "Ge[t X] Free Meals!*" clipped at the bottom) — that's a layout bug, not a design choice.
- The primary "GET STARTED" CTA is greyed out and inert until I play the box game — that's friction for zero reason.
- That asterisk on "Free Meals!*" is exactly the kind of buried-fine-print move that makes we weeknight subscribers assume there's a catch (delivery fee, first-box-only, etc.).
- No recipe imagery, no menu preview, nothing that tells me what I'd actually be cooking this week — which is the entire reason I'd compare HelloFresh against Home Chef or Marley Spoon.

## Recommendations

- Kill the box-opening game and just print the offer plainly: "Get 50% off your first box" (or whatever it is) as real hero text, not a reward for clicking.
- Make sure the actual hero headline and CTA render behind/before any modal — right now real content is visibly clipped, which reads as broken.
- Replace the asterisk gimmick with the full terms inline, small but readable, so I'm not guessing what "Free Meals!*" actually means.
- Add a "This week's menu" teaser strip right under the hero so a recipe-variety shopper like me has something concrete to judge before I even sign up.
