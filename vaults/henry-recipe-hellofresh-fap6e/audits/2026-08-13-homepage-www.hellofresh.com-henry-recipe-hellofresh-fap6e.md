# Homepage snapshot · www.hellofresh.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- First thing HelloFresh does is throw a "shake the box to see what you win" game in front of me before I've even read the headline — that's not a homepage, that's a slot machine. Feels like a brand that's more interested in gamifying my first impression than telling me what a box actually costs or what's on this week's menu.

## What's working

- **"Take Back Dinnertime!" headline** — direct, no fluff, speaks to the actual problem I'm solving on a Tuesday night.
- **"30 minutes or less" and "Pause or cancel anytime"** — that second line is doing real work for someone like me who skips weeks when traveling and doesn't want to feel locked in.
- **"91% of our customers feel healthier" stat** — light social proof, easy to read at a glance.

## What's weak

- **The "Open A Box To Reveal Your Offer!" modal** — I hate surprise charges, and this is a mystery-box UX pattern slapped on top of pricing. If you have a deal, just tell me the number.
- **It's covering the actual page** — I can't even finish reading the hero subcopy or see the "From qu[ality]... [st]ress, and" line underneath it. That's a layout fight, not a feature.
- **No visible menu preview or recipe variety** — as someone who cross-shops three meal kit services specifically for menu variety, I need to see actual dishes, not a stock photo of green boxes.

## Recommendations

- Kill the "shake the box" gimmick and put the actual discount (e.g., "$80 off your first box") directly in the hero — I'll trust a number I can see over one I have to "unlock."
- Swap the generic green-box product shot inside the modal for a real menu preview — 3-4 actual dish photos with names, since that's what gets a recipe-variety shopper to scroll.
- Let the hero copy finish rendering before slapping a modal over it — right now it reads like a bug, not a promo.
- Add a "this week's menu" or "new this week" rail right under the fold so I can judge recipe variety without digging through nav.
