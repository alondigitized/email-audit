# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is classic Disney homepage energy — confident, warm, not trying too hard. I see a laughing Black family on what looks like a water attraction, the iconic tagline, and two no-nonsense buttons to either book hotels or grab tickets. It reads like a brand that knows it doesn't have to sell you on the concept of Disney, just get you moving toward the transaction. Right now it's pushing trip initiation, not a deal.

## What's working

- **Family-on-a-water-ride hero image**: Immediate emotional resonance — that's the laugh I'm planning to manufacture for my kids. I read it in under a second.
- **"Get Tickets" CTA**: Zero ambiguity. One tap and I'm in the purchase flow. For a returning buyer like me, that's all I need.
- **"Build Your Vacation" quick-access row**: This is the exact mental model I have when I hit the site 7 months out. Seeing it surface immediately without scrolling tells me the information architecture knows why I'm here.
- **"Today's Park Hours" quick-access row**: I check this obsessively when I'm close to a trip — smart utility placement.
- **Pause button on the hero video**: High-tech-comfort signal. I appreciate that Disney doesn't autobomb me with sound and gives me control.

## What's weak

- **No Genie+ or Lightning Lane mention anywhere above the fold**: That's literally why I'm here. I'm a second-time Genie+ buyer tracking Lightning Lane windows — the homepage has no idea.
- **No Disney+ subscriber hook**: I pay for Disney+ every month. There's no acknowledgment that this makes me an existing relationship, not a cold visitor.
- **No pricing or offer signal**: "Get Tickets" with no anchor price or current promotion makes me feel like I have to brace for sticker shock. Even a "from $109/day" would lower my guard.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" is the first CTA**: It's positioned above "Get Tickets," which buries the more urgent action for a returning visitor who already has her resort picked out and just needs to finalize tickets.
- **The hero is video but I can't tell what ride that is from the freeze frame**: The Tiana's Bayou Adventure iconography on the kid's shirt is barely legible on mobile. A returning fan would catch it — a first-timer has no context.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Genie+ / Lightning Lane teaser in the hero or immediately below it**: Even a small "Make the most of every day — Lightning Lane access available" line would speak directly to planners like me.
- **Add a Disney+ member badge or returning-visitor module**: "Disney+ subscribers save on select ticket packages" requires almost no lift and converts a passive subscriber into an active buyer.
- **Flip the CTA order**: Put "Get Tickets" above "Explore Resort Hotels" — the majority of return visitors already know where they're staying.
- **Add a price anchor below the CTAs**: "Theme park tickets from $X/day" or "Ticket + hotel packages from $X" removes my hesitation and keeps me in the funnel instead of opening a comparison tab.
