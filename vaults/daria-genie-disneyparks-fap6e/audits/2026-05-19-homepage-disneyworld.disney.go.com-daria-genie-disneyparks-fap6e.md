# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Disney's classic "welcome to the dream" homepage — aspirational hero, two clean CTAs, and absolutely zero planner infrastructure. I recognize this brand immediately and I love it, but as someone who's already done this before and has a 7 AM Lightning Lane alarm set on my phone, the page is handing me a gorgeous front door with no key. It's selling the feeling, not the system.

## What's working

- **The hero image** — kids losing their minds on a ride is the emotional payoff I'm literally paying thousands of dollars for. It hits immediately.
- **"Get Tickets" CTA** — bottom button is direct and purposeful. I don't have to hunt for it.
- **"Build Your Vacation" accordion link** — exactly the language I use in my head when I'm in planning mode. That label speaks to me.
- **"Today's Park Hours" link** — practical utility right below the fold. Shows Disney knows some visitors are already on-property or in research mode.
- **Video hero with pause button** — appreciated that they give me control. I'm not waiting for an animation to finish to find the CTA.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+ / Lightning Lane mention** — I am a second-time Genie+ buyer who sets alarms. This is Disney's premium upsell product and it is completely absent from the viewport. That's a significant revenue miss on high-intent returners.
- **No My Disney Experience integration** — there's no "sign in / your upcoming trip" hook anywhere. If I booked 7 months ago and came back to manage my trip, I'd have to dig through the hamburger menu.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" leads with the hotel, not the package** — for a trip planner like me, the bundle (hotel + ticket + Genie+) is the decision, not just the hotel room. The CTA framing is transactionally narrow.
- **No seasonal or event signal** — I can't tell what's happening at the parks right now. No Festival of Fantasy dates, no MNSSHP presale teaser, nothing time-sensitive that would make me engage today vs. next week.
- **Evergreen tagline does no heavy lifting** — "The Most Magical Place On Earth" is a brand truth, not a reason to click. I already know this. Tell me what's NEW.

## Recommendations

- **Inject a Lightning Lane / Genie+ teaser below the hero CTAs** — something like "Planning your trip? Add Lightning Lane from $15/person" with a one-tap link. High-intent returners will respond immediately.
- **Add a "Sign In / My Trip" persistent entry point in the header** — replace or supplement the hamburger with a recognizable My Disney Experience icon. I should be able to see my reservation in two taps.
- **Swap the hero copy for a seasonal hook** — whatever is actually happening in May 2026 at WDW (a festival, a new attraction, a summer event) should be the headline. "Most Magical Place On Earth" could live in a subhead.
- **Surface a ticket-bundle callout in the utility links** — add a third row below "Today's Park Hours" that says something like "Ticket + Hotel Packages" to mirror how planners like me actually think about the purchase.
