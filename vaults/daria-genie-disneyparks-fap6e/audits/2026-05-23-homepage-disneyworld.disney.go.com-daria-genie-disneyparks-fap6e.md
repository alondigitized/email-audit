# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is pure Disney brand confidence — "The Most Magical Place On Earth" over a hero image of a joyful family on what looks like Tiana's Bayou Adventure, two clean pill-shaped CTAs, done. It knows it doesn't have to fight for attention. But as someone who opens the Disney site specifically to price-check ticket bundles and check Genie+ availability, I hit this page and immediately feel like I've landed in the lobby instead of the planning desk. It's gorgeous and it's generic in the same breath.

## What's working

- **The Tiana's Bayou Adventure family image** — that laughing kid soaking wet is exactly the emotional signal I need. I sent this exact vibe to my family group chat when we booked last year.
- **"Get Tickets" pill CTA** — it's right there, above the fold, on mobile, impossible to miss. One tap and I'm in the funnel.
- **"Build Your Vacation" quick link** — the accordion row below the hero is clean shorthand for a returning planner who knows what she wants.
- **"Today's Park Hours" quick link** — I check park hours obsessively during the planning phase. Having it two taps from the homepage is genuinely useful.
- **Video hero with pause control** — the pause button in the corner is a small but respectful UX touch. I'm on mobile data; I appreciate not being forced through an autoplay reel.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+/Lightning Lane mention above the fold** — I am literally the person who sets a 7 AM alarm to book Lightning Lane drops and there's no acknowledgment that this product exists anywhere I can see. That's a miss.
- **No ticket bundle or price hook** — Disney's ticket-plus-park-hopper-plus-Genie+ bundle is a real SKU I'm trying to price-compare against Universal's equivalent. Nothing here invites that conversation.
- **"Welcome to The Most Magical Place On Earth" is doing zero differentiation work** — I already know where I am. That headline is the brand talking to itself, not to me.
- **Memorial Day weekend invisible** — it's May 23rd and this page could be any day of the year. No seasonal urgency, no "Summer's almost here" hook, nothing that rewards me for showing up right now.
- **No returning-guest acknowledgment** — I've booked here before. There's no logged-in state cue, no "Continue planning your trip," no MagicBand or reservation shortcut. A high-repeat purchaser like me gets treated like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Plan your Lightning Lane strategy" or "Genie+ included with select tickets" callout strip** directly below the hero CTAs — one line, one tap, zero friction for the planner segment who already knows this product by name.
- **Swap the generic hero headline for something seasonally specific**: "Summer 2026 is filling up — grab your dates" with a soft urgency nudge tied to actual booking windows. It's still on-brand and it actually earns the visit.
- **Surface a logged-in / returning guest state** — even a "Continue your vacation plans" link above the fold for authenticated users would make me feel like the site knows me after two trips.
- **Add a ticket-bundle teaser strip** ("Tickets + Genie+ from $X/day — see what's included") between the hero and the quick links. I'm comparison-shopping and giving me a starting price keeps me on-site instead of opening a new tab.
