# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- I landed on this and thought, "Okay, a warm, family-friendly fall Disney page with a pretty hero." It's pushing "Festive Fall Fun" and a resort and package search, with special offers starting just below. I plan 7 months out and live in Lightning Lane and Genie+ land, so nothing here helps me with that. It feels like a page built for someone who hasn't decided to go yet.

## What's working

- **The search card ("Resorts & Packages" / "Tickets & Passes" tabs):** It sits right under the hero and is easy to scan. Dates, Guests and the Accessible Rooms checkbox are all in one row, so I can start planning fast.
- **The "Pro tip: For a room-and-ticket package, choose your hotel first":** This is useful, honest guidance. I appreciate a brand telling me how to avoid a booking mistake.
- **The hero photo:** A real family laughing and sharing food, with Mickey tees. It looks like my crew, not a stock-photo couple.
- **The top nav (Parks, Tickets & Passes, Places to Stay, Dining):** It's short and clear, and I know where to go in one tap.
- **"Save with Special Offers" with "View All Offers":** The section is easy to find, and the dollar and percent amounts are right in the card titles.

## What's weak

- **The hero ("Dig In to Festive Fall Fun"):** It's cute, but it says nothing about tickets, Genie+ or Lightning Lane, which is why I'm here.
- **The six-dot carousel indicator:** There are six slides, and I can only see the first. I won't wait around for the one that might be about ticket deals.
- **The search button:** It's a lone magnifying glass with no label like "Search Stays" or "Check Availability". The page doesn't say what it does.
- **"Resorts & Packages" is the default tab:** I'm a Genie+ buyer who cross-shops Universal. I'd want tickets front and center, and that's the second, greyed-out tab.
- **Offer cards:** They're cut off at the fold. "Save Up to $250/night on Select Packages" tells me nothing about dates, resorts or deadlines.
- **No loyalty or returning-guest nod:** I've bought Genie+ twice, and the page doesn't know me.
- **The floating "Help" tab on the right edge:** It's a little clunky on mobile and hovers over the hero image.

## Recommendations

- Swap the first carousel slide for a ticket or Lightning Lane message, like "Plan Your 2027 Trip: Tickets + Lightning Lane Drops Explained". Put the food-festival slide second.
- Replace the icon-only search button with a labeled one ("Search Stays" or "Find Dates"). Make "Tickets & Passes" the default tab for returning visitors, or remember the last tab used.
- Add a returning-guest strip under the search card, like "Welcome back, Daria. Your Genie+ and Lightning Lane window opens in 7 days." It should link straight to My Plans.
- Put an expiration or booking-window label on every offer card, like "Book by Nov 30" or "Stay Jan 3–Jun 12, 2027". Show a "from $X/night" price instead of "Up to $250".
