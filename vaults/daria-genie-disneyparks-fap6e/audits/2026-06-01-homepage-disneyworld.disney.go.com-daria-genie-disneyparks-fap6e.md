# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I see is a Black family completely losing it on what looks like Tiana's Bayou Adventure — pure joy, wet faces, genuine laughter — and I feel that in my chest. This is Disney at its best: it knows exactly how to sell the feeling. But as someone who's been on this site a dozen times already planning this trip, the homepage is treating me like a first-timer who doesn't know what Disney World is, rather than a returning booker who needs to get to my reservation or check on Genie+ availability.

## What's working

- **The hero image is doing heavy lifting.** A real family on what reads as Tiana's Bayou Adventure, completely overwhelmed with joy — this is the exact emotional promise Disney sells, and it hits. I feel it.
- **Two-button CTA structure is smart.** "Explore Resort Hotels" and "Get Tickets" are the two decisions a new planner needs to make, and they're right there, no hunting required.
- **"Build Your Vacation" shortcut below the hero.** This accordion row is exactly what a planner like me wants — a direct path to itinerary tools without going through the nav.
- **"Today's Park Hours" row.** I check park hours constantly during planning season. The fact that it's a single tap from the homepage is a win.
- **The video hero (pause button visible).** The pause icon tells me this is a video loop of real ride moments — that's high production value and emotionally effective.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+ / Lightning Lane visibility above the fold.** I wake up at 7 AM to grab Lightning Lane drops. I've bought Genie+ twice. There's no acknowledgment that this product even exists from the homepage — that's a miss for returning buyers who want to optimize.
- **No returning-user flow.** This homepage is identical whether I've booked 10 times or never been. Disney has my data. There's no "Pick up where you left off" or "Your reservation" hook that acknowledges I'm a repeat customer.
- **"Welcome to The Most Magical Place On Earth" is evergreen to the point of being invisible.** I've read this headline a hundred times. It says nothing about what's new, what's happening now, or why I should care today specifically.
- **No seasonal or new-attraction callout.** The bottom of screen is teasing something with a sun graphic but I can't tell what — it's cut off. There's no "New this summer" or specific attraction mention above the fold.
- **No price anchoring anywhere.** As someone who cross-shops Universal and obsesses over bundle value, I want to at least see a hint that tickets or packages start at a certain point, or that there's a deal for booking in advance.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Manage My Trip" or "My Plans" persistent button in the header** for logged-in returning visitors — a simple "Hi, Daria — view your reservation" strip would keep repeat planners from bouncing to My Disney Experience through a separate URL.
- **Surface Genie+ / Lightning Lane Multi Pass as a hero secondary CTA** or a sticky banner: "Already booked? Add Lightning Lane" — this is money on the table for returning buyers like me.
- **Swap the evergreen headline for something date-specific**: "New This Summer: [specific attraction or event]" — give me a reason this visit matters more than my last one.
- **Add a small proof-of-value element below the hero CTAs** — even just "Trusted by millions of families each year" or a TrustPilot rating badge — something that closes the loop for anyone comparison-shopping Universal.
