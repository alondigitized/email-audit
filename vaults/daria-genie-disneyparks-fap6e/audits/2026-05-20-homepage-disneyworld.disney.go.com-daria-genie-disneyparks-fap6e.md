# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly the Disney site I know — clean, on-brand, and emotionally it works immediately with those laughing kids on what looks like Tiana's Bayou Adventure. But as someone who's already booked, already bought Genie+, and is back here to figure out Lightning Lane strategy or maybe bundle a hotel upgrade, the homepage doesn't really know I exist. It's a pure acquisition page — "Welcome to The Most Magical Place On Earth" — as if I've never been here before.

## What's working

- **The ride hero image** — kids laughing on what looks like Tiana's Bayou Adventure is an emotional gut-punch. That's my kids. That's what I'm paying for. It earns the "Most Magical Place" line.
- **Dual CTA pill buttons** — "Explore Resort Hotels" and "Get Tickets" are visually distinct, clearly labeled, and stacked cleanly. I know exactly where to go.
- **"Build Your Vacation" quicklink** — below the hero, this is the link that actually matches my mental model of what I came to do. Fast to reach.
- **"Today's Park Hours" quicklink** — practical, helpful for someone mid-planning like me who's checking hours obsessively at this stage.
- **Video hero (pause button visible)** — the fact that it's a video and not a static shot suggests there's motion/emotion behind the fold; I'm curious enough to not immediately tap away.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-visitor recognition** — I booked Genie+ last trip and I'm back here planning again. The page treats me like a first-timer. No "Manage your reservation," no "Lightning Lane Multi Pass is now on sale," no loyalty status.
- **No Lightning Lane / Genie+ mention above the fold** — this is a $30/day upsell that Disney really wants me to buy, and it's invisible in the viewport. That feels like a huge missed revenue moment.
- **No pricing or offer anchoring** — as someone who tracks ticket bundles compulsively, I'm looking for a deal signal the second I land. Nothing. Not even a "Save when you bundle" nudge.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" leading CTA order** — if I'm coming for tickets (which I am), the hotel CTA getting top billing is slightly backward. Hotels are a longer decision; tickets close faster.
- **No trust/social proof signals** — no "X million guests visited in 2025," no star ratings on featured experiences, nothing that validates the magic for a cross-shopper comparing vs. Universal.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Plan My Trip" returning-visitor banner** — a thin strip above or below the hero: "Already booked? Manage reservations, add Lightning Lane, or check Park Hours" with one tap to MDE (My Disney Experience). Ship it as a sticky header component; it serves the high-intent returner without disrupting acquisition.
- **Bring Lightning Lane Multi Pass into the hero CTA layer** — swap "Explore Resort Hotels" for "Add Lightning Lane" or make it a third pill. This is Disney's highest-margin digital product and it's not on screen at all.
- **Swap CTA order** — move "Get Tickets" to the top pill and "Explore Resort Hotels" to the second. Tickets close faster and that's the primary conversion event for most visitors landing here.
- **Add a seasonal urgency strip** — it's late May, Memorial Day weekend is imminent, summer crowds peak in July. A one-line banner like "Summer dates filling fast — book now" costs nothing to ship and converts planners like me who are mid-anxiety about availability.
