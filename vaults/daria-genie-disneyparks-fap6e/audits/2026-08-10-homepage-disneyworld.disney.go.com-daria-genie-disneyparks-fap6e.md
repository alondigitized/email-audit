# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads as classic Disney World — big emotional hero, kid joy front and center, "Most Magical Place On Earth" tagline doing exactly what it's supposed to. Right now the site is pushing vacation-package building (the Check In/Check Out/Adults/Children widget) and a 4-Day, 4-Park Magic Ticket deal at $109/day. It's warm and on-brand, but as someone who's already deep into planning mode and specifically hunting Lightning Lane info, I don't see anything here yet that speaks to Genie+ or my return-visitor status.

## What's working

- The "Build Your Vacation" widget sitting right at the top is genuinely useful for someone like me who books direct — I can start a date search immediately.
- "Get Tickets" as a distinct CTA from "Explore Resort Hotels" is smart segmentation — I know exactly which one is mine.
- The hero photo (two kids laughing, one crying-laughing, "Shortcut to the Bayou" shirt referencing Tiana's Bayou Adventure) feels current and authentic, not stock-photo stiff.
- The $109/day 4-Park Magic Ticket teaser starting to show at the bottom is the kind of concrete number I screenshot and text to my husband.

## What's weak

- Zero mention of Genie+ or Lightning Lane anywhere above the fold — that's literally the thing I'm checking for every morning at 7 AM, and it's nowhere in sight.
- No personalization or loyalty cue for a returning shopper — I've bought Genie+ before, and there's no "Welcome back" or account-linked hint, just a generic "Log In or Create Account."
- The trip-builder widget defaults to hotel search, not ticket/Lightning Lane search, which buries my actual use case under a broader "book a resort" flow.
- "Save with Special Offers" section with the sun-and-swirl divider feels a little too whimsical/decorative for how information-dense I want this page to be — I'm here to compare park tickets, not admire clip art.

## Recommendations

- Add a "Genie+ & Lightning Lane" quick-link chip near the top nav or inside the trip-builder widget — I look for this by name.
- Surface a "Welcome back, [name]" or recently-viewed-tickets module for logged-in returning guests instead of a generic login link.
- Move the $109/day ticket offer fully into the fold (right now it's cut off at the bottom) — that's the strongest above-the-fold conversion driver you have.
- Swap the decorative sun-divider graphic for a tighter promo strip showing 2-3 offers side by side (tickets, resort, Genie+) so I can compare at a glance.
