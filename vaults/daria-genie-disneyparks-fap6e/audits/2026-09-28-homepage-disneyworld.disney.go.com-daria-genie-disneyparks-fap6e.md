# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads like classic peak-season Disney marketing — polished, seasonal, and built around a family enjoying snacks at Epcot with a "Dig In to Festive Fall Fun" headline. It's pushing fall travel and stay/ticket bundles hard, but there's nothing here yet about Lightning Lane, Genie+, or park reservations — the stuff I actually check obsessively every morning.

## What's working

- **"Dig In to Festive Fall Fun" hero** — on-season, warm, and the image (grandma, mom, kid sharing snacks) feels like an actual family trip, not a stock photo of strangers.
- **The booking widget sitting right under the hero** — Resorts & Packages vs. Tickets & Passes tabs, Dates, Guests, Accessible Rooms — that's useful real estate, not buried in a menu.
- **"Pro tip: For a room-and-ticket package, choose your hotel first"** — small but this is exactly the kind of insider guidance that makes me trust the site knows what it's doing.
- **Save with Special Offers section immediately below the fold** — I know it's coming even if I have to scroll one more inch.

## What's weak

- **Nothing about Lightning Lane or Genie+ anywhere above the fold.** I'm literally the person tracking 7 AM drops and this homepage doesn't even whisper it.
- **The booking widget defaults to "Resorts & Packages," not "Tickets & Passes."** As a second-time Genie+ buyer, tickets are my entry point, not a room.
- **The search button is just a magnifying glass icon** — no "Search Stays" or "Find Availability" label, so it's not an unmistakable CTA.
- **The three offer cards are chopped off at the bottom of the screen** ("Save Up to $250/night on Select..." and "Save Up to 20% this Holiday S..." literally cut mid-word) — feels unfinished on first load.
- **No loyalty or rewards signal** — I've booked with them before, nothing here says "welcome back" or references anything I've planned.

## Recommendations

- Add a "Tickets & Passes" default tab option or a visible shortcut like "Check Lightning Lane availability" near the booking widget.
- Give the search button real button copy — "Check Availability" — instead of a lone icon.
- Reframe one of the three offer cards above the fold instead of letting all three get cut off — even just showing the full first card would help.
- Surface a light "Welcome back" or recently-viewed-park module for return visitors like me who've already been through the planning gauntlet once.
