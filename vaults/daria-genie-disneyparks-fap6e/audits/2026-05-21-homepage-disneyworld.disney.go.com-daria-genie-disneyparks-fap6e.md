# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- OK, this is exactly the Disney site I know — reassuringly familiar, fully functional, and honestly a little too generic for how much I already know about this brand. It's pushing the big two: hotels and tickets. The hero is a warm family-on-a-ride shot that hits me in the heart as a mom, but there's nothing on this screen that says "hey, returning planner — here's what's new or what's on sale right now."

## What's working

- **Family ride photo** — that shot of kids laughing mid-ride (looks like the Bayou Adventure splash area) is genuinely joyful and makes me want to be there. It doesn't feel stock; it feels real.
- **"Get Tickets" CTA** — pill button, icon, clear copy. One tap. That's what I'm here for half the time.
- **"Build Your Vacation" accordion row** — I plan 7 months out. Seeing a dedicated trip-builder entry point right below the hero tells me the site understands the planning mode I'm in.
- **"Today's Park Hours" row** — fast utility link for when I'm deep in planning and need operational info quickly. That's a small but smart inclusion.
- **Clean mobile layout** — nav is minimal (hamburger + search), no crowding. On a phone at 7 AM before a Lightning Lane window opens, this matters.

## What's weak

- **"The Most Magical Place On Earth" headline** — this copy has existed since before I was born. There is zero campaign-specific messaging, no seasonal hook, nothing that tells me what's new or why to come *this summer* specifically.
- **No Genie+ / Lightning Lane mention anywhere** — this is the #1 thing I research before every trip and it's completely absent from the first screen. That's a miss for someone like me.
- **No returning-visitor recognition** — I'm logged into Disney+ and I've bought tickets before. The site treats me like a total stranger. A "Welcome back, plan your next visit" hook or a Disney+ subscriber discount callout would do a lot here.
- **No offer above the fold** — nothing. No "Save up to $X on tickets," no "Free dining offer ends soon," no bundle pricing. If I'm cross-shopping Universal right now, Disney just gave me zero financial reason to stop.
- **Pause button on the hero** — suggests there's a video playing behind the still image, but on mobile it creates a weird visual interruption in the top-right corner of the hero.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic tagline for a campaign-specific hook**: Something like "Summer Nights at Magic Kingdom — Lightning Lane now open" tells me exactly what's happening *now* and triggers my planner brain to engage.
- **Add a Disney+ subscriber discount ribbon or returning-visitor CTA**: Even a small "Disney+ members save on tickets →" banner above the hero would make me feel seen instead of anonymous.
- **Surface a ticket bundle or seasonal offer above the fold**: Show me a dollar amount or a percentage. "Tickets from $109/day" or "Save $X with a 5-Day Park Hopper" stops the Universal comparison in its tracks.
- **Add a "Plan Lightning Lane / Genie+" quick-link** in the utility row alongside "Build Your Vacation" — it's the thing I check obsessively and right now I have to dig for it.
