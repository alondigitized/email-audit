# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is exactly what I expected from Disney's mobile site — clean, confident, very "we know who you are and why you're here." The brand is leading with pure emotion (that family water ride shot is *chef's kiss*) and two strong primary CTAs above the fold. It's not trying to do too much, which I appreciate, but as someone who's already deep in the planning funnel — I have dates, I have my park days mapped, I need Lightning Lane strategy help — this homepage is greeting me like a first-timer and that's a little frustrating.

## What's working

- **The hero photo** — laughing family on what looks like Tiana's Bayou Adventure (I can see the blue shirt with Tiana graphics). That's a real Disney moment, not stock art, and it hits me emotionally right away.
- **Two-button CTA layout** — "Explore Resort Hotels" and "Get Tickets" stacked neatly; no decision paralysis, no five nav items competing.
- **"Build Your Vacation" and "Today's Park Hours" quick links** — those two utility rows below the fold line are genuinely useful. "Today's Park Hours" is exactly the kind of thing I check at 7 AM before I open the Genie+ window.
- **Clean mobile render** — hamburger menu, Walt Disney World wordmark, search icon. Nothing is broken or overlapping. Fast-feeling layout.

## What's weak

- **No returning-user recognition** — I've booked here before. There's zero acknowledgment of that. No "Continue planning your trip" module, no My Disney Experience hook, no account CTA.
- **"Get Tickets" as the primary CTA feels off for me** — I already have tickets. I'm here to plan the day, check Lightning Lane availability, maybe upgrade my tier. This CTA is aimed at the funnel top, not at me.
- **No active offer or deal** — Nothing says "Summer savings," "Save on tickets through August," or "Book a package and save." Disney runs promos constantly; not surfacing one here feels like a missed upsell.
- **Hero copy is totally generic** — "Welcome to The Most Magical Place On Earth" is the brand's eternal tagline. There's no hook to a current event, new attraction, or seasonal moment. I've seen this headline a hundred times.
- **No Genie+ / Lightning Lane mention anywhere** — This is literally one of the most Googled Disney planning terms right now. Not a whisper of it above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Plan Your Day" or "My Reservations" CTA** for returning users — even a soft "Continue where you left off" link tied to My Disney Experience login would make me feel seen instead of anonymous.
- **Swap the generic tagline for a time-anchored hook** — Something like "Tiana's Bayou Adventure is open — Lightning Lane drops at 7 AM" would make me stop scrolling immediately and feel like Disney is speaking *to planners*, not tourists.
- **Surface one concrete offer in a thin banner above the hero** — Even "Save up to 25% on select resorts this summer" gives me a reason to dig deeper instead of going straight to the app.
- **Add a "Lightning Lane / Genie+" quick link** in the utility row alongside "Build Your Vacation" — it's the #1 thing power users like me come to the site to research.
