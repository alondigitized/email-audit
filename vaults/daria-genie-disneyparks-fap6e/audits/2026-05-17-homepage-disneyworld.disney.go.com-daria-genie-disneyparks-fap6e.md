# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Disney doing what Disney does — leads with pure emotional joy and trusts the brand to close the deal. The hero is a laughing family mid-ride and the tagline is literally "The Most Magical Place on Earth," which tells you exactly where they're putting their weight. But as someone who books direct, plans months out, and has already been burned by Genie+ confusion once, this homepage is doing almost nothing to help me get to the planning tools I actually need.

## What's working

- **The family hero photo** — a real-looking Black family mid-splash, all laughing hysterically. That's the exact emotional permission slip I need to justify spending $6K on this trip. It's not a castle shot, it's a feeling shot.
- **Dual above-fold CTAs** — "Explore Resort Hotels" and "Get Tickets" cover the two biggest planning decisions I'm making simultaneously right now. I don't have to guess where to start.
- **"Build Your Vacation" quick link** — below the hero, this is exactly what I'm here to do. That label is doing real work.
- **"Today's Park Hours" quick link** — for someone who is already planning and checks this obsessively before a trip, this is a gift on the homepage.
- **Clean mobile rendering** — the viewport is tight, no horizontal scroll, icons are readable, button text is fully visible. This loads like it was built for a phone.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+ / Lightning Lane surface area** — I track Lightning Lane drops at 7 AM. There is nothing on this homepage acknowledging that exists. Disney knows I'm going to spend $30+ per person on it; they're leaving that hook completely off the table.
- **No Disney+ member recognition** — I pay for Disney+ and there's reportedly member pricing. None of that is visible. If the whole funnel is supposed to start here, this is a missed personalization layup.
- **"Welcome to The Most Magical Place on Earth" is not a value prop** — it's a slogan. It gives me no new information. Every Disney trip I've ever booked started with me already knowing this. Use the headline real estate for something that moves me forward.
- **No pricing or offer anywhere** — not even a "packages from $X" or "save when you bundle." I'm cross-shopping Universal right now and Universal shows me dollar figures upfront. Disney is asking me to click into a funnel completely blind.
- **The pause button is the only interactive affordance I notice on the hero** — which means this is a video I didn't choose to play. On mobile, that's a data and attention choice being made for me without consent.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Genie+ / Lightning Lane teaser in the CTA row** — add a third pill button or a strip below the hero: "Plan Your Lightning Lane Strategy →". It costs nothing to put it there and it's exactly what a planner-type like me would tap immediately.
- **Add a Disney+ member pricing badge near the "Get Tickets" CTA** — something as simple as "Disney+ members save 10% — sign in" would dramatically increase conversion from subscribers who don't know this offer exists or forgot to apply it.
- **Replace the generic tagline with a seasonal or planning-specific hook** — "Summer 2026 dates are open — build your trip" is infinitely more useful than "The Most Magical Place on Earth" for someone who already believes the brand.
- **Add a "start planning from $X" anchor number somewhere above the fold** — Universal does this. Removing sticker shock early increases click-through; hiding pricing until checkout creates anxiety that makes me shop around.
