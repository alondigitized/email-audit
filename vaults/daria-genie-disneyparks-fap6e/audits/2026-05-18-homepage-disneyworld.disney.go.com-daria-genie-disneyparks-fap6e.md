# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Opening this on my phone while I've got seven months of research bookmarked feels like walking into Disney's lobby — the brand is immaculate, the vibes are right, but nobody at the front desk is actually talking to me. This homepage reads as a top-of-funnel awareness play, not a tool built for the person who already knows she wants Genie+ and is price-comparing ticket bundles at 7 AM. It's beautiful. It's also almost completely non-actionable above the fold.

## What's working

- **"Get Tickets" CTA** — unambiguous, icon-supported, and above the fold. For someone arriving with intent to book, this is the exact right button to surface first.
- **Family lifestyle hero image** — the kids-laughing-on-a-ride scene is genuinely aspirational for a parent planning a trip. I felt something. That matters.
- **"Build Your Vacation" quick-link** — this is the planner's on-ramp and I'm glad it's on the first screen. One tap and I'm in research mode.
- **"Today's Park Hours" link** — practical, date-aware, shows the site understands that guests have ongoing operational questions.
- **Clean, fast-rendering mobile layout** — no clutter, no modals attacking me on load, no CLS. The pause button on the video hero is a nice touch; I appreciate not being forced to sit through it.

## What's weak

- **Zero personalization for a returning guest** — I've booked here before. My Disney Experience exists. There is no "Hi, Daria" or "Your upcoming trip" shortcut. A second-time Genie+ buyer is invisible to this homepage.
- **No ticket bundle offer or pricing signal** — I came here partly to compare ticket tier costs. Nothing above the fold (or in the visible below-fold modules) tells me what anything costs or what's on promotion.
- **No Lightning Lane / Genie+ visibility** — this is the product I actively track and plan around, and it doesn't exist on this screen. That's a miss.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" as co-equal CTA** — putting hotel exploration at the same visual weight as "Get Tickets" dilutes the hierarchy for someone who already has accommodations locked.
- **No seasonal or event hook** — May 2026 and there's no mention of summer, a festival, a new attraction, or anything time-specific. Feels like a static template.
- **No trust signals whatsoever** — no guest review counts, no award badges, no "Official site" credibility marker for someone who's cross-shopped third-party resellers.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Explore Resort Hotels" with a Genie+ / Lightning Lane CTA** — or at minimum add a third button row for trip-planning tools. The person arriving at this URL already knows where Disney World is; she needs to get into her planning dashboard.
- **Add a thin promo banner above the hero** — one line: "Summer 2026 ticket packages available — bundle and save." Even a hint of an offer stops a comparison shopper from bouncing to Undercover Tourist.
- **Surface a My Disney Experience login shortcut** in the top nav or below the hero. The hamburger menu buries it; returning planners should see it in one tap.
- **Drop a seasonal urgency signal below the hero CTAs** — something like "Popular summer dates filling fast — check availability" with a link. Costs nothing to copy-write; converts planners who are still circling.
