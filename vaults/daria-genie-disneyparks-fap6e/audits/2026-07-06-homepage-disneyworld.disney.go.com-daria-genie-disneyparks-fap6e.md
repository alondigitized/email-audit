# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- This is classic Disney — polished, joyful, and completely on-brand — but it's talking to everyone, which means it's barely talking to me. I've bought Genie+ twice, I'm seven months deep in trip planning, and the page greets me with "The Most Magical Place On Earth" and two buttons. I appreciate the family-on-a-ride energy but I came here with specific intent and this gives me zero hooks to go deeper fast.

## What's working

- **The hero image.** A real family mid-laugh on a water ride — that's literally the photo I'm trying to recreate in December. It hits the emotional bullseye without being cheesy.
- **"Get Tickets" CTA.** Big, pill-shaped, sits at the center of the screen. My thumb knows exactly where to go.
- **"Build Your Vacation" utility row.** Just below the fold, this row with the luggage icon is exactly my planning mode. It signals the site can handle multi-step trip-building, not just impulse ticket sales.
- **"Today's Park Hours" link.** That clock icon at-a-glance tells me this site knows park-obsessed people check hours constantly. It's a small thing that says "we get you."
- **Video hero with pause control.** The ⏸ button in the corner tells me this is a video, not a static image — real motion probably makes the ride moment hit harder. Accessible and respectful of users who want to pause.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+ or Lightning Lane signal.** I track LL drops at 7 AM and there is nothing on this page acknowledging that product exists. For a high-intent returning buyer like me, that's a gap.
- **No Disney+ subscriber hook.** I am literally a subscriber. Disney has my email. A single "Log in to see member benefits" prompt would make me feel recognized.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" is the wrong first CTA for a returning visitor.** I already have a hotel. Lead with tickets or planning tools, not hotel discovery — that's a first-timer journey.
- **No promotional incentive whatsoever.** No "book by X date and save," no bundle deal, no seasonal offer. It's peak summer and the page is transactionally blank above the fold.
- **The "Help" floating tab.** The jutting right-side "Help" pill looks like a support widget and reads as friction, not an invite to explore.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Explore Resort Hotels" for a "Plan Your Visit" or "Genie+ Add-On" CTA** — returnig guests aren't hotel-shopping, they're optimizing. Put the planning tool front and center for the repeat buyer.
- **Add a thin personalization bar at the top:** "Disney+ subscribers save on select tickets — log in to see your rate." One line, massive signal to me that you know who I am.
- **Surface a time-bounded offer in the hero area** — even a subtle "Book tickets for Fall before prices increase July 31" badge would convert me faster than a blank call to action.
- **Add a second image/video showing Genie+ or Lightning Lane in action** (phone tapping, the Lightning Lane entrance) — it's a differentiator Universal can't match and Disney never promotes it visually.
