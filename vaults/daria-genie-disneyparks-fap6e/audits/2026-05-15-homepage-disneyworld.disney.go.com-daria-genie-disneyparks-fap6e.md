# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expected from Disney World — big hero energy, magical family moment front and center, and the brand confidence of a place that knows it doesn't really need to sell you. It's pushing hotel bookings and ticket purchase as its two priorities, and the lifestyle image of a family losing their minds on a water ride is genuinely delightful. What it is *not* doing is talking to me, a repeat guest who already knows Genie+ exists and is here to figure out what's new, what the best bundle looks like, and whether the ticket prices have changed since I last booked.

## What's working

- **The hero photo:** That family — laughing, soaking wet, pure joy — is the single best argument for buying a Disney ticket. It does emotional work instantly.
- **Dual CTA layout:** "Explore Resort Hotels" and "Get Tickets" side-by-side as equal-weight pills is smart. I'm a direct-book person and both of those are exactly where I'd go.
- **"Build Your Vacation" and "Today's Park Hours" utility links:** These are exactly the kind of low-friction, high-intent links a planner like me reaches for the moment I open the app. Clock icon for hours, suitcase for itinerary — I didn't have to think.
- **Pause button on the video:** Small detail, but I appreciate that they give me control over the autoplay rather than just blasting motion at me while I'm trying to read.

## What's weak

- **Zero Genie+ or Lightning Lane surface-level mention:** I track Lightning Lane drops at 7 AM. I came to this homepage to plan. There is no signal anywhere that Disney knows I do this, or cares that I do this.
- **No price anchoring at all:** Not even a "Tickets from $109" teaser. For a second-time buyer actively cross-shopping Universal, that hesitation to show pricing above the fold is a miss.
- **"The Most Magical Place On Earth" is the headline:** It's iconic, I love it, but it's doing brand work, not conversion work. I already believe it. Tell me what's *new* for 2026.
- **No seasonal or current-campaign hook:** It's May 2026 — are there summer promotions? Special events? A new ride? Nothing on this screen suggests the page is updated for right now versus any other month of the year.
- **No returning-member recognition:** No "Sign In" prompt front and center, no rewards balance teaser, no Disney+ subscriber callout. I'm already in the ecosystem and the page treats me like a first-timer.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "What's New" or current-season strip between the CTA pills and the utility links** — even a single line: "Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser is back" or "Book summer dates before June 1." That's one week of work and it makes the page feel alive.
- **Surface a Genie+ or Lightning Lane hook in the hero or utility links section** — a third utility link that says "Lightning Lane Passes →" would be noticed immediately by repeat guests like me and invisible to first-timers who don't know what it means yet.
- **Show ticket pricing or a bundle teaser above the fold** — even "Packages from $X/night" underneath the "Get Tickets" button would reduce my need to click into a black box to find out if prices have gone up again.
- **Add a "Sign In" or "My Plans" shortcut in the top nav or hero area** — returning guests who book direct should be able to surface their upcoming trip with one tap, not buried in a hamburger menu.
