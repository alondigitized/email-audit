# Homepage snapshot · disneyworld.disney.go.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is classic Disney — bold, emotional, and utterly focused on making me feel like the trip is worth every dollar before I've even clicked anything. Right now they're pushing hotels and tickets front and center, which tracks for peak planning season. It's aspirational and family-forward, but it doesn't know I've already been here before.

## What's working

- **The hero video with the Tiana's Bayou Adventure family scene** hits immediately. That laughing kid in the blue BAYOU shirt is my entire reason for going. It's aspirational without being cheesy.
- **"Get Tickets" CTA** is unambiguous and placed exactly where my thumb lands. One tap to where I need to go.
- **"Build Your Vacation" accordion** is smart UX for a planner like me — I know exactly what that link contains and I'll use it.
- **"Today's Park Hours"** shortcut is a practical trust signal. Shows Disney understands that not every visitor is in planning mode — some are already there.
- **Clean, minimal mobile layout** — no ad clutter, no cookie banner obstructing the hero, no popover in my face.

## What's weak

- **No recognition that I'm a returning booker.** I've done Genie+. I've been here. There's zero personalization hook — not a logged-in state, not a "Plan your next trip" returning-visitor CTA.
- **"Explore Resort Hotels" leads the two CTAs** — but as someone who already knows where I'm staying, this is the less urgent action for me. I want tickets and Lightning Lane first.
- **Zero mention of Lightning Lane or Genie+** above the fold. That's one of the highest-anxiety purchase decisions for a planner like me, and it's completely invisible here.
- **No urgency or seasonality signal.** It's late May — Memorial Day weekend peak, summer blackout dates approaching. Nothing on screen tells me to act now.
- **No trust-building copy** around pricing, cancellation, or what's included in a ticket bundle. I've read enough ticket-bundle emails to know these details matter.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Lightning Lane & Genie+" quick-link** below "Get Tickets" — even just as a third pill CTA. It's the thing planners like me come back to the site to research and buy.
- **Surface a Memorial Day / summer-hook banner** just below the hero ("Book before June 1 — park hours extended through Labor Day"). Seasonal urgency for a planner who tracks dates.
- **Add a logged-in returning-visitor state** that shows "Your upcoming trip: July 14–19" or "Welcome back — pick up where you left off." I'm 7 months deep in this plan.
- **Move "Get Tickets" to the first CTA position** ahead of "Explore Resort Hotels" on mobile — for high-intent repeat visitors, tickets + add-ons is the conversion priority.
