# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal Mother's Day push — clean execution, on-calendar, and clearly aimed at millennial women buying for or with their kids. As a retail auditor whose sole job is the girls' shoe floor, I see a campaign that incidentally lands a child in pink sneakers front-and-center, but routes every CTA to a holiday shop, not a category page. Skechers reads here as a gifting brand trying to close a Mother's Day basket, not a footwear retailer surfacing depth of inventory.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% top banner** — applied at cart automatically, no code entry friction. Useful signal for me: I can verify whether this applies to girls' styles when I'm in the category.
- **Mother's Day hero timing** — May 8 visit, campaign landing May 10. Tight. The floral imagery + mom-daughter photo tells me what season we're in at a glance.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — no ambiguity, high contrast, fills most of the viewport width on mobile. Whatever its destination, the tap target is obvious.
- **Daughter in girls' pink Skechers** — incidental to the campaign but she's right there in what look like girls' slip-ins. First visual confirmation girls' product exists in this visit.
- **Secondary promo strip** — "25% OFF Apparel" layered under the BOGO strip. Two distinct offers parsed in under two seconds.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" goes to Mother's Day Shop, not Girls' Shoes** — I need to tap MENU, drill Kids → Girls → Shoes to reach my floor. The hero gives me zero shortcut.
- **No kids' or girls' category shortcut above the fold** — this is a mobile viewport and the only nav exposed is a hamburger MENU. I cannot reach my audit target without at least three taps.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — BOGO is shown, apparel sale is shown, but shipping cost/threshold is absent. That's a conversion question I'd flag for any buyer segment.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no star ratings, no "free returns" callout. For a first-time visitor this matters; for me it's just a gap in the credibility stack.
- **No urgency copy on the Mother's Day deadline** — campaign lands May 10, today is May 8. Two days out. There is no countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing that creates actual deadline pressure. That's a missed lever.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Girls' Shoes" quick-link tile below the hero** — even a row of two tiles (Women / Kids) would cut my path from three taps to one. Ship it in the editorial grid under the hero.
- **Add deadline copy to the Mother's Day hero** — "Ends Sunday 5/10" in small type next to the CTA, or a pill badge on the hero image. This is a two-day window and nothing communicates that.
- **Expose free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — replace or rotate the blue strip to include "Free shipping on orders $75+" so buyers know what they're working with before they drill into product.
- **Swap the BOGO strip copy to mention kids** — "BOGO 50% OFF — including Kids' Styles" would confirm applicability and save me from having to test it manually in the category.
