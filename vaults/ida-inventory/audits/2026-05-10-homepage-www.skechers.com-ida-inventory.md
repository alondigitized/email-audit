# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I'm looking at a Mother's Day gifting push, not a shoes homepage. The hero is a mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot with a scripted "The Mother's Day Shop" overlay and a single "SHOP NOW" call. There are two active promos stacked above the fold — BOGO 50% off and 25% off apparel — so the brand is clearly running a promotional cycle. Girls' shoes are incidentally visible (the daughter in the photo is wearing pink Skechers), but nothing here is structured for someone navigating to a specific category to audit depth and size coverage.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — unmissable, first thing I read, applies automatically at cart. Zero friction to understand.
- **Mom + daughter hero image** — the daughter is actually wearing pink Skechers. That's product placement in the hero for my category, even if accidental.
- **Seasonal alignment** — Mother's Day on May 10 is well-timed. The floral styling and color palette are coherent.
- **Dual-promo stacking** — BOGO on shoes plus 25% off apparel in the secondary strip gives two distinct hooks in a small vertical band. Efficient use of space.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no CLS, no broken images, no text overlap. This is the baseline and it clears it.

## What's weak

- **No direct path to girls' category above the fold** — the nav is a hamburger menu with no exposed category labels. Getting to Girls → Shoes is at minimum 2 taps after opening the menu.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is destination-ambiguous** — it almost certainly routes to a Mother's Day gift guide, not girls' shoes. For my use case that's a dead end.
- **No girls' new-arrivals signal** — nothing tells me whether the girls' category has fresh inventory. I'd have to dig.
- **No size or fit signal** — footwear-specific trust content (size guide, wide-width callout, fit guarantee) is completely absent above the fold.
- **Apparel banner is noise** — the "25% OFF Apparel" strip is irrelevant to my beat and clutters the hierarchy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a girls' category chip to the top nav strip** — even one labeled "Kids" next to the hamburger would save me a tap and keep category auditors from bouncing immediately.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for a split CTA row** — "Shop Mom" and "Shop Kids" buttons beneath the hero would serve both the Mother's Day gifting angle and parents shopping for kids in the same session.
- **Surface a new-arrivals count or "X new styles this week" badge** on whatever rail sits below the hero — this is what inventory auditors and deal-seekers actually act on.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the top banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF | Free shipping on $50+" fits in that strip and removes a friction point before the cart.
