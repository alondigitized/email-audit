# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal campaign homepage, fully committed to Mother's Day. Skechers is leading with a mom-and-daughter lifestyle hero, a BOGO 50% banner at the top, and a secondary apparel sale strip — it reads as a mid-tier family retailer that knows its audience skews parental right now. For my purposes, the girl's pink sneakers in the hero are the only direct signal that girls' footwear is part of this push, but it's a meaningful one.

## What's working

- **Girl's pink sneakers in the hero** — the young daughter is shoe-forward in the composition, feet visible and styled. That is the only above-fold proof that girls' footwear is part of this campaign, and it does real work.
- **BOGO 50% banner** — top rail, sticky blue background, instant read. It's the first thing I see. Hard to miss.
- **Mother's Day timing** — May 6, two days before the holiday. The campaign is live at peak decision window, not a day late.
- **Clean mobile layout** — single-column, no jank, no competing CTAs. The hierarchy respects the screen.
- **Secondary apparel strip** — "25% OFF Apparel" adds a second reason to engage without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- **No direct girls' or kids' category link above the fold** — the hamburger MENU is the only navigation, meaning I need an extra tap to get to my aisle. That's friction I don't have patience for.
- **"The Mother's Day Shop" CTA leads to a mixed event page** — "SHOP NOW" doesn't tell me that girls' sneakers are findable within one more tap. I'm guessing they are, but I shouldn't have to guess.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — BOGO is present but shipping cost is unknown until deeper in the funnel.
- **No trust signals or review counts** — nothing that would confirm inventory quality or sizing reliability from the homepage.
- **Loyalty program invisible** — Skechers has a rewards program; none of it is surfaced here for logged-in or returning auditors/shoppers.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Girls' Shoes" category chip or quick-link strip below the hero** — a single row of category pills (Girls, Boys, Women, Men) would let me tap straight to my aisle without opening the hamburger menu.
- **Rename or annotate the SHOP NOW button** — "Shop Girls' Styles" or at minimum "Shop Mom + Daughter Styles" tells me what's inside the event collection before I commit the tap.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — rotate the BOGO strip with "Free shipping over $X" or add it as a second line; it closes the conversion gap without requiring new assets.
- **Show one rated product tile** — a single featured girls' sneaker with a star rating below the hero hero CTA would carry trust signal weight without a redesign.
