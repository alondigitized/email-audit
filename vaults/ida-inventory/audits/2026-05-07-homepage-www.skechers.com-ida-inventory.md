# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is running a Mother's Day gifting push — mom-and-daughter hero, pink floral treatment, BOGO 50% as the lead price hook. The page is clean and the timing is right. For me this is mostly background noise; I'm here to audit girls' shoes, not gift baskets, but I note the kid in the hero IS wearing product from my category.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF banner**: Loudest element on the page. I track promo depth in my audits — this is a material discount event and it's disclosed cleanly.
- **Mom + daughter hero**: The girl is wearing pink Skechers and reads as 4T–kids-8 range. That's my beat. First in-category lifestyle context I can note.
- **Seasonal timing**: Four days out from Mother's Day, the campaign is live exactly when it should be — no calendar mismatch.
- **Clean visual hierarchy**: Banner, secondary strip, hero, CTA. My eye landed in the right sequence inside two seconds.
- **"Recently Viewed" hint**: Partially cut off at the viewport bottom — signals the site surfaces personalized data, which matters for repeat audit sessions when I'm tracking specific SKUs.

## What's weak

- **No girls' category entry point above the fold**: Only navigation available is a hamburger MENU. I can't reach Kids > Girls > Shoes in one tap from this screen.
- **Hero is gifting-focused, not category-focused**: "The Mother's Day Shop" is a bundled landing — tapping SHOP NOW sends me somewhere undefined, not directly into girls' shoes.
- **No trust signals**: No review star counts, no free-returns callout, no badge. For footwear — where kids' sizing changes every six months — that absence matters.
- **No urgency mechanics**: Mother's Day is four days away and there's no countdown, no "order by [date]" line, nothing that creates a credible deadline.
- **25% OFF Apparel strip is noise**: I audit footwear. The apparel promo takes up real estate and adds nothing for my category.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Kids category tile row below the hero**: Girls / Boys / Baby / Toddler tiles that live under the hero module — lets me bypass the gifting collection entirely.
- **Add a delivery cutoff to the top banner**: "Order by May 9 for Mother's Day delivery" costs nothing to append to the existing blue banner and turns the BOGO urgency from implied to explicit.
- **Link the girl's shoes in the hero image**: Those pink sneakers appear to be a real SKU — make the image tappable and surface a star rating inline. Converts a lifestyle shot into a product entry point.
- **Swap the SHOP NOW destination to a half-sheet category selector on mobile**: Lets gift-shoppers AND auditors filter to the right aisle in one extra tap rather than hunting through a full browse.
