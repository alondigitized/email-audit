# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a lifestyle-forward brand homepage currently pushing its Slip-ins GLIDE-STEP line to adult consumers. The creative is clean and seasonally appropriate — pool setting, spring palette — but it is selling footwear convenience to young adults, not helping me find size-run data on girls' shoes. As a category auditor, I'm here to get to the kids' aisle, and this page is not routing me there efficiently.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — visible before anything else loads, auto-applied at cart. No coupon code friction. That's the right mechanic.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — oversized, full-width button. No ambiguity about where to tap.
- **"Kids'" tab in the category strip** — it's there, at the bottom of the viewport. One tap to my category. That's the minimum viable routing and it's present.
- **Spring Apparel Sale banner** — secondary offer is clearly labeled with a "details" link. Layered promotions are surfaced cleanly, not stacked in a way that obscures either.
- **Clean render** — no layout shift, no broken images, no overlapping text. The page loaded correctly.

## What's weak

- **Hero is adult-only** — male and female adult models wearing Slip-ins by a pool. Zero children's product, zero kids'-category signal. I have to mentally filter past the entire hero to find my work.
- **"SHOP NOW" goes nowhere specific** — it presumably lands on the Slip-ins page or a general product feed. That's not where I'm going. The CTA is useless for my purposes.
- **No loyalty hook in viewport** — Skechers has a loyalty program; none of it is surfaced here. No points balance, no member pricing, no reward-tier callout. Missed retention lever.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "free returns" callout, no star ratings anywhere above the fold.
- **Kids' tab is tiny** — the category navigation strip ("Women | Men | Kids' | Recently Viewed") is rendered at small type below a large hero. For a category auditor who visits daily, this is fine. For a parent with an inch of attention span, that tab is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- **Replace or split the hero by audience segment** — show a girls' or kids' shoe story in rotation alongside the adult Slip-ins creative. Even a 50/50 hero carousel with a kids' frame would surface the category without rebuilding the page.
- **Make "Kids'" a dedicated button or card module**, not just a tab label. A thumbnail shot of the current girls' bestseller below the hero would route me in one tap and also signal to casual parents that the category is stocked.
- **Add a "Free Returns" or free-shipping threshold line** to the top banner alongside the BOGO — even a thin second line. It's the single highest-converting trust signal in footwear and it's absent.
- **Surface the loyalty program** — at minimum a one-line "Earn points on this order" hook near the CTA. It costs nothing to render and closes the gap on the returning-shopper rubric.
