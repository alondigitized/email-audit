## What's working

- **BOGO 50% strip (top bar):** Highest-visibility real estate, hard number, no asterisk clutter visible — the deal reads clean.
- **Hero image — the girl's pink sneakers:** She's wearing the product I track. That's incidental, but it's the only girls' shoe signal above the fold and it registers.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** Full-width, high-contrast, unambiguous. One tap.
- **Mother's Day campaign framing:** Seasonally correct (it's May 4), campaign isn't stale.
- **Secondary promo strip:** "25% OFF Apparel" gives a second deal layer — layered promotions signal a deal-active site.

## What's weak

- **No girls' category shortcut:** The hamburger menu means I'm two taps away from my audit category minimum. Zero quick-links to Kids > Girls above the fold.
- **"Mother's Day Shop" hero copy:** This is a gifting frame, not a shopping frame. I'm here to audit inventory counts, not shop for my mom. The campaign talks past me.
- **Apparel banner is noise:** "Shop the Spring Apparel Sale! 25% OFF Apparel" — I audit shoes. This strip is irrelevant and consumes prime secondary real estate.
- **"Recently Viewed" is cut off:** The bottom of the viewport shows a truncated chip — that's a useful return-shopper signal that's being wasted by not fitting in the viewport cleanly.
- **No size/fit signal anywhere above the fold:** For footwear auditing, I need to know quickly if they surface size coverage info. Nothing here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Kids/Girls quick-link row below the hero:** A horizontal pill strip ("Girls · Boys · Toddlers") directly beneath the "SHOP NOW" button would drop me into my category in one tap instead of two.
- **Swap the apparel secondary banner for a shoe-category promo:** "Girls' Spring Styles — BOGO 50% OFF" uses the same deal, targets footwear, and doesn't waste the strip on a different product line.
- **Surface the loyalty/rewards balance for signed-in users above the fold:** Even a small "You have 240 points" chip near the account icon would activate the returning-shopper signal and drive repeat engagement.
- **Let the "Recently Viewed" rail breathe:** Crop it cleanly or move it up — a half-visible element is friction, not a hook.
