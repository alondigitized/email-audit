# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This reads like IKEA doing what IKEA does — practical, no-nonsense, mobile-first shopping with a real percentage discount up top instead of some vague "big sale" banner. Right now it's pushing PAX wardrobe systems at an IKEA Family member price, with a smaller nod to "college essentials" for the back-to-school crowd. Solid, honest flat-pack energy, but nothing here screams "hey Kallax guy, come build something."

## What's working

- **The search bar with camera icon** — visual search is genuinely useful when I'm trying to match a Kallax insert I saw on Reddit. Good tech-forward touch for someone like me.
- **"Deals" and "Storage & organization" quick-tap chips** — one tap and I'm basically home. This is exactly how I navigate on my phone during a lunch break.
- **IKEA Family price tag on the hero** — I'm not actually sure if I'm signed in, but seeing the badge reminds me membership pricing exists, which nudges we-should-probably-join energy.
- **"10% off PAX wardrobe system purchases"** — a real number, not "up to X% off select items." Flat-pack honesty, like I said.

## What's weak

- **No actual CTA button** — the whole offer is just underlined blue link text sitting over a photo. There's no "Shop PAX" button my thumb can find fast; I'd have to tap the photo itself and hope.
- **Hero product doesn't match my use case** — PAX is a closet/wardrobe system. I came here for shelving hacks and small-space storage, not full wardrobe builds. Feels like a miss for my specific corner of the IKEA catalog.
- **"Shop college essentials for every budget" banner** — that's a dorm-room audience, not a 29-year-old who already furnished his first apartment. Feels like a leftover seasonal banner that didn't get demographic targeting.
- **No people in the hero shot** — just an empty styled closet. Doesn't tell me who this space is "for," which matters when I'm trying to picture my own apartment.

## Recommendations

- Swap the PAX wardrobe hero for a small-space or shelving-system hero (Kallax, Eket, or similar) during a rotation that targets storage/organization shoppers specifically.
- Turn the hero headline into an actual button — "Shop PAX Deals" — instead of underlined text sitting on the image.
- Replace or A/B test the "college essentials" banner for shoppers outside that segment — something like "New in: shelving & room dividers" would land better for me.
- Add a visible member-savings number (e.g., "IKEA Family members save $X more") instead of just the price-tag badge, so the loyalty hook actually converts.
