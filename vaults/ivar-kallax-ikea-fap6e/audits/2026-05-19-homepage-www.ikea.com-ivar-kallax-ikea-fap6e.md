# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so IKEA's mobile homepage is doing its thing — clean, functional, very Swedish-minimalist in energy. It's clearly a big-box furniture brand that wants me to start browsing categories or come into the Burbank store. Right now the push is IKEA Family membership savings (the "$10 for every $100" promo), and below that it's immediately into product categories. Feels like a tool more than a destination — which, honestly, is on-brand.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" category card** — first visible category, and as someone who has spent approximately 14 hours on r/Kallax, this is exactly where I start every session. Big win.
- **Concrete IKEA Family offer** — "$10 for every $100 in-store thru 5/25" is an honest, legible cashback mechanic. No vague "save more with Family," it's actual math I can do in my head.
- **Location-aware header** — "Burbank, CA" with store pin is immediately useful. I'm planning a showroom trip; knowing my store is pre-loaded saves a step.
- **Minimal friction above the fold** — no modal popups, no cookie walls crowding the viewport (or at least none visible in this screenshot), just straight to browsing.
- **IKEA Family price badge** on the lifestyle module below — signals member pricing is woven into product discovery, not just a signup ask.

## What's weak

- **No first-apartment or life-stage hook** — "Sofas & armchairs" and "Storage & organization" are generic. There's no "Setting up your first place?" or "New to IKEA?" angle that speaks to me specifically.
- **The third category card is cropped ("Ou…")** — horizontal scroll isn't obvious on mobile; I don't know what I'm missing and the caret arrow is subtle.
- **IKEA Family promo has an asterisk** — "thru 5/25*" but no asterisk explanation visible. That asterisk immediately makes me wonder what the catch is.
- **No product image in hero position** — the top third of the page is entirely chrome (nav, search, banner). There's no aspirational lifestyle shot pulling me in emotionally.
- **Cart and wishlist icons show no counts** — I add stuff to cart online weeks before my showroom trip. A badge showing "3 items saved" would be a strong re-engagement hook that's completely missing.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Your saved items" nudge** in the header or just below the search bar — something like "You have 4 items in your list · View list" would pull returning planners like me back into the funnel immediately.
- **Swap the cropped horizontal category row for a 2×2 grid** so all four categories are visible without swiping; hidden content on mobile is conversion death.
- **Put one lifestyle hero image above the category cards** — a clean shot of a real first apartment with a Kallax build or a BESTÅ unit would emotionally anchor the page before I start navigating.
- **Unpack the asterisk inline** — either write "in-store purchases only, excl. AS-IS" directly in the banner or lose the asterisk; fine print erodes the honesty of an otherwise strong offer.
