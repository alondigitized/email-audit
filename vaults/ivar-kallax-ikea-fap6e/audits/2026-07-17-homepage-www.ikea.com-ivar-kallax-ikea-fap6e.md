# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, IKEA lands with its classic clean-utility vibe — the homepage knows exactly what it is. Right now it's pushing a sleeper sofa deal under an IKEA Family badge, with "Storage & organization" literally one tap away in the category strip. For someone who named himself after a Kallax shelf, seeing shelving units in the hero background honestly made me stop scrolling mid-swipe.

## What's working

- **"IKEA Family price" badge on the hero** — I don't need to dig for my membership benefit. It's the first thing on the image, which tells me they're actively pricing me as a member, not upselling me on it.
- **"Storage & organization" category pill in the horizontal strip** — One tap. I know where my Kallax accessories and Skådis pegboards live. This strip is doing a lot of quiet heavy lifting.
- **Hero background shows cube shelving** — I don't think this was accidental. Whoever art-directed this put a KALLAX (or EKET) setup behind the sofa, and for me it creates an immediate "this is my world" signal even while the main offer is about sleep sofas.
- **Offer specificity: "10% off select sleeper sofas and sofa beds thru 8/2"** — Not "save big," not "deals await." A real number. A real deadline. Millennial brains don't need hand-holding, we need clarity.
- **Search bar is front and center, with a camera icon** — Image search is there. I've used it on Reddit to reverse-image-search IKEA hacks. Seeing it on the homepage feels relevant.

## What's weak

- **Primary CTA is literally cut off** — "Get" appears at the very bottom of my viewport and disappears. I have no idea what it says. That's a miss right at the conversion moment.
- **The hero is about sleeper sofas, not storage** — I'm here for Kallax inserts, drawer pulls, and cube organizers. The deal is fine but it's not why I opened IKEA.com. The feature could rotate to storage for someone like me.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — No free-shipping threshold, no return policy callout, no review count on any product. Might not matter for IKEA loyalists, but for someone adding $400 of furniture to a cart, I'd like a reminder that returns exist.
- **No personalization hook** — I've bought from IKEA before. No "Welcome back" greeting, no "Your recently viewed" rail, nothing. The location pin says "Covina, CA" which suggests some context is loaded, but there's no shopping history pulling through.

## Recommendations

- **Rotate the hero or add a storage-specific module directly below** — Add a "Plan your storage" lifestyle tile immediately below the sleeper sofa hero. One Kallax + Sektion kitchen tile would catch every apartment furnisher who isn't here for beds.
- **Fix or expose the CTA button** — Whatever "Get ___" says, it needs to be fully visible on the initial viewport. If it's "Get the deal," show it. Truncated CTAs are invisible CTAs.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the nav strip** — Even a "$X free delivery" chip in the horizontal pill row would reduce checkout hesitation for first-timers who don't know IKEA's delivery math.
- **Add a "IKEA Family member? Pick up where you left off" personalization strip** — Even a generic one. My Wishlist and cart are account-linked; a nudge to the wishlist icon with a badge count would make the return visit feel intentional.
