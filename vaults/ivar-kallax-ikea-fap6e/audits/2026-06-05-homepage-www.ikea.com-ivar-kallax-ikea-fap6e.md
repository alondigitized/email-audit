# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Classic IKEA energy right out of the gate — clean layout, zero nonsense, straight to the categories I actually live for. They're running a Summer Sale ("Up to 50% off") as the hero, which for someone who's been building a mental cart of Kallax shelves and a HEMNES dresser for six weeks, is a genuinely useful signal. This homepage reads like the landing page of a functional, trustworthy Scandinavian utility — not a luxury brand, not a fast-fashion spray-and-pray — and that's exactly right.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" tile front and center** — that's a direct hit. The HEMNES dresser image in that tile is literally what I've been stalking online. One tap and I'm in my aisle.
- **"Beds & mattresses" tile** — also above the fold, also exactly where I need to go. IKEA somehow knew my living situation without me logging in.
- **The Summer Sale banner** — big, red, legible. "Up to 50% off" on a first-apartment budget is not a soft nudge, it's a closing argument.
- **Camera icon in search bar** — visual search for furniture I spotted at a friend's place? That's a legitimately smart feature for someone who browses Reddit rabbit holes and screenshots random Kallax hacks.
- **Location awareness (Burbank, CA)** — it already knows my store, which means I don't have to fight the zip code form before I can check stock.

## What's weak

- **No explicit sale CTA button** — the hero banner is playing as video/carousel (there's a pause icon) but I can't see "Shop the Sale" or any tappable text CTA. That's friction where there should be momentum.
- **No loyalty/IKEA Family callout** — IKEA Family is genuinely useful (free hot dogs, member pricing on specific items) and there's zero mention of it above the fold. A millennial first-apartment buyer who doesn't know about IKEA Family is leaving free meatballs on the table.
- **"Sofas &..." tile is cropped** — the third category tile gets cut off. Sofas are a major apartment purchase; clipping that tile label looks lazy and makes me wonder what else is half-rendered.
- **No specific product or price visible** — the sale says "up to 50% off" but I have no idea if that's on the stuff I want. Show me a KALLAX 4×4 at a specific price and I'm a convert in ten seconds.
- **No free-shipping or delivery threshold mentioned** — IKEA delivery pricing is notoriously variable; surfacing a threshold here would remove a major hesitation.

## Recommendations

- **Add an IKEA Family / member pricing badge to the sale banner** — something like "Members get early access + extra 10%" sits perfectly next to "Up to 50% off" and converts fence-sitters who plan their trips around member deals.
- **Swap the generic sale hero for a first-apartment lifestyle frame** — a Kallax-lined studio wall or a HEMNES + MALM bedroom setup speaks directly to the buyer cohort that's actually browsing in June; "Summer Sale" could be anyone's tagline.
- **Surface a "Shop the Sale" CTA button** with a visible tap target below the banner headline — the video tile with a pause button is not a conversion tool.
- **Show free-delivery threshold in the top bar or below the search field** — one line ("Free delivery on orders over $X") removes the biggest mental blocker for someone pricing out a full-apartment haul.
