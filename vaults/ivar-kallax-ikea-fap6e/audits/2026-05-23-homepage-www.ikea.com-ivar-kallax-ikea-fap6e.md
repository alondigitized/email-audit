# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is IKEA doing what IKEA does — clean, functional, no-nonsense — but on mobile it's showing me a skeleton before the skeleton has dressed itself. I can see Storage & organization right there in the first tile, which is exactly my wheelhouse, but the page feels like a floor model before the price tags have been hung. The IKEA Family price callout on the bedding hero tells me there's a deal somewhere, but I can't see what it actually is.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the first category tile** — whoever ordered those tiles got it right. That dresser thumbnail is speaking directly to my apartment's chaos problem and I haven't even logged in yet.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — visual search on an IKEA mobile app is genuinely useful; snapping a room and finding matching pieces is exactly how I browse at 11pm before a showroom trip.
- **IKEA Family price badge** — it's visible, it's blue, it's on-brand. Signals membership pays off even if it doesn't show me the number yet.
- **Location context (Burbank, CA)** — knowing stock is localized to my nearest store before I've done anything is practical and reduces the friction of "is this even available near me."
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — three tiles plus a search bar. No pop-ups, no cookie banners visible, no forced modal. For a millennial who closes five interstitials before getting to any content elsewhere, this is refreshing.

## What's weak

- **No actual price on the IKEA Family hero** — the badge says "IKEA Family price" but the number is off-screen or not rendered yet. I'm being teased without being told the deal.
- **No visible Kallax, Billy, or hero product** — the storage tile shows a generic white dresser (HEMNES-ish?). For the crowd that has literally memorized SKU numbers, a recognizable hero piece would hit harder.
- **Zero social proof** — no review counts, no "4.8 stars, 12k reviews" anywhere in the viewport. For someone who reads every Reddit thread before buying a shelf bracket, this is a real miss.
- **Third category tile is half-cropped** — I can see "Ou..." which is probably Outdoor. The horizontal scroll affordance is subtle; new users might not even know to swipe.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always want to know if I'm $14 away from free shipping before I start adding to cart. That number is nowhere here.

## Recommendations

- **Put the actual IKEA Family discount number on the hero**: "IKEA Family price: from $X" or "Members save 15% on bedding this week" — the badge without the number is a tease that doesn't convert.
- **Swap the generic dresser thumbnail in Storage for a Kallax or ALEX**: These are the viral hero pieces. Show me the product I Googled at 2am.
- **Add a star-rating strip below the hero**: Even a single "⭐ 4.7 · 18,000 reviews on BESTÅ" line builds the trust that turns a browse into a cart.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in a persistent sticky strip**: One thin bar under the nav — "Free delivery on orders over $X" — is the simplest conversion tool in e-commerce and it's completely absent here.
