# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, IKEA knows exactly who pays the bills — this opens straight on an IKEA Family offer with a real dollar amount and a promo code, no fluff. It reads like the homepage of a brand that's confident you already love them. Right now it's pushing a spend-and-save deal that expires in three days, which, honestly, is perfectly timed for a holiday weekend impulse buy.

## What's working

- **"IKEA Family offer" hero with $30 off $200+** — this is my exact spend bracket when I'm doing a Kallax + accessories haul. Hitting $200 at IKEA takes about twelve minutes.
- **SITESAVE15 code inline** — I don't have to hunt for it. It's right there, underlined, copy-pasteable. That's how you do it.
- **"Ends 7/6" urgency** — three days before the deadline on July 4th weekend is smart calendar play. I'm already planning a run.
- **"Storage & organization" chip in the quick-nav strip** — literally my first tap every single time. Whoever put that second after Deals knows their audience.
- **Store location auto-detected (Burbank, CA)** — the "in-store" framing of the offer actually means something because I can see *which* store.

## What's weak

- **Zero product imagery in the hero** — it's a blue rectangle with text. I can't see a single shelf unit, no Kallax, no Eket, nothing. That's a miss for a visual category like furniture.
- **The hero CTA is underlined text, not a button** — "Save online with code SITESAVE15 at checkout" reads more like a footnote than an invitation to shop.
- **No below-the-fold preview** — I can just barely see the word "Great" at the bottom edge. No category tiles, no product rail, no sense of what's waiting if I scroll. The hook stops at the deal.
- **No reviews, ratings, or social proof visible** — nothing that tells me other first-apartment people successfully assembled this stuff.
- **IKEA Family membership sign-up not visible** — if I weren't already a member, nothing here would tell me how to become one and capture the deal.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the solid-blue hero for a lifestyle scene with the offer overlaid** — a Kallax wall in a small apartment, someone measuring a corner — keep the deal copy but give me something to want before I read the discount.
- **Make the online CTA a visible button** — turn "Save online with code SITESAVE15 at checkout" into a yellow "Shop & Save" button with the code visible beneath it. Yellow-on-blue is IKEA's brand language, use it.
- **Add a horizontal product rail immediately below the hero** — best-sellers or "trending this week" so the page earns the scroll. Right now the hero drops off a cliff.
- **Surface the IKEA Family sign-up inline** — a one-line "Not a member? Join free →" under the offer headline captures people on the fence and grows the loyalty base.
