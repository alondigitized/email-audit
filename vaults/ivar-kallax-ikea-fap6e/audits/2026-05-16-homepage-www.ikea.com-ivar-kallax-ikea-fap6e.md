# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's mobile homepage goes full utility-first — search bar up top, category tiles immediately below, loyalty banner right in my face. It's a brand that knows its shoppers have a list before they even open the app, and the experience confirms that bet. Right now they're pushing IKEA Family double-points, which ends May 18, so there's a clear campaign running.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as tile #1** — I clocked it immediately. That's not an accident, and for me it's exactly right. One tap to my category.
- **IKEA Family 2x points banner** — concrete, time-stamped (5/18), and placed right below the search bar where I can't miss it. As someone who plans a quarterly haul, doubling points on that trip is a real incentive.
- **Visual search camera icon** — the little camera in the search bar is a subtle win. I've used it to ID Kallax inserts I saw on Reddit. IKEA knowing their audience is digitally fluent shows.
- **Lifestyle image with "IKEA Family price" badge** — the bottom module previews a warmly lit living room with Scandinavian coffee table and sofa. Feels like a real apartment, not a showroom.
- **Location-aware header** — showing "Burbank, CA" with zip 91801 means the inventory and store hours are already localized. Useful when I'm planning a meatball run.

## What's weak

- **No primary CTA button anywhere above the fold** — there's zero "Shop now," "Explore storage," or "See IKEA Family deals." The category tiles function as CTAs but they don't look like buttons.
- **The loyalty banner copy is dense** — "Collect 2x the points! IKEA Family members collect 2 points for every $1 spent on home furnishing products thru 5/18.*" is a run-on. Could be cut to: "2x points until May 18 — Family members only."
- **Third category tile is cut off** — I can see something starting with "Ou..." (Outdoor?) but the tile is almost fully off-screen with no visual cue to swipe. Easy to miss the carousel entirely.
- **No people in the imagery** — the category tiles and the lifestyle image show furniture without humans in or around the space. Nothing tells me "this is a 29-year-old's apartment," which would hook me harder.
- **No new-arrivals or trending rail** — I scroll Reddit for KALLAX hacks specifically because I want to know what's new. No "Just arrived" or "Most hacked" module is a missed beat.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Small space, big storage" hero module** with a KALLAX-forward image and a "Shop storage" button — explicit apartment-furnisher framing takes 10 minutes to write and would lift click-through on the storage tile significantly.
- **Make the carousel swipeable with visible pagination dots** — the truncated third tile needs either a visible arrow or dots so I know there's more; right now I might think it's a broken render.
- **Trim the loyalty banner to a single punchy line** — "Double your points on every $1 — ends May 18" with an inline "Join IKEA Family" link for non-members is cleaner and more scannable.
- **Drop a "IKEA Family members save X%" price badge on the lifestyle image** — the "IKEA Family price" label is there but vague. Show me a real dollar figure or percentage and I'm tapping immediately.
