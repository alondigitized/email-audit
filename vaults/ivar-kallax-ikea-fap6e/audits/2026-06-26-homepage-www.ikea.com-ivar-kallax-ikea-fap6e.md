# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, IKEA Summer Sale — loud, red, unmissable. This is exactly the kind of blunt announcement I'd expect from a brand that names its stores after Swedish geography and fonts its sale signs in Futura Bold. They're clearly swinging hard on a time-limited discount event, and for someone who's had a half-built cart saved for three weeks waiting for a reason to pull the trigger, this is that reason. Not the prettiest homepage I've ever seen, but it's honest — and honestly, very IKEA.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" pill in the nav strip** — that's one tap to my exact category. Someone at IKEA knows their audience; I didn't even have to go hunting.
- **The "ends 7/2" deadline** — flat-pack honesty. I know exactly when the offer expires. No "while supplies last" weaseling. I can plan my weekend trip around it.
- **Up to 50% off, stated plainly** — no asterisk forest obscuring it, no "up to" buried in 8pt text. The hierarchy is: Sale → Discount → Deadline. That's the right order.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — I can scan a barcode from my saved-cart screenshot. High tech comfort, appreciated.
- **Location pre-set to Burbank, CA** — it knows where I am; if I want to confirm in-store availability, that friction is already gone.

## What's weak

- **No shop button** — the underlined "Hurry! Offer valid in store and online ends 7/2" text is doing the job of a CTA without actually being one. I shouldn't have to wonder if I'm supposed to tap that.
- **Zero product imagery above the fold** — I'm a visual shopper. I've been mentally placing that KALLAX 4×4 in my living room for a month. Show me a shelf, a BILLY, a HEMNES. Red backgrounds don't help me visualize my apartment.
- **No loyalty/IKEA Family mention** — IKEA Family exists and has member-only deals. If this sale has extra perks for members (it often does), that's a missed hook for someone like me who's already in the app.
- **No free-delivery threshold visible** — I'm ordering online. If I spend over X I get free delivery, but I have no idea what X is without digging. That missing signal could cost them a cart add.

## Recommendations

- **Drop a "Shop the Sale" button directly on the hero** — the underlined text is invisible as a CTA. One big button that taps into a curated Sale landing page. Ship it Monday.
- **Swap the solid red hero background for a lifestyle room shot** — show a Millennial apartment with KALLAX shelves, LACK side tables, a PAX wardrobe. Keep the sale text overlaid. Same urgency, 10× more "that's my apartment" resonance.
- **Add an IKEA Family badge to the hero** — even just "Extra 10% for IKEA Family members" in the corner pulls me toward signing in and unlocks member pricing.
- **Surface the free-delivery minimum** — a small strip under the nav ("Free delivery on orders over $X") gives me a conversion reason to add one more item to hit the threshold.
