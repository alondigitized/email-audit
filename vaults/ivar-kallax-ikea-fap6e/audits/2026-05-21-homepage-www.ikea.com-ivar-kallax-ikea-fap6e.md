# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's mobile homepage is exactly what I expected — clean, blue-and-yellow confidence, zero frills. Right now it's pushing IKEA Family membership and a tight in-store-only earn deal, plus quick-tap category tiles. It reads less like a homepage and more like a functional launchpad, which honestly fits my brain — I come here knowing what I want.

## What's working

- **IKEA Family promo strip** — the "$10 for every $100 / thru 5/25\*" line is specific and date-bounded. I noticed it immediately. If I'm already a member, that's a nudge to plan a meatball run before the deadline.
- **Category tiles (Storage & organization, Sofas & armchairs)** — "Storage & organization" is literally my life right now. One tap and I'm in Kallax territory. Fast.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — the visual search shortcut is a high-tech comfort for me. I can photograph a Marketplace find and reverse-search the IKEA dupe instantly.
- **Location chip (Burbank, CA) + postal code (91801)** — already localized. I didn't have to set my store. Reduces friction when I'm checking same-day stock.
- **Clean render** — no jank, no layout shift visible, loads fast on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No hero image whatsoever** — above the category tiles there is nothing but a search bar. For a brand this visual, that's a miss. I get no inspiration, no "oh that's a room I want to build."
- **IKEA Family benefit is text-only fine print** — the membership earn mechanic is tucked in a small hyperlinked line below the search bar. It deserves a card with a visual, not a footnote.
- **"In-store only" asterisk** — I live my life on this app. An offer I can't redeem online feels like bait. I have to read the fine print to know it's not a click-and-collect deal.
- **Third category tile is cropped ("Ou…")** — can't see what it is. That's real estate wasted on mobile. A horizontal scroll indicator or pill labels would help.
- **No product imagery in tiles** — the Storage tile has a dresser (not a Kallax), the Sofas tile has a couch. Generic. No shelving unit, no modular system visible.
- **Zero social proof above the fold** — no ratings, no "trending in your area," no "1M sold" badge. I'm a Reddit-trained buyer and I want validation signals.

## Recommendations

- **Add a lifestyle hero card** — even a single full-bleed image of a Kallax-wall or first-apartment setup above the category grid. Give me something to aspire to before I start tapping.
- **Upgrade the Family promo strip to a badge/card** — show the current earn balance or total saves if I'm logged in, and make the "in-store" restriction honest and bold, not asterisked.
- **Fix the cropped third category tile** — either reduce tile width so three fit, or add a visible "›" scroll affordance with a label so I know what's hiding.
- **Swap the dresser thumbnail in Storage for a Kallax or EKET system** — that's the hero product for first-apartment furnishers. Commit to the icon.
