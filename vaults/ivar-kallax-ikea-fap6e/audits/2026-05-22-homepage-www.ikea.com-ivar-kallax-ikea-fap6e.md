# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so IKEA's mobile homepage is doing that very-IKEA thing where it skips the fluff and jumps straight to categories — zero hero paragraph, pure utility. For a guy who already has his shopping list built in the app before he walks through the door, I respect the no-nonsense energy. Right now it's clearly pushing IKEA Family pricing and bedroom product, which... fine, but I came here planning a storage build-out, not a mattress upgrade.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the lead category tile** — I didn't even have to scroll. One tap and I'm in the aisle I live in. That's the algorithm knowing its audience, or at least a lucky sort order.
- **Location personalization at the very top** — "Burbank, CA" with a stored zip means the app remembered me. That low-friction personalization earns trust quickly.
- **Visual search (camera icon in search bar)** — I can point my phone at a piece of furniture and find it. That's the kind of utility that makes me prefer the app over the physical catalog.
- **Clean, fast-loading render** — No layout shift, no broken images. The white dresser and the beige sofa look crisp. Flat-pack honesty, visually.
- **"Sofas & armchairs" as the second tile** — I'm still building out my living room. Seeing that tile next to storage feels like IKEA read my cart.

## What's weak

- **No hero copy whatsoever** — The page goes logo → search → categories with zero orienting headline. I don't know if there's a sale, a new collection, or a reason to care today versus last Tuesday.
- **"IKEA Family price" badge with no numbers** — Showing me a yellow badge that says "IKEA Family price" without telling me what I'm saving is like showing me an Allen wrench with no instructions. I need the spec.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I always do the math on whether to buy online vs. carry-out at the store. No delivery cost signal here means I can't make that call.
- **The third category tile is cut off** — I can see "Ou..." (presumably Outdoor?) half-cropped on the right edge. That's a missed opportunity — if it's a current seasonal push, it's invisible to me.
- **No urgency or time-bound signal** — Nothing tells me prices are going up, stock is limited, or this offer ends Sunday. With IKEA, I plan ahead — but even I appreciate a nudge.

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line hero banner above the category grid** — Even something like "IKEA Family members save 15% on storage this week" gives me a reason to act today rather than plan another quarterly trip. Shippable as a single text module.
- **Show the actual Family price savings on the badge** — Replace the generic "IKEA Family price" label with "Members save 10–20%" or a specific callout like "Dresser from $149, Family price $129." Numbers convert; labels don't.
- **Surface a free-delivery threshold** — A sticky strip reading "Free delivery over $X" lets me self-qualify instantly, the way a price tag tells me if a KALLAX fits my budget.
- **Fix the cropped third category tile** — Either scroll-snap it properly or reduce to two tiles so nothing feels amputated. A half-visible tile wastes the tap zone and signals a responsive layout gap.
