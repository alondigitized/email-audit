# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's mobile homepage knows exactly who I am — it opens with Storage & organization as the first category tile and immediately hits me with a loyalty bonus that I actually have an opinion about. This looks like a utility-first, member-first brand that right now is pushing IKEA Family points and family-priced furniture. No wasted runway imagery, no lifestyle fluff — straight to the shelves.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the first category tile** — with a white multi-drawer dresser front and center, that's a direct hit for anyone who has bookmarked a Kallax hack thread. One tap from that tile to my weekend project.
- **IKEA Family 2x points banner** — visible immediately, date-bounded (thru 5/18), gives me a reason to buy *now* rather than wait for my next showroom trip.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — visual search is genuinely useful when I'm trying to ID a piece I saw in someone's apartment tour. High-tech comfort is me.
- **Location set to Burbank, CA** — the page already knows my store, which means stock availability and local pickup are probably one tap away.
- **"IKEA Family price" badge on the lifestyle image** — signals that there are member-exclusive prices on displayed products, which nudges me to log in before I shop.

## What's weak

- **No visible primary CTA button** — the page is category tiles and a banner, but there's no single action for me to take. No "Shop the bonus," no "See Family prices" — just a banner I have to act on myself.
- **No people visible anywhere in the viewport** — the dresser tile and the living room lifestyle shot are object-only. IKEA's strongest emotional selling point is "people like you living in places like this" and it's absent from this screen.
- **"Ou…" third category tile is cut off** — Outdoor? Outdoor lighting? I can't tell without swiping. Truncated labels on a carousel look unfinished, not mysterious.
- **No new arrivals or "just landed" signal** — for someone who adds to cart weeks before visiting, a "new this week" hook would keep me browsing instead of just navigating.
- **No reviews or ratings anywhere above the fold** — IKEA has cult-level product reviews; none of that social proof is being used here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop the bonus" button directly below the 2x points banner** — right now the offer sits there with no tap destination. One teal button linking to Family-eligible products would convert that banner from awareness to action.
- **Replace the object-only product tiles with lifestyle thumbnails** — show a Kallax-packed studio or a sofa in a tiny living room. IKEA's own catalog shoots are perfect for this; use them.
- **Fix the truncated third carousel tile** — either widen the tile, shorten the label, or add a visible "swipe" affordance so I know there are more categories. "Ou…" looks like a bug.
- **Surface a "New this week" or "Back in stock" rail below categories** — for a high-frequency visitor like me who checks the site between showroom trips, freshness is the hook that keeps me scrolling.
