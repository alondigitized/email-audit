# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's mobile homepage is doing what IKEA always does — cutting straight to the point, no fluff, flat-pack efficiency. It reads less like a homepage and more like a highly functional index card: here's a search bar, here's a loyalty promo, here are your categories, go find your thing. The big push right now is the IKEA Family 2x points event running through May 18th, which is genuinely useful if you're already a member (and anyone who's been to a store more than once probably is).

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the first category tile** — that white drawer unit hits immediately for anyone in first-apartment mode. IKEA knows who opens this app.
- **IKEA Family 2x points banner** — placed right below the search bar, impossible to miss, and the deadline (5/18) creates genuine urgency without being manipulative.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — visual search is a sleeper feature and having it front-and-center says this UI was designed for people who screenshot Pinterest inspo boards. That's me.
- **Localization** — "Burbank, CA" and zip code 91801 in the top bar tells me this is pulling my store context. That matters when I'm planning an in-store run for meatballs + shelving.

## What's weak

- **No hero, no story** — the page launches straight into utility mode. There's zero aspirational content above the fold. A first-apartment person like me responds to lifestyle imagery that makes a 200 sq ft studio look livable. There's none of that until you scroll.
- **Third category tile is cut off** — I can see "Ou..." on the right edge. The horizontal scroll affordance arrow is there but the clipped tile looks unfinished, not intentionally teasing.
- **No free-shipping info** — I'm planning to order stuff weeks before my quarterly trip. What's the threshold? Not mentioned anywhere above the fold (or apparently anywhere visible).
- **2x points offer lacks redemption context** — "2 points per $1" sounds great but if I don't already know what a point is worth, this is noise. No "$X toward next purchase" framing visible.
- **No "recently viewed" or personalization** — I've added things to cart online in the past. Not seeing any "pick up where you left off" hook is a miss for a high-tech-comfort repeat shopper like me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a lifestyle hero image above the categories** — a tight studio with a KALLAX unit doing serious work (books, plants, baskets) would stop me cold. Takes 10 seconds to shoot, ships next sprint.
- **Add inline point redemption value to the Family banner** — something like "2x points = $5 reward per $50 spent." Make the math legible so the offer doesn't feel like a teaser.
- **Fix or intentionally design the clipped third tile** — either widen the container so three tiles fit, or lean into the carousel pattern with a visible partial tile that's clearly designed, not accidentally cropped.
- **Bring the free-shipping threshold up to the top** — even a small "Free delivery over $X" line near the search bar would materially increase add-to-cart intent for online planners.
