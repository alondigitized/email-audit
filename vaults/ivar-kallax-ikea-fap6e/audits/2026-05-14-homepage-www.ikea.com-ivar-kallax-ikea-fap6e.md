# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- IKEA.com is doing exactly what IKEA always does — skipping the romance and going straight to utility, which honestly I respect. The first thing it hits me with is a loyalty-points promotion and then immediately shows me "Storage & organization" as the first category tile, which is like they Googled my Reddit history. This is a functional, conversion-optimized homepage that trusts the brand to do the emotional heavy lifting, but it's missing the aspirational hook that makes me *want* to furnish my whole apartment this weekend.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the lead category tile** — I know this is just an alphabetical or algorithmic placement but it hits perfectly for me. That white dresser thumbnail is exactly the energy I'm searching for.
- **IKEA Family points 2x promotion** — concrete, time-bounded, and it's above the fold. I already know I'm spending $400 this quarter; doubling my points is a real nudge to do it before 5/18.
- **Zip code + city in the top bar** ("Burbank, CA") — I know instantly it's showing me local stock and local store hours, which matters when I need that last KALLAX shelf before the weekend.
- **Visual search camera icon** in the search bar — I've 100% photographed a piece of furniture from a thrift store to find the IKEA equivalent. This is a feature I actually use.
- **IKEA Family price module** at the bottom edge — the warm-toned lifestyle room scene finally gives me an aspirational glimpse, even if it's just barely in view.

## What's weak

- **No hero copy at all** — the top of the page is a search bar and a loyalty promo. There's zero aspirational copy about what IKEA helps me build or become. For someone landing cold, this reads as a catalog, not a brand.
- **No button CTA** — the category tiles are functional but they don't create urgency or a clear singular action. A "Shop our Family deals" button tied to the 2x-points promo would push me faster.
- **No people visible** — everything I can see is product photography and text. For a millennial who wants to see how a piece actually lives in a small apartment with real humans around it, this is a miss.
- **Third category tile is cropped off-screen** ("Ou...") — whatever it is, it's being swallowed by the edge. A 3-tile row with the last one half-clipped looks sloppy on mobile and I have no idea what I'm missing.
- **Trust signals are absent** — no review counts, no "free delivery over X," no returns callout. The homepage earns zero conversion confidence outside of brand familiarity.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the search-bar-first layout with a split hero** — half aspirational lifestyle photo (a real small apartment, a Kallax-packed wall), half search/quick-categories. This gives first-timers a reason to stay and returning shoppers a fast path forward.
- **Tie the loyalty banner to a button** — transform "Collect 2x the points" from a text blurb into a banner with a tappable CTA button: "Shop IKEA Family deals →". The offer is good; it just doesn't direct me anywhere.
- **Fix the cropped third category tile** — either make it a 2-tile row with a "See all categories" link, or ensure the third tile is 50% visible as a clear scroll invite, not an accidental crop.
- **Add a free-delivery threshold line** — one sentence: "Free delivery on orders over $X" somewhere near the top. That single trust signal could lift AOV for people like me who are calculating whether to add one more bookcase.
