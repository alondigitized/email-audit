# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is *my* store — I've been inside this Burbank location three times in the last year and the site knows it (it's already showing my zip code and store). Right now IKEA is pushing sleeper sofas for the summer, which is fine but not why I'm here. What catches my eye immediately is that "Storage & organization" pill sitting right next to Deals in the nav — that's my entire personality in a category chip.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" category pill** — it's the second chip in the scroll row, right next to Deals. One tap and I'm in my kingdom. I don't even need the hero to speak to me.
- **Location-aware header** — the "Burbank, CA" store and "91755" zip showing in the top bar makes this feel personalized. I know this is pulling my nearest store without me doing anything.
- **IKEA Family price badge** — I have the IKEA Family card. Seeing that badge on the hero immediately tells me there's a loyalty price involved and it's relevant to me as a card holder.
- **Hero room styling** — the background shelving with color-blocked books, plants, and a cactus reads like a r/malelivingspace post. That's my visual language. Even if the promo is sofas, the room staging makes me want to stay.
- **Camera search icon in the search bar** — as someone who screenshots Kallax hacks on Reddit, being able to do a visual search is legitimately useful.

## What's weak

- **Hero is about sleeper sofas** — I'm not buying a sleeper sofa today. The dominant real estate is allocated to a category I'm not shopping. The storage shelves in the background are almost an accident.
- **Primary CTA is cut off** — I can see "Get" starting to appear at the very bottom of the viewport. There's no fully rendered button above the fold. That's a miss.
- **No product-level teaser in my category** — I came to look at new Kallax colorways or accessories. There's zero indication that storage products have their own deals or new arrivals.
- **No review signals or ratings anywhere visible** — I'm a Reddit-trained shopper. I want to see star counts or review badges near featured products. Nothing here.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — IKEA delivery fees matter for furniture. I don't know from this screen whether my cart will get hit with a big delivery charge.

## Recommendations

- **Add a second hero tile or carousel dot** specifically for "Storage & Organization deals" — even 10% off a Kallax or Besta unit would make this homepage feel like it knows someone like me exists.
- **Bring the primary CTA fully above the fold** — whatever "Get" resolves to (likely "Get the deal" or "Get it now"), it needs to be a tappable button visible without any scroll.
- **Surface the free delivery threshold in the header bar** — the top status bar already shows location info; adding "Free delivery over $X" in that strip is one line of copy that could meaningfully move conversion.
- **Swap the Kallax-adjacent background shelving for the actual foreground** — if the room already has those open shelves, lead with them as the product. Put a "Styled with KALLAX" tag on the background shelf unit and link it.
