# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's mobile homepage is doing what IKEA does best: cutting to the chase. I see my categories front and center, a Summer Sale banner that actually has some energy, and a search bar that trusts me to know what I want. It's functional, not flashy — very on-brand for a company that makes you build your own furniture.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the first category tile** — that's me, every time. Kallax, BRIMNES, HEMNES — this is where I live. Putting it first tile is smart.
- **"Sofas & armchairs" as the second tile** — the one-two punch of storage + seating is the exact first-apartment shopping list.
- **Location detection (Burbank, CA + 91801)** — showing my local store context without me asking builds instant confidence that stock results will be relevant.
- **Search bar with camera icon** — high tech comfort here. I can scan something from a Reddit post and find the exact item. This is a feature I'd actually use.
- **The IKEA Summer Sale banner** — big, red, unmissable. IKEA running a sale is always newsworthy to me even without the percentage attached.

## What's weak

- **No percentage or dollar amount on the Summer Sale banner** — "The IKEA Summer Sale" with no "up to 30% off" hook is a half-pulled trigger. Make me feel something.
- **No loyalty or Family member hook** — IKEA Family is a real thing with actual price differences. It's nowhere on this screen. I shouldn't have to remember it exists.
- **No lifestyle photography** — both category tiles are pure product shots against white. A Kallax wall build in a real studio apartment would speak directly to me; a floating dresser does not.
- **Third category tile is cut off** — whatever is to the right of "Sofas & armchairs" is partially clipped and unreadable. Missed discovery opportunity.
- **No product rail** — there's no "Most popular right now" or "New arrivals" strip visible. For a high-intent shopper who adds to cart weeks before a showroom trip, this matters.

## Recommendations

- **Put a "up to X% off" figure on the Summer Sale banner** — even "up to 20% off" gives me a reason to tap through instead of just registering that a sale exists.
- **Add an IKEA Family member price badge or CTA above the fold** — something like "Family member prices inside" costs nothing and captures every logged-in user's attention.
- **Swap the product-on-white category tiles for lifestyle shots** — a real Kallax build in a 500 sq ft apartment will make every person on Reddit's r/malelivingspace stop scrolling.
- **Surface the partially clipped third category** — either shrink the tiles to show three or use a visible scroll indicator so I know there's more.
