# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Clean, no-nonsense — very on-brand for IKEA. The homepage is leading with a loyalty double-points promo and jumping straight into category browsing, which is exactly how I shop the site. It's not a flashy campaign page, it's a utility-first landing that trusts you already know you want flat-pack furniture. The "IKEA Family price" module peeking at the bottom hints there are deals below the fold, but above the fold it's mostly chrome and navigation.

## What's working

- **Storage & organization category card** — that white dresser image is my actual shopping list. First tap I'd make.
- **IKEA Family double-points banner** — it's underlined, it has a deadline (5/18), and it names the mechanic clearly. I know exactly what the deal is.
- **Persistent search bar** — high in the hierarchy, full-width, with a camera icon for visual search. That's how I shop when I'm trying to ID a piece I spotted on Reddit.
- **Location already set (Burbank, CA)** — the site knows where I am, which means "check stock" in the product pages will be meaningful. Huge for IKEA since I need to actually go pick it up.
- **Clean mobile layout** — IKEA blue on black header, logo pops, no clutter. Loads feeling trustworthy.

## What's weak

- **No above-the-fold CTA button** — there's no "Shop now" or "See deals" button anywhere; the loyalty banner is a text link and the categories are cards. I have to figure out where to tap.
- **"Outdoor" category is cropped** — the rightmost tile is half off-screen with no visual affordance that it's swipeable. Looks like a bug.
- **Points system requires mental math** — "2 points per $1" means nothing to me until I know what points are worth. Compare that to "$10 off your $50 purchase" — IKEA is burying the lede.
- **No lifestyle imagery** — it's a dresser product shot on a white background and a sofa on beige. Zero aspirational staging, zero "first apartment comes together" energy.
- **Wishlist heart icon is in the header but unexplained** — as a returning shopper who pre-loads my cart, I'd want to see my saved items count or a badge there.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the product-shot category tiles for lifestyle scenes** — show a styled Kallax-wall setup or a small living room where the sofa actually lives. Product shots belong on product pages.
- **Make the loyalty offer a button, not a text link** — "Shop now to earn 2x" with a yellow CTA button would convert the banner from informational to actionable in one edit.
- **Fix the cropped "Outdoor" tile** — add a scroll indicator (dots or an arrow pill) so I know there are more categories; right now it just looks broken.
- **Surface my cart/wishlist count** — even a "3 saved items" badge on the heart icon would pull me directly back into my pre-loaded browsing session and skip the discovery phase entirely.
