# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is classic IKEA: no-nonsense, utility-forward, gets out of your way. The mobile layout skips a big hero and jumps straight to search + category tiles, which honestly respects my time. Right now it's pushing IKEA Family savings before May 25 — a tight, real deadline — though the "in-store only" asterisk immediately deflates the hype for someone browsing on their phone at 11pm.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" as the first category tile** — this is the most important real estate on the page for me. Literally my religion. The dresser image signals exactly where I need to go.
- **IKEA Family savings banner** — "$10 for every $100" is clean math I can do in my head. No confusing point systems or mystery tiers.
- **Visual search camera icon in the search bar** — as someone who screenshots Reddit Kallax hacks constantly, being able to reverse-image-search a shelf config is genuinely useful.
- **Location is set (Burbank, CA + 91801)** — I can already tell this is tuned to my store, which matters for stock checks before I make the trip for meatballs.
- **"IKEA Family price" badge on the bottom lifestyle module** — reinforces the membership value before I scroll; keeps the loyalty thread consistent.

## What's weak

- **"In-store only" on the hero promo** — I opened this on my phone. The biggest offer above the fold is useless for my current context. That asterisk is doing a lot of heavy lifting.
- **No hero image whatsoever** — the layout goes straight from search bar to promo text to category tiles. There's nothing evocative. I feel like I'm in a spreadsheet, not a store.
- **Third category tile is cut off** — the rightmost tile is half-visible with no label. That's a teaser with no payoff on this viewport; it just reads as a layout glitch.
- **No visible price anchoring on the product tiles** — the dresser and sofa images have zero pricing context. I can't tell if I'm window-shopping or deal-hunting.
- **No "new arrivals" or "trending" signal** — IKEA drops new collections seasonally and I've got zero indication if there's anything fresh to get excited about.

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin online-specific offer strip above the IKEA Family banner** — something like "Free delivery over $X or free pickup in-store" so phone browsers have a hook that's actually relevant to their context.
- **Label the cut-off third category tile or implement a carousel indicator** — show me there are more tiles to swipe, or increase the tile width so the label isn't chopped off.
- **Drop a "New this season" or "Just added to Storage" module between the category tiles and the IKEA Family lifestyle shot** — one shelf or bookcase product tile with a price would convert browsers like me faster than any banner.
- **Swap the generic lifestyle image at the bottom for a real Kallax-hack or room-divider shot** — that's the kind of image that stops the scroll for my demographic; we know every product in the IKEA lineup but we still click inspiration photos.
