# Homepage snapshot · www.ikea.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- IKEA's homepage feels like walking into the Burbank store before the meatball rush — clean, localized, and functional. They're pushing a sleeper sofa deal hard right now, which isn't my errand, but I spotted "Storage & organization" as a top pill and Kallax-style shelving units literally filling the background of the hero shot. IKEA knows their visual language, even if the current promotion isn't aimed at me.

## What's working

- **"Storage & organization" pill nav** — it's the second chip in the horizontal scroll bar, one tap and I'm home. This is exactly how I want to browse on mobile.
- **IKEA Family price badge** — small but there, top-left of the hero. It signals that I'm already in the club and that this price is my price, not the sucker price.
- **Localization header** — zip code 91755 and "Burbank, CA" tell me this is pulling my store, so the stock and availability should actually be real. That matters before a showroom trip.
- **Background hero shelving** — whoever styled that shot put white modular units with colored bins and plants behind the sofa. That's my Kallax mood board. Even promoting sofas, they're speaking the visual language I came for.
- **Camera search icon in the search bar** — millennial move. I screenshot Kallax hacks from Reddit all the time; being able to visual-search directly is genuinely useful.

## What's weak

- **Hero offer misses my shopping mission** — I came for storage and they're leading with sleeper sofas. The "10% off sofa beds" deal is dead air for me; I'll mentally skip it.
- **No visible CTA button above the fold** — "Get" is clipped at the very bottom of the viewport. I don't know if it says "Get the deal," "Get yours," or something else. That ambiguity costs clicks.
- **No product-level depth in the hero** — the shelving is background dressing. There's no Kallax callout, no "New Kallax colors," no "Shop what's behind the sofa." The visual is there but the link isn't.
- **No trust signals for online purchase** — no free-shipping threshold, no returns mention, no rating badges. If I'm adding to cart before my quarterly trip, I want to know the delivery math without hunting for it.
- **Pill nav cuts off** — there's a ">" indicator showing more categories, but I can't see what else is there without swiping. Fine, but there's no affordance telling me how many chips are hidden.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Storage & organization" sub-hero or secondary banner below the sleeper sofa hero** — even a slim strip: "Planning a Kallax build? Explore the system →" would intercept me without disrupting the sofa campaign.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in the sticky header or below the search bar** — something like "Free delivery on orders $499+" sets my cart-fill expectations before I even start browsing.
- **Make the CTA button fully visible in the viewport** — "Get 10% off" or "Shop sofa deals" should be completely readable without scrolling. Crop the hero image up 60px if needed.
- **Surface the visual-search icon more prominently with a one-line tooltip** — something like "Search by photo" next to the camera icon. Millennial impulse buyers will use it if they know it exists.
