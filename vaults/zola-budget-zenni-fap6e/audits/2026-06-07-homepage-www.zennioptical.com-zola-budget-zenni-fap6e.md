# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Zenni feels like it was built for me — the "Under $30" chip is literally the first category filter I see, the BOGO promo is impossible to miss, and the AI search bar is exactly how a 26-year-old shops for anything. Right now the brand is pushing a buy-one-get-one deal hard, which honestly? Works on me. This reads as a budget-forward, inclusive eyewear brand doing a solid job of the basics.

## What's working

- **"Under $30" chip** — it's the first filter visible in the horizontal rail and it speaks directly to how I shop. I don't need to dig through nav to find budget frames.
- **AI search bar** — "Search or let AI recommend frames" with the sparkle icon feels native to how I actually discover things. TikTok-to-search is my pipeline and this fits.
- **BOGO promo banner** — "Buy one, get one 20% off. Code: GET20" is unambiguous. I buy in multiples anyway (nine pairs, remember), so this immediately applies to my cart math.
- **Lifestyle hero with diverse humans** — the young woman with a phone is aspirational to me specifically. Real people wearing glasses > model-on-runway.
- **"New Arrivals" and "Cat Eye" chips** — both visible in the scrollable chip rail, both exactly what I tap when I'm browsing for the next color-pop frame.

## What's weak

- **No hero headline visible above the fold** — whatever the main message is, it's buried below the image crop. The hero image leads with people but no value prop copy is readable in the viewport.
- **"GET 15% OFF" floating sidebar** — the gift-box tab on the left edge feels cluttered and competes with the BOGO banner. Two competing offers with no clear hierarchy is confusing.
- **No clear primary CTA button** — the hero has no "Shop Now" or "Browse Frames" button overlaid. The chip rail sort of fills that role but it's easy to miss as navigation vs. conversion.
- **Older man in the hero** — I get that Zenni is for everyone, but the three-person hero is trying to serve everyone and ends up being a little generic for me specifically. A TikTok-coded frame moment would hit harder.
- **Trust signals missing above fold** — no review count, no "free shipping over $X," no return policy mention. I need at least one of those to feel comfortable pulling the trigger.

## Recommendations

- **Add a single hero CTA button** — something like "Shop Under $30" or "Find My Frames" overlaid on the hero image. Right now there's no obvious first tap beyond the chips.
- **Kill or merge the two competing discount offers** — either lead with BOGO or lead with 15% off, but not both floating at the same time. Pick the stronger offer and commit.
- **Add a micro trust strip below the chip rail** — one line: "Free shipping on orders over $X · 365-day returns" would push me from browsing to buying mode without scrolling.
- **Swap one hero occupant for a solo Gen Z frame moment** — a single close-up of color-pop frames on a young face (phone-filmed style) would convert way better than the group lifestyle shot for my demographic.
