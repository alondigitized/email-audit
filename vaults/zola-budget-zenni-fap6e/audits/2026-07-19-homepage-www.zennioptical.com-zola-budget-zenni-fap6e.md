# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay so literally the first thing I see is a pop-up blocking everything — classic. Zenni's out here going full discount mode with a 15% off email grab and a BOGO code in the top banner, so the vibe is deal-forward, which tracks for them. I can barely see anything underneath but there's a "CLASS-TO-RECESS" headline peeking out at the bottom which honestly sounds like my entire personality right now.

## What's working

- **BOGO 20% banner at the very top** — "Buy one, get one 20% off. Code: GET20" is exactly the kind of thing I screenshot and send to my group chat
- **"Unlock my 15% off" button copy** — "unlock" feels gamified and native to how I interact with apps, way better than "Subscribe"
- **"CLASS-TO-RECESS" campaign name** — the sliver I can see is already giving back-to-school-era TikTok energy and I want to see the rest of it
- **Modal layout is clean** — headline, explanation, input, button — no clutter, renders fine on mobile

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire experience** — I opened the app and immediately got gated. I haven't seen a single pair of frames yet. This is the anti-vibe.
- **$65 minimum order** — on Zenni?? Their thing is $6.95 frames. Asking me to spend $65 to "unlock" 15% off is giving bait-and-switch energy, and the fine print confirms exclusions on top of it
- **"New customers only"** — if I've bought from Zenni before (I have), this modal is doing literally nothing for me except blocking my view
- **The rest of the homepage is invisible** — I have zero idea what's in the nav, what styles are featured, or what the CLASS-TO-RECESS campaign even looks like

## Recommendations

- **Kill the modal or trigger it on exit-intent, not page-load** — let me see the homepage for at least 10 seconds before interrupting me; I came here to shop, not to be recruited
- **Drop or raise the $65 floor** — at Zenni's price point, $65 minimum for a "welcome" discount reads as a gotcha; a $30 minimum or flat $5 off would feel way more honest
- **Add a returning-customer route** — if I close the modal without signing up, drop a loyalty CTA or a "welcome back" banner that speaks to me specifically
- **Let the CLASS-TO-RECESS hero breathe** — whatever lifestyle/campaign imagery is behind that modal, put it front and center; that copy is already doing the right work for my demo
