# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this feels like Zenni actually knows who I am? Three girls rocking totally different frame vibes — clear, oversized-glam, and sunnies — against this bright balloon-y backdrop is giving main character energy. It's reading trendy budget eyewear for young people who treat frames like accessories, which is literally me. The horizontal pill nav with "Under $30" and "Cat Eye" right there is chef's kiss for someone who shops by vibe-category, not by prescription need.

## What's working

- **The "Under $30" pill front-and-center** — this is literally my entire shopping filter. It's the first pill in the scroll row, positioned before "New Arrivals" and "Best Sellers." That's intentional and it's correct.
- **The three-girl hero image** — one clear/minimal frame, one bold oversized pink-blazer look, one sunnies. That's basically my nine-pair rotation in three models. I see myself here.
- **Category pill row** — "Tortoiseshell," "Cat Eye," "Oversized" are all TikTok-trending shapes right now. Whoever built this nav clearly looked at eyewear FYP.
- **Camera search icon in the search bar** — upload a photo to search? That's exactly how I'd upload my Rx or a frame inspo screenshot. Super native to how I use my phone.
- **The floating 15% OFF button** — annoying positioning but honestly I would tap that immediately.

## What's weak

- **Hero headline is cut off** — "ONLY IN THE" is visible but the rest is below the fold. I have no idea what the offer or collection is. That's a miss for above-the-fold impact.
- **No CTA button visible** — I see category pills and a cut-off hero but there's no "Shop Now" or "Shop Cat Eye" button I could tap from this screen. The hero is all image, no action.
- **$89 free shipping threshold** — I buy $30 frames. I'd need three pairs to hit $89. That's not impossible for me but it's not the "easy win" it would be for someone buying one pair at $90. Feels slightly out of step with the budget-girl persona.
- **Zero trust signals** — no review counts, no star ratings, no "4.8 stars from 2M customers" badge. For a new visitor, there's nothing to establish that these cheap frames are actually good.
- **The 15% OFF gift button placement** — it's floating on the left edge over the hero, overlapping the content. On a small phone screen this is visually cluttered.

## Recommendations

- **Reveal the full hero headline** — either shorten "ONLY IN THE [X]" to fit above the fold or add a visible CTA button overlaid on the hero. Right now the biggest real estate on the page is doing nothing actionable.
- **Add a social proof strip above the fold** — something like "4.8★ from 2M+ reviews" or "As seen on TikTok" right under the nav. That's the trust signal Gen Z actually responds to before buying a $30 frame from a brand they found through an ad.
- **Drop the free shipping threshold callout to something reachable** — or add a secondary hook like "Free returns on all orders" that works regardless of cart size. Make the $30-frame buyer feel like the offer is for them.
- **Move or integrate the 15% OFF button** — make it a sticky banner or a promo pill in the nav row instead of a floating gift icon that covers the hero image on mobile.
