# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zenni is literally built for me — nine pairs deep and I basically use this app like a frame subscription. Right now it's screaming 4th of July sale, tiered discounts, and an "Under $30" pill front and center, which is my whole personality. The vibe is deal-first, which I respect, but the hero gives me nothing to look at — it's stars and text, no actual frames, no face, no color moment.

## What's working

- **"Under $30" filter pill** — it's the very first pill in the horizontal scroll and it has a little frames icon. That is my entire budget identity in one tap. Love.
- **Style-first category pills** — Cat Eye, Tortoiseshell, Oversized all in one row. I can navigate like I'm swiping through my frames drawer.
- **Tiered discount structure** — 15% off 1 pair, 20% off 2 pairs is exactly how I shop (I always buy two because the math hits). Smart.
- **AI search bar** — "Search or let AI recommend frames" with the camera icon? That's native to how I interact with apps. I'd use that.
- **Promo code displayed openly** — JULY4TH right in the banner, no buried fine print hunt. That's how you build trust with me.
- **Deadline visible** — "Ends 7/5" creates real urgency because it's literally today.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is just vibes, no product** — blue background, white stars, sale text. There's zero frame inspo here. I came to Zenni to see a cute pair of $22 cat eyes on someone, not a 4th of July graphic.
- **No CTA button visible** — the hero has the sale copy but I can't find the "Shop Now" or "Shop Sale" button above the fold. That's friction.
- **No model or face** — not a single person visible in the screenshot. For a brand where the whole point is how frames look on your face, this is a miss.
- **Loyalty/rewards nowhere in sight** — I know Zenni has a loyalty program but it's not surfaced here. A "You have X points" nudge would pull me to buy now.
- **Cart icon with no item count** — no persistent basket reminder if I've shopped before.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the star-field hero for a product lifestyle shot** — feature a person wearing color-pop frames (tortoiseshell cat eye, maybe a bold color block) with the sale badge overlaid. Keep the urgency copy, lose the generic patriotic graphic.
- **Add a visible "Shop Sale" button** in the hero before the fold — even mobile-first layouts can fit one clear pill button. Right now the call to action is implied by the sale headline, not executed.
- **Surface a loyalty/points nudge in the announcement bar** — rotate the promo code and a "Check your points balance →" message for returning shoppers like me.
- **Pull one "trending now" frame into the hero** — a single product image with "Trending: Tortoiseshell Cat Eye — $19.95" under the sale copy would convert me faster than any headline.
