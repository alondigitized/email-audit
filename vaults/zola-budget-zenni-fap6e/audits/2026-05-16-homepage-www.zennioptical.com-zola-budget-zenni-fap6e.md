# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay so immediately I get it — this is the affordable eyewear brand that gets Gen Z. The hero is three young women in color-pop frames with a balloon-party background, and the pill nav literally starts with "Under $30" which is kind of my whole personality. They're clearly running some kind of new collection drop right now, though the hero headline is frustratingly cut off so I can't tell what the actual vibe is.

## What's working

- **"Under $30" pill** — first category chip in the scrollable nav, positioned for me before I even blink. This is my budget in a button.
- **Hero imagery** — three young women, diverse, wearing different frame styles (clear, oversized, sunnies), colorful balloon background. It reads like a Pinterest board I'd actually save.
- **Camera icon in search bar** — visual search is huge for a TikTok shopper. I screenshot frames constantly and this feature tells me they get it.
- **Category pill row** — "Cat Eye," "Tortoiseshell," "New Arrivals," "Best Sellers" are exactly the filter terms I use on TikTok Shop. One tap to what I want.
- **15% OFF floating tab** — before I've done anything, there's a discount on the table.

## What's weak

- **Hero headline is cut off** — "ONLY IN THE" is all I can see. The most important copy on the page is cropped off-screen, which means the whole value prop of this campaign is invisible at first glance.
- **No button CTA above the fold** — the pills help, but there's no big obvious "Shop the Drop" or "Shop Under $30" button. The hero is decoration without a destination.
- **Floating "GET 15% OFF" tab** — I know this leads to an email-capture popup. It's a little pushy when I literally just arrived.
- **Pill labels are clipped** — "der $30" and "ersized" are clearly truncated on the left, which looks unfinished and slightly broken on mobile.
- **Zero trust signals above fold** — no review counts, no "free returns," no "100,000+ five-star reviews" badge. For a first-time visitor this is a gap.

## Recommendations

- **Resize or stack the hero text** so the full headline is visible on mobile without scrolling — if the campaign is "Only in the..." something, I need to read the whole thing to care.
- **Add a single bold CTA button** over the hero (e.g., "Shop the Drop" or "Shop Under $30") — the pill nav is great but a button closes the loop.
- **Replace the floating 15% off tab with an inline banner** below the pill nav — same offer, less dark-pattern energy, doesn't feel like it's chasing me.
- **Drop a micro trust signal** (e.g., "4.7★ from 200K+ reviews" or "Free returns on your first pair") into the shipping banner row so it pulls double duty.
