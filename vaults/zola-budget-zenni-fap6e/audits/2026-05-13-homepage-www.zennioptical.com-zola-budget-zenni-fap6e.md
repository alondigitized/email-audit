# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Zenni is giving very "girly TikTok haul" energy right off the bat — three girls in a balloon-pop color explosion, all holding frames like accessories, which honestly is exactly how I treat my nine pairs. It's clearly pushing some kind of new collection drop with that "ONLY IN THE" hero (text is cut off so I don't know the full name), and the quick-filter chips at the top are chef's kiss for how I actually shop — I'm immediately scanning for Cat Eye and Tortoiseshell.

## What's working

- **The "Under $30" quick-filter chip** — that's my entire identity as a buyer and it's the FIRST chip I see. Immediate validation that this place gets me.
- **Cat Eye + Tortoiseshell filter chips** — my two most-worn shape/pattern combos, both one tap away without even opening the nav.
- **The hero imagery** — three women who look like my friend group, not stock-photo models, styling frames like they're jewelry. That's the exact energy I bring when I pick a new pair.
- **Camera icon in the search bar** — I upload Rx photos via my phone constantly; this signals they get the mobile-native flow.
- **The color palette of the hero** — bright balloons, pink blazer, mustard yellow — this feels like a TikTok unboxing backdrop, which is my entire reference frame for frame shopping.

## What's weak

- **"ONLY IN THE" — the hero headline is cut off** — I can't read what the collection is called. If I can't read the pitch in the first second, the launch momentum dies.
- **No visible CTA button in the hero** — there's no "Shop the Drop" or "See All Styles" button above the fold. The hero just… stops.
- **The "GET 15% OFF" floating side tab** — I know it's an email capture in disguise. It's a little desperate and I instinctively ignore those. Doesn't feel earned.
- **No trust signals in sight** — no "100K+ 5-star reviews," no "free returns," nothing that tells me the purchase is safe. Big gap for a first-visit convert.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I've bought from Zenni before (probably). Show me if I have points or if I'm close to a reward. That's what gets me to buy pair number ten.

## Recommendations

- **Complete the hero headline** — if "ONLY IN THE ___" is a collection name, make sure the full text is readable on mobile without scrolling. One truncated word kills the drop energy.
- **Add a floating "Shop New Arrivals" button** in the hero section — even a small pill CTA with an arrow. Right now there's visual interest but no path forward above the fold.
- **Replace the generic 15% off email popup tab with a loyalty widget** — show returning users their point balance or a "You're $X away from a free pair" nudge. That converts me way harder than a discount I'd get regardless.
- **Inject a trust-signal strip** right below the search bar or above the hero — something like "★ 4.8 from 500K+ reviews · Free returns · Ships in 3 days" — one line, massive confidence boost.
