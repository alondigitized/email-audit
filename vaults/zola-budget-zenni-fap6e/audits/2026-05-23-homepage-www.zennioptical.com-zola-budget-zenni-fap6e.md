# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Ok so Zenni is literally built for me — nine pairs deep and I already know this site — but opening it right now feels like walking into a store where they put a giant "SALE" sign in the window and nothing else. The Memorial Day push is loud and clear, and the filter chips for Under $30 / Cat Eye / Tortoiseshell in the nav strip are actually so good, but the hero is just... yellow text on yellow. No frames, no people, no vibe.

## What's working

- **The quick-filter chip strip** — "Under $30", "New Arrivals", "Best Sellers", "Cat Eye", "Tortoiseshell", "Oversized" sitting right under the search bar is genuinely great. I can get to my category in one tap without touching the hamburger menu.
- **"BUY MORE SAVE MORE" structure** — speaking directly to someone who buys multiple pairs at once. The 15%/20% tier framing rewards exactly my behavior.
- **Deadline is real** — "Ends 5/25 | Restrictions apply." is right there in the hero. Two days out, I feel the clock without it being screamy.
- **Top banner dismiss** — the Memorial Day strip has an X and so does the "GET 15% OFF" side tab. Closing them feels native, not punishing.
- **Camera icon in the search bar** — that's visual search / Rx photo upload, which is literally my workflow. Love that it's surfaced immediately.

## What's weak

- **Zero frames visible** — the entire above-the-fold is yellow background and text. For a glasses brand, not showing a single frame in the hero is wild. I can't lust after anything.
- **No people, no lifestyle** — Zenni's whole thing is self-expression through frames, and there's not a single human face or outfit pairing in this viewport. TikTok is all about "this frame with this fit" and this delivers none of that energy.
- **"GET 15% OFF" tab** — the floating side promo tab with a gift box is cluttering the left edge. It looks like a bug until you realize it's a CTA. Not cute.
- **"Up to 25% off" vs. "15%/20% tiers"** — the top banner promises 25% and the hero delivers 20% max. Even if there's a three-pair tier buried, it reads as bait-and-switch until you dig.
- **No CTA button visible** — the hero copy is all sell and no tap. The actual "Shop Now" button must be below the fold on mobile, which is a miss for anyone who bounces fast.

## Recommendations

- **Put at least one colorful frame in the hero** — a side-by-side of three color-pop pairs against the yellow background would cost nothing and make the sale feel like it's about glasses, not a coupon.
- **Add a "3 pairs? 25% off" tier line** to the hero copy — right now the top banner implies 25% but the hero only shows 15%/20%. Make the full tier table visible: 1 pair = 15%, 2 pairs = 20%, 3+ = 25%.
- **Make the primary CTA button visible in the first screen** — move "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Under $30" into the hero card itself so there's something to tap without scrolling.
- **Swap the floating side tab for a sticky bottom bar** — "GET 15% OFF" as a pill button pinned to the bottom of the screen is less janky and more native to how Gen Z apps work.
