# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, immediately I'm like — this feels like *my* app. Three girls serving looks in colorful frames, a punchy partial headline, quick-tap chips for Cat Eye and Tortoiseshell? Zenni clearly knows their girl. It's pushing a newness story (New Arrivals prominent) with a soft discount hook baked into a floating button, which is very "swipe-up energy." Not perfect, but it's clearly not talking to my mom.

## What's working

- **The hero lifestyle image** — three distinct styles of girls, three distinct frame personalities (oversized cat-eye, bold statement, brown sunnies). That's literally how I shop: one for class, one for a date, one for the weekend.
- **Visual search icon in the search bar** — camera icon for searching glasses by image is *extremely* my behavior. I screenshot frames I see on TikTok and want to find dupes.
- **Quick-filter chip rail** — "Cat Eye," "Tortoiseshell," "New Arrivals," "Best Sellers" are all one tap. No digging through a mega-menu.
- **"GET 15% OFF" floating button** — annoying placement but the offer is real and I'd tap it to see the code before I started browsing.
- **"Under $30" chip** — that's literally my budget filter. The fact it's in the chip rail and not buried in a sidebar is huge.

## What's weak

- **Hero headline "ONLY IN THE"** — it's cut off at the bottom of the screen. The most important copy on the page is invisible. If this is "ONLY IN THE APP," that's a compelling hook that I literally cannot read.
- **$89 free-shipping threshold** — I'm a $30-frame girl. To get free shipping I'd need to buy three pairs minimum. That threshold feels like it's aimed at someone buying premium frames, not me.
- **No social proof anywhere** — no star ratings, no "X,000 reviews," no UGC badge. Zenni has a massive review count and it's not doing any work here.
- **The floating 15% off button** — the gift box icon makes it look like a pop-up gift I didn't ask for. Slightly dark-pattern vibes, even if the offer is legit.
- **"Prem..." chip is cut off** — I can see a chip on the far right that's illegible. That's a trust chip missing its label.

## Recommendations

- **Reveal the full hero headline** — if "ONLY IN THE APP" is the offer, make it visible. If it's something else, I need to see it. Truncated headlines read as broken on mobile.
- **Drop the free-shipping threshold to $59 or show a tiered bar** — "You're $29 away from free shipping" is a conversion mechanic I'd actually respond to, and it doesn't feel punishing for budget shoppers.
- **Add a micro-trust strip below the chip rail** — one line: "4.7★ · 5M+ reviews · 30-day returns." You have the receipts; use them.
- **Replace the floating 15% off widget with an inline banner chip** — something like a chip styled "15% OFF — tap for code" fits the existing UI language and doesn't feel like an ad unit.
