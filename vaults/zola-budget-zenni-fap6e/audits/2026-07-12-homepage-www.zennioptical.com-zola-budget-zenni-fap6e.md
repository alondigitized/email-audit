# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Zenni opened to a full-screen photo of a little kid in pink frames — which is cute but like, I'm not buying glasses for a child? The top banner is doing the heavy lifting with a BOGO deal, and the filter chips are actually super useful for how I shop. But the hero is telling me this is a back-to-school or kids section, not a vibe-based $30 frame haul for me.

## What's working

- **BOGO banner with actual code** — "Buy one, get one 20% off. Code: GET20" is chef's kiss because I literally collect frames the way other people collect earrings. BOGO speaks directly to my multi-pair lifestyle.
- **"Under $30" chip** — that's my sweet spot and it's the first chip I see. One tap and I'm in my budget zone.
- **AI search bar** — "Search or let AI recommend frames" with a ✦ icon is genuinely exciting to me as a tech-native. I'd tap that immediately.
- **Filter chip row** — "New Arrivals," "Best Sellers," "Cat Eye," "Tortoiseshell," "Oversized" are all the exact TikTok frame trends I care about. These chips do the work the hero image doesn't.
- **GET 15% OFF side tab** — there's a secondary offer stacked next to the main BOGO, which feels like a bonus find.

## What's weak

- **Child hero image** — the entire above-fold visual real estate is a kid in pink glasses. I almost closed the tab thinking I landed on the wrong page. Zero connection to my identity as a young adult buyer.
- **No hero copy/headline** — I can't see any text over the image. There's no "Your next $30 obsession" or seasonal hook. The hero is just… a face.
- **No primary CTA button** — the filter chips are helpful but they're not a CTA. There's no "Shop Frames" or "Find My Vibe" button anywhere in this viewport.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "4.8 stars from 1M customers," no "free returns." I'm a first-time-ish viewer here and nothing builds confidence below the promo.
- **Demographic disconnect** — if I'm going by the rubric, a child model is the furthest possible signal from Gen Z adult frame shopper energy.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the kids hero for a Gen Z lifestyle shot** — a 20-something making a face at the camera in cat-eye color-pop frames, something I'd see on my For You page. The BOGO angle sells itself if you show me who it's actually for.
- **Add a visible CTA button** — put a "Shop Under $30" or "Find My Frame" button directly in the hero area so there's a clear tap target, not just chips.
- **Drop a review count or star rating strip** — even one line like "Over 50 million frames sold ★★★★★" would make me trust this enough to actually commit to a cart.
- **Tie the BOGO to the multi-pair angle** — instead of generic "buy one get one," make the copy land for someone like me: "Stack two pairs. Pay less than one at the mall." That's a hook I'd screenshot and send to my groupchat.
