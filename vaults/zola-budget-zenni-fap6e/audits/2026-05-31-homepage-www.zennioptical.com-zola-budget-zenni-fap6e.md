# Homepage snapshot · www.zennioptical.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Zenni is giving me exactly the "affordable but make it fun" energy I'd expect — the first thing I see is "Under $30" as a shortcut chip and an AI search bar, which honestly had me at hello. The hero is lifestyle-y and diverse but the people in it skew older than my friend group, which is a vibe mismatch. Still, this clearly knows its lane: budget-friendly, frame-as-accessory brand.

## What's working

- **"Under $30" category chip** — first pill in the horizontal scroll tray, right where my thumb lands. That's my whole budget in a single tap.
- **AI-recommend search bar** — "Search or let AI recommend frames" with the little sparkle AI icon is genuinely exciting to me. Camera icon next to it probably means virtual try-on or Rx scan — huge for uploading prescriptions from my phone.
- **Trending shape chips** — "Tortoiseshell," "Cat Eye," "Oversized" are literally the TikTok frame taxonomy. Someone at Zenni knows how I shop.
- **Free shipping banner** — pinned top banner, clearly states $89+ threshold. Transparent and useful.
- **Clean mobile nav** — hamburger, logo, wishlist heart, cart. The heart (wishlist) is very me — I save frames like I save TikToks.

## What's weak

- **Hero cast skews 35-55** — the Black woman and the silver-bearded man look like my parents' age group, not mine. The phone-selfie woman on the left is the only one who reads as my generation.
- **No hero headline visible** — I can see people and sky but there's no punchy copy on the hero. Nothing is telling me *why* I should be excited right now.
- **"GET 15% OFF" sidebar tab is distracting** — the vertical floating tab with a gift emoji fights for attention in a weird way. It reads like a pop-up you'd close, not a deal you'd click.
- **"Best Sellers" and "New Arrivals" chips promise a rail that isn't there yet** — I see the label but no actual product grid above the fold. Makes me work harder to get to the good stuff.
- **Accessibility button renders large and awkward** in the top-right corner of the hero area — visually it interrupts the image.

## Recommendations

- **Swap at least one hero face for an obvious Gen Z model** — think septum ring, funky colorblock frame, phone raised for a GRWM video. Show me I belong here.
- **Add a hero text overlay** — even three words like "Frames under $30" or "New drops: cat eye" would turn the lifestyle image into a conversion moment.
- **Convert the floating "GET 15% OFF" tab into a proper sticky promo strip or inline banner** — it's a good offer buried in a UX pattern I associate with sketchy sites.
- **Surface one featured product rail above the fold** — even 3-4 product cards peeking in at the bottom of the first screen would pull me into scrolling instantly.
