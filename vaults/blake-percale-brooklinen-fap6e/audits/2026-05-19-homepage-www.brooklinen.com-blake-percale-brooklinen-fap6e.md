# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen is running a Memorial Day clearance push — 25% off sitewide, up to 50% off bundles — and structurally the page handles it well. As someone who owns the Classic Percale set and a Luxe Sateen backup, I'm already a customer here; this homepage reads like it knows me but won't say my name. It's selling the discount before it sells the craft, which is the inverse of what keeps sheet snobs loyal.

## What's working

- **25% off sitewide in the hero** — clear, credible, above the fold. No hunting, no asterisk.
- **Weave naming in Best Sellers** — "Classic Percale," "Luxe Sateen," "Washed European Linen" are exactly the terms I sort by. Brooklinen is one of the few DTC sheet brands that leads with weave, and it shows up here.
- **Secondary bundle promo strip** — the yellow band with "Up to 50% Off Bundles" is unmissable and I do refresh multiple rooms. That's a real use-case hook for me.
- **150K+ 5-star reviews callout** — the scale signals this isn't a pop-up brand. I can see star ratings on individual Best Sellers products, which is low-friction validation.
- **Flat navigation** — Bed, Bundle Savings, Best Sellers, Bath, New Arrivals, Sale. One tap and I'm in the right aisle. No buried mega-menu nonsense.

## What's weak

- **"Get your bed summer-ready with our coolest sheets this Memorial Day"** — "coolest" is pulling double duty as pun and product claim and succeeding at neither. For me, "cool" means percale weave and low GSM, not seasonal vibes.
- **Every Best Sellers product carries a "BEST VALUE" badge** — when four out of five items are "best value," the badge loses meaning entirely. Reserve it for the bundles; let the individual sets stand on quality.
- **Free-shipping threshold is not visible** — I'm about to drop $127+ on a sheet set and I have no idea where the free-shipping line is. That's a friction point that could push me to check a competitor.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment** — I've bought from Brooklinen before. There's no loyalty hook, no "you have X points," nothing that says the brand knows I exist outside of email.
- **No weave education module** — Brooklinen's biggest brand differentiator is the weave-explainer content they send in email. None of that texture is on this homepage. First-time visitors would scroll past not knowing what makes percale different from sateen.

## Recommendations

- Replace "Get your bed summer-ready with our coolest sheets" with weave-anchored copy: "Percale for hot sleepers. Sateen for cold. 25% off both." — ships this week, zero design changes required.
- Surface a free-shipping threshold in the sticky nav bar: "Free shipping over $X — you're $Y away." This is table stakes for a sheet brand at this price point.
- Trim "BEST VALUE" to one badge per scroll viewport — right now it's on the Luxe Sateen bundle, the Linen bundle, AND the Classic Percale bundle simultaneously, which trains me to ignore it.
- Add a soft returning-customer hook in the nav or hero subhead: "Welcome back — your 25% off is live" with a loyalty balance if they have one. Even a one-line acknowledgment changes the relationship.
