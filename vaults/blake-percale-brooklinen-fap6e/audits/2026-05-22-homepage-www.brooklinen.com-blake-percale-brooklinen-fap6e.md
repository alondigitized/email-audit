# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen knows exactly who it's talking to — this is a brand confident enough to lead with the product and let the sheets do the selling. Right now it's pushing a Memorial Day sitewide sale hard, with 25% off front and center and bundles layered underneath as the upsell. Clean, aspirational, and competently executed.

## What's working

- **The hero bed shot**: those warm-stripe sheets styled against a neutral waffle throw look like real percale — matte finish, crisp edges. As someone who buys by feel and knows what percale looks like in a photograph, this image does the selling before I even read a word.
- **"25% OFF Sitewide Now"**: bold, clean, center-frame. No decoding required.
- **"Get your best summer ready with our coolest sheets"**: "coolest" lands for me because I know they mean breathability — classic percale positioning.
- **The golden bundles strip below the hero**: "Up to 50% Off Bundles" is a strong second beat. I have a guest room to stock and bundles are how I shop for that.
- **Navigation bar**: Bed, Bundle Savings, Best Sellers, Bath, New Arrivals, Sale — I can get to my category in one tap, zero guesswork.

## What's weak

- **No weave language anywhere above the fold**: I'm the customer who reads every weave-explainer email, and not a single word — percale, sateen, GSM — is visible on this screen. The brand is sitting on a competitive edge and not using it.
- **No loyalty or returning-customer signal**: I've been a Brooklinen customer, presumably. There's no rewards balance, no "welcome back," no member pricing. Feels like I'm being treated as a stranger.
- **Banner copy is garbled on mobile**: The top announcement bar on the right reads something like "New and super for Super Pi" — cut off and unreadable. A banner I can't parse is a banner I ignore.
- **No trust signals at a glance**: No review count, no "free shipping over $X," no return policy mention. For a $100+ sheet purchase, I want that reassurance without scrolling.

## Recommendations

- **Add weave copy to the hero subhead**: swap "our coolest sheets" for "our crispest percale" or "long-staple percale from $X" — one word unlocks the entire sheet-snob audience.
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold to the announcement bar**: replace the garbled right side of the top strip with "Free shipping on orders $75+" — one line, always visible, zero-effort trust signal.
- **Show a loyalty nudge for returning users**: even a small "Your rewards: $X available" badge in the top-right navigation area would make me feel seen rather than anonymous.
- **Fix the announcement bar truncation on mobile**: the right-side message is unreadable at this viewport. Either shorten the copy or use a carousel so both messages display fully.
