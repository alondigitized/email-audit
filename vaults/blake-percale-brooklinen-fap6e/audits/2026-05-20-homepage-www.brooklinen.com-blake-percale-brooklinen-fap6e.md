# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Brooklinen doing exactly what I'd expect from them in late May — Memorial Day sale energy, sitewide discounts stacked with a free gift, and a Best Sellers rail that actually shows the sheet sets I care about. It reads as a premium-but-accessible linen brand that knows its customer is comparison-shopping on quality, not just price. Right now they're pushing hard on value and bundling, which is smart but risks making the brand feel a little more commodity than it usually does.

## What's working

- **"25% Off Sitewide Now" hero** — zero ambiguity, exactly the kind of hard number I scan for before I decide whether to bother
- **Free Pillow Protector Set callout** — the bonus gift with $150+ purchase is a smart hook for someone who's already in the basket-building mindset
- **"Up to 50% Off Bundles" secondary strip** — I buy in sets, not singles, so bundle deals are directly relevant; the yellow accent pulls the eye cleanly
- **Best Sellers rail with pricing** — showing "From $263" with a strikethrough MSRP on the Lux Sateen Hardcore Sheet Bundle gives me immediate value context without a click
- **"Best Value" badges on multiple products** — signals curation, not just dumping inventory; I trust brands that tell me what to buy
- **Star ratings visible in the rail** — quick credibility check without leaving the homepage

## What's weak

- **No weave/material callout in the hero** — "25% Off Sitewide" could be any linen brand; nothing says percale or sateen or thread count in the above-the-fold zone, which is where I differentiate
- **Shop by Category icons are tiny on mobile** — the row of small circular thumbnails reads as decorative, not navigational; I can barely make out what the categories are
- **No loyalty or account hook** — I'm a returning customer (I own multiple Brooklinen sets), and there's zero signal that the site knows me or rewards me for it
- **Hero lifestyle imagery is generic** — a neatly made bed is fine but doesn't communicate fabric quality the way a close-up texture shot would; I want to *feel* the weave through the screen
- **"Shop Now" CTA on the bundle strip feels low-effort** — compared to "Shop the Sale" it's forgettable; could be "Shop Bundles" for a more directed tap

## Recommendations

- **Add a weave identifier to the hero line** — even one word ("Percale. Sateen. All 25% off.") tells me this is a sheets brand with actual technical depth, not a generic home goods sale
- **Swap the circular category nav for a horizontal text+image strip** — legible category names ("Percale Sheets," "Sateen Sheets," "Duvet Covers") in a scrollable strip would let me navigate in one tap on mobile instead of squinting at thumbnails
- **Surface a returning-customer signal** — a small "Welcome back" banner or a "Your last purchase: Classic Percale" tile above the Best Sellers rail would immediately tell me the site recognizes me and rewards loyalty
- **Pull a texture close-up into the hero or secondary module** — a macro shot of fabric weave is the fastest shorthand for quality to a sheet snob; it costs nothing to swap one lifestyle shot for it
