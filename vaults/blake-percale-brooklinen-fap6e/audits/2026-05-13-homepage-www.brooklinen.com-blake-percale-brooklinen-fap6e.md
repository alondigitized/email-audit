# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen is speaking my language right now — a full "Cooling Shop" push in mid-May is exactly the kind of seasonally-aware move I respect from a bedding brand. The page reads clean, the product photography is sharp, and seeing the Classic Percale called out by name in Best Sellers made me stop scrolling. It's a confident, well-structured homepage that knows its audience is here for bedding, not a department-store runaround.

## What's working

- **"The Cooling Shop" hero**: the seasonal framing lands immediately — I know exactly what the brand is leading with this month, and it's relevant to me
- **Classic Percale Hardcore Sheet Bundle in Best Sellers**: seeing percale called out by name, with a "NEW COLOR" badge and a substantial bundle discount, is a direct hit for someone who specifically seeks percale weaves — this is the product I came to find
- **150K+ 5-star reviews section**: "Like Sleeping On A Cloud" with a real quoted review and stars is strong social proof, not just a generic badge
- **Category navigation**: "Bed," "Bundle Savings," "New Arrivals" in the top nav means I can get to my section in one tap without hunting
- **Clean mobile layout**: no overlapping text, the carousel arrows on Best Sellers are obvious, the product cards are readable at thumb-scroll speed

## What's weak

- **No returning-shopper recognition**: I've bought from Brooklinen before and the homepage gives me zero signal that it knows this — no rewards points, no "complete the set" suggestion, no member pricing
- **"Explore the Collection" is a soft CTA**: for the hero of a seasonal campaign, this is vague — I want "Shop Cooling Percale" or "Shop the Cooling Bundle," not an invitation to wander
- **No shipping threshold visible**: I can't see whether I'm close to free shipping before I even click — for bundle purchases this matters
- **No urgency on the Cooling Shop**: no "limited quantities," no "ends June 1" — the campaign feels open-ended, which deflates any impulse to act now
- **Top banner is a collab announcement, not an offer**: the DualCool collab teaser is interesting but doesn't give me a reason to click — it's awareness copy, not conversion copy

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Explore the Collection" with a weave-specific CTA**: "Shop Percale & Cooling Linen" takes the same button and makes it earn its space — it speaks to the buyer who already knows what she wants
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the hero or nav bar**: a sticky "Free shipping on orders over $X" strip above the nav costs nothing and answers the first question every returning shopper has
- **Surface a loyalty hook near Best Sellers**: a single line like "Members earn 2× points on bundles this week" between the category strip and the Best Sellers rail would convert me without a redesign
- **Put a soft deadline on the Cooling Shop**: even "Available through June" next to the CTA adds weight — an evergreen campaign headline reads as filler after the second visit
