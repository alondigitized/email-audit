# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-09-27
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a brand that knows exactly who I am — the hero copy calls out Classic Percale and Luxe Sheets by name instead of just saying "sheets," which is rare. Right now they're pushing a bundle sale (up to 40% off) stacked with 15% sitewide, wrapped in a moody, cinematic "Best. Sheets. Ever." campaign. It feels premium but sale-driven, which is a decent combo for my February-refresh brain even though it's September.

## What's working

- **"BEST. SHEETS. EVER." hero copy** — punchy, on-brand, and the subhead actually differentiates Percale vs. Luxe, which most brands skip
- **The yellow sale band** ("Up to 40% Off Bundles," "15% off sitewide") — impossible to miss, sits right below the nav
- **"Shop the Collection" rail** with SOFTEST/COOLING tags on the product tiles — that's the kind of quick-scan attribute labeling I actually use to decide what to buy
- **Two-tier CTA stack** in the hero ("Shop Sheets" + "Explore the Campaign") — gives me a fast path and a browse path

## What's weak

- **The top ticker banner is cut off** — I can only read fragments ("...our best in bed & bath!" and "Fast, free shipping on..."), which looks broken on a phone screen
- **No loyalty or rewards callout** — Brooklinen has a "Loom" style program in my memory of past visits, but nothing about it shows above the fold
- **Hero model doesn't reflect me** — sweet lifestyle shot, but nothing here signals a 35-year-old sheet-obsessive woman is the target buyer
- **"Limited time only" has no actual deadline** — no countdown, no end date, so it doesn't create real urgency for me

## Recommendations

- Fix the ticker banner truncation so the free-shipping threshold and promo text are fully readable on mobile without scrolling sideways
- Add a specific end date or countdown to the sale band ("Ends Sunday 9/29") instead of vague "limited time only"
- Surface a loyalty/rewards badge or "Welcome back" line near the account icon for returning shoppers like me
- Swap one hero frame for a flat-lay or close-up texture shot of the percale weave — sheet people like me buy on texture, not lifestyle vibes
