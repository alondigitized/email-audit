# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen opened exactly the way I'd hope — clean, editorial, and immediately in my world. The Cooling Shop hero is doing the right seasonal work for late May, and the lifestyle shot on those striped sheets (percale? linen? I need to know immediately) hits the aesthetic I'm here for. It's a brand that knows its customer is a grown adult who cares about the weave, not just the color.

## What's working

- **"The Cooling Shop" headline with attribute sub-copy** — "Lighter. Softer. Sleepier." is exactly how I think about sheets. It signals Brooklinen speaks percale and linen, not polyester-blend marketing fluff.
- **Hero lifestyle shot** — two adults on what reads as striped sateen or linen (I'm squinting at the weave already), with an organic-looking ceramic bowl of cherries. It's aspirational without being unreachable. I see myself in that bed.
- **"The Father's Day Edit" placement** — smart secondary module below the hero. Timely, actionable, doesn't clutter the main message.
- **"Shop by Category" chip rail** — seeing "New Arrivals" as a tappable chip means I don't have to hunt. That's one tap from where I'm standing.
- **"Bed" nav tab** — one tap to my department without digging into a hamburger menu.

## What's weak

- **Free-shipping threshold is cut off** — the announcement bar starts "Excl. Free shipping on…" and then I can't read the rest. If it's over $X I want to know immediately; this is the one number that changes my cart-building behavior.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I've almost certainly shopped here before. Brooklinen has a rewards program and there's zero mention of it above the fold. That's a missed re-engagement moment.
- **"Explore the Collection" CTA is soft** — it's better than "Discover," but "Shop the Cooling Shop" or "Shop Sheets Now" would convert me faster. "Explore" sounds like I'm browsing a museum.
- **No weave/fabric callout in hero** — "Lighter. Softer." tells me the feeling but not the material. A single word like "Percale" or "Linen" would make me stop scrolling immediately.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no review count, no "Free returns," nothing. I'm already bought in as a returning customer, but a new sheet snob landing here for the first time would want some social proof.

## Recommendations

- **Add weave type to the hero sub-copy** — change "Lighter. Softer. Sleepier." to something like "Crisp percale. Breathable linen." — one line that tells a sheet snob exactly what's in the shop.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold on the announcement bar in full** — "Free shipping on orders over $X" in legible type, not cropped by the viewport. This is a conversion driver for someone building a $150+ cart.
- **Swap "Explore the Collection" for "Shop the Cooling Shop"** — cleaner, more direct, less museum-tour energy.
- **Add a returning-member chip or small loyalty nudge near the CTA** — something like "Members earn points on every order" takes one line and reminds me my purchase has upside.
