# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Brooklinen is not messing around — first thing I see is a gorgeous close-up of what reads like a steel-blue sateen with that smooth, almost liquid drape I know by feel. The "New Luxier, Luxe Sheets" headline tells me they're pushing an updated version of their signature Luxe line, which is basically the product I already own and evangelize to anyone who'll listen. This homepage knows its customer.

## What's working

- **"New Luxier, Luxe Sheets" hero copy** — "softer and smoother than ever" is a direct shot at someone who already owns the Luxe and is debating an upgrade. I felt that.
- **Full-bleed steel-blue sheet close-up** — the drape and sheen are doing serious work. I can practically feel the GSM from here. Better than any model shot for this persona.
- **Rewards Exclusive band** — "$25 off with code BWHNE225" below the hero is exactly the kind of logged-in perk that keeps me coming back. It's personalized, it's specific, and it's asking me to log in, which tells me my account has value.
- **"Sheets" first in the nav** — one tap to my category. No digging.
- **"New Arrivals" in both nav and Shop by Category rail** — I can go exploratory or direct, my call.

## What's weak

- **Top banner text is nearly illegible at mobile scale** — "Save to 1/3 [something]" is too small and low-contrast to read. If there's a sitewide sale, I'm missing it entirely on first glance.
- **Free shipping threshold is cut off** — the banner says "Fast, Free shipping on…" and then it's truncated. I'd want to know that number before I start filling a cart.
- **No weave or GSM callout in the hero** — for Brooklinen's core buyer (me), "softer and smoother" is vague. Even a quick "now in 270 GSM long-staple cotton" line would close the loop. I'm reading the weave-explainer emails; the homepage should speak that language too.
- **No social proof visible** — no review count, no "over X million sold," nothing. The product is beloved; let the reviews do the heavy lifting above the fold.
- **"Learn More" as a second CTA** — this is weak alongside "Shop Now." If you want me to learn more, put a link in the copy. Two CTA buttons side-by-side dilutes the primary action.

## Recommendations

- **Make the top banner legible** — bump the font size, increase contrast, or shorten the copy to one scannable phrase. If "save up to 1/3" is the message, that's a headline, not a footnote.
- **Add a GSM or weave badge to the hero image** — "270 GSM | GOTS Certified" in a small pill overlay costs nothing to ship and converts sheet snobs immediately.
- **Swap "Learn More" for inline copy** — move any product education into a subhead under "New Luxier, Luxe Sheets" and let "Shop Now" stand alone as the singular CTA.
- **Surface a review count on the hero** — "Over 4.8★ from 50,000 reviews" next to the CTA removes any residual hesitation for a returning buyer who might be skeptical of "Luxier" as marketing speak.
