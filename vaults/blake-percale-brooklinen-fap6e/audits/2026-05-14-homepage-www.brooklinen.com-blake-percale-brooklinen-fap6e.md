# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen is running a full sitewide sale event — 25% off everything, stacked with a separate bundle deal — and using a clean bedroom lifestyle shot to frame it as a seasonal refresh moment. For me, the best thing on this page is the Best Sellers carousel: seeing "Classic Percale" and "Luxe Sateen" named explicitly is what makes me slow down. But the hero is all sale mechanics and zero material story, which for a sheet brand feels like leaving the best copy on the cutting room floor.

## What's working

- **"Classic Percale" and "Luxe Sateen" named in the Best Sellers carousel** — the moment I see those product names with pricing and swatches, I know I'm in the right place; weave-forward naming is the brand's biggest signal to buyers like me
- **Double-stacked promo: 25% sitewide + up to 50% on bundles** — the yellow strip directly below the hero makes the bundle math feel additive, not buried; this is actually how I'd shop a sheet refresh
- **Blue-and-white striped bedroom hero** — reads "clean hotel bed" which is the Brooklinen aesthetic I've bought into; the imagery is aspirational without being over-styled
- **150k+ 5-star reviews + "Like Sleeping On A Cloud" quote** — the social proof rail is well-placed and carries real weight; 150k reviews is a number that stops a cynical Millennial mid-scroll
- **"Bed" tab in primary nav** — one tap to my category from anywhere on the page, no hunting

## What's weak

- **"This shopping day" is placeholder urgency** — no countdown, no end date, no "Ends Sunday." I've been through enough Brooklinen sales to know this probably rolls into the next one; the vagueness undercuts the premium signal
- **Hero copy doesn't say the word "percale," "sateen," or "linen"** — for a brand whose entire moat is material knowledge, leading the hero with "our coolest sheets" reads like an apparel retailer not a bedding brand
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment** — I've bought from Brooklinen before and the page treats me like a stranger; a loyalty nudge or member-price badge would cost nothing and mean a lot
- **Free-shipping threshold is either missing or illegible** — the announcement bar at the top has text I can't read clearly; if it's calling out free shipping it's not doing the job
- **No weave-specific category tiles in the "Shop by Category" row** — the tiles appear to show broad categories (Bed, Bath, Pillows) rather than drilling to Percale, Sateen, Linen upfront, which is how a sheet buyer thinks

## Recommendations

- **Add one weave line to the hero overlay** — replace "our coolest sheets this shopping day" with something like "Percale. Sateen. Linen. 25% off everything." — it speaks directly to the buyer who knows what she wants and skips the generic
- **Swap "shopping day" for a hard deadline** — "Ends Sunday, May 18" or a visible 48-hour countdown makes the urgency credible; right now it reads like copy that got published without a date plugged in
- **Move Percale, Sateen, and Linen to the front of the "Shop by Category" tile row** — the material-first shopper's mental model starts at weave type, not room type; this is a zero-dev-effort tile reorder
- **Add a free-shipping callout with a legible threshold to the announcement bar** — even "Free shipping over $50" in readable type at the top converts returning visitors who are already leaning toward a purchase
