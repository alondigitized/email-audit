# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen shows up exactly the way I expect them to: clean, confident, and leading with a sitewide sale timed perfectly to Memorial Day weekend. The homepage reads as a brand that knows its customer is me — someone who's already sold on quality bedding and just needs a reason to refresh the master set. Right now it's all about the sale, and they're not being shy about it.

## What's working

- **The double-offer stack** — "25% OFF Sitewide Now" hero immediately followed by the gold "Up to 50% Off Bundles." strip means the value proposition hits you twice in under two seconds. I feel like I'm leaving money on the table if I close the tab.
- **Memorial Day timing** — "Get your bed summer-ready with the coolest sheets this Memorial Day" is a perfect hook for me. I do my annual sheet refresh in February but a Memorial Day sale on the summer-weight stuff? Genuinely tempting.
- **Best Sellers rail names the weaves** — "Luxe Sateen Core Sheet Set," "Classic Percale Sheet Set," "Washed European Linen Dreamweave Starter Bundle" — these are exactly the words I filter by. I can shop by feel from the product title alone. No one else in this space does this as cleanly.
- **150k+ 5-star reviews callout** — "Like Sleeping On A Cloud" with a real name and stars is confidence-building even for a repeat customer like me. It's a soft reminder that I'm not the only obsessive.
- **Shop by Category tiles** — I can see Linen Bedding, Sateen Bedding, and Bundle Starter tiles. One tap to the thing I actually want without navigating a mega-menu.

## What's weak

- **No returning-customer recognition** — I've probably bought from Brooklinen before. There's no loyalty hook, no "Welcome back," no reward points balance. As a repeat shopper this is a miss; Brooklinen should know I'm their best customer.
- **Free shipping threshold is invisible** — I can't see whether free shipping kicks in at $50, $100, or somewhere else. For a sheet bundle that might run $260, that's a real conversion factor and it's not surfaced.
- **No weave or fabric detail in the hero** — The hero talks about "coolest sheets" but doesn't tell me whether this is a percale summer play or sateen. I know the difference. A one-liner — "Our lightest percale yet" — would seal it for me.
- **Top notification bar text is too small/compressed** — I can see there's copy up there but I cannot read it on mobile without pinching in. If it's a shipping or promo message it's functionally invisible.
- **No urgency signal with a deadline** — "Memorial Day" implies an end date but there's no countdown, no "Ends Monday," nothing that makes me feel like I need to act today vs. tomorrow.

## Recommendations

- **Add one line of fabric intel to the hero** — "25% off sitewide — including our lightest percale" gives me the weave signal I'm looking for and costs nothing to ship.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the notification bar or directly under the hero CTA** — even a small "Free shipping on orders over $75" chip next to the button would remove a purchase friction point.
- **Add a returning-shopper hook** — even a generic "Back for more? Your picks are waiting →" with a link to bestsellers or account would acknowledge that I'm not a first-time visitor.
- **Put a "Sale ends Monday" badge on the hero** — swap or add to "Memorial Day" language so there's a real deadline. It doesn't need a countdown clock; plain text is enough for someone like me.
