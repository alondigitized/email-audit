# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen knows exactly who they're talking to right now — a sitewide summer sale with clean, airy lifestyle photography and a very direct percale callout in the Best Sellers rail. This reads like a confident, slightly minimal DTC bedding brand leaning hard into value this season. The page is tidy and purposeful, but the above-the-fold experience is almost entirely occupied by the promo stack, which means the storytelling that makes me love Brooklinen takes a back seat to discounting.

## What's working

- **The 25% OFF sitewide card** — clean, unambiguous, occupies the hero space honestly. I know exactly what I'm getting before I tap anything.
- **"Classic Percale Sheet Set" in the Best Sellers rail** — I spotted it immediately. A percale sheet snob sees her category without having to dig through nav.
- **"Like Sleeping On A Cloud" social proof block** — 150k+ 5-star reviews with a verbatim quote pulls real weight. Sarah T.'s quote is specific and believable, not marketing fluff.
- **"Up to 50% Off Bundles" secondary banner** — smart upsell. I'm the exact person who refreshes in February and buys the full bundle; that banner speaks to that habit.
- **Shop by Category visual rail** — labeled thumbnails (Bed Linens, Duvet Covers, Bundle Savings, Bath, New Arrivals, Sale) let me route to exactly my zone in one tap.

## What's weak

- **Two competing offers in one fold** — 25% sitewide vs. up to 50% bundles without clear explanation of whether they stack or which applies feels confusing. My instinct as a returning shopper is to wonder which deal is actually the best deal.
- **No returning-customer acknowledgment** — I've bought Brooklinen before. There's zero "welcome back" signal, no loyalty program callout, no rewards hook. For a brand whose whole play is repeat buyers with an annual refresh ritual, this is a miss.
- **Hero product isn't clearly labeled as percale** — the lifestyle shot is gorgeous but doesn't confirm weave type in the hero copy. As someone who buys by feel spec, I'd want to know if what I'm seeing is the Hardcore Percale or the Classic.
- **"Shopping Day" framing is vague** — is this a brand-invented sale holiday? It reads slightly awkward, like they're trying to manufacture urgency without a real calendar hook (no countdown timer, no end date).
- **No New Arrivals surface in the hero zone** — the Sale tab exists, but if I'm here for my annual February-ish refresh (okay, May this time), I'd want to see what's new in percale, not just what's discounted.

## Recommendations

- **Label the hero lifestyle image with the specific product name and weave** — e.g., "Classic Percale Sheet Set — from $127" overlaid on the bed shot so sheet-literate shoppers like me get confirmation before tapping.
- **Consolidate the two above-fold offers into one clear hierarchy** — lead with "25% off sitewide, up to 50% on bundles" in a single line so the value prop is additive rather than confusing.
- **Add a slim loyalty/rewards strip** below the hero card ("Earn points on every purchase — join Brooklinen Rewards") — this costs nothing to render and directly serves repeat buyers who are the core customer.
- **Add a deadline or countdown to the Shopping Day sale** — "Ends Sunday" or a 48-hour timer makes the urgency credible instead of ambient.
