# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen knows exactly who they're talking to, and this Memorial Day page is doing the right things: a sitewide sale front and center, a beautifully styled bed that makes me want to touch the fabric, and a secondary bundle offer stacked right beneath it. It reads like a brand confident enough to lead with the product rather than the lifestyle — which, honestly, is what I want from a sheet company.

## What's working

- **Hero bed styling** — those warm-toned striped sheets (looks like their Classic Percale in a stripe colorway) styled with a waffle weave throw say "I know what I'm selling" without needing to write "300 thread count" in the hero
- **Dual-layer offer architecture** — 25% off sitewide in the hero PLUS "Up to 50% Off Bundles" in the yellow strip right below; as someone who does an annual sheet refresh, that bundle hook is exactly where my brain goes
- **"Coolest sheets this Memorial Day"** — the copy is functional, not fluffy; I don't need you to call sheets "cloud-like dreams," I need to know they're cool for summer and on sale
- **Nav is tight and useful** — Bed, Bundle Savings, Best Sellers, Bath, New Arrivals, Sale — I can get where I need in one tap; "Bundle Savings" as a named nav item is smart for a repeat buyer like me
- **Shop by Category tiles** — circular thumbnails with "All Sheets," "New Arrivals," "Bundle Savings" visible; I can skip the sale if I want to browse by type

## What's weak

- **No loyalty or account hook** — I've bought from Brooklinen before; there's no "Hey, you have X in your Rewards" or "Member price" anywhere above the fold; that's a real miss for a returning annual buyer
- **"Dual. Free shipping on..."** in the top bar is cut off and barely legible at mobile size — if there's a free-shipping threshold I could be told, I can't read it
- **No thread count, weave, or fabric callout in the hero** — "coolest sheets" is fine copy but as someone who can name percale vs. sateen by feel, a one-line badge ("100% long-staple cotton percale") would stop me in my tracks; there's none visible
- **No person in the imagery** — the bed is beautiful but it's a prop, not a lifestyle; a single real human in soft morning light would make this feel warmer and slightly more millennial without losing the product focus
- **No urgency countdown or explicit end date** — "this Memorial Day" implies a deadline but there's no clock or "ends Monday" copy visible; it softens the urgency

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line fabric badge inside the hero** — "Classic Percale, 270 GSM, on sale now" layered over the bed image costs nothing to ship and doubles down for the sheet-literate buyer
- **Surface a returning-customer pill in the top bar** — swap "Dual. Free shipping on..." for something readable like "Free shipping $50+ · Rewards members get an extra 5%" so both new and returning buyers see a hook
- **Give the Memorial Day offer a hard end date** — "Sale ends Monday, May 26" in the hero subtext is one line; it converts without feeling manipulative
- **Test a lifestyle version of the hero** — same percale-stripe styling, same sale copy, but with a woman in her 30s sitting up in bed on a weekend morning reading or with coffee; keeps the product foreground, adds the warm human signal
