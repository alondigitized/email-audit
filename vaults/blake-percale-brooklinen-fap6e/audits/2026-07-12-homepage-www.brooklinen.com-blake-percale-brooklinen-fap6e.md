# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen is running a sitewide bundle sale with a punchy lime-green hero and very clean execution — this is a brand that knows how to look premium even in clearance mode. What it's pushing right now is bundles and the 20-40% off range, and the best-sellers rail below the fold delivers on that promise with specific product names. For me personally, the second I saw "Classic Percale Hardcore Sheet Bundle" in that rail, I basically picked up my credit card — but it shouldn't take scrolling to that tile for a sheet snob to feel seen.

## What's working

- **"Classic Percale Hardcore Sheet Bundle" in the product rail** — the word "Percale" made me stop mid-scroll; this product name is literally doing my job for me as a buyer
- **Lime-green hero background** — bold, fresh, thumb-stopping on mobile; doesn't feel cheap or clearance-rack, which is the tightrope a sale banner has to walk
- **`Shop the Sale` CTA** — above the fold, high-contrast against the background, zero ambiguity
- **"Sheets" first in the nav** — one tap to my primary category; Brooklinen knows where their core customer is going
- **"LIMITED TIME OFFER" badges on every product tile** — light-touch urgency, not screaming red countdown clocks; appropriate for the brand's tone
- **"Luxe Sateen" and "Classic Percale" named explicitly in product titles** — I know what I'm clicking on before I tap; weave-literate naming in the rail compensates for the hero's silence on the topic

## What's weak

- **Hero copy is weave-agnostic** — Brooklinen taught me what GSM means through their emails; the hero should reflect that expertise even on a sale day; "Get some faves for less" is generic department-store energy
- **No star ratings on product tiles** — I can see prices and savings amounts but not a single review count; for a $269 bundle purchase, I want to know 4,200 people loved it before I tap
- **"Surprise" undermines the brand** — calling a sitewide sale a "Surprise" when you email me every two weeks is a little hollow; Brooklinen's brand equity is built on being straight-talking experts, not hype
- **Free-shipping top bar text is unreadable at mobile scale** — I can tell there's a threshold mentioned but I cannot read it; if free shipping is the hook, it has to be legible
- **No lifestyle imagery** — it's all beautifully folded product shots; I'd like to see the sheets *on* a bed, ideally one that looks like my master bedroom, not just a stack in a studio

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line weave strip between the hero and the product rail**: "Percale sleeps cool. Sateen sleeps smooth. [Find your weave →]" — this takes a day to ship and it's the exact editorial hook I've been trained by their emails to expect
- **Layer a loyalty CTA into the hero subhead**: "Already a member? Your discount stacks. [Log in to see your price]" — converts repeat buyers from browsers to carters faster than a generic "up to 40% off"
- **Add review star counts to the product rail tiles** — even a small "★ 4.8 (1,840)" line under the product name; social proof at browse depth raises conversion on high-AOV bundles
- **Make the free-shipping threshold the second thing I read in the header**, not the third or fourth — bump it to a slightly larger font and make the dollar amount scannable at a glance; "$100+ ships free" stops the "is it worth it?" calculation cold
