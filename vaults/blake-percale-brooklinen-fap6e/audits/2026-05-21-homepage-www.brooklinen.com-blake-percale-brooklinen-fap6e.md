# Homepage snapshot · www.brooklinen.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Brooklinen knows exactly who's coming to their homepage in May and they're leaning into it hard — a Memorial Day sitewide sale with clean, aspirational bed photography and a fast path to their percale and sateen lineup. The aesthetic is calm, millennial-premium, and the product photography actually makes me want to go make my bed. It's a focused campaign push that doesn't waste my time.

## What's working

- **"25% OFF Sitewide Now" hero headline** — no ambiguity, no "up to" hedging, it just says it. I trust that immediately.
- **"Up to 50% Off Bundles" secondary banner** — the yellow/gold strip is hard to miss and bundles are exactly how I shop for guest room refreshes.
- **Classic Percale Sheet Set visible in Best Sellers** — seeing my weave in the product rail means I don't have to dig. From $81 with star ratings shown seals the deal for browsing.
- **Luxe Sateen Core Sheet Set with 35% off callout** — sale badge right on the card, crossed-out original price visible. That's the detail I need at a glance.
- **150K+ 5-Star Reviews section** — the "Like Sleeping On A Cloud" social proof block anchors trust. The number is credible and specific.
- **Shop by Category thumbnails** — fast lane to whatever I want without reading nav text. Good for a phone screen.

## What's weak

- **No weave or material callout above the fold** — the hero copy says "coolest sheets" and that's it. As someone who distinguishes percale from sateen by feel, I want to see "percale" or "sateen" in the hero. "Coolest" is vague marketing-speak.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible anywhere** — I should know my minimum before I click into a product. This is a conversion detail and it's missing from the first screen.
- **No loyalty / rewards hook** — Brooklinen has a rewards program and I am 100% their returning customer. There's no "member exclusive," no points balance tease, nothing to make me feel recognized.
- **Zero people in the imagery** — every image is a styled bed or a product stack. I'd respond to a shot of someone actually in bed with Sunday-morning energy. It's aspirational but cold.
- **"Urban things heat up. Shop this."** announcement bar copy is cryptic — I had to read it twice and still don't know what it means.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "coolest sheets" for "percale or sateen — both 25% off"** in the hero subhead. One line swap that speaks directly to fabric-literate shoppers and differentiates from every other Memorial Day sale.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip** in the announcement bar or just below the hero — "Free shipping on orders $X+" takes 12 characters and eliminates a checkout hesitation.
- **Surface a loyalty callout** — even a small "Rewards members: use your points on this sale" line turns a promo page into a retention moment.
- **Swap one Best Sellers product card thumbnail for a lifestyle shot** — a person actually wrapped in the sheets adds warmth and gives the page a human anchor.
