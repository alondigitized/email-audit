# Up to 50% off knitwear deals for every body
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent H&M Black Friday send, and the subject finally matches the body. The knitwear promise carries through three category blocks: cardigans, quarter-zips and pullovers. That is better than the 2026-11-20 "Big deals! 20% off app orders through tomorrow" email, which sold an app install and not clothes. It is about on par with the 2026-11-23 "Must-have styles up to 50% off (yes, please!)" send from earlier today.
- The hero is a black banner with red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" type over a model in a dark knit. It looks good but is generic, and it never says "knitwear". The offer, "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES", sits right below it. The "every body" angle in the subject is only loosely delivered, through a women/men/kids/baby row of buttons and a kids pair in the pullovers block.
- Then the email loses momentum. The knitwear blocks are image-plus-headline tiles with no visible product, price or per-item CTA. A big gift-card block follows, then a large block of fine print. That is the same tile format H&M has used all week. Overall it is a decent, on-season promo that is held back by a vague offer and a second pitch that dilutes the first.

## What's working

- The subject and body align: "knitwear" is backed by cardigans, quarter-zips and pullovers.
- The "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" headline is large, bold and sits directly under the hero.
- The black-and-red hero is high contrast, and the eye lands on it first.
- The category buttons (Women, Men, Baby, Kids, Find a Store) let people reach their own section quickly.
- The "buy online, pick up in-store today" bar at the top adds a useful, urgent reason to act.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50%" gives no idea which knitwear is actually discounted or by how much. Each tile shows no price, no sale tag and no savings.
- The hero says "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" but nothing about knitwear, so it fails to reinforce the subject.
- The gift-card block (red card, "SHOP E-GIFT CARDS") is a different message from the knitwear sale. It splits attention and adds length.
- The cardigan, quarter-zip and pullover tiles have no visible buttons, so it is unclear what is clickable.
- The fine print is a long, dense paragraph at the bottom. The deadline (11/25/2026) is buried there, not in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "KNITWEAR" in the hero and make the offer concrete, for example "Sweaters up to 50% off", with the end date beside it. This single change would do the most.
- 2. Show a few specific products with a was/now price in each tile, and add a clear button such as "Shop cardigans".
- 3. Move the gift-card block lower or cut it, so the email sells one thing.
- 4. Put the deadline in the body, for example "Ends 11/25", not only in the fine print.
- 5. Make the "every body" angle visible with more varied models, or drop it from the subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cardigans, quarter-zips & pullovers on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy online, pick up in store today. Family-photo-ready knits from the Black Friday sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 4pm for same-day store pickup on cardigans, quarter-zips and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML source is mostly well-formed. The probed links can't be verified, because the tracking redirects returned 403 to the QA probe. Two real defects show up in the source: images with no alt text, and an empty, stale JSON-LD block. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed with 403.** Every one is a `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h01cb6660-…&did=1427528411&rid=3326930539&erid=…` redirect. The failing links cover:
  - "Find a store" (2)
  - "shop e‑Gift cards" (4)
  - "Skip the Wait... Buy Online, Pick Up in-store…"
  - "Shop now" (2)
  - the "H&M" logo link
  - an image link
  - **Unsubscribe**
- **Likely cause:** a uniform 403 on the same redirect host, including the unsubscribe link, points to bot or WAF filtering of the probe. It doesn't confirm that the links are dead for recipients. The destinations can't be verified from this data.
- **Hidden-character link text:** two CTAs have link text of `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which contains zero-width characters (ZWNJ and BOM). That is a real source defect. It can break text-based link matching and accessibility tooling.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 76 links were probed, so the other 64 are unverified.
- **Link-level tracking:** every link uses an opaque, per-link `id=…,55165adb,<hex>` token. The same `rid` and `p2` repeat across all of them, which is consistent with per-recipient tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 20 images.** Examples:
  - `…/black-friday/men/CLOC265P01-BF26-Men-1-3x2.jpg` and the `…-2x3.jpg` variant
  - the `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs under `…/wk48/us186_s4/MSB1-US186-…_LB`
  - `…/season_05/women/cocc265p01a/FNP-CFUL271P07-2x3-gift-guide-2026-2.jp…`
- **Filename fallback:** the QA tool reports the filename (e.g. `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) as the alt text for several images. Images in the product grid are effectively unlabeled.
- **Tracking-link images:** one image, whose src is a `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` redirect, has no alt text. The QA report also flags an image with the garbage alt `KpezCX5sKk4l+pLkCeI=`, which is meaningless to a screen reader.
- **Duplicate image pairs:** every product has a 1400 and a 750 variant. The CSS shows `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` toggles, so one of each pair is hidden per viewport. A hidden duplicate with no alt can still be read by some screen readers if `display:none` isn't applied everywhere, such as clients that ignore `mso-hide` or `@media`.
- **Web fonts:** the stylesheet uses `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)`. Many clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore `@import` and fall back to system fonts. Check that the font stacks degrade gracefully.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm the doctype, language attribute, role attributes or dark-mode handling beyond what's shown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source or the QA output. The JSON-LD defects are covered in section 6, not here.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe or List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header was found.** The QA tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify it on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results header wasn't captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com` and the tracking domain is `t19.email.hm.com`, which look aligned on the same organizational domain. That is circumstantial and doesn't confirm authentication.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** an unsubscribe link exists (`…f8da4944…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`), but it returned 403 in the probe. Because it's a tokenized redirect, it can't be verified without a real click.
- **Postal address and physical footer:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, and the redirect hides the destination URLs. Because of the 403s, I can't determine the final landing pages or whether campaign parameters are appended after the redirect.
- **Malformed JSON-LD block (`application/ld+json`) in `<head>`:**
  - `"subjectLine":""` is empty, though the real subject is "Up to 50% off knitwear deals for every body".
  - `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""` and `"availabilityEnds":""` are empty.
  - `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` is a stale 2018 date left over from a template.
  - The PromotionCard has `"image":""` and `"url":""`.
  - Gmail's promotion annotations can't use this markup. Invalid markup of this kind can also be rejected by Google's schema validator.
- **Plain-text part:** 516 of 594 characters are URLs (over 70%). That is a spam-filter risk, and the text version has almost no readable content.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the links** with a real-browser user agent, or whitelist the QA probe. Confirm that `t19.email.hm.com/r/` resolves and that the unsubscribe URL works. Check the final destinations for UTM parameters.
2. **Check the raw message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. The relay may be hiding them.
3. **Add alt text** to all product and hero images. Use `alt=""` for decorative duplicates, and replace the `KpezCX5sKk4l+pLkCeI=` value and the filename-style alt values.
4. **Fix the JSON-LD block:** populate it with the real subject, offer and URL, or remove it. Remove the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date.
5. **Remove the zero-width characters** (ZWNJ and BOM) from the "Shop now" link text.
6. **Improve the plain-text part**, adding readable copy around the links, and give the web-font stacks safe system fallbacks.
