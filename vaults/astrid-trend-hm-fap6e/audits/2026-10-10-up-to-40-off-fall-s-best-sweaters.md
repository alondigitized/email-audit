# Up to 40% off fall's best sweaters
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the first send this week where the subject, hero and products all point at one thing. "Up to 40% off fall's best sweaters" is backed by a model in a cream knit vest, a bold "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline, and six knitwear tiles with sale prices. Compared with the 2026-10-09 "The new utility" send, which only carried the discount in a banner, this one puts the offer in the body. It is also a more focused follow-up to the 2026-10-08 "The Fall Event: Up to 40% off sitewide" send.
- The execution is clean, but the offer is soft. "Up to 40%" is hedged, and the sitewide claim sits above an asterisk with a long fine-print block covering exclusions. The visible prices also don't look like 40% off. The turtleneck at $19.99 is marked down from $24.99, which is about 20%. The cardigan at $31.99 is down from $39.99, also about 20%. The headline promises more than the products deliver. This is the third "Fall Event" send in four days, so it needs a reason to feel new.
- Verdict: a competent, on-theme promo that is better targeted than most of this brand's recent sends. The mismatch between the headline and the shown discounts keeps it mid-pack.

## What's working

- The subject, hero copy and product grid all say "sweaters" and "40% off", so the message is consistent from inbox to click.
- The hero is a single model in a cream knit vest under a bold, centered "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline. The eye lands on the offer first.
- Six knitwear tiles show struck-through original prices with red sale prices, so savings are visible at a glance.
- The two-button pair, "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE", is clear, and the primary button is easy to spot.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the footer includes the usual unsubscribe and "view in browser" links.

## What's weak

- The sale prices don't support "up to 40%". The visible markdowns look closer to 20%, and no tile shows 40%.
- The tiles are not individually labeled with a discount percentage, and it isn't clear whether each links to its own product page.
- The hero "SHOP NOW" is generic. The copy "luxe textures, relaxed cuts, refined details" is vague about what the sweaters are.
- The hedged "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE*" is followed by a dense legal block, and the exclusions list is long. Together they undercut the "sitewide" claim.
- The "Shop by" category block and the large footer add scroll after the products. There is no member benefit or urgency beyond a date buried in the fine print ("valid through 10/13/2026").

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the headline match the prices. Either feature products that are actually 40% off, or lead with the real range, such as "Sweaters from $19.99", and keep "up to 40%" secondary.
- 2. Put the deadline in the body, for example "Ends Monday 10/13". The fine print has the date, but the hero doesn't, and it is the one real urgency hook.
- 3. Add a "% off" tag to each tile and a specific CTA, such as "Shop sweaters" or "Shop knitwear", in place of the generic "SHOP NOW".
- 4. Add a member line, such as "Members: extra bonus reward", since the footer mentions the member reward but the body doesn't.
- 5. Trim the "Shop by" block and shorten the fine print on mobile.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Likely cause:** the ESP redirector is rejecting the automated probe, not breaking for real recipients. The same host returns 403 on the unsubscribe link, which must work for real users. Treat this as unconfirmed. The final destinations were never reached, so landing-page health is unknown.
- **Duplicate link slots.** "Shop now" and "Find a store" each appear twice, with ids `…f869e639`/`…f869e63b` and `…f869e63a`/`…f869e63c`. These are probably separate desktop and mobile variants.
- **Hidden characters in link text.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f869e5e3`, `…f869e5e5`) have link text with zero-width characters (`‌ ﻿`). That is a minor problem for text-based click analytics and screen readers.
- **Probe coverage was thin.** Only 12 links were probed and 55 skipped (footer, social and utility links). Those 55 are untested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 16 images.** Every pair is the same asset served at two sizes (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) for desktop and mobile. - **Filename as link text.** Image links are announced by filename (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`) because there is no `alt`. With images blocked, the product links have no readable label.
- **Duplicated content for screen readers.** The desktop and mobile variants rely on `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` (`display:none!important` inside `@media (max-width:699px)`). Where the media query isn't honored, both copies render and are read.
