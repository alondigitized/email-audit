# Your fall aesthetic is up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in four days to pitch "The Fall Event: up to 40% off sitewide." It follows the 10/8 "The Fall Event" email and the 10/10 sweater send. The hero is strong: a model in a dark brown corduroy jacket, with a clear "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline and two buttons directly under it. Everything above the fold works.
- Below the hero it gets generic. There are five category tiles with poetic labels ("Elevated Layers," "Subtle Details") and no product names, prices or reviews. A "Shop By" grid follows that repeats the same categories. The fine print is long and carries the real terms. The offer is online-only and ends 10/13, a deadline that appears only in that fine print.
- My judgement: it's competent and on-brand, but it recycles the same offer the subscriber has already seen twice this week. The subject line is the only new element, and it is the weakest part. It's a nice-looking reminder, not a reason to click.

## What's working

- The hero reads in one second: a moody brown-on-white model shot, then a bold "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE."
- Two clear buttons, "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE," sit right under the offer and serve both online and in-store shoppers.
- The subject's "fall aesthetic" matches the hero's brown and olive palette.
- The alternating zig-zag tiles are easy to scan and cover women, men, kids, sport and home.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 10/13 end date appears only in the fine print. The body copy has no visible urgency, so the one thing that could drive a click is buried.
- Third "Fall Event" send this week (10/8, 10/10, now 10/11). The offer is not new.
- Tile labels like "Subtle Details" and "Finishing Touches" don't say what's being sold. There are no prices, no "from $X" and no star ratings.
- The "Shop By" grid at the bottom repeats the tiles above it. It adds length, not value.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text. It says the offer excludes sale items, furniture and lamps, and designer collaborations. This undercuts "sitewide" and feels less honest. The asterisk is easy to miss.
- No loyalty or member perk is shown, even though the footer mentions a $5 Member Bonus Reward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 10/13" next to "Up to 40% off." This is the single biggest lift, because the urgency is real and currently hidden.
- 2. Make the tiles specific. Use "Women's jackets from $X" instead of "Elevated Layers," and add a price or discount badge on each.
- 3. Cut the "Shop By" block. It duplicates the tiles above it.
- 4. Add one hero product link, such as the corduroy jacket, with its price, instead of a generic "Shop Now."
- 5. Shorten the fine print and put the key exclusions (sale items, online only) near the offer.
- 6. Surface the member perk: "Members: extra $5 reward."
- 7. Vary the creative from the 10/8 and 10/10 sends so the Fall Event doesn't feel repeated.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only through 10/13. Jackets, knits and home finds.`

A uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe, points to the scanner being blocked by the redirector, not 12 dead links. Treat it as unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **The unsubscribe link also returned 403** (`…,54901d7d,f86b4b7f&…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`). If this is bot-blocking, it's benign. A real-client test is still needed because this is the highest-risk link.

- **Duplicate "Shop now" links with separate tracking IDs.** Example suffixes: `f86b4b4b`, `f86b4b4c`, `f86b4b4d`, `f86b4876`, `f86b4878`. This is normal per-slot tracking, but it makes click attribution granular.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on essentially every image.** Examples:
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` for each of 7 product assets, e.g. `…/wk-41/us154_s4/1328843003_202603_LB_0804.jpg/`.
  - The tracking-pixel-like image at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,54901d7d,1`.
- **Image-only links announce filenames.** Because the alt text is missing, the linked images ("[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]") present the filename to screen readers. When images are blocked, the link has no visible text.
- **Two image variants per product (1400px and 750px).** This is presumably a desktop/mobile swap using `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. Both variants being unlabeled doubles the a11y noise. Both are also likely to be read out unless `display:none` is applied reliably. `.is-hidden-desktop` uses `line-height:auto`, which is invalid CSS and will be ignored.
- **Webfont `@import` in `<style>`** (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`). Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore these, so fallback fonts will be used. This is a minor rendering inconsistency.
- **JSON-LD block is largely empty.** In the `<script type="application/ld+json">`, `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `description`, `image` and `url` are `""`. `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, a stale template default. Gmail may ignore this or treat it as malformed markup, and it gives no promo-card benefit.
- **Viewport and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`:** present, no issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links** with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow redirects from a residential IP, to separate bot-blocking 403s from real failures. Prioritize "Shop now" and "Unsubscribe", and capture the final destination URLs and any `utm_*` parameters.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text to every `<img>`.** Use `alt=""` for decorative images and descriptive text for linked product images. Make sure the 1400px/750px variants don't both get announced.
3. **Check raw headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass from a non-relay test seed (e.g. Gmail "Show original").
4. **Fix or remove the JSON-LD block.** Populate `subjectLine` and `url`, or drop it. The 2018 `availabilityStarts` default should go.
5. **Fix the invalid `line-height:auto`** in `.is-hidden-desktop`, using `normal` or `inherit`.
6. **Improve the plain-text part.** It is 514 of 592 characters of URLs (87%). Add readable copy around the links to reduce spam-filter risk.
7. **Adjust the QA probe.** The 12-link sample is a small part of the 64 links. Where links are repeated, probe one link per unique destination so the sample covers more of the email.
