# Up to 30% off your back-to-school checklist
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's back-to-school category reset — clean, on-message, and free of the "wait, is this bedding or blazers?" confusion that plagued 7/27's "Off-duty dressing" send. The top banner leads with "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING*," which is a smart, timely hook for an August back-to-school email, then drops straight into a bold "UP TO 30% OFF IN STORES & ONLINE" hero with a tidy six-box category grid (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Find a Store).
- The problem is this email is functionally identical in structure to nearly every H&M send in the brand history log — big headline, category grid, lifestyle photo pairs, "Shop By" carousel, app/social footer. There's no hero product, no specific item called out, and the CTA is the same generic "SHOP NOW" this brand always uses. It reads as a competent template refresh rather than a back-to-school-specific push (no backpacks, no "first day of school" styling, no kid-focused hero above the fold).
- Given the brand has sent roughly ten promotional emails in the last two weeks per the history log, this one earns a "fine, not fatiguing" grade — solid execution, weak differentiation, buried fine print on exclusions.

## What's working

- The tax-free-days banner at the very top is genuinely useful, timely content that's specific to the back-to-school moment — not generic filler.
- "UP TO 30% OFF IN STORES & ONLINE" is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss as the hero message.
- The six-box category grid (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Find a Store) gives an unambiguous, low-friction way to jump straight to a relevant department.
- The imagery is clean and render-bug-free — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Layout repeats the category picks (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) in a second "Shop By" section, reinforcing navigation without feeling redundant.

## What's weak

- No hero product or specific back-to-school item (backpack, lunch box, first-day outfit) — despite the subject promising a "checklist," nothing here reads as a checklist.
- The primary CTA is the same generic "SHOP NOW" H&M uses across nearly every send — no category-specific or offer-specific CTA copy.
- "Up to 30%" is a ceiling claim with heavy exclusions (sale items, H&M Studio, designer collabs, no stacking) buried in dense fine print at the very bottom — a classic bait-toward-best-case pattern.
- No visible urgency or deadline anywhere in the main body — the only expiration date (08/03/2026) is in the small legal text block.
- Layout is nearly identical to prior H&M sends in this history (grid → image pairs → shop-by carousel), which risks blur-together fatigue given the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual back-to-school hero product (backpack, first-day outfit, notebook-and-tee bundle) above the fold instead of a pure text/grid layout — the subject promises a "checklist" and the body delivers a generic sale banner.
- 2. Swap "SHOP NOW" for a category- or occasion-specific CTA, e.g. "Shop the Checklist" or "Start the List."
- 3. Surface a real deadline near the hero ("Ends 8/3") instead of only in the footer legal text — creates credible urgency without needing new copy.
- 4. Move the tax-free-days content into its own short section with a date range instead of a vague "coming" teaser — parents will want to know exactly when to shop tax-free.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your back-to-school list, up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tax-free days + 30% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, basics & everything in between — before the bell rings`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tax-free savings meet 30% off in stores and online`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on H&M's standard ESP template (t19.email.hm.com tracking domain) but ships with a fully broken click-tracking layer — all 12 probed links, including the unsubscribe link, return HTTP 403 — alongside missing authentication headers and several unpopulated structured-data fields.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed material links return 403**, including primary CTAs ("Shop now" x2), nav links (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home), "Find a store" (x2), "TAX-FREE DAYS" link, and the "H&M" logo link. Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h8374e412-f806-43c7-a4b3-75880b430906,6636fd27,f796ee28&did=1714879783&rid=3326930539&erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D&p1=DM5898934&p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c&p3=20260730` (link text: "Shop now"). This indicates either the tracking redirector (`t19.email.hm.com`) is down/misconfigured for this send, or requests are being blocked (e.g., bot/UA filtering) — either way, every destination in the email is currently unreachable via the tracked link.
- Two nearly-identical "Shop now" links (`f796ee28`, `f796ee2a`) point to different redirect IDs but both carry stray zero-width/BOM characters in the link text (`‌  ﻿Shop now`), which can cause rendering/accessibility artifacts in some clients and screen readers.
- No raw (non-tracked) destination URLs are visible in the truncated source, so the underlying landing pages cannot be independently verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 images missing `alt` text**, including all product images (`FNP_NA1534_s4_*` at both 1400px and 750px breakpoints) and both tracking pixel/redirect images. This fails WCAG for non-visual and image-blocked rendering.
- Mobile-responsive CSS is present and structured normally (`@media (max-width:699px)` block with `.is-hidden-mobile`, `.image-mobile`, etc.) — no structural issues found in the truncated source.
- Outlook/Yahoo-specific conditional rules present (`#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`) — no issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `application/ld+json` schema block ships with several **empty/unpopulated fields**: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"image":""`, `"url":""`. These are used by Gmail/Outlook for promotional annotations and action markup — as shipped, they will render no promo card data.
- `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` — this date is stale (2018) relative to the send context (campaign dated 2026-07-30 per tracking param `p3=20260730`), suggesting a merge-token/templating fallback value was never overwritten for this send.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Unsubscribe link itself returns 403** (`...f796ee82...&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`) — functionally, recipients cannot unsubscribe via this link as currently deployed, a CAN-SPAM risk.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not present — no evidence of RFC 2369 one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (though this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than absence in the original send).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not present — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on any link; H&M uses proprietary tracking params instead (`id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`, e.g. `p1=DM5898934`, `p3=20260730`). This is consistent internally across all links but means landing-page attribution depends entirely on H&M's own redirector resolving correctly — which it currently does not (see §2), so continuity to the destination site cannot be verified at all.
- Cannot assess landing-page alignment (e.g., does "Find a store" land on a store locator, does nav land on the right category) since every tracked link 403s before reaching a destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority 1:** Investigate why the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector is returning 403 on 100% of probed links, especially unsubscribe — this is both a compliance and revenue-blocking issue.
2. Populate the empty `ld+json` fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) or remove the schema block if promo-card markup isn't intended for this send.
3. Fix the stale `availabilityStarts` templated date (2018) before next send.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 9 flagged images, particularly product images.
5. Strip stray zero-width/BOM characters from the two "Shop now" link text nodes.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in the raw send (vs. lost in relay capture) to support one-click unsubscribe compliance.
