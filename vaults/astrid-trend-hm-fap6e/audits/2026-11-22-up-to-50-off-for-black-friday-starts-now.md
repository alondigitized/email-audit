# Up to 50% off for Black Friday starts now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's cleanest Black Friday send of the week, and the first one that sells shopping instead of the app. A black-and-white model sits under red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" type. "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" follows in bold, with SHOP NOW and FIND A STORE buttons. Compared with the 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 20%-off app-install pushes, this one finally leads with the real offer.
- The rest of the email is a generic category parade: women's layering, men's jackets, baby and kids' outerwear, then a holiday-shop block. Each block has a lovely photo and a vague headline ("The Layer Lineup", "Cold-Busters") and no price or product detail. The hero says "up to 50%" but nothing below shows what is 50% off. The red-on-black member banner ("Become a member for exclusive Black Friday access + 15% off your first purchase") is tiny and easy to miss. The fine print at the bottom is a very long block of dense gray text.
- Verdict: a solid, on-brand send that is better than the recent app-install sends, but it is still a category menu. It has no hero product and no proof that the 50% is real.

## What's working

- The offer is in the subject and the hero: "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES".
- The red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" type on a monochrome photo is a strong hero.
- The layout is clean and uniform: image, headline, one or two outlined buttons.
- Outerwear is seasonally right for late November.
- The Women / Men / Baby / Kids split makes it easy to find your own category.
- The "Shop by" tile row near the bottom works as a quick navigation recap.

## What's weak

- No product, price or sale-price callout appears anywhere. "Up to 50%" never gets a concrete example.
- The member banner with the 15%-off first purchase is tiny, red on black, and buried at the top.
- The category headlines ("Cold-Busters", "Jackets of All Trades") are clever but give no information.
- The buttons are thin and outlined, with small text. SHOP NOW carries no urgency or deadline.
- The long legal block at the bottom is a wall of tiny gray text, and the offer caveats are hard to read.
- The email is very long. Four category blocks repeat the same pattern before the holiday section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "best deals" product row near the top with 3-4 items showing was/now prices, such as "Wool-blend coat $119 → $59.99". This is the biggest lift because it makes the 50% believable.
- 2. Add a deadline to the hero, such as "Through Monday 11/25", and use a solid filled CTA like "Shop up to 50% off".
- 3. Make the member perk a full-width strip below the hero with a real button, "Join free, get 15% off".
- 4. Replace the vague category headlines with offers, such as "Women's outerwear: up to 50% off".
- 5. Cut the baby/kids and holiday-shop blocks, or merge them into the shop-by tiles to shorten the email.
- 6. Condense the legal copy and link out to full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off coats & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off outerwear, starting now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Coats, knits and gifts for everyone, online and in stores through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get an extra 15% off their first order. Join free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (responsive CSS, viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`). However, all 12 probed tracking links returned 403 to the automated probe, 14 images lack usable alt text, and the JSON-LD block ships with empty template values. Header-level checks (SPF/DKIM/List-Unsubscribe) could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on all probed links (12/12):** Every probed `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0e714891-…` redirect returned 403. This covers "Shop now", "Find a store", the "H&M" logo link and "Unsubscribe". Because every link fails identically, including the unsubscribe link, this is most likely bot or WAF blocking of the probe against the tracking redirector, not a broken campaign. It is **unverified from this data**, so confirm with a real-client click test. Only 12 of 72 links were probed.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8d7f12f`, `…f8d7f131`) have link text beginning with invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`, i.e. ZWNJ and BOM-type characters). These can affect screen-reader output and link-text matching.
- **Fully opaque tracking:** Every link goes through `t19.email.hm.com/r/`, and the destination is not visible in the HTML.
- **Empty link text in QA output:** The probe reports blank link text for the failed URLs. This is probably image-only links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 14 images are flagged, including:
  - the tracked image links at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0e714891…`
  - `…/black-friday/women/CLOC265P01-BF26-Women-2-3x2.jpg`
  - `…/CLOC265P01-BF26-Women-2-2x3.jpg`
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs under `…/wk48/us186_s4/…`

  Where the audit reports an identifier, it is an AEM filename (`cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`), not descriptive text. Most images are desktop (1400px) and mobile (750px) pairs. If both variants are in the DOM, as `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` suggest, screen readers may hit duplicates unless hidden-variant images use `aria-hidden` or `display:none`.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` is invalid, because `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value. `@media screen yahoo { … }` is a malformed media query, with no `and` and no valid feature.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` and `@import url(…/oces.css)` are ignored by Gmail and some other clients. Fallback font stacks should be verified. They are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** The source is truncated, so I could not check for a preheader, table roles, `lang` or `dir` on `<html>`, or `role="presentation"`. The `<html>` tag shown has no `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the supplied source or QA data.
- Recipient identifiers appear in every URL: `rid=3326930539` and `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D`, which is base64 of the same ID. The unsubscribe link also adds `p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. This is typical ESP behavior, but it means forwarded links expose the subscriber ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. The audit notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements make this worth verifying against the raw message from a non-relay inbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link:** Present in the body (`…f8d7f18d…&p5=9be06746…`) but returned 403 to the probe. This is probably the same bot blocking as above, but it should be tested manually.
- **Physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't assess it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** Every link is an opaque redirect (`p1=DM6232630`, `p2=fb32f588…`, `p3=20261122`), and the 403 responses prevented following it to the landing page.
- **Landing page alignment cannot be assessed** for the same reason.
- **JSON-LD defaults:** The block contains empty or stale template values:
  - `"subjectLine":""`
  - `"discountCode":""`
  - `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, which is a stale default
  - `"availabilityEnds":""`
  - `"image":""`
  - `"url":""` in the `PromotionCard`

  Gmail may ignore or mishandle this markup. The email has the subject "Up to 50% off for Black Friday starts now!", but none of it is reflected in the schema.
- **Plain-text part:** 514 of 592 characters (about 87%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% warning threshold. It is likely long tracking URLs with little readable text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the tracking links and the unsubscribe link with a browser-like User-Agent, or by manually clicking them. This tells you whether the 403s are bot blocking or real failures.
2. Check the raw message headers from a direct (non-relay) inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and use `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden` on decorative or duplicate desktop/mobile variants.
4. Strip the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" link text.
5. Populate the JSON-LD `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends`, `image` and `url` fields, or remove the block.
6. Fix `line-height:auto` and the `@media screen yahoo` query.
7. Shorten the plain-text version, or add readable copy around the URLs.
8. Capture the destination URLs of the redirects, with a proper client, to verify UTM and landing-page continuity.
