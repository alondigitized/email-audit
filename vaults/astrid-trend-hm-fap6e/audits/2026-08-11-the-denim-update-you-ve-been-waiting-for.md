# The denim update you've been waiting for
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's first pure denim category drop after two weeks of tiered-discount arcs and full-price editorial detours — "FRESH FITS" leads straight into denim across women's, men's, and kids without ever naming a price. It's a clean, well-organized category email: four clear sections (Women/Guess Who's Back, Men/Right on Trend, Kids/Built for Play, Back to Campus), each with its own product shot and CTA button, plus a "FREE SHIPPING OVER $50 FOR H&M MEMBERS" banner up top that's the only concrete offer in the whole send.
- The execution is tidy — no render bugs, consistent typography, decent product photography — but it's also the least differentiated of the recent sends. No % off, no urgency, no specific product names or fits called out, just generic section headers ("Guess Who's Back," "Right on Trend") stacked on repeat. It reads like a lookbook wearing a denim theme rather than an actual denim launch with any hook.
- Compared to 8/9's "Last day: 15%/20% off" close-out or 8/8's back-to-school discount push, this is a step back toward the vague editorial mode that scored lower (8/10's WARDROBE.NYC hit 8/10 because it was a clear designer capsule; 8/10's "Modern Romance" hit 5/10 for exactly this reason — no offer, no hook). Denim is evergreen enough that "the update you've been waiting for" needs to earn that claim with a specific product or fit; it doesn't.

## What's working

- Free shipping over $50 for members is stated clearly at the very top, above the fold.
- Clean segmentation by shopper (Women, Men, Kids 2-8Y, Kids 9-14Y) with dedicated CTA buttons for each.
- Consistent visual rhythm — hero shot, then a two-up product grid, then a labeled CTA button — repeats predictably across all four sections, easy to scan.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.
- "Back to Campus" section adds a seasonal tie-in with actual body copy, unlike the other three sections which are headline + button only.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or specific offer tied to denim anywhere — the subject promises "the update you've been waiting for" and delivers zero specifics.
- Section headers ("Guess Who's Back," "Right on Trend," "Built for Play") are generic filler that could sit on any category email, not just denim.
- No product names, washes, fits, or prices called out — nothing to click through *for* beyond "see more denim."
- Very long, repetitive scroll (four near-identical hero+grid+button blocks) before reaching any differentiated content.
- "Guess Who's Back" is a vague teaser hook with no payoff — it never says what's back or why it matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Anchor the send to one specific denim story (e.g., "wide-leg denim is back" or a named fit/wash) instead of four generic section headers — give the reader a reason "the update" is worth opening.
- 2. Add a price anchor or member perk specific to denim (e.g., "Jeans from $19.99" or "Extra 15% off denim for members") — right now the only offer is the generic $50 free-shipping banner, which isn't category-relevant.
- 3. Cut the send from four sections to two — Women/Men and Kids — to shorten the scroll and put the strongest hero higher.
- 4. Replace vague headers like "Guess Who's Back" and "Right on Trend" with concrete fit/product names.
- 5. Move "Back to Campus" (the only section with real body copy and a seasonal hook) up near the top — it's currently buried last and undersells the back-to-school tie-in.

All links share the same `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector host, `did`/`rid`/`erid` tracking params, and `p1`–`p5` campaign params — the 403 is consistent across all of them, pointing to a redirector-side block (bot/UA filtering, expired click-tracking token, or IP/geo restriction on the probe) rather than per-link misconfiguration. Recommend re-verifying from a real mail client/browser before treating as a true broken-link defect, but as measured, no link in this email currently resolves.
