# Last day: Up to 40% off for Members
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Member-gated "up to 40% off" send in about a week, and it is the weakest of them creatively. The subject, "Last day: Up to 40% off for Members," is the best thing about it, because the urgency is real. The body doesn't use it. The only deadline is "through 11/16/2026" in the fine print at the bottom. Nothing in the hero or headline says "ends tonight."
- The hero is a woman in maroon loungewear, and the copy ("cozy loungewear, ultra-soft pajamas and luxe sweaters") fits it. The offer headline is clear and has good weight. But the top banner sells membership ("BECOME A MEMBER… + 10% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE"), and the nav, category buttons and Sign In/Sign Up buttons sit between the hero and the products. A lapsed or non-member reader gets mixed signals about whether the deal is theirs.
- Below that, the product tiles are solid and show Member prices in red against regular prices. Then a "Gifts to Unwrap" block with a scarf, a beanie and slippers dilutes the loungewear story. Overall it is a competent, familiar H&M template with a strong subject line, but the last-day deadline was wasted.

## What's working

- The subject line carries a real deadline ("Last day"), and the 11/16 send date matches the 11/16 expiry in the fine print.
- "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is large, bold and sits right under the hero.
- The hero image matches the copy: maroon loungewear next to "cozy loungewear, ultra-soft pajamas."
- Product tiles show the price clearly: regular price in black, Member price in red (e.g. Sweatpants $22.99 → $17.99).
- The Women/Men/Kids buttons give an easy category route in.

## What's weak

- The deadline appears nowhere in the visible body. There is no "ends tonight" banner, countdown or CTA copy, only a line in the legal text.
- The top banner pitches joining ("BECOME A MEMBER… + 10% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE") in a "last day for Members" email. That muddles the audience, and the 10% offer competes with the 40% one.
- The category buttons and Sign In/Sign Up buttons crowd the area between hero and products. There is no single "Shop Member Prices" CTA, and the Sign In/Sign Up pair adds friction for existing Members.
- The "Gifts to Unwrap" scarf, beanie and slippers section drifts from the loungewear story. The gifting angle was already used on 11/10 and 11/14.
- The fine print is dense and long, with exclusions, return limits and a "while supplies last" caveat. That undercuts how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Add a "Ends tonight, 11/16" banner or a small countdown above or beside "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS." This is the highest-impact change, because the email's whole premise is urgency.
- 2. Replace the join-membership banner with a Member-focused one in this send. Keep the 10% new-member pitch for non-member segments.
- 3. Add one primary button, "Shop Member Prices," in place of the stacked category and Sign In/Sign Up buttons. Move the category links below the products.
- 4. Cut "Gifts to Unwrap," or move it under a lighter heading. Keep the email on loungewear and knits for a clean last-day push.
- 5. Shorten the legal copy to a one-line summary with a "Full terms" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 40% off for Members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: loungewear from $11.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member prices on sweats, sets and knits end at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweatpants $17.99, loungewear sets $38.99. Today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly intact, but all 12 probed tracked links returned HTTP 403 and the `<img>` tags have no alt text. The JSON-LD schema block is empty, and the compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed from the data captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** These are every `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hc67a4945-...` redirect probed: Shop now ×2, logo "H&M", two image links, Women, Men, Kids, Sign in, Sign up, a second Women, and Unsubscribe (`...,5514d247,f8c7b99e&...`). The pass rate was 16%.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking.** The common pattern is that the ESP redirector rejects the QA probe's user agent or IP, so this likely does not mean the links are dead for real recipients. I can't confirm either way from the data. The destination URLs were never resolved, so the failures are unverified rather than confirmed breakage.
- **Coverage is thin.** Only 12 links were probed and 65 were skipped, so most of the email's links have no QA data.
- **Wrapped links.** Every link is a `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirect with per-recipient parameters (`rid`, `erid`, `p2=079e07e2...`, `p3=20261116`). The final destinations and any UTM parameters are not visible in the data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 18 QA warnings.** Fallback text shows as the filename, e.g. `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` and `[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]`. Affected sources are `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-46/us174_s4/...` (`1295969008_202604_LB_0708.jpg`, `1193602089_202604_LB_0158.jpg`, `1357440002_202604_LB_3223.jpg`, and others). Two tracked-link images (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`) also lack alt.
- **Linked images with no alt text.** Several of these images sit inside `<a>` tags (`...,5514d247,f8c7b95b` and `...,f8c7b95c`). Those links therefore have no accessible name, so screen readers and images-off clients get the filename or nothing.

This is consistent with the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` show/hide pattern in the CSS, but both versions lack alt text. The pattern relies on `display:none!important` and `mso-hide`, so check that both images are not downloaded or announced.

Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip these, so fallback fonts will be used. This is minor and depends on the font stack, which the truncated source doesn't show.
