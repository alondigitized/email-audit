# Take a piece of NYC with you
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Take a piece of NYC with you," but nothing in the render delivers on it. The hero is a runner in H&M MOVE gear, with a marathon-season line ("Marathon season may be over, but the finish line was just the beginning"). That is the only NYC link, and it's implied, not stated. The top banner pushes the gift card, the hero sells running gear, and the middle block sells holiday pajamas. A reader gets three different pitches and no offer.
- There is no price, percentage, deadline or member benefit anywhere. It's a pure brand-awareness catalog send. The imagery is polished and the layout is clean, but the hierarchy is split. The gift card line is small and sits above the logo, so it reads like a header.
- Against recent sends, it recycles the "Holiday pajamas" block from 10/31, "Gifts to unwrap: Holiday pajamas". It also repeats the MOVE theme from 11/2's ski collection send. This is a fifth or sixth near-identical editorial send this week, and it gives less than 10/28's tiered "up to 25% off + free shipping." Verdict: attractive but unfocused, and the subject misleads.

## What's working

- The hero runner photo is strong, full-width and dynamic, with a clear headline: "H&M MOVES WITH YOU."
- The MOVE copy is on-brand and seasonal for post-marathon motivation.
- The paired buttons, "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE," are clear and readable.
- The 2x3 pajama collage is warm and giftable, and the Women/Men/Kids split makes it easy to navigate.
- The "Shop by" tiles and the footer are tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's NYC promise isn't paid off in the body, and there's no NYC-specific product or copy.
- There is no offer at all: no % off, no free shipping, no member pricing, no deadline.
- Two competing themes (MOVE and pajamas) plus a gift card banner dilute the message.
- The pajama collage has no product, price or detail, only abstract crops. The buttons go to categories, not a product.
- The pajama block repeats the 10/31 send, and there is a big white gap before it.
- The gift card banner at the very top is tiny and is not tied to a CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story and match the subject to it. If it's the NYC marathon, build the hero and copy around that, and put a real offer in the headline.
- 2. Add one concrete incentive, such as a % off MOVE or free shipping, with a deadline.
- 3. Move the pajamas to a different send, or give them their own subject, so they don't repeat 10/31.
- 4. Link the hero to one specific MOVE running product, with a price, instead of a generic "Shop now."
- 5. Remove or enlarge the gift card line, and give it a button if it stays.
