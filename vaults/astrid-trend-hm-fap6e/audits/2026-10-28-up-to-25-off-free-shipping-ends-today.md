# Up to 25% off + free shipping ends today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's last-call version of the tiered threshold offer it ran on 10/16–10/18 and again yesterday in "Stripe right: up to 25% off tops, knits & more." The stacked "15% OFF $75 / 20% OFF $100 / 25% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" headline is bold and readable at a glance. The puffer-coat hero fits late-October weather. The urgency lives only in the subject line, though. Nothing in the body says "ends today," so the email loses the deadline once it's opened.
- The body is a clean three-tile puffer-coat grid (Women / Men / Kids) followed by a "Shop By" row. It's tidy and on-season, but it shows no prices, no product names and no reviews. The one CTA is a thin outlined "SHOP NOW" button that doesn't stand out against the heavy headline above it. The fine print is long, with a spend-threshold offer and several exclusions, and it's the only place the deadline (10/28/2026) is stated.
- Overall this is a competent, repetitive promo. I'd call it a solid 6. It does its job as a reminder, but it adds nothing new to yesterday's send, and the missing countdown wastes the strongest hook it has.

## What's working

- The stacked tier headline makes the offer clear in about a second, and "+ FREE SHIPPING" is set in the same weight.
- The hero is a single puffer-coat model against a neutral backdrop. It matches the season and the copy ("Cold-weather dressing starts here").
- The Women / Men / Kids tiles split the audience cleanly, and the staggered image/text layout gives the page rhythm.
- The black-on-white layout is consistent with H&M's brand voice and looks free of render bugs. I can see no broken images or overlapping text.
- The "Shop By" row extends the email to Home and Beauty for readers who don't want a coat.

## What's weak

- The deadline appears only in the subject line and the fine print. The body has no "ends today" banner or timer.
- The CTA is a small, thin-outlined "SHOP NOW" button, and it reads as secondary to the headline.
- The product tiles show no prices, product names or sale callouts, so the discount isn't tied to any specific item.
- The offer is a spend threshold, not a flat percentage, and the headline doesn't show that a bigger basket is needed. The terms are in a long block at the bottom, which weakens the "honest offer" signal.
- It repeats the 10/27 send's offer almost exactly, with no new hook beyond the category swap to puffer coats.
- There is no loyalty or member messaging, even though the fine print references member rewards.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Add an "ENDS TONIGHT" line above the offer stack, or a countdown graphic, so the subject's urgency carries into the body.
- 2. Make the CTA a solid black filled button with a specific label like "Shop puffer coats." Add a "Spend $75+" line under the tiers so the threshold is visible without scrolling to the fine print.
- 3. Replace the lifestyle tiles with three or four priced puffer products showing sale prices, plus a rating or "selling fast" cue.
- 4. Put the main terms in one line under the CTA ("Online only. Excludes sale items.") and leave the full legal text in the footer.
- 5. Add a member line such as "Members: $5 bonus reward applies" if the program supports it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Puffer coats, up to 25% off. Ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75+ and save. Cold-weather coats for everyone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Online only through midnight. Free shipping included.`

- **Final destinations can't be verified.** Every URL is an opaque redirect, so the QA data doesn't show where any link lands.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
