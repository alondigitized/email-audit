# The new utility
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- "The new utility" is a quiet editorial send. It has a good-looking hero but no offer of its own. The only discount is the red banner at the top, "THE FALL EVENT: UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE + DEALS IN-STORE*". That is the same Fall Event H&M pushed on 10/08, now demoted to a strip above the logo. The email's real subject is the utility trend, and the subject line says so without saying anything concrete.
- The visuals are clean. An olive cargo-style trouser and bomber hero leads into an "IN FOCUS" block, then a rib-knit sweater and a wrap cardigan. The prices ($39.99, $31.99, $43.99, each with a small struck-through original) are the only price signals, and they are tiny. The hero is labeled "BARREL REGULAR WAIST JEANS" but shows cargo-style trousers and an outerwear layer. The "IN FOCUS" copy about "soft textures in a muted color palette" is also at odds with a utility theme. The story is muddled, with trend and product not lining up.
- Judgement: a pleasant browse email that is low on urgency. It follows 10/05's "In focus: '90s-inspired dressing" in the same pattern, an editorial send right after promo-heavy days. It is fine for brand maintenance and weak at driving a click.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, full-bleed model shot with generous white space, and it reads well on mobile.
- Real prices with strike-throughs on all three products make the sale concrete.
- "New Arrival" tags help the eye sort fresh product from sale items.
- The "SHOP BY" grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives easy category exits.
- The Fall Event banner ties the send to the current campaign.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried in a thin red strip at the very top, and the asterisk fine print is long and sits at the bottom.
- The hero product label says "jeans" over an image showing cargo-style trousers and a bomber jacket, so the story doesn't match.
- The "IN FOCUS" copy ("soft textures... muted color palette") undercuts the "utility" idea in the subject.
- Strike-through prices are tiny and the discount percentage isn't shown on any product.
- The hero has no button, only a small text label below it, and the first "SHOP NOW" button doesn't appear until after the fold. The two "SHOP NOW" buttons are generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Overlay "UP TO 40% OFF" on the image and make the hero itself a link.
- 2. Make the story match the products. Either show real utility pieces (cargo trousers, bombers, utility jackets) or retitle "IN FOCUS" to match the knitwear.
- 3. Fix the hero label so the product name matches what is pictured.
- 4. Show the discount (-20%, -27%) next to each price, in a larger size.
- 5. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, such as "Shop utility jackets" and "Shop knitwear".
- 6. Add an end date to the top banner. The fine print says 10/13, which is a credible deadline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links all returned 403 to the QA prober, and the HTML has several accessibility gaps (missing alt text, duplicated desktop/mobile images). The 403s are probably bot-blocking by the ESP's click tracker rather than dead links, so they can't be confirmed as real breakage. The List-Unsubscribe headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status couldn't be verified because the relay doesn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** That includes the "SHOP NOW" and "Shop now" CTAs, the "H&M" logo link, four image links and the footer "Unsubscribe". Every one is a `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h60c02d54-…,54903db2,<hex>&did=1418739122&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6124434&p3=20261009` redirect.
  - Identical 403s across unrelated link types point to the click tracker rejecting the non-browser probe (bot filtering or missing referrer/UA). They don't point to bad per-link destinations.
  - Real-world breakage is unconfirmed. The 16% pass rate is dominated by this one pattern.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 60 links were probed and 48 were skipped (footer and social links deprioritized), so the other 48 are unverified.
- **Destinations are opaque.** Every link is wrapped in a redirect, so the final landing URLs can't be seen in the source.
- **Duplicate tracked links per module:** each product block has separate hex suffixes for its image and its CTA (`f8671a63`/`f8671a64` and `f8671a69`/`f8671a6a` for images, `f8671a67`/`f8671a68` and `f8671a70`/`f8671a71` for "SHOP NOW"). This is normal ESP behavior, but the desktop/mobile image pairs get separate IDs, which splits click attribution.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in URLs:** `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` (base64 of the same rid) and `p2=0982dd0a…`. This is expected for tracking. The unsubscribe link adds `p4`/`p5`, so it is individually tokenized.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed) on:**
  - 3 image pairs under `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/global_campaigns/season_04/women/ns-curation-newsletter/wk41/` (`FNP-WA10264P38-2x3.jpg`, `-3x2.jpg`, `-2x3-2.jpg`), each served as `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`.
  - The `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` image, probably the open-tracking pixel.
  - The QA link text for these is the raw filename (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`), which is what screen readers will announce.
- **Linked images with no alt text:** the image-only links (`f8671a63`, `f8671a64`, `f8671a69`, `f8671a6a`) have no accessible name, so screen readers have nothing meaningful to announce.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile images:** each image appears at 1400px and 750px, which fits the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` toggling in the CSS. Clients that ignore `display:none` inside media queries (some Gmail and Outlook configurations) may show both or read both aloud.
- **Invalid CSS in `#optimize-css`:** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` uses an invalid value (`auto`), which is dropped. The query `@media screen yahoo { … }` is also malformed and likely ignored.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)` are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Fallback stacks aren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.
- **Truncated source:** I couldn't inspect the `<body>`, so I can't check table structure, `lang`, `role="presentation"` or `<title>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty JSON-LD fields:** the `application/ld+json` block ships with unpopulated template values: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""`, `"url":""` and `"availabilityEnds":""`.

- **Effect:** Gmail annotations will have no usable data, and the markup may be flagged as invalid.
- **Visible merge tokens:** no unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this is only partly verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe is missing:** the QA check found no `List-Unsubscribe` header. QA notes the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed. If it is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (5,000+/day) would be violated.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post is missing:** there is no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click), with the same relay caveat.
- **Authentication-Results is missing:** it wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown and can't be assessed.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** an "Unsubscribe" link is present, but its probe returned 403. Whether it works for real users is unverified.
- **Physical address and sender identity:** these are in the footer, outside the truncated source, so I can't evaluate them.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 516 of 594 characters are URLs (>70%). The text alternative is essentially a link dump and may raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Attribution relies on the ESP redirect parameters (`p1=DM6124434` as the campaign ID, `p3=20261009` as the send date). Whether UTMs are appended after the redirect can't be determined.
- **Landing pages are unresolved.** The 403s blocked redirect resolution, so I can't confirm that the "SHOP NOW" or image links go to the "The new utility" content.
- **Image folder name:** the images sit under `wk41/` and `ns-curation-newsletter`, consistent with a week-41 send on 2026-10-09. No mismatch there.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like UA and referrer, or follow redirects in a headless browser. This separates bot-blocking from real breakage and resolves the final landing URLs and UTMs.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the product images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and the 1×1 tracking pixel, and don't let linked images go unnamed.
3. Verify headers on a direct-from-ESP copy (not the AgentMail relay): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `email.hm.com`.
4. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link resolves, since its probe returned 403.
5. Fix or remove the empty or stale JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts: 2018-12-31`).
6. Correct the invalid CSS (`line-height:auto`, `@media screen yahoo`), and make sure desktop and mobile image variants don't both render or get read aloud.
7. Build a richer plain-text part with readable copy rather than mostly URLs.
8. Raise the probe budget, or add the footer and social links, so the 48 skipped links get coverage.
