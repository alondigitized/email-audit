# Up to 30% off cardis, jeans, sweats...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-forward H&M send. The hero is a bold black "UP TO 30% OFF FALL ESSENTIALS" headline over a single model in a slate fine-knit cardigan. Seven product tiles follow, each with a red sale price next to a struck-through original. It is easy to scan, and the pricing is the strongest part: $12.49 against $17.99 and $14.99 against $19.99 are concrete.
- The mismatch is in the copy. The subject promises "cardis, jeans, sweats," but the body is mostly cardigans. There's one sweatshirt and a T-shirt, and the only jeans are on models, with no jeans product tile. The headline also says "up to 30%," but the visible discounts are closer to 25–30% on a few items, and nothing is called out as the 30% item. The asterisk fine print is long and sits at the very bottom.
- It's a solid, competent promo, but near-identical to the week's other sends. It follows 10-01 "The Outerwear Edit" and 09-30 "25% off flash sale," which also used a fall-essentials discount banner. Nothing here is new or time-bound. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The hero headline is large and legible, so the offer lands immediately.
- Sale prices in red beside struck-through originals make the savings concrete on every tile.
- Product photography is consistent and shows fit well, especially on the cardigans.
- Dual CTAs ("SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE") sit right under the hero copy and are clearly visible.
- The "Shop by" category grid at the bottom gives an exit for shoppers who want men's, kids, home, or beauty.

## What's weak

- The subject promises jeans and sweats, but the body is cardigan-heavy. Jeans only appear on models, not as products.
- The offer is ambiguous. "Up to 30%" with no hero item showing 30%, and the real cap is buried in the footer asterisk.
- No deadline appears in the visible creative. The 10/07 end date is only in the fine print, so there's no urgency.
- Product tiles have no ratings, sizing, or color information, so there is no social proof to push a click.
- The three-line product copy under the hero is thin ("Sweater weather starts here…"), and the tile layout alternates between pairs and singles, which feels uneven.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the body to the subject: add a jeans tile and a sweats tile, or retitle the subject around cardigans. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero (e.g. "Ends 10/7"), and add the member perk if the recipient is a member.
- 3. Show the real maximum discount by featuring the 30% item first, with a "-30%" badge on it.
- 4. Give each product tile a specific CTA ("Shop cardigans," "Shop jeans") in place of a generic "Shop now."
- 5. Add a star rating or "bestseller" tag on a few tiles for social proof.
