# Trick-or-treat: Up to 50% off all this
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal Halloween send, and it works on that level. The hero is a smiling kid in a witch hat with a bold "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT STYLES" headline underneath. The product grid is clean, with real sale prices in red and original prices struck through. It's visually tidy and on-season, and the offer is easy to find.
- The problems are the copy and the structure. "All this" in the subject points at nothing. The body has no single primary CTA, just five outlined buttons (Baby, Kids 2-8Y, Kids 9-14Y, Home, Find a Store) that split the click. It's also a weak fit against H&M's own cadence. This is the latest in a week of near-identical discount sends, including "Up to 40% off sitewide ends tonight" on 10/13 and the 10/11 "Your fall aesthetic is up to 40% off". Halloween is a fresh hook, but the discount is the same one and it's no more urgent. Overall it's a competent 6, held back by a vague subject and a scattered call to action.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and on-theme. It signals Halloween at a glance, and the headline sits right below it.
- "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT STYLES" is large and easy to read.
- Price treatment is clear. Red sale prices with struck-through originals, such as $4.99 and $13.99, make the savings concrete.
- The "Frights & Home Delights" section extends the offer beyond kids' costumes into home decor, which widens appeal.
- Layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's "all this" is filler. It doesn't say what "this" is (costumes, decor, kids' wear).
- There's no single primary CTA. Five equal-weight outlined buttons dilute the click, and none says "Shop costumes".
- The deadline (10/21) sits only in the dense fine print at the bottom. Nothing near the headline creates urgency.
- Only about three-quarters of the grid is Halloween product. The "Shop by" block at the bottom (Women, Men, Home, Beauty) is generic and off-theme.
- Fine print is long, and "select styles" and "up to" leave the offer feeling vague.
- This is another discount email in a week of near-identical ones, so it risks blending into the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the five buttons with one dominant CTA directly under the headline, such as "Shop Halloween costumes". Put the category links in a smaller secondary row.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Through 10/21", and name the product in the subject.
- 3. Lead the grid with the best-priced, most giftable items. The $3.49 bodysuit and $4.99 top are strong hooks and should be called out.
- 4. Drop or cut the generic "Shop by" block so the email stays on one theme.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Kids' costumes + spooky decor, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skeleton tops, monster sets and ghost decor. Sale ends 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Costumes from $3.49, plus fleece throws and pumpkin décor for the home.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, but the automated QA run could not verify any of its 12 probed links: every one returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector, which points to bot blocking rather than dead links. The confirmed code-level problems are missing alt text, empty JSON-LD placeholders, and invalid CSS. Header checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) were inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They include "Shop now" (`...,54912524,f8765f3f` and `...f8765f41`), "Find a store" (`...f8765f5f`, `...f8765f6a`), the category links (Baby, Kids 2‑8Y, KIDS 9‑14Y, Home) and the Unsubscribe link (`...f8765fa4`). This is a uniform 403 on one host, including the unsubscribe link, so the likely cause is bot filtering of the QA probe. It is not confirmed breakage. Treat link health as unverified.
- **Every link is wrapped in the redirector.** The pattern is `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...&did=1418798372&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM6133305&p2=...&p3=20261014`. The final destination URLs can't be seen, so the QA run couldn't check them.
- **12 of 78 links were probed (66 skipped).** Footer, social and utility links were not checked.
- **Plain-text part is >70% URLs** (524 of 602 characters). This is a deliverability warning, since the plain-text alternative is almost entirely tracking URLs.
- **Some link text contains invisible characters.** The "Shop now" anchors contain zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`), which suggests ZWNJ/BOM padding in the anchor text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images without usable alt text:**
  - Every `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pair (9 products, for example `.../wk-42/na1563_s4/1354984002_202604_LB_5540.jpg`) is flagged as missing alt text.
  - The 1400 and 750 variants are likely a desktop/mobile swap pair, so each product image is flagged twice.
  - The same images sit inside links, so those links have no accessible name. The QA output shows their link text as `[image: cq5dam...]`.

- **Invalid CSS.** `.is-hidden-desktop{...line-height:auto!important...}` is not a valid `line-height` value and will be dropped. The `@media screen yahoo` block is also a malformed media query.
- **Web fonts loaded by `@import`.** The two `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css` rules are stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so those clients will use fallback fonts. Whether the fallbacks are adequate can't be checked from the truncated source.
