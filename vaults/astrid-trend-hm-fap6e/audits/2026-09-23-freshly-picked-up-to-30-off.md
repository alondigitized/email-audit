# Freshly picked & up to 30% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-organized, family-inclusive fall shop email — "Up to 30% off select styles" with real prices shown across Women/Men/Baby/Kids, a "You Need These" cross-category block, and a clean Shop By footer grid. It's a step up from the 2026-09-18 broken-image disaster and lands in the same solid-but-unspectacular tier as most of this brand's recent sends (the 2026-09-19 through 09-21 run of 6.00/10s).
- The hero model and cardigan shot are appealing, and the four-category quick-shop buttons right under the offer headline are a smart layout choice repeated from prior sends. But nothing here differentiates it from the last several H&M emails — same discount tier, same layout skeleton, same lack of urgency — so it reads as competent inventory, not a standout send.
- Judgement: solid execution, zero novelty. Ships fine, won't move the needle beyond baseline click-through.

## What's working

- Clear, legible "UP TO 30% OFF SELECT STYLES" headline directly under the hero image, impossible to miss.
- Four department shortcut buttons (Women/Men/Baby/Kids) appear twice — once near top, once mid-email — giving multiple easy exit points.
- Product grid shows real strikethrough pricing (e.g., $12.49 vs $17.99) on every item, so the discount is concrete, not vague.
- Family-inclusive assortment (kids' knit sets, jacquard sweater) broadens relevance beyond a single shopper type.
- No render bugs — every image loads cleanly, text is legible, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- No urgency or end-date anywhere in the body — "30% off" with no deadline invites deferral.
- Subject/hero disconnect: "Freshly picked" implies curation/editorial, but the body is a standard category-shop layout, no editorial framing to match.
- "SELECT STYLES" and the asterisked fine print undercut the offer's clarity — reader has to guess which items qualify.
- Visually near-identical structure to the 09-19/09-20/09-21 sends (hero photo, headline, category buttons, product grid) — fatigue risk for a subscriber getting the 4th similar layout in a week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline ("Ends Sunday") near the headline — this is the single biggest lever missing from an otherwise clean layout.
- 2. Replace "SELECT STYLES" with a category anchor tied to the subject ("Freshly picked knits & layers") so subject and hero copy reinforce each other instead of feeling disconnected.
- 3. Move the strongest single discount (the cardigan at $12.49, ~31% off) into the hero banner itself instead of only the generic "up to 30%" — a real anchor product builds more trust than a range.
- 4. Vary the layout from the last three sends — e.g., swap the double category-button block for a single row — to break the repetition a frequent subscriber will notice.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall layers just dropped — up to 30% off`
