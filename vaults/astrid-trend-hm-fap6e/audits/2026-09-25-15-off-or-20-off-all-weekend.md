# 15% off or 20% off all weekend
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward tiered-discount send — "15% off $75 or 20% off $100" on outerwear — and it's the clearest, most legible offer structure in this brand's recent stretch. Unlike the 09-19/09-20 "20% off $125 + free shipping" pair, this actually varies the mechanic (two spend thresholds instead of one flat minimum), which helps it not read as a straight repeat, though the underlying playbook — hero coat model, four-department shop grid, Shop By footer — is now extremely familiar from this sender.
- The hero image is strong: a single, well-lit outerwear shot with clean typography stacked directly beneath it puts the eye exactly where it should go. The rest of the email is functional but padded — two near-duplicate grid blocks (Women/Men/Baby/Kids, then Shop By Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) do a lot of scrolling work for not much new information.
- Judgement: solid, on-brand execution of a real discount, let down by the generic "shop now" CTA and by fatigue — this is at least the fourth percentage-off outerwear/category push from H&M in the last week.

## What's working

- Clean hero: one model, one coat, no clutter, offer text sits directly below in high-contrast type.
- The tiered offer (15%/$75 or 20%/$100) is genuinely legible at a glance — bold percentage numbers pop.
- Category grid (Women/Men/Baby/Kids) gives every household segment a same-scroll entry point.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, text is crisp, footer/legal block is intact.

## What's weak

- Primary CTA button just says "SHOP NOW" — generic, not tied to the outerwear hook or the offer.
- Two back-to-back grid sections (Women/Men/Baby/Kids, then Shop By Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) are redundant and push footer content far down.
- No urgency/deadline visible in the body — the fine print reveals an end date (09/27) but it's buried in legal copy, not called out in the hero.
- This is the third "weekend deal" / tiered-discount structure H&M has sent in about a week (09-19, 09-20, now this) — cadence is starting to blur together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, not just the fine print — "Ends Sunday 9/27" under the offer line would convert the buried urgency into a real driver.
- 2. Change the CTA button copy to something offer-specific, e.g. "Shop Outerwear" or "Unlock 20% Off," rather than generic "Shop Now."
- 3. Cut one of the two grid blocks — merge Women/Men/Baby/Kids into the Shop By section instead of running both.
- 4. Add a small "Member Bonus Reward" callout near the offer — the legal text mentions a $5 bonus reward but it's invisible until you read fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: 15% off $75 or 20% off $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Outerwear weekend: up to 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Trend-right coats and jackets, discounted through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $100, save 20% — outerwear edit inside.`
