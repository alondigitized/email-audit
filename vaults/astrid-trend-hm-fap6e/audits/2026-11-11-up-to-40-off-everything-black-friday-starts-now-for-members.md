# Up to 40% off everything! Black Friday starts now for Members
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a Member-exclusive Black Friday early-access send. The offer, "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS," is the strongest in H&M's recent run. It beats the tiered 15/20/25% blocks from 11/06 to 11/08. The black header banner and the big headline make the offer obvious.
- The execution is weaker than the offer. The hero's red "MEMBER-EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW" text sits on top of the model's body and the dark background. It is hard to read, and it looks like an overlap bug. The top banner pushes joining ("BECOME A MEMBER… + 15% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE"), which muddles a send meant to reward existing members. The body is a generic category grid (Women, Men, Baby/Kids, Home) with no product, price or hero item. Fine print below says "up to 40%" excludes many things, and that dense legal block is long.
- Judgement: a strong subject line and a real offer, held back by a hard-to-read hero and a scattered, non-product body. It is better than the 11/10 collab send, which had no offer. It is a bit behind the 11/10 knitwear send on focus.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is bold, centered and unmistakable, and it is the biggest discount H&M has sent in this run.
- The member framing ("Your VIP pass to the season's biggest savings") gives loyalty-driven exclusivity.
- Each category block has one short title and one clear button (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, HOME).
- The black "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE" block adds an omnichannel offer.
- The email matches the current Black Friday window, which keeps it timely.

## What's weak

- The red hero headline overlaps the model's dark clothing and background, so it is hard to read. It looks like a layout bug.
- The top banner pitches joining and a 15% first-purchase offer. That competes with the member offer and puts a second offer above the fold.
- The body shows no price, product name or specific hero item. Buttons say only "WOMEN" or "MEN," with no verb.
- The in-store block's red text on black is low contrast, and the "FIND A STORE" button is nearly invisible.
- The fine print is very long, and the "Not a Member? Join today and get an extra 10% off" line contradicts the 15% in the banner.
- There is no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put white or black type on a clean background so "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" can be read at a glance.
- 2. Remove the join-now banner, or make it a footer note. Give members one offer: "Up to 40% off, today only."
- 3. Replace the category tiles with 3–4 real products with sale prices. Make the CTAs verb-led, such as "Shop women's party looks."
- 4. Add a credible deadline, such as "Ends 11/16," near the top.
- 5. Fix the black in-store block's contrast and make its button solid.
- 6. Make the 10% and 15% new-member claims consistent, or drop them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: up to 40% off starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early Black Friday access: up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members-only markdowns on party looks, outerwear and gifts. Ends 11/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign in to unlock your VIP prices before everyone else.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links, including the unsubscribe link, returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com` click tracker. That pattern points to bot-filtering of the QA probe, so I can't confirm the links are broken. Separately, many images lack alt text, the JSON-LD block is unpopulated, and the header checks were inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on every probed link (12/12):** this covers "Shop now" (×4 variants), "Find a store" (×2), the "H&M" logo link, both image links (`...f8bc9d19`, `...f8bc9d1a`), "Sign in", "Sign up" and "Unsubscribe". Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h23ac8803-3a71-4684-93b3-6497fddce19d,5514638f,f8bc9d1b&did=1427399567&rid=3326930539...`.
- **Likely a probe artifact:** a uniform 403 across every link, including one that must work for real recipients (unsubscribe), is consistent with the ESP tracker rejecting non-browser clients. It would not be expected if the campaign's links were actually dead. This was not verified with a real client, so treat "broken" as unconfirmed.
- **Destinations can't be audited:** every link is wrapped in the redirect tracker, and 68 further links were not probed.
- **Duplicate tracked links:** the "Shop now" links appear in two variants each (`f8bc9d1b`/`f8bc9d1e`, `f8bc9cf9`/`f8bc9cfb`), and "Find a store" appears twice (`f8bc9d3b`/`f8bc9d3c`). This is consistent with separate desktop and mobile blocks (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`), so it is not a defect in itself.
- **Link text from alt fallback:** the QA tool reads the image links as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. This is a symptom of the alt-text issue below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 20 `<img>` elements flagged:**

Gmail and some other clients ignore these, so fallback fonts will be used there. This is not confirmed as a defect without seeing the font stacks, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- No other rendering issues are confirmable from the truncated source. Viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` meta tags are present, and mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media (max-width:699px)`) is in place.
