# Up to 50% off for party people
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent H&M Black Friday send. The subject promises a party angle and the body delivers it loosely. A model in a black halter top and trousers fills the hero. "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" and "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" follow, and the copy reads "go-out tops, embellished looks, and festive accessories". The subject, hero and products line up better than in the 2026-11-23 activewear send, which never showed its $9.99 price.
- The email has too many jobs. A gift-card banner sits at the top, then party clothes, then accessories, then a 20-tile "Holiday Gift Guide" collage, then a "Shop By" grid, then the app download. It has a promo hero and a gift-guide hero. The party story is strong in the first third and then dilutes. The fine print is also long, and it sits in the footer with a Member Bonus Reward caveat. That is the "buried fine print" problem.
- Overall it is solid but undistinguished. It is a clean send, but it repeats the same "UP TO 50% OFF" hero H&M has used all week (2026-11-22 through 2026-11-24 denim), and the party angle is the only fresh element.

## What's working

- The hero model in black halter and trousers fits the "party" subject and gives a clear focal point.
- "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" is a concrete, large offer that the eye reaches first.
- The product tiles show real sale prices, such as $17.99, $13.99 and $19.99, with the original prices struck through.
- The accessories block (bucket bag, bow tie, bow-trim flats) extends the party look.
- There are no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The top banner, "THE H&M GIFT CARD / THE GIFT THAT ALWAYS FITS", competes with the party offer before the hero loads.
- The 20-tile gift guide collage has no prices, no product labels and no clear click target. It is visually busy.
- The category buttons (WOMEN, MEN, BABY, KIDS, FIND A STORE) are generic. Nothing is party-specific, so the subject's promise is not carried into the CTAs.
- The long exclusion-heavy fine print, with its member reward caveats, undercuts the "up to 50%" claim.
- This is the fourth near-identical "up to 50% off" hero in four days, so the cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the gift-card banner and the gift-guide collage, or move the gift guide to its own send. Keep one story: party looks at up to 50% off.
- 2. Replace the generic category buttons with specific ones, such as "Shop party tops" and "Shop festive dresses".
- 3. Add a price-anchored hook in the hero, such as "Party looks from $13.99".
- 4. Move a short deadline line near the hero, such as "Ends 11/25", and shorten the fine print.
- 5. Label the gift-guide tiles with prices if you keep them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Party looks from $13.99, up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sparkle for less: party styles up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mesh tops, bow flats and festive knits, marked down for Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find your holiday party outfit before the deals end.`
