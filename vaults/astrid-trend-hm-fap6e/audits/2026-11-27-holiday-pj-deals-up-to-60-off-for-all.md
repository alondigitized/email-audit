# Holiday PJ deals: Up to 60% off for all!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a cohesive pajama-themed H&M send, and the subject finally matches the body. After a run of sweater, denim and party subjects, "Holiday PJ deals" leads to a hero of striped pajamas, plaid sets, and slippers. The "UP TO 60% OFF SITEWIDE" headline sits right under the "THE BLACK FRIDAY EVENT" kicker, with a SHOP NOW button below it.
- The email is long and heavy on lifestyle photography. It is built as three category blocks (women, men, kids/baby), but no product has a price and no item is linked individually. The offer is a generic sitewide percentage that is the same as every other H&M send this week. The only fresh angle is the cozy theme.
- Overall it is competent, on-theme and seasonal, but it is not differentiated. The "for all" framing is inclusive, but the email never tells me what the actual PJ deals are.

## What's working

- The subject and body match: the PJ promise is delivered with pajama and slipper imagery across the whole email.
- The "UP TO 60% OFF SITEWIDE" headline is bold and sits near the top. Free shipping over $50 for members is also visible in the top banner.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and cohesive. The striped PJ hero, plaid set and slippers fit the holiday cozy mood.
- The "for all" claim is backed up by sections for women, men, and kids and baby.
- Each section has a short, clean heading ("SETTLE IN," "COMFORT MODE," "TIME FOR BED") followed by outlined buttons, so the layout is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- No prices or product-level deals appear. "Deals" is promised in the subject but never shown.
- The hero text block is small and light. The "Now that dinner is over…" body copy is easy to miss, and the headline has to compete with a large photo.
- The CTAs are generic, thin-outlined buttons ("SHOP NOW," "PAJAMAS," "THE COZY SHOP") that don't stand out. Several overlap in purpose and send the user to broad category pages.
- The email is very long, with repeated SHOP NOW buttons and a closing "COZY SEASON" block that repeats the same message.
- The legal footer is a very dense block of fine print about exclusions and expiry. It sits against the "sitewide" claim, so the offer feels less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real prices in the hero, for example "PJ sets from $14.99," and make each product tile link to its own product page. This is the single change that would lift clicks most.
- 2. Make the headline and CTA larger and filled, in H&M red, so the eye lands on them first.
- 3. Cut the email by a third by merging the repeated shop blocks. Use one product grid per audience.
- 4. Add a specific deadline such as "Ends Sunday" or "Ends Cyber Monday" next to the 60% figure.
- 5. Use a more specific CTA such as "Shop women's pajamas" instead of "SHOP NOW."
