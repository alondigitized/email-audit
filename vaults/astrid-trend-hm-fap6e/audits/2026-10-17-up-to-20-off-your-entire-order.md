# Up to 20% off your entire order
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, readable layer-up send, but it works against itself. The subject promises "Up to 20% off your entire order." The body says "15% OFF $100 OR 20% OFF $125," and the fine print excludes sale items, gift cards, furniture, H&M Studio, guest designer collaborations and select special collections. "Entire order" isn't honest here. It's also a repeat of Friday's "15% off or 20% off this weekend" send, now with a vaguer subject.
- The render itself is solid. The headline is bold, there is one outlined SHOP NOW button, and the soft-knit photography is cohesive. The two category blocks, "SOFT TOUCHES" (women) and "FEEL-GOOD FINDS" (men), are well organized and show who the offer is for. The weakness is that the hero sits at the top as text with no product or price, and below that there is a long stretch of lifestyle imagery with no prices and no product detail.
- The judgement is a competent but low-energy reminder. It's the third offer reshuffle in a week, after "up to 40% off sitewide" (10/11–10/13) and 10/16's identical threshold deal. The "valid online only through 10/18/2026" deadline sits in the fine print and not in the headline, so the urgency is wasted.

## What's working

- The headline "15% OFF $100 OR 20% OFF $125" is large, high-contrast and the first thing the eye lands on.
- There is one clear primary CTA, an outlined SHOP NOW directly under the offer.
- The "SOFT TOUCHES" and "FEEL-GOOD FINDS" blocks split women's and men's with matching sweaters/sweatshirts/sweatpants buttons.
- The knit and fleece imagery fits mid-October, and the copy ("Layer up on all things cozy") matches the season.
- The top bar shows the member perk, "Free shipping over $50 for H&M members."

## What's weak

- The subject says "entire order" and "up to 20%," but the offer is a spend threshold with many exclusions. That is a bait-and-switch feel.
- The deadline ("valid online only through 10/18/2026") appears only in the legal text, so there is no visible urgency in the hero.
- The category buttons are generic, and no product shows a price, name or link, so nothing helps a click.
- The "SHOP BY" tiles, app links and the long legal block make the bottom half heavy. The links in the footer are low-contrast gray and hard to read.
- The email is nearly identical to the 10/16 send, so it adds to promo fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the subject to the real offer and put the deadline in it, and add "ends tomorrow" or "ends Sunday" to the hero as an eyebrow line.
- 2. Replace the lifestyle-only photo blocks with 3–4 specific products with names and prices so there is something to click.
- 3. Make the buttons verb-led ("Shop women's sweaters") and link the hero photos directly to the matching category.
- 4. Move the exclusions into a short visible line under the CTA ("Excludes sale items") so the offer feels honest.
- 5. Cut the "SHOP BY" row and app links, or shrink them. Don't send the same threshold offer two days in a row. Hold it for a final-day push.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cozy knits just got cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only through 10/18. Sweaters, sweatshirts and sweatpants for layering season.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $125, save 20%. Free shipping over $50 for members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard ESP redirect-tracking setup (`t19.email.hm.com/r/`) with responsive CSS and JSON-LD markup. All 12 probed links returned 403, which looks like click-tracker bot filtering rather than a confirmed outage. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. Confirmed code-level defects are missing alt text and an empty, stale JSON-LD block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** This covers the "Shop now" CTAs, the logo link ("H&M"), the "Free shipping over $50…" banner, the image links and the Unsubscribe link. All are on `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=haed1cf72-…`. The shared host and the uniform 403 point to the redirector rejecting the automated probe's user agent or IP. This is unconfirmed, because the QA check doesn't show whether a real client resolves them. Treat it as unverified, not as a broken experience.
- **Final destinations can't be checked.** Every link is wrapped, so landing URLs, UTM parameters and link integrity are not observable from the source or the QA data.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 58 skipped (footer, social and utility links), so the rest are unvalidated.
- **Per-recipient identifiers are in the URLs.** Each link carries `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` (the base64 of the same `rid`) and `p2=0982dd0a…`. The Unsubscribe link adds `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746…`. This is normal for an ESP, but anyone who forwards the email exposes the recipient's unsubscribe link.
- **A tracking image has no alt text.** One `<img>` uses `src=https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=haed1cf72-…,1` and looks like an open-tracking pixel. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA check flagged 13 `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-4x/…` images. Each appears as a 1400×1400 and a 750×750 `cq5dam.web.*.jpeg` pair, which suggests desktop and mobile variants. Examples are `1347468002_202604_LB_0060.jpg`, `1242877066_202604_LB_6119.jpg`, `1307896028_202604_LB_0581.jpg`, `1366847004_202604_LB_2765.jpg`, `1357440002_202604_LB_3289.jpg` and `1245648016_202604_LB_0102.jpg`. Several are the sole content of an `<a>`, so the QA link text falls back to `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. A screen reader or image-blocked client gets no usable link label.
- **The tracking-pixel `<img>` needs `alt=""`.** It is flagged as missing alt text.
- **Hidden characters in link text.** Two "Shop now" anchors have link text with leading zero-width characters (`"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which includes U+200C and U+FEFF). The padding looks like it leaked from preheader or spacing hacks into the anchor. This can cause inconsistent link-text announcements.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile images.** The 1400/750 pairs together with `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` (which use `display:none!important`) show the email ships both variants. Without `alt` on both, hidden-variant handling in assistive tech is unpredictable.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)` are ignored by Gmail and many Outlook builds, so they fall back to system fonts. This is a minor issue, assuming fallback stacks exist; the truncated source doesn't show them.
- **Truncated source.** Doctype, viewport meta and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present. Body semantics, such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables and `lang` on `<html>`, could not be assessed. The `<html>` tag shown has no `lang` attribute.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes this "may not be captured by AgentMail relay," so it can't be confirmed as missing at the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. This is required for bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, but the "From" header uses `newsletter@email.hm.com`, a subdomain aligned with the tracking host `t19.email.hm.com`. The relay stripped the headers, so alignment can't be confirmed.
- **An unsubscribe link exists** in the body ("Unsubscribe", `…f87c25e4…&p4=3326930539&p5=…`). It returned 403 to the probe, so whether it works is unverified (same probable cause as in section 2).
- **Postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it couldn't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** Wrapped redirect URLs hide the destinations. The only visible campaign identifiers are `did=1418857538`, `p1=DM6144159` and `p3=20261017`, which are ESP parameters, not UTMs.
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed.** The probes were blocked (403), so the offer on the landing pages ("Up to 20% off your entire order") is unverified.
- **Stale JSON-LD.** The `application/ld+json` block has empty `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `description`, `availabilityEnds`, `image` and `url` fields on its `EmailMessage`, `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries. `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, which is nearly eight years stale. Gmail promotion-card annotations have no usable data and an expired offer window.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe a sample of the tracked links, including Unsubscribe, with a browser-like user agent or from a real inbox. This separates bot-blocking from real failures. Don't treat the 16% pass rate as a defect until then.
2. Capture the raw message headers directly, not through the relay. This confirms `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 13 flagged `cq5dam.web.*` content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Remove the zero-width and BOM characters from the CTA anchor text. Keep preheader padding outside `<a>` elements.
5. Populate the JSON-LD `EmailMessage.subjectLine`, `DiscountOffer.description`, `availabilityStarts` and `availabilityEnds`, and the `PromotionCard` `image` and `url` fields, or remove the block.
6. Make sure the CSS web fonts have robust fallbacks, and add `lang` to `<html>`.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Truncation prevented checks of the full body, the footer address, the rest of the 58 skipped links and any UTM parameters.
