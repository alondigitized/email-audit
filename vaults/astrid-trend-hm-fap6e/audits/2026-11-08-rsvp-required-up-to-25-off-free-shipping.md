# RSVP required: Up to 25% off + free shipping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's third near-identical tiered Black Friday send in four days. It repeats the "15% OFF $75 / 20% OFF $100 / 25% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" block from 11/06 ("Up to 25% off your order starts now") and 11/07 ("15%, 20% or 25% off all weekend"). The offer is clear, but it is no longer news. The "LAST DAY" label is the only new element, and it is a small gray line above the offer.
- The subject opens with "RSVP required," which the body never explains. Nothing in the render is an RSVP: no event, no reply button, no date. The red banner at the top adds a second offer, "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE", on top of the tiered online offer. The hero is a model in a sequined black jacket, a strong, on-season image. Below it the email splits into four category blocks (women, men, baby/kids, home) with no products, prices or offer reminders.
- Verdict: a competent but fatigued send, and the subject misleads. The fatigue is the bigger problem, because a subscriber who has seen this block three times in four days will skim past it.

## What's working

- The tiered offer block is bold and scannable, with the free-shipping line as its closer.
- The sequined-jacket hero is on-season and on-brand for holiday dressing.
- The "LAST DAY" label makes the urgency credible, since the offer really is ending.
- Four category tiles, each with one button, route shoppers cleanly by department.
- The top CTA pair, "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE", covers both online and in-store shoppers.

## What's weak

- "RSVP required" promises an event the body never delivers, which is a bait-and-switch.
- "LAST DAY" is small and gray, so the one new piece of information is the easiest to miss.
- The red banner's 30% in-store offer competes with the 25% online tiers and muddies the message.
- The category tiles show no products, prices or offers, so nothing pulls the click beyond a generic button.
- The legal footer is a very long wall of text that dwarfs the content. The fine print also makes the offer feel less honest.
- It is the third repeat of the same offer block in four days (11/06, 11/07, now).

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject to match the content. Drop "RSVP" unless there is a real event, and lead with the deadline. "Last day" is the news and should be the headline.
- 2. Make "LAST DAY" large and put a countdown or an explicit deadline time in the hero.
- 3. Pick one offer. Either the online tiers or the in-store 30%, not both in the same view.
- 4. Put products and "from" prices under the category tiles, and use specific button copy such as "Shop party dresses" instead of "WOMEN".
- 5. Cut the footer legal block down to a short summary with a link to the full terms.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $125, save 25%. Shipping's on us until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Party-season dressing for the whole family, last call.`

# Technical Audit: H&M "RSVP required: Up to 25% off + free shipping"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML uses a conventional table-based layout with responsive CSS. However, all 12 probed tracked links returned HTTP 403 (16% pass rate), including the unsubscribe link. Most image `alt` attributes are missing, and the JSON-LD block is empty. The 403s are probably bot-blocking of the click-tracker, so they need confirming with a real client before being treated as outages (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** The affected links go through the click-tracker `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h67159ba0-…&did=1419248132&rid=3326930539&erid=…`. They are:
  - "Shop now" (`…f8b46abe`, `…f8b46ac0`, `…f8b46a9c`, `…f8b46a9e`)
  - "Find a store" (`…f8b46abf`, `…f8b46ac1`)
  - the headline link "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS: 30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE*" (`…f8b46a9a`)
  - the "H&M" logo link (`…f8b46a9b`)
  - image links (`…f8b46abc`, `…f8b46abd`, `…f8b46ac2`)
  - Unsubscribe (`…f8b46afe`)
- **Likely cause:** the identical 403 on every link, unsubscribe included, points to the tracker rejecting non-browser or probe traffic rather than 12 separate broken destinations. This is inference, not confirmed. The final landing URLs could not be resolved, so destination health is unverified.
- **Hidden characters in link text:** two "Shop now" links contain zero-width or invisible characters (`"‌  ﻿Shop now"`). These can degrade screen-reader output and any text-based link matching.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 76 links were probed (64 skipped, deprioritized as footer or social links), so those 64 are untested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 21 images.**
  - The product images are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, served from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-45/…`.
  - The two `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…` images also lack alt text.
  - Each product appears twice, as a 1400px and a 750px asset, which suggests a desktop/mobile image swap.
  - Because these images sit inside links, the link's accessible name falls back to the filename, as in the QA output `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `<style>@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/…/fonts.css); @import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css);</style>` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. Check that fallback font stacks are defined.
- **Display toggling:** the `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` classes and the `#MessageViewBody .hide-webkit / .show-webkit-table` rules are standard hacks. I found no defect in the visible source, but the source was truncated, so I cannot verify the fallback paths.

## 7. Recommendations
