# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened H&M and the very first thing I see is a full-screen cookie consent wall — not a hero, not a product, not a sale. The actual homepage is completely hidden behind this modal. There's a flash of what looks like a red or orange image peeking out on the left edge, but that's all. Right now H&M looks like a legal department, not a fashion brand.

## What's working

- **"ACCEPT ALL" button** is the most visually prominent element — large, black, full-width. At least the consent flow has a clear primary action so I'm not trapped.
- **"ONLY REQUIRED COOKIES" as a secondary option** is honestly kind of refreshing — I appreciate the explicit choice without burying it.
- **Clean typography** on the modal itself is on-brand minimalist, which tracks for H&M's aesthetic at least.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal blocks everything** — I landed on a homepage and got a legal document. No brand impression, no product, no reason to stay.
- **Wall of legal copy** above the buttons is dense and wordy — nobody is reading five paragraphs on a phone screen before they've even seen what the site is selling.
- **Zero commerce signals above the fold** — no hero, no offer, no brand personality whatsoever.
- **The sliver of imagery visible on the left edge** is too thin to read — could be anything. Wasted real estate.
- **Three-button layout** (Accept All / Only Required / Settings) adds cognitive load when I just want to shop.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the consent modal to a bottom banner** — show the homepage behind it immediately. Every second the full-screen wall is up is a bounce risk.
- **Lead with a single-sentence value prop** ("We use cookies to personalize your shopping experience") and a big "Accept All" button — trim the legalese paragraph entirely.
- **Surface the hero and a teaser promo behind the modal** using a semi-transparent overlay so I can see what I'm agreeing to load before I consent.
- **Consolidate to two choices max** — "Accept All" and "Manage Settings." The middle "Only Required Cookies" option splits attention and adds friction.
