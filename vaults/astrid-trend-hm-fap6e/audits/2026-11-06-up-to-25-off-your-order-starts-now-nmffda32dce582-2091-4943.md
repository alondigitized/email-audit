# Up to 25% off your order starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, tiered-threshold Black Friday send. The hero offer reads at a glance: "15% OFF $75 / 20% OFF $100 / 25% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING," set in bold type under a strong outerwear hero. It is a better, more honest offer structure than the vague "holidays, your way" send on 11/05. It also matches the "25% off flash sale" on 11/04, so the 25% number is now being recycled.
- The problems are mixed messaging and cadence. The subject says "Up to 25% off your order starts now," but the black banner at the top says "30% off select styles in-store," and the hero is labeled "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS." That is three different offers in one email. The body is three editorial tiles (Luxe Layers, First Impressions, Non-Negotiables) with no price, no product names and no deal reminder. Below that comes a wall of dense legal copy.
- Judgement: a solid, readable promo with a real offer, undercut by offer clutter and a fourth discount send in six days. It's average for the category.

## What's working

- The tiered threshold ladder is concrete, easy to scan and sits right under the hero.
- The free shipping add-on lowers the barrier to the first tier.
- The hero image is warm and seasonal, and the outerwear fits the November timing.
- The tiles are split by Women, Men, and Baby/Kids, so each shopper has a clear path.
- A "Shop by" row with category thumbnails gives a second chance to click.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete: "up to 25%" in the subject, "30% off select styles in-store" in the banner, and the threshold ladder in the hero.
- The hero text "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" is stacked over the model's jacket. The overlap is cramped and the letters are hard to read.
- The tiles ("Luxe Layers," "First Impressions," "Non-Negotiables") are cryptic. They carry no price, product or offer reminder, and each has only a small outlined button.
- The fine print is a huge, tiny-type block with exclusions. It reads as buried conditions, and the 30% in-store claim sits oddly next to the online ladder.
- It's the 4th discount-led send since 11/04, so urgency is eroding.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and make the subject, banner and hero agree. Lead with the threshold ladder and drop or separate the in-store 30%.
- 2. Put the offer in the tile CTAs, for example "Shop women's layers, up to 25% off," and show a price or product name in each tile.
- 3. Move the hero headline off the model, or use a solid color block, so it's legible.
- 4. Cut the legal block to a short summary plus a "Details" link.
- 5. Space out the discount sends, or vary the angle (gifting, new arrivals) between them.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend more, save more on outerwear through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tailored, leather, puffer and more. Free shipping on every tier.`

# Technical Audit: H&M "Up to 25% off your order starts now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, responsive build, and all 64 links go through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` tracking redirector. The QA findings are mostly probe 403s, missing image `alt` text, and header data the relay didn't capture. The only code-level defects confirmed in the source are the empty and stale JSON-LD fields and some invalid CSS.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403.** They include Shop now (`...f8b0d110`), Find a store (`...f8b0d111`), the hero/logo links (`...f8b0d0ec`, `...f8b0d0ed`) and Unsubscribe (`...f8b0d144`).
  - Every failing URL is on the same redirector host, with the same `id=h32dce582-...` and `did=1419244563`.
  - A uniform 403 on one host, including the unsubscribe link, points to bot or WAF blocking of the QA prober. It does not point to individually broken links.
  - **Not confirmed as a real defect.** Verify with a real-browser or mobile-UA click-through before calling it a failure.
- **Opaque tracking.** Final destinations can't be seen without following the redirect. Per-link IDs (`...f8b0d0ec` through `...f8b0d144`) are the only differentiator.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** Two "Shop now" anchors (`...f8b0d0ee`, `...f8b0d0f0`) are prefixed with `‌  ﻿` (ZWNJ, spaces, BOM). This is likely a preheader/spacing hack, but it pollutes link text for screen readers and click-reporting.
- **Redundant links.** The "Shop now" and "Find a store" pairs (`...d110`/`d112`, `d111`/`d113`) are duplicated, which is consistent with separate desktop and mobile blocks.
- **Coverage gap.** 52 links were skipped by the prober, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 10 images.**
  - The fallback text is the filename, so `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` are read out as link names.
  - It affects the 1400/750 pairs under `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/...` (`8149343_CLOC265P01`, `1368854001_202604_LB_0389.jpg`, `1349803001_202604_LB_0082.jpg`, `1365947001_202604_LB_0011.jpg`).
  - It also affects the tracking-redirect image (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`), which appears as an image with no alt twice.
  - Most of these images are wrapped in links, so the links have no accessible name.
  - If images are blocked, the link targets are unlabeled.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile image pairs.** The 1400px and 750px images are likely swapped with `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. Both variants lack alt, so assistive tech may announce both.
- **Invalid CSS in `.is-hidden-desktop`.** `line-height:auto!important` is not a valid value and is dropped.
- **Unsupported `@media` syntax.** `@media screen yahoo` is not valid media-query syntax, so that rule is ignored.
- **`@import` for web fonts.** The `<style>` block imports `fabric.hmgroup.com/.../fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`. Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, so those clients fall back to system fonts.
- The viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection` and `mso-hide` handling are present. No other rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.
