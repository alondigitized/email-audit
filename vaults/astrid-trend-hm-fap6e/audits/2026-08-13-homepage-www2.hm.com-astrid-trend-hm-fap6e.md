# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- I open the H&M app-site and immediately get walled off by a giant cookie consent modal — can't even see what's actually on the homepage. What I can glimpse around the edges (a soft slip-top editorial shot, a hint of sunglasses in a second image) says "trendy summer drop," but I literally cannot confirm that without tapping through legal text first. Feels like a brand that's more worried about GDPR compliance than getting me to shop in the first three seconds.

## What's working

- The nav bar (WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / HOME / BEAUTY) stays visible above the modal, so at least I know I can get to my section fast once I clear this thing
- The account, wishlist (heart), and bag icons are all right there top-right — good for me since I keep a wishlist
- What little hero image peeks through — soft, editorial, natural light — looks like it's speaking my aesthetic, not a stiff catalog shot
- The consent buttons themselves are legible and not tiny — "Accept All" and "Only Required Cookies" are both full-width and tappable

## What's weak

- The cookie modal takes up basically the entire visible screen — I can't see the hero headline, any offer, or a single CTA that's actually about shopping
- No loyalty/rewards callout anywhere I can see, and rewards balance is a big reason I keep coming back to this app
- Zero visible offer or price context — I see "$49.99" floating next to the model but no percent-off, no member price, nothing that tells me why today's a good day to buy
- The "MO..." text bottom-left is cut off by the modal, so even the one nav element trying to peek through is unreadable

## Recommendations

- Shrink the cookie modal to a bottom sheet or slim banner instead of a full-screen takeover — I should see the hero and at least one CTA before I have to make a privacy decision
- Put a Conscious/member callout in the header or a slim top strip so loyalty members like me see value immediately, not after three taps
- Surface the actual discount or "new drop" copy on the hero image itself, not just a price tag — "$49.99" alone doesn't sell me
- Once the modal's handled, make sure a rewards/points balance or "member exclusive" badge shows up without scrolling
