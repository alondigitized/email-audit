# Today only: 20% off in the app
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- H&M is running two messages in one send: the subject promises 20% off app orders, but the pre-header leads with a Stella McCartney member giveaway. A reader scanning their inbox gets two unrelated hooks simultaneously, and the body delivers the discount hero cleanly before pivoting into an app-onboarding tutorial. The "TODAY ONLY / 20% OFF APP ORDERS" headline lands well — clean hierarchy, strong spring product image — but the email then stalls.
- The biggest strategic failure is invisible unless you try to act on it: every single CTA in this email is a download button. "DOWNLOAD IOS" appears three separate times. For the majority of recipients on a member email list — people who already have the app — there is no path to shopping. The discount promise in the subject has no matching "Shop Now" button anywhere in the body.
- The lower two-thirds of the scroll is app-feature onboarding copy: Member Hub, App Exclusives, Scan & Pay, Visual Search. That content belongs in a welcome flow, not inside a "today only" promo. Urgency and education are incompatible roommates and education is losing.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is clean.** "TODAY ONLY / 20% OFF APP ORDERS" is the first thing your eye lands on after the logo — no ambiguity about the offer.
- **Subject line is tight and honest.** Thirty characters, concrete %, clear channel ("in the app"), zero spam signals.
- **Spring product image is current.** The red floral dress is on-season and makes the email feel like a live campaign, not a recycled template.
- **Loyalty benefits are surfaced.** The Member Hub block acknowledges points and rewards for subscribers who track them.
- **Email renders cleanly.** No broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage across the full scroll.

## What's weak

- **All CTAs are download buttons — the conversion path is broken for existing users.** Three instances of "DOWNLOAD IOS / ANDROID" and zero "Shop Now" or "Open in App" deeplinks.
- **Pre-header competes with, rather than amplifies, the subject.** The Stella McCartney giveaway teaser is a completely separate promotion — inbox readers get two unrelated pitches at once.
- **App-feature education mid-email kills urgency.** Scan & Pay and Visual Search tutorials have no place inside a "today only" time-pressure email.
- **The "Shop By" category grid at the bottom links to general categories, not to the discounted app inventory.** A 20% off hook should funnel into curated sale pages, not generic landing pages.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace every download CTA with a primary "Shop Now" button + a secondary "Don't have the app? Download here" fallback.** Deeplink existing users directly into the app's sale view; don't strand them.
- 2. **Strip the app-feature section entirely from this email.** Move Member Hub / Scan & Pay / Visual Search into a dedicated onboarding flow or a non-urgent send. Every scroll inch in a "today only" email should close toward a transaction.
- 3. **Align the pre-header to the 20% off offer.** The giveaway can live as a single small callout inside the body, not as the inbox teaser.
- 4. **Wire the "Shop By" tiles to filtered sale pages** so a click on WOMEN goes to women's items discounted in the app, not the women's homepage.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off everything in the app — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your H&M app exclusive: 20% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — opens automatically in the H&M app`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Women's, men's, kids', and home — 20% off until midnight`
