# The Campus Shop is now open
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- H&M is now two editorial sends in a row — yesterday was "Summer styles to wear on repeat," today it's a Back-to-Campus launch with a clean multi-department layout. After five straight days of 20%-off-sitewide hammer blows ending July 5, this pivot to concept-store storytelling makes sense as a palate cleanser. The execution is competent but diffuse: four separate editorial "shops" (BACK TO CAMPUS, COZY-CHIC, PLAY IT COOL, HOME COMFORTS) compete for attention without a single clear winner.
- The one actual offer — 15% off your next purchase when you donate clothing in-store — is buried well below the fold inside a Garment Collecting Programme callout. It's a genuinely interesting mechanism, but sandwiched between editorial sections, it reads as an afterthought rather than a reason to click. The preheader leak is the critical execution error: the "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING" banner at the top of the email becomes the inbox preview text, completely disconnecting what a subscriber reads in their inbox from what they get when they open.
- This email is pleasant and brand-appropriate, but it asks for clicks without giving a subscriber a sharp reason to act. It's a catalog page, not a campaign.

## What's working

- **Clean editorial layout** — four distinct shop sections each have a coherent visual theme; no cluttered imagery or layout breakage.
- **BTS timing is on-brand** — launching campus content in early July is normal retail calendar behavior, and H&M signals it clearly in the hero.
- **Garment Collecting Programme offer is specific** — 15% off with a clothing donation is a memorable and differentiated mechanic versus a generic percentage-off promo.
- **Render quality is excellent** — no broken images, overlapping elements, or placeholder text anywhere in the full render.
- **HOME COMFORTS section is a smart add** — rounding out campus as dorm living (not just apparel) broadens the relevance without straining the concept.

## What's weak

- **Preheader mismatch kills the open** — "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING" is the first text in the email and therefore the inbox preview. A subscriber sees a campus subject line paired with a tax-free-days preview — total non-sequitur.
- **No offer above the fold** — the 15% off Garment Collecting offer is buried halfway through a long scroll; the hero delivers zero financial incentive to click.
- **Four competing CTAs dilute intent** — WOMEN, MEN, HOME (hero), WOMEN (COZY-CHIC), HOME (HOME COMFORTS), LEARN MORE, FIND A STORE. There is no primary action.
- **"PLAY IT COOL" section has no CTA** — visible section header, no clickable button, dead end for engagement.
- **No urgency anywhere** — not even a soft "new arrivals" or "limited styles" signal; the campus shop feels like it'll always be open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — add a dedicated preheader text element before the TAX-FREE DAYS banner so the inbox preview complements the subject. Something like: "New campus fits for women, men & home — fresh arrivals just landed." This is a one-line HTML fix with measurable open-rate upside.
- 2. **Move the Garment Collecting 15% offer above the fold** — either in the hero subhead or as a slim banner below navigation. The offer is good; hiding it is waste.
- 3. **Add a CTA to PLAY IT COOL** — currently a dead-end section with no action.
- 4. **Collapse to two sections max** — BACK TO CAMPUS (apparel) + HOME COMFORTS. COZY-CHIC and PLAY IT COOL dilute what "campus" means and scatter attention.
- 5. **Introduce a soft deadline** — even "New arrivals — while styles last" creates pull that this email currently lacks entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New drops: The Campus Shop is live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Campus fits + 15% off when you donate`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Women, men & home — fresh back-to-school styles just landed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bring in old clothes, get 15% off your next campus buy`
