# 25% off flash sale starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward Black Friday early-access flash sale, and the offer is clear. "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" is the headline, set in bold type under a moody leather-jacket hero. The subject promises a flash sale and the body delivers one. Compared with the 11/01 "Today only: 20% off in the app" send, the offer is bigger and no longer needs an app install.
- The email then muddles itself. The hero text reads "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" in red over a dark jacket, and the contrast is poor. A black banner at the top pushes a different offer, "Become a member for exclusive Black Friday access + 10% off your first purchase." Further down, a black block advertises "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE." That makes three competing offers in one email: 25% online, 30% in-store, and 10% for new members. The in-store block also has dim red text on black, and the CTA is nearly unreadable.
- Overall it's an average promo. The online offer is concrete and time-bound, but the layout buries it and the legal copy is huge. A reader who skims will not be sure which discount applies to them.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" headline is bold and centered, with a credible same-day deadline.
- Paired "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE" buttons sit directly under the offer.
- The category grid (Women, Men, Baby, Kids, Home, Sport) gives easy entry points to shop.
- The subject matches the body, and the sale is timely for early November, following the 11/02 ski and 11/03 NYC sends.

## What's weak

- The red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" text on the dark hero is hard to read. The actual 25% offer isn't in the hero at all.
- Three overlapping offers (25% online, 30% in-store, 10% member) dilute the message.
- The in-store block uses dark red text on black, including a barely visible "FIND A STORE" button.
- The member banner at the top competes with the main offer before the reader reaches the hero.
- The fine print is a very long wall of text, and the exclusions are only visible there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" in the hero image, in high-contrast white or black type, and drop the red-on-dark text.
- 2. Cut the in-store 30% block, or move it to a separate send. Keep one offer per email.
- 3. Move the member-signup banner below the offer, or remove it.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the black block: use white or bright text and a visible button.
- 5. Replace the category photos with a few hero products, with prices or "from" prices, so there are more specific things to click.
- 6. Cut the legal block to a short summary with a link to full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off ends tonight at midnight ET`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: 25% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Women, men, kids and home. Online only, until midnight ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the early Black Friday deals before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is mostly sound. However, all 12 probed tracking redirects returned 403, the `List-Unsubscribe` and authentication headers could not be seen, and the linked images have no alt text. The 403s and missing headers are probably artifacts of the probe and the relay, not real failures, and I've marked them as unverified below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They are all on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=he04147e6-…&did=1419177062&rid=3326930539…`. This covers "Shop now", "Find a store", the logo link ("H&M"), the image links and Unsubscribe.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not broken links.** Every probe failed, including links an ESP would not leave dead, which points to the click-tracker's WAF rejecting the QA client. Retry with a browser-like UA or through a real client before calling any of these broken.
- **Coverage is partial.** 58 links were skipped, so the footer, social and utility links are untested.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8ac2ffa`, `…f8ac2ffc`) have anchor text prefixed with zero-width/BOM characters (`"‌  ﻿Shop now"`). These are padding hacks that can break text-based link matching and be read oddly by screen readers.
- **Duplicate destinations per CTA.** "Shop now" and "Find a store" each appear as separate redirect IDs (`f8ac301c`/`301d`/`3032`/`3033`). This is probably desktop/mobile duplication (see the `is-hidden-mobile` and `is-hidden-desktop` classes in the CSS). It doubles the link count and splits click attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images have no accessible name.** Image links report link text `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` and `[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]` (redirects `…f8ac301a` and `…f8ac301b`). This means `alt` is missing or empty on images wrapped in `<a>`.
- **Alt text is missing across the hero and product images.** The sources are `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-45/us158_s4/…`, for example `1356657003_202604_LB_2658.jpg` and `1341533005_202604_LB_3484.jpg`. The 1400px and 750px variants are paired, so each image is in the DOM twice and each copy is missing alt. That adds up to 16 alt warnings.
- **An image on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` also lacks alt.** This is likely a tracking pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
- **The JSON-LD block is mostly empty.** The schema.org script has `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `PromotionCard` `"url":""` and `"image":""`. It also has a stale `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`. Gmail may ignore or reject this markup, and the template defaults look unpopulated.
- **Webfonts load through `@import`.** The two `@import url(...)` rules (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`) are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The source was truncated, so I can't confirm the fallback font stacks.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 522 of 600 characters are URLs (>70%), so the text part is not useful and may hurt spam scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) are visible in the truncated source. The per-recipient `rid`/`erid` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unknown, and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed.
- **In-body unsubscribe exists.** It is a tracked link with `p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`, but it returned 403 to the probe. Treat it as unverified, not broken.
- **No physical address was visible** in the truncated source. This is not confirmed missing.

Re-test these headers against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox, not through the relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Destinations can't be checked.** Every probed link is wrapped in a `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirect, and the probe stopped at the 403. The final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page content were never reached.
- **The only campaign identifiers are in the redirect query:** `p1=DM6193636` and `p3=20261104`. No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Whether they are added at redirect time is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracking links with a realistic UA or from a whitelisted IP. Follow the redirects to verify final URLs, UTM parameters and landing pages, including the unsubscribe flow.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the linked hero and product images, with `alt=""` on the tracking pixel. Replace the paired 1400px and 750px images with a single responsive `<img>` or `<picture>`, or add alt to both.
3. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block, and fix the stale 2018 `availabilityStarts`.
4. Remove the zero-width and BOM characters from CTA anchor text.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the raw message. Add the one-click headers if they are missing.
6. Improve the plain-text part with readable copy, not URLs alone.
7. Add web-safe fallbacks for the `@import` fonts if none exist in the full source.
