# Last day: Up to 20% off your order
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same tiered-discount play H&M ran on 8/15 ("15% or 20% off all weekend"), just recut with "LAST DAY" urgency stapled on top. The offer block is identical in structure and nearly identical in size — "15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100" in the same bold black type. Nothing about the creative signals this is genuinely the final day of anything; it reads like the same asset with a new headline swapped in, which is a problem given the brand history shows this exact tiered offer just ran yesterday.
- Below the offer it's standard H&M grid: new-arrival product tiles (sweaters, jackets, a teddy-bear sweatshirt) at full individual prices, a "SHOP BY" category strip, then the footer. It's clean and bug-free, but it's fall-transition merchandising bolted onto a "last day" urgency subject — the mismatch between "buy this right now, today only" and "here's a lookbook of new arrivals" undercuts the urgency the subject is selling.
- Verdict: functional, on-brand, no render issues, but it's cadence fatigue in action — same offer two days running with a manufactured deadline slapped on. Middling send, not a standout.

## What's working

- Offer is unambiguous and prominent: "LAST DAY / 15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100" is the first thing the eye hits, in large bold type with no scrolling required.
- Clear category navigation immediately below the offer (Women/Men/Kids/Home buttons) gives an instant path to shop.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is clean and consistently spaced.
- Product tiles show price and "New Arrival" tags consistently, which is useful at-a-glance info.

## What's weak

- "LAST DAY" urgency is undercut by the fact that this is the identical 15%/20% tiered offer from the 8/15 send — no visual or copy signal that this deadline is real versus recycled.
- No countdown, expiring-hours callout, or any device that makes "last day" feel credible beyond the words themselves.
- The product grid below the offer (sweaters, trench coats, a teddy sweatshirt) is generic new-arrival merchandising, not urgency-reinforcing "shop before it's gone" picks — disconnect between hero message and body content.
- Preview/preheader isn't visible in the render, so there's a missed chance to reinforce urgency before the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "last day" feel earned — add a specific cutoff time ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") rather than a bare "LAST DAY" label; a real deadline is the single biggest lever for urgency-driven opens and clicks.
- 2. Swap the generic new-arrival grid for a "shop the sale" curated set — items that visibly benefit from the 15%/20% break, ideally showing strikethrough pricing.
- 3. Vary the creative from 8/15 — if this is genuinely a new deadline, the visual treatment should look different from the prior send or subscribers will register it as a repeat and tune out.
- 4. Add a subtotal-progress cue near the tiers ("spend $XX more to unlock 20%") to nudge basket size — currently the two tiers are just stated, not made actionable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 20% off $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — up to 20% off before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your cart: $75 gets 15% off, $100 gets 20%`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours to save on fall essentials`
