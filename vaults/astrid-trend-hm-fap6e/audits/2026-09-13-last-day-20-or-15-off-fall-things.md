# Last day: 20% or 15% off fall things
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "15% off $75 or 20% off $100" tiered offer H&M ran yesterday (09/12), just recut with "LAST DAY" urgency and a fall-outerwear focus — bomber jackets, leather looks, trenches. It's honest urgency this time: the fine print confirms the deal expires 09/13/2026, i.e. today, so unlike some of H&M's stretched "last chance" framing this one is real. The hero jacket, product grid (funnel-collar twill, scuba blouson, stand-up collar wool jacket, trench, kids denim jacket), and gender/age segment buttons are all clean and legible with prices visible throughout.
- The problem is repetition fatigue: this is the third send in four days built around the same 15%/20% tiered discount (09/10 editorial break aside), just with a new "LAST DAY" wrapper. A subscriber who opened yesterday's near-identical email will recognize this instantly, which caps both open and click upside even though the execution itself is clean.

## What's working

- Genuine urgency — "LAST DAY" plus fine print confirming a same-day (09/13) expiration, not a stretched deadline.
- Clear tiered offer stated in large type directly under the hero image: "15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100."
- Segment buttons (Women, Men, Baby, Kids 2-8Y, Kids 9-14Y) make it easy for a shopper to self-select.
- Product grid shows real prices ($49.99, $69.99, $44.99) with no lorem-ipsum or broken images.
- Fall category framing ("bomber jackets, leather looks and timeless trenches") is seasonally on-point for mid-September.

## What's weak

- Near-identical offer to yesterday's 09/12 "15% off or 20% off all weekend" send — this is the second consecutive day running the exact same discount tiers, just with new urgency copy slapped on.
- No single hero product or price is tied to the CTA — "SHOP NOW" at top routes to a general destination, not the jacket shown.
- Subject line buries the actual numbers behind "or" phrasing ("20% or 15% off") that's harder to parse at a glance than yesterday's clearer ordering.
- Preview text isn't visible/extractable from the render, so the inbox likely shows boilerplate rather than reinforcing copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with urgency + the bigger number first, since deadline-driven subjects outperform buried tiers: "Ends tonight: up to 20% off fall outerwear" reads faster than "20% or 15% off fall things."
- 2. Add a visible countdown or time-of-day cutoff (e.g., "ends 11:59pm ET") in the hero band — turns "LAST DAY" from a claim into a concrete deadline that drives urgency.
- 3. Link the hero image directly to the featured jacket product page instead of a generic "SHOP NOW," so the strongest visual asset in the email has a matching CTA.
- 4. Vary the creative structure from 09/12 even if the offer is genuinely the same — new hero shot, reordered grid, or different lead category — so repeat subscribers don't instantly disengage.
