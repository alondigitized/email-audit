# The Fall Event: Up to 40% off sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's biggest offer of the week: "Up to 40% off sitewide," framed as "The Fall Event." The hero is a brown knit cardigan on a model, with red "THE FALL EVENT" text laid over it. Below it, a bold "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline sits over two buttons, SHOP NOW and FIND A STORE. The offer is clear and the layout is clean. It is also the strongest creative H&M has sent since the 10/2 "Up to 30% off" email.
- The execution undercuts the offer. The red event title sits on a busy brown knit and is hard to read. The "40%" appears only below the hero, so the first screen shows no number. The four category blocks (Women, Men, Baby/Kids, Home) each link to a whole department, not a product. There are no prices, no sale tags and no product detail anywhere. For a sitewide sale, the email never shows what a discount looks like on a real item. The footer fine print says "up to," excludes sale items, furniture and lamps, and ends 10/13, but none of that is visible above the fold.
- On cadence, this follows a week of "20% off for Members" and "up to 30% off," then two no-offer editorial sends (10/6 and 10/7). A step up to 40% is a credible escalation and it reads as a real event. It is a solid send that could have been a strong one.

## What's working

- The offer is specific and an escalation. 40% beats the 30% and 20% sends earlier in the week.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" headline is big, black and unmissable once you reach it.
- The four category blocks (women, men, kids, home) are easy to scan and cover most segments.
- The model photography is consistent and fits the fall season.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The red "THE FALL EVENT" text on the brown cardigan has weak contrast. The hero never mentions 40%.
- There are no products, prices or sale tags. Each section is a lifestyle image plus a department button.
- The end date (10/13) and the exclusions appear only in the footer. The body copy has a bare asterisk ("savings of the season.*") with no visible deadline.
- The CTAs are generic, such as "SHOP NOW", "WOMEN" and "MEN". The two-button hero (SHOP NOW and FIND A STORE) splits attention away from the offer.
- Nothing speaks to loyalty. There is no member benefit despite the recent member-gated sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "40% OFF" in the hero image or directly under it, in high-contrast type. Add the end date, for example "Ends 10/13."
- 2. Replace some category blocks with 4-6 product tiles showing the original price struck through and the sale price in red. The 10/2 send did this well.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific to the department: "Shop women's cardigans," "Shop men's jackets."
- 4. Remove FIND A STORE from the hero and keep one primary CTA.
- 5. Add a line for members, such as "Members: extra Bonus Reward," so the loyalty angle is visible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off sitewide, ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Fall Event: cardigans, coats & more up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Knits, jackets and kidswear, now through 10/13. Plus a $5 Member reward.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sitewide sale ends in 5 days. Shop women's, men's, kids' and home.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, responsive email with a wrapped click-tracker (`t19.email.hm.com/r/`). The automated QA found many missing image `alt` attributes, an empty JSON-LD schema block, and 12 of 12 probed tracking links returning 403. The 403s most likely come from the tracker rejecting the QA probe, not from the links being broken for recipients, but that is unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They include "Shop now" (`...f864df73`, `...f864df75`, `...f864df51`, `...f864df53`), "Find a store" (`...f864df74`, `...f864df76`), the "H&M" logo (`...f864df50`), the image links (`...f864df71`, `...f864df72`, `...f864df77`, `...f864df78`) and "Unsubscribe" (`...f864dfb3`).
  - Every one is on the same host and path, `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=he8f62d61-...`. A uniform 403 across the CTAs, images and the unsubscribe link points to bot or UA blocking on the redirector.
  - A real user may be unaffected. Re-test with a browser-like UA before treating these as defects.
  - Destination URLs were never resolved, so I can't confirm where any link lands.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 63 were skipped. Footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Link text.** The "Shop now" anchors for `f864df51` and `f864df53` contain zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿`) before the text. This is a common preheader/padding trick, but it adds noise to the accessible name.
- **Duplicate link IDs.** Each image and its CTA have separate IDs (`f864df71` vs `f864df73`, and so on), so the same destination is tracked as separate links. This is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 20 images.** The QA flagged `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs for each product tile, all from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-41/...`. With no `alt`, clients fall back to the filename, shown as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. This is the `<img>` source behind the link text in the QA output.
  - The 1400px and 750px pairs reflect a desktop/mobile image swap using `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. With no `alt`, screen readers can announce both versions.
  - The linked logo image `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=he8f62d61-...,1` also has no `alt`. This is the "H&M" link, so that link has no accessible name apart from the text fallback.
- **Remote fonts.** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css)` are inside `<style>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore or strip these, so fallback font stacks matter. The font stacks are cut off in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.
- **Dark-mode and Outlook support** can't be assessed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The tracking URLs carry per-recipient parameters: `rid=3326930539`, with `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` as its base64 form, plus `p2=0982dd0a...`. These are populated, not broken. The unsubscribe URL adds `p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`. The source is truncated, so the body copy wasn't checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers upstream.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com`, and the tracking host `t19.email.hm.com` is on the same organizational domain, which is good for alignment. This couldn't be verified.
- **In-body unsubscribe link is present** (`...f864dfb3`, with `p4`/`p5`). It returned 403 to the probe, which is probably the same bot-blocking as above. Verify that it resolves for a real user.
- **Physical postal address and sender identification** are not visible in the truncated source and couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are wrapped in the ESP redirector, so no UTM parameters are visible in the email. Any UTMs would be appended server-side after the redirect, and this audit couldn't observe that.
- The `403` responses prevented landing-page checks entirely. Whether the "Shop now" targets match "Up to 40% off sitewide" is unverified.
- **JSON-LD block is empty or stale.** In the `<script type="application/ld+json">`, `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"url":""` and `"image":""` are all blank. `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` is a stale default. This is a leftover template schema.
  - Gmail's promotion annotations (`DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard`) will not render. The blank `subjectLine` could also trigger markup warnings.
- **Plain-text part.** 522 of 600 characters are URLs (over 70%), which can look spammy to filters. The text part is mostly long tracking URLs with little readable copy.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 12 tracking links with a browser-like UA or from a different IP, and manually verify the unsubscribe link (`...f864dfb3`) resolves for a real user.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product images. Use `alt=""` on the duplicate mobile/desktop variant so screen readers don't announce both. Add `alt="H&M"` to the logo link.
3. Populate the JSON-LD (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts/Ends`, `url`, `image`) or remove the block. Update the 2018 `availabilityStarts` placeholder.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment upstream of the relay.
5. Improve the plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw URLs, so the URL share is well under 70%.
6. Strip the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" link text. Keep them in the preheader only.
7. Expand probe coverage beyond the 12 links, and resolve the redirects to confirm the final landing URLs and UTM parameters.
