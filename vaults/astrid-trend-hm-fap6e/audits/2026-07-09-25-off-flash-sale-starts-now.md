# 25% off flash sale starts now
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- H&M has escalated to its deepest discount of the July run — 25% off, up from the 20% that anchored the July 4th week — and attached a real midnight deadline to create genuine urgency. After yesterday's downmarket price-drop email ("$6.99") and two editorial sends before that, this feels like a measured gear-shift rather than discount fatigue noise. The flash sale framing is the right call here.
- The execution is clean but not sharp. The hero does the heavy lifting — "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" is impossible to miss — but the email immediately collapses into a 6-category navigation grid with no single CTA button and no featured product. The discount creates the impulse; the body doesn't channel it anywhere specific, which bleeds click energy.
- Overall verdict: well-timed, well-headlined, undercooked in the body. The offer earns the open; the execution only partially converts it.

## What's working

- **The hero is locked:** "FLASH SALE" badge + "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" is the first thing the eye lands on — no ambiguity about what the email is or when it ends.
- **Deadline is credible:** A specific timezone (ET) on a midnight cutoff is more trustworthy than "limited time" vagueness.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs:** The category grid is crisp, images load, typography doesn't overlap.
- **Discount escalation is meaningful:** 25% is the biggest offer in this week's run, which gives the email genuine newsworthiness vs. prior sends.
- **Lifestyle imagery carries department identity:** The WOMEN, MEN, KIDS, HOME thumbnail blocks give visual variety without clutter.

## What's weak

- **No single CTA button:** The primary action is clicking a category tile labeled only "WOMEN" or "MEN" — there's no "Shop 25% Off" button that aggregates the offer to a landing page.
- **No featured product:** Unlike the July 5th close (which hero'd a blue-print summer dress), this email has no anchor item — nothing to scroll back to, nothing to screenshot for later.
- **Loyalty member benefits buried:** Whether the 25% stacks with member pricing isn't visible in the body — it's footnoted. H&M's July 5th send made this a feature; this one hides it.
- **"Up to 70% off" sub-copy undercuts the lead:** Mixing the flash sale discount with the always-on 70% sale language dilutes message clarity.
- **Category tiles lack sale context:** Nothing on the tiles says "25% off" — a shopper jumping in mid-scroll loses the offer thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a single full-width "Shop 25% Off" CTA button immediately below the headline** — before the category grid, not instead of it. Every H&M flash sale email should have one exit ramp that goes to a filtered sale page.
- 2. **Drop "always up to 70% off" from the subheadline** — it dilutes the flash sale urgency. Keep it in fine print.
- 3. **Badge each category tile with the offer** — "WOMEN — 25% OFF" on the tile copy pulls the discount down the scroll path.
- 4. **Surface one hero product** — even one editorial item (like the dress on July 5th) gives the email a reason to exist beyond the discount.
- 5. **Make member stacking visible** — if H&M members get an additional benefit, put it in a one-liner between the headline and the grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash sale: 25% off everything — midnight tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Biggest discount of the week. Shop before the clock hits 12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: your extra savings are in. Sale ends midnight ET.`

## Technical Audit — H&M Flash Sale Email

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound for major clients (Outlook, Apple Mail, Gmail) with appropriate meta tags and MSO fallbacks, but the JSON-LD structured data block contains stale/unpopulated fields that will suppress Gmail Promotions tab annotations, and `@import` font loading is blocked by most email clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
**Cannot fully assess** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. From what is visible:
- No UTM parameters are confirmed present or absent on any CTA yet.
- The `PromotionCard` schema block has `"url":""` (empty string) — if this is not populated server-side before send, Gmail's native promo card will fail to render a deep link.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
**`@import` font loading will be stripped**
```css
@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/0/northstar/css/fonts.css);
@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css);
```
Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo all strip `@import` inside `<style>` tags. These imports will silently fail; confirm system font fallbacks are defined.

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
```html
<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">
```
Missing `lang="en-US"` (or locale equivalent). Required for screen readers and affects spell-check behavior in some clients.

**Duplicate CSS rules in mobile breakpoint** — `.cta-link-mobile-border-ns`, `.set_font_interactive_standalone_link_default`, and `.set_font_heading_xlarge` are each declared twice within the same `@media (max-width:699px)` block. No functional breakage, but adds parse overhead and is a maintenance flag for the template.

**`#MessageViewBody` selector** — Present for Outlook Web App targeting. Confirm this isn't the only path to show/hide `.hide-webkit` / `.show-webkit-table` elements; Gmail ignores `#MessageViewBody`.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Stale date in JSON-LD DiscountOffer**
```json
"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"
```
This is an 8-year-old hardcoded timestamp. Either a copy-paste artifact or an unfilled template slot. It will not cause a delivery failure, but it suppresses correct Gmail Promotions tab rendering.

**Multiple empty JSON-LD fields** — `"subjectLine":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""`, `"url":""` in the DiscountOffer and PromotionCard blocks. Any field left as an empty string rather than omitted signals an unpopulated template to inbox providers.

Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM physical address or one-click List-Unsubscribe header (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) from available source.
