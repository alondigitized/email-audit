# Up to 20% off = cardi party
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-photographed cardigan/knitwear push with a legible tiered offer — "15% off $75 or 20% off $100" — riding the same weekend-deal structure H&M used on 09-20 ("20% off + free shipping ends tonight") and 09-25 ("15% off or 20% off all weekend"). The hero shot (pale blue cardigan) is genuinely nice, and the product grid below shows real prices including a markdown ($12.49, was ~$17.99) which is a good trust signal. But the offer is copy-pasted from the prior weekend send almost verbatim, just with cardigans as the new wrapper — this is the third near-identical "tiered % off" structure this brand has run in a week.
- The subject line "Up to 20% off = cardi party" is the weakest part of the send — playful but vague, buries the real offer (15%/$75 or 20%/$100), and "cardi party" reads as trying too hard rather than clear value communication. Overall this is a mid-pack, competently produced but derivative email: solid product photography, honest pricing, but zero freshness in the offer mechanic and a subject that undersells the deal.

## What's working

- Hero image is a strong, clean full-bleed cardigan shot with clear model styling — no render bugs visible.
- Offer block ("15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100") is large, bold, and unmissable directly under the hero.
- Real prices shown on every product tile, including a visible markdown ($12.49 crossed out from ~$17.99) — no vague "shop now and see."
- Clean "Shop By" department grid (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) gives easy navigation for non-cardigan shoppers.
- Single clear CTA button ("SHOP NOW") repeated at a sensible spot, not cluttered with multiple competing buttons.

## What's weak

- Subject line references "cardi party" but the actual tiered discount is the real offer — mismatched emphasis between subject and hero.
- This is the third tiered/percent-off structural twin this week (09-20, 09-25) — cadence fatigue is real for a subscriber who's opened those.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the body (the word "weekend" appears but no explicit end date/time), weaker than the 09-20 send's "ends tonight" framing.
- No social proof, reviews, or fit/sizing guidance visible on any product tile.
- Top nav is minimal (just "SHOP NOW" / "SALE") — no persona/category signal for men vs. women vs. kids until scrolling deep.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/offer mismatch — lead with the number, not the pun. The tiered discount is the strongest, clearest asset in this email; hide it behind wordplay and you lose scanners.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the body copy — "weekend" alone (with no date) is weaker than competitors' "ends tonight." Even one line like "Ends Sunday 11:59pm" would lift urgency.
- 3. Vary the creative mechanic from the last two sends — three tiered-discount emails in a week trains subscribers to skim past them. Swap in a single flat discount or a BOGO next time to break the pattern.
- 4. Add sizing/fit context on at least the hero product ("True to size" or "Runs small") — knitwear fit uncertainty is a known cart-abandonment driver.
