# Last day: 20% off + free shipping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's clearest, most complete "last day" urgency send in the run — "LAST DAY, 20% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" sits right at the top with no ambiguity, backed by category nav buttons (Women, Men, Baby, Kids, Home) right under the offer. It's essentially the same offer architecture as 8/22's sweater email and the 8/21 "Weekend deal" send, just relabeled with harder deadline language — which makes this the fourth 20%-off-flavored email in about a week.
- The body itself is a wall of merchandise: For Her, For Him, For Them, For The Home, each with a "SHOP NOW" link and eight products with prices and "New Arrival" tags, but the discount is never re-anchored to any of the individual products — you have to remember the $125 threshold from the hero as you scroll. No lifestyle hero image, no model-driven emotional pull, just grid after grid of product photography.
- It's a solid, honest, on-brand execution of a real threshold discount, but the cadence problem is real: this is a near-identical offer structure to two of the last three sends, and "last day" urgency loses credibility fast when the brand runs discount email after discount email.

## What's working

- Hero copy is unambiguous: "20% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" with "LAST DAY" urgency directly above it.
- Category shortcut buttons (Women, Men, Baby, Kids, Home) let a reader jump straight to their segment without scrolling.
- Clean, consistent grid layout — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the render.
- Good product coverage across four life categories (Her, Him, Them, Home) with visible price and "New Arrival" tags on every item.
- "SHOP BY" footer section reinforces navigation with a second visual category picker.

## What's weak

- The $125 minimum-spend threshold is buried in small subtext under the headline — most shoppers will miss it's not a blanket 20% off.
- No single hero product or lifestyle shot to anchor the eye — it's grid-first from the very top, which weakens visual hierarchy.
- "Last day" urgency is undercut by brand history: this is the fourth 20%-off-style send in roughly a week (8/15, 8/16, 8/19, 8/21, now 8/23), so the deadline reads as recycled rather than credible.
- No loyalty/member pricing or benefit shown despite H&M having a membership program.
- Product names are visually truncated ("RUFFLE-TRIMM...", "MOTIF-DETAIL BRUSHED-INS...", "POINTELLE-KN...") — a small but noticeable render/polish miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Restate "$125" and "FREE SHIPPING" as bold, same-size text as "20% OFF" — right now the qualifier reads like fine print, and shoppers will bounce when the fine print surprises them at checkout.
- 2. Add one true hero image (single outfit or lifestyle shot) above the category grid to give the eye somewhere to land before the wall of product tiles starts.
- 3. Fix truncated product names in the grid — either shorten the copy to fit or widen the text container.
- 4. Space out "last day" sends — five 20%-off-flavored emails in eight days trains subscribers to wait for the next one instead of acting on today's deadline.
- 5. Surface loyalty member pricing or a small member-exclusive badge if available, since it's absent here and would differentiate this from H&M's default promo cadence.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — 20% off orders $125+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop new arrivals in Women, Men, Kids & Home before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart, 20% lighter — today only`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails to render as a functional message: all 12 probed links (CTA, nav, and unsubscribe) return HTTP 403, and List-Unsubscribe headers are absent.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed links return **403 Broken link** errors, including every "Shop now" CTA and the H&M/Women/Men/Baby/Kids nav links. All routes through the ESP redirector `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h6cec96c5-8769-4805-9229-a273c7bb3d05,663e7a4e,...&did=1715370574&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM5971094&p2=...&p3=20260823` — the shared `id`/`did`/`rid` prefix across all links suggests the redirector itself is rejecting requests (bot/UA blocking or an expired/invalid campaign token), not that individual destination URLs are broken.
- The Unsubscribe link (`...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`) also 403s — this is a compliance-relevant failure, not just a UX one (see §5).
- Two anchors use zero-width/invisible characters in link text (`‌  ﻿Shop now`, containing U+200C/U+FEFF), likely artifacts of dynamic content insertion; harmless to rendering but worth noting as source hygiene.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 32 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including all product images (`image.hm.com/assets/hm/...`) at both 344px and 322px breakpoints, plus tracking pixels. Screen readers and images-blocked clients will show no fallback content.
- Extensive Yahoo/Gmail/Outlook conditional CSS (`#MessageViewBody`, `mso-hide`, `@media screen yahoo`) and mobile-responsive rules (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.image-mobile`) are present — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source itself.
