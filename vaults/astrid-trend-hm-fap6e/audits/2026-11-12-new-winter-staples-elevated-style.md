# New winter staples, elevated style
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a Member-gated Black Friday preview dressed up as a winter editorial. The offer is in the black banner at the top: "EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW: UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS*". Below it, the black-and-white hero of a woman in a black coat and the "WINTER STAPLES" headline pull the eye away from the discount. The email looks good, but it undersells the one thing that moves a click.
- The featured products also don't support the headline. The coat goes from $279 to $222.99, the jacket from $89.99 to $71.99, and the turtlenecks and jumper show about the same 20% cut. Nothing on screen shows 40%. The asterisk and the dense fine print block at the bottom make the promise feel soft. The subject line is generic and says nothing about the offer, which is a step back from yesterday's "Up to 40% off everything! Black Friday starts now for Members".
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand product showcase with a weak offer-to-product match and a subject line that gives up the best hook. It's an average send, about 6/10.

## What's working

- The top banner states the Member offer clearly, in red on black, with a Join-now fallback for non-members ("get 10% off on top").
- Member prices in red under each bold regular price make the saving easy to read. Examples are $279 → $222.99 and $59.99 → $47.99.
- The hero and the "BLACK FRIDAY MEMBER PREVIEW / WINTER STAPLES" headline fit the season. The copy ("tailored coats that bring together comfort, warmth, and timeless style") stays on one topic.
- The product tiles carry useful detail: the H&M Premium Selection label, "New Arrival" tags, fabric cues (wool-blend, merino) and item names.
- The layout is clean, with a single outlined SHOP NOW button, a SHOP BY category row at the end, and no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- The 40% headline isn't backed by any featured item. Every product shows roughly 20% off, so "up to 40%" feels like bait, especially with the asterisk and the long fine print.
- The subject line ("New winter staples, elevated style") leaves out Black Friday, the Member offer and any deadline. The best hook is wasted.
- The only CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" in a thin outlined button under a large hero. It's low-contrast next to the red-on-black banner.
- The product section is visually uneven. The coat tile is full-width, the jacket tile is a narrower centered image, and the rest are 2-up. There's also a large empty gap before "SHOP BY".
- The fine print runs to a long, dense block. The real deadline ("through 11/16/2026") is only in that block, so there's no visible urgency.
- It's the fifth or sixth H&M send in about a week (11/06, 11/07, 11/08, 11/10 ×2, 11/11). The Member 40% message repeats yesterday's send, which risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer in the hero. Put "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" in large type on or under the hero image. Add a visible end date ("Ends 11/16") next to it.
- 2. Show the 40% claim. Feature at least one product that is actually 40% off, or change the headline to the discount the tiles really show, such as "20% off Member prices". Make the message and the products match.
- 3. Make the CTA stronger and specific. Use a filled black button with copy like "Shop Member coats" or "Shop the Member preview". Repeat it after the product grid.
- 4. Tighten the product grid into a consistent 2-up layout with equal tile sizes, and remove the dead whitespace before "SHOP BY".
- 5. Move the key terms (end date, Member sign-in requirement) into a short visible line above the fold. Leave the full legal text at the bottom.
- 6. Space out the Member sends, or give this one a different angle such as coats or gifting, so it isn't a near-repeat of the 11/11 send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 403 probe failures all hit H&M's `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector, so they are probably bot-blocking and not dead links. That means link health is unverified. The confirmed code-level issues are widespread missing image alt text, empty JSON-LD schema fields, and a link-heavy plain-text part. Compliance headers could not be assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** These include the primary "SHOP NOW" CTAs (`...f8bec048`, `...f8bec049`), the hero image links (`...f8bec046`, `...f8bec047`), the logo link (`...f8bec03b`) and the unsubscribe link (`...f8bec095`). Every URL has the same shape: `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hae83966e-...&did=1427379252&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM6217332&p2=...&p3=20261112`.
- **The uniform 403 points to the ESP's bot or WAF filtering of non-browser probes.** It does not point to individually broken links. The probe could not confirm that any link resolves, so real-user behavior is unverified. I am not treating this as a confirmed outage.
- **Every link is an opaque redirect.** Final destinations cannot be checked from the source or the QA data.
- **Only 12 of 89 links were probed** (77 skipped), so coverage is partial.
- **Several links have zero-width characters in their text.** The "Shop now" link text is `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which contains ZWNJ and BOM characters. This is likely preheader or spacing padding that leaked into anchor text, and it can affect screen readers and click-text analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images are missing alt text (confirmed, 17 warnings):**
  - Hero images `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, repeated three times each (desktop and mobile variants).
  - Product images `64065ec5...jpg`, `65729a94...jpg`, `28503191...jpg` and `70d1d75e...jpg`, each at `?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`.
  - Two images wrapped by `t19.email.hm.com/r/...` links.
- **Image-only links have no accessible name.** The hero image links (`f8bec046`, `f8bec047`, `f8bec04a`, `f8bec04b`, `f8bec04e`) fall back to the filename (`[image: cq5dam.web...]`) or nothing when images are blocked.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile image variants** (1400px and 750px) are used with `is-hidden-mobile` / `is-hidden-desktop` classes. Both sets are in the DOM, and screen readers may read both if `display:none` isn't honored.
- **Fonts are loaded with `@import`** (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`) in a `<style>` block. Gmail and some clients strip these, so the web fonts fall back.
- **The viewport meta tag is minimal** (`width=device-width`, no `initial-scale`). This is a minor point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source or QA data. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.
- **The JSON-LD block has empty fields:** `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, PromotionCard `"image":""` and `"url":""`. `availabilityStarts` is a stale `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00` default. This is template boilerplate that was never populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **The `Authentication-Results` header was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation.
- **A visible "Unsubscribe" link is present**, but it is tracked through `t19.email.hm.com/r/...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...` and returned 403 to the probe. Verify that it works in a real browser.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **The `p1`/`p2`/`p3` parameters** are `DM6217332`, a hash, and `20261112` (the send date). They are internal tracking IDs. Any UTMs would be appended after the redirect.
- **The plain-text part is 516 of 594 characters URLs (87%).** This is a deliverability and readability issue. The text version is essentially a list of tracking links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe a sample of the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links** with a real browser User-Agent or headless browser. This will separate WAF blocking from real failures, and should include the unsubscribe link.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text to all content images.** Use `alt=""` for purely decorative ones. Give image-only links descriptive alt text so the links have an accessible name.
3. **Strip the zero-width and BOM characters** from anchor text ("Shop now").

5. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** against the raw message headers, not the relay copy, along with SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
