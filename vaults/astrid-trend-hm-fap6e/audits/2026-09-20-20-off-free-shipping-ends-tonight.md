# 20% off + free shipping ends tonight
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is functionally the same email as the 2026-09-19 "Weekend deal: 20% off + free shipping" send — same "20% off $125 + free shipping" banner, same hero sweater model, same four-category shop-by-department layout. The only real change is the subject line adding "ends tonight" urgency and the offer minimum shifting focus. That's two nearly-identical sends back to back with the same threshold offer, which starts to feel like the brand is just extending a deal rather than running a fresh campaign.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, clear hero banner, a legible four-category nav (Women/Men/Kids/Home), and dense but orderly product grids underneath. Fine print about the $125 minimum and gift-card exclusions is visible below the fold rather than hidden, which is honest. But there's no on-brand urgency treatment near the hero itself — "ends tonight" only lives in the subject line, not in the body — so once opened, the time pressure evaporates.
- Judgement: a competent, low-risk template execution that's let down by an obvious back-to-back-promo cadence issue and a missed opportunity to carry urgency into the hero creative.

## What's working

- Hero banner clearly states the concrete offer: "20% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING."
- Clean four-way category nav (Women/Men/Kids/Home) right under the hero for fast navigation.
- No render bugs — all product photography and category tiles load correctly.
- Fine print (minimum spend, exclusions, return policy) is present and not deceptively buried, it's just below the fold where expected.

## What's weak

- "Ends tonight" urgency from the subject line is completely absent from the body/hero — no countdown, no repeated deadline near the CTA.
- This is a near-duplicate of yesterday's "Weekend deal: 20% off + free shipping" send — same offer, same hero model, same layout, just a new subject.
- Hero has no visible primary CTA button directly under the banner text — the first "SHOP NOW" buttons don't appear until after the intro copy, adding friction before the first click.
- Body copy ("Build your fall wardrobe with elevated sweaters...") doesn't reinforce the urgency angle the subject promised, creating a subject/body mismatch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline directly in the hero banner (e.g., "20% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING — ENDS TONIGHT") so urgency survives the open, not just the subject line.
- 2. Vary the creative from the 09-19 send — different model/product or crop — so back-to-back opens don't feel like a repeat.
- 3. Move a "SHOP NOW" CTA button directly beneath the hero banner, before the intro copy, to cut pre-click friction.
- 4. Add a small persistent countdown or "today only" tag near the top nav for scanners who skip the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last hours: 20% off $125 + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only — 20% off your $125+ order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim, and fall staples — before the discount disappears`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Women, Men, Kids & Home while it's still 20% off`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
This H&M promotional email is built on a click-tracking redirect service (t19.email.hm.com) that is returning HTTP 403 on every probed link, including the unsubscribe link — a functional failure that blocks all user navigation and a CAN-SPAM compliance risk.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed links return **403 Forbidden**, including primary CTAs ("Shop now" x3, "SHOP NOW" x2), navigation ("Women", "Men", "H&M" logo), and both product images. Example: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h7d2fb7bb-...&p3=20260920`
- All tracked URLs share the same `did`, `rid`, and `erid` parameters, differing only by the trailing hex ID (`f8392bfb`, `f8392c25`, etc.) — consistent with a single redirect-resolution failure at the `t19.email.hm.com` tracking domain rather than per-link issues. This points to a server-side/infra problem (e.g., expired campaign ID, blocked user agent, or link-wrapping misconfiguration) rather than a content authoring error.
- Two CTA links carry visible zero-width/invisible characters in the anchor text (`‌  ﻿Shop now`), embedding a Zero Width Non-Joiner (U+200C) and Zero Width No-Break Space/BOM (U+FEFF). These don't affect rendering but indicate template artifacts left in the source and could confuse text-based link/spam scanners.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 33+ `<img>` tags are missing `alt` text, including both hero/promo images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) and all product grid thumbnails (e.g. `fabedf3f...jpg`, `95324859...jpg`, etc., each duplicated at `imWidth=344` and `imWidth=322` responsive variants). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled images.
- The 1x1 tracking pixel (`src=".../r/?id=...,1"`) also lacks `alt` — expected/benign for a tracking pixel, not a defect.
- Extensive CSS is scoped for mobile (`@media (max-width:699px)`) and Outlook/Yahoo/Gmail webkit hacks (`#MessageViewBody`, `.show-webkit-table`) — no structural issues observed in the truncated markup itself.
