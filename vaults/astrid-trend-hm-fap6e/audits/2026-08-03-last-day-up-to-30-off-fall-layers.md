# Last day: Up to 30% off fall layers
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M running the identical "up to 30% off" ceiling for a fifth straight day — 7/30's "back-to-school checklist," 8/1's "Score 30% off in-store all weekend," 8/2's "Up to 30% off ends tomorrow," and now 8/3's "Last day: Up to 30% off fall layers." The template is the same nav-grid structure the brand has used all week (member free-shipping banner, LAST CHANCE hero, category button grid, SHOP BY thumbnails), just re-skinned with "fall layers" copy over a single studio product shot.
- The execution itself is clean — no render bugs, the hero model shot is crisp, and "LAST CHANCE" paired with a deadline that actually matches today's date (08/03/2026 in the fine print) gives the urgency real teeth. But there's no single hero CTA button under the offer — the eye has six competing category buttons (WOMEN/MEN/KIDS 2-8Y/KIDS 9-14Y/HOME/FIND A STORE) instead of one "Shop the Sale" action, and "up to 30%" with "select styles marked in red" buried in legal copy is the same soft, exclusionary framing this brand has leaned on all week.
- Judgement: a competently produced but fatigued send. If this were the first 30%-off email of the week it'd score well; as the closer of a five-day identical-offer streak, it reads like inbox noise dressed up with a deadline.

## What's working

- "LAST CHANCE" + "Last day" urgency is credible — the fine print confirms the offer actually expires 08/03/2026, today's date.
- Hero product shot (gray tee, white shorts) is clean, well-lit, and renders without artifacts.
- Member free-shipping banner up top gives a concrete loyalty benefit before the fold.
- Visual hierarchy is legible top-to-bottom: banner → logo/nav → LAST CHANCE headline → hero image → category grid.

## What's weak

- No single unambiguous CTA button tied to the offer — six category buttons compete for the click instead of one "Shop the Sale" action.
- "Up to 30% off" is the exact same ceiling H&M has run since 7/30 with no escalation, making this the least differentiated version of a beat that's already stale.
- Fine print excludes sale items, H&M Studio, guest designer collabs, hosiery, swimwear, and more — the "up to 30%" headline oversells what's actually discounted.
- Single hero model/outfit doesn't map to any specific linked product — it's a mood shot, not a shoppable hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the category-grid CTA block with one dominant "Shop the Sale" button directly under the hero headline — right now nothing tells the eye where to click first.
- 2. Break the 30%-off streak or escalate it — five straight days at the same ceiling trains subscribers to ignore the number; either raise the discount for the true last day or swap to a different lever (free shipping no minimum, bundle deal).
- 3. Link the hero image to the specific outfit/product shown, not a generic category page, so the "shop this look" impulse has somewhere concrete to go.
- 4. Surface one exclusion clearly near the offer ("excludes sale items") instead of only in the footer legal block, so the CTA doesn't feel like bait-and-switch on click-through.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall reset — 30% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Knits, denim, and cozy sets before the discount disappears`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping for members — sale ends at midnight`
