- No discount fatigue — a real break from the Labor Day promo cadence, similar to 09/09 and 09/10.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer visible anywhere in the outerwear section — no "starting at," no member price, nothing to anchor a purchase decision.
- The H&M HOME "Form & Function" block is a jarring non-sequitur mid-email — it breaks the outerwear narrative the subject line set up.

- 3. Give the hero a direct "Shop This Jacket" CTA instead of a generic "SHOP NOW" that likely dumps to a category page.
