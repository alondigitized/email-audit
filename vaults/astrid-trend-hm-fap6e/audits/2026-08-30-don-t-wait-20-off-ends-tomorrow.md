# Don't wait: 20% off ends tomorrow
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is essentially yesterday's "20% off for Members starts now" email (08/29, 6.00/10) run back with an urgency spin — same "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE / 20% OFF YOUR ORDER" block in the same position, same fall-jeans hook, same navigation-button wall underneath. The offer itself is still real and unambiguous: bold "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" sits directly under the hero image with no scroll required, and the subject line finally adds a deadline ("ends tomorrow") that the previous two 20%-off sends lacked.
- The problem is what comes after the offer. Instead of one clear CTA, the recipient hits a stacked list of six near-identical outline buttons (WOMEN, MEN, KIDS 2-8Y, KIDS 9-14Y, SIGN IN, BECOME A MEMBER) before any product shot appears — that's decision paralysis dressed up as segmentation. Below that it's four flat "shop now" category slabs (Best of Trends, New Classics, Mini Favorites, Instant Hits) with zero pricing, zero differentiation, and no tie-back to the 20% offer or the "ends tomorrow" urgency. It reads like the discount was bolted onto a stock editorial template rather than the campaign driving the layout.
- Compared to the 08/26 flash sale (8/10, "25% OFF EVERYTHING UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" in bold red), this is a weaker execution of a similar mechanic — real urgency, real discount, but buried under generic navigation instead of being reinforced throughout the email. Solid, not strong.

## What's working

- "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" is large, bold, and appears immediately after the hero image with no scrolling needed.
- Subject line adds genuine time pressure ("ends tomorrow") that recent 20%-off member sends lacked.
- Hero image is clean and on-brand — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the send.
- "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" framing plus a "BECOME A MEMBER" button gives non-members a clear reason to convert.

## What's weak

- Six stacked outline buttons (WOMEN/MEN/KIDS 2-8Y/KIDS 9-14Y/SIGN IN/BECOME A MEMBER) create a wall of nearly identical CTAs — there's no single unambiguous next action.
- The deadline from the subject line ("ends tomorrow") never reappears in the body — no countdown, no repeated date, no reinforcement.
- The four product sections below (Best of Trends, New Classics, Mini Favorites, Instant Hits) show no prices, no discount mention, and no visual link back to the 20% offer — they read as filler.
- Near-identical structure to the 08/29 send one day later risks fatigue; a subscriber who opened yesterday's nearly-identical email may not see enough new here to reopen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the six-button CTA wall into one primary button ("SHOP 20% OFF NOW") with WOMEN/MEN/KIDS as secondary text links, not equal-weight buttons — right now nothing tells the eye where to click first.
- 2. Repeat the "ends tomorrow" urgency visually in the body (e.g., a red countdown strip or "Offer ends 8/31" directly under the headline) so it isn't only in the subject line.
- 3. Add the 20% callout or a sample discounted price to at least the top one or two product sections (Best of Trends, New Classics) so the sale feels connected to the merchandise, not just the header.
- 4. Differentiate this send further from 08/29's near-identical layout — swap the hero model/copy angle or lead with the urgency angle visually, not just in the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off your order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off ends at midnight — don't miss it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fall jeans, layers, and more — 20% off members-only, through tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your discount disappears tomorrow. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Campaign email fails automated QA at a 16% pass rate; all 12 probed CTA/nav links return HTTP 403, and compliance/deliverability headers required for inbox authentication and one-click unsubscribe are absent.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed links resolve through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` redirector and return **403 Forbidden**, including every "Shop now"/"SHOP NOW" CTA, the header "H&M" logo link, and the **Unsubscribe** link itself (`...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`). Since the redirector itself fails, this is likely an infra/ESP-side issue (auth-walled redirect domain, expired signed params, or QA prober being blocked) rather than a per-link authoring error — but it means **100% of tested destinations are unreachable** in this sample.
- Tracking parameter structure is consistent across links (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p3` campaign/message IDs), so the tracking schema itself appears intact — the failure is at resolution, not construction.
- 61 of 73 total links were deprioritized/skipped by the prober (footer, social, utility) — coverage is CTA-focused, not exhaustive.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including tracking pixels (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...`) and all product imagery (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants across 6 unique product images, each duplicated at two resolutions). Screen readers will announce these images with no descriptive fallback.
- Mobile-responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:699px)`) and Outlook/Yahoo-specific overrides (`mso-hide`, `#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`) are present, indicating standard multi-client defensive coding — no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded JSON-LD (`schema.org/EmailMessage` / `DiscountOffer`) has **empty values** for `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, and `url` — these structured-data fields are unpopulated, which will suppress or degrade any Gmail/Outlook promotional markup (e.g., no discount chip or countdown rendering in-inbox) even though the visible HTML presumably renders a code/date.
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) found in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe via mail client cannot be confirmed as supported.
- The in-body **Unsubscribe link is broken (403)** — this is a confirmed, concrete compliance risk: if the mailto/header-based path is also unavailable, a recipient has no working unsubscribe mechanism.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links do not use standard UTM query parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`); H&M uses proprietary redirector params (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p3`) instead. This is a vendor-specific tracking scheme, not a defect, but it means landing-page attribution can't be validated against typical UTM conventions from this data.
- Landing page alignment cannot be assessed — all sampled links 403 before reaching a destination page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Investigate the 403s on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` as priority one** — confirm whether this is a QA-prober access issue (e.g., bot-blocking, geo/IP restriction, missing session) vs. a genuinely broken redirect service; if genuine, every CTA and the unsubscribe link are non-functional for recipients.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 flagged images, particularly the 6 product images (currently relying on filename-only identification like `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`).
3. Populate the empty JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts`/`Ends`, `image`, `url`) to enable Gmail/Outlook promotional inbox markup.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the true SMTP layer (not just via this relay's capture) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against H&M's sending infrastructure, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
