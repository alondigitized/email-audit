**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is an H&M activewear send that doesn't deliver on its subject. "Activewear deals now from $9.99" promises a specific price point. The body never shows $9.99. The hero says "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" with the copy "athleisure and coordinated sets." The $9.99 hook appears nowhere in the render, so the strongest part of the subject is orphaned.
- The structure is clean. The hero is a model in a black zip top with a cream puffer. Below it are three category blocks with strong one-word headers, FASHIONABLE, FUNCTIONAL and FLEXIBLE, each pointing to a segment (women, men, girls/boys). It reads as a good, tidy template. But the lower half drifts into a generic "YOUR ONE-STOP HOLIDAY SHOP" block, a big winter-hat portrait with a store-locator CTA. That dilutes the activewear story and pushes toward in-store traffic.
- Against the 2026-11-23 "Up to 50% off knitwear deals for every body" send, this is the same skeleton with a different category. The "every body" send earned its 8.00 by carrying the subject through its blocks. This one carries the category through but drops the price. It's competent, not sharp. The red-on-black member banner at the top ("BECOME A MEMBER FOR EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY ACCESS + 10% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE") adds a second offer that competes with the 50% hero.

- The red-on-black member banner is hard to read and puts a second offer (10% off a first purchase) above the hero.
- The category blocks show no products, prices or sale badges, so there's nothing concrete to click for.
- The "YOUR ONE-STOP HOLIDAY SHOP" hat block is off-topic for activewear. Its "Find a store" CTA pulls away from the offer.
- The "Shop by" grid and long fine print make the footer heavy. The mobile hero CTA also sits below the fold.

- 2. Drop the holiday-shop block, or cut it to a single line, and keep the email on activewear.

This looks like bot-blocking of the QA probe rather than a confirmed outage. Most findings are therefore unverified rather than confirmed failures.

  - A uniform 403 on every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to ESP bot or WAF filtering of the QA user-agent. It does not point to a broken campaign. I can't confirm either way from this data, so re-probe with a browser-like UA or from a real client.
- **Final destinations are unknown.** The redirector never resolved, so I can't confirm where the CTAs land.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 66 skipped.
- **Missing link text.** The QA output shows an empty `link text` for every probed link. The "Shop now" entries that did have text carry zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`).
