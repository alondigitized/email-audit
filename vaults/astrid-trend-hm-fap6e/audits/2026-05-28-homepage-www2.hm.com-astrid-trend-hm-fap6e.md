# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- I opened H&M and the first thing filling my entire screen is a wall of cookie consent copy — no hero, no offer, no products, just legalese and three buttons. The actual homepage is completely blocked. This is literally the brand's first impression and it's a privacy disclaimer. Not exactly the vibe.

## What's working

- **"ACCEPT ALL" button** is high-contrast black-on-white with bold all-caps — easy to tap, no visual ambiguity about which button removes the blocker fastest
- **"Only Required Cookies" as a secondary option** is present, which is legally table-stakes but at least signals the brand knows GDPR exists
- **Copy legibility** is clean — readable font size, no cramped text, decent line spacing

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport modal with zero bleed-through** — I can barely make out a sliver of grey image in the background left edge; the entire merchandising canvas is hidden, so there is zero pull toward the actual site
- **Wall of legalese above the buttons** — three dense paragraphs of tracking/data-sharing explanation before I get to act; nobody reads this and it creates friction
- **No brand personality in the modal itself** — the consent copy is generic boilerplate; no H&M logo visible, no brand color except black buttons, could be any site
- **Three-button decision** — "ACCEPT ALL," "ONLY REQUIRED," and "COOKIES AND SERVICES SETTINGS" at equal visual weight creates decision paralysis rather than a clear fast path
- **No value exchange** — other brands frame consent with "unlock your personalized experience" or "join 30M members"; H&M just leads with what they're taking, not what I'd get

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the modal to a bottom banner** — let the hero and current campaign show behind it; the visual pull of the actual site is the best argument for accepting and moving on
- **Lead with brand identity** — put the H&M logo at the top of the modal, use brand red, and write one sentence like "Personalize your H&M experience" before the legal wall
- **Collapse legal copy behind a "Learn more" toggle** — show one short sentence + the three buttons; legal detail available on demand, not mandatory reading
- **Elevate the primary path visually** — make "ACCEPT ALL" a filled red button and deprioritize the other two options visually so the fast path is obvious at a glance
