# Stylish Halloween decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-shot Halloween home-decor edit that undercuts itself with a mismatched offer. The hero is a moody black tabletop with skeletal cutlery, and the "THE HALLOWEEN EDIT" headline is clear. Below it, a grid of priced products runs $12.99 to $29.99, with "New Arrival" tags. The layout is easy to scan and reads as seasonal.
- The top banner says "UP TO 30% OFF SELECT BASICS & KNITS*", and nothing in the body is a basic or a knit. Someone opening this for candlesticks and a ghost duvet has to work out that the discount doesn't apply to what they came for. The price tags on the products show no markdown, so the banner reads as a leftover from the week's promo template. Compared with the 09-28 "Fall's signature looks" send, which had no banner competing for attention, this one is less focused. It also follows five days of "15% off $75 / 20% off $100" sends, so it lands as one more daily H&M email.
- My judgment is that it's a decent home-category send with weak conversion mechanics. It has one generic CTA, no stated deadline in the visible copy, and an offer that doesn't apply to the featured products.

## What's working

- The hero image is strong and on-theme, with a dark, styled tablespace that says "Halloween" at once.
- The headline "THE HALLOWEEN EDIT" and its one-line subhead are legible and give the eye a clear entry point.
- Every product has a name, a visible price and a "New Arrival" flag where relevant, so the grid answers the price question without a click.
- The mix of 2-up and 1-up rows gives the scroll some rhythm, and the images are crisp with no broken tiles.
- The Shop By block (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives a recipient who isn't into decor another way in.

## What's weak

- The banner discount (basics and knits) has nothing to do with the decor content. It is the first thing read and it doesn't apply.
- The only CTA is a generic outlined "SHOP NOW" button. It sits under the headline and looks weak next to the hero.
- Nothing visible creates urgency. The 09/30 end date appears only in the fine print, so the deadline never reaches the top of the email.
- Product tiles carry no reviews, no member pricing and no "limited stock" cues.
- A large empty gap before "SHOP BY" and a long legal block at the bottom make the tail feel loose.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the banner match the content. Either drop it, or replace it with a decor-relevant hook such as "Free shipping on Halloween decor" or a member perk. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Halloween decor" in a filled, high-contrast button. Repeat it after the product grid.
- 3. Put a real deadline in the visible copy, such as "Ends 9/30", if the promo is genuinely time-bound.
- 4. Add a "Best seller" or star-rating cue to a few tiles, such as the ghost duvet and the candelabra.
- 5. Trim the dead space above Shop By and move the App and Store links into a more compact footer.
