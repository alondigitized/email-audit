# Today only: 15% off sitewide
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- H&M drops "Today only: 15% off sitewide" as the eighth-ish promotional email in six days — but with a critical twist: they spent the entire prior week screaming 50% off sitewide, and now they're asking subscribers to get excited about 15%. That is not a deal; it's a de-escalation dressed as urgency. A subscriber who opened the July 19 "Last day: Up to 50% off almost everything" email will look at this and feel the rug pulled.
- The mechanics are fine — clean render, short subject, visible offer headline — but the email is fighting itself. The top banner ("TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING") contradicts the primary message in the hero, eating the preheader slot and splitting the subscriber's attention before they even absorb the main offer. Meanwhile, the product grid is labeled "New Arrival" throughout, which muddies whether those items are actually included in the 15% deal.
- Verdict: surface-level execution is adequate, but the strategic context makes this one of the weaker sends in the recent run. The offer regression from 50% → 15% will feel like a downgrade to every subscriber who was paying attention, and there's no narrative bridge to explain why they should care.

## What's working

- Subject line is tight (28 chars), concrete ("15% off sitewide"), and has a real urgency signal ("Today only") — solid inbox mechanics.
- Hero headline is visually dominant: "TODAY ONLY / 15% OFF SITEWIDE" reads immediately, no scanning required.
- Clean render — all product images load, prices are visible, no broken layouts or overlapping text.
- Category grid (WOMEN | MEN | BABY | KIDS | HOME | BEAUTY) gives shoppers an immediate, obvious next step without a wall of copy.
- The "New Arrival" angle is a fresh creative pivot away from the endless sale imagery — the photography is light, editorial, and seasonal.

## What's weak

- "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING" sits at the very top of the email and likely hijacks the inbox preview/preheader slot — subscribers see "TAX-FREE DAYS ARE COMING" next to a "15% off today only" subject line, and those two messages are unrelated.
- No dedicated hero CTA button under the main offer — the only clickable action above the product grid is the nav-level "SHOP NOW," which feels passive for a time-sensitive offer.
- 15% off sitewide after a week of 50% off sitewide is an offer regression; no copy bridges the gap or reframes the value.
- Product tiles are labeled "New Arrival" with no before/after pricing — subscribers can't see their 15% savings applied anywhere in the email body.
- Cadence fatigue is at its peak: this is the 7th–8th promotional email in less than a week, and the offer is getting smaller.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Bridge the narrative from the sale.** One sentence in the hero sub-headline that explains the offer context — e.g., "The sale's over. New arrivals just landed — grab yours at 15% off today." Without a bridge, 15% reads like a mistake next to recent 50% blasts.
- 2. **Add a dedicated hero CTA button** — bold, full-width, placed directly below the "15% OFF SITEWIDE" headline. The category grid is too far down for mobile first-scroll.
- 3. **Move the Tax-Free Days banner to the footer or a secondary section.** It's a future event that doesn't support today's action. Burying it prevents it from cannibalizing the preheader and the hero moment.
- 4. **Show struck-through pricing on product tiles** so subscribers can see the 15% discount actually applied — e.g., "~~$59.99~~ $50.99." Otherwise the savings are invisible.
- 5. **Pull the email count back.** This is a CRM problem, not a creative one, but worth flagging: 8 promos in 6 days will crater open rates on future sends. Consider a 2-day gap minimum between discount emails.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New arrivals are in — 15% off today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh drop, one-day deal: 15% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New pieces just landed. Today's the best time to grab one.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mid-week refresh — new arrivals, 15% off. Ends tonight.`
