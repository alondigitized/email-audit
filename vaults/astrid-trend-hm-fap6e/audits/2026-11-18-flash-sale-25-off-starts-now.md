# Flash sale: 25% off starts now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean H&M template with a muddled offer. The subject promises a "Flash sale: 25% off starts now." The black banner at the top leads with "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS: UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES*." The hero is a kid in a Fair Isle sweater with red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" type. The 25% headline only appears below the hero, as "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET." A reader sees three different framings in one scroll.
- The execution is tidy. The brand is recognizable, the layout is clear, and the single outlined SHOP NOW button is easy to find. The deadline is credible. But the email never says what the 25% applies to. The fine print restricts the flash sale to "only from 3pm ET on 11/18/2026 through 12am ET on 11/19/2026," which contradicts "starts now" for anyone opening it in the morning. The body copy also pushes "order by 11/20 with Express Shipping," which cuts against the midnight deadline.
- Overall it's a competent but unfocused send. Urgency is built into the subject, and the body then dilutes it with a competing "up to 50%" offer and a long legal block.

## What's working

- The sender and template are instantly recognizable. The logo and nav are clean.
- The "25% OFF UNTIL MIDNIGHT ET" headline is bold and centered, with a real deadline.
- The single outlined SHOP NOW button is unambiguous and easy to tap.
- The Women/Men/Kids/Home tiles give category entry points with no render bugs.
- The seasonal framing (holiday sweaters, Thanksgiving outfit) fits mid-November.

## What's weak

- Two offers compete: "up to 50% off" in the banner and 25% in the headline. The subject only supports one of them.
- The hero image has no offer on it, only "Early Black Friday Deals." It doesn't match the "Flash sale" subject.
- "Starts now" conflicts with fine print saying the sale begins at 3pm ET. The "order by 11/20" shipping line also muddies the midnight deadline.
- The fine print is a very long block of text below the footer. It is the only place the flash sale's exclusions appear.
- The category tiles have small, left-aligned labels and no product, price or product detail. A large blank gap sits between the tiles and "SHOP BY," and the Shop By section repeats the same categories.
- Member benefits are mentioned only in the fine print. They are not visible in the creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the hero: "25% off, today only" in large type over the image. Drop or demote the "up to 50%" banner so the email matches the subject.
- 2. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop 25% off sweaters." Link it to the featured sweater collection rather than a generic shop page.
- 3. Fix the timing. Say "Starts at 3pm ET" in the subject or preheader, or change the send time to match.
- 4. Replace the duplicate Shop By block with 4 to 6 priced products that show the 25% discount.
- 5. Add a short exclusions line ("excludes sale and Studio") near the button and move the long legal text out of the visible flow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off today only, ends at midnight ET`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday sweaters 25% off until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 11/20 for Thanksgiving delivery. Today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Knits, denim and outerwear, 25% off for one day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 25% flash-sale email has 12 of 12 probed material links returning 403 and 13 images with no alt text. Its JSON-LD block is an unpopulated template, and the QA run couldn't see the List-Unsubscribe or authentication headers. The 403s are likely bot-blocking on the ESP redirector, so real-user breakage isn't confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed links returned 403. Every one is a `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h18309249-…,55155299,<hash>&did=1427460761&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6226921&p3=20261118` redirect. This includes:
  - "Shop now" (`…f8cd5940`-series IDs)
  - the headline banner (`…f8cd591a`)
  - the logo link "H&M" (`…f8cd591b`)
  - the product image links (`…f8cd593c`, `593d`, `5940`, `5941`, `5943`)
  - **Unsubscribe** (`…f8cd596e`, with extra `p4`/`p5` params)
- A uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe, points to the ESP blocking the automated prober (WAF or user-agent filtering). It doesn't point to dead campaign links. This needs confirming with a real-client click-through or a differently fingerprinted request.
- 48 links were skipped by the prober (footer, social, utility), so their status is unknown.
- Several links have no readable link text. Their text is `[image: cq5dam.web…jpeg]`, plus "‌  ﻿Shop now" with zero-width characters (U+200C/U+FEFF) prepended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 13 images are flagged.
  - Eleven are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs under `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/…`. Because the alt text is missing, the filename is what shows when images are blocked, and what screen readers announce.
  - Two more are `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1`, probably the open-tracking pixel, which should have `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate desktop and mobile images:** each product image appears twice (1400px and 750px). Visibility is toggled with `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. If the hiding isn't honored, screen readers and clients that ignore the media queries get both copies.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` is invalid, because `auto` isn't a valid `line-height` value.
  - `@media screen yahoo { … }` is a malformed media query.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The visible `<head>` also has no `<title>`, but the source is truncated, so that one is unconfirmed.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients, so the fallback stack needs to be solid. This is informational, since the fallback stack wasn't visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the remainder wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip it. The header is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. For a bulk sender, Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. That's a limitation of the relay, not a finding against the sender.
- The visible unsubscribe link (`…f8cd596e`) returned 403 to the prober. The body link is the only compliance path that could be checked, and its status is unconfirmed.
- Sender is `newsletter@email.hm.com`, and link tracking is on `t19.email.hm.com`. That's the same organizational domain, so alignment looks consistent.
- A physical postal address wasn't visible in the truncated source, so it couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Every link goes through the opaque redirector. Attribution relies on `did`, `rid`, `p1=DM6226921` and `p3=20261118`. Destination URLs and any final-hop UTMs couldn't be resolved because of the 403s.
- **Stale JSON-LD:** the `application/ld+json` block is an empty template.
  - `EmailMessage.subjectLine`, `DiscountOffer.discountCode`, `description`, `availabilityEnds`, `PromotionCard.image` and `url` are all `""`.
  - `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, years out of date.
  - Gmail promotion-card markup with empty or expired values is invalid and won't render.
- **Subject and body mismatch:** the subject says "25% off", while the body link text says "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS: UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES*". The `2026-bf-pre-deal-assets` image paths point to a different campaign asset set than the flash-sale subject. Check that the landing pages reflect the subject's offer.
- **Plain-text part:** 514 of 592 characters (87%) are URLs. This is a spam-filter risk because the text part has almost no real copy.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…` links with a mail-client-like user-agent. Resolve the redirects to get final URLs and check for UTMs.
2. Confirm the unsubscribe link (`…f8cd596e`) works for real users, and capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct inbox rather than the relay.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 11 product and banner images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. Replace the `cq5dam…` filenames.
4. Fix the CSS: replace `line-height:auto` with `normal` or a numeric value, and correct the `@media screen yahoo` query.
5. Add `lang` to `<html>` and a `<title>` if one is missing.
6. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. At minimum, fix `availabilityStarts`, `subjectLine` and `url`.
7. Add more text content to the plain-text part so it isn't mostly URLs. Remove the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" link text.
8. Align the subject's 25% offer with the body copy and landing pages (25% in the subject, 50% in the body link text).
