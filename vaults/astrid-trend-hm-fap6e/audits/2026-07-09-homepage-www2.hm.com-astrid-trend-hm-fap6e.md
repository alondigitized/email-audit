# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the very first thing I see when I land on H&M's homepage is a full-screen cookie consent wall — like, the actual homepage is completely obscured behind it. I can't see a hero, a sale banner, a product, literally nothing. Whatever H&M is pushing right now, I have no idea, because they've put a legal disclaimer modal between me and the brand.

## What's working

- The three cookie buttons ("ACCEPT ALL," "ONLY REQUIRED COOKIES," "COOKIES AND SERVICES SETTINGS") are clearly labeled and visually distinct — if I *have* to see this wall, at least the options are legible and not deceptive
- The link to "Cookie and data sharing notice" is present for anyone who actually wants to read it (very few people, but points for transparency)

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is invisible — there is zero brand, product, or campaign content accessible above the fold
- The modal takes up the full viewport with dense legal/tracking copy before any buttons; it reads like a compliance document, not a welcome mat
- No visual branding is showing — I can't even confirm I'm on H&M's site from what's visible; there's no logo, color palette, or hero in frame
- The text is small, dense, and paragraph-heavy — the opposite of how Gen Z reads anything
- Three buttons of nearly equal weight creates decision friction; "Accept All" should be the clear primary action if conversion (into the site) is the goal

## Recommendations

- **Lead with "ACCEPT ALL" as a bold primary button and demote the others** — flip the visual hierarchy so the path into the site is obvious and instant
- **Reduce the cookie body copy to 2 sentences max** with a "read more" expansion; nobody reads this wall and it just delays the actual shopping experience
- **Add the H&M logo or a thin brand bar above the modal** so I at least know I'm in the right place while I'm stuck on this gate
- **Pre-accept required cookies and drop me into the homepage immediately**, surfacing only an unobtrusive bottom banner for optional tracking — the current full-block approach is a bounce machine
