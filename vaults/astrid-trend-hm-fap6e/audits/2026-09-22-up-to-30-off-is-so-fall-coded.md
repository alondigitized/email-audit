# Up to 30% off is so fall-coded
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, workmanlike category-shop email — "Up to 30% off select styles" with a clean hero, four department shortcut buttons up top, then three well-organized editorial blocks (Time for New / Easy Upgrades / Soft & Sweet) each with a shop button. Compared to the 2026-09-19/20 "20% off $125 + free shipping" run, this one drops the free-shipping/minimum-spend hook and swaps in a vaguer "up to 30% off select styles" — which reads as a broader but weaker promise, since "up to X% off select styles" is classic ceiling-not-floor copy that seasoned shoppers discount mentally.
- The layout itself renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, good visual rhythm alternating hero/copy/button blocks. But the top of the email is oddly cluttered: there's a stray "in accordance with Privacy Notice" text fragment overlaid right on the hero image, which looks like a rendering/layout glitch and undercuts an otherwise premium-looking hero shot.
- Overall this is a mid-pack send: clean execution, reasonable structure, but a generic offer, a visible text glitch on the hero, and no urgency or personalization. Call it a 6 — better than last week's broken-image disaster (09-18) but a step down from the crisper 20%-off-with-real-threshold sends.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout with consistent alternating photo/headline/button rhythm.
- Four immediate department shortcuts (Women/Men/Baby/Kids) right under the hero headline — fast wayfinding for any shopper.
- Fine print at the bottom does spell out a real end date ("through 09/30/2026"), so the offer isn't indefinite.
- Model diversity across the three category blocks (women's, men's, kids') supports broad household relevance.
- No broken images anywhere in the body — every product shot rendered correctly.

## What's weak

- Stray text ("...in accordance with Privacy Notice") overlays the top of the hero image — looks like a rendering glitch and is the first thing the eye catches.
- "Up to 30% off select styles" is a vague, ceiling-based offer with no visible minimum spend, discount tiers, or category call-out — weaker and less concrete than the prior week's "20% off $125."
- No visible urgency in the body copy itself (the 09/30 deadline is buried in bottom legal text, not in the hero).
- No loyalty/member pricing or benefit shown despite H&M having a rewards program.
- Three sequential "shop now" blocks (Time for New / Easy Upgrades / Soft & Sweet) with generic headlines add length without adding differentiation — reads as filler between the hero and the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero image glitch — that overlaid privacy-notice text fragment needs to be removed or moved into the footer where it belongs; it's actively damaging the premium look of the top fold.
- 2. Put the real deadline in the hero copy, not just the footer fine print — "Up to 30% off, ends 9/30" turns a vague ceiling offer into a credible, time-bound one.
- 3. Replace "select styles" with a concrete category or two of the deepest discount (e.g., "up to 30% off knits & outerwear") to make the offer feel less like fine-print bait.
- 4. Consider dropping one of the three mid-body sections (Time for New / Easy Upgrades / Soft & Sweet) — three near-identical two-photo-plus-button blocks stretch the scroll without adding new information.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

The email fails 12 of the QA link checks with 403 responses across every probed material link, and is missing standard unsubscribe/authentication headers required for compliance verification.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- All 7 primary navigation/CTA links return 403 when probed: both "Shop now" CTAs (`...f841e647`, `...f841e649`), the "H&M" logo link (`...f841e646`), and the department links "Women" (`...f841e669`), "Men" (`...f841e66a`), "Baby" (`...f841e66b`), "Kids" (`...f841e66c`), plus duplicate "Women"/"Men" links (`...f841e66d`, `...f841e66e`).
- Both hero/product image tracking links (`...f841e667`, `...f841e668`) also return 403.
- All failing links share the same redirect host `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` — consistent with a single expired or misconfigured ESP click-tracking/redirect endpoint (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `t19` tracking domain) rather than 7+ independent broken destinations.
- The Unsubscribe link (`...f841e6a7`) also returns 403 via the same redirect host — see Compliance below.
- 12 material links were probed (ranked by click likelihood); 61 links were skipped as lower-priority footer/social/utility links, so additional broken links may exist unverified.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Zero-width/invisible characters (`‌`, `﻿`) are embedded in visible link text (e.g., "‌ ﻿Shop now"), likely from CDATA/ZWNJ injection in the template — cosmetically invisible but may affect screen reader pronunciation and text-based link parsing.
- 16 images are missing `alt` text, including all product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants across 7 products) and 2 tracking pixel images — fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades experience for screen readers and blocked-image clients.
- Extensive mobile-responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:699px)`) and Outlook/Yahoo-specific hacks (`mso-hide`, `#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`) are present, indicating deliberate cross-client handling; no rendering-breakage evidence beyond the alt-text and link-text issues above.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The embedded JSON-LD `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer` schema block has empty string values for `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, and `url` fields — these structured-data merge tokens failed to populate. This will prevent Gmail/Yahoo promotional markup (annotated CTA button, promo card) from rendering correctly.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, a stale value unrelated to the current send — confirms the schema block is not being populated per-send.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- The Unsubscribe link itself returns a 403 (`...f841e6a7...&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`), meaning the visible unsubscribe mechanism is non-functional as tested.

- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data.
- Note: header absence is flagged as unverified via the relay (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absent at origin — worth re-testing against raw SMTP headers before treating as a hard compliance failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Links use ESP redirect/tracking params (`id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) rather than standard UTM query parameters — no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` visible in the truncated HTML, so email-to-site campaign attribution cannot be confirmed from this source.
- Because every material link 403s at the redirect layer, actual landing-page destination and UTM continuity cannot be verified at all — this is currently the blocking issue for this category.
