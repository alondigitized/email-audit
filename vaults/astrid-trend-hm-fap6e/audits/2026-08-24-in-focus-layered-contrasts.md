# In focus: Layered contrasts
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M pivoting to full-price editorial mode again — "UTILITY CHIC" and "POWER MEETS POLISH," individually priced pieces ($37.99, $329, $89.99, $59.99), zero discount code anywhere in the render. After a run that included a genuine "LAST DAY: 20% off + free shipping" send on 8/23 and a 20% off sweater push on 8/22, this reads like a hard swap back to lookbook-style content, the same mode as "Return to routine in style" on 8/20. No offer, no urgency, no reason to act today.
- The styling itself is clean — trench coats, oversized leather, a new SculptMove activewear collection — and the visual hierarchy works fine as a catalog scroll. But there's nothing here pulling a deal-trained subscriber to click over just browsing the app later. This is a content/inspiration email dressed as a promo send, and coming one day after a hard discount deadline, it's a jarring cadence whiplash.
- Judgement: competent editorial execution, weak business email — no offer means low urgency, low open incentive beyond curiosity, and no compelling reason to act now versus any other day.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent product photography with clear pricing and "New Arrival" tags under each item.
- Two distinct thematic sections (utility layering, then activewear) give the email a magazine-style flow without feeling cluttered.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA buttons are repeated at logical breakpoints (top, after utility section, after activewear section).
- Bottom "SHOP BY" category nav (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives multiple entry points if the hero content doesn't land.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text aligned, no overlap.

## What's weak

- No discount, promo code, or free-shipping threshold anywhere in the email — a hard reversal from the last three sends (8/21-8/23) that all led with 20% off.
- Subject "In focus: Layered contrasts" is vague editorial language, not tied to either section headline ("Utility Chic" / "Power Meets Polish") shown in the body.
- Hero product shot has a price ($69.99) floating with no product name or link context directly visible.
- Activewear section ("Power Meets Polish") feels bolted on — different tone, different model, different product category — with no connective tissue to the utility layering theme above it.
- Coming one day after a "Last day: 20%" urgency send, this abrupt shift to full-price content risks feeling like a bait-and-switch on cadence (train them to expect a deal, then go quiet).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer back in the hero, or pick a different lever. If there's no active discount, lead with something concrete — "New Arrivals" badge treatment is not enough on its own to justify a send; consider bundling a small perk (free shipping threshold, loyalty points multiplier) into the hero band.
- 2. Align the subject line to what's actually in the email — "Utility Chic: new layers for fall" beats the abstract "Layered contrasts," and it matches the H1 the reader sees immediately on open.
- 3. Split the two unrelated themes (outerwear vs. activewear) into two separate sends or clearly separate them with a stronger visual break — right now they read as two different campaigns stapled together.
- 4. Add social proof or fit detail (sizing note, fabric callout) under at least one hero product — nothing here helps a shopper decide beyond price and name.
- 5. Reconsider the send day — pairing a full-price editorial push the day right after a "last day 20%" deadline reads as a letdown; either lead with a smaller "new arrivals" teaser mid-cycle or hold this until further from the last discount deadline.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Send is well-formed HTML with proper personalization/tracking wiring, but the tracked redirect endpoint (t19.email.hm.com) is returning HTTP 403 on all probed click-throughs, and both compliance headers for one-click unsubscribe are absent.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed tracked links return **403 Forbidden**, including every "Shop now" CTA, the "H&M" logo/header link, and the "Unsubscribe" link itself — e.g. `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h173c9fda-9516-4ba3-81b5-3ec0273d41b2,663ee5a9,f7e5ff7e&did=1715398057&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM5973983&p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c&p3=20260824`
- All links share the same redirector host (`t19.email.hm.com`) and campaign params (`did=1715398057`, `rid=3326930539`, `p1=DM5973983`), consistent with an ESP click-tracking wrapper (likely Bluecore/Salesforce-style), so the 403 is at the tracking-redirect layer, not per-destination — suggests the redirector itself is blocking the automated probe (bot/UA filtering) or the tracking token set is invalid/expired at fetch time.
- Two "Shop now" links have non-visible link text containing zero-width/BOM characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`) — cosmetic in rendering but worth noting for link-text auditing tools.
- Only 12 of 80 total links were probed (68 skipped) per the QA tool's click-likelihood ranking; footer/social links were deprioritized and not verified.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 20 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, spanning both the tracking-pixel/link-wrapped images (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...`) and the primary product imagery served from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/global_campaigns/...` (e.g. `FNP-WA10263P37-4x5-top.jpg`, `FNP-SS50263P03-3x4-1-desktop.jpg`). Each product image also has a duplicate desktop/mobile variant (1400×1400 and 750×750 srcs) both missing alt.
- Mobile responsive rules are present via `#optimize-css` media query block (`max-width:699px`) with explicit `.is-hidden-mobile`/`.is-hidden-desktop` toggles and mobile-specific font/padding overrides — no structural issues observed in the truncated source.
- Outlook/Yahoo-specific conditional handling present (`#MessageViewBody`, `@media screen yahoo`), indicating standard multi-client CSS hardening.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded JSON-LD (`schema.org/EmailMessage`, `DiscountOffer`, `PromotionCard`) has multiple empty string fields: `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `description`, `image`, `url` — these appear to be unresolved/unpopulated merge fields rather than intentionally blank, which would degrade Gmail annotation/promo-card rendering.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, a stale date unrelated to the current send (2026-08-24) — indicates this JSON-LD block is boilerplate that wasn't updated for this campaign.
- No visible `{{merge tag}}`-style unresolved personalization tokens in the truncated HTML body itself.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found — may be a relay-side capture gap (AgentMail) rather than absent at origin; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- An in-body "Unsubscribe" link is present and correctly tracked/wrapped, but it currently returns a 403 (see Section 2), which is a functional compliance risk — a broken unsubscribe link is a CAN-SPAM concern independent of header presence.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status of this message could not be verified via the relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links do not use standard UTM query parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`); instead they use a proprietary tracking-redirect scheme (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) that resolves server-side to the final landing URL — final destination and any UTM-equivalent params on the landing page cannot be verified because the redirect itself 403s before reaching `hm.com`.
- Because every probed link fails at the redirector, landing-page alignment (correct product/category page per creative) cannot be confirmed in this audit.

7. Recommendations
- Investigate the 403s at `t19.email.hm.com/r/` first — confirm whether this is a bot-detection/UA block against the QA prober specifically or a genuine broken/expired tracking token set; re-test with a browser-like fetch and check from a second network if possible.
- Prioritize confirming the Unsubscribe link works, given it's part of the 403 set — a non-functional unsubscribe link is a compliance risk regardless of root cause.
- Add `alt` text to all 20 flagged images, particularly the primary product images (currently empty on `image.hm.com` assets), for screen-reader/accessibility and Gmail/Outlook image-blocked fallback display.
- Populate the empty JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `image`, `url`) or remove the schema block if not actively used — the stale `availabilityStarts: 2018-12-31` suggests this markup isn't part of the current campaign build process.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at origin (check raw source outside the AgentMail relay), since RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is increasingly required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
