# Cozy & versatile activewear, up to 40% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy activewear send under the same "Fall Event: up to 40% off sitewide" banner H&M has run all week. The hero is a model in a cream fleece jacket. Below it sits a "LAYER UP" block with one outlined "SHOP NOW" button. The products have visible sale prices ($26.99, $37.99, $39.99, $41.99), and the copy is specific about the MOVE collection and SoftMove™ fabric. That specificity is its strongest point.
- The email is hurt by repetition and by weak commitment to one product. It is the fourth send in about a week built on the same 40% banner (10/8 Fall Event, 10/10 sweaters, 10/11 fall aesthetic). The only CTA is a single outlined "SHOP NOW" with no product named. The mid-section has two large lifestyle images with only a single price line each, and the final image has a lone product caption. The long legal footer has dense fine print, and the "up to 40%" claim is never tied to a specific product.
- Verdict: competent and on-persona for activewear shoppers, but it adds nothing new to the week's offer. It earns about a 6.

## What's working

- The hero is a clean, seasonal image of a cozy fleece layer, and the "LAYER UP" headline matches the subject.
- Copy names real items (super-soft SoftMove™ leggings, wide-leg joggers, oversized hoodies), so it reads as a collection and not a generic sale.
- Sale prices are shown with red current price and struck-through original, which makes the discount visible.
- The product grid labels items "New Arrival" and shows several price points. That gives a shopper something concrete to click.
- The red offer banner at the top carries the deal before the eye reaches the hero.

## What's weak

- The offer is the same "up to 40% off sitewide" banner as 10/8, 10/10 and 10/11. Cadence fatigue is real.
- There is one understated outlined "SHOP NOW" button above a lot of scroll. Nothing sits near the product grid, and the products have no individual buttons.
- The lower lifestyle image has only a "Flared Yoga Leggings" caption and no clear link cue, which leaves a dead stretch of scroll.
- Savings are small (about $3–$12 per item) and "up to 40%" is not shown on any product. The prices look more like 20–25% off than 40%.
- The legal block is a large wall of grey fine print, and a "Stores & service" and "Download app" block pads out the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the best verified discount on the hero ("Fleece leggings $37.99, was $44.99") so the "up to 40%" claim is backed by a real product.
- 2. Add a dedicated CTA under the product grid, such as "Shop warm activewear", and keep one visible button per product row.
- 3. Cut the gap between the third image and the "Shop by" block, and give the last lifestyle image a clear product link and button.
- 4. Change the creative from one send to the next. Use a different hook, such as a bundle, a member perk or a ship-by date, instead of re-running the same banner.
- 5. Trim the footer to the essentials and move the long disclaimers behind a single "See details" link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is click-wrapped through `t19.email.hm.com` and has widespread missing alt text on linked images. All 12 probed tracking links returned 403, which more likely means the ESP blocks automated probes than that the links are broken. The unsubscribe link also returned 403, so unsubscribe could not be verified, and the headers needed for authentication and one-click unsubscribe weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links returned 403**, including SHOP NOW, the hero link, the logo link and the Unsubscribe link. All are `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h046872e6-…&did=1418760032&rid=3326930539&erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D&p1=DM6127544&p2=…&p3=20261012`.
  - Every failing URL is on the same click-tracking host and uses the same format. That points to bot or WAF filtering of the probe, not 12 separately broken links.
  - **Unconfirmed:** whether these links work for real recipients. Re-test with a browser-like user agent or a real inbox click.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in the query string:** `rid`, `erid` (base64 of `3326930539`) and, on the unsubscribe link only, `p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. This is normal for ESP tracking, but these URLs identify the recipient if forwarded.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 94 links were probed (82 skipped, mostly footer and social). Link health for the rest is unknown.
- **Plain-text part:** 520 of 598 characters are URLs (>70%). The text alternative is almost entirely tracking URLs, which is a minor spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 16 images:**
  - Hero and lifestyle images are served from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/global_campaigns/.../*.jpg/_jcr...` and show their filenames (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) as fallback text.
  - Product images are `image.hm.com/assets/hm/{3a/58,a7/bb,3c/dd,08/a1}/….jpg?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`.
  - A tracking-pixel-like image on the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` URL is also missing alt text.
- **Linked images with no alt text:** the QA link text for these is `[image: cq5dam…jpeg]` and `[image: 3a58dbb9…]`. Image-blocking clients and screen readers get a filename or nothing as the link label.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** two "Shop now" links have link text with leading invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`). This is likely preheader-style padding that leaked into the anchor, and screen readers may announce it oddly.
- **Duplicate image sets:** each image appears at two widths (344 and 322; 1400 and 750). This looks like intentional desktop and mobile variants toggled with `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. If the hidden copy keeps its alt text, screen readers may read both. The truncated source doesn't confirm this.
- **Web fonts:** the `<style>` block uses `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/...oces.css)`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so those clients render the fallback fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML or QA output. The source was truncated, so this only covers what was visible.

- **Empty JSON-LD fields:** the schema.org block is mostly empty or stale. `EmailMessage.subjectLine` is `""`, `DiscountOffer.discountCode` and `description` are `""`, `availabilityStarts` is the stale `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, and `PromotionCard.url` and `image` are `""`. This is template residue, not live data. Gmail promotion annotations will not render, and the block could be flagged as invalid markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If H&M does not send them, it fails Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `newsletter@email.hm.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with aligned authentication, but this is not verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link:** present, but returned 403 on probe (`…f86f5696…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`). It likely works for real recipients, but it can't be called functional from this data.
- The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated HTML, so it could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters on any email-side URL.** The tracking redirect uses ESP parameters (`p1=DM6127544`, `p3=20261012`) instead. Whether UTMs are appended after the redirect cannot be confirmed because every redirect returned 403.
- **Landing page alignment is unverifiable.** No probed link resolved, so the destination for "Shop now" and the "FALL EVENT: UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE" link could not be compared with the email content.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like user agent or by following them from a real inbox, to separate probe blocking from real breakage. Do this before treating the 16% pass rate as meaningful.
2. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, DKIM signature) from a direct send instead of the relay.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 16 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for decorative duplicates, and give linked images alt text that describes the destination.
4. Remove the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" anchor text.
5. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block: fill `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `url` and `image`, and update or drop `availabilityStarts`.
6. Shorten the plain-text part's URL load, for example by using fewer tracked URLs there.
7. Resolve the redirects through to the final landing pages and confirm UTM and campaign parameters on the site side.
