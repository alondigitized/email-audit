- 1. **Add a price anchor in the hero** — "Bestsellers, starting at $12.99" or a simple price badge on the featured product would make this actionable. A ranked list with no prices is a teaser, not a closer.
- 2. **Give the reveal a #1 moment** — pin one clear hero item ("Our #1 this summer") with a direct product link before the tier grid. Make the cliffhanger pay off immediately.
- 3. **Ditch or integrate the Pride block** — either lead with it (make it the story) or cut it from this send. At the bottom of a bestsellers email it signals checkbox marketing.
- 4. **Rename the tiers to rank language** — "ICONIC" can stay, but swap ELITE/PRESTIGE for "#2 Most Reached-For" or similar. Rank language reinforces the bestseller concept; aspirational tier names undercut it.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `@import` for fonts (high risk):**
```css
<style>
  @import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/0/northstar/css/fonts.css);
  @import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css);
</style>
```
`@import` inside `<style>` blocks is stripped by Gmail (both webmail and Android app). Web fonts should be loaded via `<link rel="stylesheet">` in `<head>`, or the `@import` should be inside a dedicated style block that the email client won't touch. As-is, all recipients on Gmail fall back to system fonts silently.

**Issue — Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`:**
```html
<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">
```
No `lang="en"` (or locale equivalent). Screen readers cannot infer language; WCAG 2.1 § 3.1.1 requires it. Add `lang="en-US"` (or market-appropriate locale).

**No issues found:** Charset UTF-8 declared, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` present, `format-detection` for telephone suppression present, responsive breakpoint at 699px with `is-hidden-mobile`/`is-hidden-desktop` toggle pattern is correct.
