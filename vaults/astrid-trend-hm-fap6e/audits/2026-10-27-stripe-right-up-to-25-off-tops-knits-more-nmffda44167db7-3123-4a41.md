# Stripe right: up to 25% off tops, knits & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean stripe-themed catalog send with a tiered threshold offer. The hero is a model in a wide-sleeve striped knit, and the headline stacks "15% OFF $75 / 20% OFF $100 / 25% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" in bold black type. The theme is coherent and the product grid is tidy, with prices visible. The creative is easy to read.
- The weakness is the offer, which isn't the one the subject promises. "Up to 25% off" is true, but you only reach 25% at $125, and the rendered email only says that in the headline stack. The three tiers are hard to compare at a glance, and the fine print is a dense block at the bottom. Compared with the flat "20% OFF YOUR ORDER" member send on 10/24 and the "20% off expires tonight, Member" send on 10/25, this is a step back to the threshold mechanics H&M ran on 10/16–10/18. There is no member callout and no visible deadline, even though the fine print says the offer ends 10/28.
- Overall it's a solid, readable promo that does its job, but it's generic. Nothing makes it urgent or personal, and the CTAs are plain category buttons.

## What's working

- The stripe theme ties the hero, copy and both product grids together.
- The bold stacked headline puts the offer first, and the free shipping line is visible.
- The product tiles show name and price, and "New Arrival" flags mark the new items.
- The women's and men's sections each have their own CTA pair, so a clicker can go straight to their category.
- The hero photo is strong, uncluttered and on-season.

## What's weak

- The tiered offer is three lines of near-identical text, and the $75, $100 and $125 thresholds are hard to compare at a glance.
- There is no visible deadline in the body. The 10/28 end date is only in the fine print.
- The CTAs are generic outlined buttons ("WOMEN'S TOPS", "WOMEN'S SWEATERS") with low contrast and no verb.
- There is no member benefit or personalization, which is a step back from the 10/24 and 10/25 member sends.
- The legal block at the bottom is long and dense, which undercuts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear number. Put "Up to 25% off" in the hero and show the tiers as a compact three-step bar, with the end date ("Ends tomorrow") in the hero.
- 2. Make the CTAs filled, high-contrast buttons with action copy, such as "Shop women's stripes".
- 3. Add a member line ("Members: extra perks apply") to keep continuity with the 10/24–10/25 sends.
- 4. Link each product tile to its product page and add a review count or star rating to the best sellers.
- 5. Cut the legal block down to a short summary with a link to the full terms.

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off $125 + free shipping, ends 10/28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tops, knits and rugby shirts from $9.99. Free shipping on every tier.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $75, $100 or $125 and save more. Last day tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links all returned HTTP 403 to the QA probe (12 of 12 probed), and about 20 images have missing or filename-only alt text. The source has no visible merge-token failures. SPF/DKIM and List-Unsubscribe could not be verified because the AgentMail relay drops those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including "Shop now", the logo, product images, category links and the Unsubscribe link. Every URL uses the same redirector, `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h44167db7-…&did=1419019685&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6170166&p2=…&p3=20261027`.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking by the ESP click tracker, not dead links.** Each link carries a per-recipient `rid`/`erid` and a different `id` suffix (`f895fecc`, `f895fece`, `f895fecb`, `f895ff05`–`f895ff0c`, `f895ff4b`). A real failure would be unlikely to hit all of them identically, but this can't be confirmed from the data. Final destinations were never resolved, so landing-page validity is unverified.
- **Duplicate tracked links:** "WOMEN'S TOPS" and "WOMEN'S SWEATERS" each appear twice with different IDs (`f895ff07`/`f895ff09` and `f895ff08`/`f895ff0a`). The same applies to the 1400px and 750px image pairs (`f895ff05`/`06`, `0b`/`0c`). These look like desktop/mobile variants toggled by `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. If both are clicked or auto-scanned, the click data double-counts.
- **"Shop now" link text contains invisible characters.** The QA output shows a leading zero-width non-joiner and BOM (`‌  ﻿Shop now`). This is padding or preheader-style filler that ends up inside the anchor text. Screen readers and link-text analyzers may read it oddly.
- **Probe coverage was limited:** 67 of 79 links were skipped (footer and social links were deprioritized).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 20 images are missing alt text.**
  - All of them are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-44/…`, covering products `1345017005`, `1360320003`, `1373732003`, `0983240083`, `1307511006`, `0570002206`, `1333561003`, `1364469001` and `1341533003`.
  - The QA text renders them as `[image: cq5dam.web…jpeg]`, so alt is absent or equal to the filename.
  - The linked product images therefore have no accessible name, and the link text becomes the image filename when images are blocked.
- **The tracking-pixel `<img>` (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,549485a5,1`) also lacks alt.** This is low impact if the image is hidden, but it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Web fonts are loaded via `@import`** (`fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css`). Gmail and several other clients ignore `@import` in `<style>`, so those clients depend on the fallback stack. The fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source, so it could not be checked.
- **The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block is stripped by most clients** and has the empty-value problems listed in section 4.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source or the subject line. Personalization appears limited to the `rid`, `erid` and `p5` tracking parameters.
- **The JSON-LD schema block has empty or stale values:**
  - `"subjectLine":""`
  - `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""` and `"description":""`
  - `"image":""` and `"url":""` on the PromotionCard

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. It should be checked against the raw message at the original receiver.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the From domain `email.hm.com` could not be validated.
- **The body Unsubscribe link exists but returned 403 to the probe**, with the same likely cause as in section 2. It uses the redirector with per-recipient `p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. It should be tested with a real browser user agent.
- **Plain-text part:** 522 of 600 characters are URLs (87%), above the 70% threshold. The text alternative is nearly all tracking links, which is a spam-filter risk.
- The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source and is not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Attribution relies entirely on the ESP redirector parameters (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1=DM6170166`, `p2`, `p3=20261027`).
- **Final landing URLs are unknown** because every probe hit a 403 at the redirector. Whether "Women's tops / sweaters" lead to matching H&M category pages, or whether the "up to 25% off" offer matches the landing page, cannot be verified.
- The subject line says "Stripe right: up to 25% off tops, knits & more". The only category links with readable text are Women's Tops and Women's Sweaters, so the "knits" claim is only loosely supported by link text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like user agent or a headless browser. Confirm the 403s are bot-blocking and resolve the final destinations and any UTM or landing-page parameters.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product images (for example "Women's striped top"), and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and decorative images.
3. Remove the zero-width characters from the "Shop now" anchor text, or move them outside the `<a>`.
4. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. The empty `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `image` and `url` fields and the stale 2018 `availabilityStarts` are template leftovers.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message at the final mailbox. If they are missing there, add them.
6. Add more descriptive text, or reduce the link density, in the plain-text alternative.
7. Confirm the web-font fallback stack covers clients that ignore `@import`.
