# Up to 40% off gifts for her
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a Member-gated Black Friday send, framed as gifting. The hero is a woman in a cream shearling coat. Under it, "EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW: UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is the clear headline. Compared with the 11/13 outerwear send, it has the same offer and a thinner execution. There are no priced products and no hero product to click.
- The "gifts for her" angle appears only in the subject and one body line ("a few names off your list"). Below that, four category tiles (Ladies who lounge, Stocking stuffers, Active essentials, Premium sweaters) are labeled generically, and the "Stocking stuffers" tile shows a scarf and jacket. The black top banner pushes a second offer: spend $50, get a $10 e-gift card. Two offers compete for attention, and the Sign in / Sign up block undercuts the member pitch.
- Judgement: a decent, clean template send, but the fourth near-identical "up to 40% for Members" message in four days. Cadence fatigue and the missing product proof hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero image is warm and seasonal, and it suits a gifting theme.
- "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" is large and the eye lands on it first.
- The alternating image/text tiles scan easily and have no visible render bugs.
- The Sign in / Sign up block and the "10% off your first purchase" line give non-members a reason to join.
- The e-gift card banner at the top is a concrete, gift-relevant incentive.

## What's weak

- The "for her" promise is barely delivered. The tile labels are generic, and the "Stocking stuffers" tile doesn't show stocking stuffers.
- There are no products, prices or reviews, so nothing shows what 40% off actually buys.
- Two offers (40% off for Members and the $50 e-gift card bonus) split attention. The banner is red on black, which is hard to read.
- Three similar CTAs sit stacked: Shop Now, Sign In, Sign Up. The primary "Shop Now" is a generic outline button.
- The asterisked offer depends on a long legal block. The 40% is Member-only and ends 11/16, and none of this is visible above the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the category tiles with four to six priced gift products. Show the Member price next to the regular price, as the 11/13 outerwear send did.
- 2. Pick one offer to lead with. Move the e-gift card to a footer strip or make it a clearly labeled add-on.
- 3. Rename the CTA to "Shop gifts for her" and link it to a gifts-for-her collection, not the general sale.
- 4. Show the end date in the hero ("Ends 11/16") for credible urgency.
- 5. Give non-members a single join CTA and drop the separate Sign in button.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Her list, sorted: cozy gifts from $15`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member pricing ends 11/16. Knits, loungewear and stocking stuffers she'll actually wear.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus get a $10 e-gift card when you spend $50. Early Black Friday, Members first.`
