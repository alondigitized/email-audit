# Today only: 20% off in the app
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is an app-download push dressed up as a Black Friday offer. The headline, "20% OFF IN THE APP," is clear and the pink knit hero is soft and on-brand. But the 20% is locked behind an app install and a code you have to find inside the app. The only buttons in the main block are "DOWNLOAD IOS" and "DOWNLOAD ANDROID", so nothing in the email lets you shop.
- The black block below it adds a second offer, "30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE." Red text on black is hard to read, and it competes with the hero. The email now carries three offers: the top banner's member access plus 10% off, 20% in-app, and 30% in-store. The eye has nowhere to settle. The fine print is long and dense, and the "Today only" urgency in the subject isn't backed up by the body, which only says the offer is valid through 11/01/2026.
- Compared with the 10/28 "Up to 25% off + free shipping ends today" send, which had one clear tiered offer and a shoppable CTA, this is a step back. It's an acquisition email, not a conversion email. Judgement: mediocre, with a weak funnel.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF IN THE APP" headline is large and unambiguous.
- The hero image is clean, seasonal and attractive, and the knit fits the fall season.
- The "SHOP BY" category grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives broad entry points.
- The "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" framing fits the date.

## What's weak

- The 20% off requires an app download and a code, and the CTAs only link to app stores, so there's no way to shop from the email.
- Red text on a black background (the "30% OFF" block, the body copy and the "FIND A STORE" button) has weak contrast and is hard to read.
- Three overlapping offers (member access + 10% off, 20% in-app, 30% in-store) dilute the message.
- The fine print is a huge wall of text, and the exclusions (sale items, furniture, lamps and more) undercut the offer.
- The hero is not linked to a visible product or CTA, and the two outlined buttons are visually weak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and one action. Lead with the in-app 20% and add a single filled "Get the app + claim 20%" button, or drop the app gate and make it shoppable.
- 2. Move the in-store 30% to a separate send, or shrink it to a one-line strip.
- 3. Fix the contrast. Use white or bold-red type on black at a larger size, or switch to a white background.
- 4. Add a "Shop the look" product link under the hero and show the code or redemption steps in two lines.
- 5. Condense the legal copy to a short summary and link out to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off code is inside the H&M app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: 20% off, app only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Download the app, grab your code, and shop before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 30% off select styles in stores through 11/7.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly standard for a large retailer's ESP template, but the QA run found 6 of 6 probed links returning 403 (including Unsubscribe). It also found a JSON-LD block with empty fields, 4 images without alt text, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The 403s are most likely bot-blocking of the tracking redirector, not a confirmed break.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on all probed `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` redirects.** This covers "Find a store" (`...f8a2341e`, `...f8a2341f`), "Shop now" (`...f8a233bc`, `...f8a233be`), the "H&M" logo link (`...f8a233bb`) and "Unsubscribe" (`...f8a23442`).
  - Every link failed with the same status on the same host. That points to the redirector rejecting the automated probe (UA/IP filtering), not 6 independent broken links.
  - Real-user behavior is unconfirmed. Re-test from a normal browser or mail client before treating these as live defects.
- **Final destinations are not observable.** The links are opaque redirect IDs, so I can't verify they land on the app store, a deep link, or the intended product page.
- **The probe covered 12 of 52 links.** The other 40 were skipped (footer, social), so the rest of the email is unverified.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** The "Shop now" anchor text is `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which carries ZWNJ/BOM characters and leading spaces. This is likely preheader or spacing padding that leaked into the anchor. It can affect screen-reader output and link-text matching.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing alt text.**
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` (`image.hm.com/.../1370269002_202604_LB_0176.jpg/`) are the main content images. Both are likely the desktop and mobile variants of one hero.
  - Two `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` images also lack alt text. Their `src` is a redirect URL, which is likely a tracking pixel or a dynamic image.
  - With images blocked, the email renders blank where the hero should be.
- **`@import` for web fonts** (`fabric.hmgroup.com/.../fonts.css`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`). Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, so these fonts only load in clients that support it. This is only an issue if the stack lacks fallbacks, which I couldn't confirm in the truncated source.
- **Responsive and Outlook handling is present.** The source has a viewport meta tag, `mso-hide:all`, and a `max-width:699px` breakpoint. I found no issues there.
- **The HTML is truncated.** I couldn't check the body markup, such as table roles, `lang` and `dir` attributes, or the title.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{ }}`, `%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source.
- **The JSON-LD block ships empty.**
  - `EmailMessage.subjectLine: ""`
  - `DiscountOffer.description: ""` and `discountCode: ""`
  - `availabilityEnds: ""`
  - `PromotionCard.url: ""` and `image: ""`
  - `availabilityStarts: "2018-12-31T19:59:59-04:00"`, a stale hardcoded date
  - Gmail's schema parser may ignore or reject the block, so the "20% off" promotion won't surface as an annotation. It reads like an unpopulated template.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link present but returned 403** (`...f8a23442`, with per-recipient `p4`/`p5` params). This is probably the same bot-block as the other links, so verify it manually. A link that actually fails would violate CAN-SPAM's requirement for a working opt-out.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the sender. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined through the relay. Sending from `newsletter@email.hm.com` on a branded subdomain is a good sign, but I can't confirm alignment.
- **Physical postal address:** it's not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking runs through the redirector's `id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid` and `p1`–`p3` params (`p1=DM6185629`, `p3=20261101`). Any UTM tagging would be applied after the redirect, so it can't be checked here.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be verified.** The subject promises "20% off in the app", but no link target is observable. Confirm that "Shop now" lands on an app deep link or store page, and that desktop users get a sensible fallback.
- **"Find a store" appears twice** (`f8a2341e`, `f8a2341f`) with distinct IDs, which is probably desktop and mobile variants. This is fine, but the link needs to resolve.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 6 failed redirects with a browser-like UA or a real client. If they pass, whitelist the probe. If they still fail, fix the redirector. Prioritize the Unsubscribe link.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message at the sender, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC there too.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`). Use `alt=""` for the tracking images.
4. Strip the zero-width characters and leading whitespace from the "Shop now" anchor text.
5. Populate the JSON-LD (`subjectLine`, `description`, `discountCode`, real `availabilityStarts`/`availabilityEnds`, `url`, `image`), or remove the block.
6. Trim URL volume in the plain-text part (514/592 characters are URLs) by adding more descriptive text around the links.
7. Confirm the redirect targets and any UTM tagging end to end. Check that "Shop now" opens the app or app-store flow that the subject line promises.
