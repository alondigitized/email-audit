# Score 30% off in-store all weekend
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M running the exact same "up to 30% off back-to-school" promotion for the third day in a row — 7/30's "Up to 30% off your back-to-school checklist," 7/31's "Campus favorites from $6.99," and now 8/1's "Score 30% off in-store all weekend." The creative is clean and well-organized: a clear hero offer, six shoppable products with visible sale pricing, a tax-free callout, and simple dual CTAs (Shop Now / Find a Store). Nothing is broken and the visual hierarchy does its job.
- The problem is the subject-to-body mismatch. "Score 30% off in-store all weekend" promises a location-specific, time-boxed event, but the rendered email never mentions "weekend" anywhere and the hero headline actually broadens the offer to "IN-STORE & ONLINE" — undercutting the exact urgency hook that got the subject line written in the first place. Combined with three near-identical 30%-off sends in four days, this reads like inventory of a genuinely good creative being stretched past its shelf life.
- Solid execution, weak differentiation and a subject that oversells what the body delivers — call it a 7.

## What's working

- Clear single hero offer ("UP TO 30% OFF SELECT STYLES") sits right below the header with no competing message.
- Six real, priced products with visible strikethrough pricing (e.g., $14.99 was $19.99) give the offer concrete proof.
- Tax-free callout with a lifestyle photo (kids in jerseys) adds a second, distinct reason to click beyond the discount.
- Dual CTA pattern (Shop Now / Find a Store) correctly serves both online and in-store shoppers.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is tidy top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Subject says "in-store all weekend" but the body headline says "IN-STORE & ONLINE" with zero mention of weekend timing anywhere in the render — the specific urgency hook isn't backed up.
- This is the third consecutive back-to-school/30%-off send in four days (7/30, 7/31, 8/1) with no new creative angle — fatigue risk for anyone who's opened the last two.
- Member/loyalty benefit ("$5 Member Bonus Reward") is buried in the dense fine-print block at the bottom instead of being surfaced near the offer.
- No countdown, date range, or "ends Sunday" marker anywhere — the "all weekend" urgency exists only in the subject line, nowhere in the visual hierarchy of the email itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the subject: add a visible "THIS WEEKEND ONLY" or date-stamped banner near the hero so the urgency promised in the subject actually shows up when the email opens.
- 2. Differentiate from the last two sends — swap "back-to-school deals" framing for something that hasn't run twice already this week (e.g., lead with the in-store-only angle explicitly, since that's the actual news here).
- 3. Surface the Member Bonus Reward near the hero CTA instead of leaving it in the disclaimer block — it's a real incentive being wasted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `In-store only: 30% off, this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekend pass to 30% off in stores`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get an extra $5 back — in stores this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No online version of this one — find your store`
