# The best denim deals now from $19.99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, denim-focused H&M send, and the subject finally matches the body. "The best denim deals now from $19.99" is followed by a hero offering "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" and then four product grids of jeans, each with a sale price in red and the old price struck through. That is a stronger match than the 2026-11-23 "Activewear deals now from $9.99" send, whose body never showed its headline price.
- The structure is clear, but the email is very long. It runs from a small hero, through women's, men's and kids' denim blocks, into a holiday-shop block that has nothing to do with denim. The "Member perk" banner, the Klarna line and the dense legal text add noise. The best offer is "up to 50% off", and it is buried under a generic "Black Friday Deals" header. The $19.99 price point from the subject is only visible as a small price tag in the grids, not as a headline.
- Verdict: a competent shopping email that is better than the app-push sends of 11/20 and 11/21. It is still a generic H&M template with a long scroll and a diluted hero. I'd call it a 6 or 7.

## What's working

- The subject and body line up: a denim promise is followed by denim grids with visible red sale prices.
- The "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" headline sits near the top, and the SHOP NOW and FIND A STORE buttons are clearly visible.
- The product grids are labeled with the section names "Winter Washes", "Fresh Fits" and "Playful Pairs". Each item has a name and a price, so the email is scannable.
- Alternating full-width lifestyle images with 2x2 product grids gives the scroll a good rhythm.
- It continues the better direction of the 11/22–11/23 sends: shopping first, not app installs.

## What's weak

- The hero is a moody, dark, cropped model image with a generic "Black Friday Deals" header. It does not say "denim" or "$19.99", the specifics the subject promised.
- The "Member perk: shop now, pay later with Klarna" banner at the very top competes with the offer and pushes the hero down.
- The "Playful Pairs" kids' block and the "Holiday Shop" block dilute the focus. A denim email should end on denim.
- The grids have no review or fit cues, and the product text is tiny. Only one button sits above the fold, and each grid item has no visible button.
- The footer is a long wall of legal copy, and the stacked 11/20–11/24 promos make the cadence feel relentless.

- 2. Drop the Klarna banner, or move it to the footer, so the offer is the first thing readers see.
- 3. Add one specific hero product with a "Shop the Ultra High Rise Wide Jeans" button. Add a "Shop women's / men's / kids'" button row under the hero.
- 4. Cut the holiday block, or move it below a clear break. Keep the denim story to three blocks.
- 5. Add a fit or review cue to each grid, such as "Top rated" or "Baggy / Straight / Wide fit".
- 6. Shorten the legal copy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based template with responsive CSS, but 12 of the 12 probed links, including the unsubscribe link, returned 403 to the QA prober. About 35 images have no `alt` text, and the JSON-LD block still holds empty template placeholders. The 403s most likely come from the click tracker blocking automated requests, so they are unconfirmed as real failures (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They are wrapped in the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0155f7b8-…` redirector. This covers "Shop now" (several IDs, e.g. `…f8df9ee4`, `…f9f50`), "Find a store" (`…f8df9ee5`, `…ee7`), the "H&M" logo link (`…ec3`) and "Unsubscribe" (`…f9f78`).
  - A uniform 403 across every link, with the same host and the same ID prefix, points to bot or WAF filtering of the prober. A real broken-link failure would more likely show 404 or 5xx.
  - The data can't show whether the links work for real recipients. The final destinations were never resolved, so they could not be checked.
- **Recipient identifiers sit in every tracking URL.** `rid=3326930539` and `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D` are the same value in raw and base64 form. The unsubscribe link also carries `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746…`. Forwarding the email exposes these IDs, and the base64 `erid` adds nothing as obfuscation.
- **146 of 158 links were not probed.** Only the top 12 by click likelihood were checked, so coverage is partial.
- **Link text contains invisible characters.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8df9ec4`, `…ec6`) have link text prefixed with zero-width characters (`‌ ﻿`: ZWNJ and BOM/ZWNBSP). These are probably preheader-padding or spacing hacks, but they pollute accessible link names and analytics text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 35 images.** Examples:
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` (desktop and mobile hero variants, repeated across 4 modules).
  - Product grid images such as `e055cd25…jpg?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`, plus `bed7de21…`, `5e142650…` and others.
  - A tracking image whose `src` is `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h0155f7b8-…,1`. It is probably an open pixel, and it should have `alt=""` plus explicit dimensions.
  - With images blocked, the email loses its product content and CTA context.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root element is `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">`. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation without it.
- **Duplicate image sets.** Each module ships both 1400px and 750px sources (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` swaps) and both `imWidth=344` and `imWidth=322` product variants. Hidden duplicates can still be announced by assistive tech in clients that ignore `display:none`, and they inflate payload.
- **Stale asset paths.** Two hero images come from `.../global-campaigns/season-04/start-page-assets/home/week-43/...` in a `wk48` send. This points to reused or unrefreshed modules.
- **Client-compat features.** `@import` web fonts (`fabric.hmgroup.com`, `oces.css`) are ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back to system fonts. Check that the fallback font stacks are set. The `<style>` blocks are not inlined and rely on `#MessageViewBody` hacks, which is fragile.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so MSO conditional comments, preheader and footer markup could not be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If it holds on the original message, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders at this volume.
- **Authentication-Results was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated cause. Check `email.hm.com` alignment directly in DNS or on the raw message.
- **The in-body Unsubscribe link (`…f9f78`) returned 403** to the prober. Verify manually that it resolves for a real user. The link is present and prominent, which meets the basic CAN-SPAM requirement. The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source.
- **The plain-text part is 514/592 characters URLs** (over 70%). It is almost all tracking links, a minor spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters on any wrapped link.** Attribution relies entirely on the redirector's `p1=DM6236608`, `p2=0982dd0a…` and `p3=20261124` parameters. If the redirect doesn't append UTMs, the site's analytics may lack campaign and medium tagging. This can't be confirmed because the redirects were never followed.
- **The landing page is unverified.** Every probe stopped at the 403 tracker, so there is no data on whether "The best denim deals… $19.99" lands on a matching denim or sale page. Alignment is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the redirector links with a real browser user-agent, or follow the redirects from an allowlisted IP, to separate bot-blocking from real breakage. Confirm the unsubscribe link first.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to content images (product name and price) and `alt=""` to decorative images and the tracking pixel.
3. Set `<html lang="en">` (or the matching locale).
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message, bypassing the relay. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email.hm.com`.
5. Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD fields, including the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date.
6. Stop exposing a raw and base64 copy of the recipient ID (`rid`, `erid`, `p4`) in forwardable URLs, or use opaque tokens.
7. Confirm that the redirector appends UTM or campaign parameters on the landing page.
