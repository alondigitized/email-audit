# 15% off or 20% off all weekend
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M sticking to the tiered-discount playbook — "15% off $80 or 20% off $100" — a step up in transparency from the vague "up to 30% off" ceiling that dominated 7/30 through 8/3, but a step down in generosity once you actually read the thresholds. The creative is the exact same repeating-module template H&M has run all week: hero offer block, four nav pills (WOMEN/MEN/KIDS 2-8Y/KIDS 9-14Y), then FOR HER/FOR HIM/FOR THEM product grids, then a SHOP BY footer grid. Nothing here distinguishes it from 8/2's "Up to 30% off ends tomorrow" beyond the discount math.
- The hero copy ("Refresh your denim stack with new baggy, barrel, and loose-fit pairs") is the one genuinely fresh beat — it gives the email a product focus (denim) that the pure percentage-off sends lacked. But by the time a subscriber who's opened four or five of these in the last ten days sees another "SHOP NOW" wall of category buttons, the fatigue is real.
- Clean execution, no render bugs, honest math in the hero — but this is cadence-fatigue territory. A subscriber who's seen 8/1, 8/2, 8/3's near-identical sends will skim and archive rather than click.

## What's working

- The tiered offer is stated with real numbers directly in the hero (15% OFF $80 OR 20% OFF $100), not a vague "up to X%" that requires clicking through to find the catch.
- Denim-specific hero copy ("baggy, barrel, and loose-fit pairs") gives the send an actual product hook instead of being a pure discount blast.
- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks across the full scroll.
- Clear segmentation via WOMEN/MEN/KIDS 2-8Y/KIDS 9-14Y pills right under the hero, letting a shopper self-select before even reaching product images.

## What's weak

- This is the same FOR HER/FOR HIM/FOR THEM template H&M has run repeatedly this week (8/1, 8/2, 8/3) — visually indistinguishable at a glance from three prior sends.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW" and routes to broad category pages, not the specific denim styles pictured — there's no single hero product to click into.
- Member/loyalty benefit ("H&M Member Bonus Reward") is buried in the fine-print terms block at the bottom, not surfaced anywhere in the visible body for members who'd want to stack it.
- The spend thresholds ($80/$100) mean this reads generous in the headline but requires a larger basket than a flat percentage-off — no messaging softens that trade-off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace at least one "SHOP NOW" button with a denim-specific CTA ("Shop New Denim") linking straight to the featured fits, since the hero copy already sets up denim as the story — right now the click destination undercuts the specific pitch.
- 2. Break the template. Three near-identical FOR HER/FOR HIM/FOR THEM sends in a week trains subscribers to skim past this layout — swap in a single hero product shot with the denim styles called out by name/fit.
- 3. Surface the member bonus reward in the visible body (a small badge near the hero offer), not just in the bottom disclaimer — it's a real incentive currently invisible to anyone not reading terms.
- 4. Reconsider offer framing — "15% off $80 or 20% off $100" is honest but asks the reader to do math; consider "Spend $80, save 15% — spend $100, save 20%" for faster comprehension.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New denim styles: 15-20% off this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baggy, barrel, loose-fit — up to 20% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $80 for 15% off, $100 for 20% off — new fits inside`
