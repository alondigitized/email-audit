# Labor Day Event: Up to 50% off starts now
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M finally back to the "sitewide" playbook and it's a strong send — "UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" is unambiguous, bold, and above the fold, no scrolling required. Compared to the 08/29–08/31 run of "20% OFF MEMBER" (6.00/10 each), this offer is more than double the discount and applies sitewide rather than being gated to a member-only sub-block, which is a real escalation, not just a rename.
- The layout is pure category-sweep after the hero — Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Beauty tiles stacked twice (once as lifestyle pairs, once as "Shop By" thumbnails), which is redundant real estate. There's no single hero product or outfit story here; it's a discount-first, browse-everything email, closer in spirit to 09/02's "new arrivals" sweep than to an editorial send like 08/24's "Modern heritage."
- Solid, clean, on-brand, and the offer itself is the strongest lever H&M has pulled in this run. The double category grid is unnecessary bloat, and the member-upsell placement right under the CTA slightly muddies "is this offer for everyone or members only."

## What's working

- "UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" is large, bold, and impossible to miss — best headline clarity of the recent run.
- Two clear CTA buttons ("SHOP NOW" and "BECOME A MEMBER") give both existing and prospective members a next step.
- Clean black-and-white H&M branding with no render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- Category imagery (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home/Beauty) is well-lit and demographically broad, covering the full family shopper.
- Legal/fine-print block is present but pushed to the footer, not cluttering the offer itself.

## What's weak

- The category grid repeats twice (lifestyle pairs, then "Shop By" thumbnails) — pure redundancy that pushes real content further down.
- No hero product or single linked item — everything routes to category landing pages, not a specific piece.
- "Want 10% off on top? Become an H&M Member*" sits directly under the CTAs and could read as diluting the 50% headline into a smaller, member-gated number.
- No urgency/deadline visible in the body (the 09/08/2026 end date is buried in footer fine print, not in the hero copy).
- Subject says "up to 50%" but hero doesn't clarify what qualifies for the top discount vs. the baseline — could feel like bait-and-switch once shoppers click through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut one of the two category grids — keep the "Shop By" thumbnail row only, and use the freed space for a single hero product callout (e.g., "Best sellers already dropping" with a real linked item) to give people something concrete to click.
- 2. Move the end date ("Ends 09/08") into the hero as a visible urgency line — right now it only lives in dense footer legal text.
- 3. Re-order the member upsell below the category grid instead of directly under the main CTA, so "up to 50% off" reads as the singular, undiluted headline.
- 4. Add one line of specificity to the hero — e.g., "up to 50% off outerwear & layers" — since the subhead already name-checks "layers and iconic outerwear" but the offer feels generic by comparison.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
This transactional/marketing email is HTML with tracking-wrapped redirect links, all of which are returning HTTP 403 on probe — a broken click path across every CTA, image, and unsubscribe link.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed material links resolve through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` click-tracking redirector and every one returned **403 Forbidden**, including both "Shop now" CTAs, the "H&M" header link, both "BECOME A MEMBER" links, all four product images, and the footer "Unsubscribe" link.
- 403s on a tracking redirector typically indicate an expired/invalid `id`/`erid` token, bot/UA blocking on the click-tracking domain, or a link-scanning proxy (e.g., corporate email security) being blocked upstream. Since this affects 100% of probed links including Unsubscribe, this looks like a systemic redirector-side issue rather than an isolated broken link.
- 53 additional (mostly footer/utility/social) links were not probed per the tool's click-likelihood ranking — unconfirmed pass/fail status for those.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, spanning the tracking pixel/redirect image and all six product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs, e.g. `.../1352080001_202604_LB_2978.jpg/`, `.../1359341002_202604_LB_0092.jpg/`, `.../1232243010_202604_LB_6570.jpg/`, `.../1244463002_202502_LB_0859.jpg/`, `.../1341968002_202604_LB_0169.jpg/`, `.../2551016_PP_5093b_0035.jpg/`). This breaks screen-reader accessibility and leaves no fallback text if images fail to load.
- Extensive mobile-responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:699px)`) and MSO/Outlook-conditional hide/show classes (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.hide-webkit`, `#MessageViewBody`) are present, indicating multi-client rendering was accounted for at build time — no structural issues found in the truncated source.
- HTML source was truncated in this review, so full-document rendering validation (e.g., closing tags, MSO conditional comment balance) could not be completed.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded JSON-LD (`schema.org` `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard`) has empty string values for `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, and `url`, and a stale `availabilityStarts` date of `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00` — these appear to be unresolved template placeholders/merge fields that failed to populate, which will affect Gmail/Outlook annotated promotion cards and structured-data-driven surfaces.
- No visible personalization merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`) were found in the truncated HTML, but this can't be fully ruled out given truncation.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present (`p4`/`p5` params suggest subscriber/list identifiers) but currently returns a 403, same as all other tracked links — functionally broken unsubscribe is a CAN-SPAM risk if not transient.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI is not confirmed available (note: may be a relay-capture limitation rather than absence in the original send).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present on the sampled links; H&M instead uses proprietary redirector params (`id`, `did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`, e.g. `p1=DM6012929`, `p3=20260903`) for attribution, which is consistent across all sampled links.
- Because every redirect returns 403 before reaching `hm.com`, actual landing-page alignment (URL structure, page match to CTA) cannot be verified in this audit.
- Product image `src` values point directly to `image.hm.com/content/dam/...` (not through the tracking redirector) and appear well-formed, separate from the broken `<a>` click-throughs.

7. Recommendations
- Priority 1: Investigate the redirector 403s — check whether `t19.email.hm.com` is blocking the QA tool's user-agent/IP, whether the `erid`/`id` token has expired, or whether this is a genuine outage on the ESP (Responsys, given the `t19.email.hm.com` domain pattern) side. A broken Unsubscribe link is a compliance exposure and should be treated as urgent regardless of cause.
- Add `alt` text to all 12 flagged images for accessibility and graceful degradation when images are blocked.
- Populate the empty JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) or remove the schema block if not in use — empty structured data can cause inconsistent rendering of Gmail promo/annotated cards.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the ESP (re-check with a direct-to-mailbox capture rather than via the AgentMail relay, since the relay may simply not be forwarding these headers).
- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against a raw received copy, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
