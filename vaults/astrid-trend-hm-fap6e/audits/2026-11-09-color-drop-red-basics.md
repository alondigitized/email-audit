# Color drop: Red basics
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal "Color drop: Red basics" send. The hero is a four-up grid of red sweater, wide-leg pants, joggers and crewneck, with a big black "COLOR DROP: RED BASICS" headline above it. The eye lands on the product and the headline right away, and the red against white is festive without being noisy. A second "New-Season Layers" block with a Fair Isle knit adds some range.
- The offer is the weak spot. "UP TO 30% OFF NEW-SEASON JEANS AND MORE" sits in a black banner at the top. It has nothing to do with the red basics hero, and its red text on black is hard to read, especially the "+ Get 10% off your first purchase as a Member" line. The thing the email is actually about has no price or offer attached. The footer then has a wall of legal fine print, including "excluding bras" and "Final Sale" exclusions, which undercuts the honesty of "up to 30%".
- Compared with the 11/06–11/08 sends (tiered 15/20/25% off plus free shipping, 7/10), this is a step down in offer clarity. It is a brand-mood email dressed over a sale banner. It is pleasant and on-season but low-urgency, and I'd call it a 5.

## What's working

- The red-on-white four-product grid is cohesive and instantly scannable.
- The headline "COLOR DROP: RED BASICS" is bold, large, and matches the subject.
- The copy ("perfect for gifting (or keeping)") ties into the holiday season.
- Each section has a single outlined "SHOP NOW" button, so the CTAs are easy to find.
- The Fair Isle sweater image is well shot and reads as seasonal.

## What's weak

- The top banner's red text on black is low contrast. The Member line is nearly illegible.
- The banner offer (jeans, up to 30%) doesn't match the red basics hero, so the email sends two messages.
- All three "SHOP NOW" buttons are generic, with no verb plus noun, and the hero button gives no sense of price or product.
- The hero products carry no names or prices, and there is no social proof or sizing detail.
- The footer fine print is long and dense. "Up to 30%" ends up with many exclusions and a 11/11/2026 end date that appears nowhere above the fold.
- The big white gaps below each section make the layout feel loose.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer and the hero one story. Put "Red basics, up to 30% off" in the banner or headline, in white on black or in a high-contrast treatment.
- 2. Add a visible deadline ("Ends 11/11") near the hero button. It is currently buried in the fine print.
- 3. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific, such as "Shop red sweaters" or "Shop the red edit".
- 4. Add prices or "from $X" under the product grid, and link each product to its own page.
- 5. Tighten the spacing and cut or condense the legal block.
- 6. Pull the Member benefit out of the low-contrast banner and give it its own readable line.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The red sweater set, from $19.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, joggers and wide-leg pants in the season's boldest color. Up to 30% off through Nov 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gift it or keep it. Members get an extra 10% off the first order.`

# Technical Audit: H&M "Color drop: Red basics"

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links return HTTP 403 (including Unsubscribe). They all go through the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector, so this looks like bot-blocking of the QA probe, not confirmed breakage. Separately, the source has missing image alt text and an empty JSON-LD block. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because of how the relay captures headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures (12/12, all 403):** Every probed link uses the same redirector (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=ha732c7fd-…,54983c87,<linkid>&did=1419263111&rid=3326930539&…`). This includes the "SHOP NOW" CTAs (`f8b96086`, `f8b96087`, `f8b96092`, `f8b96093`, `f8b96096`, `f8b96097`), "Shop now" (`f8b96074`, `f8b96076`), the "H&M" logo link (`f8b96073`), both image links (`f8b9607e`, `f8b9607f`) and Unsubscribe (`f8b960ba`).
- **Likely cause:** A uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to the ESP rejecting the non-browser or non-recipient probe. It is not evidence of a dead campaign. This is unverified, and the QA data can't distinguish the two cases.
- **Opaque tracking:** Final destinations can't be seen from the HTML. Each link is an ID-based redirect, so destination correctness can't be confirmed from source.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 links were probed and 50 were skipped. Footer, utility and social links were deprioritized, so their status is unknown.
- **Image links:** Two links have only an image as their content (`f8b9607e`, `f8b9607f`). These are the 1400px and 750px variants of the same hero, and they resolve to the same redirector pattern.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** The QA flags 14 `<img>` elements. The 1400px and 750px variants of each image are paired (the `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` entries, with duplicate source paths ending in `1260555031_202604_PP_0204.jpg`, `1193602105_202604_PP_0206.jpg`, `0932722157_202604_PP_0205.jpg`, `1243667069_202604_PP_1302.jpg` and the `WA10265P09-Heritage-…` assets). Two more tracking-pixel-style images under `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…` are also missing alt.
- **Effect:** Screen readers and image-blocked clients fall back to the filename (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`) as the link text. That filename is also what the QA reported as link text for the image links.
- **Duplicated image markup:** Each image appears in 1400px and 750px versions. This matches the `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` pattern in the `optimize-css` block. Screen readers in clients that ignore `display:none` may read both copies. Only `mso-hide` and display toggles are used.
- **Non-issues observed:** `<meta name="viewport">` is present, charset is UTF-8, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set, and `format-detection` is set.
- **Web fonts:** The two `@import url(...)` calls for `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com` fonts are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. No fallback is visible in the truncated source, so this can't be confirmed as a problem.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated HTML. The source was truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

**JSON-LD (schema.org) is empty or stale:**

- `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`
- `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, which is a 2018 template default

Empty fields in this block are populated incorrectly or not at all. Gmail may ignore or reject the markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. A one-click unsubscribe header is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders. Verify against the raw message headers from the original send.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. This is a relay artifact. The sender domain `email.hm.com` should be checked directly.
- **In-body unsubscribe:** Present, via the redirector link `f8b960ba` with per-recipient parameters (`p4`, `p5`). The probe returned 403, so it is not confirmed working.
- **Physical postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source. This is unchecked, not a failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Tracking is carried in the redirector's `p1=DM6207806`, `p2=…` and `p3=20261109` parameters. Whether `utm_*` is appended after redirect can't be confirmed.
- **Landing page alignment:** Not verifiable. Every probe returned 403 and the final URLs weren't resolved, so there is no evidence about whether "Red basics" CTAs land on a matching page.
- **Asset paths:** The image paths reference `wk-46` (week 46) and `wk-45` (`WA10265P09-Heritage-…`). One group of assets is a week older than the rest. This could be a reused asset, but it is only a possible issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links** with a browser-like user agent or from a seeded inbox, and confirm the unsubscribe link especially. If real recipients also get a 403, treat it as P0.
2. **Add `alt` text** to all 14 flagged images, and use `alt=""` for decorative duplicates. Also add `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden` on the hidden 1400px/750px twin.
3. **Fix or remove the JSON-LD block.** Populate `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds` and PromotionCard `image` and `url`, replace the 2018 `availabilityStarts`, or drop the block.
4. **Verify the headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) from the original message source, not the relay copy.
5. **Reduce the plain-text URL ratio.** The plain-text part is 512/590 characters of URLs (86%), against the 70% warning threshold. Add descriptive text to the plain-text alternative.
6. **Check landing pages and UTMs** by following the redirects manually, and confirm each `SHOP NOW` destination matches the "Red basics" collection.
