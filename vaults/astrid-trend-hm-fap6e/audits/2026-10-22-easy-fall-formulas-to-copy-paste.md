There are no formulas, no outfit breakdowns and no "wear it with" pairings. The hero's "BELT $44.99" overlay is tiny, and the belt is not the focus of the shot. ## What's working

- The hero is striking, with a leather coat, plaid top and amber sunglasses. It sets a clear fall mood immediately.
- The "NEW FALL UNIFORM" headline is bold, with one short paragraph of supporting copy and a single clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: hero, headline, CTA, then products. There are no render bugs or broken images.
- The product callouts show name, price and a "New Arrival" tag, so prices are visible.
- The "Shop by" category grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives a useful escape route for browsers.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, discount or urgency anywhere. That is a drop-off from the 10/16–10/20 sends, with no reason to act today.
- The subject promises "formulas to copy & paste," but the body has no formulas, no outfit pairings and no "get the look" structure.
- Only two products are shown. The hero belt ($44.99) is not linked to a visible product card, and its overlay text is tiny.
- The price points ($379 pants, $99 jacket) clash with the "easy" framing, and the products are not obviously shoppable as a set.
- There is no social proof, no member benefit and no sizing or fit detail. The generic "SHOP NOW" CTA is repeated and never made specific.
- The large white gaps and the long footer, with its two legal paragraphs, dilute the email's momentum.

- 1. Make the email deliver on the subject. Build 3 literal "formulas" (e.g., "Leather pant + cream blouse + belt") with a shoppable item list under each, and a "Shop the look" CTA per formula.
- 2. Add an offer or member hook, such as free shipping or a member price, so the email has a reason to act on now.
- 3. Turn the hero's "BELT $44.99" into a real linked product card. Add a mid-price option, since the featured items skew expensive.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop the fall uniform" and give each product card its own CTA.
- 5. Tighten the vertical whitespace and move the category grid higher.

# Technical Audit: "Easy fall formulas to copy & paste" (H&M)

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links return 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` tracking redirector, and 8 images have no alt text. The HTML also has empty JSON-LD markup and a few CSS defects. The 403s are probably the redirector blocking the QA probe, not a real failure for recipients.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403s on every probed link.** The links are "Shop now" (`…f888a09d`, `…f888a09e`, `…f888a091`, `…f888a093`), the "H&M" logo (`…f888a090`), image links (`…f888a09b`, `09c`, `09f`, `0a0`, `0a3`, `0a4`) and Unsubscribe (`…f888a0c9`).
  - The 403 on the unsubscribe link is the most serious if it's real, but it is unconfirmed.
  - The same status on every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to bot or user-agent filtering by the ESP redirector. It does not point to bad individual links.
  - Re-test with a browser-like user agent or a real client before treating any of these as broken.
- **Destinations can't be verified.** Each link is a click-tracking redirect (`/r/?id=…&did=1418952644&rid=3326930539&erid=…`). The final URLs and landing-page status are hidden behind it.
- **Zero-width characters in anchor text.** Two "Shop now" links contain invisible characters (`‌  ﻿Shop now`, a ZWNJ plus a BOM/ZWNBSP). These are likely preheader/spacing hacks, but they can affect screen-reader output and link-text matching.
- **Mostly unprobed.** Only 12 links were probed and 43 were skipped, so the remaining links, including footer and social, have no status data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 8 images.** The images are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` (×3), `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` (×2), `gQ2ISmGBaPhBkS533wXA=`, and two `t19.email.hm.com/r/?…,1` sources. All are served from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm-images/global-campaigns/…/wa10264p08-fall-drop-3/`.
  - The names in the report are the QA tool's fallback labels, not actual alt values.
  - Most of these images sit inside `<a>` tags, so the links have no accessible name. They are also blank when images are blocked.
- **Empty JSON-LD (`<script type="application/ld+json">`).**
  - `EmailMessage.subjectLine` is `""`.
  - `DiscountOffer` has empty `discountCode`, `description` and `availabilityEnds`, and a stale `availabilityStarts` of `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`.
  - `PromotionCard` has empty `image` and `url`.
  - Gmail will ignore or may reject this markup, so it adds nothing.
- **Invalid CSS in `#optimize-css`.** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` is not a valid value (`auto` isn't allowed for `line-height`), so clients drop the declaration.
- **Breakpoint overlap at 699px.** `@media (max-width:699px)` and `@media (min-width:699px)` both match at exactly 699px. The two `!important` rule sets collide there.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** The `<style>` block loads `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com` fonts with `@import`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so the email falls back to the system fonts. The fallback stack is cut off in the truncated source and can't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source, and the subject has none. The source is truncated, so the body and footer are not covered.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** Every redirect returned 403, so the final URLs and their content are unknown.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in the URLs.** `rid`, `erid` and `p2` are in every link, including the unsubscribe URL. These are standard for ESP tracking, but forwarded emails will carry them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a realistic user agent, or follow redirects manually. If the unsubscribe link still returns 403 for real clients, fix it first.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all 8 images, and use `alt=""` for the tracking pixel and spacer. Image-only links need descriptive alt text so the links have accessible names.
3. Remove the empty JSON-LD blocks, or populate `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `description` and the `PromotionCard` `image` and `url`. Fix the 2018 `availabilityStarts`.
4. Replace `line-height:auto` with `normal` or a numeric value. Change `min-width:699px` to `700px` to remove the overlap.

6. Strip the zero-width characters from the CTA anchor text.
7. Trim the plain-text part so it has more text and fewer long tracking URLs.
