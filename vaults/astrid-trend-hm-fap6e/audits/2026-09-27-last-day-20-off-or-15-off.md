# Last day: 20% off or 15% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same tiered-discount promo H&M has been running all week, now repackaged with urgency ("LAST DAY") slapped on top. The hero is clean and legible — "15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100" is impossible to miss — but everything below it is an enormous, undifferentiated scroll of category grids (Women/Men/Kids/Home) with no cross-sell logic or editorial hook. It reads less like a curated send and more like a full catalog dump with a discount banner bolted on.
- Compared to 09-25's "15% off or 20% off all weekend" (6.00/10) and 09-26's cardigan-focused version (7.00/10), this send drops the product focus entirely — no hero image of a specific item, no lifestyle photography up top, just text-on-white urgency copy. The "last day" framing is the one genuinely new beat versus the last three sends, but it's a weak payoff for a subscriber who's now seen the same $75/$100 tier structure four times in a row.
- Verdict: functional and scannable, but a cadence fatigue risk — the offer is legible, the urgency is real, but the creative effort is the laziest of this stretch.

## What's working

- The offer banner up top is unambiguous — bold "LAST DAY / 15% OFF $75 OR 20% OFF $100" with no clutter.
- Four clean category-shortcut buttons (Women/Men/Baby/Kids/Home) give quick navigation for subscribers who know what they want.
- Each product tile shows price, name, and "New Arrival" tag consistently — no broken layout or missing data.
- "UP TO 30% OFF SELECT BASICS & KNITS" sub-line in the very top strip adds a second offer signal for bargain hunters.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text aligns, grid is consistent across all four categories.

## What's weak

- No hero product shot or lifestyle image — the top of the email is pure text/buttons, which is a flatter open than every recent H&M send.
- Massive length: four full category grids (8+ products) before reaching the footer is a lot of scroll for a "last day" urgency email that should be driving fast action.
- The "last day" urgency isn't reinforced anywhere below the top banner — no countdown, no repeated CTA lower in the email for people who scroll past the top.
- This is the fourth $75/$100 tiered-discount structure in five sends (09-22 through 09-27) — the offer is now stale, not urgent.
- Two competing discount frames (30% off basics/knits banner vs. 15%/20% tiered offer) create mixed messaging about what the actual deal is.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the catalog dump.** Replace the four full category grids with one hero product shot + a single "Shop the Sale" CTA, then the category shortcuts. Length is the biggest drag on this email — most subscribers won't scroll past Women.
- 2. **Reinforce urgency visually**, not just in the subject — add a countdown or "ends tonight at midnight" line near the top banner, and repeat a smaller CTA banner before the footer for scrollers.
- 3. **Resolve the two-offer confusion** — pick either the 30%-off-basics framing or the 15%/20%-tiered framing as the single lead offer; running both dilutes urgency.
- 4. **Vary the creative from the last four sends** — this brand needs a genuinely different visual (e.g., a single styled outfit shot) rather than another grid, or subscribers will start tuning these out.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last shot at 15-20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab fall basics before the discount disappears`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals across Women, Men, Kids & Home — today only`
