# The new arrivals on our fall watch list
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M defaulting to "browse everything" mode — "FALLING FOR NEW" is a category-sweep email, not the seasonal watch-list story the subject promises. There's no editorial hook like 08/27's "Modern heritage" or 08/24's "In focus," and no real discount block like the 08/29–08/31 member-20%-off run; the only offer is a top-banner "20% OFF H&M HOME with $60 order" that's oddly buried above the actual hero and disconnected from the email's real content (Desk to Dinner, Office MVPs, Hardworkers, Spice Things Up).
- The layout is clean and the render is bug-free, but it's six near-identical modules stacked vertically (photo → headline → button → photo → headline → button...), each linking to a generic category page rather than a specific product. It reads like a site directory, not a curated "fall watch list."
- Compared to the brand's recent cadence — heavy discount emails all last week, then editorial pivot on 09/01 — this is a third tonal shift in three days, and it's the weakest of the run because it has neither a strong story (like 09/01) nor a strong offer (like 08/29-31). Judgement: mediocre, forgettable, mid-pack send.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual template — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.
- The Home offer (20% off, $60 min, expiring today) is time-bound and specific, giving at least one concrete hook.
- "Shop By" footer grid gives a clear category index for anyone who scrolls past the main content.
- Model diversity across sections (women's, men's, kids, home) covers the full catalog in one send.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a curated "fall watch list" but the body is six generic category dumps (Desk to Dinner, Office MVPs, Hardworkers, Spice Things Up) with no unifying "new arrivals" or "watch list" framing.
- The one real offer (20% off Home) is stranded in a tiny top banner, disconnected visually from the Home section four scrolls down — most readers won't connect the two.
- Every CTA button says "SHOP NOW" or a category name — no product-specific links, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- No urgency, no pricing, no product detail (fabric, fit, price) anywhere in the main body — it's pure lifestyle imagery.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — six sections carry equal weight, so nothing signals "this is the thing to look at."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead story and cut the rest to a "more to explore" strip — right now six equal-weight sections compete for attention and none wins.
- 2. Move the Home 20% offer down into the actual Home section (or pull one hero offer into the top of the email) so the discount and the product are in the same place.
- 3. Swap generic CTAs ("SHOP NOW," "WOMEN," "MEN") for specific product callouts — e.g., "Shop the wide-leg trouser" — so clicks land on something a reader actually wants.
- 4. Add at least one price point or "new for fall" detail under a hero image to justify the "watch list" premise.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a heavily templated Adobe Campaign / H&M ESP structure with all links routed through a `t19.email.hm.com` click-tracking redirector; the automated crawl found this redirector returning HTTP 403 on every probed link, and several accessibility/compliance signals (missing alt text, no `List-Unsubscribe` header) are also failing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links return 403**, including primary CTAs ("Shop now" ×4, "LAST DAY TO REDEEM..."), navigation ("Women", "H&M" logo), both hero images, and the **Unsubscribe** link itself. All share the same redirector host and query pattern:
  `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hbc19991c-bba3-4f0c-af7b-e9c05a305ec8,664229af,<linkid>&did=1715612079&rid=3326930539&erid=...&p1=DM6005174&p2=0982dd0aa7b117bf086dee6ed4af7d0c&p3=20260902`
  Since every link — including unsubscribe — shares the identical 403 pattern, this is most likely a wholesale redirector/click-tracking failure (expired or malformed `id`/`erid` token, or a probe-side authentication/User-Agent block) rather than 12 independently broken destinations. Recommend confirming manually in a real client before treating as a full outage, since automated probes can be blocked by bot-detection on `t19.email.hm.com` even when the link resolves fine for real recipients.
- Two link text values are corrupted with zero-width/invisible characters: `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` (contains U+200C ZERO WIDTH NON-JOINER and U+FEFF BOM/ZWNBSP) — cosmetically invisible to users but signals a possible encoding issue in the template's text-node generation.
- 68 of 80 total links were not probed (footer/utility/social, deprioritized by design) — no data on those.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 images missing `alt` text**, including all product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants) and two click-tracking pixel/wrapper images. Screen readers and images-blocked clients (default in Outlook) will render this email with no accessible content for any product.
- Extensive `@media (max-width:699px)` rules and MSO/Outlook-specific hooks (`mso-hide`, `#MessageViewBody`, `.show-webkit-table`) are present, indicating standard multi-client responsive handling — no structural rendering issues detected in the visible markup.
- `<html>` lacks a `lang` attribute in the truncated source (not confirmed complete — verify in full source before flagging).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded JSON-LD (`schema.org/EmailMessage`, `DiscountOffer`, `PromotionCard`) has **empty string values** for `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, and `url` — these are unresolved merge fields that failed to populate. This will suppress Gmail annotations (e.g., promo/discount markup) that this schema is meant to enable.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, a stale value inconsistent with the campaign date (send date 2026-09-02) — indicates a template default that wasn't overridden by the campaign build.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than absence in the original send; cannot confirm true absence from this data alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm authentication compliance from available data.
- **Unsubscribe link itself returns 403** in the QA probe (same redirector pattern as above) — if reproducible outside the probe, this is a functional CAN-SPAM issue since the opt-out mechanism must work.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use H&M's proprietary tracking schema (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) rather than standard UTM parameters — no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` present. This is consistent with Adobe Campaign redirector conventions, not a defect, but means landing-page attribution can't be verified via UTM inspection.
- Because all links 403 at the redirector layer, **final landing-page destinations could not be verified** for content/URL alignment with email creative (e.g., whether "Women" routes to the women's category page, whether the H&M Home CTA lands on the correct promo page).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click-test 2–3 links (including Unsubscribe) from a real inbox to confirm whether the 403s are a genuine redirector outage or a probe/bot-detection false positive — the identical failure pattern across all 12 links, including unsubscribe, points to a shared redirector fault rather than per-link breakage.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 20 product/hero images for accessibility and images-off rendering.
3. Populate the JSON-LD `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`) or remove the unused schema block — empty required fields can cause Gmail to reject the markup entirely.
4. Fix the stale `availabilityStarts: "2018-12-31..."` default in the promotion schema.
5. Strip the stray zero-width/BOM characters from the two "Shop now" link text nodes.
6. Independently verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers via raw source or a header-preserving relay, since AgentMail did not capture them here.
