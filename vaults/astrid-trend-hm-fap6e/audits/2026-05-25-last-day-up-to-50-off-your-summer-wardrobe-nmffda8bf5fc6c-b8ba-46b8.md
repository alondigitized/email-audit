# Last day: Up to 50% off your summer wardrobe
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's closing send on the Memorial Day campaign — the "LAST DAY" hammer after six sends in five days. The strategy is sound: scarcity framing is the strongest conversion lever left in this series, and "UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" is front and center with no ambiguity. The category grid is clean (WOMEN / MEN / KIDS / HOME + BEAUTY), the render is tight, and the H&M Studio Resort member giveaway section is the one genuinely fresh hook in an otherwise familiar creative.
- The problem is fatigue and sprawl. Any subscriber who has opened the May 20 launch, the May 22 reminder, the May 23 swim pivot, the May 23 denim pivot, and the May 24 tops-and-shorts send has now seen the same offer in five flavors — and this email introduces no new reason to act. On a last-day close, you want punchy: hero, offer, two sections, get out. Instead this email runs five full content blocks plus a giveaway, which undercuts the "move now" urgency in the subject.
- Still, "LAST DAY" is the right call for the final send, and the execution is competent. Fence-sitters who respond to deadlines will engage; everyone else already unsubscribed mentally by May 23.

## What's working

- **"LAST DAY" urgency chip is immediate** — the label above the headline is the clearest conversion signal in this entire campaign; it changes the inbox calculus for anyone who bookmarked the sale.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE" is clean and unambiguous** — no mystery exclusion language dominating the fold, no "select styles only" asterisk visible at hero level.
- **Category segmentation self-selects well** — SEEKING SUN / ON DECK / BUILT FOR FUN / A LITTLE R&R let different household shoppers navigate without confusion.
- **Member giveaway adds novelty** — the H&M Studio Resort Bundle giveaway is the one fresh creative beat after six straight sale sends; it rewards loyalty subscribers and prompts non-members to enroll.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- **Preview text wastes the urgency** — the top banner "NEED AN ITEM SOONER? BUY ONLINE, PICK UP IN STORE" bleeds into the inbox preview slot, burning the one place to reinforce "last day ends tonight" with a subscriber scrolling past.
- **CTAs are category labels, not action verbs** — every button reads "WOMEN," "MEN," "KIDS 2-8Y" — no pull, no direction. "SHOP WOMEN" costs nothing and converts better.
- **No single hero product or price anchor** — a last-day close benefits from one specific item at a struck-through price; the sitewide lifestyle imagery is beautiful but vague. There is nothing for a fence-sitter to grab.
- **Email length contradicts urgency** — five full editorial blocks plus a giveaway plus a Shop By grid plus a footer app call-out is too much scroll for a "MOVE NOW" message.
- **Seventh send in five days** — the cadence fatigue is real and visible in the creative; there is no new angle here, no new product story, no new hook beyond "same offer, last call."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the BOPIS preheader; write urgency copy** — the top banner should still exist in the body, but a hidden preheader needs to carry the subject line's momentum into the inbox preview slot.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 50% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only — up to 50% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, shorts, swim — all still included. Don't wait.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at midnight. New arrivals still 50% off.`
- 2. **Add one hero product callout at a named price** — pull one high-performing SKU (a linen dress, a swim set) with a "was $49 / now $24" anchor. One concrete deal outperforms a sitewide banner for anyone who is still undecided.
- 3. **Trim to three sections** — WOMEN + MEN + one combined HOME/KIDS tile. Cut the scroll aggressively; the urgency and the member giveaway should be within two taps of the fold.
- 4. **Add verbs to all CTA buttons** — "SHOP WOMEN," "SHOP MEN," "SHOP KIDS." Zero cost, directional lift.
- 5. **Promote the member giveaway above the category grid** — it is the freshest content in this send and it belongs directly below the hero offer, not buried after four editorial blocks.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`@import` font loading — high severity**

```css
@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/0/northstar/css/fonts.css);
@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css);
```

CSS `@import` inside `<style>` blocks is not processed by Outlook (2007–2021+), Samsung Mail, or Lotus Notes. Web fonts silently fall back to system fonts in these clients, which can break typographic layout if the design depends on specific metrics. The correct pattern for email is `<link rel="stylesheet">` in `<head>` or inlined `@font-face` declarations, though even those are ignored by Outlook.

**Missing `lang` attribute — medium severity**

```html
<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">
```

No `lang` attribute is present on the root element. Screen readers cannot infer document language; this is a WCAG 2.1 §3.1.1 (Language of Page) failure. Should be `<html lang="en">` (or locale-appropriate value).

**`format-detection` meta — no issue**
`<meta content="telephone=no" name="format-detection"/>` is correctly set.

**Apple Mail reformatting suppression — no issue**
`<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting"/>` is present.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**JSON-LD annotation block — high severity**

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains multiple unfilled fields and one hardcoded stale date:

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `availabilityStarts` | `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` | Hardcoded 2018 date — never templated |
| `availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty — merge token not resolved |
| `discountCode` | `""` | Empty — merge token not resolved |
| `description` (DiscountOffer) | `""` | Empty |
| `subjectLine` (EmailMessage) | `""` | Empty — subject line not injected |
| `image` (PromotionCard) | `""` | Empty |
| `url` (PromotionCard) | `""` | Empty |

Gmail uses the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema for Promotions Tab rendering (deal badge, image thumbnail, expiry countdown). With `availabilityStarts` set to 2018 and `availabilityEnds` empty, Gmail's promotions enrichment either ignores the annotation entirely or renders an expired deal indicator — both outcomes suppress visibility in the Promotions tab.

The 2018 date indicates this annotation block was copied from an old template and the dynamic fields were never wired to the ESP merge system.
