- Strong, on-theme hero imagery — tennis court, net, racket, ball all reinforce "Tennis Edit" instantly.
- Clear influencer hook in the subject and hero copy ("Game. Set. Match... with stylist and content creator Sierra Rena's H&M MOVE picks") gives the send a distinct voice vs. generic product blasts.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere — no %, no $ off, no free shipping, no BOGO. Third editorial/full-price send this month with the same gap (8/17, 8/13).
- "Ace your tennis look" only makes sense to readers who already know or care about @sierrarenas — no context clue in-subject for who that is or why to trust the pick.
- Second product block (bra + tank) has zero connective copy — it just repeats the grid format with no header explaining it's part of the same edit.
- Niche category (tennis-specific apparel) for a mass email blast; most subscribers aren't playing tennis, capping relevance versus a broader "Move/athleisure" framing.

- 3. Add a one-line subhead above the second product block (e.g., "Off-court essentials") to separate it visually from the tennis-specific pieces above.
