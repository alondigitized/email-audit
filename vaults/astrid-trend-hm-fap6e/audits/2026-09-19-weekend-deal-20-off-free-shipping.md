# Weekend deal: 20% off + free shipping
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, denim-focused offer send — "20% off $125 + free shipping" — with a real minimum-spend threshold and a legible discount, plus a fall-denim editorial hook ("wide-leg to barrel jeans"). The hero model, category buttons (Women/Men/Kids), and three product tiles (Now Trending, Tried & True, Denim Delights) are all rendering cleanly with no broken images or overlapping text, which is a real improvement over the 09/18 "Fall Edit" send that had every hero and product image broken.
- The offer itself is honest and specific, but the layout undercuts it: there's no single obvious "shop this deal" button under the headline — just three plain category buttons with no visual weight, and the fine print immediately below softens the deal considerably (excludes MOVE, furniture, guest designer collabs, no gift card stacking, no cash equivalent). It reads as a solid, workmanlike promo rather than a standout send.
- Judgement: functional and on-brand, but the CTA hierarchy is weak and the discount threshold ($125 minimum) isn't disclosed until the tiny asterisk footer — that's a trust ding worth fixing before next send.

## What's working

- Hero renders fully and cleanly — no broken images, unlike the 09/18 send.
- Discount is concrete and prominent: "20% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" in large type immediately below the hero.
- Clear seasonal/category framing — denim, fall-ready fits, three sub-collections (Now Trending, Tried & True, Denim Delights) give shoppers multiple entry points.
- "SHOP BY" grid at the bottom (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) gives a clean fallback navigation path.

## What's weak

- No single dominant CTA button — Women/Men/Kids buttons look like plain outlined boxes with equal visual weight, so the eye doesn't land on one clear next action.
- The $125 minimum purchase requirement isn't visible in the headline or subhead — it only appears in the dense legal copy at the bottom, which risks feeling like a bait-and-switch when a shopper expects "20% off" to apply broadly.
- Large product images (Tried & True, Denim Delights) have plenty of dead white space around them relative to text, making the page feel long for how little content is packed in.
- "NOW TRENDING," "TRIED & TRUE," "DENIM DELIGHTS" section labels don't tell you what's actually different between them (all appear to be jeans) — redundant categorization.

## Recommendations

- 1. State the $125 minimum next to the headline (e.g., "20% off orders $125+") instead of hiding it in the asterisk footer — this is the single biggest trust/clarity fix.
- 2. Add a bold, single primary CTA button (e.g., "Shop the Denim Sale") directly under the discount headline, above the three category buttons.
- 3. Differentiate the three product sections with a concrete distinguishing detail (e.g., "wide-leg," "barrel," "kids") rather than generic labels like "Tried & True."
- 4. Tighten vertical spacing between product blocks to reduce scroll length on mobile.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Templated H&M promo email with unpopulated JSON-LD metadata and a QA scan showing all 12 probed material links returning HTTP 403.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed redirect links through `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` return **403 Broken link** — includes the primary "Shop now" CTAs (3x), header "H&M" logo link, both hero images, Women/Men/Kids nav links, and the Unsubscribe link. 12/12 probed links fail.
- Links carry ESP click-tracking params (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) consistent with a redirector; the 403 responses indicate the tracking domain/token is rejecting the request (expired preview token, bot/UA block, or an invalid `id` for this rendering), not necessarily a dead destination — cannot confirm final landing URLs since the redirector itself blocks resolution.
- Two CTA links have identical anchor text ("Shop now") but different `id` hash suffixes (`f83744ff`, `f8374500`, `f8374505`) — link text alone doesn't disambiguate which region of the email each maps to.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text: the two tracking pixel/redirect images and three content images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs for at least 3 distinct product images, plus one pixel).
- Link text on two CTAs is polluted with invisible characters: `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` contains a zero-width non-joiner (U+200C) and BOM/zero-width no-break space (U+FEFF) before the visible text — will read oddly to screen readers announcing hidden characters, and can break exact-text QA/link matching.
- One image-only link has no accessible name at all: link text renders as `"[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]"` / `"[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]"`, i.e., filename fallback rather than descriptive alt — a screen reader user gets no context on the product being linked.
- Extensive `!important`-laden responsive CSS (`optimize-css` block) targets `#MessageViewBody` (Outlook.com) and `@media screen yahoo` hacks — standard client-targeting patterns, no structural issues observed in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `application/ld+json` `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` schema has multiple empty string fields: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"description":""`, `"url":""` (image also empty). These are unresolved merge/template tokens — the structured data will validate as syntactically valid JSON-LD but semantically empty, so Gmail/Google promotional markup features (e.g., discount code display) will not render.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` — a stale/placeholder date unrelated to the 2026-09-19 send, confirming this JSON-LD block is not being populated per-send.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present in-body but returns **403** per QA scan — functionally broken one-click unsubscribe from the rendered email.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found (WARN) — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than absence at origin; cannot confirm from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click): not found — one-click unsubscribe via mail client cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — sender authentication status unknown via this relay; not evidence of an actual auth failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use H&M's proprietary redirector/click-tracking scheme (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5` params) rather than standard UTM parameters — no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` present on any probed URL, so campaign attribution depends entirely on the `p1=DM6061096` style internal IDs.
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment (e.g., does "Shop now" land on a 20%-off collection page) because every probed link 403s before reaching a resolvable destination.

7. Recommendations
- Priority 1: Investigate the 403s on `t19.email.hm.com/r/` — check whether QA prober's user agent/IP is being blocked by the redirector, or whether `id`/`did`/`rid` tokens are expired/invalid for this snapshot; if reproducible for real recipients, this is a full email-outage bug (no link in the email works, including unsubscribe).
- Priority 2: Populate the JSON-LD `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer` fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityStarts`/`Ends`, `description`, `url`, `image`) per-send instead of shipping template defaults.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 5 flagged images, particularly the 3 product images currently falling back to filename text.
- Strip the zero-width characters (U+200C, U+FEFF) from the two "Shop now" anchor texts.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at origin (may just be lost at the AgentMail relay) and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass via a raw-header capture outside the relay.
