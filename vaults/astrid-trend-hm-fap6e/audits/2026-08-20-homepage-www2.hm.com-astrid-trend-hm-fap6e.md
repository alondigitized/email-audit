# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-08-20
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- I open www2.hm.com and the first thing I actually get to interact with is a cookie consent wall, not the brand. Behind it I can see a moody editorial shot of a model in a floral slip dress, so H&M is clearly still leaning into that effortless, of-the-moment fashion image — but right now the homepage is functionally a legal disclaimer with a store attached.

## What's working

- **"TODAY ONLY: 20% OFF IN THE H&M APP"** top banner — a real, time-bound offer with urgency, and it nudges me toward the app which is exactly where a rewards member like me would go.
- **The floral slip-dress hero image** — it's genuinely a look I'd screenshot for my wishlist, moody and current, not a stiff catalog shot.
- **WOMEN already selected in nav** — one less tap for me to get where I shop most.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** — it's eating the entire left two-thirds of the screen and blocking literally everything else on the homepage. I can't see any product grid, promo section, or loyalty callout underneath it.
- **No visible loyalty/member CTA** — I'm a member-rewards shopper and there's nothing here reinforcing my membership or points balance.
- **Cut-off content at the bottom** — "DAR..." with an arrow is sliced off by the viewport/modal, so I can't tell what that module even is.
- **No visible primary "Shop Now" CTA button** — the hero has no clickable call-to-action of its own, just the image.

## Recommendations

- Shrink the cookie banner to a slim bottom strip instead of a full-screen modal so the homepage is actually visible on first load.
- Add a "Shop the Dress" or "Shop Now" button directly on the hero image.
- Surface member points or a "Member Rewards" badge near the top banner since that's a stated purchase driver for shoppers like me.
- Make sure whatever's below the fold (that cut-off "DAR..." line) isn't critical content getting orphaned by the modal.
