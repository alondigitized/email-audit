# Campus favorites from $6.99
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M running the same "campus edit / back-to-school" beat it already ran on 7/30 ("Up to 30% off your back-to-school checklist") — same 30%-off ceiling, same category-reset structure, just re-skinned with a new subject line. The subject promises "Campus favorites from $6.99," which is technically true — the Long-Sleeved Jersey Top is $6.99 — but that item is buried at the very bottom of the product grid, well after $27.99 jeans and $41.99 jackets lead the email. That's a soft bait-and-switch: the cheapest, most clickable price point is used to get the open, then never surfaces again until you've scrolled past everything else.
- The bigger structural miss is "THE FRENCH BEDROOM" section — bedding, towels, and loungewear dropped into an email whose subject and hero are entirely about campus/apparel. This is the exact same tonal whiplash flagged in the 7/27 "Off-duty dressing" send (bedding mixed into a fashion email). Two sends in five days with this same identity-crisis problem suggests it's a template default, not a one-off.
- Visually the send is clean — no render bugs, consistent editorial photography, clear pricing with strikethrough comparisons. But between the repeated back-to-school offer (two days running) and the bedding non-sequitur, this reads like a template stuffed with whatever category needed a push this week, not a purpose-built "campus" email. Solid execution, muddled targeting.

## What's working

- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks.
- Pricing is transparent with visible strikethrough comparisons on every product tile (e.g., Harper Jeans $27.99, Draped Viscose Top $12.99).
- Two clear, high-contrast CTA buttons ("SHOP NOW" / "FIND A STORE") repeated at both the top and mid-email breakpoints.
- Product grid uses consistent, well-lit flat-lay-style photography that reads as cohesive.
- Footer includes a full legal/offer-terms block, so the 30%-off claim isn't dangling without conditions.

## What's weak

- Subject line's "$6.99" hook is not the hero offer — that item (Long-Sleeved Jersey Top) is the last product shown, disconnected from the "UP TO 30% OFF" headline.
- "THE FRENCH BEDROOM" section is a hard left turn into home goods inside a campus-apparel email — no visual or copy bridge explains the jump.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible copy — "up to 30% off" has no end date shown in the body.
- This is the second back-to-school/30%-off send in two days (following 7/30's "Up to 30% off your back-to-school checklist") — subscribers who opened both get near-identical creative.
- No loyalty/member pricing or perk is surfaced despite H&M having a membership program.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the subject's price claim visible in the hero.** If $6.99 is the hook, either feature that item first in the grid or add a small "styles from $6.99" line under the "UP TO 30% OFF" headline — don't make people scroll to find the thing that got them to open.
- 2. **Cut or relocate "THE FRENCH BEDROOM."** It has nothing to do with campus fashion and undercuts the single-category focus that made 7/30's send work well. Split it into its own home-goods send.
- 3. **Add real urgency.** Nothing in the body says when "up to 30% off" ends — a "Ends Sunday" or "Tax-Free Days" style callout (like 7/30 used) would lift urgency without new creative.
- 4. **Vary the offer structure from 7/30.** Sending the same 30%-off back-to-school promise two days running trains subscribers to skip every third email — rotate in a different lever (bundle deal, first-item-free, loyalty-exclusive tier).
- 5. **Sharpen CTA copy.** "SHOP NOW" is generic twice over in one email — try "SHOP CAMPUS EDIT" up top and "SHOP JEANS" or similar under the product grid to tie the click to the specific product just seen.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Layer up for lecture halls: jackets, denim, and tops under $30`
