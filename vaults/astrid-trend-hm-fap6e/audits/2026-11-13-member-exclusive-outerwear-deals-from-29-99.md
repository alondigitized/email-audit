# Member-exclusive outerwear deals from $29.99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led Member send. The hero is a black puffer, and the offer sits right under it: "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS." Below that, price-anchored tiles show the Member price in red next to the regular price. The subject sells a specific number ($29.99), which is the right call.
- It's also the fourth or fifth Member/Black Friday beat in a week. The 11/11 send ("Up to 40% off everything!") and the 11/12 send ("New winter staples, elevated style") carried the same 40% banner. Nothing here is new except the outerwear framing and the $29.99 entry price. The "from $29.99" lead is also thin. It's a $47.99 jacket marked down to $29.99, and the only item at that price is the first tile.
- Verdict: solid execution, tired campaign. It will perform for people already in the Member funnel. It won't stand out in an inbox that has seen this offer all week.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF FOR MEMBERS" headline is large and unmistakable, and it sits right under the hero.
- Strikethrough-style pricing shows regular price and red Member price together, so the saving is visible per product.
- The outerwear focus is tight: bubble-hem jacket, scuba jacket, car coat, blouson, teddy fleece. It matches the season and the subject.
- The "Not a Member? Join now and get 10% off your first purchase" line gives non-members a path.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Three buttons stack in the hero (Shop Now, Sign In, Sign Up). Sign In and Sign Up compete with the main CTA.
- No product tile has its own CTA, and the product text is small.
- The offer feels diluted. The headline says "up to 40%," but the visible discounts run roughly 37% down to 29%. The $199 coat has no visible markdown depth.
- The deadline (valid through 11/16) is buried in the fine print, so the email has no visible urgency.
- The legal block is dense, with exclusions and a $5 Bonus Reward note. It also repeats the asterisk caveat on the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11/16." It's the cheapest lift available, and the 11/16 date already exists in the fine print.
- 2. Cut the hero to one primary button ("Shop Member outerwear") and move Sign In/Sign Up to a text link underneath.
- 3. Show the true percentage saved on each tile, for example "-37%". That makes the "up to 40%" claim concrete.
- 4. Differentiate from the 11/11 and 11/12 sends. Lead with the lowest-price hero item rather than repeating the same banner.
- 5. Add a category-specific CTA under the product grid ("Shop all Member outerwear").

# Technical Audit: H&M "Member-exclusive outerwear deals from $29.99"

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector, and the 16% QA pass rate comes from that alone. Many images lack alt text, and the JSON-LD block ships with empty fields. The 403s are most likely bot-blocking on the tracker, so I can't confirm they are real user-facing breakages.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All probed links returned 403:** the "Shop now" (×4), "H&M" logo, two image links, "SIGN IN" (×2), "SIGN UP" (×2) and "Unsubscribe" links all failed. Every one is a wrapped redirect of the form `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h01b7a92c-…,55137f9e,<linkid>&did=1427341214&rid=3326930539&erid=…`.
- **Likely cause:** a uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to the ESP tracker rejecting non-browser or datacenter clients. It does not point to per-link breakage. Treat this as unverified and re-test with a browser-like UA or a real client.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 66 links were probed (54 skipped), so the rest are unverified.
- **Empty link text:** the QA output lists blank link text for the first six failures, i.e. image-only anchors. The first two "Shop now" entries show no anchor text, and the next two have zero-width characters (`‌ ﻿`) padding "Shop now".

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 14 images are flagged. Each product image appears twice, as `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`. Examples are `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-46/us173/1347526002_202604_LB_3040.jpg/…` and `…/wk-47/1358732002_202604_LB_2102.jpg/…`. The tracking-pixel-style image at `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` also has no alt.
- **Duplicate image pairs:** the 1400px and 750px pairs are probably a desktop/mobile swap using `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop`. Without alt text, screen readers may announce both, or announce the filename.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.is-hidden-desktop` in `#optimize-css` sets `line-height:auto!important`. `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value, so the declaration is dropped.
- **Webfont imports:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)` are ignored by Gmail and some other clients, so they fall back to system fonts. Confirm that the font-family stacks include web-safe fallbacks.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so the rest of the markup (table structure, `lang`, `role="presentation"`) is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The tracker URLs carry per-recipient values (`rid`, `erid`, `p2`, `p5`), and these resolved to real values.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Destinations not resolved:** every CTA is wrapped in the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` redirector, and the 403s blocked following it. I can't verify the final landing URLs, UTM parameters, or whether they match the "$29.99 outerwear" offer.
- **Tracking params:** the only visible parameters are the ESP's own (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1=DM6212364`, `p3=20261113`). Whether `utm_*` is applied at the redirect destination is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the redirector links with a real-browser UA or from a residential IP to separate bot-blocking from real breakage. Raise the probe cap above 12 links.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product images. Use `alt=""` on decorative or duplicate desktop/mobile variants, and add alt text to the logo and tracker-wrapped images.
3. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. Fill `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `url` and `image`, and set the availability dates to the real campaign window.
4. Remove `line-height:auto!important` or replace it with `normal`.
5. Remove the zero-width characters padding the "Shop now" link text.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results from raw headers outside the relay.
