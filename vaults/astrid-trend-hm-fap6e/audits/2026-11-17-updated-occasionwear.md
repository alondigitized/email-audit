# Updated occasionwear
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial occasionwear send. A woman in a red satin blouse fills the hero, and "DRESS FOR THE OCCASION" follows with a single outlined SHOP NOW button. The red look carries through the product grid, and the layout is easy to scan. It looks like a well-made H&M email.
- The problem is that the offer and the creative don't connect. The Black Friday banner ("UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES*") is a thin strip at the very top, then the body goes to full-price "New Arrival" tiles at $29.99–$49.99. A reader can't tell whether the occasionwear is discounted. The subject, "Updated occasionwear", gives no offer, no urgency and no reason to open it.
- This is also another send in a long Member-promo run. After "Last day: Up to 40% off for Members" on 11/16 and the knitwear send on 11/15, the message has shifted to "up to 50% off" with different framing. An email with a vague subject and an unclear discount will feel like noise. Verdict: attractive but low-conviction, a mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The red hero is striking, on-season for holiday parties, and the eye lands on it first.
- The black-and-red top banner states the Black Friday offer clearly.
- Product tiles show names, prices and "New Arrival" tags, so there is useful product detail without clicking.
- The "H&M STUDIO HOLIDAY" teaser (launches Nov 19) and the shearling coat image add a second reason to engage.
- Nothing is broken: no overlapping text or missing images.

## What's weak

- The discount is disconnected from the content. The tiles show single prices with no strike-through or "now" label, so the 50% banner doesn't appear to apply to them.
- The CTAs are generic: "SHOP NOW" appears twice and "PREVIEW NOW" once. There is no verb-plus-noun like "Shop party dresses".
- The email is very long. It has six large image tiles stacked vertically, then the secondary "Shop by" grid, then a dense wall of fine print. That is a lot of scrolling and the tiles are repetitive (the red cowl-neck dress appears twice).
- The fine print under the banner is long, and the asterisk hides the real terms.
- The "Updated occasionwear" subject sells nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Connect the offer to the product. Add strike-through pricing and "Up to 50% off" badges on the tiles, and put the offer in the hero headline, not just a thin banner.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to carry a concrete offer or occasion, and write a preheader that adds a new fact instead of leaving the inbox preview to the banner text.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop party dresses" and "Shop holiday looks".
- 4. Cut the stack to 3–4 tiles, remove the duplicate red dress, and move the Studio Holiday teaser up or into a separate send.
- 5. Add a clear deadline in the banner, such as "Ends 11/21", so the urgency is credible.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday deals on satin, tailoring and statement bows. Ends 11/21.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: H&M "Updated occasionwear"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links all go through `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirects. The automated probe got 403s on all 12 probed links, which is most likely bot-blocking rather than real breakage (see section 2). The source has about 20 images with no alt text, an empty JSON-LD schema block, and a few invalid CSS declarations.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including the Unsubscribe link (`...f8cc96c6...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`). Every URL shares the same `id=h20c1873f-...,5514efef,<suffix>` and per-recipient `rid`, `erid` and `p2` values. A uniform 403 across every redirect on one host points to the ESP's bot or WAF filtering the probe client. It is not evidence of 12 separate broken links. **The destinations could not be verified**, so treat "broken" as unconfirmed.
- **Duplicate link text:** "Shop now" is the text on at least four different tracked URLs (`f8cc9682`, `f8cc9683`, `f8cc9676`, `f8cc9678`). Link text with no context is ambiguous for screen-reader link lists.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` contains invisible characters (a zero-width non-joiner and a BOM), which can interfere with text-based link handling.
- **Opaque redirects:** The final landing URLs can't be seen from the source or the probe, so the destinations can't be audited.
- **Plain-text part:** 512 of 590 characters are URLs (>70%), so the text alternative is almost entirely tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 20 images lack alt text.** The affected sources are `image.hm.com/.../women/week-46/WA10265P11-*` and `.../na90265b01/NA90265B01-2x3.jpg/_jcr...`.
  - Each hero or product image appears as a 1400px and a 750px pair (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`). The pairs are presumably desktop/mobile variants.
  - The probe fell back to filenames (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`, `[image: bY=]`, `[image: xRnrnWfXcd78=]`) as link text.
  - Several of these images sit inside links, so the link has no accessible name. With images blocked, the CTA is lost.
  - A tracking-pixel-style image at `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1` also lacks alt text.
- **Invalid CSS in the `optimize-css` block:**
  - `.is-hidden-desktop{...line-height:auto!important...}`: `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value, so the declaration is dropped.
  - `@media screen yahoo {...}` is malformed (it needs `and` or a comma), so the rule is ignored.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css)` are unsupported in Gmail and most Outlook clients, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is expected behavior, and I couldn't confirm the fallback stack because the source was truncated.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML is cut off, so the body (tables, `role="presentation"`, `lang` attribute, `<title>`) couldn't be fully reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) were found in the visible source or QA output.
- **JSON-LD block is entirely empty:** `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""` and `"url":""`. The `availabilityStarts` value is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, a stale template default. This looks like an unpopulated schema template. Gmail will ignore it or may mishandle it, and the promotion card never renders.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool notes that AgentMail may strip these headers, so **this is unconfirmed**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay, so no conclusion can be drawn.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** It is present but returned 403 to the probe, so it is unverified. It uses a per-recipient token (`p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`).
- The physical address and footer legal text are outside the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. The tracking URLs use proprietary parameters (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1=DM6224273`, `p2`, `p3=20261117`). `p3` looks like the send date and `p1` looks like a campaign ID. Whether UTMs are appended at the redirect hop can't be determined because the redirects returned 403.
- Landing-page alignment with the email content (occasionwear versus the "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" link text) cannot be verified for the same reason.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the links** with a mail-client user agent or an allowlisted IP, or resolve them through the ESP's link report. This would confirm the 403s are bot filtering and let you check the destinations and UTMs.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to all linked images, or `alt=""` on purely decorative ones. Make sure every linked image or button has an accessible name.
3. **Make CTA text unique**, such as "Shop dresses" or "Shop suits". Remove the zero-width characters from link text.
4. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD block.** Fill in `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `url` and `image`, and fix the stale `availabilityStarts`.
5. **Fix the invalid CSS:** replace `line-height:auto` with `normal` or a fixed value, and correct `@media screen yahoo`.
6. **Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the raw message from a direct inbox or seed test, not through the relay.
7. **Add a real plain-text version** with readable copy, not just a list of links.
