# Homepage snapshot · www2.hm.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the first thing I see when I open H&M is not a cute new-season drop or a "welcome back" loyalty moment — it's a full-screen cookie consent wall. The actual homepage is completely blocked. Whatever campaign they're running, whatever hero image they prepped, none of it is visible. This is my entire first impression of the brand right now.

## What's working

- **"ACCEPT ALL" button** gets decent contrast as a filled black button — at least the primary consent action is visually prominent compared to the outlined secondary options.
- **Button hierarchy** is passable — filled > outlined > outlined does suggest a preference ranking, even if I'd never call it "good UX."

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is invisible.** No hero, no offer, no product — the consent modal is full-bleed and full-height with zero glimpse of what's behind it.
- **The modal copy is a wall of legal text** — dense, small, zero brand voice. It reads like a GDPR filing, not H&M talking to a 24-year-old.
- **Zero brand presence in the modal** — no H&M logo, no brand color, no personality. I could be on any site. This does nothing to make me want to accept and keep shopping.
- **Three nearly identical buttons** slow down the decision — the text-heavy explanation and three options make me pause and re-read instead of just tapping through.
- **No peek of the homepage behind the modal** — even a blurred or dimmed hero would give me a reason to tap through. Right now there's no reward signal.

## Recommendations

- **Show a blurred/dimmed hero behind the modal** so I have a taste of what I'm consenting to see — even a glimpse of a product or campaign image would increase tap-through on "Accept All."
- **Inject brand voice into the modal copy** — shorten the legal wall to two sentences max and lead with something like "We use cookies to make your H&M experience actually good." Keep the legal link for anyone who wants it.
- **Add the H&M logo to the modal header** — I should know whose site I'm on the second this appears, not have to guess.
- **Pre-select "Accept All" as the obvious default action** with a single prominent button and collapse the cookie settings into a text link — reduce the three-button friction to one tap.
