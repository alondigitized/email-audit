# Don't miss out: Up to 60% off e-v-e-r-y-t-h-i-n-g

## Executive summary

- This is H&M's Black Friday weekend send, and it's quiet and well composed. A model in a taupe knit sweater sits above "THE BLACK FRIDAY EVENT / UP TO 60% OFF EVERYTHING". The copy is cozy ("soft knits, effortless layers") and two buttons sit under it. The visual hierarchy is clean and the offer is clear.
- The subject, "Don't miss out: Up to 60% off e-v-e-r-y-t-h-i-n-g," is where it falls short. The letter-spacing gimmick looks like a spam-filter dodge. The body's "UP TO 60% OFF EVERYTHING" is the same offer as the 11-26 send ("Up to 60% off EVERYTHING starts online now!"). The body also never delivers any urgency, since no deadline or countdown appears in the render. The only date is buried in the legal text, which says the offer expires 12/01/2026.
- Overall this is a competent but repetitive send, roughly the fifth near-identical 60%-off message this week. The creative is softer than the 11-27 "Puffer deals" send and has less to say. It lands in the middle.

## What's working

- The hero is clean: a single model, a neutral palette, and "UP TO 60% OFF EVERYTHING" in bold type directly below it.
- The header banner "FREE SHIPPING OVER $50 FOR H&M MEMBERS" gives members a concrete perk.
- The women/men/kids trio (Versatile, Dependable, Comfortable) segments shoppers with one tap each.
- The "COZY SEASON" block is seasonally on-point, with plaid and sleepwear imagery.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space and no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's "e-v-e-r-y-t-h-i-n-g" reads as a spam trick, and "Don't miss out" is generic.
- No deadline is visible in the body, so "Don't miss out" has nothing behind it. The 12/01 expiry is only in the fine print.

- The "Versatile / Dependable / Comfortable" tiles use vague labels, and there's no product, price or sale price anywhere.
- The legal block is long and dense, and the asterisked offer ("up to," exclusions) is easy to miss.

- 2. Drop the hyphenated "e-v-e-r-y-t-h-i-n-g" and use plain text.
- 3. Replace "FIND A STORE" with a second online CTA, such as "Shop knits".
- 4. Add two or three priced products with sale prices so the discount feels real.
- 5. Cut the repeated category tiles or merge them into the "Shop by" row.

## 1. Technical Summary

  - Affected link text: "Shop now" (×6), "Find a store" (×2), "SHOP WOMEN", "SHOP MEN", "SHOP KIDS" and "Unsubscribe".
  - A uniform 403 across every redirect, including unsubscribe, points to bot/WAF filtering of the QA probe, not 12 dead links. Real-client behavior is unconfirmed.
  - It still matters that the redirector rejects non-browser clients. Link scanners and privacy proxies may get the same 403.
- **The final destinations were not resolved.** The 403 stopped the redirect chain, so landing-page validity can't be verified.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 67 links were probed, and 55 footer, utility and social links were skipped.
- **The links are tracked with per-recipient identifiers.** The URLs carry `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` (base64 of the same value), `p2=0982dd0a…` and `p4`/`p5` on the unsubscribe link. These are expected for ESP tracking, but the unsubscribe link depends on a personalized, tokenized URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 14 images.**
  - The affected images are the `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` pairs. These appear to be desktop and mobile variants of the same asset. The sources are `…/wk48/us187_s4/1357642003_202604_LB_1130.jpg`, `1363170001_…_0344.jpg`, `1365195001_…_1365.jpg`, `1343388021_…_0004.jpg` and `…/week-43/HQ10264P03-FN-2x3.jpg`.

- **The JSON-LD block contains empty fields.** Gmail annotations are present, but `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `"url":""` and `"image":""` are all empty. `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`, which looks like a stale template default. Gmail may ignore or reject this markup.
- **The CSS has an invalid value.** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` uses `auto`, which isn't valid for `line-height`, so the declaration is dropped.
- **Web fonts are loaded with `@import` in `<style>`.** These are `fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css`. Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore them, so the fallback font stack matters. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the stack is defined.
- **The `.prevent-auto-links` and `#MessageViewBody` hooks are intentional.** They target Apple Mail and Webkit clients and show no defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The source is truncated, so coverage is incomplete.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so absence is unconfirmed.
- **The `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header was not found.** Without it, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **The `Authentication-Results` header is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain `email.hm.com` is not verified.
- **There is a visible in-body unsubscribe link,** but it returned 403 to the probe (`…f8e6e917…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`). Real-browser functionality is unverified.
- **The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source,** so it is not confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Links use the ESP redirector with `p1=DM6246818`, `p2` (hash) and `p3=20261128`, not `utm_*`. Campaign attribution depends on the redirector appending parameters downstream, which I couldn't observe.
- **Landing-page alignment couldn't be verified.** The 403s blocked resolution of the destinations for "Shop now", "SHOP WOMEN/MEN/KIDS" and "Find a store", so I can't confirm they match the "Up to 60% off" offer.
- **Several CTAs share the label "Shop now"** (6 distinct tracked IDs, f8e6e8e0 to f8e6e8f4). This is fine for tracking but ambiguous for screen readers, since the destinations can't be told apart.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirect links** with a browser-like User-Agent, or from a trusted IP, to separate probe blocking from real failures. Do this first for the "Unsubscribe" link, then widen the 12/67 coverage.
2. **Add alt text to all 14 images,** including the two tracked-link images. Use `alt=""` only for purely decorative images.
3. **Get the raw headers for the next run** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message. If they're present upstream, fix the relay capture. If they're absent, add RFC 8058 one-click support.
4. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD** (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `url`, `image`, `availabilityStarts`/`Ends`). Empty fields and the 2018 date are template leftovers.
5. **Fix `line-height:auto`** to a valid value, such as `normal` or `initial`.
6. **Confirm the postal address and `utm_*` handling** in the full, untruncated HTML and in the redirector's final URLs.
7. **Give repeated "Shop now" CTAs distinguishing accessible labels,** for example with `aria-label` or visually hidden text.
