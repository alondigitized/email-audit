# Up to 60% off EVERYTHING starts online now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Thanksgiving-day H&M send that takes a calm editorial approach. "THE BLACK FRIDAY EVENT / UP TO 60% OFF SITEWIDE" sits in bold type at the top, and a single SHOP NOW button follows. The discount is the strongest in the recent run. It beats the "Up to 50%" in the 11/23–11/25 sends, so it deserves to be the headline.
- The hero image works against it. It shows a stack of bread loaves tied with a red ribbon. It has no clothing and no H&M product, and it takes up about a third of the email. The caption, "Happy Thanksgiving! Here's to a day of togetherness and moments worth celebrating in style.*", is warm but vague. The asterisk leads to a long block of fine print. There is a large blank gap under the button, then a plain "Shop by" grid with no products and no prices.
- Verdict: a strong offer wrapped in weak creative. It is a holiday greeting, not a conversion email. After a week of product-led sends (denim, knitwear, activewear), this one gives the reader nothing to shop. It's a decent brand moment but a low-effort promo.

## What's working

- "UP TO 60% OFF SITEWIDE" is large, bold and impossible to miss.
- The offer is the best of the week. It steps up from the 50% in the 11/25 sends.
- The Thanksgiving tone is timely and fits the day.
- The layout is clean and simple. A single SHOP NOW button follows the headline and hero.
- The Women, Men, Kids, Home and Beauty tiles give a quick way to enter by category.

## What's weak

- The bread-and-ribbon hero has no clothing in it, so it communicates nothing about fashion or the sale.
- The "Shop by" tiles carry no prices or discount callouts. There is no product to click on.
- A large empty gap sits below the CTA and again above "SHOP BY". It makes the email feel unfinished.
- The headline says "SITEWIDE", but the dense fine print excludes sale items, collaborations, gift cards and more. That undercuts "honest offer."
- The subject says "EVERYTHING starts online now," but the body says "online and in stores." There is no urgency, deadline or end date visible above the fold.
- The Home tile image looks cropped and awkward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the bread hero with a bold fashion image. Show the offer on a model or a product grid, and keep the Thanksgiving greeting as a small line of copy.
- 2. Add a proof row of 4–6 products with "from $X" prices right under the CTA. Remove the empty gap above "SHOP BY".
- 3. Add visible urgency, such as "Ends 12/1" or "Today only: online first," near the headline.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop 60% off", and place a second CTA lower down.
- 5. Add one short line of exclusions near the headline so the "sitewide" claim is credible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `60% off sitewide, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Happy Thanksgiving: up to 60% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our biggest Black Friday event yet. Shop early online through 12/1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Coats, knits, denim and gifts, all marked down. Members get a $5 bonus reward.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: H&M "Up to 60% off EVERYTHING starts online now!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly well-formed, with a viewport meta tag, a mobile media query and a `charset` declaration. Every probed link returned 403 and the destinations could not be resolved. The 403s are probably bot-blocking by the tracker rather than real breakage. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. The source has a few real issues: empty JSON-LD fields, missing image alt text and a URL-heavy plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** They all go through the `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hb8f36035-…` redirector. That includes the logo, hero images, "Shop now" (×4), Women, Men, Find store (×2) and Unsubscribe.
- **Likely cause:** the click tracker refuses non-browser clients. A uniform 403 on every link, including the logo, points to bot-blocking, not 12 separate broken links. Treat the "Broken link" status as **unconfirmed**. A real-browser or seeded-inbox click test is needed.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 47 links were probed. The other 35 were skipped as footer or utility links.
- **Zero-width characters in link text:** two CTAs have `‌  ﻿Shop now`, which contains U+200C and U+FEFF. This can break exact-text matching in analytics and link-text accessibility checks.
- **Tracking parameters:** each link carries `rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ==` and `p2=0982dd0a…`, which are per-recipient identifiers. The redirect IDs differ per link (`f8e229bc` through `f8e22a03`), so per-link attribution is working.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 images:**
  - Two `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` images.
  - `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, both from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk48/us187_s4/…`.
- **Filename fallback:** the QA text extraction shows the hero link text as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will get no usable label for the primary linked images. This is the main accessibility defect.
- **Duplicate hero images:** the 1400px and 750px variants share one source asset, which suggests a desktop/mobile swap. Each is toggled with `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`, so a screen reader may announce both.
- **Web fonts:** the `@import` of `fabric.hmgroup.com` and `s1-cdn.hm.com` fonts is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. This is not a defect as long as web-safe fallbacks are in the font stack. They could not be seen in the truncated source.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off at `.email-layout{width:375px!importan…`. Body markup, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, `lang` and `dir` could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source. The truncation limits this check.
- **JSON-LD block is empty:** `subjectLine:""`, `discountCode:""`, `availabilityEnds:""`, and `PromotionCard` `image:""` and `url:""`. `availabilityStarts` is a stale `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00`. This looks like a template whose fields were never populated. Gmail may ignore the markup, or the empty promotion annotation could produce a malformed or blank promo card.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA report notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. H&M is a bulk sender, so the headers should be checked on the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain `email.hm.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, and no alignment problem is evident.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** it is present, with link text "Unsubscribe" and a unique `p4`/`p5` token, `.../f8e22a03&...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`. The probe returned 403, so the endpoint could not be verified. The link was probably blocked by the same bot filter.
- The physical postal address in the footer could not be confirmed because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Cannot verify.** Every CTA is wrapped in the t19 redirector and every probe returned 403, so no final landing URL or UTM parameters were observed.
- The only visible campaign identifiers are `p1=DM6243466` and `p3=20261126`, the send date. The standard UTM set (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) is not visible on the wrapper URLs. It may be appended at the redirect hop.
- The campaign tag `wk48/us187_s4` in the image paths suggests a US week-48 asset set.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow redirects in a real browser. This will settle whether the 12 FAILs are real or the tracker is blocking bots, and will show the destinations and UTMs.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero images and the two tracker-wrapped images. Use `alt=""` only for the purely decorative ones.
3. Remove the zero-width characters (U+200C, U+FEFF) from inside the CTA anchor text. If they are meant as spacing or preheader padding, move them outside the `<a>`.
4. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block. At minimum fill in `subjectLine` and the promotion `url` and `image`, and drop the stale 2018 `availabilityStarts`.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the receiving MTA. The AgentMail relay may strip them.
6. Improve the plain-text part. 520 of 598 characters are URLs, and the tracker URLs make up nearly all of it. Add descriptive text around the links, or shorten the redirect URLs.
7. Get the full, untruncated HTML to check `role="presentation"` on layout tables, the postal address, `lang`, and fallback fonts.
