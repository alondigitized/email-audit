# Today only: 20% off in the app
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, honest, single-offer send — "20% off your app order," today only — but there's no primary CTA button anywhere near the hero. The two buttons that exist are "Download iOS" / "Download Android," meaning the actual click path requires leaving the email, opening a separate app, and hunting for a code, not clicking through to shop. That's a real friction point dressed up as a discount email.
- Compared to the run of recent H&M sends, this is the first "app-exclusive" mechanic in the brand history reviewed — a clear escalation tactic to drive app installs rather than a straightforward web sale, and it comes right after two genuine non-discount editorial sends (09/14 SculptMove, 09/15 outerwear edit). It reads as a one-off growth push, not a shopping email.
- The image (single model, trench coat, no price context) and shop-by-category grid are on-brand and competently executed with zero render bugs, but the whole email fails its own job: get someone to buy something. Judgement: honest, well-produced, but a poor click generator because the ask is "go elsewhere, then find a code," not "tap here to save 20%."

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy: "TODAY ONLY" then bold "20% OFF YOUR APP ORDER" lands first, unmissable.
- Single hero image, well-lit, no layout breakage or overlapping text.
- Shop-by-category grid (Women/Men/Kids/Home/Beauty) gives a fallback path for browsing beyond the offer.
- Fine print is disclosed inline, not buried — return policy, exclusions, and validity dates are all visible in the footer.

## What's weak

- No "Shop Now" CTA tied to the discount itself — the only buttons are app-download links, so a subscriber who already has the app (or doesn't want it) has no clear next click.
- The offer is entirely app-gated ("Find your code in the app to redeem"), which is a bait-and-switch feel: the email promises 20% off but can't be redeemed from the email.
- Top nav "SHOP NOW" is a plain text link with no visual weight — easy to miss as the actual shopping entry point.
- No product/price context under the offer — the trench coat shown isn't linked to a price, size run, or "shop this look."

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible "Shop Now" button directly under the offer headline, even if it just deep-links to the app/site — the two download buttons shouldn't be the only CTAs above the fold.
- 2. Make the redemption mechanic clearer up top ("Code auto-applies in app" or show the actual code) instead of forcing a hunt once inside the app — reduces the two-step friction.
- 3. Add a price or "shop this coat" link under the hero image so the visual has a transactional path, not just a mood shot.
- 4. Strengthen "SHOP NOW" in the top nav visually (button or underline treatment) since it's currently indistinguishable from other nav text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: your code for 20% off in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off, app-only — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab your code in the app before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day only — download or open the app to save 20%.`

## Technical Audit: H&M — "Today only: 20% off in the app"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email is served via Bluecore-style redirect tracking (t19.email.hm.com) but the QA probe returned 403 on every tracked link, and the automated check logged a 23% pass rate with 6 failures.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 6 probed material links returned **403 Forbidden**, including the two primary CTAs ("Shop now"), the header logo link ("H&M"), a nav link ("Women"), "Find store", and — critically — **Unsubscribe**:
  - `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h12c43671-...&erid=...&p3=20260916` (×2, "Shop now")
  - `...f82d9182...` ("H&M")
  - `...f82d91a9...` ("Women")
  - `...f82d91be...` ("Find store")
  - `...f82d91cb...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...` ("Unsubscribe")
- Common pattern: all failing URLs share `did=1418223304&rid=3326930539&erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D`. A 403 across the full redirector domain (not just one link) suggests the tracking/redirect service is blocking the probe's request (bot/UA filtering, geo-block, or expired signed params) rather than 6 independent broken destinations — worth confirming with a real-browser fetch before treating as a hard content bug.
- Link text uses invisible formatting characters (ZWNJ `‌` and BOM `﻿`) embedded before/inside anchor text ("‌  ﻿Shop now") — likely an anti-scraping/rendering artifact from the ESP template, not a functional link issue, but note it clutters link-text extraction and screen-reader announcement.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 4 images missing `alt` text, including two content images sharing the same product photo at different resolutions (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, src: `image.hm.com/.../1360152001_202604_LB_2167.jpg`) and two tracking-pixel-style images served from the `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` redirector.
- Heavy reliance on `!important`-laden responsive CSS (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.is-hidden-desktop`, etc.) gated behind a single `@media (max-width:699px)` block — standard ESP responsive pattern, no defects observed in the truncated source.
- No MSO/Outlook conditional comments visible in the truncated excerpt — cannot confirm Outlook desktop rendering fallback from available source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The embedded `application/ld+json` schema has multiple **empty string fields**: `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`, `url`, and `description` are all `""`. These are Gmail annotation/schema.org markup (for Gmail promo actions, e.g. "View Offer" chips), not customer-facing merge tags, but empty values mean any Gmail-side promotion card / annotation integration relying on this JSON-LD will not render correctly (no discount code surfaced, no card image, no destination URL).
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00` — a stale/leftover value from template creation, inconsistent with the "Today only" 2026-09-16 send date. No other merge-token placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) observed in the truncated HTML.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe link returns 403** per QA probe — if confirmed against a live send (not just the probe), this is a CAN-SPAM risk since the required functional opt-out mechanism would be broken.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not found**. `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click): **not found**. Flagged by QA as possibly a relay artifact ("may not be captured by AgentMail relay") rather than confirmed absence — needs verification against raw headers at the receiving MTA, not just the relay-visible copy.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome): **not found** — same relay caveat applies; authentication status is unverified, not confirmed-failing.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present on any linked URL — H&M uses its own redirect/query schema instead (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`, e.g. `p1=DM6048453`, `p3=20260916` matching send date). This is a closed tracking system, not a gap, provided `p1` (campaign ID) and `p3` (date) are correctly mapped downstream in analytics — cannot verify landing-page alignment since final destination URLs are masked behind the 403'ing redirector and never resolved.
- Because every material link 403'd, **destination/landing-page alignment cannot be verified** — no confirmed final URL to check against subject line ("Today only: 20% off in the app") or CTA copy.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-verify the 403s with a standard browser session/UA (not just the automated probe) to rule out bot-blocking before escalating as broken links — a domain-wide 403 pattern points to redirector-side filtering over 6 independently broken URLs.
2. If Unsubscribe is confirmed 403 in a real client, treat as urgent — CAN-SPAM functional opt-out failure.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` by capturing raw headers at final delivery (not via AgentMail relay) before concluding they're absent.
4. Populate or remove the empty JSON-LD fields (`discountCode`, `image`, `url`, `description`) if Gmail promotional annotations are intended to render.
5. Update the stale `availabilityStarts: 2018-12-31` value in the JSON-LD schema.
6. Add `alt` text to the two product-image variants and the two tracking-pixel images.
