# Cozy holiday decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, shoppable catalog send for H&M Home's holiday launch. The 3x3 collage hero is warm and on-season, with red cushions, ornaments, plates and a teddy bear. "THE HOLIDAY SHOP IS OPEN" is a clear headline. The product grid below it has real names, prices and "New Arrival" tags, so a reader can see exactly what's on offer.
- It has no promotion at all. The only offer is the "Free shipping over $50 for H&M members" banner, which is easy to miss. After the 10/24–10/28 run of 20–25% off and threshold-discount sends, a plain "new arrivals" email lands softly. There's no deadline and no reason to click today. The hero collage shows nine products but doesn't link to any one of them. The length, about 12 product tiles across a long scroll, also dilutes the message.
- Overall it's competent, on-brand and low-urgency. It's a good browse email, not a conversion driver.

## What's working

- The hero collage sets a clear seasonal mood immediately, with red and cream holiday styling.
- Product tiles are clean and consistent, with name, price and "New Arrival" label.
- Prices are low and visible ($6.99–$49.99), which makes the range feel accessible.
- The "Shop Now" button appears at both the top and the bottom of the product section, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The "Shop by" tiles for Women, Men, Kids, Home and Beauty give an easy exit to other categories.

## What's weak

- No discount is visible. The only incentive is the free-shipping line, which is small and text-only.
- The headline copy "There's no place like home for the holidays. Deck the halls with festive details." is generic and has no urgency.
- The product grid is long and repetitive, with many near-identical cushion covers and no clear hero product.
- The CTAs are thin outlined buttons that look weak next to the hero.
- Some product names are truncated with "…" ("EMBROIDERED-…", "RUFFLE-TRIMM…").
- The "Join now for exclusive benefits" line is a thin loyalty hook, and nothing member-specific is offered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real offer, such as "20% off Home for members", and put it in the hero headline. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Make the hero clickable by product, or cut it to one hero product with a price.
- 3. Trim the grid to 6 products and lead with the best sellers.
- 4. Use a filled, high-contrast CTA button with specific copy such as "Shop holiday decor".
- 5. Add social proof, such as ratings or "Selling fast" tags.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed tracking links returned HTTP 403 in the automated check, including the Unsubscribe link. Roughly 40 images lack alt text, and the JSON-LD block ships with empty and stale values. The truncated HTML and the missing relay headers limit what else can be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h8dd8b0d2-…` links returned 403.** They cover the "Shop now" CTAs (ids `…f89e033c`, `…033d`, `…03a8`, `…03a9`, `…031a`, `…031c`), the member banner (`…0318`), the logo (`…0319`), the image links (`…033a`, `…033b`, `…033e`) and Unsubscribe (`…03cc`).
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not broken links.** Every failure comes from one ESP redirect domain, and the failures include the Unsubscribe link. An uncaught WAF or bot filter on the redirector is more likely than 12 separate dead links, but this check can't confirm it. Verify with a real-client click-through or the ESP's link report.
- **None of the links resolved to a final destination.** All 156 links go through the `/r/?id=…` redirector, and 144 of them were never probed. Their landing pages are unverified.
- **Some link texts are poor.** One CTA is `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, with zero-width and invisible characters in the anchor text. Several links have image alt text as their only text (`[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`), so the QA tool is reading filenames as link text.
- **The plain-text part is 516 of 594 characters URLs (87%).** That is above the 70% threshold. The text part is almost entirely tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 40 images are missing alt text.** Examples:
  - Hero images `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` (`…/week-43/HHMI265P02-FN-2x3-1` through `-5`).
  - Product images such as `6dd05bac…jpg?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`.
  - The tracked-link image `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1`.
- **Each image appears twice, at `imWidth=344` and `322`,** and the hero images appear as 1400 and 750 variants. This looks like desktop/mobile duplication. Without alt text on every copy, screen readers likely announce each one, and with images blocked the email shows empty slots.
- **The `<style>` block contains invalid CSS.** `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` uses `auto`, which isn't a valid `line-height` value, so the declaration is dropped. `@media screen yahoo` is also not valid media-query syntax.
- **Web fonts load via `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…)` and `https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css`.** Gmail and some other clients strip `@import`, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is expected, but only a fallback stack protects it, and I couldn't see the stack in the truncated source.
- **Beyond the truncation point I couldn't check** the `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the `<title>`. The `<html>` tag shows no `lang`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The tracking URL carries per-recipient identifiers (`rid=3326930539`, `erid=MzMyNjkzMDUzOQ%3D%3D`). The `erid` value is the base64 of the `rid` value, so it is a reversible, non-opaque recipient ID. This is a privacy note, not a rendering defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers weren't found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this isn't confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `email.hm.com`; check the alignment of the redirector domain `t19.email.hm.com` and the envelope sender in the raw headers.
- **The in-body Unsubscribe link (`…f89e03cc…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`) returned 403.** If real users get the same response, that is a CAN-SPAM problem (the opt-out mechanism must work for at least 30 days). Verify by hand.
- **The physical postal address and footer text are in the truncated portion,** so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be evaluated.** The tracking URLs carry only the ESP's own parameters (`did=1419113918`, `p1=DM6184661`, `p2=0982dd0a…`, `p3=20261030`), and the final destinations are hidden behind the 403s.
- **The campaign parameters don't map to standard `utm_*` keys,** so any analytics attribution depends on a server-side mapping at the redirector.
- **I couldn't check landing-page alignment.** No link resolved, so I can't tell whether the "Cozy holiday decor" CTAs reach a matching page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Confirm the 403s are probe-only.** Click the CTAs and Unsubscribe from a real inbox or a headless-browser context. If real users are blocked, fix the WAF or bot rule on `t19.email.hm.com/r/`.
2. **Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers** in the raw message. Add them if they're missing.
3. **Add `alt` text to every `<img>`.** Use descriptive text for content images and `alt=""` for the duplicated desktop/mobile copies (hide one set from screen readers). Give the logo and linked images meaningful alt text so image-only links have an accessible name.
4. **Remove the invisible-character padding from the CTA anchor text.**
5. **Fix the invalid `line-height:auto`.** Use `normal` or an explicit value. Also review the `@media screen yahoo` hack.
6. **Populate or remove the JSON-LD blocks.** `subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image` and `url` are empty, and `availabilityStarts` is `2018-12-31T19:59:59-04:00`, a stale boilerplate date.
7. **Rebalance the plain-text part** so it has real copy beyond the 87% tracking URLs.
8. **Re-run the audit with full-source and raw-header access.** That would cover the footer address, `lang`, the font fallbacks, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the other 144 unprobed links.
