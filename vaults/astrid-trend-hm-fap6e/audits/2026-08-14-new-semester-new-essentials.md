# New semester, new essentials
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M pivoting to a full back-to-school editorial send — "New Semester Loading..." leads into category tiles (Campus Life, Occasion Edit, Dorm Shop) and closes with a bolted-on Santos Bravos livestream promo, the same YouTube tie-in that ran standalone on 8/9 ("Live from LA"). Like the 8/11 denim drop and 8/10 "Modern Romance" send, there is zero discount language anywhere in this render — no %, no code, no "sale" banner despite a "SALE" nav link sitting right in the header that goes unexplained in the body.
- The structure is clean and well-organized for a seasonal moodboard — dorm shop, campus life, occasion edit are logically segmented — but that's also the problem: it's five distinct offers/sections stacked into one long scroll with no single hero CTA, which dilutes click intent versus a tighter, single-focus send. Compared to the 8/9 and 8/12 tiered-discount sends (7.00/10 each), this reads as another full-price content play, continuing H&M's pattern of alternating between real markdowns and lifestyle content weeks.

## What's working

- Clear seasonal relevance: "New Semester Loading..." and the sub-copy about class/dorm/friends map directly onto a back-to-school moment.
- Good visual segmentation — Campus Life, Got Plans?, Just Like Home each get their own header and paired product shots, making the email easy to skim in sections.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, buttons are consistently styled throughout.
- The Santos Bravos livestream block reuses a proven content beat from 8/9, giving the brand a non-commerce engagement hook alongside the shopping content.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or promo code anywhere in the email despite a "SALE" link sitting in the top nav — that's a bait-and-switch signal for anyone who clicks in expecting a deal.
- Too many competing CTAs (Shop Now, Women, Men, Occasion Edit, The Dorm Shop, Campus Life x2, Occasion Edit again, The Dorm Shop again, Learn More, plus 5 footer category tiles) — no clear single hero action.
- The hero model shot has zero product framing (no linked item, no price, no "shop this look") — it's atmosphere only, wasting the most valuable visual real estate.
- The Santos Bravos section feels bolted onto the bottom of an unrelated back-to-school email rather than being its own focused send, diluting both messages.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero: lead with either "Dorm Shop" or "Campus Life" as the single primary CTA above the fold, and demote the other sections to a "shop more" strip — right now five sections compete for the same click.
- 2. Add a real offer. If there's a back-to-school discount running, put the % or $ in the subject and a banner under the hero — right now the "SALE" nav link over-promises with nothing to back it up in-body.
- 3. Link the hero image to a specific product or outfit instead of leaving it a static mood shot.
- 4. Split the Santos Bravos livestream into its own send (or move it to the very bottom footer only) — it competed for attention against the seasonal message instead of standing alone like it did on 8/9.
