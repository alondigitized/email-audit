# Modern heritage
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M back in full editorial mode, and — like the 08/25 "Season shift" and 08/24 "In focus: Layered contrasts" sends — it's a discount-free storytelling email. "DARK ROMANCE" and "THE NOSTALGIA COLLECTION" lead the copy, individual product prices ($69.99, $59.99, $39.99, $49.99) are shown, but nowhere in the render is a percent-off, promo code, or free-shipping line. The subject "Modern heritage" doesn't even match either on-page storyline (Dark Romance / Nostalgia Collection), which is a real disconnect between what's promised in the inbox and what's delivered in the body.
- Visually it's clean — moody black-and-white editorial photography, consistent grid, legible product cards — but this is the fourth editorial/no-discount send in five days (08/25, 08/24 x2, and now this one), against only two real offer sends (08/26 flash sale, 08/23 last-day 20%). For a subscriber who already knows H&M runs regular percent-off promos, this reads as filler between real sales.

## What's working

- Consistent, cohesive black-and-white fashion editorial styling throughout — feels premium and on-brand.
- Clear price tags and "New Arrival" labels under each product shot make it easy to scan the assortment.
- Two distinct CTAs ("Shop Now") are well-placed after each storyline section, both visually obvious as outlined buttons.
- "Shop By" category grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) at the bottom gives a clean secondary navigation path.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is straight.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Modern heritage" appears nowhere in the visible copy — the email is actually about "Dark Romance" and "The Nostalgia Collection," a mismatch that will confuse anyone who opens expecting heritage-styled content.
- Zero discount, code, or free-shipping offer anywhere in the render — a pure full-price editorial push, same miss as 08/25 and 08/24.
- Two unrelated concepts (Dark Romance apparel, Nostalgia fragrance collection) are jammed into one email with no connective thread, diluting focus.
- Perfume hero image is visually muddled — hands/bottles overlapping with sparkly bokeh background makes the product itself hard to identify at a glance.
- "SALE" appears in the top nav bar but is never expanded on in the body — a tease with no follow-through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — either rename the subject to match the actual content ("Dark romance meets nostalgia") or restructure the email around an actual "heritage" narrative. This is the single biggest miss; a subscriber opening for "heritage" content gets goth-editorial and perfume instead.
- 2. Split the two storylines into separate sends — Dark Romance apparel and Nostalgia fragrance drop are different categories with different buyers; combining them weakens both CTAs.
- 3. Add a real offer. Given the brand's cadence (flash sale 08/26, last-day 20% 08/23), inserting even a modest "15% off new arrivals" banner here would meaningfully lift this from a pure lookbook to a converting email.
- 4. Simplify the perfume hero shot — crop tighter on the two bottles so the product is the clear focal point instead of competing with the model's hands and background sparkle.
