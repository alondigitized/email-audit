# Up to 50% off the season's bestsellers
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but generic H&M Black Friday send. The hero is strong: a black-and-white model photo with "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" in big red type, then "UP TO 50% OFF ONLINE & IN STORES" and a clear SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first. The subject, "Up to 50% off the season's bestsellers," matches the body copy ("Beat the holiday rush before the season's bestsellers sell out").
- The problem is the middle. After the hero, the email turns into a category menu: Women, Men, Baby, Kids, then a holiday gift guide, then a Shop By grid. None of those blocks shows a product, a price or a "bestseller" label. A reader who opens for bestsellers gets category buttons. It's also the sixth H&M send I've audited since 11/23, and the offer is the same "up to 50%" each time. The brand is down to rotating the subject theme, as with denim on 11/24 and activewear on 11/23.
- Verdict: solid execution, low-urgency and low-specificity content. It will do fine for people already shopping H&M and won't move anyone else.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. Red "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" over the model photo, with the 50% headline directly below it.
- The subject and body agree. Unlike the 11/23 "Activewear deals now from $9.99" send, which never showed $9.99, this one doesn't overpromise.
- The paired SHOP NOW and FIND A STORE buttons cover both online and in-store shoppers.
- The model photography is clean and consistent, and the layout has plenty of white space.
- The Women, Men and Kids blocks each carry a single clear button.

## What's weak

- No product, price or "bestseller" proof appears anywhere. The "Most Popular" and "Season MVPs" blocks are just lifestyle photos with a category button.
- The offer is vague: "up to 50%" with no hero item, no sale price and no real deadline. The only deadline is in the fine print (through 11/25/2026).
- The email is long. The gift guide and Shop By grid repeat the same destinations, and the gift guide's collage tiles are hard to read, with no products labeled.
- The H&M gift card line sits in red at the very top and competes with the Black Friday message.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of text, and it carries the real terms (exclusions, a Dec 25 return window).

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the category blocks with real bestsellers: 4–6 named products with sale prices and "was/now" pricing. This is the change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends tonight" or "Ends Monday," so the urgency is visible and not buried in the fine print.
- 3. Cut the Shop By grid and the gift guide, or fold them into one short block.
- 4. Drop the gift card line above the hero, or move it below the main offer.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific. "Shop women's bestsellers" is better than "WOMEN."
- 6. Vary the creative against the 11/24 and 11/23 sends. Give one hero product or one price-point story to each send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bestsellers up to 50% off — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our 12 most-bought pieces, now up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Knits, denim and outerwear that sell out first. Online and in stores.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Black Friday prices on the pieces everyone's buying. Shop before sizes go.`

# Technical Audit: H&M "Up to 50% off the season's bestsellers"

## 1. Technical Summary
Every probed link returned HTTP 403, including the unsubscribe link. All 12 probes went to the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` tracking redirector, so this is most likely bot-blocking of the QA probe rather than proof that real users are blocked. The source also has a placeholder JSON-LD block with empty and stale values, many images without alt text, and no captured authentication or unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403.** That covers "Shop now" (`...f8e03432`, `...f8e03434`, `...f8e03410`, `...f8e03412`), "Find a store" (`...f8e03433`, `...f8e03435`), the "H&M" logo (`...f8e0340f`), image links (`...f8e03430`, `...f8e03431`, `...f8e03436`, `...f8e03437`) and "Unsubscribe" (`...f8e0347e`).
  - Every URL shares the same host, path, `id` prefix and `did`/`rid`/`erid` parameters. A uniform 403 across all of them points to redirector-level bot filtering. I can't confirm from this data that real recipients are affected, so verify with a real-client click test.
- **Opaque tracking.** All links are wrapped in `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...&p1=DM6239481&p2=...&p3=20261125`. The final destination of each CTA can't be seen, so destination, deep-link correctness and UTM presence are unverifiable.
- **Odd link text.** The "Shop now" anchors for `...f8e03410` and `...f8e03412` start with invisible characters (ZWNJ/BOM, shown as `‌  ﻿Shop now`). This is preheader-style padding leaking into the anchor text and may affect link-text parsing and screen-reader output.
- **Coverage gap.** 74 links were skipped by the prober (footer, utility, social), so those are unvalidated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on about 20 images, per the QA data:
  - Product and campaign images have filename-style alts (`cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg`) or an opaque string (`KpezCX5sKk4l+pLkCeI=`).
    - Examples: `.../black-friday/women/CLOC265P01-BF26-Women-1-3x2.jpg`, `.../us186_s4/FP5-BS-US186-1359286001_202604_...`, `.../cocc265p01a/FNP-COCC265P01-2x3-send-out-1-gift-guid...`.
  - Two tracking-URL images (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...`) have no alt.
  - The image links are the only content of their anchors, so screen readers will announce filenames or nothing, and the CTA is lost with images blocked.
- **Duplicate image variants.** The 750 and 1400 versions of each product image appear to be paired for desktop/mobile switching (`.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`, `.hide-webkit` / `.show-webkit-table`). Both copies lack alt, so the problem doubles.
- **Web fonts.** The `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `oces.css` lines are stripped by Gmail and Outlook. This is minor; the fallback stack couldn't be checked in the truncated source.
- The viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and a 375px mobile breakpoint are present. No other rendering defects were confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The tracking parameters (`rid`, `erid`, `p2`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header was captured.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not a confirmed sender failure. Check the raw message before treating it as one.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain (`email.hm.com`) is consistent with the tracking host.
- **The in-body Unsubscribe link returned 403** (`...f8e0347e&...&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746...`). A unique-token unsubscribe link behind the same bot filtering can't be confirmed working. Confirm it manually, since this is a CAN-SPAM requirement.
- The physical postal address wasn't visible in the truncated source and can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified. All links use an opaque redirector with `p1`/`p2`/`p3` parameters (`p3=20261125` is the send date) and no visible `utm_*`.
- The landing pages for the "Shop now" and "Find a store" CTAs weren't reached because of the 403s, so email-to-page alignment is untested.
- **JSON-LD block is a placeholder** and ships with empty or stale values:
  - `"subjectLine":""`
  - `"discountCode":""`
  - `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`, which is eight years stale
  - `"url":""` and `"image":""` in the PromotionCard
  - Gmail promotion annotations won't work, and the stale date could cause the markup to be ignored or flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the tracked links and the unsubscribe URL from a real browser or client (a human click, or a probe with a realistic UA/allowlist) to settle whether the 403s are real. Fix the unsubscribe path first if it fails.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all product and campaign images, especially linked images. Use `alt=""` for decorative duplicates and don't leave filenames.
3. Remove the zero-width and BOM characters from inside the "Shop now" anchors.
4. Populate or remove the JSON-LD block: set the real `subjectLine`, offer dates and `url`/`image`, or drop the empty `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the raw message, and confirm `t19.email.hm.com` is aligned.
6. Confirm that final landing URLs carry campaign attribution (`utm_*` or the equivalent) after the redirect.
7. Extend QA probing to cover more than 12 of the 86 links (74 were skipped) and run it from a non-blocked client.
