# H&M Moves New York
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-campaign send, not a promo. "H&M Moves New York" pairs a strong running-in-NYC hero with a short paragraph about H&M MOVE and a product grid of running gear. The photography is cohesive and the city setting (bridge, yellow cab, crosswalk) feels specific, which is a nice change from the week's repetitive sale banners. For a fitness-leaning recipient it's relevant and clean.
- It sells nothing. After a week of "up to 40% off sitewide" (10/8, 10/10, 10/11, 10/12, and "ends tonight" on 10/13), there is no discount and no deadline. The only urgency is a "same-day pickup" line at the top. A subscriber trained on the Fall Event sees the sale vanish with no explanation. The email also has no loyalty hook and no clear hero product.
- The verdict is that it works as a brand-building change of pace but converts poorly. The creative is good, but the CTAs are generic category buttons, and the products have no reasons to buy now.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, on-theme image: three runners on a New York bridge with the "H&M MOVES NEW YORK" lockup in red.
- Product images are all running-specific, so the story is consistent from hero to grid.
- Technical copy ("CoolMove™", "DryMove™", "distance-tested layers") gives an actual reason to care about the gear.
- Prices ($14.99–$49.99) and "New Arrival" tags are visible under the products.
- The top banner offers free same-day pickup with an order cutoff (4PM ET), which is a credible, concrete utility.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. After the 40%-off week, the email drops the discount entirely and doesn't mention the ongoing event.
- The CTAs are generic: "WOMEN", "MEN" and "FIND A STORE" are outlined buttons with no verb and no running link.
- The product tiles have no visible CTA, and I can't tell whether each one links to its product.
- The hero image carries no headline or button, so the eye lands on the photo with no action attached.
- The "Shop By" section (Home, Beauty, Kids) sits below a running story and dilutes the focus.
- There are no reviews, sizing or fit details, and no member pricing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one clear offer or reason to act, such as "Free shipping on MOVE" or a member perk. Put it in the hero and the preheader.
- 2. Replace the generic Women/Men buttons with specific ones like "Shop women's running" and "Shop men's running".
- 3. Put a CTA button directly under the hero so the top of the email has a visible next step.
- 4. Add a "Shop the look" link on each product tile, plus one hero product with a visible rating.
- 5. Tie the email to the Fall Event, or say plainly that this send is a break from it, so the dropped discount doesn't read as a bait-and-switch.

# Technical Audit: "H&M Moves New York" (newsletter@email.hm.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` click-tracking redirector, and that includes the unsubscribe link. The 403s look like bot-blocking by the tracker, but the probes are inconclusive. The email also has widespread alt-text gaps, empty JSON-LD fields, and a URL-heavy plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 12 probed links failed with 403.** They cover "Find a store" (two links), "Need it today? Order by 4pm ET…", "Shop now" (two), the "H&M" logo, Women/Men category links, both hero images, and "Unsubscribe". Every one goes through `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3cd3b06b-…&did=1418798792&rid=3326930539&…`.
- **The cause is probably the QA prober being blocked, not broken links.** A uniform 403 across every link, including the unsubscribe link, points to bot or WAF filtering on the redirector. That means the final destination URLs could not be verified. Treat this as unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **Coverage is limited.** 62 of 74 links were skipped (footer, social, utility), so their status is unknown.
- **The tracking URLs carry a per-recipient identifier (`rid=3326930539`).** `erid` is the same value base64-encoded. This is normal ESP tracking, but it means every link in the email identifies the recipient. The unsubscribe link also adds `p4=3326930539` and `p5=9be06746…`.
- **Some link text is unusable.** The "Shop now" anchors have text prefixed with zero-width characters (`‌  ﻿`, ZWNJ/BOM), which is noisy for screen readers and link analytics. Two image links have QA link text of `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` and `[image: cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg]`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing or non-descriptive on about 17 images.** The affected sources are:
  - `https://image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-42/us162_s4/SS60264P03_NYCMarathon_3x2.jpg` and `…_2x3.jpg`
  - the `SS60264P03_01`, `_02`, `_04`, `_07`, `_09` and `_10` `_936x1404` files, each in two variants
  - the tracking-pixel-style image at `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3cd3b06b-14a9-4a73-9763-aaf7…,1`

  The QA tool reports the alt values as `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`. These are DAM filenames, not descriptions. Each product image appears twice (1400 and 750), which suggests separate desktop and mobile variants.
- **Each hero image is probably duplicated for desktop and mobile.** Screen readers may read both copies unless one is hidden with `display:none` plus `aria-hidden`. The CSS has `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop` using `mso-hide`, but the truncated source doesn't show whether the duplicates carry alt text or `aria-hidden`.
- **The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute and the document has no `<title>`** in the visible part of the source. Only `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">` is shown, and the rest is truncated.
- **Web fonts are loaded with `@import`** (`fabric.hmgroup.com/.../fonts.css`, `s1-cdn.hm.com/.../oces.css`). Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import`, so they fall back to system fonts.
- **Plain text is mostly URLs**: 520 of 598 characters (87%) are URLs. That is a poor text alternative and a minor spam-scoring risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **The JSON-LD block (`<script type="application/ld+json">`) has empty or stale fields.** These are `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`, `PromotionCard` `"image":""` and `"url":""`, and `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`. This looks like unpopulated template markup, so Gmail promotion annotations won't work.
- **No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) were found** in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not found.** The QA tool notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **The `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click) was also not found.** Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, subject to the same relay caveat.
- **The `Authentication-Results` header was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed.
- **An in-body unsubscribe link is present** (`…f873d9b1…&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`). It returned 403 to the prober, so its function is unverified. A physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters appear in any tracked URL.** Tracking uses proprietary `p1=DM6133385`, `p2=0982dd0a…`, `p3=20261014` parameters, so any UTM tagging would be applied at the redirect destination.
- **Landing pages could not be checked.** The 403s blocked redirect resolution, so I couldn't confirm that "Find a store", the same-day pickup link and "Shop now" land on matching pages.
- **Several links appear to share a destination.** The two "Find a store" links and the Women links differ only in the last segment of `id=…,f873d975` / `…978` / `…973` / `…976`. This is link-level tracking granularity, not an error.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the redirect links** with a browser-like User-Agent or the ESP's allowlisted IP. This separates real breakage from bot-blocking, especially for the unsubscribe link.
2. **Replace filename alt text** (`cq5dam.web.*.jpeg`) with descriptive alt on content images. Use `alt=""` on decorative or duplicate images. Add `aria-hidden="true"` to the hidden desktop or mobile duplicates.
3. **Add `lang` to `<html>` and a `<title>`.**
4. **Populate the JSON-LD** (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `PromotionCard.image` and `url`) and fix the stale 2018 `availabilityStarts`, or remove the block.
5. **Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** on the original message, bypassing the relay, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `email.hm.com`.
6. **Shorten or restructure the plain-text part**, which is 87% URLs, so it carries readable copy.
7. **Strip the zero-width characters** from CTA link text.
8. **Verify landing-page and UTM continuity** once the redirects resolve.
