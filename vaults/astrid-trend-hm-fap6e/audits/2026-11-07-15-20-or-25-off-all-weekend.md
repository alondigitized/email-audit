# 15%, 20% or 25% off all weekend
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M repeating the tiered Black Friday offer from the 2026-11-06 send ("Up to 25% off your order starts now"). The offer block is strong: "15% OFF $75 / 20% OFF $100 / 25% OFF $125 + FREE SHIPPING" in bold black type is the clearest thing in the email. The weekend framing in the subject ("all weekend") adds credibility. The fine print says the offer ends 11/08/2026, so the deadline is real.
- The hero lets it down. A child in a "Jolly Time" red sweater is a holiday-gifting image, but it doesn't tell a shopper whether this is for them. The black banner above it, "BECOME A MEMBER FOR EXCLUSIVE BLACK FRIDAY ACCESS + 10% OFF YOUR FIRST PURCHASE", sets up a second, competing offer. Red text on black is hard to read. Below the offer sits a long grid of category tiles with no products, prices or reasons to click.
- Overall it is a competent, repetitive promo. The offer is clear, but it is the third near-identical threshold send in four days (11/04 flash sale, 11/06 tiers, now this). Nothing new is added. It's a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The tiered offer is bold, scannable and sits right under the hero. The spend thresholds are stated plainly.
- "+ FREE SHIPPING" is included in the headline block.
- The "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE" buttons are visible and clearly styled.
- The category tiles (Women, Men, Baby, Kids, Home, Sport) let a shopper jump straight to their section.
- The hero imagery is seasonal and on-brand for holiday gifting. The deadline in the fine print (11/08) is credible.

## What's weak

- The top banner's red-on-black text is low-contrast, and its member offer (10% off a first purchase) competes with the main promo.
- The hero is a child in a novelty sweater. It doesn't match the broad "you" in "gifts and outfits for you and them", and nothing in it shows the offer applies to adults.
- The body copy, "Get a head start on the holidays with big savings on gifts and outfits for you and them.*", is generic. It names no products and gives no urgency.
- The category tiles and the second "SHOP BY" grid repeat each other, which makes the email long and redundant. No product, price or review gives anyone a reason to click through.
- The deadline appears only in the fine print. The email body never says "ends Sunday".
- The fine print is a dense wall of text with many exclusions, which undercuts the honest feel of the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the offer block, e.g. "Ends Sunday 11/8", and make it the visual anchor. Drop or demote the member banner so there is one offer.
- 2. Replace the child-only hero with a split or adult-led image, or segment the hero by gender and category.
- 3. Add 3–4 real products with prices, such as a jacket or sweater at the 25% tier, in place of the empty category tiles.
- 4. Cut the duplicate "SHOP BY" grid, and move the long legal copy behind a "Details" link.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11/06 send. Show a "what $125 gets you" basket to give the tiers a reason to exist.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: up to 25% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $125, save 25% — this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gifts and outfits for everyone, with free shipping on every tier.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Offer ends 11/8. Here's what to grab first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based template with responsive CSS, but the automated QA run cannot confirm that any tracked link resolves. All 12 probed links returned 403, and the unsubscribe headers and authentication results are missing from the capture. Most images have no alt text, and the JSON-LD block is empty.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403** (16% pass rate). They are all on `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h78ecf624-…`: "Shop now" (×4), "Find a store" (×2), the "H&M" logo, four image links and "Unsubscribe".
  - Every failure comes from the same redirector host, and the 403 is uniform. That points to bot or WAF blocking of the probe, not 12 separate broken links.
  - I can't confirm real-user breakage from this data, and the final destinations were never resolved.
  - 56 links were skipped. Footer, social and utility links were not verified.
- **Duplicate tracked links per module.** Each product has a separate 1400px and 750px image link (for example `…,55138b91,f8b35fca` and `…,55138b91,f8b35fcb`). The source uses `is-hidden-mobile` and `is-hidden-desktop` classes to swap them, so this is a desktop/mobile variant pattern. Click counts will split across the two link IDs unless they are aggregated.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** Two "Shop now" links (`…f8b35faa`, `…f8b35fac`) have link text of `"‌  ﻿Shop now"`, which carries ZWNJ and BOM characters. These can make link text read oddly in assistive tech and in text-based link matching.
- **Opaque tracking only.** There are no direct destination URLs in the captured links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 16 of the images flagged by QA.**
  - Hero and module images are `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`, served from `image.hm.com/content/dam/hm/regional-local-activities/ame/2026-ame/wk-45/…`.
  - Each of these is also wrapped in a link, so the link's accessible name falls back to the filename. This shows as `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]` in the link text.
  - The tracking pixel/image `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…,1` is also flagged. A decorative or tracking image should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Invalid or non-standard CSS.**
  - `.is-hidden-desktop{… line-height:auto!important …}` is invalid, because `auto` is not a valid `line-height` value.
  - `@media screen yahoo { … }` is a malformed media query and will be ignored in most clients.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** The `<style>` block imports `fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css` and `s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css`. Gmail and several other clients drop `@import`, so those clients fall back to system fonts. This is acceptable only if the fallback stack is defined.
- **The source is truncated.** I can't evaluate the font-stack fallbacks, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, `lang` on `<html>`, or `<title>`.
