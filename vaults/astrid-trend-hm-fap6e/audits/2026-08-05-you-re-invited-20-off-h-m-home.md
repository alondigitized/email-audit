# You're invited: 20% off H&M HOME
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's sixth back-to-school-adjacent send in a week (following 7/30's "back-to-school checklist" through 8/4's "Contrast dressing"), but it finally breaks the "up to 30% off" apparel loop by pivoting to H&M Home dorm decor — bedding, towels, a tiered tray, a duvet set. The gingham-wallpaper hero shot sets the dorm-room scene well and the product grid is specific and priced, which is more than most of this week's sends managed.
- The execution stumbles on message discipline: the very first line of the email shouts "SCORE 10% OFF YOUR ORDER WITH STUDENTBEANS!*" in caps, then two screens later the actual hero offer is "20% OFF $60." Two different percentages, two different mechanisms, no clear hierarchy between them — a reader skimming fast could easily misread which discount applies to them.
- Net: solid creative pivot, undermined by a self-inflicted offer collision and zero urgency. Middling send, better than the identical 30%-off apparel emails only because it's finally a new category.

## What's working

- Fresh category angle — H&M Home dorm decor breaks the run of near-identical 30%-off apparel emails from 7/30–8/4.
- Product grid is specific and priced: cushion cover $19.99, bath sheet $24.99, two-tiered tray $29.99, twin duvet set $39.99.
- Hero photo (gingham wallpaper, wood vanity, stool) immediately reads as "dorm room," which matches the campaign intent.
- Consistent, trustworthy footer — logo, social icons, app download, store locator all present and clean.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers stacked at the top — "10% off with StudentBeans" banner vs. "20% off $60" H&M Home headline — with no explanation of how they relate.
- "SHOP NOW" is the CTA copy on nearly every button (five separate times) with zero differentiation by section.
- No deadline or urgency anywhere in the email — nothing pushes a same-day decision.
- Sixth straight day of back-to-school-themed messaging; a subscriber who's opened this week's prior sends will recognize the beat instantly.
- No loyalty/member pricing shown despite the brand clearly having a subscriber program (StudentBeans is a third-party discount, not H&M loyalty).

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the offer collision first — pick one lead offer (20% off $60) for the hero, and demote StudentBeans to a small secondary footnote instead of the first line a reader sees.
- 2. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTAs — "Shop Bedding," "Shop Dorm Decor" — so each button earns its click.
- 3. Add a credible deadline ("Ends Sunday") to the hero offer to create real urgency.
- 4. Break the cadence pattern — after six straight BTS-flavored sends, the next email should skip the discount/BTS frame entirely (editorial or a different category) to avoid fatigue.
- 5. Test alt subject/preheader pairs:
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: 20% off H&M Home dorm essentials`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dorm room refresh — 20% off $60+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy bedding, towels & decor for your first semester.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: students save an extra 10% with StudentBeans.`

# Technical Audit — H&M "You're invited: 20% off H&M HOME"

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links (redirector, CTAs, images, unsubscribe) route through `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...` and return HTTP 403 on automated probing, and both unsubscribe-related headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) are absent — automated pass rate 16% (12 issues, 4 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12/12 probed links return **403 Broken link**, including the primary CTAs ("Shop now" ×4), the H&M logo link, both product image links, and the **Unsubscribe** link itself — e.g. `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=hd0b01b91-370c-4962-ba0a-8735cf58502c,6638a429,f7aad112&did=1714988073&rid=3326930539...`
- Because *every* sampled link on the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector domain fails uniformly with the same 403, this pattern is consistent with the ESP click-tracking domain blocking the automated prober (bot/WAF challenge) rather than each destination being individually dead. Evidence: identical 403 status across unrelated destinations (CTA, logo, unsubscribe, product images). **Manual verification in a real client is needed to confirm whether these are true dead links or prober-side blocking.**
- One link fails with a distinct error, not a 403: the StudentBeans CTA ("Score 10% off your order with StudentBeans!*") — `unknown url type: '/api/beansid-connect/entrypoint/v4-redirect?requestedUrl=%2Fv4%2Fhosted%2Fh-m%2Fus'`. This indicates the redirect target resolves to a **relative path, not an absolute URL**, which is a genuine construction defect independent of the 403 pattern above.
- 76 of 88 total links were deprioritized/skipped by the prober (footer/utility/social); only 12 "material" links were checked, so link coverage is partial.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including all primary product images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` / `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` variants) and two tracking pixels served from the `t19.email.hm.com` redirector. Screen readers will announce these images as unlabeled.
- Two CTA link texts contain embedded zero-width/invisible characters: `"‌  ﻿Shop now"` (zero-width non-joiner U+200C and a byte-order-mark/zero-width no-break space U+FEFF before the visible text). These are likely link-tracking/obfuscation artifacts and can cause inconsistent rendering or mis-announced link text in screen readers.
- Extensive mobile-responsive CSS (`.is-hidden-mobile`, `.is-hidden-desktop`, `@media (max-width:699px)`) is present and structurally sound; no broken selectors observed in the visible excerpt.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded JSON-LD (`application/ld+json`) `EmailMessage`/`DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` block has multiple **empty string fields** that appear to be unresolved merge tokens: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, `"image":""`, `"url":""`, `"availabilityEnds":""`. These should be populated dynamically per send; empty values mean Google's Gmail promotional markup (and any discount-code auto-fill) will not render correctly.
- `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"` is a stale hardcoded date, ~7.5 years before the send date (2026-08-05) — indicates a template default that was never overridden for this campaign.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** header not present in the message headers captured by the relay — no mailbox-provider-level one-click unsubscribe option exposed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) header also absent, so even if `List-Unsubscribe` were added, one-click POST unsubscribe would not be supported.
- The in-body unsubscribe link (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?...p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`) is present as required by CAN-SPAM, but it is among the links returning 403 in the automated probe — combined with missing unsubscribe headers, this is the highest-priority item to verify manually, given legal exposure if the mechanism is genuinely non-functional.
- **Authentication-Results** header not found via the AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be confirmed from available data.
- Plain-text part is 520/598 characters (87%) URLs, which increases spam-filter risk and provides a poor fallback experience for plain-text readers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use ESP redirect-tracking params (`did`, `rid`, `erid`, `p1`–`p5`) rather than conventional UTM query params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, etc.) — `p3=20260805` matches the send date, and `p1=DM5913418` appears to be the campaign ID, consistent with internal tracking rather than GA-style UTMs.
- Because all sampled redirects returned 403 during probing, the actual final landing-page URLs (and whether they carry UTM parameters, or align with the "20% off H&M HOME" offer) could not be verified. Continuity cannot be confirmed until link resolution is checked manually.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually load 2–3 of the failing links (e.g., a "Shop now" CTA and the Unsubscribe link) in a browser/real client to distinguish true 403s from prober blocking — this determines whether items in §2/§5 are launch-blockers or false positives.
2. Fix the StudentBeans redirect target so it resolves to an absolute URL instead of a relative path (`/api/beansid-connect/entrypoint/v4-redirect?...`).
3. Add `alt` text to all 12 flagged product/tracking images.
4. Populate the empty JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `discountCode`, `description`, `image`, `url`) and correct the stale `availabilityStarts` date before send.
5. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements and enable one-click unsubscribe.
6. Strip the stray zero-width/BOM characters from the two "Shop now" link text strings.
7. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment (Authentication-Results) directly with the sending ESP, since it wasn't observable via the relay.
