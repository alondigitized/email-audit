# Last day: 15% or 20% off your order
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's fourth send in the "15%/20% off" tiered-discount arc — 8/7 introduced it, 8/8 skinned it for back-to-school, and now 8/9 closes it out with "last day" urgency in the subject line, though notably that urgency doesn't make it into the hero itself. The hero image is clean — a burgundy V-neck sweater on a model, on-brand for the "fall starts here" copy beneath it — and the nav grid returns to the general WOMEN/MEN/BABY/KIDS/HOME split rather than yesterday's back-to-school skew.
- The execution is competent but flat: no countdown clock, no "ends tonight" banner, nothing in the visual hierarchy that signals this is the last chance to catch the discount. A subscriber skimming the image would have no idea this is a closing-day push — that urgency lives entirely in the subject line and the fine print at the bottom.
- Fine, not exceptional. It's the same mechanic run four days running with a slightly better subject line than 8/7 and 8/8, but the body doesn't earn the "last day" framing it's using to drive opens.

## What's working

- Hero headline "15% OFF $80 OR 20% OFF $100" is large, bold, and unambiguous — the tiered offer is legible at a glance.
- Product imagery is sharp with no render bugs — sweater, quarter-zip, and kids' Hello Kitty collab shot are all clean.
- Nav CTAs (WOMEN/MEN/BABY/KIDS/HOME) are bordered, tappable-looking buttons with clear category labels.
- "SHOP BY" category tiles give a second, visually distinct path to browse beyond the top nav.

## What's weak

- Zero visual urgency — no countdown, no "today only" badge, nothing that reflects the "last day" framing from the subject.
- No single primary CTA button tied to the offer itself (e.g., "Shop the Sale") — the hero just cuts straight to nav buttons.
- Identical structural template to 8/7 and 8/8 (hero stat, model shot, nav grid, shop-by grid) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who's opened the last few.
- Fine print reveals real exclusions (sale items, H&M Studio, guest collabs, not stackable) that undercut the headline offer's simplicity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible urgency element in the hero — a "ENDS TONIGHT" or countdown strip above/below the headline — since the subject promises "last day" but the body doesn't deliver on it visually. This is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Add a bold CTA button directly under the hero copy ("Shop the Sale") instead of relying on the nav row below the images.
- 3. Vary the layout from 8/7 and 8/8 — swap in a different hero composition or crop so repeat openers don't pattern-match instantly.
- 4. Move the discount-tier math (spend $80/$100) closer to the hero so shoppers don't need to scroll to the footnote to understand thresholds.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 15% or 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — take 20% off $100+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your fall cart before the discount disappears.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy knits, sweats, and moody hues — 15-20% off ends today.`
