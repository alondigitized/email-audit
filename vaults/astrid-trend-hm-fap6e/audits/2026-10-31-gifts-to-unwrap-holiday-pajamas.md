# Gifts to unwrap: Holiday pajamas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-season catalog send for H&M's holiday pajama shop. The 2x3 collage hero (plaid and striped pajama tops, a red bow, pink satin) looks warm and giftable. "HOLIDAY PAJAMAS" with "The gift for winding down. Made for cozy evenings and lazy mornings." makes the category obvious in about a second.
- It has no offer at all. After the 10/27 and 10/28 tiered-threshold sends ("up to 25% off + free shipping ends today") and the 10/29 and 10/30 launch sends, this one drops all price incentive. It follows the same product-grid template as the 10/30 "Cozy holiday decor" send. The Women/Men/Kids buttons help routing, but there is no single hero product and no deadline. The slippers and sweatpants in the grid are also off-topic for a "pajamas" subject, though they fit the cozy theme.
- Judgement: a pleasant browse email that is pretty but low-urgency. It will pull gift browsers and do little for anyone who wants a deal.

## What's working

- The collage hero is on-season, with strong color (red, pink, plaid) and an obvious pajama theme.
- The bold "HOLIDAY PAJAMAS" headline plus a one-line subhead gives a clear hierarchy.
- The Women / Men / Kids buttons route the reader by segment right under the headline.
- The product grid shows prices and "New Arrival" tags, so the price range ($19.99–$69.99) is visible without a click.
- The footer has a "Shop by" block, which gives extra paths into Home and Beauty.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, no member benefit and no deadline. After the 25%-off sends earlier this week, this one feels flat.
- The grid has no links or labels that tell me which product is the hero. It is 14 items with equal weight.
- The product grid mixes in slippers, boxer shorts, sweatpants, a cashmere baby jumpsuit and kids' tees. That dilutes the "pajamas" promise.
- Two products are titled just "PAJAMAS" with no detail, and no reviews, sizing or fit info appears anywhere.
- The three audience buttons are plain outlined boxes. They are visually weaker than the headline, so the CTA is understated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to buy now, such as member pricing, a bundle price, or "free shipping over $75". This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the grid from 14 items to 6–8 real pajama sets, and feature one hero set at the top with a "Shop the set" CTA.
- 3. Make the Women/Men/Kids buttons filled and use specific copy, such as "Shop women's pajamas".
- 4. Add a gift angle: a "Gift by price" strip ($25 / $50 / $75) or a delivery-by-Dec-24 cutoff.
- 5. Move the cashmere jumpsuit and kids' items into their own gifting module.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based H&M template with Northstar web fonts and a mobile `@media (max-width:699px)` layer. Every probed tracking link returned 403 to the QA probe, and about 30 images are missing alt text. The header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links returned 403**, including the Unsubscribe link, the logo ("H&M"), both "Shop now" CTAs, the hero images, and the Women/Men/Kids nav (each appears twice). They all go through the ESP redirector `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3ed521b0-…,5495f9da,<linkid>&did=1419114970&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6184724&p2=…&p3=20261031`.
- **The 403s are probably bot-blocking, not dead links.** The probe is an automated, non-browser client, and the 403 is uniform across link IDs (`f8a0efcf`, `f8a0efd0`, `f8a0efd2`, `f8a0eff0`–`f7`, `f8a0f06e`). I can't confirm the links work for real recipients or that they don't. Treat the "16% pass rate" as unreliable for that reason.
- **Probe coverage was limited:** 12 links were probed and 123 were skipped, so the product-grid links are unverified.
- **Tracking tokens:** `rid`, `erid` (base64 of `3326930539`), `p2` (a 32-char hash) and `p5` (on the unsubscribe link) are per-recipient identifiers in the URL query string. This is normal for ESP tracking, not a defect.
- **Fragile image-link markup:** one image link has an empty `alt` and its link text is the raw URL (`https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h3ed521b0-2305-4ebc-b692-6d5e…`). This looks like a tracking-pixel or image-wrapper link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images.** This covers the two hero images (`cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`) and the product-grid images, which appear at both `imWidth=344` and `imWidth=322`. Examples:
  - `78197e66…jpg`
  - `561faff6…jpg`
  - `94d4b1ff…jpg`
  - `ca2d884f…jpg`
  - `0f7e0f31…jpg`
  - `d2d07104…jpg`
  - `475b63ad…jpg`
- **Image links without text equivalents:** the hero image links expose only `[image: cq5dam…]` as link text. With images blocked, or for screen readers, the link is unlabeled.
- **Duplicate image sets:** the same images appear at two widths (344 and 322), which suggests desktop and mobile variants toggled by `.is-hidden-mobile` / `.is-hidden-desktop`. Screen readers may read both sets if the hidden variant isn't also `aria-hidden`. I couldn't confirm this because the HTML is truncated.
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">` has no `lang`. I also saw no `<title>` in the visible head.
- **Web-font dependency:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/fabric-fonts/…)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/global/hm-fonts/4.0.1/oces.css)` only work in clients that support `@import`. Gmail ignores them, so confirm the fallback font stack.
- **Mobile layout:** `.email-layout{width:375px!important;min-width:375px!important}` and `.footer-table{width:375px!important}` hard-code the mobile width. Screens narrower than 375px could scroll horizontally.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain `email.hm.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present.** The "Unsubscribe" link goes to `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?…f8a0f06e…&p5=9be06746af74de7271d9dcc8d83f5742`. It returned 403 to the probe, so I can't confirm it works for real users.
- **Plain-text part:** 514 of 592 characters are URLs (over 70%). That is a spam-scoring risk, and the text alternative carries almost no readable content.
- I could not verify the postal address in the footer because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Every link is an ESP redirect (`t19.email.hm.com/r/`) with opaque `p1=DM6184724`, `p2`, `p3=20261031` (a date) and `did`/`rid` identifiers. Campaign attribution is presumably applied at the redirect destination, which the QA probe couldn't reach.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified** because of the 403s. I can't confirm the "Holiday pajamas" CTA lands on a matching H&M page, or whether the Women/Men/Kids links go to relevant gift-guide pages.
- The image filenames (`…gift-guide-2026-US…`) suggest a US gift-guide campaign.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracking links** with a browser-like User-Agent or a headless browser to separate bot-blocking 403s from real failures. Check the unsubscribe link first.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to all product and hero images, and `alt=""` to decorative or duplicate responsive variants.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and a `<title>`.
4. **Clean up the JSON-LD** by removing the empty `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries or populating them, and dropping the 2018 `availabilityStarts` date.
5. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment,** from the original SMTP message rather than the AgentMail relay copy.
6. **Improve the plain-text part** so it has more readable copy relative to URLs.
7. **Confirm the final landing URLs** by following the redirects, and record the UTM or campaign parameters applied.
