# Get the look: @triszyk in LOTTO H&M
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- This is an influencer-collab send — H&M partnering with content creator Trisan (@triszyk) to showcase the LOTTO x H&M men's athletic/streetwear line. The creative is clean: the Lotto x H&M lockup is prominent, the hero shot of @triszyk styled on outdoor steps is editorial-quality, and the product grid shows real prices on real SKUs. In isolation it's a competent collab launch email.
- The problem is context. Yesterday's email was "Last day: 25% off + free shipping." Today H&M is sending two editorials with zero offer — this one and "Must-have pieces for summer layering" landing the same day. Subscribers conditioned to 25%-off sends will triage this as noise fast.
- Bigger structural miss: LOTTO x H&M is clearly a men's athletic collab — soccer jerseys, football shorts, coated shorts, male model throughout — but there's no visible segmentation signal. H&M's list skews female. Blasting this to the full subscriber base means most recipients see an irrelevant men's sportswear send on a day H&M already asked for their attention once. The email works for its narrow target; it wastes sends on everyone else.

- **Collab lockup leads immediately**: Lotto x H&M branding is the first thing below the nav, no guessing required.
- **Hero image earns the collab**: @triszyk styled outdoors in full look is editorial-quality and shows product in real context.
- **Product grid is complete**: Six-plus SKUs shown with names, prices ($30–$44.99), and "New Arrival" tags.
- **Dual CTA makes sense**: "SHOP NOW" and "FIND A STORE" side-by-side covers two buyer modes cleanly.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, proper spacing end to end.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer after yesterday's 25%-off close**: The discount withdrawal is jarring — no urgency, no hook beyond "look at this collab."
- **@triszyk means nothing to most H&M subscribers**: Leading the subject with an unknown handle is dead weight for anyone outside his following.
- **Male-skewed content to a female-dominant list**: No segmentation signal visible — looks like a full-list blast for a clearly gendered product.
- **Two editorial emails, same day**: The "Must-have pieces for summer layering" send also went out June 1. Back-to-back editorials compound fatigue.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic**: Doesn't reinforce the collab, doesn't create urgency. Wasted real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment to men's buyers first**: Highest-impact change. Any subscriber with men's purchase or browse history. The full-list blast for a men's-only collab is the root waste here.
- 2. **Add a reason to act today**: "First drop — limited styles" or "New arrival: shop before it sells out" gives urgency without a blanket discount. There is currently no reason to click now vs. next week.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA to be collab-specific**: "Shop LOTTO H&M" or "Get the collab" beats "SHOP NOW" — it primes the right buyer and reinforces the partnership.
- 4. **Earn the Lotto brand for subscribers who don't know it**: One hero-copy sentence — "The legendary Italian sports label meets H&M" — justifies the collab for newcomers.
- 5. **Stagger the dual June 1 sends by 24 hours**: Two editorial emails in one day after a heavy promo cycle is a cadence error; one of them should have shifted to June 2.
