# In focus: The Knitwear Edit
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is an editorial send, and it's cleaner than most of H&M's week. The hero is a model in a cream draped knit with a bold "KNITWEAR EDIT" headline, a short copy block and one SHOP NOW button. Three knitwear products follow, each with a price ($49.99) and a "New Arrival" tag. The visual story is cohesive and the eye lands on the hero first.
- The offer is the weak point. The only promo is a thin banner at the very top ("THE HALLOWEEN EDIT: UP TO 50% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE & ONLINE*"). It's tiny, it names the wrong campaign (Halloween, not knitwear), and it leaves the buyer wondering whether the knits are discounted. The products show full prices with no sale treatment. The Fall Beauty block at the bottom splits the focus with one nail polish at $6.99, which is a small, odd payoff.
- Judgement: nice to look at, but it doesn't push anyone to act. Against the 10/10 sweater send ("Up to 40% off fall's best sweaters"), which pointed one offer at one category, this one trades urgency for mood. It lands in the mid-5s.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: a cream knit on a model, with a clear "KNITWEAR EDIT" headline and a one-line seasonal pitch.
- The three product tiles are consistent, with name, price and "New Arrival" under each.
- The layout is clean and has plenty of whitespace. The single SHOP NOW buttons are easy to read.
- The theme fits the season, since fall layering matches the week's fall campaign.
- The Shop By row (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) gives a useful exit for people who aren't into knits.

## What's weak

- The "Halloween Edit: up to 50% off" banner is tiny and unrelated to the knitwear content. The email has two messages and neither is clear.
- The knits sit at full price, with no member pricing and no deadline in the body.
- The product tiles don't look clickable. There are no per-product buttons and no sizing or colour detail.
- The Fall Beauty block, with a single $6.99 nail polish, dilutes the knitwear story.
- The legal footer is long and dense, and it carries the real Halloween offer terms, so the fine print is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one message. Either put the 50% off Halloween offer in the hero, or drop it and make this a pure "new knits" send. As built, the email is neither.
- 2. Add a visible price or discount treatment, such as "From $49.99" or a member-price flag, plus a deadline.
- 3. Link each product tile to its own page and add a small "Shop" label.
- 4. Cut Fall Beauty or move it to its own send.
- 5. Add social proof such as bestseller badges or ratings.

# Technical Audit: H&M, "In focus: The Knitwear Edit"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally conventional, with media-query responsive CSS and a JSON-LD block. The automated link probe returned 403 on all 12 material links, including Unsubscribe. This is probably bot-blocking on the ESP tracker `t19.email.hm.com` and not a real outage, but it can't be confirmed from this data. Alt text is missing on 14 images, and the header-based compliance checks were inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on all 12 probed links.** This covers "Shop now" (×4), "SHOP NOW" (×4), the `Shop now` links with zero-width characters (×2), the "H&M" logo link, and "Unsubscribe". Every URL is a redirect wrapper of the form `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h8d559ea2-…,5491d32c,<linkid>&did=1418842924&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6140555&p2=…&p3=20261015`.
  - Every link shares one host and one `id` prefix, and only the link ID suffix differs (`f8792e21`, `f8792e22`, `f8792e2f` and so on). A uniform 403 across all of them points to the tracker rejecting the non-browser probe (UA or IP reputation), not 12 independent broken links.
  - **Status: unverified.** Re-probe with a browser-like UA, or follow the redirect chain from a real client, before calling these broken.
- **Unverified links.** 61 links were skipped by the probe, so their state is unknown.
- **Zero-width characters in link text.** Two CTAs have link text `‌  ﻿Shop now`, which starts with ZWNJ and BOM characters (links `…f8792d56` and `…f8792d58`). This breaks exact-text matching and may be announced oddly by screen readers.
- **Opaque tracking.** The `p2` hash and `erid` (base64 of `3326930539`) are per-recipient identifiers carried in every URL. That is normal for ESP tracking, but it means the links can't be inspected for destination without following the redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 14 images**, per QA. Examples:
  - `…/wa10264c03/WA10264C03-6-3x2.jpg`

  - Several images appear in both `1400.1400` and `750.750` variants of the same asset, probably desktop/mobile swaps. Both copies lack alt text.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source or in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Inconclusive; check the raw message at the source or with a seed inbox. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `email.hm.com` can't be validated here.
- **In-body unsubscribe link present** (`…f8792e5d&…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`) but returned 403 on probe. Treat it as unverified, not broken, for the same reason as above.

- The physical postal address couldn't be assessed because the footer was truncated.

- The JSON-LD block has empty required fields: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"url":""`, `"image":""`, `"description":""`, and a stale `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`. It was likely left as an unpopulated template, so it adds nothing for Gmail promotion cards.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `t19.email.hm.com/r/` links with a browser-like UA and by following redirects. Confirm each CTA and Unsubscribe resolves to an `hm.com` landing page. Treat the 403s as probe artifacts until then.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from raw headers, bypassing the relay.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to all 14 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for purely decorative duplicates, and add alt text to the two linked tracker images.
4. Strip the ZWNJ/BOM characters from the `Shop now` link text.
5. Fix the invalid CSS: `line-height:auto` → `normal`/`inherit`, and `@media screen yahoo` → `@media screen and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)` or remove it.
6. Populate the JSON-LD fields or remove the block.
7. Improve the plain-text alternative so it has real copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
