- The creative undersells the collab. The hero is a street-style shot of a woman in a leather bomber and baggy jeans. It reads as generic H&M outerwear, not couture, and nothing in it says Elie Saab. - The copy gives a concrete date and time (Oct 29, 10 AM ET) and says it is limited-edition and in select stores and online.
- A single outlined "PREVIEW NOW" button is unambiguous and sits right under the copy.
- After the 10/16–10/18 threshold-discount sends, a collab announcement is a genuine change of beat.

## What's weak

- The hero shows a generic leather bomber and jeans, not anything recognisable as Elie Saab couture or the collection.
- There is no product, price point or sneak peek, so "PREVIEW NOW" promises a preview the email doesn't give.
- The weather-delay explanation is buried in a small centred paragraph and isn't owned in the subject or headline.

- 3. Move the delay explanation into a short, direct line: "Weather delayed our shipment. Same collection, new date: Oct 29."
- 4. Add a member early-access line, such as "Members shop first at 9 AM ET." This gives loyalty members a reason to engage.
