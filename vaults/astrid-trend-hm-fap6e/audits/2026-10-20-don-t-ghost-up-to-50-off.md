# Don't ghost up to 50% off...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a Halloween-themed send that buries its best asset. The "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT STYLES" headline is clear, but it sits under a moody, mostly dark hero of a black-and-white striped surface with small props. Nothing in the hero says clothing or shows a product. The offer reads as "select styles" with no hint of what's discounted.
- The rest of the email drifts. After the offer block there are Kids sections ("MAGICAL MUST-HAVES", "SWEET AS CANDY"), a home vignette ("SET THE SCENE"), and a member giveaway ("H&M MOVES NYC"). The four themes compete, and the CTAs are small outlined buttons ("BABY," "KIDS 2-8Y," "HOME"), not a single strong action. The sale is also in-store and online "through 10/31," so the urgency is weak. A day earlier H&M ran a threshold deal (15% off $100 or 20% off $125), so "up to 50% off select styles" looks like a bigger, vaguer promotion.
- Verdict: the headline is strong, but the execution is scattered and the offer is too vague. It's a mid-tier send, closer to a 4 than a 6.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT STYLES" headline is bold and easy to find in the render.
- The top banner ("ORDER BY 4PM ET FOR FREE SAME-DAY PICKUP") adds a concrete, time-bound service benefit.
- The Halloween theme matches the date, 11 days before 10/31.
- The member giveaway block gives loyalty members a reason to engage and a sign-in/join CTA.
- The kids product photography is clean, with large images that are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero image shows no product, so the eye doesn't land on anything you can buy.
- "Select styles" and "select" categories are vague, and the offer doesn't say which items are discounted.
- The CTAs are small, low-contrast outlined buttons with category labels ("BABY," "HOME"), not action copy.
- Kids, home, a giveaway and a sale all compete, and there's no single primary CTA.
- The very long fine-print block takes up a large share of the bottom of the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a product-forward hero, such as a styled kids' Halloween outfit, with "UP TO 50% OFF" as the overlay and a single filled "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- 2. Name the categories the discount covers, such as kids, home and women, with price examples like "Tees from $6."
- 3. Cut the email to 2–3 modules. Move the member giveaway to its own send or a smaller footer slot.
- 4. Make the CTA copy verb-led, for example "Shop kids Halloween" and "Shop home decor," in filled buttons.
- 5. Set an explicit deadline ("Ends 10/31") in the headline area.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Halloween markdowns: up to 50% off, ends 10/31`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kids, home and more. Shop online or in-store through 10/31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Every probed link returned HTTP 403, including the unsubscribe link. That pattern is most likely the tracking redirector (`t19.email.hm.com/r/`) blocking the automated probe, not a confirmed dead link. Beyond that, the source has missing image alt text, empty JSON-LD fields, no `lang` attribute, and an unverified compliance and authentication state.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures:** All 12 probed links failed with 403. They cover "Find a store" (x2), "Need it today? Order by 4pm ET…", "Shop now" (x2), the "H&M" logo link, two image links, "Baby", "KIDS 2‑8Y", "KIDS 9‑14Y" and "Unsubscribe". All use the same redirect pattern: `https://t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=h6d6b7d0a-…,5492cdd6,<linkid>&did=1418907094&rid=3326930539&erid=…&p1=DM6150736&p2=…&p3=20261020`.
- **Likely cause:** A uniform 403 across every link, including the logo, suggests bot filtering on the redirector. I can't confirm from this data that real recipients get 403s, so verify with a real client or a browser User-Agent.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 82 links were probed and 70 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- **Wrapped destinations:** Every link is a click-tracking redirect, so the final destinations couldn't be inspected.
- **Hidden characters in link text:** "Shop now" links contain zero-width characters (`‌ ﻿Shop now`). They are harmless visually but add noise to link text and analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check found 14 images with no alt text. The alt text falls back to the filename, for example `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg`. These come in 1400/750 pairs per product, which suggests separate desktop and mobile variants. Several are wrapped in links, for example the `f882ca13` and `f882ca14` links. Screen readers announce those links as filenames, or nothing at all.
- **Tracking pixel:** One image with a `t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=…` src, which looks like the open-tracking pixel, also lacks alt text. An empty `alt=""` would be correct here.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html style="border:0;margin:0;outline:0;padding:0">` has no `lang` attribute, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/…/fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://s1-cdn.hm.com/…/oces.css)` are not supported by many clients, such as Gmail and Outlook. Confirm the font stack has fallbacks. The truncated source doesn't show this.
- **Mobile toggling:** The `.is-hidden-mobile` and `.is-hidden-desktop` rules depend on `@media (max-width:699px)`. Clients that strip `<style>` blocks may show both variants, so duplicate image and content blocks could render twice.
- **Plain-text part:** 524 of 602 characters are URLs, which is over the 70% warning threshold. The plain-text version is almost entirely long tracking links.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated source. Recipient identifiers appear in the URLs (`rid`, `erid`, `p2`), but they are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found in the captured headers. The QA report notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, the email lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. The report attributes this to the relay.
- **In-body unsubscribe:** An "Unsubscribe" link exists (`…f882ca5b…&p4=3326930539&p5=9be06746…`) but returned 403 to the probe. This is probably the same bot filtering as the other links. Test it manually, because a failing unsubscribe link is a CAN-SPAM violation.
- **Physical address:** The truncated source doesn't show a postal address in the footer, so this can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** No `utm_*` parameters are visible on any link. Attribution runs through the redirector's own parameters (`p1=DM6150736`, `p3=20261020`). Whether UTMs are appended at redirect time is unknown.
- **Landing pages:** Because every probe returned 403, none could be checked against the "up to 50% off" subject line.
- **Empty JSON-LD:** The `application/ld+json` block has empty values: `"subjectLine":""`, `"discountCode":""`, `"description":""`, and `"image":""`/`"url":""` on the `PromotionCard`. It also has a stale `"availabilityStarts":"2018-12-31T18:59:59-05:00"`. Gmail promotion annotations will not show the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually verify the unsubscribe link and a sample of the CTAs from a real client or browser User-Agent to rule out real 403s.
2. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw message headers. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the original message, not the relay copy.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 14 flagged product images. Use `alt=""` for the tracking pixel and other decorative images.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Populate the JSON-LD fields (`subjectLine`, `description`, `discountCode`, `url`, `image`) with current values, or remove the block.
6. Strip zero-width characters from CTA link text.
7. Make the plain-text part carry readable text, not mostly tracking URLs.
8. Check whether UTMs are appended at redirect time, and test the landing pages.
