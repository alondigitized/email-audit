# One less gift to find
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is H&M's first gifting send of the season, and it carries no offer. After four days of threshold deals (15% off $100 or 20% off $125 on 10/16, 10/17 and 10/18), the "HOLIDAY GIFTS" email has no discount, no deadline and no member pricing. The subject, "One less gift to find," is a friendly hook, but the body doesn't deliver on it. It doesn't point to a specific gift that solves anything.
- The hero is a six-tile collage: a bag, loafer, heart purse, perfume, leather glove and tie. It is stylish and clearly says "gifts," and it feels more premium than the prior week's promo emails. The tiles aren't labeled or priced, and none of them appears in the product grid below. The grid shows a sweater, slippers and two pajama sets. Pajamas and slippers are an easy gift category, but the email never frames them as gifts. Four products is a thin sample for "everyone on your list."
- My judgment: a competent seasonal opener that works as brand warmup but is weak at driving clicks. It would do better as a gift guide with prices, or as a "gifts under $25" angle. The $19.99 slippers and shorts hint at that angle, but the email doesn't use it.

## What's working

- The six-tile hero collage reads as "gifts" immediately. The warm, high-contrast photography is cohesive.
- The "HOLIDAY GIFTS" headline is bold, and the one-line subcopy covers her, him, babies and kids.
- The WOMEN / MEN / KIDS buttons split the audience cleanly.
- The product tiles show real prices ($44.99, $19.99, $39.99, $19.99), and the "New Arrival" tag on the slippers adds a little freshness.
- The email moves away from the repetitive threshold-discount pattern of 10/16–10/18, so it feels like a new beat.

## What's weak

- There is no offer at all. The subject promises less work, but the email gives no savings, free shipping or deadline.
- The hero collage is not clickable-looking and has no labels or prices. It isn't clear whether the tiles link to products, and none of the hero items appears in the grid.
- The product grid is only four items, and none is framed as a gift. The sweater title is truncated ("RIB-KNIT BATWING-SLEE... SWEATER").
- The CTAs are plain outlined boxes, with WOMEN full-width and MEN/KIDS split. They are visually weak and generic, with no verb.
- There is a large blank gap below the product grid. The bottom half is mostly navigation: shop-by tiles, app links and legal text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to act now: a gift-by date, a "free shipping on gift orders" line, or a reminder that the 15%/20% threshold offer still applies.
- 2. Make the hero tiles shoppable with labels and prices (e.g., "Leather gloves $29.99"), or replace the collage with one hero and a "Gifts under $25 / $50" row.
- 3. Expand the grid to 6–8 products and frame them as gifts: "Cozy gifts for her," "Gifts for him."
- 4. Replace the generic outlined buttons with filled, specific CTAs such as "Shop gifts for her."
- 5. Remove the blank gap and trim the footer navigation so the content sits tighter.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
All 12 probed links, including Unsubscribe, returned HTTP 403 from the `t19.email.hm.com` click-tracking redirector. This is probably bot-blocking of the QA probe rather than a real outage, but it can't be confirmed from this data. There are also widespread missing `alt` attributes, empty JSON-LD fields, and no confirmable compliance headers (the relay likely dropped them).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Uniform 403s:** Every probed link failed with 403. That includes the CTAs ("Shop now" `...f87fdf19`, `...f87fdf1b`), the logo (`...f87fdf18`), the hero images (`...f87fdf75`, `...f87fdf76`), the Women/Men/Kids nav (`...f87fdf77`–`7c`) and Unsubscribe (`...f87fdfb9`).
- **Likely cause:** A 100% failure rate across one redirect domain points to bot or WAF rejection of the probe's user agent. Links that fail identically, including Unsubscribe, are more likely a probe artifact than 12 independent broken links. Re-test with a browser-like UA or a real client before treating these as outages.
- **Opaque destinations:** All links are `/r/?id=...` redirects, so final landing URLs are not visible in the HTML.
- **Coverage:** Only 12 of 77 links were probed (65 skipped), so the remaining links are unverified.
- **Zero-width characters:** The "Shop now" link text begins with invisible characters (`‌  ﻿`, which looks like ZWNJ plus a BOM). These are usually preheader or spacing hacks, but they pollute link text for screen readers and the QA parser.
- **Duplicate links:** Women/Men/Kids appear twice each (`f87fdf77`–`79` and `7a`–`7c`). These are probably desktop and mobile variants toggled by `.header-show-desktop-only` and `.header-show-mobile-only`, which means duplicated links for non-CSS clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 13 instances flagged:**
  - The tracked logo or link image (`t19.email.hm.com/r/?id=...,1`) is flagged twice.
  - The hero images `cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg` (16x9 gift-guide) and `cq5dam.web.750.750.jpeg` (2x3 gift-guide) are flagged.
  - Four product images are flagged at both `?imWidth=344` and `?imWidth=322`: `692b8fc6…`, `ca2d884f…`, `2107001f…` and `78197e66…`.
- **Why it matters:** Several of these images are inside links, so the screen-reader name falls back to the filename or tracking URL (the QA output shows link text `[image: cq5dam.web.1400.1400.jpeg]`). If images are blocked, the hero and product links are empty.
- **Mobile/desktop duplication:** The 344px and 322px variants of each product suggest duplicated markup for mobile and desktop. This depends on `display:none` and `mso-hide`, which not every client honors, and could cause double rendering.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fabric.hmgroup.com/...)` and `oces.css` in `<style>` are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. This is a graceful fallback, not a defect, provided fallback font stacks exist (not visible in the truncated source).
