# The holidays, your way
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "The holidays, your way," but the email is a fashion editorial for dressy occasion wear. The hero shows a model in a white halter top and black trousers under "THE ART OF DRESSING UP," with copy about jacquard, tailoring and tassel details. The only offer is a thin black banner at the very top: "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY DEALS: 30% OFF SELECT STYLES IN-STORE*". It is red text on black, so it is hard to read, and it applies in stores only. The hero, body copy and CTAs never mention it.
- The visuals are polished and the hierarchy is clean. There is one hero, one headline and one button, then two product tiles and a category grid. But the email gives the shopper no deadline and no online deal. The "30% off" applies only to in-store hangtag styles. The items shown are tagged "New Arrival" at $49.99, so they probably aren't discounted at all. A subscriber who saw "25% off flash sale starts now" yesterday would find this a step down. Judgement: good looking, but it does little commercially and the subject doesn't match what's inside.

## What's working

- The hero is strong, with a clear "THE ART OF DRESSING UP" headline and a single "SHOP NOW" button right below.
- The visual hierarchy is clean. The hero, two product tiles and the category grid read in a sensible order.
- Products show price and "New Arrival" tags ($49.99 jacket, $49.99 satin dress), which gives a concrete price anchor.
- The "SHOP BY" grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Beauty) offers an easy path for people who don't want occasion wear.
- The season fits: party dressing in early November. There are no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- The only offer is in a red-on-black banner with poor contrast, and it is in-store only. Online shoppers get nothing.
- The subject says "your way" and implies gifting or holiday variety. The body is occasion wear for one aesthetic.
- Both "SHOP NOW" links in the header and the hero are generic, with no product or category named.
- There is no deadline in the visible copy. The dates only appear in the fine print at the bottom.
- The long legal block, the asterisked offer and a missing "View in browser" link up top add friction. The "30% off select styles" claim is buried and conditional, which makes the offer feel less honest.
- Nothing in the render signals a loyalty benefit or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero, in large type. For example, "30% off select styles, in stores through Nov 7." If an online equivalent exists, lead with that. Drop the red-on-black banner.
- 2. Match the subject to the content, either an occasion-wear angle or a real offer.
- 3. Replace the two generic "SHOP NOW" buttons with specific ones, such as "Shop party dresses" and "Shop jackets."
- 4. Add a visible date, for example "Ends Nov 7," next to the offer.
- 5. Cut the fine print down to a short line with a link to full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Party dresses from $49.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off in stores through Nov 7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jacquard, satin and tassel details for every holiday invite.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday deals, in store only. See what's marked down.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

## 7. Recommendations

7. Shorten the plain-text part or add more descriptive text around the long tracking URLs.
