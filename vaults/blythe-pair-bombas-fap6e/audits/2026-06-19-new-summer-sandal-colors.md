# NEW Summer Sandal Colors
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's second sandal-focused email in eight days — the Sunrise Flip Flop launched June 12, and now they're back with two new colorways for the Summer Cush Sandal. The structure is nearly identical to the June 9 compression colorway drop (which scored 5/10): hero image, color-specific CTAs, feature icons, cross-sell shelf, done. What saves this from feeling like a cut-paste job is the "WHICH ONE ARE YOU GRABBIN'?" section at the bottom, which turns a passive announcement into a light choice mechanic, and the "More Summer Cush" shelf, which opens up the broader footwear catalog for subscribers who aren't sandal buyers yet.
- The email does its job as a launch announcement. You know what's new (Dawn Light, Fog Harbor), you know what it is (Summer Cush Sandal), and the feature icon row — Lightweight Tick Sole, Waterproof + Easy-to-Clean, Cushy + Supportive — does real work for a category (sandals) that Bombas is still introducing to a sock-first audience.
- The core miss is the same as the June 9 compression drop: no price, no urgency, no reason to click today versus never. A colorway reveal with no offer attached is a nice email that gets filed away by lunch. Good execution of an inherently low-conversion email type.

## What's working

- **Color-specific CTAs from the first screen**: "Shop Dawn Light" and "Shop Fog Harbor" immediately frame the decision as a color choice, not a catalog browse — meaningfully reduces friction.
- **Feature icon row hits at the right moment**: The three icons appear directly under the product shot, answering "why would I buy a Bombas sandal?" at exactly the right beat.
- **"WHICH ONE ARE YOU GRABBIN'?"**: The blob-shaped CTAs with casual copy add personality and re-anchor the color choice at the bottom of the email without feeling repetitive.
- **"More Summer Cush" cross-sell shelf**: Flip Flops, Slides, and Clogs as secondary exits is smart — it widens the funnel for subscribers who aren't drawn to this specific colorway.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, no empty fields. Footer is complete.

## What's weak

- **No price, no offer, no urgency**: "New colors are here" gives subscribers zero reason to act before the email is archived. The June 9 compression colorway drop had the exact same weakness.
- **"Summer cush" assumes familiarity**: Opening body copy with the product nickname without spelling out "Summer Cush Sandal" loses anyone who doesn't have the SKU memorized.
- **Sandal fatigue is beginning**: Two sandal/footwear emails in one week (June 12 + June 19) starts to feel like category saturation for a brand whose credibility is built on socks.
- **Zero social proof**: A single "4.8 stars, 1,200+ reviews" line near the feature icons would do real conversion work for a category that Bombas is still establishing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give subscribers a reason to click today**: Even "Starting at $X" in the hero, or "Free shipping on your first sandal pair," turns an FYI into an event. The June 12 Sunrise Flip Flop email (8/10) worked better because it felt like a launch, not an update.
- 2. **Spell out the full product name on first mention**: "Summer cush just got two new colors" → "The Summer Cush Sandal just got two new colors."
- 3. **Add price or a hook to the hero subhead**: Replace the current layout with "Meet Dawn Light + Fog Harbor — $XX, waterproof and built for summer."
- 4. **Insert social proof next to the feature icons**: One line — "4.8 stars · 1,200+ reviews" — would close the trust gap for a product the audience is still learning.
