# 25% Off All Kids' Gifts
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the kids' cut of the gifting send Bombas ran earlier today ("25% Off Cozy Gifts for Your Whole List"). The hero reads "25% OFF GIFTS FOR KIDS" in heavy black type over three smiling kids in festive socks, with a "Plus Everything Else" burst. It is clear and on-season, and the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The body is a well-organized kid-gifting catalog: Cutesy Gifts gift boxes, Gripper Socks, a Sesame Street collab and Gripper Slippers. Each block has a banner and a product row, so it scans easily. The weak spot is the three buttons under the hero: "Shop Women, Shop Men, Shop Kids" on a kids-gifting email. Only one of them matches the hero, and it dilutes the focus.
- This is the fifth "25% off" Bombas send since 11/21, and the second gifting angle in one day. The creative is strong, but the cadence is exhausting. Judgement: well-executed and relevant to a parent, but it adds little to the 11/23 "Cozy Gifts" send.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF GIFTS FOR KIDS" hero is bold, specific and visible immediately.
- Real kids modelling the socks make the gifting angle feel warm and believable.
- Banner-plus-product-row modules (Cutesy Gifts, Gripper Socks, Gripper Slippers) give a clear, scannable rhythm.
- The Sesame Street collab block has a distinct yellow "Shop the Collab" button, which is the only strong, specific CTA in the body.
- The "100% Happiness Guaranteed" band and the "Stuff Those Stockings" closer reinforce trust and the seasonal tone.

## What's weak

- The hero buttons are Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids, which undercuts a kids-only message.
- The product rows have tiny, low-contrast captions and no prices or sale prices. Nothing shows what 25% off actually costs.
- Most banners (Cutesy Gifts, Gripper Socks, Gripper Slippers) have no visible button, so the click path is unclear.
- There are no reviews, ratings or star social proof anywhere in the render.
- There is no deadline. "The Black Friday Sale" badge gives no end date, so urgency is weak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero buttons match the message: a single "Shop Kids' Gifts" button, or "Shop Toddler / Shop Youth / Shop Baby."
- 2. Add sale prices to the product captions, e.g. "$30 → $22.50," and make the product images and captions clearly clickable.
- 3. Add a visible CTA to every banner, such as "Shop Gift Boxes" or "Shop Grippers."
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Monday 11:59pm PT") and a ratings line on the hero products.
- 5. Space out the sends. Fold this into the earlier gifting email, or segment it to parents instead of repeating the offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a well-structured, table-based template with dark-mode meta tags, an `role="article"` wrapper and a preheader. It scored a 38% pass rate in QA because of one hard link failure (400), 11 product images with missing alt text, and a duplicated `xmlns` attribute. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they don't confirm a defect in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure:** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link returned **HTTP 400**.
  - URL: `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/J6SzTP6Q81JinaRkl62WxA~~/AAQRxRA~/UVwYmtxff…`
  - This is the only hard failure. A 400 on an SMS-signup link could be the destination rejecting the probe or a malformed or expired redirect target. Re-test it from a real client before calling it broken.
- **Not confirmed broken:** these 429s are rate limiting on `clicks.bombas.com`, not dead links.
  - "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "BOMBAS" (logo), "25% OFF GIFTS FOR KIDS. Plus Everything Else", "The Black Friday Sale" and "The Cutest Gifts…"
  - The probe sent about a dozen requests in a burst, which likely triggered the limit.
- **Unverified:** 4 links were not probed because of the 70s time budget (`…/2yWWiB44ESw8YMZW`, `…/LM2jL7_NHu6mAMGH`, `…/2yWWiB44ESw8YMZW`, `…/2qXzUw0_QPGsR1-_`). 40 links in total were skipped. These are neither passes nor failures.
- **Tracking structure:** every link is wrapped in a `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…~~/AAQRxRA~/…` redirect, and the open pixel is at `clicks.bombas.com/q/xX9yesFJp5…`. This is consistent, with no mixed or unwrapped tracking domains seen in the QA data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 product images** (all `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`). Examples:
  - `11648-musicalfriendsmix-youth-unisex-layflat-1-tra…`
  - `11649-winterfriendsmix-toddler…`

  - `11583-alpine_red_mix-baby…`

  - `11279-purple_mist-toddler…`
  - `11275-maplered-youth…`
  - `9691-blushclay-baby…`
  With images blocked or a screen reader in use, these show as nothing, or as the filename. If they are inside links, the links have no accessible name.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `clicks.bombas.com/q/xX9yesFJp5…` has no alt. This is the open pixel and is expected, but `alt=""` is preferable.
- **Duplicate attribute on `<html>`:** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice. This is invalid markup, and strict parsers may reject it.
- **Minor CSS redundancy:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice in the `max-width:600px` block, with conflicting values (`max-width:50%` vs none). This is low impact.
- **Not an issue:** `lang`/`xml:lang`/`dir` are set, `color-scheme` meta is present, the viewport meta is present, and Outlook `mso` conditionals are in place.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No confirmed violation.** The QA tool itself notes that the headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay.
  - `List-Unsubscribe` not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The header checks should be re-run against raw `.eml` source from a direct-delivery inbox. If the headers are absent there too, this becomes a real issue for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot be verified.** All destinations sit behind encoded `clicks.bombas.com` redirects, and the probe recorded only the 400/429 responses, not the final URLs. UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) are therefore unconfirmed.
- Landing-page alignment is also unconfirmed. The 429s blocked any check that the "25% OFF GIFTS FOR KIDS" and "Shop Kids" links reach a kids' gifts collection.
- The `DiscountOffer` microdata window is `2026-11-21` to `2026-12-02` (23:59 -05:00), which is valid as of 2026-11-23.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link (400)** from a real browser or mobile client and fix the redirect or SMS-signup endpoint if it fails.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 product images** (e.g. `alt="Bombas Musical Friends kids' sock mix"`). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. **Remove the duplicate `xmlns`** on `<html>` and de-duplicate the `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` rules.
4. **Re-run the compliance checks on raw source** to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, rather than relying on the relay's view.
5. **Make link probing less bursty.** Throttle the requests or follow redirects with backoff, so the 429s and the 4 unprobed links resolve to a real final URL and UTM check.
6. **Optional:** shorten the plain-text part. 10,711 of 12,709 chars (84%) are tracking URLs, which can look spammy to text-only filters.
