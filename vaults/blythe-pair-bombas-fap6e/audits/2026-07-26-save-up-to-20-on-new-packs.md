# Save Up to 20% On New Packs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's fourth product-launch dressed as a "sale" email in the recent stretch, and it's the same trick as always: a headline discount ("up to 20% off") slapped onto what is functionally a four-category new-arrivals roundup — Everyday Ankles, Vintage Stripes, UltraLite Running, plus a generic "more new packs" catch-all. The hero executes cleanly (bold type over a warm lifestyle sock shot, a "Newness Alert" badge, two clear CTAs), but the "up to 20%" framing never gets specific again after the top banner — no product in the four separate grids below shows a price, a strikethrough, or a discount tag.
- Compared to 2026-07-18's "Stock Up with 20% Off" (8/10), which led with one unambiguous discount and one job to do, this email dilutes that same offer across four unrelated product stories and never repeats or reinforces it visually. It reads more like the 07-13 Slouchy Rib launch (7/10) with a discount headline bolted on for open-rate lift.
- Judgement: solid production values, zero render issues, but this is an offer used as bait to get you into a browse-everything email rather than a genuine discount push. Middle-of-the-pack send.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous and above-the-fold: bold "NEW & UP TO 20% OFF PACKS" with a "Newness Alert" badge lands the eye immediately.
- Clean, consistent visual system across all four product sections — same color-blocked headline treatment, same two-button CTA pattern, easy to scan.
- The UltraLite Running section breaks pattern with neon yellow-green CTAs against a stone-colored hero photo, which pops and signals a genuinely distinct sub-line (Bombas Sport).
- No render bugs — all product thumbnails load, text doesn't overlap, footer trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed, B Corp) render fully.
- Gender-segmented CTAs (Shop Women / Shop Men) appear consistently, reducing friction for readers who know which they want.

## What's weak

- The "up to 20% off" is stated twice at the very top and then never appears again — no per-product pricing, no strikethrough, no badge on any of the eight product tiles below. A reader has no idea which pack actually hits 20%.
- Four distinct product stories (Everyday Ankles, Vintage Stripes, UltraLite Running, "more new packs") crammed into one email dilutes focus — this is a browse email wearing a sale-email subject line.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — "just-released" and "up to 20%" don't expire, so there's no reason to act today versus next week.
- No pricing shown at all, discounted or otherwise — a serious click-through blocker for anyone price-sensitive.
- Kids category gets one small text link at the very bottom ("MORE NEW PACKS HERE") after four full product sections for Women/Men — feels like an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or "$X off" callout on at least the hero product and the top grid — "up to 20%" with zero visible prices anywhere is the single biggest trust/click gap in this email.
- 2. Cut to two sections max (lead category + one more) instead of four — Everyday Ankles, Vintage Stripes, UltraLite Running, and "more packs" is too much ground for one send; save two for the next email.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") to convert the vague "up to 20%" into something that motivates same-day action.
- 4. Give Kids equal visual weight to Women/Men somewhere in the body, not just a footer text link.
- 5. Reuse the neon-yellow CTA treatment from the Sport section more sparingly elsewhere — right now it's the most eye-catching CTA in the email, on the section that isn't the lead discount offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% Off New Sock Packs — Today Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just Dropped: New Packs, Up to 20% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh colorways in Ankles, Vintage Stripes & UltraLite Running.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New pairs just landed — shop packs and save.`
