## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or deadline anywhere. Nothing tells the reader why to buy today, especially after the 9/27 20%-off packs email.

- The email is long for a single message: three sections and two grids all say "chunky ragg."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Structurally sound email (role/lang attributes, dark-mode meta, preheader, responsive CSS), but one probed CTA returned a 400 and a few markup and accessibility defects are confirmed in the source. Authentication and unsubscribe-header status could not be verified because of the relay, and most links were not fully probed (38% QA pass rate).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure:** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link returned **HTTP 400**. It is a `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/ovt63ERy9bEk...` tracked link. The QA output shows an empty resolved target, so the tracker may be redirecting to an SMS or non-HTTP destination. A 400 to an HTTP probe doesn't prove a user-facing break, but this should be tested manually.
- **Not failures:** 6 links ("Shop Women", "Shop Men", "BOMBAS") returned **429** from `clicks.bombas.com`. That is the tracker rate-limiting the probe, so their final destinations are unverified.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links hit the 70s time budget (`.../eoyS8a1hZ9Nm...`, `.../DPZSyPbPqOLg...`, `.../yFD0WKEGvgLn...`, `.../3AQzekJjykpL...`). 23 links were skipped by design. Link coverage is incomplete.
- Every link is wrapped in a long click-tracking redirect (~700+ chars). This inflates the plain text (see §5).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on 5 images:
  - 4 product images from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/...` (`11430-navy_red_mix...`, `11429-navy_ivory_mix...`, `10746-fawn...`, `10746-midnight_navy...`). Since these are linked product images, they will be blank or unlabeled when images are off and for screen readers.
  - The `clicks.bombas.com/q/...` image is the open-tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute** on `<html>`: `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice. This is invalid markup, although clients tolerate it.
- **Malformed MSO block:** in `<o:officedocumentsettings>`, `<o:allowpng>` is never closed or self-closed (standard form is `<o:AllowPNG/>`). Outlook's XML parsing of the DPI settings may be affected.
- **Small mobile text:** `.font13` drops to `font-size:10px; line-height:10px` at ≤480px and `9px/9px` at ≤470px. `.font13Pad` is `9px`. These are below common legibility minimums (~11–14px) and iOS may not enlarge them, since `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%` is set.
- Minor: `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are declared twice in the same media block, which is redundant but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible portion of the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing. QA attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers. **These are unverified, not confirmed defects.** Check the raw message at the sending domain (`hello.bombas.com`) for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and one-click unsubscribe.
- Plain-text part is **86% URLs (7792/9029 chars)**, above the 70% threshold. Cause is the tracking-wrapped links. This is a modest spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reported no failure for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All hrefs are opaque `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...` redirects, and 429s and time limits prevented resolving final destinations. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link. If it's an SMS/opt-in flow, confirm it resolves to a valid page or `sms:` handler on mobile.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` on `<html>` and fix the MSO block to `<o:AllowPNG/>` and `<o:PixelsPerInch>96</o:PixelsPerInch>`.
4. Raise the `.font13` and `.font13Pad` mobile minimums to at least 11–12px.
5. Re-run the link probe with throttling and backoff to clear the 429s, and resolve the 4 unprobed links and the redirect chains to verify UTMs.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message headers rather than the relay copy.
7. Consider a shorter or branded click-tracking domain to reduce URL bloat in the plain-text part.
