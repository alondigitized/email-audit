# Brand New Suede Slippers
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led launch for the new Suede Scuffs, with no offer. The hero is the strongest part. Four suede slippers in slate, cream, espresso and camel sit on a warm wood backdrop. The "NEW SUEDE SCUFFS STYLE" headline is big, and the "Shop Women" and "Shop Men" buttons sit right on the image. A recipient knows what's being sold within a second.
- The rest of the email is a catalog. A lifestyle shot lists three benefits (temp-regulating shearling, multi-layered cushioning, soft premium suede). Two product cards follow, then a second "Sunday Slipper" block. It fits Bombas's recent run of drops with no offer, like the Oct 4 "All New Sunday Slipper Colors" and the Oct 9 "NEW Luxe Merino Cashmere Colors". Nothing here gives a reason to act today, and the Sunday Slipper block repeats what was sent six days ago.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but a launch announcement rather than a conversion driver. No price, no urgency, and no reviews leave the click to curiosity alone. I'd put it at a solid 6.

## What's working

- The hero is immediate. The slippers are large and well lit, and the "NEW SUEDE SCUFFS" headline is bold.
- The Shop Women and Shop Men buttons are dark and high-contrast, and they sit inside the hero.
- The benefits overlay (temp-regulating, multi-layered cushioning, soft suede) gives a quick product rationale.
- The "Plush. Premium. Put-together." copy is on-voice and short.
- The layout is clean and ends in a clear women's/men's split.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or shipping incentive. Nothing makes today better than next week.
- The product cards for the Suede Scuffs and Sunday Slippers show no price, rating or reviews.
- The Sunday Slipper block recycles the Oct 4 send, which dilutes the "brand new" message.
- The product cards and the bottom "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN" links are small. The footer links are blue underlined text, which looks off next to the hero buttons.
- The "SLIDE 'EM ON" closer is weak, and the blue links clash with the brand look.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or a reason to buy in the hero, such as "From $XX" or a free-shipping threshold. A launch with no price gives low-intent readers nothing to act on.
- 2. Show ratings or a short quote on the product cards. Slippers are a comfort-and-fit purchase.
- 3. Cut the Sunday Slipper block or label it as a restock with a specific color. Otherwise it reads as a repeat of Oct 4.
- 4. Make the bottom CTAs real buttons that match the hero buttons, with specific copy like "Shop Women's Scuffs".
- 5. Name the gifting angle or the season, since it's October, to add some urgency.

## Technical Audit

The one confirmed link failure is the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" tracked link, which returns HTTP 400. Several product images lack alt text. Compliance headers could not be verified, because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure (HTTP 400):** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link, `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/nSx5lTndLZhl_n1rWfCxFQ~~/AAQRxRA~/97ACjlBL…`.
  - The QA check recorded its link text as empty, so the anchor may have no text node, such as an image-only or sprite link.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirector usually means the encoded destination payload is malformed or truncated. It could also be a bot-filter rejection.
  - This is the only link that failed outright, so treat it as a real defect and retest it manually.
- **Inconclusive (HTTP 429):** the probe was rate limited on 7 links. The URLs are `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…~~/AAQRxRA~/…` for "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "BOMBAS", "So Fluffing Plush", "NEW SUEDE SCUFFS STYLE", the "Temp-regulating shearling…" copy link, and "SHOP WOMEN". These are not confirmed broken. Rate limiting is a probe artifact from hitting the same redirector host repeatedly.
- **Unprobed:** 20 links were skipped by design and 4 more were cut off by the 70s budget. The unprobed URLs begin `…/TWBKewWN…`, `…/D110Ms30…`, `…/zZDssHue…` and `…/6TRYXdav…`. Coverage is incomplete, so the **38% pass rate** reflects the skipped and rate-limited links rather than 62% broken links.
- **Click-tracking structure:** all links go through the `clicks.bombas.com` wrapper (the `~~/AAQRxRA~` pattern). This is consistent, and the host is a first-party branded domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images** (`assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/…`):
  - `11259-camel-adult-Female-layflat-1-transparent.png`
  - `11258-walnut-adult-Male-layflat-1-transparent.png`

  - `11289-darkespresso-adult-Male-layflat-1-transparent.png`

  With images blocked, or for screen readers, these will be announced by filename or skipped. If the images sit inside product links, the links become unlabeled.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/nv8hv6Hpo7jY…` is the open-tracking pixel. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is cleaner.

- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice in the 600px media query, with conflicting `max-width` values (`50%` and then `width` only). The later rule wins, but it's maintenance noise.
- **Passing items:**

  - The `role="article"` wrapper has an `aria-label`.
  - Viewport and `color-scheme` meta tags are present.
  - Outlook (MSO) fallbacks are present.

## 7. Recommendations
