# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas being Bombas — clean, athletic, and leaning hard into their Sport line right now. The four-tile category grid loads fast and puts Socks right in front of me, which I appreciate. But as a loyal returning customer who already owns half their catalog, the "20% Off First Order" banner at the top is speaking directly past me to someone who has never opened this app.

## What's working

- **The Socks tile placement.** Top right, immediate, no digging. One tap from homepage to my category — that's the whole game for me.
- **BOMBAS SPORT hero.** Women mid-sprint, socks visible on every pair of legs in the frame. As someone who thinks about what's on their feet during a run, this registers.
- **Free Standard Shipping on Orders $75+.** Visible immediately. When I'm stocking up on 8–10 pairs for holiday gifting, $75 is a lay-up threshold.
- **Category tile layout.** Four clean tiles (Bralettes & Underwear, Socks, Slippers & Shoes, T-Shirts) tell me instantly the brand is more than socks now — I'm always mildly curious about the slippers.
- **The teal sock imagery in the Socks tile.** That specific folded-socks shot is satisfying in the way only sock people understand. I noticed it immediately.

## What's weak

- **"20% Off First Order with Code COMFORT20."** I'm a repeat customer and this banner is shouting at someone who isn't me. It reads as a new-customer acquisition play pinned at the top of every session, including mine.
- **Zero donation/giving messaging visible above fold.** The bee logo is there, but no "every purchase funds a donation pair" callout — this is core to why I evangelize Bombas to my family, and it's invisible on arrival.
- **No loyalty hook.** I'm logged out and there's no "Earn rewards" or "Bee Better member" prompt. If I were on the fence, there's nothing pulling me toward signing in.
- **Cookie consent modal covering the bottom of the Sport hero.** The modal lands right over the lower portion of the runners — it's legal necessity but it's also the worst possible place for it to land on a sport imagery shot.
- **No urgency.** Nothing I see says "this run of socks is limited" or "sale ends Sunday." For someone who bulks up at end-of-year, a seasonal window cue would move me to cart faster.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "20% Off First Order" for a returning-customer offer.** Session-detect logged-out state and serve "Unlock Member Pricing — Log In" or surface a loyalty tier teaser. The acquisition offer is wasted on me and mildly irritating.
- **Add one line of giving copy to the top banner rotation.** Even "Every purchase = a donated pair" as a rotating strip would remind me — and any new visitor — why I started buying here in the first place.
- **Make the Socks tile a proper CTA button.** Keep the image, but overlay a "Shop Socks →" pill button. Right now it relies on me knowing the tile is tappable.
- **Move cookie consent to a bottom sheet that doesn't cover hero content.** Slide it up from the bottom edge — keeps it compliant, stops it eating the visual centerpiece.
