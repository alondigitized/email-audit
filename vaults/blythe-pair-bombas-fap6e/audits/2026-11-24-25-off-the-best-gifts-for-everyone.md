# 🎁 25% Off the Best Gifts for Everyone 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built gifting send, and it is the third gifting beat from Bombas in about 24 hours. The hero reads "25% OFF GIFTS FOR EVERYONE" in big cream type over fuzzy slipper-socks on a holiday green background. Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit right under it. The offer lands first and the paths are obvious.
- The body splits into three color-blocked tiles: Gifts for Her, Him and Kids. Each has its own button and a short line of copy. A three-product row follows. The structure is tidy, but it duplicates the 11/23 "25% Off Cozy Gifts for Your Whole List" and "25% Off All Kids' Gifts" sends almost exactly. A subscriber who got both would see the same idea a third time.
- Verdict: solid execution, low novelty. The cadence is the main problem, because there is no new reason to click that wasn't in yesterday's emails.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "25% OFF" in oversized type, with a seasonal "Sleigh Your Gift List" kicker.
- Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit directly under the hero, so there's no scrolling to act.
- The three gift tiles use distinct colors and lifestyle photos, and each has its own button. They scan quickly.
- The copy has personality: "Even if he said he doesn't need anything."
- The product row names specific items, such as the Women's Gripper Slipper and the Fair Isle ornament gift boxes.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 11/23 gifting sends and the "25% off" message that has run since 11/21. Nothing is new.
- Each audience gets a button, but none gets a specific product, price or hero item. The tile buttons all read "Shop [Audience]", which is generic.
- The product row has no prices, no ratings and no individual CTAs. The images are small and plain next to the tiles above.
- The offer details are small and buried at the bottom ("See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included"). The exclusion shows up late.
- The tiles and hero repeat the same three audience CTAs, so there are about nine buttons for one decision.
- There is no urgency or deadline anywhere in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and a reason to act now, such as "Ends Monday" or "Ships by Dec 24", in the hero. Without it, a third identical gifting email gives no reason to click today.
- 2. Pick one hero product, such as the slipper with its price and a "Shop the Gripper Slipper" button, instead of three generic audience buttons.
- 3. Cut the redundancy. Drop the hero button row or the tile buttons, and keep one set.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the product row, and link each item individually.
- 5. Move the "gift cards not included" exclusion up beside the offer.
- 6. Vary the creative from 11/23 so the three gifting sends don't look identical.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 images.**
  - Three are product images on `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`: `12038-cocoacloudmix-adult-female-layflat-2`, `11265-berrymauve-adult-Female-layflat-3` and `11645-heritagegreenmix-adult-male-layflat-2`.

- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`.** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice in the opening tag. Duplicate attributes are invalid XHTML and strict parsers may reject them. Browsers and mail clients generally tolerate it.
- **Plain text is mostly URLs.** 7,639 of 10,447 characters (73%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. That is a spam-filter signal and leaves little readable text.
- **Positives:** `role="article"`, `aria-label`, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, a `prefers-color-scheme` block, an MSO conditional and a hidden preheader are all present.
