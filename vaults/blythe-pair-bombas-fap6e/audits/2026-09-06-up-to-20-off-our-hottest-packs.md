# Up to 20% Off Our Hottest Packs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas' first real discount send in the run — "Up to 20% Off Packs" — and it's structured as a five-category roundup (Everyday Essentials, Support & Relief, Support/Compression, Go the Distance, Polished Pairs) all wrapped under one pack-focused masthead. It's essentially the same "collection rounder" format as 2026-09-05's "Fall Favorites" and 2026-08-30's "New Season" send, just with an actual discount attached this time — which is the one thing those prior sends lacked.
- The hero is clean and the offer is legible at a glance (bold "UP TO 20% OFF PACKS" over a multi-leg sock lineup), but "up to 20%" is vague — no indication which packs hit the ceiling versus the floor. Below the fold it's the same five-banner, ten-product-thumbnail wall the brand keeps repeating, and by this point in the cadence (6th email in 8 days) that structure is fatigue, not novelty.
- Solid, on-brand, no render bugs — but this is a mid-pack email elevated slightly by having a real percentage-off hook.

## What's working

- Clear discount hook in the hero ("UP TO 20% OFF PACKS") with dual Shop Women/Shop Men CTAs immediately visible.
- Consistent, readable section structure — each category banner pairs a lifestyle photo with two product thumbnails and its own CTA pair.
- "Trending Now" kicker plus multi-pair sock photo gives a believable seasonal/social-proof signal.
- Zero layout or image-rendering issues — grid, spacing, and typography are all clean throughout.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforce the brand's values at the footer.

## What's weak

- "Up to 20%" with no per-category or per-product discount breakdown — the reader can't tell if compression packs or dress packs are the ones actually worth clicking.
- No urgency or end date anywhere in the hero or body — the offer could be evergreen, which undercuts click urgency.
- Five nearly-identical category banners in a row is the same long-scroll format as the last two roundups; nothing differentiates this send's layout from "Fall Favorites" or "New Season" besides the discount headline.
- No pricing shown on any product thumbnail — a shopper has to click through just to see what 20% actually translates to in dollars.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual price/percentage next to at least the hero pack (e.g., "Run Ankle 6-Pack — now $XX") so the discount isn't abstract.
- 2. Add a deadline ("Ends Sunday") to the hero — right now nothing signals why to act today versus next week.
- 3. Cut the roundup to 2-3 categories max, or lead with the categories that actually carry the 20% discount and demote the rest — five full banners is too much scroll for one offer.
- 4. Badge each thumbnail with its own %-off so shoppers can scan for the best deal instead of guessing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 20% Off Sock Packs — Ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Packs, Now 20% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock the drawer: compression, dress, and everyday packs all discounted.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mix, match, and save — pack deals live now.`

# Technical Email Audit — Bombas "Up to 20% Off Our Hottest Packs"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MSO/VML-conditional HTML email with responsive breakpoints; automated QA shows a 50% pass rate driven mainly by missing alt text and unconfirmed authentication/unsubscribe headers, not by broken markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 6 of 12 probed material links (all "Shop Women"/"Shop Men" CTAs routed through `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...`) returned HTTP 429 during probing — rate-limited, not confirmed broken, but click-through cannot be verified from this check.
- 5 links were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget (e.g. `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/npv7VAtua2qDasx8sXaIzQ~~/...`), status unknown.
- 36 of 48 total links were deprioritized/skipped (footer/utility/social) per the probe's click-likelihood ranking — expected behavior, not a defect.
- All observed CTA links use ESP click-tracking redirects (`clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...`) consistent with a standard tracking wrapper.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images are missing `alt` text, including product imagery (e.g. `12412-cherry_berry_mix-adult-female-ankle-layflat-...`, `11396-forest_navy_mix-adult-male-ankle-layflat-FA2...`) and a tracking pixel (`clicks.bombas.com/q/oZmB6pPMKBy_mReuyWkEQA~~/...`). Screen readers will announce filenames or skip these entirely.
- Preheader/hidden-text block uses valid `display:none`/`mso-hide` pattern with zero-width joiner padding — correctly hidden from rendered view, no issue.
- Dark-mode support present via `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)` and `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` meta tags — no issues found.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and VML namespace declarations present — no issues found.
- Duplicate `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` attribute on the `<html>` tag (declared twice) — harmless but technically invalid markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags or personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence, but cannot be confirmed present.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable via this relay.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per the probe's own methodology note ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), though its target URL/status wasn't itemized in the findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA links route through the `clicks.bombas.com` click-tracking domain rather than exposing final landing-page URLs directly, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from this source without following the redirect chain.
- No raw `bombas.com/...?utm_...` destination URLs are present in the visible HTML to audit directly.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 9 flagged images, particularly the 8 product shots (currently using internal SKU-style filenames as fallback text).
2. Re-run link probing outside of rate-limit conditions to confirm the 6 throttled (429) CTA links and the 5 unprobed links actually resolve.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers are present in the raw SMTP headers directly from the sending ESP (not just via the AgentMail relay) to validate one-click unsubscribe compliance.
4. Confirm `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) at the receiving MTA to validate deliverability posture; relay may be stripping this header.
5. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute on the `<html>` tag.
