# Back In Stock: The Best Golf Socks
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-category restock send, and the hero does its job. "CUSHIONED FOR THE COURSE" sits over a close crop of golf shoes and socks, with a neon "BACK IN STOCK" badge and Shop Women / Shop Men buttons visible right away. The message is clear in about two seconds: golf socks are back. Bombas Sport branding in the header keeps the red-and-black system from the 11/06 "Seamless Cami Is Finally Back" send intact.
- The weak spot is the lower half. After the second lifestyle block, the product row is two tiny 3-pack thumbnails floating in a lot of white space. They have no price, no color names and no reviews, and they are not obviously clickable. The "FORE-WARNED, THEY'LL GO FAST" line adds urgency, but nothing backs it up (no stock count, no deadline). The send also has no offer, like the 11/07 slipper send and the 11/05 cozy-socks send, so it relies on product appeal alone.
- Judgement: a competent, niche, no-offer restock that will do well with golfers and be ignored by everyone else. It is well built but under-merchandised.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The golf-green background, shoes and ball make the category obvious instantly.
- The "BACK IN STOCK" hexagon badge is bold and answers the subject line immediately.
- The Shop Women and Shop Men buttons sit inside the hero, so there is a CTA above the fold.
- The sub-headline names concrete benefits: targeted cushioning, breezy mesh knit, sweat-wicking yarns.
- The second block ("TEE OFF IN TOTAL COMFORT") has a magnified knit-detail circle that shows off the product construction.
- The brand-trust row (One Purchased/One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) is on-voice and reassuring.

## What's weak

- The product thumbnails are small and surrounded by empty space. There are no prices, no review stars and no color labels.
- There is no offer or member benefit, and nothing gives a reason to buy now.
- The CTAs repeat three times (hero buttons, block buttons, the underlined "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN" links) with identical generic copy. The text links look dated next to the polished buttons above them.
- "FORE-WARNED, THEY'LL GO FAST" claims scarcity but gives no proof, such as which sizes or colors are low.
- The yellow SMS sign-up bar at the very top pushes the hero down and competes with it. The same text-club ask returns in the footer icon row ("Get Texted Exclusive Offers").

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the product row. Make the 3-pack images larger, and add a price, color names, a star rating, and a "Shop Women's 3-Pack" button under each.
- 2. Make the scarcity real. Write "Low stock in men's 8-12" or "Last restock sold out in X days" instead of a generic line.
- 3. Cut the CTA repetition to one pair of buttons in the hero and one under the product row, and drop the text-link pair.
- 4. Add one reason to buy now, such as a gift angle ("Golf gift under $X") or a bundle price.
- 5. Move the SMS bar below the hero or remove it for this send.

## Technical Audit

The QA pass rate was 33%, but most of that comes from rate-limited and unprobed links, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed by QA: two links return 400.**
  - "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" (`https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/7cAP6MG2MAzzYr-zo2o5wQ~~/AAQRxRA~/...`)
  - "Sign up for texts to stay up to date on holiday drops and sales. Sign Up →" (`https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/nhiqUy9no_u4t_uXNc8Gng~~/AAQRxRA~/...`)
  - Both go through the same click-tracking domain and serve SMS opt-in. A 400 could be a real failure, or the redirect target could be rejecting the automated probe (for example, an `sms:` deep link or a bot-protected form). The QA data can't tell which. Verify manually.
- **Not a defect:** six "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "BOMBAS SPORT" and "BACK IN STOCK" links returned 429 (rate limiting). QA marks these as possibly valid.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 30 links were probed, and 4 were unprobed after the 70s budget (e.g. `.../FvjgvtpDJ-k4TuyF4H5tLA~~/...`). Their status is unknown.
- All links are wrapped in `clicks.bombas.com` redirects, so final destinations can't be seen from the source.
