# Psst, 25% Off Everything's Still On
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Black Friday reminder with a clear hero: "THERE'S STILL TIME FOR 25% OFF" in heavy black type on a sage-green panel, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly beneath it. The eye lands on the offer first. The subject, "Psst, 25% Off Everything's Still On", matches the hero, so the promise is consistent.
- It is also the fifth or sixth near-identical 25% send in four days, after "🎁 25% Off the Best Gifts for Everyone" and "Up to 40% Off With Packs, Oh Yeah" on 11/24, and "⭐ 25% Off Sitewide" on 11/22. Nothing here is new. The "still live" framing has no deadline, so urgency is weak. The product grid is also uneven: two items sit on white backgrounds and two on black, so the sock packs look small and lost.
- Judgement: a competent, low-novelty reminder. It's fine as a cadence filler, but it gives a subscriber no new reason to click.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and sits above the fold, with three category buttons right under it.
- The subject and hero copy line up, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The "Popular Styles On Sale" grid names specific products (Plush Sunday Slipper, Holiday Half Calf 4-Pack).
- The "One Purchased, One Donated" block reinforces brand trust without clutter.
- "Gift Cards Not Included" is stated next to "See Offer Details", so the exclusion isn't buried.

## What's weak

- The cadence is repetitive. It repeats the same 25% beat as the 11/22 and 11/24 sends.
- "Still on" has no end date or time, so there's no credible urgency.
- The sock-pack images are small on a black background. They look unfinished next to the slipper photos on white.
- The product tiles show no prices, sale prices or ratings, so there's no proof of the discount or social proof.
- The footer repeats the Shop Women/Men/Kids links in small blue underlined text, which clashes with the polished hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight" or "Ends Cyber Monday", to the hero and subject. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Show sale prices with the original price struck through, plus star ratings, on the four product tiles.
- 3. Fix the image treatment. Use consistent backgrounds and crop the sock packs larger.
- 4. Differentiate from earlier sends with one new hook, such as a best-seller or a gift-by date.
- 5. Replace the footer text links with buttons that match the hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slippers, packs and kids' socks, all 25% off until 11:59 PM tonight.`
