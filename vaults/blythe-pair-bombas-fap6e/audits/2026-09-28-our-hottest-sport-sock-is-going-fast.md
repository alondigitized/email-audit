## What's weak

- Subject says "Our Hottest Sport Sock Is Going Fast" but nothing in the email identifies a single hero product or signals scarcity/selling-fast — this is a full-collection launch, not a going-fast item.
- No price, no percentage discount, no loyalty/member pricing visible anywhere — pure lookbook with no purchase incentive.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no "while supplies last," no date) despite the subject explicitly promising urgency.
- Four nearly identical product-pack thumbnails (ankle, half-calf, quarter, calf) blur together with minimal differentiation beyond pack imagery — a shopper skimming won't easily tell why one style beats another.

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — either add an actual scarcity signal to the email (a specific style, a stock callout, a "last chance" framing) or rewrite the subject to match what's actually here: a new collection launch.
