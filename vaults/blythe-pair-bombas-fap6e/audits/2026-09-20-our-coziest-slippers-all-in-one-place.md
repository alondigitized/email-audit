# Our Coziest Slippers, All in One Place
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas' first slipper-only send in this run, and it's a clean, well-organized category showcase — three slipper lines (Sunday, Gripper, Suede Moccasin) each get a hero banner, a one-line benefit, and matched product shots. Compared to the sprawling multi-category "Fall Shop" send (2026-09-12) or the discount-driven pack emails (2026-09-13, 2026-09-06/07), this one is tighter and more visually cohesive — every section follows the same rhythm, which makes it easy to scan.
- The catch: there is no discount anywhere in this email. No %, no $ off, no BOGO — just "Shop Women / Shop Men" and a new-product callout on the moccasins. After a run of emails that leaned on offers or restocks, this is a pure lookbook, which will underperform on click-through for a deal-driven subscriber even though the presentation is the strongest of the batch.
- Verdict: great visual merchandising, weak commercial hook — this reads more like a lookbook than a converting promo email.

## What's working

- Consistent, repeatable section template (banner + product pair) makes the email easy to skim on mobile.
- Clear product differentiation — Sherpa vs. grippy vs. shearling-lined gives each slipper line a distinct reason to click.
- "New" badge on Suede Moccasins draws the eye to the freshest item.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed, B Corp) reinforce brand identity at the footer.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or urgency anywhere — the strongest CTA is a generic "Shop Women/Men," not tied to any specific offer.
- Subject promises "all in one place" but that's a mild value prop, not a reason to open today.
- Three near-identical CTA blocks (Shop Women / Shop Men) repeated three times plus once at the bottom is repetitive without variation in copy.
- No social proof, reviews, or sizing/fit info visible for products that live and die on comfort claims.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer or bundle deal (e.g., "Save 15% when you buy 2 pairs") — this category has zero price anchor right now, which is the single biggest lever to lift clicks.
- 2. Replace repeated "Shop Women/Shop Men" CTAs with product-specific links ("Shop Sunday Slippers," "Shop Gripper Slippers") to shorten the path to purchase.
- 3. Add a short line of social proof under at least one hero (e.g., star rating or "our #1 rated slipper").
- 4. Tighten the subject to name a specific slipper style instead of the category as a whole.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Shearling-Lined Suede Moccasins Are Here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 Ways to Cozy Up Your Feet This Fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sherpa, grippy, or shearling — pick your cozy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our warmest slipper lineup just dropped.`
