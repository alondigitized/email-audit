# 25% Off Sitewide: New Styles Just Added
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "25% off" send in about 36 hours, and it is the same sale again. The hero is strong: "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in heavy black type over a snowy sock shot, with a terracotta "New Styles!" badge and Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons right below it. The offer lands in about a second. The body then repeats the recent run. "Up to 40% Off Packs" is a rerun of this morning's 11/15 send, and the Slipper Shop and Gift Guide blocks add little.
- The "New Styles" angle is the only fresh element, and it gets weak support. The retro striped sock with the gondola embroidery is the real newness, but it sits in a mid-email tile labeled "Early Holiday Exclusive". The product grid below it shows slippers and ankle socks, not the new style. A subscriber who got the 11/14 and earlier 11/15 sends will read this as a repeat.
- Judgement: well-built, on-brand and easy to scan, but it is a weak sitewide send. The subject promises new styles, and the email never puts the new styles first or gives a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in huge black type on a bright snow background, with the badge acknowledging new product.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit directly under the hero. A shopper can pick a path with no scrolling.
- The intro line, "We just added a new spin on some best-selling socks to the sale", gives the reason for the send in plain, friendly language.
- The alternating dark-green and light tiles give clear rhythm and easy scanning. The photography is warm and seasonal.
- The footer is clean: the Gift Cards Not Included disclosure, an Offer Details link, the B Corp mark and the "One Purchased, One Donated" brand cue.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the render is time-bounded. There is no end date and no countdown, so there is no reason to click now instead of tomorrow.
- The hero never shows the new style the subject promises. It shows a generic four-sock cluster, and the new retro sock appears only in the second block.
- Three stacked tiles each carry three buttons, which makes about 11 near-identical "Shop Women/Men/Kids" CTAs. That is repetitive, and none of them name a product.
- The "Popular Styles On Sale" grid has no prices, no sale marks, no ratings and no reviews. Pale product shots on gray look flat next to the tiles above.
- "Up to 40% Off Packs" repeats the hero of this morning's 11/15 send. This is the third near-identical promo in two days.
- The sitewide discount hides behind fine print: "ANYONE ELSE CRAVING SOME COCOA?" is a confusing headline, and the "Gift Cards Not Included" exclusion sits under it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the new style. Make the hero the retro striped gondola sock with "NEW + 25% OFF", and demote the generic cluster. This ties the subject, hero and offer together.
- 2. Add a real deadline. Use "Ends Monday" or a countdown in the hero. Without it, a third promo in two days gives no reason to act today.
- 3. Collapse the repeated buttons. Use one gender split in the hero, then link tiles straight to the product or collection ("Shop the Gondola Crew", "Shop Slippers").
- 4. Put prices on the product grid, for example "$X → $Y with 25% off", plus a star rating on at least one product.
- 5. Space the sends. If a sale reminder lands the same day, use a different angle, such as the slipper or gift focus, rather than re-running the packs tile.
- 6. Replace the "cocoa" headline with a plain closing CTA such as "Shop the Sale".

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% Off Ends Monday: New Socks Just Dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The retro gondola crew is here, and it's included in the sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slippers, packs and gifts, all 25% off through Monday.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Duplicate attribute on `<html>`:** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice. This is a well-formedness error under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers and validators will reject it.
- **`<meta>` inside the body:** the `<meta itemprop="description|availabilityStarts|availabilityEnds">` tags sit inside `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/DiscountOffer">`. They are not valid in XHTML body content. Clients that don't read microdata ignore them.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are defined twice in the `@media (max-width:600px)` block, with conflicting values (`max-width:33%` vs. none). The later rule wins, so this is harmless but sloppy.
- **Positives:**
  - `lang`, `dir` and `xml:lang` are set.
  - The wrapper has `role="article"`, `aria-roledescription="email"` and `aria-label`.
  - Viewport, color-scheme and dark-mode media queries are present.
  - There is an MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.
  - Both preheader divs are hidden with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`.
- Image `alt` text, table roles and CTA markup are beyond the truncation, so they can't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
