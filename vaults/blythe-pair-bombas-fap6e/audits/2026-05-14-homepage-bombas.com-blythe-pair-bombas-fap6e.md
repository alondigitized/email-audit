# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably a Bombas homepage — clean grid, strong brand voice, that familiar dark navy bar up top — but right now it's leading with sport and performance, not the giving mission or the classic comfort story I actually buy into. The BOMBAS SPORT hero feels like they're pitching me on a new category rather than greeting a longtime loyalist. No donation counter, no "one purchased = one donated" language anywhere in this viewport, which is the first thing I scan for.

## What's working

- **The category grid layout** — four clean tiles (Bralettes & Underwear, Socks, Slippers & Shoes, T-Shirts) let me get to Socks in one tap. No hunting through a mega-menu.
- **The Socks tile image** — stacked teal/blue folded socks, clean product shot. It actually makes me want to check what's new this season.
- **Free Standard Shipping on Orders $75+** — visible in the top bar without any effort. I know instantly whether I hit the threshold on my usual end-of-year haul.
- **BOMBAS wordmark + clean typography** — brand recognition is instant. No ambiguity about where I am.

## What's weak

- **Zero giving-mission language** — not a single mention of "for every pair purchased, a pair donated," no donation counter, no Giving Partnership callout. This is the whole reason I became a customer. A returning loyalist like me scans for it reflexively, and its absence feels like the brand de-emphasizing what made it special.
- **"20% Off First Order" as the headline promo** — this is acquisition bait front-and-center in the top bar. I've been buying Bombas for years and the most prominent offer on the page literally excludes me.
- **BOMBAS SPORT as the hero** — the running-women lifestyle shot is energetic but it's selling me on a category pivot rather than confirming why I love this brand. No cozy, no comfort, no mission.
- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — it covers the bottom third of the BOMBAS SPORT section on mobile. Not a catastrophic bug, but it forces a dismiss before I can read what's below the grid.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the first-order promo bar for a returning-member benefit.** Even something like "Free shipping on all orders for members" or "Members earn 2x points this month" would immediately signal that they know I exist. Push the new-customer code below the fold or into a dismissible banner.
- **Add one line of mission copy above or below the category grid** — "Every purchase = a pair donated" takes six words and zero design lift. Put it right under the nav. I should never have to scroll to find it.
- **Test a split hero: left side BOMBAS SPORT, right side "The socks that started it all."** Mobile gets a swiping carousel between sport and core. That way the performance push coexists with the comfort/giving story I actually connect with.
- **Surface a loyalty hook at the top.** Even a small "Log in to see your rewards" nudge next to the cart icon would make me feel seen as a repeat buyer instead of a prospect.
