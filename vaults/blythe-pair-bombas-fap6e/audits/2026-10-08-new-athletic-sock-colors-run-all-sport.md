# New Athletic Sock Colors: Run + All Sport
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, product-led colorway announcement, and it matches Bombas's recent pattern of drops with no offer (Oct 6 "New Socks for Festive Fun," Oct 5 "Incoming: New Comfy Dress Socks"). The hero is strong: a dark panel with a chunky "NEW COLORS" headline, three tight sock shots in mint, magenta and forest green, and "Run + All Sport" in a lime tag. Two "Shop Women / Shop Men" buttons sit right under it, so the eye lands where it should.
- Below the hero, the email loses momentum. Both product-grid rows show small pack images floating in large empty white bands. Those bands read as dead space or slow-loading images. The pack names have no price, no color count and no visible link treatment. The lower half is also repetitive: each section offers Shop Women and Shop Men again, and "Show Your Colors" adds a third pair of links.
- Overall it's a competent 6, the same as every other send in this run. It's clean and on-brand, but it gives a subscriber no reason to act today. This is the sixth Bombas email in about eight days, and none of them has had an offer or a deadline.

## What's working

- The hero has clear hierarchy: big "NEW COLORS" headline, a three-image sock strip, and the "Run + All Sport" tag all point to a single message.
- The two-section split (All Sport and Running) is easy to scan. Each section has its own headline, a one-line benefit and its own buttons.
- Benefit copy is short and concrete ("targeted cushioning," "breezy mesh knit," "soft, sweat-wicking yarns").
- The "Shop Women / Shop Men" buttons are large, high-contrast and tappable. Neither the buttons nor the sections have broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer trust row (One Purchased, One Donated; 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforces brand values without cluttering the body.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or deadline anywhere, so there is nothing to act on today.
- The product-grid images are tiny and surrounded by large white gaps, which wastes the most scannable part of the email. The pack captions are small, plain text with no price or color count.
- The Shop Women / Shop Men pair repeats four times (hero, All Sport, Running, "Show Your Colors"), and the product images and captions don't look clickable. Every path leads to a gender category rather than a specific pack.
- The intro line "Two of our fan-fave athletic socks just got new colors" is large but vague. It doesn't name the socks or the colors.
- The email has no reviews, ratings or member-pricing signal to push someone from browsing to buying.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to act now. Put a deadline, a bundle price or a "free shipping on 2+ packs" line in the hero. This single change would lift the email more than any layout fix.
- 2. Fix the product grids. Enlarge the pack images to fill their column, cut the surrounding white space, and add price and a "6 new colors" label under each name. Make each image and name a direct link to that pack's page.
- 3. Replace the repeated gender button pairs with one button per product tile, such as "Shop the All Sport 4-Pack," so the CTA names a product.
- 4. Add one proof point near the top, such as a star rating or "most-loved athletic sock," to support the "fan-fave" claim.
- 5. Put the new color names in the copy (for example, "Pine, Berry, Mint") so the email shows the news instead of just announcing it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New colors for the sock you run in`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our runner-favorite sock, now in 6 new colors`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All Sport and Run Ankle Socks, fresh colors, same cushioning.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your pair: women's and men's packs from $X.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Bombas, "New Athletic Sock Colors: Run + All Sport"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound, with a hidden preheader, dark-mode CSS and ARIA roles. Two points need a closer look: one 400 on the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link, and 5 images with no alt attribute. The 38% pass rate mostly comes from rate-limited (429) and skipped probes, not confirmed failures. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Possible broken link (400):** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" at `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/HhoJXxJkjhyHZ-hZLBYVJg~~/AAQRxRA~/Dk7d-…`.
  - Unlike the other tracked URLs, which end in `~~`, this one has no terminator in the QA output. The report may have truncated it, and a cut-off token would itself return 400.
  - A 400 on an SMS-signup link can also be bot-rejection by the destination. Re-test the full URL manually before treating it as a defect.
- **429 responses are not failures:** "Shop Women" and "Shop Men" (several instances each) and "BOMBAS SPORT" all returned 429 from `clicks.bombas.com`. The rate limit is likely a side effect of probing ~12 links on one tracking domain in quick succession. Their destinations are unverified.
- **Incomplete coverage:**
  - 23 links were skipped, and 4 were unprobed after the 70s time budget (`…/i7GXeKARfB7bTuGuYRCcTw~~`, `…/ENkYp8fFjGhC9AAYpAYHuA~~`, `…/SuLbKoFXw252PSqWEe7LTg~~`, `…/lbBMjEAk9pICnKMZST-6cQ~~`). These are neither pass nor fail.
  - Overall link health is therefore unknown, not poor.
- **Link wrapping:** All links go through a click-tracking redirect, `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/<payload>`. The payload is 500+ characters per link. That is normal for this ESP, but it is why the plain-text part is >70% URLs (8024 of 9252 chars).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images):**
  - Four product images on `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`: `11841-berry_ganache_mix-adult-female-ankle-layflat`, `11840-blue_pebble_mix-adult-male-ankle-layflat-HO2`, `11850-pebble_sea_mix-adult-female-ankle-layflat-HO`, `11849-slate_green_mix-adult-male-ankle-layflat-HO2`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show or announce nothing useful. The four filenames are readable product names, so alt text is easy to add.
  - The fifth is the open-tracking pixel, `clicks.bombas.com/q/noDTUaaOyG67…`. It should carry `alt=""` to be explicitly decorative.
- **Duplicate attribute:** The `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. This is invalid markup, though browsers and email clients usually tolerate it.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each defined twice in the 600px media query, with slightly different values (`33.33%/33%` vs `33%`). The later rule wins, so this is harmless but untidy.
- **Positives:**
  - `role="article"`, `aria-label`, `lang` and `dir` are set.
  - The viewport meta tag and the `color-scheme` meta tags are present.
  - There are MSO conditional comments and a hidden preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible HTML. The source was truncated, so the rest is not covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA tool itself notes the headers may not be captured by the relay. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check them against the original message in a raw-header view.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Verify it in a direct-delivery test.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be confirmed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The wrapped links hide destination URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be checked from the HTML or QA data. The tracking tokens do not expose campaign parameters. Resolve the redirects to final URLs to check:
- the `utm_*` values
- that the Shop Women, Shop Men and BOMBAS SPORT destinations match the "Athletic Sock Colors: Run + All Sport" theme

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link with the full, untruncated URL.
2. Re-run link probing with throttling and retries so 429s resolve, and with a longer time budget to cover the 4 unprobed and 23 skipped links.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a raw-header capture, not through the relay.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for the main CTAs and record the final URLs and UTMs.
7. Check the plain-text part for readable link labels, not only long tracking URLs.
