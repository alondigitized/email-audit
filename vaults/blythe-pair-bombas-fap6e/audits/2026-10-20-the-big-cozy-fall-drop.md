# The Big Cozy Fall Drop
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**

- This is a tidy seasonal roundup with no offer. A "Fall Cozy Drop" hero with a textured grey sock and a Shop Women / Shop Men button pair leads into four category blocks: Lodge, House Gripper, Sweatshirt and Leg Warmers. The creative is warm and on-season, and the layout is easy to scan. As a catalog it works. As a reason to click today, it doesn't.
- It also lands in a streak. Bombas has sent a no-offer product drop nearly every day: the Saturday Suede Boot on 10/19, Merino on 10/17, Marvel on 10/16 and Golf on 10/15. The one send with an actual hook was the "up to 20% off" email on 10/18, and this one drops it. "First day of cozy season" is a nice line, but it gives no deadline and no price. ## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Fall Cozy Drop" overlaid on a close-up sock photo, with two obvious Shop Women and Shop Men buttons visible immediately.
- The intro line, "It's officially the first day of cozy season," ties the send to the moment and sets the tone.
- Each category gets a lifestyle banner and a clean product row, with a consistent rhythm that is easy to skim.
- Product captions show pack counts (4-Pack, 3-Pack), which answers a basic buying question.
- The footer keeps the brand's proof points: One Purchased = One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed and the Help Center.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price and no deadline anywhere. After the 10/18 "up to 20% off" send, this feels like a step backward.
- The "LEG WARMERS" banner headline sits low-contrast over a busy snowy photo and is hard to read.
- All three leg warmer items carry the identical caption "Women's Ribbed Leg Warmers", so shoppers can't tell the colorways apart.
- The product rows have large white gaps and small, floating product shots, and none look individually clickable.
- The closing CTA ("More cozy down here" with two small underlined text links) is much weaker than the hero buttons.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one reason to act now. Put a bundle price or free-shipping threshold in the hero, or name a single hero product (the Lodge 4-Pack) and give it the lead slot. The other three categories can then follow as secondary rows.
- 2. Fix the leg warmer captions with colorway names (Oatmeal, Taupe, Black) and add prices to every product label.
- 3. Raise the contrast on the Leg Warmers banner and tighten the spacing around product shots.
- 4. Turn the closing text links into the same black buttons as the hero.
- 5. Don't send a no-offer drop on consecutive days. Alternate product drops with an offer-led send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Filenames used as link names:** the QA link text for linked images shows the filename (`[image: 12036-mauve_taupe_mix-…]`). Screen readers will announce the filename, or the whole tracking URL, as the link name. The fix is `alt=""` for decorative images and descriptive alt for linked products.

- **Malformed `<html>` tag:** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` is declared twice. Clients tolerate it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Plus side:** the preheader is present ("Soft & cushy socks galore."), `lang`/`dir` are set, and there is an MSO font fallback block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so the finding is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders, so check the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC could not be verified through the relay. This is unknown rather than a failure. The From domain `hello.bombas.com` should be checked directly.
- **Plain-text part is 86% URLs** (8721 of 10159 characters), from the long tracked links. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only recipients. Shorter tracking tokens, or fewer URLs in the text part, would help.
- **Unconfirmed:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure, but I can't confirm its presence.

## 7. Recommendations
