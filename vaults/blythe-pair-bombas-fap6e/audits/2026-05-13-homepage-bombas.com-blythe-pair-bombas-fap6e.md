# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Bombas is doing a full summer pivot — sandals and soft basics are front and center, and socks, the product that built this brand, are buried as one tile in a four-category grid. The top banner immediately tells me I'm being treated as a new customer, which I am absolutely not. It's a clean-looking site that clearly knows what it's doing structurally, but it has zero memory of me.

## What's working

- **The 2×2 category grid** — socks is right there, one tap, no hunting required. For me, that's the only thing I came to buy and I can find it instantly.
- **The free-shipping threshold** — "$75+" is prominent and believable; my end-of-year sock stock-up easily clears that bar.
- **Layout cleanliness** — no clutter, no overlapping text, no broken images. Bombas keeps the page readable even on a small phone viewport.
- **"Log In" in the nav** — it's there; I can get to my account and hopefully see order history.

## What's weak

- **"20% Off First Order with Code COMFORT20"** — I've bought from Bombas every year for the past six years. Leading with a first-order code is a slap in the face for a repeat buyer. This banner should be swapped for something a returning customer can actually use.
- **Zero mission/give-back copy** — I bought into Bombas because for every pair sold, one gets donated. That's the whole thing. I don't see a single word about it above the fold. If the donation counter messaging has gone quiet site-wide, I'm going to notice — and I already am.
- **Socks are one of four tiles, not the hero** — the big lifestyle photo below the grid is sandals. Sandals. Socks are the brand's origin and their best-known product, and they're getting equal billing with bralettes and T-shirts.
- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — it covers the bottom third of the sandal lifestyle shot. Not a catastrophic bug but it obscures whatever messaging was below the fold and it's the first thing my eye goes to.
- **No product preview in the Socks tile** — the tile shows folded teal fabric that reads more like a spa towel than a sock product shot. A real sock — ankle, no-show, calf — would be more motivating.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the first-order banner with a returning-customer offer** — even "Stock up and save: 15% when you buy 6+ pairs" would land for someone like me without alienating new visitors with a second banner slot.
- **Add one line of mission copy to the hero or nav strip** — "1 purchased = 1 donated" used to be inescapable. Bring that back above the fold. It's the reason half your loyalists are loyalists.
- **Promote the Socks tile to hero status** — make the full-width lifestyle shot a sock shot, not sandals, or at minimum let me carousel between the two. Socks is the flagship; treat it like one.
- **Show a sock product photo in the Socks tile** — replace the folded teal fabric with an actual sock flat-lay or on-foot shot so it's immediately recognizable.
