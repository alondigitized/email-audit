# ⏰ Up to 40% Off Packs: Grab Your Faves
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid last-call send with a clear hero. "UP TO 40% OFF PACKS FINAL HOURS" sits in heavy cream type on deep maroon, over three sock shots, with salmon Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly beneath. The eye lands on the offer first, and the deadline is credible: the Early Holiday Sale ends tonight at midnight PST.
- The problem is cadence. This is the second Bombas send on 11/17-11/18 for the same sale. The earlier one was "🚨 25% Off Everything Is Still On!", and this one repeats the 11/15 "Up to 40% Off Packs: Stock Up on Your Favorites" angle. A subscriber has seen this offer about five times in four days, so the urgency is real but the fatigue is real too.
- The body is long for a "final hours" message. Four product tiles, a gift row, a gifting grid, a donation block and a footer all sit below the hero. They dilute the last-call push, and the product tiles carry no prices or savings. The deadline is also repeated in a plain block near the bottom, where it is easier to miss. Overall it is competent, but it is not the sharpest send of the run.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and legible. Headline, three socks shots and three buttons are all above the fold.
- "FINAL HOURS" and "ends tonight" give the urgency a credible, specific deadline.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons are clear, and the top nav repeats the same split.
- The product grid and gift row show real SKUs with names, such as the Men's Solids Calf Sock 4-Pack and the Fair Isle Ornament Gift Box.
- The footer deadline block states the terms plainly: "Gift Cards Not Included | See Offer Details".

## What's weak

- No prices, sale prices or percent-off badges appear on any product tile, so "up to 40%" is never shown applied to anything.
- The body is long and diffuse for a last-call email. The gifting grid and the One Purchased One Donated block compete with the sale.
- The Gifting Made Easy tiles reuse "Shop Women / Men / Kids" labels, which duplicates the hero buttons.
- The product tiles have no buttons or CTA copy. They rely on images being clickable, and the small captions are hard to read.
- The deadline repeats in a small block near the bottom, below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put sale prices or "40% off" badges on the product tiles, and cut the body to the hero plus one product row.
- 2. Add a countdown or time-specific line near the top, for example "Ends 11:59pm PST tonight".
- 3. Link the hero buttons to specific collections, such as "Shop Packs", rather than three broad gender categories.
- 4. Drop the donation and gifting blocks from final-hours sends.
- 5. Fix the cadence: send one clean last-call email rather than two on the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 40% off packs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off sock packs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Midnight PST is the cutoff. Calf, ankle and gift-box packs are included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before the Early Holiday Sale ends.`

## Technical Audit: Bombas, "⏰ Up to 40% Off Packs: Grab Your Faves"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken link (HTTP 400 on the SMS-signup CTA), duplicate `xmlns` attributes on `<html>`, and missing alt attributes on 8 images. The headers that would show compliance and authentication status weren't captured, so those can't be judged. The 38% pass rate is mostly probe noise from 429 rate limiting, not broken links.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure (400):** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" → `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/EgISM6RoMAGZ7JAXJ3Q4Ng~~/AAQRxRA~/BWRgLkEz…`. The redirect rejected the request. A 400 from a tracking redirect usually means a malformed or expired token, or a bot-rejecting endpoint. Verify it manually before treating it as a hard break.
- **Unverified, not failures:** 7 links returned 429 ("Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids" ×2 each, "Shop Slippers"), all on `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…`. The server rate-limited the probe, so these are neither confirmed good nor bad.
- **Probe coverage gaps:** 12 links were probed, 34 skipped by ranking, and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget (`…/Y1ICvSmSCbwpe8_ZPWuirA~~/…`, `…/EE5eHdSCS0a8BL_dIFUHUw~~/…`, `…/Ys3ZHReW_0U2Vj0LO1yV3w~~/…`, `…/-pIBvLKlcwdzZCVMb6d2xw~~/…`). Most of the email's links are untested.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/72MIbbpsPhMmZ5E1aX5vUA~~/…` is an open-tracking pixel flagged for missing alt. This is cosmetic. It should carry `alt=""`.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 7 product images.** All are on `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`, for example `mixed-layflat-product-transparent_d552793b…`, `7991-mixed-4u-layflat-1-transparent.png` and `plum-moss-mix-layflat-product-transparent.png`. Screen readers will fall back to reading file names. Images with images-off in the client will show no text.
- **Duplicate attribute in the root tag:** `<html … xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" lang="en" dir="ltr" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` declares `xmlns` twice. This is invalid markup and strict parsers may reject it. Most clients tolerate it.
- **Conflicting CSS in the `max-width:600px` media query:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each defined twice with different values (`max-width: 50%` vs. none; `33.33%`/`33%` vs. `33%`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Present and correct:** `role="article"` with `aria-label`, `lang`/`dir`, a viewport meta tag, color-scheme meta tags with a `prefers-color-scheme` block, and an MSO fallback. Most of the HTML source was truncated, so alt text and structure beyond the head and preheader can't be fully assessed.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Nothing after the truncation point was checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo need both headers, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer was truncated out of the source, so neither could be confirmed in the body. The QA tool says it always probes an unsubscribe link, but no result for it is listed.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,867 of 11,513 characters (86%) are URLs. The long tracking-wrapped links dominate the text version. This is a minor spam-score risk.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. Every link is wrapped in `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…` with an opaque token. The probes either got 429 or didn't follow the redirect to a final destination, so the UTM parameters and landing URLs aren't visible. The `schema.org/DiscountOffer` markup has `availabilityEnds` of `2026-11-17T23:59:00-05:00`, so the offer ends today. That is consistent with the "⏰" subject line, and the landing page should be checked to confirm it still shows the offer.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Open the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link in a real browser or SMS-capable client. If it still returns 400, regenerate the tracking link or fix the destination.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 product images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute from `<html>`.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass). This is what settles section 5.
5. Re-run the link probe with a slower rate or a longer time budget, and resolve each redirect chain, so the 429 and unprobed links get a real verdict and UTMs can be checked.
6. Optionally, shorten the tracked URLs or add a text-friendly version to bring the plain-text URL ratio down.
