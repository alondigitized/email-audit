# New & Trending: Our Hottest Packs
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's first send in the recent run with an actual offer. "Up to 20% off" sits in a hero badge and again in the line "Stock up and save up to 20% on the packs everyone's loving right now." That breaks the no-offer streak from Oct 9–17 ("Warm Up With New Merino Wool," "New MARVEL Compression Just Landed"). The "Fall's Hottest Packs" headline over three lifestyle photos is bold and easy to read.
- The execution is uneven. The lifestyle banners are strong, but the product grids between them show tiny pack thumbnails floating in large white gaps. The images look underscaled and the captions are small. The "up to 20%" is also vague. It doesn't say which packs qualify or whether there's a deadline. The email ends with a closing line, "Packs on packs on packs," over three small text links, which is a weak finish for a send that is meant to drive sales.
- Verdict: a decent promo that is held back by sparse product presentation and no urgency. It's a step up from the no-offer drops, but it doesn't use the offer well.

## What's working

- The hero headline "FALL'S HOTTEST PACKS" is large, on-brand and clear, and the seasonal framing fits mid-October.
- The "Up to 20% Off" badge in the hero and the copy line below it make the offer visible early.
- The Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids buttons in the hero are clear and sit directly under the images.
- The lifestyle banners ("Everyday Go-Tos," "Merino Meets Cashmere," "Dressy Pairs") break the email into easy sections, each with a short benefit line.
- Each banner has a Women/Men button pair, so the segmentation is clean.

## What's weak

- The product grids show tiny pack images with big white space around them, so they look unfinished. The captions are small and long.
- There are no prices, sale prices or review stars on any product, so the 20% off isn't tied to anything concrete.
- "Up to 20%" has no end date, no eligible-product detail and no code, so there's no urgency.
- Almost every CTA goes to a generic Women/Men category split. There are no individual product CTAs.
- The closing "Packs on packs on packs" section uses small blue text links that are weaker than the black buttons above.
- The email is very long, at about 4,400px of scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the product tiles larger and fill the frame, and add the sale price with the original price struck through. Right now the offer isn't connected to any product.
- 2. Add a deadline or a clear condition to the offer ("Ends Sunday," or "20% off 4+ packs").
- 3. Shorten the email to two banners with one product row each, and link each product tile directly to its product page.
- 4. Replace the text-link close with the same black buttons used in the hero.
- 5. Add star ratings or a "best seller" tag to back up the "hottest packs" claim.

- **Positives:**
