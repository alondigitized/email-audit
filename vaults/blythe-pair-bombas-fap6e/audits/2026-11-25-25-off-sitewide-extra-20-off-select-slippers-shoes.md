# 25% Off Sitewide + Extra 20% Off Select Slippers & Shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a tightly built slipper-focused Black Friday send. The hero reads "SELECT SLIPPERS & SHOES ARE 40% OFF (And Everything Else Is 25% Off)" over a photo of cozy footwear, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly under it. The offer is clear and the eye lands on it first. After four days of near-identical "25% off everything" sends (11/21 through 11/25), this is the first one to give a subscriber a new reason to open.
- The body does the work. Three category banners (Sunday Slippers, Gripper Slippers, Gripper Booties, Cabin Booties) each lead into product tiles with struck-through regular prices and sale prices, for example Women's Sunday Slipper Reg. $85, Sale $51. A burgundy "UP TO 40% OFF SLIPPERS" banner closes it out.
- The weak spot is offer framing. The subject says "Extra 20% Off", the hero says "40% Off", and the sub-line says "25% Off". A reader has to work out that 25% plus an extra 20% comes to roughly 40%. The email is also long, and it never states a deadline, which is odd for Black Friday week.

## What's working

- The hero puts the 40% slipper offer in big pink and cream type on a dark blue panel, with the 25% sitewide fallback in a parenthetical so nobody feels excluded.
- Every product tile shows Reg. and Sale prices, and the savings are visible without any math.
- Each category banner has a short, voicey line, such as "The fluffy sherpa pairs that sold out in a flash last year." Together with "Dibs, anyone?" this keeps the Bombas tone.
- The Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons are repeated at the top and bottom, so the primary action is available at both ends.
- The render is clean: no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The offer language is inconsistent across the subject ("Extra 20%"), the hero ("40%") and the sub-line ("25%"). Most subscribers won't do the compounding in their heads.
- No deadline is visible anywhere, such as "ends Monday" or a countdown, so there is no credible urgency.
- The email is a long scroll with four category blocks and eight or more products. Most of that sits below the first screen.
- The pricing fine print is thin. "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" sits low in the body, in small type.
- The Shop Women, Men and Kids buttons are generic. A slipper-led email should link to a slipper collection, not the whole audience split.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one number and use it everywhere. Lead with "40% off slippers", and mention "25% off the rest" only as secondary copy.
- 2. Add a deadline line under the hero, such as "Ends Cyber Monday midnight PT".
- 3. Replace the hero buttons with product-led CTAs like "Shop Slippers 40% Off" and "Shop Gifts". Cut the audience split from the hero.
- 4. Trim the body to two category blocks, Sunday Slippers and Gripper Slippers. Put the bestseller first, with a "sold out last year" or low-stock cue on it.
- 5. Add star ratings or a review count to each product tile for social proof.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our cozy slippers: 40% off through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sunday, Gripper and Cabin styles. Plus 25% off everything else.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Duplicate attribute on `<html>`:** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice in the same tag. This is invalid markup. Most clients tolerate it, but strict XHTML parsers may not.
- **Very small mobile type:** `.font13` drops to `font-size:11px` at ≤570px, `10px` at ≤480px and `9px` at ≤470px. `line-height` is set equal to the font size (9px at the smallest breakpoint). `.font13Pad` is also `9px`. This falls below common legibility minimums and will be hard to read and to tap. It is likely legal or footer text, which I couldn't confirm.
- **Redundant, conflicting CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice in the ≤600px query. `.mobile-third` is first `width:33.33%; max-width:33%`, then `width:33%`. The later rule wins, so the first is dead code with inconsistent values.
- **Second hidden spacer div:** it uses `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`, but has no `mso-hide:all`, unlike the preheader div. Outlook desktop may render the `&nbsp;&zwnj;` padding.
- **Structured-data mismatch:** the `schema.org/DiscountOffer` block has `itemprop="description" content="UP TO 40% OFF"`. The subject line says "25% Off Sitewide + Extra 20%". The offer text doesn't match the subject. Gmail only honors microdata for whitelisted senders, so the impact is low.
- **Present and correct:**
  - `<meta name="viewport">`, `lang`/`dir`, and a `<title>`.
  - `role="article" aria-roledescription="email" aria-label`.
  - `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags.
  - `prefers-color-scheme` rules.
  - MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`.
- Image `alt` text and table `role="presentation"` could not be checked.
