# 25% Off EVERYTHING: Black Friday Is On
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, on-brand Black Friday send. The hero "EVERYTHING'S 25% OFF" sits in huge cream type on a dark green panel with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly beneath it. The offer lands in about a second, and the 25% is the only number on the page. That is the strength.
- The weakness is repetition. This is at least the third "25% Off Everything" send in 48 hours. It follows 11/26's "👀 25% Off Everything: Beat the Friday Rush" and "Dig In: 25% Off Everything", and 11/25's "Psst, 25% Off Everything's Still On". The creative is near-identical: the same hero formula and the same three-button row. "Today's the big day" is the only new beat. Nothing escalates, and the subject says "Black Friday Is On" as if it were news.
- The body is solid but generic. Best sellers and a "Something Soft for Everyone" grid give browsing paths. But the best-seller block has no prices, ratings or per-product CTAs, and the product tiles are inconsistent. Some are on white squares that clash with the black background, and the sock packs float small. It's a competent 6, held back by cadence fatigue and a weak lower half.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. "EVERYTHING'S 25% OFF" in giant type with a lightning-bolt graphic sets clear visual hierarchy.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit right under the hero, so the CTA needs no scrolling.
- "Today's the big day" adds a bit of credible urgency in the intro line.
- The "Something Soft for Everyone" grid covers slippers, underwear and tees, widening the offer beyond socks.
- The "One Purchased, One Donated" block reinforces brand trust and fits the giving season.

## What's weak

- It repeats the same 25% sitewide message sent several times in the past two days, with no new hook.
- The best-sellers block has no prices, no sale price, no star ratings and no CTA. The product tiles themselves don't look clickable.
- The product images are visually inconsistent. White-background slipper shots sit on black, and the sock packs are tiny with lots of dead space.
- The offer-details link is small, and "Gift Cards Not Included" is easy to miss. The bottom "Shop Women/Men/Kids" links are small blue underlined text, off-brand next to the hero buttons.
- The lower half is long, with badges and social icons, and it dilutes the click path.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate the send. Give it one new reason to open, such as a "final hours" timer, a new bundle, or a free-shipping threshold. Repeating a 25% hero a third time trains people to ignore it.
- 2. Add prices and ratings to the best-seller tiles ("$18 → $13.50", "4.8★ · 12k reviews"), and make each tile a labeled button.
- 3. Fix the product imagery. Use consistent cut-out or on-color backgrounds and bigger sock-pack shots.
- 4. Style the bottom CTAs as real buttons that match the hero, and make the offer-details text more legible.
- 5. Trim the footer badges so the email ends right after the donation block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off all socks & slippers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: your 25% ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best sellers from $13 — slippers, packs & gifts, all 25% off today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on cozy before Cyber Week prices change`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a well-structured, table-based XHTML Transitional template with dark-mode meta, an ARIA article wrapper and a hidden preheader, all sent through click-tracking redirects on `clicks.bombas.com`. The QA run found no hard failures. The open items are missing `alt` attributes on 5 images, an unverified unsubscribe/authentication posture, and link verification that was incomplete because of 429s and a time budget.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 6 probed links returned HTTP 429 ("Shop Women" ×2, "Shop Men" ×2, "Shop Kids" ×2 and "Shop Slippers"). All are `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...` redirects. A 429 means the click-tracker rate-limited the QA probe, not that the link is dead. Their final destinations are **unverified**.
- **5 links were not probed** because the 70s time budget ran out. They are neither pass nor fail:
  - `.../xCx8fx0DG8L0kXHSBzN4eQ~~/...`
  - `.../_uTOiam4_kOpNvRFymlVSw~~/...`
  - `.../rziCyFj8rCMSAmcjj7DQmQ~~/...`
  - `.../gAR73KB0P9YZ9iNJf3jTLQ~~/...`
  - `.../aJ6J7hIScgMD6jBSXDREzg~~/...`
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 28 skipped, so most of the email's links are unchecked.
- **Every link is wrapped**, so destination URLs can't be read from the source. The "Shop Women", "Shop Men" and "Shop Kids" links each appear twice with distinct per-instance IDs (e.g. `jtqy9RIV4kF57h4OhBRizw~~` and `tnobu-gS9V26gPFtZbpSSw~~`) but a shared destination segment (`NreeeP1K_8MVJvw5...`). That is normal per-placement tracking.
- **Wrapped URLs are very long, over 900 characters in places.** This is what drives the plain-text warning: URLs make up 8916 of 10288 characters (87%), which exceeds the 70% threshold. It is a spam-filter and readability risk for the text alternative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** The `alt` attribute is absent on:
  - `11291-dusty_purple-adult-female-low_back_slip_on_1`
  - `12108-fairislemix-adult-female-layflat-1-transpare...`
  - `11265-midnight_navy_chevron-adult-male-ankle-layfl...`
  - `mixed-layflat-product-transparent__3_.png`
  - `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/kmYK3l-_lO-uf2G7atks2w~~/...`, which is most likely the open-tracking pixel.

  Screen readers may announce file names or URLs, and image-blocked clients show nothing. The tracking pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`.** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice in the opening tag. This is invalid markup. It is low risk, but strict parsers can reject it.
- **Positives confirmed in source:** `lang`/`dir` are set, `role="article"` with `aria-label` is present, viewport and `color-scheme` meta tags are present, there are MSO conditionals, and `prefers-color-scheme` rules exist.
- **Limit:** the HTML was truncated, so the body markup couldn't be fully audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. Because the source is truncated, this covers only that portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a verified sender defect. If the headers are genuinely absent, the email lacks one-click unsubscribe. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this capture. This is a capture limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is listed, and the footer is outside the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. Every destination sits behind `clicks.bombas.com` redirects that returned 429 or went unprobed. As a result, no landing URL, UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign` and so on) or promo-code carry-through could be confirmed. The `itemprop` metadata states `25% OFF`, valid 2026-11-21 to 2026-12-02, but landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 4 product images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel `clicks.bombas.com/q/kmYK3l-_...`.
2. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` on `<html>`.
3. Re-probe with throttling or retry/backoff, and a longer time budget, to resolve the 6 rate-limited and 5 unprobed links. Then capture final URLs to check UTMs and landing alignment.
4. Check the raw message (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` to confirm RFC 8058 support and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Shorten tracked URLs or generate a leaner plain-text part, since 87% of it is URL text.
6. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address are present, because they are outside the captured HTML.
