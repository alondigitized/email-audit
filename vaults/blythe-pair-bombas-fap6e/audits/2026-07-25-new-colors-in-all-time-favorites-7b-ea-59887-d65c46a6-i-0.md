# New Colors in All-Time Favorites
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a core-catalog refresh email — new colorways across Bombas's four sock silhouettes (Ankles, Quarters, Half Calf, Kids), with zero discount, zero urgency, and zero price mention anywhere. It's the least differentiated send in this brand's recent run: no launch narrative like Slouchy Rib ("YOU'RE S..."), no gym/court use-case hook like All Sport, just "favorite everyday pairs are back in brand new colors." That's a shrug of a value prop.
- The execution is clean and on-brand — the hero shot is warm and well-lit, the silhouette breakdown (Ankles/Quarters/Half Calf/Kiddos) with the block-color section headers is an efficient organizing structure, and the swatch rows under each section do real work showing color range without extra copy. But structurally this is nearly identical to the 07-16 All Sport and 07-15 Dressy Lightweight sends — same layout skeleton, same "Shop Women / Shop Men" button pairs, same trust-badge footer. Recipients who've opened the last several Bombas emails will recognize this shell instantly, and recognition without a fresh reason to click is a liability, not a strength.
- Judgment: functional, well-organized, but the weakest kind of Bombas email — a colorway refresh dressed up as a launch, with no offer and no urgency to compensate for a fairly generic subject line.

## What's working

- The four-section breakdown (Ankles, Quarters, Half Calf, Kiddos) with alternating photo/color-block layout is easy to scan and lets shoppers self-select by silhouette.
- Each section has its own Shop Women / Shop Men (or Shop Now for Kids) CTA pair, reducing friction versus one homepage-only link.
- Swatch thumbnails under each section clearly communicate "new colors" as a visual fact, not just a claim.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforce brand identity without hard-selling.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- No offer, discount, or urgency anywhere — this is pure browse-and-shop, which is a weaker ask than the 07-18 20%-off send that scored 8.0.
- The hero copy ("Your favorite everyday pairs are back in brand new colors") is generic filler; it doesn't say which colors, how many, or why now.
- Visually and structurally near-identical to the last several product-launch emails in this brand's cadence — same header treatment, same button pairs, same footer — creating fatigue risk for anyone who's opened multiple recent Bombas sends.
- The Kiddos section photo is charming but disconnected from the "new colors" narrative — no swatches shown for kids' colors like the other three sections get.
- Subject line "New Colors in All-Time Favorites" is accurate but not compelling — it under-promises relative to what a genuine best-seller refresh could sell (comfort proof, top-rated status, etc.).

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the hero a concrete hook — name the specific new colors or count ("6 new colors just dropped") instead of vague "brand new colors" copy; specificity is what's missing across nearly every element here.
- 2. Add a light incentive or bundle nudge (e.g., "Mix & match 4-packs" or a small first-time/loyalty discount) since there is currently zero price-based reason to act now — this brand already knows discount sends outperform (07-18 hit 8.0/10 largely off a visible 20% code).
- 3. Add a short social-proof line under one section ("Our #1 rated sock, restocked in new colors") to justify "All-Time Favorites" in the subject.
- 4. Differentiate the Kids section with its own swatch row like the adult sections get, so the visual language stays consistent end to end.
- 5. Vary the layout shell from the last 2-3 sends (different color-block treatment, different hero crop) to avoid template fatigue for repeat openers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Best-Sellers, Now in New Colors`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ankles, Quarters, Half Calf & Kids — all restocked in fresh shades.`
