# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Bombas knows what it's doing on mobile — clean grid, immediate category access, and the promo banner is right there at the top. But this feels like a brand talking to a new customer, not me — the woman who has been gifting your socks at every holiday for five years. The "Bombas Sport" hero is striking but it's not where my head is when I open this app looking to restock or grab a few extra pairs for my nephew's stocking.

## What's working

- **The "Socks" category tile** is exactly where my finger goes first — it's in the top-right of the grid, labeled cleanly, and doesn't make me dig through a menu.
- **The promo banner** is doing real work even if the 20%-off-first-order code isn't for me — the free shipping at $75 is relevant every time I stock up, and it's right there.
- **The four-tile category grid** is a genuinely good mobile pattern. No carousel friction, no guessing. I can see Socks, Slippers & Shoes, and T-Shirts all in one glance — that covers most of my annual Bombas gifting sweep.
- **The Bombas Sport hero** is visually arresting — multiple women in motion, white socks visible in several pairs of shoes. It signals that socks are central to the identity even when socks aren't the headline.

## What's weak

- **"20% Off First Order" is completely useless to me.** I've been a customer for years. Leading with a new-customer offer on what might be a returning-customer visit is a miss — there's zero acknowledgment that I exist.
- **No donation / giveback messaging anywhere above the fold.** The buy-one-give-one is why I became a Bombas person. If I hadn't already internalized it, nothing on this screen would tell me about it.
- **The cookie modal is eating the bottom third of the Bombas Sport hero.** Yes, it's dismissible, but on first load it covers enough of the page that the sport section reads as visual noise rather than content.
- **No reviews, trust signals, or social proof above the fold.** For a brand selling premium socks, a "4.8 stars / 50K reviews" badge goes a long way and there's nothing like that visible.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-customer hook in the banner strip** — swap the second line for something like "Rewards members: free shipping on every order" or show a loyalty balance for logged-in users instead of a first-order code.
- **Give the Socks tile its own hero or at least a badge** — "Our #1 product" or "1 pair donated with every purchase" on the tile itself would remind me why I'm here and why I keep coming back.
- **Move the giveback counter above the fold** — even a small "75M+ pairs donated" lockup next to the logo makes the mission visible without scrolling and it's the thing that makes me evangelical.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default after X seconds or shrink it to a bottom strip** — full-screen overlays on mobile kill the first impression; a slim persistent bar is less disruptive.
