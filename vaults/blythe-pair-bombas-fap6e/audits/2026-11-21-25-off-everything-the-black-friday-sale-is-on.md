# 25% Off Everything: The Black Friday Sale Is On
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday launch. "25% OFF EVERYTHING IS HERE" in heavy black and green type on cream is unmissable, and the Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit right under it. A subscriber knows the offer and where to click within a second.
- The problem is cadence. It is the same 25% sitewide deal as 11/15, 11/16 and 11/17, and then a 40%-off packs send on 11/18. ## What's working

- The hero is dominant. "25% OFF EVERYTHING IS HERE" reads instantly, and the offer is unambiguous.
- The Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit directly under the hero. They are dark green on cream and high contrast.
- The one-line subhead, "From your favorite everyday pairs to the coziest slippers, it's all 25% off," restates the offer in plain language and sets up the product grid.
- The "Something Cozy For Everyone" tiles use real lifestyle photography across women, men, kids, slippers, underwear and tees. The brand voice and seasonal tone are consistent with the earlier winter sends.
- The offer is honest. "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" is stated plainly near the footer, with no hidden catch.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline. Compare "ENDS TONIGHT" on 11/17 and "FINAL HOURS" on 11/18. This hero has no urgency at all.
- The offer is the same 25% as 11/15–11/17, with no escalation. Black Friday framing alone doesn't justify a fresh open.
- The "Popular Styles On Sale" grid is visually uneven. The slippers sit on white and the sock packs on black, so the packs look small and floaty. The captions are tiny, and there are no prices, sale prices or ratings.
- The lower half is button-heavy. The cozy tiles carry six more Shop buttons, then a repeated "GO GO GO" row of Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids links. That repeats the hero, and the blue underlined links clash with the rest of the design.
- The footer badges ("One Purchased, One Donated", "100% Happiness Guaranteed") are good trust signals, but they sit at the very bottom, after most readers have stopped scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Monday" or "Black Friday–Cyber Monday". That single line fixes the biggest gap, the missing urgency.
- 2. Give returning subscribers something new, such as an extra 10% off slippers as on 11/16, a free gift above a threshold, or early access for loyalty or text members.
- 3. Move the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" tile up near the hero. It is the only member-benefit hook, and it is currently buried.
- 4. Fix the product grid. Use consistent tile backgrounds, add "$XX → $YY" sale prices and star ratings, and cut to four products with a single "Shop Bestsellers" button.
- 5. Remove the redundant "GO GO GO" link row and either shrink or merge the cozy tiles.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
Confirmed issues:
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`.** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice (once after `lang="en" dir="ltr"` and once before `xmlns:v`). Duplicate attributes are invalid XML and HTML. Browsers and most email clients tolerate it, but strict parsers and validators will fail.
- **Redundant duplicate CSS rules in `@media only screen and (max-width:600px)`.** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice with conflicting values: `.mobile-third` has `max-width: 33%` in one declaration and no max-width in the other. This is low severity, since the later declaration wins and the effect is mostly harmless.
- **Generic `<title>Bombas</title>`.** It doesn't describe the content for assistive tech or web-view contexts. Low severity.

Checked, no issues:
- `lang`/`dir`/`xml:lang` are set.
- `role="article"` with `aria-roledescription="email"` and an `aria-label` are present.
- The viewport meta is present.
- `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared in both the meta tags and the `<html>` style.
- `prefers-color-scheme: dark` rules are present.
- The MSO conditional comments (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `PixelsPerInch`) are correct.
