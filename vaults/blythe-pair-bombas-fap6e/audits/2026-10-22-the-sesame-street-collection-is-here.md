# The Sesame Street Collection Is Here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a polished co-brand launch with warm, playful creative and a clear "Shop the Collab" button in the hero. It carries no price offer. The only incentive is the give-back line, "For every pair purchased, another is donated." That is on-brand but doesn't give anyone a reason to act today. It also reads as the seventh straight non-discount send in about a week, after "The Big Cozy Fall Drop" on 10/20 and the Saturday Suede Boot on 10/19. Only the 10/18 "up to 20% off" send broke that run.
- The copy contradicts itself. The subject says the collection "Is Here," but the first line of the body says "There's still time to shop the Sesame Street collection." That tells a subscriber it already launched and this is a re-send. The urgency is vague, and the novelty the subject promises is undercut.
- Verdict: good-looking, safe and forgettable. The Sesame Street IP is a strong hook, but the email doesn't use it for anything beyond a few themed blocks.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: a teal field, character-themed socks and a co-branded Sesame Street × Bombas lockup, with a yellow "Shop the Collab" button above the fold.
- The "C is for Comfort" and "Craving Something Cozy?" blocks use the brand's playful tone and alternating layouts, which gives the scroll a good rhythm.
- The "Where Kindness Meets Comfort" block ties the one-for-one donation to a warm family image. It is the most emotionally effective block in the email.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. The nav (Women / Men / Kids) includes Kids, which fits this collection.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer, and no price, discount or bundle appears anywhere.
- The subject says "Is Here" while the body says "still time," which signals a stale launch.
- The "Shop Now" buttons in the two mid blocks are small and don't say which product they lead to.
- The "COMFORT YOU CAN :COUNT: ON (AH, AH, AH)" pun is cute but hard to parse. It is followed by a plain underlined "SHOP THE COLLAB" text link that looks weak next to the hero button.
- The Women / Men split used in recent sends is gone. Nothing shows which product or size the collection covers (kids, adults, grippers).

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete reason to act now. Use a credible deadline or a bundle, such as "Free shipping on Sesame Street through Sunday" or a bundle price. Put it in the hero.
- 2. Fix the subject and hero so they agree. Say "still time" in both, or drop it from the body.
- 3. Name the product in each mid-block CTA ("Shop Kids Grippers", "Shop Slippers") and link each to a specific product page.
- 4. Show a price or star rating on a few hero products. Right now there is no social proof.
- 5. Replace the text-link CTA with a real button and shorten the pun headline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Sesame Street × Bombas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grippers, slippers and crew socks, and every pair donates one.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft, cushy socks for monsters of all ages. Shop before they sell out.`

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

2. Re-probe the six 429 links and the four unprobed ones with throttling and retry/backoff, or a longer time budget, to get definitive status and resolve the UTM/landing destinations.
