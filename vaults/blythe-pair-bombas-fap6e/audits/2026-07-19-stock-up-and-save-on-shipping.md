# Stock Up and Save On Shipping
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward free-shipping threshold email from Bombas — "Orders $75+ / FREE SHIPPING" in large hero type, followed by three department CTAs. The offer is clear and the layout is clean, but the email is functionally a one-trick pony: one trigger condition, one benefit, no product featured, no urgency, no end date. It reads as a mid-week gap filler after last week's 20% off code send (July 18), and it's a step down from that — free shipping is a weaker incentive than a direct discount, especially for a brand whose average cart likely already clears $75.
- The creative is competent but minimal. The sock lifestyle shot (a woman's leg in star-print socks against a warm orange background) is on-brand, but the product is incidental — there's no hero product being sold, no new launch attached, and no editorial reason to shop today versus tomorrow. "Signed. Sealed. Delivered." is a nice brand voice moment but it lands mid-scroll after the real message has already been delivered.
- Overall this email will surface the offer reliably to anyone with purchase intent, but it won't create intent. It's a retention nudge, not a conversion driver.

## What's working

- **Offer clarity is instant.** "Orders $75+ / FREE SHIPPING" in oversized type — the value prop is legible in under one second.
- **Three-CTA row is well-executed.** Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids as dark-pill buttons directly under the hero is a clean, frictionless department split.
- **Brand voice holds.** "Signed. Sealed. Delivered." is playful and consistent with Bombas's tone — a small earned moment in an otherwise utilitarian send.
- **Trust badges land appropriately.** One Purchased = One Donated and 100% Happiness Guaranteed reinforce the brand's values at the bottom without feeling bolted on.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- **No urgency whatsoever.** The offer has no end date and no countdown — there is zero reason to shop today rather than next week.
- **No product in the hero.** The sock is decorative; nothing links directly to a specific product. The email drives to homepages, not to curated collections or featured SKUs.
- **Weak anchor for a $75 threshold.** The email doesn't show what $75 gets you — no product bundles, no "here's what a $75 cart looks like." The threshold floats without grounding.
- **"Get Texted Exclusive Offers" badge is unexplained.** The SMS icon with no CTA or link reads as visual noise rather than a channel-switch opportunity.
- **Cadence concern.** This follows the July 18 "20% Off" code send by one day. Going from a hard discount to a softer shipping perk in 24 hours risks training subscribers to wait for the real deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** "Free shipping on $75+ — this weekend only" turns a passive perk into an action trigger. Even a 3-day window would lift clicks measurably.
- 2. **Feature a specific product or bundle to anchor the $75 threshold.** Show two or three best-sellers that together hit $75, with "Add all three — ships free" copy. Give the subscriber a shopping path, not a department menu.
- 3. **Elevate the subject line.** "Stock Up and Save On Shipping" is descriptive but weak — "save on shipping" is not a bold offer. Lead with the free shipping and the threshold in the subject itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Shipping on Orders $75+ — This Weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack Your Sock Drawer and Ship Free`
- 4. **Replace the decorative hero shot with a styled flat-lay of 3-5 products** at various price points that demonstrate how easily you hit $75. The lifestyle image is pretty but doesn't do selling work.
- 5. **Convert the SMS badge into a live CTA.** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers → Text JOIN to XXXXX" — or remove it entirely. A badge with no action is dead weight.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Orders $75+ ship free — here's what we'd fill a cart with`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Just hit $75 and ship free — all weekend.`
