# Back in Stock: Smooth, Seamless Cami
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas doing a genuine restock of a discrete, named product — the Smooth, Seamless Cami — with clean, confident hero art and a clear "sells out again" urgency hook. Against a stretch where Bombas has bounced between colorway drops (Ragg 8/17), restocks (Ultra Strong 8/14, Farmer's Market 8/19), and a best-of roundup (8/16), this one lands closer to the Farmer's Market send: a real "back in stock" moment on a product people actually search for, not a repackaging exercise.
- The execution is tight — bold typographic hero, one unambiguous CTA, a three-icon feature strip (Smooth & Flexible / Built-in Bra / Adjustable Straps) that answers the exact questions a shopper has about a cami, and lifestyle photography that actually shows fit. The "PLEASE AND TANK YOU" pun is a little forced but harmless.
- Where it falls short of a top-tier send: there's no offer at all — no price, no %, no urgency deadline beyond vague "sells out again" language — and the CTA area doesn't reinforce the "back in stock" news with any social proof (no reviews, no "your size is back" personalization). It's a solid, on-brand restock email, comparable to the Farmer's Market send but without that email's stronger seasonal/print hook.

## What's working

- Bold, legible hero headline ("THE CAMI IS BACK") that reads instantly even at thumbnail size.
- Single clear CTA ("Shop Now") with high-contrast button placement directly under the hero.
- Three-icon feature callout (Smooth & Flexible, Built-in Bra, Adjustable Straps) answers real product questions a cami shopper has before clicking.
- Lifestyle photography shows the cami styled three different ways (casual set, tank layer, going-out top), which helps a shopper picture wearing it.
- Trust/brand-value icons (one purchased one donated, 100% happiness guarantee) reinforce Bombas' known value prop without cluttering the hero.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere in the email — no % off, no price shown, nothing beyond "back in stock."
- Urgency is vague ("might want to scoop it up before it sells out again") rather than time-bound or stock-level specific.
- No social proof (star ratings, review count) despite this being a returning fan-favorite product — a missed chance to say "back by popular demand" with numbers.
- Secondary CTA is just "SHOP WOMEN," which dilutes focus away from the cami itself rather than deep-linking to the product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a specific urgency signal — "Limited restock" or "Only back for a limited time" with an actual constraint, or note prior sellout speed ("sold out in under a week last time").
- 2. Swap "SHOP WOMEN" secondary CTA for a cami-specific one ("Shop the Cami") so both CTAs reinforce the same product instead of splitting attention.
- 3. Add a one-line social proof callout under the feature icons (e.g., star rating or "10,000+ sold") since this is a returning favorite, not a new item.
- 4. Consider a small "back by popular demand" badge on the hero image itself to visually reinforce the restock story.
