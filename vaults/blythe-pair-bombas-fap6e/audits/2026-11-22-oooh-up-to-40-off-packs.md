# Oooh, Up to 40% Off Packs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, well-organized Black Friday packs send. "UP TO 40% OFF WITH PACKS" in heavy black type on a dusty-rose sock photo is the first thing the eye lands on. Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit directly under it, and a "25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE" strip anchors the bottom of the hero. The offer is honest and layered: 40% on packs, 25% on everything else.
- The body is a category tour: Casual, Cozy, Compression, Sport, Dress. It is easy to scan, but it is long, and the product shots are small and floating on a black background. There are no prices, no sale pricing, no ratings and no per-product CTAs. The email also repeats the 11/18 "Up to 40% Off Packs" send almost word for word. A subscriber who has had a week of 25%-off and packs emails (11/15 to 11/21) will see this as the fifth near-identical promo. The 11/19 ski and snow launch was the only variety.
- Verdict: solid execution and a clear hero, but low novelty and a thin product layer. It is an average send that mostly reuses the 11/18 offer.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: huge "40% OFF" type, a "WITH PACKS" qualifier and a "25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE" fallback.
- The Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit right under the headline, with no scrolling needed.
- The "Black Friday Sale" sticker badge ties the send to the season.
- The "Why stop at one pair? Grab a few of your favorites" copy explains the pack-savings logic in one line.
- The category blocks (Casual, Cozy, Compression, Sport, Dress) have bold headers and short descriptors, so a reader can jump to what they care about.

## What's weak

- Product tiles show names only, with no prices, no sale prices and no stars or reviews. The offer never shows up on the products.
- The product images are small and sit on a pure black background with low contrast. Their gray-on-black captions are hard to read.
- The email is very long, with five stacked category blocks and a lot of dead black space between them.
- The black section clashes with the warm, light hero and makes the lower half feel heavy.
- The footer CTAs "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN / SHOP KIDS" are small blue underlined links, a visual step down from the hero buttons.
- Fine print is hidden behind a small "See Offer Details" link under the closing line, and the email never says how long the sale runs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline and a price to the hero. For example, "Ends Monday: 40% off packs." Right now the urgency is missing entirely.
- 2. Show a price or "from $X/pair" on each product tile, plus a star rating on the bestsellers. Make each tile a "Shop the 4-Pack" button instead of a bare caption.
- 3. Cut the email to three categories, or lead with a single best-selling pack, so the hero product gets a deep link instead of every path going to a category.
- 4. Lighten the lower background or enlarge the product images, so the socks stand out and the captions are readable.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11/18 send. Use gift-pack framing, "stock up for the holidays," or a loyalty angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off sock packs, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy 4 pairs, save up to 40% (plus 25% off the rest)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Casual, cozy, compression and more, all bundled for Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on your favorite pairs, then save 25% on everything else.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 images.**
  - **Hero and product images (10):** all served from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`. They include `11566-berrysandmix-adult-female-layflat-1-transpar…`, `11565-alpine_sea_mix-…`, `floral-mix-layflat-product-transparent__2_.png` and `sea-rouge-mix-layflat-product-transparent.png`. Without alt text, images-off clients and screen readers get no label. Where the image is the only content of a link, the link has no accessible name.
  - **Tracking pixel (1):** `clicks.bombas.com/q/PfJV0_ZgAiVqymjW931F7w~~/…` is almost certainly the open pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute:** the `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. That is invalid markup, though most clients tolerate it.
- **Conflicting CSS in the `max-width:600px` block:**
  - `.mobile-half` is defined twice, as `width:50%; max-width:50%` and then `width:50%`.
  - `.mobile-third` is defined twice, as `33.33%/33%` and then `33%`.
  - The later rule wins, so the effect is minor.
- **Positive checks:** `role="article"`, `aria-label` and `lang` are set, and the `color-scheme` meta tags are present.
