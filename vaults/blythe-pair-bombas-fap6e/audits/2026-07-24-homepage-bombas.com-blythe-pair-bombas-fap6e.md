# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first screen and I'm immediately clocked — feet in socks, summer campaign, my favorite tab ("Socks") already right there in the nav strip. Bombas knows what it is: a sock brand that also does other stuff, not the other way around. The page is pushing a summer push with a cheerful "HELLOOO, SUMMER" headline, though that cookie modal is doing its best to body-block the moment.

## What's working

- **The hero image.** Multiple feet in colorful socks — mauve, charcoal stripe, gray/white stripe — shot from below with good light. That's exactly the visual that makes me want to restock.
- **Category pill navigation.** "Socks" is the second tap after "Bralettes & Underwear" — I can get to my category before the hero even finishes loading. Fast.
- **The banner strip.** "20% Off First Order with Code COMFORT20" plus free shipping at $75 stacked cleanly in two lines. The threshold is reachable in one sock haul for me, so even though the discount isn't for me as a returning buyer, the free-ship message lands.
- **"HELLOOO, SUMMER" headline energy.** The playful spelling and all-caps treatment is very Bombas-coded and very Millennial-friendly. It doesn't try too hard.
- **No aggressive interstitials.** The cookie modal is standard and dismissible — no email gate, no countdown popup. I appreciate the restraint.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal timing.** It drops right over the hero headline, cutting off "HELLOOO, SUM—" mid-message. Not a bug but bad timing — first impression is obstructed.
- **"20% Off First Order" is not for me.** As a loyal, multi-year Bombas buyer, the lead offer is literally targeted at someone who isn't me. The returning-customer experience starts with an irrelevant discount.
- **Zero loyalty/donation counter messaging.** The buy-one-give-one counter is part of Bombas' entire identity and the main reason I evangelize them. It's not visible anywhere on this first screen — that's a missed emotional hook for a repeat buyer like me.
- **No hero CTA button.** There's no "Shop Now" or "Shop Socks" button on the hero — just navigation pills. The call to action is ambient, not explicit.
- **Log In sits in the top nav quietly.** No "Welcome back" personalization, no rewards balance visible — the brand doesn't acknowledge I exist as a repeat customer.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-customer loyalty callout to the banner strip.** One line below the first-order discount: "Members: check your rewards balance →" — costs no space, speaks directly to me.
- **Surface the donation counter.** Even a small "X million pairs donated" badge overlaid on the hero or as a sub-headline below the hero image would do enormous work for word-of-mouth buyers like me. That number is the brand's heartbeat.
- **Add a single hero CTA button.** "Shop Socks" as a pill button over the hero image — doesn't need to be huge, just needs to exist so the eye has somewhere to land besides the category strip.
- **Time the cookie modal to fire after a 3-second delay** (or on scroll) so the hero gets one unobstructed moment above the fold on mobile.
