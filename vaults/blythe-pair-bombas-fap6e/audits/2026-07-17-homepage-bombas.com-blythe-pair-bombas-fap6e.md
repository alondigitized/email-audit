# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Bombas knows its lane — socks are front and center before I even scroll, and the "HELLOOO, SUMMER" energy is exactly the kind of playful, lowercase-millennial vibe I expect from this brand. But the cookie consent modal is eating my CTAs, and the fact that the only offer visible is 20% off a *first* order tells me Bombas is spending its homepage real estate recruiting newbies rather than rewarding loyalists like me. And — I'll say it — zero mention of the donation model above the fold feels like a miss for the one thing that makes me *evangelize* this brand.

## What's working

- **Socks-first hero image** — Two pairs of summery, print socks styled on real legs. I opened Bombas.com *because* of socks. I see socks immediately. That's a win.
- **"HELLOOO, SUMMER" headline** — Fun, warm, not trying too hard. Feels like something my friend would text, not a press release.
- **Top banner promo strip** — Clear, readable, stacked: discount code up top, shipping threshold right underneath. I don't have to hunt for either.
- **Four-category nav bar** — Bralettes & Underwear / Socks / Slippers & Shoes / T-Shirts. One tap to Socks. That's the hierarchy I want.
- **Seasonal relevance** — It's July. This is a summer campaign. They're not showing me a winter knit in 90-degree weather.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocking CTAs** — The entire lower half of my screen is a consent overlay. I cannot see or tap any CTA button. The first thing a new user sees after the hero is a legal wall, not a reason to click through.
- **"20% Off First Order" is wasted on me** — I've bought Bombas for four years. This offer literally does not apply to me, and it's the *only* offer visible. No loyalty reward, no returning-customer acknowledgment — I might as well be a stranger.
- **Zero buy-one-give-one messaging** — The BOGO donation model is *why I gift these at Christmas*. It's my talking point when friends ask why I spend $15 on socks. It's nowhere above the fold. Baffling.
- **No social proof visible** — I cannot see a single review count, rating, or "X pairs donated" counter. That counter used to be a big emotional hook. Its absence feels like a retreat.
- **Hero imagery shows legs only, no lifestyle context** — I get the art direction, but "legs and socks on a peachy background" is a product shot wearing a lifestyle hat. It doesn't show me how socks fit into a summer day or a summer gift stack.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the donation counter above the fold** — Even a one-liner in the banner strip: "Over 100M pairs donated." That single stat is Bombas's sharpest differentiator and best conversion tool for people like me. Ship it in the promo bar this week.
- **Add a returning-customer banner variant** — A/B test a banner for logged-in users that shows "Welcome back — your rewards balance is X" or a loyalty-exclusive promo. The COMFORT20 code is dead weight for anyone past their first order.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal to a corner, not a full overlay** — Or at least make the CTA buttons visible before consent is required. Right now the modal is literally eating the purchase funnel.
- **Add one sentence of mission copy to the hero** — Something like "Socks that give back — one pair donated for every pair you buy" directly under the headline. It's brand DNA. It should be there.
