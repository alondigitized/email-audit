# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is 100% my store, and Bombas knows it — the homepage opens straight on socks, summer patterns, and a clean-bright vibe that I associate with them. The brand looks confident and seasonal (HELLOOO, SUMMER is very Bombas). The frustrating part: a fat cookie-consent modal is sitting on top of whatever CTAs are below the hero, and the one big promotional hook visible is the "20% Off First Order" code — which does me exactly zero good as someone who has been buying these socks since 2017.

## What's working

- **The sock hero image** — three different pairs of summer socks (stripe, paw print, floral star), tight close-up, colorful background. As a sock evangelist this is exactly the "convince me I need all three" shot I want to see.
- **"HELLOOO, SUMMER" headline** — playful, brand-consistent, confirms the page is current. Bombas has always had this slightly enthusiastic voice and I'm here for it.
- **The flat nav category strip** — "Socks" is one tap from where I'm standing. No digging through a hamburger menu.
- **The top banner combo** — stacking the welcome discount AND the free-shipping threshold in one narrow bar is efficient. I clock both in half a second.

## What's weak

- **"20% Off First Order" is dead copy for me** — I'm a multi-year repeat buyer. This is the single most prominent offer on the page and it literally does not apply to me. There's no "member pricing" or "returning customer perk" in sight.
- **Cookie consent modal blocking the CTA zone** — the hero is great but the buttons below it are completely buried. I can't even see what they say. That's friction at exactly the wrong moment.
- **Zero donation/give-back signal above the fold** — this is Bombas. The one-for-one mission is why I converted in the first place. I expect to see a running tally or at least a nod to it on the homepage; there's nothing.
- **No social proof** — no review counts, no "best-seller" badge, no star ratings. For a brand that legitimately has cult-level sock fans, the homepage leaves that equity on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the first-order code for a loyalty-aware banner** — detect returning visitors (or just show both tiers: "New? 20% off. Back again? Free shipping starts at $50 today"). The current banner alienates the most valuable segment.
- **Add a one-liner donation counter** — even a "X pairs donated and counting" strip in the sub-nav or footer of the hero keeps the mission visible and reminds me why I'm loyal.
- **Dismiss or shrink the cookie popup on mobile** — move it to a slim bottom bar so it doesn't eat the CTA buttons. The buttons are literally invisible right now.
- **Drop a "Most Gifted" or "Fan Favorites" rail** — I'm a gifter, I buy in multiples, I want a shortcut to the packs I always give. A curated best-seller block would reduce friction to cart.
