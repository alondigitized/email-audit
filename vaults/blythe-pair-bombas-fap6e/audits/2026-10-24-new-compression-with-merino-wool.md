# New Compression With Merino Wool
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized product education email with no offer. The hero is strong: "COMPRESSION + MERINO WOOL" in big type over three legs in the new socks, with a Shop Women / Shop Men button pair directly below. Three blocks follow (Merino Wool Blend, Everyday, Travel). Each pairs a lifestyle image with a use-case line ("Great for: chilly weather / long shifts / travel days") and a 15-20mmHg spec, then shows women's and men's 3-pack product shots.
- The structure is the best part. The "Great for" lines turn a commodity product into a situation you can picture. The weakness is the same as in most of Bombas's recent sends, such as "New Dressy Showstoppers" (10/23) and "The Big Cozy Fall Drop" (10/20): there is no price, no deadline, and no reason to buy today. The 10/18 "Up to 20% off" send showed the brand can do an offer, and this one reverts to a catalog. The product tiles are also not obviously clickable, there are no prices, and the closing CTA is two small blue text links that are weaker than the hero buttons.
- Judgement: a good-looking, informative launch email that will earn browsing but not urgency. It is a 6, and it would be a 7 or 8 with a single offer line.

## What's working

- The hero names the product, the new ingredient and the "New" badge in the first screen, with Shop Women / Shop Men buttons right there.
- The "Great for: chilly weather / long shifts / travel days" lines make each block about a use case, not just a spec.
- The 15-20mmHg spec is repeated in each block, so the compression level is clear and consistent.
- The women's/men's split is parallel throughout, with each product tile labeled by name.
- The Wirecutter badge on the Everyday packs adds credible social proof.
- The layout is clean, with no visible render bugs or broken images.

## What's weak

- No offer, price or deadline appears anywhere, which continues the run of no-offer sends since 10/18.
- No product tile shows a price, so there is no reason to click through.
- The closing "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN" is small blue underlined text, much weaker than the black hero buttons.
- Only the Everyday section has social proof, and no star ratings or review counts appear.
- The email is long, with three near-identical product-pair blocks, so the Merino hero product is diluted by the Everyday and Travel packs, which are not new.
- The "One Purchased, One Donated" and "100% Happiness Guaranteed" badges are decorative and have no copy explaining them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive under the hero, such as "Free shipping on 3-packs" or a price-per-pair line, so the first screen includes a reason to act today.
- 2. Put prices on the product tiles, plus a rating line such as "4.8★ (2,100 reviews)".
- 3. Make the closing CTA match the hero with the same black buttons, and add a specific CTA such as "Shop Merino Compression".
- 4. Cut the email down to the new Merino product and feature Everyday and Travel in a smaller strip.
- 5. Explain the guarantee and give-back badges in a short line.

However, one tracked CTA ("Get Texted Exclusive Offers") returned a hard 400, seven images lack alt text, and the subscription and authentication headers could not be verified. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure:** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" returned **400** on `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/mIA1EM-AMxk_41uitaXmsg~~/AAQRxRA~/IVDZ3FW8…`. It is the only hard link failure. Possible causes are an SMS-signup destination that rejects non-browser requests, or a malformed or expired redirect target. Re-test it in a real browser or on a device before treating it as broken.
- **Unverified, not failed:** six links returned **429** ("Shop Women", "Shop Men", "SHOP WOMEN", "SHOP MEN", the MERINO WOOL BLEND product block, and the two `12124-sea_green_mix` image links). Rate limiting by the click-tracking domain is the likely cause, so these are not defects.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links were skipped after the 70s budget (`…/5ed9DGCj…`, `…/I1dAs9gF…`, `…/cWmprNr7…`, `…/vmHi60BX…`). Their status is unknown. In total, 22 of 34 links were never probed.
- **Redundant tracked links:** `12124-sea_green_mix` and `11495-chai_grey_mix` each appear twice with different `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…` IDs. This looks like image-plus-text link pairs, which is normal, but it splits click attribution across two IDs for one destination.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (7 images):**
  - `12124-sea_green_mix-adult-femalemale-knee_high-lay` (×2)
  - `11837-floralmix-adult-female-layflat-1-transparent`
  - `11836-searougemix-adult-male-layflat-1-transparent`
  - `11495-chai_grey_mix-adult-femalemale-knee_high-lay` (×2)
  - the tracking pixel `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/9qYqDs…`
- **Filename as link text:** the product images are wrapped in links, and the QA link text for them is `[image: 12124-sea_green_mix-…]`. Screen readers will announce the filename.
- **Pixel alt:** the open-tracking pixel should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Malformed `<html>` tag:** it declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. That is invalid for XHTML 1.0 Transitional and can trip strict parsers, though most clients tolerate it.
- **Duplicate CSS rules:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each defined twice in the first media query, with conflicting `max-width` values (50% vs. unset, 33% vs. 33.33%). The later rule wins, so this is low severity.
- **Done correctly:** `lang`/`dir`, `role="article"` with `aria-label`, viewport and `color-scheme` meta tags, MSO conditionals, and a hidden preheader ("Ache-relief meets cozy.").
