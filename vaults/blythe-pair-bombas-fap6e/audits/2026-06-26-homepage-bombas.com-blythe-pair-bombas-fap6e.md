# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Bombas is leading with summer sock vibes and a colorful lifestyle shot that honestly hits — two pairs of patterned crew socks against a warm pink-and-orange backdrop, with the headline "HELLOOO, SUMMER" below. Very on-brand, seasonally current. But there's a cookie consent modal sitting right on top of the CTAs, which is the first actual friction I'm hitting as soon as I land. And the only promo in the top banner is "20% Off First Order with Code COMFORT20" — useless to me, a multi-year repeat buyer.

## What's working

- **Sock-forward hero image:** Two pairs of patterned crews — one with red stripe, one with floral/star print — are exactly the aesthetic that gets me to stop scrolling. This is the product I'm here for, shown well.
- **"HELLOOO, SUMMER" headline:** Casual, warm, Millennial-friendly. Doesn't try too hard. Matches my reading register.
- **Category nav with "Socks" front and center:** It's one tap from landing to my category. That navigation strip — Bralettes & Underwear / Socks / Slippers & Shoes / T-Shirts — is clean and functional.
- **Dual banner value props:** Promo code AND free shipping threshold in a single strip. Efficient use of space even if the promo isn't for me.
- **Free shipping at $75+:** Easily reachable when I'm doing a stocking-stuffer haul. That's a useful anchor.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal covering the CTAs:** This is the worst timing. I land, I'm into the socks, I'm ready to tap — and a consent wall is sitting exactly where the call-to-action buttons live. The × is visible but it's small and the modal is large. Friction right at the point of intent.
- **"20% Off First Order" is dead air for me:** I've ordered from Bombas before. This offer doesn't apply to me and yet it's the most prominent copy in the entire top strip. No returning-customer offer, no loyalty reward, nothing. Feels like they forgot I exist.
- **Zero donation messaging above the fold:** The entire reason I tell friends about Bombas — one purchased, one donated — is not visible anywhere in this screenshot. For a new visitor this is a missed trust builder; for me it's a small but real disappointment, like the mission is now a footnote.
- **No trust or social proof signals:** No review stars, no "4.8 stars from 50,000 reviews" badge, nothing. For a sock brand competing on quality, that's a gap.
- **Log In has no reward hook:** "Log In" sits in the nav but it's completely plain. No "Log in to see your rewards" or balance indicator. Missed.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 5 seconds or trigger it on scroll** instead of firing immediately on load. The CTAs should be tappable the moment I land.
- **Swap the first-order promo banner for a returning-customer-aware message** (or at minimum add a second line: "Members: Log in for your rewards"). A/B test logged-in vs. guest banner copy.
- **Add one line of mission copy to the hero area** — something like "Every purchase = a pair donated" beneath the headline. It doesn't need a module, it needs one sentence. That's the emotional hook for buyers like me.
- **Surface a review count or credibility badge** near the hero — "Over 10 million pairs donated" or "4.8★ from [X] reviews" would do real work here.
