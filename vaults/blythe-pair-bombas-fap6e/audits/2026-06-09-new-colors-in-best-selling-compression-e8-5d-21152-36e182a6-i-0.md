# New Colors in Best-Selling Compression
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-colorway launch for Bombas's Everyday Compression line — not a sale email, which is a meaningful pattern break after five consecutive discount sends (June 1–7). The creative leads with a clean "Everyday Compression Refresh" hero, then walks through three product tiers — Medium Support, Comfort Cushion — each with gendered shop CTAs. That tiered structure is solid and educates the reader, but the email's core problem is that nothing here creates urgency or a reason to click today. New colors alone is a thin hook.
- Coming off four back-to-back archive-sale emails (peak: June 4's 40%-off launch at 9/10), this full-price re-introduction of a core SKU will feel like a step backward to anyone who just bought at a discount. The timing is awkward, and the email knows it — there's no offer, no badge, no social proof. It relies entirely on the product being desirable enough to pull a click at full price.
- Honest judgment: this is competent but inert. It does nothing wrong, but it does very little right beyond showing the product clearly. A subscriber who just survived four consecutive sale emails is unlikely to feel urgency here.

## What's working

- The hero copy "Everyday Compression Refresh" + "New Colors" is clean and specific — clear product focus, no vague language.
- Gendered CTAs (Shop Women / Shop Men) appear consistently throughout, reducing scroll-and-search friction.
- The tiered structure (Medium Support → Comfort Cushion) communicates product differentiation without requiring a landing page visit.
- Product imagery is clean and shows the actual socks clearly — colors and silhouettes read well.
- "LESS STRESSIN' MORE COMPRESSION" at the bottom is the most energetic copy in the email and would have been a better subject line.

## What's weak

- No offer — this is a full-price new-color email dropping the week after a 40%-off sale; the conversion gap is jarring.
- No urgency signal anywhere — no "limited colors," no "new this week" badge, nothing that creates a reason to click now versus later.
- No social proof — compression socks are a high-consideration purchase (fit, mmHg, use-case) and there's no review count, no star rating, no "best seller" callout.
- The mmHg callout ("15–20 mmHg") appears only in the Medium Support section and is tiny — the most important spec for a compression buyer is buried.
- The footer trust badges (100%, etc.) are too small to read at this render size and add no credible signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a reason to buy today.** Add a "New Colors, Same Day Delivery" or "Just Dropped — Free Shipping" hook to manufacture urgency. Full-price new colors with no hook is the weakest possible conversion setup.
- 2. **Make the mmHg spec the hero detail, not a footnote.** Compression buyers compare 15–20 vs. 20–30 mmHg actively; surface it in the hero or section headline, not in a caption.
- 3. **Add a review count or star rating** to at least one product module — "4.8 stars · 12,000+ reviews" would do more work than any copy here.
- 4. **Subject line is functional but flat.** "New Colors in Best-Selling Compression" reads like an inventory update, not a reason to open.

The leading `.` is absent. The selector matches an element named `mobile-third`, not the class `.mobile-third`. The class is correctly defined at the `@max-width:480px` breakpoint (`.mobile-third { width: 33% !important; }`), but the 600px breakpoint rule is dead code.
