# ⭐ 25% Off Sitewide: Your Guide to the Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built Black Friday roundup. The hero says "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in huge cream type over a plush red slipper, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly under it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the offer is easy to understand.
- The body works as the "map to the sale" the subject promises. It has four stacked tiles: packs at up to 40% off, ankle socks, the slipper shop and a Marvel gift collection. Each tile has its own gender-split buttons. It is organized and on-brand, but it is the fifth or sixth near-identical Black Friday send in a week. The 11/21 "25% Off Everything" launch and the 11/22 "Up to 40% Off Packs" email already covered this ground, and this one repeats both offers.
- The weak spots are the "Popular Styles On Sale" grid, which has no prices and no reviews, and a lot of button repetition. Overall it is a solid, honest, slightly redundant send.

## What's working

- The hero offer "25% OFF EVERYTHING" is unmissable, with the Black Friday Sale badge and the three shop buttons right below it.
- The "Consider this your map to the sale" line frames the email as a guide, which matches the subject.
- The tiles have clear hierarchy: a bold headline, one line of copy and category buttons on each.
- The slipper and cozy-sock imagery fits the season, and the "New Colors" sticker adds a reason to look at the ankle socks.
- The "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" line near the bottom is honest about exclusions.

## What's weak

- The email repeats the 25% off and 40% off packs offers from the 11/21 and 11/22 sends. It gives no new reason to act today.
- The "Popular Styles On Sale" grid shows no prices, no sale callouts and no ratings. The product images are small, so there is no social proof or price anchor.
- There are about 13 Shop buttons plus three text links. That much choice dilutes the primary action.
- Nothing in the email states a deadline. The urgency is only implied by "Black Friday."
- The Marvel tile is a gifting detour that doesn't tie to the sale offer, and it has no price or discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm", and a countdown if possible. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put price and discount data on the popular-styles grid, for example "$24 → $18" with star ratings, and link each item to its own product page.
- 3. Cut the secondary tiles to two and keep one button per tile, or send men and women to the best-seller.
- 4. Move the 40% off packs offer to the top as the tiered value story, since it is the stronger discount.
- 5. Tell the Marvel tile what to buy: a specific bundle with a price.
