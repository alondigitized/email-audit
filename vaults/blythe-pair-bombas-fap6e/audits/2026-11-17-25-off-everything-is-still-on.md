# 🚨 25% Off Everything Is Still On!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, honest last-call send. The hero reads "25% OFF SITEWIDE ENDS TONIGHT" in heavy black type on pale blue, over a row of festive socks. Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit directly beneath it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the deadline is credible: the footer says "Ends tonight, midnight PST."
- The body is a long gift-guide scroll, with Gifts for Her, Him and Kids blocks, each followed by four product tiles. It's well organized, but it's long for a final-hours email. The product tiles don't show prices or reviews, and the tile captions are small. Nothing under the hero adds a reason to act now beyond the deadline already stated.
- The 11/16 "Everything's Still 25% Off!" send made the same "ends tomorrow" point. This is the third consecutive day of the same 25% sale. The siren emoji adds urgency, but the offer is not new. The 11/16 "Extra 10% Off Slippers" send was the sharper one because it added a new incentive. Verdict: solid execution, low novelty, fatigue risk.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large, high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- "Ends tonight" in the hero is backed by a specific "midnight PST" deadline in the footer.
- The three Shop buttons right under the hero let the recipient pick a category immediately.
- The gifts-by-recipient blocks (Her, Him, Kids) match the holiday gifting mindset.
- The footer repeats the deadline and the three category links, which gives a second chance to click.

## What's weak

- There are no prices, sale prices, ratings or reviews on any product tile, so there is no social proof or price anchor.
- The email is very long for a last-call send, and the product captions are tiny.
- The 25% offer is the same one sent on 11/14, 11/15 and 11/16, so a subscriber sees nothing new.
- The footer deadline and offer exclusion ("Gift Cards Not Included | See Offer Details") are small. The footer CTA links are small underlined text, not buttons.
- The product tiles for the slippers and gift boxes link to individual items, but nothing marks bestsellers. That undercuts the hero's "best-selling gifts" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a countdown timer or a "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PST" banner above the hero. It makes the deadline unmissable and visually fresh.
- 2. Cut the body to a single "Bestsellers" row of 4 to 6 products with sale prices and star ratings.
- 3. Show the sale price on every tile, for example "$24 $18".
- 4. Make the footer CTAs real buttons and move the offer exclusions closer to the hero.
- 5. Add a new hook so it isn't the fourth identical sale email, such as free shipping with a delivery-by-Christmas cutoff.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 25% off socks & slippers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gift sets and best-selling slippers, all 25% off until midnight PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order tonight to get gifts under the tree in time.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, with a preheader, dark-mode meta tags, `role="article"` and responsive classes. The QA run found one hard link failure (HTTP 400), 12 images with no alt attribute, and an extremely long tracking-URL footprint. The header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be evaluated because the relay didn't capture the headers. The 38% pass rate reflects probing limits (429s, a 70s budget) more than confirmed breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure (400):** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link returned 400 on `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/Y5YRbtak6y0RFQuJjX1Btw~~/AAQRxRA~/8u0ExDV…`. This is the only hard failure. It is the SMS-signup CTA, so it needs a manual click-through. A 400 from a tracking redirector can mean a malformed or expired token, or the redirector rejecting the probe's user-agent. The data doesn't say which.
- **Inconclusive (429):** six links were rate-limited by `clicks.bombas.com`. These are "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", and the three "GIFTS FOR HER/HIM/KIDS" tiles. A 429 is not a failure, so don't count them as broken.
- **Unprobed:** 5 links were not checked because of the 70s budget (e.g. `…/ouI70TvZWIZ-A0pgfMipNA~~/…`). 38 more were skipped by ranking. Link coverage is therefore partial.
- **Tracking:** every link is wrapped in the click-tracking domain `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…~~/AAQRxRA~/…`. This is normal, but the final destinations can't be seen without following the redirect.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/TzbNeOVvDckKTxQtRhy-NQ~~/…` has no alt attribute. A tracking pixel should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 product images** (assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/…/ctfassets.net/…), for example `11647-skiersmix-adult-female-layflat-1-transparent` and `11583-alpine_red_mix-baby-unisex-calf-layflat-HO26`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show as nothing or as filenames.
  - If any image is the only content of its `<a>`, that link has no accessible name. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm this.
- **Tiny type on small screens:** `.font13` drops to `font-size:9px; line-height:9px` at ≤470px. `.font13Pad` is `9px` at ≤480px. Both are below common legibility minimums.
- **Duplicate attribute on the root element:** `<html … xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" … xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` declares `xmlns` twice. Mail clients tolerate it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Plain-text part:** 10336 of 12097 characters (about 85%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold the QA check uses, and long tracking URLs in the text part can hurt spam scoring and readability.
- **Positives, no issue:** the viewport, `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags, an MSO fallback style block, a hidden preheader, and `lang`/`dir` attributes are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `*|…|*`. The source is truncated, so the check is limited to the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw `.eml`.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is the same relay limitation, and nothing in the data shows a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** both fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. Because every URL is a `clicks.bombas.com` redirect, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified from the source or the probe results. The 429s also blocked following the "Shop Women/Men/Kids" and gift-tile redirects. The offer metadata (`DiscountOffer`, "25% OFF", ends `2026-11-17T23:59:00-05:00`) is consistent with the subject, but the probe never reached a landing page to confirm it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Manually test the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link** (the 400). Fix it if it is really broken. If it is a probe artifact, note that the probe user-agent gets rejected.
2. **Re-probe with throttling.** Space out requests to `clicks.bombas.com` and raise the time budget so the 6 rate-limited and 5 unprobed links get a real result.
3. **Add `alt` to all 12 product images**, using `alt=""` for decorative ones and descriptive text for linked ones. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Raise the minimum mobile font size** for `.font13` and `.font13Pad` from 9–10px to at least 11–12px.
5. **Remove the duplicate `xmlns`** on `<html>`.
6. **Inspect the raw message headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and read the full HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. **Follow the redirects** to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages once the rate limiting is dealt with.
