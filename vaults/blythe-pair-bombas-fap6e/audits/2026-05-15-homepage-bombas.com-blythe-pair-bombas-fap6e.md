# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I know this brand — I *am* this brand's customer — and my first thought is: where are the socks? Bombas has clearly pivoted to pushing their Sport line and expanded categories like bralettes and T-shirts, which is fine, but if I come home to bombas.com expecting the sock-first experience I love, I'm getting a sportswear brand instead. Clean, competent, but the social mission that makes me gift these every Christmas is nowhere in sight.

## What's working

- **"Socks" category tile** — it's in the first four tiles, big thumbnail of folded socks, one tap. I don't have to hunt. That matters.
- **"Free Standard Shipping on Orders $75+" banner** — this is exactly the nudge I need when I'm building a stock-up cart. $75 is a soft target for me, not a stretch.
- **Category grid layout** — four tiles, clean labels, no mystery-meat navigation. The mobile grid is actually better than most fashion brands I shop.
- **Clean render** — no layout shift, no broken images, no placeholder text. The page just loads and works.

## What's weak

- **BOMBAS SPORT as the primary hero** — I'm a sock evangelist. The hero is marketing athletic tops and leggings to me. I feel like I accidentally walked into the wrong store.
- **"20% Off First Order" promo banner** — I've been buying Bombas for years. This discount is literally not for me. It's the first thing I read and the first thing I mentally discard. Stings a little.
- **Zero give-back messaging** — "Buy One, Give One" is *why* I'm an ambassador for this brand. The donation counter, the shelter-pairs milestone, the impact language — none of it is visible. This is the brand's biggest loyalty driver and it's not on the homepage I can see.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I'm logged out and there's nothing to remind me I have history here. Just a bare "Log In" with no value proposition attached.
- **Cookie modal blocking bottom of screen** — it's obscuring whatever is below the BOMBAS SPORT hero; I can't tell if there's more relevant content underneath.

## Recommendations

- **Replace or split the hero with a socks-first unit** — BOMBAS SPORT can live below the fold. The above-the-fold hero should lead with socks (maybe a gifting or everyday-comfort angle) and carry the Give-Back story. Two sentences about donation impact would do more for my loyalty than any apparel photo.
- **Add a "Welcome Back" returning-customer banner** — swap the first-order 20%-off strip (or add a second strip) with something like "Members: your perks are waiting — Log In" or a loyalty-points reminder. I'm your repeat buyer; treat me like one.
- **Surface the donation counter or impact number** — even a subtle "X,XXX,XXX pairs donated" strip would re-anchor the brand promise. This is the detail I notice when it goes quiet, and it's gone quiet here.
- **Add a "Shop Socks" CTA to the hero** — if Bombas keeps the SPORT hero, at minimum plant an unambiguous secondary CTA over the hero image pointing to socks. Something like `Shop Socks` in a pill button would take 20 minutes to ship and immediately serve people like me.
