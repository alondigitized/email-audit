# Just Dropped: All New and Sooo Cozy ☁️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product-drop email with nothing to act on. "ALL NEW COZY" in heavy type over a wool-textured background is a strong hero, and the photo of four pairs of socks in oatmeal, mauve and grey fits the fall mood. The eye lands on the headline, then the three black buttons, then the photo.
- It has no offer, no named product, no price and no deadline. The only product information is one line of copy: "Wool yarns, cashmere blends, and sherpa linings." Every button goes to a gender category page. Compare the 2026-09-27 "Up to 20% Off Packs for Fall" send, which at least gave a reason to click now.
- Bombas has sent a fall-cozy email almost daily. 9/24 was "Feels Like Fall: Super Cozy Staples" and 9/29 was "The Perfect Fall Sock Is Here". This one covers the same wool, sherpa and fall ground without a new angle. It looks good, but it's a mid-funnel brand beat and it will draw fewer clicks than the promo sends.

## What's working

- The "ALL NEW COZY" headline is bold and legible. The hierarchy runs headline, then buttons, then photo.
- The lifestyle photo shows the actual textures: chunky knit, marled yarn and the honeycomb arch-support pattern. It reads as cozy without any copy.
- The three "Shop Women / Men / Kids" buttons sit above the fold with high contrast and clear verb-plus-noun copy.
- The materials line (wool, cashmere blends, sherpa) gives a concrete reason to care.
- The footer trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed, B Corp) reinforce the brand. I saw no broken images or layout problems.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, product name or deadline. "Just Dropped" has no urgency and no payoff.
- The CTAs are generic category links, and they appear twice: black buttons at the top and blue underlined links at the bottom. The second set adds nothing, and the blue underlined style clashes with the Bombas look.
- The Bombas script wordmark is cropped at the bottom of the hero photo and eats space that could show products. It looks decorative and may be deliberate.
- The ☁️ in the subject and the ❄ in the hero don't match. The cloud emoji also says "soft", not "fall".
- The body copy is one generic sentence. Nothing tells me which sock is which or what the new items are called.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name and link specific products. Replace the three category buttons with a grid of two to four named new socks. Each should have a name, price and "Shop [Name]" button, and the hero should link to the best seller. This one change would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Give the "Just Dropped" send a reason to act now, such as early access, a member price or a limited-quantity note. At the moment it's a catalogue page.
- 3. Cut the duplicate bottom CTA row, or turn it into something different such as a gift guide or a "shop by material" link.
- 4. Space this send away from the other fall-sock emails, or give it its own angle like "new colors" or "new cashmere blend". Four near-identical fall sends in a week trains people to skim.
- 5. Move the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" badge up. It's the only conversion hook in the email and it's buried in the footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is well-structured: it has a viewport meta, dark-mode support, `role="article"` and a preheader. However, the redirect-wrapped links could not be verified. One link returned 400, seven were rate-limited, four were unprobed, and the header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay. The source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Possible broken link (400):** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" (`https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/2lAOGLwHvJ8rTZux92_XPA~~/...`) returned HTTP 400. This is the only hard failure, and it is probably an SMS-signup CTA. The 400 could be caused by the probe (missing UA or params) rather than a real break. Treat it as unconfirmed and check it manually.
- **Rate limited (429), not confirmed broken:** "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "BOMBAS", "Just Dropped. ALL NEW COZY", "SHOP WOMEN" and "SHOP MEN" all returned 429 from `clicks.bombas.com`. The 429s indicate throttling of the click-tracker, not dead links. The QA run therefore verified none of the primary CTA destinations.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links were skipped when the 70s budget ran out (`.../0k-AgD8ok_...`, `.../m1vzJccSLfJdt...`, `.../UzQX7XknvULk...`, `.../yeaPfovDAmEbF...`). They are neither pass nor fail.
- **Redirect dependency:** every link, including the logo ("BOMBAS"), goes through `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...~~/AAQRxRA~/...`. These are long opaque tokens. If the tracker throttles clicks, as the 429s suggest, real recipients could be affected too, but the data doesn't show that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the image at `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/ZNG0uyj9LszE1-KyW3E0og~~/AAQRxRA~/WSTiCefDJ4eE...` has no `alt`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking/open pixel, in which case this is low impact. Confirm that it isn't a content image.
- **Invalid markup:** the `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. Duplicate attributes are invalid, and most clients ignore the error.
- **Very small mobile text:** `.font13` shrinks to `font-size: 9px; line-height: 9px` at max-width 470px (10px at 480px), and `.font13Pad` goes to 9px. Text this small is below readable and accessible minimums on mobile.
- **Preheader:** the second hidden div (the nbsp/zwnj filler) lacks `mso-hide: all`. It is probably harmless, but Outlook may render the filler.
- **No issues found with:** viewport, `color-scheme` meta, the `prefers-color-scheme` rules, `lang`/`dir`, the article role, the title, or the MSO conditional blocks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, for the same relay reason. The From domain `hello.bombas.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is normal.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** these could not be verified because the HTML is truncated before the footer.
- **Plain-text part:** 5664 of 6558 characters (86%) are URLs, which trips the >70% heuristic. This is driven by the very long tracking URLs. Low risk, but the text part is not meaningfully readable.
