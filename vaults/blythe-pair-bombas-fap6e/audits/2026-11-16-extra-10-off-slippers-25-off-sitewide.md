# Extra 10% Off Slippers! (+25% Off Sitewide)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the sharpest send in the recent run, and the reason is the offer stack. "Everything's 25% Off + Extra 10% Off Slippers" gives a returning subscriber a new reason to open. The last four sends (11/14 "25% Off Everything", 11/14 "25% Off All Our Top Styles", 11/15 "Up to 40% Off Packs", 11/15 "25% Off Sitewide: New Styles Just Added") all repeated the same 25%. This one adds a category-specific sweetener and a seasonal product that fits the "cozy" moment.
- The execution is clean. The hero is a pink slipper photo with heavy black "EXTRA 10% OFF SLIPPERS" type and three audience buttons. Below it, each slipper family gets a branded card with a star rating, a "New Colors" or "New Patterns" badge, and a women's/men's product pair. Nothing looks broken. The weak spots are the pricing math and the product tiles. The stacked discount is never explained, no tile shows a price, and there is no deadline. The sale is now five days old, so fatigue is real.
- Verdict: a strong category pivot on a tired sitewide sale. It would be better with one line of math and a visible end date.

## What's working

- The subject and hero lead with a new incremental offer ("Extra 10%") instead of repeating the 25% banner.
- The "EXTRA 10% OFF SLIPPERS" headline in heavy black type is the first thing the eye lands on, with Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons directly under it.
- Star ratings (4.6, 4.7, 4.8) and "New Colors / New Patterns" badges give social proof and novelty without extra copy.
- The women's/men's product pairs under each card make shopping by gender quick and show the actual product.
- The dark footer with the "Everything's 25% off" reminder and a repeated CTA row catches readers who scroll to the end.

## What's weak

- The stacked discount is not explained. It is unclear whether 10% applies on top of 25%, and "*Exclusions Apply" sits in tiny type under the headline.
- No price or sale price appears on any product tile, so there is no concrete number to anchor the savings.
- There is no deadline or urgency. After five days of "25% off", the email gives no reason to act today.
- The product tiles are small, and the captions are tiny white text on black. They are hard to read on mobile.
- The body copy "This is your cue to get cozy" is generic and doesn't say what the offer is worth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one line of math under the hero, such as "Slippers from $X after both discounts," and show a final price on each product tile. This would lift clicks more than any other change.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday midnight," in the hero or preheader.
- 3. Move "*Exclusions Apply" out of the tiny type and into a visible legal line directly under the buttons.
- 4. Make the product tiles larger and tap-linked to individual products, with legible captions.
- 5. Replace "Shop Kids" on the slipper hero if kids' slippers have little inventory, or route it to a real kids' slipper page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slippers just got an extra 10% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack it: 25% off + 10% more on slippers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sunday, Gripper and suede styles. Extra savings end Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our coziest slippers, now 35% off with the stack. Ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound (responsive, dark-mode meta, `role="article"`). The one confirmed failure is a 400 on the SMS-signup link. The 38% QA pass rate is mostly an artifact of rate limiting (429) and the probe time budget, so most links are unverified rather than broken. The source was truncated, and compliance headers and the footer were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed failure:** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" returns **400** at `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/T-vZBitAuELibeLUG79ukQ~~/AAQRxRA~/3UMJvkFTMN8f…`. This is the only hard failure. A 400 on a tracked redirect can mean a malformed or expired token, or a bot-rejection response, so re-test it from a real client.
- **Inconclusive (429):** "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "BOMBAS" (logo), and the three slipper hero and CTA links all returned 429 from `clicks.bombas.com`. These are not confirmed broken. The probe rate-limited itself against a single click-tracking host.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links hit the 70s time budget (`…/e8KMS00eldmHIEO7uWQoAw~~/…`, `…/3pbSPli3DubGPMOHrkXBWw~~/…`, `…/qwxK4pAgTdYhtjz-w9O46A~~/…`, `…/J_UQzmqEPewpOdyCiKdIsw~~/…`). 31 more were skipped as low priority. Only 12 of 47 links were probed.
- **Tracking:** Every link is wrapped in `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/<id>~~/AAQRxRA~/<blob>`, and the open pixel is `clicks.bombas.com/q/…`. The destinations are opaque, so the click-tracking wrapper hides the final URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (9 images):**
  - 8 product images from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…` have no `alt`: the `10895-feather_beige_leopard…`, `11289-darkespresso…`, `11265-berrymauve…`, `11265-midnight_navy_chevron…`, `11269-chailattehtr…`, `11301-deep_water_blue…`, `11259-camel…` and `11280-ganache…` files. If they are the only content of their links, screen readers will announce the link with no name or fall back to the filename or URL.
  - The 9th is the open-tracking pixel `clicks.bombas.com/q/UbxMAyf2…`. It should carry `alt=""`, but the omission is harmless in practice.
- **Malformed root element:** `<html>` declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. This is invalid XHTML, though clients tolerate it.
- **No issues found** with the viewport meta, `lang`/`dir`, the `role="article"` landmark, the hidden preheader, or the Outlook conditional blocks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so tokens further down are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message from a direct send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. From `bombas@hello.bombas.com` the From domain is a subdomain of `bombas.com`, which is consistent with alignment, but that is not verified.
- The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source and the QA output reports no unsubscribe result, so neither can be confirmed.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,220 of 10,986 characters (84%) are URLs. The long click-tracking URLs inflate the plain-text part. That is a spam-filter risk but not a compliance violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be verified. The tracking wrapper hides the destination URLs and any UTM parameters, and the probe did not follow redirects to final landing pages. The 429 responses blocked even basic destination checks for the hero and category links. The `DiscountOffer` microdata carries `description="25% OFF"` with `availabilityEnds` 2026-11-17T23:59-05:00, and no landing-page offer data was fetched to compare against it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link (400) from a real browser or client. If it still fails, fix the redirect token or the SMS-signup destination.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` on `<html>`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original headers rather than the relay copy.
5. Shorten or reduce the tracked URLs in the plain-text part.
6. For QA tooling: throttle probes per host, and raise the 70s budget or follow redirects with retries and backoff. That would separate 429s from real failures and show the final URLs and UTM parameters.
