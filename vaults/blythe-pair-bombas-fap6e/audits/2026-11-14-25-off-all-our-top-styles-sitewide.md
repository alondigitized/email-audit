# 25% Off All Our Top Styles, Sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the sale email the 11/13 teaser ("You're Invited to The Early Holiday Sale") set up. The hero is strong. "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in heavy black type on pink, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly under it, puts the offer and the action in the first screen. Nobody has to hunt for the deal.
- The body is a long, repetitive scroll. Five themed bands (Casual Comfort, Cozy Go-Tos, Everyday Compression, Bombas Sport, Fancy Shmancy) each repeat the same pattern: a model photo, two buttons, then two product tiles. That is a lot of vertical space for one message. The subject also says "top styles," but the body reads like a full catalog tour rather than a short list of best sellers.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand sale send that delivers on the teaser. It is held back by the length and by the lack of a real deadline. Compared with the earlier "25% Off Everything: The Early Holiday Sale Is Here" send the same day, it is close to a duplicate.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF EVERYTHING" hero is unmistakable and legible, with the offer and three audience buttons together above the fold.
- The "Fan Faves / Top Sellers" nav strip at the top matches the "top styles" promise in the subject.
- Each category band has a clear color identity (navy, forest green, rust, black with neon, burgundy), so the sections are easy to scan.
- Product tiles carry specific names and details, such as "Everyday Compression Knee High Sock 3-Pack (15-20mmHg)." That helps a shopper who knows what they want.
- The footer trust badges (One Purchased, One Donated; 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforce the brand without clutter.

## What's weak

- No deadline appears anywhere. "The Early Holiday Sale" is a label, not urgency, and there is no end date or countdown.
- The scroll is very long. There are five bands, each with two buttons and two product tiles, and the Fancy Shmancy and Sport sections sit far below the fold.
- Each band has two small buttons plus two linked products, which makes four click targets per band. That splits attention.
- "Gift Cards Not Included | See Offer Details" is buried in small footer text. The "Everything" claim has an exclusion that readers only find at the bottom.
- There is no review or social-proof content in the body. Bombas has strong ratings it could surface on the product tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real end date to the hero, for example "Ends Monday 11/17." This is the single biggest lift, because the offer is otherwise open-ended.
- 2. Cut the body to three bands, or show one hero product per band with a rating and a price after discount. A shorter email would hold attention to the end.
- 3. Show the sale price on each product tile, such as "$18 → $13.50." The email promises 25% off but never shows what that means in dollars.
- 4. Move the gift-card exclusion up next to the hero as a short line, so the "Everything" claim feels honest.
- 5. Add one line of social proof, such as a star rating or a "most-loved" tag, on the top sellers.
