- Structurally it's the same three-block hero-to-detail cascade Bombas has run all week (main hero → "Tons of Cush" feature → "A Lil' Scrunch" feature), just re-skinned with new copy and imagery. ## What's working

- Hero image is genuinely tactile — hands actively pulling on the sock, texture and knit detail clearly visible, strongest visual of the week's run.
- Dual CTA pattern (Shop Women / Shop Men) is present on every single block, so there's no ambiguity about where to click.
- Color-blocked sections (maroon → tan → blue) give clear visual separation between the three product beats without needing dividers.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no discount, no free shipping, no bundle deal — this is a pure product-awareness send with nothing to click for beyond "new colors exist."
- Third template repeat of "hero shot → feature block → feature block → dress for the weather" structure this week (matches 8/10 and 8/11 almost beat-for-beat).
- "DRESS FOR THE WEATHER YOU WANT" section is just bare text links (SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN) with no supporting image — a visual dead zone right before the footer.
- Subject "New Ragg Colors Just Dropped" is nearly interchangeable with 8/10's "New Wide Calf Colors for Fall" — same shape, same lack of specificity on what colors.

- 1. Add a concrete offer or bundle hook (e.g., "3-pack starting at $X" or a seasonal bundle price) — this is the single biggest lever; a well-shot product with zero price incentive is a soft click for anyone not already sock-shopping.

- 3. Replace the bare-text "Dress for the Weather You Want" section with a small image swatch strip of the new colors — gives the eye something to land on before the footer instead of a dead zone.
- 4. Vary the template shape from the last two sends — even swapping block order or adding a size/fit callout would break the "same three color-blocks" fatigue pattern.
