# 👀 25% Off Everything: Beat the Friday Rush
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent Black Friday send, but it is the second "25% Off Everything" email from Bombas today, after "Dig In: 25% Off Everything" (also 11/26). The hero is strong: "25% OFF SITEWIDE" in huge cream type on a terracotta panel, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons right under it. A reader gets the offer and the next step in about two seconds.
- The weakness is that the subject promises urgency ("Beat the Friday Rush") and the body doesn't deliver it. The only time cue is the small line "Why wait for tomorrow? Get a head start on Black Friday tonight." There is no deadline, no end date and no countdown. After 11/25's "Psst, 25% Off Everything's Still On" and 11/24's gifting sends, a subscriber has seen this offer four or five times in three days. The scroll below the hero is tidy but is mostly a recap of earlier sends.
- Verdict: well built and clear, but it is repeat creative with soft urgency. It will convert people already inclined to buy and add little for anyone else.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: a huge "25% OFF SITEWIDE" with a clear "Black Friday Is Here" kicker.
- Three audience buttons directly under the hero route women, men and kids with no friction.
- The "Trending Now: Sale Favorites" grid shows real products (Sunday Slipper, Holiday Half Calf 4-Pack, Gripper Slipper, Solids Ankle 4-Pack), which gives the email some merchandising.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF SLIPPERS" banner in magenta has its own Shop Now button, so the deeper discount stands out from the sitewide offer.
- No broken images or layout glitches, and the brand voice and colour system are consistent.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete deadline anywhere. "Tonight" and "Beat the Friday Rush" are vague, so nothing makes you act now.
- It repeats the earlier creative: sitewide 25%, the 40% slippers banner and the "Gifts for Your Whole List" block all appeared in sends over the past three days.
- The product grid has no prices, sale prices or ratings, and the product tiles don't look obviously clickable. The shoppable section gives no price reason to click.
- The "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" fine print is tiny and sits below the fold, away from the offer.
- The page runs long, with four stacked modules. The One Purchased One Donated block and the footer badge row add length without adding a reason to buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight at midnight ET", or a countdown. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Don't send two near-identical sitewide emails on the same day. If you do, give the second a different angle, such as a best-sellers send or a size and low-stock send.
- 3. Put prices and a "25% off" tag on each product tile, and make the tiles clearly linked.
- 4. Cut the gifting grid and the donation block, or move them lower. Lead with products and the slipper deal.
- 5. Move a short "25% off sitewide, gift cards excluded" line up under the hero so it reads as honest rather than buried.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Slippers 40% off + 25% off the rest, tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get Black Friday pricing a day early. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best-selling socks and slippers, all 25% off tonight.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
**Issues**
- **Duplicate attribute on `<html>`:** `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` appears twice (`<html xmlns="…" lang="en" dir="ltr" xmlns="…" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>`). This is invalid markup and a strict XML parser would reject it. Most email clients tolerate it, so the impact is low.
- **Sub-legible mobile type:**
  - At ≤480px, `.font13` is set to `font-size:10px; line-height:10px`, and `.font13Pad` to `font-size:9px`.
  - At ≤470px, `.font13` drops to `9px/9px`.
  - These sizes are below common 11–12px minimums for mobile legibility.
  - Line-height equals font-size, so lines will be tight or clipped.
  - Which elements use `.font13` can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice in the `max-width:600px` block with conflicting values (`max-width:50%` vs none, `33.33%` vs `33%`). The later rule wins, so the earlier `max-width` declarations are partly dead. This is cosmetic only.

- The `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags and the `prefers-color-scheme` rules are in place.
