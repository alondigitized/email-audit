# Fresh Colors: No Show + Cotton Modal Underwear
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident product-refresh send with no offer. The "THE COMFIEST UNDERWEAR" hero is big and bold. It has two model shots on a blush panel, Shop Women / Shop Men buttons directly under them, and a "New for Fall" tag. The eye lands in the right place, and the two-fabric structure (No Show, Cotton Modal) is easy to scan.
- The weakness is the same one as in the last week of Bombas sends. There is no price, no member perk and no deadline. Compared with the 10/27 socks send ("Your Favorite Socks in New Colors") and the 10/26 running-sock send, this is the sixth consecutive "new colors, no offer" email. Underwear is also a category shift away from the sock-led run, and nothing in the email ties it to a sock buyer.
- Verdict: well built, but it asks the reader to click on brand affection alone. It's a solid 6.

## What's working

- The hero is loud and unambiguous: oversized "THE COMFIEST UNDERWEAR" with paired Shop Women / Shop Men buttons.
- Each fabric gets its own colour-block module with a one-line benefit, "Barely-there feel and designed to disappear under your clothes" and "Breathable and incredibly soft, wash after wash."
- The product grids name specific items (Hipster 3-Pack, Plunge Bralette, Boxer Brief 6" 3-Pack), so the clicks have somewhere specific to land.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The "One Purchased, One Donated" and "100% Happiness Guaranteed" badges give trust cues near the bottom.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, bundle savings or deadline anywhere, so there is no reason to click now.
- The product tiles have no prices, no reviews and no star ratings.
- The mid-email product images are inconsistent. Some tiles are lifestyle crops and others are flat-lay packshots with a lot of white space.
- The closing "UPGRADE YOUR UNDERWEAR" block repeats the hero's two shop links as small blue underlined text. It looks off-style next to the black buttons.
- The men's half is thin. The Cotton Modal section has two flat-lay tiles and no model.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive. A "3-pack from $X" callout or a member price in the hero would lift click-through more than any other change. Without a discount, a "Free shipping over $X" line would still help.
- 2. Add price and star rating under each product tile.
- 3. Make the closing links match the hero: use the same black buttons, not blue text links.
- 4. Put model photography in the Cotton Modal section so the men's half matches the women's.
- 5. Space out new-colour sends, or give each one a different hook, as the last six days have all used the same "new colours" angle.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound (preheader, `lang`, `role="article"`, dark-mode meta and CSS, responsive breakpoints). The confirmed issues are one 400 on the SMS-signup link, missing alt text on 6 product images, and a duplicated `xmlns` attribute. Header-level compliance and final link destinations could not be verified from the capture. The QA pass rate is 38%, but most of that comes from rate-limited and unprobed links, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (400):** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" (`https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/r6X_idY5xx2Vaky_VK8L2A~~/...`) returned HTTP 400. This is the only hard failure. The link probably resolves to an SMS-signup flow that rejects non-browser requests, so a manual check in a real client is needed to rule out a bad redirect.
- **Rate-limited (429):** 6 links were throttled: "Shop Women", "Shop Men", the NO SHOW and COTTON MODAL tiles, "BOMBAS", "THE COMFIEST UNDERWEAR" and "New for Fall". A 429 is not evidence of a broken link, so these are unresolved rather than failed.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links hit the 70s probe budget and were never checked (`.../P35w8s4kmjNATe1pxFqfEw~~`, `.../9_tIxm50vNNiKSosYXRGJA~~`, `.../RfuuKfJkJWMgLrc_xzpGKw~~`, `.../rk8Clnhh_HllpfdGEA0xKg~~`). 24 links in total were skipped as low priority.
- **Tracking structure:** Every link is wrapped in an opaque `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/<id>~~/AAQRxRA~/<blob>` redirect. This is expected for ESP click tracking, but destination URLs and UTMs can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 product images):** all are served from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/...`. The filenames flagged are:
  - `8274-purplemist-adult-Female-onmodel-2-transparent`
  - `12074-olive_birch_mix-adult-female-mid_rise_hipster`
  - `8252-dark_olive_green-adult-female-plunge_bralette`
  - `purple-mist-dark-green-olive-layflat-product-trans`
  - `12062-navy_gray_sea_pattern_mix-adult-male-boxer_brief`
  - `plaid-skier-print-mix-layflat-product-transparent`

  With images off or a screen reader, these render as nothing or as filenames.
- **Tracking pixel:** the 7th flagged image (`clicks.bombas.com/q/G1xI5Cdi7kpcT3e7KQB-cQ~~/...`) is the open-tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""`, but this is low severity.
- **Invalid markup:** the `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice (`<html xmlns=... lang="en" dir="ltr" xmlns=...>`). Duplicate attributes are invalid, and some strict parsers will reject them.
- **Small mobile text:** at ≤480px, `.font13` shrinks to 10px with 10px line-height, and to 9px at ≤470px. `.font13Pad` is 9px at ≤480px. This is likely footer or legal copy, but it falls below common legibility minimums.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice with different `max-width` values. This is harmless but sloppy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. QA notes these are probably stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender failure. Check them in the original raw message. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the HTML is truncated, so neither could be confirmed. QA always probes an unsubscribe link, and none was reported failing.
- **Plain-text part:** 7,736 of 9,044 characters (86%) are URLs. This is a result of the long click-tracking URLs. A URL-heavy text part can raise spam scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The tracked redirects were not resolved (429s and unprobed links), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed. The one resolved failure is the SMS link (400).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Open "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" in a real client and confirm it reaches the SMS-signup page. Fix the redirect if it does not.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 6 product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.

4. Raise the mobile `.font13` and `.font13Pad` sizes to 11–12px or more.

6. Capture the raw RFC 5322 message to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Also confirm the footer has an unsubscribe link and a physical address.
