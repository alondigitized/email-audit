# 10% Off: Build Your Own Pack
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas back on the "Build Your Own Pack" discount beat, three sends after 2026-09-06's five-category "Up to 20% Off" roundup and 2026-09-07's tighter "Save Up to 20% With Packs" follow-up. The offer here is weaker in raw terms — 10% vs. the prior 20% — but the creative is the cleanest of the bunch: one hero block, "PICK FOUR FAVES AND SAVE," four sock product shots connected by plus signs, two clear CTAs, and the discount stated once in a clean scalloped badge. No five-category clutter, no roundup-of-roundups fatigue.
- The tradeoff is obvious to anyone who's seen the last two sends: this reads as a discount step-down. A subscriber who opened 2026-09-06 or 2026-09-07 expecting up to 20% off packs and now sees 10% may feel the brand quietly walked back its own offer, especially with near-identical "pack" framing three times in eight days. The body below the fold is mostly filler (trust badges, no second product moment) — there's no reason to scroll past the hero.
- Net: well-executed single-offer creative, undercut by a discount that's half of what this same list saw a week ago and cadence fatigue from three pack-themed sends in the run.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous hero message — "PICK FOUR FAVES AND SAVE" is instantly parsed, no competing headlines.
- The four-product-plus-sign visual literally shows the "build a pack" mechanic instead of just telling you.
- Two segmented CTAs ("Shop Women" / "Shop Men") right under the hero, both in a consistent brand-brown button.
- Offer is stated plainly in a badge ("10% Off Custom Packs") with no buried asterisks or fine print visible.
- No render bugs — clean spacing, sharp images, legible type throughout.

## What's weak

- 10% off is a step down from the 20% off packs offer this same list received on 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-07 — reads as a downgrade, not a fresh deal.
- Third "pack" themed send in eight days (09-06, 09-07, now 09-13) — the angle is showing real repetition fatigue.
- No Kids CTA despite "Kids" in the top nav — pack-builders with kids get no path in.
- Below the fold is pure filler (donation badge, happiness guarantee, text-offer prompt, help center) with no second product hook or urgency element to justify the scroll.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the email — nothing pushes a "shop now" over a "shop later."

## Recommendations

- 1. Either restore the 20% pack discount or justify the drop (e.g., frame 10% as a new "everyday" pack price vs. 20% as a limited flash event) — right now it just looks smaller with no explanation.
- 2. Add a Kids CTA button alongside Women/Men since the nav promises it and pack-building applies just as well to kids' socks.
- 3. Replace one of the generic trust-badge rows below the fold with a second product/styling moment (e.g., a "how to build your pack" mini-guide or bestseller pairs) to give scrollers a reason to keep going.
- 4. Add a light urgency cue (e.g., "this week only") to differentiate from the evergreen tone of the last few sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Mix, Match, Save: Your Custom Sock Pack`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick any 4 pairs and we'll knock 10% off — no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your go-to pairs, bundled and discounted.`
