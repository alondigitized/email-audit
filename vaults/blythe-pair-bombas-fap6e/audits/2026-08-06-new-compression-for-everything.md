# New Compression for Everything
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas launching a new compression sock category, and it's the clearest, most organized send in the recent run. The hero — "COMPRESSION MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT" over a five-person lineup styled for different use cases (office, yoga, travel, hiking) — sets up a genuinely useful mental model, and the body delivers on it cleanly: four named tiers (Everyday, Medical Grade, Travel, Sport) each with a compression-strength gauge icon, mmHg spec, and a one-line use case. That's real product information a shopper actually needs before buying compression socks, which is rare in this brand's recent emails.
- Where it loses points is sameness and length. Four near-identical section blocks (photo + tan/sage/green panel + gauge icon + two buttons), followed by two product-pair rows each, makes for a long, repetitive scroll with no visual break or ranking of what's most important. Nothing here is broken, but nothing surprises you either — it's a well-organized catalog page dropped into an email, not a hooked, prioritized send.
- Solid, useful, slightly monotonous. This beats 8/1 (Dinner Party), 8/2 (six-way grid) and 8/3 (hiking) on clarity and restraint, but doesn't hit the 7/31 Marvel collab's visual excitement.

## What's working

- New-category launch is instantly legible: "New Styles" kicker + bold "COMPRESSION MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT" headline states the offer with zero ambiguity.
- Each of the four tiers (Everyday, Medical Grade, Travel, Sport) pairs a compression-level gauge icon with a specific mmHg range and a plain-English use case ("Made to fight fatigue on long-haul flights") — genuinely useful sizing/fit-adjacent info for a category shoppers are often unfamiliar with.
- Gender-split CTAs ("Shop Women" / "Shop Men") are repeated consistently at every tier, keeping navigation frictionless throughout.
- Trust/loyalty footer icons (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed, Text for Exclusive Offers, Help Center) reinforce brand values without cluttering the main offer.
- No render bugs — clean product photography, consistent alignment, legible type throughout.

## What's weak

- Four back-to-back sections with the same layout (photo/panel/gauge/two buttons) plus product-pair grids make this a long, monotonous scroll — nothing signals which tier is new, best-selling, or most relevant to the reader.
- No price or offer anywhere — no %/$ off, no bundle price, nothing beyond "3-Pack." For a brand that leans on urgency in other sends, this one has zero.
- No social proof (review stars, "best seller" tags) on any of the eight product tiles, despite compression being a category where trust/efficacy claims usually need backing.
- "FIND YOUR PERFECT SQUEEZE" section near the bottom is just two text links (Shop Women/Shop Men) — a missed chance for a quiz or sizing-guide CTA given how education-heavy the rest of the email is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a single hero CTA tied to the most differentiated tier (Medical Grade or Sport — these are the true category-expansion story) instead of four co-equal panels; demote the other three to a compact grid below so the eye has one clear first move.
- 2. Add a starter price or intro offer ("3-Packs from $36" or "Free shipping on your first pair") — right now there's no purchase incentive anywhere in the email.
- 3. Replace the plain "FIND YOUR PERFECT SQUEEZE" text links with a "Find Your Fit" quiz-style button — it fits the educational tone already established by the gauge icons and would lift click-through for undecided shoppers.
- 4. Add a one-line review/rating snippet under at least the hero (e.g., "4.8★ from 2,000+ reviews") to backstop the medical/compression claims.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Compression Socks for Every Day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 Compression Levels, One New Category`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From everyday comfort to medical-grade support — find your squeeze.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Travel, workouts, recovery, and more. New compression socks are here.`
