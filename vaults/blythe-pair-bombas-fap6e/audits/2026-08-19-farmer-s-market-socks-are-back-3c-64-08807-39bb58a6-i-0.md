# Farmer’s Market Socks Are Back
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas doing a genuine restock with a real hook — Farmer's Market Ankle Socks are "back in stock" plus two new fruit prints (tomatoes/lemons return, cherries and strawberries join). Against a two-week stretch that's alternated between colorway drops (Ragg Socks 8/17, wide calf 8/8) and full relaunches (Dinner Party 8/11, Ultra Strong restock 8/14), this lands closer to the Ultra Strong send — a real "it's back" moment, not just a new hue on an existing SKU, and the produce-as-sock-print concept is genuinely fun and on-brand.
- The creative execution is strong: the hero shot of feet stacked on tomatoes and a lemon against mustard-yellow is distinctive and immediately legible as "farmer's market," and the die-cut "Back in Stock" badge does real work. But the email undersells itself structurally — no visible price or discount anywhere, the fruit-photo strip (strawberries/cherries) doesn't actually show the new sock prints on feet, and by the time you reach the two product tiles it's just plain white socks with no farmer's-market pattern visible in most of the shots.
- Solid, distinctive creative, thin on commercial urgency — a 6/10 send that could be a 7-8 with a price point and a clearer sock-on-foot product shot.

## What's working

- The hero image concept (socks balanced on produce) is genuinely clever and differentiates this from a typical flat-lay sock email.
- "Back in Stock" badge gives an honest, credible reason to open/click — this isn't manufactured urgency.
- The body copy clearly explains what's new (tomatoes/lemons return + two new fruits) without over-explaining.
- Clean visual hierarchy: hero → explainer copy → new-fruit teaser → product tiles → CTA, easy to scan on mobile.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) are visible without cluttering the main offer.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or bundle value anywhere in the email — a restock send with zero commercial hook beyond "it exists again."
- The two product tiles show a plain oatmeal-colored ankle sock and a tiny 4-pack thumbnail — neither clearly shows the tomato/lemon/cherry/strawberry prints the copy is hyping.
- "SHOP WOMEN" is the only real link besides the hero button — there's no visible path to men's or kids' Farmer's Market styles, which feels like an oversight for a "new batch" launch.
- The cherries/strawberries photo strip is just produce photography, not the actual new sock designs, so the "two new fruits" tease doesn't pay off visually.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a specific price or bundle callout (e.g., "4-Pack $XX" or "Save on the bundle") near the product tiles — right now there's no reason to click over just browsing later.
- 2. Replace the plain product-tile photography with on-foot or flat-lay shots that clearly show the tomato, lemon, cherry, and strawberry patterns — the current tiles don't deliver on the "new fruits" promise.
- 3. Add a men's/kids' CTA if those lines exist, or clarify in copy that this is women's-only if it is.
- 4. Swap the produce photo strip for a preview of the actual new cherry/strawberry sock designs, keeping the fruit imagery as a secondary accent rather than the whole panel.
