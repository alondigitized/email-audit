# A Lil' Guide to New Fall Slippers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas' cleanest structural send in a while — a genuine seasonal roundup ("Fall's Coming Fast") organized around three named products: Sunday Slippers, Gripper Slippers, and Suede Moccasins, each with its own hero banner, material callout, and matched men's/women's product shots. No render bugs, no lorem-ipsum, no broken layout — every section is legible and on-brand.
- But it's also the fourth-in-a-row Bombas send with zero price or discount anywhere in the email, following the exact pattern of the 8/26 catalog send and the 8/24 wool blend send. There's no urgency, no offer, nothing time-bound — just "here's what we sell." The suede moccasins even carry a "New" badge that's stale copy recycled from the 8/15 launch and 8/22 follow-up, so a subscriber who's opened those two already gets a third "new" claim on the same product.
- Solid seasonal creative, no commercial hook. Score reflects a well-executed but promo-less catalog email.

## What's working

- Strong seasonal hero: "FALL'S COMING FAST" with leopard-print slipper lifestyle shot immediately sets a seasonal frame.
- Clear dual CTA in the hero ("Shop Women" / "Shop Men") gives immediate path-of-least-resistance navigation.
- Three-product structure (Sunday, Gripper, Suede Moccasin) each gets its own material/benefit line ("Sherpa-covered and cushy," "Grippy and flexible," "Shearling-lined and luxe") — genuinely differentiates the products instead of one generic pitch.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — matched men's/women's pairs, no visual defects.
- Trust badges at the bottom (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforce brand identity without cluttering the main content.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or offer anywhere in the entire email — pure catalog browsing with no reason to act now.
- "New" badge on the Suede Moccasins is stale — this exact product launched 8/15 and was already re-promoted 8/22; a third "new" claim reads as recycled, not fresh.
- No urgency or seasonal deadline despite the "Fall's Coming Fast" framing — the copy implies timeliness but there's no actual time-bound hook (early access, limited run, ships-by date).
- Final CTA section ("Slippers, Sorted") is just two plain text links, a weak close after three well-designed product sections.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer — even a modest one (free shipping over $X, bundle discount on 2+ pairs) would convert this from a lookbook into an email with a reason to click today. This is the single biggest lever: four price-less sends in a row is a pattern worth breaking.
- 2. Drop or replace the "New" badge on Suede Moccasins — it's now the third time this product has been flagged new to the same subscriber base; swap for "Back in Stock" or a material fact if there's nothing genuinely new.
- 3. Give the hero a deadline hook — "shop before the first frost" is thematically there but not functionally urgent; add a dated promo window.
- 4. Strengthen the closing CTA block — replace the two plain text links with a styled button matching the hero's CTA treatment.
