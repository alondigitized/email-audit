# ⏰ Final Reminder: Up to 40% Off Select Styles
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth consecutive Bombas archive-sale email in four days — following June 4 (launch, 9/10), June 5 (follow-up, 6/10), and June 6 (reminder, 8/10) — and the law of diminishing returns is fully in effect. The creative is essentially the same cascading "UP TO 40% OFF" hero for the second day running; anyone who has been in the inbox all week will feel they're looking at a rerun. The one genuine addition that earns this send its keep is the hard timestamp: "SALE ENDS 6/8 · 12PM ET" planted prominently below the hero. That specific deadline is real urgency, not just "Final Reminder" copy.
- Execution is clean. No render bugs, three well-labeled CTAs, tight subject line. But the preheader ("Last reminder to save on select styles. Scoop 'em up while you can.") completely wastes the preview slot by echoing the subject's own messaging, and there is still no specific product with a before/after price to convert the fence-sitters this final-day send is meant to reach.
- Judgement: A mechanically competent, on-brand close to a well-paced sale sequence — but creative fatigue and a missed preheader opportunity cap its ceiling. Anyone motivated already clicked on days 1–3; this email is working a smaller, harder audience.

## What's working

- **Hard deadline is specific and prominent:** "SALE ENDS 6/8 · 12PM ET" gives an exact moment — far more actionable than yesterday's vague reminder framing.
- **Subject line is tight:** ~44 chars, specific % discount, single credible emoji, no spam stacking.
- **Three-CTA structure routes cleanly:** "Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids" avoids a generic homepage dump and respects the subscriber's segment.
- **Visual hierarchy is immediate:** The bold 40% OFF cascading hero is readable even thumbnail-small — no ambiguity about what this email is selling.
- **No friction:** Opens straight to the hero with no "view in browser" gate or broken-image gaps.

## What's weak

- **Preheader echoes the subject, adds nothing:** "Last reminder to save on select styles" after a subject that says "Final Reminder" is the same word twice. The preview slot is wasted on a fourth-day fatigued audience who needs a product hook, not another reminder label.
- **No new creative — this is day 2 of the same hero:** The cascading 40% OFF pattern is identical to June 6's send. Repeat openers will feel recycled.
- **No specific product featured:** A fence-sitter on day 4 needs "Our #1 crew sock: $18 → $10.80, sizes selling out" — not a generic discount banner. A named product with a crossed-out price converts harder than percentage alone.
- **Trust badges render too small:** The four icons below the CTAs (likely happiness guarantee, B Corp marker) are real brand equity but invisible at this size — they're not doing work for the close.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor one specific product with before/after pricing.** Add a secondary module: product name, original price struck through, sale price, and a stock-scarcity flag ("Only a few sizes left"). This is the single highest-lift change available on a final-day send to a depleted audience.
- 2. **Rewrite the preheader to deliver new information.** The preview slot should not re-echo "reminder." Use it for a product hook, a social proof line, or a quantity urgency callout that makes reopening feel worth it.
- 3. **Scale the trust badges to a readable module.** "100% Happiness Guaranteed" and B Corp belong as legible lines near the CTA block on a closing email — that's where hesitant buyers look for permission to purchase.
- 4. **Compress the hero creative on a repeat send.** Consider swapping the tiled 40% OFF pattern for a cleaner, single-shot product image to signal this is a different email — not the same one resent.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Broken CSS selector (`.mobile-third` missing leading dot):**

In the `@media only screen and (max-width:600px)` block:

```css
mobile-third { width: 33% !important; max-width: 33% !important; }
```

This is missing the `.` class prefix. It targets an element named `mobile-third`, which does not exist. Any element using `class="mobile-third"` in the email body will not receive this responsive width. The corrected form `.mobile-third` appears later in the same block — the first instance is dead code.

**Issue — `<title>` placed outside `<head>`:**

The `<title>Bombas</title>` tag appears after `</head><body>` opens (following the schema.org `<div>`). In strict XHTML 1.0 Transitional, `<title>` is required inside `<head>`. Outlook's HTML parser may ignore it; some clients render it as visible text. It should be moved inside `<head>`.

**Issue — Duplicate `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` declaration:**

One instance is in `<head>` with `charset=UTF-8`, a second appears in the body region with `charset=utf-8`. The duplicate is harmless in most clients but is malformed markup and should be removed.

**Issue — Duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`:**

```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ... xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">
```

`xmlns` is declared twice. Duplicate attributes are invalid in XHTML; parsers will silently drop the second occurrence, but this is a template authoring error worth correcting.

**No issue — ARIA and language attributes** are correctly applied on the wrapper div:

```html
<div role="article" aria-roledescription="email" aria-label="Email from Bombas" lang="en" dir="ltr">
```

**No issue — Dark mode support** is present via `@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark)` with `.darkbg`, `.darkcolor`, `.dark-mode-hide`, `.dark-mode-show` — correct pattern.
