# Up to 40% Off Select Styles: Archive Sale
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's archive clearance email — 40% off discontinued styles, the deepest discount they've sent since at least mid-May (compare to the 20% off pack promos on May 24–25). The hero executes well: bold "UP TO 40% OFF" typography, clean layout, hard deadline. Against the recent cadence — this is the seventh email in eleven days — the bigger number does give it a reason to exist.
- Where it falls apart is the body. There are zero product images below the hero. An archive sale, by definition, means specific inventory that's going away; not showing any of it is a missed conversion opportunity. The email is essentially a billboard with three category links. The top-banner promo code (COMFORT20, extra 20% off) is the most compelling detail in the email — stack it with the 40% and you're close to 52% off — and it gets one line in a header strip and disappears. That math should be the hero.
- A broken trust badge ("?") in the icon row is a quality-control miss on an otherwise clean send.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF" typography is unmissable — visual hierarchy delivers immediately.
- Hard deadline, "SALE ENDS 6/8 - 12PM ET," is specific and credible.
- Subject at 41 chars lands cleanly on mobile; no spam signals.
- Women/Men/Kids segmentation gives every subscriber a relevant lane.
- COMFORT20 stacking discount is genuinely differentiated from the May pack promos.

## What's weak

- Zero product photography in the body — an archive sale with no archive items shown.
- Stacking offer (COMFORT20 + 40% = effectively up to ~52% off) buried in a banner and never reinforced.
- One trust badge is broken, displaying a "?" placeholder icon — visible quality gap.
- "Archive picks" copy is vague — nothing explains what qualifies as an archive style vs. the regular site.
- Seventh email in eleven days; the subscriber's inbox patience is the real constraint here, not the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show the inventory.** Pull 3–4 specific archive SKUs with before/after pricing. The last email with actual product shots (the June 2 sheer floral socks, rated 8/10) outperformed the May pack promos that were just category links.
- 2. **Surface the stacking math.** "40% off archive styles. Stack COMFORT20 for an extra 20% — up to 52% off" is a real headline. Put it in the hero, not a header strip.
- 3. **Fix the broken badge** before it ships. One broken icon on a minimal-design email is disproportionately visible.
- 4. **Add the deadline to the subject.** "Archive Sale: 40% Off — Ends June 8" converts "archive sale" intrigue into actual urgency without adding length.
- 5. **Write a custom preheader.** The top banner is leaking as preview text in most clients; a purpose-built preheader tied to the deadline would lift open rate.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed bug — missing CSS class dot selector:**
```css
/* line ~44 in <style> block */
mobile-third { width: 33% !important; max-width: 33% !important; }
/* should be: */
.mobile-third { width: 33% !important; ... }
```
The missing `.` means `.mobile-third` elements will not collapse to one-third width on screens ≤600px. Three-column product grids will render at full width instead of tiling correctly.

**Duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>` tag:**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ... xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">
```
Technically invalid XML. Benign in most clients but will cause strict XML parsers to reject the document.
