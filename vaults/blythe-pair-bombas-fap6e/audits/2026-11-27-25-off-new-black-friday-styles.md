# 25% Off NEW Black Friday Styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Black Friday send, and its only real news is that it offers new styles. The hero is strong. "25% OFF NEW STYLES" sits in heavy black type with the red lightning bolt, a "Surprise Drop" badge, and Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons directly beneath. The eye lands on the offer first, and the festive sock photo sets the season.
- The problem is cadence and sameness. This is the fifth or sixth "25% off" send in about 48 hours, after "25% Off EVERYTHING: Black Friday Is On" earlier today and "👀 25% Off Everything: Beat the Friday Rush" on 11/26. The discount is identical. The new-styles angle is the only reason to open this one, and the body only half delivers on it. It features two products, a Merino calf sock and the Sunday Slipper, and says "Limited-Edition" for both. It's a fair send but not a standout. I'd rate it mid-pack, about 6.

## What's working

- The hero is loud and unambiguous: "25% OFF NEW STYLES" in huge type with the lightning-bolt motif.
- The "Surprise Drop" badge adds a bit of novelty and urgency, and it separates this send from the earlier "Everything" blasts.
- The Women, Men and Kids buttons appear immediately under the hero, which makes shopping easy.
- The two limited-edition story blocks (Merino Calf Socks, Sunday Slippers) are easy to scan and seasonally on point.
- Product tiles are labeled by gender ("Women's Merino Wool Blend Calf Sock 3-Pack"), so shoppers know what they're clicking.

## What's weak

- No prices appear anywhere, neither the original nor the sale price. The offer is "25% off" with nothing concrete in dollars.
- It's the fifth-plus 25% send in two days, so the "new" framing is the only freshness. The offer fatigue is visible to anyone who has been following along.
- The sock thumbnails are small and floaty, with a lot of dead white space around them. They look weak next to the big lifestyle blocks.
- The fine print is a small underlined "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" under the closing headline. It's easy to miss.
- The closing "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN / SHOP KIDS" links are small blue underlined text. They look unlike the hero buttons and are less visible than a button would be.
- There is no urgency or deadline. For Black Friday, nothing says when the sale ends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline, such as "Ends tonight" or "Ends Cyber Monday". Nothing in the email makes this offer feel scarce, and that's the biggest gap.
- 2. Show real prices on the product tiles, for example "$X → $Y with 25% off". This turns a generic discount into a concrete deal.
- 3. Link the hero buttons to the new-arrivals collection. Right now it's unclear whether they go to the new styles or the whole site.
- 4. Make the product images larger and tighter, and add a "Shop 3-Pack" or "Shop Slippers" button per block.
- 5. Turn the closing text links into the same button style as the hero.
- 6. Add a social-proof line, such as star ratings on the Sunday Slipper, which is called a "best seller".
