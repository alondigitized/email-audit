# Up to 40% Off: Load Up on Packs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest of today's Bombas sends on offer clarity. It's the only one that introduces a different mechanic, "Up to 40% Off With Packs," with "25% Off Everything Else" as a banner at the bottom of the hero. The oversized white-on-photo headline, the red Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons and the "Black Friday Sale" badge land the message in about a second.
- The body is a long category tour: Casual, Cozy, Compression, Sport and Dress, each with a banner and two product tiles. It's well organized and on-brand, but it's very long, and every tile is a small image with a tiny caption. Nothing in the body adds urgency, and the closing "TIME TO STOCK UP AND SAVE" has no deadline. This is Bombas' third send on 11/27 alone, after "25% Off NEW Black Friday Styles" and "25% Off EVERYTHING: Black Friday Is On." The pack angle is a real differentiator, but the cadence makes it feel like more of the same.
- Verdict: a solid, clear promo with a fresh hook. It's held back by no deadline, a very long scroll and sender fatigue.

## What's working

- "UP TO 40% OFF WITH PACKS" is large and high-contrast over the sock photo, so the offer reads immediately.
- The "25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE" banner keeps the sitewide deal visible without competing with the pack offer.
- The Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit directly under the headline, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- Category banners (Casual, Cozy, Compression, Sport, Dress) each have a short benefit line, which makes the pack angle easy to browse.
- Product captions name the exact pack size, such as "Women's Everyday Compression Knee High Sock 6-Pack (15-20mmHg)," so shoppers can see what they'd get.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No deadline appears anywhere in the body. "Time to stock up and save" is soft urgency on the last day of the sale.
- Product tiles are small and low-impact next to the big banners, with no prices, no "was/now" and no per-pair math to show what "up to 40%" actually means.
- The email is very long. The five-category structure pushes the closing CTA far below the fold.
- Only the closing section has a text link for "See Offer Details," so the 40% versus 25% exclusions are easy to miss.
- It's the third Bombas send on 11/27, after "25% Off NEW Black Friday Styles" and "25% Off EVERYTHING: Black Friday Is On," so it risks being skipped as a near-duplicate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight" or a countdown, in the hero and again in the closing CTA. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Show per-pair pricing on the product tiles, for example "6-pack from $X ($Y/pair)," and make the tiles larger and clickable.
- 3. Cut the body to the 2–3 best-selling pack categories and send the rest to the CTA.
- 4. Make the exclusion details visible near the hero, not just in the footer-level link.
- 5. Spread out the day's sends, or differentiate this one clearly, for example by making it a segmented "packs" send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Packs are up to 40% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stock up: 6-packs up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 25% off everything else. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Compression, cozy, sport & more, from $X/pair.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has sound responsive and dark-mode scaffolding, but the audit found one probable broken CTA (the SMS signup link returned 400), 11 images without alt text, and a duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`. The 38% QA pass rate is mostly an artifact of 429 rate-limiting on the click tracker. Most link destinations could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probable broken link (400):** "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" → `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/Nh5FXueVfKocxDesZrQQuQ~~/AAQRxRA~/XvCHLQR9…`.
  - The QA data shows no destination and no link text in the failure line. This is likely an SMS-signup link that fails for non-browser probes, but a 400 from the tracker means a recipient may hit it too. It needs a manual click-through.
- **Inconclusive, not failures:** six links returned 429 ("Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "BOMBAS", "UP TO 40% OFF WITH PACKS", "The Black Friday Sale. 25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE", plus "Why stop at one pair?"). `clicks.bombas.com` throttled the prober, so these are neither pass nor fail.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 49 links were probed. 37 were skipped by ranking and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget (`…/hyphYBtHkD6a9lWMbSexwg~~/…`, `…/YRZhxwozoicTJVNehg64fQ~~/…`, `…/etJIyJFFLQ4EL5Sn9y-5HA~~/…`, `…/AA6Laf57hkXN-OduaIE-Mw~~/…`).
- **Tracker URL length:** the tracking URLs run roughly 600–900 characters. This is the direct cause of the plain-text warning (10,612 of 12,325 characters, or 86%, are URLs).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 images.**
  - Ten are product images served from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/https://images.ctfassets.net/…`: `berry-neutrals-mix-layflat-product-transparent.png`, `7994-mixed-adult-4-layflat-1-transparent.png`, `11622-purple_red_mix-…`, `11616-rouge_green_mix-…`, `floral-mix-…`, `sea-rouge-mix-…`, `11841-berry_ganache_mix-…`, `11849-slate_green_mix-…`, `11922-cherry_coupe_mix-…` and `11882-argyle_dot_mix-…`. If these are linked product tiles, the links have no accessible name, and with images blocked there is no fallback text.
  - The eleventh is the open-tracking pixel `clicks.bombas.com/q/W9dX5It0…`. It is decorative, so it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate attribute:** the `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. This is invalid markup, and parsers or validators may reject or warn on it.
- **Present and correct:** `lang`/`dir`, `role="article"` with `aria-label`, `color-scheme` meta, `prefers-color-scheme` rules, MSO conditionals, the viewport meta and a preheader.
- The HTML source was truncated, so nothing beyond the head and first blocks could be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The remainder of the body was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the origin. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed through the relay. Verify against `hello.bombas.com` DNS or an original-message header.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA run always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure for it. The footer was truncated, so the postal address could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Could not be assessed. Every link is wrapped in the `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…` redirector, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs are not visible in the source. The 429s and 400 stopped the probe from following redirects. The offer metadata is internally consistent: `schema.org/DiscountOffer` has `availabilityEnds` 2026-12-02 and the description "UP TO 40% OFF".

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually resolve the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link on desktop and mobile. If it returns 400 for real users, fix the SMS-signup redirect.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ten product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute from `<html>`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from the original message, not the relay copy.
5. Re-run link QA with throttling or backoff for `clicks.bombas.com`. That would resolve the 429 links and the four unprobed links, and would let you check UTM parameters and landing-page continuity.
6. Consider a plain-text part with shorter or untracked URLs, or fewer links, to lower the URL ratio.
