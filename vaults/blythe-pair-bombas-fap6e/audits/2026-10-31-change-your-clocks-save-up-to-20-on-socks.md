# Change Your Clocks + Save Up to 20% on Socks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's first send in the recent run with an actual offer. "Up to 20% off" sits on a yellow badge in the hero and is repeated in the line under it. Since 10/24 the audited sends (merino compression, slippers, running socks, marl socks, underwear, merino color refresh) were all product-refresh emails with no discount. A real price hook after six no-offer sends is the strongest thing here. The "LESS LIGHT, MORE SOCKS" hero is witty and seasonal, and it ties to the daylight-saving change.
- The execution is weaker than the idea. The hero image is a stack of folded socks in a diorama. It doesn't show any product detail, and the offer is vague. The "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" block then shows two slippers, not socks. The slippers have no price, no deal and no link cue, and they don't match the sock-pack promo in the subject. The offer says "packs" and "up to 20%" but never says which packs or what the 20% applies to.
- Verdict: a clear, on-brand, seasonal send with the best hook in this run, held back by a vague offer and a mismatched middle section.

## What's working

- The "LESS LIGHT, MORE SOCKS" hero is witty and timely, and it connects to the clock change.
- The yellow "Up to 20% Off" badge sits inside the hero image, so the offer is visible immediately.
- Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids buttons sit right under the hero, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The bold dark hero and the heavy "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" banner give a clear hierarchy.
- The footer trust badges (One Purchased, One Donated; 100% Happiness Guaranteed; Help Center) reinforce brand trust.

## What's weak

- "Up to 20%" is vague. There's no sense of which packs qualify, no end date and no promo code.
- The product grid shows Sunday Slippers, not socks. That contradicts the "stock up on packs" message.
- The slippers have no price, no reviews and no sale marker, so there's no reason to click them over the hero buttons.
- "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" claims personalization, but it's the same two products everyone gets, split by gender.
- The "IT'S TIME FOR A CHANGE" block repeats the Shop Women / Men / Kids links a second time as small underlined text. It adds nothing, and the sun-icon banner above it is decorative filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer concrete. Name the packs and the deadline, e.g. "Up to 20% off 6-packs through Sunday." Put that in the line under the hero.
- 2. Replace the slippers with best-selling sock packs showing the sale price next to a struck-through original price. If the slippers stay, give them a clear role, such as a "Cozy add-on" label.
- 3. Add urgency, such as "Ends Sunday night" or "Extra hour, extra savings."
- 4. Add social proof under the products, such as a star rating or review count.
- 5. Cut the duplicate nav links and the sun banner so the product grid and the offer get more room.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra hour tonight. Up to 20% off socks.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall back: up to 20% off sock packs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up on 6-packs before the sale ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy packs for darker mornings, all on sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (email-client role/lang attributes, dark-mode metas, MSO conditionals). The QA run is only partly conclusive: the 38% pass rate comes mostly from 429 rate limits and a time-budget cutoff, not from confirmed dead links. The one hard failure is a single 400, and the compliance headers could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

It is probably an SMS-signup link, and a 400 from a click-tracker can be a probe artifact (the QA probe's request may be rejected as malformed or as a bot) rather than a user-facing break. It needs a manual click-through before it counts as a defect. The failure record also shows an empty link label before "(link text: …)", so I could not confirm what the link resolves to.

These are "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "Change your clocks, then stock up…", "BOMBAS", "LESS LIGHT, MORE SOCKS. Up to 20% Off" and "SHOP WOMEN". The probe hit the `clicks.bombas.com` tracking domain too fast. This is a probe limitation, not evidence of an email defect.
- **Unverified links:** Four links were never probed because the 70s budget ran out. They are `…/f/a/R9aE1dIP…`, `…/1exHKfzY…`, `…/R9Hnb05o…` and `…/7Q0MRdnR…`. Another 17 links were skipped by design (footer and social). Link coverage is therefore partial, about 12 of 29 probed.

- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also relay-dependent.
