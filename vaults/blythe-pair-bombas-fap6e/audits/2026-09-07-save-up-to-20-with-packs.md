# Save Up to 20% With Packs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas doubling down on the "Up to 20% Off Packs" beat from yesterday's send (2026-09-06), just tighter and simpler. The hero is clean — sock-clad feet, a burst badge, bold "PACK SAVINGS" type, and three clear category CTAs (Shop Women/Men/Kids) all above the fold. It's the most stripped-down execution of this discount message so far in the run.
- But the body collapses fast: after the hero, it drops to two tiny product thumbnails (Women's and Men's Solids Ankle Sock 4-Pack) with no price, no percentage callout per product, and a "SOCKS, UNDIES, + TEES THIS WAY" line that's disconnected from the "packs" promise the whole email is built on. The brand-values icon grid (donation, guarantee, texting, help center) eats the bottom third of the email — real estate that should be reinforcing the offer, not restating boilerplate.
- This is the third pack/discount email in four days with the same 20%-off framing and nearly the same badge treatment as 2026-09-06. Subscribers who opened yesterday's roundup will read this as a repeat, not a new idea. Solid top, thin middle, generic bottom — 5.5/10.

## What's working

- Hero is legible at a glance: badge ("Up to 20% Off"), bold headline ("PACK SAVINGS"), and three distinct CTAs all visible without scrolling.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, buttons are properly styled.
- "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" banner adds a personalization cue even if generic.
- Trust icons (donation match, happiness guarantee) are consistent brand signals a repeat buyer would recognize.

## What's weak

- No price or per-pack discount shown on either featured product — just "Women's Solids Ankle Sock 4-Pack" with zero pricing context.
- "SOCKS, UNDIES, + TEES THIS WAY" undercuts the pack-savings focus by pivoting to unrelated categories mid-email.
- Product thumbnails are small and visually secondary to a giant "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" black banner that says nothing concrete.
- Nearly identical offer/badge/copy to 2026-09-06's "Up to 20% Off Our Hottest Packs" — this reads like a resend, not a new send.
- Bottom third is pure boilerplate (donation match, guarantee, text signup, help center) with no offer reinforcement or urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the discount on the product cards themselves — show strikethrough/sale price or "20% off packs of 4+" directly under each thumbnail so the offer is where the eye lands second (after the hero).
- 2. Cut "SOCKS, UNDIES, + TEES THIS WAY" — it dilutes a single-minded pack promo into a generic catalog nudge. Replace with a second pack callout (e.g., a third pack style or a "mix and match" CTA).
- 3. Differentiate from yesterday's send — vary the hero art direction or add a specific end-date/urgency line since this is now the second near-identical discount email in 48 hours.
- 4. Shrink the trust-icon grid or move it fully below the fold; use the reclaimed space for one more product pack with visible pricing.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match, and save on 4-packs — ends soon.`
