# 25% Off Cozy Gifts for Your Whole List
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth straight "25% off" Bombas send since 11/21, and it's the first one built around gifting. The hero reads "25% OFF ALL THE GIFTS" in big cream type over a sock photo, and Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons sit directly under it. A "The Black Friday Sale" banner and the line "Your guide to gifts for everyone, organized by what they love most" frame the rest. The offer lands in about two seconds.
- The body is organized by recipient: classic style, cozy, always on their feet (compression), and sport. Each block has a colored panel and a photo, with a two-product row beneath it. It's tidy and on-season. The product rows show small packshots on black with tiny captions, and I can't see prices or any individual CTA buttons. The email also never says when the sale ends, so there's no deadline.
- Verdict: a competent gift guide that's a real change of angle after the 11/22 "Guide to the Sale" and the packs sends. But the offer and layout are the same as the last several, and the body gives you nothing to click except the generic shop buttons at the top and bottom. Fine, not special.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "25% OFF ALL THE GIFTS," is the first thing the eye lands on, with category buttons right below it.
- The gifting angle feels fresh after the "25% off everything" and packs sends.
- The recipient-based sections ("For the one with classic style", "For the one who loves being cozy") make browsing easy.
- The bold colored panels give each section its own identity and keep the long scroll rhythmic.
- Gift-box products (Ornament Gift Box, Merino Wool 4-Pack Gift Box) are on-theme for the season.

## What's weak

- The sale has no end date or other urgency, which is odd for a Black Friday send.
- The product packshots are small on a big black field, and the captions are tiny and hard to read.
- No prices, ratings or reviews appear on any product, so there's no sale-price anchor and no social proof.
- Nothing in the body is visibly clickable except the top and bottom shop links, and there's no per-section button.
- The black background with dark text-light captions makes the lower half feel heavy, and the footer links are small.
- Cadence is heavy: this is the fourth near-identical 25% message in three days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Cyber Monday" or a countdown, to the hero or the banner. That single change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Add a visible button to each recipient section ("Shop classic gifts", "Shop cozy gifts") and show sale prices under the products.
- 3. Enlarge the product images and captions, and add star ratings or a "best seller" tag to at least one product per section.
- 4. Include the stocking-stuffer or under-$X angle and the "gift cards not included" exclusion up high, so the fine print isn't buried at the bottom.
- 5. Space out the sends, or make them differ more in offer and creative.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound, with a role/aria-labelled wrapper, a dark-mode meta and CSS, MSO conditionals and a preheader. QA found one probed link returning 400, nine images with no `alt` attribute, and no authentication or `List-Unsubscribe` headers in the captured data. The 38% QA pass rate mostly reflects probe rate-limiting, not confirmed breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probable broken link (400):** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link returned HTTP 400 at `https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/_HMPeIE6mFZ4E16NnLZPag~~/AAQRxRA~/NVhp3OmK…`. It is the only hard failure. A 400 from the tracking redirector could mean a malformed or expired tracking token, or an SMS-signup destination that rejects non-browser requests. It needs a manual click-through.
- **Rate-limited (429), not confirmed broken:** "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Shop Kids", "BOMBAS", "25% OFF ALL THE GIFTS", "The Black Friday Sale" and "FOR THE ONE WITH CLASSIC STYLE…" all returned 429 from `clicks.bombas.com`. The server throttled the probe, so these links may be valid.
- **Unprobed:** 4 links were not checked because the 70s budget ran out (e.g. `…/CwUMevj-97nZzaFDJ8DhpQ~~/…`, `…/hab-62nqldRu4-fl5paOTQ~~/…`). 33 more were skipped by design as footer/social links. Link coverage is incomplete.
- **Tracking URL length:** the click-tracking URLs run to roughly 1,000 characters each. They are fine for HTML but bloat the plain-text part (see Section 5).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 9 images (WCAG 1.1.1):**
  - 8 product images from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/…`, e.g. `12038-cocoacloudmix-adult-female-layflat-2-transpa…` and `11645-heritagegreenmix-adult-male-layflat-2-transp…`.
  - The open-tracking pixel at `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/gqea2WiGKLp9loHcmgcC5Q~~/…`.
  - Product images with no `alt` get read out by screen readers by filename or URL. They also show nothing when images are blocked. The pixel should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate attribute:** the `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. This is invalid markup, and strict XML parsers or validators will reject it. Most email clients tolerate it.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each defined twice inside the same `@media (max-width:600px)` block, with conflicting `max-width` values (`.mobile-third` is `max-width:33%` then `33%`). This is low severity.
- Present and correct: `lang`/`dir` set, `role="article"` with `aria-label`, viewport meta, `color-scheme` meta, `prefers-color-scheme` rules and a hidden preheader div.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `*|…|*`. The source was truncated, so the lower part of the message could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements would be violated. Check the raw message at the sender's MTA.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The same relay caveat applies. The From domain is `hello.bombas.com`, and the tracking links use `clicks.bombas.com`, so the subdomains are consistent.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,504 of 11,212 characters (about 85%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is driven by the long click-tracking URLs. It is a minor spam-filter risk.
- A body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be confirmed because the HTML was truncated. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for one appears in the output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is wrapped in `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…` redirects, so the destination URLs and UTM parameters are not visible in the source. The probes either returned 429 or did not follow redirects to a final URL. No mismatch was found, but none could be ruled out. The 25%-off offer is also marked up as schema.org `DiscountOffer` with `availabilityEnds` 2026-12-02T23:59:59-05:00. Whether the landing pages show the same offer was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" and fix or replace the tracking URL if it returns 400.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`, and de-duplicate the `.mobile-half`/`.mobile-third` rules.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message headers, not through the relay.
5. Re-run link probing with throttling or retry-after handling to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed links. Follow redirects to capture final URLs and UTMs.
6. Consider shorter tracking URLs, or a leaner plain-text part, to reduce the URL ratio.
