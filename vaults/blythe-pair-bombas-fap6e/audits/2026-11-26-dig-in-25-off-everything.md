# Dig In: 25% Off Everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday send. The hero is "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in huge cream type on a maroon panel with a cyan lightning bolt. Two photos sit under it, kids in socks and a flat-lay of holiday socks, with three cyan Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids buttons. The eye lands on the offer first, and the CTAs are unmistakable.
- The body is weaker. The "Trending Styles" product grid shows small socks floating on a black background with tiny captions, no prices, and no visible buttons. The black canvas swallows the product shots. "Gifting Made Easy" is a decent four-tile block, but it is the third or fourth gifting beat in a week. The send is also near-identical to 11/25's "Psst, 25% Off Everything's Still On" and 11/23's "25% Off Cozy Gifts for Your Whole List". A real subscriber has now seen the same 25% offer for six days running.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but it is another repeat of the same offer. Nothing here gives a reader a new reason to open or click. The "Dig In" turkey angle is the only fresh element.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: "25% OFF EVERYTHING" is the first thing you read.
- The three Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons are bright cyan and high-contrast on maroon.
- "The Black Friday Sale" banner and the turkey line, "Before the turkey hits the table," tie the send to the season.
- The "One Purchased, One Donated" block and the 100% Happiness Guaranteed badge add brand trust.
- The Gifting tiles have clear, labeled category buttons, including a slippers tile.

## What's weak

- The four product tiles are small and lost on the black background. There are no prices, no sale price, and no visible button.
- The "Trending Styles" banner takes a large block of space but sends the reader nowhere obvious.
- The "See Offer Details | Gift Cards Not Included" fine print is tiny and sits at the bottom. "Gift Cards Not Included" is an exclusion you only find after scrolling.
- The footer link row ("SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN / SHOP KIDS") is a default blue that clashes with the palette.
- The gifting section repeats creative from the 11/23–11/24 gift sends.
- The lower half is long and repetitive, with several blocks competing for the same click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the floating product grid with product cards showing a crossed-out price, the sale price and a "Shop Now" button. Light card backgrounds would make the socks legible.
- 2. Add a deadline. "Ends tonight" or "Ends Cyber Monday" would give the 25% a reason to act on today. Without one, six days of the same offer train subscribers to wait.
- 3. Cut the Trending Styles banner and fold the gifting block into one tile row. This would shorten the email and sharpen the click path.
- 4. Move the exclusions, such as gift cards, up to sit with the offer so the terms read as honest.
- 5. Restyle the footer links to match the brand palette.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off everything, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Turkey Day deal: 25% off all the socks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our hottest Black Friday styles, all 25% off. Ends Cyber Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Holiday crews, slippers and gift-ready packs. Free shipping over $X.`

# Technical Audit: Bombas "Dig In: 25% Off Everything"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly well-built (preheader, `role="article"`, dark-mode meta, MSO conditionals). The confirmed defects are 5 images without alt text, a duplicate `xmlns` attribute and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The link, header and UTM checks are inconclusive because the probe was rate-limited and the relay dropped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Of 12 probed links, 7 returned HTTP 429 from `clicks.bombas.com`. A 429 is a rate-limit response, so these are inconclusive rather than failed. They are "Shop Women" (2), "Shop Men" (2), "Shop Kids" (2) and "Shop Slippers" (1).
- **5 links were never checked.** The probe hit its 70s budget before reaching `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/7vmZVhEo…`, `dmE8n8ez…`, `o2NrdGzX…`, `2v7ln7GP…` and `cqL---H5…`. Their status is unknown, not passing.
- **Redirect-wrapped links.** All CTAs go through `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/…`, so final destinations can't be read from the HTML. The Women, Men and Kids links each appear twice with different first tokens and a shared second segment (for example `8F5zY1fml4oM…` for Women). That suggests a repeated nav and CTA block, which is expected.
- **28 links were skipped by design** (footer and social). Nothing is reported about them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **5 images are missing alt text** (QA warning, confirmed):
  - `12108-fairislemix-adult-female-layflat-1-transpare…`
  - `11566-berrysandmix-adult-female-layflat-1-transpar…`
  - `11577-fairisle_mix-adult-male-calf-layflat-HO26-tr…`
  - `11565-alpine_sea_mix-adult-male-ankle-layflat-HO26…`
  - `clicks.bombas.com/q/j58cMAC74AX…`. This looks like the open-tracking pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
  - The four product images are content, so a screen reader or an image-blocking client gets nothing for them.
- **Duplicate attribute on the root element.** `<html … xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" lang="en" dir="ltr" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` declares `xmlns` twice. This is invalid markup. It is probably harmless in most clients, but a strict parser may reject it.
- **Minor CSS redundancy.** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are defined twice in the 600px media query. This has no functional impact.
- **Good practice found.** The source has `lang`/`dir`, `role="article"` with an `aria-label`, a preheader, `meta color-scheme`, and a `prefers-color-scheme` block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. Coverage is partial because the HTML was cut off.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender fault. It needs a check against the raw message at the original receiver. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. This is a relay artifact. The From domain `hello.bombas.com` with link tracking on `clicks.bombas.com` appears consistently branded.
- **Plain-text part is 8712 of 10130 characters URLs (86%)**, over the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate it, which can slightly raise spam-filter scores.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be assessed.** The footer was truncated from the source and no unsubscribe result appears in the QA output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
This could not be verified. The 429 responses blocked redirect resolution, so the UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. The embedded `schema.org/DiscountOffer` metadata (25% OFF, valid 2026-11-21 to 2026-12-02) matches the subject line's "25% Off Everything".

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute from `<html>`.
3. Inspect the raw original message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff for `clicks.bombas.com`, and cover the 5 unprobed links. Resolve the redirects to verify the final URLs and UTM parameters.
5. Improve the text-to-URL ratio in the plain-text part, for example by using shorter tracking links or fewer links.
6. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) and any merge tokens.
