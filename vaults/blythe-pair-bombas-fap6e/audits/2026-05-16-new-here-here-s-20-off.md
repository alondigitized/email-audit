# New Here? Here's 20% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Bombas is running a classic mission-led welcome email: the 20% new-subscriber discount is functionally buried in a small green top banner, and the hero real estate goes entirely to "ONE PURCHASED ONE DONATED" and the donation story. This is a deliberate brand choice, not an accident — Bombas has built its identity around the one-for-one model — but the tradeoff is that the discount, which is the stated hook of the subject line, never gets a visual payoff inside the body.
- The category browsing structure is smart: six horizontal product bands cover everyday, ache-relieving, slippers, performance, fashion, and value packs. That breadth works well for a welcome email aimed at an unknown-intent new subscriber. For someone who walks regularly, the functional copy ("ache-relieving wonders," "performance-enhancing marvels") is genuinely relevant and differentiated from generic apparel retailers.
- The email is clean and renders without obvious bugs. But it asks a new subscriber to browse without a nudge to actually use the code they were just promised, and there's no CTA that says "shop with 20% off" — only undifferentiated "Shop Women / Shop Men" buttons throughout. The email earns goodwill but leaves conversion on the table.

## What's working

- **Top banner is readable and code is explicit** — "COMFOR20" is spelled out at a glance, no hunting required.
- **Category breadth is well-matched to a new subscriber** — six product types with short, voice-forward descriptors give a complete picture of the brand without overwhelming.
- **Functional benefit copy** ("ache-relieving wonders," "performance-enhancing marvels") signals product quality and is directly relevant to comfort-seeking buyers.
- **Mission credibility at scale** — "200 million+ donations" with a "LET'S MAKE IT 250 MILLION" progress frame is specific enough to feel real, not hollow.
- **Render is clean** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping text or broken blocks visible.

## What's weak

- **The offer never gets a hero moment** — the 20% off promised in the subject lives only in the top banner; there is no body-level CTA that reinforces the code or creates urgency to use it.
- **No expiration or deadline anywhere** — without a "expires in X days" signal, the discount has no urgency to act now vs. bookmark and forget.
- **Every CTA is a category link, not a code-reinforcing action** — "Shop Women / Shop Men" repeated 12+ times dilutes focus; none of them say "apply 20% off" or even hint at the offer.
- **Mission-first hierarchy buries the conversion hook** — a new subscriber who skimmed the subject for the 20% off lands on a donation story; without scrolling, the connection to the code is lost.
- **No gender-neutral or "Shop All" entry point** — every CTA binary-splits to Women/Men, which may misalign with some new subscribers before they've signaled a preference.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a body-level offer banner or button** — immediately after the hero, place one large CTA: "Use Code COMFOR20 — Shop All." This is the single highest-impact change; the subject promises 20% off and the body never delivers on it visually.
- 2. **Add an expiration** — even "Code valid for 7 days" in the top banner and footer would meaningfully lift urgency without feeling deceptive.
- 3. **Reorder the narrative** — swap the hero: put a split image (product + mission badge) so the offer and the one-for-one story land simultaneously. Currently you must choose mission or offer; you don't have to.
- 4. **Collapse the CTA count** — six product sections × two buttons = 12 identical-looking buttons. Pick one or two hero categories, give them a clear design hierarchy, and let the others live in a secondary row.
- 5. **Personalize entry if possible** — "New here?" in the subject is fine but the body never reflects it. One line — "Since this is your first order…" — would close the loop from subject to body and increase perceived relevance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Welcome to Bombas — 20% off your first pair`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off code is inside (expires Friday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Socks that give back — every purchase donates a pair to someone in need.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code COMFOR20 at checkout. Good for 7 days.`
