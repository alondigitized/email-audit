# Up to 40% Off Packs: Stock Up on Your Favorites
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the follow-through on the Early Holiday Sale, and it works as a stock-up push. The hero reads "UP TO 40% OFF PACKS" in heavy black type over a pale blue sky and a sock shot, with "25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE" directly below. The tiering is clear: bundles get the deeper discount and everything else gets the base sale. It tells a returning buyer to go bigger on multi-packs, which is a sound way to lift order value.
- The body is a tidy category stack: Socks, Underwear, Tees, then the "Four Purchased, Four Donated" block. Each band has its own colored header and a Women/Men button pair. The product grids have specific pack names, so a subscriber can see what they'd be buying. Against the 11/14 sends ("25% Off Everything" and "25% Off All Our Top Styles, Sitewide"), this one adds a new reason to click, which is the 40% pack tier. That keeps the third sale-week email from feeling like a repeat.
- The weakness is that the discount never shows up at product level. There are no prices, no "was/now," and no indication of which packs get 40% versus 25%. The product images are small on a black background and the captions are tiny. The email is competent but low on urgency: nothing says when the sale ends.

## What's working

- The hero states a concrete tiered offer: "UP TO 40% OFF PACKS" with "25% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE" under it.
- Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit right in the hero, so the first screen has a clear action.
- The category bands (Socks, Underwear, Tees) use distinct colors, which makes the long scroll easy to scan.
- Product captions name the exact pack ("Women's Marl Ankle Sock 4-Pack"), which gives the click a specific target.
- The "Four Purchased, Four Donated" closer reinforces the brand mission and ends on a trust note.

## What's weak

- No prices or discount badges appear on any product. The 40% claim is never tied to a specific item.
- No deadline is visible, even though the sender name says "Early Holiday Sale" and the earlier teaser promised an event window.
- Product thumbnails are small and float on black with a lot of dead space, and the white captions are tiny.
- The footer link row ("SHOP WOMEN / MEN / KIDS") is in default blue underlined text on black, which looks off-brand and is hard to read.
- Women's and Men's buttons in each band go to category pages, not to the packs shown. The email promises specific packs, then sends you to a general category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a "40% OFF" badge and a price (or "from $X/pair") on every pack tile, and link each tile to its own product page. This would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Add a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Monday at midnight," and use it in the preheader as well.
- 3. Enlarge the product images and place them on a light or colored tile instead of black, so the sock and underwear detail reads.
- 4. Restyle the footer shop links in cream or white to match the brand.
- 5. Add a line of social proof, such as a star rating or "bestseller" tags, on the pack tiles.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
**Confirmed issues**
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute.** The `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice, along with `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o`. Duplicate attributes are invalid in XHTML/XML. Most clients are tolerant, but strict parsers and validators will flag it.
- **Redundant, conflicting CSS in `@media only screen and (max-width:600px)`.** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are each declared twice. The first `.mobile-third` sets `width:33.33%; max-width:33%`, and the second sets `width:33%`. The last rule wins, so the declarations are inconsistent and the first ones are dead code.
- **Second hidden preheader-padding div lacks `mso-hide:all`.** It uses `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden` only. The first preheader div includes `mso-hide: all !important`. Outlook desktop can ignore `display:none` on some elements, so the filler (`&nbsp;‌` repeated) may render as whitespace.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>Bombas</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor and only affects "view in browser" and some assistive-tech contexts.

**Present and correct**
- `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"` are set.
- `role="article"`, `aria-roledescription="email"` and `aria-label` are present.
- The viewport meta tag is set.
- `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared.
- The MSO conditional styles are valid.
- The Gmail `u+.body` hack is present.

I can't verify image `alt` text, since the tables and images are truncated. The dark-mode classes `.darkbg`, `.darkcolor` and `.darkBorder` are defined, but I can't confirm they're applied to any elements.
