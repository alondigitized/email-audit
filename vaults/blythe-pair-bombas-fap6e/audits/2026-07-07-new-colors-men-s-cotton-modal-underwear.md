# New Colors: Men’s Cotton Modal Underwear
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's Cotton Modal Underwear new-colors drop email — a clean, well-structured launch send that arrives as the sixth or seventh Bombas email in seven days, following the same "new colors" template the brand ran for Compression on June 30 and Running on July 2. The formula is familiar: dark hero, fabric feature section, product grid, buried-discount callout, closing CTA. It works, but it works on autopilot.
- The biggest missed opportunity is the positioning inversion: the hero leads with "FEEL THE SOFTNESS" (brand attribute) while burying "UP TO 20% OFF" mid-scroll. Underwear is a considered replenishment category — price and pack value are the real conversion driver, not the softness pitch. The June 30 compression send had the same flaw; this email didn't learn from it.
- The creative is render-clean, the product names and sizing are explicit, and "LIGHT & FLEXIBLE / Move freely in our Cotton Modal blend" is a credible feature differentiator. But at daily send frequency, a functional subject like *New Colors: Men's Cotton Modal Underwear* barely clears the attention bar.

## What's working

- **Hero is render-perfect**: dark background, male model, bold headline, "Shop the Drop" badge, and "Shop Now" button — no broken images, clean layout top to bottom.
- **Specific product names in the grid**: "Men's Cotton Modal Blend Boxer Brief 8" 3-Pack," "5-Pack," and single-unit names appear under each image — sizing detail visible without clicking.
- **"LIGHT & FLEXIBLE" feature callout** breaks up the product grid and gives the send a functional story, not just a color catalog.
- **Pack-savings mechanic explained**: the "UP TO 20% OFF / That's how much you can save with packs" block gives the discount context and pushes the multi-unit basket behavior Bombas wants.

## What's weak

- **20% off is buried mid-scroll**, not in the hero — the discount that should close the sale is competing for attention with the fourth product tile rather than anchoring the open.
- **Subject is purely informational**: "New Colors: Men's Cotton Modal Underwear" reads like a warehouse update, not a pitch. Bombas landed "THE 4-PACKS OF JULY" four days ago — this subject is a significant step back.
- **Daily send cadence is working against open rates**: this is the brand's seventh email in seven days; "Cadence feels right" is false for any engaged subscriber.
- **No social proof**: no star ratings, no review count, no "best-seller" badge — underwear is a tactile product where social proof matters for new colorway adoption.
- **"FEEL THE SOFTNESS" CTA copy is too generic**: "Shop Now" inside the hero and "Shop Now" at the footer are interchangeable; neither tells the user what to expect on click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the hero with the offer, not the attribute.** Move "Up to 20% Off in Packs" into the hero subbody or swap the hero CTA to "Shop Packs & Save 20%." The softness message can live in the feature section where it already belongs.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to carry the discount.** The 20% pack offer is the hook — it should be in the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save up to 20%: New Cotton Modal Colors`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New underwear colors — pack up and save 20%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh cotton modal colors just landed. Buy in packs to unlock the discount.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boxer briefs, new colorways, same breezy fit — up to 20% off when you pack.`
- 3. **Add a best-seller or review signal** under the lead product tile — even "4.8★ from 12,000+ reviews" pulls uncertain buyers over the line for an underwear replenishment.
- 4. **Differentiate the final CTA copy.** "TIME FOR AN UNDERWEAR UPGRADE → Shop Now" is interchangeable with every other Bombas footer. Replace it with "Build Your Pack" or "Get 20% Off in a Pack" — a verb + benefit + product.

## 1. Overview


The leading `.` is missing, making this an element selector (targets a literal `<mobile-third>` element, which doesn't exist).
