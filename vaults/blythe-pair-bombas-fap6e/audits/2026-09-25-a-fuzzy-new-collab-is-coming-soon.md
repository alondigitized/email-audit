# 🍪 A Fuzzy, New Collab is Coming Soon
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser, not a product email — Bombas x Sesame Street, and the CTA isn't "shop" or even "save the date," it's "give us your phone number." That's a big ask for zero concrete product info: no launch date, no price, no pack detail, just four Muppets on a cream card and "Dropping Soon." Compared to the brand's recent run of sock roundups and slipper showcases (2026-09-20, 2026-09-19), this is a genuine change of pace — a licensed IP collab is more attention-grabbing than another compression-sock cut — but the email itself does almost no selling. It's pure anticipation-building, and it reads that way.
- The craft is clean: the illustrated Sesame Street characters (Rosita, Cookie Monster, Elmo, Oscar) are on-brand and charming, the dotted border and cream card feel distinct from Bombas' usual black-and-white grid, and "SEE YOU ON THE STOOP" is a nice bit of voice. But there's no reason to open this over any other Bombas email except curiosity about the collab itself, and the single CTA (SMS opt-in) is friction for anyone who just wants email updates.
- Judgement: a fine teaser for existing superfans, weak for driving action from a casual subscriber — it should have paired the reveal with at least a hint of scope (kids' line? adult socks? release week?) to justify the SMS ask.

## What's working

- The four-character illustration grid is instantly recognizable and charming — strong IP usage without looking like clip art.
- Dual-logo lockup (Sesame Street street sign + Bombas wordmark) clearly signals a real, official partnership.
- The cream/dotted-border card is a nice departure from Bombas' usual black-and-white template, giving the collab its own visual identity.
- "SEE YOU ON THE STOOP" is a fun, on-theme line that ties the Sesame Street reference into Bombas' brand voice.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) are present for brand credibility even in a teaser.

## What's weak

- Zero product detail — no hint of what's actually being made (socks? slippers? kids or adult?), so there's nothing to get excited about beyond "Muppets exist."
- No date, not even a vague one ("this fall," "next week") — "Dropping Soon" carries no urgency.
- The only CTA is an SMS signup, which is higher-friction than an email click-through and excludes anyone who doesn't want another text channel.
- Subject line cookie emoji doesn't clearly connect to Sesame Street (cookie = Cookie Monster, but it's a stretch as a subject hook) and undersells the actual news (a licensed IP collab is a bigger deal than the emoji implies).
- No countdown, waitlist page, or landing content shown — just redirects the click into another opt-in loop, which risks a big drop-off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give one concrete detail — a release week, a category ("kids' socks," "the whole family"), or a teaser price — so the CTA has something to anchor to. Right now there's nothing to be "first to know" about beyond the logo pairing itself.
- 2. Make email itself a capture path, not just SMS — add "Notify Me by Email" as a secondary CTA under or beside the SMS button so subscribers who don't want texts aren't locked out entirely.
- 3. Lean harder into the Sesame Street nostalgia in the subject line — the current one buries the actual news.
- 4. Add a date-bound tease ("Landing the week of Oct 6") even if vague, to convert "dropping soon" from filler into urgency.
