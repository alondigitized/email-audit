# 25% Off Everything: The Early Holiday Sale Is Here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the send the 11/13 teaser ("You're Invited to The Early Holiday Sale") set up. The hero finally delivers the offer: "25% OFF EVERYTHING" in huge cream type on terracotta, with the "Early Holiday Sale" scalloped label above. The eye lands on the offer first. Three model photos and Shop Women / Shop Men / Shop Kids buttons give a fast category split. It's the clearest Bombas send in this run.
- Below the hero it loses some steam. The "Popular Styles On Sale" grid has two sock products that nearly vanish into the black background. Neither the products nor the section carries a sale price, so the 25% never attaches to anything concrete. The fine print ("Gift Cards Not Included | See Offer Details") is small, but it is disclosed. The dark footer works fine.
- Judgement: a strong, honest sale announcement that is better than the last six sends. It should be 7 or higher, but the product grid and the missing deadline hold it back.

## What's working

- "25% OFF EVERYTHING" is the dominant element, so the offer is unmissable.
- Three-way Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons in salmon on terracotta are clear and tappable.
- The terracotta and cream palette matches the 11/13 teaser, so the two sends read as one campaign.
- "Something Cozy For Everyone" tiles show the range: socks, slippers, underwear, tees.
- The exclusion is stated plainly under the footer headline, with an offer-details link.

## What's weak

- The sock product images in "Popular Styles On Sale" are transparent-background cutouts on black. They are small and hard to see, while the slipper photos sit on bright white boxes. The grid looks uneven.
- No sale prices, no strikethroughs, and no ratings on the featured products.
- No end date or urgency. "Save on good stuff before it goes" is vague. Last send said "Starts Saturday," and this one doesn't say when it ends.
- The "Better to Be Fashionably Early" footer link row is blue underlined text on black, which looks like a default style and is low contrast.
- The "Popular Styles" products and the sale hero don't link to a single hero item. Only the product captions are visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday" or "Through 11/17", so the offer has credible urgency.
- 2. Show sale prices on the product grid, such as "$14 $18.67", and add a star rating to each.
- 3. Put all four products on the same light tile so the sock images don't disappear into the black.
- 4. Restyle the bottom links as salmon buttons, matching the hero.
- 5. Move the "Gift Cards Not Included" exclusion up under the hero line.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Socks, slippers, tees and more. Sale ends Sunday.`
