# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I've been a Bombas customer for years and I know this brand's visual language — clean, confident, a little utilitarian. Right now the homepage is doing what it always does: category grid, promo banner, sock lifestyle shot. It feels safe and familiar, but I'm immediately clocked as a new customer based on that "First Order" offer, which is a small miss when I have a full order history with them.

## What's working

- **Socks tile placement** — top-right, equal prominence with underwear, slippers, and T-shirts. I see my category in about half a second.
- **Sock lifestyle photo** — two pairs of mesh ankle socks in earthy/navy tones. This is exactly the kind of product photography that makes me pause. Real texture, real styling, not a flat lay.
- **Dual promo banner** — "20% Off First Order" + "$75+ free shipping" in one strip. High information density, no scroll needed.
- **Clean nav** — hamburger + search + log in + cart. No clutter. I can get anywhere from here.

## What's weak

- **"First Order" framing excludes me entirely** — I'm a repeat buyer who stocks up annually. This promo is invisible to me and subtly signals the brand is in acquisition mode, not retention mode.
- **Zero donation/give-one messaging visible** — Bombas built its identity on the buy-one-give-one story. Not a single word about it above the fold. For me, that's a miss. It's part of why I gift these and buy in bulk.
- **No loyalty hook** — No rewards callout, no "you have X points," no member pricing signal. Feels like a stranger's homepage, not mine.
- **Cookie consent modal** is blocking the bottom third of the screen, obscuring any content below the sock lifestyle image. First impression is partially obstructed.
- **No seasonal relevance** — It's late May, Memorial Day weekend territory. No sale event, no limited-time hook, nothing that makes today feel different from any Tuesday in February.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "First Order" for a loyalty/returning-member banner variant** — if I'm logged in (or the URL carries an email signal), surface "Stock up for summer — member pricing unlocked" or at minimum hide the new-customer promo for returning visitors.
- **Add one line of donation copy to the promo strip** — "Every pair purchased donates a pair to someone in need" costs zero visual real estate in the banner and is the reason half your loyalists are loyalists.
- **Anchor a Memorial Day sale or a "summer socks" campaign tile** in place of one of the four generic category tiles — right now T-Shirts feels like filler for me in that grid.
- **Lazy-dismiss the cookie modal** or move it to a slim bottom bar — the current overlay kills the sock lifestyle image, which is the best thing on the page.
