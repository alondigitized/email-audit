- The hero image and headline promise "COMPRESSION IN BLOOM" florals, but the pictured sock is subtle pink with barely-visible dots — the actual floral pattern only shows up in a small secondary thumbnail lower in the email, a mismatch between headline and hero visual.
- No offer or price anywhere — pure product/education email with nothing to create urgency to click now.
