# Up to 20% Off Trending Packs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Bombas's fifth email in nine days, and the creative is finally doing some merchandising work. The "Save on Popular Packs" hero gives me a real reason to look — packs are the highest-AOV format, and "up to 20% off" pinned to bestsellers is a credible promise. The four tiled sections (Daily Drivers, Relief & Support, Cue The Ooohs & Aaahs, Ready. Set. Go.) segment by use-case, each with dual gender CTAs and product swatches underneath. It's the most useful piece of inventory navigation they've sent this week.
- The downside is volume fatigue. After Sunday's "we picked these for you," Saturday's risk-free guarantee, Friday's testimonials, and Thursday's welcome 20%, this lands as the fifth pitch in a row. The format is also nearly identical to the May 19 send — same use-case tile architecture, same dual-gender split CTAs, same swatch grid. If I'm not buying after four emails, the fifth doesn't earn its open.
- Verdict: solid creative, lazy cadence. The packs angle is genuinely new but the wrapper is recycled.

## What's working

- Hero is clear: orange block, two-line value prop, "Shop Women / Shop Men" buttons render correctly.
- Use-case framing (Daily Drivers, Relief, Cushioning, Performance) makes the catalog scannable.
- Color/style swatches under each tile show actual variety — feels like a catalog, not a banner.
- "New + Trending" tag pinned to the hero gives the promo a freshness reason.
- Footer "OOOH, HOW TRENDY" mini-tile (heart / pride / star) adds a soft-merchandising tail without competing with the hero.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth email in nine days; "Up to 20%" after a 20% welcome offer feels redundant.
- "Up to 20% off" is hedged — no tile actually shows a struck-through price, so the discount is invisible until PDP.
- Every section's CTA is generic "Shop Women / Shop Men" — no product-specific entry point.
- No urgency or deadline — "Trending" is the only time signal and it's vague.
- Hero "SAVE ON POPULAR PACKS" buries the discount number; the "up to 20% off" sits in 8pt subhead under the buttons.

## Recommendations

- 1. Surface the discount in the hero headline, not the subhead. "20% Off Best-Selling Packs" beats "Save on Popular Packs."
- 2. Show one actual struck-through price on at least one tile — proof the discount exists.
- 3. Add a deadline ("Through Memorial Day" — it's May 24, the holiday is sitting right there).
- 4. Replace one of the four use-case tiles with a single-hero product card linking to a specific bestseller, so there's a non-homepage click target.
- 5. Pull cadence back — skip a day before the next send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off our best-selling packs — through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pack deal: 20% off Daily Drivers, Relief, Performance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Memorial Day pricing on the four packs everyone reorders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Daily Drivers, Relief & Support, Performance — 20% off the packs.`
