# Homepage snapshot · bombas.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Oh, they're running an Archive Sale — this is exactly the kind of thing I open email alerts for. The hero is a full sock party: different styles, colors, varied skin tones on the legs, yellow background popping hard. It reads as a brand that knows its product is the story. That said, the cookie consent wall is sitting right over the hero copy like an uninvited house guest, and I can't see whether there's any donation-counter or BOGO messaging anywhere, which, as a Bombas loyalist, I'm already quietly clocking.

## What's working

- **The sock hero image**: multiple styles, textures, colors across different legs and skin tones — this is exactly what I look at when I'm deciding which new colorways to add to the collection. It stops the scroll.
- **Archive Sale banner**: top-of-page, high contrast, dark background. Clear offer, visible immediately. "Up to 40% off" is the kind of signal that makes me tap before I even read the rest.
- **Free shipping threshold**: "$75+" is surfaced right in the banner alongside the sale. For me, a multi-pack sock order easily clears that — it removes the "is this worth it" friction early.
- **Category grid**: four clean buttons — Socks is right there, one tap. No buried nav required.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero**: the banner-text "UP TO" is cut off mid-sentence because the cookie overlay is eating the bottom third of the screen. I don't know if that's "40% off" or "10% off" until I dismiss the modal. That's real friction on the most important message on the page.
- **Zero donation/giving messaging above the fold**: as a buy-one-give-one buyer first, this is the thing that made me a Bombas person. I don't see it anywhere. Not in the banner, not in the nav, not in the hero. For a new visitor this might not matter; for me it's a small betrayal of what the brand is supposed to lead with.
- **No loyalty or returning-customer hook**: I've spent real money here. A rewards balance, a "welcome back" nudge, member pricing — nothing. The Log In button is there, but it's not selling me on *why* to log in.
- **"Archive Sale" label is vague**: is this overstock? Discontinued colorways? Last season's styles? No context makes it feel slightly discount-bin adjacent when it should feel curated.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the giving counter in or directly below the hero**: even one line — "Every purchase funds a pair donated" — would differentiate this from any other sock flash sale and speak directly to why people like me are loyal in the first place.
- **Dismiss or compress the cookie modal**: it's blocking the primary campaign copy. Either auto-collapse it to a bottom strip or ensure the hero headline is fully visible before the modal renders.
- **Add a returning-customer hook to the nav or banner**: something like "Member? Log in to unlock [X]% extra." Even a one-line nudge turns the Log In button from an afterthought into a value prop.
- **Label what "Archive" means**: one line of sub-copy — "Previous-season styles, same quality" — transforms a discount feel into a smart-shopper moment.
