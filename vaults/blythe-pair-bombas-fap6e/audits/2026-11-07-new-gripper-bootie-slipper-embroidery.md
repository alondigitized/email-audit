# New Gripper Bootie + Slipper Embroidery
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built slipper catalog send with no offer. Each section pairs a lifestyle hero with a magnified texture circle (sherpa, suede, grip sole) and a product-pair row below it, and the "HOW COZY IS TOO COZY?" header carries the message. The hierarchy is easy to scan, and the three Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons sit in the hero.
- It is also the fifth product-led send from Bombas in a week, after 11/01 "Holiday Socks Are Here", 11/04 "New Ski & Hiking Socks", 11/05 "New Cozy Socks" and 11/06 "Seamless Cami Is Finally Back". None of them has an offer. The last one with a real incentive was 10/31 "Save Up to 20% on Socks". Subscribers are being trained to expect "newness" with no reason to act now.
- The weak spot is the subject line and the preview. "New Gripper Bootie + Slipper Embroidery" is a product-spec line, and the only preview-like text I can see is the top banner, which reads "Sign up for texts…". The creative is a 7, the inbox pitch a 5.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: "HOW COZY IS TOO COZY?" with a sherpa close-up, and three Shop buttons inside the hero.
- The circular texture callouts (sherpa, suede and shearling, grippy sole) show tactile detail that photos alone can't.
- Each section is consistent: lifestyle image, then a women's and men's product pair with labels.
- The warm terracotta and brown palette fits the season, and nothing looks broken or overlapping.
- Kids gets its own "COMFY KIDDOS" block with a dark Shop Kids button, which serves family shoppers.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, no price and no deadline, so there is no reason to buy today.
- The product tiles don't show a price, and none of the product rows has its own button. The only CTAs are the generic Shop Women / Men / Kids buttons.
- There are no reviews, ratings or bestseller flags to back up the claims.
- The "Modern Moccasin" and "Gripper Slippers" sections run long for what is essentially one product family, so the email is tall (about 4,600px) and the kids section sits far down.
- The subject names the "Gripper Bootie" and "Embroidery", but the embroidery is a small detail in one section and isn't the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a reason to act.** Put a member price, a bundle deal or a "ships by Dec 15 for gifting" line in the hero. That does more than any other tweak.
- 2. **Link each product pair to its own PDP.** Add "Shop Bootie" and "Shop Moccasin" buttons under the pairs, with the price on each tile.
- 3. **Add social proof.** A star rating or "bestseller" tag on the Gripper Slipper would help. It is the most practical item, and it is buried.
- 4. **Cut the email to three sections** and move Kids higher. Lead with the bootie, since it is the new product in the subject.
- 5. **Fix the preheader.** Right now it appears to default to the SMS signup banner.

1. Re-test the SMS signup link (400) with a browser-like request, and fix the destination if it fails.
