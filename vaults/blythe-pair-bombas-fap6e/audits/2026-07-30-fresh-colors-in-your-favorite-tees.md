# Fresh Colors in Your Favorite Tees
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas's category-expansion pitch into tees — moving off socks and slippers into "Pima & Premium Weight T-Shirts... back with all new hues." It's a clean, minimal execution: a single strong hero image (model in a plum tee against a sage-green backdrop), one clear headline treatment ("It's Tee Time"), and duplicate Shop Women/Shop Men CTAs both above and below the fold. No discount, no urgency, no price — this is a pure product-launch/restock email, same playbook as "New Colors in All-Time Favorites" (2026-07-25, 5.00/10) and "New Gripper Socks in Fours Heights" (2026-07-27, 7.00/10).
- Where this one stands out from that mid-tier group: the visual hierarchy is genuinely excellent — hero image, headline, CTA buttons, all landing in the right order with zero clutter — and the brand trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed, B Corp) do real work reinforcing Bombas's mission-driven positioning. But it's also a flat restock/color-refresh email with no hook beyond "new colors," and the tees themselves are barely shown — one guy, one shirt color, no flat-lay or color swatch grid to sell the "all new hues" claim.
- Judgement: solid, on-brand creative that undersells its own headline. If the story is "new colors," show more than one color.

## What's working

- Hero image is high-quality, well-composed, and the sage-green/plum color pairing feels fresh and seasonal.
- "It's Tee Time" is a fun, on-brand headline with clear pun logic tied to the product.
- Shop Women / Shop Men CTA buttons appear twice (hero + body), giving the eye multiple clean entry points.
- Trust badges (donation match, happiness guarantee, B Corp certification) are legible and reinforce brand credibility without cluttering the offer.
- No render bugs — clean rendering, no overlapping text or broken assets.

## What's weak

- The email promises "all new hues" but shows exactly one shirt in one color — no swatch strip or multi-color flat-lay to back up the claim.
- No Kids CTA despite "Kids" being in the top nav — inconsistent with the two-audience (Women/Men) focus below.
- Zero product specificity: no price, no fabric callout beyond "Pima & Premium Weight" in a single sentence, no fit/sizing detail.
- Entirely offer-free — fine for a launch email, but combined with no urgency or price anchor, there's little reason to act now vs. later.
- "STOCK UP ON THE GOOD STUFF" is a generic filler line that doesn't add new information after the headline already made the point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a color swatch row or 3-up flat-lay under the hero showing the actual new hues — this is the single biggest miss since "new colors" is the entire premise and the email shows one color.
- 2. Add a Kids CTA to match the nav and the Women/Men button pattern, or drop Kids from the nav if tees aren't offered there.
- 3. Insert one concrete product-detail line (price, or "$X-$Y" range) near the CTA buttons to reduce friction before click.
- 4. Swap "STOCK UP ON THE GOOD STUFF" for a line that names the fabric benefit (e.g., "Soft Pima cotton, zero shrink, all new colors") to give the second CTA row a reason to click beyond repetition.
- 5. Consider a soft urgency signal ("Just dropped") since this is a launch, not a sale — cadence-appropriate urgency without faking a discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Tee Colors Just Dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Tee, Now in New Hues`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pima & Premium Weight tees, restocked in fresh seasonal colors.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft, breathable, and back in new shades for summer.`
