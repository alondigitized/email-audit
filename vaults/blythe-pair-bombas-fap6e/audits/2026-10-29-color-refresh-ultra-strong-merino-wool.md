## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (lang/dir, `role="article"`, viewport, color-scheme meta, hidden preheader). The QA run is mostly inconclusive: the 38% pass rate comes largely from 429 rate-limiting of the click-tracking domain, with one real 400 and several header checks that the relay may have hidden.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **One 400 error:** the "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" link (`https://clicks.bombas.com/f/a/LHPvGnPuKKhsXsiuOmIzSQ~~/AAQRxRA~/5NI1sQR3…`) returned HTTP 400. It is the only hard failure. A 400 on a tracked redirect can mean a malformed wrapped destination, or the probe being rejected (likely for an SMS-signup target). Re-test it manually before treating it as broken for real users.
- **429s are not failures:** these links returned 429 and are unverified, not broken. Affected links are "Shop Women", "Shop Men", "BOMBAS", "SHOP WOMEN", "SHOP MEN", "Ultra Strong Merino Blend…" and "3X TOUGHER…". All go through `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/…`, so the tracking host is rate-limiting the probe.
- **Four links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out (`…/AJ2gpVu6FF9k…`, `…/2yUEu7bc2s5h…`, `…/no1N8s_Cx06W…`, `…/G5sNtms6Ntu3…`). They are neither pass nor fail.
- **Redirect URLs are very long** (several hundred characters of opaque payload). This is why the plain-text part is 5744/6898 characters of URLs (>70%), a minor spam-filter signal.
- **Image with missing alt text:** `https://clicks.bombas.com/q/EKTPIYRN60F8G8ps1QeuIg~~/AAQRxRA~/F9uv1YRw…`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking pixel rather than content, but the truncated HTML can't confirm that. If it is a pixel, add `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Duplicate `xmlns` attribute:** `<html>` declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice. That is invalid markup. It is usually tolerated but can trip strict XML-based parsers or validators.
- **Tiny mobile type:** `.font13` drops to `font-size:9px; line-height:9px` at ≤470px and `10px/10px` at ≤480px. `.font13Pad` uses 9px. That is below common legibility minimums (about 11–14px), and a line-height equal to the font size risks clipping.
- **Dead CSS:** `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` are declared twice in the same media query with conflicting values (`max-width:50%` vs none, `33.33%` vs `33%`). The later rule wins, so this is harmless but sloppy.
- No other issues found in the visible source. Dark-mode classes (`.darkbg`, `.darkcolor`) can't be verified for coverage in the truncated HTML.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. Check the raw message at the origin.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified. The HTML is truncated before the footer, and no unsubscribe URL appears in the probe results.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Every destination is wrapped in `clicks.bombas.com` redirects, and the probes either returned 429 or were skipped, so final landing URLs and UTM parameters were never resolved. The link text ("Shop Women", "Shop Men", "Ultra Strong Merino Blend") can't be matched to its landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually resolve the 400 on "Get Texted Exclusive Offers" (a single browser-like GET) to confirm whether it is a real defect.
2. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff and a longer budget. This clears the 429s and covers the 4 unprobed links, and should lift the 38% pass rate.
3. Inspect the raw `.eml` from the original send for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't judge these from the relay copy.
4. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` on `<html>`, and add `alt=""` to the `/q/` pixel image.
5. Raise the mobile `.font13` and `.font13Pad` minimums and fix the line-height.
6. Follow the redirects to capture final URLs and confirm UTM parameters and landing alignment.
