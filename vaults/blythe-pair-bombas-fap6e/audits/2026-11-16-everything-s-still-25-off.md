# Everything’s Still 25% Off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean last-call send. "25% OFF SITEWIDE / ENDS TOMORROW" in big periwinkle type on navy is the first thing the eye lands on, with Shop Women, Shop Men and Shop Kids buttons right under it. The deadline is credible and stated twice: "tomorrow at midnight PST" in the body and "ENDS TOMORROW, 11/17" in the footer block.
- For a subscriber, though, this is the fifth near-identical 25% send since 11/14. This morning's "Extra 10% Off Slippers! (+25% Off Sitewide)" at least added a new angle. This one adds nothing beyond the clock, so it works as a reminder and not as news. The body is also long. It has two product grids, a gift grid and a four-tile "Gifting Made Easy" block, all after a hero that already says everything.
- Verdict: solid deadline email, but it is fatigue-prone and sprawling. The urgency is the only fresh thing in it, and the product sections dilute it.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer and the deadline in one block: "25% OFF SITEWIDE, ENDS TOMORROW."
- Three category buttons sit directly under the headline, so the click path is short.
- The copy "Still time to shop, no time to wait!" is on-brand and gives the date and timezone ("tomorrow at midnight PST").
- Bombas's terracotta, cream and navy look is consistent with its earlier sale sends, so the email is instantly recognizable.
- The footer repeats the deadline with "Gift Cards Not Included | See Offer Details," so the exclusion is disclosed and not buried.

## What's weak

- Cadence: the same 25% offer has now gone out on 11/14, 11/15 and 11/16, and nothing here is new.
- The product grids are weak. Items have names but no prices, no sale price and no reviews, so "Popular Styles On Sale" never shows what the sale is worth.
- The product images are inconsistent. Some slippers are on grey tiles while the sock packs float small on white, so the grid looks uneven.
- Each product has a caption but no visible button, so it is unclear how to click.
- "Gifting Made Easy" is a late and long detour. Its four tiles (Shop Women, Men, Kids, Slippers) repeat the hero buttons, and the footer then adds a third set of text links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one tight product row and a single deadline line. A last-call email should be short, with the countdown as the star.
- 2. Add real prices to each product, for example "$18 $13.50", plus a star rating, so the 25% off is concrete.
- 3. Lead with the offer the subscriber can't get again, such as "Last day for the extra 10% off slippers," if that promo is still live. A new reason to act beats a reminder.
- 4. Drop the "Gifting Made Easy" block, or keep only one gift CTA. The footer text-link row is redundant.
- 5. Put a time or countdown in the preheader and hero ("Ends 11:59pm PST") so it reads as precise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends at midnight: 25% off socks & slippers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Early Holiday Sale closes tomorrow 11/17. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gift-ready socks and slippers, all 25% off for one more day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (table layout, `role="article"`, `lang`, preheader, dark-mode meta). The confirmed defects are 7 content images with no alt text and a duplicated `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay did not capture the headers, so they are not confirmed failures. Link validity is also unconfirmed, since 7 probed links returned 429 and 5 were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Seven probed links returned 429.** These are "Shop Women" ×2, "Shop Men" ×2, "Shop Kids" ×2 and "Shop Slippers" ×1, all on `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/...`. This is a rate-limit response, not a broken link. The primary nav CTAs are still unverified.
- **Five links were not probed** because of the 70s time budget. Examples: `.../nfG7brZhiGUMQTMIHfEe0A~~/...`, `.../k6ZnXLQZ7lmYfB3tfDhe6A~~/...`. A further 35 links were skipped by design as footer or social links.
- **The tracking wrapper hides the destinations.** Every link goes through `clicks.bombas.com/f/a/<id>~~/AAQRxRA~/<payload>~`. The final landing URLs and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Each category CTA appears twice with different tracking IDs.** For example, "Shop Women" uses `DP54BiPM...` and `WtwKqxfA...` but shares the destination payload prefix `6irzqB3NXwkzD8-ioRIclUXOzcLX_emRDv-_dlXJQ4zdN0JIlCqE2ZT-LFpcbW6k`. That is expected for per-placement click attribution, not a defect.
- **The plain-text part is 9,907 of 11,409 characters URLs (87%).** This comes from the long tracking URLs and exceeds the 70% threshold. It is a minor spam-scoring and readability risk for the text alternative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Seven content images have no alt text.** They are served from `assets.bombas.com/image/fetch/c_scale,w_600,q_auto/https://images.ctfassets.net/09hbx69ra4p2/...`. The affected images are:
  - `10895-feather_beige_leopard-adult-female-low_back_`
  - `11566-berrysandmix-adult-female-layflat-1-transpar`
  - `11265-midnight_navy_chevron-adult-male-ankle-layfl`
  - `mixed-layflat-product-transparent__3_.png`
  - `12038-cocoacloudmix-adult-female-layflat-2-transpa`
  - `11265-berrymauve-adult-Female-layflat-3-transparen`
  - `11645-heritagegreenmix-adult-male-layflat-2-transp`

  With images blocked or a screen reader running, these are announced as filenames or skipped. If they are linked product tiles, the links lose their accessible names.
- **An eighth image with no alt is `clicks.bombas.com/q/3_GqHnX8...`.** This is likely the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`, but the impact is negligible.
- **The `<html>` tag declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` twice.** This is a duplicate attribute, which is invalid markup. Most clients tolerate it, but it can trip strict parsers.
- **The media-query block defines `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` twice with different values.** The later rule wins, so `max-width` is dropped on the second pass. This is low impact.
- No other issues were found. `lang`, `dir`, `role="article"`, `aria-label`, the viewport meta tag, a hidden preheader with `mso-hide` and MSO fallback styles are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `*|...|*`. The remainder of the HTML was not available, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a violation. If the headers really are absent, that conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed.** The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `hello.bombas.com` aligns with the tracking domain `clicks.bombas.com`.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source.** QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but the result isn't listed, so I can't confirm it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** The click wrappers are opaque and the 429s blocked resolution of the redirect targets.
- **The structured data is consistent with the subject line.** `itemtype="http://schema.org/DiscountOffer"` has `description="25% OFF"` and `availabilityEnds="2026-11-17T23:59:00-05:00"`, which matches "Everything's Still 25% Off!" and today's date (2026-11-16). The `itemtype` uses `http://`, but schema.org accepts it.
- **Landing page alignment can't be checked** because no destination resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited and 5 unprobed links with throttling or retry and backoff. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm the destination URLs and UTM parameters.
3. Check the raw `.eml` or the sending ESP for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, rather than relying on the relay copy.
4. Remove the duplicate `xmlns` attribute on `<html>`, and de-duplicate the `.mobile-half` and `.mobile-third` rules.
5. Trim the plain-text part by shortening tracking URLs or adding more descriptive text around them.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to confirm the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and absence of merge tokens.
