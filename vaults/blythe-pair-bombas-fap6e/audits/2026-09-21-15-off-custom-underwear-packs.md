# 15% Off Custom Underwear Packs
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Bombas' "Build Your Own Pack" beat again, now pointed at underwear instead of socks — a category pivot that's more interesting than another sock discount, but it's a near-identical structural rerun of 2026-09-13's "10% Off: Build Your Own Pack" send. Same black-button-pair layout, same "Shop Women / Shop Men" split, same trust-badge row at the bottom. The one real design flourish is the "15% OFF PACKS" starburst sticker overlapping the hero photo, which is a stronger scroll-stopper than the flat banner text Bombas has used in prior sends.
- The offer itself is confusingly stacked: the hero says "15% Off Packs," the subhead says "Save 15% when you build a pack of 3+," and then two lines down it says "UP TO 20% OFF PRE-MADE PACKS" — three different numbers in the first screen, which undercuts the clarity a discount email needs. The hero photo also only shows men's underwear despite both "Shop Women" and "Shop Men" CTAs sitting right under it, which is a real mismatch for anyone landing there for the women's line.
- Net: fine execution on brand fundamentals, but the repeated "Build Your Own Pack" structure plus a muddled offer hierarchy keeps this in the same mid-tier band as its predecessors.

## What's working

- The orange starburst "15% OFF PACKS" sticker is a clear, high-contrast focal point that draws the eye immediately.
- Two distinct CTAs ("Shop Women" / "Shop Men") let the reader self-select by category with one tap.
- Trust badges (One Purchased One Donated, 100% Happiness Guaranteed) reinforce brand identity below the fold.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with generous white space and no render bugs.

## What's weak

- Three different discount figures appear above the fold (15% off packs, 15% off 3+ pairs, up to 20% off pre-made packs) with no clear hierarchy explaining which applies when.
- Hero image shows only men's underwear, but the CTAs directly below promote both Women's and Men's shopping — no visual representation for the women's audience.
- This is structurally the same "Build Your Own Pack" template as 2026-09-13's send, just re-skinned for underwear — repetitive creative for a subscriber who's seen that beat recently.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — the offer reads as evergreen, which won't drive immediate action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the offer to one number and one mechanic in the hero — pick either "15% off packs of 3+" or "up to 20% off pre-made packs" as the lead, and demote the other to a secondary line so the reader isn't doing math to figure out their actual discount.
- 2. Swap the hero photo for a mixed-gender pairing (or run two hero variants) so the image matches both CTAs beneath it.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("ends Sunday," "48 hours only") to convert the evergreen tone into urgency.
- 4. Vary the template from the 2026-09-13 pack send — new layout or photography angle — so repeat subscribers don't pattern-match this as the same email.
