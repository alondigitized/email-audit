# Homepage snapshot · www.freshdirect.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- I come to FreshDirect every week knowing exactly what I want, and the first thing that greets me is a banner screaming about $50 off my "first order" — a deal I can't touch — followed immediately by a modal I have to dismiss before I can see anything else. This homepage is doing heavy lifting for acquisition while completely ignoring the people who are already paying the bills. Right now it reads like a signup funnel, not a loyalty machine.

## What's working

- **Search bar placement** — "What's on your shopping list?" sits right at the top. As someone who lands with a list already in her head, I'd go straight there if the modal weren't in the way.
- **New-customer banner is dismissible** — the X button is clearly visible; they're not hiding the close target.
- **FreshDirect branding is clean** — the green leaf logo and yellow wordmark are immediately recognizable, zero confusion about where I am.

## What's weak

- **SNAP EBT modal eating the viewport** — it's the single largest element on screen and it's not relevant to me. I have to consciously hunt for the X and dismiss before I can do anything, which is friction I didn't need on a reorder visit.
- **"Welcome to FreshDirect" hero copy** — that's new-customer onboarding language, not a message for someone who's been scheduling Thursday delivery windows for two years.
- **NEW50 banner is dead air for returning customers** — I see it, register it's not for me, and it just makes me feel slightly forgotten.
- **Category icons below the hero are partially visible but unlabeled** at this fold — I can see a sparkle, a bag, a price tag, a truck, a bowl, but I can't read what they lead to without scrolling/dismissing the modal first.
- **Zero personalization signal** — no "Welcome back, Freya," no recently-ordered items, no "your next delivery is Thursday" hook. My account is invisible to this page.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the NEW50 banner for a returning-customer hook** — if the user is logged in, show their next delivery window or a loyalty-points balance instead of a discount they're ineligible for.
- **Remove or delay the SNAP EBT modal** — push it to a secondary page or a dismissible shelf at the bottom; don't let it eat the above-the-fold experience for every single visitor on load.
- **Update the hero copy from "Welcome to FreshDirect" to something for loyalists** — something like "Your Thursday delivery, curated fresh" signals the brand knows who its regulars are.
- **Surface one returning-customer offer above the fold** — free delivery on next order, a member-only producer spotlight, a seasonal add-on (fresh stone fruit this week). Give me a reason to feel rewarded, not just tolerated.
