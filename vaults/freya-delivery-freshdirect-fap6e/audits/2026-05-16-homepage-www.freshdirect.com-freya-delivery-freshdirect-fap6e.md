# Homepage snapshot · www.freshdirect.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- What I'm seeing isn't really a homepage — it's a full-screen app interstitial blocking the actual site. FreshDirect is aggressively pushing me to their app before I've seen a single product, offer, or navigation element. The food photography behind it looks warm and fresh, but the entire experience is gated behind a conversion wall the moment I land.

## What's working

- **Flat-lay food photography:** The background shot is genuinely appetizing — a rotisserie chicken, carrots, avocados, cold brew, olive oil. These are real-person groceries, not aspirational chef props. It reads like my actual FreshDirect order.
- **Clean mobile rendering:** The modal is centered, the green logo mark is recognizable, the two buttons are tappable-size and visually distinct from each other.
- **"Continue in browser" escape hatch:** It's there. It's visible. It's not buried in fine print. I appreciate that.

## What's weak

- **The entire viewport is an app-download wall:** I came to shop, not to make a platform decision. I already know they have an app — I've been ordering for years. This is wasted real estate on a returning customer.
- **Zero offer messaging:** No free delivery, no promo, no member discount — nothing to reward the click. The copy "Get the fastest, easiest shopping experience in our app" is a feature claim, not a value exchange.
- **No personalization or recognition:** As a weekly subscriber-level customer, this page treats me like a stranger. No "Hey, Freya, your usual order is ready to reorder" — nothing.
- **Both CTAs compete:** "Shop in the app" (green) vs. "Continue in browser" (white outline) — the hierarchy is fine, but both are about *where to shop*, not *what to shop*. I'm already trying to shop. Just let me in.
- **No local producer callout:** I read every email about new local producers. This homepage has zero editorial surface for that content. Missed connection.

## Recommendations

- **Show a delivery-window hook instead of an app wall:** "Your next delivery window: Saturday 8–10am — shop now" would get me tapping immediately. Use the interstitial real estate for something actionable, not a platform pitch.
- **Add a returning-customer track:** If I'm logged in (or cookied), suppress the interstitial entirely and drop me onto a personalized homepage with my recent categories and any active promos.
- **Embed one concrete offer in the interstitial copy:** Even "Free delivery on orders over $75 in the app" gives me a reason to click the green button rather than just tapping past it.
- **Surface a local producer teaser in the background:** Swap one of the background items for a branded local-producer product with a small overlay — "New from [Farm Name] this week." It costs nothing visually and speaks directly to the customer segment that reads the emails.
