# Homepage snapshot · www.freshdirect.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is FreshDirect doing what FreshDirect always does — leading with a big new-customer acquisition banner that means absolutely nothing to me, a person who has been ordering from them every single week for years. The search bar is prominent and the category shortcuts are sensible, but the whole page reads like they've never heard of me. It's a grocery delivery service that forgot it has groceries — and loyal customers — to show.

## What's working

- **The search bar** — "What's on your shopping list?" is perfectly tuned for me. I know what I want on a Thursday evening and I don't need to browse. Big, white, impossible to miss.
- **Horizontal shortcuts row** — "See the season's best," "Discover something new," "Last chance! Save big" are genuinely useful quick-filters. I tap "Last chance! Save big" regularly when I'm close to an order.
- **"Popular now" rail** — Right flavor: real produce (blueberries, bananas), no aspirational lifestyle product I'd never buy. Practical.
- **Dismissible promo banner** — The X on the NEW50 banner means I can kill it and get to the homepage I actually want. Small mercy.

## What's weak

- **"Get $50 OFF your first order!"** — This is the loudest above-the-fold message and it is completely irrelevant to any returning customer. I've placed dozens of orders. This banner makes me feel like a stranger in my own account.
- **Hero video with illegible copy** — The "Welcome to FreshDirect" hero is blurry, autoplay, and the body copy is unreadable at a glance. This is an onboarding screen for someone who doesn't know what FreshDirect is. I've been scheduling truck windows for years.
- **Zero loyalty or returning-customer signals** — No rewards summary, no "your next delivery," no recently viewed items. Nothing says FreshDirect knows I exist.
- **No ratings or price on "Popular now" products** — I can see Jumboz blueberries and a banana bunch. No price, no reviews, no weight. One tap away from the product page, but I'm going in blind.
- **"Get 60 days free delivery" shortcut** — Another acquisition offer sitting in prime real estate. I already have delivery. This wastes a shortcut slot for me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the NEW50 banner for a returning-customer module** — If I'm logged in, show my next delivery window, my FreshDirect Plus status, or a "reorder your favorites" prompt. The acquisition banner should only fire for logged-out/new sessions.
- **Replace the blurry "Welcome" hero video with a seasonal produce hero** — Something crisp: a flat-lay of summer stone fruit, copy like "Peak season starts now." Visually stops the scroll without requiring me to squint at a buffering video.
- **Add price + a single review count to the "Popular now" tiles** — Even "$2.99/lb · ★ 4.7" is enough to remove friction. Right now I'm clicking blind.
- **Make one shortcut slot a returning-customer action** — "Your weekly list" or "Reorder from last week" would be an immediate hook for subscribers like me.
