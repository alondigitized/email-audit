# Homepage snapshot · www.freshdirect.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very "we know you're new here" energy for someone who's been getting a Thursday delivery window for like three years. The $50-off-first-order banner and then an immediate SNAP EBT modal slamming down over the categories row makes it feel like I landed on a new-customer funnel, not my usual reorder page. It's clean and on-brand green-and-orange FreshDirect, but right now it's optimizing for acquisition, not for me.

## What's working

- **The search bar ("What's on your shopping list?")** — this is the fastest path for me since I already know exactly what I'm buying every week, so I'd probably just tap in and skip everything else.
- **The $50/NEW50 offer disclosure** — at least the fine print (min order $99) isn't hidden in tiny gray text somewhere I'd have to hunt for.
- **The hero video with first-pick produce/custom-cut meat/seafood copy** — it's on-message for why I pay a premium over Amazon Fresh, even if it's pitched at a new user.

## What's weak

- **The SNAP EBT modal firing immediately and blocking the categories row** — I don't use SNAP, so this is a full-screen interruption that's not for me, and it's covering content I actually want to see (that partially-hidden "Ready in minutes" tile looks like it could be quick delivery, which I DO care about).
- **"$50 off your first order"** — I'm not a first-order customer. There's zero visible acknowledgment that I've ordered before — no "welcome back," no reorder shortcut, no loyalty balance.
- **Two stacked interruptions on load** (top promo bar + modal) — that's a lot of chrome before I even see a single product or category I can tap into.
- **No visible delivery-window or truck-schedule info** — that's the single thing I check first every single week, and it's nowhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- Swap the first-order banner for returning users into a "Reorder your last delivery" or "Your usual truck day: [zip]" module — I'm way more likely to engage with that than a new-customer discount I can't use.
- Don't stack the SNAP EBT modal on top of the categories row on load — let me see "Ready in minutes" and the other icons before throwing an interstitial at me.
- Surface my delivery window / zip-code truck schedule right under the search bar — that's my actual daily decision driver, not a discount code.
- Add a quick "Reorder favorites" or recently-purchased rail right below the hero so returning shoppers have a one-tap path.
