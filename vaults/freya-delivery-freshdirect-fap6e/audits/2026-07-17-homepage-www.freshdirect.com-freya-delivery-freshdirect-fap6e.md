# Homepage snapshot · www.freshdirect.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- I open FreshDirect — my grocery lifeline — and the first thing that ambushes me is a SNAP EBT modal filling half my screen. I have nothing against SNAP, but I'm a weekly customer who knows this app cold, and before I can even clock the hero or tap a category, I'm dealing with a popup I have to dismiss. Underneath it, there's a "Welcome to FreshDirect" video hero and a new-customer $50 offer screaming at the top of the page — neither of which is for me. This homepage is not talking to its regulars.

## What's working

- **Top banner offer:** "$50 OFF your first order!! Use code: NEW50" is prominent, clearly labeled with the minimum, and legible. Good acquisition mechanic — just not for me.
- **Search bar:** "What's on your shopping list?" is dead center and immediately actionable. As a high-freq shopper I'd use this to skip all the noise and get to my oat milk. That's the save here.
- **Category icon strip:** Partially visible below the hero — I can see icons for what looks like deals, delivery, and a few food categories. If the modal weren't in the way, this would be a fast path.

## What's weak

- **The SNAP EBT modal:** Takes up the majority of the visible screen on load, covers the category strip and hero, and offers no obvious "not relevant to me, skip" affordance beyond a small X. Jarring and disorienting for anyone who isn't here for SNAP.
- **"Welcome to FreshDirect" hero copy:** I order here every week. This copy treats me like I've never heard of the brand. Zero personalization signal.
- **New-customer $50 promo in the top banner:** I cannot use this. It's noise that makes me feel like the site doesn't know who I am.
- **No returning-shopper hook at all:** No "your account," no loyalty status, no "last ordered" shortcut, no streak or rewards balance. FreshDirect has my order history — none of it is surfaced.
- **Partially-obscured content:** Because of the modal, I literally cannot see the right side of the category strip or whatever is in the "Reac..." section on the right edge.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or retarget the SNAP modal:** If the user has a prior order history or a logged-in session, do not fire this modal. Reserve it for new or unknown visitors where it may be genuinely helpful.
- **Swap the welcome hero for a "back to your list" module:** Show my most-recently-ordered items or my saved list front-and-center. FreshDirect's entire value prop is rhythm and habit — lean into it.
- **Replace the new-customer promo banner with a loyalty/streak hook for returning users:** Something like "Your delivery window is open — get your order in by Thursday 9pm" would make me feel seen and create urgency that actually applies to me.
- **Give the category icon strip more real estate above the fold:** Move it up, or at least don't let a modal bury it. That row is the fastest path to conversion for regulars.
