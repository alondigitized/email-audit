# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm genuinely stopping mid-scroll here — that gold charm is *stunning*. The "Pandora Wonders" campaign is hitting different; this is clearly a new collection drop, and they led with exactly the kind of elevated, editorial charm imagery that makes me want to immediately find out what's in it. It reads luxury, it reads milestone, and it's fully speaking my language as a charm collector.

## What's working

- **"Explore our new charms" subhead** — this single line of copy does so much work for me. I don't need to wonder if there's anything new; it tells me immediately there's a drop to investigate.
- **The hero charm close-up** — gold swirl design with a round diamond? That is aspirational and specific. It looks like a limited-edition piece, which triggers my "I need to track this" instinct.
- **"PANDORA WONDERS IS HERE" headline** — campaign name is evocative and launch-energy. "Is Here" signals urgency without a timer, which I find more elegant than countdown clocks.
- **Clean mobile navigation bar** — search, account, and bag icons are all immediately accessible in one tap. For a returning customer like me who knows what she wants, that's smooth.
- **SHOP NOW CTA placement** — bottom-of-frame, bar-style button. Hard to miss, easy to tap on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing or offer context** — I can see a gorgeous charm, but I have no sense of price range, whether there's a launch promo, or if my loyalty points apply. For a purchase-window shopper like me, that friction matters.
- **No loyalty/membership acknowledgment** — Pandora ME program is a big deal to collectors. A "Members get early access" or even a small badge would've made this feel like it was made for regulars, not just first-timers.
- **Hero is video (pause/mute controls visible)** — on a slow mobile connection this could be a perf risk. The pause/mute bar at the bottom is visible, which slightly distracts from the CTA line.
- **No secondary content visible** — the hero takes the full viewport. I can't see if there's a "new arrivals" rail or a collection grid one tap below, so the page feels like a single bet.
- **"FEEDBACK" tab on the right edge** — that pink vertical tab is visually distracting and looks like a site survey widget, not a Pandora brand element. It competes with the hero.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty ribbon above the hero** — a slim bar that says "Pandora ME members: earn 2x points on Wonders charms through July 31" would activate every collector on the page without touching the hero design.
- **Drop a price anchor into the CTA bar** — "SHOP NOW · Charms from $45" gives me the permission I need to tap. Right now SHOP NOW floats without any context.
- **Replace or overlay the feedback widget** — that pink "FEEDBACK" tab is a jarring third-party element. Push it below the fold or ghost it so it doesn't pull the eye away from the hero.
- **Add a single urgency line under the headline** — "Limited edition · Shop before they're gone" would convert collectors like me who treat launches as timed events.
