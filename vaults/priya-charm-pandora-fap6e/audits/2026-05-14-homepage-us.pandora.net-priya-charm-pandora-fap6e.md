# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is serving full art-gallery energy right now — the "Garden of Dreams" hero is illustrated, botanical, and genuinely beautiful. As a charm collector, I immediately clocked what looked like charms integrated into the floral artwork, which made me lean in. But this is clearly a brand-awareness moment more than a shopping moment — there's zero offer, zero loyalty hook, and zero "new drops this week" teaser above the fold.

## What's working

- **"Garden of Dreams" illustrated hero** — the painterly botanical aesthetic is stunning and feels very premium charm-brand. The visible charm-like ornaments dangling in the illustration are a smart native placement.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA bar** — dark, high-contrast, spans the width of the viewport. Impossible to miss.
- **No intrusive modal** — I landed clean, no email-capture popup eating the hero. That's rare and I respect it.
- **Spring/Mother's Day timing** — this campaign screams "buy a charm for mom" without saying it out loud, which is exactly right for May 14th.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer above the fold** — I'm a window shopper who gets pulled in by "20% off today only." Nothing here gives me financial urgency.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — for a charm-stack collector, "Shop New Charms" or "Explore the Garden Drop" would have me tapping immediately. This CTA tells me nothing about what I'm walking into.
- **No new-drop teaser** — I track limited-edition charm drops weekly. The hero doesn't hint at what's actually new in the collection.
- **No loyalty callout** — I'm a returning customer and I have zero idea if I have rewards to spend or if there's a member event.
- **"FEEDBACK" sidebar tab** — that coral vertical tab stuck to the right edge is a UI distraction right in the middle of an otherwise elegant hero.

## Recommendations

- **Overlay a slim "New This Week" chip on the hero** — something like "3 new Garden of Dreams charms just dropped" keeps collectors like me from bouncing to Instagram to find out what's new.
- **Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with collection-specific copy** — "Discover Garden of Dreams Charms" or "Shop the Drop" tells me exactly what I'm getting and signals limited-edition urgency.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards strip directly below the hero** — even "Earn points on every charm | Check your balance" would stop me from leaving to check the app separately.
- **Surface a concrete Mother's Day offer** — a free gift with purchase or free engraving threshold banner (even a thin one above the hero) would convert my browsing into a cart.
