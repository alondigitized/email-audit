# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- Opening Pandora right now and I'm immediately hit with a full-screen sale blast — "SALE UP TO 70% OFF*" in massive white type over a dark ocean background. This is pure markdown-everything, clear-the-inventory energy. As a brand, Pandora is communicating one thing only: deep discounts available now. For me as a charm collector who tracks limited-edition drops, this is interesting — but I'd need to dig past this generic sale wall to find out if anything I actually want is in the mix.

## What's working

- **The sale magnitude is legible in under one second** — 70% off reads instantly, which stops a thumb-scroller
- **EXTRA10 code is surfaced inline** — I don't have to hunt for it in email or a pop-up; it's right there under the hero headline
- **FREE standard shipping with no stated minimum** — that's a meaningful friction-reducer at checkout, and it's visible without scrolling
- **"Limited time only" copy** — creates mild urgency without being aggressive; it's honest enough
- **Clean mobile layout** — no crowding, no overlapping elements, icons are well-spaced in the nav bar

## What's weak

- **Zero charm or jewelry imagery** — the hero is a dark teal/ocean abstract texture; I have no visual confirmation I'm on a jewelry site without already knowing the brand
- **No charm category entry point above the fold** — there's a hamburger menu but no shortcut to "Charms," "New Arrivals," or "Limited Editions" that I can tap without hunting
- **Generic "SHOP NOW" CTA** — it tells me nothing about what I'm shopping; compare to "Shop Charms on Sale" or "Find Your Charm"
- **No loyalty program hook** — as someone who tracks drops and has buying windows, I'd want to see "Club members get 15% more" or "Sign in to see your rewards" — nothing like that exists here
- **Asterisk fatigue at the bottom** — "Select styles only" + "Terms & Conditions Apply" stacked under an already-asterisked headline makes the 70% feel more theoretical than real

## Recommendations

- **Swap the abstract ocean background for a wrist-stack lifestyle shot** — show actual charms on a rose-gold and sterling bracelet, styled on a Millennial woman; that's the product and the persona in one image
- **Add a "Charms on Sale" CTA secondary to SHOP NOW** — give charm collectors a direct lane so we're not dumped into a generic sale page with 300 SKUs
- **Surface a loyalty hook in the sub-copy** — something like "Pandora Club members save an extra 5%" directly below the EXTRA10 line would pull in repeat buyers without a redesign
- **Replace "Limited time only" with an actual deadline** — "Sale ends July 6" is more motivating than the vague phrase, and it feels less like hedge language
