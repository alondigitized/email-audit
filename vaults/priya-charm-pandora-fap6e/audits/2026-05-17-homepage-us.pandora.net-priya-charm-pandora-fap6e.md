# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so Pandora is fully leaning into an illustrated, artsy aesthetic right now — the "Garden of Dreams" hero is lush and dreamy, very Pinterest-core, and I can actually spot two charms in the frame which immediately got my attention. But the above-the-fold experience is almost entirely vibes: there's no offer, no loyalty hook, nothing telling me what's new in the collection or what I'd be clicking into. Beautiful brand moment, weak shopping moment.

## What's working

- **The illustrated hero art** — those hand-drawn blue and pink tulips with the two charms embedded in the scene (I can see what looks like a garnet sphere charm top-left, and a flower cluster charm bottom-right) made me stop. As someone who obsesses over limited-edition charm drops, that teaser is genuinely effective.
- **"GARDEN OF DREAMS" as a collection name** — as a charm collector I immediately want to know what's in this collection. The name does real work.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA placement** — dark bar anchored at the bottom of the hero, distinct from the illustrated background. It doesn't fight for attention; it earns its spot.
- **Seasonal alignment** — May is my main shopping window (Mother's Day!), and a spring garden theme lands exactly right for the moment I'm most likely to buy.
- **Video hero with controls visible** — the pause/mute controls in the bottom right tell me this is a video, which signals effort and craft. I appreciate that they don't autoplay sound.

## What's weak

- **Zero Mother's Day urgency** — I opened this in my peak buying window and there is no "Gift for Mom," no countdown, no "Ships by Sunday" messaging. That is a genuinely missed conversion opportunity for someone like me.
- **No offer whatsoever** — no "Free gift over $X," no "20% off new arrivals," nothing. Pandora does run promos — the absence here feels like a content timing miss, not an intentional premium stance.
- **Navigation is fully hidden** — hamburger-only on mobile means I can't see "Charms," "Bracelets," or any category at a glance. One tap just to find my section is friction I shouldn't have to tolerate.
- **No loyalty / Pandora Club hook** — I track my purchases for the loyalty tier and I can't see my status, my points, or any member-pricing signal. That's a returning-customer miss.
- **No product specificity** — two charms are artfully embedded in the illustration but they're not labeled, not priced, not tappable. They're decoration, not shop-entry points.
- **"FEEDBACK" tab** — that rotated vertical sidebar widget on the right edge is visually intrusive against the illustrated hero. Small thing, but it reads as unpolished.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Mother's Day Gifts" strip or banner above the hero** — even a thin persistent bar reading "Mother's Day • Ships by Sunday May 11 • Free shipping over $65" would immediately signal relevance and create urgency for my exact buying window.
- **Make the two embedded charms tappable** — annotate the garnet sphere and the flower cluster charm in the hero with a small "+tap" pip linking directly to each PDP. Turn art into a shop entry point.
- **Surface a "New in Garden of Dreams" category chip row** — one scrollable row of 4–5 category chips (Charms, Bracelets, Sets, Gifts under $75) just below the hero fold so I can jump to my section in one tap without going into the hamburger menu.
- **Add a Pandora Club / loyalty nudge in the nav area** — even a small "Hello, Priya — 840 pts" line under the account icon for signed-in users would activate my returning-shopper instinct the moment I land.
