# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is running a straight-up sale push right now — "Sale Up to 50% Off + 10% with code EXTRA10" is basically the entire above-the-fold experience. It feels more like a flash deal page than a charm brand. I can see they want the click, but there's zero product in sight and nothing that says "charms" to me specifically.

## What's working

- **The stacked sale headline** — "SALE UP TO 50% OFF / +10% OFF WITH CODE EXTRA10" is clear, bold, and impossible to miss. I register the offer in under a second.
- **Free shipping callout** — "FREE shipping on ALL orders!" is a genuine hook; no threshold friction, no asterisk hiding it.
- **Single CTA discipline** — there's only one button ("SHOP NOW"), so I'm not split between competing actions.
- **Clean render** — the mobile layout is tight. No competing banners, no pop-over modal forcing an email capture before I can see the page.

## What's weak

- **Zero product imagery** — I'm a bracelet curator. I want to see a rose-gold charm stack. The background is abstract dark teal — could be a tech brand or a spa. Nothing here signals "jewelry."
- **No charm or collection context** — Is this sale on charms? Rings? Everything? "Shop Now" dumped into a 50,000-SKU catalog is not helpful to me.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I'm a returning Pandora customer. Where's my points balance or "members get extra 15%" nudge? This page treats me like a cold stranger.
- **"Select styles only"** — buried at the very bottom in tiny gray text. After screaming 50% off + extra 10%, the fine print erodes trust. Limited-edition charms I'm tracking are almost certainly excluded.
- **No personalization or seasonal relevance** — it's late June 2026. No summer charm drop, no "new in charms" teaser, no milestone gifting angle (wedding season, graduations). The page is seasonally blank.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the abstract teal backdrop for a close-up lifestyle shot** of a rose-gold + sterling charm stack on a wrist — that's the product, that's the customer.
- **Add a "Charms included in sale" category chip** directly under the SHOP NOW button — something like "Charms · Bracelets · Rings" so I can tap directly into my category instead of landing cold in a sale grid.
- **Surface a loyalty hook** ("Pandora Club members earn 2× points on sale items") as a sub-line below the CTA — this costs nothing and re-engages returning customers.
- **Replace "Select styles only" fine print with a positive specificity statement** — e.g., "Hundreds of charms included" — so the offer feels expansive rather than like a bait-and-switch.
