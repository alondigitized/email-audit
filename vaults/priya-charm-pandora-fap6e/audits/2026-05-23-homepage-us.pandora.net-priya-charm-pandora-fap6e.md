# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is leading with its Moments line and a new summer collection drop — the hero is literally a woman holding up a charm bangle like a viewfinder, which is pretty clever and charming. This reads like a premium fashion brand that knows its core product, but the above-the-fold experience is almost entirely one big video hero with very little supporting information. For me specifically, the product imagery is everything I came for, but I need more than vibes to pull out my card.

## What's working

- **The Moments bracelet-as-viewfinder visual** is genuinely clever — she's literally looking through a bangle stacked with blue charms. As someone who curates stacks, I immediately clocked the detail work.
- **"MAKE WAVES WITH PANDORA MOMENTS"** — the headline names the product line explicitly, so I know in two seconds this is about charm bracelets, not rings or necklaces.
- **"SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" CTA** is low-pressure and direct. I track new charm drops weekly, so this is exactly what I'd tap.
- **The video format** (I can see the pause/audio controls) suggests lifestyle motion — a moving stack of charms would stop my thumb.
- **Seasonal alignment** — a summery "waves" collection launching in May is timed right for my Mother's Day/birthday purchase window.

## What's weak

- **Zero loyalty callout** — Pandora has a rewards program, and I have points. Nothing above the fold acknowledges me as a returning customer or shows a balance.
- **No offer or threshold** — not even "free shipping on orders over $X." For a brand where I might spend $80–$150 on a single charm drop, that matters.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — there are no visible category shortcuts (Charms, Bracelets, New Drops). I have to hunt.
- **The pink "FEEDBACK" tab** on the right edge partially overlaps the hero visual. It's a UX research widget that shouldn't be customer-facing.
- **No social proof** — no "4.8 stars · 12,000 reviews" badge, no "fan favorite" callout. For charm collecting, community validation drives decisions.

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin loyalty/rewards strip under the nav** showing points balance or "You're 200 points from your next reward" — returning shoppers should feel recognized immediately.
- **Overlay a free-shipping threshold on the hero** (e.g., "Free shipping on orders $75+") in the lower-left corner; it's one line of copy that lifts AOV.
- **Swap the hamburger-only nav for 3–4 visible category chips** below the hero CTA — "Charms," "Bracelets," "New Drops," "Sets" — so I can one-tap to my category without hunting.
- **Remove or reposition the "FEEDBACK" tab** — it's sitting on the hero image and reads as a broken UI element to shoppers who don't recognize it as a survey tool.
