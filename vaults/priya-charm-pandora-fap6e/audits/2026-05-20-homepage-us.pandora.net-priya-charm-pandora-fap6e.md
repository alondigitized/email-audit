# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Pandora opened with a mood board, not a shop. The "Garden of Dreams" hero is gorgeous — illustrated florals, a dangling charm peeking through the petals — and it reads immediately as a limited-edition drop. For someone who tracks charm releases weekly, this visual language hits. But above the fold there's nothing to tell me what this collection is, what it costs, or why I should care beyond the vibe.

## What's working

- **The charm cameo in the hero:** A jewelry piece is literally visible hanging off the illustrated flower — that tiny detail hooks me instantly as a charm hunter
- **"Garden of Dreams" campaign name:** Evocative, seasonal, feels like it could be a limited-edition collection name I'd search later
- **Clean mobile nav:** Logo, search, account, cart, and hamburger in one tight row — not cluttered
- **Video hero format:** The pause and sound icons tell me this is a motion piece, which creates a richer experience for a launch drop
- **High-contrast SHOP NOW bar:** Dark strip against the pink hero makes the CTA unmissable

## What's weak

- **Zero collection context above the fold:** I don't know if "Garden of Dreams" is 5 charms or 50, a collab, or a permanent line — there's nothing beyond the name
- **No offer whatsoever:** I'm in the prime Mother's Day window and there's no "Free engraving," no loyalty member offer, no free shipping callout — nothing that rewards me for showing up
- **No charm/category shortcut:** With the hamburger-only nav, getting to the charms section is at minimum two taps away — there's no "Shop Charms" or collection strip below the hero
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic:** For a named collection launch this specific, the CTA should be "Explore Garden of Dreams" or "Shop the Drop" — "SHOP NOW" could be any retailer
- **No social proof at all:** No "New this week," no "Limited edition," no review counts — nothing that creates urgency

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-liner beneath "GARDEN OF DREAMS":** Something like "20 new charms — limited release" anchors the campaign as a defined drop and gives me a reason to act now
- **Replace or supplement "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Garden of Dreams":** A named CTA converts better on collection launches and feels intentional, not templated
- **Add a thin promotional strip above or below the hero:** Even "Free shipping on orders $75+ | Club members get early access" would convert me from browser to buyer in the Mother's Day window
- **Surface a category pill row below the hero scroll:** "Charms / Bracelets / Rings / New Arrivals" — one-tap access, no hamburger required
