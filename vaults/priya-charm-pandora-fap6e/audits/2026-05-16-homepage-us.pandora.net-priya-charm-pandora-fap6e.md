# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is serving me a full-bleed illustrated floral fantasy right now — "Garden of Dreams" with a charm literally dangling from a painted flower — and as someone who has been watching their spring drops all week, I recognize this instantly as their Mother's Day push. The brand feels elevated and dreamy, which I love, but the page is doing almost nothing to get me from "oh pretty" to "add to cart."

## What's working

- **"Garden of Dreams" editorial hero** — the illustrated botanical art with a charm physically hanging from the flower stem is chef's kiss for a charm collector. It shows the product *in context*, not just on a white background.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — dark pill at the bottom left, high contrast against the pink background, immediately tappable on mobile.
- **Seasonal alignment** — hitting this homepage on May 16th, the Garden of Dreams framing maps perfectly onto Mother's Day gifting season. Whoever scheduled this campaign knows the calendar.
- **Clean mobile render** — the illustrated art scales beautifully to this viewport; no cropping weirdness, no overlapping nav elements.

## What's weak

- **No offer anywhere** — if you're targeting a milestone buyer like me, a "Free engraving on orders over $75" or "Mother's Day delivery guaranteed" banner would close the loop. Right now there's no financial hook at all.
- **Zero returning-shopper recognition** — I've bought from Pandora before. There's no "My Wishlist," no "Your Rewards," no loyalty balance — I'm treated like a stranger.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — I want to know *what* I'm shopping. Charms? The new Garden collection? A "Shop Garden Charms" label would cut through instantly.
- **No urgency copy** — it's Mother's Day week and there's no shipping deadline, no "Order by May X" callout, nothing to trigger the "I need to act today" reflex.
- **No product rail visible** — I have to tap through a hamburger menu or a generic CTA to get to anything specific. One glanceable charm strip below the hero would massively accelerate discovery.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New: Garden of Dreams Charms — Shop the Collection" sub-CTA** directly below "SHOP NOW" so tapping takes me to the specific charm drop, not an ambiguous landing page.
- **Inject a Mother's Day shipping deadline strip** at the very top — a thin dark banner: "Order by May 20 for guaranteed Mother's Day delivery." That's shippable today and it converts.
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" label** for "Shop Garden Charms" — one word change, massive clarity gain.
- **Add a horizontal scrollable charm rail** just below the hero with 4–5 specific pieces from the Garden collection so I can tap directly into a product without navigating menus.
