# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- OK this one actually got me — the hero is basically *my wrist* staring back at me. Pandora is leading with Moments charm bracelets, ocean-themed charms, a woman holding her stack right up to her smile, and the headline is dead-on for a new collection drop. This reads like a brand that knows its core buyer, but above the fold it's all emotion and zero offer — no price hook, no loyalty nudge, nothing to close.

## What's working

- **The hero imagery is elite.** A close-up of a woman beaming while her charm bracelet — packed with blue, teal, and shell-type charms — fills the frame. That's not a model, that's a moment. It mirrors exactly why I wear charms.
- **"MAKE WAVES WITH PANDORA MOMENTS"** lands the collection name right in the headline. As someone who tracks limited drops weekly, knowing this is a Moments collection instantly orients me.
- **"SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" CTA** is clear, placed cleanly at the bottom of the hero, and unambiguous about what I'm getting into.
- **Video hero with controls** — pause and sound icons are visible, which tells me this is dynamic content. A brand investing in a motion hero for a new collection drop signals this isn't a dusty homepage.
- **Ocean/wave theme is seasonally current.** Late May, summer is coming, and "make waves" is the right energy for a charm collector who just finished buying Mother's Day gifts and is eyeing what's next.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever.** I just burned a Mother's Day budget and I need a reason to keep going. A "members get 20% off," a "new arrivals + free shipping," anything — there's nothing here.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only.** No quick-tap category strip. If I want to go directly to "Charms" or filter by "Rose Gold" I'm hunting through a collapsed menu instead of tapping a tab.
- **No loyalty hook.** Pandora has a loyalty program and I'm a repeat buyer. The fact that my rewards balance or member status is invisible above the fold is a missed retention play.
- **The "FEEDBACK" tab** (pink, rotated vertically on the right edge) is visually distracting and bleeds into the hero. It's a UX survey tool, not a brand element — it undercuts the editorial feel of the hero.
- **No social proof, no charm count, no "this drop sold out in 48 hours" urgency cue.** For limited-edition charms, that kind of signal is what makes me act instead of saving to a wishlist.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty/member badge to the hero.** Something like "Members: Early Access to New Arrivals" overlaid on the CTA or as a sub-banner. Takes one week to ship and immediately differentiates returning customers from first-timers.
- **Introduce a persistent category strip below the nav.** "Charms · Bracelets · New · Gifts" — even four tappable pills gets me to my category in one tap instead of hunting through the hamburger.
- **Put the free-shipping threshold in a slim announcement bar above the hero.** "Free shipping on orders $50+" costs nothing and converts fence-sitters. I need to know this before I start building a cart.
- **Remove or relocate the "FEEDBACK" survey tab.** It's breaking the immersion of a beautiful editorial hero — push it into the footer or a post-scroll trigger.
