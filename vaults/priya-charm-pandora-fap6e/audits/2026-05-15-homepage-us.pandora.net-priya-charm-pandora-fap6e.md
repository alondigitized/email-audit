# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is going full whimsy right now — the "Garden of Dreams" campaign is dreamy, feminine, and charming (literally), and as someone who lives for charm milestone moments, this aesthetic speaks my language immediately. That said, the homepage is almost entirely vibes above the fold: beautiful illustration, a charm peeking out of a flower, and a "SHOP NOW" that leads... somewhere. I'm intrigued but not yet guided.

## What's working

- **The charm-in-the-flower visual:** I spotted it immediately — a red/pink apple (or strawberry?) charm hanging from an illustrated bell flower. For me that's a stop-the-scroll moment. It's product and story in one frame.
- **"Garden of Dreams" headline:** Aspirational without being cheesy. Hits the milestone-collector mindset — charms *are* dreams made tangible.
- **Spring/Mother's Day timing:** May is prime Pandora season for me (I always buy at Mother's Day windows) and a garden campaign in full bloom feels intentional and current, not stale.
- **Video hero with mute/pause controls:** Signals there's motion happening — likely animated botanicals — without forcing autoplay audio. That's respectful UX.
- **Clean mobile layout:** Logo, search, account, cart, hamburger — everything is where I expect it. Nothing fighting for space.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is directionless:** I collect charms. I want "Shop Charms," not a generic destination I have to trust. One tap of faith is fine — zero reassurance is not.
- **No offer, no urgency:** It's Mother's Day week and there is zero promo information above the fold. If Pandora is running a gift-with-purchase or a limited charm drop, I have no idea from this screen.
- **No loyalty hook:** I almost certainly have Pandora points or a club membership. Showing me my status or a "members earn double this weekend" strip would take me from warm to hot.
- **No human in the hero:** The illustration is beautiful but it's fully botanical — no wrist shot, no styled stack, no one wearing the jewelry. I want to see the charm on a bracelet, not floating in a garden.
- **"FEEDBACK" tab on the right edge:** It's a minor thing but that magenta-ish "FEEDBACK" rotated tab cuts into my visual field and feels like a sticky note someone forgot to pull off before publishing.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop New Charms"** — one word change, zero effort, immediately relevant to the people who converted on this campaign before.
- **Add a Mother's Day promo strip above the hero:** Even a thin bar — "Celebrate Mom: Free gift bag with $75+" — converts warm traffic in May without redesigning the hero.
- **Layer a wrist shot into the illustration:** The botanical art is the hero, but at least one inset or corner showing an actual charm bracelet styled against the garden background closes the gap between beautiful and buyable.
- **Surface a loyalty badge or points reminder for signed-in users:** "You have 240 points — use them on this collection" would make me tap before I even finish reading the headline.
