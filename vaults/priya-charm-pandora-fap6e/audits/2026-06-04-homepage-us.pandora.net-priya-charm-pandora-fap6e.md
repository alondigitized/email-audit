# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is clearly leaning hard into the Moments charm line right now — this "Make Waves" campaign screams summer collection drop, which honestly is my language. The hero is beautiful and on-brand, but above the fold it's basically one big mood image with a single CTA and zero offers, which means they're asking me to do all the heavy lifting of wanting to buy without giving me any reason to act right now.

## What's working

- **"MAKE WAVES WITH PANDORA MOMENTS" headline** — calling out the Moments line by name is smart; it signals to collectors like me that this is a charm-specific campaign, not a generic brand push
- **The charm bowl visual** — that close-up of a dish full of blue/teal charms is exactly the content I pause for on Instagram; it activates the "I want to see what those individual charms are" reflex immediately
- **Single-focus hero** — there's no clutter, no competing banners; the brand clearly has confidence in the collection and it shows
- **Seasonal relevance** — "Make Waves" in early June feels intentional and fresh, not recycled from last quarter

## What's weak

- **Zero offer above fold** — I'm a window buyer (Mother's Day, birthdays), and a small "free bracelet over $X" or "new charm + bracelet bundle" would turn browse into buy intent instantly; nothing here does that
- **No limited-edition signal** — I track charm drops weekly and this could be a major LE launch, but the hero gives me no urgency hook; "new collection" is vague
- **No quick-tap category navigation** — I can see a hamburger menu but no exposed nav links; if I want to go directly to "Moments Charms" or filter by metal color (I stack rose-gold + sterling), I have to dig
- **No trust or social proof above fold** — no review count, no "bestseller" badge, nothing that signals other collectors are already grabbing these
- **The feedback tab** — that pink "FEEDBACK" sidebar tab is a minor but persistent visual distraction on an otherwise clean hero

## Recommendations

- **Add a charm-specific urgency strip above the hero** — even a simple "New Summer Charms — Limited Drops Weekly" banner at the top would speak directly to subscribers like me who are already in the habit of checking
- **Swap vague CTA for a collection-specific one** — "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" is fine, but "SHOP MAKE WAVES COLLECTION" would feel more like a curated drop and less like a clearance rack browse
- **Surface a free-shipping or free-bracelet threshold below the headline** — something like "Free Moments bracelet when you build a stack of 3+" would be a conversion magnet for buyers already thinking in charm combos
- **Expose 2-3 nav shortcuts under the hero** — "Charms | Bracelets | Gift Sets" as quick-tap chips would let returning collectors skip straight to their category without a full menu open
