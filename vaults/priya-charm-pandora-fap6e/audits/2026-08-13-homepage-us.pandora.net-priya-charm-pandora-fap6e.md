# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, gorgeous hero shot — that stacked bangle-and-rings moment is very "my aesthetic" — but this reads like a general jewelry brand video ad, not a charm bracelet destination. Pandora is pushing a new "Essence, evolved" nature-inspired collection front and center, and while it's beautifully shot, there's zero mention of charms, no offer, and nothing pulling me toward my actual Sunday-scroll habit of hunting limited-edition drops.

## What's working

- **The video hero itself** — soft, cinematic, a woman wearing stacked gold bangles and rings. It's tasteful and on-brand for a "bracelet curator" mood board even if it's not selling me charms directly.
- **Nav bar has both "Charms" and "Bracelets" as top-level tabs** — I can get to my category in one tap without digging through a mega-menu.
- **"SHOP NOW" button is unmistakable** — dark pill button, high contrast, sits right under the headline. No hunting for it.
- **Page feels clean and uncluttered** — no popups, no forced email capture blocking my first look.

## What's weak

- **"NEW NATURE-INSPIRED JEWELRY" is category-agnostic** — I collect charms one milestone at a time, and this headline doesn't tell me if there's a single new charm in this drop.
- **No offer or loyalty callout anywhere above the fold** — I track drops weekly specifically because Pandora usually dangles something (member pricing, early access). This screen gives me nothing to act on.
- **That "©Disney" credit in the corner is a tease with no context** — if there's a Disney charm collab in here, show it! That's exactly the kind of limited-edition thing I'd tap immediately.
- **The pink "FEEDBACK" tab hugging the right edge of the hero is a little jarring** — it visually competes with the video and looks like a stray UI element rather than a designed part of the page.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "Shop Now" doesn't tell me if I'm shopping charms, bracelets, or the whole new collection. It's asking me to trust a click, not making a promise.

## Recommendations

- Swap the eyebrow line from "PANDORA ESSENCE, evolved" to something that name-checks charms directly — even "New charms inspired by nature" would hook me instantly.
- Surface that Disney credit as an actual visible module — a small "New: Disney x Pandora charms" strip under the hero would turn a mystery into a click.
- Add a thin promo/loyalty strip right under the nav (member pricing, free shipping threshold, or "Charm Club" rewards balance) so there's a reason to act today, not just browse.
- Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop New Charms" or similar so the CTA actually routes my expectation to my category.
