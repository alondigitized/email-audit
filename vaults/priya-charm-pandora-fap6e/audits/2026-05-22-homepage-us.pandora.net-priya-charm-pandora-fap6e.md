# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Pandora opened strong — that image of charms spilling out of a bowl is 100% speaking my language, and "Make Waves with Pandora Moments" is a clean, on-brand summer drop message. But this is basically a one-slide homepage on mobile: gorgeous hero, one CTA, and then... nothing else above the fold. For someone like me who checks in weekly for limited-edition drops, there's no urgency hook, no loyalty callout, no "here's what just landed" — just vibes and a button.

## What's working

- **The charm bowl imagery** — showing charms loose in a bowl rather than worn on a wrist is a fresh, editorial choice that feels curated, not mass-market. I immediately want to dig through that bowl myself.
- **"MAKE WAVES WITH PANDORA MOMENTS"** — naming the Moments line in the headline directly tells returning collectors like me this is about charms, not rings or earrings. That specificity earns attention.
- **"SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" CTA** — it's in the right place, legible against the dark footer bar, and the copy is direct. No "Discover" or "Explore" — it says shop, which I appreciate.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no popups, no aggressive banner overlays, no "10% off if you subscribe" modal blocking the hero. It loads and breathes.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer visible** — I'm a buyer at the Mother's Day and birthday windows, and May 22 is squarely in that zone. Not showing me any promotion — even "free shipping on $75+" — is a miss. I might assume there's nothing and bounce.
- **No loyalty hook** — I know Pandora has a loyalty program, but there's nothing on screen reminding me of my points balance or member-exclusive drops. That's a retention lever left on the table.
- **CTA isn't category-specific** — "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" could mean anything. "SHOP NEW MOMENTS CHARMS" would have me tapping instantly vs. wondering if this is all about rings.
- **No urgency or scarcity signal** — I track limited-edition charm drops weekly. Nothing here tells me this is a limited run or time-sensitive. The "Make Waves" collection could feel like it's always available.
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — all categories are hidden behind a menu icon. I can't see "Charms" or "Bracelets" in one tap without opening the nav.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Limited Edition" or "Mother's Day Ends [Date]" urgency strip** directly below the nav bar — even a thin one-line banner. It's May; that window is closing and I need to know.
- **Swap the generic CTA to "SHOP NEW MOMENTS CHARMS"** — one word change, huge relevance lift for charm collectors without alienating other shoppers if you test it.
- **Surface a loyalty/points callout on the hero** — a small badge like "Earn 3x points on new arrivals" sits cleanly in the lower corner of the hero without cluttering the image.
- **Add a horizontal scrolling "Just Dropped" charm rail** immediately below the hero — five to six charm thumbnails with names and prices. It's the single fastest way to get me from hero to product page.
