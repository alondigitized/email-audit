# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is leading with a full-bleed artistic hero — illustrated botanicals, a charm dangling off a painted flower stem, dreamy pink palette — and the campaign is called "Garden of Dreams." It reads immediately as a spring collection launch, and the aesthetic is unmistakably Pandora: romantic, layered, feminine. What it is *not* doing is making me feel like a known customer or giving me any reason to act right now.

## What's working

- **The hero charm detail** — there's a recognizable charm piece hanging off the illustrated flower. As a bracelet curator I clocked it immediately; that is exactly the micro-storytelling Pandora does well.
- **"GARDEN OF DREAMS" headline** — evocative, millennial-coded language. It doesn't scream sale, it whispers *occasion*, which fits how I actually think about buying charms (milestone, gift window, limited edition).
- **Clean mobile layout** — logo, search, account, cart, and hamburger are all in the right spots. No clutter fighting the hero.
- **Spring timing** — May is Mother's Day window, which is my single biggest buying trigger. A garden theme is seasonally smart even if it doesn't say "Mother's Day" explicitly in this viewport.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook whatsoever** — Pandora has a rewards program (Pandora Club) and I have points. Nothing here acknowledges me as a returning customer. A simple "Your rewards: X points" in the nav would change my posture immediately.
- **Zero offer above the fold** — Mother's Day is *right now* and the hero is giving me mood board, not incentive. No "Free shipping on orders over $X," no "Gift with purchase," nothing.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — where am I going? New charms? The Garden of Dreams collection specifically? Mother's Day gifts? I'd tap it, but I shouldn't have to trust it.
- **No person in the imagery** — the hero is illustrated, artistic, beautiful — but I can't see a wrist stacked with charms. That aspirational "I want my arm to look like that" moment is missing.
- **Video controls suggest autoplay video** — the pause and audio icons are visible, meaning this is a video hero. If it's loud on autoplay that's an immediate bounce for me in public.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Explore Garden of Dreams"** — specificity increases tap confidence, and it ties the CTA to the named campaign.
- **Add a Mother's Day urgency strip above or below the hero** — one line: "Order by May 10 for Mother's Day delivery. Free shipping over $X." That's shippable in an afternoon and converts my existing intent into action.
- **Surface a loyalty badge in the nav bar** — even a small "Club Member" chip next to the cart icon tells me this brand knows me. That alone lifts my conversion likelihood.
- **Include one lifestyle close-up of a stacked bracelet** — a wrist shot of the Garden of Dreams charms on a rose-gold Moments band would connect the illustrated world to the physical product I'm actually buying.
