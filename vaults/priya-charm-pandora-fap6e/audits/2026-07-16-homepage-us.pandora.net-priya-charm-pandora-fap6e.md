# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Pandora is leading with a new collection drop — "PANDORA WONDERS IS HERE" — and as someone who literally checks Pandora for new charm releases every week, this immediately has my attention. The hero is moody, jewel-focused, and fantasy-coded, which feels on-brand for a limited-edition charm launch. But above the fold there is zero promotion, no loyalty hook, and no price anchor — it's all drama, no deal.

## What's working

- **"Explore our new charms" subhead** — this is the hook I'd read first before the big headline even registers. Charm drop vocabulary, exactly the language I use internally when I open the app.
- **The hero charm itself** — that gold whimsical design with crystals is genuinely beautiful and unfamiliar, which signals this is something I haven't seen before. That's enough to make me want to tap.
- **"PANDORA WONDERS IS HERE"** — the campaign name is specific and evocative. It feels like an event, not just a sale. That framing works for collectors who think in terms of eras and drops.
- **Clean video hero** — the pause and sound-toggle controls at the bottom tell me this is video, which keeps it feeling premium and editorial rather than static-banner.
- **Minimal nav chrome** — the hamburger + icon row doesn't crowd the experience; it keeps the focus on the product.

## What's weak

- **Zero promotional offer above the fold** — I'm a window shopper who times purchases around Mother's Day and birthdays. If there's a sale on right now, I can't see it and I'm not scrolling to hunt for it.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — it should say "Shop Pandora Wonders" or "Explore the Collection." I want to know I'm going to the Wonders drop, not some generic landing page.
- **No loyalty/rewards signal** — I have a Pandora club account and I've accumulated points. Not seeing my balance or any member-exclusive messaging is a missed retention play.
- **Partially visible woman on the right edge** — whatever lifestyle context that model is supposed to add is lost because she's cropped out. Either give her the full frame or remove her; right now she's visual noise.
- **No trust signals whatsoever** — no "free shipping on orders over $X," no review counts, nothing. For a charm that might run $75–$120, I want some reassurance before I tap.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to "Shop Pandora Wonders"** — anchors the tap to the specific campaign and reduces bounce when landing on the collection page.
- **Add a slim promo strip above or below the hero** — even a "Free shipping on orders $75+" would meaningfully reduce my hesitation. It's one line, it can ship tomorrow.
- **Surface a loyalty/points badge in the header or hero overlay** — a small "✦ Member Perks" chip or my points balance would make me feel seen as a returning customer.
- **Either widen the lifestyle shot on the right to full-bleed or remove it** — the half-visible cropped model reads as a broken layout on mobile. Lean fully into the product close-up.
