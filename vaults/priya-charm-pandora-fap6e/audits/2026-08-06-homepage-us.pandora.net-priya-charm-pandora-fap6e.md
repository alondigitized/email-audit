# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Pandora's leaning hard into a moody, editorial "Essence, evolved" campaign right now — all soft-focus hands and gold rings, very elevated jewelry-brand energy. It reads premium and on-brand for Pandora, but as someone who lives for charm drops, I'm scrolling this looking for my bracelet fix and instead getting rings. Nothing wrong with the mood, but it's not speaking to me specifically yet.

## What's working

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — sits in a clean dark bar at the bottom of the hero, easy to spot and tap.
- **Cinematic hero visual** — the clasped-hands, ring-stacked shot feels premium and editorial, which fits Pandora's brand even if it's not my category today.
- **"NEW NATURE-INSPIRED JEWELRY" headline** — bold, legible, immediately tells me there's a fresh drop, which is exactly the kind of signal I scan for as someone who tracks limited releases weekly.

## What's weak

- **No charms or bracelets anywhere above the fold** — I'm a bracelet-and-charm shopper and this entire hero is rings. Feels like a miss for my segment specifically.
- **That pink "FEEDBACK" tab** — it's wedged into the right edge of the hero image and looks like a stray dev/testing widget, not a polished part of the page. Pulls my eye away from the product shot.
- **No offer or loyalty callout visible** — I'm a Pandora Club member type of shopper, and there's nothing about rewards, points, or even a seasonal discount up top.
- **"©Disney" credit with no context** — intriguing (collab?) but there's zero copy explaining it, so it just sits there as a mystery watermark.

## Recommendations

- Swap in a hero rotation that includes a charm-bracelet moment — even alternating rings/bracelets in the video would catch my eye faster.
- Add a small rewards or "Pandora Club members: shop new arrivals first" ribbon above or below the headline.
- Move or restyle that pink feedback tab so it doesn't look like a UI glitch sitting on top of the campaign image.
- If the Disney tie-in is real, put a one-line teaser ("Disney x Pandora collection") near the credit so it reads as a feature, not an accident.
