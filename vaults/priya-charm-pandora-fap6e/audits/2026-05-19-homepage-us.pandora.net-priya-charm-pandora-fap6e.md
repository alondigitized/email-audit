# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Pandora is leading with full-brand energy right now — the "Garden of Dreams" hero is lush, dreamy, and very on-aesthetic for a charm collector who lives for botanical drops. That said, above the fold this feels like a brand awareness moment, not a selling moment: no offer, no loyalty hook, no charm category shortcut. Beautiful to look at, but I have to do the work to get to what I actually came here for.

## What's working

- **The "Garden of Dreams" campaign visual** — the illustrated charms woven into botanical linework is gorgeous and immediately signals this is a new limited-edition drop, which is exactly the content I check weekly
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA placement** — anchored to a dark footer bar that contrasts cleanly against the pink hero; I will not miss it
- **Seasonal timing** — floral garden campaign in mid-May, right at Mother's Day, is perfectly timed for my biggest annual buying window
- **Video/animated hero** — the pause and audio controls tell me this is a motion piece, which elevates the launch-event feeling of a limited collection

## What's weak

- **Zero offer above the fold** — no free-shipping callout, no member pricing, no "new arrivals" badge; for a returning customer like me, there's nothing to reward loyalty before the scroll
- **No charm-specific navigation visible** — I can't tap directly to charms, bracelets, or new arrivals without hitting the hamburger menu and hunting; one extra tap = friction I notice
- **No human model or lifestyle context** — the hero is fully illustrated; I can't see how the charm actually looks stacked on a wrist, which is the first thing I want to see
- **No loyalty/rewards signal** — Pandora has a club program; not seeing it above the fold is a miss for a high-frequency buyer like me
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — doesn't tell me whether I'm landing in charms, the Garden collection, or the full catalog

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line loyalty hook to the hero bar** — something like "Club member? Unlock early access to Garden of Dreams" sits right next to "SHOP NOW" and costs nothing to ship
- **Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for collection-specific copy** — "Shop Garden of Dreams" or "Explore the Charms" removes ambiguity and keeps me in the right mental frame
- **Add a free-shipping strip above the hero** — a thin banner ("Free shipping on orders $50+" or "Free shipping through Mother's Day") requires minimal layout work and directly impacts conversion for my AOV
- **Surface one charm or bracelet image from the collection** — even a small product thumbnail peeking at the bottom of the viewport would tease me toward scrolling and confirm this is charms-first, not just a campaign landing
