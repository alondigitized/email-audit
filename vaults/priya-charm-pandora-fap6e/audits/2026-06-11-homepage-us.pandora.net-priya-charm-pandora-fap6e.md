# Homepage snapshot · us.pandora.net · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, immediately I'm intrigued — Pandora is opening with a close-up charm shot of what looks like a nautical drop, charms dangling against warm skin, and that copy "FOUND AT SEA. DISCOVERED BY YOU." hits the right poetic note for someone who treats every charm like a story. This reads like a limited-edition collection launch, which is exactly my trigger-point. That said, above the fold it's just the hero and a single "SHOP NOW" — no price context, no loyalty hook, nothing to confirm I'm getting anything extra as a repeat buyer.

## What's working

- **"FOUND AT SEA. DISCOVERED BY YOU."** — that copy is doing real work. The "discovered by you" framing taps directly into the collector identity. I feel seen as a curator, not just a shopper.
- **The charm close-up shot** is gorgeous and specific: I can make out individual charms (blue enamel, what looks like a starfish or sea motif, a pearl-style piece). As someone who tracks drops weekly, seeing actual charm details in the hero is a green light to keep scrolling.
- **Clean mobile layout** — logo, search, account, cart, and hamburger are all accessible in a single row. No clutter.
- **The "SHOP NOW" button** is clearly separated from the hero text, dark pill on a dark-image background, not buried.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer signal above the fold** — I'm a repeat buyer and I have no idea if there's a current promo, a club member rate, or a free-shipping minimum. If I hadn't planned to browse anyway, there's nothing pulling me in.
- **No loyalty/rewards acknowledgment** — Pandora has a loyalty program and I participate. The homepage doesn't acknowledge me as a returning customer at all; it reads like an acquisition page, not retention.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — I'd tap "Shop the Found at Sea Collection" or "Explore the Drop" far faster. The CTA copy doesn't carry the campaign's own energy.
- **No urgency or scarcity cue** — limited-edition drops are my thing, but there's no "new arrival," "just dropped," or "limited quantities" signal here. The word "fresh" is in the sub-headline but it's small and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for campaign-specific CTA copy** — "Explore Found at Sea" or "Shop the Drop" costs one line of text and immediately raises the tap rate for charm-focused shoppers like me.
- **Add a thin loyalty/rewards strip below the hero** — one line: "Pandora Club members earn double points this week" would make returning buyers feel prioritized and immediately differentiate the experience from first-visit browsing.
- **Surface a charm count or "X new charms in this collection"** — a small badge or sub-copy like "14 new charms" gives collectors a concrete reason to click now vs. later.
- **Add a "Free shipping on orders over $X" anchor** somewhere visible above fold — even a small persistent strip at the very top; it removes friction at the decision moment.
