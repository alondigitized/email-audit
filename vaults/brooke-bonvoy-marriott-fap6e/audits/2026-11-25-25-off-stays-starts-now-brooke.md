# 25% Off Stays Starts Now, Brooke 🎉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first Cyber Sale send. The hero says "Members Save 25% in the App" over a turquoise beach photo, with "OFFER ENDS TUESDAY, DECEMBER 1" underneath. The offer and deadline are both clear. It also fits the season, and it is a better fit than the card-acquisition sends from 11/05 and 11/10.
- The problem is the "in the App" gate. Everything after the hero funnels the reader to scan a QR code, and a QR code in an email that is probably being read on a phone is a dead end. There is no big button. The only web fallback is a small link, "marriott.com offer page", and it gives 15% instead of 25%. That is a buried downgrade.
- The lower half is a long scroll: a destination grid, a partner logo strip, three secondary deals and a paid ad banner. The main offer gets diluted. Compared with the 11/17 "Start Your Vacation Countdown" send, which was a mismatched card pitch, this one delivers on its subject. But it asks for an extra step the reader will often skip.

## What's working

- The hero states a concrete offer ("25%") and a deadline (Dec 1) in large type that the eye lands on first.
- The Book by / Stay between boxes state the booking window (Dec 1) and the stay window (Nov 29 to May 2, 2027) in one place.
- The subject delivers on the body. The 25% claim matches the hero, unlike the 11/14 "15,000+ Points, Unwrapped" send.
- The destination grid with city labels gives easy, scannable inspiration.
- The seasonal timing is right: Cyber Sale, Black Friday and holiday savings are all present.

## What's weak

- The primary action is a QR code with no tappable button. A phone reader can't scan a code on their own screen.
- The fallback offer is only 15% and sits in small text, which feels like a bait-and-switch.
- The email runs very long, with a destination grid, partner logos, three secondary deals and an ad. The main CTA is buried.
- The "30% off" Boutiques deal and the "double points on cruises" deal compete with the 25% headline.
- The "Brooke" personalization shows up only in the subject. The body has no member-tier or points hint.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the QR block with a large "Open in App and Save 25%" button that deep-links to the app or the app store. Keep the QR code only as a desktop fallback.
- 2. Put the 15% web fallback in a visible secondary button, so mobile-web readers don't feel locked out.
- 3. Cut the destination grid to 4 to 6 tiles, and make each tile link to a specific hotel or region page.
- 4. Move the Boutiques, Cruise and Holiday Savings blocks into a single row, and drop the ad banner.
- 5. Add a countdown or "Ends Tuesday" in the preheader and near the CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke, 25% off stays ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% Cyber Sale price: app only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book by Dec 1 for stays through May 2027. Open the app to claim it.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beach, city or mountains: 7,500 hotels are 25% off for members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard table-based, ESP-tracked template with Outlook/VML fallbacks. The QA run couldn't verify most of its links: 11 of 12 probes failed, 10 with 403 and 1 with a probe-side encoding error. Several compliance and deliverability checks also flag missing items, but part of that evidence may come from the AgentMail relay or truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on 10 of 12 probed links.** Every link goes through `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a214.../<linkid>/<msgid>?__dU__=...&__F__=...`. The failures include "find & reserve", the eight "Book a stay in extraordinary destinations such as {Hawaii, Miami, Mexico, Las Vegas, New Orleans, London, Los Angeles, Seattle, Atlanta}" links and UNSUBSCRIBE. A uniform 403 across the same redirector, including the unsubscribe link, points to the click-tracker rejecting the QA probe as a bot. It doesn't show that the links are broken for real recipients. Treat it as unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **"Download and Save 25%" probe error.** `'ascii' codec can't encode character '\xa0' in position 17`. This is an exception in the QA tool, not an HTTP result. A non-breaking space is probably in the link text or header handling, so the primary CTA has no valid probe result. Whether the `href` itself contains `\xa0` can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Final destinations unresolved.** The wrapped links can't be followed, so destination and redirect-chain health are unknown.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 50 links were probed (38 skipped by ranking), so the rest are unaudited.
- **Tracking pixels.** Many `sli.marriott.com/imp?s=...&stpe=pixel|static|default` impression images are present. They are normal for dynamic content and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA run flagged 11 images. One is the content image `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a214135223a99b576e966a3578/35abfcb219b14ec800004c5a42963aa1`, which needs descriptive alt or `alt=""` if decorative. The other 10 are `sli.marriott.com/imp?...` tracking and impression images. These should carry `alt=""` and are low risk, but some may be dynamic-content slots that do render visibly.
- **Web fonts.** The email loads Typekit (`use.typekit.net/yft3kyd.css`) and `cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/...` fonts. Each has a fallback stack (Times New Roman, Arial, Brush Script MT), and the Outlook-conditional CSS overrides them. No issue.
- **Other rendering checks.** Viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, MSO DPI fix and a 480px media query are present. I found no defects in the visible portion. The source was truncated, so the body wasn't audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. "Brooke" renders in both `<title>` and the subject, with no unresolved tokens in the visible HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text part.** The QA run shows `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. If the relay didn't strip it, this hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** Both were not found. The QA tool says the relay may not capture these headers. Marriott likely sends them, but this can't be confirmed from the data, so inspect the raw message.
- **Physical address.** `[WARN] No physical address detected`. The footer was likely cut off by the HTML truncation, so this needs a check against the full source.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not present, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC are unknown. The sender is `marriottbonvoy@email-marriott.com` and tracking uses the same domain, so alignment is plausible.
- **In-body unsubscribe.** An UNSUBSCRIBE link exists (`.../35abfcb219b14ec80000021ef3a0bceb/...`), but it returned 403 to the probe. Its function is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. All destinations sit behind the `email-marriott.com/T/...` redirector, which returned 403, so no final URL, UTM parameters or landing page could be inspected. No UTM parameters appear on the visible wrapped URLs. That is expected for an ESP-wrapped link and isn't a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow the redirects with a headed client. Treat the 403s as a probe limitation until a real-browser request fails.
2. Fix the QA tool's handling of non-ASCII characters (`\xa0`) by encoding with UTF-8 and normalizing whitespace in link text. Then re-test "Download and Save 25%", the primary CTA.
3. Pull the raw MIME message to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the text/plain part. Then confirm the footer address in the untruncated HTML.
4. Add `alt` text to the content image `...35abfcb219b14ec800004c5a42963aa1`, and `alt=""` to the `sli.marriott.com/imp` pixels.
5. Resolve the redirect chains, then verify UTM parameters and landing page alignment.
