# Enjoy Up to 15% off Your NYC Stay, Free Cocktails, and More
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a five-hotel Moxy NYC roundup sent from Marriott's Bonvoy sender. Each property gets the same offer: up to 15% off plus two free cocktails or margaritas. The structure is clean. A big Chelsea hero room shot comes first, then four dark alternating image-and-copy blocks, each with a purple "Book Now" button. It's easy to scan and looks like a real brand. The offer is also the most concrete one in recent sends. It beats the vague "Four Escapes, One Beautiful Season" from 2026-10-02.
- The problem is that nothing tells me why to book now. There is no travel window, no deadline, no promo code, no minimum stay, and no mention of Bonvoy points or member rates. "Up to 15%" with "and more" in the subject is soft. I'm also shown five near-identical offers with five identical buttons. The email never says which hotel to pick, so I have to compare them myself.
- Overall it's a competent but generic hotel-deal email. It's a clear step up from the card-acquisition sends of late September, but it stops short of being persuasive.

## What's working

- The Chelsea hero image is strong. A skyline-view bedroom sells the NYC premise immediately.
- The offer is concrete and repeated consistently: up to 15% off plus free cocktails or margaritas at every property.
- The layout alternates image left and right across dark panels, which keeps five similar blocks from feeling monotonous.
- The purple "Book Now" buttons are large, high-contrast and unmistakable.
- Each block names a specific neighborhood (Chelsea, Times Square, East Village, Williamsburg, Lower East Side) and a concrete amenity, such as a rooftop bar, a lobby lounge or a Japanese restaurant. That gives each property its own pitch.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline, booking window or stay-by date anywhere, so there is zero urgency.
- "Up to 15%" is hedged. Some blocks say "premium rooms" and others say "your stay", which makes the offer inconsistent and a little confusing.
- Bonvoy member pricing and points earning aren't mentioned, even though this comes from Marriott's loyalty program.
- All five buttons say "Book Now" and nothing more. There is no price, no "from $X" and no differentiation, so the choice is left to me.
- The large Marriott brand-portfolio grid and legal footer take up the bottom fifth of the email. They add nothing to the Moxy pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and a stay window to the hero, for example "Book by Oct 31 · Stay through Jan 15". Urgency is the biggest missing piece.
- 2. Lead with one hotel and one price, such as "From $149/night at Moxy NYC Chelsea". Collapse the other four into a compact "More Moxy NYC" strip with a "from" rate each.
- 3. Show the member angle: "Bonvoy members: 15% off + earn points on every night". That would raise value and relevance for this recipient.
- 4. Make the buttons specific: "Book Chelsea", "Book Times Square" and so on.
- 5. Pull the cocktail offer into a visible badge on the hero image, because it's the most distinctive hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `NYC from $X a night + 2 free cocktails at Moxy`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rooftop views, free margaritas, 15% off NYC`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five Moxy NYC hotels, each with a rooftop bar. Book by Oct 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bonvoy members: save up to 15% and get two free drinks per stay.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has several head-level defects: an empty `<title>`, a malformed viewport tag, and a malformed robots tag. It also has a 34-character plain-text part and six images without alt text. Every probed tracked link returned 403, so none of the destination URLs, UTM parameters or unsubscribe behavior could be verified. This is probably a bot block on the click-tracker rather than dead links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 10 probed links on `email-marriott.com/T/...` returned 403. They include "Book Your Stay in NYC", "Find & Reserve", five "BOOK NOW" links, "FIND HOTELS" and "UNSUBSCRIBE".
- A uniform 403 across every link, including unsubscribe, points to bot or WAF filtering on the tracking redirector (Epsilon/Harmony). It does not point to broken links. The final destinations are unconfirmed, so the "Broken link" FAILs should be treated as unverified, not as confirmed defects.
- The tracked URLs are very long, with a `__F__=` blob of about 500 to 700 characters. Some clients and link scanners truncate or mangle URLs that long.
- Two image links (`.../d45ca69d6735477d0000021ef3a0bcc5/...` and `.../...bcc8/...`) have no usable link text. The QA output shows "[image: images]" for both.
- Only 12 of 30 links were probed, so 18 footer and social links are unchecked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Malformed viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial scale=1.0">` uses `initial scale`, which should be `initial-scale`. Some clients may ignore the initial-scale value.
- **Malformed robots tag:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The value is invalid as written.
- **Missing alt text on 5 images** (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images?id=...`):

  At least two of these are wrapped in links, so those links have no accessible name.
- The tracking pixel `email-marriott.com/O/v61000001a12226b201a8ae27369fe94b70/d45ca69d6735477d00004c5a42963aa1` has no alt text. This is low severity for a pixel, but `alt=""` is preferable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. QA notes they may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay and not a confirmed failure. The From domain (`email-marriott.com`) matches the tracking domain, which is good for alignment.
- **Body unsubscribe link:** An "UNSUBSCRIBE" link is present, but the 403 means its function is unverified.
- **Physical postal address:** It is not visible in the truncated HTML, so it could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. Every link is wrapped in an opaque redirect (`email-marriott.com/T/...?__dU__=...&__F__=...`), and the 403s blocked resolution. No UTM parameters are visible in the email source, and any campaign tagging is encoded in the redirect. Landing-page alignment with the NYC offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracked links with a browser-like user agent or a real-browser follow, and resolve the redirect chain to confirm the destinations and UTMs. Until then, treat the 403s as probe artifacts.
2. Fill in `<title>`, fix the viewport to `initial-scale=1.0`, and change the robots value to `noindex` (or remove the tag).
3. Add descriptive alt text to the five Epsilon images, especially the linked ones. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.

5. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't rely on the relay-captured copy.
