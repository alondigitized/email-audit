# Discover New Stays and Member Offers at Lenexa City Center
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott Bonvoy doing a two-hotel grand-opening push for Lenexa City Center — a market and property type nobody's persona has cared about in this thread before. Compare it to the run of destination/points sends (Vegas 9/23, W Hotels, bonus-points registration): those at least had scale (a whole destination, a big points number) or personalization ("Brooke's Account"). This one is generic, small-stakes, and offers "exclusive member savings" without ever stating a rate or percentage — that's a real weakness against a rubric that rewards concrete offers.
- The layout itself is clean and on-brand — the autumn hero photo, black pill CTA, brand-family footer are all standard Bonvoy design language and render without bugs. But there's zero personalization (no "Brooke" anywhere, unlike three of the last four sends), zero urgency beyond a soft November 30/15 date, and the core CTA is emotionally flat ("Book Now and Save" — save how much?).
- Judgement: a competently produced but low-impact send. It reads like an automated new-property-opening notification rather than a targeted offer, and it will underperform the Vegas and points-registration emails in this same thread.

## What's working

- Hero image is warm, seasonal (fall foliage), and renders cleanly with no artifacts.
- Clear single visual hierarchy: hero → headline → offer copy → CTA button, easy to scan.
- Two distinct offers (AC Hotel savings + Residence Inn bonus points) are separated into clear visual blocks rather than crammed together.
- Brand footer with full portfolio logos reinforces trust/scale of the loyalty program.
- Both CTAs are present and visually distinct ("Book Now and Save" button, "Plan Your Stay" link).

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere — "exclusive member savings" and "3,000 bonus points" (for the second hotel only) is the only quantified benefit; the AC Hotel offer never states a percentage or dollar amount.
- Subject line ("Discover New Stays and Member Offers at Lenexa City Center") is generic and geography-specific to a market most recipients won't recognize or care about.
- No personalization — no name, no member tier, no point balance, a step down from the "Brooke" sends earlier in this thread.
- Preheader ("Weekend Savings and Bonus Points Await at Two Newly Opened Hotels") just restates the subject's theme instead of adding new information.
- Two competing offers/CTAs in one email dilute focus — a recipient interested in points has to keep scrolling past the savings pitch and vice versa.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a number. Replace "exclusive member savings" with an actual percentage or rate (e.g., "20% off weekend stays") — vague savings language kills click-through more than anything else here.
- 2. Personalize the subject/preheader with the recipient's name or tier, consistent with the brand's stronger recent sends (9/18, 9/15, 9/10 all used "Brooke").
- 3. Pick one hero offer for the subject/hero section and demote the second property to a smaller cross-sell — right now both openings compete for attention.
- 4. Add a real urgency device tied to the grand opening (e.g., "Book by Oct 15 for the lowest rate") rather than a soft "valid through November 30."
