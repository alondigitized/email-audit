# Your Fall Getaway, Four Ways
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a four-destination "local packages" email with no number in it. The subject promises "Four Ways," and the hero says "Four Escapes, One Beautiful Season." The body then asks me to "save on our best local packages" without saying how much or giving a code, a date, or a rate. After the last few sends, that reads as soft. The 9/30 email ("Act Fast to Get up to 55% Bonus Points") and the 9/24 Starbucks 5X email both led with a hard number. This one leads with a mood.
- The creative is good. The family-on-a-city-street hero is warm and on-brand, and the 2x2 grid (New York Marquis, Residence Inn LAX, SpringHill Anaheim, Westin Vancouver) is clean and easy to scan. Each tile has its own "BOOK NOW" link. The problem is that the copy is all atmosphere ("the city is yours to explore," "restorative views") and none of it is a reason to click today.
- Judgement: a pleasant brochure rather than a promotion. It's fine for browsing, but it won't drive urgency. I'd put it near the bottom of the recent run, above the card-acquisition sends only because it isn't pushing a credit card.

## What's working

- The hero photo is warm and aspirational. The family walking with shopping bags matches the fall city-break framing.
- The 2x2 hotel grid is easy to scan, with a consistent image-headline-copy-CTA pattern.
- Each tile has its own "BOOK NOW" CTA, so the click goes to a specific property.
- Naming specific hotels and cities (Times Square, LAX/Manhattan Beach, Anaheim, Vancouver) makes it feel more concrete than a generic destination blast.
- It's a clean, non-pushy break from the run of points and credit-card sends.

## What's weak

- There's no visible offer. "Best local packages" has no %, $, points, or rate, so the savings are never quantified.
- There's no deadline or urgency anywhere in the body, and no booking or stay window.
- The hero CTA, "EXPLORE," is vague. It's a pill button where the tiles use plain text links.
- The "BOOK NOW" links are small text links with a chevron, so they're easy to miss and weak against the photos.
- The "Take the Scenic Way" block ends in "VIEW MORE" with no destination or offer. It's filler, and it dilutes the four-hotel story.
- The headline is a brand tagline rather than a deal: "Four Escapes, One Beautiful Season."
- The second row's body copy sits at different heights across the two columns, so the CTAs don't line up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero. Name the saving, such as "Save up to 25% on fall stays" or "Members get 2X points." Right now the email has nothing to win a click.
- 2. Add a booking window to the hero ("Book by Oct 31, stay through Dec 15").
- 3. Replace "EXPLORE" with a verb and noun, such as "See fall packages."
- 4. Turn the tile CTAs into real buttons and add a price on each tile ("from $189/night").
- 5. Drop or sharpen the "Take the Scenic Way" block. Either tie it to a real offer or remove it.
- 6. Add a Bonvoy member-rate callout. Loyalty is Marriott's best lever, and it's absent here.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracking redirects (`email-marriott.com/T/...`) returned 403 to the QA prober on every probed link, and one link failed on a relative redirect target, so click-through could not be verified. The plain-text part is only 34 characters, and 10 images have no alt text, including images that are the only content of a link. Automated QA pass rate was 21%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on all probed tracked links**: "Save on our best local packages this fall.", "» Find & Reserve", "EXPLORE", the `header_find_m.png` link, the `images` link, and "UNSUBSCRIBE" (`.../e9b8f1a3113948c40000021ef3a0bce2/...`). The 403s are probably the ESP click-tracker blocking non-browser clients. The final destinations were never resolved, so the links are unverified rather than confirmed broken.
- **"My Account" link error**: `unknown url type: '/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?lk=1000301947&nck=1028103536&ck=11847352'`. The redirect resolved to a **relative path** with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve this against the tracking domain (`email-marriott.com`), which would 404 there. The probe's HTTP client rejects it outright. This is the one failure that looks like a real defect.
- **Bot-gated tracking links**: the 403s also mean the QA data can't confirm whether the unsubscribe link works.
- **Long opaque query strings**: the `__dU__` and `__F__` parameters run well over 400 characters per link. This adds to HTML size, but no failure is evidenced.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 10 images**:
  - `header_find_m.png` (`media.4at5.net/.../header_find_m.png`).
  - Eight Epsilon `ContentHandler/images?id=...` images (ids `359d2654…`, `7be9e527…`, `3f67ada7…` ×2, `177bec9d…`, `6384debf…`, `9dcdc472…`, `ae8d7a62…`).
  - The open-tracking pixel `email-marriott.com/O/.../e9b8f1a3113948c400004c5a42963aa1`. A pixel without alt is low impact.
- **Links with no accessible name**: the probe reported link text as `[image: header_find_m.png]` and `[image: images]`. These are image-only links whose images have no alt, so screen readers get no label.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**: present in the head.
- **Viewport meta typo**: the source reads `content="width=device-width, initial scale=1.0"`. It should be `initial-scale=1.0`, so mobile clients may ignore the scale directive.
- **Robots meta typo**: `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is low impact for email.
- **Invalid CSS**: `vertical-align: text-top .6em` in the `sup` rule is not a valid value. Clients ignore it.
- **Web fonts**: they are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and fall back to Arial and Times New Roman in the `@media` rules, so no issue found.

2. **Re-probe the tracked links with a browser User-Agent or allowlist the QA prober**, and confirm that the unsubscribe link resolves.
3. **Add alt text to the 10 images**:
   - Use meaningful alt text on all linked images, especially `header_find_m.png` and the Epsilon hero and tile images.
   - Use `alt=""` on the open pixel.
4. **Generate a full plain-text MIME part** with the content and key links. The current 34 characters is unacceptable.
5. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message**, bypassing the relay.
6. **Fix the head markup**:
