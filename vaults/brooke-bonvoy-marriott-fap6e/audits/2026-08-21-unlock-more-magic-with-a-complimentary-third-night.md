# Unlock More Magic With a Complimentary Third Night
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-property play for The Viv Hotel, Anaheim (a Tribute Portfolio brand), built around a "Stay two nights, get the third free" offer. It's a clean, well-organized send — pool hero, clear headline, one dominant black CTA, then four themed sections (rooms, dining, pool, Anaheim attractions) each with its own yellow button. Compared to the card-acquisition and points-bait sends this brand has been running (2026-08-18's "New Members" email misfiring on an existing Bonvoy member, 2026-08-14's kitchen-sink account update), this one is refreshingly honest: the subject matches the hero, the offer is concrete, and there's no bait-and-switch.
- The weakness is targeting, not execution. Brooke's recent history skews points/card-offer engagement (150K points send scored 8/10, JW Marriott Anaheim spotlight scored 9/10) — this email doesn't mention Bonvoy points at all until the footer brand-wall, and there's zero personalization ("Brooke" doesn't appear anywhere, unlike five of the last six sends). It's a solid generic hotel promo, but it under-uses what this brand knows about her.

## What's working

- Subject and hero headline match exactly — "complimentary third night" / "Stay two nights, get the third free" — no bait-and-switch.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("Book Your Trip") sits right below the offer with clean visual hierarchy.
- Property imagery (pool, guest room with bunk beds, rooftop dining, Anaheim skyline) is high quality and varied, giving a real sense of place.
- Consistent black/yellow CTA pattern throughout makes every section scannable, even skimming fast.
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy.

## What's weak

- No personalization anywhere in the body — "Brooke" never appears, breaking the streak of the last several sends and losing any segmentation signal.
- No Bonvoy points tie-in despite Brooke's clear engagement pattern with points offers (the 2026-08-12 and 2026-08-18 sends leaned hard into points).
- Offer has no visible expiration or booking-window — "for a limited time" language is absent, so there's no urgency to act now.
- Four nearly identical gray CTA blocks back-to-back (rooms, dining, pool, attractions) create scroll fatigue with little visual variation.
- This follows the 2026-08-07 JW Marriott Anaheim property spotlight (9/10) by two weeks — another single-Anaheim-property email risks fatigue on that specific market.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a booking deadline / stay-by date near the hero CTA — "free third night" with no urgency reads as an evergreen offer, not something to act on today.
- 2. Personalize the hero with Brooke's name and/or her Bonvoy tier, and add an "Earn Bonvoy points on this stay" line — ties the offer into the loyalty narrative the brand has been building all month.
- 3. Differentiate the four gray sections visually (alternate background tones or vary the CTA color) so the email doesn't feel like four copies of the same block.
- 4. Cut one of the four secondary sections (dining or pool) — the room and Anaheim-attractions blocks carry the strongest booking intent; four full CTAs dilutes the primary "Book Your Trip" ask.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails delivery/compliance basics and every probed link returns a 403 or timeout, indicating either broken link-tracking domain config or the QA prober being blocked — both merit investigation before send.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 8 probed content links (`https://email-marriott.com/T/...`) return HTTP 403, including the primary CTAs "Find & Reserve," "Stay Two Nights, Get the Third Free," "BOOK YOUR TRIP," and "FIND HOTELS."
- The "Tribute Portfolio" logo link timed out entirely rather than resolving.
- All failing links share the same click-tracking redirector host (`email-marriott.com`) and a consistent `/T/{id}/{id}/{uuid}` path pattern, suggesting a systemic redirector/config issue (e.g., campaign not yet activated, IP/UA allowlist blocking the QA fetcher, or an expired tracking token) rather than per-link errors.
- The UNSUBSCRIBE link (`.../73aa0833c0e341f90000021ef3a0bcda/...`) also 403s — see Compliance below, this is the more serious instance of the same root cause.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

None have descriptive `alt` attributes, which will render as blank/unlabeled content for screen readers and when images are blocked.
- HTML uses standard Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if !mso]>`) and MSO-specific CSS resets (`mso-line-height-rule`, `mso-table-lspace/rspace`) — no rendering-specific issues found in the visible source beyond the alt-text gaps above.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders were visible in the truncated HTML source. No issues found based on available evidence.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link present in markup but returns HTTP 403 when probed — functionally broken unsubscribe is a CAN-SPAM risk if reproducible outside the QA harness.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI is unavailable, and this header is effectively required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — even if `List-Unsubscribe` were added, one-click POST unsubscribe would not be supported without it.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from this data; flag as unknown, not confirmed-failing.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot assess UTM parameterization or landing-page alignment: all link destinations use opaque tracking redirects (`__dU__` / `__F__` tokens) that resolve server-side, and every probe attempt 403'd/timed out before reaching a final landing URL. No final destination URLs are available in evidence to check against expected pages.

7. Recommendations
- Investigate the redirector/tracking domain (`email-marriott.com`) for the 403s on all 8 probed links, including the unsubscribe link — check whether the QA prober's IP/UA is blocklisted, or whether the sending campaign/token is not yet live.
- Fix the unsubscribe link 403 as a priority — confirm end-to-end resolution before send.
- Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
