# Brooke's Account Update: 20,000 Points Await
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink email pretending to be a personalized account update. The subject promises "Brooke's Account Update: 20,000 Points Await," but the render shows a generic hero offer (NY vs. LA getaway) followed by a sprawling scroll of five more unrelated promos — Euro Summer bonus points, resort life, hot 35% off, Coca-Cola loyalty tie-in, event points, an awards vote ask, a new St. Regis property, and an adults-only resort roundup. Nothing about it reads as an "account update" — there's no points balance, no personal activity summary, no expiring-points warning. It's a full-catalog newsletter wearing an urgent, personalized subject line.
- Compared to the brand's recent sends — the sharp, single-offer 2026-08-03 "60,000 Bonus Points" card push (9/10) or the focused 2026-08-07 JW Marriott Anaheim spotlight (9/10) — this one dilutes a strong hook (20K points, name-personalized subject) across too many competing CTAs. The account header does show "0 points" and "Member," which is a real personalization signal, but the subject's promise and the body's content don't match, which is the email's core flaw.

## What's working

- Personalized account strip at top ("Brooke Bonvoy," "0 points," "0 nights this year") gives real segmentation signal.
- Hero visual (lush hotel exterior with guests) is clean, high-quality, and the "20,000 bonus points" headline is legible without obvious render bugs.
- Clear single CTA button on the hero ("BOOK NOW") with good contrast against the cream background.
- Consistent brand voice and typography throughout — no lorem ipsum, no broken images, no overlapping text anywhere in the scroll.
- Loyalty member benefits are visible via the account bar and multiple "member" callouts (resort points, 2x event points).

## What's weak

- Subject says "Account Update" but the email is a generic multi-offer promo blast — no actual account activity, no personalized point summary beyond the static "0 points" header.
- Six-plus competing offers (Euro Summer, resort life, 35% off, Coca-Cola, event points, awards vote, new St. Regis, adults-only resorts) bury the headline 20,000-point offer instead of reinforcing it.
- No visible expiration or urgency tied to the 20,000-point offer itself — "end-of-summer getaway" is vague, not a real deadline.
- The Coca-Cola "Classic Is Checking In" module and Frequent Traveler Awards vote ask feel like filler unrelated to a "points await" narrative and dilute focus further.
- Extremely long single-column scroll with no visible re-anchoring back to the 20K offer after the hero — a reader who doesn't act in the first screen has no reminder later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email actually match the subject: lead with a real points-update module (current balance, points expiring, points needed for next reward) instead of a generic 20K promo — right now "Account Update" is a bait subject for a catalog email.
- 2. Cut the email to 2-3 modules max: hero offer + one complementary offer + membership tier status. Drop the Coca-Cola cross-promo and awards vote from this send; they belong in a separate campaign.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the 20,000-point hero offer (e.g., "book by Aug 31") — right now nothing in the hero conveys urgency despite the subject implying it.
- 4. Re-state the primary CTA lower in the email (e.g., after the "Membership Works Harder" section) since the scroll is long and readers may lose the original offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke, Your 20,000 Points Expire Soon — Book Now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20,000 Bonus Points on Your Next NY or LA Stay`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your account shows 0 nights this year — here's a fast way to change that.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick New York or Los Angeles and lock in 20K bonus points.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
This Marriott Bonvoy promotional email uses a heavily-obfuscated third-party ESP tracking layer (`email-marriott.com`) that returns HTTP 403 on the large majority of probed links, and ships with no plain-text fallback and no detectable List-Unsubscribe headers.

## Link & Tracking Issues
- **9 of 12 probed links return HTTP 403**, including primary CTAs ("find & reserve" ×2, "Find a Resort," "Get Away Now," "Marriott Bonvoy" logo ×2, and both "Redirects to Marriott Bonvoy offers landing page" links). All routed through the ESP redirect domain `email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a...` with `__dU__`/`__F__` obfuscated tokens — the 403s indicate either bot/probe blocking on the redirector or genuinely broken tracking links.
- **"MY ACCOUNT" link fails with a URL-parsing error**: the redirect target decodes to a relative path — `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=10797685&lk=1000301947` — that is not a valid absolute URL, suggesting a malformed encode step in the ESP's link-wrapping pipeline.
- **"UNSUBSCRIBE" link itself returns 403** (see Compliance below) — this compounds the missing List-Unsubscribe header issue since the in-body fallback is also broken.
- 40 of 52 total links were not probed (deprioritized footer/social/utility links per probe ranking); unconfirmed status for that tail.

## Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 images missing `alt` text**, spanning both functional creative assets (`cocacola.gif`, `double_points.gif`, `star.png`, `cuncm.gif` from `media.4at5.net`) and tracking/impression pixels (`sli.marriott.com/imp?...`) and the open-tracking pixel (`email-marriott.com/O/...`). Tracking pixels should be marked `alt=""` (decorative) rather than left absent; content images need descriptive alt text for screen readers.
- Heavy reliance on MSO conditional comments and multiple `@font-face` declarations (Aldine, Swiss families via `cache.marriott.com`) with `Times New Roman`/`Arial` fallbacks — standard for Outlook compatibility, no issues found in the visible markup.
- One font asset (`AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff`) is loaded over **plain HTTP** (`http://cache.marriott.com/...`) as a fallback source while its `.woff2` counterpart uses HTTPS — mixed-content risk in HTTPS mail clients that enforce upgrade-insecure-requests, though woff2 will load first in modern clients.

## Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Subject line and presumed body reference "Brooke's Account Update" — no raw merge-tag syntax (e.g., `{{first_name}}` or `%%FIRSTNAME%%`) is exposed unresolved in the visible source, indicating personalization resolved correctly for this send.
- No broken/unresolved token artifacts found in the truncated HTML provided.

## Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, or genuinely absent; cannot confirm compliance from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header not found** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **In-body UNSUBSCRIBE link returns HTTP 403** — combined with the missing headers, this is the most material compliance risk: if both the header-based and body-link unsubscribe mechanisms are non-functional, the message may not meet CAN-SPAM's functioning opt-out requirement.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires one; may be present in the untruncated footer but was not found by automated scan.

## Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All tracked links route through the `email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a...` ESP redirector rather than direct-to-domain UTM-tagged links, so campaign attribution parameters (if any) are opaque/base64-style encoded in `__dU__`/`__F__` params, not standard UTM query strings — cannot verify `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` presence or landing-page alignment from this data.
- Given the 403 failures on 9 of 12 links, end-to-end continuity from email to landing page **cannot currently be verified** — the redirects are failing before reaching any Marriott destination page.

## Recommendations
1. Investigate and fix the systemic 403s on the `email-marriott.com` redirector — this affects primary CTAs and the unsubscribe link; treat as high priority given compliance exposure.
2. Correct the malformed relative-URL encoding behind the "MY ACCOUNT" link so it resolves to an absolute URL.
3. Add a plain-text MIME alternative (current version is 0 chars) to improve deliverability and spam-filter scoring.
4. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually set at the sending MTA (verify outside the AgentMail relay, e.g., via raw header capture at a direct SMTP endpoint) and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC via Authentication-Results at the true receiving MTA.
5. Add `alt` attributes to all 14 flagged images — descriptive text for content images, `alt=""` for the tracking/impression pixels.
6. Serve the `AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` fallback over HTTPS to eliminate the mixed-content source.
7. Verify a physical mailing address is present in the untruncated footer; if absent, add one for CAN-SPAM compliance.
