# 50 Years of Hospitality. Endless Reasons to Stay.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-property push for The Westin Bonaventure Hotel & Suites, Los Angeles, wrapped in a "50 years of hospitality" milestone hook — 50% off the second night on weekend stays. It's a cleanly built email: strong hero image of the hotel's distinctive glass towers, a clear anniversary headline, and four well-organized modular sections (parking, book-early, group points, team travel) each with its own CTA. Visual hierarchy is good — the offer is stated in the first screen, the CTA button is unmissable mint-green against dark cards.
- Where it falls short is targeting and cadence. Compared to the last several Marriott/Bonvoy sends in this thread (Boutiques retail, card offers, The Viv third-night deal, the movie-inspired content roundup), this is yet another single-property, one-off promo — the fifth different narrative frame in two weeks for essentially the same "book a room" ask. The "50 years" milestone angle is a decent hook, but it's Bonaventure-specific and irrelevant unless Brooke has any tie to that property or L.A. Nothing here is personalized beyond generic Bonvoy branding — no name, no status tier callout, no "based on your recent search" logic.
- Solid craft, weak relevance. This reads like a stock property-anniversary template pushed to the full list rather than something built for Brooke specifically.

## What's working

- Hero image is high-quality and immediately identifies the property (distinctive Bonaventure glass cylinders).
- The 50% off offer is stated clearly and early, both in the preheader and directly under the headline.
- Primary CTA ("CELEBRATE AND SAVE") is large, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- Section CTAs are all specific verb+noun pairs (BOOK WITH PARKING, GET DOUBLE POINTS, SCORE YOUR PERKS) rather than generic "Learn More."
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent end to end.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no name, no status tier, no reference to past stays or search behavior, despite Bonvoy clearly having that data (per the 8/14 "Brooke's Account Update" send).
- Single-property offer (Bonaventure, L.A.) with no evident reason it's relevant to this recipient — no "near you" or "you've stayed here before" signal.
- Fine print is buried: "50% off 2nd night" only applies to "weekend stays," a constraint not visible until the small preheader text and never restated with dates in the body.
- Four consecutive gray-card sections (parking, book-early, points, teams) all look identical in structure, creating visual monotony after the first screen.
- The 50th-anniversary framing is generic filler copy ("golden era of hospitality," "make the most of your experiences") that doesn't build urgency or specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real personalization layer — surface Brooke's Bonvoy status/points or a "based on your last search" line near the hero; this is the single biggest lift since the last six sends have shown Marriott has this data and isn't using it here.
- 2. State the weekend-stay restriction and any expiration date directly under the 50% off headline, not buried in the preheader — builds honesty and credible urgency.
- 3. Cut one of the four secondary sections (parking and book-early overlap in purpose) to shorten scroll and reduce card fatigue.
- 4. Replace generic "50 years of hospitality" copy with a concrete anniversary detail (e.g., "since 1976") to make the milestone feel earned rather than templated.
- 5. Vary the section card backgrounds/layout (alternate light/dark or image-left/image-right) so the four promo blocks don't blur together.
