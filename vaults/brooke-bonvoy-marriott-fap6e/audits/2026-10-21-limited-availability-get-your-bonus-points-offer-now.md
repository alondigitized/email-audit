# Limited Availability! Get Your Bonus Points Offer Now.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-purpose Bonvoy "buy points" promotion. The hero says "Get up to 55% Bonus Points While They Last," and the body gives a Nov 23 deadline, a 2,000-point minimum and a new 200,000-point annual cap. After the card pitch on 10/20 and the stay-bonus send on 10/19, it's the third Bonvoy email in three days. A real subscriber would feel that cadence.
- The render itself is solid. The orange "OFFER ENDS SOON" banner, serif headline and hero image lead the eye to the offer. The three-tile urgency row and the black "Buy Points" button follow in a sensible order. The weakness is the inbox side. The subject, "Limited Availability!", has no number and no deadline, and the 55% figure is nowhere in it. Nothing readable in the render would work as preview text. The fine print also says the bonus percentage depends on "the quantity available," so "55%" is a ceiling, not a promise.
- Judgement: well built and worth sending, but under-sold in the subject line. Buying points is a niche action, and the subject gives no reason to open it over the other two Bonvoy emails this week.

## What's working

- The headline puts "up to 55% Bonus Points" up top, so the offer is clear within a second.
- The orange "OFFER ENDS SOON – BUY POINTS NOW" banner adds urgency without crowding the page.
- A real deadline ("through November 23") backs up the "While They Last" framing.
- The page is personalized with "Brooke," plus a masked membership number (XXXXX2045) in the login note.
- The black solid "Buy Points" button and the outlined "Gift Points" button show which action is primary.
- The "buy up to 200,000 bonus points" note (50,000 above the usual limit) gives heavy buyers a concrete reason to act.

## What's weak

- The subject has no number or date, and it drops the strongest asset, the 55%.
- No usable preheader is visible in the render. The first readable text is the banner, so the inbox preview is wasted.
- The offer is hedged. "Up to 55%" is headlined, but the terms say the percentage varies with available quantity. The urgency tiles ("Once gone, the offer will be reduced") also repeat the same message three times.
- The body says "through November 23," while the terms say transactions run "between 9:00 a.m. ET September 29, 2026, and 11:59 p.m. ET November 23." The fine print also has a typo ("avaialble").
- The lower "Buy Points and Redeem Them Your Way" block and the full brand-logo grid dilute the one ask. The lifestyle block also has a weak "» BUY POINTS" text link.
- It's the third Bonvoy send in three days, and the 10/19 "Up to 4,500 Bonus Points per Stay" email had a similar urgency angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the number and the date.** This is the biggest lift. Name the 55% and the Nov 23 end date, and use "Brooke" to stand out from the other Bonvoy sends this week.
- 2. **Add real preheader copy.** It should add the minimum (2,000 points) or the new 200,000 cap instead of repeating the subject.
- 3. **Be honest in the hero.** Say "up to 55%" in the subject too, and move the "quantity varies" caveat up near the CTA so the terms don't read as buried.
- 4. **Cut the three urgency tiles to one line** and put the deadline date next to the button.
- 5. **Remove the brand-logo grid and the lifestyle block** from a transactional offer like this, or move them below the footer.
- 6. **Space out the sends.** Don't send a points-purchase promo the day after a card acquisition email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke, 55% bonus points on every purchase through Nov 23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy points by Nov 23: up to 55% bonus`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2,000+ points and the bonus is yours. Annual limit raised to 200,000.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bonus percentage drops as availability runs out, so lock yours in.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links, plain-text part and image alt text show real problems. Every probed link either returned 403 or failed to resolve (11% pass rate), and the multipart text version is empty. Several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on 9 of 12 probed links.** All are `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a15e3cdf88a48e01f4bbcfb648/...` redirects. They cover "find & reserve", "» find & reserve", "Buy points", "» Buy Points", "Gift points", both header/logo "Marriott Bonvoy" links, the animation image link and "UNSUBSCRIBE". This is likely ESP bot filtering of the QA probe rather than a dead link, but the destinations are unverified.
- **Two probes failed with `unknown url type`.** The "MY ACCOUNT" and "Marriott Bonvoy" (brands) links fail on relative paths:
  - `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12210950&lk=1000301947`
  - `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12210950&lk=1000421047`
  
  The redirect target or `Location` header is probably a scheme-less path. That would break in any client that doesn't resolve it against the tracking domain. The cause isn't confirmed, so this needs a manual check.
- **Weak link text.** The same "Marriott Bonvoy" text points to different destinations: lk=…4bcc4/…bcc7 against the brands link …bcd2/…bcd3.
- **Tracking URL length.** The `__F__` payloads run to roughly 600–1000 characters per link, which is heavy for some clients.
- **Probe coverage.** 16 links were skipped, so footer, social and utility links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `q4-points-reminder-26_hero_d.jpg`
  - `q4-points-reminder-26_hero_m.jpg`
  - `q4-points-26_mod-1.gif`
  - `q4-points-26_mod-2.gif`
  - `q4-points-26_mod-3.gif`
  - the tracking pixel `…/O/v71000001a15e3cdf88a48e01f4bbcfb648/4168fecdbb7d4f7500004c5a42963aa1`

  The five content images carry the offer and need alt text. The pixel only needs `alt=""`.
- **Hero is duplicated as `_d` and `_m` variants.** If both are plain `<img>` tags with no reliable show/hide, screen readers may announce both. The source is truncated, so this isn't confirmed.
- **`@font-face` uses `format('otf')`.** The valid value is `opentype`, so that source is likely ignored. The fallback `.woff` sources still work.
- **Insecure font URLs.** The `.woff` fallbacks use `http://cache.marriott.com/...`, which is mixed content.
- **Typekit stylesheet.** `<link href="https://use.typekit.net/yft3kyd.css">` is dropped by most clients, so those fall back to Arial or Times New Roman. That's an intended fallback, not a defect.
- **Clean elements.** The `lang` attribute, viewport meta, MSO conditionals and `<title>` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens. The `nck`, `ck` and `lk` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Plain-text part is empty** (QA FAIL: "Text version is 0 chars"). This is a deliverability and accessibility risk.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header captured.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Marriott, as a bulk sender, should send both.
- **No physical address detected.** The HTML source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. This needs a footer check.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown.** There was no `Authentication-Results` header. Sending from `email-marriott.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Unsubscribe link.** It is present (`…bcd7`) but returned 403 to the probe. Verify it manually.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters.** Attribution is by `enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck&ck&lk` through the ESP redirect, which is a proprietary convention rather than a defect.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** All probed CTAs returned 403 or an unresolvable URL, so no final destinations were observed. Nothing confirms that "find & reserve", "Buy points" or "Gift points" land on matching pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracking links with a real-browser user agent, or by following redirects manually. This separates bot-blocking 403s from real breakage.
2. Fix or verify the two relative-path redirect targets, `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi` and `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi`. Make them absolute `https://www.marriott.com/...` URLs.
3. Add a real text/plain part with the offer, CTA URLs and unsubscribe link.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the hero and the three `q4-points-26_mod-*.gif` images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
6. Confirm the physical address in the full footer HTML.
7. Change the font `format('otf')` to `format('opentype')` and the `http://` `.woff` URLs to `https://`.
8. Disambiguate the repeated "Marriott Bonvoy" link text.
