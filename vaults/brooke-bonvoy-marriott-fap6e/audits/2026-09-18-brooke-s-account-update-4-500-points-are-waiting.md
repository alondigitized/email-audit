# Brooke's Account Update: 4,500 Points are Waiting
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott Bonvoy running a bonus-points registration reminder for Brooke — "Earn up to 4,500 Bonus Points on Stays," register once and earn 1,500 points on any 2+ night stay through November 22, with an extra 3,000 at resorts. It's functionally the same campaign as the 9/10 "Registration Is Now Open, Brooke!" send (6.00/10), just re-packaged with a new subject line built around the point count instead of the registration verb. That's a real cadence problem: two emails inside eight days pushing the identical offer reads as a nag, not a nurture sequence.
- Past the hero, the email sprawls into a kitchen-sink digest — Hawaii/Mexico vacation packages, a cruise sweepstakes, Vegas savings, a staycation push, a third-party home-goods ad insert, an "Ask Bonvoy" AI feature, a Super Bowl sweepstakes, 20% off "Cozy Up to Savings," and a three-property showcase (JW Marriott, Westin, Sheraton) — all in one send. It's comprehensive to the point of being unfocused; nothing after the hero gets real visual weight, and the actual account-specific hook (Brooke's point balance, the 4,500-point offer) gets buried under a dozen unrelated pitches by the time you scroll halfway down.
- The top third is genuinely solid — clean loyalty header, honest point balance, clear CTA. The bottom two-thirds dilute it. Net judgment: a decent reminder email wrapped in a bloated newsletter that will underperform on click-through because there's no single next action to take.

## What's working

- Personalized loyalty header up top shows Brooke's actual point balance and member status before any sales pitch — good trust signal.
- Hero section states the offer plainly: "Earn up to 4,500 Bonus Points on Stays" with a clear breakdown (1,500 base + 3,000 resort bonus) and explicit dates (Sept 22–Nov 22).
- Single unambiguous black "REGISTER NOW" button directly under the hero copy — no guessing what to do first.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no placeholder content visible anywhere in the six-plus screens of content.

## What's weak

- This is the same bonus-points registration campaign as the 9/10 email just seven days later — repetitive cadence with no new information beyond a reworded subject.
- The email buries its own hero under 8+ unrelated offer modules (Vegas, cruise sweepstakes, Super Bowl, boutique furniture ad, staycation, three-property showcase) — no clear secondary priority.
- A third-party "boutique home decor" ad is inserted mid-email between Marriott content blocks, which breaks brand cohesion and reads like clutter rather than curation.
- Subject promises "4,500 Points are Waiting" (sounds like points already earned), but the body reveals it's a future-earn registration offer — a mild expectation mismatch.
- The dense legal/terms block at the bottom is unusually long and visible, which undercuts the "simple, honest offer" feel of the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a single-offer email. The registration reminder deserves its own focused send — kill or radically shorten everything after the hero and the "Stay or Go" section. A cleaner, shorter email will out-convert this digest.
- 2. Fix the subject/body mismatch — either make the subject clearly conditional ("earn up to") or make the body match the "waiting" framing (e.g., show pending/unclaimed points, not a future promo).
- 3. Space this out from the 9/10 send — don't re-push the identical bonus-points campaign twice in the same two-week window; use this slot for something new instead.
- 4. Move the third-party ad insert out of the middle of the Marriott content stream, or drop it — it damages brand trust to have unrelated furniture ads inside a loyalty email.
- 5. Trim the fine-print block or move it to a linked page — a visibly massive terms wall works against the "simple offer" pitch.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next stay could earn 4,500 Bonvoy points, Brooke`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register by Nov 22 — stay 2+ nights, earn the rest automatically.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No booking required yet. Just register to start earning.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email fails delivery/compliance checks and ships with a majority-broken link set: 8 of 12 probed material links return 403s or malformed URL errors, and both plain-text fallback and unsubscribe header support are missing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 6 of 12 probed links return HTTP 403 through the ESP redirect wrapper (`email-marriott.com/T/...`), including primary CTAs "find & reserve," "» find & reserve," and both "Marriott Bonvoy" logo/header links, plus the "UNSUBSCRIBE" link itself.
- 3 links resolve to relative, protocol-less paths that fail URL parsing entirely (`unknown url type`): `/sign-in.mi?returnTo=...`, `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?...`. These are un-prefixed relative URLs baked into the tracking wrapper's destination parameter — they lack a scheme/host and cannot resolve outside Marriott's authenticated app context (link text: "REGISTER NOW," "MY ACCOUNT," hero image alt-target, and body copy link).
- One link ("» discover the strip") failed with `Connection reset by peer` — indicates a possible server-side block or rate-limiting on the destination host, not a client-side HTML defect.
- Net result: primary CTA, account, registration, and unsubscribe paths are all non-functional in this test context.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, including tracking pixels (`sli.marriott.com/imp?...`, expected to be empty/decorative — acceptable) but also a content image (`stay_local1.gif`) and an open-tracking pixel (`3d7a4f941246462500004c5a42963aa1`) with no `alt=""` fallback declared.
- Heavy reliance on custom `@font-face` declarations (Aldine, Swiss, Good-Karma) with MSO-conditional fallbacks to Times New Roman/Arial — fallback chain is present and correct.
- One font URL uses `http://` instead of `https://` (`http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/.../AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff`, and the italic variant) — mixed-content risk in TLS-enforced mail clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Subject line and body reference "Brooke's Account Update" — recipient name token appears correctly resolved (no unresolved `{{merge}}` or `%%TOKEN%%` artifacts visible in the truncated source).
- No broken/unresolved merge tags found in the visible HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than the sender, per QA note).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — same caveat.
- The in-body "UNSUBSCRIBE" link itself returns a 403 in the automated probe — this is a functional finding independent of header presence; if reproducible outside the test harness, it's a CAN-SPAM functional-compliance concern since the required opt-out mechanism does not resolve.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — status unknown, flagged as relay-side limitation, not confirmed sender misconfiguration.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in any probed link — all tracking is handled via the proprietary `email-marriott.com/T/...` wrapper with opaque `__dU__`/`__F__` tokens instead of standard UTM query params, so campaign attribution in analytics tools expecting UTM tagging would not be captured.
- Destination paths embedded in the wrapper (`/sign-in.mi`, `/loyalty/promotion.mi?promotion=WL26`, `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi`) reference Marriott's `.mi` application routes — consistent with expected loyalty/account platform naming, but cannot be verified as reachable given the relative-URL parsing failures above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the 403-returning wrapper links (find & reserve, Marriott Bonvoy logo, UNSUBSCRIBE) — verify the ESP redirect domain isn't blocking automated/QA user agents, or that tracking tokens haven't expired/been misconfigured.
2. Correct the 3 relative destination URLs (`/sign-in.mi...`, `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi...`) to fully-qualified `https://www.marriott.com/...` URLs before wrapping in the tracking redirect.
3. Add a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative (current fallback is 0 chars) — required for spam-filter and accessibility compliance.
4. Add `alt=""` to decorative/tracking images and descriptive `alt` text to `stay_local1.gif`.
5. Change the two `http://` font URLs to `https://` to eliminate mixed-content warnings.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) independent of the AgentMail relay, since these could be sender-side gaps rather than test artifacts.
