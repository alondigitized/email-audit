# Kick-start your membership with up to 125K Points + Automatic Elite Status
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a "New Member Offer" push for the Marriott Bonvoy Boundless® Visa card — welcome the new Bonvoy member (Brooke), then upsell the co-brand card with 125,000 bonus points, automatic Silver Elite status, and $100 in airline credits. It's a card-acquisition email, not a stay/points-purchase pitch like the last several sends in this thread (2026-08-29 Bonus Points, 2026-08-25 scratch-off offer) — so at least the beat is different this time.
- Execution is competent but bloated: the offer is repeated near-verbatim four separate times (hero block, points calculator, benefits grid, and a second full card block near the footer) with four "APPLY NOW" buttons, plus a second, weaker card offer (Bonvoy Bold, no annual fee) crammed in right before the footer. That's a lot of redundancy for one inbox scroll, and it dilutes urgency rather than reinforcing it. The "$95 annual fee" fine print sits directly under every CTA, which is honest, but the sheer repetition reads more like a landing page pasted into an email than a tightly edited send.
- Net: decent targeting (new-member card acquisition is a real moment to strike), clean visual build, no render bugs — but it overstays its welcome and buries the one thing that would actually move a new member: why THIS card versus the free Bold card offered lower down.

## What's working

- Clear hero hierarchy — "Earn up to 125,000 Bonus Points" is the first thing the eye hits, large and bold.
- Personalization is present ("Brooke, earn up to 125,000 Bonus Points...") tying the offer to the individual.
- The points-math visual (0 + up to 125,000 = 125,000) is a nice concrete "see your balance grow" device that's more persuasive than plain text.
- Elite status benefits are itemized clearly (Priority Late Checkout, 10% More Points, Member Rates) with simple iconography.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The identical card offer (125K points, $95 fee, Apply Now) appears four times — hero, calculator, benefits grid, and again near the footer — which reads as padding, not persuasion.
- A second, competing card offer (Bonvoy Bold, no annual fee, 45,000 points) is dropped in right before the footer with almost no visual separation, undercutting the primary $95-fee card's value case.
- The lifestyle hero image (cabana daybeds) is generic aspirational travel stock — nothing ties it to Boundless card benefits specifically.
- Preview text isn't distinct from the subject — reinforces rather than complements it, wasting a rare second line of inbox real estate.
- "Earn up to 125,000 Bonus Points" leads with a headline number that requires $6,000 total spend in 6 months to hit — the qualifying detail is legible but three tiers deep in small print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the card block down to two instances max (hero CTA + one final CTA before footer) — the repeated identical block adds scroll fatigue without adding persuasion.
- 2. Move or clearly demote the Bonvoy Bold (no-fee) offer — right now it competes with the primary card in the same visual weight, right where a hesitant reader would be deciding.
- 3. Replace the generic cabana photo with an image showing the physical card or a Silver Elite perk in action (e.g., late checkout, lounge) to tie the visual to the actual benefit being sold.
- 4. Front-load the spend tiers next to the 125K headline number instead of only in the calculator/fine print, so the offer isn't misleadingly simple at first glance.
- 5. Write a preview line that adds new information instead of echoing the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke, your new member card offer: 125K points + Silver status`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You're in. Now unlock 125K points and Elite status, Brooke`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Silver Elite status, 15 Elite Night Credits, and $100 in airline credits — all with one card.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 75K after $3K spend, plus 50K more at $6K. Ends 9/9.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This Marriott Bonvoy email uses a heavily nested MSO/VML template with extensive custom @font-face declarations, but suffers from broken tracked links (403s), missing plain-text fallback, and undetected authentication/compliance headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 of 12 probed material links return errors**, all routed through the Epsilon/Harmony click-tracking redirector (`email-marriott.com/T/...`):
  - `find & reserve` (CTA, link ID `...bcc9`) — 403
  - `» find & reserve` (link ID `...bcc6`) — 403
  - `Marriott Bonvoy` header logo link (link ID `...bcc4`) — 403
  - `Marriott Bonvoy` header logo link (link ID `...bcc7`) — 403 (duplicate/second instance)
  - `MY ACCOUNT` (link ID `...bcc8`) — link error: `unknown url type: '/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=11075111&lk=1000301947'` — the underlying destination path was not correctly wrapped/encoded by the redirector
  - `UNSUBSCRIBE` (link ID `...bce6`) — 403
- A 403 on the tracking domain itself (rather than the final destination) indicates the redirector service (`email-marriott.com`) is rejecting the request — this could be link-expiry, bot/referrer blocking, or a malformed `__dU__`/`__F__` token, and affects both primary CTAs and the unsubscribe link.
- 31 additional (lower-priority/footer/social) links were not probed; scope of breakage beyond the 12 sampled is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Two images missing `alt` text:
  - `915718cbbf38448c00004c5a42963aa1` (tracking pixel or content image, src: `email-marriott.com/O/...`)
  - `chevron_down@2x.png` (src: `images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../chevron_do...`)
- Template loads 8+ custom web fonts (Aldine/Swiss families in EOT/OTF/WOFF/WOFF2/TTF/SVG) via `cache.marriott.com`; two of the `url()` references use plain `http://` instead of `https://` (`AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff`, `AldineforMarriott-Italic.woff`), which will be blocked as mixed content in HTTPS mail clients that render remote CSS.
- Heavy MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if gt mso 9]>`) present for Outlook compatibility — structure appears standard for enterprise ESP output; no additional rendering issues confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) visible in the truncated HTML/subject.
- No issues found in the visible source, but coverage is limited by the truncation — body content with personalization fields was not included in the provided excerpt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found — one-click/mailbox-level unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe (POST) unsupported or not captured.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the source — CAN-SPAM requires this in every commercial email.
- **In-body unsubscribe link**: present (`UNSUBSCRIBE` link, link ID `...bce6`) but currently returns a **403 error**, meaning the required functional opt-out mechanism is broken.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay.

Combined, this is a compliance risk: no verifiable physical address, no verifiable unsubscribe headers, and a broken unsubscribe link is a functional CAN-SPAM violation, not just a UX bug.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All tracked links route through the Epsilon/Harmony redirector (`email-marriott.com/T/...?__dU__=...&__F__=...`) rather than exposing UTM parameters directly in the email HTML, so UTM alignment with landing pages cannot be verified from source alone.
- The one link where the underlying destination path is exposed (`MY ACCOUNT` → `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=...&ck=...&lk=1000301947`) uses ESP-specific campaign params (`enrollmentSourceCode`, `nck`, `ck`, `lk`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — consistent with Marriott's internal tracking scheme, not a defect per se, but not GA/GA4-compatible without server-side mapping.
- Cannot confirm landing page alignment for the `find & reserve` CTAs or account links since all sampled destination links returned errors before reaching the final URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the 403s on tracking links** — investigate whether the redirector tokens (`__dU__`/`__F__`) are expired, IP/referrer-blocked, or malformed at send time; prioritize the CTA (`find & reserve`) and `UNSUBSCRIBE` links as compliance/revenue-critical.
2. **Repair the `MY ACCOUNT` link** — the redirector is failing to wrap `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?...` correctly ("unknown url type"); validate URL encoding of the destination before wrapping.
3. **Add a plain-text MIME part** — current plain-text version is 0 characters; required for deliverability and accessibility (screen readers, text-only clients).
4. **Restore/verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers** — needed for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook.
5. **Add a physical mailing address** to the footer to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
6. **Add `alt` text** to the two flagged images (tracking pixel and `chevron_down@2x.png`).
7. **Change the two `http://` font URLs to `https://`** to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTML-rendering mail clients.
8. **Verify Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)** independently via a raw header capture, since the AgentMail relay did not surface this header.
