# Scottsdale Villas, Golf, and More at The Phoenician
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a polished, on-brand destination send for The Canyon Suites at The Phoenician in Scottsdale. The hero image is strong and the headline "DISCOVER THE VILLAS AT CANYON SUITES" is clear. The first block is the best part. It gives a concrete product (eight freestanding villas, two to four bedrooms, full kitchens, private patios, breakfast for two, pool access) and a single clear button, "RESERVE YOUR VILLA."
- The email then splits into three equal dark cards: savings, golf and holidays. They compete for attention, and none has a number, a date or a price. "Plan ahead for more savings" promises a discount without saying how much. The offer is the weak point, and the email reads as brand storytelling rather than a reason to book now. Compared with the loud 11/05 Bonvoy card send ("Seven Days Only. 200K Bonus Points."), it has no urgency or hard value.
- Judgment: good creative and a clear first block, but a soft offer and no member angle. It would sit mid-pack in the inbox.

## What's working

- The hero photo of the desert, the mountain and the resort sets the mood immediately and is a good first impression.
- The villa headline, copy and "RESERVE YOUR VILLA" button give a clear primary path.
- The villa copy lists specific amenities (kitchens, patios, breakfast, pool complex), so a reader can picture the stay.
- The alternating image and text cards are clean, with strong contrast, readable text and no visible render bugs.
- The holiday card with the decorated tree fits the early-November timing.

## What's weak

- "Plan ahead for more savings" and "prepaid rate" give no percentage, dollar amount or date, so the offer feels vague.
- Nothing creates urgency. There is no deadline, no availability note and no booking window.
- Bonvoy members get no visible benefit. The Bonvoy logo grid sits at the bottom with no points, member rate or elite perks.
- Three secondary CTAs ("BOOK YOUR TRIP," "DISCOVER NOW," "LEARN MORE") dilute the main one, and the last two are generic.
- The long brand-logo grid and legal footer are heavy for a single-property send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as "Save 15% when you book by Dec 1" or a member-rate or points hook, and repeat it in the savings card.
- 2. Cut to two modules (villas and golf). Drop the holiday card or fold it into a seasonal line.
- 3. Replace the generic button copy with specific verbs: "Book the Golf Package," "See Villa Rates."
- 4. Add a Bonvoy line near the top, for example "Members earn points on every villa stay."
- 5. Add one social-proof or rating element, such as a review quote or an award.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Private 2–4 bedroom villas at the base of Camelback Mountain, plus unlimited golf packages.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Every link is wrapped in an `email-marriott.com/T/...` click-tracker, and all 12 probed links failed (403 or timeout). Several head-level meta tags are malformed, and the plain-text part and four images have problems. The link failures are probably a probe artifact, since the tracker isn't returning a destination to the QA client, but the email's actual links remain unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on 7 probed links.** These are "Plan Ahead and Discover a New Private Desert Retreat", "Find & Reserve", "BOOK YOUR TRIP", "FIND HOTELS", "UNSUBSCRIBE" and two image links. All are `https://email-marriott.com/T/v61000001a1afb2c.../…`.
- **Timeouts on 4 probed links.** These are "Discover the Villas at Canyon Suites", "RESERVE YOUR VILLA", "LEARN MORE" and one image link (`<urlopen error timed out>`).
- **Likely a probe artifact.** The 403s and timeouts all hit the same ESP tracker domain, and the links are per-recipient tokens (`__dU__`, `__F__`). The pattern points to bot filtering or rate limiting, not dead links. I can't confirm either way from the data.
- **Unsubscribe is unverified.** The `UNSUBSCRIBE` link returned 403, so I can't confirm it resolves to a working opt-out.
- **Long tracking URLs.** Several run past 700 characters (e.g. the `BOOK YOUR TRIP` link). This isn't a confirmed defect, but very long URLs are more likely to be truncated or mangled by some clients and scanners.
- **Probe coverage.** 16 links were skipped, so footer and social links are unchecked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text.** Four `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images?id=...` images have no alt (ids `4bc75862…`, `43af6e8f…`, `f547de54…`, `cde5dadc…`). The QA tool reads their link text as `[image: images]`, so any image-only link has no accessible name. Those links are at least `.../bcc5`, `.../bcc8`, `.../bccb`.
- **Tracking pixel without alt.** `email-marriott.com/O/v61000001a1afb2c…/5785234f0a2047d900004c5a42963aa1` has no alt. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is preferred.
- **Malformed viewport meta.** The source has `content="width=device-width, initial scale=1.0"`. It should be `initial-scale=1.0`, so some clients may ignore the scale directive.
- **Invalid robots meta.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Clients that show the title lose it, and screen readers get no document name.
- **Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags.** There is one for `telephone=no` and one for `address=no`. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Plain-text part.** It is only 34 characters (QA FAIL). Clients that show the text version, and some spam filters, get essentially no content.
- **`lang="en"`.** This is set on `<html>`, which is good.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source and QA data show no unresolved tokens or placeholders. The `__dU__` and `__F__` parameters are tracker-generated and intentional.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation and not evidence of failure.
- **In-body `UNSUBSCRIBE` link is present.** It returned 403 to the probe (see section 2).
- **Physical postal address.** I can't confirm it because the source is truncated before the footer.
- **From and sending domain.** The From address (`marriott@email-marriott.com`) matches the tracking domain `email-marriott.com`, so the sender and links are aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All CTAs go through the opaque tracker, and no final destinations were resolved because the probes got 403s or timeouts. I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing-page match or redirect chains. The subject names The Phoenician, and the visible CTA text ("Discover the Villas at Canyon Suites", "RESERVE YOUR VILLA") is consistent with that. Whether those links land on matching pages is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link QA with a browser-like user agent and fewer parallel requests, and follow redirects to capture final URLs and UTMs. In particular, resolve the `UNSUBSCRIBE` link.
2. Add alt text to the four `images.harmony.epsilon.com` images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Fix `initial scale=1.0` to `initial-scale=1.0`, change `no index` to `noindex`, and fill in `<title>`.
4. Supply a real multipart plain-text body that mirrors the CTAs and includes an unsubscribe URL.
5. Check the raw message at the source, not through the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
6. Re-inspect the full, untruncated HTML to confirm the footer address and check for any unresolved merge tokens.
