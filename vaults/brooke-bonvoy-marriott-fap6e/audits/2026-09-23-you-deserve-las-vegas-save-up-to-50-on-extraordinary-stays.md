# You Deserve Las Vegas ✨ Save Up to 50% on Extraordinary Stays.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott Bonvoy running an MGM Collection/Las Vegas destination push, and it's one of the stronger, more focused sends in this thread — a clear step up from the scattered W Hotels (9/4) and Moxy Portland (9/18) property pushes. The Bellagio fountains hero shot, a legible "Save Up to 50%" headline, and a concrete daily $100 F&B credit give the eye somewhere to land immediately, and the deadline (book by 9/28/26) creates real urgency rather than vague "extraordinary savings" filler.
- Where it loses ground is stacking: after the discount block, it pivots into a bonus-points registration pitch (a near-repeat of the 9/10 and 9/15 sends), then a "Moments" experiential auction (MICHELIN dinner, bidding), then a full brand-directory footer. Three distinct offers in one email dilutes the primary CTA — a reader has to decide which of three "BOOK NOW / REGISTER TODAY / BID NOW" buttons actually applies to them.
- Net: strong top half, cluttered by the third scroll. Worth sending as-is for a Vegas-interested segment, but should be split into two emails (deal + points registration) for cleaner conversion tracking.

## What's working

- Hero image (Bellagio fountains at night) is high-quality, on-brand, and instantly signals "Las Vegas."
- Discount headline and dollar-specific F&B credit ($100/day) are concrete, not vague.
- Deadline is explicit and credible (book by Sept 28, 2026, for stays through Aug 2027).
- Named, linked hotel properties (Bellagio, Cosmopolitan, ARIA) give a real, findable destination for the click.
- Visual hierarchy in the first fold is clean: dark background, gold accent text, single button.

## What's weak

- Three separate offers/CTAs (Book Now, Register Today, Bid Now) compete for attention in one email.
- The bonus-points block repeats the exact mechanic from the 9/10 "Registration Is Now Open" and 9/15 emails — cadence fatigue for a subscriber like Brooke.
- No personalization anywhere in the visible render (generic "You Deserve Las Vegas," no name, no tier callout), despite prior sends in this thread using "Brooke" directly.
- The MICHELIN-dinner "Moments" auction section is a big topic swing this late in the email and risks being ignored entirely after two prior CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split into two emails: one pure Vegas-deal send (hero + discount + F&B credit + Book Now), one bonus-points registration send — matches what's already working in the 9/10 send and removes CTA competition.
- 2. Personalize the headline with name/tier ("Brooke, Your Las Vegas Escape: Save Up to 50%") since this brand clearly has that data and used it as recently as 9/18.
- 3. Move or cut the "Moments" MICHELIN auction block — it's a fundamentally different offer type and buries the primary deal.
- 4. Add a countdown or "X days left" element near Book Now to sharpen the Sept 28 urgency visually, not just in body copy.
