# Homepage snapshot · www.marriott.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like the Bonvoy front door for a points person: the search bar is the whole page, and the brand pushes the credit card hard. The hero, "Your Next Trip Starts Here" over a beach aerial, is pretty but generic. Nothing says St. Regis, Ritz-Carlton or a points sweet spot. The first thing sold to me is a co-brand card ("Earn 3 Free Night Awards + up to $100 Airline Credit"), not a stay.

## What's working

- **Search module:** "Destination / 1 NIGHT / Mon, Oct 26 - Tue, Oct 27 / Find Hotels" is large, high-contrast and thumb-friendly. Prefilled tonight-plus-one dates suit last-minute work stays at a Courtyard.
- **Top nav:** "Book, Offers, Brands, Credit Cards, Marriott Bonvoy, Meetings & Events" gets me to Offers in one tap, which is where I'd go to check promos.
- **Tab bar:** "Member Exclusives" is the right hook for an elite member, and one tap away.
- **Program language:** "Free Night Awards" is the correct term. It tells me the copywriter knows the program.
- **Portland card:** "DISTINCTLY NORTHWEST / Portland, Oregon" is a good editorial image that makes me want to browse destinations.

## What's weak

- **Hero headline:** "Your Next Trip Starts Here" is generic. It says nothing about points, elite status or luxury stays.
- **Hero image:** a beach aerial with two tiny figures. It isn't a property, so I get no sense of St. Regis or Ritz-level experiences.
- **Ask Bonvoy (Beta):** the AI search is gated behind sign-in, and the row is a full-width bar I have to read before I get to anything useful.
- **Credit card promo:** it takes the first card slot, followed by a second, generic "Marriott Bonvoy® Credit Cards" card with an orange card illustration. Two of the three visible cards are the same pitch, so it reads as ad inventory.
- **Points and award info:** I can't see any points balance, award search, "Points + Cash" or category/free-night-cert info. As someone who tracks Cat 1-8 and the 50K certs, I'd have to dig for the thing I care about.
- **Tab bar edge:** "Unique Ways to Stay" runs to the right edge, and the black "Feedback" tab overlaps the end of the pill.
- **Nothing for status:** I'm a Titanium and there's no elite recognition anywhere, even in the sign-in prompts.

## Recommendations

- Replace the beach hero with a St. Regis or Ritz-Carlton property shot, and add a subhead such as "Redeem at Ritz-Carlton. Earn on every stay." That would speak to premium redeemers.
- Add a slim logged-out value strip under the search bar, for example "Members: free Wi-Fi, mobile check-in, member rates", with a "Join free" button. This would fill the loyalty gap and give new visitors a reason to sign in.
- Add a "Search with points" toggle to the search card. Award-focused members could then start a points search without leaving the page.
- Make the first two cards different. Keep the Free Night Awards card, replace the generic "Credit Cards" card with a destination or "Member Exclusives" offer, and add one line of terms under "Earn 3 Free Night Awards…" so it feels honest.
