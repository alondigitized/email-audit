# Inside: 3 Reasons to Get Away
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer Boundless card acquisition send. The hero says "EARN 3 FREE NIGHTS" with a black strip, "PLUS, EARN UP TO $100 IN AIRLINE CREDITS*", and a plane icon. The offer is easy to read, and the card art, bullets and orange APPLY NOW button sit in a tidy block below it.
- The subject doesn't match the body. "Inside: 3 Reasons to Get Away" promises a travel-inspiration list. The body is a credit card pitch with a $3,000 spend requirement and a $95 annual fee. A subscriber who opens expecting trip ideas gets an application ad. This is also the third card send in about three weeks. The 11/05 and 11/10 Boundless sends, the 11/17 Brilliant send and now this one all push the same product. The send arrives a day after the 11/25 member-only 25%-off-stays Cyber Sale email, which was a much better fit for a leisure traveler.
- Verdict: competent creative, weak subject, stale cadence. It reads as a reflexive card send, not a targeted one.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and the offer is instantly clear: "EARN 3 FREE NIGHTS" plus "$100 in airline credits."
- The card image beside the bullets makes the product concrete, and "Brooke" personalization appears in the body copy.
- The orange APPLY NOW button is clear and repeated after the benefits block.
- The "$95 annual fee" sits directly under each button, so the cost isn't buried.
- The benefits row (17X points, Free Night Award, Silver Elite) is scannable and uses consistent iconography.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "3 Reasons," but the body shows 3 free nights and then 3 benefits. The count and framing don't line up with a get-away theme.
- The offer needs $3,000 of spend in 3 months, and that sits in a bullet, with asterisks and "Offer Details" links doing the rest. Compared with the 11/05 and 11/10 sends, the offer feels less urgent. No deadline is visible.
- The second half repeats the first. There are two identical APPLY NOW blocks with the same fee line, and no new reason to click.
- The brand-logo grid takes up a large block with no CTA and no link context. It's filler between the offer and a long legal footer.
- There is no social proof, no destination imagery and no travel emotion, even though the subject sells a "get away."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the card pitch, or make the body match the subject. For a card send, say so up front and name the offer.
- 2. Add a real deadline. Every earlier Boundless send had one (the 11/10 send said "ENDS 11/11"), and this one has none.
- 3. Put the spend threshold in the hero strip, for example "after $3,000 in 3 months," so the offer feels honest rather than fine print.
- 4. Replace the logo grid with one destination photo and a CTA such as "See where 3 nights takes you."
- 5. Space out card sends. Three in three weeks, plus a member-only sale in between, trains the subscriber to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run recorded 8 failures. Most are 403s from the ESP click-tracker on `email-marriott.com/T/...`, which are likely probe blocking rather than real breakage. The confirmed defects are a 0-character plain-text part, relative-path link destinations, and two images missing alt text. Authentication and unsubscribe-header results are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on tracked links (5 of 12 probed).** The affected links are "find & reserve", "» find & reserve", both "Marriott Bonvoy" links, and "UNSUBSCRIBE". All are wrapped as `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a217c6eb1db8aa51369fea47bc/<id>/8e48b4b0-25e5-4b10-bff5-97c2dfe07636?__dU__=…&__F__=…`.
  - A uniform 403 across every wrapper, including unsubscribe, points to bot or UA filtering of the probe. It is not confirmed broken for real recipients.
  - Re-test from a real client or a browser-based probe before treating these as defects.
- **Relative-path destinations (likely real).** Two links failed with `unknown url type`:
  - `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12869080&lk=1000301947` ("MY ACCOUNT")
  - `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?...&lk=1000421047` ("Marriott Bonvoy")
  - The destination has no scheme or host. If the redirect resolves against `email-marriott.com` instead of `marriott.com`, these will 404 on the tracking domain.
  - Confirm by checking the `Location` header of those two `/T/` URLs.
- **Very long tracking URLs.** The `__F__` payloads run to roughly 500 characters or more. This is not a defect, but it raises the risk of truncation in plain-text rendering and some clients.
- **Probe coverage.** 20 links were skipped as low priority, so footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 2 images.**
  - `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a217c6eb1db8aa51369fea47bc/8e48b4b025e54b1000004c5a42963aa1` looks like the open-tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` to it.
  - `benefits_100K_D@2x.png` is hosted on `images.harmony.epsilon.com`. It needs descriptive alt text if it carries content.
- **Ambiguous link text.** Several links share the text "Marriott Bonvoy", and two share "find & reserve", but they have different destinations (distinct tracking IDs `…bcc4`, `…bcc7`, `…bcd6`; `…bcc9`, `…bcc6`). This is poor for screen-reader link lists.
- **Font declarations.**
  - Two `@font-face` rules use `url('http://cache.marriott.com/…woff')`, which is non-HTTPS. Clients that load web fonts may block it as mixed content.
  - `format('otf')` is not a valid format hint. It should be `format('opentype')`, so those sources are likely ignored.
  - Both are low impact because fallbacks (`Times New Roman`, `Arial`) are defined.
- **Lang and meta.** `lang`, `xml:lang`, `viewport`, the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta and the MSO DPI fix are all present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated HTML. The `nck`/`ck`/`lk` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is empty (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** This is a deliverability and accessibility defect. It cannot be attributed to the relay, so treat it as real.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these headers. Verify against the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Check the sending domain's DNS directly.
- **Unsubscribe link.** It is present in the body, but the probe returned 403 (see section 2). Verify manually.
- **Physical address.** It cannot be confirmed because the HTML source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible on any destination. The links use ESP wrappers plus Marriott-internal parameters (`enrollmentSourceCode=INCK`, `nck`, `ck`, `lk`). Campaign attribution is therefore probably handled server-side, but this can't be confirmed from the data.
- The 403s prevented the probe from following the main CTAs ("find & reserve"), so landing-page alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a populated `text/plain` alternative part, since the current one is empty.
2. Check the redirect `Location` headers for the two relative-path links (`/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi…`, `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi…`). They should point to absolute `https://www.marriott.com/…` URLs.
3. Re-probe the 403 tracking links with a browser-like client to separate bot-blocking from real failures, and verify the unsubscribe link first.
4. Inspect the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and descriptive alt text to `benefits_100K_D@2x.png`.
6. Give links with the same text distinguishing labels, for example `aria-label` or more specific text.
7. Switch the `http://cache.marriott.com` font URLs to `https://`, and change `format('otf')` to `format('opentype')`.
8. Treat the 21% pass rate with caution. Most of the failures are probe artifacts, not confirmed recipient-facing breakage.
