# Three Ways to Stay in Santa Monica and Save
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-property push for Sandbourne Santa Monica. The subject promises "three ways," and the body delivers two offers plus a dining module. The hero reads "STAY FIVE NIGHTS AND SAVE UP TO 20%." The first card repeats the 20% offer, the second adds 5,000 Bonvoy points per stay, and the third is Marelle restaurant content. The layout is clean and the photography is good. The rooms, pool and food shots look like a real getaway.
- The "three ways to save" framing is misleading. Two of the three are savings (20% off, 5,000 points), and the third is a restaurant blurb with no discount. The 20% offer is also shown twice, once in the hero and again in the first card. The five-night minimum is stated in the hero but buried in the card, and nothing shows a price, a date window or a deadline. It's a nice brand email, but it's soft on urgency and specifics.
- For this recipient, a Bonvoy member who just received a flood of points and card emails (the 10/19 "4,500 Bonus Points per Stay" send, the 10/21 "Bonus Points Offer"), a hotel-specific offer is a welcome change. It reads as a mid-tier promo: pretty, clear, and easy to ignore. It's also a destination I haven't shown interest in.

## What's working

- The hero image is bright and aspirational, and the headline states the offer in plain words: "Stay five nights and save up to 20%."
- The Autograph Collection logo in the header gives the email a clear brand identity.
- The alternating image/text cards give the email a good scanning rhythm, and the CTA buttons in light blue stand out against the dark panels.
- The Bonvoy 5,000-point offer is called out as its own module, so loyalty value is visible.
- The Santa Monica detail (Pier, Third Street Promenade, heated pool) makes the destination concrete.

## What's weak

- "Three Ways to Stay… and Save" oversells. The third module, Marelle dining, has no savings at all.
- No dates, deadline or booking window appear anywhere, so there's no urgency.
- The five-night minimum is the real catch on the 20% offer. The first card ("A Coastal Reset with 20% Off") drops it, which makes the offer look less honest.
- The hero CTA, "Book and Save," is generic. The card CTAs ("Book Now," "Reserve Today," "Learn More") are all different, but none says what the click leads to.
- The long Marriott brand-logo grid and the footer take up as much space as the content, with no extra value for this offer.
- The pale blue buttons have low contrast against the white hero area, so the first CTA feels weak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the promise. Either lead with a true two-offer story ("Two ways to save") or add a real third deal to the dining module.
- 2. Put the five-night minimum and a booking window ("Book by Nov 30, stay through Mar 15") next to every mention of the 20%.
- 3. Merge the duplicate 20% module with the hero, and use the freed slot for a rate example such as "from $X/night, 5 nights."
- 4. Make the CTAs specific and consistent: "Book 5 nights, save 20%," "Earn 5,000 points," and "See Marelle's menu."
- 5. Cut the logo grid down to a short row or move it below the footer links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Santa Monica: 20% off 5 nights + 5,000 points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Save 20% on a beachside Santa Monica stay`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five nights or more at Sandbourne, with ocean views and a heated pool. Book by Nov 30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 20% off with 5,000 Bonvoy points per stay. Heated pool, Pier access, and a beach bundle included.`

# Technical Audit: "Three Ways to Stay in Santa Monica and Save"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several malformed `<meta>` tags, an empty `<title>`, and a plain-text part of only 34 characters. All 9 probed tracking links returned 403, but the identical failure on every link, including UNSUBSCRIBE, points to bot-blocking of the probe rather than a real outage. The QA data is inconclusive on link health, authentication headers, and continuity.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 9 probed links returned 403.** They are all `https://email-marriott.com/T/v61000001a167616c2bbccad96e96c65848/.../7310878a-f801-409a-821a-6d7c8f549f95?__dU__=...&__F__=...` redirects. Affected link text:
  - "Find & Reserve"
  - "Stay Five Nights and Save up to 20%"
  - "BOOK AND SAVE"
  - "BOOK NOW"
  - "RESERVE TODAY"
  - "FIND HOTELS"
  - "UNSUBSCRIBE"
  - two image links
- **Likely a probe artifact, not confirmed breakage.** A uniform 403 across distinct link IDs on one redirect domain looks like WAF or bot filtering, which is common for ESP click-trackers. Real recipients may be redirected correctly. The 19% pass rate is probably understated for the same reason. Verify with a real-browser or recipient-context click-through before calling these broken.
- **Image links have no accessible name.** Two linked images (`.../7310878af801409a0000021ef3a0bcc5` and `...bcc8`) surface as link text `[image: images]`.
- **Very long URLs.** The `__F__` tokens run several hundred characters (the "BOOK AND SAVE" URL is roughly 700). That is not a defect by itself, but it raises truncation risk in plain-text clients and some security gateways.
- **Coverage gap.** 16 links were skipped by design, so footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial scale=1.0">`. The directive should be `initial-scale=1.0`. As written, some clients may ignore it and lose responsive scaling.
- **Malformed robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The directive should be `noindex`. This is low impact in email.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Missing alt text** on 4 images served from `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images?id=`:
  - `564f6221-535a-4c75-a455-2f9edd1bacf4`
  - `2ba7367b-7a1f-4476-b072-b74fb605b3f6`
  - `59f725b7-7dfb-4cc5-8aac-1733595d5ca9`
  - `40ce1fe2-e462-46a4-a823-87dffed5ab3c`

  Two of these appear to be inside the linked images flagged above, so their alt text is the only accessible name those links get. The fifth flagged image, `email-marriott.com/O/v61000001a167616c2bbccad96e96c65848/7310878af801409a00004c5a42963aa1`, looks like the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Plain-text part is 34 characters (FAIL).** Clients and filters that use the text part get almost no content, and a near-empty text part is a spam signal.
- **No other issues found** in the truncated source. The `lang="en"` attribute, MSO conditionals, and font fallbacks are present as far as can be seen.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed, and verify against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation, not a finding about the sender. Check the raw headers of a direct-delivery copy.
- **In-body unsubscribe link is present** (`UNSUBSCRIBE`, `.../7310878af801409a0000021ef3a0bcd7/...`). It returned 403 to the probe, so functionality is unverified.
- **Physical postal address** is not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from this data. Every CTA is wrapped in an `email-marriott.com/T/...` redirect that the probe could not follow, so final destinations, UTM parameters, and landing-page alignment with the "Santa Monica / Save up to 20%" offer are unknown. No UTM parameters are visible on the wrapper URLs. They may be applied at redirect time.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the CTA and unsubscribe links with a browser-realistic user agent or a real browser session. Confirm the final URL, status, and UTMs before treating the 403s as defects.
2. Fix the viewport meta to `initial-scale=1.0` and the robots meta to `noindex`.
3. Add a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Add `alt` text to the four Epsilon-hosted images. Describe the destination for the linked ones. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Provide a real plain-text alternative that includes the offer, CTA URLs, and unsubscribe link.
