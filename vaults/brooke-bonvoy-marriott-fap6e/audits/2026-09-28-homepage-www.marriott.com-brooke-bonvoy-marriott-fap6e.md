# Homepage snapshot · www.marriott.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads like the Marriott Bonvoy homepage doing exactly what it's supposed to do for me: booking widget front and center, Bonvoy branding everywhere, and a fat 100K-point credit card bump card sitting right there above the fold. It's pushing a Vegas "limited-time" hotel deal, a New Hampshire fall foliage story, and that Bonvoy Boundless card bonus — nothing screaming "elite member," but nothing insulting either.

## What's working

- The **100,000 Bonus Points + $100 in Airline Credits** card offer — that's exactly the kind of concrete, points-hoarder bait that gets my attention immediately.
- **"Find Hotels" search widget** front and center with dates already defaulted to today — zero friction to start a search.
- **Member Exclusives tab** in the sub-nav — tells me there's a reason to log in, even before I do.
- **Ask Bonvoy (Beta)** AI search teaser — feels like the brand is investing in something new, which I like to see as a long-time member.

## What's weak

- **"Your Next Trip Starts Here"** hero headline is filler copy — it could be any OTA, not a program built on Cat 1-8 sweet spots and no-blackout-date language.
- No visible **points balance, status tier, or free-night cert reminder** — as a Titanium member I'd expect at least a peek at that once logged in, and this screenshot gives no hint the brand knows who I am.
- **"Limited-Time Offer"** on the Las Vegas card has no actual deadline attached — no date, no countdown, so it doesn't create real urgency for me.
- No pricing, member rates, or point-redemption values shown anywhere — nothing here tells me what a room actually costs in cash or points.

## Recommendations

- Swap the generic "Your Next Trip Starts Here" hero line for something that nods to status/points, like "Your Titanium perks are waiting."
- Add a real deadline to the Vegas "Limited-Time Offer" card — a date or countdown, not just the label.
- Surface a points/certs teaser above the fold for logged-in-eligible users (e.g., "You have a Free Night Award available") to reward the loyalty behavior the program is built on.
- Show a member-rate or points-value callout next to the search bar so the redemption math is visible before I even search.
