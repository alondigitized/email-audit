# Start Earning Now: Get up to 4,500 Bonus Points per Stay.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a Marriott Bonvoy bonus-points promotion. Earn 1,500 points on stays of 2+ nights, and 3,000 more at resorts, with registration required and an end date of November 22. The offer is clear and the deadline is real. It's a reminder, not a first announcement, so the "Here's your reminder" line is honest. Compared with the 2026-09-30 "Act Fast to Get up to 55% Bonus Points" send, this one is calmer and less urgent.
- The weak spot is the subject line. "Up to 4,500 per Stay" is the sum of two conditions: 2+ nights, plus resort properties. The body has to unpack that, and the headline reads better than the subject. A tall blank white band under the golf-resort hero also breaks the flow just before the explainer copy. It looks like a failed or empty image block, and a subscriber would notice it.
- Everything below the second CTA is filler: "More Reasons to Relax," an app promo and the brand-logo grid. It dilutes a single-offer email. Cadence is also heavy. This is at least the fifth Bonvoy promo in about ten days, after the 10/13 Boundless card send, the 10/10 account-update catch-all and the 10/09 Moxy NYC roundup. Overall it's a competent, mid-pack promo, around a 6.

## What's working

- The headline states the offer in plain numbers: "Earn 1,500 Bonus Points on Stays of 2+ Nights and 3,000 More at Resorts."
- A salmon "OFFER ENDS NOVEMBER 22" tag sits above the headline, so the deadline is the first thing seen.
- The black "REGISTER NOW" pill is high-contrast and appears twice, so no one has to scroll to find it.
- The golf and wellness resort hero is aspirational and fits the "resorts" half of the offer.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace, a consistent serif and sans pairing, and an on-brand look.

## What's weak

- A large blank white gap sits between the hero image and the body copy. It looks like a missing image or module and pushes the explainer text far down.
- The "up to 4,500 per stay" math is never shown simply. The subject adds two conditional bonuses into one number, and the asterisk definition of a "stay" is buried at the bottom.
- The hero image has no link context. It's a generic resort aerial with no property, price or destination tied to the offer.
- The lower modules (a "More Reasons to Relax" split image with a weak "EXPLORE RESORTS" text link, an app download block and the full brand-logo grid) compete with the one registration action.
- The sender's cadence is heavy. Another points promo this soon after the 10/13 and 10/06 sends dulls the urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the empty band under the hero, and move the offer explainer directly under the image.
- 2. Put the math in the first body block, as a two-line visual: "2+ nights = 1,500 pts. Resort stay = +3,000 pts."
- 3. Cut the secondary modules, or limit them to a single "Browse eligible resorts" button. Keep one goal: Register Now.
- 4. Change the CTA to something more specific, like "Register for Bonus Points." Also bring the asterisk terms up near the offer.
- 5. Drop the subject's sentence-ending period and shorten it to under 50 characters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Register by Nov 22: 4,500 bonus points per resort stay`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 1,500-point bonus is waiting, ends Nov 22`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stay 2+ nights, earn 1,500 points. Resort stays earn 3,000 more. Register first.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Register now. This bonus ends November 22 and applies to participating hotels.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is well-formed (lang, title, MSO/Outlook fixes), but it ships with no plain-text part and every probed link failed QA. The link failures look mostly like click-tracker bot blocking and redirect-handling artifacts, not confirmed customer-facing breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on tracked links (6 of 12 probed failures).** The links are on `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a1544d14ad8a50fff4bbcfb648/<id>/f5da9a8c-3e74-46dc-ba63-97becd5db32b?__dU__=…&__F__=…`. The affected link texts are "find & reserve", "» find & reserve", "Marriott Bonvoy" (×2) and "UNSUBSCRIBE".
  - A uniform 403 across all of these points to ESP click-tracker bot or user-agent filtering. It doesn't prove the links are broken for real recipients.
  - The failure still means the QA probe can't verify any destination, including the unsubscribe target.
- **"unknown url type" on 4 links.** Targets are relative paths: `/sign-in.mi?returnTo=%2Floyalty%2Fpromotion.mi%3Fpromotion%3DWL26` ("Register now" ×2, "Go to the promotion registration page.") and `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12175560&lk=1000301947` ("MY ACCOUNT").
  - The probe received a host-less path, so either the tracker's redirect `Location` is relative or the probe mishandles the redirect chain. I can't tell which from the data.
  - A relative `Location` would resolve against `email-marriott.com` and be a real bug. Check this manually with `curl -I` using a browser User-Agent.
- **Duplicate CTAs with distinct tracking IDs.** "Register now" appears at least twice, with separate tracker IDs (`…0bcc9`, `…0bccb`). This is normal for per-placement tracking, not a defect.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 19 were skipped, so the footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The image at `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a1544d14ad8a50fff4bbcfb648/f5da9a8c3e7446dc00004c5a42963aa1` has no `alt`. The `/O/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel. If so, it should carry `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
- **Insecure font URLs.** `@font-face` for `Aldine-Regular` references `http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/MarriottDigital/AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` and the Italic `.woff`. The `.woff2` sources are HTTPS and listed first, so the impact is limited. Clients that block mixed content could drop the HTTP fallback.
- **Wrong XHTML namespace.** `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` should be `http://`. It's harmless in practice.
- **Text-size adjust.** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` in the global style block disables iOS text scaling. That is a minor accessibility regression.
- **External stylesheet.** `<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://use.typekit.net/yft3kyd.css">` is stripped by many clients. The font stacks have `Arial`/`Times New Roman` fallbacks, so there's no breakage.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens appear, though the HTML was truncated. The `MY ACCOUNT` URL carries per-recipient identifiers (`nck=1028103536&ck=12175560&lk=1000301947`). That's expected for loyalty deep-linking.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part missing (FAIL).** QA reports the text version as 0 chars. This is a confirmed deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **Unsubscribe headers not found (WARN).** Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was captured. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the origin.
- **Physical address not detected (WARN).** The HTML is truncated, so the footer may contain one. This is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results absent (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay doesn't expose the header. Don't treat it as a failure.
- **Unsubscribe link present.** The "UNSUBSCRIBE" link exists but returned 403 to the probe, so its function is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM params.** Every CTA is wrapped in an `email-marriott.com/T/…` redirect, so the destination `utm_*` parameters can't be seen. Marriott's own attribution parameters (`enrollmentSourceCode=INCK`, `nck`, `ck`, `lk`) are visible on the My Account link.
- **Landing alignment.** "Register now" routes through `/sign-in.mi?returnTo=/loyalty/promotion.mi?promotion=WL26`, which is an auth-gated landing. This is consistent with the "Start Earning Now" promo, but I couldn't resolve the final pages because of the 403s and relative-URL errors.
- **Unverified.** Landing-page match is unconfirmed for all links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text MIME part.** It's the only fully confirmed defect.
2. **Re-probe the tracked links** with a browser-like User-Agent and manual redirect following. Resolve the relative `Location` on the `/sign-in.mi` and `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi` links. If the 403s persist for real clients, escalate to the ESP.
3. **Verify unsubscribe from the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present at the origin, and that the UNSUBSCRIBE link resolves.
4. **Confirm the footer address** against the full, untruncated HTML.
5. **Set `alt=""`** on the tracking pixel (`…4c5a42963aa1`).
6. **Minor cleanup:**
   - Switch the `http://cache.marriott.com` font URLs to HTTPS.
   - Correct the XHTML namespace.
   - Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` from the `*` selector.
7. **Adjust the QA tool.** It scored this email at 12% largely from probe limitations. Treat click-tracker 403s as "unverifiable", not "broken", so scores aren't inflated.
