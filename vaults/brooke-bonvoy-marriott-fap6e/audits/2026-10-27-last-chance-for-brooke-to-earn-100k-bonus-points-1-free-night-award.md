# Last Chance for Brooke to Earn 100K Bonus Points + 1 Free Night Award
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing push for the Marriott Bonvoy Business American Express card. It offers 100,000 bonus points plus a Free Night Award. The offer needs $8,000 of spend in 6 months and ends 11/4/2026, a week from today. The "Last Chance" framing is credible and the headline offer is concrete. I could only read the legal copy clearly. The hero, benefit cards and CTAs I read from the downscaled render, so the finer details there are less certain.
- The send is a card-acquisition email, and it is the fourth in this series after 10/6, 10/20 and 10/13. A subscriber who has seen "100K points" three times in three weeks will register this as more of the same. The only new element is the deadline. It is a business card going to a leisure-style "Brooke" persona, which weakens relevance.
- The legal block is the weak spot. It is a wall of fine print that makes up most of the email's length. Seeing it buried under the CTAs undercuts the "honest offer" feel. The $8,000 spend threshold is not in the headline, so the offer looks easier to earn than it is.

## What's working

- The "Last Chance • Offer ends 11/4" header gives the deadline immediately.
- The hero states one offer clearly: 100,000 points plus a Free Night Award.
- "Apply Now" buttons appear near the top and again after the benefits, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The benefit cards, such as the member-pricing and late-checkout style perks, make the card feel more than a points grab.
- The Bonvoy branding is consistent and the sender is recognizable.

## What's weak

- The $8,000 spend and 6-month window are not in the hero, so the headline reads better than the offer is.
- The legal and terms block is very long, with several paragraphs of dense text below the CTA.
- This is the fourth near-identical card pitch in about three weeks, after 10/6, 10/13 and 10/20, so fatigue is likely.
- It promotes a business card to a persona that reads as a leisure traveler, which looks mistargeted.
- The "Last Chance" deadline is tempered by the generic "Offer ends" phrasing, which gives no hour or countdown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the spend requirement beside the headline, for example "100K points + Free Night after $8,000 in 6 months", and keep the legal block a collapsed link.
- 2. Make the deadline specific: "Ends Nov 4 at 11:59 PM MST".
- 3. Segment business-card sends to people who show business-owner signals. Send leisure-focused members a consumer card or a stay offer instead.
- 4. Cap card sends at one per two weeks, or vary the creative when you repeat an offer.
- 5. Add a hotel-value example, such as "Free Night worth up to 50,000 points", so the reward feels tangible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke: 100K points + a Free Night ends Nov 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 100K-point offer ends in 8 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $8,000 in 6 months. Free Night Award worth up to 50K points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Apply by 11:59 PM MST Nov 4 to lock in the bonus.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a real deliverability gap: the plain-text part is empty (0 chars). Every probed tracked link (all `email-marriott.com/T/...`) returned 403 or an error, so QA could not verify any destination. Several of these look like probe artifacts rather than defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the body markup was not audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on tracked links:** all five probed redirects returned 403: "find & reserve" (two links), "Marriott Bonvoy" (two links) and "UNSUBSCRIBE". They are all on `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a17e519.../<id>?__dU__=...&__F__=...`. A uniform 403 across the ESP click-tracking domain, including unsubscribe, points to bot-blocking of the QA fetcher, not broken links. This is unconfirmed, and the real destinations were never reached.
- **"MY ACCOUNT" link error:** `unknown url type: '/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12319936&lk=1000301947'`. The target is a root-relative path with no scheme or host. Either the redirect `Location` is relative (which the probe can't follow) or the destination is malformed. Real mail clients and browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracking host. That would send the user to `email-marriott.com/loyalty/...` instead of `marriott.com`, which is a risk. Verify the redirect target manually.
- **Coverage:** only 12 of 55 links were probed, and 43 were skipped as footer or social links. Their status is unknown.
- **Tracking params:** the `__dU__` and `__F__` tokens are very long, over 400 characters per URL. This is normal for ESP tokens and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing plain-text part:** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. This is a multipart/alternative gap. It hurts spam scoring and affects text-only clients and screen-reader or accessibility pipelines.
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a17e519fc5b689446e966a3578/0b7cddf6fe87419900004c5a42963aa1` is likely the open-tracking pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
  - `CS0692145-icon_more-points_top.png`
  - `CS0692145-icon_late-checkout_top.png`
  - The last two are `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/.../SOLOS/` icons. Each needs either descriptive alt text or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Font declarations:** some `@font-face` sources use `http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/.../AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` and `...-Italic.woff`, which is insecure transport. Several `src` entries use `format('otf')`, which is not a valid format hint (the valid value is `opentype`). Impact is low because fallbacks to `'Times New Roman'` and Arial are defined.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders "Brooke" and no unresolved tokens are visible in the supplied source. The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are reported missing. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation and can't be assessed from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe:** a visible "UNSUBSCRIBE" link is present, but it was not verified because the probe got a 403.
- **Postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it is not confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Final destinations unknown:** the 403s mean QA could not resolve any redirect chain. UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed for the "find & reserve" or "Marriott Bonvoy" CTAs.
- **Only visible param set:** the "MY ACCOUNT" link carries Epsilon-style IDs (`enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=...&ck=...&lk=...`) and no `utm_*` parameters. Attribution relies on those proprietary IDs and the redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a text/plain alternative part with the main copy and key links. This is the one confirmed high-priority fix.
2. Re-probe the tracked links with a real-browser User-Agent, or manually follow the redirects, to separate bot-blocking from real 403s. Do this first for UNSUBSCRIBE and the "find & reserve" CTAs.
3. Check the "MY ACCOUNT" redirect `Location` and make sure it is an absolute `https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?...` URL.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and the decorative icons, or real alt text where the icons carry meaning.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw message, bypassing the relay.
6. Switch the `http://` font URLs to `https://`, and change `format('otf')` to `format('opentype')`.
