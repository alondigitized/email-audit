# Set the Scene for Your Next Trip, Brooke
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a content-magazine send dressed up as personalization — "Set the Scene for Your Next Trip, Brooke" promises something tailored, but what follows is a generic "movies and TV inspired travel" roundup with a dozen competing links (New Orleans filming spots, Emily in Paris trivia, Game of Thrones tours, a "which type of traveler are you" quiz). It reads like a travel-blog digest, not a Marriott offer email.
- Buried about a third of the way down is the actual business point: 150K Points + $250 Back on the Brilliant Amex card. It's visually flattened to the same weight as a trivia game and a theme-park photo — no color pop, no separation from the editorial content around it. Compare this to 2026-08-12's "Brooke, for a Limited Time: Earn 150,000 Bonus Points," which led with that exact same 150K offer as the hero and scored 8/10. Here the identical offer is demoted to filler in the middle of a scroll-heavy content dump.
- Judgement: this is Marriott's weakest recent send. It has no single clear CTA, no urgency, and repeats an offer from just over a week ago but strips out all of the visual prominence that made it work the first time.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero image (a lantern-lit bridge/pagoda scene) ties into the "cinematic destinations" theme without looking stocky.
- The name personalization in the subject line ("...Your Next Trip, Brooke") is a real signal, even if the body doesn't follow through.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent block-to-block.
- The black-background Disney/theme-park module and trivia module create some visual rhythm breaking up the white content blocks.

## What's weak

- No single clear primary CTA — there are at least 10 distinct links/buttons (Explore 8 Cinema-Inspired Escapes, Find Your Fun, Movie Buffs' Guide, Learn More, Find Out Now, Discover 10 Destinations, Reveal Your Results), which splits attention and buries the actual money offer.
- The 150K Points + $250 Back card offer — the only real business-impact item — is visually undersized, given no hero treatment, and sandwiched between theme-park and trivia content.
- This is a near-repeat of the 2026-08-12 150K offer with none of the urgency or visual punch that made that version an 8/10; here it reads as an afterthought.
- Extremely long scroll (looks like 10+ content sections) for an inbox open — most readers will bail before reaching the September travel-timing block or the traveler-type quiz at the bottom.
- "Destinations That Leap Off the Screen and Into Your Itinerary" is vague, general-audience travel-blog copy, not something that reads as relevant to a specific Bonvoy member's account or spend.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero: either this is a card-acquisition email (lead with 150K + $250, big and bold, matching the 2026-08-12 execution) or a content/inspiration email — don't try to be both. Splitting focus is the single biggest drag on this send.
- 2. If keeping the movie/TV theme, cut the module count by half — 10+ CTAs in one email guarantees decision paralysis. Keep 3-4 strongest sections (New Orleans, Emily in Paris trivia, the card offer) and cut the rest to a footer link.
- 3. Give the points offer visual weight equal to or greater than the hero image — right now a photo of Mickey and Minnie outranks a $250 statement credit in visual hierarchy.
- 4. Add urgency to the card offer if any deadline exists (the 2026-08-12 send didn't specify one either, but doing so here would differentiate this repeat send from being a pure duplicate).
- 5. Shorten the email significantly — this is one of the longest Marriott sends audited so far and most of the back half (September travel tips, traveler-type quiz) reads as filler.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails the majority of automated QA checks — 6 of 12 probed material links return HTTP 403 (including Unsubscribe), the plain-text MIME part is empty, and no List-Unsubscribe/List-Unsubscribe-Post headers were captured, indicating both link-tracking and compliance-layer failures.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 6 of 12 probed links return **403 Broken link** errors, all routed through the ESP click-tracking redirector `email-marriott.com/T/...`: link texts "Find" (2 instances), "Explore 8 Cinema-Inspired Escapes", "Find Your Fun", "Discover 10 Destinations", "Find & Reserve", and **Unsubscribe**.
- One additional link ("Find") fails with "unknown url type" pointing to a raw loyalty deep link: `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=10890232&lk=1000320053` — this appears to be an unresolved/malformed relative path embedded inside the tracking wrapper's `__dU__` payload rather than a fully qualified URL, which is likely the root cause of the 403s (the redirector can't resolve destination).
- All broken links share the same tracking domain prefix and payload structure (`__dU__=`, `__F__=` params), suggesting a systemic redirect-service issue rather than isolated broken links.
- 41 of 53 links were deprioritized/skipped in probing (footer/utility/social); only material CTAs and the unsubscribe link were checked.

- 10 images missing `alt` attributes, including all 6 open/tracking pixels from `sli.marriott.com/imp?...` (expected/low-impact for pixels) and one content image `e3d7cbf3cf5f4dbc00004c5a42963aa1` served from `email-marriott.com/O/...` (impacts screen-reader users).
- Web fonts (Aldine, Swiss721BT) are loaded via `@font-face` from `cache.marriott.com` with MSO-conditional fallbacks to Times New Roman/Arial — correctly guarded for Outlook, no issue found there.
- No structural rendering failures reported by QA beyond the alt-text warnings.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected, consistent with the above.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected via the relay — authentication status cannot be verified from available data.
- Note: the header-related warnings are flagged by QA as possibly relay-capture artifacts, not confirmed absences — treat as unverified rather than confirmed failures.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All observed links route through the `email-marriott.com/T/...` click-tracking redirector rather than exposing direct UTM-tagged destination URLs, so landing-page/UTM alignment cannot be verified from the HTML source alone — this is opaque by design (ESP redirect wrapper) and not itself a defect, but combined with the 403s it means downstream UTM/landing continuity cannot be confirmed at all for 6 of 12 material CTAs.

7. Recommendations
1. Investigate the click-tracking redirect service for `email-marriott.com/T/...` — 6 of 12 probed CTAs (50%) return 403, including Unsubscribe; prioritize fixing Unsubscribe immediately given CAN-SPAM exposure.
2. Resolve the malformed/unresolved destination URL for the second "Find" link (relative loyalty path `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?...` causing "unknown url type").
3. Regenerate the plain-text MIME alternative — current text part is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and violates multipart best practices.
4. Add `alt` text to the content image `e3d7cbf3cf5f4dbc00004c5a42963aa1`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (verify at the MTA/ESP level, independent of relay capture) to support one-click unsubscribe compliance.
