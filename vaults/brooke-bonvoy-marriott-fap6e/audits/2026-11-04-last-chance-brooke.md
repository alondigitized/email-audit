# Last Chance, Brooke!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a Marriott Bonvoy bonus-points promo for stays, and it is a resend of the 10/19 send, "Start Earning Now: Get up to 4,500 Bonus Points per Stay." The offer is the same: 1,500 points on 2+ night stays, plus 3,000 more at resorts, with the Nov 22 end date. The "Last Chance, Brooke!" framing is not credible, because the countdown timer shows 18 days left. The urgency tag and the timer contradict each other, so the email feels like a nudge it can't back up.
- The structure is clean. A countdown sits on top, then a headline and a "Register Now" button, then a beach hero photo. The offer is visible without scrolling. The weak spots are a blank gap under the hero image, where something looks like it failed to load, and a body of generic lifestyle modules (resorts, app download) that dilute the single registration action. This is the third or fourth points promo in about two weeks, alongside the card pitches on 10/20 and 10/27.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand template with a mismatched urgency claim and a visible render gap. It is a middling send that could lift with a more honest subject line.

## What's working

- The headline "Earn up to 4,500 Bonus Points per Stay" states a concrete, quantified offer.
- "Register Now" is a dark pill button, shown twice, with an unambiguous verb.
- The body copy names the mechanics: 1,500 points on stays of 2+ nights, 3,000 more at resorts, ending November 22.
- The aerial beach hero is attractive and matches the resort theme.
- The "Brooke" personalization appears in both the subject and the body.

## What's weak

- "Last Chance!" sits next to a countdown showing 18 days, so the urgency is not believable.
- A large blank white gap under the hero image shows a faint circle, which looks like a broken or unloaded image or spinner.
- The "More Reasons to Relax" and "Download App" modules pull attention from the registration action. Their links are small text, not buttons.
- The "Featured property" link is a tiny underlined line with no image or offer, so it adds nothing.
- The footer's long brand logo block pushes the legal copy far down the page.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the urgency honest. Either drop "Last Chance" until the final days or tie it to a real deadline. Keep the countdown, but make the subject match what it shows.
- 2. Fix the blank gap under the hero, or remove it. Whatever was meant to load there should either render or be cut.
- 3. Move the points math above the fold. Show a simple example, such as "2-night resort stay = 4,500 points," near the first button.
- 4. Cut the secondary modules down to one, or turn them into a real CTA such as "Find a resort that qualifies."
- 5. Reduce the repeated brand-logo block and the footer clutter.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stay 2+ nights and earn 1,500 points, or 4,500 at resorts. Registration required.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your bonus points offer ends Nov 22. Register in one tap.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA shows 13% pass, but most link failures are probably probe artifacts: 403s from the ESP click-tracker and a relative-redirect parse error. The issues the source or QA data actually supports are the empty plain-text part, missing compliance signals in the captured data, insecure `http://` font URLs, and one image with no alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on tracked links (5 links):** "find & reserve", "» find & reserve", two "Marriott Bonvoy" links and "UNSUBSCRIBE" all return 403. All are `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a1a6776afca5ef5d369fb45280/...`. Wrapped ESP click-tracking URLs often block non-browser user agents, so this likely reflects the probe rather than real-user breakage. It is **unverified**. The UNSUBSCRIBE link failing the probe should be rechecked with a browser-like client.
- **Relative redirect target (3 links):** The two "Register now" links and "MY ACCOUNT" fail with `unknown url type: '/sign-in.mi?returnTo=%2Floyalty%2Fpromotion.mi%3Fpromotion%3DWL26'` and `'/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12467436&lk=1000301947'`.
  - The redirect `Location` is a host-less path. If that is what the tracker really emits, browsers would resolve it against `email-marriott.com` (the tracking domain), not `marriott.com`. That would send users to a 404.
  - It may instead be a probe-side parsing limit. Confirm with `curl -sI` on one of these URLs.
- **Duplicate destinations:** The two "Register now" links have different tracking IDs (`...bcc9` and `...bccb`) and different `__F__` payloads. Both fail with the same error and the same destination.
- **Identifiers in the URL:** The "MY ACCOUNT" destination carries `nck=1028103536&ck=12467436&lk=1000301947`. These are campaign/link IDs and probably not PII, but they are exposed in the query string.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 31 links were probed; 19 were skipped. Link health for the rest is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** One image has no alt text: `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a1a6776afca5ef5d369fb45280/de2901aff184474e00004c5a42963aa1`. The `/O/` path pattern suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` to it.
- **Insecure font URLs:** `@font-face` for `Aldine-Regular` loads `http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/MarriottDigital/AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` and `...-Italic.woff` over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed content will drop them, and the `Times New Roman, serif` fallback applies. The `.woff2` sources are HTTPS.
- **Wrong XHTML namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` should be `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This has no practical rendering effect.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS text resizing, which is an accessibility concern. It conflicts with the later `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%` on `.body-fix` and `table, td`.
- **Web font dependency:** `<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://use.typekit.net/yft3kyd.css">` is an external stylesheet that many clients strip. The `Good-Karma` class falls back to `Brush Script MT, serif`.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, the viewport meta tag and the MSO DPI fix are all present.
- **Limit of this check:** The HTML source was truncated, so the body markup (alt text, table roles, hidden preheader) could not be reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject and `<title>` both render "Last Chance, Brooke!" with the name resolved, and no unresolved token patterns appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part:** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. The message appears to be HTML-only. This is a deliverability and accessibility risk.
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. It may be in the truncated footer, so this needs a check of the full source.
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header is absent from the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `email-marriott.com` matches the tracking domain, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **Unsubscribe link:** An "UNSUBSCRIBE" link is present, but the probe returned 403. See section 2.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They cannot be verified, because every link is wrapped in the `email-marriott.com/T/...` tracker and the real destinations were not resolved.
- **Where tracking lives:** The only visible destination parameters are `returnTo=%2Floyalty%2Fpromotion.mi%3Fpromotion%3DWL26` and `enrollmentSourceCode=INCK`. Attribution depends on the ESP tracker rather than UTMs.
- **Landing flow:** "Register now" sends users to `/sign-in.mi` with a `returnTo` pointing at the promotion page (`promotion=WL26`). Whether the promotion survives login could not be tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 403 links with a browser-like User-Agent, or follow redirects manually. Start with UNSUBSCRIBE.
2. Run `curl -sI` on one "Register now" or "MY ACCOUNT" tracked URL. Confirm the `Location` header is absolute (`https://www.marriott.com/...`) and not host-less.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part. It is currently 0 chars.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, and for a footer postal address.
5. Switch the `http://cache.marriott.com/...` font URLs to `https://`.
6. Add `alt=""` to the tracking-pixel image.
7. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` from the `*` rule.
8. Resolve the `email-marriott.com/T/...` links end to end in a real client and check the final URLs for UTM parameters and landing-page match.
