# Seven Days Only. 200K Bonus Points.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud card-acquisition send for the Marriott Bonvoy Boundless Visa. It leads with a live countdown (6 days, 15 hours), a huge "100,000 BONUS POINTS" hero and a card image. The deadline is credible: 11/11 is one week out. The hierarchy is excellent, and the eye goes straight to the number.
- The offer is confusing, though. The subject says "200K Bonus Points," but the hero says 100,000 plus a $100 airline credit. Only in the body does the math appear: the 200,000 comes from the 100K bonus plus a second 100K, and it requires $3K of spend in 3 months. The subject promises more than the hero shows. Click-through also depends on whether this is the third or fourth card pitch in a month, which is what the history suggests.
- Judgement: strong execution and urgency, weakened by the subject/hero mismatch and card-fatigue. It is a good email for a card-curious reader and a mediocre one for a points-and-stays subscriber.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (6:15:3:54) plus "ENDS 11/11" gives a specific, visible deadline.
- The giant "100,000 BONUS POINTS" hero is unmissable on a dark background.
- Both "APPLY NOW" buttons are orange, high-contrast and unambiguous, one near the top and one after the math.
- The "0 + 200,000 = 200,000" points calculator makes the value tangible.
- The $95 annual fee sits directly under each CTA, which reads as honest.

## What's weak

- The subject says 200K, but the hero shows 100,000 + $100. The reader has to scroll to reconcile them.
- "Seven Days Only" does not match the "6 days" on the timer, which is a small credibility nick.
- The body explains the $3K spend requirement only in small text. The headline hides the spend threshold.
- The brand portfolio logo block is long, low-value filler before the footer.
- There is no social proof or product-fit detail, such as the free night award or earning rates.
- The long legal footer takes up a third of the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Show "200,000 Bonus Points" with "100K + 100K" below, or change the subject to 100K.
- 2. Put the spend condition ("after $3K in 3 months") in the hero subline, not only in the body.
- 3. Add the card's everyday benefit, such as a free night award or points per dollar, next to the second CTA.
- 4. Cut the brand logo grid, or collapse it to a single row.
- 5. Use only one CTA, or make the second CTA copy specific.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooke: 6 days left for 100K bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our biggest Boundless offer ends 11/11`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $100 in airline credits. $95 annual fee. Terms apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $3K in 3 months and earn 200K points total.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has no confirmed rendering defects, but the QA run couldn't verify the click-tracking links, including UNSUBSCRIBE (403 or unresolvable redirect). The plain-text part is empty, and the auth and compliance headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on tracked links.** `email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a1abf7e…` returned 403 for "find & reserve" (`…0bcc9`), "» find & reserve" (`…0bcc6`), both "Marriott Bonvoy" logo links (`…0bcc4`, `…0bcc7`) and "UNSUBSCRIBE" (`…0bcda`). A uniform 403 across one ESP click-tracking domain looks like bot-blocking of the probe. It is not proof the links are dead, so treat them as unverified.
- **Redirects with relative targets.** "MY ACCOUNT" (`…0bcc8`) and two "Marriott Bonvoy" links (`…0bcd5`, `…0bcd6`) failed with `unknown url type: '/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?…'` and `'/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?…'`. The redirect response contains a relative path with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve this fine, but some clients and scanners won't. This is likely a probe limitation, not a user-facing break.
- **Coverage.** Only 12 of 31 links were probed. The 19 skipped are mostly footer and social links, so their status is unknown.
- **Link text vs. destination.** "Marriott Bonvoy" is used for the logo link (403, unresolved) and for two links to `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi`. The link text doesn't describe the brands page.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is empty (0 chars)**, flagged `[FAIL]`. Text-only clients and some spam filters penalize this.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://live.harmony.epsilon.com/live/wi/countdownTimer/c651e3a0-…/o` is a live countdown image with no `alt`. If images are blocked, the "seven days" deadline is lost to screen-reader and image-off users.
  - `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a1abf7e…/e340c6e1de0f499300004c5a42963aa1` is probably the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **@font-face declarations (visible head only):**
  - Several use `format('otf')`, which is not a valid format hint (should be `opentype`).
  - Aldine-Regular and Aldine-Regular-Italic list `http://cache.marriott.com/…woff` as a fallback, which is insecure and may be blocked.
  - Both are low severity because `'Times New Roman'` and `Arial` fallbacks are defined.
- The HTML was truncated, so the body structure, `role="presentation"` on tables, and dark-mode handling couldn't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied source or QA data. Per-recipient identifiers (`nck=1028103536&ck=12486222&lk=…`, `__dU__`) resolved correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The relay is the likely cause, and the From domain is `email-marriott.com`.
- **No physical address detected.** This may be in the truncated footer, so it needs a check against the full source.
- **In-body unsubscribe link exists** ("UNSUBSCRIBE", `…0bcda`) but returned 403 on probe, so its function is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** Visible destinations use Marriott's own scheme (`enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=…&ck=…&lk=…`) behind the `email-marriott.com` redirector, which is consistent and not an error.
- **Landing pages not verified.** The primary CTAs ("find & reserve") returned 403, so the destination page couldn't be checked against the "200K Bonus Points" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `email-marriott.com/T/…` links with a browser-like User-Agent, and follow relative `Location` headers. This confirms whether the 403s and `unknown url type` errors are probe artifacts. Confirm the UNSUBSCRIBE link and both "find & reserve" CTAs first.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part that includes the offer, CTA URL, unsubscribe URL and postal address.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the Epsilon countdown image (for example "Offer ends in …"), and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the physical address in the footer. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original message rather than the relay copy.
5. Change `format('otf')` to `format('opentype')` and serve the `.woff` fallbacks over HTTPS.
