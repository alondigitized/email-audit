# Ends 9/9: Get up to 50% Bonus Points, Brooke
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott Bonvoy's "buy points for a bonus" pitch — spend real money now through September 9 and get up to 35–50% extra points depending on how much you purchase. It's visually one of the cleaner sends in this thread (soft hammock/beach hero, tidy tier cards, a legible headline), but there's a real crack in the render: the hero headline promises "up to 50% Bonus Points" while the tier cards directly below only show 35% and 40%, with no visible card for the 50% tier the headline advertises. That's the kind of inconsistency a subscriber notices in three seconds and it undercuts the whole offer's credibility.
- This also continues the pattern from 2026-08-25's "Reveal Your Limited-Time Offer" — another vague, gimmicky "REVEAL OFFER" CTA button instead of a plain "Buy Points" or "See My Bonus." Combined with the fact that this is fundamentally an upsell (asking Brooke to spend cash to buy points, with the annual purchase cap quietly raised to 200,000 points buried in body copy), it reads more like a revenue play dressed as an "exclusive offer" than a genuine loyalty benefit.
- Net: attractive art direction, real urgency date, but a visible number mismatch and a vague CTA both drag it down. Middle-of-the-pack for this brand's recent cadence.

## What's working

- Clean hero: aspirational hammock/beach couple photo with a legible serif headline sets a relaxed, on-brand tone.
- "Your Exclusive Offer Awaits" eyebrow + personalized "Brooke" mention give it a members-only feel.
- Concrete deadline ("now through September 9") creates real, credible urgency without resorting to caps or exclamation points.
- Secondary module ("Redeem Points Your Way") gives a reason beyond the transaction — a nice added-value beat.
- Full property/brand portfolio grid at the bottom reinforces scale and trust.

## What's weak

- The headline says "up to 50% Bonus Points" but the visible tier cards top out at 40% — a direct, visible contradiction between hero copy and offer detail.
- There's an odd blank/tan rectangle to the right of the hero photo that looks like a cropped or partially-loaded image element, not intentional whitespace.
- "REVEAL OFFER" is a curiosity-gap CTA, not a description of what happens next — the same gimmick this brand used in the 2026-08-25 "Reveal Your Limited-Time Offer" send.
- The ask itself is friction-heavy: you have to log in with membership number/password before you can even see or act on the offer, and the fine print about the annual purchase limit being raised to 200,000 points is buried well below the CTA.
- No social proof or reason-to-believe (no reviews, no "member favorite" badge) — it's a bare financial transaction pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline/tier mismatch first — either add a visible 50% tier card or change the headline to "up to 40%" so the hero promise matches what's actually shown.
- 2. Replace "REVEAL OFFER" with a direct CTA like "Buy Points Now" or "See My Bonus %" — stop reusing the reveal gimmick, it reads as evasive on a money-ask email.
- 3. Surface the raised annual purchase limit (150K → 200K) as a visible benefit near the tier cards, not buried in body copy below the fold — it's actually a strong hook for high-volume buyers.
- 4. Fix or crop the blank image block beside the hero photo — it currently reads as a broken/missing asset.
- 5. Add a specific use-case reminder near the CTA (e.g., "That's enough for 3 free nights in Bali") to make the points bonus feel concrete rather than abstract.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Turn 40,000 points into a free night — bonus ends September 9`
