# Brooke, for a Limited Time: Earn 150,000 Bonus Points
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott's biggest card-acquisition number yet — 150,000 Bonus Points plus a $250 statement credit — topping the 125K offer that ran five times between June and July and the 60K/no-annual-fee push from 2026-08-03. The escalation itself is the story: Marriott has now cycled through 60K → 125K (5x) → 150K in about six weeks, which reads less like a special moment and more like an arms race against subscriber fatigue. The hero band (limited-time badge, big point number, Apply Now button) is clean and mirrors the format that worked on 2026-08-03.
- Where it falls short of that 08-03 send is credibility of urgency: "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" appears as static badge copy with no countdown, date, or expiration — the exact lever that made the 07-23 "125K + 1 Free Night" send work (13-day countdown timer) and that this email skips. The body is dense with card-benefit tiles (Free Night Award, Credit, Elite Status, Featured Properties, points-per-dollar chart) which is thorough but pushes past what most recipients will scroll through on a promo they've now seen restructured four times this summer.
- Verdict: solid, on-brand execution of a well-worn template, but the escalating-number-with-no-real-deadline pattern is starting to feel like inflation rather than urgency, and cadence fatigue is a real risk at this point.

## What's working

- Clean hero: big "Earn 150,000 Bonus Points" headline, tropical resort photo, and a single unambiguous "APPLY NOW" button above the fold.
- Card art (Brilliant Amex physical card) is shown clearly, reinforcing what's actually being sold.
- Structured benefit breakdown (Free Night Award, $300 credit, Elite status, 25 Elite Night Credits) gives concrete, specific value instead of vague brand talk.
- Points-earning comparison chart (1x/2x/6x/21x) visually explains why the card is worth it, which most competitor card emails skip.
- Footer/legal and account-management links are present and properly formatted, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- No real deadline — "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" is a static badge with no date or countdown, weaker than the countdown-timer version that worked on 2026-07-23.
- This is the third distinct point-value tier for essentially the same Brilliant Amex pitch in six weeks (60K → 125K ×5 → 150K), and a repeat subscriber will notice the number just keeps climbing.
- Email is very long and benefit-tile-heavy; the actual offer (150K + $250 credit) gets buried under Free Night Award mechanics, Elite status tiers, and a "Featured Properties" logo wall before getting back to the CTA.
- No personalization visible in the hero (no "Brooke" in the visible body copy, only implied via subject) — a missed chance to reinforce it matches the loyalty-tier tone of past sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real, visible deadline — replace the static "LIMITED-TIME OFFER" badge with an actual date or countdown timer, the way 2026-07-23 did; that was the single biggest urgency driver in this brand's recent history.
- 2. Trim the body — the Free Night Award, Elite status, and points-chart sections could collapse into one "why this card pays off" module; right now the offer competes with itself for attention.
- 3. Repeat the CTA immediately after the points-earning chart, not just at top and bottom — high-intent scrollers reading the math should see "APPLY NOW" right there.
- 4. Since this is the third pitch of the same card type this summer, add a differentiator line (e.g., "this month only" or reference to the higher point total) so returning subscribers understand why this offer beats the one they saw in July.

# Technical Email Audit — Marriott Bonvoy "150,000 Bonus Points"

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 23% pass rate with 7 failures and 3 warnings. Critical issues: multiple 403 link failures (including the unsubscribe link itself), a malformed deep-link URL, missing plain-text MIME part, and no email authentication headers captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 of 12 probed links failed.** All routed through the same click-tracking redirector (`https://email-marriott.com/T/v710000019ff5e4cbd9aa3192369ff0b0c8/...`) and returned **403 Forbidden**:
  - "find & reserve" (2 instances, distinct tracking IDs `...0bccd`/`...0bcd0`)
  - "Marriott Bonvoy" header/logo link (2 instances, `...0bccb`/`...0bcce`)
  - **"UNSUBSCRIBE"** (`...0bce8`) — see Compliance, this compounds the missing List-Unsubscribe header
- **"MY ACCOUNT" link error:** the tracking redirector resolves to `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=10763827&lk=1000301947` — a relative path with no scheme/host, causing "unknown url type" in the QA resolver. This is either a malformed redirect target on the sender's tracking platform or a relative-URL bug in link construction.
- 33 of 45 total links were deprioritized/skipped by the probe (footer, social, utility links) per standard ranking — not evidence of additional breakage, just unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images missing `alt` text:**
  - Tracking pixel: `f342b46459c94a6400004c5a42963aa1` (open-tracking beacon — low impact, but should still carry empty `alt=""`)
  - `CS0634426-icon_more-points_top.png`
  - `CS0634426-icon_room-upgrades_top.png`
  - `CS0634426-icon_late-checkout_top.png`
  These three icon images appear to convey benefit content (more points / room upgrades / late checkout) and lack accessible text alternatives for screen readers.
- Heavy reliance on custom `@font-face` web fonts (Aldine, Swiss) served from `cache.marriott.com`, with MSO/Outlook fallback via conditional comments — standard practice, no fallback gaps observed in the truncated source.
- One font source (`AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff`) is loaded over **plain HTTP** (`http://cache.marriott.com/...woff`) while all sibling font URLs use HTTPS — a mixed-content risk in HTTPS mail clients that render inline styles.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- First-name token "Brooke" resolved correctly in both the Subject line and `<title>` tag — no unresolved `{{merge}}` tags found.
- However, the `<title>` tag renders as **`Brooke,fora Limited Time&#58; Earn 150,000 Bonus Points`** — missing whitespace between "for" and "a" ("fora" instead of "for a"). This indicates a template concatenation bug around the personalization token, likely a missing space in the string template feeding the `<title>` element (the Subject line does not show the same defect, so the bug is localized to title construction).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be a relay capture gap rather than sender omission).
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click POST unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- **In-body UNSUBSCRIBE link returns 403** — this is the higher-severity finding: even if header-based unsubscribe exists, the visible CAN-SPAM-required unsubscribe mechanism in the email body is currently broken, which is a direct compliance risk regardless of header status.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from the available headers (relay-side capture gap noted by QA).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All primary CTAs route through the sender's click-tracking domain (`email-marriott.com/T/...`) rather than exposing direct destination/UTM parameters, so campaign-level UTM alignment cannot be verified from the HTML source alone — obscured by the redirector.
- The one link where the true destination is exposed (MY ACCOUNT → `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=...&ck=...&lk=...`) uses proprietary enrollment/click tracking params rather than standard UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) — consistent with Marriott's internal Epsilon/Harmony tracking rather than GA-style UTM tagging, but not itself an error.
- Given 6 of 12 probed links 403, landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed for the "find & reserve" or Bonvoy logo CTAs — this should be re-tested once the 403s are resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority fix:** Investigate the 403s on the click-tracking domain (`email-marriott.com/T/...`) — likely an expired/misconfigured redirect map entry (Responsys/Epsilon Harmony), affecting the primary CTA, logo link, and — critically — the unsubscribe link.
2. Fix the malformed relative-URL redirect target behind the "MY ACCOUNT" link so it resolves to an absolute URL.
3. Add `alt` text to the three benefit icon images (`more-points`, `room-upgrades`, `late-checkout`) for accessibility; add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Fix the missing-space template bug producing "fora" in the `<title>` tag.
5. Change the HTTP font source (`AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff`) to HTTPS to eliminate mixed-content risk.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (may just be an AgentMail relay capture gap) — verify with a direct SMTP header capture rather than relying on this pipeline alone.
7. Add a non-empty plain-text MIME part; current fallback is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability and violates general best practice (and is a CAN-SPAM/UX consideration for text-only clients).
