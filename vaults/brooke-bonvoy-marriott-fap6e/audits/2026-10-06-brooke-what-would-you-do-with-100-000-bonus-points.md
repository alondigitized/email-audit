The hero reads "Earn 100,000 Bonus Points" with the card art, a "$650 annual fee" line and an APPLY NOW button. That is a mild bait-and-switch of tone. It is solid execution of a generic acquisition template, with no seasonal or persona-specific angle.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "Earn 100,000 Bonus Points", is large and sits above the first APPLY NOW button.
- The points-math strip (current points + welcome offer = potential total) makes the value tangible.
- The benefits section has clear, scannable blocks with icons or imagery: elite status, dining credit, free night, night credits.
- The CTA is repeated mid-email and near the bottom, so there is no scrolling hunt.
- The Marriott Bonvoy branding is consistent and the layout renders cleanly, with no visible broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The subject frames a playful "what would you do" question, but the body is a credit card application with a $650 annual fee, a mismatch of expectation.
- No urgency or deadline is visible, so there is no reason to apply today.
- The legal and disclosure text takes up roughly the bottom half of the email. It buries terms and makes the offer feel less honest.
- The send is again a card-acquisition message from a brand whose recent history includes similar sends. The audience sees repetition, not relevance.
- No destination, hotel or aspirational travel imagery answers the subject's question about what you'd do with the points.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the creative answer the subject's question. Use a hero showing what 100K points buys (for example, "5 free nights at a Category 5 hotel") before the card art.
- 2. Disclose the annual fee and spend threshold in the first screen, so the fine print isn't doing the explaining.
- 3. Add a credible deadline for the offer, if one exists.
- 4. Cut or collapse the legal block behind a "terms" link, or move it to a compact footer.
- 5. Segment: suppress this card pitch to people who got a similar card email in the past week.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Brooke, What Would You Do With 100,000 Bonus Points?"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a conventional table-based email with web fonts and ESP click-wrapped links. It has one likely real defect (a host-less "MY ACCOUNT" link) and a missing plain-text part. The five 403s on tracked links, including UNSUBSCRIBE, are probably bot-blocking by the tracker rather than dead links, so they are unverified. Header-based compliance checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **MY ACCOUNT link (likely real defect).** The QA error was `unknown url type: '/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=11944740&lk=1000301947'`.
  - The destination is a path with no scheme or host. Either the source href was relative and the ESP wrapped it, or the tracker's redirect returns a relative `Location`.
  - A relative destination won't resolve reliably from an email client.
  - The truncated source can't show which case it is. Check the pre-wrap href in the template.
- **403 on 5 tracked links (unverified).** The affected links are `find & reserve` (×2), `Marriott Bonvoy` (×2) and `UNSUBSCRIBE`.
  - All are on `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a110cb2…/<link-id>/761c3a43-8ec6-47eb-b5d3-26a22a5569e6?__dU__=…&__F__=…`.
  - A uniform 403 across different link IDs on one tracking host points to bot or WAF filtering of the probe's user agent, not broken campaign links.
  - This does not show the links fail for real recipients. The QA's "23% pass rate" is therefore not a reliable measure of link health.
  - Re-probe with a browser-like UA, or follow the redirect chain manually, to confirm.
- **Destinations not verified.** Because the probes were blocked, the final landing URLs were never observed. 12 links were probed and 32 were skipped.
- **Duplicate CTAs.** `find & reserve` and `» find & reserve` use distinct link IDs (`…bcd0` and `…bccd`). This is fine for per-placement tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `@font-face` format hint.** Every `src` list includes `format('otf')`. The valid CSS token is `format('opentype')`, so browsers ignore those sources. The `.eot`/`.woff`/`.woff2`/`.ttf` fallbacks still work, so the impact is low.
- **Insecure font URLs.** `Aldine-Regular` and `Aldine-Regular-Italic` list `http://cache.marriott.com/…woff` as a fallback source. These can be blocked as mixed content or upgraded. The `.woff2` HTTPS source comes first, so the impact is low.
- **Missing alt text.** One image has no alt text: `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a110cb213cb242266e96638918/761c3a438ec647eb00004c5a42963aa1`.
  - The `/O/` path pattern, as opposed to `/T/` for clicks, suggests this is the open-tracking pixel. If so, it is low severity.
  - Add `alt=""` anyway.
- **Positives.** The source has a `<title>`, `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"`. It also sets viewport, `X-UA-Compatible`, `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tags, and has MSO conditional comments.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML was truncated, so I could not check body-level issues such as image dimensions, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or text sizes.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. "Brooke" is resolved in both the `<title>` and the subject. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The link parameters (`nck`, `ck`, `lk`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports `[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing or too short: Text version is 0 chars`. A missing text/plain part hurts deliverability and accessibility.
  - It could be an artifact of the AgentMail capture, but treat it as real until a raw message shows otherwise.
- **List-Unsubscribe headers not captured.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - QA notes the relay may strip them, so compliance is unconfirmed rather than failing.
  - Marriott's `email-marriott.com` sending domain makes it likely they are present. Verify against the original raw message.
- **Authentication unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link.** An `UNSUBSCRIBE` link is present. Its 403 is unverified (see §2).
- **Physical address.** It is not visible in the truncated source, so I could not verify it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible.** The only visible parameters are ESP tracker tokens (`__dU__`, `__F__`) and Marriott loyalty identifiers (`enrollmentSourceCode=INCK`, `nck`, `ck`, `lk`).
- **Final URLs not observed.** Every probed link resolves through `email-marriott.com/T/…`, and all but one were blocked, so I couldn't confirm UTM propagation or landing-page alignment with the "100,000 Bonus Points" offer.
- **No evidence of a mismatch.**

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the MY ACCOUNT href.** Make it an absolute `https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?…` URL.
2. **Re-run link QA with a browser-like UA or a manual redirect follow.** This should clear the 403 false positives and let you check the final landing URLs, UTMs and offer alignment. Confirm in particular that UNSUBSCRIBE resolves.
3. **Add a text/plain part.** Confirm against the raw MIME whether it exists and was only dropped by the relay.
4. **Check the raw headers for compliance.** Look for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment).
5. **Clean up the CSS.** Replace `format('otf')` with `format('opentype')` and switch the `http://cache.marriott.com` font URLs to HTTPS.
6. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel** (or give meaningful alt text if it is a visible image).
