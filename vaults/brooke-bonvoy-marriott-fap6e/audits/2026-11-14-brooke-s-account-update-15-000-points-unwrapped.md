# Brooke's Account Update: 15,000+ Points, Unwrapped
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "15,000+ Points, Unwrapped." The body doesn't deliver it. The account header shows 0 points, 0 nights this year, and Member status. A subject built on a points total sits on top of a zero balance, and "Brooke's Account Update" implies news about the account. What follows is a generic seasonal promo roundup. A subscriber would feel misled.
- The render also has a serious problem. Nearly every image slot is a blank box with a small placeholder icon, including the hero and all the offer cards. The email is mostly white space and text. The strongest copy is the hero offer, "earn 5,000 bonus points" on 3+ night stays, with a clear "BOOK NOW" button. The page then splits into about eight competing offers: Cyber Week, holidays, gift a holiday, 27,000 ways to welcome 2027, Super Bowl, barware, and JW Marriott Costa Rica. Nothing leads.
- Judgement: weak. Personalization is real, with the member name and a member bar, but the offer is muddled and the images fail. It is a step down from the sharp 11/05 "Seven Days Only. 200K Bonus Points." send (8/10). Its density is closer to the 11/06 Phoenician send (6/10).

## What's working

- The personalized header shows the member name, "Member" status, points, nights and an "as of 11/12/2026" stamp. It signals a real account-aware send.
- The hero has a concrete offer, "earn 5,000 bonus points for each day you trade cool winter settings for warm, sun-soaked oases," with a clear black "BOOK NOW" button.
- The black Cyber Week banner stands out against the white page and breaks up the scroll.
- The section headers ("Seasonal Offers Worth Celebrating," "Membership Makes the Season Special") give the long scroll some structure.
- The footer is complete, with a brand portfolio grid and unsubscribe and preferences links.

## What's weak

- Almost every image is a blank box with a small placeholder icon, including the hero and all the module cards. Large empty rectangles dominate the email.
- The subject's "15,000+ Points" doesn't appear anywhere visible in the body. The header shows 0 points, which contradicts it.
- There are far too many competing offers: 5,000 points, up to 20%, up to 35%, 27,000 points, Cyber Week, Super Bowl and barware. No single hero wins.
- The Cyber Week banner has no real offer or CTA button, just "Make sure to download the Marriott Bonvoy app."
- The Super Bowl sweepstakes fine print takes up about a screen of footer text. The email is very long, and the CTAs are small text links in orange.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the imagery first, with proper hosted images and alt text. A text-only render of this length is unacceptable.
- 2. Make the subject match the body. Either surface the "15,000+ points" figure in the hero, or drop the claim.
- 3. Cut the email to one hero and two supporting offers. Move the Super Bowl sweepstakes and barware to a separate send.
- 4. Turn the Cyber Week banner into a real offer with a button.
- 5. Personalize the offers to the member's zero-night status, for example "Earn your first 5,000 points."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (lang, charset, viewport, MSO conditionals). However, the email ships with no plain-text part, and the single probed link that failed for a non-bot reason is a relative destination path. Eight probe failures against the ESP click tracker are mostly bot-blocking, so they need a manual check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative destination (likely real):** The "Book a stay with Homes and Villas by Marriott Bonvoy." link failed with `unknown url type: '/en/offers/GLB27KNOV26-Earn-27,000-Bonus-Points'`. The redirect target is a path with no scheme or host. Either the source href was authored as a relative path, or the tracker returns a relative `Location`. A client that doesn't resolve it would fail.
- **Link text and target mismatch:** The same link's text is "Book a stay with Homes and Villas…", but its target slug is `GLB27KNOV26-Earn-27,000-Bonus-Points`. It points to a generic bonus-points offer, not a Homes & Villas page. The slug also contains a literal comma.
- **403s on `email-marriott.com/T/...` (probably bot-blocking):** Eight links returned 403: "find & reserve" (×2), "BOOK NOW", the two Caribbean hero image links, "Get Away Now", the "Marriott Bonvoy" logo and UNSUBSCRIBE. All share the `/T/v71000001a1d7b3da65c30ce5f4bbcfb648/…` redirector, and the final destinations were never reached.
  - A uniform 403 across every tracked link, including unsubscribe, points to the click tracker rejecting a non-browser client. It does not show that recipients are broken.
  - This needs a browser-based or seed-inbox click test before it is called a defect.
- **Duplicate tracked targets:** The two "find & reserve" links share an identical `__F__` prefix. The two Caribbean image links use different link IDs (`…bcd8` and `…bcda`), so per-placement click attribution looks intact.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 55 links were probed (43 skipped), so the pass rate of 19% is not representative of all links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** `turtlebay1.gif`, `festive.gif` and `spirited.gif` (`https://media.4at5.net/email_domains/mar/24086/images/…`) have no alt. These look like content images, so their message is lost with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://sli.marriott.com/imp?s=…` (about 11 beacons) and the open pixel `email-marriott.com/O/…/584a557ee4de44e200004c5a42963aa1` have no alt. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly, which is low severity.
- **Zoom blocked:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` disables iOS text resizing. The `.body-fix` rule later sets `100%`, but the global `none` rule still applies elsewhere.
- **Non-HTTPS font URLs:** The `@font-face` fallbacks use `http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/.../AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` and `...-Italic.woff`. These are mixed content in some clients and will be blocked, although font fallbacks are defined.
- **Namespace typo (harmless):** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` should be `http://`. It has no rendering impact.
- **External stylesheet:** `<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://use.typekit.net/yft3kyd.css">` is stripped by most clients, but the Aldine/Swiss stacks have `Times New Roman` and Arial fallbacks.
