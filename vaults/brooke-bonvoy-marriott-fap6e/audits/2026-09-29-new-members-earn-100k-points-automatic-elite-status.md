# New Members: Earn 100K Points + Automatic Elite Status
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a Boundless credit card acquisition email dressed as a welcome message. The eyebrow reads "You're Officially In," and the body opens with "Brooke, welcome to Marriott Bonvoy!" But the only ask is "APPLY NOW" for a card with a $95 annual fee. It gets the offer up top: 100,000 bonus points plus $100 in airline credits, with a card image below. The 0 + 100,000 = 100,000 points math graphic is the best part of the email.
- The subject promises "Automatic Elite Status," and the email delivers that only as Silver status, buried in the benefits grid. It also needs $3,000 spend in 3 months, and both are asterisked. The 100K headline is real, but the fine print and fee sit close to the CTA. The email is clean and well structured, but generic.
- For a subscriber who has just seen a run of points and travel sends, this is another card pitch. The 9/26 Amex Business "Last Chance" email was the same category. The "welcome" framing risks feeling misleading if Brooke is already a member.

## What's working

- The 100,000-point headline with the $100 airline credit sub-head is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The points math graphic (0 + 100,000 = 100,000) makes the value concrete and personalized to Brooke's balance.
- The $95 annual fee is shown right next to the CTA, so it doesn't feel hidden.
- The six-icon benefits grid (17X, 3X, 2X, Free Night, Silver, 15 Elite Night Credits) is easy to scan.
- The orange "APPLY NOW" buttons are unmistakable and repeat three times.

## What's weak

- The "Automatic Elite Status" in the subject is only Silver, the lowest tier. It is an overpromise relative to the subject.
- The $3,000-in-3-months spend requirement sits in small bullet text, not in the hero.
- The "New Member Offer" and "You're Officially In" framing contradicts the card-application ask.
- The three near-identical APPLY NOW blocks and the repeated 100,000 headline make the email long and redundant.
- The Bold card (45K, no fee) is a weak, easily missed fallback. The featured W Costa Rica property and the brand-logo grid are off-topic filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero honest about the offer: it's a card, with Silver status. That would give more credible clicks and fewer disappointed ones.
- 2. Cut the second "Earn 100,000" block and one APPLY NOW, and move the spend requirement into the hero.
- 3. Drop the "You're Officially In" welcome language unless it's actually a new-member send.
- 4. Give the no-fee Bold card real space, since it may convert people put off by the $95 fee.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA pass rate is 23%. Five of the 12 probed links failed, but four of those are HTTP 403s from the ESP click-tracker, which probably reflects bot-blocking of the probe rather than a user-facing break. The confirmed defects are a relative-path redirect target on the "MY ACCOUNT" link and an empty plain-text part (0 chars).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect target (confirmed):** the "MY ACCOUNT" tracked link (`email-marriott.com/T/.../aa69579a6dd247b50000021ef3a0bcc8/...`) resolved to `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=11819316&lk=1000301947`. The probe raised `unknown url type`, so the redirect appears to return a scheme-less, host-less path. Browsers resolve that against `email-marriott.com`, which is the tracking domain and not `marriott.com`. It may 404 there, and it needs a manual click test.
- **403 on tracker links (unconfirmed):** "find & reserve" (`...bcc9`), "» find & reserve" (`...bcc6`), both "Marriott Bonvoy" logo links (`...bcc4`, `...bcc7`) and "UNSUBSCRIBE" (`...bce1`) all returned 403 at `email-marriott.com/T/...`. A uniform 403 on every tracker URL points to bot or user-agent filtering, not per-link breakage. These links are unverified, not proven broken.
- **Tracking URL length:** the `__F__=` payloads run to several hundred characters. This is normal for this ESP, but it raises the risk of truncation or wrapping in plain-text or quoted-printable rendering.
- **Probe coverage:** 26 of 38 links were skipped, so footer and social links are unaudited.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the image `aa69579a6dd247b500004c5a42963aa1` (`https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a0ecba819fb861626e966a3578/...`) has no `alt`. The `/O/` path is the tracker's open-pixel pattern, so this is likely a 1x1 pixel. It still needs `alt=""` to be explicit.
- **Insecure font URLs:** `@font-face` for `Aldine-Regular` and `Aldine-Regular-Italic` lists `http://cache.marriott.com/aka-fonts/MarriottDigital/AldineforMarriott-Roman.woff` and `-Italic.woff`. Those are non-HTTPS fallbacks, and clients may block them as mixed content.
- **Invalid font format hint:** `format('otf')` on the Aldine-Light, Swiss-Light and Swiss-Regular sources is not a valid CSS format string. It should be `format('opentype')`, and browsers will skip those sources. The `woff2` and `woff` fallbacks still work.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML is cut off after the head, so body-level checks (table roles, heading semantics, VML buttons, dark-mode support) could not be done. The head does include `lang`, `xml:lang`, `<title>`, the viewport meta and the Outlook DPI fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or in the subject line. The `nck`, `ck` and `lk` values on the MY ACCOUNT link are resolved numeric IDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (confirmed):** the QA check reports the text version at 0 chars. A missing text part is a deliverability and accessibility gap, and it can raise spam scoring.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post absent:** the QA check notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are truly absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC unknown:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not captured, so it cannot be assessed. The sender is `marriottbonvoy@email-marriott.com`, and the tracking domain matches it (`email-marriott.com`), which is good for alignment.
- **Body unsubscribe link:** it exists (`UNSUBSCRIBE`, `...bce1`) but returned 403 to the probe, so it is unverified. The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible on the one resolved destination. Marriott's own `ck`, `nck`, `lk` and `enrollmentSourceCode=INCK` parameters serve as its tracking scheme, so this is not necessarily a defect.
- Landing pages for the primary CTAs ("find & reserve") could not be resolved because of the 403s. Alignment with the subject's "100K Points + Automatic Elite Status" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text alternative that carries the offer, the CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link.
2. Fix the tracker redirect for `...bcc8`. It should emit an absolute `https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?...` Location.
3. Re-probe the tracker URLs with a browser-like user-agent, or click-test them manually, to separate probe blocking from real failures. Verify the unsubscribe link first.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message. Capture `Authentication-Results` from a direct inbox copy instead of the relay.
5. Switch the `http://cache.marriott.com/...` font URLs to HTTPS and replace `format('otf')` with `format('opentype')`.
6. Add `alt=""` to the tracking-pixel image.
7. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML so the body markup and the footer address can be checked.
