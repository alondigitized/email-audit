# More Portland. Less Ordinary.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Moxy Hotels — another Marriott brand debut in this thread, following W Hotels on 9/4 — running a single-property push for Moxy Portland Downtown built around a "welcome cocktails + late checkout" package. It's a lifestyle-brand play (cocktail-hand photo, bold nightlife imagery, playful all-caps headline copy) rather than a points or card pitch, and it's clean and on-brand for Moxy's "bold design, social spaces" identity. There's a real, specific offer (two welcome cocktails + 2 p.m. late checkout, plus a five-night-stay discount tease), two distinct CTAs, and no render bugs.
- Where it falls down for this persona: there's zero Bonvoy point/status mention despite Brooke clearly being an active, engaged Bonvoy member (see 9/1, 9/15 sends), no price or percentage-off number anywhere, and the whole email banks on Portland as a destination Brooke has to already want to visit — there's no reason-to-travel hook. It's a nice-looking single-city, single-property ad with no loyalty layer and no urgency, so it reads as generic top-of-funnel content rather than a targeted push.

## What's working

- Hero image is vivid and on-brand — cocktails + phone + neon-lit street signals nightlife/social energy consistent with Moxy's identity.
- Two distinct, high-contrast magenta CTAs ("BOOK YOUR ESCAPE," "EXTEND THE FUN") are easy to spot against black/white sections.
- Concrete, tangible perks are named: two welcome cocktails and 2 p.m. late checkout, not vague "special offer" language.
- Clean visual hierarchy — hero photo, bold headline, short body copy, CTA, then a secondary dark-block upsell module.
- Second module ("Stay Longer, Play Longer") smartly cross-sells a longer stay with a skyline visual of the actual destination.

## What's weak

- No Bonvoy points, elite status, or member pricing anywhere — a jarring omission given Brooke's active Bonvoy engagement in every prior send this month.
- No discount number or price point — "exclusive savings" is claimed in the preheader but never quantified in the body.
- No urgency or deadline — nothing tells Brooke why she should book Portland now versus in three months.
- Portland is presented with zero reason-to-go — no event, season, or occasion tying the destination to this moment (no fall foliage, festival, etc.).
- The brand directory footer (all Marriott brand logos) adds visual clutter/length without helping this specific CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a quantified, Bonvoy-tied offer — e.g., "20% off + double points" — so it isn't just a generic amenity package; this is the single biggest lift available given how points-driven Brooke's engagement has been all month.
- 2. Give a reason to act now: name a date range or seasonal hook for Portland (e.g., "Fall in Portland: book by Oct 15").
- 3. Trim or move the Marriott brand-family directory below the unsubscribe block — it dilutes the single-property message.
- 4. Add one line of social proof or a review snippet under the hero copy to build trust in the specific property.
- 5. Tighten the preheader so it's not generic marketing-speak — pair it with something concrete from the body (welcome cocktails, late checkout).

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails the majority of automated checks: all major CTA links return 403 through the click-tracking redirector, the Marriott Bonvoy footer link resolves to an invalid relative URL, and the plain-text fallback is essentially empty.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed CTA links route through `email-marriott.com/T/...` (Epsilon Harmony click-tracking) and return **403** on probe, including "Find & Reserve," "Discover Portland Like a Local," "BOOK YOUR ESCAPE," "FIND HOTELS," both hero images, and "UNSUBSCRIBE." 403 on a tracking redirector typically indicates an expired/invalid tracking token or a probe being blocked (e.g., bot/UA filtering) — cannot confirm from this data alone which.
- Two "Marriott Bonvoy" links decode to `unknown url type: '/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?lk=1000421047&nck=1028103536&ck=11629432'` — the destination is a protocol-relative/relative path missing a scheme+host, which the tracking redirect wrapper failed to resolve to an absolute URL.
- 9 of 21 total material links were deprioritized/skipped per the QA tool's click-likelihood ranking (footer/social links); this is expected tool behavior, not a defect.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Three images are missing `alt` text:
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images?id=3ffa0fe3-91fc-4ab7-b4a8-73324ee3cdb0`
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images?id=3a16c37a-6415-41c4-b6be-372258a2e801`
  - `https://email-marriott.com/O/v61000001a0b303b67f9fb2d7f4bbc780a0/17cf3b18fd21468700004c5a42963aa1` (open-tracking pixel — expected to lack alt, low severity)
- Two of the three affected images are content images used as link anchors ("[image: images]" link text in the QA report), so missing alt text also degrades link accessibility for screen readers, not just image rendering.
- Heavy reliance on custom `@font-face` web fonts (Aldine, Swiss, Janna, GillSansMTStd) with EOT/TTF/WOFF/WOFF2 sources hosted on `cache.marriott.com`; no evidence of a fallback stack failure, but this is a common source of unstyled/fallback-font rendering in clients that block remote font loading (e.g., Outlook desktop, some webmail).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge-token or personalization artifacts (e.g., unresolved `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or blank name fields) are visible in the truncated source provided.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present in the markup but returns **403** on probe (see Section 2) — if this reproduces for real recipients, it is a CAN-SPAM functional-unsubscribe violation, not just a QA artifact.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed; QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a sender-side omission.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found, consistent with the above — cannot confirm RFC 8058 one-click support.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified from this data; flagged as relay-capture limitation, not a confirmed auth failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible on any link; all outbound links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony click-tracking (`email-marriott.com/T/...`) with opaque `__dU__`/`__F__` tokens instead, so campaign attribution on the landing side depends entirely on the tracking platform's redirect resolution — which is currently failing (Section 2).
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment (destination content/brand match) since all destination URLs are masked by the broken tracking redirect and none resolved successfully during probing.

7. Recommendations
- Investigate the 403s on the `email-marriott.com/T/...` redirector as the top priority — this affects every CTA and the unsubscribe link; confirm whether it's an expired tracking token, geo/UA blocking, or an actual downstream outage.
- Fix the malformed Bonvoy destination (`/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?...`) so it resolves to an absolute URL before being wrapped in the tracking redirect.
- Add `alt` attributes to the three flagged images, prioritizing the two used as clickable link anchors.
- Populate a real plain-text MIME part (currently ~34 characters) to satisfy multipart/alternative best practices and reduce spam-filter risk.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) are actually present at the SMTP layer — re-test outside the AgentMail relay if header capture is suspected to be the limiting factor.
