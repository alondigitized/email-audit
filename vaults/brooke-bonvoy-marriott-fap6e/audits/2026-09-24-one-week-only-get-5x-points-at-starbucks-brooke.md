# One Week Only: Get 5X Points at Starbucks, Brooke
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Marriott Bonvoy's Starbucks crossover promo — link your Bonvoy and Starbucks Rewards accounts during "Marriott Bonvoy Week" (Sept 21–27) and earn 500 points after three qualifying Starbucks purchases. It's a clean, single-offer send: one hero banner, one CTA, one clear mechanic, unlike the scattered multi-property pushes this thread has run before (W Hotels 9/4, Moxy Portland 9/18).
- The lifestyle collage up top is pleasant but generic — nothing in it visually signals "Starbucks" until you scroll to the dual-logo lockup, which is actually the strongest moment in the email. The offer itself is honest and well-explained (three purchases, $2 minimum, one week only), but it's a modest reward (500 points, roughly $2-3 in redemption value) dressed up with "5X" framing that doesn't fully deliver — the subject leads with "5X Points" but the body quickly reframes to a flat 500-point cap, which undersells the multiplier promise.
- Solid, on-brand, low-friction send. Not a must-click, but well-targeted to an engaged Bonvoy member and free of the brand-dilution problem that hurt the W Hotels and Moxy sends.

## What's working

- Dual-logo Bonvoy + Starbucks lockup makes the partnership instantly legible.
- Single, unambiguous CTA ("LINK NOW") in a clear black button with good contrast.
- Benefits are broken into three clean icon tiles (access, double stars, point transfer) that are easy to scan.
- Fine print is present but not intrusive — full T&Cs are pushed below the fold rather than cluttering the hero.
- "Upcoming Marriott Bonvoy Weeks" section sets expectation for cadence, which is a nice transparency touch.

## What's weak

- Hero collage has zero Starbucks visual cue (no cup, no green, no coffee context) — feels like a stock lifestyle shoot repurposed from another campaign.
- Headline says "500 Points" while the subject promises "5X Points" — these are two different framings of the same offer and create a slight mismatch between inbox promise and inbox delivery.
- The actual point value (500 points ≈ low-value redemption) is underwhelming next to bold "5X" language — borderline over-promise.
- Second CTA ("LEARN MORE") competes with the primary "LINK NOW" button and dilutes the single-action design.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rework the hero image to include a visible Starbucks cup/branding moment — right now the visual gives no hint this is a coffee partnership until 400px down.
- 2. Align headline and subject on the same framing — either lead with "5X" everywhere or "500 points" everywhere, not both.
- 3. Cut the "LEARN MORE" button in the benefits section; one CTA per email keeps focus on "LINK NOW."
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit "ends Sept 27" date near the hero CTA — the urgency currently only lives in the subject line, not the body.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Link your accounts before Sept 27 to start earning.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing/tracking-redirect layer is broken for most CTAs (multiple 403s and malformed URLs), and the message lacks required deliverability/compliance signals (plain-text part, List-Unsubscribe headers).

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 tracked links return HTTP 403 when probed: "find & reserve" (×2), "Marriott Bonvoy" header logo (×2 instances, redirect IDs `...bcc2` and `...bcc5`), and the "UNSUBSCRIBE" link (redirect ID `...bcd3`). All route through the `email-marriott.com/T/...` click-tracking redirector before failing.
- 2 links produce "unknown url type" errors because the tracking wrapper's destination parameter is a relative path rather than an absolute URL: `/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=11734368&lk=1000301947` ("MY ACCOUNT") and `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=11734368&lk=1000421047` ("Marriott Bonvoy" footer link, appears twice: `...bcce`, `...bccf`). These will fail to resolve for any client that doesn't prepend a base domain.
- Net: 9 of the 12 probed material links fail (75% failure rate on primary CTAs), including the unsubscribe link itself.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 images missing `alt` text: the header/logo image (`...4c5a42963aa1`), `ICON-TICKETS.png`, `ICON-STARS.png`, `ICON-TRANSFER.png`, and `ICON.png` (all served from `images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../SOLOS/CS0668698/`). Screen readers will announce nothing for these elements.
- No other structural rendering issues surfaced in the QA pass (MSO/Outlook conditional comments and mobile media queries are present as expected for this template).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Subject line and preheader personalize correctly ("...Brooke") — no unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIRSTNAME%%`) observed in the truncated source or subject.
- No issues found in the visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present in-body but returns a 403 when probed — this is a functional compliance failure, not just a UX bug, since a broken unsubscribe mechanism does not satisfy CAN-SPAM's functioning opt-out requirement.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) is not exposed at the header level; may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence, but cannot be confirmed either way from available data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found — same caveat; RFC 8058 one-click support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All CTAs route through a Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style click-tracking redirector (`email-marriott.com/T/...`) rather than exposing direct UTM-tagged destination URLs, so UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the encoded `__dU__`/`__F__` payloads alone.
- The two "unknown url type" failures (Section 2) confirm at least two destinations are malformed relative paths (`/loyalty/myAccount/...`, `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi`), meaning even if the redirector resolved, the final hop is misconfigured and would not land on a valid page.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the click-tracking redirect service returning 403s for the primary CTA, brand-logo, and unsubscribe links before send — this blocks conversion and unsubscribe compliance.
- Correct the two relative-path destinations (`/loyalty/myAccount/default.mi...`, `/marriott-brands/explore-our-brands.mi...`) to fully-qualified absolute URLs.
- Add `alt` attributes to the 5 flagged images (header image + 4 ICON-*.png assets).
- Include a non-empty plain-text MIME part (currently 0 chars) for text-only clients and spam-filter scoring.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the ESP (confirm independently of the AgentMail relay capture, since the relay may simply be stripping them).
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `email-marriott.com` via a direct header check, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
