# Last Chance to Earn 200,000 Bonus Points! ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud last-call send for the Marriott Bonvoy Boundless Visa. The black hero carries "LAST CHANCE 100,000 BONUS POINTS + $100 in Airline Credits" and the deadline is clear: "ENDS 11/11". The card art sits right under it, and the eye lands on the offer first. It follows the 11/05 "Seven Days Only. 200K Bonus Points." send (8/10), which used the same creative with a countdown.
- The offer math is muddled. The subject says "200,000 Bonus Points" and the hero says "100,000 BONUS POINTS". The body then explains that 200K is the 100K bonus plus further points earned from $3K of spend, and the "0 + 200,000 = 200,000" tally reinforces that. A reader scanning the hero will see 100K, then see 200K in the body copy and the subject. The tally graphic is a good idea, but the hero should state the number the subject promises.
- There is also a render bug: a small broken-image icon sits above the black banner text. Overall it's a competent, repetitive card-acquisition send that loses a point or two to a mismatched headline and the broken image.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is strong. "LAST CHANCE" and the huge "100,000" give the eye one place to land.
- The deadline is credible and repeated: "OUR BIGGEST BOUNDLESS POINTS OFFER ENDS 11/11" in the banner, and again in the body.
- The two identical orange APPLY NOW buttons, one above and one below the offer details, are unambiguous. The $95 annual fee is disclosed right under each.
- The "0 + 200,000 = 200,000 Total Points" tally makes the value concrete and personalized with an "As of 11/9/2026" date.
- Body copy addresses the reader by name ("Brooke") and explains the spend requirement ($3K) in plain bullets.

## What's weak

- The hero says 100,000 while the subject and body say 200,000. That inconsistency undermines trust.
- A broken-image icon appears in the black banner above the "OUR BIGGEST BOUNDLESS POINTS OFFER ENDS 11/11" line.
- The $3K spend requirement and the "up to $100" airline credit conditions are only in the body bullets and fine print. The hero's "+ $100 in Airline Credits" reads as unconditional.
- The brand-logo grid and the long legal footer take up about half the email and add nothing for a card-acquisition send.
- This is the second near-identical send in a week (11/05 and 11/10), so the urgency feels manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Show "200,000 BONUS POINTS" with "after $3K spend in 3 months" as a subhead, or change the subject to 100K. One number should appear everywhere.
- 2. Fix or remove the broken image in the banner.
- 3. Put the key condition ("after $3K in purchases") in the hero and the first button's sub-line so the offer feels honest.
- 4. Trim the brand grid and compress the footer. The CTA already appears twice, so the email can end sooner.
- 5. Add one concrete redemption example, such as "200K = about 4 free nights at a Category 5 hotel", to lift click intent.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tomorrow: 200K Bonvoy points + $100 credit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Brooke, your 200K points offer expires 11/11`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 200K after $3K in purchases. $95 annual fee. Apply by 11/11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The biggest Boundless offer of 2026 closes tomorrow. Here's how the points add up.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from an Epsilon-tracked template (email-marriott.com redirect wrapper), but 11 of 12 probed links fail. Each destination URL has literal whitespace in its query string, and the message has no plain-text part. Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed destination URLs (high).** All 11 probed material links, including UNSUBSCRIBE, fail with "URL can't contain control characters". Each decoded destination has two literal spaces after the `ck` value:
  - `/default.mi?enrollmentSourceCode=INCK&nck=1028103536&ck=12574296  &lk=1000301966` ("find & reserve")
  - `/rewards/myAccount/default.mi?...ck=12574296  &lk=1000301947` ("MY ACCOUNT")
  - `/profile/email/unsubscribe.mi?...ck=12574296  &lk=1000301969` ("UNSUBSCRIBE")
  - `/a1/marriottbonvoyboundless/200K/AEL/offerdetails?nck=1028103536&ck=12574296  &lk=2000535943` ("See offer details")
  - `/marriott-brands.mi?...&lk=1000421047` (2 "Marriott Bonvoy" links)
  - `/web/oao/application/card?sourceCode=J86K&action=guest&mcd=CCOFFER-US-CH-SOLO_ACQ-0513-ACQ_PT200k-6XFK-J86K&alt...` (3 "APPLY NOW" and hero-image links)
  
  The pattern looks like a merge field next to `ck=` that resolved to padding or an empty value. Browsers may percent-encode the spaces, but strict clients and link scanners reject them. The probe error may be stricter than real-world browser behavior, so this is a confirmed malformation with an unconfirmed user-facing impact.
- **Redirect chain.** Every link goes through `https://email-marriott.com/T/v71000001a1c5bfe790b30c0f6e96c65848/<linkid>/6c9d9619-060a-4d30-b46f-bb439616514c?__dU__=...&__F__=...`. The `__F__` payload is about 500–900 characters per URL. That is long but within normal limits.
- **Duplicate links.** The "APPLY NOW" CTA and the hero image link point to the same card-application target with different link IDs (`...bccb`, `...bcce`, `...bcca`). This is fine for per-placement tracking.
- **Coverage gap.** 19 links were skipped by the prober, so footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - `https://live.harmony.epsilon.com/live/wi/countdownTimer/cc102370-dcbf-11f1-9e53-5f75b00963ef/o`: a live countdown image with no alt, so screen-reader and images-off users get no deadline information.
  - `https://email-marriott.com/O/v71000001a1c5bfe790b30c0f6e96c65848/6c9d9619060a4d3000004c5a42963aa1`: this looks like the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Font declarations.**
  - `@font-face` for `Aldine-Regular` and `Aldine-Regular-Italic` load `.woff` from `http://cache.marriott.com/...`, which is non-HTTPS and can be blocked as mixed content.
  - `format('otf')` is not a valid format hint (it should be `opentype`), so those sources are ignored.
  - Fallbacks (`'Times New Roman'`, Arial) are present, so the impact is minor.
- **Outlook.** The source includes `o:PixelsPerInch` DPI handling and MSO conditionals, so there is no issue in the visible portion. The HTML was truncated, so I did not audit the rest.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The double-space after `ck=12574296` in every tracked URL suggests a trailing merge value (or empty field) inside the link template. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- The visible source shows no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens. Otherwise, no issues found.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text fallback missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". If this is not a relay-capture artifact, it is a deliverability and accessibility defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unverified rather than confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this data. The envelope domain `email-marriott.com` aligns with the visible From domain, which is a positive sign only.
- **Physical address not detected.** The HTML was truncated, so the footer may contain one. Confirm in the full source.
- **In-body unsubscribe link is malformed.** It is the same whitespace defect as above, on `/profile/email/unsubscribe.mi?...&lk=1000301969`. A broken or fragile opt-out path is a CAN-SPAM risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters.** None of the final URLs carry `utm_*`. Attribution relies on proprietary parameters (`nck`, `ck`, `lk`, `sourceCode=J86K`, `mcd=CCOFFER-...`). This is consistent but not analytics-standard.
- **Landing alignment can't be verified.** The probe failed before reaching any final page, so the offer, "200,000 points" and "$100 airline credits" copy can't be checked against the card-application page.
- **Credit-card links share one domain path.** The CTAs go to `/web/oao/application/card?sourceCode=J86K`, but the host is not visible in the truncated QA string.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the template merge field that emits whitespace after `ck=12574296`, and trim or URL-encode it (`%20`) before the redirect wrapper stores the destination. Re-probe all links afterward.
2. Prioritize the unsubscribe URL as the first fix.
3. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
4. Verify the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. Also confirm the footer postal address in the full HTML.
5. Add descriptive alt text to the countdown image (e.g. the offer deadline) and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Switch the font URLs to HTTPS, and use `format('opentype')` or remove the `otf` entries.
7. Consider adding `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` for cross-platform analytics.
