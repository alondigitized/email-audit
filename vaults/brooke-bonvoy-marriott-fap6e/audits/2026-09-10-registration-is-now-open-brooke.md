# Registration Is Now Open, Brooke!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a bonus-points registration promo — register, then earn 1,500 bonus points on stays of 2+ nights anywhere, plus 3,000 more at resorts, valid September 22 through November 22. It's a clean, on-brand build with aerial overwater-bungalow photography that immediately signals "resort/luxury," and the CTA is repeated and unambiguous ("Register Now"). But the offer itself is a soft ask — no discount, no dollar value attached to the points, just an abstract number that most subscribers can't translate into value without doing math.
- Compared to the brand history in this thread, this lands closer to the 28/8/29 points-bonus send (7/10) than to the higher-scoring Labor Day retail push or the welcome-offer email (8/10) — it's a legitimate loyalty mechanic, just less immediately compelling than a percentage-off or dollar-amount hook. It's competent, on-brand, and visually strong, but the value prop requires the reader to already know what Bonvoy points are worth.
- Judgment: solid mid-tier send — good craft, unexciting hook.

## What's working

- The hero aerial shot of overwater bungalows is striking and immediately establishes a "resort escape" mood.
- "Register Now" CTA is a black pill button, repeated twice, high-contrast against cream background.
- Clear registration window given (September 22 – November 22) with concrete deadline framing.
- Secondary sections (Explore Resorts, Download App, Featured Property) add structured cross-sell without cluttering the hero.
- Brand tier chart at the bottom (Marriott Bonvoy portfolio logos) reinforces the ecosystem/loyalty value for members.

## What's weak

- Headline offer ("1,500 / 3,000 bonus points") isn't converted to a dollar equivalent, so the actual value is opaque to most readers.
- No personalization signal visible anywhere in the render (no "Brooke," no member tier, no points balance) despite the subject line naming her.
- No urgency device beyond the small body-copy date range — nothing time-pressured near the hero or CTA.
- Large amount of dead white space between the hero image and the body copy block, pushing the second CTA further down than needed.
- "Featured property" (Le Meressine Zanzibar) is disconnected from the main offer — it's not clear if a stay there qualifies for the resort bonus tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Translate the points into a tangible value near the headline (e.g., "1,500 points ≈ a free night" or "worth up to $X") — this is the single biggest lift since the offer as written requires math most readers won't do.
- 2. Add the recipient's name or tier status near the hero to justify the personalized subject line — right now the email reads as a mass send despite "Brooke" in the subject.
- 3. Tighten the space between the hero image and the body copy so the second CTA appears sooner on scroll.
- 4. Add a countdown or "ends [date]" badge near the top CTA instead of burying the date range in body copy.
- 5. Connect the Featured Property call-out explicitly to the bonus tiers (e.g., "This resort qualifies for the 3,000-point bonus").
