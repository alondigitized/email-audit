# Homepage snapshot · www.marriott.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a brand that knows exactly who's landing on it before sign-in — a points-first, status-first traveler, not a bargain hunter. The hero isn't chasing me with a room-rate discount, it's dangling 5,000 bonus points on European stays, which is exactly the currency I actually care about. Right now they're pushing a member-exclusive points bonus and a Boundless Visa "4 Free Nights" pitch — classic Bonvoy playbook.

## What's working

- **"Member Exclusive Offer" tag on the hero** — instantly signals this isn't for randoms, which is the whole appeal of chasing Titanium.
- **The Bonvoy Boundless Visa strip** ("Earn 4 Free Nights + $100 in Airline Credits") — this is squarely in my wheelhouse; I already stack co-brand card nights with cash bookings.
- **"Ask Bonvoy" AI search teaser** — as someone who's tech-comfortable and lives inside the app, I'm curious what an AI concierge does with my status/history once I sign in.
- **Autograph Collection property tagged on the hero image** ("Domes Novos Santorini") — soft brands are where I burn points for the aspirational stays, so surfacing one by name (not just "Resorts") is a smart touch.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 5,000 Bonus Points" is vague** — no minimum stay length, no qualifying dates, no cap details visible. A points hoarder like me reads "up to" as "probably won't hit that number."
- **No visible points balance or status tier** — since I'm not shown as signed in, there's no personalization cue (my Titanium status, my nearest free-night cert threshold, anything I've already earned). Feels generic until I log in.
- **Zero human presence in the hero** — an empty infinity pool at sunset is pretty, but there's no lifestyle cue telling me who this trip is "for."
- **The date search bar defaults to a 1-night stay** — most of my bookings are multi-night redemption stretches; a 1-night default undersells what the site can actually do for me.

## Recommendations

- Swap "Up to 5,000 Bonus Points" for a concrete, unconditional number or add a one-line qualifier right under the headline ("on stays of 2+ nights, valid through 9/30") so it doesn't read as bait.
- Surface a points balance or "X nights to your next Free Night Award" ticker in the hero band once a session/cookie shows a returning Bonvoy member — even pre-login, a "Welcome back" treatment would help.
- Add a small human figure or couple to the hero lifestyle shot — right now it reads more like a real-estate listing than a getaway.
- Give the Boundless Visa module a rewards-tier callout (e.g., "Especially valuable for Titanium/Ambassador members") to make the card pitch feel less like a mass-market upsell.
