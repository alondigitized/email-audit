# Save 20% now 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash for later 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's kitchen-sink send as every other day this week, and the subject is a straight repeat. "Save 20% now 😍 Earn Kohl's Cash for later 🤩" already went out on 09-30. The week's other subjects ("Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩" on 10-02, "Extra 20% off savings…" on 10-01) differ only in wording. The offer is real: code YOUSAVE20 for an extra 20% off, and $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent through Oct. 18. But the body doesn't deliver it up top.
- The render opens with a bare pink "EXTRA" wordmark. A Kohl's Cash badge and a Women/Men/Kids/Home button grid follow. After that comes a family-pajamas hero, a Mom gift-shop banner, and a baby and toddler sale with Carter's 50% off. Further down are toys, home decor and four product tiles. The code that makes the headline 20% work, YOUSAVE20, sits in a barcode block at the very bottom. Several other coupons also hide down there: HOME10, TOYS10 and the baby-clothing $10 off $50. The email has a lot of offers and no single hero.
- Nothing is broken in the render, and the October timing fits. As a send it's competent, but it's an undifferentiated promo, and the subject isn't what the hero shows.

## What's working

- The "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | Earn: 5% REWARDS" strip at the top gives loyalty members a personalized touch.
- The "Ends Oct. 4" and "Oct. 5-18" deadlines on the coupons give real, credible urgency.
- The product tiles are concrete: Ninja SLUSHi $349.99, a $29.99 jeans pair, a Serta topper at $137.99–$209.99. Prices are visible, and each tile links to a specific item.
- The $10-off-$50 coupon blocks are clear and easy to understand within each category (baby, toys, home).
- The email is seasonally current, with holiday jammies, gift shopping, early toys and decor.
- Nothing renders badly: no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject promises 20% off, but the top of the email shows a bare "EXTRA" wordmark with no number. The code YOUSAVE20 is buried near the footer.
- There is no single hero. The email has six or more competing offers (pajamas, Mom gifts, baby sale, toys, decor, products) and many "Shop Now" links.
- The Women/Men/Kids/Home button grid pushes the content down before any offer appears.
- This subject was already sent on 09-30, and the week has had near-identical sends daily. It feels like the fifth identical promo.
- The CTAs are generic ("Shop Now", "Explore the Gift Shop"). The coupon fine print and the "Coupons do not apply to select brands" line are small and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the headline offer in the hero. Show "EXTRA 20% OFF" in large type with the code YOUSAVE20 and a "Shop 20% off" button, and remove the nav grid above it.
- 2. Cut the body to one primary offer and two secondary ones. Move the baby, toys and home $10 coupons into a compact strip.
- 3. Make CTA copy specific, such as "Get 20% off pajamas" or "Shop baby deals", instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Rotate the subject and creative. Don't repeat a subject within the same week. Pick one angle per send (for example, "Kohl's Cash ends Oct. 18" on one day and "Code YOUSAVE20" on another).
- 5. Move the coupon codes above the products and make the exclusions more readable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Code YOUSAVE20: extra 20% off, ends Oct. 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent, through Oct. 18`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOUSAVE20 on pajamas, baby gear and home. Ends Oct. 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kohl's Rewards members: $10 off $50 on toys, baby and home.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a fixed-width (640px) table layout with a mobile media query at 500px. The source shows malformed markup, four tracking pixels, and content images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits and a truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as real failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA probe skipped 69 links as tracking redirects (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Four tracking pixels before the first content block:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d4c330db-…` (bare `<img>` directly under `<body>`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?HSDUC34N7VPETDYNGGHZGEVTTY.60265`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)
- **Redundant opens:** Three separate open trackers fire. They add third-party requests and can inflate or skew open metrics.
- **Broken tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` close tag with no matching opener (`<img … alt=""></custom>`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** These QA findings are valid. Images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` and the product image `58125192_2s_2x` have no `alt`. If they carry offer text (as the "Save 20%" subject suggests), that text is lost to screen readers and with images blocked.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""`.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early at that point, which can push the later `<style>` and `<link>` handling into unpredictable territory.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The 1×1 tracking table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables do have it.
- **CSS errors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Duplicate font loading:** Both `<link>` and `@import` request Montserrat and Roboto. Roboto isn't in the visible `font-family` stacks, and the duplicate loads add blocking requests in clients that support them.
- **DOCTYPE mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE is paired with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`). The source is truncated, so personalization further down is unchecked.
