# Find top hosting finds for less 🙌 Plus, save 20%.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a standard Kohl's long-scroll deal wall. The hero is better than most of the recent run. "Hosting finds so good, even Mrs. Claus would approve" has a clear seasonal hook, and "Up to 30% + save with coupon" sits right under it with a Shop Now button. The "hosting" theme then dissolves into a sprawl of unrelated modules. That sprawl is the main weakness.
- Below the hero the email shows the same pile as the Nov 16 and Nov 15 sends: Online Only Daily Deals, a niche-gift banner, two $10-off coupons, a Preview the Deals teaser, Klarna, pickup and delivery tiles, four products, and three coupon barcodes. The subject promises "save 20%", and the real 20% code (SAVEMORE) is buried near the bottom. A recipient has to scroll about 80% of the email to redeem the offer named in the subject.
- Overall it's competent and on-brand, but it isn't focused. It's a 7 on offer clarity and a 5 on discipline. After a week of near-daily Kohl's sends ("Get 20% off | Check off your holiday list" yesterday, the 40% push on Nov 15), this one feels like more of the same.
- One caveat: the screenshot was heavily downscaled, so I can't read small print or exact copy with full confidence.

## What's working

- The hero is seasonal and has personality. The Mrs. Claus line and the holiday-table photo clearly signal hosting season.
- A "Shop Now" button sits directly under the 30% off callout, so the hero is actionable immediately.
- The Rewards header shows "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member" and "Earn 5% Rewards", plus an "Ends Nov. 21" line. This makes the loyalty angle and the deadline visible at the top.
- The product cards show real prices: the Ninja SLUSH at $299.99, the Levi's jeans at $44.97, and the Serta topper at $137.99–$209.99. They give concrete reasons to click.
- The coupon block at the bottom with barcodes and codes (SAVEMORE, TOYS10, HOME10) makes redemption straightforward.

## What's weak

- The subject's "save 20%" offer only appears in the final coupon block. The hero leads with 30% off, so the subject and hero disagree.
- There are too many competing modules: Daily Deals, niche gifts, a Deals preview, Klarna, pickup and delivery, and products. The eye has no single path after the hero.
- The "hosting" theme is dropped after the first two sections. Women, Men, Kids and Toys & Games tiles take over.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now". None names a product or category.
- It's the latest in a near-daily run of similar deal walls with stacking coupons. The Kohl's Cash, $10-off and 20%-off layers are hard to parse and feel like fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the subject and hero agree on one offer.** Either lead the hero with the 20% code, or change the subject to the 30% off. Put SAVEMORE next to the hero button instead of at the bottom.
- 2. **Cut the middle third.** Drop the Klarna, pickup and delivery tiles, and the niche-gift banner. Keep one hosting hero, one coupon strip, and one product row.
- 3. **Keep the hosting theme going.** Add a row of hosting products (serveware, linens, kitchen) with prices, instead of switching to the Ninja, towel, topper and jeans mix.
- 4. **Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs.** For example, "Shop hosting under $30" and "Get 30% off kitchen & dining".
- 5. **Fold the three coupons into one "Your savings" block** with a single deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hosting for less: up to 30% off kitchen & dining`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Mrs. Claus approved: 20% off hosting, ends Nov. 21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEMORE on serveware, linens and more. Rewards members earn extra.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tablescapes, bedding and kitchen finds, plus $10 off $50 with coupon.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Find top hosting finds for less 🙌 Plus, save 20%."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has several markup defects: an invalid element in `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and an empty `<title>`. It also has accessibility gaps (zoom disabled, missing alt text) and four stacked tracking pixels. The compliance, authentication, UTM and link-destination checks can't be confirmed here. The HTML is truncated and the relay didn't capture headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **77 links were not probed.** They all use redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), so destinations and HTTP status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Four tracking pixels sit at the top of `<body>`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/72f1b622-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  - Open counts will be inconsistent across them. The Adobe `demdex.net` call adds a third-party request.
- **Empty parameter:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png` URL has no value. That suggests a dynamic field that didn't resolve.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 13,689 of 19,412 plain-text characters are URLs (70.5%), from the long `qs=` tokens. This can raise spam-filter scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" … alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened. Some parsers will mishandle it.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children. Parsers will close `<head>` early, and Gmail strips microdata anyway.
- **Empty title and favicon:** `<title></title>` and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`. The empty `href` can trigger a request to the current document URL.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  - If these carry product or offer content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels with no alt:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/72f1b622-…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute. The `o.gif` flag looks like a QA false positive, because the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the pixel wrapper table (`width="1" height="1"`) and the preheader table `<table width='640' … class="container">` have no `role`, while the other layout tables do. The preheader table also sets `display:block` on a `<table>`.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded via `<link>` and `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and Outlook ignore them, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is defined, so this is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (e.g. `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) in the visible source.
- **Subscriber identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. It is a recipient-level ID, so it is worth confirming this is intended in image requests.
- The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified failure**. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` and the click domains `click.s.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com` share the `kohls.com` organizational domain, which is favorable for alignment.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this may be a false negative. Verify against the full source.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible. Links are wrapped in redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), so final landing URLs, UTM carry-through and offer alignment (the "20%" claim) couldn't be checked.
- **Campaign identifiers:** `mi_ecmp=1026053_20261117` and `email_name=261117_DG_Email_Tuesda…` are present on the images. The `email_name` value is truncated in the data, so its full form is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move or delete the `<div itemscope>` block in `<head>`. Place the schema markup in `<body>` or drop it.
2. Add a `<title>`. Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the pixels that lack it.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels. At minimum, confirm the `demdex.net` call is required.
6. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL.
7. Pull the raw message headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the full untruncated HTML. That will settle the compliance warnings and the footer address check.
8. Run a redirect-following probe on a sample of the 77 `click.s.kohls.com` links to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
