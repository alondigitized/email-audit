# Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE SHIPPING 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fifth Deal Days send in about three days. It follows the 10-06 "Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + Kohl's Cash = 🎉 🛍️ 💸" and "KOHL'S DEAL DAYS ALERT 🚨". It is a long, competent kitchen-sink promo: Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash buttons, a Daily Deals block, a "4 Days of Deals" block, Sephora, Carter's, Amazon devices, coupon codes and free shipping. The pieces are clean and the offers are real, but there is no single thing to look at.
- The hero is the weakest part. The "Deal Days" block is a large maroon slab with faint, low-contrast lettering, and it spends the top of the email without stating an offer. The first real savings message ("Today's Featured Daily Deals") sits below it, behind two utility buttons. The rest is a dense stack of 50%-off tiles that all look alike.
- Verdict: functional and on-brand, but it adds to subscriber fatigue. Its only new lever is the "FREE SHIPPING" headline, which the 10-05 and 10-06 sends already used. The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I'm judging layout and structure, not fine print.

## What's working

- Daily Deals tiles pair a big price or percent with a product photo and a "Shop Now" button, so each one is easy to scan.
- Deadlines are credible and specific, such as "Ends October 8" on the 4 Days of Deals block and "Ends Oct. 7" on the Amazon tile.
- The Kohl's Rewards member banner and the "Earn Kohl's Cash" button put loyalty value near the top.
- The product grid near the bottom links to specific items (Ninja drink maker, towels, jeans, mattress topper) with visible prices.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero is a mostly empty maroon block with faint "Deal Days" lettering and no stated offer.
- Too many competing offers: 50% off, 25% off with coupon, $10 off $50, Kohl's Cash, free shipping, Sephora and Amazon. Nothing is clearly the lead.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now," and there are about a dozen of them.
- Fine print and exclusions ("Coupons do not apply," "Select styles") are repeated on many tiles, and a long legal block closes the email. That weakens the sense of a clear deal.
- The cadence is too heavy. Five near-identical Deal Days messages in three days risks unsubscribes and image-blocking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around one line, for example "Free shipping, no minimum, through Oct. 8," with one button. Move the nav and Kohl's Cash buttons below it.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four sections and drop the repeated "Coupons do not apply" lines.
- 3. Make CTAs specific: "Shop 50% off kitchen," "Get the $10 off coupon."
- 4. Space out or merge the sends. Don't send a fifth message that mostly repeats the fourth.
- 5. Fix the hero contrast so the "Deal Days" text is readable.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deal Days ends tomorrow. Use code YOUSAVE25 for an extra 25% off.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE SHIPPING"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with Movable Ink dynamic images and a long list of tracking links. The HTML source has several markup defects: an invalid element in `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, and an invalid CSS selector. Most content images lack alt text. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't pass the headers through. The source was also truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 73 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMjJ9...`. The `qs` blob is opaque, so the final destinations and any redirect-chain problems are unverified.
- **Four open or tracking pixels before the layout:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/4a483017-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023340_2026107`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?566FCPOGCKYEFBMGKR2FAUQ25A.60268&d=60268&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)

  The redundancy adds requests and third-party calls. The 0×0 demdex image is more likely to be dropped by clients.
- **Malformed markup:** the open.aspx pixel is followed by a stray closing tag, `<img ... alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid element in `<head>`:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization"><meta itemprop=...></div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early and start `<body>` at that point. Gmail also strips microdata, so it has no effect there.
- **Empty `<title>`** (`<title></title>`) and **empty favicon** (`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`).
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:**
  - Content images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x`. With images off, or for screen readers, the content is empty or unlabeled.
  - Tracking pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` is a false positive. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`:** the preheader table (`width='640' ... class="container"`), the 1×1 pixel table and the AAM table.
- **CSS defects:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so the selector matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto` inside the media query is not a valid value.
- **Redundant font loading:** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded through both `<link>` and `@import`. The font stack falls back to Arial and Helvetica, so this is a performance issue only.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The subscriber identifiers in the pixel URLs (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is likely the same relay limitation.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not in the truncated source, so they cannot be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages:** these cannot be verified. All 73 links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects that were not resolved.
- **Campaign identifiers:** these appear only on image URLs, for example `email_name=261007_DG_Email_Wednesd...` and `mi_ecmp=1023340_2026107`. There is no visible evidence of campaign parameters on click links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`, or move it into `<body>`. Delete the stray `</custom>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
3. Fill in `<title>`. Remove or populate the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix `.ReadMsgBody`, remove `max-height: auto`, and add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Consolidate the four open pixels where possible, and drop the duplicate `@import` or `<link>` font loading.
7. For the audit pipeline, capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relayed copy. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check UTM parameters and destinations. Raise the HTML truncation limit so the footer can be audited.
