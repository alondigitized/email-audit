# 10% off our most-searched beauty 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a "New and Only at Sephora" product-discovery email with a 10% Insider code bolted on top. The subject sells 10% off "our most-searched beauty," but the body is a long catalog of exclusives: Kayali Yum Vanilla Cupcake, Sol de Janeiro, Haus Labs, Saie, Dr. Dennis Gross and Phlur. Nothing in the body says "most-searched." The 10% claim is there (Insider badge, code ALLONSALE), but it's competing with a free Sephora x Parachute gift banner, a P.S. about same-day delivery and a second Savings Event block.
- Execution is polished. The Kayali hero is clear, the exclusive grid has clean "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" labels, and the Clean badges add trust. But this is roughly the seventh Sephora Savings Event send in a week. Compared with 11/03's "Luxe Under $40/$60/$70" price-tier edit, this one has weaker price specificity. Most tiles show a price range or a single price, with no savings framing.
- Judgment: solid and on-brand, but the subject and body don't match, and the email is too long and has too many offers. It would land as an average to slightly above-average send.

## What's working

- The Kayali "JUST DROPPED" hero has one clear product story and a visible "SHOP NOW" button.
- The Insider 10% badge with the 11/3–11/9 dates and code ALLONSALE sits at the top, so the offer is visible immediately.
- "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" labels and Clean badges give each tile a reason to click.
- Product tiles show brand, product and price, which helps shoppers decide.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." block with Bestselling Perfume and Top-Rated Foundations is a good category split.

## What's weak

- The subject says "most-searched," but the body is built around new and exclusive products. The promise and the content don't match.
- There are too many competing messages at the top: the free Parachute gift, the 10% badge, the code, the P.S. about same-day delivery with code SPEEDY, and the "DON'T MISS OUT" strip.
- The grid has eight or more tiles with small type and no individual CTA buttons. That is a long scroll with diffuse intent.
- It repeats the same Savings Event pitch as the earlier sends (the 11/04 lip-balm edit, 11/03 Luxe Beauty). This is cadence fatigue.
- The legal fine print is very long, and the barcode at the bottom is an awkward ending.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align subject and hero. Either rename the email around what is actually in it (new and exclusive), or lead with real "most-searched" bestsellers.
- 2. Cut the top stack to one offer: 10% off with the code. Move the gift and same-day delivery lower down.
- 3. Trim the grid to 4–6 tiles. Add per-tile "Shop" links and a price or savings line.
- 4. Move the Spot it. Shop it. bestsellers higher, since they carry social proof.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier sends in this event so the inbox doesn't see another identical promo.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering and personalization defect (an empty name slot, "Hi ,") and one confirmed deliverability defect (no plain-text part). The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay, not because of a confirmed fault. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links are redirect-wrapped through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ac312e58bb2888f4bbcfb920/…?__dU__=…`. The final destinations and any UTM parameters are not visible in the HTML.
- The QA probe covered only 12 of 53 links (41 skipped by design, deprioritized as footer/social). The probe reported no failing links among those 12. The skipped links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0px; font-size:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>`. It is hidden correctly, but it has no `width`/`height` attributes and no `alt`. This is what the QA "missing alt" warning flags first.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on three images:
  - the tracking pixel (harmless, since it is hidden);
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_14.jpg`;
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_15.jpg`.

  Both stitched images are on `images.harmony.epsilon.com`. They are likely content images, so images-off clients and screen readers get nothing for them.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The `min-width:700px` prevents the container from shrinking. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only resize specific classes (`.mobile`, `.mobileHeaderLogo`, and similar). So the 700px table may force horizontal scroll or zoom-out in clients that ignore or strip media queries.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in the `<style>` element. Conditional comments are not valid there, so the `img.mso-size` rule is unlikely to apply in Outlook. Other clients may drop the rest of the `@media` block as well.
- **Invalid robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is an accessibility concern.
- **JSON-LD:** the block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` (empty value) and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. Empty values in structured markup can make Gmail's promo annotations unreliable or ignored.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as "Hi ," with no first name, and there is no fallback value. The token resolved to an empty string. Either the recipient has no first name on file or the merge field has no default.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, again because of the relay. The sender `shop@beauty.sephora.com` is a subdomain of `sephora.com`, so alignment should be checked on the raw message.
- QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set. Its presence in the body, the physical postal address and the CAN-SPAM footer could not be confirmed because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues can be confirmed from the data. Every link goes through the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirector, so UTMs, if any, are applied server-side and are not visible in the source.
- The preheader says "Save on EVERYTHING through 11/9", while the subject says "10% off our most-searched beauty". Landing-page alignment with either claim was not verified. Nothing in the QA output confirms a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting fallback.** Add a default (for example "Hi there,") or suppress the greeting when the first name is empty. This is the only confirmed FAIL on the personalization side.
2. **Add a multipart plain-text part.** The text version is 0 chars. A missing or empty text part hurts deliverability and accessibility. It should include the main CTAs and the unsubscribe URL.
3. **Add `alt` text** to `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_14.jpg` and `_15.jpg`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Move the MSO conditional** out of the `@media` block into its own `<!--[if mso]><style>` block. Reconsider `min-width:700px` on the wrapper (use `max-width:700px; width:100%` with an MSO ghost table). Drop `maximum-scale=1`.
5. **Verify headers on the raw message** (for example by sending to a Gmail test inbox and using "Show original"). Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
6. **Fix or remove the JSON-LD** `DiscountOffer` with its empty `discountCode`, and correct `content="no index"` to `noindex`.
7. **Raise probe coverage** beyond 12 of 53 links, or resolve the redirector chains for the skipped links, so UTM parameters and landing destinations can be audited.
