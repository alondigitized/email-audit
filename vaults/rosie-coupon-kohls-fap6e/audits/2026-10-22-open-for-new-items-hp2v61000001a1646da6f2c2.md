# 🎉 Open for new items
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals catalog email with no offer in it. The subject, "🎉 Open for new items", says nothing about beauty, a product or a deal. The body is better than the subject. A big brown "NEW ARRIVALS" hero with a Jung Saem Mool cushion compact leads into a clean 2x4 grid of priced products: Saie, Paula's Choice, Phlur, Living Proof, Benefit, Kate McLeod, Make Up For Ever and Bounce Curl.
- Below the grid, three promo tiles split attention: "First Access: Holiday Drops" (24 hours only), a "Bigger is Better" value-size tile, and a "Just In from Kayali" lip balm block. The "24 HOURS ONLY" tile is the only urgency in the email, and it sits in the bottom third.
- Compare this with the 2026-10-21 send, "Choose one of two FREE sample bags," which had a concrete gift-with-purchase hook. Today's email is a soft discovery blast, and it lands in an inbox that has had a Sephora send every day since 10/17. Expect a weak open and a modest click. I'd call it a competent catalog with no reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "NEW ARRIVALS" in big type, one "SHOP NOW" button, and a single compact image.
- The product grid is well organized. Each item has a NEW or ONLY AT SEPHORA tag, a name and a price, and the Saie and Benefit sets show a "$X value" figure.
- The "Just Arrived & So Good" section gives the email a clear theme and a reason to scroll.
- The Insider header strip ("You have 0 points") ties the email to the loyalty program, and the same-day delivery and free-shipping line carries the service message.
- Holiday-flavored items (Kate McLeod gift set, Benefit gift set, "Holiday Drops") fit the season.

## What's weak

- There is no offer anywhere. No %, points multiplier, gift or code appears, so nothing gives a reason to buy today.
- The subject is generic and says nothing about beauty. It could be any retailer's "new stock" blast.
- Four promos compete: hero, grid, the 24-hour tile, and Kayali. The best urgency hook ("24 HOURS ONLY") is buried mid-email.
- The "NOW AT SEPHORA" block sells new brands (f. VACATION, Jung Saem Mool, Anecdotes, UNITE Hair) with a vague "Shop New Brands" link. It names no product.
- The Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar near the bottom looks unfinished. The "INSIDER" cell has an odd white box and a red marker, and the "0 points" status is never turned into a nudge.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one concrete hook. Put a points multiplier, a free sample, or the "24 hours only" holiday early access in the hero. A new-arrivals grid works as supporting content, but it doesn't give anyone a reason to act today.
- 2. Rewrite the subject so it names a category, a brand or a deadline. The generic subject is the biggest drag on opens.
- 3. Move the First Access tile above the grid, since it holds the only real urgency.
- 4. Use personalized CTAs ("Shop new makeup", "Shop holiday sets") instead of a bare "SHOP NOW".
- 5. Use the Insider strip to say what 0 points could earn, e.g. "Earn points on these new arrivals". Fix the tier bar's rendering.
- 6. Cut the cadence. Daily sends make this one blur into the others.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  - Both look like tracking beacons. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers and "image missing alt" lint rules ignore them.
- **Tracking pixel carries a numeric user ID.** `mi_u=4970269939971072` is in the pixel URL. This is normal for ESP tracking, but it is a per-recipient identifier in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Header image lacks alt text.** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-.../2021_Navs_Header_06`) has no alt. If it carries navigation labels, that content is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Invalid robots meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The space makes the directive invalid for any parser that honors it.
- **Pinch-zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1" />` prevents zooming on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text-size controls are disabled.** The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`, `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This stops user and client text resizing.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`. Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports a FAIL with "Text version is 0 chars", so there is no `text/plain` alternative. This may be a relay capture artifact (see section 5), but treat it as a real issue until confirmed in the raw MIME.
