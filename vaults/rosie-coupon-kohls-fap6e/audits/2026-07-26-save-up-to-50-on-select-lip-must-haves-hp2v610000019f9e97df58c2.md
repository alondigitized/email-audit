# Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves ⭐
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This send has a real subject/body mismatch. The subject promises "up to 50% on select lip must-haves," but the top of the email is a 30% off Charlotte Tilbury banner tied to National Lip Day — a smaller number than advertised, and the very next thing after it is a completely unrelated "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" luxury new-arrivals hero (YSL cushion foundation, Marc Jacobs blush stick) that has nothing to do with lips or a sale.
- From there the email turns into Sephora's now-familiar new-arrivals grid (Sarah Creal, Fara Homidi, Armani Beauty, Westman Atelier, Hourglass, Dolce&Gabbana, Dior, Givenchy), most of it full-price. A handful of those are lip products (Hourglass Blur Lip Balm, Dior Glow Stick, Givenchy Lip Oil Gloss), but none are marked down — so the "50%" in the subject line never actually materializes anywhere in the render.
- This is the mirror image of the 2026-07-25 "Something special awaits...This is what your hair wants" send, which undersold its actual lip sale in the subject. Here Sephora oversells: the subject is the strongest, most specific thing about this email, and the body doesn't deliver on it. Judgement: functional and clean production, but the core promise is broken, which will cost trust with repeat subscribers.

## What's working

- The Charlotte Tilbury banner is genuinely well-targeted: concrete 30% off, "Today only" urgency, and it's directly tied to the National Lip Day hook.
- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in a long email.
- "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA," and "CLEAN" badges are used consistently and add real scannability to the product grid.
- Beauty Insider point balance ("you have 0 points") and full loyalty-tier footer reinforce the membership program without extra clutter.
- Category shortcut tiles (damaged hair, skincare, Dyson) at the bottom give secondary paths to purchase for readers who skip the lip content entirely.

## What's weak

- The subject's "up to 50%" offer is nowhere in the visible email — the only markdown shown is 30% off one brand, not "select lip must-haves" broadly.
- The hero directly under the sale banner ("You Deserve to Treat Yourself") is a generic luxury pitch with zero connection to lips or a sale, breaking momentum right after the open.
- Lip products that do appear later (Hourglass, Dior, Givenchy) are shown at full price with no sale tag, contradicting the subject's premise.
- The email reads as two or three unrelated campaigns stapled together (lip sale banner → luxury hero → new-arrivals dump → hair/skincare tiles → Dyson), with no single throughline.
- "Shop Now" is reused as the CTA on every single button in the email, giving no visual or copy distinction between the lip sale and the new-arrivals content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: replace the "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" luxury hero with an actual lip-sale hero showing the discounted products and the real percentage off, directly under the Charlotte Tilbury banner. Right now the subject's biggest promise disappears after one banner.
- 2. If 50% off is real anywhere, surface it above the fold with specific SKUs; if the max is 30%, fix the subject to say 30%, not 50% — the current gap reads as bait-and-switch on a rescan.
- 3. Cut the "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" luxury block from this send entirely, or move it to a separate email — it competes with and dilutes the lip-sale message this email is supposed to be about.
- 4. Vary CTA copy by section ("Shop the Lip Sale," "Shop New Arrivals," "Shop Dyson") so the eye can distinguish sale content from browse content.
- 5. Space this out from the 2026-07-25 lip-day send — two lip-focused emails within 24 hours risks fatigue for the same subscriber segment.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lip must-haves, marked down for National Lip Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new arrivals from Hourglass, Dior, and Givenchy`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop today's markdowns before they're gone`
