# FREE SHIPPING + Kohl's Cash = Kohl's Deal Days 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's sixth Deal Days email in roughly 36 hours — the June 23 pair, the June 24 pair, and now two more on June 25. The creative is structurally identical to every previous send: the same "KOHL'S DEAL DAYS" hero banner, the same "Today's Featured Daily Deals" grid, the same category nav, the same clearance footer. The only genuine pivot in this send is leading the subject with "FREE SHIPPING + Kohl's Cash," surfacing loyalty perks that previous subjects buried. That's the right instinct. The ALL CAPS execution and the sheer cadence weight erase most of the benefit.
- Inside the body, the email is competent. Discount percentages are specific — 50%, 40%, 60%, up to 75% clearance — products are shown with prices and individual Shop Now buttons, and the "8 DAYS OF DEALS" call-out is the freshest creative element, framing the event as a week-long calendar commitment rather than another "daily deal" spike. The $5.99 anchor price is a nice concrete hook. But everything else is a pixel-for-pixel copy of yesterday's sends, including the layout, color palette, and section structure.
- The honest read: a subscriber who has already opened four of these emails in 36 hours has pattern-matched to "another Deal Days send" before they even click. Kohl's is burning list equity with diminishing returns on each successive send, and no amount of good in-body execution rescues an exhausted cadence.

## What's working

- **Kohl's Cash surfaced in the subject**: Naming a loyalty benefit as the subject hook is the strongest differentiation from the previous four sends — it gives members a concrete reason to re-engage.
- **Concrete discounts are above the fold**: 50%, 40%, 60%, and 75% clearance are all visible without scrolling — no vague "great deals" language.
- **"8 DAYS OF DEALS" section**: Naming the full event duration gives urgency a credible calendar frame rather than another one-day spike.
- **$5.99 anchor price**: A specific low-dollar price point in the 8-day section makes value feel real, not aspirational.
- **Individual product CTAs**: Each deal has its own "Shop Now" button — low friction to act on the specific thing that catches the eye.

## What's weak

- **Sixth nearly-identical email in 36 hours**: No meaningful creative differentiation from the June 23–24 sends; the layout is copy-pasted.
- **ALL CAPS "FREE SHIPPING" in subject**: Fires spam-pattern recognition in the peripheral inbox scan, even for a recognized sender.
- **No urgency on the "Featured Daily Deals"**: If these are today-only, there is no visible expiry language or deadline — the time pressure implied by "featured daily" is not reinforced.
- **Cluttered layout**: Eight-plus separate offers across three distinct sections with no single hero product — the eye has nowhere to land.
- **Kohl's Cash mechanic not explained in body**: The subject teases it but the body doesn't show a clear earn-and-redeem explainer visible in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make Kohl's Cash the creative center, not a subject tease**: Since that's the new hook this send adds, the hero section should show the earn tiers visually ("Spend $50 → earn $10 Kohl's Cash") rather than repeating the same deals grid.
- 2. **Add a "today only" deadline to featured daily deals**: A visible "Expires tonight at 11:59 PM CT" line converts browsing into urgency.
- 3. **Drop ALL CAPS in the subject**: "Free shipping + Kohl's Cash = Deal Days 🤩" reads identically without triggering spam reflexes.
- 4. **Single hero product, not eight competing sections**: Pick the 60%-off item or the $5.99 anchor and give it a full-width treatment — scatter-shot grids suppress click concentration.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn Kohl's Cash on every Deal Days order 🤩`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off + free shipping — Deal Days, Day 3`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your rewards: every purchase earns Kohl's Cash through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's featured deal: 60% off + free shipping, no minimum.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Deal Days" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is functionally deliverable but carries several confirmed compliance gaps (missing CAN-SPAM address, absent List-Unsubscribe headers) and a malformed HTML element that may confuse renderers. Authentication status is unverifiable through the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) or `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce MC). Destination URLs and UTM integrity cannot be verified without following redirects.

**Four distinct tracking pixel domains** are present in a single email:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b3c87f58...` — CoherentPath open pixel (missing alt text)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` — Movable Ink open pixel (has `aria-hidden="true"`, correct)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?V36LVIH...` — Salesforce MC open pixel (missing alt text)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (missing alt text)

This is a high pixel load; no functional defect, but each pixel is an additional failure point for privacy-focused clients that block trackers.

**Stray `</custom>` closing tag** immediately after the Salesforce open pixel `<img>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." alt=""></custom>
```
`<custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most renderers will silently ignore it, but it is malformed markup that could break strict XML parsers or future linting.

**Empty favicon `href`:**
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```
Causes an unnecessary HTTP request to the email client's base URL.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is an WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure and is increasingly blocked by browsers/WebKit by default regardless of the directive.

**9 images missing alt text** — confirmed by QA. Notably, these include both tracking pixels (acceptable to leave empty with `aria-hidden`) and what appear to be Movable Ink personalized content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). Content images must have descriptive alt text; only decorative/tracking images should have `alt=""`:
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` — likely content images, all missing alt

**Redundant Google Fonts loading** — Roboto and Montserrat are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` in the same `<head>`, doubling font requests in clients that support both:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">
<!-- then: -->
@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...");
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as "no subject" in clients that display the document title (e.g., some webmail preview tabs).

### 5. Compliance

**`[WARN]` No physical mailing address detected** — CAN-SPAM Section 5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. Not present in the visible HTML. If it appears below the truncation point, disregard; otherwise this is a legal violation.

**`[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5K/day). Its absence risks inbox placement demotion or filtering. The QA note indicates this may be a relay capture issue, but it must be confirmed at the MTA level.

**`[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Gmail requires this alongside `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders. Absence is a policy violation for high-volume sends.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** — This suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on clicks from webmail clients. Does not affect CAN-SPAM directly, but combined with missing List-Unsubscribe it signals a compliance posture worth reviewing.

**`[WARN]` SPF/DKIM status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured by the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. This must be verified at the raw message level (`Authentication-Results: mx.google.com; dkim=pass ...`).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 destination links pass through click-tracking redirects and cannot be directly inspected for UTM parameters. The Movable Ink image params (`mi_ecmp=1015936_2026625`) follow a campaign-ID pattern consistent with analytics attribution, but UTM presence on the final landing URLs (`kohls.com`) is unconfirmed.
