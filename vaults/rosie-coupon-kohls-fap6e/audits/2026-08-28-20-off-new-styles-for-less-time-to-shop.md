# 20% off 🤗 New styles for less 🤩 Time to shop 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Friends & Family 20%-off coupon that's now run five-plus times since 2026-08-24, but the send finally does something different: it puts a real product hero on top. "Denim Deal Days" — starting at $17.59, with a lifestyle shot and rewards-member status bar — leads before the coupon banner even shows up. That's the strongest execution of this promo cycle so far, echoing the 2026-08-26 "Shop Denim Deal Days" send rather than just reskinning the coupon again.
- But the email overcorrects into kitchen-sink territory below the fold: Denim Deal Days, then Sephora 50%-off beauty, then a "Picked with you in mind" grid mixing a blender, bath towels, women's pants, and a mattress topper. Four unrelated categories crammed into one send dilutes the denim hero that opened strong. The subject line itself is generic ("New styles for less") and undersells the actual specific hook (Denim Deal Days, $17.59 starting price) that's sitting right in the hero image.
- Net: good hero creative, weak subject-to-hero connection, and a body that sprawls past the point of a clear single CTA. Call it a 6/10 — on par with the rest of this week's cadence, saved from lower by the denim visual.

## What's working

- Denim Deal Days hero is a specific, priced offer ("Starting at $17.59," "Reg. $29.99-$49.99") with a real lifestyle photo, not just coupon copy.
- Category buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) sit right under the top banner for fast navigation.
- Rewards status bar ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS") is visible near the top, giving a personalization signal.
- Wallet coupon barcode is embedded near the bottom with a visible code/PIN, reducing friction for the actual redemption.
- Kids' denim (Girls/Boys) photography is bright, warm, and clearly differentiated from the adult shots.

## What's weak

- Subject line ("New styles for less") doesn't mention denim, the $17.59 price point, or Friends & Family — it's the vaguest subject of this whole campaign run.
- The body sprawls across four unrelated offers (denim, Sephora beauty, home goods, mattress topper) with no clear single CTA — the eye has nowhere consistent to land after the hero.
- Sephora 50%-off block interrupts the denim narrative and then the email pivots again to a random "Picked with you in mind" product grid — the throughline is lost.
- Same 20%-coupon-plus-Kohl's-Cash mechanic and Aug. 30 deadline as every prior send this week — no new offer, just new packaging.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization claim isn't backed by any visible signal (no name, no purchase history reference) — it reads as generic merchandising copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the denim hero instead of generic "new styles" copy — it's the freshest, most specific asset in the email and the subject buries it.
- 2. Cut the email after Denim Deal Days + the 20%-off coupon reinforcement; move Sephora and the home-goods grid to a separate send or a "you might also like" footer, not competing hero blocks.
- 3. Add a visible countdown or day-count ("5 days left") near the Aug. 30 deadline — five sends in, urgency needs to feel like it's actually closing in, not repeating.
- 4. If "Picked with you in mind" stays, back it with an actual personalization cue (past category, browsing history) or drop the claim — right now it reads as a stock header over random SKUs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Denim Deal Days: jeans from $17.59 + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off is almost gone — plus new denim`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jeans starting at $17.59 with your Friends & Family coupon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Aug. 30 — shop Denim Deal Days before the coupon expires.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated promo email (Kohl's/Coherent Path platform) with 70 tracking links, six 1x1 tracking pixels, and no image alt text; delivery-header capture appears incomplete on the relay side.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links present, routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` — automated HTTP probing was skipped for these (expected behavior for redirect-domain links, not itself a defect).
- 6 tracking pixels detected: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d17e69a1-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?KP363DUXKLFEPLP2BXCCQQJ4TE...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe AAM), plus two `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting pixels — consistent with open/click tracking + Adobe Audience Manager sync, no anomalies found.
- `mi_ecmp=1020712_2026828` and `mi_u=604230016` params are consistent across pixels/links, indicating campaign ID tagging is intact.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 8 flagged images (1 click-tracker, 1 open pixel, 1 AAM pixel, 5 retargeting pixels) are missing `alt` text. These are 0/1px tracking assets, not content images, so this has no visible accessibility impact — low severity, but trivial to fix (`alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"`, already applied to one pixel but not the others).
- Malformed markup: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...KP363DUXKLFEPLP2BXCCQQJ4TE.60269&d=60269&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag. Not spec-valid HTML; most email clients will ignore it, but it should be removed.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, viewport meta, and responsive `@media` breakpoints at 500px are present and structurally standard.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) found in the visible source. `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `d_sid` are populated with resolved values, not raw tokens — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected — QA flags this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe headers live in message headers, not HTML. Needs header-level verification against the raw MIME source.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected, same caveat — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the visible/truncated HTML. This is a hard CAN-SPAM requirement; if it's genuinely absent (vs. just truncated out of this excerpt, likely in the footer), it's a compliance violation. Recommend checking the untruncated source before flagging as confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — status unknown, same AgentMail relay caveat as above.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use proprietary Coherent Path tracking params (`mi_mid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`) rather than standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — this is redirect-based attribution, not a UTM scheme, so standard UTM continuity checks don't apply here.
- Cannot confirm final landing-page destination or param passthrough since all links resolve through `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirectors, which were skipped by the HTTP probe (per QA INFO note) — destination alignment is unverified.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` consistently to all 8 tracking-pixel `<img>` tags (currently only 1 of 8 has it).
- Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
- Verify raw MIME headers directly (not via relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` — the QA warnings are inconclusive due to the AgentMail relay capture gap, not confirmed absences.
- Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the full (non-truncated) footer; if genuinely absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring immediate fix.
- Sample-resolve a few `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirect links manually to confirm they land on the intended pages, since automated probing skipped them.
