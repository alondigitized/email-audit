# Save up to 40% while the Back-to-School Sale lasts 🕛
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Back-to-School send from Kohl's this week, and it's basically a remix of 8/19-8/20's playbook — same school-hallway hero kid, same "up to 40% off" banner, same category cascade underneath (Tops $7.99, Jeans up to 30%, Bras $19.99, Backpacks 30%, Bedding 60%, Bath up to 50%). The subject "Save up to 40% while the Back-to-School Sale lasts" even signals its own staleness with "while it lasts," which reads more like end-of-run fatigue than urgency. Nothing here is broken — hierarchy is clean, pricing is specific, and the "Rewards Member / 5% Rewards" status bar up top is a nice loyalty touch — but a subscriber who opened Monday's or Tuesday's near-identical email has already seen this exact structure twice.
- The one genuinely new element is the green "TOMORROW — Don't miss millions in instant Kohl's Cash" banner, which teases yesterday's separate Kohl's Cash Giveaway send rather than adding anything to this email's own value prop. Everything below that is a well-executed but fifth-verse-same-as-the-first BTS blast.
- Judgment: solid execution, weak differentiation — this is cadence fatigue setting in, not a creative miss.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy: hero banner → nav pills → category cascade with bold red % callouts, easy to scan top to bottom.
- Specific, honest pricing throughout (Tops $7.99, Bras $19.99, Ninja Slushi $349.99) with strikethrough "Reg." pricing for comparison.
- Loyalty status bar at the very top ("Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn 5% Rewards") is a real personalization signal.
- "Picked with you in mind" section adds a light personalization layer with named products (Ninja Slushi, Gloria Vanderbilt jeans).
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean end to end.

## What's weak

- This is the third email in four days using the exact same school-hallway-kid hero and "up to 40%" banner format — near-duplicate creative fatigue.
- The green Kohl's Cash banner is a jarring insert — different colorway, different topic, breaks the BTS narrative mid-scroll instead of being its own beat.
- Subject's "while the sale lasts" is vague, low-urgency phrasing compared to the sharper "40% off... back to saving" and "Save up to 40%... 📚" subjects earlier this week.
- Category cascade (Tops, Jeans, Bras, Backpacks, Bedding, Bath) is nearly identical in order and offer depth to the 8/20 and 8/21 sends — no new categories or escalated offers to justify a fifth send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate this send from the four prior BTS emails this week — swap the hero to a new scene/model, lead with the Kohl's Cash giveaway or Clearance 70% (both are fresher hooks buried mid-email) instead of recycling the same 40%-off banner.
- 2. Cut or relocate the green Kohl's Cash banner — it interrupts the BTS flow; either open with it as its own hero or drop it and let the standalone giveaway email carry that message.
- 3. Sharpen the subject with a concrete deadline instead of "while it lasts" — pair with the "Select styles on sale until August 23" copy already in the hero.
- 4. Pull the "Clearance up to 70%" section higher — it's a stronger, fresher number than the repeated 40% and is currently buried two-thirds down.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Back-to-School Sale Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Coherent Path/Kohl's ESP template with heavy tracking pixel instrumentation; it fails several CAN-SPAM/authentication QA checks and has widespread missing alt text on tracking images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links present, all skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains like `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com` — not itself a defect, but unverified for dead-link risk).
- Multiple beacon/pixel trackers fire on open: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (Coherent Path), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` (multiple, appears 7x), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (open tracking). This is a dense first-party + third-party tracking stack but functionally consistent with retail ESP norms.
- No broken/malformed `href` values observed in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed), all 1x1 tracking pixels: `10c3c5e4-d04b-42e0-9c7a-582387d1de4b`, `o.gif`, `event` (Demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons. Low practical impact since these are invisible tracking pixels, not content images, but worth noting for strict WCAG compliance.
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, Outlook `xml:o`/`xml:v` namespaces, and responsive `@media max-width:500px` rules — indicates multi-client rendering support (Outlook, mobile, webmail).
- Google Fonts (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`) loaded via `<link>` + `@import`, gated behind `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` so Outlook falls back to system fonts — correct pattern, no issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — appropriate for transactional/marketing email, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the truncated source. `mi_ecmp`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe` query params on beacon URLs appear to be unpopulated/empty (e.g. `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe`) — likely inert personalization fields on a tracking pixel, low impact but flagging as a possible unpopulated-token pattern worth spot-checking upstream.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header detected** — may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML alone.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; likely present in the untruncated footer but not confirmable from the provided source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown; could be a relay-side capture issue rather than an actual auth failure.

All four compliance/deliverability warnings are attributable to either the AgentMail relay's header capture or content truncation — none are confirmed hard failures, but none can be ruled out either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking params observed use Kohl's/Coherent Path proprietary schema (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_ecmp=1020324_2026822`) rather than standard `utm_*` params — cannot verify UTM-based landing page attribution from this source.
- `email_name=260822_DG_Email_Sat_PM` present on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` beacons, consistent with the send date (2026-08-22) and campaign type — internal naming appears aligned.
- Destination/landing page URLs not visible in the truncated source (all links are wrapped `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` redirects), so landing-page alignment cannot be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (explicit empty alt, not omitted) to all 10 tracking pixel `<img>` tags for accessibility-checker compliance.
2. Confirm with ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` headers are actually absent at the SMTP layer or just not captured by the AgentMail relay — verify with a raw header capture outside AgentMail.
3. Confirm physical mailing address is present in the untruncated footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring immediate fix.
4. Clean up empty query params on beacon URLs (e.g., `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`, `mi_kc_event_pe` with no value) — low priority, but indicates possibly broken personalization feed upstream.
