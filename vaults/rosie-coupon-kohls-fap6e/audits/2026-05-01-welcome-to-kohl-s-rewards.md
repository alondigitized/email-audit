# Welcome to Kohl's Rewards!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-01**
## Executive summary

- A clean, structured onboarding email that walks new Kohl's Rewards members through the three-step earn-track-spend loop. The design is disciplined — green brand palette, a warm lifestyle hero, and a numbered explainer sequence that reads fast. However, the welcome email's biggest job (make the new member feel excited and oriented enough to act) is undercut by a weak CTA placement, generic headline copy, and a rewards summary section that's too small to register. This email educates adequately but converts poorly.
- Solid onboarding communication with good program explanation, but the single CTA ("Sign In & Start Earning") arrives late, is visually small, and asks for a login rather than driving an immediate shopping moment. Opportunity cost is real — welcome emails consistently outperform all other trigger types.

## What's working

- **Numbered 3-step structure** (Earn It / Track It / Spend It) is immediately legible and sets expectations without overwhelming the reader.
- **Color discipline** — consistent Kohl's green with white type creates clear visual rhythm.
- **Lifestyle hero image** — warm, aspirational (mother and child), fits the family-friendly brand.
- **"Score Rewards. Shop Your Fave Brands. Repeat."** tagline at the bottom is punchy and memorable.
- Footer is well-organized with Kohl's Card, Kohl's App, Kohl's Coupons quick links.
- Social icons are present and on-brand.

## What's weak

- **CTA is buried and singular.** "Sign In & Start Earning" appears near the bottom of a long scroll, and it's not visually dominant. A welcome email should have a primary CTA much higher — ideally below the hero — and a secondary one reinforcing it.
- **No personalization visible.** No name, no city, no mention of prior purchases or categories. "Welcome to the Fam" is warm but generic.
- **The "Track It" step (Step 2)** is the weakest — the supporting copy is tiny and the $5 Kohl's Cash mechanic is buried. This is the most motivating part of the program and gets the least visual weight.
- **Hero headline "Welcome to the Fam"** is friendly but soft. It communicates no material benefit in the first three words.
- **No urgency or activation hook.** Nothing triggers the member to act NOW rather than later. No first-purchase bonus, expiring offer, or limited-time incentive is surfaced.
- **Bottom brand logos section** ("Lauren Conrad" etc.) is so small it's nearly illegible and adds clutter without clear purpose for a new member.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a primary CTA button directly below the hero** — "Start Shopping & Earning" beats "Sign In" because it frames the action as a reward, not a task.
- 2. **Lead with a benefit in the subject and hero** — "You're in. Here's $5 to get started" or similar would outperform a generic welcome in virtually every A/B test.
- 3. **Increase the visual weight on the Kohl's Cash mechanic** — the "$5 Kohl's Cash for every $50 earned" is the program's core hook; it should be the largest, most scannable element after the hero.
- 4. **Add a first-purchase incentive or bonus Kohl's Cash activation** to create urgency. Welcome email is the highest-leverage moment to close the loop between sign-up and first purchase.
- 5. **Remove or enlarge the brand logo row** — at current size it reads as noise, not inspiration.
- 1. **Fix missing `alt` text** on `35995924_20x` image — provide descriptive text or `alt=""` if purely decorative.
- 2. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta to satisfy WCAG 1.4.4.
- 3. **Verify physical address** exists in the email footer (likely in truncated HTML); add it if absent — CAN-SPAM violation risk.
- 4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are present at the MTA level and not stripped by the relay. Add `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` if missing.
- 5. **Validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment** for `s.kohls.com` sending domain independently of the relay.
- 6. **Probe all 25 tracking links** manually or in a non-relay environment to confirm destination reachability and UTM coverage.
- 7. **Swap `@import` for a `<link>` tag** (placed before `<style>`) for Montserrat — improves font load reliability across clients that support web fonts.

## Technical Audit — Kohl's Rewards Welcome Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard table-based HTML email with MSO VML conditionals and a responsive CSS media query block; structure is well-formed. QA scan surface three compliance-level header gaps and one missing image alt attribute; authentication status is unverifiable through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **25 click-redirect links unprobed.** All tracking links were skipped by the HTTP prober, so link health (dead links, redirect chains, final destination reachability) is unconfirmed. Manual spot-check of CTA destinations is required before treating this email as fully validated.
- **No link probe data means no UTM verification** — see §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Missing `alt` on image | `src: https://images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/35995924_20x?scl=1` — QA flagged `alt` attribute absent |
| `user-scalable=0` in viewport meta | `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` — blocks pinch-zoom, WCAG 1.4.4 failure |
| Google Fonts via `@import` inside `<style>` | Gmail (non-Google Workspace) strips `<style>` blocks; Montserrat will silently fall back to Arial in those clients. The MSO Arial fallback is correct, but no fallback exists for Gmail web |
| `margin: 0 !important` applied to `a` | `body, table, td, a { margin: 0 !important; }` — blanket application to anchor elements can conflict with button/CTA padding in certain Outlook builds |
| `@-ms-viewport` rule present | Deprecated, no effect in any current client; adds noise |

No layout-breaking issues are confirmed from the truncated source, but the full body was not available for complete review.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[%...%]`, or similar) visible in the provided HTML excerpt. Full-source review recommended to confirm no dangling tokens exist in the truncated sections.

### 5. Compliance

| Finding | Severity | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | WARN | QA: "Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay" — header may be present upstream but stripped in transit. Verify raw headers at the MTA level before assuming non-delivery |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` absent (RFC 8058) | WARN | One-click unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (≥5k/day threshold). If Kohl's volume qualifies, this is a deliverability risk, not just a best-practice gap |
| No physical mailing address detected | WARN | CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. QA found none — confirm it exists in the truncated footer section before flagging as a true violation |
| `Authentication-Results` header absent | WARN | SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown through relay. Verify DNS records (`kohls.com` DMARC policy, `s.kohls.com` SPF/DKIM alignment) independently |
