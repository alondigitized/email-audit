# Save 20% | Semi-Annual Intimates Sale = perfect fits at perfect prices.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is another technically clean Kohl's send — same solid production run logged since 7/30, no clipped starbursts, no broken art, nav buttons and product tiles all line up. The hero does what the subject promises: $19.99 bras (reg. $42–$54) front and center, with a genuinely useful fit-type nav (Racerback, Wire Free, Minimizer, T-Shirt, Push-Up, Strapless) right under it.
- Where it falls down is focus. The email opens with a generic "extra 20% off" banner before you ever see a bra, and the "Picked with you in mind" block below the intimates section pivots hard into a Ninja Slushi maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and jeans — none of which relate to intimates or to each other. The Ultimate Clearance Event banner is also the same creative already logged in the 8/2 through 8/5 sends this week.
- Judgement: solid execution on the category hero, undercut by a grab-bag cross-sell block and reused clearance creative. Middle-of-the-pack for this run — worth sending, not worth celebrating.

## What's working

- Hero delivers on the subject line: bras hero image, $19.99 price, "Bras for women. Select styles." directly matches "Semi-Annual Intimates Sale."
- The fit-type quick-nav grid (Racerback, Wire Free, Minimizer, T-Shirt, Push-Up, Strapless) gives shoppers a fast path to their exact need — a nice, category-specific touch.
- Clean technical render throughout: no clipped starbursts, no broken images, tiles and buttons align correctly.
- Kohl's Rewards status bar and "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" banner both appear near the top, reinforcing loyalty value for a member.

## What's weak

- The "Picked with you in mind" cross-sell grid (Slushi maker, bath towel, mattress topper, jeans) has no relationship to intimates or to each other, diluting the category focus the subject promised.
- The Ultimate Clearance Event banner is the same creative used in the 8/2–8/5 sends — adding to cadence fatigue already flagged in this run.
- A generic "Take an extra 20% off" banner sits above the intimates hero, so the subject's specific hook isn't the first thing a reader sees.
- Subject line runs long (71 characters) and uses an odd "=" connector that reads like ad copy rather than something a person would write.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the intimates hero, not the generic 20%-off banner — put "Bras for women, $19.99" at the very top so the subject's promise is the first thing seen.
- 2. Replace the mixed cross-sell grid with intimates-adjacent items (loungewear, sleepwear, robes) to keep the whole email on-theme.
- 3. Swap the reused Ultimate Clearance banner for creative specific to this send, or drop it — the same banner across 4+ sends this week reads as filler.
- 4. Tighten the subject line and drop the "=" construction.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Semi-Annual Sale: Bras from $19.99 (plus 20% off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bra, your way — 20% off + Semi-Annual pricing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Racerback, wire-free, push-up & more — all on sale through 8/23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$19.99 bras, reg. $42-$54. Extra 20% off ends 8/16.`
