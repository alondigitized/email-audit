# Labor Day Sale + $10 off your $25 purchase STARTS TODAY 👋
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine Labor Day flagship send, and it shows — $10 off $25 plus $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 spent is a real, stackable offer, not another Friends & Family reskin, and it leads the hero instead of getting buried below the fold. The email then sprawls into the now-familiar Kohl's kitchen-sink format: kitchen appliances, jeans, loungewear, activewear, towels, Sephora, luggage, rugs, shoes, team apparel, a Levi's spotlight, and an 85% clearance banner, all before the coupon reappears at the bottom with barcodes.
- Compared to 2026-09-01's sports lead or 2026-08-31's 85%-off clearance lead, this one earns its subject line — the $10-off coupon is real, immediate, and visible without much scrolling, and it's reinforced with barcode/promo-code creative at the bottom for people who print or screenshot. But the sheer number of category blocks (13+ separate merchandising modules) dilutes the Labor Day hero fast, and by the time you hit the clearance banner and the "Picked with you in mind" grid, the coupon has been forgotten twice over.
- Solid, offer-forward open; execution drags on the click side because there's no clear single next step after the hero.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: $10 off $25 + $5 Kohl's Cash per $25, in large legible type with a green accent badge.
- The coupon is echoed again near the footer with a scannable barcode and explicit code (SHOP10 / HOME15), useful for in-store shoppers.
- Category CTAs are consistent — every module has its own "Shop Now" button, so navigation is never ambiguous.
- The Levi's block break from the grid pattern (photography instead of product-on-white) is a nice visual pacing device mid-email.
- No render bugs — clean grid, product photography loads, prices and percentages are all legible.

## What's weak

- Way too many merchandising blocks stacked with no hierarchy — after the hero, everything (appliances, jeans, towels, Sephora, luggage, rugs, shoes, team gear) reads at equal visual weight, so nothing stands out as "the" Labor Day pick.
- The clearance banner ("Save up to 85%") competes directly with the Labor Day hero for attention and undercuts the $10-off narrative — is the deal 85% off or $10 off $25?
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" / "Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply" disclaimers repeat on nearly every module, which chips away at how honest the headline offer feels.
- No visible loyalty/member-specific benefit callout despite Kohl's Rewards being a real program — footer just states membership status generically.
- By the time a reader reaches the second coupon reminder at the bottom, they've scrolled past a dozen unrelated categories — high drop-off risk before the CTA that matters most.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the module count by half. Pick 4-5 Labor Day-relevant categories (outdoor, home, apparel) instead of running every department — this is the single biggest lever for both clarity and click-through.
- 2. Move the clearance banner out of this email entirely, or subordinate it visually below the Labor Day coupon block so it doesn't compete as a second "big number."
- 3. Anchor the "select brands excluded" disclaimer once near the hero instead of repeating it after every single category, so the recurring friction reads less like bait-and-switch.
- 4. Add a single sticky or repeated "Take $10 off" CTA button near the top third of the email (not just hero + footer) so the offer isn't easy to forget mid-scroll.
- 5. Surface Kohl's Cash balance or Rewards tier status by name if the recipient is a member — right now it's generic "REWARDS MEMBER" status text, not personalized.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: $10 off $25 + $5 Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day starts now: $10 off your $25 purchase`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your coupon with $5 Kohl's Cash for every $25 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop home, apparel & more before the Labor Day coupon expires Sept 7.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Kohl's Labor Day email uses a heavy multi-vendor tracking stack (CoherentPath/Cheetah Digital, Adobe AAM, Movable Ink) with no detected unsubscribe headers or sender authentication data available; 43% of automated QA checks passed.

- Open/click tracking chain confirmed: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1 pixel, Cheetah/CoherentPath), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `/p/rp/...` (Movable Ink, multiple instances), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (Responsys open tracking).
- 69 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable to final destinations from static analysis) — link destination integrity for CTAs could not be verified and should be manually click-tested before send.
- No malformed or broken raw `href` URLs found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images flagged with missing `alt` text, all tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, and six `.png` beacons under `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...`). These are 0-height/invisible tracking pixels, so the missing alt is low-impact for screen readers but should still be `alt=""` with `role="presentation"`/`aria-hidden="true"` for strict HTML validation — three of the eight already have `aria-hidden="true"` set correctly (the `mi.kohls.com/p/up` pixel), the rest do not.
- Responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block present with standard mobile stacking/scaling classes (`.container`, `.col`, `.w-sm-320`) — mobile responsiveness appears implemented.
- MSO conditional blocks present for Outlook (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`), indicating standard desktop-Outlook fallback handling.
- Content-bearing image alt text could not be assessed from the truncated source (only tracking pixels appear in the QA findings) — recommend full-source spot check for hero/CTA image alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_mid` (member ID) and `mi_cid` (campaign ID) parameters are present in tracking URLs — standard Movable Ink personalization identifiers, appear correctly populated (non-empty values).
- One parameter of note: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` beacon URL is empty — a redemption end-date merge field did not resolve. If this field feeds subscriber-facing content elsewhere in the email (not shown in truncated source), it should be verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the analyzed content. CAN-SPAM requires this in every commercial email; the truncated HTML did not include a footer, so this may simply be outside the captured excerpt rather than truly absent — flag for confirmation against the full email footer.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found in headers — sender authentication status is unverified via this channel.

- CTA link destinations could not be verified (see Section 2 — 69 links skipped by probe); landing page alignment cannot be confirmed without manual click-through.

1. Manually click-test a sample of the 69 skipped tracking/redirect links to confirm they resolve to the correct, live landing pages.
