# ENDS TODAY: 30% off your favorite Kohl's brands ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's 11th promotional email in eight days — and a near-carbon-copy of yesterday's "2 days only 👀 Save 30% on the Kohl's brands you love" send. The only material difference is the urgency reframe: "ENDS TODAY" replaces "2 days only." The creative is identical: APT.9 / TEK GEAR / Sonoma hero grid, same four category tiles (Women / Men / Kids / Home), same clearance section at the bottom. For a subscriber who opened yesterday's email, this registers as a reminder, not a send.
- Mechanically, the email is clean. "ENDS TODAY" in all-caps bold with the 30% hero below it is a tight above-the-fold moment, and the clearance callout (up to 70%) adds a genuine secondary hook. The render is clean, the CTAs are legible, and the layout holds.
- The problem is cadence. Eleven emails in eight days with a near-identical template teaches subscribers to tune it out. The "ENDS TODAY" subject line earns its urgency, but only because yesterday set it up — and subscribers who missed yesterday's email get no context on what's ending or why it matters. This is a competent day-two urgency close, nothing more.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TODAY" dominates above the fold** — the urgency hierarchy is immediately clear; the eye goes from header to deadline to offer in one pass.
- **Dual-offer structure earns a second look** — 30% off Kohl's brands as the hero, then a hard-red CLEARANCE banner (save up to 70%) keeps readers scrolling who weren't grabbed by the first offer.
- **Three private-label brand logos (APT.9, TEK GEAR, Sonoma)** anchor the "Kohl's brands" framing and give the discount concrete meaning rather than leaving it abstract.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds across the full email.

## What's weak

- **This is yesterday's email with a find-and-replace on the subject** — subscribers who opened "2 days only" yesterday will feel the repeat immediately; no new product, hero, or creative distinction.
- **No Kohl's Cash call-out visible in the body** — the entire July run has leaned on Kohl's Cash as a loyalty lever; removing it from the body makes this feel like a downgrade versus prior sends.
- **Category tiles (Women / Men / Kids / Home) go straight to landing pages**, not to a specific hero product or curated selection — high-intent clicks land on a generic grid.
- **Preview text is indeterminate from the render** — the small header text that inbox clients pull as preview is not legible; if it's the standard "view in browser" link, it wastes the second line of inbox real estate.
- **"Styles that say designer. Prices that say otherwise."** is a brand tagline, not offer copy — it adds length to the scroll without contributing to conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one specific product hero** — pick a single item from APT.9 or Sonoma (a dress, a jacket, a bedding set) and make that the above-the-fold visual instead of the lifestyle collage. A specific product at 30% off converts better than a promise of 30% off everything.
- 2. **Restore Kohl's Cash to the body** — every other July send made "earn Kohl's Cash" a second CTA. Dropping it from this send breaks the pattern and reduces perceived value for loyalty members.
- 3. **Write a real preheader** — if the preview is leaking junk, even 30 characters of "APT.9, Sonoma & more — today only" would lift open rate on mobile.
- 4. **Cut the tagline section** — "Styles that say designer. Prices that say otherwise." reads like filler on a day-two urgency close; replace with a specific item or a tighter category headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `ENDS TODAY: 30% off APT.9, TEK GEAR & Sonoma ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last chance: 30% off Kohl's brands today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Sonoma, APT.9 & TEK GEAR — offer ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's the final day for 30% off + up to 70% off clearance`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "ENDS TODAY: 30% off" (2026-07-08)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a multi-ESP stack (CheetahMail/CHP, MessageMedia/mi.kohls.com, Demdex/Adobe Audience Manager) with standard table-based layout. The primary technical concerns are missing alt attributes on all tracking pixels, unverifiable compliance headers through the AgentMail relay, and a malformed custom HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped by QA prober** — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URLs could not be validated; broken destinations would go undetected.

**Three independent open/impression pixels fired on load:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2fe131f8...` (CheetahMail open pixel)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb...o.gif` (MessageMedia open pixel)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?5ZZAS35U...` (secondary ESP open pixel)

Triple-counting opens across systems will inflate reported open rates and make attribution unreliable across platforms.

**Malformed tag in open-tracking block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
<custom>   ← unclosed, non-standard tag
```
`</custom>` closes a tag that was never opened with a matching element. Some parsers will insert repair nodes; behavior in Outlook is undefined.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 tracking/pixel images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). All are 1×1 pixels or decorative tracking assets — they should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to be screen-reader neutral. Currently only the `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"`.

Affected assets (sample):
- `2fe131f8-ad78-4345-92fe-4ce436942f45` (open pixel)
- `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` (content renders from mi.kohls.com)
- `916d6fa08d185233.png` — URL contains unfilled merge token fragment: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen` (value is empty — see §4)

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 violation. Modern mobile email clients may ignore it, but it is still a risk for recipients with low vision.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` provides no document label; screen readers announce the email as untitled.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unfilled token in image URL:**
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pen
```
Found in `src` of `916d6fa08d185233.png`. The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter has an empty value, suggesting a merge token that evaluated to null. If this controls a promotional end-date render, the image may be serving incorrect or fallback content silently.

No other visible merge token failures in the truncated source, but 22 unprobed click links may also carry unfilled parameters.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be verified because all 22 destination URLs are behind click-redirect wrappers (`click.chp.kohls.com`) that the QA prober skipped. No UTM values are visible in the HTML source itself.

The `&email_name=260708_DG_Email_Wednesda` parameter appears on several `mi.kohls.com` image URLs — this is the internal campaign identifier and appears correctly set, though it is truncated in the QA log (likely truncated at capture, not in the actual URL).

**Gap:** Without probing click-redirect destinations, there is no confirmation that UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are being passed through to kohls.com landing pages.
