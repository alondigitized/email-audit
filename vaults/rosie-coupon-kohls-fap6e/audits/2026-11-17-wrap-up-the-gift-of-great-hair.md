## What's working

- The hero, "HOT HAIR GIFTS" with a "Wrap 'em up" kicker and a SHOP NOW button, carries the subject's promise straight into the body.
- The "ONLY AT SEPHORA SETS" green band frames exclusivity, and it names the brands and shows same-day delivery and pickup.
- Each product tile has the brand, a set description and a price, and many show the value ("$53 value," "$101 value"). That makes the gift sets easy to compare.
- "NEW • LIMITED EDITION" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags add scarcity and exclusivity cues without shouting.
- The render looks clean. I can't see any broken images, overlapping text or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The Dior mini banner at the very top is a different category from the subject. It uses the first-screen space and sends a mixed message.
- No deadline is visible for the hair sets. "Cyber Week is Coming!" signals future promos, which gives people a reason to wait.
- The CTAs are generic: "SHOP NOW," "SHOP TO REDEEM," "SEE THE FULL CALENDAR." None says what you'll get.
- The bottom third is crowded: Spot it. Shop it, Jo Malone, Cyber Week, sample bags, four category tabs, a credit-card promo and a long block of legal text. Repeating the "Holiday Gift Guide" tiles adds length without adding focus.
- There are no ratings or reviews, even though this is a gift purchase where social proof matters.

- 2. Add one real deadline or hook, such as free gift wrap, a ship-by date for holiday delivery, or a price cap ("Hair gift sets under $60"). Right now there's no reason to act today.
