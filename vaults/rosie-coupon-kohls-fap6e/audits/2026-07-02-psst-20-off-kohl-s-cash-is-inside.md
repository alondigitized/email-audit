# Psst ... 20% off + Kohl’s Cash is inside 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's third "20% off + Kohl's Cash" email in 24 hours — yesterday brought two back-to-back variations ("20% off w/code GOSHOP20 + Kohl's Cash 🎊" in the morning, "20% off AND Kohl's Cash ... immediately yes 👏" in the afternoon), and now July 2 opens with a "Psst" whisper framing the exact same offer. The creative here is legitimately solid: a clean 20%-off hero, a well-organized multi-category layout covering home, outdoor, Keds, and family apparel, and no render bugs in sight. But the offer has no freshness — it's the same code, same cash back, same deal subscribers just deleted twice yesterday.
- The "Psst ... is inside" subject is meant to feel like a secret, but secrets only land when you haven't already shouted the same thing twice in the last 18 hours. The Keds branded section is the most interesting editorial choice — a named brand drop gives the email a reason to exist beyond pure discount mechanics. Everything else is executed competently, but it's filler at this point in the cadence.
- Verdict: technically fine, strategically redundant. A subscriber burned by yesterday's duplicate cadence is not opening a third identical offer on day two.

## What's working

- **Clean hero hierarchy** — the 20% off message and "Shop Now" CTA land above the fold with no visual clutter.
- **Keds brand moment** — "The shoe of summer plans" is a named, editorially distinct section that breaks the pure discount monotony.
- **Multi-category coverage** — home, outdoor, footwear, and family apparel are all visible, giving multiple entry points for different intent states.
- **No render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping elements or broken gaps visible.
- **Kohl's Cash prominently surfaced** — the loyalty benefit is visible in the hero, not buried.

## What's weak

- **Offer déjà vu is terminal** — this is the third "20% off + Kohl's Cash" in under 24 hours; the "Psst" novelty conceit fails because the secret is already fully public.
- **No deadline visible** — neither the hero nor the body sections surface an expiration date, so there's zero urgency to act now vs. later.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic across every section** — every product block ends the same way; nothing pulls a specific category shopper to a decisive click.
- **Home + outdoor + shoes + apparel in one email = diluted** — the Keds section feels grafted in; the email has no single editorial identity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pause the cadence or evolve the offer** — three 20%-off sends in 24 hours trains subscribers to ignore and unsubscribe. If the code is still live, at minimum rotate the lead category (lead with Keds today, not a fourth generic 20% hero).
- 2. **Add an expiration** — "20% off ends tonight" or "through July 4th" gives a subscriber a reason to act on this email specifically rather than assuming the deal is always there.
- 3. **Make "Psst" pay off** — if the subject is a tease, the hero should deliver a genuinely insider-feeling offer (early access, exclusive SKU, member-only pricing). A 20% sitewide code is the opposite of a secret.
- 4. **Collapse categories, sharpen CTA** — pick one story (summer outdoor living OR back-to-school Keds) and let the CTA reflect it: "Shop Patio Deals" or "Shop Keds" beats "Shop Now" on every click metric.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Keds + 20% off: summer's looking good`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kohl's Cash stacks on top. Use code at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Outdoor, Keds, apparel — all 20% off through the holiday.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "20% off + Kohl's Cash" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard ESP-delivered HTML4 table layout with VML/MSO conditional blocks. The automated QA pass surfaces compliance gaps (missing CAN-SPAM address, unsubscribe headers not captured) and widespread missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. No broken destination links confirmed, but destination integrity cannot be verified.
- **Three separate pixel trackers** are fired on open:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9b2098c4-...` (CoherentPath)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (MessageGears)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?JXCADD7...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  Four independent open-tracking systems on a single email is atypical — any discrepancy between them will produce conflicting open-rate metrics across platforms.

- **Malformed custom tag** in open-pixel markup: `</custom>` on line `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">` — the closing `</custom>` tag is invalid HTML and will produce a parse error in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including content images (`.png` files served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`) and all tracking pixels. Tracking pixels legitimately carry `alt=""`, but content images must have descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-off rendering environments (Outlook, many mobile clients).
  - Affected content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
- **`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-to-zoom — an accessibility failure for low-vision users and a violation of WCAG 1.4.4.
- **`<title>` tag is empty** — screen readers and some email clients surface the title; leaving it blank is a missed accessibility opportunity.
- **Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — doubles the font HTTP requests. The `<link>` path is already guarded by `<!--[if !mso]>` conditionals, but the redundancy adds load overhead with no benefit.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- One content image includes an unresolved or empty merge token: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` and `mi_kc_event_pen` (no value) in the query string for `916d6fa08d185233.png`. These appear to be unfired personalization variables — either a render-time failure or the offer has no expiry/event data, in which case the token should be stripped from the URL rather than passed empty.
- No orphaned `{{` / `%%` / `*|` style merge syntax visible in the truncated source — no runaway token issues detected.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — not captured by the AgentMail relay. Major ISPs (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail) use this header to surface the unsubscribe button in the mail chrome. Its absence or non-delivery increases spam complaint risk.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders since February 2024) cannot be confirmed. If the header exists at the ESP level but is stripped by the relay, this needs to be confirmed at the raw SMTP layer.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated source — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. This may be present in the non-truncated footer; must be verified against the full HTML.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />`** is present and correct — prevents search engine indexing of the email view.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is set, which suppresses the `Referer` header on outbound clicks. This will cause UTM attribution to appear as direct traffic if the landing page relies on referrer rather than query-string parameters for source detection.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **`no-referrer` meta tag** (noted above) means any landing page tracking that falls back to HTTP `Referer` will fail — all attribution must rely entirely on UTM query parameters in click URLs.
- UTM parameters on the 22 tracked click links cannot be verified without resolving the redirect chain through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. If those click-tracking redirects strip or fail to pass UTM params to the destination, attribution will be lost.
- The `email_name=260702_DG_Email_Thursday` parameter in several image src URLs confirms a campaign naming convention is in use, but this is pixel-level (open tracking only), not click-level UTM.
