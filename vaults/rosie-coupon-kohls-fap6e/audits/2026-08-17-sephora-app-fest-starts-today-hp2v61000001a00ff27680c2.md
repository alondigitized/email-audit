# 💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's biggest structural swing in the recent send history: a full day-by-day calendar for "App Fest," running Tuesday 8/18 through Sunday 8/23, each day with its own offer block (free trial minis, 24-hour early access, the credit-card 25%/500-points combo, a 4X points day, YSL early access, Clinique/Too Faced/Pureology tiered discounts). Compared to the last week of flat product grids ("The Drop-Everything Edit," "Beauty forecast," "For The Ages"), this is a genuinely different creative shape — it's a promo calendar, not a merch wall, and it gives the email an actual reason to exist beyond "here are eight products."
- The problem is the App Fest hook is app-exclusive and this is an email — the entire mechanic (10% off, 25% off, 500 bonus points, 4X points) requires opening the Sephora app to redeem, which the email itself flags in fine print ("STACKS WITH ONLY THE APP") without ever showing what's actually in the app or why I'd bother. It's also very text/banner-dense: seven stacked colored bands of "DAY / OFFER / STACK NOW" with almost no product photography until deep in the scroll, so it reads like a terms sheet before it reads like a shopping email.
- Net: more differentiated and higher-utility than the last several sends (a real calendar of dated offers beats another "trending now" grid), but execution leans hard on banner text and app-gating rather than product desire, so it lands mid-pack rather than a clear win.

## What's working

- Clear campaign identity — big "APP FEST" wordmark and "STARTS TODAY" banner establish this as an event, not a routine promo.
- Day-by-day structure (Tue–Sun) with distinct headline offers per day gives a reason to keep opening emails/app all week, unlike single-shot promos.
- The Sephora Credit Card block (25% off + 500 bonus points) is a concrete, high-value stacked offer with clear terms language.
- "5 More Days of App Fest" recap section near the bottom re-summarizes the week cleanly for anyone who scrolled past the top.
- Brand-name draws (YSL, Clinique, Too Faced, Pureology) are called out by logo/name, giving recognizable anchors mid-scroll.

## What's weak

- Everything above the fold is a colored text banner — no product shot, no model, no face for the email until well past the first screen, which is a step backward from the "New Roz Milk Mousse" hero-image sends.
- The 10% off headline offer is the weakest one in the whole calendar, yet it's the one occupying the top banner and hero real estate.
- Heavy reliance on tiny asterisked "*Exclusions/terms apply" under nearly every single band — by the 4th one it reads as buried fine print stacking, which the rubric explicitly dings.
- The whole event is gated to the app ("STACK NOW," "JOIN NOW," in-app-only banners) but the email never shows a screenshot of the app experience or explains what "STACK NOW" actually does when tapped.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — every day gets the same size/weight treatment, so there's no signal for which day's offer is actually the best one to act on today (8/18).

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with today's specific offer and a product image, not the full 6-day calendar — someone opening on 8/17 doesn't need Sunday's YSL offer competing for hero space; put a real product shot behind the 10%-off/App Fest hero so it's not just typography on a lilac field.
- 2. Cut the day-by-day list to a scannable strip (small calendar chips) and let the email deep-link to one strong offer, with "see full calendar in-app" — this kills the fine-print stacking problem by reducing the number of asterisked blocks from ~7 to 1-2.
- 3. Show what "STACK NOW" leads to — a screenshot or preview of the app view — since the whole event's mechanic is app-only and currently invisible.
- 4. Move the Credit Card 25%/500-points block (the strongest single offer) higher, near the top, instead of burying it on Thursday's row.
- 5. Tighten preview text to name today's actual offer instead of restating the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App Fest is live — 10% off + bonus points today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 days, 6 drops: App Fest starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today only: 10% off + a free gift, no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New offer every day through 8/23 — see what's up first`
