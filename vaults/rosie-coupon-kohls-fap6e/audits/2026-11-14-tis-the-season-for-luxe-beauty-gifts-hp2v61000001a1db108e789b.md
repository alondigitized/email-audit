- "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags add scarcity and exclusivity.

## What's weak

- The top banner is a Dior trial-size redeem with a $40 purchase condition. It competes with the "luxe gifts" promise and sits above the hero.
- The send stacks at least seven competing modules: trial size, hero, luxe, grid, credit card, Gisou and Jumbo Sizes. There is no single offer.
- The "Gifts for Everyone" hero has no discount or price. It's a mood banner.
- The grid products have no visible CTAs or reviews, so each tile asks for a blind click.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense, and the 4X points offer is buried in a small box.

- 2. Move the credit-card points offer into a thin banner. The 4X points offer is the best offer in the email, and right now it sits mid-page.
- 3. Cut the grid to four or six products with star ratings, and add a "Shop now" label to each.
- 4. Give the hero a concrete hook, such as "Sets from $30," instead of "Gifts for Everyone."
- 5. Space out gift-guide sends. Three in four days will train people to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 56 links were probed (44 skipped). The QA output gives no per-link status, so link health is unconfirmed for the other 44 links.
- **Open pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>` has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. This is the image the QA "missing alt" warning refers to. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Broken-link findings:** none shown in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block has no rule that overrides this min-width, so mobile clients may scroll horizontally. The truncated source may contain an override.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. Non-Outlook parsers will treat it as junk tokens, so the `img.mso-size` rule, and possibly the adjacent `.mobileHeaderLogo` rule, can be dropped. Move the conditional comment outside the `<style>` block.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute. Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is also pointless in email.
- **JSON-LD:** the `DiscountOffer` has `"discountCode": ""` and the `PromotionCard` has no properties. Most clients strip `<script>` in email, so this is dead markup.
- **Weak title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 0 chars. This is a FAIL for deliverability and accessibility (no non-HTML fallback).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Cause:** the greeting isn't in the truncated source, so the cause can't be confirmed. It may be a recipient with no first name on file, or a test inbox with no profile.

## 7. Recommendations

4. **Mobile width (medium):** add a mobile override for `min-width:700px` and `width:700px` on the wrapper table, or use fluid or hybrid layout.
