# $10 off your $25 purchase + Kohl's Cash + Memorial Day Sale  = a full cart 🛒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth Memorial Day email in four days, and the wheels are coming off the strategy. The offer — $10 off $25 plus Kohl's Cash — is identical to what landed on May 22 ("$10 off your $25 purchase + Memorial Day Sale? Yes & yes 🙌"), and the creative skeleton is the same multi-tier discount ladder the brand has been running since mid-May. The subject line even uses the same dollar amounts, just rearranged into a different equation metaphor.
- To its credit, the email executes cleanly. The hero coupon badge is prominent, the discount ladder (50% → 30% → 20% → 25% → 50%) gives category shoppers a reason to scroll, and the "DRESS LIKE THE GOALS DEPEND ON IT" athletics section is the sharpest creative moment in the send. Kohl's Cash appears at both hero and footer, reinforcing the loyalty value.
- But a fourth consecutive Memorial Day blast with the same $10-off punchline doesn't feel like a campaign — it feels like a store that can't stop yelling. For subscribers who've already opened May 21, 22, and 23, this is friction, not value.

## What's working

- **Coupon badge placement is strong** — the $10 off / Kohl's Cash badge sits high-right in the hero where the eye naturally goes after the headline.
- **Multi-tier discount ladder** — 50%/30%/20%/25%/50% across categories gives different shopper types a reason to scroll; the savings feel varied rather than monolithic.
- **"DRESS LIKE THE GOALS DEPEND ON IT" break** — a concrete editorial theme amid the generic sale language; the athletic section stands out visually.
- **Kohl's Cash double-exposure** — both in the hero badge and the earn section at the bottom reinforces the loyalty benefit without feeling repetitive within the email itself.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout overlap, consistent typography throughout.

## What's weak

- **Same offer, fourth day in a row** — the $10 off $25 + Kohl's Cash equation has now appeared verbatim across May 21, 22, and today; a loyal subscriber has already made their decision.
- **Subject line is 75+ characters** — heavily truncated on mobile; the equation payoff ("= a full cart 🛒") almost certainly disappears in most inbox previews.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs throughout** — every section uses the same generic button copy regardless of category; a "Shop Bedding" or "Shop Sneakers" CTA would pull clicks from the right buyer.
- **Discount ladder flows oddly** — 50% → 30% → 20% → 25% → 50% is not a descending or ascending logic; a subscriber scanning top-to-bottom gets confused about what's actually the lead deal.
- **No explicit deadline visible in hero** — "Memorial Day" implies urgency but no "ends Monday" or specific date is surfaced in the hero block, softening the time-bound pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Hard-stop the cadence** — this should have been the final Memorial Day send, not the fourth. A consolidation email ("Last Day — everything in one place") with a fresh hook would outperform another reissue.
- 2. **Surface an explicit deadline in the hero** — add "Ends Monday" or "Today–Monday only" beneath the coupon badge; it converts urgency from implied to actionable.
- 3. **Shorten and sharpen the subject** — the current subject truncates on mobile before the payoff. Choose one: the dollar offer *or* the equation hook, not both stacked.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: $10 off $25 + 50% off storewide`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day ends tonight — $10 off your $25`
- 4. **Reorder the discount ladder** — lead with the deepest offer (50% off) and work down to maintain a sense of escalating discovery for scrollers, not a disjointed zigzag.
- 5. **Make CTAs category-specific** — "Shop Shoes," "Shop Home," "Shop Men's" lift click relevance and reduce the cognitive load of a "Shop Now" sea.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `50% off bedding, 30% off apparel, shoes, home — ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your $10 off coupon + up to 50% off — Memorial Day ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Memorial Day / $10 off $25" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional build with three separate tracking pixel domains and 22 click-redirect links. QA returned a 33% pass rate driven by missing compliance headers and pervasive missing alt attributes; one malformed closing tag in the pixel block is also present.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click links unverified** — all pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com`; QA skipped HTTP probes on redirect domains, so broken-link detection is blind for this send.

**Three distinct tracking/pixel domains in play:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath click tracker
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears open/render pixels
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM segment sync pixel: `d_sid=13245196`)

**Malformed HTML around the open-pixel block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?STNJUZDXYOSETNFMIUEZCFXBRM.60256&d=60256&bmt=0"
     width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The closing `</custom>` is an unknown/invalid tag — almost certainly a template variable that failed to resolve (e.g., `<custom>…</custom>` wrapper stripped mid-render). Browsers are lenient but this is a parser error and a signal of a broken template token.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts triple-loaded** — Roboto and Montserrat are requested via two `<link>` tags *and* a `@import` block inside `<style>`. This fires redundant network requests in clients that execute `<head>` CSS (most webmail). MSO conditional comments correctly guard against this for Outlook.

**10 content/tracking images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` (only `o.gif` has `aria-hidden`); content images missing alt text break screen-reader traversal.

| Image | Type | Missing |
|---|---|---|
| `c04f9bd7-…` (chp.kohls.com pixel) | Tracking | `alt=""` |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Open pixel | `alt=""` (aria-hidden present) |
| `event` (demdex.net) | AAM sync | `alt=""` |
| `2a5adc5a…`, `f441d45d…`, `c2f81e9a…`, `415c8a18…`, `69494b48…`, `78308d1d…`, `916d6fa0…` (mi.kohls.com) | Content renders | `alt=""` |

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents users from pinching to zoom on mobile. Accessibility violation under WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as a blank tab title in preview pane clients (some Samsung Mail, webmail). Should be the subject line or brand name.

**Empty favicon link** — `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` is a no-op but triggers a spurious HEAD request to the sender domain root in some clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

`mi_u=604230016` is a hardcoded subscriber ID present across all `mi.kohls.com` pixel URLs — this is expected (MessageGears injects at send time), not a merge-token failure.

`mi_ecmp=1013151_2026524` (campaign ID) is consistent across all pixels — no token bleed or mismatch detected.

No unrendered merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the truncated source. The `</custom>` artifact (section 2) is the only evidence of a template token that did not resolve cleanly.

`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` appears with an empty value in one pixel URL:
```
&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe…
```
This empty redemption-end-date param suggests a Kohl's Cash expiry date field was not populated for this send variant.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay; may be present at MTA level — needs raw header verification |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Not found; one-click unsubscribe required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk senders (>5k/day) since Feb 2024 |
| Physical mailing address | **WARN** | Not detected in truncated source; may be in footer of full HTML — must confirm |
| SPF/DKIM (`Authentication-Results`) | **WARN** | Header absent in relay capture; sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (subdomain of `kohls.com`) — alignment unverifiable |
| `noindex, nofollow` robots meta | Pass | Present and correct |
| Unsubscribe link in body | Unverifiable | Behind click-redirect; not probed by QA |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is the highest-severity compliance gap — Gmail now suppresses or tabs bulk mail from senders that don't support one-click unsubscribe.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**`no-referrer` meta breaks analytics attribution:**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">
```
All outbound clicks will arrive at `kohls.com` with no `Referer` header. If Kohl's analytics relies on referrer for email channel attribution (rather than UTM params alone), clicks will be misclassified as Direct traffic.

**UTM params unverifiable** — all 22 links route through click-redirect domains that QA did not probe. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended at redirect resolution cannot be confirmed from this source alone. The `email_name=260524_DG_Email_Sunday` param in pixel URLs confirms campaign-level tagging exists in the MI system, but that is server-side tracking, not URL-based analytics.

---
