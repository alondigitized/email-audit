# Save 20% | Score fall-ready styles during the Season's Best Shoe Sale 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth or seventh Kohl's send in a week with the same shell. The top banner reads "20% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18." Below it are the green Kohl's Cash strip and the red Home / Women / Men / Kids nav buttons. The only new part is the shoe-sale hero, which is a decent collage of a pink sneaker, a white Nike and dark loafers. It carries "Featuring up to 40%."
- The subject promises "Score fall-ready styles during the Season's Best Shoe Sale." The email doesn't deliver that focus. The shoe hero takes about one screen. After it come pajamas, a Clearance 70% banner, a "Picked with you in mind" grid of a Ninja drink maker, towels, a mattress topper and jeans, and then two barcode coupons. A shoe-intent reader gets pulled into a general department-store flyer.
- The email is competent, but it is also noisy and repetitive. It has three different percentages (20%, 40%, 70%), duplicated nav blocks, and a long fine-print footer. Nothing here is a reason to open today's send over yesterday's.

## What's working

- The shoe hero is clean. The three-shoe stack is visually strong, and the "up to 40%" offer sits beside it.
- The 20% off coupon banner sits at the very top. The deadline (Oct. 18) is credible and visible.
- The coupon codes are in the body. YOUGET20 and 10HOME are shown with barcodes, PINs and end dates, so the offer is easy to redeem.
- The Kohl's Rewards status line ("Kohl's Rewards member, earn 5% rewards") and the Kohl's Cash strip add loyalty value.
- The free-returns footer ("Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027") reduces purchase risk.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a shoe sale, but shoes get one block of a very long email. Pajamas, clearance, a blender, towels and a mattress topper dilute it.
- The email stacks 20%, up to 40% and up to 70% with "coupons do not apply to select brands" caveats. The hierarchy is muddy, and the 40% line has the exclusion text right under it.
- Nav-button blocks repeat three times (Home/Women/Men/Kids twice, plus Women/Men/Girls/Boys). That is a lot of repeated buttons.
- The shoe hero has no product detail, such as a price, a named style or a brand. Only the generic "Shop Now" link is there.
- The footer fine print is long and dense. It undercuts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the shoe sale the whole email. Use the hero, then four category tiles (boots, sneakers, loafers, clogs) with real prices, and cut the pajamas, clearance, kitchen and towels. That would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Pick one headline offer. Lead with "40% off shoes" and move the 20% coupon to a slim strip.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop boots & sneakers."
- 4. Collapse the nav buttons to one set.
- 5. Show 2 to 3 named shoes with prices and star ratings, such as the Nike Court Vision at a sale price.
- 6. Don't repeat the same subject frame ("Save 20% | …") after a week of the same line. Try a subject built on the shoe offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off shoes, plus an extra 20% 👟`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Boots, sneakers & loafers: shoe sale ends Oct. 18`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your extra 20% coupon works on top. Plus earn Kohl's Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall boots, white sneakers and loafers, marked down through Oct. 18.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20% | Score fall-ready styles during the Season's Best Shoe Sale 👉"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has several validity and accessibility defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a viewport that blocks zoom. Many content images also lack alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay and truncation limits. None of them can be confirmed as real failures from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 69 tracking links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, `click.chp.kohls.com`) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are untested.
- **Four tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/72f3c079-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`, and the Adobe Audience Manager beacon `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Duplicated open tracking is redundant and adds third-party requests. The `demdex` call is cross-domain.
- **Malformed markup on the open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no matching opener.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed):** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and the product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58380081_0_2x` and `58379268_2_2x`. If these images are blocked or read by a screen reader, the content is lost.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel. The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` pixel and the `demdex` beacon do lack `alt`.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will move it into `<body>`, and email clients ignore the microdata anyway.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` prefix, so it matches nothing. It should be `.ReadMsgBody`.
- **Duplicate meta tag:** `format-detection` appears twice. This is harmless, but it is a cleanup item.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via `<link>` and `@import`. Gmail strips both, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is declared, so this is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.
- A subscriber identifier is embedded in tracking URLs: `mi_u=604230016`, plus `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on the open pixels. This is normal ESP behavior, but those URLs should be treated as personal data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Without raw headers this can't be called a sender defect. Check the original message in a direct-delivery mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Physical address:** the QA check found none. The source is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source and unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible. Attribution runs through the encoded `qs=` redirect payload and `mi_*` parameters (`mi_ecmp=1023907_20261015`, `email_name=261015_DG_Email_Thursd...`), which are consistent across assets.
- Final landing URLs can't be checked because the probe skipped all 69 tracked links. Whether the "Season's Best Shoe Sale" landing page matches the 20% offer is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the two `images.am.kohls.com` product images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fill in `<title>`, remove the stray `</custom>`, and drop or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
4. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`, or remove it.
5. Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
6. Consolidate the duplicate open pixels, and reconsider the `demdex` beacon if the privacy footprint matters.
7. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated source and on raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`). Also probe the redirect destinations of a sample of tracked links, since the compliance and continuity warnings can't be settled from this data.
