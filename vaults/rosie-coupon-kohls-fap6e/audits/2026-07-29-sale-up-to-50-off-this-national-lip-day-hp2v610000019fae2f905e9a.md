# SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day 💄
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Sephora Lip Day send this week where the subject and hero actually agree: "SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day" opens to a hero banner that literally reads "Get up to 50% off Select Lip Picks." Compare that to 07-25 through 07-28, where every single day promised one thing in the subject and delivered something else in the hero — this send finally closes that gap.
- But the honesty problem hasn't fully disappeared, it's just gotten quieter. Scan the 8 products in the grid and the best discount on offer is MAC at 40% off ($25→$15) — nothing in this email actually hits the "up to 50%" the subject and hero both promise. It's a softer version of the same overpromise that's dogged this campaign all week.
- Execution-wise this is the cleanest send of the run: tight 2-column grid, consistent pricing format, loyalty tier bar, no render bugs. The flaw is smaller than prior days but it's still there, and this is at least the fifth lip-themed Sephora email in seven days, which is real fatigue for anyone who's been getting all of them.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy are aligned for the first time this week — both say "up to 50% off" lip picks, no bait-and-switch on the headline claim.
- Clean 8-product, 2-column grid with clear strike-through/sale pricing on every item.
- Single unambiguous "SHOP NOW" CTA button sits directly under the hero offer, no scrolling needed.
- Beauty Insider points counter and tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) give a loyalty touchpoint.
- No visual bugs — images, pricing, and layout all render cleanly top to bottom.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50% off" is never actually delivered — the top discount shown is 40% off (MAC), so the headline number is still inflated, just less egregiously than earlier in the week.
- This is at minimum the 5th lip-focused Sephora send since 07-24 — a subscriber who's opened the last few is seeing the same beat again.
- CTA copy is the generic "SHOP NOW," repeated three times with no product- or category-specific language.
- Zero reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere on the 8 featured products.
- The lower half (Dae mousse, Touchland/Biodance "$35 and under," Buy More Save More) pulls focus away from the Lip Day theme the subject sold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline number — change hero copy to "up to 40% off" (or lead with the MAC deal specifically) so the subject/hero claim matches what's actually in the grid. This is the one thing still undermining an otherwise honest send.
- 2. Swap "Shop Now" for category-specific CTA copy like "Shop Lip Deals" to sharpen click intent and differentiate from the generic buttons used in the cross-sell blocks below.
- 3. Add star ratings under the top 2-3 products (MAC, Charlotte Tilbury) — easy social-proof lift with no layout rework.
- 4. Cut or move the non-lip cross-sell (Dae, Touchland/Biodance) out of this send — given five straight days of lip promos, adding unrelated categories on top just adds noise instead of relief.
- 5. Give the lip sale itself a visible end date/time, the way the "Buy More, Save More" block already does ("Ends 8/2") — right now the flagship offer has no credible urgency.
