# Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash + free shipping = WOW 🎉
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eighth Deal Days email in four days, and it arrives with the one escalation the campaign has been saving: a "TODAY ONLY" instant Kohl's Cash offer — $20 earned on the spot when you spend $50+ — placed at the very top of the email. That's the right creative call. It's the first genuinely time-gated loyalty lever in this entire run, and it distinguishes this send from the preceding seven in a way "new deals daily" never could.
- The problem is everything below the hero is a copy-paste of the previous seven sends. The same Deal Days template, the same "FEATURED DAILY DEALS" header, the same six-pod product grid, the same category nav strip. Subscribers who opened any prior send are looking at a familiar frame with a mutation at the top. "WOW 🎉" in the subject line is the sixth or seventh emoji exclamation in four days from this brand — it no longer signals anything.
- The instant Kohl's Cash offer is strong enough to partially rescue this send on click-through. But at this cadence, even a genuine escalation is fighting through inbox scar tissue to land.

## What's working

- **The TODAY ONLY Kohl's Cash hero is the right escalation.** Compared to the June 25 sends ("Daily Deals AND free shipping? Say less 🤗"), this is the first hard same-day expiry on the loyalty reward — real urgency where previous sends had none.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** The Kohl's Cash banner leads, the "Shop Now" button follows naturally, the eye doesn't have to hunt.
- **Concrete dollar-amount offer.** "$20 Kohl's Cash instantly when you spend $50+" is specific and credible — not "up to X% off everything," which is vague.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, text is legible, layout holds.

## What's weak

- **Eight sends, one template.** The Deal Days section below the Kohl's Cash hero is structurally identical to every prior send. This email reads as a hero swap on a recycled shell.
- **Subject buries the lead.** "Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash + free shipping = WOW 🎉" is 57 characters of serial listing. "INSTANT Kohl's Cash" is the only novel hook — it's surrounded by noise.
- **"WOW 🎉" has no signal left.** Between this and June 25's "Say less 🤗" and "🤩" and "🙌," the emoji-exclamation tail has become wallpaper.
- **No personalization anywhere.** At send eight, still no category segmentation, no name, no past-behavior signal. The entire list gets the same grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the Kohl's Cash — strip everything else.** The TODAY ONLY hook is strong enough to carry the subject alone. The free-shipping callout belongs in the preheader.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: Get $20 Kohl's Cash instantly (spend $50+)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Instant $20 Kohl's Cash — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping + daily deals inside. Expires tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deal Days keeps rolling — your $20 KC won't wait.`
- 2. **Make the expiry concrete.** "TODAY ONLY" is soft. "Expires tonight at 11:59 PM CT" with a visible countdown or timestamp turns the hero into a genuine action trigger.
- 3. **Retire the Deal Days template for this send.** If the subject promises a Kohl's Cash event, build the email around it: hero banner → two category tiles relevant to the subscriber's history → single footer CTA. Drop the full six-pod grid that every subscriber has already ignored twice.
- 4. **Segment the product grid or cut it.** If segmentation isn't available, a tighter edit (two or three spotlights instead of six pods) reads fresher than the same grid. Fewer pods, higher confidence in each one.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Kohl's eighth Deal Days email in four days, and it arrives with the one escalation the campaign has been saving: a "TODAY ONLY" instant Kohl's Cash offer — $20 earned on the spot when you spend $50+ — placed at the very top of the email. That's the right creative call. It's the first genuinely time-gated loyalty lever in this entire run, and it distinguishes this send from the preceding seven in a way "new deals daily" never could.

The problem is everything below the hero is a copy-paste of the previous seven sends. The same Deal Days template, the same "FEATURED DAILY DEALS" header, the same six-pod product grid, the same category nav strip. Subscribers who opened any prior send are looking at a familiar frame with a mutation at the top. "WOW 🎉" in the subject line is the sixth or seventh emoji exclamation in four days from this brand — it no longer signals anything.

The instant Kohl's Cash offer is strong enough to partially rescue this send on click-through. But at this cadence, even a genuine escalation is fighting through inbox scar tissue to land.

## 2. What worked

- **The TODAY ONLY Kohl's Cash hero is the right escalation.** Compared to the June 25 sends ("Daily Deals AND free shipping? Say less 🤗"), this is the first hard same-day expiry on the loyalty reward — real urgency where previous sends had none.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** The Kohl's Cash banner leads, the "Shop Now" button follows naturally, the eye doesn't have to hunt.
- **Concrete dollar-amount offer.** "$20 Kohl's Cash instantly when you spend $50+" is specific and credible — not "up to X% off everything," which is vague.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, text is legible, layout holds.

## 3. What didn't

- **Eight sends, one template.** The Deal Days section below the Kohl's Cash hero is structurally identical to every prior send. This email reads as a hero swap on a recycled shell.
- **Subject buries the lead.** "Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash + free shipping = WOW 🎉" is 57 characters of serial listing. "INSTANT Kohl's Cash" is the only novel hook — it's surrounded by noise.
- **"WOW 🎉" has no signal left.** Between this and June 25's "Say less 🤗" and "🤩" and "🙌," the emoji-exclamation tail has become wallpaper.
- **No personalization anywhere.** At send eight, still no category segmentation, no name, no past-behavior signal. The entire list gets the same grid.

## 4. What I'd change

1. **Lead the subject with the Kohl's Cash — strip everything else.** The TODAY ONLY hook is strong enough to carry the subject alone. The free-shipping callout belongs in the preheader.
   - **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: Get $20 Kohl's Cash instantly (spend $50+)`
   - **Subject Alt B:** `Instant $20 Kohl's Cash — today only`
   - **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping + daily deals inside. Expires tonight.`
   - **Preheader Alt B:** `Deal Days keeps rolling — your $20 KC won't wait.`

2. **Make the expiry concrete.** "TODAY ONLY" is soft. "Expires tonight at 11:59 PM CT" with a visible countdown or timestamp turns the hero into a genuine action trigger.

3. **Retire the Deal Days template for this send.** If the subject promises a Kohl's Cash event, build the email around it: hero banner → two category tiles relevant to the subscriber's history → single footer CTA. Drop the full six-pod grid that every subscriber has already ignored twice.

4. **Segment the product grid or cut it.** If segmentation isn't available, a tighter edit (two or three spotlights instead of six pods) reads fresher than the same grid. Fewer pods, higher confidence in each one.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Daily Deals + INSTANT Kohl's Cash" (2026-06-26)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a well-structured HTML 4.01 table-based build with standard multi-ESP tracking infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears + Adobe Audience Manager). The primary issues are missing alt attributes on tracking/pixel images, unresolvable compliance headers at the relay layer, and a malformed HTML tag in the open-tracker block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but these could not be validated.

**Three separate open-tracking pixels in use simultaneously:**
- `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/9651f9a6...` (Coherent Path)
- `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif...` (MessageGears)
- `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?HKAXRARP...` (separate ESP)

Triple-firing open pixels inflates open rate metrics and may cause discrepancies across platforms. Confirm this is intentional deduplication strategy, not a copy-paste error from template assembly.

**Malformed HTML tag in open-tracker block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
`</custom>` is an invalid closing tag. No matching opening tag exists. Parsers will handle this gracefully, but it signals a template authoring error.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). These include both tracking pixels and apparently content images:

| Image | Type | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| `9651f9a6...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | Open pixel | Low — aria-hidden acceptable |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Open pixel | Has `aria-hidden="true"` ✓ |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel | Low |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Likely content images | **High** — if these are product/banner images, screen readers are blind |
| `52819850_roots_2x` (images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/) | Product image | **High** — product name not readable |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa08...` | Unknown | Medium |

The `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/52819850_roots_2x` URL pattern confirms at least one is a product image, not a pixel — missing alt text here is a real accessibility failure.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a known accessibility regression; WCAG 1.4.4 requires content to be resizable up to 200%.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>`. Some email clients display this in the tab or subject preview area.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** — `<link>` tag + `<link>` tag + `@import` for the same Roboto and Montserrat URLs. Redundant, adds latency in webfont-capable clients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source (no `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, or similar unfired placeholders visible).

The `mi_ecmp` parameter in pixel URLs (`mi_ecmp=1016002_2026626`) and `email_name` parameter (`email_name=260626_DG_Email_Friday`) are populated, indicating campaign tagging fired correctly at send time.

**No issues found** in this category based on available source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA relay did not surface it. If absent at the MTA level, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native unsubscribe button. This is a deliverability risk for bulk senders under Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Google/Yahoo for senders of >5k/day. Absence here is a compliance gap if not present at MTA level.

**No physical mailing address detected in source** — CAN-SPAM §7(d) mandates a valid physical postal address in the email body. This could be in the truncated portion of the HTML, but QA did not find it in what was parsed. Verify it appears above the fold of the footer in the full render.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unknown via the relay. Sending domain `s.kohls.com` should have DKIM configured; confirm at MTA.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are present** — correct practice for email HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

All 22 click links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains — UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain, which was skipped by QA probe.

The `mi_ecmp` tracking parameter (`1016002_2026626`) is consistently applied to pixel URLs, suggesting a coherent campaign ID, but whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) survive the click redirect to kohls.com cannot be verified from this source alone.

**Recommendation:** Run one sample link through the redirect chain manually to confirm UTM parameters are appended to the final kohls.com destination URL.
