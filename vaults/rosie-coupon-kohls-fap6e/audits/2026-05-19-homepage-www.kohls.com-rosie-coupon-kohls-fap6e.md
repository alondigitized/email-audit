# Homepage snapshot · www.kohls.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, I drove all the way to the digital mall and the door is locked — Kohl's slapped me with an "Access Denied" page before I even got inside. There's no hero, no Kohl's Cash reminder, no pretty Sephora display, nothing. Just a cold error message and a reference number that means absolutely nothing to me.

## What's working

- The error text is at least readable — large, high-contrast black on white, so I can technically understand what happened without squinting.

## What's weak

- **The entire page is an error block** — "Access Denied. You don't have permission to access http://www.kohls.com/ on this server." I can't see a single product, offer, or navigation element.
- **No fallback or redirect** — there is no "Try again," no link back home, no "Visit our app" button, nothing. I am just stranded.
- **The reference number** (`#18.eea5dc17.1779195386.4ad16e0`) and the raw EdgeSuite URL look like tech gobbledygook to me — I have no idea what to do with that.
- **Completely blank canvas below the error text** — the rest of the page is pure white emptiness, which makes this feel even more broken and abandoned.
- **Zero brand identity** — no Kohl's logo, no color, no friendly copy, nothing to reassure me I'm even in the right place.

## Recommendations

- **Show a branded error page** — if you're going to block traffic, at least put the Kohl's logo, a friendly "Something went wrong on our end" message, and a big red "Go to Kohl's Home" button so I don't just close the tab.
- **Offer the app as a fallback** — "Having trouble? Try the Kohl's app" with a download link would save the session for mobile users like me.
- **Fix the bot/geo detection** — if I'm a real customer hitting this from a phone and getting blocked by Akamai, that is a conversion-killing false positive that needs to be tuned immediately.
- **Add a customer service escape hatch** — a phone number or chat link on the error page so loyal customers (like me, who has a Yes2You account!) can still get help instead of walking away frustrated.
