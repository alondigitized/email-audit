# Get 20% off | Perfect time to find new favorites ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll catch-all, and the hero does not deliver on the subject. The subject promises "Get 20% off", but the top of the render shows only a small "Take an extra 20%" banner. It sits over a cropped green sweater photo, with "Ends Nov. 21 / Shop Now" and a "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" badge. A "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn 3% Rewards" strip is a real personalization touch, but it competes for attention at the very top.
- After the hero, the email loses focus. It runs through a Gift Shop block ("Gifts $25 & Under"), stocking socks, a $10-off toys coupon with Grinch, Cuddl Duds at 20%, an "Online Only Daily Deals" banner, a "Preview the Deals Now" teaser, Klarna, pickup and delivery strips, and a four-product grid. Each module is competent, but nothing is the one idea. Like the Nov 16 "Get 20% off" send, the opening promise is stronger than the creative that follows.
- Judgment: workable and brand-consistent, but cluttered. The coupon stack at the bottom (20% off, $10 off $50 home, $10 off $50 toys, Kohl's Cash) is the real value, and it is buried below roughly eight modules.

## What's working

- The Rewards status strip at the top ("Rewards Member | Earn 3% Rewards") gives personalization and loyalty visibility.
- The "$25 & Under" gift tile with For Her / For Him / For Teens / For Kids is a clear, seasonal navigation aid.
- The product grid shows concrete prices with strike-through originals (e.g. $299.99 Ninja, $44.97 jeans). That is honest and easy to scan.
- The coupon section at the bottom has scannable barcodes, codes, and "Ends Nov. 21" deadlines, which makes the offers redeemable.
- The seasonal hooks (gifts, stockings, early Black Friday preview) match the date.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is weak. "Take an extra 20% off" is small text over a busy photo, and the 20% in the subject is not the dominant element.
- The email is a very long scroll with at least eight competing modules and no single hero product or CTA.
- The tone swings between "Online Only Daily Deals", "Preview the DEALS Now / Come back Nov. 22-26", and the 20% promo. Mixing "ends Nov. 21" with "come back Nov. 22" splits urgency.
- The Klarna, pickup, delivery, and "Shop made simple" strips add service clutter between the deals and the products.
- The fine print and disclaimers appear in several places ("Coupons do not apply to select brands", "Not valid on Sephora"). This works against the offer feeling clean.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: a large "20% OFF" with a single high-contrast "Shop Now" button and the coupon code SAVEMORE visible in the first screen.
- 2. Move the coupon stack (20% off, $10 off $50 home, $10 off $50 toys) directly under the hero instead of at the bottom.
- 3. Cut the module count to about four: hero, gifts under $25, a product grid, and the coupons. Move Klarna, pickup, and delivery into a slim footer strip.
- 4. Resolve the deadline messaging. Lead with one date (Nov. 21) and tease Nov. 22 only in a single small line.
- 5. Put one hero product with a review or rating line near the top, to give the email social proof it currently lacks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends Nov. 21 — code SAVEMORE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% + $10 off $50 inside 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus gifts under $25 and Kohl's Cash on $50 — all through Nov. 21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 3 coupons before Friday's early Black Friday deals drop.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is mostly sound: table layout, `lang="en"`, a Montserrat/Arial fallback stack and an Outlook VML namespace. It has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, zoom disabled) and widespread missing `alt` attributes. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers were not captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links unverified.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect URLs (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`). Destination validity and redirect health are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Four 1x1 tracking beacons** load before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9e089e79-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026117_20261118`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel.** `<img src=".../open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened. Parsers will drop it, but it is invalid.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** has little effect in most clients. It could suppress referrer data on landing, which affects attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 12 images** (from the QA output):
  - Content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, `59781016_Product_hgdt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF`, `60699446_4_2x`, `60699446_3_2x`.
  - Beacons: the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel and the `demdex` pixel. These two have no `alt` at all, and only the `mi.kohls.com` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"`.
  - For the content images, missing `alt` means screen readers read out the filename or URL, and nothing shows when images are blocked.
  - The beacons should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.** An empty `href` can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with nested `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head at the `<div>` and move the following content into the body.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`** on the 1x1 beacon `<table>` and the AAM table. The main layout tables do have it.
- **Web fonts** are loaded via `@import` and `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is safely guarded, and the Arial/Helvetica fallback covers clients that ignore it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source.
- Image URLs embed a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u=604230016`). This is a tracking ID, not a rendering defect.
- The source is truncated, so tokens in the body and footer could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domains differ (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`).
- The visible in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be confirmed because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `href` values visible in the truncated source are redirect-wrapped (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), so final landing URLs and any UTM parameters cannot be verified.
- Campaign identification appears to use `mi_ecmp=1026117_20261118` and an `email_name=261118_DG_Email_Wednes...` value on image URLs rather than UTMs. Whether these carry through to the landing pages is unverified.
- The 74 skipped link probes mean there is no evidence on landing-page status or on whether the "20% off" offer reaches the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the hero GIF and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Add `alt=""` to the beacons.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`, or replace it with JSON-LD or a body-level block. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag.
4. Populate `<title>` and remove or populate the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
5. Re-run the audit with the raw RFC 822 headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for the 74 tracked links to verify the landing URLs and any UTMs.
7. Consolidate the four tracking beacons if they are redundant, to reduce the number of image requests.
