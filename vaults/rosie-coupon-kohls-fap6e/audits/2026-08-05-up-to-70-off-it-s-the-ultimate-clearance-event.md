# Up to 70% off? It's the ULTIMATE Clearance Event 👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, technically solid Kohl's render — no clipped starburst graphics, no garbled art, nav buttons and product tiles all line up correctly. But the content doesn't back up the headline. The hero promises "Save up to 70%" sitewide clearance, then the entire body pivots into a Home-category showcase (bath towels at 40% off, throws starting at $12.99, and a brand-new Martha Stewart cookware/kitchen launch) with nothing in the body actually substantiating a 70%-off item. That's the same disconnect flagged in the 2026-08-03 "🚨 Save up to 70% during the ULTIMATE Clearance Event 🚨" send — a big clearance number up top, then a body that reads like a Home-department newsletter.
- Worse, this is functionally a rerun of that same 08-03 theme two days later, using nearly identical hero copy and structure. A subscriber who opened both would notice the repeat instantly, and "Introducing Martha Stewart Collection" — a new product launch — sits awkwardly inside an email framed around clearing out old stock.
- Net: well-produced, poorly targeted. Solid execution, weak strategy. Lands mid-pack, in line with the 08-03 send it echoes.

## What's working

- Hero banner is legible and hierarchy is obvious — red block, bold "70%," white "Shop Now" button, no visual clutter.
- Four flat nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) give an easy path into the site right below the hero.
- Rewards status bar ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | Earn 5% REWARDS") is visible immediately, reinforcing loyalty value.
- Individual product blocks (bath towels, throws, Martha Stewart set) each carry real pricing detail (e.g., "Reg. $14.99-$17.99"), not vague copy.
- Footer is fully clean — social icons, app badges, and legal text render without breakage.

## What's weak

- The "up to 70% off" headline is never backed up anywhere in the body — the actual visible discounts are 40% off bath and a flat $12.99 throw price, a meaningful gap between promise and proof.
- "Introducing Martha Stewart Collection" is a new-launch callout wedged into a clearance-themed email — it contradicts the "everything must go" framing of the hero.
- This is the second "Ultimate Clearance Event" send in three days (after 08-03's "🚨 Save up to 70% during the ULTIMATE Clearance Event 🚨") with a near-identical hero — noticeable repetition for anyone who reads both.
- No urgency mechanism anywhere (no countdown, no "ends tonight") despite "event" framing implying a limited window.
- "Summer picks that get invited back next year" is a generic evergreen tile grid, unrelated to the clearance premise, diluting the email's single-offer focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real 60-70%-off item directly under the hero — right now the biggest number in the email appears once and is never proven. If 70% is the anchor, the first product block should hit close to it.
- 2. Pull the Martha Stewart launch into its own send — a "new collection" story undercuts a clearance narrative and should run separately from "everything must go" messaging.
- 3. Add a visible deadline (e.g., "Ends Sunday") to the hero — nothing here currently signals urgency despite "Event" branding.
- 4. Space this out from the 08-03 send — running the same "Ultimate Clearance Event" hero twice in a week reads as repetition, not escalation.
- **Subject Alt A:** `70% off clearance: bath, throws, kitchen & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ultimate Clearance ends Sunday — up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real markdowns across Home, Bath & Kitchen — before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40-70% off select styles. Shop before restock.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — Kohl's "Ultimate Clearance Event" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSO/Outlook-conditional HTML table layout with heavy third-party tracking (CoherentPath/mi.kohls.com, Adobe AAM) but is missing baseline CAN-SPAM/authentication signals and alt text across all tracked images; one malformed tag was also found in the tracking beacon block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 64 tracking/redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (click.chp.kohls.com, click.s.kohls.com, mi.kohls.com domains) — destinations could not be verified as live/non-404.
- Open-tracking is implemented via a fake 1x1 `<img>` pointing at a `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` **click**-redirect endpoint (not a dedicated open-tracking gif), e.g.:
  `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/156759c0-b8a2-4cfb-af7c-fd99b0b2a5c4?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v3.eJyFkD9LA0EQxbP3L...`
  This forces an HTTP redirect just to register an open, adding unnecessary latency/failure surface versus a static pixel.
- Malformed markup around the secondary open-tracking pixel — a stray closing tag with no matching opener:
  `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?XCZUKV57C7JE7DHA6L5ETCGLCE.60264&d=60264&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`
  The `</custom>` tag is orphaned and should be removed; most mail clients will silently ignore it, but it indicates unclean template output.
- Multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting/personalization pixels carry an empty query parameter, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (redemption end date), suggesting a merge field failed to populate for this send. `mi_kc_event_pen` is truncated in the sample and could not be fully evaluated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty — will show a blank browser-tab/preview title in webmail clients that render it.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href` — a no-op/broken favicon reference.
- Every tracked image in the QA scan is missing `alt` text (7 confirmed instances: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` beacon, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`, and four `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels). These are 1x1 tracking pixels, so the missing alt has no visual impact, but screen readers/accessibility scanners will still flag them; standard practice is `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (already present on one, `o.gif`, but not the others).
- `html lang="en"` and a responsive viewport meta are correctly present. Media queries for sub-500px viewports are defined (`.container`, `.w_50pc`, etc.), so mobile responsiveness scaffolding looks intact from what's visible.
- `noindex, nofollow` and `no-referrer` meta tags are set — expected for transactional/marketing mail, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `{{merge_tag}}`-style placeholders are visible in the truncated source.
- One likely personalization failure: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (redemption end date) renders empty in 4 of the tracking pixel URLs — if this field is meant to carry a promo end-date for attribution/reporting, it is not populating.
- `email_name=260805_DG_Email_Wednesda` (truncated) indicates the send is correctly dated/named for 2026-08-05, consistent with a Wednesday deploy naming convention — no anomaly there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header: missing.** Not detected by automated QA — may be a relay/capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence, but cannot be confirmed from the HTML alone since unsubscribe headers live in the message headers, not the body.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header: missing.** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed present; if truly absent, this violates Gmail/Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements for high-volume senders.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the scanned HTML — CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. This may be present in the truncated/un-scanned footer portion of the template, but the automated check did not find it in what was analyzed.
- **Authentication-Results header: missing** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm sender authentication compliance from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on any visible link. Kohl's uses a proprietary parameter scheme instead (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`), consistent with a CoherentPath/martech-driven ESP rather than GA-style UTM tracking.
- The actual CTA/product links were not present in the truncated HTML sample (only tracking/AAM/open pixels were visible), so landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the available source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the orphaned `</custom>` tag after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
2. Add `alt=""` (and `aria-hidden="true"` where missing) to all tracking pixels to pass accessibility scans cleanly.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject line or brand name for correct webmail tab/preview rendering.
4. Remove or populate the empty `favicon` `href`.
5. Investigate why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` renders empty in the retargeting pixels — likely a merge-field/data-feed gap in the personalization system.
6. Confirm (outside this HTML-only scan) that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and a physical address are present in the actual delivered message — if genuinely absent, these are compliance blockers, not just QA-relay artifacts.
7. Re-run the audit against the full (non-truncated) HTML to verify CTA link destinations and confirm email-to-site landing page alignment.
