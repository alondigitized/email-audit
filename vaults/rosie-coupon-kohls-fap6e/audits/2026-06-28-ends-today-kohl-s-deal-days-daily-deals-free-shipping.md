# Ends today: Kohl's Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE shipping ⏳
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's twelfth Deal Days email in six days, and it arrives on the actual final day of the campaign — which means "Ends today" is finally, legitimately true. That's the one thing this send has going for it that none of the prior eleven did: real closing urgency. The "6 DAYS OF DEALS" sub-section in the body even leans into the campaign retrospective framing, which is a reasonable editorial choice for a finale send.
- The problem is that Kohl's burned through all its urgency ammo on days one through five. Every prior send has had some variation of "today only / ends tonight / better hurry" language, so by day twelve the subscriber's trust in "Ends today" is near zero. The creative is structurally identical to the prior eleven: KOHL'S DEAL DAYS hero banner, Instant Kohl's Cash bar, category nav strip, stacked deal tiles with "Shop Now" CTAs. There is nothing in this send that signals "finale" — no countdown clock, no "last chance" hero, no cumulative savings callout that rewards the loyal subscriber who's been reading all week.
- The email itself is competently assembled and renders cleanly. The issue is cadence-induced immunity, not creative execution. If a subscriber has already decided to buy, this will convert. If they haven't clicked in six days, nothing here gives them a new reason to start.

## What's working

- "6 DAYS OF DEALS" section is the only creative beat that's new — it frames the final day as a campaign close rather than just another daily send.
- Instant Kohl's Cash and Super REWARDS Cash both visible — loyalty reinforcement is present for members who track their earnings.
- The 60% off deal tile is the strongest visual in the email; high discount depth draws the eye even on a small render.
- Free shipping callout is persistent throughout — reliable conversion lever that the persona responds to.
- Date-stamped "Friday, June 28" under the daily deals header ties the content to a specific day, reinforcing the "ends today" claim.

## What's weak

- The hero is identical to eleven previous sends — a subscriber who opened even two of those emails gets no new visual signal that this is the finale.
- "Ends today" urgency has been so thoroughly worn out by this campaign that it will read as noise, not as a genuine deadline, regardless of whether it's actually true this time.
- Subject line is cluttered with three separate offers joined by plus signs ("Deal Days + Daily Deals + FREE shipping") — it reads like a stack dump, not a single compelling hook.
- "Shop Now" CTA copy appears uniformly across every deal tile — no product-specific language, no urgency baked into the button.
- No countdown clock or hour-specific deadline visible anywhere — a legitimate final-day email should show "Ends at midnight" or similar.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rebuild the hero as a finale, not another daily deal.** Replace the generic DEAL DAYS banner with "Last 24 hours — up to 60% off + free shipping ends midnight" and add a live countdown. This single change earns back credibility for the "Ends today" claim.
- 2. **Collapse the subject to one dominant hook.** Three offers joined with "+" dilutes all three. Pick the deepest deal (60% off) or the loyalty hook (Instant Kohl's Cash) and lead with it.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTA copy per tile.** "Shop Women's 60% off" beats "Shop Now" every time — it confirms the specific deal and the target audience in the button itself.
- 4. **Add a closing loyalty callout above the footer.** "You've had 6 days to shop — here's a look at the Kohl's Cash you could have stacked" is a simple personalization hook that rewards the engaged subscriber and creates FOMO for the non-clicker.
- 5. **Tighten the deal tile count.** A finale email doesn't need eight stacked tiles — three hero deals at large size would drive more focused attention than eight at small size.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 60% off + free shipping ends tonight ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days finale — 40–60% off + free shipping, last day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Six days of deals close at midnight — grab free shipping before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at Instant Kohl's Cash + today's deepest discounts.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard table-layout HTML build using Kohl's CoherentPath/MessageGears stack with functional responsive breakpoints. The primary technical concerns are missing alt text on 10+ images (including tracking pixels), unverifiable compliance headers through the relay, and one malformed HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears). Destination URLs could not be verified. No broken links confirmed, but no live resolution was possible.

**Three separate open-tracking pixels fired:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9d649013-...` (CoherentPath open pixel)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (MessageGears open pixel)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?P7QAXPZVK7CEXJBQO6JOQVLVWM...` (MessageGears second open pixel)

Three concurrent open-tracking pixels is redundant and inflates open rate metrics across platforms. Likely a dual-platform send artifact.

**Additional tracking pixel:** `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync pixel firing on open.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML tag** in the open-tracking block:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid and unclosed. In lenient parsers this is silently ignored, but it can cause downstream DOM parse errors in strict validators and some ESPs.

**10 images missing alt text** (QA confirmed). Affected assets include:
- All three open-tracking pixels (`9d649013-...`, `o.gif`, `event`)
- Six content/rendition images at `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` (hero and product images)

Tracking pixels with `alt=""` is acceptable — but the `aria-hidden="true"` attribute is only present on one of the three pixels (`o.gif`). The CoherentPath pixel (`9d649013-...`) and the MessageGears open pixel (`open.aspx`) lack both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`, which means screen readers may announce them.

The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` content images missing alt text are a real accessibility gap — these appear to be visible product/promotional images based on their naming convention (`260628_DG_Email_Sunday`).

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 failure (Resize Text).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=604230016`** — subscriber ID is present and populated in all `mi.kohls.com` tracking URLs. No unfired/raw merge tokens detected in the truncated source.

The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter is blank. If this is a redemption end date field, it fired unfilled. This is low-risk for an image URL parameter but indicates a suppressed or missing data value in the send context.

The absence here is likely a relay capture gap rather than a true header omission, but cannot be confirmed from available data.
