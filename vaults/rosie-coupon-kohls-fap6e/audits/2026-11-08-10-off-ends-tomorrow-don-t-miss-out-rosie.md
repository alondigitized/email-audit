# 10% off ends TOMORROW ⚠️Don’t miss out, Rosie!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a Sephora Savings Event send with too many competing messages. The subject promises "10% off ends TOMORROW," but the top banner leads with a different offer: free same-day delivery with a $75 spend and code SPEEDY. Below it are a countdown timer, a 30% Sephora Collection medallion and a 10% Insider medallion, and a "Holiday Value Sets" hero. Further down are a free-gift offer (a Parchute headband and washcloth set at $100, code UNREADY), a Gift of Beauty tile, a fragrance tile and a gift guide. A subscriber gets about six offers and three promo codes (SPEEDY, ALLONSALE, UNREADY) in one scroll.
- The urgency is credible. The "FINAL HOURS" countdown and the "ends tomorrow" subject agree, which is rare for this brand. The product grid is clean, with named brands, prices and "NEW • LIMITED EDITION / ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags. But this is the latest in a long run of near-identical Savings Event sends (11/03 through 11/07), so the urgency language is the only thing that sets it apart.
- Judgement: a competent, well-rendered promo that is diluted by a cluttered top. It will perform on urgency but not on clarity.

## What's working

- The countdown timer with "FINAL HOURS" gives the "ends tomorrow" subject a visible payoff.
- The 30% / 10% medallions are bold and easy to read, with their date windows stated.
- The product grid names brands and prices (Summer Fridays $25, Charlotte Tilbury $37 value, Makeup by Mario $32) and has "Clean" badges.
- The "Holiday Value Sets" hero has one obvious "SHOP NOW" button.
- Barcodes at the bottom let the offer be redeemed in stores.

## What's weak

- Three different promo codes (SPEEDY, ALLONSALE, UNREADY) in one email create decision friction.
- The subject leads with "10% off," but the free same-day delivery banner is the first thing seen, so the hierarchy doesn't match the subject.
- The 10% medallion appears twice with different date windows, which makes the actual deadline unclear.
- The body drifts into catalog browsing (Gift of Beauty, fragrance, gift guide) after the hero, which dilutes the urgency.
- The fine print is very long, and the "Stacks with Sephora Savings Event" free-gift offer is tucked under a $100 threshold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top to one offer. Lead with 10% off and the deadline, and move the free same-day delivery and free-gift offers to a footer strip.
- 2. Use one code for the whole email, or none for Insider members.
- 3. Trim the lower modules (Gift of Beauty, fragrance, gift guide) into one "Last-chance picks" row linked to specific products.
- 4. Put the countdown directly under the hero and state a single deadline, e.g. "Ends 11/9 at 11:59pm PT".
- 5. Shorten the fine print and link out to terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rosie, your 10% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 10% off + free same-day delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off Sephora Collection. Sale ends at 11:59pm PT tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Holiday value sets, limited editions and a free gift at $100.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an empty name slot in the greeting ("Hi ,"). It also has no plain-text part (0 chars), and the HTML has structural problems that may affect mobile rendering. The compliance and authentication warnings may come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 material links were probed and 36 were skipped. The QA data reports no failures among the probed links, but 36 links are unverified.
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links go through opaque tracked redirects, for example `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1bba84998beadfff4bbcfb648/.../?__dU__=...`. Final destinations can't be checked from the truncated source.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two.
  - A hidden pixel at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`, styled `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider`.
- **Missing alt on both pixels:** Both fail the QA alt-text check. Neither is user-visible, but a screen reader or an images-off client may announce the raw filename or URL.
- **JSON-LD block:** It declares `"@type": "DiscountOffer"` with `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. The empty code and the missing fields mean the markup carries no usable data. It is also loaded over `http://schema.org/`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** The outer table is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block does not override it. Without an override elsewhere (the source is truncated), this forces horizontal scroll on mobile.
- **Malformed CSS in the media query:** The block contains `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` inside the `@media` rule. HTML comment and conditional syntax is not valid CSS there. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it: `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `.mobileHeaderTierBI`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Plain-text fallback:** [FAIL] The text version is 0 chars. Text-only clients, some spam filters and accessibility tools get nothing. Missing plain text is also a deliverability signal.
- **Alt text:** The two pixels above are the only images flagged. Add `alt=""` to both.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` uses an invalid value (it should be `noindex`). It has no effect in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The QA check found "Hi ," with the name field empty.
- **Inconsistent with the subject line:** The subject renders the name correctly ("Don't miss out, Rosie!"). The body token is failing or has no fallback, so the two merge sources disagree.
- **Fix:** Add a default value for the greeting token, or drop the name and comma when the field is empty.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** [WARN] Not found. The QA note says the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** [WARN] Not found. This matters for one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require from bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** [WARN] Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check them on the raw message, not through the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** The QA data says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm either element.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable from the source:** The visible links are opaque redirects, and the only campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider` on the open pixel. No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are exposed.
- **Next step:** Resolve the redirects and check that parameters survive to the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting token. Add a fallback or conditional so "Hi ," can't render.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part, since the current one is 0 chars.
3. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block into its own `<style>` or a conditional comment.
4. Make the 700px wrapper fluid, for example `width:100%; max-width:700px`, and keep the `min-width` only in MSO conditionals.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
6. Inspect the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
7. Re-run link probing on the 36 skipped links, or at least a sample, and resolve the redirects to confirm landing pages and UTM carry-through.
