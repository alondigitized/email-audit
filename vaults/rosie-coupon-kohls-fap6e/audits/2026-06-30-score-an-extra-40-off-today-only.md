# 🚨 Score an extra 40% off TODAY ONLY 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a one-day flash discount email — "TODAY ONLY, 40% off" — arriving on June 30, which Kohl's is framing around the Fourth of July / 250th birthday of the country. After Deal Days mercifully ended on June 28 and cleared with a solid clearance send on June 29, this is the brand's second genuine pivot in two days: a new promotional mechanic (stacked coupon), a holiday hook (Independence Day / USA's 252nd birthday), and actual product imagery that matches the season (USA soccer/national team gear, July 4th red-white-blue lifestyle shots, summer clothing at $9.99). That's a meaningful upgrade in variety and relevance compared to the Deal Days grind.
- The problem is execution. The hero discount — 40% off — leads cleanly, but the fine print ("with coupons," "some exclusions apply") is visible even at thumbnail scale, which immediately erodes the "TODAY ONLY" credibility. The creative then fractures into four distinct story threads: flash discount, soccer clearance at $9.99, 250th-birthday patriotic gear, and Kohl's Cash pickup promo. None of them get enough room to breathe, and the scattershot layout undercuts the urgency the subject line was trying to build.
- Clear judgement: better than the Deal Days death march, but still a "three things fighting for first" email that would have landed harder as a single focused send on either the flash discount or the July 4th moment.

## What's working

- **Holiday creative is timely and specific** — "250th birthday / 252nd pickup" copy and patriotic lifestyle imagery make this feel like a real seasonal send, not a recycled template.
- **$9.99 soccer gear callout** is concrete and eye-catching — a specific price point performs better than a percent-off alone.
- **"TODAY ONLY" hero in the banner** — the time constraint is prominently placed and visible without scrolling.
- **Free $5 Kohl's Cash store pickup strip** adds a loyalty member benefit that breaks the visual monotony and gives members a secondary reason to click.
- **Multiple nav categories (Women, Men, Kids, Shoes, Home)** are visible early, giving scanners a direct path without relying on the hero CTA.

## What's weak

- **"40% off with coupons" fine print visible in hero** — the asterisk/qualifier undercuts the punch of the headline before the eye even leaves the banner.
- **Four distinct messages compete for attention** — flash discount, $9.99 soccer, July 4th birthday, and Kohl's Cash pickup are all fighting for the same email; none wins.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — appears at least twice with identical copy; no specificity about *what* to shop.
- **"250th / 252nd" copy is quirky but confusing** — "Perfect for the 250th birthday AND the 252nd pickup" is a clever wordplay but reads as a typo at first glance.
- **Bottom wallet/app strip is dense** — small icons and micro-copy (Kohl's Card, Customer Service, Store Locator, Kohl's Coupons) add noise at the end rather than a clean call to action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to one story.** Either make this the July 4th / USA gear email, or make it the 40%-off flash coupon email. Mixing both dilutes both. The 250th-birthday angle is genuinely fresh — lean in.
- 2. **Remove or defer the $9.99 soccer section.** It's good creative but it belongs in its own send; here it confuses the discount mechanic and the holiday frame.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero to fold in the coupon naturally** — "40% off today with your coupon" is less punishing than fine print under a bolder headline. Better: show the coupon code prominently in the hero so it feels like insider access, not an asterisk.
- 4. **Give the July 4th lifestyle block a real CTA** — "Shop Patriotic Styles" or "Shop 4th of July Looks" vs. the third "Shop Now."
- 5. **Tighten the subject line** — the double 🚨 is noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 40% off with your coupon — July 4th styles included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off coupon expires tonight — USA gear + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $9.99 soccer styles & free $5 Kohl's Cash on store pickup`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Celebrate the 250th — extra 40% off with your coupon, today only`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 40% Off Today Only" (2026-06-30)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard Kohl's multi-ESP stack (CoherentPath for click-tracking, Movable Ink for personalized images, Adobe Audience Manager for identity). Four compliance warnings and one malformed HTML tag require attention; the SPF/DKIM pass-through gap is the highest-severity item.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links unverifiable.** All CTA links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) or `click.s.kohls.com`, which the QA prober correctly skipped. Destination URLs and UTM parameter presence cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain manually.

**Three tracking pixel domains active:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/cf08dfaf-...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954b.../o.gif` — Movable Ink view pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync

**Malformed HTML on open pixel container (severity: medium):**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?2TTI6C373PTULJ6G..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   ← invalid closing tag; should be </div>
```
The `</custom>` tag is not valid HTML. Most clients will recover silently, but it can break DOM parsing in strict renderers and will fail HTML validators.

**Empty favicon href:**
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```
Harmless at runtime but generates a console error in webmail clients that render HTML.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Zoom disabled — accessibility violation:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
`user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 1.4.4 (Level AA) requires text to be resizable up to 200% without loss of content. Several major email clients (Apple Mail, Gmail app) respect this directive.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the title when the email is opened in a browser or forwarded. An empty title reduces accessibility and degrades the experience in webmail preview panes.

**10 content images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed):
- The three tracking pixels (`cf08dfaf`, `o.gif`, `event`) are correctly `aria-hidden` or dimensionless — acceptable.
- Six Movable Ink content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) have no `alt` attribute. These are rendered product/promo images; when images are blocked, recipients get broken image icons with no context.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading:**
The same two font families are loaded three ways — via `<link>` tags AND `@import` rules inside a `<style>` block. This causes duplicate HTTP requests on clients that support external CSS:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">  <!-- link -->
@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto..."); /* also import */
```
Remove one method; `<link>` is preferred in email as it loads earlier.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) detected in the visible HTML. Personalization appears resolved at send time — `mi_u=604230016` is a concrete user ID in all Movable Ink URLs, and `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in one image URL has an empty value, suggesting an optional conditional field that was left blank rather than an error.

`email_name=260630_DG_Email_Tuesday` is present in several `mi.kohls.com` image URLs — consistent with the send date (June 30, 2026, Tuesday). No issue.

### 5. Compliance

| Issue | Severity | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | High | Required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (>5k/day). QA could not confirm header presence due to AgentMail relay gap — verify at MTA level. |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent | High | One-click unsubscribe mandated by Gmail and Yahoo since Feb 2024 for bulk senders. Same relay caveat applies. |
| Physical mailing address not detected | High | CAN-SPAM §5 requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. Not present in the truncated HTML — if absent from the full email, this is a legal violation. |
| `Authentication-Results` header not captured | Medium | SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown due to AgentMail relay not forwarding the header. Cannot confirm deliverability posture from the captured source. |

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is present. This is a deliberate privacy signal but has no functional compliance impact since all links go through click-redirect domains anyway.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameter verification is **blocked** — all 22 outbound links route through CoherentPath (`click.chp.kohls.com`) and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains. The QA prober correctly skipped these. To confirm UTM coverage (source, medium, campaign, content), the redirect chain must be resolved manually or via an allowlisted probe.

The `mi_ecmp=1016237_2026630` parameter in Movable Ink image URLs encodes a campaign identifier consistent with the send — this is internal tracking, not UTM.

**No referrer leakage risk:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on all outbound navigation, which is consistent with using explicit click-tracking rather than referrer-based attribution.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers at the MTA**, not via downstream relay. Gmail/Yahoo require these for bulk senders — their absence risks inbox demotion or spam classification.
2. **Confirm physical address presence** in the full (non-truncated) email body. If missing, add it immediately — CAN-SPAM requires it in every commercial email.
3. **Fix the `</custom>` malformed tag** on the `click.s.kohls.com` open pixel `<div>` wrapper. Replace with `</div>`.
4. **Add `alt` text to all Movable Ink content images.** Use descriptive fallback text (e.g., `alt="Extra 40% off promo banner"`) so image-blocked recipients understand the offer.
5. **Remove the redundant Google Fonts `@import`** — keep the `<link>` tags only to eliminate duplicate font requests.
6. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta to restore pinch-to-zoom for accessibility compliance.
7. **Populate the `<title>` tag** with a meaningful value (e.g., the email subject line) for screen reader and webmail compatibility.
8. **Resolve the redirect chain for 5–10 sampled CTAs** to confirm UTM parameters are present and correctly formatted before the next send.
