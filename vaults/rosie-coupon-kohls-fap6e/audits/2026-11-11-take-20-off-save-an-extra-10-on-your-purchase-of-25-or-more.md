# Take 20% off | Save an extra $10 on your purchase of $25 or more 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall, and the subject undersells it. The subject leads with "20% off" and "$10 off $25+", but the render opens on a "Take $10 off your purchase of $25 or more with coupon, Ends Nov. 11" hero next to a Kohl's Cash tile. The 20% offer isn't in the hero. The email only gets to 20% further down, in a "Take an extra 20% off" barcode block and an "Up to 20%" cold-weather accessories module.
- The render is very long, 7,339px tall and shown here at about 185px wide. I can read the layout and large type but not the fine print. After the hero come a military-appreciation "30% off" banner, a nav row, "Up to 40%" coats, boots "starting at 29.99", gift lists, a "Preview the Deals Now" Black Friday teaser, Klarna and delivery partner modules, a product grid, and a stack of barcodes and coupon codes. It's a dozen competing messages, and the one real deadline, Nov. 11, is easy to miss.
- Compared with the Nov 10 "Stack 20% off + $10 off your $25+ purchase" send, this one is nearly the same creative and the same offers. The subject is less punchy. A subscriber who got the Nov 8 and Nov 9 sends will see this as one more daily promo. Verdict: functional and on-brand, but forgettable and fatiguing.

## What's working

- The hero puts the $10 off $25+ coupon at the top with a visible "Shop Now" link and an "Ends Nov. 11" deadline.
- The Kohl's Rewards Member and Kohl's Cash banner at the top gives loyalty members a reason to engage.
- The barcode and coupon blocks near the bottom ("Take an extra 20% off", "Take $10 off") make the offers redeemable in store.
- The "Preview the Deals Now" teaser with "Come back Nov. 22-26" seeds the next send and fits the season.
- I can see no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps. The email renders cleanly.

## What's weak

- Too many modules compete: military discount, accessories, boots, coats, gift lists, Klarna, same-day delivery and a product grid. No single hero wins.
- The subject's 20% offer isn't the first thing in the body, so the subject and hero don't match.
- Most CTAs are generic "Shop Now" or "Learn More", and the exclusion language ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") appears on multiple modules.
- It's the same deal wall at the same cadence as the Nov 8–10 sends, so urgency is blunted. Only the hero shows a deadline.
- Several product blocks have small price text that is hard to read on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer and one hero: the $10-off coupon with the Nov. 11 deadline, a "Shop Coupon Deals" button, then a short product row. Move military, accessories, boots and delivery into a footer strip or drop them.
- 2. Make the subject match the hero. Lead with the $10 and the expiry, not a pipe-separated list.
- 3. Use CTA copy that names the benefit, such as "Get $10 off $25" or "Shop coats up to 40% off", instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Send fewer of these. After a week of near-identical emails, hold the "last chance" framing for the real final day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on the main layout tables, media-query fallbacks). It has several verifiable defects: content images without alt text, malformed head and tracking-pixel markup, zoom disabled, and a 70% URL plain-text part. Compliance items and UTM alignment can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated and the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking domains.** 77 links go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` (opaque, SFMC-style) and were not probed, so no destination or status is verified.
- **Stray closing tag.** The open pixel is `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=60274&bmt=0" …></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opening tag and is invalid markup.
- **Pixel stack.** There are four separate tracking or open pixels:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/668dfd5e-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Identifier in URLs.** Image URLs carry `mi_u=604230016`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. These are recipient-level identifiers sent to the image host on every render.
- **Possible empty dynamic value.** `916d6fa08d185233.png` has `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value. A Kohl's Cash redemption date may have failed to populate. The URL is truncated, so this needs a check.
- **Plain-text part.** 13,612 of 19,388 characters (about 70%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk. It follows from the 77 long tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png` have no alt text. If these carry the "20% off" or "$10 off $25" offer, it is invisible with images blocked and to screen readers.
- **Pixel alt text.**
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` is a false positive, because it has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` (1×1) and `demdex.net/event` (0×0) have no `alt` attribute. `demdex` is only a pixel, so give it `alt=""`.
  - The first pixel is also a bare `<img>` directly in `<body>`, outside any table.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure.
- **Empty title and icon link.** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid markup in head.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close the head at that point.
- **CSS typo.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Missing table roles.** The preheader table (`width='640' … class="container"`) and the 1×1 tracking tables have no `role="presentation"`.
- **Fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load from Google Fonts via `@import`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present, so this is fine.

## 7. Recommendations

2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.

   - Move the `schema.org` div out of `<head>`.

   - Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.

4. Check what the Movable Ink image URL with `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` renders. If the date is blank, fix the feed.
5. Re-run QA against the raw MIME message with full headers and un-truncated HTML. Then confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, the footer address, and the unsubscribe link.
