# Take 20% off | Find fall home must-haves for less 🏠
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is another technically clean Kohl's send — consistent with the run logged since 7/30, no clipped starbursts, no broken product images, nav buttons and tiles all line up correctly on both banks of "Home / Women / Men / Kids" navigation. The hero does its job in isolation: "Take an extra 20% off with coupon, ends Aug 16" in bold red is unmissable, and it's immediately backed by two on-theme home offers (50% off bedding, 20% off kitchen/dining) that actually support the "fall home must-haves" subject line.
- Where it falls apart is scope creep. After the home-focused open, the email pivots hard into an "ULTIMATE CLEARANCE EVENT — Save up to 70%" banner and a "Picked with you in mind" grid that's a Ninja Slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and women's jeans — only two of which are remotely "home," and none of which read as personalized despite the copy claiming otherwise. A subscriber who opened for fall home deals gets steered into a generic clearance grab-bag by the midpoint.
- Solid production, muddled targeting. This is a competent but not standout Kohl's send — call it middle-of-the-pack for this brand's run.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "20% off with coupon, ends Aug 16" is the first thing the eye hits, in large red type against a clean dotted background.
- The two content blocks directly under the hero (green bedding, yellow floral dinnerware) genuinely match the subject's "fall home" promise, with clear pricing and "Shop Now" CTAs.
- Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") is surfaced right at the top — a nice loyalty touch for a member opening this.
- Coupon code, PIN, and barcode are all rendered cleanly near the bottom, making the offer redeemable at a glance without a separate click.
- No visual defects anywhere in the render — every product photo, banner, and button displays correctly.

## What's weak

- The "Ultimate Clearance Event — Save up to 70%" banner mid-email competes with and dilutes the 20%-off home narrative the subject line sold.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" promises personalization but shows a slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and jeans — a generic assortment, not a tailored one.
- Three different discount levels (20%, 50%, 70%) appear in one email with no clear hierarchy of which applies where, which reads as cluttered rather than generous.
- The preview text leaks "View this email in a browser" directly into visible preheader copy.
- Subject and hero commit to "home," but by the second half the email is functionally a general clearance blast — a bait-and-switch feel consistent with past Kohl's "Ultimate Clearance" sends that over-promised on the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Ultimate Clearance Event" banner from this send entirely, or move it to its own dedicated email — it undercuts the home-focused promise the subject line just made.
- 2. Make "Picked with you in mind" actually home-only (or drop the personalization language) — swap the slushi maker and jeans for home-adjacent items like the bedding or dinnerware already featured above.
- 3. Pick one hero discount tier and lead with it consistently instead of stacking 20%/50%/70% across the same email.
- 4. Clean up the preheader so "View this email in a browser" doesn't leak into the visible preview text.
- 5. Link the primary hero CTA to a specific home collection page, not just a generic category shop.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Take 20% off fall bedding, decor & dinnerware`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall home refresh starts at 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New bedding, dinnerware & decor — plus Kohl's Cash on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off home essentials, ends Aug 16. Earn Kohl's Cash too.`
