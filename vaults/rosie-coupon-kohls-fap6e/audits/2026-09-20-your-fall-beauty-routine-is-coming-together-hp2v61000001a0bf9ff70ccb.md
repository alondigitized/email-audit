# Your fall beauty routine is coming together 🍂
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's fall skincare "new arrivals" send, and the subject "Your fall beauty routine is coming together 🍂" is one of the few subjects this brand has sent recently that actually maps to the hero — "FALL INTO NEW BEAUTY" and "YOUR DIEUX RITUAL" both open the email, so there's no bait-and-switch this time. But the tradeoff is a wall-of-products layout: after the hero, it's ten-plus near-identical product cards (Salt & Stone, Fenty, Emi Jay, Kayali, Huda, Josie Maran, Caudalie, LoveShackFancy) stacked vertically with no single clear CTA or discount, so it reads as a catalog scroll rather than an offer.
- There's a real render bug at the very top — a floating "Get a Trial Size... Selena Gomez" banner sits above the fold with no visible product image, which looks broken or unfinished before the reader even reaches the fall hero. Overall this is a mid-tier send: subject/hero alignment is genuinely good (a first for this history), but the lack of any price/percentage offer and the top-of-email glitch cap it below the stronger 4X-points send from 9/19.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy match — "fall beauty routine" leads directly into "FALL INTO NEW BEAUTY," unlike several bait-and-switch sends earlier this week.
- Clear seasonal framing with a warm, on-brand model image reinforcing "refresh your lineup for the season."
- "Clean at Sephora" and "Cruelty-Free" badges are visible on multiple products, giving trust signals for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- New-launch labeling ("NEW," "NEW + Limited Edition," "Only at Sephora") is consistently applied, helping a browsing reader spot exclusives fast.
- Loyalty footer ("Beauty Insider," sign-in fields) and a secondary "4X Points on Fragrance" banner give returning members something concrete near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The top banner ("Get a Trial Size... Selena Gomez / Rare Beauty") shows no product image — just text over a blank/light box, which looks like a broken render before the hero even loads.
- No concrete discount or price-off offer anywhere in the primary hero — this is pure "browse new arrivals" with no urgency or savings hook.
- The body is an undifferentiated stack of 8+ product cards with equal visual weight — there's no hierarchy telling the eye where to click first.
- Preview text is not visible/complementary in this render — nothing in the image confirms preheader copy adds anything beyond the subject.
- "4X Points on Fragrance" and "New and Under $50" banners near the bottom compete with the main fall narrative, diluting the single-theme framing set up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the broken trial-size/Selena Gomez banner at the top — it undermines trust before the reader reaches the actual fall content.
- 2. Add one concrete offer to the hero (e.g., "20% off new arrivals" or a bundle deal) instead of leaving "FALL INTO NEW BEAUTY" as pure discovery copy — this brand's best-performing send in this history (9/19, 4X points) worked because it paired a season hook with a tangible reward.
- 3. Cut the product wall from 8+ cards to 3-4 hero SKUs with a "Shop the Edit" CTA, moving the rest to a "See more" link — reduces scroll fatigue and creates real hierarchy.
- 4. Consolidate the bottom banners (4X Points, Under $50) into a single "More ways to save" module instead of two competing CTAs after the main content.
