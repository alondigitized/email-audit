# Doorbuster deals + up to 50% off Black Friday deals + an extra 20% off 🤯 ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same ~12,000px Kohl's Black Friday deal wall that has landed all week, with yet another "Doorbuster" subject line on top. Compared with the earlier "DOORBUSTERS + up to 50% off deals + 20% off are here 👀" and "Doorbusters + 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash = JACKPOT 🏆" sends today, the creative is effectively unchanged. The render is clean, with no broken images or overlaps that I can see.
- The top is the strongest part. A snowy hero carries "Black Friday Deals", "up to 50% off", "20% off" and "$15 Kohl's Cash" badges. A "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip and a "3 Days Only: DOORBUSTERS" block follow, with price-point tiles such as $10.39, $12.79 and $2.39. The hierarchy up there is clear and the offers are concrete.
- Below that it turns into a long stack of category tiles with many competing badges (50%, 40%, 60%, 65%, $10, $99.99), and no single CTA stands out. With three or more near-identical sends already today, a real subscriber would likely skip this one. I'd call it a solid render with weak differentiation, and the cadence is the main problem.

## What's working

- The hero stacks the key offers (up to 50% off, 20% off, $15 Kohl's Cash) in the first screen.
- The "3 Days Only / DOORBUSTERS" banner adds credible urgency.
- Specific price points ($10.39, $12.79, $2.39, $15.99) make the doorbusters feel concrete.
- Free shipping is called out in its own strip.
- The category nav pills (Bed & Bath, Home, Women, Men, Kids, Beauty) make the long scroll easier to navigate.

## What's weak

- At roughly 12,000px, the email buries its best deals under dozens of tiles.
- Many tiles carry "Coupons do not apply" or "Select styles" fine print, which hurts how honest the offer feels.
- Nearly every tile has a small "Shop Now" link, so no single CTA is dominant.
- It is the 3rd or 4th near-identical send today, so cadence fatigue is likely.
- The subject stacks three offers and two emojis, which reads cluttered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the top 6–8 doorbusters and one big "Shop Doorbusters" button. A shorter email would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Stop sending near-duplicate creative the same day. Consolidate to one send, or differentiate each by category.
- 3. Put the 20% off coupon code or Kohl's Cash redemption window next to the hero CTA, and move the "coupons do not apply" exclusions out of the tiles.
- 4. Segment the tiles by recipient category (home, apparel, toys) instead of showing everyone the full wall.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based Kohl's template with VML/MSO fallbacks, and it uses four separate tracking and pixel vendors. The main confirmed problems are missing alt text on content images, a viewport meta that disables zoom, a few markup defects, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The QA run (50% pass, 0 errors, 4 warnings) could not verify headers, link destinations or the footer, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **104 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. The one visible link is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNzMs…`, a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) redirect.
- **Four tracking beacons sit at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b04fca79-…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (width/height 0, Adobe Audience Manager)

  Three of them are open-style pixels. This adds third-party requests and inflates open counts, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will preload all of them.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="…open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener. Some clients may render it strangely.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the page's own URL in some clients.
- **Image URL parameters are inconsistent:** `email_name=261127_DG_Email_Fri_PM` appears on `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` and `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`. It is absent on `69494b48a79b4240.png` and `78308d1d9adb0751.png`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images have no alt text.** The QA run flagged these product and banner images:
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`

  If the images are blocked, the offers and product names are unavailable to screen readers and to image-off views.
- **The pixel warnings are lower priority.** The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so that flag is a false positive. The `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels have no `alt` attribute, which is cosmetic.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (a WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **The title is empty.** `<title></title>` leaves no document title for clients that show one.
- **Minor:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XML namespaces (`xmlns:o`, `xmlns:v`), and `meta name="format-detection"` appears twice. Neither breaks rendering.
- **Fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load from Google Fonts. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are declared, so this is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. User-level identifiers are present in pixel URLs (`mi_u=604230016`), but that is expected tracking behavior.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers. Bulk senders need RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe for Gmail and Yahoo.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. Nothing here shows a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible, because the source is truncated. They are unverified, not missing.
- **Plain-text part:** 18,364 of 24,232 characters (about 76%) are URLs. This is a deliverability risk. It most likely comes from long SFMC and click-tracking URLs repeated across the 104 links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **The destination and its UTMs cannot be checked.** Every probed link is a redirect (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), and the 104 tracking links were skipped. UTM parameters would only show on the final landing URLs.
- **Campaign identifiers exist only on image URLs:** `mi_ecmp=1026828_20261127` and `email_name=261127_DG_Email_Fri_PM`. They are not confirmed on the click links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image listed in section 3. Use `alt=""` on decorative or pixel images.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the markup: delete the stray `</custom>`, remove or populate the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`, and give `<title>` a value.
4. Cut redundant open beacons. Keep one, and review whether the `demdex` third-party pixel is needed.
5. Make the tracking parameters consistent. Add `email_name` to all `mi.kohls.com` image URLs.
6. Shorten or reduce the click-tracking URLs, or add a hand-authored plain-text part, to lower the URL ratio.
7. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm landing pages, UTMs and the footer unsubscribe link.
