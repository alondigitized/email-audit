# Save 20% | Step into the Season's Best Shoe Sale 👟👡👢
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent seasonal shoe send with a real hero. "Season's Best Shoe Sale" carries "40% off", it sits over a clean boots, sneakers and clogs collage, and category tiles for Boots, Mary Janes, Loafers and Clogs follow. The 70% Clearance band and a product grid lower down give the message a second beat. That is better targeted than the kitchen-sink promos Kohl's ran on 10/8 ("Last chance ⏳ Shop Kohl's Deal Days + get Kohl's Cash 🤑") and the coupon-only sends of 10/9 to 10/11.
- The top of the email undercuts it. The banner above the nav looks broken. It is a mostly empty maroon block with only "Ends Oct. 18." and a clipped "$100 or more with coupon. Ends Oct. 12." visible. The "Extra 20% off" headline that the subject leads with isn't readable. So the subject promises 20% and the first screen doesn't deliver it. The hero then promotes 40% off, and the body also carries 70% off, a $20 coupon, a $10 coupon and Kohl's Cash. That is five offers for one inbox visit.
- Verdict: a good shoe-category idea in a muddy wrapper. It is a subscriber's eighth or ninth Kohl's coupon email this week, with the same codes and the same Oct. 18 deadline. It earns a decent score, but it won't stand out.

## What's working

- The "SEASON'S BEST SHOE Sale" hero is visually strong. The type is bold and the shoe collage is on-season (fall boots, loafers).
- "Featuring up to 40% off" is a concrete number with a visible "Shop Now" link.
- The category tiles (Boots, Mary Janes, Loafers, Clogs) let a shopper self-select by style, and "Complete one pair of your look, one at a time" has a friendly voice.
- The Kohl's Cash strip and the "KOHL'S REWARDS® MEMBER · Earn 5%" status line make loyalty benefits visible.
- The product grid shows clear prices and originals (Ninja Slushi $299.99 vs $349.99, Women's Gloria Vanderbilt jeans $29.99 vs $52.00). The coupon codes are in scannable barcode blocks with end dates.

## What's weak

- The top banner is garbled. A large empty maroon area holds only "Ends Oct. 18." and a clipped "$100 or more with coupon. Ends Oct. 12." The lead offer is unreadable.
- The subject says "Save 20%", but the 20% message is missing from the first screen. The hero says 40% and Clearance says 70%, so the offers conflict.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid is a Slushi machine, a bath towel, a mattress topper and jeans. None of it is shoes, so it breaks the theme.
- The email is very long, with Home/Women/Men/Kids nav twice, four coupon blocks and dense fine print. The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" exclusion is small and sits in the hero.
- The cadence is stale. The same coupon codes and Oct. 18 deadline have run since 10/9, so there is no fresh urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the top banner so "Extra 20% off with coupon" and "$20 off $100+" are legible in the first screen. Make it a single clean line, because it is the offer the subject sells.
- 2. Pick one headline offer for the shoe theme. Make the subject, banner and hero agree on one number (for example, "Up to 40% off shoes + extra 20% with coupon").
- 3. Swap the off-theme "Picked with you in mind" grid for shoes (the boots and sneakers shown in the hero) with real prices and review stars.
- 4. Cut the second nav and the Clearance band, or move them below the coupons. Drop the coupon blocks to one or two.
- 5. Give each CTA a verb plus a noun, such as "Shop Boots" or "Shop Mary Janes", instead of a generic "Shop Now".
- 6. Vary the creative. Don't re-send the same coupon stack with a new subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Boots, loafers & clogs: up to 40% off 👢`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall shoes up to 40% off + extra 20% (ends 10/18)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mary Janes, loafers and boots are marked down. Use code YOUGET20 for 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your fall shoe refresh, plus $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is mostly standard ESP output, but it has several validity and accessibility defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, and many images with no `alt`. The source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe block weren't available. The compliance and authentication warnings are therefore unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 67 links go through tracking redirects (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...`) and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and status can't be confirmed.
- **Four separate open/analytics beacons** load before the content:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/2e255fb2-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023727_20261012`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?DIWXWXCJ7QJENPMK54LBCM6RH4.60263&d=60263&bmt=0`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)
- **Redundant trackers:** the three open pixels from different systems are redundant. The demdex beacon is a third-party request that some clients will block.
- **Malformed pixel markup:** the `open.aspx` pixel is closed with `</custom>`, which has no matching opening tag: `<img ... alt=""></custom>`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** QA flagged 11 images. These include likely content images such as `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` and `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, plus the product image `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`. With images blocked, these render as blank space or filenames for screen-reader users. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels also have no `alt`, and the `demdex` pixel has no `aria-hidden`. Only `o.gif` carries `aria-hidden="true"` (and it has no `alt` value).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and move the div into the body.
- **Layout tables without roles:** the preheader table (`<table width='640' ... class="container">`) and the 1×1 beacon tables have no `role="presentation"`. The main wrapper tables do have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Empty dynamic parameter:** one image URL ends `...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...`. An empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` suggests an unpopulated field, though the effect on rendering can't be confirmed.
- **Recipient ID in image URLs:** every `mi.kohls.com` image URL carries `mi_u=604230016`, a per-recipient identifier. Anything that proxies or caches these images exposes it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed (QA warnings):**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found.
  - No physical address detected.
- **Why they're unconfirmed:** the QA tool itself attributes the missing headers to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so the address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. Treat all of these as data gaps, not confirmed violations.
- **Sender domain:** the From is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, and the tracking links use the same `kohls.com` family of domains (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com`), so there is no visible domain mismatch.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source.
- Campaign identity appears only as `mi_ecmp=1023727_20261012` and `email_name=261012_DG_Email_Monday` on image URLs. These are not on the click links.
- Click destinations are hidden behind opaque `qs=` tokens, so landing page alignment and attribution parameters can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the markup: remove the stray `</custom>`, fill in `<title>`, and remove or populate the empty favicon `href`. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden` to every tracking pixel.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Consolidate to one open pixel and review whether the `demdex` beacon is needed.
6. Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` renders empty.
7. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer address. Also run the HTML check on the full footer.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
