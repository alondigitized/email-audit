# Homepage snapshot · www.kohls.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, I tapped on the link and this is just not right — all I got was a big bold "Access Denied" message staring me in the face like I did something wrong. No store, no deals, no Kohl's Cash reminder — nothing. Whatever server is running this site slammed the door on me before I could even see a single product, and that reference number at the bottom means absolutely nothing to me. Kohl's is my store, and I can't even get in the front door.

## What's working

- The error text is at least legible — large, clear font, easy to read on a phone screen (though that is cold comfort)
- Nothing broken visually within the error itself — it rendered its own sad little message cleanly

## What's weak

- The "Access Denied" heading is the first and only thing I see — completely fatal for any shopper intent
- The reference number (`#18.9d0ed217.1778962178.aa51d355`) and the edgesuite URL below it are pure tech gibberish — a 56-year-old on her phone has no idea what to do with this
- No fallback messaging, no "try again," no link back to a search page, no phone number for help
- No Kohl's branding whatsoever — the page is completely unbranded, so I don't even feel like I'm in the right place
- A Boomer shopper who gets this is not going to troubleshoot — she's going to close the tab and go to Macy's

## Recommendations

- Add a branded, human-friendly error page: Kohl's logo, warm copy like "Oops, something went wrong — let's get you back to the deals," and a clearly labeled "Retry" button
- Surface a customer service phone number on any error screen — many of my generation will pick up the phone instead of retrying; make it easy
- Include a direct link to the weekly ad PDF on the error page as a fallback — I came here for deals and I should be able to get them some way
- Fix the Akamai block rule that is incorrectly denying legitimate mobile traffic — this should never reach a real customer
