# Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the highest tier Kohl's has run in the entire streak since 09-10, and the flat 40% coupon (replacing the segmented 30%/20%/15% Card/Rewards/Nonmember stack) is a genuine structural change worth noting to a subscriber who's seen ten-plus near-identical sends. But past the hero, this collapses into the exact same "Fall must-haves for homes" hosting/home theme as recent sends, now stacked with a barcode, a printed coupon code, and a Kohl's Cash mechanic layered on top of the discount — three separate savings vehicles in one email is a lot to parse.
- For a home/lifestyle-focused recipient this lands fine on relevance, but the email is long, repetitive in its offer messaging (40% appears three separate times: hero, mid-email reminder, and barcode section), and leans on "Coupons do not apply" fine print on several featured products, which undercuts the headline offer's honesty.
- Verdict: modest step up from the 09-15 to 09-18 run (5-6/10 territory) because of the 40% escalation, but still a cluttered, home-category-only email that will read as "the same Kohl's email, bigger number" to anyone who's opened the last few.

## What's working

- Flat 40% off is clearly the largest hero number in this brand's recent history, giving the subject line real teeth.
- Hero banner has strong visual hierarchy — pink coupon box with bold white "40% off" is the first thing the eye lands on.
- Kohl's Cash callout ("Plus, earn Kohl's Cash") is a clean, un-cluttered green band directly under the hero, reinforcing a second reason to shop.
- Category nav buttons (Home, Women, Men, Kids) are unambiguous and give multiple clear entry points.
- Product photography (kitchen shelf, Ninja espresso machine, vacuum) is clean, no rendering bugs, no broken images.

## What's weak

- "Coupons do not apply" appears on both featured appliance products (Ninja espresso maker, floor care vacuum) — the two most expensive/prominent items in the email are excluded from the 40% headline offer, which is a bait-and-switch signal.
- The email repeats the 40%-off message three times (hero, "Go ahead and shop" section, barcode block) without adding new information each time — redundant rather than reinforcing.
- "Warning: Your guests may never have" is a weak, generic pun that doesn't map to a specific offer or urgency.
- This is still a single-category (home/fall hosting) push, same beat as 09-17 through 09-19's prior sends, just with a bigger discount number slapped on.
- Long scroll depth (this is one of the longest Kohl's renders in the recent history) buries the Women/Men/Kids/Accessories cross-sell section far below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject/hero with the 40% number but pair it with one unambiguous, unexcluded hero product — right now the two biggest-ticket items shown are explicitly carved out of the discount, which contradicts the headline.
- 2. Cut the repeated 40%-off restatement down to two mentions (hero + barcode/redemption section) — the mid-email "Go ahead and shop" repeat adds no new info.
- 3. Replace "Warning: Your guests may never leave" with copy tied to the actual discount tier (e.g., "Up to 40% off hosting essentials") to keep urgency concrete rather than punny.
- 4. Move the Women/Men/Kids/Accessories/Beauty grid higher — it's currently buried past three separate home-category product blocks, which will lose non-home shoppers before they scroll that far.
- 5. Given this is the 40% peak after a long escalation ladder (20→30→40 over two weeks), flag in copy that this is a ceiling/rare tier ("our biggest discount this month") to justify the size and create real urgency instead of implying it's routine.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our biggest markdown yet: 40% off + Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off fall home favorites, ends Sept. 20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen, floor care, and bedding — all at your biggest discount this fall`

## Full review

- No issues found with link structure itself beyond the alt-text gaps noted below.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images missing `alt` text, all tracking/beacon pixels (1x1 GIFs/PNGs): `4fe61540...`, `o.gif`, `event` (Demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons. These are non-visual tracking pixels, so missing alt is low-impact but still fails strict accessibility checks; recommend `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (already applied to one pixel: `o.gif`) for consistency across all of them.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) with MSO/Outlook conditional comments — standard for cross-client email rendering, no issue.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px via media query; no rendering issues identifiable from truncated source.
- `<img ... /></custom>` on the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel line appears malformed — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag. Likely a template artifact; won't break rendering in most clients but is invalid HTML.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Query params (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe`) indicate per-recipient/per-campaign personalization is handled server-side at send time; `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` (Kohl's Cash redemption end date) appears empty in one beacon URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe`) — possible unpopulated merge field, though this is a tracking pixel, not user-facing content, so impact is limited to analytics attribution.
