# Enjoy 20% off home, shoes & styles for the crew 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 20%-off Friends & Family coupon running for the sixth day straight, but for once it doesn't just reskin the hero — it stitches together three distinct sends into one long email: the F&F coupon block, a full "Back to School" category grid, and a "SO-Back" sweepstakes push, before finally landing on a real product grid (towels, a Ninja slushie maker, jeans, a mattress topper). That's actually more product-forward than most of this week's sends, but it also means the email is enormous and the coupon offer — the thing that's supposedly the headline — gets buried under two other unrelated campaigns before you even reach anything with a price on it.
- The category tile grid ("Men," "Girls," "Boys," "Shoes," "Home") right under the hero is a nice touch versus yesterday's more generic banner, and the Back to School section has real segmentation value for a parent persona. But as a subscriber who's seen "Take 20% off" five times since 2026-08-24 (2026-08-24 "IT'S HERE," 2026-08-28's Martha Stewart send), this is fatigue territory — the coupon is now wallpaper, and stacking a sweepstakes and a school section on top makes it feel like three emails duct-taped together rather than one coherent send.
- Verdict: better product depth than most of this week's sends, but the length and lack of hero focus will cost it opens and drag click-through down from the Martha Stewart send's 7.00.

## What's working

- Category tile grid (Men, Girls, Boys, Shoes, Home) under the hero gives instant multi-persona navigation instead of one generic CTA.
- The product grid near the bottom has actual prices and strikethrough discounts (Jeans $26-$29.99 off $52, mattress topper $137.99-$209.99 off $229.99-$349.99) — real, scannable value.
- Rewards status bar at the very top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS") personalizes for logged-in members.
- Barcode-in-wallet close is now a consistent, recognizable brand signature across sends.

## What's weak

- Three unrelated campaigns (F&F coupon, Back to School, SO-Back sweepstakes) are crammed into one email with no visual throughline — it reads like it was assembled by three teams.
- The coupon hero photo is the same washed-out shirt-only model crop used across multiple recent sends — no new creative.
- Subject promises "home, shoes & styles for the crew" but the actual hero only says "20% off," and home/shoes don't appear until deep in the category grid — subject overpromises specificity the top of the email doesn't deliver.
- Real priced products (the actual purchase decision) don't appear until roughly two-thirds of the way down — most readers won't scroll that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the priced product grid (jeans, mattress topper, towels, Ninja maker) up directly under the hero, and cut the Back to School and sweepstakes sections into their own separate sends — this email is doing three jobs badly instead of one job well.
- 2. Replace the hero photo with something that visually ties to the subject line's "home, shoes & styles" promise instead of a generic shirt crop.
- 3. Since this is the sixth F&F variant in six days, swap the static "20%" headline for a countdown/urgency treatment ("Ends tomorrow, Aug. 30") to justify one more send.
- 4. Trim the sweepstakes and Back to School sections to a single link/banner each if they must stay, rather than full category grids.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off + real deals on jeans, mattresses & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% off ends Aug. 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jeans from $26, mattress toppers from $137 — plus 20% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Coupon ends Sunday — shop home, shoes & school in one trip`
