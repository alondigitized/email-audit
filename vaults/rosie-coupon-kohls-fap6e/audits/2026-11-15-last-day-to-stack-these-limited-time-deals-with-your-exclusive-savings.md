# LAST DAY to stack these limited-time deals with your exclusive savings ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the Nov 15 closer in Kohl's Super Saturday run. The creative is a long deal wall: a "Super Saturday Deals" banner with a Kohl's Rewards exclusive (about 30% off with a coupon, "2 days only, ends Nov. 15"), category pills for Home, Women, Men and Kids, then about a dozen product tiles. Most tiles are maroon, and each has a "Shop Now" link. The email closes with clearance at up to 70%, a Door Busters preview, Klarna and same-day delivery, a "Picked with you in mind" row, and coupon barcodes. The render looks clean, with no broken images that I can see.
- It works as a last-day push, but it's the same template Kohl's has sent all week. It follows the Nov 14 "Reveal your savings" sends and the Nov 12 "HOURS LEFT for $10 off" send. "Last day" lands here, but a subscriber has already seen "last day" and "hours left" from this brand several times. The wall of tiles also buries the one offer the subject promises.
- Judgement: competent, on-season and honest about the deadline, but forgettable. It gives the reader a dozen things to look at and no single reason to click.

## What's working

- The deadline is credible: "Ends Nov. 15" ties to the subject's "LAST DAY".
- The Rewards callouts ("take $10 off your home purchase of $50 or more with coupon") sit between tile groups and give members a concrete benefit.
- The category pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids) at the top let people skip to what they want.
- Tiles have a consistent layout, with a price or percentage on a color block and a "Shop Now" link. That makes the scroll easy to skim.
- The coupon codes and barcodes near the bottom give a usable way to redeem.

## What's weak

- The subject's "stack these deals with your exclusive savings" promises a stacking mechanic. The body is a generic grid, and stacking isn't explained anywhere I can see.
- There are about a dozen offers and no single hero, so the eye has nothing to land on after the banner.
- The "Shop Now" links are small text links. The call to action isn't prominent compared with the colored offer blocks.
- The email is very long. Clearance, Door Busters, Klarna, delivery and the personalization row all come after the offers, so late sections get little attention.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" line and the dense fine print undercut the "exclusive savings" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero: the Rewards 30% coupon, with a large "Use my 30% coupon" button and a countdown to midnight. Then show 4 to 6 tiles instead of 12.
- 2. Make the stacking promise real. Show a small "coupon + Kohl's Cash + Rewards" example with actual numbers, or drop "stack" from the subject.
- 3. Replace the "Shop Now" text links with full-width buttons that name the category, such as "Shop jeans" or "Shop sweaters".
- 4. Cut the Klarna, delivery and "Picked for you" sections below the fold, or merge them into one slim strip.
- 5. Vary the creative between sends. This is the fifth "last chance" email in a week, so give the final send something visibly different.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: jeans, sweaters & more up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 30% coupon expires at midnight. Tap to use it.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally functional (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on the main layout tables, responsive media queries). The visible source has accessibility gaps (missing alt text, an empty `<title>`, zoom disabled) and malformed markup. The HTML was truncated, so the unsubscribe footer, UTMs and most links could not be inspected, and header-based compliance checks are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** They are all click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Four stacked tracking pixels appear before the content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/caad9589-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  They overlap in function (open tracking and audience sync) and add four third-party requests. The `demdex.net` pixel is an Adobe Audience Manager sync, which some clients and privacy tools block.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 14,048 of 19,800 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This is the likely cause of the plain-text ratio warning and makes the text part hard to read.
- **Empty parameter in an image URL:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` has no value in a Movable Ink image URL. A dynamic content field may be failing to populate, which is worth confirming against the rendered image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, flagged by QA:**
  - Tracking pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/caad9589-...` (the only `<img>` with no `alt` at all), `o.gif`, and `demdex.net/event`.
  - Content images from Movable Ink (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`): `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and likely more (the QA list is truncated).

  If these are the offer or hero images, a recipient with images blocked or using a screen reader gets no offer information. The pixels should have `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this gives assistive tech and web-view rendering no document title.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">`. `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` block pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` has a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no opener.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Missing `role="presentation"` on two tables:** the 1×1 pixel table and the AAM table (`<table width="1" height="1" ...>`, `<table width="100%" align="center" ...>`).
- **`@import` for Google Fonts:** `Montserrat` and `Roboto` are imported, and the font stacks fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. Gmail ignores `@import`, so it will use the fallback. This is not a defect, just a note.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- Recipient identifiers are embedded in pixel URLs (`mi_u=604230016`). This is expected, but the plain-text URLs and image URLs expose a stable user ID.
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty (see section 2). This is the only sign of a possible unpopulated dynamic field.
- The subject line's "your exclusive savings" could not be checked against the body because the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. Check the raw headers at the source before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is an expected relay artifact, not a confirmed failure. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a subdomain, which is consistent with a dedicated sending domain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not in the truncated HTML, so CAN-SPAM body requirements cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links are wrapped in opaque SFMC redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be assessed from the source. None were resolved.
- Campaign identifiers are visible in the pixel URLs (`mi_ecmp=1025884_20261114`, `email_name=261114_DG_Email_Myster...`). The date stamp matches the send date, so there is no sign of a stale or mis-dated asset.
- No landing-page verification is possible because the HTTP probe skipped all tracked links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the Movable Ink content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`), and `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fill in `<title>`, remove the stray `</custom>` tag and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. Check the raw headers at the sending source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link and address.
5. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the Movable Ink image URLs.
6. Consolidate the overlapping open pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls.com/o.gif`, `open.aspx`) if the analytics stack allows.
7. Follow the tracked links with a real HTTP client to confirm final destinations, UTMs and 200 status, since 79 links went unprobed.
8. Fix the plain-text part. It is over 70% URLs, so it needs readable anchor text or fewer inline URLs.
