- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer fragrance picks — up to 20% off`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `KAYALI, Tom Ford, Phlur + more — shop the summer edit now`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 — Resize Text). `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** applied globally to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td` — this suppresses iOS automatic text scaling and can render small text unreadable on older iOS devices.
- **No `lang` attribute** on the `<html>` element. Screen readers cannot infer language; also fails WCAG 3.1.1.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">`** — the value `no index` has a space and is not a valid robots directive (should be `noindex`). Functionally harmless for email but signals copy/paste error.
- **Single breakpoint at 480px only** — no 600px breakpoint. Many tablet and large-phone clients (600–768px) will render the desktop layout unscaled.
- `display: block!important;display: table!important;` double-declaration on `.show`/`.appear` classes is intentional Outlook fallback, not a bug.
