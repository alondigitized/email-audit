# Ready, set, SAVE 🎉 Black Friday is here with 50% OFF ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong Black Friday send because it commits to one offer. A black sequin hero shows "Savings, Coming In Hot" over a giant "50% OFF" and a single SHOP NOW button. Product shots sit directly beneath it. The eye lands on the number and the button immediately, with no competing message above the fold.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. Two "30% OFF" tiles (Paula's Choice and Fenty Beauty, then Farmacy) are followed by a product grid, a "We Think You'll Love These" row, a Cyber Week 30% banner, a store locator and a holiday gift guide. The second and third tiles repeat the hero's visual formula, so the hero's 50% is diluted by 30% offers. The 4X points banner at the very top competes with the main offer, and the "Hi, Rosie" greeting is the only visible personalization.
- It is better than the 11/26 and 11/25 sends, which stacked several 30% tiles with no dominant offer. It is roughly on par with the earlier "Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊" send from the same day. It is a near-duplicate of that one, so cadence works against it. Overall it is a well-executed, trustworthy promo, but it is not differentiated.

## What's working

- The "50% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, with one clear SHOP NOW button.
- The offer has a visible end date ("online through 11/29") and the 30% tiles say "TODAY ONLY!", so the urgency is credible.
- The top banner shows 4X points with the code CYBERSCENT, which gives members a reason to buy this weekend.
- The product grid shows brand, product name and sale prices (for example Dr. Jart $52 → $36.40), so shoppers can judge value without clicking.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero's 50% is undercut by a 30% tile, another 30% tile and a "30% off select brands" banner further down. The email never says which products the 50% applies to.
- The email is very long. The store locator, holiday gift guide, footer and barcode sit under a catalog that already ran out of focus.
- The hero CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW", and the later buttons ("SAVE NOW") are no more specific.
- This is the second "50% OFF" Black Friday send today and the fifth or sixth Cyber Week send in a row. Subscribers will see it as repetition.
- The fine print is small ("Available on in-stock merchandise only. Excludes fragrance"), and the hero offer carries a dagger footnote. That weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name what the 50% covers in the hero, for example "50% off [category/brand]". If it is selective, say so up front instead of in the footnote.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero plus one or two modules, and drop the locator and gift guide from this send. Move the 4X points into the hero as a secondary line.
- 3. Differentiate this from the earlier send today by sending only to non-openers, or by changing the creative or offer.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop 50% off skincare".
- 5. Show ratings or best-seller badges on the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ends Sunday: skincare, makeup & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rosie, your Black Friday 50% off is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 4X points on gift sets with code CYBERSCENT, through 11/30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated skincare and makeup, marked down through 11/29. Shop online or in store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has a confirmed broken personalization slot (`Hi ,`) and no plain-text part (0 chars). The automated QA run could not verify SPF/DKIM or the unsubscribe headers, so those are unconfirmed. Overall QA pass rate is 38%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 37 skipped. The QA data reports no broken probed links, but the skipped 37 are unverified.
- **Redirect wrappers:** Links go through the ESP redirector, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a21d4f623fa7d5fef4bbc780a0/...?__dU__=...`. Final destinations can't be seen from the source, so landing-page validity isn't confirmed beyond the probes.

  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa...` is a second beacon with no alt attribute. Its `mi_u` parameter looks like a per-recipient identifier.
- **Alt text on pixels:** Missing alt on these hidden pixels has low accessibility impact. Both are flagged by QA, and adding `alt=""` clears the warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing plain-text part (FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed-width layout:** The wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. Unless the truncated remainder overrides it, this overflows below 700px viewports, and the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only target class names.
- **Malformed conditional comment:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>` without a closing `-->`. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it, including `.mobileDisc` and `.footerBanner`.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email clients.
- **Layout and language:**
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - Layout tables have no `role="presentation"` (e.g. `<table ... class="full-wrap tbl_cntr">`).
- **JSON-LD block:**
  - It has an empty `"discountCode": ""`.
  - It uses `PromotionCard`, which isn't a valid schema.org type.
  - Most clients strip `<script>`, so this is harmless but unclean.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting (FAIL):** The QA check found `Hi ,`, meaning the first-name field rendered empty. There is no fallback value. This is confirmed. It may be because the test inbox has no profile name, but the template should still handle empty values (e.g. `Hi there,`).
- No unresolved raw tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`) were reported.

- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured through the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure. The footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm the physical address.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge field, or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the `<!--[if mso]>` block so it sits outside the `@media` rule, or close the comment properly.
5. Make the 700px container fluid, e.g. `width:100%; max-width:700px`, and add `lang` and `role="presentation"`.
6. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication headers against the raw message, not the relay copy.
8. Change `no index` to `noindex`, or remove the tag. Clean up the JSON-LD or drop it.
9. Raise the probe budget so more of the 37 skipped links are checked, and confirm UTM or tracking parameters on the final landing URLs.
