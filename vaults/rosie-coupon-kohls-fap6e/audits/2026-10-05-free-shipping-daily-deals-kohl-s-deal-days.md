# FREE SHIPPING + Daily Deals = Kohl's Deal Days 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the same-day sequel to this morning's "Kohl's Deal Days: Up to 50% off deals + Daily Deals start TODAY 🙌", and it reads like a second pass of that email. Below the top it's a competent Kohl's kitchen-sink promo. The Daily Deals block opens the body (60% off bedding, 30% off Skechers shoes, 30% off fleece), followed by "4 Days of Deals" tiles. Then come clearance up to 80% off, a "Picked with you in mind" product grid, coupon barcodes and a Kohl's Cash earn offer. The "Ends Oct 8" deadline and the "free returns through January 31, 2027" line give it real seasonal credibility.
- The weakest thing I can see is the top of the email. A tall, empty burgundy block sits above the "Enjoy Free Shipping" and "Earn Kohl's Cash" buttons, with only a speck of copy in it. If that's a hero that didn't load, the subject's promise (free shipping plus Daily Deals) has no visual payoff. The reader lands on a blank slab and has to scroll to find the offer.
- Overall it's a solid 6, and it's cluttered. Free shipping with no minimum is a good hook, but it sits in a small button and a footer line. The subject is a sound summary, but it's a second Deal Days send in one day, so subscribers will start tuning out.

## What's working

- "Enjoy Free Shipping" and "Earn Kohl's Cash" sit as paired buttons right under the top block, so the two main perks are easy to spot.
- The Daily Deals tiles are clean and scannable. Each has a big percent-off number, a category, a short exclusion line and a Shop Now button.
- The Skechers 30% off tile gives a recognizable brand name in the first third of the email.
- The "Ends Oct 8" deadline and "Free returns through January 31, 2027" give believable urgency and reassurance.
- The product grid shows original and sale prices (for example the Ninja SLUSH at $229.99 against $549.99), which makes the savings concrete.

## What's weak

- The top block is a big blank burgundy area. Either the hero didn't render or it carries almost no content, and it pushes the offer below the first screen.
- There are far too many offers: Daily Deals, 4 Days of Deals, clearance, a product grid, two coupons and a Kohl's Cash promo. No single message wins.
- Nearly every tile carries "Select styles" or "Coupons do not apply," and the extra 25% coupon only applies to some. The exclusions are everywhere and dilute the headline numbers.
- Every CTA is "Shop Now," so none says what you'd actually get.
- This is the second Deal Days send today, after the 10-04 trio of "mystery savings" emails. The cadence is heavy, and today's two sends are close to duplicates.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or replace the hero. Put one bold line over the top block, such as "Free shipping, no minimum. Daily Deals through Oct 8," with a single button.
- 2. Cut the body to one or two offer stacks. Keep Daily Deals and clearance, and drop the "Picked with you in mind" grid or move it to a personalized send.
- 3. Group the exclusions into one fine-print line instead of repeating "Coupons do not apply" on every tile.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs, such as "Shop Skechers" or "Shop bedding deals."
- 5. Don't send two Deal Days emails the same day. If you do, make the second one clearly different, for example "Today's Daily Deals" with only that day's three tiles.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Skechers 30% off + free shipping through Oct 8`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding up to 60% off, Skechers 30% off. New Daily Deals every day through Oct 8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No minimum for free shipping, plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "FREE SHIPPING + Daily Deals = Kohl's Deal Days"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup defects (a `<div>` inside `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an invalid CSS selector) and missing alt text on content images. It also stacks four tracking and open pixels. The compliance and authentication items the QA flagged can't be confirmed from the data provided, because the source is truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links can't be verified.** 73 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, and the visible links are opaque redirects (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`). Destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Four open or tracking pixels** fire on load:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/88edc211-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1×1)
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023201_2026105` (1×1)
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?EP5KGNFUOMBUVO3MUCVMRBCKI4.60269&d=60269&bmt=0` (1×1)
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (0×0, third-party Adobe Audience Manager)
- The overlapping open pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls`, `click.s`) produce redundant requests and can inflate open counts in analytics. The `demdex.net` call is a third-party request that privacy-focused clients may block.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in `mi.kohls.com` image URLs and looks like a per-recipient ID.
- **Stray tag.** `<img src=".../open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` contains a closing `</custom>` with no matching opener, so the markup is malformed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA flagged these images, and the source confirms the first, third and fourth:
  - `88edc211-…` has no alt attribute and no `aria-hidden`.
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event` has no alt attribute.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) have no alt text.
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x` has no alt text.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` and `images.am` URLs read as content images, so with images blocked the email would have no text equivalent.
  - **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is reported as missing alt, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** There is no document title for assistive tech or web-view rendering.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` there makes parsers close the head early, and the microdata is unlikely to be processed by email clients anyway.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Dead CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches an element named `ReadMsgBody` and does nothing.
- **Invalid CSS value.** `max-height: auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Duplicate font loading.** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice, once by `<link>` and once by `@import`. Both are web fonts, and the `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is present.
- **Duplicate meta tag.** `meta name="format-detection"` is declared twice (`telephone=no`, `date=no`). Only one may take effect.
- **Table roles.** The 1×1 tracking table and the AAM table lack `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncated body can't be checked beyond that.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe and one-click headers.** The QA reports `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) as not found. The QA itself notes AgentMail may not capture them, so these are **unconfirmed**, not proven absent.
- **Physical address.** None was detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the footer genuinely lacks one, it violates CAN-SPAM.
- **Authentication.** `Authentication-Results` is not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown (relay limitation). The From domain is `s.kohls.com`; alignment can't be assessed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link.** It isn't visible in the truncated source, so it can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs can't be assessed.** Click URLs are opaque redirect tokens (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), and the probe skipped all 73, so final destinations and UTM parameters are unknown.
- **Visible campaign IDs.** The image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1023201_2026105` and `email_name=261005_DG_Email_Mon_PM`, so campaign tagging exists on the Kohl's side. Whether it carries to the landing page can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the `59107814_2_2x` product image. Add `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>` or remove it. Delete the stray `</custom>`. Remove or populate the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`. Add a `<title>`.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, drop `max-height: auto`, and keep only one font-loading method.
5. Consolidate the redundant open pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls.com`, `click.s`) and reconsider the third-party `demdex.net` call.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer's physical address.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracked links in a browser to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
