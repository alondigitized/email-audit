# Save 20% | $10 off your $25+ purchase ends tonight ... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth or fifth near-identical "last day" push this week. It follows the Nov 11 "LAST DAY for $10 off your $25+" send and the Nov 10 "Stack 20% off + $10 off" send. The hero is accurate: a green "Take an extra 20%" tile sits next to a bold "$10 off" tile with an "ENDS TODAY" tag. The subject's "ends tonight" matches the render, and it is the clearest part of the email.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long deal wall. It has a military-appreciation 30% banner, a holiday party edit, 70% clearance, a Preview Deals teaser, services, product picks and barcode coupons. The nav buttons repeat three times, and the page runs about 6,500px with no dominant second offer. Urgency is real, but fatigue is higher still. A subscriber who has seen the same $10 coupon for three days will skim it.
- Judgement: a competent, accurate last-chance send. It is bloated and repetitive, and it is not memorable.

## What's working

- The hero pairs two concrete offers (extra 20% and $10 off $25+) with an "ENDS TODAY" tag. The eye lands on them first.
- The subject's "ends tonight" matches the on-image "Ends Nov. 11" deadline, so the urgency is credible.
- The Kohl's Rewards member strip with "Earn 5% Rewards" sits at the very top.
- The barcode coupons at the bottom (SAVEMORE, SHOP10) are practical and redeemable in store.
- Product tiles show clear prices, for example the Sertä mattress topper at $137.99–$209.99.

## What's weak

- The red Home / Women / Men / Kids button blocks repeat three times and take up a lot of the scroll.
- The military 30% banner competes with the hero for attention. It is irrelevant to most recipients.
- The email is very long, with clearance, a Nov 22–28 deals preview, Klarna, DoorDash and Instacart blocks. It dilutes the ending-tonight message.
- Many sends this week have repeated the same $10 offer and the same Nov. 21 deadline. That erodes urgency.
- The "Shop Now" CTAs in the hero are small text links rather than a prominent button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one product row and the coupon codes. Keep one CTA, a real button saying "Shop now, ends tonight".
- 2. Collapse the repeated nav blocks into a single nav bar.
- 3. Segment the military banner to the relevant audience instead of showing it to everyone.
- 4. Add a countdown timer to the hero to make the deadline concrete.
- 5. Skip a send when nothing has changed from the previous day.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, 30% off for military families and up to 70% off clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based 640px template with four separate open/tracking pixels and 76 opaque click-redirect links. The source has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport that blocks zoom. QA's compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts rather than confirmed failures (see 5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **76 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`. The `qs` payload is opaque, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four independent open or tracking beacons are loaded:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/7b009cdb-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  The overlap adds requests and gives inconsistent open counts. The demdex call is a third-party Adobe Audience Manager sync.
- **Malformed markup on the `open.aspx` pixel.** It is `<img ... alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no opener. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid.
- **The first pixel is not hidden.** `<img src="https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/..." width="1" height="1">` sits directly in `<body>` with no style, `alt` or wrapper. It can render as a visible 1px element or gap in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA on 7 images:**
  - The tracking pixels (`7b009cdb-...`, `o.gif`, `event`) are low impact. Only `o.gif` carries `aria-hidden="true"`, and the demdex pixel has `height="0"`.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images are the real problem: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. They appear to be Movable Ink dynamic content, and I can't confirm that from the truncated source. If they are content images, screen readers will read the filename, and nothing shows when images are blocked.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **A `<div itemscope ...>` containing `<meta itemprop>` sits inside `<head>`.** This is invalid. Browsers and some clients close the head early and move it into the body.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.** This is invalid and can trigger a request for the current document.
- **The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional**, but the source uses XHTML-style self-closing tags (`/>`) and `xmlns:` attributes. This mix is valid in practice but inconsistent.
- **Role attributes are inconsistent.** The main layout tables have `role="presentation"`. The 1×1 pixel table and the AAM table do not.
- **Web fonts are loaded twice.** Roboto and Montserrat are each requested via both `<link>` and `@import`. Roboto does not appear to be used, since the body font stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. That is an unnecessary external request.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear. The per-recipient values (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_mid=01a1ca43-...`) are resolved. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers. This is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers before treating it as one.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is expected.
- **No physical address was detected.** The source is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed. Re-run against the full HTML.
- The in-body unsubscribe link could not be verified for the same reason.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible portion.** Tracking uses Movable Ink parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1025678_20261111`, `email_name=261111_DG_Email_Wed_Mi...`) and the opaque `qs` payload. The campaign ID has a date stamp, so UTM-based analytics would have to be attached at the redirect, which can't be verified.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be checked.** The probe skipped the redirects, and the destinations are hidden behind the `qs` payload.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Use `alt=""` on decorative pixels, and hide the first pixel with `style="display:none"` or move it into the existing pixel container.
3. Fix the invalid markup:
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the `itemscope` block out of `<head>` or convert it to JSON-LD.
   - Remove `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
4. Consolidate the four beacons to one primary open pixel plus the necessary Movable Ink pixel. Review the demdex sync for privacy and performance.
5. Drop the unused Roboto font request and the duplicate `@import`.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and the raw headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, the physical address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links (follow redirects) to verify destinations and whether `utm_*` is appended.
