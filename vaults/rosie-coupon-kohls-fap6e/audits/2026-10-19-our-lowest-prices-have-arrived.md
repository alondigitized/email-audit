# Our LOWEST prices have arrived 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, scannable seasonal price-drop email, but it has no real hook. Kohl's is saying "lowest prices of the season" for the whole week of Oct 19–25, and the subject line has no number behind it. The body does have numbers (sweaters from $19.99, fleece from $9.99, towels from $3.99, up to 60% off bedding), but none of them appear in the subject.
- The big change from the Oct 10–18 sends is that the "extra 40% off with coupon" shell is gone. This is a fresh campaign with a new subject and preheader style. That is a real reset after a week of near-identical coupon emails, and it is the strongest thing about this send. The structure is repetitive, though: ten maroon "Lowest Prices of the Season" tiles stacked down a very long scroll, each with one price, one "Shop Now" and one product photo. There is no single hero, so the eye has nowhere to land.
- Verdict: a competent catalog-style send with a flat subject line. Its main job is to make people feel that now is the time to buy. It does that well in the body, but the subject line gives up the best material.

## What's working

- The offer language is concrete, with "Starting at 7.99 / 19.99 / 17.99 / 9.99" and "Reg." strike-through comparisons on each tile.
- The "Select styles on sale now through Oct. 25" deadline gives the week a clear end date.
- The categories are timely: boots, coats, fleece, flannels, jeans and bedding all fit the fall season.
- The Rewards status and "Earn 5% Rewards" in the header, plus the Kohl's Cash strip, reward members visibly.
- The Home / Women / Men / Kids / $10 & Under / $20 & Under buttons give quick navigation to what you want.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a strong reassurance for holiday shoppers.

## What's weak

- The subject line says "lowest prices" but names no category, discount or deadline, so it reads like generic sale copy.
- All ten tiles look the same, with no featured item or size hierarchy. The email is very long and the clearance banner ("up to 85% off after an extra 50% off") is small and easy to miss.
- Every CTA is "Shop Now", so none says what you'll be shopping for.
- "Select styles," "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and the dense footer fine print make the offers feel less honest than the headline prices suggest.
- The "Picked with you in mind" block near the bottom (Ninja, towel, mattress topper, jeans) feels random, and its prices are in a different style from the tiles above.
- I couldn't read any preheader text in the render, so I can't confirm it adds anything beyond the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the best number in the subject line and use it as the lead hero, for example "Sweaters from $19.99, fleece from $9.99". Specific prices beat "lowest prices".
- 2. Make the first tile or hero larger and move the 85% clearance claim next to it. Right now it competes with the first category tile.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific copy such as "Shop sweaters", "Shop boots" or "Shop coats".
- 4. Cut the tiles to five or six and drop the weakest ones (towels, kitchen) so the email is shorter.
- 5. Show the Kohl's Cash earn offer and the Oct 25 end date together near the top, since they both drive urgency.
- 6. Make the clearance banner a full-width secondary hero rather than a small side callout.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall fleece from $9.99 + earn Kohl's Cash 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Coats up to 50% off, plus clearance up to 85% off. Ends Oct 25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with a `lang="en"` root, a mobile media query and an `!mso` font fallback. The HTML has a few real code defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport that disables zoom. The QA compliance warnings (43% pass rate) come mostly from the relay stripping headers, and the source was truncated before the footer, so most of them can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque wrapped links:** 69 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. Example: the preheader link `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...` carries an encoded `qs` blob.
- **Four tracking systems fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/151e027d-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60268`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  This is not a defect, but the redundancy affects open-rate attribution.
- **Malformed markup:** the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opening tag. Strict parsers may handle it inconsistently.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document's own URL.
- No broken links were confirmed, since the probe skipped all tracking domains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. `<div>` is invalid there, and parsers may close the head early and push the following elements into the body.
- **Missing alt attributes (flagged by QA):**
  - Content images with no `alt` at all: `58966978_1sa_2x`, `_2sa_2x`, `_11sa_2x` and `_14sa_2x` on `images.am.kohls.com`.
  - Movable Ink images such as `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` and `f441d45d2128ae53.png`. If these carry text or offers, they are unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked.
  - The tracking pixels `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` also have no `alt`. These are low severity, but `alt=""` is still correct.
- **Missing table roles:** the 1×1 pixel table and the "AAM" table have no `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables.
- **Web fonts:** the `@import` for Roboto and Montserrat is unsupported in Gmail and some clients. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback in the body style covers this.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. Personalization seems to run through Movable Ink image URLs (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_ecmp=1024155_20261019`), which can't be checked offline. The rest of the body is truncated.

## 5. Compliance
These items are unconfirmed:
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both missing, but it notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them. Verify against the raw message headers before treating this as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sender domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Physical address:** QA found none, but the footer is cut off in the truncated source, so this could be a false positive.

No confirmed violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTMs:** none are visible. Click destinations are hidden inside `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` wrappers, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- **Campaign identifier:** `email_name=261019_DG_Email_Monday` appears only on Movable Ink image URLs, not on any visible link.
- **Product images:** the `58966978_*sa_2x` images suggest a product-specific module. Whether its links land on the matching PDP is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` to allow zoom.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`, or use JSON-LD.
3. Fill in `<title>` (for example, the subject line). Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the product images (`58966978_*sa_2x`) and any Movable Ink images that carry copy or offers. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
6. Re-run the QA checks against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer address. Run the footer check on the full, untruncated HTML.
7. Resolve the wrapped `click.s.kohls.com` links with a redirect-following check to confirm the final URLs, UTMs and landing pages.
