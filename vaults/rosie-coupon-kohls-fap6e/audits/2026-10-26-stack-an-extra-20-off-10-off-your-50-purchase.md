# Stack an extra 20% off + $10 off your $50+ purchase 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, busy Kohl's send that pitches stacked savings: an extra 20% off with a coupon plus $10 off $50+. The subject leads with that stack, but the render doesn't. The top hero is a gift-box-plus-Kohl's-Cash image with a small "Ends Nov. 1 Shop Now" strip. The headline "Holiday finds that do a lot (for a little)" is soft. The actual codes (SAVE20, GOSHOP10, JEWELRY10) are buried at the bottom as barcodes.
- It also carries the "The North Kohl's" holiday block that debuted in earlier today's "Welcome to The North Kohl's 🎄" send. Kohl's is now stacking that concept on top of a product catalog. The result has too many modules: home, kitchen, bedding, bath, the elf-village section, a "Picked with you in mind" grid, fulfillment options, and three coupons. It is a clear break from the nine near-identical "lowest prices of the season" sends from Oct 19 to 25, and the coupon stack is a real offer. But the best part of the offer is hidden.
- Verdict: a strong subject line on an email that is too diffuse to deliver on it. It's worth opening, but the click path is muddled.

## What's working

- The subject names two concrete offers, "extra 20% off" and "$10 off $50+", so the value is clear before opening.
- The header strip shows "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | Earn 5% REWARDS", which makes the loyalty tie-in visible.
- The category tiles (Home, Women, Men, Kids) give quick navigation. The home hero uses a big "20%" next to a clean product image.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid shows real products with prices, such as the Ninja SLUSH at $299.99 and the Serta mattress topper.
- The bottom has several fulfillment options: Free Store Pickup, DoorDash and Instacart same-day delivery, and free shipping through Jan 31, 2027.

## What's weak

- The stacked offer from the subject never appears as a clear hero. The coupon codes and barcodes sit at the very bottom of a roughly 6,500px email, below the fold by a wide margin.
- The top hero image of a gift box and Kohl's Cash has little copy. It doesn't say what the offer is.
- The North Kohl's elf-village block repeats earlier today's concept and adds clutter between the offer and the products.
- The email carries too many CTAs: Shop Home, Shop Now, Start Exploring, Style Square, Cozy Corner, Gift List Lane and more. There is no single dominant action.
- Fine print is dense, and "Coupons do not apply to select brands, details" sits near the products. That weakens the sense that the offer is honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer to the top. Use a hero like "Extra 20% off + $10 off $50" with the code SAVE20 visible and a "Shop Now" button. Put the barcodes lower down as a reference.
- 2. Cut the page by half. Drop the North Kohl's village block or move it to its own send, since it duplicates this morning's email.
- 3. Reduce the CTAs to one primary button and one secondary tile row.
- 4. Add a deadline in the hero. "Ends Nov. 1" is currently small.
- 5. Put the 20% off module higher than the Kohl's Cash gift image.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.s.kohls.com`) and Movable Ink (`mi.kohls.com`) build. Its main confirmed problems are missing alt text on 10 flagged images and several malformed or invalid head elements. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and most links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 71 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains), so none of the destinations or status codes were checked. Every visible link is an opaque redirect, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`.
- **Multiple stacked tracking pixels:** There are four tracking pixels, and the first two are in the body before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/502d8273-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  The overlap is redundant and adds load-time and blocking risk.
- **Inconsistent Movable Ink parameters:**
  - `email_name=261026_DG_Email_Mon_PM` appears on `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` and `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`.
  - It is absent on `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`, so attribution may be incomplete for those assets.
- **Empty parameter:** `916d6fa08d185233.png` carries `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value. See section 4.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** 10 images have no `alt` attribute.
  - Seven are the dynamic Movable Ink `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`).
  - Three are tracking pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/502d…`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`.
  - The `rp/*.png` images are probably content images, so with images blocked or a screen reader they would have no text equivalent.
  - The `o.gif` pixel is flagged by QA even though the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. Treat it as a false positive.
  - The `502d…` and `demdex` pixels do lack `alt`. They are decorative, so they should get `alt=""` instead.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" … alt="">` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:**
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, where `<div>` isn't allowed. Parsers may implicitly close the head and push the following elements into the body.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Viewport restricts zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/…)` and `<link>` tags are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is acceptable, and the fallback stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` attributes. This works in practice but is inconsistent.
- **Layout:** The layout tables use `role="presentation"`, which is correct, but the 1×1 tracking tables (`<table width="1" height="1">`) don't.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the retrieved portion.
- **Possible empty dynamic value:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL has no value. If the redemption end date is meant to be populated, this image may render a blank or fallback date. The URL is truncated, so this needs a manual check.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found. QA attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so it is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo require RFC 8058 one-click for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed.
- **SPF, DKIM, DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so status is unknown.
- **From domain:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of `kohls.com`, so alignment is plausible but unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link:** It wasn't visible in the retrieved portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the retrieved HTML. Click URLs are opaque SFMC `?qs=` tokens, so campaign parameters can't be seen at the email level.
- **Landing pages:** No destination was resolved. Because all 71 links were skipped, deal and landing-page alignment with "Stack an extra 20% off + $10 off your $50+" is unverified.
- **Campaign identifiers:** `mi_ecmp=1024540_20261026` and `email_name=261026_DG_Email_Mon_PM` are present on image requests only. The send date in them matches the 2026-10-26 send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and add `alt=""` to the `502d…` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag, move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>` (or use JSON-LD), and fix the empty `<title>` and the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the redundant open pixels if the vendors allow it.
5. Make `email_name` consistent across all Movable Ink image URLs.
6. Confirm that `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` is populated in production sends.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated message with raw headers to verify:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
   - the footer address and unsubscribe link
   - the resolved redirect destinations and any UTM parameters
