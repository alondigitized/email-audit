# ✏️ Save up to 40% during the Back-to-School Sale 📚
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the Back-to-School send Kohl's has clearly been building toward this week, and it's the most on-brief execution I've seen from them in a while — subject says "40% off during the Back-to-School Sale," and the hero delivers exactly that, with a kid in a school hallway locker shot and a bold "up to 40% off back-to-school essentials storewide" banner. No mismatch this time, unlike the recent daily-template sends (8/16's "Your deal is waiting to be revealed" vs. its generic coupon hero).
- Where it falls apart is length and focus. This is a mega-catalog blast — Shoes, Activewear, Basics, Kitchen, Home, Back to College, a sweepstakes, then a hard pivot to a "Semi-Annual" intimates sale with a bra/panty model shot that has nothing to do with back-to-school, then random "picked with you in mind" grab-bag items (a blender, jeans, a mattress topper). By the time you hit intimates and mattress toppers, the back-to-school premise from the subject line is long gone.
- Net: strong hero, weak follow-through. It's a good subject/hero pairing bolted onto Kohl's usual everything-but-the-kitchen-sink template.

## What's working

- Hero image and copy directly match the subject line — kid + school hallway + "40% off back-to-school essentials" is immediate and clear.
- Clean category nav bar (Women, Girls, Boys, Shoes, Home) right under the hero for fast wayfinding.
- Consistent pink/cream color-blocking and bold percentage callouts (40%, 35%, 50%) make scanning easy.
- Specific pricing throughout (e.g., "$1.99–8.99," "$349.99," "$26.00–29.99") with "Shop Now" CTAs on every module.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- The email drifts entirely off-theme by the second half — a "Semi-Annual" bra/panty sale and a "Picked with you in mind" grab-bag (Ninja blender, mattress topper, jeans) have zero connection to back-to-school.
- It's extremely long for a promotional email; by the time a reader scrolls to the intimates section, the original hook is forgotten.
- Repetitive module structure (image + % off + "Shop Now") six-plus times in a row gets visually monotonous with no big secondary CTA to break it up.
- "Back to College" (dorm decor) is squeezed in as just one more module instead of being elevated — it's actually a stronger back-to-school-adjacent angle than intimates or the blender.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep Shoes, Activewear, Basics, Back to College, and the sweepstakes; drop or move the intimates Semi-Annual sale and the random "picked with you" grab-bag to a separate send. A back-to-school email should stay back-to-school.
- 2. Move "Back to College" up near the top, right after the hero — it's the second-most relevant category and currently buried mid-scroll.
- 3. Add a second, larger CTA button lower in the email (e.g., after Basics) to reduce scroll fatigue and give a re-entry point.
- 4. If Kohl's Cash and the Semi-Annual sale must stay, badge them clearly as "Also this week" so they don't read as broken promises against the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off Back-to-School: shoes, backpacks & dorm decor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Locker-ready looks, up to 40% off 🎒`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New kicks, hoodies & dorm must-haves — sale ends 8/23`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Backpacks to bedding, all in one stop`
