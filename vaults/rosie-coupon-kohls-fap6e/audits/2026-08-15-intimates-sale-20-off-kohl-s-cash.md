# Intimates Sale 😊 20% off 😍 Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's daily production template running since 7/30, and today it's aligned reasonably well to its subject — "Intimates Sale, 20% off, Kohl's Cash." The hero coupon banner (extra 20% off) leads, followed almost immediately by a "Semi-Annual" intimates block with a $19.99 bra offer, model shot, and clear category pills (Racerback, Wire Free, Minimizer, etc.). That's a legitimate match between subject and lead creative.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the bra section: a FLX activewear "up to 40% off" banner, then a grab-bag "Picked with you in mind" grid (bath towel, cocktail slushie maker, jeans, mattress topper) that has nothing to do with intimates and dilutes the campaign focus established up top. This is the same structural problem seen in the 8/11 send ("Ultimate clearance") and 8/12 sends — Kohl's stacks 3+ unrelated categories into one email rather than committing to the subject line's promise.
- For a subscriber who opened specifically for the intimates sale, the first screen delivers; scrolling past it dilutes the message. Solid but not tight — a 6-7 range send.

## What's working

- Subject-to-hero match: "Intimates Sale" subject lands directly on a "Semi-Annual" bras sale with a specific $19.99 price and swatch/model imagery.
- Clear, specific offer stack: extra 20% off coupon + Kohl's Cash callout, both visible without much scrolling.
- Category pills (Racerback, Wire Free, Minimizer, Push-Up, T-Shirt, Strapless) give a fast path to a specific fit need — useful and specific.
- No render bugs — layout is clean, images loaded, text is legible, buttons render correctly.
- Rewards status bar up top ("Spend $0 to score your next reward") is a real personalization/loyalty touch.

## What's weak

- The email drifts hard off-subject after the bra section — activewear, towels, a slushie maker, jeans, and a mattress topper all appear with zero connection to "Intimates Sale."
- Model/product images for the bras are underwhelming in variety — mostly one hero shot plus small thumbnails, no size-range or fit diversity shown.
- "Picked with you in mind" grid reads as generic cross-sell filler, not personalization — undermines its own "great taste" claim by showing a bath towel and a drink maker next to jeans.
- Subject line's emoji stacking (😊 20% off 😍 🤑) is spammy-feeling and doesn't match the more restrained tone of the actual creative.
- Coupon code section at the bottom repeats the same 20% off offer a third time, which feels redundant rather than reinforcing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the unrelated cross-sell grid (towels/slushie maker/jeans/mattress topper) from this specific send, or move it to a generic weekly email — it actively works against the "Intimates Sale" promise and costs attention.
- 2. Trim to two campaigns max (Intimates + one adjacent, e.g., loungewear) instead of three (Intimates, Activewear, general home/random grid).
- 3. Show 2-3 body types/sizes on the bra model shots — "All of your bras can be your good bra" is a nice inclusive line but the visuals don't back it up.
- 4. Tone down the subject line emoji stacking — it reads as spam-adjacent versus the brand's actual creative tone.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's ESP (Cheetah/CoherentPath + Adobe stack) transactional-style HTML email with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and widespread missing `alt` text on tracking/content images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open tracking: 3 separate pixel/beacon calls fire on load — `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b90ad418-...` (1x1 img), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1019846_2026815`, and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?QHNDYOP65VYUVMUQSX2XXM7MLY.60269&d=60269&bmt=0`. Triple redundancy suggests overlapping ESP (CoherentPath) + legacy Responsys (`click.s.kohls.com`) tracking, not a single unified pipeline.
- Adobe Audience Manager beacon: `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` fires unconditionally in an empty `<table>` — third-party ad-tech call embedded directly in the email body.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... alt=""></custom>` — a stray, unopened `</custom>` closing tag follows the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel. Invalid HTML; harmless in most renderers but indicates unclean template generation.
- 68 tracking/click-redirect links present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains — not itself a defect, but destination integrity for these links is therefore unverified in this pass).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9+ images flagged with missing `alt` text, including tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `open.aspx` — acceptable since decorative/invisible) but also content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` — these are likely visible creative/promo images and lack alt text, a real screen-reader accessibility gap.
- `<title></title>` is empty — no email title/preview fallback for clients that surface it.
- Uses legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and `.ExternalClass`/`ReadMsgBody` resets — standard defensive email-client CSS, no issues found.
- Google Fonts loaded twice redundantly (both `<link>` and `@import` inside `<style>`) for the same two families — not a bug, but unnecessary duplicate network requests in clients that support both.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) visible in the provided source. Subscriber-level personalization is carried via hashed/opaque IDs in tracking URLs (`mi_mid=01a003ca-8880-7000-856c-431ba8ca3501`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`) rather than in-body merge fields — no issues found in the visible markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — required-in-practice for bulk senders (Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender rules); absence means this message may not qualify for one-click unsubscribe in inbox UI. Note QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than Kohl's omitting it — cannot confirm sender-side absence from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe (POST) support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this data; cannot confirm authentication posture.
- Visible unsubscribe mechanism in-body was not present in the truncated HTML segment provided — cannot confirm an in-body unsubscribe link/footer exists; flag for follow-up on the untruncated source.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — standard email hardening, no issues found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign attribution uses Kohl's proprietary params (`mi_ecmp=1019846_2026815`, `mi_u=604230016`, `email_name=260815_DG_Email_Saturda…`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`) rather than standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` — cannot assess GA/analytics continuity without visibility into the final landing-page URL structure.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm with Kohl's/ESP whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — verify at the raw SMTP/header level outside the AgentMail relay, since the relay may be stripping them before QA capture.
2. Add `alt` attributes to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images if they render visible content (not verifiable as pixels vs. creative from filename alone — recommend visual confirmation).
3. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel to produce valid HTML.
4. Deduplicate the Google Fonts loading (drop either the `<link>` or the `@import`, not both).
5. Follow up on Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) status via a direct-inbox header capture, bypassing the relay, to confirm deliverability posture.
