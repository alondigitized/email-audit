# Homepage snapshot · www.kohls.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I can see the homepage clearly. Writing Rosie's review now.
- Honey, Kohl's knows I'm coming back. My coupons are sitting right at the top — two of them, with real dollar amounts I can use today — and the hero is a woman who looks like me throwing a Memorial Day patio party. This homepage is pushing outdoor living hard for the holiday weekend, and that timing is just about perfect for where my head is at right now.

## What's working

- **"2 Coupons" section expanded by default** — I can read "$10 off $25 in store & online" without doing a single thing. That is exactly the hook that keeps me loyal to this app.
- **"$10 off GOSHOP10" card** — A promo code printed right on the card so I don't have to dig through an email. I appreciate that deeply.
- **"15% off your home purchase"** — With the hero pushing patio living, these two are doing teamwork. I'm already doing the math on a patio chair before I scroll.
- **"My Store: Alhambra"** — Knows where I shop. These coupons work in that store. That personalization makes me feel like a regular, which I absolutely am.
- **The hero woman** — Sun hat, white top, sitting on a patio with a child beside her and big tropical plants in the background. That is a lifestyle image aimed at me, not at my daughter.

## What's weak

- **No Kohl's Cash balance shown** — My whole system is built around stacking Kohl's Cash on top of coupons on top of a sale. Showing me coupons without my balance is like handing me half a recipe.
- **Right coupon card is clipped** — The 15% off card is cut off at the edge. I can read "15% off / your home purchase" but "details" is truncated to "deta" and the rest scrolls offscreen behind a ">" arrow. A coupon I can't fully read is one I'll second-guess at checkout.
- **"Shop Patio and Outdoor Living" is a text link, not a button** — It's underlined, fine, but on a phone it doesn't pop as a tap target. I almost scanned right over it.
- **The Levi's sponsored banner sits between my coupons and the hero** — I came here for my deals and my store, not a paid ad. That interruption slows the flow from "I have coupons" to "here's what to spend them on."
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need that number. Do I hit free shipping at $35 or $49? That determines whether I add a throw pillow to the cart or not.

## Recommendations

- **Show my Kohl's Cash balance in the same widget as the coupons** — One line: "$14.00 Kohl's Cash available · 2 Coupons loaded." I am in purchase mode the second I see that combination in one place.
- **Make "Shop Patio and Outdoor Living" a real button** — Give it a border or a fill. Medium-tech shoppers on a phone need a button that looks like a button, not a hyperlink in a paragraph.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the coupon strip** — A single small line under the coupon cards: "Free shipping on orders $35+" lets me size my cart before I even browse.
- **Push the sponsored Levi's banner below the hero** — First-party deals should flow directly into first-party content. The ad break kills the momentum between "I have coupons" and "here's what to buy."
