# Extra 20% off + $20 off your $100+ purchase 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, coupon-forward Kohl's send. The top banner is "20% off with coupon" next to "$20 off your $100+ purchase," followed by a Kohl's Cash strip and a "Rewards Member, Earn 5%" status line. The deal is clear in two seconds. The barcode coupon block at the bottom (YOUGET20, EXTRA20, a Rewards-only $10 off $50) is genuinely useful.
- The email then loses focus. After the offer banner you get a 40% off shoe hero, a Levi's 40% off block, a "record harvest for fall favorites" category grid and four "picked with you in mind" products. That is a lot of competing messages for a subject line that promised two coupons. Coming right after the Deal Days run (10-08's "Last chance ⏳ Shop Kohl's Deal Days + get Kohl's Cash 🤑" and 10-07's "New Daily Deals ✅ Free Shipping ✅ Kohl's Cash ✅"), this reads as the tenth promo in about five days. A subscriber has already seen the coupon, the Kohl's Cash and the product grid in nearly identical form.
- Verdict: a competent coupon email, but forgettable in this cadence. The subject matches the body, but the body dilutes it.

## What's working

- The subject offers match the top banner exactly: 20% off and $20 off $100+. There is no bait-and-switch between inbox and body.
- The barcode coupon block with codes, PINs and "Ends Oct. 12/18" gives a clear redemption path.
- The Rewards status bar ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn: 5% REWARDS") makes the email feel personal.
- The "Take $10 off your home purchase of $50" Rewards-exclusive coupon and the "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" line add member value.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid shows large prices with strikethrough originals (the Ninja SLUSHI at $229.99 against $349.99, for example). That makes the discount tangible.

## What's weak

- The top of the email shows an empty dark maroon block next to the nav buttons. It looks like a missing image or dead space and pushes the real hero down.
- There are too many competing offers: 20% off, $20 off, 40% off shoes, 40% off Levi's, Kohl's Cash and $10 off home. No single offer dominates.
- Nearly every promo carries "Coupons do not apply to select brands" or "select styles." The exclusions undercut the headline coupon.
- The shoe hero is a big image with a small "Shop Now" link and a lot of empty space. The Women/Men/Girls/Boys button rows appear twice, which adds scroll before any product.
- The legal and fine-print footer is very long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Lead with the two coupons, put the barcode block right under the banner, and keep one category row plus one product row. Move Levi's and the shoe sale to a separate send.
- 2. Fix the empty maroon block at the top and make the first screen the coupon banner with a single "Shop with coupon" button.
- 3. Replace the repeated Women/Men/Girls/Boys button rows with a single set.
- 4. Add a deadline to the subject. The body says Oct. 12 and Oct. 18, but the inbox line has no urgency.
- 5. Give the exclusions one plain sentence near the hero instead of scattering them across blocks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your extra 20% off ends Oct. 18 🎉`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$20 off $100 + extra 20%: use both this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two coupons, one cart. Plus Rewards members get $10 off home.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Codes inside: YOUGET20 and EXTRA20. Shoes and Levi's up to 40% off.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based layout with a 640px container and a mobile media query. Nearly all links go through click-tracking redirects (72 skipped by the QA probe). The HTML has several markup defects (missing image alt text, an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag). The QA header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay artifacts, not confirmed sender faults. The physical-address warning can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 72 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking redirects. Their destinations are unverified.
- The visible links use the ExactTarget/Salesforce redirect format, for example `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwMjQs…`. The destination is encrypted inside the `qs` parameter, so UTM or landing-page checks aren't possible from the source.
- Several tracking pixels are stacked at the top of the body:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9753f5ba-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023504_2026109` (1x1)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?LPCSW4C2TPYE5MYQAA7LST42LA.60264&d=60264&bmt=0` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (0x0, Adobe Audience Manager)
  
  Several open/analytics beacons are redundant and add load time. The `mi_u` value (604230016) looks like a recipient identifier in the URL.
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images carry long query strings (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, `email_name=261009_DG_Email_Friday`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`, `mi_kc_event_pe…`). Some of these parameters are empty, for example `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`. That suggests a merge field that didn't populate. This is only partly visible, since the string is truncated.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- Nine or more images are missing alt text. The QA output lists:
  - Tracking pixels: `9753f5ba-…`, `o.gif`, `event`. Add `alt=""`. Only the o.gif pixel has `aria-hidden`.
  - Content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `58380081_0_2x`, `58379268_2_2x`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`. These are image-served content. With images blocked, the email may be unreadable and screen readers will announce filenames. The list is truncated, so there may be more.
- Malformed markup: `<img … alt=""></custom>` has an orphan closing tag.
- `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers may move it into the body.
- `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the page URL.
- The `<style>` block begins with `ReadMsgBody { … }`, which lacks the `.` or `#` selector prefix and so is ignored.
- Web fonts (Roboto, Montserrat) load via `@import` and `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is fine, but many clients strip them. The fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is present.
- The DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional, while the markup mixes XHTML-style attributes. This is a minor inconsistency.
- `role="presentation"` appears on the outer tables, but the preheader table and the 1x1 tracking table lack it.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source. Empty parameters such as `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` should be checked against the full HTML.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both headers were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this isn't confirmed as a sender issue. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can't be assessed. This is expected via the relay. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, which is a subdomain of the brand's domain and should align.
- **Physical address:** the QA check found none. The source was truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm it. Kohl's footers normally include an address, so this is likely a false positive or a parsing miss, to be verified against the full HTML.
- The in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be verified from the truncated source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- Not assessable. All destinations are encrypted in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, and the probe skipped them. No UTM parameters are visible.
- The campaign identifier `email_name=261009_DG_Email_Friday` is carried in image URLs only. Whether it carries through to the landing pages is unknown.
- The subject's offer ("Extra 20% off + $20 off your $100+") can't be matched against landing pages without resolving the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `alt` text to all 9+ listed content images. Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels.
2. Fill in `<title>` and remove the orphan `</custom>` tag.
3. Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or use JSON-LD.
4. Drop `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Remove or fix the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
6. Fix `ReadMsgBody` (it should be `.ReadMsgBody`).
7. Consolidate the redundant open-tracking pixels.
8. Verify the compliance warnings against the raw message (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results, footer address). Pulling the raw message directly from the Kohl's seed inbox, not via the relay, would settle this.
9. Resolve the redirect chain for the primary CTAs, to check destinations and UTM parameters, and confirm that the `mi_kc_*` empty parameters are populated.
