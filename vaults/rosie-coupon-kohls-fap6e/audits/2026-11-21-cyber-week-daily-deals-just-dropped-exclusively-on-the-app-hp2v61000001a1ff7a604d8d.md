# Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a purple-sequin Cyber Week send that tries to be an app-download push, a sample-bag giveaway, a 30% Benefit deal, a daily-deals calendar, gift cards, Kérastase and a credit card pitch all at once. The strongest part is the hero: a huge "30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics" with a black SHOP NOW button, plus a clean "Great Deals Comin' In Hot" calendar running 11/22 to 11/30. The calendar gives a subscriber a reason to come back daily.
- The weakness is the top of the email. The "FREE* Sample Bag" banner with code BAGITFIRST sits above the real hero, with small print and a $95 minimum spend. The subject promises "daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app," but the body only drops one deal today (Benefit, today only), and the other days are a teaser. Below the calendar the email turns into a catalog (gift cards, 30% off a Cyber Week deal, Kérastase, gift guide, five footer promos). Compared with the 11/13 "Meet the best of the best" send and the 11/14 luxe gifts send, this is the same crowded Sephora template, though the 30% hero is sharper than most of them.
- Verdict: a solid hero wrapped in clutter, with an app-only gate that adds friction for anyone who doesn't have the app.

## What's working

- The hero "30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics" is huge and unmissable, with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- The product shot (Roller Lash, Benetint, Hoola) is on-brand, and the sequin background feels like Cyber Week.
- The 11/22 to 11/30 calendar creates an anticipation and return-visit loop, and it names brands per day.
- The "Today only!" and "APP ONLY" labels add credible urgency.
- The footer benefits (free shipping, Sephora Credit Card, Texts) are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The "FREE* Sample Bag" banner sits above the real hero and competes with the 30% offer. Its asterisk and "$95 min. spend" are tiny, so it feels like buried fine print.
- The 30% deal is app-only, and the only app CTA is the generic "SHOP NOW." The email never says "Download the app," so non-app users have no obvious path.
- The calendar tiles are small and dense. The brand names and logos are hard to read at mobile size, and the 11/22 to 11/30 dates share one button ("SEE ALL DEALS").
- The lower half (gift cards, "UP TO 30% OFF," Kérastase, gift guide, five footer promos) dilutes the focus and adds a second, differently worded 30% offer.
- The legal copy is a long block of tiny text at the bottom, which weakens the honesty read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the sample-bag banner, or move it below the hero. Lead with the 30% Benefit offer and make the app gate explicit with a button that says "Get 30% off in the app."
- 2. Cut everything after the calendar except one module (Kérastase or the gift guide). The gift cards and the "up to 30% off" tile can go to the next send.
- 3. Make the calendar scannable: a larger brand logo per day, a % off per day, and one "Remind me" CTA.
- 4. Put the app download link above the fold for people who don't have the app.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Benefit, today only (in the app)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `9 days of Cyber Week deals — day 1: Benefit 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Roller Lash, Hoola and Benetint are 30% off until midnight, app only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tomorrow: Kenra, Olehenriksen, Milk Makeup. See the full 11/22 to 11/30 lineup.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The `min-width:700px` forces horizontal scrolling or shrink-to-fit on clients that ignore the `max-width:480px` media query.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup in `<style>`:** An `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media` rule. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS, so the `.mso-size` rule is likely ignored in Outlook.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has little practical effect in email.
- **Missing language and roles:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute, and the layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. Screen readers will announce them as data tables.
- **Plain-text part missing:** The QA check reports a text version of 0 chars. This is a `[FAIL]` for deliverability and for text-only or accessibility clients.
- **JSON-LD block:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block includes `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`. The empty value is invalid structured data, and most clients strip `<script>` anyway.
