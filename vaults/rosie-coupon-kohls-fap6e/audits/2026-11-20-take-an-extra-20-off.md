# Take an extra 20% off 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall. This time the subject and hero match. "Take an extra 20% off" is the hero headline, with "with coupon. Ends Nov. 21 / Shop Now" directly under it. That is more honest than the Nov 16, 18 and 20 sends, where "Get 20% off" led the subject but the hero was weaker. The render is very small (about 139px wide in the preview), so I can read headlines and layout but not the fine print.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. It runs from Home/Women/Men/Kids nav pills, to a Black Friday "Dec 30" style graphic, to a dozen product tiles (Garmin/Fitbit, Bissell, Tonies, Beats, Hommedics heaters), then gift guides, Haggar, shoes, a "Preview the Deals" teaser and a Kohl's Cash block. The mix is tech, home, gifts, apparel and footwear, which is the pattern from the Nov 17–20 sends. The tall tail ends in a coupon barcode section and a dense footer.
- Judgement: a competent promotional send with a credible deadline and a clear hero offer, held back by length and by a hero image that is small relative to the page. The subject is the cleanest of the recent run, but the body is the same catch-all.

## What's working

- The subject, "Take an extra 20% off," matches the hero headline, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The "Ends Nov. 21" deadline sits next to a visible "Shop Now" button at the very top.
- Category pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids) right under the hero give a quick route into the catalog.
- Product tiles carry concrete numbers ("Up to 30%", "Starting at 79.99", "99.99", "50%"), so deals read at a glance.
- The coupon code and barcode block at the bottom makes the offer redeemable in store as well as online.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with 12+ product blocks across unrelated categories (smartwatches, vacuums, heaters, shoes, towels). Nothing is clearly the hero.
- Repeated "Coupons do not apply" notes on many tiles undercut the 20% headline. A shopper sees the coupon offer and then learns it excludes much of what's shown.
- The "Preview the Deals" / Black Friday teaser and the Kohl's Cash block compete with the 20% coupon. That is three offers fighting for attention.
- The hero is a thin banner. The first big image is a snowy graphic and a "30" tile that don't show a product, so the eye doesn't land on the offer.
- The footer and legal text are dense and tiny at the bottom, which adds to the sense of a catch-all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one coupon story: the 20% coupon, the code, the deadline, and one row of 4–6 products that actually qualify. Move Black Friday preview and Kohl's Cash to a separate send.
- 2. Enlarge the hero and put a product or lifestyle image behind "20% off" with a coupon code in text, so the offer is visible without scrolling.
- 3. Mark which tiles are excluded up front ("Applies to most home and apparel"), or drop the excluded items from this send.
- 4. Add a countdown or "Last day Nov. 21" at the top to sharpen the urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends tomorrow, Nov. 21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon: last call before Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code at checkout on home, apparel and gifts. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $10 Kohl's Cash on $50+ home orders. Ends Nov. 21.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Take an extra 20% off 🙌"

Only the first part of the HTML was available, so I limited findings to what the source and QA data show. Items I couldn't confirm are marked as such.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, uses a mobile media query, and has working tracking infrastructure. Its main code problems are invalid markup in the head, a zoom-blocking viewport meta tag, missing alt text on content images, and four stacked tracking pixels. Several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 82 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unvalidated. The links are wrapped in `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`, an opaque Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style redirect.
- **Two click-tracking systems:** The email mixes `click.s.kohls.com` (SFMC) and `click.chp.kohls.com` (the `/o/b611334c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` open pixel). Four tracking beacons load before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b611334c-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026315_20261120`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Pixel blocking:** The `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel is a bare `<img width="1" height="1">` outside any hidden container. If its host is blocked, a broken-image icon may render.
- **Malformed markup:** The open pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="…open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`.
- **Empty parameter:** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value, so a Movable Ink parameter appears to have been left unpopulated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid head markup:**
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will move it into the body.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element.
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, including `2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751` and `916d6fa08d185233`
  - If these are product or promo images, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` was flagged for missing alt, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a tracking pixel. `kohls.demdex.net/event` really has no alt attribute, but it is a pixel, so this is low severity.
- **Layout markup:** The preheader table has no `role="presentation"`, though the main layout table does.
- **Fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load through `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, with Arial/Helvetica fallbacks. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved tokens:** None visible in the captured source.
- **Subscriber ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image URLs, which means a persistent subscriber identifier is exposed there.
- **Empty value:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is the one possible failed merge, noted above.
- **Truncation:** I can't check the rest of the body for tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers.
- **Physical address:** The QA check found no address. The footer was cut off in the captured HTML, so I can't confirm it is absent.
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the click domains are on `kohls.com` subdomains, which looks aligned.
- **Plain text:** It is 71.6% URLs (14,574 of 20,342 characters), driven by the long tracking URLs. This raises a spam-scoring risk and is poor for plain-text readers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** I couldn't verify them. Every destination is hidden behind an opaque `qs=` redirect and the probe skipped all of them.
- **Campaign identifiers:** `mi_ecmp=1026315_20261120` and `email_name=261120_DG_Email_Friday` appear on Movable Ink images and are consistent with today's date, 2026-11-20.
- **Landing pages:** Alignment can't be assessed.
- **Report inconsistency:** The summary says "0 issues, 5 warnings" with a 38% pass rate, but the findings list shows far more than 5 warnings.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and promo images. Give decorative images and pixels `alt=""`.
3. Fix the head markup:
   - Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`.
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Remove or populate `<link href="">`.
   - Add a `<title>`.
   - Correct `.ReadMsgBody`.
4. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the Movable Ink URLs.
5. Consolidate the four tracking beacons where possible, and hide the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel inside a zero-size container.
6. Re-run QA against raw MIME headers (not the relay copy) to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
7. Capture the full HTML, including the footer, to check the physical address and the unsubscribe link.
8. Resolve the tracking redirects with a follow-redirects pass to confirm status codes, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
