# Homepage snapshot · www.kohls.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a first — I opened up Kohl's dot com on my phone and instead of seeing my Kohl's Cash and a nice sale banner, I got a big bold "Access Denied" staring back at me. Honestly, this isn't even a homepage — it's an Akamai EdgeSuite block page, plain white with an error message. Not exactly what I drove all the way here digitally for.

## What's working

- Nothing is working. The page didn't load. There is no homepage to evaluate — only an Akamai EdgeSuite "Access Denied" error with a reference number (`#18.9d0ed217.1780137027.b41b3d94`).

## What's weak

- **The entire page is blocked.** The server returned an access-denied response, meaning I never got to see Kohl's at all.
- **No fallback or redirect.** There's no "tap here to try again" link, no friendly error page, no suggestion to visit the app or try a different way in.
- **No branding whatsoever.** Not a Kohl's logo, not a hint of their red color scheme — I could be looking at any generic server error from 1999.
- **Zero information density.** Just a reference number and a CDN URL — completely meaningless to a shopper like me who doesn't know what "edgesuite" is.

## Recommendations

- **Add a branded error page.** Kohl's should have a custom 403/blocked page with their logo, a warm message ("Looks like something went sideways — try the Kohl's app!"), and a link to their iOS/Android app.
- **Provide a human-readable recovery path.** Show a customer service phone number or chat link so I'm not just stranded staring at a reference number.
- **Review bot-detection rules.** If legitimate mobile shoppers are hitting this block, Kohl's is sending real customers away empty-handed — that's lost Kohl's Cash redemptions and lost sales.
- **Add a retry / redirect CTA.** Even a simple "Refresh the page" button styled in Kohl's red would reduce bounce here.
