# Save 15% | Set the scene for summer get-togethers ☀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's summer home-entertaining push, and the narrative spine — home refresh → host more guests → dress for the party — is more coherent than the average Kohl's blast. The "Your budget gets a summer break" line does real work as a value framing, not just a discount announcement. The 40% off on outdoor home goods is prominently badged in the hero, and the Kohl's Cash mechanic shows up early.
- That said, this is the seventh Kohl's email in ten days, and the formula is wearing thin. The subject promises 15% off, but that coupon only materializes at the very bottom of the scroll — a structural mismatch that trains subscribers to skip straight to the coupon rather than engage with the creative. The "Dress for the occasion" fashion module is the same kitchen-sink afterthought that appeared in the May 11 send; it breaks the home-entertaining narrative without adding enough incremental appeal to justify its placement.
- Verdict: a competent seasonal send, slightly more focused than last week's activewear blast, but the coupon-below-the-fold structure and high send frequency are the same unresolved problems.

## What's working

- **Home entertaining narrative arc** holds across all three modules (refresh → host → dress), giving the email more thematic coherence than typical Kohl's scatter-shot sends.
- **40% off badge** is prominently placed in the hero section — stronger discount signal than the 15% in the subject.
- **"Your budget gets a summer break"** is a punchy, benefit-led headline that earns its space.
- **Kohl's Cash mechanic** surfaces early in the email, reinforcing loyalty value before the main scroll.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping elements, no layout breaks visible.

## What's weak

- **15% off is buried at the bottom** — the subject line leads with it, but shoppers who skim the hero get 40% messaging, then scroll-fatigue out before reaching the actual coupon.
- **Fashion ("Dress for the occasion") module feels grafted on** — it breaks the home-entertaining story and looks identical to the apparel tack-on in the May 11 send.
- **No visible deadline on any offer** — neither the 40% off nor the 15% coupon carries a credible urgency signal.
- **Seven emails in ten days** — cadence is at a fatigue threshold; subscribers conditioned to ignore these will skim past even a genuinely focused send.
- **Free sports tee banner** is crammed into a thin strip that reads as visual noise, not a real offer hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the 15% coupon in or just below the hero** — if the subject promises it, deliver it immediately. Putting it at the bottom of a multi-module email is a trust penalty.
- 2. **Add a visible expiration date to both offers** — even "through Sunday" would meaningfully lift click urgency without requiring a redesign.
- 3. **Kill or reframe the fashion module** — replace it with host-relevant apparel (patio dress, linen shirt) that stays in the summer-entertaining story, or cut it entirely and let the home modules breathe.
- 4. **Tighten the free sports tee banner** — give it a dedicated small section with one clear CTA rather than a thin strip that reads as fine print.
- 5. **CTA copy specificity** — swap generic "Shop Now" for something like "Shop Outdoor Entertaining" or "Shop Patio" to reinforce the theme at the click moment.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Set the summer table — 40% off, Kohl's Cash back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patio decor, outdoor dining, and the hostess outfit — all on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 15% off coupon + 40% off outdoor entertaining. This weekend only.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 15% | Set the scene for summer get-togethers"

**From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-05-16

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is rendered with legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional markup and a table-based layout with MSO conditional comments — standard for broad client compatibility. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance headers (likely lost at the AgentMail relay layer), absent alt text on all tracking pixels and several content images, and an invalid custom HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all outbound links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. This is expected ESP behavior, but HTTP probe validation was not possible.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems are active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath behavioral tracking
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears (ESP open pixel)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open tracker (likely Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel

**Malformed HTML in open-tracking block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
<custom>
```
The `<custom>` tag is an unclosed, non-standard element immediately following the tracking img. This is likely a template variable or merge tag that did not render — it will produce an invalid DOM node in standards-compliant parsers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` element is empty:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the window/tab title; an empty title degrades accessibility in webmail clients that expose it.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Prevents user zoom on mobile. This blocks accessibility for low-vision users and violates WCAG 1.4.4.

**Alt text missing on 10 images** (confirmed by QA), including content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`:
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`

Tracking pixels at minimum need `alt=""` (which some have) or `aria-hidden="true"` — the AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) has neither.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading** — Roboto and Montserrat are each loaded three times (via `<link>`, `@import`, and second `<link>`). This adds unnecessary DNS/TCP overhead and can delay render in webmail clients that honor external stylesheets.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) detected in the visible source.

The `<custom>` orphan tag noted in §2 is the only candidate for a failed template substitution.

QueryString parameters on image URLs include `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1012666_2026516` — these appear to be properly resolved recipient/campaign IDs, not unfilled placeholders.
