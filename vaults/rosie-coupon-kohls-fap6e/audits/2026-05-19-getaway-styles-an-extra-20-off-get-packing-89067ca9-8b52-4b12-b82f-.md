# Getaway styles + an extra 20% off? Get packing 🧳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's breaks its 15%-off streak with "an extra 20% off" — the first discount escalation in six sends, and that bump matters. The vacation-theme creative is seasonally on-point for late May, and the hero delivers the offer clearly before anything else. This is an above-average Kohl's blast: the narrative has an actual hook (pack for summer, save more) rather than the kitchen-sink dumps of the May 9–15 wave.
- That said, the structural problems are the same ones this brand has never fixed: "Shop Now" is a lazy CTA on every section, the home-goods block undercuts the travel editorial, and no deadline is visible anywhere in the body. The loyalty card pitch at the bottom is now a permanent fixture that does nothing for subscribers who aren't cardholders.
- The 20% "extra" framing implies a stack on top of existing sale prices — that's genuinely the strongest offer this brand has sent in the entire review window. The subject earns its open, but the body doesn't capitalise on the momentum.

## What's working

- **Discount escalation lands.** 20% vs. the previous six sends all sitting at 15% — this feels like a real reward for staying subscribed.
- **Vacation editorial is coherent.** Beach imagery, flip flops, travel-adjacent copy — the theme holds across the first two sections without breaking into random categories immediately.
- **4.8-star rating visible in-body.** Social proof appears attached to a product tile; rare for Kohl's and adds credibility.
- **Hero hierarchy is correct.** The eye hits the 20% offer badge before the navigation bar, before the category tabs, before anything else.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" on every single button.** Five or six generic CTAs across the email; none tells you what you're shopping for. "Shop Swimwear," "Grab the Looks" — anything with a noun would lift this.
- **Home-goods section breaks the travel story.** "Welcome to the 'I want to have it all home' section" is clever copy but kills the getaway narrative mid-email. This block would belong in a separate send.
- **No visible deadline.** "An extra 20% off" with no end date reduces urgency to zero. The May 17 send had a "TODAY ONLY" badge; this one doesn't.
- **Loyalty card block is dead weight for non-cardholders.** The 7.5% / 20% card pitch at the bottom is the fourth or fifth time it's appeared in this series and converts no one who hasn't converted already.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero.** "Ends Tuesday midnight" (or similar) would transform a passive browse into a timed decision. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Rewrite every CTA button with a noun.** Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop Vacation Looks," "Find My Outfit," "Browse Swimwear" — anything that signals category.
- 3. **Cut the home section or move it below the fold.** Keep the first 2–3 modules travel-only; break home into its own dedicated send.
- 4. **Clarify "extra."** "An extra 20% off" is powerful but confusing. Show the stack explicitly: "20% off on top of sale prices" — one line of fine print in the hero would close that cognitive gap.
- 5. **Retire the loyalty card module** for non-cardholders. Replace with a "Kohl's Cash earned on this order" reminder, which is relevant to *everyone*, not just cardholders.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pack for summer. Save an extra 20%.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20%-off getaway sale starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off select styles — ends Tuesday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Vacation-ready looks, without the vacation price tag.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Getaway styles + an extra 20% off"

**Sender:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-05-19

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders with functional responsive structure but contains a stray invalid HTML tag, empty `<title>`, redundant font-loading requests, and a `user-scalable=0` viewport directive that violates WCAG 1.4.4. Compliance headers are absent from the captured relay headers, and no physical address was detected in the body.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tracking pixels (4 distinct systems):**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath open pixel (personalization platform)
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears engagement pixel (2 separate pixels: `o.gif` and `rp/*.png`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — secondary ESP open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync

Triple-pixel firing (`chp`, `mi`, `s.kohls.com`) for a single open event is over-instrumented; each fires a separate DNS+HTTP round-trip before render completes.

**Click links:** 22 tracked URLs skipped by HTTP probe (all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains). UTM coverage on final destinations cannot be verified from this source — see §6.

**Stray/invalid closing tag:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?GKOC4RWGD3FU7KQF46G4IHRKKY.60256..."
     width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. While most email clients will silently ignore it, it is a malformed DOM node and could cause parser errors in strict renderers.

**Empty favicon link:**
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```
Triggers a redundant GET request to the email's base URL (undefined in email context); should be removed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some email clients (notably Samsung Mail, older Outlook WebView) display the `<title>` value as the email window title. An empty value is a minor rendering gap.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1,
      maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Blocks pinch-to-zoom. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Mobile email clients that honor this directive prevent low-vision users from magnifying content.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading:**
Fonts are loaded via both `<link rel="stylesheet">` and `@import url()` for the same font families (Roboto, Montserrat). This doubles the font requests in clients that support external CSS:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">  <!-- request 1 -->
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...");</style>  <!-- request 2 -->
```
Remove the `@import` block; the `<link>` tags are sufficient.

**Missing alt text — 12 images flagged:**
Tracking pixels (1×1 `o.gif`, `event`, open-pixel beacon) appropriately use `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` on the `o.gif` instance — that's correct. However, content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am.kohls.com` are missing alt text. These appear to be product/hero images, not pixels, and require descriptive alt attributes for screen reader users and image-blocked rendering.

Notable: `52819850_roots_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`) is clearly a product image — no alt text.

This value is identical across all `mi.kohls.com` pixel calls. If this is a seed/QA inbox it is expected; if this appeared in a live send it indicates the personalization token failed to resolve and was replaced with a fallback static ID (or the default template value was never replaced). Verify against MessageGears send logs.

No unresolved `{{merge}}`, `[%`, or `*|`-style tokens visible in the truncated HTML source.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |

| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |

**CAN-SPAM physical address:** The HTML excerpt does not contain a visible postal address. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical address in the message body. This may exist in the non-truncated footer — confirm against full source. If absent, this is a compliance defect.

**One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 / Gmail/Yahoo 2024 requirement):** The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header means bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo addresses will not satisfy the one-click requirement enforced since February 2024. This can depress deliverability and increase spam placement rate.

**`no-referrer` meta tag:**
```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```
Suppresses referrer header on outbound clicks. This is intentional for privacy but means analytics platforms receiving clicks from this email will log referrer as `(direct)` / `(none)` unless UTM params are present on every link — making UTM completeness critical (see §6).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 click-through links are behind `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirects. The final destination URLs and their UTM parameters cannot be evaluated without resolving the redirect chains.

**What is known:**
- `mi_ecmp=1012842_2026519` appears in image-tracking URLs, confirming campaign-level tracking is configured in MessageGears.
- Given the `no-referrer` meta (§5), **every** click URL must carry UTM params to preserve channel attribution — there is no referrer fallback.

**Action required:** Spot-check 3–5 resolved click destinations to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present. The `utm_medium=email` value must be consistent across all CTA links for GA4/Adobe Analytics to attribute correctly.
