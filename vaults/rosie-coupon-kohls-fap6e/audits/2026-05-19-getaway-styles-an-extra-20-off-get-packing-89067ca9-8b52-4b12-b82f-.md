# Getaway styles + an extra 20% off? Get packing 🧳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's breaks its week-long streak of 15%-off sends by bumping to 20% and anchoring around a vacation/getaway narrative — a smart seasonal pivot heading into Memorial Day weekend. The hero lands cleanly: a woman in vacation-ready clothes, "styles that leave money in your wallet," and the 20% badge visible in the header without scrolling. That's genuinely better structure than most of the recent kitchen-sink blasts.
- The goodwill evaporates fast. By the time the home goods grid appears ("I want it all home section"), the email has fractured into five different storylines: vacation apparel, a 40% savings module, sandals/accessories, comfort outfits, and then an entire home department. The Kohl's Rewards card pitch at the bottom adds a sixth. None of these sections is bad in isolation; crammed into one send they fight each other.
- Bottom line: the upgraded discount and vacation hook give this a stronger opening act than the May 15 or May 17 sends, but the structural sprawl limits how far that lead can carry. It's a competent mass send, not a targeted one.

## What's working

- **Discount escalation is noticeable.** Jumping from 15% (six of the last seven sends) to 20% signals genuine event, not noise — a subscriber paying attention will clock the upgrade.
- **Vacation narrative is seasonally timed.** Late May getaway framing ahead of Memorial Day is earned, not forced.
- **Hero copy is punchy.** "Vacation styles that leave money in your wallet" does double duty: category signal + value prop in one line.
- **Kohl's Rewards 7.5% callout is specific.** Naming the rewards rate rather than vague "earn points" language adds credibility.
- **Layout renders cleanly.** No broken images, overlapping text, or lorem-ipsum visible.

## What's weak

- **Home section is a narrative rupture.** Patio furniture, bedding, and cookware have no connection to "get packing" — the subject promise collapses mid-scroll.
- **"Save 40%" module is ambiguous.** It's not immediately clear from the render what the 40% applies to or whether it stacks with the 20% coupon — a trust-eroding moment.
- **Seventh email in ten days.** The cadence has crossed from frequent into fatiguing; the 20% bump is doing real work just to keep the open rate from sliding further.
- **No urgency anchor in the body.** The subject teases "get packing" but nothing in the email body names an expiry date for the 20% offer.
- **CTA copy is generic.** "Shop Now" repeated across five modules and "Explore the collection" once — only one verb+noun pairing in the entire email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the home section or spin it off.** This send has a clear vacation spine — enforce it. If home has to appear, one tile max, framed as "don't forget travel towels / beach chairs."
- 2. **Add an expiry to the 20% badge.** Even "offer ends Tuesday" in the hero sub-copy would convert fence-sitters without requiring a redesign.
- 3. **Clarify the 40% module.** Name the category it applies to and state explicitly whether it stacks with the 20% coupon — ambiguity on stacking is a click killer.
- 4. **Tighten CTA copy.** Replace at least the hero "Explore the collection" with "Shop Getaway Looks" and the accessories "Shop Now" with "Shop Sandals & Bags."
- 5. **Rest the list 48 hours.** Seven emails in ten days means the next one will fight a depressed open rate regardless of discount size.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off vacation styles — ends soon 🧳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pack this: 20% off + Kohl's Cash on getaway looks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swimwear, sandals, and dresses — all 20% off through Tuesday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your summer getaway wardrobe costs less than you think.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Getaway Styles + Extra 20% Off"

**From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-05-19

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard table-based layout with MSO conditionals and a dual Google Fonts load (both `<link>` and `@import`). The primary technical concerns are missing compliance headers (captured only at the relay layer), pervasive missing alt text across tracking and content images, and unverifiable authentication state.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probing was skipped for these; destination URLs and final redirect targets were not validated.

**Three distinct pixel/tracking systems** are firing:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

The Salesforce open pixel contains a malformed closing tag: `</custom>` instead of `</div>` (line: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`). This is a stray element that may cause parser warnings in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**12 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA scan. Affected assets span both tracking pixels (where `alt=""` is acceptable) and content images (where it is not):

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `o.gif`, `event`, `63718a74...` | Tracking pixel | `alt=""` is correct practice — these are flagged but acceptable |
| `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` | Content images (via `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`) | Missing `alt` — screen readers get nothing; images-off rendering is blank |
| `52819850_roots_2x` | Product image (`images.am.kohls.com`) | Missing `alt` — product identity lost without images |
| `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` | Likely content/CTA | Missing `alt` |

The `<title>` element is empty (`<title></title>`), which affects VoiceOver and some email client tab labels.

`user-scalable=0` is set in the viewport meta — this blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility regression.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import url()` simultaneously for both Roboto and Montserrat — redundant double-fetch; no functional breakage but adds latency on cold loads.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

One partially-resolved merge token detected in a tracking pixel parameter:

```
&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```

The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value is empty — the token appears to have been cleared/nulled rather than populated. This is likely a suppressed conditional field but should be confirmed; if it represents an expiry date, an empty value could affect downstream logic in MessageGears.

No unresolved `{{token}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`-style tokens detected in visible markup.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**List-Unsubscribe header not found** — QA relay did not capture this header. If absent at send time, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render their native unsubscribe UI, increasing spam complaint rate. RFC 2369 requires this for bulk mail.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. Gmail has enforced this for bulk senders (>5K/day) since February 2024. Missing this header puts the domain at risk of delivery demotion.

**Physical mailing address not detected** by automated scan. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in the message body. This may be present in the truncated HTML not provided — requires manual verification against full source.

**Authentication-Results header not present** in the captured headers — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the relay capture. `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain; DKIM alignment should be verified directly.

`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set — this suppresses referrer headers on outbound clicks, which may interfere with attribution in Google Analytics if UTM parameters are not present on every link (see section 6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM / Landing Page)

All 22 tracked links route through `click.s.kohls.com` redirects — UTM parameter presence on destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain. Given that `referrer: no-referrer` is set (section 5), UTM parameters on destination URLs are the **only** attribution mechanism available. If any destination URL is missing UTMs, that session will appear as direct traffic.

The `email_name=260519_DG_Email_Tuesday` parameter appears on several `mi.kohls.com` pixel URLs, indicating campaign tagging is in place at the ESP layer — but this does not substitute for UTMs on click-through URLs.
