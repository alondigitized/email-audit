# EXTENDED: Enjoy 40% off for an extra day 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth 40%-off email in roughly four days, and the "EXTENDED" prefix in the subject line creates an immediate credibility problem: the hero banner inside still reads "ENDS TONIGHT." The subscriber opens expecting a reprieve and instead gets the same urgency language that has been running since July 18. That contradiction — extension promise vs. tonight's deadline in the body — is the single biggest problem in this send, and it undercuts whatever trust the extension was supposed to rebuild.
- Structurally, the email is doing three jobs at once: selling the 40%-off extension, re-running the 85%-off clearance angle from July 20, and promoting FLX athletic wear with a third discount tier. None of those jobs gets done cleanly. The clearance section is a straight lift from four days ago; the FLX section ends with "View Leggings & Styles" — a category CTA, not a product one. The email is clean and renders well, but it reads like three separate emails stapled together with the wrong hero.
- The extension headline had one chance to act like an apology and a fresh value prop. Instead it mailed in the same creative.

## What's working

- The 40% OFF hero is immediately legible — the offer is visible in under two seconds with no scrolling.
- The clearance callout (Up to 85% off) provides a genuine second tier for subscribers who already ignored the 40% offer all week.
- Kohl's Cash mention in the footer gives loyalty members a nudge that goes beyond the headline discount.
- The FLX section diversifies into athletic wear — the one product category not hammered this week — keeping the email from being a pure repeat.

## What's weak

- "ENDS TONIGHT" in the hero banner directly contradicts "extended for an extra day" in the subject line — a credibility kill shot that no amount of strong copy elsewhere fixes.
- Three simultaneous discount levels (40%, 85% off clearance, and FLX's separate offer) fragment focus; there's no clear single reason to click.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" — zero specificity, zero pull toward any particular product or category.
- The "Welcome to the 'I want it all' section" label is filler copy that carries no offer signal and wastes prime scroll real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero/subject contradiction first.** Change "ENDS TONIGHT" in the body to "Extended — one more day" or "Final hours, now through [date]." This is a one-field change that eliminates the trust gap. Nothing else matters if the subscriber opens the email and immediately catches the brand in a messaging conflict.
- 2. **Make the extension the entire story, briefly.** A two-section email — the extension hero + a single product spotlight — would be cleaner than this three-category sprawl. Or at minimum, make the extension banner explicitly address the prior deadline: "We heard you — one more day."
- 3. **Give the clearance section its own send.** The 85%-off angle is strong enough to carry a standalone email; burying it as section two here wastes the hook that outperformed everything this week (the July 20 clearance send was the highest-rated in the cycle).
- 4. **Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific copy.** "Shop Home Deals," "Shop Clearance," "Shop FLX" would each add enough specificity to improve click intent.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `One more day: 40% off extended`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `We extended it — 40% off + 85% clearance`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus clearance up to 85% off — both end tomorrow`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your extra 24 hours start now — shop before it's gone`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking links skipped** by the HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears). Redirect chains are expected; no broken destinations confirmed but none verified.

  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/93de04a3...` — Coherent Path open pixel

- **Adobe Audience Manager pixel** present: `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — third tracking domain in addition to the two ESPs.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`<title>` is empty** — screen readers and tab previews get no label.
- **`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag prevents pinch-to-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **13 content/tracking images missing `alt` text** — QA flagged all of them. Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `open.aspx`) should have `alt=""` (they have none); product images (`55869996_root2_2x`, `55405645_root_2x`) lack descriptive alt, breaking screen reader context.
- **Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` appears after the MessageGears open pixel `<img>` — not a standard closing tag, likely a templating artifact left unrendered.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** — empty `href` causes a 404 request to the root domain in some clients.
- **Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` rules for identical families) — redundant round-trips with no rendering benefit.
