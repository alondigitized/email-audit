# Get 20% off & discover the top 100 gifts of the season 💯 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eleventh near-identical promo since Oct 26, and it's a better-organized gift guide than the Nov 3 "It's a great day to take an extra 20% off 🙌" send. The top of the render is a rewards status bar ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS"), then a "Shop Now" button and four category pills. The "TOP 100 GIFTS" hero follows, so the subject's gift angle does show up near the top. The "Get 20% off" half of the subject, though, appears only as small "+20% off w/coupon" tags on individual tiles and again near the bottom ("Take an extra 20% off").
- The email is very long. The body runs from the gift hero through a Sephora event block, delivery and pickup info, a four-product row, and three barcode coupons. The Sephora block stacks 30% and 20% off, which competes with the main offer. A shopper skimming gets "gifts, sales everywhere" and no single dominant deal.
- Verdict: seasonally on target and well-crafted in places, but it tries to sell everything at once. It's a solid mid-pack send, and the repeated 20% stack is what holds it back.

## What's working

- The "TOP 100 GIFTS" hero matches the subject's gift promise and is visually the strongest element at the top.
- Seasonal framing (snow, wrapped gifts, a holiday tree and "Retro Cheer / Heritage Holiday / Enchanted Winter" tiles) fits the date.
- Concrete price points ($37.99 toys, $249.99 Ninja SLUSH, $119.99 Ninja Foodi) make the offers tangible.
- The "A very good gift list for a very real budget" line gives the gift-giving angle a clear voice.
- Free shipping and returns through Jan 31, 2027, plus $5 Kohl's Cash, speak to holiday friction.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long. It has too many modules: gifts, Sephora, delivery, product grid and three coupon barcodes.
- The headline "20% off" is not the hero. It shows up as small tile tags and a late "Take an extra 20% off" block.
- The Sephora 30%/20% off banner pulls attention from the Kohl's offer.
- The top "Shop Now" button and the category pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids) sit above the real hero. That repeats the Nov 3 problem of burying the offer.
- The mix of "w/coupon," "Select styles," "Coupons do not apply" and "excludes Sephora" is fine-print-heavy and hurts how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "20% OFF" and the code in the hero, next to "TOP 100 GIFTS," with one primary button such as "Shop the top 100 gifts."
- 2. Cut the Sephora module and the product row from this send, or move them to a separate send. Keep one gift grid and one coupon block.
- 3. Move the rewards status bar and category pills below the hero.
- 4. Group the coupon barcodes into one "Your offers" section and simplify the exclusions language.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier 20% sends, with a gift-by-recipient or gift-by-price entry point.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is built on responsive tables with an MSO fallback, but it has several markup defects and many accessibility gaps. These include stray tags, no `<title>`, zoom-blocking viewport settings, and about 17 content images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come mostly from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so most of them can't be confirmed as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 73 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are redirect domains. Destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Two tracking stacks:** The email mixes Salesforce Marketing Cloud links (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`) with Coherent Path (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/…`). It also fires an Adobe Audience Manager pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`). That is up to four pixels and redirect domains per open, which adds failure points and image-blocking exposure.
- **Malformed markup:** The SFMC open pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener. Browsers ignore it, but it's invalid.
- **Opaque links:** The `qs=` tokens are opaque, so UTM presence can't be checked (see section 6).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flags about 17 images:
  - Content images with no alt: `59551810_1_2x`, `59551810_2_2x`, `59551810_3s_2x`, `59551810_4_2x`, `59553321_1_2x`, `59392300_roots_2x`, `261103_EM_Sephora-HSE-Secondary-All-Tiers-Happening-Now.gif`, and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` set (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, etc.).
  - If these are linked images, the links have no accessible name. If images are blocked, the links render empty.
  - The Sephora GIF has no alt, so any text it carries is lost with images off.
- **Pixels without alt:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e167e01d-…` (1×1) and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
  - `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so that QA warning is a false positive.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. The parser closes the head at that point, so everything after it is parsed into the body. Schema.org microdata also isn't supported by email clients.
- **Empty icon href:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Broken selector:** The `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` CSS rule is missing its leading `.`. It matches a nonexistent element and does nothing.
- **Font loading:** `@import` and `<link>` loads for Google Fonts are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and other clients that strip them fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, which the font stack handles.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[…]` tokens are visible in the truncated source.
- One image URL contains an empty parameter: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe…`. This may be an unpopulated dynamic field, or it may be a legitimate empty value for this campaign. It can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- Per-recipient identifiers are embedded in image URLs (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_mid=01a1a223-…`), so those images aren't cacheable or shareable across recipients.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the sender. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Physical address:** None detected. The source is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed. Check the footer, which is not in the reviewed excerpt.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. This is a relay artifact, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** Not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** Not verifiable. Links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` tokens and the 73 tracking links were not resolved. The only campaign identifiers visible are `email_name=261103_DG_Email_Tuesday` and `mi_ecmp=1025110_2026113` on image URLs.
- **Landing pages:** No final-URL or landing page data was captured, so alignment can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all content and linked images, including the Sephora GIF. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Populate `<title>`. Move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`. Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">` and the stray `</custom>`.
4. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector (missing the leading `.`).
5. Check the raw headers at the sender for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Check the footer for a physical address and unsubscribe link. The relay and truncation may have hidden all of these.
6. Check that `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` is populated when it should be.
7. Resolve the wrapped links, probing past the redirect domains, to confirm UTM parameters and final landing pages. Consider consolidating the overlapping SFMC, Coherent Path and Adobe pixels.
