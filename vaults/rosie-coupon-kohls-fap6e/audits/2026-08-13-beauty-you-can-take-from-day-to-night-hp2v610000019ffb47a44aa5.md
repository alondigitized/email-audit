## What's weak

- "All Day. All Night." hero has zero product information — no name, no price, no visible CTA target beyond a generic "SHOP NOW," so it's pure mood with nothing to act on.
- The subject line's "day to night" concept isn't reflected anywhere after the hero banner — the eyeshadow launch, the 8-item grid, and the closing modules are all disconnected products with no throughline.
- Eight-item grid has no header explaining why these particular products are grouped (bestsellers? new? recommended?) — reads as an inventory dump.

- Three separate "shop now" mini-CTAs stack at the bottom (fragrance minis, moisturizers, lip lineup) diluting the one CTA that should matter most.

- 2. Give the 8-product grid a section header ("Your Beauty Edit" / "Bestsellers This Week") so it reads as curated rather than random inventory.

- 4. Cut one of the three bottom mini-promo blocks (fragrance minis / moisturizers / lip lineup) — three competing CTAs after an 8-item grid is CTA fatigue.
