# Save 20% | Give your home a cozy fall refresh 🍁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send in three days built on the same three-tier segmented offer structure (30% cardholders / 20% Rewards members / 15% nonmembers) that showed up in the 09-10 "Customer Appreciation" send — but this time it's wrapped in a genuine seasonal theme, "Cozy Fall Goals," rather than another generic percentage-off stack. The personalization is real: the status bar reads "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards" at the very top, and the tiered offer box immediately below reinforces which rate applies.
- Where it falls down is focus. After the tier box and hero, the email sprawls into five distinct merchandising zones — home decor, an "I want it all" 4-up grid, an NFL/NCAA gear break, a "picked for you" product grid, then a coupon code — with no single clear hero product or CTA. It reads like five separate emails stapled together rather than one "fall refresh" story. The bedding hero image and copy are strong, but the eye has too many competing CTAs (Shop Now, Shop Home, Decor, Kitchen & Dining, Bath, Furniture, NFL, NCAA...) to know where to click first.
- Solid seasonal creative undercut by an unfocused, sprawling layout. 6/10.

## What's working

- The tiered discount box (30%/20%/15%) is visually clear with distinct color blocks per tier, and the member's own tier is easy to spot given the status bar above it.
- "Cozy Fall Goals, your budget can get behind" is a genuine seasonal hook with a matching autumnal color palette (cream, forest green, terracotta) that's a real departure from the flat Labor Day red-and-white stack.
- The bedding hero photo (blue waffle-knit bedding, stacked folded throws) is well-composed and directly supports the "cozy fall" theme.
- Category navigation bar (Women/Men/Kids/Home) near the top gives quick wayfinding for non-home shoppers.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, pricing is legible.

## What's weak

- Five separate merchandising sections (home hero, 4-up "I want it all" grid, NFL/NCAA gear, "picked for you" products, coupon code) compete for attention with no clear single hero offer — this dilutes the "cozy fall" narrative the subject promises.
- The NFL/NCAA fan-gear section is a jarring tonal break from "cozy fall home refresh" — it belongs in a different campaign entirely.
- Too many CTA buttons (at least 10 distinct buttons) dilute the primary action; a scanning reader has no obvious next click.
- The coupon code block ("REWARDSDS20... Ends Sept. 20") is buried near the bottom after a long scroll, when it's arguably the single most actionable element in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one theme. Keep the tier box, hero, and "I want it all" home grid; move NFL/NCAA gear and the generic "picked with you in mind" product grid to a separate send — this alone would sharpen the click path significantly.
- 2. Move the coupon code (REWARDSDS20) up near the hero, not buried after four scrolling sections — pair it directly with the "Shop Home" CTA so the discount and action are co-located.
- 3. Pick one hero CTA ("Shop Fall Bedding") instead of stacking "Shop Home," "Shop Now," and four category buttons within the first two screens.
- 4. Trim the 4-up "I want it all" grid to 2 items relevant to the fall/cozy theme (Bedding, Bath) and cut Kitchen & Dining/Furniture, which don't reinforce the seasonal hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cozy up for less: 20% off fall home refresh`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall bedding upgrade is 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, bath & decor — up to 30% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards Members: your 20% off is ready to use`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's — "Save 20% | Give your home a cozy fall refresh" (260911_DG_Email_Friday)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-ESP-vendor Kohl's template (CoherentPath + Adobe/Demdex + Kohl's mi.kohls.com tracking stack) with heavy pixel tracking and MSO/Outlook conditional markup; automated QA shows deliverability-header gaps and widespread missing alt text, but rendering markup itself is well-formed for cross-client compatibility.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Multiple concurrent tracking systems fire on load: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/89540b46-...` (1x1 open beacon), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel, `mi_ecmp=1021596_2026911`), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?JPE2URC5PUMUTHWCZ52F2R6H7M.60265` (Sailthru/CoherentPath-style open tracker), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon) — four separate open-tracking calls before body content renders.
- All hyperlinks are routed through `click.s.kohls.com` redirect wrappers (63 total tracking-domain links per QA), consistent with standard click-tracking; automated HTTP probing was skipped for these (expected/by design for redirect wrappers, not a defect).
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom></div>` — a stray, unopened `</custom>` closing tag appears after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML; most email clients will silently ignore it, but it should be cleaned up at the source template level.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 of 10 flagged images have no `alt` attribute, including the primary content hero image (`57448461_Product_fahm26_Home_Hero_GIF.gif`) — with images blocked (default in Outlook/Gmail on first load), sighted users relying on alt fallback text and screen-reader users both lose all context for the hero visual.
- Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `up/88954bbbbcab3c0e`, `rp/*.png`) also lack `alt=""`; these are non-content beacons and should carry empty `alt=""` (some already do via `aria-hidden="true"`, e.g. the `open.aspx` pixel, but most do not) to avoid announcing raw filenames to assistive tech.
- MSO conditional blocks (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`), `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media max-width:500px` rules are present and structured correctly for Outlook/mobile fallback — no issues found here.
- `<title></title>` is empty — minor, does not affect rendering but is a missed opportunity for inbox preview/tab labeling in webmail clients that surface it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) in the truncated source.
- `mi_cid` (`67791fba63db790b`) and `mi_mid` (`01a091d7-4780-7000-be75-232b7c1a2898`) recipient-identifier params are populated (not literal placeholders), indicating personalization tokens resolved correctly at send time.
- No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected. Required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo (per their 2024 bulk-sender mandates); either genuinely absent or stripped by the AgentMail relay — cannot be distinguished from HTML alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — same caveat; without it, one-click unsubscribe cannot function even if `List-Unsubscribe` is present.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the parsed HTML (truncated source ends before footer, so this may be a truncation artifact rather than a true absence — footer region was not included in the source provided).
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not detected — status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm authentication pass/fail from available data.
- Net: the three header-level warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay not surfacing/forwarding raw SMTP headers rather than confirmed sender-side failures — flag as "unknown/unverifiable," not confirmed violations, except the missing footer address, which should be re-checked against the full (non-truncated) HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking is consistent across pixels: `mi_ecmp=1021596_2026911`, `email_name=260911_DG_Email_Friday`, `mi_u=604230016` appear uniformly on the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-permission pixels.
- All content links route through `click.s.kohls.com` wrapper redirects rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs to kohls.com — actual landing-page UTM parameters are only visible after redirect resolution, which the QA pass intentionally skipped (63 links). Cannot confirm final landing-page UTM alignment or working destination URLs from this data; recommend a follow-up unwrapped-redirect check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm with the sending platform (CoherentPath/Kohl's ESP) whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — re-run the QA check against raw SMTP headers outside the AgentMail relay to rule out a relay-stripping false positive.

3. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag following the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
4. Verify the physical mailing address is present in the email footer (not visible in the truncated source provided) — required for CAN-SPAM regardless of relay behavior.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 63 wrapped `click.s.kohls.com` links by resolving redirects to confirm they land on live, UTM-tagged pages and don't 404/loop.
