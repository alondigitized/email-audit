# Save 20% 😃 Shop Doorbusters 🤩 Get up to 50% off Black Friday Deals 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same ~12,000px Kohl's Black Friday deal wall that has landed all week, now with a Thanksgiving greeting on top. Compared with yesterday's "Doorbusters 🛍️ Extra 20% off 🛍️ $15 Kohl's Cash" send, the subject adds nothing new. It repeats the same three hooks: 20% off, Doorbusters, and up to 50% off. The render is clean, but I could only read it at a heavily reduced size. Small copy like fine print and the preheader wasn't legible, so I'm not judging those.
- The top of the email works. There's a "Happy Thanksgiving!" banner, a snowy hero with "Black Friday Deals" and 50%/20% badges, a $15 Kohl's Cash block, a free-shipping strip, and a "Win up to $100 Kohl's Cash" sweepstakes tile. Below that, a gold-striped "Door Busters" header introduces a grid of price-led product tiles with "Shop Now" buttons. The structure is organised, but it's long and dense.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but it is the fourth or fifth near-identical send in four days. The subject's three emojis and three stacked offers read as noise. Treat it as a reliable repeat, not a standout.

## What's working

- The Thanksgiving greeting and "Doorbusters" framing match the actual holiday moment.
- The hero stacks the offers in a clear order: 50% off, 20% off, then $15 Kohl's Cash.
- The "Door Busters" section header is bold, with gold striping that separates it from the rest.
- Product tiles carry large price or percent callouts and a consistent "Shop Now" button, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- Named category blocks (Style Square, Gift List Lane, Shark, LEGO) add some hierarchy. The category nav row near the top helps shoppers jump to their section.
- The render is clean. I saw no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- It runs ~12,000px with dozens of tiles and no single hero product. The eye has nowhere to settle after the hero.
- Every CTA reads "Shop Now". None names a product or category.
- Many tiles say "w/coupon" or "Select styles", which makes the real price hard to judge. The coupon dependency makes the offer feel conditional.
- The subject uses three different emojis and three offers, which looks spammy. It also repeats Tuesday's and Wednesday's hooks, so it doesn't stand out in the inbox.
- Competing blocks (sweepstakes, Kohl's Cash, free shipping, the 20% coupon) all sit in the first screen and dilute each other.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to one clear hero, such as the doorbuster of the day, plus 4–6 category tiles. Right now the length buries the best offer.
- 2. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs, such as "Shop doorbuster deals" or "Shop fleece $10.39".
- 3. Show the coupon-applied price on each tile, so "w/coupon" stops being a hidden condition.
- 4. Use one emoji in the subject at most, and lead with the one offer that differs from earlier sends.
- 5. Don't send a fourth Black Friday email without a new angle, such as a countdown, a limited stock count, or a Rewards-only perk.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is a standard table layout with a responsive `@media (max-width: 500px)` block. The source has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. Four tracking or measurement beacons sit above the preheader. The compliance checks are mostly inconclusive because the headers and footer weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 89 tracking links on `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` and `click.chp.kohls.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destination validity and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Four open or measurement pixels** load before any content:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5d0e8614-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026772_20261126`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, rendered with `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Stray `</custom>` tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is `<img … alt=""></custom>`. This closing tag has no opener and is invalid HTML.
- **Empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`:** The image URL `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png?…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…` carries this parameter with no value. See section 4.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** The QA flagged alt as missing on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`. If these are content or promo imagery, the email is unreadable with images blocked and to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels:** The same QA list includes the tracking pixels `o.gif`, `event` and `5d0e8614-…`. The first two pixels are wrapped in `aria-hidden` or have `alt=""`, so they are low priority. The `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel and the `demdex` pixel have no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** Some clients and screen readers use this.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` there, so everything after it, including `</head>`, is mis-parsed. The `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` also has an empty href, and the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
- **Web fonts:** The `@import` and `<link>` fonts from `fonts.googleapis.com` are guarded by `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks are present. No issue.
- **Inconsistent hiding:** The 1×1 pixel images use `width="1" height="1"`, and the first has no `alt`. The `demdex` image uses `width="0"`, and some clients ignore zero dimensions.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[[ ]]`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **Possible empty merge value:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is blank on `916d6fa08d185233.png`. If this is a Kohl's Cash redemption end date, the field resolved to empty for this seed address. This is plausibly benign for a non-member, but it can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` is a subscriber-level identifier embedded in every `mi.kohls.com` image URL. This is a privacy note, not a functional bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **Physical address:** The QA reports none detected, but the HTML source provided is truncated before the footer. This is **unconfirmed**.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is absent because the relay didn't capture it. No pass or fail evidence is available, and `s.kohls.com` is the visible From domain.
- **Plain-text part:** 15,813 of 21,620 characters (73%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. Tracking URLs make the text part link-heavy. This is a spam-filter and accessibility risk for text-only readers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters observed:** Links use opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, and attribution uses proprietary `mi_*` parameters.
  - Image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1026772_20261126`.
  - Image URLs carry `email_name=261126_DG_Email_Thu_PM`.
  - The date stamp `20261126` matches the send date.
- **Landing pages not verified:** The click redirects were skipped, so destination URLs, final-URL parameters and landing alignment can't be checked from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag after the `open.aspx` pixel.
2. Move the `<div itemscope>` out of `<head>`, or use JSON-LD or inline body markup instead.
3. Populate `<title>`, drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`, and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3, and `alt=""` to the three remaining decorative pixels.
5. Reduce the URL-to-text ratio in the plain-text part, for example by shortening tracking links or trimming duplicate links.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and the footer address. The current QA gaps may be relay artifacts.
7. Verify the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` against a seed with a Kohl's Cash balance.
8. Run a redirect-following check on a sample of the 89 click links to confirm landing URLs and any UTM carryover.
