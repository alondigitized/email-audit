# HOURS LEFT: 20% off savings are almost gone ...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's leaning on the same broken-hero pattern flagged repeatedly in this brand's recent history — July 27's clipped "20% off," July 28's garbled starburst — and it's happening again, arguably worse this time. The top banner is a visual mess: "EXTRA EXTRA EXTRA" repeats in a tiled watermark behind a red ribbon reading "ENDS," with a percentage number that's largely obscured/cut off before "% off with coupon. Ends July 28." The single most important pixel in a "hours left" urgency email is illegible on first glance.
- Below the broken hero, the email actually recovers well — clean category nav buttons, a legible "ENDS TODAY / Extra 20%" model shot, and a well-organized stack of home and back-to-school sections with clear pricing (up to 40%, starting at $3.99, up to 40% again) and consistent Shop Now CTAs. The wallet coupon section at the bottom with barcodes and PINs is a nice concrete touch. But the subject promises "hours left" urgency and the header undermines that promise visually before the reader gets anywhere.
- Judgement: functional email dragged down by a genuinely broken hero render, again. This is a repeat production defect, not a one-off — three sends in a row (7/27, 7/28 first send, and now this) have had a visually compromised top banner.

## What's working

- Category nav (Women/Men/Kids/Home) renders cleanly as four solid, legible buttons right up top.
- The "ENDS TODAY — Extra 20%" model shot mid-email is sharp, on-brand, and pairs a real product-category offer with lifestyle imagery.
- Home goods section (kitchen/dining, bedding, bath) has clear, specific pricing call-outs (Up to 40%, Starting at $3.99, Up to 40%) each with its own Shop Now link.
- Back to School section uses real photography across six sub-categories (Girls, Juniors, Shoes, Boys, Young Men, Backpacks) — good breadth without feeling cluttered.
- Bottom-of-email wallet coupons show actual scannable barcodes, codes, and PINs — concrete and usable in-store.

## What's weak

- The hero banner — the email's first impression — is visually broken: "EXTRA EXTRA EXTRA" text tiles/bleeds behind the "ENDS" ribbon and the percentage figure is cut off/illegible.
- This is the third consecutive send with a damaged top banner (per history: July 27 clipped hero, July 28 garbled starburst) — a recurring production bug, not a one-time glitch.
- Subject says "Hours Left" but nothing in the visible hero communicates a countdown or time pressure — the urgency has to be inferred from "Ends July 28" in small text.
- No loyalty/rewards personalization beyond the generic "Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn 5% Rewards" strip at the very top — no name, no personalized offer tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero banner rendering before anything else ships.** This is the third broken hero in a row — treat it as a P0 production bug, not a copy tweak. QA the coupon/percent badge graphic across clients before send.
- 2. Make the "hours left" urgency visible in the hero itself (a countdown-style callout, not just tiny "Ends July 28" text) so the subject line's promise is paid off visually.
- 3. Add first-name personalization near the top ("Alon, here's your extra 20%") to differentiate from the last five near-identical Kohl's sends this week.
- 4. Move the wallet-coupon barcodes higher — they're the most actionable, concrete content in the email and currently require a full scroll to reach.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Kohl's promotional email built on a standard MSO-compatible table layout with multiple third-party tracking pixels; core rendering is sound, but the message fails several compliance and accessibility checks confirmed by both the HTML source and QA scan.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary CTA and body links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Cordial/CoherentPath ESP click-tracking) with an encrypted `cp_tp` payload — expected redirect-wrapping behavior, not a defect.
- 60 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected for click-tracking domains; not itself an error, but means final destination URLs are unverified in this pass).
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open pixel), `kohls.demdex.net/event` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-permission/retargeting pixels. No single point of failure, but redundant open-tracking (three separate open pixels) is worth flagging as inefficiency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **8 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA: the `chp.kohls.com` tracking pixel, `mi.kohls.com/p/up` open pixel, `demdex.net/event` beacon, and five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels. These are all tracking/beacon images (0x0 or 1x1), so missing alt is low-impact for screen readers but should still carry `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` for spec compliance — only the AAM pixel and one `mi.kohls.com` pixel currently do this correctly.
- Responsive breakpoints defined via `@media (max-width: 500px)` with appropriate `.container`, `.w_50pc`/`.w_60pc`/`.w_35pc` fluid-width classes — standard responsive email pattern, no issues found.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) present for Outlook `PixelsPerInch` handling — no issues found.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... /></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag appears after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel inside a `<div>`. Not expected to break rendering in email clients (most HTML email renderers are lenient), but it's invalid HTML and should be cleaned up at the source template level.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) detected in the truncated source.
- Tracking parameters include recipient-level identifiers (`mi_mid=019fa7f3-ca80-7000-843b-91f24741ca7e`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`) — functioning as expected for personalized click attribution, not a merge-token failure.
- No issues found in visible source, though full personalization can't be confirmed since HTML was truncated before the body content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 2369 one-click unsubscribe header missing or not captured by relay. Cannot confirm mailbox-level unsubscribe support.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click POST unsubscribe unsupported/unconfirmed, required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (effective since Feb 2024).
- **No physical mailing address detected** (QA WARN) — CAN-SPAM requires a physical postal address in every commercial email; not present in the scanned content (may be in truncated footer — flagging as unconfirmed/high-priority to verify).
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown via the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm sender authentication for this message.
- Sender domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated subdomain, consistent with standard ESP authentication practice, but this is not itself evidence that SPF/DKIM passed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use proprietary CoherentPath/Cordial tracking params (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_ecmp=1018547_2026728`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — this is expected for Kohl's ESP stack, not a defect, but means GA/UTM-based attribution won't apply directly unless the landing page independently maps these IDs.
- Because all 60 outbound links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-tracking domains), landing page destination and alignment could not be verified in this audit — recommend manual click-through of primary CTA to confirm it resolves to the intended offer page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in the raw MIME (may be relay-stripped rather than genuinely absent) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
2. Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the (truncated) footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` independent of the AgentMail relay capture.
4. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the 6 tracking pixels currently missing them, matching the pattern already used on the AAM and `mi.kohls.com` pixels.
5. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
6. Manually verify the primary CTA's `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect resolves to a live, matching landing page, since automated probing skipped it.
