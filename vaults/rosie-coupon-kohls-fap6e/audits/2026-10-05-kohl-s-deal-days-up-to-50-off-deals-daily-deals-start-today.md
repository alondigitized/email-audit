# Kohl’s Deal Days: Up to 50% off deals + Daily Deals start TODAY 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a proper event email. "Deal Days" gets a bold magenta hero with "Up to 50% off" and "Plus, take an extra 25% off with coupon." Kohl's Cash and Rewards sit right under it. The render is clean and the offer is easy to read. The Deal Days framing is a real improvement over this week's "mystery savings" sends, which promised a reveal the body never delivered.
- The body is still the usual Kohl's kitchen sink, and it runs to roughly 8,400px. The daily-deal tiles cover kitchen appliances, intimates, jeans, dress shirts, bedding, towels, smart devices, toys, fleece and shoes. There is a Clearance banner, a "Picked with you in mind" product grid, a Kohl's Cash promo, a coupon code and a barcode. Nothing is the hero after the first screen. Almost every tile carries a different percentage (50%, 25%, 40%, 80%), which dilutes the headline.

## What's working

- The Deal Days hero is bold and the "Up to 50% off" number is readable at a glance.
- The "Daily Deals start TODAY" and "Ends Oct 8" framing gives credible, specific urgency, unlike the vague "mystery savings" sends of 10-04.
- Product tiles show real photos with a large offer number (50%, 25%, $18.74, $19.99) and a "Shop Now" link.
- Kohl's Rewards members' "$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more" callouts add loyalty value.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long, with 10+ daily-deal tiles plus Clearance, a picks grid, a coupon and a barcode. The ending is a pile of competing offers.
- Offer math is stacked and confusing: "50% off + 25% off w/coupon," "up to 50%," "up to 80%," "starting at $2.99."
- Nearly every CTA reads "Shop Now," so none is specific to the product above it.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Select styles" exclusions repeat throughout, and the dense fine print at the bottom undercuts the honesty of the offer.
- This is yet another near-daily Kohl's promo, following the 10-02 to 10-04 sends, so cadence fatigue is likely.

- 1. Cut the body to the hero, 4 to 5 daily-deal tiles and one coupon block. Move the rest to the site. This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Pick one headline mechanic and make every tile support it. Right now "50% off," "25% off with coupon," $10 Rewards and Kohl's Cash all compete.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop kitchen deals," "Shop jeans from $18.74" and "Shop 4 Days of Deals."
- 4. Put the coupon code (YOUSAVE25) and the Oct 8 end date near the top, not at the bottom.
- 5. Cut the subject to under 50 characters and drop the emoji, or lead with the daily-deal hook.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Deal Days email

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based layout with `lang="en"` and responsive media queries, but it has several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, invalid elements in `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and zoom lock. Four stacked tracking pixels and many images without alt text are also present. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains. Examples are `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMjB9...`. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Four open or tracking pixels load before the first visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1365de24-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  This is redundant, and each adds a third-party request. The demdex pixel (`width="0" height="0"`) is the Adobe Audience Manager sync.
- **Opaque `qs=` tokens hide the real destinations.** UTM parameters can't be read from the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This hurts webmail tab titles and accessibility tooling.
- **Viewport zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` there and treat the rest as body content. Also, `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` open tag.
- **Broken CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Missing alt text.** The QA tool flagged these images:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x`

  These look like content images, so with images off the offer content may be blank. The first tracking pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`) and the demdex pixel also lack an `alt` attribute. The QA flag on `o.gif` appears to be a false positive, because the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image.
- **Doctype and fonts.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is mixed with `role="presentation"` and self-closing syntax, which is tolerable. Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import`; clients that strip them fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered tokens** (such as `%%`, `{{ }}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source.
- **Empty parameter.** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...`, an empty value. This may be an unpopulated dynamic field, but it can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Identifiers in URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image URLs, along with the internal campaign name `email_name=261005_DG_Email_Monday`. This is subscriber-level data exposed in request URLs, not a rendering bug.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer not visible.** The HTML is truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.
- **Sender.** The From domain `s.kohls.com` is consistent with the tracking domains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All click URLs are opaque `qs=` redirects that were not probed, so the UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. The only visible campaign identifier is `email_name=261005_DG_Email_Monday` on image URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fill in `<title>` and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`, and delete the stray `</custom>`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` to the pixels that have no `alt` attribute.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels to reduce third-party calls.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have dropped them. Re-run the check on the untruncated source to confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links manually to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
8. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter.
