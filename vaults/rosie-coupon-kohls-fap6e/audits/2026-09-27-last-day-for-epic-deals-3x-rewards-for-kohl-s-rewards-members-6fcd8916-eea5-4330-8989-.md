# LAST DAY for Epic Deals & 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh "EPIC DEALS" send since 09-20, and it's the same purple hero, hot-pink tile-stamp, coupon-barcode-footer skeleton as every prior one — the only real change is the "LAST DAY" urgency wrapper and a reshuffled grid (Ninja blender, towels, jeans, mattress topper). The urgency framing ("ENDS TODAY," "LAST DAY," 3x rewards deadline) is the one genuinely useful addition this week, but it's bolted onto a template so worn that even legitimate urgency will read as more of the same. Nothing here is broken, but nothing here is fresh either — this is a cadence problem more than an execution problem.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" badge top-right and "LAST DAY" in the subject give real, credible urgency (deadline: Sept 27).
- Clear category nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) up top helps scanning.
- Kohl's Cash 3x rewards banner is prominent and ties to a specific date range (Sept 27–Oct 4).
- No render bugs — all product images loaded, pricing tiles are legible, layout is intact end to end.
- Grid covers a wide span of categories (apparel, home, toys, jewelry, appliances), so most browsers will find at least one relevant tile.

## What's weak

- Visually identical to the six-plus "EPIC DEALS" sends this week — same purple masthead, same hot-pink price stamps, same barcode-coupon wall at the footer.
- No single hero offer — it's a long undifferentiated scroll of ~15 tiles, so the eye has nowhere specific to land.
- "Coupons do not apply" appears repeatedly on deal tiles (Huggar suits, floor care), undercutting the "epic" framing with fine-print friction.
- Toy bundle age-range tiles (0-12 Months through 12+ Years) are generic and not targeted to any visible recipient persona.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with ONE hero deal above the fold tied to the urgency message — right now "LAST DAY" sits over a generic bag/purse image with no specific offer attached, wasting the strongest asset in the email.
- 2. Break the visual sameness — even a different accent color or hero photo this week would help subscribers distinguish this from the prior six sends.
- 3. Move "Coupons do not apply" language out of the primary tile copy (small note or footer) so it doesn't visually compete with the deal price.
- 4. Trim the tile count — 15+ categories in one scroll dilutes urgency; a "last day" email should feel curated, not exhaustive.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cart's waiting — deals and 3x rewards both end at midnight.`

1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's Coherent Path-templated promotional email with heavy multi-pixel tracking; core links are functional but compliance headers and image accessibility are incomplete per QA scan.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links (click.chp.kohls.com, click.s.kohls.com) were skipped by automated HTTP probing — link destinations not independently verified in this pass.
- Multiple beacon/pixel endpoints present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/3ff8d33e-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?...`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?UKMCXKPPB6DULFMUZS25SPHOZ4...`, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager). - `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present in `<head>` — expected for transactional/marketing email, not a rendering defect.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 11 images flagged missing `alt` text, including content images (e.g. `57746756_2_2x` — product image) and multiple 1x1 tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, various `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). Tracking pixels missing alt is low-impact (some carry `aria-hidden="true"` already, e.g. the `mi.kohls.com` beacon), but the product image (`57746756_2_2x`) missing alt text is a genuine screen-reader accessibility gap.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (`HTML 4.01 Transitional`) and MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) confirm Outlook-targeted defensive markup — standard for this ESP.
- Responsive `@media (max-width: 500px)` block present with mobile-specific width/class overrides — mobile rendering path appears intact.
- Stray malformed markup: `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag appears after the no-referrer pixel `<div>` — invalid HTML that should be cleaned up, though unlikely to affect major clients' rendering.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- Query params on tracking links (`mi_cid=67791fba63db790b`, `mi_mid=01a0e361-4d80-7000-98be-491c961039d2`, `mi_u=604230016`) indicate per-recipient personalization tokens are populated (non-placeholder hex/ID values) — no evidence of broken personalization.

- **List-Unsubscribe header**: Not found by QA scan. Flag as WARN only — likely an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than confirmed absence, since header-level unsubscribe is commonly stripped by intermediate relays.

CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email — this is a real compliance gap if confirmed absent in the full source (footer was not included in the truncated excerpt, so this should be verified against the full HTML before treating as conclusive).

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` attributes to all content images at minimum (e.g. `57746756_2_2x`); decorative/tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` for consistency (already done in one instance).
