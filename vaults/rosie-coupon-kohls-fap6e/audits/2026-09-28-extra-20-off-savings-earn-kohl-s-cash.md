# Extra 20% off savings + earn Kohl's Cash ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink Kohl's send. The subject promises "Extra 20% off savings + earn Kohl's Cash," but the top of the render is a nav-button grid and a "Clearance up to 70%" banner. The 20% code (YOUSAVE20) doesn't appear until the coupon stack at the very bottom. A subscriber has to scroll about 5,000px to find the offer the subject line sold.
- Compared with the 09-28 "Starts today: Take 20% off new faves" send, which broke the purple "Epic Deals" rut, this is a retreat to a generic multi-category blitz. It's a different palette (magenta and cream instead of purple), but the same shape: several competing heroes and a barcode-coupon footer. Clearance, "Elevated Everyday" workwear, a Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale and a "Picked with you in mind" product grid all fight for the eye, and none of them leads with the 20%. It's the second or third 20%-themed send in one day. Cadence fatigue is the real risk.
- Judgement: competent execution with clean images and no render bugs, but it has no single hero offer. It reads as a template sending shoppers to the Wallet page.

## What's working

- Every product tile and category button renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid shows concrete prices ($349.99 Ninja, $29.99 women's jeans, $2.99–$9.99 towels) with original prices, which gives shoppers something specific.
- The Kohl's Rewards member-exclusive $10-off-$50 callouts for baby and toys give members a clear benefit.
- The "Take an extra 20% off" coupon block has a visible code and barcode. That is practical and redeemable in store.
- The "Clearance up to 70%" banner is the strongest visual anchor in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject's 20% offer is buried in the bottom third, so the subject and the hero don't match.
- Four or more competing sections (Clearance, Workwear, Baby Stock-Up, Picked for you) leave the eye with no single landing spot.
- The top nav-button grid (Women / Men / Kids / Home) pushes real content down and is a generic, non-personalized first impression.
- The fine-print block and the barcode-coupon footer are long and dense. They add friction and make the offer feel conditional ("Coupons do not apply to select brands").
- The Kohl's Cash message competes with a second "Earn" line at the top, so the reward mechanic is unclear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% off code (YOUSAVE20) in a hero banner at the very top with one button, "Shop 20% off," and drop the nav grid.
- 2. Cut the body to two modules: the extra-20% category picks and one member-exclusive block. Move Clearance and Baby Stock-Up into separate sends.
- 3. Add a "Ends Oct. 4" deadline badge next to the hero offer instead of only in the fine print.
- 4. Collapse the fine print into a short summary and a "details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends Oct. 4 — code inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% + $10 Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use YOUSAVE20 on clearance, workwear and baby picks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend through Oct. 4.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has real structural defects: a stray `</custom>` closing tag, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport that blocks zoom. At least 7 content images and 3 tracking pixels have no `alt`. Compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed, because the header warnings look like relay artifacts and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 71 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`). Destinations, redirect health, and link rot are unverified.
- **Four separate tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/8c571e68-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…&d=60266&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  These are redundant opens from at least 3 vendors (Movable Ink, ExactTarget/SFMC, Adobe Audience Manager). Each adds a failure point and a third-party request.
- **Visible pixel:** The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) sits directly under `<body>` with no hiding wrapper (`width="1" height="1"`, no `style`, no `alt`). It may render a stray 1px element or line gap in some clients.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>`, which is invalid markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** QA flagged all of these. The truncated list ends mid-entry, so there may be more.
  - Content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`. Without `alt`, image-blocked or screen-reader users get nothing, or the file name.
  - Pixels: the three tracking pixels above (`8c571e68-…`, `o.gif`, `event`).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` is an empty reference.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and move the following markup into the body. Schema.org microdata is also generally unsupported in email clients.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The tables `<table width="1" height="1" …>`, `<table width="100%" align="center" border="0" …>` and the preheader `<table width='640' …>` don't have it, while the main layout tables do. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(…googleapis…)` and `<link>` to Google Fonts are stripped by Gmail and others. The `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is present, so the impact is low.
- **Fixed width:** The preheader table has a hard `width: 640px` in its inline style. It relies on `.container { width:100% !important }` at ≤500px, which fails in clients that strip `<style>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source, and the subject line has none. Subscriber-level identifiers such as `mi_u=604230016` in image URLs appear to be resolved. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Not confirmed as defects:** The `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` warnings all carry the QA note "may not be captured by AgentMail relay." SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed either.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected, but the footer falls after the truncation point. Re-check against the full HTML or raw MIME before treating it as a violation.
- **Unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Landing pages unverifiable:** All `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` links use opaque encoded redirects, and QA skipped probing them. UTM parameters and landing alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** Image URLs carry `email_name=260928_DG_Email_Mon_PM` and `mi_ecmp=1022622_2026928`, which suggests campaign-level tagging via Movable Ink.
- **Referrer meta:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present, but email clients largely ignore it, so it doesn't reliably affect site attribution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7+ content images. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the three tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`. Move or remove the `itemscope` div from `<head>`. Fill in or remove the empty `<title>` and the `<link href="">` favicon.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Add `role="presentation"` to the three unmarked layout tables.
5. Consolidate the open-tracking pixels. Wrap the first one (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) in a hidden container like the others.
6. Re-run QA on the raw MIME, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and the footer's physical address and unsubscribe link.
7. Resolve a sample of the 71 `click.s.kohls.com` links (follow redirects manually) to confirm status codes and UTM presence on the final URLs.
