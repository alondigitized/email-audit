# 📣 Epic Deals + 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members 📣
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "EPIC DEALS" category-blitz Kohl's has sent since 09-20, and the fatigue is now visually obvious — same purple hero banner, same hot-pink "EPIC DEAL" tile stamps, same barcode/coupon footer, just another reshuffled grid (fleece, decor, LEGO, towels, shoes, bedding, jeans, floor care). The one genuinely new element is the "3x rewards" hook in the hero and subject, which at least gives loyalty members a reason this send differs from 09-22's near-identical "3x rewards" email — but it's the same offer repeated two days later.
- The layout itself is clean and scannable, with a clear nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) and consistent deal-tile formatting, so mechanically it works. But there's no single hero product or offer — it's twelve-plus tiles of undifferentiated inventory, which forces the reader to do all the work of finding something relevant. Bolted onto the bottom is an unrelated Hispanic Heritage Month module and a "Picked with you in mind" personalization block that reads as generic cross-sell, not true personalization.
- Judgement: mechanically sound template, but zero creative differentiation from the four other Epic Deals sends this week, and the subject/preheader don't do enough to signal why *this* one is worth opening.

## What's working

- Clear nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) lets scanners jump straight to their category.
- Loyalty hook ("3x rewards," "$10 off $50" coupon) is visible near the top for Rewards members.
- Deal tiles are uniform and legible — price, regular price, and "Shop Now" are consistent across all twelve+ tiles.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Barcode/coupon block at the bottom is functional and easy to screenshot for in-store use.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer or hero product — it's an undifferentiated grid, so nothing draws the eye first.
- Subject and hero copy are near-identical to the 09-22 "3x rewards" send just two days prior — same claim, same framing.
- "Picked with you in mind" section (Ninja blender, towels, jeans, mattress topper) reads as generic cross-category filler, not real personalization.
- Hispanic Heritage Month module is dropped in with no visual or thematic connection to the deals content around it, feeling like an afterthought.
- Coupon exclusions ("Coupons do not apply") appear on two tiles (shoes, floor care) right next to the discount, undercutting the offer's credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero deal (highest discount or most inventory-relevant) and make it the actual top-of-email hero instead of a generic banner — give the reader one clear reason to open beyond "3x rewards" repeated from Tuesday.
- 2. Cut the tile count from twelve+ to 6-8 and group by the nav categories shown (Women/Men/Kids/Home) so the grid feels like curated categories, not a warehouse dump.
- 3. Move or drop the Hispanic Heritage Month module — if it stays, connect it visually/thematically to the deals rather than sandwiching it between LEGO and jeans.
- 4. Differentiate this send from 09-22's now that both lead with "3x rewards" — add a distinct angle (e.g., "final day," category-specific) so the brand history doesn't read as a copy-paste.
- 5. Remove or relocate "Coupons do not apply" disclaimers away from the discount callout, or at minimum make them less prominent.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your $10 off $50 coupon before Sept. 27`
