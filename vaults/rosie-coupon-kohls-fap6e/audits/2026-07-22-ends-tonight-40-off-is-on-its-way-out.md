# ENDS TONIGHT: 40% off is on its way out ...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is, at minimum, the eleventh Kohl's promo in four days — and the second "ends tonight" signal within a single calendar day, sent hours after the July 22 "EXTENDED: Enjoy 40% off for an extra day" email explicitly bought more time. A subscriber who opened both messages this morning and this afternoon faces an immediate contradiction that no creative execution can paper over. "Extended" then "ends tonight" on the same day is the textbook definition of bait-and-switch cadence, and it torches whatever urgency credibility remained after nine earlier sends.
- The email body itself is competent but structurally bloated: a 40% hero, a 50% activewear block, a family outfit grid, an 85% clearance module, and a coupon/barcode section all stack vertically without a dominant through-line. The 85% clearance — the one offer that still carries surprise value — is recycled from the July 20 email that scored 9/10 and is again buried well below the fold, where most mobile scanners will never reach it.
- Judgment: in isolation, as a final-day sale closer after a week of silence, this email would work fine. In context — the eleventh consecutive promo, with a same-day extension notice already in the inbox — it reads as a brand that has spent its urgency budget down to zero.

## What's working

- **40% hero is immediately legible**: large type, clear "Shop Now" button, no ambiguity about what the offer is.
- **50% off activewear and shoes** is a sharper, category-specific hook than the blanket sitewide offer — relevant to fitness-adjacent subscribers.
- **Kohl's Cash surfaced early**: loyalty earning appears close to the top, which rewards members notice.
- **85% clearance module** still carries genuine deal-hunter appeal, even if placement wastes it.
- **Clean render**: no broken images, overlapping text, or layout failures visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Same-day credibility collapse**: an "EXTENDED" email went out this morning; "ENDS TONIGHT" this afternoon is a direct contradiction that any subscriber who opened both will catch instantly.
- **Cluttered visual hierarchy**: five distinct content blocks compete — the eye has no clear place to land after the hero.
- **Subject is a retreaded burn**: "ENDS TONIGHT" was the exact subject line on July 19's "40% off ENDS TONIGHT 🚨" — the phrase has no remaining urgency value for engaged subscribers.
- **No personalization anywhere**: subject, hero, and CTAs are all generic sitewide — no category, no product, no name.
- **Clearance buried**: the 85% off hook — the most compelling offer in the email — is well below the fold where mobile scanners won't reach it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the hierarchy: lead with 85% clearance, relegate 40% to secondary.** The clearance number is the only offer with remaining surprise value. Burying it mid-scroll is the single most wasteful choice in this send.
- 2. **Sync send logic to prevent same-day contradiction.** Never fire an "ends tonight" message on the same calendar day an "extended" notice went out. The credibility damage outweighs any incremental urgency lift.
- 3. **Use the activewear/shoes offer in the subject line** — it's the most persona-specific hook and it's currently invisible from the inbox.
- 4. **Cut the coupon/barcode block or push to footer.** It breaks visual flow and the in-store use case dilutes digital CTA intent.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 50% off activewear + 85% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab clearance deals before the 40% off window closes`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, activewear, and clearance finds — all on sale today`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "ENDS TONIGHT: 40% off" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML4 email with standard multi-client compatibility patterns, but carries multiple compliance gaps and pervasive missing alt text across tracking and content images. Auth header visibility is obscured by the AgentMail relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** — all external links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but redirect chain depth is unverified.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9c5b74ba...` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?E5AL66...` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

Redundant open-tracking pixels across three vendors is unusual and increases page weight; confirm all three are intentional and contractually required.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The stray `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers (though most email clients will recover silently).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 11 images flagged**, including content images (product shots from `images.am.kohls.com`) and all tracking pixels. Tracking pixels should carry `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` (the MessageGears pixel does this correctly; the others do not). Content images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked rendering.

Confirmed missing alt on:
- `9c5b74ba-7a6d-47f4-b168-3f33051f56e0` (CoherentPath pixel)
- `event?d_sid=13245196` (Demdex sync pixel)
- `55869996_root2_2x` (product image via `images.am.kohls.com`)
- 8 additional `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` content render pixels

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>` — some clients (notably Gmail web) display this in the tab or preview area.

**`user-scalable=0` set** in viewport meta — blocks zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**Google Fonts loaded three times** for the same families (one `<link>`, one `@import` each for Roboto and Montserrat) — redundant fetches on clients that render external CSS, minor load overhead.

**MSO conditional targets `gte mso 9`** — covers Outlook 2000+, which is broader than necessary; `mso 16` would suffice for any live Outlook version, though this causes no functional harm.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge token literals (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%FIRSTNAME%`) detected in the truncated source. MessageGears user ID is populated: `mi_u=604230016`. CoherentPath and MessageGears `ecmp` campaign IDs are present and resolved.

One content render pixel carries a partial token:
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```

The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter value is **empty** — this may indicate a missing countdown/expiry date merge field that failed to populate. If this drives dynamic content (e.g., a timer or offer end date), the blank value warrants investigation in MessageGears template logic.

However, no UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible on click-through URLs in the provided source — all 22 links route through `click.s.kohls.com` redirect, so UTM presence on destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving one redirect chain.

The `email_name=260722_DG_Email_Wed_PM_` value in several pixel URLs provides a human-readable campaign slug, but this exists only in pixel calls, not in destination URL query strings where analytics would capture it.

**Risk:** If the SFMC redirect does not append UTMs to the landing page URL, GA4/Adobe Analytics will attribute the session as direct/none rather than email.
