# 20% off is waiting 👀 Score more savings when you open a Kohl's Card ...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 30%/20%/15% tier stack Kohl's has run daily since 09-10, now bolted onto a "Home essentials" theme with a Martha Stewart collection push. The layout is the longest and most cluttered yet — status bar, tier stack, Kohl's Card pitch, Kohl's Cash banner, four category buttons, a home hero, a Martha Stewart cross-promo, four more category buttons, a six-icon "just one thing" grid, four product tiles, a coupon reminder, and a barcode — all before the footer. Nothing here signals urgency or scarcity beyond the same static "Ends Sept. 20" that's been sitting unchanged for over a week.
- The one departure from the norm is genuine: leading with home/kitchen and the Martha Stewart collection is a real content shift versus the apparel-heavy sends of the past week. But it's buried under seven scrolls of repeated maroon CTAs and the same tier-stack banner subscribers have now seen roughly a dozen times. Fatigue risk is real, and the email is simply too long for a promotional send.

## What's working

- The home/kitchen + Martha Stewart section is a genuinely different creative angle from the last several sends (bedding/dining/appliances vs. apparel).
- Product tiles at the bottom show real prices with strikethroughs (Ninja Slushi $349.99, Serta topper $91.99-$139.99), giving concrete purchase signals.
- The tier-stack banner remains legible and color-coded, so returning subscribers can parse their discount tier instantly.
- Barcode + promo code (REWARDS20) at the bottom gives in-store shoppers a usable fallback.

## What's weak

- Extreme length — at least 10 distinct content blocks and 15+ CTA buttons before the footer, which will make most recipients bail before reaching the Martha Stewart or product-tile sections.
- Same static "Ends Sept. 20" deadline that has appeared unchanged across nearly every send since 09-10 — no real urgency left in it.
- Subject line ("20% off is waiting... Score more savings when you open a Kohl's Card") buries the Martha Stewart / home content entirely — the actual creative differentiator isn't referenced at all.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled: the eye has no single landing point, with three competing heroes (tier stack, home essentials, Martha Stewart) stacked back to back.
- Cadence fatigue is severe — this is essentially the eleventh+ near-identical tier-stack send in two and a half weeks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half — pick one hero (either home essentials or the Martha Stewart launch, not both) and drop the "just one thing" six-icon grid, which duplicates the category buttons already shown twice above it.
- 2. Lead the subject with the actual new content (Martha Stewart collection) instead of the generic Kohl's Card pitch that's run in prior subjects too.
- 3. Replace the static "Ends Sept. 20" language with a countdown framing ("3 days left") to inject real urgency instead of a stale deadline.
- 4. Move the Martha Stewart collection to the top given it's the only true novelty in this send — burying it after two tier-stack/category blocks wastes the differentiation.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kitchen just got a Martha Stewart upgrade (20% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the new Martha Stewart collection, plus 20-30% off home essentials`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Appliances, bedding, decor — all discounted through Sunday`

- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for transactional/marketing email, no issue.
