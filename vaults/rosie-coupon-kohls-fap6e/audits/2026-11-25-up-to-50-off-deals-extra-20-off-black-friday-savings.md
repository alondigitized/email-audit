# Up to 50% off deals + Extra 20% off = Black Friday savings 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's Black Friday deal wall that has landed all week, about 12,000px of stacked category blocks. The render is clean. The strongest read is the top of the email: a snowy hero with a "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge, a "Stores open Friday at 5 AM" banner, a category chip grid (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Shoes & Boots, Toys, Beauty), and a free-shipping bar. The subject's "Up to 50% off + Extra 20% off" is visible in the many "50% + 20% w/coupon" blocks.
- The scroll is the problem. Dozens of near-equal tiles (coats, boots, Carter's, Skechers, appliances, Christmas trees, Ninja, pajamas, bedding, fleece, Sephora) compete with no single hero product. A Door Busters teaser and a "Win up to $500 Kohl's Cash" sweepstakes block close the email, which dilutes the focus further. I could only read the downscaled screenshot, so small fine print, such as the exclusions behind the "w/coupon" badges, wasn't legible.
- Judgment: a competent, on-brand promo, but it's the fifth or sixth near-identical send this week. It's a serviceable 6, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero and top banner give the urgency hooks up front: the "Stores open Friday at 5 AM" banner and the $15 Kohl's Cash badge.
- The category chip grid under the hero lets a shopper jump straight to their section.
- The free-shipping bar ("Free shipping with a $25 purchase") adds a low-friction benefit.
- The product tiles use big price numerals (60%, 15.99, 65%, 40%, 29.99, 7.99, 11.99), so the deals scan quickly.
- Kohl's Rewards member callouts (the "extra 10% off" strips in the appliance and kitchen sections) give members a reason to feel recognized.
- I saw no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breakage anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- The roughly 12,000px length with 20+ tiles buries the offer, and there's no clear single hero product.
- The offers are layered (percent off, plus 20% with coupon, plus Kohl's Cash, plus member extras). Several tiles carry fine print like "Coupons do not apply," which reads as exclusionary.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" that links to a category rather than a specific product.
- The sweepstakes block and Door Busters teaser at the bottom distract from the shopping path.
- It repeats the creative and offer structure of the Nov 24 and Nov 25 sends ("Save 20% + get an extra 10% off home with Kohl's Rewards"), so it's pure cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer and six to eight tiles. Put the "extra 20% w/ coupon" code and its expiry in the first screen.
- 2. Segment by category affinity (home, apparel, kids) and lead each version with that category's best offer, instead of sending one wall to everyone.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific verb + noun CTAs ("Shop coats from $60 off", "Get the Ninja for $29.99") linked to those products.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes and Door Busters teaser to a separate send, and cap Black Friday week at one or two sends per day.
- 5. Show the exclusions near the offer in plain language, so "w/coupon" doesn't feel like a bait-and-switch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends tonight: code inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Doors open 5 AM Friday: your 50% off list`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend. Coupon inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Coats, boots, and Ninja at 29.99. See what's marked down most.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based HTML with heavy tracking, five separate tracking or analytics beacons and 94 redirect-wrapped links. The source has several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, and a broken CSS selector. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay capture and the truncated HTML, so they are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 94 links go through click-redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). The QA run skipped the HTTP probe for all of them, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Many open and tracking beacons:** the source loads five pixels before the main content.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/61efee3d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026695_20261125`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
  - Body images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…`
  
  This is redundant and adds third-party requests. `demdex.net` is a frequent target of tracker and privacy blocklists.
- **Per-recipient IDs in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_mid=…` are repeated in every `mi.kohls.com` image URL. These can be exposed if the email is forwarded or archived.
- **Malformed markup:** the open-tracker `<img … alt="">` is closed with `</custom>`, an invalid stray tag that follows it.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 16,698 of 22,504 plain-text characters (74%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long `qs=` tokens inflate the text part and can raise spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on images:** 61 images have no `alt`. QA samples include `59980052_2s_2x`, `60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x` (product images), plus the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images. If these carry offers or product names, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. Two of the images QA flagged (`o.gif`, `demdex`) are 1×1 or 0×0 pixels. `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so that flag looks like a QA false positive. The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) and the demdex pixel have no `alt` at all and should get `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` leaves the HTML view and some clients without a title.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **`<div>` in `<head>`:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits before `</head>`. Parsers will close the head and start the body early. Many clients strip `itemscope` microdata anyway.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the page URL in some clients.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
- **Webfont dependencies:** `@import` and `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The `@import url(...)` syntax is inconsistent between its two lines. Most clients ignore `@import` and `<link>` in email, so the `Montserrat,Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif` fallback stack is what will actually render.
- **Duplicate meta:** `<meta name="format-detection">` appears twice, with different content.
- **Doctype:** a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` attributes, which is a mismatch but works in practice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking IDs (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`) are populated. The HTML was truncated, so the body copy and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both WARN as not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) is not supported. Check the raw message headers on the original delivery.
- **Physical address:** QA flagged it as not detected. The supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Check the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com` and the click domain is `click.s.kohls.com`, which are consistent. Alignment itself is unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible:** attribution runs through the opaque `qs=` token (`click.s.kohls.com`) and `mi_ecmp=1026695_20261125`, so landing URLs and any UTM parameters can't be verified. The click probe was skipped for all 94 links.
- **Campaign name exposed:** `email_name=261125_DG_Email_Wed_PM` in the image URLs is an internal campaign identifier. It is not a bug, but it is visible to recipients and inspectors.
- **Landing page alignment:** unverified, because the redirects were not followed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run the redirect probe (or a full click-through crawl) on a sample of the 94 tracking links. Confirm final URLs, status codes, and that the `Up to 50% off` and `Extra 20% off` offers match the landing pages.
2. Check the original headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and for the footer address. Don't treat the warnings as confirmed failures until you have seen the raw message.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels that lack it.
4. Fill in `<title>`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0`.
5. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` or remove it. Remove the stray `</custom>` and the empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
6. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector (`.ReadMsgBody`) and drop the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
7. Consolidate the five tracking pixels where possible, and shorten the tracking URLs so the plain-text part falls under the URL-ratio threshold.
