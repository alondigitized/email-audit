# Last day ⏰ Save 20% + get $10 off your $50+ purchase of cozy styles & more 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seventh straight send on the 20% off plus $10 off $50+ stack, and the "Last day" urgency is the only new element. It gets that urgency right. An "ENDS TODAY" banner sits at the top, and the barcode coupons at the bottom say "Ends Nov. 1." The subject, the hero and the coupons all agree on the deadline, which is more honest than most of this week's sends.
- The body is far too long, though. A cozy-styles hero with a Shop Now button and two priced product blocks (sweaters from $19.99, boots from $18.39) is a good start. Then it switches to a "North Kohl's" holiday-village block, a Top 100 Gifts tile, a "Picked with you in mind" grid, delivery services, and four coupon barcodes. The urgent offer ends up competing with everything else Kohl's wanted to promote. It's a solid, credible last-day push, buried under a template that has been recycled all week.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" is visible at the top, and the coupon codes repeat "Ends Nov. 1," so the urgency is credible.
- The hero, "Holiday comfort at comfortable prices," matches the "cozy styles" in the subject, and it has a clear Shop Now button.
- The product blocks show concrete prices ("Starting at 19.99" for sweaters, "18.39" for boots) with the 20% coupon math spelled out.
- The loyalty header ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn: 5% REWARDS") and the Kohl's Cash badge give members a reason to act.
- The bottom coupon codes (SAVE20, GOSHOP10) are scannable and easy to copy.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. The North Kohl's village, Top 100 Gifts and delivery sections dilute the "last day" message.
- The "North Kohl's" block has been a fixture all week. Oct 28, Oct 31 and now Nov 1 all carry it, so it reads as filler.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid shows a drink maker, a bath towel, a mattress topper and women's jeans. It has no tie to cozy styles or to the persona's focus.
- Fine print is heavy, and the 20% off and $10 off conditions only appear at the bottom as "details" links. The top hero doesn't state the $10 off $50+ offer visibly.
- Several competing CTAs (Style Square, Gift List Lane, Hosting Headquarters, Sephora + Kohl's, Start Exploring) split attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the two product blocks and the coupons. Move North Kohl's and Top 100 Gifts into the next send.
- 2. Put the offer in the hero image: "20% off + $10 off $50+. Ends tonight." The offer should sit next to the Shop Now button.
- 3. Put one coupon block with a clear "Add to wallet" or "Copy code" button directly under the hero, not at the bottom.
- 4. Replace the generic "Picked with you in mind" grid with sweaters and boots in the persona's category, or drop it.
- 5. Add a countdown timer or a "Ends 11:59 PM CT" line for stronger urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 20% off + $10 off $50 ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call on cozy sweaters from $19.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 20% coupon and $10 off $50+ expire at midnight. Sweaters from $19.99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boots from $18.39 after coupon. Add your codes to your wallet before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard 640px table layout with a heavy third-party tracking stack (SFMC, Kohl's `mi.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com`, Adobe `demdex`). The visible source has several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, product links and UTM parameters could not be checked, and the header and compliance warnings are mostly relay artifacts.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation not possible:** 70 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains). Destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Four tracking systems fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/8e24096f-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024962_2026111`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=60270`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- The overlap adds requests and failure points.
- **Third-party beacon:** `kohls.demdex.net` is an Adobe Audience Manager call from inside the email. It is blocked by some privacy proxies and is a data-sharing consideration.
- **Malformed markup on the SFMC open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ... alt=""></custom>` has a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opener. Some clients may mishandle it.
- **Subscriber ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image URLs, including content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=...`). Every image request carries a user-level identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="...maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request to the document URL in some clients.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">...</div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will move it, and email clients ignore microdata anyway.
- **Missing alt on content images:** QA flags `<img>` tags with no alt attribute, including these product/content images:
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` pixel and the `demdex` `event` pixel have no alt attribute in the source. Add `alt=""`.
- **QA false positive:** the `o.gif` pixel is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image. Only the content images above are real alt issues.
- **Table roles:** the 1×1 tracking tables and the `width='640'` preheader table have no `role="presentation"` (the main layout tables do).
- **Font loading:** web fonts load via both `<link>` and `@import` (Roboto, Montserrat). The CSS names a fallback stack (`Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`), so this is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. Check against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. Sending is from `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a dedicated subdomain.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Re-run on the full source.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source. Verify it in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** no `utm_*` parameters are visible. Links go through the SFMC redirect (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`), whose `qs` payload is opaque. Campaign attribution depends on the SFMC and `mi_*` parameters.
- **Campaign identifier:** `email_name=261101_DG_Email_Sunday_...` appears on image URLs, but the value is cut off.
- **Landing pages:** unverified, since the link probe skipped all tracking links. Whether the offer in the subject (20% off plus $10 off $50+) matches the landing pages cannot be assessed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Add a descriptive `<title>`.
3. Remove or populate the empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
4. Delete the stray `</custom>` tag after the `open.aspx` pixel.
5. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or remove it.
6. Add `alt` attributes to the seven flagged content images and `alt=""` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
7. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels where possible, and review whether the `demdex` beacon is needed.
8. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer address, the unsubscribe link and the UTM parameters.
9. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message rather than the relay copy. Until then, treat the header warnings as inconclusive.
10. Follow the redirect chains offline for a sample of the 70 tracking links to confirm they land on live pages.
