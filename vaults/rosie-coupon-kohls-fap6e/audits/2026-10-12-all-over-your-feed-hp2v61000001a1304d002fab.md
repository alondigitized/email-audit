# All over your feed 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email with a vague subject. "All over your feed 🤩" promises social-media buzz, and the body does open on a "TRENDING ON SOCIAL" banner. But the first thing in the render is a different promo: "FREE Emi Jay Trial Size" with a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button. Two heroes compete, and the subject lines up with the second one rather than the offer.
- Below that the email runs a long stack of product grids: "We Think You'll Love These," "Trending Picks for You," and "Spot it. Shop it." Each has a sub-hero, including auto-replenish at 15% off, a Venmo promo, and "Florals for Fall." The Insider tier strip (Insider / VIB / Rouge) and a "Savings Event coming soon" banner sit near the top and bottom. It looks like Sephora, it renders cleanly, and it shows plenty of prices. It has no single idea, though. It's the same everything-bagel as the 10/11 "Beauty for literally everybody" and 10/9 "Hey, Insider" sends.
- My judgement is competent but unfocused. The free trial-size gift is the strongest hook, and the subject ignores it.

## What's working

- The free Emi Jay trial size is a concrete, tangible offer at the very top, with a visible "SHOP TO REDEEM" button.
- The Insider / VIB / Rouge tier strip and the "Savings Event" badges signal member benefits.
- The product grids show real prices and "NEW / LIMITED EDITION" tags, so shoppers get product detail without clicking.
- The "Florals for Fall" and Savings Event teasers fit the current season.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject "All over your feed 🤩" is vague. It never mentions the free gift, the Savings Event, or a product.
- The top has three stacked messages: the savings badges, the trial-size gift, and the "Trending on Social" banner. The eye has no single landing spot.
- The body is very long, with at least six sections after the hero. The Venmo and auto-replenish blocks dilute the beauty story.
- The free gift is gated by a "$30+ purchase," shown in small text and an asterisk below the hero. Fine print is heavy at the top and bottom.
- The "Savings Event coming soon" badges tease a sale without a date. That is a missed urgency hook.
- The cadence is heavy. This is another near-daily send after 10/11, 10/9, 10/8 and 10/7. The 10/7 "ENDS TODAY" 2X points email was the clearest of the run.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero and align the subject to it. Lead with the free Emi Jay trial size and put "Trending on Social" below it as a product story.
- 2. Cut the body to hero, one product grid and one loyalty line. Move the Venmo and auto-replenish blocks to a different send.
- 3. Put the Savings Event dates in the top banner so "coming soon" becomes a real deadline.
- 4. State the $30 minimum next to the button, not only in the footnote.
- 5. Give the buttons specific copy, such as "Claim your free Emi Jay mini" and "Shop the Dedcool clips."
