# FINAL HOURS for our LOWEST prices + up to 85% off clearance 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's ninth "lowest prices of the season" send since Oct 19, and the only new element is the urgency. The subject says "FINAL HOURS" and the hero says "ENDS TODAY." Yesterday's "LAST DAY to shop the deals before they're gone ⏳" already played that card, so "final hours" a day later reads as a repeated deadline. A subscriber who has seen this creative eight times will recognize it and skip it.
- The render itself is clean and scannable. A maroon hero holds "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON," a "Featuring Clearance up to 85% after an extra 50% off" callout, and a green "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" bar. Below that is a long stack of category tiles, each with a "Shop Now" link and a price anchor such as "Starting at 7.99" or "Up to 60% off." The sheer length of the stack (about ten tiles plus a gift section and a product grid) dilutes the "final hours" message.
- Overall it is a competent promo that works as a price catalog, but not a fresh or personalized one. The subject is better than its predecessors only because it names the deadline and the 85% clearance hook.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer clearly: "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON," "ENDS TODAY," and the 85% clearance callout are all visible at the top.
- The "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" bar and the "Earn 5% Rewards" member line add loyalty value without cluttering the hero.
- Each tile pairs one category with one price anchor (for example "Starting at 3.99" for towels and "Up to 60%" for luggage), so it is easy to scan.
- Quick-nav buttons (Home, Women, Men, Kids, $10 & Under, $20 & Under) sit right under the hero and let a shopper jump straight to a category.
- The "Free returns through January 31, 2027" strip reduces purchase risk.

## What's weak

- The deadline is credible only for the first send. "ENDS TODAY" follows yesterday's "LAST DAY" and eight earlier sends with the same creative.
- The email is far too long, with ten or more near-identical "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON" tiles. The repeated label on every tile adds noise.
- The footer fine print is dense, and the "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Select styles" caveats appear repeatedly. This undercuts how honest the offer feels.
- The hero has no single dominant CTA. A "Shop Now" button, a "Shop Clearance" button, and four category buttons all compete.
- The gift-guide and "Picked with you in mind" blocks are generic and don't read as personalized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the 3–4 strongest tiles (clearance, home, boots, towels) and put a countdown or an "11:59 PM tonight" deadline in the hero. That makes "final hours" feel real instead of repeated.
- 2. Merge the two hero CTAs into one primary button, "Shop Clearance," and demote the rest.
- 3. Remove the repeated "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON" label from each tile and use the space for the price.
- 4. Move the Kohl's Cash redemption deadline (Oct 26) up into the hero. It is the strongest time-bound reason to act.
- 5. Hold the cadence. After eight near-identical sends, suppress or vary the creative for engaged subscribers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: boots from $17.99, towels $3.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kohl's Cash must be redeemed by Oct 26. Free returns through Jan 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance goes to 85% off after an extra 50%. Tonight only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup defects (an invalid `<div>` in `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, and missing alt text on content images). Most compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** They are on `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` and similar redirect domains, so the destination URLs and their status are unverified.
- **Four separate open/tracking beacons fire per open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2f71927b-…` (no `alt`, no `style`, visible 1×1 `<img>` directly in `<body>`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="…open.aspx…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` closes an element that was never opened.
- **Empty param in an image URL.** `…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…` has a blank value, which suggests an unpopulated dynamic field. The source is truncated, so I can't see how many other URLs are affected.
- **Plain-text part is 13,788 of 19,504 characters URLs (about 71%).** That is above the 70% threshold the QA check uses and is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text.** The QA check flags many images without alt text. Several are real content images:
  - `58967014_1Ls_2x`, `58967014_13Ls_2x`, `58967014_11Ls_2x`, `58967014_12Ls_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`)
  - the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`)
  - Whether these `.png` files are text banners or decorative can't be told from the source. If they are banners, the offer text is lost with images off.
- **Likely false positive.** `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel. The `demdex` and `click.chp` pixels do lack `alt` and should get `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zooming.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid head markup.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will end `<head>` early there, and the `<meta itemprop>` tags are not valid in an email anyway.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load via `fonts.googleapis.com` `<link>` and `@import`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, but the fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present, so there is no break.
- **Table roles.** The tracking tables and the preheader table lack `role="presentation"`, while the main layout tables have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens appear in the visible source.
- The empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value (see section 2) is the only sign of an unfilled dynamic field.
- A per-recipient ID (`mi_u=604230016`) is embedded in image URLs. It is a normal tracking pattern, but it makes those URLs identifying.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed warnings.** "List-Unsubscribe header not found", "List-Unsubscribe-Post not found" and "Authentication-Results not found" are attributed by the QA data itself to the AgentMail relay not exposing headers. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, not failed.
- **Physical address.** "No physical address detected" can't be verified. The HTML source is truncated before the footer, which is where the address and unsubscribe link normally sit. This needs a check against the full message.
- **From header.** `kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with the tracking domains. Alignment can't be verified without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible links use ESP redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`). Kohl's own `mi_*` and `email_name=261025_DG_Email_Sun_PM` parameters appear on images only. No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated source.
- Final landing URLs and any UTM or campaign parameters are not verifiable because the redirects were not resolved.
- Two images (`69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`) lack the `email_name` param that sibling images carry (`mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` only), so campaign attribution is inconsistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA against the full HTML and raw headers. This is needed to resolve the address, unsubscribe and authentication warnings.
2. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check the final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and banner images. Set `alt=""` on the remaining tracking pixels.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Move the `<div itemscope>` out of `<head>` or drop it, delete the stray `</custom>`, and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
6. Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` is blank and make sure the `email_name` param is consistent across images.
7. Consider consolidating the four open pixels, and trim the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
