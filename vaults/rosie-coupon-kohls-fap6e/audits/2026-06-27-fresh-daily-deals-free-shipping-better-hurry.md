# Fresh Daily Deals + FREE SHIPPING ... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eleventh Deal Days email in five days — and the creative is, for the eleventh time, structurally identical to every previous send. The "KOHL'S DEAL DAYS" hero banner, the Instant Kohl's Cash sub-header, the category nav strip, the "TODAY'S FEATURED DAILY DEALS" section with stacked 50%/40%-off tiles, and the "9 DAYS OF DEALS" countdown all appear again unchanged. The subject line ("Fresh Daily Deals + FREE SHIPPING ... better hurry") tries to inject novelty with "fresh" and urgency with "better hurry," but after ten near-verbatim predecessors that framing rings completely hollow.
- The email itself is competent retail execution: clean layout, recognizable brand, no render bugs, clear CTAs. The Instant Kohl's Cash banner ($20 on $50+) at the top is the one genuine hook. But the campaign was explicitly positioned as closing on June 26 ("Instant Kohl's Cash ends tonight") — so seeing the same offer alive on June 27 with "better hurry" urgency is a credibility-killer for anyone who got yesterday's send.
- **Judgement:** Solid template, dead campaign. The email scores reasonably on mechanics but the serial sends have exhausted subscriber patience and the urgency is now fictional.

## What's working

- **Instant Kohl's Cash** ($20 on $50+) is prominently called out in a yellow banner near the top — this is the one concrete offer with real dollar value.
- **Visual hierarchy is clear** — hero → Kohl's Cash → category nav → featured deals flows top-to-bottom without confusion.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text is crisp, layout is intact throughout.
- **Category-specific deal tiles** (Bedding, Shoes, Apparel, Home goods) give browsers multiple on-ramps.

## What's weak

- **False urgency — again.** June 26's email said "Instant Kohl's Cash ends tonight." It did not end. "Better hurry" today means nothing.
- **Eleventh send, same template.** "9 DAYS OF DEALS" counter and category-deal structure are pixel-for-pixel repeats; there is zero creative freshness despite the "Fresh" subject line claim.
- **No seasonal or new-product hook.** Every deal tile is the same category rotation (bedding, shoes, apparel) with no new featured item to pull a click.
- **"Better hurry" copy is unattributed urgency** — no deadline date, no inventory signal, nothing specific to anchor the rush.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop sending until the offer actually changes.** One more identical email damages list trust more than a quiet day would. If the campaign must continue, rotate creative and introduce a genuinely new featured product.
- 2. **Put a hard deadline date on the urgency** — "Shop by midnight tonight" or "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM" instead of the vague "better hurry."
- 3. **Surface one new product or exclusive** in the hero tile to justify the word "Fresh."
- 4. **Rotate the deal categories** — Bedding + Shoes + Apparel has appeared in eight consecutive sends; flip in a new vertical (outdoor, kitchen, beauty) to give repeat openers something to look at.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains. This is expected for click tracking but means link health (404s, redirect chains) cannot be verified automatically.

Three distinct tracking pixel vendors are active simultaneously:

- `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Selligent)
- `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager)

No issues with the links themselves, but the overlap creates redundant open-tracking calls and increases page weight.

Malformed HTML tag detected in open-pixel block:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?TIMBRMZ5Q5FU5M4FUYDI73HFPA.60264..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and has no matching opening tag. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it is a markup error.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA scan. All are functional/tracking images or dynamic content images served from `mi.kohls.com`:

| Image | URL fragment |
|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5de14407...` |
| Open pixel | `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` |
| AAM pixel | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` |
| Content images (×7) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` |

Tracking pixels (1×1) should have `alt=""` explicitly set — several are missing it entirely vs. relying on the attribute being absent. The `aria-hidden="true"` pattern is correctly applied to the `o.gif` pixel but not consistently to the others.

`<title>` tag is empty (`<title></title>`). Screen readers and some clients use the title element; it should match the subject line or be a descriptive fallback.

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-to-zoom — an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile.

`max-height: auto !important` in the mobile stylesheet (`img { max-height: auto !important; }`) is not a valid CSS value. `auto` is the default; this is a no-op but indicates a copy-paste error.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — WARNING:** No physical mailing address detected in the truncated source. This is a required CAN-SPAM element. The source may be truncated before the footer, but the QA scanner did not detect it — requires manual footer verification.

**List-Unsubscribe — WARNING:** Header absent from the captured relay copy. RFC 2369 `List-Unsubscribe` and RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) headers are missing. These are likely present on the original SMTP envelope but stripped by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use these headers to surface the native unsubscribe UI; their absence degrades deliverability signals.
