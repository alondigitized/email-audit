# Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email posing as a personalized one. The subject, "Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this," promises curation. The body runs two promos at the top, then a "Hot Sellers" block, a Huda Beauty feature, a "Personalized Beauty Edit," a "Top Beauty" row, and three editorial tiles. The most concrete offer is the top banner: six free trial sizes with $105 minimum spend, code MORE4U, app only. It sits in a short, cramped strip, much like the Dr. Groot send on 10/8. Competing for the eye right below it is a huge "Hot Sellers" block with a big pink hero and a second code, HUDA4X, for 4X points.
- The email asks for too many things. Two promo codes, an app-only condition, a 4X points offer, a 24-hour first-access drop, and a skincare feature all appear within one scroll. Compare the 10/7 send, which scored 8 by leading with one giant "ENDS TODAY / 2X INSIDER" and a single code. This one has no single hero. The "Personalized Beauty Edit" is also a weak spot. It shows Chanel, amika, and Tom Ford twice, and the "Top Beauty" row repeats the Tom Ford fragrance. That reads as generic merchandising, not personal curation.
- Judgment: competent but unfocused, and the subject undersells the actual offer. A mid-pack send, roughly a 5. Sephora sent a similar app-and-points promo on 10/5 and the 10/6 sends, so the cadence is heavy.

## What's working

- The top banner is concrete: free trial sizes, a $105 spend threshold, and a visible code (MORE4U).
- The "BEAUTY INSIDER 4X" badge on the Huda hero makes the loyalty benefit visible, and the code HUDA4X is shown.
- The Huda Beauty hero image is large and polished, and it clearly features a product.
- The product rows show brand, name, and price, which gives useful detail at a glance.
- The footer mentions Same-Day Delivery and in-store pickup, which lowers friction.

## What's weak

- Two different promo codes and two different offers compete at the top, so nothing is the single hero.
- The free-sample banner is crammed into a small strip with a tiny badge, and its "Exclusions/terms apply" text is cut off at the right edge.
- The "Personalized Beauty Edit" repeats the Tom Ford Figue Erotique fragrance in the next row, which undercuts the "personalized" claim.
- The offers carry heavy conditions: app only, a minimum spend, a date limit, and long fine print at the bottom. That weakens the sense that the offer is honest.
- The email is very long, with three editorial tiles and a Dieux feature that dilute the click path.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons in the Spot it. Shop it. tiles and the Hot Sellers block are generic and go to category pages, not specific products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the free-trial-size offer, make it a full-width block with the code and a clear button, and move the Huda 4X promo below it.
- 2. Make the personalization real. Show products related to something the subscriber has browsed or bought, and don't repeat items.
- 3. Drop the "Top Beauty" row, or the duplicate Tom Ford item, to shorten the email.
- 4. Put the app-only condition in the button copy, for example "Get 6 free samples in the app," so the condition isn't discovered at the end.
- 5. Give the buttons specific copy ("Shop Huda Easy Bake") instead of "Shop Now."

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYxMDAw…`: the path and host look like a tracking or open-pixel image. It should have `alt=""` at minimum.
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`: a nav header image. Without alt text the nav is unreadable when images are blocked and for screen readers.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This stops users from scaling text on iOS and similar clients.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute, so screen readers must guess the language.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`, and the space makes it invalid. It is also a no-op in most email clients.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message. This is minor.
