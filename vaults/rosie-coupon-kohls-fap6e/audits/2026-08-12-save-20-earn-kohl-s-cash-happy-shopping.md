# Save 20% 👏 Earn Kohl's Cash 👏 Happy shopping 👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's production template that's been running since 7/30 — Rewards status pinned above the fold, red "extra 20% off" coupon banner, green Kohl's Cash strip, nav buttons, then a fall-home lead-in ("Get ahead of fall. Stay ahead of your budget.") that stacks bedding, kitchen/dining, the twice-yearly Bali semi-annual intimates event, FLX activewear clearance, and a "picked for you" product grid. It's a lot of ground covered in one send, and unlike some recent versions there's no subject/hero mismatch this time — the copy stays generic ("Save 20%") rather than over-promising a specific theme it doesn't deliver.
- Technically it renders clean: no clipped starbursts, no broken art, nav buttons align, product tiles are intact, and the barcode/coupon code block at the bottom is legible. The email is doing its job as a workhorse discount blast, but it's visually and structurally near-identical to the 8/8 and 8/9 sends — same layout skeleton, same 20%-off-plus-Kohl's-Cash mechanic, just reshuffled categories. A daily subscriber has now seen this exact frame five-plus times in two weeks.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand, bug-free execution of a template that's overdue for a refresh — the offer and layout are getting stale even though nothing here is broken.

## What's working

- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum, tiles and nav all line up.
- Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn: 5% Rewards") is visible above the fold, giving loyalty members an immediate personalized anchor.
- The 20% off coupon code and PIN are clearly legible in the barcode block at the bottom, reducing checkout friction.
- Multiple concrete price points are shown (Bali bras $19.99, mattress topper $137.99-$209.99, jeans $24.99-$26.00), not just vague percentages.
- Subject and hero copy are consistent this time — no repeat of the 8/10 "Fall home" mismatch bug.

## What's weak

- The layout is a near-exact repeat of the 8/8 and 8/9 sends — same red coupon banner, same green Kohl's Cash strip, same nav-then-category cascade — cadence fatigue is real for anyone opening daily.
- Hero copy "Get ahead of fall. Stay ahead of your budget." is generic filler that doesn't point to a single clear offer — the eye has too many competing CTAs (Shop Home, Shop Now x4, Shop Active Sale) before it lands anywhere specific.
- The FLX "up to 40% off" module includes small print ("does not apply to select brands") right under the headline, which reads as a mild bait risk on an otherwise bold claim.
- No product-level reviews or social proof anywhere in the body, despite six-plus product callouts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer instead of five competing modules — pick either the semi-annual Bali event or the 20% sitewide coupon as the single above-the-fold CTA, push the rest below a clear "more ways to save" divider.
- 2. Vary the template shell — even swapping banner color or restructuring the nav row would break the visual sameness after a week of identical framing.
- 3. Move loyalty personalization further into the offer — "5% Rewards" is shown but not tied to a specific dollar benefit; show projected Kohl's Cash earned on a typical cart.
- 4. Tighten the FLX module — put "excludes select brands" in a less prominent, single-line disclaimer rather than immediately under the 40% claim.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard ESP-generated (Kohl's/Coherent Path) marketing email with tracking pixels, redirect-wrapped links, and MSO/legacy HTML fallbacks; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped from HTTP probing (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com` domains) — link destinations could not be verified as reachable/non-broken.
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` (x6), `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), and an open-tracking beacon at `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?SAD7DQBP2SZU5K4X6CVGW73SKU.60262&d=60262&bmt=0`.
- No issues found with link structural integrity beyond the above probe-skip limitation.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images missing `alt` text (QA-confirmed): the primary open-tracking pixel, `o.gif`, the Demdex `event` pixel, and 6 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render-tracking pixels. Most of these are 1x1 tracking pixels, so the practical accessibility impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` (empty, not missing) to be explicit no-content images for screen readers — several already correctly use `alt=""`/`aria-hidden="true"` (e.g., `o.gif`) while others have no alt attribute at all.
- Legacy MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and Outlook-specific CSS (`#outlook a`, `mso-table-lspace`) are present, indicating Outlook desktop rendering support.
- `<meta name="viewport" ... user-scalable=0>` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility anti-pattern for low-vision users.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px via `@media only screen and (max-width: 500px)` with `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` classes — standard mobile-responsive pattern, no issues found.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — a stray unmatched closing `</custom>` tag follows the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML (no matching opening tag) and may cause unpredictable DOM handling in strict parsers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible source.
- Personalization is handled server-side via query params on tracking URLs (`mi_mid=019ff6b3-b400-7000-8c16-0efdf6707707`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`) rather than client-visible tokens — no issues found.
