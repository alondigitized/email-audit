# Your budget is going to love these low prices 🥰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "lowest prices of the season" send in about four days. It follows Oct 19's "Our LOWEST prices have arrived 🙌", Oct 20's Kohl's Cash send and Oct 21's two sends. The body is a solid, scannable price catalog: a bold magenta hero, a tidy stack of "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON" tiles, and a Kohl's Rewards/Kohl's Cash strip near the top. As a landing page it works.
- The subject line is the weak link. "Your budget is going to love these low prices 🥰" is warm but has no number, no category and no deadline. It's the same vague message as Oct 21's "Prices so low, filling your cart is a little too easy 🛒", and it gives a subscriber who has seen four of these no reason to open a fifth.
- Verdict: good creative, tired campaign. The cadence and the generic subject cost this send more than any layout issue.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and instantly readable. "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON" sits on saturated magenta with a "Shop Now" button and "through Oct. 25" in view.
- Each tile has one clear job: a big price or percent, a short descriptor, and a "Shop Now" link. Examples are "Up to 40% off LEGO", "Starting at 7.99 Long-sleeve tees" and "5/$30 panties".
- The Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash strip is visible near the top, so member value shows without scrolling.
- Category nav chips (Home, Women, Men, Kids, $10 & Under, $20 & Under) give a fast path to the shopper's own category.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product block with Reg. vs. sale pricing adds concrete value proof further down. Examples are the Ninja SLUSH at $299.99 and towels at 1.99–8.99.
- I saw no broken images or layout breakage in the render.

## What's weak

- Ten near-identical tiles with the same "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON" header dilute the message. Nothing stands out as the one offer to act on.
- The upper hero also carries a competing "Clearance up to 85% off" panel. That splits attention between two headline promos.
- The "Shop Now" CTAs are generic and repeated about 10 times, with no product or category in the button copy.
- Fine print and exclusions pile up ("Select styles", "Coupons do not apply to select brands"), and the footer legal block is very long. That undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- The gift-guide and holiday module mid-email shifts the tone from price-led to gifting. The email gets long, and the core price message gets buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the cadence. Five lowest-price sends in four days trains people to ignore the series. Hold the next one until there's a real change, such as a new tier, a Kohl's Cash window or a category spotlight.
- 2. Put one concrete number in the subject. The body has plenty to choose from: LEGO up to 40% off, boots from 17.99, towels from 3.99.
- 3. Cut the tile stack to the 4–5 strongest categories, and put the 85% clearance and rewards callouts in a single strip rather than competing with the hero.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop LEGO deals" or "Shop $7.99 tees".
- 5. Move the Kohl's Cash earn/spend deadline up under the hero so urgency is visible without scrolling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LEGO up to 40% off + tees from $7.99 🧱`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Boots $17.99, towels $3.99 — thru Oct. 25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 85% off clearance, and prices end Oct. 25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has real markup and accessibility defects: invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, a viewport that blocks zoom, and missing `alt` on content images. The compliance and authentication warnings come mostly from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 78 tracking links were skipped because they are redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels before the visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/dd53af8c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024337_20261022`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Malformed pixel markup:** The `open.aspx` pixel is `<img … alt="">` followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag. This is invalid HTML and may be parsed unpredictably by some clients.
- **Unhidden pixel:** The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) is a bare `<img width="1" height="1">` with no `alt` and no hiding wrapper, unlike the others.
- **Plain-text part:** 13,866 of 19,598 characters (71%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The cause is 78 long tracked links with `qs=` blobs. This is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `alt` is missing on 15 images in the QA data, and these are real defects:
  - Product images: `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58966978_{1sa,2sa,11sa,14sa}_2x`.
  - Image-rendered content from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`). If these carry offer text, that text is invisible with images off or to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels:** The pixel images (`o.gif`, `demdex.net/event`, `click.chp…`) should carry `alt=""`. They are decorative, so this is low severity.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early, and Gmail and other clients will mostly ignore the schema markup.
- **Missing roles:** The preheader table (`<table width='640' … class="container">`) and the tracking tables lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the outer layout tables.
- **Responsive gap:** The container is fixed at `width="640"` and the responsive rules only apply at `max-width: 500px`. Widths between 500px and 640px aren't handled.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and some clients strip these, so Montserrat falls back to Arial/Helvetica.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. Per-recipient IDs such as `mi_u=604230016` and `d_sid=13245196` are populated. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so the sender's own configuration can't be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the header wasn't captured. The sender is `kohls@s.kohls.com`, an aligned subdomain, but this is unverified.
- **Physical address:** None was detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is probably a visibility gap and not a confirmed violation. Verify against the full source.
- **Unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the provided source for the same reason.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Redirects:** All links use encrypted SFMC-style redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7…`). Destination URLs and any UTM parameters can't be seen or validated.
- **Campaign identifiers:** Campaign tracking appears to use `mi_ecmp=1024337_20261022` and `email_name=261022_DG_Email_Thursd…` on image URLs, not `utm_*`. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible.
- **Landing pages:** Landing-page alignment can't be assessed because the probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product images and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Set `alt=""` on the pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel. Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or drop it.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Populate `<title>`, and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
5. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables. Consider breakpoints between 500px and 640px.
6. Reduce the plain-text URL ratio by using fewer tracked links, or add more descriptive plain-text copy.
7. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or the full HTML to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer address and the unsubscribe link.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check landing pages and UTM or `mi_*` parameter continuity.
