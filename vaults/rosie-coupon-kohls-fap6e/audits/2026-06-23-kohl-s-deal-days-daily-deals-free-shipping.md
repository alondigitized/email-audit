# Kohl’s Deal Days = Daily Deals + free shipping 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's second email sent on June 23 — the brand history already shows "JUST IN: Kohl's Deal Days + Free Shipping + Daily Deals 🤗" from the same date, and the two emails are covering near-identical ground. The creative here is actually the more substantive of the two: it has a clear Deal Days hero, a properly segmented "Today's Featured Daily Deals" module with real percentage-off figures (70%, 50%, 60%), and a separate "6 Days of Deals" overview section with specific prices ($9.99, $21.99) and deeper discounts (65% off luggage, 75% off beauty). The email does a lot of structural work well.
- The fatal problem is cadence, not creative. Sending two Deal Days emails on the same day with overlapping messaging turns what could be a strong launch send into inbox noise. A subscriber who opened the first one this morning is now looking at a structural remix of the same deal — same event name, same free-shipping hook, same category grid. The subject also partially cannibalizes itself by echoing "Deal Days = Daily Deals," a redundancy that wastes the hook.
- On its own merits, this is a competent multi-offer promotional email that executes the format Kohl's has run before. As the second touch on the same day, it's going to get buried or ignored.

## What's working

- **Multi-tier offer structure is clear:** "Today's Featured Daily Deals" vs. "6 Days of Deals" gives the email two distinct reasons to scroll — one urgent (today only), one event-level (6-day window).
- **Specific price points visible ($4.99, $9.99, $21.99):** Dollar anchors alongside percentages are more credible than percentages alone.
- **Deep discounts are prominent:** 65% off luggage and 75% off beauty are genuinely compelling numbers, placed visually before the clearance CTA.
- **No visible render bugs:** Layout is clean, images load, text doesn't overlap, CTA buttons are present throughout.
- **Category nav tabs at top:** Lets a scanner jump directly to their department without needing to scroll the full email.

## What's weak

- **Second email on the same day duplicates the launch:** The "JUST IN: Kohl's Deal Days" send already introduced this event; this email has no meaningful escalation to justify a second touch.
- **Subject is redundant:** "Deal Days = Daily Deals" restates the same concept twice with no new information added.
- **No visible deadline or date anchor:** "6 Days of Deals" should say *when* the six days end — a specific date would add real urgency.
- **Free shipping is buried as a secondary hook:** It appears as a footer banner rather than a top-of-email value prop; on mobile, subscribers may never reach it.
- **No Kohl's Cash or loyalty messaging visible in the body:** Given the history of Kohl's Cash as a retention lever, its absence here is a missed engagement signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Consolidate the two June 23 sends into one:** This email has the better body creative; the "JUST IN" subject from the first send is the better hook. One email combining both would have performed better than two diluting each other.
- 2. **Add a hard date to the event:** "Deal Days end Sunday" or "through June 28" costs nothing and makes the 6-day window feel concrete instead of vague.
- 3. **Move free shipping to the hero:** Placing it beneath the Deal Days banner — not the footer — would make it a purchase-trigger rather than an afterthought.
- 4. **Fix the subject redundancy:** "Deal Days" and "Daily Deals" mean the same thing to the subscriber; swap one for the benefit.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Days kicks off: 70% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 days of deals start now — free shipping included`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today: bedding to 70% off, luggage to 65% off — new deals drop daily`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your first daily deal is live — shop before it's gone`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Deal Days" Email

**Date:** 2026-06-26 | **From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **QA Pass Rate:** 33%

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a sound legacy HTML4 table layout with appropriate MSO/VML conditionals, but has meaningful compliance gaps (missing physical address, unverified unsubscribe headers) and widespread missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images. SPF/DKIM authentication status cannot be confirmed through the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **22 tracking links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains. Individual link destinations and HTTP status codes could not be validated.
- Three distinct tracking/pixel systems active simultaneously:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath click tracker)
  - `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Sailthru open pixel + rendered-path images)
  - `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager segment sync pixel, `d_sid=13245196`)
- **Malformed HTML tag** at line containing the open-tracker: `</custom>` is an invalid closing tag immediately after the `<img>` for `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`. This is a stray/orphaned tag that will produce a parse error in strict renderers.

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 11 images**, including non-decorative content images:

| Image | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `1e48fb9f...` (CoherentPath pixel) | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Missing alt |
| `o.gif` (open pixel) | `mi.kohls.com` | Missing alt |
| `event` (AAM pixel) | `kohls.demdex.net` | Missing alt; `width="0" height="0"` but no `aria-hidden` |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` (6 content images) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` | Missing alt on what appear to be product/promo images |

The open-pixel `o.gif` has `aria-hidden="true"` — correct. The AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and CoherentPath pixel lack both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`, which is a WCAG 2.1 failure for decorative images used as tracking beacons.

**`<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0">`** — disabling user scaling is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text) and flagged by modern accessibility auditors.

**`<title></title>`** — empty document title. Screen readers announce this; it should be set to match the subject line or a meaningful label.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` in `<style>`) for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat). This is redundant and adds two unnecessary round-trips in webmail clients that honor external CSS.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u=604230016` user ID token in Marigold pixel URLs appears correctly substituted. The rendered-path image URLs include `email_name=260623_DG_Email_Tue_PM` — consistent with a June 23 send, not the June 26 date on this audit; confirm this is the correct email artifact.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — cannot confirm presence |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Not detected — one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Apple Mail requirement since Feb 2024) likely non-functional |
| Physical mailing address | **FAIL** | Not detected in truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM §7(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address |
| `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` | Pass | Present |
| `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` | Pass | Present |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header is the highest-priority compliance issue: Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI without it, increasing spam complaints. This is enforced for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024.

The email name token in Marigold image URLs (`email_name=260623_DG_Email_Tue_PM`) uses an internal campaign label, not a standardized UTM value — if this feeds analytics it will appear as a raw internal string rather than a mapped campaign name.

### 7. Recommendations

2. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are set at the ESP/MTA layer (`kohls.com` sending infra). If the AgentMail relay strips them, test raw headers via a seed address outside the relay.
3. **Remove `</custom>` stray tag** after the `click.s.kohls.com` open-tracker `<img>`.

**P1 — Fix in next revision cycle:**
4. **Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`** to all tracking pixels (CoherentPath, AAM). For content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`), add descriptive alt text.
5. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta tag.
6. **Set a non-empty `<title>`** (e.g., `Kohl's Deal Days — Daily Deals + Free Shipping`).
7. **Deduplicate Google Fonts loading** — keep one `<link>` per family, remove the redundant `@import` block.
