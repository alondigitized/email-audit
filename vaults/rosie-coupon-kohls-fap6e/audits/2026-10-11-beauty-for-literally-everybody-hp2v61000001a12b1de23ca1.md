# Beauty for literally everybody
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email under a vague subject. "Beauty for literally everybody" promises nothing concrete. The body opens with a "Choose a Trial Size" strip (free mascara with code MASCARA, online only with a $40 purchase), then a giant "BEAUTY YOUR WAY" hero. The hero is a mood image with no offer. The real news, the Kayali Yum Body Collection, sits below it with a "SHOP NOW" button.
- Below that is a six-tile grid of new and limited-edition products: Kiehl's, ILIA, Dyson, Josie Maran, Rare Beauty, One/Size, Dae. Then come a Korean sunscreen tile, a 4X points on Huda Beauty tile (code HUDA4X, 10/8 to 10/14, app only) and a "New in Skincare" tile. Almost everything is "new" or "only at Sephora", and the offers are scattered and small.
- The email is well produced and on brand, but it has no single focus. The strongest offer, 4X points, is buried near the bottom. It is better than the 10/9 "Hey, Insider" send only in polish. It is weaker than the 10/7 "ends today" email, which had one offer and one button. Judgement: average, about 5/10.

## What's working

- The trial-size strip puts a concrete free gift (mascara, code MASCARA) at the very top with a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button.
- The pink and purple gradient and the large model image look polished and match the Sephora brand.
- The product grid labels each item with the brand, a "NEW" or "LIMITED EDITION" tag and a price with a value comparison, e.g. "$72 ($110 value)".
- The Kayali block has a clear headline, a one-line benefit and a visible "SHOP NOW" button.
- The 4X points tile gives a specific code and a specific date window.

## What's weak

- The hero "BEAUTY YOUR WAY" carries no offer, no product name and no CTA, so it takes the most valuable space for nothing.
- The 4X points deal for Huda Beauty is buried below the product grid, and it is app-only.
- The trial-size strip is small and cramped. It also repeats the format of the 10/8 "FREE Dr. Groot trial size" email, which was also criticised for a cramped hero.
- The email carries too many competing messages: trial size, Kayali, six products, sunscreen, Huda points, skincare brands, Beauty Insider. None gets priority.
- The product tiles show no ratings or reviews, so there is little social proof.
- The footer fine print is long and dense, and the free-gift terms are buried there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Open with one hero. Either lead with the Kayali launch or move 4X points to the top with a button, and cut the grid to three or four products.
- 2. Make the trial-size strip a full-width banner with the code and the $40 minimum stated up front.
- 3. Add ratings or "best seller" cues to the product tiles.
- 4. Give the points offer its deadline in the headline, as the 10/7 "ends today" send did.
- 5. Put "Shop Kayali" or "Shop Huda Beauty" in button copy instead of generic "SHOP NOW".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is mostly sound, but the QA run has one hard failure: the plain-text part is empty (0 chars). The source also has several accessibility gaps, including 7 images with no alt text, and two header checks that couldn't be verified. The HTML is truncated, so the findings below are limited to what's visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. Only 12 of 59 links were probed and 47 were skipped, so coverage is about 20%. The skipped links are footer, utility and social links, and an unsubscribe link is always probed. That limit applies to everything below.
- `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is an image with no alt text. It looks like an open-tracking pixel. It should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, which matters for screen readers.
- All other flagged images are served from `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/...`, a third-party ESP CDN. The probes reported no failures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images:**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`
  - `20261011_Beauty_for_All_Stitched_revised.gif`
  - `20261011_Beauty_for_All_Stitched_rev1_12.jpg`

  The "Stitched" filenames suggest sliced images that carry the content and links. With images blocked or a screen reader on, those sections would be blank or unlabelled.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` has a space. The valid token is `noindex`, so the directive is likely ignored. This only matters for the web-view version.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't match the subject line, "Beauty for literally everybody". This is minor and affects the web-view version and some clients' title display.
- **Text-size lock:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is set on `td`, `div`, `a` and other elements. That disables user text scaling in WebKit clients. This is minor.
- The mobile CSS uses `*[class=...]` attribute selectors with `!important`. Nothing in the visible source is broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source has no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 chars.** This is a real defect, not a relay artifact. A missing text/plain part hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients and some assistive tools with nothing.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. I can't tell whether Sephora omits them or the relay dropped them. Check the raw `.eml` before treating it as a sender fault.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the QA tool expected this from the relay. No issue can be asserted.
- An unsubscribe link was probed, per the QA note. The footer content and physical address are outside the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. The truncated source and the QA output show no destination URLs, UTM parameters or redirect results. Tracking links route through a redirect domain, and the probe results don't expose their final destinations. The pipeline should capture the final landing URL and any UTM params for the top CTAs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain-text part (high):** Add a `text/plain` alternative. This is the only hard failure.
2. **Alt text (medium):** Add descriptive alt text to the five "Stitched" and header images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. **Viewport (medium):** Remove `maximum-scale=1` so users can zoom.
4. **Robots meta (low):** Change `no index` to `noindex`.
5. **Unverified headers (QA tooling):** Check whether AgentMail strips `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If it does, capture the raw headers separately so these checks stop returning WARN.
6. **Link coverage (QA tooling):** Raise the probe limit or log skipped links. Also record each CTA's final URL and UTM params so section 6 can be assessed.
