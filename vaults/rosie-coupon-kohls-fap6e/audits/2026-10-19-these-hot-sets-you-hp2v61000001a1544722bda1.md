# These hot sets + you = ⚡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email under a subject that tells you nothing. "These hot sets + you = ⚡" teases "sets," and the body does deliver a "Meet Must-Have Sets" grid. But the hero is a different product, "The Healthy Hair & Scalp Kit," a charity-tied limited edition. Above it sits a Savings Event teaser ("30% off," "Coming soon") and a free Scent Mini with code APPLELOVE. The email has three competing offers before the set grid starts. The eye has no single place to land.
- The set grid is solid. It has eight limited-edition or Sephora-exclusive sets with names, contents and prices, including Armani, Fenty and Glossier. The sets also show value against retail, such as "$55 ($80 value)." Below the grid, three more modules (Korean haircare, perfumes, Haus Labs lip liner) push the email into a long catalog. It follows the pattern of the 10/17 "Beauty this good moves fast 👀" and 10/12 "All over your feed 🤩" sends. Those also had vague subjects over stacked promos.
- Judgment: good product merchandising, weak focus. A recipient sees a Savings Event that isn't live yet and a free sample with a purchase minimum, then has to scroll to get to the sets the subject promised.

## What's working

- The set grid shows value clearly, with prices and retail values ("$25 ($80 value)", "$98 ($170 value)").
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" labels give real scarcity, and they match the 10/13 makeup-sets send.
- The Clean at Sephora badges and recognizable brands (Armani, Fenty, Glossier, Kulfi) add trust.
- The hero has a clear "SHOP NOW" button and a bold, readable headline.
- The Beauty Insider strip with the points balance gives a loyalty hook.

## What's weak

- The top of the email stacks three promos, with the Savings Event "Coming soon" tease and the APPLELOVE Scent Mini ahead of the hero. Nothing leads.
- The hero is a hair-and-scalp kit, but the subject promises "hot sets," and the sets don't appear until after the hero.
- The Memorial Sloan Kettering donation paragraph is a large block of text in the middle of the email, and it pushes the product grid down.
- The grid products have no individual CTA buttons and no ratings or reviews.
- The footer fine print is long, dense and tiny, and it buries the Scent Mini terms ($40 purchase, online only).
- The email runs long and adds three more modules after the grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the sets the hero. Lead with the set grid, or a single hero set, and move the Savings Event teaser to a slim banner.
- 2. Cut the top stack to one offer: either the Scent Mini or the Savings Event teaser.
- 3. Shorten the charity copy to one line and link out for the details.
- 4. Add a "Shop the set" button or price pill to each tile, and add star ratings.
- 5. Remove the last three modules, or move them to a separate send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Eight limited-edition sets, with the price and retail value shown. Shop before they sell out.`
