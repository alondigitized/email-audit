# Last chance ⏳ Shop Kohl's Deal Days + get Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eighth Deal Days-era send in about four days, so a subscriber has seen it before. It's a long kitchen-sink promo: Daily Deals, bedding, LEGO, luggage, pajamas, jeans and Clearance, with a Kohl's Rewards $10-off-$50 bar between sections. Everything below the top is competent and scannable. The offers are concrete (40%, 60%, up to 80% off, plus 25% off with coupon), and the "Ends October 8" banners match the "Last chance" subject.
- The weak spot is the top. The hero area renders as a large, nearly empty magenta block, and the real content doesn't start until the "Enjoy Free Shipping" and "Earn Kohl's Cash" pills. On a phone, the first screen shows almost no offer. The send also adds nothing new beyond the "last chance" framing, after "Kohl's Deal Days + Kohl's Cash are here today, gone tomorrow 👋" and the 10-07 sends.
- Verdict: a solid, honest promo that fatigue and a dead hero hold back. It's about a 6, in line with the recent sends in this run.

## What's working

- Subject urgency ("Last chance ⏳") matches the "Ends October 8" banners in the body, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The offers are concrete and stacked in plain numerals: 40%, 60%, 20% off, "Up to 50%", "Now up to 80% off", plus 25% off with coupon.
- The green Kohl's Rewards bar ("$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more with coupon") repeats the member benefit without cluttering the layout.
- Product tiles show real prices ($229.99 Ninja SLUSH, $1.99 washcloth, $52 jeans), which gives a click reason beyond category banners.
- Barcode coupons and codes at the bottom (YOULSAVE25, SMOKE) are redeemable in-store, which fits Kohl's omnichannel brand voice.

## What's weak

- The top hero renders as a big empty magenta block. The offer doesn't appear above the fold, and the first scroll feels like dead space.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" or "Shop Here". None says which product or category you're getting.
- The email is far too long, with 15+ modules, and the "Last chance" message gets diluted by the "4 Days of Deals" and Clearance sections.
- Exclusions are stacked in fine print ("Coupons do not apply," "Select styles," "Excludes Sephora at Kohl's purchases"), which undercuts the headline percentages.
- It repeats the last week's creative almost unchanged, so a subscriber has little reason to treat this as different.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or replace the hero. Put one big offer there: "Last day: 25% off + $10 Kohl's Rewards," with a single "Shop today's deals" button, visible in the first screen.
- 2. Cut the body to 6–8 modules and lead with the three Daily Deals, since the deadline is that day. Drop Clearance and the second product grid.
- 3. Make CTA copy specific, like "Shop bedding 40% off" or "Get the LEGO deal," instead of "Shop Now."
- 4. Pull the exclusions into one clear line near the offer, not scattered footnotes, to keep the offer honest.
- 5. Suppress or de-escalate cadence. After seven near-identical sends, make the final send visibly different, such as a countdown-style "Ends tonight" block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 25% off + Kohl's Cash ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deal Days wraps up today — your $10 reward inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last day for Daily Deals, up to 80% off clearance, and Kohl's Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take an extra 25% off with code YOULSAVE25 before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Kohl's "Last chance ⏳ Shop Kohl's Deal Days + get Kohl's Cash 🤑"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based template with several markup defects: a `<div>` inside `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, and a zoom-blocking viewport. It also has four stacked tracking pixels and many images with no `alt`. The source was truncated, and all 73 links go through redirect domains, so the destinations, UTM parameters, footer and unsubscribe link could not be checked.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking links were skipped** by the QA probe. They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`. The final destinations are hidden in the opaque `qs=` payload, so link health is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Four tracking beacons load at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d72d1999-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60267`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- Four stacked pixels means redundant open tracking. Image-blocking clients and Apple MPP will make open data from these unreliable.
- **Stray `</custom>` tag** follows the `open.aspx` `<img>`, which has no opening tag. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** in the head can trigger a request back to the document URL in some clients.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close the head and start the body there. Schema.org microdata doesn't belong in a head either way.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and screen readers use it, so it should be filled in.
- **Zoom is blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) on mobile.
- **Missing `alt` attributes.** QA flagged these images:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` has no `alt` attribute at all, and the other pixels use `alt=""`.
  - Content images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`, all served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`.
  - Product image `59107814_2_2x` from `images.am.kohls.com`.
  - The list was truncated, so there may be more.
- If the offers are baked into those images, image-blocked or screen-reader users get no offer content.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 pixel table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Doctype and namespaces:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v`, which is non-standard but common for Outlook.
- **Minor CSS:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so the rule has no effect.
- **Web fonts:** both `<link>` and `@import` load Google Fonts (Roboto, Montserrat). Gmail and Outlook ignore these, and the fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is present. Loading each font twice is redundant.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Possible empty personalization field:** an image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...`, so the Kohl's Cash redemption end date is blank in that request. This may be intentional for recipients without a Kohl's Cash balance, but it can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- Per-recipient identifiers are embedded in pixel URLs (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_mid=01a11a86-...`). That is normal for tracking but means the HTML is recipient-specific.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA couldn't find either header. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed and not a verified sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the original message.
- **No physical address detected** (CAN-SPAM). The footer is in the truncated portion of the source, so this may be a false positive. Confirm against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown because of the relay. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the tracking domain `click.s.kohls.com`, but that proves nothing about authentication.
- **Unsubscribe link:** it isn't visible in the provided source, so it is unverified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be evaluated.** Destinations are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, and the probe skipped all 73 links. The only campaign identifier visible is `email_name=261008_DG_Email_Thu_PM` on `mi.kohls.com` image URLs.
- **Landing page alignment** with the "Deal Days" and "Kohl's Cash" offer couldn't be verified for the same reason.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Move `itemscope`/`<meta itemprop>` out of `<head>` (or drop it), remove the stray `</custom>`, and fill in `<title>`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and `59107814_2_2x`. Add `alt=""` to the first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`). Add `role="presentation"` to the pixel and preheader tables.
4. Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">` and the duplicate font loading, and fix `.ReadMsgBody`.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels if they are redundant.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated message with raw headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, the Authentication-Results values, the physical address, and the unsubscribe link.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check UTM parameters, landing pages and the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value.
