# Instant Kohl's Cash Giveaway ✅ In Store Only ✅ Starts Today ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different beat than the four Back-to-School blasts Kohl's has run this week — the subject leads with "Instant Kohl's Cash Giveaway" and the hero banner up top ("Don't miss millions in instant Kohl's Cash giveaways up to $100. In store only. No purchase necessary. While supplies last.") is the actual headline offer, not the usual "up to 40% off" framing. Problem is the render buries that offer almost immediately — right below the giveaway banner is another hero for "Back to School... up to 40% off storewide," which is the same creative Kohl's has run essentially unchanged since 8/19-8/21. The email reads like two campaigns stitched together, and the more novel, higher-curiosity offer (free Kohl's Cash giveaway) gets one banner before the eye is redirected to the now-stale 40%-off cascade.
- The category cascade underneath (Shoes 40%, Activewear 40%, Basics 40%, Kitchen 35%, Home $3.99, Back to College 50%) is a near-verbatim repeat of the 8/21 and 8/20 sends, and the in-store-only giveaway is the one piece of real news, but it's given the least visual real estate relative to its subject-line prominence. Given this is the fifth Back-to-School-adjacent send in six days, fatigue is a real risk, and this one doesn't do enough to differentiate itself past the top banner.
- Bottom line: strong, unusual subject line undercut by an email that reverts to the same 40%-off/BTS template as every prior send this week. It'll get opened on subject-line curiosity but the body doesn't deliver on that hook fast enough.

## What's working

- The top banner clearly states the giveaway mechanic — "up to $100," "No purchase necessary," "In store only," "While supplies last" — matching the subject line's urgency.
- "STARTS TODAY" pill is a legible, credible urgency cue tied to a specific date-relative claim.
- Category grid (Women/Men/Girls/Boys/Shoes/Home) gives a clear secondary navigation path for shoppers not interested in the giveaway.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, pricing and strikethrough treatment (Reg. $42-$54, etc.) is legible throughout.
- Semi-Annual Sale section further down (bras $19.99, panties 5/$35) has clean, specific pricing.

## What's weak

- The giveaway offer — the actual subject-line hook — gets pushed off-screen almost immediately by a full "Back to School up to 40% off" hero that's a repeat of 8/19-8/21 creative.
- No details on how the giveaway actually works (how do you know if you won, what's the range below $100, any minimum purchase context despite "no purchase necessary") until a buried "details" link.
- The email is extremely long — giveaway, BTS cascade, Sears-Annual bras/panties sale, "Picked with you in mind" grab-bag (blender, towels, jeans, mattress topper) — with no throughline connecting the sections back to the giveaway premise.
- This is the fifth BTS-adjacent Kohl's send in six days (8/17 fall home, 8/18 intimates, 8/19 x2, 8/20, 8/21, now 8/22) — the 40% cascade is visually identical to 8/20 and 8/21, which will read as reused creative to a frequent opener.
- "In store only" is a meaningful limitation on the headline offer that only appears in small text within the banner — easy to miss at a glance, and it undercuts click-through since there's no online CTA tied to the giveaway itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the whole email with the giveaway, not just the top banner.** Move the BTS 40%-off hero down below a proper giveaway explainer section (how it works, how to claim, store finder CTA) — right now the giveaway is one banner deep before the email reverts to Tuesday's template.
- 2. **Add a real CTA for the giveaway** — "Find a Store" is present but tiny; make it a primary button-style CTA since the offer is in-store only and needs to drive foot traffic specifically, not the generic Shop Now used everywhere else.
- 3. **Cut or consolidate the tail sections** (Semi-Annual bras/panties, "Picked with you in mind" grab-bag) — they dilute an email that opened on a giveaway hook with unrelated inventory-clearance content.
- 4. **Vary the BTS cascade creative** — it's now run near-identically three sends in a row (8/20, 8/21, 8/22); swap product photography or reorder categories so repeat openers don't immediately recognize it as a repeat.
- 5. **Surface "in store only" more prominently** near the subject-matching banner so mobile users aren't surprised there's no online redemption path.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your instant Kohl's Cash giveaway starts now 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No purchase necessary. Stop by any Kohl's store today for your chance to win.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 40% off Back to School — while giveaway supplies last.`
