# 🚨2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This email follows through on the subject at the top. A banner reads "Earn Points Faster on Top Brands, Ends 10/7, use code SAVEPOINTS," with 2X and 5X badges. After that it turns into a holiday gift-set catalog. "Holiday Vaults Are Here" and a "Get Gifting" button lead into six "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA + LIMITED EDITION" sets, each with a price and a "value" figure. They run from about $50 to $272. A points-multiplier strip (2X, 3X, 4X, 5X) repeats the Insider message further down.
- The problem is that the email runs two campaigns at once. The subject sells "ends tomorrow" urgency on points, but the largest creative block by far is the vault gallery, and the vaults have no deadline. The email then adds a gift-idea tool ("TRY IT"), a First Access holiday drops tile, an Auto-Replenish tile, a "$100 purchase" tile with code SAVETEN, and a Kohl's barcode. This is the third points-multiplier message in about six days, after the 2026-10-01 "STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points" send and the 2026-10-05 app early access send. It's long and crowded, and the one-day deadline gets buried.
- Judgement: a credible subject with real urgency, followed by a scroll-heavy body that dilutes it. It's a decent send, not a sharp one.
- Caveat: the render I had was heavily downscaled, so I read layout, headings and prices but could not read the fine print.

## What's working

- The subject and the top banner match: points multipliers, "Ends 10/7," and a visible promo code.
- The vault gallery has a consistent layout, with a price and a "value" anchor on every set. The savings framing is clear.
- "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA + LIMITED EDITION" tags add scarcity and exclusivity to the gift sets.
- The 2X/3X/4X/5X Insider tier strip is a clear visual for loyalty benefits.
- The gift-set theme fits the season, with holiday gifting starting in early October.

## What's weak

- The "ends tomorrow" deadline fits only the top banner. The body is mostly an undated catalog, so the urgency fades after the first screen.
- The email is extremely long. It has about six vault blocks, then a gift finder, a First Access tile, an Auto-Replenish tile, a code tile and a Kohl's barcode. That is too many calls to action.
- Several codes and offers compete: SAVEPOINTS for points, SAVETEN for "10% off your $100 purchase," and "$100 off"-style framing. Shoppers have to work out which to use.
- The points hero is small compared with the vault imagery. The 2X/5X badges are tiny, so the subject's main promise gets less visual weight than the products.
- This is the third points email in six days, which reads as fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one job. Either make this a points-deadline email with a big "ends tomorrow" banner and a small vault teaser, or make it a vault email and move the points deadline to the subject of a separate send.
- 2. Make the points hero larger and add a countdown or "Ends 10/7 at 11:59 PM PT," with one button that goes to Sephora Collection.
- 3. Cut the body to three vaults and drop the Kohl's barcode, Auto-Replenish and the second code tile. Fewer blocks means more clicks per block.
- 4. Use one code, or none, so the offer is clear.
- 5. Don't send a third multiplier email in a week. Fold it into one earlier send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2X points + 5X on Sephora Collection ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow's the deadline for 5X points on Sephora Collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEPOINTS by 10/7. Plus holiday vaults from $50, only at Sephora.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your points boost ends tomorrow. Gift sets are worth up to 3x what you pay.`
