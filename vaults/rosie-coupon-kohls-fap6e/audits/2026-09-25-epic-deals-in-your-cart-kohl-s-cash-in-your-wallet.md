# Epic Deals in your cart 🛒 Kohl’s Cash in your wallet 💸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "EPIC DEALS" purple-hero, category-blitz layout Kohl's has run at least six times since 09-20 — same masthead, same pink "EPIC DEAL" tile stamps, same coupon/barcode footer wall — but this time it's wrapped in cart-abandonment framing ("Epic Deals in your cart 🛒 Kohl's Cash in your wallet 💸") that the actual body never delivers on. Nothing in the render shows an actual cart item, a specific product the recipient left behind, or a Kohl's Cash balance — it's the identical generic deal-tile grid (jeans, towels, luggage, Sephora, toys) just relabeled with personalization language it doesn't back up.
- That mismatch is the real problem: the subject promises "your cart," the body shows nobody's cart. A recipient who opens expecting to see what they abandoned gets another blitz of unrelated category tiles, which reads as bait-and-switch dressed up as retargeting. Judgment: functional render, zero personalization behind the personalization copy, and the seventh near-identical send in a week — fatigue plus a broken promise is worse than just fatigue alone.

## What's working

- Renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Sephora+Kohl's $5-off-$49 beauty tile is a distinct, clearly-priced offer that stands out from the generic deal grid.
- Percentage/dollar deals are specific and visible throughout (5.99, 25% off, 60% off, 50% off).
- Kohl's Cash and Rewards 3x messaging is reinforced near the top, consistent with loyalty members' expectations.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Epic Deals in your cart" but the visible body contains no cart contents, no abandoned-item callout, and no personalized product — pure category blitz.
- "Kohl's Cash in your wallet" implies a specific balance, but no dollar amount or wallet status is shown anywhere in the render.
- This is at least the seventh "EPIC DEALS" purple-hero send since 09-20 (09-20, 09-21 x2, 09-22, 09-23 x2, 09-24) with the identical banner, tile-stamp, and barcode-footer structure — visual fatigue is severe.
- Visual hierarchy is a flat wall of same-size tiles; nothing signals "this is about your cart" as promised by the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the core promise gap: if this is a cart-recovery email, the hero must actually show the specific item(s) left in cart and the actual Kohl's Cash dollar amount — right now the send doesn't deliver what the subject sold. If it can't be dynamically personalized, don't use cart/wallet language in the subject at all.
- 2. Break the "EPIC DEALS" template — after 6+ identical sends this month, this creative needs a new banner treatment, not another reshuffled tile grid, or open rates will keep decaying.
- 3. Move Sephora+Kohl's (a genuinely differentiated offer) or a real cart-item callout above the fold — right now it's buried after ~8 generic tiles.
- 4. Add a visible Kohl's Cash balance/countdown near the top if the subject is going to reference it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `You left something epic in your cart 🛒`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash expires soon — plus what's in your cart`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Come back for it — free shipping on $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kohl's Cash redeemable through 10/4 — don't let it expire`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Kohl's promotional email built on standard ESP tracking/pixel infrastructure; core compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) were not detected in the captured source, and accessibility markup is missing on most images.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 78 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (domains: `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`), so live-link validity is unconfirmed.
- Open-tracking beacon present: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?XHXZ7J5P6N4E7PZPRROPSZXG2I.60263&d=60263&bmt=0` — malformed markup: this 1x1 `<img>` is immediately followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag.
- Multiple 1x1 pixel trackers confirmed: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-tracking pixels.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images flagged with missing `alt` attributes, including both tracking pixels (expected/acceptable — should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden` for spec compliance, which only one of them does) and at least one content image (`57746756_2_2x` — a real product image lacking alt text, which is a genuine accessibility gap for screen reader users).
- Malformed HTML: unmatched `</custom>` closing tag noted above; may cause unpredictable DOM handling in strict renderers (though most email clients silently ignore unknown tags).
- MSO conditional comments and responsive breakpoint (`max-width: 500px`) present — standard Outlook/mobile fallback structure, no issues found.
- `<title></title>` is empty — not a functional bug, but the tag is present and valid.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) detected in the truncated source; `mi_cid`/`mi_mid`/`mi_ecmp` values are populated identifiers, not broken tokens.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not found — may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than absence at origin; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support without header data.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found — same caveat; if genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of 2024+.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires one; not confirmed absent since source is truncated, but not visible in the captured portion.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — likely a relay capture limitation; cannot verify sender authentication status from this data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use proprietary redirect/query-string tracking (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` encoded payload) rather than standard UTM parameters — cannot verify landing page alignment since destination URLs are obscured behind the redirect service and were skipped by the probe.
- Campaign identifiers present in pixel URLs (`mi_ecmp=1022443_2026925`, `email_name=260925_DG_Email_Friday`) suggest internal campaign tagging, but no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters observed in the truncated source.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` text to the content product image (`57746756_2_2x`); set `alt=""` on purely decorative/tracking pixels.
- Remove the orphaned `</custom>` tag near the open-tracking pixel.
- Re-verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay) before treating their absence as a compliance finding — current WARNs may be capture artifacts.
- Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the email footer (likely beyond the truncation point) to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
- If full destination URLs are available, verify they resolve to live, matching Kohl's landing pages and carry consistent UTM/campaign parameters.
