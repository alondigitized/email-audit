# 🔥 Epic Deals 🔥 Who's fired up? 🙋‍♀️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "EPIC DEALS" category-blitz Kohl's has sent since 09-20 — same purple hero banner, same pink "EPIC DEAL" tile wall, same coupon/barcode footer, just a reshuffled product grid (kids tees, bedding, sweaters, jeans, jewelry, handbags, floor care, LEGO, a Ninja blender, a Shark-style vacuum, towels, jeans, a mattress topper). The subject line "Who's fired up? 🙋‍♀️" is the only real variation this time, and it's a weak one — it adds a forced-enthusiasm hook without adding any new information about the offer.
- The layout itself is clean and renders without bugs: clear "EPIC DEAL" price tags, readable Shop Now buttons, a member-rewards banner, and a "Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale" callout with a real date range (through Oct. 4). But there's no single hero offer — it's a wall of 12+ unrelated deal tiles, so nothing dominates the eye, and a subscriber who's seen four of these in a week has no reason to believe this one is more urgent or valuable than the last.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero render issues — but creatively exhausted. This is repetition dressed up with a new emoji, and the brand history confirms the fatigue is real.

## What's working

- No render bugs — every tile, price, and button displays cleanly with no overlapping text or broken images.
- Kohl's Rewards 3x-rewards banner and member-pricing coupon reinforce loyalty value for a member (persona is a Rewards member per the header).
- The "Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale" module stands out visually with its own yellow banner and a concrete date ("through Oct. 4"), giving it more credibility than the generic tiles around it.
- Individual deal tiles are specific (8.99 tees, up to 60% off bedding, $10 off $50 coupon) rather than vague.

## What's weak

- This is the same category-blitz structure run on 09-20, 09-21 (x2), and 09-23 already — the brand history shows zero creative variation, just tile reshuffling.
- No dominant hero offer — 12+ competing tiles with no visual hierarchy means the eye doesn't land anywhere specific.
- Subject line "Who's fired up? 🙋‍♀️" is filler enthusiasm with no product/offer specificity, a step backward from even the prior "EPIC DEALS" subjects.
- "Epic" is doing a lot of work across five sends this week for what are mostly routine markdowns (up to 60% off, $10/$50 coupons) — the framing has outrun the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop running the identical category-blitz layout for a fifth time in a week — rotate in a single-hero-offer format (one flagship deal + a short secondary list) to reset the pattern before subscribers tune out entirely.
- 2. Pick one anchor deal (e.g., the 60% off bedding or the $10/$50 kids coupon) and make it the visual hero above the fold instead of burying it in a tile grid.
- 3. Replace the "who's fired up" subject with something that names an actual product or dollar figure — vague hype subjects score worse than specific ones for this list.
- 4. Give the Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale its own send — it already has a real date-bound hook (Oct. 4) that's getting diluted inside a generic deals wall.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 71 tracking/click-redirect links present (click.chp.kohls.com, click.s.kohls.com) — skipped in HTTP probe per QA, so live destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/861d22a8-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render-tracking pixels.

- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, immediately after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` open-tracking pixel. Not spec HTML; may cause unpredictable DOM handling in strict renderers.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Final landing page destinations are unverifiable since all 71 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (redirect domains excluded from live checks).
- Product image domain (`images.am.kohls.com`) differs from tracking domains (`s.kohls.com`, `chp.kohls.com`) — consistent with Kohl's known CDN split, not an issue.

7. Recommendations
- Add empty `alt=""` to all tracking/beacon pixels (open.aspx, o.gif, demdex event, rp/*.png images) to ensure screen readers skip them.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the product image `57746756_2_2x`.
- Remove the orphaned `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
- Populate `<title>` with a non-empty value.
- Confirm via raw SMTP/header capture (outside this relay) that List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results headers are actually present — current WARNs may be relay artifacts rather than true compliance failures.
- Confirm the physical mailing address exists in the full (untruncated) footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring remediation.
