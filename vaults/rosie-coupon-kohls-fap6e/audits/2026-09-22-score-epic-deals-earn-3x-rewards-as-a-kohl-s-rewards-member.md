# Score Epic Deals + earn 3x rewards as a Kohl's Rewards member 🙌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third near-identical "EPIC DEALS" category-blitz send in two days — same purple/pink hero banner, same wall of nine-plus individual deal tiles, same coupon-code-and-barcode footer Kohl's has run on 09-21 twice already. The only real change from the 09-21 sends is the hook: instead of leading purely with percentage-off deals, this one puts "earn 3x rewards" front and center in both the subject and a dedicated green rewards banner up top.
- The tile wall itself is a strength in isolation — it's genuinely diverse (sweaters, towels, shoes, tees, fleece, panty multipacks, Sephora beauty, bedding, decor, a Ninja blender, bath towels, jeans, a mattress topper) so there's something for almost any shopper. But that breadth is also the problem: there's no hero product or single offer for the eye to land on, and a subscriber who's now seen this exact layout three times in 48 hours is going to skim past it fast. This is fatigue creep dressed up as a new angle.
- Judgement: functional but tired. The 3x rewards angle is a fine differentiator on paper, but visually it's buried below a hero banner that looks like every other Kohl's send this week.

## What's working

- The "3x rewards" green banner near the top is a genuinely new element versus the 09-21 sends, giving loyalty members a distinct reason to open beyond generic %-off.
- Tile variety is broad and well-organized by category (Men/Women/Kids nav bar up top helps orient quickly).
- Specific percentages and starting prices are visible on nearly every tile (25% off shoes, 60% off bedding, 40% off decor, etc.) — no vague "shop now" ambiguity.
- The Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale callout mid-email adds a genuine secondary hook not present in prior sends.

## What's weak

- Visual layout is the same repeating tile-grid template as 09-21's two EPIC DEALS sends — a subscriber will recognize this instantly and disengage.
- No single hero offer or hero product — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first; it's a wall of nine-plus roughly equal-weight tiles.
- The rewards banner ("3x rewards," "Maximum rewards. Minimum effort.") appears twice in the email with slightly different framing, which reads as redundant rather than reinforcing.
- Cadence problem: this is at least the fourth "EPIC DEALS"-branded send in under a week, which risks training subscribers to tune the subject line out entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with ONE hero deal (pick the strongest single-category offer, e.g. 60% off bedding or the Ninja blender) above the fold, then follow with the tile grid — give the eye a landing spot before asking it to scan nine items.
- 2. Consolidate the two rewards callouts into one clear placement instead of repeating the 3x-rewards message twice in different visual treatments.
- 3. Break the "EPIC DEALS" branding cadence — three-plus sends with the same name/layout in under a week erodes open rates; rotate creative even if the underlying coupon structure stays similar.
- 4. Move the Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale higher — it's the one genuinely distinct hook in this send and it's buried two-thirds down.

- 71 tracking/redirect links (click.s.kohls.com, click.chp.kohls.com) were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe — these are expected redirect-wrapper domains, not necessarily broken, but destination resolution is unverified.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels present and firing to distinct vendors: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (CoherentPath open), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` (open tracking), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx...` (secondary open beacon), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=...` (Adobe Audience Manager). No issues with presence; flagging only because they're the source of the alt-text warnings below.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- All flagged images (11 total) are 1x1 tracking pixels or `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-tracking beacons missing `alt` attributes, per QA output — e.g. `src="https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196"`, `src="https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?..."`. Low impact since these are non-visual pixels, but one flagged image, `57746756_2_2x` (`https://images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/57746756_2_2x?scl=1`), is a real product image missing alt text — this does affect screen-reader users and should be fixed.

- Stray `</custom>` closing tag appears after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel `<div>` with no matching opening tag — invalid HTML, though unlikely to cause rendering issues in mail clients.

- No `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters observed on visible links in the truncated excerpt; unable to confirm landing-page alignment without the full link list and resolved destinations.

7. **Recommendations**
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post presence at the raw SMTP header level (not just rendered HTML) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance as of their 2024 policy.
- Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the (untruncated) footer; add one if missing — CAN-SPAM violation risk otherwise.
