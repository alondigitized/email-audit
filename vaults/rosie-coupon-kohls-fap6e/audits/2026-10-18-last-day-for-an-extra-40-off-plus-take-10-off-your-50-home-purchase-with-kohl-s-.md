# LAST DAY for an extra 40% off 😍 Plus, take $10 off your $50+ home purchase with Kohl's Rewards.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the final-day version of the Kohl's coupon send. The shell is the same one from Oct 10 through the "40% off + Kohl's Cash is worth a look 👀" send on Oct 17. The hero is the "You get an extra 40% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18." block, with a red stacked "ENDS TONIGHT" and a Shop Now button. The deadline is credible here because it really is the last day. It also adds a second offer, $10 off $50+ in home, which is why the subject carries two claims.
- The hierarchy works: the 40% number is the biggest thing on the page. Below it the email turns into a long scroll. The Kohl's Cash strip and four red nav tiles come first, then a Halloween/home block, then a fall block, then a product grid, then coupon barcodes. Two of the section headlines are visibly clipped, so the middle looks broken. For a subscriber who has had this email nearly daily for a week, the only real news is "tonight."
- Verdict: competent, urgent and very familiar. The send earns its open from the deadline alone. The render glitches and the stack of competing offers hold back the click.

## What's working

- The "extra 40% off" hero is the largest element, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" in the hero matches "LAST DAY" in the subject, and the Oct. 18 date is stated in the hero and again at the coupon.
- The coupon code and barcode sit at the bottom, with the $10-off home code and PIN beside it. A shopper can redeem straight from the email.
- The product grid shows concrete prices: the Ninja SLUSH at $299.99 (was $349.99), towels at $2.99–9.99, and jeans at $29.99.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" closer reduces purchase anxiety.

## What's weak

- Two headlines are clipped. The Halloween section's text reads "sons to decorate ..." and the next one reads "Plus, two more rea", so the copy is cut off mid-word.
- The email carries too many offers at once: 40% coupon, $10 off $50 home, up to 40% Halloween, Kohl's Cash, and Sephora.
- The hero "Shop Now" and the section buttons are all generic, with no verb-plus-category copy.
- This is at least the seventh near-identical Kohl's coupon email in a week (see Oct 14, Oct 15 and Oct 16). The nav tiles (Home/Women/Men/Kids) and the Kohl's Cash strip push the actual products far down.
- Preheader text is "Shop. Save. Earn Kohl's Cash. Rewards" plus the view-in-browser link. That is generic and wastes the best inbox real estate after the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped headlines so "Plus, two more reasons to decorate…" renders fully. These bugs sit in the middle of the scroll, where trust drops.
- 2. Lead with one offer. Make the 40% coupon the only hero, and move the $10-off-home offer into a secondary strip below the hero rather than the subject line.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop home deals" and "Get 40% off fall styles."
- 4. Cut the nav tiles or move them under the first product row, so products appear earlier.
- 5. Shorten the subject to roughly 50 characters and drop the second sentence. Use the preheader for the $10 home offer instead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: extra 40% off at Kohl's 😍`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: 40% off + $10 off home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use your coupon by midnight, plus $10 off $50+ in home with Kohl's Rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall styles, Halloween decor and Kohl's Cash all wrap up tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Kohl's email with an opaque ESP click-redirect layer (68 tracked links) and four 1×1 tracking pixels. The visible source has several HTML validity and accessibility defects: stray markup, content images without alt text, and a viewport that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed because the headers weren't captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links:** All 68 links go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects, so the QA probe skipped them. Destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Redundant open trackers:** There are four 1×1 beacons before any content.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/38082607-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024103_20261018`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`, a third-party Adobe Audience Manager call
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` has a `</custom>` tag with no opener, which is invalid markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** Seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`) have no alt attribute. So does `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`. With images blocked or on a screen reader, these are unlabeled.
- **Pixels missing alt:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/38082607-...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt`. Add `alt=""`.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That one is fine.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty head elements:** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will force-close the head at that point, and the microdata is meaningless in email clients.
- **Missing table roles:** The preheader table and the tracking-pixel tables lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the available source. The truncated HTML can't rule them out further down. The subscriber ID (`mi_u=604230016`) is in pixel URLs, as expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are not found, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this isn't a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` was not checked directly.
- **Footer:** The footer, physical address and visible unsubscribe link are beyond the truncation point and can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible. Links use opaque `qs=` tokens, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- **Campaign IDs:** `mi_ecmp=1024103_20261018` and `email_name=261018_DG_Email_Myster...` are consistent with the 2026-10-18 send date.
- **Landing pages:** Not verified, because the redirects were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the eight content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels that lack it.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` (or drop it), and fill in `<title>`. Remove or populate the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the redundant open pixels where possible.
5. Re-run QA with full raw headers to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links to verify final URLs, UTMs and landing-page status. Also audit the untruncated footer for the address and unsubscribe link.
