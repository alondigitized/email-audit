# Up to 70% off ULTIMATE clearance 😉 Plus, an extra 20% off.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's is stacking two campaigns into one send here — a "Semi-Annual" intimates event (Jockey, Fruit of the Loom, Vanity Fair, Warners) up top, then the "Ultimate Clearance Event" (up to 70% off) further down. The subject leads with clearance, but the actual hero real estate is the extra-20%-off coupon banner and the semi-annual bras section — a subscriber scanning top-to-bottom would be three screens deep before hitting the thing the subject promised.
- This is the same visual system logged across the 7/30–8/10 run (rewards status bar, red coupon banner, green Kohl's Cash strip, nav buttons) and it's technically clean — no clipped starbursts, no broken art, everything aligned. But the subject/hero mismatch is a repeat of the 8/10 "Fall home finds" send where subject copy and hero content diverged. This one's less severe (both promos are legitimately in the email) but it still buries the lede.
- Solid execution, muddled prioritization. A subscriber opening for "70% off clearance" scrolls past four bra product tiles before finding it.

## What's working

- Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member," 5% earn rate) pinned above the fold reinforces loyalty membership immediately.
- The extra-20%-off coupon is bold, legible, and has a clear deadline ("Ends Aug. 16").
- Clearance section (further down) is genuinely strong: 70% off starburst, four concrete priced products (Ninja Slushi $349.99, towels $2.09-9.99, mattress topper $137.99-209.99, jeans $24.99-26.00) with visible strikethrough pricing on two items.
- No render bugs — clean alignment on nav buttons, product tiles, and images throughout.
- Barcode + coupon code (GET20, PIN 3573) reprinted near the bottom for easy in-store redemption.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Ultimate clearance," but the email opens with Semi-Annual bra/intimates content — clearance doesn't appear until roughly 60% down the page.
- Two nearly identical nav bars (Home/Women/Kids/Men) appear twice, once for each campaign — redundant and adds scroll without adding value.
- The intimates section, while clean, is generic stock-photo lingerie merchandising with no persona-specific signal — doesn't match a shopper looking for home/clearance deals.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization claim over the clearance grid is unearned — a drink maker, bath towel, mattress topper, and jeans is a generic assortment, not curated for an individual.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with clearance, since that's what the subject and preview promise — move the "Ultimate Clearance Event" banner and at least two clearance products above the Semi-Annual intimates block, or split this into two separate sends (bras vs. clearance) to avoid diluting either message.
- 2. Cut one of the duplicate nav bars — one Home/Women/Men/Kids strip near the top is enough.
- 3. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with honest merchandising language ("Today's best clearance deals") since the product mix clearly isn't personalized.
- 4. Add urgency to the clearance section itself — only the 20%-off coupon has a deadline; the 70%-off clearance has none.
