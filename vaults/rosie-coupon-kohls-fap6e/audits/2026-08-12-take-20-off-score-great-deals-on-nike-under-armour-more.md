# Take 20% off | Score great deals on Nike, Under Armour & more 🙌
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's production template that's been running daily since 7/30 — Rewards status bar, red 20% off coupon banner, green Kohl's Cash strip, nav buttons, then category creative below. Today the subject promises "great deals on Nike, Under Armour & more," and the render actually delivers on that for once — an Under Armour "Up to 40% off" hero with a real athletic model, followed by a tight product grid showing Under Armour, Nike, Tek Gear, and FLX gear. That's tighter subject-to-hero alignment than the 8/10 "Fall home finds" send that mismatched its own subject.
- Where it falls apart is scroll depth: after the activewear block, the email pivots hard into an unrelated "already thinking about fall" section, then a "Picked with you in mind" grid selling a slushi maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and jeans — none of which have anything to do with the subject line's Nike/Under Armour promise. It's a bait-and-switch structure even though the top of the email is honest.
- Solid open bait, decent top-of-funnel execution, but the email overpromises on focus and then wanders — same structural problem this brand keeps having, just with the mismatch pushed lower in the scroll instead of in the hero.

## What's working

- Subject-to-hero alignment is real this time: Under Armour "Up to 40% off Activewear and shoes" hero matches "Nike, Under Armour & more" in the subject.
- Clean, well-lit product photography — Nike sneakers, Under Armour hoodie, FLX and Tek Gear apparel all render sharply with no broken images.
- Clear, unambiguous "Shop Active Sale" CTA button directly under the activewear offer.
- No layout bugs, overlapping text, or lorem ipsum anywhere in the render — technically clean top to bottom.
- Rewards status and coupon code (GET20) are visible and legible above the fold, consistent with brand pattern.

## What's weak

- The activewear focus promised in the subject dies after one section — the rest of the email sells home goods, beauty, jeans, a mattress topper, and a slushi maker with zero connection to Nike/Under Armour.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" reads as generic personalization copy sitting over a random assortment (slushi maker, bath towel, mattress topper, jeans) — it doesn't feel picked for anyone.
- Extremely long, low-density scroll — by the time a reader hits the barcode/coupon repeat near the bottom, the original subject-line offer is long forgotten.
- Same escalating-then-abandoning-the-hook problem seen in the 8/10 and 8/9 sends: strong open bait, weak follow-through past the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the activewear section, or push all non-apparel merchandising (home, beauty, mattress, slushi maker) into a separate send — a subject that says "Nike, Under Armour & more" should stay in-category for at least 2-3 scrolls, not one.
- 2. Replace the generic "Picked with you in mind" module with actual related items (more sneakers, more activewear, matching accessories) if it stays in this email at all.
- 3. Move the 20% off coupon code/barcode closer to the activewear hero instead of repeating it at the very bottom after readers have scrolled past six unrelated categories.
- 4. Tighten "already thinking about fall" — it's a second campaign shoehorned into an activewear-branded send; give it its own email like the 8/10 "Fall home finds" one already sort of tried to.
