# Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest send in the recent Cyber Week run, because it finally has a single dominant offer. The black sequin hero reads "Black Friday Starts Online Now: 50% OFF" in huge type, with one SHOP NOW button and a product collage under it. The eye lands on the number first. That beats the stacked "30% OFF" brand tiles from 11/24 to 11/26, which competed with each other.
- Everything below the hero dilutes it. The fine print says the 50% applies to "select gifts" and runs 11/27–11/29, but it is "in store and online" in the body, which contradicts the "Online Now" headline. Below the hero sit a "Want more Cyber Week deals?" product grid, a "You Might Also Like" row, a 30% tools banner, a store locator, a gift guide and a long utility footer. The top strip is a separate 4X points offer with code CYBERSCENT, so three different offers compete in the first screen. The 50% claim is also never tied to a named product or brand.
- Judgment: a good hero attached to a very long, cluttered email. The headline is better than the body.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF hero is huge, high contrast, and unmistakable, with a clear SHOP NOW button directly under it.
- The product collage (palettes, perfume, hair tools, lip products) signals gift sets and variety.
- The product tiles carry specific prices, such as Dr. Jart $52 → $36.40 and MAC Lip Liner $25 → $17.50, plus "Only at Sephora" tags.
- It continues the purple/black sequin look from the earlier Cyber Week sends, so the brand voice is consistent.
- The in-store barcode and code block make the offer usable in stores.

## What's weak

- The hero says "Online Now," but the subcopy says "In store and online starting 11/27 through 11/29," so the channel and timing are muddled.
- The 50% applies only to "select gifts," and the footnote says "Available on in-stock merchandise only." No specific eligible products are shown in the hero, so the discount looks fuzzy.
- The email stacks too many other offers: the 4X points banner, the 30% tools banner, the credit card offer and the app download. The 4X points banner above the hero competes with the main offer.
- The "Want more Cyber Week deals?" and "You Might Also Like" grids are generic, and none of the shown prices come close to 50% off.
- The store-locator map and hours block, the gift-guide pills and the dense footer make the email very long for a single-offer send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the hero down to one product grid of items that are actually 50% off. Drop the store map, the second grid and the 30% tools banner.
- 2. Fix the date and channel language so the hero, subcopy and subject all say the same thing, for example "Online now, in store 11/27–11/29."
- 3. Move the 4X points strip out of the top slot, or reserve it for loyalty members in a separate send.
- 4. Name the eligible gift categories (fragrance sets, palettes, hair tools) in the hero so "select gifts" isn't vague.
- 5. Shorten the subject. The current one has two emojis and is about 55 characters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off gifts starts now at Sephora 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: 50% off select gift sets, 3 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fragrance, palettes & hair tools, online now through 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn 4X points on fragrance gift sets with code CYBERSCENT.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** three images have none.
  - The two tracking pixels (`o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/…` image) are non-content. The `o.gif` pixel already has `aria-hidden="true"`, and the other pixel is hidden with `display:none`. An empty `alt=""` is still the clean fix.
  - `images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../20261126_Cyber_Week…` is a content image. Without alt text, image-blocking clients will show nothing for it.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper table uses `min-width:700px; width:700px`. This can force horizontal scrolling in clients that ignore or strip the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules (Gmail non-GANGA accounts, for example).
- **Malformed CSS:** an `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't belong inside CSS, and some parsers will drop the following rules (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and later).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** the block has `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type, which aren't valid markup. Most clients ignore it, but Gmail may try to parse it.

## 7. Recommendations
