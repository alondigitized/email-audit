# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third "50% off select beauty" send in four days (following 8/29's "Select brands are 50% off 🤑" and 8/28's "50% off select beauty starts today 💋"), and the fatigue shows — the offer itself is now buried under a wall of unrelated content. The top third is entirely consumed by a Rouge/loyalty banner and a "The Next Big Thing" new-arrivals showcase before a long scroll of individual product cards (L'Occitane, L'Oréal Professionnel, Touchland, Skylar, Jung Saem Mool) that have nothing to do with the subject line's 50% off hook.
- The actual 50% off module doesn't appear until roughly two-thirds down the email — a pink circular badge naming Patrick Starrr, Living Proof, and Clinique with a "TODAY ONLY" callout. By the time a scroller gets there, they've already passed a dozen full-price product blocks that dilute the urgency the subject line promised. Compared to 8/28's version, which led with "GET 50% OFF" as the hero, this is a structural regression — the subject over-promises what the layout delivers.
- Verdict: the offer is real and time-bound, but the email buries its own lede. This reads like a new-arrivals newsletter with a deals badge stapled in halfway down, not a deals email.

## What's working

- The 50% off module itself is concrete — named brands (Patrick Starrr, Living Proof, Clinique), a visible product shot, and a "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag.
- "New" and "Only at Sephora" and "Clean at Sephora" badges are used consistently and are easy to scan on individual product cards.
- No render bugs — images, text, and grid layout all display cleanly with no overlaps or broken assets.
- The loyalty banner at top ($5 off at 250 points, 4X points) is relevant if the recipient is a Beauty Insider member and reinforces personalization ("You have 6 points").

## What's weak

- The 50% off offer — the entire premise of the subject line — doesn't appear until well past the midpoint of the email, after a "New" arrivals section and six unrelated product cards.
- Visual hierarchy is wrong for the subject line: "THE NEXT BIG THING" is the actual hero, not the discount.
- This is the third near-identical "50% off select beauty" email in four days, and each has featured different named brands with no explanation of why the list keeps changing — reads as recycled scaffolding, not a fresh event.
- The subject's "Take a closer look 👀" hook doesn't pay off anywhere in the visible copy — no product close-up, comparison, or "look" moment ties back to it (the swipe/before-after foundation module is the closest fit but it's unrelated to the 50% off deal and near the bottom).

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 50% off module to the hero position, above "The Next Big Thing" — the subject line sold the discount, so the discount should be the first thing in the fold.
- 2. Cut or shrink the six-card product dump (L'Occitane through Jung Saem Mool) — none of it supports the subject line and it pushes the actual offer past several scrolls.
- 3. Either drop the "👀 Take a closer look" hook or actually build a visual around it (a swipe/zoom product shot tied to the discounted items) — right now it's an unearned curiosity gap.
- 4. Vary the named-brand rotation language across this week's sends ("today's deal," "new drop," etc.) so three near-identical subject/offer emails in four days don't read as the same email restyled.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off starts now: Clinique, Living Proof & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 50% off these 3 beauty picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patrick Starrr, Living Proof, Clinique — half off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 50% off deals are live. Shop before they're gone.`
