# 40% off is here today, gone tomorrow ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Same 40% flat coupon that's now run three straight days (09-18 segmented stack → 09-19 flat 40% → 09-20 "here today, gone tomorrow"), and this send doesn't earn its urgency framing — the hero banner still just says "Ends Sept. 20" with no countdown clock, no "last chance" badge, nothing that visually escalates from yesterday's identical "40% off + Kohl's Cash" send. It's the longest, most sprawling layout in the streak yet: hero coupon, category nav, "Free Spirit Boho" trend block, kids/men lifestyle block, a second "just one thing" nav grid, a third product grid (Sephora, shoes, home), then four hero SKUs with real prices, then a coupon reminder block, then the footer. That's six distinct content zones before the reader hits an actual priced product.
- The one thing genuinely new here is the barcode + coupon code (CM4H0NEK6NL4R) rendered directly in the body — that's useful and something prior sends in this streak haven't shown. But it's buried near the bottom, after four unrelated merchandising blocks, which undercuts the "here today, gone tomorrow" urgency the subject promises.
- Judgement: a functional but fatigued send. The creative escalation (subject line urgency) isn't matched by layout urgency, and the sheer length works against a reader who's seen this exact coupon twice already this week.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible — big red "40% off" is the first thing the eye hits, no clutter around it.
- Real, specific priced SKUs appear (Ninja Slushi $349.99, Serta mattress topper $91.99–139.99, Gloria Vanderbilt jeans $19.99–26.00) with original-price strikethroughs, which supports genuine price-comparison shopping.
- Coupon code and barcode are shown directly in the email body — a subscriber can shop without hunting through "Wallet."
- Category nav (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeats at multiple scroll depths, useful for a long email.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "gone tomorrow" urgency but the hero and coupon blocks still just say static "Ends Sept. 20" — no clock, no red flag, nothing visually escalated versus yesterday's send.
- Six separate content zones before you reach real priced products is a lot of scroll for a promo email; the "Free Spirit Boho" trend block and the second nav grid are redundant with each other.
- This is the third day running the same 40% number — a subscriber who opened 09-19's "Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash" will recognize this as a re-skin, not new news.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization copy sits over a generic bestseller grid (slushi maker, bath towels, jeans, mattress topper) with no visible personalization signal — it reads as a stock phrase, not real targeting.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add real urgency to the hero, not just the subject.** A countdown or "Final Day" badge on the 40% banner itself would make the subject line's promise credible instead of cosmetic.
- 2. **Cut the email in half.** Merge the "Free Spirit Boho" trend block and the second Home/Women/Men/Kids nav grid — they're doing the same job twice.
- 3. **Move the coupon code + barcode up**, right under the hero, since that's the one truly actionable/new element and it's currently the second-to-last thing before the footer.
- 4. **Since this is day three of 40%, use the tier escalation itself as urgency** ("last day at 40% — tomorrow it drops") rather than a generic deadline restate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 40% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab your coupon code before it resets tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kohl's Cash included — but only through today.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Standard ESP-generated (Coherent Path/CHEETAH platform) promotional email with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and widespread missing alt text (43% pass rate).

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- Redirect chain uses `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`, and `mi.kohls.com` domains; QA skipped HTTP probing on 65 tracking/redirect links (expected for click-wrapped URLs, not itself a defect).
- Multiple parallel tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e60641f3-...` (1x1), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?R6XFGBTVIIIEDK3MMZVZDVAVI4.60264&d=60264&bmt=0` (second open pixel), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager beacon) — four separate open/tracking beacons is redundant but not broken.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the `click.s.kohls.com` open-pixel `<div>`. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- CTA link uses long opaque query-string token (`?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMDV9...`) typical of ESP click-tracking; cannot verify destination from truncated source.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Nine images confirmed missing `alt` attributes per QA (tracking pixels are exempt/expected, but content images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` appear to be creative/promo images based on filenames under `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`, not just beacons — these need alt text for screen-reader users).

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available markup.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in QA scan — required by CAN-SPAM/Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender rules; may be a relay-capture artifact rather than a true absence, but flag for verification.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe non-compliant with 2024+ Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements if genuinely absent.

- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown; cannot confirm authentication via this scan.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**

- CTA destination URL is opaque (click-tracked `qs=` token); cannot verify it resolves to a live, matching landing page from the truncated source.

7. **Recommendations**
- Remove stray `</custom>` closing tag in the open-pixel block.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` creative images.
