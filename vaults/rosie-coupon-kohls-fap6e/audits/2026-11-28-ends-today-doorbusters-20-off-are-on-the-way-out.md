- The product tiles use small images and tiny type, and most "Shop Now" links are text rather than buttons.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based layout with four open-tracking pixels and 104 click-redirect links. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and link destinations could not be inspected. The QA run passed 38% of checks, with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** The probe skipped all 104 links because they are tracking redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination status and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Four open-tracking pixels in one message:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/286ddf54-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026908_20261128`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party)

  Several redundant pixels add request overhead and failure points. Where `referrer: no-referrer` and image blocking apply, they may under-count opens.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 18,468 of 24,318 characters (76%) of the plain-text part are URLs, above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and a poor plain-text reading experience.
- **Malformed markup:** The open.aspx pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opening tag. The `<head>` also contains `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` with an empty href.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233) and the product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. If the hero or offer copy is baked into these images, it is invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** Most of the "missing alt" warnings are tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `286ddf54-…`). Add `alt=""` to them and `aria-hidden="true"` where it is missing. The first pixel sits directly in `<body>` and is not hidden.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid head content:** The `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` inside `<head>` is invalid. Parsers will relocate it, and email clients ignore microdata anyway.
- **Missing table roles:** The tracking tables, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack `role="presentation"`, unlike the main layout tables.
