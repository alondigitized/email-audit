# Your 40% off savings are here today, gone tomorrow ...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth 40%-off email in roughly 36 hours — following three sends on July 18–19 with the same discount — and it shows. The subject's urgency hook ("here today, gone tomorrow") is a transparent rehash of yesterday's "ends today" language, which strips it of any credibility before the inbox is even opened. A subscriber who has been ignoring this offer for two days is not going to act because the phrasing shifted slightly.
- Inside the email, the creative is cleaner than the cadence deserves. The linen hero has genuinely good copywriting ("Light and breezy linen (at a tight and breezy cost)" is a double-meaning win), the photography is bright and seasonal, and concrete price points ($34.99, $29.99) are visible without hunting. The multi-category layout — women's, men's, shoes, home — hedges against any single reader, but also means no one feels like this was sent specifically for them.
- The 40% coupon itself is buried near the bottom behind three product sections and a home-goods grid. For an email whose entire premise is the coupon, that's a structural inversion. The "DEAL A" banner at the very top appears clipped or cut off, which introduces a render-quality question mark right at the fold. Bottom line: competent execution on a premise that is dead from cadence exhaustion.

## What's working

- **"Light and breezy linen (at a tight and breezy cost)"** — clever wordplay that earns a second read; rare for a Kohl's subject block.
- **Concrete price points are front-and-center** — $34.99 and $29.99 appear directly in the product sections, reducing the cognitive gap between "offer" and "what it costs me."
- **Summer seasonal fit** — linen, sandals, patio/outdoor toys all map cleanly to late July; the creative is timely.
- **Kohl's Cash callout is present** — loyalty stack (discount + earn) is visible for members, which adds a second hook.
- **Home-goods grid is scannable** — Bedding / Bath / Kitchen / Patio / Decor as labeled tiles gives category shoppers a fast visual jump-off.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue kills the urgency claim** — "today, gone tomorrow" is hollow when the identical 40% offer arrived yesterday and the day before; the subscriber has already learned this deadline is fake.
- **"DEAL A" banner at the top appears clipped** — the large display text reads as cut off, which damages trust at the literal first impression.
- **40% coupon is buried** — the primary reason anyone opens this email doesn't appear until well past the fold, after three product modules and a home grid; it should lead.
- **"No room required" tagline for home goods is confusing** — the phrase reads as "you don't need a room," which is the opposite of the intended meaning; it should be "any room, on us" or similar.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs throughout** — every section uses the same generic call to action; none gives a reason to click this section over any other.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the coupon, not the product editorial.** Hero block should be the 40% off badge + barcode / wallet link + a single product category teaser. The current structure makes you scroll past three shopping modules to find the thing the subject promised.
- 2. **Enforce a send cadence rule.** Four 40%-off emails in 36 hours is the root problem. One daily send with the coupon; suppress repeat audiences who've already opened or clicked.
- 3. **Fix or remove the "DEAL A" banner.** It's the first thing the eye hits and it looks broken. Either show the full text or replace it with the offer mechanic.
- 4. **Rewrite the home-section tagline.** "All you need to upgrade your home (No room Required)" → "Every room. 40% off." — specific, parallel, no ambiguity.
- 5. **Make CTAs category-specific.** "Shop Linen" / "Shop Men's Tops" / "Shop Sandals" over the generic "Shop Now" so each tap feels intentional.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off linen, tops & more — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Linen from $21 after your 40% coupon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your coupon is in the Wallet — tap before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Linen, sandals, and home — all 40% off through tonight.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**13 content images missing `alt` text** — all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render images lack alt attributes. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Gmail app on mobile data-saver) leaves these slots blank with no fallback text.

- `611d2b1debdc5ae0.png` / `b0aad5bed92b660a.png` — contain `left_promo_code=LPJ9Y27GJLXBX` (likely promo code tile)
- `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` suffix on `916d6fa08d185233.png` — appears to be a dynamic expiry image with empty date parameter (see also §4)

**Three tracking pixels missing `alt=""`** — `42fdc230`, `o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no alt attribute at all. Should be `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` for screen reader hygiene (two of three lack `aria-hidden`).

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` prevents screen readers from announcing the email window title.

**`user-scalable=0`** in viewport meta — disables pinch-to-zoom, an accessibility regression for low-vision users; WCAG 1.4.4 failure.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
