# LAST DAY to save 20% + earn Kohl's Cash ... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seventh "20% off + Kohl's Cash" email since July 1 — a cadence so relentless it has become inbox wallpaper. What saves this send is the TEK GEAR activewear hero: instead of opening on the usual department-store grid, this email leads with a category and a concrete price point ($7.99), which is the first genuine creative differentiation in the run. The clearance block beneath — "new markdowns taken," up to 70% off — adds a second value layer and gives scrollers a reason to stay.
- The barcode/coupon treatment (code GOSHOP20, ends July 6) is specific and trust-building. Layout renders cleanly with no visible bugs. These are real strengths.
- The problem is the subject line: ALL CAPS "LAST DAY" combined with the trailing "... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️" reads like a spam-filter audition, not a credible deadline. After six near-identical sends in five days, subscribers have been conditioned to treat these as interchangeable blasts — so the "last day" claim lands skeptically even when it's true. The CTAs throughout are a universal "Shop Now," squandering the category specificity the hero section earns.

## What's working

- TEK GEAR hero with entry price ($7.99) gives the email a category anchor the prior six sends completely lacked.
- Clearance module callout — "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN, Save up to 70%" — adds a second value driver and creates genuine scroll incentive.
- Coupon code (GOSHOP20) and end date (July 6) are explicitly visible; the deadline is specific, not vague.
- Kohl's Cash earn rate is surfaced in the footer, reinforcing the loyalty stack.
- Clean render — no broken images, overlapping text, or template artifacts.

## What's weak

- ALL CAPS "LAST DAY" in the subject is the single loudest spam-adjacent signal available; sentence-case "Last day" carries identical urgency without the shout.
- Seven near-identical "20% off + Kohl's Cash" emails in five days have exhausted subscriber trust in urgency claims from this brand.
- Every CTA across three distinct modules reads "Shop Now" — zero specificity and zero differentiation between activewear, lifestyle, and clearance.
- No social proof anywhere in the email — no star ratings, no bestseller tag, no review count on the TEK GEAR hero.
- The "... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️" subject tail feels like a copy tic rather than voice; it adds length without adding meaning.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject line case.** Drop ALL CAPS immediately — swap to sentence case and sharpen the offer. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Make every CTA module-specific.** "Shop Now" → "Shop TEK GEAR," "Shop Clearance," "Shop Women's" — one line of copy each, zero design changes required.
- 3. **Add a proof point to the TEK GEAR hero.** Even "top-rated" or a star-rating badge would give the section credibility the price anchor alone can't fully carry.
- 4. **Strengthen the deadline specificity.** "Ends midnight July 6" is more credible than "LAST DAY" — add the time if possible.
- 5. **Give the clearance block a scale signal.** "200+ new markdowns" or "3,000+ styles on sale" contextualizes the 70% headline and makes it feel curated rather than a catch-all.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off + Kohl's Cash — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `TEK GEAR from $7.99 • 20% off ends July 6`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns added. Code GOSHOP20 expires at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 70% off clearance + extra 20% off. Today only — no extensions.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "LAST DAY 20% + Kohl's Cash" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered HTML 4.01 Transitional build with no merge token or rendering blockers. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance signals (unsubscribe headers, physical address) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — all links route through `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce MC) and `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath). No dead-link confirmation possible; monitor ESP click-tracking dashboard for 4xx redirects post-send.

**Three distinct tracking pixel domains in use simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — Salesforce MC open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?...` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

Three separate open-pixel vendors firing on render is unusual and indicates a multi-ESP / DMP stack with no consolidation. Each pixel is a redundant round-trip and a potential cross-device fingerprinting vector. Not a deliverability blocker, but worth confirming all are intentional.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag present in the pixel block (`...alt=""></custom>`). This is invalid HTML and has no matching open tag — browsers will silently ignore it, but strict parsers and some ESPs may flag it.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>`. Several email clients (Outlook on Windows, Apple Mail preview) surface the title tag in threading or accessibility trees. An empty title is a minor screen-reader gap.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile and fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4.

**Alt text missing on 9 flagged images**, including content images (not just tracking pixels):
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png` — likely promotional/product images based on `email_name=260705_DG_Email_Sunday` param context. If images are blocked, these render as empty boxes with no text fallback.
- The three tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, UUID pixel) are missing `alt=""` — they should carry an explicit empty alt to suppress screen-reader announcement.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype** — not a bug, this is standard defensive email practice for Outlook compatibility.

**Google Fonts loaded three times** (two `<link>` tags + one `@import` block for each family). This is redundant but unlikely to cause rendering failures.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA relay did not capture this header. Major inbox providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail) require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) for one-click unsubscribe on bulk mail. Missing RFC 8058 support will suppress the native unsubscribe UI in Gmail for senders above the 5k/day threshold, directly impacting complaint rate.

**No physical mailing address detected in HTML source** — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. This may be present in the truncated portion not provided; confirm before flagging as a live violation.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured by AgentMail relay** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unverifiable from the audit data. Kohl's sends from `s.kohls.com` (Salesforce subdomain); DKIM alignment to `kohls.com` should be confirmed via a live header inspection.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present** — correct practice for suppressing indexing and referrer leakage on email-viewed-in-browser versions.
