# Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's dedicated Hair Daily Deals send, and for once subject and hero are aligned — "Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care" leads straight into "Hair Daily Deals / Get up to 50% OFF Hair Faves" with a same-day-delivery kicker underneath. It's a category-specific promo with a real calendar-of-deals structure (a 7-day markdown grid), which is a step up from the vague "50% off select beauty" bait-and-switch this brand ran on 9/18. The Briogeo call-outs with actual strike-through pricing ($42→$22.40, $42→$29.40, $39→$27.30) are the strongest, most concrete part of the email.
- Where it falls apart is length and focus: this is a genuinely long scroll (hair deals → 7-day markdown calendar → 20% off value-size hair care → holiday alerts → perfume → value sets → loyalty tiers → footer), and by the time you hit the second "20% off" section the email has already made its point twice. For a hair-specific send, the perfume and "Value Sets" blocks are pure filler that dilutes the category focus the subject promised.
- Solid, on-brand, better-than-average discipline for this brand's cadence — but overstuffed and would benefit from being cut by a third.

## What's working

- Subject, preheader, and hero all agree on the same offer (hair care, up to 50% off) — no bait-and-switch, unlike the 9/18 send.
- Concrete strike-through pricing on three named Briogeo SKUs gives the offer real teeth instead of vague "% off select" language.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge plus a 7-day markdown calendar creates credible, specific urgency without feeling gimmicky.
- Second hair-care block (Olaplex, K18, Ouai, Amika) adds recognizable named brands with visible dollar values, reinforcing category relevance.
- Clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- The email runs on well past the hair-care premise — perfume, "Value Sets," and holiday text-alert sign-up have nothing to do with hair and dilute the subject's promise.
- Two separate "20% off" / "50% off" hair sections back to back is redundant; it should be one consolidated hair module.
- The 7-day deals calendar is visually dense and small — hard to scan quickly, especially on mobile, and undercuts its own urgency by listing a full week of deals under a "TODAY ONLY" banner.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" sits right under the pricing, which slightly undercuts the "run don't walk" urgency copy above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the hair-care sections — drop perfume, value sets, and holiday alerts entirely for a hair-specific send; a category email should stay in category.
- 2. Merge the two hair markdown blocks into one scannable module instead of two separate "deals" sections with overlapping logic (percent-off + dollar strike-through).
- 3. Simplify the 7-day calendar into a single "This Week's Hair Deals" strip with day + one headline product, not a dense grid — legibility on mobile is a real risk here.
- 4. Move "Ends 10/7 at 1pm PT" up next to the hero badge instead of only in body copy, so urgency is visible at a glance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: up to 50% off hair + free same-day delivery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hair Daily Deals are live — save up to 50%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Briogeo, Olaplex, K18 & more — new markdowns every day this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free same-day delivery with code DELIVERED`
