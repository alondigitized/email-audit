## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competently produced Kohl's send — no clipped starbursts, no garbled art, in line with the run of solid renders logged since July 30. The hero is unambiguous: a red-and-gray split-diagonal banner with a Tek Gear model holding a basketball, "UP TO 40% OFF Activewear and shoes," and a legible black "Shop Active Sale" button. Product strip crops above and below (Nike, adidas, Tek Gear apparel and shoes) reinforce the Active Sale theme without overcrowding it.
- The problem is message discipline: right after the Active Sale hero, the email pivots hard into a second, unrelated "ULTIMATE CLEARANCE EVENT — Save up to 70%" banner with its own CTA, which competes directly with the subject line's promise and dilutes the single offer this send was supposed to be about. That's the same split-focus issue flagged in the 2026-08-03 clearance send (7.00/10) — Kohl's is running two campaigns in one email again, just with the order flipped.
- Solid render, muddled offer hierarchy. This lands mid-pack — better executed than 8-03's clearance send, but a step behind the tightly-focused Active Sale/Denim/Friends & Family sends from July 30–Aug 1 that scored 9.00.

## What's working

- Hero banner is legible and on-brand: bold "UP TO 40% OFF," clear category callout (Activewear and shoes), specific fine print (coupon exclusions) instead of vague terms.
- Primary CTA "Shop Active Sale" is a real button, high contrast, sits right under the offer.
- Product crops (shoes, adidas apparel, activewear model) visually match the subject line's Nike/adidas/Tek Gear promise.
- Rewards status bar ("Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn 5% Rewards") is personalized and visible near the top for members.
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The second "ULTIMATE CLEARANCE EVENT" banner and its "Shop Now" CTA directly compete with the Active Sale hero, splitting the email's focus into two offers with no clear primary.
- Preheader ("Plus, take up to 70% off during the ULTIMATE Clearance Event") leads with the secondary offer, not the subject's Active Sale/Nike/adidas hook — confusing sequencing before the reader even opens.
- Category nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) are generic store-wide links, not tied to the activewear theme — a missed chance to deep-link to "Shoes" or "Activewear."
- No specific product callouts (price, size, model name) under the hero — just category-level crops, so there's nothing to click toward besides "shop everything."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Clearance Event block entirely from this send, or move it below a fold-clear divider with lower visual weight — the subject promised Nike/adidas/Tek Gear, and the body should deliver one offer, one CTA path.
- 2. Fix the preheader to lead with the Active Sale offer instead of the Clearance Event, so preview text matches subject intent.
- 3. Swap the generic Home/Women/Men/Kids nav row for activewear-specific category links (Shoes, Women's Activewear, Men's Activewear) directly under the hero.
- 4. Add one product-specific callout (e.g., a featured Nike shoe with price) beneath the hero to give the CTA something concrete to land on.

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Kohl's/Cordial-platform promotional email using MSO-conditional table layout and multi-vendor tracking (Kohl's `click.chp`/`click.s`, `mi.kohls.com`, Adobe AAM `demdex.net`). Automated QA reports a 43% pass rate driven primarily by missing accessibility attributes and undetectable compliance headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 47 tracking/redirect links detected and skipped for HTTP probing (click-tracking domains `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — link destinations were not validated for reachability.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?MYDLDZHXGYDEFEA3NUTJJJQT2E.60268&d=60268&bmt=0`.
- Malformed markup around the open-tracking pixel: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- Multiple redundant tracking beacons fire in sequence: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels — expected for this ESP stack, but confirm all are intentional (7 separate 1x1 pixels/images found in QA output).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **8 images missing `alt` text**, all tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, and five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons) plus the primary click-tracking pixel. These are non-content pixels, so the fix is `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (already applied correctly on `o.gif`, but missing on the others) rather than descriptive alt text.
- Legacy `DOCTYPE HTML 4.01 Transitional` with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) — standard for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issue.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width: 500px` with appropriate `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm`/`.w-sm-*` classes — mobile responsiveness appears implemented.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — correct for transactional/marketing email, prevents indexing if the HTML leaks to a public URL.
