# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar email, and it's the second rewards-catalog send in five days. It follows the Nov 6 "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle." The hero, "November rewards are here!", sits on a dark teal banner with a single SHOP NOW button and a "New rewards are here!" badge. The subject asks "Did you know that points = rewards?" The body delivers a long catalog of point-redeemable items, so the email is on-brand and matches the subject.
- The execution is a long scroll of catalog tiles. A Summer Fridays brand spotlight comes first, with four bundles at 2,000 to 7,500 points. A "750+ Point Rewards" grid follows. It has Too Faced, Beauty of Joseon, Tower 28, Dolce&Gabbana, Sulwhasoo, Ciele and a Chris McMillan salon experience. Every tile shows a point cost and a scarcity count such as "40 coming soon," "32 available now" or "9 available now." That scarcity is the most credible urgency in the email. There is no price offer, and the hero doesn't say how many points the recipient has. Points value is the whole pitch, and it is never personalized.
- The email is clear, tidy and on theme, but it reads as a catalog dump. The hero says "scroll to see" without showing one reward. The subject is a weak "did you know" question that lacks a concrete hook. The email is acceptable for engaged members. It wouldn't win over a lapsed one.

## What's working

- The hero names the campaign ("November rewards are here!") and has one clear SHOP NOW button.
- The "Hi, Rosie" greeting at the top is a light personalization signal.
- Each tile shows its point cost and a stock count, which gives credible scarcity.
- Summer Fridays gets a brand spotlight with a clear hierarchy of bundles by points tier.
- The Holiday Gift Guide band near the footer has category pills (Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Hair) and keeps the seasonal angle.
- The rewards cover a wide price and points range, from 750 points to 10,000 for the salon experience.

## What's weak

- The hero is text and a product shot but shows no hero reward. "Scroll to see" pushes the payoff below the fold.
- The email is very long, with 12 or more tiles across two sections. There is no single hero reward to anchor on.
- It doesn't show Rosie's points balance or how close she is to any reward, so "points = rewards" is generic.
- The small-print tile copy and the footer's legal text are tiny and dense. The Rewards Bazaar terms are hard to read at inbox size.
- The tail has four stacked secondary promos (free shipping, Sephora Credit Card, "build your loves list," app download). They dilute the message after the catalog.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member's points balance and the best reward she can afford now. Example: "You have 1,200 points: pick a reward." This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put one hero reward in the banner, such as the Summer Fridays tote bundle, so the email isn't a text-only promise.
- 3. Cut the catalog to six tiles, sorted by the points tier the member qualifies for, instead of listing everything.
- 4. Put the "X available now" stock counts into the subject or preheader, since scarcity is the real urgency.
- 5. Move the credit card, app and shipping strips into one slim footer block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rosie, your points can get you Summer Fridays`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New rewards, limited stock: 750 points and up`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tote bundles and Too Faced mascara are going fast. See what your points unlock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Some rewards have fewer than 10 left. Redeem before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a fixed-width 700px table layout with Epsilon/Harmony click tracking. Two automated checks failed: an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,") and a 0-character plain-text part. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified, probably because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** 12 material links were probed with no failures reported, and 33 lower-priority links (footer, social) were skipped. The skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel is missing `alt`** (QA WARN). The element is `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; … mso-hide: all"/>`. It is hidden, so there is no real accessibility impact. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would still clear the warning.
- All visible links go through `app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a…?__dU__=…`. This is a standard redirect wrapper, and I found no defect in it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width, not fluid.** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The only responsive rule is `@media (max-width:480px)`, and it adjusts font sizes and image dimensions without fluid-izing the table. On narrow viewports this risks horizontal scroll or client auto-shrink.
- **Malformed `<style>` block.** An HTML conditional comment sits inside the `@media` block: `…}<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge {…`. CSS parsers can drop the rule that follows or the whole remainder. That would affect `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `.mobileHeaderTierBI`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner` and `.mobileDisc`. This is the most likely cause of mobile-specific rules failing.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients.
- **`<script type='application/ld+json'>` is stripped or ignored by most clients.** It also contains an empty `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard`.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Alt text on content images could not be assessed.** The source is truncated, and QA flagged only the pixel.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** QA detected a greeting with an empty name, e.g. "Hi ,". The merge field resolved to an empty string with no fallback. The matching markup is beyond the truncation point, so I can't quote it.
- I found no unresolved raw tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than proven missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, and I can't assess alignment from this data.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters.** The email is HTML-only (no `multipart/alternative` text part), which can hurt spam filtering and accessibility.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified, because the footer is outside the truncated source. QA always probes an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. Every destination is behind the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirect. The truncated source and QA data show no final URLs or UTM parameters, so I can't confirm UTM tagging or landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting:** add a fallback in the merge field (e.g. "Hi there,") or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Add a plain-text part:** send `multipart/alternative` with a meaningful text version.
3. **Verify the headers at the source:** check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw message or through a seed inbox, since the relay may be hiding them.
4. **Move the MSO conditional out of the `<style>` block**, into its own `<!--[if mso]><style>…</style><![endif]-->` block, so the later mobile rules aren't dropped.
5. **Make the layout fluid:** use `width:100%; max-width:700px` and remove `min-width:700px`.
6. **Allow zoom:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
7. **Minor cleanup:** `alt=""` on the pixel, `lang="en"` on `<html>`, `noindex`, and drop the empty JSON-LD `DiscountOffer`.
8. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the resolved link chain** to cover UTM continuity and the footer compliance elements.
