# Kohl’s Cash + 20% off = 4th of July treats 🎇
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's sixth "20% off + Kohl's Cash" email since July 1 — but it earns its send in a way yesterday's clearance companion didn't. The 4th of July theming is genuinely fresh: "Great price after great price. (Our version of a parade.)" is the best headline this campaign has produced across the entire six-email run. The patriotic imagery (American flag swimwear, star-spangled tops), the store-pickup bonus ($5 Kohl's Cash for orders $25+ picked up today), and the holiday subject line all justify the existence of this send in a way the July 2 "Psst" clone never did.
- The weakness is cadence math. By email six in 96 hours with functionally the same discount, unsubscribe pressure is real. The core "GOSHOP20 + Kohl's Cash" mechanism has been repeated so many times that even a well-dressed version of it risks being auto-skimmed before the clever copy registers. A subscriber who already has GOSHOP20 memorized has zero reason to open today unless the subject signals something genuinely new — and "4th of July treats" does just enough work there.
- Execution is clean: strong visual hierarchy, no render bugs, clear CTAs, and a multi-section layout that covers Women/Men/Kids/Home without feeling chaotic. The store pickup callout is a smart channel bridge for a holiday when you actually might want your item today. Solid send — better than its predecessors, dragged down only by accumulated cadence debt.

## What's working

- **Headline is the best in this campaign run.** "Great price after great price. (Our version of a parade.)" is on-brief, self-aware, and seasonally specific — a meaningful step up from the "Psst" and "immediately yes" subject-line gimmicks used earlier this week.
- **Store pickup CTA is genuinely useful on the 4th.** "$5 Kohl's Cash on orders $25+ when you pick up in store today" is holiday-contextual and cross-channel — someone who actually needs a party outfit today can act on this.
- **Patriotic product imagery is cohesive.** American flag swimwear, star-print tops, and red/white/blue throughout the hero section make this feel like a curated holiday push, not a generic discount blast.
- **Discount ladder is visible upfront.** 20% off + up to 40% + up to 50% off are all visible before the fold; the savings math is obvious without requiring a click.
- **Kohl's Cash integration is thorough.** Earn + in-store pickup bonus + footer loyalty callout — three reinforcing touchpoints without feeling repetitive.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is doing all the CTA heavy lifting — everywhere.** Every section uses the same generic button copy. A "Get My 4th Fit" or "Shop Patriotic Picks" on the hero would convert better than the sixth consecutive "Shop Now."
- **No hard deadline is stated in the email body.** The 4th of July implies today is the last day, but nowhere in the visible copy does it say "offer ends tonight" or "today only." The store-pickup call has "today" language; the main 20% off block doesn't.
- **Six emails in four days is cumulative friction.** The creative is the freshest of the run, but a subscriber who opened the July 3 clearance email 18 hours ago is one nudge away from unsubscribing. No single element in the body acknowledges the frequency or offers a "snooze" / preference option.
- **The multi-section layout dilutes the hero.** Women → Men → Kids → Home → Beauty → Shoes in a single scroll creates decision fatigue. One strong 4th of July hero SKU with an actual product link would outperform five category buckets.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an expiry line to the hero.** "Offer ends July 4 at midnight" or even "Today only" injected under the GOSHOP20 code would do more lift than any creative tweak. The 4th of July hook implies it but never says it.
- 2. **Differentiate at least one CTA.** Replace the hero "Shop Now" with "Shop 4th of July Picks" and the women's section with "Shop Women's." Specific verb+noun CTAs consistently outperform generic ones.
- 3. **Cut two sections from the body.** The email covers six categories. Trimming to three (Women, Home, Kids' deals) would tighten the message and reduce decision paralysis. Save Beauty and Shoes for a dedicated send when there's actual news.
- 4. **Surface the store-pickup offer higher.** "$5 Kohl's Cash for in-store pickup today" is the only truly time-anchored, day-specific hook in the email — move it above the multi-category grid, not below it.
- 5. **Suppress the heaviest-click-segment from this send.** Anyone who clicked the July 3 email within the last 24 hours has already seen the offer. Suppressing recent clickers from this blast protects deliverability and list health.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off + Kohl's Cash ends tonight 🎇`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 4th of July fits — 20% off, in store today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + earn Kohl's Cash on everything. Offer expires July 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the 4th of July sale — pick up in store today and earn a $5 bonus.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "4th of July" Email (2026-07-04)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email deploys three separate tracking stacks (CoherentPath, Salesforce MC, MessageInstill/Adobe) with redundant open pixels and duplicated font loading. Four compliance warnings from the QA relay are partially attributable to header stripping at the AgentMail layer, but the missing physical address flag warrants verification against the full (untruncated) HTML.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed closing tag on Salesforce open pixel:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?63NHMLFUXPVERHL7..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most clients will ignore it, but it invalidates the document and may cause parse errors in strict renderers.

**Three parallel open-tracking stacks deployed:**
- CoherentPath: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/74bc45fe-...`
- Salesforce MC: `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
- MessageInstill: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif`
- Adobe Audience Manager: `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

Redundant pixels add page weight and create duplicated open-event signals across analytics platforms, making attribution ambiguous.

**22 click links unverifiable:** All routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains. QA probe was correctly skipped; destination URL UTM coverage cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 1.4.4 failure for low-vision users. Apple Mail and some Android clients ignore this, but it's a risk.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (notably older iOS Mail) display the `<title>` as a preview fallback. Empty value may display the sender domain or nothing.

**9 content images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA):
The 6 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` render-path images and 2 CoherentPath/Demdex pixels all lack `alt=""`. Tracking pixels should at minimum carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` (the `o.gif` pixel correctly has both; the others do not).

**Duplicate Google Fonts loading:**
Both `<link rel="stylesheet">` and `@import url()` are present for Roboto and Montserrat within the same `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` conditional block. This fires two HTTP requests per font family on every non-Outlook client, adding ~200–400ms latency with no benefit.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`:**
Strips the `Referer` header on all outbound clicks. Combined with click-redirect links, this means Kohl's analytics will see no referrer data on landing pages — all traffic appears direct. Intentional for privacy but worth noting as it affects site-side attribution.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) are visible in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u=604230016` parameter on MessageInstill pixels appears to be a resolved subscriber ID. No issues found in visible source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN): Not present in captured headers. Could be stripped by AgentMail relay — confirm against raw SMTP headers. Gmail auto-inserts an unsubscribe prompt only when this header is present; its absence suppresses the Gmail-native unsubscribe UI and increases spam complaint risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** (QA WARN): Required for Gmail and Apple Mail one-click unsubscribe compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements). If absent in actual delivery headers, this is a deliverability compliance gap for >5k/day senders.

**Physical mailing address not found in truncated HTML** (QA WARN): CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. The HTML source is truncated — this may appear in the footer. **Must verify in full source before flagging as a violation.**

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** (QA WARN): SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown via relay. Cannot confirm authentication posture from available data.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 click links are opaque redirect URLs through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Without resolving the redirects:
- Cannot confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to destination URLs.
- Cannot confirm landing pages are live or match the offer (Kohl's Cash + 20% off, July 4th).
- The `no-referrer` meta (§3) means any UTM params passed via `Referer` header would be dropped regardless; UTM params would need to be explicit in the destination URL query string.

The `email_name=260704_DG_Email_Saturday` token on MessageInstill pixels confirms the campaign identifier is wired correctly on the tracking side.
