# 20% off + $10 off your $50+ purchase = holiday gifting made easy 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, dense Kohl's gift-guide send. The subject promises a simple stack: 20% off plus $10 off $50+. The render only partly delivers that. The top hero is a Kohl's Cash and coupon visual with a "Ends Nov 1 | Shop Now" strip. The three actual coupon offers sit at the very bottom as barcodes: "Take an extra 20% off," "Take $10 off your purchase of $50 or more," and a $10-off jewelry coupon. A reader who opens for the stack has to scroll to the end to find it.
- The middle is a catalog of gift categories: "For Her/Him/Kids," a "Top 100 Gifts" banner, 60% off jewelry, Columbia, home decor, a Sephora at Kohl's 25% off block, and a row of priced products. Everything is competing, and about a dozen "Shop Now" buttons give no single dominant CTA. It does feel seasonal, and the gift-guide framing is a step away from the identical "lowest prices of the season" sends of Oct 19-25. It is closer to the Oct 26 "Stack an extra 20% off + $10 off your $50+" send, which is nearly the same offer again.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand promo that tries to do too much. The offer is strong, but the layout buries the stack that the subject leads with.

## What's working

- The subject names the stacked offer, 20% off plus $10 off $50+, and gives a theme, holiday gifting.
- A "Gift List Lane" and gift-shop section ("Presents for your nice list, niche kid and no-clue list") add a fresh gifting angle and persona-style segments.
- The priced product tiles, such as the $299.99 drink maker and the $29-$99 towels, give concrete anchors.
- Loyalty is visible: the Kohl's Rewards header, "Earn 5% rewards," and the Kohl's Cash and "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" blocks.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- The stacked-coupon offer from the subject is only fully spelled out in the barcode block at the bottom, after roughly 90% of the scroll.
- There are too many competing modules (gifts, jewelry, Columbia, home, Sephora, products, delivery) and about a dozen similar "Shop Now" buttons. No single CTA wins.
- The mix of price-led and brand-led messaging (60% off jewelry, 25% off Sephora, $10 off $50+) muddies which discount applies to which items.
- The fine print is heavy: "Coupons do not apply to select brands" and "Excludes Sephora" sit near the offers. That works against an easy, honest offer.
- At 66 characters, the subject is too long for mobile. The equals-sign formula reads like a template.
- The cadence is crowded. This repeats the Oct 26 "Stack an extra 20% off + $10 off your $50+" offer within a day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the coupon stack to the hero. Put "Extra 20% off + $10 off $50+" with code and dates in the first screen, and move the barcodes to a secondary position.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules: the offer, one gift guide by recipient, and one product row. Move Sephora to its own send.
- 3. Use one primary button, "Shop gifts + save," and make the others text links.
- 4. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and add a date, since "Ends Nov 1" is already in the hero.
- 5. Add a deadline to the preheader and hero, since the strip is easy to miss.
- 6. Space out the near-duplicate stack sends so the offer feels fresh.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off + $10 off $50: gift smarter 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your gift list, solved: 20% off through Nov 1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVE20 + $10 off $50+ stack on gifts for her, him and kids. Ends Nov 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top 100 gifts, jewelry up to 60% off, and Kohl's Cash while you shop.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup errors (a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`) and a heavy tracking footprint of 83 wrapped links plus 5 beacon pixels. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **83 tracking links skipped:** The probe skipped them because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, status or redirect chain was verified.
- **Opaque wrappers:** Links use `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…`, so the destination can't be read from the source.
- **Beacon pixels:** Five appear before the main content.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/edc5713f-…` (1x1, no alt)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016…`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (`width="0" height="0"`)
  - Together these are three open-tracking pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls`, `open.aspx`), one Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`demdex`) and one more `mi.kohls.com` image.
- **Malformed tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is `<img … alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no opener. Browsers ignore it, but it's invalid markup.
- **User ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image URLs (`mi.kohls.com/p/up/…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/…`). If it identifies a recipient, it is exposed in every image request.
- **Plain-text part:** 14,673 of 20,447 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which matches the QA warning. This follows from the 83 long tracking links and may hurt deliverability scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged 14 images.
  - Tracking pixels: `edc5713f-…`, `o.gif`, `event`. These should carry `alt=""`.
  - Likely content images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`) and the product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58373420_1_2x`, `59205009_2_2x` and `59205009_3_2x`. Screen readers will announce the filenames, and with images blocked there is no fallback text. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether the `.png` files carry the offer copy.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize failure.
- **Empty title and favicon:** `<title></title>` is empty. `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request back to the document URL.
- **Head markup:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is invalid there and forces the parser to close the head early. Microdata is also not supported by mail clients.
- **Duplicate meta:** `format-detection` is declared twice (`telephone=no` and `date=no`). Some clients honor only one, so combine them into `telephone=no, date=no`.
- **Web fonts:** Montserrat and Roboto load from Google Fonts. The `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is low risk.
- **Layout tables:** The layout tables I can see use `role="presentation"`. The 1x1 tracking table at the top does not, but it is hidden.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The source is truncated, so the body can't be fully checked.

- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are missing. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a sender defect.
- **Physical address:** None was detected. The truncated source ends at the preheader, so the footer wasn't inspected. Verify against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. Also likely a relay artifact.
- **From domain:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment can't be verified without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the retrievable source. Attribution runs through opaque `qs=` tokens and Movable Ink parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1024601_20261027`, `email_name=261027_DG_Email_Tuesda…`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_p…`).
- **Landing pages:** They weren't resolved, so alignment with the "20% off + $10 off $50+" offer couldn't be checked. Resolve the redirects to confirm the final URLs and whether the offer code or promo carries through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA with the full HTML and raw headers to settle the footer address, `List-Unsubscribe`/`-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Follow the 83 tracking redirects to get final URLs and status codes, and check for UTM or promo parameters on landing pages.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and descriptive alt text to the content images listed above (`58373420_1_2x`, `59205009_*`, `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`).
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the markup:
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move or remove the schema.org `<div>` in `<head>`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
   - Remove the empty-href favicon `<link>`.
   - Merge the two `format-detection` metas.
6. Consider consolidating the beacon pixels (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls`, `open.aspx`, `demdex`) and shortening the plain-text part's URLs to bring the 72% URL ratio down.
