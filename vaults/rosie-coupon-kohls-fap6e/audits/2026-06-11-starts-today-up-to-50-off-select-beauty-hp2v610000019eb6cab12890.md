# STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🎀
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's opening salvo for a "Major Summer Deals" campaign — a direct replay of the late-May Stacking Weekend playbook. The dual-incentive hook (up to 50% off + 500 bonus points) lands fast in the hero, which is the right call. "STARTS TODAY" in the subject mirrors the May 26 "Starts today! You have 10% off 🎉" activation send nearly word-for-word, which will feel familiar — possibly too familiar — to subscribers who got both.
- The email is significantly content-heavy: three discount tiers (50%, 20%, 25%), a Beauty Insiders upsell module, a product carousel, a secondary 25%-off loyalty offer, a new-launch section, and a 100-point rewards teaser all stack below the fold. That's six distinct calls-to-action competing for the click. The brand's signature pink-and-black visual language is crisp, but the sheer length dilutes the urgency the subject promises.
- Clear, on-brand execution of a tried formula. Not a new idea, but Sephora's audience responds to it — the question is whether June subscribers have already tuned it out after three near-identical sends in the past three weeks.

## What's working

- **Hero loads the offer immediately.** "Major Summer Deals / UP TO 50% OFF + 500 BONUS POINTS" is visible before any scroll — the subject promise is fulfilled at the top.
- **Tiered discount structure is visually distinct.** The three offer tiles (50% / 20% / 25%) give the eye clear anchors and reduce the "where do I click?" problem.
- **Beauty Insiders tier messaging is present.** The "Beauty Insiders Get More" module reinforces loyalty value and nudges non-members toward sign-up.
- **Seasonally credible.** "Major Summer Deals" framing is appropriate for June 11 and feels like a genuine campaign launch rather than a clearance sweep.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Déjà vu subject line.** "STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty" is structurally identical to three recent sends. A subscriber who got the May 22 launch, the May 26 urgency doubles, and the June 1 expiry reminder will not feel this is new news.
- **No campaign end-date visible.** "STARTS TODAY" creates opening urgency but the hero doesn't show when the deal closes. Without a deadline, there's no reason to click now vs. later.
- **Content overload below the fold.** Six discrete offer/content modules (new launch, 100 points, FAVES section) crowd the scroll. Each one is a dilution of the 50%-off headline.
- **"Sephora Sale" sender name.** The display name should just be "Sephora." The "Sale" qualifier reads like a sub-brand or a spam-adjacent keyword and weakens the sender trust signal.
- **No personalization in the hero.** The May 26 welcome send addressed Rosie by name. This house-file blast has no segment or name hook, making it feel mass-broadcast against a background of personalized prior sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero.** The single highest-lift change: put "Ends Sunday" or "Through June 15" under the 50%-off lock-up. "STARTS TODAY" without an end date kills purchase urgency.
- 2. **Trim to three modules max.** Cut the new-launch and FAVES sections into a separate send. Let 50% off + 500 points own the email completely.
- 3. **Fix the sender name.** Change "Sephora Sale" → "Sephora" in the from-name.
- 4. **Rotate the subject formula.** Three near-identical "50% off select beauty" subjects in 20 days will erode open rates. Lead with Summer or the specific product angle instead.
- 5. **Inject a deadline into the preheader** to reinforce CTA urgency since the hero alone doesn't carry it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Major Summer Deals start now — up to 50% off 🎀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer upgrade: 50% off + 500 bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the savings before they end — 500 bonus points at $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `50% off select beauty + a bonus point boost. Ends soon.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` on viewport meta (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Blocks user-initiated pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) violation. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied too broadly (confirmed)**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Suppresses OS-level accessibility font scaling across all body elements. Should be scoped to the outer wrapper `<table>` only, not `body` and all inline elements.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` (confirmed)**
```html
<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` present. Screen readers use this to select the correct voice/language engine. Required for WCAG 3.1.1.

**`robots` meta value malformed (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`"no index"` (with space) is not a valid directive. Correct value is `"noindex"`. Harmless in most email clients (which strip meta tags), but invalid if the HTML is ever rendered in a browser context.
