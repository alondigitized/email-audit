# Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a cleaner Cyber Week send than the 11/21 and 11/22 ones, and it has a real hero. A purple-sequin banner reads "Massive Haul Incoming. Get 30% off these top brands for 24 hours–only at Sephora." Right below it sit two big "30% OFF" tiles for Ole Henriksen and Milk Makeup. The offer is specific, the deadline is credible and the SHOP buttons are visible. For about the first third of the email, the eye lands where it should.
- Then it sprawls. After the hero there is a product grid headed "Cyber Week Deals Are Here," a rhode "Limited-Edition" block and a "Spot it. Shop it." pair (up to 30% off skin and hair tools, 30% off MAC). Next come a Black Friday app teaser, a store locator for Kohl's, a Holiday Gift Guide, a 4X points barcode and a long fine-print block. That is five or six ideas stacked under one clear one. It is the same catalog-style sprawl as the 11/13 and 11/14 sends.
- The subject is fine, but "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️" is a generic announcement. It names no brand and no number. I'd call this a competent promo that is better at the top than the bottom. It's a 6 or 7 whose best asset, the 30% offer, never reaches the subject line.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" tiles for Ole Henriksen and Milk Makeup are huge and easy to read. They are the clear focal point.
- The "24 hours–only" framing gives the offer a deadline that reads as real.
- Every offer block has its own visible CTA button (SHOP ALL DEALS, SAVE NOW, SHOP NOW).
- The purple-sequin look is consistent with the 11/21 and 11/22 Cyber Week sends, so the campaign feels cohesive.
- The product grid shows named products with struck-through and sale prices (for example Touchland, Summer Fridays, LANEIGE), which gives shoppers a concrete price anchor.

## What's weak

- The email runs far too long. It has about eight distinct modules, and the hero offer is buried under store hours, a gift guide and barcodes.
- The top banner promotes 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT. That competes with the 30% hero before the hero even appears.
- The "Black Friday Preview: 50% Off" teaser steals urgency from the 24-hour 30% deal and tells people to wait for 11/26.
- The product grid prices are tiny and low-contrast, and the section feels like filler.
- The Kohl's store locator with a map and hours is a utility block. It doesn't belong in the middle of a promo, and it adds length.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the two 30% brand tiles, one product grid and a single footer CTA. Move the store locator, gift guide and barcode lower or remove them.
- 2. Drop the 4X points banner, or move it below the hero, so the first thing a subscriber sees is the 30% deal.
- 3. Save the Black Friday 50% app teaser for its own send on 11/25 or 11/26. Right now it undercuts the 24-hour deadline.
- 4. Put the day-specific brands in the subject and preheader so the offer shows up in the inbox.
- 5. Make the grid prices larger and bolder, and add one star rating or "Best Seller" tag per product.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ole Henriksen, Milk Makeup and more, 30% off until tonight. In store and online.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️" (Sephora)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering bug (an empty greeting slot) and no plain-text part. Several compliance and authentication headers couldn't be checked because the relay probably stripped them. The QA pass rate is 38%, and the HTML I was given is truncated, so the footer and most links weren't visible to me.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. Only 12 of 49 links were probed and 37 were skipped as lower priority, so the rest are unverified.
- Open-tracking pixels:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1` is a 1x1 pixel with a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`).
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a second, hidden image (`display:none; mso-hide:all`).
- Having two separate tracking systems is normal. The hidden `app.sephora.com` image is blocked wherever images are off, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection preloads both, so open rates will be inflated.
- The JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. Both are empty, and `PromotionCard` isn't a standard schema.org type. Gmail's promotion annotations will get no usable data from them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. A `@media (max-width:480px)` block exists, but the fixed minimum width risks horizontal scrolling in clients that ignore or partly support media queries. Because the source is truncated, I couldn't confirm that inner tables override it.
- **Invalid CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. A conditional comment there isn't valid and may break the rules that follow it in some parsers.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is minor and has no real effect in email.
- **Alt text:** the QA check flagged `o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/…` image as missing alt text.
  - `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel, so that flag is a false positive.
  - The `app.sephora.com` image has no `alt` attribute and is hidden, so the impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would clear the flag.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is low impact for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the name field is empty. This is a confirmed bug.
  - There is no fallback value for when the recipient's first name is missing, so the email shows broken text.
  - The `mi_u` ID shows that profile data is available, so the likely cause is a missing first-name attribute rather than a template-wide failure.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found.** **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA output says AgentMail may not capture these headers. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo can't offer one-click unsubscribe, which their bulk-sender rules require. These are unconfirmed, because the relay is a likely cause.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown here, and the relay is again the likely reason. Run a direct-to-seed-inbox test to confirm.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters.** The email is HTML-only, which is a spam-filter signal and an accessibility gap. This may also be a relay artifact, so check the raw MIME on a direct send.
- The footer was cut off, so I couldn't verify the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM). The QA probe says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- I couldn't assess UTM parameters or landing-page alignment. The HTML is truncated before any CTA `href`, and the QA output gives no landing-page or redirect data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1`, which is in the pixel URL, not on links.
- The ID marks this as Day 1 of an 8-day series, so each day's destination should be checked separately.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting.** Add a fallback for the first-name merge field (for example `Hi there,`) or suppress the greeting when the field is empty.
2. **Add a plain-text part.** Send a `multipart/alternative` message with a real text version.
3. **Confirm the headers on a direct send.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay. If they're truly absent, add them.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block** out of the `@media` rule and into its own `<style>`.
6. **Clean up the structured data.** Populate `DiscountOffer.discountCode` or remove it, and drop the empty `PromotionCard`.
7. **Add `alt=""`** to the `app.sephora.com` tracking image.
8. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so the footer, CTAs and UTM parameters can be checked.
