# Save 20% | Find the intimates you want at prices you'll love ❤️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's production template that's been running since 7/30, but today it's actually well-aligned to a real campaign: "Semi-Annual" intimates sale (bras, panties, shapewear) matches the subject line and hero copy exactly — no mismatch like the 8/10 "Fall home finds" send. Rewards status bar, red 20% off coupon banner, Kohl's Cash strip, then nav buttons up top, followed by a clean run of intimates products with specific prices ($19.99 bras, 25% off Jockey, 5/$35 panties, 25% off shapewear) and decent-looking product photography.
- After the intimates block it sprawls into back-to-school home goods, a sweepstakes, and unrelated picks (a blender, towels, a mattress topper, jeans) — the usual Kohl's kitchen-sink approach. It's not a bad execution, but it's the same daily red/green/pink template subscribers have seen for two straight weeks, and this is at least the sixth near-identical "Save 20%" send in that window.
- Solid, on-message hero this time; verdict is a middling-to-decent script execution dragged down by fatigue and template sameness.

## What's working

- Subject and hero visually agree — "intimates" copy, "Semi-Annual" banner, and product grid (bras, Jockey, panties, shapewear) all match.
- Specific prices and percentages are visible throughout ($19.99, 25% off, 5/$35, 25% off shapewear).
- Clear, unambiguous red "Shop Now" CTA buttons repeated after each product block.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.
- Model demographic (women, intimates/loungewear) matches the product category shown.

## What's weak

- This is at least the sixth "Save 20%" Kohl's send in two weeks — cadence fatigue is real and the subject doesn't differentiate itself from 8/9, 8/10, 8/10, 8/11, 8/12 sends.
- After the intimates section, the email sprawls into unrelated categories (back-to-school, sweepstakes, blender, mattress topper, jeans) that dilute the intimates focus promised in the subject.
- The coupon code is buried at the very bottom in a barcode graphic — someone who scrolls to check out on the go has to hunt for it twice (once at top banner, again at bottom).
- Emoji in subject (❤️) is a small but recurring pattern across this brand's recent sends — not fresh anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Trim the email to just the intimates offer and cut the back-to-school/sweepstakes/random-picks sections into a separate send — a focused email converts better than a kitchen-sink one, and it would actually deliver on what the subject promises.
- 2. Vary the subject-line formula — five near-identical "Save/Take 20%" subjects in a row trains subscribers to skip without opening.
- 3. Move a clear "use code GET20" callout near the top hero, not just buried in the bottom barcode block.
- 4. Add a small "ends Aug 23" countdown near the hero since the Semi-Annual sale window is longer than the coupon window (Aug 16) — right now those two deadlines are easy to conflate.
