# 📣 This just in: 20% off is coming in hot 🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- The hero banner — the single most important pixel in this email — is visibly broken. The pink "20% off" callout is clipped on the left edge, so it reads as "n extra / 0% off" instead of "an extra 20% off," with the ribbon graphic ("2 DAYS") overlapping the discount box. This is the first thing a recipient sees, and it fails at communicating the headline offer clearly.
- Past that stumble, the email actually recovers well: real product photography (denim, dresses), specific price points ($28.79, $27.99, starting at $31.99), a loyalty-status callout ("Kohl's Rewards Member"), and a clearance banner with a distinct 70% off tier. It's more substantive than several recent Kohl's sends that leaned entirely on banner graphics with no real merchandise shown.
- Net: good bones, undermined by a hero-image rendering bug that a subscriber would notice in the first half-second. Fix the clipping and this is a solid mid-pack send; as rendered, it undercuts its own headline offer.

## What's working

- Real product shots with actual prices and "Reg. $44.99" comparisons make the 20%-off math tangible, not just a banner claim.
- Loyalty status ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" / "Earn: 5% REWARDS") is surfaced right under the logo — good use of membership data.
- Two physical coupon barcodes with codes and PINs ("SAVINGS," "WOMENS20") plus explicit expiration ("Ends July 28") reduce checkout friction for in-store shoppers.
- Category nav buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) repeat three times through the email, giving multiple low-effort entry points.

## What's weak

- The primary hero banner is clipped/overlapping — "20%" renders as "0%" with the ribbon graphic cutting into the discount callout and "an extra" cut down to "n extra." This is a hard render bug, not a design choice.
- Preview text repeats "20% off" verbatim from the subject line instead of adding new information, then leaks "View this email in a browser" straight into the inbox preview.
- Subject line's "coming in hot" is vague hype with no concrete deadline, even though the body has one ("Ends July 28") — that urgency doesn't make it into the subject/preview.
- Per brand history, this is at least the sixth-plus promotional Kohl's send in a week (five "Instant Kohl's Cash" emails July 22–26); this one pivots to a flat 20%-off/denim push, which helps freshness but the underlying cadence problem remains.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero clipping first.** This is a shipped-render bug on the highest-visibility asset in the email — QA the hero banner across clients before the next send; right now the discount is nearly illegible.
- 2. Rewrite the preview text so it doesn't just restate the subject's "20% off" — use it to name the denim/dress hero products instead, and strip the "View this email in a browser" tail from the visible preview.
- 3. Pull the "Ends July 28" deadline into the subject line itself — a real 2-day countdown is stronger than "coming in hot."
- 4. Lead the "Complete the look" CTAs with the actual denim price point (e.g., "Jeans from $27.99") rather than generic "Shop Now" buttons — the price is the differentiator already proven further down the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 days only: 20% off denim, dresses & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off coupon expires July 28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New denim styles from $27.99 — plus 20% off with your coupon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: stack an extra 20% off through Tuesday.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Promotional Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a CoherentPath/Cheetah Digital-style ESP template with standard MSO/Outlook conditionals and mobile media queries; automated QA passed 43% of checks, flagging compliance header gaps, accessibility, and one markup defect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`), so destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Multiple first-open/read tracking beacons are present: `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/1b20c61f-...` (1x1 px, doubles as the primary open-tracking pixel), `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?AFMUHOHIBV7EPMN2VKHWMM5ILY.60266`, and an Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) call to `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Three redundant open-tracking mechanisms in the same send is notable but not a defect.
- Multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render-tracking pixels share `mi_ecmp=1018469_2026727&email_name=260727_DG_Email_Monday`, confirming consistent internal campaign tagging.
- One render-pixel URL contains an empty parameter: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (redemption end date) — see §4.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup**: the AAM open-tracking `<img>` is followed by a stray, unmatched closing tag: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60266&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no corresponding `<custom>` opening tag anywhere prior — this is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- **Missing/empty alt attributes** (QA-flagged, 7 images): the `click.chp.kohls.com` open pixel and the `kohls.demdex.net` AAM pixel have no `alt` attribute at all; five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` render pixels are also flagged. Note: for 1x1 tracking pixels, `alt=""` (as correctly used on the `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` beacon) is the correct pattern — the flagged pixels should be updated to match rather than treated as content-accessibility failures.
- Responsive `@media (max-width: 500px)` rules and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` / MSO conditionals are present and structured correctly in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One render-tracking pixel contains an unresolved/empty merge value: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (truncated before a second parameter `mi_kc_event_pe...`). If this is meant to carry a redemption end date, it is not being populated — worth confirming with the ESP whether this is expected to always be blank or is a broken token.
- No `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or other unresolved personalization placeholders found in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found (QA warning). Cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level; note the QA tool flags this may be due to the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected in the scanned HTML — CAN-SPAM requires one. The provided source is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed as a true absence; it may simply be outside the captured excerpt.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM): not found — authentication status unknown, again potentially a relay-capture limitation rather than a sender-side issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` parameters are present on any visible link. Kohl's uses proprietary tracking params instead (`mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`, `mi_mid`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`, `email_name`), which is consistent internally but means landing-page attribution depends entirely on Kohl's own redirect/analytics stack, not GA-style UTM conventions.
- Actual landing page destinations cannot be verified — all 61 outbound links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` / `click.s.kohls.com` redirectors, which were skipped by the HTTP probe, so alignment with final landing pages is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the orphaned `</custom>` closing tag near the AAM tracking pixel — invalid markup with no functional purpose.
2. Set `alt=""` on the `click.chp.kohls.com`, `kohls.demdex.net`, and the five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` tracking pixels for consistency with the one pixel that already does this correctly.
3. Confirm whether `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is intentionally blank or a broken personalization token.
4. Verify with the sending platform (or check raw headers directly, not via relay) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually being sent — the current warnings may be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a real compliance gap.
5. Confirm a physical mailing address is present in the footer (outside the truncated excerpt) to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
6. If cross-platform analytics attribution matters, consider layering standard UTM params alongside the proprietary `mi_*` params so landing-page traffic can be tied back to this send in non-Kohl's tooling.
