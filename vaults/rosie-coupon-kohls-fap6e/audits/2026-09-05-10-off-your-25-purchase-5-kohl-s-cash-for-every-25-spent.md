# $10 off your $25 purchase ✅ $5 Kohl's Cash for every $25 spent ✅
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "$10 off $25 + Kohl's Cash" send in the last week — near-identical to 2026-09-03's "$10 off your $25 purchase STARTS TODAY" and yesterday's Labor Day Cash send. The Labor Day banner and dual-coupon hero are strong and clear up top, but the email then sprawls into 15+ category modules (tees, bedding, activewear, dresses, floor care, towels, dining, shoes, fleece, clearance, "picked with you in mind") with no real narrative — it reads like a catalog dump rather than a curated Labor Day event.
- The functional stuff is solid: legible offer, barcodes for in-store use, clear nav pills repeated three times. But repetition fatigue is now the dominant story for this brand — subscribers who opened Sunday's or Monday's nearly-identical coupon email have already seen this exact structure twice.
- Judgement: competent execution, weak differentiation. Score accordingly — average performer, not a standout, and increasingly a candidate for "delete without opening" given the cadence.

## What's working

- Labor Day hero banner is bold and on-theme with clear dual offer ($10 off $25 + $5 Kohl's Cash per $25).
- Two visible barcodes near the bottom let a subscriber shop in-store without hunting for a code.
- Category nav pills (Women/Men/Kids/Home) repeat consistently, aiding quick navigation on a long scroll.
- No rendering bugs — images load, pricing is legible, layout is clean throughout.
- Reward tier status ("Kohl's Rewards Member, Earn 5% Rewards") is visible at the very top for personalization.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical "$10 off $25" structure this week — zero new creative differentiation from 9/3 and 9/4 sends.
- The email sprawls across 10+ unrelated category blocks with no hierarchy after the hero — floor care sits next to dresses next to a vacuum, with no organizing theme beyond "everything is on sale."
- "Coupons do not apply to select styles/brands" appears three separate times, which undercuts the "$10 off $25" and "15% off" headline offers.
- The Ninja blender and mattress topper "picked with you in mind" block feels randomly inserted, not personalized to any visible signal.
- CTA copy is the same "Shop Now" repeated 15+ times with zero variation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half — pick 4-5 hero categories max and drop the rest to a "see everything on sale" link. This is the single highest-leverage fix; scroll fatigue is killing engagement more than any copy issue.
- 2. Differentiate this send from 9/3 and 9/4 — if the offer is truly identical, don't send a third near-duplicate; either escalate the offer or skip today's send.
- 3. Move the barcodes/coupon codes to just below the hero, not buried after 15 scrolls — that's the actual actionable content for in-store shoppers.
- 4. Drop or justify the "picked with you in mind" block — if it's not truly personalized, relabel it or cut it.
- 5. Vary CTA copy by category ("Shop Tees", "Shop Bedding") instead of generic "Shop Now" x15.
