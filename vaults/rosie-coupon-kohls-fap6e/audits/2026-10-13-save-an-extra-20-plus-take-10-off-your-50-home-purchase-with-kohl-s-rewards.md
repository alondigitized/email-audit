# Save an extra 20% 🙌 Plus, take $10 off your $50+ home purchase with Kohl's Rewards.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kohl's coupon send again. The big "Take an extra 20% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18" banner sits at the top. Under it is a green Kohl's Cash strip and the Home / Women / Men / Kids nav tiles. The subject leads with the same extra 20%, but today's twist is the "$10 off your $50+ home purchase" offer, and the body does follow through. A "Pumpkin spice your space" hero carries a $10 Rewards badge. Bedding at 50% off, Halloween décor at up to 40% off and kitchen appliances at 40% off follow, so the home angle is delivered.
- It also runs long. A Clearance "up to 70%" block repeats the nav tiles, then a product grid, two barcode coupons, a Kohl's Cash earn strip, a free-returns banner and a wall of fine print. Compared with the 10/12 "Season's Best Shoe Sale" send, which had a single clear hero, this one reads as a template with a home skin. Anyone who has seen this week's near-identical sends will skim past it.
- Verdict: a competent, well-rendered promo with a clear offer and a credible Oct. 18 deadline. It's too dense and too familiar to feel targeted.

## What's working

- The top banner states the offer plainly: "20% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18."
- The $10-off-$50 Rewards badge sits on the "Pumpkin spice your space" hero, so the subject's home promise is visible immediately.
- The category blocks (bedding 50%, Halloween 40%, appliances 40%) are specific, seasonal and well photographed.
- The product grid shows real prices with "Orig." strikethroughs, such as the Ninja SLUSHI at $299.99 against $349.99.
- The barcode coupons with codes (YOUGET20, 10HOME) and "Ends Oct. 18" make redemption concrete.
- No broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- Two competing primary offers (20% off and $10 off $50) plus a Clearance 70% block and a Kohl's Cash earn offer dilute the hero.
- The Home / Women / Men / Kids tiles appear twice and push the actual hero below the fold.
- Nearly every CTA is "Shop Now", which is generic and not a verb plus a noun.
- There is no reviews or social proof anywhere.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" caveat and the dense fine print undercut the honesty of the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the hero the $10-off-$50 home deal if home is the story, and push the generic 20% banner and Clearance block down or into a footer strip.
- 2. Remove the first set of nav tiles, or merge both sets into one, so the hero lands within the first screen.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop bedding deals" or "Get Halloween décor".
- 4. Add star ratings under the product grid.
- 5. Cut the clearance block from this send, since it competes with the home theme.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Bedding up to 50% off + $10 off $50 for Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Halloween décor, bedding and kitchen deals end Oct. 18. Coupon inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Rewards $10 off is ready. Home deals up to 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's 2026-10-13 "Save an extra 20%" email

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, responsive Movable Ink and Adobe-instrumented template. It has several markup defects (stray `</custom>`, `<div>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`) and four tracking pixels. Compliance, authentication and UTM checks can't be confirmed. The relay stripped the headers, and the supplied source is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click tracking.** 65 links go through redirect domains, for example `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...`. The QA skipped the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect-chain length and final status codes are **unverified**.
- **Four tracking pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/caf0f7d8-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?3YY5IYB5...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third party)
- **Redundant opens.** Three first-party open pixels plus one third-party pixel inflate open counts and add request latency.
- **Pixel placement.**
  - The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`) sits directly in `<body>` with no `alt`, no `display:block` and no hiding style. It can render a stray line box in some clients.
  - The `demdex.net` pixel uses `width="0" height="0"`, which some clients ignore or treat inconsistently. Gmail may strip it.
- **Possible empty parameter.** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value (`916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...`). This suggests a dynamic field that resolved to blank. The URL is truncated, so this isn't fully confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src=".../open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>`. This is invalid markup.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will relocate it, and the microdata is unlikely to be read by mail clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request to the document base URL.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) in clients that honor it.
- **Broken CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element type.
- **Invalid declaration.** `max-height: auto !important` in the mobile `img` rule is not a valid value.
- **Missing alt text.** The QA flagged 10 images. Evidence from the source:
  - **Content images, unconfirmed.** Seven are not tracking pixels: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`. `58379268_2_2x` is probably a product image. I can't confirm from the truncated source that these have no `alt` at all. Blocked-image rendering and screen-reader output are the risk.
  - **Pixels, confirmed.** `caf0f7d8-...` and `event` are pixels with no `alt`. `o.gif` is a pixel.
  - **Likely false positive.** The source shows `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so that flag is probably a checker false positive for intentional empty alt. Only the `caf0f7d8-...` and `demdex` pixels lack `alt` in the visible source.
- **Missing role.** The 1×1 wrapper tables (`<table width="1" height="1">` and the AAM table) lack `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables have it.
- **Doctype.** `lang="en"` is present. The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is mixed with `xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o` and XHTML-style `/>` tags. This is tolerated by clients but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source.
- Per-recipient identifiers are present: `mi_u=604230016` and the Movable Ink `mi_mid`/`mi_cid` values. These are resolved.
- Coverage is partial because the source is truncated. The empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value is the only suspect field (see §2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Not found. The QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so I can't call it a sender defect. One-click unsubscribe support is **unverified**.
- **Physical address.** Not detected. The footer isn't in the truncated source, so this is **unverified**. If the full HTML lacks it, it is a CAN-SPAM violation.
- **Authentication-Results.** Absent (relay artifact). SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is **unknown**. Domain alignment looks consistent: the From address `Kohls@s.kohls.com` and click domains `click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com` all share `kohls.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link.** Not visible in the supplied portion, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the supplied URLs. Campaign attribution is carried by the ESP and Movable Ink identifiers instead: `mi_ecmp=1023774_20261013` and `email_name=261013_DG_Email_Tuesda…`.
- Because all 65 links are click-redirect wrapped and unprobed, I can't verify that the landing pages match the "$10 off $50+ home" or "extra 20%" offers, or that parameters survive the redirect.
- No findings can be confirmed here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Markup cleanup.**
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or drop it.
   - Add a non-empty `<title>`.
   - Remove `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
   - Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
   - Change `max-height: auto` to `none`.
2. **Alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` to the seven content images. The pixels `caf0f7d8-...` and `event` should get `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"`, as `o.gif` already has.
3. **Pixel consolidation.**
   - Give the first pixel `style="display:block"`, or move it into a hidden wrapper.
   - Consider dropping redundant open pixels.
   - Use `width="1" height="1"` on the demdex pixel instead of 0.
4. **Zoom.** Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. **Verify empty dynamic fields.** Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` is blank in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL.
6. **Re-run QA on the raw message.** Use the original `.eml` or full headers, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer physical address and the unsubscribe link.
7. **Follow the redirects.** Resolve a sample of the 65 `click.s.kohls.com` links to check final URLs, status codes, UTM or campaign parameter retention and landing-page match.
