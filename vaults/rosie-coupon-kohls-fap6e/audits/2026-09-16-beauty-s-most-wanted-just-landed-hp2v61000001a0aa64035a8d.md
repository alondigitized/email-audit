# Beauty’s most wanted just landed 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is another Sephora "Beauty's Biggest Hits" kitchen-sink send, and it's structurally the same bait-and-switch this brand has run all week: subject promises "Beauty's most wanted just landed," but the top of the email is a "50% off Select Beauty" banner (Fresh, Sephora Collection, Fenty), not new arrivals. The actual "Beauty's Biggest Hits" hero doesn't show up until the second screen, and it's vague ("They've earned their spot") with no product visible in the hero itself.
- Once you scroll past the top banner, this is actually a decent, well-organized product grid — eight named products with real prices, a Charlotte Tilbury launch callout, and a clean bottom section with two live promos (15% off Claspe/K18 haircare, 5X points) plus a Kérastase 24-hour flash sale. It's busy, but at least it's honest busy — real prices, real product shots, no broken renders.
- The chronic problem is subject/hero mismatch. Compare to 2026-09-12's "50% off select beauty⏰" (7.00/10), which led with the discount and delivered the discount first — that was the cleanest send in this run precisely because subject and hero matched. This one reverts to the "new arrivals" tease over a discount hero, same trick as "Select brands are 50% off today 🤑" (5.00/10) and the K18 send (5.00/10).

## What's working

- Top banner offer is concrete and honest: "50% off Select Beauty," today only, brands named (First Aid Beauty, Sephora Collection, Fenty Beauty).
- Strong, real product grid below the fold — 8 items, all named with brand, product, and price (Eadem $24, Jo Malone $175, Estée Lauder $52, YSL $185, etc.).
- Charlotte Tilbury "New From" section has a clear single hero product, price, and specific claim (airbrushed blur, weightless).
- No render bugs — images loaded, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Bottom of email stacks multiple live, dated promos (15% off haircare through 9/16, 5X points on 9/16, Kérastase 24-hour sale) giving real urgency signals.

## What's weak

- Subject says "most wanted just landed" (implies new/trending arrivals) but the actual top hero is a 50%-off clearance-style banner — mismatch, same complaint as multiple prior sends this week.
- "Beauty's Biggest Hits" hero section has no product image or specific offer — just a "Shop Now" CTA and vague copy ("They've earned their spot"), wasting the second scroll on nothing concrete.
- Email is extremely long (8+ distinct sections/offers before the footer) — no single clear primary CTA, diluting the "most wanted" narrative the subject promised.
- Preview text (not visible in screenshot but implied by top banner "Enjoy Same-Day Delivery...Exclusions/terms apply") is generic account/shipping boilerplate, not a hook that complements the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: lead with an actual "Biggest Hits" product carousel (top 3-4 bestsellers with images/prices) right under the header, not a vague banner with no product.
- 2. Cut the top 50%-off banner or move it below the "Biggest Hits" section — right now it steals the top slot from the thing the subject promised.
- 3. Give the "Beauty's Biggest Hits" section a real product image in the hero itself (it currently has none) — that's the single highest-leverage fix since it's what the subject sold.
- 4. Trim the email — six distinct offers (50% off, Biggest Hits, Charlotte Tilbury, product grid, haircare/points, Kérastase) is kitchen-sink fatigue continuing this week's pattern; pick two.
