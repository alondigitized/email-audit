# Deals up to 50% off + Extra 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall, roughly 12,000px tall. It stacks the three Black Friday levers from the subject: up to 50% off, an extra 20% off, and $15 Kohl's Cash per $50. The top shows a snowy hero with a Kohl's Cash badge, then a row of category pills (Women, Men, Kids, Home, Toys, Shoes & Boots, Beauty) and a "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip. Below that, product tiles run in a steady rhythm of "Shop Now" links with prices and percent-off numbers.
- The email is coherent and on-season, and it renders cleanly. But it is the same giant catalog you've been getting all week, from the 11/21 "Ends today: 20% off" send through the three sends on 11/22. For an athletic-leaning shopper, the only on-target content is a small Skechers shoe tile at about 40% off, buried mid-scroll. Nothing here is built for a runner or gym-goer.
- Verdict: a competent promo wall with no personalization, and one more send in a saturated week.

## What's working

- The subject's three offers (50% off, extra 20%, $15 Kohl's Cash) are all visible in the render, so the promise matches the content.
- The category pill row near the top gives a fast route to Shoes & Boots.
- Tiles carry concrete prices and percent-off numbers, such as "15.99 boots" and "99.99 floor care". Shoppers can judge value without clicking.
- Large "Up to 50% off" banner blocks break up the scroll and give it some rhythm.
- A closing "Here's your direct route to even more deals" banner repeats the offer for people who scroll to the bottom.

## What's weak

- The email has no focal point. The hero, the category pills and about 25 tiles compete, so the eye has no single place to land.
- The athletic content is a single Skechers tile, with no activewear hero. Only the "Fitness, mattress, toppers and pads" tile (about 60% off) is near fitness, and it's a mattress promo.
- Fine print repeats on nearly every tile: "Select styles. Coupons do not apply." That undercuts the "Extra 20% off" headline and feels like bait-and-switch.
- The Kohl's Cash badge in the hero is small at mobile size, so the strongest differentiator is easy to miss.
- This is at least the sixth Kohl's send since 11/21, and the creative is near-identical to the 11/22 "$15 Kohl's Cash" subject send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer and 6-8 tiles. Lead with the Kohl's Cash mechanic, since it's the only offer that differs from the last six sends.
- 2. Segment by category affinity. Athletic or footwear browsers should get a hero with running shoes and activewear, and the Skechers tile should move to the top.
- 3. Put the exclusions in one clear line under the hero, such as "Extra 20% off excludes select brands". Don't repeat it on every tile.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy like "Shop running shoes" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 5. Cap the send cadence during Black Friday week, or vary the creative so each send has a reason to exist.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based and has fallback fonts and a mobile media query. The markup has several validity problems, and QA could not confirm the compliance headers or any link destinations. The source is truncated, so the footer and most links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **92 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and final status codes are therefore unverified. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNjksInQiOjU…`, where the destination is encoded in the opaque `qs` blob.
- **Plain-text part is over 70% URLs** (16,344 of 22,139 chars). The long tracked URLs inflate it, which is a spam-filter risk signal.
- **Four tracking beacons are stacked in the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/62cd36f7…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  This is redundant and adds third-party requests. Opens are double-counted across platforms.
- **Stray `</custom>` closing tag.** It follows the `open.aspx` `<img>` and has no opening tag. It is invalid markup and could be mishandled by some parsers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This matters for web-view and accessible rendering.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** A `<div>` is invalid in `<head>`. Browsers and web-view parsers will implicitly close `<head>` there, which pushes `</head>` content into the body. The microdata is also useless in email.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.** It is invalid and can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **`maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta disables pinch-zoom.** This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Images with no `alt` attribute (QA flagged 62 across the email).** The ones with a `<img … alt="">` and `aria-hidden` (`o.gif`) are intentional decorative pixels, so those flags are likely false positives. Likely real issues:
  - `62cd36f7…` has no `alt` attribute at all.
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event` has no `alt`.
  - Product images `60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`) have no `alt`.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png` and others) have no `alt`. If these carry offer text, such as the Kohl's Cash banners, that content is invisible with images blocked.
- **The first tracking pixel is not hidden.** It is a bare `<img width="1" height="1">` directly after `<body>`, with no `display:none` or `alt`. This is minor.
- **Google Fonts load through both `<link>` and `@import`.** Support is limited in Gmail and Outlook, but the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so there is no real issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens are visible** in the available source.
- **A dynamic parameter is empty.** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The Kohl's Cash redemption end date is blank. It may be intentional, but it is worth verifying because the subject advertises $15 Kohl's Cash.
- The visible portion cannot confirm the rest of the content.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. It is not proven missing at the sender.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, which should be checked against the raw headers.
- **No physical address was detected.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed**. A Kohl's footer would normally carry one.
- **Unsubscribe link:** it was not visible in the truncated source, so it cannot be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be verified.** The links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` redirects (SFMC-style) with no readable destination, and QA skipped probing them.
- **Campaign identifiers are consistent in the visible source.** They are `mi_ecmp=1026513_20261123` and `email_name=261123_DG_Email_Monday`, and the date matches the send. No `utm_*` parameters appear in any visible URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the head markup. Move or remove the `<div itemscope>`, populate `<title>`, drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`, and remove the stray `</custom>`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images, especially the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` banners and the `images.am.kohls.com` product images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on tracking pixels.
4. Consolidate the four open and tracking beacons, and add `display:none` to the leading pixel.
5. Look into the blank `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value to confirm the Kohl's Cash end date renders.
6. Re-run QA against the raw message with full headers. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass for `s.kohls.com`. Also check the footer for a physical address and an unsubscribe link.
7. Resolve the redirect chains with a followed-redirect probe to validate landing pages and UTM carry-through.
8. Reduce the plain-text URL ratio by shortening tracking URLs or adding more readable plain-text copy.
