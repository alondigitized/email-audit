# Open for the beauty icons
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- "Beauty's Most Wanted" is a full-scale hero moment for Chanel's Coco Mademoiselle Crush Absolu launch, and visually it's Sephora's best-produced send in the recent run — a genuine editorial photograph (model in pearls, moody restaurant lighting, holding the bottle) instead of another flat product grid or trending-picks screenshot. But structurally it's the same email as every other recent Sephora send once you scroll past the hero: eight sequential product blocks with no organizing logic (shampoo, eye cream, EDT, argan oil, hair serum, hand sanitizer, EDP, blush balm), followed by App Fest and Beauty Insider promos bolted onto the bottom.
- The trial-size banner at the very top competes with the fragrance hero for attention — two offers before the reader has even oriented, and the trial-size one (NARS/Fenty free with $30 purchase) is generic and repeats a beat from prior sends. The fragrance itself is a strong, specific hook (new Chanel launch, $162-195, exclusivity around scent), but the CTA "Shop To Redeem" up top and "Shop Now" for the fragrance dilute a single clear path.
- This is a step up from the flat grids of 8/13 and 8/15, closer to the differentiated editorial swing of 8/14's rhode Spotwear launch — but it still buries a genuinely strong hero (a major Chanel fragrance drop) under an unrelated product dump. Grade: solid creative, poor discipline on focus.

## What's working

- The Chanel fragrance hero image is genuinely premium — real editorial photography, not a stock product shot, and it's the first thing the eye lands on after the banner.
- "THE NEW FRAGRANCE" section gives concrete product info: name, scent notes ("ambery... magnetic"), price range ($162-195), and fulfillment options (pickup, curbside, Same-Day Delivery).
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges on Kérastase and Juliette has a gun products add credible exclusivity signaling.
- Star-rating badges appear on a few products (Josie Maran, Touchland, Juliette has a gun), giving light social proof.
- Clean top nav bar and consistent black/white/blush color system keep the whole email visually cohesive despite the grab-bag middle section.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers stacked before any single focal point: the free trial-size banner, then the fragrance hero — the reader has to choose what to look at first.
- The eight-product grid (Oribe, Shiseido, Maison Margiela, Josie Maran, Kérastase, Touchland, Juliette has a gun, blush balm) has zero connective theme — it reads as generic catalog filler after a strong, specific hero.
- After the fragrance section builds momentum, the email pivots away from it entirely and never returns — no second fragrance CTA near the bottom.
- App Fest and Beauty Insider blocks at the bottom are visually cramped together (pink/red App Fest tile directly against red Insider Celebration tile), competing for the same red palette and diluting each other.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section header is vague filler copy that doesn't tell the reader what's coming.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the trial-size banner or move it below the fold.** Let the Chanel fragrance be the undisputed single hero — right now two offers open the email and split attention immediately.
- 2. **Replace the 8-product undifferentiated grid with a curated "pairs well with your fragrance" or "new launches" theme** — give the mid-email real estate a reason to exist, the way rhode's Spotwear send (8/14) stayed focused on one launch.
- 3. **Add a second, lower CTA back to the Chanel fragrance** (e.g., a "Discover Coco Mademoiselle Crush Absolu" strip right before the footer) so the strongest asset in the email isn't abandoned after one scroll.
- 4. **Give App Fest and Beauty Insider Celebration visual separation** — different color treatment or more whitespace between them so they don't blur into one red block.
- 5. **Sharpen "Spot it. Shop it." into a real theme label** — e.g., "Fall's Must-Haves" or similar, so the section justifies its own existence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `A new Chanel scent just landed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Coco Mademoiselle Crush Absolu is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet the ambery, magnetic new Chanel fragrance — shop now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: your free trial-size pick with any $30 order.`
