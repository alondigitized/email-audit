# Save 20% + get an extra 10% off home with Kohl's Rewards 🏠💚
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's Black Friday deal wall, about 12,000px of stacked category blocks, that has landed all week. The subject sells a home-specific perk: an extra 10% off home with Kohl's Rewards. The render doesn't deliver on it. The top of the email is the generic snowy hero with the "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge, then a category nav and a free-shipping bar. The home message appears only as a small green "Kohl's Rewards members take an extra 10% off your purchase with Home coupon" strip. It shows up under Kitchen Appliances and again lower down, and both are easy to miss.
- Below that it is a scroll of price-led tiles: "Starting at 2.79", "40%", "65%" and "50% + 20% w/coupon". Many tiles carry "select styles" and "coupons do not apply" fine print. The render is clean, with no broken images. The brand is trusted and the deal stack is real. But the email never gets a recipient to the home offer, and the volume of near-identical sends dulls it.
- Judgement: a competent but undifferentiated send. The subject angle is the right idea, but the creative should have been rebuilt around home instead of reusing the all-category wall.

## What's working

- The subject picks a specific segment hook (home plus Rewards) instead of repeating the generic "20% + up to 50%" line from the 11/24 sends.
- The render is clean. Images load, text doesn't overlap, and the Kohl's branding is consistent.
- Price-led tiles with specific numbers ("Starting at 2.79", "65%", "40%") give scannable, concrete value.
- The "Kohl's Rewards" banners acknowledge members, which suits the subject's loyalty framing.
- The Sephora at Kohl's block near the bottom adds a cross-category reason to keep scrolling.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't match the subject. It leads with $15 Kohl's Cash, not the home 10% perk.
- The home perk is a thin green strip buried below the fold. It doesn't match the weight the subject gave it.
- At about 12,000px with dozens of "Shop Now" buttons, there is no single primary CTA and no clear hierarchy.
- Many tiles say "select styles" or "coupons do not apply", and several offers stack (20% off, up to 50% off, Kohl's Cash, Rewards 10%). The offer is hard to read as honest.
- Most of the creative is apparel, toys, beauty, luggage, electronics and Fitbit. Only a few tiles (kitchen appliances, cookware, bath, furniture) are home.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build a home-first version for the segment this subject targets. Put "Extra 10% off home with Rewards" in the hero with one CTA ("Shop Home Deals"). Cut the all-category wall to a short home grid of 6 to 8 tiles, with no apparel or Fitbit.
- 2. Make the offer math explicit and single-threaded: one headline offer, with the code or coupon behavior shown in the hero. Move the fine print out of each tile.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific verb-plus-noun copy such as "Shop Kitchen Appliances" or "Shop Bedding".
- 4. Cut the cadence. This is another near-identical send after the 11/24 "Take 20% off AND save up to 50% on Black Friday Deals 🎉" email, so the email needs a distinct creative idea beyond the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rewards members: extra 10% off home 🏠`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards bonus: 10% off home, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off with coupon on kitchen, bedding & more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen, bath & decor deals, with your Rewards coupon applied.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Save 20% + get an extra 10% off home with Kohl's Rewards"

The audit covers only the truncated HTML source and the automated QA output. Anything after the preheader isn't visible, so the footer, the unsubscribe link and the merge tokens couldn't be checked.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a mobile media query and four tracking pixels. The source has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>` and viewport zoom disabled. Compliance and deliverability findings are unconfirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the footer is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **93 tracking links weren't probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on click-redirect domains such as `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Four tracking beacons in the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/af45e5d2-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, a third-party domain)
- **Malformed tag after the open pixel.** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` ends in a stray `</custom>` that was never opened.
- **No `utm_*` parameters are visible** in the truncated source. The `mi_*` parameters (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1026699_20261125`) look like the sender's own tracking scheme.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** A `<div>` is invalid there, and parsers will close the head early. The result is that `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`, which also has an empty href, and the schema.org meta tags may not be handled as intended.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flagged `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` (all on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`). It also flagged the product images `59991147_2_2x`, `60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. If these carry the content, they are unreadable with images off or to screen readers.
- **Alt-text findings for tracking pixels.**
  - `o.gif` is a QA false positive, because the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
  - The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all. They should have `alt=""`.
- **Invalid CSS.**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width:100% }` is missing its `.` class selector, so it never matches.
  - `max-height: auto` is not a valid value.
  - `td,th,div,p,a {}` in the MSO block is an empty rule.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`.** The preheader table (`width='640'`) and the 1×1 pixel table lack it, while the main layout tables have it.
- **External fonts.** Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import`. The `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack covers clients that strip them, so this is not a defect.
- **`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `robots noindex`** are harmless in email but have no effect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The tail of the document is truncated, so this is not a full clearance.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) weren't found.** QA notes this may be the AgentMail relay stripping them, so it is unconfirmed for the original send. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements (2024) would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` wasn't captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The From domain `s.kohls.com` and the link domains are all `kohls.com` subdomains, which is consistent for alignment.
- **Footer not visible.** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address can't be verified from the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** All 93 links go through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirects, and none were resolved.
- **No `utm_*` parameters are exposed** in the visible source. The campaign identifier appears only as `mi_ecmp=1026699_20261125` and `email_name=261125_DG_Email_Wednes…` on image URLs.
- The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter on `916d6fa08d185233.png` has an empty value. It may be an unpopulated dynamic field, but that is only plausible, not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`. Use `<script type="application/ld+json">` or place it in `<body>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and the product images). Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`, and fill in `<title>`.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, drop `max-height: auto`, and remove the empty `href=""` on the favicon link.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw original message, not the relay copy. Do the same for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check landing pages and `utm_*` retention. The probe was skipped for all 93.
7. Review the footer, which was cut off, for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
8. Add an audit-tool exception so `alt=""` is not flagged as missing alt text (the `o.gif` false positive).
