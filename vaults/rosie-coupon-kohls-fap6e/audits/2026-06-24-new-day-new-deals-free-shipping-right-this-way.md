# New day, new deals + free shipping right this way 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth Deal Days email in roughly 36 hours — and the creative has been copy-pasted almost verbatim from the June 23 pair. The "New day, new deals" framing is the only substantive pivot from yesterday's sends, and it's honest: the countdown timer, the rotating 50%/40%/60% featured deals, and the $7.99/$14.99 price-point grid do represent fresh inventory. But the structural template, the color palette, the Deal Days logo placement, and every CTA are identical to the June 23 pair audited here. Subscribers who opened all three emails are now seeing the same email a fourth time with a different clock.
- The smartest element is the countdown timer — "Check back for new deals in XX:XX:XX" — which gives this send a reason to exist that the prior ones didn't have. It converts Deal Days from "another sale" into a time-sensitive daily mechanic, which is the right conceptual move. The clearance section (up to 75% off) adds a second escalation that the June 23 sends didn't lean into.
- The core problem is cadence exhaustion. A subscriber who opened and clicked on June 23 has already processed this layout twice. The fourth send needs to do something structurally different — a category focus, a "last chance for Day 1 deals" hook, anything — to justify the inbox slot. As-is, this will suppress open rates and accelerate unsubscribes from the segment most likely to actually buy.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer adds genuine daily urgency** — it transforms "new deals" from marketing copy into a mechanical, time-bound reason to act now rather than later.
- **Three featured-deal percentage anchors (50%, 40%, 60%) are high and prominent** — the eye immediately reads "meaningful discount" before reading any product copy.
- **Clearance up to 75% off is a true escalation** — nothing in the June 23 emails went there; this gives deal-hunters a new reason to scroll.
- **Clean render, no visual bugs** — the layout holds across all sections; no broken images or overlapping text.
- **Specific dollar price points ($7.99, $14.99)** anchor value concretely alongside the percentage-off deals.

## What's weak

- **This is the fourth near-identical email in ~36 hours** — the layout, Deal Days logo, color palette, and CTA style are indistinguishable from the June 23 pair. Cadence burnout is real here.
- **"8 DAYS OF DEALS" contradicts the June 23 email's implied 6-day event window** — if the event started June 23, "8 days" means it runs through July 1, but the launch email positioned it shorter; the inconsistency erodes event credibility.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are generic throughout** — not one button names a category ("Shop Intimates," "Shop Sneakers"), so click intent is left entirely to the user.
- **The featured daily deals section shows intimates/lingerie as deal #1** — for a broad-audience send, leading with a bra isn't the strongest universal hook; shoes or home goods would have broader pull.
- **No Kohl's Cash mention visible** — it was presumably part of earlier Deal Days sends and its absence here removes a retention hook that Kohl's loyalists specifically look for.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate the template structurally** — day 2 of a multi-day deal event should look different from day 1. Even swapping the layout orientation (e.g., horizontal deal cards instead of stacked) would signal "this is new."
- 2. **Swap the lead deal** — lead with shoes or home, not intimates. The broader the lead hook, the wider the audience captured before the scroll decision.
- 3. **Make CTAs specific** — change every "Shop Now" to "Shop [Category]" (Shop Sneakers, Shop Kids, Shop Clearance). This one change alone would lift click-through on a multi-deal layout.
- 4. **Resolve the 8-days-vs-6-days confusion** — pick a count and be consistent across all sends; inconsistency makes the event feel improvised.
- 5. **Re-introduce Kohl's Cash** — even a banner line ("Earn Kohl's Cash on every purchase") closes the loyalty loop for returning shoppers.
- 6. **Throttle the cadence** — four emails in 36 hours is the single biggest deliverable change. One send per day maximum during Deal Days.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Today's deal drops: 50% off, 40% off, 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals every day — these are live right now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance just hit 75% off. Free shipping, no code needed.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "New day, new deals + free shipping" (2026-06-24)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a solid HTML 4.01 Transitional/VML foundation with appropriate MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. The main technical concerns are missing alt attributes on tracking pixels, compliance header gaps attributable to the AgentMail relay layer, and a malformed HTML tag in the open-tracking block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped by HTTP probe** — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but redirect chain depth and final landing page UTM fidelity could not be validated.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems in use simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open tracker (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)

Four parallel open-tracking calls per recipient increases page-load overhead in preview pane and may trigger duplicate-open inflation across reporting platforms.

**Malformed closing tag** in the open-tracking block:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
<custom>   ← invalid element; should be </div>
```
The `<custom>` tag is unclosed markup that will cause DOM parsing errors in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). Affected assets include all three open-pixel images and eight content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`:

```
2ab9d941-12bc-40ba-8508-947837721d70 (CoherentPath pixel)
o.gif (MessageGears pixel)
event (Demdex pixel)
2a5adc5afb647aad.png
f441d45d2128ae53.png
c2f81e9ac8daee44.png
415c8a18fc2950ea.png
69494b48a79b4240.png
78308d1d9adb0751.png
916d6fa08d185233.png
```

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (already present on the MessageGears pixel — inconsistent). Content images missing alt text fail WCAG 2.1 AA and render as broken icon + filename in image-blocked clients.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 2.1 AA failure (Success Criterion 1.4.4).

**`<title>` element is empty** — assistive technologies announce the email with no document title.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** for both Roboto and Montserrat — redundant double-load. The `<link>` tags are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`/`<!--<![endif]-->` but the `@import` block is not inside the same conditional, so MSO clients may attempt the `@import` fetch unnecessarily.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `[%email%]`) detected in the truncated source. The MessageGears pixel carries `mi_u=604230016`, indicating a resolved subscriber ID — personalization pipeline appears to have fired correctly for this sample.

One content image URL contains a trailing `&` with no trailing parameter:
```
...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
This suggests an unpopulated dynamic parameter (`mi_kc_redm_end_dte` and `mi_kc_event_pe`) was left as empty string rather than omitted. Depending on the image server's behavior, this could surface broken images for recipients where those fields are null.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** by QA relay. This is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced February 2024) for senders exceeding 5,000/day. May be present upstream and stripped by AgentMail — should be verified against raw SMTP headers at the MTA.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe will not function in Gmail/Apple Mail without this header pair.

**No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated HTML. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) mandates a valid postal address. The full email body was not provided — this should be verified against the complete rendered output, as it is typically in the footer.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present** — correct for suppressing email indexing and referrer leakage.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 outbound links pass through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains. UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs could not be confirmed because HTTP probing of redirect domains was skipped by the QA tool. This should be spot-checked manually for at least the primary CTA links.

The `email_name=260624_DG_Email_Wed_PM` parameter on several image URLs provides a consistent campaign identifier, but it appears only on image tracking URLs — not confirmed on click URLs.
