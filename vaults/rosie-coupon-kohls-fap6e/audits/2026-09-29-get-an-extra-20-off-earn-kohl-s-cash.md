# Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink Kohl's send. The subject promises "an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash." The hero is a bare pink "EXTRA" wordmark with no number and no code. The top of the email is a Cash badge and a Women/Men/Kids/Home button grid. Below that is a "Clearance up to 70%" banner, a LEGO Extravaganza block, a Top Toys block, a denim block and a product grid. The 20% code (YOUSAVE20) only appears in the barcode stack at the very bottom, so the headline promise is barely visible where you'd expect to see it.
- It is nearly a rerun of yesterday's 09-28 "Extra 20% off savings + earn Kohl's Cash ✨". The subject is the same with an emoji swapped, and the body is the same stack of coupons and category blocks. A subscriber who has seen ten near-identical sends this week will skim it. The toy and LEGO content is the freshest part, since "Top Toys you'll be glad you got early" has a seasonal hook.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, but the hierarchy is muddy. About six competing sections fight for the click, and the actual mechanics of the offer sit in a footer.

## What's working

- The Kohl's brand is instantly recognizable, and the "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member" banner reinforces loyalty.
- The LEGO Extravaganza and "Top Toys… early" blocks add a seasonal, holiday-lead-in angle. The "$10 off $50 toy purchase with coupon" banner is concrete.
- The product tiles are specific and priced, such as the Ninja Slushi at $349.99 and the Serta topper marked down from $229.99, so there is real price anchoring.
- The coupon stack at the bottom (Kohl's Cash, home, kids, toys) gives clear barcodes with "Ends Oct. 4" dates.
- The render is clean, with no overlaps or broken images.

## What's weak

- The "EXTRA" hero states no offer. The 20% value, the code and the deadline are all missing above the fold.
- The email carries too many competing modules: clearance, LEGO, toys, denim, a denim guide and a picks grid. There is no single dominant CTA.
- The CTA copy is generic ("Shop Now" repeated), and the four top buttons are plain category labels.
- The essential terms are buried. The code and expiry are at the bottom, and the top preheader-style line is tiny grey text that's hard to read.
- The denim block ("Denim in Demand") has nothing to do with the toy and LEGO story, so the message splits in two.
- The cadence is stale, with a subject that nearly duplicates yesterday's.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Extra 20% off — code YOUSAVE20 — ends Oct 4" with a single "Shop with 20% off" button. Move the barcode and coupon logic up.
- 2. Cut the email to one theme, LEGO/toys or clearance, with denim held for a separate send. Fewer modules would lift clicks more than any copy change.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs like "Shop LEGO sets" or "Get $10 off toys".
- 4. Add a real deadline to the subject and hero, since "Ends Oct 4" is currently only in the footer.
- 5. Vary the subject week to week so it doesn't duplicate 09-28.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% code is inside 🎁`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The Kohl's email has several code-level problems: 1×1 tracking pixels with no alt text, a malformed closing tag, an empty `<title>`, and a duplicated viewport setting that blocks zoom. The QA run couldn't confirm the unsubscribe headers, authentication results, or a physical address. The source was truncated, so anything below the preheader is unverified.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped 67 tracking or click-redirect links (HTTP probe skipped), so none of their destinations were checked. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMTR9...`.
- There are four separate tracking or open pixels before the first content block:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b0d55c57-...` (1×1, no `alt`, not inside a hidden container)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?TN2ORR7C2M5UVBN3OW4IHE5WPE.60263`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- Multiple open trackers add redundant third-party requests. The demdex call adds a cross-domain request to a third party.
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request back to the current document URL in some clients.
- The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` tags do nothing in email clients.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` ends with a stray `</custom>` closing tag that has no matching opening tag. This is invalid HTML and can break parsing in strict clients.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients and screen readers use the title as the accessible document name.
- **Zoom blocked:** the viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which stops pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern for low-vision users.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged at least 10 images with no `alt` attribute, and the list was cut off. The flagged images are:
  - The pixels: `b0d55c57-...`, `o.gif`, `event`.
  - Seven content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`.
  - The pixels should have `alt=""`. The content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`) need descriptive `alt`, or the offer text is lost when images are blocked.
- **Misplaced markup:** the `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` block sits inside `<head>`. That is invalid, and browsers will move it into `<body>`. It also uses `http://` for the schema URL.
- **Font loading:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` tags are guarded with `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and Outlook ignore them, so they fall back to Arial. This isn't a defect, but the web fonts only load in some clients.
- **Legacy CSS:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its `.` class selector, so it never applies.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables have `role="presentation"`. The tracking-pixel tables do not, but they are 1×1, so the effect is minor.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[[...]]` appear in the visible source. The recipient identifiers (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`) are in the tracking URLs. The source is truncated, so I can't check the body or footer.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a definite defect.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found.** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No physical mailing address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one. The source was truncated, so the footer wasn't visible. I can't tell whether the address is missing or just cut off.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be verified because of the relay. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the tracking domain `click.s.kohls.com`, which is consistent with a properly configured sender, but that isn't proof.
- The visible-body unsubscribe link wasn't in the captured portion.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Every link seen goes through a click-redirect wrapper, `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML. The redirect destinations were never resolved.
- Image URLs carry the campaign identifiers `mi_ecmp=1022726_2026929` and `email_name=260929_DG_Email_Tuesday`. Only the campaign IDs are visible. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` values appear in the captured source.
- One image URL is cut off at `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...`. `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is an empty parameter, so the Kohl's Cash redemption end date is not populated in that image request. This may mean a dynamic-content field failed to render.
- Landing-page alignment for "extra 20% off + Kohl's Cash" can't be verified without following the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix the malformed markup.** Remove the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>` or replace it with valid markup.
2. **Add alt text.** Set `alt=""` on the three tracking pixels. Add descriptive `alt` on the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and include the offer text (for example "Extra 20% off").
3. **Remove the empty `<title>`.** Populate it with the subject line.
4. **Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to allow pinch-zoom.
5. **Check `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`.** It is empty in at least one image URL. Confirm that the Kohl's Cash expiry date is being populated.
6. **Consolidate the open-tracking pixels.** Four pixels are redundant, so keep only the ones that are needed.
7. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML and raw headers.** Capture `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address directly from the original message, not the AgentMail relay. Follow the click-tracking redirects for a sample of the 67 links to verify UTM parameters and landing pages.
8. **Clean up the head.** Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector (add the `.` prefix), and either populate or remove `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
