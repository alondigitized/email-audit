# STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a points-multiplier send, and the render leads with it. A huge red "2X" with "INSIDER" under it is the first thing the eye lands on. "Earn 2X points on your entire purchase and 5X on all Sephora Collection" is easy to get at a glance. The product grid underneath ("Earn 2X points on top picks") shows Tom Ford at 810 points, Armani Beauty at 348 and Summer Fridays at 48, which is a clever way to make points feel tangible.
- The email then dilutes itself. After the points story it adds a Cardmembers "Earn Even Faster" block, a Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal "Shop now, pay later" strip, a "Hair Faves up to 50% off" banner (TODAY ONLY), two editorial tiles, a gifting block, and a very long legal footer. It reads as five emails stacked together. Compare the 9/22 send, "4X points on fragrance (yes way!)", which stayed in one lane. This one is a solid loyalty push with too many competing asks, and the repeated "USE CODE: SAYPOINTS" gating adds friction.
- The subject line is the weakest piece. It is long and all-caps up front, and the 5X Sephora Collection detail would be cut off on mobile.

## What's working

- The giant "2X" hero is unmistakable and sits above the fold.
- Each product tile shows its point value ("EARN 810 points", "EARN 348 points"), which makes the loyalty offer concrete.
- Products are shown with price, so you can compare the price against the points payoff.
- The "Ends 10/7" deadline and the "STARTS TODAY" banner give credible urgency.
- The "SAYPOINTS" code appears under every product, so the redemption path is consistent. The "Cardmembers Earn Even Faster" block gives that segment an extra reason to engage.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- The email is too long and carries too many secondary stories: pay-later, Hair Faves 50% off, editorial tiles, gifting and a Beauty Insider promo. They compete with the points hero.
- "Use code: SAYPOINTS" repeated under every product, plus a second code in the Same-Day Delivery P.S., makes the offer feel like homework.
- The heavy fine print and exclusions (the footer is a wall of tiny text) undercut how honest the offer feels. The 5X offer also isn't explained until you read the body.
- The hair banner ("Up to 50% off Hair Faves – TODAY ONLY") arrives after the points hero and muddies which offer matters. This is the same two-headed structure as the 9/29 "From your feed to your cart" send.
- The subject runs about 87 characters and starts in ALL CAPS, which reads shouty and truncates early on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the 2X/5X hero and the product grid, and move the pay-later strip, hair banner and editorial tiles to a different send.
- 2. Put the code in the hero button only, or auto-apply it. One "Shop Sephora Collection" button would beat a code repeated on every tile.
- 3. Add a 5X Sephora Collection product row. The subject promises it, but the body leads with high-ticket 2X items like Tom Ford, so the Collection tie-in is thin.
- 4. Move the key terms into a short line under the hero instead of burying them in the footer.
- 5. Add a clear deadline in the hero ("Ends Oct 7").

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your points just doubled (through Oct 7)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS: 2X on everything, 5X on Sephora Collection, through 10/7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Insiders: top picks earn up to 810 points. Ends 10/7.`
