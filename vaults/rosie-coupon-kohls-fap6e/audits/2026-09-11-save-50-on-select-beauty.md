# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's "50% off Kiehl's/Bobbi Brown/Azzaro" flash hero, glued onto a much bigger Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar catalog underneath — the same two-emails-welded-into-one pattern this brand has run all week (9/6, 9/7, 9/9). The top third is legible and does its job: a black hero with "50% off° Select Beauty," a "TODAY ONLY" badge, and a clear "SHOP NOW" button naming three specific brands. That's honestly the strongest, most specific top-of-funnel this brand has sent this week — Kiehl's/Bobbi Brown/Azzaro is more concrete than the generic "select beauty" language from 9/7 and 9/9.
- Where it falls apart is scroll depth: the moment you clear the hero, it's a full rewards catalog (100-point rewards, 250-point rewards, "Spot it. Shop it.", holiday drops teaser) with no more urgency or above-the-fold coherence — a loyalty program page stapled to a flash sale. For a non-VIB/Rouge subscriber, most of this scroll is irrelevant filler between the deal and the footer.
- Given the brand's recent run (9/9 at 5.00, 9/10 at 8.00), this lands closer to the 9/9 execution — decent hero, bloated body — worth a mid-range score.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: black-on-white "50% off° Select Beauty" with a "TODAY ONLY" starburst and a single "SHOP NOW" CTA button.
- Named brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro) give the offer a concrete, shoppable feel instead of vague "select beauty."
- Clean, on-brand red/black/white Sephora palette throughout — no color clashes or off-brand styling.
- No render bugs: images load, text doesn't overlap, no lorem ipsum or broken layout blocks.
- Rewards catalog section is well-organized with clear product shots, names, and point values (100/250-point tiers).

## What's weak

- The email is essentially two campaigns bolted together — the flash 50% sale ends after one screen, then it pivots hard into a full Rewards Bazaar catalog with zero connective tissue.
- "Exclusions/terms apply*" sits directly under the offer with no detail on what's excluded — the classic fine-print-adjacent-to-headline pattern that erodes trust.
- Repeating ribbon text "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" tiled across a banner reads as filler/spam-adjacent rather than a real CTA.
- No visible loyalty tier context up top (I don't know if I'm INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) even though the whole back half of the email is point-redemption content gated by tier.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the 6th Sephora promo this week with the same red/black flash-sale hero formula, just re-skinned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: the flash 50% off deal should stand alone and end with reinforcement of the offer (countdown, brand grid, or "shop these 3 brands" tiles) — not fall straight into a loyalty catalog. That's the single highest-leverage fix; right now half the email cannibalizes the other half's attention.
- 2. Move the tier badge (INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) to the top so the rewards section below feels personalized instead of generic.
- 3. Replace the repeating "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" ribbon with a single, real subhead — it currently reads as filler, not persuasion.
- 4. State what's excluded from the 50% off in-line, not just "Exclusions/terms apply*" — even one clause ("excludes fragrance sets") builds more trust than a bare asterisk.
- 5. Given five near-identical 50%-off sends in a week, rotate the creative format entirely for the next send (e.g., a single-brand spotlight) to reset fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown & Azzaro: 50% off today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off is live — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new Rewards Bazaar drops you've already earned`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the flash sale, then redeem 100+ points below`
