# Kohl's Deal Days + Kohl's Cash are here today, gone tomorrow 👋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seventh Deal Days-era send in about four days, and it's a long product grid with a slightly different headline. Subject and body are consistent: "today, gone tomorrow" matches the "Ends October 8" banners. The page is a tidy stack of discount tiles (30%, 40%, 40%, 25%, 40%, 50%, 25%, 40%) with "Shop Now" under each, plus Kohl's Cash and Rewards callouts.
- The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can only read the large type. The hero area under the header looks nearly empty, with only a small "Deal" mark and no clear headline. The strongest message therefore doesn't land until the "Today's Featured Daily Deals" block. After that it's a kitchen-sink scroll with no priority. Compared with the 10-05 send, which had a bold magenta "Up to 50% off" hero, this one gives the eye no single place to land.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand and clean, but fatigued. It repeats the 10-07 "New Daily Deals ✅ Free Shipping ✅ Kohl's Cash ✅" email almost one-for-one.

## What's working

- "Ends October 8" appears in several places, and "gone tomorrow" in the subject matches it, so the urgency is credible.
- Each tile has a big percent-off number, a category and a "Shop Now" link, so the offers are easy to scan.
- Kohl's Rewards members get a $10-off-$50 home callout, and Kohl's Cash is mentioned, so loyalty benefits are visible.
- The "Picked with you in mind" block shows real prices (e.g. $229.99 Ninja SLUSH, $52 jeans), which gives some product-level specifics.
- The Shop Now, Clearance (up to 80% off) and free-returns block gives a reason to keep scrolling.

## What's weak

- The top hero looks nearly blank, so the email has no headline offer above the fold.
- The email is about 8,400px tall, with eight near-identical tiles and a clearance section after that, so no one offer stands out.
- Every tile carries "Coupons do not apply to select brands" or "Select styles". That repeated exclusion language weakens trust in the offers.
- "Shop Now" is the CTA copy on every tile. It names no category and carries no urgency.
- The creative and cadence closely repeat the 10-06 and 10-07 sends, so readers will find nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build a real hero: one bold line such as "Ends tomorrow: up to 50% off + $10 Kohl's Cash", with a single primary button, so the top of the email carries the offer.
- 2. Cut the 8-tile grid to 3–4 of the best deals and move the rest to a "See all Daily Deals" link.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category CTAs like "Shop Nike socks" or "Shop kitchen deals".
- 4. Say the exclusions once, in one footnote, instead of repeating them on every tile.
- 5. Use the 10-08 "last day" angle to differentiate this send from the near-identical 10-07 one, and consider holding it to one send per day.

## Technical Audit: Kohl's "Deal Days + Kohl's Cash" email

### 1. Technical Summary
The template is a table-based layout with a mobile breakpoint, but it has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` placed inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>`, and an empty icon `href`. It also carries four separate open-tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication could not be confirmed because the relay dropped the headers and the source is truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 click-tracking links were not probed.** They go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=<opaque token>`, so destination URLs can't be checked from the source.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5f7e5480-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023415_2026108` (1x1)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?25A3HQ3ISP7ENKKX57QZS526EE.60266&d=60266&bmt=0` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (0x0)
  
  These add request overhead and give inflated or duplicate open counts. The `demdex.net` call is a third-party Adobe Audience Manager endpoint.
- **Recipient and campaign identifiers sit in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` and `email_name=261008_DG_Email_Thursda…` are in the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs, so they are in plain text for any proxy or forwarded copy.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opener. That's invalid markup, and some parsers may mishandle it.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text, 12 images flagged by QA:**
  - **Tracking pixels (4):** the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` images have no `alt` attribute at all. The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. QA flags it anyway, which looks like a false positive, since empty alt is correct for decorative pixels. Add `alt=""` to the first two.
  - **Content images (8):** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x` were flagged, but the truncated source doesn't show their alt attributes. Some or all may be missing real alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is inside `<head>`.** That's invalid, and parsers will close the head at that point.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** has an empty `href`.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`** on the preheader table (`<table width='640' … class="container">`) and on the 1x1 tracking tables. Other layout tables have it.
- **CSS typo:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches nothing. The Outlook block also has an empty rule, `td,th,div,p,a {}`.
- **Fixed 640px width against a 500px breakpoint.** The preheader table has `width: 640px`, but the mobile override only applies at `max-width: 500px`. Viewports of 501–640px may scroll horizontally.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded for non-Outlook clients only, and the font stack has `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks. No issue.

### 7. Recommendations
