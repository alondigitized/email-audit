# Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a rewards-catalog email, not a Savings Event email, even though the sender name says "Sephora Savings Event." The hero is "November means new rewards!" under a Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar banner. Below it sit two grids of redeemable products: a 100-point tier (Saie, Huda Beauty, Moroccanoil, Dior, Phlur, Fenty, AKT London, Gisou) and a 250-point tier (Byredo, Moroccanoil). The 10% off "ALLONSALE" code is only a small badge at the top, and a "free gift with purchase" promo sits further down.
- The subject, "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle," is the weakest part. "0 points" tells the reader they have nothing to spend, and the 250-point bundle is a minor module near the bottom, not the hero. On the 11/5 send, "10% off our most-searched beauty" also didn't match its body. The mismatch is the same kind of problem here. The body is well built, but the subject undersells the 10% offer and sets up a pitch the email doesn't lead with.
- One caveat on this review: the render is about 7,900px tall and was shown to me heavily downscaled. Fine print, small text and the preheader are partly unreadable, so my reads of those are approximate. Overall it is a competent catalog email with a mismatched, awkward subject. It is the seventh or so Sephora send in the last week.

## What's working

- The 100-point and 250-point tiers are clearly labeled with large "100 POINT REWARDS" and "250 POINT REWARDS" headers, so the grids scan quickly.
- Each product cell has the brand, product name, point cost and an "ONLINE ONLY" tag. The structure is consistent and uncluttered.
- The "ALLONSALE" 10% code with the 11/3–11/9 window is visible near the top, so the offer and deadline are credible.
- The in-store barcode at the bottom gives a redemption path for the 11/3–11/9 sale.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." module, the Kérastase feature and the holiday gift guide add category variety for a beauty shopper.

## What's weak

- The subject centers on "0 points," which tells the reader they have nothing to spend. That is a discouraging hook.
- The 250-point bundle in the subject is a small block near the bottom of the email. The hero is a generic "new rewards" message.
- The top of the email stacks several competing messages: the Insider Challenge, the 10% code and a free-delivery banner. No single offer owns the first screen.
- The email is extremely long, with several competing modules and dense legal text. The 10% offer is easy to lose among the catalog.
- No product in the grids is individually featured or tied to a review or rating. The reward grid feels like a menu, not a recommendation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the subject and the hero on the same offer. If the send is about rewards, lead with a real reward the reader can afford. If it is about the 10% event, move the code up and make it the hero.
- 2. Cut the module count. Pick one rewards message and one sale message, and drop the Kérastase and holiday gift-guide blocks from this send.
- 3. Feature one hero reward, such as the Dior or Byredo item, with a larger image and a specific button like "Redeem Miss Dior for 100 points."
- 4. Show the reader's real point balance in the first screen if it is above zero. Segment so that readers with zero points get the 10% code instead.
- 5. Shorten the legal block, or collapse it behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend 100 points on a Dior or Fenty full-size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New rewards are in: 10% off ends 11/9`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick from 12 new Insider rewards, from 100 points. Use code ALLONSALE before 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn up to 500 points with the Insider Challenge, and take 10% off through Nov 9.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The tracking pixel `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...'>` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). It is hidden, so the impact is low, but adding `alt=""` would clear the check.
- **No plain-text part:** The text version is 0 chars (QA FAIL). Clients that render plain text, and spam filters that score the text/HTML ratio, get nothing.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The space makes the value invalid. It is harmless in email clients.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block of the `<style>` tag. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS, so the MSO sizing rule likely won't apply and may break the rules that follow it.
- **Fixed width:** The outer table is `width:700; min-width:700px`. On narrow viewports this forces horizontal scroll unless the client rescales the email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Unused JSON-LD:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type with no properties. Gmail won't use either, so they are dead markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting:** The greeting renders as "Hi ," with the name field empty (QA FAIL). The slot is not guarded by a fallback.
- **Points value:** The subject is "Use your **0** points on a 250-point bundle". The points value rendered as 0, while the preheader says "Get three 100-point rewards for just 250 points." The 0 is probably a default or null value for a recipient with no loyalty data. Because the name is also empty, the likely cause is the recipient record, not a template bug. Either way, the subject can show a misleading value with no fallback.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found (QA WARN). The relay may not capture it, so it is unconfirmed, not proven missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found (QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is expected through the AgentMail relay and can't be assessed here.
- **Footer links:** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are outside the truncated source and weren't verified. The QA probe says an unsubscribe link was included in the probed links, with no failure reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The source is truncated and every link is wrapped in an ESP redirect, so there is no visible evidence of UTM parameters or the final landing URLs. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the name token (e.g. "Hi there,") and suppress or alter the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Guard the points token in the subject. If the value is 0 or null, use a non-points subject variant.
3. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.
4. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw message at the sending MTA. Check the SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment of `beauty.sephora.com` from the original headers, not the relay copy.
5. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block into its own `<style>` block.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and fix `content="noindex"`.
7. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
8. Re-run link probing on the 40 skipped links. Confirm that the redirect destinations carry UTM parameters.
