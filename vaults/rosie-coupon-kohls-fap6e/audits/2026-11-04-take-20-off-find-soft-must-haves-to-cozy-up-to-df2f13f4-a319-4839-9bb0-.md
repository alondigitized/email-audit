# Take 20% off + find soft must-haves to cozy up to 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's twelfth near-identical send since Oct 26, and the subject's "Take 20% off" is not what the render leads with. The top is a rewards status bar ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards"), then a "Shop Now" button and four category buttons: Home, Women, Men, Kids. The first hero is a holiday brand banner, "Holidays just like you remember. (Maybe even better.)", with kitchen appliances starting at $15.99 "with 20% off of coupon". The 20% appears only in fine print.
- The "soft must-haves" angle does appear, in the second block: "Cozy must-haves that say 'just one more movie.'" It shows throws from $11.99 and matching family pajamas at "25% + 20% off w/coupon". That block carries the subject's promise, but it sits well below the fold. The rest follows the usual Kohl's pattern: a gift guide, "Free returns through January 31, 2027", $5 Kohl's Cash, and same-day delivery. It ends with a product grid and barcode coupon, "Take an extra 20% off", code SAVEBIG, ending Nov. 5.
- Judgement: a competent, busy, mid-funnel promo. It has real price points but no single focus. It repeats the Nov 2–3 beats, though the last-day urgency (ends Nov. 5) is only mentioned at the bottom.

## What's working

- Concrete price points are visible: $15.99 appliances, $11.99 throws, 25% + 20% off pajamas.
- The cozy throw and pajama block matches the "soft must-haves" subject and is seasonally right.
- The rewards status bar and the "Take an extra 20% off" barcode coupon with a code and an end date make redemption easy.
- The Shop Now buttons are clear maroon, with a consistent brand look.
- The "Free returns through January 31, 2027" and "Free & Easy Store Pickup" panel adds some honest reassurance.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer in the subject is not the hero. The first screen is a nav bar, category buttons and a holiday brand banner.
- The email is very long, with at least six competing sections: appliances, throws, pajamas, gift shop, delivery, and "Picked with you in mind" products. Nothing stands out as the one offer.
- The offer is hard to read. "$15.99 with 20% off of coupon" and "25% + 20% off w/coupon" are stacked, and the exclusions are in dense fine print.
- The Ninja SLUSHI at $249.99 and the $229–$349 mattress topper don't fit the "cozy" theme. The "Picked with you in mind" block does not read as personalized.
- The end date (Nov. 5) is only at the bottom, so urgency is weak. This is the same 20% message sent for the twelfth time.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero: the cozy throws and pajamas, with "20% off" in large type and "Ends Nov. 5" next to it. Move the holiday brand banner and appliances lower.
- 2. Cut the gift guide, delivery and product-grid blocks, or collapse them into a single row of links. Aim for about half the length.
- 3. Move the barcode or code (SAVEBIG) near the top and put a deadline on it.
- 4. Make the offers plainer: show the final price after the coupon, not "Reg. / with 20% off of coupon".
- 5. Make the "Picked with you in mind" block truly personalized, or retitle it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with four separate tracking or open pixels and about 68 wrapped click links. The visible source has several markup problems (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, missing alt text) and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** The QA run skipped all 68 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Redundant open and tracking pixels:** There are four pixels before any content:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/5c6848b9-...` (1x1, no `alt`)
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016...`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (`width="0" height="0"`, no `alt`)

  Four vendors fire on open: Coherent Path, Movable Ink, Salesforce, and Adobe AAM. This adds request weight and gives conflicting open counts.
- **Subscriber ID in URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` is in the query string of the Movable Ink image URLs. It will appear in any forwarded copy or proxy log.
- **Stray tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` has a closing `</custom>` with no matching opener. It is malformed and may disrupt parsing in strict clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged 10 images. Two of these look like false positives:
  - `o.gif` and the `open.aspx` pixel already have `alt=""`, which is valid for decorative images. The `o.gif` one also has `aria-hidden="true"`.
  - The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixels truly have no `alt` attribute.
  - Seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) are flagged. These look like content images, so missing `alt` would matter. I can't confirm each from the truncated source.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization"><meta itemprop=...></div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will move it into `<body>`, so it won't work as intended.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Ineffective CSS:** The selector `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches nothing.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via `<link>` and `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, but a fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is present. This is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. The truncated body means this is only partly verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA output itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. For bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo, one-click unsubscribe is required.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These aren't visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. Links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` and `click.chp.kohls.com`, and the destination URLs and any UTM parameters are encoded or truncated. The only campaign identifier visible is `email_name=261104_DG_Email_Wednesd...` on Movable Ink image URLs. No landing-page resolution was tested, because the QA run skipped redirect probing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the invalid markup: remove `</custom>`, move the `schema.org` block out of `<head>`, and remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta, and give the email a real `<title>`.
4. Consolidate open and tracking pixels where possible, and keep the subscriber ID `mi_u` out of image URLs if you can.
5. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector (`.ReadMsgBody`) or remove it.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also run a redirect probe on a sample of the 68 tracked links to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
