# LAST DAY to shop the LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON ⏳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the campaign closer for Kohl's "Lowest Prices of the Season" series that started June 1. The "LAST DAY" subject finally has real teeth — the Kohl's Cash earn deadline printed as "through 6/7" in the footer actually validates the urgency claim for the first time in the series. But the creative is copy-paste identical to the June 6 send: same hero image, same clearance badge, same waterfall of category tiles with "Shop Now" buttons. If a subscriber opened the last three emails, they have already seen this email.
- The email structure is competent — hero leads with the 85% clearance badge, product tiles give category breadth, the Kohl's Cash loyalty block closes the loop. The ALL-CAPS double-barrel subject ("LAST DAY" + "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON") is the biggest unforced error: it reads like a panic broadcast, likely trips spam heuristics, and undercuts the otherwise credible "last day" moment.
- Verdict: a functional campaign closer that punches below its weight. The urgency is genuine for the first time all week; the execution doesn't use it.

## What's working

- "LAST DAY" + timer emoji is the strongest subject hook in the series since the June 1 launch — and it's actually true this time, which the Kohl's Cash "through 6/7" footer confirms.
- The 85% clearance badge in the hero still carries visual weight as the single highest-value number on screen.
- Kohl's Cash earn section at the bottom creates a second, loyalty-specific reason to transact today that's distinct from the clearance pitch.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no layout gaps, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Double ALL-CAPS in the subject ("LAST DAY" and "LOWEST PRICES OF THE SEASON") stacks spam signals; one CAPS phrase would be enough.
- Fourteen-plus "Shop Now" buttons across undifferentiated tiles is analysis paralysis — there's no primary CTA driving to the highest-converting category.
- Creative is visually identical to the June 6 send; a subscriber who opened that email is looking at a duplicate.
- No personalization anywhere — not name, not category affinity, nothing to distinguish this from a blast.
- "$6.99" price callout buried mid-scroll is the most specific number in the email but is visually orphaned with no product name visible at this render size.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill one ALL-CAPS block in the subject.** The double-barrel triggers spam filters and reads as shouting. "Last Day:" as a prefix, sentence case for the rest.
- 2. **Pick one hero CTA and make it category-specific.** Replace the generic hero "Shop Now" with something like "Shop Clearance" or the single highest-performing tile. Reserve "Shop Now" for secondary tiles.
- 3. **Add a countdown timer or hours-remaining callout** in the hero — "Ends tonight at midnight" turns the abstract urgency into something a subscriber can feel.
- 4. **Differentiate the hero image from the June 6 send.** Even swapping to a flat-lay product image versus the couple photo signals "new email."
- 5. **Personalize the opening line** — even "[First name], today's the day" in the preheader moves the needle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Day: up to 85% off clearance ends tonight ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — Kohl's clearance + Kohl's Cash deadline`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kohl's Cash earning window closes tonight too — $10 for every $50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 85% off clearance + earn Kohl's Cash — ends 6/7`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Lowest Prices of the Season" (2026-06-07)

### 1. Technical Summary

A large-scale promotional send built on a standard MSO-compatible table layout with three separate tracking pixel systems active simultaneously. No critical rendering failures detected, but compliance gaps and missing alt attributes on tracking pixels are confirmed issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Three concurrent tracking systems firing on open:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath engagement pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/` — Messageinfo open tracker (`mi_u=604230016`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — Kohl's ESP open pixel (Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**22 click links unprobed** — all pass through tracking redirects (likely `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be validated.

**Malformed HTML tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."> </custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is a stray/invalid element immediately after the third open-pixel `<img>`. This is a code generation artifact and may cause parser warnings in strict validators.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 tracking/pixel images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA):

| Image | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `d09ca1c2...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | CoherentPath pixel |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Messageinfo open pixel |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | Adobe AAM sync |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Reporting pixels |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Reporting pixels |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly, not omit the attribute — screen readers may attempt to announce the src URL in its absence.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, an accessibility barrier:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**`<title>` element is empty** — some email clients surface the title as a preview fallback; leaving it blank wastes that slot and may cause accessibility tool confusion.

**`<html lang="en">` is present** — no issue.

**MSO VML namespaces declared** — consistent with Outlook rendering requirements, no issue.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address not detected** (QA WARN): No postal address found in the visible HTML. Required by 15 U.S.C. § 7704(a)(5). This may be present in the truncated footer — must be verified against full source.

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN): Not captured by the AgentMail relay. Major ESPs (Gmail, Yahoo) use this header to surface the unsubscribe button in the client chrome. Its absence increases spam-folder risk and violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (>5K/day).

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** (QA WARN): RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policy since February 2024.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Sending domain is `s.kohls.com`; authentication posture cannot be confirmed from available data.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` is present** — correct for email HTML, prevents accidental indexing.
