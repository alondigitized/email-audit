# 20% off savings you can't miss 😃 Plus, $20 off your $100+ purchase 🥰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, coupon-forward Kohl's send that is long and sprawling. The top banner does the work. "20% off with coupon" sits next to "$20 off your $100+ purchase," with a Kohl's Cash strip and a "Rewards Member, Earn 5%" status line below it. Both offers have clear numbers and an "Ends Oct. 18" deadline, which is a longer window than the last few sends.
- Below the banner the email splits its attention. A Halloween home hero (Mickey pumpkin, "Up to 40% + save with coupon"), a $10 Rewards offer, fall and Christmas decor tiles, a Season's Best Shoe Sale and a four-item "Picked with you in mind" grid all compete. The coupon block near the bottom, with barcodes and codes YOUGET20, EXTRA20 and 10HOME, is the most useful part. It is buried at the bottom, after a lot of scrolling.
- Compared with the 10-09 "Extra 20% off + $20 off your $100+ purchase 🎉" send, this is the same offer in a different layout, and it follows closely behind the Deal Days run (10-06 through 10-08). A subscriber sees the same 20%-off-plus-$20 message again. The email executes the offer well but adds nothing new, and the cadence is the weakest part.

## What's working

- The top banner puts both offers in large type with "Ends Oct. 18" visible at a glance.
- The Rewards status line and the "$10 off $50+ home" offer give members a visible benefit.
- The email is seasonal: Halloween, fall, Christmas and a shoe sale all fit the date.
- The coupon block at the bottom lists codes, PINs and expiry dates. A shopper can copy them without hunting.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- Too many competing heroes: home, shoes, a Rewards offer and a product grid. The eye has no single path.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" or "Shop Fall." None is a verb plus a specific product.
- The best offer details (codes and PINs) are at the very bottom of a roughly 5,700px email.
- Several offers stack with different thresholds and exclusions ("Coupons do not apply to select brands"). The dense fine print at the bottom undercuts the "savings you can't miss" promise.
- This is another near-identical 20%-off-plus-$20 send within days of the Deal Days run, so urgency and novelty are low.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero (the 20% off + $20 off banner) and move the coupon codes directly under it. Drop the shoe sale block or send it separately.
- 2. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Get 20% off home decor" or "Shop Halloween décor under $30," instead of repeated "Shop Now."
- 3. Lead the subject with the concrete deadline, since "Ends Oct. 18" is the only real urgency lever here.
- 4. Space out the sends. A tighter, differentiated cadence matters more than any single email's copy.
- 5. Make the preheader about the coupon or the Rewards perk, and remove the "view in browser" tail.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off + $20 off $100 — ends Oct. 18`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your coupon: 20% off today, plus $20 off $100+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOUGET20 and Kohl's Rewards for an extra $10 off home.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Halloween decor, fall bedding and shoes up to 40% off through Oct. 18.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a mobile media query, `lang="en"`, and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables. The source has four stacked tracking pixels, malformed markup, and widespread missing `alt` text. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations unverified.** 67 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Examples are `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUw…` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. The `qs` tokens are opaque, so destinations, redirect chains, and expiry can't be validated.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/df4baf99-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=…`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?YXSM243IRQ…&d=60265&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Redundant opens.** Three separate open-tracking beacons (`click.chp`, `mi.kohls.com`, `click.s`) add requests and make open metrics inconsistent. Apple MPP and image-blocking clients will skew all of them.
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` closes a `<custom>` element that was never opened. It looks like a leftover ESP template artifact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes.**
  - The QA check lists at least 10 images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `58379268_2_2x`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, plus pixels.
  - The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` files are Movable Ink dynamic content images. The `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x` file looks like a product image.
  - With images blocked, or for screen readers, these show nothing or announce the filename.
  - The QA list is truncated, so there may be more.
- **Pixel images.** The first 1×1 pixel (`click.chp…`) has no `alt` and no `aria-hidden`. The `mi.kohls.com/p/up/…/o.gif` pixel does have `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, but the QA tool still flags it. The `kohls.demdex.net` pixel has no `alt`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Clients and screen readers that use the title get nothing.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on mobile clients that honor it.
- **Empty icon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the root element carries `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` attributes. This is common in email and harmless, but not valid.
- **Web fonts.** Google Fonts are loaded through `<link>` and `@import`. They are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded. Gmail and some other clients strip them, and the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback covers that. No issue beyond that.
- **Misplaced markup in `<head>`.** The `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will usually hoist it into `<body>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, and `mi_mid` values are populated. The source is truncated, so the footer and later sections weren't reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Header warnings are likely relay artifacts.** The QA tool reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), or `Authentication-Results`. Its own notes say these may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed either way. Check them against raw headers in the receiving mailbox. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Physical address not found.** The QA tool did not detect a postal address. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Re-run on the full source.
- **Visible unsubscribe link not confirmed.** It would be in the footer, which isn't in the provided source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters confirmed.** None are visible in the provided source. The only visible attribution is Movable Ink and ESP parameters: `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1023493_2026109`, and `email_name=261009_DG_Email_Fri_PM`.
- **Landing-page alignment unchecked.** Campaign-level `utm_*` values, if any, are inside the opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` tokens. The skipped HTTP probes mean the landing pages for the "20% off" and "$20 off $100+" offers weren't checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the Movable Ink `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` files and `58379268_2_2x`. Add `alt=""` to every tracking pixel.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Fill in `<title>`, and either remove or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Consolidate open-tracking pixels. Keep one ESP pixel and the Movable Ink pixel, and review whether the `click.chp` and Demdex pixels are both needed.
5. Move the `schema.org` Organization `<div>` out of `<head>`, or remove it.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer for the postal address and unsubscribe link. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` from the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracked links in a real browser to confirm the final landing URLs, `utm_*` parameters, and that the offer matches the subject.
