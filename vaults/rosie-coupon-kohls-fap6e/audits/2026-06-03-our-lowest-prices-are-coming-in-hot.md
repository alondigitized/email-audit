# Our LOWEST prices are coming in hot 🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's third consecutive "Lowest Prices of the Season" send in three days — the campaign opened strong on June 1 with "STARTS TODAY: Low, low prices & up to 85% off clearance" (8/10), and this email is running the same creative playbook without meaningfully advancing it. The hero banner is clean and the 85% clearance badge is the most credible signal in the email, but a subscriber who opened Tuesday already knows the positioning.
- The body is a department-store dump: seven or eight category sections stack vertically with identical "Shop Now" button copy across all of them, no priority ordering, and no single editorial choice about what this subscriber should care about. The Father's Day gift section buried near the bottom is the lone fresh seasonal hook — and it's so deep in the email that most readers never reach it.
- **Judgment:** Competent but fatigued. The brand is leaning on a campaign hook that already peaked two days ago, and the multi-category structure prevents any real urgency from landing.

## What's working

- **85% clearance badge** in the hero provides a concrete, specific anchor — the strongest credibility signal in the entire email.
- **Specific price points ($9.99, $7.00, $19.99)** alongside percentage discounts give deal-seekers fast scanning value.
- **Father's Day gifts section** is the only fresh seasonal angle in the send and correctly targets a time-sensitive purchase occasion.
- **Kohl's Cash earn reminder** at the bottom is smart loyalty reinforcement and a known conversion driver for repeat shoppers.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage visible.

## What's weak

- **Campaign fatigue is real:** This is the third "Lowest Prices of the Season" email in three days; the hook was fresh June 1 and is now familiar wallpaper.
- **No single hero focus:** The email tries to be everything to everyone across women's, kids', home, and Father's Day — diffusing urgency across eight sections instead of concentrating it on one.
- **Generic "Shop Now" CTAs throughout:** Identical button copy on every section signals that no one made an editorial call about this subscriber's actual interest.
- **Father's Day buried at the bottom:** The only genuinely time-sensitive hook in the email is the last content block — most readers have already bounced before reaching it.
- **Subject line hides the number:** The 85% clearance figure that does the heaviest lifting is inside the email and invisible at the inbox level.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the 85% figure in the subject line** — it's the most credible claim in the email and zero subscribers see it from their inbox.
- 2. **Promote Father's Day to the hero or give it its own dedicated send** — a standalone Father's Day email this week would outperform a cameo in a clearance blast.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs** — replace every "Shop Now" with the category ("Shop Women's Clearance," "Shop Dad's Gifts") so each section carries its own weight.
- 4. **Add a visible deadline** — even "deals valid through Sunday" would unlock the urgency signal that is entirely absent here.
- 5. **Rotate the sender name back to just "Kohl's"** — "Kohl's Lowest Prices of the Season" as a FROM name is clunky and burns recognition value.
- **Subject Alt A:** `85% off clearance — our lowest prices of the season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The clearance rack hits 85% off. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on everything you buy this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Father's Day gifts + summer clearance deals inside.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Lowest Prices of the Season" (2026-06-03)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered promotional send (MessageGears / `s.kohls.com`) with three distinct tracking pixel systems firing in the `<body>`. Core HTML is structurally sound but contains one malformed tag and several accessibility gaps; compliance signals are degraded by the AgentMail relay stripping headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed open-pixel wrapper** — The `click.s.kohls.com` open-tracking pixel block closes with an orphaned `</custom>` tag:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?BO37QIYYRDPEHIRNVLCM2J4VCI.60257..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>
```

`<custom>` is not a valid HTML element. While most email clients will silently ignore it, it invalidates the document and could cause rendering edge cases in strict parsers.

**22 click-redirect links unverified** — All destination URLs route through `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears click-tracking). The QA probe correctly skipped these; no broken final destinations could be confirmed or ruled out.

**Three concurrent pixel systems firing:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path (personalization/recommendations)
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears open/render tracking
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel

No technical defect here, but the AAM pixel (`d_sid=13245196`) fires unconditionally with no consent-gate visible in the source, which may be relevant for state-level privacy laws (see §5).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed). Affected images include both tracking pixels and apparent content images:

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c8e336d6...` (chp.kohls.com) | Tracking pixel | Missing `alt=""`; no `aria-hidden="true"` |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Open pixel | Missing `alt=""` |
| `event` (demdex.net) | AAM sync pixel | Missing `alt=""` |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...`, `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com rp/) | Render-proof pixels | Missing `alt=""` |
| `54462797_2s_2x` (images.am.kohls.com) | Content image | Missing `alt` — screen reader will announce filename |

The `o.gif` pixel already has `aria-hidden="true"` on its wrapping `<td>` but still lacks `alt=""` on the `<img>` itself. All 1×1 tracking pixels should carry both `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` on the `<img>` element.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Several clients (notably Gmail web, Apple Mail) surface the email title in tab/window labels. An empty title is a minor a11y gap for screen-reader users navigating multiple windows.

**Empty favicon href:**
```html
<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">
```
Will trigger a redundant HTTP request to the email's base URL in some clients. Should be removed or pointed at a real asset.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — Non-standard value; the spec requires `user-scalable=no`. Functionally equivalent in most clients today but technically incorrect.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA confirmed) — The AgentMail relay did not surface this header. If it was set at the ESP layer and stripped in transit, Gmail and Apple Mail will fall back to their own unsubscribe prompts, which is acceptable. If it was never set, this is a deliverability and compliance gap — major ISPs (Google, Yahoo) require `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** (QA confirmed) — One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo for senders above 5K/day threshold since February 2024. Its absence will suppress the one-click UI in Gmail.

**No physical mailing address detected in truncated source** (QA confirmed) — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. This may be present in the portion of the HTML not included in the truncated source; it should be verified against the full render.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured headers. This is likely a relay artifact, not a sending failure, but should be verified in the raw message source from a delivered copy.

**AAM pixel fires unconditionally** — `kohls.demdex.net` is an Adobe cross-site tracking domain. California (CCPA) and other state laws may require opt-out or consent before firing behavioral tracking pixels. No consent check is visible in the HTML.

All 22 links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` redirects, which will append or carry tracking on the outbound leg. The `mi_ecmp=1013887_202663` and `email_name=260603_DG_Email_Wednesda` parameters on render-proof pixels confirm campaign attribution metadata is present at the ESP level.
