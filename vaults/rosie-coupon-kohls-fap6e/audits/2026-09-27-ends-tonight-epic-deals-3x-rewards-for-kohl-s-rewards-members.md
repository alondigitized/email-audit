# ENDS TONIGHT 🏃‍♀️‍➡️ Epic Deals + 3x rewards for Kohl's Rewards members ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth "EPIC DEALS" send since 09-20, and by now it's less an email than a template with a subject-line variable swapped in — same purple hero banner, same hot-pink "EPIC DEAL" price tiles, same barcode/coupon footer wall that's carried every single send this week. The only change from yesterday's "LAST DAY" version is the header, which now says "ENDS TODAY," and a light reshuffle of the product grid (fleece kids' pajamas, décor, LEGO, towels, jeans, floor care, mattress topper).
- The saving grace is that the offer stack itself is legible: 3x rewards, a Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale, and a $10-off-$50 coupon are all genuinely time-bound and visible without digging. But a subscriber who's opened even three of this week's sends has seen this exact skeleton before, and "ENDS TODAY" from a brand that said "LAST DAY" yesterday and "ENDS TONIGHT" in the subject reads as manufactured urgency rather than credible scarcity.
- Verdict: functionally fine, creatively exhausted — this is fatigue-by-repetition, not a bad single email.

## What's working

- Clean "ENDS TODAY" status bar up top gives immediate urgency context before any scrolling.
- Category nav (Home/Women/Men/Kids) right under the hero helps segment users into their interest area fast.
- Discount tiles are legible and consistent — big price/percentage callouts, clear "Shop Now" links throughout.
- The 3x Rewards banner and Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale section break up the monotony of price tiles with distinct green branding.
- No visible render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.

## What's weak

- This is the same purple-hero, pink-tile, barcode-footer skeleton run at least nine times since 09-20 (per brand history) — zero creative differentiation from the prior four days.
- "Coupons do not apply" disclaimers appear repeatedly (athletic shoes, floor care, mattress topper) undercutting the "everything's on sale" feel.
- No hero product image — the top of the email is a stock bag photo with a discount wreath graphic, not an actual epic deal.
- Urgency language is now inconsistent across sends (LAST DAY → ENDS TONIGHT subject → ENDS TODAY banner), which reads as a template variable, not real scarcity.
- Product grid is generic and unsegmented by persona — kids toys sit next to mattress toppers next to jeans with no attempt at personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the template. After nine-plus near-identical sends, the single highest-leverage change is a different visual skeleton — swap the purple/pink hero for an actual lifestyle photo tied to fall/harvest season, since the render still shows generic "F A L L" gourd décor tiles that could be reused wholesale.
- 2. Fix the urgency inconsistency — pick one deadline framing and hold it across subject, preview, and in-body banner instead of LAST DAY/ENDS TONIGHT/ENDS TODAY rotating language.
- 3. Lead with fewer, sharper offers instead of a 12-tile grid — a subscriber who's seen this five times this week is fatigued by scroll depth, not short on choices.
- 4. Move the Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale (genuinely differentiated, "only twice a year") higher — it's currently buried mid-scroll under generic price tiles and is the most credible unique hook in the email.
- 5. Reduce "coupons do not apply" call-outs or bundle them into a single disclosure line rather than repeating per-tile — it deflates trust in the "epic" framing.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-generated marketing email (Kohl's/CoherentPath platform) with tracking pixels and click-redirect links; automated QA flags gaps in compliance headers and image accessibility.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Multiple tracking/analytics endpoints present: `click.chp.kohls.com` (1x1 pixel), `mi.kohls.com` open/click pixels, `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (open tracking beacon).
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains, not itself a defect — flagged here for transparency, not as an issue).
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking block — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 11 images missing `alt` text, per QA output, including both tracking pixels (`efa38477-d44a...`, `o.gif`, `event`, and 7 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons) and one content image (`57746756_2_2x` product image at `images.am.kohls.com`). Tracking pixels are low-impact, but the product image lacking alt text is a genuine accessibility gap for screen reader users.
- Legacy HTML4 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and `<style>` resets — standard for cross-client email rendering, no issues found.
- Truncated source prevents full accessibility audit of remaining body content.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe` query params observed in tracking URLs — appear to be populated (no unresolved `{{merge_tag}}`-style placeholders visible in the truncated source).
- No issues found in visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence; cannot confirm from HTML source alone.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058) not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; cannot confirm absence definitively since source is truncated, but flagging per QA output.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — authentication status unknown, likely a relay capture gap rather than a sender-side failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking/redirect URLs (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) use opaque tokenized IDs (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `qs=` encoded blob) rather than standard UTM parameters — consistent with ESP click-tracking redirects, not a defect, but prevents direct verification of destination/landing-page alignment from source alone.
- Cannot confirm final landing page match since redirect targets were not resolved (skipped per QA probe settings).

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` text (or empty `alt=""` for purely decorative/tracking images) to the 11 flagged images, prioritizing the content image `57746756_2_2x`.
- Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking block.
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers and Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM) directly against raw SMTP headers rather than relying on relay capture, since AgentMail may not be preserving them.
- Confirm physical mailing address is present somewhere in the full (non-truncated) email body/footer to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
