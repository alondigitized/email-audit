# Holiday hits, all under $50 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Holiday hits, all under $50," and the body mostly delivers. The "Gifts Under $50" hero has a SHOP NOW button, and the one-per-brand product list below it gives prices ($26, $27, $50, $26, $32, $39, $28). The Planet Aware "Clean at Sephora" badges add a trust cue. The email is on-season.
- The top banner undercuts the subject, though. It leads with "Spend More, Get More Fragrance," a free trial-size redeem with code FRAGSMGM, which is a different offer from the one the subject sold. This matches the Nov 10 "best NEW holiday sets" send, where the banner also led with something else (innisfree trial sizes). The email then runs very long, with seven product tiles, a BNPL block, a Shark Beauty block, a rhode block, a category strip and a footer. Nearly every tile is a bare product with no per-item button, so the eye has nowhere to go. My view is that the concept is good, but the stacked secondary offers dilute it.
- The email says nothing about Insider points or member pricing, even though it comes from "Sephora Insider." It also arrives the same day as other Nov 12 sends, which makes this the Nth promo this week.

## What's working

- The "Gifts Under $50" hero matches the subject and has a clear SHOP NOW button.
- Each product tile shows a price and a value comparison, such as "$26 ($36 value)" for Laneige.
- "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags add scarcity and exclusivity.
- Clean at Sephora badges mark which items qualify.
- The seasonal festive-red look and bow decor read as holiday gifting.
- The Klarna, Afterpay and PayPal block lowers price friction.

## What's weak

- The top banner (the fragrance trial-size redeem with a promo code) competes with the subject's under-$50 promise.
- The email is very long, and the many stacked blocks (BNPL, Shark Beauty, rhode, gift guide) dilute the hero.
- Product tiles have no visible per-item CTA, so the shopper has to guess what is clickable.
- There are no reviews, ratings or social proof on any product.
- Insider or loyalty benefits are not mentioned in the body, despite the "Sephora Insider" sender.
- The footer fine print about the promo code is dense and small, and the redeem terms for FRAGSMGM are buried there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the top banner match the subject. Lead with "Gifts Under $50" and move the fragrance redeem lower, or give it its own send.
- 2. Cut the email to the hero plus 4 or 5 products, and drop the BNPL, Shark Beauty and rhode blocks, or move them to another send.
- 3. Add a "SHOP NOW" or "Add to bag" button under each product, with a specific verb and noun.
- 4. Add star ratings or "best seller" labels to the tiles.
- 5. Add an Insider line, such as "Earn points on every gift."
- 6. State the redeem terms next to the promo code instead of only in the footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering failure (an empty `Hi ,` greeting) and one deliverability failure (a 0-character plain-text part). Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated because of AgentMail relay capture limits. The 38% pass rate is dominated by missing headers, not by confirmed template defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in ESP redirects (`https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1d01fd8aaa6c336f4bbc780a0/...?__dU__=...`). Final destinations can't be seen from the HTML source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 38. It reported no probe failures for the 12, but the 38 skipped links are unverified.
- There are two tracking pixels:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a hidden open pixel, inline-styled `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched` is a second beacon.
  - `mi_u` is a per-recipient identifier in a URL.
  - Neither pixel is an error, but both lack `alt`, as QA flagged.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Probable CSS parse bug:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in `<style>`. Non-Outlook parsers treat this as part of the selector. That likely invalidates `img.mso-size` and probably the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`. The conditional comment belongs outside the `@media` block.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper table uses `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible CSS has no override for the `.tbl` class on mobile, which risks horizontal scrolling. The source is truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`. The `<title>` is the generic "Sephora" instead of the subject.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.
- **JSON-LD problems:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which I believe isn't a schema.org type. Gmail will likely ignore it.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged both tracking images (`o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/...` pixel). Use `alt=""` since they are decorative.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The merge field resolved to empty with no fallback.
- The test recipient probably has no first name on file, so this is a missing default rather than a broken field. The template still needs a fallback (for example `Hi there,` or dropping the greeting) so it degrades cleanly.
- The empty `"discountCode": ""` in the JSON-LD is a second unpopulated slot.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and is an accessibility fallback gap. The relay may have dropped it, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers. These are unconfirmed, not proven absent. Check against the raw message at the original receiver.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Sender is `shop@beauty.sephora.com`.
- The physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but I can't confirm it from the excerpt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only campaign identifier I can see is `mi_ecmp=20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched` on the beacon.
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed from the source or QA data.
- Resolve the redirect chain for the top CTAs to verify this.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback for the first-name merge field, for example `%%first_name|there%%`-style syntax in the ESP's equivalent.
2. Add a `text/plain` part to the multipart message.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
6. Pull the raw MIME to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers. Treat these as unverified until then.
7. Expand link probing to cover the 38 skipped links, or at least the redirect-resolved CTAs. Verify UTM and landing-page continuity from those results.
