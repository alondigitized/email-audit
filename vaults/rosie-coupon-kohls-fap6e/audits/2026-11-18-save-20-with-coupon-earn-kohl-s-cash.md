**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a Kohl's long-scroll catch-all, and it never settles on one idea. The hero is a "Take an extra 20% off" banner with "Ends Nov. 21 / Shop Now" and a "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" badge. Right after it come a gift-guide tile grid (For Her, For Him, For Kids, Stocking Stuffers), an "Online Only Daily Deals" banner, a Preview the Deals promo, a services block, a product row, and three barcode coupons. That is six or seven messages in one send, and the offer is split across 20% off, $10 off $50, and Kohl's Cash.
- The hero is cramped and the "20%" is partly obscured by the snowy graphic, so the headline offer is weaker than it should be. The seasonal gift imagery is good and fits the date. The best-converting content sits at the bottom: the Ninja SLUSHI at $299.99, the towel at $2.99–$9.99 and the Levi's 505 at $44.97 show real prices. The coupon codes SAVEMORE and HOME10 are also down there, after a lot of scrolling.
- Compared with the Nov 15–17 sends in your history ("HOURS LEFT for 40% off", "Find top hosting finds for less"), this one has a weaker, less urgent offer: 20% instead of 40%. It's the fourth or fifth Kohl's send in about four days. It is functional but diluted. I'd call it an average promotional send.

## What's working

- The seasonal gift-guide tiles (North Pole lane, Explore the Gift Shop, For Her/Him/Kids) match the holiday moment.
- The hero has a visible "Shop Now" button and a clear end date (Nov. 21).
- Real product cards with strike-through prices (Ninja $299.99 from $349.99, Levi's $44.97 from $74.95) make the savings concrete.
- The barcode coupons with codes and PINs (SAVEMORE, HOME10) make redemption easy in store or online.
- The "Shopping made simple" block (Klarna, free store pickup, DoorDash/Instacart/Uber Eats) adds convenience and a $5 Kohl's Cash on pickup hook.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers: 20% off, Kohl's Cash, $10 off $50, $10 off a home purchase of $50+, Daily Deals, Preview Deals and the Doorbusters teaser all compete for the same click.
- The hero's "Take an extra 20% off" is small, and the "20%" is partly hidden behind the snowy art. It doesn't dominate.
- The best price-led product content is buried far down the scroll.
- A lot of the offer detail is in dense, tiny fine print. "Coupons do not apply to select brands" is a caveat that makes the offer feel less honest.
- "Preview the Deals" promotes deals that aren't available yet, and "Check back for new deals through Nov. 18" adds friction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a single hero offer, "20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash", in large type. Put the SAVEMORE code right under it and move the product row directly below.
- 2. Cut the gift-guide tiles to one row, or move them below the products.
- 3. Collapse the three barcode coupons into one coupon module.
- 4. Drop or shrink the "Preview the Deals" and delivery blocks to one line.
- 5. Put the Ninja and Levi's deals in the first scroll to give the email a concrete price anchor.

- **70 tracking links were skipped.** They are all on redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination resolution and link health are unverified, and the HTTP probe was skipped by design.
- **There are four separate open/tracking pixels before the visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/6e1e02f1-…` (1×1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026045_20261117`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (the Adobe Audience Manager sync, `height="0" width="0"`)
- **Stray `</custom>` closing tag:** it follows the `open.aspx` `<img>`. It is invalid HTML and has no matching opening tag.
- **Internal naming is exposed in image URLs:** `email_name=261117_DG_Email_Tue_PM` appears on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`. This is minor, but it is metadata visible in client-side URLs.
- **Fonts load from an external host:** `fonts.googleapis.com` is loaded via `<link>` and `@import`. It is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook desktop is unaffected. This is an extra third-party request and a privacy-relevant fetch.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing `alt`, flagged by QA:**
  - Content images: `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/…`).
  - Likely content images on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. - **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` fails WCAG 1.4.4 in clients that honor it.

- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables use `role="presentation"`, but the tracking-pixel tables (`width="1" height="1"`) and the AAM table don't.
- **Stray `<style>` rule:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` or `#` selector prefix, so it is ineffective.
