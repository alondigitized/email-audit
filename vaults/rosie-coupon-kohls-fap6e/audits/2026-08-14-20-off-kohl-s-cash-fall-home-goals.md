# 20% off + Kohl's Cash + fall home goals 🍂 🏠
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's production template running since 7/30, and today it's actually well-matched to its subject line — "20% off + Kohl's Cash + fall home goals." The hero leads with a clean 20% off coupon banner, then pivots straight into a Home-styled vignette (framed art, ceramic vases, wood console) and a "cozy up to even more savings" Decor callout. The subject's promise and the top-of-email creative line up, which is more than several recent sends managed.
- Where it drifts is the middle third: an "Ultimate Clearance Event" up to 70% off block gets shoved in between the home-decor lifestyle shot and a "Picked with you in mind" product grid that's mixed-category (a slushie maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and women's jeans) rather than home-focused. It reads like three campaigns stapled together rather than one coherent fall-home story. Still, the top fold does its job, and there are no render bugs.

## What's working

- Subject ("fall home goals") and hero visual (framed art, plants, ceramic vases, wood console) are genuinely aligned — a rarity in this brand's recent cadence.
- 20% off coupon is big, red, and impossible to miss above the fold.
- Kohl's Rewards status bar ("Spend $0 to score your next reward") gives a personalized loyalty hook right at the top.
- Clean category nav (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeated twice for easy scanning.
- No visual glitches, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The "Ultimate Clearance Event" (70% off) block breaks the fall-home narrative established at the top — it's a bolt-on, not an extension.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product grid is category-incoherent: a slushie maker and jeans next to a mattress topper undercuts the "your great taste" personalization line sitting right above it.
- Bottom half is dense with stacked promo blocks (clearance, coupon reminder, wallet CTA, Kohl's Cash terms) — fatigue sets in well before the footer.
- "Picked with you in mind" implies personalization but the products look like generic bestsellers, not tailored picks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Ultimate Clearance Event" block from this send entirely, or move it to its own email — it dilutes the fall-home focus that's actually working here.
- 2. Make the "Picked with you in mind" grid actually home-relevant (or genuinely personalized) instead of a random assortment; if it can't be personalized, rename it to something honest like "Trending now."
- 3. Move the Decor 20%-off tile higher, right under the hero, since it's the strongest link back to the subject line.
- 4. Trim one of the two repeated Home/Women/Men/Kids nav blocks — showing it twice adds length without adding value.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8+ images (all tracking pixels and the AAM beacon) are missing `alt` text per QA. For 1x1 tracking pixels this is standard practice (`alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` is already used correctly on the `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` pixel), but the `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`, and `demdex.net` pixels lack even an empty `alt` attribute — should be normalized to `alt=""` for consistency/screen-reader hygiene.
- Head includes `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` and MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) — correct Outlook/Apple Mail defensive markup.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width: 500px` with `.w_50pc`/`.w_60pc`/`.w_35pc` fixed-pixel overrides (207px/260px/154px) — typical fluid-hybrid pattern, no issues found in the visible source.
- `<title></title>` is empty — minor, affects webmail tab/preview text in some clients.
- Truncated source prevents full accessibility audit of body copy, CTA buttons, and footer; recommend a full-source pass for heading structure, color contrast, and CTA link text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are present in the visible/truncated source. No issues found in what's available — note that body content beyond the truncation point was not inspected, so this is not a complete guarantee.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by QA. This may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence — confirm directly against raw SMTP headers before treating as a compliance gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed; Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (effective for high-volume senders) require this header.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the parsed source — CAN-SPAM requires this in the footer. Given the source is truncated before the footer, this may be a truncation artifact rather than a genuine omission; needs verification against the full HTML/footer.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay. From `s.kohls.com` subdomain, DKIM alignment should be verifiable separately via mail server logs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Visible click-tracking URLs use Kohl's own `click.chp.kohls.com` / `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains rather than raw UTM-tagged destination URLs, so UTM parameters on final landing pages can't be confirmed from this source (65 links were skipped from HTTP probing for this reason).

- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (e.g., promo code / 20% off + Kohl's Cash offer carried through to destination) without following the redirect chain, which QA intentionally skipped.
