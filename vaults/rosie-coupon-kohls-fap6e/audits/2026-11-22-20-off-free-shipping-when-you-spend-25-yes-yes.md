# 20% off + FREE SHIPPING when you spend $25 ... yes & yes 🥳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall. The subject promises two things: 20% off and free shipping at $25. The render delivers both, but weakly. The top of the email is a snowy hero with a "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge, so the subject's 20% off isn't the first thing the eye lands on. Free shipping appears as a thin banner just below the category nav. The 20% coupon only shows up near the bottom, in a "Take an extra 20% off" block with a barcode and code.
- Below that, the email is a dense stack of tiles: 50% off toys and electronics, luggage at 65% off plus 20% with coupon, LEGO at 40%, shoes at 40%, Christmas trees, cookware, and small appliances at $79.99 and $299.99. It reads as a real Black Friday blowout. But it's the same format as every Kohl's send I've audited this week, from "Take an extra 20% off 🙌" on 11/20 to "STARTS TODAY: 20% off + tons of 50% off deals" earlier today. This is the second send today and the fifth in three days, and the creative barely changes. The subject is the freshest thing here.
- Verdict: solid and on-season, but the lack of a single focal offer and the fatigue from the cadence hold it at a decent-not-great level. One caveat on my read: the render is very tall and was displayed small, so I'm confident about the layout and offers but not about fine print or small copy.

## What's working

- The subject's "yes & yes" has some personality, and the 20% off plus $25 free-shipping pairing is concrete.
- Category nav buttons ($10 & Under, Women, Men, Kids, Toys, Shoes & Boots, Beauty) sit right under the hero and let a shopper jump straight to their category.
- The tiles have bold percentage badges and visible "Shop Now" buttons, and the product photography is clean.
- Kohl's Cash, the sweepstakes ("Win up to $350 Kohl's Cash") and the Rewards header give members extra reasons to engage.
- The free-shipping line is repeated in the footer, so the subject's promise is reinforced.
- The Black Friday seasonal framing (snowy hero, gift guides, Christmas trees) fits the date.

## What's weak

- The hero leads with Kohl's Cash, not the 20% off or free shipping named in the subject, so the subject and top of the email don't match.
- Free shipping is a slim strip under the nav, easy to miss.
- The 20% coupon is buried near the footer, with no coupon code or button at the top.
- Many tiles carry "coupons do not apply" or "select styles" fine print, which undercuts the 20% promise.
- Too many equal-weight tiles and no clear hierarchy, so the eye has nowhere to settle.
- Repetition: the second send today, after "STARTS TODAY: 20% off + tons of 50% off deals" and "HOURS LEFT: Extra 20% off ⏰".

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: a "20% off + free shipping at $25" banner with the coupon code and an "Apply & Shop" button, moved to the very top.
- 2. Move the coupon and barcode block up, or add a code chip beside the hero.
- 3. Cut the tile count by about a third and lead with 3-4 hero categories. Keep the rest as nav links.
- 4. Move the exclusion language into one plain line near the offer, so each tile isn't a separate asterisk.
- 5. Space the sends out. Two near-identical promos in one day trains subscribers to ignore both.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built template (ExactTarget-style `click.s.kohls.com` plus mPulse `mi.kohls.com` tracking). It has real accessibility and markup defects, notably missing `alt` attributes and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed from the captured data, because the HTML is truncated and the AgentMail relay strips headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 86 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains), so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unconfirmed. The links are opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` blobs.
- **Four tracking beacons before any visible content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9b385154-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  The overlapping open-tracking is redundant, and the Adobe demdex pixel is a third-party call.
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag, which has no opening tag: `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`. An empty href resolves to the document URL in some parsers.
- **Plain-text part:** 15,282 of 21,042 characters (about 73%) are URLs, which exceeds the QA threshold of 70%. The tracking-heavy text part is a minor spam-signal risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:** QA flagged 13 images. The ones that matter are the content images:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`

  These look like dynamically rendered content images. With images blocked or on a screen reader, they are unreadable.
- **False positive in the QA list:** `o.gif` already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image. The `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel and the demdex pixel have no `alt` attribute at all, but they are tracking pixels.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close the head there, and this markup does nothing in email clients.
- **CSS defects:**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it matches nothing.
  - `max-height: auto` is not a valid value.
  - `maximum-scale` and `meta name="format-detection"` are each declared inconsistently or twice.
- **Fonts:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are specified, so there are no issues here.
- **Good practice:** the layout tables use `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible (truncated) HTML. Subscriber-specific identifiers are embedded in image URLs (`mi_u=604230016`, `mi_cid=…`, `mi_mid=…`). These are expected for personalization and tracking, but they mean anyone who gets the image URL can identify the subscriber. The remainder of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both missing. The QA note itself says this may be a relay capture artifact, so I'm not treating it as confirmed. Check the raw headers at the sending MTA.
- **Physical address:** QA found none, but the captured HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed. Re-check it against the full source.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is absent because of the relay. Sender is `kohls@s.kohls.com`, and the tracking domain `click.s.kohls.com` shares the sender subdomain, which is good for alignment. Verify authentication by inspecting the original message.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible:** the visible URLs carry no `utm_*` parameters. Click links are opaque `qs=` tokens, so final landing URLs and any UTM tagging can't be verified.
- **Campaign tagging on image URLs only:** `mi_ecmp=1026460_20261122` and `email_name=261122_DG_Email_Sun_Mi…` appear on image URLs. The date matches the send date.
- **Offer alignment unchecked:** the subject offer (20% off + free shipping at $25) can't be matched to landing-page content, because no destination was resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the offer and product images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `kohlsprod/60699446_*`). In particular, make sure the "20% off + free shipping $25" message is readable with images off.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fill `<title>`, drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, and remove the stray `</custom>`.
4. Fix `.ReadMsgBody` and remove `max-height: auto`.
5. Consolidate the redundant open pixels, and consider whether the demdex third-party pixel is needed.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. Don't rely on the relay capture.
7. Resolve a sample of the 86 skipped tracking links, with a real GET and redirect-chain trace, to confirm landing pages and UTM tagging.
8. Reduce URL density in the plain-text alternative below 70%.
