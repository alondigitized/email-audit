# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Sephora Savings Event send in a row. Last week's "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊" (10/30) and "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍" (10/31) already used this offer. The subject is nearly identical to those, with "Save" swapped in for "Get" and a lipstick emoji for the smiley. A subscriber who has seen three versions of this line will skim past it.
- The body is a long holiday gift catalog. The 30% off Sephora Collection offer sits in a small gold medallion at the very top with the code ALLONSALE and dates of 10/30–11/9. The loudest element is the "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" banner directly underneath, so the eye goes to gifting and not to the discount. The Jo Malone block, the "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" product grid, "Spot it. Shop it.", Skincare Gift Sets and the Holiday Gift Guide all pull attention away from the one deal the subject sells.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send with a stale subject and a body that fails to deliver on it. It lands around 7 because the brand is trusted and the offer is real.

## What's working

- The Sephora wordmark, "HAPPENING NOW" banner and "Savings Event" header make the sender instantly recognizable.
- The offer is concrete: a gold "30% OFF" medallion, code ALLONSALE, and 10/30–11/9 dates.
- The product grid shows prices with strikethrough values, for example Dieux at $49 against a $70 value and the Holiday Women's Perfume Sampler at $98 against $170. That makes the savings easy to read.
- The "NEW · LIMITED EDITION" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" labels add exclusivity.
- The scannable barcode at the bottom is a useful in-store redemption path.
- The render has no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 30% medallion is small and crowded at the top. "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" is the visual hero, so the discount reads as secondary.
- The email is extremely long, with at least six distinct modules. The deal is never repeated mid-scroll or near the product grid.
- Most of the featured products (Jo Malone, MERIT, DAE, GISOU) are not Sephora Collection, so it is unclear whether the 30% applies to them. The exclusions are buried in dense fine print at the bottom.
- The subject repeats the 10/30 and 10/31 sends almost word for word, with no urgency or new angle.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" with no verb-plus-noun specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 30% off Sephora Collection offer the hero: a full-width banner with the code and an end date, ahead of the generic gifting banner.
- 2. Add a Sephora Collection product row so the featured items match the offer. Use the sale items, not Jo Malone and Dieux, which are likely excluded.
- 3. Cut the email to three modules: the offer, a product grid and one gifting block. Move the rest to the site.
- 4. Add a mid-email repeat of the code and dates so a scroller never loses the deal.
- 5. Change the CTA copy to something like "Shop Sephora Collection 30% off".
- 6. Move the exclusions into one visible line near the offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% off is waiting, 7 days left 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lip, eye and face faves from $8. Use ALLONSALE at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus gift sets under $50 that aren't on the sale list.`

  Both are non-content images, so `alt=""` would be correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper table has `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only adjusts font sizes and image dimensions, so the layout can overflow on narrow viewports.
- **Malformed CSS:** an `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` conditional sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Outside Outlook, parsers will likely drop `img.mso-size` and probably the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows it. This is inferred from CSS error recovery, not observed.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **JSON-LD:** the block includes `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` and a bare `"@type":"PromotionCard"`. The empty code and incomplete promotion card are invalid or no-op markup.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Plain text:** the text part is 0 characters (QA FAIL). The email is HTML-only, which hurts accessibility and deliverability. The relay may have dropped it, so check the raw message.
