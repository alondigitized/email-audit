# 4X points on all hair care ‼️ and styling tools ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-mechanics email built around a 4X points event on hair care and styling tools — a sensible promotional framework for Sephora insiders who track rewards math. The upper half executes well: offer is above the fold, the "4X" badge is visually prominent, and "BE YOUR OWN STYLIST" gives the event a coherent editorial identity anchored by Dyson, Drybar, and Shark. The deadline and code appear to be present in the header strip, which is the right structural move.
- The problem is what happens below the hero. The email pivots into a "MAKEUP'S LATEST LINEUP" secondary block that has nothing to do with the hair care points event. That detour breaks the offer's momentum, dilutes the category focus, and makes the email feel like two separate sends stitched together. The product grid itself renders fine — no obvious broken images — but the individual product shots are small enough that the styling tools don't have the aspirational pull they need to drive clicks.
- After the July 16 "10% off your $100+ purchase" send and the editorial volume of the past week, this is actually a well-positioned offer — points multipliers feel additive rather than discounting, which avoids promo fatigue. But that goodwill is partly squandered by the unfocused second half.

## What's working

- **4X badge and offer are unmissable above the fold** — the header strip and bold "4X" callout communicate the deal before the eye even reaches the hero.
- **"BE YOUR OWN STYLIST" gives the event an editorial frame** — it's not just a loyalty mechanic, it has a campaign identity with named brands (Drybar, Dyson, Shark).
- **Promo code and deadline visible in header strip** — subscribers don't have to hunt for how to activate the offer.
- **Multi-product grid reinforces category breadth** — showing range across hair care and tools supports the "ALL hair care" claim.
- **Beauty INSIDER footer branding** — consistent loyalty program identity reinforces that this email is member-relevant.

## What's weak

- **"MAKEUP'S LATEST LINEUP" section mid-email kills category focus** — a 4X-hair-care email that pivots to makeup is a structural mismatch; it signals the template wasn't fully committed to the offer.
- **"MAJOR BEAUTY MOMENT / The next big party vibe" hero copy is vague** — this reads as editorial filler rather than selling the points multiplier.
- **Styling tool product images are too small** — Dyson and Shark tools are aspirational, premium SKUs; they need more visual real estate to justify the click.
- **Double ‼️ in subject line reads spammy** — one emoji as punctuation is fine; two identical exclamation-mark emojis in a single subject looks like it was written for maximum inbox noise.
- **No social proof visible** — given that this runs adjacent to the "Obsessed? Same." viral-content send from July 12, adding a star rating or "bestseller" badge on one or two hero tools would have been easy lift.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the makeup section or move it below a hard rule.** The hair care event deserves the full email. If cross-sell is a requirement, push "MAKEUP'S LATEST LINEUP" into a narrow footer band with a single "and more →" link — don't give it a full block.
- 2. **Replace the "MAJOR BEAUTY MOMENT / The next big party vibe" hero with offer-first copy** — something like "4X Points This Weekend. Pick Your Tool." The existing copy buries the mechanic instead of amplifying it.
- 3. **Feature one hero product with large imagery** — a single Dyson Airwrap or Drybar brush shot at full width, priced with the points math shown (e.g., "Earn 1,200 points on a $300 tool"), does more conversion work than twelve thumbnail-sized grids.
- 4. **Fix the subject line double ‼️** — one is enough, and appending "and styling tools ‼️" after the first emoji makes it feel like the line was edited in a hurry.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on hair care & styling tools — this weekend only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Style up: 4X points on Dyson, Drybar & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn on every tool and treatment through Sunday — use code at checkout`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insider points work harder this weekend — hair care included`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — `<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">` has no `lang="en"` attribute. Screen readers cannot infer document language; WCAG 3.1.1 violation.

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

- **`robots` meta malformed** — `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` uses a space; the correct token is `noindex` (no space). Unlikely to affect email delivery but indicates a template copy/paste error.

- **`<title>` is generic** — `<title>Sephora</title>`. Not a rendering bug but will appear in browser-preview tabs and some preview panes without campaign context.
