# It's a great day to take an extra 20% off 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth near-identical promo since Oct 26, and the subject's promise is the least visible thing in the email. The subject says "extra 20% off." The top of the render is a rewards status bar, category buttons, and a "Holidays just like you remember" North Kohl's hero. The only mention of the 20% is the small "Take an extra 20% off, Code: SAVEBIG, Ends Nov. 5" barcode block at the very bottom. Compared with the Nov 1 "Ends soon ⏳" and "Last day ⏰" sends, which had an "ENDS TODAY" banner up top, this one has no urgency where it matters.
- The body is solid but crowded. It has product tiles with real prices ($15.99 kitchen appliances, $11.99 throws, $249.99 Ninja SLUSHI), a 70% clearance banner, free returns through Jan 31, 2027, and DoorDash/Instacart delivery. That is plenty of reason to click, but with so many competing offers the eye has no single place to land. Judgement: a decent promo that undersells its own subject line.

## What's working

- Prices are concrete and visible, with original prices shown (e.g. $119.99 Ninja Foodi, $229.99–$349.99 Serta topper).
- The Kohl's Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member · Earn 5% Rewards") at the top personalizes the email.
- The Clearance block (red, "Save up to 70%") is the strongest single hierarchy moment.
- Seasonal framing (cozy throws, matching pajamas) fits early November.
- Convenience perks stack well: free returns through Jan 31, 2027, free store pickup, and same-day delivery.

## What's weak

- The extra 20% code (SAVEBIG) sits at the bottom, so the subject's headline offer is buried.
- Nothing in the visible top section says "extra 20% off" or gives a deadline. "Ends Nov. 5" only appears in the footer coupon.
- Most CTAs are generic "Shop Now" buttons, and the category nav is repeated twice.
- Offers compete with each other: 20% off, up to 70% clearance, 25% off + 20% pajamas, $5 Kohl's Cash, and delivery. No single hero offer.
- This is the tenth send on this stack since Oct 26. The "great day to take" framing has no hook beyond repetition.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Extra 20% off · Code SAVEBIG · Ends Nov. 5" block to the top, above the North Kohl's hero, with a "Copy code & shop" button.
- 2. Cut the repeated category nav and fold the product tiles into one grid with the 20% price shown after discount.
- 3. Use one CTA verb and noun per section (e.g. "Shop kitchen deals," "Shop throws") instead of "Shop Now."
- 4. Add a deadline to the hero ("Ends tomorrow").
- 5. Reduce the 70% clearance block to a single line so it doesn't compete with the 20% offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off + 70% off clearance 🛍️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVEBIG through Nov. 5 on cozy throws, kitchen deals & more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free returns through Jan 31 and same-day delivery.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Kohl's email has structural HTML defects, including a stray `</custom>` tag and a `<div>` inside `<head>`. It also has missing alt text on many images and four stacked tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication could not be verified. The QA header warnings look like relay capture gaps, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** They go through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, which is an encoded redirect, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open**, from three vendors:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/021e23d0-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **The first pixel is unhidden.** It sits directly in `<body>` as a bare `<img width="1" height="1">`, with no `alt` and no wrapper like the others have. It can render as a stray 1px inline element, and a blocked pixel could show a broken-image icon.
- **A subscriber identifier is in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears on several `mi.kohls.com` image URLs, so anyone who forwards or inspects the email can see it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup:** the open-pixel tag is closed with a stray `</custom>` (`<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`). Parsers handle this inconsistently.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is inside `<head>`.** `<div>` is invalid there, so browsers close the head early and the later head content moves into the body. Email clients also strip microdata.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is a minor problem in web view and for assistive tech.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** has an empty href, which triggers a request for the current URL in some web-view contexts.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flagged these:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `.../f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `.../c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `.../415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `.../69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `.../78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `.../916d6fa08d185233.png`

  The `/p/rp/` images are likely the content blocks, so they need real `alt` text. The `alt`-less pixel warnings (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `o.gif`, `demdex.net/event`) are low impact, and `o.gif` already has `aria-hidden`.
- **The `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` selector has no leading dot.** It targets a nonexistent element, so the rule never applies.
- **Fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via both `<link>` and `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`. The `@import` is redundant. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present.
- **Doctype/lang:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `role="presentation"` on tables, which is acceptable. `lang="en"` is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncation means the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw headers on the original message.
- **No physical address was detected.** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed. Verify that the footer has a postal address and an unsubscribe link.
- **Authentication-Results is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a branded sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper setup, but this is unverified.
- **The `robots: noindex, nofollow` and `referrer: no-referrer` metas** do not affect compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are visible** in the exposed portion. Attribution appears to use Movable Ink parameters instead: `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1025022_2026112` and `email_name=261102_DG_Email_Mon_PM`.
- **CTA destinations are hidden behind the encoded `qs=` redirects.** The 66 skipped links mean landing-page alignment with the "extra 20% off" offer could not be checked.
- **The campaign name is `Email_Mon_PM`.** The email was received on 2026-11-02, a Monday, so the name is consistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` (or drop it), and fix the empty `<title>` and `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images, and wrap the unhidden first tracking pixel in a hidden container.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector (add the dot) and drop the redundant `@import`.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results and the footer address.
6. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links to confirm landing pages, UTM params and destination status.
