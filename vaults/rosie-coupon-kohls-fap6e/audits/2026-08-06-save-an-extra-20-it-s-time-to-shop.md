# Save an extra 20% ... it's time to shop 🛍️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is another clean Kohl's render — no clipped starbursts, no garbled art, nav buttons and product tiles all line up, consistent with the run of solid technical sends logged since 7/30. The hero does its job: bold red "Take an extra 20% off with coupon. Ends Aug. 16." sits right under the Rewards Member status bar, so the eye lands exactly where it should.
- The problem isn't execution, it's message discipline. After the hero, the email stacks five more distinct offers — Ultimate Clearance (70%), activewear (40%), Haggar partnership (20%), fall home goods, and a "picked with you in mind" product grid — before finally circling back to the wallet coupon at the bottom. Any one of these could be its own send. Crammed together, none of them lands with real force, and it reads like a full-catalog dump wearing a "20% off" headline.
- This also lands the same week as the 8/2 "Clock's ticking ⏰ 20% off + Kohl's Cash END TONIGHT" and "HOURS LEFT for 20% off" sends — the third or fourth 20%-off coupon variant in a week. The render quality keeps this from being a bad email, but the offer itself has stopped feeling special.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unambiguous: coupon %, deadline (Aug. 16), and a single "Shop Now" button, all above the fold.
- Rewards Member status bar ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" + "Earn 5% REWARDS") gives a personalized, loyalty-forward opener.
- No visual bugs anywhere — starbursts aren't clipped, model photography is sharp, product tiles and prices all render cleanly.
- The "Picked with you in mind" section shows real prices, strikethrough comparisons (Serta topper $209.99 orig $349.99), and specific SKUs, not generic filler.
- Kohl's Cash math is spelled out ("$10 for every $50 spent") rather than left vague.

## What's weak

- Six competing offers (20% coupon, 70% clearance, 40% activewear, 20% Haggar, fall home, product grid) dilute the single clear CTA the hero promised.
- Fine print ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") repeats three separate times across sections — an honesty flag, not a one-off disclaimer.
- Subject line carries no urgency or specificity beyond the %, and the hero's real deadline (Aug. 16) never makes it into the subject/preview.
- This is the third-plus "20% off" coupon variant from Kohl's this week (8/2 "Clock's ticking," 8/2 "HOURS LEFT") — cadence fatigue is setting in even though today's creative is fresh.
- No reviews or social proof anywhere in a fairly long scroll of product placements.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** Lead hard with the 20% coupon + one supporting category (activewear or clearance), and hold Haggar, fall home, and the full product grid for a separate send. One offer, one hero, one CTA converts better than six competing for attention.
- 2. **Pull the real deadline into the subject line.** "Ends Aug. 16" is buried in the hero image but absent from the subject — that's the single strongest urgency lever available and it's unused.
- 3. **Consolidate the fine print.** Three repeats of "Coupons do not apply to select brands" reads as evasive; state it once, clearly, near the coupon code.
- 4. **Vary the offer framing this week.** Four "X% off, ends soon" coupon emails in a row trains subscribers to skim past them — rotate in a non-coupon hook (new arrivals, a single hero product) to break the pattern.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off ends Aug. 16 — here's your code`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off coupon expires Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 you spend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Activewear, fall home, and clearance — all under your coupon`

## Technical Audit: Kohl's — "Save an extra 20%..." (260806_DG_Email_Thursday)

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on the Cheetah Digital / CoherentPath ESP stack (`chp.kohls.com`, `storage.googleapis.com/coherentpath-contentlibrary`) with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup; automated QA returned a 43% pass rate driven mostly by missing alt attributes and unverifiable compliance headers rather than rendering defects.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel via `click.chp.kohls.com/o/bb147fb0-...?mi_mid=019fd6ba-e380-7000-b928-dd95af783328&cp_tp=v3.eJyF...` (base64/zlib-encoded `cp_tp` payload).
- Secondary open pixel: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1019194_202686`.
- Third open-tracking beacon: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?XXCAHSWAKOOEZDPNO55JYWRUJI.60263&d=60263&bmt=0` — **malformed markup**: the `<img>` tag is immediately followed by a stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag (`... alt=""></custom> </div>`). This is invalid HTML and should be removed.
- Adobe Audience Manager beacon: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.
- QA flagged 68 tracking/click-redirect links as skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolved), so final destination URLs and status codes for CTA links could not be verified in this pass.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Proper MSO conditional block for Outlook (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and non-MSO font loading (`<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guarded Google Fonts `<link>`/`@import`), plus a 500px mobile media query — no structural rendering issues found.
- 9 images flagged by QA as missing `alt` attributes, all tracking/measurement pixels (`bb147fb0-...`, `o.gif`, `event` (demdex), and six `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-targeting pixels). Some sibling tracking pixels in the same document do carry `alt=""` (e.g., the `o.gif` beacon at `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...`), so the fix is simply adding empty `alt=""` consistently to the remaining pixels for accessibility-scanner compliance.
- The stray `</custom>` tag noted above is also an accessibility/parsing risk since it can affect how assistive-tech DOM parsers interpret surrounding elements.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png` pixel URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an **empty value** and a truncated `mi_kc_event_pen` parameter — consistent with an unpopulated/failed merge field (expected a redemption end-date value). Recommend confirming this merge token resolves correctly in the live send rather than passing through blank.
- No subscriber-facing personalization tokens (e.g., first name) are visible in the truncated body; cannot confirm merge-field rendering in visible content from the provided source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it; recommend confirming directly against raw SMTP headers.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat as above.
- No physical mailing address detected in the parsed content — CAN-SPAM requires one; since the source is truncated, this should be re-verified against the full email body/footer before treating as confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay; recommend checking headers at the receiving MTA directly.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Insufficient data to assess: all 68 outbound/CTA links route through `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe, so final landing-page URLs and any UTM parameters could not be captured or validated in this pass.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup with no matching open tag.

4. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay) to rule out a capture artifact vs. an actual compliance gap.
