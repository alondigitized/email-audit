# Take 20% off ... your summer home refresh starts today ☀️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean render — no clipped starbursts, no garbled badges, a real departure from the broken-hero pattern flagged repeatedly in this brand's late-July stretch (July 27–28). The blue "FRIENDS & FAMILY" banner with the circular "20% off" badge is fully intact and legible, on par with the July 30 send that scored 9.00.
- But the subject oversells what's actually up top. "Your summer home refresh starts today" promises a Home-focused send, and instead the hero is the same generic sitewide Friends & Family 20% coupon Kohl's runs across every category — the actual Home content (the flamingo/pineapple "Hello Summer" block, "Summer for the Home" 50% off) doesn't show up until several screens down, after Women/Men/Kids/Home nav buttons and a Kohl's Cash banner.
- This also isn't Kohl's only July 31 send — brand history already logged "Special 20% savings are sweet 🍦 Kohl's Cash is the cherry on top" the same day. Two near-identical 20%-off Friends & Family emails in one day is cadence fatigue, not a targeted campaign. Solid render, mediocre targeting execution — call it a 7.

## What's working

- Hero banner renders fully intact — text, badge, and coupon terms all legible, unlike the clipped/garbled heroes from July 27–28.
- Rewards status ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER") and "Earn 5% REWARDS" are visible immediately at the top, reinforcing loyalty value.
- The mid-body "Hello Summer" section (flamingo, pineapple, ice cream maker, planters) is a cohesive, on-season visual moment.
- Coupon code, PIN, and barcode are all clearly presented near the bottom for easy redemption.
- Multiple clear category CTA buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home, then Bedding/Bath/Patio/Decor) make navigation unambiguous.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a "summer home refresh" but the hero graphic is a generic sitewide offer — Home content is buried well below the fold.
- The preview/preheader text leaks "View this email in browser" junk right alongside the actual teaser copy.
- Three competing "up to" percentages (20%, 50%, 70%) across the send blur which offer is actually the headline deal.
- This is the second Kohl's Friends & Family send on July 31 alone — brand history shows the same 20%-off beat already sent hours earlier.
- No single "home refresh" hero product is linked — just generic category buttons, despite the subject's specific promise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align the hero creative with the subject — if the subject promises a home refresh, lead with a Home-specific hero image/offer above the fold instead of the generic Friends & Family banner.
- 2. Clean up the preheader — strip "View this email in browser" out of the visible preview text and replace with real teaser copy.
- 3. Pick one lead offer instead of stacking 20%/50%/70% — competing percentages read as confusing, not generous.
- 4. Consolidate sends — two Friends & Family 20%-off emails in one calendar day risks list fatigue; merge into a single send.
- 5. Add a hard deadline to the subject itself rather than just "starts today" to strengthen urgency.
