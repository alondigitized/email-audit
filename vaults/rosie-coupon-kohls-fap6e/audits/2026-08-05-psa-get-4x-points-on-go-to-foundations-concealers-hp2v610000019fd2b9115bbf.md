# PSA 📣 Get 4X points on go-to foundations & concealers.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third distinct mechanic in six days — after the Aug 1 "Beauty Insider Celebration" and the Aug 4 "So hot right now" trend grid, Aug 5 pivots to a loyalty-points push: 4X points (not a discount) on foundations and concealers, capped off with a deadline that's the same day the email lands ("Ends 8/5"). That's a real structural shift, and the execution is clean — a dozen products laid out in a repeatable EARN/USE CODE pattern with product-specific copy (wear time, formula, coverage type) rather than generic "shop now" filler.
- The catch is the offer itself is soft. Points aren't cash, there's no visible dollar or percentage discount, and "Exclusions may apply" sits right under the hero alongside checkout-only redemption fine print at the bottom. For a subscriber scanning an inbox, this reads less like a deal and more like a loyalty-program nudge — solid for someone who's already bought into Beauty Insider, weak as a subject-line hook for anyone comparing it against the 50%-off Lip Day sends from the week prior.
- Net: well-built email, mediocre offer. Ships fine as-is, but it won't outperform the 9-10 scoring days like 07-30's foundation sample giveaway.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unambiguous and on-category immediately: "Your Foundation & Concealer Offers" with a clear "STACK 'EM UP" button, no scrolling needed to get the gist.
- Every one of the ~12 products carries its own points value, a short formula/benefit line, and a repeated "USE CODE: GETMORENOW" — consistent, scannable structure top to bottom.
- Loyalty mechanics are visible and reinforced twice: the points ladder per product, plus the INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE tier bar and a separate "$5 off with 250 points" callout near the footer.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken image slots, no lorem ipsum across a genuinely long, asset-heavy email.
- Secondary modules (New Summer Fridays app promo, Hot Hair Accessories) add relevant cross-sell without crowding the primary offer above the fold.

## What's weak

- The offer is points-only — no $ or % discount visible anywhere, which is a weaker hook than the 50%-off Lip Day or free-sample sends this brand ran the prior week.
- "Ends 8/5" is same-day urgency with zero lead time for anyone who doesn't open first thing — this reads as last-minute rather than planned.
- "Exclusions may apply" sits directly under the hero, and the redemption terms at the very bottom (checkout-only, 4X on ALL foundation, 5X on Sephora Collection specifically) undercut the "honest offer" read — the real mechanics are buried in 8pt fine print.
- No social proof anywhere — no star ratings, no "best seller" or "loved by" badges on a 12-product grid where differentiation would help.
- Subject line runs long (~54 characters) and carries no personalization or urgency cue itself; all the real urgency ("Ends 8/5") is stranded in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the subject line — "Ends 8/5" is the single most actionable fact in this email and it's invisible until you open. Fixing this alone would lift open rate more than any body-copy change.
- 2. Translate points into a dollar equivalent somewhere near the hero (e.g., "4X points = up to $X back") — points-only offers underperform against cash/percent framing for subscribers comparing across brands.
- 3. Move the exclusions/redemption fine print into a single clear line under the hero instead of splitting it between the top banner and the footer — reduces the "buried terms" feel.
- 4. Add a lightweight social-proof tag ("#1 rated," "Bestseller") to at least the top 2-3 products to break up the visually repetitive grid.
- 5. Give the same-day deadline more visual weight in the body — a countdown or bolded "Today only" treatment near the hero would match the urgency the copy implies but doesn't visually deliver.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free samples in store through 8/5.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Tower 28, Dior, Charlotte Tilbury & more — 4X points today only.`
