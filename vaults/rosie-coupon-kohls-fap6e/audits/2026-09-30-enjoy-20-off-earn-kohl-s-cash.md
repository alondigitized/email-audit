# Enjoy 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 👏
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's kitchen-sink send, and it repeats the structural problem from 09-29 ("Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩") and 09-28 ("Extra 20% off savings + earn Kohl's Cash ✨"). The subject promises 20% off plus Kohl's Cash. The hero is a bare pink "EXTRA" wordmark with no number. The Cash badge and a Women/Men/Kids/Home button row follow. The 20% code (YOUSAVE20) only appears in the barcode block at the very bottom, so the lead offer is buried.
- Below the hero, the email runs four unrelated stories. There is a Baby & Toddler Stock-Up Sale with $10 off $50, then a Carter's 50% off and toy 20% off block. Next come an "Elevated Everyday" workwear and boots block, a "Three reasons to decorate" home block with another $10 off $50, and a "Picked with you in mind" product grid. The fall and Halloween decor and the dated Oct. 4 baby deadline keep it seasonal. The email still gives no single reason to click. It is the same template with the subject line swapped again.
- Verdict: competent, on-brand and bug-free, but unfocused. It is the fifth or sixth near-identical 20% + Cash send in three days, so a subscriber has no reason to treat it as new.

## What's working

- The render is clean: no broken images, overlapping text or layout gaps across a very long email.
- The Baby & Toddler block leads with a clear "$10 off your $50 purchase" and a dated deadline (ends Oct. 4).
- The status bar at the top ("Kohl's Rewards' Member · 5% Rewards") shows the recipient's loyalty status.
- The product grid shows specific items with prices: Ninja Slushi at $349.99, a Serta topper from $137.99 to $209.99, a bath towel at $2.99 to $9.99.
- The seasonal content (Halloween, Fall and Christmas decor tiles) fits the calendar.

## What's weak

- The hero is just "EXTRA", with no percentage, no code and no CTA. The subject's 20% offer is invisible until you scroll to the barcodes.
- There are too many competing offers: the 20% code, Kohl's Cash, $10 off baby, $10 off toys, $10 off home, 50% off Carter's and 20% off toys. Nothing stands out.
- Almost every CTA says "Shop Now", so the buttons don't say which product or category they lead to.
- The content jumps between baby and toddler, workwear and boots, and home decor with no connecting idea.
- The coupon codes, Cash terms and exclusions sit in tiny fine print at the bottom, so the offer reads as less honest than the subject implies.
- This is a repeat of the 09-28 and 09-29 sends, which also led with "extra 20% off + Kohl's Cash".

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Extra 20% off" with a visible code (YOUSAVE20) and one "Shop 20% off" button. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the email to one or two stories. Either baby and toddler, or workwear and home, but not all of them.
- 3. Replace each "Shop Now" with a specific CTA, such as "Shop Carter's 50% off" or "Get boots from $54.99".
- 4. Move the Kohl's Cash earn rate and dates above the fold, next to the code.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 09-28 and 09-29 sends, and cap the cadence.

# Technical Audit: Kohl's, "Enjoy 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 👏"

The main confirmed problems are missing alt text on content images, an empty `<title>`, invalid markup in `<head>`, and a zoom-disabling viewport tag. The compliance and authentication warnings look like relay artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with a responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block and a MIME-safe structure. It has several code-hygiene defects (invalid head markup, a stray closing tag, an empty title) and heavy pixel tracking. Most QA warnings are relay artifacts rather than real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque links:** 71 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe. The visible link is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi...`, with an encrypted `qs` blob, so destinations can't be verified from source.
- **Four open or analytics pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/bd0e2366-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  Redundant open tracking has no functional impact, but the demdex call is a third-party data-sync request.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." ...></custom>` closes an element that was never opened. It is invalid HTML and some parsers may mishandle it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** QA flagged `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images, including `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. These are content or promo images, so images-off clients and screen readers get no content.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged but already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels genuinely lack `alt`, but they are hidden 1x1 or 0x0 images.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` gives no accessible document title.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Browsers will move it into `<body>`, and the schema microdata has no effect in email clients.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element.
- **Missing table roles:** the 1x1 tracking table and the AAM table lack `role="presentation"`, though the layout tables have it.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded via `<link>` and `@import`, inside the `<!--[if !mso]>` guard. The stack falls back to `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so this is acceptable.
- **Legacy doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with an `xmlns:v` VML namespace, which is fine for Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source, but the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance

- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is an aligned subdomain of `kohls.com`, but alignment can't be verified.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Kohl's typically includes one, so treat it as likely a truncation artifact.

4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and add `alt=""` to the hidden pixels that lack it.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels if possible.
6. Investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter in the Movable Ink image URL.
