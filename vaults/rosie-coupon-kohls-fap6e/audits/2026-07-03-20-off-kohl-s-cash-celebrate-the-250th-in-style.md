# 20% off + Kohl's Cash? Celebrate the 250th in style 💥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth "20% off + Kohl's Cash" email in roughly 48 hours — yesterday's "Psst ... 20% off + Kohl's Cash is inside" was the third — but this one earns its existence more than the prior two: the 250th anniversary / July 4th editorial hook gives the send a real reason to exist, and the patriotic red-white-blue visual theme is executed consistently throughout. The hero structure is tight: 20% off leads, Kohl's Cash appears in the subhead, and the "Shop Now" button is unmissable. The "Party-ready essentials for the whole crew" lifestyle section mid-email is the creative highlight — thematically sharp and well-timed for this weekend.
- The problem is offer stack pollution. The email piles a 40% off section and a 70% clearance banner on top of the 20% hero deal, which muddies the primary message for a subscriber who has already seen this exact offer three times this week. The "And the fifth, the sixth" copy — presumably a July 5–6 post-holiday shopping angle — is the freshest urgency lever in the email and is completely buried.
- Better than yesterday's "Psst…" send; weaker than it should be given the legitimate holiday hook available to it.

## What's working

- **Patriotic visual theme is cohesive**: red-white-blue palette, beach/outdoor imagery, and the "250th in style" concept thread through every section cleanly.
- **20% off is the undisputed visual anchor in the hero**: large, bold, no hunting required.
- **Kohl's Cash callout lands in the hero subhead**: two-line benefits are visible above the fold without scrolling.
- **"Party-ready essentials for the whole crew"**: the lifestyle mid-section is the strongest editorial moment — thematically motivated and broad enough to serve the whole family.
- **Clearance 70% off as a secondary tier at the bottom**: smart placement as a price-ceiling breaker for value shoppers still scrolling.

## What's weak

- **Three-discount pileup**: 20% off hero, then 40% off mid-email, then 70% clearance at the bottom — too many tiers for one send; the primary message gets lost.
- **"And the fifth, the sixth" is opaque**: the implied July 5–6 weekend urgency is smart but the copy reads like an incomplete sentence; most subscribers won't parse it.
- **All CTAs are generic "Shop Now"**: four or five "Shop Now" buttons across different sections, none pointing to a specific product or category destination.
- **No visible offer deadline in the hero**: July 4th is implied but no hard expiry shown; the urgency relies entirely on inference.
- **Cadence has hit the wall**: fourth near-identical 20% + Kohl's Cash email in 48 hours; the holiday hook mitigates but doesn't eliminate fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the deadline, not the discount** — every Kohl's email this week opens with "20% off." The differentiator here is the holiday weekend. Flip the hierarchy: "This weekend only — 20% off + Kohl's Cash." Make the urgency do the work the discount alone can no longer do after four sends.
- 2. **Cut one of the three discount tiers** — pick the 40% off section or the 70% clearance, not both. Three stacked discount levels in one send signals promo desperation and trains subscribers to wait for a better number.
- 3. **Fix the "And the fifth, the sixth" section** — rename it something immediately legible: "Shop the long weekend" or "Stock up through Sunday." The insight is good; the copywriting buries it.
- 4. **Make at least one CTA product-specific** — "Shop Women's 4th of July Picks" or "Shop Party Essentials" beats a fifth "Shop Now" and raises click confidence because the destination is clear.
- **Subject Alt A:** `This weekend only: 20% off + Kohl's Cash 💥`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your July 4th weekend discount is inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Celebrate the 250th in style — offer ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn Kohl's Cash on everything this weekend.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Functional HTML structure with no broken links detected, but compliance signals are degraded by the AgentMail relay (headers not captured) and the email carries 11 images missing `alt` attributes — the majority of visible content images. Authentication status is unverifiable from captured headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs confirmed, but link health is unverifiable from this capture.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing on open:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath CDP)
- `mi.kohls.com` (Marigold/Sailthru)
- `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud — `open.aspx`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` (Adobe Audience Manager)

No issues with the links themselves, but the `<custom>` tag after the SFMC open pixel (`</custom>`) is an orphaned/invalid HTML element — likely a template artifact from the ESP that should be `</div>` or removed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 content images missing `alt` text** — including what appear to be product/offer images (e.g., `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`) served via `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`. Tracking pixels correctly use `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`, but the content images do not. In image-blocking clients (Outlook default, Gmail Promotions tab), recipients see blank space with no fallback text.

Specific offenders from QA:
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` — no `alt`
- `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png` — no `alt`
- (7 additional images)

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, an accessibility barrier (WCAG 1.4.4).

**`<title>` tag is empty** — screen readers and some clients announce "Untitled" or nothing.

**Google Fonts double-loaded**: Roboto and Montserrat are each requested via both `<link>` and `@import url()` inside the same `<style>` block, doubling the font fetch requests with no benefit.

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the truncated source. Marigold subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` is populated in pixel URLs, indicating personalization tokens resolved at send time.
