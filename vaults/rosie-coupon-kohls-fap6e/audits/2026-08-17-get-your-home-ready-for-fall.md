# Get your home ready for fall 🧡🏠
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This one actually delivers on its subject line — "Get your home ready for fall" leads straight into "Read the room... FALL'S COMING" over a warm living room shot, then cascades through bedding, decor, bath, and kitchen/dining categories that all genuinely fit a fall-home theme. Compare that to yesterday's "Extra savings are here today, gone tomorrow" mismatch or 8/16's "ENDS TONIGHT" bait — this is the most subject/hero-aligned Kohl's send of the week.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the home section. It tacks on back-to-school apparel, a sweepstakes, a Sephora beauty insider offer, and a grab-bag "picked with you in mind" product wall (a drink maker, towels, jeans, a mattress topper) — none of which has anything to do with fall home or with each other. It reads like four separate campaigns stapled into one template, which is the same daily-production-template problem flagged in every recent audit, just less severe here because the top actually lands.
- No discount is attached to the fall-home content itself — the only concrete offers are Rewards status/5% (generic, always-on) and the Sephora 10% off $100+ beauty purchase, which is off-topic. A subscriber who opens for cozy fall decor gets a strong first screen and then a cluttered, unfocused scroll.

## What's working

- Hero copy ("Read the room... FALL'S COMING") and imagery (fireplace, warm living room) directly match the subject line — a rare clean hit this week.
- Clear category cascade under the fall theme: Shop Home → Shop Bedding → Shop Decor → Bath / Kitchen & Dining, each with its own visual and CTA button.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible and consistently styled (maroon pill buttons throughout).
- Rewards status bar up top gives a personalized, member-relevant anchor before the pitch starts.

## What's weak

- No explicit discount or price tied to the fall-home products — just lifestyle imagery and "Shop" buttons, no urgency or savings hook.
- Back-to-school, sweepstakes, Sephora beauty, and the "picked with you in mind" product grid are all bolted on with zero connection to the fall-home theme or each other — visual whiplash after a strong start.
- The "Picked with you in mind" section (drink maker, towels, jeans, mattress topper) is a random product dump, not a curated cross-sell — undermines the "your great taste" personalization claim.
- Long, cluttered scroll (this render is ~6,500px) dilutes the strong hero; a subscriber who came for fall decor has to wade through unrelated sales pitches to find more.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to fall-home only (hero → bedding → decor → bath/kitchen), and drop back-to-school, sweepstakes, and Sephora into separate sends — this send's whole strength is thematic focus, don't spend it.
- 2. Add a concrete offer to the fall-home hero (e.g., "20% off decor & bedding") — right now there's no price incentive attached to the on-theme content, only generic Rewards messaging.
- 3. If cross-sell sections must stay, add a one-line transition header per section (e.g., "Also worth a look:") so the jump doesn't feel like a template collision.
- 4. Replace the generic "picked with you in mind" grid with items actually related to fall/home refresh, or cut it — jeans and a drink maker undercut the personalization claim.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cozy up your space for fall 🍂`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Coherent Path (Kohl's ESP) transactional/marketing template with heavy tracking instrumentation but incomplete accessibility and compliance signal capture at the header level.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary open-tracking pixel: `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/eb5e504e-cb79-4e98-a49d-16a63a9dbe17?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v3...` — functions as a 1x1 tracker but is also the first element rendered in `<body>`, ahead of any content.
- Secondary open pixels: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1019949_2026817` and `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?Q7CWGGTOJDYUVAHFWLDWPUTQG4.60262&d=60262&bmt=0`.
- Malformed markup: the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` tracking `<img>` is immediately followed by a stray closing tag `</custom>` with no matching opening tag — invalid HTML that some renderers may not silently ignore.
- Adobe Audience Manager beacon: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` fires unconditionally with no visible consent gate in the truncated source.
- 71 tracking/redirect links (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`) were skipped by automated HTTP probing per QA — link validity/destination unconfirmed; recommend manual spot-check of at least the primary CTA redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 of 8 flagged images have no `alt` attribute (QA-confirmed), including all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` open/render-tracking pixels and the `demdex.net/event` beacon. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (not omitted) plus `aria-hidden="true"` — one pixel (`mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif`) already does this correctly, the others don't, indicating inconsistent implementation rather than a template-wide limitation.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and `.ExternalClass` resets present for Outlook — no issues found there.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px with `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` classing present; cannot verify actual stacking behavior from truncated source.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — correct for email context.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `*|...|*`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_mid=01a01005-a300-7000-a758-482d35dfc584` and `mi_cid`/`cp_cid` values in the tracking URL appear correctly resolved to actual IDs, not literal placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected (QA WARN). If genuinely absent at the MTA level, this is a CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058 gap; more likely explanation is relay capture loss — flag for header-level verification at source (not just via AgentMail relay).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header**: not detected (QA WARN) — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed functional.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in scanned content (QA WARN) — required under CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5). Could be in an untruncated footer region; needs confirmation against full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not detected (QA WARN) — deliverability/spoofing posture unverified via this relay.
- Sending domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain, consistent with standard DKIM/SPF alignment practice, but cannot confirm pass/fail without the header.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links use Coherent Path's proprietary `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`, `cp_tp`/`cp_cid` params rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — this is expected for this ESP and not a defect, but means GA/GA4-style UTM attribution won't apply unless a separate UTM layer is appended downstream of the redirect.
- Cannot confirm final landing-page URL or UTM presence post-redirect since the redirect chain was excluded from automated HTTP probing (71 links skipped) and the truncated HTML doesn't expose destination CTAs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the 7 tracking pixels currently missing it, matching the one pixel that already does this correctly.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup.
3. Verify at the source MTA (not just via relay) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent, since RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed here.
4. Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the full (non-truncated) email footer to satisfy CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5).
5. Manually resolve at least the primary CTA redirect chain (one of the 71 skipped tracking links) to confirm it lands on a valid, matching page.
6. Confirm `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass) directly from raw headers rather than relying on relay capture.
