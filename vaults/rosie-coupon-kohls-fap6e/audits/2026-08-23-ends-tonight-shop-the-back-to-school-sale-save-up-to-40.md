# Ends tonight ⏰ Shop the Back-to-School Sale & save up to 40% 🚌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is essentially the sixth Back-to-School send this week wearing yet another subject-line costume, and by now the format is fully rote: school-hallway hero kid, "ENDS TODAY"/"Ends tonight" urgency banner, up to 40% badge, then the same category cascade (Activewear 40%, Jeans 30%, Basics 40%, Uniforms 50%, Bedding 60%, Kitchen 40%) that ran nearly verbatim on 8/19–8/22. The one real structural difference from 8/23's earlier "ENDS TODAY" send is the So-Back Sweepstakes block and a "Picked with you in mind" personalized-feeling module near the bottom with actual prices (Ninja Slushi $349.99, towels $1.99–$8.99, jeans $26–$29.99, mattress topper $91.99–$139.99) — that's a legitimately good, concrete touch.
- The render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text — and the urgency is credible since this is genuinely the last day of a dated sale ("on sale now through August 23"). But the email is fatigued: this is the second "ends tonight/today" deadline email in one day (following the "ENDS TODAY: Up to 40% off" send earlier), which is redundant cadence rather than escalation. Subscribers who already saw the first deadline email today have no reason to open a second one with nearly the same promise.
- Judgement: solid execution, tired strategy. Score it as competent-but-repetitive, same tier as the week's other sends.

## What's working

- Deadline framing is credible — "ENDS TODAY" status bar plus "Select styles on sale now through August 23" gives a real, dated reason to act now, not manufactured urgency.
- Clean render throughout — no layout breaks, no lorem ipsum, all category tiles and hero images load correctly.
- "Picked with you in mind" module at the bottom adds specific prices and product variety (drink maker, towels, jeans, mattress topper), which is more concrete than the generic category cascade above it.
- Clear visual hierarchy — hero banner and 40% badge are the first thing the eye hits, followed by an obvious "Shop Now" CTA.
- Category shortcut buttons (Women/Men/Girls/Boys/Shoes/Home) right under the hero let a scanner jump straight to their department.

## What's weak

- This is the second "ends tonight" urgency email today after the earlier "ENDS TODAY: Up to 40% off" send — same deadline, same core offer, diminishing returns on a subscriber who already saw one.
- The category cascade (Activewear/Jeans/Basics/Uniforms/Bedding/Kitchen) is a near-exact repeat of 8/19–8/22's layout and percentages — zero new visual creative this week.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears repeatedly as fine print under nearly every category, which chips away at the "up to 40%" headline promise.
- The So-Back Sweepstakes block competes with the sale CTA rather than reinforcing it — it splits attention right when urgency should be driving straight to checkout.
- No loyalty-specific offer differentiation visible beyond the standard "Earn 5% Rewards" status bar — no member-exclusive early access or bonus tied to the deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a second same-day "ends tonight" email echoing the morning's deadline send — if a second touch is warranted, differentiate it hard (e.g., lead with the So-Back Sweepstakes or the "Picked with you in mind" personalized picks instead of repeating the same hero).
- 2. Move the "Picked with you in mind" priced product module up near the top — it's the most concrete, click-worthy content in the email and it's buried below six generic category blocks.
- 3. Cut or shrink the repeated "Coupons do not apply to select brands" disclaimer — one mention near the top is enough; repeating it under every category undermines the 40% claim.
- 4. Give the sweepstakes its own follow-up send rather than sandwiching it mid-deadline-email where it dilutes the single CTA.
- 5. Vary the hero creative from what ran 8/19–8/22 — same photo/banner style four sends in a row reads as autoplay, not urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Back-to-School savings up to 40% end at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart's about to lose 40% off — sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus enter to win $200 in Kohl's Cash before time runs out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ninja Slushi, towels from $1.99, jeans from $26 — final hours`
