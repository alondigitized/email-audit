# 40% off ends TONIGHT ⏳ Don't miss the Kohl's Cash.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-day version of the week-long Kohl's coupon send, and it is the best-aligned one. The subject says "ends TONIGHT" and the hero backs it up. A "You get an extra 40% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18." block sits next to a red "ENDS TONIGHT" stack, with a maroon "Shop Now" button. The deadline is credible and the discount is easy to read.
- The rest of the email is the same shell as Oct 10–17. The same green Kohl's Cash strip and red Home / Women / Men / Kids buttons are there. Below them come the Starting at 19.99 tops, Levi's up to 40% off, Season's Best Shoe Sale, "Three reasons to decorate" and the "Picked with you in mind" product grid. A subscriber who got the Oct 17 and earlier sends has seen all of this. The urgency fix is real, but the body is recycled and very long. The 40% hero competes with a $10-off home offer, Kohl's Cash, a shoe sale and a Levi's offer.
- Verdict: a solid last-call send with a clear deadline, held back by clutter and repetition. It's about a 7 for a Kohl's send.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TONIGHT" stack and the "Ends Oct. 18" line in the hero make the deadline credible and match the subject.
- The "extra 40% off" numeral is large and sits at the top, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The "Shop Now" button is visible and clear.
- Barcodes and codes sit near the bottom (extra 40% off, $10 off $50+), so the coupons are easy to redeem.
- Concrete price points (19.99, 299.99 vs. Orig. 349.99) make the product grid tangible.

## What's weak

- The "ENDS TONIGHT" stack repeats the text with faded, partly clipped lines, so it reads as a visual glitch next to the Kohl's Rewards badge.
- The body has too many competing offers: 40% coupon, Kohl's Cash, $10 off $50+, 40% off shoes and Levi's. Nothing tells you which one to use first.
- The recycled shell hurts the cadence. Oct 17 and Oct 18 already ran the same layout, so the Oct 18 email feels like a seventh near-copy.
- The hero's "Shop Now" doesn't say which category it leads to, and the Home / Women / Men / Kids buttons add a second nav layer before any product.
- The disclaimers are long and dense, and the email as a whole is very long to scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body for last-day sends. Keep the hero, the Kohl's Cash strip and one product row, and drop the Levi's, shoe and decorating sections.
- 2. Fix the "ENDS TONIGHT" stack. Use one clean line with a time ("Ends 11:59 PM CT tonight").
- 3. Put the coupon code in or right under the hero so people don't scroll to the bottom to find it.
- 4. Make the CTA more specific, for example "Shop 40% off now".
- 5. Make the Kohl's Cash message one line: earn $10 for every $50 spent, with the dates.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 40% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 40% off + $10 Kohl's Cash ⏳`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your coupon expires Oct. 18. Tap to shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash per $50. Free returns through Jan 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray closing tag, an empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport) and many images without alt text. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because they depend on headers the relay didn't capture and on HTML that was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not verified:** 64 links were skipped as click-redirect domains (for example `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…`). The QA data can't confirm their destinations or status codes.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?DUTZPNXUQU3EVLH6KLNGCR4GWM.60266&d=60266&bmt=0" … alt=""></custom>` has a `</custom>` with no opening tag. It is invalid HTML.
- **Four separate open or tracking beacons:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d2355805-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  This is redundant but not broken. Open rates will be unreliable wherever images are proxied or blocked.
- **Referrer meta tag:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is ignored by most email clients. It can't be relied on to suppress referrers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and move it and anything after it into the body.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it matches nothing. It should be `.ReadMsgBody`.
- **Missing alt attributes:**
  - The tracking pixels `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d2355805-…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - The `o.gif` pixel and the `open.aspx` pixel do have `alt=""` (the `o.gif` one with `aria-hidden`), so those two QA warnings are likely false positives.
  - The content images are not confirmed as decorative. These are `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/{2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233}.png` and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`.
  - If any of them carry the offer or CTA, the message is lost when images are blocked.
- **Layout tables:** the 1×1 pixel table and the table containing the demdex pixel lack `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables do have it.
- **Web fonts:** `@import` and `<link>` for Google Fonts are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Clients that strip them fall back to Arial/Helvetica, which is declared, so there is no functional issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible HTML. The rest of the source was truncated, so this isn't confirmed for the full message.
- Subscriber-level identifiers are embedded in image URLs: `mi_u=604230016` and `d_sid=13245196`. These are resolved, not broken. The campaign name is also exposed in `email_name=261018_DG_Email_Myster…`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** the QA check did not find either. It flags that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender failure. Check the raw message headers to confirm.
- **Physical address:** not detected in the truncated HTML. The footer was cut off, so this is **unconfirmed**.
- **`Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** the header is absent, as expected via the relay. Authentication status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a subdomain, but alignment can't be verified.
- The in-body unsubscribe link wasn't visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Destinations unverified:** click URLs are opaque ESP-wrapped redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), and none were resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- **Campaign ID only on image URLs:** campaign identifiers (`mi_ecmp=1024100_20261018`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) appear only on image and pixel URLs. Whether the click-throughs carry matching attribution is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
2. Fix the markup:
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the schema.org `<div itemscope>` out of `<head>`, or convert it to JSON-LD.
   - Populate `<title>` and remove the empty favicon `<link>`.
   - Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the pixels that lack it. Add meaningful `alt` text to content images that carry the offer or CTAs, in particular the "40% off" and "Kohl's Cash" content.
4. Verify the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Check the full, untruncated HTML for the footer address and unsubscribe link.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm destinations and UTM/attribution parameters. The audit pipeline should follow `click.s.kohls.com` redirects instead of skipping them.
