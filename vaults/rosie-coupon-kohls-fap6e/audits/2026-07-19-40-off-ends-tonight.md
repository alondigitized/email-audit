# 40% off ENDS TONIGHT 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fifth 40%-off email in roughly 36 hours, following four sends on July 18–19 carrying the same core discount. The "ENDS TONIGHT 🚨" urgency in the subject is a near-direct echo of the "here today, gone tomorrow" send reviewed earlier today — and by now the repeat subscriber has been trained to read every "final call" as a soft deadline that will reset by morning. That cadence exhaustion is the ceiling on this email's performance, full stop.
- Inside the email, however, there's genuine new content worth opening for. The "Vacation feel. Staycation prices." hero is the freshest creative in this entire run — visual, seasonal, and distinct from the coupon-coupon-coupon monotony. More importantly, the clearance callout (up to 70% off) is a real number that hasn't appeared in any prior send: for a deal-aware subscriber who skipped the last four, this is the hook that might actually land.
- Execution is clean — no render bugs, Kohl's Cash loyalty hook near the top, and a clear top-to-bottom hierarchy from hero offer → category grid → clearance. But the CTAs are all generic "Shop Now," the 40% requires a buried coupon, and no product specificity gives the click decision something to grip. This is a professionally assembled email undermined entirely by the send cadence around it.

## What's working

- **Clearance escalation is the real story.** Up to 70% off is the first new number in this entire campaign run — the most likely reason a subscriber who deleted the prior four would stop scrolling.
- **Hero creative is genuinely seasonal.** "Vacation feel. Staycation prices." is timely summer positioning and visually well-executed — the strongest lifestyle imagery in this run.
- **Kohl's Cash loyalty hook is visible above the fold.** Serves loyalty members without making them hunt for it.
- **Category grid gives multiple entry points.** Women / Men / Kids / Accessories tabs in both the main body and clearance section reduce friction for different shopper types.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout end-to-end; all images load, no overlapping or broken elements.

## What's weak

- **Fifth 40%-off email in 36 hours destroys the urgency premise.** "ENDS TONIGHT" means nothing when "tonight" has ended three times already this week.
- **"Shop Now" does no conversion work.** Used across every CTA in the email with no verb+noun specificity — "Shop Vacation Styles," "Claim Your Coupon," or "Shop 70% Off Clearance" would each do real work.
- **40% off requires a coupon, buried in fine print.** The headline number isn't the real offer; repeat subscribers have learned to distrust it.
- **🚨 in the subject reads as desperation.** After four consecutive urgency sends, a siren emoji signals volume, not value.
- **No product anchor.** No featured item, no star-rated product callout, no price-before/after — nothing to connect the click to a specific thing the shopper wants.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with clearance, not the recycled 40%.** "Up to 70% off" is the freshest number in this email; move it above the fold as the hero offer and let the 40% coupon be the secondary story.
- 2. **Give every CTA a specific job.** Replace all "Shop Now" instances with destination-specific copy: "Shop Women's Sandals," "Claim 40% Off," "Browse Clearance Markdowns."
- 3. **Name a specific product in the hero section.** One featured item with a before/after price gives the click something concrete to land on.
- 4. **Acknowledge the cadence, even briefly.** A single honest line — "Tonight's the last call, for real" — earns more trust than the fourth consecutive performative deadline.
- 5. **Retire 🚨 from the subject line.** Replace with something seasonal (☀️, 🌊) that reinforces the staycation creative instead of flagging urgency fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 40% off + clearance up to 70%`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only — new clearance + 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns just dropped. Your 40% coupon is inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Vacation staples from $14.99. Clearance starts at 70% off.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off ENDS TONIGHT" (2026-07-19)

### 1. Technical Summary

One unresolved merge token (`%%offer_evnt_nm%%`) constitutes a broken send — recipients received a raw template variable. Compliance headers are absent or unverifiable through the relay, and 13 content images lack alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all outbound links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`; HTTP probing was blocked. Destination URL validity and UTM parameter integrity cannot be confirmed from this audit.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is set at the document level. This strips the `Referer` header on click-throughs, which can silently break any server-side referrer-based attribution that runs alongside UTM params.

**Empty favicon href** — `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` will trigger a 404 against the base URL in some rendering environments.

**Redundant font loading** — Roboto and Montserrat are loaded three times each: once via `<link>`, once via `@import` inside an `<!--[if !mso]>` block, and a second `@import` for Montserrat. In webmail clients that process all three, this causes redundant network requests.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Invalid HTML — stray `</custom>` tag:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?KJMJNWJ4..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is orphaned; there is no matching `<custom>` open tag. Strict parsers may mis-render surrounding content.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` causes some email clients (notably Outlook on Windows) to display the subject line in the tab/window title bar instead of a cleaner fallback.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` prevents pinch-to-zoom. This violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) for subscribers with low vision.

**13 content images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed). Notable examples:
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png` — likely a hero/offer image; no alt
- `611d2b1debdc5ae0.png` / `b0aad5bed92b660a.png` — URLs include `left_promo_code=LPJ9Y27GJLXBX`, suggesting these are promo code display images with no text fallback

Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `f3d514fb…`) are also flagged, but `alt=""` is the correct value for 1×1 pixels — confirm these have `alt=""` set rather than the attribute omitted entirely.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL] Unresolved token: `%%offer_evnt_nm%%`**

A raw Eloqua/CDP merge token was delivered to recipients. This is a broken send — the variable was not substituted before dispatch. Identify the upstream template step where substitution occurs and confirm the fallback value is defined; absent that, the send should not have exited QA.

---

### 5. Compliance

**No physical mailing address detected in the HTML excerpt.** CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. If it exists below the truncation point, no issue; if not, this is a legal violation.

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — the QA relay did not capture it. Gmail and Yahoo (Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) require this header for >5k/day senders. If Kohl's is above that threshold and the header is genuinely missing, deliverability to both providers is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — one-click unsubscribe is now required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders. Missing this header alongside `List-Unsubscribe` compounds the deliverability risk.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown through the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm the sending domain `s.kohls.com` is properly authenticated. This should be verified at the MTA level.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM validation blocked** — all 22 click links use click-redirect wrappers; final destination URLs and their UTM parameters could not be probed. Manual spot-check of unwrapped URLs recommended before treating attribution as clean.

**`email_name=260719_DG_Email_Mystery`** appears in multiple tracking pixel URLs (e.g., `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png?...&email_name=260719_DG_Email_Mystery`). The `_Mystery` suffix is likely an internal campaign name placeholder that was not replaced — a secondary token substitution failure separate from `%%offer_evnt_nm%%`. Verify this is intentional and not a second unresolved variable.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** (repeated from §2) — referrer stripping applies to site-side referrer checks as well, which may interfere with any server-side campaign landing page logic that falls back to referrer when UTM params are absent.
