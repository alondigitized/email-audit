# 🚂 Get 20% off + Take $10 off your $50+ cart AND explore The North Kohl's 🚂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Kohl's send in three days to pitch the 20% off plus $10 off $50+ stack. The Oct 26 "Stack an extra 20% off + $10 off your $50+ purchase 😍" and Oct 27 "20% off + $10 off your $50+ purchase = holiday gifting made easy 🎁" sent the same offer. This one leads with the offer in the subject, but the render is mostly "The North Kohl's" storytelling. The top hero is the gift box plus Kohl's Cash visual with "Ends Nov 1 | Shop Now". Below it is a big illustrated holiday-village concept, which first appeared in the Oct 26 "Welcome to The North Kohl's" send.
- The creative is charming and distinctive. A snowy village, an elf and themed vignettes (cozy corner, kitchen, toy box) feel like a real change from the price-grid sends. The weakness is that the concept section runs four tall blocks, each with a separate "Shop Now" and almost no price or product detail. The actual coupons, with codes SAVE20, GOSHOP10 and JEWELRY10, only show up as barcodes at the very bottom. Someone who wants the 20% has to scroll past roughly 80% of the email.
- Judgement: a good brand-feel send that under-delivers on its own subject line. The offer is clear at the top, and the middle is pretty but slow. Moving the coupon strip up and tightening the concept would turn it into a strong send.

## What's working

- The hero shows the gift-box and Kohl's Cash visual with "Ends Nov 1" and a "Shop Now" link, so the deadline and offer are visible at the top.
- The Kohl's Rewards status line ("KOHL'S REWARDS® MEMBER, Earn: 5% REWARDS") is personalized and shows loyalty benefits.
- The North Kohl's illustrated village is distinctive and seasonal, and it stands out from the price-catalog grids of the past week.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product row shows real prices ($29.99 Ninja SLUSH, $2.99–9.99 towels, $52 jeans).
- The footer shows each coupon with its code, barcode and expiry date, which makes redemption easy in store.

## What's weak

- The 20% and $10 off coupons are buried at the bottom, even though the subject promises them.
- Four large themed sections each carry their own "Shop Now" with no prices, so the eye has nothing concrete to land on.
- The copy ("the softest spot on the map", "fast track to favorite-parent status") is cute but doesn't say what is on offer.
- The email is very long, with dense footer fine print and a "Coupons do not apply to select brands" disclaimer.
- The email repeats the same stack offer as Oct 26 and Oct 27, which adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a compact coupon strip ("20% off with code SAVE20 + $10 off $50+, ends Nov 1") directly under the hero, and make that the first CTA.
- 2. Cut the village to two vignettes. Put a price or "from $X" and a specific product on each.
- 3. Move "Picked with you in mind" higher, since it is the only section with real prices.
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Get my 20% off", instead of a generic "Shop Now".
- 5. Put the exclusions closer to the offer so it feels more honest.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon is inside The North Kohl's 🚂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVE20, plus take $10 off $50+. Ends Nov 1.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 68 tracking links. These are `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects with opaque payloads, so destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Four tracking beacons before any content:**

  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024675_20261028`

- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in every Movable Ink URL. This is standard for Movable Ink, but it is a per-recipient ID in image URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - Content-style images on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`.
  - Pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`, both with no `alt`.
  - I'd treat the `rp/*.png` images as the real problem, since they likely carry the offers. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing from them.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, so it is already handled correctly.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request back to the document URL in some clients.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will usually move it into the body, which can add an unintended element.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stacks fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. No issue found.
- **Tables:** `role="presentation"` is used on the layout tables, and the container is 640px with a mobile breakpoint at 500px. No issue found.
