# Get 10% off must-haves AND a FREE gift with purchase ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a lip-balm edit under a "Mistletoe Must-Haves" banner, sent as part of Sephora's ongoing Savings Event. It's a strong product story, but it doesn't match the subject line. The subject promises "10% off" and a "FREE gift with purchase." In the render, the top of the email leads with a free same-day delivery banner (code SPEEDY) and a "Stack with other offers" strip. The first thing the eye lands on is the "MISTLETOE MUST-HAVES / SHOP NOW" hero, not the 10% or the gift. I could not read the screenshot clearly enough to confirm where, or whether, the gift with purchase appears in the body.
- The body is a vertical stack of limited-edition lip products. Summer Fridays, Laneige, Eadem, Olehenriksen, Kayali, Gisou and Experiment each get a flavor-named product shot, a "Clean at Sephora" badge and a price around $24. The imagery is appetizing, the flavor names are easy to scan, and nothing looks broken. Near the bottom, "Best of Concealer," "Fragrance Gift Sets," a 250-point reward option and a holiday gift guide dilute an otherwise focused email.
- Verdict: this works as a pretty product catalog and is weak as an offer email. It is also the same-week cadence problem from 11/3 ("💎 10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY 💎") and 11/2 ("STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍"). This send changes the products shown and the gift angle in the subject, but the 10% message hasn't moved.

## What's working

- The "Mistletoe Must-Haves" hero ties the email to the season and gives it a clear theme.
- The product modules share one layout: brand, tag line, flavor names and price. They are easy to scan.
- The "NEW FLAVORS / ONLY AT SEPHORA / LIMITED EDITION" labels add real scarcity and novelty.
- The free same-day delivery offer has a code (SPEEDY) and a visible deadline (11/9).
- The render shows no broken images or overlapping text, even at this length.

## What's weak

- The subject's two offers, 10% off and a free gift, are not the hero. The hero is a themed banner with a generic "Shop Now."
- The email is very long, and the last third (concealer, fragrance, points, gift guide) pulls attention away from the lip story.
- Prices of about $24 sit in small text under each product, and nothing shows what 10% off does to them.
- The "Clean at Sephora" badge repeats between modules and takes space without adding information.
- Several offers compete: free delivery, 10% off, a gift with purchase and 250 points. None of them clearly leads.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the subject's promise in the hero. Show "10% off + free gift with purchase" as the largest element, with an unambiguous button such as "Shop lip must-haves."
- 2. Show sale prices on each product, for example "$24 → $21.60 with 10% off," so the discount is visible where the decision happens.
- 3. Cut the email to the lip story plus one secondary module, and drop the concealer, fragrance and gift-guide blocks.
- 4. Add reviews or a bestseller badge to at least one product to give some social proof.
- 5. Break the same-week pattern of "10% off" subjects by sending fewer, more differentiated emails.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback:** The text part is 0 chars [FAIL]. Without it, spam filters may score the message worse and text-only clients get nothing. If the relay stripped it, this is a capture artifact. As captured, it is a failure.
- **Missing alt text:** Both flagged images are the hidden tracking pixels above. The practical impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
