# Enjoy an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seventh send in the same tier-stacking cycle since 09-10, and it's the most cluttered execution of the bunch. The status bar ("Rewards Member · Earn 5% Rewards") and three-tier stack (30% Card/20% Rewards/15% Nonmembers) are now boilerplate I recognize instantly from the past six sends — familiarity is turning into wallpaper-blindness. Where the 09-12 "Home essentials" send earned a 7 by leading with a thematic hook, this one reverts to a kitchen-sink layout: fall lifestyle shot, sweaters, shoes, clearance banner, then a random four-item grid (a slushie maker, a bath towel, jeans, a multi-cooker) with zero connective thread.
- The barcode-in-email touch ("View coupons in Wallet") is a nice mobile-first execution detail, and the pricing is concrete throughout. But the email has no single hero product or moment — it's a scroll of six separate merchandising blocks competing for attention, which is a step backward from 09-12's cleaner thematic version.
- Judgment: mid-pack, dragged down by cadence fatigue and a hero section that's now indistinguishable from prior sends. 5/10 territory.

## What's working

- Concrete, layered pricing throughout (sweaters "starting at $29.99," shoes "starting at $19.99," clearance "up to 80% off") gives multiple price-conscious entry points.
- The coupon code (REWARDS20) and barcode are both visible directly in the email, reducing friction to redeem in-store.
- Category nav bars (Women/Men/Kids/Home) appear twice, giving quick wayfinding for a scanning reader.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid uses real product photography with clear pricing per item, no broken images or placeholder text.

## What's weak

- No single hero product — the email stacks sweaters, shoes, clearance, and a random four-item grid (drink maker, towel, jeans, slow cooker) with no unifying theme, unlike 09-12's "Home essentials" send.
- The three-tier offer stack at top is now visually identical to the last four sends — a recognizable subscriber sees the same maroon/pink card layout and may tune it out.
- "Ends Sept. 20" appears small and easy to miss at the very top; there's no second urgency callout near the CTA or coupon code lower in the email.
- The "Picked with you in mind" personalization claim is undercut by an incoherent product mix (a cocktail maker next to a bath towel) — it reads as generic cross-sell, not curation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lifestyle/category hook (as 09-12 did with "Home essentials") and reorganize the body around it instead of stacking six unrelated merchandising blocks — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Cut the "Picked with you in mind" grid or make it genuinely coherent (e.g., all home items, or all items tied to the fall theme) — a slushie maker next to a bath towel undermines the personalization claim.
- 3. Repeat the "Ends Sept. 20" deadline in bold near the coupon code/barcode section at the bottom, not just once at the top.
- 4. Vary the three-tier offer card's visual treatment (color, layout) from the last several sends so repeat subscribers don't pattern-match it as identical.
