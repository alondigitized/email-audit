# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-program email that is trying to be a catalog. The subject asks "Did you know that points = rewards?" and the top block answers with "Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points" and a "Join the Challenge" button. Straight below it sits a second hero, "Rewards Bazaar: October rewards are here!", with its own "Shop Now" button. Two heroes with two CTAs split the attention before the reader reaches the content.
- The rest is a very long Rewards Bazaar catalog. A Laura Mercier brand spotlight comes first, then a 750+ point rewards grid. Each item shows a point cost and an "X available now" count. The scarcity counts (12, 16, 1 available) are a good touch. The point tiers run up to 30,000 for the New York hotel stay, so most of the page is out of reach for a typical member. It also reads as a repeat of the Rewards Bazaar block in the 10/2 "Up to 50% off hair and brow" send, and it follows a 10/6 points-multiplier email by hours.
- Verdict: a competent member-engagement send with no price offer, a muddled top and a long scroll. It is not a conversion driver.

## What's working

- The point cost and live stock count on every reward ("350 available now", "1 available now") create credible scarcity.
- The Rewards Bazaar section is on-brand for Sephora Insider, with consistent product photography and clean tile spacing.
- The mix of price points (350 up to 30,000) shows the range of what points can buy.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlaps.
- The "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE" badge on the hero gives a clear reason to look now.

## What's weak

- The subject is a vague teaser. The "points = rewards" idea doesn't map to a specific offer, and the body opens with "up to 500 points" instead.
- Two stacked hero blocks ("Join the Challenge" and "Shop Now") fight each other.
- It's a long catalog with no clear single product, so nothing pulls the eye to one reward.
- It repeats the Rewards Bazaar creative seen on 10/2, and it lands the same day as the "2X points… ends tomorrow" send, so cadence feels heavy.
- The challenge terms ("up to 500 points") sit behind an asterisk and dense fine print. Nothing visible says what the challenge is.
- The top-tier rewards (30,000 points) are aspirational. The bottom third of the email is low-relevance for most members.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with either the 500-point challenge or the Bazaar, and move the other lower or into a single line.
- 2. Cut the catalog to 3–4 rewards, and favor the ones a member can afford at 350–1,000 points.
- 3. Put the challenge mechanics in the visible copy: what to do, by when, and how many points.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the challenge so the urgency is credible.
- 5. Tell members what their balance can buy. Personalizing to their points total would be the biggest single lift.
