# Save an extra 20% ... now's the time to shop 🛒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall. It's the fifth or sixth send in four days, and it comes after the Nov 11 "LAST DAY for $10 off your $25+" and the Nov 9 "Save an extra 20% 🙌" sends. The render is legible at this size. It opens on a red "Ends Nov. 21 · Shop Now" hero with a Kohl's Cash tag, then runs through sweaters, Lauren Conrad, Gloria Vanderbilt jeans, gifting, a toy coupon, a "Deals Preview" teaser and a home-goods grid. The thumbnail shows no obvious broken images.
- The weakness is focus. The subject promises "an extra 20%" with "now's the time", but the hero is a banner with a Kohl's Cash graphic and tiny text. The 20% offer is never the dominant element I can make out. About ten different offers compete: $24.99 sweaters, $29.99 jeans, $10 off toys, $10 off home, $5 Kohl's Cash, and Door Busters coming Nov. 22. A subscriber who has seen this same coupon pushed all week gets little reason to act today.
- Judgement: competent and seasonal, but generic and over-frequent. It earns a middling score, and fatigue is the biggest risk.

## What's working

- A persistent top nav (Home, Women, Men, Kids) sits right under the hero, so browsing paths are clear.
- Concrete price points ($24.99 sweaters, $29.99 jeans, $2.99–$9.99 towels) give real value anchors.
- The "Gifts Under $25" and "Gift Shop" tiles fit the holiday season.
- The Kohl's Rewards member status bar at the top personalizes the email, and the Kohl's Cash tag adds loyalty value.
- The "Preview Deals Now" teaser for Door Busters (Nov. 22) gives subscribers a reason to come back.

## What's weak

- The subject's "extra 20%" has no clear hero. The top banner text is small and the offer is buried.
- Too many offers and CTAs fragment attention, and the scroll is very long.
- The fine print is dense and the coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom are cramped, so they're hard to use.
- Cadence is fatigued: it's near-identical to the Nov 9–12 sends with the same $10-off and 20%-off language.
- "Now's the time to shop" is vague urgency with no stated deadline in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one hero: a big "Extra 20% off" with a code or auto-applied note, the end date, and a single "Shop 20% off" button above the fold.
- 2. Cut the body to 3–4 modules, with the coupon codes in one clear block near the top.
- 3. Suppress or throttle sends to recent openers so the same coupon isn't repeated daily.
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" CTAs with specific verbs and nouns, like "Shop sweaters from $24.99".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (empty `<title>`, invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, zoom-blocking viewport) and uses several third-party tracking pixels. Most compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the headers weren't captured and the HTML is truncated. Click destinations are hidden behind tracking redirects, so UTM and landing-page alignment couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 78 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Links use opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...` tokens, so destination, status and redirect chain are unverified.
- **Four separate open/tracking pixels** appear before the content:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/c61a815c-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025734_20261112`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  These add third-party requests, and the demdex call is a cross-domain Adobe Audience Manager sync.
- **Malformed markup:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag (`...&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`), which has no matching opening tag.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request to the page URL in some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` containing `<meta itemprop>` tags sits inside `<head>`. This is invalid, and clients will drop or relocate it. The schema.org markup likely has no effect.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - The QA check flags seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`). These appear to be content images, though the source is truncated, so I can't confirm their role.
  - It also flags three tracking pixels (`c61a815c-...`, `o.gif`, `demdex.net/event`). Only `o.gif` has `aria-hidden`, and the other two have no `alt` at all.
- **Font loading:** Google Fonts are loaded through `<link>` plus `@import` for Roboto and Montserrat. These are stripped in Gmail and some Outlook builds, so the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback applies. The fallback is declared, so this is informational.
- **Doctype/syntax mix:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is harmless in practice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the available source. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only what was provided.
- The pixel URLs carry a persistent recipient identifier (`mi_u=604230016`) and a message ID (`mi_mid=01a1ce57-...`). This is expected for Movable Ink, but the identifier is exposed in image URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe headers:** QA reports `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected. The footer is cut off in the truncated source, so this is unconfirmed. Check the full HTML.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- **Plain-text part:** 13,788 of 19,552 characters (about 70.5%) are URLs. This is a spam-scoring risk, driven by the long tracking URLs.
- The visible HTML shows no unsubscribe link, but it's truncated, so I can't confirm one is missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM unverified:** no `utm_*` parameters are visible. All links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`, so UTM presence can't be verified from the source.
- **Attribution parameters:** attribution appears to rely on the redirect token and Movable Ink parameters. The visible ones are `mi_ecmp=1025734_20261112` and `email_name=261112_DG_Email_Thursd...`.
- **Landing-page alignment:** not testable, because the redirects were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
2. Fill in `<title>`, and remove or fix the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
3. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or drop it, and delete the stray `</custom>` tag.
4. Add descriptive `alt` to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images, and use `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` on the tracking pixels.
5. Consolidate redundant tracking pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif`, `open.aspx`, `demdex.net/event`) where possible.
6. Re-run the audit with the full raw message source (not the truncated HTML or the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer address, and the unsubscribe link.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects to check final URLs, status codes and `utm_*` parameters.
