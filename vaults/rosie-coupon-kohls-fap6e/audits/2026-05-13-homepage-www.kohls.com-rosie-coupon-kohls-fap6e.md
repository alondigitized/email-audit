# Homepage snapshot · www.kohls.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Oh good, they put the coupons right up front — I can see "30%, 20% or 15% off" and "15% off in store & online" before I even get to the hero. That's Kohl's knowing their customer. The hero itself is pushing patio and outdoor living, which, honestly? I wasn't planning to buy a chair today, but now I'm looking at that cushion. Very Kohl's of them.

## What's working

- **"2 Coupons" accordion section** — This is the first thing I see after the search bar. Two live offers laid out in cards I can tap into. That's how you greet Rosie.
- **Kohl's Card tier callout** — "30%, 20% or 15% off with a Kohl's Card" tells me exactly which discount bracket I'm in. No guessing, no clicking "details" for the basics.
- **"15% off in store & online"** — I can stack this. That's the magic word. "In store & online" means I can screenshot it at the register.
- **"The home upgrade you won't overthink (or overspend on)"** — That parenthetical is doing real work. Budget-conscious, self-aware, a little funny. That's my kind of copy.
- **"My Store: Alhambra"** — Recognizing I'm a local regular feels good. Makes the app feel like it knows me.

## What's weak

- **Sponsored OUAI banner** — A paid ad for a hair repair balm drops right between my coupons and the hero. It breaks the rhythm and feels like I'm being sold to before Kohl's even talks to me.
- **No Kohl's Cash or rewards balance** — If I have $30 Kohl's Cash sitting there, I want to see it the moment I open the app. That's what gets me into the cart.
- **No Sephora at Kohl's mention** — For a shopper like me, Sephora is the in-store anchor. Nothing above the fold nods to it.
- **CTA is a text link, not a button** — "Shop Patio and Outdoor Living" is underlined text on a dark hero. A clear button would get more taps, especially on a phone screen.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if I'm $12 away from free shipping before I decide whether to add something.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the rewards balance in the header or right below My Store** — Show me "You have $30 Kohl's Cash" before I see anything else. That's conversion bait that costs nothing to display.
- **Add a "Sephora at Kohl's" promo strip above or below the coupon section** — Even a one-liner: "Sephora deals inside — plus your 15% off." That cements my reason to tap deeper.
- **Replace the OUAI sponsored ad slot with a Kohl's-owned promo strip** — A "Free shipping on $49+" bar would do more for conversion than a third-party beauty ad I didn't ask for.
- **Make "Shop Patio and Outdoor Living" a pill button** — White text on a filled red or dark button, same width as the hero image. More tappable, cleaner on mobile.
