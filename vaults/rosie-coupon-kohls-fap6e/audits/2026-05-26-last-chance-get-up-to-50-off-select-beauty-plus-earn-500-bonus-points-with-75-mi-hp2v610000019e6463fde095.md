# LAST CHANCE 🥺 Get up to 50% off select beauty—plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 min. spend.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Stacking Weekend" deadline push — the same dual-incentive creative from the May 22 send (50% off select beauty + 500 bonus points at $75) now dressed in LAST CHANCE urgency. Sephora is playing the classic "we reminded you, now we're closing the loop" move. The hero is immediately legible: the 50% OFF badge and bonus-points call are stacked visually and land without scrolling. Below the fold, the email pivots into a long-scroll content stack — must-have product picks, Auto-Replenish, a new color launch, Limited-Edition shade, and a store locator — which dilutes the urgency the top half builds.
- The execution is competent and on-brand, but the email is doing too many jobs at once. When a deadline email runs this long, the urgency bleeds out before the subscriber finishes. The subject line has the right information but is nearly 100 characters — it will truncate badly in most mobile clients, and "LAST CHANCE" in all-caps is a minor spam-pattern signal. The core offer is honest and well-supported; the send is just bloated.
- Compared to the May 22 original, this closing push adds meaningful deadline pressure but loses the cleanliness of a single-focused send. The cadence is also getting heavy: five Sephora emails in thirteen days. A subscriber who noticed the May 22 "Stacking Weekend" email and didn't convert is the right target; anyone else may be starting to tune out.

## What's working

- **Hero lands hard and fast** — "50% OFF" is the first thing the eye hits; the points incentive is stacked directly beneath it with a visible SHOP NOW CTA.
- **Dual-incentive structure is still compelling** — discount + loyalty points in a single transaction is a strong hook and is clearly communicated.
- **"LAST CHANCE" product grid below the fold** — titled section with actual product imagery adds credible scarcity to specific SKUs.
- **Beauty Insider branding consistent throughout** — loyalty tier identity is woven into the footer, reinforcing membership value.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Subject is ~97 characters** — "LAST CHANCE 🥺 Get up to 50% off select beauty—plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 min. spend." will truncate after "select beauty" on most mobile clients, losing the points hook entirely.
- **"LAST CHANCE" in all-caps** — this is the same signal Sephora's spam filter would flag in a competitor's email; it's a minor trust/deliverability friction point.
- **Email is too long for a deadline push** — after the urgency hero, the email layers in Auto-Replenish, Color Work, Shop & Ship, and a store locator. Each section is fine in isolation, but stacked under a closing-sale hook they sap momentum.
- **No personalization visible** — the May 14 and May 20 triggered emails used Rosie's name prominently; this house-file blast drops that entirely, making it feel generic after the personalized flow.
- **Same hero creative as May 22** — visually identical "Stacking Weekend" layout. A subscriber who opened four days ago sees an almost identical top half and may dismiss it as a duplicate send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tighten the subject to under 55 characters** — move the specific offer into the preheader and make the subject do one job: create urgency + signal the discount. The full offer doesn't need to be in the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `LAST CHANCE: 50% off beauty ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Stacking Weekend deal expires soon 🥺`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn 500 bonus points on $75+ — today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `500 bonus points still on the table. Shop before it's gone.`
- 2. **Swap the hero visual** — even a headline copy swap ("Final Hours" over "Stacking Weekend") or a countdown-style graphic differentiates this from the May 22 original and signals to the subscriber that something has changed.
- 3. **Cut everything below the "LAST CHANCE Must-Haves" product grid** — remove Auto-Replenish, Color Work, Shop & Ship, and store locator from this specific send. Deadline emails should end at the offer. Move those modules to a standalone send after the sale.
- 4. **Add a visible deadline** — "Offer ends tonight at midnight PT" somewhere in or directly beneath the hero CTA. The subject says "last chance" but the email body never states when it expires, which weakens the urgency.
