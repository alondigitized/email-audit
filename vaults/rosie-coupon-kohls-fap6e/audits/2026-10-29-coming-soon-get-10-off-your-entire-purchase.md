# COMING SOON: Get 10% off your entire purchase‼️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Savings Event teaser, and it is stronger than the daily catalog sends in your recent history. The top block holds a single idea: a gold "30% OFF" medallion for the Sephora Collection (10/30–11/9) and a white "10% OFF" medallion for Insiders (11/3–11/9), under a "COMING SOON" banner. The hero is large and the dates are visible. The offers are tiered, so a base-tier Insider has to read the fine print to learn that the big discount is on Sephora's own brand.
- The subject line ("Get 10% off your entire purchase") oversells. It promises one clean 10%, but the body shows two tiers and several code variants (ALLONSALE, SCSAVE, in-store mention). The "COMING SOON" framing is also soft. The email asks the reader to do nothing today except "Build Your Basket."
- Below the hero, the email slides back into the usual long catalog column: category tiles, six "Save on These Faves" products, an editorial block, a Seth Rogen gift beauty block, and a holiday gift guide. That is a lot of scroll for a teaser, and it dilutes a good headline. The verdict is a strong hero on a weak and overlong send. It's the best event email in the recent run, but it's still a reminder, not a conversion.

## What's working

- The two medallions (30% off Sephora Collection, 10% off for Insiders) are the first thing the eye lands on, with clear dates.
- "Insider, Get Ready to Save on Everything" frames the email for members and sets expectations.
- Specific codes (ALLONSALE, SCSAVE) tell the reader how to redeem online and at kohls.com.
- The category tiles (Makeup, Skincare, Hair, Fragrance, Value Sets, Tools & Brushes) give clear paths into the catalog.
- "Beauty on Demand" (pick up in store, curbside, same-day delivery, free shipping) removes fulfillment friction.

## What's weak

- The 10% off is dated 11/3–11/9, so the offer in the subject is not live yet. The "COMING SOON" subject and the hero both ask for a future action.
- "Entire purchase" in the subject conflicts with the 30% Sephora Collection tier and the exclusions in the fine print.
- The "Build Your Basket" CTA is vague. It doesn't say what to do now or where it leads.
- Product picks ("Save on These Faves") show prices ($168, $24, $58, $165) but no discount, which undercuts the savings message.
- The email runs very long, with multiple competing blocks (Spot it. Shop it., Skincare Gift Sets, Seth Rogen, Holiday Gift Guide) after the hero. The fine print is dense and small, and the dates and exclusions are hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero: lead with the real deal and the date. If the date is the hook, say so.
- 2. Cut the lower half. Keep the hero, the category tiles and one product row. A teaser should drive one action: add to wish list or save the date.
- 3. Replace "Build Your Basket" with a verb-and-noun CTA tied to what is live now, such as "Save Your Faves to Your Loves List."
- 4. Put the discount on the product cards (for example, "was $X, now $Y on 11/3"), so the savings are concrete.
- 5. Pull the code and date details out of the small copy and into a short, readable line under the medallions.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Insider sale starts 11/3: 10% off, 30% off Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Save the date: Sephora Savings Event, 11/3–11/9`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sephora Collection is 30% off from 10/30. Insiders get 10% off 11/3–11/9. Codes inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Build your wish list now so checkout is fast when the sale opens.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-personalization defect (empty greeting slot) and no plain-text part. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay did not capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible plus the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links go through the Epsilon/Sephora redirect domain, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1880a14f693af7d6e96c569c8/...?__dU__=...`. No destination URLs are visible in the truncated source.
- Only 12 of 52 links were probed (40 skipped), so the unprobed links are unverified. The QA output reports no probe failures for the 12 that were checked.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...` (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`). It is hidden, so the missing alt text flagged by QA has no practical accessibility impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL, 0 chars):** the email is HTML-only. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. The only responsive rules are inside `@media (max-width:480px)`, so clients that ignore media queries (e.g. Gmail with non-Google accounts) will render a 700px layout on narrow screens.
- **Malformed `<style>` block:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS, so the rule is likely ignored or may break parsing in some clients.
- **No document language or generic title:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute and `<title>` is just "Sephora".
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no email impact but is invalid.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting slot (QA FAIL):** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` because the name field is empty. The cause is either a missing first-name fallback or no first name on the recipient record.
- **Empty JSON-LD discount code:** the JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`. A blank code field was shipped in the schema markup, and the `PromotionCard` object has no properties.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** these are not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be assessed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters cannot be verified. Links use opaque redirect tokens (`__dU__=...`), and the destination URLs are not exposed in the captured source or QA data.
- Landing-page alignment is not assessable with the available evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the first-name token (e.g. `Hi there,` when empty) or suppress the greeting. This fixes the confirmed `Hi ,` defect.
2. Generate a multipart/alternative plain-text version. This fixes the 0-char text part.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block into a separate `<style>` or standalone conditional.
5. Remove or populate the empty `discountCode` JSON-LD object and the empty `PromotionCard`.
6. Add `lang="en"` and a descriptive `<title>`, and correct the robots meta to `noindex`.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the redirect destinations and UTMs.
