# 🎉 EPIC DEALS START TODAY 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's dropping the daily 30/40% coupon framing entirely and going wide with a category-blitz "EPIC DEALS" layout — nine-plus separate deal tiles (Carter's, kitchen appliances, home, toys, clothing, towels, bras, dresses, flannels) plus a clearance banner plus two more coupon barcodes at the bottom. It's the most cluttered send in the recent streak, trading the single flat-percentage clarity of 09-19/09-20 for a scattershot "something for everyone" approach. Sitewide "Epic Deals" branding also reads generic — no specific date-driven event name, no clear single hero offer.
- The good news: it's genuinely varied (kids, home, toys, clothing, mattress toppers) and the 3x Rewards banner plus multiple stacked coupons at the bottom gives loyalty members real reasons to click. But there's no single dominant CTA — a subscriber's eye has nowhere obvious to land first, and the email is exhausting to scroll through on mobile. Compared to 09-20's flat 40% send (7.00/10), this trades a clean urgency hook for breadth, which likely hurts open/click intent even though it may perform fine for browsers already primed to shop.

## What's working

- Category nav bar (Home/Women/Men/Kids) up top gives quick wayfinding for anyone who does open.
- Multiple concrete price points are visible (50% off Carter's, up to 60% off Home, $3.99 towels, $21.99 bras) — real numbers, not vague "epic" language.
- 3x Rewards banner with expiration ("Ends Sept. 27") adds credible urgency for members.
- Product photography is clean and renders correctly — no broken images or overlapping text anywhere in the scroll.
- Multiple loyalty-specific coupons (toys $10 off, kids/baby $10 off, $10 Kohl's Cash per $50) reward existing members clearly.

## What's weak

- Subject line "🎉 EPIC DEALS START TODAY 🎉" is generic, emoji-bookended, and has zero specificity — no percentage, no category, no urgency beyond "today."
- No single hero offer — nine-plus deal tiles of similar visual weight mean the eye has nowhere to land first, unlike the flat-40%-coupon sends that led the week.
- The email is extremely long (six-plus screens of scrolling) with clearance, coupons, and legal footer stacked on top of the deal tiles — cognitive overload for a mobile skim.
- After a week of flat 40% coupon messaging (09-19, 09-20), this pivot to scattered percentage-off-by-category deals feels like a reset rather than a continuation, which may confuse subscribers expecting the same coupon.
- Bottom-of-email double coupon block (toys $10 off + kids/baby $10 off + $10 Kohl's Cash) repeats offers already shown above, adding redundancy rather than new info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero deal (the 50-60% off Home tile is the strongest visual/numeric combo) and lead with it above the fold; demote the rest to a secondary grid below, cutting total tile count roughly in half.
- 2. Replace the generic "EPIC DEALS START TODAY" subject with the strongest concrete number in the email — 60% off Home is the biggest percentage shown.
- 3. Consolidate the three separate coupon/barcode blocks at the bottom into one summary panel — right now Rewards members have to parse three near-identical offers.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the subject/hero, not just buried in the Rewards banner — "Ends Sept. 27" should be front and center given how many prior sends leaned on urgency.
- 5. Trim the email length — six screens is too long for a promo; cut clearance and the lowest-ticket tiles (towels, dresses) to tighten focus.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Epic Deals: 50-60% off, ends Sept. 27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn 3X Kohl's Cash through Sept. 27 — Rewards members only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Home, kids, toys & more — all on epic deal today`
