# Up to 40% off .... back to school means back to saving 💯
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest hero-to-subject match Kohl's has sent all week — "Up to 40% off... back to school means back to saving" leads directly into a school-hallway hero shot of a kid, a bold red banner reading "up to 40% off back-to-school essentials storewide," and category nav buttons (Women/Men/Girls/Boys/Shoes/Home) that broaden the pitch beyond just school supplies. It echoes yesterday's 8.00/10 "Save up to 40% during the Back-to-School Sale" send almost beat for beat — same offer tier, same seasonal framing, two days in a row.
- Below the fold it's the usual Kohl's daily-deal cascade: Bed & Bath $3.99, Shoes up to 40% off, Tees $7.99, Tops $12.99, Dresses up to 30% off, Beauty up to 50% off — each with a clear "Reg. $X.XX" strikethrough and a "Shop Now" link. It's dense but well-organized, and the pricing specificity is a genuine strength. The sweepstakes block and "Picked with you in mind" recommendation grid pad the email out further than necessary, but they don't undercut the core offer.
- This is a solid, on-brief send — not best-in-class flair, but no mismatches, no render bugs, and the back-to-school urgency (deadline through Aug 23) is real and visible. Call it a 7.5, rounding to 7 for the repetitiveness with yesterday's near-identical creative.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are perfectly aligned — "40% off... back to school" appears in the subject and again in the red banner directly under the hero image.
- Concrete, credible deadline ("Select styles on sale through August 23") gives real urgency instead of vague "limited time" language.
- Every product tile has specific pricing with strikethrough "Reg." comparison ($3.99 vs $4.99–$5.99, $7.99 vs $9.99, etc.) — this is honest, scannable pricing.
- Category nav buttons up top (Women/Men/Girls/Boys/Shoes/Home) give multiple entry points for different shopper needs in one send.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- This is essentially a rerun of yesterday's Back-to-School email (8/18: "Save up to 40% during the Back-to-School Sale") — same 40% figure, same seasonal hook, back-to-back sends risk fatigue for anyone who opened both.
- The email is very long — hero, 6 product categories, a sweepstakes CTA, a "fresh favorites" grid, and a second "picked for you" grid before hitting the footer; most scrollers won't make it past the fold.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" / "Coupons and Kohl's Cash do not apply" fine print sits directly under two offers (Shoes, Beauty), which chips away at the otherwise clean, honest-pricing feel.
- The sweepstakes block ("Want to win a $200 Kohl's Gift Card...") competes with the core sale message rather than reinforcing it — it's a detour mid-scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate today's send from yesterday's — if the offer tier (40%) hasn't moved, lead with a different angle (e.g., a specific bundle price or new category) rather than repeating the same headline framing two days running.
- 2. Move the "Coupons do not apply" disclaimers to a single footnote instead of interrupting the Shoes and Beauty offers directly — keeps the pricing feel honest without breaking flow.
- 3. Trim the email — cut either the sweepstakes block or the second "Picked with you in mind" grid; consolidate into one clear scroll rather than three stacked promo zones.
- 4. Move the Aug 23 deadline up into the subject line itself to sharpen urgency at the inbox level, not just in the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school 40% off ends Aug 23 — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 40% off school essentials, storewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bed & Bath from $3.99, shoes up to 40% off, tees $7.99 — through Aug 23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus, earn Kohl's Cash on every order.`
