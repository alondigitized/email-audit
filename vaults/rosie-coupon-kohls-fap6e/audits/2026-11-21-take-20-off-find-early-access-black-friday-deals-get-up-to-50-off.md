## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "Take 20% off | Early Access Black Friday"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, tracking-heavy build (84 click-redirect links, 4 tracking pixels). It has markup validity problems and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The header and footer compliance checks can't be confirmed from the data provided. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 84 links are click-redirects**, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...` and `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`. The QA probe skipped all of them, so no destination was verified (200, redirect chain or 404). Link health is unknown.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open**, from three vendors:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/95305427-...` (1x1, no `alt`, no `aria-hidden`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant open tracking and adds request weight.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (14,928 of 20,682 chars). This is caused by the long `qs=` tokens and is a known spam-filter signal.
- **Stray closing tag:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&bmt=0" ... alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on images.** QA flagged 12 or more, and the list is truncated. Affected images:
  - the pixels `95305427-...`, `o.gif` and `event`
  - content-looking images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233)
  - product images: `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_3_2x` and `60699446_4_2x`
  
  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer content is unavailable.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport meta** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:**
  - a `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared twice
- **Remote Google Fonts via `@import` and `<link>`.** These are stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so they fall back to Arial. The font stack handles this correctly.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and mobile breakpoints at 500px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) in the visible HTML.
- **Likely empty dynamic field:** the image URL `...mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p...` has an empty value. This is probably the Kohl's Cash redemption end date, which is blank for this recipient. It is worth checking that the module doesn't render a blank or broken date.
- The subscriber identifier `mi_u=604230016` is in the query string of every pixel and image URL. This is standard, but it is a persistent ID in third-party-fetchable URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results are not found.** The QA tool attributes these to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. They are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.
- **"No physical address detected"** is unconfirmed. The HTML is truncated before the footer, where the address and unsubscribe link normally sit.
- **No confirmed compliance issues.** The data is insufficient. Verify headers against the raw RFC 822 message.
- `From: "Kohl's" <Kohls@s.kohls.com>` uses a subdomain consistent with the click domain `click.s.kohls.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** The destinations are hidden behind opaque `qs=` tokens, so UTM and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- The campaign identifiers are on the image URLs only: `mi_ecmp=1026309_20261120` and `email_name=261120_DG_Email_Fri_PM`. The `20261120` matches today's send.
- Resolve a sample of redirects, including the 20% off and Early Access CTAs, and compare them to the subject's offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images. Add `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"` to the 1x1 pixels.
2. Fix the markup:
   - remove the stray `</custom>`
   - move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` or drop it
   - remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`
   - de-duplicate `format-detection`
   - fill in `<title>`
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Consolidate the four open pixels to reduce redundancy.
5. Reduce long `qs=` tokens, or ensure the plain-text part is trimmed, to lower the URL ratio.
6. Check the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` field for blank rendering.
7. Re-run QA against the raw message with full headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the untruncated footer. This will confirm or clear the three compliance warnings.
8. Resolve redirect chains for the 84 links to verify the final URLs and UTMs, and that no destination returns 404.
