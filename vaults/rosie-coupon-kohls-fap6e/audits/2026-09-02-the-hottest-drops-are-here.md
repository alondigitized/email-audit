# The hottest drops are here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is another Sephora "New Drops" product dump — "BYREDO HAS ARRIVED," Dior, rhode, OUAI, Jung Saem Mool, KAYALI, Touchland, amika, all stacked vertically with "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges. It's the same format as 8/30's "Your A-list-beauty edit" (also a product-block scroll), just with a different brand lineup. The subject "The hottest drops are here" is generic hype copy with zero specificity — no offer, no product name, nothing that separates it from a dozen other "new arrivals" sends.
- The one genuinely interesting hook — "50% off Select Beauty," complete with a "TODAY ONLY" badge — is crammed into the top banner above the actual hero, which is confusing: is this a discount email or a new-drops email? The layout answers that question by burying the discount and making "JUST DROPPED" the real hero, which undercuts the one concrete offer in the whole send.
- Given the brand's last five sends already ran variations of "50% off" (8/28, 8/29, 8/31) and "new arrivals dump" (8/30), this is cadence fatigue on two fronts at once — a stale discount banner glued onto a stale format. Score accordingly: mid-pack, nothing broken, nothing compelling.

## What's working

- Clean, legible product blocks — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent brand-name + tagline + price treatment throughout.
- "Clean at Sephora" and "Planet Aware" badges are visible on rhode and amika blocks, giving some values-based differentiation.
- The 15% off / Charlotte Tilbury two-up module partway down adds a second concrete offer beyond the top banner.
- BYREDO callout ("only at Sephora") is a genuine exclusive-brand hook that stands out from the generic new-arrival noise.

## What's weak

- Subject line "The hottest drops are here" carries zero information — no brand, no category, no urgency beyond adjective inflation.
- The one real offer (50% off, TODAY ONLY) is stranded in a tiny banner at the very top, disconnected from the "JUST DROPPED" hero that follows — visual hierarchy sends the eye to a product shot, not the discount.
- Eight-plus brand blocks in a row with no curation or "you'll love this because..." framing — it reads as a catalog scroll, same problem flagged in the 8/30 "A-list-beauty edit" email.
- No sizing/fit/ingredient detail on any product — just name, tagline, price, "shop now."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story: either lead with "50% off select beauty today only" as the hero (it's a proven, higher-scoring format per 8/28's 6.0/10) or lead with new-drops — don't stack both above the fold with no connective framing.
- 2. Cut the product list from 8+ brands to 3-4 curated picks with a one-line reason each ("for your skin type," "the one everyone's asking about") instead of a flat catalog scroll.
- 3. Replace the generic subject with a named brand or product to give it a concrete hook.
- 4. Move the "15% off Charlotte Tilbury" offer higher — it's a real, specific offer buried at the bottom.
