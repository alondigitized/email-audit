# Save 20% | Head-to-toe fall styles & stacks of Kohl's Cash await ...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kohl's coupon send again, the same shell you've seen all week. The 20% banner, the Kohl's Cash strip and the red Home / Women / Men / Kids buttons all repeat from Oct 10–15. This version adds a "Modern Heritage" fall hero, $29.99 sweaters and jeans, a shoe sale block and a gift-shop section. The offer is real and the deadline (Oct. 18) is credible. But it's the seventh near-identical send in a week, and the subject's "Head-to-toe fall styles" promise is delivered as a long scroll, not a focused story.
- The top of the email is where it falls down. The "20% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18" banner looks clipped under the header, and a block of tiny legal text sits right beneath it, crowding the Kohl's Cash strip. The eye has no clean first landing spot. Further down, the sections compete with each other: fall apparel, a shoe sale, gift shop tiles, then four unrelated products. The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom are the real conversion tool, but they sit about 5,000px below the fold.
- Judgement: a competent loyalty-driven promo that's weighed down by sameness, a cluttered top and no single hero. It's a 6.

## What's working

- The offer is concrete and repeated: 20% off with coupon, $29.99 sweaters and jeans, and up to 40% off shoes.
- The deadline is clear and consistent: coupon ends Oct. 18, with a Kohl's Cash redemption window of Oct. 19–25.
- Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash are visible at the top and again at the bottom, which gives members a reason to act.
- The "Family: outfitted. You: under budget." sections show real prices ($29.99, Reg. $29.99–$49.99) next to clear "Shop Now" buttons.
- The bottom coupon block (code YOUGET20 and $10 off $50+ with 10HOME) with scannable barcodes makes redemption easy in store.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a nice trust-closer.

## What's weak

- The top banner appears cut off under the header, and tiny legal copy sits directly beneath it. It reads as a layout bug and buries the main offer.
- The preheader area leads with a "last day to start checking off your gift list" line, which doesn't match a subject about fall styles and Kohl's Cash.
- There's no single hero. Fall fashion, shoes, gifts and home products all fight for attention in one very long scroll.
- The subject is 68 characters and ends in a trailing "..." that looks truncated. On mobile the "Kohl's Cash await" payoff is likely cut off.
- The creative is the same as the Oct 12–15 sends. Oct 15's "Save 20% | Score fall-ready styles during the Season's Best Shoe Sale" already used the shoe block, and Oct 12's "Step into the Season's Best Shoe Sale" led with it.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears several times, which weakens the 20% headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the top of the email. Show the 20% coupon banner whole, move the legal copy to the footer, and put the Kohl's Cash strip directly under the banner.
- 2. Pick one story per send. Either lead with "Modern Heritage" fall styles or the shoe sale, and cut the gift shop and home product grid to a single row.
- 3. Move the coupon code and barcode up, right under the hero, as a "Show this code" button. Right now it's the last thing in the email.
- 4. Differentiate this send from the week's other emails. Use a new lead product, or a "last chance" frame that is truthful about Oct. 18.
- 5. Shorten the subject so the payoff lands before truncation. Make the preheader state the code and deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 20% on fall styles + Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% coupon ends Sunday ⏳`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOUGET20 on sweaters, jeans and boots through Oct. 18. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash per $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters and jeans from $29.99, plus free returns through Jan. 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based SFMC/Movable Ink build with several markup defects (stray `</custom>`, `<div>` in `<head>`, empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport). It also stacks four tracking pixels and has missing alt text on content images. The truncated source and the AgentMail relay mean the compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`. The `qs=` tokens are opaque, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Four separate beacons load at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/fa427148-…` (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60264`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant open tracking and adds third-party requests.
- **Persistent identifiers in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears on `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. Anyone who sees or forwards the image URLs also sees that identifier.
- **Possible missing data.** One image URL carries an empty parameter: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. That is the Kohl's Cash redemption end date. The seed recipient may have had no Kohl's Cash data, so the personalized image could render a fallback. I can't confirm this from the truncated URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `…open.aspx?…&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has a stray closing tag with no opener.
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will end `<head>` early there, and the `<meta itemprop>` tags land in `<body>`.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element, not `.ReadMsgBody`.
- **Missing alt text on images:**
  - **Tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` (this one has `alt=""`, so QA flagged it incorrectly) and `kohls.demdex.net/event`. The first and third need `alt=""`. The demdex pixel also has `width="0" height="0"`.
  - **Content images:** `58379268_2_2x`, `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`. If these carry offers or copy, there is no text fallback when images are blocked.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 tracking table, the `width="100%" align="center"` AAM table, and the preheader table (`font-size:11px; color:#666666`) have no `role`. The main wrapper tables do have it.
- **Font loading:** Montserrat is pulled in by both `<link>` and `@import`, so it loads twice. Roboto is also loaded, but visible styles only use Montserrat. `@import` in a `<style>` block is ignored by Gmail, so those clients fall back to Arial.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The only personalization concern is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` above. The rest of the HTML was truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not pass these headers through, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw `.eml` or a direct-to-Gmail seed to settle it.
- **Physical address:** the check reported none. The source I received is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. Verify against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (`Kohls@s.kohls.com`).
- **Visible-source items:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. All visible CTAs are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, which QA did not resolve. The only visible parameters are on images (`email_name=261016_DG_Email_Friday`, `mi_ecmp=1023977_20261016`). No UTM parameters are visible, so I can't confirm landing-page alignment or campaign attribution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA check against the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers. That settles the address, List-Unsubscribe and authentication warnings before they're treated as real findings.
2. Resolve a sample of `qs=` links with a redirect-following GET to get the final URLs, status codes and UTMs.
3. Fix the markup: remove `</custom>`, move the schema.org block into `<body>` or use JSON-LD, fill in `<title>`, and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels, add meaningful alt text to the content images, and add `role="presentation"` to the three layout tables.
6. Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and drop either the `<link>` or the `@import` for Montserrat.
7. Consider consolidating the four open and tracking beacons.
8. Check the personalized image URLs for the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter.
