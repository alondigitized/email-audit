Each has a green "Clean at Sephora" badge, and the product names and prices are readable. ## What's working

- The clean badge on every product gives the grid a consistent, scannable signal that matches the subject.
- The grid is easy to read, with a short name, a "NEW" or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag and a price on each item.
- The 4X Points banner states a specific benefit, a deadline (10/21) and a code.
- The Kosas hero image is soft and shows the product shades.
- The Insider tier strip (Insider / VIB / Rouge) and "Rewards Bazaar" link give members a visible loyalty hook.

## What's weak

- Two promos sit above the hero. The 4X Points banner is about moisturizers and the Kosas gift is a lip gloss, and neither is the clean story in the subject.
- The Kosas gift is hedged: "online only," "$30+ purchase," plus an asterisk. It reads as bait-and-switch at a glance.
- There is no single dominant CTA. "Shop to Redeem," "Shop Now," "Shop Now >" and "Shop Now >" all compete, and none names a product.
- The lower half runs three unrelated tiles (auto-replenish, Black-owned brands, rhode) with no connection to clean beauty, so the theme drops off.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text, and a barcode repeats the offer code.

- 1. Lead with the clean hero and move the points and Kosas banners below the grid, or drop one of them. One theme per send.
- 2. Put one concrete reason to click in the hero, such as "New: Kosas shades + free gloss with $30."
- 3. Cut the lower tiles that have nothing to do with clean beauty, or save them for a separate send.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Shop new clean makeup" instead of repeated "Shop Now."
- 5. Shorten the subject to a single idea.
