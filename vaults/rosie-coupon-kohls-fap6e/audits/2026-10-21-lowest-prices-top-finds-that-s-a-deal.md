# Lowest prices 🤝 Top finds ... That's a deal 😉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "lowest prices of the season" send in about three days. It follows "Our LOWEST prices have arrived 🙌" and "Looking for our LOWEST prices? Right this way 👉" on Oct 19, and "Our LOWEST prices + Kohl's Cash = 🛒 🛍️ 🤑" on Oct 20. The body is a well-built price catalog. A maroon hero says "Lowest prices of the season" with a "Shop Now" button. A "Featuring Clearance, save up to 85% after an extra 50% off" callout sits beside it. A Kohl's Cash strip and category pills follow. Below that is a long stack of "Starting at $3.99 towels", "$17.99 boots", "$19.99 sweaters" and similar tiles, each with its own Shop Now button.
- The subject line is the weak part. "Lowest prices 🤝 Top finds ... That's a deal 😉" is three vague fragments joined with emoji and an ellipsis. It names no number, category or deadline, and it reads like the previous three subjects reshuffled. The render is clean and the offers are strong, but the inbox moment doesn't reflect that.
- Verdict: good body, forgettable subject, and a cadence problem. A subscriber who got the Oct 19 and Oct 20 sends would probably skip this one.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Lowest prices of the season" has a visible Shop Now button, with the 85% clearance callout beside it.
- The product tiles lead with concrete prices, such as "Starting at $3.99" and "Reg. $29.99–$69.99". They are easy to scan.
- Kohl's Rewards status and the Kohl's Cash earn strip are shown near the top, which speaks to members.
- The category pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids, $10 & Under, $20 & Under) give quick navigation.
- The seasonal modules (Halloween, Fall, Christmas) and the "through Oct. 25" window keep it timely.
- The render has no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject has no specific hook. "Top finds" and "That's a deal" are filler, and the ellipsis looks unfinished.
- This repeats the same "lowest prices" creative and message from Oct 19 and Oct 20, so cadence fatigue is the biggest risk.
- The email is very long, with about a dozen near-identical maroon tiles. The strongest offers (75% off Sephora, 60% off Samsonite) sit deep in the scroll.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now", so there is no category-specific button copy.
- Exclusion language ("Coupons do not apply", "Excludes Sephora at Kohl's") is small and appears on many tiles.
- The preheader is not legible in the render, so I can't confirm it adds anything to the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the send one headline offer in the subject and hero. Lead with the 85% clearance callout or one hero category instead of a dozen equal tiles.
- 2. Cut the tile stack to the top 4–5 offers and move the Sephora and Samsonite deals higher.
- 3. Vary the creative from the Oct 19 and Oct 20 sends. If the offer is unchanged, add a fresh angle such as a deadline or a category spotlight.
- 4. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with category CTAs such as "Shop boots from $17.99" or "Shop clearance".
- 5. Make the exclusions clearer in one place instead of repeating them in small print on each tile.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Kohl's email uses an Oracle/Marketing Cloud-style table layout with four tracking pixels and 77 wrapped click-tracking links. The source has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, zoom disabled). Most compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the HTML was truncated and the relay headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **77 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four tracking or open pixels fire at the top of `<body>`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/671fefc5-…`: 1x1, no `alt`.
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024278_20261021`: has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager): `width="0" height="0"`, no `alt`.
- **Malformed markup after the `open.aspx` pixel.** It is `<img … alt=""></custom>`, and `</custom>` has no matching open tag. Some parsers will mishandle it.
- **A numeric user identifier is exposed in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in plain text on multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs. The `qs` values on the click links are opaque, but this one is not.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 13,689 of 19,415 characters (about 70.5%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk. The cause is long tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** QA flagged these, and this one matches the source:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58966978_{2sa,1sa,11sa,14sa}_2x`
- **Likely false positive on `o.gif`.** It has `alt=""`, which is valid for a decorative image. The tracking pixels also don't need descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` makes some clients request the page URL.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head implicitly there, so the `<meta itemprop>` tags end up misplaced. Most clients strip schema markup anyway.
- **External web fonts.** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` for Roboto and Montserrat are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the `font-family` stacks fall back to Arial. Many clients ignore these fonts, so this is low severity.
- Layout tables use `role="presentation"`, and Outlook VML and MSO conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The visible per-recipient values are the `mi_u` and `qs` identifiers noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed defect. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require them.
- **No physical address was detected.** The source I received is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm this. The QA check is the only evidence.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC are unknown.** `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured. The sender is `kohls@s.kohls.com`, and the click domains (`s.kohls.com`, `chp.kohls.com`) are consistent with it.
- The visible source doesn't show the unsubscribe link, so its presence and working state aren't confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. Click links are wrapped in encrypted `?qs=` redirects, and the probe was skipped. No UTM parameters are visible in the available source. The `email_name=261021_DG_Email_Wednes…` and `mi_ecmp=1024278_20261021` params appear only on image URLs, so campaign attribution on click-through can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Fix the markup: remove the stray `</custom>`, move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, populate `<title>`, and delete the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `58966978_*sa_2x` images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp` and `demdex` pixels.
4. Stop putting the raw numeric `mi_u` ID in image URLs. Use an opaque token instead.
5. Cut the plain-text URL ratio by shortening tracking URLs or trimming link counts in the text part.
6. Re-run the audit with the untruncated HTML and raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`). Then confirm the footer address, the unsubscribe link, and the final landing-page UTMs by following a sample of the redirects.
