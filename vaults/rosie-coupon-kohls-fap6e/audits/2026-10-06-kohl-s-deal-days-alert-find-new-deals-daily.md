# KOHL'S DEAL DAYS ALERT 🚨 Find new deals daily ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Kohl's Deal Days send in two days. It follows this morning's "Up to 50% off deals + Daily Deals start TODAY 🙌" and the same-day "FREE SHIPPING + Daily Deals" email. The render is clean and the offer is real: a "Featured Daily Deals" lead block, then a "4 Days of Deals" section with percent-off tiles. The email is organized well enough. It just isn't new.
- The "ALERT 🚨" subject promises news, and the body delivers a refreshed grid of the same event. The page is also very long, with a Kohl's Rewards banner repeated under several tiles, a Halloween/Fall/Christmas coupon block, a "Picked with you in mind" section, two coupon barcodes, and a wall of fine print. The Daily Deals at the top have the best hierarchy. By the time you reach the barcodes, the email reads as a catalog.
- My verdict: competent but redundant, a 6 like its siblings. The "new deals daily" angle is the only fresh idea, and the subject buries it behind an alarm emoji.
- My read is from a heavily downscaled render. Headline offers and layout are clear, but I can't read small print or exact prices.

## What's working

- The Daily Deals block leads with big price and percent callouts: 99.99 vs. a regular price, 50%, 40%.
- "Ends October 8" gives the event a credible end date.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" banner reduces purchase risk.
- The Rewards and Kohl's Cash messaging stays visible, with the "$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more" callout.
- Each tile pairs one product photo with one offer, so the grid scans quickly and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- It's the third Deal Days email in about two days, so the "new deals daily" claim is hard to credit.
- The CTAs are repeated, small "Shop Now" text links, not one dominant button.
- The coupon barcodes and the "Coupons do not apply to select brands" notes undercut the headline percentages.
- The deals come after the header, utility bar, and category nav, which pushes the hero offer down on mobile.
- The length and the number of competing modules (daily deals, 4 days of deals, coupon vote, picks, barcodes) dilute the primary message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the Daily Deals plus one Rewards/Cash module, and put the barcodes and fine print behind a single link. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Make "today's deal" the subject, with a specific product or price, so it isn't read as a repeat of this morning's send.
- 3. Add a single full-width CTA button after the Daily Deals, such as "Shop today's 4 deals".
- 4. Space the Deal Days sends out, or merge them into one daily-refresh email.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a Kohl's Deal Days send. Its HTML has a malformed tag, several tracking pixels with no alt text, and layout code that depends on `<table>`/`display` hacks. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the HTML I was given. The source is truncated, so I can't verify the footer, the unsubscribe link or most of the 73 links.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 73 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains such as `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...`. Their destinations are unverified, not broken.
- Four tracking and analytics pixels load on open: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/58df40a9-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`, and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Clients that block images will miss all of them, and the demdex (Adobe Audience Manager) pixel is a third-party tracker.
- `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...">` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag. That tag has no matching opener, so the HTML is invalid.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):** 10 or more `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute. They include the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `58df40a9-...`), the content images `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233), and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59107814_2_2x`.
  - The pixels should have `alt=""`. Screen readers will announce the content images by filename or URL.
  - The truncated HTML may hide more images with the same problem.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` has no text.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. That blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(...fonts.googleapis.com...)` is loaded in a `<style>` block. Gmail and Outlook ignore it, and the fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) covers those clients.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `lang="en"` set. That's acceptable.
- **Structure outside `<head>`:** the `<div itemscope ...>` block sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid HTML. Most clients strip it, and it does nothing there.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables use `role="presentation"`. The 1x1 tracking tables, the AAM table and the preheader table don't.
- **Preheader table:** it has `width='640'` and `width: 640px` on a `display: block` table. The `.container` mobile override should handle small screens.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=604230016` and `d_sid=13245196` values are resolved per-recipient IDs.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA didn't find either header. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay it, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The footer is past the truncation point, so I can't confirm it. Kohl's normally includes an address, which makes a false positive likely. Verify it against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header wasn't captured. SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `s.kohls.com` are unverified and need a check on the original message.
- **From domain:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with authenticated sending.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing page alignment.
- `email_name=261006_DG_Email_Tuesday` appears on the image URLs, so there is campaign identification on the asset side. The `mi_kc_*` parameters on the last image URL are empty (`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`), which suggests unpopulated dynamic fields in that URL. It's cut off, so I can't say what it affects.
- One thing to flag: the subject says "Find new deals daily", but the `email_name` token ends in `Tuesday`. That's only an internal label and not a defect.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after `open.aspx`, and close all tags.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images and the product image `59107814_2_2x`. Check the truncated remainder for more.
3. Fill in `<title>`, and either give the favicon `href` a value or remove the tag.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>`. Use JSON-LD or drop it.
6. Verify the following on the raw message and the full HTML, since this audit couldn't confirm them:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers
   - the physical address in the footer
   - SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment
   - the unsubscribe link's destination
7. Resolve a sample of the 73 click-tracking URLs to confirm they land on live Kohl's pages with consistent UTM or campaign parameters.
8. Check the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` and `mi_kc_event_pe...` parameters in the last image URL. Confirm whether that empty value is intended or a dynamic-content failure.
