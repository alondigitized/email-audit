# Ends tonight ⏰ Up to 50% off early Black Friday deals are going fast ...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's Early Black Friday send again, and the new part is the "Ends tonight" urgency. The hero banner reads "Hurry, deals depart tonight" and sits over a snowy "Black Friday Deals" graphic. It shows "Up to 50% off" and "20% off w/coupon", with a "Shop Daily Deals" button and a free-shipping line below. The offer hits first and is easy to read.
- Below the hero, the email is a very long wall of modules. They cover Daily Deals (60%, 70%, 40%), themed gift lanes (Gift List Lane, Style Square, Cozy Corner, Hosting Headquarters), price points ($7.99, $14.99, $19.99, $27.99), and a Sephora and Fitbit/Garmin section. A "Preview the Deals Now" teaser sits near the bottom. The screenshot is 680x12000 and shows at about 113px wide, so I can read the layout and the big numerals but not the fine print.
- This is the 15th or so near-identical Kohl's send since late October. It follows the Nov 8 "Save BIG with up to 50% off…" email and the Nov 7 "LAST CHANCE to earn $15 Kohl's Cash…" email, and I'd expect inboxes to be fatigued. The "ends tonight" urgency is credible and the best part of this one. The creative itself is the same deal wall, so it's a solid but unremarkable send.

## What's working

- The hero states the urgency and the offer together: "Hurry, deals depart tonight" with 50% off and 20% off with coupon.
- The "Shop Daily Deals" button and the free-shipping line are visible near the top.
- Big numerals (60%, 70%, 50%, $7.99) make the deals scannable even at thumbnail size.
- Themed lanes (Gift List Lane, Cozy Corner, Hosting Headquarters) give the long scroll some structure.
- Kohl's branding and the Kohl's Cash / loyalty footer are consistent and trusted.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, about 12,000px, with no clear single hero product or single ask.
- It repeats the same creative as the Nov 7 and Nov 8 sends, so only the subject line is new.
- Many modules carry "Coupons do not apply" and "Select styles" qualifiers. That muddies the honesty of the "up to 50%" and "20% off w/coupon" headlines.
- Nothing in the body reflects who the recipient is: no category focus and no personalization.
- The ending "Next stop… Preview the Deals Now" teaser competes with the "ends tonight" message by pulling attention to a future event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus three or four modules, and make the final hours the whole story. For example, add a countdown timer and one "Shop Doorbusters" button repeated at the bottom.
- 2. Lead with one concrete number, such as "50% off ends at midnight", and drop the competing 20%-with-coupon message from the hero.
- 3. Move the "Preview the Deals Now" teaser to the next send, so this one is about tonight only.
- 4. Put the exclusions ("Coupons do not apply") next to the relevant tile instead of in many small notes.
- 5. Add one personalization hook, such as a category the recipient has browsed or a Kohl's Cash balance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 50% off early Black Friday ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Daily Deals from 40% off, plus an extra 20% with coupon. Midnight cutoff.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated picks from $7.99 are still in stock. Tonight only.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Early Black Friday (kohls@s.kohls.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a heavily instrumented, mostly table-based layout with four separate open and tracking pixels, 91 opaque click-redirect links, and several markup errors. Only the first part of the source was available, and the header-based compliance checks were inconclusive because of the relay. Findings below are limited to what the HTML and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 91 links are on tracking or redirect domains, such as `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`. The QA run skipped HTTP probing, so none of the destinations, redirect chains or expiry were validated.
- **Opaque destinations:** the `qs=` payload is an encoded blob, so the final landing URLs can't be seen in the source.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b1a5effc-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025487_2026118`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`

  These are redundant and add third-party requests. `demdex.net` is a third-party audience-sync domain.
- **Recipient ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the query string of image requests, such as `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`. It is a persistent recipient identifier, and it is exposed to any proxy or cache that logs image URLs.
- **Malformed markup:** the `open.aspx` image tag is closed with a stray `</custom>` instead of being self-closed. It sits in `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">`.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 16,076 of 21,877 plain-text characters (73%) are URLs, so the plain-text part is largely unreadable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid head content:** the `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` inside `<head>` is invalid. Parsers will end the head early and push the following content into the body.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a request back to the document URL.
- **Missing alt text, content images:** the QA check flags these `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images:
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `916d6fa08d185233.png`

  Also flagged is `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59592198_2_2x`. Screen readers will announce filenames, and the images disappear with images off.
- **Missing alt text, pixels:** the tracking pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `o.gif`, `demdex.net/event`) have no `alt`. They should use `alt=""`. Only `o.gif` carries `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Layout tables without `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 tracking table, the AAM table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) are missing it. The main wrapper tables do have it.
- **Selector typo:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading `.`, so the rule matches nothing.
- **Duplicate font loading:** Google Fonts is loaded twice, through `<link>` and `@import`, for both Roboto and Montserrat. The `@import` will fail in clients that strip `<style>` imports, and the stack falls back to Arial.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is mixed with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is cosmetic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values are resolved in URLs (`mi_u`, `d_sid`, `mi_mid`). The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are truly absent, the email isn't RFC 8058 one-click compliant. Gmail and Yahoo require that for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** the check found none. The source is truncated before the footer, so this is **unverified** rather than a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The domain alignment that can be seen is good: the From domain is `s.kohls.com` and the click tracking runs on `click.s.kohls.com`.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** not observable in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** these can't be verified. Every visible link is a `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirect, and none of the 91 were resolved.
- **Campaign identifiers:** the image URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1025487_2026118` and `email_name=261108_DG_Email_Sunday`. These are consistent with the 2026-11-08 send date, but no destination-side parameters can be matched against them.
- **Landing page alignment:** not testable without resolved URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed in section 3.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta, and give the document a non-empty `<title>`.
3. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`, or remove it. Fix the stray `</custom>` and drop the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Consolidate the four open and tracking pixels where possible, and review whether `mi_u` needs to appear in image URLs.
5. Re-run the audit with the full raw message, including headers. That would settle List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results and the footer address, which are the main open compliance questions.
6. Resolve the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects in a follow-up pass. That would verify the final URLs, redirect-chain length, UTM parameters and landing-page status.
