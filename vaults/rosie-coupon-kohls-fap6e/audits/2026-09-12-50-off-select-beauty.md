# 50% off select beauty⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest Sephora send in the recent run — a straightforward "GET 50% OFF" hero followed by an actual shoppable product grid with real strikethrough pricing on every item (Clarins $48, Estée Lauder, OUAI, Gucci $94, Benefit $14.50, etc.). Unlike the 9/6–9/11 sends that welded a flash-sale banner onto an unrelated brand takeover or awards showcase, this one delivers exactly what the hero promises: 50% off, and here are ten specific products at their marked-down price.
- The tradeoff is genericness — "select beauty" with a rotating grid of unrelated brands (skincare, fragrance, mascara, sunscreen) isn't targeted to any one category, so it reads as a general sale blast rather than a personalized pick. But execution-wise it's tight: no render bugs, clear visual hierarchy, real prices visible without hunting.
- Compared to 9/10's 8.00/10 "kitchen-sink" send, this is arguably more coherent because there's no bait-and-switch between subject and body — the subject says 50% off, the email is entirely 50%-off products.

## What's working

- Hero delivers on the subject immediately — "GET 50% OFF" is the first thing you see, no scroll needed.
- Every one of the ten grid products shows a specific strikethrough price and sale price ($42→$21, $188→$94, $29→$14.50), which is rare specificity for this brand's emails.
- "TODAY ONLY" callout with three featured products (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) adds credible urgency near the top.
- Clean single-narrative structure — no second unrelated campaign bolted underneath, unlike 9/6, 9/7, 9/9, 9/11.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) reinforces loyalty relevance for members.

## What's weak

- "Select beauty" in the subject and hero is vague — no category or brand hint, so it's hard to know if there's anything for you before opening/scrolling.
- Product grid mixes fragrance, skincare, mascara, sunscreen, and lip gloss with no organizing logic — feels like an arbitrary sale sweep, not curation.
- The ⏰ emoji in the subject implies urgency/deadline but nothing in the body states when the sale ends beyond "Today Only" applying to just 3 of the 10 products — inconsistent urgency.
- Same maroon/pink gradient hero treatment this brand has repeated all week — visually indistinguishable from prior sends at a glance.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the grid or name a category in the subject.** "50% off select beauty" tells a recipient nothing about whether their categories (e.g., skincare vs. fragrance) are included — naming even one hero category would lift open rates for a category-loyal shopper.
- 2. **Make "Today Only" apply consistently, or drop it.** Right now three products are time-boxed and seven aren't in the same email — pick one urgency story.
- 3. **Add a scarcity or count signal** ("50% off 50+ products, today only") to justify the ⏰ in the subject.
- 4. **Vary the hero visual treatment** — five straight days of the same pink/maroon gradient risks banner blindness even with good copy.
