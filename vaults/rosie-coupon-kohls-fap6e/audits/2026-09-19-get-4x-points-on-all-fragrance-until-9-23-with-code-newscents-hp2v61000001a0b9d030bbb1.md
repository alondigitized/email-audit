# Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest structural match between subject and hero this brand has sent in the last week — "Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS" leads directly into the top banner "4X POINTS¹ ON ALL FRAGRANCE," the code is printed in both places, and the deadline (9/23) is consistent throughout. No bait-and-switch here, unlike the 09-18 and 09-15 "50% off" sends in this brand's history.
- The problem is what comes after the top banner. Immediately below the fragrance promo, the email pivots to "Get a Mini" (a different free-gift-with-purchase mechanic, different terms) and then a full "BEAUTY FOR ALL" hero unrelated to fragrance, followed by a long kitchen-sink scroll of unrelated new-arrival tiles (Benefit, Peter Thomas Roth, Olaplex, Glossier, Fresh, BeautyBlender). The fragrance offer is real and clearly stated, but it occupies roughly the top 15% of a very long email before dissolving into generic catalog noise — a pattern consistent with this brand's other recent sends even when the headline claim itself is honest.
- Net: honest subject-to-hero match, single clear offer up top, but it's buried under an enormous unrelated scroll that dilutes the CTA and makes the email feel like a general newsletter wearing a fragrance-promo subject line.

## What's working

- Subject and top banner match exactly — "4X points on all fragrance" appears in both, with the same code (NEWSCENTS) and the same deadline (9/23).
- Clear, single, unambiguous offer: 4X Beauty Insider points per dollar on fragrance, with the code visibly reprinted a second time over the Prada bottle image.
- Strong visual anchor — the dark, moody Prada Amber Nuit product shot with "4X" badge gives the eye a clear place to land.
- No render bugs — images loaded, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Footer includes a second restatement of the offer with exact dates ("Present this email at checkout... September 17–September 23, 2026"), reinforcing urgency for in-store shoppers.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long and pivots away from fragrance almost immediately — "Get a Mini" (Burberry Goddess) and "BEAUTY FOR ALL" / "His New Scent Lineup" compete with the primary offer within the first two screens.
- After the fragrance section, six unrelated new-arrival tiles (makeup, hair, skincare, brushes) take over the rest of the email with no connection to the fragrance promo — this is the same "kitchen sink" structure flagged in the 09-16 and 09-14 sends.
- No urgency treatment (countdown, bold red date, "ends in X days") on the main banner itself — the 9/23 deadline is present but visually flat, easy to skim past.
- No fragrance-specific social proof, scent notes, or "shop by scent family" navigation to help a shopper act on the 4X offer specifically — it points to one hero product (Prada) and then abandons the category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half — move "Get a Mini," "BEAUTY FOR ALL," and the six new-arrival product tiles to a separate send. A 4X-points fragrance promo should be a focused, short email with a fragrance grid (multiple brands/price points), not a single hero product followed by unrelated categories.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or bold "Ends Wednesday 9/23" treatment directly under the "4X POINTS" banner — the date is currently in fine print only.
- 3. Replace the single Prada hero with a 4-6 item fragrance carousel (men's/women's, various brands) so the offer feels shoppable beyond one $150+ bottle.
- 4. Tighten the subject slightly for mobile truncation and lead with urgency:
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on fragrance — ends 9/23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last days: earn 4X points on every scent`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code NEWSCENTS at checkout — in stores or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock your fragrance shelf and rack up Beauty Insider points before Wednesday.`
