# Take 20% off vacation styles for your next getaway 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's sends the vacation-styles theme for the second time in nine days — the May 19 "Getaway styles + an extra 20% off? Get packing" email was essentially this same offer and framing, and subscribers who opened that one arrive here with immediate déjà vu. That email scored an 8; this one doesn't earn the same grade because novelty is the one thing you cannot recycle. The timing compounds the problem: subscribers just survived a five-email Memorial Day barrage, and this lands on day one of the reboot as a near-replay.
- The email body is competent and clean — the 20% hero is front and center, the $4.99 sandals anchor is the sharpest price signal in the email, and Kohl's Cash visibility is strong throughout. But the "vacation" promise in the subject isn't kept in the body: the sections link to generic Women/Men/Kids/Home category tabs rather than a curated getaway capsule. The "Summer styles for getting everyone out the door...eventually." copy is trying to be charming but is too soft for a send that needs to snap fatigued subscribers out of scroll-past mode.
- Bottom line: operationally solid, strategically lazy. After six days of Memorial Day email and one prior vacation send, this needed a sharper creative hook to earn the open.

## What's working

- **Hero offer is unambiguous** — "Take an extra 20% off with coupon · Ends May 31" is top of the email, no scroll required, no ambiguity.
- **$4.99 sandals anchor** — "Starting at $4.99, Reg. $5.39–$14.29" is the most specific price signal in the email and creates an entry-level pull.
- **Kohl's Cash visibility** — "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent through May 31" appears prominently, and the rewards bar at the top reinforces member value.
- **Coupon disclosed in the hero** — "with coupon" appears in the hero itself, not buried; that's honest merchandising even if the exclusions aren't shown.
- **Clean render** — No broken images, overlapping copy, or layout failures visible anywhere in the webview.

## What's weak

- **Near-identical to May 19** — Same 20% discount, same "vacation/getaway" theme, different subject phrasing; a subscribed shopper who opened the earlier send has already processed this offer.
- **Category spread dilutes click intent** — Four navigation tabs (Women, Men, Boys, Girls) plus a clearance section scatter the call-to-action rather than channeling it toward one hero destination.
- **"Shop Now" everywhere** — Every CTA button says "Shop Now"; not one names the destination (Dresses, Sandals, Clearance), which is a missed conversion lever in a multi-section email.
- **Vacation theme without vacation product** — The subject says "getaway styles" but the body sells dresses and sandals without any editorial curation; the theme is in the copy, not in the experience.
- **Seventh email in ten days** — The cadence alone suppresses opens regardless of offer quality; the brand is drawing down goodwill earned earlier in the week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Earn the vacation theme with product** — Link to a curated "Pack your bags" collection or editorial page rather than generic Women/Men categories; the May 19 send failed this same test and fixing it here would differentiate the sends.
- 2. **Replace every "Shop Now" with the destination** — "Shop Dresses," "Shop Sandals from $4.99," "Browse Clearance" — specific verb + noun CTAs materially lift click-through in multi-section email layouts.
- 3. **Surface the compound offer in the hero** — "20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash" is the real story; the May 24 send did this well. Right now the two benefits are separated across the email and don't compound in the reader's mind.
- 4. **Add one scarcity signal** — "Ends May 31" is three days out but feels flat. One specific item — "These sandals are almost gone" or a sizes-running-low callout — would give the deadline teeth.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sandals from $4.99 + 20% off — your getaway checklist`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, sandals, clearance to 70% — ends May 31`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Curated getaway styles + Kohl's Cash through May 31`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard HTML4 table layout with multiple ESP and third-party tracking stacks (Coherent Path, Message Intelligence, Adobe Audience Manager/Demdex). The main technical concerns are a stray invalid closing tag in the rendered body, redundant font loading, missing CAN-SPAM physical address, and unverifiable compliance headers at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** by QA probe — all wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect domains. Destination URLs and any UTM parameters on landing pages cannot be verified from source alone.

Three distinct tracking pixel stacks are present:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/{uuid}` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/…/o.gif` — Message Intelligence open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…` — ESP open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

No broken or 4xx-returning links detected (redirect domains intentionally skipped).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Stray invalid closing tag.** The ESP open-pixel `<div>` block contains a bare `</custom>` tag with no matching opener:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""><custom>
```
This is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers or accessibility tools.

**Empty `<title>` element.** `<title></title>` — no document title set. Screen readers and preview panes that fall back to `<title>` will show nothing.

**Empty favicon href.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` will generate a spurious GET request to the email's base URL in some clients.

**Double font loading.** Both Roboto and Montserrat are loaded via `<link>` *and* `@import` in the same `<head>`, resulting in two network requests per font family in clients that execute CSS:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto…">
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto…");</style>
```

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta.** Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Evidence: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">`.

**11 images missing alt text** (QA-confirmed), including all Message Intelligence render-pixel images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`) and the Coherent Path open pixel. Tracking pixels with `aria-hidden="true"` or `alt=""` are acceptable; the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` images appear to be content-adjacent render pixels and should have `alt=""` at minimum.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unfired/exposed merge token syntax (`{{…}}`, `[%…%]`, `%%…%%`) visible in the truncated source. Subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` and campaign ID `mi_ecmp=1013401_2026528` are present and appear correctly resolved — no placeholder leakage detected.

### 5. Compliance

**Physical mailing address absent** from visible HTML. CAN-SPAM §7(d)(6) requires a valid physical postal address in the message body. QA did not detect one; if it appears below the truncation point this warning can be cleared, but it should be confirmed.

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** at the relay layer. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (enforced since 2024) mandate this header for senders at Kohl's volume. Absence may be an AgentMail relay capture gap, but cannot be confirmed clean.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found.** One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo for high-volume senders. Same caveat — may be stripped by relay.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown. Given Kohl's sending infrastructure (dedicated `s.kohls.com` subdomain), alignment is expected but unverified.

`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are both present — correct for email HTML.

All 22 CTA links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains. UTM parameters on final landing pages cannot be inspected from source.
