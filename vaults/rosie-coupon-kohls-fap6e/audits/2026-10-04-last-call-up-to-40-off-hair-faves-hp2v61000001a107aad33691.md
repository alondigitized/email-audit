# LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves," and the top banner delivers it. "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" sits at the top with a "TODAY ONLY" tag and a "SHOP NOW" button. After that, the email drifts. The hero is a huge "Minis Make It Easy" block with a Miu Miu travel spray, followed by an eight-tile minis grid. A subscriber who opens for last-call hair deals scrolls past a full screen of travel-size fragrance and skincare before seeing any hair product again.
- This is the same pattern as the 2026-09-29 "From your feed to your cart" send and the 2026-09-28 Living Proof send. A hard-offer strip sits on top of an unrelated editorial body. The real hair content ("Now Starring: Glossy Hair") only appears about two-thirds of the way down, next to a skincare sets tile. The 2026-10-02 "50% OFF select brow products" send scored 8 because the subject, hero and CTA were one thing. This one is three or four things.
- The offer is also hedged. "Up to 40%" with "Save big on PATTERN by Tracee Ellis Ross, Vegamour, and more" is vague, and there's a delivery P.S. and a points-multiplier block (2X, 5X) competing further down. The send is competent and on brand, but the subject's urgency is not carried through the body. Judgment: an average send that should have been a one-offer email.

## What's working

- The top banner matches the subject. "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" with a "TODAY ONLY" tag and a SHOP NOW button is visible immediately.
- The urgency is credible. "Today only" lines up with the "last call" subject.
- Named brands (PATTERN, Vegamour) give the hair offer some specificity.
- The Insider sender and the Beauty Insider footer make the loyalty context clear.
- Each minis tile has a clear product name and a price with the full-size value in parentheses, which is useful for comparison.

## What's weak

- The hero and most of the body are about Minis, not hair, so the subject's promise is buried under about 70% unrelated content.
- The hair banner is a small strip with a small CTA, and the large "Minis Make It Easy" hero outranks it visually.
- "Up to 40%" has no hero product, price or image of the discounted hair items. Only the brand names are listed.
- Three more competing messages follow: the points event (2X/5X), the skincare sets tile and the Same-Day Unlimited promo. This dilutes a "last call" send.
- The email is very long, with a dense legal footer and a barcode. That is heavy for what is a one-day offer.
- The 2026-10-04 cadence is repeating the "up to X% off hair" structure from 2026-09-29 and 2026-09-28, so it risks reading as the fourth near-identical promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make hair the whole email. Lead with a big hero of two or three discounted hair products with prices, a clear "40% off, ends tonight" and one SHOP HAIR DEALS button. Move Minis to a separate send.
- 2. If the Minis content has to ship, put it below a full hair product grid, not above it.
- 3. Name a hero product and a concrete deal, e.g. "PATTERN Curl Shampoo, $X (was $Y)," instead of "up to 40% off."
- 4. Add a live countdown or "ends 11:59 PM PT" instead of the generic "today only."
- 5. Cut the points, Same-Day and skincare blocks, or collapse them into one footer line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 40% off PATTERN, Vegamour & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call for 40% off hair 👀 (today only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop PATTERN, Vegamour and more before the deal ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Curl creams, scalp serums and styling picks, all marked down through tonight.`
