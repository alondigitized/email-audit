# Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof 🤍
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises a clean, urgent offer — "Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof" — but the render delivers almost the opposite of what was promised. The email opens with a "Complimentary Kérastase" GWP banner, then a moody black-and-white "Beauty's Most Wanted" hero, then a full scroll-length product catalog (Kérastase, YSL, NARS, Sincerely Yours, MAC, Gisou, Sol de Janeiro, REFY) before finally landing on a small "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" banner roughly two-thirds down. Living Proof isn't visibly named or shown anywhere in the frame.
- This is the classic Sephora catalog-dump pattern this brand keeps repeating (see 2026-09-25's "New beauty to obsess over," 6.00/10), except here it's worse — the subject makes a specific, narrow promise ("Living Proof," "40% off," urgency framing) and the creative doesn't deliver on it visually. A reader who opens on the strength of "Living Proof" will scroll past six unrelated brand blocks before finding a vaguely-related hair offer with no Living Proof branding visible.
- Judgement: this is a subject/creative mismatch email dressed up as a haircare deal. It'll underperform its subject-driven open rate on click-through because the hero doesn't pay off the promise.

## What's working

- The hero image ("Beauty's Most Wanted") is a clean, well-composed portrait shot — high production value, no render bugs.
- Complimentary Kérastase GWP banner at the very top gives an immediate, concrete incentive before any scrolling.
- "Clean at Sephora" badges are used consistently and clearly on relevant products (Sincerely Yours, Gisou).
- "Today Only" badge on the Hair Faves banner adds credible urgency where it does appear.
- Layout is technically clean throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or lorem ipsum across this very long render.

## What's weak

- Living Proof is not visibly present anywhere in the screenshot — the subject's core promise isn't paid off in the creative at all.
- The 40%-off hair offer is buried roughly two-thirds down a long scroll, well past the fold on any device.
- Hero section ("Beauty's Most Wanted") has zero connection to the subject line — it's a generic new-arrivals tease, not a deal.
- The email reads as an undifferentiated catalog scroll (7+ brand blocks) rather than a focused, single-offer send the subject implies.
- No visible product imagery for Living Proof specifically, even within the Hair Faves module — just generic bottle silhouettes without brand-specific hero shot.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the Living Proof/Hair Faves offer to the hero position.** If the subject leads with it, the first screen a reader sees on open must show it — right now the hero is a disconnected GWP/portrait combo, and the actual promised deal is buried mid-scroll. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Show actual Living Proof product shots with the % off callout in the Hair Faves module**, not a generic silhouette — right now there's no brand recognition moment for the exact product the subject named.
- 3. **Cut or reorder the catalog blocks.** Six-plus unrelated brand tiles between the subject's promise and its payoff will cause drop-off; either trim to 2-3 tiles or move Hair Faves above them.
- 4. **Tighten subject/hero alignment going forward** — this brand's history shows the strongest performers (Hair Daily Deals 9/23, 4X fragrance points 9/22) are the ones where subject and top-of-email visually match immediately.
