# Get 20% off AND $20 off your $100+ purchase ... let's shop 🛒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, coupon-forward Kohl's send. The top banner does the work: "20% off with coupon" sits beside "$20 off your purchase of $100 or more," and both say "Ends Oct. 18." The Kohl's Cash strip and the "Rewards Member | Earn 5%" status line sit right under it. The offer is easy to understand, but it is the same one as the 10-09 sends ("Extra 20% off + $20 off your $100+ purchase 🎉" and the "20% off savings you can't miss" version). This is another repeat of that creative.
- The body is a long, sprawling mix: Modern Heritage hero, family sweaters and jeans at $29.99, a Season's Best Shoe Sale at 40% off, Under Armour at 25% off, then a "Picked with you in mind" product grid. Nothing competes with the coupon banner, but the body has no single hero. The barcode and coupon-code block near the bottom is useful for redeeming. The long legal block makes the email feel heavy.
- Overall judgment: solid and clear, but it is a repeat send with a diffuse middle. It is mid-pack at about 6.

## What's working

- The top banner puts both offers together with a credible deadline: "Ends Oct. 18."
- The Kohl's Cash strip and the "Earn 5%" member line reinforce loyalty value.
- The "Modern Heritage" hero is seasonal and has a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The product price points ($29.99 sweaters and jeans, $229.99 Ninja) are specific and easy to scan.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom (YOUGET20, EXTRA20, 10HOME) make redemption easy.
- "Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a good trust line.

## What's weak

- Cadence: this is the third near-identical $20-off/20%-off send in two days (see 10-09), so it feels like fatigue.
- Many sections compete: family, jeans, shoes, Under Armour, picked-for-you, and wallet. There is no single hero.
- The shoe and Under Armour blocks say "Coupons do not apply," which clashes with the coupon-led headline.
- The "Picked with you in mind" grid is generic for a recipient with no visible personalization.
- The dense legal and fine-print block at the bottom is long, and the "select styles/brands" exclusions are noticeable.
- Several section CTAs are identical "Shop Now" or Women/Men/Kids/Shoes button rows, so the copy is not specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to one hero and 4-6 products tied to the coupon, so the coupon is the story instead of competing categories.
- 2. Don't promote categories that exclude the coupon in a coupon email. Separate those sections or label them clearly.
- 3. Space out sends. Vary the offer rather than repeating the same $20/20% pair across three days.
- 4. Make CTAs specific, e.g. "Shop sweaters from $29.99" rather than "Shop Now."
- 5. Add real personalization: recent categories, or a "your Kohl's Cash expires" line.
- 6. Move the coupon codes and barcodes higher, close to the top banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 2 coupons end Oct. 18 🛒`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters and jeans from $29.99, plus earn Kohl's Cash on every $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack both coupons, and returns are free through January 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based Kohl's/Cordial-style send with several markup defects (stray `</custom>`, `<div>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`), four 1x1 tracking pixels, and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as real defects. The tracked links are opaque and weren't probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links were skipped.** They sit on click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwMjUs…`). The QA run didn't probe their destinations, so I can't confirm they resolve or where they land.
- **There are 4 separate open/analytics pixels:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/2ad0ac13-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023616_20261010`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?XV7TE73XBMPETALACTDV5TWTRI.60269&d=60269&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  Each adds a request and a failure point. Two different open-tracking systems (`click.chp` and `click.s`) suggest overlapping vendors.
- **`mi_u=604230016` is a recipient identifier in image URLs.** It is repeated on content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…`), so the ID reaches the image host on every render. This is common practice, but it is worth a privacy review given the Adobe `demdex` call.
- **The open.aspx pixel markup is malformed.** It is followed by a stray `</custom>`: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Eleven images are missing `alt`.** Seven are content images: `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`. `58379268_2_2x` is a product image, also missing `alt`. The rest are pixels. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those blocks convey nothing.
- **The first pixel has no `alt` at all.** `<img src="https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/2ad0ac13-…" width="1" height="1">` should carry `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` like the others. The `demdex` image has no `alt` either.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** Some clients and web views use it as the page title.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** That is invalid HTML. Parsers may hoist it into `<body>`, and Gmail strips microdata anyway.
- **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.** It is harmless but invalid.
- **The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional** while the `<html>` tag carries XML namespaces. This is a legacy mismatch and low risk.
- **Web fonts load with fallbacks.** Roboto and Montserrat load from `fonts.googleapis.com` via `@import` and `<link>`, inside `<!--[if !mso]>`. Montserrat falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, so there is no breakage, but clients that block remote fonts will render the fallback.
- **The body markup has no obvious `role="presentation"` gaps in the visible portion**, although the pixel tables lack it. No other issues were visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and the subject (`Get 20% off AND $20 off your $100+ purchase ... let's shop 🛒`) contain no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FNAME]`. The truncated HTML means the rest of the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is an unconfirmed capture gap, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`, and the tool expects the relay to omit the header. Verify against the original message.
- **No physical address was detected.** The source is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed. The footer, including the unsubscribe link and postal address, still needs a check.
- The QA summary is internally inconsistent. It reports "0 issue(s), 4 warning(s)" but lists more than 15 warnings, so the 43% pass rate is not reliable.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 70 click URLs are opaque `?qs=` tokens and were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the 20% off / $20 off $100+ offer can't be checked. The only visible campaign identifiers are on image URLs: `mi_ecmp=1023616_20261010` and `email_name=261010_DG_Email_Saturd…` (truncated). There is no evidence either way on whether they pass through to the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images, including `58379268_2_2x` and `2a5adc5afb647aad.png` through `916d6fa08d185233.png`. Use `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` on all pixels, including the first `click.chp.kohls.com` one and the `demdex` one.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`, move or delete the `<div itemscope>` in `<head>`, fill in `<title>`, and drop the empty favicon `<link>`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Consolidate the duplicate open-tracking pixels if the vendors overlap.
5. Re-run the QA against the original message, not the relay copy. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and the footer's address and unsubscribe link.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links (follow the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects) to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM or campaign parameters.
