# 🔔 Don’t miss 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, Rosie
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third send of the Stacking Weekend promotional umbrella in five days (July 1, 3, 5). The July 3rd "Ends 7/7" send already scored a 9/10 — this one is the same offer, same weekend, one tick further into urgency fatigue. The name personalization in the subject ("Rosie") is a genuine differentiator from the prior sends and will arrest the inbox scan. But the moment the email opens, it leads with "BEAUTY WITH A FOLLOWING" — a lifestyle brand line that squanders the energy the subject line built. The 30% off offer lives in the subject and presumably the banner, but the hero creative doesn't confirm it.
- The render is clean, the Paula's Choice and NARS editorial choices are smart brand-authority picks, and the multi-section grid is Sephora-quality production. The problem is structural: this is a discount urgency email wearing an editorial newsletter's clothes. Too many sections (new launches, brand spotlight, feed grid, loyalty footer) dilute the one job this email should be doing — getting Rosie to click through and apply 30% off before the weekend ends.
- Verdict: well-executed but campaign-weary. The prior two Stacking Weekend sends did the heavy lifting; this one is fighting the subscriber's "I've seen this" filter without enough new ammunition.

## What's working

- **Name personalization in the subject** is the sharpest inbox hook in this campaign series — "Rosie" forces a second look where a generic subject would scroll by.
- **Paula's Choice as the featured editorial brand** is an authority-brand pick that has a fanatical community; it's the right "new from" name to anchor the editorial half of this email.
- **NARS spotlight** gives the email a second distinct anchor beyond the Sephora Collection discount and elevates the prestige feel.
- **SHOP NOW CTA** is visible above the fold with clear contrast — no hunting required.
- **Clean render throughout** — product photography loads, no overlapping text or layout breaks across all sections.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy doesn't carry the offer** — "BEAUTY WITH A FOLLOWING" is brand-building language, not a discount confirmation. Subscribers who skim the body will exit without registering the 30% off.
- **No visible deadline inside the email body** — the July 3rd send said "Ends 7/7" explicitly in the subject; this one drops it. "Don't miss" urgency without a specific end date reads as vague pressure, not actionable information.
- **Third send, same deal, five days** — the Stacking Weekend has now been "don't miss"-worthy since July 1. The cadence is spending down goodwill that the campaign won in the first two sends.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "SHOP NOW" doesn't close the loop on the deal. A subscriber with the subject's offer in mind needs confirmation at the button, not a plain imperative.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the discount in the hero banner or headline** — a visible "30% OFF ALL SEPHORA COLLECTION" overlay on the hero image closes the subject-to-body loop and prevents confused drop-off from subscribers who opened but skim.
- 2. **Restore the end date** — the July 3rd send (9/10) said "Ends 7/7"; this send drops it. Bring "Ends 7/7" back into the hero or the banner. That single change turns "don't miss" from ambient pressure into an actual deadline.
- 3. **Make the CTA specific to the offer** — change "SHOP NOW" to "SHOP 30% OFF" or "CLAIM THE DEAL" so the click moment confirms what the subject promised.
- 4. **Prune to one editorial pick** — for an urgency-framed send, choose Paula's Choice or NARS, not both. Let the discount breathe rather than competing with two brand spotlights and a feed grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `🔔 Ends 7/7: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, Rosie`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rosie — your 30% off expires tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sephora Collection, Paula's Choice & NARS — all on sale this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off ends Monday — here's everything worth adding to your bag`
