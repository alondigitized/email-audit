# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's "50% off" daily-deals email, and it's built around a real, immediate offer — not a vague seasonal nudge. The hero is clean: big "GET 50% OFF" headline, a "TODAY ONLY" module with three named products and struck-through prices (Clinique lipstick $25→$12.50, Lawless foundation $47→$23.50), which is exactly the specificity these sends usually lack. It reuses the same daily-deal-calendar skeleton flagged in the brand history (App Fest, 8/17 and 8/24) — "Week 1/2/3" teaser grids with no product names — so the concept isn't new, but the execution here is sharper because the top module has real prices instead of just thumbnails.
- The email gets busy fast: after the 50% module it stacks a hair-tools cross-sell, an Afterpay 10%-off pitch, and an Olive Young K-beauty banner, all competing with the primary offer before you even hit the footer. That's a lot of asks in one scroll. Still, the top third does its job — clear offer, clear discount, clear CTA — so this lands as a solidly average-to-good promotional send, better than the 8/23 new-arrivals dump but not as tight as the 8/20 Olive Young co-brand.

## What's working

- "GET 50% OFF" is the first thing the eye hits, large and unambiguous, with a visible "SHOP NOW" button directly beneath it.
- The "TODAY ONLY" module shows named products with actual struck-through prices (Clinique, Lawless, Skinfix) — concrete, not generic.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, no overlap or broken layout.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) and points balance ("You have 0 points") are visible, which is genuine loyalty personalization.

## What's weak

- The "Week 1/2/3" grids repeat the exact App Fest calendar format already used twice in recent sends (8/17, 8/24) with no product names or prices — just images, so they add scroll without adding info.
- Three unrelated promos (hair tools, Afterpay 10%, Olive Young) are crammed below the main offer, diluting focus and making the primary 50%-off CTA compete for attention.
- "Save big on select faves" and "Exclusions/terms apply" language buries the catch — no indication of which categories are excluded, right under a big 50% claim.
- Superscript "a" on "GET 50% OFF" with no visible footnote near the hero forces the reader to hunt in the fine print at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "TODAY ONLY" module and the deals-calendar teaser — drop hair tools, Afterpay, and Olive Young into a separate send. One offer, one email, converts better than four competing asks.
- 2. Put actual product names on the Week 1/2/3 grid images (like the "TODAY ONLY" module already does) instead of leaving them as bare thumbnails — repeat visitors need a reason to check back.
- 3. State the exclusion category up top in plain language (e.g., "excludes fragrance & tools") instead of relying on a superscript "a" with no adjacent footnote.
- 4. Swap the generic "Shop Now" CTA on the hero for something tied to the actual deal, e.g. "Shop Today's Deals."

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off ends tonight: your 3 daily deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clinique, Lawless & Skinfix — new deals drop daily`
