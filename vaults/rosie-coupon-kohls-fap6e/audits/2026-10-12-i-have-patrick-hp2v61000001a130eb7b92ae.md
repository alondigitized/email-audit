## Executive summary

- This is a content email with no offer in it. The subject, "I HAVE PATRICK 📞📞📞," is a meme-style tease that pays off only at the fourth tip. The body is "The Chat": "Experts Know Best," followed by four makeup tips from Mario, Mary Phillips, Violette and Patrick Starrr. Each tip has a large portrait and a signature. The editorial design is polished and feels like a creator-led newsletter.
- The email gives the reader nothing to act on. I can see no discount, no code, no hero product and no CTA button. The only link-like elements are the small "SHOP NOW" in the Beauty Insider footer and the app download strip. A Breast Cancer Awareness block, with a donation note and a Memorial Sloan Kettering story, closes the body. It is a sincere block but it competes with the beauty content.
- Verdict: strong brand voice and good visuals, but weak commercial pull. It works as engagement content and underperforms as a revenue email. It also follows several promo-heavy sends this week (the 10/07 "2X points" send, the 10/08 Dr. Groot send), so the change of pace helps open rates more than clicks.

## What's working

- The four expert tips with signatures read as authentic and personal, and are on-brand for Sephora.
- Large, well-cropped portraits create a clear visual hierarchy and make the email easy to scan.
- The format is a clear break from the discount-heavy cadence of the past week, such as the 10/07 "2X points" send.
- The tips are useful: contour shading, color correcting and the "bouche mordue" lip are concrete and shareable.
- The Breast Cancer Awareness block gives the email a values angle that fits the October timing.

## What's weak

- There is no CTA button and no offer anywhere, so the email gives the reader nowhere to go.
- None of the products the experts mention (the contour shade, the lipstick, the color corrector) are linked or named for purchase.
- The subject's 📞📞📞 and all-caps text are cryptic, and "Patrick" is the fourth of four tips, so the subject promises something the opening doesn't deliver.
- The long charity block pulls the reader away from shopping, and its fine print is small and hard to read.
- Loyalty content is limited to a footer "Beauty Insider" strip, with no member benefit shown.

- 1. Add a "Shop Mario's pick" style button under each tip, linking to the exact product the expert used. This is the biggest lift, because the email currently has no click path.
- 2. Lead with Patrick Starrr, the most recognizable name, and make the subject match what the reader sees first.
- 3. Add one concrete incentive near the top, such as a Beauty Insider points boost on the featured products.
- 4. Move the charity block below a product row, and add a clear "Learn more" or donation link.
- 5. Add social proof, such as ratings or "most loved" tags, on the featured products.
