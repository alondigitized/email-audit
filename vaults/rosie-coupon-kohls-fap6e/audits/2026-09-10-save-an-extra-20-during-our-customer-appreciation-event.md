# Save an extra 20% during our Customer Appreciation Event 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the same segmented-tier trick Kohl's ran on 2026-09-10 ("20% off with Kohl's Rewards"), but this time it's paired with actual account personalization — a status bar at the very top literally reads "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS." That's a genuine step up from the generic tier stack: it's telling me which of the three boxes (30/20/15%) applies to me before I even scroll. The "Customer Appreciation Event" framing is new copy, not another Labor Day recycle, which matters after a week-plus of near-identical $10-off-$25 sends.
- Below the fold it's standard Kohl's kitchen-sink: category nav, a "Free Spirit Boho" fashion moment, home categories, then a "Picked with you in mind" product grid with real prices. Nothing broken, nothing stale-looking for the season (fall home decor, boho layering pieces). The weak point is the same one this brand always has — too many competing CTAs and no single hero product, so the eye has to do real work to find the offer that actually applies to a Rewards Member.
- Judgement: a legitimately above-average Kohl's send because of the account-status personalization, but the mid-email sprawl keeps it from being a great one.

## What's working

- Account-status bar at the top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS") is real personalization, not just segmented copy — a subscriber sees their own status immediately.
- Three-tier offer block (30% Card / 20% Rewards / 15% Nonmember) is legible with a clear "Ends Sept. 20" deadline.
- A visible coupon code (REWARDS20) with barcode near the bottom makes the offer actionable in-store, not just online.
- Product grid at the bottom has real prices and strikethrough comparisons (e.g., mattress topper $137.99–$209.99 vs $229.99–$349.99), which is concrete enough to justify a click.
- No render bugs — images, category tiles, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer or hero product — the email opens with three overlapping percentage tiers, then six category buttons, then a fashion moment, then more categories, then a product grid. There's no obvious "click here" moment.
- "Customer Appreciation Event" is vague dressing on the same $-off-with-a-tier mechanic Kohl's has run all month; it doesn't feel meaningfully different in substance from 2026-09-10's Rewards email.
- The Kohl's Cash banner ("Plus, earn Kohl's Cash") is squeezed in without a specific dollar amount up top, even though the footer reveals it's $10 per $50 — that number should be in the visual, not buried below.
- Subject doesn't mention that this is a tiered/member offer, so nonmembers open expecting 20% and get 15%.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the applicable tier directly under the subject/status bar as a single big callout ("You get 20% off + 5% Rewards") instead of forcing the reader to scan three boxes — use the account-status data to suppress the other two tiers entirely for logged-in sends.
- 2. Pull one hero product (the mattress topper or the Ninja Slushi, both have strong price drops) into the top fold as the visual anchor, with everything else below it.
- 3. State the Kohl's Cash dollar amount ($10 per $50) in the green banner instead of "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" — vague benefit language undersells a concrete number.
- 4. Trim the category-button wall (Women/Men/Kids/Home, then Bedding/Bath/Kitchen/Decor/Storage/Floor Care) to 3-4 max; it's diluting focus on the actual event.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off is ready — Rewards Member exclusive`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Customer Appreciation: your rate is 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cardholders get 30%, you get 20% — ends Sept. 20`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's promotional HTML template using nested tables/MSO conditionals for Outlook compatibility, with multiple tracking pixels and click-redirect links; compliance headers (unsubscribe, authentication) are absent from the captured payload.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped by automated probing (redirect domains, e.g. `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — link destinations not verified.
- Three separate open/tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (1x1), and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` (1x1) — redundant multi-vendor open tracking (Kohl's CHP + Kohl's mi.kohls.com), not a functional defect but worth noting for load overhead.
- Adobe Audience Manager pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`) fires unconditionally on open.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... aria-hidden="true" /></custom>` — a stray, unopened `</custom>` closing tag follows the tracking pixel `<img>` inside the `<td>`. Invalid HTML; may cause unpredictable DOM handling in strict renderers.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- All 8 flagged images (tracking pixels + `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` rendering-pixels) are missing `alt` text. Tracking/rendering pixels are non-content (1x1) so this is low-impact, but confirm the same holds for any content/hero images further down the truncated source (not visible in this excerpt).
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` present — expected for transactional/marketing email, not a defect.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) with MSO conditional comments — standard practice for Outlook table-based rendering, no issue.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px via media query with `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm` classes — structurally sound as far as visible.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_u` query params on tracking links appear populated (not literal placeholders), consistent with correct merge/personalization resolution at send time.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — could not confirm RFC 2369 compliance from available data (QA note: may be a relay capture gap rather than sender omission).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; not yet confirmed present/absent since source is truncated before the footer, where this normally appears.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown from this capture.
- Note: these are QA/relay-visibility gaps, not confirmed sender violations — footer and full header set were not available in the provided data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Rendering-pixel URLs carry `mi_ecmp=1021519_2026910` and `email_name=260910_DG_Email_Thu_PM` — consistent internal campaign tagging.
- No destination/landing-page URLs were resolvable — all 67 outbound links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` / `click.s.kohls.com` redirectors and were skipped by the probe, so final-landing-page UTM alignment cannot be verified from this data.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the stray `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup should be removed.
- Verify presence of `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` in raw SMTP headers directly (outside the relay) to distinguish a real compliance gap from a capture limitation.
- Confirm a physical mailing address exists in the footer (not present in truncated source).
- If budget/load time is a concern, consolidate the three separate open-tracking pixels (CHP, mi.kohls.com, demdex) if they serve overlapping purposes.
- Spot-check a sample of the 67 redirect links manually to confirm final landing-page UTM parameters align with `mi_ecmp`/`email_name` campaign tags used in the pixels.
