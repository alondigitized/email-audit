# ENDS TONIGHT⌛️ 20% off is on its way out ... 🛒💨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's long-scroll deal wall. The "Super Saturday Deals" banner with the 20% off coupon badge does match the subject's "ENDS TONIGHT / 20% off". The subject, banner and a "Take an extra 20% off" coupon block at the bottom all agree on the offer. That is better than the 11-18 send, where the subject promised "Get 20% off" and the hero showed only a small banner.
- Below the banner the email turns into the usual grid of 40%, 50%, 60% and $6.99 tiles, with "Coupons do not apply" on several of them. Those exclusions undercut the headline 20%. It also arrives alongside the earlier 11-21 sends, "Ends today: 20% off 👀" and "Take 20% off | Find Early Access Black Friday Deals". The "ends tonight" urgency is credible but not new, because the same coupon has been "ending" all week.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send that reads as routine. The ending-tonight angle gives it a small lift over the others.
- The screenshot is a 680x10769 image shrunk to a thin strip, so I could read the headlines and layout but not the fine print.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: the "Super Saturday Deals" banner carries a 20% off badge with "Shop Today" and an "Ends Nov. 21" cue.
- The "Your express ticket to savings" entry row (Home / Women / Men / Kids) gets shoppers into a category quickly.
- Product tiles use large "60%", "40%", "50%" and "$6.99" numerals, so the offers are easy to scan.
- The Kohl's Rewards boxes ("$10 off your home purchase of $50 or more") and the Kohl's Cash block give member benefits.
- The coupon barcode blocks at the bottom (the "Take an extra 20% off" coupon and Kohl's Rewards exclusive) make redemption concrete.
- I saw no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The grid has about 15 product and category tiles, with no single hero product or hierarchy below the banner.
- Several tiles read "Coupons do not apply". That contradicts the "20% off" headline and makes the offer feel less honest.
- The mid-email "Preview the Deals" Black Friday teaser splits the urgency. "Ends tonight" competes with "more Black Friday deals are coming soon."
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now", so none of them says what you would buy.
- Cadence is heavy. This is at least the third 20%-off-ending send of the day or week, which weakens "on its way out".

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero honest and singular: put "20% off ends at 11:59 PM tonight" with the coupon code visible in the banner, and move the exclusions into one line directly under it.
- 2. Cut the grid to 6-8 tiles and lead with the categories that actually take the coupon. Drop the tiles marked "Coupons do not apply", or move them to a separate "Doorbusters (no coupon)" block.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop Home Deals" or "Shop Kids Pajamas".
- 4. Put a live countdown or "Ends at midnight CT" cue in the banner so the urgency isn't only in the subject.
- 5. Stop sending near-duplicate 20%-off emails within a single day. Consolidate into one last-chance send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off expires tonight ⏳`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use your coupon on home, kids & more before Super Saturday ends. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 60% off select styles and $10 Kohl's Rewards savings, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a mobile media query, but three things limit the audit. The HTML source was truncated, the header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) were not captured by the relay, and all 79 tracking links were skipped. Confirmed issues are malformed head/body markup, missing alt text on content images, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and a link-heavy plain-text part.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verifiable:** QA skipped 79 links on tracking domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unconfirmed.
- **Several tracking pixels are stacked:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/a68e9e0b-…` (1x1, no alt)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  This is not a defect, but the first is a bare `<img>` placed before any wrapper table.
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is closed with `</custom>`, which has no matching opener: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. Some clients may issue a request to the base URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed):**
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`

  The `rp/` images are probably rendered promo content. With images blocked, the "ends tonight / 20% off" message may be invisible.
- **Tracking pixel false positives:** QA flags `o.gif` as missing alt, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct. The `demdex.net/event` and `click.chp.kohls.com` pixels have no `alt` (the latter is 1x1 with no `alt` or `aria-hidden`). These are low severity.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this affects the web-view and accessibility title.
- **Invalid head markup:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` sits inside `<head>`. `<div>` is not permitted there, and parsers will push it into `<body>`.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is used alongside XML namespaces and self-closing tags (`<meta … />`). This is tolerated in practice but is inconsistent.
- **Positive:** layout tables use `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"` is set, MSO conditionals are present, and the Montserrat/Roboto fonts have Arial/Helvetica fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The subscriber identifier `mi_u=604230016` is appended to pixel URLs and resolved. The truncated source means the lower body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Kohl's is a major sender and likely includes them. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo require them, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The source is truncated before the footer, so this is **unconfirmed** and needs a check of the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the sending subdomain, but alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters:** all links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, so final landing URLs and UTMs can't be audited from the source.
- **Campaign ID:** the only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=1026403_20261121` plus `email_name=261121_DG_Email_Sat_Mi…`. Its date matches the send date (2026-11-21) and the "ENDS TONIGHT" subject.
- **Landing page alignment:** not testable, because the HTTP probe was skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text** to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` promo images and the two `60699446_*` product images. The offer must be readable with images off.
2. **Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Fix the stray `</custom>` tag** after the `open.aspx` pixel.
4. **Move the schema.org `<div itemscope>`** out of `<head>`, or remove it. Email clients don't consume it.
5. **Populate `<title>`** and drop the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
6. **Reduce plain-text URL density.** 14,043 of 19,771 characters (71%) are URLs, which can raise spam scoring. Shorten the tracking URLs in the text part and add real copy.
7. **Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml`** to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and the footer address. Also resolve one sample redirect per CTA to check final URLs and UTMs.
