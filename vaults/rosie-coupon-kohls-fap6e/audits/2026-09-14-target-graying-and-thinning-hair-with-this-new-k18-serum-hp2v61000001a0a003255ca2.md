# Target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is another Sephora kitchen-sink send with the same bait-and-switch structure this brand keeps running: subject promises "NEW K18 serum" for graying/thinning hair, but what actually opens is a generic "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" hero banner that has nothing to do with hair, K18, or greying. You have to scroll past that unrelated flash-sale hero and a "TRENDING NOW" filler banner before you even reach the before/after hair photo and K18 copy the subject promised.
- Once you get there, though, the K18 section itself is genuinely strong — a real before/after scalp photo with "LESS GREY" / "MORE HAIR" callouts, a specific clinical stat ("up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys"), and clear pricing ($120 / $75). That's a legitimately good, targeted hero — it's just buried third in the scroll instead of leading.
- After the K18 block, the email reverts to a sprawling, unrelated product grid (fragrance, lip liner, dry shampoo, facial essence) and then a fresh promo (4X points, "New for Fall," Holiday Drops) — none of which reinforces the subject line at all. Net: a good idea (K18 hair regrowth) undermined by bad sequencing and a distracting header.

## What's working

- The before/after scalp photo is a strong, specific, believable visual proof point for the "graying and thinning hair" claim.
- Clinical stat callout ("up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys," "results from a 12-week clinical study") gives real credibility.
- Pricing is transparent and visible ($120 serum, $75 mask) with no hidden fine print at that section.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" exclusivity badge is a clear differentiator.
- No rendering bugs — images, badges, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The subject's hero content (K18) is not the first thing you see — a generic "50% off Select Beauty" banner leads instead.
- A "TRENDING NOW / Thank us later" filler banner adds a second unrelated detour before reaching the promised content.
- After the K18 section, the email sprawls into an unrelated product grid (perfume, lip liner, dry shampoo) that dilutes the single-topic promise of the subject.
- Multiple competing CTAs (50% off, Trending Now, K18, 4X points, New for Fall, Holiday Drops) fragment attention rather than funneling to one action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the K18 hero. Move the before/after photo and clinical stat directly under the header — that's the entire reason this email exists per the subject line; everything else can follow below it.
- 2. Cut or relocate the "50% off Select Beauty" and "TRENDING NOW" banners below the K18 block, or drop them from this send entirely — they actively compete with and delay the promised content.
- 3. Add a direct "Shop K18" CTA button immediately under the clinical stat instead of making users scroll further for pricing/purchase action.
- 4. Trim the unrelated product grid (perfume/lip liner/dry shampoo) — if this is meant to be a hair-focused send, keep it hair-focused, or spin the rest into a separate "New Arrivals" email.
