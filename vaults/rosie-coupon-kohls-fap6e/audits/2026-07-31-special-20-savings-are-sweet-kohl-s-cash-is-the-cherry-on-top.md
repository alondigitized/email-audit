# Special 20% savings are sweet 🍦 Kohl's Cash is the cherry on top 🍒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is visually the cleanest Kohl's send in this stretch — the "FRIENDS & FAMILY" hero banner with the circular "20% off" badge renders fully intact, centered, and legible, a real contrast to the clipped/garbled hero graphics logged repeatedly in late July. Layout is tight end-to-end: navbar, hero, Kohl's Cash strip, category nav, product tiles, home-goods grid, and a real barcode/coupon block near the footer.
- The problem isn't the render, it's the campaign fatigue. Brand history shows a "Friends & Family... extra 20% off... Ends Aug. 2" send already went out on 2026-07-30 at 9.00/10. This email carries the identical hero, the identical 20%-off F&F coupon, and the identical "Ends Aug. 2" deadline — just repackaged behind a new dessert-themed subject line ("sweet... cherry on top") one day later. A subscriber who opened yesterday's email will recognize this instantly as the same offer resent.
- Execution earns credit; strategy doesn't. This is a well-built wrapper around a stale promo.

## What's working

- Hero banner is fully intact and legible — no clipping or garbling, unlike the July 27–28 sends.
- Rewards status ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER") and earn-rate ("Earn 5% REWARDS") are shown right at the top, a nice personalization touch for logged-in members.
- Coupon code, PIN, barcode, and expiration are all clearly printed near the footer — no hunting for the deal.
- Product tiles pair price with context ("Starting at $19.99, Reg. $24.99-$29.99 + save with coupon") rather than a bare percentage.
- Clear section CTAs throughout ("Shop Now" appears with each major offer block, plus category buttons for Women/Men/Kids/Home).

## What's weak

- This is the same "Friends & Family... 20% off... Ends Aug. 2" offer that already went out 2026-07-30 — subscribers get zero new value, just a reskinned subject.
- The preview text leaks "View this email in browser" junk directly alongside real copy, which is sloppy and undercuts the "summer steals" teaser.
- Subject line (66 chars) doesn't mention Friends & Family at all, so it under-promises relative to what the hero actually delivers — a mismatch between inbox promise and opened-email payoff.
- Every CTA button says "Shop Now" — six identical instances with zero differentiation between shorts, sandals, women's, men's, and home categories.
- No social proof or reviews anywhere in the body despite featuring specific priced products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the same F&F 20%/Aug 2 offer under a new subject line — either escalate the offer (stack Kohl's Cash tiers, add a second coupon) or hold this slot for genuinely new creative. Repeat sends of an unchanged promo train subscribers to skip opens.
- 2. Make CTA copy category-specific instead of generic "Shop Now" — e.g., "Shop Shorts," "Shop Sandals," "Shop Bedding" — so each button carries its own scent trail.
- 3. Fix the preview text so it doesn't visibly concatenate real copy with "View this email in browser" — move that link out of the visible preview snippet.
- 4. Align subject with hero: either name Friends & Family in the subject or drop the F&F framing from the hero to reduce inbox-to-open dissonance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends & Family: an extra 20% off, ends Aug. 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards status unlocks 20% off + Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shorts from $19.99, sandals up to 20% off — plus $10 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: your extra 20% off coupon is loaded and ready to use in store or online.`
