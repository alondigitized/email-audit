# ENDS TONIGHT: Your deal is waiting to be revealed 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's daily production template — Rewards status bar, red coupon banner, green Kohl's Cash strip, nav buttons — running since 7/30, and today it's the worst mismatch of the week. The subject promises a "mystery deal... waiting to be revealed," implying a scratch-off or countdown gimmick, but the hero delivers a plain pink banner with the word "DEA" visibly cut off at the right edge — that's not a reveal mechanic, that's a broken or badly cropped image. Below the fold it's the standard Semi-Annual intimates push (bras, Jockey, panties, shapewear) and a 70% Ultimate Clearance banner, neither of which has anything to do with a "revealed" deal.
- Compare to 8/15's "Intimates Sale 20% off Kohl's Cash" (7/10), which at least matched subject to hero cleanly. Today the subject is pure clickbait for a mystery-deal hook the email never pays off, and the hero graphic reads as visually broken rather than intentional.
- Judgement: weak send. The cropped hero image is a real production defect, and the copy strategy (mystery reveal) isn't honored anywhere in the body.

## What's working

- Rewards status bar and "Spend $0 to score your next reward" personalizes the top of the email.
- Green Kohl's Cash strip and red coupon banners keep brand color consistency throughout.
- Semi-Annual intimates section has clear per-product pricing ($19.99, 25% off, 5/$35, 25% off) with visible model photography.
- "Picked with you in mind" product grid gives concrete SKUs (towel, blender, jeans, mattress topper) with specific price ranges.

## What's weak

- The hero banner text is cut off — only "DEA" is visible, not "DEAL" — a visible render/crop bug in the most important real estate of the email.
- Subject promises a "deal waiting to be revealed" but nothing in the body delivers a reveal mechanic (scratch-off, countdown, mystery box); it's just the standard intimates + clearance template.
- No urgency treatment anywhere despite "ENDS TONIGHT" in the subject — no countdown timer, no "today only" banner in the hero.
- Extra 30% off coupon code is buried near the bottom after two full promo sections, undermining the "your deal" framing that should be front and center.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cropped hero image immediately — "DEA" cut off is a shipped bug, not a design choice; QA render at full width before send.
- 2. If running a "mystery deal reveal" subject, build a hero that actually delivers a reveal moment (e.g., a scratch-off graphic or "Your deal: EXTRA 30% OFF" stated big and bold) instead of generic intimates creative.
- 3. Move the 30% off coupon code to the hero — it's the actual "deal" and currently it's the last thing in the email.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or "ends tonight at midnight" bar near the top to justify the urgency in the subject line.
