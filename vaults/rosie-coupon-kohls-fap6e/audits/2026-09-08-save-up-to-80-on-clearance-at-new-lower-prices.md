# Save up to 80% on clearance at new lower prices 🙌
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine break from the Labor Day $10-off-$25 treadmill Kohl's has run all week (six near-identical sends between 2026-09-03 and 2026-09-07), and that alone makes it feel fresher in the inbox even though "clearance" is itself a recurring beat. The hero is clean and does its job: "CLEARANCE / NEW LOWER PRICES / Now up to 80% off" with a single "Shop Now" CTA, no clutter competing for attention. But the email quickly sprawls — a lifestyle collage for 20% off fall/Halloween decor, then a "Picked with you in mind" product grid with a $349.99 slushi maker, bath towels, jeans, and a men's polo, none of which cohere around one offer or one shopper.
- The core problem is honesty of the headline offer: "up to 80% off" is classic clearance bait — the four "picked for you" products shown are not clearance-priced or discounted at all (they're just merchandise at various price points), so the specific hero promise never gets backed up by visible proof in the body. That's a bigger long-term deliverability/trust risk than another Labor Day repeat would have been.
- Judgement: solid subject and hero, forgettable execution once you scroll — a 4/10 clearance email pretending it earned a "picked with you in mind" personalization line it doesn't demonstrate.

## What's working

- Hero headline is unambiguous: "Now up to 80% off," bold red banner, one visible "Shop Now" CTA button.
- Clean top-of-funnel navigation (Home/Women/Men/Kids) gives an easy alternate path if clearance isn't the shopper's interest.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact end to end.
- Rewards status bar ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" / "Earn 5% REWARDS") is a nice loyalty touch visible right at the top.

## What's weak

- The "80% off" hero offer is never substantiated anywhere else in the email — none of the four featured products show a strikethrough or clearance price.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" reads as generic/parasocial copy sitting over a completely generic product grid (slushi maker, towels, jeans, polo) — no actual personalization signal.
- Too many competing CTAs and offers stacked in one email: clearance 80% off, then 20% off fall/Halloween decor, then four unrelated product picks — no single throughline.
- The lifestyle collage section (chair, pillows, vase, candle) is moody but doesn't clearly connect to a shoppable link beyond "Shop Now" underneath — weak visual hierarchy toward a specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Prove the 80% off claim — show at least 2-3 clearance items with strikethrough pricing right under the hero banner, not four full-price "picked for you" items further down.
- 2. Cut the email to one offer. Either this is a clearance email or a fall/Halloween decor email — the polo/jeans/towel grid at the bottom belongs in a different send.
- 3. Replace the vague "Picked with you in mind" personalization line — if it's not actually personalized, don't imply it is; swap for "Clearance bestsellers" or similar honest framing.
- 4. Add urgency to the clearance banner (a date or "while supplies last") since clearance inventory is inherently time-bound and this rubric signal is currently absent.
