# Save 20% & earn Kohl’s Cash. It’s that easy 😊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a Rewards-status email, and Kohl's leads with it: "Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards" pinned above the hero, then a clean 20% off coupon banner, then a green "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" strip. It's a technically flawless render — consistent with every send in this run since 7/30 — nav buttons align, product tiles are crisp, no clipped starbursts or broken art anywhere down the length of this long email.
- Where it breaks from the pack is structure: this is a mega-catalog send stacking activewear, Levi's, fall home, kitchen, and "Picked with you in mind" personalized-sounding recommendations (a coffee maker, bath towel, mattress topper, jeans) all under one subject line about 20% off + Kohl's Cash. The subject promise is honest and matches the top of the email, but by the time a reader scrolls past six distinct category pitches, the original hook is diluted. Compare to 8/5's Active Sale send, which stayed on one theme and scored higher for it.
- Solid, on-brand, technically clean — but this is a kitchen-sink send that will perform on the loyalty/Kohl's Cash mechanic more than on any single product story.

## What's working

- Rewards member status and 5% Rewards line sit right at the top — real personalization signal for a loyalty subscriber.
- The 20% off coupon banner with code, expiration ("Ends Aug. 16"), and a visible barcode near the bottom removes friction — reader doesn't need to hunt for the code.
- Kohl's Cash mechanic is spelled out concretely ("Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent") with a redemption window, not just teased.
- Category nav (Home/Women/Men/Kids) up top gives multiple clear exits for different shoppers.
- No render bugs across a long, image-heavy email — a real production accomplishment at this length.

## What's weak

- Six unrelated category pitches (activewear, Levi's, fall home, kitchen/bath, "picked for you" mixed bag) crammed into one email dilutes the single offer promised in the subject.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy)" pairs a coffee maker, a bath towel, a mattress topper, and jeans — that's not a coherent personalized recommendation, it's a random four-SKU grid, which undercuts the personalization claim.
- The Levi's 30% off and activewear "up to 40% off" blocks both note "Coupons do not apply to select brands," burying exclusions in small print under bold headline percentages.
- By the fifth scroll section, hierarchy is lost — there's no single hero product to click, just a wall of category doors.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections max: the 20% off + Kohl's Cash offer, and one featured category (pick activewear or fall home, not both) — this single change would do the most to restore a clear primary CTA.
- 2. Move the exclusion language ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") next to the percentage callout itself, not buried below the CTA button, so the offer reads as honest up front.
- 3. Replace the "Picked with you in mind" grid with an actually coherent set (e.g., all home, or all apparel) so the personalization claim isn't undercut by a random product mix.
- 4. Add urgency reinforcement mid-email, not just at the bottom — repeat "Ends Aug. 16" near the fold so scrollers who don't reach the barcode still see the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off + Kohl's Cash — your Rewards status is active`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Member perk: 20% off everything, ends Aug. 16`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend, thru 8/23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall home, Levi's, activewear — all under your 20% coupon.`
