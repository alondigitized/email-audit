# TODAY ONLY: Can't-miss savings await 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the first genuinely new hero angle in nearly a week of Kohl's sends: "TODAY ONLY" urgency stacked on Denim Deal Days, starting at $21.99, followed by a Levi's-specific 30%-off callout. It's a real departure from the Friends & Family 20%-off treadmill this brand has been running since 2026-08-24 — but it buries the actual "today only" mechanic. The banner screams urgency, yet the coupon block near the bottom shows "extra 30% off, Ends Aug. 30" and a barcode — so the urgency is real (today is the last day) but the copy doesn't clearly connect "TODAY ONLY" hero text to the specific deadline, making it feel like generic hype rather than credible scarcity.
- Visually this is one of the stronger Kohl's sends I've reviewed this week — clear denim hero, a coherent secondary Levi's push, then a logical cascade into fall home categories and "picked for you" cross-sell tiles. It's cluttered by the bottom (bedding, bath, kitchen, decor, towels, jeans, mattress topper all crammed in), but the top third does its job well.
- Verdict: strong hero, weak urgency execution, and still overloaded lower in the email — a 6/10 send that's better than most of this week's reskins but not a standout.

## What's working

- Bold, high-contrast "TODAY ONLY" repeating-text banner immediately signals urgency and is impossible to miss.
- Denim Deal Days hero has a real price anchor ($21.99, reg. $29.99–$49.99) instead of vague "% off" language.
- The Levi's-specific tile ("Up to 30% off. Jeans. Select styles.") gives a recognizable brand name distinct from the generic Kohl's coupon churn.
- Category nav bars (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeated twice give easy wayfinding for scanners.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- "TODAY ONLY" in the hero never states what happens today — no explicit expiration time or connection to the 30%-off coupon shown near the bottom.
- The email sprawls into unrelated categories (bedding, bath, kitchen, decor, towels, mattress topper) that dilute the denim focus promised by the subject line.
- The extra-30%-off coupon block is pushed almost to the very bottom, past a wall of home-goods tiles — most readers will never scroll that far.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization claim isn't backed by anything that looks personalized (a bath towel, a slushi maker, jeans, a mattress topper is a random assortment, not a tailored pick).

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30%-off coupon and barcode to just below the hero, and change the hero subhead to state the actual deadline ("Ends today, Aug. 30") — right now the strongest offer is the least visible.
- 2. Cut the email after the Levi's/Denim Guide sections, or split home-goods into a separate send — the subject promises denim savings, not towels and mattress toppers.
- 3. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with an actual denim/home crossover framing if the assortment can't be personalized, since the current label overpromises.
- 4. Add a real countdown or explicit end-time to "TODAY ONLY" so the urgency claim is credible, not just decorative repetition.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Levi's up to 30% off, plus $10 Kohl's Cash on $50+`

- Secondary tracking pixels: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, multiple `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (retargeting pixels, one per creative block), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager).

- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — expected for transactional/marketing email rendering, not a defect.

- Because all 74 outbound links are tracking-redirect URLs, actual landing-page destinations (and whether they match the "TODAY ONLY" promo messaging) could not be verified — this was explicitly skipped by the QA probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results directly against raw SMTP headers (not the relay-processed copy) to rule out a relay-capture false negative before escalating as a compliance gap.
2. If List-Unsubscribe-Post is genuinely absent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support to stay compliant with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
3. Add empty `alt=""` attributes to the 8 flagged tracking pixels (cosmetic HTML-validity fix, not a real accessibility risk since they're non-content images).
4. Fix the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — invalid markup with no matching open tag.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 74 tracking-redirect links manually (outside the automated probe) to confirm they resolve to live, correct landing pages rather than 404s or mismatched promos.
6. Populate or remove the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter on the `916d6fa08d185233.png` pixel if it's meant to carry a redemption end date.
