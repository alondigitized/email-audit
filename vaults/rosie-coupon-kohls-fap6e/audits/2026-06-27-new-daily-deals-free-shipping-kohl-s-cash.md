# New Daily Deals ✅ Free Shipping ✅ Kohl's Cash ✅
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth Deal Days email in five days. The June 26 evening send ("Instant Kohl's Cash ends tonight") explicitly positioned itself as the campaign closer — and yet here we are on June 27 with the same Instant Kohl's Cash badge back at the top of the email. That single continuity issue is the biggest story of this send: it reads as either a last-minute reversal or a deliberate bait-and-extend, and subscribers who acted on last night's urgency now have reason to feel played.
- Setting that aside, the email itself is structurally identical to the eight sends before it: the Deal Days hero, the color-coded category nav strip, stacked discount sections (40% off, 70% off, 50% off, clearance 75% off), and a parade of specific price points ($9.99 dress shirts, $15.99 slow cooker, $3.99 towels, $21.99 vacuum). The creative executes well on deal density — there are real, scannable prices throughout — but nothing has changed from yesterday, or the day before.
- Bottom line: this email would be a solid 8 if it were the third send in the campaign. As the tenth, it is noise. The subject's ✅ checklist format is a minor creative refresh but doesn't rescue the core problem: cadence-fatigued subscribers have seen every one of these deals and already decided whether to act.

## What's working

- **Specific dollar prices are prominent throughout** — $9.99 shirts, $15.99 slow cooker, $3.99 towels, $21.99 vacuum give the eye concrete anchors and make comparison easy.
- **"8 DAYS OF DEALS" counter** creates genuine campaign-scoped urgency and tells subscribers how much runway is left.
- **Clearance 75% off** section at the bottom acts as a value escalation for deal-seekers who scroll, and the purple/pink lifestyle shot breaks the visual monotony of the stacked sections above it.
- **No render bugs** — the layout is clean, images load, text sits correctly in every section.
- **✅ subject format** is the one creative departure from the prior nine emails; it scans quickly and avoids the exclamation fatigue the earlier sends leaned on.

## What's weak

- **Instant Kohl's Cash reappearing after "ends tonight"** (June 26 evening send) is a credibility problem — subscribers who rushed to shop last night will notice immediately.
- **The creative is copy-pasted from nine prior sends** — same hero banner, same nav strip, same section cadence. There is nothing here that would surprise a subscriber who opened any of the June 23–26 emails.
- **Category nav strip is too color-compressed** at this render width — the buttons blur together and don't read as distinct entry points on mobile.
- **No social proof, no reviews, no "X people shopped this today"** — at this stage of the campaign, a bestseller signal or urgency-by-popularity callout would do more than another percentage badge.
- **Subject says "New Daily Deals" but nothing in the body establishes what is new** vs. yesterday — the word "new" is doing work the body can't support.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Acknowledge the Kohl's Cash continuity honestly.** If it was extended, say so: "We extended it — Kohl's Cash is still live." Silence on the reversal of "ends tonight" will cost more trust than the extension earns in sales.
- 2. **Introduce a "bestsellers today" or "going fast" callout** — e.g., "X sold in the last 24 hours" next to the $9.99 dress shirt. Campaign Day 10 needs social proof, not more percentage badges.
- 3. **Collapse the deal sections to three** — the stacked 40%/70%/50%/75% off sections dilute rather than amplify. Pick the two sharpest categories and give them space.
- 4. **Replace the category nav strip with countdown-to-end** — "X days left in Deal Days" is more motivating than a grid of department colors that all blur together.
- 5. **Retire or refresh the hero banner.** The "KOHL'S DEAL DAYS" graphic is unchanged from June 23. Swapping even the color treatment signals a new day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Day 8 of 10: $9.99 shirts, $15.99 slow cooker + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash is still live — here's what's selling today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `75% off clearance + towels from $3.99 — deals change daily`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `These are today's sharpest prices — see what's new vs. yesterday`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "New Daily Deals" Email

**Date reviewed:** 2026-06-30

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard HTML4 transitional table layout with MSO VML conditionals and a responsive media query stack — all structurally sound. The flagged issues cluster around missing compliance signals (likely stripped by the AgentMail relay) and widespread missing alt text on tracking/retargeting pixels.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/redirect links skipped by probe.** All destination links pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` before resolving. This is expected for a tracked send, but means broken destination URLs would not be caught by the automated probe.

Three separate open-tracking pixels are present simultaneously:

| Pixel | Domain | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `a0acc4ff…` | `click.chp.kohls.com` | CoherentPath click/open tracker |
| `o.gif` | `mi.kohls.com` | Movable Ink open tracker |
| `open.aspx` | `click.s.kohls.com` | ESP open tracker (Salesforce MC) |

Triple-pixel stacking is not a bug, but Apple MPP will fire all three on delivery, inflating open metrics across all three platforms independently.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and will be rendered as literal text or ignored depending on the parser. It should be removed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 tracking/pixel images missing `alt` text.** The QA checker flagged all of these:

- Open pixel (`a0acc4ff…`) — no `alt`
- Movable Ink pixel (`o.gif`) — no `alt`
- Adobe Audience Manager pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) — no `alt`
- 7× Movable Ink retargeting images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/…`) — no `alt`

Tracking pixels that are invisible by design should carry `alt=""` (empty string) explicitly, not omit the attribute. Omitting it causes screen readers to announce the raw filename or URL. The open pixel and AAM pixel currently have no `alt` attribute at all.

`user-scalable=0` is set in the viewport meta tag:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
This prevents users from pinching to zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure and a known accessibility concern on mobile.

`<title>` tag is empty:
```html
<title></title>
```
Some email clients surface the title as a preview label or tab name; an empty title is a minor omission.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` for the same two families (Roboto, Montserrat), causing redundant HTTP requests on non-MSO clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) are visible in the truncated source. The Movable Ink retargeting pixel URLs include `mi_u=604230016`, indicating subscriber-level personalization was resolved at send time.

One retargeting pixel URL contains a trailing truncated parameter:
```
…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value is empty and `mi_kc_event_pe` is cut off in the provided source. If this reflects the actual rendered pixel URL, it may indicate an unfilled merge token for a Kohl's Cash expiry date.

**No issues found** with conditional logic or dynamic block rendering based on the available source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address not detected** by QA scan. This is a required element. The source is truncated, so the address may exist in the footer; confirm it is present in the full rendered email.

**List-Unsubscribe header not found.** The QA checker notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence. However, if the header is genuinely missing:
- Gmail and Apple Mail will not show the one-click unsubscribe button in the UI.
- RFC 8058 (`List-Unsubscribe-Post`) is also not confirmed, meaning one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy for >5k/day senders) may not be functional.

These should be verified against the raw SMTP headers, not just the relay-captured version.

**Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay snapshot. For a sender of Kohl's volume, authentication failure would cause significant inbox placement issues — verify directly via a seed inbox test.

`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />` are both present — correct practice for email HTML to prevent indexing and referrer leakage.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 destination links pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) or `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) redirect chains. Because the probe skipped these, UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs cannot be confirmed from this audit.

The Movable Ink retargeting pixel carries `mi_ecmp=1016060_2026627` — this appears to be an internal campaign code, not a standard UTM. If UTM params are applied only at the redirect layer and not surfaced to GA4/analytics on the destination page, attribution may be incomplete.

No landing page probing was possible due to redirect chains being skipped.
