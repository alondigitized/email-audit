# Select brands are 50% off 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a "50% off Select Beauty" email, and it opens with a genuinely concrete hook — a pink circular badge reading "50% off" with named brands (Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, MAC Cosmetics) and a "TODAY ONLY" callout next to a lipstick product shot. That's a real, specific offer, echoing the same daily-deal format Sephora ran on 8/28 ("50% off select beauty starts today").
- The problem is everything after the hero. The email immediately pivots into a "Real Luxury" curated editorial section — four moody lifestyle spreads (Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance, Hair Care) with premium pricing ($46-$195) that has nothing to do with the 50%-off deal up top. It reads like two different emails were stapled together: a flash-sale teaser glued onto a full-price prestige-brand showcase. Anyone drawn in by the discount headline scrolls into a wall of full-price product spotlights, which is a bait-and-switch structure even if unintentional.
- Given the brand history, this is another example of Sephora's recurring "strong hero, unfocused body" pattern (see 8/28 and 8/23 sends). The hero alone would justify a 6-7; the disconnect with the body pulls it down.

## What's working

- Hero badge is legible and offer-forward: "50% off Select Beauty," pink circle graphic draws the eye immediately.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency callout is visible near the top without extra scrolling.
- Named brands (Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, MAC Cosmetics) in the hero give the discount concrete scope rather than vague "beauty" language.
- Clear "SHOP NOW" CTA button directly under the hero offer.
- Section headers (Real Luxury, Next-Level Skincare, Feel-Good Fragrance, Hype-Worthy Hair Care) are stylistically distinct and easy to scan.
- "Buy Now, Pay Later" (Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal) module and Beauty Insider footer are on-brand and consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- The 50%-off deal is never reinforced anywhere below the hero — no product in the "Real Luxury" section carries a discount, and several are marked "NEW" or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" (full price), directly undercutting the subject line's promise.
- No indication of which products/categories the 50% off actually applies to beyond three brand names in tiny hero text — "Exclusions apply" is visible but unreadable at this size, which reads as buried fine print.
- The email is extremely long (7+ screen-heights) with four nearly identical "lifestyle photo + copy blurb + 2 products" blocks — high scroll fatigue for a "shop the deal" send.
- Visual/thematic whiplash: dark, moody, prestige-editorial styling (black backgrounds, script fonts) sits awkwardly against the bright, urgent, giveaway-style hero.
- The "rhode" and "New in Skincare" modules near the bottom feel bolted on and further dilute the single-offer focus the subject line promised.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reinforce the 50%-off offer throughout the body, not just the hero.** If Too Faced/Drunk Elephant/MAC are the actual discount brands, give them their own dedicated product grid immediately after the hero with visible struck-through pricing — don't bury the deal under unrelated full-price luxury content.
- 2. **Cut the email length roughly in half.** Four full lifestyle sections plus a payment module plus a "new from rhode" plug plus "new in skincare" is too much for one send — pick the strongest 2 sections or split into a separate curated-editorial send (which is what 8/26's "Clean at Sephora" already is).
- 3. **Move exclusions/fine print out of illegible micro-text** — either state the category clearly ("Fragrance & Makeup only") or drop the vague badge copy.
- 4. **Add a persistent sticky reminder of the deal** (e.g., a thin banner or repeated small badge) so scrollers don't lose the offer context by section three.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Too Faced, Drunk Elephant & MAC`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off beauty pass expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up before this one's gone — top brands, half off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three cult favorites, one huge discount, 24 hours only.`
