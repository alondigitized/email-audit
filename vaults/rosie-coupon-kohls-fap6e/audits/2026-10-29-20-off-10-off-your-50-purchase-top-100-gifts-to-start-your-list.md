# 20% off ✅ $10 off your $50+ purchase ✅ Top 100 gifts to start your list ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fourth straight send pushing the 20% off plus $10 off $50+ stack, after Oct 26, Oct 27 and the two Oct 28 emails. The subject has three checkmarks, and the render only delivers the first two well. "Top 100 gifts" does appear, but only as a tile in the middle of a very long email, with "Shop Now" as its button. The hero is the usual Kohl's Cash plus gift-box image, with "The North Kohl's" branding and a "Shop Now" button.
- The email is a long gift-guide and everything-else scroll. After the hero it has a nav row, a "Gift List Lane" sign, a For Her / Him / Teens / Kids grid, Toy Box and Top 100 tiles, and home content. Then come a Sephora at Kohl's 25% off block, a product grid, services, and a stack of barcodes. The offers are real, but none of them is the clear focus. Home decor and Sephora compete with the gift-list theme the subject promises.
- Overall it's competent and on-brand, but it's cadence fatigue. Someone who got the same stack three days running has no new reason to open or click. The Top 100 list is the one fresh element, and it's undersold.

## What's working

- The hero ties the offer to a Kohl's Cash visual, and "Ends Nov 1" gives a concrete deadline.
- The gift grid (For Her / Him / Teens / Kids) is easy to scan and fits the holiday season.
- Every offer has a scannable barcode and code at the bottom: 20% off, $10 off $50+, jewelry and watches, and Kohl's Cash.
- The reassurance block (free returns through Jan 31, 2027, free store pickup, same-day delivery) removes friction.
- "Top 100 Gifts" gets its own tile, so the subject's third promise is at least linked to a landing spot.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with many competing themes (gifts, hosting, home, Sephora, product picks). The hero offer gets diluted.
- Nearly every button reads "Shop Now," so none of them is specific.
- The Top 100 gifts tile sits mid-email, so the third checkmark in the subject doesn't match the hero.
- The Sephora block advertises a different deadline (Oct 29) and a different mechanic (Beauty Insiders only) in the middle of the main offer.
- The email repeats the same stack as three recent sends (Oct 26, 27, 28) with only the gift-list framing changed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Put "20% off + $10 off $50+" in large text, with one button reading "Get the Top 100 Gifts."
- 2. Cut the email to the hero, the gift grid, the Top 100 tile and the coupons. Move home and Sephora to separate sends or segments.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with specific verbs ("Shop gifts for her," "Shop toys under $25").
- 4. Put the coupon codes right under the hero so a shopper can use them without scrolling to the bottom.
- 5. Vary the creative day to day, so the same stack doesn't read as spam.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Top 100 gifts list is ready 🎁 + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Start your gift list: 20% off + $10 off $50+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top 100 gifts, plus codes to stack through Nov 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Toys, tech and stocking stuffers. Your coupons are inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Most of the 5 QA warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay (missing headers) and the truncated HTML (footer not visible). The source does have real defects: invalid markup (`</custom>`, a `<div>` in `<head>`), an empty `<title>` and favicon href, viewport zoom lock, and several images with no `alt`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing skipped:** 77 links on `click.s.kohls.com` and similar redirect domains were not probed, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** there are four in the first lines of `<body>`:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/468b9741-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel:** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` ends with a stray `</custom>` tag that has no opener.
- **Empty dynamic parameter:** the image URL for `916d6fa08d185233.png` has `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value. This looks like an unresolved Movable Ink variable. The `mi_kc_event_p…` parameter after it is cut off, so I can't check it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** the QA scan flags 12 images. Real content images include:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png`

  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205009_2_2x`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59205009_3_2x`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, any offer or product information in them is lost.
- **False positive:** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image. The `click.chp.kohls.com` and `kohls.demdex.net` pixels do lack `alt`. They are tracking pixels, so this is low impact.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request to the document's own URL in some clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and may drop the following `<style>` or `<link>` elements. Schema.org microdata is also not supported by Gmail's email markup.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the 1×1 pixel table, the AAM table (`width="100%" align="center"`) and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no role. The main layout tables do have it.
- **Mixed syntax:** an HTML 4.01 doctype is used with XHTML-style `/>` tags and mixed quote styles. This is low risk.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `kohlsprod/59205009_*` images. Add `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` and move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`. Use `<script type="application/ld+json">` instead if markup is wanted.
3. Fill in `<title>` and drop or fill the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter in the Movable Ink image URL.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated raw message. That would let you confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the `Authentication-Results` values and the footer address.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links (follow the redirects) to check final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page offers.
