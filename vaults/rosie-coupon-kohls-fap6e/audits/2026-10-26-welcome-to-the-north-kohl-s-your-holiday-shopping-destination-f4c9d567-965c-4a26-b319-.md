# Welcome to The North Kohl’s 🎄 Your holiday shopping destination 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- After nine near-identical "lowest prices of the season" sends since Oct 19, this is a real change of pace. Kohl's drops the price-catalog grid for a branded holiday concept, "The North Kohl's". It has an elf and a snowy village hero, plus editorial tiles (Hosting Headquarters, Style Square, Cozy Corner). It's the first send of the week that feels like a campaign instead of a markdown dump, and the render is clean.
- The concept is charming, but the offer is buried. The top gift-box hero carries only "Ends Nov. 1 / Shop Now" and a "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" badge, with no number attached. The real money sits far down: the $10 off $50 jewelry, SAVE20 for an extra 20% off, and $10 Kohl's Cash per $50. The tiles are lifestyle copy ("Looks that'll steal attention from the tree") with generic "Shop Now" buttons. It's a good brand-building send but a weaker conversion send than Oct 25's "FINAL HOURS" email.
- Judgement: a smart reset of the creative rhythm, but the email under-delivers on the offer. Lead with the deal and let the North Pole theme support it.

## What's working

- The holiday-village concept breaks the week's repetitive "lowest prices" creative and gives the inbox something fresh.
- The Rewards status bar ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn 5% Rewards") makes the loyalty benefit visible at the top.
- Category nav pills (Home, Women, Men, Kids) give fast routing right under the hero.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product grid shows concrete prices: Ninja Slushi $299.99 (was $349.99), towels $2.99–$9.99, jeans $52.
- The bottom coupon stack (SAVE20, DOSHOPF10, JEWELRY10) with barcodes and PINs is usable in-store, and the free returns through Jan 31, 2027 line reduces holiday purchase anxiety.

## What's weak

- The hero has no number. "Ends Nov. 1" and "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" don't say what the deal is.
- The editorial tiles (Hosting, Style Square, Cozy Corner) use clever copy but repeat the generic "Shop Now" button and name no product or price.
- The email is very long, and the strongest offers (20% off, $10 off $50) sit near the bottom, behind a lot of scrolling.
- The jewelry block piles on stacked claims: "$10 off $50+", "60% off + 20% off w/coupon", and "select styles".
- Dense fine print at the footer and the "view in browser" line at the top add friction.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero, for example "Extra 20% off + $10 Kohl's Cash per $50", with a button that says "Shop holiday deals". Keep the gift-box art but add the number.
- 2. Move the coupon stack (SAVE20 and the others) up to just below the hero, and cut the long editorial tiles to two.
- 3. Replace each tile's "Shop Now" with a specific CTA such as "Shop hosting essentials" or "Shop cozy slippers", and name one hero product with a price.
- 4. Shorten the subject to roughly 50 characters and put the deadline or offer in it.
- 5. Write a real preheader that carries the deal, so the first line of the email isn't view-in-browser text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Welcome to The North Kohl's 🎄 Extra 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday deals are here: 20% off + Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAVE20 and earn $10 Kohl's Cash per $50. Ends Nov. 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gifts, hosting finds and cozy looks, plus free returns through Jan. 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Kohl's holiday send (`261026_DG_Email_Monday`) with ESP-managed click tracking and several tracking pixels. The HTML has a few markup defects and missing alt text on content images. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided, because the HTML is truncated and the relay doesn't expose headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** All of them use redirect domains such as `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`. The QA tool could not check destinations, status codes or redirect chains. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Several beacons load on open.** These are `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5f56d143-…`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Four open-tracking calls to three vendors is heavy. It also means open-rate data will be inflated or fragmented, and Apple MPP and image blocking will skew it.
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="…open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>`. It is invalid markup, though browsers and mail clients usually ignore it.
- **Empty parameter.** The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png` URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with no value. This may be an unresolved dynamic field. The URL is truncated, so I can't confirm it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flagged `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` and `59199073_Product_hdgt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF`. Some of these are `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` dynamic render images. If they carry the content, images-off and screen-reader users get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels.** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute.
- **False positive in QA.** `o.gif` is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Screen readers and some clients have nothing to announce.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">…</div>` sits inside `<head>`. `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href, which can trigger a stray request to the page URL.
- **Fixed-width tables.** The tables use `width="640"` with a `.container` class and an `@media (max-width:500px)` override. This is a standard pattern and fine.
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded through both `<link>` and `@import` inside a `!mso` block. The fallback stacks (`Arial, Helvetica`) are present. The double load is redundant but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source.
- The only suspect is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted above.
- `mi_u=604230016` is a per-recipient identifier embedded in image URLs. It is a privacy note, not a defect.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** QA reports both missing, but its own note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address.** QA found none, but the HTML is truncated before the footer. This is unconfirmed. Check the full source.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a plausible aligned sending subdomain, but I have no header evidence.
- No confirmed compliance violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Links go through `click.s.kohls.com` redirects with opaque `qs` tokens. No visible `utm_*` parameters appear in the truncated source, so campaign attribution rests on the ESP's `email_name=261026_DG_Email_Monday` and `mi_ecmp=1024543_20261026`.
- Landing pages were not fetched, so I can't verify destinations or UTM persistence.
- No confirmed issues.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, the hero GIF). Add `alt=""` to the two pixels that lack it.
2. Fill in `<title>`. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`. Remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`. Delete the stray `</custom>`. Move the schema.org `<div>` into `<body>`, or use JSON-LD.
3. Check whether the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter is intentional.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Then check the full HTML footer for the postal address and unsubscribe link.
5. Run the 66 tracked links through a redirect-following check (for example, a headless fetch) to confirm destinations, status codes and UTM or `mi_*` parameter carry-through.
6. Consider consolidating the open-tracking pixels from four vendors down to the ones that are needed.
