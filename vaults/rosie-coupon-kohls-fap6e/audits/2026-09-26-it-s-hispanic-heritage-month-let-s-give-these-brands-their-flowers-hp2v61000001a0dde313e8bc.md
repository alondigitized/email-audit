# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's Hispanic Heritage Month send, and it's structurally different from the brand's usual promo cadence — it's a founder-story email, not a discount blast. "Unidos, Somos Más" leads into a genuine editorial layout: founder portraits, quotes ("Ana is a brand that invites you to celebrate who you are"), and brand-by-brand callouts for AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, and Rare Beauty. It's a nice change of pace from the last several sends (4X points, Hair Daily Deals, "new beauty to obsess over") that were pure catalog scrolls.
- The problem is the top of the email undercuts the whole premise. The hero banner is a generic "Up to 30% off Hair Faves" markdown promo that has nothing to do with Hispanic Heritage Month — so anyone scanning the first screen sees a hair sale, not the story the subject promised. The Hispanic Heritage content doesn't start until you scroll past that banner, which is a mismatch this brand has repeated before (see 2026-09-20's "fall beauty routine" subject vs. mismatched hero).
- Once you're into the body, execution is solid: founder photos build trust, "SHOP AND SUPPORT" is a smart CTA that reframes purchase as solidarity, and the Selena Gomez / Rare Beauty section is a strong recognizable anchor. But the email runs long (six brand sections plus a Founders Face to Face video plus more unrelated deals at the bottom), diluting the focus. Overall a good creative concept let down by a header that doesn't match the story and a body that goes on two sections too long.

## What's working

- Founder portrait + quote format for each brand (AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia) builds authenticity and matches "give these brands their flowers."
- "SHOP AND SUPPORT" as repeated CTA copy is more specific and on-theme than a generic "Shop Now."
- Rare Beauty / Selena Gomez section is instantly recognizable and gives the email a strong anchor.
- "Founders Face to Face" video callout (Dez Perkins and Denise Vasi) adds a distinct content format beyond static product tiles.
- Clean section-to-section visual rhythm — alternating photo/product-tile layout reads well scrolling.

## What's weak

- Hero banner ("Up to 30% off Hair Faves") has zero connection to Hispanic Heritage Month — it's the first thing seen and actively contradicts the subject line's promise.
- Email is overloaded: six brand features, a video module, then unrelated MAED/Dezi Skin tiles and a K-Beauty/haircare promo bar at the bottom — the Hispanic Heritage Month focus gets buried under generic merchandising by the end.
- No single unifying "shop the collection" CTA — a reader has to click into each brand individually with no umbrella landing page visible.
- Bottom third (4X points K-Beauty, 20% off Value-Size Hair Care, Sephora Credit Card) feels bolted on and dilutes the campaign's emotional through-line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hair-sale hero with a Hispanic Heritage Month hero banner (the "Unidos, Somos Más" module currently buried below it) — put the campaign's own message first, not a disconnected promo.
- 2. Cut the email after Rare Beauty/Founders Face to Face — move MAED, Dezi Skin, K-Beauty, and Hair Care promos to a separate send so this one stays thematically pure.
- 3. Add one umbrella CTA near the top ("Shop the Collection") linking to a dedicated landing page for all featured brands, for readers who don't want to scroll through six sections.
- 4. Consider adding a short line on where proceeds/support go (i.e., what "shop and support" concretely funds) — makes the emotional pitch more credible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the founders behind Hispanic Heritage Month at Sephora`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 Latine-founded brands to shop this Hispanic Heritage Month`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, Rare Beauty — hear from the founders`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Founder stories, exclusive launches, and brands worth celebrating`
