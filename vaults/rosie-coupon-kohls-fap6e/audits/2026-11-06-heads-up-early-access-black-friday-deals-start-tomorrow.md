# Heads up ... Early Access Black Friday Deals start tomorrow 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a "heads up" teaser, and it leads with the right message: Early Access Black Friday starts tomorrow. The hero banner says "STARTS TOMORROW" over a Black Friday sign, with 20% off with coupon and $15 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent. A Rewards status bar sits above it, so the member benefit shows before any product does.
- Below the hero it turns into a long, repetitive scroll of Shop Now buttons, category tabs, a Top 100 Gifts block, jewelry and fleece at 20% off, a $37.99 toy tile, a 70% clearance block and four product tiles. Compared with the Nov 5 "20% off won't stick around forever" send, which was the thirteenth near-identical send since Oct 26, this is the fourteenth. The only new angle is "Black Friday starts tomorrow." It is a credible urgency hook, but the body doesn't build on it. The hero's "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" sign looks partly obscured by the overlaid copy.
- Verdict: a solid teaser subject on top of a body that is the same promo stack as the last two weeks. Subscribers who have seen thirteen of these will likely skim past it.

## What's working

- The "STARTS TOMORROW" hero and "Early Access" framing give a real, time-bounded reason to act.
- The Rewards status bar and the "$15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" banner surface member value early.
- The 20% off with coupon is large and easy to find in the hero.
- The product tiles show concrete prices with original prices struck through (Ninja SLUSHi $249.99, Serta mattress topper $229–$349.99).
- The Wallet coupon code and barcode near the bottom make the offer easy to redeem.

## What's weak

- The body is the same stack as the last two weeks: 20% off with coupon, gifts and Kohl's Cash. Nothing in it is specifically "early access," so there's no payoff for the subject's promise.
- The hero's "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" sign looks partly hidden behind the overlaid "% off" copy, which weakens the headline.
- The email is very long. It has multiple Shop Now buttons and repeated Home/Women/Men/Kids tab rows, so no single CTA stands out.
- Several tiles carry "Select styles" and "Coupons do not apply" exclusions. Fine print is dense at the bottom and the coupon rules are layered.
- The "Going on now (No waiting required)" block competes with the "starts tomorrow" message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email actually about early access. Put one hero with the exact start time, the Rewards-member advantage and a single "Set my reminder" or "Shop sneak peek" button, then cut the body to about three sections.
- 2. Fix the hero so the "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" sign is fully visible and nothing overlaps the headline.
- 3. Remove the duplicate category tab rows and the repeated generic Shop Now buttons. Use specific CTAs such as "Shop 70% off clearance."
- 4. Pick one stacked offer to lead with (20% off or $15 Kohl's Cash) and move the other lower down.
- 5. Preview 3–4 actual doorbuster items with their Black Friday prices to make "tomorrow" concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Access starts tomorrow: your Black Friday head start`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow: Black Friday deals open early for Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off with coupon and $15 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent. Nov 6–8 only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Set your cart now: top doorbusters unlock at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`, an empty `<title>` and an empty favicon href) and incomplete alt text on content images. Compliance and authentication items could not be confirmed because the HTML is truncated and the relay dropped the headers. The QA run scored a 43% pass rate, with 0 issues and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 74 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, `click.chp.kohls.com`). Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unknown.
- **Four tracking/analytics beacons load at the top of the body:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9dcfd65e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025278_2026115` (1x1)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…` (1x1)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, 0x0)
  
  The three open pixels overlap and add third-party requests. This is a design choice, not a defect.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the pixel and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. These URLs are fetched on open and can be cached or proxied, so the identifier is exposed there.
- **Malformed markup on a tracking pixel.** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` ends with an unmatched `</custom>` tag. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed).** Content images have no `alt` attribute:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - Product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59551810_1_2x`, `_2_2x`, `_3s_2x` and `_4_2x`
  
  With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, linked images have no accessible name.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for missing alt.** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `o.gif` and `demdex.net/event` are also flagged. Only `o.gif` carries `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. Add `alt=""` to the other two, and treat these as low severity.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and web views use it as the page title.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** A `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` containing `<meta itemprop>` tags sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not allowed there, so parsers implicitly close the head, and the markup is dropped or ignored in most clients.
- **Empty favicon href.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` resolves to the document URL in some clients and can trigger a spurious request.
- **Minor hygiene.**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags.
  - Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import` are both used, so the font is requested twice. They sit in an `!mso` block, and the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.
  - Layout tables correctly use `role="presentation"`, but the 1x1 and AAM tables lack it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The remaining body is truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All items below are warnings from the QA run, not confirmed defects:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture it, so the cause is probably the relay, not the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **No physical address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one. The supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't tell whether it is missing or just cut off.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided.
- Links are opaque encoded redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7…`), and the 74 tracking links were not probed.
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the captured portion.
- Campaign identifiers appear only on image URLs: `mi_ecmp=1025278_2026115` and `email_name=261105_DG_Email_Thu_PM`.
- Landing-page alignment needs the redirects resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-run the footer and header checks** on the full HTML and raw headers, bypassing the relay. Confirm the physical address, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating them as defects.
2. **Resolve the 74 tracking links** with a redirect-following probe. Record final status codes, landing URLs and whether UTM or `mi_*` parameters survive.
3. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to the 7 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the 4 `59551810_*` product images. Add `alt=""` to the remaining pixels.
4. **Fix the markup:** remove the stray `</custom>`, move the schema.org markup out of `<head>` (or drop it), remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`, fill in `<title>`, and drop the duplicate `format-detection` meta.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta.
6. **Consolidate redundant open pixels** if the analytics stack allows, to cut third-party requests and the exposure of `mi_u` in image URLs.
