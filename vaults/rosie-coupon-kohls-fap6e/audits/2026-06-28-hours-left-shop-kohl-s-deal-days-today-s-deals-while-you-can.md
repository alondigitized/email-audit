# HOURS LEFT ⏰ Shop Kohl's Deal Days + today's deals while you can 🛍️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the companion "hours left" push to the "Ends today" email that already landed earlier on June 28 — making it Kohl's thirteenth Deal Days email in six days and the second claim of final-day urgency on the same calendar day. The visual package is identical to every previous send: Deal Days hero, category nav, stacked percentage-off deals, clearance callout. Nothing has changed in the creative.
- The email works on pure catalog merit — 70% off jewelry, 40% off women's fashion, clearance at 75%, and a clean visual hierarchy that surfaces deals without clutter. The render is bug-free and the CTAs are unambiguous. But by the time this lands in someone's inbox, they've already seen "Ends today" from Kohl's. The second urgency claim on the same day doesn't add credibility — it erodes it. The campaign has burned its goodwill credit.
- Judgment: a structurally competent email undermined entirely by cadence. If you had only sent two Deal Days emails this week and this was the first same-day push, the scores would be two points higher across the board.

## What's working

- **Offer depth is real** — 70% off, 40% off across multiple categories, and 75% clearance give the scanner multiple reasons to click.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — hero → daily deals → 6 days of deals → clearance flows without cognitive friction.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are high-contrast and well-placed** — no hunting required.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text visible.
- **Clearance 75% callout is a strong scroll-stopper** — the red CLEARANCE block at the bottom earns its placement.

## What's weak

- **Second "last day" email on the same day kills urgency credibility** — subscribers who saw "Ends today" this morning now see "HOURS LEFT" this afternoon and the alarm stops ringing.
- **Subject line is 65+ characters** — truncates on most mobile clients; "today's deals while you can" is dead weight.
- **"HOURS LEFT" in all-caps at the front** — spam-signal risk; mirrors the kind of countdown-clock manipulation that trains inbox filters and reader cynicism simultaneously.
- **No new hook in the creative** — the 70%/40%/clearance stack is the exact same structure as the previous twelve sends. Nothing visually signals "this is different."
- **No personalization visible anywhere** — no name, no category preference, no "your saved items" hook despite this being a closing-hours push where personalization would have maximum impact.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Consolidate to one same-day send.** The "Ends today" email should be the last. A second same-day send with different subject copy doing the same job tells subscribers the urgency was theater.
- 2. **Lead with a single killer deal, not a catalog grid.** At the campaign's final hours, one hero product — say, the 70%-off jewelry — with a tight shot and a price anchored against retail creates more pull than five simultaneous 40% offers.
- 3. **Cut the subject to under 50 chars.** "HOURS LEFT" as a standalone punches hard. Everything after "Deal Days" is filler that gets clipped anyway.
- 4. **Drop the all-caps opener.** "Hours left: Kohl's Deal Days ends tonight" reads as less panicked and more trustworthy.
- 5. **Add one personalization hook.** "Your Women's picks — last chance" or "You've been watching shoes — 40% off ends in hours" would dramatically lift relevance at final-hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hours left: Kohl's Deal Days ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — 70% off, 40% off, 75% clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today's featured deals are still live — shop before it closes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the clearance, the daily deals, and your saved items — last call.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Deal Days" Email (2026-06-28)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for a bulk promotional send but carries several confirmed compliance gaps (missing CAN-SPAM address, absent List-Unsubscribe headers) and two low-severity HTML defects that could cause minor rendering issues in strict clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) or `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath). Destination URLs and UTM parameter coverage cannot be verified without following redirects.

**Four distinct tracking mechanisms** are stacked:
| Pixel | Domain | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/908ec9b3…` | CoherentPath |
| Open pixel | `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?D5NF…` | Salesforce MC |
| Render tracking | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/…` | Movable Ink |
| Audience sync | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` | Adobe AAM |

No broken link errors were returned by QA probes. Stacking four open-tracking pixels is not an error, but it adds ~4 additional network requests on open.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[BUG] Stray `</custom>` closing tag** — the Salesforce open-pixel block reads:
```html
<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">
  <img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   ← orphaned close tag
</div>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it is malformed and could trip strict-mode XML parsers.

**[BUG] Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as a blank tab title in webmail preview clients (Gmail web, Outlook.com). Should contain the preheader or campaign name.

**[BUG] Empty favicon href** — `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` issues a network request to the email server's root path on open in some webmail clients. Should be removed entirely.

**[WARN] 12 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA, including two content images (`54951211_4S_2x` from `images.am.kohls.com`) and ten tracking/reporting images from `mi.kohls.com`. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (already present on the Movable Ink gif: `alt=""` is correct there); the product image `54951211_4S_2x` likely needs descriptive alt text for screen reader users. Evidence:
```
src: https://images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/54951211_4S_2x?scl=1
```

**[WARN] Viewport restricts zoom** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure for accessibility-sensitive audiences.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) visible in the truncated source. Personalization appears to be delivered via Movable Ink server-side rendering at open time (`mi_u=604230016` in image URLs), which is the correct pattern.

`email_name=260628_DG_Email_Sun_PM` is hardcoded in Movable Ink image parameters — this is expected; it is a campaign identifier, not an unresolved token.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] No physical mailing address detected** — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. None is visible in the truncated HTML. If present in the below-fold section not included in the truncated source, this is not an issue — confirm against full render.

**[FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface one-click unsubscribe. Its absence places full unsubscribe burden on the in-body link and degrades deliverability reputation at volume. QA confirmed header not captured via AgentMail relay.

**[FAIL] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)** — required for Gmail's one-click unsubscribe compliance (mandatory for senders >5k/day since February 2024). Missing this header on a brand of Kohl's send volume is a significant gap.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown via the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm authentication posture. Kohl's sending domain `s.kohls.com` should have valid DKIM signing; this is likely a relay capture gap rather than an actual auth failure, but should be verified against raw headers.

**[INFO] `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are correctly set** — appropriate for email to prevent indexing and suppress referrer leakage.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot fully audit UTM coverage because all 22 destination links are wrapped in click-redirect URLs (`click.s.kohls.com`) that were skipped by the probe. The following is inferable from the source:

- CoherentPath click URLs carry `mi_cid` and `cp_cid` parameters — these are platform-internal, not GA4/UA UTM params. If landing pages rely on `utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign` for attribution, these must be embedded inside the redirect destination, not in the wrapper URL.
- The `no-referrer` meta policy means direct webmail navigation will not pass `document.referrer` to the landing page. This is mitigated if all links go through the click-redirect wrapper, which should carry attribution parameters in the destination URL — but this cannot be confirmed without unwrapping the links.
- `mi_ecmp=1016109_2026628` is Movable Ink's campaign tag — this does not substitute for UTM params in GA4.
