# 40% off ends today + bring home new must-haves ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's third 40%-off email in less than 24 hours — following "Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash" and "Open for your 40% off deal" both on July 18. The urgency in the subject ("ends today") is theoretically the strongest it's been, but subscribers who received yesterday's pair already know the 40% off playbook and the credibility of "ends today" has taken a real hit. The body doesn't reinforce the deadline anywhere visible — the hero copy pivots to "Priced right for one more hour outside," which is evergreen lifestyle language, not a closing-window push.
- The creative itself is competent: tiered discounts (75%, 50%, 40%), a clear sectioned scroll through Home → Bedding/Bath → Kitchen/Decor → FLX Activewear, and a bottom-of-email coupon barcode with code and Kohl's Cash terms all visible. The layout renders cleanly with no visible bugs. But the sheer breadth — five distinct product categories in one scroll — dilutes any single-category urgency. A subscriber looking to buy outdoor furniture has to mentally filter past bedding, activewear, and decor to find the thing they actually want.
- The bottom coupon block is the most useful element in the email. The barcode, explicit promo code, Kohl's Cash earn rate, and expiration details are all visible and legible. That is what a deal-first subscriber actually wants, and Kohl's delivers it here — it just takes too long to reach it.

## What's working

- **Coupon block is fully rendered** — barcode, promo code, expiry, and Kohl's Cash terms all visible without leaving the email.
- **Tiered discount structure** (75% → 50% → 40%) creates a visual escalation that reads as progressively better deals.
- **Seasonal hero copy** ("Priced right for one more hour outside") lands naturally for July and pairs with appropriate outdoor/living room imagery.
- **FLX Activewear section** is visually distinct (dark block, contrasting type) and breaks up the home-goods scroll before the coupon.
- **Loyalty icons row** (Kohl's Card, Cash, Rewards, Coupons) at the footer reinforces the ecosystem for existing members without being intrusive.

## What's weak

- **"Ends today" is in the subject but absent from the body** — no visible deadline banner, countdown, or closing-soon copy reinforces the urgency once the email is opened.
- **Five competing categories** (outdoor, bedding, bath, kitchen/decor, activewear) creates decision paralysis; there is no single hero offer to anchor on.
- **50% and 40% sections both carry coupon exclusions** ("coupons do not apply" visible on the outdoor toys block) — a trust-eroding fine-print signal right next to a discount claim.
- **Third 40%-off email in under 24 hours** — the July 18 pair ("Enjoy 40% off" + "Open for your 40% off deal") already played this card twice, making today's "ends today" feel like a continuation of yesterday's urgency theater.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the deadline into the body hero.** Add a visible "Offer ends midnight tonight" line directly under the 40% off headline. The subject promises urgency; the email body doesn't deliver it.
- 2. **Collapse to one editorial category.** Pick outdoor/patio OR home decor for July 19, not all five. A focused send converts better than a department-store scroll.
- 3. **Surface the coupon barcode above the fold.** The most useful asset is buried at the bottom. Promote it to the second panel so deal-seekers don't have to scroll past five product blocks.
- 4. **Remove or soften "coupons do not apply" adjacent to discount claims.** Flag exclusions in a footer footnote, not inline with the primary offer.
- 5. **Kill the emoji on a closing-urgency email.** ⏳ reads playful on a casual send; on a "last chance" send it undermines credibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends tonight — shop before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 40% off home + outdoor essentials`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor furniture, bedding, bath — plus earn Kohl's Cash on every order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 40% coupon expires today. Use it on home, outdoor, activewear & more`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off ends today" (2026-07-19)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One unrendered merge token (`%%offer_evnt_nm%%`) confirms a broken personalization pipeline that will display raw template syntax to recipients. Compliance gaps (missing physical address, absent List-Unsubscribe headers) and 14 images missing `alt` text are the remaining high-priority issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination URLs detected**, but all 22 click-tracked links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains: `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`). Destination reachability is unverified.

Three separate open/pixel tracking systems are firing on load:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/01bdb5e5...` (CoherentPath)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` (MessageInfo)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?3D7CVIZ...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

No functional issue, but triple-pixel stacking means four outbound requests on open — relevant if deliverability filtering is tightening.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` appears immediately after the SMC open pixel `<img>` tag. This is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**14 content/product images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, open-pixel) correctly use `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`, but all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` renderable images and the product image (`kohlsprod/55405645_root_2x`) lack `alt` attributes entirely.

Notable offenders:
- `55405645_root_2x` — product image with no alt
- `611d2b1debdc5ae0.png`, `b0aad5bed92b660a.png` — images with promo codes embedded in the URL (`left_promo_code=LPJ9Y27GJLXBX`) but no alt text; if images are blocked, offer code is invisible to the recipient

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile — accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

`<title>` tag is empty. Screen readers and some clients use this for tab/preview labeling.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` simultaneously for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — duplicate requests, no functional breakage but unnecessary overhead.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[FAIL]** Raw merge token `%%offer_evnt_nm%%` found in rendered HTML source. This token was not substituted at send time. Recipients will see the literal string. Location not pinpointed from truncated source, but the QA scanner confirmed it exists in the delivered message body.

No other unresolved tokens detected.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1017930_2026719`) and `email_name=260719_DG_Email_Mystery` appear consistently across all `mi.kohls.com` image tracking URLs, indicating campaign-level tracking is wired. However:

- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in the truncated source. If click-redirect URLs do not append UTMs before landing, GA4/analytics attribution will be lost or fall back to direct traffic.
