# Save 20% | Get the fall looks you want for less 🍂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Kohl's coupon send you've seen all week, now in a fall skin. The "Save 20%" banner, the Kohl's Cash strip and the Home / Women / Men / Kids tiles all repeat from the Oct 10–13 emails. The creative below that is new: a "Modern Heritage" boots-and-knitwear hero, a Halloween/Fall/Christmas decor block, and four priced products.
- The offer is clear and the deadline is credible. The top banner says "20% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18," and the barcodes at the bottom repeat it. But the email is long and has too many entry points. Nav tiles, a hero, four category tiles, three decor tiles and a product grid all compete, and the "Modern Heritage" hero is a style story that doesn't mention the offer.
- Overall it's a competent, safe send with no render bugs. It's also the fifth near-identical promo in five days, so it is easy to ignore.

## What's working

- The 20% coupon banner with "Ends Oct. 18" sits at the very top, so the offer lands first.
- The Rewards status ("KOHL'S REWARDS' MEMBER, Earn 5% REWARDS") and the green Kohl's Cash strip give loyalty members visible benefits.
- The fall hero (boots, knit, "Modern Heritage") matches the season and the subject's "fall looks."
- The product grid shows real prices with strikethrough originals: $299.99 (was $349.99) for the Ninja SLUSHI, $29.99 for the jeans (was $52.00).
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom, plus "Free returns through January 31, 2027," lower purchase friction.

## What's weak

- The "Modern Heritage" hero never mentions 20% off, so the main visual doesn't carry the main offer.
- "Shop Now" is used for both the banner and the hero, and it is generic.
- The email stacks two coupons (20% and $10 off $50+ home), Kohl's Cash, and a "Coupons do not apply to select brands" caveat. That dilutes the single message and makes the offer feel less clean.
- It is very long, with six or more distinct modules. The decor section ("Three reasons to decorate. (Four, if this coupon gets a vote.)") pulls away from the fashion promise in the subject.
- Cadence is a problem. Oct 10 through Oct 13 all carried the same coupon with different subject lines, and this is the same message again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer into the hero. Overlay "Extra 20% off fall styles" on the boots/knit image and use a specific button such as "Shop fall boots."
- 2. Cut the email to about 60% of its length. Keep the banner, one hero, the four category tiles and the product grid, and drop the decor block or send it as its own campaign.
- 3. Make the $10 home coupon a secondary strip rather than a second headline offer, and move the "coupons do not apply" caveat closer to the banner.
- 4. Vary the creative or the offer between sends. Today's repeat of the same coupon is why this reads as noise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off boots & knits, ends Oct. 18`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall wardrobe, 20% off through Sunday 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Coupon inside: boots, sweaters and jackets. Plus $10 off home with Rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Only 4 days left. Stack Kohl's Cash on top of 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is mostly table-based and responsive, but it has malformed HTML (a stray `</custom>` tag and a `<div>` inside `<head>`), an empty `<title>`, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 65 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. The visible preheader link is `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`, an opaque Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) `qs=` blob.
- **Stacked tracking pixels before any content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/fd8b302f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (1x1, no `alt`)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023836_20261014`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?PKS6BDVF…&d=60267&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, `width="0" height="0"`)
  - Together these are four separate open or tracking beacons across three vendors. Some mail clients may block them, and open counts will not agree across systems.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` closes a `<custom>` element that was never opened. This is a template merge artifact. Browsers and clients ignore the stray tag, but it is invalid HTML.
- **Identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the query string of `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. This is a stable user ID in cacheable image requests, which is a minor privacy and caching concern.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (QA-confirmed):**
  - Tracking pixels: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/fd8b…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` at all. `o.gif` has `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct. The QA flag on it is a false positive.
  - Content images with no alt in the QA list: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and the product image `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`.
  - If these `rp/*.png` files are rendered promo or text images, the offer content is invisible with images off or to screen readers.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` and move it into `<body>`.
- **Tracking tables without `role="presentation"`:** the 1x1 pixel table and the `AAM` table have none, while the main layout tables do. This is minor.
- **Dead selector:** the CSS rule `ReadMsgBody { width:100% }` is missing its leading `.`, so it has no effect. This is minor.
- **Web fonts:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the font stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. This is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[%…%]` tokens appear. The only template defect is the stray `</custom>`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **From address:** `Kohls@s.kohls.com` is a sending subdomain of the brand domain. Alignment can't be verified without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none can be verified. Links are SFMC `qs=` encrypted redirects (`click.s.kohls.com`), and the 65 tracking links were not followed. Campaign attribution appears to ride on `mi_ecmp=1023836_20261014` and `email_name=261014_DG_Email_Wednes…` (mi.kohls.com image URLs), not on visible UTMs.
- **Landing page alignment:** unverified, because the destinations were never resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML and the raw `.eml`. This settles the physical address, the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
2. Resolve a sample of the SFMC `qs=` links with a redirect-following probe, and record the final URL and any UTM or campaign parameters.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images (`rp/*.png`, `58379268_2_2x`). Add `alt=""` to the pure tracking pixels.
4. Fix the template: remove the stray `</custom>`, move the schema.org `<div>` into `<body>` or use JSON-LD, populate `<title>`, drop the empty favicon `<link>`, and correct the `.ReadMsgBody` selector.

6. Consider consolidating the four tracking beacons, and moving `mi_u` out of image URLs.
