# 2 days only 👀 Save 30% on the Kohl's brands you love 💕
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth promotional email in seven days, and for the first time in this streak, it actually earns a hard stop in the scroll. "FIRST TIME EVER" is the biggest departure from the relentless "20% off + Kohl's Cash" template — and pairing it with a genuine step-up to 30% off gives it real teeth. After nine sends that blurred into one, the visual contrast lands.
- The catch is buried right in the hero badge: "No Kohl's Cash earned." Every prior email in this July run led with Kohl's Cash as a co-benefit. Removing it here, without any compensation in the framing, risks feeling like the brand is trading one loyalty perk for another. Loyal shoppers will notice.
- The private-label angle — AFT 9, TEK GEAR, SONOMA — is a narrower offer than the sitewide promotions in the prior nine sends. That's actually a feature if you care about these brands, but it undercuts the "FIRST TIME EVER" drama slightly: this isn't 30% off everything, it's 30% off Kohl's own labels.

## What's working

- **"FIRST TIME EVER" breaks ten days of wallpaper.** After nine identical subject templates, this hero copy actually compels a look.
- **30% beats 20% — the escalation registers.** The bump reads as a meaningful reward for staying subscribed through the storm.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean and immediate.** The badge with "30%" in large type dominates the fold; the eye goes there first with no ambiguity.
- **Two distinct lifestyle headlines add product-story depth.** "Styles that say designer" and "Summer picks that get invited back" give the grid sections narrative purpose beyond price.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **"No Kohl's Cash earned" is a loyalty downgrade with no spin.** It's disclosed — points for transparency — but it's not positioned as a trade-off. It just sits there in fine print as a caveat.
- **"FIRST TIME EVER" is overselling a private-label-only sale.** The claim implies sitewide historic precedent; the actual scope is narrower, and readers will feel that.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are generic.** With a brand-specific private-label story, "Shop Kohl's brands" or "Shop the Kohl's exclusives" would do more work.
- **Cadence is the elephant in the inbox.** Ten sends in seven days erodes every urgency signal no matter how strong the individual email is.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reframe the Kohl's Cash exclusion.** Lead with "No Kohl's Cash — because the price IS the deal" rather than burying it as fine print. Turn the caveat into a positioning statement.
- 2. **Sharpen the CTA copy to match the private-label story.** "Shop Kohl's exclusives" or "Shop SONOMA, TEK GEAR & more" converts better than "Shop Now" when the offer is brand-specific.
- 3. **Use one hero product to anchor the 30%.** A single standout item (a TEK GEAR jacket, a SONOMA throw) with an actual before/after price would make "FIRST TIME EVER" viscerally real.
- 4. **Trim cadence or suppress re-sends.** No individual email can overcome inbox fatigue at 10-in-7-days. This is the most impactful systemic fix.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a standard MSO-compatible table layout with three distinct tracking pixel systems firing in parallel. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance elements (physical address, unsubscribe headers) and pervasive missing alt text across tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com` redirect domains; destination URLs and HTTP status could not be verified.

**Three separate tracking pixel systems are active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e4b22597...` — CoherentPath behavioral tracking
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb...o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel

The open pixel at `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?PB5572T4...` is immediately followed by a `<custom>` closing tag (`</custom>`) which is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict renderers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images are missing `alt` text**, including:
- The open-tracking pixel at `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e4b22597...` — technically intentional for trackers, but should carry `alt=""`
- 6 content images served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` with campaign parameters (`email_name=260707_DG_Email_Tuesday`) — these appear to be rendered content images, not trackers, and should have descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-blocked clients

`maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta prevents zoom — a WCAG 1.4.4 failure that harms users with low vision.

`<title>` tag is empty (`<title></title>`), which degrades screen reader experience and preview text fallback in some clients.

The `.hide-lg { display: none !important; }` rule in the global stylesheet (not scoped to a media query) permanently hides desktop-targeted elements by default with no counterpart show rule at wider breakpoints — likely a class-naming bug that could suppress content on desktop clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source. Subscriber ID is passed via `mi_u=604230016` on tracking pixels, confirming per-recipient resolution is functioning.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in HTML source.** CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. Not present in the visible truncated source — may exist below the fold, but QA did not detect it.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent.** Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for native unsubscribe UI; its absence increases spam folder placement risk and removes one-click unsubscribe for ESP-level compliance (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` also missing).

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured** by the AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unverifiable from this data. If DKIM is failing, inbox placement will degrade regardless of content.

The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag suppresses referrer on outbound clicks — this is intentional for privacy but means GA4/analytics on landing pages will receive direct/none traffic instead of email attribution unless UTM params are appended to every link (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 outbound links pass through `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` redirect chains — UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects (blocked by QA probe).

The `no-referrer` meta policy (noted above) makes UTM params on destination URLs **mandatory** for any attribution. If any CTA links resolve to bare `kohls.com` URLs without `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=...`, the traffic will be unattributable in analytics.
