# Labor Day Sale + $10 off your $25 purchase ENDS TONIGHT 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth send of the same "$10 off $25 + $5 Kohl's Cash" Labor Day stack in a week, and it finally leans hard into "ENDS TODAY / ENDS TONIGHT" urgency at the very top with a countdown-style red banner — that's the right instinct for a closing-day send. But visually it's the most cluttered version yet: category tiles, a wall of category-by-category percentage-off blocks (tees, bedding, activewear, dresses, floor care, bath towels, kitchen, shoes, fleece), then a separate clearance block, then a "picked for you" product grid, then barcodes at the bottom — it reads like every prior week's email pasted into one scroll rather than a focused last-chance push.
- The good news is the offer stack (10/$25 + $5 Kohl's Cash + 15% coupon) is legible and the deadline is finally explicit ("Ends Sept. 7" on the barcodes). But burying that clean offer under nine unrelated category promos undercuts the urgency the subject line promises — a true "ends tonight" email should be short, scannable, and almost entirely about the deadline and the coupon, not a full-catalog dump.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, and better than the mid-week filler sends because it has real urgency — but the layout works against its own message. 6.5/10 territory, same as the rest of this week's run.

## What's working

- Bold red "ENDS TODAY" ribbon and "LABOR DAY" hero are the first thing the eye hits.
- The $10-off-$25 and $5-Kohl's-Cash offers are both stated in large, legible type in the hero block.
- Barcodes near the bottom make the coupons usable in-store without extra steps.
- Explicit expiration ("Ends Sept. 7") finally appears on the coupon codes, closing a gap from earlier sends this week.
- No rendering bugs — images, tiles, and text blocks all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Nine separate category promo blocks (tees, bedding, activewear, dresses, floor care, towels, kitchen, shoes, fleece) dilute the "ends tonight" urgency into a routine catalog scroll.
- No single hero product or clear "shop the last-chance sale" CTA — nine "Shop Now" buttons compete for attention.
- "Coupons do not apply to select styles/brands" appears three separate times, undercutting the honesty/simplicity of the offer.
- This is the eighth near-identical promo mechanic this week (following six days of "$10 off $25 + $5 Kohl's Cash" variants) — fatigue risk is real by now.
- The clearance block ("up to 85% off") and the "picked with you in mind" product grid compete with the Labor Day urgency message rather than reinforcing it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the top hero + one clear CTA ("Shop the Last Hours of Labor Day") and move all nine category tiles below a fold or drop half of them — an "ends tonight" email should feel urgent and short, not exhaustive.
- 2. Consolidate the three "coupons do not apply" disclaimers into a single footnote near the coupon barcodes instead of repeating mid-scroll.
- 3. Move the countdown/deadline reinforcement (e.g., "11:59pm tonight") right under the hero, not just on the barcode block at the very bottom.
- 4. Trim the clearance and "picked for you" sections out of this specific send — they're competing offers that blunt the single-minded urgency the subject promises.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Few hours left: Labor Day $10/$25 + $5 Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Midnight cutoff — stack your coupon before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your $10 off + $5 Kohl's Cash both expire at 11:59pm`
