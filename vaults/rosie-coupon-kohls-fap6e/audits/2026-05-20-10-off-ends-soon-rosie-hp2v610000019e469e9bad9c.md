# 10% off ends soon, Rosie
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the expiry-reminder leg of the triggered sequence that began with the May 14 welcome email ("Take 10% off, Rosie," scored 9/10 in this audit trail). Same code (SE3ARPH), same 10% promise, same recipient — but now with a live countdown timer and "ENDS TODAY" urgency. On that narrow mission — get Rosie to act before midnight — the email mostly delivers. The countdown clock, hero offer, and named personalization stack cleanly.
- The creative is mostly typographic this time, which is the right call; a last-chance email doesn't need editorial depth. What it does need is a CTA that matches the offer, and "SHOP NOW" doesn't do that. The bigger problem is the footer, where the in-store barcode section contains visibly repeated "sephora.com" text — "on sephora.com, and sephora.com, and sephora.com" — which reads like a broken merge tag and undercuts the brand finish.
- The "Wondering Why You Got This?" block earns its space and likely reduces unsubscribes from confused new members. But compared to the welcome send, this email adds nothing new — it's pure urgency, which is acceptable for a final reminder, as long as the execution is clean. The footer bug means it isn't.

## What's working

- Countdown timer with "ENDS TODAY" label makes the deadline feel concrete and immediate — 12 hours left when rendered.
- First-name personalization appears in both subject and hero copy ("Rosie, here's a discount to start shopping."), which the May 14 welcome email also used effectively.
- Promo code SE3ARPH is displayed prominently in the hero and repeated in the in-store barcode section — good for multi-channel redemption.
- "Wondering Why You Got This?" section is trust-building and reduces abandonment from new members who don't yet understand the Beauty Insider trigger logic.
- Product recommendation grid below the fold gives shoppers a product entry point rather than dumping them at the homepage.

## What's weak

- "SHOP NOW →" CTA is generic — a direct-offer send with a specific promo code deserves "Redeem 10% Off" or "Apply SE3ARPH."
- Footer barcode copy visibly repeats "sephora.com" three times in a single sentence — clear merge tag or template render failure.
- The Kohl's/Sephora barcode block appears without a clear channel label, creating confusion for sephora.com shoppers who wonder if their discount is limited to Kohl's.
- Expiry copy says "Valid through 5/20/2026" but gives no hour — the countdown shows ~12 hours remaining, but a user who reads the fine print can't verify when specifically it expires.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the footer merge bug first.** The repeated "sephora.com" copy must be corrected before this template fires for the next cohort — it's visible and brand-damaging.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA.** Change "SHOP NOW" to "Redeem 10% Off" — mirrors the subject, removes ambiguity, reduces the click-to-purchase mental gap.
- 3. **Add a specific expiry time to the hero.** "Expires tonight at 11:59 PM PT" next to or below the countdown would convert better than "ends soon" for users who open the email with hours to spare.
- 4. **Label the Kohl's barcode block explicitly.** A small header — "Shopping at Sephora at Kohl's? Use this code:" — separates the two redemption paths cleanly.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — SE3ARPH still works`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your welcome discount disappears at midnight. Use it now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `SE3ARPH is still good — but only for a few more hours.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta (confirmed):**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) violation. Recommendation: remove `maximum-scale=1` or raise to `maximum-scale=5`.

**Issue — Blanket `text-size-adjust: none` (confirmed):**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to essentially all elements. Overrides OS-level accessibility font scaling for users with low vision. The `.sup` and `td` selector already carries this rule; the broad selector is redundant and harmful.

**Issue — `robots` meta uses invalid syntax (confirmed):**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`no index` (with a space) is not a valid robots directive. The correct value is `noindex` (no space). Most crawlers will ignore the malformed directive. Low severity in email context but indicates a template-level copy-paste error.

**Note — `alt` attributes:** Not assessable from truncated source. Verify all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` text (or `alt=""` for decorative images).
