**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in a row for the Sephora Savings Event, and it works as a reminder of the 10/30 launch email, "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊". The gold "30% OFF" medallion with code ALLONSALE sits at the very top, dated 10/30–11/9, and the barcode at the bottom adds the Insider 10% off. The offer is clear. The email then loses focus. Right under the offer, a huge "BEAUTY for YOU" serif hero pushes a skincare launch (Eadem, Yise Beauty) instead of the Sephora Collection promo the subject advertises.

- Overall it's a solid, trusted brand email with a credible offer and no render bugs. It's weaker than the 10/30 launch email, because the subject and the hero point at different things.

- The gold "30% OFF" medallion with "Use code ALLONSALE" and "SHOP NOW" is a concrete offer at the top, with dates (10/30–11/9).
- The barcode block at the bottom spells out both savings, 30% off Sephora Collection and 10% off the rest of the purchase, with in-store and Kohl's options.

- The gold medallion is cropped by the product images at the top. It is small relative to the hero below it.
- It is the third near-identical event send in three days, after the 10/29 teasers and the 10/30 launch, so fatigue is likely.

- 2. Move the barcode or code reminder up near the top, with "Ends 11/9".
- 3. Cut the grid to about six items and keep the Valentino, K18, and gift guide blocks to one.

## Technical Audit: "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based 700px layout and uses redirect-wrapped tracking links. Two automated checks failed: an empty greeting name slot and a 0-character plain-text part. Several header-level checks could not be confirmed because the capture goes through the AgentMail relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Links use the redirect form `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1924b6.../bf61a3f3-18e4-4eeb-986a-c2df237128f4?__dU__=...`. This is a normal ESP wrapper. The QA probe flagged no broken links among the 12 probed.

- **Open pixels:**

  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261031_CS_Curated_For_You_BI` is a second tracking pixel.
  - The `mi_u` value looks like a per-recipient identifier.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Both tracking pixels lack `alt`. The QA tool flagged them, but they are hidden `display:none` images, so the real accessibility impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would still clear the warning.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't work inside CSS rules. The block is ignored at best and may break the following `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule in some parsers.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules adjust fonts and logo size only, so the layout is not fluid on narrow clients without scaling.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Dead JSON-LD:** The block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip `<script>`, so it is dead markup.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,` with no name. This is likely a missing first-name fallback on the sender side. The HTML provided is truncated, so I can't point to the exact selector.
- **No raw tokens:** No unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens are confirmed in the visible source.

### 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA note says the relay may not capture these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and it can't be verified from this capture.
- **Unsubscribe in body:** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed. No result was reported for it in the data provided.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters:** This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could be a relay capture artifact, but treat it as a failure unless the raw MIME shows a `text/plain` part.
- **Postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Landing URLs not visible:** The truncated source hides the final destinations behind `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- **Campaign tag:** The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261031_CS_Curated_For_You_BI` on the pixel. Its "Curated For You" label doesn't match the subject's sitewide "ALL Sephora Collection" offer. This is a tagging inconsistency, not a confirmed bug.
- **Tracking style:** The sender uses `__dU__` redirect parameters, not standard `utm_*` params.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add a first-name fallback to the greeting, such as "Hi there," when the name is empty.
2. Include a `text/plain` alternative part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe link.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the original message, not the relay copy.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule.
6. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
7. Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`, and remove the empty JSON-LD `discountCode`.
8. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Then verify the destination URLs, the postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link.
