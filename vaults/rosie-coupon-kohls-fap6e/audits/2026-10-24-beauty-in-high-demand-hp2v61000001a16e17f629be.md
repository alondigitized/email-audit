# Beauty in high demand 📈
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email with a real offer at the top. The free Paula's Choice trial sizes (code LOVEPAULA, $45+ purchase) are the only hard incentive. The giant "BEAUTY for YOU" hero sits right underneath and takes over. The eye goes to the hero, not the gift, and the gift is the thing most likely to drive a coupon-minded shopper to check out.
- The subject, "Beauty in high demand 📈", is vague and could front any of the last seven sends. The body doesn't back up the "in demand" claim. It's mostly Summer Fridays lip balms plus a Trending grid. After 10/23's "Oh. My. Minis." and 10/22's "Open for new items", this is another untargeted catalog send with a shallow offer. It's competent and clean, not compelling.

## What's working

- The free Paula's Choice trial sizes with a visible code (LOVEPAULA) sit in the very first block, with a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button.
- The Summer Fridays hero is clean. Two named shades are listed with prices ($24), so the product is concrete.
- The "Trending & Worth the Hype" grid shows brand, product and price on every tile. It's easy to scan.
- The "Florals for Fall" and "Makeup Minis" blocks give a seasonal, giftable feel. The "Clean at Sephora" and "Only at Sephora" badges add trust and exclusivity.
- The Insider/VIB/Rouge strip and the "free shipping" line surface loyalty benefits. The render is clean, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried in a "BEAUTY for YOU" hero. The free gift is a small banner above it, and the $45 minimum is in tiny fine print.
- There are at least six competing themes: Paula's Choice, Summer Fridays, Trending, Emi Jay, Makeup Minis and Florals. No single click path stands out.
- The subject promises "high demand", but nothing in the body shows scarcity or a sales rank. The 📈 emoji is the only nod to it.
- Most CTAs are a generic "SHOP NOW" with no deadline. The only dated urgency is the free-gift code, and it has no expiry shown.
- The footer is heavy. It carries a long block of exclusions and legal text, plus an app-download banner that distracts from shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free gift the hero. Put the Paula's Choice trial sizes in the full-width top block, with the code, the $45 minimum and an end date in plain type. Shrink "BEAUTY for YOU" to a secondary tile.
- 2. Cut the body to three blocks: gift with purchase, one bestseller grid, one seasonal tile. Drop the Emi Jay and Makeup Minis tiles, or move them to a separate send.
- 3. If the subject says "in demand", prove it with a labeled "Top 5 this week" row, a sell-through note or review counts.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific copy, such as "Get my free trial sizes" or "Shop Summer Fridays".
- 5. Add an expiry date to the code and say it in the subject or preheader.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Paula's Choice sizes with $45+ 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer Fridays' new shades are here 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code LOVEPAULA at checkout. Plus new Summer Fridays lip balm shades, $24.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our top-rated skincare and makeup this week, with a free gift on orders $45+.`

- The `evtEmail` pixel URL carries `apikey=nQc7BFt78yJBvfYDKtle9APd5RrX984i` in the query string. It is client-visible by design, but confirm it is a public, scoped, write-only key.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This blocks user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- The `<html>` tag shows no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>`), so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is an invalid value (should be `noindex`). It is harmless in email but sloppy.
- `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic and has no per-send context.
- Four images lack alt text:
  - Two `evtEmail` pixels (`gway/v1/evtEmail?…`).
  - The open pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…`).
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…`).
- The three pixels are decorative and should carry `alt=""`. The nav header is a content image, so it needs real alt text. If it is a nav image, its links are unlabeled when images are blocked.
