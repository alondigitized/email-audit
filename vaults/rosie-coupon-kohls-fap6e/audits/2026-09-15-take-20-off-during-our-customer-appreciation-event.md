# Take 20% off during our Customer Appreciation Event 🥳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same three-tier segmented stack (30% Card Holders / 20% Rewards / 15% Nonmembers) Kohl's has run essentially every day since 09-10, now relabeled "Customer Appreciation Event" — a name that implies something special but delivers the identical math as the 09-14 and 09-15 sends already in this history. The layout has genuinely nice fashion editorial photography (the "These layers are about to get a full-time job" outerwear block) and a strong clearance banner, but it's buried under the now-standard status bar, tier stack, and category nav that opens every one of these emails.
- The "Customer Appreciation" framing is the one new wrinkle, and it's a missed opportunity rather than a differentiator — nothing in the creative actually appreciates the customer (no personalized thank-you, no loyalty milestone, no exclusive early access). It's a repackaged discount cycle. For a subscriber who's seen the 30/20/15 stack nine times in five days, this reads as the tenth remix, not an event.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy: eye lands on the pink/red offer band immediately, then flows to outerwear editorial.
- No render bugs — images, pricing, and layout are all intact and legible.
- Clearance section (70% off) is visually distinct and stands out with its own bold red block.
- Product pricing block (jeans, mattress topper, bath towel) is specific and scannable with orig./sale prices shown.
- "Picked with you in mind" section adds a personalization cue even if generic.

## What's weak

- "Customer Appreciation Event" name is a bait-and-switch — same 30/20/15 stack as every prior send, no actual appreciation gesture (no free gift, no early access, no bonus points).
- Extreme email length (5+ screens) buries the clearance and product picks far below the fold.
- Ninth+ nearly identical structural template in under a week — status bar, tier stack, category nav pattern is now instantly recognizable and fatigue-inducing.
- CTA sprawl: "Shop Now," "Learn More," "Shop Outerwear," multiple category buttons — no single dominant next action.
- Coupon code (REWARDS20) appears only near the bottom after a wall of content, functionally hidden from anyone who doesn't scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "Customer Appreciation Event" mean something distinct — a bonus Kohl's Cash multiplier, early clearance access, or a surprise perk — otherwise drop the name and stop training subscribers to ignore "event" language.
- 2. Move the coupon code and outerwear hero above the fold; cut the redundant category-nav row that repeats after nearly every section.
- 3. Since this is the brand's ~10th tier-stack send this cycle, break pattern entirely for one send — lead with clearance (70% off) as the single hero instead of nesting it mid-email.
- 4. Trim length by half — five distinct shoppable moments (outerwear, women/men/kids/boots, clearance, picked-for-you, coupons) is too many asks in one email.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh outerwear, clearance up to 70% off, and your reward tier savings`
