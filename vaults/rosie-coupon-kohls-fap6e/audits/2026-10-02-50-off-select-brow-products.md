# 50% OFF select brow products 🤨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's cleanest hard-offer send in the recent run. The subject and hero match: "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT BROW PICKS / TODAY ONLY" sits on a warm yellow-peach gradient, with a single white "SHOP NOW" button under it. The eye lands on the offer first, and there's no editorial detour like the 9/29 "From your feed to your cart" send or the Kérastase gift mix-up in the 9/28 Living Proof email.
- The product grid carries the email. Eight tiles show brand, product name, struck-through original price and red sale price. That makes the discount concrete: Benefit gel $28 → $14, Sephora Collection pencil $16 → $8. The weakness is that "up to 50%" is mostly honest only for the two Sephora Collection items. Several tiles are 30% off or less (Grande serum $73 → $51.10, Glossier $24 → $16.80), so the headline oversells the grid. The "INSIDER, You have 0 points" strip is a small personalization hint, but it's no benefit to this recipient.
- Overall it's a solid, focused daily-deal send. It's well executed and a bit generic, and it carries the brand's usual heavy footer and Beauty Insider module below the fold.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a big "UP TO 50% OFF" headline, "TODAY ONLY" urgency and one SHOP NOW button.
- The subject, hero and grid all agree on one category (brow), so nothing is bait-and-switch.
- Each tile shows brand, product, struck-through price and red sale price, so the savings are visible without a click.
- The Sephora Collection tiles are exactly 50% off ($16 → $8), which backs up the headline.
- The fine print states the exact window, 12:01am to 11:59pm PT on 10/02/26, which makes the deadline credible.

## What's weak

- Most products are well under 50% off (30–35% on the Glossier, Grande and Anastasia tiles), so "up to 50%" is a stretch.
- The tiles aren't visibly individually linked. There are no per-product CTAs or ratings, and no reviews or social proof.
- The "introducing point of view" banner and the Beauty Insider block pull attention away from the deal. The tier bar (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is confusing and adds nothing.
- The red sale price on yellow is low-contrast and slightly hard to read.
- The product titles are truncated with "..." ("Lamination...", "Defining...", "Eyebrow with..."), so key details are cut off.
- Offer terms, exclusions and the "no promo code" language are buried in dense footer text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the grid with the true 50% items (the two Sephora Collection tiles) and add a "% off" badge to each tile, so the "up to 50%" headline is matched by what's visible.
- 2. Make each tile clearly clickable with a short CTA or star rating, to add social proof.
- 3. Cut the "point of view" and Beauty Insider modules from a Daily Deals send, or move them lower and shrink them.
- 4. Use a darker sale-price colour, or put the price on a white background, for legibility.
- 5. Show full product names, or drop the truncation, and add a shade or type hint (clear gel vs. pencil).
