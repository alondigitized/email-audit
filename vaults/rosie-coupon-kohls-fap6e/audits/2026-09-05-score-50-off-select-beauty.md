# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's fourth "50% off select beauty" send in a week, and it's the most generic-looking one yet — a pink-to-purple gradient hero with "GET 50% OFF" in giant serif type, a "TODAY ONLY" module of three specific SKUs (Benefit, Supergoop, Rare Beauty), then a wall of eight more products at 50% off. It's functionally identical in structure to 8/31's "Take a closer look" and 8/29's "Select brands are 50% off" — same discount, same layout pattern, different product grid.
- The good news is this one actually shows prices and specific products up top (Benefit brow pencil $28→$14, Supergoop sunscreen, Rare Beauty blush $27→$13.50), which is more concrete than some recent sends that buried the offer in vague copy. But by the fourth iteration of the same 50%-off promo, the subject line and hero no longer differentiate — this reads like sale fatigue, not a fresh event.
- Judgement: a competent execution of a tired mechanic. The visible offer and pricing are honest and clear, but there's nothing here that justifies opening this over the three nearly identical emails already sent this week.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states "GET 50% OFF" with no ambiguity, and a single unmissable "SHOP NOW" button sits right below it.
- The "TODAY ONLY" module shows real before/after pricing (e.g., $28→$14) on three named products, which is more specific than vague sitewide-sale copy.
- "FREE shipping† for Beauty Insiders" is called out directly under the headline, adding a second concrete benefit.
- The product grid below is clean, evenly spaced, with legible product names, brands, and prices — no overlapping text or broken images.
- Loyalty tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is visible near the footer for members.

## What's weak

- This is the same 50%-off mechanic as 8/28, 8/29, and 8/31 — zero visual or copy differentiation from the last near-identical send four days ago.
- The "a" superscript after "50% OFF" and the "†" and "‡" markers signal fine print/exclusions right in the hero, which undercuts the "clean" 50% off claim before you even scroll.
- The eight-product grid at the bottom is an undifferentiated dump (skincare, makeup, haircare mixed with no logic) — it reads like inventory clearance, not a curated pick.
- No urgency element beyond "TODAY ONLY" for the top module — the rest of the sale has no visible end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop repeating the same 50%-off creative — rotate in a different mechanic (points multiplier, GWP, category-specific) like the "Stacking Weekend" email did on 9/4, or this becomes background noise subscribers tune out.
- 2. Move exclusions/fine print language out of the hero band entirely (or shrink it) so the 50% claim reads clean at first glance.
- 3. Curate the bottom grid around a theme (e.g., "skincare picks" or "under $30") instead of a flat 8-item list — gives the eye a reason to scan instead of scroll past.
- 4. Add a real deadline (date/time) to the sitewide portion of the sale, not just the "TODAY ONLY" module, to create credible urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Benefit, Rare Beauty & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off picks + free shipping, Insider`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, today's exclusive markdowns on Benefit and Rare Beauty`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on us — shop before today's deals end`
