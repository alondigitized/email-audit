# Get 10% off when you stock up on new palettes 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a palette-focused Savings Event send, and it's one of the more on-theme ones in the recent run. The "Hot New Palettes" hero has a single clear "SHOP NOW" button, with two medallions above it: 30% off Sephora Collection and 10% off for Insider with code ALLONSALE. The subject promises 10% off new palettes, and the body delivers a long, scannable edit of Huda Beauty, Patrick Ta, Danessa Myricks, Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Natasha Denina, KVD, ABH, NARS, MBMM and Makeup by Mario, all with prices shown.
- The email is also overloaded. A free-same-day-delivery banner, two discount medallions, a gift-with-purchase module (UNREADY), a hair gift set block, a "Meet the Latest" block, a store-opening block and a gift guide sit on top of the palette story. The email is about 9,400px tall, so most of that never gets read. The eye lands on the discount medallions and the hero before it reaches any product.
- Verdict: a solid, relevant send with a good product edit, but it's diluted by stacked offers. It reads as the seventh or eighth near-identical Savings Event email since 11/2. The palette angle is a genuine improvement on the 11/6 rewards-catalog send, but the cadence is still heavy.

## What's working

- The "Hot New Palettes" hero matches the subject line, so the email delivers what the inbox promised.
- Both medallions state the offer and its dates: 30% off 10/30–11/9 and 10% off 11/3–11/9. That makes the deadline credible.
- Product tiles show brand names, "NEW" or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" flags, and prices (for example $55 and $72). Shoppers can compare without clicking.
- The feature blocks for Huda, Patrick Ta and Danessa Myricks use big imagery with a named hero product, which gives the email a clear editorial rhythm.
- The Sephora brand voice and design are consistent and trusted, and the render has no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- The top banner stacks free same-day delivery, 30% off and 10% off, which creates a three-offer pile-up before the hero.
- The medallions say "30% off" but give no hint that it applies only to Sephora Collection. That blurs the offer and feels slightly misleading.
- The lower half is a pile of unrelated modules: the UNREADY gift offer, hair gift sets, EADEM, the Kohl's store locator and a holiday gift guide. These dilute the palette message.
- Product tiles are small and tightly packed in a two-column grid, with tiny labels and no per-product CTA text.
- The footer is a wall of legal fine print, and the barcode at the bottom adds length. The email is far too long for mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower half (hair, EADEM, Kohl's, gift guide) and end after the palette grid. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Collapse the top to one offer. Use "10% off new palettes, code ALLONSALE" with one medallion. Drop the free-delivery strip to a slim line.
- 3. Label the 30% medallion "Sephora Collection only" so the exclusion is honest.
- 4. Make each tile tappable with a clear "Shop" link and a shade or finish detail, such as "12 shades" or "shimmer + matte".
- 5. Add one review or best-seller signal, such as star ratings on two or three palettes.
- 6. Lighten the 30%/10% dual-medallion pattern, which has run unchanged all week. Give the final weekend of the sale its own creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New palettes, 10% off — 3 days left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Huda, Patrick Ta, Dior: new palettes 10% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code ALLONSALE through 11/9 on limited-edition shadow and cheek palettes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Holiday palettes from Huda, NARS and more, with prices from $55.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two QA failures: an empty greeting slot (`Hi ,`) and no plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication headers couldn't be verified through the AgentMail relay. The HTML source is truncated, so findings below cover only the visible head, preheader and first tracking block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage gap:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 47. The skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not validated. No broken links were reported among the 12 that were probed.
- **Tracking pixels (two):**
  - A visible 1×1 `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970269939971072&mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider`.
  - A hidden `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` styled `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - Both are standard open-tracking pixels. The `mi_u` value is a per-recipient identifier in the URL.
- **Empty structured data:** the JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`. The empty code carries no value. The `PromotionCard` entry has no properties.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` attribute at all (confirmed). It is hidden, so the impact is minimal.
  - The `o.gif` warning looks like a false positive. The source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Fixed-width layout:**
  - The wrapper is `width:700px; min-width:700px` on the `tbl tbl_cntr` table.
  - The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't override that width. This may cause horizontal scroll on mobile, but I can't confirm it from the truncated source.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. A conditional comment there is not valid CSS, and some parsers may drop the rules that follow it.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Document structure:**
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.
  - `<title>` is the generic "Sephora".
- **Preheader:** the text ends mid-name ("…and PATRICK TA"). It may be cut off by truncation. Verify it against the full source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,`. The template emits a blank first-name field without a fallback. The recipient is identified (`mi_u=4970269939971072`), so the profile name was missing or unmapped for this inbox. The source needs a fallback such as `Hi there,` or the greeting should be suppressed when the name is empty.
- No raw unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) were visible in the shown source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text alternative is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` part. This hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the sender. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown through the relay. The `From` domain is `beauty.sephora.com`.
- An unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I couldn't verify the unsubscribe link target or the physical postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters** are visible in the retrieved source. Campaign attribution appears to use `mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider` on the Sephora tracking domain instead.
- Landing-page alignment couldn't be checked: no CTA hrefs are in the truncated source, and the QA output doesn't report landing URLs or destination status for the 12 probed links.
- The JSON-LD `discountCode` is empty, so there is no machine-readable code to match against the "10% off" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge field (`Hi there,`) or drop the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Add a `text/plain` part.
3. Check the raw headers at the sender for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query.
6. Correct `no index` to `noindex`.
7. Add `lang`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and `alt=""` on the hidden pixel.
8. Populate or remove the empty `discountCode` in the JSON-LD.
9. Re-run QA against the full HTML, with a higher probe limit, to cover the 47 skipped links, UTM parameters and landing pages.
