# 20% off + Kohl's Cash?  Must be your lucky day 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's twenty-second-ish promotional email in sixteen days and it is, structurally, the same email that landed on July 15 ("Save 20% + get Kohl's Cash to top it off 🤑") — same 20% coupon banner, same Kohl's Cash callout, same multi-category nav strip, same fashion hero, same clearance 70% block, same Beauty Insider 10% footer. The only meaningful creative variation is the subject line's "lucky day" phrasing, which is a minor copywriting hat-tip to novelty without delivering any actual novelty in the email body.
- The creative execution is competent — the hierarchy is clear, the coupon is readable, the lifestyle imagery is lifestyle-appropriate — but the email delivers nothing a regular Kohl's subscriber hasn't seen at least ten times this month. At some point the 20% coupon stops reading as an offer and starts reading as table stakes. That point was probably around July 8th.
- Verdict: a technically acceptable send with zero creative differentiation from its recent predecessors. The subject line's playful framing is wasted on a body that doesn't follow through.

## What's working

- **Coupon banner is legible and prominent** — "20% off" reads instantly at the top, valid dates visible.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — the eye moves: coupon → Kohl's Cash callout → fashion hero → product grid → clearance → Beauty Insider. No confusion about reading order.
- **Clearance 70% block has high contrast** — red badge on dark background pops; the category nav (Home, Shoes, etc.) is actionable.
- **Beauty Insider 10% reward is a loyalty differentiator** — it's the one signal that isn't available to every shopper; it addresses a specific sub-segment.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text isn't clipped, layout is intact on the visible viewport.

## What's weak

- **Subject line promise vs. body reality mismatch** — "lucky day" implies something special or exclusive; the body delivers the same recurring 20% coupon. The novelty framing is immediately deflated.
- **Fashion hero is apparel-generic** — the models and styling are broadly mid-market; no specific trend, occasion, or demographic hook that would sharpen relevance.
- **Kohl's Cash callout is vague** — the banner mentions "Plus earn Kohl's Cash" but doesn't state the earn rate or minimum spend threshold visibly. A subscriber has to already know how it works.
- **Clearance section lacks specificity** — "Up to 70% off" across Home, Shoes, Men's, Women's, Kids tells you nothing; no anchor price or lead product to make it feel like a deal worth clicking.
- **Cadence fatigue is now a structural problem** — this is the same email the recipient got yesterday and the day before. Brand history shows this pattern has been running for 16 days straight.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rotate the lead offer** — A 20% coupon that appears every single day is no longer an offer; it's a price floor. Even one send that leads with a specific hero product ("These sandals are $19.99 today") would break the pattern and re-earn attention.
- 2. **Make the Kohl's Cash earn rate explicit** — "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent" is a concrete, scannable fact. "Plus earn Kohl's Cash" is noise a desensitized subscriber ignores.
- 3. **Anchor the clearance section with a product** — Show one specific item, its original price, and the cleared price. "Women's linen pants: was $49, now $14" converts better than a category badge.
- 4. **Subject line: lean into specificity or scarcity, not generic "lucky day"** — The current subject line sets an expectation the email doesn't meet.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends soon + 70% clearance drops today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash is waiting — plus 20% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend — valid through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance up to 70% off: home, shoes, women's and more`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "20% off + Kohl's Cash" (2026-07-16)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard promotional ESP send using Coherent Path (chp.kohls.com) for click-tracking and MessageGears (mi.kohls.com) for pixel/open tracking. No structural HTML errors detected, but three compliance gaps and a pervasive alt-text omission pattern warrant attention.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by HTTP probe** — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageGears) and `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path). Expected behavior; no broken link signal, but destination URLs cannot be verified without live probe.

**Three distinct tracking pixel stacks active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open pixel (1×1)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` — MessageGears open pixel (1×1)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync (0×0)

No direct issue, but the Demdex pixel (`d_sid=13245196`) fires a third-party cross-site tracking request on open. Under strict CSP or privacy-hardened clients this may be silently blocked without fallback.

**`<custom>` tag on line:**
```html
</custom>
```
Orphaned closing tag immediately after the open-pixel `<img>`. Not valid HTML 4.01 Transitional (the declared DOCTYPE). Benign in practice but signals template assembly artifact.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all tracking/pixel images** — 11 flagged by QA:

| Image | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `fc29b776...` (chp.kohls.com) | Coherent Path open pixel |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | MessageGears open pixel |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM segment pixel |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` | Content render-proof images with `email_name=260716_DG_Email_Thursda` |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` | Additional mi.kohls.com assets |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly (currently absent) to satisfy screen readers and WCAG 2.1. The content images (`email_name=260716_DG_Email_Thursda`) are missing descriptive alt text, which degrades the experience when images are blocked.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents pinch-zoom on mobile:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Common in legacy email templates but worth flagging.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>`. Screen readers announce the window title; leaving it blank is an accessibility gap and forces clients to display the raw subject line or nothing.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** for Roboto and Montserrat — redundant double-fetch. The `<link>` tags are already inside the `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` guard, then `@import` repeats them in the same block. This doubles font requests on non-Outlook clients.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated source (no `{{`, `%%`, `*|` patterns). The `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1017720_2026716` parameters in pixel URLs are resolved, indicating successful personalization at render time.

`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in one pixel URL (`916d6fa0...`) has an **empty value**, suggesting a Kohl's Cash expiration date merge tag that did not resolve:
```
&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...
```
This is a pixel parameter, not visible to the recipient, but indicates a suppression or data gap in the Kohl's Cash redemption date field for this send segment.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not detected** — QA relay did not capture this header. CAN-SPAM does not mandate the header, but Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements require it for senders above 5K/day. Kohl's volume almost certainly exceeds this threshold. Absence could trigger inbox demotion.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not detected** — One-click unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy. If this header is absent at the MTA level (not merely undetected by the relay), this is a deliverability compliance gap.

**SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured by AgentMail relay. Sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (subdomain). Cannot confirm alignment without raw headers. Note: the `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag is correctly set, which prevents link click referrer leakage.

**Physical address** — not visible in truncated source; must appear per CAN-SPAM §7(a). Assumed present in the footer (below truncation point) — cannot confirm from provided source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters cannot be verified** — all 22 outbound links route through `click.s.kohls.com` redirects. UTM attribution depends entirely on what the ESP appends post-redirect. No UTM parameters are visible in the raw HTML href values, which is expected for click-tracked ESP links but means there's no fallback if the redirect chain breaks.

**`email_name=260716_DG_Email_Thursda`** (truncated at 30 chars) appears in multiple render-proof pixel URLs. This is an internal campaign tag, not a UTM param, but its truncation suggests a field length issue in the pixel assembly template.

**`<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`** is present — correct, prevents email mirrors from being indexed.
