# Shop Denim Deal Days for great jeans at great prices 👖
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is finally a real category send — Denim Deal Days is a specific, product-led hero instead of the fourth reskin of the Friends & Family coupon. The "Find an outfit these jeans don't work with. We'll Wait." headline is genuinely good copy, and the Women/Men/Kids denim layout with actual product flat-lays is the most concrete visual merchandising this brand has shown in the recent run.
- But the Friends & Family 20% banner is still stapled to the top — same "Ends Aug. 30" coupon that's now run five days straight — and the email doesn't stop at denim: it tacks on a full second Clearance 70% block plus an unrelated "Picked with you in mind" grid (a coffee/drink maker, bath towels, women's dress pants, a mattress topper). That's three different offers and four different categories competing for one send, which muddies what should have been a clean single-category feature.
- Net: better creative concept than the last several sends, undercut by scope creep and coupon fatigue. A subscriber who's seen five F&F banners this week will glaze past the top third entirely.

## What's working

- Denim Deal Days has a real, specific headline and browsable date window ("now through Sept. 2").
- Women/Men/Kids segmentation with matching flat-lay + lifestyle photo pairs makes the offer easy to shop by household member.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.
- Clear repeated CTA pattern (pink "Shop Now" pills under each section) is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The Friends & Family 20% banner still leads the email — it's the fifth appearance of an identical coupon in three days, so it's dead weight at the top of a denim-themed send.
- Three unrelated offers stacked in one email (F&F 20%, Denim, Clearance 70%, then a random product grid) dilutes the "Denim Deal Days" premise the subject promised.
- No specific price or % off is shown for denim itself — the hero says "select styles on sale" with no number, weaker than the 70% clearance banner right below it.
- The bottom "Picked with you in mind" grid (drink maker, towels, dress pants, mattress topper) has nothing to do with denim or the subject line — pure category noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or shrink the Friends & Family banner to a thin footer strip — it's earned no new real estate after five straight sends; let denim own the top of the email.
- 2. Put a real number in the Denim Deal Days hero (e.g., "Jeans from $19.99" or "Extra 20% off denim") — vague "select styles on sale" underperforms the concrete 70% clearance banner beneath it.
- 3. Cut the "Picked with you in mind" mixed-category grid from this send or move it to a separate cross-sell email — it undercuts the denim focus the subject sold.
- 4. Consider dropping Clearance 70% from this send entirely, or reorder it below the coupon wallet close so denim is the single hero offer through the fold.
