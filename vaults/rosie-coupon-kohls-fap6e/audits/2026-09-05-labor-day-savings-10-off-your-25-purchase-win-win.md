# Labor Day savings + $10 off your $25 purchase ... win-win 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "$10 off $25 + Kohl's Cash" send in a week, and it's the most sprawling version yet — the hero coupon stack (2026-09-01's clearance banner + this week's recurring $10/$25 + $5 Kohl's Cash combo) is now bolted onto a genuine wall-to-wall grab-bag: jeans, kitchen appliances, tees, activewear, bath towels, luggage, rugs, athletic shoes, team apparel, clearance, and a "picked with you in mind" module with a Ninja slushie maker, bath towels, a mattress topper, and jeans. There's no organizing idea beyond "everything is on sale," and the fatigue from 2026-09-03/09-04's near-identical coupon sends is compounding rather than resetting.
- The hero itself is functional — big type, a clear $10-off callout, a Kohl's Cash add-on, and a category nav bar right below it — so anyone who opens will immediately understand the top-line offer. But the email overstays its welcome: by the time a reader hits "Picked with you in mind" two-thirds of the way down, the personalization framing rings hollow next to a slushie maker and a mattress topper with no obvious connection to any prior browsing signal.
- Verdict: solid top-of-funnel hero, weak everything after — this reads like a merchandising dump wearing a Labor Day coupon as its hook, and it's competing directly with the brand's own better sends this week (09-01 sports, 09-02 home) for attention.

## What's working

- The hero headline ("LABOR DAY", $10 off $25, +$5 Kohl's Cash per $25) renders cleanly with strong contrast and no layout bugs.
- Category nav buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) sit right under the hero, giving an immediate secondary path for browsers who aren't coupon-motivated.
- Individual merchandising blocks (jeans, kitchen appliances, towels, luggage) each have a clear price, discount %, and single "Shop Now" CTA — consistent formatting throughout.
- The clearance banner ("Save up to 85%") is visually distinct (dark background, large percentage) and breaks up the scroll.
- Barcode/coupon codes at the bottom are legible and clearly split into two offers (SHOP10, HOME15).

## What's weak

- No hero product image — the top of the email is pure text/coupon, unlike 09-02's home send which led with real merchandising photography.
- The email is exceptionally long (11+ discrete category modules plus a clearance section plus a "picked for you" section) with no visual hierarchy telling the reader which offer matters most.
- "Picked with you in mind (your great taste makes it easy.)" reads as fake personalization — a slushie maker, bath towel, mattress topper, and jeans have no coherent thread and undercut the copy's promise.
- This is the brand's fourth-in-a-row "$10 off $25 + Kohl's Cash" mechanic — subject and hero are barely differentiated from 09-03 and 09-04's sends, and the fatigue is now visible in the brand history trend line.
- Coupons carry "does not apply to select brands" exclusions on multiple categories (athletic shoes, team apparel), which chips away at the "win-win" subject line's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half — pick 3-4 hero categories max instead of running through nearly every department; a shorter, punchier send would raise both open-to-click follow-through and perceived relevance.
- 2. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with actual behavioral personalization (recently viewed / same category as past purchase) or drop the personalization framing entirely — right now it reads as generic filler that erodes trust.
- 3. Differentiate this from the last four coupon sends — either escalate the offer meaningfully or pivot the creative angle (like 09-02's Martha Stewart home launch did) instead of reusing the same $10/$25 + Kohl's Cash mechanic a fifth time.
- 4. Add a real lifestyle hero image up top — every recent well-scoring Kohl's send (09-01, 09-02) led with a merchandising photo, not bare text/coupon graphics.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop jeans, kitchen, home & more before the sale ends`
