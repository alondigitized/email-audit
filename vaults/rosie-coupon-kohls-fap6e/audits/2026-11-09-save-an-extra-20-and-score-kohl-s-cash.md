# Save an extra 20% 🙌 And score Kohl's Cash.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's holiday-shopping wall, and it's more focused than the Nov 8 and Nov 9 Early Black Friday sends. The top of the email has a "Take an extra 20%" hero with an "Ends Nov. 21 · Shop Now" line. Below it sit Home, Women, Men and Kids buttons, then a long run of holiday decor, gift and Kohl's Cash modules. The subject's two promises, "extra 20%" and "Kohl's Cash", both show up above the fold, which is more than I could say for the "STARTS TODAY" send earlier today.
- I could only read this at 192px wide, so I'm going on the large numerals and layout and can't vouch for fine print. The structure is clear: hero, category nav, gift guides, $10-off-$50 Kohl's Rewards banners, a "Preview the Deals" tease, services (Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart), four product tiles, and barcodes with coupon codes. It's a competent promotional email, but it tries to do too much, and it's the same deal-wall template subscribers have seen almost daily since late October.
- Verdict: solid, trustworthy and unremarkable. The extra 20% and Kohl's Cash carry the email, and everything below the first screen dilutes them.

## What's working

- The "Take an extra 20%" hero with a visible deadline (Ends Nov. 21) and a Shop Now button sits at the top and matches the subject.
- The Home / Women / Men / Kids nav buttons are large and let a skimmer self-select quickly.
- The holiday gift modules (Gifts Under $25, Stocking Stuffers, For Her / For Him / For Kids) fit the season.
- Kohl's Cash and Rewards offers ($10 off every $50, "Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent") are repeated and easy to spot.
- The product tiles show big price numerals ($2.99–9.99, $249.99, $169.99, $137.99–209.99), which gives them a clear price anchor.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom make redemption in store straightforward.

## What's weak

- At about 7,000px tall, it's very long, and the real offer gets buried among services, gift guides and coupon blocks.
- The hero doesn't say what the 20% applies to, and the "Shop Now" copy is generic.
- The Kohl's Cash earn offer and the $10-off-$50 coupons are close but different, and they compete for attention.
- The coupon fine print and exclusion notes ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") take up a lot of space at the bottom, and the offer reads as conditional.
- Compared with the Nov 7 to Nov 9 sends ("Up to 50% off early Black Friday", "LAST CHANCE…"), the creative feels like another rotation of the same template.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer. Put the extra 20% and the Kohl's Cash earn window in a single module with a specific CTA, and push the services strip (Klarna, DoorDash, Instacart) to a footer link.
- 2. Make the CTA say what the shopper gets, for example "Shop Extra 20% Off Home" instead of "Shop Now".
- 3. Show the coupon code in the hero so the offer is redeemable without scrolling.
- 4. Drop the near-duplicate $10-off-$50 banners and keep one.
- 5. Vary the creative day to day, or pull back on cadence. Subscribers have seen near-identical deal walls on Nov 7, 8 and 9.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 4 warnings, 43% pass rate). The real defects are malformed head/body markup, a zoom-disabling viewport tag, many images without alt text, and four stacked tracking pixels. The compliance warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** 72 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...`. Destination URLs, redirect health and UTM presence could not be verified.
- **Four separate open/tracking pixels** across three vendors:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/04ff6312-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025536_2026119`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=60277&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  The duplication adds request weight and skews open counts. The first pixel lacks `alt` and `aria-hidden`. The demdex pixel has `width="0" height="0"`, which some clients treat as a broken image.
- **Likely empty dynamic value:** the image URL `...916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe...` has an empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`. This may be an unresolved redemption end date. It is partly truncated, so confirm against the full source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup:**
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `div` is invalid there and forces the parser to close `<head>` early, which can push the `meta` and later head content into the body.
  - A stray `</custom>` closing tag follows `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...">` with no matching opener.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="...maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** (empty href).
- **Missing alt text:** QA flags 11 images. Four are tracking pixels, where `alt=""` would be correct. The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) look like content or promo images. If they carry text or offers, they are unreadable to screen readers and with images off.
- **Table roles:** the tables wrapping the pixels, the `<!-- AAM -->` table and the preheader table have no `role="presentation"`, while others do.
- **Font loading:** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice, by `<link>` and by `@import`, one of them with an unquoted URL. Both are inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is unaffected, but the duplication is redundant.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the visible source.
- Per-recipient IDs such as `mi_u=604230016` are embedded in image URLs. This is expected for dynamic images, but note the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` above.
- The source is truncated, so the body and footer could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The warning itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against raw headers from a direct send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **In-source markup:** `lang="en"` is present, and `meta robots` and `referrer` are set. No other issues found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not assessable:** all 72 links go through `click.s.kohls.com`, so landing URLs and UTM parameters can't be seen.
- **Campaign tagging:** it exists on image URLs only (`email_name=261109_DG_Email_Mon_PM`, `mi_ecmp=1025536_2026119`). It is consistent across the `mi.kohls.com` images.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `itemscope` div and its `meta` tags out of `<head>` into `<body>` (or use JSON-LD). Remove the stray `</custom>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport tag.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images.
4. Consolidate the four pixels. Check that `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` populates in the sent email.
5. Fill in `<title>`. Remove or populate the empty favicon `<link>`.
6. Check the raw delivered message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the footer address. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML.
7. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` links with a redirect-follow check to confirm landing pages and UTM carry-through.
