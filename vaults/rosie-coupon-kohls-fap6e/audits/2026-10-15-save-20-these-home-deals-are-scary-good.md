## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has no confirmed blocking defects, but it has several HTML validity problems, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and four stacked tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication WARNs come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they aren't confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links weren't probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Imq…`), so destination health is unverified. Their `qs=` tokens are opaque, so the final landing URLs can't be read from the source.
- **Four tracking pixels in the body**, each from a different system:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/e42612a3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1023904_20261015`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?WG2FBQGPXQ4URBXGVDGYSNBWGM.60267&d=60267&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  - The redundancy adds requests and makes open counts unreliable. The Adobe demdex pixel is also a third-party call.
- **Stray closing tag.** `<img src="…open.aspx…" alt=""></custom>` has no matching `<custom>` opener. This is malformed HTML, and some parsers may mishandle it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will usually close the head early and move the div into the body.
- **Empty `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.** It is pointless and can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Fonts are loaded twice,** via both `<link>` and `@import` for Roboto and Montserrat. Clients that don't support web fonts fall back to Arial/Helvetica.
- **Alt text is missing on two pixels.** The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` images have no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Possible false positives in the QA alt check.** The `o.gif` and `open.aspx` images have `alt=""` in the source, which is correct for decorative images. The checker still flagged `o.gif`.
- **Unconfirmed alt gaps.** The `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images and `58379268_2_2x` (images.am.kohls.com) look like content or product images, but their tags are cut off in the truncated source. If they truly lack `alt`, that is a real accessibility gap, because they carry the offer content.
- **Layout.** `role="presentation"` is used on the main layout tables, but the first tracking table (`width="1" height="1"`) and the AAM table lack it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient IDs (`mi_u=604230016`, `d_sid=13245196`) are populated. The rest of the body is truncated, so this isn't verified there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and Authentication-Results are not found.** The QA tool itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message or Gmail's "Show original" before treating them as sender defects.
- **Physical address not detected.** The visible source ends before the footer, so this is unconfirmed. Check the full HTML.
- **Hidden `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` are present.** These are harmless, and no compliance impact is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** All visible click URLs are wrapped in ESP redirects (`click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`), and the `qs` payload is opaque.
- **Campaign metadata is carried in `mi.kohls.com` pixel params instead** (`mi_ecmp=1023904_20261015`, `email_name=261015_DG_Email_Thu_PM`). That is consistent with a Movable Ink setup.
- **Landing-page alignment couldn't be tested,** because the redirects were skipped. The "Save 20%" offer can't be matched to a destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `</custom>` and move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport tag.
3. Fill in `<title>`, and remove the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon" href="">`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex` pixels. Confirm that the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `58379268_2_2x` images have meaningful `alt` text.
5. Consolidate redundant open-tracking pixels, or document why four are needed.
6. Drop the duplicate font loading (keep either `<link>` or `@import`).
7. In the QA tooling:
   - Skip images with `alt=""` in the missing-alt check.
   - Pass raw headers through the relay, or label header findings as "unverifiable" instead of WARN.
   - Run the footer and address check on the untruncated HTML.
   - Resolve redirect chains for a sample of the 65 links to verify UTMs and landing pages.
