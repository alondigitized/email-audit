# Open for 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🥳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a stronger send than the week's usual Kohl's coupon email, though it still uses the same shell. The "2 DAYS ONLY" red block and the big "extra 40% off" banner with "Ends Oct. 18" put a real offer in the hero. That is an escalation from the 20% banner on Oct 12–16. The green Kohl's Cash strip and the red Home / Women / Men / Kids buttons are the same ones you've seen all week.
- The body is a pile of competing promos. The Season's Best Shoe Sale (up to 40% off) leads, followed by Haggar suits (up to 20% off), then Clearance (up to 70% off). Below those are four product tiles, and then a block of coupon codes and legal text. I counted at least five different percentages. The 40% banner is the clear winner, but the lower sections pull attention away from it. The coupon banner is the strongest piece. The fine print ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") makes the offer feel less honest than the headline.
- Judgement: a good headline offer, and the subject emoji is fine. The email is too long and carries too many messages. It is an upgrade on the 20% sends but not a break from the pattern.

## What's working

- The "extra 40% off with coupon. Ends Oct. 18" banner is large, sits at the top, and has a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The "2 DAYS ONLY" red block adds visible, believable urgency that matches the Oct. 18 end date.
- The member strip ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS' MEMBER | Earn: 5% REWARDS") makes the email feel personal.
- The Season's Best Shoe Sale hero has a strong boot photo that fits the fall season.
- The "Free returns through January 31, 2027" footer line takes some of the risk out of buying.

## What's weak

- At least five percentages (40%, 20%, 70%, 40% again and 10% off) compete for attention. The shoe sale's "up to 40%" also echoes the coupon's 40%, which is confusing.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" disclaimers sit under several sections, and the legal block at the bottom is dense. Both make the offer feel less clear.
- The email is very long. It contains the same Home / Women / Men / Kids nav twice, plus a Kohl's Cash strip, so the shell feels repetitive.
- The "Picked with you in mind" products (a $299 frozen drink maker, a bath towel, a mattress topper, classic jeans) don't hang together. They read as generic, not personalized.
- The subject says "Open for 40% off", but the banner says "an extra 40%" with a coupon. The subject leaves out the coupon requirement.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to one story: the 40% coupon plus one hero category. Drop the Haggar and Clearance sections, or push them into a single "More deals" row.
- 2. Say the coupon condition in the subject, and don't repeat the same offer in the shoe-sale block.
- 3. Use one nav block, not two.
- 4. Make "Shop Now" specific, for example "Shop Shoes at 40% Off."
- 5. Put the coupon code in the first screen, so people don't have to scroll to find it.
- 6. Build the product tiles from the recipient's browsing or purchase history, not a random mix.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends tomorrow: shoes, boots & more 👢`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days only: extra 40% off + Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use your coupon on fall boots, sneakers and home. Ends Oct. 18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member code is inside, plus 5% back in Rewards.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Open for 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🥳"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an ESP-wrapped (`click.s.kohls.com`) send with four stacked tracking pixels and several malformed head and body constructs. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because AgentMail doesn't relay those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most links are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque click tracking:** 68 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so none of their destinations were probed or verified. Example: `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi...`.
- **Four tracking beacons fire on open**, which is redundant and adds load and privacy exposure:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/838b4888-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1024046_20261017`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60268&bmt=0`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, with `width="0" height="0"`)
- **Stray closing tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. It is invalid markup left over from the template.
- **Empty parameter:** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is blank in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL. This is likely an unpopulated dynamic field. The URL is truncated, so I can't confirm it.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** `mi_u=604230016` appears in the `mi.kohls.com` image URLs. This is a persistent identifier in third-party-fetched assets, which is low risk but worth knowing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (real impact):**
  - `58379988_Product_ctft01_ShoeSale_Hero_GIF`
  - `58379268_2_2x`
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`
  
  With images blocked, or with a screen reader, these show as filenames or nothing.
- **Pixels without `alt`:** the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` and `demdex.net/event` pixels also lack alt. These are low impact, but `alt=""` would be cleaner. The `o.gif` pixel does have `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early, so the following elements fall into the body and the schema.org microdata is unreliable. Gmail strips it regardless.
- **Duplicate meta:** `format-detection` appears twice.
- **Unused font:** the Roboto stylesheet is loaded but the body font stack is `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica`. This is a wasted request, though harmless since it sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.
- **Layout tables:** the pixel wrapper `<table width="1" height="1">` and the AAM table lack `role="presentation"`. The main containers have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter (see §2) is the only sign of a possible missing dynamic value.
- Personalization is not verifiable beyond the truncation point.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **inconclusive, not a confirmed defect**. Verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so status is unknown. The From address is `Kohls@s.kohls.com`, a subdomain. Check alignment on the raw message.
- **Physical address:** not detected, but the footer was truncated from the source, so this is **unverified**. Re-run the check on the full HTML.
- **Unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Attribution appears to rely on Kohl's proprietary parameters (`mi_ecmp=1024046_20261017`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `email_name=261017_DG_Email_Myster...`) and the encoded `qs=` blob.
- Landing pages could not be resolved because all 68 links are redirect-wrapped and skipped. Landing-page alignment, link rot and redirect chains are unverified.
- The campaign date `20261017` in `mi_ecmp` matches the send date.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. That covers the footer unsubscribe link and physical address, the full link list, and the remaining images.
2. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the relay-driven WARNs as sender defects until you do.
3. Resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` links with a redirect-following probe to check final URLs, status codes and UTM/campaign parameters.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero GIF and the content images. Use `alt=""` on the pixels.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Fix the template hygiene issues: the stray `</custom>`, the empty `<title>`, the empty favicon `href`, the `itemscope` div inside `<head>`, the duplicate `format-detection` meta, and the unused Roboto font.
7. Consolidate the four open and tracking beacons where possible, and investigate the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value.
