# So hot right now 💯
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- "So hot right now 💯" is a trend-roundup email — a scattershot grid of ~10 products (skincare, fragrance, makeup, hair tools) under the banner "BEAUTY for YOU / Ready to try these trending picks?" It's a real structural departure from the single-offer sends this brand has been running (National Lip Day loop through 07-31, Beauty Insider Celebration on 08-01), but the subject line itself is nearly a rerun — "So hot right now" lands four days after 07-31's "Hot on social for a reason 🌶️," so the "hot/trending" beat is being repeated, not the offer.
- The top of the email is cluttered before the reader ever sees the hero: a "GET 4X POINTS" banner, then a "Get a Mini" Jo Malone offer, then finally "BEAUTY for YOU." Three competing asks stacked before any single message lands is a hierarchy problem, and it means the actual hero (the trending picks grid) is buried below two other offers.
- Where this email does work is depth — a real assortment with prices, "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "NEW" flags, a Beauty Insider mystery-reward tease, and the loyalty tier bar at the footer. It reads as a legitimate content/discovery play rather than another markdown blast, which is a nice change of pace after a week of near-identical Lip Day pushes. But the subject line promises nothing specific, there's no single clear CTA to rally around, and "So hot right now" repeating the "hot" language from three sends ago will read as fatigue to anyone tracking cadence.

## What's working

- Genuine assortment variety — fragrance, skincare, makeup, and a hair tool all represented with clean product photography and visible prices.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA," "NEW," and "LIMITED EDITION" flags on multiple items (ViktorRolf, Kérastase, NEST, Laneige) give real reasons to click beyond "it's trending."
- The 4X points offer is concrete and time-bound ("Ends 8/5"), and it's tied to loyalty currency rather than a generic discount, which fits repeat Insider shoppers.
- No render bugs — clean grid alignment, all product images loaded, footer/legal block intact.
- Loyalty ladder (Insider/VIB/Rouge) and Rewards Bazaar are visible in-body, reinforcing membership status without requiring a click.

## What's weak

- Three separate offers (4X points banner, Get a Mini, BEAUTY for YOU hero) are stacked above the fold with no visual priority — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- Subject line "So hot right now 💯" carries zero information — no category, no product, no offer — and it echoes 07-31's "Hot on social for a reason," so subscribers who opened that one get déjà vu.
- No preview/preheader text is showing at all in this render — that's wasted real estate in the inbox line right next to the subject.
- The K18 Scalp Serum block asks for an app download for "24-hour early access" instead of a direct shop link — that's friction dropped into an otherwise browse-and-buy email.
- Primary CTA copy is just "SHOP NOW" repeated four times across the email (hero, mini, skincare) — nothing differentiates which "shop now" leads where.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one lead offer. Either open with the 4X points banner and drop "Get a Mini" below the trending grid, or lead with "BEAUTY for YOU" and move the points banner to a secondary slot — don't stack three asks before the fold.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to name at least one category or product from the grid so it's not interchangeable with 07-31's send.
- 3. Add real preheader copy that previews a specific item (e.g., the Shark Beauty tool or the Laneige limited-edition mask) instead of leaving it blank.
- 4. Swap the K18 "download the app" CTA for a direct product link, or clearly label it as an app-exclusive perk so it doesn't read as a dead end.
- 5. Vary CTA copy by section ("Shop the Edit," "Claim Your Mini," "See the Tool") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cooling LED masks, cult Kérastase serum, and more you're about to see everywhere`
