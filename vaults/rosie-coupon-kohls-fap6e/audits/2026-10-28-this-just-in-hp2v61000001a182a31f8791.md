# 🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a catalog email with a reasonable offer on top, and the subject does nothing to set it up. "🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟" is generic. The body opens with a free Carolina Herrera Good Girl Jasmine fragrance mini ("Shop to redeem") and a "Savings Event" banner with a "Coming Soon" badge. The hero then moves to "Something New Is Here" with a red butterfly-shaped Carolina Herrera bottle. Those are two competing messages, and the hero is the prettier one. The free mini is the real incentive, and it gets squeezed into a small strip.
- Below the hero is a tidy 2x4 "New Must-Haves" grid. Each item carries a "New • Only at Sephora" or "Limited Edition" tag and a price: Ouai $62 value, Menokin $39, Valentino $198. After that come "Spot it. Shop it." tiles for hair sets and body care, and a "Holiday Gift Hits" banner. It reads as a catalog, which is what Sephora's recent sends have been: 10/27 "We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒", 10/26 "These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️", 10/25 "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞". Nothing here gives a subscriber a reason to treat today's send differently from yesterday's.
- Judgement: a solid, well-rendered Sephora catalog send. It is held back by a throwaway subject, a free-gift offer that loses to the hero, and a "Coming Soon" tease that gives you nothing to act on today.

## What's working

- The free Carolina Herrera fragrance mini is a concrete incentive near the top, with a "Shop to redeem" button.
- The product grid is easy to scan. Every tile has a brand name, product name, price and an exclusivity or limited-edition tag.
- "Only at Sephora" and "Limited Edition" labels add credible scarcity and a reason to shop here.
- The Beauty Insider / VIP / Rouge tier strip near the bottom keeps loyalty visible.
- The layout has no visible render problems: no broken images, overlaps or gaps.
- Seasonal framing ("Holiday Gift Hits", minis and sets) fits the late-October gifting run-up.

## What's weak

- The subject says nothing about beauty, fragrance or the offer. "THIS JUST IN" in all caps with star emojis could come from any retailer.
- Three headline messages compete: the free mini, the Savings Event ("Coming Soon") and "Something New Is Here". The eye lands on the butterfly bottle first, not the offer.
- The "Savings Event" banner is a tease with a "Learn More" button and no date or rate you can act on, which adds noise.
- Most CTAs say "Shop Now" or "Shop to Redeem". They are generic and don't name a product or category.
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is a very long, dense block. Offer conditions such as the purchase minimum are hard to find from the hero.
- Nothing time-bound is visible for the free mini, so there is no reason to click today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free mini the hero, with the deadline and the minimum purchase stated up front. Move "Something New" below it, or drop the "Coming Soon" banner until it is live.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to name the fragrance and the gift. Today's subject carries neither.
- 3. Give each CTA a specific verb and noun, such as "Get your free mini" or "Shop new fragrance", instead of repeating "Shop Now".
- 4. Cut the grid from 8 products to 4–6 so the hero offer doesn't get buried.
- 5. Put the purchase minimum and end date next to the offer instead of leaving them only in the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New fragrance drop + a free mini inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add a Good Girl Jasmine mini to your order, plus 8 new Sephora-only finds.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `That red butterfly bottle is here. Free mini with your purchase.`
