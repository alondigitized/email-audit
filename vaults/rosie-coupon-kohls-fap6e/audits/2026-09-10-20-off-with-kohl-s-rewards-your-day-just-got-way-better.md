# 20% off with Kohl's Rewards ☀️ Your day just got way better 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely segmented, personalized offer — three distinct rates (30% Card Holders / 20% Rewards Members / 15% Nonmembers) stacked at the very top, which is a real step up from the flat "$10 off $25" stack Kohl's ran all last week. Since the subject specifically calls out "Kohl's Rewards" and "20% off," and I'm being treated as a member, the top block lands directly. The membership status bar ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS") is a nice touch that reinforces this isn't a generic blast.
- Past that strong open, though, the email sprawls. It's an enormous kitchen-sink send — apparel, shoes, clearance, home goods, a blender, jeans, a polo — with no single hero product or clear "click here first" moment after the top offer block. Compared to 2026-09-09's "FALL EDIT" seasonal reset (6.00/10), this reverts to the undifferentiated multi-category grab-bag Kohl's leans on constantly. Solid open, mediocre click-through.

## What's working

- Clear three-tier personalized discount block right at the top (30%/20%/15% by status) with a visible barcode coupon near the bottom too.
- Membership status line ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER") is a real personalization signal, not boilerplate.
- Category nav pills (Women/Men/Kids/Home) appear twice, giving multiple easy entry points.
- "Kohl's Cash" green banner and the clearance "Now up to 80% off" block are visually distinct and easy to scan.
- Individual product tiles have clear pricing, including strikethrough original price on the jeans (Orig. $52.00 → $29.99).

## What's weak

- No single hero CTA — after the top coupon block, the email fragments into sweaters, shoes, clearance, a blender, towels, jeans, and a polo shirt with no hierarchy telling me where to click first.
- The lifestyle hero photo ("There's a time and place for these looks. It's now.") is vague, generic copy that doesn't connect to any specific product or season despite today being September 10.
- Mixing a $349.99 drink maker and bath towels into a "Picked with you in mind" grid undercuts the personalization framing established at the top.
- Coupon code (REWARDS20) is buried near the bottom as a barcode graphic instead of being repeated near the top CTA buttons.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product grid down to 2-3 categories max and put one clear hero product/CTA directly under the top offer block — right now the eye has nowhere obvious to land after the coupon tiers.
- 2. Move the coupon code/barcode up near the first "Shop Now" button so the offer and the redemption mechanism aren't 10 scrolls apart.
- 3. Replace the vague lifestyle copy ("There's a time and place for these looks. It's now.") with something tied to the actual discount tiers or a fall-specific hook.
- 4. Drop the "Picked with you in mind" catch-all block (blender + towels + jeans + polo) — it dilutes the personalized-member framing the top of the email worked hard to establish.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Card Holders: 30% off. Rewards Members: 20% off. Ends 9/20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member rate is ready — shop before it ends Sunday.`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's "20% off Kohl's Rewards" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a legacy HTML4.01/MSO-conditional template with functional tracking infrastructure (open pixel, Adobe AAM beacon, click-redirect wrapper), but ships without standard list-management headers and with widespread missing `alt` attributes on tracking/content images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary open-tracking beacon: `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/5d41be4f-90e9-47e8-8987-af1ab6d9d36b?mi_mid=01a08b9e-4300-7000-bfd0-09bc5ae79a5f&cp_tp=v3.eJy...` — opaque base64 `cp_tp` payload (CoherentPath platform), 1x1 pixel.
- Secondary open tracker: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1021535_2026910` (Cheetah/Marigold-style ESP tracking, `mi_ecmp` = campaign ID).
- Third open tracker: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?X5P3YWXLWK4ULKXRPXZVV5PDY4.60266&d=60266&bmt=0` — note this `<img>` is wrapped in a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag, which is invalid markup (likely a template-engine artifact) but not rendering-breaking since browsers/clients ignore unrecognized tags.
- Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) pixel: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — third-party tracking, no alt text (flagged below).
- QA flags 63 tracking/click-redirect links skipped from HTTP probing (expected — these are one-time-token click redirects, not directly probeable without consuming the token).
- All visible link infrastructure points through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com` (legacy Kohl's ESP), suggesting two tracking/redirect systems are active in parallel — worth confirming this is intentional and not a leftover migration artifact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional with MSO/VML namespaces — standard for Outlook desktop compatibility, no issue.
- 8 images confirmed missing `alt` text per QA scan, all tracking pixels/redirect images:
  - `5d41be4f-...` (click.chp.kohls.com open pixel)
  - `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com/p/up/...)
  - `event` (kohls.demdex.net AAM pixel)
  - `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` (all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` render-tracking pixels)
  - These are all functionally invisible 1x1 tracking pixels, so missing `alt` has no user-facing accessibility impact, but `alt=""` with explicit `aria-hidden="true"` (as correctly done on the `o.gif` pixel) should be applied consistently to the others for screen-reader hygiene.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present — standard/correct for transactional marketing email, not an issue.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px with `.container`, `.w_50pc`/`.w_60pc`/`.w_35pc` fluid-width overrides present — standard mobile-responsive email pattern, no issue found in the visible CSS.
- Google Fonts (`Roboto`, `Montserrat`) loaded via `<link>` and `@import` for non-MSO clients — fonts will fall back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` in clients that block remote font loading (most webmail clients), which is expected/acceptable behavior, not a bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible source. `mi_mid=01a08b9e-4300-7000-bfd0-09bc5ae79a5f` in the tracking URL is a resolved per-recipient member ID, not a broken token. No issues found in the visible portion — note the HTML is truncated, so body content (offer blocks, personalized product recs) was not fully reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found (QA WARN). CAN-SPAM does not strictly require this header, but its absence removes one-click unsubscribe from Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook UI, which is functionally material for a bulk commercial sender.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found (QA WARN) — without this, even if `List-Unsubscribe` were present, one-click unsubscribe (POST-based) would not work; only the older `mailto:`/link form would.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data. This may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than an actual authentication failure — flag for header-capture verification, not necessarily a sender-side issue.
- Physical mailing address and in-body unsubscribe link (both CAN-SPAM requirements) are not visible in the truncated HTML — cannot confirm compliance from available source; footer content was cut off.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM/landing alignment)
- Campaign identifiers present in tracking pixels: `mi_ecmp=1021535_2026910`, `mi_u=604230016`, `email_name=260910_DG_Email_Thursda...` (truncated) — internally consistent naming (`260910` = campaign date code, matches send date 2026-09-10).
- No outbound CTA/product links were visible in the truncated source (only tracking pixels and the AAM beacon were captured) — cannot verify UTM parameters on destination links or confirm alignment with a landing page, since QA explicitly skipped the 63 click-redirect links (opaque single-use tokens, not directly resolvable to a final URL without consuming them).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` consistently to all 7 remaining tracking-pixel `<img>` tags (currently only the `o.gif` pixel has it).
2. Fix the malformed `</custom>` closing tag around the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel — remove the stray tag or wrap it correctly.
3. Verify whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually absent at the source (Kohl's ESP) or simply dropped by the AgentMail relay before reaching the QA scanner — re-check against raw SMTP headers if possible.
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from mail server logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here and its absence is inconclusive on its own.
5. Re-run the audit against the full (untruncated) HTML to verify CAN-SPAM footer requirements (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) and to capture outbound CTA link UTMs for continuity checks — this review's compliance/continuity sections are limited by source truncation.
