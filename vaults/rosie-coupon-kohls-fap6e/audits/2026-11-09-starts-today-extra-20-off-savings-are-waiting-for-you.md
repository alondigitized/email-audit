# STARTS TODAY: Extra 20% off savings are waiting for you 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is another long Kohl's deal wall, and the subject doesn't match it. The subject promises "STARTS TODAY: Extra 20% off", but the top of the email is a "Kohl's Rewards Member / Kohl's Cash" banner, a four-button nav (Home, Women, Men, Kids) and a holiday sweater hero ("Get wrapped up in the comfort, not the cost"). The "Take an extra 20% off" offer doesn't appear until the coupon section near the bottom. The Sephora event ("ENDS TODAY", 30% / 20% off) and the "Preview the Deals Now" module are both stronger than the hero for urgency, so the email never has a clear lead.
- I could only read this render at about 170px wide, so I'm going by layout and the large numerals. It has about 10 modules: a hero, a sweater and fashion grid, member-exclusive $10-off offers, Sephora, a Black Friday preview, services, picks, and four coupon codes. That is a lot for one send. After the Nov 6–8 run of "Up to 50% off" and "LAST CHANCE" emails, this reads as the same creative with a new subject line. My call is average: the brand and the offer stack are solid, but the focus is muddy and it repeats earlier sends.

## What's working

- The Sephora at Kohl's block has a clear "ENDS TODAY" with big 30% and 20% numerals, a visible "Shop Now" button and an end date.
- The member-exclusive "$10 off $50 toy purchase" and "$10 off $50 home purchase" cards give concrete, loyalty-linked offers with clear green CTAs.
- The coupon section at the bottom lists codes (SAVEMORE, TOY10, HOME10) with barcodes, so the offers are easy to redeem.
- The product-pick grid shows prices ($2.99–$9.99, $249.99, $169.99, $137.99–$209.99), so shoppers can judge value quickly.
- The service strip (Klarna, Doordash/Instacart same-day, free store pickup) tells shoppers how to buy for the holidays.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Extra 20% off" is buried in the bottom coupon section and absent from the hero.
- The email carries too many competing offers (sweaters from $24.99, Sephora, Kohl's Cash, $10-off coupons, a Black Friday preview), so there is no single focus.
- "Starts today" conflicts with the "Ends today" Sephora block. It's confusing which deadline applies.
- The hero mixes a Rewards banner, a nav row and a sweater promo, so the eye doesn't land on one offer.
- It is a very long scroll with dense fine print and several codes, and it feels like the same wall as the Nov 6–8 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the "Extra 20% off" offer with its code (SAVEMORE) and end date in the hero, as a large badge with one "Shop now" button, and move the sweaters below it.
- 2. Cut the email to three modules: the hero offer, the member $10-off offers, and a product-pick grid. Move Sephora into its own send.
- 3. Reconcile the deadlines. Say what starts today and what ends today, rather than mixing them.
- 4. Drop the nav row or move it into the footer.
- 5. Add social proof such as star ratings on the product picks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today: extra 20% off, code SAVEMORE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off starts now ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $10 off $50 for Rewards members. Code inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters from $24.99, free store pickup, and your coupon codes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, with a 640px container and a mobile breakpoint at 500px. The HTML source has several markup defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, an empty `<title>`, and content images with no alt text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided. The source was truncated, and the header checks come from the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 77 links were skipped by the QA check because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…`). Destination status, redirect chain and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Four separate tracking pixels before the main content:**
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/d02426e5-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&…`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
- **Plain-text part:** 13,470 of 19,226 characters (about 70%) are URLs. This is a deliverability risk and makes the text part hard to read. It follows from the long `qs=` redirect tokens.
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is written as `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` has no matching opening tag. It is invalid and may confuse some parsers.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (QA-confirmed):** `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`, and `https://images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/59392300_roots_2x?scl=1`. If these are the offer, product or CTA images, the email is unreadable with images blocked or with a screen reader.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`, `o.gif` and `kohls.demdex.net/event`. These are low impact. Only `o.gif` carries `aria-hidden="true"`, so the other pixels should get `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` as well.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 issue.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `<meta itemprop>` children sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Most clients strip it, so it gives no benefit.
- **Missing table roles:** the 1×1 spacer table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) lack `role="presentation"`. The main wrapper tables do have it.
- **Web fonts:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is protected. The `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The subscriber ID `mi_u=604230016` is populated in the image URLs. The remainder of the source was truncated, so the check is incomplete.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
- **Physical address:** the QA check did not detect one. The HTML was truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed from the data provided. Verify against the full source.
- **Unsubscribe link in body:** not visible in the truncated source. Verify it in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Cannot verify:** no UTM parameters are visible, and the destinations are hidden behind `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, which were not probed. Attribution appears to rely on the ESP's `qs` token.
- **Campaign ID in image URLs:** the images carry `email_name=261109_DG_Email_Monday` and `mi_ecmp=1025540_2026119`, which can be used to tie campaign tagging together.
- **Offer and landing alignment:** the "Extra 20% off" offer cannot be checked against a landing page without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images listed in section 3. Use `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` on all tracking pixels.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>`, fill or remove `<link href="">`, add a real `<title>` (or the subject line), and move the schema.org `div` out of `<head>`.
3. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and check the full HTML footer for the physical address and unsubscribe link. Treat those three warnings as unconfirmed until then.
5. Consolidate the four tracking pixels where possible, and shorten the redirect tokens to improve the text-to-URL ratio in the plain-text part.
6. Resolve a sample of the 77 redirect links in a browser or with `curl -IL` to confirm destinations, status codes and UTM or campaign parameters.
