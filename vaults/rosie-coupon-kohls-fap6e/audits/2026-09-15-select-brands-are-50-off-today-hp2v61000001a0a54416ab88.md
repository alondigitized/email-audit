# Select brands are 50% off today 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact bait-and-switch structure Sephora has run all week — the subject "Select brands are 50% off today 🤑" promises a straightforward discount, but the actual hero that greets you is "BEAUTY'S LATEST," a new-arrivals launch (InnBeauty Project cleanser/moisturizer) with zero percent-off pricing anywhere near it. The 50% off banner exists, but it's a thin strip crammed above the header, subordinate to a full-page new-launch showcase and then a "Don't Miss Out On These Faves" product grid with no visible discounts at all — full price on every single item (Tower 28 $36, Kiehl's $72, Rare Beauty $16).
- Compare to 9/10's "Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands" (8/10) or 9/12's "50% off select beauty" (7/10) — those at least got a real hero with the discount up top before pivoting to catalog. This one buries the deal even harder: it's essentially a new-product launch email wearing a discount subject line as a costume, plus a discount banner nobody asked to see next to eight full-price "faves."
- Judgment: this is one of the weaker sends in the recent run — the subject overpromises, the layout underdelivers, and a shopper who opens for the 50% off will scroll past six sections before finding anything resembling a sale, and even then, no items in the grid are shown at the discounted price.

## What's working

- The 50% off banner is visible immediately at the top with a "TODAY ONLY" badge, giving urgency and a clear CTA ("SHOP NOW").
- Clean, uncluttered visual hierarchy per section — no overlapping text or broken images anywhere in the render.
- "Clean at Sephora" badges are consistently applied, useful trust signal for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- Good product variety in the grid (skincare, hair, fragrance, makeup) with add-to-cart buttons on every tile.

## What's weak

- Subject promises 50% off, but the dominant hero content ("Beauty's Latest," InnBeauty Project) is a new-launch push with no discount attached — classic subject/body mismatch.
- None of the 8 grid products show strikethrough or sale pricing — it's a full-price catalog glued under a discount subject line.
- The 50% off banner has no visible expiration time or specific brand list — "Select Beauty" and fine-print asterisks do the heavy lifting with no clarity on what qualifies.
- Email is extremely long (6+ full sections: banner, new launch, grid, body moisturizers, Cult Gaia, fragrance minis, footer) — the actual offer gets diluted by section six.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move an actual discounted product carousel directly under the 50% off banner — right now the discount and the merchandise are disconnected; a shopper can't tell what's actually 50% off.
- 2. Add a real deadline (e.g., "Ends tonight at midnight") to the banner instead of just "TODAY ONLY" — vague urgency reads as filler after four sends this week using the same trick.
- 3. Cut the email length — five distinct campaigns (new launch, faves grid, body moisturizers, Cult Gaia, fragrance minis) in one send dilutes the primary offer; split into a discount-only send and a new-launch send.
- 4. Show discounted prices in the product grid, or don't call this a 50%-off email — pick one narrative.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick + new InnBeauty Project drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: new dual-step barrier repair from InnBeauty Project`
