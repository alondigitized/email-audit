# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's Stacking Weekend hero — same maroon 50%-off + 500-points banner from every send since 9/4 — but the bulk of the email is a full-length EADEM brand takeover: "Elevate with EADEM," eight sequential product modules ("Spray and Soothe," "Boost Radiance," "Capsule Faves," "Patch It Up," "Glow On," "Get Sun-Soaked," "Mini Essentials") each with its own hero shot and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" CTA. It's essentially a single-brand catalog dump wearing a Stacking Weekend hat.
- The subject promises a sitewide beauty discount, but a reader opens into eight straight screens of one skincare brand before the discount reappears as a small "50% off Select Beauty" tile near the bottom. That's a real subject/body mismatch — anyone who opened for the 50%-off-plus-points hook has to scroll past almost the entire email to find it again.
- Competent execution on each individual module (clean product photography, consistent "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge, clear "SHOP NOW" CTAs), but as a full send it's poorly prioritized: the promoted offer is buried under brand marketing that doesn't match the subject line at all.

## What's working

- Hero banner (50% off + 500 bonus points) is bold, on-brand maroon, and immediately legible.
- Each EADEM module follows a consistent template — product shot, headline benefit, "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag, "SHOP NOW" link — making the long scroll easy to skim.
- Product photography is clean and consistent (styled flat-lays with props like the fabric backdrop and rock).
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "up to 50% off select beauty," but the actual discount doesn't reappear until deep in the email, after 8+ EADEM product modules — massive mismatch between subject and body priority.
- The email is extremely long for a single send; a reader has to scroll through an entire single-brand catalog to reach the second offer, "Real Luxe," "Hare's Latest Lineup," and the Virtual Beauty Mixer.
- This is now the 6th Sephora send in a row (per brand history) leaning on the same maroon Stacking Weekend hero — creative fatigue is setting in.
- No urgency or deadline visible anywhere in the EADEM section, despite the subject's hourglass emoji implying time pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: a tight, offer-led Stacking Weekend send and a separate EADEM brand spotlight — bundling them buries the discount that earned the open.
- 2. Move the "50% off Select Beauty" module up directly under the hero so it isn't orphaned near the footer.
- 3. Add a visible countdown or explicit end date/time to the hero to justify the ⏳ emoji in the subject.
- 4. Trim the EADEM section from 8 modules to 3-4 hero SKUs — the repetition dilutes rather than builds interest.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off beauty offer ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the savings before this offer disappears`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: new EADEM skincare, only at Sephora`
