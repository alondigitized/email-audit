# 📣 Starts today: Take 20% off new faves 🤗
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This one actually breaks the "Epic Deals" purple-hero rut — new "Cozy Finds" hibernation theme, warm neutral palette, a $10-off-$50 home coupon banner, and a full home-category grid (bedding, bath, decor, kitchen, storage, rugs). It's a legitimate seasonal pivot after nine straight near-identical category-blitz sends, and the home-goods styling (throw pillows, blankets) reads current for fall.
- But it's stuffed — the email keeps going long after the "20% off new faves" subject promise is paid off, tacking on a "just one thing" apparel/home/beauty nav strip, a toy/kids co-branded rewards push, then four unrelated product tiles (a drink blender, a towel, jeans, a mattress topper) before finally landing on the coupon/barcode wall. The subject sells one clean idea; the body sells six different departments with no throughline.
- Net: better creative variety than the recent Kohl's cadence, but it's an unfocused kitchen-sink send that buries its own hero offer under too many competing asks. Middle-of-the-pack.

## What's working

- Genuine creative refresh — warm "Cozy Finds" home theme breaks the fatigue from nine straight purple "Epic Deals" sends this week.
- Clear top-of-email hierarchy: EXTRA 20% off banner → clearance 70% banner → $10 off $50 coupon, all above the fold.
- Category iconography (bedding, bath, decor, kitchen, storage, rugs) is clean, circular, and easy to scan.
- Home decor product photography (pillows/blankets) is styled and appetizing, not generic stock.

## What's weak

- Message sprawl: subject promises "20% off new faves" but the body pivots through home, apparel, kids, toys, beauty, and mattresses — no single hero product or category wins the eye.
- The "Your 'just one thing' plan had a good run" apparel nav strip feels like a leftover module bolted onto a home-themed email, breaking the cozy narrative.
- Four bottom product tiles (drink maker, towel, jeans, mattress topper) have zero connective tissue to "new faves" or to each other — it reads like inventory overflow, not curation.
- Coupon/barcode wall at the bottom repeats the "Extra 20%" and "$10 off $50" offers already shown above, adding redundancy instead of new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — keep the Cozy Finds home hero + coupon banner + category grid, and drop the apparel nav strip, the toy/kids rewards block, and the four random product tiles entirely. Let the subject's promise map to one clean visual story.
- 2. Move the $10-off-$50 coupon code and barcode directly under the Cozy Finds hero instead of repeating it in a footer wall — reduce friction for the reader who already decided to buy.
- 3. If home, kids, and beauty all need a slot, split into two sends (home-focused vs. everything-else) rather than cramming one email with six departments.
- 4. Swap "Shop Now" for category-specific CTA copy on the hero (e.g., "Shop Cozy Finds") to reinforce the theme instead of a generic verb.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New faves, 20% off — starts today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $10 off $50 on bedding, bath & decor`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Blankets, pillows & more — extra 20% off today only`
