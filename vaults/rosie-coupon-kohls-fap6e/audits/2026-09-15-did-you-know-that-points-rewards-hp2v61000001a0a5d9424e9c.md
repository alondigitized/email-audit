# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's Rewards Bazaar send, and unlike most of the brand's recent sends in this history, the subject actually matches the hero — "Did you know that points = rewards?" leads into "REWARDS BAZAAR: Grab September rewards while you can!" No bait-and-switch here, which breaks the pattern flagged in the 9/12 through 9/15 sends in this thread.
- The problem is this email isn't for everyone — it's a points-redemption catalog, useful only to Beauty Insider members who are sitting on a meaningful point balance and know roughly what they're worth. There's no dollar-value framing, no "you have X points" personalization visible in the hero, and the whole email is a scroll-through catalog of free-with-points items rather than a percent-off deal. For a member who already tracks points, this is genuinely useful; for anyone else it reads as noise.
- Execution-wise it's clean — real product photography, clear point costs, scarcity counters ("16 AVAILABLE NOW") on every item. That scarcity mechanic is a nice touch this brand hasn't leaned on in prior sends I've seen. Solid, honest, narrow-audience email.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are aligned — "points = rewards" leads directly into the Rewards Bazaar hero, no bait-and-switch like 9/14's K18 or 9/15's "50% off" sends.
- Every catalog item shows a clear points cost and a live scarcity counter (e.g. "30 AVAILABLE NOW," "3 AVAILABLE NOW"), which reads as more honest urgency than a generic countdown clock.
- Clean tiered product grid (750+ point tier) with real photography, no placeholder or broken images.
- Nav bar shows Insider tier status (INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) which is a nice loyalty touch even though this reader's own tier isn't highlighted.

## What's weak

- No personalized point balance in the hero — "You shopped and earned points" is generic; the email never says how many points I actually have, which is the single most useful data point for this send.
- The top banner ("You have 0 points") contradicts the entire premise of the email if that's genuinely my balance — that's either a render bug or a targeting failure sending a rewards-redemption email to a 0-point account.
- Items skew high-cost (4,500–15,000 points) with only one entry near a realistic redemption threshold, so most readers with modest balances can look but not redeem.
- No women's/men's or category filtering — it's a flat scroll of makeup, hair, skincare, all at once, no visual hierarchy pointing to "what's redeemable for you."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or clarify the "You have 0 points" banner — if that's a real dynamic field, this email should never have sent to a 0-point account; if it's a placeholder bug, it undercuts the entire premise on every send.
- 2. Surface the actual point balance in the hero copy itself ("You have 2,450 points — here's what you can grab"), not just in the tiny top banner.
- 3. Sort or filter the grid by "redeemable now" vs "aspirational" so low-balance members see something achievable first.
- 4. Add a lower point-tier section (100–500 points) above the fold so this isn't exclusively a big-spender catalog.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your points can get you this today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `September rewards are almost gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See what your Beauty Insider points can unlock this month`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New rewards just dropped — limited quantities`
