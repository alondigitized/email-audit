# LOW PRICES + up to 85% off clearance won't be here forever 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's third "Lowest Prices of the Season" send since June 1 — and the campaign is visibly running on fumes. The subject's urgency hook ("won't be here forever") is a textbook move when brand managers know repeat-open rates are dropping; adding scarcity language to a five-day-old theme is patching, not refreshing.
- The email body itself is technically clean: no broken images, proper visual hierarchy, and the clearance badge lands hard in the hero. Below the fold it unfolds as a pure catalog blast — patio chairs, women's intimates, hair care, kids' clothing, men's casual, outdoor gear, home goods — all at different discount depths. That breadth is a hedge, not a strategy; it guarantees no single persona feels spoken to.
- The one genuinely fresh element is a Father's Day gifting module near the bottom, and that's the problem: sent June 6 with nine days to the holiday, that's the angle that earns today's send its reason to exist. It's buried behind six category modules when it should be the hero.

## What's working

- **Hero clarity:** "Lowest Prices of the Season" + "Clearance 85% off" badge establish the value proposition before the first scroll.
- **Clean render:** No broken images, overlapping text, or layout issues — the email displays correctly across the visible frame.
- **Kohl's Cash in the footer:** An incremental loyalty pull for members who are calculating total value, not just sticker discount.
- **Father's Day module:** Timely seasonal hook that genuinely differentiates this send from June 3, even if it's undersold by its placement.
- **Layered discount depths:** Mixing 50%-off and 40%-off sections creates a natural scroll pattern — the eye keeps moving expecting a better deal.

## What's weak

- **Campaign fatigue is baked in:** June 1 ("STARTS TODAY: Low, low prices"), June 3 ("Our LOWEST prices are coming in hot"), June 6 ("LOW PRICES...won't be here forever") — a subscriber who opened all three is now watching Kohl's cry wolf.
- **No hard deadline:** "Won't be here forever" is soft scarcity. No anchor date, no cutoff, no reason to act today instead of tomorrow.
- **ALL CAPS "LOW PRICES" in the subject:** Reads as a coupon insert, not a curated send. Weakens brand tone and trips mild spam heuristics.
- **Father's Day is buried:** The freshest reason to open this specific email is five scroll lengths down instead of in the hero.
- **No single product story:** The hero links to a category, not a product moment — nothing specific enough to earn an immediate, purposeful click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Promote Father's Day to the hero or second panel.** "9 days left to get him something good — up to 85% off" justifies June 6 on its own and stops this from reading as a June 1 rerun.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline in the body.** "Clearance prices end Sunday" changes the calculus for fence-sitters in a way that "won't be here forever" never will.
- 3. **Kill the ALL CAPS in the subject.** "Low prices + up to 85% off clearance won't be here forever" is already a fine subject line — the caps add nothing and cost brand equity.
- 4. **Collapse the category grid from seven to three.** Pick the highest-conversion categories for this list, give them room, and let the rest breathe in a secondary "more deals below" block.
- 5. **Move Kohl's Cash above the product modules.** It's a loyalty accelerator that drives cart behavior — show it to members before they decide whether to click through.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 85% off — 9 days left for Father's Day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Prices end Sunday: up to 85% off clearance + gifts for dad`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patio, home, kids, and dad-worthy gifts — all on clearance now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the lowest prices of the season before they're gone this weekend`

## 1. Overview

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Lowest Prices of the Season" (2026-06-06)

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail ESP build (Coherent Path / MessageIgnite stack) with no critical rendering blockers. Primary concerns are missing compliance signals in captured headers and pervasive missing `alt` text on tracking pixels and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.s.kohls.com` (ESP click-tracker). Not a bug, but means destination URL health is unverified for this send.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageIgnite open/render pixels (`o.gif`, `rp/*.png`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open pixel (Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern)

No broken pixel URLs detected. No issues with link structure itself, but the `<custom>` tag at line:

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```

contains an invalid `</custom>` closing tag with no matching open tag — this is malformed HTML and will generate a parse error in strict validators, though most email clients will silently ignore it.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 10+ images** per QA, including non-decorative content images:

| Image | URL Pattern | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/644bc273...` | Missing `alt` |
| Render pixel | `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...o.gif` | Missing `alt` |
| AAM sync | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=...` | Missing `alt` |
| Content images | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5a...png` (×5+) | Missing `alt` |
| Product image | `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/54462797_2s_2x` | Missing `alt` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (already present on the `o.gif` pixel — that one is correct). Content/product images need descriptive `alt` text for screen readers and images-off rendering.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Accessibility anti-pattern; violates WCAG 1.4.4.

**Google Fonts loaded via three mechanisms** (two `<link>` tags + two `@import` rules for the same families). Redundant — doubles font load requests in clients that support external CSS. Non-blocking but wasteful.

**`<title>` element is empty** — `<title></title>`. Most email clients ignore this, but it surfaces in some accessibility trees and tab labels.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE** — expected for broad email client compatibility; not a bug, noted for context.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent** from captured headers. CAN-SPAM does not mandate this header, but Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since 2024) require it for senders above 5K/day threshold. Kohl's volume almost certainly triggers this requirement. May be a relay-capture gap rather than a true omission — needs verification against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Same caveat: may be stripped by AgentMail relay. Gmail enforces this for bulk senders.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in captured HTML** — CAN-SPAM §7(a)(3) requires a valid physical postal address. The truncated source does not include it; likely present in the footer (below truncation). Needs confirmation against full HTML.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain `s.kohls.com` should be checked via DNS for DMARC policy alignment. Not confirmable from available data.
