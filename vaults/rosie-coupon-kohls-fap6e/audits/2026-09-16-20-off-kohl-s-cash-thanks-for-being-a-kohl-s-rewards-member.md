# 20% off + Kohl's Cash ... thanks for being a Kohl's Rewards member 🙌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's tenth-plus send since 09-10 and the tier-stack banner (30% Card/20% Rewards/15% Nonmembers) hasn't moved an inch — same layout, same colors, same copy skeleton as every prior send this cycle. What's actually new here is the "Cozy Fall Goals" home creative underneath, and the "Picked with you in mind" personalized product grid further down (Ninja Slushi machine, bath towel, jeans, mattress topper) — that's a genuinely different beat than the home-only focus of 09-12's send. But the top third is now pure fatigue: a subscriber who's opened three of these this week has already seen this exact status bar and tier stack, so the eye has nothing new to anchor to until scrolling past the fold.
- The subject line finally breaks from the generic "20% off" pattern by explicitly naming Kohl's Cash and thanking the recipient for Rewards membership — that's a small personalization win worth noting since most of this cycle's subjects have been interchangeable. But the body doesn't deliver on that personalized-thank-you framing until deep in the email; the top is identical tier-stack furniture.
- Net: solid execution on the fall-home creative and the "picked for you" grid, but this is clearly promo #10 in a saturated cycle, and a subscriber's inbox fatigue is a real drag on both open and click likelihood regardless of the creative quality.

## What's working

- The "Cozy Fall Goals" hero has a real seasonal hook with styled bedding imagery and a clear "Shop Home" CTA.
- The "Picked with you in mind" section shows actual priced products (Ninja Slushi $349.99, jeans $26-29.99, mattress topper $137.99-209.99) — concrete and specific, not generic banners.
- Category nav (Women/Men/Kids/Home) is clean and above the fold.
- Kohl's Cash coupon code and barcode are visible and usable without another click.
- No render bugs — images, text, and layout all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The top-of-email tier stack (30%/20%/15%) is now a verbatim repeat of nearly every send since 09-10 — zero visual differentiation from yesterday's or the day before's email.
- The subject's "thanks for being a Rewards member" personalization isn't reflected anywhere near the top of the email — it's generic tier-stack furniture instead of a personalized greeting.
- Email is extremely long (5+ screens) with NFL/NCAA gear, home goods, and random products all competing — no single hero offer.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands: details" — fine-print exclusion language sits right above the final CTA, undercutting the "savings started" close.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the personalized thank-you the subject promises — swap the generic tier-stack header for one line acknowledging Rewards status before the coupon stack, so the email delivers what the subject sold.
- 2. Cut the email length — NFL/NCAA gear and the four-tile "I want it all" grid dilute focus; pick one seasonal hero (fall home) and cut the rest to a single scroll.
- 3. Move the "Picked with you in mind" personalized grid up near the top — it's the most differentiated content in the email and it's buried past four other sections.
- 4. Vary the tier-stack visual treatment (color, layout, or copy) since this is its ~10th consecutive appearance — even a re-order of tiers would break the pattern-blindness.

- 63 tracking/click-redirect links present (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`), consistent with standard ESP click-tracking wrapping; not independently probed per QA notes.

- Tracking pixels lacking `alt` is low-impact (some already carry `alt=""` correctly, e.g. the `o.gif` and `event` pixels per source), but the hero product GIF missing `alt` is a genuine accessibility gap for screen readers.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and responsive `@media (max-width:500px)` block are present — no structural rendering issues found in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` query params on the primary tracking pixel appear to carry resolved subscriber/campaign IDs, not raw template tokens — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (QA warns this may be a relay/capture artifact of AgentMail rather than the sender's actual header).
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header not detected — same caveat.
- No physical mailing address detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this; cannot confirm absence vs. truncation without the footer content.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — sender authentication status unverified via this relay.
- These four findings are flagged WARN, not confirmed failures — the truncated source and relay-based capture limit certainty.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links use `mi_ecmp=1021870_2026916` and `email_name=260916_DG_Email_Wednesd...` style params rather than standard UTM (`utm_source`/`utm_campaign`) — this is CoherentPath's native attribution scheme, not a defect, but means landing-page UTM alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- Destination/landing URLs are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` redirects; final landing page not resolved in this data, so continuity to the actual product/offer page cannot be confirmed.

7. Recommendations
- Add `alt` text to the hero product GIF (`57448461_Product_fahm26_Home_Hero_GIF.gif`) at minimum; decorative tracking pixels can retain `alt=""`.
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers exist at the actual SMTP layer (not just via this relay capture) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.

- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly at the receiving MTA rather than relying on relay-forwarded headers.
