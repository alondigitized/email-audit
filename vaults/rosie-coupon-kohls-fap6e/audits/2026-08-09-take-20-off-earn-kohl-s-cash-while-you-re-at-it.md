# Take 20% off & earn Kohl's Cash while you're at it 💪
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the same reliable Kohl's production template that's been running since 7/30 — Rewards status pinned above the fold, a bold red 20% off coupon banner, a green Kohl's Cash strip, then category nav and a well-lit Under Armour Active Sale hero. No render bugs: no clipped starbursts, no broken art, tiles and nav all line up like every prior send in this run.
- Where it drifts from the norm is stacking: this send crams two full promo events (Active Sale up to 40% off, then a separate "Ultimate Clearance Event" up to 70% off) plus a "Picked with you in mind" product grid, plus a barcode-and-coupon-code block near the bottom. It's more email than usual, and the two big percentage callouts (20%, 40%, 70%) competing for attention actually dilute the hero rather than reinforce it.
- Technically clean, on-brand, nothing broken — but message hierarchy is muddier than the 8/5 Active Sale send, which had one clear headline offer instead of three stacked ones.

## What's working

- Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member | Earn: 5% Rewards") is pinned at the very top, same proven pattern as the 8/8 Rewards send.
- Hero art (Under Armour model) is sharp, well-lit, and the "UP TO 40% OFF" callout is legible against the red/black diagonal background.
- Coupon code, PIN, and expiration ("Ends Aug 16") are all visible without needing to click — reduces friction for a subscriber who just wants the code.
- Product grid ("Picked with you in mind") shows real prices, strikethrough original prices, and specific SKUs (Vanderbilt jeans, Serta mattress topper) — concrete, not generic.
- Nav buttons (Home/Women/Men/Kids) repeat twice, giving two clean exit points into shopping.

## What's weak

- Three competing percentage offers (20% site-wide, 40% activewear, 70% clearance) fight for the eye — there's no single "this is the offer" moment like the 8/5 Active Sale send had.
- Subject promises "20% off & Kohl's Cash" but the actual hero leads with Active Sale (40% off Under Armour/Nike), which isn't mentioned in the subject at all — a mismatch between what's promised and what's shown first.
- The bottom half (barcode, coupon code, T&Cs wall of text) is dense and utilitarian right after a colorful clearance banner — a hard tonal drop-off.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization claim is generic (towel, drink maker, jeans, mattress topper) — nothing here reads as actually tailored to a specific shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and lead the subject with it. Right now subject says "20% off + Kohl's Cash" but the visual hero is Active Sale — align them so the first thing a reader sees confirms what got them to open.
- 2. Cut from three promo tiers to two. Active Sale (40%) and Clearance (70%) back-to-back reads as noise; fold one into a secondary CTA lower down instead of a full banner treatment.
- 3. Move the coupon code/barcode block up near the hero — readers who already decided to shop have to scroll past two more promo banners to find it.
- 4. Make "Picked with you in mind" earn its copy — swap in items from the recipient's browse/purchase history if that data exists, or drop the personalization claim if it's a static grid.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 40% off activewear (Under Armour, Nike) + 20% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your coupon code is inside — plus Kohl's Cash on top`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Active Sale, Clearance, and 20% off — all before Aug 16`
