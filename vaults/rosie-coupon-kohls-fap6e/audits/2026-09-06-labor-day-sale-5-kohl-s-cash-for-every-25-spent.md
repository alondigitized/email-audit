# Labor Day Sale ☀️ $5 Kohl's Cash for every $25 spent 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is yet another Labor Day / $10-off-$25 / $5-Kohl's-Cash send — the sixth variant of this exact promo stack in a week (following 2026-09-03's "STARTS TODAY," 2026-09-04's flagship, and 2026-09-05's two near-duplicates). The hero is at least legible this time: LABOR DAY banner, $10 off $25 coupon, $5 Kohl's Cash callout, clean nav pills (Women/Men/Kids/Home). But past the hero it collapses into the now-familiar Kohl's kitchen-sink scroll — nine-plus category tiles (jeans, dresses, home decor, furniture, throws, bras, handbags), a Clearance banner, a "Picked with you in mind" grab-bag, then a second coupon reminder block at the bottom repeating the same $10/$25 and 15% home offers already shown above.
- The offer itself is real and stackable (own it), but the email has zero new creative energy versus the last four sends — it's the same modular template with reshuffled tiles. A subscriber who's opened three of these this week has already seen this exact value prop.
- Judgment: functional, on-brand, offer-honest — but fatigued creative and repetition make this one of the weaker sends in this week's run. 5.5/10 territory.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly with no layout bugs — coupon amounts ($10, $5) are large and legible at a glance.
- Category nav pills (Women/Men/Kids/Home) give an immediate scan path for someone shopping a specific department.
- Percent-off callouts (40% jeans, 30% dresses, 60% furniture, 85% clearance) are bold and easy to spot while scrolling.
- Diverse, real product photography (models, home goods, furniture) — no lorem-ipsum or broken images.
- Coupon codes and expiration dates are spelled out at the bottom (SHOPD, HOME15, Sept. 7/8-20) for people who scroll that far.

## What's weak

- This is the sixth near-identical $10-off-$25 + Kohl's Cash email in about a week — cadence fatigue is real and this send adds nothing new.
- The body is an undifferentiated grab-bag of 9+ unrelated categories (jeans, decor, furniture, bras, handbags, appliances) with no unifying theme or hierarchy after the hero.
- The $10/$25 and 15%-off-home coupons are repeated twice (once in hero, once at the bottom) — redundant rather than reinforcing.
- "Picked with you in mind" personalization language sits over generic best-sellers (blender, towels, mattress topper, jeans) with no visible personalization signal.
- Legal/coupon fine print block at the very bottom is dense gray text — not a bug, but reinforces this is a mass-blast template rather than a curated send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending this exact offer stack daily — consolidate to one strong Labor Day send with a hard deadline instead of five variations in seven days; if a second touch is needed, change the creative concept, not just the subject line.
- 2. Cut the body to 3-4 categories max and drop the redundant bottom coupon block — repeating the hero offer verbatim doesn't add urgency, it adds scroll fatigue.
- 3. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with an actual personalization hook (browsing history category, past purchase category) or drop the phrase — it currently reads as a lie.
- 4. Lead clearance (85% off) higher in the scroll since it's the strongest single number in the email but currently buried two-thirds down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance for Labor Day savings — $5 Kohl's Cash per $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the coupon, then the Cash — both expire this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, jeans, decor — all in on the Labor Day deal`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-generated (Kohl's/CoherentPath) transactional-marketing HTML with heavy tracking pixel usage; automated QA found unaddressed accessibility gaps and unconfirmed compliance headers, though these may be artifacts of the relay/probe environment rather than the live send.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary click-tracking domain: `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath ESP) wrapping a large opaque `cp_tp=v3.eJy...` payload token — standard redirect wrapping, not inherently a defect.
- Secondary open-tracking beacon: `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?GUZBTDIZZPQEJG7KIMFPK23UME.60262&d=60262&bmt=0` (1x1, standalone `<div>` — malformed `</custom>` closing tag present with no matching opening tag; minor HTML validity issue but won't affect rendering).
- Additional tracking pixels via `mi.kohls.com` (multiple `/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/*.gif` endpoints) and Adobe Audience Manager (`kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`).
- QA flags 67 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect-wrapped links; not a confirmed defect, just unverified reachability).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images/pixels flagged with missing `alt` attributes (QA findings), including the primary open-tracking pixel, the AAM event pixel, and 7 `mi.kohls.com` tracking pixels. These are all invisible tracking beacons (0x0/1x1), so the missing `alt` has no practical screen-reader impact, but strictly fails WCAG alt-text checks.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (`HTML 4.01 Transitional`) and MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) present — standard defensive pattern for Outlook desktop rendering, not a bug.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width: 500px` with corresponding `.hide-sm`/`.show-sm`/`.stack-sm-*` utility classes — mobile-responsive structure in place.
- Malformed markup: stray `</custom>` closing tag with no opening `<custom>` element (noted above under tracking).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp` query params present on tracking links — these are populated subscriber/campaign IDs, not raw tokens, indicating personalization resolved correctly at send time.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- QA: `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — required for bulk senders under CAN-SPAM/RFC 2369; absence may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than the original send, but cannot be confirmed from HTML source alone.
- QA: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.
- QA: No physical mailing address detected in the (truncated) HTML — CAN-SPAM requires a physical postal address in every commercial email. This could not be verified because the source was truncated before the footer; recommend confirming against the full HTML before treating as a confirmed violation.
- QA: `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this capture path.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query params observed on the visible links; Kohl's uses proprietary ESP tracking params instead (`mi_ecmp=1021288_202696`, `email_name=260906_DG_Email_Sunday`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pen...`). This is a vendor-specific analytics scheme, not a broken-continuity issue, but means standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't parse these links.
- Landing page destination cannot be verified — all sampled links resolve through the `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect wrapper with opaque tokens, and QA explicitly skipped HTTP probing on these 67 links, so final-destination/landing-page alignment is unconfirmed.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive or empty `alt=""` with `role="presentation"`/`aria-hidden="true"` explicitly on all tracking pixels (currently only the AAM pixel and open pixel have partial hidden attributes) to pass automated accessibility scans cleanly.
- Remove the orphaned `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) directly against raw SMTP headers from the original send, since AgentMail relay capture may not preserve them — do not treat as confirmed CAN-SPAM violations without that check.
- Verify presence of the physical mailing address in the untruncated footer HTML before flagging as non-compliant.
- If click-through attribution needs to integrate with standard web analytics, consider whether landing pages also register the `mi_ecmp`/`email_name` params or require UTM mapping downstream.
