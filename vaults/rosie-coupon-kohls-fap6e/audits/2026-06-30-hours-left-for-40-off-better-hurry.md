# HOURS LEFT for 40% off ... better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the companion "hours left" push to the morning "🚨 Score an extra 40% off TODAY ONLY 🚨" send that already ran on June 30 — Kohl's is running the exact same playbook they executed on June 28, when an "Ends today" email was followed a few hours later by an "HOURS LEFT" emergency email. The June 28 "HOURS LEFT" send scored 8/10 because it arrived at the tail of a legitimately-ending campaign; this one is structurally identical but is the second same-day urgency email, not the first — and arrives after thirteen days of near-daily deal blasts.
- The email itself is cleaner than it deserves to be. The 40% off hero is crisp, the lifestyle "Outfit your family without outrunning your budget" section gives the email an editorial feel that the pure-discount Deal Days sends lacked, and the clearance 70%-off section adds a second tier of value that is genuinely new relative to the morning send. The loyalty member ribbon and inline coupon code (OOOHOP40 + barcode) are executed well.
- The core problem is cadence credibility, not creative. After fourteen-plus emails in six days, "HOURS LEFT" reads as a template, not an emergency. A reader who bought on June 28 when they were told "hours left" and then saw another Deal Days email on June 29 and another on June 30 is trained to disbelieve this. The urgency well is dry.

## What's working

- The "Outfit your family without outrunning your budget" hero is a real value proposition headline — the best copy in the email and a departure from pure discount-speak.
- Loyalty member callout ("KOHL'S REWARDS | MEMBER · Earn 5% REWARDS!") anchors the top rail and gives members a reason to feel specifically addressed.
- Inline coupon code (OOOHOP40) plus printed barcode means the offer works in-store too — genuinely useful creative execution.
- The 70% clearance section is a genuine editorial addition that wasn't in the morning send — it creates a second reason to click beyond the coupon discount.
- Clean render throughout: no broken images, no layout breaks, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Free shipping is nowhere to be seen — the June 28 "Ends today" and "HOURS LEFT" sends both featured free shipping prominently; dropping it here is a quiet step backward that a conditioned Kohl's subscriber will notice.
- "HOURS LEFT" + ALL CAPS + running emoji is the same visual treatment as June 28's "HOURS LEFT ⏰" — when the template repeats verbatim, urgency evaporates.
- No personalization signal anywhere in subject, preheader, or hero — not even a first name or a "your member deal" hook.
- Four category nav buttons (Women / Men / Kids / Home) after both the hero and the clearance section dilutes the click direction: there's no single strongest CTA.
- The "TODAY ONLY" and "HOURS LEFT" framing competes with itself across two same-day emails — readers who got the morning send and now get this one learn the urgency is a broadcast template, not a genuine deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with what's new vs. the morning email.** The clearance 70% is real news — make it the subject, not the buried second section. The morning send owns "40% off." This send should own "70% off clearance, new markdowns just dropped."
- 2. **Drop "HOURS LEFT" from the subject.** It was used on June 28. Using it again on June 30 tells the reader the clock is fake.
- 3. **Restore free shipping.** The June 28 "Ends today" email featured "FREE SHIPPING" in the subject. Removing it here silently takes away a benefit subscribers have been conditioned to expect.
- 4. **Pick one CTA.** Either the 40% coupon or the clearance 70% — not both. Double offers reduce conversion by diffusing attention.
- 5. **Personalize the loyalty callout in the subject.** "Your Kohl's Rewards member deal: 70% off clearance" is infinitely more targeted than "HOURS LEFT for 40% off."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your member deal: up to 70% off clearance — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance markdowns + 40% off coupon, Ends June 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Activewear and more while the new markdown prices last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping when you use code OOOHOP40 in cart`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "HOURS LEFT for 40% off" (2026-06-30)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard promotional HTML email built on a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with table-based layout and MSO conditionals. The primary technical concerns are missing alt text on tracking pixels, unverifiable compliance headers via the AgentMail relay, and a malformed custom tag in the open-pixel markup.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all routes through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. HTTP status of destination URLs cannot be confirmed; broken redirects would be silent.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems in use simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears MTA pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

No functional issue, but the pixel stack adds ~3 network requests on open, all of which are 0×0 or 1×1 and aria-hidden/alt-less (see §3).

**Malformed tag in open-tracking block:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML. Most parsers will silently ignore it, but it is a template authoring error that could cause unexpected behavior in strict parsers or be flagged by inbox spam filters scanning for malformed markup.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text on all tracking pixels** — 9 images flagged by QA:

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/beeb3395...` |
| MTA pixel | `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` |
| AAM sync | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` |
| Render pixels (×4) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` |
| Additional render pixels (×3) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` |

Tracking/beacon images should carry `alt=""` (empty string) and `aria-hidden="true"` to be transparent to screen readers. Several are missing both. The AAM pixel and CoherentPath pixel are completely missing `alt` attributes.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 2.1 failure (1.4.4 Resize Text).

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>`. Some clients (notably Gmail on desktop) surface the email title; this leaves it blank, which can also slightly affect spam scoring.

**Legacy doctype** — `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is the correct choice for broad email client compatibility; no issue.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** — Roboto and Montserrat are each loaded twice (once via `<link>`, once via `@import url()`). This results in duplicate font HTTP requests. The `@import` method also blocks rendering in some clients. Only the `<link>` tag is needed.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%FIRST_NAME%`) visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` parameter in MessageGears pixel URLs confirms subscriber-level personalization is resolving at send time.

One potential gap: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` appears empty in the render pixel URL:
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
This may indicate an unpopulated dynamic field (a deal end date or event parameter). If this value is used for countdown logic server-side, the empty token warrants investigation.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not confirmed** — QA warns the header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. If absent at the SMTP layer, this violates Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements (mandatory for >5k/day senders). Must be verified against raw message headers, not the relay capture.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post not confirmed** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders. Same caveat — relay may not be surfacing it, but absence would be a deliverability risk.

**No physical mailing address detected in truncated HTML** — CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address. The HTML is truncated so this may appear in the footer; QA flagged it as absent. Needs confirmation against full source.

**Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status cannot be confirmed via the relay. Kohl's sends from `s.kohls.com` (a dedicated sending subdomain), so authentication infrastructure likely exists, but the relay is not surfacing the verification result. This is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sender issue.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 destination URLs are behind `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` redirectors, so final landing page URLs and UTM parameters cannot be inspected without following the redirect chain. QA skipped all 22.

**Risk:** If the CoherentPath or MessageGears redirect infrastructure is misconfigured, all links could silently 404 or land on wrong pages with no UTM attribution. Recommend manually clicking a sample of links (CTA, logo, footer nav) to confirm:
1. Redirect resolves to kohls.com (not an error page)
2. UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present on the final URL
3. Landing page offer matches the "40% off" subject line claim
