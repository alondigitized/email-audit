# ✨ Sephora App Fest ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the day-by-day App Fest calendar the brand history flagged back on 8/17 ("Sephora App Fest starts today") — same skeleton, now rebranded as "Yes, there are more deals!" and pushed to a second wave covering 8/25–8/28. Structurally it's dense and legible: a trial-size offer up top, a colorful "APP FEST" banner, then four stacked day blocks (Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri) each with its own brand lineup, discount badge, and countdown framing, followed by a Beauty Insider bonus-points challenge and a "Spot it. Shop it." trend grid.
- The problem is the same one the 8/17 send had: this is a calendar, not an offer. The hero doesn't lead with a number — it leads with "APP FEST" branding and "YES, THERE ARE MORE DEALS!", which is vague hype copy, not a hook. You have to scroll past the trial-size box and the banner just to find the first real discount (25% off Tuesday's Tarte/Dyson/Sephora Collection block), and even then it's segmented into four different day-specific offers that only apply on their specific date — meaning three-quarters of this email is not actionable the day it's opened.
- Verdict: competent production, repeated structural idea, weak single-offer clarity. This lands in the same mid-tier band as the 8/17 original — a 5-6, not higher, because the core weakness (no single clear CTA, offer fragmented across days) hasn't been fixed since the last time this format ran.

## What's working

- Clean day-by-day visual system — each day gets a distinct color block, badge, and brand logos, so scanning for "what's live today" is easy once you're in the email.
- The 25%/25%/10%+Afterpay progression across Tue–Thu gives a sense of escalating value without needing new copy each time.
- Beauty Insider "+500 Bonus Points" section is well-isolated with a clear purple callout and a single unambiguous CTA ("JOIN NOW").
- No render bugs — images, badges, and text all display cleanly with good contrast throughout.
- "Beauty Under $25" and "Spot it. Shop it." sections give lower-commitment browsing options for anyone not excited by the day-specific brand drops.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer — the top of the email is a trial-size upsell, then a branding banner, then a vague "more deals" line; the actual discounts don't appear until well into the scroll.
- Repeats the exact structural idea from the 8/17 "App Fest starts today" send (same day-block calendar format) — a subscriber who opened both in one week gets no new experience, just new brand names.
- Offers are date-locked and fragmented (Tue = 25% off Tarte/Dyson, Wed = 25% off Anastasia/MUFE, Thu = 10% off + Afterpay, Fri = Charlotte Tilbury early access) — if opened any day other than the current one, most of the email is irrelevant.
- Subject and hero copy ("Yes, there are more deals!") carry zero information — no percentage, no brand name, no urgency anchor visible before scrolling.
- Fine print is heavy ("Exclusions/terms apply" appears four separate times) which undercuts the "just shop and save" simplicity the layout is going for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with today's actual offer, not the calendar concept — e.g., swap "Yes, there are more deals!" for the Tuesday-specific "25% off Tarte, Dyson & Sephora Collection" so whoever opens it *today* sees an actionable number immediately, and demote the full week's calendar below the fold as a "see what's coming" strip.
- 2. Cut the trial-size box from the top — it competes with App Fest for the first-scroll real estate and isn't part of the App Fest narrative at all; move it below the fold or drop it from this send.
- 3. Consolidate the fine print into a single "Exclusions/terms apply to all App Fest offers, see below" line instead of repeating it four times — reduces visual noise without losing compliance coverage.
- 4. Differentiate this from the 8/17 App Fest email structurally — e.g., lead with a "your top 3 picks based on your Beauty Insider history" strip instead of a generic brand list, since the format itself is now a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off Tarte, Dyson & more — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `App Fest Week 2: today's 25% off drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stacks with other offers. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New brands drop daily through Friday 8/28`
