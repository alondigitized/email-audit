# Save on your faves: up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's second "25% off shampoos and conditioners" send in 48 hours — June 19 led with "Woo-hoo 🎉 Up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🫧," and this email recycles the same offer and category with marginally reshuffled copy ("Save on your faves"). The hero holds up: "EVERYDAY ICONS" is a clean anchor, the discount is legible above the fold, and the SHOP NOW button is clearly visible. That's where the wins end.
- Below the fold the email fragments badly — Sephora Collection Pro Brushes, a black jacket image that has no business being in a hair care send, a skincare module, an Insider enrollment block, and a loyalty CTA all compete for attention. It reads like a weekly digest that happened to pin shampoo at the top rather than a focused promotional send.
- The deeper problem is cadence. Two near-identical hair care offers 48 hours apart signals either poor list suppression or a campaign already running on fumes. Any subscriber who opened June 19 is reading a duplicate. That single fact caps this email's potential more than any creative fix.

## What's working

- Hero copy is clear and unfussy — "EVERYDAY ICONS / Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners" lands the offer in under three seconds.
- SHOP NOW CTA button is high-contrast and immediately tappable above the fold.
- Hair product imagery in the hero is on-category and unambiguous.
- "Earn 500" loyalty signal mid-email adds concrete member value on top of the discount.
- "#71 Best" ranking callout is a rare social proof moment in an otherwise offer-only send.

## What's weak

- Second identical hair care offer in 48 hours — opens from a real subscriber drop because this reads like a resend, not a new campaign beat.
- A black jacket appears in the lower email body — deeply confusing in a Sephora beauty context; no context, no caption, wrong category.
- Email sprawls across brushes, skincare, and the Insider program — dilutes the stated shampoo/conditioner focus and creates a cluttered scroll experience.
- No expiry date anywhere in the visible email — "up to 25% off" without a deadline gives subscribers permission to defer indefinitely.
- Subject line runs ~68 chars and will truncate on most mobile clients before "conditioners" is seen.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the repeat or reframe it as a last-chance send** — if the June 19 offer is still live, this should be "Final hours: 25% off hair care ends tonight," not a fresh creative re-send. Duplicating the hero without urgency framing reads as a mistake.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends Sunday" or a specific date in the subject and hero converts fence-sitters. No other change would lift revenue more immediately.
- 3. **Investigate and remove the jacket asset** — if it's a mislabeled file or wrong module, cut it. If it's a black styling tool in ambiguous packaging, reshoot it and add a label.
- 4. **Collapse to two content sections max below the fold** — keep the brushes upsell (logical cross-sell for hair care users), drop everything else to a single loyalty/Insider block.
- 5. **Shorten and sharpen the subject** — drop "select" and "save on your faves" framing; lead with the discount and a deadline.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** (line: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`) — blocks user-initiated zoom on iOS. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is flagged by Gmail's accessibility linter. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>` element** — the opening tag is `<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">` with no `lang="en"`. Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) cannot determine language for pronunciation. Add `lang="en"`.

- **`text-size-adjust: none` applied to nearly every element** — the selector `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td` globally disables font scaling. This suppresses the OS-level accessibility setting for users who rely on larger text. Scope this only to elements where auto-sizing actually breaks layout (typically just `td`).

- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is malformed** — the correct value is `noindex` (no space). While search engines don't index raw email HTML, some ESPs cache a web-view version; the malformed value means the intended no-index directive is silently ignored.

- **`<title>Sephora</title>`** — generic, not email-specific. Web-view and some clients display this as the tab/window title. Minor, but `Save on Your Faves | Sephora` would be more precise.
