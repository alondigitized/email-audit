# Save 20% ... go ahead, tell a friend ☺️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Same Friends & Family 20% coupon that's been running since 2026-08-24, now on its fourth appearance in three days — this send just leans harder into home/kitchen categories (cookware, Mingle & Co dinnerware, bedding/bath) instead of apparel. It's a well-built email visually — clean hero, clear category cascade, tight coupon/barcode close — but there's zero new offer logic here, just a re-skin of the same 20% + Kohl's Cash mechanic with a fresh hero image.
- The "tell a friend" subject line is the one genuinely different beat versus 8/24-8/25, but the body doesn't deliver on it — there's no referral link, share button, or friend-specific mechanic anywhere in the render. That's a subject-line/body mismatch that will cost opens on the next send if noticed.
- Solid execution, stale strategy. This is the same coupon Kohl's has now run four times in 72 hours with escalating creative fatigue.

## What's working

- Clear top-of-fold hierarchy: "Friends & Family — Take an extra 20% off with coupon" is unmissable, with "Ends Aug. 30" urgency directly under it.
- Kohl's Rewards Member status is called out at the very top ("Status: Kohl's Rewards Member · Earn 5% Rewards"), which is real personalization.
- Category buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home, Bedding/Bath/Decor/Storage) are large, high-contrast, and easy to tap.
- Coupon-in-wallet close with barcode, PIN, and expiration date visible removes friction for in-store shoppers.
- Sephora at Kohl's callout (25% off Beauty Insiders, "Today Only!") adds a distinct, time-bound secondary offer.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "tell a friend" but there's no referral mechanic, share link, or friend-focused copy anywhere in the body — pure bait-and-switch on the subject.
- This is the fourth near-identical 20%-off Friends & Family send since 8/24 with no new offer tier or escalation — pure creative reshuffle.
- Very long, scroll-heavy email (7+ full category sections) before reaching the actual coupon/barcode — buries the CTA a subscriber already knows by heart.
- "Elevate every room without the elevated prices" and "Proof you can teach an old house new tricks" are two different taglines fighting for the same home-goods space within a few screens of each other — diluted messaging.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject's "tell a friend" promise real — add an actual share/referral CTA in the body (e.g., "Share this code" button), or drop the friend-referral framing entirely since it's not delivered.
- 2. Move the coupon barcode/PIN block up near the hero — regular subscribers don't need 7 sections of category browsing before the thing they opened for.
- 3. Cut one of the two home-goods sections (cookware/dinnerware vs. bedding/bath/decor) — they're redundant in this send and add scroll fatigue.
- 4. Differentiate this from 8/24 and 8/25 sends with a category-specific escalation (e.g., "+ extra 10% off Home this weekend only") rather than the identical flat 20%.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Kohl's referral email uses a standard ESP tracking pipeline (Kohl's/AgentMail-relay) with heavy pixel tracking and MSO/media-query responsive markup; automated QA flags authentication-header visibility, missing physical address, and widespread missing alt text as the primary technical defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 82 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped for HTTP probing (domains: `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — link destinations could not be verified as resolving; recommend manual spot-check of a sample before send.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`, plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` opens — consistent with standard open-tracking/Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beaconing, no anomalies found.
- Open-tracking beacon `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?FHTVQH4ORFYELHWPBZP55RL6TM.60267&d=60267` is wrapped in a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag — invalid markup that some strict parsers/clients may mis-render (evidence: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images flagged with missing `alt` text, all tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, five `p/rp/*.png` opens, `14aad519-...` click pixel). These are non-content 1x1 pixels, so the absence of alt text carries low user-facing accessibility risk, but `alt=""` should be set explicitly for spec compliance rather than omitted.
- Malformed `</custom>` closing tag noted above (Section 2) is also a rendering/validation defect.
- Responsive CSS present (`@media (max-width:500px)` block with `.w_50pc`, `.w_60pc`, `.container`, etc.) and Outlook-specific MSO conditional blocks are correctly structured — no issues found in the provided source for mobile/Outlook handling.
- `<title></title>` is empty — minor, affects browser-preview tab display in webmail if HTML is opened directly; low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are present in the truncated source. Tracking URLs contain static/session identifiers (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `d_sid`) rather than unresolved merge fields — no issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules; may not have been captured by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent at origin, but cannot be confirmed from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (POST) support unconfirmed.
- **No physical address detected** in the provided HTML — CAN-SPAM requires a physical mailing address in every commercial email; not present in the truncated source (may be in the untruncated footer, not captured here).
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay path.
- Sending domain is `s.kohls.com` with tracking on `click.chp.kohls.com` / `click.s.kohls.com` / `mi.kohls.com` — consistent subdomain structure for authenticated bulk mail, but header-level auth couldn't be confirmed (see above).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query parameters are visible; tracking instead uses vendor-specific params (`mi_ecmp=1020560_2026826`, `email_name=260826_DG_Email_Wednesd...`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`) via the CoherentPath-style click-tracking platform. This is a proprietary tracking scheme rather than UTM — landing-page analytics attribution should be verified against this campaign ID format rather than assuming GA/UTM parsing.
- All primary CTA links route through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect wrapper (skipped by automated probe) — final landing destination and its parameter pass-through to the site could not be confirmed in this audit.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at origin (check raw SMTP headers outside the AgentMail relay) — required for Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender compliance.
2. Verify a physical mailing address is present in the full (non-truncated) footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.
3. Manually resolve a sample of the 82 skipped tracking links to confirm they redirect to valid, non-error landing pages.
4. Add explicit `alt=""` to the 9 flagged tracking-pixel images for markup/spec correctness.
5. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
6. Populate the empty `<title>` tag.
7. Independently verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication (e.g., via a direct mail-flow test) since Authentication-Results wasn't observable through this relay.
