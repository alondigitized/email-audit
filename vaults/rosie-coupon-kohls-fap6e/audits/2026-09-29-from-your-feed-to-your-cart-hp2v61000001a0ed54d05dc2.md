# From your feed to your cart 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a two-headed email. The top banner is a hard-offer hair promo ("Up to 50% off Hair Faves," "TODAY ONLY"). Right under it, the real body is a "Social Stars" editorial with a Cult Gaia fragrance hero and a 14-tile product grid. The subject, "From your feed to your cart," only describes the second half. Someone opening for the social-trend angle gets a strong offer in the banner they didn't expect. Someone who wanted a deal has to scroll past a large "SOCIAL STARS" block to find the rest.
- The product execution is decent. Prices are visible on every tile, "Only at Sephora," "Clean" and "Limited Edition" badges add some substance, and the Cult Gaia hero is attractive. But the email tries to sell hair deals, viral fragrance, BNPL, a Korean skincare block and Beauty Insider tiers in one send, so nothing owns the hierarchy. - The Cult Gaia hero is a clean, high-contrast image with the four price points listed under it.

- No broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a social-trend story, but the strongest content, 50% off hair, isn't mentioned in the subject or preview.
- There are too many competing modules: hair banner, Social Stars, Cult Gaia, the 14-tile grid, Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal, the 20% value-size hair block, Korean skincare and Beauty Insider. There's no single dominant CTA.
- "Shop Now" is repeated generically across four blocks, and the grid tiles carry no visible CTA.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text with several footnote stacks. The offer language ("up to," multiple daggers and exclusions) reads less clean than the banner suggests.
- The BNPL "Shop now, pay later" block and the Korean skincare hero feel like filler that dilutes the trend story.
- The brand's cadence this week is heavy: 9/28 Living Proof, 9/26 Hispanic Heritage, 9/25 New Beauty, 9/23 Hair Daily Deals. The hair offer here largely repeats that 9/23 beat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one story per send. Either make the 50% off hair deal the hero and the subject, or drop the top banner and run the Social Stars trend email cleanly. Right now the subject and the top banner point in different directions.
- 2. Put the deadline in the subject if the hair offer stays on top: "Today only" is the strongest urgency in the email and it isn't used.
