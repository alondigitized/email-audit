# $10 off your $25 purchase + Memorial Day Sale? Yes & yes 🙌
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's second consecutive Memorial Day send with the same $10-off-$25 offer — yesterday's subject read "Memorial Day Sale ✅ $10 off your $25 purchase ✅ Kohl's Cash ✅" and today's reads nearly the same thing with a tone shift to "Yes & yes 🙌." The campaign isn't escalating; it's repeating. For anyone who opened yesterday and didn't convert, this lands as a nudge. For anyone who opened and bounced, it lands as noise.
- The body is a classic Kohl's kitchen sink: 30% off women's, $24.99 intimates, 30% off jewelry, 50% off men's shirts, 30% off patriotic apparel, 40% off luggage, a home-purchase extra-15%-off strip, and then a full Sephora co-brand block mid-scroll. The stacking offer — $10 off + Kohl's Cash + Sephora points — is genuinely strong in aggregate, but the email fragments badly because it's trying to speak to every possible shopper in one scroll.
- The honest read: this is a solid promotional execution that's being undermined by back-to-back cadence and a subject line that isn't doing anything the May 21 send didn't already do. If you converted yesterday, you're annoyed. If you didn't, there's no new reason to act.

## What's working

- **$10 off $25 badge is visible immediately** — the offer threshold is low enough that almost any cart clears it, which is the right hook to keep prominent.
- **Memorial Day timing is airtight** — sending on May 22 with the holiday on May 26 gives the offer a credible implied deadline.
- **Kohl's Cash + Sephora stacking** — the co-brand block surfaces a genuinely differentiated mechanic (Beauty Insider point stacking) that Kohl's competitors can't replicate.
- **40% off luggage block** — timely post-holiday-weekend travel framing; the visual of a rolling suitcase reads the season well.
- **No visible render bugs** — images load, text is clean, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- **Consecutive send, same offer** — this is the same $10-off-$25 + Memorial Day anchor as yesterday's email. No price escalation, no new hook, no reason to reopen.
- **Subject line regression from May 21** — "Yes & yes 🙌" adds playfulness but subtracts specificity compared to yesterday's checkmark-structured version; it also runs longer (~58 chars vs. ~53).
- **Kitchen-sink layout dilutes intent** — six distinct category sections plus a Sephora co-brand block means no single CTA wins; the eye has nowhere to land after the hero.
- **Sephora block mid-scroll feels misplaced** — it interrupts the Kohl's apparel flow and reads like a separate email stapled on; the "Stacking Weekend" framing is under-explained for anyone not already in the Sephora ecosystem.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs are generic throughout** — no single CTA tells you what specifically you're shopping for.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero and commit to it.** The $10-off-$25 + Kohl's Cash + Sephora stacking is a genuinely unique three-way offer — lead with that mechanic as a single narrative rather than burying the Sephora block in the middle of six category rows.
- 2. **Add an explicit expiration date to the hero.** "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline but never states it. "Ends Monday 5/26" on the offer badge converts the urgency from implied to concrete.
- 3. **Cut 2-3 category sections.** Women's + luggage + home covers the core use cases; 50%-off men's shirts and the patriotic apparel block are filler that add scroll without adding conversion pressure.
- 4. **Upgrade the CTAs.** Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop 30% Off Dresses" or "Stack Your Kohl's Cash" — something that matches the specific section it's anchoring.
- 5. **Differentiate the subject from yesterday.** The brand already proved the $10-off-$25 hook yesterday; today's send needs a new angle (the Sephora stacking is that angle).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stack it: $10 off + Kohl's Cash + Sephora points this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day ends Monday — $10 off any $25 purchase`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn Kohl's Cash + Beauty Insider points in the same cart. Offer ends 5/26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Six categories, one coupon, three days left. Low bar to clear.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "$10 off $25 + Memorial Day Sale"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard HTML 4.01 table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and responsive media queries — structurally sound for broad client compatibility. Primary technical concerns are missing alt text on tracking/retargeting pixels, unverifiable authentication status through the AgentMail relay, and absent `List-Unsubscribe` headers in the captured headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped** by the HTTP probe — all routed through click-redirect domains (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`). This is expected behavior; no broken destination links were confirmed, but destinations could not be verified.

**Three distinct pixel/tracking systems detected:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath behavioral tracking pixel (1×1, no alt)
- `mi.kohls.com` — Moveable Ink personalization pixels (`o.gif`, multiple `rp/*.png`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open tracking (Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern)

No issues with the tracking infrastructure itself, but see Section 3 for alt text violations.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML. While browsers will silently discard it, this is a code hygiene issue that could cause unexpected behavior in strict parsers or future template tooling.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA scan. Breakdown:

| Image | Purpose | Alt Present |
|---|---|---|
| `f8ec39ec...` (chp.kohls.com) | CoherentPath pixel | No |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | Moveable Ink open pixel | No |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel | No |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` | Moveable Ink retargeting/personalization | No (7 images) |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string, explicitly set) and `aria-hidden="true"` per WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1. Most have neither attribute. The `o.gif` pixel does include `aria-hidden="true"` — that one is compliant. Others are not.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` — this blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure for accessibility-sensitive deployments.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>`. Screen readers announce the document title when an email is opened in a browser tab or certain accessible clients. An empty title is a minor but real accessibility gap.

**Google Fonts double-loaded:** Roboto and Montserrat are loaded via both `<link>` tags and `@import` inside a `<style>` block simultaneously. This causes two network requests per font family, adds render latency, and has no benefit.

### 5. Compliance

Kohl's volume far exceeds that threshold. The header may exist at the MTA level and simply not be relayed through AgentMail, but it cannot be confirmed from this capture.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header — not captured** (QA WARN). RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by the same Google/Yahoo policy. Same caveat — may exist upstream.

**SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication — unverifiable** (QA WARN). `Authentication-Results` header not present in the relay capture. Sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (subdomain), which typically carries its own alignment records. Cannot confirm pass/fail from available data.

**`robots` meta tag present:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` — correct and expected for email HTML. No issue.

**`referrer` meta present:** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` — suppresses referrer on outbound clicks, which can cause UTM attribution to fail in GA4 (see Section 6).

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not visible in the truncated source. Cannot confirm presence or absence; should be verified in the full footer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**`no-referrer` policy conflicts with UTM attribution.** The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag instructs browsers not to pass a `Referer` header on navigation. If any click-redirect links forward without appending UTM parameters directly to the destination URL, GA4 will record the session as direct traffic rather than email. This is only safe if every destination URL carries explicit UTM parameters in the click-redirect chain — which cannot be confirmed without probing the 22 skipped links.

**`email_name` parameter present** on several Moveable Ink pixel URLs: `email_name=260522_DG_Email_Friday`. This is consistent internal naming but is not a UTM parameter and will not flow into GA4. Confirm that click-through URLs append `utm_campaign`, `utm_source=email`, and `utm_medium=email` explicitly.
