# 20% off 🛍️ $10 off your $50+ purchase 🛍️ Styles your closet needs 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's sixth straight send leading with the 20% off plus $10 off $50+ stack, after Oct 26–30. Today's subject adds "Styles your closet needs" to the same two-offer formula. It is a long, busy email. The top hero looks cropped, with the "% off" text clipped, and the "Cozy style well with…(Especially" headline is cut off. The eye has to work to find the offer.
- The email does include real, useful content: three coupon barcodes at the bottom, free returns through Jan 31, 2027, and Kohl's Cash earning details. The apparel lead (kids' loungewear at $11.99 with 20% off, sweaters, boots) is mixed with home and appliance picks, so the "closet" subject promise is only partly kept. The "North Kohl's" theme from Oct 28 and Oct 31 is still the creative wrapper.
- My judgment: a competent but fatigued promo. It would do fine for a loyal Kohl's shopper, but it adds nothing new after six days of the same stack.

## What's working

- The Rewards status and "Earn 5% Rewards" strip at the top acknowledges membership.
- The Kohl's Cash banner sits near the top, with a visible "Ends Nov. 1 · Shop Now" deadline.
- A specific price anchor appears: "Starting at $11.99 with 20% off coupon, Reg. $14.99–$54.99."
- The three barcodes (SAVE20, GOSHOP10, JEWELRY10) at the bottom make the offers redeemable and clear.
- The free-returns and same-day delivery block reduces purchase friction.

## What's weak

- The hero "off" text is clipped and partly hidden behind the "+" graphic, so the main 20% offer is hard to read at the top.
- The "Cozy style well with… (Especially" headline is truncated mid-phrase, which looks like a layout bug.
- The email is very long, with many unrelated sections (festive home, appliances, jeans, towels), and the "closet" promise gets diluted.
- The apparel hero shows a child in a robe. That has little connection to a "styles your closet needs" subject for an adult shopper.
- Coupon codes and fine print sit at the very bottom, so the stack is not explained clearly up top. The "$10 off $50+" offer is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Show "20% OFF + $10 OFF $50+" as clean, unclipped text above the fold, with one CTA button ("Shop 20% off").
- 2. Cut the home and appliance sections, or move them to a separate send. Keep the email focused on the apparel that the subject promises.
- 3. Move the coupon codes higher, right after the hero.
- 4. Break the repetition. Lead with a new beat, such as a weekend deadline or a category-specific hook, instead of the same stack for a sixth day.
- 5. Repair the truncated "Cozy style…" headline or remove it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has several structural defects: body-level markup inside `<head>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. It also has widespread missing `alt` attributes and four stacked tracking pixels. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and the 71 redirect links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on 71 tracking links (click-redirect domains). Destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed. Links use opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im…` tokens.
- **Four tracking beacons before any content:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9c04ece5-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`, a third-party Adobe Audience Manager call
- **Empty parameter:** One image URL contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The empty value suggests an unpopulated dynamic field.
- **Inconsistent parameters:** The first four `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs carry `email_name=261031_DG_Email_Sat_PM`. The later ones (`69494b48…`, `78308d1d…`, `916d6fa0…`) don't, so tracking is inconsistent across modules.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` ends with a stray `</custom>` closing tag that has no opening tag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will close the head early and may push later head content into the body.
- **Empty head elements:** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** Nine images lack `alt`, and the QA list is truncated, so there may be more.
  - Content images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d…`, `c2f81e9a…`, `415c8a18…`, `69494b48…`, `78308d1d…` and `916d6fa0…`. With images blocked, these render as blank space or filename text.
  - Tracking pixels: the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `kohls.demdex.net` pixels also lack `alt`, and the first has no `aria-hidden`. Screen readers may announce the filename.
- **Table semantics:** The pixel-wrapper table (`width="1" height="1"`) and the AAM table have no `role="presentation"`, though the main layout tables do.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with XML-style syntax and `role` attributes. This is low severity.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded twice, by `<link>` and by `@import`. Fallback stacks are present, so this is a minor performance issue only.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source.
- `mi_u=604230016` is a subscriber ID embedded in image URLs. This is expected, but it makes the image URLs user-identifying.
- The empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value (see section 2) is a possible dynamic-field failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are flagged missing. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** Missing for the same relay reason, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **Physical address:** Flagged as not detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed either way.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. Attribution relies on `qs=` tokens and `mi_*` and `email_name` parameters.
- Because the links are wrapped redirects that were not probed, I can't confirm where they land or whether the landing pages match the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>`. Use JSON-LD in the body or drop it.
3. Delete the stray `</custom>` tag. Fill in `<title>` and remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all seven content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). Add `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` to the tracking pixels, and `role="presentation"` to the pixel-wrapper tables.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels, and review the third-party `demdex.net` call for privacy and consent.
6. Check the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter in the ESP template, and apply `email_name` consistently across modules.
7. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` headers and the full, untruncated HTML. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the physical address and the unsubscribe link. Probe a sample of the 71 redirects to check landing pages and UTM continuity.
