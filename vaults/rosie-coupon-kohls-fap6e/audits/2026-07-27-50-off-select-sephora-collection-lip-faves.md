# 50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Same subject/body mismatch playbook Sephora has run all week. The subject promises "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves," and the top banner does open with "Get 50% off a Select Sephora Collection" — so far so good — but that's the entire lip-faves payoff. Everything below the fold is a completely different story: a new-fragrance launch (RUHVEDA, INFLUXIOUS), a nine-item new-arrivals grid spanning blush, body butter, hair mist, lip liner, BB cream, scalp toner, and gloss, then app-download and 4X-points promos. "Lip faves" gets one small product image in the hero collage and never resurfaces.
- This is the fourth straight audited Sephora send (7/24 sunscreen, 7/25 hair, 7/25 lip/hair, 7/26 lip) where the subject's specific promise is used as a hook and then abandoned for a generic multi-category dump. The pattern itself is now the story — a subscriber opening this expecting a curated lip sale lands on a fragrance launch instead, for the second time in two days.
- The offer is real and the top banner executes it cleanly (bold %, clear CTA, National Lip Day tie-in, visible product shots), so this isn't broken — it's just misdirected volume dressed as a targeted deal.

## What's working

- Hero banner delivers on the subject immediately: "Get 50% off a Select Sephora Collection" with legible lip-product photography (lipstick, gloss, liner) right up top.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA is bold, high-contrast, and appears twice in the first two sections.
- "Just Landed at Sephora" fragrance section has strong visual identity — clean product bottles, "Black Owned at Sephora" badge adds credibility/discovery value.
- New-arrivals grid is well organized with consistent NEW/CLEAN/exclusivity badges and prices visible on every tile.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject says "lip faves" (plural, curated); the email delivers one lip banner then pivots entirely to fragrance and a nine-item generic new-arrivals dump — most of which isn't lip product at all.
- The 50% off offer has no visible expiration or urgency cue beyond "Today only" in tiny text under the CTA — easy to miss on a skim.
- Nine-product grid with zero curation narrative (just "NEW" repeated) buries the actual lip SKUs (Urban Decay Lip Toy liner, Fenty Gloss Bomb) among blush, body butter, and scalp toner — a subscriber who opened for lip deals has to hunt.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the second lip-themed subject line in three days (7/26 "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves") with a near-identical bait-and-pivot structure — the list is starting to feel automated rather than curated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the body to match the subject: after the 50% hero, show 4-6 actual Sephora Collection lip SKUs with prices/swatches instead of pivoting to fragrance — if fragrance and new arrivals need to ship this week, give them their own send.
- 2. Add a real countdown or explicit end-time ("Ends tonight at midnight PT") next to the CTA instead of a footnote-sized "Today only."
- 3. If curation isn't feasible, retitle the subject to match what's actually inside ("New fragrance drops + this week's must-haves") rather than promising lip specificity the email doesn't deliver.
- 4. Move the Urban Decay and Fenty lip products (already in the grid) up near the hero banner so the "lip faves" payoff is visible without scrolling past fragrance and skincare.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Sephora Collection lips`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lip faves are 50% off — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the lip liners, glosses and stains everyone's stocking up on.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `National Lip Day markdowns, live for today only.`
