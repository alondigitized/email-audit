# Your personal holiday shopper, inside ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send in a row for the Sephora Savings Event, after "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍" earlier today and "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊" on 10/30. This time the subject sells the AI Beauty Chat feature, not the discount. The gold "30% OFF" medallion with code ALLONSALE still sits at the top, labeled "HAPPENING NOW". Subject and body then pull in different directions.
- The body is a long stack of color blocks: gold, purple, green, then gold again. Each block carries its own headline and CTA: "TRY IT," "LEARN MORE," "SHOP NOW." The AI chat phone mock-up is the creative centerpiece, and it is the strongest idea here. It shows real products with prices (a $100 Kayali set, a $499 Dyson) and answers a relatable prompt: "She's always on the go but never checks a bag. Any gift recs?" The deal then gets buried. Beyond the top block, the discount is only a footer barcode and dense fine print.
- Verdict: a polished, seasonally right email with good tooling on show. A subscriber who got the 30% off email hours ago gets little new from it, and the subject gives no reason to open.

## What's working

- The "HAPPENING NOW" banner and 30% OFF medallion put the offer, dates (10/30–11/9) and code ALLONSALE first.
- The AI Beauty Chat mock-up demonstrates the feature with real products and prices instead of just describing it.
- The "She's always on the go but never checks a bag" bubble speaks to gift-shopping and reads as human.
- The "$35 and Under Gift Sets" block gives a concrete price anchor and a "LIMITED EDITION" urgency cue.
- The category pills (Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Hair Gifts) give a clear way into the catalog.
- "Hi, Rosie" with the Insider tag and the in-store barcodes for 30% off and 10% off carry the loyalty benefit.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a "personal holiday shopper" but never mentions 30% off, the most compelling thing in the email.
- Three stacked color blocks (gold, purple, green) each push a different CTA. "TRY IT," "LEARN MORE" and "SHOP NOW" compete, with no single primary action.
- The "Beauty on Demand" and "Shop Gifts Now" blocks are thin. The Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal/Venmo row is payment-options filler with no product.
- The legal copy and barcodes at the bottom are very long, and the deal terms are small and hard to read.
- Nearly every headline is generic ("Get Gift Ideas Instantly," "Beauty on Demand"), with no hero product linked.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the subject, then use the AI chat as the reason to click. Lead with the 30% off, since subscribers have already seen three sends about it.
- 2. Cut the purple "Beauty on Demand" and green payment-options blocks, or fold them into one slim strip. That shortens the email and raises the share of attention on the chat demo and the $35-and-under sets.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA, such as "Ask the Beauty Chat," in a consistent button style. Make the others secondary text links.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy, for example "Shop gifts under $35" instead of "SHOP NOW."
- 5. Put a countdown or "Ends 11/9" line next to the CTA. The current deadline sits only in the top medallion.
- 6. Move the barcode higher, or add a "Show barcode in store" button near the top, and shrink the legal text.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Describe who you're shopping for. Get picks with 30% off, code ALLONSALE.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, fixed 700px Epsilon/Harmony send. Its main confirmed defects are an empty first-name merge slot (`Hi ,`) and a missing plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication findings come from headers the relay may not have captured, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect tracking:** Links are wrapped in Sephora/Epsilon click-trackers, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a192b425a591fa326e96c569c8/c72011c4fa5647ff0000021ef3a0bcc2/...?__dU__=...`. The final destination URLs are not visible in the truncated source.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 39 material links were probed (27 skipped). The QA data confirms nothing about the skipped links, so their status is unknown.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...'>` is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`).
  - QA flags it for missing `alt`.
  - It has no `width`/`height` attributes.
  - Because it is hidden, the accessibility impact is negligible. It should still carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` for validity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback:** `[FAIL]` The text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability, and clients or users that need text-only get nothing.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. `min-width:700px` forces horizontal scrolling on clients that ignore or strip the `@media (max-width:480px)` rules (e.g. Gmail non-Google accounts, some Android clients).
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom where it is honored, which is an accessibility regression.
- **Invalid CSS/HTML:**
  - `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments inside CSS rules are not valid there, and they risk breaking the following rules (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, etc.) in some parsers.
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` uses an invalid value. The directive is `noindex`. It also has no practical effect in email.
- **JSON-LD:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block includes `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. The empty code is an unpopulated template field, and `PromotionCard` is not a valid schema.org markup type for Gmail actions. Scripts are also stripped by most clients, so the block adds nothing.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is minor.
- **Missing alt:** Only the tracking pixel was flagged. No other missing-alt issues are confirmed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting:** `[FAIL]` The greeting renders as `Hi ,` with an empty name field. The merge field has no fallback value, so the recipient sees a broken salutation. This is a confirmed rendering bug.
- **JSON-LD `discountCode: ""`:** Another empty template slot. It is invisible to recipients.

2. **Add a plain-text part:** Generate a multipart/alternative text version with the CTA URLs and unsubscribe link.
3. **Verify the raw headers:** Check the original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating the compliance warnings as real.
4. **Make the layout responsive:** Remove `min-width:700px` and use `max-width:700px; width:100%` (with an MSO ghost table if needed).
5. **Clean up the head:**

   - Fix `no index` to `noindex`, or drop the tag.
   - Remove the JSON-LD with its empty `discountCode` and invalid `PromotionCard`.
6. **Tidy the tracking pixel:** Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
