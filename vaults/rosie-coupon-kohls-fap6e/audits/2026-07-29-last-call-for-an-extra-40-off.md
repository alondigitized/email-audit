# 📞 LAST CALL for an extra 40% off 📞
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the second "40% off" send from Kohl's on July 29 alone — brand history already logged "Extra 40% off + Kohl's Cash? Better hurry" earlier the same day at 8.00/10, and this "LAST CALL" version is running the identical 40%-off-with-coupon mechanic, same expiration date, same visual template. As a recipient who already saw that email today, this reads as a duplicate nudge rather than a distinct communication.
- On pure execution, though, it's clean. The hero renders intact — no clipped starburst, no garbled percentage badge like the July 27/28 sends — with "40% off" large, centered, and legible, plus a visible coupon code (SAVE40, PIN 5345) and barcode further down. The activewear cross-sell (Nike/adidas/Under Armour, priced at $8.99 and $7.79) and the category grid are well laid out with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The email is technically solid but strategically repetitive. Sending the same offer twice in one day under a "last call" framing risks training subscribers to ignore the first send and wait for a better-worded follow-up — or worse, to feel spammed.

## What's working

- Hero is fully intact and legible: "40% off" is the largest element on the page, centered, with a clear "Shop Now" CTA — a real continuation of the fix seen in the July 29 morning send.
- Coupon code, PIN, and barcode are all visible and usable without opening a separate wallet screen.
- Rewards member status ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER," "Earn: 5% REWARDS") is shown right under the logo, reinforcing loyalty value.
- Kohl's Cash mechanic ($10 per $50, Aug 2–16) is clearly stated near the footer for shoppers planning ahead.
- No render bugs anywhere in the scroll — clean category tiles, clean product photography, consistent brand red/maroon palette.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of the same-day "Extra 40% off + Kohl's Cash? Better hurry" send — identical offer, identical deadline, same day. Poor cadence discipline.
- "View this email in browser" text sits directly next to the preview copy at the very top, adding friction/junk before any real content.
- Primary "Shop Now" CTA routes to a generic shop, not a specific category, despite the persona-relevant activewear section directly below it.
- No sizing, fit, or availability detail on any featured product — just price and "Select styles."
- No social proof (reviews, ratings) anywhere in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the duplicate send. If both "Extra 40% off" emails must go out the same day, differentiate them by audience segment (e.g., non-openers of the first send only) rather than blasting the full list twice.
- 2. Make the primary CTA specific — swap the generic "Shop Now" in the hero for something tied to the top-performing category (e.g., "Shop Activewear") since that's the section immediately below it.
- 3. Move "View this email in browser" out of the primary preview real estate so the actual preheader copy isn't sharing space with junk.
- 4. Add one line of urgency math to the hero (e.g., "on $50, that's $20 back") to make the 40% concrete rather than abstract.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ Your 40% off coupon expires today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it with Kohl's Cash before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Activewear, home, beauty — all 40% off today only`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Email Audit — Kohl's "LAST CALL 40% off"

## 1. Technical Summary
Legacy table-based HTML4.01 ESP template with four stacked open/click-tracking systems (Coherent Path/Cheetah Digital, Adobe Audience Manager, two Kohl's-hosted pixel endpoints). Source contains one malformed-markup defect and one blank tracking parameter; authentication and unsubscribe headers were not observed by automated checks.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Four distinct tracking mechanisms fire on open/click: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (Coherent Path redirect pixel with encrypted `cp_tp` payload), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (Kohl's open-tracking pixels), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (secondary open pixel), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager).
- QA: 62 tracking/click-redirect links skipped from HTTP probing (expected behavior for wrapped redirect URLs — destinations not independently verified).
- Malformed markup at the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel: `<img ... alt=""></custom>` — a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener. Invalid HTML; likely swallowed silently by renderers but indicates a template-generation bug.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images flagged missing alt text by QA. Evidence: the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel, the `demdex.net` pixel, and 5× `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all — inconsistent with the `o.gif` pixel, which correctly uses `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. For 1×1 tracking pixels, empty `alt=""` (not omission) is the accessible pattern; recommend aligning all pixels to that convention.
- Redundant font loading: Roboto/Montserrat are loaded both via `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">` and again via `@import` inside a `<style>` block (both wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`). Most clients that respect one will already have loaded via the `<link>` tag; the `@import` is dead weight in every client that matters here.
- `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with `itemprop` meta tags sits inside `<head>` — invalid placement (a `div` is not permitted in `<head>`), and schema.org markup has no function in an email (no crawler consumes it). Combined with `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` (also meaningless in email context), this reads as unedited boilerplate carried over from a web-page template.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge syntax (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[%...%]`) found in the visible source.
- One pixel URL contains a blank parameter: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` (redemption end date) in `.../916d6fa08d185233.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1018633_2026729&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe`. Given the "LAST CALL" expiring-offer framing, an empty expiration value is worth confirming against the ESP campaign config.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found (QA WARN). May reflect the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than sender omission — not independently confirmable from available data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found (QA WARN). If genuinely absent, this fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status unknown from available data.
- Physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM requirements): not visible — the provided HTML is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters appear anywhere in the visible markup. Kohl's uses proprietary Coherent Path/Cheetah Digital parameters instead (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `cp_tp`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`), consistent with ESP-native attribution rather than GA/UTM tagging.
- CTA destination URLs and any post-redirect landing pages are not present in the truncated source — landing page alignment cannot be evaluated from available evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
- Fix the orphaned `</custom>` tag near the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
- Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com`, `demdex.net`, and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels to match the `o.gif` pixel's pattern.
- Drop the redundant `@import` font loading since `<link>` tags already request the same fonts.
- Remove or relocate the schema.org Organization block and the `noindex`/`referrer` meta tags — non-functional cruft in an email context.
- Confirm whether the blank `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` parameter is expected or a misconfigured merge field.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers outside the AgentMail relay before treating these as sender-side compliance gaps.
- Request the full (non-truncated) HTML to verify CAN-SPAM footer requirements and CTA/UTM landing-page alignment.
