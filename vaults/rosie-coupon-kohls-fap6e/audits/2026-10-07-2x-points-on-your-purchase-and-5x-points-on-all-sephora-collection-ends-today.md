# 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection ends today❗
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest of the recent Sephora sends because it does what the subject says. The top of the email is a big red "ENDS TODAY" over a huge "2X INSIDER" and a "USE CODE SAVEPOINTS" button. It is the same offer as the 10/6 "ends tomorrow" send, now with real last-day urgency. A P.S. strip adds free same-day delivery with code DELIVERED, ending at 1pm PT.
- The second half goes back to the usual Sephora catalog. Product tiles for Tom Ford, amika, Summer Fridays, EADEM, Jo Malone and Versace carry points badges (810, 88, 48, 48, 350, 276). Below that are Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal financing, a Holiday Drops block, a Gift of Beauty block, a "Spend More, Get More" sample block and Beauty Insider tier tiles. The points badges are a good idea, but this is a very long scroll for a single-day deadline.
- My judgement: a strong, clear top block with a weak, bloated tail. The sale fine print is also hard to read.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TODAY" banner and the oversized 2X give a clear offer and credible urgency in the first screen.
- The "USE CODE SAVEPOINTS" button has the code on it, so there is no hunting for it.
- The points badges on each product tile (810 points on Tom Ford, 350 on Jo Malone) make the reward concrete in each product's own terms.
- The "See What Your Points Can Do" tiers (100+, 250, 300 and 750+ points) show shoppers what they can redeem.
- The cardmember callout ("Earn an extra point per dollar") speaks to loyalty members.

## What's weak

- The subject says "ends today", but the Holiday Drops, Gift of Beauty and Beauty Insider tiers pull attention away from the one deadline.
- The P.S. same-day-delivery strip (code DELIVERED, 1pm PT) is a second offer with its own deadline, which competes with SAVEPOINTS.
- The "Last chance" product grid shows no product prices or names in several tiles, and the Tom Ford tile at $405 reads as a luxury pick, not a broad one.
- Dense, tiny legal text fills the bottom third.
- The "use at Kohl's" barcode and code block at the very bottom adds clutter after the legal text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the product grid except one secondary CTA. A last-day email should end at the deadline.
- 2. Fold the delivery offer into the hero or drop it, so the email has one code.
- 3. Add one more button after the product grid, such as "Shop 2X points now", so scrollers get a second CTA.
- 4. Put the points badge and price on every product tile in the same format.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2X points on everything, 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: use SAVEPOINTS for double points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVEPOINTS, plus free same-day delivery until 1pm PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your points are worth more until midnight. Here's what to grab.`
