# 40% off ENDS TONIGHT 🕛
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the night-close on a day Kohl's already sent one email — the earlier "TODAY ONLY: 40% off⏳" audited at 7/10. The subject upgrade from "TODAY ONLY" to "ENDS TONIGHT 🕛" is the only material change; the body creative is structurally identical. That's the central tension here: the urgency close is the right instinct, but nothing in the email itself escalates from the morning send. A subscriber who opened both learns nothing new.
- On its own terms, the email executes cleanly. The 40% badge dominates the hero, Kohl's Cash appears twice (hero and footer), no images are broken, and the product spread — outdoor furniture, kids' water toys, kitchen appliances — covers the Father's Day gift-buying mindset sensibly. The closing CTA block ("Take an extra 40% TODAY ONLY") in the orange-highlighted strip is the strongest copy in the email, and it lands late.
- The campaign has now run for roughly ten days with near-identical offer cadence. This send earns its urgency on the day; it doesn't earn its place as a second email.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TONIGHT" subject is a genuine step up** from the morning send's "TODAY ONLY" — time-to-midnight is more urgent than time-within-day.
- **40% badge in the hero is dominant** — visual hierarchy is unambiguous; the offer reads before any other element.
- **Kohl's Cash surfaced twice** (hero panel and footer strip) — this matters for the loyalty-motivated repeat buyer who thinks in terms of stacking.
- **"Something for everyone (even the picky ones)"** category grid is a smart gift-mode reminder on the day itself.
- **Clean render** — images load, no layout breaks, no lorem-ipsum or merge-tag leaks visible.

## What's weak

- **Body is a copy of the morning send** — identical badge, identical category pods, identical "Shop Now" CTAs. A second daily send with no incremental offer or new product angle is punishing for inbox fatigue.
- **Four product pods across four unrelated categories** — outdoor → kids' pool → kitchen blenders → kitchen storage reads as a clearance blast, not a curated gift guide.
- **Every CTA says "Shop Now"** — no category noun attached. "Shop Outdoor," "Get Kitchen Deals," "Shop Kids' Swim" would reduce decision paralysis.
- **No anchored product price** — showing one concrete before/after ("Was $89.99, now $53.99") does more persuasive work than a standalone percentage badge.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate the PM send with a real hook** — "Here's what's selling fast before midnight" with 3–4 specific trending items from the morning's top categories would give openers a reason to act rather than a reason to skip. The morning send should establish, the night close should close.
- 2. **Cut to 2 categories, go deeper** — two product pods with 2–3 specific SKUs each outperforms four shallow pods. Depth creates urgency; breadth creates scrolling.
- 3. **Replace generic CTAs** — every "Shop Now" should become "Shop [Category] Deals." One verb + one noun minimum.
- 4. **Add one anchored price** — a single "Was $X / Now $Y" example converts the abstract percentage into a felt saving.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends at midnight — don't miss it 🕛`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off everything at Kohl's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outdoor, kitchen, kids — all 40% off until tonight only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash on every $50 spent — expires tonight`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off ENDS TONIGHT" (2026-06-21)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail ESP build (Coherent Path + Marigold/MessageMedia stack) with no critical rendering blockers. Primary technical concerns are missing compliance elements and pervasive missing alt text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (Coherent Path) and `click.s.kohls.com` (MessageMedia/Marigold). This is expected behavior for a dual-ESP setup; redirect chain validity could not be confirmed by automated checks.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems active simultaneously:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — Marigold open/unique pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — MessageMedia open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync pixel

Four independent open-tracking systems firing on render is unusually high and may inflate open-rate metrics if any system double-counts. The AAM pixel (`demdex.net`) also performs cross-site identity resolution on open, which carries GDPR/CCPA exposure if consent logic is upstream-only.

**Malformed tag detected:**
```html
<img src="...open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The stray `</custom>` closing tag after the tracking pixel is invalid HTML. Most parsers will ignore it, but it will surface as a markup validation error.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on 10 images** confirmed by QA, including all six content image tiles served from `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`:
- `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`

Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, open pixel) appropriately have `aria-hidden="true"` or empty `alt=""` — those are not issues. The content images missing alt text will render as broken image icons in image-off environments (e.g., Outlook default) with no fallback text.

**`<title>` tag is empty:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the title on email load; an empty title degrades accessibility in some clients.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — an accessibility regression for low-vision users and a violation of WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

**Google Fonts loaded three ways** (two `<link>` tags + `@import` inside `<style>`). The triple-load is redundant and adds unnecessary HTTP round-trips on supporting clients. Non-supporting clients (Outlook, Gmail app) will fall back to `Arial`/`Helvetica` correctly.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

One image URL contains an unfilled or conditionally empty merge token:

```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
(`916d6fa08d185233.png` — trailing `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` has no value)

This suggests a dynamic expiry date or event parameter was not populated for this recipient segment. If this image is a countdown timer or conditional banner, it may render incorrectly or pull the wrong asset variant.

No plaintext merge tokens (`{{name}}`, `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, etc.) visible in the truncated HTML source — personalization appears image-based.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** by QA relay. RFC 2369 compliance requires this header for bulk mail; its absence increases spam-filter scoring on Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected.** Gmail and Yahoo have enforced one-click unsubscribe for senders above 5,000/day since February 2024. Missing this header is a deliverability risk for high-volume sends.

**No physical mailing address found** in the truncated HTML. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. This may be present below the truncation point — needs full-source verification before flagging as a violation.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is set in the head. This suppresses referrer data on outbound clicks, which will cause GA4/Adobe Analytics to misattribute email-driven sessions as Direct traffic unless UTM params are fully self-contained on every link (see §6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameter coverage could not be verified** because all 22 links are behind click-redirect domains (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) and the QA probe skipped them. If UTM params are injected by the ESP at redirect time, the `no-referrer` meta (§5) will not interfere. If UTMs are baked into destination URLs, coverage needs spot-check via manual link expansion.

**`email_name=260621_DG_Email_Mystery`** appears in six image URLs — confirms the internal campaign identifier is `DG_Email_Mystery`. Verify this matches the UTM campaign value on destination links to ensure attribution consistency across platforms.
