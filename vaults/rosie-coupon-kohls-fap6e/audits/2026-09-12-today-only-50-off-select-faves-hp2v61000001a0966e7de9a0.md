# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This one buries its own lede. The subject promises "50% off select faves" but the actual hero — "HOT AND TRENDING," a full-bleed Kilian Paris perfume launch with pricing up to $150 — has nothing to do with the deal. The 50%-off banner is squeezed into a thin strip at the very top, above the fold but visually subordinate to a giant new-fragrance-collection ad directly beneath it.
- Once you scroll past the perfume launch, the email turns into exactly the kitchen-sink new-arrivals grid this brand runs constantly: Saie, Laneige, Gisou, Obart, Nars, Josie Maran, Armani Beauty, Touchland — eight new products with real prices, followed by yet more sub-promos (Favorites Kits, Defined Curls, Holiday Drops). It's the same "two emails welded together" pattern flagged on 9/11 and 9/6, just with a fragrance launch standing in for the usual Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar.
- This lands squarely mid-pack: the discount is real and visible, but the subject/hero mismatch and the sheer sprawl of unrelated content work against a clean click.

## What's working

- The 50% off banner at top is legible and states a real, named-brand offer (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency badge is visible and credible given the subject line.
- The new-arrivals grid has genuine, specific pricing on every item (e.g., Nars Lip Liner $26, Touchland $28/$32 value) — no placeholder or lorem-ipsum content.
- Clean product photography throughout with consistent "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA," and "Clean at Sephora" badges.
- No render bugs — layout, images, and badges all display correctly.

## What's weak

- The subject's core promise (50% off) is visually demoted below a giant, unrelated "HOT AND TRENDING" Kilian Paris fragrance hero — a repeat of the same subject/hero disconnect flagged on 9/10 and 9/9.
- The Kilian fragrance prices ($37–$150) are the opposite of a deal, which is jarring directly under a 50%-off banner.
- The email is very long and unfocused: fragrance launch → 8-item new-arrivals grid → kits → curls → holiday drops, with no single clear priority after the first fold.
- The 50% off banner never repeats or reappears once you scroll past it, so anyone browsing the new-arrivals grid loses the discount context entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 50% off deal into the actual hero position, or at minimum repeat a slim 50%-off reminder banner between the fragrance launch and the new-arrivals grid — right now the discount promise evaporates after one screen.
- 2. Either cut the Kilian Paris fragrance launch from this send or clearly separate it as "New Launch (not discounted)" so it doesn't read as a broken promise directly under the 50%-off banner.
- 3. Trim the new-arrivals grid from 8 items to 3-4 hero picks; move the rest to a "See more new arrivals" link.
