# New summer styles + Instant Kohl's Cash Event = win-win 🏆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's second email on July 23 — the same day as "JUST IN: Get Instant Kohl's Cash + score 3X rewards" (scored 7/10 this morning). Where that send was focused and single-threaded, this one jams two separate campaigns into one scroll: a summer swimwear event at 50% off and an early Back-to-School push, all loosely duct-taped together under the Instant Kohl's Cash umbrella. The "win-win 🏆" subject is exactly the kind of formula line you write when a creative brief has two owners and neither will yield.
- The 50% off swimwear lead is the strongest element — concrete, seasonal, and legitimately above the fold. But it has to share the email with a 40%-off section, a 20%-off section, a BTS category grid across six sub-categories, and a coupon barcode strip. No single message wins, and the subscriber who opens looking for swim deals will scan right past the BTS grid without registering it. The reverse is also true.
- Structurally, this is a batch-and-blast send wearing a loyalty hat. The Instant Kohl's Cash mechanic — which was the smart break from the 40%-off grind — gets diluted by the noise around it. Sending it twice on the same day to the same list compounds the damage.

## What's working

- **50% off swimwear is the right lead** — most specific offer in the email, naturally timely in late July, and it shows up first after the hero
- **"INSTANT" hero banner is visually arresting** — the yellow treatment grabs the eye and separates this send from the prior 40%-off wall of emails
- **3X rewards callout in the sub-header** — members can see the loyalty stack benefit without scrolling
- **BTS category grid is clean and organized** — Girls / Juniors / Women / Boys / Young Men / Backpacks with strong model photography; would work well as a standalone send
- **Bottom coupon + barcode strip is actionable** — the $10 Kohl's Cash + earn mechanic are both present, with a scannable in-store barcode

## What's weak

- **Two campaigns, zero clear winner** — summer clearance and Back-to-School are competing for the same column inch; neither gets enough room to land
- **Three percentage-off sections cascade with no hierarchy** — 50%, 40%, 20% each with their own "Shop Now" button means the subscriber has to make a meta-decision before they can even click
- **Second send today, twelfth-plus in under a week** — the "win-win 🏆" subject does nothing to justify the re-interrupt; cadence trust is nearly spent
- **No visible deadline anywhere in the render** — after the "ENDS TONIGHT" / "EXTENDED" credibility collapse from 7/22, subscribers now look for expiry signals; this email provides none, which perversely kills urgency
- **"Win-win" subject is low-signal filler** — the formula structure (X + Y = Z) telegraphs a brief that couldn't pick a single story

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split the email.** The 50%-off swimwear event is a legitimate standalone send. BTS deserves its own send with a "get ahead of the rush" angle. One brief, one email, one CTA.
- 2. **Add a visible expiry to the Kohl's Cash offer.** "Instant Kohl's Cash — earn through Sunday" would reintroduce credible urgency without repeating the "ENDS TONIGHT" disaster.
- 3. **Collapse to a single primary CTA.** Pick 50% swimwear as the hero action; demote 40%/20% to a slim secondary rail, not full-width sections competing for clicks.
- 4. **Lead the subject with the biggest number.** 50% beats "win-win" in an inbox scan every time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off swimwear — earn Instant Kohl's Cash today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer swim at half price + cash back on every order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Event ends Sunday — plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash® for every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Kohl's Cash starts the moment you shop. No waiting.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "New Summer Styles + Kohl's Cash Event"

**From:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-07-23

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Rendering and legacy compatibility are solid; the primary issues are compliance gaps (missing physical address, absent unsubscribe headers) and pervasive missing alt text on both tracking pixels and content images. Authentication status is unverifiable through the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click links skipped by HTTP probe** — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but redirect chain health cannot be asserted.

Three distinct pixel/beacon systems are active in a single send:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — CoherentPath pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — Maropost/MessageGears open tracker
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

No functional issues, but redundant tracking layers add request overhead on open and increase the number of third-party domains the email client must contact.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `<img ...><custom>` — a stray `<custom>` closing tag appears after the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` beacon. This is invalid HTML and may cause parser errors in strict clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** per QA findings, including:

- Tracking pixels (acceptable to omit alt, but should carry `alt=""` explicitly — two of three do not)
- Content images via `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` — these appear to be hero/product images rendered through a personalization CDN. Missing alt on content images fails WCAG 2.1 AA and degrades the experience for screen-reader users and image-blocked clients

`o.gif` pixel at `mi.kohls.com` carries `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` — correct pattern; should be replicated on the CoherentPath and demdex pixels.

`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` — `user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a common email pattern but fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

`<title></title>` is empty. Screen readers announce the email title; a meaningful value improves accessibility.

Media query breakpoint is `max-width: 500px` — covers most mobile viewports, but some Android devices at 360px–414px may behave unexpectedly with the fixed `207px`/`260px`/`154px` pixel overrides in `.w_50pc`, `.w_60pc`, `.w_35pc` rather than true percentage values.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the visible HTML (e.g., no `{{first_name}}` literals). Personalization appears to be handled server-side before send.

`mi_u=604230016` in tracker URLs is a static subscriber ID in the sample — correct, not a literal token.

`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in one image URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) has an **empty value** for what appears to be a Kohl's Cash redemption end date parameter. If this is a merge field that failed to populate, it could indicate a data-merge failure for the Kohl's Cash event expiry date — a functionally significant value in this specific send.
