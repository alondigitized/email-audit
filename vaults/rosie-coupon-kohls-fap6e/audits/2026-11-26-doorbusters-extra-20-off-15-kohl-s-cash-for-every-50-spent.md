## Executive summary

- This is the same ~12,000px Kohl's Black Friday deal wall that has landed all week, with a "Doorbusters" hook. It's a clean render. A snowy hero carries "Black Friday Deals", 50% off and 20% off badges, and a $15 Kohl's Cash badge. Below it come a free-shipping strip, a "Win up to $100 Kohl's Cash" sweepstakes tile, a "3 Days Only Doorbusters" banner, and dozens of price-led product blocks, each with its own "Shop Now" button.
- The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can read headline numbers and layout but not fine print or small copy. The offer stack is strong and specific: $47.99 luggage, $15.99 puffer jackets, $14 Sephora palettes, $10 fleece, $7.99 and $11.99 items, and 40% off plus 20% off. The email is also exhausting. Every block competes for attention, and the hero offers three discount mechanics at once (50%, 20% and $15 Kohl's Cash). By the third send this week, a subscriber who already knows the sale will skim it or archive it.
- Judgement: competent, on-season, and well priced. It's also undifferentiated from the last six sends. The "Doorbusters 3 days only" frame is the only fresh angle, and it's buried below the hero.

## What's working

- Concrete price points ($47.99, $15.99, $14, $10, $7.99) make the offers tangible.
- The "3 Days Only / Doorbusters" banner adds credible urgency, with a strong black-and-gold treatment.
- Every product block has its own "Shop Now" button, so the CTAs are unambiguous.
- Kohl's Cash and Rewards appear in the hero, in a "Win up to $100" tile and in a footer line. That serves members well.
- No broken images or overlapping text appear in the render.

## What's weak

- The email is about 12,000px long, with too many stacked offers. There's no single hero action.
- The hero shows three discount mechanics at once (50% off, 20% off, $15 Kohl's Cash), which dilutes the lead message.
- The "Doorbusters" angle sits below the hero, so the subject's promise isn't what the eye lands on first.
- Repeated "Coupons do not apply" and "Select styles" caveats weaken the sense of an honest offer.
- This is at least the fifth near-identical Black Friday wall since 11/24 ("Doorbusters are here for 3 days only" went out earlier today), so cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero, the Doorbusters. Put the 3-day timer and the top 3 doorbuster prices above the fold, and move the Kohl's Cash and 20% coupon to a secondary strip.
- 2. Cut the body to 6–8 blocks and send the rest through category links. Long, repetitive walls are what trained subscribers to skip.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop luggage $47.99" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Suppress or vary sends to recent openers. Today's earlier Doorbusters send and this one are nearly the same email.
- 5. Put the real deadline (day and time) in the hero, not just "3 days only".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Kohl's Black Friday send (ESP click domain `click.s.kohls.com`) with solid structure (640px container, `role="presentation"` tables, mobile media queries). It has malformed markup, several unlabeled images, a viewport that blocks zoom, and heavy tracking. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data. The QA run passed 38%, with 0 errors and 5 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **84 tracking links were not probed.** The `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects are opaque, so destination and status are unverified.
- **Four separate tracking pixels fire before any content.**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b86d7766-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1026774_20261126`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  The demdex (Adobe Audience Manager) beacon is a third-party cross-domain call.
- **Malformed markup:** the `open.aspx` pixel's `<img …>` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener (`…alt=""></custom>`). Some clients may handle this badly.
- **Possible empty dynamic value:** one image URL (`916d6fa08d185233.png`) contains `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` with an empty value. That looks like an unresolved Kohl's Cash redemption-end-date field. The URL is truncated in the QA output, so confirm it in the full source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Content images with no alt:** `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` (all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/`). These are likely the offer images, so with images blocked or in a screen reader the offers are lost. The QA list may be truncated.
- **Pixel images:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no alt. Neither is hidden from assistive tech, though the demdex pixel is sized 0×0.
  - The QA warning for `o.gif` looks like a false positive. The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative image.
- **Invalid head structure:** a `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, and `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. Both are harmless in most clients but invalid.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with XML-style `/>` self-closing tags.

- **No physical address was detected.** The footer is outside the truncated source, so this is unconfirmed. Kohl's footers normally include one, so verify against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** Tracking uses the ESP's opaque `qs=` token plus `mi_*` params (`mi_ecmp=1026774_20261126`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `email_name=261126_DG_Email_Thu_Mi…`). Attribution depends on Kohl's own `mi_` system rather than standard UTMs.
- **Landing-page alignment could not be checked.** All 84 links were skipped, so nothing confirms that the doorbuster, extra 20% off or $15 Kohl's Cash offers match their destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag and move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the seven offer images, and `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and demdex pixels.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and set a real `<title>`.
4. Check whether `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty in the actual send, and fix the template fallback if so.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and the footer address, using the original headers rather than the relay copy.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm landing URLs and parameters.
7. Shorten tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce how many links it carries.
8. Consider consolidating the four open-tracking pixels.
