# 20% off ENDS TODAY ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same broken hero pattern flagged in the July 27 send ("This just in: 20% off is coming in hot") — again. The top banner's percentage badge is visually garbled: "ENDS" sits above a red/orange starburst shape where the actual discount number is illegible, cut off inside the badge graphic, with only "off" readable below it. For an email whose entire subject line promise is "20% off ENDS TODAY," having the number unreadable in the hero is a real problem — it's the second time in two days this brand has shipped a hero with the offer number obscured.
- Below the broken hero, the email recovers reasonably well: clean department nav (Women/Men/Kids/Home), a legible "Take an extra 20%" callout tile, a strong "UP TO 40% OFF" activewear block with Nike/Under Armour imagery, and a long scroll of category tiles (home, bedding, adidas accessories) ending in barcoded coupons in Wallet. It's a comprehensive, on-brand catalog email — but comprehensive isn't the same as effective, and the exclusion language stacks up fast ("Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears twice).
- Judgement: functional but undercut by a recurring, avoidable creative bug. Kohl's keeps shipping the same hero rendering issue — that's a production/QA gap, not a one-off.

## What's working

- Loyalty status bar at the very top ("KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER · Earn: 5% REWARDS") gives the member an immediate, personalized reason to feel targeted.
- Department nav buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) right under the hero give fast wayfinding with no scrolling needed.
- The activewear block (Nike/Under Armour, "UP TO 40% OFF," starting price "$7.99") pairs a real photo, a clear discount, and specific pricing in one glance.
- Coupons are pre-loaded into a barcode block near the bottom ("View coupons in Wallet") — genuinely useful for in-store shoppers.

## What's weak

- The hero's percentage number is unreadable — obscured inside the starburst badge — repeating the exact defect from the July 27 email ("clipped... reads as 'n extra / 0% off'"). This is the single most important pixel in the email and it's broken again.
- Repeated tiled "EXTRA EXTRA EXTRA % OFF" background text behind the hero reads as visual noise/watermark rather than a design element, and makes the hero busier at the exact moment it needs to be clearest.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears twice (activewear sale, adidas accessories) — undermines the headline offer by excluding a chunk of what's pictured right next to the disclaimer.
- No urgency reinforcement near the bottom half of the email (bedding/home/patio section) — the "ends today" energy from the subject line evaporates halfway through the scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero badge rendering** — this is the third instance across two brand-history entries (July 27, and now July 28) of the discount number being clipped or illegible in the hero graphic. This needs a QA gate before send, not a post-hoc catch; it is directly suppressing the one number the subject line promises.
- 2. Drop the tiled "EXTRA EXTRA EXTRA % OFF" background texture — it competes with the actual number instead of framing it.
- 3. Consolidate the two "coupons do not apply to select brands" disclaimers into a single, smaller-but-still-visible line near the top rather than repeating exclusion language twice — repetition draws more attention to the carve-out, not less.
- 4. Add a second "Ends today" urgency marker around the home/bedding section so the deadline pressure carries through the full scroll, not just the top third.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends tonight ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off + up to 40% on activewear — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 20% off coupon + up to 40% off activewear, before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: stack an extra 20% off sitewide, ends tonight`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Email Audit: Kohl's — "20% off ENDS TODAY ⏰"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard MSO-defensive HTML4.01 markup with multiple third-party tracking integrations (ESP open-pixel, Adobe AAM, Coherent Path). QA reports a 57% pass rate driven mainly by missing authentication/unsubscribe headers and unlabeled tracking images; no functional link or compliance failures were confirmed in the available source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **62 tracking/redirect links skipped** by the QA HTTP probe (click-redirect domains) — destination liveness could not be automatically confirmed.
- At least **4 distinct tracking mechanisms fire on open**: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (Coherent Path), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` (ESP open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (secondary open pixel), and `kohls.demdex.net/event` (Adobe AAM). Six additional `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons are also present — this is a heavier-than-typical tracking payload for a single send.
- The `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` pixel is loaded as a bare `<img>` (fires unconditionally on open) yet its domain is named "click" and carries an encoded `cp_tp=v3.eJy...` payload typical of click-attribution — functionally it behaves as an open pixel despite the click-oriented naming/payload.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **8 images flagged missing alt text** by QA (`0f5440cf-...`, `o.gif`, `event` [demdex], and 6× `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`). Treatment is inconsistent: the `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"` and `alt=""`, while the `chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels have no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Malformed HTML**: 
  ```html
  <img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60268&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
  ```
  A stray `</custom>` closing tag appears with no matching opening tag — invalid markup that can trigger unpredictable DOM handling in strict renderers.
- **Duplicate font loading**: Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice — once via `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=...">` and again via `@import url(...)` for the same two families inside `<style>` — redundant network requests in clients that honor both.
- DOCTYPE HTML 4.01 Transitional + MSO conditional comments + 500px mobile breakpoint are standard, defensible email patterns. No issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge/personalization tags (e.g., first-name greeting) are visible in the portion of the source provided; the truncation cuts off before body content, so this can't be fully assessed.
- One tracking pixel URL contains an **empty merge parameter**: `...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe` — `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` (redemption end date) renders with no value. Worth verifying server-side, since this looks like the field that would back the "ENDS TODAY" deadline claim.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — may not be captured by the relay, but should be confirmed at the SMTP layer.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown via this relay.
- In-body unsubscribe link/physical mailing address could not be assessed — the provided HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters observed on any visible link; tracking instead uses proprietary ESP parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, e.g. `mi_ecmp=1018552_2026728`). Confirm the receiving site/analytics stack is configured to parse these `mi_*` params rather than expecting standard UTM, since default GA4 setups won't auto-attribute them.
- The 62 skipped tracking/redirect links mean end-to-end alignment between click destination and landing-page campaign params could not be verified programmatically.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add consistent `alt=""` (or `aria-hidden="true"`) to all tracking-pixel `<img>` tags — currently inconsistent across the 8 flagged images.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag in the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` block to fix invalid HTML.
3. Drop one of the two Google Fonts loading methods (`<link>` or `@import`) to eliminate duplicate font requests.
4. Verify `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` populates correctly — it's empty in the captured pixel URL and appears tied to the offer's stated deadline.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC and RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe/List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually set at the sending MTA, since none were observable through this relay.
6. Manually spot-check a sample of the 62 skipped tracking links to confirm redirect targets resolve and carry correct campaign parameters for site-side attribution.
