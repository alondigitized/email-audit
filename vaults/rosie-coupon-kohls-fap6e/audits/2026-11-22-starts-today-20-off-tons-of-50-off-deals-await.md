- The subject and body agree this time: "20% off" and "50% off" both show up as badges and tags.
- The "Stores open today at 5 AM" line gives credible same-day urgency.
- The "Free shipping with a $25 purchase" strip sits right under the hero and lowers the barrier to buying.
- Themed modules ("Cozy Corner", "Style Square", "More Black Friday Deals made for the nice list") group the products and break up the scroll.
- The closing "Here's your direct route to more deals" block repeats the BF 50% off offer and the Kohl's Cash win-a-prize promotion with a "Shop Now" button.

## What's weak

- The roughly 20 stacked tiles carry equal weight, so nothing says "start here." It's a wall of deals.
- The hero leads with "$15 Kohl's Cash". The subject's headline offer, 20% off, is not the dominant element.
- Many tiles say "w/coupon" and "select styles", which implies exclusions and gives the offer a fine-print feel.
- Most tiles have small text and no individual CTA buttons. Only a few "Shop Now" links are visible, and they are tiny.
- The email is extremely long, with several dense price tiles ("2.79", "7.99", "11.99", "14.99"). This is mobile-hostile and makes scanning hard.
- It repeats the cadence of earlier sends: 11/20 "Take an extra 20% off 🙌", then three more 20%-off sends on 11/21, and now this.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the single offer named in the subject: a big "20% off" coupon with code and deadline, and one "Shop now" button.
- 2. Cut the deal wall to 6–8 tiles, with a tappable button on each.
- 3. Collapse the "select styles" and "w/coupon" qualifiers into one clear line under the hero.
- 4. Reduce cadence or differentiate sends. Five near-identical 20%-off emails in three days trains people to ignore them.
- 5. Segment by category (home, apparel, kids) so the top module matches the recipient.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Kohl's Black Friday, "STARTS TODAY: 20% off + tons of 50% off deals await ➡️"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based layout with valid responsive CSS, but it has several markup defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, empty `<title>`). Many content images are missing alt text, and the viewport meta blocks user zoom. Compliance headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source is truncated, so footer and link-level findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **94 tracking links were not probed.** The click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status is unverified. The visible wrappers are `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` and `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`.
- **Stacked tracking pixels.** There are at least four in the first lines of `<body>`:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/82083f76-...` (1x1)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, 0x0)
- **Malformed close tag.** The `open.aspx` pixel sits in `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;"><img ... alt=""></custom></div>`. `</custom>` has no matching open tag and is invalid HTML.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 16,698 of 22,509 characters (74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-scoring risk for the text alternative.
- **Plain-text part is bloated by tracking URLs.** The long `qs=` tracking URLs are what push the text part past the 70% threshold.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed).** `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png` and `916d6fa08d185233.png` have no alt. The same applies to the product images `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60479404_1_2x`, `60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. With images blocked, the offer content is likely invisible and screen readers get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels with missing alt.**
  - The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/82083f76-...`) has no `alt` and no `aria-hidden`. This one is a real omission.
  - `demdex.net/event` also has no `alt`.
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` appears to be a false positive, because the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Empty favicon link.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` can trigger a request to the page URL.
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` there makes parsers close the head early, which can push the following content into the body. Schema.org microdata is also not supported by major email clients.
- **Duplicate meta.** `format-detection` appears twice (`telephone=no` and `date=no`). Only one value may be honored.
- **Doctype mismatch.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is declared alongside `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` attributes and self-closed tags (`/>`).
- **Web fonts.** Roboto and Montserrat load via both `<link>` and `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`. This is a duplicate load, and it is ignored by Outlook and Gmail. The fallback stack `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is acceptable.
- **Layout tables.** Layout tables have `role="presentation"` (good), but the 1x1 tracking table lacks it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source.
- **Subscriber ID in URLs.** `mi_u=604230016` appears in image and link query strings. This is a per-recipient identifier, so rendered images and links are personalized and not safe to forward or cache.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (unconfirmed).** The QA tool noted that AgentMail may strip it. I can't confirm the header is missing at the sender (`kohls@s.kohls.com`).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (unconfirmed).** Same limitation, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unknown. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Physical address not detected (unconfirmed).** The source is truncated, so the footer is likely cut off. Verify against the full HTML before treating this as a violation.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `s.kohls.com` is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Meta tag.** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `referrer: no-referrer` are present, which is not a compliance issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters visible.** Visible links use opaque `qs=` redirect tokens, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the shown source. Campaign attribution relies on the vendor's own params:
  - `mi_ecmp=1026463_20261122`
  - `email_name=261122_DG_Email_Sunday`
  - `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`, which is empty on the final visible image, so a parameter is passed with no value.
- **Landing pages can't be verified.** Because the 94 redirects were skipped, I can't confirm that landing pages match the "20% off / 50% off" offer.
- **Internal naming in URLs.** `email_name=261122_DG_Email_Sunday` is exposed in image URLs. It is harmless, but it leaks internal campaign naming.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text** to all content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `images.am.kohls.com/...`) and `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` to the unmarked pixels (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/...`, `demdex.net/event`).
2. **Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Fix the head and body markup:**
   - Move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>`, or drop it.
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Populate `<title>`.
   - Remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
   - Dedupe `format-detection`.
4. **Consolidate tracking pixels.** Four opens beacons add weight and several third-party requests.
5. **Reduce the plain-text URL ratio** by using shorter tracked links or more descriptive text.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers** to verify the unsubscribe headers, footer address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. **Resolve the redirect chains** in a separate probe to confirm landing pages and add `utm_*` parameters if the site analytics need them.
