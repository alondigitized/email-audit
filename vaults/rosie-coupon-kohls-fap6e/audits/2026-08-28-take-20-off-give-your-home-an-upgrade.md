# Take 20% off + give your home an upgrade 🏡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Friends & Family send in five days, and it finally has a coherent hero idea — the Martha Stewart collection launch and a "every room deserves an upgrade" home category grid — instead of just reskinning the 20%-off coupon banner again. Visually it's the most product-dense of the run: a slow cooker hero, four home-category tiles (Kitchen & Dining, Bedding, Bath, Decor), then a whole second section for sports gear (MLB/NCAA/NFL/NHL jerseys) and a "picked with you in mind" grid of unrelated items (towel, drink maker, jeans, mattress topper) that reads like a leftover personalization block bolted onto a home-themed email.
- The top of the email is strong — clean coupon banner, clear nav, a genuine editorial hook with a named designer collaboration. But it sprawls badly below the fold: home, sports, and "picked for you" are three different emails stitched into one, none of them get real depth, and the actual 20% coupon code doesn't show up again until deep in the footer next to a barcode. Judged against the last five days of near-identical F&F sends, this is a content upgrade but not a strategic one — still the same offer, still the same "Ends Aug. 30" deadline, just with more stuff crammed in.

## What's working

- The Martha Stewart collection callout is a genuine point of difference from the last five sends — a named designer, a real hero product (slow cooker) with lifestyle shots (baking cookies, mixing a bowl).
- The four-tile home category grid (Kitchen & Dining, Bedding, Bath, Decor) gives a clear, scannable path into the site with distinct product photography per tile.
- Top-of-email hierarchy is clean: coupon banner → Kohl's Cash strip → nav bar → hero, no overlapping text or broken images visible.
- Pricing is shown directly on the "picked with you in mind" tiles (e.g., "$26.00–29.99, Orig. $52.00"), which is more concrete than most of this week's sends.

## What's weak

- Three unrelated content blocks (home, sports jerseys, "picked with you in mind") are jammed into one email with a "Give your home an upgrade" subject — the sports section has nothing to do with the subject line or the home-upgrade hero.
- The 20% coupon — the entire subject-line hook — isn't reinforced again until the very bottom of the email next to a barcode; there's no mid-email reminder near the product grids.
- "Picked with you in mind (Your great taste makes it easy.)" groups a bath towel, a drink maker, jeans, and a mattress topper with zero shared logic — it undercuts the personalization framing rather than supporting it.
- Same "Ends Aug. 30" deadline and same F&F coupon mechanic as every send since Aug. 24 — five-plus repeats in, this is fatigue territory for anyone opening daily.
- Primary CTA is diffuse: three separate "Shop Now"/"Shop Home" buttons compete for attention instead of one clear hero action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one theme. Either this is the Martha Stewart/home-upgrade email the subject promises, or it's a general F&F send — pick one and drop the sports jersey block and the grab-bag "picked with you" grid, which dilute the home story the subject is selling.
- 2. Move a coupon reminder (code + deadline) directly under the hero product, not just in the footer barcode — readers who scroll past the top banner currently lose the offer entirely until the end.
- 3. Replace "Picked with you in mind" with actual home-adjacent cross-sell (e.g., more Martha Stewart SKUs, or the same four categories at different price points) so the whole email stays on-theme.
- 4. Consolidate to one primary CTA button style/color tied to the hero, and make secondary category buttons visually subordinate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the Martha Stewart collection + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Martha Stewart for the kitchen, 20% off through Sun.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slow cookers, bakeware and more — plus your Friends & Family coupon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Martha Stewart collection just landed. Coupon inside.`

## Technical Audit: Kohl's Friends & Family Email

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard Kohl's/Coherent Path (Cordial) marketing template with heavy open/click tracking pixels; automated QA shows 43% pass rate driven by missing alt text, absent compliance headers, and no unsubscribe headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 65 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains: `click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) — link destinations are unverified.
- Multiple independent tracking pixels fire on open: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1 img, not wrapped in a `<table>`/hidden container like the others), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...gif`, `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`, and an Adobe Audience Manager call to `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`. Four distinct tracking vendors on a single open is notable redundancy but not a defect per se.
- The `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel is wrapped in a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opening tag — invalid markup (`<div>...<img .../></custom></div>`).

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 9 of 9 flagged images have no `alt` attribute (QA-confirmed), including all `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-mail/personalization pixels and the AAM tracking pixel. These are tracking pixels, not content images, so the accessibility impact is low, but best practice is `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (already applied to the `o.gif` pixel but not the others) to explicitly mark them decorative for screen readers/HTML validators.
- Uses legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and Outlook-specific `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` — expected/appropriate for cross-client email rendering, not a defect.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present and correctly scoped for email (prevents indexing if the HTML leaks to web, strips referrer from outbound clicks).

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- `mi_ecmp=1020697_2026828` and `email_name=260828_DG_Email_Fri_PM` values in the `mi.kohls.com` pixels are resolved (not placeholder tokens), consistent with a live send rather than a template preview.
- One personalization param is empty: `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` pixel URL has no value — likely an unpopulated redemption-end-date token, worth confirming with the ESP if that field is expected to be populated for this campaign.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in provided headers (QA WARN). Note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm sender-side absence from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the captured HTML/headers. CAN-SPAM §7704 requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email; this is a real compliance gap if the footer (likely truncated out of this excerpt) doesn't contain it. Recommend confirming against the full/untruncated HTML before escalating, since the source provided is explicitly truncated before the footer.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query params are used; Kohl's uses proprietary tracking params instead (`mi_ecmp`, `mi_cid`, `cp_tp`, `cp_cid`, `mi_u`, `d_sid`). This is a vendor-convention choice (Cordial/Coherent Path platform), not a bug, but it means standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't parse these links.
- All primary CTA links are wrapped through `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect tracking — actual landing page destinations were not resolved (skipped per QA), so on-site landing page alignment (correct category page, live promo, working page) cannot be verified from this data.

**7. Recommendations**
- Confirm the physical mailing address is present in the untruncated footer; if genuinely absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation requiring immediate fix.
- Add `alt=""` to the remaining tracking pixels missing it, for markup-validator/accessibility-scanner cleanliness.
- Fix the stray `</custom>` closing tag around the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` pixel.
- Since List-Unsubscribe/List-Unsubscribe-Post/Authentication-Results are all reported missing, verify directly against raw SMTP headers (not the relay-forwarded copy) before concluding sender non-compliance — the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers rather than the sender omitting them.
- Spot-check one or two of the 65 skipped tracking redirects manually to confirm they resolve to live, correct landing pages (QA could not probe these programmatically).
