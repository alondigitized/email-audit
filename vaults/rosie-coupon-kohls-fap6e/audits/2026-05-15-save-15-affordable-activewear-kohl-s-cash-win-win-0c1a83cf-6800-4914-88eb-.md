# Save 15% | Affordable activewear + Kohl's Cash ... win-win 🏆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a mid-tier Kohl's activewear send that front-loads a decent TEK GEAR story — family in colorful athletic wear, specific entry price at $12.99, clear "busy families" copy — but immediately undermines it by cutting to a Sol de Janeiro fragrance module. The subject promises activewear and Kohl's Cash; the body delivers activewear, fragrance, lifestyle categories, and a 15% coupon buried at the very bottom. The offer that's in the subject line doesn't actually appear until you've scrolled past everything else.
- The bigger structural problem is cadence fatigue. This is at minimum the fifth consecutive 15% off email from Kohl's this week (May 6, 8, 9, 10, 11 all ran the same mechanic). A subscriber who opened any of those is now being asked to treat "Save 15%" as news. It isn't. The "win-win 🏆" closer in the subject is doing PR for a deal the recipient has seen four times already.
- Clear judgement: this is a competent, unremarkable execution that wastes a legitimate hook (affordable summer activewear) by stuffing fragrance and lifestyle modules around it instead of committing to the lane the subject promised.

## What's working

- **TEK GEAR hero is specific** — starting price of $12.99 is visible in the module, and the family shot (woman + two kids) matches the "busy families" copy cleanly.
- **Kohl's Cash prominently placed twice** — once in the top banner and once in the footer coupon block, so loyalty signal is hard to miss.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout drift visible anywhere in the email.
- **"Busy families require teamwork" line** — small thing, but it ties lifestyle relevance to the product category in a way that's fresher than "shop activewear."

## What's weak

- **15% off offer is buried at the bottom** — the entire value proposition in the subject line (Save 15%) doesn't appear until after the hero, the TEK GEAR module, the Sol de Janeiro block, and a lifestyle category row. The hero copy ("Higher style points. Lower price points.") is vague brand-speak, not an offer.
- **Sol de Janeiro fragrance module is completely off-theme** — subject said activewear and Kohl's Cash; a dark-background fragrance mini collection in the middle of the scroll is a jarring detour that dilutes the activewear message.
- **"Shop Now" is the only CTA copy across all modules** — zero specificity. "Shop TEK GEAR," "Shop Women's Activewear," or "See $12.99 picks" would each pull harder.
- **Offer discount is identical to four prior emails this week** — 15% off has appeared on May 6, 8, 9, 10, and 11. Subscribers are fully desensitized to this number from this sender.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 15% offer into the hero** — lead with the coupon block or a callout banner immediately below the nav, not after two full content modules. The subject promises the discount; the body should deliver it above the fold.
- 2. **Cut the Sol de Janeiro module or relegate it to a secondary position** — if it must stay (paid placement), put it after the activewear story wraps, not mid-scroll where it breaks the category narrative.
- 3. **Sharpen CTA copy** — replace every "Shop Now" with category-specific copy: "Shop TEK GEAR," "Shop Kids' Activewear," "Browse $12.99+ Picks."
- 4. **Rotate the offer mechanic** — a dollar-off or a tiered deal would feel fresh against four consecutive 15% sends. Even "15% + free shipping" reframes the deal enough to register as new.
- 5. **Add urgency to the subject** — the current subject has no expiry signal. Even "thru Sunday" would lift open rate among deal-seekers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TEK GEAR from $12.99 + 15% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Affordable activewear + 15% off thru Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Busy families, this one's for you — TEK GEAR starts at $12.99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your savings: 15% off now, Kohl's Cash back on top.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect links skipped** — all routed through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probe cannot verify destination integrity or detect redirect chains; dead links would go undetected by automated QA.

**Three distinct tracking pixel systems** are active simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path (personalization platform)
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` and `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/...` — MessageGears
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager

No broken pixel endpoints detected, but the parallel open-tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `open.aspx`, and `b0c766cf...`) create triple-counted open events across three platforms — expected for multi-platform stacks but worth noting for metrics reconciliation.

**Malformed HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag on the `open.aspx` pixel `<div>` — not a standard element; likely a legacy ESP artifact. No rendering impact in major clients but invalid markup.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all tracking pixels and several content images** (11 warnings from QA):

| Missing alt | Image |
|---|---|
| Open pixel | `click.chp.kohls.com/o/b0c766cf...` |
| Open pixel | `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` |
| AAM pixel | `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` |
| Content images | `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` `aria-hidden="true"` (the `o.gif` pixel has both — inconsistently applied). Content images without alt text render as broken image icons in image-blocking clients and fail WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — screen readers announce no document title; some spam filters flag empty titles.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 violation.

**`max-height: auto`** in the mobile media query (`max-height: auto !important`) — `auto` is not a valid value for `max-height`; browsers silently ignore it, but it indicates a copy-paste error in the stylesheet.

**Font double-loading:** Roboto and Montserrat each load via both `<link>` and `@import` inside the same `<![endif]-->` block — redundant round-trips on non-MSO clients.
