# These EPIC DEALS are off the chart 📈
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's ninth "EPIC DEALS" category-blitz send since September 20 — the exact same purple hero banner, hot-pink "EPIC DEAL" tile wall, and coupon/barcode footer that has now anchored every send this week (09-20 through 09-26). Nothing here distinguishes this from yesterday's "Epic Deals right this way 👉" beyond a reshuffled product grid (Carter's kids clothing, kitchen appliances, home/bedding, toys, FLX activewear, dresses, flannels, plus a Halloween "too good to ghost" module bolted on near the bottom).
- The individual deal tiles are genuinely clear — big bold percentages, product photography, unambiguous "Shop Now" buttons — so on a tile-by-tile basis this is competent execution. But the whole is worse than the sum of its parts: at nine near-identical sends in a week, the subject line itself ("off the chart 📈") is now overcompensating for fatigue rather than reflecting real urgency, and a reader who's seen this brand six times this week has no reason to believe this send is different.
- Judgement: solid template, badly overused. The creative execution doesn't move the needle — cadence is the problem, and this send does nothing to fix it.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy in each deal module — pink "EPIC DEAL" tag, bold percentage, product shot, single "Shop Now" CTA repeated consistently.
- No render bugs — all images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.
- Concrete, varied offers across categories (50% off kids clothing, up to 60% off home, 30% off toys, up to 40% off dresses) rather than one vague sitewide claim.
- Kohl's Cash / Rewards 3x callout near the top gives loyalty members a specific reason to act before Sept 27.
- Halloween "too good to ghost" module is a legitimate seasonal touch that's at least new creative versus recent sends.

## What's weak

- Template fatigue is now the headline problem — same purple hero, same pink tile stamps, same barcode footer run at least eight times in a week (09-20 through 09-26).
- Subject line "off the chart 📈" reads as hype inflation with nothing backing it up — no new offer tier, same discounts as prior sends.
- No single hero offer — nine-plus categories compete for attention with zero prioritization, so the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- The "Kohl's Rewards 3x" and Kohl's Cash banner is visually secondary despite being the most differentiated benefit in the email.
- Coupon/barcode wall at the footer is visually heavy and repeats the same restriction pattern subscribers have now seen all week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut send frequency or force real variation.** Nine near-identical sends in a week is the single biggest drag on this email's performance — no creative fix beats simply not sending a tenth lookalike tomorrow.
- 2. **Pick one hero deal and lead with it**, instead of a flat wall of nine categories — force a decision in the top fold (e.g., the 60% home deal, which is the largest number on the page).
- 3. **Promote Kohl's Cash/3x Rewards into the hero position** since it's the one benefit this send has that recent sends didn't emphasize as strongly — that's the actual news, not "epic deals."
- 4. **Retire "EPIC DEALS" as the evergreen banner language** — it's lost all meaning after a week of reuse; swap in specific, rotating campaign names tied to the actual promo mix.
- 5. **Move the Halloween module up** — it's the freshest creative in the email and currently buried two-thirds down.
