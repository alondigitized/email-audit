# Find your new favorite jeans 👖 Plus, save 20%.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Kohl's send this week with a real hook. After "Get an extra 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩" on 10-02 and the "Save 20% now 😍" repeat earlier today, a jeans-led subject is a welcome change. The body only half delivers on it. The email still opens with the same bare pink "EXTRA" wordmark, the Kohl's Cash banner, and four category buttons (Women, Men, Kids, Home). The jeans hero doesn't appear until well below that.
- The jeans block itself is decent: a "Great Price, Starting at 27.99" badge, a "Shop Now" button, and a collage of denim photos. But the headline is a coy "best-kept secret? The price tag." The fine print reads "Reg. $29.99-$44.99 + save with coupon," so the 27.99 starting price is only about $2 below the low end of the regular range. The 20% in the subject is really the coupon code YOUSAVE20, and it sits near the bottom of the email. After the jeans, the email drifts into clearance, a slushie machine, towels, a mattress topper and coupons.
- Verdict: a good subject on top of a kitchen-sink body. A cleaner jeans-first email with the code up front would perform noticeably better.

## What's working

- The subject leads with a concrete product category, which is a real change from the week's repetitive "20% + Kohl's Cash" subjects.
- The sender is instantly recognizable, and the "Kohl's Rewards Member / Earn 5% Rewards" status bar personalizes the top.
- The jeans hero has a clear price badge ("Starting at 27.99") and a clean photo grid that shows fit and wash variety.
- The coupon section is well structured, with barcodes, codes, PINs and "Ends Oct. 4" deadlines that make the urgency credible.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The top of the email is cluttered: the "EXTRA" wordmark with no number, the Kohl's Cash banner, and four category buttons all sit above the jeans hero.
- The 20% off in the subject has no code or number near the jeans hero. The code YOUSAVE20 is buried near the bottom.
- The "Starting at 27.99" price against a $29.99 regular price is a weak anchor, and "save with coupon" is vague.
- The second half drifts into unrelated products (slushie maker, bath towels, mattress topper) and a clearance 70% banner that competes with the jeans story.
- Category buttons repeat three times (Women/Men/Kids/Home, Women/Men/Girls/Boys, Home/Women/Men/Kids), so the email has no single dominant CTA. The "Shop Now" buttons are generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it a jeans-first email. Put the jeans hero directly under the logo and drop the "EXTRA" wordmark and the four category buttons above it. Show "Extra 20% off jeans, code YOUSAVE20" in the hero itself.
- 2. Fix the price story. Show the real after-coupon price ("As low as $22") instead of "Starting at 27.99" against a $29.99 regular price.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop women's jeans" or "Shop men's jeans". Use one gender-split CTA pair, not three button rows.
- 4. Cut the clearance, slushie and towel blocks. Keep one secondary module at most, and move the coupon stack up so the deadline (Oct. 4) is visible earlier.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings or "top-rated fits," to the denim products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Jeans from $22 with code YOUSAVE20 👖`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next favorite jeans, 20% off through Oct. 4`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Women's, men's and kids' denim, plus earn Kohl's Cash. Ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code YOUSAVE20 at checkout. Fits for everyone, starting at 27.99.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly functional, but it has several malformed or invalid constructs and missing accessibility attributes. Its 67 tracked links could not be probed, so destinations and UTMs are unverified. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header and footer data the QA run did not capture, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** All 67 links were skipped as tracking redirects. Links use opaque SFMC-style wrappers (`https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…`), so destinations, 4xx/5xx status and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Pixel stack:** There are four 1×1 beacons, each on a different third-party or tracking host:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/949a6dd7-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)

  These add redundant requests, and the `demdex` call is third-party.
- **Exposed identifiers:** Image URLs carry `mi_u=604230016` (a subscriber ID) and `email_name=261003_DG_Email_Sat_PM` (an internal campaign name) in plaintext query strings.
- **Empty parameter:** The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the `916d6fa08d185233.png` URL is empty. This may mean a dynamic-content field failed to populate. It is only a possible sign, and I can't confirm the image rendered incorrectly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Content images `2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png` and `58125193_1s_2x` have no `alt`. If any carry text or product content, a screen reader or an image-blocked client gets nothing.
- **Beacon alt text:** The tracking pixels `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` also lack `alt`. `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif` already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so that QA flag is a false positive.
- **Zoom blocked:** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - A stray `</custom>` closes the `open.aspx` image, which has no matching open tag.
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. This is invalid and can make parsers close `<head>` early.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href.
  - The CSS selector `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` lacks the leading `.` and matches nothing.
  - The `format-detection` meta tag is duplicated.
- **Layout tables:** Several lack `role="presentation"`, including the `width="1" height="1"` pixel table and the `<!-- AAM -->` table. The main tables do have it.
- **Fonts:** Roboto is loaded twice (a `<link>` and an `@import`). Both are non-MSO only and have Arial/Helvetica fallbacks, so there is no functional break. The `@import url(...)` rules are stripped by Gmail.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible (truncated) source has no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens. The one concern is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** The List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and Authentication-Results headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip or fail to capture them, so these are capture gaps, not confirmed defects.
- **Physical address:** QA found no physical address, but the supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm this either way.
- **Confirmed:** `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present. Neither affects compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Destinations are hidden behind `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=` wrappers, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. The only campaign identifiers are Movable Ink style (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp=1023096_2026103`, `email_name=…`) on image URLs. Landing-page alignment with the "jeans / save 20%" subject cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images, and `alt=""` to the remaining pixels (`click.chp…/o/…`, `demdex…/event`).
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the markup:
   - Delete the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the `schema.org` div out of `<head>`.
   - Populate `<title>` and remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
   - Change the `ReadMsgBody` selector to `.ReadMsgBody`.
4. Add `role="presentation"` to the remaining layout tables.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to reduce third-party requests, and avoid putting subscriber IDs and internal campaign names in image query strings.
6. For the QA pipeline:
   - Capture the raw message headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results).
   - Run the footer check on the full, untruncated HTML.
   - Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of tracked links to check UTMs and landing pages.
   - Treat `alt=""` as valid for decorative images, since the `o.gif` flag is a false positive.
