# Shop Epic Deals + earn 3x rewards with Kohl's Rewards 😊
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact "EPIC DEALS" category-blitz from 09-21 running again — same purple/red hero banner, same nine-plus deal-tile wall (bras... wait, this send swaps in bedding/kitchen/luggage/beauty/toys tiles), same structure the brand ran the day before at 5.00/10. The only real change is the subject/hero framing: instead of leading with the deal wall, it leads with "earn 3x rewards with Kohl's Rewards," which is a weaker hook than a flat percentage-off coupon and a step backward from the 40% flat-coupon streak Kohl's ran 09-18 through 09-20.
- The email itself is competently built — clear tile grid, readable pricing, a distinct Sephora-at-Kohl's insert, a toys age-range chooser, and a "picked with you in mind" cross-sell block — but there's no single hero offer, no urgency deadline in the primary CTA, and the "3x rewards" angle requires the reader to already understand Kohl's Rewards math to feel motivated. It reads as inventory-clearing wallpaper, not a targeted send.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero render bugs, but strategically flat — this is the brand's default "everything on sale" template with a rewards teaser bolted onto the subject line, not a genuinely new campaign beat.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent tile grid — each deal has a price, a product shot, and a "Shop Now" CTA with no visual clutter.
- The green "3x rewards" banner near the top is visually distinct and easy to spot against the red hero.
- Sephora-at-Kohl's insert (purple background) breaks up the monotony of red tiles and signals a real cross-brand tie-in.
- Toys age-range selector (0-12 months through 12+ years) is a nice segmentation touch that helps a parent skip straight to relevant products.
- No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem-ipsum — everything renders cleanly.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer — nine-plus competing tiles (5.99, 3.99, 19.99, 7.99, 25%, 60%, 50%, 60%, 75%) mean the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- The subject's "3x rewards" hook barely shows up in the visual hierarchy — it's a thin green strip below a much louder "EPIC DEALS" banner, so the promised angle is buried.
- No visible deadline/urgency on the primary hero CTA itself (fine print like "Ends Sept. 27" is present but small and scattered across different offers, not consolidated).
- Several tiles carry exclusion language ("Coupons do not apply to select brands") right under the price, which undercuts the offer's honesty at a glance.
- This is the same tile-wall format the brand ran 09-21 with a 5.00/10 score — repetition risk for anyone who got both.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero deal (the 60% bedding or 75% beauty tile are strongest) and make it the full-width top banner instead of burying it in row four/six of a scroll — a decisive hero lifts open-to-click conversion more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Move the "3x rewards" mechanic out of the thin green strip and either drop it from the subject or give it real visual weight (its own banner with an icon/badge) so subject and hero agree.
- 3. Consolidate all deadlines into one bold banner line ("Ends Sept. 27") near the top instead of scattering them per-tile.
- 4. Trim the tile count from nine-plus to four or five — a shorter, higher-contrast deal set will out-convert the current wallpaper approach.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bedding, kitchen, beauty: up to 75% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn 3x rewards through Sept. 27 — plus Epic Deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sephora, toys, luggage & more — all in one place`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 3x rewards multiplier ends Sunday`
