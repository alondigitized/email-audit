# Back-to-School Sale 🎒 Up to 40% off 😍 Happy shopping 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Back-to-School send from Kohl's this week, and it's the biggest, most exhaustive version yet — same "up to 40% off" hero as 8/19, but this time followed by a genuinely deep category cascade: Tops $7.99, Jeans up to 30%, Bras $19.99, Backpacks 30%, Bedding 60%, Bath 50%, Hanes Basics 20%, a sweepstakes, then a 70% clearance module and a "Picked with you in mind" grid. It's comprehensive to a fault — by the time you hit clearance you've forgotten the subject line was about school supplies.
- The hero itself is clean and on-brief: kid in a school hallway, bold "SCHOOL" wordmark, red "up to 40% off back-to-school essentials sitewide" banner, single Shop Now CTA. That's a strong open few seconds. But this email tries to be both a back-to-school email and a general weekly circular, and the length works against it — Kohl's Rewards/Kohl's Cash banner, six nav buttons, then eight-plus separate offer modules before you even reach clearance. Compared to 8/19's tighter, better-matched sends (8.00/10 both), this one dilutes its own hero by burying it under unrelated categories (mattress topper, slush maker, bath towel) that have nothing to do with school.
- Verdict: solid hero, bloated body. It reads more like Kohl's dumping every active promo into one email than a curated back-to-school moment — decent but not their best work this week.

## What's working

- Hero cleanly matches the subject: "up to 40% off," school hallway imagery, single unambiguous "Shop Now" CTA.
- Specific, concrete pricing throughout ($7.99 tops, $19.99 bras, 30% backpacks) rather than vague "sale" language.
- Kohl's Cash banner and Rewards status bar reinforce loyalty value for members.
- Clean red/white/cream color system stays consistent across every module — no render bugs or broken images visible.
- Backpacks and school-adjacent categories (Tops, Jeans, Bags) are logically prioritized near the top.

## What's weak

- The email sprawls well past back-to-school into bedding, bath, mattress toppers, a slush maker, and general clearance — undercuts the subject's focus.
- No single dominant CTA after the hero — by the fifth+ module (Bras, Bedding, Bath, Hanes) the eye has no clear priority, everything is "Shop Now" at equal weight.
- Clearance module (70% off) and "Picked with you in mind" grid compete directly with the back-to-school offer for attention at the bottom.
- Sweepstakes module ("SO-BACK Sweepstakes") is visually disconnected — different color treatment, breaks the red/cream rhythm established everywhere else.
- Subject's emoji stacking (🎒😍🛍️) doesn't match the more restrained, clean visual tone of the actual email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep Tops, Jeans, Backpacks, Bras, Basics; drop Bedding, Bath, Slush Maker, and Mattress Topper into a separate "Home Refresh" send. This is the single biggest lift — it makes the whole email match its own subject line.
- 2. Move Clearance and "Picked with you in mind" out entirely, or compress into one small module at the very bottom — right now they visually compete with the back-to-school hero.
- 3. Restyle the sweepstakes block to match the red/cream system instead of standing apart — currently reads like a bolted-on unit.
- 4. Simplify the subject to drop the double emoji and redundant "Happy shopping" tail, which adds length without adding information.
