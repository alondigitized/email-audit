# 20% off AND Kohl's Cash ... immediately yes 👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's second email on July 1 — the brand history already shows a "20% off w/code GOSHOP20 + Kohl's Cash" send earlier today — making this a same-day duplicate with a chattier subject line twist. The email itself is structurally solid: a clear hero discount, a featured brand (TEK GEAR), a family lifestyle section, and a prominent clearance module that stacks on the primary offer. The visual hierarchy works. The creative is clean and free of render issues.
- The biggest problem is the subject line's "immediately yes" framing. If the 20% coupon is future-dated (the hero copy appears to read "from July 7"), that's a bait-and-switch experience — the subscriber opens expecting immediate action and finds they need to wait a week. Combined with being the second Kohl's email of the day, this erodes the credibility Kohl's rebuilt after the June 28–30 Deal Days burnout.
- The clearance "Save up to 70%" module is the sleeper hit here: concrete, immediately usable, no ambiguity. That's the strongest hook in the email and it's buried below the fold.

## What's working

- **TEK GEAR callout with price anchoring** — "Starting at $7.99" next to the activewear shot gives a tangible entry point. Shoppers know immediately whether to keep scrolling.
- **Kohl's Cash is front and center** — both in the hero banner and reinforced at footer. Loyalty lever is well-placed.
- **Clearance module is high-contrast and specific** — red "CLEARANCE" header + "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN" + "Save up to 70%" is the clearest, most action-ready copy in the email.
- **Category grid (Women / Men / Kids / Shoes)** — covers the broadest possible family shopper without feeling chaotic.
- **No render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- **"Immediately yes" subject is potentially deceptive** — if the 20% coupon starts July 7, opening this email on July 1 to find a future-dated offer is a trust break, not a delight.
- **Second email of the day with the same core offer** — subscribers who already got the GOSHOP20 email this morning gain nothing from this send; it reads as inbox clutter and risks unsubscribes.
- **"Shop Now" CTAs everywhere, zero specificity** — three separate "Shop Now" buttons compete with each other; none tell you what you're actually clicking into.
- **Clearance is buried** — the strongest immediately-usable offer (70% off) doesn't appear until mid-scroll; it should be co-headlined with the coupon.
- **No urgency anchor on the primary offer** — end date for the 20% coupon doesn't appear until the fine-print barcode section at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the clearance + coupon combo in the hero** — "70% off clearance + extra 20% off with GOSHOP20" is a stronger opener than the coupon alone. The stacking story is the actual value proposition.
- 2. **Put the coupon end date in the hero** — "Ends July 6" or "Valid July 1–6" above the fold removes the ambiguity that "immediately yes" creates.
- 3. **Don't send a second near-identical email the same day** — consolidate or give this second send a distinct angle (e.g., clearance-only focus), not the same code + a different subject phrasing.
- 4. **Make the CTAs specific** — "Shop TEK GEAR," "Shop Clearance," "Shop Women's Styles" each outperform three identical "Shop Now" labels.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Ends July 6: extra 20% off + new clearance markdowns`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Stack it: 20% off coupon on top of 70% clearance`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns just dropped — plus your GOSHOP20 code inside.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code GOSHOP20 through July 6 — and shop fresh clearance up to 70% off now.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "20% off AND Kohl's Cash" (2026-07-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML4 email with standard ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageGears). Primary concerns are missing compliance signals at the header layer (likely artifacts of AgentMail relay capture) and pervasive missing alt text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-tracking links skipped** — all route through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. Destination URLs could not be probed, so broken-link status is unverified.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing simultaneously:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open/click tracker
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageGears open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open pixel (ESP identity unclear)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager segment sync

**Invalid HTML tag:** `</custom>` closing tag at line ~`<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..."><custom>` — orphaned tag, not a valid HTML element. Low impact in email clients but technically malformed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA scan. Affected assets:

| Image | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ee50ff3d...` (click.chp.kohls.com) | Coherent Path open pixel |
| `o.gif` (mi.kohls.com) | MessageGears open pixel |
| `event` (kohls.demdex.net) | AAM pixel |
| `2a5adc5a...`, `f441d45d...`, `c2f81e9a...`, `415c8a18...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Rendered content images |
| `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...` (mi.kohls.com/p/rp/) | Rendered content images |

Tracking pixels intentionally have no alt text — that's standard. However, the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` images appear to be **rendered content images** (product/promo art), not pixels. Missing alt text on those is a real accessibility gap; screen readers will announce the filename hash instead of describing the content.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-to-zoom. Accessibility issue for low-vision users; also flagged by WCAG 1.4.4.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` provides no document label for screen readers or preview panes.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** — Roboto and Montserrat are each requested twice (once via `<link>`, once via `@import url()`). Redundant round-trips, no functional breakage.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) detected in the visible HTML fragment. The `mi_u=604230016` subscriber ID is present and resolved in all pixel URLs, confirming merge fired correctly for this recipient.

`mi_ecmp=1016329_202671` campaign ID is consistent across all `mi.kohls.com` pixel URLs — no mismatch.

No issues found with personalization token rendering.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — not detected by QA relay. If absent at send time (not just a capture artifact), this violates Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (February 2024 mandate). Evidence: QA flag, header not found.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. Same caveat: may be relay artifact, but must be verified.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected** in HTML fragment — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. Not visible in the provided HTML. May be present in the truncated portion; must confirm.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown. `Kohls@s.kohls.com` sending domain should have DKIM-signed mail; verify DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` → `kohls.com`.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 destination URLs are behind click-redirect wrappers and could not be probed. UTM parameter presence on landing pages is **unverified**.

The `mi_ecmp` and `mi_mid` values in pixel URLs follow MessageGears conventions but are not UTM parameters — if Kohl's analytics stack relies on `utm_source/utm_campaign` on landing pages, that tracking must be injected by the click-redirect handler, not the email HTML itself. No evidence either way from the available source.

`email_name=260701_DG_Email_Wed_PM` is present in several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` image URLs — consistent naming convention, no mismatch detected.
