# 20% off won't stick around forever (hint, hint) ⏰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's thirteenth near-identical send since Oct 26. Like the Nov 4 "Take 20% off + find soft must-haves" email, the subject's promise isn't what the render leads with. The subject says "20% off won't stick around forever," but the top of the render is a rewards status bar ("Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER | Earn: 5% REWARDS") and a Shop Now button over a snowy hero. The 20% only shows up about a third of the way down, as "20% + 20% off w/coupon" on holiday home decor. The real coupon, "Take an extra 20% off, Code: SAVEBIG, Ends Nov. 5," is at the very bottom.
- The email is a long, crowded scroll: decor, a holiday party edit, a clearance block ("Save up to 70%"), delivery and returns services, and a four-product grid. Nothing is clearly the hero. The "Ends Nov. 5" date matches the subject's urgency, but the render never says so up top.
- Judgement: competent but diffuse. The subject promises one deadline-driven offer, and the body delivers a catalogue.

## What's working

- The "Ends Nov. 5" coupon with a barcode and code SAVEBIG makes the deadline concrete and redeemable.
- The "Save up to 70%" clearance block is high contrast and the strongest price signal in the render.
- The product grid shows real prices, including a strikethrough on the Ninja Slushi ($349.99 to $249.99).
- The Kohl's Rewards status bar personalizes the top of the email.
- The "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" band reduces purchase risk.

## What's weak

- The subject's 20% offer is buried: it shows only as a small "20% + 20% off w/coupon" tile mid-email, and the coupon itself is at the bottom.
- There are two Home/Women/Men/Kids nav bars and several competing Shop Now buttons, so there's no single primary CTA.
- The fine print and coupon-exclusion text at the bottom is dense.
- The "Picked with you in mind" product grid mixes kitchen, towels and mattress toppers with no clear tie to the holiday theme.
- Because the offers stack (20%, 70% clearance, "+20% w/coupon"), it's hard to tell what the 20% actually applies to.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the coupon block (extra 20% off, SAVEBIG, ends Nov. 5) to the top, under the header, and make it the hero.
- 2. Cut the layout to one or two themes, such as decor plus the party edit, and drop the duplicate nav bar.
- 3. Use one primary CTA, such as "Shop 20% off," and repeat it consistently.
- 4. Show a countdown or "Ends tonight" label near the top to match the subject.
- 5. Tie the product grid to the hero offer and label which items qualify.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 20% off, code SAVEBIG`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off expires today ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 70% off clearance and free returns through Jan. 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code SAVEBIG on holiday decor, party looks and more before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup has several malformed-HTML and accessibility defects (invalid `<head>` content, a stray `</custom>` tag, missing alt attributes, zoom disabled). The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and the truncated source, so they are unconfirmed. Sender: `Kohls@s.kohls.com`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains), so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown. Visible examples are opaque `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7Im...` tokens.
- **Stacked tracking pixels:** There are four before the first content table. All are 1×1 images, and each adds a third-party request on open.
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/f53e0453-...`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Malformed tag:** The `open.aspx` pixel is followed by a stray `</custom>` (`<img ... alt=""></custom>`). No `<custom>` element was opened, so this is invalid markup. Some parsers or sanitizers may mishandle it.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`. An empty href resolves to the document URL, so a client may request the email itself.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` with child `<meta>` tags sits inside `<head>`. A `<div>` is not allowed there, so parsers implicitly close the head and open the body early. This can misplace the `<style>` and `<link>` content that follows, and Gmail and Outlook strip schema microdata anyway.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw headers of the actual message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results`, and confirm the footer address. This resolves four of the QA warnings.
2. Move the `itemscope` div and its meta tags out of `<head>`, or drop them. Remove the stray `</custom>`. Give `<title>` and `<link rel="shortcut icon">` real values, or remove the icon link.
