# Doorbusters + 20% off + $15 Kohl's Cash = JACKPOT 🏆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same ~12,000px Kohl's Black Friday deal wall that has landed all week, with a new "JACKPOT" subject on top. The top of the render is solid. A "Stores open today at 5 AM" banner sits over a snowy hero with a 20% off badge and a "$15 Kohl's Cash" block. A "Free shipping with a $75 purchase" strip and a "Win up to $500 Kohl's Cash" sweepstakes tile follow. The "Doorbusters 3 days only" band comes next.
- Below that, the email is a long stack of category blocks. Each pairs a product photo with a stacked price badge, such as "60% + 20% off w/coupon", "$15.99" and "$29.99", and each has a "Shop Now" link. It renders cleanly, and I see no broken images or overlaps. The screenshot is heavily shrunk, so I can't read the fine print, and what I describe is from the layout and the larger text.
- The email fits the moment but doesn't stand out from the week's other sends. It has too many offers and no single focal point. Compared with yesterday's "Doorbusters 🛍️ Extra 20% off 🛍️ $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent", this subject reuses nearly the same three hooks. The only new element is "JACKPOT", and nothing in the body delivers on it. Verdict: competent, repetitive, and a 6–7 at best.

## What's working

- The hero stacks three clear offers: 20% off, a $15 Kohl's Cash block and free shipping.
- The black-and-gold "Doorbuster" badges make deals scannable, and the prices are large.
- Time-bound urgency is credible: "Stores open today at 5 AM" and "3 days only".
- The sweepstakes tile ("Win up to $500 Kohl's Cash") adds an engagement hook beyond the discount.
- The render is clean, with no visible broken images or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- There is no single hero. About 20 blocks compete for attention, and the eye gets nothing to land on.
- The "JACKPOT 🏆" subject promises a prize-like payoff, but the body is a standard deal wall. The only real jackpot is the sweepstakes, and it's buried near the top.
- Many blocks say "Coupons do not apply" or "Select styles", which undercuts the "+20% off w/coupon" headline.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now".
- This is another near-identical Black Friday send. It follows 11/26 "Doorbusters are here for 3 days only" and 11/26 "Doorbusters … $15 Kohl's Cash", so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to a hero plus 4–6 doorbusters. Put the $15 Kohl's Cash and the 20% coupon in one hero with a single button.
- 2. Make the subject honest and shorter. Drop "JACKPOT", or tie it to the sweepstakes only.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Shop 60% off kitchen" or "Get the $29.99 Nike jacket".
- 4. Move the exclusions into one visible line near the hero. Right now they repeat in every block.
- 5. Suppress or segment repeat Black Friday sends. Send a last-chance variant to non-openers instead of a fifth full wall.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several markup-validity and accessibility defects: a stray `</custom>` tag, an `<itemscope>` div inside `<head>`, missing alt text on content images, and zoom disabled in the viewport meta. The automated compliance and authentication warnings come mostly from relay and truncation limits, so they are unconfirmed. The source also shows an empty dynamic parameter (`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`) on a Kohl's Cash image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **102 tracking links were not probed.** All are redirect wrappers, such as `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`. Destination status, redirect chains and expiry can't be verified from this data.
- **Four tracking beacons load before any visible content**, from four separate vendors:
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/d5545e9e-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant open tracking, and one failing host could delay image loading.
- **Plain-text part is 75% URLs** (18,012 of 23,864 chars) because of long `qs=` tokens. This is a spam-filter risk, and it is the QA tool's `>70%` threshold, not a fabricated figure.
- **The recipient-level ID `mi_u=604230016` is embedded in image URLs.** The same identifier is sent to every image host, which is a privacy and caching consideration.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray `</custom>`:** the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx…` `<img>` is followed by `</custom>`, which has no opening tag. This is malformed HTML.
- **`<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`.** It is invalid there, and parsers will close `<head>` early and move the div and metas into `<body>`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** and **`<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`** (empty href).
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text on content images**, per QA:
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`
  - `…/f441d45d2128ae53.png`
  - `…/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`
  - `…/415c8a18fc2950ea.png`
  - `…/69494b48a79b4240.png`
  - `…/78308d1d9adb0751.png`
  - `…/916d6fa08d185233.png`
  - `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x`
  - `…/60699446_3_2x`
  
  If these carry offers or CTAs, the content is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Beacon images lacking `alt`:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` and `kohls.demdex.net/event` have no `alt` attribute and should get `alt=""`.
  - The QA flag on `o.gif` looks like a false positive, because the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the markup uses XML-style `/>`.** This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.
- **Empty dynamic field:** image URL `…916d6fa08d185233.png?…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…` has a blank `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`. This is the Kohl's Cash redemption end date, and the subject promotes $15 Kohl's Cash. It may render without dates or fall back to a default. Confirm against the rendered image.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation. Verify against raw headers from a direct send.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed either way.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** absent because of the relay. The From domain `s.kohls.com` aligns with the tracking domains, but authentication status is unknown.
- **No confirmed violations.**

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** The visible links are all wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…` redirects, and the final URLs weren't resolved.
- **Campaign identifiers are present on images only:** `mi_ecmp=1026832_20261127` and `email_name=261127_DG_Email_Friday`. Whether they carry through to the landing pages is unconfirmed.
- **Landing-page alignment** with the doorbusters, 20% off and $15 Kohl's Cash offers can't be assessed, because the probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag, and move the `itemscope` div and `<meta itemprop>` tags out of `<head>` (use JSON-LD or drop them).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/` and `demdex` beacons.
3. Drop `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Populate `<title>` and remove the empty `rel="shortcut icon"` link.
4. Fix the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` value in the personalization feed, and check that the Kohl's Cash expiry renders.
5. Consolidate the four open-tracking pixels to reduce load and privacy surface.
6. Verify the unsubscribe headers, authentication results and footer address from a direct, non-relay send. Resolve a sample of the 102 tracked links to confirm UTM carry-through and 200 status.
7. Shorten the plain-text part, for example with shorter `qs=` tokens or fewer links, to bring URL density below 70%.
