# 40% off is here ... time to shop 🛒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eighth 40%-off email in roughly four days. After the July 20th send finally broke the cycle with an 85%-off clearance headline, this one snaps back to the same "40% off" formula that has now worn a groove into the inbox. The subject — "40% off is here ... time to shop 🛒" — is essentially a merger of two previous subjects ("Open for your 40% off deal" and "40% off ends today") without any of the urgency or novelty that made those marginally defensible.
- Inside, the email is genuinely packed: a "TODAY ONLY" hero on Kohl's brands, an 85% clearance block, an FLX athletic section at up to 50% off, a family apparel grid, a grocery/home section, a coupon barcode strip, and a Kohl's Cash earn reminder. That density is a double-edged sword. There's real value here for a Kohl's shopper, but no single offer gets enough breathing room to land. The eye doesn't know where to go first.
- The honest verdict: this email has the bones of a solid promotional send, but it arrives as the caboose of an exhausted campaign. A subscriber who has opened and ignored the last three 40%-off emails is not going to be moved by "is here ... time to shop."

## What's working

- **Multi-tier offer stack is genuinely strong** — 40% off Kohl's brands + 85% clearance + 50% FLX athletic all in one scroll gives deal-seekers multiple entry points.
- **FLX athletic section has real visual energy** — the lifestyle imagery breaks the sea of product grids and points toward a specific, differentiated product line.
- **"TODAY ONLY" urgency in the hero is prominent** — even if the credibility is shot by cadence, the placement and color contrast make it visible immediately.
- **Kohl's Cash earn reminder is present** — loyalty members can see the earning mechanic without hunting for it.
- **No visible render bugs** — the email renders cleanly; no broken images, overlapping text, or layout collapse observed.

## What's weak

- **Subject is the same offer for the eighth time** — "40% off is here" is a near-word-for-word echo of prior sends; there's zero signal that a subscriber should open this one versus the last three.
- **Visual hierarchy is fragmented** — five distinct product sections with competing "Shop Now" CTAs mean no single offer dominates, and the eye exits before committing to any.
- **"TODAY ONLY" credibility is effectively zero** — this subscriber has seen 40% off labeled as today-only, ending-today, and here-today-gone-tomorrow across multiple consecutive days.
- **CTA copy is entirely generic** — every button reads "Shop Now" or "Shop [Category]"; not one drives toward a specific, compelling action.
- **Grocery/home section feels off-brand mid-email** — the "Great taste you can take home" block arrives after athletic and family apparel and reads like a separate email grafted in; it diffuses focus further.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Change the subject entirely** — lean on the multi-offer stack rather than the nth 40%-off restatement. The 85% clearance is actually the headline; use it or pair it with FLX.
- **Subject Alt A:** `85% off clearance + 50% FLX athletic — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New week, new deals — 85% off clearance starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off Kohl's brands with your coupon — all stacking today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance prices, FLK drops, and Kohl's Cash — three reasons to open.`
- 2. **Lead with the sharpest offer, not the most repeated one** — swap the hero from "40% off Kohl's brands" to the 85% clearance block. That's the differentiated claim; 40% off is noise at this point.
- 3. **Kill or consolidate the grocery/home section** — it arrives too late and for too different an audience. Either remove it or surface it only in a segmented send to buyers of those categories.
- 4. **Give FLX athletic a dedicated CTA** — "Shop FLX Now" or "Shop Athletic Drops" is more compelling than "Shop Now" and targets the subscriber who showed interest in activewear.
- 5. **Rest 40% off for at least 48 hours** — no copy or creative change will fix a frequency problem. If Kohl's sends this again tomorrow, the entire 40%-off frame will be meaningless to the subscriber.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "40% off is here" (2026-07-21)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but carries three compliance gaps (missing unsubscribe headers, no confirmed physical address), a likely unfired merge token in an image beacon URL, and an invalid custom HTML tag. Duplicate Google Fonts loading adds unnecessary overhead.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links unprobed** — all clicks route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) and `click.s.kohls.com`; final destinations and UTM passthrough could not be verified by QA.

**Three distinct tracking pixels in play:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/db17c9a5...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbb.../o.gif` — Marigold/Sailthru open pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**Invalid closing tag on open pixel:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?2OGBJMZQVIY...">
</custom>   ← invalid element; should be absent or </div>
```
`</custom>` is a non-standard element that will be misinterpreted by strict parsers and may cause rendering issues in some clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** — flagged by QA across tracking pixels and content images including `55869996_root2_2x` (a product image on `images.am.kohls.com`). Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive text.

**Mobile zoom disabled:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
`user-scalable=0` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Gmail Android and some accessibility tools will flag this.

**Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title></title>` renders as blank in clients that surface the title (e.g., some webmail preheader fallbacks).

**Duplicate font loading** — Roboto and Montserrat are loaded twice each: once via `<link>` and again via `@import` inside `<style>`. The MSO conditional comment gates the `<link>` tags correctly, but the `@import` rules are outside any conditional, causing double requests in non-MSO clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Likely unfired token in image beacon URL:**
```
https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png
  ?mi_u=604230016
  &mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721
  &mi_kc_redm_end_dte=        ← empty value
  &mi_kc_event_pe             ← value missing / truncated in source
```
`mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` has no value. If this is a Marigold personalization field (e.g., offer end date for dynamic content), it did not populate. The truncated `mi_kc_event_pe` param is also suspicious.

**Subscriber ID appears correctly resolved:** `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721` are present and consistent across all beacon URLs. `email_name=260721_DG_Email_Tuesday` is also consistently applied.

No `{{token}}`, `%%var%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`-style literals visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| List-Unsubscribe header | MISSING | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) | MISSING | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |
| Physical mailing address | NOT DETECTED | QA found no address in parsed HTML; may be in truncated lower body |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | UNKNOWN | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture |

**List-Unsubscribe** absence is the most critical: Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for native unsubscribe UI; missing it increases spam complaint rate and risks filtering. **RFC 8058** compliance (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is now enforced by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders.

**Physical address** must be verified in the full HTML below the fold — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires it in the body. If absent, this is a legal violation.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM passthrough unverifiable** — all 22 destination links are wrapped in `click.chp.kohls.com` redirects which QA skipped. Cannot confirm whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are appended to final kohls.com URLs.

**Campaign naming is consistent in beacons** (`email_name=260721_DG_Email_Tuesday`, `mi_ecmp=1018062_2026721`) but these only appear in image tracking URLs, not in click-through links — so attribution depends entirely on CoherentPath's redirect behavior.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />`** is set in the head. This suppresses the HTTP Referer header on all link clicks, which will cause kohls.com analytics to show direct traffic rather than email referral for any non-UTM-tagged links.
