# LAST CHANCE to earn $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's Early Arrival Black Friday email again. The creative is the same long deal wall as the Nov 6 and Nov 7 sends, and the "LAST CHANCE" subject is the only new part. The screenshot is 680x12000 shown at about 113 wide, so I can read the layout and the large numerals but not the fine print or small product copy. At the top is a snowy "EARLY ARRIVAL / Black Friday Deals" banner with "Up to 50% off" and "20% off" panels. Below that is a "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge and a free-shipping line. After those come a category nav grid, a "Shop Daily Deals" block, and many themed modules with big "50% off" and "+20% off w/coupon" numerals.
- The subject promises one thing, the $15 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent. The body mixes in the 50% off, the 20% coupon, the daily deals and free shipping, so the Kohl's Cash offer is only one of five competing messages. "LAST CHANCE" is credible urgency, but Kohl's has sent this offer in a similar form since Nov 6 ("TODAY ONLY earn $15 Kohl's Cash"). A real subscriber would see the repeated "last chance" and discount it.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand promo that is too dense to convert well and has no fresh angle. It will perform like the Nov 6 and Nov 7 sends, around 7/10 for business impact.

## What's working

- The "$15 Kohl's Cash" badge sits right under the hero, so the subject's promise is visible near the top.
- The offer numerals ("50%", "60%", "70%") are large and scannable.
- The category nav grid near the top helps shoppers jump to a section.
- Many "Shop Now" links go to specific categories or products rather than one homepage link.
- The Black Friday theming is on-season and consistent with the brand's recent sends.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete (up to 50% off, 20% coupon, Kohl's Cash, free shipping, Daily Deals), so the subject's single message gets diluted.
- The email is very long. Dozens of modules repeat near-identical "Up to 50% off + 20% off w/coupon" tiles.
- The "Coupons do not apply to select brands" exclusion language repeats under many modules and reads as buried fine print.
- "LAST CHANCE" follows "TODAY ONLY" from Nov 6, which weakens the urgency.
- The product CTAs read as generic "Shop Now", with no specific product or benefit named.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer, the $15 Kohl's Cash per $50, and put the spend-and-earn mechanic in the hero. Show the deadline as a clock or date and use one primary button, "Earn My $15 Kohl's Cash".
- 2. Limit the body to 3-4 modules (Daily Deals, top categories, one featured product) instead of 15 or more.
- 3. State the exclusions once in the footer instead of under each tile.
- 4. Stop reusing "TODAY ONLY" and "LAST CHANCE" on consecutive sends. Use a real end time such as "ends 11:59 PM CT".
- 5. Use the preheader to add something the subject doesn't say.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $15 Kohl's Cash for every $50 🏃‍♀️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $15 Kohl's Cash per $50 expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off and an extra 20% with coupon. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $50, get $15 to use later. Daily Deals and free shipping included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects (stray `</custom>` tag, `<div>` inside `<head>`, empty `<title>`, zoom-blocking viewport) and missing alt text on content images. Compliance can't be confirmed: the QA warnings come from missing headers the relay may have stripped, and the HTML provided is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 91 tracking links were skipped by the probe, so none of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` CTA destinations were validated. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed breakage.
- **Plain-text weight:** 16,076 of 21,892 characters (73%) of the plain-text part are URLs. That is over the 70% threshold and is a mild deliverability and readability risk. It comes from long redirect URLs.
- **Four tracking vendors fire per open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` (1x1 pixel)
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif` (1x1 pixel)
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...` (1x1 pixel)
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (`width="0" height="0"`)

  Three of the four open pixels are redundant. The two 1x1 pixels, `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` and `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`, have no inline hiding style. The `chp` pixel has no `alt` and sits directly under `<body>`.
- **Malformed tag:** the `open.aspx` pixel is closed with `</custom>` (`<img ... alt=""></custom>`). This is an invalid orphan closing tag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA flagged alt-less images. Two groups matter differently:
  - **Tracking pixels** (`22255771-...`, `o.gif`, `event`): `o.gif` has `aria-hidden="true"` and an `alt=""` attribute, so it is cosmetic. The `chp` pixel and `demdex` image have no alt at all, so add `alt=""`.
  - **Content images** (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`): these are real and need descriptive alt text. If these images carry the offer, it is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` is placed inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at that point.
- **Empty favicon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`, which can trigger a request to the document URL.
- **Duplicate font loading:** Montserrat is loaded by both `<link>` and `@import`, and `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared twice. Neither is harmful, but both are redundant.
- **Mixed doctype:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is used with XHTML-style `/>` self-closing tags. This is low severity.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** the preheader table (`width='640'`) and the 1x1 and AAM tracking tables have no `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables do have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) appear in the visible source or subject line. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may not expose these headers, so treat this as unconfirmed, not a sender fault. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this scale.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The provided HTML is truncated before the footer, so this can't be confirmed either way.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the tracking host `click.s.kohls.com`, which is a good sign, but alignment isn't proof.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** all click URLs are opaque `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirects, so UTMs and landing-page alignment can't be verified. The probe skipped all 91 links.
- **Campaign identifier:** the only visible one is in image URLs: `email_name=261106_DG_Email_Fri_PM`, with `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp=1025350_2026116`. The `261106` date suffix suggests a send date of 2026-11-06 (Friday), which fits the "LAST CHANCE" subject. There is no mismatch to flag.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to the seven `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` content images. Add `alt=""` to the `chp` and `demdex` pixels.
2. **Fix the viewport meta:** remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`.
3. **Fix the markup:**
   - Remove the stray `</custom>`.
   - Move the `schema.org` div out of `<head>`, or use JSON-LD.
   - Fill in `<title>`.
   - Remove the empty favicon `<link>`.
4. **Consolidate tracking pixels:** drop redundant open pixels where possible, and give the remaining ones a consistent hiding style.
5. **Re-run compliance checks on raw headers and the full HTML:** confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and the footer's address and unsubscribe link. The current warnings may be artifacts of the relay and the truncation.
6. **Verify click destinations** by resolving a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com` redirects in a follow-up pass, to confirm UTMs and landing-page alignment.
