# Extra 20% off savings are waiting ...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's is now roughly fifteen promotional emails deep in eleven days, and this one makes no attempt to escape the rut. The structure is identical to the last several sends: 20% off banner, women's lifestyle hero, a men's panel, sandals, catch-all product grid, Kohl's Cash footnote. What saves this email from being entirely forgettable is the linen headline — "Light and breezy linen (at a light and breezy cost)" is actual copywriting, and the specific price points ($34.99, $29.99) lend the discount real credibility rather than pure percentage theater.
- The problem is cadence math. After the July 8–9 closing blitz of 30%-off "ENDS TODAY" emails, this email asks shoppers to get excited about 20% off again — a step-down that already landed badly in the July 9 morning send. This one compounds it. "Savings are waiting" is among the softest urgency phrases in retail email, and it's being used for the fifteenth time this fortnight.
- Judgment: technically clean, seasonally coherent, strategically inert. This email would land fine if it were the third or fourth send of the month. As the fifteenth-plus, diminishing returns are setting in hard.

## What's working

- "Light and breezy linen (at a light and breezy cost)" is a tight, on-tone headline — the wordplay earns its place and the category is immediately clear.
- Specific price anchors ($34.99, $29.99) alongside the 20% banner give the discount concrete legs, unlike the vaguer hero copy from the July 10 "fill your cart" send.
- Seasonal coherence holds: linen, sandals, and "summer picks" all pull in the same direction.
- Kohl's Cash earn/redeem dates are visible in the footer (through Jul 19, redeem Jul 19–30) — actual calendar urgency rather than phantom urgency.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds.

## What's weak

- "Savings are waiting" in the subject is limp — zero urgency, zero differentiation from any prior send.
- "With coupon" appears on every product price tile but is never disclosed at the banner level, so the deal is gated on a mechanism the hero doesn't acknowledge.
- All three primary CTAs are "Shop Now" — none of them tell you what you're shopping for.
- "Summer picks that get voted back next year" is unquantified and unlinked; it reads like ad copy posturing as editorial.
- The Kohl's Cash date (Jul 19) is a real urgency driver buried in the footer — it should be above the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Disclose the coupon at the banner level.** The 20% offer is genuine, but shoppers who bounce at checkout because they didn't realize a coupon was required won't convert. One line in the banner fixes it.
- 2. **Make CTAs category-specific.** "Shop Linen," "Shop Sandals," "Shop Men's Shirts" — match the button copy to the hero panel it lives under.
- 3. **Move the Kohl's Cash cutoff date above the fold.** "Earn through Jul 19" is the only real deadline in this email; it's wasted in the footer.
- 4. **Cut the men's shirt panel or fold it into the grid section.** The linen story and the sandals story are complementary summer narratives; the men's patterned shirt is an orphan that dilutes both.
- 5. **Put a deadline in the subject line.** The Jul 19 Kohl's Cash cutoff is the strongest urgency signal available — use it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Linen looks under $35 — coupon inside, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn Kohl's Cash through Jul 19 — extra 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clip your coupon at checkout — linen, sandals, summer picks`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn Kohl's Cash this week only — redeem Jul 19–30`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 20% off savings are waiting"

**Sender:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-07-11

### 1. Technical Summary

Structurally sound HTML4 email with standard Kohl's ESP infrastructure (Coherent Path + MessageMedia). Primary concerns are missing authentication header passthrough via the AgentMail relay, absent one-click unsubscribe compliance headers, and pervasive missing `alt` text on tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

22 click-tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe — all routed through `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`. This is expected behavior; no broken destination URLs were detected, but destination integrity cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects.

Three separate pixel/beacon systems are active in this send:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — Coherent Path open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — MessageMedia open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open tracker
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel

**Issue:** The HTML contains a malformed tag on the third open pixel:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
```
The `</custom>` closing tag is invalid HTML and will produce a parse error in strict renderers. It is likely harmless in practice but indicates a templating artifact that should be cleaned.

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (the `o.gif` pixel does this correctly — the others do not). Content images must have descriptive alt text for screen reader users and images-off rendering.

**`<title>` is empty.** The `<title></title>` tag is blank, which some clients (notably older Outlook) use as the preview or window label.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** prevents users from zooming, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure and blocked in iOS 10+.

**Google Fonts** loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` for both Roboto and Montserrat — redundant double-load, though the `!mso` conditional guard means Outlook skips it correctly.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** via AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM and Gmail/Yahoo sender requirements (effective Feb 2024) mandate this header for bulk senders. The header may exist on the original SMTP envelope but is not passing through the relay — this needs verification at the MTA level.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not detected.** One-click unsubscribe is required by Google and Yahoo for senders above 5K/day. Absence here either means the header is being stripped by the relay or was never set. If stripped, Gmail may demote deliverability.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured message. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` is an ESP subdomain — alignment with `kohls.com` DMARC policy depends on DKIM signing by Kohl's key, not the ESP's. This should be verified at the relay.

**Physical address** — not visible in truncated source; assumed present in footer per CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5). Needs confirmation against full HTML.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1017307_2026711`) is used consistently in MessageMedia tracking URLs but functions as an internal campaign ID, not a UTM parameter. It is not human-readable and will not surface in Google Analytics/GA4 without a UTM mapping layer.

No `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` parameters are visible in the tracking pixel URLs. Whether these are appended to destination click URLs cannot be confirmed without resolving the 22 skipped tracking links. If UTM params are absent from destination URLs, site analytics will attribute Kohl's email traffic to direct/none.
