# Oooh, it’s 50% off select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises "50% off select beauty" but that's nowhere near what greets you when the email opens — the hero is "The Hair Styling Awards," a hair-product performance showcase with testimonial quotes, category winners, and a "Take the Quiz" module. You have to scroll past roughly ten screens of haircare content before the 50% off Stacking Weekend banner finally shows up, stacked awkwardly next to a 15%-off-shampoo offer and the recurring 500-bonus-points circle. This is a genuine subject/body mismatch, not just a busy layout.
- Compared to the run of Stacking Weekend sends since 9/4 (last seen 9/8 at "LAST CHANCE... 50% off"), this is the first one where the flagship 50%-off beauty offer is demoted to a minor footer module rather than the hero. It reads like two campaigns — a Hair Styling Awards newsletter and the ongoing Stacking Weekend promo — got merged, and whoever wrote the subject line pulled from the wrong campaign.
- Net: strong production values (clean product cards, consistent badges, testimonial quotes) but a badly misaligned subject that will frustrate anyone who opens expecting a beauty deal and lands in a hair-awards showcase instead.

## What's working

- Hero banner is crisp: bold "The Hair Styling Awards" title with a clear "12 outstanding performers" subhead and visible "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Consistent, well-organized product grid by category (Sleekness & Shine, Definition, Volume) with star ratings, testimonial pull-quotes, and green "verified" checkmarks that build credibility.
- "Free Hair Trial Size" teaser up top gives an immediate low-friction incentive before the main content.
- No visible render bugs — images, prices, and badges all load cleanly with consistent spacing.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "50% off select beauty" but that offer doesn't appear until deep in the scroll, buried below ~10 screens of unrelated hair-award content — a bait-and-switch feel.
- No clear single primary CTA — the email juggles hair trial size, awards quiz, 15% shampoo/conditioner, 500 bonus points, AND 50% off beauty, diluting focus.
- The 50% off Beauty module itself is visually small and cramped next to a competing 15%-off offer, undercutting what should be the star deal per the subject.
- Extremely long single-scroll email (haircare-heavy) risks fatigue before the reader ever reaches the promised discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to match the actual hero content (Hair Styling Awards) — sending "50% off beauty" copy against this email is misleading and will tank trust/click-through once opened.
- 2. If the intent really is to promote the 50% off beauty deal, move that banner to the top of the email, above the Hair Styling Awards content, matching the subject's promise.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA per send — either lead this as a Hair Styling Awards editorial or a Stacking Weekend promo, not both diluted together.
- 4. Enlarge and isolate the 50% off Beauty module from the competing 15% shampoo/conditioner offer so it doesn't get lost.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Hair Styling Awards: 12 winners, judged by real reviews`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We tested every hairstyling product. These 12 won.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus your free hair trial size, on us`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shine, definition, volume — the category winners are in`
