# Time for something new 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's second "new arrivals" grid in three days — same skeleton as the 08/08 "Ready, set, shop" send, just with a fresh "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero and a rotated product lineup (MAC, Too Faced, Josie Maran, Fenty, Nest New York, Banu, and six more). Structurally it's competent: same-day delivery banner up top, a mascara trial-size promo, a hero with two anchor products, then a scannable NEW-tagged grid, then App Early Access / Try It Love It / fragrance modules, then the loyalty tier footer. Nothing is broken and the brand voice is consistent, but there's no real offer beyond the trial-size mascara card and the vague "$10 bonus card" banner — this is a browse email, not a convert email.
- The subject "Time for something new 🛍️" is generic enough that it barely differentiates from 08/08's "Ready, set, shop—new beauty has landed," and a subscriber who opened that one three days ago may feel this is a rerun. Solid execution, weak differentiation and weak urgency — this lands mid-pack for the brand, not a standout.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered hero ("SOMETHING NEW IS HERE") with a clear single CTA button ("SHOP NOW") that's easy to spot on mobile without excess scrolling.
- Product grid is well organized — consistent NEW/limited-edition/clean tags, price visible on every card, two-column layout scans easily.
- Top banner stacks two real, specific offers (Same-Day Delivery/free shipping threshold, $10 bonus card with 75+ spend) rather than vague copy.
- Fenty Beauty by Rihanna and Josie Maran cards include a "$26 value" and "Clean at Sephora" callouts — concrete, credible detail that supports the click.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- No hero-level discount or timed urgency — the only real offer is the trial-size mascara card, which competes visually with the "SOMETHING NEW" hero instead of being subordinate to it.
- Subject and hero copy are both generic "new arrivals" framing, nearly interchangeable with the 08/08 send two days prior — high risk of cadence fatigue for a daily-ish opener.
- The grid is 12 products across ~6 unrelated brands and categories (skincare, fragrance, makeup, hair) with no organizing narrative — it reads more like a catalog dump than a curated story.
- Preview text isn't visible/distinct in the screenshot — no complementary preview copy reinforcing the subject with a stronger hook.
- "Fragrance Worth the Hype" and "Try It, Love It" modules are buried after two full screens of scrolling — most mobile readers won't reach them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the hero a real, specific offer or narrow the "new" claim to one flagship drop (e.g., lead with Fenty Beauty by Rihanna's limited-edition duo) instead of a 12-SKU multi-brand dump — a focused hero converts better than a browse grid.
- 2. Differentiate the subject from 08/08's near-identical "new arrivals" framing — name a specific brand or product to break the sameness pattern the brand history shows building up.
- 3. Move the trial-size mascara card so it doesn't visually compete with the hero CTA — either lead with it as the hero offer or push it lower as a secondary module.
- 4. Add a real preview/preheader that teases a specific product or brand rather than defaulting to boilerplate — right now there's no evidence of complementary preview copy doing any work.
- 5. Trim the grid to 6-8 products organized around one theme (e.g., "clean beauty picks" or "this week's launches") rather than an unthemed 12-product sprawl.
