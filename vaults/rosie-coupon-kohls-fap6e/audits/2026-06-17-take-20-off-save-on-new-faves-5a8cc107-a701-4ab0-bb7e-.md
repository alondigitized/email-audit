# Take 20% off & save on new faves 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seventh Father's Day-adjacent email in ten days, and the creative strategy has broken down. Sent June 17 — four days before Father's Day — this should be a laser-focused urgency close. Instead it arrives as a kitchen-sink send: general savings hero, an athletic TEK GEAR mid-section, a Father's Day "Last Chance" block, a Pride Month add-on, and a footer coupon. None of these sections earns full attention because all of them compete.
- The subject line makes it worse. "Take 20% off & save on new faves" deliberately abandons Father's Day framing just when calendar pressure was the brand's single strongest lever. The June 15 send ("Get gifts Dad loves (even in a time crunch)") scored a 9/10 precisely because it leaned into urgency. This send walks that back two days later and dilutes the moment.
- The 20% off offer is real and the Kohl's Cash earn deadline (June 21) is surfaced — those are genuine assets. But they're buried inside a scroll-heavy, four-section email that asks the reader to decide what they're shopping for rather than telling them. The brand needed one clear message here: you have four days. This is not that email.

## What's working

- **20% off badge is prominent** in the hero section — the core offer is not hidden.
- **Kohl's Cash earn-through deadline (June 21) is shown** in the footer, giving the coupon real stakes.
- **Father's Day "LAST CHANCE" block** finally uses the calendar pressure the brand has been sitting on since June 11.
- **TEK GEAR product category** is a credible standalone pitch with its own visual identity and defined CTAs (Kids / Men).
- **Pride Month block** is tasteful and brief — doesn't overstay its welcome.

## What's weak

- **Subject line abandons Father's Day urgency** with 4 days left — "new faves" is exactly the wrong pivot for this calendar position.
- **Four competing hero sections** destroy visual hierarchy; no single offer owns the scroll.
- **All CTAs say "Shop Now"** or are category tabs — zero copy specificity across the entire email.
- **Same 20% off offer for the seventh consecutive email** — deal fatigue is real by this point; nothing has changed to justify a fresh open.
- **Pride Month module is logistically jarring** sandwiched between a Father's Day Last Chance block and the footer coupon — the editorial logic doesn't hold.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one section, one ask.** The June 17 send should be Father's Day only — "4 days left, guaranteed delivery by Father's Day, here's your 20% off." Cut TEK GEAR, cut Pride to a footer link, eliminate the general "new faves" hero entirely.
- 2. **Subject must signal the deadline.** "New faves" is the wrong hook four days out — it reads like the brand forgot what week it is.
- 3. **Add a delivery guarantee** if Kohl's can support it ("order by [date] for guaranteed Father's Day delivery") — that's the only information that actually moves a June 17 decision.
- 4. **Swap "Shop Now" for action-specific CTAs** — "Shop Men's Gifts," "Shop Tek Gear for Dad," etc. One verb, one noun.
- 5. **Move the Kohl's Cash earn deadline above the fold** — "$10 Kohl's Cash for every $50, through June 21" is a genuine urgency driver buried in the footer.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Take 20% off & save on new faves" (2026-06-17)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally functional but carries an invalid HTML element, redundant font-loading, a cluster of missing alt attributes on content images, and several compliance gaps (missing physical address, no confirmed one-click unsubscribe, no verifiable SPF/DKIM). Three separate impression-tracking pixels from different vendors suggest fragmented measurement infrastructure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid closing tag in body**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?VXP6OGMMJBOUHL5JQQH4WIL3SA.60269..." alt=""></custom>
```
`</custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Some parsers will surface this as a stray node; in strict XML-mode renderers (older Exchange/OWA) it may corrupt the surrounding table cell.

**Three simultaneous open-pixel vendors**
The email fires impression pixels from three distinct systems on load:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/ada16839...` — CoherentPath
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1015413_2026617` — MessageGears
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?VXP6OGMMJBOUHL5JQQH4WIL3SA.60269` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager (fourth, for segmentation)

Multiple open-tracking signals produce non-reconciled open counts across platforms; any discrepancy is untraceable to a single source of truth.

**22 click links un-probeable**
All CTA links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` redirects. QA could not confirm destination URLs, UTM integrity, or 404 status for any of them.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the document title when entering the email. An empty title gives no context; should be `<title>Kohl's: Take 20% off</title>` or similar.

**Zoom disabled via viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">
```
`user-scalable=0` / `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom on iOS/Android. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize text) failure and an accessibility concern for low-vision users.

**11 content images missing alt text**
All images flagged by QA are missing `alt` attributes or have empty ones where descriptive text is needed. The most impactful is the product image:
```
https://images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/54594744_5_2x
```
Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, open pixel) should carry `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` — the o.gif pixel does have `aria-hidden="true"` but still lacks `alt=""`.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading**
Montserrat and Roboto are loaded via both `<link>` elements and `@import` inside `<style>`:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700&display=swap" ...>
<!-- AND -->
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700&display=swap);
```
This is a double HTTP request per font family on clients that load web fonts. Deduplication is trivial.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Truncated `email_name` parameter**
Multiple `mi.kohls.com` image URLs carry:
```
email_name=260617_DG_Email_Wednesd
```
The value is cut off mid-word ("Wednesd" → presumably "Wednesday"). If this parameter is used for campaign attribution or reporting dashboards, the truncation will cause mismatches or null lookups.

**Empty merge tokens on final image**
```
https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/916d6fa08d185233.png?...&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
`mi_kc_redm_end_dte` and `mi_kc_event_pe` are empty — either conditional content that was not suppressed when unpopulated, or unfilled merge fields. These will log empty strings into reporting and may indicate a template logic gap.

**No unresolved `{{` / `%%` tokens visible**
In the truncated source no raw merge syntax is visible. Confirmed only for the portion provided.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| List-Unsubscribe header | **Not confirmed** | QA: header absent or not relayed by AgentMail |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058 one-click) | **Missing** | QA: header not found — Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirement |
| Physical mailing address | **Not found** | QA: no address detected in truncated HTML; CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5) violation if absent from full email |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | **Unknown** | QA: Authentication-Results header not captured by relay |
| noindex/nofollow meta | Pass | `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` present |
| no-referrer meta | Noted | `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` will suppress referrer on any non-tracked link clicks — intentional but worth confirming |

The `List-Unsubscribe-Post` gap is the highest-risk item: Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender policies require one-click unsubscribe on commercial mail above 5k/day. If Kohl's volume exceeds that threshold, absence of this header risks deliverability penalties.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable**
All 22 CTAs are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` redirects. The final destination URLs — and whether they carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` — cannot be confirmed from the HTML source. No UTM parameters are visible in the pre-redirect layer.

**`no-referrer` meta suppresses fallback attribution**
For any user who right-clicks → opens in browser, or whose client does not follow the click redirect, the `no-referrer` meta tag will strip referrer from the HTTP request. Any session that lands on kohls.com without a UTM will also lack referrer, making it unattributable.

**Campaign ID consistency**
`mi_ecmp=1015413_2026617` appears consistently across MessageGears image pixels. This is the expected internal campaign identifier but is not visible in the SFMC or CoherentPath click-tracking paths — cross-system reconciliation requires a mapping table.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**Critical**
1. **Add physical mailing address** to email footer if not present in the untruncated body — CAN-SPAM mandatory.
2. **Add `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header** (RFC 8058) for one-click unsubscribe compliance with Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy.
3. **Remove `</custom>` tag** on line immediately following the SFMC open pixel — invalid element, potential rendering breakage in strict parsers.

**High**
4. **Remove `user-scalable=0` / `maximum-scale=1`** from viewport meta — accessibility failure, blocked zoom.
5. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the product image (`54594744_5_2x`) and all content images; assign `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` to all tracking pixels.
6. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` header** is present at SMTP layer (may be a relay capture gap — verify against raw headers from a seed inbox).

**Medium**
7. **Fix `email_name` truncation** — `260617_DG_Email_Wednesd` is cut off; audit the template variable length limit in the MessageGears send configuration.
8. **Suppress or populate** `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` and `mi_kc_event_pe` — empty parameters indicate unfired conditional logic.
9. **Deduplicate Google Fonts loading** — remove either the `<link>` tags or the `@import` rules, not both.
10. **Add non-empty `<title>`** for screen reader context.

**Low**
11. **Consolidate open-pixel vendors** (CoherentPath + MessageGears + SFMC + Adobe AAM) or document the reconciliation process — four systems tracking opens will produce divergent counts without a defined source of truth.
12. **Verify UTM parameters** on all 22 click destinations by probing the redirect chain in a controlled environment — currently unauditable from HTML alone.
