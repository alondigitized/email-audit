# Get 20% off | Check off your holiday list 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a long-scroll Kohl's gift-guide email with a subject line that promises something the top of the render doesn't deliver. The subject says "Get 20% off", but the hero I can see leads with "Ends Nov. 21 / Shop Now" and a "Plus, earn Kohl's Cash" tag. The 20% appears only in the coupon block at the bottom: "Take an extra 20% off", with a barcode and "Ends Nov. 21". A subscriber who opens for 20% off has to scroll roughly the whole email to find it.
- The body is a clear gift-by-budget structure: "$25 & Under" for her and for him, "$50 & Under" for the host, and toys at $50. That is useful for holiday shoppers, and the Rewards-member "$10 off $50 toys" offer adds a second hook. But the email then stacks a Shark deal, an "Online Only Daily Deals" banner, a "Preview the Deals" app promo, a Klarna/Doordash/Uber Eats services block and a product grid. It is a deal wall with no single dominant message. This is the same long-scroll pattern as the Nov 14–15 Super Saturday sends, and it lands in the same 7/10 band as those sends.
- Overall the email is decent and on-season but not sharp. It would be stronger if the 20% off coupon were the hero.
- Caveat: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so fine print and small text were not legible. My read rests on the large copy and layout.

## What's working

- The gift-by-budget tiles ("$25 & Under", "$50 & Under", For Her / For Him / For the Host) are easy to scan and fit the holiday shopping moment.
- A visible Kohl's Rewards / Kohl's Cash header and the "$10 off $50 toys" member offer give loyalty members a clear benefit.
- The product grid shows real prices (Slushie maker $299.99, mattress topper, jeans $29.99, Keurig $159.99), so there is something concrete to click.
- Deadlines are stated ("Ends Nov. 21", "Nov. 22–25" for the preview) and the stacked coupons with codes and barcodes are shoppable.
- I see no obvious broken images or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- The 20% off headline from the subject is buried in the bottom coupon block instead of the hero.
- Too many competing messages: gift guide, Shark deal, daily deals, app preview, Klarna, delivery partners and the product grid. There is no single dominant CTA.
- CTA copy is generic ("Shop Now", "Explore the Gift Shop") rather than a specific verb plus noun.
- The Shark tile reads "45% off… Coupons do not apply," which cuts against the 20% coupon story.
- This is another high-frequency Kohl's send after the Nov 14–15 run of "40% off" and "Kohl's Cash" emails. A new "20% off" message right after 40% off may look like a weaker offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% coupon in the hero with a code or a "Add to Wallet" button, and move the gift-budget tiles below it.
- 2. Cut the Klarna/delivery/app-preview blocks, or move them to the footer, so one offer leads.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop gifts under $25" and "Get my 20% off".
- 4. Clarify which items the coupon excludes, near the hero, so the Shark "coupons do not apply" message doesn't feel like a contradiction.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 20% off is inside — gifts from $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus earn $10 Kohl's Cash on $50+ toys. Coupon ends Nov. 21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gifts for her, him and the host — all under $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a wrapped-link Kohl's send (71 tracked links, 4 tracking pixels) with several markup defects: an empty `<title>`, a stray `</custom>` tag, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The header and compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results, physical address) are unverified because of the relay and the truncated HTML, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not probed:** All 71 tracking links were skipped, e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`. Destination validity and redirect health are unverified.
- **Four tracking pixels fire on open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/df7be6cf-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1025992_20261116`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  This is redundant, and the Adobe Audience Manager pixel (demdex) is a third-party call.
- **Malformed markup:** `<img src="…open.aspx?…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` has an unmatched `</custom>` closing tag. Some parsers may mishandle it.
- **Pixels with no alt text:** The first pixel (`click.chp.kohls.com/o/…`) has no `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Empty icon link:** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href. It is harmless but should be removed.
- **Invalid head content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will move it into the body or drop it.
- **Missing alt text, content images:** QA flagged `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (2a5adc5afb647aad, f441d45d2128ae53, c2f81e9ac8daee44, 415c8a18fc2950ea, 69494b48a79b4240, 78308d1d9adb0751, 916d6fa08d185233) and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/60699446_4_2x` and `60699446_3_2x`. If these carry offers or product content, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **QA false positive:** The `o.gif` pixel has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` in the source, which is correct for a decorative image. The QA check is treating empty alt as missing.
- **Mixed fonts:** The email loads Google Fonts through both `<link>` and `@import`, guarded by `<!--[if !mso]>`. Fallbacks are declared (`Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`), so this is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. The per-recipient `mi_u=604230016` parameter resolved. The `mi_ecmp=1025992_20261116` and `email_name=261116_DG_Email_Monday` values match today's send date (2026-11-16). The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `s.kohls.com` is aligned with the `click.s.kohls.com` link domain.
- **Physical address:** Not detected. The footer is in the truncated portion of the HTML, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.

3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` and `kohlsprod/60699446_*` images. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex` and `click.chp.kohls.com` pixels.
4. Consolidate the four open pixels where possible.
5. Re-run the compliance checks against raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the full, untruncated HTML (footer address, unsubscribe link).
6. Fix the QA check so `alt=""` on decorative images is not flagged. Also reconcile its summary ("0 issues, 4 warnings, 43% pass") with the 15+ warnings it lists.
