# Save 20% with Kohl's Rewards & get more for less 🤗
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eighth send in the same tier-stacking cycle since 09-10, and it finally pivots — the top band is still the tired 30%/20%/15% segmented stack, but this time it's wrapped around a genuinely different creative concept: "The Denim Guide," with a wide-leg/straight/bootcut breakdown and a "STYLE IT" outfit-building section. That's the first real editorial idea this brand has run in a week of near-identical promo blasts, and it shows some effort to escape the fatigue I've been flagging.
- But the email doesn't know when to stop. After the denim content it stacks a Sephora+Kohl's 30% off Benefit Cosmetics banner, then a 70% clearance banner, then four unrelated clearance products (a slushie maker, bath towels, women's pants, a slow cooker), then a coupon wallet section with three more barcodes. It's five different emails glued together, and the denim hook — the one good idea here — gets buried by scroll five.
- Judgement: better creative concept than recent sends, but the execution drowns it in bolt-on promos. This reads as "let's cram every active campaign into one email" rather than a focused send.

## What's working

- The Denim Guide section has a clear, single visual hook (wide-leg/straight/bootcut) that's more engaging than another plain tier-stack banner.
- "STYLE IT" outfit accessory grid gives a concrete styling angle, which is new for this brand's recent cadence.
- Category nav buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) appear twice, giving easy wayfinding for a long email.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long and multi-themed: denim guide → Sephora/Benefit → clearance → coupon wallet, with no unifying thread.
- The three-tier segmented offer (30%/20%/15%) at the top is the same structure run daily since 09-10 — pure repetition, no escalation or new framing.
- Clearance product grid (slushie maker, bath towel, pants, slow cooker) is randomly assorted and disconnected from the denim theme that opened the email — undercuts the "curated" framing.
- Three separate barcodes/coupon codes near the bottom (REWARD620, coupon detail, single-use coupon) create clutter and decision fatigue right before the footer.
- Subject promises "get more for less" generically, but the strongest visual asset (denim) isn't mentioned at all in the subject line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two sends: one focused purely on The Denim Guide + Style It (the actual creative idea), one for clearance/Sephora — bundling everything kills the denim hook's impact.
- 2. Move the tier-stack offer band below the denim hero, or shrink it — six days running the identical 30/20/15 structure means it's now wallpaper, not a hook.
- 3. Cut the clearance grid to 2 items max or drop it from this send entirely — a slushie maker and a slow cooker have nothing to do with the denim/styling narrative.
- 4. Consolidate to one coupon/barcode at the bottom instead of three — three separate codes reads as spammy paperwork, not incentive.
- 5. Rewrite the subject to lead with denim since it's the strongest visual asset in the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Find your perfect jean fit — wide-leg, straight & bootcut`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Denim Guide: 20% off Rewards + your fit, solved`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide-leg, straight, or bootcut — plus how to style it head to toe.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New: shop denim by fit, then complete the look.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Kohl's promotional template with heavy tracking/pixel infrastructure; automated QA flags gaps in unsubscribe headers, sender authentication visibility, and missing alt text across nearly all images.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 76 tracking/click-redirect links detected (`click.s.kohls.com`, `click.chp.kohls.com` domains) — HTTP probing was skipped for these per QA policy, so live-destination validity is unconfirmed.
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/5fcfc489-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager), plus several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` re-permission/tracking pixels.
- Malformed markup: `<img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` open-tracking `<div>`. This is invalid HTML and should be removed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 images flagged missing `alt` text by QA, including the visible product image `57448464_roots_2x` (`images.am.kohls.com`) — this one impacts screen-reader users since it's content-bearing, not just a tracking pixel.
- Tracking/beacon pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `up/...`, `rp/*.png`) also lack `alt`, though `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` (as used on the `o.gif` pixel) is the correct pattern — the AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and several `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*` pixels lack even empty `alt`/`aria-hidden`, which is a minor a11y hygiene gap (low impact since they're 0-dimension).
- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) and MSO conditional comments indicate template is built for Outlook desktop compatibility — no rendering defects observed in the truncated source itself.
- Google Fonts loaded via `@import` and `<link>` with no fallback web-safe stack declared inline beyond `Montserrat,Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif` on `<body>` — acceptable, low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) visible in the truncated source.
- Tracking URLs carry dynamic identifiers (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp=1021753_2026914`, `d_sid=13245196`) that appear correctly populated (no literal placeholder strings like `undefined` or empty token brackets).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected by QA. Note this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than an actual sender omission — cannot confirm from HTML body alone since unsubscribe headers are SMTP-level, not HTML.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the truncated HTML — CAN-SPAM requires this in the footer; cannot confirm absence definitively since source was truncated before the footer, but flagging per QA output.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm sender authentication posture from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identifiers present and consistent across pixels: `mi_ecmp=1021753_2026914`, `email_name=260914_DG_Email_Monday` — naming convention suggests date-coded campaign (2026-09-14, "DG" segment, Monday send).
- Click links route through `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirect wrapper with opaque base64/encoded query strings — actual landing page destinations and UTM parameters are not visible in encoded form and were not resolved (HTTP probe skipped per QA policy for tracking domains). Cannot confirm landing-page alignment without decoding/following these redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag in the open-tracking pixel block — invalid markup.
2. Add `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` to the Adobe AAM pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) and remaining `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` beacons for consistency with the pattern already used elsewhere in the template.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content image `57448464_roots_2x` — this is the one alt-text gap with real accessibility impact.
4. Verify with the sending platform (not derivable from this HTML) that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and a physical address are actually present at the SMTP/footer level — QA's non-detection may be a relay capture limitation rather than a true compliance gap; confirm rather than assume either way.
5. If landing-page continuity needs verification, resolve a sample of the `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=...` redirect links directly to confirm final destination and UTM tagging match campaign ID `1021753_2026914`.
