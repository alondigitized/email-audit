# Save 15% | Set the scene for summer get-togethers ☀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's seasonal pivot from Mother's Day to summer entertaining — a logical calendar shift after a full week of May holiday sends. The hero is "Your home gets a summer refresh. Your budget gets a summer break." which is a clean, dual-payoff line. The structural spine is Home → Outdoor/Patio → Occasion Dress → 15% coupon → Kohl's Cash, and that sequence actually makes sense as a narrative arc for summer hosting.
- The problem is execution density. The email packs five content blocks into a phone-length scroll, the home/patio imagery is small and dark, and the category CTAs ("Shop Rugs," "Shop Patio," "Shop Now") are repeated so many times they lose any punch. The 15% offer doesn't appear until near the bottom — anyone who doesn't scroll never sees the coupon that the subject line promises.
- Judgment: a competent transition send that undersells the offer. The creative concept is sound, the hierarchy is not. After six consecutive 15%-off Kohl's sends in the last ten days, this one needs to work harder to feel distinct.

## What's working

- **Subject line is the strongest element** — "Save 15% | Set the scene for summer get-togethers ☀️" names the discount, the occasion, and the season in under 55 chars. Beats the generic "15% off awaits" phrasing from the May 9 send.
- **Seasonal pivot is timely and coherent** — home + outdoor + occasion dress reads as a genuine summer hosting package, not a random assortment.
- **Up to 40% off callout** in the patio/outdoor block adds a second value signal beyond the 15% coupon.
- **Kohl's Cash mechanic is visible** in the footer block — loyalty members can see the earn benefit without hunting.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean across the visible viewport.

## What's weak

- **15% coupon is buried below the fold** — the subject promises it but the hero module doesn't show it; someone who skims the top half misses the core offer entirely.
- **Patio/home product imagery is small and low-contrast** — the "Shop Rugs / Shop Patio / Shop Bedding" grid uses tiny thumbnails that don't sell the product.
- **Generic CTA copy repeated throughout** — "Shop Now," "Shop Rugs," "Shop Patio" appear three times each with no differentiation or urgency.
- **Occasion dress module feels bolted on** — the women's fashion block at the bottom ("Dress for the occasion… and then some") is tonally disconnected from the home/entertaining narrative set up by the hero.
- **No deadline or urgency signal visible** — the 15% offer appears without an end date, making it feel like another perpetual Kohl's coupon rather than a time-bounded reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 15% coupon code to the hero** — put the discount code (or at minimum "15% off sitewide" + "use code SUMMER15") directly under the hero headline. That's the offer the subject line sells; the email should deliver it above the fold.
- 2. **Add a deadline** — even "Valid through Sunday" would differentiate this from the six near-identical sends in the last 10 days and give a real reason to click today vs. later.
- 3. **Replace "Shop Now" with verb + category** — "Shop Outdoor Furniture," "Shop Summer Rugs" — specific enough to pre-qualify the click and reduce bounce.
- 4. **Drop the fashion block or make it thematic** — if it must stay, reframe it as "Dressed to host" with a summer-party styling angle to stay on-narrative.
- 5. **Enlarge the patio hero image** — the outdoor/entertaining concept is the seasonal differentiator; one full-width lifestyle shot of a backyard table setting would do more than a 3-up thumbnail grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 15% | Your summer hosting upgrade starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 40% off | Summer patio refresh — this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, patio furniture, outdoor finds — plus earn Kohl's Cash through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your backyard's calling. 15% off + up to 40% on patio gear, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 15% | Summer Get-Togethers" (2026-05-16)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered promotional send with no merge token anomalies or broken links detected. Primary technical concerns are compliance gaps (CAN-SPAM physical address absent, unsubscribe headers not captured), missing alt text on tracking pixels, and unauthenticated delivery attribution due to AgentMail relay stripping or not forwarding authentication headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 click-redirect URLs skipped** — all links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com`. HTTP probes were skipped as expected for tracking redirects; no broken destination links were confirmed, but destination resolution was not verified.

**Three distinct tracking pixel domains in use:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — Coherent Path behavioral pixel
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears open/render pixels (3 instances with `mi_u=604230016`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open tracker (malformed closing tag: `</custom>` instead of `</div>`)

**Malformed tag evidence:**
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   <!-- ← invalid closing tag; should be </div> -->
```
This stray `</custom>` is a minor but real HTML error that could cause rendering inconsistencies in strict parsers.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all tracking pixels** — 10 images flagged:

| Image | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c2dd5842-...` | `click.chp.kohls.com` | Behavioral pixel, no `alt` |
| `o.gif` | `mi.kohls.com` | Open pixel, no `alt` |
| `event` | `kohls.demdex.net` | AAM sync pixel, no `alt` |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` (7 images) | `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` | Render pixels, no `alt` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`. The `o.gif` pixel has `aria-hidden="true"` but no `alt`; the others have neither. The Coherent Path pixel (`c2dd5842`) has no `alt` or `aria-hidden`.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Common in email but worth flagging.

**`<title>` is empty** — `<title></title>` provides no context for screen readers that announce document title.

**`<html lang="en">`** — correctly set; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source. `mi_u=604230016` is consistent across all MessageGears pixel calls, indicating a resolved subscriber ID. No `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` placeholders visible.

One render pixel carries a potentially unresolved parameter:
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe
```
The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value is empty — this may be an unfilled optional merge field or a truncation artifact. If this field is supposed to carry an expiry date, it silently failed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params)

All 22 links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirectors. UTM parameters on destination URLs were not resolvable without following redirects. The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`1012666_2026516`) serves as a campaign identifier within MessageGears but does not replace standard `utm_campaign` / `utm_source` / `utm_medium` attribution for GA4.

If destination URLs do not carry UTM params post-redirect, campaign traffic will be attributed as direct/none in web analytics. This is unverifiable from the source alone but is a common failure mode in ESP redirect chains.
