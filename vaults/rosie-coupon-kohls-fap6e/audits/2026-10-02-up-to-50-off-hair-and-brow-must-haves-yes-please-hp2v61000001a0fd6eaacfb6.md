# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells "up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves." The body opens with something else. The first screen has a thin National Brow Day strip, then a huge "Rewards Bazaar: October means new rewards!" block. The Hair Faves 50% offer, which is half the subject's promise, sits near the bottom as a "TODAY ONLY" banner. Anyone who opens for the discount has to scroll through about two-thirds of a very long email to find it.
- The email is also overloaded. It has a brow strip, the Rewards Bazaar, a 100-point grid, a 250-point grid, a 2X/5X "Earn points faster" block, a "First Access: Holiday Drops" tile, a 20% off value-size hair care tile, the Hair Faves banner, and then the Beauty Insider footer. It is competent and on-brand, but it reads as a loyalty catalog with a sale attached.
- The cadence also hurts. This is the second 50%-off brow/hair send today. The earlier one, "50% OFF select brow products 🤨", was the cleaner hard-offer version, and it followed the 2X points send on 10/01. Subscribers have now seen the points push and the brow/hair 50% push back to back. I'd call it a middling send that works against its own subject.

## What's working

- The Sephora and Insider branding is instantly recognizable, and the brand voice is consistent.
- The brow strip at the top carries a clear "Limited time only" tag and a SHOP NOW button above the fold.
- The 100- and 250-point tiers show product names and point costs, so the loyalty value is concrete.
- The "TODAY ONLY" label on the Hair Faves banner gives credible urgency.
- No broken images or overlapping text are visible, and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The hero (Rewards Bazaar) doesn't match the subject. The 50% hair offer is buried near the bottom.
- Too many competing messages: rewards, 2X/5X points, holiday drops, 20% off, brow, and hair. There is no single focal point.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP NOW >".
- The footer has a wall of dense fine print and a long barcode block. It makes the offers feel conditional.
- The brow offer repeats today's earlier brow send, which adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer the subject promises. Make "Up to 50% off Hair & Brow, today only" the hero, and move Rewards Bazaar below it or into its own send.
- 2. Cut the number of modules by about half. Drop the 250-point tier and the holiday drops tile from this send.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs such as "Shop 50% off hair" and "Shop 50% off brows."
- 4. Merge the brow and hair offers into one block instead of a thin brow strip and a separate bottom banner.
- 5. Don't send the same offer twice in one day. Fold the second send into the first or hold it for the next promotion.
- 6. Write a real preheader so the shipping boilerplate stops showing in the inbox.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: up to 50% off hair and brow 💗`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 50% off brow and hair favorites`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brow Day picks and Hair Faves, marked down through tonight. Insiders also get new rewards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save big on Bio Ionic, UNITE, and more. Ends today.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback missing (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** This is the only hard failure. It hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - the `app.sephora.com/O/...` image
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, a nav header image, so its link labels are likely inaccessible to screen readers and to clients that block images
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom where it is honored.

- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">`** uses an invalid token; the standard value is `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **`<title>Sephora</title>`** is generic.
- The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, VML/Office namespaces and Outlook-specific CSS (`mso-*`, `#outlook a`) are present and correctly formed. The visible CSS is clean.
