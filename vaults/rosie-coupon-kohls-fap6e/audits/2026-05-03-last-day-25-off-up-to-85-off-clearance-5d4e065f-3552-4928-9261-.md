# LAST DAY: 25% off + up to 85% off clearance ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a dense, multi-offer Kohl's Friends & Family email firing on its last day. The headline 25% off + up to 85% clearance is clearly communicated, the visual hierarchy is functional, and the email is render-clean. However, it reads as a department-store broadcast — no personalization, no single hero product to anchor attention, and the category grid mid-scroll fragments focus across six+ departments simultaneously. For a male-leaning persona, the predominant visual styling (pastel kitchen, accessories, soft-home lifestyle imagery) creates mild demographic misalignment. Solid mechanics, average targeting.

## What's working

- **Dual-offer clarity.** The 25% Friends & Family discount stacks visibly with up to 85% clearance — two strong reasons to click, clearly separated.
- **Last-day urgency.** "Time's running out" section with an explicit deadline (Mother's Day May 11) and a countdown clock graphic keeps urgency credible rather than manufactured.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, text isn't clipped, buttons are visible. Clean execution.
- **Kohl's Cash call-out.** Bottom section promoting a $10 Kohl's Cash earn is a meaningful loyalty hook that adds incremental motivation.
- **Clearance block is visually prominent.** The bright teal/green CLEARANCE 85% banner at the bottom is high contrast and hard to miss.

## What's weak

- **No personalization.** No name, no "because you bought X," no category preference signals — pure broadcast.
- **Category fragmentation.** Bedding, Kitchen Appliances, Women's Clothing, Accessories, Beauty all compete simultaneously mid-scroll. No single hero product is featured.
- **Demographic mismatch.** Lifestyle imagery skews strongly toward women's home/fashion/beauty. The only male signal is a nav tab. A male persona has to do mental work to find relevance.
- **CTA copy is generic.** Nearly every button reads "Shop Now." Nothing category-specific like "Shop Men's" or "Find Her Gift."
- **The "20% off" sub-offer near the top creates confusion.** There's a 25% headline AND a 20% module — the visual proximity makes the deal math feel complicated rather than simple.
- **No price anchors on featured products.** The appliance and bedding imagery shows no price or original vs. sale comparison — forces a click just to know if the deal is worth it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse the offer message.** Lead with one offer (25% off everything) and let clearance be a secondary module, not a co-equal hero. Two leads dilute both.
- 2. **Swap one lifestyle shot for a product + price.** Show a KitchenAid mixer at $X was $Y. Concrete > abstract.
- 3. **Personalize at least one CTA.** "Shop Men's Picks" or "Your Clearance: Men's" costs nothing and lifts CTR on personas not aligned to the default imagery.
- 4. **Make the deadline more prominent higher up.** "Today only" language is buried in the middle third — move the hard deadline to the hero subhead.
- 5. **Drop one content module.** The email has six+ categories competing. Remove Beauty or Accessories for a male persona; brevity is conversion.

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "LAST DAY: 25% off + up to 85% off clearance"

**Sender:** kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-05-03

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured HTML4 email with competent MSO/VML fallbacks and responsive media queries. Primary concerns are missing alt text on all tracking pixels, unverifiable SPF/DKIM auth status through the AgentMail relay, and a plain-text version that is nearly entirely URLs.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** — all outbound links route through `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect infrastructure; HTTP probing was skipped on all 22. Destination URL integrity cannot be confirmed from this audit.

Three distinct tracking pixel vendors detected:
- `click.chp.kohls.com` — primary open pixel (Cheetah Digital)
- `mi.kohls.com` — MessageGears pixel (`o.gif`, `p/rp/*.png`)
- `kohls.demdex.net` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel (`event?d_sid=13245196`)
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — Salesforce Marketing Cloud open tracker

No issues with pixel load order or duplication, but the triple-vendor tracking stack increases page-weight and introduces three additional third-party DNS lookups before content renders.

**Malformed tag:** `<custom>` closing tag found in the open-tracker block:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...">
</custom>  ← invalid/non-standard tag
```
This is invalid HTML and may trigger strict parsers or spam filters.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all tracking pixels and multiple content images** — 11 flagged by QA:

| Image | Missing alt |
|---|---|
| `2645e220...` (Cheetah open pixel) | yes |
| `o.gif` (MessageGears pixel) | yes |
| `event` (Demdex pixel) | yes |
| `2a5adc5a...png` through `916d6fa0...png` (6 content images) | yes |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (already present on the MessageGears `o.gif`: `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` — correct). The Cheetah and Demdex pixels are missing both attributes.

**`<title>` tag is empty** — `<title></title>`. Screen readers and some clients announce the title; an empty title degrades accessibility and may reduce preview-pane labeling.

**`user-scalable=0`** in viewport meta — disables pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Low-severity for email but worth noting.

**Font loading redundancy** — Montserrat and Roboto are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` in the same `<head>`. This doubles the font request and adds render-blocking latency on clients that support web fonts. One method should be removed.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the HTML source. Subscriber ID is passed as a URL parameter (`mi_u=604230016`) in MessageGears pixels, confirming the send was personalized at the pixel level.

No issues found.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address:** QA did not detect a physical mailing address in the plain-text version. The HTML source was truncated; if the address appears only in the visual footer image (not as text), it would fail CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) because image-blocking renders the address invisible.

**List-Unsubscribe header:** Not captured by the AgentMail relay. Major ISPs (Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail) require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) for one-click unsubscribe on bulk senders. If the header is genuinely absent — not just relay-invisible — this is a compliance gap and a deliverability risk under Google/Yahoo 2024 sender requirements.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`** — suppresses HTTP Referer on outbound link clicks. This is intentional for privacy but means referrer-based analytics on the landing page will show "direct" instead of email, potentially undercounting email-attributed sessions.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com` or `click.chp.kohls.com` redirectors; UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without following the redirect chain. The MessageGears pixel URLs include `email_name=260503_DG_Email_Sunday` and `mi_ecmp=1011580_202653`, indicating campaign-level tagging is configured at the pixel layer.

The `no-referrer` meta tag (see §5) will suppress referrer headers on all click-throughs, which conflicts with any referrer-based attribution on Kohls.com. UTM params in destination URLs would compensate — but that cannot be verified without resolving the redirect chain.
