# Save an extra 20% ✅ Plus, earn Kohl's Cash ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the Kohl's coupon send you've already seen all week. The top banner is the same: "Extra 20% off with coupon, ends Oct. 18" beside "$20 off your purchase of $100 or more, ends Oct. 12." The Kohl's Cash strip and "KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER, Earn 5%" line sit under it. Compared with the Oct 11 "20% off savings for you 😍" and Oct 10 "Get 20% off AND $20 off" sends, only the subject line and some of the product mix are new.
- The body is a long scroll with five different hooks: a $19.99 tops hero, Levi's up to 40% off, a Season's Best Shoe Sale at up to 40% off, Under Armour at 25% off, then a product grid. The grid runs from a $299.99 Ninja SLUSHI to a $2.99–$9.99 towel to $29.99 jeans. The coupon block near the bottom is the most useful part, with codes YOUGET20, EXTRA20 and 10HOME and barcodes. But "Coupons do not apply to select brands" appears repeatedly, which weakens the headline 20%.
- Judgement: a competent but fatigued send. The offer is clear and the render is clean. Nothing here is new, and the subject undersells the real hook, which is the stackable 20% plus $20 plus Kohl's Cash.

## What's working

- The top banner puts the 20% and $20 offers side by side with visible deadlines, so the value is clear in seconds.
- The Kohl's Rewards status line and the "earn Kohl's Cash" strip make member value visible right under the offer.
- The coupon block at the bottom ("Take an extra 20% off," "Take $20 off," "Rewards exclusive: $10 off $50 home") gives codes, PINs, barcodes and expiry dates. That is practical and honest.
- "Shop now. Decide later. Free returns through January 31, 2027" is a strong reassurance line for fall and holiday shoppers.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or gaps.

## What's weak

- The email repeats the same coupon creative as the Oct 9, 10 and 11 sends, so it reads as a near-duplicate and not as news.
- The Oct. 12 deadline on the $20 coupon is the only real urgency, and it's small. The 20% runs through Oct. 18, which softens it.
- There are too many hooks and too many CTAs. The Shop Now buttons, the Women/Men/Kids/Shoes button blocks and the product tiles compete, and no single product leads.
- "Coupons do not apply to select brands" shows up in the Levi's, shoe and Under Armour blocks, and the "Orig. $349.99"-style price tiles carry fine print. The exclusions undercut the 20% promise.
- CTAs are generic ("Shop Now," "Shop Women"), and there's no review or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the coupon stack and the clock. Make the first screen "20% off + $20 off + Kohl's Cash" with one "Shop with coupon" button, and put "$20 coupon ends tomorrow" front and center.
- 2. Cut the body to one hero and 4–6 products. Drop the Under Armour and Levi's blocks, or fold them into a single "Brands that qualify" row.
- 3. Say which brands are excluded once, near the top, instead of repeating "Coupons do not apply" in every block.
- 4. Use specific button copy such as "Shop boots, 40% off" or "Get my $20 coupon," not "Shop Now."
- 5. Vary the creative from the last three coupon sends, for example lead with the Kohl's Cash earn window (Oct 19–25 redemption).
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 coupon ends tomorrow + extra 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack 20% off + $20 off, then earn Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code YOUGET20 or EXTRA20 before the $20 coupon expires Oct. 12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend, plus up to 40% off shoes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) send with a heavy tracking stack. It has four separate beacons and 66 click-redirect links. The HTML has several markup defects and accessibility gaps. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay or the truncated source, so they aren't confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 66 links were skipped as tracking domains (e.g. `https://click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`). QA cannot confirm that any of them resolve or land correctly.
- **Four open or analytics beacons in one message:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/c7a52ccb-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (third-party Adobe Audience Manager)
- **Open-count skew.** The overlapping open pixels from three systems make open-rate reporting hard to reconcile.
- **Identifier in the pixel URL.** The `mi.kohls.com` pixel carries `mi_u=604230016`, which looks like a per-recipient ID.
- **Possible empty merge value.** A dynamic image URL ends in `…&mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_p…`. The Kohl's Cash redemption end date is empty, which may mean an unpopulated field. The source is truncated, so this isn't confirmed.
- **Broken markup after the SFMC open pixel.** The tag is `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?…" … alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` closing tag has no opener.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text.** QA flagged 11 or more images without `alt`.
  - Content images: `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`) and `images.am.kohls.com/is/image/kohlsprod/58379268_2_2x`. With images off, or in a screen reader, these convey nothing.
  - Pixels: `click.chp…`, `o.gif` and `demdex.net/event` lack `alt=""`. The first has no `aria-hidden` either.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid head content.** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers end the head at that point, so the schema meta tags end up in the body.
- **Empty favicon.** `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`.
- **Ineffective CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width:100% }` is missing its leading dot, so it matches nothing.
- **Invalid CSS value.** `max-height: auto` in the mobile media query.
- **Duplicate meta tag.** `format-detection` is declared twice.
- **Missing table roles.** The tracking-pixel table and the preheader table (`width='640'`) have no `role="presentation"`. The main wrappers do.
- **Unhidden pixel.** The first pixel, `<img src="https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/…" width="1" height="1">`, sits directly in `<body>` with no hiding wrapper or styles. It can create a stray line box at the top.
- **Web fonts.** Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are used for Montserrat and Roboto. This is acceptable because `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is the fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible HTML.
- The empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted in section 2 is the only possible gap.
- The rest of the body is truncated, so personalization can't be fully assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** QA found neither header. It notes AgentMail may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message.
- **Physical address.** QA found none, but the footer is outside the truncated source. It can't be confirmed either way.
- **Authentication-Results.** Missing, expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `s.kohls.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain.
- **Third-party tracking.** `demdex.net` and the per-user IDs in pixels may need disclosure in the privacy policy. That is a legal check, not an HTML finding.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every link is wrapped in an SFMC redirect, so no UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible.
- The only campaign identifiers in the source are `mi_ecmp=1023670_20261011` and `email_name=261011_DG_Email_Sun_PM` on image URLs.
- Continuity can't be assessed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the content images (`mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `58379268_2_2x`). Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` to the pixels.
2. Remove `</custom>`. Move the `itemscope` div out of `<head>` or replace it with JSON-LD in the body.
3. Fill in `<title>`. Remove the empty favicon `<link>`. Fix `.ReadMsgBody` and `max-height: auto`.
4. Drop `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Hide the first pixel in a `display:none` or `font-size:0` wrapper. Consider consolidating the overlapping open pixels.
6. Check that the `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` value populates in every Kohl's Cash image URL.
7. Re-run the compliance checks on the full raw `.eml` (headers and untruncated footer). That should settle the List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, physical-address and Authentication-Results warnings.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify landing pages and UTM tagging.
