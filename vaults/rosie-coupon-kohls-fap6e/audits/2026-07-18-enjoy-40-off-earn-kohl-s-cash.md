# Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Kohl's finally breaks the monotonous 20%-off run that has defined every send since early July, doubling down to 40% off — a legitimate escalation that a deal-aware subscriber would clock immediately. On paper, this is the most aggressive discount email in the recent run. In execution, though, it buries the lead: the hero block is all about "Priced-right summer ESSENTIALS" with a male lifestyle model, and the 40% off coupon doesn't appear until the lower body — below a back-to-school section. The subject promises 40% and the email doesn't reinforce it until the scroll.
- The back-to-school angle (Girls / Juniors / Boys / Young Men / Backpacks grid, "Showing up in style, down to a science") is the first genuinely fresh category story in this sixteen-day run. It's timely for a late-July send and gives the email a reason-to-exist beyond the standard coupon blast. That's real progress.
- The fundamental problem is the same one that's plagued the last twenty-plus emails: this brand is sending so frequently (twenty-third-ish email in sixteen days) that even a genuine offer upgrade gets absorbed as background noise. The step-up to 40% deserves more visual prominence than the hero gives it.

## What's working

- **40% off is a meaningful break from pattern** — every prior email in this run was 20% off; doubling the headline discount gives this send a real reason to open.
- **Back-to-school grid** (Girls, Juniors, Boys, Backpacks) is the first category-specific story in the run and lands at the right time in the calendar.
- **Kohl's Cash deadline anchored** — "Earn Kohl's Cash through July 28" provides a credible, visible deadline that the subject line alone lacks.
- **Coupon barcode visible in body** — reduces friction for in-store redemption; subscriber doesn't have to hunt for it.
- **Clean render, no broken images or layout bugs** visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **40% off is buried below the hero** — the subject's headline promise only appears in the lower coupon block; the hero leads with "summer ESSENTIALS" lifestyle copy, creating a bait-and-switch feel on scroll.
- **Male model headlining the hero** — with Women's and Men's tabs both present, the male-forward hero dilutes relevance for a majority-female apparel audience like Walker.
- **Six-plus CTAs with no hierarchy** — "Shop Now," "Shop Women's," "Shop Men's," "Shop Back to School," "View coupon in Wallet," and grid category links all compete; there's no single action the email is driving toward.
- **Subject lacks urgency hook** — "Enjoy 40% off + earn Kohl's Cash" doesn't give the July 28 Kohl's Cash deadline or any other expiry signal; opens will underperform the offer's actual strength.
- **Cadence fatigue is severe** — twenty-third send in sixteen days; even a genuine offer upgrade struggles to land when the inbox has been this saturated.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the body with the 40% off** — move the coupon/discount block to the top, immediately under the nav. If the subject promises 40%, the first content block should deliver it. Summer essentials and back-to-school can follow.
- 2. **Add the July 28 deadline to the subject line** — the deadline exists in the email; surfacing it in the subject creates genuine, credible urgency that this subject currently lacks.
- 3. **Female-forward hero for women's category** — if Walker is the persona, a women's lifestyle shot (or at minimum a split hero) would outscore the male-only lead.
- 4. **Consolidate CTAs to one per section** — pick one primary action per content block; the "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" split under the hero already does this well; replicate that logic throughout.
- 5. **Inject the Kohl's Cash earned rate** into hero copy — e.g., "Earn $10 for every $50" is more concrete than "earn Kohl's Cash."
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ends soon — Kohl's Cash through July 28`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Biggest discount of the month: 40% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Summer essentials + back-to-school picks — your 40% coupon is inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kohl's Cash earns through July 28 — here's your 40% off code`
