Below the hero come a military-appreciation "30% off" banner, a gift guide, stockings, a Grinch toy tile, home categories, a Doorbuster preview, delivery options, a product grid and a stack of coupon barcodes. ## What's working

- The "$10 off" numeral with an "ENDS TODAY" tag is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The subject and the hero agree, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The coupon codes and barcodes at the bottom (SHOP10, SAVEMORE) make redemption easy.
- Kohl's Rewards and Kohl's Cash member perks are visible, and the military 30% banner adds a second, distinct benefit.

## What's weak

- The email is a very long wall of modules after the hero, and the single offer gets diluted.
- The 20% off promised in the subject's second half is only a small line near the top, not a hero of its own.
- CTAs are mostly generic ("Shop Now", "Learn More").
- The sequence of "last day" and "hours left" sends this week (Nov 10, Nov 11 twice) undermines the urgency.
- The coupon exclusions ("not valid on select brands", "Kohl's Cash not valid on Sephora") are fine print buried at the bottom.
- The apparent "Ends Nov. 11" deadline in a Nov 12 send would make the urgency stale. This needs verification.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one product row and the coupon block. Move the gift guide, Grinch tile and delivery options to other sends.
- 2. Give the 20% off its own hero tile, or drop it from the subject. Right now the subject sells two offers and the render commits to one.
- 3. Stop sending near-identical "last day" mails. Use one final-hours send with a real countdown, and suppress it for anyone who already redeemed.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with specific verbs, such as "Shop $25+ gifts" or "Get my $10 off".
- 5. Check the send date against the coupon end date before it goes out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has malformed markup (a stray `</custom>` tag, a `<div>` inside `<head>`), an empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport, and four stacked tracking pixels. Many content images lack `alt`. The HTML was truncated, and the relay didn't capture headers, so the footer, unsubscribe link and authentication can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA check skipped 81 click-redirect links (HTTP probe skipped), so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- Four tracking or beacon images load in the first few hundred bytes of the body:
  - `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/f6f0072f-...` (no `alt`)
  - `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=...`
  - `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager, third-party, `width="0" height="0"`)
- The open-pixel `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx...">` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag, which is invalid.
- The last flagged image URL has an empty dynamic parameter, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=`. That may mean a Movable Ink data field didn't resolve.
- The `mi_u=604230016` parameter is a user-level identifier in every image URL. It is not a bug, but it exposes the subscriber ID to anything that logs image requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** there is no document title for clients that surface it, such as web view.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization">` sits inside `<head>`, where parsers will implicitly close the head and push it into the body. Also, `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty `href`.
- **Missing alt text on content images:** the hero `59781016_Product_hgdt30_HolidayGifting_Hero_GIF` and the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad`, `f441d45d2128ae53`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44`, `415c8a18fc2950ea`, `69494b48a79b4240`, `78308d1d9adb0751`, `916d6fa08d185233`). If images are blocked or a screen reader is in use, the offer content is lost.
- **QA false positive:** `o.gif` is flagged for missing alt, but the source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a pixel. The `demdex` and `open.aspx` pixels are also decorative, so only the `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel truly lacks `alt`.
- **Fonts:** the Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. That is acceptable. The outer container uses `role="presentation"`, which is correct.
- **Doctype and namespaces:** the HTML 4.01 doctype is combined with `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` attributes. This is a harmless, common Outlook pattern.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML: there are no unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens. The one exception is the empty `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed failure. Kohl's is a bulk sender, so Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The HTML was truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com` (`Kohls@s.kohls.com`), and alignment with the click domains `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` is untested.
- **Plain-text part:** 14316 of 20091 characters (71%) are URLs, which crosses the QA threshold. This is a minor spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The visible tracking uses Movable Ink parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=1025677_20261111`, `email_name=261111_DG_Email_Wed_PM`) rather than `utm_*`. `mi_ecmp` carries the send date 20261111, which matches the send.
- All 81 links are behind redirectors, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be verified from this data.
- Some images carry `email_name` and others (`69494b48…`, `78308d1d…`, `916d6fa0…`) do not, so campaign attribution parameters are inconsistent across Movable Ink images.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment before treating the compliance warnings as real.
2. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer for the physical address and unsubscribe link.
3. Resolve the 81 redirects in a follow-up probe to capture final URLs and UTM/landing alignment.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text to the hero and `mi.kohls.com` content images, and `alt=""` to the remaining pixels.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`, and populate `<title>`.
6. Fix the markup: delete `</custom>`, move the schema.org `<div>` out of `<head>` (or use JSON-LD in the body), and drop or fill the empty `<link rel="shortcut icon">`.
7. Check why `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` is empty, since it may be a dynamic-content failure.
8. Fix the QA tool so it doesn't flag images that already have `alt=""`.
