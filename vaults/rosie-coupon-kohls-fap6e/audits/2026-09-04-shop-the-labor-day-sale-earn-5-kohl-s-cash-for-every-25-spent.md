# Shop the Labor Day Sale + earn $5 Kohl's Cash for every $25 spent 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the flagship Labor Day send, and it's basically a rerun of yesterday's "$10 off your $25 purchase STARTS TODAY" email with a different subject line and no visible new creative energy. The hero stack is the same offer architecture — $10 off $25, $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 — presented in the same red/cream color-blocked layout, then it dives into the same grab-bag category tiles (Carter's kids, floor care, bedding, home decor, clothing, luggage, rugs, shoes, team gear, Sephora beauty). Nothing here signals "last day of the sale" urgency or wraps the campaign — it reads like a mid-campaign refresh, not a milestone send.
- The offer itself is genuinely good (stackable $10/$25 + $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 + extra 15% off home with coupon), and the layout hierarchy is clean with bold pink CTAs throughout. But by the fourth-plus similar-looking send in eight days, the sameness is the story — a subscriber who opened the 09-03 email already knows this offer.
- Judgement: solid execution, stale delivery — this is a copy-paste of yesterday's structure with a swapped subject, and the brand history shows this cadence is starting to blur together for the reader.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states both stackable offers ($10 off $25, $5 Kohl's Cash per $25) with bold, legible numerals.
- Category nav bar (Women/Men/Kids/Home) up top gives fast wayfinding for a shopper who doesn't want to scroll.
- Consistent pink/red CTA buttons ("Shop Now") are easy to spot throughout a very long scroll.
- "Picked with you in mind" section shows specific SKUs with real prices ($349.99 Ninja coffee maker, $2.99–9.99 towels), which reads more personalized than the category tiles above it.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, pricing is legible.

## What's weak

- This is a near-identical structural repeat of the 09-03 "$10 off your $25... STARTS TODAY" email — same offer, same layout shape, just a new subject line and reordered tiles.
- No "last chance" or closing-day urgency anywhere in the hero despite this plausibly being a late-cycle Labor Day send — subject promises earning Kohl's Cash but not urgency.
- The email is extremely long (11+ category tiles plus a Sephora insert plus a "Picked for you" block) before reaching the footer — heavy scroll fatigue with no signal of where the "best" deal is.
- Barcodes/coupon codes at the very bottom are visually disconnected from the top-of-email hero offers, forcing the reader to scroll all the way down to redeem.
- "Coupons do not apply" is repeated on nearly every tile (Carter's, floor care, athletic shoes, team gear) — undercuts the sense of one big cohesive sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate this send from yesterday's — if it's genuinely a new offer, lead with what's actually new (e.g., the $5 Kohl's Cash per $25 mechanic front and center) rather than repeating the $10/$25 hero verbatim.
- 2. Add a deadline chip in the hero ("Ends 9/7" or similar) so the email creates urgency instead of reading as open-ended.
- 3. Move the coupon codes/barcodes into the hero or a sticky recap near the top — don't make readers scroll past 10+ tiles to redeem.
- 4. Trim the category tile count or group them into a single scannable grid — 11+ separate full-width tiles is fatigue-inducing on mobile.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: stack $10 off, $5 Kohl's Cash & 15% off home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off home, kids, shoes and more — through Sept 7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Labor Day Kohl's Cash starts adding up now.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's marketing template with heavy multi-vendor tracking (CoherentPath/mi.kohls.com, Adobe AAM); several compliance/accessibility gaps appear in automated QA but authentication headers and unsubscribe fields could not be evaluated from raw HTML alone.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 63 tracking/redirect links (click.chp.kohls.com, click.s.kohls.com, mi.kohls.com) were skipped by the HTTP probe as expected for redirect infrastructure — not itself a defect, but means click-through destinations are unverified.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels present: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/f023ac65-...`, `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe AAM), plus 7 additional `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels — consistent with standard open/engagement tracking, no anomalies found.
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...&d=60262` sits inside a `<div>` but is malformed: closing tag is `</custom>` instead of `</div>` — an invalid/mismatched tag in the source.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images confirmed missing `alt` text (QA findings): the CoherentPath tracking pixel, `o.gif`, the Adobe AAM `event` pixel, and 5 `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` pixels. These are tracking pixels rather than content images, so the missing alt has no user-facing rendering impact, but `alt=""` should still be set explicitly for HTML validity (several already omit `aria-hidden` where one sibling pixel does include it).
- Malformed `</custom>` closing tag noted above may cause unpredictable DOM parsing in strict renderers.
- Responsive breakpoints (`@media max-width:500px`) and MSO conditional comments are present and structured correctly; no rendering defects found in the visible markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tokens/placeholders (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) present in the truncated source — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in headers captured (QA WARN). May be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm root cause from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the QA scan of this email — CAN-SPAM requires one. Flag for follow-up since it wasn't visible in the truncated HTML region either.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM)**: not found in headers — authentication status unknown, likely an artifact of the relay path rather than a sending-domain issue, but unconfirmed.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking parameters use Kohl's/CoherentPath's own scheme (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `mi_ecmp`, `cp_tp`) rather than standard UTM params — no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` observed in the visible links. Cannot assess landing-page alignment since destination URLs are redirect-wrapped and were skipped by the probe (63 links unresolved).
- No issues found in what's visible, but continuity cannot be fully verified given all click targets route through `click.chp.kohls.com`/`click.s.kohls.com` redirectors.

7. Recommendations
- Confirm with AgentMail relay config whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are being stripped in transit vs. genuinely absent at send — re-test capturing raw SMTP headers before flagging Kohl's directly.
- Verify presence of a physical mailing address in the untruncated footer HTML (likely below the truncation point) before escalating as a CAN-SPAM violation.
- Fix the mismatched `</custom>` closing tag on the `click.s.kohls.com` open-tracking pixel `<div>`.
- Add explicit `alt=""` to the 8 flagged tracking-pixel images for HTML validity, even though they carry no visible content.
- Follow at least a sample of the 63 redirect links to confirm final landing pages resolve and align with the sale (Labor Day / Kohl's Cash) promoted in the subject line.
