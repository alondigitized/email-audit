# Extra 20% off ✅ Up to 70% off clearance ✅
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's fifth "20% off + Kohl's Cash" email in roughly 72 hours — but unlike the prior two near-identical sends on July 2 and July 3 (the "Psst" and the "Celebrate the 250th" editions), this one finally pairs the coupon with a legitimate second headline: up to 70% off clearance with new markdowns taken. That makes the subject line the most substantively differentiated of the recent batch. The activewear lead ("Looks for working out. Or thinking about it.") with a $8.99 entry price is a strong value hook, and the clearance section with a hard "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN" call-out gives a genuine reason to visit beyond the now-fatigued coupon.
- The structural problem isn't the creative — it's the calendar. A subscriber who opened the "Celebrate the 250th" email from this same morning has now seen this exact discount structure five times since July 1. The 70% clearance angle is the only new oxygen in the room, and it's buried below two other sections. The email earns its send but not its place in a 72-hour flood.
- Judgment: a competent promotional email that would score higher if it ran in isolation. The clearance angle is legitimately valuable; the cadence fatigue is the real damage.

## What's working

- **Dual offer in subject lands clearly** — "Extra 20% off ✅ Up to 70% off clearance ✅" communicates two distinct savings moments without confusion.
- **Activewear starting-at price is arresting** — $8.99 with 20% coupon applied, with the original $14–$39.99 range visible, makes the math feel real and good immediately.
- **"NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN" clearance framing** — stronger than a generic "save up to 70%" banner; implies action was just taken and urgency is real.
- **Kohl's Cash earn/redeem window is explicit** — "through July 5, redeemable July 8–15" in the footer gives real dates, not vague "soon."
- **Rewards member status in the top bar** — "Earn 5% REWARDS" in the header reinforces loyalty value without requiring a separate section.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTAs throughout are interchangeable** — three separate "Shop Now" buttons in the scroll with no product-specific copy; nothing pulls you toward a decision.
- **Clearance section is third** — the genuinely differentiated angle (70% off, new markdowns) is buried below activewear and shoes; it should lead or at least be second.
- **Two apparent codes create confusion** — the hero references a coupon code, and the barcode at bottom shows "GOSHOP20 / PIN No. 1"; a subscriber shouldn't have to reconcile which to use.
- **No single hero product** — every section is category-level, which diffuses attention; a specific $8.99 Tek Gear piece with "add to cart" would convert better than "browse activewear."
- **Cadence is untenable** — this is the 5th substantially similar email since July 1; the "extra" in the subject doesn't feel extra anymore.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with clearance, not activewear** — "NEW MARKDOWNS TAKEN: up to 70% off" is the freshest signal in this email. Move it to the hero, use the 20% coupon as a modifier ("stack your extra 20% on top"), not the headliner.
- 2. **Give each CTA a destination noun** — "Shop Clearance," "Shop Activewear," "Shop Athletic Shoes" instead of three "Shop Now" generics.
- 3. **Resolve the dual-code ambiguity** — show one clear code prominently in the hero with "use at checkout" instruction; the barcode is fine in the footer but shouldn't introduce a second code label.
- 4. **Feature one specific product** — pick the single best-performing clearance item, show it hero-sized with price + clearance savings math, and link directly to the PDP.
- 5. **Compress the middle sections** — the activewear and shoes blocks are redundant with the coupon already established; a single "All apparel + shoes" band frees space for a stronger clearance visual.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns + extra 20% off — July 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off, new markdowns taken. Plus your 20% coupon.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kohl's Cash earns through July 5 — stack it on clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Just marked down. Take an extra 20% on top.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Extra 20% off / Up to 70% off clearance"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

HTML4.01 Transitional with MSO/VML conditionals targeting Outlook; tracking stack spans four vendors (CoherentPath, MessageGears, Adobe Audience Manager, a Kohl's-owned click domain). One malformed closing tag and several accessibility violations confirmed in source; three compliance headers unverified due to relay capture gap.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed HTML around open-tracking pixel**
The open-pixel `<div>` is closed with `</custom>` instead of `</div>`:
```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?IXUTWN25S44ELPKRVEGM3DEMNQ.60267..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>  ← invalid tag
```
This is a DOM corruption that may cause downstream layout breakage in strict parsers.

**22 click links unprobed**
All destination URLs route through `click.chp.kohls.com` (CoherentPath) or `click.s.kohls.com`. QA skipped HTTP probes on all 22 — broken redirect targets, destination 404s, and missing UTM params cannot be confirmed.

**Four-vendor tracking pixel stack**
Pixels from `chp.kohls.com`, `mi.kohls.com`, `kohls.demdex.net`, and `click.s.kohls.com` all fire on open. No issue per se, but any single-vendor outage silently drops a tracking signal with no fallback.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers and some desktop clients (Outlook, Apple Mail) surface the title; leaving it blank provides no context.

**`user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). iOS Mail and Android Gmail clients respect this and block zoom for low-vision users.

**8 images missing `alt` text**
QA flagged all three open/tracking pixels plus five content images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`). Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (present on the `mi.kohls.com` pixel via `aria-hidden="true"` — correctly handled there); content images require descriptive alt text.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading**
Both a `<link>` tag and a CSS `@import` load the same Roboto and Montserrat families:
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...">
<style>@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Roboto...");</style>
```
This doubles the font request overhead and adds ~100–300ms latency on first render in clients that execute `@import`.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%token%%` patterns visible in the captured source. `mi_u=604230016` in pixel URLs appears to be a correctly merged subscriber ID (not a placeholder). `email_name=260703_DG_Email_Fri_PM` confirms send-date stamping is functioning.

No issues found in visible source — full merge verification requires the complete HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not confirmed**
QA could not capture these via the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender mandates (effective Feb 2024) require `List-Unsubscribe` with `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for one-click RFC 8058 compliance. If the headers exist upstream but are stripped by the relay, this is a monitoring gap; if they are genuinely absent, this is a deliverability risk with Gmail for high-volume sends.

**Physical mailing address not detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in every commercial email. The QA check did not find one in the captured HTML. This may be present in the untruncated body — must be verified against the full source.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured. The sending domain `s.kohls.com` should show `dkim=pass` and `spf=pass`; absence of the header in relay capture means authentication posture cannot be confirmed for this send.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `mi_ecmp=1016571_202673` is present in pixel URLs, confirming server-side campaign attribution is wired. However, since all 22 click links were proxied through CoherentPath/Kohl's click domains and skipped, UTM parameter presence on destination landing pages (`kohls.com`) cannot be confirmed by this audit. A manual spot-check of one CTA URL is needed to verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` pass through the redirect chain intact.
