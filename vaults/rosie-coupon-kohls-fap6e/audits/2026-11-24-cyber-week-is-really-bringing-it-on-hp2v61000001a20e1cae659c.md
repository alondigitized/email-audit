# Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a Cyber Week catalog send that mostly works on its hero. The purple-sequin "It's Good to Be You" block leads into three stacked 30% off brand tiles: Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People. Each has its own "SAVE NOW" button, so the offer is clear and easy to scan. It is cleaner than the 11/21 and 11/22 sends, which tried to cram in more ideas.
- The weak spot is the top of the email. Before the hero you get a red "Cyber Week is here! Save up to 50%" bar, then a "FREE* Sample Bag" block with a GRAMYBAG code. That is three competing offers (up to 50%, a free bag, 30% off) before the main deal. Below the hero, the email loses focus. It runs through "Treat Yourself for Less," "Chosen Just for You," Sol de Janeiro, a Dae gift set, Korean beauty and a holiday gift guide, all at once. Those sections add depth but dilute the 30% message.
- Overall this is a solid, seasonal send, but it still reads as a sprawling catalog. It also repeats the purple-sequin Cyber Week creative from the last three days. A subscriber would see this as the fourth near-identical beat, following "Cyber Week deals are HERE" on 11/23 and the 11/22 app push.

## What's working

- The 30% off tiles are big, high-contrast and repeated, with a clear "SAVE NOW" button on each.
- The sequin hero with "24 hours–only at Sephora" gives a credible deadline.
- The Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People logos are recognizable, and product shots sit next to each deal.
- "Treat Yourself for Less" shows struck-through prices (for example $10 to $7 and $24 to $19.20), which makes the savings concrete.
- Sephora branding and the purple Cyber Week styling are consistent.

## What's weak

- The top stack of the red banner, the sample bag and the hero offers three competing deals before the main one.
- Heavy fine print ("*Available on in-stock merchandise only," the long legal footer and "Exclusions/terms apply") hurts the honest-offer feel.
- The lower half has too many modules: Sol de Janeiro 25% off, a Dae gift set, Korean beauty up to 30% off and a four-button gift guide. They scatter attention.
- The sample bag requires a $90 minimum spend and a code, and that detail is easy to miss.
- This is the fourth near-identical sequin send in four days, which signals fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top to one offer. Drop the sample bag or move it below the 30% tiles, so the hero is the first thing the eye lands on.
- 2. Trim the lower half to one secondary module, such as Sol de Janeiro 25% off, and remove the generic gift guide.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop Kiehl's 30% off" instead of repeated "SAVE NOW."
- 4. Put the deadline in the hero as a clear time ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT") rather than "24 hours."
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier Cyber Week sends so the series doesn't feel repetitive.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rosie, your Cyber Week 30% off picks 💜`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People, all 30% off for 24 hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free sample bag with $90 spend. Ends tonight.`

# Technical Audit: Sephora "Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based layout with ESP redirect tracking. The QA run found two confirmed defects: an empty first-name merge slot and a missing plain-text part. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the captured data. The source is truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirect wrappers:** Links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a20e1cae659c8ff3f4bbe5be68/...?__dU__=...`. The final destination can't be read from the source.
- **Low probe coverage:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 41. No probe results (status codes, redirect chains) were supplied, so I can't confirm the links resolve.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>`
  - It is hidden and has no `alt`, which is the source of the QA missing-alt warning.
  - It sits in the preheader cell after the closing `</div>`.
  - This is low severity because the image is not user-visible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout:** The wrapper table is `width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block only changes font sizes and logo/icon dimensions. No visible rule overrides the 700px min-width, which may cause horizontal scrolling on mobile. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm this.
- **Invalid conditional comment:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media` rule in the `<style>` block. Conditional comments don't work inside CSS, so the MSO sizing rule is likely ineffective.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **Empty structured data:** The JSON-LD has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. The markup is empty and unlikely to be used by Gmail.
- **Missing alt:** The only alt warning from QA is the hidden tracking pixel above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `"Hi ,"` with the name field empty. The first-name merge value is missing, and there is no visible fallback such as "Hi there,". The test inbox likely has no profile data, but the template should still have a default.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header** was captured.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058)** was captured.
  - QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed.
  - If they are truly absent, the email lacks one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, but alignment can't be verified.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 characters.** The message is HTML-only. This hurts deliverability and accessibility for text-only clients.
- **Footer details:** The physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion of the source, so I can't confirm them. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible. All links go through `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects with an opaque `__dU__` token, so UTM parameters could only be read from the final destination URLs.
- **Landing page alignment:** Not verifiable, because no probe results or resolved destinations were provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a fallback to the first-name merge**, for example "Hi there,", and suppress the comma when the value is empty.
2. **Ship a multipart/alternative message** with a real text/plain part, using the preheader copy "STACK. SAVE. ENJOY." and key link text.
3. **Confirm in the raw sending path** (not just the relay) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC passing and aligned.
4. **Make the layout fluid:** Use `max-width:700px; width:100%` instead of `min-width:700px`, and add mobile table overrides.
5. **Fix the head markup:**
   - Move the MSO rule out of the `@media` block.
   - Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
   - Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
   - Use a descriptive `<title>`.
   - Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. **Re-run link probing on all 53 links**, or at least the unprobed CTAs, and record the final URLs and UTM parameters.
