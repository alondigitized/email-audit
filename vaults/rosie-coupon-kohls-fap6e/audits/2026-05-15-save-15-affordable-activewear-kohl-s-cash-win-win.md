# Save 15% | Affordable activewear + Kohl's Cash ... win-win 🏆
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Another Kohl's kitchen-sink blast, but this one has a sharper spine than last week's sends: the subject names a category ("affordable activewear"), a discount (15%), and a loyalty mechanic (Kohl's Cash) in one line. The TEK GEAR hero immediately backs that up with a $12.99 price point — that's a rare moment of lead-with-the-number discipline for this brand. The activewear thread holds through two full content modules (TEK GEAR + family active gear), which is more thematic focus than the May 11th "summer style upgrades" send managed.
- Then Sol de Janeiro hijacks the middle. A dark-background fragrance/beauty partnership block lands between the activewear content and the category tiles — it's visually jarring and narratively incoherent. The subscriber opened for activewear; they're now reading pocket-sized scents.
- The 15% off coupon — the actual primary offer — is buried at the very bottom behind four scrollable content blocks. This is the core execution failure: the subject promises 15% off, but you have to earn it by scrolling past beauty products to claim it.

## What's working

- TEK GEAR hero leads with a concrete $12.99 price — specificity beats the vague "up to X% off" pattern used in recent sends
- Subject line naming three distinct value props (discount, category, loyalty) in one breath is efficient and scan-friendly
- Family active gear block reinforces the activewear theme and adds demographic breadth (Women / Men / Kids / Shoes tabs)
- Kohl's Cash earn callout ($10) is a loyalty signal that rewards members; visible without needing to scroll past the coupon
- Rendering is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent visual weight throughout

## What's weak

- Sol de Janeiro insert mid-scroll is a category non-sequitur that breaks the activewear narrative entirely; feels like a paid-placement drop rather than editorial curation
- The 15% off coupon — the explicit subject line hook — is dead last in the email, below four content modules
- "Shop Now" is the CTA copy everywhere: generic, non-specific, identical across a beauty block and a kids' activewear block
- No urgency signal anywhere — no "ends Sunday," no "limited stock," nothing to pull the click today rather than never
- Cadence pressure: this is the sixth Kohl's promo in ten days; the 15% offer is structurally identical to the May 11th send, risking discount fatigue

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 15% coupon to module one, directly under the hero.** The subject promises it; deliver it before anything else. Every scroll below that is a bonus, not a prerequisite.
- 2. **Cut or relocate the Sol de Janeiro block.** If this is a brand partnership, it deserves its own dedicated send or a clearly labeled "Also in-store this week" sidebar — not a full-width interrupter in an activewear email.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** "Offer ends Sunday" on the coupon block converts fence-sitters. No deadline present in this send.
- 4. **Differentiate the CTA copy by block.** "Shop TEK Gear," "Shop Kids Active," "Shop Women's Styles" each reduce generic scroll-fatigue better than four identical "Shop Now" buttons.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Save 15% on TEK Gear + earn Kohl's Cash — today only`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `$12.99 activewear + 15% off your whole order 🏆`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `TEK Gear from $12.99 — plus stack your Kohl's Cash on top.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 15% off code is inside. Activewear, shoes, and more.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email uses a standard table-based layout with CoherentPath and Kohl's proprietary click-tracking infrastructure. The QA relay did not capture authentication or unsubscribe headers, leaving compliance and deliverability status unverified for this capture session.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped** — all click-through URLs route through `click.s.kohls.com` and `click.chp.kohls.com` redirect chains; HTTP probing was blocked, so destination validity and UTM integrity could not be confirmed programmatically.

Three distinct pixel/tracking systems are firing:
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/...` — CoherentPath open pixel
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/...` — Movable Ink personalization pixel
- `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` — Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?...` — secondary open tracker (ESP-side)

No broken link errors were returned. The `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` in `<head>` is an empty `href` — harmless but unnecessary noise.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on 11 images**, including tracking pixels and at minimum 6 content images (`*.png` via `mi.kohls.com`):


Tracking pixels correctly use `aria-hidden="true"` on the Movable Ink pixel (`o.gif`) but the CoherentPath pixel (`b0c766cf...`) and Adobe pixel (`kohls.demdex.net/event`) have no `alt=""` or `aria-hidden`, which will produce spurious screen reader announcements.

**Malformed HTML:** `</custom>` closing tag appears after the second open-tracker `<img>` with no matching opening tag — invalid HTML that may confuse parsers in some clients.

`user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta tag is a known accessibility violation (prevents zoom for low-vision users), though this is an industry-common pattern in email.

Google Fonts are loaded via both `<link>` and `@import` simultaneously for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — redundant requests, minor performance waste.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}` or `%7C` artifacts) visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1012612_2026515` values in Movable Ink URLs appear correctly resolved. The Adobe AAM pixel (`d_sid=13245196`) is a static segment ID — expected.

The `mi_kc_redm_end_dte=` parameter on the `916d6fa08d185233.png` Movable Ink URL has an **empty value** — this may indicate an unfilled dynamic token for a Kohl's Cash redemption end date:

This should be confirmed against the intended personalization logic; if it was meant to render a date-conditional image, the empty value could be causing the wrong creative to serve.
