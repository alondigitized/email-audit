# Take 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash starting today 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Friends & Family send audited yesterday (2026-08-24, "IT'S HERE: 20% off + Kohl's Cash," 7.00/10) — same 20%-off coupon, same "Ends Aug. 30" deadline, same barcode/coupon-in-wallet close — just a different subject line and a much longer, denim-first body. The hero is clean and the offer is unambiguous, but this send buries its own coupon under a sprawling denim campaign, a sweepstakes, a "picked for you" product grid, and a full footer before you ever hit the actual redemption mechanics.
- The good news: F&F + Kohl's Cash is a real, differentiated offer relative to the week of generic Back-to-School blasts, and "Rewards Member" status is visible at the very top for personalization. The bad news: at 5800px this is one of the longest, most kitchen-sink emails in this brand's recent cadence, and the subject/preview don't do the hero justice.

## What's working

- Clear "FRIENDS & FAMILY — Take an extra 20% off with coupon" hero banner immediately below the logo, no scrolling needed.
- "Status: KOHL'S REWARDS MEMBER" visible at the very top — real personalization signal, not generic copy.
- Denim pricing is concrete and layered (starting at $17.59, 20% off coupon, Levi's up to 30% off) with a clean "Shop Now" CTA directly under the hero.
- Coupon code (SHOPPERS), PIN, and expiration date are all visible near the bottom rather than hidden.

## What's weak

- The email is enormous — denim, Levi's, Denim Guide, Back to School categories, sweepstakes, and a four-item "picked for you" grid all precede the actual coupon/redemption block near the bottom.
- Subject line ("Take 20% off + earn Kohl's Cash starting today") doesn't mention "Friends & Family," which is the actual differentiator versus this week's five Back-to-School sends — it reads like just another generic percentage-off email.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled: the F&F 20% hero is immediately followed by Denim Deal Days as a second, competing hero, so the eye doesn't have one clear landing point.
- No preview/preheader text worth crediting — nothing complementary shown, just the "view in browser" fallback pattern implied by a blank preview slot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject and preview with "Friends & Family" explicitly — it's the actual news this week and gets lost as generic "20% off."
- 2. Cut the body by half: fold Denim Deal Days, Levi's, and the Denim Guide into one condensed module instead of three consecutive full-width sections — most subscribers won't scroll past the second hero.
- 3. Move the coupon code/PIN/expiration block up near the top hero instead of after sweepstakes and product grids — reduce friction between "I want this" and "here's how."
- 4. Give the Kohl's Cash green banner its own clear CTA (e.g., "See how much you'll earn") instead of just a status strip — right now it's informational, not clickable-feeling.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, earn $10 Kohl's Cash for every $50 you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Denim starts at $17.59 — before your extra 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an ESP-templated (Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style) HTML email from Kohl's using the `s.kohls.com`/`chp.kohls.com` tracking domains; core markup is boilerplate MSO/Outlook-safe code, but automated QA flags gaps in unsubscribe headers, sender authentication visibility, and alt-text coverage on tracking pixels.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 71 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.chp.kohls.com`, `click.s.kohls.com`) were skipped by the automated HTTP probe — destination validity for the actual offer CTAs is unconfirmed.
- Multiple tracking/beacon endpoints fire on load: `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1b08a894-...` (1×1 px), `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif` (open pixel), `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` (secondary open tracker), and `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` (Adobe Audience Manager/Demdex beacon). Triple-redundant open tracking (3 separate open pixels/beacons) is present but not itself a defect.
- Malformed markup: the Demdex-adjacent block closes with a stray `</custom>` tag that has no matching opening tag (`<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;"> <img ... width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom> </div>`), which is invalid HTML and may cause unpredictable parsing in strict renderers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 tracking/beacon images flagged with missing `alt` attributes: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/1b08a894-...` pixel, `mi.kohls.com/.../o.gif`, `kohls.demdex.net/event`, and five `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retargeting pixels. These are non-content 1×1 pixels, so the accessibility impact on screen readers is minimal, but best practice is `alt=""` explicitly (some sibling pixels in this same email already do this correctly, e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` img has `alt=""`).
- MSO conditional comments and `@media (max-width:500px)` responsive block are present and structured correctly for Outlook/mobile fallback.
- Full body content beyond the head/tracking block was truncated, so on-image alt text for actual creative/CTA images (vs. tracking pixels) cannot be verified from this source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the captured HTML — only opaque tracking IDs (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `d_sid`). No issues found in the visible source; unable to confirm behavior for the truncated body content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a capture artifact of the relay rather than true absence, but as observed this fails RFC 2369 support.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) is not confirmed supported.
- **No physical mailing address detected** — CAN-SPAM requires a physical postal address in the body; not present in the captured/truncated HTML.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Beacon/pixel URLs carry internal campaign identifiers (`mi_ecmp=1020416_2026824`, `email_name=260824_DG_Email_Mon_PM`) consistent with SFMC MobileID campaign tracking, but these are not standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params.
- The 71 click-through links were not probed (skipped as tracking-redirect domains), so destination landing pages and any UTM parameter alignment with the site cannot be verified from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (check raw headers at the source MTA, not just via the AgentMail relay capture) to support one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- Verify a physical mailing address is present in the (truncated) footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `s.kohls.com` independent of this relay's header capture, since `Authentication-Results` was not observed.
- Add explicit `alt=""` to the remaining tracking/beacon `<img>` tags for markup consistency (low impact, easy fix).
- Fix the stray `</custom>` closing tag to produce valid HTML.
- Spot-check a sample of the 71 skipped tracking links manually to confirm they resolve to correct, live landing pages.
