# Save 20%  I Time for a home refresh (inside & out) 🏡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's eighteenth-or-nineteenth promotional email in fourteen days, and it does something slightly more considered than the recent run: it leads with home (blenders, bath towels, bedding) and then pivots to back-to-school apparel. That's two coherent category stories bundled into one email, which at least justifies the send more than yesterday's "20% off, here's everything" catch-all. The 40% hero discount is a genuine step up from the 20% that has appeared in every prior send this month — that's the strongest signal in the email.
- The back-half pivot to back-to-school (Girls, Juniors, Shoes, Boys, Young Men, Backpacks) undercuts the home narrative. These are two separate emails stapled together, and neither gets the real estate it deserves. A subscriber who opened for the blender deal is now being asked to shop kids' clothes before they've hit scroll depth.
- The overall verdict: marginally better than the last week's clone emails because it has two distinct category anchors and a 40% lead offer, but the template, cadence, and visual language are indistinguishable from the prior fifteen sends. Fatigue is real.

## What's working

- **40% lead discount** — stepping up from the perpetual 20% actually creates contrast; it will read as a genuine escalation to anyone paying attention.
- **Home category focus at the top** — blender, bath towels, and bedding form a coherent category story, which is more defensible than the recent lifestyle-model hero + "everything" layout.
- **Back-to-school section is clearly labeled** — "Showing up in style, down to a science" is a workable headline; the category nav (Girls / Juniors / Shoes / Boys / Young Men / Backpacks) is clear and scannable.
- **Kohl's Cash earn reminder is present** — the "$25 earn" callout reinforces loyalty value.
- **No visible render bugs** — images load, text is readable, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- **Two emails in one body** — home refresh and back-to-school are distinct shopping missions; combining them dilutes both.
- **The 20% banner still appears mid-email** — after leading with 40%, reverting to 20% (bath towels section) feels like a bait-and-switch step-down.
- **No time-bound deadline is visible** — "savings are waiting" is vague; nothing communicates when the offer expires, which removes urgency.
- **Hero CTA copy is generic** — "Shop Home" and "Shop Back to School" do the bare minimum; no product specificity or benefit language.
- **Cadence is the dominant negative** — this is the eighteenth-plus email in fourteen days; even a good offer drowns in inbox noise at that frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two sends** — home refresh and back-to-school are calendar-perfect right-now topics, but they fight each other in one email. Send home today, back-to-school tomorrow (or skip one entirely).
- 2. **Lock in a deadline on the 40% offer** — "Ends Tuesday" or "This weekend only" makes the 40% feel credible rather than evergreen.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero CTA around the 40% product** — "Shop 40% off kitchen" beats "Shop Home" by giving the click a purpose.
- 4. **Drop the mid-email 20% reversal** — if 40% leads, the body should sustain it or explain the tier difference explicitly.
- 5. **Pull the back-to-school grid into its own email** — with a subject that owns the BTS angle cleanly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off home finds — blenders, towels, bedding`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your home refresh starts at 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, earn Kohl's Cash on every $50 — ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Blenders, bath towels, bedding — shop the steepest discounts of July`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save 20% / Home Refresh" Email

**Sender:** Kohls@s.kohls.com | **Date:** 2026-07-14

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a well-structured HTML4 transitional skeleton with VML/MSO conditional blocks and a standard responsive breakpoint at 500px. The primary technical concerns are missing compliance signals (unsubscribe headers, physical address) and widespread missing `alt` text on tracking and content images — the latter likely suppressed intentionally on tracking pixels but problematic on content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**22 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-through URLs route via `click.chp.kohls.com` and `click.s.kohls.com`, which is expected for ESP redirect tracking. No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but they could not be validated.

**Three distinct tracking pixel stacks are present:**
- `click.chp.kohls.com/o/{uuid}` — Coherent Path behavioral tracker
- `mi.kohls.com/p/up/` — Movable Ink open pixel
- `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx` — ESP open pixel (Salesforce/ExactTarget pattern)
- `kohls.demdex.net/event` — Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) sync pixel

**Malformed custom tag:** Line `</custom>` appears immediately after the `click.s.kohls.com` open pixel `<img>`. This is invalid HTML and may cause parse errors in strict renderers.

```html
<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
</custom>   ← invalid closing tag
```

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 images missing `alt` text** per QA findings. Three are intentional tracking pixels (acceptable with `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"` — the open pixel at `click.chp.kohls.com` is missing both attributes). The remaining 8 are content images hosted at `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/` (Movable Ink rendered personalization images):

```
2a5adc5afb647aad.png, f441d45d2128ae53.png, c2f81e9ac8daee44.png,
415c8a18fc2950ea.png, 69494b48a79b4240.png, 78308d1d9adb0751.png,
916d6fa08d185233.png
```

Content images without `alt` text render as broken placeholders in image-blocking email clients (Outlook default, many corporate clients) and fail WCAG 2.1 AA for screen readers.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure and blocked in iOS 10+.

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**Empty `<title>` tag** — subject line is not populated into `<title>`, which affects VoiceOver/NVDA announcement on email open.

**Google Fonts loaded via both `<link>` and `@import`** for the same families (Roboto, Montserrat) — redundant double-fetch in clients that support web fonts.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) detected in the truncated source — tokens appear fully rendered.

Movable Ink `mi_u=604230016` and `mi_ecmp=1017539_2026714` parameters are present and consistent across all `mi.kohls.com` image URLs, indicating the render-time personalization pipeline resolved correctly for this recipient.

One content image URL contains an apparently empty merge field:
```
mi_kc_redm_end_dte=&mi_kc_event_pe   ← trailing empty params
```
This suggests a conditional block with an unfilled date field. If `mi_kc_redm_end_dte` controls a countdown timer or offer expiry display, this recipient may be seeing a broken or default state.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| List-Unsubscribe header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — may be present upstream but stripped |
| List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |
| Physical mailing address | **WARN** | Not detected in HTML source (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5)) |
| `robots` meta | Pass | `noindex, nofollow` present |
| `referrer` meta | Pass | `no-referrer` present |

**CAN-SPAM physical address** is a hard requirement. If it's present in an image-only footer (common in Kohl's templates), it is invisible when images are blocked and likely does not satisfy the statutory requirement for plain-text disclosure.

**Unsubscribe headers** — the WARN from AgentMail relay is likely a relay-layer artifact (headers may exist on the original SMTP envelope but not forwarded). Confirm by checking raw message headers in the originating ESP. If genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Apple Mail one-click unsubscribe compliance gap as of 2024 Google/Yahoo sender requirements for bulk senders.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 click links are wrapped in `click.chp.kohls.com` or `click.s.kohls.com` redirects — destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from the source alone.

**Cannot verify:**

Recommend spot-checking 2–3 CTA links by following the redirect chain to confirm UTM parameters survive the ESP redirect.
