# Happening now: up to 50% off select beauty and 500 points with $75 spend
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's Stacking Weekend email again — the same maroon 50%-off-plus-500-points hero from 9/4's "Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points" send, but this time it's stapled to a much longer email: a "Free Hair Sample Set" module, a dense NEW-arrivals product grid (Bobbi Brown, Jo Malone, drip ii, Oribe, Touchland, EADEM, Dermalogica, MERIT), a second 50%-off module name-dropping Selena Gomez/Rare Beauty, then a 15% off haircare stack, a perfume launch module, and a lip launch module. It's less a single offer than five offers duct-taped together.
- The hero itself is genuinely clean — big legible "50%" circle plus a "+500" points circle, one clear "SHOP NOW" button — but everything below it dilutes that clarity. By the time a reader hits the second "50% off Select Beauty" module three screens down, the headline offer has already been diluted by hair samples and a dozen product tiles.
- Given this is functionally a repeat of yesterday's Stacking Weekend push wrapped in more content, not a new campaign, cadence fatigue is real. It's competently built and offer-rich, but it reads as inbox clutter for anyone who opened 9/4's version less than 24 hours ago.

## What's working

- The hero cleanly stacks two distinct mechanics (50% off + 500 bonus points) in circular badges with one unambiguous "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the second 50%-off module creates real urgency without shouting.
- Loyalty tiers (Insider / VIB / Rouge) and the 500-points redemption terms are visible near the footer, which matters for a Beauty Insider subscriber.
- No visual bugs — every product tile, badge, and image renders cleanly with prices attached.
- The barcode/in-store redemption codes at the bottom (SEPTBONUS) are a nice omnichannel touch for people who shop in-store.

## What's weak

- Five distinct offers (50% off, free hair samples, 15% off haircare, perfume launch, lip launch) compete in one email with no clear priority — nothing tells the reader which one matters most.
- This is essentially a rerun of 9/4's Stacking Weekend hero one day later, just with more bolted on — a subscriber who opened yesterday's email will recognize the reused creative immediately.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" appears after nearly every module, which reads as a soft warning that the headline offer is narrower than it looks.
- The product grid section has no section header explaining why these specific items are grouped (no "New This Week" or similar label), so it reads as a generic dump.
- CTA copy is "SHOP NOW" everywhere — same verb, same noun, six-plus times — giving the reader zero signal about what's different behind each button.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** Keep the 50%-off + 500-points hero and the loyalty-tier footer; move the hair samples, 15% haircare, perfume, and lip launches into separate sends or a "More ways to save" single-module teaser. One offer per email converts better than five diluted ones.
- 2. **Differentiate CTA copy per module** — "Shop Hair Samples," "Stack Your Points," "Shop New Perfume" instead of six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 3. **Don't resend the identical hero creative two days running** — vary the visual treatment from 9/4 even if the promo mechanic is the same, so subscribers don't immediately clock it as a repeat.
- 4. **Move exclusions/terms language to a single footnote** rather than repeating it after every module — it currently reads as friction stacked on friction.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 50% off + 500 points expire tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack it: 50% off beauty, 500 bonus points, $75 spend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free hair samples with $45 spent — while supplies last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only — Rare Beauty, Benefit, and Supergoop join the 50% off list`
