# Stack 20% off + $10 off your $25+ purchase while you can 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is another Kohl's holiday deal wall, and the subject promises more focus than the body delivers. The subject says "Stack 20% off + $10 off your $25+ purchase." The hero shows "Take $10 off your purchase of $25 or more with coupon" with "Ends Nov. 11," and "30% off" and "Kohl's Cash" appear just below it. A reader gets three or four stacking mechanics before the first scroll. The codes (SAVEMORE, SHOP10, and others) are all at the bottom, so "stack" is never explained near the top.
- Below the hero it becomes a gift-guide catalog: Gift List Lane, For Him, For Her, For Kids, $25 & Under, then Simply Vera Wang, home categories, Clearance "up to 70%," delivery partners and four products. It's seasonal and well produced. But it is roughly the 15th near-identical Kohl's send since late October, after the Nov 9 "Save an extra 20% 🙌 And score Kohl's Cash" and the Nov 8 Early Black Friday sends. The $10-off-$25 hook is the one fresh, concrete thing here, and the layout buries it under category tiles.
- Verdict: a strong-enough offer in a bloated, familiar wrapper. It will get opens from loyal subscribers, but the click goes to whichever tile catches the eye, not to the stacking promise.

## What's working

- The subject leads with a concrete, low-threshold offer ($10 off $25+). That is easier to act on than the earlier "up to 50% off" language.
- The hero has a clear "Shop Now" link and a visible deadline ("Ends Nov. 11").
- The gift-guide tiles (For Him, For Her, For Kids, $25 & Under) are seasonal and easy to scan.
- Product cards show real prices ($2.99–$9.99 towels, $249.99 Ninja SLUSHi, $169.99 Ninja Foodi), which gives the eye something concrete.
- The delivery and pickup block (Doordash, Instacart, Klarna) is a useful holiday-timing signal.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers sit in the top screen: $10 off, 30% off, Kohl's Cash, military discount. The "stack" promise isn't visually resolved.
- The email is about 7,900px long. The fine print, barcodes and codes are at the very bottom, so a mobile reader has to scroll to learn how to redeem.
- The hero banner's offer text and the "Shop Now" link are small relative to the large category buttons below it. The eye lands on the red nav buttons first.
- The military "Thank you for your service" banner takes prime space near the top and is irrelevant to most recipients.
- Cadence fatigue: the same coupon-wall structure has gone out almost daily, and the creative looks like Nov 8–9.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top screen to one offer. Make "$10 off $25+" the hero with a single button, "Shop $10 Off," and move the 30% and Kohl's Cash blocks lower.
- 2. Put the redemption code (SHOP10) and the "Add to Wallet" action next to the hero, not in the footer.
- 3. Move the military banner below the first product block or segment it to eligible recipients.
- 4. Replace the five-plus category tiles with one curated "Under $25 gifts" row, so the $25 threshold connects to products.
- 5. Reduce frequency, or send a distinct creative on days when the offer hasn't changed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$10 off $25 ends tomorrow — code SHOP10 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $25 that unlock $10 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off home and Kohl's Cash. Coupon ends Nov. 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add it to your wallet and stack it at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based layout with `lang="en"`, mobile media queries and MSO conditionals. Four tracking pixels fire, the head contains invalid markup, and the viewport blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided (truncated HTML, relay-stripped headers), and none of the 81 links were probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were never HTTP-probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=ABl7ImQi…`), so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Four separate tracking and open pixels load per open:**
  - `click.chp.kohls.com/o/ed8938c4-…`
  - `mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif`
  - `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?qs=…`
  - `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`
  
  The redundancy adds requests and can inflate open counts. Apple MPP and image blocking make the figures unreliable regardless.
- **Malformed markup after the open.aspx pixel:** `<img … alt=""></custom>` has a stray closing tag with no opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid.
- **The `click.chp.kohls.com` pixel sits directly under `<body>`** (not inside a table), and the 1×1 table that follows it adds an extra layer of hidden markup.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (14,316 of 20,104 chars), which is a spam-filter signal. The tracking URLs are long and fully encoded.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** no document title for web-view and assistive-tech contexts.
- **Invalid head content:**
  - `<div itemscope itemtype="http://schema.org/Organization"><meta itemprop=…></div>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers can implicitly close the head at the `<div>`.
  - `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` has an empty href and triggers a pointless request in web view.
- **Missing alt text (QA flagged 11 images):**
  - Confirmed in the source: the `click.chp.kohls.com/o/…` pixel and the `kohls.demdex.net/event` pixel have no `alt`. These are 1×1/0×0 pixels, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""`.
  - **QA false positive:** `mi.kohls.com/…/o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative image.
  - **Unverified but likely real:** the `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`2a5adc5afb647aad.png`, `f441d45d2128ae53.png`, `c2f81e9ac8daee44.png`, `415c8a18fc2950ea.png`, `69494b48a79b4240.png`, `78308d1d9adb0751.png`, `916d6fa08d185233.png`) sit in the truncated portion. If they are offer or product images, missing alt leaves the content unavailable when images are blocked. The QA tool may also be treating `alt=""` as missing.
- **`role="presentation"` is inconsistent:** it is on the main wrapper and container tables but missing on the 1×1 pixel table, the AAM table and the preheader table.
- **Web fonts:** Roboto and Montserrat load via `@import` and `<link>` to Google Fonts, inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is declared, so this is acceptable. `@import` is unsupported in some clients, and the fallback handles that.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The rest is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are absent at the sender, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.kohls.com`.
- **No physical address was detected.** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this cannot be confirmed. Verify against the full source.
- **Unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source and not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters are not verifiable.** Links are wrapped in `click.s.kohls.com/?qs=…`, an opaque encoded redirect, and the HTTP probe was skipped, so UTM propagation and final landing URLs are unknown.
- **The only visible campaign identifiers are in image URLs:** `mi_ecmp=1025607_20261110` and `email_name=261110_DG_Email_Tuesda…`. These identify the campaign and send date but are not site-attribution parameters.
- **Offer alignment (20% off plus $10 off $25+) cannot be checked** against landing pages without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Fill `<title>`, remove `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">`, and move the `schema.org` `<div>` out of `<head>` or delete it.
3. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag.
4. Add `alt=""` to the `click.chp.kohls.com` and `demdex.net` pixels. Give content images in `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` meaningful alt text.
5. Consolidate redundant open pixels where possible.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message headers, not the relay copy. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
7. Rerun the audit with full HTML to confirm the footer address and unsubscribe link, and resolve the redirects to verify landing URLs and UTMs.
8. Shorten tracking URLs to reduce the plain-text URL ratio.
