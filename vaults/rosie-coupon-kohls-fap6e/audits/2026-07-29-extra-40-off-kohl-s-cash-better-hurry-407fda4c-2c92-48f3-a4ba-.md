# Extra 40% off + Kohl's Cash? Better hurry 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest render Kohl's has sent in this stretch — the "Extra 40% off" hero banner is fully intact, centered, and legible, a real departure from the clipped/garbled hero graphics flagged in the July 27 ("This just in: 20% off is coming in hot") and July 28 ("20% off ENDS TODAY") sends. The layout does its job: deep-maroon hero up top, Kohl's Cash cross-sell banner, four clean category buttons, then two product call-outs with real prices, a clearance banner, and the coupon barcode at the bottom.
- The problem isn't execution, it's repetition and pricing clarity. This is at least the fourth "act now" discount mechanic in eight days (July 22–26 ran Instant Kohl's Cash five times; July 27–28 ran the 20%-off countdown twice), and the "Better hurry" subject line is the same urgency beat recycled again. On top of that, the polo product block shows three different price points ($24.99 reg, $19.99 sale, $11.99 with coupon) stacked in six lines of text, which undercuts the "simple 40% off" promise the hero just made.
- Net: strong render, weak novelty. A subscriber who's opened three of the last five Kohl's emails will recognize this instantly and may skim past it.

## What's working

- Hero renders correctly this time — "40% off" is centered, unclipped, and readable at a glance, unlike the July 27/28 sends.
- Rewards status ("Kohl's Rewards Member," "Earn 5% Rewards") is shown right at the top, reinforcing loyalty value before any ask.
- Coupon code, PIN, and barcode are all present and legible near the bottom — no missing/broken coupon graphic.
- Category navigation (Women/Men/Kids/Home) appears twice, giving multiple clickable entry points beyond the generic "Shop Now."
- Product shots include real prices and comparison to regular price, giving a tangible sense of savings.

## What's weak

- The polo product block stacks three prices ($24.99 reg → $19.99 sale → $11.99 with coupon) with no clear math shown — a scanning reader won't immediately know their actual price.
- Subject line and hero both lead with "hurry"/urgency, the same lever used in the July 26–28 sends — this is now a predictable pattern, not a signal.
- No personalization visible anywhere (no name, no "items you viewed," no size/category tailored to the recipient).
- "Find summer looks that give your budget a break | View this email in a browser" sits above the fold, adding pre-hero friction before the offer.
- Fine print block at the very bottom is dense (coupon eligibility, Kohl's Cash rules, Sephora terms) — not a bug, but it dilutes the "simple 40% off" promise made in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Simplify the polo pricing to one clear number ("$11.99 with coupon" only, move reg/sale price to a strikethrough sub-line) — three stacked prices is the single biggest clarity loss in the body.
- 2. Break the urgency-every-email pattern — since this is the 4th+ "hurry" send in a week and a half, swap to a value-forward angle (e.g., lead with Kohl's Cash earn-rate or a specific category) to avoid urgency fatigue eroding open rates.
- 3. Add one personalization hook (last-viewed category or "Women's picks for you") to differentiate from the identical storewide blasts running this month.
- 4. Move category buttons (Women/Men/Kids/Home) up, directly under the hero, so there's a specific-category CTA above the fold instead of only generic "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off ends tonight — plus Kohl's Cash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off coupon (SAVE40) + $10 Kohl's Cash`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tops from $20.99, polos from $11.99 — coupon applies at checkout`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your 40% off with $10 Kohl's Cash per $50 spent`

# Technical Audit: Kohl's — "Extra 40% off + Kohl's Cash?"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Kohl's/CoherentPath ESP template with heavy multi-vendor tracking (CoherentPath, Kohl's internal `mi.kohls.com`, Adobe Audience Manager); several header-level compliance signals could not be verified and one malformed tag was found in the tracking pixel block.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Primary click-tracking redirect: `https://click.chp.kohls.com/o/bbc0ea32-cf9c-4539-929a-7bb988b93403?mi_mid=019fae9a-ac00-7000-ab65-c3adb65af85e&cp_tp=v3.eJyN...` — this is also used as a 1x1 open-tracking pixel (`<img width="1" height="1">`), separate from the dedicated open-tracking pixel below.
- Dedicated open pixel: `https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?WX4UP6V62QDEFBPJUCJCPQSONI.60263&d=60263&bmt=0`
- Additional impression/measurement pixels: `https://mi.kohls.com/p/up/88954bbbbcab3c0e/o.gif?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1018645_2026729` and multiple `https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png` retina/relay pixels.
- Adobe Audience Manager (AAM) beacon: `https://kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196`.
- QA flags **67 tracking/redirect links skipped from HTTP probing** — link destinations behind `click.chp.kohls.com` and `mi.kohls.com` are opaque redirect wrappers, so live-URL validity (200 vs. broken) is unconfirmed for the bulk of links in this email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images flagged with missing `alt` text**, all tracking/measurement pixels (`bbc0ea32...`, `o.gif`, `event`, four `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/*.png`, `57055646_1s_2x` product image, `69494b48...`, `78308d1d...`, `916d6fa0...`). The product image (`57055646_1s_2x`) missing alt text is the one user-facing accessibility gap; the rest are tracking pixels where `alt=""`/`aria-hidden` would be the correct fix rather than a missing attribute.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` disables pinch-to-zoom — an accessibility concern for low-vision users (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize Text conflict).
- Malformed markup: `<img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>` — stray closing `</custom>` tag with no matching opening tag, inside the open-tracking `<div>`. Not fatal in most clients but is invalid HTML and could trigger inconsistent DOM handling in strict renderers (e.g., some webmail sanitizers).
- Empty `<title></title>` — some webmail/email clients fall back to this for tab titles or previews.
- Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional with legacy MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) — standard/expected for Outlook desktop compatibility, not a defect.
- Responsive breakpoint at 500px (`.container`, `.w_50pc`, etc.) is present and correctly structured for mobile reflow.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization artifacts are visible in the provided source. `mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`, `mi_u` are tracking/subscriber IDs, not content merge fields — no issues found. Note: HTML is truncated before the main offer/body content, so body-level personalization (e.g., name insertion, Kohl's Cash balance) could not be fully audited.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true sender omission; cannot confirm from HTML alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) not confirmed supported.
- **No physical mailing address detected** — CAN-SPAM requires one; not present in the truncated HTML. Given the source is cut off before the footer, this may simply be outside the captured excerpt rather than genuinely absent — flag for re-check against full HTML/footer.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via the AgentMail relay path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are present on any visible link; Kohl's uses proprietary tracking params instead (`mi_ecmp=1018645_2026729`, `mi_kc_redm_end_dte`, `mi_kc_event_pe`, `email_name=260729_DG_Email_Wednesd...`, `cp_cid=8ba359f132e3c109`).
- All outbound offer links route through the `click.chp.kohls.com` redirector rather than direct-to-site URLs, so final landing-page destination/alignment cannot be verified from this source (consistent with the 67 skipped-probe links in QA).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (not omit the attribute) to all tracking/measurement pixels (`o.gif`, `event`, `/p/rp/*.png`, open-tracking pixel) to pass automated accessibility checks cleanly.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product image `57055646_1s_2x` — the one customer-visible image currently missing it.
3. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag in the open-tracking `<div>` block.
4. Reconsider `user-scalable=0` in the viewport meta to avoid blocking pinch-zoom for accessibility.
5. Re-verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results against raw SMTP headers directly from the source MTA (not just the AgentMail relay capture) before concluding they're genuinely absent.
6. Confirm CAN-SPAM physical address is present in the (truncated) footer of the full email.
