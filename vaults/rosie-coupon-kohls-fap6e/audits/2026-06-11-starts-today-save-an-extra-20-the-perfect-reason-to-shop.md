# STARTS TODAY: Save an extra 20% ... the perfect reason to shop 🛒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Kohl's second Father's Day send of the season, arriving four days out from June 15 (the Kohl's Cash earn deadline) and ten days before Father's Day itself. The timing is right and the 20% off hook is real, but the email can't decide what it wants to be: it opens as a men's gifting play (FLX golf shirts, shoes, character gifts), then pivots to a completely off-persona beauty/skincare "HAPPENING NOW" section at the bottom with its own promo code. That tonal split hurts.
- Compared to the June 8 send — which opened with the tight "You're under budget. Dad's over the moon" headline — this one feels assembled rather than crafted. The hero banner (20% off + Father's Day countdown) is solid, but below it the email sprawls across five separate product buckets, each with its own "Shop Now" button, none of which pulls harder than the others. The Kohl's Cash callout ($10 when you spend $50) is a loyalty signal that earns trust; the scattered layout dilutes it.
- The email will convert on volume and brand familiarity, not on creative merit. It's a B-tier execution of an A-tier calendar moment.

## What's working

- **Father's Day urgency is well-timed** — four days to earn Kohl's Cash through June 15 is a real, credible deadline.
- **Kohl's Cash callout is prominent** — the $10/$50 earn badge is visible and reinforces loyalty value.
- **FLX golf shirts with an anchored $24.99 price point** are a natural gift suggestion for the season; the price ladder works.
- **No render bugs** — the email displays cleanly across all sections with no broken images or overlapping text.
- **Preheader ("Time's running out to get your gifts for Father's Day") genuinely complements the subject** rather than repeating it.

## What's weak

- **Beauty/skincare "HAPPENING NOW" section at the bottom completely breaks the Father's Day framing** — it reads like a second email was stitched onto the bottom of this one.
- **Five separate product sections, all labeled "Shop Now"** — no visual hierarchy tells the reader which gift to actually buy; it's a category dump.
- **Subject line is overlong (63 chars) and trails into dead copy** — "... the perfect reason to shop 🛒" after the ellipsis adds zero information.
- **No social proof anywhere** — no star ratings, no "most gifted" badge, nothing that would reassure a last-minute buyer choosing between an FLX polo and Nike elsewhere.
- **The June 8 "You're under budget. Dad's over the moon" headline has been abandoned** with nothing of comparable sharpness to replace it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the beauty section entirely or send it as a separate email.** It degrades the Father's Day coherence and splits the reader's intent at exactly the moment you want them to click to men's gifts.
- 2. **Consolidate the five product sections to two**: one men's apparel hero (FLX golf shirts + shoes with starting prices) and one "if you're stuck" catch-all like character/novelty gifts. One CTA each with specific copy.
- 3. **Rewrite CTA buttons** from "Shop Now" → "Shop Golf Gifts" / "Shop Men's Shoes" — any verb + specific noun lifts click rate.
- 4. **Tighten the subject line** to under 50 chars; front-load the time signal, drop the ellipsis.
- 5. **Add a "Most-gifted this Father's Day" badge or any social-proof hook** on the lead product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off Father's Day gifts — starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 days left: extra 20% off Dad's gift 🛒`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `FLX golf shirts from $24.99. Earn $10 Kohl's Cash.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `He'll actually use this — shop men's gifts now.`

## Technical Audit — Kohl's "Save an extra 20%" (2026-06-11)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but has a malformed HTML tag, an empty `<title>`, and a high volume of accessibility violations from untagged tracking pixels. Compliance signals (List-Unsubscribe, physical address) are absent from what the relay captured, which is the most significant deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tracking infrastructure identified:**
- CoherentPath click tracking: `click.chp.kohls.com`
- MessageGears open/render tracking: `mi.kohls.com`
- Adobe Audience Manager sync pixel: `kohls.demdex.net`
- Responsys open pixel: `click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx`

**Issues:**
- **22 click links unverifiable** — all wrapped in redirect domains; destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from source. QA correctly skipped HTTP probing.
- **`email_name` parameter appears truncated** in several `mi.kohls.com` render-pixel URLs: `email_name=260611_DG_Email_Thursda` (cut off at 30 chars). Evidence: `src="https://mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png?mi_u=604230016&mi_ecmp=1014914_2026611&email_name=260611_DG_Email_Thursda"`. Low impact for recipients but breaks campaign reporting if the field is used for segmentation.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML tag:**
- `</custom>` appears as a stray closing tag immediately after the Responsys open pixel `<div>`:
  ```html
  <img src="https://click.s.kohls.com/open.aspx?..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>
  ```
  `<custom>` is not a valid HTML element. Most clients will silently ignore it, but it is an invalid construct and could cause issues in strict parsers or future-proofing.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
- `<title></title>` — no document title set. Screen readers announce the title when a message is opened; an empty title degrades accessibility.

**Empty favicon href:**
- `<link href="" rel="shortcut icon">` — empty `href` triggers a redundant HTTP request to the current document URL in some environments. Should be removed or set to a valid resource.

**Missing alt text — 10 images flagged by QA:**

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `click.chp.kohls.com/o/9bd80fde...` | CoherentPath open pixel | No `alt` attribute, no `aria-hidden` |
| `kohls.demdex.net/event?d_sid=13245196` | AAM sync pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/2a5adc5afb647aad.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/f441d45d2128ae53.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/c2f81e9ac8daee44.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/415c8a18fc2950ea.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |
| `mi.kohls.com/p/rp/69494b48a79b4240.png` | Render pixel | No `alt` attribute |

Note: `mi.kohls.com/p/up/o.gif` correctly has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` — that pattern should be applied to all the above.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:**
- `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-to-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) and is blocked entirely on iOS 10+.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — not captured**
QA confirms the header was not found via the AgentMail relay. This may be a relay-layer capture gap rather than a genuine absence, but it cannot be verified. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for one-click unsubscribe; if missing in transit, bulk sender compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements) is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not captured**
Same caveat as above. Without this header, one-click unsubscribe (as opposed to mailto-based) is not supported at the protocol level.

**Physical mailing address — not detected in HTML source**
CAN-SPAM §7 requires a valid physical postal address. QA did not detect one in the truncated source provided. This must be confirmed against the full HTML — if the footer was cut off before rendering, it may still be present further down. If absent, this is a legal compliance gap.

**Authentication-Results — not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown for this message as received. The sending domain is `s.kohls.com` (Responsys subdomain); authentication configuration for this subdomain should be verified independently.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 22 links are wrapped in CoherentPath (`click.chp.kohls.com`) or Responsys (`click.s.kohls.com`) redirectors, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are not inspectable from source. Cannot confirm whether landing pages carry consistent UTM attribution. This should be spot-checked by following at least one tracked CTA link manually and verifying `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` on the destination.
