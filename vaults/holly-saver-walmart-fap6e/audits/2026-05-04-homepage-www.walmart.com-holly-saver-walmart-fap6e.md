# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- I'm looking at Walmart's mobile homepage and it's doing exactly what I'd expect — blue, clean, and pushing Mother's Day hard. The big hero is a Walmart+ free delivery pitch tied to the holiday, which is timely. Below the fold there's a Nintendo Switch deal and a 40% off Crocs banner, plus an app download prompt that's eating up a ton of screen real estate. Classic Walmart: functional but crowded.
- **5/10**
- + Hero copy reflects current campaign / season — "Mother's Day gifts as fast as today" is timely and on-point
- + One concrete offer is visible: "Free Delivery" called out in the hero
- + One unmistakable primary CTA is visible: "Try Walmart+" button in the hero
- + Visual hierarchy is reasonably clear — eye lands on the hero first
- + No render bugs visible — layout is clean and images load
- − No returning-shopper hook (no "Welcome back Holly," no recent items, no Walmart+ member balance shown)
- − Hero imagery (teddy bear + balloons) doesn't speak to a Gen X household manager; skews younger/gifting
- − No loyalty/membership benefits visible without scrolling beyond the app-download banner that's blocking the view
- − The app download modal is swallowing half the screen, obscuring content
- − No explicit dollar-off or percentage-off in the hero itself — the 40% Crocs deal is there but small

## What's working

- **"Mother's Day gifts as fast as today" hero** — I'm a household manager, and I've absolutely waited until the last minute on this. Same-day/fast delivery messaging hits me right where I live.
- **"Free Delivery" callout** — That's the number-one reason I pay for Walmart+. Seeing it front and center validates the membership.
- **"Rollbacks & More" navigation tab** — That word "Rollbacks" is the reason I open Walmart in the first place. Having it one tap away in the nav is exactly right.
- **"40% off Crocs" tile** — A real percentage off a name brand, no buried asterisk, straightforward. That's the kind of deal I'll tap.
- **Location set to Alhambra, 91801** — It knows my zip, which means pickup and delivery results will be relevant. No friction.

## What's weak

- **App download banner is hijacking the screen** — The "Get the app" overlay is covering the bottom third of visible content. I'm already on your site; stop asking me to download the app. This is pure dark pattern for a returning user.
- **Hero image (teddy bear and balloon)** — This is cute for a 25-year-old buying for their mom, but I *am* the mom. Nothing here speaks to me as the shopper managing the household; it speaks to someone shopping *for* me.
- **"Try Walmart+" CTA in the hero** — I might already be a Walmart+ member. There's no recognition of that. A returning member sees an upsell they may have already bought, which feels tone-deaf.
- **No prices or specific products in the hero** — "Gifts as fast as today" is a promise, but what gifts? Give me a product, give me a number. Don't make me guess.
- **"Spanish / Español now available" banner with an X** — This is taking up a full strip of real estate at the top. Fine feature, but it shouldn't be a persistent pop-in eating my screen on load.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the teddy-bear hero for a lifestyle flat-lay of practical Mother's Day wins** — think flowers + a candle + a mug with a price callout. Speaks to me as the buyer *and* the recipient, and shows actual shoppable product.
- **Detect Walmart+ membership and swap "Try Walmart+" to "Your free delivery is ready"** — a signed-in member should see confirmation of value, not a re-upsell. This is a quick personalization win.
- **Kill or shrink the app-download interstitial** — auto-dismiss it for users who arrived via a mobile browser session longer than 10 seconds, or for signed-in members. It's burying the deals I actually came for.
- **Add one Rollback price tile directly below the hero** — something like "Bounty 12-pack, was $18.97, now $14.97" would stop me mid-scroll immediately. That's the Walmart I know.
