# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Walmart knows exactly who I am today: a Walmart+ member who's also a mom, right before Mother's Day. The "Moms & members know best" hero is almost embarrassingly on-point for me. They're pushing Flash Deals hard — and with "Rollbacks & More" as the first nav tab, this feels like my store, set up for my kind of shopping run.

## What's working

- **"Moms & members know best" hero** — hits me on two levels simultaneously. I'm a mom and I'm a Walmart+ member. That's not accidental; that's smart Mother's Day CRM.
- **"Rollbacks & More" as the first nav tab** — I think in Rollbacks. Putting it first is my language, my mindset.
- **Flash Deals with real struck-through prices** — I track unit prices in my head. Showing me $29.99 crossed out and $17.96 gives me the math I need instantly.
- **"Get it Fast" nav tab** — Walmart+ grocery delivery is why I'm here half the time. That tab tells me the fulfillment options are one tap away.
- **Location set (Alhambra, 91801)** — store-aware experience means I know the inventory is local. Trust goes up.

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button** — the carousel has pause/arrows but no "Shop Member Deals" or "See your savings" call to action. I'd have to guess where to tap.
- **Flash Deal product mix is random** — windshield wipers, a body scale, and clearance bedding in the same row. There's no category coherence; it feels like a clearance dump, not a curated deal event.
- **Arctus Twin pricing is confusing** — "Now $9x" from $107 but "Options from $179"? That's contradictory. I'd pause and distrust the whole deal row.
- **No grocery / household staples visible** — my whole reason for being on Walmart.com is weekly grocery and household runs. Nothing in the above-the-fold Flash Deals speaks to paper towels, cleaning supplies, or pantry staples.
- **Spanish banner takes up real estate** — it's dismissible, but it's the first thing below the header. Not relevant to my household; it just delays getting to the deals.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Member Deals" button to the hero** — "Moms & members know best" sets the promise; give me one tap to collect it. A yellow Walmart button with "See your member savings" would immediately pay off the copy.
- **Swap the Flash Deals product mix for household essentials** — show me Tide, Bounty, or Gain at a rollback price. That's my weekly shopping list. Wipers and a body scale feel like returns inventory.
- **Fix or remove the Arctus Twin pricing card** — "Options from $179" above a $107 original price is broken logic. Either fix the copy or pull the card; it erodes trust in the entire deal row.
- **Surface Walmart+ delivery benefit copy** — one line like "Free delivery on groceries today — Walmart+" below the hero would remind me why my membership is worth it and prompt me to build my cart.
