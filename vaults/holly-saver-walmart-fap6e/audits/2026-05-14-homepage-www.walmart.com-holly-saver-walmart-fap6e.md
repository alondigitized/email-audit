# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Walmart is clearly in Walmart+ push mode right now — that blue hero is all about membership, not groceries. As someone who already has Walmart+, the "Hey future" headline honestly reads like an ad aimed at people who haven't joined yet, not a returning member like me. The useful stuff (categories, Rollback deals, my actual shopping list needs) is buried below a tier of marketing noise.

## What's working

- **Walmart+ member earn tile** — I can see the 5% reward prominently. That speaks directly to the cheapskate in me. I know that number by heart, but it's good they keep it front and center.
- **Location bar set to Alhambra, 91801** — pickup/delivery context is right there at the top. I don't have to hunt for whether delivery is available at my zip.
- **Wishlist tile ("Save your faves & drop a hint")** — actually useful for household managers like me who maintain running lists. That's a real behavior I use.
- **Clean layout** — no layout shifts, no broken images, tiles load coherently.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero copy** — this means nothing to me. I came here to restock paper towels and check cereal prices, not ponder my aspirational identity. This reads like a Walmart+ acquisition campaign aimed at people who haven't signed up, not a welcome mat for loyal shoppers.
- **No Rollback or price-cut signal above the fold** — Rollbacks are WHY I'm here. Not a single "was $X / now $Y" badge visible. That's the biggest missed opportunity for a value-hunter persona.
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — this is active real estate wasted on a feature I will never use. It's eating space above the hero and I'd dismiss it immediately.
- **"Brands for you" = three Iams sponsored ads** — I may or may not have a dog, but three identical sponsored pet-food tiles stacked in a row look like a feed glitch, not curation. This section feels like it's monetizing page space, not serving me.
- **Categories are obscured** — the pet and health tiles are cropped and unreadable. I can't tap a category I can't fully see.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Hey future" hero for a returning-member value-prop** — something like "Your weekly run starts here — Rollbacks in Grocery, Paper Goods & More" with a visual of a full cart or pantry. I'm already a member; speak to my weekly habit.
- **Add a Rollback strip just below the hero** — even 3-4 items with "was / now" pricing would stop me from scrolling past. That's the hook that makes me feel like I caught a deal.
- **Replace the "Ship to another country" dismissible banner with a free-delivery threshold reminder** — "Free delivery on orders $35+" is something I actually weigh every single week. That real estate is worth more than an international shipping pitch.
- **Fix the category tile crop** — either show full labels or reduce the number of tiles so each one is legible and tappable. Half-visible text is dead UI.
