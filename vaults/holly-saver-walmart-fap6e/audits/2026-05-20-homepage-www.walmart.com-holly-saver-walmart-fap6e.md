# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I've been on this app more times than I can count and this homepage is pushing a credit card at me when I just want to see what's on Rollback this week. Walmart is clearly in a "Walmart+ upsell" moment right now, and it's all over the hero. The "Brands for you" section looked promising for half a second — until I saw every tile is marked "Sponsored."

## What's working

- **Location/delivery bar** — "Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801" is the first thing below the logo and it's set to my address. That's exactly the feature I lean on every week and it's right where I expect it.
- **Walmart+ member earn tile** — I see "5%" and a card image immediately. Even if I'm not getting the card today, it reminds me my membership has value.
- **Tampax in "Brands for you"** — It's literally on my list this week. Not going to pretend that didn't catch my eye.
- **Clean open** — No hard modal, no forced popup, no cookie wall blocking the page. It just loaded.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero copy** — Means absolutely nothing to me. I opened the app to check chicken thighs and laundry detergent prices, not to contemplate my future.
- **Zero grocery or household deals above the fold** — My entire reason for being here is invisible. No Rollback strip, no produce photo, no "This week's savings" banner. Nothing.
- **Middle tiles are blurry and truncated** — "onn [?]", "Pet me...", "Hea Visi..." — I literally cannot read these. Feels like the page didn't finish loading.
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — My account is set to Alhambra, 91801. This banner is taking up space for a feature I will never use.
- **"Brands for you" is all paid ads** — Every single tile says "Sponsored \| Legal." That's not personalization, that's an ad unit. Don't call it "for you" if it's just whoever paid to be there.

## Recommendations

- **Show known Walmart+ members their actual week's savings in the hero** — "This week: members save $X on groceries" with a "View deals" button is more useful to me than "Hey future" and a credit card pitch.
- **Add a Rollback strip immediately below the hero** — A horizontal scroll of three to five Rollback-tagged grocery or household items would get me tapping instead of going straight to search.
- **Fix the category tile row** — Either fewer tiles with readable labels and icons, or make the tiles bigger. On mobile right now they're unreadable noise.
- **Drop the "Ship to another country" banner for US-registered accounts** — That's wasted prime real estate for most of the user base.
