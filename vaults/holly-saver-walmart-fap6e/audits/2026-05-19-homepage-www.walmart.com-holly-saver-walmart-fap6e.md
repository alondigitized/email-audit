# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened the app expecting to see what's on Rollback this week or a deal on paper towels, and instead I get "Hey future" and a pitch to sign up for Walmart+—which I already have. Below that are category tiles so clipped I can't even read what they say, and the "Brands for you" section is just three sponsored dog food ads. This looks like Walmart is talking to someone who's never shopped here before, not the person who places a Walmart+ delivery order every Tuesday.

## What's working

- **Location-aware delivery bar** — "Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801" is right up top. I don't have to type my zip every time. That's the one thing that says "we know you."
- **Walmart+ badge in the hero** — even if the messaging is acquisition-focused, the brand is at least leading with membership front and center, which is relevant to me.
- **Hamburger + search bar** — the search field is prominent. When nothing else on the page is useful, at least I can type "Bounty" and go straight there.
- **Dismissable notification banner** — the "Ship to another country" bar has a clear X. It's irrelevant to me but at least it doesn't trap me.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero copy** — I'm 45, I manage a household, I need toilet paper and chicken thighs. This headline reads like a college-enrollment ad. It does nothing for me.
- **Truncated category tiles** — "License...", "Pet me...", "Hea...", "Visi..." — I genuinely cannot tell what categories these are without tapping blind. This is a usability failure on mobile.
- **"Brands for you" = all sponsored ads** — three "Sponsored | Legal" cards in a row is not a browsing experience, it's a billboard wall. No Rollback items, no grocery picks, just paid placements.
- **Zero Rollback or price-drop visibility** — Rollbacks are Walmart's reason to exist for me. Not one appears above the fold.
- **No grocery/household entry point** — my primary shopping category isn't reachable in one tap from what I can see. The nav is behind a hamburger.
- **Cart shows $0.00** — I'm a repeat shopper. A "reorder your last haul" or "your list has 8 items" hook would be massively more useful than a new-member pitch.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Hey future" hero for a Rollback strip** — lead with "This week's Rollbacks: Paper towels, $0.27/sq ft" or whatever the real deal is. Existing members don't need a membership pitch; they need a reason to fill the cart now.
- **Fix the category tile labels on mobile** — either reduce the grid to 4 tiles with full readable labels, or drop the overlay text and use icon + single-word labels. "Pet," "Health," "Vision" — done. No truncation.
- **Surface a "Reorder" or "Your list" shortcut** — for weekly-repeat shoppers, a "My items" or "Last order" quick-tap above the fold is more valuable than any promotional hero.
- **Add a Memorial Day weekend deal banner** — it's May 19th. A "Stock up for the long weekend — Rollbacks on grilling + paper goods" strip would be timely and land for someone like me.
