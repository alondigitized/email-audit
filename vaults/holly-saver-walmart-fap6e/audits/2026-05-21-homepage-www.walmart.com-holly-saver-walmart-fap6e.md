# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Walmart knows I'm here to stock the pantry and grab a deal — "Tons of pantry faves" is the first thing I see in the top-left tile, which is exactly why I opened this app. That said, the page greets me with two competing interruptions right out of the gate: a banner pushing international shipping I will never use, and a full-width modal asking me to download the app on the phone I'm already browsing on. Once I mentally clear both of those, the actual homepage is a tight tile grid pushing groceries, Skechers, outdoor toys, and a 50%-off flash — all feeling like a busy Saturday-morning aisle in the best way.

## What's working

- **"Tons of pantry faves" tile** — that yellow tile is the first thing my eye lands on and it's exactly why I'm here. One tap and I'm in grocery.
- **Location set at the top** — "Alhambra, 91801" with a clear pickup-or-delivery toggle tells me Walmart already knows my store. That saves me a step I hate on every other retailer's site.
- **"Comfy shoes from Skechers" with actual product photos** — three real pairs of shoes, no model, no fluff. I can see the colorways immediately. This works for me even if shoes weren't my plan today.
- **"Up to 50% off" outdoor toy tile** — May means bounce houses and sprinklers for the backyard. That tile catches my eye as a second-basket item.
- **App store rating visible in modal** — even though the modal is friction, the 4.5-star rating is a real trust signal if I'm on the fence about downloading.

## What's weak

- **"Ship to another country" banner** — I shop Walmart because everything ships domestically fast. This notification is dead noise for me and eats real estate at the top of my screen.
- **App download modal obscures the bottom of the page** — I'm already on my phone browsing the site. Blocking page content with a "Get the app" overlay before I've even seen what's below the fold is the kind of thing that makes me tap X and feel slightly annoyed.
- **Zero Walmart+ / membership visibility** — I pay for Walmart+ specifically for delivery and rollbacks. Not seeing any member-price badge, free-delivery callout, or rewards balance is a miss. That's the hook that keeps me loyal and it's nowhere in this viewport.
- **Pantry faves tile has no price signal** — I track unit prices in my head. A tile that says "pantry faves" with zero price context ("prices as low as $1.98") doesn't give me a reason to tap urgently.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "Ship to another country" banner with a Walmart+ benefit strip** — swap it for "Free delivery on this order — Walmart+ member" with a green checkmark. I'm already a member; remind me why.
- **Add a unit-price callout to the pantry faves tile** — a single line like "Stock up from $0.98" converts a browsing mindset into an add-to-cart mindset immediately.
- **Delay the app download modal until after 30 seconds on page** — let me see the deals first. Interrupting before I've evaluated anything is the fastest way to earn a tap-X instead of a tap-download.
- **Give the right-column Skechers tile a price anchor** — "Starting at $39" or a Rollback badge would turn a lifestyle tile into a conversion tile for the shoe shopper in me.
