# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I've been shopping Walmart long enough to know their homepage by heart, and today it looks like they're pitching me on pet toys and sideline chairs — fine, whatever, it's spring. What I notice right away is that app install banner eating up the bottom third of my screen before I've even had a chance to look around. I'm a Walmart+ member, I spend real money here every week, and the first thing they show me is a prompt to download an app I already have. That's the kind of thing that tells me the homepage team doesn't know who their regulars are.

## What's working

- **"Up to 50% off" tile** — The yellow background makes it pop and the discount is the kind of number that stops me from scrolling. That's a Rollback mindset, and it works.
- **"Chairs, coolers & more sideline gear"** — Good seasonal read for May. It's the kind of stuff I'm already starting to think about for the backyard and the kids' games.
- **"Big savings on pet toys & more"** — The corgi photo is genuinely charming and the word "savings" is doing exactly what it needs to do for someone like me who never pays full price.
- **Search bar front and center** — When I know what I want (and I usually do), I go straight to search. It's big, it's obvious, it works.
- **Location bar showing my zip** — "Alhambra, 91801" tells me pickup and delivery are already dialed in. That's one less tap.

## What's weak

- **App download modal blocking the page** — I'm already on the Walmart app or website. This banner ("The easiest way to shop, check out & track your orders") shoves itself over the bottom of the screen before I've had a single interaction. It's the exact opposite of knowing your customer.
- **No Walmart+ mention anywhere above the fold** — I pay for this membership. There should be a "Welcome back, member" hook, a reward balance, a members-only deal — something. This page could be for anyone.
- **No grocery or household essentials visible** — My weekly trip is paper towels, cereal, yogurt, cleaning supplies. None of that is represented in what I can see. Walmart's bread and butter is my pantry run, and it's not on the homepage.
- **"Up to 50% off" with no specificity** — What's 50% off? Clothing? Seasonal? I have to tap to find out, and without a category name I can't tell if it's worth the tap.
- **"Ship to another country" banner at the top** — This is not for me. It's prime real estate and it's being used for an edge-case feature with an X to close. That's wasted space on a returning shopper's screen.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Walmart+ member greeting with a current perk** — Even one line: "Member deal this week: 10¢/gal off fuel" or "Free delivery on your next grocery order" tells me my membership is doing something. Ship it as a personalized banner swap in the header row.
- **Add a grocery essentials tile above the fold** — Swap out the "Ship to another country" banner for a "Stock up & save on grocery staples" tile with one concrete unit-price example (like "Great Value milk: $3.48"). That's my trigger.
- **Kill or delay the app install modal for known Walmart+ users** — If I'm logged in and a Walmart+ member, suppress the app nag entirely. I'm already here; don't make me close a popup before I can shop.
- **Add a category label to the "Up to 50% off" tile** — "Up to 50% off kids' clothing" or "Up to 50% off seasonal decor" — just name the category so I know in two words whether to bother tapping.
