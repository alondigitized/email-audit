# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is my store — I'm on it every week, Walmart+ member, know the Rollback section better than the employees do. The homepage is doing that thing Walmart always does: throw a clearance label on everything and call it a day. Right now I'm seeing a gaming monitor, some fancy French skincare water, and ASICS sneakers as the lead items, which, fine, but that's not why I opened the app. The Walmart+ promo at the top is the one thing that actually makes sense for me.

## What's working

- **Walmart+ banner** — I'm already a member and it's right there. Even the trial language signals this is my platform, not some random storefront.
- **Location lock** — "Alhambra, 91801" and the "Pickup or delivery?" bar at the top tells me this page knows where I am. That's the one piece of personalization that actually matters for my weekly run.
- **Clearance badge** — Yellow pill, hard to miss. I trained myself to look for that color. It's doing its job.
- **Specific price callouts** — "Now $100.80" with a strikethrough on $139.99 is the exact format I trust. No percentage games, just the number.

## What's weak

- **"Discover Great Brands" leading with a gaming monitor** — I'm an exurban household manager. I don't need a 29.5" curved UWFHD display. That's wasted real estate for me on a grocery run.
- **"Ship to another country" dismissal banner** — This popup blade is taking up prime viewport space and has nothing to do with my shopping. I'd have already Xed it out before reading this far.
- **Zero grocery or household signals above the fold** — My actual reasons for being on this app — dish soap, paper towels, the cereal that was on Rollback last week — are invisible.
- **"$35 min. See T&C. 1 trial/mbr."** — The Walmart+ messaging is there but the fine print is weirdly prominent. It reads like a disclaimer, not a benefit pitch.
- **No search suggestions or categories visible** — For medium-tech-comfort me on mobile, I need nav shortcuts fast. Hamburger menu is there, but nothing pre-loaded for my typical categories.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the featured product row** for grocery/household clearance items or current Rollback staples — paper goods, pantry, cleaning. That's what a Walmart+ grocery subscriber actually came for.
- **Kill or auto-dismiss the "Ship to another country" blade** for returning logged-in members. I live in Alhambra, not abroad. It's just clutter.
- **Promote the Walmart+ free delivery threshold front-and-center** ("Free delivery on orders over $X — you're a member") instead of the trial-focused "$35 min. See T&C." messaging. I already pay for this; remind me why that's good.
- **Add one-tap category shortcuts** under the location bar: Groceries | Household | Electronics. Three buttons, mobile-thumb-sized, and I'm in the right aisle in one tap instead of digging through the hamburger.
