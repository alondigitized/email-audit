# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is classic Walmart mobile — functional, slightly chaotic, always pushing the Walmart+ card angle. Right now it's leading with some kind of free shipping or free delivery offer and a Walmart+ member rewards push, which is at least in my lane since I already pay for Walmart+. But the above-the-fold experience feels half-loaded and scattered — I can't tell what the "Free" hero is actually offering without squinting, and the bottom third of my screen is literally grey placeholders still loading in.

## What's working

- **Location-aware pickup/delivery strip** ("Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801") — this is the first thing I check. It means my order will actually route to my store, not some random warehouse.
- **Walmart+ member earn section** — the 5% back credit card card is right there, front-row. As someone who already has Walmart+, it catches my eye even if I'm not clicking it today.
- **"Free" hero** — whatever the full offer is (free shipping, free delivery trial?), the word "Free" in that big blue hero is the right word to lead with for me. I'm wired to stop scrolling when I see it.
- **Wishlist module ("Save your faves & drop a hint")** — I actually use this before big shopping runs to pre-load my list. Nice to have it visible.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy is cropped and unreadable** — "Free" is huge but the supporting text ("Almost for some" or whatever it says) is illegible. I have no idea what the actual offer is or what I need to do to get it. That's a fail.
- **Grey placeholder boxes at the bottom** — half the screen didn't load. On a slow connection or first visit this looks broken. I notice this stuff.
- **Category tiles are tiny and cut off** — "Pet me...", "Hea...", "Visi..." — I can barely read these. Grocery isn't even visible. That's my number one reason to be on Walmart.com and it's nowhere above the fold.
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — this is eating prime screen real estate. I'm in Alhambra, I want groceries delivered to my house, not international shipping options. This feels like a system notification misfired.
- **Walmart+ credit card push** — I get it, but leading the content row with a credit card signup pitch when I'm trying to do my weekly grocery shop is the wrong priority. I'm not here to open a card; I'm here to order dish soap.

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite and expand the hero so the full offer is readable** — "Free delivery every day with Walmart+" with a "Start Shopping" button. Right now "Free" alone with cropped subtext is clickbait, not a CTA.
- **Add a prominent Grocery / Household shortcut above the fold** — replace the "Ship to another country" banner with a "Shop Groceries" or "Reorder your last cart" chip. That's my primary use case and it's invisible.
- **Fix the lazy-load** — the grey placeholder boxes should render before the hero or at minimum show skeleton content, not blank rectangles. This looks broken on mobile.
- **Move the Walmart+ credit card push below the category grid** — let me get to my shopping first, then upsell me on the card. Sequence matters for household managers who are task-focused.
