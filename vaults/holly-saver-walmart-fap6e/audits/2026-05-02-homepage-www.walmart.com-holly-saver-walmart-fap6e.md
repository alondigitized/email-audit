# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the Walmart I know — blue and yellow, no fuss, straight to deals. It's pushing Mother's Day and Walmart+ membership right at the top, which makes sense for early May, and the Flash Deals section is doing the heavy lifting immediately below the fold. It feels functional and familiar, not fancy, which is fine by me.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **"Flash Deals — Up to 65% off"** lands immediately below the hero — that's where my eyes go first and it delivers. A weighted heating pad for $29.95 and a hair dryer marked down from $199 are real products at real savings, not fluff.
- **Location bar showing "Alhambra, 91801"** tells me the site already knows where I am, so "Get it Fast" actually means something for my zip code.
- **Category pill nav ("Rollbacks & More", "Mother's Day", "Get it Fast")** is compact and scannable on mobile — I can tap directly into what I need without hunting.
- **"Moms & members know best"** hero ties Walmart+ to the Mother's Day moment cleanly. I'm already a Walmart+ member so this feels like it's speaking to me, not selling me.
- **Struck-through original prices** (e.g., $199.99 → $49.39) give me the instant unit-price comparison I'm always doing mentally.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy is cut off** — "Moms & members know best" and then the banner just... ends. I can't see what the offer actually is without tapping into it. The carousel pause button is right there but I don't know what I'm pausing for.
- **"Spanish / Español now available!"** banner is eating prime real estate at the top. I get why it's there but it's not relevant to me and there's no quick way to dismiss it without it bouncing back next visit.
- **Product names are truncated** — "33"x17" XXXL Electric..." and "Qoosea Eter..." tell me nothing. On mobile I need a little more label so I can decide whether to tap.
- **No grocery or household callout above the fold.** That's literally why I open the app every week. The hero is gifting-focused and Flash Deals skews toward electronics/personal care. My staples feel invisible.
- **Cart shows $0.00** with no loyalty balance or Walmart+ badge — missed opportunity to remind me I have benefits loaded.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Weekly Groceries" or "Your Pickup Order" shortcut pill** alongside "Rollbacks & More" and "Mother's Day" — Walmart+ members doing weekly grocery pickup should land on their list, not a gifting hero.
- **Surface the Walmart+ benefit in the cart badge** or sticky header — show cash-back balance or free delivery status so members feel the value every session, not just during promotions.
- **Expand product card labels** to at least two lines on mobile — truncation kills confidence; I won't tap on something I can't identify.
- **Collapse or auto-dismiss the Spanish language banner after one session** — give it a persistent close so it doesn't re-appear and eat top-of-page space for users who've already seen it.
