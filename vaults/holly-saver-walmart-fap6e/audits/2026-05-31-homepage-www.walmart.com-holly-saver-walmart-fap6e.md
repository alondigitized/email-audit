# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is peak Walmart mobile — functional, a little cluttered, heavy on the "deals" framing. The first thing I see is a Walmart+ member pitch and a Flash Deals section loaded with a mattress, an electric bike, and a trampoline. That's not my Tuesday grocery run, but the bones are familiar and I know exactly where to go from here.

## What's working

- **Walmart+ "Drive more, spend less" banner** — as a member, that message lands. It confirms my membership has value even if I'm not seeing what that value is right now.
- **"Flash Deals — Up to 65% off" heading** — clear, scannable, big savings signal I register immediately. I know to look here first.
- **Location set to Alhambra, 91801** — the store knows where I am, so pickup and delivery are already scoped. One less tap.
- **Specific before/after pricing** ("Now $9.99 / was $49.00") — I track unit prices in my head. I like seeing actual numbers, not percentages alone.

## What's weak

- **"Ship to another country" notification banner** — absolute noise for a domestic grocery run. It's covering precious above-fold real estate and I'm dismissing it on instinct.
- **Flash Deals product mix** — electric bike, full-size mattress, trampoline. None of that is on my list this week. The algorithm is not doing its job.
- **No grocery or household staples hook** — I'm here for Rollback on paper towels and the weekly shop. Walmart's actual superpower for me is invisible above the fold.
- **Cart shows $0.00** — I get this is a cold open, but no "pick up where you left off" nudge means I'm starting from scratch mentally.
- **Walmart+ carousel has a pause button but no CTA copy visible** — what do I do? Sign up? Check benefits? It's vague.

## Recommendations

- **Swap Flash Deals product mix for grocery + household staples** — show me Rollback on Tide, Great Value milk, or paper goods. That's my reason to be here every week, and it's not on screen.
- **Add a "Your Rollbacks" or personalized deal strip** above Flash Deals — surface the items I actually buy on Rollback. Even a "Based on your past orders" rail would keep me from bouncing to the search bar.
- **Replace the "Ship to another country" banner with a free-shipping or pickup-threshold reminder** — something like "Free pickup ready in 2 hours" is relevant. International shipping is not.
- **Add a visible Walmart+ member benefit callout** — instead of an acquisition pitch, tell me what I'm getting today: free delivery, fuel savings, early access. Make the membership feel active, not just a sign-up ask.
