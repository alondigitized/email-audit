# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I open Walmart and the first thing I see is a Croc and a baby high chair — okay, fine, it's Walmart, they sell everything. But I'm a Walmart+ member doing a weekly grocery and household run and there's zero acknowledgment of that. The app download pop-up is taking up half my screen when I almost certainly already have the app. Right now Walmart looks like it's pushing fashion and baby gear, not the value-packed weekly staples I come here for.

## What's working

- **"Up to 45% off" tile with the high chair** — even if I don't need a high chair, seeing a percentage deal above the fold gets my deal-hunter brain engaged
- **"Save on fashion / Shop now" tile** — short, direct copy, no fluff; matches my reading style
- **Location bar ("Pickup or delivery? · Alhambra, 91801")** — I actually appreciate this being pinned at the top; confirms my pickup store is set before I start my list
- **Clean layout underneath the banners** — the tile grid itself is uncluttered once you mentally dismiss the overlapping pop-ups

## What's weak

- **App download overlay** — I'm a weekly Walmart+ shopper; I have the app. This overlay is burning my screen real estate and making me tap X before I can even start shopping
- **"New from Free Assembly" hero tile** — that model looks 25 and is styled like a Gen Z influencer. I don't see myself, and I don't shop Free Assembly on a grocery run
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — irrelevant to me, and it's prime top-of-screen space wasted on a feature I'll never use
- **Zero Walmart+ acknowledgment** — I pay for that membership every year; there's no balance, no member-only deal badge, nothing to make me feel recognized
- **No grocery or household essentials visible** — my primary shopping category is completely absent above the fold
- **No free-shipping or Walmart+ delivery threshold called out** — that's one of the main reasons I'm here

## Recommendations

- **Replace the app-download overlay with a Walmart+ member welcome strip** — show my membership status, a personalized deal, or my free delivery threshold for today's order; that earns my loyalty in 5 seconds
- **Swap the "New from Free Assembly" lifestyle shot for a household essentials deal tile** — cleaning supplies, pantry staples, or a Rollback on paper towels would stop my scroll instantly
- **Kill the "Ship to another country" announcement banner** — give that space to a Rollback callout or a time-sensitive weekly deal
- **Add a "Your Weekly Picks" or "Reorder Favorites" module above the fold** — even a single-row rail of my last five purchases would turn this from a browse session into a reorder session in under 30 seconds
