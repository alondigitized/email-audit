# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is classic Walmart — functional, familiar, and laser-focused on deals. The Flash Deals section with clearance-tagged pricing loads immediately below the fold, which is exactly what I come here for. But the hero is basically a big blue "Hey" with a Walmart+ logo cycling through a carousel — not telling me anything useful about what's on sale this week.

## What's working

- **Pickup or delivery? bar** — my location is already saved and it's the second thing I see. That's the single most important feature for how I shop and it's front-and-center.
- **Flash Deals module** — "Up to 65% off" with visible clearance-tagged prices ($198.49 was $249.99) gives me real numbers right away. I don't have to dig.
- **Clearance tag badge** — yellow "Clearance" label on the bike is the kind of visual shorthand I've trained my eye to look for in the Rollback aisle. Same muscle memory works here.
- **Struck-through original prices** — I can do the math in my head instantly. That's how I shop.
- **Search bar prominence** — big, centered, labeled "Search Walmart." One tap and I'm hunting for paper towels.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy "Hey"** — this tells me absolutely nothing. A big blue screen with "Hey" and a Walmart+ logo is wasted above-the-fold real estate. Where's this week's grocery deal or the Rollback I should know about?
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — I live in Alhambra. This is noise I have to manually X out. Who is this for?
- **Featured Flash Deals are not my category** — a kids' bike, a solar animal repellent, and a kayak paddle in the first three slots. I buy groceries, cleaning supplies, and household staples. None of that is showing.
- **No star ratings on product cards** — I need to see reviews before I tap. The cards show price and an Options button but nothing about whether other people actually bought and liked these items.
- **No free-shipping threshold or Walmart+ delivery perk callout** — I'm a Walmart+ member. The hero could be reminding me that my groceries deliver free. Instead it just says "Hey."

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero from "Hey" to a specific weekly offer** — "This week: Rollbacks on pantry staples" with a real product image and a Shop Now button would do more work than an animated Walmart+ logo.
- **Personalize the Flash Deals rail to purchase history** — I've ordered dish soap, laundry detergent, and paper towels dozens of times. Surface those categories first, not kayak paddles.
- **Add star ratings to Flash Deal product cards** — even a small "4.3 ★ (1,204)" line under the price would give me the confidence signal I need to tap.
- **Kill the "Ship to another country" banner for domestic accounts** — or at minimum don't default it open. It reads as clutter and forces an unnecessary tap.
