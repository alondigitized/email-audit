# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I recognized this as home the second I saw the Walmart spark, and the "Moms & members know best" banner made me feel seen for half a second — then I looked at what's actually being pushed below it and saw a $183 floor mirror and a $145 K18 hair treatment. That is not my grocery run. Walmart is clearly running a Mother's Day push right now, and the member messaging is right, but the product mix on this screen is way off for someone like me who opened the app to restock the pantry.

## What's working

- **"Moms & members know best" hero** — that phrasing is me. I'm a Walmart+ member and a mom running a household; this is one of the rare times a retailer says something that doesn't feel generic.
- **Location bar ("Pickup or delivery? — Alhambra, 91801")** — I know immediately that the app knows where I am and can route my order to in-store pickup. That matters every week.
- **Search bar is front and center** — at medium tech comfort, I always search first, and Walmart puts it exactly where I expect it.
- **"Discover Great Brands" + "Shop all" rail** — even if the specific products aren't for me today, the format is familiar and scannable.

## What's weak

- **Product mix is completely off for my trip** — a $183 floor mirror, $145 K18 hair serum, and what looks like a $105 nightstand are not pantry staples. If Walmart knows I'm a Walmart+ grocery subscriber, this grid should be showing Rollback on paper towels, not luxury hair care.
- **Hero CTA is clipped** — the carousel bottom is cut off and I can't read the actual button. I don't know what tapping the hero would do.
- **No Rollback badges anywhere in view** — Rollback is the single biggest reason I choose Walmart over Target. Not one is visible.
- **"Now available! Ship to another country" banner** — irrelevant to me and takes up valuable real estate above the hero. I'd close it but it's annoying that it's there at all.
- **No grocery or household entry point above the fold** — not a single category chip or shortcut to "Grocery," "Cleaning," or "Household Essentials" is visible without scrolling or tapping the hamburger.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the product grid to purchase history** — if I'm a Walmart+ grocery subscriber, show me Rollback on items I buy weekly (laundry detergent, chicken broth, trash bags), not editorial brand picks aimed at a different shopper.
- **Add a Rollback or member-savings badge** to at least one featured product on this screen — even one "Was $X, Now $Y" signal would activate my "never pays full price" reflex immediately.
- **Make the hero CTA readable** — either reduce the carousel height or pull the CTA button fully into the viewport so I know what I'm being asked to tap.
- **Swap the "ship to another country" notification for a Walmart+ benefit reminder** — "Free delivery on your next grocery order" would land for me and reinforce why I pay for the membership.
