# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- I opened Walmart.com on my phone and immediately got slapped with a "Robot or human?" CAPTCHA modal asking me to press and hold a button to prove I'm not a bot. As a Walmart+ member who shops this site every single week, that is a genuinely insulting way to greet a loyal customer. Behind the modal I can just barely make out a "Discover Great Brands" section with some shoe and apparel thumbnails, but I can't interact with anything until I clear this hurdle.

## What's working

- **Location strip ("Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801")** — This is the single most Walmart+ thing on the screen. It's telling me my delivery zone is already set, which matters when I'm deciding between pickup and grocery delivery. I appreciate that it's right up top.
- **Search bar is front and center** — I often arrive knowing exactly what I want (specific brand of detergent, exact paper towel pack count). The prominent search bar at least lets me bypass the blocked homepage if I can get past the modal.
- **Cart icon with price** — The $0.00 cart badge is standard but fine. Lets me know I'm signed in (or at least recognized) at a glance.

## What's weak

- **CAPTCHA blocking the entire homepage** — "Robot or human? Activate and hold the button to confirm that you're human." I shop here every week. I have a Walmart+ subscription. This is a terrible first impression for a logged-in or returning customer.
- **"Now available! Ship to another country" banner** — This is irrelevant noise for an exurban household manager doing her weekly grocery and household run. It's taking up real estate above the fold for zero reason from my perspective.
- **Zero above-the-fold offer visibility** — I can see "Discover Great Brands" and what looks like sneakers and a dress behind the modal, but no price, no rollback badge, no savings hook. Nothing is pulling me in.
- **No Walmart+ recognition** — The cart shows $0.00 and there's no member greeting, no rewards balance, no "Welcome back" — even though I should be a known customer.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the CAPTCHA for known/authenticated Walmart+ members** — If I'm logged in with a Walmart+ account and have a purchase history, there is no reason to gate me behind a bot-detection press-and-hold. Fix the session recognition.
- **Replace the "Ship to another country" banner with a Rollback or weekly-deals strip** — That banner means nothing to 95% of weekly grocery shoppers in Alhambra. Swap it for the actual deal du jour (e.g., "This week's Rollbacks — save on pantry staples").
- **Surface a Walmart+ member benefit above the fold** — Even a single line like "Walmart+ member: free delivery on orders over $35" reminds me why I pay for the subscription and nudges me to hit the threshold.
- **Show one concrete savings hook in the hero** — A specific dollar or percent amount ("Save up to 40% on household essentials") gives me a reason to scroll rather than just dropping me into a blocked screen.
