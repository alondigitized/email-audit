# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Walmart doing what Walmart does — a busy, tile-heavy homepage pushing a lot of things at once. Right now it feels like it's leading with a Walmart+ acquisition pitch ("Hey future") more than a shopping-trip homepage for someone who already has the membership. The sponsored "Brands for you" section and half-loaded content at the bottom make this feel less like a polished welcome mat and more like a work in progress.

## What's working

- **Walmart+ hero carousel** — I see my membership product right away. Even if the copy is vague, it validates my loyalty and hints at benefits.
- **Location + pickup/delivery bar** — "Alhambra, 91801" and the pickup/delivery toggle are right there on load. That matters a lot when I'm placing a grocery order.
- **Transparent sponsored labels** — Every "Brands for you" tile has "Sponsored | Legal" underneath. No pretending those are organic recommendations.
- **Search bar prominence** — It's front and center. I know what I need, I'm going straight there. That's my move.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero copy** — That's not talking to me. I'm 45, I'm restocking dish soap and checking Rollback prices. "Hey future" sounds like a college kid ad.
- **Cut-off tile text** — "Save up…", "License…", "Pet me…", "Hea Visi…" — all truncated. I can't tell what half these tiles are without tapping, and I'm not going to tap blind.
- **No grocery or household entry point above the fold** — My two main categories aren't visually represented anywhere I can see before scrolling.
- **Loading placeholders at the bottom** — Those gray bars mean the page wasn't done loading when this rendered. That's friction I don't need when I'm pulling up the app in the pickup line.
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — I live in Alhambra and I do grocery pickup. I don't need this and it's burning real estate.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Hey future" hero with an active-member savings hook** — Something like "Your Walmart+ week: free delivery + member prices on groceries" would speak directly to why I pay for this membership.
- **Show at least one grocery or household tile above the fold** — Even a single "Restock your pantry" tile would catch my eye immediately.
- **Surface a concrete Rollback or member deal with a number** — "Save $0.50/lb on ground beef today" or "Members: $2 off your next grocery order" is what actually stops the scroll.
- **Kill the "Ship to another country" banner by default** — Serve it contextually, not as the first thing a suburban grocery shopper sees at the top of the page.
