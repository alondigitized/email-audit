# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Walmart's pushing Walmart+ hard right now — the whole top half of the screen is basically a pitch for the membership and its perks. It feels like a store that knows I'm already a regular but is still trying to upsell me on something instead of just getting me to my list. Functional, recognizable, but nowhere near as focused on getting me to checkout as I'd expect from the app I open every single week.

## What's working

- **Walmart+ member earn tile with 5% cashback** — I'm already a Walmart+ subscriber for the grocery delivery. Seeing that 5% earn reinforced right away feels good. That's money back on my weekly run.
- **Location bar ("Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801")** — It knows where I am and is asking the right first question. That's the most relevant toggle for how I actually shop.
- **Search bar** — It's big, prominent, and dead center. When I know what I need, I go straight there. It's not buried.
- **"Save your faves & drop a hint Wishlist" tile** — Genuinely useful for a household manager. I can flag things for the family without hunting later.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero** — Vague, aspirational, completely disconnected from why I'm here. I opened this app to reorder dish soap and check if coffee is on Rollback. "Hey future" means nothing to me.
- **No grocery or household tile visible above the fold** — Pet, Health/Vision, bikes — but no produce, no pantry staples, nothing that maps to my actual weekly run. That's a miss for Walmart's biggest use case.
- **"Now available! Ship to another country" banner** — I'm in Alhambra grabbing groceries. This banner is noise and it's eating real estate above the hero.
- **"Brands for you" sponsored tiles** — Three tiles, all labeled "Sponsored," all partially cropped so I can't read the brand names. Feels like filler, not curation.
- **Gray loading bars at the bottom** — The page wasn't done rendering when the screenshot was taken. On my phone on the Walmart app, a slow/choppy load makes me just hit the search bar and ignore everything else.
- **No Rollback callouts anywhere** — Rollback is the reason I check Walmart every week. Not one banner, not one badge.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Hey future" with a grocery-forward hero** — Something like "Your weekly run, with Rollback deals" or "Walmart+ members save more on every cart." Tie the hero directly to the delivery/pickup use case shown in the location bar.
- **Add a Rollback or "Deals this week" horizontal rail** — One swipeable strip of actual Rollback products (paper towels, pasta, chicken) would get my thumb moving immediately.
- **Kill the "Ship to another country" banner** — Or make it conditional: don't show it to users whose stored location is a domestic address. It's irrelevant and takes up the first line of real estate.
- **Surface grocery/pantry/household as tappable category chips** — Even three chips (Grocery, Household, Personal Care) below the location bar would cut my time-to-category in half.
