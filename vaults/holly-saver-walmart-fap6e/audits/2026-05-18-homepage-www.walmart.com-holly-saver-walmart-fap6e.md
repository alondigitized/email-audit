# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Walmart leaning hard into Walmart+ membership messaging right now — "Hey future" as the hero copy feels like a subscription push, not a grocery run helper. As someone who already has Walmart+, I land on this and instantly feel like it's talking to someone who isn't signed up yet, not a loyal member stocking up for the week. The page is also clearly mid-load — bottom half is just gray boxes — which isn't a great first impression on mobile.

## What's working

- **"Pickup or delivery?" strip with saved location** — Alhambra, 91801 is already set. That's exactly the kind of friction-free start I want when I'm building a grocery list on my phone.
- **Walmart+ in the hero** — I clock it in half a second. Even if the "Hey future" copy is vague, the Walmart+ spark logo tells me my membership is recognized.
- **Telehealth card** — "Telehealth, your way" is genuinely interesting to me as a 45-year-old. That's a benefit I might not know Walmart offers, and it's above the fold.
- **Wishlist card ("Save your faves & drop a hint")** — This is a solid practical feature for a household manager who tracks what everyone in the house needs. I appreciate seeing it surfaced.
- **Dismissible "Ship to another country" banner** — It has an X. It's not blocking the whole screen. Fine.

## What's weak

- **"Hey future" hero copy** — This tells me nothing. What about my future? A deal? A new feature? It reads like a brand campaign talking to a 22-year-old, not a household manager doing her Sunday shop.
- **No grocery or household anchor above the fold** — My primary reason to open Walmart.com is food, paper towels, cleaning supplies. None of that is visible. The bike and telehealth cards are fine, but they're not why I'm here.
- **Gray placeholder blocks at the bottom** — The page is clearly still loading content. On a Sunday grocery-list session, I do not want to watch a page load itself.
- **No visible Rollback or current deal** — Walmart's whole identity to me is price savings. I see zero price copy above the fold. Not one $/% number.
- **Cart shows $0.00** — This is probably my cart, but it reads as a brand-new-visitor page, not a "welcome back, Holly" page. No personalization signal at all.
- **"Save up..." card text is cut off** — The bicycle promo is clipped. I can't tell what the deal is without tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with the weekly ad or a Rollback strip directly under the nav.** I know Walmart's prices are good — remind me with a real number: "Rollbacks this week — ground beef $3.47/lb." One line of actual pricing would do more for engagement than "Hey future."
- **Swap the hero for a Walmart+ member welcome state.** Since I'm already logged in (location is set), show me something member-specific: my last order, my Walmart Cash balance, or a "Members save $X this week" teaser. I'm already in the club — sell me on staying, not on joining.
- **Move the grocery/household category tiles above the lifestyle cards.** The bike and telehealth tiles are fine deeper in the page, but my first tap should be able to get me to Produce, Pantry, or Household Essentials without opening the hamburger.
- **Fix the lazy-load on mobile.** The gray placeholder grid at the bottom should have skeleton UI or load faster. On a 4G connection, landing on half a page hurts trust.
