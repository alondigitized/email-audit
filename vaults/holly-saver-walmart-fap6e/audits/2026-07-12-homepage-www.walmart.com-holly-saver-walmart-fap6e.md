# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I opened Walmart.com expecting to see my weekly deals front and center — rollbacks on paper towels, maybe a grocery pickup banner — and instead I'm staring at a candle, a tub of protein powder, and what looks like a hair tool. The "Discover Great Brands" section feels like Walmart trying to compete with Ulta and GNC at the same time. It's a fine store but this homepage is not talking to me on a Saturday morning with a cart to fill.

## What's working

- **"Pickup or delivery?" bar with my zip (Alhambra, 91801)** — that's actually my favorite feature of Walmart's app; it knows where I am and what fulfillment mode I'm in without me asking
- **"Add" button is right on the product card** — I don't have to go to a detail page just to toss something in; one-tap add is exactly how I shop when I'm moving fast
- **"Reduced price" badge on the Ascent protein** — the strikethrough from $48.99 to "Now $39.19" is clear and honest; I can read that math in half a second
- **Search bar front and center** — when this page isn't showing me what I need, the search field is right there, prominent, and I'll use it

## What's weak

- **"Discover Great Brands" as the hero section** — I'm not here to discover brands; I'm here to refill Tide and grab bananas. This feels like it's aimed at a first-time Walmart shopper, not a Walmart+ member who logs in every week
- **No Walmart+ hook anywhere visible** — I pay $12.95/month and I can't see my membership reflected on this page at all. No "Free delivery with Walmart+" badge, no member savings callout, nothing
- **"Ship to another country" notification banner** — dismissible, fine, but it's eating real estate above a section I don't care about. I want rollbacks, not international shipping promos
- **Funboy + Bose banner** — inflatable pool floats and headphones on July 12 feel a bit random. Not bad, but it's not speaking to a grocery/household run at all
- **Candle and hair product as featured items** — Allswell and Amika are fine products but they're not why I opened Walmart. If the algorithm personalized these for me based on my history, I can't tell — there's no signal that this is curated for me vs. anyone

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Discover Great Brands" for a Walmart+ member welcome rail** — show my rewards balance, a "continue shopping" row with my last few items, or at minimum a "Walmart+ benefit: free delivery on your next order" badge so I feel recognized the second I land
- **Put a grocery / household essentials shortcut strip directly under the location bar** — a single horizontal scroll of icons (Grocery, Household, Personal Care, Rollbacks) would let me reach my category in one tap instead of hunting the hamburger menu
- **Replace the international shipping banner with a rollback countdown** — a "This week's Rollbacks — ends Sunday" strip with 3-4 household staples would actually convert me; the country-shipping message sends me to the X button immediately
- **Show savings context on product cards** — "You saved $9.80" rather than just the new price; I track unit prices mentally and I want confirmation I'm getting the deal
