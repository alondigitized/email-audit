# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Walmart is pushing Walmart+ membership front and center right now with a bold "Hey future" campaign in the hero, which — fine, I'm already a member, so what's in it for me today? The category tiles below it are a cluttered, half-cut-off grid that makes me squint to read them, and the "Brands for you" section is nothing but La Roche-Posay sponsored ads. For a weekly grocery-and-household run, this homepage is making me do all the work.

## What's working

- **Location/delivery bar** — "Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801" is exactly the first thing I need to confirm before I do anything. That's in the right place.
- **Walmart+ hero visibility** — As a member, seeing the Walmart+ spark in the hero at least tells me my membership is recognized on this visit.
- **Telehealth tile** — The "Telehealth, your way" card is actually interesting for a 45-year-old managing a household; it's a service I don't associate with Walmart but could use.
- **Wishlist tile** — "Save your faves & drop a hint Wishlist" is a real feature I'd use before a birthday or holiday run.

## What's weak

- **No groceries anywhere** — I'm here for my weekly grocery and household run. Produce, pantry staples, paper goods — zero trace of them above the fold. This is Walmart's core business and my core reason for visiting.
- **Cut-off category tiles** — "onn. [blurry]", "Hea Visi...", "License..." — I can't read what I'm supposed to tap. That's just broken on mobile.
- **"Brands for you" = sponsored dump** — Three La Roche-Posay ads labeled "Sponsored" presented as personalization. I see through this immediately and it erodes trust.
- **"Hey future" hero copy** — This means nothing to me. What am I getting today? What's the deal? Give me a Rollback number or a free-delivery pitch, not a tagline.
- **"Ship to another country" banner** — This is taking up screen real estate for a feature I will never use. Why is this the top notification I'm seeing?
- **No prices, no Rollbacks, no deals** — The entire above-the-fold has zero pricing signals. Walmart built its brand on price; this homepage hides it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "Hey future" hero with a seasonal grocery/household offer** — "Stock up for summer. Rollbacks on 500+ pantry items" with a specific product image would speak directly to my weekly run.
- **Pin a grocery category shortcut in the first row of tiles** — Move Groceries (with a produce or staples image) to position one in the category grid so I can tap it in under two seconds.
- **Drop the "Ship to another country" banner** — Replace with a Walmart+ member benefit callout ("Free same-day delivery on your next order") that's actually relevant to the members who see this.
- **Replace the sponsored "Brands for you" section with a Rollback rail** — Show me five items with crossed-out original prices. That's what I came for, and it's honest about Walmart's value prop.
