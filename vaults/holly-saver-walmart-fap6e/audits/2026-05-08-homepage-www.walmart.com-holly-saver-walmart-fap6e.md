# Homepage snapshot · www.walmart.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Walmart's homepage is pushing summer fun — road trips and trampolines — when what keeps me coming back week after week is Rollback prices on paper towels and same-day grocery delivery. The first thing I actually see when I open this is a full-screen prompt to download the app, which is annoying because I'm clearly already on my phone browsing the site. No Walmart+ badge, no weekly savings strip, no grocery content at all above the fold.

## What's working

- **Location/pickup toggle ("Pickup or delivery? Alhambra, 91801")** — this is exactly how I use Walmart, and it's correctly set to my area right at the top. That alone tells me this store knows me.
- **Search bar front and center** — I shop Walmart mobile search-first anyway; this placement is right.
- **"Up to 45% off" tile** — I see a discount number and my brain immediately wants to know what's in it. That's the right instinct for someone like me who tracks unit prices.
- **"Everything for summer road trips" tile** — it's seasonally on point and the open car hatch image is practical, not aspirational fluff.

## What's weak

- **App download modal dominates the viewport** — I am on my phone, on Walmart.com, and the first thing I have to do is dismiss a "get the app" overlay. If I'm a Walmart+ member I almost certainly already have it. This is friction on top of friction.
- **"Now available! Ship to another country" banner** — completely irrelevant to me. I'm buying Bounty and ground beef in Alhambra, not shipping internationally. This wastes the best banner slot on the page.
- **No Walmart+ signal anywhere** — I pay for this membership for the free delivery and fuel savings, and there's zero acknowledgment of it. No "Member prices" badge, no rewards balance, nothing. That's a missed retention moment every single visit.
- **Grocery and household essentials are absent** — my entire shopping mission — weekly grocery + household run — is invisible above the fold. Trampolines are fine but that is not why I open this app every Thursday.
- **No urgency on the 45% off offer** — no end date, no countdown, no "today only." The discount teaser is there but there's nothing pushing me to act now.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the app modal for returning users / known mobile sessions** — or at minimum move it to a dismissible bottom sheet, not a full-screen takeover. Prioritize showing me the store.
- **Swap the "ship to another country" banner for a Walmart+ member strip** — even something simple like "Welcome back, Holly — free delivery on groceries this week" would land hard for a loyalty subscriber.
- **Add a Rollback or Weekly Essentials tile above the fold** — one grocery-adjacent tile (pantry staples, cleaning products, a full-cart lifestyle image) signals immediately that this is my store for my actual list.
- **Put a deadline on the 45% off offer** — "Ends Sunday" costs nothing to add and gives me a reason to tap now instead of closing the tab.
