# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- I opened saksfifthavenue.com on my phone and instead of a homepage, I'm staring at a "WE'RE SORRY" wall. The page has detected "unusual activity" and is blocking the request entirely — no hero, no product, no navigation, nothing shoppable. Whatever Saks is right now, it is not accessible to me in this moment, and that is the whole story.

## What's working

- The "WE'RE SORRY" typographic treatment is at least on-brand aesthetically — serif, clean, unfussy. If this were a billboard for a very sad luxury brand, it would work.
- The background image bleed (what appears to be a street / city scene) suggests the real homepage might have lifestyle imagery underneath — I can just barely see it ghosted behind the modal.

## What's weak

- **The block page itself** — I cannot see any product, any navigation, any offer, any loyalty hook. I'm a SaksFirst Diamond member opening the app and I get a bot-detection wall. That is a catastrophic first impression.
- **The copy is accusatory** — "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" and "Automated (bot) activity" makes me, a real customer, feel like a suspect, not a VIP.
- **No fallback CTA** — there is no "Try again," no "Call our stylist," no "Shop in-app instead" escape hatch. The page is a dead end.
- **IP listed publicly** — showing my IP (75.140.9.0) in the error message is a weird, technical overshare that belongs in a server log, not a luxury brand UX.
- **Zero persona relevance** — nothing about Pre-Fall drops, designer RTW, handbags, or anything I care about is visible.

## Recommendations

- **Add an authenticated bypass for loyalty members** — SaksFirst Diamond cardholders should be fast-tracked past bot-detection or offered an instant app-redirect before hitting this wall. I should never see this page.
- **Rewrite the error copy in brand voice** — swap the clinical "unusual activity" language for something warm: "Something's off on our end — try refreshing or open the Saks app to keep shopping."
- **Add a visible escape CTA** — a "Try again" button or "Open in the Saks app" deep link keeps me in the funnel instead of bouncing to a competitor.
- **Suppress the IP address from the customer-facing copy** — that belongs in the developer console, not the luxe experience layer.
