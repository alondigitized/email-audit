# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get the homepage — I got a bot wall. Saks flagged my visit as suspicious activity and served a full-screen "WE'RE SORRY" interstitial instead of letting me shop. For a brand that prides itself on white-glove service, this is a jarring first impression. Whatever I was supposed to see — Pre-Fall drops, my SaksFirst rewards, the new Saint Laurent arrivals — I saw none of it.

## What's working

- The typography on the error card is clean and on-brand (serif, elegant spacing) — Saks at least styled their wall.
- The message is polite rather than aggressive ("We're Sorry" vs. "Access Denied"), which is a small grace note.

## What's weak

- **Complete content failure** — as a SaksFirst Diamond member opening the site on my phone, I'm looking at a bot detection page. Zero product, zero offers, zero loyalty recognition.
- **No fallback path** — the interstitial gives me a list of reasons why I might be blocked (bot activity, IP 75.140.9.0, developer tools) but offers no "tap here to continue" or CAPTCHA. I'm just... stuck.
- **IP flagging displayed to the user** — showing `IP 75.140.9.0` to a consumer is unnecessary and mildly unnerving. I don't know what that means, and it makes me feel surveilled.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment** — if I were signed in, this is the moment my Diamond status should be doing some work. Instead, the system treats me like a threat.
- **JavaScript disabled as a suggested cause** — I'm on a modern iPhone with Safari. This message makes Saks feel technically broken, not me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" tap-through** so legitimate customers aren't hard-blocked. A challenge is better than a dead end.
- **Remove the IP address from the consumer-facing error copy** — it's technical noise that erodes trust without helping the user.
- **Implement a signed-in user bypass** — SaksFirst Diamond members who are authenticated should never hit a bot wall without at least a soft challenge first.
- **Add a direct link to the app** ("Shop on the Saks App →") so mobile users who are blocked on web have an immediate off-ramp that converts instead of bounces.
