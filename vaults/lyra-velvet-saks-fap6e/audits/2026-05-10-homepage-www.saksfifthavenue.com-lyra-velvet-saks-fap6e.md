- The error page at least renders legibly — text is readable, not broken layout-on-layout

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is inaccessible** — I'm a real, high-value customer and I got blocked; this is the worst possible first impression
- **"WE'RE SORRY" in oversized serif** reads condescending, not apologetic — the tone is off for any luxury context
- **No bypass or recovery path** — there's no CAPTCHA, no "tap to continue," no customer service link, nothing for me to do except leave
- **No brand identity visible** — Saks logo, nav, or any luxury cue is completely absent; this could be any generic error page
- **IP flagging is exposed in plain text** — seeing `IP 47.41.15.19` on a consumer-facing page feels technical and cold, not premium

## Recommendations

- **Add a branded CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" tap-through** — let legitimate users self-identify and continue in one step, rather than dead-ending them
- **Wrap the error in the full Saks chrome** — header, logo, and a soft "We'll have you shopping in a moment" message maintains the brand register even during friction
- **Replace the IP-exposure bullet** — show "We detected unusual activity from your device" without exposing raw IP; it reads technical and punitive in a luxury context
- **Wire a direct CS escape hatch** — a "Chat with a stylist" or "Call SaksFirst concierge" link turns a block into a brand-service moment instead of a bounce
