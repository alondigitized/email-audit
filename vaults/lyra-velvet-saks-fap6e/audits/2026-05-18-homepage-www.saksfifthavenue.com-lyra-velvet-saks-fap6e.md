# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened Saks on my phone and got a "WE'RE SORRY" bot-block wall — no hero, no product, no navigation, nothing. What I see is a white modal over a blurred street-scene background with an apology message accusing my device of suspicious behavior. This is not a luxury retailer homepage; this is a digital bouncer treating me like a threat before I've done a single thing.

## What's working

- The typography is clean and on-brand for Saks — the serif "WE'RE SORRY" at least looks like it came from a design system rather than a raw 500 page.
- The blurred lifestyle photo behind the modal suggests there *is* a real homepage behind this wall, which gives me faint hope.

## What's weak

- **The entire experience:** I opened my own retailer's site and was treated like a bot. As a SaksFirst Diamond member this is embarrassing — I spend five figures a year here.
- **Zero recovery path:** No "try again" button, no alternative (open app, call us, log in instead) — I am literally stranded.
- **IP accusation in plain text:** "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" with my IP address printed out (`75.140.9.0`) reads as confrontational and accusatory, not protective or welcoming.
- **No brand voice at all:** "Cannot process your request right now" could be any utility site. Saks has a whole editorial identity and none of it shows up here.
- **JavaScript/developer-tools bullet:** Listing "use of developer or inspection tools" as a suspicious behavior is alienating to any tech-comfortable shopper — which I am.

## Recommendations

- **Add a SaksFirst login bypass:** Authenticated members should never hit a bot wall. Tie the block-page logic to session/cookie state so Diamond cardholders get waved through immediately.
- **Replace the accusatory IP paragraph** with a single line: "Something looked unusual — tap below to continue" plus a visible "Continue to Saks" CTA button.
- **Offer the app as a fallback:** A deeplink "Open in the Saks app" button turns a dead end into a channel redirect with zero friction.
- **Apply brand voice:** If this block must exist, at least write it as Saks — "We'll be right with you. Something flagged your session — here's how to continue." Keep the tone warm and premium, not forensic.
