# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- I opened saksfifthavenue.com and got a wall — not a welcome. The page I'm staring at is a bot-detection interstitial: "WE'RE SORRY / We've noticed some unusual activity." No hero, no campaign, no navigation, no products. Whatever Saks is pushing right now is completely invisible because I never got past the gate.

## What's working

- The "WE'RE SORRY" headline is at least legible — clean serif type, no visual clutter in the error state itself.
- The block page does explain *why* it happened (unusual activity, bot detection), which is mildly transparent.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is inaccessible.** As a SaksFirst Diamond member opening this on my phone, I'm getting treated like a threat actor. That's a catastrophic first-impression failure.
- **No bypass or recovery path.** There's no "tap here to verify you're human," no CAPTCHA, no redirect to the app — just a dead end with a bullet list of reasons I might be a bot.
- **IP-level blocking without member recognition.** The page cites IP `75.140.9.0` as suspicious. A Diamond-tier card holder should never see this — the absence of any session/loyalty awareness is glaring.
- **No fallback content.** Not even a minimal brand moment: no logo, no "shop the app," no phone number. Just a blurred street photo behind a sorry-card.
- **Copy is clinical and cold.** "We cannot process your request right now" reads like a bank error, not a luxury brand.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a soft-fail recovery CTA** — something like "Tap to continue to Saks" with a lightweight verification, rather than a hard stop with no exit.
- **Exempt authenticated SaksFirst sessions from bot-detection blocks** — loyalty members hitting a wall on a known mobile IP is a churn trigger, not a security win.
- **Replace the generic blurred background** with the actual Saks brand mark so the error state at least feels on-brand, not generic SaaS.
- **Add an app deep-link fallback**: "Having trouble? Open in the Saks app" — recovers the mobile session and protects conversion.
