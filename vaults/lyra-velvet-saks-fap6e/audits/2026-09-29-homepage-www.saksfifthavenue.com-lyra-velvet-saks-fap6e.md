# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Saks doing what Saks does: a polished, editorial fall-campaign hero (Scarlett White in a leopard jacket and skirt, shot in front of the flagship) sitting between two pink promo strips. It's pushing Friends & Family 25% off New Arrivals and a spend-based gift card, so it reads a little more promo-driven than I want from a luxury house. The mood is right for my Pre-Fall shopping, but nothing here says "SaksFirst Diamond, we know you."

## What's working

- **The Scarlett White hero:** The leopard co-ord against the Saks & Company facade feels aspirational and seasonal. It's the kind of image that makes me tap.
- **Category nav:** DESIGNERS, NEW, CLOTHING, BAGS and SHOES are all one tap away. It's clean and not overcrowded.
- **"FRIENDS and FAMILY 25% OFF New Arrivals":** It's on the right inventory. I shop drops, so this is the one promo I'd use.
- **Restrained layout:** There's no pop-up, no shouting and plenty of white space, so it still feels like Saks.

## What's weak

- **Nothing for SaksFirst:** I'm Diamond tier and the page treats me like a stranger. There's no member pricing, points or early access.
- **"Shop Now" on the hero:** It's tiny, sits far right in thin text, and is easy to miss. The pink "SCARLETT WHITE" wordmark on the left carries more visual weight than the CTA.
- **The blank gray strip at the very top:** It looks broken or like a missing announcement bar.
- **Two pink discount strips bracketing the hero:** They make the page feel like a promo site. The gift-card strip in particular is tiny, with dense fine print.
- **No bags:** The homepage doesn't show a single handbag, even though BAGS is in the nav and it's my top category.
- **Undated offers:** "Limited Time" doesn't tell me when to act, and both promos need me to dig through DETAILS or a code.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member strip.** Put "Welcome back, Lyra – SaksFirst Diamond: early access to Pre-Fall" in the blank gray band at the top, and use the space to surface member perks.
- **Turn "Shop Now" into a real button.** Make it a solid white or black pill with "Shop the Scarlett White Edit" and put it under the model, not on the far right.
- **Put a product rail under the hero.** Show four or five bags and jackets with prices (leopard pieces from the campaign plus "New Arrivals") so a product page is one tap away.
- **Add a real deadline to the promos.** Change "Limited Time" to "Ends Oct 6" and fold the code into the strip so it's auto-applied or one-tap copy.
