# Homepage snapshot · www.saksfifthavenue.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This reads exactly like Saks should — editorial, moody, a little NYC-in-autumn romantic, with the "Guest of Honor" split panel doing double duty as inspiration and CTA. It's pushing event dressing (dress-code-worthy pieces) and a straightforward free-shipping incentive, which tracks for where we are in September heading into event season. It looks like a brand that knows its customer buys for occasions, not just closets.

## What's working

- **The "Guest of Honor" split hero** — pairs an aspirational lifestyle shot with a direct, on-theme headline. It's not generic "New Season" copy, it's actually selling a moment (a wedding, a gala, a dinner I need an outfit for).
- **Visible handbags in the hero shot** — the red belt bag and the structured clutch in the model imagery are doing quiet product merchandising without needing a banner.
- **FREE SHIPPING ON $150+ with a "SHIPSAKS" code** — low threshold, easy to clear on a single dress or bag, no mental math required.
- **Clean nav with BAGS, DESIGNERS, SHOES, JEWELRY as top-level tabs** — I can get to my categories in one tap without digging.

## What's weak

- **No SaksFirst / Diamond tier acknowledgment anywhere above the fold** — as a Diamond member this is the first thing I look for, and there's nothing here recognizing me as a repeat, high-value shopper.
- **That empty gray bar at the very top** — looks like a dead ad unit or unrendered banner space. It's the first thing my eye hits and it's just... blank.
- **No account/sign-in state visible** — just a search icon and a wishlist heart. I can't tell if I'm logged in or if there's a personalized "Welcome back" anywhere.
- **"SHOP NOW" leads to a campaign, not a product** — for someone ready to buy, this is one extra tap away from an actual item.

## Recommendations

- Swap that blank top banner for a SaksFirst member strip — even something simple like "Diamond Members: Complimentary Returns + Early Access" would immediately make me feel seen.
- Add a small "Welcome back" or recently-viewed rail right under the hero for returning shoppers like me.
- Pair the free-shipping banner with a second line of member-specific perks (points multiplier, stylist access) to reinforce loyalty value, not just shipping cost.
- Make "SHOP NOW" on the Guest of Honor panel deep-link to a curated product grid, not a landing page, so I'm one tap from actually shopping.
