# Members, save on gifts for everyone on your list
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth member-event send in a week, and it is the cleanest version of the red hero so far. "Member Holiday Event / Spend $250+ / Get 35% Off" is unmistakable, and the copy finally says why: "find the perfect gift for everyone on your list." The 35% matches the 11-25 "Black Friday savings on mountain gear" send. The subject, though, is generic and doesn't mention the 35%.
- Below the hero, the email becomes a catalog: three best-seller grids (women's, men's, kids') with a button after each. There are 12 products, and none of them links to a specific offer. The gifting angle is only a loose frame for what is a general category sweep. A late footer band repeats the old "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" and "Up to 40% Off" offers, which contradicts the 35% headline.
- Judgement: a solid, well-built send with a clear hero, but it is diluted by the catalog and the conflicting footer offers. The "gifts" theme also gets weaker the further you scroll.

## What's working

- The saturated red hero puts "Get 35% Off" front and center, with one white "Shop Now" button.
- The gifting copy ("everyone on your list") fits Thanksgiving week and gives the send a different angle from 11-20 through 11-25.
- The three segments (women's, men's, kids') each have a labeled "Shop … Best Sellers" button, which makes the email easy to navigate.
- The product photography is consistent, with named products such as the Nuketo jacket and THERMOBALL™ Traction Mule.
- The XPLR Pass loyalty strip and the free-shipping line in the footer reinforce the member framing.

## What's weak

- The subject says "save" but gives no number, so the 35% / $250 hook is lost in the inbox.
- No product tile shows a price, a discount, or a gift price point, so the "gifts" promise is never delivered.
- The footer tiles ("Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives", "Up to 40% Off Select Styles") set different offers against the 35% hero. The 30% tile is also stale, repeating the 11-24 send.
- The product labels are white text on busy photos, and some are hard to read, for example "Women's Shelbe Raschel Hoodie" and "Mudder Trucker Hat".
- The "Exclusions and terms apply" line under the hero is tiny, and the $250 threshold is easy to miss on a mobile screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the number and the threshold, and cut the footer offer tiles that contradict it.
- 2. Add "gift under $100" or "stocking stuffer" groupings with real price points, so the gift framing is more than decoration.
- 3. Add a "Shop Gifts" button to the hero and a sale tag on each product tile.
- 4. Put dark scrims under the product labels, or move the labels below the images.
- 5. Make the $250 threshold and the end date larger and clearer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member gift list: 35% off $250+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jackets, beanies, and slippers for everyone, ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Member pricing on full-price gear, plus free shipping with XPLR Pass.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several confirmed HTML/CSS defects: invalid CSS declarations, invalid table nesting, and two images without alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the capture path (AgentMail relay) did not expose the headers. The source was truncated, so footer and link findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 45 tracking links on `click.e.thenorthface.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health, final destinations and 404s are unknown. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Hidden honeypot link:** the first anchor wraps a hidden 1×1 image. The markup is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im…" target="_blank"><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`. This looks like an intentional spam-trap or bot-detection link, but it is a clickable tracked link that no human sees. Scanners or accessibility tools that follow it could register false clicks.
- **No other issues** were visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`. It should have `alt=""`.
  - `261126-XPLR-thanksgiving-US_12.jpg` has no `alt`. This appears to be a content image, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get no fallback.
- **Invalid CSS in `<body style>`:** `font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', …, Arial, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;` is missing a `;` after `sans-serif`. The `margin` and `padding` declarations are swallowed into the font-family value, so the declaration is dropped and the body-level reset does not apply. The `<style>` block's `body { margin: 0 auto !important; padding: 0 !important }` partly covers this.
- **Invalid CSS rule in `<head>`:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', 'Helvetica Neue Medium', … sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property name, so the rule is invalid and ignored.
- **Unclosed media query:** the `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` block in the second `<style>` has no closing `}` before `</style>`. Most parsers auto-close it, but strict clients may drop the mobile rules (`.hide`, `*[class*="show"]`).
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper" …>` directly, with no `<tr><td>`. That is invalid HTML and can cause inconsistent rendering across clients. The outer table and the inner preheader `<table>` also lack `role="presentation"`, unlike `#backgroundWrapper`.
- **Minor:** the preheader div declares `font-size` and `color` twice (1px/11px and #333333/#999999). The later value wins, so this is harmless but sloppy. The `<title>` is the generic "The North Face".
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, MSO/VML namespaces and the `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated source cannot confirm the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. This is unconfirmed and cannot be treated as a sender violation. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, for the same relay reason.
- **Footer not visible:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.
- **Plain-text ratio:** 8,511 of 10,106 characters (about 84%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This exceeds the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. It is a consequence of long `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…` tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are opaque redirects (`?qs=` payloads), so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be seen, and the 45 tracked links were not probed. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to `261126-XPLR-thanksgiving-US_12.jpg`, and `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`.
2. Fix the body style by adding `;` after `sans-serif`. Add `font-family:` to the `table, td` rule. Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
3. Wrap the nested `#backgroundWrapper` table in a `<tr><td>` of the outer table, and add `role="presentation"` to the outer and preheader tables.
4. Re-capture the raw message headers directly from the sending domain (`e.thenorthface.com`) or a seed inbox. That will confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Resolve the 45 tracked links through a redirect-following check to verify destinations and UTMs, and capture the full HTML to verify the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Shorten the plain-text URL load, for example with less redundant link text in the text part, to get below the 70% threshold.
