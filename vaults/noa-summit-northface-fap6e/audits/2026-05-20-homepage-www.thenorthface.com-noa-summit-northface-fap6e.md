# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened the homepage and got hit with a bot verification wall before I could see anything. What I *can* see is a Memorial Day sale banner at the top ("Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25."), the TNF logo and hamburger nav, a sliver of what looks like a rocky cliff hero image, and a full-screen "BEFORE WE CONTINUE... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" challenge modal. As a returning XPLR Pass member opening this on my phone, this is a cold, friction-heavy first impression — the brand I trust to get me up a mountain can't get me past the front door.

## What's working

- **The announcement banner** is doing real work — Memorial Day, 25% off, specific end date (5.25) — clear, time-boxed, no weasel language.
- **The rocky hero imagery** peeking behind the modal feels on-brand for the outdoor adventure aesthetic I associate with TNF. Even 30% visible, it signals expedition-level gear, not mall activewear.
- **The nav bar** is clean and minimal: logo, search, cart, menu. No clutter.

## What's weak

- **The bot gate is the entire first experience.** "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" on a flagship retail homepage in 2026 is a conversion killer. I'm a logged-in XPLR Pass member — this should not be happening.
- **No XPLR Pass recognition** in the banner or anywhere visible. TNF has a loyalty program they actively promoted to me, and there's zero signal that I'm seen as a member.
- **"Shop Sale" in the banner is a tiny text link**, not a button. On mobile, that tap target is undersized and easy to miss amid the announcement bar copy.
- **Zero product or category content is visible** — I can't see Summit Series, FUTURELIGHT, Denali, or anything that speaks to why I shop here specifically.

## Recommendations

- **Move XPLR Pass member recognition above the fold** — even a simple "Welcome back, Noa — your points balance: 1,240" in the announcement strip would make the banner feel personal instead of generic.
- **Replace the bot gate with a Cloudflare Turnstile or silent challenge** — the "Press & Hold" modal is jarring and likely misfiring on legitimate mobile users; it's costing real conversion.
- **Make "Shop Sale" a tappable pill button** in the announcement bar — increase the tap target and add visual affordance so it reads as a CTA, not a hyperlink.
- **Surface a PFC-free or sustainability callout in the hero** — I cross-shop Patagonia partly for the activism; TNF has the "Renewed" and responsible materials story but it's buried. Even a one-line tag on the hero image earns trust from me.
