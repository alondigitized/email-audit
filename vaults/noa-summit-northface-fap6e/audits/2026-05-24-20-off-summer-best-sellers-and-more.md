# 20% off summer best sellers and more.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is The North Face's sixth promo email in eleven days and at this point the campaign is eating itself. The subject line promises "20% off summer best sellers and more" — but the email I open shows a $150 spend threshold for the 20%, then immediately below it a separate "Up to 25% Off Select Gear" Memorial Day block with no threshold. So the bigger discount in the body undercuts the headline offer, and there are no actual "summer best sellers" pictured — just one blue jacket on a model and a generic landscape tile.
- The XPLR Pass gate at the top is the same recruitment hero from the 5/23 send, and the 25% Memorial Day block is the same creative from 5/14 and 5/16. This is a stitched-together rerun, not a fresh send. The subject line bears almost no relationship to what's actually in the email.
- Verdict: low-effort recombination of two prior campaigns under a misleading subject. A subscriber who clicked expecting summer best sellers gets a loyalty wall and a leftover Memorial Day banner.

## What's working

- The "ENDS 5.25" deadline in the Memorial Day block is credible and concrete.
- Two clear CTAs ("Join Now" and "Shop Sale") with pill buttons that read cleanly.
- Footer category nav (Men's / Women's / Kids') gives a fallback path for anyone who bounced off the hero.
- Brand mark and XPLR Pass lockup are clean and on-brand at the top.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "summer best sellers" — none appear in the email. Bait.
- The 20% offer requires $150 spend; the 25% offer below it doesn't. The bigger number wins, making the headline offer feel like a worse deal.
- Single editorial shot of a blue jacket is not "summer" — it's a windbreaker on a model in a beanie. Season mismatch.
- Memorial Day creative is recycled from the 5/14 and 5/16 sends — third time this month.
- No products, prices, or "best seller" merchandising anywhere in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Match the subject to the body.** If you're going to say "summer best sellers," show 3-6 actual best-selling summer products with names and prices. If the lead offer is really XPLR + Memorial Day, say that in the subject.
- 2. **Pick one offer per email.** Stacking "$150+ for 20%" against "Up to 25% off" in the same scroll makes both look worse. Lead with 25% and drop the threshold block, or vice versa.
- 3. **Swap the hero image.** A blue insulated jacket on a model in a knit beanie is wrong for a "summer" send. Use shorts, tees, sun hoodies, sandals, packs.
- 4. **Add a "best seller" grid** — four products with star ratings and prices — so the click has somewhere to land beyond "Shop Sale."
- 5. **Cool the cadence.** Six promo emails in 11 days is bordering on fatigue; the 25% creative has now appeared three times.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tees, shorts, sun hoodies and packs the trail crew keeps buying back.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Memorial Day ends Monday. Shop the bestsellers before sizes go.`

# Technical Audit — The North Face "20% off summer best sellers"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated email (likely Salesforce Marketing Cloud, given `camarker-inner` / `stylingblock-content-wrapper` classes). Structurally sound but missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers and has minor accessibility/markup defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 27 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe — unreachable links cannot be verified live, but no broken on-page anchors were detected in source.
- Plain-text alternative is **78% URLs (5461/6994 chars)** — heavily skewed toward tracking URLs vs. readable copy, which can degrade spam scoring and screen-reader output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attribute** on spacer image `TNF-spacer-100px_01.jpg` (https://image.e.thenorthface.com/lib/fe3811717164047a741772/m/1/TNF-spacer-100px_01.jpg). Spacers should carry `alt=""` (empty, decorative) to silence screen readers — absent attribute leaves behavior undefined.
- Malformed inline `style` on `<body>`: `font-family:'…sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;` — missing semicolon after the `sans-serif` font stack collapses three declarations into a broken `font-family` value, dropping `margin`/`padding` resets.
- CSS rules for `table, td` and `h1–h6` declare a font stack with **no `font-family:` property name** — the declarations are invalid and ignored by parsers.
- Outer `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` is never closed before nested `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` opens — Outlook/MSO may render layout inconsistently.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[[…]]` merge tokens detected in source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** in captured message — may be a relay capture gap, but cannot be confirmed present.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe not advertised; Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (effective Feb 2024) mandate this for senders >5k/day.
- **Authentication-Results header not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status cannot be verified from this sample.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable — all 27 destination links route through `e.thenorthface.com` click-tracking redirects and were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page status cannot be confirmed from source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers to meet RFC 8058 / Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender rules.
2. Fix `<body style="…sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">` — insert `;` after the font stack so `margin`/`padding` resets apply.
3. Add `font-family:` keyword to the `table, td { … }` and `h1–h6 { … }` rules, or remove the dead declarations.
4. Add `alt=""` to `TNF-spacer-100px_01.jpg` (and audit other spacer/decorative images).
5. Verify the outer `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4">` is properly closed; consider removing the redundant outer wrapper.
6. Expand plain-text alternative with real copy so the URL-to-text ratio drops below ~50% to improve deliverability scoring.
7. Re-run capture through AgentMail relay to confirm `Authentication-Results` and `List-Unsubscribe` headers are actually absent vs. stripped in transit.
