# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- TNF is leading with their Memorial Day sale right now — banner, hard deadline, XPLR Pass incentive, the whole playbook. The hero image is moody and cinematic (think basalt columns, mist, waterfall energy), which is exactly the outdoor aspirational vibe I come to this brand for. But the second I land, a modal shoves into my face asking me to join XPLR Pass — and I've been an XPLR Pass member for years. Hard pass on the friction.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner** — clear, deadline-stamped, clickable. "Ends 5.25" with a "Shop Sale" link is exactly the kind of urgency signal that makes me want to tap through before I forget.
- **Hero landscape imagery** — misty basalt columns / waterfall scene reads as legitimately epic, not just stock-photo-outdoorsy. This is the brand I trust to make gear that belongs in that environment.
- **XPLR Pass surface area** — even though the modal misfires for me personally, the fact that the loyalty program gets above-the-fold real estate signals TNF knows loyalty is a conversion driver. The 10% off hook is solid for non-members.
- **Logo + minimal nav** — the header is clean. TNF wordmark is immediately recognizable. Search and cart icons are right where I expect them.

## What's weak

- **XPLR modal targeting** — showing a "Join XPLR Pass" recruitment modal to someone already enrolled is a data/personalization failure. This should be suppressed for signed-in members and replaced with a rewards balance or member-exclusive deal.
- **Zero hero copy visible** — I cannot see a single word of headline or subhead on the hero. If copy exists below the modal cutoff, it's inaccessible until I dismiss the modal. The hero is doing all heavy lifting on vibes alone.
- **No product or category entry points** — the entire visible page is hero image + modal + banner. I have no idea what categories are featured, what's on sale, or how to get to Summit Series or Nuptse jackets. Everything is behind the hamburger.
- **Modal blocks the hero CTA zone** — whatever button or campaign copy sits below the hero image is invisible. Likely a "Shop Now" or sale CTA is being completely buried.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the XPLR modal for authenticated/cookied members** — or swap it for a "You have X XPLR Points — apply to this sale" strip. One personalization rule, big loyalty signal.
- **Surface hero copy above the modal cutoff** — even a single headline overlaid on the upper half of the hero image would establish campaign context before my eye hits the modal. Right now the hero is mute.
- **Add one category strip below the hero** — Summit Series / Nuptse / Denali tiles, three icons, three taps. Get me into gear I actually buy in two taps, not buried behind a hamburger menu.
- **Pin free-shipping threshold to the banner** — "Free shipping on orders $100+" costs one line of copy and closes a major conversion gap, especially for a sale event where I'm calculating cart value in my head.
