# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- TNF knows I'm an XPLR Pass member before I even get to browse — the sticky member banner with a concrete "$150+ = 20% off, Ends 5.25" offer is exactly the kind of returning-shopper hook I expect from a brand I've been loyal to for years. The sale hero underneath (Up to 25% off select gear) reads like a warehouse-sale moment, which my inner deal-tracker appreciates. But the experience is immediately torpedoed by a full-screen bot-verification wall that makes me feel like I walked up to the TNF store and got stopped by a bouncer before I could touch anything.

## What's working

- **Member sticky banner** — "Members, spend $150+ and get 20% off. Ends 5.25" is the single strongest signal on this page. It's personal, time-bounded, and financially concrete. As someone who actively manages my XPLR balance, this is the first thing that would make me stay.
- **Dual-offer layering** — the member banner stacks on top of the sitewide "Up to 25% off select gear" hero, which feels like I'm getting an insider rate on top of a public sale. That layered discount framing activates the deal-stacking part of my brain.
- **XPLR logo above the fold** — even though the modal cut it off, catching a glimpse of the XPLR mark tells me there's loyalty content below. I know to scroll for it.
- **Clean nav structure** — TNF logo, search, bag, hamburger — four icons, nothing extra. Easy to orient on mobile.

## What's weak

- **The bot wall is a full-stop experience killer** — "BEFORE WE CONTINUE… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" with a reference ID plastered over the homepage is jarring and brand-eroding. This is the first impression after the nav. On mobile it fills the entire above-the-fold area. If I weren't already a loyal customer, I'd bounce immediately.
- **"Up to 25% off select gear" is vague** — "select gear" is a classic weasel qualifier. What gear? Is the Summit Series in? The Nuptse? Denali fleeces? I need to know if this sale touches my actual category or if it's just Venture 2 raincoats nobody wants.
- **No sustainability or PFC-free signal anywhere** — one of my main reasons to stay TNF vs. cross-shopping Patagonia is their stated commitment to move away from PFCs. There's nothing above the fold (or visible at all in this screenshot) that reinforces that. This is table stakes for someone like me.
- **"Shop Sale" as a text link, not a button** — a tappable CTA this important should look like a button. The underlined text sits against a dimmed hero and doesn't have the visual weight it needs on a 390px phone screen.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or soft-launch the bot wall on return visitors** — if I'm already cookied as an XPLR member, skip the hard challenge or at minimum delay it until I attempt a second interaction. A bot-check modal on every page load for known customers destroys the loyalty experience you're trying to build with the member banner.
- **Name the sale categories in the hero** — swap "up to 25% off select gear" for something like "Summit Series, Fleece & Packs — up to 25% off" so I know in one glance whether my category is in-scope before I tap through.
- **Promote the PFC-free milestone** — add a small callout strip or badge on the hero ("Now PFC-free across all outerwear" or similar). This is the kind of thing that makes me choose TNF over Patagonia on a given week, and right now Patagonia says it louder.
- **Turn "Shop Sale" into a real button** — solid black pill, white text, full-width or 60% width, directly under the hero headline. Make it look like something worth tapping.
