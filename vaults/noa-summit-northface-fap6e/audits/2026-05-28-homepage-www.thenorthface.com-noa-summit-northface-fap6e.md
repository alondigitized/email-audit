# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, classic TNF vibe — clean, muted earth tones, outdoorsy lifestyle grid, all very on-brand. But I open the app and immediately get hit with a modal asking me to join XPLR Pass... which I'm already a member of. The brand looks polished and intentional, but the homepage is clearly in acquisition mode right now, not member-retention mode, and that's a friction point for me personally.

## What's working

- **XPLR Pass double placement** — banner strip + modal makes sure absolutely no one misses the loyalty offer; for people who aren't members yet, this is a hard-to-ignore recruitment moment
- **2×2 lifestyle grid** — the four images together feel editorial and intentional; the woman with the sling pack + bucket hat against the mountain backdrop is genuinely appealing to me, that's exactly the Summit Series crossover aesthetic I'm into
- **Muted earth palette** — olive, tan, stone — it's cohesive and doesn't feel like a clearance rack screaming at me
- **Clean nav** — TNF wordmark + logo, search, bag, hamburger; not cluttered, easy to parse at a glance

## What's weak

- **No hero copy at all** — four lifestyle photos with zero headline, no campaign name, no product callout; I have no idea if there's a new Summit Series drop, a PFC-free initiative, a spring collection — nothing
- **Modal fires for existing members** — showing me a "Join XPLR Pass" prompt when I'm already enrolled is a straight-up miss; the site isn't recognizing me and it's making me feel like a stranger
- **No visible shopping path above the fold** — the "Join Now" CTA leads me to a loyalty signup, not a product page; the actual hamburger nav is the only path to shop, and that requires an extra tap before I can even see categories
- **No urgency or seasonal hook** — no "new arrivals," no "summer drops just landed," no PFC-free callout; nothing that tells me why I should care today specifically

## Recommendations

- **Add a campaign headline to the hero grid** — even a single line overlaid on one of the four images ("Summit Series. Now PFC-Free.") would give me a reason to keep scrolling vs. just looking at pretty photos
- **Suppress the XPLR modal for logged-in members** — swap it for a "Welcome back, Noa — your 10% is waiting" banner instead; or surface my member status + points balance right in the banner strip
- **Surface one shopping CTA above the fold** — "Shop New Arrivals" or "Explore Summit Series" as a visible button below the grid before the modal fires would give me a path forward without having to open the hamburger menu
- **Add a seasonal drop hook to the banner** — "XPLR members get early access to the Summer '26 drop" would be so much more compelling than generic 10% off copy that I've seen a hundred times
