# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened TNF's homepage on my phone and hit a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" wall before I could see anything. What I can actually see is almost entirely a bot-verification modal blocking the page — the brand is invisible except for a skinny rewards banner at the top and a sliver of what looks like a dark jacket hero peeking behind the overlay. For a brand I genuinely love and actively shop, this is a frustrating way to greet me.

## What's working

- **Rewards banner** — "Rewards are here. Sign in to check your status" is front and center. As an XPLR Pass member this is the one thing that speaks directly to me and would pull me back in if the page were actually accessible.
- **TNF logo + nav bar** — clean, minimal, and recognizable even on a blocked screen. The brand ID is intact in the header.
- **Partial hero image** — the little I can see (dark TNF jacket, colorful layered look beneath it) is exactly the Summit Series / layered aesthetic I shop. Would land for me if it were actually visible.

## What's weak

- **Bot verification wall** — the entire homepage is behind a "Press & Hold" challenge. This is the dominant above-the-fold experience and it's brutal. A returning XPLR Pass member on mobile should not be getting Cloudflare'd on the homepage.
- **Zero offer visibility** — even the rewards banner just says "check your status," not "you have X points" or "members get 20% off this week." No hook, no urgency.
- **No navigation usable** — hamburger menu is visible but meaningless when a modal is blocking the page.
- **No campaign messaging visible** — I can't tell if it's a Summit Series drop, a warehouse sale, or a seasonal push. The hero behind the modal is a ghost.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the bot-wall triggering on organic mobile traffic** — if real XPLR Pass members are hitting this challenge on a standard phone browser, that is a conversion-killing bug that needs to ship a fix this week.
- **Personalize the rewards banner** — swap "Sign in to check your status" for "Welcome back, Noa — you have X points" post-login. The hook is there; use it.
- **Surface a concrete offer in the rewards banner** — even "Members get free shipping today" would add urgency without a dedicated promo strip.
- **Once the page loads, put Summit Series or seasonal layering hero front and center** — the peek of the jacket behind the modal suggests they may already be doing this, but it's invisible right now.
