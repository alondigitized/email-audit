# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a modal asking me to join XPLR Pass — which I'm already a member of, so that's immediately annoying. Underneath all that clutter there's a forest-path hero and a pair of stacked sale banners, so the brand is clearly running an end-of-May push to move inventory. It reads as a brand that knows it has a loyalty program but hasn't figured out how to use it intelligently.

## What's working

- **Dual-layer member messaging** — the top banner ("Members, spend $150+ and get 20% off. Ends 5.25") and the mid-page XPLR Pass module both reinforce the same offer, which creates coherent urgency for someone who cares about the program.
- **Hard deadline** — "Ends 5.25" appears twice in the visible viewport. That's a real date two days out and it will actually make me think about whether I need to restock before the sale ends.
- **Sale banner with clear discount** — "Up to 25% off select gear. Shop Sale" against the trail background is legible and direct; no mystery about what's happening.
- **XPLR Pass badge** — the circular logo is recognizable to me; it signals this brand speaks my language even before I read the copy.

## What's weak

- **"Join XPLR Pass for 10% Off" modal targeting me as an existing member** — this is the most friction-heavy element on the page. If I'm logged in or recognized, showing me a recruitment popup for a program I already belong to is either lazy segmentation or a cookie/session miss, and it makes me trust the personalization less overall.
- **No gear visible** — the hero is a trail photo with no actual product in frame. There's no Nuptse puffer, no Summit Series jacket, nothing I can covet above the fold. The page is all offer and zero inspiration.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — on mobile there are no pinned category tiles, no "Women's" or "Summit Series" shortcut. One more tap between me and my category is one reason to bounce.
- **XPLR Pass module cuts off** — "Spend $150+ / Get 20% Off" is truncated at the bottom of the screen because the join modal sits on top. The actual member offer is partially unreadable.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the "Join XPLR Pass" modal for a member-aware welcome strip** — if I'm cookied as a member, show me my points balance or "You're $X away from your next reward" instead of a recruitment popup. Ship this with a simple session check.
- **Put one hero product in frame** — drop a lifestyle shot of the Summit Series jacket or a layered-look flatlay into the hero background. Gear in the image = reason to stay.
- **Add two or three category shortcut tiles below the banner** — "Women's," "Summit Series," "Clearance" as tappable pills. Reduces the nav depth from 2 taps to 1.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — I didn't see it anywhere in this viewport. I'm likely to spend over it anyway, but showing "Free shipping on $125+" reinforces that I should round out my cart.
