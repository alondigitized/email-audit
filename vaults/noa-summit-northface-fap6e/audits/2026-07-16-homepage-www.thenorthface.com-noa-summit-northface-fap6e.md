# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- TNF opens with a confident, dark lifestyle shot and copy that feels seasonal and considered — "New Year. Fresh Gear." is clean and energetic. But "Back to Campus" is unmistakably college-coded, and I'm 31 years old with a mortgage, not a meal plan. The brand still knows me as a returning customer (that XPLR Pass banner is doing real work), but the hero campaign is clearly aimed at an 18–22 crowd, not someone who's been tracking Summit Series drops for the last eight years.

## What's working

- **XPLR Pass banner at the very top** — "Rewards are here. Sign in to check your status." hits me before I even see the logo. That's the right spot for a loyalty hook on mobile.
- **Ghost button category CTAs in the hero** — "Women's," "Men's," "Backpacks" are clear, accessible, and don't fight the image. I know exactly what to tap.
- **"New Year. Fresh Gear." headline** — concise, a little punchy, works for fall gear refresh. Not cringe.
- **Lifestyle hero image** — group of young adults outdoors in layered TNF gear, casual and real-feeling, not a sterile product shot. This matches how I actually wear the stuff.
- **"Layer Up" tile appearing below fold** — that category name is literally how I shop TNF. That's me.

## What's weak

- **"Back to Campus" eyebrow copy** — I aged out of this framing nine years ago. It signals this campaign wasn't made for me even if the clothes are.
- **No XPLR member pricing or points balance visible** — the banner brings me in but doesn't close the loop. If I'm signed in, show me my points or a member-exclusive offer.
- **No concrete discount or offer anywhere above the fold** — I know TNF doesn't always do deep discounts, but even "free shipping on orders over $X" or "XPLR members earn 2X points" would give me a reason to act now vs. later.
- **Hero image is visibly Gen Z–skewed** — the vibe in the photo reads college orientation week, not a 31-year-old who just got back from a winter mountaineering course.
- **No PFC-free, sustainability, or product technology callout** — for someone who cross-shops Patagonia partly because of their values messaging, TNF is leaving that card unplayed.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the XPLR top banner from generic to personalized** — if I'm a signed-in member, show my points balance and the next reward tier milestone right there. Don't just tease me with "check your status."
- **Add a second hero slide (the video is paused anyway) that speaks to performance** — Summit Series, Futurelight, or PFC-free tech. Give me something that rewards knowing the product line.
- **Drop a membership pricing or shipping incentive below the headline** — even a single line like "XPLR members: free shipping, always" above the CTA buttons is enough to keep me from bouncing.
- **Retitle the eyebrow or create a parallel campaign** — "Back to Campus" for the college drop, "Back to the Mountain" or "Fresh Season" for XPLR Pass / performance loyalists. Both campaigns, different surfaces or a second hero slot.
