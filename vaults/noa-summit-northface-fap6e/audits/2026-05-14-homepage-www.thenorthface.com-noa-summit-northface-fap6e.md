# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I land on TNF's homepage and the first thing I get is a bot-check wall — "BEFORE WE CONTINUE... Press & Hold" — blocking nearly everything below the fold. What little I can see is promising: a Memorial Day Sale banner calling out 25% off through 5.25, a moody canyon hero in the background, and the standard minimal nav. TNF is clearly running a sale push right now, but I'm being held hostage by a Cloudflare verification screen before I can see a single product.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner strip** — "Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25. Shop Sale" is clean, specific enough, and dated correctly. I know exactly what the event is and when it expires.
- **Hero landscape imagery (partially visible)** — the dark, columnar basalt canyon behind the modal reads epic and outdoorsy. Very on-brand for Summit Series territory; it's the kind of imagery that makes me want to see more.
- **Minimalist nav** — logo, search, bag, hamburger. Clean and fast on mobile. Nothing to trip over.

## What's weak

- **Bot verification modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" takes over the screen before I've seen a single product or category. This is the worst possible first impression, especially on mobile.
- **Hero copy is cut off** — "MEMORIAL DAY SALE | ENDS 5.25" peeks out from behind the modal but is not fully readable. The headline investment is wasted.
- **No XPLR Pass hook visible** — as an XPLR Pass member, I expect to see member-exclusive pricing or early access callouts. Nothing here for me specifically.
- **"Up to 25% off select gear"** — the hedge on "select" is a minor friction point. What's actually on sale? I can't tell from this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Exempt logged-in XPLR Pass members from bot-check friction** — if I'm already authenticated, skip the Cloudflare wall entirely. Rewarding members with a frictionless experience is table stakes.
- **Surface XPLR Pass member pricing in the banner strip** — swap or supplement the generic "Up to 25% off" with "Members: extra 10% stacked — sign in to activate." One line, huge loyalty lift.
- **Make the hero CTA visible even with a bot wall** — if the verification must run, overlay it on top of a fully rendered hero so the brand promise is visible. Right now it's a gray void.
- **Specify sale categories in the banner** — "Summit Series, Nuptse, and Denali up to 25% off" beats "select gear" every time. I track drop calendars; tell me what's actually marked down.
