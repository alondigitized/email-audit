# Discover what’s new for November
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean new-arrivals catalog. It follows the 11-06 "Discover jackets down for the cold." send, which promoted the same veterans 20% offer (11.5–11.12). The offer is better handled here than on 11-06. It sits in a gray top banner and again in a full-width closer with "ENDS 11.12" and a "Shop Now" button. The middle of the email is still a generic grid of products with no prices and no reason to pick one over another.
- The hero is a strong, on-brand image of a model in a bronze down hood. The headline "New gear is here." is a weak hook. The gender and kids split with three category buttons is a sensible way to segment. A shopper outside the veterans eligibility group sees no offer at all, and the 20% only applies to verified military members. This is the second send in two days with the same promo, so it feels repetitive.
- Verdict: competent and polished, but the layout is a catalog with a deadline buried at the bottom. It reads as a 5/10.

## What's working

- The hero photo of a model in a bronze down jacket is striking and seasonally right for early November.
- The 20% veterans offer appears twice, at the top and in the closer, and the closer carries a clear "ENDS 11.12" deadline.
- The three category buttons (Women's, Men's, Kids' New Arrivals) let shoppers pick their own path.
- Product tiles are consistent and named specifically, for example "Men's 1996 Retro Nuptse Jacket" and "Men's THERMOBALL™ Snow Triclimate® Jacket".
- The XPLR Pass loyalty strip with free shipping is visible in the footer.

## What's weak

- The offer is exclusive to verified military members, so most recipients get no discount from this email.
- The 20% banner at the top is small gray text on a gray background and is easy to skip.
- None of the 10+ product tiles shows a price, a rating or a badge, so nothing separates them.
- "New gear is here." is generic, and the body copy about "brisk days and chilly nights" is filler.
- The "Verify Your Status" and "Shop Now" buttons in the closer are small, and the "Terms apply." line is tiny.
- This is a near-repeat of the 11-06 send, so it adds little new to the inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the veterans offer. Make the top banner a bold, high-contrast block, and put a "Verify & save 20%" button next to the hero.
- 2. Add prices or a "from $X" line to each tile, plus one or two badges such as "New" or "Best seller". Trim the grid to 6–8 products.
- 3. Replace the hero headline with something specific, such as the jacket name plus its use case.
- 4. Give non-veterans a reason to click, such as free shipping through the XPLR Pass or a gift-guide angle.
- 5. Space out the sends. Two days of the same promo should be one email with a reminder, not two.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send with all links wrapped in `click.e.thenorthface.com` redirects. The visible source has malformed CSS and table nesting. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the relay didn't capture headers. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** All 47 links go through `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…`. The QA probe skipped them, so destinations and HTTP status are unverified.
- **Honeypot link:** The first link in the body wraps a 1×1 `display:none` image (`…/m/1/honeypot_pixel.png`) and points to `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Imci…`. It looks deliberate, as a bot-click trap. It leaves an empty anchor that a keyboard user might still tab to. Scanners that follow every link could also register false clicks.
- **Plain-text part:** 8,841 of 10,443 characters (about 85%) are URLs. The long `qs=` tokens inflate the text part, which can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `font-family:` property:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif; }` and the `h1…h6` rule's font list have no `font-family:` property. Both declarations are invalid and ignored, so those elements fall back to the inherited font.
- **Missing semicolon in the body style:** `<body style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">` has no `;` after `sans-serif`. The browser drops the `font-family` declaration. The inline `margin` and `padding` are probably lost too, though the stylesheet `body` rule sets them as a backup.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4">` directly contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` with no `<tr><td>` between them. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, as does the inner `<table><tr><td>` that holds the preheader. Clients repair this inconsistently, and screen readers may announce these as data tables.
- **Unclosed `@media` block:** In the second `<style>` block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed before `</style>`. Parsers auto-close it, but strict clients may drop the rules.
- **Missing alt text:** The `honeypot_pixel.png` image has no `alt`. It is hidden, so the practical impact is minimal.
- **Redundant preheader styles:** `font-size` and `color` are each declared twice in the `.preheader` inline style. This is harmless.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, the UTF-8 charset and the MSO conditional comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens. The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not captured:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were all not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip or omit them, so these are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer not verified:** The body unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be checked.
- **Sender:** `reply@e.thenorthface.com` is a subdomain of the brand's own domain, which is consistent with the click and image domains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable:** Every link is wrapped, so UTM parameters, if any, sit behind the redirect. UTMs and landing-page alignment could not be assessed.
- **Next step:** Follow the redirects with a real client and check the final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Repair the CSS:** Add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and `h1…h6` rules, add the missing `;` in the `<body>` inline style, and close the `@media` block.
2. **Fix the table structure:** Wrap the nested tables in `<tr><td>` and add `role="presentation"` to the outer and preheader tables.
3. **Add `alt=""` to the honeypot image:** Also confirm it isn't keyboard-focusable and that your link scanners exclude it.
4. **Check the headers at the source:** Capture the raw message at the receiving mailbox to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay's warnings are not proof of absence.
5. **Resolve the tracked links:** Follow the 47 redirects in a headless run to verify status codes, final URLs and UTM parameters.
6. **Review the plain-text part:** The URL-heavy text part could use shorter links or a leaner text version.
7. **Re-run on the full HTML:** Repeat the audit on the untruncated source to cover the footer, unsubscribe link and any merge tokens.
