# Explore 30% off holiday exclusives
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led jacket send. The hero is a model in a rust-brown puffer, and "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" sits right under it with one dark "Shop Holiday Exclusives" button. The eye lands on the offer first. It is closest to the 11-17 "Save 30% on exclusive jacket styles" send, which was the best of the week, but the body is weaker.
- The 2x5 grid of jackets is easy to scan and covers women, men, girls, boys, kids and baby. But none of the tiles show a price, a sale price or a rating, so the 30% never turns into a real number. The red "Member Holiday Event / Spend $250+ / Get 30% Off" block at the bottom repeats the 11-20 to 11-22 creative almost exactly. It adds a second, conditional offer that sits awkwardly next to the unconditional "Save 30%" at the top.
- Judgement: solid and competent, but it is the 7th near-identical 30% promo in eight days. It is a 6 or 7, not an 8, because of the offer confusion and the cadence fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A strong portrait, big "Save 30%" type and a single button make the offer obvious in about two seconds.
- The grid covers the whole family. Women's, men's, girls', boys', kids' and baby tiles let each segment find its product.
- The labels are readable. Each tile has a white product name on the image, such as "Men's Lone Peak Triclimate 2 Jacket".
- The XPLR Pass bar at the top ("Join XPLR Pass and unlock 15% off your next purchase") adds a loyalty hook without crowding the hero.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The two offers conflict. The top says "Save 30%" with no condition, and the bottom says "Spend $250+ / Get 30% Off" for members. A reader can't tell whether 30% applies to everything or only after a threshold.
- No tile shows a price, a was/now price or a rating, so there is no concrete value and no social proof.
- Only one CTA label is used, "Shop Holiday Exclusives", and it appears twice. The 10 tiles are the only product-specific paths. Nothing signals urgency or a deadline.
- The cadence is stale. The red member block is a copy of the 11-20, 11-21 and 11-22 heroes, and the subject "Explore 30% off" is a softer version of 11-17's "Save 30% on exclusive jacket styles".
- White text over the lighter images (the cream Cragmont coat, the light-grey Frost Fall jacket) has weaker contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the offer. Either lead with one clear 30% message and state the condition once, or drop the red member block from this send. If the member tier stays, label it clearly as an extra.
- 2. Put real prices on the tiles, for example "$199 → $139", plus a star rating on a few of them.
- 3. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Cyber Monday" or "Through 11/28". The 11-24 timing makes that credible.
- 4. Make the buttons specific: "Shop Women's Jackets" and "Shop Men's Jackets" rather than a repeated generic button.
- 5. Add a dark gradient behind the tile captions so the white labels stay readable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off jackets, fleece & parkas — ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Frost Fall Jacket is 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Holiday exclusives for the whole family, plus 15% off with XPLR Pass.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Puffers from $99. Sale ends Cyber Monday.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **47 tracking links were not probed.** All are on `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=...`, an opaque click-redirect domain. Destination status, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Honeypot link.** The first anchor wraps `<img src="https://image.e.thenorthface.com/lib/.../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;">`. It has no visible text and goes to a tracked URL. This is a standard spam-trap or bot-click detector, so it is intentional. It does add a hidden click-tracked link to the markup.
- No broken or malformed `href` values were found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Unclosed `@media` block.** In the second `<style>` block (inside `<!--[if false]><!-->`), `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed before `</style>`. Clients that parse strictly may drop the mobile rules (`.showMobile`, `*[class*="show"]`, `.hide`).
- **Invalid CSS declaration.** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property name, so the rule is ignored.
- **Malformed inline style on `<body>`.** `font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ..., sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;` has no semicolon after `sans-serif`. The whole `font-family` declaration is invalid, and `margin` may be dropped with it.
- **Invalid table nesting.** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" ...>` contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` directly, with no `<tr>/<td>`. The outer table is also never closed in the visible source. Browsers and Outlook will repair this differently.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`.** The outer `<table bgcolor>` and the inner `<table> <tr> <td>` around the preheader have no `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding`, or `cellspacing`. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **QA alt-text warning.** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`. It is `display:none` and 1×1, so the real impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would clear the warning.
- **Hidden preheader.** It uses `display:none` plus padding with `&nbsp;&zwnj;` entities. This is a standard pattern with no issue.
- **Unverified.** The truncated source means the remaining images, alt text, and `<a>` accessibility can't be checked.
