# Gifts under $100 with no limit on adventure.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide email with a price-point hook, "Gifts Under $100", and not much else. The hero is a tidy flat-lay of a shearling hat, mittens, a blue beanie, a dry bag and a yellow bottle. Beneath it sit "A little gift goes a long way." and a clear dark button. The product grid below is the strongest part, because it shows eight real, affordable items. But none of them has a price, a name or its own link, so "under $100" is a claim you have to take on faith.
- The email also repeats itself. "Gifts Under $100" appears as a button twice, then "Shop Gift Guide" appears over a tent photo, then there's a 30% Holiday Exclusives banner at the bottom. That's four CTAs pointing at overlapping destinations. The best offer, 30% off in Black Friday early access, is buried in the last block. It was the headline of the 11-13 send and the 11-15 fleece send. The 15% XPLR Pass offer is a text link at the very top.
- My judgment is a decent seasonal send that spreads its attention too thin. It's more of a mid-funnel catalog than an offer-led email, and it undersells the 30% promotion that the brand ran so well over the past few days.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and seasonal, with a flat-lay on snow that suggests gifting. The headline and copy are easy to read.
- The "Under $100" framing gives a concrete, budget-friendly angle on gift shopping.
- The eight-product grid on dark green has good contrast and shows variety, including mittens, a beanie, a bottle, a sling bag, a vest and a kids' hat.
- The footer shows the XPLR Pass loyalty program with "Free Shipping always. Points on every purchase."
- The Black Friday Early Access eyebrow with "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" keeps continuity with the 11-13 and 11-15 sends.

## What's weak

- The 30% offer sits at the bottom, below three other CTAs. The white eyebrow text "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS" and the headline lose contrast over the couple's jackets and the lighter parts of the photo.
- The product grid has no prices, no names and no individual links. It's one big "Gifts Under $100" button for eight items.
- "Gifts Under $100" is repeated, and "Shop Gift Guide" is a third near-identical destination. The tent image with its low-contrast, thin white label reads like a filler block.
- The 15% XPLR Pass signup offer at the top is a small underlined text link and easy to miss. It's also unclear whether the 15% and the 30% can be combined.
- It's the fifth or sixth gift-themed send in two weeks, after "Discover gifts for everyone on your list" (11-10) and "Go further with our best-selling gifts" (11-14), so the creative is wearing thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the money. Put "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" in the hero, or at least directly after it. Then use the under-$100 grid as supporting proof. Pick one offer instead of stacking several.
- 2. Make the grid shoppable. Add price tags ("$45", "$79") and link each tile to its product page. That proves the "under $100" claim and gives readers a reason to click.
- 3. Cut the CTAs from four to two: one "Gifts Under $100" and one "Shop 30% Off". Drop the tent block, or fold it into the guide link.
- 4. Put real contrast under the early-access type, using a darker gradient or a solid band. Make the XPLR 15% signup a real button.
- 5. Space out gift sends and vary the angle: gifts for her, gifts for the kids, last-day shipping, and so on.

- **Subject Alt B:** `8 gifts under $100 that actually keep them warm`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early access is open: 30% off Holiday Exclusives, plus stocking stuffers from $25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mittens, beanies and bottles, all under $100. Free shipping for XPLR Pass members.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: The North Face, "Gifts under $100 with no limit on adventure."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with several CSS and table-structure errors. It also has a hidden honeypot link with no alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be confirmed, because the QA relay did not capture those headers and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Hidden honeypot link:** the first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im...">` wrapping `<img src="https://image.e.thenorthface.com/lib/fe3811717164047a741772/m/1/honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;">`.
  - The anchor has no visible content, so it is an empty link in the tab order.
  - Security scanners and prefetchers may click it and inflate click metrics. This is my inference. The HTML doesn't confirm it.
- **Tracking links not probed:** 44 links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the QA probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Domain alignment:** `click.e.`, `image.e.` and the From domain `e.thenorthface.com` share the same subdomain. No issue found there.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no alt attribute (QA WARN). It is a decorative tracking element, so `alt=""` is the right value.
- **Invalid font-family declarations:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property, so the whole rule is dropped. The `h1–h6` rule has the same defect on its first declaration.
- **Missing semicolon on `<body>`:** the inline style is `...Arial, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;`. The `font-family` declaration swallows `margin`, so the body margin from the inline style is lost. The `<head>` rule `body { margin: 0 auto !important; padding: 0 !important }` partly compensates.
- **Unclosed @media block:** the `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` in the second `<style>` block is never closed before `</style>`. Browsers usually recover from this. Strict parsers and some webmail sanitizers may drop the rules.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" ...>` directly contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` with no `<tr><td>` between them. Several inner tables (`<table> <tr> <td>`) have no `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Duplicate properties:** the preheader `<div>` declares `font-size` and `color` twice, with the later values winning. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it reads "The North Face".
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, VML and Office namespaces are declared, the preheader is hidden correctly, and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is handled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The portion after truncation is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both headers as not found (WARN).
  - QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. It is unconfirmed.
  - If they are truly absent, the email has no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these can't be verified because the HTML is truncated before the footer.
- **Plain-text part:** 8,322 of 9,925 characters (about 84%) are URLs (WARN). Tracking URLs are bloating the text alternative, which can raise spam-filter scores.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Every link is wrapped in an opaque `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=...` redirect, and the QA probe skipped all 44 of them. The final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page match for the "under $100" offer were not observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers from the original sender, not the relay copy. If they are missing, add them.
2. Check the original headers for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `e.thenorthface.com`.
3. Add `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`. Consider whether the hidden link should be in the markup at all, given the empty-link and scanner-click concerns.
4. Fix the CSS: add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and `h1–h6` rules, add the missing `;` in the `<body>` inline style, and close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
5. Restructure the outer `<table>` to wrap the inner table in `<tr><td>`, and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Shorten the plain-text part so tracking URLs are not 84% of it.
7. Re-run QA with the full HTML and resolved redirect destinations to cover the footer, UTM parameters and landing-page continuity.
