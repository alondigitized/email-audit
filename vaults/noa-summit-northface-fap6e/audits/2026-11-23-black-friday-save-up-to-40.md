# Black Friday: Save up to 40%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the actual Black Friday send, and it is more of a catalog than the member-event emails from 11-20 to 11-22. The hero is a muted, desaturated snowy mountain with "Black Friday Sale / Save Up to 40%" in thin white type over it. The offer is clear, but the visual punch is weak. The hero looks washed out and the headline weight is light for the biggest sale weekend of the year.
- Below the hero, a 2x2 grid (Women's, Men's, Holiday Exclusives, Kids') shows good model photography, and a dark "Shop Sale" button follows. Then comes a saturated red XPLR Pass block, "Spend $250+ / Get 30% Off", with Women's and Men's Best Sellers buttons. The red block is the loudest thing in the email and it sells a different offer. It also repeats the creative from the previous three days. Two stacked offers, 40% and 30%, make the reader do math: is 30% better than 40%, and which one do I get?
- Overall it is competent and on-brand but not exciting. The category tiles are good for discovery. The hero undersells the event and the offer architecture is muddy.

## What's working

- "Save Up to 40%" is a concrete, scannable offer with a "Black Friday Sale" eyebrow that fits the date.
- The 2x2 tile grid has clean, well-lit model photography and clear category labels. It covers women, men, kids and holiday.
- The red XPLR Pass block is a strong visual anchor, and it reinforces loyalty value with "Free Shipping always. Points on every purchase."
- The XPLR Pass strip at the top gives a fast secondary hook: 15% off the next purchase.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap.

## What's weak

- The hero is low-contrast. White thin type on a pale, hazy mountain photo hurts legibility and urgency.
- The offers conflict: "Up to 40%" in the hero against "30% off" in the member block. The member offer needs a $250 spend and excludes sale items, which is unclear.
- There is no deadline or end date anywhere, so "Black Friday" is the only urgency.
- The tiles have no product names, prices or per-item discounts, so "up to 40%" is never made concrete.
- The "Shop Sale" button is repeated twice, and the member block has two competing buttons. The copy is generic.
- The 30% member block is the same red creative as 11-20, 11-21 and 11-22, so it feels stale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around a saturated, high-contrast image with bold type, and add a deadline such as "Ends Monday".
- 2. Reconcile the two offers in one line, for example "Sale up to 40% for everyone. Members take 30% off full-price gear over $250." Then say clearly whether the two stack.
- 3. Put real products with prices in the tiles, for example "Nuptse Jacket $229 (was $330)", so the 40% feels concrete.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop Up to 40% Off Jackets" instead of "Shop Sale", and keep one button per block.
- 5. Refresh the member block creative so it differs from the previous three days, or drop it from this send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday jacket deals are live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best sellers, new arrivals and holiday exclusives, plus 30% off for XPLR Pass members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters on puffers, fleece and kids' gear. Free shipping for members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e.thenorthface.com`, `image.e.thenorthface.com`). The visible markup has several malformed CSS and table-structure defects. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Honeypot link:** the first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im…"><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`.
  - It is a hidden trap link, probably used to detect bots and link scanners. Security scanners that auto-follow links can trigger it, which would produce false click and engagement data.
  - The `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. The QA check flagged this, and it's the only confirmed accessibility failure in the data.
- **Unverified links:** 40 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 7,566 of 9,130 characters (about 83%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This is probably long `qs=` redirect strings. It's a minor spam-scoring risk, and the plain-text part may need shorter or fewer links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
All of these are in the visible `<head>` and `<body>` source:
- **Invalid body style:** `style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', …, Arial, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;"` is missing the `;` after `sans-serif`. Clients will likely drop the `margin: 0; padding: 0;` declarations.
- **Malformed CSS rule:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', 'Helvetica Neue Medium', … }` has no property name, such as `font-family:`. The whole rule is invalid, so the intended table and cell font stack is never applied. `h1–h6` has the same problem, a font list with no property name before `!important`.
- **Unclosed `@media` block:** in the `<!--[if false]><!-->` style block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) { … }` is missing its closing `}` before `</style>`. Parsers may discard the mobile rules `.showMobile`, `*[class].hide` and `*[class*="show"]`, or apply them inconsistently.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` is followed directly by `<!-- Begin Preheader Block --> <table id="backgroundWrapper" …>`, with no `<tr><td>` between them. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, `border`, `cellpadding` and `cellspacing`. Browsers and clients will hoist or reparent the inner table unpredictably.
- **Conditional comments:** `<!--[if false]><!-->` and `<!--[if true]>` are non-standard patterns, because the standard forms are `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and `<!--[if mso]>`. Outlook and non-Outlook clients may therefore get the wrong stylesheets.
- **Other markup:** `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag are present. Many nested tables carry `role="presentation"`, but the outer table does not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated source. The rest of the body wasn't available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, and it shouldn't be treated as a sender defect without checking the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool expected this absence via the relay.
- **Footer:** the truncated HTML doesn't include the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification couldn't be checked.
- **From address:** `reply@e.thenorthface.com`, which is a subdomain of the brand domain. That is consistent with the click and image domains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All visible links go through `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…`, an opaque redirect. The destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not exposed in the source or probed. The landing-page match to the "Black Friday: Save up to 40%" offer can't be assessed from the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `body` inline style by adding the missing `;` after `sans-serif`.
2. Add the missing `font-family:` property to the `table, td` and `h1–h6` rules.
3. Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block with `}` so the mobile rules apply reliably.
4. Wrap the inner `backgroundWrapper` table in a `<tr><td>` inside the outer table, or remove the redundant outer table. Add `role="presentation"` and the other table attributes.
5. Replace the `[if false]` and `[if true]` conditional comments with the standard `[if !mso]` and `[if mso]` forms.
6. Add `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`. Also confirm that scanner or bot hits on the honeypot link are filtered out of click metrics.
7. Check the raw MIME headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy. Check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address from the full HTML.
8. Resolve a sample of the `click.e.thenorthface.com` links with a redirect-following probe. Confirm they return 200 and carry UTM parameters.
9. Review whether the plain-text part needs its tracking URLs shortened.
