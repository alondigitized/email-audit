# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- TNF is leading with their loyalty push hard — an XPLR Pass modal is literally the first thing I see, stacked on top of a pretty solid alpine hero. As an active XPLR member, this whole screen is misfiring at me personally, but the underlying brand signals (rugged terrain, technical layering jacket) are exactly my aesthetic. Right now it's less "homepage" and more "sign-up interstitial."

## What's working

- **Top promo strip** — "$20 off $200 with XPLR + SMS opt-in" is a genuinely useful hook; $200 AOV is reachable when I'm restocking Summit Series layers.
- **Hero imagery** — the blue technical jacket against rocky alpine terrain is 100% my visual language; this is Summit Series energy.
- **XPLR modal CTA** — the "Join Now" button is oversized, high-contrast, impossible to miss — good execution even if the targeting is off.
- **Clean layout** — hamburger nav, cart, search — no clutter, the hierarchy even under the modal is tidy.

## What's weak

- **Modal as the entire above-the-fold experience** — I can't see the actual homepage; I'm just staring at an XPLR acquisition play. Already a member. This is friction, not welcome.
- **Male-only imagery** — the hiker is male. I'm a 31-year-old woman who spends real money on Summit Series. Show me someone who looks like me in a Nuptse or Denali.
- **No campaign narrative visible** — there's no seasonal context, no drop announcement, no PFC-free callout, nothing that tells me *what TNF is about right now*. The brand is invisible behind the modal.
- **No nav shortcut to my category** — hamburger-only on mobile means my path to Women's Summit Series is an extra tap away before I've even seen any product.
- **Zero trust signals** — no review counts, no sustainability badge, nothing that differentiates TNF from any other DTC modal.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or delay the XPLR modal for recognized members** — if I'm logged in or cookied as an XPLR member, show me my points balance and a "member deal" instead of a join prompt. One personalization rule, big loyalty payoff.
- **Swap the hero model or go lifestyle-mixed** — shoot a female athlete in the same alpine scene, or carousel between genders; a single male-only hero above the fold is a passive signal that this isn't for me.
- **Surface a campaign headline on the hero** — even a two-word overlay like "Summit Season" or the specific collection name tells me what to explore next. Right now the image is voiceless.
- **Add a PFC-free or sustainability badge to the top strip** — one line next to the XPLR offer. Costs nothing, speaks directly to why I choose TNF over Patagonia on a given purchase.
