# Members, take 35% off your order when you spend $250+
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-structured member send in the run, mostly because it adds a real new hook. The hero is now olive green instead of the red used on 11-20 through 11-26. The headline "Spend $250+ / Get 35% Off" is unchanged, but "+ Unlock a Borealis Backpack for $39" gives the offer a tangible reward. The backpack image sits directly under it and shows exactly what you'd get. The "FOUR DAYS OF MEMBER EXCLUSIVES" eyebrow and the red "TODAY ONLY" tag add urgency.
- The weak point is the body. After the hero, the email becomes a long, uniform grid of jackets with white-on-photo labels, and nothing in it ties back to the offer. The gear is all down jackets and parkas, which is a different category from the Borealis backpack the hero pushes. The product labels are hard to read over the photos, and the two promo tiles at the bottom ("Save 50% on Holiday Exclusives", "Up to 50% Off Select Styles") contradict the 35% headline.
- Judgement: a strong hero with a sprawling, mixed-message body. It's a solid send but not a standout, because it is the sixth member-event email in eight days.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer clearly: "Spend $250+ / Get 35% Off" in large white type on solid olive.
- The Borealis backpack for $39 is a concrete reward and gives the usual member promo a new angle.
- "Four days of member exclusives" and "TODAY ONLY" create credible time pressure.
- The black "Shop Now" button sits on its own in the hero and is easy to find.
- The category CTAs ("Shop Women's / Men's / Kids' Best Sellers") break the grid into sections with a clear next step each.
- The XPLR Pass footer reminds members of free shipping and points.

## What's weak

- White product labels sit over light photos, such as the Arctic Parka and Aconcagua tiles, and are hard to read. The labels also sit very close to the image edges.
- The "Shop Now" button is generic, and the fine print under it is tiny and low-contrast.
- The 50% tiles at the bottom ("Save 50% on Holiday Exclusives", "Up to 50% Off Select Styles") are small and compete with the 35% hero offer.
- The body is a long run of jackets with no prices, ratings or reviews. The backpack hero is never connected to the product grid.
- Nothing differentiates this from the earlier member sends in sequence and offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the body to the offer. Add a short "Add the Borealis for $39" module with a button after the first product row, or cut the grid to two rows. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Put the labels on a solid strip under each image, or darken the photo bottoms, so the names are readable.
- 3. Change "Shop Now" to "Shop Member Event" or "Claim 35% Off".
- 4. Resolve the 35% vs 50% conflict. Drop the bottom tiles or label them as separate, non-member offers.
- 5. Add prices or "from $X" on at least the hero jackets, plus a star rating.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: $39 Borealis when you spend $250`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four days of member exclusives. Your backpack deal ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Unlock the Borealis for $39 with any $250+ order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard SFMC-built template (`click.e.thenorthface.com` / `image.e.thenorthface.com`) with no hard failures: the QA run found 0 issues and 4 warnings. Real code-level defects in the visible markup are invalid table nesting, malformed CSS, and an empty hidden tracking link. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 46 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and final status codes are unverified.
- **Opaque tracking tokens:** Links use `https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im...` tokens, so UTM parameters can't be inspected from the source.
- **Honeypot link:** The first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7ImQi..."><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`.
  - This is a bot-click trap. It is harmless to recipients, but the anchor has no visible content or accessible name.
  - Only the `<img>` is hidden, so the `<a>` remains a keyboard tab stop and can be announced as an empty link.
  - Hiding the anchor itself would fix this.
- **Plain-text part:** 8653 of 10267 characters (about 84%) are URLs, mostly long `?qs=` tokens. This is a deliverability and spam-scoring risk, but it is inherent to ESP link wrapping.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid nesting:**
  - `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" ...>` contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` directly, with no `<tr><td>` between them. This is invalid HTML.
  - Browsers foster-parent the inner table out of the outer one, and Outlook's Word engine may handle it differently.
  - The outer table also has no `role="presentation"`, and neither does the inner `<table> <tr> <td>` around the preheader. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Malformed inline style:** The `<body>` style reads `font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;`. The missing semicolon invalidates the whole `font-family` declaration, so the body loses its font stack. The inline `margin: 0` is lost too, though the `<style>` block's `margin: 0 auto !important` still applies.
- **Malformed CSS rules:**
  - `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... }` has no `font-family:` property, so the rule does nothing.
  - The `h1–h6` rule has the same flaw. Its `margin: 0px` is likely unaffected.
- **Unclosed media query:** The second `<style>` block's `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed. Browsers auto-close it at `</style>`, but stricter clients may drop the block.
- **Outlook width:** `max-width: 600px` is set on `#backgroundWrapper` and `<td>` with `width="100%"`. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, so it may render full width unless a fixed-width MSO ghost table exists later in the truncated source.
- **Missing alt text:** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt` attribute (QA warning confirmed). Use `alt=""`.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, and a hidden preheader div.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and subject line contain no unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The source is truncated, so the rest of the email is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither was found. The QA tool attributes this to AgentMail relay capture, so this is unconfirmed and not a verified sender defect. Verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain `e.thenorthface.com` can't be confirmed against the relay.
- **Footer:** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Links use opaque `qs=` tokens and were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment with the "35% off $250+" and "Daily Drop Borealis Backpack $39" offers can't be confirmed. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) in the raw original message rather than the relay copy. If they are absent there, add them.
2. Repair the markup:
   - Wrap `#backgroundWrapper` in a `<tr><td>` of the outer table.
   - Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
   - Add the missing semicolon after `sans-serif` in the body style.
   - Add the missing `font-family:` property to the `table, td` and heading rules.
   - Close the 480px `@media` block.
3. Hide the honeypot anchor itself (`display:none` or `aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1"`) and set `alt=""` on the pixel.
4. Resolve a sample of the 46 tracking links with a real-browser or redirect-following probe. Confirm the final URLs and UTMs.
5. Add an MSO conditional fixed-width (600px) wrapper if one is not already in the truncated portion.
6. Re-run the audit against the untruncated HTML and original headers to cover the footer, address, merge tokens and authentication status.
