# Borealis—our best-selling collection every year
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-product-line catalog spread — every Borealis backpack/bag variant, one after another, color swatches and all. It's the collection-deep-dive cousin of the August 6/7/10 backpack-promo run, except this time there's no discount attached to the hero product; the only offer ("New Sale Styles + Up to 30% Off") is buried at the very bottom as an afterthought banner unrelated to Borealis itself.
- The subject line — "Borealis—our best-selling collection every year" — is honest and specific about the product, which is a plus, but it's copy that reads like an internal merchandising note, not something written to earn an open. Compare to August 10's "Ends today: Spend $150+, get 20% off," which had urgency and a number; this has neither.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand, no render bugs, good product photography, clear repeated CTA pairing (Backpacks/Bags) — but it's a long, undifferentiated scroll of eight near-identical backpacks with no hierarchy telling you which one to care about. It reads more like a PDP catalog dump than a marketing email built to convert.

## What's working

- Zero render bugs — every product image, swatch row, and price-adjacent copy block renders cleanly.
- Consistent, on-brand black/white typography and layout throughout the scroll.
- Backpacks/Bags CTA buttons repeat at top and bottom, giving two clear exit points regardless of scroll depth.
- Lifestyle hero shot (model wearing the pack) at the top anchors the collection contextually before the product grid starts.
- XPLR Pass loyalty banner and free shipping are visible at both header and footer for member reinforcement.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or offer tied to any Borealis product — the only offer (30% off sale styles) appears at the very bottom, disconnected from everything above it.
- Eight backpacks in a row with no differentiation cues (best seller badge, "new," price) — pure fatigue by product #5 or #6.
- No visible pricing anywhere in the email, which is a real friction point for a bag purchase decision.
- No social proof — no star ratings or review counts, unlike the August 14 best-sellers send which did include them.
- Subject line has zero urgency or numeric hook, weak against inbox competition.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or bundle offer to the hero product and lead with it — right now there's no reason to click today versus any other day. Pull the 30% off banner up near the top, or scope it explicitly to Borealis.
- 2. Cut the product list from 8 to 3-4 with a "most popular" or "best seller" badge on 1-2, so the eye has somewhere to land instead of scroll fatigue.
- 3. Add star ratings/review counts under at least the top 2-3 products — the August 14 send proved this pattern works for this brand.
- 4. Tighten the subject line with a concrete hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Borealis backpacks: new colors, same bestseller`
- **Subject Alt B:** `8 ways to carry Borealis this fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh colorways just dropped on our #1 pack`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Campus-ready packs, totes & bottle holders — shop the lineup`
