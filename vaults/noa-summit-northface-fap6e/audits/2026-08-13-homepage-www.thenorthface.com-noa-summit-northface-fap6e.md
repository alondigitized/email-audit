# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open this expecting the Summit Series drop calendar and instead I get a yearbook. This is very clearly a Back to Campus push — collage grid of college kids clutching backpacks and hoodies, "New year. Fresh gear." It's a well-produced page for a brand that knows its aesthetic, but right now it's talking to a 19-year-old freshman, not a 31-year-old who already owns three Nuptse jackets.

## What's working

- The **XPLR Pass banner** at the very top ("Join XPLR Pass and get 10% off your next purchase") — I'm already a member, so it's nice that it's front and center even if it's not personalized to me.
- **"Shop Women's" / "Shop Men's"** buttons are clean, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- The **photo collage grid** is genuinely well shot — good energy, on-brand casual outdoor styling, no stock-photo stiffness.
- Nav bar has **Footwear** and **Bags & Gear** as their own tabs, so my usual categories are one tap away.

## What's weak

- The entire hero is a **"Back to Campus" / "Class of 26" concept** — yearbook superlatives like "Most Likely to Succeed" and "Best Dressed." I am not shopping for a dorm room.
- **Every model in the collage reads college-age.** Not a single lifestyle shot suggests someone my age doing an actual Summit Series thing — no alpine, no trail, no "I've been buying this brand for a decade" energy.
- The **20% Off popup** is restricted to students and teachers — it interrupts my scroll with an offer I literally cannot use.
- That popup also **overlaps the category strip below it**, cutting off the third button (reads "Backpa—" and stops).
- No mention of **PFC-free / sustainability messaging** anywhere above the fold, which is usually a big reason I stick with this brand over cheaper competitors.

## Recommendations

- Swap the "Back to Campus" collage hero for a rotating/segmented hero — show a technical layering or alpine lifestyle shot to anyone who isn't in the 18–22 age bracket based on browsing history.
- Move or dismiss the student/teacher popup for logged-in XPLR Pass members — don't lead with an offer I'm ineligible for.
- Turn the generic "Join XPLR Pass" banner into a **recognized-member** version for people already enrolled ("Noa, you have $X in rewards" or similar).
- Add a one-line PFC-free / sustainability callout near the top nav — it's a real differentiator for me and it's completely absent right now.
