# Early Black Friday savings on fleece are here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, offer-led fleece send. "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusive Fleece" sits right under a stacked-fleece hero, with a "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS" eyebrow above it. It is a near-repeat of the 11-13 "Black Friday Early Access: Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" send, now narrowed to one category. That narrowing helps, because the offer and the product now match.
- The body is a long, even grid of men's, women's, kids' and baby fleece. Every product gets equal weight, so nothing stands out as the hero. There are no prices, no sale markers and no reviews. A shopper sees "30% off" once at the top and then has to take it on faith. Both "Shop Exclusives" buttons are clear, but they sit at the very top and the very bottom of a long scroll.
- It's a competent, honest send, and it's stronger than the 11-14 gift catalog. It would be better with product-level price proof and a stated end date, and with less repetition of the 11-13 offer.

## What's working

- The offer is concrete and sits above the first button: "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusive Fleece."
- The stacked-fleece hero shows the category right away, and the texture reads as soft and warm.
- The "Shop Exclusives" button is dark, high-contrast and unambiguous, and it appears twice.
- The product grid is clean and organized, with large-then-two-up rows by gender and kids. Product names are specific, such as "Women's Cragmont Fleece Coat."
- The XPLR Pass loyalty banner at the top and the loyalty block in the footer both stay visible.

## What's weak

- No prices or "was/now" figures appear anywhere, so the 30% isn't shown on any product.
- No end date or deadline is visible, so the urgency is only the "early access" wording.
- Nothing is badged "Exclusive," even though the offer is for "Holiday Exclusives." A shopper can't tell which items qualify.
- The email is very long. Every product has equal weight, and the vests and kids' items dilute the fleece focus.
- The XPLR Pass strip is tiny and gray at the top and competes with the offer. The "15% off your next purchase" line works against a 30% offer that is already live.
- Product tiles carry no reviews, ratings or color and size cues.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on every tile, showing the original price struck through next to the 30% price, and add an "Exclusive" badge to each qualifying item. This would lift click-through more than any other change.
- 2. State the deadline in the hero, for example "Early access ends Sunday at midnight."
- 3. Cut the grid to 6 or 8 products and lead with the best seller, adding a star rating to it.
- 4. Move the XPLR Pass 15% line below the main offer, or fold it in as "Members: extra shipping perks." Right now it competes with the headline.
- 5. Add a second CTA in the middle of the email, after the women's block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off fleece, early access ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early access: 30% off Cragmont fleece`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Exclusive styles for men, women and kids, before Black Friday opens to everyone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft, warm and 30% off. Shop the Cragmont and Cabin fleece first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with several CSS and table-structure defects in the visible source, plus a hidden honeypot link. The compliance and authentication warnings are unverified because the AgentMail relay may have stripped those headers. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and most link checks could not be done.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links:** All 41 links use the `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…` redirect, and the QA probe skipped them. Destination status and landing URLs are unverified.
- **Honeypot link:** The first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im…"><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`. This is a deliberate spam-trap link. Some filters treat hidden links as a negative signal.
- **Plain-text part:** 7,755 of 9,322 characters (about 83%) are URLs. The long `qs=` tokens inflate the text part and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid body style:** `<body style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', …, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">` has no semicolon after `sans-serif`. The browser likely drops the whole declaration, so the font-family, margin and padding all fail to apply.
- **Broken CSS rule:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', 'Helvetica Neue Medium', … sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property, so the rule is invalid.
- **Unclosed media query:** In the second `<style>` block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed before `</style>`. The mobile `.showMobile` and `*[class*="show"]` rules are at risk in clients that don't auto-close.
- **Invalid table nesting:** The outer `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` directly, with no `<tr><td>` between them. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding`, `cellspacing` and `border`, so it gets default spacing and may be announced as a data table. The inner `<table> <tr> <td>` around the preheader has the same missing attributes.
- **Honeypot accessibility:** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`, as QA flagged. Because it is wrapped in a focusable `<a>` with only a `display:none` child, it is an empty link with no accessible name. Keyboard and screen-reader users can land on it.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `<title>`, and a `[if gte mso 9]` Outlook DPI block are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source, though it is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA notes AgentMail may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.thenorthface.com` matches the tracking and image subdomains, which is consistent but not proof.
- **Footer and unsubscribe link:** The unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The `qs=` redirects hide the landing URLs and any UTM parameters, and the probe skipped them. The visible source gives no evidence of UTM presence or absence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add the missing semicolon in the body `style` and add `font-family:` to the `table, td` rule.
2. Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block in the second `<style>` and wrap the outer table's content in `<tr><td>`.
3. Add `role="presentation" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0"` to the outer and preheader tables.
4. Give the honeypot link `tabindex="-1" aria-hidden="true"` and `alt=""` on the image. Move it out of the focus order, or place it off-screen rather than inside an anchor.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` from the original sender to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the redirect chains by following one or two `click.e.thenorthface.com` links to check status codes, final URLs and UTM carry-through.
7. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the footer, the unsubscribe link and the physical address.
