# Member Holiday Event exclusive savings are here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-structured North Face send of the week. A saturated red hero leads with "Member Holiday Event / Spend $250+ / Get 30% Off" and a white "Shop Now" button. It builds on the offer-led 11-17 "Save 30% on exclusive jacket styles" send and the 11-13 and 11-15 Black Friday Early Access sends. The new element is the XPLR Pass member gate.
- After the hero, the email becomes a 2x4 grid of jackets plus a kids row, with no prices, no reasons to choose one over another, and white captions sitting on pale backgrounds. The brief is clear, but the body does little to sell anything.
- The offer also has a mixed message. The top bar promises 15% off for joining XPLR Pass, the hero says 30% off with a $250 spend, and the footer banners say "Save 30%" and "Up to 40% off." A subscriber has to work out which discount applies to them. Verdict: a solid, expected holiday send that is more offer-confusing than the 11-17 email.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: the red block and the large "Get 30% Off" fill the first screen, and the Shop Now button is clearly visible.
- The member framing ("Member Holiday Event", the XPLR Pass badge) gives the offer a reason to exist, and a loyalty program is visibly present.
- The jacket grid is clean and consistent. Models are photographed in a uniform style, and the range covers women, men, girls, boys and kids.
- The email fits the season. Down parkas and the holiday and Black Friday framing match the 11-13 to 11-17 run.
- The footer carries clear secondary paths: store locator, app, resale, and the XPLR Pass loyalty banner.

## What's weak

- The white product captions ("Women's Hydrenalite Down Hoodie", and so on) sit on pale gradient backgrounds. They are hard to read, especially on the cream and grey tiles.
- The grid tiles show no prices, no sale marks and no "30% off" cues, so the discount disappears right after the hero.
- The offer messaging is inconsistent. The top bar says 15%, the hero says 30% with a $250 minimum, and the bottom banners say 30% and "Up to 40%."
- The terms are buried. "Exclusions and terms apply" is tiny text under the hero button, and $250 is a meaningful threshold for kids' jackets.
- CTA copy is generic. There are two identical "Shop Now" buttons, and nothing for a specific category.
- There is no deadline or event end date, so nothing makes the member event feel urgent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the member price on every product tile, for example "$X → $Y with XPLR Pass." This is the single biggest lift, because the grid would then sell the offer instead of just showing jackets.
- 2. Resolve the offer hierarchy. Pick one headline discount and drop the 15% join bar and the "Up to 40%" banner, or clearly label each as a different tier.
- 3. Fix caption legibility with dark text or a gradient scrim behind white text.
- 4. Add an end date to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 5. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop Women's Jackets" and "Shop Men's Jackets."
- 6. Pull the terms out of the 8px text. State "30% off full-price gear when you spend $250+" in the hero body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off full-price gear for XPLR Pass members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 30% off $250+ ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Down parkas, fleece and kids' jackets included. Not a member yet? Join free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $250, save 30% on new arrivals and best sellers. Exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: The North Face, "Member Holiday Event exclusive savings are here"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e.thenorthface.com`, `image.e.thenorthface.com`) with several CSS and table-markup defects in the visible source. The automated QA flagged 4 warnings and 0 failures. The compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated because the relay stripped the headers, and the HTML provided is truncated. Both gaps limit this audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 47 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status are unconfirmed, so no broken-link claim can be made.
- **Honeypot link:** `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Imci…">` wraps `honeypot_pixel.png` (1×1, `display: none`) as a bot-detection trap. This is deliberate and not a defect, but it is a tracked link that real users never see. Keep it excluded from click-rate reporting.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 8889 of 10487 characters (about 85%) are URLs. The long `?qs=` click-tracking URLs dominate the text/plain alternative and make it hard to read. Spam filters may weigh this negatively.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
Confirmed in the visible source:
- **Invalid CSS declaration:** `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', 'Helvetica Neue Medium', … Arial, sans-serif; }` is missing the `font-family:` property. The rule is dropped by all clients. The same font stack in `h1…h6` is also malformed, though its `!important; margin: 0px;` may partly parse.
- **Missing semicolon in the inline body style:** `style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;"`. There is no `;` after `sans-serif`, so the font-family declaration is invalid and `margin`/`padding` are likely swallowed. The head `<style>` `body` rule partly compensates for the margin and padding.
- **Unclosed `@media` block:** in the second `<style>` block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed with `}` before `</style>`. Parsers may drop the rules or tolerate them, depending on the client. This block holds the `.showMobile`, `.hide` and `*[class*="show"]` mobile rules.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` directly contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` with no `<tr><td>` wrapper. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding` and `cellspacing`. Outlook and some webmail clients may re-parent or add spacing. The inner `<table><tr><td>` after `camarker-inner` also lacks `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce it as a data table.
- **Missing alt:** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`. Because it is `display:none` it is not in the accessibility tree, so the real impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would still clear the warning.
- **Conditional comments:** `<!--[if false]><!-->` and `<!--[if true]>` are used atypically. `[if false]` is not recognized by any client and is not equivalent to the standard `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` pattern. Behavior across clients is unpredictable.
- `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, the `<title>`, and the VML/Office namespaces are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear. Tokens in the truncated portion cannot be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. Treat this as **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud usually supports it.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer is in the truncated portion, so its presence is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 47 tracked links were not resolved, and the visible HTML contains only opaque `?qs=` tokens. The UTM parameters and landing pages are encoded in the redirect and were not verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the CSS:** add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and heading rules, add `;` after `sans-serif` in the body inline style, and close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
2. **Fix the table structure:** wrap `#backgroundWrapper` in `<tr><td>` or remove the outer table, and add `role="presentation"` to every layout table.
3. **Replace `<!--[if false]><!-->`** with the standard `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` form.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to `honeypot_pixel.png`.
5. **Shorten the plain-text version** so the URL ratio drops, for example by using shorter link wrappers or fewer links.
6. **Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` from the sending ESP:**
   - Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
   - Confirm the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
   - Resolve the redirect chains of the tracked links to check UTMs and destination status.
   - Check the untruncated HTML for merge tokens and the footer.
