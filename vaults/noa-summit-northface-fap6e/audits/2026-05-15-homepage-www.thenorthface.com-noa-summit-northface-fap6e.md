# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened TNF.com and immediately got wall-checked by a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" anti-bot gate. That's the dominant above-the-fold experience right now. Behind it I can make out what looks like a dramatic basalt-column landscape hero and a Memorial Day Sale banner — the brand looks like it's in sale mode for the long weekend. But the bot check is eating the whole hero, so any actual content strategy is invisible to me on first load.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale promo strip** — "Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25. Shop Sale" is the one thing that loads clearly and it's exactly the kind of seasonal drop I track. I'd actually use this.
- **Promo banner deadline** — "Ends 5.25" is a concrete, credible date that creates real urgency without being fake-countdown manipulation.
- **Branding consistency** — the TNF half-dome logo and clean nav bar are crisp and immediately recognizable. No brand confusion even mid-bot-check.
- **Partial hero landscape** — what I *can* see behind the overlay looks like a dramatic geological formation (looks like basalt columns?), which reads as legit outdoor/adventure and not generic sportswear.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check modal obliterates the above-the-fold experience** — "BEFORE WE CONTINUE... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" is the first full sentence I read on the page. That's a brutal first impression for a brand that wants to feel premium.
- **Zero XPLR Pass / loyalty visibility** — as a member, I want to see my benefits or member pricing immediately. Nothing. The bot check might be eating it, but it's still not visible.
- **No category nav exposed on mobile** — it's a hamburger menu, meaning I can't see Women's / Summit Series / Nuptse without an extra tap. For someone who knows exactly what she came for, that's friction.
- **"Up to 25% off" without a floor** — that "up to" phrasing always makes me wonder if it's 5% on the good stuff I actually want.
- **Hero campaign context completely blocked** — I can barely see "MEMORIAL DAY SALE | ENDS 5.25" text ghosted behind the modal. The whole hero investment is wasted.

## Recommendations

- **Serve the bot-check as a lightweight interstitial before the full page loads, not a blocking modal on top of it** — let the hero render behind it so there's something worth returning to after I confirm.
- **Pin an XPLR Pass member badge or "Welcome back, Noa — you have X points" strip to the top bar** — returning logged-in members should feel recognized before they even see the hero.
- **Expose Women's / Summit Series as quick-tap chips below the nav bar on mobile** — don't make me open a hamburger to find my category. Two or three pill buttons ("Women's," "Summit Series," "Sale") would cut one tap.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the promo banner** — "Up to 25% off + free shipping over $X. Ends 5.25." Two signals in one strip.
