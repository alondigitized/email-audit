# Last chance to get 20% off $150+.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing send in a six-email Memorial Day sprint — and it shows. The subject ("Last chance to get 20% off $150+") primes the reader for a shopping action, but the primary hero CTA is "Join Now" to enroll in the XPLR Pass loyalty program. The discount is the reward for joining, not for shopping, and the email doesn't telegraph that until you're already inside. The gap between what the subject promises and what the CTA delivers is the single biggest problem here.
- Structurally the email is clean: the dark hero renders sharply, the urgency markers ("MEMBER EXCLUSIVE | ENDS TODAY" and "MEMORIAL DAY SALE | ENDS 5.25") are placed correctly, and a fallback "Shop Sale" block exists below the fold for anyone who doesn't want to join. But that fallback is buried under a model shot that carries no copy, meaning a reader has to scroll past a silent image to find the simpler shopping CTA.
- The May 23 send ("XPLR Pass exclusive: Spend $150+ & get 20% off") ran this exact loyalty-recruitment hero. Yesterday's send ran the same offer again. By the third iteration on the same creative in four days, this email is selling to a decided audience — those who wanted to join already did, and those who didn't have heard the pitch twice. "Last chance" urgency on a stale offer lands flat.

## What's working

- The dark hero block creates strong visual contrast and immediately communicates the 20% off / $150+ mechanic — no hunting for the deal.
- "ENDS TODAY" and "ENDS 5.25" are both present and clearly placed — urgency is unambiguous.
- Subject line is 33 characters — clean, scannable on mobile, no spam signals.
- The two-block structure gives members and non-members separate paths ("Join Now" vs. "Shop Sale"), which is a reasonable architecture even if execution undercuts it.
- No broken images, no text overlap, no layout issues — clean render.

## What's weak

- Subject-to-CTA mismatch: "Last chance to get 20% off $150+" implies shop-and-save, but landing in the email requires loyalty enrollment first — unannounced friction.
- The preheader ("Join XPLR Pass™ Loyalty Program | Free Shipping. Points for Rewards.") tips off the loyalty ask before the open, which can suppress clicks from non-members who've already decided against joining.
- The middle block — a full-width model in a jacket — carries zero copy and zero CTA. It's a scroll tax between the two functional blocks.
- The fine print beneath "Join Now" is unreadably small at mobile scale; exclusions are invisible, which will erode trust on redemption.
- This is the third loyalty-recruitment send using the same $150+/20%-off offer in four days; audience fatigue is not a speculation, it's a certainty by this point in the series.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the block order.** Put "Up to 25% Off Select Gear / Shop Sale" above the fold. The subject primes a shopping action — deliver it first. Keep the loyalty pitch as a secondary block for the non-member who hasn't joined. This closes the subject-to-hero gap and reduces friction for the majority of your list.
- 2. **Rewrite the preheader to match the urgency frame, not the loyalty frame.** The current preheader contradicts the subject's deal tone. Replace it with something that reinforces the sale deadline.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Memorial Day sale ends tonight — shop activewear, trail shoes, and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours: up to 25% off gear before the sale closes at midnight.`
- 3. **Swap "Join Now" for benefit-forward copy.** "Join Now" is institutional. "Unlock 20% Off" or "Claim Member Pricing" frames the same action as a gain, not a task.
- 4. **Kill the silent image block.** The model photo between the two CTAs has no copy, no link, no purpose. Either add a product name + "Shop Now" link directly on the image, or remove it and let the two functional blocks stack tightly.
- 5. **Subject line alternatives for a true "last chance" send:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: Memorial Day sale ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends today — up to 25% off before it's gone`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — The North Face "Last Chance 20% Off" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has two confirmed CSS authoring bugs that silently break font rendering across all clients, plus missing compliance headers that will fail strict inbox provider checks (Gmail, Yahoo). All other issues are warnings pending full-source or header access.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Issue: 27 click-tracking redirects unprobed**
QA skipped HTTP-level verification for all 27 links routed through the `e.thenorthface.com` click-redirect domain. Broken destination URLs, destination 404s, or misconfigured redirect chains cannot be ruled out without probing.

**Issue: Plain-text body is 78% URLs**
QA reports 5,461 of 6,978 plain-text characters are URLs. This is a strong spam-filter signal (Spamassassin `URI_RATIO` rules). The plain-text part was almost certainly auto-generated from HTML without stripping tracking URLs — a common ESP misconfiguration.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Bug: Malformed `font-family` declarations (silent fail)**
Two CSS rules are missing the property name entirely — they contain only the value:

```css
/* table, td rule */
table, td {
  'HelveticaNeue-Medium', 'Helvetica Neue Medium', ...;  /* ← missing font-family: */
}

/* heading rule */
h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 {
  'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ...  /* ← missing font-family: */
}
```

Both rules are dropped by all CSS parsers. Fallback fonts from parent/body apply, but the intent is broken.

**Bug: Malformed `body` style attribute**
```html
<body style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium'... sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">
```
Missing semicolon after the `font-family` value — `margin` and `padding` are part of the font-family string, not separate declarations. Both resets are ignored.

**Warning: `align-content` used in table-based layout**
`.disclaimer` and `.showMobile` use `align-content: center !important`. This is a flexbox/grid property; it has no effect in table-based email layouts and is ignored by all major email clients.

**Warning: Spacer image missing `alt` attribute**
```
src: https://image.e.thenorthface.com/lib/fe3811717164047a741772/m/1/TNF-spacer-100px_01.jpg
```
Should be `alt=""` (empty string) to satisfy screen reader passthrough and avoid readers announcing the filename. Currently has no `alt` attribute at all.

**Minor: Unclosed `@media` block (truncation — unconfirmed)**
The `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)` block inside the `<!--[if false]><!-->` section appears to be missing its closing `}` in the truncated source. If confirmed in the full source, this invalidates all subsequent CSS rules in that `<style>` block for Gmail/Apple Mail.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge token syntax (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) is visible in the truncated source. Preheader text ("Join XPLR Pass to save.") is intentionally static.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Fail: `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Yahoo require this for bulk senders (>5k/day) as of Feb 2024. Absence increases spam folder placement risk and violates sender guidelines at scale.

**Fail: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe (required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance) depends on `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Not present per QA.

**Warning: Authentication-Results unverifiable**
The AgentMail relay did not surface an `Authentication-Results` header, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. If `reply@e.thenorthface.com` is not aligned with the DMARC policy on `thenorthface.com`, delivery will degrade.

**Cannot confirm: CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link**
Source is truncated — footer content including physical address and unsubscribe link is not visible. These must be present for CAN-SPAM compliance; flag for manual footer check.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Cannot confirm: UTM coverage on 27 tracked links**
All 27 destination URLs route through `e.thenorthface.com` redirects and were not probed. UTM parameter presence (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) on final landing pages is unverifiable. If click-redirect strips or rewrites params, attribution breaks.

**Cannot confirm: Offer-to-landing-page alignment**
Subject line and preheader reference "20% off $150+" and "XPLR Pass." Whether the CTA landing pages enforce the same offer conditions (minimum, loyalty gate, expiry) cannot be verified without probing the redirect destinations.
