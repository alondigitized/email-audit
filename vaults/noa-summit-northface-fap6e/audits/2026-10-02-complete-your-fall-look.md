# Complete your fall look
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, pretty, offer-free lookbook. The pink puffer hero and "Fall style from head to toe" headline set the mood well. The rest is four repeating blocks of lifestyle photo plus 2x2 product grid, closing on a British Millerain Capsule banner. It's tasteful and on-brand, but nothing tells me to act today. There's no price, no deadline and no reason beyond "it's fall."
- It's also a step back from the best recent sends. The 9/17 "Just in: The North Face X Aimé Leon Dore" email had a real reason to open. The 9/22 "Jackets for shoulder season" send had an editorial hook. This one is closer to the 9/28 "New patterns and colors for fall layers" catalog: a scroll of products with no argument. Two near-identical layer roundups in five days, with a third pitch (the Millerain capsule) tacked on the bottom, dilutes the message.
- Judgement: a competent brand-building send that will earn browsing but few clicks. It needs one hero, one reason and one price point.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: a clear lifestyle shot, a bold headline, and a single black "Women's Layers" button right under it.
- The product grids are clean and consistent, with named items (Nuketo Synthetic Insulated Jacket, Chabot 2.0 Mock-Neck, Never Stop Bucket Bag) on white backgrounds.
- The outfit-building idea (jacket, top, pants, hat, bag, shoes) matches the "complete your look" subject.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlapping text or layout gaps across the full scroll.
- The XPLR Pass banner at the top and bottom keeps loyalty visible without crowding the content.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer: no percentage, no dollar amount, no deadline. The free shipping in the XPLR Pass banner is a membership perk, not a reason to buy now.
- The email is about 9,700px tall, and the same photo-plus-grid pattern repeats four times. By the third block the eye glazes over.
- No prices, ratings or sizing cues appear on any product card, so there's nothing to help someone decide.
- There are three competing CTAs ("Women's Layers" twice, "Shop the Collection"), and the Millerain capsule at the bottom pulls attention to a different story.
- The subject is generic and gives no reason to open now. It could have been sent any week of fall.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero outfit and one reason. Lead with a single complete look, show its total price or a member price, and cut the grids from four to one or two.
- 2. Put a price on every product card, and add a "from $X" or member-price line to the hero so the XPLR Pass benefit becomes a concrete incentive.
- 3. Move the British Millerain Capsule to its own send. As the tail of this email it's buried and it dilutes the layers story.
- 4. Make the CTA copy a verb plus a noun, such as "Shop women's jackets" instead of "Women's Layers."
- 5. Space out the layering roundups. The 9/22, 9/28 and 10/2 sends are the same story in different clothes.

- **Subject Alt B:** `3 layers, 1 outfit: build it here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Puffer, fleece, and pants that work together. Members get free shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your jacket first. We've matched the rest.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: The North Face, "Complete your fall look"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud build (`click.e.` / `image.e.thenorthface.com`) with malformed CSS and table nesting in the visible source. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the AgentMail relay didn't expose them. The HTML is truncated, so findings cover only the head and preheader.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links.** QA skipped 53 links on `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…` (SFMC encoded tokens). Destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Honeypot link.** The first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABB7InYi…"><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`.
  - It is a hidden link, probably a bot trap. Security scanners that follow every link may register it as a click, which would inflate click metrics.
  - I can't confirm that intent from the source alone.
- **Plain-text part.** QA reports 9,333 of 10,969 characters (about 85%) are URLs, which is well over the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs dominate the text alternative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS declarations.**
  - `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property name, so the declaration is dropped.
  - The `h1…h6` rule has the same problem. Its `margin: 0px` still applies.
  - `<body style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … Arial, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">` is missing a semicolon after `sans-serif`. The inline `margin` and `padding` are invalid and ignored. The head rule `body { margin: 0 auto !important; padding: 0 !important }` partly masks this.
- **Unclosed `@media` block.** The second `<style>` block opens `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` and is never closed before `</style>`. Most clients recover, but stricter parsers may drop the rules.
- **Invalid table nesting.** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" style="width: 100%…">` directly contains another `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` with no `<tr>/<td>` wrapper. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding` and `cellspacing`. Clients repair this inconsistently.
- **Missing alt text.** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt` attribute (QA-confirmed). Impact is low because the image is `display:none`, but it is still a link-wrapped image with no accessible name.
- **Generic title.** `<title>The North Face</title>` doesn't describe the email.
- **Preheader.** The preheader div declares conflicting `font-size` (1px, then 11px) and `color` values. It stays hidden by `display:none`, so there is no visible impact.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, MSO/VML namespaces and the Outlook `OfficeDocumentSettings` block are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The body is truncated, so this is not verified for the rest of the email.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers (QA, unverified).**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA attributes these to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers, so I can't classify them as sender defects.
  - If the headers are genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe would be missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Footer.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be evaluated.
- **Sender alignment.** `reply@e.thenorthface.com` shares its subdomain with the click and image hosts. This is consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Every link is an encoded `?qs=` redirect, so no UTM parameters or final landing URLs are visible in the source. The links also weren't probed, so landing pages and any redirect chain are unknown. Only the redirect host is confirmed, and it sits on the sender's own subdomain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the CSS:**
   - Add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and `h1–h6` rules.
   - Add the missing `;` in the `<body>` inline style.
   - Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
2. **Repair the table structure.** Wrap the nested `#backgroundWrapper` in a `<tr><td>`, and add `role="presentation" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0"` to the outer table.
3. **Honeypot.** Add `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`. Filter its clicks from engagement reporting if it is a trap.
4. **Plain-text part.** Shorten the text alternative, for example by using cleaner short links in the text version.
5. **Re-run the audit on the raw `.eml`.** That would verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and show the footer unsubscribe link and address.
6. **Resolve a sample of the `click.e.` links** with a redirect-following probe. This would confirm final landing URLs and UTM presence.
