# Black Friday savings on mountain gear
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent member-event send that bumps the offer from the 30% of 11-20 through 11-22 to **35% off with $250+ spend**. The saturated red hero ("Member Holiday Event / Spend $250+ / Get 35% Off") puts the offer first, and a single "Shop Snow" button sits right under it. Below the hero the email is clean and product-led: women's, men's and kids' snow, each with a hero shot, a two-up of jackets and pants, and its own dark CTA.
- The weakness is mixed messaging. The hero says 35%, but the bottom banners say "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" and "Up to 40% Off Select Styles". That repeats the 11-24 and 11-23 sends, so three different percentages end up in one email. The subject is generic, too. It doesn't mention 35% or the member event, which is the strongest hook.
- Judgement: well-built and easy to scan, but it's the latest of seven sends in eight days. It leaves its best offer out of the subject and undercuts it with competing discounts further down.

## What's working

- The red hero leads with the offer, and "Get 35% Off" is the largest text on the page.
- The $250+ threshold and the "Member Holiday Event" framing give a loyalty angle.
- Each segment (women's, men's, kids') has its own big image and its own CTA, so the next click is obvious.
- The layout is clean: large hero image, then a two-up of jackets and pants. I see no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer carries the XPLR Pass loyalty strip, which keeps the member benefit visible.

## What's weak

- The 35% hero conflicts with the 30% and "up to 40%" banners at the bottom, so the offer reads as muddled.
- "Exclusions and terms apply" is tiny, low-contrast text under the CTA, and the $250 threshold is easy to overlook.
- Every CTA goes to a category ("Shop Snow", "Shop Women's Snow"), not to a specific product, and there is no price, review or sizing detail.
- There is no deadline. "Black Friday" implies urgency but nothing states when the offer ends.
- The long, three-segment scroll buries the secondary banners, which are the only place the Black Friday offer appears.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject carry the strongest hook (35% off with $250+) and drop the generic "mountain gear".
- 2. Reconcile the offers. Either remove the 30% and 40% banners, or label them clearly as separate sales.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Cyber Monday" or "Ends Nov 30".
- 4. Move the terms (the $250 threshold and exclusions) up to a legible size next to the CTA.
- 5. Add price or "from $X after 35% off" under the hero products, and link them to individual product pages.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: The North Face, "Black Friday savings on mountain gear"

The HTML source was truncated after the preheader, so findings cover only the head, the first wrapper tables and the QA output. Nothing below the preheader (body content, footer, unsubscribe link, physical address) could be inspected.

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has several CSS and table-markup defects. One is an invalid `font-family` declaration that also swallows `margin: 0`. The plain-text part is 83.5% URLs (7944/9513 characters). The QA warnings on headers and link probing are mostly relay or tooling limits, not confirmed sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Hidden honeypot link.** The first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im…" target="_blank"><img src="…/honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`.
  - Only the `<img>` is hidden. The anchor itself stays in the DOM and tab order as an empty link with no text and no `alt`. QA flagged the missing `alt`.
  - Hidden links can count against spam scoring. They can also make security scanners register a click.
- **Unverified links.** QA skipped 42 tracking links because they are on click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect-chain or status check was done. The links use opaque `?qs=` tokens, so UTM parameters cannot be read from the source.
- **Branded tracking domains.** `click.e.thenorthface.com` and `image.e.thenorthface.com` sit under the same `e.thenorthface.com` subdomain as the From address. No domain mismatch is visible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid body `font-family`.** The `<body>` inline style is `font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', …, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;`.
  - It is missing a semicolon after `sans-serif`, so the browser drops the whole `font-family` declaration and the `margin: 0` that follows it. Only `padding: 0` survives.
  - The `<style>` block's `body { margin: 0 auto !important }` limits the damage to the font stack.
- **Missing property name.** The `<style>` rules `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif; }` and the `h1…h6` rule have a font stack with no `font-family:` property name. The `table, td` rule is therefore empty. The `h1…h6` declaration is invalid and ignored, but its `margin: 0px` still applies.
- **Unclosed media query.** In the second `<style>` block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed before `</style>`. Browsers usually recover, but strict parsers may drop the mobile rules (`.showMobile`, `*[class].hide`, `*[class*="show"]`).
- **Invalid table nesting.** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" …>` contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` directly, with no `<tr><td>` between them.
- **Missing table attributes.** The outer table and the inner `<table><tr><td>` after `camarker-inner` have no `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding` or `cellspacing`, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Outlook width.** The 600px width is set only through `max-width: 600px` on the table and `td`. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, and no MSO fixed-width wrapper is visible, so the layout may stretch to full width.
- **Preheader.** `font-size` and `color` are each declared twice in the inline style. The later values win, so this has no effect on rendering.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, and `X-UA-Compatible` meta.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`). The rest of the body was not visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** QA did not find either header. Its note says the relay may strip them, so this is unverified. It is not confirmed missing, and RFC 8058 one-click support cannot be judged from this capture.
- **Authentication-Results.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the header was not captured. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Footer.** The visible footer, unsubscribe link and postal address were truncated, so they could not be checked.
- **Plain-text part.** URLs make up 7944 of 9513 characters. Long click-tracking URLs dominate the text version, which raises spam-filter risk and makes the plain text hard to read.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. The links are opaque `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=` redirects that QA did not resolve, so destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Body font-family.** Add the missing `;` after `sans-serif` in the body inline style, and prefix the font stacks in the `table, td` and `h1…h6` rules with `font-family:`.
2. **Media query.** Close the unterminated `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
3. **Table markup.** Fix the table nesting, and add `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding="0"` and `cellspacing="0"` to the layout tables.
4. **Outlook width.** Add an MSO conditional 600px wrapper table (or a `width="600"` fixed table) for Outlook.
5. **Honeypot.** If it must stay, set `alt=""` on the image. Add `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabindex="-1"` to the anchor, or hide the whole anchor instead of just the image.
6. **Plain text.** Shorten the URLs in the text part, or use fewer links there, to bring the URL ratio well below 70%.
7. **Headers.** Re-test with raw headers from a direct send, not the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
8. **Links and UTMs.** Resolve a sample of the 42 `click.e.thenorthface.com` links with a redirect-following check to verify destinations, UTM parameters and status codes.
9. **QA capture.** Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and remaining links.
