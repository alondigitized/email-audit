# Save 30% on exclusive jacket styles
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest kind of North Face send: an offer-led jacket email with a clear hero. "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusive Insulated Jackets" sits in large type under a tight portrait of a model in a puffer, with a "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS" eyebrow above it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the single "Shop Exclusives" button is unambiguous.
- It is also a near-repeat. The 11-13 send, "Black Friday Early Access: Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives", and the 11-15 fleece send, "Early Black Friday savings on fleece are here", already hit the same 30% beat. This one narrows the category to insulated jackets, which helps. The offer is still the same, and by the third send in five days it reads as cadence fatigue rather than news. The six-tile grid below the hero is clean, but it is only captioned photos with no prices, no sale marks and no reviews.
- Judgement: well executed and clear, but low on novelty. It will convert for people shopping jackets and do little for everyone else.

## What's working

- The offer is the headline: "Save 30%" in big black type with the "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS" eyebrow gives instant seasonal context.
- The hero portrait is a tight crop of a puffer and hood, so the product category is obvious at a glance.
- The two "Shop Exclusives" buttons, one under the hero and one after the grid, are high-contrast and use the same copy.
- The 2x3 grid covers women, men, boys and girls, and kids, so every household segment sees something.
- The XPLR Pass strip at the top and the loyalty block in the footer make member perks visible without competing with the offer.

## What's weak

- The 30% offer is a repeat of the 11-13 and 11-15 sends, so it feels stale.
- The grid tiles show no prices, no sale price and no "30% off" badge, so the discount is not tied to any product.
- The tile captions are white text over busy photos, and several sit on light backgrounds. "Women's Monarch Triclimate Jacket" and "Kids' Frost Fall Hooded Jacket" are hard to read.
- The copy says "exclusives" but doesn't say what the exclusion is, or whether the deal has an end date. There is no deadline anywhere.
- The "Shop Exclusives" CTA is generic. It doesn't name the product, and both buttons go to the same place.
- There are no reviews or social proof, although the 11-14 send showed that testimonials work for this brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline and a price anchor. For example, "Ends Sunday" in the eyebrow, and a "was $X, now $Y" line under each tile. This single change would lift both urgency and click rate.
- 2. Put a readable gradient or solid caption bar under each tile's text, and add "30% off" on every tile.
- 3. Change the button copy to "Shop 30% Off Jackets" and make it the same verb plus noun on both buttons.
- 4. Add one review or rating on the best-selling Frost Fall jacket, as the 11-14 send did.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11-13 send, or add something new such as free shipping through XPLR Pass or a gift-by date.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Parkas, puffers and styles you can only get here, 30% off before Black Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template with tracked links, a hidden honeypot link and a preheader. It has several CSS and markup defects, and the QA warnings about headers and plain text can't be fully confirmed. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and body content were not audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking links (not verifiable):** QA skipped 39 links on `click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=...`. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Honeypot link:** The first link in the DOM wraps a hidden 1×1 image:
  `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7Im...">` containing `<img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;">`.
  - This is a deliberate bot-click trap. Any scanner or link-prefetcher that follows it will register a click, so click metrics may be inflated.
  - The image has no `alt` attribute, which QA also flagged (see section 3).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** QA reports 7337 of 8917 characters (about 82%) are URLs. Long `qs=` tokens in every link produce this. The plain-text alternative is barely readable, and the URL density is a minor spam-score risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid body style:** `<body style="font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... Arial, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;">` has no `;` after `sans-serif`. The font-family declaration is malformed and the inline `margin`/`padding` are swallowed. The `<head>` rule `body { margin:0 auto !important; padding:0 !important }` partly masks this.
- **Missing property names in the `<style>` block:**
  - `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', ... sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:`, so the whole rule is invalid.
  - The `h1…h6` rule has the same problem. Its font stack is dropped, and `margin:0px` probably still applies.
- **Unclosed `@media` block:** The `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` block inside the `<!--[if false]><!-->` style tag is never closed before `</style>`. Browsers usually recover, but some clients (Gmail and others that rewrite CSS) may drop the whole block, including `.showMobile` and `*[class*="show"]`.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" ...>` directly contains `<table id="backgroundWrapper">` with no `<tr>/<td>`. Browsers and some clients reparent it unpredictably.
- **Missing `role="presentation"`:** The outer `<table bgcolor>` and the inner `<table>` inside `.camarker-inner` lack it. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Outlook width:** `#backgroundWrapper` uses `style="max-width: 600px"` with `width="100%"`. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, and no MSO fixed-width ghost table appears in the visible source, so the layout may stretch.
- **Small text on mobile:** `.disclaimer { font-size: 8px }` at `max-width: 400px` is below readable and accessible size.
- **Missing alt:** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`. Impact is low because it is `display:none`, but add `alt=""`.
- **Minor CSS hygiene:**
  - The preheader div declares `font-size` and `color` twice.
  - `.footer` is declared twice.
  - `.padding {}` is empty.
  - Breakpoints are inconsistent at 400px, 401px and 480px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens. The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are absent in the captured data. QA itself notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses an aligned subdomain, `reply@e.thenorthface.com`, and images are served from `image.e.thenorthface.com`, which is consistent but not proof of authentication.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not in the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 39 links are encoded SFMC `?qs=` redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing pages are not visible, and QA skipped the HTTP probes. No misalignment is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the CSS syntax errors:**
   - Add the `;` after `sans-serif` in the body inline style.
   - Add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and `h1–h6` rules.
   - Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block.
2. **Repair the table structure:** Wrap the nested `#backgroundWrapper` in `<tr><td>`, and add `role="presentation"` and `cellpadding`/`cellspacing` to all layout tables.
3. **Add an MSO ghost table:** Use `<!--[if mso]>` with a fixed 600px width so Outlook respects the layout.
4. **Raise `.disclaimer` above 8px:** Use at least 10–11px on mobile.
5. **Add `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`:** Filter honeypot hits out of click reporting.
6. **Check the plain-text part:** Review the multipart text for readability and URL density, and consider shorter links.
7. **Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml`:** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run the audit with link-redirect resolution (HEAD or GET with redirects followed) on a sample of the 39 tracking links to confirm UTMs and landing pages.
