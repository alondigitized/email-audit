# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- I tapped into thenorthface.com and got stopped cold by a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" bot-check modal sitting right on top of the homepage. Behind it I can make out a moody, cloud-and-mountain hero with a model in a beanie and olive jacket, an XPLR Pass badge, and a "Member Exclusive | Ends 8.10 — Spend $150+, Get 20% Off" banner — so the bones look like a legit outdoor-activist brand mid-promo, but I can't actually get to any of it.

## What's working

- **The banner copy**, "Member Exclusive | Ends 8.10, Spend $150+, Get 20% Off*" — that's exactly the kind of dated, specific offer that gets me to act before I forget.
- **XPLR Pass badge on the hero image** — as an actual XPLR member, seeing that icon front and center signals the page knows its loyalty base exists.
- **The hero model's styling** — beanie, olive utility jacket, glasses — reads like someone in my world, not a 20-year-old trail runner.

## What's weak

- **The "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" modal** — this is a hard stop. I can't shop, I can't browse, I can't even see the full hero headline. It's the single biggest thing wrong with this visit.
- **Cut-off hero text** — "Spend $150+, Get 20%" is sliced in half by the modal, so even the one offer I can see is rendering incompletely.
- **No visible nav path to categories** — hamburger menu, search, account, and bag icons are all up top, but I can't tap any of them until I clear the bot check.

## Recommendations

- Fix or gate the bot-check trigger so it's not firing on a normal mobile pageview — this is costing real sessions.
- If a challenge must run, make it a lightweight inline banner, not a full-screen block over the hero.
- Once past it, make sure the promo banner text doesn't get visually truncated by any overlay in the DOM stacking order.
- Surface the XPLR Pass member perks as a tappable element right on the hero, not just a static badge, since I'm already logged into that program mentally.
