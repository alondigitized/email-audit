# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened TNF and got immediately wall-blocked by a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" interstitial — the entire homepage experience is gated behind a bot-check modal. The only real brand signal I can read is the Memorial Day promo banner at the top ("Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25."), and a blurred rocky-terrain hero sitting behind the challenge. As a loyal XPLR Pass member who visits this site regularly, being treated like a bot on load is a jarring, trust-eroding first impression.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day promo banner** — "Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25." is clear, time-bounded, and immediately scannable. I see a sale and a deadline without hunting for it.
- **Clean nav chrome** — The top bar (search, cart, hamburger) is minimal and uncluttered; once the modal clears, navigation architecture looks sane.
- **Reference ID displayed** — Transparency on the bot-check (showing a reference ID) at least signals there's a real system behind this, not just a broken page.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check interstitial as the primary above-the-fold experience** — A "Press & Hold" CAPTCHA replacing the hero is catastrophically bad for first impressions. Returning XPLR Pass members get no recognition treatment here.
- **Hero image completely inaccessible** — Whatever Summit Series or seasonal campaign is running behind that modal is invisible. The brand storytelling is zero.
- **No loyalty hook anywhere visible** — I'm a member. There's no "Welcome back" signal, no points balance teaser, nothing that rewards my relationship with this brand.
- **No category navigation** — The hamburger is collapsed; I can't see whether Summit, Women's, or Gear is one tap away.
- **Sale banner is the only conversion lever** — If someone bounces before completing the bot check, the 25% off message does nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Exclude authenticated/known users from bot-check challenges** — If I'm logged into my XPLR Pass account or have a session cookie, skip the interstitial. Punishing your most loyal shoppers on load is exactly backwards.
- **Surface the XPLR Pass balance/tier in the nav bar** — Even a small "Points: 1,240" chip next to the cart icon would immediately signal that TNF recognizes me and rewards me.
- **Make the promo banner link actionable** — "Shop Sale" is there, but the entire experience below it is inaccessible. If the gate is unavoidable, place the offer CTA inside or adjacent to the modal so users have something to act on while they confirm.
- **Reveal a sliver of the hero below the modal** — Even 20% of a Summit Series lifestyle shot peeking under the challenge box would tell me the brand experience I'm about to enter is worth the friction.
