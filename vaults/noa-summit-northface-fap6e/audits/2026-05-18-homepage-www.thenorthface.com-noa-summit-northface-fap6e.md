# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is TNF doing what TNF does — leading with a sale and a gorgeous landscape hero to anchor the mood. The canyon shot reads "backcountry aspirational" immediately, which is exactly my lane. Right now the brand is pushing Memorial Day clearance hard, which I respect for its clarity, but the XPLR Pass modal firing on top of the hero CTA is the most annoying thing on the screen and I'm already a member.

## What's working

- **The canyon hero image** is genuinely stunning — moody, vertical, outdoor. It earns the brand positioning instantly without saying a word about gear.
- **The top-of-page promo banner** ("Memorial Day: Up to 25% off select gear. Ends 5.25") is doing double duty: it anchors the sale and communicates urgency before I even process the hero.
- **XPLR Pass modal** surfaces the loyalty program without requiring any scroll — even if the timing is bad, the visibility is right for new visitors.
- **The "Ends 5.25" deadline** on the hero copy is concrete and credible. No vague "while supplies last" — I know exactly when I need to act.

## What's weak

- **The XPLR modal obscures the hero CTA** — the primary "shop the sale" button is cut off and unreadable. If I'm already a member (I am), this modal is friction, not a feature.
- **"Select gear" is doing a lot of vague work** — I have no idea if Summit Series or Nuptse product is included until I tap through. That uncertainty slows me down.
- **Zero sustainability or PFC-free signal above the fold** — for a brand competing with Patagonia on values, this is a missed opportunity. Patagonia would have found a way.
- **No category shortcuts** — just a hamburger menu. If I want to jump straight to Women's Jackets or Summit Series, I have to dig, not tap.
- **No person in the hero** — the canyon is beautiful but nobody's wearing anything. I can't see how that Denali would layer.

## Recommendations

- **Minimize the XPLR modal by default for recognized members** — show a persistent loyalty-balance chip in the nav instead. For non-members, the modal timing is fine; for me it's just noise.
- **Restore the hero CTA above the modal** — even if the modal exists, the primary "Shop the Sale" button should never be obscured. Dock the modal to the bottom edge so it doesn't eat the action.
- **Add a single "What's included" link to the offer copy** — one tap to a pre-filtered sale page with Summit/Nuptse/Denali actually marked saves hesitation and drives deeper clicks.
- **Drop one PFC-free or responsible-materials badge** into the hero subhead — something like "Featuring our DWR-free lineup" costs nothing and gives me a reason not to go check Patagonia.
