# Go further with our best-selling gifts.

- This is the third send in four days to repeat the same pieces. The 11-13 "Black Friday Early Access: Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" email put that offer in the hero, and that was the brand's best recent send. ## What's working

- The testimonials are specific and gift-framed ("I bought this as a gift for my wife and it was a huge success"). They answer whether a gift will land.
- The alternating photo and green text-block layout is easy to scan, and each product has its own link.
- The hero, "Best sellers for a reason.", sets a clear theme with a strong snowboarder shot.
- The star ratings and "Reviews from The North Face customers" line add proof on the accessories row.
- The XPLR Pass loyalty strip appears at the top and again in the footer, so member value is visible.

## What's weak

- The 30% Black Friday Early Access offer sits in the last banner. The black "BLACK FRIDAY EARLY ACCESS" text over the dark photo is also hard to read.
- The hero CTA, "Best Selling Gifts", is a generic category button. Nothing specific is promised.
- The Shop Gift Guide banner has no copy beyond the button and no offer, so it feels filler-like.
- The creative recycles the previous day's couple photo and the same 30% message. That's repetition fatigue after the 11-12 and 11-13 sends.
- Every product CTA is a small underlined text link ("Shop the Jacket"). They're easy to miss and don't make the offer feel urgent.
- No prices are shown, so a gift buyer can't judge budget.

- 1. Move "Save 30% on Holiday Exclusives" into the hero and put a deadline on it. The best-seller proof can follow it.
- 2. Turn the text links into real buttons that name the product and the saving, such as "Get the Nuptse, 30% off".
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" under each product, and group gifts by budget.
- 4. Make the final banner's text white or add a scrim so it reads against the photo.
- 5. Don't send the same offer and photo two days running. Rotate the hero creative or change the angle.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`click.e.thenorthface.com`, `image.e.thenorthface.com`) with several invalid CSS and table-nesting constructs. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque tracking:** All 42 links were skipped by the QA probe as tracking domains. Their destinations and any UTM parameters can't be confirmed from the source. The sample link is `https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=ABp7ImQi…`, and the destination is encoded in the `qs` token.
- **Honeypot link:** The first element in the body is `<a href="https://click.e.thenorthface.com/?qs=…"><img src=".../honeypot_pixel.png" width="1" height="1" style="display: none;"></a>`. It appears to be a deliberate spam-trap or bot-click detector. Security scanners that follow every link will register a click on it, which can distort click metrics.
- **Plain-text part:** The QA check found 7,944 of 9,544 characters are URLs (83%), above the 70% threshold. Long `qs=` tokens inflate the plain-text part, which is a minor spam-scoring risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid body style:** The body style reads `font-family:'HelveticaNeue-Medium', …, sans-serif margin: 0; padding: 0;`. The semicolon after `sans-serif` is missing, so the declaration is invalid and the inline `margin: 0; padding: 0` is dropped. The `<style>` rule `body { margin: 0 auto !important; padding: 0 !important }` partially masks this, but clients that strip `<style>` will not.
- **Missing property names:**
  - `table, td { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … sans-serif; }` has no `font-family:` property.
  - `h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 { 'HelveticaNeue-Medium', … !important; margin: 0px; }` has the same problem.
  - Both declarations are discarded, so the intended font stack is never applied through CSS.
- **Unclosed `@media` block:** In the second `<style>` block, `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) {` is never closed. The last rule (`*[class*="show"], *[class*="show"] img {…}`) is followed directly by `</style>`. Parsers may drop the mobile rules (`.showMobile`, `.hide`, `*[class*="show"]`).
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table bgcolor="#f4f4f4" style="width:100%…">` is followed immediately by `<!-- Begin Preheader Block --> <table id="backgroundWrapper" …>`, with no `<tr><td>` between them. The outer table also lacks `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding` and `cellspacing`. The nested `<table>` inside `td.stylingblock-content-wrapper.camarker-inner` also lacks `role="presentation"`, so screen readers may announce layout tables as data tables.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `honeypot_pixel.png` has no `alt`. The image is `display:none` and inside a link, so impact is low, but an empty `alt=""` is the safe value.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>The North Face</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. Minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the non-truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The QA check did not find it. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.thenorthface.com` and its tracking subdomains look aligned, but this is not verified.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
It can't be assessed. Click URLs are opaque redirects, and the probe skipped them. There is no evidence of broken destinations or missing UTM parameters, and none can be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the body style by adding the missing `;` after `sans-serif`.
2. Add `font-family:` to the `table, td` and `h1–h6` rules, and remove the stray `!important`.
3. Close the `@media (max-width: 480px)` block in the second `<style>`.
4. Add `<tr><td>` between the outer and `#backgroundWrapper` tables. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
5. Add `alt=""` to `honeypot_pixel.png`. Exclude honeypot clicks from engagement reporting.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the tracking links with a redirect-following probe to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
7. Reduce plain-text URL density by shortening tokens or trimming the plain-text part.
