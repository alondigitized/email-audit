# Homepage snapshot · www.thenorthface.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, TNF is doing a Memorial Day push and at least they're being upfront about it — 25% off, hard deadline of 5.25, dramatic canyon hero. Very on-brand visually, very them. But the second I land on the page I'm hit with a "Join XPLR Pass" bottom sheet that literally buries the shop CTA, which is wild because I'm already a member and this is doing nothing for me right now.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day double-tap** — banner strip AND hero both reinforce "25% off, Ends 5.25" so the message lands even with a quick glance.
- **Hero photography** — that canyon-with-light-shaft shot is genuinely beautiful. Feels like a Summit Series campaign, not just retail filler. My kind of aspiration.
- **Clear deadline** — "ENDS 5.25" is hard to miss. Creates real urgency without being sleazy.
- **XPLR Pass surface** — the program name is at least visible, which signals the brand takes membership seriously. Just aimed at the wrong audience at the wrong moment.

## What's weak

- **XPLR modal blocking the CTA** — the primary shop button is covered. I literally cannot tap through to shop without first dealing with the join sheet. As a current member this is extra annoying.
- **No category entry points above the fold** — no Women's, no Summit Series, no Nuptse — just a hamburger menu. One fewer tap to my gear would meaningfully lift engagement.
- **Join-focused XPLR prompt to an existing member** — if TNF knows I'm logged in (or has a cookie), this should show my points balance, not a "Join Now" CTA.
- **No model in the hero** — the canyon is gorgeous but I can't see how a Nuptse or Denali actually looks on a real person right now. The lifestyle connection is one step removed.
- **Free-shipping threshold invisible** — for a sale driving basket-building behavior, not showing it above the fold is leaving money on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or auto-suppress the XPLR modal for logged-in members** — show a loyalty-balance chip ("You have 430 XPLR points to redeem") instead of a join prompt. That's a conversion accelerator, not friction.
- **Add a Women's / Summit Series quick-link strip** directly below the hero so I can tap into my category in one shot without opening the hamburger.
- **Swap "Join Now" sheet for a compact persistent banner** for non-members so the hero CTA is never buried — the offer is still surfaced, just not as a wall.
- **Inject a styled shot (Nuptse or Denali on a real person in the field)** as a secondary module immediately below the hero to anchor the sale in actual product.
