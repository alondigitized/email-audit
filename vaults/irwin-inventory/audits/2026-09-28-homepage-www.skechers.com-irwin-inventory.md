# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- I opened the app expecting to walk the sale rack and instead I got handed a product launch. Skechers is spending prime top-of-page real estate — the whole hero panel — on the new Aero Series running shoe at full freight, with the actual markdown activity ("Extra 10% OFF Everything," "20% OFF Boots & Outdoor Styles") shoved into two thin strips above and below the logo. This reads like a performance-tech brand launch today, not a clearance operation, and that's a problem for someone whose whole job is checking whether the discount is real.

## What's working

- The top ribbon — "Extra 10% OFF Everything, Applied at Cart" — is honest about where the discount lands. No guessing games, no "up to."
- "Step into Fall! 20% OFF Boots & Outdoor Styles" is a clean, seasonally-relevant offer with a direct Shop link and a Details link right next to it — that's the kind of transparency I respect.
- The category strip (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning, Aero Burst) is real merchandising language, not vague lifestyle copy — it tells me these are fit/comfort categories, which at least narrows the field even if it's not the sale rack.

## What's weak

- Position #1 on this page — the entire hero banner — is spent on the Aero Series launch. That's full-price real estate. If I'm auditing "what's the brand pushing," the answer is a new running shoe, not inventory clearance, and that's a mismatch for anyone landing here looking for a deal.
- No "Sale" or "Clearance" entry point in the visible nav row. I have five comfort-tech categories and zero path to a markdown rack in one tap.
- The "SAVE 20% OFF / 4 AVAILABLE OFFERS" bar pinned at the bottom is collapsed — I can't see what those four offers actually are, which colors/sizes they touch, or whether they stack. That's exactly the kind of vague stacking language that makes me want to open it up and check before I believe it.
- Zero size or stock signals anywhere on this screen — which I expect, this is a homepage, not a PLP, but it also means the homepage isn't doing anything to set expectations about availability before I click deeper.

## Recommendations

- Put a real "Shop Sale" or "Clearance" tile in that top category row alongside Slip-ins/Wide Fit/Arch Fit — right now the deal-seeker has no direct lane.
- Expand the "4 AVAILABLE OFFERS" bar by default, or at least show a one-line summary of what they are, instead of making me tap to find out.
- Swap the hero for a rotating slot — Aero Series launch is fine for a runner, but alternate it with a markdown-focused hero (e.g., "Boots & Outdoor, 20% off, shop the rack") so the top position isn't permanently full-price.
- Add a size-availability or "selling fast" cue to the promo strip itself — even a small "limited sizes" flag up top would tell me what I'm walking into before I burn a tap.
