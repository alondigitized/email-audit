# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a big comfort-footwear brand pushing walking shoes and one blanket promo, "20% OFF Sitewide Applied at Cart." The hero is a polished lifestyle shot, and nothing above the fold tells me which styles are actually in stock or where the sale rack starts. I came to audit the sale rack and I'm not shown a door to it.

## What's working

- **"20% OFF Sitewide" top bar:** it's the first thing I read and it's a specific number, not "deals inside."
- **Member reward strip ("Earn a $5 Reward on $75+"):** it gives a concrete threshold I can check against a basket.
- **Fit-oriented nav (Slip-ins, Wide Fit, Arch Fit, Max Cushioning, Aero Burst):** it's one tap to width-specific shopping. That matters to me because I tally availability per (color, width).
- **Hero couple:** a man around my age in a normal city setting is believable, and the shoes are clearly visible.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab:** a returning-shopper convenience that's easy to find.

## What's weak

- **No Sale / Clearance entry in the visible nav.** My whole job starts at the sale category, and I'd have to dig through MENU.
- **Hero headline clipped at the top.** "WHERE" is cut off and "SHOP MEN'S WALKING" is truncated at the hero's bottom edge. Both look like layout breakage.
- **The product rail is almost invisible.** Below the "Women's Walking Styles" tab there's blank space and then shoe tops peeking out. I can't see a price, position, rating or size from here.
- **Sticky "SAVE 20% OFF — 4 AVAILABLE OFFERS" bar** covers the area where the first products should be. It's a stack of offers I'd have to open to understand.
- **No stock, shipping or returns signals.** There's no free-shipping threshold, no returns line and no review stars anywhere above the fold.
- **"LIMITED TIME!" with no end date** is urgency without a deadline.

## Recommendations

- Add a visible "Sale" link as the first or last item in the nav row (Slip-ins · Wide Fit · … · **Sale**), and add a "Shop Sale" button to the hero.
- Fix the hero crop: pull the headline down so "WHERE" isn't clipped, and shrink the type or reduce the buttons' vertical stacking so both CTAs sit fully inside the image. Put them side by side.
- Collapse the empty gap under the tabs so the first row of product cards shows with price, star rating and a "Low stock" or size-availability cue.
- Put a date on the promo: "20% off sitewide — ends Sunday 11:59pm PT," and replace "4 AVAILABLE OFFERS" with the one or two terms that matter, in plain text.
