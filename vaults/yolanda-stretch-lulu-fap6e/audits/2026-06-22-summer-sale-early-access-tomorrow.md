# Summer Sale: Early Access tomorrow
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's member early-access teaser for the Summer Sale — a notification email, not a shopping email. Its single job is to get loyalty members to have the app ready before the sale drops tomorrow. Orange hero, large "SUMMER SALE" type, one CTA. That's it.
- The format is strategically sound: a pre-sale primer reduces morning-of friction and rewards members before the general public. But the execution is uncharacteristically thin for a brand that just ran six consecutive emails with specific hero SKUs, fabric names, and category callouts. Members get "Summer Sale" and nothing else — no discount depth, no featured category, no anchor products. Compared to the June 21 restock trigger (which named the Groove High-Rise Notched-Hem Crop 10" with editorial specificity), this is a missed opportunity to build anticipation.
- The single biggest execution miss: the body copy says "Make sure notifications are on so you know when it goes live" — the correct ask — but the CTA button says "Download the App." For a loyalty email audience that already has the app, asking them to download it is a tone-deaf non sequitur. That disconnect undercuts the one conversion action this email is built around.

## What's working

- Orange hero creates an immediate visual gut-punch; "SUMMER SALE" in oversized white type is impossible to miss
- "Membership Early Access" badge correctly signals exclusivity and segmentation before the hero even loads
- "tomorrow" is the kind of hard deadline that actually modifies behavior — more credible than "limited time" or "while supplies last"
- Single-focus layout with no competing modules; the message lands in under three seconds
- Consistent with lululemon's stripped-down editorial voice — no product clutter

## What's weak

- Zero offer depth — members don't know if this is 20% off or 50% off, which categories, or whether Align and ABC are included
- "Download the App" CTA contradicts the body copy that correctly asks for notification opt-in; a loyalty audience already has the app
- "Heating up early" headline adds no information — it's a tone filler, not a hook
- No hero product or even a category tease; after six product-specific emails in a row, this send gives members nothing concrete to anticipate
- No urgency mechanics beyond "tomorrow" — no time, no window, no member-only pricing signal

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the CTA.** Change "Download the App" → "Turn On Notifications" or "Open the App." The body copy already tells you WHY (notifications); the button should match that action for members who already have the app.
- 2. **Add a sale hook.** One line: "Up to X% off Align, Scuba, ABC, and more" transforms a bland announcement into an event worth showing up for.
- 3. **Reframe the hero headline.** "Heating up early" is forgettable. "Your Summer Sale starts 24 hours early" or "Members get in first" makes the exclusivity feel earned.
- 4. **Tease one anchor product.** Even a partially obscured hero SKU or a blurred product image builds anticipation — the June 21 restock email is the right reference for this treatment.
- 5. **Add a time.** "Early access opens at 8 AM ET tomorrow" is the kind of detail that prompts calendar behavior and notification setup.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Member Access: Summer Sale drops tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You're in first — Summer Sale starts tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to X% off. Turn on app notifications so you don't miss it.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Align, Scuba, ABC and more — yours before everyone else.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is lululemon's member early-access teaser for the Summer Sale — a notification email, not a shopping email. Its single job is to get loyalty members to have the app ready before the sale drops tomorrow. Orange hero, large "SUMMER SALE" type, one CTA. That's it.

The format is strategically sound: a pre-sale primer reduces morning-of friction and rewards members before the general public. But the execution is uncharacteristically thin for a brand that just ran six consecutive emails with specific hero SKUs, fabric names, and category callouts. Members get "Summer Sale" and nothing else — no discount depth, no featured category, no anchor products. Compared to the June 21 restock trigger (which named the Groove High-Rise Notched-Hem Crop 10" with editorial specificity), this is a missed opportunity to build anticipation.

The single biggest execution miss: the body copy says "Make sure notifications are on so you know when it goes live" — the correct ask — but the CTA button says "Download the App." For a loyalty email audience that already has the app, asking them to download it is a tone-deaf non sequitur. That disconnect undercuts the one conversion action this email is built around.

## 3. What didn't

- "Heating up early" headline adds no information — it's a tone filler, not a hook
- No hero product or even a category tease; after six product-specific emails in a row, this send gives members nothing concrete to anticipate
- No urgency mechanics beyond "tomorrow" — no time, no window, no member-only pricing signal

## 4. What I'd change

1. **Fix the CTA.** Change "Download the App" → "Turn On Notifications" or "Open the App." The body copy already tells you WHY (notifications); the button should match that action for members who already have the app.

4. **Tease one anchor product.** Even a partially obscured hero SKU or a blurred product image builds anticipation — the June 21 restock email is the right reference for this treatment.
5. **Add a time.** "Early access opens at 8 AM ET tomorrow" is the kind of detail that prompts calendar behavior and notification setup.
