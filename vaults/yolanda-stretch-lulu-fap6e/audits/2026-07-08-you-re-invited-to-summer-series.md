# You’re invited to Summer Series
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Summer Series launch email — a deliberate pivot away from the six-email Summer Sale drumbeat that ran June 22 through July 7. The hook is a free class program: 50,000 spots, Yoga/Pilates/Sculpt, in stores/studios/events all summer. The design is clean and editorial — orange paint splash echoes the brand's Summer Sale orange palette but the message is entirely different: this is community and movement, not discounting.
- The email does its job structurally. Hero → program description → "Learn More" → category product grid → "We think you'll also love" cross-sell. The cadence pivot is smart; after seven consecutive promotional sends, an invitation to free classes reads like a palate cleanser. But the subject line "You're invited to Summer Series" squanders the best hook in the email — **50,000 free spots** — which is buried below the fold as body copy.
- Verdict: A well-executed program email that undersells its own most compelling claim. The free-class hook is strong; the subject and primary CTA copy don't reflect that strength.

## What's working

- **50,000 free spots** is a concrete, credible quantity hook — it signals scale and legitimacy instantly.
- The editorial hero ("Find your space" + orange paint splash) creates visual separation from the sale emails that preceded it — this feels intentional, not fatigued.
- Four category CTAs (Women's Yoga, Women's Pilates, Men's Yoga, Men's Workout) turn an event email into a shopping email without feeling forced — the "classes call for fresh gear" bridge copy is clean.
- "We think you'll also love" product grid adds a second conversion layer for subscribers who won't register for classes.
- No render bugs — layout holds cleanly across the full render.

## What's weak

- **Subject is opaque**: "You're invited to Summer Series" tells you nothing — is it a sale? A collection drop? An event? The 50,000-free-spots hook should have been the subject.
- **"Learn More" CTA is the weakest possible copy** for an event registration — it reads passive, especially against something as concrete as claiming a free class spot.
- No urgency signal anywhere — "all summer long" diffuses any impulse to act now; no deadline, no spots-filling language, no counter.
- Loyalty/member benefits not mentioned — given that recent sends (June 22 "Early Access") leaned on member exclusivity, this program email misses a personalization beat.
- Class format and location specifics are vague in the email body — "stores, studios, and events" is broad; a city callout or "find your class" prompt would raise relevance.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the free-spots hook** — the 50,000 number is unusually specific and credible; it should be the first thing a subscriber reads.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50,000 free spots. Find your class this summer.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free yoga, Pilates + Sculpt — all summer long`
- 2. **Replace "Learn More" with action copy** — "Claim your spot" or "Find classes near you" turns a passive click into an intent signal.
- 3. **Add urgency to the offer block** — even "spots are filling fast" or a callout like "Classes start [date]" would prompt action without being dishonest.
- 4. **Surface a city personalization token** in the program description — "Join us in [City]" would lift click-through meaningfully on an event-registration email.
- 5. **Acknowledge the member angle** — if Summer Series has early/exclusive access for loyalty members (as the sale did), say so; it's a proven trigger for this list.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `50,000 free spots. Yoga, Pilates + Sculpt — find classes near you.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Roll out your mat. Free classes all summer, in your city.`
