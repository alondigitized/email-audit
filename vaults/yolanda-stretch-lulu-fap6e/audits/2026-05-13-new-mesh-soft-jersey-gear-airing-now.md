# New Mesh Soft Jersey gear, airing now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, aspirational product-launch email for lululemon's Mesh Soft Jersey line. The creative concept earns its cleverness: "Mesh Soft Jersey" + "airing now" + "On air" in the hero copy all work the same air-through-fabric idea without beating you over the head with it. The photography is premium — a man suspended mid-jump against a wide blue sky — and the editorial pacing feels right for a brand that sells $128 shorts.
- What's missing is any commercial hook. There's no price point, no offer, no urgency beyond "airing now." This is a pure new-arrival awareness send, which is a legitimate play for lululemon's loyal base, but it leaves money on the table for anyone who isn't already primed to buy. The CTA button is tiny and visually buried below the product grid.
- Solid execution of brand storytelling. Weak on conversion mechanics.

## What's working

- **The editorial concept holds**: "On air / airing now / air can always come through" threads throughout without feeling forced.
- **Hero image is strong**: the mid-jump silhouette against a blue sky is on-brand, season-appropriate, and visually arresting.
- **Product detail is specific**: "Knit with hundreds of tiny openings, so cool air can always come through" is a real benefit statement, not vague luxury language.
- **No render bugs**: clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- **"Shop new styles this week" product grid**: multiple colorways and silhouettes give the reader real browsing surface.

## What's weak

- **No offer, no price, no discount**: the entire email is awareness-only — a lapsed or price-sensitive subscriber has zero financial reason to click.
- **CTA button is visually lost**: the small button below the product grid barely registers; there's no button in the hero section where eyes actually land first.
- **"Another set? Easy." section is vague**: the bottom module feels tacked on without a clear product or copy payoff visible at this resolution.
- **No personalization**: no first name, no "your favorites," no loyalty tier acknowledgment — this reads as a mass blast.
- **Product grid models lack name/price overlay**: you can see the styles but can't tell what anything costs without clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hero CTA button** immediately below the "On air: Mesh Soft Jersey" headline — don't make the reader scroll to find a way to shop.
- 2. **Surface at least one price**: even "From $68" anchors value and separates browsers from buyers.
- 3. **Make the product grid shoppable in-email**: add product name + price overlay on each tile (lululemon's main site does this; the email should match the experience).
- 4. **Sharpen "Another set? Easy."** — this needs a specific product name or benefit to land.
- 5. **Personalize the opener** if member data is available — "Your next training set" beats "New gear."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer training set just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of tiny openings — so cool air never stops moving.`
