# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon knows exactly who they're talking to — a woman who sweats on purpose and does it in a studio. The Everlux hero with the Pilates model is speaking directly into my soul, and the two-CTA layout feels clean on mobile. Right now they're pushing a hot Pilates / sweat-performance story, which honestly couldn't land more on-brand for someone like me.
- **8/10**
- + Hero copy & imagery explicitly speak to my persona: "hot Pilates' spiciest moves" + a woman in a deep studio lunge wearing teal lululemon — that's my Tuesday morning
- − No returning-shopper hook: no loyalty balance, no "Welcome back," no member-pricing badge — I'm a loyalist and this page doesn't know it
- + Concrete offer above fold: "Get 15% off regular-priced items" in the top banner
- + Unmistakable CTAs above fold: two buttons, both visible, both readable
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: hero dominates, search bar above it, CTAs anchor below
- + No render bugs: page looks pixel-clean on mobile
- + Demographic signals match: ~30-ish female studio instructor, not a track-and-field energy
- + Current campaign: Everlux / hot Pilates reads fresh, not stale
- − Loyalty/membership benefits: not visible without scrolling — big miss for a weekly WMTM checker like me
- − Offer honesty: "15% off regular-priced items / Sign Up" with no visible fine print — conditional on signup action, exclusions unclear

## What's working

- **Everlux hero image + copy**: The model's pose and the Everlux fast-drying angle is exactly what I think about before a heated vinyasa class. "Hot Pilates' spiciest moves" is fun copy that doesn't feel cringe.
- **Dual CTAs ("Shop Women's What's New" / "Shop Warm-up and Workout")**: Both are legible white buttons on the dark hero. On mobile I can tap either without squinting or zooming.
- **15% off banner**: Anchored at top, dismissible — not an intrusive modal. The offer is specific enough to be real.
- **Search bar placement**: For someone who knows exactly what she wants (ABC Pant Reg in size 6), front-and-center search is the right call on mobile.
- **No forced interstitial**: No cookie wall or splash screen blocking the hero — I can actually see the page.

## What's weak

- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment**: I check WMTM every week. I've spent enough on Define jackets to fund a small retreat. This page gives me nothing that acknowledges I'm a returning member — no points, no member-only early access, nothing.
- **Two CTAs dilute each other**: "Shop Women's What's New" and "Shop Warm-up and Workout" overlap in intent. Pick one, own it.
- **No product specificity**: "Everlux" is a fabric tech story, not a product. I can't see a specific pant or jacket to tap into. The hero doesn't make me want to buy *a thing* — just a vibe.
- **15% off requires a Sign Up**: If I'm already a member, this banner is noise at best, mildly annoying at worst — it implies I'm not yet in the system.
- **No We Made Too Much / sale signal**: That's genuinely how I shop and it's buried. A small "Sale" chip or "WMTM is live" mention would get me scrolling faster.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-member smart banner**: Swap the generic "Sign Up for 15% off" for a logged-in state that says "Your member perks are live — explore WMTM" or shows a points balance chip. High effort on personalization, massive signal for loyalists.
- **Replace the duplicate CTA with one action tied to a specific product**: Kill "Shop Warm-up and Workout," keep "Shop Women's What's New," and add a secondary anchor like "Shop Everlux Styles" — product-first, not category soup.
- **Surface the Like New / resale badge somewhere in the first scroll**: I use the take-back program. One small "Like New available" mention or icon in the nav would make me feel seen without disrupting the hero.
- **Add a WMTM / sale teaser strip directly below the hero**: Even "We Made Too Much — new markdowns every Tuesday" with a tap arrow keeps me on the page and converts weekly check-in behavior into a session.
