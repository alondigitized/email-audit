# What’s new: Nulu™ fabric’s reflective side
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a beautiful editorial send that doesn't know what it's selling. The Nulu™ satin-panel story is shot well: a dark, moody palette, yoga poses and a hooded puffer. But the subject promises "reflective side" and the body never shows reflectivity or a hero product. The hero is a model in a down puffer, the headline is "Hush the Holiday rush.", and two generic buttons sit underneath.
- Then a red-orange "black friday sale" banner lands mid-email with no percentage, no date and no price. It continues the creative from your 11/19 "Black Friday Sale is in motion" and 11/21 "Black Friday Sale's new contenders" sends. Those emails at least led with the sale, and this one buries it. Mixing a calm yoga/Nulu theme with a loud sale band leaves two stories competing, and neither wins.
- Verdict: strong brand polish, weak commercial clarity. It reads as a mood board.

## What's working

- The photography is cohesive and premium, with consistent warm-neutral backdrops and dark apparel. It feels unmistakably lululemon.
- The layout has no visible render bugs: no broken images, overlap or gaps.
- The subject names a concrete product (Nulu™) and stays short enough for mobile.
- The Black Friday banner keeps the email in the current season and ties it to the recent sends.
- The closing "We think you'll also love" grid and the footer links (Download the App, Find a Store) give people a way to keep browsing.

## What's weak

- The "black friday sale" band has no offer. "Hundreds of styles, in-store and online" is vague, and it sits in small type beside the headline.
- The hero shows a puffer, but the subject sells Nulu fabric. Subject and image don't match.
- The two top buttons, "Shop What's New" and "Shop Yoga", are generic and compete with each other.
- Most of the email is full-bleed imagery with tiny, low-contrast captions ("Shop Coats & Jackets →"). The links are hard to spot.
- The "reflective side" promise is never visibly delivered. The images show satin panels, not a reflective effect.
- It's very long, and the email never names one product or price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the Nulu satin-panel product and show the reflective effect, or lead with Black Friday. Don't split the email.
- 2. Put one concrete offer above the fold. Name the discount or the member perk in the header.
- 3. Replace "Shop What's New" and "Shop Yoga" with one specific button, for example "Shop Nulu Align™ Tops".
- 4. Put price, colour and a product link under each Nulu image so the images can be clicked and compared.
- 5. Shrink the middle. Cut the repeated yoga frames to cut scroll length and get the sale band higher.
- 6. Add a visible deadline to the sale band.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New Nulu™ satin panels + Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Satin-panel Nulu™ is here, and Black Friday prices are live.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New yoga styles that shine, plus hundreds of sale picks.`
