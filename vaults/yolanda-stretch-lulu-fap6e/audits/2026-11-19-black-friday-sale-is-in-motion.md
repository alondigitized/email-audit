# Black Friday Sale is in motion
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, confident Black Friday send. The oversized lowercase "black friday sale" on a red-orange field is unmistakable, and it continues the look of the 11/15 teaser and the 11/16 "Early Access is live" send. The hero only says "Hundreds of styles. Right here, right now." It has no percentage and no dollar amount. The real offer only shows up lower down, in the strikethrough prices ($128 to $84, $58 to $44, $148 to $99).
- The structure is clear: hero, one dark pill CTA, a four-tile category grid, then six priced products. The hero is strong, but it sells the event and not the deal. The best proof is in the product grid, which a subscriber has to scroll past the categories to reach. The scale of the discounts (about 25–35%) is never stated, and the fine print is small and low contrast.
- Overall this is a solid event send, better than a normal product-launch email, but it doesn't use the offer well. After the 11/18 Silent Type launch and 11/17 puffers, the brand is now in promo mode, and the gap between loud creative and vague copy is the main weakness.

## What's working

- The hero is a scroll-stopper, and the red-orange type carries over from the 11/15 and 11/16 sends.
- One pill CTA, "Shop Black Friday," appears twice, at the top and after the products, so the action is unambiguous.
- The category tiles (Women's, Men's, Accessories, Shoes) route different shoppers quickly, and the models are well shot.
- Six products show strikethrough pricing, so the savings are visible and credible.
- The footer is clean, with an app download link and a gift guide link.

## What's weak

- The hero and headline state no number. "Hundreds of styles" is vague, and the discount depth is never named.
- Six products all link to the same "Shop Black Friday" CTA, with no sign of per-product links, so the product detail is only decorative.
- The fine print is tiny and low contrast. It has visible gaps where text seems to be missing ("online at [blank] or [blank], and the lululemon app"), which reads as a render flaw.
- There is no membership benefit or early access cue, even though the earlier early-access send created that expectation.
- The "The best of what moves you." section is generic copy, and it adds a second CTA ("Shop Best of Black Friday →") that competes with the main pill.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the hero: "Up to 30% off" or "Deals from $44". The offer is currently visible only after scrolling past the category grid.
- 2. Move the priced products above the category tiles, or add a price-led strip directly under the hero.
- 3. Add a deadline to the visible body copy ("Ends Nov 30"). It is currently buried in the fine print.
- 4. Fix the gaps in the legal copy and raise its contrast.
- 5. Collapse the two shop links (the pill and "Shop Best of Black Friday →") into one primary action.
- 6. Add a member line ("Members: extra perks") to reward the early-access audience.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: Define jacket $84, Classic Tee $44`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 30% off, now through Nov 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of styles marked down. Jackets from $84, tees from $44.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Black Friday Sale runs through Cyber Monday. Shop the best of it.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA data shows no confirmed delivery-breaking defects. The two "broken link" failures are probably bot-blocking 403s, and the compliance and authentication warnings come from missing relay-captured headers, so they are unverified rather than confirmed. The one real code-level defect is four footer images with missing alt attributes. The HTML provided is truncated, so the body, footer and unsubscribe markup were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **`https://shop.lululemon.com` (link text "lululemon.com") returned 403** and **`https://shop.lululemon.com/en-ca` (link text "lululemon.ca") returned 403.** A 403 is not a 404 or DNS failure. Retail sites commonly return 403 to non-browser HTTP probes, so this is likely a false positive from the QA probe. It is not confirmed as a real user-facing break. Both links should be re-checked with a browser-like request.
- **47 tracking links were skipped** (click-redirect domains, no HTTP probe). Their destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- Both flagged links point to the bare `shop.lululemon.com` host, not to a Black Friday landing page. That is a continuity observation, covered in section 6, rather than a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four `<img>` elements lack alt text:** `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right`, src `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`. The images are probably decorative arrows beside footer links. They should carry an explicit `alt=""`, because a missing attribute can lead some screen readers to read out the filename or URL.
- The `<head>` includes `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `:root { color-scheme: light dark; }`. It also has dark-mode targeting for Gmail (`u + .body`), Outlook.com (`x_` classes) and Outlook Android (`\0` hacks). No rendering defect is evidenced.
- The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, with `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"` set. No issue.
- Body fonts reference `'Saans-Regular'` and `'KHGiga-Light'` with fallbacks (`Helvetica Neue`, `Times New Roman`). This is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The truncated body could not be checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found,** and **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. This is unverified, not a confirmed violation. Raw headers from the original message are needed to confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe. The sender is `hello@e.lululemon.com`, an SFMC-style subdomain, and these headers are normally present on messages from such senders.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM and campaign parameters could not be evaluated. All 47 tracking links were skipped, and the two probed links are bare homepage URLs with no UTM parameters.
- The subject is "Black Friday Sale is in motion". Neither link evidence nor probe results show where the CTAs land, so alignment with a Black Friday landing page is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt=""` to the four `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images.** This is the only confirmed fix.
2. **Re-probe `shop.lululemon.com` and `/en-ca`** with a browser User-Agent or a headless browser to rule out a bot-block false positive. Add a rule to the QA tool so a 403 on a known retail domain is downgraded to a warning.
3. **Capture raw headers** (original `.eml` or a direct inbox seed) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Until then, treat the compliance and authentication warnings as unknown.
4. **Resolve a sample of the 47 skipped tracking links** to check final destinations and UTM parameters, and whether they reach the sale landing page.
5. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML,** not the truncated source, to cover the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and any remaining merge tokens.
