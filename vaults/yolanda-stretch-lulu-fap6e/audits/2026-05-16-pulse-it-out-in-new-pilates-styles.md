# Pulse it out in new Pilates styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a Pilates-category product-launch email from lululemon — clean layout, consistent brand voice, and a legitimate Nulu fabric story anchored by an ambassador quote. The concept is sound: Pilates is hot, lululemon has credible gear, and the ambassador quote ("so I can get into any position that I want to") is the best line in the email.
- The problem is the hero. "Find your space" ran four days ago on May 12's yoga-styles email — same visual language, same tagline, now recycled onto a Pilates drop. A subscriber who opened both will feel like they've seen this email before, which deflates any sense of "new." The cadence makes it worse: this is the seventh lululemon email in eight days. Even well-executed sends lose impact at that frequency.
- No discounts, no urgency, generic "Shop Now" CTA. The email earns attention through brand equity alone rather than doing the work to pull someone to checkout. It's a serviceable product announcement but below the standard lululemon set with the May 13 triggered-reminder email (9/10) that actually gave a subscriber a reason to act *now*.

## What's working

- **Nulu fabric callout** — naming the specific technology adds credibility and is the kind of product detail a Pilates subscriber cares about.
- **Ambassador quote** — specific, physical, and on-brief; "so I can get into any position" is the strongest copy moment in the email.
- **Subject line category signal** — "Pilates styles" immediately qualifies the audience; a yoga-only subscriber knows this isn't for her.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text visible in the render.
- **Product grid** — "Shop new styles this week" section gives multiple entry points (tops, leggings, accessories) without feeling chaotic.

## What's weak

- **"Find your space" hero is recycled creative** — identical tagline used on the May 12 yoga launch; a real subscriber opening both will disengage.
- **No offer or incentive** — pure launch email with no discount, no free shipping, no member pricing; nothing pulls the conversion lever.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is maximally generic** — zero tension between the Pilates context and the CTA copy; "Shop Pilates" or "Try the Nulu collection" would do actual work.
- **Seventh email in eight days** — cadence fatigue is real; this send lands at the wrong time in the rotation to break through.
- **No urgency signal** — "new styles this week" is soft; without a deadline or stock message, there's no reason to click today vs. tomorrow vs. never.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire "Find your space" immediately** — it ran four days ago on a different category email; pick a Pilates-native hero line ("Control is the move" / "Hold the position").
- 2. **Make the CTA earn the click** — swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Nulu Pilates" or "Try the new Pilates collection" — verb + specific noun tied to the story you just told.
- 3. **Add a light urgency hook** — even "New arrivals, limited sizes" or "Just landed — selling fast in [size]" gives the subscriber a behavioral reason to click before next week's email buries this one.
- 4. **Give members something** — a member-early-access note or a loyalty points reminder converts existing brand loyalists who are already subscribed but not discounting-seekers.
- 5. **Compress the cadence** — bundling the May 13 and May 16 launches into a single "two new drops" email would reduce subscriber fatigue and preserve open rates for the sends that matter.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed bug — invalid `letter-spacing` values in `.guest-voice-*` classes:**

```css
.guest-voice-lg { letter-spacing: -2%; }   /* INVALID */
.guest-voice-md { letter-spacing: -2%; }   /* INVALID */
.guest-voice-sm { letter-spacing: -1%; }   /* INVALID */
```

`letter-spacing` does not accept percentage values per CSS spec — only `<length>` (px, em) or `normal`. These declarations will be silently dropped by all rendering engines, so the intended tight tracking on KHGiga font elements will not apply. The equivalent heading rules (`h1`/`h2`/`h3`/`h5`) correctly use `px` values and are unaffected.

**Fix:** Replace with em equivalents, e.g. `letter-spacing: -0.02em`.

**Dark mode implementation — no issues:**
- `#000001` (`.black`) and `#fdfeff` (`.white`) are intentional near-black/near-white hex tricks to prevent Gmail and Outlook from inverting true `#000000`/`#ffffff` in dark mode. Correct practice.
- Three-tier dark mode targeting (Gmail `u + .body`, Outlook.com `[class~="x_"]`, Outlook Android `\0` hack) is complete and technically sound.

**`img` reset CSS — no issues:**
```css
img {display:block; border:0; padding:0; outline:0; height:auto;}
```
Prevents image gaps in table-based layouts. Correct.
