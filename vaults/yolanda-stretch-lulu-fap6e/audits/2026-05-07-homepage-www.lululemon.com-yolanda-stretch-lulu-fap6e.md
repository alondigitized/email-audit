# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get is a "New here?" email-capture modal that's literally blocking the entire homepage — which, as someone who has been buying Define jackets and ABC pants here for years, feels like a slap. The brand looks polished and premium as always, but right now it's leading with a first-time-buyer acquisition play that's completely irrelevant to me. I had to look around the modal edges to even tell I was on lululemon.

## What's working

- **"Stretch the possibilities" headline** — genuinely good wordplay for the brand and lands a little differently when you're a yoga teacher; it's not targeted at me but it *fits* me
- **"Email Me 15% Off" CTA** — copy is direct and action-oriented, no ambiguity about what happens when I tap
- **Top announcement bar** — "Get 15% off regular-priced items. Sign Up" is a clean, scannable reinforcement of the offer before the modal even fully loaded
- **Clean modal design** — no clutter, no competing visuals, just a single conversion path; modal itself is well-executed even if the strategy is wrong for me

## What's weak

- **"New here?" positioning** — I've bought probably 15 items here. This modal tells me the site has no idea who I am, which is jarring from a brand this premium
- **Homepage completely blocked** — I can't see a hero, a category rail, a We Made Too Much banner, literally nothing until I close this modal. That's a bounce risk for someone on mobile who's impatient
- **No loyalty/member hook anywhere** — no "Welcome back, log in to see your points" or member pricing badge — a huge miss for a brand with a strong loyalty following
- **Fine print placement** — the asterisk disclaimer at the very bottom of the modal ("valid online for 14 days. Additional terms and conditions apply.") feels like it's trying to hide the catch
- **No imagery** — this is a visual brand and the first thing I see is a text-and-form modal with zero product photography

## Recommendations

- **Swap the cold-acquisition modal for a returning-user-aware gate**: if I have a cookie or an account, show me "Welcome back — your points balance is X" or a We Made Too Much teaser instead of the new-user signup pitch
- **Move the email capture to a slide-in or footer unit** rather than a full-screen blocker — let me see the homepage first, earn the email later
- **Surface a loyalty/member benefit CTA above the fold** ("Members get early access to We Made Too Much — log in") to immediately give me a reason to engage as someone already in the ecosystem
- **Add a product visual to the modal** — even a single ABC pant or Define jacket shot would make this feel like lululemon instead of a generic SaaS signup form
