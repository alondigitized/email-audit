# What's new: Everyday Cotton Blend T-Shirts
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Everyday Cotton-Blend Tee launch — pure new-arrival editorial in the same vein as the July 22 Pace Breaker™ send, minus that email's tighter narrative focus. The creative is confident: a clean hero shot against a blue-and-white wall, a second styled moment against a rust-red backdrop, and a "shop new styles this week" grid that does real merchandising work. But the email tries to do three jobs at once — cotton tee launch, casual styling, and a SuperLoft fleece plug at the bottom — and by the time you hit "Heavy on the softness" the original tee message has been diluted.
- The photography and button hierarchy are on-brand and legible, and "A tee above the rest." is a decent, low-key headline. Where it loses points against the recent run is cadence and focus: this lands one day after the August 4 color-blocking send, and the fleece cross-sell at the bottom competes with the tee for attention instead of reinforcing it.
- Net: a solid, clean execution that's let down by scope creep and daily-frequency fatigue, not by craft.

## What's working

- Clean hero image with a legible headline ("A tee above the rest.") and two immediately visible CTA buttons.
- Strong secondary lifestyle shot (blue long-sleeve layered look) gives the fabric a real-world context beyond a studio tee shot.
- "Shop new styles this week" product grid adds browsing variety and a "See all" escape hatch for undecided shoppers.
- Fabric/performance detail called out in copy ("Soft Pima cotton... sweat-wicking, quick-drying") gives a concrete reason to believe, not just a product name.
- No visible render bugs — images, buttons, and spacing all look intact.

## What's weak

- No offer, discount, or urgency of any kind — this is fine for editorial but limits the click case for a value-driven persona.
- The email bolts on a second product story (SuperLoft fleece) at the bottom, splitting focus right when the tee message should be closing strong.
- This is the second lululemon send in as many days (Aug 4 color-blocking → Aug 5 cotton tee) — cadence is running hot and risks fatigue.
- Subject is flat description with zero curiosity or urgency hook ("What's new:" is now a recurring, slightly stale prefix across this brand's recent sends).
- No loyalty/member pricing or benefit surfaced anywhere, despite the footer referencing a Membership program.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the SuperLoft fleece block entirely or move it to its own send — a single-product email converts better than a two-product email, and this brand already has a proven standalone fleece send (July 29, 8.00/10).
- 2. Add one concrete hook to the subject/hero — a fabric claim ("quick-drying") or a "back in stock" / limited-run cue would beat a flat "what's new" label.
- 3. Pull "Soft Pima cotton... sweat-wicking, quick-drying performance" up into the hero copy block instead of burying it under the second image — it's the only real differentiator in the email and it's currently mid-scroll.
- 4. Space this out from the Aug 4 send — back-to-back daily sends to the same list compress open rates; a 3-4 day gap would let each land on its own.
- 5. Give "Shop What's New" a more specific label ("Shop Everyday Tees") since "What's New" is now reused across nearly every recent subject/CTA in this brand's cadence and reads generic.
