# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon came in with their whole chest — "Find your space" over a yoga lifestyle hero, with a literal "Explore Yoga & Pilates" CTA sitting right on the fold. This brand reads premium and unapologetically yoga-first, which is exactly my thing. Right now they're pushing new women's arrivals through a yoga/sensation fabric story, and honestly? It landed.

## What's working

- "The naked sensation that shaped yoga, with more room to breathe" — that subhead is specific enough to signal a fabric story (Nulu? something new?); it made me stop scrolling mid-read
- "Explore Yoga & Pilates" as the second CTA puts my category one tap away without any navigation hunting
- The 15% off banner is top-of-page and the offer language is clean — I can process it in half a second
- The full-width search bar just below the header means if I know what I want (Define jacket, ABC pant), I'm there in two taps
- Outdoor backdrop with yoga context feels current and aspirational without being try-hard

## What's weak

- No returning-member love — I'm a loyalist and there's no "Welcome back" hook, no rewards points balance, nothing that says lululemon knows I've been here 50 times before
- "Regular-priced items" in the offer banner instantly made me do WMTM math — as a weekly We Made Too Much checker, this offer doesn't reach my deal zone and that's a small but real buzz-kill
- Zero product imagery: the model's midsection is cropped and blurred; I can't actually see what's new — it's mood, not merch
- "Shop Women's What's New" is too broad for where I am in the funnel — I'm already thinking yoga, and this CTA could be for running or golf for all I know

## Recommendations

- Swap the primary CTA copy from "Shop Women's What's New" to "New in Yoga & Pilates" — you already know I'm in the yoga funnel from the hero, stop making me guess which category bucket "What's New" lands in
- Add a secondary text link below the main offer that says "See We Made Too Much" — one extra line, zero design lift, saves your WMTM-loyal shoppers a navigation step
- Put at least one product thumbnail in the hero (the Define jacket in a new colorway would nail it here) — right now the image is vibes-only and doesn't give me anything to react to
- Surface the free-shipping threshold somewhere above the fold — I genuinely cannot remember it and it changes whether I combine orders or let a cart sit
