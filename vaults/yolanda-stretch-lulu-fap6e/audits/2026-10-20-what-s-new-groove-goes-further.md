# What’s new: Groove goes further
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-made mood send for the Groove yoga line. The warm terracotta and amber photography is cohesive, and the models are in real poses (a side bend, a kneeling twist), not static catalog stands. "Get into a Groove." and "Endlessly flexible yoga pants flow beyond." land cleanly. For a stretch and yoga persona, it speaks directly to the category.
- It's still a brand-mood email with no price, promo, deadline or member perk, like the Oct 6, 7, 13 and 14 sends. The only concrete product claim is "Nulu™ fabric brings softness so light, it feels uplifting." The product links are tiny grey captions ("Shop Groove Cinch →") that are easy to miss. The three black pill buttons all read as generic "Shop" labels.
- Judgement: pretty and on-brand, but it gives no reason to click today. It's a mid-pack send, slightly stronger than the Oct 13 Steady State email because the fabric line gives it something to say.

## What's working

- The hero image, the amber studio set and the flexible poses all sell "flexibility" and "flow" without extra copy.
- The headlines are short and legible against the white background, and the first pill button sits right under the hero headline.
- The Nulu™ line is a real fabric benefit, which most of the recent lululemon sends lack (the Oct 14 Pace Breaker email was the exception).
- The product captions name specific items (Groove Cinch, Flow Y Bra, Groove Flare, Groove Wide-Leg), so a click lands on a named product.
- The "Shop new styles this week" six-up grid adds a quick catalog scan at the bottom.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the footer is clean.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, deadline or member benefit anywhere, so nothing creates urgency.
- The product-link captions are small grey text with weak contrast under the photos. The images look clickable, but the labels are easy to miss on mobile.
- All three black pill buttons are generic: "Shop What's New", "Shop Groove" and "See all". None names a product or benefit.
- The email is very long, with about eight image blocks, and the Nulu fabric claim sits mid-scroll.
- The "Add on for smooth transitions" hoodie and sweatshirt section drifts from the Groove story and dilutes the focus.
- The six-up grid has no product names, prices or captions.
- It is the fifth lululemon send in roughly two weeks (Oct 6, 7, 13, 14, 20), and the creative feels interchangeable with the earlier ones.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook near the top, such as a member perk, a "new colours" tag or a limited-quantity note. Anything real would beat a pure mood send.
- 2. Move the Nulu fabric line up next to the hero, since it's the only functional claim.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, e.g. "Shop Groove pants" and "Shop Nulu bras".
- 4. Make the product-link captions larger and darker, and add a price or a colour count.
- 5. Cut the hoodie block or the six-up grid to shorten the scroll.
- 6. Run product-named subject lines against the mood ones on the next send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Groove pants, now in Nulu™ softness`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The yoga pant that flows with you`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nulu™ fabric, four fits, one flexible feel. Shop Groove.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bend, reach, repeat: new Groove colours are here.`
