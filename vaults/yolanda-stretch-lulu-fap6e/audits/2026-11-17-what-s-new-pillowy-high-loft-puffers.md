# What’s new: pillowy High Loft Puffers
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-shot editorial send for the High Loft puffer line, and it reads like a magazine spread. The hero shows a man and a woman in cream and charcoal puffers against a dry, golden landscape. The headline, "New, insulated High Loft jackets turn the outdoors into an invitation," sits directly under it with a coral "Shop What's New" button. The photography is cohesive, with muted olive and brown tones throughout.
- The email has no offer. It is all mood and product story: UltraPeak insulation in a brick-red block, then a GORE-TEX "Quiet confidence" section. The Black Friday banner near the bottom is the only commercial hook, and it is buried below roughly eight screens of imagery. Our own 11/16 send ("Black Friday Sale: Early Access is live") led with that banner. This one demotes it to a footer.
- Verdict: strong brand craft, weak commercial pull. It works for a subscriber who is already shopping for outerwear. For everyone else it is a pretty email with a thin reason to click now.

## What's working

- The hero photography is striking and consistent, and it sells the "pillowy" puffer feel immediately.
- The brick-red UltraPeak block breaks up the scroll and gives the insulation claim a clear visual anchor.
- The women's and men's split links ("Shop Women's Coats & Jackets", "Shop Men's Coats & Jackets") route shoppers by category.
- The GORE-TEX "Quiet confidence" section adds a concrete product detail: noiseless fabric, available online and in select stores from November 20.
- The coral buttons are consistent with the brand and easy to spot.

## What's weak

- There is no price, percentage or member benefit above the fold, so the first screen has no reason to act today.
- The Black Friday Early Access banner sits near the bottom, after a very long scroll. It is the strongest commercial message in the email and the least visible.
- The CTAs are generic. "Shop What's New" appears twice and points at a whole category, not at a puffer.
- Several secondary links ("Shop Women's Coats & Jackets →", "Explore Outerwear →") are small text links that are easy to miss.
- The grids show no product names, prices or colours, so a shopper can't tell which jacket is which.
- The footer fine print is tiny and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Black Friday Early Access banner into the top third, right under the hero. It is the only urgency in the email, and the 11/15 and 11/16 sends already built that momentum.
- 2. Change the primary CTA to "Shop High Loft Puffers", and link it to the puffer collection instead of "What's New".
- 3. Add product names and prices under the grid images, and tag a hero product with a visible price.
- 4. Cut one of the two image grids and shorten the email, so the GORE-TEX story and the final banner come sooner.
- 5. Make the women's and men's links full buttons instead of small text.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lofty UltraPeak insulation, and Black Friday early access in the app.`
