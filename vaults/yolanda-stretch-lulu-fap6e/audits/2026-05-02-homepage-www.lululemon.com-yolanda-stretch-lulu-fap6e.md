# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- It's Mother's Day mode, full send — lululemon is leaning hard into gifting and celebrating moms right now, which makes sense for the calendar but doesn't quite speak to me as someone shopping for my own studio wardrobe. The brand still looks clean and premium the way I expect, but this first screen is about inspiration and gifting, not the functional activewear discovery I usually come here for.
- **5/10**

## What's working

- **15% off banner up top** — I see it immediately and it's tempting enough that I'd actually tap "Sign Up" if I'm not already in. That discount on regular-priced items is real money on an ABC pant.
- **Prominent search bar** — Huge win on mobile. I know exactly what I want half the time (Define jacket, Align size 4), so being able to search without digging through menus feels respectful of my time.
- **Clean, minimal nav** — Logo, profile, bag, menu. Nothing cluttered. Very much the lululemon aesthetic I trust.
- **"Shop Women's What's New" CTA** — Even buried in a Mother's Day hero, this pill-button gives me the direct path I actually want.

## What's weak

- **"Mom's an icon" hero** — I'm 31 and I'm not shopping for my mom right now, I'm shopping for myself. This framing pushes me into gift-giver mode immediately, which isn't why I opened the app.
- **Hero image doesn't show product** — The close-cropped face shot is editorial and artsy, but I can't see a single piece of clothing. As someone who comes here specifically to assess fit and fabrication, that's a miss.
- **"Explore the Gift Guide" as second CTA** — Two CTAs stacked, both pointing toward Mother's Day. No path to "shop for yourself" unless I use the search bar or dig into nav.
- **No We Made Too Much callout** — I check WMTM weekly. It's not above the fold at all, and that's a habit lululemon trained me into. Missing it here feels like a gap.
- **Carousel arrows visible but no context** — I can see the pause/left/right controls at the bottom, which means there are more slides — but there's no hint of what's coming next. On mobile, most people won't swipe.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop for Yourself" secondary strip** below the hero — something like "New in Studio" or "Your ABC moment" — so self-shoppers have a clear lane that isn't gift-adjacent.
- **Swap the face-only crop for a lifestyle shot that shows the product** — keep the "Mom's an icon" headline but let me see the Align or Define in action so I understand what's being celebrated AND sold.
- **Surface a We Made Too Much teaser above the fold** — even a small banner or sticky tab. It's a conversion driver for loyal customers like me and it's completely invisible right now.
- **Show slide indicator dots** on the carousel so I know there's more content worth scrolling — three dots under the hero would get me to swipe.
