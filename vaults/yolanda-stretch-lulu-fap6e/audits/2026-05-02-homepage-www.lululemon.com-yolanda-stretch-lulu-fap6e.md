# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Okay, so the entire hero is "Mom's an icon." with a gift guide push — it's Mother's Day mode, full stop. I get it, it's early May, but I came here to check if there's anything new in the Define or maybe browse We Made Too Much, and instead I'm immediately being routed toward gifting. That's not why I opened this app on a Saturday morning.

The 15% off banner at the top actually caught my eye first — that's the most relevant thing on this screen for me. But when I read it more carefully, "regular-priced items," that's a Sign Up offer, so probably for new email subscribers? I'm already on the list. If that's a loyalty perk I'm missing out on, I'd want to know — but there's zero clarity on who it's actually for.

The hero image itself is clean, very lululemon — tight crop on a woman's face, white jacket vibes, minimal styling. I respect the restraint. But "our designs draw inspiration from every move" is doing a lot of vague heavy lifting. What does that mean for me right now? Show me the Align, show me the Define, show me what dropped this week. The two CTAs — "Shop Women's What's New" and "Explore the Gift Guide" — at least give me an escape hatch into new arrivals, but I have to scroll past a Mother's Day pitch to get there.

For me specifically, this homepage is basically a waiting room. I know what I want (ABC pants in a new color, Define restocks, anything on We Made Too Much), and none of that is surfaced here. The seasonal campaign is fine — I'm not mad about it — it's just not for me today.

**5/10**
