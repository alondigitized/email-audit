# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon just opened straight to yoga — like they *knew* I was coming. "Find your space" with the Nulu copy underneath is basically speaking my language before I even tap anything. This homepage is in full lifestyle mode right now, pushing Women's new arrivals and a yoga/pilates pivot that feels very intentional, not accidental. Clean, confident, and absolutely targeting me.

## What's working

- **"The naked sensation that shaped yoga, with more room to breathe."** — That's the Nulu fabric description and every yoga teacher knows exactly what they're selling. I felt seen immediately.
- **"Explore Yoga & Pilates" CTA** — One tap to my exact category. No hunting through nav.
- **Pill-shaped dual CTAs** — Two paths without confusion: new arrivals or category drill-down. Smart hierarchy.
- **15% off regular-priced items banner** — Even if I'm already signed up, that copy reminds me there's value here and makes me think twice before bouncing.
- **Full-width search bar** — I know exactly what I want half the time (ABC Pant, Define Jacket). This lets me skip the browse entirely.
- **Outdoor lifestyle imagery** — Nature + movement is very "yoga teacher heading to the park." Not a sterile studio shot.

## What's weak

- **No Like New / resale callout** — I check the Like New program religiously. Zero mention above the fold. That's a loyalty behavior they're not reinforcing.
- **"Sign Up" for 15% off** — If I'm already a member (I am), this banner is irrelevant noise. No personalized hook for return visitors.
- **No We Made Too Much signal** — My weekly ritual is checking WMTM. Not even a hint of it in this viewport. That's a missed conversion trigger for price-conscious loyalists like me.
- **Hero subtext font size** — "The naked sensation that shaped yoga, with more room to breathe." is slightly small on mobile — I had to squint a little. The payoff copy is great but it's competing with the large hero headline.
- **No trust signals** — No review count, no "free shipping over $X," nothing to bridge from browse to buy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Like New from $X" strip** below the hero or in the announcement banner rotation — even one line acknowledging the resale program pulls loyal, sustainability-minded shoppers deeper.
- **Rotate the announcement banner for logged-in members** — swap the "Sign Up" offer for a personalized hook like "Your Member access: We Made Too Much is live" to reward return visitors instead of ignoring them.
- **Bump the hero subtext type size by 2-3px** — the Nulu descriptor is the best copy on the page and it's getting lost; it earns more visual real estate.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** to the banner or sub-hero strip — something like "Free shipping over $75" is a conversion nudge that costs nothing to add.
