# What’s new: bold colour blocking
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's color-blocking new-arrivals push, and it's the most visually confident send in the recent run — navy/burgundy/orange/lime blocks against grey and white product backdrops, with "sets the tone." as the hero line playing directly off the color-blocking concept. It's another pure editorial send (no discount, no promo) continuing the pattern from "Steady State SuperLoft™" (2026-07-29, 8/10) and "Pace Breaker™" (2026-07-22, 8/10) — lululemon is clearly leaning into new-arrival storytelling over markdown emails this summer.
- Where this one pulls ahead of most of the July sends is density and variety: seven distinct product/lifestyle shots, two CTA buttons up top ("Shop What's New" / "Shop Studio Essentials"), and a mid-email "August goes for bold" section that reframes the same color story as a monthly edit. It's long, but each block earns its place with a specific linked product.
- The gap is the offer layer — there's no loyalty callout, no urgency, nothing time-bound, which caps this at solid-editorial rather than high-conversion. For a subscriber who already loves lululemon, this is a scroll-worthy lookbook; for anyone on the fence, there's no hook to convert browsing into buying today.

## What's working

- Bold, on-theme hero: "sets the tone." headline is a clever tie-in to the color-blocking subject line, and the navy/burgundy hero image sells it immediately.
- Two clear top-of-email CTAs ("Shop What's New," "Shop Studio Essentials") give both a broad and a focused path within one scroll.
- Every product image has its own labeled shop-link ("Shop Like a Cloud Bra →", "Shop Align Jogger →", etc.) — no ambiguous or homepage-only links.
- Strong color consistency throughout — every shot (lime, orange, burgundy, navy) reinforces the "bold colour blocking" premise instead of drifting off-theme.
- Clean render, no layout bugs, no overlapping text, no broken images.

## What's weak

- No offer, no urgency, no price mention anywhere — pure aspirational editorial, same gap as most recent sends.
- No loyalty/membership benefit surfaced in the body itself (only fine print at the bottom references membership benefits, easy to miss).
- Very long single-column scroll (7+ image blocks) with no visual break or section anchor until "August goes for bold" two-thirds down — risks fatigue before reaching later products (Accessories, Scuba Sets).
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or availability signals on any featured product.
- Subject "bold colour blocking" undersells what's actually a broad multi-category drop (bras, joggers, shorts, accessories, outerwear) — reads narrower than the email delivers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a light urgency or loyalty hook near the top CTAs — even "Members get early access" would lift this from lookbook to sales driver without breaking the editorial tone.
- 2. Trim the scroll — 7 image blocks is a lot for one send; consider cutting to 4-5 hero products and moving the rest to a "Shop the Full Edit" landing page.
- 3. Move "August goes for bold." higher — it's a strong reframe of the whole email's premise and is currently buried past the midpoint.
- 4. Surface one sizing/fit detail (e.g., "cropped fit" or "true to size") on at least the hero product to reduce click hesitation.
- 5. Alt subject/preheader tests:
- **Subject Alt A:** `New colour blocking: bras, joggers, sets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `August's boldest colour drops`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Standout shades, studio-ready styles — shop the full edit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Navy, burgundy, orange — new performance styles just landed.`
