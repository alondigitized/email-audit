# Summer Sale's hottest picks
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Summer Sale closing-push email, landing June 30 — eight days after the Early Access tease (June 22) signaled the sale opening. The format is a sale catalogue: punchy orange hero, eight category-grid tiles split across Men's and Women's, a secondary "Don't miss the moment" CTA, and then a lululemon credit card promotional block tacked on at the bottom. The strategic logic is sound — remind subscribers the clock is ticking, surface every shopping entry point, move the stragglers — but the execution stops short of closing an undecided buyer. There is no visible discount percentage anywhere in the email, no hero product anchoring the offer, and no social proof. "Summer Sale" is doing all the heavy lifting.
- The credit card block is the biggest misstep. The email has successfully built urgency through the hero ("Hot today. Yours tomorrow.") and the secondary call-out ("Don't miss the moment"), then deflates it by pivoting to a $300 credit pitch. A subscriber who was warming toward a cart click is now thinking about a financial product.
- Verdict: a functional, on-brand sale reminder that will generate collection-page clicks, but it's a traffic email, not a conversion email. Compared to the editorial launches in this cadence (Flow Y, Beach Ball Blue, LuluLinen), this is significantly lower energy.

## What's working

- **Orange hero is immediately legible** — "SUMMER SALE" in all-caps against the brand orange reads instantly on mobile and creates clear visual hierarchy.
- **Category grid covers the full funnel** — Men's, Women's, shorts, pants, hoodies, accessories, and an "Under $100" price-anchor tile give every shopper a relevant entry point.
- **"Hot today. Yours tomorrow." is smart urgency copy** — concise, poetic, and doesn't lean on tired "ENDS SOON" clichés; consistent with lululemon's elevated brand voice.
- **Two "Shop Summer Sale" CTA buttons** — one immediately below the hero intro and one after the grid means no one has to scroll far to find the action.
- **"Under $100" tile acts as implicit price anchor** — even without a percentage off, it signals accessible pricing exists.

## What's weak

- **No visible discount percentage or dollar-off callout** — at this stage of a sale cycle a subscriber expects to see "up to 40% off" or similar; its absence makes the offer feel vague.
- **Credit card block breaks urgency momentum** — inserting a financial product pitch between the sale content and the footer resets attention at exactly the wrong moment.
- **No hero product** — every prior high-performing email in this cadence (Flow Y, Groove Crop restock, Beach Ball Blue) led with a single named product. This email has eight equally-weighted tiles, so nothing is memorable.
- **No social proof** — no ratings, no "bestseller" labels, no "X people bought this week" signals on any tile — all easy conversion helpers at the end of a sale.
- **Cadence fatigue risk** — this is the seventh or eighth lululemon email since June 16; even loyal subscribers may be starting to filter-scan.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add the discount range to the hero** — even a single line of "Up to X% off" beneath "SUMMER SALE" would anchor value and remove the biggest friction point. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Pull the credit card block out of this email** — it belongs in a dedicated send, not interrupting a sale push. Replace it with a "Selling fast" row featuring two or three specific named products with a low-stock signal.
- 3. **Promote one hero product across the top** — pick the single best-performing Summer Sale item, name it, show it large, link directly to the PDP. The grid can live below.
- 4. **Add a "Bestseller" or star-rating badge to at least one tile** — minimal production lift, meaningful credibility signal at the point of click decision.
- 5. **Hard-date the deadline** — "Hot today. Yours tomorrow." is good copy but vague; a "Sale ends July 1 at midnight" line would convert fence-sitters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to [X]% off: Summer Sale picks going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last days: our most-wanted Summer Sale styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes are moving — shop the styles everyone's grabbing now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `These are the ones going first. Don't wait on them.`
