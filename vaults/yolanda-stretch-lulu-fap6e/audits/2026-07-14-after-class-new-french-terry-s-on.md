# After class, new French Terry’s on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's post-Summer Sale pivot — the brand is exiting discount mode and returning to full-price editorial with a French Terry launch story. The subject ("After class, new French Terry's on") is casual and lifestyle-forward, a clear tonal reset from the urgency-driven "Summer Sale faves, moving fast" send three days ago (July 11). The blue palette continues — lululemon has now anchored three consecutive hero sends in blue tones (Walnut Crunch July 11, Summer Series July 8, this one) — which is becoming a visual signature but risks feeling repetitive to frequent openers.
- The email is well-rendered and on-brand. Three sections flow cleanly: French Terry hero, a stretch/comfort layer (hoodies + shorts), and a bags/accessories play. A bottom product grid rounds it out. The weakness is structural: no sale hook, no price, no urgency, and CTAs are small text links rather than buttons. This is a "keep the brand warm" send rather than a conversion driver.
- Honest judgment: solid execution of a low-stakes maintenance email. It looks good, delivers on the subject, but won't generate strong click volume.

## What's working

- **Hero image earns the subject** — the oversized French Terry pullover is immediately visible, photographed in natural light with lifestyle framing that matches "after class" energy.
- **Three-section narrative has flow** — comfort (French Terry) → movement (stretch/shorts) → carry (bags/accessories) is a coherent post-class lifestyle arc.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text doesn't overlap, no broken-image gaps.
- **Subject line is punchy and under 50 chars** — 35 characters, mobile-safe, and the lifestyle phrasing ("after class… on") is casual in a way that fits lululemon's voice.
- **Blue palette is cohesive** — the color story holds across all three hero sections, giving the email a unified editorial feel.

## What's weak

- **No price, no offer, no urgency** — after a multi-week summer sale drumbeat, this email asks for a full-price click with zero incentive. That's a hard pivot to make without a strong product story hook.
- **CTAs are text links, not buttons** — "Shop What's New >" and "Shop Hoodies & Sweatshirts >" are easy to miss on mobile; no visual hierarchy separates them from body copy.
- **Accessories section feels like filler** — "Get the space to give it your all" for bags is a disconnected pivot after two apparel sections; it dilutes the French Terry focus.
- **Bottom product grid is generic** — a mix of tanks, shorts, and skirts with no unifying story; doesn't reinforce the French Terry launch theme introduced in the hero.
- **Cadence fatigue risk** — this is roughly the 10th lululemon email in three weeks. The brand is pushing hard, and a low-offer maintenance send at this frequency risks inbox tune-out.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one concrete offer or loyalty hook** — even "Free shipping on new arrivals today" or "Earn double points on French Terry styles" would give the click a reason. New-arrivals emails without an incentive underperform.
- 2. **Upgrade CTAs to buttons** — replace text links with pill-style buttons (matching the sweatshirt's blue) so the primary action is scannable on mobile in under 2 seconds.
- 3. **Cut the accessories section or make it earn its place** — either lean fully into the French Terry launch (show 5-6 French Terry SKUs with the fabric callout) or replace the bag section with a "complete the look" module that ties back to the hero outfit.
- 4. **Tighten the product grid** — the bottom 6-product grid should feature only the French Terry launch items shown in the hero, not a catch-all newness dump.
- 5. **Preheader should do work** — if the preheader is just boilerplate or repeating the subject, rewrite it to a product callout (e.g., the breathability/fabric detail mentioned in the hero body copy).
- **Subject Alt A:** `New French Terry drops: wear it straight from class`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The after-class uniform just got an upgrade`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Naturally breathable, all-day comfortable — shop the new French Terry styles`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New hoodies, sweatshirts + shorts in French Terry — shop what's new`

| Saans-Regular | `https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Regular.woff` |

The CDN is blocking external `GET` requests to the font origin. Every `@font-face` rule in the `<head>` references these paths; all will silently fail. The fallbacks (`Helvetica Neue, sans-serif` and `Times New Roman, serif`) are reasonable but the brand-specific editorial weight (`KHGiga-Light`) has no close system analog.

**Note on extracted URL syntax:** The QA tool captured a trailing `)` on four of the five URLs (e.g., `Saans-Light.woff)`). This suggests the CSS `url()` closing paren may be unquoted and the extractor grabbed it — worth verifying the raw `@font-face` src values to rule out a double-paren parse error in addition to the 403.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing `alt` text — three instances:**

```
src: https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$
```

All three occurrences of `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` are missing `alt` attributes. These appear to be navigational arrow icons in the footer. Screen readers will announce the raw filename. Fix: `alt=""` for decorative use, or `alt="→"` / `alt="Next"` if they are functional links.

**Font fallback rendering:** With the 403s above, `h1`/`h2`/`h3` headers (56px / 40px / 30px, `letter-spacing: -2px`) will render in `Helvetica Neue`. The tight negative tracking may look incorrect on Windows where Arial substitutes — not catastrophic but visually off-brand at large display sizes.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — not found.**
Required for Google and Yahoo bulk senders (>5k/day threshold, enforced February 2024). Absence means Gmail may suppress the native unsubscribe button and flag deliverability. SFMC can inject this via Send Classification or a custom header on the sending profile.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not found.**
One-click machine unsubscribe is now mandatory under the same Google/Yahoo policy. Without this, automated list-hygiene tools (e.g., Apple Mail's "Unsubscribe" button, Gmail's one-click) cannot process removals server-side.

**Caveat:** Both headers may be present on the actual SMTP envelope but not captured by the AgentMail relay. Verify directly against a raw `.eml` or Postmaster Tools header trace before filing a defect against SFMC config.

**`Authentication-Results` — not captured.**
SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this audit. If DKIM is failing at the relay boundary, `d=lululemon.com` alignment would be broken and DMARC could reject or quarantine. Confirm via Google Postmaster Tools or a `check-auth2` at mail-tester.com.

**CAN-SPAM physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, but standard for lululemon sends — confirm footer contains street address before flagging.
