# What’s new: Big Cozy’s comeback
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-shot mood send for the Big Cozy fleece line. The warm wood-cabin photography is cohesive, and the hero (a woman in a pale oversized quarter-zip and shorts) sells comfort at once. The two coral "Shop What's New" and "Shop Big Cozy" buttons directly under the headline give an obvious next step.
- It has no offer, no price, no member benefit and no deadline. "New Big Cozy fleece provides a sense of relief from the season's demands" is pleasant but vague. Compared with the 2026-10-28 "Early Access: Bestsellers in new editions" send, which gave members a reason to act now, this one gives no reason to click today. It is another entry in the run of "What's new" and mood sends (10-20, 10-27, 11-03, 11-04), so it won't stand out in the inbox.
- Verdict: strong creative, weak commercial hook. It's a competent brand-building send and would not be a standout on conversion.

## What's working

- The hero image is warm and cohesive, and the oversized fleece reads as comfortable at once.
- Two clear coral buttons sit right under the headline, so the eye goes from image to headline to CTA.
- The layout is easy to scan: hero, 2x3 editorial grid, two named products, a red "Warmth that sparks curiosity." block, then a "latest drop" grid.
- Named products ("Scuba Merino Wool-Blend", "Smooth Spacer") give a few specific click targets beyond the category.
- The seasonal timing fits a mid-November cold-weather push.

## What's weak

- There is no price, percentage, shipping offer or member perk anywhere, so nothing gives a reason to act now.
- The headline copy is abstract ("a sense of relief from the season's demands") and says nothing about the product.
- The two stacked buttons overlap in purpose. "Shop What's New" and "Shop Big Cozy" split the primary action.
- The email is long, with about five image grids. The red "Warmth that sparks curiosity." block is a poetic header with no clear point, and the product grids carry no names, prices or reviews.
- The bottom links ("Download the App", "Shop What's New", "Find a Store") are large and faint, and they repeat CTAs that already appeared.
- The lululemon wordmark in the top-right corner sits very tight against the edge.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook above the fold, such as member early access, free shipping or a limited-time restock window. Right now the email has no urgency.
- 2. Replace the two buttons with a single "Shop Big Cozy" button and give it the visual weight.
- 3. Rewrite the headline to say what the product is and what is new. "Big Cozy is back: barrel-leg pants and funnel-neck pullovers" does this.
- 4. Add product names, prices and a colour or size note under the grid items, and cut the "latest drop" grid, which is generic.
- 5. Give the wordmark more margin from the right edge.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Big Cozy is back, in new barrel-leg fits`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Big Cozy fleece just restocked`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New funnel-neck and barrel-leg styles are here. Shop before sizes go.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: first look at the new Big Cozy fleece edit.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML head is well-formed: `lang="en"`, charset, viewport, `color-scheme` and dark-mode hooks are all present. The QA run turned up one confirmed defect (3 footer images with no alt text). The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not available.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were confirmed. 39 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains.
- Redirect resolution, final destinations and link health are unverified for all 39.
- No issues found in the evidence available.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** three `<img>` elements have no alt text. They all use `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`.
  - The filename suggests a decorative arrow icon.
  - Decorative images should carry an explicit `alt=""`. An absent `alt` attribute lets some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
  - If an arrow is the only content of a link, it needs descriptive alt text or an `aria-label` on the link.
- The head declares `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, `<meta name="viewport">`, `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes`, and VML/Office namespaces, with client-specific dark-mode border fixes. No issues found there.
- `font-weight: 380`, `570` and `-2%` letter-spacing are non-standard values. They are only used on custom fonts (`Saans`, `KHGiga`) with Helvetica Neue and Times New Roman fallbacks, so this is not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) are visible in the truncated source, and QA flagged none. The body was cut off, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not a confirmed defect:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. The sender (`hello@e.lululemon.com`) is on a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with an ESP sending with proper authentication.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked because the footer was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed. All 39 links are click-tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs.
- No issues found in the evidence available.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (or meaningful alt text if the arrows are the only content of a link) to the three `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images.
2. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or the full MIME source, not the relay-processed message. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment).
3. Add redirect-following for the tracking domains, or a configured allowlist, so the 39 skipped links get their final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters checked.
4. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. That would allow checks on merge tokens, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the remaining `alt` attributes.
