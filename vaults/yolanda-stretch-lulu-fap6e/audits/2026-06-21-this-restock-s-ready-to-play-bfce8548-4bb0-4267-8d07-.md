# This restock’s ready to play
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's back-in-stock trigger email, anchored by the Groove High-Rise Notched-Hem Crop 10" as the hero product — with capris framed as a cultural comeback via a winking "And just like that" reference. Below it, a six-product grid spans pants, shorts, and tops with color swatches. It's clean, on-brand, and technically solid.
- The problem is context. This lands June 21, a day after the "New summer styles easing your way" roundup (June 20), which itself followed daily sends on June 16, 17, and 18. Subscribers who've been getting one email per day from lululemon all week are primed to glaze over. A restock email needs to work harder to justify its spot in an already-crowded inbox cadence — and this one doesn't. There's no urgency language, no sold-out countdown, no "only a few left" framing. "Back in stock" is implied by the label, but the body copy pivots to trend storytelling ("retro-inspired cropped length") rather than scarcity.
- The embedded AmEx credit block at the bottom is jarring — a financial offer dropped into an athletic-wear roundup. It reads as a monetization insert, not a subscriber benefit, and dilutes the brand moment.

## What's working

- **Pop-culture hook lands cleanly.** "And just like that, capris are back" is smart — specific enough to feel editorial without overpromising.
- **Hero product has real copy.** Waistband detail, hem detail, and silhouette description give the Groove Crop more dimension than a typical product card.
- **Color swatches on all six grid products** let the reader assess availability at a glance without clicking — good mobile UX.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** Hero image → product name → descriptive copy → "Shop Now" → grid. No visual noise competing with the primary product.
- **"Back In Stock" label** does the heavy lifting of signaling the email type immediately, even if the subject line doesn't.

## What's weak

- **Subject line conceals the trigger.** "This restock's ready to play" buries the back-in-stock signal behind a sports idiom. A subscriber on the fence won't know *what* restocked or *why* now.
- **No scarcity language in the body.** A restock email's natural urgency is "these sold out before" — but there's no "quantities are limited," "grab your size," or "sold out last time" framing anywhere.
- **AmEx credit block breaks the brand moment.** Sticking a financial co-brand offer between the product grid and the footer turns the email into an ad vehicle partway through.
- **Six-product grid with no editorial hierarchy.** The Groove Crop gets the hero, but then capris, wide-leg pants, shorts, a tank, and two other bottoms are presented as equals. No "these restocked fastest" or prioritization signal.
- **Heavy cadence, zero acknowledgment.** Coming a day after a similar multi-product email with no differentiation in tone or format, this blurs into the previous send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put scarcity in the body copy.** The subject promises urgency ("This gear moves fast") but the email doesn't deliver it. Add one line under the hero — "These sold out in [X] days last time. Sizes are already moving." — and it becomes a real trigger email.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to name the product or category.** "Capris are back — and they're going fast" is more specific and preserves the pop-culture hook. The current subject reads as gym-speak, not scarcity.
- 3. **Remove or relocate the AmEx block.** If it must stay, put it in the footer below the fine print, not as a design section in the email body.
- 4. **Prioritize the grid.** Add a "Most requested restock" or "Sold out fastest" badge to one or two grid items so the back-in-stock narrative extends beyond the hero.
- 5. **Differentiate cadence tone.** Given this is the 5th email in 6 days, open with something that earns back the subscriber's attention — even "You asked, we restocked" acknowledges the context.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Capris are back — and they're going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your most-wanted styles just restocked`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Groove Crop sold out fast last time. Sizes are already moving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Back in stock: capris, Align shorts, Scuba pants, and more.`
