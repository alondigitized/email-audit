# Hit reset in new shades of Steady State
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is another well-shot lululemon mood send. This one is men's-focused, with a warm brown, brick-red and forest-green palette. The hero is a man in a chocolate-brown Steady State hoodie against dark wood, and the photography stays cohesive from top to bottom. It looks premium. It is also close to offer-free: there is no price, no percent off and no deadline, and a reader can't tell why to click today.
- The first scroll has two coral CTAs, "Shop What's New" and "Shop Steady State", stacked directly under the headline. They are generic and nearly redundant. The mid-email "Women's Lounge / Men's Lounge" split is a hedge, and it reads oddly in an otherwise male-cast send. The red "Warmth that sparks curiosity." block switches to coats and jackets, which dilutes the Steady State story. It reads as a catalog with no single hero product.
- Judgement: a competent brand-building send that will earn a few passive clicks but won't drive urgency. It sits in the 5-6 range and matches the brand's recent pattern, such as today's earlier "Big Cozy's comeback" (6/10) and the 11-04 "Winter 2026" drop (5/10).

## What's working

- The hero image is strong. A man in the dark Steady State set against warm wood sells coziness right away.
- The palette is cohesive: brown, brick red and forest green carry through every module and fit the season.
- The copy "Steady State sets in plush SuperLoft™ fabric ease the way to recovery mode" names a fabric and a use case.
- The editorial collage of fit and texture shots shows the product from many angles.
- The red "Warmth that sparks curiosity." block gives the layout a visual break and a clear second chapter.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer anywhere: no price, no percent off, no free shipping, no member benefit, no deadline.
- The two stacked coral CTAs ("Shop What's New" and "Shop Steady State") are generic, and the first is a vague duplicate of the second.
- The send isn't focused on one thing. It runs from Steady State to Lounge to Coats & Jackets to a six-item grid, so no single product or destination stands out.
- The product grid at the bottom has no names, prices or colors, and only "See all" to click.
- The "Shop Men's Coats & Jackets" link in the red block is tiny and easy to miss. The Women's/Men's Lounge captions are small too.
- Women are almost absent from the creative, apart from one image, yet the email is not clearly framed as a men's send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook above the fold. Examples: a member-pricing line, "Free shipping on $99+" or a named colorway drop. Without it, nothing separates this from the last six sends.
- 2. Merge the two CTAs into one specific button, such as "Shop Steady State Hoodies", and link it to a product category.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (the coats block and the six-item grid) or make them clearly secondary. Pick Steady State as the single story.
- 4. Add product names and prices to the grid so it supports a decision. Add a star rating or review count to one hero item.
- 5. Make the headline carry the news: name the new colors. "New shades" is in the subject but not visible in the hero.
