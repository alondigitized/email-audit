# What’s new: a next-level rest day
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Steady State SuperLoft™ fleece launch, positioned as a "rest day" product story — cozy hoodies and sweatpants shot in moody, low-light editorial style with a man lounging near a punching bag. It's a well-executed lifestyle send: strong photography, clean hierarchy, no render bugs — but it's also the fourth "new fabric/fit" product-education email in two weeks (following "Four fits, all ease" on 9/1 and the fall Yoga launch on 8/29), and it tacks on an unrelated tennis section at the bottom that dilutes the "rest day" concept entirely.
- There's no offer, no price, no urgency anywhere in this email — it's pure brand/product storytelling. For a subscriber who's already seen three similar product-launch sends this week, the incremental reason to click is thin unless SuperLoft specifically excites them.
- Judgment: competent creative, weak incremental value — this reads like a content calendar filler more than a considered send, and bolting on tennis gear at the bottom undercuts the "rest day" narrative it spent the whole email building.

## What's working

- Hero image is striking — warm, moody lighting immediately signals "premium loungewear," not a generic product shot.
- Two clear CTAs above the fold ("Shop What's New" / "Shop Steady State") sit right under the value prop copy.
- Product grid ("Shop new styles this week") gives easy category browsing with a "See all" catch-all.
- Copy is tight and on-brand ("The difference between routine and ritual is that extra softness").

## What's weak

- No offer, discount, or price anywhere — this is pure editorial, which caps click urgency.
- The tennis section at the bottom ("Bring the racket") is a jarring non-sequitur against a "rest day" theme — it belongs in a separate send.
- Extremely long, image-heavy scroll (7+ full-width images) before reaching the product grid — mobile readers have to scroll a lot to find something shoppable.
- Fourth similar "new product/fit" send in under two weeks (following 9/1 tees, 8/29 yoga pants) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone not already invested in fleece.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tennis section entirely, or split it into its own send — it breaks the "rest day" theme and adds scroll with zero thematic payoff.
- 2. Add a concrete hook — even a soft one like "new colors" or member early access — since there's currently zero urgency or price signal to justify clicking now vs. later.
- 3. Move the product grid ("Shop new styles this week") higher — right now it's buried after 5+ scrolls of pure lifestyle imagery.
- 4. Trim the lifestyle image block from 4-5 shots down to 2-3; the message ("ultra-plush fleece") is established after the first two.

- **Subject Alt B:** `SuperLoft is here — softer than your usual hoodie`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ultra-plush fleece built for slow mornings and lower reps.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hoodies and sweatpants in our softest fabric yet.`
