# Align low-rise styles are here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-first launch send for Align low-rise. The hero is a tall, cropped shot of dark plum leggings on a light-grey ground. "Low rise, down to stretch." is a good headline. The black "Shop Align" pill is clear and sits right under the copy. The first screen tells you what's new and what to click.
- The email then loses focus. After the Align story and a six-item grid, it pivots to a full red "black friday sale" banner, a "Right here, right now" women's/men's sale module, and an in-store Wild Dot Multi print feature. That's four campaigns in one send. The Black Friday banner names no discount, so the sale is advertised without an offer. The page is about 5,000px tall, which is a lot of scroll for a one-product-story email.
- Compared with the 11/21 "Black Friday Sale's new contenders" send, which also never said what the sale was, lululemon is repeating the same gap. It is a solid launch email that tries to do too much and has no concrete offer.

## What's working

- The headline "Low rise, down to stretch." is short and gives the new fit and the benefit in one line.
- The hero product shot is large, uncluttered and shows the product, so the eye lands there first.
- The single black "Shop Align" button is unambiguous and high-contrast.
- The two-up low-rise leggings and shorts under the hero reinforce the new silhouette.
- The six-item "latest Align drop" grid gives easy entry points across bottoms and tops, with consistent photography.
- The Black Friday banner is bold and ties in with the 11/19 and 11/21 sends.

## What's weak

- The Black Friday banner names no discount, percentage or deadline, so the sale reads as a vague tease.
- There are four competing stories (Align low-rise, Black Friday, Women's/Men's sale, Wild Dot print), which dilutes the launch.
- The hero shows only a cropped body with no face or lifestyle context. It's tidy but cold, and there's no fit or feel detail beyond the copy.
- The grid labels are small grey text with no prices, colours or reviews, so there is nothing to compare.
- The "Right here, right now" module has weak copy and its "Shop Now" link is tiny.
- The footer links ("Download the App", "Shop What's New", "Find a Store") are low-priority and repeat the nav.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one job for the email. Either end after the Align grid with a single Black Friday strip that states the offer, or make it a sale email with the offer in the hero.
- 2. Put a concrete offer in the banner, such as "Up to X% off" or "Ends Cyber Monday". Without one it's decoration.
- 3. Add price, colour count or a rating to the grid items, and make each tile's label a clear link.
- 4. Show a lifestyle or on-body shot of the low-rise fit beside the hero so the new rise is visible in context.
- 5. Cut or move the Wild Dot feature to a separate send. It's a store-first story that doesn't fit here.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our softest fabric, a new fit. Shop before the best sizes go.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

All three are `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right`, src `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`. Screen readers may read out the filename or URL. - **Minor (CSS):** `.guest-voice-lg`, `.guest-voice-md` and `.guest-voice-sm` use `letter-spacing: -2%` and `-1%`. In CSS, `letter-spacing` takes length values, and percentages are not reliably supported. Most clients will drop the declaration, so the spacing will be inconsistent across clients. Use `px` or `em` instead.
- **Positive:** the `:root { color-scheme: light dark }` and `<meta name="color-scheme">` tags are present. Dark-mode border fixes are applied for Gmail, Outlook.com, and the Outlook Android app.
