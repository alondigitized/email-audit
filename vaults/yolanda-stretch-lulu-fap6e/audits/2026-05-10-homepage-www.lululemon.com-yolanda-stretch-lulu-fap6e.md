# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I respect it — lululemon opens with a new jacket drop and copy that is basically written in studio-speak. "Breathes easy" + two-way ventilation is exactly the kind of functional storytelling that keeps me coming back to this brand. Right now they are pushing the License To Train Textured Jacket hard, and as someone who checks new jacket releases religiously, they have my attention within two seconds of landing.

## What's working

- **"Breathes easy." headline**: Short, sensory, functional — this is how I actually talk about gear with my students. It stops the scroll instantly.
- **Sub-copy names the tech**: "Two-way ventilation" is specific enough to signal quality without being a spec sheet. That functional credibility is why I trust this brand with a $148 jacket.
- **Two CTAs with different intents**: "Shop Women's What's New" is for browsers like me; "Shop Tracksuits" targets someone with a more specific mission. Smart mobile merchandising to serve both modes.
- **Full-width search bar above the fold**: I know this site well enough to search directly. Having it right there saves me three taps.
- **Clean mobile-first layout**: The hero fills the screen without any clutter. On my phone this feels intentional and premium, not squeezed.

## What's weak

- **Zero WMTM presence**: We Made Too Much is my weekly ritual and one of lululemon's biggest loyalty retention tools. Not a single hint of it above the fold is a real miss for engaged repeat buyers like me.
- **Sign Up banner talks past me**: I'm already in the ecosystem. Showing me an acquisition offer instead of "You have 340 points" or "Sweat Collective pricing is live" makes me feel like a stranger on my own homepage.
- **"Shop Tracksuits" CTA disconnects from the hero**: The whole page is about a jacket, then one of only two CTAs sends me to tracksuits. The logic break is noticeable on a small screen.
- **No fit or returns reassurance**: I always fit-test in store before buying on mobile. Not a word about free returns or size guides above the fold adds real friction for a mobile purchase.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the acquisition banner for a returning-member hook**: If I'm authenticated, show my points balance and a "New in your size" shortcut. Save the 15% sign-up offer for unauthenticated sessions — it's wasted on me right now.
- **Add a WMTM strip or teaser CTA in the nav or below the hero**: Even a small "This week's We Made Too Much picks are live →" would pull me straight into the funnel where I'm most likely to convert.
- **Replace "Shop Tracksuits" with "Shop Jackets" or "Explore License To Train"**: The hero is about a jacket. Both CTAs should stay in that story.
- **Drop a one-liner below the CTAs**: "Free shipping on orders $100+ · Free returns" — that one sentence would reduce my mobile hesitation significantly and costs zero design real estate.
