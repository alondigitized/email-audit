# Black Friday Sale: Early Access is live
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the send the 11/15 teaser, "Tomorrow: Black Friday Early Access," promised, and it delivers. The red-orange hero repeats the same oversized lowercase "black friday sale early access" treatment, so the continuity is strong. The "Membership Early Access" tag and the "Members, this is your moment" line make the exclusivity clear. It is also the first lululemon email in this history with real prices: four hero products with strikethrough markdowns.
- The weak point is the offer framing. The hero is pure mood and shows no number. The only discounts are small gray print under four products ($128 to $84, $68 to $49, $78 to $59), and the hero line says nothing about how much you save. The legal block is also dense, and it holds the real constraints: app-only, members only, ends Nov 18 at 11:59 p.m. PT. "Only on the app" is a friction point, because the CTAs are generic "Shop Early Access" buttons.
- Overall this is the strongest lululemon send in the recent run, mainly because of the prices and the credible deadline. It still hides its best information.

## What's working

- The hero repeats the teaser creative, which pays off the previous day's send.
- "Membership Early Access" at the top, plus "Members, this is your moment," makes the exclusivity clear.
- Four named products with strikethrough prices give shoppers something concrete (e.g., Define Cropped Jacket $128 → $84).
- The 2x2 product grid is clean and easy to scan, and it covers both women's and men's.
- Two black pill CTAs are unmistakable, and the Women's/Men's split lets people self-sort.

## What's weak

- There is no headline discount. Nothing says "up to X% off," so the hero carries no offer.
- The deadline (Nov 18, 11:59 p.m. PT) is only in the fine print at the bottom, not near the hero or CTA.
- "Only on the app" is buried in a short line of body copy, and both CTAs are the same generic "Shop Early Access."
- The prices are tiny and gray, so the savings don't register.
- The legal block is long and dense, and it contains the real conditions: members only, not in stores or online, no cash value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer and deadline in the hero or just under it, for example "Members: up to 30% off · ends Nov 18." That single change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Make the CTA say what happens: "Open the app to shop Early Access." That sets expectations for the app-only requirement.
- 3. Make the sale prices bold and larger, and add a "% off" or "Save $44" tag to each product.
- 4. Add a short "ends Wednesday" urgency line above the product grid.
- 5. Pull the three key conditions (app, members, dates) out of the legal block into one visible line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: Early Access starts now, ends Nov 18`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday Early Access is live in the app`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Define jacket $84, Hotty Hots $49. Members only, 3 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign in to the app to unlock member-only prices before Wednesday.`
