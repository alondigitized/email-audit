# Out now: breathable winter running gear
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a handsome editorial running send that undersells its own offer. The moody hero shows a runner in a pale shell against a dark sky, and the "Freezing becomes fired up." headline is memorable. The imagery is consistent and premium, as you'd expect from lululemon. But the email spends about 80% of its length on lifestyle photography before it admits there is a Black Friday sale.
- The subject promises "breathable winter running gear" and the body delivers that. Then a red "black friday sale" banner appears near the bottom with a vague line: "Hundreds of styles, in-store and online." There is no percentage, no dollar amount and no date. The "Right here, right now" copy is the only sign of urgency, and it is weak. Compared with Skechers' loud Black Friday sends this week, this reads as a brand-first email with a promo bolted on.
- Verdict: good creative, weak commercial punch. It would work as a brand email in October. On 11/24, two days before Black Friday, the offer should be in the hero.

## What's working

- The hero image and the "Freezing becomes fired up." headline are on-season and set the winter running theme right away.
- The photography is consistent: the same moody sky, the same earth tones and clear product visibility in motion.
- The split of men's and women's modules, each with its own CTA (Shop Men's Running, Shop Women's Running), lets the reader self-select.
- The red Black Friday banner stands out against the muted palette and is the one strong visual break.
- The sender is recognizable, and the footer links (Download the App, Find a Store) are tidy.

## What's weak

- The sale is buried. The Black Friday banner sits about 80% of the way down, with no discount, no end date and no code.
- The hero CTA, "Shop What's New," points at new arrivals, not the sale, and the first CTA is small and easy to miss.
- No product names, prices or fabric details appear, so "breathable" is never explained. There is no reviews or social proof either.
- The scroll is very long: roughly 6,000px of imagery with many CTAs competing, so the eye has no single focus.
- The "Beyondfeel Waterproof Running Shoe" block is a different story from "breathable" and dilutes the theme. Its tiny text link is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Black Friday offer into the hero or directly under it, with a real number ("Up to X% off") and an end date.
- 2. Make one primary CTA, such as "Shop Winter Running Sale," and repeat it. Cut the secondary "Shop What's New" variants.
- 3. Show three or four hero products with names and prices. Add a short "why breathable" callout (fabric, vents) to back the subject line.
- 4. Cut the email length by about a third: drop one men's/women's image cluster and the footer-adjacent link rows.
- 5. Give the shoe module a proper button, or remove it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Breathable layers for cold-weather miles, plus Black Friday styles, in-store and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shell, tights and shoes built for sub-freezing runs. Sale ends soon.`
