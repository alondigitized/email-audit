# New jackets Define the season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led send built around one hero: the Define jacket. A tight crop of the cream jacket's seams and zipper opens the email, and "A defining moment." sits right under it. The five-jacket grid shows the new Velvety Knit, Insulated, Cropped Nulu, Oversized Luon and a classic Luon in different colourways. It's easy to scan and clearly the strongest product-focused send of the recent run. Compared with the 11-11 mood sends ("Hit reset in new shades of Steady State", "What's new: Big Cozy's comeback"), this one shows real SKUs.
- The email loses focus in the second half. A brick-red "Presence is the gift" yoga-gifts block and an "activate early access" app banner follow the jackets. That's three competing messages, and the early-access banner reads like a membership benefit but has no clear offer. There's no price, no discount, no deadline, and a single "Shop Define" button carries the whole top section. It's a polished catalog, not a persuasive campaign.
- Verdict: good creative, soft conversion mechanics. It's a solid 6 on brand strength, and it would be a 7 or 8 with a price or a time hook.

## What's working

- The tight crop of the jacket's contouring seams and zipper makes a distinctive, on-brand hero that lands the "defining" pun.
- The 2-up, 1-up, 2-up product grid adds visual rhythm. Each jacket has a caption naming the fabric (Velvety Knit, Nulu, Luon), which helps comparison.
- The "Shop Define" button is a dark pill with good contrast, placed right under the headline copy.
- The brand is instantly recognisable: the logo is top right and the typography is consistent.
- The headline and body copy are short, and the body names the two new styles (Insulated and Velvety Knit).

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or deadline anywhere, so nothing creates urgency.
- Only one CTA button covers five products, and the individual jacket tiles don't look like they have buttons or prices.
- The red "Presence is the gift" yoga-gifts block pulls attention away from the jackets with a different category. Its "Shop Yoga Gifts →" link is small and weak.
- The "activate early access" banner says members shop first "only on the app" but doesn't say what is available early. It reads as vague, and the "Download the App" link is tiny.
- The footer repeats the app and store links ("Download the App", "Shop What's New", "Find a Store"), which adds clutter.
- The disclaimer about Membership benefits applies to nothing visible in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the jacket story. Remove the yoga-gift block, or move it to a separate send, so the jackets are the only message.
- 2. Add price and fabric cues under each jacket caption, for example "Define Jacket *Luon · $128". Make each tile obviously tappable.
- 3. Give the membership banner a concrete benefit, such as "Members: first access to Define Insulated, today only." If there is no offer, drop the banner.
- 4. Name the fit and use case in the copy (warm-up layer, cold-morning run) so the jackets feel seasonal.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific and repeat it below the grid: "Shop new Define jackets".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Velvety Knit and Insulated, with the contouring seams you know.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get first look on the app today.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed: missing alt text on 3 images.** All three are `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` (`https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`).
  - If they are decorative arrows beside text links, they should carry `alt=""`.
  - If they sit inside a link with no text, the link needs an accessible name.
  - A missing `alt` attribute makes some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Confirmed: invalid CSS.** `.guest-voice-lg`, `.guest-voice-md` and `.guest-voice-sm` use `letter-spacing: -2%` and `-1%`. Percentage values are not valid for `letter-spacing` in most email clients, so they are dropped. The other headings use px (`-2px`, `-1px`) correctly. This only affects the guest-voice classes.
- **Confirmed: non-standard font weights.** `font-weight: 380`, `570` and `300` are variable-font values. Clients without variable-font support fall back to the nearest standard weight, and the `Saans-*` and `KHGiga-Light` custom fonts fall back to `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial` or `Times New Roman`. The fallbacks are declared, so this is low severity.
- **Good practices present:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, `charset=utf-8`, viewport meta, `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` for dark mode, `x-apple-data-detectors` overrides, and the MSO/VML namespaces.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance
