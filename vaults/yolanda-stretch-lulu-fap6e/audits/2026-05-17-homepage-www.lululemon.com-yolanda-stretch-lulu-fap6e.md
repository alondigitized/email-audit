# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon knows exactly who they're talking to right now — this is unambiguously a yoga-first homepage and I felt that immediately. "Find your space" with the Nulu fabric call-out and a direct "Explore Yoga & Pilates" CTA? That's my language. The brand looks dialed-in and seasonal, though the lack of any loyalty hook or returning-customer signal means they're not treating me like someone who's been buying Aligns since 2019.

## What's working

- **"The naked sensation that shaped yoga, with more room to breathe"** — This is an unmistakable Nulu fabric reference. As a Align/Nulu devotee, this headline stops my scroll. It's benefit-led and fabric-specific, not vague.
- **"Explore Yoga & Pilates" CTA** — One tap from the hero to my exact category. No hunting through hamburger menus.
- **Poolside outdoor lifestyle imagery** — The outdoor, warm-toned setting with two women in athletic wear reads spring/summer studio commute energy. Matches my lifestyle.
- **15% off banner** — Visible immediately, not buried. Even if I'm already a member, the offer orients me to value.
- **Prominent search bar** — Above the hero fold, which as a high-tech-comfort shopper I actually use. Quick access to "ABC pants" or "Define jacket."

## What's weak

- **No loyalty/member acknowledgment** — I have a lululemon account and I've spent thousands here. The homepage has zero personalization signal — no "Member Exclusive" badge, no rewards balance, nothing that says they know me.
- **"Shop Women's What's New" is a weak first CTA** — "What's New" is vague. I'm not a browser-for-discovery shopper — I'm a yoga teacher checking if the new Align colorways dropped. "What's New in Yoga & Pilates" would be far sharper.
- **No We Made Too Much visibility** — I check WMTM every week. It's not visible above the fold, which means my highest-intent use case isn't served without digging.
- **No social proof** — No star ratings, no "10,000+ reviews," no "as seen in" — nothing that gives a first-impression trust signal to back up the premium price point.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the primary CTA** from "Shop Women's What's New" to "New in Yoga & Pilates" — it removes ambiguity and routes me directly to my category with zero extra taps.
- **Add a member chip in the top banner** — swap "Sign Up" for a dual message: "Sign in to see your member discount" or show a loyalty balance for logged-in users. I shouldn't feel like a stranger on a brand I've been loyal to for years.
- **Surface a We Made Too Much teaser** — a small secondary banner or module ("WMTM drops weekly — shop now") would capture repeat buyers like me who arrive with that specific intent.
- **Add one trust signal to the hero area** — a "Free shipping on orders over $X" line or a star-rating strip ("Rated 4.8/5 by 200K+ reviews") anchors the premium positioning and edges me toward conversion.
