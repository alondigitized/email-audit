# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is my brand doing what it does — athletic empowerment, clean visuals, zero apology. Right now lululemon is putting a Collegiate Tennis Champ front-and-center, which isn't my world at all (I'm a yoga teacher, not a court athlete), but the "Focus is power" energy translates. The email-signup 15% offer up top signals they're in acquisition mode, not loyalty mode.

## What's working

- **The 15% off top banner** — I check We Made Too Much every Friday. A concrete percentage off regular-priced items is the fastest way to make me do math on that Define jacket I've been circling.
- **"Focus is power" as a tagline** — that's yoga-brain language even though she's holding a racket. It lands.
- **The hero woman herself** — she looks my age, she's moving, she's in lululemon gear that could plausibly sub for studio wear. I don't hate the vibe.
- **The full-width search bar** — I know exactly what I want (ABC pants, Define jacket). Having search right below the header lets me skip the hero entirely and get there in three keystrokes.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no jank, no layout shift, no blurry assets. This is a brand that respects my screen.

## What's weak

- **Tennis is a category miss for me** — I wear lululemon to teach at the studio and commute in ABC pants. A court-performance story isn't wrong, but it's not pulling me in.
- **No We Made Too Much or sale teaser above the fold** — that is genuinely one of the highest-intent reasons I open this app, and it's invisible.
- **No loyalty or account recognition** — I don't see my points, a "member exclusive" badge, or anything that says lululemon knows I'm a returning customer and not a stranger.
- **No Like New callout** — I've bought from the Like New program and it's a legit differentiator. It doesn't exist on this screen.
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — no category chips, no pinned "Yoga" or "Pants" entry points. If I want to find studio wear, I have to dig.

## Recommendations

- **Pin a "We Made Too Much" chip in the persistent header or add it as a tappable category strip** — it's a loyalty signal and a high-intent traffic driver that should never be buried.
- **Add a second hero carousel slide leading with yoga/studio lifestyle** — keep the tennis story for Ayan fans but let the opening impression speak to the core audience that lives in Define jackets.
- **Surface a loyalty/member welcome module** — even a one-line "You have $20 in rewards" bar in the header would make returning customers feel seen and nudge conversion.
- **Test adding a Like New badge or module directly below the hero** — sustainability resonates with millennial buyers and separates lululemon from every other athletic brand.
