# What's new: Steady State's stripes
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure mood send for the Steady State line, and it's well made. The cobalt puffer, navy and cream stripes, and soft blue studio backdrop hold together visually, and the "Emphasis on comfort." headline gives it a clear idea. The hero is strong and the stripe motif carries through every frame.
- It's still an email with no reason to act now. There's no price, no promo, no member benefit and no deadline. The headline, "Bold stripes underline the cottony-soft feel of new Steady State sets," is evocative but doesn't say what the product costs or why to buy today. The only primary button is a generic "Shop What's New," which is the same sort of non-specific CTA the brand used on 2026-10-07 ("Just in: a new drop of fall styles") and 2026-10-06 ("What's new: iconically plush puffers").
- This reads as the seventh near-identical "What's new" editorial in about three weeks. A subscriber who liked the last few will browse it, and one who didn't will skim past it. The execution is good and the commercial pull is weak.

## What's working

- The hero is arresting. The cobalt puffer against the grey-blue backdrop lands in the first screen.
- The Steady State stripe is a consistent visual thread across the full-length shot, the detail crops and the product grid.
- The copy is short and tactile ("cottony-soft," "naturally breathable fleece"), and the "Emphasis on comfort." headline is confident.
- The "Complete the look" block (shoes, coats and jackets) and the "Shop the latest drop" grid give a clear route into shoppable product.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No offer, price, promo or member benefit is visible anywhere. The only fine print is the generic Membership disclaimer in the footer.
- The primary CTA, "Shop What's New," is generic and sends you to the whole assortment instead of Steady State.
- Section links like "Shop Steady State →" and "Shop Lounge Sets →" are tiny text links under the images and are easy to miss.
- The product grid at the bottom has no names, prices or colours, and no reviews or social proof, so there's nothing to evaluate before clicking.
- The email is very long. The "See all" button sits at the very bottom, after a lot of scrolling.
- The concept leans on one close-up of a model squinting into a hood. It's charming but doesn't show the product's fit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete reason to click in the hero, such as a price point, a member perk, or "Free returns" or "New colours, just landed". Right now there's no commercial hook.
- 2. Replace "Shop What's New" with a specific CTA like "Shop Steady State" and send it to the collection page. Make the in-image links proper buttons.
- 3. Add a price or product name under each grid item (for example "Steady State Jacket, $128"), plus a rating or "Bestseller" tag where it applies.
- 4. Cut the email by a third. Keep the hero, the stripe story, and one grid with a button near the top.
- 5. Vary the cadence and the subject formula. "What's new:" has been the template several times in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Steady State, now in stripes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our softest fleece, with stripes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cottony-soft sets in new navy and cream stripes. Shop the collection.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Breathable fleece you'll want to live in, plus the puffer everyone's asking about.`
