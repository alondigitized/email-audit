# Early Access: fall 2026 is here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's fall 2026 members-early-access launch — a full brand editorial send built around "a new baseline," heavy on moody studio-shot lifestyle imagery and light on product specifics. It follows the August 31 "First look: select styles for fall 2026" teaser almost exactly — same September 3 early-access date, same greige/olive palette, same crop-heavy editorial styling — except this time it delivers the access rather than just teasing it, which is the right sequencing.
- The email is gorgeous but functionally thin: nine large lifestyle photos, two CTAs ("Shop Early Access"), and almost zero product information — no item names, prices, fabrics, or sizing. It reads as a brand-awareness piece, not a shopping email. For a subscriber who already got the "coming soon" teaser three days ago, this is the payoff moment, and it undersells it by not showing what's actually available.
- Strong art direction, weak commerce execution. This will perform on open (recognizable sender, credible early-access hook) but underperform on click-through because there's nothing concrete to click toward.

## What's working

- The "Members, you're up first with Early Access" copy is a clear, credible loyalty hook and ties directly to the Aug 31 teaser's promise.
- Hero image renders cleanly with no layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- Consistent, cohesive visual identity across all nine shots — same location, palette, and cast, giving it a genuine "collection drop" feel.
- "full drop coming soon" closer sets clear expectations for a follow-up send and creates a natural next touchpoint.
- Diverse cast and mix of men's/women's product broadens the email's relevance beyond one demographic.

## What's weak

- Zero product names, prices, or fabric callouts anywhere in the email — a subscriber can't tell what "a new baseline" collection actually contains.
- Only two CTAs across a very long, heavily-scrolling email; most of the images aren't linked to anything specific.
- "Tap into the potential of the season" is vague filler copy that says nothing about the product.
- This is the third fall-2026-preview beat in four days (Aug 31 teaser, Aug 29 "wide open" launch, now this) — cadence risk of fatigue before the "full drop" even lands.
- No sizing, fit, or availability details visible — a repeat miss also flagged in the Aug 29 and Sep 1 audits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add product names/prices under at least the 3-4 hero looks — turn this from a lookbook into a shoppable email; this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Link individual images to their product pages instead of only the two generic "Shop Early Access" CTAs.
- 3. Replace the vague "Tap into the potential of the season" line with a concrete descriptor of what's in the collection (fabric, silhouette, key pieces).
- 4. Trim the image count — 9 large photos before the CTA is a lot of scroll for two links; cut to 5-6 and add a mid-email CTA to reduce friction.
- 5. Given the tight cadence with the Aug 31 teaser, consider merging future "coming soon" and "early access" beats into fewer, higher-density sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members-only: fall 2026 drops today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your early access window is open — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New fits, new layers, first dibs for members.`
