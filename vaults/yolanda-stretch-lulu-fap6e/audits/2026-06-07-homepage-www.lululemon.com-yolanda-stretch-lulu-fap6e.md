# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- OK, lululemon knows exactly what it's doing — this is clean, confident, and immediately recognizable as *the* activewear brand for people who take their practice seriously. Right now they're leading with the Align Short and a "famous for its range" headline, which honestly feels like they wrote it for me. The top-of-screen offer banner keeps it from feeling too precious.

## What's working

- **"Famous for its range" hero headline** — the double meaning (range of motion + product range) lands immediately for a yoga teacher. This is me.
- **Align Short + model pose** — she's mid-forward-fold in those golden shorts. That's my morning. I see myself in this shot without lululemon having to try hard.
- **15% off banner with "Sign Up" CTA** — it's at the very top, easy to spot, and the copy is clean. For a first-time browser it's a solid hook.
- **Full-width search bar** — I'm a mobile shopper and I always search for specific styles (ABC, Define). Having search front-and-center above the fold saves me from hunting through the hamburger nav.
- **"Buttery-soft Align Short stretches far beyond practice"** — "beyond practice" speaks directly to the studio-to-street lifestyle I actually live. That's not generic copy.

## What's weak

- **Hamburger-only navigation** — no quick-tap categories visible. As someone who wants ABC pants or We Made Too Much drops, I have to dig through a menu instead of tapping a rail. One extra tap = more friction on mobile.
- **No loyalty or Like New callout** — I shop the Like New resale program regularly and I'm definitely in their email list. No "member pricing," no rewards balance, no Like New callout. That's a miss for a repeat customer like me.
- **"Shop Women's What's New" is vague** — the hero calls out the Align Short specifically, then the CTA just sends me to a general "What's New" page. I'd rather tap "Shop Align Shorts" or even "Shop Yoga" and land somewhere focused.
- **No We Made Too Much / sale entry point** — I check WMTM every week. It's not even a whisper above the fold. For deal-hunters, there's no signal that markdowns exist.

## Recommendations

- **Add a second CTA for "We Made Too Much"** beneath the primary button — even a small text link. It signals savings-aware shoppers are welcome without diluting the hero.
- **Replace "Shop Women's What's New" with "Shop Align Shorts"** to close the loop with the hero copy — the headline and the subhead both point at Align, the button should too.
- **Surface a loyalty/member hook in the top banner** — swap or rotate the 15% banner to include a "Members earn double points this week" line. I'm already a customer; remind me there's upside to being logged in.
- **Add a horizontal category pill-nav below the search bar** — even just Yoga / Run / Train / Like New. Four taps, massive improvement in findability on mobile.
