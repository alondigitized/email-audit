# Black Friday Sale: up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Black Friday send, and it finally states the offer in the body. "Up to 50% off select styles in-store and online" appears under the hero, which is more than the Skechers-style teasers in the history did. The hero is a bold red-and-black field with oversized lowercase "black friday: time to lock in". It is striking and on-brand, but the 50% figure sits in small body copy rather than the hero. The eye lands on the headline and has to scroll to learn the deal.
- The body is clean. It has a pill CTA, a 2x2 grid of dark editorial photos (women's, men's, pants, shorts) and a quiet footer. There are no visible render bugs. The weak points are two near-duplicate CTAs ("Shop Black Friday" and "Shop Best of Black Friday"), category tiles with no product names or prices, and the 11/30 deadline buried in fine print. It's a competent, targeted-enough send that undersells its own offer.

## What's working

- The offer is concrete and visible: "up to 50% off select styles in-store and online", with "in-store and online" in bold.
- The hero is high-impact and consistent with the Black Friday look the brand has used through the week.
- The 2x2 grid gives clear category entry points (Women's, Men's, Pants, Shorts) with arrow-labelled links.
- The dark palette and moody photography feel premium, and the models read as active-wear shoppers.
- Layout is clean, with no broken images or overlap.

## What's weak

- The "50%" is not in the hero. The headline "time to lock in" is a vibe line with no value.
- Two near-identical pill CTAs, "Shop Black Friday" and "Shop Best of Black Friday", compete and dilute each other.
- The deadline (ends November 30, 11:59 pm PST) is only in the fine print. The hero shows no urgency, even though today is Black Friday itself.
- The grid has no product names, prices or sale markers, so nothing shows what is actually discounted.
- The footer links ("Explore our Gift Guide", "Download the App") are low-contrast grey on near-black.
- The tiny, dense fine print includes "offer may be modified or ended at any time", which weakens trust.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "black friday: up to 50% off" with an end-date line such as "Ends Monday". This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Merge to one primary CTA, "Shop up to 50% off", and drop the second pill, or make it a different action such as "Shop Best Sellers".
- 3. Add a price or discount tag to each grid tile, for example "Align Pant, $59".
- 4. Surface the Cyber Monday deadline above the fold.
- 5. Raise the contrast of the footer links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: up to 50% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Lock in up to 50% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store and online through Cyber Monday. Women's, men's, and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your shortcut to getting in the zone: leggings, shorts, and pants on sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The sampled source (`lululemon`, "Black Friday Sale: up to 50% off") is a mature SFMC template with dark-mode and Outlook handling. The only confirmed defect is four footer arrow images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this capture. The "57% pass rate" is mostly skipped or unverifiable checks, not failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified, not broken:** 28 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so there was no HTTP probe. Link validity, redirect chains and destination status are untested.
- No broken links were observed. Nothing in the data shows a malformed `href` or a mismatch between link text and destination.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** 4 instances of `<img>` missing alt text. All use the same source: `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`.
  - These are decorative arrows. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
  - If the attribute is absent entirely, some readers announce the filename.
- The `<head>` has `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes` for light and dark. It also includes VML and Office namespaces and Gmail, Outlook.com and Android dark-mode CSS hacks.
- Fonts are `Saans-Regular` and `KHGiga-Light` with fallbacks (`Helvetica Neue, sans-serif`, `Times New Roman, serif`). The fallbacks are reasonable. The font files themselves aren't visible in the truncated source.
- The rest of the HTML is truncated, so I can't assess the body structure, the `<title>`, or other images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. `Authentication-Results` was also absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The QA output itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers. I'm not treating them as sender defects.
- The From address `hello@e.lululemon.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign for authentication alignment, but that is inference, not evidence.
- The visible `<head>` has no unsubscribe or physical-address evidence. The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 28 links were skipped and the truncated source doesn't show any `href` query strings, so UTM presence and consistency, and landing page alignment, are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (or `alt="Right arrow"` if the arrows are meaningful) to the four `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images. This is the only confirmed fix.
2. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` capture, or direct from the sender, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
3. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow without click-registering, or sample a few) to check final destinations and UTM parameters.
4. Raise the HTML truncation limit, or analyze the footer separately, to confirm the unsubscribe link, physical address and any merge-token leakage.
5. In the QA tooling, report skipped and unverifiable checks separately from passes, so the pass rate isn't misleading. Here it is 57% with 0 issues.
