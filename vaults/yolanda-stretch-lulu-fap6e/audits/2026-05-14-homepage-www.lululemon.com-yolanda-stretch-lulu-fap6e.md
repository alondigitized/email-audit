# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon knows exactly who they're talking to right now. The second I land on this page I see "Find your space" above a close-crop yoga lifestyle shot with "Explore Yoga & Pilates" right underneath — this is built for me. The brand looks clean, premium, and very intentional, pushing a yoga-centric spring campaign. The 15% off banner at the top is a nice pull, though it's clearly aimed at getting me to sign up rather than rewarding me for already being here every Tuesday.

## What's working

- **"Find your space" headline**: Three words and I already feel understood. Yoga teachers are literally always talking about creating space — in the body, in the studio, in the schedule. This copy doesn't feel like a campaign slogan, it feels like my studio's vibe board.
- **"The naked sensation that shaped yoga, with more room to breathe" subheadline**: That's Nulu fabric language, and as someone who teaches in the Define jacket and owns multiple pairs of ABC pants, I know exactly what they're referencing. This speaks directly to the fabric loyalists, which I am.
- **Dual CTAs — "Shop Women's What's New" + "Explore Yoga & Pilates"**: Two options, not one. I might want to browse everything new, or I might want to go directly to yoga. They respected both impulses and put them both on the screen without making me dig into a nav hamburger.
- **15% off banner with clear sign-up CTA**: Visible immediately, dismissible with an X, not an intrusive overlay. Good friction management on mobile.
- **Carousel indicators (pause, prev, next)**: Subtle but visible at the bottom — tells me there's more to see without forcing it on me. I like the control.

## What's weak

- **No returning member hook**: I've been shopping here for years. There's nothing that acknowledges that — no "welcome back," no rewards balance, no "your saved items" nudge. The 15% offer is pure acquisition energy, and it makes the page feel like it doesn't know if I exist yet.
- **Close-crop hero image is evocative but vague**: The photo is beautiful — intimate, body-positive, yoga-adjacent — but it's so tightly cropped I can't actually see the product being featured. I'm a fit-tester by habit; I want to see the silhouette, the waistband, the inseam length. Right now I'm looking at a shoulder and some light.
- **No product visibility**: Not a single product card, thumbnail, or We Made Too Much teaser above the fold. I check that section weekly. There's zero nudge toward it here.
- **No free-shipping threshold**: For a $100+ AOV category like this, I always want to know the free-shipping number up front. It's not here.

## Recommendations

- **Show the actual product in the hero**: Keep the lifestyle mood but pull back the crop enough to see the silhouette of whatever Nulu piece this is. Even a partial waistband or a full-leg shot would let me evaluate fit before I commit a tap.
- **Add a "Back in Stock" or "We Made Too Much" entry point**: Put a second banner or a quick-link strip below the hero. A large chunk of my sessions start because I'm checking markdowns — give me a shortcut.
- **Surface a returning-member moment**: Even a slim bar that says "Member? See your rewards" with a person-icon tap would transform this from a cold acquisition page into something that acknowledges my loyalty.
- **Drop the free-shipping threshold into the promo banner**: Swap "Sign Up" standalone into something like "Sign up for 15% off + free shipping on $75+" — doubles the utility of that one line of real estate.
