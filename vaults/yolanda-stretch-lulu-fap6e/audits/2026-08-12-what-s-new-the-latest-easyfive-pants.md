# What’s new: the latest EasyFive Pants
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's EasyFive™ pant launch — a men's-only editorial send built entirely around "a stretch beyond denim." The hero shot is strong: a single model in an olive jacket and khaki EasyFive pant against a stark grey sky, immediately establishing the silhouette. It's another pure new-arrival push with no promo lever, in the same vein as the Steady State SuperLoft (2026-07-29) and Pace Breaker (2026-07-22) sends.
- Where it falls short of those two is focus. Pace Breaker built a tight "Lock In. Level up." narrative around one product; this email sprawls across three EasyFive fits, a knit polo, a track jacket, a tee, then a full six-product "Shop new styles this week" grid that has nothing to do with EasyFive at all. The review-quote block with the customer testimonial is a nice trust signal, but by the time the reader gets there the email has already lost its thread.
- Solid product photography, weak editorial discipline. This lands mid-pack — competent but unfocused.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered hero with clear product visibility (olive jacket, khaki EasyFive pant) and no render bugs.
- Customer review block ("They are lighter weight than jeans, yet maintain their shape well") with 5-star rating adds credible social proof mid-email.
- Consistent grid layout and product photography style throughout — no broken images or layout breakage.
- "Shop EasyFive →" and "Shop What's New →" links appear multiple times, giving the reader several entry points.

## What's weak

- No visible price, member pricing, or any offer — pure editorial with zero concrete incentive to click now.
- Product grid dilutes the EasyFive focus fast: by row two you're looking at a knit polo, a track jacket, and a cotton tee that aren't EasyFive pants.
- The "Shop new styles this week" section at the bottom is an unrelated six-item grab-bag (henleys, hoodies, a hat) that reads like a second, bolted-on email.
- No sizing, fit, or fabric detail visible anywhere despite the whole premise being "structured pants with a touch of flex" — a fit-curious shopper gets no substantiation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Shop new styles this week" grid entirely or split it into a separate send — it undercuts the EasyFive focus this email spent its first two-thirds building.
- 2. Add one concrete fabric/fit callout near the hero (e.g., "4-way stretch, tailored through the seat") to back up the "beyond denim" claim visually, not just in copy.
- 3. Move the customer review higher, right under the hero, so the trust signal lands before attention drifts to the secondary polo/jacket/tee grid.
- 4. Trim the product grid to EasyFive fits only (Classic-Straight, Loose-Fit, Slim-Straight) and drop the polo/jacket/tee — let one category own the email.
