# Black Friday Sale's new contenders
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This email has a loud Black Friday banner and a tidy product grid, but it never says what the sale is. The red-orange hero with oversized lowercase "black friday" continues the look of the 11/15, 11/16 and 11/19 sends. The headline, "Our new starting lineup.", then promises "in-demand styles moving quick." I see no percentage, price, or member perk anywhere above the fold.
- Six product tiles (Define, EasySet, Align, Steady State, Wunder Train, Pace Breaker) and an accessories backpack hero follow. The layout is clean and scannable, and nothing is broken. But "Shop the Sale" doesn't tell me what I'm saving. The only offer terms are in tiny fine print at the bottom, including the Nov 30 deadline, and the copy never mentions members or loyalty.
- Judgement: it's a competent brand send, but with no offer or deadline up top it's weak for Black Friday week. It's the fourth Black Friday-themed send in a week, so it needs a reason to stand out and doesn't give one.

## What's working

- The hero banner matches the 11/15, 11/16 and 11/19 creative, so the campaign reads as one story.
- The hierarchy is clear: banner, headline, one pill CTA, then the grid.
- The product grid is clean, with consistent photography and a mix of men's and women's models. Each tile has a "Shop [Name] →" label.
- The "Shop the Sale" and "Shop Best of Black Friday" buttons are high contrast and easy to find.
- Nothing is broken in the render. Images load and spacing is even.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or percentage appears anywhere in the visible hero or body. The email asks me to "shop the sale" without saying what the sale is.
- The deadline (Nov 30, 11:59 PM PT) and the terms sit in tiny fine print near the bottom. The urgency is almost invisible.
- "Our new starting lineup" and "new contenders" use sports-roster language that tells me nothing about price or fit.
- The accessories backpack hero takes a lot of space for a single product, with no product name or price.
- Nothing speaks to members or perks, and there are no reviews, sizes, or price points on any tile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero, for example "Up to X% off" or a named price, with the Nov 30 deadline beside it. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Add prices or "from $__" under each product tile, and name the one or two best sellers.
- 3. Surface member benefits in a single line under the headline if the recipient is a member.
- 4. Cut the backpack block to a smaller tile, or replace it with a second product row.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop Black Friday leggings".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: new styles, now on sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wunder Train, Align + more, Black Friday prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deals end Nov 30. See the styles moving fastest.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Leggings, tees and joggers, now in the Black Friday sale.`

The "38% pass rate" mostly reflects those skipped checks, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Footer links returned 403 (probe):**
  - `https://shop.lululemon.com` (link text "lululemon.com")
  - `https://shop.lululemon.com/en-ca` (link text "lululemon.ca")

- **Generic `<title>`.** It is `lululemon` and does not match the subject. This is minor.
- **Positives:**
  - `<html lang="en" dir="ltr">`
  - `viewport`
  - `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags

  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`
- No rendering defects can be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect. Verify against the raw message at the source mailbox.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

1. Add `alt=""` (and `role="presentation"` if desired) to the four `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images.
2. Re-test the two footer URLs with a browser-like User-Agent or a headless browser before treating the 403s as broken links.

4. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message instead of the relay copy. Until then, treat the compliance and deliverability warnings as inconclusive.
