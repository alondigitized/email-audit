# Black Friday hit its peak: 500+ new styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, on-brand Black Friday send that never says what the deal is. The red-and-black hero with oversized lowercase "new to black friday sale" continues the look of the 11/15, 11/16, 11/19 and 11/21 sends, so it's instantly recognizable. The headline is ragged and staggered, which reads as a design choice rather than a bug.
- The weak point is the offer. "500+ styles added. Get a move on—in-store and online." gives a count but no discount, no price, and no member benefit. The 11/21 send had the same problem, and the 11/19 "Black Friday Sale is in motion" send scored 8 mostly because its sale framing was stronger. The sale terms are buried in tiny footer fine print, with a deadline of Nov 30 at 11:59 pm PST.
- Verdict: strong brand look, thin content. It's a competent reminder that doesn't give anyone a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and unmistakable, and it matches the earlier Black Friday sends.
- There is one clear, repeated primary CTA: a black "Shop Black Friday" pill, placed above and below the grid.
- The 2x2 grid (Women's, Men's, Leggings, Pants) gives easy category entry points and has clean, consistent photography.
- The layout is clean with no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- "In-store and online" is a useful detail for a Black Friday shopper.

## What's weak

- No visible discount, price, or percentage anywhere above the footer.
- The "500+ new styles" count is not a reason to buy, and the grid doesn't show which items are new or on sale.
- The deadline (Nov 30, 11:59 pm PST) appears only in the fine print, so there is no visible urgency.
- The grid links have no product names or prices, and the models are posed in ways that show little of the fit or detail.
- The dark footer links ("Explore our gift guide", "Shop What's New") are low-contrast grey on near-black.
- It is the fifth near-identical Black Friday creative in two weeks, which risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero or the line under it. Name what's discounted and by how much, or state the member benefit, in one concrete line. Even "Markdowns on 500+ styles" is better than the current copy.
- 2. Pull the deadline out of the footer into a visible line such as "Ends Nov 30."
- 3. Label the grid tiles with a price or "from $X", and use the leggings and pants tiles to show specific products rather than categories.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 11/19 and 11/21 sends, for example with a product-led hero or a countdown.
- 5. Raise the contrast of the footer links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `500+ new styles just joined Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Black Friday markdowns, ends Nov 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New leggings, pants and tops, in-store and online through Nov 30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh arrivals added to the sale. Find your size before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path, not from the email itself. The HTML was truncated, so the body links, UTM parameters, and footer could not be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Present and correct:** `<html lang="en" dir="ltr">`, `<meta charset="utf-8">`, a viewport meta, `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags with a matching `:root` rule, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
- **Observation, not a defect:** custom fonts (`Saans-*`, `KHGiga-Light`) have Helvetica Neue, Arial, or Times New Roman fallbacks. Any `@font-face` declarations fall in the truncated portion, so I couldn't check whether they load.
- The "57% pass rate" with 0 issues and 3 warnings is a scoring artifact. The warnings are mostly capture limitations, not email defects.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body could still contain some.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and `Authentication-Results` headers were not found. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not evidence that lululemon omits them.
- **Not verifiable:** the footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated source.
- **Needed to settle it:** check the raw `.eml` or the original message headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the List-Unsubscribe pair.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The body links and their UTM parameters fall in the truncated HTML, and the 28 tracking links were not resolved. Resolving the redirect targets would let you check landing-page alignment with the "500+ new styles" subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images. This is the only confirmed fix.
2. Fix the capture pipeline before drawing compliance conclusions. Preserve raw headers from the relay (or fetch the original `.eml`) so the List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results checks produce real results.
