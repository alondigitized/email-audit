# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, lululemon knows exactly who they're talking to right now — this is a Pilates-girl moment and I am very much that girl. The Everlux push with the "hot Pilates' spiciest moves" copy feels like they wrote it after stalking my Instagram. Clean, confident, and very on-brand for a premium studio-lifestyle label hitting a cultural moment hard.

## What's working

- **"Hot Pilates' spiciest moves" copy** — this is so specifically me. It's playful, current, and nails the cultural moment around hot Pilates without being cringe. As a yoga teacher who cross-trains in hot Pilates, I felt seen instantly.
- **Dual CTA structure** — "Shop Women's What's New" vs. "Shop Warm-up and Workout" gives me two entry points without making me choose between a content journey or a utility browse. Love having both options.
- **The 15% offer in the banner** — specific number, regular-priced qualifier stated upfront, sign-up mechanic is clear. It's honest enough that I don't feel tricked.
- **Everlux hero model** — she's doing a yoga side-stretch, she looks like she could be teaching a class, not performing in a photoshoot. Relatable and aspirational at the same time.
- **Search bar visible above the fold** — I shop with intent (ABC pants, Define jacket) and having search ready immediately is clutch on mobile.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook for me as a returning customer** — I already shop here weekly for We Made Too Much. Where's my rewards balance? Where's the "member price" badge? The 15% Sign Up promo is clearly targeting new email subscribers, not rewarding me.
- **No free-shipping callout** — this is standard info I want before I tap into a product page. Not seeing it above the fold makes me feel like I have to remember to check.
- **No urgency on the 15% offer** — is this forever? Ends Sunday? Runs out at 50 sign-ups? Without a deadline it feels like a permanent banner, which makes me tune it out.
- **No quick-access nav category strip** — on mobile I want a horizontal scroll of Women / New Arrivals / We Made Too Much / Sale without having to open the hamburger menu.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Member exclusive" or rewards-balance badge** next to the cart icon for signed-in users — even a "🌙 Your points: 340" chip would make me feel recognized and accelerate a conversion.
- **Add a thin category pill-strip** below the search bar (Women / We Made Too Much / New / Sale) — saves a menu tap, which is the difference between browse and bounce on mobile.
- **Put a deadline on the 15% banner** — "Ends Sunday" or "Today only" turns it from wallpaper into urgency. Right now it reads like a permanent fixture I learned to ignore.
- **Swap the second CTA "Shop Warm-up and Workout"** for something more specific like "Shop Everlux Styles" to keep the hero message coherent and drive fabric-discovery engagement.
