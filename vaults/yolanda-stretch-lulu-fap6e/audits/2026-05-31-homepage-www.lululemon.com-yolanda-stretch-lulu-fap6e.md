# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the vibe is clean and confident — very lululemon. They're leaning into a new Bermuda short launch with "More room to Groove," which honestly feels like it was written for me. That said, the above-the-fold is doing a lot of brand work and not a ton of shopping work — no product rails, no loyalty nudge, just one hero and a pill button. It reads more like an editorial magazine spread than a storefront.

## What's working

- **"More room to Groove" headline** — loose, fun, Millennial-coded copy that doesn't feel too trying-hard. I smiled. The sub-copy ("loose through the leg and buttery soft all over") does actual product work, not just vibes.
- **15% off banner with a close button** — the X matters. It doesn't trap me, which immediately builds goodwill. The offer is real and above the fold.
- **Hero model casting** — she looks like me or someone in my studio. Not a 19-year-old elite athlete, not a mom in a minivan. Just a put-together woman in her 30s wearing something I'd actually wear to commute in.
- **Clean mobile layout** — lululemon's app-level polish is showing. Nothing is cramped, the search bar is accessible, font sizing is comfortable to read at arm's length.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty or member hook for existing customers** — I'm probably in lululemon's top 20% of spend, and the only offer I see is "Sign Up." Give me my points balance, a member-exclusive price, something that says *you know me.*
- **"Shop Women's What's New" is category-wide, not specific** — I came for pants or jackets; landing on "What's New" puts me in a browse-everything funnel. A second CTA like "Shop Bermuda Shorts" next to the body copy would do real conversion work.
- **Zero product thumbnails above the fold** — I can't see price, reviews, or even a second colorway before tapping. For a high-AOV brand where fit drives decisions, one product card would build purchase confidence faster.
- **Hamburger-only navigation on mobile** — I can't get to "Pants" or "Jackets" in one tap. Everything is buried behind the menu. That's friction for a Tuesday morning "quick check before class" shopping session.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Member Exclusive" label or rewards-balance chip** in the nav bar next to the bag icon — even "You have 450 points" would make me feel seen and accelerate purchase.
- **Swap the single hero CTA for two side-by-side pills**: "Shop Bermuda Shorts" and "Shop Women's What's New" — let the headline drive the specific launch while the second button catches the browser.
- **Surface a horizontal category strip** (Pants / Jackets / Shorts / Accessories) just below the hero, replacing some white space — one tap to my category on mobile is table stakes.
- **Add a "Like New" or "We Made Too Much" badge/link** somewhere above the fold — I check WMTM every week; a persistent hook would convert me faster than almost anything else on this page.
