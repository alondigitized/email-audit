# Not just new—new for you
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's "personalized picks" recommendation email — a hero product (Polartec 200 Series Fleece Half Zip) followed by a "picked for you" grid of six items spanning tops, hoodies, outerwear, and leggings, closed with an Amex co-brand ad. It's a straightforward algorithmic-recommendation format the brand hasn't run in this recent stretch of sends — a genuine break from the moody editorial and single-product-launch emails that dominated 2026-09-06 through 2026-09-16.
- The personalization framing ("Already picked just for you") is a smart pivot after weeks of generic launch pushes, but the execution doesn't back up the promise — there's no visible offer, no price, no urgency, and the grid reads as a generic assortment rather than anything that looks tailored to a specific shopper. Compared to 2026-09-10's "Spacer Tracksuit" send (6/10, praised for being clean and product-led), this one is product-led but diluted by six competing SKUs with no hierarchy.
- Net: solid change of pace creatively, but it's a browse-inducing email, not a click-and-buy one — there's nothing to create urgency or make someone stop scrolling.

## What's working

- Clear, single hero product up top (fleece half-zip) with a full-bleed lifestyle shot and legible copy block beneath it.
- "Picked just for you" framing is a fresh angle versus the last six sends, which were all generic launch/collection pushes.
- Clean grid layout for the six secondary products, each with name, color swatches, and consistent product photography.
- No render bugs — images, text, and layout all display correctly with consistent spacing.
- Amex co-brand placement at the bottom is compact and doesn't compete with the core product content.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer anywhere in the email — no member price, no %/$ off, nothing that gives a reason to click now versus later.
- "Just for you" personalization claim isn't visible as true personalization — the grid looks like a generic bestseller/new-arrivals assortment, not a tailored pick.
- No urgency or time-boundedness at all — nothing to prompt action today.
- Six competing product CTAs after the hero dilute focus — no clear single next step ("Shop Now" only appears under the hero).
- Subject and preview don't reflect the specific hero product (fleece half-zip) shown in the render, so there's a mismatch between promise and payoff.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible incentive — even a modest one (free shipping threshold, member-exclusive early access window) — since a "picked for you" email with zero offer gives no reason to act now versus browsing later.
- 2. Make the personalization claim visible — reference the actual signal ("Based on your recent Fleece browsing" or a name token) rather than generic "just for you" copy that reads as boilerplate.
- 3. Cut the grid from six items to three-four and add individual "Shop" links under each, not just the hero — right now only the top product has a CTA.
- 4. Align subject/preview to the actual hero product so open-to-payoff is coherent.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your fleece era starts here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Picked for you: Polartec fleece + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The half-zip we think you'll wear on repeat`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cold-weather comfort, curated for you`
- 5. Given three of the last four sends leaned heavily editorial, keep leaning into this product-grid format for future sends — but pair it with the offer/personalization work above.

## 1. Overview
