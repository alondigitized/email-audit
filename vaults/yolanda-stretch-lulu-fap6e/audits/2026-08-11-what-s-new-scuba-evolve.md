# What’s new: Scuba Evolve
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's Scuba Evolve launch — a franchise refresh (Scuba is an established lululemon line, not a brand-new product) wrapped in "Obsession, renewed." editorial copy. Visually it's on par with the brand's strongest recent sends: a bold magenta/orange/grey palette against a stone-and-sky backdrop, confident full-bleed hero photography, and a clean two-model cover shot that immediately signals "new season colorway" rather than generic promo filler.
- Where it falls short of the July 22 Pace Breaker™ send is narrative discipline — this email has five separate copy blocks (Obsession renewed → SuperLoft Light fabric → roomier fits → accessories) each with their own micro-CTA, which fragments attention rather than building toward one clear action. It reads more like a lookbook than a single-offer marketing push, consistent with the brand's pattern of no-promo, editorial-only new-arrival emails.
- No render bugs, no broken images, on-brand typography throughout. Solid but not top-tier — it's a "new colorway of an existing product" story dressed up as a bigger launch, and the email correctly plays it as quiet editorial rather than oversells it.

## What's working

- Hero image is striking — two models, strong color contrast (deep pink vs. sage/grey), immediately legible as a new drop.
- Dual CTA under the hero ("Shop What's New" / "Shop Scuba Evolve") gives both a broad and a specific path with no ambiguity in button copy.
- Fabric/fit callouts ("Naturally breathable SuperLoft™ Light fabric," "Roomier fits and ribbed side panel details") give a concrete product detail a shopper would actually care about — texture and fit, not just aesthetics.
- Consistent brand voice and clean footer utility links (Download the App, Shop What's New, Find a Store) add legitimate secondary paths without clutter.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no price, no %, no loyalty angle — which is fine for editorial but caps urgency and click motivation.
- Five stacked sections each with a different micro-CTA ("Shop Scuba →", "Shop Accessories →") dilutes the primary action; a reader has to decide which link matters most.
- Subject "What's new: Scuba Evolve" is nearly identical in structure to four of the last six sends ("What's new: Everyday Cotton-Blend," "What's new: bold colour blocking," "What's new: Steady State SuperLoft™") — the formula is getting repetitive for a regular subscriber.
- No social proof, no sizing/availability info, no urgency signal anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the mid-email CTA sprawl — pick one hero product (the pink pullover or the sports bra) and drive every secondary block toward "Shop Scuba Evolve" instead of splintering into Scuba/Accessories/What's New.
- 2. Break the "What's new: ___" subject formula this cycle — it's now the 5th nearly-identical construction in six sends; try a benefit-led angle instead.
- 3. Add one concrete hook (early access window, "just landed" timestamp, or member-only preview) to give a reason to open today vs. later.
- 4. Surface a sizing/fit detail (e.g., "true to size, now with more room through the hip") near the roomier-fit copy — it's the one rubric signal on real product detail that's currently missing.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Breathable fabric, roomier fits — see the new colorways.`
