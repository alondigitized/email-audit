# New running gear steps into the light
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a dark, atmospheric mood send for lululemon's reflective running line. A runner in dusk light opens the email, and the nighttime photography is cohesive and well shot. The "New, reflective gear lights up when you do." headline and the "Visibility-boosting accents keep confidence high when light runs low" line give it a real product benefit. That puts it in the same camp as the Oct 14 "Pace Breaker, built tougher" send, which also named a concrete fabric benefit.
- It is still a brand-image email with no offer. There is no price, no product name and no loyalty hook. The reader sees about eight stacked images of reflective detail and has to guess what to click. The two "Shop What's New" buttons go to a generic destination. The Holiday Shop banner mid-email also competes with the running story. The images are strong but are not tied to any product.
- Verdict: good creative, low conversion mechanics. It is a solid 5-6 send for a runner, like the Oct 13-27 mood sends. It would lift with named hero products and a reason to act now.

## What's working

- The reflective-gear concept fits the season. Darker evenings make visibility a timely benefit.
- The opening headline states a benefit ("lights up when you do"), not just a mood.
- The photography is cohesive. The dark blue and black palette with pink and neon-yellow accents holds together.
- The ambassador credit (Montana Farrar-Seaton) adds a credible human touch.
- The red "Shop What's New" button stands out against the dark background.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or product name anywhere in the email.
- Both CTAs read "Shop What's New", which is generic and not tied to a specific product.
- The middle of the email is a long run of near-identical dark image tiles with no copy. Mobile readers will lose the thread.
- The Holiday Shop banner ("Give the feeling.") shifts the message away from running right when the reader is engaged.
- The "We think you'll also love" grid shows no names or prices, and the dark tiles at the top are hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name and price the hero products. Add 2-3 callouts like "Fast and Free Tight, reflective, $128" next to the images, with links straight to those product pages.
- 2. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop reflective running gear", and place it right after the first image block.
- 3. Trim the image stack from about eight tiles to four. Put a short benefit line under each remaining one.
- 4. Move the Holiday Shop block below the product grid, or send it separately.
- 5. Add a member line, such as "Members get free shipping", if the recipient is a member.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Be seen on your next night run`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Reflective details on tops, tights and shorts. Shop the new drop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Visibility that keeps confidence high when light runs low.`
