# Summer Sale's softest sets
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon's sixth Summer Sale email since June 22 and the fourth since June 30. Rather than another catalogue dump, the brand pivots to a deliberate color story: every product shown in Lilac Play, all sets, all soft-handle fabrics. That editorial restraint is smart at peak sale fatigue — it gives a repeat opener a visual reason to stop rather than delete. The orange-on-lavender contrast lands well, and "Matching sets in Lilac Play bring a fresh feel to every move" is actual copy, not a placeholder.
- What the email surrenders for that editorial control is conversion mechanics. There is no discount percentage in the hero, no "was / now" pricing on any product card, and no closing deadline — "Don't miss the moment" is an inert phrase on day six of a sale. A subscriber who bought the July 2 "150+ new picks added" email and came back for lilac matching sets will find the products. A subscriber on the fence needs a number, and this email refuses to give one.
- Strongest read: a brand-building send dressed as a sale email. It maintains lululemon's voice and visual system impeccably, but leaves closing urgency entirely off the table.

## What's working

- The orange sale banner against the all-lilac product grid creates instant visual contrast — the hero registers as "sale" before a single word is read.
- "Matching sets in Lilac Play" is a specific, merchandised editorial angle — feels like a curated table, not a random product pull.
- Product cards are clean: images load, prices are visible, layout has no render errors.
- "Shop Summer Sale" CTA is unambiguous and appears both above and below the product grid.
- Subject line at 26 characters is mobile-safe, uses alliteration ("softest sets"), and names the sale — solid inbox hygiene.

## What's weak

- No discount percentage anywhere in the visible frame — a sale email with no number ("up to 40% off") treats the discount as assumed, which it isn't for a new or lapsed subscriber.
- No end date or deadline — "Don't miss the moment" is filler; this phrase has appeared across multiple lululemon sends without a timestamp to back it up.
- The single-colorway format abandons any subscriber who doesn't want lilac — no "Shop all colors" escape hatch is visible in the scroll.
- No loyalty signal, member pricing, or early-access callout — the June 22 email built anticipation for members specifically; by day six, that equity has been fully spent and not refreshed.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the discount number in the hero.** "Up to 40% off" (or whatever the real range is) directly under "SUMMER SALE" turns this from brand content into a buying trigger. Single highest-impact change.
- 2. **Add an end date.** "Ends July 8" or "Last 48 hours on these sets" makes "Don't miss the moment" land with actual weight.
- 3. **Add one cross-color escape valve.** A small "Shop the full sale palette →" link at the bottom of the product grid retains subscribers who scrolled past "not my color."
- 4. **Surface a loyalty hook.** Even a one-liner ("lululemon members: your early restock access is live") gives the loyalty cohort a reason to click that strangers don't have.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale softest sets — up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Lilac sets on sale — ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Matching sets in Lilac Play, now in the Summer Sale — shop before they sell out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your softest summer set is on sale right now`

Five web font assets return 403 on `globalstaticassets.lululemon.com`, causing complete brand font fallback across all clients. Compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, DKIM/SPF) are absent from the captured relay headers, creating deliverability and one-click unsubscribe risk.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Font CDN — 5 broken asset requests (HTTP 403)**

All five custom fonts fail to load:
```
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Light.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Regular.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Medium.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-SemiBold.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/KHGiga-Light.woff
```

All five fall back to `Helvetica Neue` / `Times New Roman`. The 403 response indicates the CDN is blocking unauthenticated cross-origin requests — likely a missing CORS policy or expired CDN signed-URL scheme on `globalstaticassets.lululemon.com`. This affects `Saans-*` (body, headlines, CTAs) and `KHGiga-Light` (guest-voice serif display). **Severity: high** — affects every email client that supports web fonts.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not found**

The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) under their 2024 enforcement policy. Absence risks inbox placement degradation or bulk-folder routing.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header — not found**

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is absent from relay-captured headers. Gmail requires this for bulk senders. If the header exists at the MTA layer but was stripped by the AgentMail relay, this finding should be re-verified against raw MTA headers directly.

**Authentication-Results — not found**

SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from the captured headers. The sending domain `e.lululemon.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain) should have both SPF and DKIM configured — absence in captured headers is likely a relay artifact, but must be confirmed against the original SMTP envelope.

**CAN-SPAM** — footer content not visible in the truncated source, so physical address and unsubscribe mechanism presence cannot be confirmed from this review.
