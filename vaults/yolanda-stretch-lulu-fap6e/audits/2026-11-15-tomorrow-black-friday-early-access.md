# Tomorrow: Black Friday Early Access
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, on-brand teaser built to push app installs, not to sell product. The red-orange hero with "be first: black friday sale" in oversized lowercase type is unmistakable and stops the scroll. After the hero, the email gives the reader nothing to act on. There is no discount figure, no product, no category and no model. The only promise is "our biggest Early Access drop ever."
- The offer is also gated. Early Access is "only on the app," for signed-in Members, with the real deadline (Nov 18, 11:59 p.m. PCT) in grey fine print. The footer nav ("Explore our gift guide," "Shop What's New") pulls attention away from the single app-download job. Compared with the 11/14 "Sets for a season of ease" and 11/12 jackets sends, which at least showed product, this one is all hype. It will drive curiosity and app downloads from existing fans, but it won't convert anyone who needs a reason.
- Judgement: strong creative and a credible urgency hook, but a thin, gated offer. It's a 6-7 send that could be an 8 with one concrete number.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and cohesive. Big white type on saturated red reads instantly and signals "event," a clear break from the recent mood sends.
- "Tomorrow" in the subject and "starting tomorrow" in the body make the urgency credible.
- The single dark "Download the App" button is high contrast and unambiguous.
- Member framing ("Membership Early Access") gives a loyalty-exclusivity angle.
- The layout is clean and short, with a clear top-to-bottom flow and no render bugs.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer: no %, no $, no product. "Discounts vary by item" is the only pricing information.
- The end date (Nov 18) and the real conditions sit in small grey fine print. The offer feels gated and hard to trust.
- "Only on the app" adds friction. A non-app-user has to install something before seeing any value.
- The footer link list repeats "Download the App" and adds distracting exits (gift guide, Find a Store) that dilute the single goal.
- "Get notified of restocks and launches. Opt-out anytime." is a confusing, off-topic line right under the CTA.
- The hero has no product or models, so nothing signals the stretch/yoga focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero or the first line, for example "Up to 40% off, app-only, Members first." One specific figure would lift the email more than anything else.
- 2. Move the deadline ("Ends Nov 18") out of the fine print and into the body copy next to the button.
- 3. Add a 3-4 product row (leggings, align-style tops, wrap layers) so the reader sees what the sale covers.
- 4. Make the CTA say what happens: "Get Early Access in the App."
- 5. Cut the footer nav to one or two links and delete the "restocks and launches" line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: Black Friday starts tomorrow, in-app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early access to Black Friday opens tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members-only deals on leggings, layers and more. Ends Nov 18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Open the app first. Prices drop tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is well-formed for email, with a `lang`, a viewport meta tag, dark-mode handling and a VML/Outlook namespace. The QA run found one 403 on a legal link, four footer images without `alt`, and three header warnings that the AgentMail relay probably caused. The source was truncated and 19 tracking links were not probed, so the body, footer and links could not be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] 403 on the Membership Terms link:** `https://shop.lululemon.com/help/legal/terms-of-use#lululemon-membership-terms` (link text "lululemon Membership Program"). A 403 on a public legal page most likely means bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe rather than a dead link. It is unconfirmed, so re-test with a browser user agent. The QA report also logged an empty text field for this link, so check that the anchor in the source has the expected text.
- **19 tracking links skipped:** Their redirect domains were excluded from the HTTP probe, so their destinations and resolution are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 4 instances:** `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right`, `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`. These are likely decorative chevrons beside footer links, so they should carry `alt=""`. With the attribute missing, some screen readers announce the filename.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.guest-voice-lg`, `.guest-voice-md` and `.guest-voice-sm` set `letter-spacing: -2%` or `-1%`. Percentages are not valid for `letter-spacing`, so clients drop the declaration. Those headings fall back to default tracking, unlike the `px` values used on `h1` to `h3` (`-2px`, `-1px`).
- **Fonts:** `font-weight: 380` and `570` are non-standard weights. They will snap to 400 and 700, or behave inconsistently, where the custom `Saans-*` and `KHGiga-Light` faces don't load. The fallbacks are `Helvetica Neue, sans-serif` and `'Times New Roman', serif`.
- **Positive controls:** The source has `<html lang="en" dir="ltr">`, `<meta charset="utf-8">`, `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes` meta tags and `:root` rules, and Gmail and Outlook.com dark-mode targeting (`u + .body`, `x_` prefix, `\0` hacks). It also has `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` resets and `format-detection`. No issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body is not covered by that check.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. This is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw `.eml` or the original message headers before treating it as a failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The relay is again the likely cause. The From domain is `e.lululemon.com`, so check the SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records for it directly.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unverified. The 19 click-tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters, destination URLs and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The only resolved link is the 403 terms URL above, which has no UTM parameters. That is appropriate for a legal page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Retest `https://shop.lululemon.com/help/legal/terms-of-use#lululemon-membership-terms` with a real-browser user agent to rule out a false positive from WAF blocking.
2. Add `alt=""` to the four `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images, or a descriptive alt if they are meaningful.
3. Change the `letter-spacing` values on `.guest-voice-*` from `%` to `px` or `em` (for example `-0.02em`).
4. Inspect the raw message source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture pipeline so these checks stop returning false warnings.
5. Resolve the 19 tracking links with a redirect-following probe (GET, follow redirects) so UTMs and final destinations can be checked.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and any merge tokens.
