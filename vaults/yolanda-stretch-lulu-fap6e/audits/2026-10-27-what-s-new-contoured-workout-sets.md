# What’s new: contoured workout sets
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-shot mood send for lululemon's Wunder Train line. It has a chocolate-brown set with cream piping against a dusty blue-grey wall, and the "Your quiet edge." header sits above a large hero of a woman mid-stretch holding a medicine ball. The Everlux™ line is the only real product claim, and it is easy to find: "In the Wunder Train Piped Set's breathable Everlux™ fabric, you've got more."
- There is no price, promo or deadline anywhere. After the Oct 6, 7, 13, 14, 20 and 21 sends, this is the seventh in three weeks that sells atmosphere instead of an offer. The product story is strong, but nothing gives a subscriber a reason to act today. The lower half also drifts. Bags, coats, joggers and shoes are all one-line "Shop X →" tiles, then a 6-tile "We think you'll also love" grid ends in a generic "See all" button.
- Judgement: well-made and on-brand, but low-urgency. It works for someone already shopping the line and does little for anyone else.

## What's working

- The brown, cream and blue-grey palette is cohesive, and the piped detailing reads clearly in every crop.
- The single black "Shop What's New" pill under the headline is an unambiguous primary CTA.
- The Everlux™ breathability line gives one concrete product benefit, unlike most of the recent mood-only sends.
- The 2x2 detail crop (bend, reach, waistband, leg piping) shows fit and movement better than a flat catalog shot.
- The Membership Early Access block at the bottom is a loyalty hook, and the footer is tidy and legible.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or deadline, so the email gives no reason to click now.
- "We've got more, too." and "We think you'll also love" are two stacked cross-sell sections. They dilute the Wunder Train story, and the generic "See all" button makes it worse.
- The Membership Early Access block is tiny and sits below the fold. The loyalty angle is buried instead of used.
- The secondary tiles ("Shop Bags →", "Shop Joggers →") have small, low-contrast link text.
- The cadence is repetitive. It is another "What's new:" subject, as on Oct 13 and Oct 20.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the cross-sell sections to one row, or drop them, and end on a single Wunder Train CTA. This would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Put a concrete hook in the hero. Examples are a member early-access window, a "new arrival, limited sizes" note, or a price on the set.
- 3. Move the Membership Early Access block up beside the hero CTA.
- 4. Make the CTA product-specific: "Shop the Wunder Train set" instead of "Shop What's New".
- 5. Add fit or fabric proof near the hero, such as sizing range, a review snippet or a "most-loved" tag.
