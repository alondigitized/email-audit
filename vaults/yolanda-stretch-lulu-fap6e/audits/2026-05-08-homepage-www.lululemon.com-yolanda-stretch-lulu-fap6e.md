# Homepage snapshot · www.lululemon.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- This is lululemon doing what lululemon does — clean, confident, product-first. The hero is a new training jacket with a "Breathes easy" headline that feels very much in my lane as a studio teacher, and the 15% off banner up top is the first thing I see. It reads as a brand that knows I already know it, and it's pushing newness over deals.

## What's working

- **"Breathes easy." headline** — short, sensory, speaks directly to a yoga teacher who lives in ventilated fabrics. That landed in under a second.
- **License To Train product callout** — naming the jacket and the feature (two-way ventilation) in the sub-copy is the right move on mobile; I don't have to hunt for "what is this?"
- **15% off banner at the top** — it's the first thing I read. For someone who checks We Made Too Much every week, an offer above the fold is a real hook.
- **Two CTA buttons** — "Shop Women's What's New" and "Shop Tracksuits" give me two landing points; I'm not stuck with one generic destination.
- **Minimal nav chrome** — logo, search bar, account icon. Clean and fast. I can search "ABC" immediately.

## What's weak

- **No yoga/studio category shortcut** — The CTAs route to What's New and Tracksuits. As a yoga teacher, I want "Studio" or "Yoga" or "Align" one tap away — that's my first filter every visit.
- **No Like New or We Made Too Much surface** — Both are things I check religiously, and neither is visible here. A loyal, sustainability-minded customer is walking right past her favorite section.
- **No returning-member hook** — I have a lululemon account, but the page treats me like a stranger with an acquisition-focused "Sign Up" banner. No loyalty points balance, no "pick up where you left off."
- **"Shop Tracksuits" feels off for my session** — Tracksuits skew streetwear/casual. For someone who came to find Define jacket restocks or ABC pants, this CTA is misdirection.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Tracksuits" for "Shop Studio" or "Shop Yoga"** — that second CTA slot is wasted on a category that doesn't fit my visit intent; surface the yoga/studio category directly.
- **Add a WMTM teaser module or "Last chance" strip** — even a thin banner ("We Made Too Much: new markdowns today") above or below the hero would turn weekly WMTM checkers into same-session converters.
- **Replace the acquisition sign-up banner with a member-logged-in state** — show loyalty points or a member-exclusive offer for signed-in users; save the "Sign Up" nudge for unrecognized visitors.
- **Test a second hero slide for yoga/Align/Define** — the carousel arrows suggest there are more slides; the first slot is training, but a Define jacket or Align moment as slide two would immediately serve my use case.
