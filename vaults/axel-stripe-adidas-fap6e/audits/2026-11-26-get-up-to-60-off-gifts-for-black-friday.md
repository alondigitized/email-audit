# Get up to 60% off gifts for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Black Friday send from adidas in a week (11-18, 11-21, 11-23, now 11-26), and the first with a visible countdown timer. The "ENDING SOON" banner (02 days, 20 hours) with an enormous "UP TO 60% OFF" hero makes the offer and the deadline unmistakable. The "gifts" angle in the subject is not delivered in the body. The only gift-flavored copy is one sentence about "holiday gifting."
- The middle of the email is thin. The Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons are generic, and the three Top Sellers (BADBO 1.1 Low, Campus 00s, Trefoil quarter zip) carry no prices or discount badges, so nothing shows what 60% off actually means. The Harper Murray volleyball giveaway then takes over the bottom half with a full-size hero and its own CTA. That splits attention away from the sale, and it is member-only content in a mass promo.
- Judgment: a competent, honest promo with strong urgency but weak product proof. It is roughly a 6, in line with the recent sends. It is nearly a repeat of the 11-23 "Up to 60% off for Black Friday is live" email, plus a timer.

## What's working

- The countdown banner gives credible, specific urgency, and it is new compared with the earlier Black Friday sends.
- The "UP TO 60% OFF" hero is huge and legible over a warm red photo, so the offer lands immediately.
- The CTAs are consistent black buttons with arrows, and they are easy to scan.
- The preview line "Ready, set, go: savings up to 60% off" is real copy that carries the offer.
- The Top Sellers row gives three concrete products in a clean three-up layout.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "gifts," but the body shows no gift guide, gift categories or price points.
- The Top Sellers show no prices, sale prices or discount percentages, so the savings stay abstract.
- The Harper Murray giveaway takes up about a third of the email and competes with the sale for the click.
- The Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons are stacked, left-aligned and generic, and each leaves a lot of empty space beside it.
- The first screen is mostly header and banner. The hero image is about 450px wide and sits off-center on a 680px canvas, which looks slightly misaligned.
- This is the 4th near-identical Black Friday blast in 8 days, so the cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add sale price and "% off" badges to each Top Seller, for example "$65 $39", so the 60% claim shows up on real products.
- 2. Make the gift angle real: add a "Gifts under $50 / $75 / $100" row in place of, or above, the Men/Women/Kids stack.
- 3. Move the Harper Murray giveaway below the footer-adjacent area, or send it only to adiClub members, so the sale keeps the whole body.
- 4. Collapse the three shop buttons into a single row, or one primary "Shop Black Friday" button, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- 5. Make the hero full-width and left-align the text with it, so the layout looks intentional.
- 6. Vary the creative from the 11-23 send so subscribers don't see the same email again.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts up to 60% off — ends in 2 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday gift list, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Campus 00s, Samba-style classics and more. Deals end Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Countdown's on: gift-ready styles for him, her and the kids.`
