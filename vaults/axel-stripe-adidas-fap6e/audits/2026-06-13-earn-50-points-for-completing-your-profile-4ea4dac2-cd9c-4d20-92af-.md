# Earn 50 points for completing your profile
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is an adiClub onboarding / profile-completion nudge — not a promotional send. The value exchange is explicit (50 points for completing your profile) but the email body completely buries the hook: the subject line is the only place "50 points" appears. Inside the email, all you get is generic "let's get to know each other" copy and a single gray CTA button. There's no reinforcement of the point value, no reminder of what 50 points actually gets you, and no urgency whatsoever.
- The creative is minimal but functional — clean layout, recognizable adidas typography, and the adiClub logo above the fold. The hero image is a group of smiling young people which is fine but does nothing for a subscriber who cares about product. This email knows what it wants to ask but forgets to argue why you should bother.
- Coming off the recent Summer Kickoff 40%-off sends (June 3, June 12) and the adiClub FIFA Rewards email on June 16, this lands as a quiet housekeeping chore in the inbox — the kind of email that gets opened once and then ignored if the CTA doesn't hook you immediately. It doesn't.

## What's working

- **adiClub brand is prominent** — logo appears in the top-right, tying this to the loyalty program cleanly.
- **Subject line delivers the offer** — "Earn 50 points for completing your profile" is concrete, specific, and accurately describes what's inside.
- **Single CTA, no distraction** — one button ("COMPLETE MY PROFILE →"), no competing links in the body.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, image loads, text is legible, social icons appear correctly.

## What's weak

- **50 points is not mentioned anywhere in the email body** — the entire incentive lives only in the subject line; the body just says "you'll also get 50 adiClub points" buried in running copy at small size.
- **"What are 50 points worth?" is never answered** — no translation to dollar value, discount level, or tier progress.
- **Hero image is generic** — smiling group shot has no product, no sport, no style signal; it could be any loyalty email from any brand.
- **No urgency** — no expiry on the offer, no deadline, nothing that makes "do this now" feel necessary.
- **CTA button is gray and understated** — the button blends into the layout rather than commanding action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Bring the 50-point value into the hero copy** — replace "Let's get to know each other" with something like "Complete your profile → unlock 50 points ($5 toward your next order)" so the value is visceral, not abstract.
- 2. **Add a deadline** — even "Offer expires in 7 days" would sharply increase click pressure.
- 3. **Upgrade the CTA button** — use adidas black or brand red rather than gray; make it visually dominant.
- 4. **Translate points to money or reward** — one line: "50 points = $5 reward" or "one step closer to Gold tier" turns an opaque currency into a real motivator.
- 5. **Swap the hero image** — show a product the subscriber would actually want, connected to what completing a profile unlocks (personalized recommendations in your category).
- **Subject Alt A:** `You're 50 points away — complete your profile`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 minutes → 50 adiClub points (here's what they're worth)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fill in your preferences and we'll surface gear made for you — plus 50 points land automatically.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `50 points = $5 toward your next order. Takes under a minute.`
