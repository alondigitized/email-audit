# The only bag you'll need all year
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a thin, single-product email dressed up with a subject line ("The only bag you'll need all year") that doesn't match what's actually on offer — three backpacks and a "back to school" hook, not a year-round positioning. The hero image is genuinely decent: three backpacks (white/black, blue, tie-dye) staged on school chairs against a brick wall, which reads as authentic and on-theme. But there's no price, no discount, no urgency, and the whole email is one screen of content stretched thin with a lot of white space above and below.
- Against the brand history, this is another back-to-school backpack push sitting right next to "School-ready in Samba" (2026-09-03) and "Own the basketball court and the classroom" (2026-09-08) — three back-to-school sends inside a week, two of which ("classroom" and this one) don't actually deliver a coherent classroom/school narrative beyond a product shot. That's fatigue territory for a subscriber, and the subject line here overpromises versatility the email never earns.
- Judgement: functional but forgettable. The visual is fine, the CTA is clear, but there's no offer, no urgency, and a subject line disconnected from the body. Score mid-low.

## What's working

- Hero image is real, well-composed lifestyle photography (three backpacks on school chairs, brick wall) — no stock-photo staleness.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW" with arrow) appears twice, both times high-contrast black-on-white and unambiguous.
- Header bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") gives a secondary, low-commitment entry point.
- Copy is short and readable, no lorem-ipsum or render bugs.
- adidas logo placement is clean and centered, reinforcing sender identity.

## What's weak

- No offer at all — no %/$ off, no free shipping, nothing. It's a full-price product push.
- Subject line ("the only bag you'll need all year") is not supported by the email, which is squarely a back-to-school backpack pitch — a mismatch between promise and payoff.
- Excessive dead white space top and bottom with almost no content — the email feels unfinished/short for what should be a hero send.
- Preview/preheader text ("Handle everything the school year throws at you with bags for every need") just restates the subject's theme rather than adding new information.
- Third back-to-school-flavored send in a week (following Samba 09-03 and "classroom" 09-08), risking repetitive-cadence fatigue for the same persona.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer (e.g., "20% off backpacks" or a bundle deal) — right now there's zero reason to act now versus any other day.
- 2. Fix the subject/body mismatch — either write a subject about back-to-school backpacks specifically, or broaden the body to justify "the only bag you'll need all year" (show a gym bag, travel duffel, etc., not just three backpacks).
- 3. Tighten the layout — cut the top/bottom whitespace and add a second product module (e.g., duffels/lunch bags mentioned in body copy but never shown) to justify the "for every need" claim.
- 4. Space this out from the other two back-to-school sends this week to avoid subscriber fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Back-to-school backpacks, 20% off this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New backpacks for the whole school run`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, duffles, and lunch bags — all in one place`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Still time to gear up before the bell rings`
