# Save up to 40% off right now
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a bare, generic sitewide sale email — "Summer Essentials Sale, up to 40% off," no product shot, no jersey, no athlete, just a moody macro-texture hero image (looks like a folded shoe fabric) with stacked type over it. It's clean and legible, and the three category CTAs (Men/Women/Kids) are a genuine usability win — most adidas sends in this run bury you at a single homepage link. But there's zero specificity: no hero product, no named collection, no urgency, and "Summer Essentials" in late August reads stale, not seasonal-relevant.
- Compared to the brand's recent run — the 2026-08-19 "Collaborations Archive Sale" was similarly thin (one hero, one CTA, no products), and this one only improves on it by adding the three-way category split. It's a step up from that send but still sits well below the Messi jersey drop from 2026-08-20, which at least had a real product and story to click toward.
- Judgement: functional and honest (discount is upfront, no code needed, exclusions disclosed plainly), but forgettable. It'll get clicked by someone already in a buying mood, not someone the email talks into it.

## What's working

- Clear, singular offer stated twice (hero image + body copy): "up to 40% off," no code needed.
- Three distinct category CTAs (Shop Men / Women / Kids) let the reader self-segment instead of landing on a generic homepage.
- No render bugs — text is sharp, buttons are legible, hero image loads cleanly.
- Fine print ("Exclusions apply") is visible in-body, not hidden in a footer wall of legalese.

## What's weak

- No product is shown anywhere — no shoe, no apparel, nothing to visually anchor "summer essentials."
- "Summer Essentials Sale" in a late-August send feels like a leftover seasonal push, not a current campaign (compare to the brand's back-to-school and fall-forward messaging elsewhere).
- No urgency or deadline — "up to 40%" with no end date invites "I'll get to it later" and no follow-through.
- No adiClub loyalty tie-in, despite adiClub being a recurring beat in this brand's cadence (see 2026-08-19 newsletter).
- Hero headline typography (justified letter-spaced caps) is stylish but low-contrast against the dark, busy background in places — legible but not a strong scannable focal point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real hero product shot (a shoe or apparel item actually included in the sale) instead of an abstract texture background — this is the single biggest lift available; specificity drives clicks far more than mood photography.
- 2. Put a real deadline on the sale ("Ends Sunday," "48 hours only") — right now there's no reason to act today.
- 3. Swap "Summer Essentials" for language that matches the actual calendar moment (back-to-school, transition-to-fall) so it doesn't read like a rerun.
- 4. Add an adiClub member-pricing line if applicable, consistent with the loyalty beats this brand runs elsewhere.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off summer styles — no code needed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off before fall drops`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Men, Women & Kids favorites before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusions apply — see what qualifies for 40% off.`
