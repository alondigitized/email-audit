# Bad Bunny F50 Ghost Sprint
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident single-product drop email — adidas Originals x Bad Bunny F50 Ghost Sprint — and it's the strongest execution of "one hero, one story" the brand has sent this cycle. Two full-bleed shots of the shoe (a hero side profile and a moody rear/sole detail crop) on a rich burgundy backdrop do the heavy lifting, and the Bad Bunny co-brand lockup gives it instant collab credibility that generic sale emails lack.
- But it's a release announcement dressed as if the reader can just buy it — and they can't, really. "Available at select stores*" buried under a beautiful hero, with the asterisk resolving to "not guaranteed, first-come first-served, six stores nationwide" is a bait-and-switch for anyone outside NYC/LA/Chicago. That's a real problem for open/click intent: most subscribers will click "SHOP NOW" expecting to buy, then discover it's a regional drop lottery.
- Compared to the brand's recent history — the 09-12 "$80 and under" send (6/10, clean and honest) and the 09-08 "basketball court and classroom" miss (3/10, subject didn't match content) — this lands in the middle: gorgeous creative, but the offer mechanics undercut it. Strong visual send, weak on the fine-print honesty signal.

## What's working

- Two hero product shots — a full side profile and a dramatic rear/sole detail crop — both render cleanly with no image breakage.
- The Bad Bunny x adidas Originals lockup logo is a clear, recognizable co-brand signal placed right after the hero image.
- Copy is tight and specific: "reinterpretation of the adidas Originals F50 with the Ghost Sprint," naming the silhouette and materials (spacer mesh, structural cage).
- Two distinct, well-differentiated CTAs — "SHOP NOW" and "FIND STORE NOW" — correctly signal this is a limited/in-store release, not a standard e-comm push.
- Consistent black/white/burgundy brand palette throughout, no layout breakage or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The core offer is misleading at a glance: "Available at select stores*" reads like a minor caveat, but the asterisk reveals only six U.S. stores carry it and stock isn't guaranteed — that's exclusionary for the vast majority of recipients.
- No price is shown anywhere in the email, unusual for a product-drop send and a missed signal for purchase intent.
- The top utility bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW") is a generic, mismatched CTA competing with the actual hero story right below it — confusing hierarchy at the very top of the email.
- No urgency or release-date framing (drop date, time window) despite this clearly being a hype/limited release — a missed lever for a collab drop.
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or "notify me" / waitlist option for the ~99% of subscribers who don't live near one of the six stores.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the store-availability caveat out of fine print and into the hero copy itself — e.g., "Drops in 6 cities only" as a visible eyebrow line — so expectations are set before the click, not after.
- 2. Add a waitlist/notify CTA for subscribers outside the six markets instead of sending everyone to the same "SHOP NOW"/"FIND STORE NOW" pair that will dead-end for most.
- 3. Cut the generic "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" top banner — it dilutes the collab story and should either be removed or replaced with drop-specific info (release date/time).
- 4. Add a price or "coming soon" price range — even collab drops with limited stock benefit from a visible price point.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bad Bunny's F50 Ghost Sprint drops — 6 cities only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Bad Bunny x F50 Ghost Sprint is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited release, in-store only — find your nearest drop location`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Reserved for six cities. See if yours made the list.`
