# Now in stock
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean best-sellers grid with a bolted-on adiClub promotion, and it has no offer. The Samba hero is attractive and the four-product grid shows price, star rating and a Prime badge. The pitch is "newest shoes," though, and nothing tells the reader to act now. Compare it with the 2026-11-05 "Week of Deals last call" send, which had a countdown and up to 50% off. This one is a catalog page.
- The email also splits its attention. The top half sells sneakers, and the bottom half switches to an MLS Cup Playoffs and Apple TV points-redemption module. The two halves share no theme. The subject, "Now in stock," promises availability, but the body shows no stock or sizing signal. The strongest part is the Pokémon Superstar cluster. It is distinctive, but the email never names the Pokémon collab in a headline.
- Judgement: competent and tidy, but low-urgency and unfocused. About a 5.

## What's working

- The Samba hero is large, well lit and instantly on-brand.
- Each product tile shows price, star rating and name, so a shopper can judge value without clicking.
- The Pokémon Superstar styles are eye-catching and feel timely for gifting season.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are high-contrast black with an arrow and are easy to find.
- The adiClub x Apple TV module is a real member benefit, with a clear "Redeem Now" button.

## What's weak

- There is no discount, code or deadline anywhere. After the 30% COZY send on 11-10, this reads flat.
- The Pokémon collab is never named in a headline, so the strongest hook is buried in product captions.
- The MLS/Apple TV block has nothing to do with the sneaker content above it and dilutes the single-CTA focus.
- The headline "Elevate your sneaker game" is generic, and the "Discover the latest and greatest" body line is filler.
- The tiny "Subscription required" text beside the MLS graphic is unreadable. The Prime badges add clutter without adding an offer.
- The top preheader line is a plain underlined text link, which looks unstyled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one reason to act today: a code, free shipping, or a "Pokémon drop is live" headline. Use the hero for the Pokémon pairs, not a generic Samba.
- 2. Move the adiClub x Apple TV module to its own send, or cut it to a thin footer strip.
- 3. Replace the "Elevate your sneaker game" headline with something concrete, such as "The Pokémon Superstars just landed."
- 4. Add a size-availability or low-stock cue to back up "Now in stock."
- 5. Give the main grid a single named CTA, like "Shop Pokémon Superstars," instead of repeating "Shop Now."
