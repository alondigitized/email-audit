# Save up to 60% during Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, honest, high-contrast Black Friday send. The "UP TO 60% OFF" hero is huge and legible, and nobody will miss the offer. Compared with the 11-18 "Up to 60% off: Black Friday first access ends today" send, it drops the countdown banner. That also drops the only credible urgency adidas has been running all week.
- The email is generic. It has no product, no price and no deadline. It has three gendered "Shop" buttons that all go to category pages. It reads as the public-launch version of the member-first-access campaign already sent on 11-15, 11-16, 11-17 and 11-18. A subscriber who has seen four versions of "up to 60% off" this week gets no new reason to act.
- Judgement: well executed, low on information. It will work as a reminder and not as a driver. Expect a solid-but-undifferentiated click rate, with little lift over the previous sends.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in white type over the dark red brick and red tracksuit, and the model's smile adds warmth. The eye lands on "UP TO 60% OFF" first.
- The red hero image matches the Black Friday mood without relying on a timer.
- The body copy is short and plain. It names product types (classic sneakers, jerseys, cozy fleece) and says "for yourself, family and friends".
- The three black CTA buttons are large, evenly spaced and tappable. Each is verb + noun.
- Nothing is broken. The layout is single-column, with no overlapping text and no missing images.

## What's weak

- No deadline. The countdown banner from 11-18 is gone, so there is no reason to click today instead of next week.
- No product or price appears anywhere. The hero is a lifestyle shot, and "classic sneakers, jerseys, cozy fleece" never shows a single item.
- The CTAs are three equal category buttons, and all of them lead to a category page. Nothing tells the reader which button holds the best deals.
- The preheader repeats the subject ("deals up to 60% off" vs "Save up to 60% during Black Friday"). It reads as brand filler: "celebrate the season the 3-Stripes way".
- It is the fifth near-identical "up to 60% off" message in about a week. The creative and the offer have not moved since the first-access sends.
- The preheader sits as underlined text at the top of the body and looks like a link. The footer copy ("Stories, styles and sportswear since 1949") is dead space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline. Restore a countdown or a line such as "Ends Cyber Monday". Time-bounding is the single biggest lever.
- 2. Replace the lifestyle hero, or add a row below it, with 3–4 specific products showing a before and after price (for example, "Samba $100 → $70").
- 3. Make the CTA row pull its weight. Use one primary "Shop all deals" button, with secondary links that go to hero products or deal categories instead of the demographic split.
- 4. Rewrite the preheader so it adds information (the deadline, or a named product) instead of repeating the subject. Make the top line plain text without an underline.
- 5. Differentiate this send from the previous four. Either escalate (for example, an extra 10% off for members) or change the story.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sneakers, fleece & jerseys, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Cyber Monday. Sneakers, fleece and gifts for everyone on your list.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Samba, Superstar and Ultraboost deals are live now. Members get free shipping.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a real deliverability defect: the plain-text part is 0 chars (QA FAIL). Several `http://` image sources and two images missing alt text also show up. The truncated HTML and the relay-stripped headers mean compliance, personalization and UTM checks can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking redirects not verified.** 21 links on the click-redirect domain were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA WARN), so I can't confirm destinations or redirect chains. The one visible example is `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1fc4c6b06-e6d7e1f2-...`.
- **Insecure tracking URL.** That click-domain URL is served over `http://`, and the same `http://` host appears as an image `src` (see section 3). The `CI0/...` path is probably an open-tracking pixel, but the truncated source doesn't confirm it.
- **Mixed scheme.** Hero and other assets use `https://dv.adidas.com/o/...`, while the arrow icon uses `http://image.link.adidas.com/...`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images (QA WARN).** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is used 6 times (`alt="arrow"`). The tracking image `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` is also `http://`. Clients that block mixed content may not load them. The tracking one would then miss opens.
- **Missing alt text.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/2307719c-090e-494b-8ac1-8cd62673ecaa?cp_tp=...` has no alt. `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` (`...XI=452`) has no alt. The second is probably a tracking pixel, where `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Arrow icon alt.** `alt="arrow"` on a purely decorative icon adds screen-reader noise. `alt=""` would be better.
- **Head and layout.** `<html lang="en">` is present. The viewport meta is `initial-scale=1.0` only, with no `width=device-width`. The page uses an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with table layout and media queries at 639/640px. Nothing there is broken per the source I have.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and subject ("Save up to 60% during Black Friday") contain no unresolved tokens. The source is truncated, so the footer and body weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. A single-part HTML email is a spam-filter signal and gives no accessible fallback.
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Not found, but QA says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed defect. The sender is a large brand that is very likely to include them.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found for the same reason, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain, `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable from the data. Every click goes through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects that were not probed. The final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are therefore unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain alternative.** It is the only confirmed failing check.
2. **Move tracking and image hosts to HTTPS.** That covers `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com`.
3. **Fix alt text.** Set `alt=""` on decorative arrows and the tracking pixel, and add descriptive alt to `dv.adidas.com/o/2307719c-...`.
4. **Re-run QA with raw headers.** Use the original `.eml`, or a relay that preserves `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. **Follow the redirects in a headless browser.** This would resolve the 21 skipped links and let you check UTMs and the landing pages.
6. **Optional:** use `width=device-width` in the viewport meta.
