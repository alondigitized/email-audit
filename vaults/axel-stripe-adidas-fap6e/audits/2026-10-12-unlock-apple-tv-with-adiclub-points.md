# Unlock Apple TV with adiClub points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the monthly adiClub newsletter, and it opens with its best idea. The Apple TV hero is a black block with three posters (The Dink, Slow Horses, an MLS ball) and a clear "Redeem Now" button. Swapping points for 2 or 3 months of Apple TV is a fresh, concrete loyalty perk, and the subject leads with it.
- The email then turns into a stack of three giveaways: the Samba OG Shoe of the Month, the 2026 Rookie Class signed footballs, and the Evo SL Exo College Pack. Each has its own CTA, so the Apple TV offer stops being the one thing to do. The newsletter format also means there is no deadline and no point cost anywhere, so the one real offer has no price attached.
- It's decent for a loyalty send but not sharp. Compared with the Oct 6 "Up to 50% off our favorites for fall" send (7/10), which had one clear number, this one asks you to do more with less to anchor on. And it lands on the heels of the Oct 10 text-signup send and the Oct 11 Club América jersey send, so it adds to a near-daily cadence.

## What's working

- The Apple TV hero is the strongest visual: a black field, three posters, and an instant read of what's on offer.
- "Redeem Now" is a solid black button directly under a short explanation, so the primary action is easy to find.
- The copy is specific about the perk: 2 or 3 months, MLS matches, Apple Originals, no ads.
- The member-only framing ("Exclusively for adiClub members") fits a loyalty newsletter.
- The Samba OG on orange and the rookie-class photo grid break up the middle of the email with strong imagery.

## What's weak

- The email never says how many points the Apple TV subscription costs. "Terms apply" is the only qualifier, so the offer feels incomplete.
- Four CTAs compete (Redeem Now, Enter Now twice, Enter Now at the bottom). Nothing tells you which one matters.
- The giveaway links are plain underlined text, so they look weaker than the black buttons.
- There is no deadline on anything, so nothing creates urgency.
- The thin serif "Your adiClub October Newsletter is here" line at the very top looks unstyled next to the polished adidas header. The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / Shop Now" bar adds a fifth call to action before the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. State the cost in the hero, for example "Apple TV for 2,500 points", and add a visible end date. This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the three giveaways to one, or move them below a divider labeled "Also for members". Keep Apple TV as the only hero.
- 3. Style the giveaway links as buttons that match the Redeem button.
- 4. Add a line showing the member's own points balance, so "redeem" feels reachable.
- 5. Remove the top "Shop Now" bar from loyalty sends, or style the preheader line like the rest of the header.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus MLS matches, no ads. See how many points you need.`

# Technical Audit: adidas, "Unlock Apple TV with adiClub points"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clear structure (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, media-query breakpoints at 640/639/480/414/320px). The only hard QA failure is the missing plain-text part. This looks like the open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop it and undercount opens. It is also an unencrypted request that exposes the recipient-specific token.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/c787502a-8f76-4603-8940-030eac9cea05?cp_tp=…` has no `alt`. With images blocked or a screen reader, nothing conveys the Apple TV / adiClub offer.

- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. The `@media` rules may not trigger consistently in some mobile clients. The responsive CSS partly compensates.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is legacy but works with the table-based layout.
- **Fonts:** The `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `src: local('Arial'), …`. No web font files are loaded, so all clients fall back to Arial or Helvetica. This is a consistent fallback, not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appeared in the truncated source. The rest of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text alternative:** The text part is 0 characters, so this is likely a multipart/alternative with no `text/plain` part, or the relay dropped it. Missing text parts can raise spam scoring and break text-only or accessibility clients. Confirm in the raw MIME whether the sender omitted it or the relay stripped it.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them. - **[WARN] Authentication-Results:** The header is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay. The sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 16 tracked links are wrapped in `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects whose final destinations were not resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the Apple TV / adiClub offer could not be confirmed. The image URL carries a `cp_tp=` token, which looks like a content-personalization payload rather than a campaign UTM.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as sender defects.
2. Confirm whether a `text/plain` part exists. If it is missing, add one that includes the offer and the unsubscribe URL.

4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `dv.adidas.com` offer image, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.

6. Follow the redirect chain on the 16 tracked links, using a browser or `curl -L`. This will confirm the final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
