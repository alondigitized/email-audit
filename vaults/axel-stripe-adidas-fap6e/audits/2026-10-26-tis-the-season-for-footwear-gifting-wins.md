# 'Tis the season for footwear gifting wins
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, thin gifting send built around one idea: footwear for the whole family at $80 and under. The hero photo of two low-profile 3-Stripes sneakers (tan suede, black leather) on tiled steps is moody and on-brand. The "GIFTING WINS FOR $80 & UNDER" headline gives a clear price anchor.
- It's still a price ceiling, not a deal. There's no percent off, no code, no deadline, and no named product. "Shop Men" and "Shop Women" send people to broad category pages. The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner above the hero adds a second CTA that dilutes the message.
- It sits in the same lane as the 10-20 "Gear up for an active holiday season" send and the 10-18 low-profile sneaker send. This is the third generic holiday-gifting pitch in about a week, and the shoes look nearly the same as the 10-18 hero. It's an adequate 5, and it doesn't reward a subscriber who has already seen the theme twice.

## What's working

- The $80-and-under price cap gives shoppers a concrete budget to act on.
- The hero photography is strong. Light and shadow on the tile steps make the two shoes look premium.
- The black Shop Men and Shop Women buttons are bold, high-contrast and clearly labeled.
- The copy is short, readable and easy to scan on mobile.
- The preheader adds real information, the price point, instead of repeating the subject.

## What's weak

- There's no discount, code or deadline, so nothing creates urgency. This is a price band, not a promotion.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner competes with the hero. It's also slightly offset from the hero image, with its edges not aligned to the photo.
- The two CTAs go to generic Men and Women pages. There's no kids option, even though the body copy says "for the entire family."
- No shoe is named and there are no prices, ratings or reviews, so it's hard to know what you'd actually get at $80.
- The email repeats the holiday-gifting theme from 10-18 and 10-20 with no new hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name a hero product with a price tag. For example, "Gazelle Indoor $80" with a direct product-page link would replace two generic category CTAs.
- 2. Add a real incentive or deadline, such as free shipping or "order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery."
- 3. Cut the black best-sellers banner or move it below the hero so the offer is the only top-of-email idea.
- 4. Add a Shop Kids button to match the "entire family" promise.
- 5. Add a row of three to four priced products under the hero so a click lands on something specific.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts for the whole family, all $80 & under`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3-Stripes gifts under $80 — order by Dec 15`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers for men, women and kids, with free shipping on holiday orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gazelle, Samba and more, starting at $65. Ships in time for the holidays.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` hosts) with a responsive `@media` setup. Its confirmed defects are cleartext `http://` image sources, missing alt text, and a viewport meta tag without `width=device-width`. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because of relay capture limits.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains, and final URLs are unverified.
- **Cleartext tracking image.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a17a41a6e6-a21c51e5-.../RrgldzL8ARIho-0mohJ-cTMzivBtRQ21lmKKaLbs2n4=452` loads over `http://`. It has no alt text and looks like the open-tracking pixel. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, but a client that blocks mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop it, so open tracking will under-count.
- **Duplicate `http://` asset, four times.** The `arrow` image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is referenced four times. The same asset is likely available over HTTPS on the same CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/680c0b40-56b6-487e-b45b-eacd6d7c7ed8?cp_tp=...`
  - The tracking image above (`...n4=452`)
  - Both carry no alt, so with images off the content is unlabeled. If the first is a content or CTA image, it is also inaccessible to screen readers. The tracking pixel should use `alt=""`.
- **Viewport meta.** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale as intended. The `@media` rules still work in most clients that honor them.
- **Plain-text part.** QA reports the text version as 0 chars (**FAIL**). This could be a relay capture artifact rather than a missing part, so confirm against the raw MIME. If it is real, the email is HTML-only (spam-score penalty, no fallback for text-only clients).
- **Fonts.** The `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` `@font-face` rules use only `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica'), ...`. No web font file is loaded, so every client renders system fallbacks. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Mixed-content risk.** Items under section 2 apply here too: a mixture of `http://` and `https://` image sources.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The truncated source cannot rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unverified, not a confirmed violation**. Check the original message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this volume.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent from the relay, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The `us-news.comms.adidas.com` sender subdomain is aligned with the brand domain, but no pass or fail can be reported.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source does not show them, so I can't confirm either.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is harmless in email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All 17 tracking links were skipped and their destinations are hidden behind the `click.comms.adidas.com` redirect wrapper. UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be confirmed from the HTML or the QA data. The only parameter visible is `cp_tp=...` on the `dv.adidas.com` image asset.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Serve every image over HTTPS.** Change the `arrow` asset (`http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/.../b0c011a2-...jpg`) and the open-tracking pixel (`http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`).
2. **Add alt text.** Use descriptive alt on `dv.adidas.com/o/680c0b40-...`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. **Fix the viewport meta:** `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
4. **Verify the 0-char plain-text part** in the raw MIME. If it really is missing, add a `text/plain` alternative.
5. **Re-run QA against the original message** to settle the compliance questions:
   - List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers
   - SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results
   - The footer unsubscribe link and physical address
6. **Resolve the redirect chains** on the 17 tracking links to capture final URLs and UTM parameters. The unresolved links are the main gap in this audit.
