- This is step four of the adiClub onboarding sequence — after Welcome (May 15), Points education (May 17), App download (May 20), and now a profile-completion ask with a concrete 50-point bribe. It's the cleanest, most actionable send in the sequence: a specific reward, an unambiguous CTA, and a clear "why" sidebar. The formula is working.

## What's working

- Concrete reward up top — "earn 50 adiClub points" is the entire pitch and it's stated in the subject, headline, and benefits panel.
- Primary CTA is unambiguous — "COMPLETE MY PROFILE" black button, verb + noun, no ambiguity.
- "Why update your profile?" sidebar earns the click by listing three specific benefits.
- Visual hierarchy is clean — adiClub logo → hero → headline → CTA → benefits, top to bottom, no clutter.
- Hero model demographic (young, casual sportswear) reads on-brand and matches the audience.

## What's weak

- Subject is functional but flat — "Complete your profile to earn 50 adiClub points" reads like a system notification.
- Headline "TELL US ABOUT YOU" is a little corporate-survey; doesn't sell the upside.
- "EARN 50 POINTS" appears as a secondary CTA in the benefits panel — unclear if it's a separate link or restatement, mild redundancy with the primary button.
- Body copy is a wall of small grey text — readable but easy to skim past on mobile.
- No time bound — points offer feels open-ended, which reduces any urgency to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the reward in the headline. "TELL US ABOUT YOU" buries the value; flip the hero to "GET 50 POINTS IN 60 SECONDS" or similar.
- 2. Add a soft deadline ("Complete by [date]") to give the email a reason to be opened *now* rather than archived for later.
- 3. Tighten the body paragraph — three short lines beats one dense block.
- 4. Show what 50 points buys (e.g., "halfway to a $5 reward") so the points feel tangible, not abstract.
- 5. Drop or merge the second "EARN 50 POINTS" link — one CTA, said louder.

# Technical Audit: adidas "Complete your profile" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email shows multiple infrastructure issues including a malformed CTA link with embedded whitespace/control characters, references to staging image hosts in production, and mixed HTTP/HTTPS asset loading. Compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are not observable via the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Critical — malformed primary CTA**: "Earn 50 points" link contains a URL-decoded space and a line break inside the `tkn=` parameter (`...UkrsPo9CaLH2p3` followed by whitespace before the next segment). The QA probe failed with `URL can't contain control characters`. This will break click-tracking and may render the CTA non-functional in stricter clients.
- 23 tracking/redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing — actual destination health on `go.adidas.com/ihha/...` cannot be confirmed from this run.
- The 8 "broken (403)" font URLs are false positives from the QA scraper — trailing `)` from the CSS `url(...)` syntax got captured as part of the URL. Not a real issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content**: Multiple images served over `http://image.link.adidas.com/...` (e.g. `b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`, `37231f63-3f72-48f3-8047-2650e11742e8.gif`). Gmail and many corporate clients block or proxy-rewrite non-HTTPS image sources.
- **Staging host in production**: At least 4 images reference `image.staging.link.adidas.com` (`2373748f-...gif`, `b9aa5d2a-...jpg`, `5bf3fd66-...jpg`). Staging hosts typically have no SLA, may be firewalled, and can disappear without notice.
- **Missing alt text** on at least 5 distinct image assets, including the hero `b9aa5d2a-...jpg` and the `2373748f-...gif`. Screen reader users get nothing; image-blocked clients show empty placeholders.
- Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional — standard for email, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`) detected in the truncated source. The visible `tkn=` parameter on the CTA appears to be a server-side encrypted user token rather than a leaked merge variable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header **not observed** in captured headers — may be a relay capture limitation, but cannot be confirmed present.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) **not observed** — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules since Feb 2024.
- `Authentication-Results` header **not observed** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverifiable from this capture.
- Physical postal address and visible unsubscribe link in the body cannot be verified from the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
CTA URL carries a full attribution payload:
- `cm_mmc=AdiEmail_CLC-_-None-_-MemOnb_Comms3-_-Marketing-_-UpdateProfile-_-None-_-dv:eCom-_-cn:Welcome-_-pc:None`
- `cm_mmc1=US`, `cm_mmca3=94IW5HF2JM6L4FZA`, `cm_mmca4=4134868`
- AppsFlyer params present: `af_adset=MemOnb_Comms3`, `af_ad=UpdateProfile`, `af_channel=Welcome`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`

No standard `utm_source/medium/campaign` params — adidas uses the proprietary `cm_mmc` schema, which is consistent with their stack. Continuity is acceptable **if** the malformed token doesn't break the redirect chain (see §2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the CTA token immediately** — the embedded whitespace/newline in the `tkn=` parameter is the highest-severity issue and likely breaks the primary conversion path.
2. **Replace all `image.staging.link.adidas.com` references** with the production `image.link.adidas.com` host before send.
3. **Upgrade all `http://image.link.adidas.com` URLs to `https://`** to avoid mixed-content blocking.
4. **Add descriptive `alt` attributes** to all content images; use `alt=""` only for decorative spacers.
5. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers** are present at the MTA level — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
6. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment** independently (e.g., direct inbox header view), since the relay capture stripped `Authentication-Results`.
