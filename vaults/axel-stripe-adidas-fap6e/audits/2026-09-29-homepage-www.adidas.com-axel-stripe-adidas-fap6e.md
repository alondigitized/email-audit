# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas leading with performance running. The Berlin Marathon 2026 hero pushes "Faster. Fastest. ADIZERO." The cookie modal sits right on top of the hero and takes most of the first screen. Below it, "Shop the latest drops" is the only thing that feels close to my lane.

## What's working

- **The "Faster. Fastest. ADIZERO." line and the finish-tape shot.** The copy is short and confident, and the BMW Berlin finish-line photo makes the brand look culturally current.
- **The adiClub shipping strip.** It gives me a reason to be signed in, and it's the one offer I'd actually use.
- **The nav.** MEN, WOMEN, KIDS, SALE, SPORTS and NEW & TRENDING are clean, and "NEW & TRENDING" is where I'd go for drops.
- **The cookie modal buttons.** "ACCEPT ALL" and "REJECT NON-NECESSARY COOKIES" get equal visual weight, so it doesn't feel like a trick.
- **"SHOP THE LATEST DROPS."** The four tiles have collab and artist energy (a yellow lightning-bolt graphic, the Ed Reed / Malachi Toney tile), which is the closest thing here to hype.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal covers the hero.** It hides the middle panel and cuts the headline off, so my first impression is a legal wall.
- **The hero is performance running.** There is no Samba, Gazelle or Originals lifestyle imagery, and nothing that says "Y-3."
- **"SHOP NOW."** It doesn't tell me where I'm going: running shoes, a collection, or a race-day capsule.
- **The "SHOP THE LATEST DROPS" tiles.** They sit at the fold with no shoe names, no prices and no "Confirmed" or launch-date cue, so I can't tell what's hot.
- **The free-shipping strip.** It's gated behind adiClub with no threshold shown, and the chevron makes me tap to learn the terms.
- **No trust signals.** I see no reviews, ratings, returns policy or size guidance in the first screen.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal.** Show it as a slim bottom banner or after the first scroll, so the hero and headline read uncut on first load.
- **Personalize the hero for signed-in members.** If the account badge means I'm logged in, swap the marathon panel for an Originals hero, like a Samba or Gazelle colorway, with a "Shop Originals" button.
- **Label the drop tiles.** Add the product name, price and a "Confirmed drop" or launch-time chip to each of the four tiles.
- **Put the adiClub terms in the strip.** Change it to something like "Free shipping for adiClub members. Join free" so nobody has to open a dropdown.
