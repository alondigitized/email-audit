# Anthony Edwards 3 With Love
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a product-launch send for the Anthony Edwards 3, and it is mostly a brand piece with a thin sales case. The sole-view hero is striking, with orange and green outsole detail on black. The headline is clean and the Hyperboost angle is a real story. But there is no price, no offer and no product name on a shoe card. The only deadline is "while supplies last," buried in the footer.
- The layout works against the launch. A generic black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner sits above the hero and competes with it. The shoe is shown upside down, so you see the outsole, not the shoe. The second image is a model in a track jacket with no link to the shoe. Compared with the 11/05 "Week of Deals last call" send, which had a countdown and up to 50% off, this one gives a shopper less reason to act today.
- Judgement: well-shot and on-brand, but it is a soft announcement with no price and no urgency. It suits a basketball fan and means little to anyone else.

## What's working

- The outsole hero is distinctive and sets the product apart from the usual side-profile shot.
- The headline "ANTHONY EDWARDS 3: WITH LOVE" is large and easy to read.
- The copy gives one concrete product claim: Hyperboost in basketball for the first time, with high cushioning and energy return.
- The two black CTAs have strong contrast and sit together under the copy.
- "Find Store Now" fits a send that is built around in-store availability.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or member benefit anywhere in the email.
- The top banner, "Explore the latest adidas best sellers," pulls attention away from the launch and sends people to a different place.
- The shoe is shown only from the sole, so the upper, colorway and silhouette are never visible.
- The model image in a track jacket has no tie to the shoe or the campaign. It reads as filler.
- The store list in the footer is long, and the real caveat, "first-come, first-served while supplies last," is in small print.
- The preheader link is plain underlined serif text, and it looks unstyled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic best-sellers banner with one launch banner that carries the price and the release status.
- 2. Add a side-profile shot of the shoe, or the shoe on foot, next to the sole shot. Put the price and colorway in a small caption.
- 3. Move the supply caveat into the body. "Limited first run, while supplies last" is credible urgency and it is honest.
- 4. Put Ant in the shoe, or use a product-led image with the shoe on court. Cut the unrelated jacket shot.
- 5. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop Anthony Edwards 3", and keep "Find a Store" as the secondary link.
- 6. Clean up the preheader so it uses styled copy, not an underlined link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ant's first Hyperboost hoops shoe is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Anthony Edwards 3: in stores and online`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hyperboost cushioning, low-cut speed. Limited first run.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Where you from is how you play. Shop the AE 3 now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a sound responsive structure (`lang="en"`, media queries, `mso-font-alt` fallbacks). Automated QA scored it 50% with one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several WARNs: non-HTTPS image sources, missing alt text, and unverifiable headers. The HTML is truncated, so findings about the body below the head are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed tracking links:** 18 links on `click.comms.adidas.com` (path prefix `/CI0/...`) were skipped as click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and final status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking host:** at least one tracked asset is requested over plain HTTP: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1b8b6514b-f71c68ad-.../iL0yVmABEdp2nkCP_GbBgFZDk...=452`. It is an `<img>` with no alt text, which looks like the open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may fail to record opens.
- **No other defects found:** the QA data shows no broken or malformed `href`s.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Non-HTTPS images:** the "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is flagged four times. It is the same asset, reused four times, and the host is HTTP-only in the source. Some clients may block or rewrite it, so the arrow icons could drop out.
- **[WARN] Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/9bb6976d-ef9d-4d38-a681-69929b2463ba?cp_tp=...` has no `alt`. It appears to be a content image served from `dv.adidas.com`. With images blocked, screen readers and image-off clients get nothing for it.
  - The tracking pixel (`...=452`) also has no alt. An empty `alt=""` would be correct there, but the attribute is absent.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` omits `width=device-width`. Behavior varies by client, and the mobile media queries may not apply consistently.
- **Fonts:** `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared with `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica')...`, so no webfont is actually loaded. They always render as Arial or Helvetica. This is harmless, but the `@font-face` blocks, duplicated in a second `<style>`, add weight without benefit.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email and not an issue in itself.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Body personalization can't be confirmed, since the HTML is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text alternative:** the text version is 0 chars. This is a multipart/alternative gap that hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could be a relay capture artifact, but treat it as real until the raw MIME is checked.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the original message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` absent:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown through the relay. The From domain, `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, aligns with the tracking domain `comms.adidas.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit in the truncated portion, so they are not verified here.
- **Noindex and referrer metas:** `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present. Neither is a compliance issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable from the data provided. All 18 links are wrapped in `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects, and the probe was skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations weren't resolved. Image URLs carry only `cp_tp` and CDN parameters.

3. **HTTPS:** serve `image.link.adidas.com` assets (the "arrow") and the `click.comms.adidas.com` pixel over HTTPS.
4. **Alt text:** add meaningful `alt` to `dv.adidas.com/o/9bb6976d-...`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
