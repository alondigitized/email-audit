# Unlock more rewards with the adidas app
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third email in adidas's adiClub onboarding sequence — sent five days after the welcome send and three days after the points-education email (May 17, "Earn adiClub points to unlock more rewards"). The brief is to convert new members into app users, with a Spotify partner-offer hook and a 15% voucher as the twin pull. It's a competent execution of a standard loyalty-app push, but the structural decision to bury the voucher below the app-download section creates a hierarchy problem: the headline sells the app, not the money — and the money is what would actually move someone to act.
- The Spotify integration is the email's strongest single asset. Seeing a recognizable third-party brand with a hard value ("3 months of Spotify with your purchase") in the hero phone mock immediately elevates perceived app value beyond a generic download ask. That signal is being used well as the top-of-scroll hook.
- The 15% OFF voucher with a June 03 deadline and a visible promo code is real, actionable offer content — and it's wasted below a multi-paragraph app tutorial. As the third branded touch since May 15, the cadence is pressing the edge of acceptable; much more and open rates will soften.

## What's working

- **Spotify partner hook is arresting**: The phone mock with a recognizable third-party brand makes the app feel immediately valuable, beyond just loyalty point accumulation.
- **15% voucher with a hard deadline** (June 03, 2026) and a visible promo code gives the second half of the email genuine lift.
- **Benefit bullets are sharp and scannable**: "10 points," "notified instantly," "early access" — each lead with the payoff, not the mechanic.
- **"REDEEM ONLINE →" CTA** is specific and direct; avoids the generic "Shop Now" trap.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, no layout bugs visible anywhere in the email.

## What's weak

- **The 15% voucher is below the fold**: A subscriber who doesn't scroll past the app-pitch section never sees the strongest reason to engage with this email.
- **Two competing primary CTAs** ("GET THE APP" vs. "REDEEM ONLINE") dilute each other; neither gets the full weight of the email behind it.
- **"Recommended for you" reads as a template module**: The three product images show generic category picks — one appears to feature accessories near a dog — signaling zero personalization at day 5 of onboarding.
- **No personalization anywhere**: Five days in, still no name, no reference to signup behavior, nothing that signals adidas knows who this person is.
- **Cadence pressure building**: Three emails in five days. The sequence is beginning to feel like volume over value.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the voucher, make the app the mechanism.** Restructure so "HERE'S YOUR 15% OFF" is the hero, with the app download positioned as the route to redeem it ("Download the app to redeem — or use the code at checkout"). The Spotify offer anchors the app-value proof below it.
- 2. **Collapse to one CTA.** If the goal is app installs, make "REDEEM IN THE APP" do double duty — one tap that installs and applies the code. If the goal is voucher redemption, drop the app section to a secondary module.
- 3. **Add a personalized opener.** Even "Hi [first name]" above the headline, or swapping the subhead to "Your adiClub voucher is ready" instead of the generic "DOWNLOAD NOW AND START EARNING REWARDS," reduces third-email churn risk meaningfully.
- 4. **Retire the template product grid** or replace it with category signals from onboarding data — what the member browsed, what they've added to cart, or even just the category they registered under.
- 5. **Extend the send gap by one day**: A 4-day gap before the next loyalty touch would preserve open rates through the rest of the sequence without sacrificing momentum.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 15% off expires June 3 — redeem it here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spotify + adidas app: here's what's inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Download the app to redeem — or use the code online at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10 bonus points, early access drops, and 3 months of Spotify. One download.`
