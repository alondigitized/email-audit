# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Bro. I literally cannot see the homepage. adidas's security system hard-blocked me with a wall of error text and a reference code. No hero, no products, no campaign — just the three-stripe logo floating above the worst possible message you can show a customer: "we don't want you here." For a guy who checks this app daily, this is embarrassing for the brand.

## What's working

- **Logo placement** — the adidas trefoil mark is centered and clean at the top. At least I know whose wall I just hit.
- **Error typography** — the all-caps bold copy is readable and on-brand (it literally uses the adidas typeface), which is darkly funny given the context.

## What's weak

- **The entire page** — I came to shop Sambas and got a security block instead. Zero content, zero product, zero reason to wait it out.
- **"A security issue was automatically identified"** — that reads like I did something wrong. I didn't. I'm a loyal customer who pre-orders drops. Treating me like a bot is a brand-trust killer.
- **Reference Error: 0.49d7ce17.1783418407.8fc0e66** — raw error codes exposed to end users scream "we didn't design a fallback." This is a 2014 problem in 2026.
- **No recovery path** — not a single CTA, no "try again," no link to the app, no customer service number. Completely stranded.

## Recommendations

- **Design a proper fallback page** with a "Try again" button, app store link, and customer service contact — this error state will hit real users and right now it's a dead end.
- **Soften the security block copy** — "We're having trouble verifying your connection" is less accusatory than "a security issue was automatically identified." Don't make loyal customers feel suspected.
- **Add a reference-code lookup tool or at minimum hide the raw hash** — surface a friendly error ID, not a CDN trace token.
- **Route blocked traffic to the app** — if the web session can't be verified, drop a banner that says "Open in the adidas app" with a deep link. Don't lose the conversion entirely.
