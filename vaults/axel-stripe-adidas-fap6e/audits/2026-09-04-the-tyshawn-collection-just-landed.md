# The Tyshawn Collection just landed
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine named-athlete collaboration launch — Tyshawn Jones, skateboarding pro — with two authentic action shots (skater walking with board, skater mid-push on a crosswalk) that actually look like real lifestyle photography rather than stock catalog product shots. It's more editorial and specific than most of this brand's recent sends, and it lands closer to adidas Skateboarding's actual subculture than the school-uniform Samba/Gazelle emails from earlier this week.
- The problem is the email is really two unrelated campaigns stapled together: a Tyshawn apparel launch up top, then a "Back to School Backpacks" block at the bottom with a clear vinyl backpack that has nothing to do with skateboarding or Tyshawn. That backpack shot is also the only genuinely current-season visual in the email — the hero imagery reads more like fall/winter (hoodies, overcast wet streets) which is a slight mismatch for a September 4 send in most US climates.
- This scores mid-pack for the brand: distinctive hero content pulls it above the recent Samba/JENNIE/Collab-Archive run, but the CTA "SHOP NOW" is generic on both sections, there's no price or offer anywhere, and the bolted-on backpack section dilutes what could've been a tight, focused drop email.

## What's working

- The Tyshawn Jones hero photography is authentic and on-brand for skate culture — grainy, candid, outdoor — not another studio product-grid shot.
- Headline "NEW IN: THE TYSHAWN APPAREL COLLECTION" is bold, high-contrast, and immediately readable.
- Two clear "SHOP NOW" CTAs with directional arrows, both above and below the fold.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.
- The utility bar up top ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") gives a fallback path if the shopper isn't into the Tyshawn line specifically.

## What's weak

- No offer or price anywhere in the email — pure product announcement, nothing to create urgency to click now versus later.
- The Back to School Backpacks section is a jarring non-sequitur — different product, different season message, no visual or copy bridge from the skate content above it.
- CTA copy is generic ("SHOP NOW" x2) — doesn't call out the collection or the backpack, missing a chance to be specific.
- Hero mood (wet pavement, hoodies, overcast light) reads cold-weather, not early-September.
- No named specific product/price/size info — just "explore the collection," so the shopper has to click blind to see what's actually for sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two focused sends.** The Tyshawn launch deserves its own dedicated email; bolting Back to School Backpacks onto the bottom drags down an otherwise strong single-collab story. If budget requires combining, at least add a transition line ("Gear up top to bottom") to bridge them.
- 2. **Name a product and price.** "NEW IN: THE TYSHAWN APPAREL COLLECTION" with zero SKU or price forces a blind click — surface one hero item ("Tyshawn Track Jacket, $85") to give the eye something concrete to latch onto.
- 3. **Make CTA copy specific.** Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for "Shop the Tyshawn Collection" and "Shop Backpacks" respectively.
- 4. **Add urgency or newness framing** — "just landed" is in the subject but nowhere in the body; a "Available now" or "Limited release" tag near the hero would reinforce it.
