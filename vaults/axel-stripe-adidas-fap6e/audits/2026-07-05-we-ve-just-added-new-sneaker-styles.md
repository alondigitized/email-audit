# We’ve just added new sneaker styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas's "new arrivals" catalog email — a product grid with no offer, no urgency, and a hero that already ran on June 19 (Toy Story Samba) and again in passing since. The Adizero Dropset Pro occupies two of the four grid slots and was *also* the hero of a separate adidas send today (scored 5/10). So on July 5, adidas sent two emails, and the second one recycles the star product of the first. That's a coordination failure, not a strategy.
- The creative itself isn't broken — "UPDATE YOUR SNEAKER ROSTER" is punchy, the product photography is clean, star ratings are visible, and the layout is coherent. But the absence of any offer turns this into a look-book with a SHOP NOW button. No discount, no member pricing, no deadline — just full-price shoes the subscriber may have already seen twice this week.
- The preheader leaked garbled copy ("Content with style and freshness as you. Get new sneaker adidas dr.") which is the most fixable thing in the whole email and the first thing a subscriber reads before deciding to open.

## What's working

- **"UPDATE YOUR SNEAKER ROSTER"** is an unusually good headline for adidas — direct, action-oriented, persona-appropriate.
- **Hero product shot** of the Toy Story Samba is high quality and immediately readable.
- **Star ratings** (4.8 on the hero, visible on grid) add credibility without clutter.
- **SHOP NOW** button is clearly visible and unambiguous.
- **adidas CREATOR+** member tagging on product tiles is a nice loyalty signal even without a discount.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever** — full prices only, on a weekend when the brand was running 30% off as recently as June 30. This feels like a step backward.
- **Adizero Dropset Pro appears twice in the four-item grid** (two colorways) AND was the hero of the other July 5 send — the grid is burning half its real estate on a shoe the subscriber already saw today.
- **Preheader is broken** — "Content with style and freshness as you. Get new sneaker adidas dr." is clearly truncated or mis-authored; it reads like a template fragment.
- **Toy Story Samba as hero** is a repeat — the collab was already the subject of the June 19 send, and it's now being positioned as a "new" addition.
- **Four emails in six days** (June 30, July 3, July 5 ×2) with no offer on the last one will accelerate unsubscribes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — this is the single highest-ROI fix. A broken preheader is visible before the open; everything else is downstream.
- 2. **Either attach an offer or don't send** — a member-only discount (even 15% CREATOR+ exclusive) would turn this from a browse prompt into a conversion event. The June 30 "30% off" set expectations the subscriber still has.
- 3. **Cut one Adizero slot from the grid** — replace one Dropset Pro colorway with a genuinely new product to justify the "just added" subject claim.
- 4. **Make the CTA product-specific** — "Shop Sneakers" or "Shop New Arrivals" beats the homepage catch-all "SHOP NOW."
- 5. **Don't lead with Toy Story Samba as hero again** — it was the June 19 send's whole premise; using it again without a new angle (collab story, limited restock) makes the "just added" positioning dishonest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New sneaker drops: Samba, Adizero + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added to your roster — shop the latest drops`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four styles worth rotating in. Shop them while sizes last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Toy Story Samba, Adizero Dropset Pro, and more — just landed.`

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
The header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. If genuinely absent from the MIME envelope, this violates Google and Yahoo's February 2024 bulk sender requirements (mandatory for >5K/day senders). It also prevents Gmail's native "Unsubscribe" button from appearing.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. Required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender policy for commercial mail at scale. If adidas is routing unsubscribes through a footer link only (visible in the HTML), that satisfies CAN-SPAM but not the one-click mandate.
