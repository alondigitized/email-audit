# Fresh arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean sneaker-catalog send with no offer. A hero of a cream and silver Samba-style shoe sits above "ELEVATE YOUR SNEAKER GAME" and a 2x2 grid of four shoes with prices and star ratings. Three of the four grid shoes are Pokémon Superstars, which follows the 10/23 "adidas I Pokémon" launch. The ratings (4.6 to 4.9) and "prime options available" badges are the best parts. They give the grid some proof and a delivery angle.
- The email has no reason to click today. Nothing is discounted, there's no deadline, and "Fresh arrivals" is not backed up by anything marked new. The hero shoe has no name or price, so the biggest visual is the least actionable. The grid is mostly Pokémon, so the "latest and greatest in the adidas shoe lineup" copy oversells the range.
- It's competent and on-brand but forgettable. It sits in the same cluster of thin sends as 10/26 "'Tis the season for footwear gifting wins" and 10/23 "adidas I Pokémon". That's six adidas emails in about ten days, so the cadence works against it.

## What's working

- The product grid shows price, star rating and shoe name together, so the viewer can judge each shoe without clicking.
- The ratings (4.6 to 4.9) are real social proof, which most adidas sends in the history lack.
- The "prime options available" badges add a delivery or convenience hook.
- The layout is simple: logo, hero, headline, grid, one black "SHOP NOW" button. The button contrasts well with the white page.
- The Pokémon Superstar colourways (the yellow Pikachu ones especially) are eye-catching and match a live collab.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, no deadline and no loyalty angle, so the only reason to click is curiosity.
- The hero shoe has no label, price or visible link, so the largest image can't be acted on.
- The subject says "Fresh arrivals", but nothing is marked "new" or dated. The Samba-style hero and the Handball Spezial aren't obviously new releases.
- The preheader renders as underlined text at the very top of the email, above the logo. It reads like a stray link.
- "SHOP NOW" is generic, and one button under four different products gives no product-level path. The headline and subhead ("Discover the latest and greatest…") are filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one thing the hero and give it a reason to act. Name and price the lead shoe, and add a deadline or member perk if there is one (for example, "Members get free shipping through Sunday").
- 2. Make "Fresh arrivals" literally true. Add "NEW" tags with release dates, or lead with the Pokémon drop and say so.
- 3. Link each grid shoe to its own product page, and swap the single button for "Shop Pokémon Superstars" or "Shop the new Spezials".
- 4. Replace the headline and subhead with specifics, such as "4 new pairs, from $110. Rated up to 4.9."
- 5. Fix the preheader so it doesn't show as visible text at the top of the body.
