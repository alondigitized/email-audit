# adidas 5.10's Sticky Secret
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is an editorial brand-story email for adidas Five Ten's STEALTH rubber technology — no offer, no discount, just a heritage narrative. After a tight onboarding sequence (welcome May 15 → points May 17 → app May 20) plus an F1 collab send just yesterday, this is a sharp creative pivot: from loyalty mechanics and collab hype to 40 years of climbing rubber history. That pivot is risky in a crowded inbox — new subscribers are still in the "why should I keep these?" phase, and a pure brand story with no incentive is a tough sell.
- The execution is clean. The hero image of climbing holds is arresting, the headline is confident, and the copy is genuinely good — "the line you weren't sure you had in you" lands. But the email has no offer, no product to click into specifically, and a "SHOP NOW" CTA that points nowhere interesting in the frame. It reads like a print magazine page dropped into an inbox.
- The story is worth telling. The send timing and CTA execution undercut it.

## What's working

- **Hero image is genuinely compelling** — bright climbing holds, active hands, immediately visual and product-relevant.
- **Headline "ADIDAS FIVE TEN'S STICKY SECRET" earns the open** — specific product name + intrigue word makes a better-than-average subject-to-headline payoff.
- **Copy quality is above average** — "40 years on, STEALTH is still sticking. To rock, to pedals, to moments that count." is actually good brand writing.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no layout breaks, legible on the screenshot at every level.
- **Product history is specific** — 1985, Yosemite, grade 5.10, the bike world crossover — details that build credibility.

## What's weak

- **No offer anywhere** — after five emails in a week, a frictionless entry point (even "free shipping on 5.10" or a member price) is missing.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is maximally generic** — no product name, no category anchor, no reason to believe the click lands on something specific.
- **Top banner bleeds into preview text**: "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" has zero relationship to the subject line and wastes the preheader slot entirely.
- **No loyalty tie-in** — this subscriber just joined adiClub and earned points; there's no bridge from the heritage story to their membership or rewards.
- **Send cadence is getting dense** — this is email #5 from adidas in 7 days; a brand story send is a fatigue risk without a pull incentive attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Attach a product anchor to the CTA** — change "SHOP NOW" to "SHOP FIVE TEN STEALTH SHOES" and link to the 5.10 collection page, not the generic homepage. Single highest-impact fix.
- 2. **Replace the top banner preheader** — "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" wastes the preview text slot. Write a dedicated preheader that amplifies the subject.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `40 years. One rubber. Still the standard for climbers and riders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The shoe born on a 5.10 climb — and why it still wins.`
- 3. **Add an adiClub member hook** — one line bridging the story to loyalty: "adiClub members get early access to the Five Ten collection." Connects the last four emails to this one.
- 4. **Consider a softer subject line variant for the fatigued subscriber segment** — the current subject is strong editorially but leans curious over urgent; test a value signal for the less-engaged cohort.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The rubber born in Yosemite — and why climbers still trust it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Five Ten's STEALTH: 40 years, still the standard`
