# Own the basketball court and the classroom
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "the basketball court and the classroom" — but there is zero classroom, back-to-school, or "own the court" narrative in the actual render. What loads is a basketball hero (two kids on a court) followed by a bolted-on repeat of the September 5 "Win the Go Full Hybrid Kit" giveaway module. This is the second time in two weeks (see 2026-08-29 "Cozy classroom essentials") that the subject line describes content that simply isn't in the email — a pattern worth flagging to whoever owns subject-line QA.
- The basketball section itself is fine: a real, warm lifestyle photo of two kids in adidas gear on a gym court, a clean "BASKETBALL FOR ALL" headline, and a SHOP NOW CTA. But then the email pivots hard into an unrelated giveaway (Adizero Dropset Pro shoes, gym bag, POLAR GRIT X2 smartwatch) that has nothing to do with basketball or classroom — it's literally recycled creative from the 2026-09-05 hybrid-kit giveaway send.
- Net: two disconnected campaigns stapled into one email, neither of which matches the subject. As a parent shopping back-to-school basketball gear, I'd feel like I opened the wrong email.

## What's working

- The hero photo is a genuine, well-lit lifestyle shot of two kids on a real basketball court — authentic, not stock-looking.
- "BASKETBALL FOR ALL" headline and body copy are on-topic and clear.
- Two distinct, high-contrast black CTA buttons (SHOP NOW, SIGN UP) are easy to spot.
- Top utility bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") gives a low-friction secondary path in.
- No broken images or rendering glitches — everything displays cleanly.

## What's weak

- Subject says "classroom" — there is no classroom, school-supply, or back-to-school content anywhere in the render.
- The giveaway module (hybrid training kit) is unrelated to both basketball and classroom, and is a near-exact repeat of the 2026-09-05 "Win the Go Full Hybrid Kit" email — two sends in three days pushing the same giveaway.
- Preheader ("This year, take the court in style.") only reinforces the basketball half of the subject, leaving "classroom" completely unaddressed anywhere in the email.
- The email reads as two unrelated campaigns taped together rather than one coherent story.
- No pricing, discount, or urgency anywhere — just "shop now" and "sign up," both low-commitment asks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/content mismatch first — either add real back-to-school/classroom content (locker-room style sneakers, school-appropriate apparel) or drop "classroom" from the subject entirely. This is the second occurrence of this bug type in two weeks and is the single biggest credibility issue.
- 2. Stop reusing the hybrid-kit giveaway verbatim three days after its own dedicated send — either give basketball its own full email or replace this block with a basketball-specific product grid (shoes, jerseys, backpacks).
- 3. Add one concrete offer (%, $, or BOGO) — there's no discount signal anywhere in this email, which is unusual for this brand's recent cadence.
- 4. If keeping both sections, add a short transition line ("...and don't forget your hybrid training gear") so the jump doesn't feel like two merged emails.
