# Up to 60% off: Black Friday first access ends today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest and most honest send in the recent run. The black countdown banner reads 00 days, 17 hours, 33 minutes and 26 seconds, and the hero says "adiClub / BLACK FRIDAY / UP TO 60% OFF" over models carrying shopping bags. Subject, hero and body all say the same thing: members' first access ends today. Nothing competes with the offer, unlike the 2026-11-16 "BadBo" and "Care Bears" sends, where product drops fought the countdown for attention.
- The weakness is that it's generic. There's no product, no price and no hero SKU, only "up to 60%" and three audience buttons. It's also a near-repeat of 2026-11-14 "adiClub members get up to 60% off for Black Friday early", and the countdown has been in every send since. A subscriber who has already seen it three times has a reason to skip it. The real urgency, 17 hours left, is the only new information, and the body copy under-sells it.
- Verdict: a clear, credible last-call email that works as a reminder but gives a deal-hunter nothing concrete to click on. It's solid, but it isn't inventive.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: "UP TO 60% OFF" in huge white condensed type is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The countdown shows less than a day left, so the urgency is credible and fits the "ends today" subject.
- The "LAST CHANCE FOR FIRST ACCESS" headline and the one-sentence body repeat the offer and deadline without padding.
- The lifestyle image of three people walking with shopping bags is on-theme and shows the offer in use.
- The subject names the discount, the event and the deadline.

## What's weak

- There's no product, price or hero SKU anywhere. "Iconic sneakers, jerseys, fleece, and more" is a category list, not a reason to click.
- The three stacked black buttons (Shop Men / Women / Kids) are left-aligned, ragged in width and visually identical. They push the decision onto the reader and take up a lot of vertical space.
- The hero image is narrower than the layout, with a visible gap on the right. The preheader also shows as a plain underlined link in the top-left corner.
- The countdown has run in nearly every send since 2026-11-14, so it has lost novelty. This is the fourth or fifth adidas send in about five days.
- "Up to 60%" with no sense of what is actually discounted leaves the offer feeling vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a row of 3-4 specific discounted products with sale prices (for example "Samba $59, was $100") below the hero. This is the single biggest lift, because it turns a vague "up to" into something concrete.
- 2. Replace the three stacked buttons with one primary CTA, "Shop member deals", and make the Men / Women / Kids links secondary and side by side.
- 3. Widen the hero image to full container width and make the buttons the same width.
- 4. Cut cadence. Collapse the last two days of pre-deadline sends into one, so the "ends today" send carries real weight.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: adiClub 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `17 hours left for member pricing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, jerseys and fleece, up to 60% off until midnight. Members only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your adiClub early access closes today. Public Black Friday starts tomorrow.`
