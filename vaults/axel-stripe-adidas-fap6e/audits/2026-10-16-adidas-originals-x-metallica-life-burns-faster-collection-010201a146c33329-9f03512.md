# adidas Originals x Metallica ‘Life Burns Faster’ Collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a two-topic adidas send. The lead is the first adidas Originals x Metallica collaboration. The second block is an adiClub giveaway for the Anthony Edwards 3 "Cold Blooded." The Metallica hero is the strongest part. A white three-stripe tee with the red logo hangs in a dim backstage set with road cases and tape reels. It reads as authentic tour merch and gives the eye a clear place to land.
- The email is held back by what's around that hero. A black "best sellers" banner sits on top of it and pulls attention before the Metallica story starts. The body copy is a dense paragraph with no price, no product name and no urgency. The giveaway then takes more than half the length with its own hero and CTA. Compared with the 2026-10-12 adiClub newsletter, where the Apple TV redemption was one clear idea, this one asks the reader to care about a band collab and a basketball sweepstakes at once.
- Overall it's a well-shot but unfocused brand send, and I'd rate it average. The tee photo earns a click only from people who already want Metallica merch.

## What's working

- The Metallica hero is a strong, on-concept image. The tee, road cases and tape reels sell the tour-merch angle.
- The giveaway hero is striking. The chrome and orange pyramid with the basketball and sneaker is a clear scroll-stopper.
- Both CTAs are solid black buttons with arrows and sit in consistent positions under each block.
- The giveaway names a concrete prize: two pairs, one signed, one in your size, plus a chrome display.
- The adiClub tie-in gives members a reason to feel recognized.

## What's weak

- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner pushes the hero down and sends the reader to an unrelated destination.
- The Metallica copy is one long, abstract paragraph about "design language" and "the Trefoil." It names no product, price or drop date, and the reader can't tell what to buy.
- Both CTAs read "Shop Now" or "Enter Now," so nothing says which product or collection they lead to.
- The giveaway has no deadline and no entry mechanics, so there's no credible urgency.
- The Metallica block shows a single tee and no model. The reader can't judge range, fit or who it's for.
- The preheader duplicates the first line of the body, which is nearly the subject again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the best-sellers banner and split the two topics into separate sends, or lead with only one. Metallica fans and sweepstakes entrants are different audiences.
- 2. Show three or four products in the Metallica block with names and prices, such as the tee, a hoodie and a Trefoil piece. Say whether the drop is limited, and when it sells out.
- 3. Rewrite the Metallica body to two short lines, and make the CTA specific, like "Shop the Metallica tee."
- 4. Add a deadline and a one-line entry rule to the giveaway, like "Enter by Oct 23 — adiClub members only."
- 5. Write a real preheader that adds new information instead of repeating the first line of the body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Metallica x adidas Originals is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tour merch, reimagined: Metallica x adidas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tees, hoodies and Trefoil graphics from the Life Burns Faster residency. Shop the drop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: adiClub members can win signed Anthony Edwards 3s.`

# Technical Audit: adidas Originals x Metallica 'Life Burns Faster' Collection

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound: HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype, `lang="en"`, charset meta, and a table-based layout with media-query breakpoints. Two issues are confirmed. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **18 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.comms.adidas.com`, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **The open-tracking pixel is served over plain HTTP.** The pixel is `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a146c33329-9f035129-c702-4904-871d-10e4f1321ca2-000000/eUsdAomIGPCccmsYSYLtys5z2GSv5xhIrOTIMcK1Z-k=452`. ## 5. Compliance
