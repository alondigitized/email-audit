# 🎁 Welcome to adiClub! Explore your rewards
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a triggered welcome email for new adiClub members, and adidas executes the onboarding brief cleanly. The email does exactly what a welcome send should: confirms the enrollment milestone ("ADICLUB LEVEL 1 UNLOCKED"), explains the benefit structure concisely, and drops a tangible 15% welcome voucher with a hard expiry date. That's a solid three-act structure.
- The weakness is above-the-fold real estate on mobile. The hero lifestyle image is atmospheric but earns no conversion work — the actual 15% offer lives below a full-bleed photo and a bulleted benefit list. A new member who opens and doesn't scroll never sees the voucher. That's the single biggest gap in an otherwise competent send.
- The "Member Picks" grid at the bottom is category-level (Shoes, Accessories, Clothing, New Arrivals), not personalized. For a Day 1 member with no purchase history, this is fine — but it adds length without adding pull. The email is honest, on-brand, and render-clean. Functional, not inspired.

## What's working

- **"HERE'S 15% OFF" voucher block is visually distinct** — the section break, large typographic treatment, and personal promo code make it easy to scan and act on.
- **Benefit bullets are crisp** — Free Shipping, Early Access, Members-Only Products are scannable in under five seconds.
- **Hard expiry date (May 29, 2026) is visible** — specific enough to feel real, close enough to create mild urgency.
- **"ADICLUB LEVEL 1 UNLOCKED" confirmation at the top** — immediately validates the recipient's enrolment action; high reassurance signal.
- **No render bugs** — layout holds, images load, typography is clean throughout.

## What's weak

- **15% offer is below the fold on mobile** — the hero image + intro copy + bullet list pushes the voucher below the scroll threshold for smaller screens.
- **Subject doesn't mention the voucher** — "Explore your rewards" is vague when "Here's your 15% welcome voucher" is sitting in the body.
- **"View this email online" leaks as potential preheader content** — depending on HTML ordering, this junk text may show in the inbox preview rather than the benefit hook.
- **Member Picks grid is category-generic** — four category CTAs (Shoes, Accessories, Clothing, New Arrivals) for a brand-new member add scroll depth without personalization payoff.
- **"LEARN MORE →" CTA above the voucher** — sends the click to a benefits page rather than straight to redemption; introduces an unnecessary detour.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the voucher, not the benefits list.** Move the "HERE'S 15% OFF" block above the bullet list — or merge them. The 15% off IS the welcome; make it the first thing after the headline.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to include the offer.** The voucher is the strongest hook and it's buried inside.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 15% welcome voucher is ready, adiClub`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Welcome to adiClub — here's 15% off your first order`
- 3. **Set an explicit preheader that leads with the offer, not nav junk.**
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 15% off voucher expires May 29 — redeem it now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping, early access, and a welcome voucher inside`
- 4. **Replace "LEARN MORE →" with "REDEEM YOUR 15% OFF →"** — one CTA for the voucher, not two competing actions (learn vs. redeem).
- 5. **Trim or remove the Member Picks grid for this first send.** Save category merchandising for email 2 or 3 when there's purchase signal to work with.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024) for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. Its absence risks inbox placement degradation and bulk-folder routing.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` method) is also missing. This is now a hard requirement for Gmail bulk senders. Recipients cannot use the native Gmail unsubscribe button, which increases spam complaints.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header not captured**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown via the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm authentication is wired correctly. Sending domain is `us-news.adidas.com`; DMARC alignment for this subdomain against the `adidas.com` org domain should be verified independently.

26 click-tracking links were not probed, so UTM parameter presence on destination URLs cannot be confirmed. For a welcome email this is a high-priority gap — attribution for first-touch conversions in adiClub will be lost if UTMs are absent or malformed behind the redirects.
