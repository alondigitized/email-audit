# Check out this month's adiClub benefits, here is your August Newsletter
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the monthly adiClub newsletter, and it's structurally different from every other email in this brand's recent run — it's a benefits digest, not a product sale. There's no discount, no price, no urgency; it's four modules stitched together (World Cup ball sweepstakes, Strava integration, a Penn State mascot tracksuit giveaway, and generic "redeem points" copy). It reads as a members-only value-add rather than a purchase push, which is honest to its subject line but means it does almost nothing to drive a transaction.
- The hero — a soccer player in a red World Cup kit against a hot-pink backdrop — is the strongest visual in the email, and "OWN A PIECE OF HISTORY" paired with a signed match ball sweepstake is a genuinely interesting hook for a sports-engaged subscriber. But it collapses fast: three more asks stack below it (Strava connect, Penn State tracksuit, generic point redemption) with no visual hierarchy differentiating what matters most, so the email drifts into a scroll of disconnected asks rather than one coherent story.
- Compared to the brand's recent run — "$50 gift card for $40" (2026-08-15, clean single offer), "Samba Goes Metallic" repeat (2026-08-16) — this is the first "newsletter" format seen, and it's fine for what it is: a loyalty touchpoint, not a revenue email. Judged as a newsletter it's mid-pack; judged as a marketing email meant to convert, it's weak.

## What's working

- The hero image (soccer player, pink background, "OWN A PIECE OF HISTORY") is visually striking and stops the scroll.
- The FIFA World Cup ball sweepstake is a genuinely unique, high-interest hook not seen in prior sends.
- Section framing is consistent — headline, one-line body, black CTA button repeats cleanly across modules.
- No render bugs — all four images loaded, text is legible, no overlap or broken assets.
- adiClub branding (green wordmark, member-only framing) is present throughout and matches the subject's promise.

## What's weak

- Four unrelated CTAs (ball sweepstake, Strava connect, tracksuit giveaway, points redemption) compete with no clear primary action — the eye has nowhere obvious to land after the hero.
- The Penn State Nittany Lion mascot module is jarring and off-brand next to a World Cup hero shot — no visual or thematic connection to the rest of the email.
- "UNLOCK SAVINGS ON YOUR FAVES" promises savings but shows zero products, prices, or discount amounts — just a group photo and a black button.
- No personalization — no member name, no adiClub points balance, no tier status, despite this being a "just for members" email.
- The email is long with heavy vertical scroll for four modules that don't build on each other, increasing fatigue before reaching the bottom CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero action. Lead hard with the World Cup ball sweepstakes — it's the only module with real intrigue — and demote Strava/Penn State/points redemption to a smaller "more member perks" strip below, not full-width modules of equal visual weight.
- 2. Show the member's actual adiClub points balance near the top. "Turn your adiClub points into savings" is generic; "You have 340 points — redeem now" would give the reader a personal reason to click.
- 3. Cut or shrink the Penn State tracksuit module — it's regionally irrelevant to most subscribers and visually disconnected from the World Cup / soccer theme carried elsewhere.
- 4. Add real numbers to the savings module — even "up to 30% off Samba and running shoes" beats the vague "save on almost everything."
