# Catch the adidas | Pokémon collection and evolve your style.

## Executive summary

- This is a two-topic adidas send. The lead is the adidas | Pokémon collection, and the second block is a Spezial gifting push. The Pokémon hero is the strongest part. Pikachu in yellow stands out against a black field, the co-brand lockup is clear, and it's playful enough to stop a thumb. The "Let's goooo! Catch… evolve your style… won't want to trade" copy is on-voice for fans.
- The email never shows a single Pokémon product. It's three Pikachu illustrations and no shoe or apparel, so a reader can't tell what the collection is, what it costs or whether it's their size. The Spezial block below is a clean shoe shot, but it repeats the "gift a classic" idea from the 2026-10-08 Samba send. ## What's working

- The Pikachu hero is bold, high-contrast and instantly recognizable, with a clear adidas × Pokémon lockup.
- The headline "ADIDAS | POKÉMON" is large and gives the first block a clear hierarchy.
- The body copy plays on Pokémon vocabulary ("catch," "evolve," "trade") and matches the subject.
- The Spezial photo is a warm, tactile shoe shot that looks premium against the black Pokémon block.
- The black "SHOP NOW" buttons are large and easy to see, and the layout is simple to scroll.

## What's weak

- No Pokémon product is visible, so the collection is promised but never shown.
- Both CTAs read "SHOP NOW." They don't say what you'll shop, such as sneakers, tees or kids' sizes.
- There is no price, offer or deadline anywhere, so nothing gives a reason to act today.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner is wider than and offset from the hero below it. It also pulls attention to a third, unrelated message.
- The "Gift Spezial" block is a second, unrelated story. It dilutes the Pokémon send and echoes the 2026-10-08 Samba gifting email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace one Pikachu with a real product grid of 3-4 Pokémon pieces, such as a sneaker, a tee and a hoodie, with prices. This one change would lift click likelihood the most.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Pokémon sneakers" and "Shop the Spezial."
- 3. Give the Pokémon drop a hook, such as "Limited drop," a launch date or early access for adiClub members. Without one it's a catalog, not a campaign.
- 4. Cut the best-sellers banner, or align it to the hero width and move it below the content.
- 5. Split the Spezial gifting story into its own send, or move it under the Pokémon block as a clearly secondary "Also gift-ready" strip.
- 6. Tighten the subject to under 50 characters.
