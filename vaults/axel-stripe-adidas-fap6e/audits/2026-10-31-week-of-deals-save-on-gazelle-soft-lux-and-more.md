# Week of Deals: Save on Gazelle, Soft Lux, and more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand adidas sale send. It has a strong, moody hero, one clear offer (up to 50% off), and three plain CTAs. It does the basics well, and the "Week of Deals" hero looks premium. The black track jacket on red paper stands out in the inbox.
- It also buries its own pitch. The subject promises Gazelle and Soft Lux, but the hero shows a track jacket, and no Gazelle or Soft Lux product appears anywhere. The 50% figure sits below a 600px hero, so it's likely off-screen on mobile. The three buttons (Men, Women, Kids) send a shopper to a category page instead of the product the subject sold.
- Compared with the 2026-10-30 "Fresh arrivals" send (6/10) and the 10-29 "Final Rush" send (6/10), this one has a real price hook, which those lacked. It's still a generic seasonal blast with a mismatched hero. Solid but not sharp.

## What's working

- The offer is concrete: "UP TO 50% OFF" is large, plain, and easy to read.
- The hero is striking, with high-contrast red and black, and the "W E E K  O F / D E A L S" lettering gives it a premium feel.
- The three black CTA buttons are unmissable, with clear verbs and arrows.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, overlaps, or clutter.
- The copy ties the sale to the season ("Start your holiday shopping"), so it reads as current.

## What's weak

- The subject names Gazelle and Soft Lux, but the hero shows a track jacket and no product from either line appears.
- The "up to 50%" headline sits below the hero, so on mobile the offer isn't visible without scrolling.
- "Week of" has no end date and "while supplies last" is vague, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Men, Women, and Kids buttons send people to category pages, with no specific product, price, or review shown.
- The preheader "Special deals all week long." mostly repeats the subject's "Week of Deals" and adds nothing new.
- This is the fourth or fifth adidas send in about a week (10-26 gifting, 10-28 Dropset Pro, 10-29 Final Rush, 10-30 Fresh arrivals). A deal blast on top of that risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero image ("UP TO 50% OFF") and show the products named in the subject. A 2x2 grid of Gazelle, Spezial, and Soft Lux with sale prices would beat a lone jacket.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT", and use a countdown or date in the headline.
- 3. Replace the generic gender buttons with product-led CTAs like "Shop Gazelle deals" and "Shop Soft Lux sets", with Men/Women/Kids as secondary links.
- 4. Show sale prices or percent-off badges and a few star ratings, as the 10-30 "Fresh arrivals" grid did.
- 5. Rewrite the preheader to add information (see below).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Gazelle & Soft Lux, this week only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Gazelles just went on sale (through Sunday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select Gazelle, Spezial, and Soft Lux up to 50% off. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bestsellers marked down for 7 days. Sizes go fast.`
