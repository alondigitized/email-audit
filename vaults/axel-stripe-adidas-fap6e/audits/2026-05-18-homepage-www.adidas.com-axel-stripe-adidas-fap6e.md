- **The adidas wordmark** renders correctly at the top — at least I know I'm on the right domain before things go sideways.

- **Full site block** — I get a security wall instead of a homepage. Zero products, zero campaigns, zero navigation. I cannot tap anything.
- **Reference error code displayed raw** — `0.1f756268.1779099071.2400245` means nothing to me. It's dev-facing noise shoved in front of a customer.
- **No recovery path** — No "try again," no app store link, no "visit a store near you," no retry CTA. I'm just stranded.
- **No adiClub or CONFIRMED mention** — Even in a degraded state, the brand could surface the app or loyalty program. It doesn't.

## Recommendations

- **Add a fallback CTA on the block page** — "Open in the adidas app" with an App Store deep link so loyal customers like me don't just leave.
- **Replace the raw error code with a human-readable message** — "We detected unusual activity. If this was a mistake, try refreshing or clearing your cookies." That's actionable; `0.1f756268` is not.
- **Surface the adiClub app prompt** — Even a blocked page is real estate. A one-liner like "While we sort this out, shop on the adidas app" with a QR code or store link keeps me in the funnel.
- **Ship a health-check or status page link** — If it's an outage, I want a link to status.adidas.com so I know it's not just me.
