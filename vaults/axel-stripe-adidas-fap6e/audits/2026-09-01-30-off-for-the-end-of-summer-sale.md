# 30% off for the End of Summer Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competent execution of a genuine sitewide discount — the strongest offer type adidas can send, and it's presented plainly: 30% off with code EXTRA, a real dollar-and-star product grid, and a firm end date buried in the footer (Sept 8). It doesn't try to dress up thin inventory with vague "collaborations" language like the 08-27 archive-sale send, and it's not a bait-and-switch like some of the "up to 40%" framing from mid-August — the code and percentage are stated once, clearly, in the H1.
- Where it falls short is urgency and specificity in the parts that actually drive opens: the subject and hero both say "End of Summer Sale" with no date, no countdown, no reason to act today versus Friday. The four featured products (a $150 running shoe, $80 trackpants, a $60 slip-on, a $102 track top) are also a completely ungendered, uncategorized grab-bag — there's no attempt to route a "men's" or "women's" shopper toward something relevant before they hit the generic SHOP MEN/WOMEN/KIDS buttons.
- Compared to 08-26's "$50 gift cards are only $40" (5/10, praised for blunt math), this send has a better core offer but weaker urgency copy — it's a middle-of-the-pack adidas email that would benefit most from putting the Sept 8 deadline in the subject line instead of the fine print.

## What's working

- 30% off with a specific code (EXTRA) stated directly in the H1 and body — no vague "up to X%" hedging.
- Four real product tiles with prices, star ratings, and review counts render cleanly — no broken images or lorem ipsum.
- Three unambiguous, high-contrast black CTA buttons (Shop Men / Women / Kids) sit right below the offer, above the fold.
- Prime badges on two products add a small trust/social-proof signal not seen in prior adidas sends in this run.
- Hero image (dark botanical leaves, serif "THE END OF SUMMER SALE" type) is moody and on-brand, distinct from the flat logo-only hero of 08-27's collaborations email.

## What's weak

- The actual expiration date (Sept 8, 11:59pm PST) is only in dense legal fine print at the bottom — nowhere in the subject, preview, or hero.
- Preview text ("End the season on a high note.") is generic filler that adds no offer detail and barely differs from the subject's own sentiment.
- Product grid is completely ungendered/uncurated — a $150 men's running shoe next to a women's-styled track top gives no persona targeting before the shopper clicks through.
- No urgency device anywhere above the fold — no countdown, no "ends Tuesday," nothing that would make me open this over the four other summer-sale emails in my inbox this week.
- "Exclusions apply*" appears in the body but the exclusion list (Gazelle, Samba, Y-3, NMD, Superstar, etc.) is long enough to cut out most of what people actually want — it's honest in that it's disclosed, but it undercuts the "30% off" headline more than the layout admits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the subject line — "30% Off Ends Sept 8" beats a vague seasonal sentiment and directly fixes the biggest open-likelihood gap.
- 2. Rewrite the preheader to carry a second selling point (the code, or a specific category) instead of restating the "end of summer" mood.
- 3. Split or gender the product grid — even a simple two-column "For Him / For Her" split would let the SHOP MEN/WOMEN buttons feel earned rather than generic.
- 4. Surface 2-3 of the biggest exclusions ("excludes Samba, Gazelle, Superstar") near the offer copy, not just in the footer — protects trust when a shopper clicks through expecting the code to work on the shoe they actually want.
- 5. Add a lightweight countdown or "6 days left" badge to the hero image itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% Off Everything — Ends Sept 8`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Week of Summer: 30% Off With Code EXTRA`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code EXTRA takes 30% off full-price and sale styles through Sept 8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, apparel, and more — 30% off before summer's gone.`
