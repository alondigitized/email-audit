# Out now: adidas Originals by JENNIE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine celebrity-collaboration launch email, and it's the cleanest single-hero-shot email in this recent run — one striking image of JENNIE (BLACKPINK) in a navy adidas Originals x JENNIE set, a real named collab, and no gimmicky discount noise. Compared to 2026-08-25's Billie Jean King send, this is the same playbook: recognizable person, product on body, brand story in the copy. It works as a launch announcement.
- Where it falls flat is that it's an announcement, not an offer — there's no price, no discount, no urgency, and the copy ("FUNCTIONAL GRACE," "reimagines the brand's iconic heritage") is aspirational fashion-speak rather than anything actionable. For a subscriber who isn't already a JENNIE fan, there's nothing pulling them toward a specific product, size, or price point — just "go shop" and "go find a store."
- Solid brand execution, weak conversion mechanics. This will perform on brand equity and fan enthusiasm, not on the rubric signals that drive clicks for a general subscriber.

## What's working

- Strong, on-brand hero image — JENNIE in the actual collab piece, well-lit, no render bugs.
- Clear collaborator identity (JENNIE name + adidas Originals trefoil lockup on the image itself).
- Two distinct, well-labeled CTAs (Shop Now / Find Store Now) with directional arrows.
- Top utility bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") gives a fallback path for non-collab shoppers.
- Store-locator disclaimer with named cities/malls signals real in-store availability, not vaporware.

## What's weak

- No product name, price, or size/fit detail is visible — a subscriber can't tell what they're actually buying.
- No urgency or scarcity beyond "distributed first-come, first-served" buried in fine print.
- Only one product image is shown; no sense of the full collection (shoes? more apparel?).
- Copy is vague brand-voice fluff ("Functional Grace," "iconic heritage") rather than concrete product info.
- Preheader ("A futuristic vision of modern femininity") is disconnected from and doesn't complement the literal subject/JENNIE framing well for a broad audience.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a second product tile or carousel showing 2-3 more pieces from the collection with visible price — right now there's zero pricing anywhere in the email, which is unusual even for a launch drop.
- 2. Replace vague fine-print urgency ("first-come, first-served... while supplies last") with a visible on-image or subhead callout like "Limited drop — shop before it sells out."
- 3. Give the hero copy one concrete anchor — a price, a release detail, or "Available now in [X] pieces" — so it's not pure mood copy.
- 4. Tighten the preheader to name JENNIE and the collab explicitly instead of generic "futuristic femininity" language.
