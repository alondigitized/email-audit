# FIFA World Cup 26™ Mexico Third Jersey
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas's FIFA World Cup 26 launch vehicle for the Mexico Third Jersey, and the premise is strong — a cultural product, a timely tournament, and an emotionally resonant identity angle. The preheader ("Every detail reflects the passion, identity, and spirit of Mexico") is the best line in the email. Unfortunately the hero image does the opposite of what a product launch needs: the jersey is swallowed by darkness. A black-on-black render makes the product nearly invisible. If you can't see what you're being sold, the rest of the copy is arguing into a void.
- The email then pivots mid-scroll to a Strava x Adiclub partnership module — the same structural problem that hurt the May 25 Anthony Edwards hybrid and was flagged as a pattern then. Launching a culturally loaded national jersey deserves a single-topic email, not a tacked-on fitness tracker promo.
- Three CTAs (SHOP NOW / SHOP ALL FIFA WORLD CUP 26™ / FIND STORE NOW) create choice paralysis where a single directive should live. The line "Shop online at your local adidas store.*" is grammatically contradictory — "online" and "at your local store" are different channels — and the asterisk goes unexplained in the visible copy. Bottom line: a legitimately good product moment, poorly executed.

## What's working

- Preheader copy is the standout: "Every detail reflects the passion, identity, and spirit of Mexico" emotionally extends the dry product name without repeating it.
- Subject line is precise and timely — FIFA World Cup 26™ Mexico Third Jersey lands in under 40 characters, mobile-friendly and zero spam signals.
- The "SHOP ALL FIFA WORLD CUP 26™ →" secondary CTA correctly orients fans who want the broader collection, not just one SKU.
- Strava x Adiclub copy is clean and functional for adiClub members who care about activity tracking.

## What's weak

- The hero image is nearly all black — the jersey is invisible. This is the product launch's only visual moment and it fails to show the product.
- Three competing CTAs (SHOP NOW, SHOP ALL FIFA WORLD CUP 26™, FIND STORE NOW) dilute the primary action; a launch email needs one clear directive.
- "Shop online at your local adidas store.*" is contradictory copy; the asterisk has no visible resolution.
- Strava x Adiclub is completely unrelated to FIFA/Mexico and fragments what should be a focused cultural launch.
- No price, no inventory signal, no urgency mechanism — nothing tells the reader why now vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the hero image first** — the product must be visible. A black jersey can still be showcased against contrast (a pitch, a stadium, stadium lighting). An invisible product is an invisible sell.
- 2. **Collapse to one CTA** — "SHOP THE MEXICO THIRD JERSEY" as a green button, nothing else above the fold. Move "SHOP ALL WORLD CUP 26™" to a secondary row below body copy.
- 3. **Remove the Strava module** — save it for a standalone adiClub email. Don't dilute a cultural product story with unrelated partnerships.
- 4. **Add urgency or scarcity language** — "Available in limited sizes for World Cup 26" or "Official kit, shipping now" tells the reader this has a real shelf life.
- 5. **Fix the contradictory CTA copy** — pick one channel: either "Shop now at adidas.com" or "Find this jersey in stores near you." Not both in the same sentence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mexico Third Jersey — Official Kit for World Cup 26`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Mexico Third Jersey. Made for the world stage.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Available now — limited sizes. Heritage design, World Cup 26 ready.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The jersey rooted in pride. Shop online or find a store near you.`

## Technical Audit — adidas FIFA World Cup 26™ Mexico Third Jersey

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard failures: a broken dynamic-content link and a missing plain-text MIME part. Additionally, a single HTTP-only image asset is referenced eight times, which will be blocked in security-hardened clients, and two images carry no alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken dynamic content link**
The QA prober resolved `https://dv.adidas.com/o/3e9b4806-236c-4ed2-8488-2cf3a6ffef40?cp_tp=v3.eJ...` and received an internal redirect to a relative path with an unknown URL scheme:

```
unknown url type: '/g/3e9b4806-236c-4ed2-8488-2cf3a6ffef40?txn=dc562580-5e94-11f1-bbb9-26d090a92706'
```

This is a server-side routing error on `dv.adidas.com` — the dynamic content service returned a relative URI as if it were an absolute redirect target. Any link or image tied to this token ID will fail to resolve for recipients.

**[INFO] 36 tracking links skipped**
All click links route through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirect chains. Landing page destinations and UTM parameters cannot be verified without following the full redirect. No further action here unless a destination audit is required separately.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] HTTP-only image asset — 8 occurrences of the same file**
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
This is the "arrow" CTA icon, served over plain HTTP, repeated across all call-to-action rows. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all proxy or hard-block non-HTTPS image sources. Every CTA arrow will render as a broken image in those clients. The fix is to serve this asset from `https://image.link.adidas.com/...` — the CDN almost certainly supports TLS already.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on at least 2 images**
- `3e9b4806-236c-4ed2-8488-2cf3a6ffef40` (the broken dynamic content image above) — `alt=""` or absent
- `3eCkozz58krV0JT_nGgpSnKrDt1BrOiCPqWw-F568a4=452` (pixel/tracker via `click.comms.adidas.com`) — alt absent

The tracker pixel with no alt is standard practice, but the product/hero image missing alt text is an accessibility gap and will render as a blank in image-off environments.

**[INFO] Font-face declarations load local system fonts only**
Both `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` `@font-face` blocks reference `local()` sources only — no remote font URLs. This is intentional for email (avoids external font fetch failures) and causes no rendering defect; system fallbacks (Arial/Helvetica) load as designed.

**[INFO] HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Standard for broad Outlook/legacy client compatibility. No issue.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The QA relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true absence — but if the header is genuinely missing, Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the native unsubscribe UI for bulk senders, which violates both platforms' 2024 sender requirements.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click machine unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo for senders above the volume threshold adidas certainly exceeds. Absence of this header (again, possibly a relay artifact) is a compliance gap.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status for `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is unverifiable from the relayed headers. This is likely a relay-capture limitation; adidas's sending infrastructure is well-established. If headers can be pulled from raw SMTP delivery, verify SPF/DKIM alignment between `adidas.com` and `us-news.comms.adidas.com`.

**[INFO] `ROBOTS: NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW` and `referrer: no-referrer` meta tags are present** — correct defensive posture for email HTML.
