# Check out this month's adiClub benefits, here is your November Newsletter
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a members-only newsletter built around four giveaways (MLS fan prize, BW Army Shoe of the Month, Billie Jean King, Toca Boca discount). It is on-brand for adiClub and reads as a reward, not a sale. After a week of "Up to 60% off" Black Friday sends (11-18, 11-21, 11-23), it is a sharp change of tone. That could be a welcome break or a missed chance, because this is the Black Friday week and there is no discount anywhere.
- The hero is strong: a pink Inter Miami-style jersey with the 3-Stripes cushions on a graphic rug. But the headline banner is cropped ("OUR adiclub NEWSLETTER • Y"). That is a visible render bug at the very top. The body is one long scroll of four equal-weight "ENTER NOW" links. Nothing says which prize matters most.
- The bottom countdown banner ("Member Appreciation Weekend", 15 days) is the best hook for adiClub members, and it sits below the footer links. It deserves a spot much higher. Overall this is a solid loyalty touch with weak prioritisation. It is better for engagement than for revenue.

## What's working

- The hero image is polished, colourful, and clearly shows the MLS jersey prize.
- Every giveaway gets a plain-language headline and a one-line description.
- The black "ENTER NOW" buttons and underlined links are consistent and easy to find.
- The Member Appreciation Weekend countdown gives members a reason to come back.
- The Toca Boca collab is a fresh, playful piece that breaks up the layout.
- The tone is honest and has no hidden terms. The asterisk on the BW Army shoes is explained at the bottom.

## What's weak

- The "YOUR adiclub NEWSLETTER" banner is clipped at both edges, so it reads "OUR … Y".
- There is no price offer in Black Friday week, only entries to win things.
- Four giveaways with the same "ENTER NOW" label compete with each other, and there is no single hero action.
- The countdown and member weekend teaser sit below the fold, under the footer-adjacent area.
- The Toca Boca block is a large illustration, and its benefit ("exclusive discount") is vague with no number.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" bar at the top is a generic distraction from the newsletter's content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Member Appreciation Weekend countdown up, directly under the hero, and say what members get (early access, extra % off).
- 2. Fix the clipped newsletter banner so it fits the full width.
- 3. Choose one primary giveaway and use a distinct CTA. Make the others smaller secondary links with specific names ("Win BW Army shoes").
- 4. Put a member perk number in the Toca Boca copy (for example "20% off in the Toca Boca World webshop").
- 5. Add one product or sale link to a Black Friday member price, so the email doesn't leave money on the table.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: it has `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and responsive media queries. QA flagged one hard failure, an empty plain-text part (0 chars), plus an uncaptured-header gap and two image issues. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com/...`). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **One image loads over plain `http://`.** The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a2103fccb6-f6bccf37-df46-4e8c-b3de-30eb8f74a3af-000000/dLlsbX68h45vdoEEfG-cOGfbW...` image is flagged "Image uses http://". The `/CI0/` path looks like an ESP open-tracking pixel. Mixed-content blocking by some clients would suppress open tracking. The same host serves HTTPS elsewhere, so switching it to `https://` is low effort.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on `dv.adidas.com/o/6b49e8bc-0420-4dfe-9775-a602ffb3d6f3?cp_tp=...`.** If this is a content image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description. This is a real issue.
- **Missing alt on the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` pixel.** For a tracking pixel this is low severity, but it should carry `alt=""` so it is explicitly marked decorative.
- **The plain-text part is empty (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** Text-only clients and accessibility tools get nothing. Some spam filters also penalize HTML-only mail.
- The HTML source shows no other confirmed rendering defects. Web fonts (`AdihausDIN`, `AdineuePRO`) resolve to local Arial/Helvetica, so they have fallbacks. A global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` rule is worth a check but is not confirmed as a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The source is truncated, so tokens further down are unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this may be a capture artifact rather than a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, subject to the same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be checked. The sender domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not verified.** They are likely in the truncated footer. The source does show footer CSS classes such as `.mobile-footer-padding-block` and `.mobile-privacy-transactional`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 17 links go through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages are hidden. The probe was skipped, so no redirect chain was followed. No mismatch is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text MIME part.** This is the only hard QA failure.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the `dv.adidas.com/o/6b49e8bc-...` image, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. **Serve the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` pixel over HTTPS.**
4. **Check the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and read the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results directly. The AgentMail relay may be hiding them.
5. **Re-run QA with the full, untruncated HTML.** Then follow a sample of the redirect links to check UTM tagging, landing pages, and the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.
