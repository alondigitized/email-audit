# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened adidas.com and got hit with a wall — literally. Instead of a homepage, I'm staring at a security block with the trefoil up top and big bold text telling me I can't get in. No products, no drops, no hero, nothing. For a brand I check daily, getting locked out like I'm a bot is a genuinely bad look.

## What's working

- **The trefoil logo** renders cleanly and is immediately recognizable — brand identity survives even on an error page.
- **The error message is legible** — clean sans-serif, high contrast, nothing ambiguous about what happened (even if it's terrible news).

## What's weak

- **Full homepage is inaccessible** — I see "UNFORTUNATELY WE ARE UNABLE TO GIVE YOU ACCESS TO OUR SITE AT THIS TIME." That's it. No product, no offer, no CTA.
- **Reference Error code (`0.49d7ce17.1778695740.2f09e9fc`)** is exposed with zero user-friendly explanation or recovery path visible above the fold.
- **"A security issue was automatically identified"** — adidas just accused me of being a threat when I'm one of their best customers. That framing stings.
- **No fallback navigation** — not even a "go back" button or a search bar to try again.
- **Zero commerce content** — every single rubric signal for business impact, engagement, and conversion is zeroed out.

## Recommendations

- **Add a user-friendly recovery CTA** — something like "Try again" or "Open the app instead" so a real customer doesn't just bounce forever.
- **Suppress the raw reference error code** for end users — route it to a support flow or at minimum pair it with a "Report this issue" link.
- **Geo/bot logic needs tuning** — if I'm hitting this on a standard mobile open, adidas is miscategorizing real shoppers as bots and torching conversion.
- **Offer a deep link to the app** — if the web is locked, give me one tap to the CONFIRMED app so the session isn't a total loss.
