# Up to 50% off our favorites for fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain, no-frills sale send. It has a moody plaid-and-three-stripes hero reading "ADIDAS FAVORITES", a "UP TO 50% OFF FOR FALL" headline, one line of copy, and three buttons for men, women and kids. The offer is clear and the "no code needed" line removes friction. The layout is clean and nothing is broken.
- The creative says almost nothing, though. "Favorites" and "iconic styles" never name a single product. The hero is an abstract fabric close-up with no shoe, jacket or model, so there's no reason to click beyond the discount itself. After a week of single-product launches (SL72 on 10-04, Samba on 10-02, the Hellstar drop on 10-05), this reads as a generic markdown blast.
- Judgement: a competent but forgettable clearance email. The 50% is probably carrying all of it. "Up to" hides how deep the discount is on most items.

## What's working

- "Up to 50% off" is stated in the subject, the headline and the body copy, so the offer is never in doubt.
- "No code needed" removes a common friction point and builds trust.
- The black buttons have strong contrast and use a verb plus a noun ("SHOP MEN").
- The layout is uncluttered, with a clear top-to-bottom flow from hero to headline to buttons.
- The 3-Stripes plaid hero is on-brand and fits the fall framing.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product or person in it, so it gives no visual hint of what's discounted.
- "Up to 50%" with no example items or prices gives no sense of the real savings.
- Three stacked buttons with the same weight make you pick a gender before you've seen anything you want.
- There is no urgency or end date, so there's no reason to act today.
- The preheader shows as a small underlined link at the top of the render, and the body copy is generic ("Fan fave pieces are popular for a reason").

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the abstract hero with two or three real products, each with its sale price and a "was/now" pair. Name the product (e.g., Samba, Ultraboost), not "favorites".
- 2. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Sunday", and a member-pricing line for logged-in subscribers.
- 3. Lead with the deepest, most credible number ("Samba, Gazelle & more, 40–50% off") instead of a vague "up to".
- 4. Make the buttons side by side, or personalize the primary one to the subscriber's category, so a single path stands out.
- 5. Rewrite the body copy to give a reason for the sale, such as the fall wardrobe, cold-weather gear or a specific category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Samba, Gazelle & more: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall favorites just dropped in price`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed. Shop sale prices on shoes, jackets and more through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved fall styles, now up to 50% off. Sizes go fast.`
