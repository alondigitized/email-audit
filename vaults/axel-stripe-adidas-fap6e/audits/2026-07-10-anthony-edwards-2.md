# Anthony Edwards 2
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas's launch email for the Anthony Edwards 2 in what they're calling Ant's signature blue colorway. The photography earns its space — a rich, saturated blue shoe against a matching blue background is a striking, coherent visual identity. The second image giving a low side-angle profile of the shoe reinforces the silhouette nicely.
- The problem is the same one that plagued the July 5 Adizero Dropset Pro send: there's no offer here. No discount, no launch incentive, no limited-window hook. After the strong 30% off summer clearance email on June 30, adidas has sent three consecutive emails (Dropset Pro, new styles grid, now AE2) with zero financial pull. The subscriber who clicked through to grab a deal last week is now being asked to pay full price with no reason to act today.
- The copy is serviceable but thin — "built to rise with you, on the court and beyond" is the kind of line that could appear in any shoe launch email. For a signature athlete shoe, where the player's identity is supposed to be the emotional anchor, you expect more Ant and less boilerplate.

## What's working

- **Hero image is visually bold** — the all-blue-on-blue treatment gives this email a distinct identity that breaks inbox grid monotony.
- **Dual CTAs are well-placed** — "SHOP NOW" and "FIND STORE NOW" together serve both online and in-store intents cleanly.
- **Technical detail is present** — the "Innovation Meets Performance" section naming Propulsion Plate, Articulated Fangs, and Lightstrike+ gives performance buyers something to evaluate.
- **Preheader does the job the subject can't** — "The latest Anthony Edwards | basketball shoe is here. Shop now" adds context that makes the bare subject line intelligible.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever** — after a 30% off send on June 30, this full-price launch email has nothing to pull a purchase forward.
- **Subject is almost empty** — "Anthony Edwards 2" is the product SKU name, not a subject line; it does zero emotional work for anyone not already tracking Ant's signature line.
- **Anthony Edwards himself is invisible** — his name is in the copy but there's no photo of the athlete, no quote, no personality. This could be any generic shoe launch.
- **The hero copy is generic** — "impossible to ignore," "built to rise with you" are filler phrases; for a signature athlete shoe the copy should feel like Ant's voice.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give Ant a face in the email.** The entire value proposition of a signature athlete shoe is aspirational identification. A single image of Edwards in game action wearing the AE2 would do more than any copy line.
- 2. **Add a launch-week hook.** Even something light — "free shipping through Sunday" or "buy before July 14 and get early access to AE3 drops" — creates a reason to click now vs. later.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero copy around Ant specifically.** Pull an actual quote from Edwards, or write in his documented voice (brash, competitive, fun). "One goal" is wasted on generic copy.
- 4. **Tighten the "Innovation Meets Performance" section.** "First step to final buzzer" is good. "Believe That" as a closing beat needs a stronger setup — right now it lands cold.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect URL — unknown protocol scheme**

The click-tracking wrapper contains a relative-style path (`/g/...`) rather than an absolute URL as the destination argument:


The redirect resolver at `dv.adidas.com/o/` is decoding the `cp_tp` payload and producing a relative path instead of an absolute destination. ### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Mixed-content HTTP images — may be blocked**

The same arrow image is served over HTTP six times:
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
Gmail, Outlook (modern), and Apple Mail all block mixed-content HTTP resources when the email is viewed over HTTPS. The arrow asset will fail to render in the majority of environments. Fix: change the `src` scheme to `https://`.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**

The QA relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This header is required for Gmail bulk-sender compliance (Google's Feb 2024 mandate for >5k/day senders) and expected by most major ISPs. If the header is present at the MTA but stripped by the AgentMail relay, this is a relay configuration issue — but if it is genuinely absent, the send is out of compliance.

One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender policy) depends on `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` appearing alongside a `https://` unsubscribe URL in `List-Unsubscribe`. Its absence puts the send at risk of deliverability penalties from Gmail and Yahoo.
