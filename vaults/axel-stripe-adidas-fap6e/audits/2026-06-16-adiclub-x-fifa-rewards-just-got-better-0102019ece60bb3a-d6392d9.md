# adiClub x FIFA Rewards just got better 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the most strategically distinct adidas send in weeks. After a run of Summer Kickoff / 40%-off emails (June 3, June 12) that were structurally nearly identical, this is a genuine break in cadence — a loyalty program announcement built around a real World Cup partnership hook. The three-reward stack (double points, signed Messi, game-used ball) is legitimately compelling content, and the timing with the World Cup is self-evidently right.
- The core problem is that the best copy is buried. The subject says "just got better 🔥" — the single vaguest possible lede — while the actual hooks (DOUBLE POINTS, signed Messi prizes, official match ball giveaway) sit inside the scroll. A subscriber who didn't open will never know. The preheader compounds this with navigation boilerplate ("View product lines, promotions and rewards for connected members") instead of a second punch.
- The inside of the email earns more than the outside deserves. The structure is clean, the partnership branding is immediate, and the three distinct offer blocks are well-differentiated. But the account-linking ask as the primary CTA — before any product offer fires — is a friction layer that will blunt conversion, especially for members who are on the fence about why connecting is worth it.

## What's working

- **Cadence break is real**: after two near-identical "Summer Kickoff" blasts, this reads as a different kind of send — a loyalty program update — so it doesn't feel like a rerun.
- **Partnership branding is immediate**: adiClub + FIFA Rewards logos side-by-side in the dark hero communicate "official collab" without needing text to explain it.
- **Three offer mechanics are clearly differentiated**: double points (transactional), signed Messi prize (aspirational), game-used ball (collectible) — each block has its own visual and CTA.
- **CTA copy is unusually clear**: "CONNECT ACCOUNTS →" is a specific verb-noun CTA, which is better than the generic "Discover" or "Shop Now" that adidas defaults to.
- **World Cup timing**: the entire email feels current; no stale promo language, no recycled assets.

## What's weak

- **Subject line buries the lead**: "just got better 🔥" signals nothing concrete — the signed Messi prize and double points never make it to the inbox.
- **Preheader is navigation boilerplate**: "View product lines, promotions and rewards for connected members" reads like default template copy, not a hook.
- **No hard deadline on "limited time"**: the double points card says "for limited time" with no date; this kills the urgency that would actually drive account-connection clicks.
- **Account-linking is a cold ask as the primary CTA**: the email leads with a conversion that requires trust before it offers value — a "why should I?" moment that nothing in the hero answers quickly enough.
- **Signed Messi and game ball prizes are undersized visually**: the most emotionally arresting offers are small cards at the bottom; they deserved more hero treatment to justify the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the best hook in the subject** — "Win signed Messi gear" and "2x points on FIFA jerseys" are the actual story; "just got better" is the editorial label no one cares about.
- 2. **Rewrite the preheader to add a specific benefit or deadline** — it has 60 characters to do the job the subject leaves undone.
- 3. **Add a real date to "limited time"** — even "through June 30" turns a soft nudge into a hard reason to act now.
- 4. **Reframe the hero around the member benefit, not the program mechanic** — "Connect accounts. Earn 2x points on every FIFA jersey." is a clearer value prop than "GET MORE REWARDS."
- 5. **Elevate the Messi prize** — signed Messi merch is a genuine aspirational hook; it could appear in the hero callout or at minimum get a larger tile treatment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win signed Messi gear — connect adiClub x FIFA`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2x points on FIFA jerseys. Plus enter to win Messi's signature.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double points on FIFA World Cup jerseys through [date] — adiClub members only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Signed Messi jersey giveaway inside. Connect accounts to enter.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken Movable Ink redirect**
The dynamic image pixel fires to a Movable Ink open-tracking URL but the internal redirect resolves to a malformed relative path:


### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] HTTP image sources (2 instances, same asset)**

Gmail, Outlook 365, and Apple Mail all block mixed-content images by default. This asset will display as a broken image icon on those clients unless upgraded to HTTPS.

**[WARN] Missing alt text (2 images)**

The beacon is expected to be invisible (`alt=""`), but the Movable Ink image is likely visible content. Missing alt means screen readers skip it entirely and image-disabled clients show blank space with no fallback text.
