- Segmented CTAs (Shop Men / Women / Kids) give a low-friction next step for different shoppers.

## What's weak

- No offer whatsoever — no discount, no price, no urgency signal of any kind.
- Preview text ("New Samba colors, ready for the halls.") just restates the subject/headline concept instead of adding new information.
