# Up to 60% off for Black Friday is live
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud Black Friday announcement. The "UP TO 60% OFF / BLACK FRIDAY" hero is enormous and legible, and the black-and-white button stack is easy to scan. The offer is honest, with no buried fine print.
- It's also the third near-identical beat in a week, after "Save up to 60% during Black Friday" on 11-21 and "Up to 60% off: Black Friday first access ends today" on 11-18. "Is live" is the only new information, and it carries no deadline or reason to act today. The countdown banners from the earlier sends are gone, which removes the urgency that made the 11-18 version work.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but forgettable. The email gives the shopper no deadline, no named product and no price point, so there is little reason to click now.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. White condensed type over a saturated red and charcoal photo puts the discount in the first screen.
- The photo of two models in adidas apparel (the red track jacket with 3-Stripes) is on-brand and warm.
- The three CTAs (Shop Men, Shop Women, Shop Kids) are high-contrast, left-aligned buttons with verb + noun copy.
- The page is short and uncluttered. It has one headline, one paragraph and three buttons.
- The scrolling "BLACK FRIDAY UP TO 60% OFF" ticker above the hero reinforces the offer without adding clutter.

## What's weak

- Nothing is time-bound. No end date, countdown or "ends Monday" appears anywhere, so the urgency is empty.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" bar above the hero sells full-price best sellers, which contradicts the sale message and competes with the main CTAs.
- No product, price or example is shown, such as "hoodies from $X". "Jerseys, fleece, sneakers, and more" is vague.
- The body copy is generic ("take your gifting game to the next level") and repeats the preheader.
- adiClub member pricing isn't mentioned, even though earlier sends leaned on first access. This looks like a step back from the 11-18 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline: a countdown, or "Ends Cyber Monday" in the hero. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Remove the "best sellers" bar, or turn it into a sale-priced best-sellers module.
- 3. Add a row of 3–4 product tiles with sale prices and "was/now" pricing below the hero.
- 4. Replace the generic paragraph with one line on what's discounted and, if applicable, member perks.
- 5. Collapse or reorder the CTAs, for example leading with the highest-converting category, and add "Shop all deals".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is on: up to 60% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hoodies, Sambas & more: up to 60% off now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jerseys, fleece and sneakers from $20. Members get early access to extra deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest sale of the year ends Cyber Monday. Shop before sizes go.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"` and responsive media queries. Automated QA recorded 1 FAIL (no plain-text part) and several WARNs: header capture gaps, `http://` image sources and missing alt text. Two of these, the plain-text and header results, may come from the AgentMail relay rather than the sender.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 22 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped in the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are **unverified**. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Click tracking uses `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` and image hosting uses `image.link.adidas.com`. Both are on a sending subdomain of the brand domain, which is good for alignment.
- The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` image URL is served over `http://`, so the tracking request is unencrypted and may be blocked or rewritten by clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** six instances of the same image, `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` (alt="arrow"), use a non-HTTPS source. A seventh, the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...=452` image, also uses `http://`. Clients that block mixed content will show broken images.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/5e58caf6-b9e4-46a5-b71b-0eecff8af634?cp_tp=...`
  - the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...=452` image
- If the `=452` image is a tracking pixel, an empty `alt=""` is appropriate. Otherwise, add descriptive alt text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` omits `width=device-width`. The email relies on media queries, so this can affect scaling in some clients.
- The `@font-face` rules use `local()` sources with Arial fallbacks. I found no rendering issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source showed no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%name%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The visible portion is also too short to confirm that no personalization exists.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback:** the text version is 0 chars. A missing `text/plain` part can hurt deliverability and accessibility. Confirm against the raw MIME to rule out a relay capture artifact.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip them, so verify them in the original message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this volume.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a brand subdomain, which supports alignment, but this is not confirmed.
- The in-body footer unsubscribe link and the physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 22 tracking links were skipped and the source is truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "Up to 60% off for Black Friday" can't be assessed. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all images over HTTPS, in particular `image.link.adidas.com/.../b0c011a2-...jpg` and the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` image.
2. Add alt text to `dv.adidas.com/o/5e58caf6-...`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative or a tracking pixel.
3. Add a `text/plain` alternative to the multipart message, or verify that it exists in the original message and wasn't dropped by the relay.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw headers.
5. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the raw `Authentication-Results`.
6. Change the viewport meta tag to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
7. Improve the QA tooling. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow `click.comms.adidas.com` to the final URL) so UTM and landing-page checks can run, and capture raw headers so the compliance warnings stop being ambiguous.
