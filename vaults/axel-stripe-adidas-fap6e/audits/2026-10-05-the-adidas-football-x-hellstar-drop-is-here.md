# The adidas Football x Hellstar drop is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a collab drop with a second, unrelated message attached. The top half is the adidas Football x Hellstar cleat. It has a dark, moody hero of icy-grey flame-pattern cleats on wet turf, and the co-brand lockup is clean. The eye lands on the shoe first, which is right. The bottom half switches to a "2026 Rookie Class Giveaway II" for adiClub members, with three NFL rookies holding footballs. It has its own headline, copy and two more buttons.
- The two halves split the attention. A Hellstar drop is a hype-and-scarcity product, and this email gives it no price, no date, no sizing and no urgency. The giveaway section is mostly text, and it ends in "Learn More" and "Participate" with nothing to say what the prize is or when the entry closes. For a football-cleat buyer it's decent. For a general sneaker subscriber it's a niche send with no hook.
- Cadence is the other problem. This is the fourth daily send in the last four days, after the SL72 spotlight on 10/4, the Chicago Marathon on 10/3 and "Spots 🤝 Samba" on 10/2. Each one pushes a different product, so the inbox looks like a stream and none of them stands out. Judgement: a handsome hero and a weak, split message. It's average.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The cleat is close-up and lit, with the adidas × Hellstar lockup at the top, so it reads as a collab immediately.
- The Hellstar section has one product and one button, "SHOP NOW", directly under a short brand-voice blurb.
- The preheader "Hellstar hits the turf 🏈🔥" is short and adds a football cue the subject doesn't have.
- The rookie panel shows three athletes with real faces and strong portrait styling. It gives the adiClub tie-in a human angle.
- The subject is clear and under 50 characters. The sender name is instantly recognisable.

## What's weak

- There is no price, release date, sizing note or scarcity cue anywhere. A "drop" email with no "when" or "how many" wastes the hype.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner sits above the hero and is indented differently from the hero and body. It looks misaligned and pulls attention to a generic ask before the collab.
- The giveaway section is long and text-heavy. It names three athletes but doesn't say what the prize is or when entry ends. "Learn More" and "Participate" next to each other are redundant.
- There are four "shop/learn" buttons and two separate stories. It's unclear which one the email wants you to act on.
- The preheader shows as a small underlined link at the very top of the body, which looks unpolished.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send. Make the Hellstar drop its own email, with the release date, price, sizes and a "limited run" cue. Move the rookie giveaway to a separate adiClub send, or at most a one-line footer teaser.
- 2. Remove the generic "best sellers" banner, or move it below the hero so the collab comes first.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with a specific CTA such as "Shop the Hellstar cleats", and add a visible price and a "while sizes last" line.
- 4. If the giveaway stays, cut it to two sentences, state the prize and the deadline, and use a single "Enter the giveaway" button.
- 5. Hide the preheader text from the visible body, or style it properly.
