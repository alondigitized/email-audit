# Gold details. Messi legacy. Limited drop.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a one-idea send with a strong hero. Two Messi shots in Argentina colors, a gold-numbered #10 jersey on the left and a waving Messi on the right, anchor the email. The "AFA MESSI FAREWELL COLLECTION" headline and a single black "Shop Now" button sit below. The emotional pull is real. The body copy is thin, and the email offers no price, no product names and no reason to act today.
- The subject "Gold details. Messi legacy. Limited drop." promises scarcity, but nothing in the render backs up "limited". There is no countdown, no stock note and no drop date. The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner above the hero pulls attention off the Messi story. It also reads as a leftover template slot, and it sits 20px right of the hero image's left edge. Compared with the Oct 18 low-profile terrace shoe send, which at least named shoes and a gifting angle, this one leans entirely on the Messi name.
- Verdict: a pretty, on-brand fan email that is weak on conversion mechanics. It will do well with soccer and Argentina fans and be ignored by everyone else.

## What's working

- The split hero pairs a product shot (jersey with gold #10, boots below) with a human moment. The pairing sells both the garment and the story.
- The sky-blue and white palette against dark navy and gold is instantly recognizable and reads as Argentina.
- The headline is large, plain and legible, and it names the collection.
- The Messi quote adds emotional weight, and the copy ties back to the "gold detailing" promised in the subject.
- The layout is short and clean, with one dominant black CTA that is easy to find.

## What's weak

- The email has no offer, price or product name. The copy never says which jersey, which boots or what they cost.
- "Limited drop" has no support anywhere in the body: no date, quantity or deadline.
- The generic "best sellers" banner competes with the hero and dilutes the Messi story. It is also slightly misaligned with the image below it.
- The preheader renders as an underlined link at the very top of the email, which looks unpolished.
- The body paragraph has a stray space before the closing quote and a double space after "Lionel Messi." The single "Shop Now" is generic and sends people to the whole collection.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name one hero product and its price. For example, put "Argentina Messi Farewell Jersey, $XX" under the image and link the CTA straight to that jersey.
- 2. Make "limited" real. Add a visible drop date, an "available while supplies last" line, or a member-early-access note for adiClub.
- 3. Remove the best-sellers banner, or move it below the main story so it stops competing with the hero.
- 4. Change the CTA to "Shop the Messi jersey", or "Get the farewell jersey".
- 5. Fix the text polish: the quote spacing, and the preheader showing as visible text at the top.
- 6. Add a second row of 2-3 product tiles (jersey, boots, track jacket) so there is more than one way to click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Messi's farewell jersey is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The gold #10 Messi jersey: drops now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Argentina colors, gold details, Messi's signature logo. Jersey from $XX.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `adiClub members get first access to the AFA Messi Farewell collection.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a standard table-based template with responsive media queries. The confirmed defects are an empty plain-text part, plain `http://` image and tracking URLs, and missing alt text on two images. Compliance headers and link destinations couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links weren't probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure image hosts.** The "arrow" image is served over HTTP, and the QA output lists it twice: `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`. Some clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources.
- **Insecure tracker.** The image `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1528f2366-3cee8dcc-3700-4e2c-ac64-41f339100f62-000000/uKqHJvpuSv9Han9cCXcEAphyvv8n2dTaFLsBVhugZa0=452` is also plain HTTP. It looks like an open-tracking pixel, which is an inference from the `click.comms` path and the missing alt text. If a client blocks it, opens go uncounted.
- **Referrer policy.** `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. Web-view or landing-page referrer attribution will be lost, but this appears intentional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/bafa57ed-ea85-4c8e-b8e8-24b5d918289c?cp_tp=...` has no `alt`. It is a content image served from the DV asset host, so images-off clients and screen readers get nothing. The tracker above also lacks `alt`; that has little user impact if it is a pixel.
- **Viewport meta.** It is `initial-scale=1.0` with no `width=device-width`. The `@media` rules cover mobile clients, so the impact is low.
- **Fonts.** The `@font-face` rules for AdihausDIN and AdineuePRO point only at `local('Arial')`, `local('Helvetica')` and similar, so the brand fonts never load. This is a graceful fallback to system fonts, not a defect. `mso-font-alt: Arial` is set for Outlook.
- **Global `p` rule.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` centers all paragraphs and may override inherited alignment in some blocks. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Truncation.** The HTML was cut off, so the body markup, the tables and any VML/MSO fallbacks weren't audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The body is truncated, so this is limited to the portion supplied.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check found a text version of 0 chars. A multipart email with no `text/plain` alternative hurts spam scoring and accessibility. First rule out a capture or relay artifact, since AgentMail may strip it.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers.** The QA tool itself says this may be a relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify it against the raw original message.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `adidas@us-news.comms.adidas.com`, and alignment couldn't be tested.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 13 tracking links were skipped, so final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unknown. No issues are asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain text:** confirm whether the original message has a `text/plain` part. If it doesn't, add one.
2. **Unsubscribe headers:** check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC the same way, since the relay hides them from this audit.

4. **Alt text:** add descriptive `alt` to the `dv.adidas.com/o/bafa57ed-…` image. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. **Audit tooling:** follow the redirects on the 13 tracking links, with a GET and the click-tracker allow-listed. That would let a re-run check UTM parameters and landing-page continuity.
