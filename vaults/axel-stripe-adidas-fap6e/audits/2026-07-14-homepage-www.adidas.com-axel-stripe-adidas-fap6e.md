# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Bro. I opened adidas.com and got a security block — not a homepage, not a hero, not a Samba drop. Just the trefoil logo, a reference error code, and a wall of bold text telling me I can't get in. This isn't a brand experience, it's a bouncer turning me away at the door. I can't even tell what adidas is pushing right now because they literally didn't let me see it.

## What's working

- The adidas trefoil wordmark renders correctly — at minimum I know I hit the right domain and didn't land on a phishing clone.
- The error message is written in plain English ("A security issue was automatically identified") — at least they told me something went wrong instead of a raw 403 with no context.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is inaccessible — there is no content, no navigation, no product, no CTA whatsoever.
- The reference error code (`0.4ed7ce17.17840232O5.28725a5b`) is displayed raw on-screen with zero UX treatment — no "copy error ID" button, no support link, nothing actionable.
- No recovery path offered — there's no "try again," no link to customer support, no suggestion to clear cookies or try a different browser.
- The bold headline "UNFORTUNATELY WE ARE UNABLE TO GIVE YOU ACCESS TO OUR SITE AT THIS TIME." is aggressive and accusatory in tone — it implies I did something wrong rather than acknowledging a platform-side failure.
- Zero brand expression below the logo — the only thing adidas communicated to me today is that I'm blocked.

## Recommendations

- Add a **recovery CTA** beneath the error — a "Try Again" button or a link to `support.adidas.com` so I have somewhere to go instead of just staring at a wall of bold text.
- Rewrite the headline copy to be brand-appropriate and less accusatory — something like "We'll be right back" reads friendlier than "UNABLE TO GIVE YOU ACCESS."
- Surface the **adiClub / membership login link** even on the error page — a loyal member hitting this screen should at least see a path to their account or app.
- Instrument a **soft retry flow**: detect the block, offer a "Continue as Guest" or "Open in App" button, and redirect to the adidas app where purchase intent can still be captured.
