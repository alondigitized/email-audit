# ⏳ Last day to redeem your voucher
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a voucher-expiry reminder — the entire job is to get a subscriber to redeem their personalized 15% off code before May 27. The urgency mechanism is solid: a unique-looking promo code ("WELCOME3YR-8791"), a hard deadline, and a clean teal CTA button. The subject line does exactly what it needs to do.
- The problem is the email doesn't trust itself. Below the voucher block, adidas has bolted on the "Complete Your Profile for 50 Points" module — the exact same block that anchored the dedicated May 22 email four days ago — plus an app-download section. A subscriber who got that profile email on May 22 will feel like they're being nagged. The voucher email's one job is to drive redemption; everything else is noise.
- The product grid at the top is the right instinct (show me what to buy with the code), but the three thumbnail images are small and unanchored to the 15% discount — there's no price shown on the products, no "use your 15% on this" connective tissue. Fix the sequencing — voucher details first, product suggestions second, and kill the profile/app modules entirely — and this becomes a tighter, higher-converting email.

## What's working

- **Subject line urgency is sharp.** "⏳ Last day to redeem your voucher" is under 40 characters, uses the clock emoji once and tastefully, and delivers genuine time-pressure without exclamation-mark spam.
- **Personalized-looking promo code builds trust.** "WELCOME3YR-8791" reads like a genuinely unique code tied to this subscriber's account, not a mass broadcast blast code — that legitimizes the urgency.
- **Voucher terms are disclosed up front.** "Only valid on full-priced items until May 27, 2026" appears directly below the CTA — no buried fine print.
- **CTA button color is distinct.** The teal/green "REDEEM ONLINE >" button breaks cleanly from the white background and is visually identifiable.

## What's weak

- **Profile-completion module is a direct repeat from May 22.** Subscribers who received the dedicated adiClub points email four days ago will tune this out; worse, it dilutes the redemption message.
- **Product grid comes before the offer details.** The voucher block — the actual reason for the email — is the third section, not the first. The eye scans products before learning how to get a discount on them.
- **No price displayed on product thumbnails.** Three shoe images with no price means there's nothing to activate the "oh, 15% off THAT" mental connection.
- **Three competing goals kill focus.** Redeem code → Complete profile → Download app. One email, three asks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the structure: voucher block first, product grid second.** The deadline is the hook; the products are the reward. Put "HERE'S 15% OFF — expires tonight" above the fold, then show the three product picks as "here's what to spend it on."
- 2. **Kill the profile and app modules.** This send's only KPI is redemption clicks. The May 22 email already made the profile pitch. Let this one be singular.
- 3. **Add price to the product thumbnails.** Even approximate price ranges ("from $65") let the subscriber run the mental math on what 15% saves them.
- 4. **Make the CTA more specific.** "SHOP NOW WITH 15% OFF" beats "REDEEM ONLINE >" — it combines the action and the incentive in the button copy itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 15% off expires tonight — here's what to spend it on`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏳ Tonight only: 15% off with your adiClub voucher`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code WELCOME3YR-8791 at checkout before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full-priced styles, 15% off — your voucher is about to expire`

## Technical Audit — adidas "Last day to redeem your voucher"

### 1. Technical Summary

All 8 custom font assets return HTTP 403 from the S3 origin, guaranteeing fallback rendering in every client. One CTA link contains unencoded whitespace in the URL, making it technically malformed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Malformed CTA URL (unencoded space)**
The "Complete my profile" link contains a literal space character in the URL path:
```
...tkn=wywsMJxPH%2Ff27N1I1awRPs0A9SrXTw2cQ2J9POo8tBup3Sw62gOkKEyTSIkpmo8%2Fb UkrsPo9CaLH2p3...
```
The space between `%2Fb` and `UkrsPo9Ca` is unencoded. RFC 3986 requires `%20`. Depending on the redirect handler at `go.adidas.com`, this will either break the token validation or truncate the URL at the space, producing a broken or incorrect destination.

**INFO — 22 tracking links skipped**
All click-redirect links via `go.adidas.com` were not probed. Destination integrity cannot be confirmed by this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — All 8 custom fonts returning 403**

Both `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` families (Regular + Bold, woff2/ttf/woff formats) are hosted on `s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com` and return 403. The bucket policy is blocking public access. All clients will render the `mso-font-alt: Arial` fallback. Affected assets:
- `AdihausDIN-Regular.woff2` / `.ttf`
- `AdihausDIN-Bold.woff2` / `.ttf`
- `adineuePRO-Regular.woff2` / `.woff`
- `adineuePRO-Bold.woff2` / `.woff`

**WARN — 14 images missing `alt` text**

Multiple content images have empty or absent `alt` attributes, including repeated instances of `2373748f...b.gif` (appears 7 times) and key content images `b9aa5d2a...jpg`, `5bf3fd66...jpg`. Screen readers will skip these silently; images-off clients will render blank space.

**WARN — 2 images served over HTTP (mixed content)**

Two assets use `http://` origins:
- `http://image.link.adidas.com/.../966ef092...png`
- `http://image.link.adidas.com/.../37231f63...gif`

HTTPS-strict clients (Gmail, Outlook.com) will block these. Switch to `https://image.link.adidas.com/...`.

**WARN — Image CDN subdomain is `image.staging.link.adidas.com`**

The majority of images (12+) are being served from `image.staging.link.adidas.com` — a staging subdomain — in what appears to be a production send. If this subdomain is rate-limited, firewalled, or decommissioned post-campaign, images will break for anyone who opens the email after deployment.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) are visible in the truncated source. Cannot fully confirm without the complete HTML.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The QA relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This is required by Google and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5K/day senders). If the header is present upstream but stripped by the relay, re-verify directly against raw headers.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for bulk senders) depends on this header alongside a `List-Unsubscribe` mailto/https value. Absence means Gmail will not render the native unsubscribe button and may route to spam.

**WARN — SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Sending domain is `us-news.adidas.com`; DKIM alignment should be verified independently before treating deliverability as confirmed.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The "Complete my profile" CTA includes `cm_mmc` attribution params and an AppsFlyer deep link with `af_channel=Welcome`, `af_adset=MemOnb_Comms5`. UTM/attribution parameters are structurally present, but the malformed token (unencoded space, see §2) means the destination URL may not resolve correctly, breaking both the user experience and attribution tracking for this specific CTA.

Plain-text version is 72% URLs by character count (7,352 / 10,249 chars). This is a strong spam-filter signal; plain text should contain human-readable content, not URL dumps.
