# WORLD CHAMPIONS. SPAIN 🇪🇸⚽️🏆
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas's post-final victory send — dropped the morning after Spain clinches the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Where yesterday's "THE FINAL AWAITS" built anticipation, this one delivers the payoff. The gold "ESPAÑA" typography is the right creative swing for a championship moment, and the dual-beat structure — shop the winners collection, then enter to own the match-used trophy — is smart: one beat converts, one builds loyalty.
- The biggest structural flaw isn't in the body — it's what leaks before anyone opens. The top-of-email banner ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") is generic evergreen copy that will render as the preview text in most inboxes, completely disconnecting from one of the most specific moments in the campaign calendar. A subscriber who saw "THE FINAL AWAITS" yesterday and opens their inbox this morning expecting follow-through gets "best sellers" as their first impression.
- Overall: the creative earns its moment. The preheader kills the open before the send even gets a chance.

## What's working

- **"ESPAÑA" in gold typography is visually punchy** — communicates championship celebration without a single word of explanation.
- **Timing is perfect** — this is the post-win send the morning after Spain lifts the trophy; the moment-relevance is as high as it gets.
- **"SHOP SPAIN WINNERS COLLECTION" is the best CTA copy this brand has shipped in this audit series** — specific verb + noun, miles ahead of the "DOWNLOAD THE APP" whiff from the July 15 send.
- **The adiClub trophy contest is a genuine hook** — the match-used trophy as a prize is aspirational and credible, a strong loyalty beat.
- **Clean render, no visual bugs** — both image blocks load, both CTAs are visible, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **The persistent "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner is completely mismatched** — it's generic evergreen copy sitting at the very top of a historic moment email, and it's exactly what leaks as preheader in most clients.
- **No price offer anywhere in the email** — this is collection-push plus contest, with zero sale incentive for the fence-sitter who wants to buy Spain gear but won't at full price.
- **"WORLD CHAMPIONS. SPAIN" subject is all-caps** — a mild spam-filter signal and a visual shout that doesn't land as elegantly as it could in a crowded inbox.
- **The trophy contest body copy is dense and small** — on mobile the paragraph reads like fine print when it should be a one-line hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the top banner with moment-specific preheader copy** — this is the highest-leverage change. The banner/preheader slot is worth more than any other line in this email on World Cup morning.
- 2. **Add a 24-hour flash offer tied to the win** — even 10–15% off Spain gear with a midnight expiry creates the urgency this email currently lacks and would push fence-sitters into the collection.
- 3. **Reduce the trophy contest to a single punchy line** — something like "The match-used trophy. adiClub members can win it. Enter now." — dense body copy kills mobile scroll depth.
- 4. **Test a mixed-case subject line** — ALL CAPS registers as urgency-shouting and triggers soft spam signals; mixed case reads cleaner and more confident on a championship morning.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spain are World Champions. Shop the collection.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `La Roja wins it all. Your kit starts here.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The match-used trophy could be yours — adiClub members, enter now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spain's gear, Spain's trophy — shop winners + enter to win.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard failures and several warnings indicate deliverability and rendering gaps: a broken redirect link will produce a dead click for at least one CTA, and the complete absence of a plain-text fallback will trigger spam filters and cause rendering failures in text-only environments.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect — `/g/` path returns unknown URL type**
The click-tracker at `dv.adidas.com` is resolving one destination URL to a `/g/` path (`/g/0cb7d81d-1da7-45b0-9d5f-9823c82c0e5f?txn=3a4cb700-...`) which the QA probe flagged as `unknown url type`. This means at least one CTA click will produce a broken experience. The source link is:
```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/0cb7d81d-1da7-45b0-9d5f-9823c82c0e5f?cp_tp=v3...
```
The `/o/` → `/g/` redirect chain is not completing correctly. Likely a misconfigured or expired deep-link in the `cp_tp` payload.

**[WARN] 14 tracking links skipped during HTTP probe**
All click-redirect URLs (`click.comms.adidas.com`) were skipped by the probe. Link health for the remaining 14 CTAs is unverified.

**[WARN] One image served over HTTP**
The tracking pixel/image at `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f7caaf337-...` uses a plain HTTP src. Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook proxy images over HTTPS; a non-HTTPS src may be blocked or load broken in strict clients.
```
src: http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f7caaf337-53cc9494-5454-4b45-8f61-bc45f9ea2225-000000/...
```

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**
Both flagged images have empty or absent `alt` attributes:
- `https://dv.adidas.com/o/0cb7d81d-...` (likely a hero or product image)
- `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f7caaf337-...` (tracking/footer image)

Images-off rendering (common in Outlook and corporate environments) will show blank boxes with no context for these elements.

**No issues found** with character encoding (`UTF-8` declared), viewport meta, or iOS telephone-detection suppression — all correctly set.

**Minor:** `@font-face` declarations for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` point exclusively to local system fonts (`Arial`, `Helvetica`, `Calibri`) with no remote font source. This is intentional for email compatibility but means the declared custom typefaces will never load on any client — the font-face block provides no value beyond the `mso-font-alt` fallback. Not a bug, but dead code.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, this violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since February 2024 for senders >5k/day). The QA note acknowledges this may be a relay capture gap — confirm against raw headers before treating as a definitive failure.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for bulk senders) depends on this header alongside `List-Unsubscribe`. Same caveat as above — verify against raw SMTP headers.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed via the relay capture. The sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` should be authenticated; this needs verification at the MTA level before send.

No missing physical postal address or unsubscribe mechanism was detected in the HTML body (footer typically carries these for CAN-SPAM compliance — present but not shown in truncated source).

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The broken `/g/` redirect (see §2) means at least one CTA lands on an error page rather than the intended product or campaign URL — this is the most critical continuity failure.

The `cp_tp` parameter contains a base64-encoded payload (Adidas's internal campaign tracking). UTM parameters are embedded within the encoded blob and cannot be independently verified from the truncated source, but the redirect failure means they are irrelevant for the broken link until the routing issue is resolved.

No issues found with other observable link structures.
