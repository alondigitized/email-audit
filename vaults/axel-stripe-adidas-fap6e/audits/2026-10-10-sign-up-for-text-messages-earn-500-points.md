# SIGN UP FOR TEXT MESSAGES, EARN 500 POINTS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a utility send: adidas wants your phone number. It leads with a clean lifestyle hero of four stylish friends and a dog, then a blunt all-caps headline that states the deal. The deal is 500 adiClub points for opting into texts. The layout is simple, with one black button and no clutter. The hierarchy works.
- The offer is weak and abstract. 500 points has no stated dollar value, and the copy says only "discount vouchers, rewards, and more." The fine print limits it to new text sign-ups and delays payout up to 5 days. The hero shows apparel and a dog and says nothing about points or texting, so the image and the ask don't connect. A visible "Hi ," with a blank name makes it feel like a mail-merge miss.
- Compared with the recent product-led sends, like the 10/08 Samba gifting email and the 10/06 "Up to 50% off our favorites for fall," this is a low-value transactional ask. It's fine for list growth, but a subscriber has little reason to open it or act.

## What's working

- The headline is large and blunt, and it states the exchange (text sign-up for 500 adiClub points) at a glance.
- There is one clear black CTA with an arrow, and no competing links.
- The hero is warm and on-brand: the 3-Stripes styling shows through on a diverse group, and the dog adds personality.
- The asterisked fine print is visible and sits right below the copy, not buried in the footer.
- Layout is clean and uncluttered, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- "Hi ," renders with an empty name, which looks like a personalization failure.
- The 500 points have no tangible value. There is no "$X in rewards" and no example of what they buy.
- The hero has no tie to the offer: no phone, no points badge, no text-message visual.
- The top line reads "For best results, view email online," which wastes the preheader slot.
- The copy is a plain block with no bullets or bold, and the "new text sign-ups only" restriction is easy to miss.
- There is no urgency or deadline, so nothing prompts action today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a value anchor in the hero or headline, such as "500 points = $5 off your next order." Points without a dollar translation don't motivate.
- 2. Fix the blank "Hi ," by falling back to "Hi there," or dropping the greeting.
- 3. Put a points badge and a phone visual on the hero, or replace the hero with a graphic that shows the exchange.
- 4. Add a real preheader so the "view email online" line stops showing in the inbox preview.
- 5. Break the copy into two or three short lines with the bonus bolded, and show the new-sign-ups-only condition up front.
- 6. Make the CTA specific, for example "Text me 500 points."
- **Subject Alt A:** `500 adiClub points, just for texting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your phone number is worth 500 points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Opt in to adidas texts and the points land in your account within 5 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Be first to drops and exclusive offers, and get rewarded for it.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "SIGN UP FOR TEXT MESSAGES, EARN 500 POINTS" (adidas)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound, with a table-based layout, a hidden preheader and consistent click tracking. The deliverability and accessibility gaps are a 0-char plain-text part, missing image alt text and an `http://` open pixel. The 20 "doubled-domain" QA failures are false positives. The "Hi ," greeting failure is reported by QA but I could not confirm it in the truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Doubled-domain QA failures are false positives.** Every flagged URL has the form `https://click.comms.adidas.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.adidas.com%2F...`. This is the ESP's standard click-wrapper: the tracker domain followed by the URL-encoded destination. It is not a merge error, so these failures should not count against the email. The QA rule should be fixed to ignore encoded destinations after `/CL0/`.
- **Open pixel uses `http://`.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a127371a7d-95bc23e6-...` is the tracking pixel (flagged as "missing alt" and "uses http://"). Mail clients that block mixed content may drop it, which would undercount opens.
- **Duplicate destinations with different click IDs.** The hero URL `/us/go/signup/phonenumber_text` appears under four placements: HeroImage (`/1/` and `/2/`), HeroCTA1 (`/1/` and `/2/`) and HeroCTA1Icon. This is expected for separate image and text links and is not a defect.
- **No broken links confirmed.** 16 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are redirect domains, so none were validated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** QA flags:
  - `l2clptetxrt7ax9lgabs.png` (the header logo, linked to `adidas.com/us`)
  - `hic1xz4qube7jvarpmoe.png`
  - `yinhvkknpbvmeav6ykqx.png`
  - `upsysvjqqehperxqygf9.png`
  - the CI0 pixel

  Because the images are linked, there is no accessible name for the links either. The link text surfaces as `[image: ...png]`, which is what screen readers and images-off clients get. The sign-up CTA is in the hero image, so images-off users lose it. `SIGN UP HERE` also exists as live text, which partly mitigates this.
- **Web fonts have fallbacks.** The `@font-face` rules use `mso-font-alt: Arial`. The source also includes `lang="en-US"`, `dir="ltr"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and a viewport meta tag. No issues found there.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (see §5).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting slot (QA, unverified).** The check reports `Greeting has empty name field (e.g. "Hi ,")`. The truncated HTML doesn't include the greeting, so I can't confirm it. If real, it is a merge fallback failure. It may also be an artifact of the test inbox having no first name on file.
- **Per-recipient token in the URL.** The signup URL carries `token=UuhWrrAo%252F...`, a double-encoded recipient token, and the `cm_mmca4` UUID. Both are populated and well formed, so this is working personalization.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text alternative missing (FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign, but nothing here confirms alignment.
- **In-body unsubscribe link not confirmed.** The footer links visible in QA are My Account, Privacy Statement, Support and `adidas.com/us`. No unsubscribe link appears in that list, and the HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm one exists. The physical postal address is also not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Naming is consistent and parseable.** `cm_mmc=AdiEmail_SNL-_-Other-_-20261010_Other_October_None-_-Marketing-_-HeroCTA1-_-...` follows one convention across all links, and the `HeroCTA1` / `HeroImage` placement tags allow per-element attribution. The campaign ID is `c37bc5b5-ec59-4834-9bcd-06e2551bde80`.
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** The email uses adidas's `cm_mmc*` and AppsFlyer (`af_*`, `pid=email`, `is_retargeting=true`) parameters instead. GA-based tooling won't attribute these unless adidas maps them. This is a configuration choice, not an error.
- **Landing page matches the offer.** The subject says to sign up for texts, and the CTA goes to `/us/go/signup/phonenumber_text`. Header and footer links go to `/us`, `/us/account-login`, `/us/help` and the privacy policy.
- **Footer links carry the campaign parameters.** Privacy, Support and Account links all include the full `cm_mmc` / `af_*` string. This is harmless, but it adds URL length and attributes footer clicks as marketing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** Generate it from the HTML, with the signup URL, an unsubscribe link and the address.
2. **Add `alt` text to all content images.** Use `alt="adidas"` for the logo and descriptive alt text for the hero (for example, "Sign up for text messages, earn 500 points"). Use `alt=""` on the open pixel.
3. **Serve the open pixel over HTTPS.** Change `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` to `https://`.
4. **Check the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, since the relay may have hidden them.
5. **Check the greeting fallback.** Confirm the first-name merge has a fallback (for example, "Hi there,") and that the "Hi ," result isn't a real send.
6. **Fix the QA rule.** Skip the doubled-domain check for URLs after `/CL0/` where the embedded domain is the encoded destination. This removes 20 false failures.
