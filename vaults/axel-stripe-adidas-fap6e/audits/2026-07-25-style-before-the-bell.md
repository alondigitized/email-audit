# Style before the bell
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth back-to-school-adjacent adidas send in six days, and it's the thinnest of the batch. "FIRST DAY FITS" leans on lifestyle mood — three teens, a bike, a brick schoolyard backdrop — rather than any concrete offer, price point, or specific product callout. Compare this to July 20's "$80 and under" send, which had a real hook; this one asks you to "shop tracksuits, sneakers and more" with zero specifics, no price, no percentage off, nothing that differentiates it from a generic lookbook.
- The creative itself is well shot — good casting, good styling, the denim-and-red-jacket combo pops — but the email gives you nowhere to click except "SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN," which are just category dumps, not a curated destination. This reads like a placeholder brand-awareness email between the bigger campaign beats, not a send built to convert.
- Judgement: a competent lifestyle image carrying a hollow offer. It'll get some opens off brand recognition and BTS timing, but click-through is weak because there's no product to land on.

## What's working

- Clean top nav bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers" + SHOP NOW) gives an immediate, low-friction CTA above the fold.
- Hero image is well art-directed — diverse cast, authentic styling (track jacket, cargo shorts, bike), consistent with the "back to school" beat from the last two sends.
- "FIRST DAY FITS" is a tight, on-theme headline that ties BTS timing to the product without being corny.
- Two clear gender-segmented CTAs (SHOP MEN / SHOP WOMEN) with consistent button styling and directional arrows.
- No render bugs — everything is crisp, aligned, and loads cleanly.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer anywhere in the body — no %, no $ threshold, no BOGO. Every other recent adidas send (BTS $80-and-under, World Cup) had a sharper hook.
- Preview text ("Denim, comfy sets, sneakers and more.") just lists categories instead of teasing a reason to click.
- CTAs dump into broad "Men" / "Women" category pages, not a curated BTS shop or a hero product — that's friction for someone who wants to act on the "first day fits" idea right now.
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or loyalty/member pricing visible despite three teens/kids clearly being the target demo — no adiClub tie-in like the July 18 send had.
- This is the third-plus BTS beat in under a week with no escalation — no new discount, no new product drop, just a re-skinned lifestyle shot, which risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real, visible offer in the hero band or headline — even "Back to school, up to 30% off" would instantly lift both open and click scores; right now there's nothing to anchor urgency to.
- 2. Replace "SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN" with a curated "SHOP FIRST DAY FITS" CTA linking to a specific BTS collection page, not a full category browse.
- 3. Swap the preview text for something that complements rather than restates the subject — right now subject and preview both just gesture at "clothes for school."
- 4. Pull in an adiClub loyalty callout given the recurring member-benefit thread in this campaign (July 18 send used it well).
- 5. If there's no discount to offer, at least name a specific product (e.g., the Samba Metallic or ADIZERO Evo SL ZIP from recent sends) to give the CTA something concrete to land on.

- **Subject Alt B:** `First day fits, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tracksuits, denim and sneakers picked for the first bell`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals for the first day back`

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders, or unresolved template variables (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) were present in the visible/truncated source. No issues found — note that the source was truncated, so this covers only the captured portion.

- **Authentication-Results header:** not found via the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture. Given the sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, this should be verified directly against the originating MTA rather than assumed.
- No footer-based/body unsubscribe link content was included in the truncated HTML to confirm CAN-SPAM body-level compliance (physical address, opt-out link) — flagged as unverified, not as a failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params)
The truncated HTML did not expose resolved destination URLs (links use `click.comms.adidas.com` tracking redirects), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the available source. Recommend capturing full/unwrapped link destinations for this check.
