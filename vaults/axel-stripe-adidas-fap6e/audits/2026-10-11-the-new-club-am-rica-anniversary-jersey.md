# The new Club América anniversary jersey
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a club-heritage send with two messages stacked together: the Club América 110th-anniversary jersey, and a quiz giveaway. The hero is strong. Three models in cream and navy Originals pieces stand against fireworks, under a big "CLUB AMÉRICA" wordmark. It looks celebratory and on-brand. The problem is that the email is named for a jersey and never shows one. The models wear a tee, a track jacket and a hoodie, and the only other image is a flat crest on a blue square.
- The giveaway ($500 gift card plus product) is the only real hook, and it sits in the bottom half behind a crest graphic. A black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" bar above the hero adds a third message and pulls attention away from the point. The email is polished but vague. There is no price, no product shot and no deadline, so a fan has little reason to click now. Compare the 10/6 "Up to 50% off our favorites for fall" send, which had a clear offer. This one is pure brand storytelling.

## What's working

- The hero is atmospheric and cohesive. The cream, navy and fireworks palette fits an anniversary and holds the eye.
- The "CLUB AMÉRICA" wordmark over the image makes the topic obvious at a glance.
- The subject, headline and body copy tell one consistent anniversary story.
- Both CTAs are solid black buttons with arrows, so they are easy to find and tap.
- The giveaway ($500 gift card) gives a reason to engage that doesn't depend on a discount.

## What's weak

- No jersey appears anywhere. The email promotes "the new jersey" but shows a tee, a track jacket, a hoodie and a crest.
- The crest panel is a huge, low-information block. It pushes the giveaway far down and adds scroll without adding product.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" bar is off-topic. It also looks misaligned, sticking out wider than the hero below it.
- The two sections don't line up. The giveaway headline and body are indented differently from the jersey headline and body, and the gray body text is lighter.
- Both CTAs are weak. "SHOP NOW" is generic, and there is no price, no sizing or availability, and no deadline for the quiz.
- The preheader text sits as a small underlined link at the top, which looks unstyled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the jersey in the hero, or directly under it. Use a clean front-and-back product shot with the price. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the crest block to a small badge. Move the giveaway up or make it a compact band, and add an end date.
- 3. Remove the best-sellers bar, or move it to the footer. One email should carry one job.
- 4. Sharpen the CTA copy: "Shop the jersey" and "Enter to win $500".
- 5. Align the two text blocks to one grid and one text color.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Club América's 110th jersey is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Win $500 + the Club América 110 kit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the anniversary jersey, then take the quiz to enter the giveaway.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Quiz entry = a shot at a $500 gift card and the full collection.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based, responsive build with media queries and `lang="en"`. The QA run flagged one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several image and header warnings. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking, not verified:** 15 links go through tracking or redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com/...`), and the probe skipped them. Redirect resolution, destination status and final URLs are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a129925107-0c109dab-.../9W557JBLqT90ES1_YKYSoNZ_3-s2vwf1EIPMdkvP6kQ=452` loads over `http://`. This is most likely the open-tracking image. Some clients block it or warn about mixed content, so open-rate data will be incomplete.
- **Insecure image asset:** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` ("arrow") uses `http://` and appears twice. The same asset host presumably serves HTTPS, so this is a template fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/8c3ca7a7-fe71-43f3-bb4d-51e02dec7f3d?cp_tp=...` has no `alt`. If it is a content image, it renders as a blank block with images off and a screen reader announces the filename.
- **Tracking pixel alt:** The `click.comms.adidas.com` pixel also lacks alt text. Use `alt=""` with explicit `width="1" height="1"`, since a pixel is decorative.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale to device width, though the `max-width:639px` media queries mostly mitigate it.
- **Redundant CSS:** `@font-face` for AdihausDIN and AdineuePRO is declared twice (the "Yahoo Android App" block repeats the first). Both use only `local('Arial'|'Helvetica'...)` sources, so no custom font loads. This adds weight but breaks nothing.
- **Global paragraph style:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` applies to every paragraph and could override left-aligned copy unless inline styles counter it.
- **Alt text on "arrow":** This is fine if the image is decorative and `alt=""` is intended. The QA output doesn't show the actual alt value, so I can't confirm.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated portion. The remainder was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text alternative:** The text part is 0 chars. A missing text/plain part in a multipart/alternative message raises spam-filter scores and breaks text-only clients. If AgentMail only captured the HTML part, this could be a capture artifact. Verify against the raw MIME.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the original headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `adidas@us-news.comms.adidas.com`; alignment with `adidas.com` was not tested.
- **Footer:** The footer was truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found that can be confirmed. Destination URLs are hidden behind click-tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment (Club América anniversary jersey) cannot be assessed. The `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` tag suppresses the referrer in the web view, which limits attribution there.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part. If it is missing, generate one.
2. Check the original headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment, bypassing the relay if needed.
3. Serve `image.link.adidas.com/...b0c011a2...jpg` and the `click.comms.adidas.com` pixel over HTTPS.
4. Add `alt` to the `dv.adidas.com` image (descriptive if it is content, `alt=""` if decorative). Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
6. Follow the 15 tracking redirects with a real HTTP client to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
7. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and look for unrendered tokens.
