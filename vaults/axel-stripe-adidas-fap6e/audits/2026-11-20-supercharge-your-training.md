# Supercharge your training
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a calm, editorial send that carries no discount. The Runner's World "Shoe Awards" badge on the Hyperboost Edge hero is the strongest asset, and it is credible third-party proof. After the Black Friday countdown banners in the 2026-11-14 through 2026-11-18 sends, this one drops the offer entirely. That is a refreshing change, but it leaves the email with nothing to act on this week.
- The email splits its attention. The top half sells a running shoe and the bottom half is an F1 team bag giveaway for adiClub members. The subject "Supercharge your training" promises a training story, and only the first half delivers it. The Mercedes-AMG hero is the better-looking image, and it pulls the eye away from the product.
- Verdict: a decent product-story send with good craft. It is weak on offer and urgency, and the two-topic structure hurts it. I'd call it a mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The Runner's World 2026 Shoe Awards badge gives real social proof on the hero.
- The two-panel hero (athlete in motion plus shoe close-up) shows the product clearly.
- The Hyperboost Edge copy is specific: "first adidas non-plated super trainer," Hyperboost Pro foam, heel-to-toe cushioning.
- The black CTA buttons are high-contrast and easy to find.
- The F1 giveaway image is striking and on-brand for the adiClub audience.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or member benefit anywhere in the Hyperboost section.
- Two stacked CTAs ("Shop Hyperboost" and "Shop Marathon") compete, and "Shop Marathon" is unexplained.
- The top banner, "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / Shop Now," sends people to generic best sellers above a running-shoe hero.
- The giveaway is a second, unrelated story. It has no deadline and no entry mechanics, and a giveaway for one prize is a weak click reason.
- The preheader at the top is an underlined text link. It reads as a stray line and repeats the Runner's World claim.

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with Hyperboost Edge and drop the giveaway to a slim footer strip, or send the giveaway as its own email.
- 2. Add a price and a member perk to the shoe hero, for example "$X, free shipping for adiClub members."
- 3. Cut the generic best-sellers banner or replace it with a link to the Edge product page.
- 4. Collapse the two CTAs into one ("Shop Hyperboost Edge") and send the marathon audience a separate email.
- 5. Add an entry deadline to the giveaway.
