# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is very clearly adidas leaning into Samba mania — huge hero type, tight product macro shot of the three stripes, "SHOP THE LATEST DROPS" teasing SPEZIAL and an Anna Hall drop underneath. It reads like a brand that knows exactly who's landing on this page. Only thing standing between me and the goods right now is a cookie consent wall parked dead center.

## What's working

- The Samba hero — massive close-up of the stripes and toe box is exactly the kind of shot that stops my thumb, this is my most-worn silhouette
- "SHOP THE LATEST DROPS" rail teasing SPEZIAL and an Anna Hall collab — that's the "what's hot" signal I actually look for before a CONFIRMED drop
- "FREE STANDARD SHIPPING WITH ADICLUB" pinned in the top bar — I'm already adiClub, so that's a frictionless nudge
- "Save up to 40% on tons of styles for fall" banner is a real, specific number, not vague "Sale" copy

## What's weak

- The cookie modal is sitting right on top of the hero, blocking half the "SAMBA" wordmark and the supporting copy — first thing I see isn't product, it's a consent form
- No human model or lifestyle shot in the hero, just product macro — fine for the drop itself but doesn't sell the fit/styling angle
- No countdown or deadline on the "up to 40%" sale, so there's zero urgency pushing me to act today vs. next week
- Nav has SALE and SPORTS but no direct "Originals" or "Samba/Gazelle" quick link — I still have to hunt

## Recommendations

- Move the cookie banner to a slim bottom strip instead of a centered modal — let the Samba hero breathe on first load
- Add a deadline chip to the "up to 40%" banner ("Ends Sunday") to create real urgency for a guy who tracks drop windows
- Surface an "Originals" quick-link in primary nav since that's where Samba/Gazelle/Spezial actually live
- Show adiClub member pricing or points balance inline in the top bar since I'm already logged in (account icon shows a "1")
