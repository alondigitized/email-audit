# Top shoes $80 and under for the new school year
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a slim, single-hero email built around one clear price point — "$80 and under school shoes" — with a strong, well-shot lifestyle image of three classic sneakers on concrete steps. It's the cleanest concept adidas has run in this brand history: no gendered promo confusion, no mismatched subject/body like "The only bag you'll need all year" or "Own the basketball court and the classroom." The price threshold is the whole pitch, and the hero image backs it up visually with recognizable, wearable product.
- Where it falls short is depth: there's no actual product grid, no prices shown on the specific shoes pictured, and no urgency or deadline. It's essentially a single banner ad stretched into an email — attractive, on-brand, but thin compared to 2026-09-10's "Fall Kickoff Sale" which had four real products with prices, discounts, and ratings visible.
- Solid, on-theme creative that undersells itself by not showing the receipts (actual prices, specific SKUs) behind the headline claim.

## What's working

- Single, clear value prop in the headline — "$80 AND UNDER SCHOOL SHOES" is unambiguous and specific.
- Hero image is genuinely appealing — three distinct silhouettes (Sambas/Gazelle-style) styled naturally on steps, good lighting, no render bugs.
- Back-to-school seasonal relevance is clear and timely for September.
- Two clean, high-contrast CTAs ("SHOP MEN" / "SHOP WOMEN") give an unambiguous next step.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- No prices are shown anywhere despite the entire hook being a price threshold — "$80 and under" is asserted, not demonstrated.
- No actual product tiles/grid — just one lifestyle photo and text, so there's nothing to click into except a generic category page.
- No urgency or time-bound element (no "ends Sunday," no countdown).
- The top-of-email utility bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers" / SHOP NOW) is a redundant, competing CTA that dilutes the single hero message below it.
- No loyalty/adiClub tie-in, unlike the 2026-09-12 "Shoe of the month giveaway" send earlier the same day — a missed cross-sell moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add 2-4 actual product tiles under the headline showing shoe name + price (e.g., "Samba OG — $70") so the $80-and-under claim is substantiated and clickable at the SKU level, not just the category level.
- 2. Drop or merge the top utility bar into the main CTA — having "SHOP NOW" at the top and "SHOP MEN/WOMEN" at the bottom splits attention with no added value.
- 3. Add a light urgency cue (e.g., "While sizes last" or a sale end date) since back-to-school shopping is time-sensitive by nature.
- 4. Tie in adiClub points or member pricing if eligible, consistent with the loyalty angle used in same-day sends.
