# New adidas | Care Bears collection
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a licensed-collab launch email with a Black Friday member countdown stuck on top. The Care Bears hero is the strongest asset. A pastel gradient, a blue Superstar with a bear perched on it, and a cloud with a heart all make it feel playful and gift-worthy. The black banner above it ("BLACK FRIDAY FIRST ACCESS / Up to 60% Off for Members", 03:03:43:26) is the only real offer, but nothing in the email connects it to this collection.
- The problem is that the offer and the product don't match. The collection is mostly toddler and kids' sets and two Adistar sneakers. Only one item shows a discount ($52, down from $65, -20%). That's nowhere near "up to 60%," so a member who clicks expecting deep savings finds full price on three of the four products. Compared with the 2026-11-14 send ("adiClub members get up to 60% off for Black Friday early", 7/10), which committed to the offer, this one is a new-arrival announcement with a sale banner attached.
- Verdict: a pretty launch email with a mismatched promise. It's fine for fans of the collab, but it won't move a general shopper. I'd put it around 5/10.

## What's working

- The hero is eye-catching: a gradient, a hero Superstar and the Care Bears art give it clear novelty.
- The "ADIDAS | CARE BEARS" headline and the single black "SHOP NOW" button are clean and unambiguous.
- The product grid shows price, star rating and product name, which gives the reader useful detail.
- The countdown timer adds urgency, and the black banner draws the eye.
- The first-line preheader ("Full of bold color and feel-good comfort.") is real copy, not junk.

## What's weak

- The "up to 60% off" banner doesn't match the collection. Three of the four products are full price and the fourth is only 20% off.
- The audience is unclear. The apparel shown is infant and toddler sizing, but nothing says so, and the sneakers have no size cue.
- "SHOP NOW" is generic, and one button points to the whole collection rather than to a specific product.
- The grid is uneven. The two top images have different heights and the price lines don't align, so the layout looks slightly off.
- The "prime options available" badges are small and add clutter without a clear benefit.
- The preheader appears at the top in plain underlined text, which reads like a rendering artifact.

## Recommendations

- 1. Decide what the email is. Either lead with the collab and drop the sale banner, or show the member discount on the collection items. If the discount applies, show the price with the savings on every tile.
- 2. Label the sizing ("Baby & toddler sets," "Kids' sneakers") so the right parent knows it's for them.
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Shop Care Bears sets."
- 4. Put the strongest product (the pink crew set or the Adistar) in the hero position, linked directly to it.
- 5. Align the grid tiles so image heights and price baselines match.
