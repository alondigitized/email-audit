# The Legend Returns. The Legacy Continues.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely strong storytelling email — Billie Jean King, in the flesh, holding up the blue suede Campus/Gazelle silhouette that bears her name, is the best "athlete meets product" hero shot in adidas's recent run, edging out even the Messi jersey send from 2026-08-20. The legacy narrative ("A Legend Returns... a tribute to a legacy that continues to inspire generations") is specific and earns its emotional weight instead of being generic filler. The tennis-ball flat-lay of the actual shoe is a nice detail shot that ties the product back to King's sport.
- Where it falls apart is structure: this is really two unrelated emails stapled together. Halfway down, the Billie Jean King narrative just stops and a second campaign — a World Cup 26 signed match ball sweepstakes for adiClub/FIFA Rewards members — appears with zero transition. That's a jarring context switch that dilutes both stories and forces a reader who cares about one to scroll past the other.
- Judgement: strong creative, weak editorial discipline. This should have been two sends.

## What's working

- Billie Jean King as the hero is a real athlete, not a generic model, and she's holding the actual product — direct, authentic tie-in.
- Copy is specific and grounded ("Billie Jean King's iconic blue suede shoe," not vague "new drop" language).
- The tennis-ball flat-lay product shot is a smart secondary image that reinforces the tennis heritage story.
- Two clear, high-contrast CTA buttons ("SHOP NOW," "FIND STORE NOW") sit right under the narrative copy.
- No render bugs — clean typography, no overlapping elements, no broken images.

## What's weak

- Two unrelated campaigns (shoe launch + World Cup sweepstakes) are crammed into one email with no visual break or section header — reads like two emails pasted together.
- The sweepstakes section gates entry behind "adiClub & FIFA Rewards connected members" but never states what a non-member should do — dead end for anyone not already enrolled.
- Preheader text is generic browser boilerplate underline styling (looks like an unstyled link), which is a minor but visible polish miss.
- The top banner ("Explore the latest adidas bestsellers") is a third, disconnected CTA competing with the hero story before the reader even gets to Billie Jean King.
- No price, discount, or release date visible anywhere — pure brand storytelling with no purchase urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split into two sends.** The Billie Jean King shoe story and the World Cup ball sweepstakes deserve separate emails — combining them cuts the impact of both and confuses the primary CTA.
- 2. **Drop or relocate the top bestsellers banner.** It competes with the hero narrative for the first thing a reader sees; move it to the footer or cut it entirely so Billie Jean King is the very first thing in view.
- 3. **Add a release/availability detail near the CTA** — a date, "available now," or "limited pairs" — to convert emotional interest into urgency.
- 4. **For the sweepstakes block, add a one-line "not a member? Join adiClub free" fallback** so non-members aren't dead-ended.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The shoe that started it all, reissued`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A tribute to a tennis icon — shop the reissue before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited pairs, available online and in select stores`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via legacy table/media-query architecture with generally sound responsive scaffolding, but fails deliverability and compliance checks: no plain-text alternative, missing unsubscribe headers, and several HTTP (non-HTTPS) asset/tracking links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 23 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.comms.adidas.com` domain) were skipped by automated HTTP probing — status/validity unconfirmed, not evidence of failure.
- One tracking pixel/image served over **plain HTTP**: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a036dd2b12-4560f9dc-f4c0-4652-8d2f-8c5b5dbbbf15-000000/frp8dW5Iwt1X48hVGR3qu_vPu...` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), which would silently break open/click tracking for this element.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Decorative "arrow" image served over HTTP (not HTTPS), repeated 5x in the QA log: `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` — likely to be blocked/broken in HTTPS-only mail clients.
- Two images missing `alt` text:
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/3e335488-3c8c-407b-b530-d3ba96d8f222?cp_tp=...`
  - `frp8dW5Iwt1X48hVGR3qu_vPumXXgX2fXPegav46oTA=452` (the same HTTP tracking pixel noted above)
  Missing alt text degrades screen-reader accessibility and leaves no fallback text if images are blocked by default (common in most mail clients).
- Markup targets legacy Outlook/Yahoo/mobile-webkit rendering (HTML4 transitional doctype, `mso-font-alt`, ExternalClass resets, tiered media queries at 639/640/480/414/320px) — consistent with standard ESP template conventions, no structural defects observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens found in the visible source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules; may be present on the raw MIME message but not captured by the relay, or genuinely absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — without this, one-click (POST-based) unsubscribe cannot function even if a List-Unsubscribe header exists.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay; recommend confirming authentication independently before treating as a failure.
- Sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, a subdomain distinct from the visible display name "adidas" — not itself a compliance issue, but worth confirming alignment with DMARC policy on the root `adidas.com` domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Destination/landing URLs were not resolvable — all outbound links route through the `click.comms.adidas.com` tracking/redirect domain and were skipped during automated probing (23 links), so final landing-page URLs and UTM parameters could not be verified in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all image and tracking-pixel assets over HTTPS — replace the `http://image.link.adidas.com/...` arrow asset and the `http://click.comms.adidas.com/...` tracking pixel with HTTPS equivalents to prevent client-side blocking.
2. Add `alt` text to the two flagged images for accessibility and image-blocked fallback rendering.
3. Add a plain-text MIME alternative (currently 0 chars) — required by CAN-SPAM and improves deliverability/spam scoring.
4. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to enable one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058), required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender guidelines.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly at the sending MTA, since the relay did not expose an `Authentication-Results` header for verification.
6. Resolve final landing URLs behind the `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects to confirm UTM parameters and destination-page alignment.
