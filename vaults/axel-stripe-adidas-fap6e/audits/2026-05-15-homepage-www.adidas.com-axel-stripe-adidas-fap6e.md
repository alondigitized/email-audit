# Homepage snapshot · www.adidas.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Bro. I opened adidas dot com on my phone and got locked out like I'm a bot trying to scalp Sambas. What I'm looking at is a full-page block screen — no hero, no product, no CTA — just the trefoil logo, a cryptic reference error code, and an all-caps message telling me I'm not welcome. This isn't a brand experience, it's a bouncer turning me away at the door. Massive fumble for a brand that already has my loyalty.

## What's working

- **Logo rendering:** The three-stripe trefoil and wordmark are clean and bold. At least the brand ID is intact at the top — that's something.
- **Typography is on-brand:** The all-caps blocked headline uses adidas's signature heavy sans-serif. If this were actual content, the type treatment would feel right.

## What's weak

- **The entire page is an access-denied wall:** `Reference Error: 0.4cf10f17.1778868876.13ae328` — this means nothing to me as a shopper and communicates nothing useful.
- **No recovery path visible:** The block screen says "A security issue was automatically identified" but gives me no button, no link, no "try again" CTA, no support contact above the fold.
- **Zero product presence:** I came here to check if the new Samba colorway dropped. There is literally nothing to see.
- **The error is a bounce trigger, full stop:** Any engagement or conversion likelihood collapses to zero the moment I see this screen.
- **"What could have caused this?" is the last visible copy:** That heading — partially cut off at the bottom — reads like a FAQ I have to scroll to read, which is the wrong UX for a frustrated shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Implement a friendlier CAPTCHA or soft verification flow** instead of a hard block — something like a checkbox challenge or a "tap to confirm you're human" interstitial so real shoppers like me aren't permanently bounced.
- **Add a fallback CTA to the block page:** "Having trouble? Open in a new browser" or a direct link to the adidas app — keep me in the funnel even if the web is blocked.
- **Surface a support contact or reference code explainer:** Right now the reference error `0.4cf10f17.1778868876.13ae328` is meaningless. A one-liner like "If you think this is a mistake, contact support with code [X]" preserves trust.
- **Consider showing a static product image and copy even on the block page:** Even a non-interactive hero with "New Sambas dropping now" keeps the brand moment alive while the security gate resolves.
