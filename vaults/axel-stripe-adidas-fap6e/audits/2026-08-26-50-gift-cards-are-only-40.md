# $50 gift cards are only $40
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward gift-card promo, and it's the most transactionally clear offer adidas has sent in this run — no vague "up to 40% off" like 2026-08-21's Summer Essentials Sale, just a plain, honest math statement: $50 card for $40. The jersey-texture hero (green/white 3-Stripes fabric) reads clean and on-brand even without a specific shoe or athlete anchoring it.
- The back half of the email is a bolt-on: a signed World Cup ball sweepstakes for adiClub/FIFA Rewards members, unrelated to the gift-card offer above it, dragging focus in two directions in one send. Compared to the Billie Jean King (2026-08-25) or Messi (2026-08-20) hero emails, this one has no athlete tied to the actual offer — the soccer player photo belongs to the sweepstakes module, not the gift card. It's honest and functional but forgettable.

## What's working

- The core offer is dead simple and stated three separate times (hero banner, headline, body copy) — no ambiguity about what's being sold.
- "SHOP NOW" and "ENTER NOW" are both clear, verb-led CTA buttons with visible black contrast blocks.
- Fine print discloses real constraints (dates, 4-card limit, no combining) without burying the core deal.
- No render bugs — clean image loads, readable type, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Top-of-email unsubscribe-style link ("Take home more savings with adidas gift cards") sits above the logo, an odd, junk-like element in that position.
- Two unrelated offers (gift card discount + World Cup ball sweepstakes) compete for attention in one email with no visual separation or "also" framing.
- No urgency/countdown visible in the hero itself — the expiration date only shows up in dense legal fine print at the very bottom.
- The soccer player photo is a generic pink-background stock-style shot, not a named athlete, weaker than the brand's recent Messi/Rodman/BJK hero choices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split into two focused sends (or lead hard with one and footnote the other) — a gift-card discount and a signed-ball sweepstakes serve different buying intents and diluting both.
- 2. Move the expiration date ("through Aug 28") into the hero or subhead as a countdown — "3 days left" beats it being locked in the legal block.
- 3. Clean up the top preheader-style link — it reads like unsubscribe junk sitting above the logo.
- 4. If keeping the sweepstakes module, cut a still frame of the actual signed ball/Yamal instead of a generic posed shot, so it reads as the actual prize.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy a $50 gift card for $40 — ends Aug 28`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 discount on adidas gift cards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack savings before the school-year shoe run.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 days left: 20% off adidas gift cards.`
