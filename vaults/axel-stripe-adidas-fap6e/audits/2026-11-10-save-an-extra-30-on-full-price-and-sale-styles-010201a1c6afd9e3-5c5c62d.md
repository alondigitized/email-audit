# Save an extra 30% on full price and sale styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain, single-offer sale send: an extra 30% off with code COZY. The offer is clear and the sender is recognizable. The execution is thin. The hero is a dark, cropped three-stripe image with "THE HOLIDAY SALE" in serif type, and it shows no product at all. Under it is a big "30% OFF HOLIDAY SAVINGS" headline and three black gender buttons.
- The email undercuts itself in two ways. A black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner sits above the hero and competes with the real offer. The exclusions paragraph then removes the products this brand is known for: Gazelle, Samba, Campus, SL 72, Spezial. Those are the hero sneakers of the last two weeks' sends. This is the 3rd sale-flavored send in about 10 days. Compared with the Nov 5 "Week of Deals last call" email, which had a countdown and a product hero, this one has less urgency and less to look at.
- Verdict: a functional but bland promo. The discount is real, but nothing in the creative shows what it applies to.

## What's working

- The offer is specific and easy to find: "30% OFF HOLIDAY SAVINGS" is the largest text below the hero, with the code COZY in the body copy.
- The brand is recognized immediately from the logo and the three-stripe motif.
- The three large black buttons (Men, Women, Kids) are easy to tap, and the arrow icons make them look clickable.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The "ends Nov 11" window is stated, so the deadline is credible.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product, so it doesn't show what the 30% applies to. It's an atmospheric crop that reads as texture.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner above the hero is a second CTA that competes with the sale message.
- The exclusions list removes Gazelle, Samba, Campus, SL 72 and Spezial, the models adidas has been pushing. Subscribers who clicked those earlier sends will feel baited.
- The code COZY is buried in body copy instead of shown as a copy-ready element, and the buttons don't say the code applies.
- The deadline is only in the 8-point-style fine print, so the email shows no urgency up top. The preheader "Start your holiday shopping early." also undersells it.
- The 30% headline is nearly the same size as the hero text, so the hierarchy is a bit flat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with real product, for example a hoodie, a track jacket or a Soft Lux piece that is actually eligible, with a "30% OFF · CODE COZY" overlay. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the code in a bordered box and add a deadline line such as "Ends Nov 11".
- 3. Remove the best-sellers banner or move it below the main CTAs.
- 4. Show 4 to 6 eligible products with sale prices so the offer feels tangible. Mention Ultraboost and Stan Smith carefully, since only select styles qualify.
- 5. Say up front which famous models are excluded, rather than only in the fine print.
- 6. Make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop men's with COZY".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Code COZY: extra 30% off through Nov 11`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 30% off ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use COZY at checkout on hoodies, jackets, and more. Ends Nov 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full price and sale styles, one code. Exclusions apply.`
