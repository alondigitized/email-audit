# adiClub members get up to 60% off for Black Friday early
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain, honest member-access send. The hero says "adiClub / BLACK FRIDAY / UP TO 60% OFF" in white on red, and the eye lands on the offer first. It is a stronger and clearer offer than the 2026-11-10 "Save an extra 30%" send, which needed a code. It is also the first recent adidas email with real member-exclusive framing.
- The body gives up the momentum, though. "Up to 60%" has no hero product, no deadline and no price proof, and the only CTAs are three identical black Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons. The copy also asks the member to "Log in now" before shopping, which adds friction right at the click. Between the two gifting sends on 2026-11-12 and the Samba and sale sends before that, this is the fourth or fifth promo in about a week, so it needs a sharper hook to stand out.
- Judgement: a decent 6-7 on offer clarity, held back by missing urgency and no product. It reads as an announcement, not a reason to click today.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and legible: "UP TO 60% OFF" is readable at a glance, and "adiClub" sits right above it.
- Member exclusivity is explicit, with "adiClub" in the hero and "BLACK FRIDAY FIRST ACCESS" as the headline. That gives a reason to feel special.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with one hero, one headline, one short paragraph and three buttons. No render bugs are visible.
- The buttons are high-contrast black with white text and arrows, and the copy is a verb plus a noun.
- The preheader, "Score the season's best gifts for less.", adds the gifting angle instead of repeating the subject.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline, end date or countdown. "First access" implies a window, but the email never says when it opens or closes.
- No products are shown, so "up to 60%" is an abstraction. The hero is a close-up of fabric with no shoe, apparel or price.
- The three CTAs are equal-weight, split by gender, and all go to category pages. Nothing steers a shopper toward the best deals.
- "Log in now to shop" adds friction, and "up to 60%" gives no indication of what is actually discounted.
- The hero image sits left-aligned and stops short of the right edge, leaving dead white space. The headline and body text are left-aligned and feel sparse under a very loud banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one or two hero products with before/after prices (for example, a Samba or Ultraboost at "$X → $Y") under the hero. Proof of a real discount would do more than anything else here.
- 2. Add a real deadline: "Members only: starts [date], ends [date]" in the hero or headline.
- 3. Replace the generic Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons with one primary CTA ("Shop member deals") and keep the gender links as secondary text links. Drop "Log in now", or make it a "Sign in to unlock" button in the hero.
- 4. Fix the subject. "Black Friday early" is clumsy, and 56 characters will truncate on mobile.
- 5. Make the hero full width, and add the member perk such as early access or free shipping.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members first: up to 60% off Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early Black Friday access is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `adiClub-only prices on Sambas, Ultraboost and gifts. Log in to unlock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access ends [date]. Shop top styles before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed deliverability defect, an empty plain-text part (0 chars). It also has several insecure `http://` image and tracking sources and two images without alt text. Auth and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **21 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`), so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking asset.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1d8ab83c7-f5f34a02-ad68-4d41-83c9-031cfcfcb0f7-000000/UNDAZlGtdWYwg9T71fj41U4Rg...` is loaded as an `<img>` over plain HTTP. It is likely the open-tracking pixel. Clients may block it or flag mixed content, which would under-report opens.
- **Wrong sort of host for tracking.** The same `click.comms.adidas.com` host serves both click links and the image, so an HTTP-only pixel is inconsistent with the rest of the setup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing.** [FAIL] The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. Clients and spam filters that prefer or score the text part will see nothing.
- **Six `http://` "arrow" images.** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` appears 6 times. Non-HTTPS sources may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. The same asset is probably reachable over HTTPS on the same host.
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/7a465e05-8e07-4089-855f-1cd407d71fe1?...`
  - the click pixel image `UNDAZlGtdWYwg9T71fj41U4RgHPA3njotGuqmUGasrQ=452`
  
  The first matters most. If it is a content image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. The pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport meta is incomplete.** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. Mobile clients that honor the tag may not size the layout correctly, though the media queries are keyed on `max-width` and may still work.
- **Web fonts don't load.** The `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` only use `local('Arial'/'Helvetica'/...)` sources. They never fetch a font file, so rendering falls back to system fonts. This is harmless but the declarations do nothing.
- **Doctype and language.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional and `<html lang="en">` is set. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`). The tail of the HTML was not visible, so this is not a full-document confirmation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** Both are WARN, and the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a proven sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so the raw headers should be checked on the original message.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`. The relay hides the result, so no pass or fail can be claimed.
- **Footer content not verifiable.** The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 21 links are redirect-wrapped (`click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`) and were skipped, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to 60% off / adiClub" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain alternative.** Fix the 0-char text part (the one [FAIL]).
2. **Serve all images over HTTPS.** Change the six `b0c011a2-...jpg` "arrow" references on `image.link.adidas.com` and the `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` pixel to `https://`.
3. **Fix the missing alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` to the `dv.adidas.com/o/7a465e05-...` image. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta.**
5. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment) directly, bypassing the relay.
6. **Resolve the redirect-wrapped links in a follow-up run.** This covers the destinations and UTM parameters on the 21 links, plus a check of the footer unsubscribe link and address.
