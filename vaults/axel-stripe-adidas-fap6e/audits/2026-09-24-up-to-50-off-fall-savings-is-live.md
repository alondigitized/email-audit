# Up to 50% off fall savings is live 🍂
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-repeat of the 2026-09-19 "Up to 50% off fall savings on your favorites" send (8.00/10) — same pink ribbon "FALL KICKOFF SALE" hero, same layout skeleton, swapped-in product grid. That's fine; sitewide sale creative doesn't need to be reinvented every week, and the earlier version scored well for a reason. But this send is a slightly weaker execution of the same idea: the product grid mixes a lifestyle photo (Mexico EQT Track Top) into what's otherwise a shoe carousel, and Prime-badging on three of four products injects a fulfillment detail that has nothing to do with adidas' brand voice.
- The core mechanics are sound — real percentages (-20% to -60%), star ratings with review counts, three clear category CTAs (Men/Women/Kids), and a bold "UP TO 50% OFF STARTS NOW" headline that lands immediately below the fold-friendly hero. Genuinely strong execution of a proven sitewide-sale template, dinged only by small inconsistencies in the product grid and a top utility banner that competes with the sale hero for attention.

## What's working

- Hero headline "UP TO 50% OFF STARTS NOW" is bold, unambiguous, and impossible to miss.
- Three clear segmented CTAs (Shop Men / Shop Women / Shop Kids) let a shopper self-select instantly.
- Real discount percentages and star ratings with review counts on every product build credibility.
- "No code needed" removes friction that kills conversion on promo emails.

## What's weak

- The Coca-Cola Samba OG collab and Mexico EQT Track Top are odd inclusions in a generic "fall sale" grid — no context on why these specific SKUs were chosen over more obvious bestsellers.
- "Prime options available" badge appears on 3 of 4 products with no explanation — reads like a stray marketplace integration, not adidas voice.
- Top black utility banner ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW") competes with the sale hero directly below it — two competing offers stacked at the very top.
- Preview text ("Get ready for fall with new styles.") undersells the sale and doesn't mention the 50% off number that's in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top black "best sellers" banner entirely — it's a second, weaker CTA competing with the sale hero for the first thing the eye sees. Let the Fall Kickoff Sale hero be the only headline above the fold.
- 2. Drop or explain "Prime options available" — if it's a real fulfillment perk, give it one line of context; otherwise remove it, since it reads as noise against adidas' otherwise clean brand voice.
- 3. Swap the Mexico EQT Track Top lifestyle shot for another shoe product to keep the grid visually consistent (3 sneakers + 1 apparel model shot breaks pattern-matching skim).
- 4. Add explicit fall-related framing to the preview text so the open decision reflects the actual offer, not generic seasonal copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off is live: fall styles from $44`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall restock, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Samba, Adizero, and more — up to 50% off, no code needed`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall Kickoff Sale: prices already slashed on bestsellers`
