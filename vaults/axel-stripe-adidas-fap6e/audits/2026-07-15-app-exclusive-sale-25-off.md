# App exclusive sale: 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, brand-consistent sale email with one serious mechanical flaw: the sole CTA is "DOWNLOAD THE APP," which is the wrong action for the overwhelming majority of the audience — people who are already subscribed adidas customers and almost certainly have the app. The offer itself, 25% off in-app, is undercut by recent history: adidas ran 30% off sitewide on June 30 with code SAVE. A loyal subscriber will notice the regression, and no framing in this email attempts to justify it.
- The visual execution is solid. The dark hero with "APP EXCLUSIVE SALE" and the large "25% OFF IS LIVE" headline read fast and clearly. The 2x adiClub points bonus is a nice layer for members, but it arrives buried in the body copy rather than featured as a headline incentive.
- Bottom line: a well-designed email pointing at the wrong door. If your recipient already has the app, the only CTA leaves them with nothing to tap. Fixing the CTA alone would materially lift clicks.

## What's working

- **Offer is immediately legible.** "25% OFF IS LIVE" dominates the viewport — no scrolling needed to understand what the email is about.
- **App-exclusive framing creates scarcity.** "App exclusive" signals this isn't the same deal available in a browser — there's a reason to act via a specific channel.
- **adiClub 2x points is a smart loyalty layer.** Stacking a points multiplier on top of the discount rewards members who engage; this differentiation from the June 30 sitewide deal is the strongest argument for this send.
- **No visible render bugs.** Layout holds, text is legible, no broken images or overlapping elements.
- **Subject line is tight and specific.** At 28 characters, "App exclusive sale: 25% off" is mobile-safe and unambiguous.

## What's weak

- **"DOWNLOAD THE APP" is the wrong CTA for existing users.** Active subscribers are already app users. This button either does nothing useful (if they have the app) or triggers an App Store redirect mid-shopping intent. The copy should be "OPEN THE APP" or "SHOP THE SALE."
- **No deadline.** "For a limited time" is filler. June 30's send had the same vague framing. A concrete date ("ends Sunday" or even "ends July 18") would add real urgency.
- **25% < 30%.** The June 30 summer clearance ran 30% off sitewide. This is 25% off app-only — narrower channel, lower discount. Nothing in the email acknowledges or reframes this; it just presents the lesser deal as if there's no prior offer to compare against.
- **No products shown.** The email is a discount announcement with zero product imagery. Subscribers who engage with product-first emails (the AE2 launch, the Coca-Cola collab) get nothing to anchor desire.
- **Preheader appears to be leaking opt-out/utility text.** The very top of the email shows small utility text (opt-out link area) that likely bleeds into the inbox preview, wasting the preview slot entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the CTA copy immediately.** Change "DOWNLOAD THE APP" to "OPEN THE APP — SHOP THE SALE" or split into two versions: "DOWNLOAD THE APP" for cold audiences and "SHOP IN-APP NOW" for known app users (segmentable by app-open history). This is the single highest-impact change.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline.** Even if it's 48 hours out, "Ends July 17 at midnight" converts "for a limited time" into action. The July 13 adiClub newsletter had a clear giveaway cutoff — apply the same discipline here.
- 3. **Pull in one featured product.** A single aspirational product image (a shoe, a kit) with "included in sale" copy transforms this from an announcement into a shopping trigger.
- 4. **Lead with the member upside.** "2x adiClub points + 25% off = your best app haul yet" is a stronger frame than presenting the discount as the headline and the points as a footnote.
- 5. **Set a real preheader.** Whatever is leaking at the top needs to be replaced with a dedicated preheader line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members only: 25% off + double points, app only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off ends Sunday — app exclusive`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your points while you save — adiClub members get 2× this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full price and sale styles included. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — adidas "App exclusive sale: 25% off"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a 38% automated pass rate driven by one broken CTA deep-link, a zero-byte plain-text fallback, mixed HTTP/HTTPS image loading, and unverifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers at the relay layer. Core rendering structure is sound.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken deep-link — primary CTA**
The click-redirect at `https://dv.adidas.com/o/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?cp_tp=v3.eJ...` resolves to an inner destination with an invalid URL scheme:
```
/g/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?txn=343a8d00-7ffa-11f1-89bb-9295eb1db778
```
`/g/...` is not a recognized URL scheme (not `https://`, not `adidas://`, not a valid universal link pattern). This will silently fail in most email clients — the user taps the CTA and nothing opens.

**[WARN] 16 tracking links unvalidatable**
All click links route through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects. Destination endpoint health and UTM integrity could not be probed.

**[WARN] Open/pixel tracker over HTTP**
`http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f63bb29a1-2deacd48-f2ae-47ff-8b27-e38150ed715d-000000/51cTD7Xi79jW1JbX3VxMt-ey5...` — tracking pixel served over HTTP; blocked by default in Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients, invalidating open-rate data for this send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two HTTP image sources — may be blocked**
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
This "arrow" asset appears twice and is served over HTTP. Gmail strips non-HTTPS images by default; the asset will render broken for a large share of recipients.

**[WARN] Missing `alt` text on two images**
- Dynamic Movable Ink image: `src https://dv.adidas.com/o/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?cp_tp=...` — no `alt` attribute. If the Movable Ink render fails, recipients see nothing.
- Tracking pixel `51cTD7Xi79jW1JbX3VxMt-ey5...` — `alt=""` should be explicit even for decorative/tracking images.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` blocks**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared twice — once in the main `<style>` block, once in the "Yahoo Android App" style block. Both fall back to local system fonts only (no remote `src: url()`). No rendering impact, minor HTML bloat.

**[INFO] HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Older but standard for broad email client compatibility; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `*|`, `%%`) visible in the source. Subject line carries no personalization — consistent with a broadcast promotional send.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback: 0 characters**
The MIME message has no text/plain part. Several spam filters (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) penalize HTML-only messages. Also a weak CAN-SPAM signal.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
QA relay did not see this header. If it's missing at the ESP level (not just a relay artifact), Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the native unsubscribe UI and this send violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (effective Feb 2024, enforced for >5k/day senders).

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click unsubscribe required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender policy. Absence here compounds the above risk.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown via relay. Sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`; DMARC alignment with `adidas.com` in the From display name should be confirmed independently.

**[INFO] `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`**
This instructs the browser not to send a `Referer` header when following email links. Does not affect CAN-SPAM directly but will suppress referrer-based attribution in GA4/Adobe Analytics for any recipient who opens in a browser.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[FAIL] Primary CTA broken at the link layer**
The broken `/g/...` scheme means the "app exclusive sale" CTA does not reach the app or a fallback web page. No UTM parameters can be attributed from this click.

**[WARN] UTM integrity on 16 tracked links unverified**
All links go through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects, which could not be resolved. Cannot confirm that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended on final landing pages.

**[WARN] `no-referrer` meta suppresses organic referrer data**
Even for working links, browser-based opens will report `(direct)` instead of the email referrer in analytics.
