# Another Miami original
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine new-drop email built around a real athlete and a specific soccer jersey — Lionel Messi, seated, in the Inter Miami CF 2026 Third Kit — and it's the strongest hero shot in this brand's recent run. The pink-carpet/Miami-apartment styling is distinctive, on-brand for "Miami original," and the product shot of the folded jersey on the dock bench reinforces the location story cleanly. This is a legitimate product launch, not a reheated Samba metallic or a vague "archive sale."
- But the email bolts on a second, unrelated offer — the $50-for-$40 gift card promo — right under the jersey CTA, same pattern this brand hits repeatedly (seen 2026-08-16, 2026-08-15). It's not confusing here since the gift-card module is visually separated with its own bold typographic slide, but it does dilute what should be a single-minded jersey launch into a split-focus send. For my persona this is also a swing-and-miss on relevance: I don't follow MLS, don't buy replica soccer jerseys, and Messi/Inter Miami isn't a demographic hook that lands for me.
- Judgement: well-executed creative, honest offer, no render bugs — but it's a soccer jersey drop, which is a narrow category appeal, not a broad "adidas bestsellers" email.

## What's working

- Strong, distinctive hero image — Messi seated in a plainly "at home" Miami setting, wearing the new kit, immediately visible without scrolling.
- Clear product name and description ("INTER MIAMI CF 2026 THIRD JERSEY") directly under the hero, with a black "SHOP NOW" button right there.
- Second product shot (folded jersey on dockside bench) gives a clean, unobstructed look at the actual garment and colorway.
- Gift-card module is visually distinct (bold white-on-navy slab type) so it doesn't get mistaken for jersey pricing — no bait-and-switch feel.
- No render bugs — text is crisp, images loaded, buttons are legible, layout holds together top to bottom.

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere for the jersey itself — a "shop now" with zero pricing signal is a soft CTA for anyone comparison-shopping.
- Two unrelated offers competing in one email (jersey drop + gift-card arbitrage) — same structural habit flagged in the 2026-08-16 and 2026-08-15 sends.
- Zero personalization or loyalty signal — no adiClub mention, no member pricing, despite the brand's own newsletter (2026-08-19) making a big deal of adiClub benefits.
- Persona mismatch — this is a soccer/MLS jersey; nothing in subject or hero signals relevance to a non-soccer-fan buyer, so it reads as a category-narrow blast, not a targeted send.
- Top utility bar ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW") is a generic, disconnected header that has nothing to do with the jersey story below it — wasted real estate above the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the generic "best sellers" utility bar at the top — it competes with the hero for the first-glance slot and isn't about this jersey at all. Replace with a one-line teaser tied to the jersey ("New: 2026 Third Kit").
- 2. Add a visible price under the "INTER MIAMI CF 2026 THIRD JERSEY" headline — "SHOP NOW" without a number is a weak CTA for considered soccer-kit purchases.
- 3. Either drop the gift-card module from this send or give it its own separate email — a jersey launch and a gift-card arbitrage offer serve two different buying motivations and diluting focus lowers click-through on both.
- 4. Add adiClub member perk framing (early access, points on this jersey) since the brand's own newsletter just spent an entire send building up that program — use it here to give a reason for loyalty members to act now.
- 5. Segment this send to soccer/MLS-engaged subscribers only; broad-blasting a replica-jersey drop to non-soccer buyers wastes the strong creative on an audience that won't convert.
