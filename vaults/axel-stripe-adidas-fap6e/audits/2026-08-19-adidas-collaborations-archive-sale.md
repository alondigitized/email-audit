# adidas Collaborations Archive Sale
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the barest email in the recent adidas run — one hero image, one headline, one CTA, no product shots at all. It's honest but it's also thin: "Collaborations Archive Sale" tells you there's a discount on unspecified past collabs, but shows you nothing to actually want. Compare this to "Samba looks good in metal" or "Real Madrid 26/27 third jersey" — those at least put a shoe or jersey in front of you. Here the hero art is just a moody sand/water texture with stacked wordmark text; it reads more like a lookbook cover than a shopping prompt.
- There's also a top-of-email banner ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") competing with the actual campaign message directly below it — a smaller-scale version of the bolt-on problem this brand keeps repeating (Samba Metallic, Real Madrid jersey, Care Bears). The layout is clean and legible, but there's nothing here that tells a shopper which specific "collaboration" they're getting 30% off — Yeezy? Ivy Park? Bad Bunny? Y-3? That ambiguity is the email's core weakness.
- Judgement: functional but forgettable. No render bugs, but no reason to click beyond a vague discount and a nostalgia-flavored headline.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, no broken images, renders correctly top to bottom.
- The "Up to 30% off" discount is stated twice (top link and body copy), so the offer is unmissable.
- Two clear CTAs ("Shop Now") — one in the top banner, one at the bottom — both with unambiguous button styling.
- Brand mark (adidas logo, trefoil) is present and consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- Zero product imagery — no shoe, no apparel, nothing that shows what's actually on sale, unlike every other recent adidas send that at least features a hero product shot.
- "Collaborations Archive" doesn't name a single collaborator (no Y-3, no artist, no partner brand), so there's no hook for anyone with a specific collab preference.
- Top banner ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") is a mismatched, generic module bolted onto a specific sale email — same bolt-on pattern flagged in the 2026-08-07 and 2026-08-13 sends.
- Hero image is abstract/textural (sand and water) rather than product-forward — it looks like a fragrance or lifestyle ad, not a footwear sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the abstract hero art with an actual grid or hero shot of 3-4 flagship collab silhouettes (e.g., a Y-3, a Human Race, a Wales Bonner piece) so the shopper can see what "archive" means in product terms.
- 2. Drop or reconcile the top "best sellers" banner — it dilutes the single-offer clarity this email otherwise has; if kept, retheme it to match the collab sale rather than a generic best-sellers plug.
- 3. Name at least one or two participating collaboration lines in the body copy to give the subject line teeth and something concrete to search for.
- 4. Add a deadline — "limited time" with no date is the weakest form of urgency; a specific end date would lift both open and click behavior.
