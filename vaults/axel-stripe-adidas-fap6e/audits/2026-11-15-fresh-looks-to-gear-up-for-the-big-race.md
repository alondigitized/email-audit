# Fresh looks to gear up for the big race 🏁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This email sells three different things and commits to none of them. The black countdown banner ("BLACK FRIDAY FIRST ACCESS / Up to 60% Off for Members", 04:07:43:29) is the only real offer. It sits above a Las Vegas lifestyle hero that has nothing to do with a sale, and nothing links the two. Neither the Neon Lights nor the After Hours Collection carries a price, a deal or a product. The subject promises "big race" gear, but the email is mostly mood photography.
- The countdown banner is a good device. It also repeats the 2026-11-14 send, "adiClub members get up to 60% off for Black Friday early", which opened with the same offer 24 hours earlier. The creative is stronger than the plain red hero from yesterday, but a subscriber has now seen the 60% message twice in two days. The email then ends on a sweepstakes for signed boots that has little to do with either the sale or the race.
- Verdict: attractive and on-brand, but unfocused. The offer is a banner rather than the lead, and every CTA says "SHOP NOW" without naming a product.

## What's working

- The countdown timer gives credible, visible urgency and puts the 60% member offer first.
- The Las Vegas night photography is cohesive and premium, and the F1 partner logos (Mercedes-AMG Petronas, Audi Revolut) anchor the "race" theme.
- The layout is clean: wide hero images, short copy, and high-contrast black CTA buttons that are easy to spot.
- The sweepstakes section is a fresh hook that rewards adiClub membership and FIFA Rewards connection.

## What's weak

- The 60% banner is not tied to any product or CTA, so the offer has no path to a click.
- Both "SHOP NOW" buttons are generic, and the two collections show no prices, product names or close-ups.
- The subject says "Fresh looks to gear up for the big race", but the body never says which race or why it matters.
- The footer-adjacent sweepstakes block adds a third message (soccer boots) to an email already split between fashion and a sale.
- The "Shop the new Las Vegas Collection." text link at the top is small and gray, and it reads as leftover preheader text.
- The hero models wear dark, similar outfits, so it is hard to tell what is being sold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one job per send. Either lead with the member Black Friday sale and show specific products at specific prices, or run the Las Vegas collection launch without the sale banner.
- 2. Link the countdown banner to a members-only sale page, and add a "SHOP MEMBER PRICES" button directly under it.
- 3. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" buttons with named ones, such as "Shop Neon Lights hoodies" and "Shop After Hours looks".
- 4. Show two or three products under each collection hero, with prices or "from $X".
- 5. Move the sweepstakes into a separate send, or cut it to a single line.
- 6. Fix the subject so it matches the body. The race is Las Vegas, so say that.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 60% off starts in 4 days`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Neon Lights and After Hours, plus early Black Friday access for adiClub members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member countdown is on. Shop the Las Vegas Collection first.`
