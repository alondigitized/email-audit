# Check out this month's adiClub benefits, here is your August Newsletter
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the adiClub monthly newsletter, and it's structurally different from every other adidas send in the recent history — no discount code, no "% off" banner, no jersey drop, just four benefit modules (sweepstakes, Strava integration, sweepstakes, points redemption). That's honest to the "member benefits digest" framing in the subject line, and it's a nice break from the repetitive metallic-Samba/jersey reheats logged on 8/16 and 8/13. But as a purchase driver it's nearly inert: three of the four CTAs point to sweepstakes entries or account-linking, not products, and the one commerce CTA ("Redeem Now") is vague about what's actually on sale.
- The visual execution is clean — good use of a pink hero, warm beige and green section blocks to separate content, and readable black CTA buttons throughout. No render bugs. But this reads more like a loyalty-program status update than a marketing email, and for a subscriber who just wants to know what's new/discounted, there's nothing to grab onto until you scroll past two sweepstakes offers.
- Verdict: well-built, on-brand, zero render issues — but low commercial urgency. This is a "nice to have opened" email, not a "must click" one.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy — hero image, bold "OWN A PIECE OF HISTORY" headline, and a black "ENTER NOW" button all land in the first screen.
- Distinct section backgrounds (pink hero, cream Strava/Penn State block, green adiClub photo block) make the newsletter easy to scan in segments.
- Consistent CTA button styling (black pill, white caps text) across all four modules — no design drift.
- The FIFA World Cup 26™ signed match ball offer is a genuinely differentiated, specific prize, not generic "win big" copy.
- No broken images, overlapping text, or placeholder content anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The lead offer is a sweepstakes entry, not a purchasable product or discount — weakest possible hook for driving revenue.
- "UNLOCK SAVINGS ON YOUR FAVES" / "Redeem Now" never states an actual discount amount or which points-to-dollar ratio applies — buried vague value prop.
- Three of four content blocks require an extra action (enter a sweepstakes, connect a third-party account) before any value is realized — high friction, delayed gratification.
- The Penn State Nittany Lion tracksuit giveaway is hyper-regional/collegiate and won't resonate outside that fanbase, yet it's given equal visual weight to the World Cup ball offer.
- No pricing, product imagery, or "shop the sale" link anywhere — someone opening this for a deal will bounce immediately.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the points-redemption/savings module instead of the sweepstakes — put an actual dollar or percentage figure in the hero ("Turn 500 points into $20 off") so there's one unambiguous, high-value offer above the fold.
- 2. Cut or demote the Penn State tracksuit giveaway — it's too niche for a national send; if kept, move it below the fold as a smaller module.
- 3. Add at least one shoppable product tile (e.g., "Shop Samba" or "Shop Running") so subscribers who don't care about sweepstakes still have a commerce path.
- 4. Make the Redeem Now CTA specific — swap generic "save on almost everything" for a concrete example discount.
