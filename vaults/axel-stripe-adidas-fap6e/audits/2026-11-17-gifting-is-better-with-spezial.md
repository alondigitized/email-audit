# Gifting is better with Spezial
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third adidas send in four days with the Black Friday First Access countdown bolted on top (see 2026-11-16 "BadBo by adidas x Bad Bunny" and "New adidas | Care Bears collection"). The pink Handball Spezial hero is lovely, but it sits under a black banner that says "Up to 60% Off for Members" while the headline below says "Holidays made with Spezial." The email never says whether the Spezials are part of the sale, so the offer and the product don't connect.
- Below the hero it splits again. Three apparel shots of adiSignature tracksuits have no copy, no price and no link. Then a sweepstakes ("Win an exclusive adiSignature style kit") pulls attention away from the gifting pitch. The email is tidy but sells three things: Spezial, adiSignature apparel and a giveaway. Only the countdown carries urgency, and it's generic.
- Verdict: polished and on-brand, but unfocused and repetitive. It's a 5, in line with the recent adidas range.

## What's working

- The pink suede Spezial hero is a strong, giftable image with a clear focal point.
- The countdown banner (01:08:53:27) puts a concrete member offer, up to 60% off, at the top.
- The Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons are clear, high-contrast and segment by recipient, which suits gifting.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The banner offer and the Spezial hero are disconnected. It's unclear whether Spezial is discounted.
- The three Shop buttons are stacked, small and left-aligned, with no price, product name or colorway to click for.
- The three tracksuit images have no caption or link cue, and they belong to a different collection than Spezial.
- The sweepstakes section dilutes the gifting message and gives no entry details or deadline.
- The countdown banner is the same creative as the last several sends, so it reads as wallpaper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either make this a Spezial gifting email with a shop-by-recipient grid, or run the giveaway as its own send.
- 2. Tie the offer to the product. Say "Spezial: members save up to 60%," or drop the banner if Spezial is excluded.
- 3. Replace the empty three-image strip with priced, named products (Spezial colorways with price and a "Shop Spezial" CTA).
- 4. Make the CTAs specific ("Shop Spezial for Him") and place them side by side.
- 5. If you keep the giveaway, add the deadline and a one-line entry rule.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spezial gifts, members save up to 60%`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The sneaker everyone wants this holiday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handball Spezial in blush pink, plus early member pricing before Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Spezial for him, her and kids. Members get first access.`

- **No destination URLs are visible in the truncated source**, so I can't confirm any broken link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing.** QA reports a text version of 0 chars. Text-only clients get nothing, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Two images have no alt text:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/cbf6dd86-30bc-4a6a-87d8-f146f994ce79?cp_tp=...`
  - The tracking pixel `q-MYhEpo1CPif1rOSmP6cLn9EWszT4DMKOIJZHTamCM=452`
