# adidas Originals x Metallica
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-collab announcement with no offer. The hero is strong: a white Originals tee with red Metallica lettering, hung in front of road cases and tape reels. It reads as a real tour-merch moment, and for a Metallica or Originals fan it's a good opener.
- Below it the email falls apart. The copy is a paragraph of press-release language with no price, no product names and no sizing, so there is nothing to click for. A second, unrelated block (the 2026 Rookie Class Giveaway III) then pulls the reader toward adiClub and football. Two stacked CTA buttons at the bottom ("Learn More" and "Participate") split attention further. A black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner at the top also competes with the hero.

## What's working

- The Metallica tee hero is striking and on-brand, with the red logo popping against the black and white.
- The headline "ADIDAS ORIGINALS X METALLICA COLLECTION" is large and states the collab clearly.
- The "Shop Now" button is a solid black block with an arrow, easy to find after the copy.
- The adiClub giveaway rewards loyalty members, with named athletes and a clear "each month, new athletes drop" cadence.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or shipping offer anywhere, so nothing creates urgency.
- The top black banner, "Explore the latest adidas best sellers", has a separate "Shop Now" button that has nothing to do with the Metallica story.
- The second half is an unrelated football giveaway, so the email has two competing stories and three more buttons.
- The body copy is dense brand-speak ("reimagines classic tour merchandise through the lens of...") and names no products.
- The giveaway text says "Each drop gives... Each month, new athletes drop..." in a repetitive, filler tone, and nothing says how to enter.
- The only product shown is one tee, with no grid and no price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the giveaway block, or move it to its own send. One story per email: Metallica only.
- 2. Replace the paragraph with a 3 to 4 item product grid (tee, hoodie, a Samba or Gazelle if one exists) with prices and a "Shop the drop" CTA.
- 3. Remove the generic "best sellers" banner, or swap it for a collab-specific line such as "Limited drop, while sizes last".
- 4. Add a credible scarcity cue if one is true (limited run, drop date or sizes).
- 5. Rewrite the preheader so it adds new information instead of repeating the body copy.
- 6. If the giveaway stays, collapse the two buttons into one "Enter the giveaway" CTA.
