# The Club América 110th anniversary jersey
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-purpose product-story email for the Club América 110th anniversary jersey. It has no discount, no urgency and no member perk. The hero photo is strong: three models in cream and navy retro pieces in front of fireworks, with a big "CLUB AMÉRICA" wordmark. That makes it a fan and collector pitch, not a promotion.
- The timing is the problem. It lands on 11-23, the same day as "Up to 60% off for Black Friday is live" (7/10). It follows a run of loud Black Friday sends on 11-18, 11-21 and 11-23, and it breaks from that with a calm editorial note, like the 11-20 "Supercharge your training" send (5/10). A subscriber who just saw "UP TO 60% OFF" has little reason to care about a full-price jersey today. The email is honest and tidy, but it asks for a purchase without giving a reason to buy now.
- Verdict: a competent catalog-style send that suits a Club América fan and won't move a general adidas list. It sits at the low end of the 5 to 6 range.

## What's working

- The hero image is high quality. Warm cream and navy against fireworks looks celebratory, and the Trefoil and "CA" crest details show the product.
- The "CLUB AMÉRICA" wordmark in the hero makes the topic clear within a second.
- The layout is simple: logo, hero, headline, one paragraph, one black "SHOP NOW" button. Nothing competes for attention.
- The body copy is on-theme ("110 years… passion and pride") and the footer is plain, with nothing buried.
- The models look like fans, and the mix of people reads as authentic for a football-culture drop.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, urgency or launch date. The email gives no reason to act today.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner sits above the hero. It splits the CTA and points away from the jersey, and it looks offset against the hero image.
- The headline repeats the subject and preheader, and the body paragraph is generic. There is no price, no sizing or fit note, and no home or away detail.
- The "SHOP NOW" button copy is generic. The jersey itself is only shown as a worn shirt and is never shown on its own.
- Sending this in Black Friday week with no sale tie-in risks a click rate well below the sale emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to buy now. That could be "Available now", a price, a limited-quantity note, or the Black Friday offer if the jersey is included. A line such as "Now at adidas.com. Sizes selling fast." is the cheapest fix.
- 2. Remove or demote the "best sellers" banner so the jersey is the only CTA on the page.
- 3. Change the button to "Shop the jersey" or "Get the 110th jersey", and show a flat product shot with the price.
- 4. Cut the repeated headline and subject. Use the copy to say something new, such as the colorway story, the retro details and the sizes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links weren't probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Open-tracking pixel served over HTTP.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a209615dbb-d10fa1fb-…/ynjmtXMFp-2TfaPKDK8hkFWAjHg2AqE5-VJYvOS_IkU=452` is flagged as an image with no alt text and a non-HTTPS source. The `/CI0/` path pattern suggests it is the open pixel. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will not register opens, so open-rate data will be undercounted.
- **Insecure `arrow` image, listed twice.** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` uses `http://`. Clients may block or rewrite it, which would drop the arrow glyph.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on a content image.** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/c315d58d-23b3-4fda-ad1d-789a795180f3?cp_tp=v3…` has no alt. If it is a product or hero image, it is unreadable when images are blocked and invisible to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel.** The pixel above should have `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so it is treated as decorative.
- **Viewport meta is incomplete.** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. This is mostly harmless in email clients, but the tag is non-standard.
- **Font fallbacks.** The `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `local('Arial'/'Helvetica'/…)` sources, so no web font loads. Rendering depends on the fallback stack in the inline styles, which I couldn't see in the truncated source.
- **Doctype.** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is common for email and not an issue in itself.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Tokens further down the body can't be ruled out.
