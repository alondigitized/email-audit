## What's working

- The hero photo is tactile and on-brand. The gum sole, suede texture and gold colorway on sand make a strong first image.
- There is one idea and one product, so the email is easy to read.
- The "ADIDAS ORIGINALS X BAD BUNNY" headline sits right under the co-brand lockup, so the collab is clear.
- Both "SHOP NOW" buttons are high-contrast black and easy to spot.
- The copy gives real product detail: premium honey suede, leather lining and a classic gum outsole.

## What's weak

- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner sits above the hero and competes with it. It sends people to a generic destination before they reach the drop.
- Nothing creates urgency. There is no launch date, no "while supplies last" and no raffle or member access.
- Both CTAs say only "SHOP NOW". Neither names the shoe, the colorway or a price.
- The subject and creative repeat the 2026-10-13 send. Nothing new is added to the story.
- The tag in the photos reads "06.23.24", which looks stale. There is also no price, sizing or availability cue.
- The black banner bar is slightly wider than the 620px image column below it, so the left and right edges look a little misaligned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the "best sellers" banner, or move it below the drop. Let the hero and Solar Gold CTA carry the email.
- 2. Add a reason to act now: a launch date, "Available now in limited sizes", or adiClub early access. Show the price near the first button.
- 3. If the 10-13 send already ran, don't resend it unchanged. Send it only to non-openers with a new angle, or use a fresh image set without the old date tag.
- 4. Rewrite the buttons to be specific, such as "Shop Gazelle Solar Gold" and "Get your size".
- 5. Add a small row of sizes or availability, or a "low stock" signal, to lift click intent.

- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `lang="en"`. This is typical for email, with no issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve every image and the tracking pixel over HTTPS (`image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com`).
