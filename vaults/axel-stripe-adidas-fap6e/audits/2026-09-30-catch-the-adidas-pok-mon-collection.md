- This is a pure brand-and-fandom send with no offer. The hero is a black canvas with three Pikachu poses and the adidas Originals × Pokémon lockup. Compared with the 2026-09-24 "Up to 50% off fall savings is live 🍂" send (8/10), which had a clear offer, this email has nothing to act on. ## What's working

- The Pikachu hero is bold and high-contrast. It's instantly recognizable and gives the eye a clear first landing spot.
- The adidas × Pokémon lockup is credible. The Originals trefoil and Pokémon logo make the co-brand obvious.
- The copy is short and playful. "One collection you won't want to trade" fits the theme.
- There's a single black CTA with an arrow. It's unambiguous and there's no competing link.

## What's weak

- There's no product. The email shows only cartoon art and never a shoe, hoodie or tee, so there's nothing to click toward.
