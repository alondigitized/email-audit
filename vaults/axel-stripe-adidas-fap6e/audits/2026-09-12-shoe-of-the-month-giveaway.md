# Shoe of the month giveaway
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-branded adiClub member giveaway for the "September Shoe of the Month" — a single hero product (Hyperboost Euphoria), a clear headline, and one unambiguous "ENTER NOW" CTA. It's a nice change of pace from the brand's recent sitewide-discount churn (compare 2026-09-10's "40% Off" sale email), and it reads as genuine loyalty-program content rather than another markdown push.
- But there's no discount, no price, and no urgency — it's a sweepstakes, not a purchase incentive, so it naturally caps out on business impact even though the execution is tidy. It also leans entirely on a static product shot against a plain green backdrop, with a stray "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner sitting awkwardly above the giveaway hero, diluting the single-offer focus the rest of the email otherwise nails. Solid, focused send — just a low-stakes one.

## What's working

- Single, clear CTA ("ENTER NOW") with no competing links in the body.
- Hero product shot is sharp, well-lit, and instantly reads as a basketball/running shoe silhouette.
- adiClub branding is prominent and reinforces this is a loyalty-member perk, not a mass blast.
- Headline and body copy are short and legible — no clutter, no dense fine print until the footer.
- Asterisked disclosure ("Winners will be able to select...based on available inventory") is honest and not buried.

## What's weak

- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner at the top competes with the giveaway CTA and doesn't belong in a single-purpose sweepstakes email.
- No visible deadline or entry window — "Don't miss your chance" implies urgency but gives no actual date.
- No product specs (price, sizes, colorways) since it's a giveaway, which is fine narratively but leaves nothing to click toward if you don't win.
- Flat, static hero (studio shot on green) — no lifestyle/action element compared to recent Tyshawn or basketball-hero sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or relocate the "best sellers / SHOP NOW" banner — it splits attention from the one CTA this email exists to drive.
- 2. Add a concrete entry deadline ("Enter by Sept 30") to convert "don't miss your chance" into real urgency.
- 3. Swap the studio product shot for an on-foot/lifestyle image of the Hyperboost Euphoria to add visual energy.
- 4. Consider adding entry odds or a running counter ("Over 2,000 entries so far") to boost perceived stakes.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `One lucky adiClub member wins a free pair. Enter before it closes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No purchase, no price tag — just a chance to win Hyperboost Euphoria.`
