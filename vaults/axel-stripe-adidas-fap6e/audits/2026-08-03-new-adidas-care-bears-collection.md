# New adidas | Care Bears collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a collaboration-launch email — adidas x Care Bears — bolted onto a back-to-school backpack pitch, and the two halves never quite agree on what the email is about. The hero art is genuinely charming: a Superstar sneaker rendered as a cloud-surfing scene with a Care Bear perched on the tongue, pastel gradient sky, rain cloud and sparkles worked in as design elements rather than slapped-on stickers. It's the most visually distinctive creative in this brand's recent batch, better than the flat lifestyle photography of "Style before the bell" (2026-07-25) or the generic grid-dump of "Shop the latest trending styles" (2026-07-27).
- But it's a launch email, not a sale — no discount, no price, no urgency — and it tacks on an unrelated backpack module that dilutes the one thing the email had going for it: a clear, single, novel hook. Compare to "Welcome Penn State" (2026-07-30, 7/10), which also had zero discount but stayed disciplined with one collection, one message. This one splits attention between a nostalgia collab and a back-to-school errand, and the Care Bears section itself never shows an actual product shot — just concept art — so a reader can't tell what they'd be buying.
- Judgment: a strong, distinctive top half undercut by a bolted-on second offer and the total absence of real product photography for the headline collab. Call it a 6/10 execution on a good creative idea.

## What's working

- The hero art is the most original visual in this brand's recent sends — a sneaker-as-cloud illustration with the Care Bear integrated into the composition, not just overlaid as a logo.
- Two clear black "SHOP NOW" buttons with arrow icons are unmissable and unambiguous.
- Clean visual hierarchy: black top banner → hero image → headline → body copy → CTA, repeated for each module, easy to scan on mobile.
- "Full of bold color and feel-good comfort" preview line is genuine, on-theme copy, not boilerplate.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- No actual Care Bears product is shown — the hero is concept art of a sneaker, not a real shoe photo, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- Two unrelated offers (Care Bears collab + back-to-school backpacks) compete in one email with no visual or narrative bridge between them.
- Zero price, discount, or urgency anywhere — pure top-of-funnel awareness, which caps how hard this can convert.
- The top black banner ("Explore the latest adidas best sellers") is a third, entirely separate CTA competing with the two below it before the reader even gets to the Care Bears content.
- Copy is thin: "unlock the magic" is vague collab-speak that doesn't tell the reader what's actually different about this product (materials? exclusive colorway? kids' sizing?).

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a real product shot of the actual Care Bears sneaker/apparel — concept art is great as a hero, but the reader needs to see the thing they'd buy before hitting "Shop Now." This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Split into two emails or clearly subordinate the backpack module — a nostalgia collab and a back-to-school utility push are different psychological triggers and diluting them both.
- 3. Cut the top "best sellers" banner from this send — it's a third competing CTA that undercuts the collab's focus in the first inch of the email.
- 4. Add one concrete detail to the Care Bears copy — release date, limited quantities, or "available now" — anything to move it from vibes to action.
- 5. If this is a limited drop, say so — collabs like this typically sell out; that urgency is currently completely absent.
