# adidas I Pokémon
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a thin, single-idea launch announcement for the adidas | Pokémon collection. The hero is three Pikachus on black with the adidas Trefoil and Pokémon logo lockup. It reads clearly as a licensed collab, and the Pikachu art is the only thing that holds attention. There are no product shots, no prices and no offer, so the email gives the reader nothing to evaluate.
- The copy is playful ("Let's goooo! Catch the new... evolve your style... won't want to trade"). It fits the theme but says nothing concrete. The email never shows what is in the collection. It could be apparel, shoes or kids' items, and the reader can't tell. Compared with the 2026-10-22 "Bad Bunny Gazelle Solar Gold" send, which at least showed the sole and a lace close-up, this one shows no shoe at all. Compared with the 2026-10-16 Metallica send, it gives no sense of the range.
- Judgement: a lazy drop announcement that leans entirely on IP nostalgia. It will work for Pokémon fans and underperform with everyone else.

## What's working

- The black background with bright yellow Pikachu has strong contrast, so the hero is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The adidas Trefoil and Pokémon lockup on the right makes the collab clear immediately.
- There is a single, unmistakable black "SHOP NOW" button with an arrow, and it sits right under the copy.
- The layout is clean, with plenty of white space and no render breakage.
- The headline "ADIDAS | POKÉMON" is simple and legible.

## What's weak

- The email shows no product at all. The hero is character art, so there is nothing to click for.
- It has no offer, price, release date or urgency.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner above the hero pulls attention away with a second "SHOP NOW" that has nothing to do with Pokémon.
- The hero image and the banner are not aligned with each other or with the text column, so the left and right edges look ragged.
- The preheader reads like a repeat of the body copy. "Featuring some of your Pokémon favorites." says nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a product grid under the hero with 3-4 real items (a shoe, a tee, a hoodie) with prices, and link each to its own PDP. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put a release or availability line in the hero ("Available now" or "Drops Oct 24"). Add credible urgency if there is a limited run.
- 3. Remove the best-sellers banner, or move it below the collection so it doesn't compete with the hero.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop the Pokémon collection".
- 5. Align the hero and banner to the same column width as the text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pikachu x adidas is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The adidas | Pokémon drop: shoes, tees, hoodies`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Pokémon sneakers and apparel, available now at adidas.com.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the full collection before your favorites sell out.`
