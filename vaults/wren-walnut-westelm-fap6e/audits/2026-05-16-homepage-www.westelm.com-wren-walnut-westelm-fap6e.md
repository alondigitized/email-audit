# Homepage snapshot · www.westelm.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened West Elm and the very first thing I see is a full-screen modal blocking me from the entire homepage. As a Key Rewards member who already shops here, being greeted with a "sign up for our newsletter" gate feels like the brand has no idea who I am. What little I can glimpse behind it — "emma chamberlain × west elm" in a tiny banner at the top — actually sounds interesting to me, but I can't get to it because this modal is planted squarely in the way.

## What's working

- **"2% back across all our brands" callout** — as a Key Rewards member I know this is real value, and spelling out Williams Sonoma and Pottery Barn makes the cross-brand benefit feel tangible
- **"emma chamberlain × west elm" peek** — even at tiny text behind the modal, I clocked it immediately; a collab with a millennial-beloved creator is exactly my cultural wavelength
- **Clean modal layout** — the hierarchy is legible: headline → body → inputs → CTA → dismiss; no chaos

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport modal as the first interaction** — I came to browse rugs and brass shelving, not to file a form. This is the digital equivalent of a store associate blocking the entrance to hand you a clipboard.
- **Ignoring my existing membership** — West Elm knows I'm a Key Rewards member; serving me an acquisition modal is a wasted touch. This slot should be a returning-member welcome back, not a first-time signup pitch.
- **The homepage is 100% invisible** — hero image, featured categories, the emma chamberlain collab, any active sale — all of it unreachable until I deal with the modal. That's not a dark pattern, that IS the whole experience right now.
- **Lengthy SMS legal disclaimer** — three lines of carrier-liability boilerplate sits between the mobile field and the CTA button; it visually weighs the form down and signals "we're going to text you a lot."

## Recommendations

- **Recognize logged-in or cookied Key Members and skip the acquisition modal entirely** — serve them a slim welcome-back banner ("Welcome back, Wren — your rewards balance is X") instead of a form they already filled out.
- **If the modal must show, swap the generic newsletter pitch for the emma chamberlain collab** — "See the Collab First: New arrivals from Emma's edit, in your inbox" is more aspirational and campaign-relevant than the generic "sales & more" copy.
- **Move the SMS legal copy below the fold of the modal or into a collapsible** — it shouldn't visually compete with the primary CTA.
- **Add a member-only promo offer to the modal** — e.g., "Key Members get early access + double points this weekend" gives me a reason to submit even if I'm already signed up; it doubles as a loyalty retention play.
