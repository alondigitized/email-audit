# Homepage snapshot · www.westelm.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened westelm.com and immediately got a modal covering the entire screen asking for my email before I could see a single product. For a brand that knows I'm a Key Member — they say so right in the popup — this feels like a classic case of shouting at someone who already walked through the door. What I can see through the thin sliver of background imagery looks like outdoor or living-room furniture, but I genuinely cannot tell what season or campaign is running.

## What's working

- **Key Member callout in the modal:** "become a Key Member and earn 2% back across all our brands, including Williams Sonoma and Pottery Barn" — as someone who already holds a Key Rewards membership, this validates the program and reminds me why cross-brand loyalty matters. It's the one line that actually speaks to me.
- **Clean modal design:** The typography is crisp, the two-field form (email + optional mobile) is easy to parse, and "NO THANKS" is clearly labeled — not hidden in gray 8px type.
- **Optional mobile field:** Marking the phone number as optional feels respectful rather than coercive.

## What's weak

- **The modal itself:** This is the single biggest problem. I cannot see the homepage. Not a category. Not a hero. Not a rug sale. Just an email gate. As a returning Key Member who already gets their emails, this is friction, not welcome.
- **No active offer in the capture prompt:** If you're going to interrupt me, at least give me 10% off my next order. "Find out first" is a weak value prop when I'm already subscribed.
- **SMS legal disclaimer:** The small-print paragraph about wireless carriers, STOP to cancel, data rates may apply — it's four lines of legal copy that makes the whole interaction feel like I'm signing up for something sketchy, not a furniture brand I trust.
- **Zero product visibility:** The background is barely legible. I have no idea what's on sale, what's new, or why I should care today.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for logged-in or returning Key Members** — if the browser has a cookie or I'm authenticated, skip the email gate entirely. I'm already in the loyalty program; treat me like it.
- **Add an active offer to the modal:** "Sign up and get 15% off your next order" or "Unlock free shipping on your next purchase" — something that makes the interruption worth tolerating.
- **Swap the modal trigger to exit-intent or 30-second delay:** Let me see the homepage first so I have a reason to want emails. Firing on page load before I've seen a single product is the fastest way to lose me.
- **Surface the rug sale or seasonal campaign in the modal background:** Right now the background imagery is decorative and vague — use that real estate to show the actual campaign running this week.
