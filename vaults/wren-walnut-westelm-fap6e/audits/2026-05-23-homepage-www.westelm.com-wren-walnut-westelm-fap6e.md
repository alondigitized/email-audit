# Homepage snapshot · www.westelm.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- West Elm hit me with a full-screen email capture modal the second I landed — I literally cannot see the homepage. What I get instead is a newsletter sign-up pitch with a Key Rewards hook buried in the sub-copy. The brand feels like it knows I'm a returning member but is still treating me like a cold lead who needs to be funneled into an email list before I can shop.

## What's working

- **Key Rewards cross-brand callout** — "earn 2% back across all our brands, including Williams Sonoma and Pottery Barn" is actually a real differentiator I care about. That multi-brand angle is legitimately useful for someone like me who already cross-shops the WSB family.
- **Modal structure is clean** — headline is readable, fields are well-spaced, "NO THANKS" dismiss is visible (not hidden). No dark-pattern trickery with tiny X buttons or fake close zones.
- **Email + SMS dual capture** — offering mobile as optional rather than required is respectful. Points for not forcing it.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I cannot see a single product, lifestyle image, seasonal campaign, or category nav. I have no idea what West Elm is promoting right now.
- **2% back is a weak lead** — For a returning Key Member who already knows the program, "earn 2% back" is not exciting. It should be "You're already a Key Member — here's what's new."
- **SMS fine-print block is visual noise** — Four lines of legal disclaimer text in gray takes up ~20% of the viewport. It makes the modal feel like a compliance form, not an invitation.
- **"SIGN UP NOW" as the primary CTA is the wrong message for a returning shopper** — I'm already in the ecosystem. This modal treats me like a stranger.
- **Seasonal intent is invisible** — I genuinely cannot tell if West Elm is in a Memorial Day sale, a summer launch, a new collection drop — zero context.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for logged-in Key Rewards members** — if I'm cookied from a prior visit, skip the email capture entirely and take me straight to the campaign. I'm not a cold lead.
- **Replace "SIGN UP NOW" copy with "Get Early Access"** — reframes email sign-up as a privilege, not a data harvest. More resonant for the member-acquisition angle they're pitching.
- **Shrink the SMS disclaimer into a toggle** — collapse the four-line legal block behind a "View terms" inline link. Reclaim that vertical space for a lifestyle image thumbnail that shows me what I'd actually be signing up to see.
- **Add a "What's new this week" teaser image behind the modal** — even a blurred or dimmed lifestyle shot of a product drop gives me a reason to dismiss and browse rather than bounce.
