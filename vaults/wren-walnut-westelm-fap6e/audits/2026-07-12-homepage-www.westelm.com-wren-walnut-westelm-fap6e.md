# Homepage snapshot · www.westelm.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- I opened West Elm and immediately got a full-screen email capture modal — the actual homepage is almost entirely obscured. What I can see is West Elm positioning itself around The Fall Collection and Key Membership perks, but as an existing Key Rewards member I'm being asked to sign up for something I already have. The brand reads upscale and clean, but this is not the welcome-back energy I was hoping for.

## What's working

- **Key Member 2% back pitch** — cross-brand stacking with Williams Sonoma and Pottery Barn is genuinely compelling for someone already in that ecosystem; it's the one thing here that makes me pause
- **"The Fall Collection" callout** — just barely visible behind the modal, but the framing is timely and aligns with how I shop (fall rugs, throw season, etc.)
- **Modal render quality** — west elm logo, field labels, button — all crisp, no janky padding, nothing broken
- **Optional mobile field** — respecting that SMS is opt-in and not required is the right call; it's labeled clearly

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal on load** — this is the dark pattern to end all dark patterns. I came to browse The Fall Collection and I'm immediately blocked by a gate asking me to hand over my email and phone number before I've seen a single product
- **No recognition I'm already a Key Member** — if I'm logged in or cookied, this modal should not be showing me a Key Membership acquisition pitch; it's the equivalent of a hotel asking a loyalty member to "join our rewards program" at check-in
- **SMS legal block is enormous** — the fine print about "automated marketing text messages," carrier rates, and how to text STOP takes up a third of the modal; it signals friction before a single product is seen
- **Zero furniture or home context** — this modal could be for any brand; there is no image, no room scene, no rug, no brass fixture — nothing that speaks to why I'm here
- **Hero completely blocked** — I genuinely cannot evaluate the homepage because it is hidden behind this gate

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for logged-in / recognized Key Members** — I should see the homepage, not a sign-up form; save acquisition flows for guest sessions only
- **If the modal must show, add a lifestyle thumbnail** — a single moody fall room shot in the modal background would tie the email pitch to the product world instantly
- **Condense the SMS disclosure** — link to a "Message & data rates may apply" page instead of printing three sentences of legalese inline; it reads like distrust, not transparency
- **Test a banner strip instead of a modal** — a sticky top banner ("New fall arrivals + 2% back as a Key Member — join now") lets me see the actual homepage while still capturing email intent
