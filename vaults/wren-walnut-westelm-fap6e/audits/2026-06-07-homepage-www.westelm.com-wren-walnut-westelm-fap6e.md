# Homepage snapshot · www.westelm.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see when I open West Elm is a full-screen email capture modal — not the homepage, not a product, not a sale. It's bold, it's brand-appropriate in its typography, and I get the Key Rewards pitch baked right in, which at least feels relevant to me. But this is absolutely a hard gate before I've seen a single piece of furniture, and on mobile it's eating 100% of my screen.

## What's working

- **Key Member call-out in the modal body** — "earn 2% back across all our brands, including Williams Sonoma and Pottery Barn" is exactly the cross-brand perk I care about. As a Key Rewards member this actually makes me pause instead of immediately tapping "NO THANKS."
- **"New summer styles" banner** — just barely visible behind the overlay at the top of the screen; confirms the site is current-season, not stale.
- **"NO THANKS" escape hatch** — it's there, it's visible, and it's not obscured. No fake X-button games.
- **Clean modal typography** — the West Elm wordmark and font treatment are on-brand; even the interstitial looks like West Elm.

## What's weak

- **The modal IS the homepage** — I opened West Elm to see rugs and brass fixtures and instead I'm reading SMS legal fine print. On mobile this is an immediate bounce risk.
- **No offer in the capture modal** — if you're going to gate me with a full-screen overlay, give me something: "Sign up and get 10% off your first order." Right now it's just "give us your phone number." Hard pass.
- **SMS legal copy is massive and scary** — "Wireless Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages" is not the vibe when I'm trying to shop sofas.
- **Zero product visibility above the fold** — I can't see the Andes sectional, I can't see any rug sale, I can't see any summer campaign imagery. The homepage is functionally invisible.
- **No urgency or deadline** — even if I wanted to engage with the modal, there's nothing that makes me feel like I'm getting something finite.

## Recommendations

- **Add a discount incentive to the email modal** — "Join + get 15% off your next order" turns this from a chore into a reason to sign up; I'd actually consider it as a Key Rewards member who stacks offers.
- **Reduce the SMS legal block** — link to a "terms apply" disclosure rather than pasting four sentences of carrier legalese inside the modal; it tanks trust.
- **Swap to a slide-in or bottom-sheet modal on mobile** — a half-sheet that reveals 50% of the homepage hero behind it lets me see the product I came for while still capturing the email.
- **Surface the current promo in the modal itself** — if there's a summer sale happening (the banner suggests it), name it: "New summer styles are here — be the first to shop." Copy the campaign into the capture hook.
