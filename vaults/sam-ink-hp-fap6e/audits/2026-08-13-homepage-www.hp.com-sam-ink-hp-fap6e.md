# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Clean, simple homepage — no clutter, loads fast-looking, easy on the eyes. But the whole thing is built around "Back to School" laptops and PCs, and I'm here for ink and printer supplies, not backpack season. Feels like a general electronics store today, not the HP I know as my Instant Ink guy.

## What's working

- The "Save up to 65%... free shipping... 30% off HP Home Care Packs" line is specific enough that I know roughly what I'm getting, not just vague "big sale" talk.
- The blue "Shop" button stands out against all that white space — no hunting for it.
- "Our Products" row right below the fold with a Printers icon means I don't have to think hard, one scroll and I'm basically there.

## What's weak

- The hero is 100% laptop/PC energy — two women working on laptops by a window. Nothing here says "printer guy" or "ink subscriber" to me.
- No HP Rewards callout anywhere above the fold. I'm a member, and there's nothing acknowledging that or showing me a balance/points hook.
- "Shop" as button copy is generic — shop what, exactly? Doesn't tell me if it applies to printers at all.
- No sign this page knows I've bought from them before — no recently-viewed, no "reorder your Instant Ink" nudge, nothing personalized.

## Recommendations

- Swap in a rotating hero panel (or dot #2/3 in that carousel) that's printer/ink-specific for returning Instant Ink subscribers — something like "Never run out of ink" instead of generic Back to School.
- Put an HP Rewards points balance or "Welcome back" strip right under the nav — I'm already logged in half the time, use that.
- Change "Shop" to something like "Shop Back to School Deals" so the CTA tells me what I'm clicking into before I tap.
- Move the Printers/Cartridges tile up or duplicate it as a small promo strip since that's my actual reason for visiting.
