# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- This is HP going straight for the jugular on printer subscription — a guy literally standing on a pile of smashed printers, which honestly made me snort because I've been *there* in my head. The HP All-In Plan pitch is front and center, promising a new printer plus ink delivery plus 24/7 support for $7.99 a month. As someone who's deep into Instant Ink and has replaced two printers when the subscription math stopped making sense, this homepage feels like it was written about me.

## What's working

- **The smashed-printer hero image** is genuinely funny and disarming — a dude in a tie standing triumphantly on a pile of destroyed equipment. It communicates "we know printers are a nightmare" without saying it that bluntly.
- **The price anchor of $7.99/month** is visible immediately. For someone evaluating whether Instant Ink is still the right call, a bundled all-in price is exactly the comparison I want to make.
- **The copy brevity** — three lines of body text — respects my time. I'm not reading a wall of bullet points on my phone while standing in the home office.
- **Clean mobile layout**: the HP logo, nav icons, image, copy, and CTA all fit without clutter. Nothing is fighting for space.

## What's weak

- **"Learn" is a terrible CTA verb.** I already know about printers. "See Plans," "Get Started," or even "Start at $7.99" would tell me what happens when I tap it. "Learn" sounds like a Coursera button.
- **No HP Rewards hook.** I'm logged into HP somewhere in my account history. This page treats me like a stranger. No "Check your Rewards balance," no "HP+ members save more" — nothing. That's a miss.
- **No specifics on the printer itself.** "Get a new printer" — which one? Is it a color inkjet? A laser? I have a color photo printer at home and I need to know if this plan applies to what I actually use. The vagueness creates friction.
- **Zero trust signals.** No review counts, no "X million subscribers," no money-back mention. For a $7.99/month subscription ask from a company whose ink pricing has frustrated me in the past, I need a little more reassurance.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Learn" with "See All-In Plans"** — tells me exactly what the tap delivers and echoes the offer language already on screen.
- **Add a one-line HP Rewards callout** below the CTA: "HP Rewards members earn points on every plan." It costs nothing to display and it's the hook that makes me feel recognized rather than cold-prospected.
- **Name a printer model** in the subtext or show a product shot alongside the smashed-printer chaos — "Includes the HP OfficeJet Pro" gives me something concrete to compare against what I already own.
- **Drop a credibility line** under the button: "Trusted by 2M+ subscribers" or "Cancel anytime" — something that defuses the subscription skepticism a Gen X guy like me always has.
