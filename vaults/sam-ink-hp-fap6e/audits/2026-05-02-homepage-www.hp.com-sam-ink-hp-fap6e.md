# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- HP opened with the one thing they actually know about me: printers are a pain in the ass, and I've proven it by swapping machines every time my Instant Ink situation goes sideways. The imagery is funny in a Gen X, Office Space kind of way — dude standing on a pile of smashed printers with a baseball bat — and the offer underneath is basically a direct pitch at my exact situation. This feels less like a homepage and more like an ad that intercepted me right when I needed it.
- **9/10**

## What's working

- **The hero image** — a guy in a dress shirt standing on destroyed printers holding a baseball bat is immediately legible to me. That's my relationship with printers. No copy needed.
- **"HP All-In Plan" label** — putting the product name above the headline grounds it. I know immediately this isn't just venting, it's a product pitch.
- **The headline itself** — "Printer problems? We've got a plan for that." is direct and colloquial. Doesn't oversell. Doesn't use jargon.
- **The value proposition line** — "Get a new printer, ink delivery, and 24/7 support from $7.99/month" answers the three questions I always have: what do I get, does it include ink, and what happens when it breaks. All in one sentence.
- **$7.99/month anchor** — specific price, upfront, no asterisk drama at this stage. That's rare.

## What's weak

- **"Learn" CTA** — weakest possible button copy. Learn what? "See plans" or "Get started" does real work. "Learn" feels like a stall.
- **No social proof above the fold** — I'm a Gen Xer with medium tech comfort. I'm not signing a subscription without knowing someone else didn't get burned. No ratings, no subscriber count, nothing.
- **No mention of HP Rewards** — I'm enrolled. If the All-In Plan accrues points or interacts with my existing account, I can't tell. That's a missed hook for a returning customer.
- **No visible scrollable content** — the page feels truncated. I have no signal that anything else exists below. No peek of the next card, no motion, nothing pulling me down.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Learn" for "See plans & pricing"** — specificity closes the anxiety loop for anyone on the fence about a subscription commitment.
- **Add a one-line trust stamp under the CTA** — something like "Join 10M+ HP All-In subscribers" or "Cancel anytime" in small muted text. Subscriptions make Gen X nervous; a single line de-risks it.
- **Surface an HP Rewards callout** — even a small "Earn rewards on every plan" badge near the price. I'm already in the loyalty program; remind me this plan works with it.
- **Add a partial-reveal of the next section** — crop the top edge of whatever follows so I can see there's more. Right now the page feels like it ends at the fold.
