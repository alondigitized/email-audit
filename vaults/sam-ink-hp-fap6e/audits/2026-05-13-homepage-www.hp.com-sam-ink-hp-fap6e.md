# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- HP's homepage is running a clean Memorial Day Sale push — bold headline, actual percentage savings called out, and a "Shop" CTA that's hard to miss. The focus is clearly on hardware (I can see an all-in-one desktop and what looks like a 2-in-1 laptop), which makes sense for the sale season. As someone who's here mainly about printers and Instant Ink, this landing doesn't speak to me directly — but the sale energy is at least credible and not cluttered.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline** — clear, timely, and the 72% savings figure grabs attention fast. I know immediately there's a reason to be here right now.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — this is actually relevant to me. Home Care Packs often include ink/service bundles, so that line caught my eye even if it's buried in the body copy.
- **"Free shipping"** callout in the offer copy — I don't have to hunt for it; it's right there in the first paragraph.
- **"Financing options available for all credit scores"** — practical, no-nonsense, Gen X approved. Not flashy, just useful.
- **Clean layout on mobile** — no layout shift, no overlapping elements, single-column reads naturally top to bottom.

## What's weak

- **No printer or ink angle anywhere above the fold** — I came to hp.com because I care about my printer and Instant Ink subscription. Nothing on this screen addresses that. I'd have to go hunt.
- **No HP Rewards hook** — I'm a loyalty member. There's no "X points waiting" or "member pricing unlocked" signal. That's a missed win for a returning customer.
- **"Shop" CTA is too generic** — Shop what? Laptops? Printers? All of it? I have no idea if tapping it gets me anywhere near ink or printers.
- **Hero image is all desktop/laptop hardware** — the monitor and 2-in-1 are clearly the featured products. Nothing for the home printer segment.
- **No navigation category shortcuts visible** — on mobile, the hamburger is there but no quick-tap category tiles (Printers, Ink, Laptops) that would let me self-route fast.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Printers & Ink" quick-link tile below the hero CTA** — the sale is fine but give me a one-tap path to my category right on the hero screen rather than forcing me into a full nav dig.
- **Surface HP Rewards / Instant Ink status for logged-in users** — even a small "Welcome back, Sam — 1,240 points" strip at the top would make me feel like this page knows me.
- **Swap the generic "Shop" button for "Shop the Memorial Day Sale"** or at minimum add a second CTA like "Shop Printers" so the two biggest product lines split the traffic intentionally.
- **Reference Instant Ink in the promo copy** — "30% on HP Home Care Packs" is close but vague; say "30% on Instant Ink plan upgrades and Home Care Packs" and you've just made the offer feel personal to subscribers like me.
