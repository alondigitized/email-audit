# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- I opened hp.com expecting to see some printer deal or maybe an Instant Ink offer, and instead I'm staring at a press release about enterprise software. The whole above-the-fold screen is HP telling me they won a Gartner award for "Digital Employee Experience Management Tools" — that's not why I'm here. This reads like a B2B landing page, not a consumer hardware homepage.

## What's working

- **Clean mobile layout** — the HP logo, nav icons (search, cart, account, hamburger), headline, and CTA button all render crisply at phone width. No overlap, no truncation.
- **Clear visual hierarchy** — even though the content is wrong for me, I can tell at a glance what the page wants me to do (tap "Learn"). The type scale is bold and readable.
- **Blue "Learn" CTA** — it's obvious, tappable, and contrasts well against the white background. The button shape is clean.

## What's weak

- **Wrong audience, wrong hero** — "HP Workforce Experience" and "Digital Employee Experience Management Tools" is corporate IT buyer language. I'm a guy with a photo printer at home. HP makes great printers and this page acts like that's not even a business line.
- **No Instant Ink hook** — I'm literally an active subscriber and there's not a single mention of ink, Instant Ink, or supplies above the fold. That's a miss.
- **No HP Rewards presence** — I accumulate points and that loyalty program is one of the main reasons I stay with HP. Nothing here acknowledges I might be a returning customer.
- **"Learn" CTA goes nowhere I care about** — the button copy is fine but it routes to an enterprise software pitch. That's a dead end for me.
- **Hero image is B2B dashboards** — I'm looking at what appear to be analytics UI screenshots. Completely irrelevant to a color printer owner.
- **No concrete offer** — no percentage off, no free ink promo, no bundle deal. Nothing to make me feel like opening the site was worth it.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a consumer-focused offer** — even a 20%-off printer deal or a "Your next ink cartridge is covered" Instant Ink hook would immediately speak to the majority of people who type hp.com on their phone.
- **Surface HP Rewards in the header or a sticky banner** — something like "You have 1,200 HP Rewards points — here's what they'll get you" creates instant re-engagement for returning buyers like me.
- **Add a product category strip below the hero** — four tappable tiles: Printers, Ink & Toner, Laptops, Accessories. That one row would let me route myself in under two seconds.
- **Rotate enterprise content off the homepage or relegate it to a secondary slot** — if HP wants to chase CIOs, great, but don't lead with Gartner Magic Quadrant copy on the main consumer URL.
