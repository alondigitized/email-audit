# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a Memorial Day Sale hard — it's front and center, no ambiguity. The homepage looks like a hardware store right now: desktops, laptops, and a "save up to 72%" hook that grabs attention immediately. As an Instant Ink subscriber who mostly cares about printers and ink, the homepage is pitching me the wrong aisle, but at least it's clean and I can tell a sale is on.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline** — no ambiguity. I immediately know there's a sale and why. That's the one thing I need to decide whether to keep reading.
- **"Save up to 72%"** — a real, specific number. Way better than "big savings" or "don't miss out."
- **Free shipping call-out** — buried in body copy but still there, which matters to me since I'm not going to a store.
- **Financing for all credit scores** — as a Gen X guy who's bought a few printers and laptops over the years, that's a reassuring line. No gatekeeping.
- **Clean mobile layout** — single-column, no clutter, loads fast. I'm not fighting the page.

## What's weak

- **No Instant Ink or printer mention anywhere** — I'm literally an HP ink subscriber and the homepage doesn't acknowledge that category exists. The hero is all desktops and laptops.
- **No HP Rewards hook** — I have points sitting somewhere in my account. Not a single callout telling me my balance, that I'm earning, or that member pricing exists.
- **"Shop" CTA is too generic** — where am I going? Shop what? Printers? Laptops? The sale generally? One step removed from useful.
- **"Select products" fine print in the lead paragraph** — it's subtle but I notice it. 72% off select products could mean almost nothing applies to what I actually want.
- **Hero imagery is desktop-heavy** — all-in-one PC and laptop on the screen. No printer, no ink, no accessories. For a company whose Instant Ink program is a major subscription business, that feels like a miss for returning customers.

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin loyalty/Instant Ink strip above the hero** — something like "HP Rewards member? Your points are waiting." or "Instant Ink subscribers: check printer deals →" It's one line and retains subscribers who might otherwise bounce.
- **Split the "Shop" CTA into two tappable options** — "Shop PCs" and "Shop Printers & Ink" so I'm not dropped into an undifferentiated sale page. This costs almost no design real estate on mobile.
- **Make the CTA copy more specific** — "Shop Memorial Day Deals" or "Shop Printers on Sale" is actionable. "Shop" alone is lazy.
- **Add a trust anchor under the CTA** — a single line like "Free returns · HP Rewards points on every order" would do a lot to confirm I'm in the right place before I tap.
