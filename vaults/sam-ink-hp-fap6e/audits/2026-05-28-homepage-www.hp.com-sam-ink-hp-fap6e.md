# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- HP is clearly running a Memorial Day push here, and the sale numbers are aggressive — up to 72% off sounds like they mean business. But as someone who came to this page because I care about printers and Instant Ink, this hero is talking past me entirely: it's all desktops and laptops, nothing that says "hey, your ink subscription situation is sorted here." Feels like a big-box circular that buried the category I actually shop.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline** — bold, instantly legible, sets the context. I know exactly what season I'm shopping in.
- **"Save up to 72%" plus free shipping** — those two together in one sentence are a quick gut-check reason to keep reading instead of bouncing.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — as someone who maintains HP gear, that line caught my eye. It's more specific than the headline number and actually relevant to my world.
- **"Financing options available for all credit scores"** — I've bought printers and laptops outright, but having this stated upfront is low-friction reassurance if I'm eyeing a larger purchase.
- **Page renders cleanly on mobile** — no layout jank, no overlapping elements, loads looking sharp.

## What's weak

- **Zero printer or Instant Ink presence in the hero** — if I'm an HP loyalist specifically because of the ink subscription, the page gives me no reason to think HP knows that about me.
- **"Shop" CTA is completely generic** — clicking it sends me to... Memorial Day sale? Printers? Laptops? No idea. "Shop Printers" or "Shop the Sale" would tell me more.
- **No HP Rewards hook** — I have HP Rewards. There's no balance, no member badge, no "you've got X points" nudge anywhere above the fold. That's a missed re-engagement opportunity.
- **Hero imagery doesn't connect to me** — an all-in-one desktop and a laptop/convertible are not what I came here for. The runner-in-the-field screen art is forgettable.
- **No urgency deadline** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies an end date but nothing on screen says when it ends. A "Ends May 26" line or even "this weekend only" would sharpen it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a printer/Instant Ink tile below the hero** — even a small "Ink & Printers" category card with the Memorial Day discount applied would catch me immediately and tell me my category is in the sale.
- **Swap "Shop" for "Shop the Sale" with a sub-label like "Printers, PCs & More"** — takes 5 minutes and immediately reduces ambiguity for someone like me who's wondering if my category qualifies.
- **Surface HP Rewards for logged-in visitors** — a one-line "You have X Rewards points — use them this weekend" banner above the hero would turn a returning customer into an active buyer.
- **Add a countdown or explicit end date** — "Sale ends May 26 at midnight" next to the CTA would give the Memorial Day framing actual urgency teeth.
