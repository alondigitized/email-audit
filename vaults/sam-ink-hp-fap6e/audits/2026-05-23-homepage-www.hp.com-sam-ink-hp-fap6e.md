# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, HP is running a Memorial Day Sale — I can see that right away, big headline, decent discount numbers. But I came here as someone who lives and dies by my printer and Instant Ink subscription, and what I'm looking at is a pretty desktop all-in-one and a laptop. Classic HP move: the homepage is always pushing the hardware you don't necessarily need instead of talking to the customer base you've already got.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale headline** lands immediately — I know what the page is doing before I even read a word of body copy. Clean.
- **"Save up to 72% and get free shipping"** — two things I care about, stacked in one sentence. Free shipping on printer supplies matters to me.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — this is actually the most relevant thing on the page for someone like me. I didn't buy a care pack with my last printer and regretted it. That line caught my eye.
- **Mobile layout is tight** — logo, search, cart, account, hamburger all where I expect them. No fumbling.
- **Financing for all credit scores** — I appreciate that they disclosed this upfront rather than burying it after you've already picked a product.

## What's weak

- **No mention of printers, ink, or Instant Ink** anywhere above the fold. I'm the subscriber who keeps HP's recurring revenue alive and the homepage doesn't even nod at me.
- **"Shop"** is one of the laziest CTA labels I've seen. Shop what? The sale has multiple product categories — give me a direction.
- **No HP Rewards callout** — I have points sitting there. If you reminded me I had rewards balance, I'd be halfway to adding something to my cart already.
- **The hero product** is a beautiful all-in-one desktop. I'm not in the market for a desktop. The laptop peek in the corner doesn't help either. Nothing printer-adjacent in sight.
- **No urgency deadline** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies Monday, but there's no "ends Monday May 26" or countdown anywhere. I have no FOMO.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Instant Ink / ink & toner strip below the hero** — one horizontal banner that says something like "Stock up: 30% off ink & paper during the Memorial Day Sale." That's the version of this page I actually want.
- **Surface HP Rewards balance in the nav or a logged-in banner** — even a generic "Check your Rewards balance →" link next to the account icon would pull me in.
- **Give the CTA button real copy**: "Shop Memorial Day Deals" or even "Shop the Sale" — two extra words that tell me where I'm going.
- **Add a hard deadline to the hero**: "Sale ends Monday, May 26" right under the headline. You've already got the seasonal hook — finish the urgency play.
