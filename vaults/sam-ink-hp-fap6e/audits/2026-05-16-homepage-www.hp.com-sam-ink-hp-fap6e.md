# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- HP's homepage is running hard on Memorial Day — "save up to 72%" is the big swing, and they're pairing it with free shipping and HP Home Care Pack discounts. It reads like a big-box sale event, which isn't a bad thing for a guy like me who buys direct from HP when there's a legit deal. The hero product shots — an all-in-one desktop and what looks like a convertible laptop — are clean and recognizable, but nothing here is speaking to my Instant Ink life specifically.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline + 72% off claim** — big, bold number stops the scroll. I'm a value buyer, and 72% off makes me curious enough to at least tap.
- **Free shipping callout** — right in the body copy, no hunting for it. For direct-to-HP orders I always want to know this upfront.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — this is the most on-persona signal on the page for me. I've been burned by printer issues; a discounted care pack is genuinely interesting.
- **Financing for all credit scores** — not my thing personally, but it signals HP is trying to close the deal at every income level, which means prices here are accessible.
- **Mobile render** — clean, no layout breakage, the all-in-one hero image and the convertible thumbnail are both crisp.

## What's weak

- **No Instant Ink / ink subscription mention anywhere** — I'm an Instant Ink subscriber and HP's homepage says nothing about it. That's a miss for retention.
- **"Shop" CTA is too vague** — where does it go? Deals page? Everything? "Shop Memorial Day Sale" would at least set expectations.
- **No HP Rewards hook** — I have HP Rewards points and there's zero mention of member pricing or loyalty status. A returning direct buyer gets no love here.
- **The all-in-one desktop hero** — I'm not in the market for a new desktop right now. The lifestyle shot (person running in a field, visible on the monitor screen) is a bit random and doesn't connect to how I use HP gear at home.
- **"Select products" and "select HP Home Care Packs"** — double "select" in the same paragraph starts to feel like asterisk territory before I've even clicked.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Instant Ink / ink module above the fold or just below** — even a small banner strip: "Already on Instant Ink? Check your plan deals." That's direct revenue retention.
- **Swap the generic "Shop" button for "Shop Memorial Day Deals"** — small copy change, big clarity gain. I'd know exactly where I'm landing.
- **Surface HP Rewards balance or a "member pricing" badge** — I'm logged in presumably; show me something personalized. Even "You have X points — use them today" would keep me engaged.
- **Add a dedicated printer/ink tile below the hero** — a quick-tap row for Printers, Ink & Toner, and Home Care Packs would let me get to my actual use case in one tap without scrolling through laptops.
