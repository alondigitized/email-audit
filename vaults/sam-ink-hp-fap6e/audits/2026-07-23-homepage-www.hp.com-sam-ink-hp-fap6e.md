# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a Back to School push hard right now — that's the entire above-the-fold story. There's a 72-hour flash sale banner up top, and the hero is all about helping students find gear. As a 49-year-old guy who subscribes to Instant Ink and replaces printers when the subscription acts up, none of this is aimed at me — I'm not buying a laptop for a kid today, and I see zero mention of ink, printers, or my rewards account.

## What's working

- **72 Hour Flash Sale banner** — "Save up to 60%, free shipping storewide, financing for all credit scores" packs three reasons to click into one strip. The "Shop" hyperlink is obvious. This would catch me if I were already in the market for something.
- **Clean mobile layout** — Logo, search, cart, account, and hamburger are all where I expect them. No clutter. I can find the nav in one tap.
- **Page renders fast and clean** — no jank, no layout shift, no broken assets visible. HP's mobile execution here is solid.

## What's weak

- **Back to School hero is the wrong story for me** — I'm 49, no kids in school, here to check ink pricing or snag a deal on a replacement printer. This hero sends me the message that HP's homepage isn't for me today.
- **Zero Instant Ink / ink subscription presence** — I'm literally a subscriber and I see nothing that acknowledges that relationship. No "Manage your Instant Ink plan" or "Check ink status" hook.
- **No HP Rewards callout** — I have an account. If my rewards balance were visible, I'd be way more likely to engage. It's not there.
- **Generic "Shop" CTA** — "Shop" could mean anything. It goes nowhere specific from what I can see. "Shop Printers" or "Shop Ink" would immediately tell me if this visit is worth my time.
- **Student lifestyle photo** — Young woman studying at a coffee shop table does not resonate with a home-office Gen X guy who color-prints photos and documents.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a printer/ink lifecycle message** — something like "Your Instant Ink subscription, upgraded" or "Best home printers on sale now" with an actual printer product shot. Back to School gear push belongs in a secondary banner, not the full hero.
- **Add a returning-customer module just below the nav** — "Welcome back, Sam — you're on Instant Ink. Check your plan or shop ink accessories." HP has the login data to do this; they're not using it.
- **Make the flash sale banner more specific to print** — "Up to 60% off printers, ink & paper — free shipping" would stop someone like me cold. Right now it reads as a catch-all.
- **Replace the generic "Shop" CTA with a split of two category buttons** — "Shop Printers" and "Shop Ink" side by side. One tap to my category, done.
