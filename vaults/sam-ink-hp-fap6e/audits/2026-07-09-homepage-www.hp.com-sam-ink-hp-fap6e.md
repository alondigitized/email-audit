# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- HP's homepage is running a 4th of July Sale and leading with PCs and monitors — a laptop and an all-in-one sitting side by side in a clean white product shot. As someone who comes to HP.com specifically for printers, ink, and the occasional accessory, this homepage doesn't speak to me at all. I feel like I walked into the wrong aisle at Best Buy.

## What's working

- **"4th of July Sale" headline** — big, bold, seasonally relevant. I see it immediately and know there's a deal.
- **"Save up to 71% + free shipping" subtext** — two concrete value props in one line. Free shipping especially matters to me on bulkier printer purchases.
- **HP Home Care Packs callout** — the 30% off protection plan mention is smart. I've bought these before. It's buried under the hero but it's there.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no hamburger menu fighting the content, no pop-up ambush, page loads without jank.

## What's weak

- **Zero printer or ink presence** — I'm an Instant Ink subscriber and my whole relationship with HP is through ink. The hero might as well be a Dell ad for how relevant it is to why I'm here.
- **"Shop" CTA is meaningless** — Shop what? PCs? Ink? Cases? This is the least specific call-to-action possible. Where am I going when I tap it?
- **No HP Rewards acknowledgment** — I'm signed in (presumably) and the page doesn't know I exist. No rewards balance, no "welcome back," no member pricing. That's a miss for a returning customer like me.
- **No navigation categories above the fold** — the hamburger is my only path to printers. That's one extra tap I shouldn't have to take.
- **No urgency signals** — "4th of July Sale" but no countdown, no "ends July 15," nothing to make me act now vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the PC-centric hero for a split hero** that gives ink/printer customers a lane — even a secondary strip below the main hero that says "Instant Ink subscribers: manage your plan or shop ink here" would recapture returning customers like me.
- **Replace "Shop" with a specific CTA** like "Shop Sale" or "See All Deals" — or better, split it into two buttons: one for PCs and one for Printers & Ink, since those are clearly different buyer journeys.
- **Surface HP Rewards balance or a member-only price** above the fold for logged-in returning customers — even a small "Your Rewards: 450 points" badge would make me feel seen.
- **Add a deadline** to the sale — "Ends July 13" costs nothing to add and turns a passive browser into an active shopper.
