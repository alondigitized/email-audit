# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- HP is leading with Memorial Day deals on desktops and all-in-ones — big headline, clean layout, straightforward offer. As someone who comes to HP.com mostly for printer supplies and Instant Ink stuff, the whole above-the-fold is basically selling me things I'm not shopping for right now. Feels like a solid sales event, just not one aimed at me.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline** — big, legible, immediate context. I know exactly what season and what the hook is without reading a word of body copy.
- **Dual-product hero shot** — showing a desktop and what looks like a laptop/tablet in the same frame gives a sense of breadth without clutter.
- **HP Home Care Packs callout** — "30% on select HP Home Care Packs" is the one line that speaks even slightly to someone who owns HP hardware and thinks about protecting it.
- **Free shipping mention** — baked into the subhead, not buried. I don't have to hunt for it.
- **Financing for all credit scores** — I appreciate that they stuck this in. It's not condescending, just practical.

## What's weak

- **No printer or Instant Ink anywhere above the fold** — I'm an HP Instant Ink subscriber. I've bought multiple printers from HP. The homepage has zero visual or copy acknowledgment that printers are a product category. A Memorial Day sale on ink or printer hardware would've had me immediately.
- **"Shop" CTA is the vaguest possible button** — shop *what*? Desktops? All accessories? It doesn't pull me into anything specific.
- **HP Rewards is invisible** — I'm in the loyalty program. My balance, my tier, a member-only deal — none of it is surfaced. The account icon is up there but that's it.
- **No urgency signal** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline, but there's no end date, no countdown, nothing that makes me feel like I need to move now versus next week.
- **The hero feels like a PC store, not an HP ecosystem play** — HP sells a lot more than desktops. No printers, no accessories, no ink. If I'm a new visitor I might not even know they make printers from this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a printer/ink promo strip above or below the hero** — something like "Instant Ink members: 20% off ink & paper this Memorial Day" with a direct link. Takes maybe a day to ship and immediately makes me feel seen.
- **Swap "Shop" for a split CTA or secondary link** — "Shop PCs" as the primary is fine, but add "Shop Printers & Ink" as a secondary text link below. Don't make me dig into the hamburger menu to find my category.
- **Surface HP Rewards in the nav or subhead** — even a small "HP Rewards member? See your exclusive deals →" line above the hero works. I log in more if I know there's something waiting for me.
- **Pin a deadline to the sale** — "Ends May 26" or a simple countdown chip next to the headline. Memorial Day has a known end date; use it.
