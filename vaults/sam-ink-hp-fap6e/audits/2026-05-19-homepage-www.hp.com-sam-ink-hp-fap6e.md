# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a Memorial Day Sale push and they're leading with desktop PCs and laptops — fine if I'm in the market for a new machine, but I came here primarily thinking about printers and ink, and this above-the-fold experience doesn't speak to that at all. The sale copy is decent and the page is clean, but there's zero acknowledgment that I'm an existing HP customer with an Instant Ink subscription and Rewards points sitting there. Feels like a storefront that doesn't recognize me.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline** — big, bold, unmistakable. I know immediately there's a sale on.
- **"Save up to 72%"** — a specific, aggressive number that stops the scroll. Even if the top of the range is on niche items, that's a real hook.
- **"Free shipping"** — no threshold mentioned, which reads as a clean offer. Good for the Sam Ink brain that hates calculating minimums.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — this is actually relevant to me. I do care about protecting equipment. It's buried in body copy but it's there.
- **"Financing options available for all credit scores"** — low-pressure, inclusive framing. Doesn't feel like a hard sell.
- **Clean mobile layout** — HP logo, search, cart, account, hamburger menu. No clutter, no broken UI.

## What's weak

- **No printer or ink category presence above the fold** — the hero hardware is a sleek all-in-one desktop and what looks like a convertible laptop. Zero printers, zero ink. I'm an Instant Ink subscriber on hp.com and the page doesn't acknowledge that product line at all.
- **"Shop" CTA is generic** — tapping this goes... where? The printer section? The laptop section? I have no idea. Compare to "Shop Memorial Day Deals" or "Shop Printers & Ink" — much better.
- **No HP Rewards hook** — I have Rewards points. Show me my balance or at least a "member bonus" callout. This is an owned loyalty program and there's no trace of it above the fold.
- **No urgency signal** — Memorial Day implies a deadline but there's no date, no "ends Monday," no countdown. The urgency is implied, not stated.
- **Product imagery is PC-forward** — the visual emphasis on AIO desktops and convertibles is fine for that shopper, but it visually erases the printer/supplies category that represents a significant chunk of HP's repeat buyers.

## Recommendations

- **Add a second hero tile or promo strip explicitly for printers and ink** — "Memorial Day Printer Deals + Save on Instant Ink" would land directly for me and a lot of HP's most loyal repeat customers.
- **Replace the generic "Shop" button with a category-split CTA** — "Shop PCs" and "Shop Printers" side by side, or at minimum a nav strip below the hero with tappable category chips.
- **Surface HP Rewards in the header or a persistent banner** — even a simple "You have X points" or "Earn 3X Rewards this weekend" gives returning customers a reason to stay on the page rather than going to Amazon.
- **Add a deadline to the Memorial Day copy** — "Ends Monday, May 26" costs nothing and adds real urgency without feeling like a dark pattern.
