# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- HP's opening shot is dead-on — a guy in a dress shirt standing on a pile of smashed printers with a baseball bat. That's basically my entire relationship with printers in one image. The HP All-In Plan pitch is exactly the kind of thing I've been half-researching since my Instant Ink subscription started acting up last fall. Feels like a company that knows its customer. That said, the rest of the page is invisible to me above the fold — one hero, one vague "Learn" button, and that's it.

## What's working

- **The printer-smashing hero image** — Gen X visual humor, immediately relatable. I've wanted to do exactly that to my last three printers. Stops the scroll cold.
- **"HP All-In Plan" label + headline combo** — names the product and addresses the pain point in two lines. No confusion about what this is.
- **$7.99/month anchor price** — gives me a number before I have to click anything. That matters. I don't like being teased into clicking just to see pricing.
- **"ink delivery, and 24/7 support"** in the subtext — that's the value stack I actually want to know about as an Instant Ink customer comparing plans.

## What's weak

- **"Learn" CTA** — for a subscription pitch, "Learn" sends me to an education page, not a sign-up flow. Should be "Get Started" or "See Plans." I want to act, not go to school.
- **No HP Rewards acknowledgment** — I have an account and probably points sitting there. A returning customer hook ("You have 450 points") would have me engaged immediately. Nothing here signals they know I exist.
- **Zero navigation categories visible** — I might want to grab a new printer or a replacement cartridge while I'm here, but the only option above fold is to "Learn" about one plan. No quick-jump to printers, ink, or accessories.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "as seen in," no "over 1M subscribers." For a subscription product asking for a monthly commitment, that matters.

## Recommendations

- **Change "Learn" to "See Plans & Pricing"** — tells me exactly what I get when I tap, reduces friction, and moves me toward a decision rather than more reading.
- **Add an HP Rewards "You're logged in / X points" chip in the nav** — even just seeing my account recognized creates loyalty pull. I check my rewards balance more than I check my email.
- **Add a second CTA line below the button** — something like "Already an Instant Ink customer? Compare plans →" This captures upgraders like me who are specifically here because something changed with their current subscription.
- **Surface 2-3 thumbnail category links below the hero** — Printers / Ink & Toner / Laptops — so people who aren't here for the All-In Plan have an obvious next step without opening the hamburger menu.
