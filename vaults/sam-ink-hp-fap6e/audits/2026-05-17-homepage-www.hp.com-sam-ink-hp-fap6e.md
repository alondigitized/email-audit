# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a Memorial Day Sale and leading with it hard — desktops and a convertible laptop front and center, big bold headline, one button. It looks clean and competent, like a retailer that knows how to run a sale event. What it doesn't do is acknowledge that I'm an existing Instant Ink subscriber who came here looking for printer deals, ink bundles, or HP Rewards — there's zero of that above the fold.

## What's working

- **"Memorial Day Sale" headline with 72% off claim** — that number is big enough to stop a scroll. I notice it immediately and it reads like a real sale, not filler.
- **"30% on select HP Home Care Packs"** — as someone who thinks about printer protection and service plans, this actually lands for me. It's a niche detail that shows HP is bundling value beyond just hardware.
- **"Financing options available for all credit scores"** — useful if I'm about to drop $400 on a new all-in-one. No-judgment framing is appreciated by someone my age who's watched financing pitches get condescending.
- **"Free shipping"** mentioned directly in the body copy — I always look for this before I even think about converting.
- **Clean mobile layout** — logo, four nav icons, big hero image, clear text block, one button. No clutter. HP has gotten better at mobile over the years.

## What's weak

- **No printer in the hero** — I'm an Instant Ink subscriber and the whole page is desktops and laptops. If I'm coming back to check on a deal for my home office printer setup, I have to dig for it.
- **"Shop" is the most generic CTA possible** — where does it go? The whole Memorial Day sale? A landing page? A specific category? I have no idea, and I'm not confident it'll drop me somewhere relevant.
- **Zero HP Rewards callout** — I'm in the rewards program. No acknowledgment of that above the fold feels like a missed retention play. Even a small "Earn points on every purchase" badge would have me feeling seen.
- **No urgency signal** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline but there's no date, no countdown, nothing. I don't know if this runs through the weekend or ends tonight.
- **Hero product imagery is aspirational but generic** — the all-in-one monitor shows a person in a field. That's beautiful photography but says nothing about why I'd buy *this* computer.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Printers & Ink" category chip or banner below the hero** — even a small row of three tappable categories (Printers, Ink & Toner, Laptops) would let returning customers self-sort fast without digging into the hamburger menu.
- **Surface HP Rewards in the header or just below the hero** — a single line like "HP Rewards members earn 2× points during Memorial Day Sale" costs nothing and would have me tapping immediately.
- **Replace "Shop" CTA copy with something specific** — "Shop the Sale" or "See All Deals" at minimum. Better: "Shop Printer Deals" as a secondary CTA alongside the main one.
- **Add an end date to the sale headline** — "Memorial Day Sale — Ends May 26" is a tiny change that creates real urgency without being manipulative.
