# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- HP is going straight for the jugular with "Printer problems? We've got a plan for that." — and honestly, as someone who's cycled through more printers than I'd like to admit whenever Instant Ink starts acting up, that headline hits. The homepage feels focused, not cluttered. HP is clearly pushing the All-In Plan subscription hard, and they've built the whole above-the-fold experience around it. Clean, confident, and surprisingly on-point for who I am.
- **7/10**
- + hero copy explicitly speaks to my persona — "Printer problems?" is literally my life, and the HP All-In Plan pitch (printer + ink delivery + support) maps directly to my Instant Ink relationship
- − no returning-shopper hook — no HP Rewards balance, no "Welcome back," no loyalty callout above the fold
- + concrete offer visible — "72 Hour Flash Sale. Save up to 62% on select products, free shipping storewide"
- + primary CTA visible — "Shop" link in the promo banner is present, though it's text, not a button
- + visual hierarchy is clear — promo strip → hero image → headline → sub-copy reads in logical order
- + no render bugs — page is clean, no overlap, no broken images
- + demographic signals match — middle-aged office worker guy in the hero is basically my archetype
- + page reflects a live campaign — "72 Hour Flash Sale" feels active and time-sensitive, not stale
- − loyalty/membership benefits not visible — HP Rewards is nowhere above the fold
- + offer feels honest — "up to 62%" is the usual vague ceiling, but free shipping storewide is stated plainly

## What's working

- **"Printer problems? We've got a plan for that."** — this is exactly the right hook for someone like me. No jargon, no corporate speak. Pure Gen X problem-solution framing.
- **The hero image** — a guy in office clothes standing on a pile of smashed printers with a baseball bat is darkly funny and relatable. I've felt that way myself after an Instant Ink billing cycle went sideways. It communicates frustration AND resolution at the same time.
- **The 72 Hour Flash Sale strip** — it's the first thing I read. "Save up to 62% on select products, free shipping storewide" plus financing for all credit scores is a real offer, not a placeholder. That's a reason to keep scrolling.
- **"From $7.99/month" sub-copy** — clear, low-friction price anchor. I can immediately evaluate whether this is worth upgrading from my current Instant Ink tier.

## What's weak

- **No HP Rewards anywhere** — I'm an HP customer. I have points. There's zero acknowledgment that I'm a returning buyer with a loyalty relationship. That's a missed warm-welcome opportunity.
- **"Shop" as the only CTA** — it's a text link in a promo strip, not a button. The All-In Plan headline below doesn't have a visible CTA button in this viewport. I'd have to scroll to find out how to actually sign up for it.
- **No product specifics** — I can't tell which printers are in the Flash Sale, or whether the All-In Plan applies to a printer I'd want. Nothing anchors the claim to a real SKU.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — four icons across the top. No quick-tap category shortcuts for Printers, Ink, or Accessories. For someone who knows exactly what they want, this adds friction.

## Recommendations

- **Add an HP Rewards balance or "Welcome back" chip in the nav bar** — even a small "Your rewards: 240 pts" would make returning customers feel recognized and nudge them toward redemption.
- **Replace the text "Shop" link with a real button** — something like `Start for $7.99/mo` directly under the All-In Plan headline, above the fold on mobile, makes the conversion path obvious.
- **Anchor the Flash Sale to 2–3 specific products** — show a printer model with a crossed-out price and the sale price. "Up to 62% off" is too vague; a specific deal like "HP OfficeJet Pro 9015e — was $249, now $129" would convert faster.
- **Add one printer/ink category shortcut chip below the hero** — a row of tappable pills (Printers | Ink & Toner | Accessories) would let me skip navigation entirely and get to what I need in one tap.
