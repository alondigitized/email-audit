# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a 4th of July Sale push — laptops and all-in-ones front and center, discounts up to 71%, free shipping thrown in. As someone who buys printers and ink, not computers, this hero doesn't speak to me at all. Feels like a solid consumer electronics promotion aimed at anyone who might be in the market for a new PC, but I'm not, so it reads like a billboard on the wrong highway.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 71%" headline** — that number stops the thumb. Even if I'm not shopping PCs, I clock that and wonder if accessories or printers are also on sale.
- **Free shipping callout** — this is an instant yes-credit for me. I hate paying for shipping on ink cartridges and accessories.
- **"Financing options available for all credit scores"** — smart inclusion. Gen X still thinks about this; not everyone has perfect credit, and calling it out removes a mental barrier.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no hamburger-menu chaos, the logo, cart, and search are all right where I expect them. Took me zero seconds to orient.
- **Blue CTA button** — high contrast, easily tappable on a phone. No ambiguity about what to press.

## What's weak

- **Hero is entirely PC-focused** — I came here as an Instant Ink subscriber. Seeing laptops and monitors as the lead story feels like walking into my hardware store and getting pushed toward power tools when I just need paint.
- **No Instant Ink hook anywhere** — this is a massive miss for a returning HP customer. My subscription is the stickiest relationship I have with this brand, and it's invisible above the fold.
- **"Shop" is the laziest CTA copy possible** — shop WHAT? For me? Printers? PCs? The button tells me nothing about where I'll land.
- **HP Home Care Packs mention feels out of place** — I just bought a printer, not a new PC. That upsell is for someone else's journey, not mine.
- **No rewards or account acknowledgment** — I'm an HP Rewards member. Zero indication that logging in would surface my balance, my subscription status, or a personalized deal.

## Recommendations

- **Add an Instant Ink / supplies secondary banner strip** — a narrow bar below the hero saying "Instant Ink subscribers: your ink is on its way / shop compatible cartridges" would immediately tell me this homepage knows I exist.
- **Swap "Shop" CTA for something category-specific** — at minimum "Shop the Sale" so I know I'm going to a curated sale page, not the homepage root. Better yet, test "Shop Printers & Ink" vs "Shop PCs" based on logged-in account type.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards chip in the nav or top bar** — a single line "Hi Sam, you have 240 HP Rewards points" converts returning customers faster than any hero image.
- **Add a second hero tile or carousel dot for printers/ink** — a swipeable second slide targeting consumables buyers would cost almost nothing to ship and would catch people like me who showed up for ink, not a laptop.
