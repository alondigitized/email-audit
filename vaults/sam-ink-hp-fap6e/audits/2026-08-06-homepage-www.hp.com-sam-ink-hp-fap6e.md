# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- I tapped into HP's homepage expecting to see printers, ink, maybe a laptop deal — instead I got a big photo of a classroom full of twenty-somethings on laptops and a blue button with nothing written on it. If I didn't already know this was HP, the logo in the corner is the only thing telling me. Whatever they're pushing right now, I can't tell you, because the page isn't telling me either.

## What's working

- The HP logo and top nav icons (search, cart, account, menu) are clean and recognizable — at least I know I'm on HP's site and where the basic tools are.
- The carousel dots at the bottom tell me there's more content coming if I swipe, so the page isn't a total dead end.

## What's weak

- **The primary CTA button is blank.** Solid blue rectangle, no label. I have no idea what tapping it does — buy something, learn something, sign in?
- **No headline or body copy anywhere above the fold.** Whatever campaign this hero image was built for, the message didn't load.
- **The hero image is a classroom scene** — a woman presenting to students on laptops. That's not me. I'm not shopping for a school fleet, I'm refilling Instant Ink and maybe eyeing a printer replacement.
- **Nothing about printers, ink, or rewards is visible**, which is the whole reason I come to HP's site in the first place.

## Recommendations

- Fix the CTA button text — right now it's shipping broken to real customers, and that alone will tank conversions.
- Swap the classroom/education hero for something that speaks to home-office and small-business buyers — a printer on a desk, a person printing photos, anything closer to my world.
- Surface an Instant Ink or rewards-member callout above the fold — I'm already in the program, give me a reason to check my status or reorder.
- Put an actual headline on the hero so I know what HP wants me to do before I even reach the button.
