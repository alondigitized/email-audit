# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- HP's homepage is running a 4th of July Sale push — clean, minimal, and product-forward. Two white desktop/laptop devices are front and center, with a bold discount headline below. As someone who buys direct from HP and tracks their Instant Ink relationship carefully, this looks like an HP I know: corporate-clean, not flashy. Right now they're selling PCs and care plans, not printer ink — which is both fine and slightly beside the point for me.

## What's working

- **"Save up to 71%" headline** — that's a big number. Even if "select products" waters it down, the eye locks on 71% and I'm at least reading the next line.
- **Free shipping callout in the same sentence** — they baked it into the offer copy, not buried below. Good move.
- **Clean product imagery** — the laptop + monitor pairing reads as "home office setup," which is exactly my world. White finish feels premium without being intimidating.
- **Single "Shop" CTA button** — no decision fatigue. One thing to tap. I respect the restraint.
- **HP Care Packs mention** — I've seen these upsell pitches before and honestly it's smart to plant the seed in the hero. 30% off a protection plan gets my attention as someone who's already bought multiple printers direct.

## What's weak

- **No printer or ink angle anywhere** — I'm an Instant Ink subscriber. I buy printers. HP's homepage doesn't know or care. Zero ink-specific signal above the fold.
- **"Select products" caveat** — 71% sounds incredible until you remember that "select products" almost always means two refurbished models from 2023. Feels like bait.
- **No account/loyalty hook** — I'm an HP Rewards member. I'm logged out, no prompt, no "sign in to see your deals." That's a miss on a returning customer.
- **Generic "Shop" CTA** — Shop what? PCs? Ink? Accessories? A button that says "Shop Sale" or "See Printers" would do real work here.
- **No price anchoring on a specific product** — showing the device but no price means I can't quickly gauge if this sale is real or marketing math.

## Recommendations

- **Add an ink/printer pathway above the fold** — even a small secondary CTA like "Instant Ink subscribers: see your deals →" would stop me from bouncing to Amazon.
- **Personalize for returning users** — if I'm logged in or they recognize my email from Instant Ink, surface my plan status or rewards balance in the header. HP has the data; use it.
- **Sharpen the CTA copy** — "Shop 4th of July Sale" or "See All Deals" beats the blank "Shop." Tell me where I'm going.
- **Show one real price** — anchor the sale with a specific product and its before/after price. "HP OfficeJet Pro: was $199, now $129" would make the 71% claim credible instead of suspicious.
