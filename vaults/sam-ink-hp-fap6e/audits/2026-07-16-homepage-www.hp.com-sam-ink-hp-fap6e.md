# Homepage snapshot · www.hp.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- HP is running a Back to School push right now — full stop, that's the whole story above the fold. The page looks clean and professional, which is what I expect from HP, but as someone who's here about printers and Instant Ink, not helping a college kid pick a laptop, this feels like I knocked on the wrong door. It's a big brand doing seasonal marketing, and I'm not the season.

## What's working

- **"Back to School guide" headline** — big, readable, instantly understood on a phone screen; HP nailed the type size here
- **Single blue "Shop" CTA** — no choice paralysis, one button, I know what to tap
- **Clean mobile layout** — hero image is not cluttered, text is not overlapping anything, renders fast
- **Seasonal alignment** — July is exactly when Back to School should be running; at least HP isn't serving me Christmas in March

## What's weak

- **No printer or ink signal anywhere** — I'm an Instant Ink subscriber and I can't see a single nod to printers, ink plans, or my product category above the fold
- **"Your best school year starts here"** — I graduated 27 years ago; this copy literally has nothing to do with my life
- **HP Rewards is completely absent** — I have an account (the person icon is there) but there's no rewards balance, member offer, or loyalty callout to pull me in as a returning customer
- **No specific savings** — "savings for every budget" is the kind of copy that means nothing; give me 20% off, a dollar amount, something real
- **Young female student hero image** — nothing wrong with the image, but it's 100% not aimed at me; there's no secondary hook for anyone outside that profile

## Recommendations

- **Swap the static hero for a personalized returning-customer strip** — if I'm logged in via the account icon, surface my Instant Ink plan status and a "reorder or upgrade" CTA; this costs HP nothing and converts loyal customers immediately
- **Add a secondary promo banner below the hero** — something like "Instant Ink members: 3 months free on plan upgrades" would stop me from bouncing straight to the hamburger menu
- **Replace "savings for every budget" with an actual number** — "Up to 30% off Back to School picks" gives me a reason to tap; vague copy gives me nothing
- **Put a small HP Rewards balance callout in the nav or just below it** — even a "Your Rewards: 450 pts" line next to the account icon would make me feel seen as a returning shopper
