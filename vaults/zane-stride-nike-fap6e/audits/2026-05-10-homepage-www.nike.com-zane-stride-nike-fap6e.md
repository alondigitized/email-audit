# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Nike's homepage is doing what Nike does best right now — leading with a performance narrative, not a discount. The Pegasus 42 campaign with "SAME WORKHORSE. NEW WINGS." is exactly the kind of shoe-release storytelling I'm here for, and the outdoor editorial vibe is on point. But as a Member who logs miles daily and buys through the app, there's zero loyalty signal, zero personalization, and zero reason to feel like this homepage knows who I am.

## What's working

- **"SAME WORKHORSE. NEW WINGS." hero** — punchy, punchy, punchy. This is Gen Z runner copy. It respects that I know the Pegasus lineage without over-explaining it.
- **Pegasus 42 name-drop in the subtext** — "Get inside the mind—and under the feet—of Ethan Strand and Pegasus 42." I know Ethan Strand is a Nike athlete. That specificity hits different than a generic "the all-new shoe."
- **Dual CTA: Shop + Watch** — the "Watch" button is smart for a runner who's going to want to see the campaign film before buying. That's exactly how I research gear.
- **Outdoor editorial photography** — trail setting, natural light, someone in a post-run pose. Matches the NRC-app-wearing, Strava-posting energy. This isn't a studio product shot.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, the swoosh stays out of the way, the hamburger menu doesn't intrude on the hero.

## What's weak

- **Zero Member personalization** — I'm logged in (or at least should be recognized as a returning user) and the page is completely anonymous. No "Hey Zane," no rewards points balance, no member-exclusive callout. That's a miss for a Nike Member.
- **"Shop" CTA is too generic** — for a product-launch hero this specific, the button should say "Shop Pegasus 42" not just "Shop." I don't want to land on a generic PLP.
- **No free-shipping mention** — as a Member, free shipping is one of my main perks. Not seeing it here means I can't use it as a conversion nudge.
- **No category navigation exposed** — the hamburger menu hides everything. If I want to jump to Running > Men's Shoes in one tap, I can't. I have to commit to opening the menu.
- **Carousel with no content preview** — I can see four dots below the hero suggesting multiple slides, but I have no idea what's on the other slides. No thumbnail strip, no text preview. Could be lifestyle, could be another shoe drop I'd care about.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop" button copy to "Shop Pegasus 42"** — the hero is specific enough to earn a specific CTA. Land me on the Pegasus 42 PDP or filtered PLP, not the full catalog.
- **Add a Member strip directly below the hero** — a thin bar reading something like "You're a Member. Free shipping + member pricing unlocked." Costs almost nothing to render, converts people like me who are already bought in.
- **Surface the carousel content** — show thumbnail chips or a short text label ("Pegasus 42 / Men's Running / Air Max Dn") below the dots so I know what I'm navigating into.
- **Expose a Running category tab in the sticky nav** — even just "Running" next to the hamburger would let me skip the menu entirely. One tap is the standard I expect on the Nike app; the site should match.
