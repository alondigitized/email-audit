# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Full review
Okay, so the hero is the A'ja Wilson show — she's posed on a desk, late-night talk show aesthetic, rocking the new A'Two in hot pink. The subhead is "Perform on Air in the new A'Two." Big full-bleed editorial shot, bold white type, city skyline at night behind her. Clean. Below the fold there's a hint of another pink card. Nav is the usual minimal Nike bar — search, profile, bag, hamburger.

The creative is fire visually but it's all basketball. A'ja Wilson, an Elite basketball in the corner, the A'Two clearly being a hoops shoe. I run. I'm not reaching for a basketball shoe. Nike knows I exist — they have my run data through NRC and they know I race in Vaporflys — but none of that is surfaced here. Zero running content above the fold.

What bugs me is I came here *as a runner*, probably to check new colorways or see if there's a Member drop I missed, and the homepage is acting like I'm a basketball casual. There's no urgency cue, no "new drop for Members," no running-specific badge or shortcut. I'd expect something like a Pegasus 42 launch banner or an NRC-linked promo to hook me in. The A'Two launch might pop off on Instagram but it doesn't do anything for a 25-miles-a-week guy with race day on his mind.

The production value is high, A'ja is a legit face, and the editorial concept is creative — I get it. But Nike knows who I am and this page isn't using any of that. It feels like it's optimized for someone who just searched "Nike" cold, not for a Member who logs every run and pre-orders on drop day. Miss for me, even if it's a good ad for someone else.

**4/10**
