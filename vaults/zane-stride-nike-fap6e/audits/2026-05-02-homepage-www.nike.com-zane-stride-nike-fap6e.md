# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Nike's homepage right now is a full A'ja Wilson moment — late-night show aesthetic, pink hoop shoes, all attitude. As a brand move it's clean and confident. But I came here as someone who just finished a 10-miler and wants to know if the new Vaporfly colorway dropped, and this tells me nothing about that. It's a basketball campaign landing page dressed as a homepage.
- **4/10**

## What's working

- **The A'ja Wilson hero shoot** — production quality is genuinely high. The desk-on-rooftop late-night conceit is a vibe, and the pink A'Two pops hard against the dark city skyline. It's scroll-stopping even if it's not for me.
- **Minimal nav chrome** — search, member icon, bag, and hamburger. Clean. I know exactly where my account is without hunting.
- **"Perform on Air" subhead** — nails the tech-to-lifestyle bridge in four words. Short, punchy, Gen Z cadence.

## What's weak

- **Zero runner signal above the fold** — NRC, ZoomX, Vaporfly, marathon training, Strava connect — none of it. If I weren't a loyal member I'd bounce immediately thinking this site isn't for me.
- **"Explore" as the sole CTA** — soft. I'm on my phone at 7am post-run. I want "Shop Running" or "Member Drops." "Explore" is what you put when you don't know what action you want me to take.
- **No member recognition or personalization** — I'm logged in (there's a member icon in the nav) but the hero is completely cold. Nike has my run data, my purchase history, my preferred categories. None of that is being used here.
- **No urgency or drop indicator** — if there's a new member-exclusive dropping today or this week, I can't see it. That's the primary reason I open the Nike app cold.

## Recommendations

- **Slot a personalized "Because you run" module below the hero** — even a thin strip showing my recent NRC stats + a shop-running CTA would bridge the disconnect between the campaign hero and my actual intent.
- **Swap "Explore" for a split CTA row** — "Shop Basketball" next to "Shop Running" so both audiences aren't forced through the same funnel.
- **Add a member drop ticker or badge** — a sticky pill at the top ("🔴 Member drop live: Air Max Dn8") costs nothing and is the exact reason app-first runners like me check the homepage.
- **Pull in a performance hero module** — even one secondary card beneath the fold showing a Vaporfly or Pegasus collab would signal that the running community has a seat at this table today.
