# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Nike opened with exactly the shoe I'd actually race in — the Pegasus 42 — and the "Same Workhorse. New Wings." line hits the right note for someone who treats their trainer like a tool, not a trophy. The vibe is aspirational trail runner, which is 90% of my weekend aesthetic. That said, the homepage is doing zero work to close me — no member price, no shipping hook, nothing that makes me feel like an NRC member vs. a random visitor.

## What's working

- **"Same Workhorse. New Wings." headline** — short, punchy, no fluff. Speaks directly to runners who already love the Pegasus and want to know why to upgrade. That's me.
- **Pegasus 42 as the hero product** — I run in the Peg series for my easy days. Seeing it front and center tells me the site knows what I'm here for before I even scroll.
- **Dual CTA ("Shop" + "Watch")** — the "Watch" button is a smart addition for someone like me who will absolutely watch a 90-second video about Ethan Strand's training block. That's the content-to-commerce funnel working.
- **Carousel indicator + pause button** — tells me there's more content below without being pushy. Mobile-native feel, which I expect from Nike.
- **Lifestyle outdoor imagery** — trail setting, natural light, real runner vibes. Not a studio white-background sneaker shot. This matches how I post on Strava.

## What's weak

- **Zero member recognition above the fold** — I'm a Nike Member. The site has no idea. No "Welcome back, Zane" bar, no member pricing badge, no NRC integration. I feel like a stranger.
- **No performance creds for the Pegasus 42** — "New Wings" is evocative but vague. I want to see "React X foam" or "4.2oz lighter" or something that tells my runner brain why this upgrade matters.
- **"Shop" is ambiguous** — Shop what? The Peg 42? All running? Men's? One tap to the wrong PLP and I'm bouncing.
- **No price, no offer, no shipping hook** — the page does nothing to reduce friction. I know Nike ships free for Members but there's no reminder here. That's a missed conversion moment.
- **Navigation is fully hidden** — hamburger only. I can't see "Running" or "Men's" without a tap. On a homepage this intent-heavy, I want my category one tap away, not two.

## Recommendations

- **Add a slim member-welcome bar** just under the nav: "Member pricing + free shipping unlocked" — one line, keeps me in the funnel instead of opening a new tab to price-compare.
- **Make the "Shop" CTA specific**: "Shop Pegasus 42" not "Shop" — one word change, zero design effort, eliminates ambiguity and drops me on the right PDP.
- **Surface one performance stat in the subheadline** — change "Get inside the mind—and under the feet—of Ethan Strand and Pegasus 42" to something like "Ethan Strand. Pegasus 42. 4% lighter React X." Facts close runners.
- **Expose a sticky "Running" or "Men's Shoes" shortcut** below the hero carousel — a horizontal pill nav (Running / Training / Lifestyle) that stays visible as I scroll would cut navigation friction in half on mobile.
