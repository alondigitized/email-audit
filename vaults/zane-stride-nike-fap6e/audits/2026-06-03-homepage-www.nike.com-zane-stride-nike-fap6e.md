# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Alright, I open Nike.com and I'm immediately hit with a neon green and hot pink soccer campaign — "BREAK 'EM," two footballers in France and Brazil kits running at me, and a split-choice between Mercurial Superfly and Vapor cleats. Nike is clearly going hard on the football (soccer) drop right now. Big energy, very Gen Z visually — but I'm a runner, not a footballer, and this has nothing to do with my Vaporfly, my ZoomX stack, or my next half-marathon.

## What's working

- **Neon split backdrop (green + magenta):** Immediately high-energy, very current Gen Z colorway — stops the scroll even if the sport doesn't match me.
- **"Choose your fast" sub-copy:** Compact, athletic, decision-oriented language. As a runner who picks shoes by race type, that framing actually lands.
- **Dual CTA layout:** "Shop Mercurial" + "Learn More" as distinct pill buttons — hierarchy is obvious, no guesswork.
- **Clean mobile layout:** Nav is minimal (hamburger, bag, search, profile), hero fills the viewport, no clutter competing for attention.

## What's weak

- **Zero running content above the fold:** I'm a Vaporfly owner and NRC daily user — there's not a single running shoe, trail shot, or race graphic visible. This homepage doesn't know I exist.
- **No Member hook:** Nike Membership is one of my main reasons to shop Nike (free shipping, early access). No badge, no "Member Early Access" banner, nothing. That's a miss for a logged-in returning shopper.
- **No offer or pricing signal:** No discount, no "Free shipping for Members," no threshold. I came to browse and there's zero financial pull.
- **Hamburger-only nav:** I can't get to Running in one tap. I have to open the full nav menu just to find my category — friction right out of the gate.
- **"BREAK 'EM" headline is cut off:** The right side of the headline is cropped. On mobile it reads "BREAK 'EM" but part of it disappears into the edge — mildly sloppy.

## Recommendations

- **Swap hero or add a second carousel slide for running:** Even a single running-focused card (Pegasus, Vaporfly, Alphafly) after the soccer hero would capture runners who swipe.
- **Surface the Member benefit in the nav or a sticky banner:** "Free shipping — Member" takes one line. It's a massive conversion driver for the segment Nike already has logged in.
- **Add a one-tap "Running" shortcut below the hero:** A horizontal category pill row (Running · Training · Lifestyle · Football) would cut path-to-category from 3 taps to 1.
- **Show the account/Member state above the fold:** If I'm logged in, replace the generic profile icon with "Hi, Zane" or a rewards-point nudge — that's a personal hook that costs nothing to ship.
