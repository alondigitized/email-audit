# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Nike's leading with the USMNT right now — playing card-themed hero, "2026 jerseys" push, very World Cup energy. It's a clean, confident creative execution and I get what they're doing — the 2026 World Cup hype machine is real. But as someone who literally ran 7 miles this morning and has three tabs open for new Vaporfly colorways, this homepage has zero for me above the fold. Nike feels huge right now, just not huge in a way that talks to runners.

## What's working

- **Playing card creative for USMNT** — it's visually punchy, and as a Gen Z guy I notice it. The aces/face-card framing with Tyler Adams and the squad is legitimately cool concept work.
- **"Shop" CTA** — dark button, high contrast, front and center. No confusion about what to do next if soccer jerseys are your thing.
- **Clean mobile render** — the nav bar (search, profile, cart, hamburger) is minimal and familiar. Zero clutter at the top. Feels like an app, which I respect.
- **Current campaign timing** — tying to 2026 is the right bet in May 2026. Nike knows the cultural moment.

## What's weak

- **Zero running content above the fold** — I'm a runner. No ZoomX, no Pegasus, no training gear. This entire viewport is jerseys for a sport I watch but don't shop for.
- **No Member/loyalty hook visible** — I have Nike Membership and free shipping is literally why I buy here. That's completely invisible from this screen.
- **"Shop" is a dead-end CTA for me** — it's one tap to a soccer jersey PDP or collection, not a running shoe. The button is clear but the destination isn't mine.
- **No offer** — "Rep your team" is vibes, not value. No discount, no threshold, no exclusivity signal. Nothing that triggers the "I need to act now" feeling.
- **Carousel with 5 dots** — there are more slides below but I have no idea if any of them are relevant to me. The pause button (bottom right) tells me it's auto-advancing, which I find annoying.

## Recommendations

- **Add a runner-coded slide to the carousel rotation** — even if the USMNT hero is first, a ZoomX/race-day slide at position 2 would catch me before I bounce. I can see there are 5 slides — use one for running.
- **Surface the Member free-shipping badge in the nav bar or just below it** — one line: "Free shipping for Members. You're in." That's all it takes to remind me I have a reason to stay.
- **Give the CTA destination context** — change "Shop" to "Shop Jerseys" so runners know they can swipe past without missing their category. Specificity keeps intent clear.
- **Pin a thin promotional strip above the hero** — even a single line like "New ZoomX colorways just dropped" with a tap-through would segment the audience and stop runner-me from bouncing.
