# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Nike opens strong with a cinematic Cristiano Ronaldo hero — dramatic orange lighting, bold type, full-bleed mobile. It's a premium brand flex that screams athlete-first. Right now they're clearly pushing a CR7 seasonal campaign, not performance running, so as a guy who opened this app looking for my next training kick, the first screen isn't exactly talking to me.

## What's working

- **The CR7 hero image** is genuinely cinematic — that orange backlit profile shot stops the scroll. Even if it's not my category, it communicates "this brand does big things"
- **"HIS LEGEND LIVES ON" copy** is punchy and emotional in a way Gen Z athletic branding should be — bold caps, no filler words, no corporate fluff
- **Button placement** is perfect for mobile — "Shop Cristiano Ronaldo" sits right at comfortable thumb-tap range above the carousel dots
- **Clean render** — full-bleed image, no cumulative layout shift, no jank visible

## What's weak

- **Wrong sport for my session** — I opened this to check if anything dropped in running. CR7 is a footballer. The entire above-the-fold viewport gives me zero signal that Nike carries ZoomX or has anything for my next long run
- **Member benefits buried or absent** — I have Nike Member free shipping. That's literally why I shop here over other sites. Not a single mention of it on this screen
- **No secondary nav visible** — just a hamburger menu. One tap to Running isn't there; I have to dig
- **No urgency that means anything to me** — "his story isn't over yet" is vague. Is there a limited CR7 drop? A restock? Give me a reason to move fast
- **Four carousel dots** suggest there's more, but autoplay without a clear reason to swipe means most people won't — and whatever else Nike is promoting is invisible

## Recommendations

- **Swap carousel slide 1 for a running/training hero on days after major drops** — or at minimum serve a personalized first slide to returning Members based on category history; a 26-year-old runner who's bought ZoomX before should see a running hero, not a global campaign
- **Add a sticky Member chip in the header** — a small "Member" badge next to the account icon instantly signals that free shipping + exclusive access is active, converting Members into repeat buyers without a full section
- **Make the hamburger expand into a visible category strip** — even a slim "Men | Running | Training | SNKRS" pill bar under the nav would let me tap Running in one move instead of hunting
- **Give the CR7 campaign a deadline or a hook** — "Limited CR7 colorway drops July 12" outperforms "his story isn't over yet" every single time for driving taps
