# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Opened Nike.com expecting member drops or something race-season relevant, and the full viewport is a NikeSkims collab pushing "feminine new colors, prints, and silhouettes." I'm a dude who tracks splits on Strava — this is not my aisle. Nike is clearly in full brand-collab mode right now, but whoever served me this on mobile either doesn't know I'm logged in or just doesn't care.

## What's working

- **Clean, minimal nav bar** — swoosh, search, bag, profile, hamburger. App-native layout, feels native. Zero friction to get to search if I know what I want.
- **Hero renders sharp and fast** — no layout shift, imagery loads fully, the floating-bodies art direction is visually arresting even if it's not for me.
- **"Shop" CTA is unambiguous placement-wise** — centered, rounded pill, white-on-dark, clearly tappable. Good mobile tap target.
- **Carousel pagination dots + pause button** — shows there's more content below/next, gives me a reason to swipe instead of scroll away entirely.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is women's product** — "feminine new colors, prints, and silhouettes" in the subhead kills any relevance for me in the first three seconds.
- **Zero running signal above the fold** — no ZoomX, no NRC integration, no race-season callout, nothing that signals Nike knows I'm here for performance.
- **No member hook** — I have Nike Member status and get free shipping. There's no "Member" badge, rewards balance, or exclusive drop teaser anywhere on screen.
- **"Shop" CTA is vague** — Shop *what*? If that button goes to NikeSkims women's, I've tapped a dead end. CTA copy does no filtering work.
- **No offer or urgency** — no price signal, no "new drop," no deadline. Nothing that makes me feel like now is the time.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero to logged-in Member gender/category history** — if I've bought ZoomX and running gear before, serve me a running hero, not a collab targeted at someone else entirely.
- **Add a Member strip above or below the hero** — even a simple "Free shipping on your next order · Member exclusive" banner keeps the membership value prop in frame.
- **Swap "Shop" for category-specific CTA copy** — "Shop NikeSkims" at minimum so I know what I'm tapping; ideally surface a parallel "Shop Men's Running" CTA for non-target visitors.
- **Add a race-season urgency hook** — it's May, marathon and half-marathon season is live. A "Race Ready" or "New ZoomX drop" carousel slide would catch my attention immediately on swipe.
