# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Nike hits the door with a full-bleed Mbappé drop — "RECORDS SHATTERED. 58 and counting." The glitch-art treatment is loud and intentional, and for a second it actually stops me mid-scroll. This is Nike in full hype machine mode: celebrity athlete, abstract visuals, zero product specifics. As a runner who logged 6 miles this morning and already has my eye on the next Vaporfly colorway, this is speaking my language of performance but not quite my sport.

## What's working

- **"RECORDS SHATTERED. 58 and counting."** — punchy, aggressive, economy of words. This is how Gen Z reads. I didn't need three paragraphs to understand the vibe.
- **The glitch-art visual** — chromatic aberration, melting colors, face distorting under pressure. Honestly it's a stop-the-scroll moment. Nike's clearly commissioning real creative direction here, not a stock photo.
- **Dual CTA pattern** — "Shop Mbappé" and "Watch ▶" together. Lets me buy immediately or go deeper if I want the story. That's a smart split for impulse vs. considered shoppers.
- **Search icon with sparkle/AI indicator** in the nav — that small signal tells me this is a modern, tech-forward experience, which tracks with how I like to shop.
- **Carousel with pause control** — visible dots and pause button means I'm in control of the cadence. No forced autoplay that hijacks my session.

## What's weak

- **Mbappé is a soccer player** — I'm a runner clocking 25 miles a week obsessing over VO2 max. There's zero running content above the fold. I have to go find my section.
- **No Member hook anywhere** — I pay for Nike Membership specifically to get free shipping and early access. Not a single mention of it here. Missed upsell on loyalty I've already opted into.
- **No price signal, no offer** — not even "Free shipping for Members." Feels like a brand campaign, not a store. I can't act on vibes alone.
- **Nav is icon-only** — four icons (search, account, bag, hamburger). No exposed category links like "Running," "Men's," or "New Arrivals." One extra tap to get where I'm going.
- **Sub-copy "Hungry for more..." feels generic** — cool line for a football highlight reel but tells me nothing about what I'm shopping for or why now.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Member/loyalty strip** just below the hero — even one line: "Nike Members: free shipping + early access to drops." I already converted; remind me why that matters.
- **Add a running-focused carousel slide** — swap or rotate in a ZoomX or Pegasus frame. One hero for Mbappé fans, one for people like me who opened this app to restock race-day gear.
- **Make the search bar text-expanded instead of icon-only** — a placeholder like "Search running shoes, Vaporfly, Jordan..." instantly maps the product catalog for me without a tap.
- **Inject product context into the CTA** — "Shop Mbappé's Mercurial" instead of just "Shop Mbappé" so I know before I tap whether it's footwear, apparel, or both.
