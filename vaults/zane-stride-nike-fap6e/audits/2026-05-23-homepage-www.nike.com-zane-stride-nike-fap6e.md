# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Nike's homepage is leading hard with the Mad 90 Pack — a soccer-forward, street-culture campaign built around Air Max 90 colorways. The visual is raw and Gen Z coded (skull-and-crossbones flag, tatted guys, washed orange), which gets my attention fast. But as someone who came here to check if my Vaporfly restock dropped or to browse member deals, there's zero running intel above the fold.

## What's working

- **The aesthetic is dialed in for me**: skull flag, tattoos, washed orange — this is Gen Z streetwear energy. I'm not scrolling away immediately.
- **Dual CTA format ("Shop the Collection" + "Watch ▶")**: giving me both a buy path and a content path is smart. I actually might tap Watch first.
- **Bold, minimal hero copy**: "MAD 90 PACK" in all-caps white over a dark image — instant read, no clutter.
- **Carousel dots**: I can tell there are 4 slides, which signals more to explore — keeps me from assuming this is all Nike has going on right now.
- **Sparkle icon on search**: looks like there's an AI search feature baked in. That's a high-tech-comfort win for me specifically.

## What's weak

- **Zero running content**: I came here as a runner. No Vaporfly, no NRC integration, no "for members" race-day drop. Above the fold this might as well be a soccer site.
- **No membership or loyalty hook**: I have a Nike Membership and free shipping — none of that is surfaced. Biggest missed retention signal for a returning buyer.
- **No offer or price signal**: not even a "Members: Free Shipping" strip. No reason to buy *now* vs. later.
- **"Rediscover Nike Soccer's most iconic eras"** — "Rediscover" is soft copy for what could be a fire drop. The skull flag is way more urgent than the headline.
- **No personalization visible**: Nike has my data (NRC, past purchases, member status) and none of it is showing up.

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin member/loyalty strip above the hero**: even "Members: Free shipping on every order" in a black bar costs nothing and immediately signals value to returning shoppers like me.
- **Rotate the hero carousel to surface running content for known runners**: if Nike knows I use NRC, slot the Vaporfly Next% or a race-day drop into position 2 of the carousel automatically.
- **Sharpen the hero subhead**: swap "Rediscover Nike Soccer's most iconic eras through the Air Max 90" for something shorter and more visceral — the visual is doing all the work; the copy is playing it safe.
- **Make the Watch CTA video autoplay inline**: tapping a "Watch" button and getting redirected somewhere feels dated. Show me a 10-second loop right here.
