# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Opened the app and Nike hit me with NikeSkims — like, okay, I get it's a big collab, but I'm here to find my next training shoe and instead I'm looking at a bunch of figures lying on the floor in crop tops. This homepage reads fashion-first, women's-first, not "fastest guy in his run club"-first. Nike clearly has the performance catalog, but none of it is above the fold right now.

## What's working

- **Clean nav bar** — swoosh, search, profile, bag, and hamburger are exactly where I expect them on mobile. Zero friction to get to search.
- **Hero visual has impact** — the top-down overhead shot with multiple figures is arresting. I noticed it immediately, even if it's not for me.
- **"Shop" CTA is unobstructed** — white pill button sits clean against the image, no confusion about where to tap.
- **Page carousel indicator** — three dots signal there's more to swipe through, which at least tells me this isn't the only story Nike is running right now.

## What's weak

- **NikeSkims hero is a complete miss for a male runner** — the copy literally says "feminine" and the entire visual language (soft pastels, reclined figures in bodywear) has zero overlap with my use case or my demographic.
- **No performance hook above the fold** — no ZoomX, no Vaporfly, no NRC mention, no running content whatsoever. I run 25 miles a week and Nike's homepage doesn't acknowledge that at all.
- **"Shop" CTA is too generic** — tapping "Shop" on a NikeSkims hero drops me into a women's collab, not into Men's Running. That's a wasted tap.
- **Zero member/loyalty signals** — I'm a Nike Member, I shop app-only specifically for the perks, and nothing above the fold acknowledges that. No badge, no balance, no exclusive drop teaser.
- **No urgency or offer** — there's nothing pushing me to act now vs. close the app and open SNKRS instead.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for returning male members** — if Nike knows I'm logged in (they do — I use the app-only), the hero should reflect my run history or at minimum serve a men's/running creative. Dynamic hero by member segment is table stakes here.
- **Add a persistent member badge or drop teaser to the nav bar** — even a small "Member Drop: Vaporfly 3 available now" strip below the nav would make me feel seen and keep me in the app.
- **Swap "Shop" CTA copy to match the hero** — if NikeSkims is the story, the button should say "Shop NikeSkims" so I know exactly what I'm tapping into and can decide to skip it or not.
- **Surface a second hero card proactively** — the carousel has 3 slides but on first load it shows the collab most irrelevant to me. Auto-advance or personalize card order so I see the running story without having to swipe.
