# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I open Nike.com and immediately get NikeSkims — women lying on the floor in studio-stretch bodysuits, feminine prints, Kim K collab energy. This is not my world. Nike is clearly pushing the NikeSkims collection hard right now, and as a guy who just wants to check ZoomX drops or see what's new in the run category, this feels like I walked into the wrong store.

## What's working

- **Clean visual hierarchy** — the NIKESKIMS wordmark is massive and the "Shop" CTA is unmissable; Nike knows how to build a hero module
- **Render quality** — the aerial photo loads crisp, no layout shift, no broken images; the mobile experience is technically solid
- **Current campaign** — NikeSkims is a real, active collab; the page isn't showing a stale holiday sale in May

## What's weak

- **Wrong audience lead** — the entire above-fold experience is targeted at women into studio/lifestyle wear; I run 25 miles a week and this has zero pull for me
- **"Shop" CTA is a dead end** — no category specificity; Shop *what*? Clicking it from my perspective is a shot in the dark
- **Zero member / loyalty signal** — I'm a Nike Member, I get free shipping, I chase exclusive drops; none of that is surfaced. The account icon in the nav is the only trace of membership and it's tiny
- **No performance / running hook** — nothing about Vaporfly, ZoomX, running drops, or anything that would make a runner feel seen
- **No offer or threshold** — no free-shipping callout, no member pricing badge, no reason to act *now*

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for returning members** — if I'm logged in (Nike knows I am), swap the hero to a Running category lead or at minimum show a "Back for your run?" banner rail below the hero
- **Add a sticky member-benefits strip** — even a thin bar reading "Nike Member: Free Shipping + Exclusive Access" gives me a reason to stay; it's one line of copy and it's already true
- **Swap "Shop" for a directional CTA** — "Shop NikeSkims" immediately tells me this is a specific collab and lets me self-select out fast; a second button "Shop Running" right next to it would catch the segment Nike is otherwise losing on this screen
- **Surface a navigation shortcut rail below the hero** — a horizontal scroll of icons (Running, Training, SNKRS, New Drops) would let me route myself in one tap without hunting the hamburger menu
