# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Opened Nike's homepage and got hit immediately with a Kylian Mbappé football campaign — big editorial energy, clearly a global brand moment. It feels premium and current, but I'm here for running gear and this page is all football, all the time. The production is clean and the photo is fire, but zero of my actual needs as a runner are addressed above the fold.

## What's working

- **"WHO ELSE BUT KYLIAN" headline** — short, punchy, confident. That's my copy register exactly. Gen Z doesn't need three adjectives. This one lands.
- **Full-bleed hero image** — Mbappé mid-action in match kit fills the entire viewport. High production, no clutter, no competing modules. Respects my attention.
- **Dual pill CTAs** — "Shop France" and "Shop Mbappe" are both visible, distinct, and unambiguous. Two clear paths, no guessing.
- **Clean mobile chrome** — search, profile, bag, and hamburger in the top bar, nothing overlapping. The pause button on the carousel is a small but thoughtful touch — gives me control.
- **Carousel pagination dots** — signals there's more content if I swipe, which keeps me from assuming this is the whole picture.

## What's weak

- **Zero running relevance above the fold** — I'm 25 miles/week, ZoomX on race day, NRC on the watch. This hero is 100% football. Nothing here speaks to my actual shopping intent.
- **No Member hooks whatsoever** — I have Nike Member status and get free shipping. That benefit should be front and center on a return visit, but the page treats me like a cold anonymous visitor.
- **No promotional offer visible** — no discount, no threshold, no "member exclusive." The page asks me to shop but gives me no reason to buy *today*.
- **"Shop France" CTA is confusing** — Am I buying a kit? National team merch? French streetwear? The CTA is clear as a button but opaque as a destination. I have to guess what I'm tapping into.
- **No urgency or scarcity signals** — the carousel is paused-able but there's no "limited drop," no countdown, nothing that makes me feel like I need to act now.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the returning-member hero** — if Nike knows I'm a Member (and their app does), surface a "Welcome back, Zane — new ZoomX drops this week" strip above or below the hero. Even a thin loyalty bar would do it.
- **Swap one of the CTA pills for a running entry point** — "Shop Running" alongside "Shop Mbappe" doubles the funnel relevance without touching the editorial hero. One pill for football, one for my category.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the nav bar or just below** — "Free shipping for Members" as a sticky banner costs nothing and converts people like me who already qualify.
- **Clarify "Shop France" copy** — rename it "Shop France Kit" or "National Team Gear" so I know what I'm tapping before I commit.
