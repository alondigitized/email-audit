# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Opened the app expecting a shot of ZoomX foam or a SNKRS drop and got a cozy fleece ad for back-to-school instead. Reads like Nike is running a general apparel sale right now, not talking to runners at all — no shoe in sight, no member drop, just a hoodie vibe and a discount code.

## What's working

- The promo strip up top ("BACK TO SCHOOL SALE, EXTRA 25% OFF, CODE: DAYONE") in that neon green is impossible to miss — clean above-the-fold offer.
- "Explore" and "Watch" are two distinct, low-friction CTAs, and the "Watch" pill with the play icon signals there's a video/content angle, which fits how Nike usually builds hype.
- Copy tone ("It's just a sweatsuit until it's not") is playful and on-brand for Gen Z — doesn't feel corporate.

## What's weak

- Hero is 100% lifestyle fleece — zero running, zero shoe, zero performance tech. If I'm here for Vaporflys or a training update, this tells me nothing.
- No Member callout anywhere above the fold. I run through the app specifically for Member-exclusive access and free shipping — that's the hook that gets me to actually open Nike over a random shoe site, and it's just not here.
- Imagery is two women in a bedroom — cute campaign, but it's not remotely speaking to a guy who's logging Strava splits and racing in Vaporflys.

## Recommendations

- Swap the studio-fleece lifestyle hero for a running-specific module (or at minimum rotate it into a carousel) — a shot of someone mid-stride in the latest Vaporfly or Pegasus would immediately hook me.
- Surface Member perks (free shipping, early access to drops) in the top promo strip alongside the 25% off code — right now that value prop is invisible.
- Add a "New Running Arrivals" or "SNKRS Drops" rail directly under the hero so a runner doesn't have to dig through the hamburger menu to find their category.
- Make "Explore" specific — "Shop Fleece" tells me exactly what I'm tapping into instead of a vague verb.
