# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Opened Nike and got England football — full stop. The homepage is running a campaign for England's national team with bold athlete imagery and that "MAD ENOUGH TO BELIEVE" headline. Looks clean and the energy is right for the brand, but if I showed up looking to cop a new ZoomX or check my Member drops, this tells me nothing. Nike clearly has a tournament moment happening right now, and they're riding it hard.

## What's working

- **"MAD ENOUGH TO BELIEVE" headline** — punchy, confident, short. Gen Z copy energy, reads instantly on a small screen
- **Full-bleed hero image** — players on a pitch in England kits with clean Nike branding; the visual is dynamic and premium, no stock-photo vibes
- **"Shop England" CTA** — pill-shaped, white, high contrast against the dark image; impossible to miss and unambiguous about where it takes you
- **No clutter** — the nav is minimal (search, account, bag, hamburger), nothing competing with the hero; this respects my attention span
- **Render quality** — no layout shift, no broken elements; loads feeling like a premium brand on mobile

## What's weak

- **Zero running content** — I am literally here because I run. No Vaporfly, no Pegasus, no ZoomX, no race-season messaging anywhere in viewport
- **No Member hook** — I have a Nike Member account with free shipping and access to exclusive drops. Nothing acknowledges that. Not even a profile indicator showing I'm logged in
- **No offer** — "Shop England" is not a deal. There's no urgency, no savings, no member-exclusive price point to pull me in
- **Football is a detour for me** — July is race season. I want to see what's dropping for runners, not football kits I'll never wear
- **Nav requires extra tap** — to reach Running, I need to open the hamburger; no category strip is visible, so my path to product is buried one interaction deep

## Recommendations

- **Add a thin promo strip above the hero** — one line: "Member: Free Shipping on Every Order" or "ZoomX Vaporfly drops this week — Members first." Costs nothing, immediately signals value for signed-in runners
- **Swap or rotate the hero for running during race season** — or at minimum show a dual-story carousel so runners see something relevant within the first swipe; a single-campaign homepage locks out everyone who isn't here for football
- **Surface a category strip below the fold** — Running / Training / SNKRS / Football as horizontal scroll chips, so I can tap Running without opening the full hamburger menu
- **Show my Member status visibly** — a small badge or "Hey Zane" near the account icon tells me Nike sees me as a Member, not a stranger; this is table stakes for retention
