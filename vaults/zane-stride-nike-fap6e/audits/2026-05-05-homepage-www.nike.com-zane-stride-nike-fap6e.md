## What's working

- **Visual clarity:** The Pegasus G shot is genuinely clean — high-key lighting, mud splatter, vivid pink midsole. Even if it's not my shoe, the photography is Nike at its best.
- **Minimal nav chrome:** Top bar is lean — swoosh, AI search (I see that sparkle icon), account, bag, menu. Nothing cluttering my first glance.
- **"For best results, use daily" subhead:** That's a dry, Gen Z-coded joke and I caught it. Copy register feels right for my age group even if the shoe is wrong.
- **Carousel indicator:** Four dots at the bottom tell me there's more to swipe through, so I know not to bounce yet.

## What's weak

- **Golf shoe as hero for an undifferentiated homepage:** If I'm not a golfer (I'm not), the lead product is irrelevant and my thumb is already heading toward the back button.
- **"Shop" CTA is the laziest possible copy:** Shop *what*? Running? Golf? Everything? It tells me nothing about where I'd land.
- **Zero member/loyalty signal:** I get free shipping as a Nike Member. That's a huge hook they're not using. No "Member Exclusive" badge, no rewards balance — nothing that rewards me for being logged in.
- **No running content visible at all:** 25 miles a week and there's not a single road shoe, track flat, or NRC callout anywhere in this viewport.
- **No concrete offer:** Competitors are always dangling something. I see no price break, no threshold, no exclusive-access callout.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero based on Member account data:** I'm logged in (account icon is right there), Nike knows I run. Swap the Pegasus G golf hero for a Vaporfly Next% or Pegasus 41 shot for returning runners. Even a carousel slot 1 swap based on purchase history would move the needle.
- **Surface NRC Member perks above the fold:** A single strip — "Free shipping + member-exclusive drops. You're in." — costs nothing to add and is my #1 reason to stay loyal to Nike vs. going to Road Runner Sports.
- **Change "Shop" to something product-specific:** "Shop Pegasus G" or even "Discover Running Golf" if the crossover is the angle. Vague CTAs lose Gen Z fast.
- **Add a second carousel tile immediately visible to runners:** A "Built for 25-mile weeks" or "Race day ready — ZoomX" hero as slide 2 would catch me before I swipe away. Carousel dots already exist — use them.
