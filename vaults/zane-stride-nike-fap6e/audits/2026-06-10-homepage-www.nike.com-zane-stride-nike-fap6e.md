# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- Nike's mobile homepage is locked into a campaign moment — dark, cinematic, full-bleed video hero, zero noise. "RIP THE SCRIPT / Instinct over everything" hits the right frequency for a 26-year-old who thinks in Strava splits and race PRs. That said, this is brand energy, not a buying surface. There's no running-specific hook, no member pricing, and nothing that tells me a Vaporfly drop or a member exclusive is waiting one tap away.

## What's working

- **"RIP THE SCRIPT" headline** — short, punchy, all-caps. That's exactly the language that makes me stop scrolling. It's not trying to explain itself, which is right.
- **"Instinct over everything." subline** — one sentence, no fluff. This is how I actually think about a race. It earns the vibe.
- **Dual CTA ("Shop" + "Watch ▶")** — I notice the video timestamp "6:01" in the corner. Giving me the option to watch the full creative before buying is a smart engagement play.
- **Dark cinematic visual** — the moody atmospheric frame looks premium, not bargain-bin. Consistent with Nike's top-tier positioning and the kind of thing I'd screenshot.
- **No clutter in the nav bar** — search, profile, cart, hamburger. Clean. No banner ads or pop-ups eating my screen real estate.

## What's weak

- **"Shop" is too generic** — tapping it leads where exactly? Running? Lifestyle? Collab drops? As a runner checking for Vaporfly news, I want "Shop Running" or "New Arrivals" not a blank destination.
- **Zero membership signal above the fold** — I'm a Nike Member. I get free shipping and member exclusives. None of that is surfaced here. That's a miss when loyalty is one of Nike's biggest retention levers.
- **No product specificity** — "Instinct over everything" is a great line for a campaign, but I can't tell if this is about a new shoe, a training kit, or a film. No product image, no SKU hint.
- **No offer, no urgency** — nothing telling me there's a reason to act today vs. next Tuesday.
- **Below-fold content is completely hidden** — the two partial editorial tiles just barely peeking in give me no signal about what's coming. No anchor to keep scrolling.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop" for "Shop Running" or "Shop the Drop"** — even a two-word CTA change would reduce dead-click uncertainty and pull performance-focused shoppers straight to the right category.
- **Add a member strip above or below the hero** — a single line like "Members get free shipping + early access" costs nothing visually and activates one of Nike's strongest retention levers for people who are already in the ecosystem.
- **Surface a product silhouette in the hero** — even a subtle shoe or apparel shadow overlaid on the dark cinematic background tells me what the campaign is selling. Right now it could be a film, a brand manifesto, or a pair of shoes — I genuinely can't tell.
- **Make the below-fold tiles more visible** — crop the first two modules so ~30% of the next content row is visible. Give me something to scroll toward.
