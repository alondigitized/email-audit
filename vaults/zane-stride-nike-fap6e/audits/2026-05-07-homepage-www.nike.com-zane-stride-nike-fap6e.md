# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Nike opens with a trail runner — Ethan Strand, flat on the ground post-effort, laces on what looks like a Pegasus 42 — and the line "SAME WORKHORSE. NEW WINGS." That's a direct swing at my lane. This homepage knows what it is: a brand play first, a selling floor second. It looks like Nike is building hype around the Pegasus 42 launch and using a storytelling format (Shop + Watch dual CTA) to do it.

## What's working

- **"SAME WORKHORSE. NEW WINGS."** — this headline hits. Short, punchy, two-word rhythm. It talks to a runner who already trusts Pegasus and wants to know if the 42 earned its upgrade. That's me.
- **Dual CTA "Shop" + "Watch"** — the Watch option acknowledges I consume run content (I'm on NRC, I watch race recaps), so offering the film alongside the buy is smart. Keeps me engaged even if I'm not ready to purchase in this moment.
- **Ethan Strand reference in the subtext** — name-dropping a real athlete with credibility in the running world signals this isn't a glossy ad, it's a performance story. I respect that.
- **Hero image composition** — the resting runner in dry grass with hills behind looks like a real training day, not a studio shot. That visual register feels authentic to how I'd post on Strava.

## What's weak

- **No member/loyalty hook above fold** — I'm a Nike Member. That membership should be doing work on the homepage: a balance, an exclusive drop badge, an "only for members" callout. I see nothing. The app does this better than the web.
- **"Shop" is a vague CTA** — I'm looking at Pegasus 42 content but the button just says "Shop." Not "Shop Pegasus 42," not "Get the 42." I have to infer it. Small friction, lazy copy.
- **No running category shortcut in the visible nav** — the hamburger menu hides everything. There's no persistent "Running" tab in the visible header. For a site where running IS the identity, that's a miss.
- **Zero price or offer signal** — I know Nike doesn't discount Pegasus at launch but even a "Free Shipping for Members" banner would reduce bounce for someone who's weighing app vs. web.
- **Carousel dots with no category context** — I can see there are two slides but no hint what slide 2 is. No reason to swipe.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member-status ribbon or badge to the hero CTA** — "Shop. Free shipping for Members." One line. Costs nothing to ship, directly rewards the loyalty behavior I'm already doing.
- **Rename the CTA to "Shop Pegasus 42"** — you have the product name in the subtext, carry it into the button. Drops ambiguity, increases tap confidence.
- **Pin a "Running" shortcut in the visible nav bar** — one tap to my category without opening the hamburger. Even a small text link at the nav level does the job on mobile.
- **Give me a peek at slide 2** — show a thumbnail or label on the second carousel dot so I know there's something worth swiping for. "Next: Race-day builds" or similar.
