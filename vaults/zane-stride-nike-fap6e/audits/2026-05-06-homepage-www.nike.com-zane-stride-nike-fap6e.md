# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Bro, Nike just served me a golf shoe on the homepage. I run 25 miles a week — I live in the Pegasus, the Vaporfly, the Invincible — and the first thing I see is the Pegasus G crushing turf on a fairway. The brand presentation is clean and the creative is actually pretty sick, but they completely missed who I am the moment I landed.

## What's working

- **The shoe photography is genuinely excellent** — overhead dynamic angle, turf spray, the hot pink midsole pops against that sky blue background. As a sneakerhead who checks SNKRS, this kind of product hero actually holds my attention.
- **Typography hierarchy is clean** — "BUILT FOR COUNTLESS ROUNDS" in heavy bold all-caps reads instantly on a phone screen; the subhead at smaller weight below it doesn't compete.
- **CTA is frictionless** — the "Shop" pill is centered, white, obvious. One tap and I'm in.
- **Carousel indicator + pause control** — shows there's more content to swipe through, which is the right nudge for discovery.

## What's weak

- **Golf shoe as the hero for a running-heavy audience** — the Pegasus G is a running-inspired golf shoe. The "Countless Rounds" copy and turf imagery signal golf immediately. I'm not on the course, I'm on Strava.
- **Zero Member/loyalty signal** — I'm a Nike Member, I expect to see my status or exclusive-access hooks the moment I open the app. None of that is present above the fold.
- **No offer or incentive** — no free shipping callout, no Member-exclusive pricing badge, nothing that rewards me for being logged in.
- **"Shop" CTA copy is generic** — it doesn't tell me what I'm shopping. "Shop Pegasus G" or "Explore Running" would give me a clearer destination.
- **No navigation rail visible** — there's no tab bar or category strip. The only navigation is a hamburger menu and icons, which means reaching Running is at least two taps away.

## Recommendations

- **Rotate the hero to running for the default view** — if Nike has Member data on me, serve a running-first hero (Pegasus 41, Vaporfly 3, Invincible) rather than a golf crossover product. Even a geo/demographic signal would be better than nothing.
- **Add a Member welcome strip above the hero** — a thin banner reading "Welcome back, Zane · Member Exclusive: Free Shipping on All Orders" costs nothing to add and immediately signals value for logged-in users.
- **Make CTA copy product-specific** — swap "Shop" for "Shop the Pegasus G" or, if the hero rotates, tie CTA copy to each slide's product.
- **Inject a category quick-link row below the hero** — Running / Training / Jordan / SNKRS — give me one-tap access to my category without opening the hamburger.
