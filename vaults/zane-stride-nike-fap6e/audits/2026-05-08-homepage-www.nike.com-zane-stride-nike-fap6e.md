# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Nike is clearly pushing the Pegasus 42 hard right now — this is a straight performance running campaign, no lifestyle fluff. The hero is crisp, the shoe is front and center, and the athlete (Ethan Strand) signals this is for real runners, not hypebeasts. As someone who logs 25 miles a week on Pegasus, this is basically speaking directly at me — I just wish there was more of a member hook to reward the fact that I'm literally already in the app.

## What's working

- **"SAME WORKHORSE. NEW WINGS." headline** — as a Pegasus runner this reads as an upgrade promise, not a rebrand. That lands.
- **Ethan Strand + Pegasus 42 pairing** — naming a specific athlete and specific shoe model (not just "our newest shoe") signals legitimacy to anyone tracking the running world.
- **Two-CTA split ("Shop" / "Watch")** — giving me a transactional path and a content path in the same breath respects that I might want to see a doc before I drop $140.
- **Trail/nature imagery** — not a studio white-background shoot. The field and mountain backdrop scans as lifestyle-authentic for a running-obsessed Gen Z dude.
- **Clean mobile nav** — Swoosh, search, profile, bag, hamburger. No clutter. Everything I need is one tap away.

## What's weak

- **Zero member/loyalty acknowledgment** — I'm a Nike Member, I buy app-only drops, and there's no "Member Exclusive" badge, no rewards balance, nothing that says "we know you." That's a miss when personalization is table stakes in 2026.
- **No price or offer anywhere** — even a "Free shipping for Members" strip would make this actionable instead of just brand-storytelling.
- **"Shop" is vague as a CTA** — shop *what*? In the campaign context it's probably fine, but "Shop Pegasus 42" would make the tap feel purposeful.
- **No urgency or scarcity signal** — a new shoe launch is the exact moment to show "just dropped" or limited colorway framing. None of that is here.
- **Can't reach Men's Running in one tap** — no category strip, everything goes through the hamburger. That's one extra friction step for someone who knows what they want.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Just Dropped — Shop Now" badge or timestamp** to the hero to inject scarcity energy consistent with a new release moment.
- **Swap "Shop" → "Shop Pegasus 42"** — one word change, zero design cost, zero ambiguity about where the tap goes.
- **Surface a Member-exclusive callout strip** immediately below the hero (even a thin banner: "Free standard shipping on all orders for Members") — I'm logged in, reward that.
- **Add a brief product rail below the hero** showing the Pegasus 42 in 2–3 colorways so I can see options before committing to a PDP tap — eliminates the feeling of flying blind into a product page.
