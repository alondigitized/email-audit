# Homepage snapshot · www.nike.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Nike's opening this with heavy aspiration energy — "NEVER IF, SIMPLY WHEN." hits like a motivational poster, and the Back to School promo strip is hard to miss. But the hero is a pre-teen kid in a striped shirt, which tells me immediately this page isn't coded for me. As someone checking SNKRS drops and mapping Strava routes, I'm already half-checked-out before I see a single piece of performance gear.

## What's working

- **Promo banner in neon green** — "EXTRA 25% OFF SELECT STYLES / CODE: DAYONE" is literally the first thing after the nav. Hard to miss, feels like a real offer I'd copy-paste before I even look at product.
- **"NEVER IF, SIMPLY WHEN."** — the copy has that short-punch, athlete-mindset register that resonates with how I think about training blocks. It's not running-specific but it's at least in the right emotional neighborhood.
- **Clean mobile layout** — swoosh top-left, icon row top-right, zero clutter. The page doesn't fight itself visually, which matters when I'm checking on my phone between intervals.
- **Back to School timing is current** — it's late July, the banner is seasonally accurate. That's table stakes but I've seen brands miss it.

## What's weak

- **Hero model is a pre-teen kid** — I'm 26, I run 25 miles a week, I'm tracking pace zones on my Apple Watch. A child in a striped shirt is zero percent my vibe and signals the homepage isn't even thinking about me.
- **No Member / NRC hook above the fold** — I have a Nike Member account, I chase exclusive drops, I use the NRC app daily. The page acts like I'm a cold stranger. Not even a "Welcome back" or a rewards callout.
- **CTA buttons are cut off** — I can see the button shapes at the very bottom of the viewport but the copy is illegible. I shouldn't have to guess what I'm tapping.
- **No performance / running signal** — ZoomX, React foam, training gear, Vaporfly — nothing in the above-the-fold zone signals Nike's running category exists.
- **"One brick at a time."** — slightly cryptic as a sub-headline. It sounds like a kids' sports-motivation poster, not something that gets me amped to drop $200 on race shoes.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to a performance runner segment** — show a 20-something in motion, mid-stride, Vaporfly on foot. "SIMPLY WHEN." lands harder when the image earns it with actual running content.
- **Surface the Member hook in the promo strip** — change one panel of that tri-part banner to "Member Exclusive: Extra 10% stacked" so Member-logged-in users see their loyalty pay off immediately.
- **Make the primary CTA legible above the fold** — bump the hero image up or shorten it so the button clears the viewport edge on a standard phone screen. I shouldn't have to scroll to see what I'm clicking.
- **Add a running category shortcut under the nav** — one horizontal chip rail ("Running | Training | Lifestyle | New Drops") saves me the hamburger-menu dig and keeps a runner like me in the funnel.
