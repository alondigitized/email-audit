# Homepage snapshot · www.saatva.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Saatva comes out of the gate looking like a real luxury brand — not a startup foam brand pretending to be one — and that earns immediate respect. Right now they're pushing a Memorial Day sale hard with a specific dollar figure and a deadline, which is exactly the kind of event I've been waiting to shop. The cookie consent wall cutting across the lower half of the screen is genuinely annoying, but the bones here are solid.

## What's working

- **"Visit a viewing room" CTA** — This is the single smartest above-the-fold choice on the page. I've been burned by ordering a mattress online and hating it for eight years. The fact that Saatva is leading with "come see it in person" before they even try to sell me tells me they understand buyers like me.
- **Memorial Day sale banner** — Specific. "$625 off." A real deadline: May 14. "Best Price Guaranteed." That's the kind of language that makes me stop scrolling and take the sale seriously. I wait for this.
- **"Luxury Mattresses Made Affordable" headline** — Not "sleep better tonight" or some nebulous wellness promise. They said luxury, they said handcrafted, they said comfortable prices. That's a value proposition I can hold them to.
- **The search bar** — Medium-tech-comfort me appreciates that there's a prominent "What can we help you find?" bar. I don't always want to navigate a menu; I want to type "organic cotton" and see what comes up.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is a conversion killer** — It's covering roughly the bottom 40% of the visible hero, including whatever might be below the CTA. I can barely see the background image. On a phone, this is the first thing I'm reading, and it's about data processing. First impressions matter.
- **The hero background image** — I can barely make it out through the blur and the modal overlay. It looks like water or sky — atmospheric but not product-forward. I want to see a beautifully made bed with real ticking fabric. Show me what I'm buying.
- **No mention of coil construction, organic cotton, or white-glove delivery above the fold** — These are Saatva's actual differentiators from every foam startup. The headline could belong to any mattress company. I shouldn't have to scroll to find out why this isn't a Purple or a Casper.
- **No financing teaser** — Saatva mattresses start at $900+. I always read the financing terms twice. There's no "financing available" or "pay over time" callout anywhere visible. That's a miss for a purchase at this price point.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the blurry atmospheric background with a tight lifestyle shot of a real coil mattress** — show the tufted top, the edge support, someone in their 40s actually sitting on the edge of the bed evaluating it. Make the product the hero, not the concept.
- **Add a subhead line to the hero copy**: something like "Handcrafted innerspring, organic cotton — delivered and set up by our team." That single line would separate Saatva from every bed-in-a-box competitor without requiring a scroll.
- **Surface the financing line directly under the CTA button** — even just "Financing from $X/mo." in small gray text. Buyers at this price point are thinking about it whether you mention it or not; surface it first.
- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default on return visits, or at minimum shrink it to a slim bottom bar** — a sticky bottom strip handles consent compliance without nuking the hero experience on mobile.
