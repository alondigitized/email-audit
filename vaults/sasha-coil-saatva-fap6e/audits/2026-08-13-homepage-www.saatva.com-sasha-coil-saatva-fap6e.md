# Homepage snapshot · www.saatva.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- I typed in the URL and got a wall of pop-up before I even saw the mattress. Between the email-capture modal, a cookie banner peeking out underneath it, and a chat bubble in the corner, this reads like a brand that's more interested in my email address than showing me why their coils are worth a showroom trip. I can tell it's a premium sleep brand from the "Official Mattress... of Team USA" badge and the muted sage color palette, but I haven't actually seen the product yet.

## What's working

- **The sale banner up top** — "15% off Classic Mattress Bundle | Ends 8/13" gives me a real number and a real deadline, which is exactly the kind of thing I wait all spring for.
- **"Visit a Viewing Room" in the nav** — this is the one line on the whole screen that speaks directly to my skepticism about bed-in-a-box. I want to touch the coils before I buy, and Saatva knows that.
- **"Official Mattress... of Team USA" credibility badges** — a small trust signal that this is an established, serious brand, not a fly-by-night DTC startup.

## What's weak

- **The email-capture modal covers the entire hero** — I can't see the actual mattress, the actual headline, or any product photography. First impression is a form, not a bed.
- **A cookie consent banner is stacked underneath the modal, half cut off** — "you consent to our and third parties' use of cookies..." with an "I Understand" button competing for my thumb before I've seen anything else.
- **A chat bubble ("We're here to help") is already popped open in the bottom-left**, adding a third layer of clutter on top of the modal and cookie banner.
- **The $50-off modal offer undercuts the 15% banner offer** — two different discounts stacked on each other reads confusing, not generous, and makes me want to read the fine print twice, which I always do anyway.

## Recommendations

- Kill the email modal on first visit — let me see the hero image and headline before asking for my inbox. Trigger it on exit-intent or after a scroll instead.
- Resolve the cookie banner and modal into one clean stack, not two overlapping panels fighting for the same tap.
- Lead with a lifestyle hero shot of a real bedroom setup (or the viewing room itself) so I can see the coil mattress, not just badges and a form.
- Make "Visit a Viewing Room" more prominent — that's the single strongest hook for someone like me, and right now it's a small nav link buried among five others.
