## What's working

- **The promo strip is doing serious work.** "Shop Before 5/21 to Beat The Rush—Memorial Day Sale is Live! | Save up to $625 off" is specific, time-bounded, and dollar-denominated. I don't have to decode a percentage off MSRP I don't know.
- **"Visit a viewing room" as the primary CTA is a credibility move.** It tells me without saying it that Saatva isn't a mattress-in-a-box. I've been burned before. The fact that they have showrooms is a genuine differentiator and putting it in the primary CTA is smart.
- **The hero copy hits my vocabulary.** "Handcrafted mattresses" and "luxury" are words I respond to — and pairing them with "affordable" defuses the sticker shock I expect at this tier.
- **The search bar placement is clean.** Right below the header on mobile, so if I already know what I want (Saatva Classic, innerspring, queen), I'm one tap away from finding it without digging through a hamburger menu.
- **The urgency is real.** Two days left. I'm actually checking this on a Monday evening in mid-May. This is the spring sale window I wait for.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal on mobile is a wall.** It's covering roughly the bottom third of the hero image before I've seen anything. On a phone, that's the main event. Asking me to deal with your data policy before I've registered the brand promise is friction at exactly the wrong moment.
- **"Visit a viewing room" is aspirational, not transactional.** I'm on my phone. I probably won't be driving to a showroom tonight. There's no secondary CTA that says "Shop the Sale" or "See Mattresses" for the majority of visitors who are browsing, not trip-planning.
- **No financing detail above the fold.** I read financing terms twice before any big purchase. I know Saatva has financing but I can't see any of it — not even a "0% APR" callout — from what's visible here.
- **No white-glove delivery mention.** Saatva's delivery is one of their best differentiators. For me, that's a baseline expectation, but for someone deciding between Saatva and a competitor, not seeing it on the hero is a missed signal.
- **Hero image is almost entirely obscured.** The cookie modal blocks it. I get a thin slice of what looks like a coastal lifestyle background. I have no idea if there's a bed, a bedroom, a person — nothing to connect emotionally to the product.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary CTA beside "Visit a viewing room."** Something like "Shop the Sale" that links directly to the mattress collection — so mobile browsers who aren't ready to plan a showroom visit still have a path into the funnel.
- **Move the cookie consent to a slim bottom bar instead of a modal overlay.** A full-width bottom banner accomplishes the same legal requirement without eating the hero on mobile. This is standard on every premium retail site I trust.
- **Surface financing in the hero sub-headline or under the CTA.** One line: "0% APR financing available." That's what I'm calculating before I tap anything.
- **Add "Free white-glove delivery" as a trust line below the CTA button.** It's a Saatva signature and it immediately separates them from bed-in-a-box competitors without taking up much real estate.
