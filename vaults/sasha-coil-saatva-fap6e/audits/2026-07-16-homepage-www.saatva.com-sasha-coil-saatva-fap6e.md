# Homepage snapshot · www.saatva.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened Saatva expecting to land on a premium bedding site — and instead got a popup blocking everything. The entire above-the-fold experience is a modal asking for my email before I've seen a single product, image, or reason to trust them. The brand signals luxury, but this move screams desperation. I came here to evaluate a $2,000 mattress and they opened with a "give us your email first."

## What's working

- **The $50 offer is legible and specific.** "$50 on Your Next Mattress" tells me exactly what I'm getting. No vague percentage games.
- **The modal's visual design is clean and on-brand.** The Saatva wordmark in warm copper, serif typography, and teal CTA button all feel premium — not discount-bin.
- **The email field + single CTA is friction-appropriate.** One box, one button. A Gen X buyer with medium tech comfort can handle this without feeling tricked by a multi-step flow.
- **The X button is clearly visible** in the top right, so I'm not trapped.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire first experience.** I came to evaluate a mattress, not to submit my email to a form. This is exactly backwards for a considered, high-AOV purchase.
- **"Join our email list for expert articles and tips"** — that's a value proposition for a newsletter subscriber, not a $2,000 mattress buyer. I don't care about articles. I care about coil count, organic cotton certification, and white-glove delivery windows.
- **"CONTINUE" is ambiguous CTA copy.** Continue to what? The checkout? The site? A quiz? For a brand selling a premium product, this vague verb does not inspire confidence.
- **The $50 off says "Next Mattress"** — implying I've already bought one, or setting up a condition I can't verify. That phrasing is slightly off and I'd read it twice wondering if there's a restriction I'm missing.
- **Zero trust signals in the modal** — no "365-night trial," no "free white-glove delivery," no star rating. Any of those would make me feel better about handing over my email.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal by default on direct navigation from organic search.** I typed in saatva.com — I already know you exist. Save the email capture for after I've engaged with a product page.
- **If the modal must exist, swap "expert articles and tips" for a trust statement** — something like "Join for early access to our annual sale + white-glove delivery updates." That speaks to me.
- **Replace "CONTINUE" with a specific action verb** — "Claim My $50" or "Get the Offer" eliminates ambiguity and converts better for a considered buyer.
- **Add one trust signal below the button** — a single line: "365-night trial · Free white-glove delivery · No-commitment unsubscribe" would do it without redesigning the modal.
