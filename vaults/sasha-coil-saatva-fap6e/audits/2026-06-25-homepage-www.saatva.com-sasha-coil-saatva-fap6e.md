# Homepage snapshot · www.saatva.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- Saatva is leading with exactly the right brand positioning for someone like me — "Luxury" and "handcrafted" are words I actually believe in, and the Fourth of July sale banner with a concrete dollar amount tells me there's a reason to act now. But there's a mismatch I can't ignore: the hero CTA is "Visit a viewing room," which is the literal opposite of why I'm on my phone instead of at a mattress store. This is a premium brand that knows its audience on paper but hasn't fully translated that to a mobile-first experience.

## What's working

- **"Up to $625 off" banner with the deadline** — "Shop Before 6/25" is today. That creates genuine urgency on my first scroll, not manufactured scarcity.
- **"Handcrafted mattresses, bedding & furniture"** — the word "handcrafted" does a lot of work for a skeptic like me. It signals coil-and-spring construction, not foam poured in a box.
- **"Visit a viewing room" as the hero CTA** — I'm skeptical of buying blind, and this tells me Saatva has physical showrooms. Even if I don't go, it says they stand behind the product enough to show it in person.
- **Search bar front and center** — at medium tech comfort, I want to type "classic coil" and get there fast. That bar being so visible is appreciated.

## What's weak

- **Ocean/sky hero background** — I came here to buy a mattress. The background imagery looks like a travel brand or a spa. I want to see a properly dressed bed, a close-up of organic cotton, or an actual showroom set. The abstraction doesn't help me visualize sleeping in it.
- **"Visit a viewing room" cuts against my shopping mode** — I'm on my phone *because* I don't want to make a trip. This CTA should be "Shop the Sale" or "Find My Mattress," with a secondary link to the locator for those who want it.
- **Cookie consent modal covers the fold** — it's the first interaction I have to complete before I can see the bottom of the hero. By the time I dismiss it, some momentum is lost.
- **Zero trust signals below the headline** — no review count, no "free white-glove delivery" mention, no financing teaser. For a $1,500+ mattress, I need those facts before I even think about tapping.
- **No financing visibility** — I read financing terms twice before I commit. There is absolutely nothing here about payment options, and that's a significant gap for a luxury brand at this price point.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the ocean background for a premium bedroom lifestyle shot** — linen duvet, natural light, someone living in the space. Give me something I want to wake up in.
- **Change the primary CTA to "Shop the Fourth of July Sale"** and move "Find a Viewing Room" to a secondary text link underneath — this respects my intent to shop online first.
- **Add a thin trust strip beneath the hero CTA** — three icons: "Free white-glove delivery," "365-night trial," "0% APR financing available." These are the three things that move a premium buyer like me from browsing to considering.
- **Surface a specific product above the fold** — even a single hero mattress name ("Classic | From $1,099") with a "Shop Now" link gives me a destination rather than a brand statement.
