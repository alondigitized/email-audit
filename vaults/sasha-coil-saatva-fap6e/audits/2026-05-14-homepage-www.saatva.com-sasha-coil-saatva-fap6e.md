## What's working

- **"Visit a viewing room" CTA** — this is the most on-brand thing I've seen in mattress e-commerce. I want the showroom experience; they've named exactly that. It earns my tap.
- **Memorial Day sale banner with specifics** — "Up to $625 off | Ends 5/14 | Best Price Guaranteed" is three pieces of actual information in one strip. I don't have to hunt for the sale.
- **"Handcrafted" + "Luxury" language in the hero subtext** — signals coil construction and quality without needing to say it outright. This is a brand that knows what it's selling and to whom.
- **Search bar above the fold** — for someone like me who already knows what she wants (Saatva Classic, probably), being able to go directly is a small but real convenience.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal covering the bottom half of the screen** — it lands before I've seen anything below the hero. It's aggressive and immediately makes me feel tracked rather than welcomed.
- **Hero background is abstract/oceanic blue** — it doesn't anchor me in a bedroom or a premium sleep context. For a mattress brand pitching tactile luxury, showing me what feels like water is a missed opportunity.
- **No product imagery above the fold** — I can't see a coil, a fabric texture, or a mattress profile. "Handcrafted" is a word; I want a visual.
- **No financing information visible** — I read the payment terms before I add to cart. If there's a 0% APR offer buried somewhere, that's money left on the table for this hero.
- **No white-glove delivery mention** — I consider that a baseline expectation; if it's not visible early, I start to wonder whether it's included or an upsell.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the abstract blue hero background with an in-situ bedroom lifestyle shot** — a real room, real bedding, real scale. Show me what a Saatva mattress actually looks like on a bed I'd own.
- **Add a thin trust strip below the hero**: "Free white-glove delivery | 365-night trial | 15-year warranty" — these are Saatva's actual differentiators and they belong on screen one.
- **Surface the financing offer in the banner or directly below the hero copy** — "From $X/month — 0% APR available" pulls skeptical Gen X buyers who will finance a $2K mattress but only if the terms are visible before they get to checkout.
- **Delay the cookie modal by 3–5 seconds or trigger on scroll** — let me see the page before you ask for consent. The modal right now reads as a roadblock, not a courtesy.
