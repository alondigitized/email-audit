# Homepage snapshot · www.saatva.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a brand that actually knows what it's doing — "Luxury Mattresses Made Affordable" with "handcrafted" in the subhead tells me immediately this is not a foam-in-a-box company. The Memorial Day sale banner is front and center, and the deadline (5/18) is specific enough that I believe it. What's killing the experience on first load is that enormous cookie consent modal sitting right on top of everything I actually want to read.

## What's working

- **The sale banner** — "SALUTE TO SAVINGS MEMORIAL DAY SALE! Up to $625 off | Early Access GONE 5/18" is exactly the kind of spring-sale flag I wait for. Specific dollar figure, hard deadline. I'm paying attention.
- **"Handcrafted" in the subheadline** — that single word separates Saatva from the foam-in-a-box crowd before I've scrolled an inch. As someone who specifically wants premium coil construction, that word earns trust immediately.
- **"Visit a viewing room" CTA** — I'm skeptical of buying a mattress blind online. Knowing they have physical showrooms, right in the primary CTA, actually makes me more willing to browse online first because I know there's a fallback.
- **The search bar** — "What can we help you find?" at the top is low-friction for someone like me who knows what she wants and doesn't want to hunt through a hamburger menu.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal is enormous** — it covers the lower third of the hero and blocks whatever product modules or trust signals are below. On mobile, this is the first interactive thing I'm forced to deal with, and it kills momentum right out of the gate.
- **No product imagery visible** — the hero background is a serene teal/blue scene; I can't make out a mattress, a bedroom, or a product. For someone evaluating a $1,500+ purchase, I want to see what I'm buying, not a mood.
- **No trust signals above the fold** — no review count, no award badges, no "free white-glove delivery" callout. Saatva's white-glove delivery is a genuine differentiator, and it's nowhere in what I can see.
- **Financing detail absent** — I read the financing fine print twice before I buy anything this expensive. There's no mention of 0% APR, monthly payments, or Affirm/Klarna visible. That's a missed conversion hook for me.
- **Hamburger-only nav on mobile** — my category is one tap away only if I know to open the menu. Nothing about mattress types, firmness, or organic options is surfaced at the top level.

## Recommendations

- **Add "Free white-glove delivery + setup" to the hero subhead or a strip below it** — this is a real differentiator that would resonate immediately with a buyer like me who considers it a baseline expectation, not a bonus.
- **Swap the abstract teal hero background for a bedroom lifestyle shot** — show a beautifully made bed with visible coil structure callout or a quality-fabric close-up; this is a tactile purchase and the photography should do selling work.
- **Move the cookie consent to a slim bottom bar** instead of a full-screen modal — get it out of the hero entirely so the product and sale info can land without interruption.
- **Add a financing teaser line near the CTA** — something like "From $X/mo with 0% APR — see details" directly under the "Visit a viewing room" button captures the consideration-stage buyer who's calculating before committing.
