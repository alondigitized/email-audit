# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This looks like Royal Caribbean in full sale mode. "SCORE MORE SALE" and "UP TO $800 OFF" sit on top of a looping video of kids splashing in a ship's pool. It's aimed squarely at families and deal-hunters. The booking bar is right there, and I like that, but nothing on screen speaks to a Diamond guy who books a year out.

## What's working

- **The search bar ("Cruising to / Leaving from / Leaving on / Search cruises")** sits right under the hero. I can jump straight to a sailing without hunting.
- **The nav (Cruises, Destinations, Ships, Deals, Loyalty, Manage my cruise)** covers everything I do. "Manage my cruise" up top is a good touch for someone who lives in the app.
- **The yellow "Shop now" button** stands out against the blue water.
- **"3rd and 4th guests sail free"** is a real stackable hook, even though it's not aimed at me.
- **Ship pool-deck imagery** is what I care about. Ships are my thing.

## What's weak

- **The "Have an account?" pop-up** says "exclusive offers and pricing" but names no tier. A Diamond wants to see "Diamond perks" or my points, not a generic prompt.
- **"Loyalty" is only a nav word.** There's no Crown & Anchor tier, no perks, no member pricing on the first screen.
- **The subline text ("+ 3rd and 4th guests sail free") is hard to read** where it crosses the light, busy pool video.
- **The video is low-res and shaky.** For a $5,000+ purchase it feels like a phone clip, not a flagship Oasis-class ship.
- **The cookie banner takes the bottom of the screen** and hides whatever comes next. A yellow shape peeks out from behind it.
- **"Up to $800" and "Up to $100 off"** with no visible terms. I've been burned by "up to" before.

## Recommendations

- Show a personalized strip for signed-in or recognized members, like "Welcome back, Diamond. Your perks + this sale," in place of the generic sign-in tooltip.
- Swap the kid-splash clip for a crisp, high-res Oasis-class deck shot, or rotate hero variants by audience. Adults on the Boardwalk or at sail-away would work.
- Put a one-line "Save up to $800 per stateroom, see terms" under the offer, plus a visible sale end date.
- Make the CTA specific: "Shop Caribbean deals" instead of "Shop now," and add a "Loyalty members: see extra savings" link right next to it.
