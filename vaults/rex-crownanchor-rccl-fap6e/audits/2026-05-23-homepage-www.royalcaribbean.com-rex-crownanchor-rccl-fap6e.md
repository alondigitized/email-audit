# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean is leading with a Memorial Day sale — $900 off, 3rd and 4th guests free, bonus savings on longer sailings. That's a stacked offer and they're not hiding it. But as a Diamond member who's been on this site more times than I can count, I'm immediately annoyed: there's zero recognition that I've sailed with them dozens of times. No "Welcome back, Rex" personalization, no Diamond rate, no loyalty hook anywhere above the fold. The brand looks energetic and the deal is real, but this homepage treats me exactly like someone who's never heard of Royal Caribbean.

## What's working

- **"UP TO $900 OFF" headline** — that's the number that makes me stop scrolling. Specific dollar savings beat vague percentage deals for me every time.
- **3rd and 4th guests sail free callout** — I often book trips with extended family. This speaks directly to how I think about value on group sailings.
- **"Find a cruise" search widget** — immediately below the hero, destination + port + date dropdowns ready to go. I can get to itinerary results in two taps without navigating anywhere.
- **Memorial Day timing** — sale feels current and credible, not recycled inventory.
- **"Shop now" button** — gold/yellow, high contrast against the blue background, hard to miss on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor recognition** — I'm a Diamond member. I've spent tens of thousands of dollars with this brand. The profile icon in the top right corner does nothing to acknowledge that. No "Diamond rates available," no loyalty balance, no personalized hook. This is the biggest miss.
- **Water slide hero image skews too young** — the person on the slide looks like a teenager. I cruise for the itineraries, the dining, and the entertainment — not the waterpark. For a Diamond member booking his fifth cruise of the year, this image does nothing.
- **"Up to $900 off" hides the real savings** — the fine print (3rd/4th guests, 6+ nights) means the headline number is misleading unless you're booking a specific scenario. I know how to read these conditions, but it still feels like a bait figure.
- **No end date on the Memorial Day sale** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies urgency but shows no countdown, no specific expiration. That's a missed conversion lever.
- **No itinerary context** — no ships, no destinations, no departure ports shown. I want to know which Oasis-class ships are included in this deal before I tap anything.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Diamond/Crown & Anchor member banner** — if I'm logged in (or even if I'm cookied from a prior session), show my tier and a personalized savings hook: "Diamond rates included — your $900 is waiting." That single change turns a generic sale page into a loyalty play.
- **Swap the water slide hero for an Oasis-class lifestyle shot** — show the ship at sea or the Boardwalk at dusk. That image speaks to the returning cruiser and still communicates scale and premium experience.
- **Add a sale expiration date to the hero** — "Ends May 26" or a simple countdown would push me to search right now instead of bookmarking and coming back.
- **Add a "Destinations this deal applies to" strip** — four or five destination thumbnails (Caribbean, Bahamas, Europe, Alaska) below the search widget would give me a reason to start browsing even before I fill in the search fields.
