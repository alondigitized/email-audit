# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean knows how to open with the wallet punch — "$900 off" and "3rd and 4th guests sail free" is exactly the kind of stacking language I've trained myself to watch for at NextCruise desks. But here's what bugs me: I've got five cruises a year on my Diamond card, and this page treats me exactly like the first-timer who stumbled in from a Google ad. No tier recognition, no pre-loaded Crown & Anchor perks — just a generic sale banner and a search bar. It looks like a polished booking engine, not a home for loyalists.

## What's working

- **"UP TO $900 OFF" headline** — that's the first number that'll stop a deal-stacker mid-scroll. I know "up to" means YMMV, but the anchor is strong enough to make me lean in.
- **"3rd and 4th guests sail free"** — this is real money for family sailings. I've got grandkids I drag on Adventure of the Seas runs; this language lands for me.
- **"Search cruises" bar** — placed front-and-center below the hero. One tap, I'm already typing "Oasis June 2027." No hunting through nav.
- **"FINAL CALL: SAILINGS SELLING OUT NOW" strip** — the hot-pink urgency banner at the bottom is aggressive but honest for peak summer inventory. I believe it — I've been burned before waiting.
- **Pause button on the carousel** — shows they're thinking about accessibility and user control. Small thing, but I notice it.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor recognition** — I'm Diamond. My login should surface my tier, my points, my next tier milestone. This page looks identical whether I'm logged in or not. That's a miss.
- **"Up to $50 off on Summer Getaways"** — this feels like small-ball copy sitting right under a $900 headline. The contrast makes the $50 look like a footnote, not a perk.
- **No ship or destination imagery** — an ocean aerial is pretty but tells me nothing. Show me an Oasis-class pool deck. Show me Labadee. Give me something to project myself onto.
- **No specific sailings or itineraries called out** — the "Shop now" CTA goes... somewhere. For a planner who books a year out, I want a hook like "Mediterranean 2027 starting at $X" to kick off the search.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — three lines and a profile icon. Where's the quick link to "My Cruises" or "Crown & Anchor"? One extra tap is one extra second of friction.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero for logged-in Diamond members** — surface their tier badge and points balance in the header; swap the generic sale copy for "Rex, your Diamond benefits apply to this sale — $X additional savings."
- **Swap the ocean aerial for a ship lifestyle shot** — Oasis-class pool deck, the FlowRider, a private island beach club scene. Something that sells the experience, not just the water.
- **Add at least one itinerary-level hook** — "7-Night Caribbean from $X pp | Departing June 14" with a direct "Book now" CTA that pre-populates the search. I'll still compare, but you've given me a starting point.
- **Give the urgency banner a date** — "FINAL CALL" with no deadline is soft. "Offer ends May 19" makes me act. Right now it reads like wallpaper.
