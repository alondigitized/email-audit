# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This reads like Royal Caribbean's stock "big number" landing page — "$850 OFF" in a gradient-outline font over a moody sunset shot of a ship pulling away. I clocked the ship as Star of the Seas from the nameplate at the stern, so this is an Icon-class boat, not Oasis, which tells me the marketing team is leaning hard into the newest hardware right now. It's a generic acquisition push, not anything built for a five-cruises-a-year Diamond guy like me.

## What's working

- **"Up to $850 off" headline** — big, legible, states a real dollar figure instead of a vague "Save Now."
- **The search widget** (Cruising to / Leaving from / Leaving on) — sitting right under the fold, this is exactly how I plan a year out, so I can start narrowing without hunting for it.
- **Sign-in prompt in the corner** — flags that pricing changes once I log in, which is the right instinct for someone who's Diamond and expects member rates.

## What's weak

- **No Crown & Anchor callout anywhere above the fold** — I'm Diamond, five sailings a year, and there's not a single Diamond/loyalty-tier badge or point balance in sight, just a generic "Loyalty" nav tab.
- **"Shop now" is a placeholder verb** — for a cruise line this should say "Find your sailing" or "See deals," "Shop now" feels like it got copy-pasted from an apparel site.
- **No deadline on "Instant Savings"** — no "ends [date]" or countdown, so it doesn't feel urgent, just evergreen wallpaper.
- **No stacking detail** — I stack BOGO + OBC + The Key on every booking; this banner shows one flat discount stack ($850 + $150 + free guests) with zero mention of onboard credit or The Key, which are the two things that actually move me.

## Recommendations

- Swap "Shop now" for a specific verb tied to the search widget, e.g. "Find Your Sailing," and move the search bar fully above the fold on mobile so it's not half-cut-off.
- Add a small Diamond/Crown & Anchor strip near the sign-in prompt — even just "Diamond members save an extra X% + OBC" — to catch return cruisers before they scroll.
- Put a real date on the $850 offer ("Ends 9/30") so it reads as a NextCruise-style deposit window instead of permanent wallpaper.
- Mention The Key or onboard credit explicitly in the offer stack — that's the actual lever for someone who books a year ahead and cross-shops perks.
