# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean's homepage is running hard on a "Kickoff to Summer Cyber Sale" and they're not being subtle about it — $850 off, free third and fourth guests, extra savings on longer sailings. As someone who books Oasis-class a year out and stacks every offer I can find, this is speaking my language. The brand looks aggressive, promotional, and squarely in summer-booking mode, which is exactly where I'd expect them to be in mid-May.

## What's working

- **"Up to $850 Off" headline**: That's a big number and it stopped my scroll immediately. Paired with the underwater lifestyle shot, it feels like adventure, not a clearance rack.
- **The stacked offer copy**: "3rd and 4th Guests Sail Free" and "Up to $100 off longer cruises (6+ nights)" are both specific and additive. I mentally started stacking these on top of my NextCruise deposit before I even tapped anything.
- **The "FINAL CALL: SAILINGS SELLING OUT NOW" urgency strip**: That hot-pink bar at the bottom is aggressive but effective. As someone who books early, that phrase makes me wonder what's left on my preferred sailing dates and gets my thumb moving.
- **The cruise search bar**: Prominent, clean, one tap to start shopping. I don't need to hunt through a nav.
- **Video/animated hero**: The pause button is visible, which tells me there's motion here. A kid underwater in a pool is on-brand RCCL summer fun.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor recognition**: I'm a Diamond member. I've given Royal Caribbean five cruises a year of my life. This homepage has no idea who I am. No "Welcome back," no Diamond pricing callout, no loyalty-tier badge — nothing. A logged-in Diamond member should see something different than a first-timer.
- **The "Shop now" CTA is generic**: I know what I'm shopping for — Oasis-class, Caribbean, 7 nights. "Shop now" tells me nothing about what's on the other side of that tap.
- **No specific ship imagery**: The hero is an underwater pool shot of a kid, not an Oasis or Icon. For a Crown & Anchor member, I want to see the ship I'm dreaming about, not a generic resort vibe.
- **No urgency mechanics with dates**: The "Cyber Sale" doesn't say when it ends. "FINAL CALL" is in the strip but no countdown timer, no "ends May 22" — just vibes-based urgency.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Crown & Anchor recognition for logged-in members**: If I'm signed in as a Diamond member, replace "Shop now" with "Your Diamond Offer" and show my tier in the nav. That one change makes me feel seen instead of anonymous.
- **Swap the underwater kid for a ship deck shot**: A pool deck on Wonder of the Seas or Icon's Royal Promenade would anchor this to the Royal Caribbean product I actually buy. The kid underwater could be any resort brand.
- **Add a sale-end date to the hero**: Change "Kickoff to Summer Cyber Sale" to "Cyber Sale — Ends May 25" (or whatever the real date is). A countdown chip next to the CTA would double the urgency of "FINAL CALL" below.
- **Add a loyalty benefit line under the offer stack**: One line — "Diamond members save an additional 5%" or "Combine with Crown & Anchor perks" — turns a generic promo into a member-only hook they can stack.
