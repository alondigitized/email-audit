# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean is leading with their "All-In Sale" right now — big headline dollar number, stacked deal bullets, and a gold "Shop now" button that pops against the dark background. I recognize that rock climbing wall immediately — that's a signature Oasis-class feature — but the site opened with a giant cookie consent drawer covering a third of my screen before I could even breathe. What I do *not* see is anything that tells me, a Crown & Anchor Diamond member who's booked his fifth cruise of the year, that this brand knows who I am.

## What's working

- **"Up to $850 Off" headline** — that's a number big enough to stop a deal-stacker mid-scroll. Specific dollar figures beat "save big" every time.
- **Bullet stacking of secondary offers** — "3rd and 4th guests sail free" is a real hook for group bookings; I travel with extended family and this goes right into my mental math.
- **"Shop now" CTA button** — the yellow/gold pill is visually distinct from the dark hero and the blue background. I see it instantly.
- **Rock climbing wall imagery** — I know that wall. That's an Oasis-class signature. It tells me immediately this is a ship experience, not a beach resort.
- **Clean mobile layout** — nav is tucked into a hamburger, logo is centered, the offer hierarchy is legible even on a small screen.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor acknowledgment** — I'm Diamond. I've logged five cruises with this brand this year. The homepage has no idea I exist. No "Sign in to see your member rate," no Diamond perks callout, nothing.
- **Young climber imagery** — the person on the rock wall looks maybe 16. I'm 55 and my cruising circle is in their 40s–60s. The visual says "adventure for kids," not "premium experience for the loyalist who keeps Royal afloat."
- **Cookie consent drawer** — it swallows the bottom third of my viewport. On mobile that's brutal. It hits before I've had a second to orient myself.
- **"Shop now" CTA copy** — I don't "shop" for a cruise the way I shop for a shirt. "Book your voyage" or "Find your deal" would feel more premium and cruise-specific.
- **No urgency signal** — the sale has no end date, no countdown. I'm a guy who watches NextCruise deposit windows. Tell me when this expires.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Crown & Anchor banner or sign-in prompt** — even a thin strip that says "Diamond member? Sign in to see your exclusive rate" would make me feel recognized without requiring a full account integration. Ship it in a week as a simple conditional header.
- **Swap the teen climber for a 50s couple or a multi-gen family group** — Royal's bread and butter is repeat loyalists and family groups. Show me someone who looks like me enjoying a sea day on the Lido deck, or a Diamond cocktail hour moment.
- **Add a sale expiration date to the hero** — "Offer ends July 31" directly under the "$850 off" line. One line of copy, zero dev effort, doubles urgency for trip-planners like me.
- **Shrink the cookie consent to a footer strip** — or auto-dismiss it for returning visitors. Covering a third of a mobile hero with legalese on the first impression is conversion hemorrhage.
