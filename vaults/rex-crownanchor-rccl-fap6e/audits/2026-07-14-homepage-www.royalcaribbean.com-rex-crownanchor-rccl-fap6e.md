# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean is pushing hard on a "The All-In Sale" with some legitimately stacked value — $850 off, 3rd/4th guests sail free, and $100 off 2026 sailings. The hero ship is Utopia of the Seas, which I recognized before I even read the hull text. What kills the first impression is a massive cookie consent wall that buries roughly 40% of the screen before I can do anything — that's an embarrassing UX fumble for a brand that knows I've booked five times with them.

## What's working

- **"THE ALL-IN SALE" headline with three stacked value props** — $850 off, 3rd/4th free, $100 off 2026 is exactly the kind of multi-layer deal I try to stack. I read all three bullets in two seconds.
- **Utopia of the Seas hero image** — choosing their newest Oasis-class ship as the visual is the right call for loyalists like me. I have opinions about that aft neighborhood deck, and this got my attention immediately.
- **Yellow "Shop now" CTA** — high-contrast, above the fold, no ambiguity about where to tap.
- **Crown & Anchor crown-and-anchor logo centered in the nav** — the brand mark alone does loyalty signaling work.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is enormous and poorly timed** — it erupts immediately and covers roughly 40% of the viewport, including the bottom of the hero. This is the first thing I have to deal with before I can engage with a deal that could be worth $850 to me.
- **No Crown & Anchor / Diamond acknowledgment** — if I'm logged in (or even if I'm not), there should be some returning-customer recognition. "Welcome back, Diamond member" or "Your exclusive rate" would change my entire read of this page.
- **"Shop now" is generic CTA copy** — for a brand this specific, "Book a 2026 Sailing" or "View Sale Departures" would convert someone like me who knows exactly what he wants.
- **No urgency signal** — the sale has no countdown, no "ends Sunday," no "limited sailings at this rate." For someone who watches NextCruise offers like a hawk, that absence makes me wonder if this is always running.
- **No NextCruise or deposit-offer language** — I actively shop those. One line acknowledging NextCruise credit stacking would close me faster than anything else on this page.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Diamond/Crown & Anchor recognized state above the fold** — even a lightweight "Exclusive Diamond rate applied" badge near the offer copy would make me feel seen and accelerate the booking decision.
- **Swap "Shop now" for "View 2026 Sale Sailings"** — ties the CTA directly to the "Up to $100 off on 2026 sailings" bullet directly above it; reduces the click-to-consider friction for someone who books a year out.
- **Add a sale deadline or countdown chip to the hero** — "Ends July 20" is enough. I will book faster if I believe the $850 is time-gated.
- **Reduce the cookie modal's visual footprint or delay it by 3 seconds** — let the deal land before the legal interruption. The offer is strong; don't bury it behind boilerplate the moment I open the app.
