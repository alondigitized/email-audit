# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean's leading with a "BEAT THE CLOCK" sale — up to $850 off, third and fourth guests sail free, $50 bonus on short getaways. The bones are solid: deal-forward, clean search widget right up front, unmistakable CTA. What's missing for me personally is any acknowledgment that I've been on five ships this year. I land on this page as a total stranger, not a Crown & Anchor Diamond member who's already pre-qualified.

## What's working

- **"BEAT THE CLOCK / UP TO $850 OFF" hero combo** — the dollar figure is big enough to make me stop scrolling. $850 is real money even on a balcony sailing.
- **3rd and 4th guests sail free line** — I do occasionally pull the kids along on summer sailings, so this sub-bullet is actually relevant to how I book.
- **Inline search widget** — "Find a cruise" with Cruising to / Leaving from / Leaving on dropdowns is right there, zero hunting required. I can go from hero to search results in two taps.
- **Pause button on auto-play** — at least they gave me the control. I hate when I can't stop the motion.
- **"Shop now" CTA color contrast** — yellow-gold on the blue/sky hero is very readable on a phone screen in daylight.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor recognition** — I'm logged in (profile icon is right there in the nav), and the page has no idea I'm Diamond. No "Welcome back, Rex" strip, no Diamond-tier benefit reminder, nothing. This is a miss for a loyalty program that's supposedly one of Royal's biggest retention tools.
- **Adventure imagery skews wrong** — The zip-line/outdoor-activity shot feels like it's targeting someone's first Royal cruise, not a lifer. Show me an Oasis atrium or a suite sunset balcony and I'm in.
- **"Shop now" CTA copy** — I book cruises, I don't "shop" for them. "View deals" or "Book now" would read better to someone who's done this 25+ times.
- **No urgency clock visible** — The name is literally "BEAT THE CLOCK" but there's no countdown visible. The name promises time pressure; the page doesn't deliver it.
- **Short-getaway bonus buried** — "+ Up to $50 bonus on all short getaways" is the third line in a three-bullet list and nearly disappears. As someone who books short Bahamas runs between big sailings, that should be louder.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the hero strip for logged-in Crown & Anchor members.** Even a one-line banner — "Diamond member? Your exclusive rate is applied." — signals Royal sees me, not a first-timer.
- **Swap the zip-line shot for ship or destination imagery.** An Oasis-class pool deck, CocoCay aerial, or suite balcony converts better for my segment than an adventure activity stock shot.
- **Add a real countdown to the "BEAT THE CLOCK" headline.** Show me "Offer ends June 6 at midnight" with a live timer. You named the promo after urgency — prove it.
- **Elevate the short-getaway bonus.** Break it out as its own CTA or badge — "Short Getaways: + $50 bonus" — instead of burying it as the third bullet below the fold.
