# Homepage snapshot · www.royalcaribbean.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Royal Caribbean's mobile homepage opens with a punchy, dollar-specific savings headline and a dead-simple search widget — that's the right muscle memory for someone like me who's already got a browser tab open to price-check a Harmony sailing. What's conspicuously absent is any acknowledgment that I've sailed this brand 25-plus times; the homepage treats me the same as someone who just Googled "cheap cruise." The offer is real, the execution is clean, but the personalization gap is a miss for the most valuable customers on the water.

## What's working

- **"INSTANT SAVINGS UP TO $1,000 OFF" headline**: Specific dollar figures are how I evaluate whether a sale is worth booking now versus waiting. This one passes the sniff test immediately.
- **3rd and 4th guests sail free bullet**: Even as a frequent couple/solo cruiser, that pulls me in — I'm always looking for an excuse to bring the adult kids on an Oasis sailing.
- **Find a Cruise search widget**: Destination, homeport, date — the three fields I actually punch in are right below the hero, no scrolling required. That's the fastest path from intent to booking.
- **Gold "Shop now" CTA**: Pops hard against the blue background on a small phone screen. I'd tap it before I had time to second-guess.

## What's weak

- **Zero Crown & Anchor recognition**: I'm Diamond. Five sailings a year. Not one line acknowledging my status or that this $1,000 offer stacks with my member benefits. This homepage treats me like I found the URL in a banner ad.
- **Hero imagery skews 25 years too young**: Two athletic guys in their 30s doing paddleboard-adjacent activities. That's not who carries a Crown & Anchor card. The aspirational shot needs to reflect who actually books Oasis-class suites.
- **No deal expiration date**: "INSTANT SAVINGS" sounds urgent but there's no deadline copy. "Book by June 30" would convert me today instead of letting me park the tab.
- **$50 short-getaway bonus is a noise line**: Sitting below a $1,000 headline, it reads like a consolation prize. It's obscuring the main offer more than adding to it.

## Recommendations

- **Drop a single Crown & Anchor callout into the offer strip**: "Diamond members get additional OBC — stacks with this offer." Six words. The return on loyalty recognition is real and it costs nothing to show.
- **Replace the water sport hero with an Oasis-class deck or theater scene featuring guests in the 45–65 range**: That's who sails this brand at this frequency. Show them the entertainment and amenities they're actually paying for.
- **Add a sale end date next to the $1,000 figure**: "Ends June 30" or a simple countdown badge creates the urgency the headline is currently missing.
- **Pre-fill the search widget for returning users**: If I'm logged in, default "Cruising to" to Caribbean and "Leaving from" to my nearest port. The site knows I sail out of Port Canaveral every spring.
