# Free $30 eGift Card
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal summer promotion from Chewy offering a free $30 eGift card with a $100 qualifying purchase using code SUMMER. The concept is solid — a gift-with-purchase offer tied to a recognizable seasonal hook ("Ready, set, summer") with warm lifestyle imagery of a cat and dog that immediately signals the brand. The execution is clean and uncluttered.
- The problem is that the offer mechanics feel like they have a catch. The fine-print block at the bottom is visually dense and clearly lengthy, which will create hesitation the moment a motivated shopper scrolls down. More critically, this email doesn't reflect what Chewy is best at — subscription convenience, auto-ship savings, or pharmacy value (which their last two audited emails led with). A one-time promo code that requires a $100 basket to unlock $30 back is a moderate lift; it's not the generous, loyalty-reinforcing play the brand history suggests Chewy is capable of. Decent email, soft execution.

## What's working

- **Hero offer is immediately readable** — "Free $30 eGift card" + code SUMMER + $100 minimum is visible in the first 3 lines, no scrolling required.
- **Lifestyle imagery lands well** — the cat and dog photo is warm and demographically broad; signals this is a pet brand within milliseconds.
- **Shop segmentation (dog / cat / all) is smart** — the three quick-shop tiles let different pet owners self-select immediately after the hero.
- **"Top picks for you" section** — adds a product-discovery hook that gives the email a reason to scroll.
- **24/7 customer service line** is prominently displayed, reinforcing Chewy's customer-trust moat.

## What's weak

- **Fine print is visually threatening** — the bottom of the email is a solid wall of dense grey text; any engaged shopper who scrolls there will feel the friction and doubt the offer's simplicity.
- **No urgency signal** — there is no expiration date visible in the hero or anywhere above the fold; "summer" isn't a deadline.
- **The "Top picks for you" row shows generic/brand-level products**, not personalized items based on your pet history — Chewy has the purchase data to do better here.
- **CTA button is small and pale blue** — it sits inside a thin band and doesn't punch with contrast relative to the white background above it.
- **No auto-ship or loyalty angle** — given that the prior June send pushed pharmacy convenience and the May send was an account onboarding email, this one misses the obvious "stack this with your auto-ship order" nudge that would make the $100 threshold feel easy to hit.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero.** "Offer ends July 20" or "This week only" turns a passive promo into a decision. Right now there's no cost to waiting, so most people will.
- 2. **Shrink the fine-print wall or move it behind a collapsed toggle.** The wall of grey text signals "complicated" and undercuts the simple "free $30" hook above it.
- 3. **Make the CTA button larger and higher-contrast.** The current button is easy to miss; a full-width or wider button in a bolder color would drive more clicks.
- 4. **Tie the offer to auto-ship.** "Add to your auto-ship order and hit $100 automatically — then grab your $30 eGift card" would make the threshold feel effortless for existing subscribers.
- 5. **Personalize "Top picks for you" with your pet's actual purchase history** (or at least species-filtered products if they know you have a dog).
