# Cyber Week savings kick off now! 🥾 💥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, well-built Cyber Week blast. "CYBER WEEK" in huge magenta type is the first thing you see. "Up to 50% off starts now.*" and a "Shop deals" button follow immediately, so the offer and CTA land in one glance. The dachshund carrying a plush toy adds warmth. The rest of the email is a clean stack of modules, each with its own button.
- It's also the third Chewy promo in eight days. The 11/16 "Holiday shopping: The snuggle is real" send and the 11/18 "Up to 40% off is in your stocking" send both led with 40% off. This one raises the headline to 50%, which is a real escalation. But the "up to" claim sits on top of a dense fine-print block with a CYBER10–CYBER50 code ladder. The creative is strong, and the offer is less transparent than it looks.
- Overall it's solid, polished retail execution with an escalating offer. Nothing stands out beyond the bigger number.

## What's working

- The "CYBER WEEK" headline dominates, and the "Up to 50% off" line and "Shop deals" button sit directly under it.
- The dachshund with a plush toy is charming and on-brand, and it gives the hero a focal point.
- The Shop dog, Shop cat and Shop all deals tiles give quick category paths right after the hero.
- "Buy 2, get 3rd FREE" is a second concrete offer, which gives a different reason to click for people who skip the percent-off line.
- The "Top picks for you" strip, the 24/7 chat and phone options, and the trust icons (free returns, happy pets guaranteed) reinforce confidence.

## What's weak

- There are five or more "Shop" CTAs, all with generic copy ("Shop deals," "Shop now," "Reveal your deals"). The email asks for several different clicks and never makes one stand out.
- The "Surprise savings end soon" flash deals module is vague. It teases without saying what the deal is or when it ends.
- The fine print under the footer is dense. It lists codes CYBER10 through CYBER50 and says "Valid through 12/2/26," so the real discount depends on a code and cart rules. That clashes with the bold "50% off" headline.
- The hero is very tall. The dog image pushes the category tiles and the second offer well below the first screen on mobile.
- The "Top picks for you" thumbnails are tiny, with no prices or product names. The "for you" framing promises personalization the module doesn't deliver.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the CTA count. Keep "Shop deals" in the hero and one secondary offer button, and fold the Flash Deals teaser into it.
- 2. Put the real deadline in the visible copy, for example "Ends 12/2," so urgency doesn't depend on the fine print. Show the code or auto-applied logic near the headline so "up to 50%" feels honest.
- 3. Shrink the hero image so the category tiles and the Buy 2 get 3rd FREE offer appear earlier.
- 4. Make Top picks real: larger images, product names and sale prices.
- 5. Space out sends. A big reveal feels weaker when 40% off arrived on 11/18 and the promo cadence is this tight.
