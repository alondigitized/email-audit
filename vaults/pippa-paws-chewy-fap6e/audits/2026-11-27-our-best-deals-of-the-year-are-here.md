# Our best deals of the year are here 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid Black Friday send, and it fixes the problem with the 11-25 "Your midweek deal: Up to 50% off 💰" email. That one promised 50% off in the subject and never showed the number. Here a French bulldog in plaid pajamas sits on a candy-cane pillow. Under it is a huge navy "Up to 50% OFF", then "Toys, treats, beds, and more.*" and a "Shop deals" button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The second block, "ONE DAY ONLY: Free $35 eGift card with your $100 order of select Purina faves", adds real urgency and a second hook. The "Shop dog / Shop cat / Shop all deals" tiles help with category routing. The weak points are the vague subject, the small and tentative "Shop deals" button, and a dense wall of fine print. The footer promo codes (CYBER10 through CYBER50) are confusing and undercut the "honest offer" feel. The email is a clean 7, a bit below the 8s of the 11-23 and 11-24 Cyber Week sends, mostly because the subject says nothing concrete.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% OFF" hero is enormous, high contrast, and sits directly under a magenta "BLACK FRIDAY" tab. The offer is unmissable.
- The dog in pajamas on a candy cane pillow is warm and seasonal, in line with the brand's holiday creative.
- "ONE DAY ONLY" with a "Free $35 eGift card" is a second, concrete, time-bound offer with its own "Shop now" button.
- The "Shop dog / Shop cat / Shop all deals" tiles split by pet type fast.
- The trust row (24/7 service, free shipping with Chewy+, free returns, happy pets guaranteed) and the "Fast, free shipping with Chewy+" header strip give loyalty and reassurance cues.

## What's weak

- The "Shop deals" button is small relative to the giant headline and doesn't match the hero's visual weight.
- The fine print is a dense block with multiple codes (CYBER10, 15, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50). It makes it unclear which code gets the hero discount, and the "Up to 50% OFF" claim feels buried in conditions.
- The eGift tier is hard to parse: it needs a $100 spend on "select Purina faves", and "select" is not defined in the render.
- The "Top picks for you" strip has tiny product thumbnails with no prices, names or offers, so it adds little.
- The Black Friday hero closely repeats the 11-23 and 11-24 "50% off" creative. Cadence is heavy and the hero blends with the earlier sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero CTA bigger and more specific, for example "Shop 50% off dog & cat deals", at roughly twice the current button size.
- 2. Put the code, or a note that the discount is automatic, next to the hero instead of only in the footer. This removes the guesswork.
- 3. Add a countdown or "Ends tonight" line to the eGift block, and name the eligible brands.
- 4. Replace the "Top picks" thumbnails with larger tiles showing name, price and a "was/now" figure.
- 5. Shorten the fine print to the codes that apply, with a "See full terms" link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: $35 eGift card + up to 50% off 🐶`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toys, treats, beds & more. Plus a free $35 eGift card with $100 of Purina.`
