# Your midweek deal: Up to 50% off 💰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a weak send for a Cyber Week email. The subject promises "Up to 50% off", but the render never shows that number. The hero is a huge navy block with a pale gift box and the line "Holiday goodies for your pet". There is no percentage, no product and no deadline in it. The most striking element is the empty space above the box, which reads like a banner that failed to load. Compare the 11/24 send ("50% OFF" in navy under a magenta tab) and the 11/23 send ("Why wait until Friday for up to 50% off?"). Those put the offer in the hero, and this one doesn't.
- The lower half is better. The Shop dog, Shop cat and Shop all deals tiles are clean, and the dog and cat photo with "Surprise cyber savings → Reveal your deal" is warm. But it hides the actual offer behind a mystery mechanic. The real terms (CYBER10 through CYBER50 codes, 5 items per order, valid through 12/2) are only in the fine print.
- Judgement: a Cyber Week send that doesn't show its discount, and the 4th Cyber Week send in three days. Expect it to underperform the earlier 8/10 sends.

## What's working

- The Chewy+ strip ("Fast, free shipping with Chewy+") and the "Cyber Week" tab keep it on-brand and on-season.
- The Shop dog, Shop cat and Shop all deals tiles are large, tappable and segment well by pet type.
- The dog and cat photo is the warmest, most human moment in the email.
- The pink CTA buttons contrast well against the navy.
- The trust row (24/7 service, free returns, 100% happy pets) and the live-chat and phone buttons reduce friction.

## What's weak

- The hero shows no discount number. "Up to 50% off" from the subject appears nowhere in the render.
- There is a large empty navy void under the "Cyber Week" tab. It reads like a broken or missing image and pushes the real content below the first screen.
- "Holiday goodies for your pet" in thin type on a washed-out lavender box has weak contrast and no urgency.
- "Reveal your deal" and "Surprise cyber savings†" hide the offer behind fine print. The code ladder (CYBER10 to CYBER50) is buried at the bottom.
- "Top picks for you" is tiny, with four unlabeled products and generic "Shop now" links, so there is no price or reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "UP TO 50% OFF" in the hero in large type, as in the 11/24 send, and remove the empty space above the gift box.
- 2. Show the deadline ("Ends tonight" or "Through 12/2") next to the offer instead of in the footer.
- 3. Replace the mystery "Reveal your deal" with a concrete code or deal, such as "Use CYBER50 at checkout".
- 4. Make "Top picks" real, with product names, prices and star ratings, and cut it to two or three items.
- 5. Vary the creative from the last three sends. A midweek angle could be a single hero product with a price.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it uses table layout with MSO conditionals, a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, and first-party tracking domains. The QA run couldn't verify link destinations or authentication, because of 429 rate limiting and missing relay headers. The confirmed defects are an empty `<title>` and five images flagged for missing alt text, one of which is a linked image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were found.** The 7 probed links that failed all returned 429 (rate limiting), not 404 or 5xx. These are the "Holiday goodies for your pet. | Shop deals", "Shop dog", "Shop cat", "Shop all deals", "Chewy", the image link and "Up to 50% off*" links. A 429 from `clicks-paws.chewy.com` is consistent with throttling of the QA prober, so these are unverified rather than failing.
- **Coverage gap:** 5 links were not probed because the 70s time budget ran out. They are the `clicks-paws.chewy.com/f/a/y3xm3lOK…`, `MOktmk6C…`, `jPiqAHDp…`, `hN1E9vLU…` and `rmNe2Jvm…` links. 18 links were skipped by design as footer or social links, so final destinations for roughly 23 of 30 links remain unconfirmed.
- **Link wrapping is consistent.** All links use the same tracking domain, `clicks-paws.chewy.com/f/a/…`, which matches the sender subdomain `paws.chewy.com`. That is good for deliverability.
- **Very long redirect URLs.** Several wrapped URLs exceed 700 characters. This isn't a defect by itself, but it is why the plain-text part is 85% URLs (11,656 of 13,681 characters).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is present in `<head>`. It should carry the subject or a descriptive string, since some clients and web views use it.
- **Missing alt text (5 images, per QA):**
  - `https://meow.chewy.com/p/rp/7ffd0ba076493042.png` is wrapped in a link (`https://clicks-paws.chewy.com/f/a/6e0mW05T…`), and the QA link text shows `[image: 7ffd0ba076493042.png]`. A linked image with no alt gives screen-reader users no link name, so this is a real accessibility failure.
  - `https://meow.chewy.com/p/up/04ebeb1c…/o.gif` and `https://clicks-paws.chewy.com/q/n4Vsh4lT…` look like open-tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and are low priority.
  - `footer-icons_returns.png` and `footer-icons_guaranteed.png` are probably decorative or paired with live text, so they need `alt=""` or a descriptive alt. The check can't tell a missing `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` handling, MSO/Outlook conditionals and a mobile stacking breakpoint at 620px are all present.
- **Web font:** `chirp.media.chewy.cloud/fonts/fonts-chewy-v2.css` is loaded inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so it degrades safely.
- The source was truncated, so the body markup, including the preheader, couldn't be checked beyond the head.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source or in the subject.
- Tracking pixel URLs carry `mi_u=979488259` and `mi_ecmp=2026-11-25-W-EarlyBlackFriday-NotSettled…`. These are resolved values, not leaked tokens. The `mi_u` value is a recipient identifier in a query string, which is normal for this ESP's pixel but worth knowing.
- This check covers only the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself notes these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed: it could be a capture artifact or a real omission. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, and a major retailer almost certainly sends them.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, again attributed to the relay. This is unverified, not a failure.
- The probing note says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set. The footer was not visible in the truncated source, so the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified. Every CTA is a wrapped redirect through `clicks-paws.chewy.com/f/a/…`, and the 429s stopped the prober from resolving final URLs. Whether campaign parameters survive to the landing page is unknown.
- The campaign slug `2026-11-25-W-EarlyBlackFriday-NotSettled` is visible only in the pixel URLs.
- CTA labels such as "Shop dog", "Shop cat", "Shop all deals" and "Up to 50% off*" can't be matched to their destinations without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` to the linked image `7ffd0ba076493042.png`. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and any decorative footer icons.
2. Populate `<title>` with the subject line or a short descriptor.
3. Re-run the link probe with a throttle, retries with backoff, or a longer time budget to resolve the 7 rate-limited and 5 unprobed links. Then check their final URLs for UTM and landing page alignment.
4. Fix the relay capture of raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`), or inspect the original message source. Until then, treat the compliance and authentication results as unverified.
5. Pass the full, untruncated HTML to the audit so the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and body markup can be checked.
