# Save up to 40% on gifts for good pets
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, on-brand Chewy holiday blast. The hero does the work: a big pink "UP TO 40% OFF" over a gingerbread toy, holiday snacks and mouse toys. Your eye lands on the offer first, and the "Shop deals" button sits right inside the arch. It looks like the holiday season, and nothing is broken.
- The weakness is the offer's honesty and urgency. The hero says "up to 40%", but the fine print reveals a tiered code system (EARLY10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40). It also has a 5-item limit, a 3-orders-per-customer limit and a 11/23 end date. None of that is visible above the fold. The deadline is not in the hero at all, so a real urgency lever is wasted. The subject line, "gifts for good pets", is warm, but the body is just generic holiday product with nothing personalized.
- Judgement: solid and safe, with no standout idea. It's a clear step up from the legal-notice and account-confirmation sends in the brand history. It's roughly on par with the 2026-07-13 "Free $30 eGift Card" promo, and it's weaker on specificity because the real discount depends on a code you can't see.

## What's working

- The hero is large and high-contrast. "40% OFF" is the unmistakable focal point.
- The "Shop deals" button is placed inside the hero, with three big tiles (dog, cat, all deals) below it for fast category routing.
- The festive creative (gingerbread toy, candy-cane snack bag, ribbon) fits the season without clutter.
- The trust strip (24/7 service, free shipping with Chewy+, free 365-day returns, happy pets guaranteed) is clean and reassuring.
- There are no render bugs. Images load, text is legible and the layout is tidy.

## What's weak

- The offer mechanics (EARLY10 through EARLY40, tiered by what you buy, 5-item limit) are buried in small grey fine print. "Up to 40%" is never tied to anything specific.
- There is no visible deadline. The 11/23 end date only appears in the legal text, so there is no urgency in the hero.
- "Top picks for you" shows four tiny products, each with an identical "Shop now" link. There are no prices, no names and no discount badges, so it reads as generic rather than personalized.
- "Shop dog" and "Shop cat" are the only segmentation. The "for you" label promises more than the email delivers.
- The footer is long and dense, with no loyalty or Autoship angle, and the hero says nothing about the code needed at checkout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code and the deadline in the hero, for example "Use code EARLY40 · ends 11/23". This is the biggest lift. It makes the offer concrete and adds credible urgency in one line.
- 2. Add price and discount badges to the Top picks (for example "$12.99, was $17.99") and replace the four identical "Shop now" links with product names.
- 3. Make "Top picks for you" truly personalized, using dog or cat segmentation. Right now it is not.
- 4. Surface the Chewy+ and Autoship savings above the fold if the recipient is a member.
- 5. Test a gift-guide framing, such as "Gifts under $20", since the subject says "gifts".
