# ## 1. Overview

Bath & Body Works is running a final push for the $2.95 Carry-Along Cuties deal — following the same two-email flash-deal formula used on May 19 with the $1.95 car freshener ("take it for a spin" in the morning, "final hours" at night). The morning send today ("going somewhere?", scoring 9/10) was stronger; this evening email is an urgency close. 

### Key Points:
- **Subject and Hero Section**: Effective and clear.
- **Email Layout Below Fold**: Overloaded with multiple campaigns (Nautical collection, Grogu collab, Buy 3 Get 3 promotion, member reactivation).
- **Cadence Issues**: Two emails in one day on the same product can erode customer trust.

### Summary:
The email is functional for its intended purpose but suffers from a cluttered layout and poor cadence. The hero section is strong, but everything below it detracts from the primary goal of closing the deal.
