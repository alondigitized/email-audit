# final hours on skin-loving suds...🫧
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This one is actually coherent — "final hours" + "$4.95 select hand soaps" + code HANDFUL lines up cleanly with the subject, and the hero image (soaps, lotion, aloe, callouts for "Hydrate all day" / "Nourish & condition") does real work showing what's on offer. It's the first email in this brand's recent run that isn't a bait-and-switch between subject and hero.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold: a holiday teaser ("It's never too early for wonder... Cue the Magic"), a "Now hiring" recruitment block, then a generic "We think you'll love" product rail, a brand-trust blurb, and a utility nav — five unrelated modules stacked under one legitimate offer. The urgency signal ("Final hours") is real and specific, which is a step up from the 09/20 flash-sale send that withheld the discount entirely, but the email still can't resist padding out with off-topic content that dilutes the one message worth acting on.
- Judgement: solid top, bloated everything else. If a subscriber opens for the soap deal, they get it immediately — that's the win. But nobody needs the hiring pitch or the holiday teaser competing for attention in the same breath as a "final hours" clock.

## What's working

- Subject, "Final hours" eyebrow, headline, and hero image are all telling the same story — no mismatch, unlike 09/22's soap/merry split or 09/21's fragrance/buy-3-get-3 split.
- The offer is concrete and scannable: $4.95, up to 24 per customer, code HANDFUL, visible barcode for in-store use.
- Hero image callouts ("Hydrate all day," "Nourish & condition") add real product context without extra copy.
- Clear single CTA button ("Shop now") directly under the price.

## What's weak

- Immediately below the offer, the email pivots to a holiday teaser ("Cue the Magic") that has nothing to do with hand soap or urgency — this is the same scattergun habit seen in the 09/19 and 09/20 Wallflowers sends.
- A "Now hiring" recruitment module sits mid-email — irrelevant to a shopper who opened for a soap deal, and visually heavy (full-width photo + dark CTA) enough to compete with the actual sale.
- "We think you'll love" recommends a candle and two unrelated body creams instead of reinforcing the hand-soap offer (e.g., complementary lotions, refill bundles).
- No countdown timer or explicit end-time despite "final hours" messaging — the urgency is asserted, not visualized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the holiday teaser and hiring blocks entirely from this send, or move them to a separate email — "final hours" urgency emails should stay single-minded.
- 2. Add a visible deadline (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") near the price to make "final hours" credible rather than just a label.
- 3. Swap "We think you'll love" for complementary soap/lotion pairings to reinforce the one offer instead of diluting it.
- 4. Tighten the subject for mobile and add a number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `final hours: $4.95 hand soaps, use HANDFUL`
- **Subject Alt B:** `ends tonight — $4.95 skin-loving suds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up before it's gone — up to 24 per customer, code HANDFUL.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hydrating hand soap, $4.95 each. In stores & online, tonight only.`
