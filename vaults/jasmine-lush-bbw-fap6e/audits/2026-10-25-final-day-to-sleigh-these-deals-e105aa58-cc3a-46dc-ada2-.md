# final day to sleigh these deals. 🛷
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same $5.95 mists-and-body-spray hero Bath & Body Works has run since 10/22 ("starts now - can't-miss deals", "don't sleep on these gift-worthy deals", "ready, set, gifts for everyone", and yesterday's "activate shopping mode"). The hero is clean. An "Ends today" bar sits over "Save on perfect gifts" and a big **$5.95**, with a "Shop now" outline button. The eye lands on the price first.
- The creative has not changed in four days, so the urgency reads as manufactured. "Final day" is credible only if you haven't seen "starts today" on the same offer three times already. The body is thin: three small product tiles with no prices, a brand-values banner, and a utility link list. There are also big blank gaps in the lower half of the render, and the footer links appear to have no legible text. I'd call it a competent but fatigued send, and the deadline is the only new thing in it.

## What's working

- The "Ends today" bar and the large $5.95 make the offer and deadline obvious at a glance.
- The hero is a single offer with one button, and the pink-and-holiday imagery is on-season.
- The Rewards and free-shipping-at-$50 strip at the top gives member and shipping value without crowding the hero.
- The Ocean and Snowflakes & Cashmere bottles show exactly what the $5.95 buys.

## What's weak

- The hero is a repeat of four sends since 10/22, so the "final day" urgency lands weakly.
- The three product tiles (Winter Candy Apple, Fresh Balsam, Into The Night) show no price or deal tag. It's unclear whether they're part of the $5.95 offer, and the Wallflowers refill isn't a mist or body spray.
- "Our feel good formula" is low-contrast white text on a cream image and is hard to read. It's also off-message for a last-day sale.
- There are large empty white gaps below "Find a store" and in the footer. Only partial underlines are visible, so the footer links look blank.
- The "Shop now" button is a thin outline that sits low in the hero on a busy background.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the repeated hero. If the $5.95 mists really end today, show a real countdown or a "last chance: ends tonight at midnight" line. Otherwise give subscribers a fresh angle, such as a best-sellers grid.
- 2. Put "$5.95" on every product tile, make each tile link to its product, and keep them to mists and body sprays.
- 3. Make the CTA a filled button with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop $5.95 mists".
- 4. Replace or drop the "feel good formula" banner. It dilutes a last-day sale and its text is illegible.
- 5. Fix the blank spacing and make the footer links legible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`/`camarker-inner`) send with 39 click-tracked links, and it has no confirmed hard failures. Six images lack alt text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed.** 39 links on tracking or redirect domains were skipped, so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Open-tracking image.** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=102526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_BIGDAY_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL` carries a recipient-level ID (`e=`) and a campaign code (`c=`). It is a different domain from the sender domain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) and the image domain (`image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`).
- **Unneeded font request.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts does not serve. It adds a render-blocking external request and a tracking surface, and the font stack already falls back to Helvetica/Arial.
