## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a tracked ESP template (images on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, click tracking via redirect domains) with dark-mode CSS. The confirmed code-level problems are 5 images with no alt text, an `@import` for a font that can't be served, and markup that starts a `<table>` inside `<head>`. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so I can't confirm any destination resolves or lands on a live page.
- **The tracking pixel is flagged as an image.** The sixth alt-text warning is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=103126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_10953W_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD`. It is a third-party open-tracking beacon, so an empty `alt=""` is correct and this one is not a defect. It is blocked by default in privacy-focused clients (such as Apple Mail Privacy Protection), so open-rate data from it will be unreliable.
- No broken or malformed links were found in the visible source. Beyond the tracking pixel, the HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the rest.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images** (all under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`):
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  These are probably the promo imagery, so images-off users and screen readers get nothing. If the offer text (such as "$10.95 3-wicks") is baked into the images, it is invisible to them.
- **Invalid font `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host, so it adds a wasted network request. `@import` in email `<style>` is also stripped or ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers the failure.
- **Markup structure:** the source goes straight from the `<meta>` tags in `<head>` to `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">`, with no `</head>` or `<body>` visible. Browsers recover from this, but some clients handle it inconsistently. The `u + #body a` selector (Gmail link-color reset) only works if `<body id="body">` exists, and I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Minor:** the `<title>` is `Bath &amp; Body Works ` with a trailing space. The charset is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`), which is harmless. The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional alongside HTML5-style metas.
- Positives: `lang="en"` is set, `role="presentation"` is on layout tables, and `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas are present with dark-mode overrides.
