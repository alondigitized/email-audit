# did you shop THE 50% off sale? 🙌 it's the final countdown...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth day of the same 50% off campaign, and it's the least differentiated send of the run. The burnt-orange "Half off. Full spree. 50% off Entire store" hero is the same one from 10/02, 10/03 and this morning's "last day" send. A black "Final hours" bar sits above it. The offer is clear and the number dominates. A subscriber who got the 10/04 "last day. 🔴50% off everything*" email has seen all of this already.
- The body is weaker than the hero. Below it are four "New" editorial blocks (fragrance mist, Home Sweet Holidays, a bigger cleanser, Glow & Gather candles), then a second "Final hours / 50% off | Entire store / Shop now" block. After that come a "You might like" row, a "Our feel good formula" banner, and a wall of fine print. The urgency competes with a new-arrivals catalog, and the "New" labels dilute the countdown message.
- The subject is where the send stumbles. "did you shop THE 50% off sale?" is a curiosity hook, but it's oddly worded, and the ALL-CAPS "THE" adds nothing. It also lands as a guilt-trip after the earlier "last day" email. I'd call this a competent but redundant closer.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a giant "50% off" in white on burnt orange, with "Entire store" beneath it.
- The black "Final hours" bar above the hero adds credible urgency.
- Four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) sit right under the product lineup. They give quick shopping paths.
- The "Free shipping with any $50 purchase" bar is visible near the top and adds a second concrete incentive.
- The second "Final hours / Shop now" block lets a scroller convert after the editorial content.

## What's weak

- The creative is a repeat of the 10/02, 10/03 and earlier 10/04 heroes, so there's nothing new for a fourth day.
- The "New" editorial blocks pull attention toward full-price launches during a countdown. The "Final hours" message is buried mid-email.
- The "You might like" row has small, pale, low-contrast product images. It carries no prices or sale callouts.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner and the "More to love" links add noise after the closing CTA.
- The subject line promises a question but delivers a hard sell. "THE" in caps looks like emphasis for its own sake, and the 🙌 doesn't fit the "final countdown" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or collapse the four "New" blocks into one thin strip, or drop them on the last-chance send. Put the second "Final hours / Shop now" block directly under the hero. The email's only job is converting the remaining hours.
- 2. Make the subject state the deadline and the offer. Lead with "Tonight" or a specific cutoff time, because the footer says the sale ends 10/05 at 5:59 AM ET. Use that.
- 3. Add a countdown timer or an explicit end time ("Ends 1:59 AM") in the hero bar instead of the generic "Final hours."
- 4. Show a sale price on the "You might like" products, such as the $3.50 foaming hand soap. Stronger product tiles would give the email something real to click.
- 5. Tighten the fine print above the fold of the footer. The offer is "50% off | Entire store. Exclusions apply," so use the asterisk language the subject lines already use, and keep the legal block shorter.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 50% off the entire store`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call. Half off everything closes at 1:59 AM ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candles, soaps, body care, Wallflowers. All 50% off until the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart won't get a better price this fall. Shop before midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.e2` / `image.e2`) send with valid sending-domain alignment and a working open pixel. The source has structural HTML errors (content tables inside `<head>` and between `</head>` and `<body>`), 6 images without alt text, and 48 click-wrapped links that were not probed. The missing authentication and unsubscribe headers are probably relay-capture artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified redirects:** 48 links on tracking/redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unknown. The QA data shows no confirmed broken links.
- **Second tracking vendor:** One image is served from `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFF_FH_BAL_G6M_V2`. The `e=` value looks like a per-recipient identifier. It is a different host from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, so it is a separate dependency, and if it is slow or blocked that slot fails independently. Given the subject line, this is likely a dynamic countdown image, but the truncated source doesn't confirm it.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?WEGR2FWJXCRU7HOJCOJ2QX3FWM.70249&d=70249&bmt=0` is well-formed (1×1, `alt=""`, in a `mso-hide:all` table). No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images):**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - the Everest image above

  With images blocked, or for screen readers, any offer or countdown content carried by these images disappears.
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` sits inside `<head>`, wrapping the `<style>` block.
  - Further wrapper tables appear after `</head>` and before `<body>` (`<!-- Begin variables -->`, `<!-- Begin Preheader and Header -->`).
  - A `<table>` is nested directly inside another `<table>` (`<!-- Header LOGO -->`) with no `<tr>/<td>` between them.

  Clients vary in how they repair this. Some hoist or drop the misplaced `<style>` blocks, which would remove the dark-mode and responsive CSS.
- **Mobile class not applied:** The media queries target `.email-container`, but the visible 600px layout tables (`style="width: 600px;"`) don't carry that class. As far as the visible source shows, the layout stays fixed at 600px on small screens.
- **Web font imports:**
  - `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that, to my knowledge, isn't in the Google Fonts catalog. It likely returns an error and does nothing.
  - `@import` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, and falls back to `Helvetica, Arial` in all cases.
- **Redundant markup:** `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` is declared twice (`<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`). `<meta charset="utf-8">` duplicates `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Neither causes harm.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used alongside `<meta charset>` (an HTML5 construct). This is tolerated by clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens or placeholder syntax appear in the visible source (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`). The source is truncated, so the footer and body weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` matches the click and image subdomains, which is consistent with alignment, but that isn't proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All CTAs sit behind `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects that weren't probed, and the truncated source doesn't expose final URLs or UTM parameters. The only campaign identifier visible is the Everest `c=100426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFF_FH_BAL_G6M_V2`, which is consistent with a 50%-off promo campaign naming scheme.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add it to the 5 CDN images and the Everest image. If the Everest image is a countdown, use text like "Sale ends [date/time]".
2. **Markup:** Move the `<style>` blocks into a single `<style>` in `<head>`, and move all wrapper tables inside `<body>`. Insert `<tr><td>` around the nested "Header LOGO" table.
3. **Mobile:** Add `class="email-container"` to the 600px layout tables, or add `max-width:100%` to them.
4. **Fonts:** Remove the `Helvetica+Neue` `@import`. Keep only the web-safe stack.
5. **QA coverage:** Resolve the tracking links through the redirect chain, using GET requests with a mobile user agent. Then check the final URLs for UTM params and landing-page status.
6. **Headers:** Capture raw headers from the original MIME (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat those three warnings as unverified rather than failures.
