# weekend plans:❣️50% off the entire* store.❣️this NEVER happens. 🥳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong offer-led send. The hero is a burnt-orange block with "Half off. Full spree. 50% off Entire store" in huge type, over a full lineup of fall products. Nothing competes with the 50% number. The "Starts today" banner above it adds urgency.
- Below the hero the email goes soft. Four "New" editorial tiles (fragrance mist, Home Sweet Holidays, bigger cleanser, Glow & Gather candles, Midnight Rouge) all promote new product and say nothing about the sale. Only the line "save on every.single.thing" ties them back. The offer is front-loaded, and the body reads like a standard new-arrivals email.
- The subject line is a real mismatch with the brand's own history. After a week of $2/$3/$4 ladders and "final hours" soap sprees, "this NEVER happens" is hard to believe. It reads as the 4th or 5th "biggest deal" in a few days. The hero is good, but the cadence and the asterisk-heavy fine print erode the credibility of the claim.

## What's working

- The 50% hero is oversized and high-contrast, with the product lineup right under it. The eye lands on the offer first.
- Four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) sit directly under the hero and give a quick path to what the reader wants.
- The seasonal product mix (Frosted Cranberry-style fall scents, a leaf candle, holiday tiles) fits the date.
- The top banner adds a second incentive: free shipping on any $50 purchase, plus Rewards messaging.
- The "Starts today" bar marks the sale as live now.

## What's weak

- The "entire*" asterisk in the subject leads to a very dense, tiny fine-print block at the bottom. The "this NEVER happens" claim is hard to square with the brand's recent daily deals.
- The five editorial tiles say "New" but show no sale price or discount. The offer drops out right after the hero.
- No visible end date or countdown in the body. The Oct 3 to Oct 5 window appears only in the fine print.
- There is a large empty gap above "You might like," and the three product tiles there are small, with no prices. No reviews or social proof appear anywhere.
- No primary "Shop now" button. The only CTAs are four category buttons and small text links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero, for example "Sat–Mon only" or a countdown. Right now the real deadline is buried in the footer.
- 2. Replace the "New" tiles with sale-priced bestsellers showing "50% off" and a before/after price. Link each tile to a specific product.
- 3. Add one explicit primary button under the hero ("Shop 50% off") alongside the category buttons.
- 4. Tone down "this NEVER happens," which is not credible given the last week of sends. Name the specific hook.
- 5. Add a short exclusions line near the hero, such as "Excludes select items," so the asterisk feels honest.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Redirects not verified.** 49 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains, and link health are unconfirmed.
- **Domains.**

- **Open pixel.** The 1×1 pixel (`width="1" height="1" alt=""`) sits in a `width="0" height="0"` table with `mso-hide:all`. No issues found there.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing `alt` on 6 images.** Five are `lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/*.png` (`4851c40c-…`, `d6327aef-…`, `00a9ac12-…`, `7b7c94ef-…`, `bbebdf75-…`). The sixth is the `everestengagement.com` image above. Screen readers will read the filename or URL, and nothing shows when images are blocked.
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - Content tables carrying `class="stylingblock-content-wrapper"` are placed inside `<head>`, after the `<meta>` tags and before `</head>`.
  - More wrapper tables sit between `</head>` and `<body bgcolor="#ffffff" …>`.
  - Parsers will hoist these into the body, so behavior varies by client.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but `<meta charset>` and `/>` syntax are mixed in. `Content-Type` and `charset` are declared twice.
- **Outlook DPI fix may not apply.**
  - The `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` / `PixelsPerInch 96` XML appears in two conditional blocks (`[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]`), duplicating it.
  - Both blocks are inside a `<td>` rather than the real `<head>`. Outlook may ignore them.
- **Styles inside body-level tables.**
  - The main `<style>` block (responsive rules and `@media` queries) is inside `<td class="stylingblock-content-wrapper camarker-inner">`.
  - Clients that only honor head-level `<style>`, such as Gmail, may drop it.
- **Responsive rule has no visible target.**
  - The `@media (max-width:600px)` rules rely on `.email-container`, `.mobile-padding`, and `.logo-image`.
  - In the visible source, the outer width tables use only `style="width: 600px;"` with no `email-container` class.
  - Mobile behavior depends on the truncated remainder, so this can't be confirmed.
- **Web fonts.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue…')` points at a family Google Fonts does not host, so the request likely fails and falls back harmlessly. `@import` is also ignored by many clients. A second `@import` loads Source Sans Pro, which isn't referenced anywhere in the visible CSS.
- **Dark mode.**
  - The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`.
  - The only visible dark-mode rules force `color:#ffffff !important` on `.darkmode-white` and `.apple-dark-white`, scoped to iOS and WebKit via `@supports`.
  - Backgrounds are hardcoded white or `!important`.
  - Contrast on iOS depends on where those classes are applied, which is outside the visible source.
- **Positives.** `<html lang="en">`, a `<title>`, and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Add `alt` text to all 6 images.** Use descriptive text for any image carrying offer content, and `alt=""` only for decorative images.
2. **Fix the document skeleton.** Move the content-wrapper tables out of `<head>` and put the `<style>` and MSO blocks in the real `<head>`. Keep a single `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.
3. **Make the responsive classes reachable.** Add `class="email-container"` to the 600px tables, or confirm in the full source that it's applied. Consider `max-width:600px; width:100%`.
4. **Remove the dead `@import` rules** (Helvetica Neue and Source Sans Pro) or replace them with a font that exists.
5. **Review the Everest image** to confirm it is intentional. If it is, check that its data handling is covered by the privacy policy.
6. **Fix the QA tooling.**
   - Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay strips them, so these checks stop returning inconclusive warnings.
   - Follow the redirects on `click.e2…` links with a safe HEAD or GET to record the final URLs and UTMs.
   - The summary line says "3 warning(s)", but 10 individual warnings are listed. The count appears to be per category.
   - Use the untruncated HTML for the audit, since the unsubscribe footer and merge-token checks depend on it.
