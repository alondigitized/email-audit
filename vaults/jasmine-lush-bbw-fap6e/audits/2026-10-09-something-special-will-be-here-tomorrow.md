# something special will be here tomorrow...🍁😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser that gives the game away. The subject promises "something special" tomorrow, and the email shows a $5.95 deal on select full-size mists, body spray and body cream under a "Starts tomorrow" banner. The hero is strong: a red field, a huge "$5.95" and glossy Platinum bottles, so the eye goes straight to the price. The subject, though, doesn't hint at fragrance or price, so the sender gets no curiosity payoff and no preview of the deal in the inbox.
- The body is generic and drifts after the hero. The "You might like" row shows hand soap, kitchen spray and a Wallflowers refill, none of which relate to the $5.95 mists and body cream. The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks are filler. The fine print is long and dense, and it holds the only end date (October 12), which the hero never mentions. Compared with the 10/08 "75% off fall goodness" send, the offer here is far less dramatic and the 🍁 in the subject has nothing to do with the creative.
- Verdict: a competent hero attached to a weak subject and a diluted body. It's fine as a reminder, but it won't drive anyone to act before tomorrow.

## What's working

- The "$5.95" is huge, white on deep red, and impossible to miss.
- "Starts tomorrow" in the dark bar sets expectations clearly and fits the teaser subject.
- The hero names the categories (full-size mists, body spray, body cream), so the deal is concrete.
- The header carries a free-shipping-at-$50 banner and a Rewards prompt, which adds a second reason to buy.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "Start your list" button is vague. It doesn't say what you'll land on or why you should go there.
- The hero shows no end date. The Oct 12 close and the 24-item limit appear only in the footer fine print.
- The product row (Leaves hand soap, Kitchen Lemon spray, A Quiet Escape Wallflowers) doesn't match the $5.95 offer, so the email loses focus.
- The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" sections feel like template filler. The email also repeats "Find a store" and "Buy online, pick up in store" links.
- The 🍁 in the subject doesn't match a red fragrance hero. Four near-identical promos in the last three days (10/06 "tomorrow" teaser, "tons of deals," 10/07 "final hours") also create fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deal in the subject. The email already reveals it, so a teaser that withholds it wastes the open. Name the product and the price.
- 2. Replace the three unrelated products with the actual $5.95 items (mist, body spray, body cream), each linked to its own page.
- 3. Put the dates in the hero: "Oct 9–12 · limit 24."
- 4. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Shop $5.95 mists."
- 5. Cut the "Feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks, or move them below a tighter, price-focused product section.
