# ⌚ hours left to shop $5.95 body care.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is B&BW's "final hours" closer on the $5.95 Select Body Care event — the second email they've sent today on this exact offer (the morning send was "treat yourself. 💌 $5.95 body care today only." at 6/10). The hero is competently built: "FINAL HOURS" overlay on aspirational fragrance imagery, $5.95 front-and-center, "New Scents Incoming" framing that makes a sale feel like an arrival rather than a clearance. That's the right instinct.
- But the email doesn't stop there. Below the hero is a hand sanitizer deal ($2), hand soap deals ($3.50), a Halloween/fall "Cast a Spell on Your Senses" candles section with an unanchored "$10 off," and a full Summer Sale 75%-off slider with product category tabs. That's five distinct offers fighting for attention in a single send. The hand care section is a direct carryover from yesterday's Clean Hands Event (7/17) — it feels like deadline-driven copy-paste, not intentional curation.
- The verdict: a capable technical execution dragged down by offer fatigue and a cluttered layout that asks too much of a subscriber who's already seen this deal once today. B&BW has now featured $5.95 body care in at least four emails since 7/15 — anyone who was going to buy already has.

## What's working

- "FINAL HOURS" overlay on the hero lands immediately — no reading required to understand the urgency
- $5.95 price is the dominant visual element in the hero; it wins the hierarchy battle at the top
- "New Scents Incoming" positions the sale as launch energy, not a distressed-inventory close
- Barcode in the hero makes in-store use obvious without burying it in the footer
- "Cast a Spell on Your Senses" fall candles section is a smart early tease for B&BW's biggest season — contextually relevant for late July

## What's weak

- Five separate promotional sections below the hero with multiple "SHOP" buttons create genuine decision paralysis — the eye has nowhere to land
- Hand sanitizer ($2) and hand soap ($3.50) sections are yesterday's Clean Hands Event stitched in — off-brand for a fragrance-forward closing send
- The candles "$10 off" has no visible base price — off what? — making the deal feel vague and untrustworthy
- This is the second $5.95 body care email on 7/18; a subscriber who saw the morning send has already made their decision
- No "Shop Now" CTA in the hero itself — the primary offer section has no button, only the secondary sections do

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip this to one offer.** Remove the hand care and summer sale sections entirely. The $5.95 closer should be one thing: hero → 3–4 specific product tiles at $5.95 with individual links → single "Shop Body Care" CTA. One decision, one click.
- 2. **Add a concrete deadline.** "FINAL HOURS" is vague. "Ends at midnight" or a countdown element (even static: "Sale ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET") gives subscribers a reason to act now vs. later-never.
- 3. **Give the CTA real copy.** Replace every instance of "SHOP" with "Shop $5.95 Body Care" in the hero CTA and "Grab Yours Before It's Gone" in the closer.
- 4. **Use "New Scents" as the hook, not the backdrop.** If there are genuinely new scents arriving, name two or three and link directly. "New scents, same $5.95 deal — here for hours only" is a stronger reason to click than a generic fragrance photo.
- 5. **Move the Summer Sale to a footer link line.** One line — "Plus: Summer Sale up to 75% off →" — rather than a full section competing with the headline offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: $5.95 body care ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⌚ $5.95 body care closes at midnight — don't miss it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New scents at the same price — grab yours before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select body care, $5.95 — final hours online and in-store.`

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "$5.95 Body Care" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on HTML 4.01 Transitional via Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Two structural defects are present: content (preheader table and open pixel) rendered outside the `<body>` tag, and multiple unexpunged internal template artifacts left in HTML comments. List-Unsubscribe compliance headers were not captured at the relay, which is the highest-risk finding.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **24 click-tracking links** all route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. QA skipped HTTP probing on all 24 — redirect chain health and final landing page availability are unverified.
- **Open pixel** (`https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?CV6EVKT7GR2UTEQ25SOSSKPGYA.70250&d=70250&bmt=0`) is correctly rendered as 1×1 with `alt=""`.
- **Everest/Validity engagement pixel** (`https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=071826_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_595TBCTRIO_FH_BAL_G6M_V`) loaded as an `<img>` with no `alt` attribute — QA confirmed.
- **Two `<img>` tags with empty `src`** — QA confirmed. Empty `src` triggers unnecessary server requests in some clients and will produce console errors.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Content outside `<body>` (structural bug):**

```html
</head>
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
<table ...>  ← preheader + open-pixel tables rendered here, BEFORE <body>
</table>
<body style="...">  ← body opens here
```

Two tables — including the preheader text and open-pixel — are emitted between `</head>` and `<body>`. Gmail typically moves pre-body content into the body; Outlook and other clients handle this inconsistently. The preheader text "there's still time to find a new fave.💜" lives in this pre-body region.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import` with inconsistent weight specs:**
- `<head>` import: `Source+Sans+Pro&display=swap` — no weight variants
- Body `<style>` block: `Source+Sans+Pro:wght@400;700&display=swap` — includes 400 and 700 weights

Gmail strips `<style>` blocks from `<body>`, so weight variants 400/700 will not load in Gmail. The two imports also make redundant HTTP requests to `fonts.googleapis.com`.

**`<style>` block inside `<body>` `<td>`:** Gmail's CSS inliner will strip this entirely. Any styles defined only here are non-functional in Gmail.

**Missing alt text (QA confirmed, 3 images):**
- `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` — promotional image, no `alt`
- Everest pixel `<img>` — no `alt` attribute
- Two empty-`src` `<img>` tags — no `alt` attribute

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens visible in rendered content (no `%%Variable%%` placeholders left unfilled).

**Unexecuted shell token in comments (appears twice):**

The backtick shell substitution was never executed — it was emitted as a literal comment. If this was intended to stamp a build date, the value is absent. Not user-visible, but indicates the pre-send template pipeline did not fully process this file.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA WARN):** Required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance (policy effective Feb 2024 for senders >5k/day). Absence may be a relay-capture gap, but if absent at delivery, BBW risks inbox demotion and spam classification.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (QA WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is a hard requirement for Google and Yahoo bulk senders. Missing this will trigger spam folder placement at Google.

**`Authentication-Results` not captured (QA WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status for sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is unknown from this relay capture. Cannot confirm authentication alignment.

**Physical mailing address / unsubscribe link in footer:** HTML source is truncated — presence of CAN-SPAM-required physical address cannot be confirmed from available source.

All 24 CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` click redirectors. Final destination URLs and UTM parameter presence cannot be inspected because HTTP probing was skipped on all tracking domains and the HTML source is truncated before link `href` values are visible.
