# 75% off body care?! we're in. 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' July 1 opener — approximately email twenty in the Semi-Annual Sale blitz — and the 75% off body care hero is the brand firing its biggest caliber yet in the campaign. The scarcity frame ("Once they're gone, they're gone") is clean and earned at this stage: SAS subscribers know inventory genuinely depletes. The dual-offer structure (75% body care up top, $7.99–$10.99 candles in the middle) gives price-sensitive shoppers two entry points without muddying the message.
- The structural problem is the Halloween Collection module sitting mid-email. On July 1, dropping a Halloween tease inside a Semi-Annual Sale email is either very smart (B&BW loyalists expect early Halloween drops) or tonally whiplashing — pivoting from "buy now" SAS urgency to "order soon for Halloween spirits." It fragments the single-message focus at exactly the moment subscriber fatigue is at its peak in this blitz.
- Compared to the June 26 "Pretty Woman / Biggest Price Drop Yet" send (the standout of this campaign at a 9/10), this one is competent but formulaic. The "we're in. 🤩" subject framing — the brand enthusiastically co-signing their own deal — is a cute device, but not enough to distinguish email twenty from the dozen before it.

## What's working

- **75% hero is impossible to miss** — the percentage dominates the top fold with bold typography stacked on lifestyle product imagery.
- **Dual price-point structure** — candles at $7.99–$10.99 give a lower-stakes second offer to subscribers who already have enough lotion.
- **"Once they're gone, they're gone" scarcity** — has earned credibility this deep in SAS; feels real, not manufactured.
- **Satisfaction guarantee banner** ("WE JUST WANT YOU TO LOVE IT! Guaranteed.") near the footer is an underrated trust signal that most SAS emails skip.
- **Subject is mobile-length and concrete** — under 35 chars, includes a specific number, scannable at a glance.

## What's weak

- **Halloween Collection module is tonally jarring** — it splits the SAS urgency narrative mid-scroll and asks the subscriber to hold two incompatible mental frames at once.
- **All CTAs say "Shop"** — generic across every section with no destination signal; "Shop 75% Off Body Care" and "Shop Candles from $7.99" would each pull harder.
- **No deadline visible** — "once they're gone" is passive scarcity; an actual end date or "Today Only" banner would close the loop.
- **No member/loyalty signal** — at email twenty, a visible members-only perk (even "Members: extra 10% stacked") would re-engage the fatigued core base.
- **TOP OFFERS thumbnail grid is too small** — five-across micro-thumbnails with tiny category labels add clutter without providing enough product context to drive a click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give CTAs destination copy** — "Shop 75% Off Body Care" and "Shop Candles from $7.99" each point to a specific PLP; "Shop" alone points nowhere.
- 2. **Add a visible sale-end date** — a "Sale ends Sunday" or "Final 48 hours" ribbon converts passive scarcity into a counted-down action window.
- 3. **Move Halloween to its own dedicated send** — or drop it below the TOP OFFERS grid so it doesn't interrupt the SAS narrative on first scroll.
- 4. **Surface a members-only exclusive** — one line is enough; loyalty customers are the most likely repeat buyers at this point in the blitz.
- 5. **Replace the thumbnail grid with 2–3 featured products at price** — readable product cards with visible dollar amounts outperform five tiny icons.
- **Subject Alt A:** `75% off body care ends soon — get in.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `final call: 75% off body care 🛒`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candles from $7.99 too. But once they're gone, they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart is about to thank you. 75% off body care + candles from $7.99.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "75% off body care" (2026-07-01)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud stack with Everest engagement tracking. Two classes of issues stand out: unfilled merge tokens in image URLs (breaking per-subscriber tracking) and compliance headers that are unconfirmed at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Unresolved subscriber merge token in image URLs**
`mi_u=` is empty across all 5 content images on `mi.bathandbodyworks.com`:
```
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/7b321191c78d6fa5.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=070126_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_75OFFSELBC_AM_
```
`mi_u` is a Marketing Intelligence subscriber identifier. An empty value means image-open tracking is anonymous — subscriber-level engagement data is lost for this send.

**Unresolved build-time template tag**
The comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` appears twice in the HTML (once before `<body>`, once inside the preheader block). This is a shell command that was never interpolated — it should emit a value like `ET07.26`. It is inert as a comment but indicates a broken pre-send template processing step.

**23 click-redirect links skipped**
All CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Final destination URLs were not probed, so redirect-chain integrity and landing page UTM alignment cannot be confirmed from the available data.

**Open-tracking pixel**
Present and correctly structured at:
```
https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?7WJJTLYLQAOUXHWFNDUVRBF5FQ.70254&d=70254&bmt=0
```
No issue, but image has empty `alt=""` — correct for a 1×1 pixel.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**9 images missing `alt` text (QA confirmed)**

| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| 5 content images on `mi.bathandbodyworks.com` | No `alt` attribute |
| Logo on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` | No `alt` attribute |
| Everest engagement pixel (`bbwus.everestengagement.com`) | No `alt` attribute |
| 2 images with empty `src=""` | No `alt` + broken src |

Images-off rendering (common in Outlook and B2B inboxes) will show blank boxes or broken image icons with no text fallback for any of these.

**Two `<img>` tags with empty `src=""`**
Empty src causes unnecessary HTTP requests (a GET to the document's own URL) in some older clients, and shows broken image icons. These should be removed or replaced with `src` values.

**Duplicate `@import` for Google Fonts**
`Source+Sans+Pro` is imported once in `<head>` (weight 400 only) and again in a `<style>` block inside the body table (weights 400 and 700). The in-body import is non-standard and will be ignored by some clients. Only the `<head>` import matters; the in-body one adds redundant HTTP overhead.

**`<meta charset>` placement**
The `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" charset=utf-8>` tag appears after the MSO conditional comments rather than as the first child of `<head>`. In strict parsers this can cause character-set detection to fall back to a default before the declaration is read.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=` empty (critical)** — Described above in §2. The merge tag that should populate the subscriber ID was not resolved before send.

**AMPscript NTF/APT logic present but output not visible**
Three AMPscript comment blocks reference `@NTF_Flag` and `@APT` variables. The downstream output of this logic (e.g., animated preview tag variant) is not visible in the HTML excerpt, so correctness cannot be confirmed — but the variables' presence confirms conditional personalization is in use.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — unconfirmed (QA warn)**
The AgentMail relay did not capture this header. It may exist on the originating MTA but was stripped in transit. If absent, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native unsubscribe button, and bulk-sender compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements) is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — unconfirmed (QA warn)**
One-click machine unsubscribe (required for high-volume senders under Google/Yahoo requirements) is not confirmed present. Same relay-capture caveat applies.

**SPF/DKIM authentication — unconfirmed (QA warn)**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured. Sending domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`; authentication status for that subdomain is unknown from available data.

Physical mailing address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated HTML — cannot confirm or deny; assumed present in the non-truncated footer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**No standard UTM parameters on image URLs**
Content images use `mi_ecmp=070126_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_75OFFSELBC_AM_` (proprietary MI tagging). This works within the BBW/Salesforce ecosystem but does not populate `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` in GA4 or other non-MI analytics tools, meaning site-side attribution for image-driven traffic is absent.

**Click-link UTM alignment unverifiable**
Because all 23 CTAs are wrapped through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, whether UTM params are injected into final destination URLs before the redirect resolves cannot be determined from this data. This is a gap in coverage, not a confirmed defect.
