# make a wish 🪄✨ all candles are buy 1 get 1 FREE for a limited time.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean BOGO candle send that repeats the 2026-11-09 "ALL candles are buy 1 get 1 free" email. The hero is strong. "ALL candles Buy 1 get 1 free" sits in big type over a lit candle, with a "Shop now" button next to it. "Lowest-priced item is free" is stated right under the offer, so there's no buried fine print.
- The rest of the email dilutes the offer. Below the hero, the 11/11 "A Thousand Wishes" fragrance launch takes over, followed by a dermatologist-tested block, a crystal "Drops" waitlist and tiny product tiles with no prices. A candle shopper who scrolls past the hero finds almost nothing candle-related. The email also has large blank white gaps mid-body and a mostly invisible footer, which look broken.
- Verdict: a good offer in a weak layout. It works for someone who clicks the hero, but it is the second candle BOGO in three days. It also sits next to the four "final hours" sends from the past four days.

## What's working

- The hero offer reads in about two seconds: "ALL candles Buy 1 get 1 free" in large type, with a visible "Shop now" button.
- "Lowest-priced item is free" is stated plainly under the headline, which makes the offer feel honest.
- The lit candle photo is on-category, matches the offer and gives the eye an obvious landing spot.
- The 11/11 "Time to make a wish" framing fits the date and ties into the subject's "make a wish" line.
- "Limited time" on the black bar above the hero adds urgency.

## What's weak

- There are big empty white blocks between the "Magic happens" section and the dermatologist block, and again below the footer. They read as missing images or content.
- The footer links are barely visible, so the unsubscribe and legal area looks broken.
- After the hero, the email pushes fragrance, a waitlist and a "feel good formula" story instead of candles. Nothing else supports the BOGO.
- The three product tiles are small and have no prices, ratings or links to candles.
- "Limited time" has no actual end date. That is weak next to the "today only" and "final hours" urgency in the previous sends.
- The "Rewards" line at the top is tiny and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the fragrance and dermatologist blocks with a candle grid of four to six hero scents. Each tile needs a name, a price, a "BOGO" badge and a link. That keeps the whole email about the offer.
- 2. Fix the blank gaps and the invisible footer. Check these in dark mode and in clients that block images.
- 3. Add a real end date or time to the offer, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59pm". It's better than "limited time", and it lets you vary the cadence instead of repeating "final hours".
- 4. Change "Shop now" to "Shop BOGO candles" so the button says what it leads to.
- 5. Move the Rewards callout to a visible spot and say what members get on top of BOGO.
- 6. Cut the subject to under 50 characters. Drop the all-caps FREE and the double emoji.

- **Subject Alt B:** `your candle BOGO is on, through Sunday 🕯️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick any two. The lower-priced one is on us. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3-wick favorites, holiday scents and the new Thousand Wishes, all BOGO.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`, `camarker-inner`) with dark-mode and Outlook handling. QA flagged no hard failures. The warnings are missing alt text on 6 images and header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) that the AgentMail relay may have stripped. The HTML is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 42 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party tracking domain:** Tracked URLs use `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, for example `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B1G1CDL_AM_BAL-NTF-OPT`. This does not match the sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, and the images use the aligned `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. That is a mixed-domain setup, though common with ESPs.
- **Google Fonts import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family I don't believe Google Fonts hosts. It most likely fails silently, so the `Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial` fallback stack applies. Most clients strip `@import` anyway, so this is a wasted request rather than a visible break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 6 images):**
  - `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `…/d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `…/00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `…/7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `…/bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - The `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` image, which is likely the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Invalid structure in the visible source:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the `<meta>` tags, with no visible `</head><body>` before it. The HTML is truncated, so this may be an extraction artifact. If it's real, a table inside `<head>` is invalid and clients may parse it unpredictably.
- **Doctype and charset:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `<meta charset="utf-8">`. The page also has `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so the charset is declared twice. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Duplicated Outlook XML:** `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` appears in both the `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` blocks. It is redundant and not harmful.
- **Dark mode:** `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` rules are present, and `color-scheme` is declared. No defects visible in the truncated CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The remainder of the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe missing:** QA did not find the header. It notes that the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so I can't confirm it's absent from the original message.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post missing (RFC 8058):** This is the same caveat, and the same unconfirmed status applies.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `bathandbodyworks@e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and the image host `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` share the subdomain, which is consistent with alignment, but that is not evidence of it.
- **Footer not verifiable:** The visible source ends before any unsubscribe link or physical postal address, so those CAN-SPAM elements couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 42 click-through links are redirect-wrapped (`everestengagement.com`), so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters aren't visible. The campaign code `c=111126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B1G1CDL_AM_BAL-NTF-OPT` does reference the B1G1 candle promo. Whether the landing page matches is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images listed above, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixel.
2. Capture the raw original message headers, rather than the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
3. Resolve a sample of the 42 redirect links, following redirects with a browser-like client, to check destinations, status codes and UTM parameters. Confirm they land on the B1G1 candle promotion.
4. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and to confirm whether the `<table>` really sits outside `<body>`.
5. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for Helvetica Neue and rely on the system font stack.
6. Drop the duplicate `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` block and the duplicate charset declaration.
