# kiss $3.95 lip care goodbye. 😘 final hours.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-duplicate of this morning's "$3.95 lip care today only. 💄" send, with the banner swapped from "Today only" to "Final hours." The hero is the same: "Pucker up, winter," a big **$3.95**, a lip mask and balm, and a "Shop now" button. The eye lands on the price first, which is right. It is a clean, price-led hero.
- Below the hero the email turns into a multi-offer catalog. There is a Wallflowers "10 for $30" block, a "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" body care block, a "You might like" row, and a feel-good formula banner. The subject promises one thing, lip care, and the body spreads attention across three offers. Unlike the 11/16 Wallflowers email, the "10 for $30" offer is stated this time, but it sits well below the fold. The code "MOISTURIZE" and the barcode line are useful, but they are small and easy to miss.
- Verdict: solid hero and a credible deadline, but it is the second lip care send in under 24 hours. The lower half dilutes the message. It would score a 6 or 7 if it were the first send. As the repeat, it is weaker.

## What's working

- The **$3.95** price is huge and sits next to the product. It is readable at a glance.
- The "Final hours" banner above the hero gives credible, time-bounded urgency.
- The subject and hero match. "Lip care" and "$3.95" appear in both.
- The in-store barcode and "online, use code MOISTURIZE" line make the offer easy to redeem.
- "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" and "10 for $30" are concrete offers with their own "Shop now" buttons.

## What's weak

- It is the second lip care email in a day. Same creative, same price, only the banner changed. Fatigue is likely.
- The "Up to 24 per customer with code" line and the "Dermatologist approved" badge are tiny and compete with the price.
- The lower modules (Wallflowers, body care, "You might like") pull attention away from the lip care offer the subject promised.
- The "You might like" row has small, muted products with no prices and no reviews.
- The fine print is a long wall of text. The "Limit 24" and exclusions are buried there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send to a single-offer email, or fold it into one "last chance" email for the whole day. Don't send an identical-hero email twice in 24 hours. If the repeat stays, make the lip care hero the only major block.
- 2. Put the code **MOISTURIZE** inside the hero as a visible chip or in the button copy. Today it sits below the fold of the hero.
- 3. Change the button copy from "Shop now" to "Get $3.95 lip care."
- 4. Add star ratings and a "best seller" cue to the lip mask.
- 5. Cut the body care and Wallflowers blocks down to one compact row, or move them under a clear "Also ending tonight" header.
