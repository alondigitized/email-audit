# 🧴gentle reminder. $3.50 is here for one day only.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the $3.50-hand-soap promo the brand keeps rerunning, this time with a "gentle reminder" framing and a barcode built for in-store scanning. The hero is clean — clear price, clear product, clear code — but it's stacked directly on top of a second, arguably stronger offer ("Fall candle event, lowest price of the season, $11.95 all 3-wick candles") that's been the headline of two of the last three sends. Putting the reused candle deal beneath a reused soap deal makes the top of this email feel like leftovers rather than a fresh urgency play.
- Compared to 2026-09-11's "$11.95 for NEW fall favorites" (7.00/10) and 2026-09-10's live-fire candle launch (8.00/10), this send has less editorial spark — no fall styling, no seasonal copy, just a bathroom-counter product shot and a barcode. It's functional and honest (no buried fine print, no reveal gimmick like 2026-09-07's flash sale), but it reads as filler in a week that's already run this soap promo and this candle price point.
- Judgment: a decent transactional send for someone already in the market for hand soap, but it's not going to stand out in an inbox that's seen "$3.50 today only" and "$11.95 lowest price" multiple times this week already.

## What's working

- Barcode + in-store code (FEELGOOD) is genuinely useful and rare among these brand sends — makes the offer actionable both online and in-store.
- Price and product are the very first thing the eye hits — no scroll needed to see the offer.
- "Buy 3 Get 3 Free" body care offer further down adds a second concrete deal without feeling like clutter.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage.
- "We think you'll love" product row gives clear cross-sell CTAs (Shop buttons) with product names visible.

## What's weak

- The candle offer ($11.95 all 3-wick, "lowest price of the season") is a near-verbatim repeat of the 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-11 sends — same price, same claim, third time this week.
- "Gentle reminder" as a subject frame undercuts urgency — it signals this is a rerun, not a new event, which is honest but low-energy.
- No seasonal or lifestyle styling in the hero — just a faucet/counter product shot, flatter than the farmhouse/fall creative seen in recent sends.
- Free shipping banner ($50 threshold) is generic boilerplate, adds no differentiation.
- Nothing distinguishes today specifically — "one day only" appears in the subject but isn't visually reinforced with a countdown or bold urgency treatment in the hero itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't stack two repeated offers back to back.** Lead with whichever deal hasn't been run this week, or combine soap + candle into one "today only" multi-deal banner instead of two full-width sections.
- 2. **Add visible urgency to the hero**, not just the subject — a "today only" badge or countdown treatment on the $3.50 soap image would justify the "gentle reminder" framing.
- 3. **Vary the candle creative** — same Pumpkin Apple 3-wick shot for the third time risks banner blindness; rotate scent or angle.
- 4. **Tighten the subject** — drop the soft "gentle reminder" opener and lead with the number, which tests stronger for this brand's cadence.
