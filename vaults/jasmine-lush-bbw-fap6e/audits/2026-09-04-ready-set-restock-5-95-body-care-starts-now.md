# ready, set, restock. 🏃 $5.95 body care starts now.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Feel-Good Fall Event" $5.95 body care hero (Santal Ridge wash bottle, water-splash shot) that ran under the "something to feel good about starts tomorrow" subject also logged today (2026-09-04, 6.00/10) — same creative, different subject line, sent the same day. That's a same-day duplicate send with a repackaged subject, which is a cadence red flag even before judging the creative itself.
- On its own merits the hero is clean: sharp product photography, a clear $5.95 price, "300+ items to choose from," and a visible Shop Now button above the fold. But the email doesn't stop at one offer — it stacks a second "$1.95 hand cream + promo code" block and a third "$13.95 3-Wick Candles" block before even getting to a "We think you'll love" grid. Three distinct offers plus a merch carousel in one email dilutes the primary CTA and makes it read as an inventory dump rather than a targeted send.
- Judgement: solid execution, weak strategy — good photography and honest pricing undercut by same-day duplication with the "feel good" send and by CTA-fatiguing offer-stacking.

## What's working

- Hero price ($5.95) and product are unambiguous, with a legible "Shop now" button directly under the offer.
- "STARTS TODAY" and "TODAY ONLY" ribbons give real urgency without ALL-CAPS shouting or excessive punctuation.
- Product photography (water splash on the Santal Ridge bottle) is high quality and on-brand.
- The promo code block (MAINSQUEEZE) is clearly presented with an in-store barcode option, useful for a "buy online, pick up in store" shopper.
- "Made without sulfates, parabens or dyes" callout on the hand cream adds a real, specific selling point.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of today's earlier "something to feel good about" send — same hero image, same $5.95 body care offer, different subject line, same day. That's cadence fatigue, not a fresh creative.
- Three separate offers ($5.95 body care, $1.95 hand cream, $13.95 candles) compete for attention before any secondary product grid — no single clear primary CTA.
- "Enjoy up to 24 per customer" / "Up to 24 per customer" language on both the hero and hand cream reads as bulk-buy/hoarding messaging rather than a personal treat — slightly off-putting framing.
- Long unsubscribe/legal footer block with dense fine print at the bottom is standard but adds visual weight with no upside.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending two emails with the same hero on the same calendar day — pick one subject/creative pairing per day, per persona segment, to avoid subscriber fatigue.
- 2. Cut the email to one hero offer ($5.95 body care) and move the hand cream and candle offers into a follow-up send or a "shop more deals" secondary section without full duplicate CTAs.
- 3. Replace "24 per customer" language with framing that feels like abundance/self-care ("stock up on your favorites") rather than a purchase-limit disclaimer.
- 4. Move "We think you'll love" personalized grid higher, right under the hero, so the email doesn't feel like three flat sales stacked in a row.

- **Subject Alt B:** `your Santal Ridge favorite is back — $5.95`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $1.95 hand cream and $13.95 candles while they last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `300+ body care picks starting at $5.95 — today only`

# Technical Email Audit — Bath & Body Works "ready, set, restock" campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC/ExactTarget) template (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, `cl.S7.exct.net`, `exct.net` open-tracking pixel) using an HTML4 doctype with legacy MSO conditional blocks. Automated QA passed 57% of checks with 3 warnings and no hard failures, concentrated in accessibility (missing alt text), insecure image transport, and missing authentication/unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?MDSZZF6J4WIE5PIUWOBR4PL5CQ.70252&d=70252&bmt=0` — standard SFMC open tracking, functioning as expected (HTTPS, 1x1 img).
- 39 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for redirect-domain links; not independently verified as live/non-404 in this pass).
- No broken or malformed `href` syntax observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images missing `alt` text** (QA-confirmed): `LiveContent.aspx` dynamic content image, `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`, `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`, `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`. Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled/skip them.
- **Insecure image source**: `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Most mail clients (Outlook, Gmail) will block or warn on mixed-content HTTP images inside an otherwise HTTPS-served email — likely to render as a broken/blocked image for a portion of recipients.
- External `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=...')` used twice (Helvetica Neue, Source Sans Pro) inside `<style>` blocks. Google Fonts `@import` is unsupported/stripped by most major clients (Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail on iOS in dark mode edge cases), so this is dead weight that adds latency without a rendering payoff — layout correctly falls back to the `font-family` stack, so this is cosmetic risk, not a functional bug.
- Preheader div (`your cozy season essentials are here. 🧣🍁🍎`) does not match the subject line copy (`ready, set, restock... $5.95 body care`) — this is a leftover/stale template preheader artifact, not a content judgment call; worth flagging as a template QA miss.
- Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional with full MSO conditional comment scaffolding — appropriate/expected for cross-client email compatibility (Outlook desktop rendering engine), no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`) or AMPscript artifacts visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing** (QA-confirmed) — may be a relay-side capture issue (AgentMail) rather than absence at the source; cannot confirm from HTML body alone since one-click unsubscribe is a header-level, not body-level, mechanism.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed; if genuinely absent at the ESP, this is a compliance gap under Gmail/Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements for senders at this volume.
- **Authentication-Results header missing** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer in the truncated HTML — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM physical-address and unsubscribe-link requirements are met in the body since the source was cut off before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links route through the SFMC click-tracking domain (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters in the visible source — standard redirect-wrapper behavior, not itself an issue, but means UTM/landing-page alignment can't be verified without following redirects (out of scope for static HTML parsing).
- One directly visible destination URL: `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=090426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_595TBC_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL_` — uses a proprietary campaign-code query param (`c=090426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_595TBC_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL_`) rather than standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` params. This is consistent internally but won't populate standard UTM-based analytics (e.g., GA4) unless the landing page has separate mapping logic — flagged as a continuity risk if GA4/UTM attribution is expected downstream.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to the 4 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for purely decorative images; descriptive text for the LiveContent dynamic image and product PNGs).
2. Change `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` to `https://` to prevent client-side blocking of that dynamic content block.
3. Remove the unused Google Fonts `@import` rules — no functional benefit given near-universal client stripping, and it adds an external network dependency at render time.
4. Sync the hidden preheader text with the actual subject line/offer to avoid a stale-template artifact reaching inboxes.
5. Verify at the ESP/relay level whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — if AgentMail is stripping them in relay, that's a deliverability/compliance risk independent of the source template; if never sent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against send logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observable via this relay path.
