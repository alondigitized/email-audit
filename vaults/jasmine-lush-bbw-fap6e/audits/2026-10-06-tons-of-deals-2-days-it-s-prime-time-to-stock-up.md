# tons of deals. 🤑 2 days. ⏳ it's prime time to stock up. 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same-day follow-up to this morning's "tomorrow, it's 👏 all 👏happening.👏" teaser. It reuses the same five red holiday tiles: $12.95 3-wick candles, $4.95 body care, $5.95 single-wick candles, $3.50 foaming hand soaps and $3.50 Wallflowers refills. That's good for clarity, since every tile leads with a huge price. It's bad for freshness, because a subscriber who opened the teaser has now seen the same creative twice in one day.
- The email is easy to scan, and the prices are low enough to be the real draw. But five tiles of equal weight leave no single hero. Then a "Fall sale / Up to 75% off" bar sits below them and muddies the message. The subject's "tons of deals" is vague next to the specific prices inside, and "prime time" is a Prime Day-style hook the creative never pays off.
- Judgement: a solid, mechanically sound promo, but a repeat send with a generic subject. It lands mid-pack, behind the 10/05 "rake in the savings" send, which had one clear hero.

## What's working

- Each tile leads with a huge price in white on red ($12.95, $4.95, $5.95, $3.50), so the offer is the first thing you see.
- Every tile has the same layout and a "Shop now" button, so the CTA is consistent.
- The "2-day offer" and "Today only" labels give credible, specific urgency.
- The free-shipping-over-$50 bar and the Rewards banner at the top add value without crowding the hero.
- The holiday product photography (nutcracker candle, Fresh Balsam soap) fits the season.

## What's weak

- The same five tiles appeared in this morning's teaser, so the send reads as a repeat.
- The "Fall sale / Up to 75% off" bar below the tiles competes with the tile prices. It also feels off-season next to the red holiday creative.
- The tiles mix "Online exclusive," "Today only" and "2-day offer," and the In-store and online coverage differs between tiles. The ending date is unclear.
- The white outline "Shop now" buttons are thin and low-contrast against the busy red bokeh photos.
- The fine print is a very long block at the bottom, with different dates and restrictions per product. That undercuts the "tons of deals" simplicity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero instead of five equal tiles. Make the $3.50 soaps or the $12.95 candles a full-width hero with the end date, and shrink the other offers to a row.
- 2. Drop the 75% fall-sale bar from this send, or move it above the fold only if it is the real offer. Two competing sale messages dilute both.
- 3. Use solid, high-contrast CTA buttons, and make the copy specific ("Shop $3.50 soaps" instead of "Shop now").
- 4. State one clear end date in a banner ("Ends Oct 8") instead of mixing "Today only" and "2-day offer."
- 5. Don't re-send identical creative on the same day. Change the hero or add a "ends tomorrow" frame so it feels like a new beat.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements containing `<style>` and MSO conditional comments appear before `</head>`.
  - Further wrapper tables sit between `</head>` and `<body>`, so content is outside `<body>`.
  - Clients may hoist, drop or reparse this unpredictably.
- **Duplicate charset declarations.** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" ... charset=UTF-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Doctype mismatch.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used alongside HTML5-style `<meta charset>` and `lang`.
- **Ineffective web font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host. `@import` is also unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stack handles it, so the request is wasted.
- **Missing alt text on 5 images** (all under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`):
