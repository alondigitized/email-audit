# ⏳ only a few hours left for $2.75 Wallflowers.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "ends today" Wallflowers send in 24 hours, and it carries the same offer as the 2026-11-08 "lowest price of the YEAR. ends today." email. The black "Final hours" bar sits over a hero of five red and green refills hanging from ribbons. "Lowest price of the year" and a big **$2.75** sit below them, with a "Shop now" button at the bottom of the hero. The price is the first thing the eye lands on, and the urgency is credible.
- The email loses focus below the hero. A second "Shop now" block pushes a $14.95 3-wick candle with a model hero ("Give 3 wicks of wonder"). A third block promotes "Up to 30 days of room-filling fragrance" with no price. Those two blocks pull attention away from the one deal that expires tonight. The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" sections then add clutter after the close.
- Verdict: a solid, price-led closer, but it is a near-repeat of today's earlier send, and the lower half dilutes the urgency.

## What's working

- The "Final hours" bar sits directly above the hero, so the deadline is clear at once.
- The large **$2.75** with "Lowest price of the year" is the clear visual anchor, and the "Shop now" button sits right under it.
- The Wallflowers refills hang festively from ribbons, which fits the season.
- A free-shipping bar ("any $50 purchase") and a Rewards plug sit at the top for members.
- The "You might like" row shows a Wallflowers refill, which keeps the product tied to the offer.

## What's weak

- The cadence is heavy. Today's earlier send, yesterday's and the day before's all ran the same $2.75 message, so this one reads as a fourth near-duplicate.
- The 3-wick candle block, with a model hero and a $14.95 price, competes with the $2.75 hero. It is a different offer on the same deadline.
- The room-filling fragrance block has no price or offer, so its "Shop now" is vague and adds length.
- Only the hero and the price are visible without scrolling. The "Up to 24 per customer" limit is tiny and faint.
- The email is very long. After the offers come a brand-values block, a "More to love" list and a dense fine-print wall.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the candle and "30 days of fragrance" blocks, or fold them into one small cross-sell below the hero. Tonight's email should have a single offer.
- 2. Make the deadline specific. "Ends 11:59 PM ET" is more credible than "Final hours".
- 3. Move the "Up to 24 per customer" limit next to the price. It is a reason to buy in bulk, and it is a quiet piece of honesty.
- 4. Show the individual refill scents with names on the hero, so a shopper sees a product they might want.
- 5. Vary the creative across the sequence. Don't send the same hero on the day's second and third sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `⏳ Ends at midnight: $2.75 Wallflowers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: stock up on $2.75 refills (limit 24)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lowest price of the year ends tonight. Limit 24 per customer.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorite holiday scents, $2.75 each until 11:59 PM ET.`
