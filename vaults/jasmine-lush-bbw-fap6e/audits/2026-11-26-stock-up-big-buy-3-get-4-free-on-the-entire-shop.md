# stock up BIG: buy 3 get 4 FREE on the entire shop*. 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Buy 3, Get 4 FREE offer BBW has pushed all week, in a competent but tired execution. The hero does its job. "ONLY ONCE A YEAR" over a gift-bag photo, then a red block with "ENTIRE STORE BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in giant white type, makes the offer impossible to miss. A second $40 gift-with-purchase module sits below it, and the page ends with a clear Black Friday 2026 hours banner.
- The problem is repetition. This is at least the fifth send in two days with this deal, after "we NEVER do this. buy 3, get 4 FREE on EVERYTHING*" and "feast first. then shop." earlier today. "Stock up BIG" adds no new angle or urgency. The email also stacks two offers and four equal-weight category buttons, so the click path is diluted.
- Verdict: a solid, on-brand reminder with nothing new to say. It will convert people who were already going to buy, and it wears down everyone else.

## What's working

- The "ENTIRE STORE BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" block is huge, high-contrast and readable in a second.
- The category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) route shoppers by interest.
- The "Online only | In stores tomorrow" banners and the Thanksgiving closure note in the footer banner give honest context on timing.
- The Rewards and free-shipping-over-$50 strips at the top add member and value hooks without crowding the hero.
- The "You might like" row with three named products (Champagne Toast, Winter Candy Apple, Mahogany Teakwood Intense) gives some specific, tappable product content.

## What's weak

- The offer is the same as the previous four sends, so there is no fresh reason to open or click.
- The two-offer stack (Buy 3 Get 4 plus the $40 gift with $40 purchase) and the four category buttons give no single dominant CTA.
- The "*" is backed by a wall of dense fine print at the bottom. "Free items are the 4 lowest priced in each set of 7" is a small gray line under the hero, so the real mechanic is easy to miss.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY 2026" marquee strip at the very top is cropped at both edges ("CK FRIDAY 2026"), which looks slightly sloppy.
- Almost no people or lifestyle imagery appears, apart from the one crop in the $40 gift module. There is no social proof or reviews either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical offer. Lead each send with a new hook: a specific bundle example ("7 candles, pay for 3"), a last-chance clock, or a best-seller. That change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Put the math in the hero. Show a worked example, such as "Pick 7, pay for 3", right under the headline rather than in small gray text.
- 3. Use one primary CTA ("Build my 7") and demote the category buttons.
- 4. Move the $40 gift module into a separate send, or shrink it, so it doesn't compete with the main offer.
- 5. Add a countdown or a "tonight only" marker, and fix the cropped marquee.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build (`stylingblock-content-wrapper camarker-inner`) with solid dark-mode and Outlook scaffolding. QA flagged 3 warnings, and the main confirmed defects are missing alt text on 5 images and a non-functional font `@import`. The header-based warnings (unsubscribe, authentication) are unverified, because the relay likely strips those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were validated. Treat them as unverified, not as passing.
- **Click and image domains differ.** Click tracking goes through the third-party host `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112626_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B3G4_ICYMI_BAL_G6M_V3`. Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, and the sender domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. The click domain is not aligned with the sender or image domain. This is common practice, but it adds a reputation dependency on a separate host.
- **Tracking-pixel markup.** QA reported an `<img>` whose identifier is the `everestengagement.com/ea/...` URL, which looks like an open-tracking pixel, with no `alt`. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 content images, confirmed by QA.** All are on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  If these carry offer text (for example "buy 3 get 4 free"), that content is lost with images blocked or in a screen reader. Add `alt=""` only if they are purely decorative.
- **Ineffective web-font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host. Gmail and many clients also strip `@import`. The `.font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers rendering, so this is wasted markup only.
- **Possible structure problem.** In the excerpt, the `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no visible `</head><body>`. If that holds in the full source, block content sits inside `<head>`. The source is truncated, so verify this against the full HTML.
- **Redundant markup (minor).** There are duplicate `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` blocks (`[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]`). There is also both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`. Neither is harmful.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta, dark-mode handling (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`), and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the available source. The per-recipient `e=200511831233` parameter is populated. Coverage is limited by truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** The RFC 8058 one-click header is absent for the same reason. It matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, so confirm against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Check it in a direct-delivery test, such as a Gmail "Show original" view.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address are not verifiable** in the truncated source. QA lists no in-body failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
