# just like summer, 50% off select items won't last forever. ☀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different creative from the six-day Buy 3 Get 3 Free flood — a "sweet end to summer" 50% off apple-orchard hero paired with a second, separate $6.95 body care offer under a "TODAY ONLY!" banner. That's actually the problem: two competing offers stacked in one email (50% off select items, then a flat $6.95 with a barcode/promo code) muddies which deal the reader should act on, and the fine print at the bottom ("Select items," "up to 24 per customer," date-restricted "50% off entire store stores only") suggests both offers have real restrictions the hero doesn't disclose.
- Visually it's clean — nice seasonal apple photography, clear "Shop now" CTAs, a barcode for in-store use is a thoughtful touch — but nothing here says "your category." It's generic seasonal storewide messaging, not the fragrance/candle-forward creative that's driven the recent Fall Scent Event sends.
- Net: better production value than the BOGO spam train, but two offers competing for attention and vague "select items" language keep this in solid-but-unremarkable territory.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed and genuinely seasonal (apples, warm light) — a break from the repeated diffuser/candle shot used all week.
- Two distinct, visible CTAs ("Shop now" on hero, "Shop now" on $6.95 tier) with clear button styling.
- "TODAY ONLY!" banner and barcode-for-in-store block add credible urgency and a practical in-store path.
- Product grid ("We think you'll love") gives concrete named products (Champagne Toast, Wallflowers Refill, Wild Blackberry Jam) with individual Shop buttons.

## What's weak

- Two competing hero offers (50% off select items vs. $6.95 body care with a promo code) split attention instead of leading with one clear headline deal.
- "Select items" is vague — no product examples or category shown in the hero to tell the reader what's actually discounted.
- This is the same brand that's sent six Buy 3 Get 3 Free emails in the past five days; pivoting to a different, less generous-sounding offer (50%/$6.95) right after a storewide BOGO blitz risks feeling like a step down, not an upgrade.
- Fine print reveals real restrictions (date window, exclusions, no stacking with other codes) that aren't hinted at above the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with whichever converts better (likely the $6.95 body care deal, since it's concrete and code-driven) and demote the other to a secondary module — don't run both as equal top-of-email CTAs.
- 2. Replace "Select items" with 2-3 named categories or product shots in the hero so the discount feels tangible.
- 3. Surface the promo code (SOGOOD) near the hero CTA, not just next to the second offer — right now a skimmer might click "Shop now" on the apple hero and never see the code.
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit end time next to "TODAY ONLY!" to make the urgency concrete rather than just a banner label.
- **Subject Alt A:** `today only: $6.95 body care + 50% off select items`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your last day for 50% off summer favorites ☀️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `plus $6.95 full-size body care with code SOGOOD`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ends tonight — shop before select items sell out`
