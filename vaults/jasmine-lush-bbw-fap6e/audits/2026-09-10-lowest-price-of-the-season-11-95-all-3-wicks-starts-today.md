# LOWEST price of the season. ⭐️ $11.95 all 3-wicks. ⭐️ starts today.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the actual live-fire launch of the "Fall Candle Event" teased yesterday's send ("something really big starts tomorrow," 2026-09-09, 9.00/10) — and it delivers on the promise cleanly. The hero is exactly what was foreshadowed: $11.95 all 3-wick candles, "lowest price of the season," a moody lit-candle shot with autumn leaves, and a clean "Starts today" band up top. It's a real, specific, honest discount with no reveal-button gimmick and no buried fine print in the hero itself.
- Where it loses points versus yesterday's teaser is focus: the email immediately stacks a second, unrelated offer (Buy 3 Get 3 Free body care) right below the candle hero, competing for the same eyeball real estate. Two big offers in one scroll dilutes which one a reader is actually supposed to act on first, even though both are legitimate value.
- Still, this is a strong, on-brand, well-produced send that matches the current campaign and reads as premium rather than urgency-spam. Solid mid-to-upper tier email for the brand.

## What's working

- Clear, specific, high-value hero offer: $11.95 3-wick candles, framed as "lowest price of the season."
- Strong visual hierarchy in the hero block — dark moody background, bold price, single "Shop now" CTA button.
- "Starts today" banner creates credible, non-manipulative urgency (no countdown clock gimmicks).
- Product shot is high quality and on-theme (Leaves candle, autumn styling with berries/leaves).
- Second offer (Buy 3 Get 3 Free body care) is stated plainly with no fine-print bait-and-switch in view.

## What's weak

- Two major, unrelated offers (candles vs. body care) compete in the same short scroll, splitting attention right after the hero.
- "Up to 24 per customer" purchase-limit language sits awkwardly under the hero image, a slightly odd note to lead with visually.
- The "We think you'll love" product grid uses generic gray placeholder-style backgrounds that look a bit flat/templated next to the richer photography above.
- No loyalty/member pricing or perks called out despite this being a rewards-heavy brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the two offers into separate emails or clearly subordinate the body care offer (smaller module, no full hero treatment) so the $11.95 candle deal stays the single takeaway.
- 2. Move or shrink the "Up to 24 per customer" line — it reads like a limiting/negative note directly under the exciting price reveal.
- 3. Upgrade the "We think you'll love" tile backgrounds to match the styled photography used elsewhere in the email.
- 4. Add a loyalty callout (e.g., "Rewards members get X") somewhere above the fold to reinforce membership value.
