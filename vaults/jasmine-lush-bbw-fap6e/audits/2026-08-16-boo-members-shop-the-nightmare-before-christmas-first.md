# 👻 boo! members shop The Nightmare Before Christmas first.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works ditches the candle/soap discount treadmill for a genuine event send: early access to the Disney/Tim Burton "Nightmare Before Christmas" collaboration. This is a different beat than the last five sends (2026-08-11 through 2026-08-15), which were all $2.95-$5.95 hand soap and candle price plays — so cadence-wise, this is a welcome break from repetition, and the "members shop first" framing gives it a real exclusivity hook instead of another coupon stack.
- The hero art is genuinely strong — moody purple-to-cream gradient, the glowing moon, the hand-drawn Burton-style logo lockup — it's the best-looking creative in this brand's recent run. But the email undercuts its own hero: there's no CTA button anywhere near it. You get a striking image and then a hard cut to a generic "TOP OFFERS" grid of unrelated category icons (Fragrance, Candles, Wallflowers, Men's Shop) that have nothing to do with the Nightmare Before Christmas collection being advertised.
- Net: gorgeous, differentiated top-of-funnel creative let down by a broken path to purchase. This should have been the strongest BBW send in the recent run; instead it's an average one because the click path doesn't follow through on what the hero promises.

## What's working

- The hero art (glowing moon, hand-lettered Nightmare Before Christmas logo, silhouette vines) is distinctive and on-brand for the film, a clear step up in production value from the recent soap/candle sends.
- "TOMORROW ONLY! ONLINE ONLY!" banner at the very top gives immediate, credible time-boxed urgency.
- "Members shop first" plus the footer fine print (exact access window: Aug 16, 6:00 AM ET–Aug 17, 5:59 AM ET) signals real loyalty-tier exclusivity, a fresh angle versus the price-ladder emails this brand has been running all week.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- The hero image has zero CTA — no "Shop the Collection" button, no link cue, nothing. A reader who's excited by the moon graphic has no obvious next step.
- Below the hero, "TOP OFFERS" / "TODAY'S DEALS" shows generic category tiles (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Soaps, Men's Shop, Home Care, Gifts) that are completely disconnected from the Nightmare Before Christmas product line — it reads like a different email got pasted underneath the hero.
- No actual product shots from the collaboration (candles, soaps, or packaging in the film's aesthetic) anywhere in the body — you never see what you're actually being invited to buy first.
- No visible price or discount — this is fine for an access-only send, but combined with the missing CTA and missing product shots, there's genuinely no path to a transaction anywhere in this email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real CTA button directly under or over the hero — "Shop Early Access" or "Shop The Collection" — linked to the collaboration page. This is the single biggest fix; right now the best-looking creative in weeks has no button.
- 2. Swap the generic "TOP OFFERS" category grid for 3-4 actual product shots from the Nightmare Before Christmas line (candle, soap, wallflower in the film's art style) so readers see what they're getting early access to.
- 3. Pull the exact access window ("Aug 16, 6AM ET–Aug 17, 5:59AM ET") up from the footer fine print into the visible body copy — it's a strong urgency lever that's currently buried.
- 4. Consider surfacing "member" status/tier language in the hero itself (e.g., a small "Rewards Members Only" badge) to reinforce exclusivity where the eye actually lands.
- **Subject Alt A:** `👻 early access starts tomorrow: Nightmare Before Christmas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `members only: shop Nightmare Before Christmas 24hrs early`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The full collection unlocks for you first — Aug 16, 6AM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Candles, soaps & more from the Burton collab. Members shop first.`

# Technical Email Audit — Bath & Body Works "Nightmare Before Christmas" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) table-based HTML email with AMPscript/personalization comments intact but largely unresolved in this render; automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and multiple images without alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?HGD2R7Q3GX2UJJLJLPNECWEPZU.70252&d=70252&bmt=0` (1x1, standard SFMC click-tracking domain).
- 29 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirect domains — not itself an error, but means link-destination validity is unverified).
- No broken or malformed `href` attributes observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images flagged with missing/empty `alt` text, two of which have **empty `src` attributes** — this is a rendering defect, not just an accessibility gap, since an empty `src` can cause the browser to re-request the current page or render a broken image icon in some mail clients:
  - `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` (src present, alt missing)
  - Two images with both `src` and `alt` empty
  - `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=2005118…` (alt missing, appears to be a tracking/engagement pixel mislabeled as a content image)
- Hidden preview-text padding block (long string of `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` repeated ~450 times) is present and correctly wrapped in `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden` — no leakage into visible body expected, but the sheer repetition count is excessive and adds ~9KB of dead markup to payload size.
- `@import` Google Fonts declared twice (once in `<head>`, once again inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper`) — redundant, and `@import` is unsupported in Outlook/Windows Mail regardless, so it silently fails there with no fallback font-stack adjustment.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) correctly scoped for Outlook DPI handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved AMPscript merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- Several AMPscript-related **HTML comments** are present but appear to be documentation/placeholders rather than live code: `<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript...-->`, `<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->`. These are inert comments, not executable AMPscript, so they pose no rendering risk — but the fallback logic they describe (no default APT if NTF_Flag is "N") should be verified against the live send if this template is reused for non-NTF campaigns, since the comment explicitly states there's no fallback.
- No issues found with rendered personalization in the available excerpt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — required for CAN-SPAM one-click compliance at scale and for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements; may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence, but should be confirmed against raw headers at the ESP.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — without this, one-click unsubscribe cannot be offered by mailbox providers, increasing spam-complaint risk.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable from this capture; cannot confirm authentication compliance either way.
- No in-body unsubscribe link/footer was present in the truncated HTML excerpt to independently verify.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query parameters observed; BBW uses a proprietary tracking-code scheme instead, e.g. `c=081526_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_NBC_PREV_BAL_G6M_V11` on the `bbwus.everestengagement.com` engagement pixel — consistent with SFMC + Everest Engagement stack, not a UTM/GA integration.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (destination URLs were skipped by the prober per QA findings) — flagging as unverified rather than an issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results are present in the raw ESP-side headers (not just the AgentMail-relayed copy) — these are the highest-severity items for deliverability/compliance.
2. Add `alt` text to all four flagged images, and fix the two images with empty `src` attributes (likely a template placeholder that failed to populate).
3. Remove the duplicate `@import` font declaration — keep one, and confirm a web-safe fallback font-stack is set for Outlook.
4. Trim the redundant hidden-preheader padding string; ~450 repetitions is far beyond what's needed to push preview text and adds unnecessary payload weight.
5. If this template is reused outside NTF (New-to-File) campaigns, verify the APT fallback behavior noted in the AMPscript comments, since no default is defined.
