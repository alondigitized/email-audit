# 🙋 heads up - buy 1, get 1 free candles end today.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a "First Flicker of Fall" BOGO candle push, and it's a strong, focused open even by BBW's own recent standards. The hero is clean: one product (Pumpkin Pecan Waffles candle), one offer (Buy 1, Get 1 Free, lowest-priced free), one deadline ("Ends Today"), one button. No stacking three unrelated deals under the fold like the 08-06/08-07 sends — this is more disciplined.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold. A second full "Ends Today" promo (men's body care/cologne) crashes right into the hero with zero visual separation, followed by a CEW Beauty Awards finalist plug that has no offer at all, then a category grid. By the third screen the email has lost its own thesis — is this a candle BOGO email or a general fall catalog blast? The subject sells one clean idea; the body sells four.
- Net: a genuinely good hero pulled down by bloat. Solid open, good click on the hero, but anyone scrolling past screen one drifts out of "candle" territory the subject promised.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed — the candle is the clear focal point, autumn leaf prop reinforces "fall," no clutter.
- Offer is unmissable: "BUY 1, GET 1 FREE" in large bold type with "lowest-priced item is free" fine print right there, not buried.
- "ENDS TODAY" banner appears twice, reinforcing urgency without feeling shouty (no exclamation points, no all-caps overuse).
- Single clear CTA button ("SHOP") directly under the offer, no scrolling needed to find it.
- No render bugs — clean image loads, aligned text, no overlap.

## What's weak

- The second "ENDS TODAY" block (men's body care/cologne) immediately below the candle hero dilutes the subject's single-topic promise — a reader who opened for candles hits an unrelated offer within one scroll.
- CEW Beauty Awards section has no discount, no CTA urgency, and no candle tie-in — it reads as brand ego copy sandwiched between two real offers.
- "Lowest-priced item is free" and "Limit 24" disclaimers are small and easy to miss relative to the giant offer type above them — borderline bait-and-switch feel if a shopper assumes both candles are free.
- Visual hierarchy resets three times (candle hero → body care hero → beauty awards hero) instead of one dominant hero, weakening the single strong CTA principle from the rubric.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the men's body care/cologne block out of this send entirely — it's the single biggest thing hurting subject/body alignment. If BBW wants to cross-sell, put it in a footer strip, not a second full hero.
- 2. Move the CEW Beauty Awards block to the very bottom (or a separate send) — it has no offer and breaks momentum between two real deals.
- 3. Bump the "lowest-priced item is free" and quantity/exclusion fine print to a slightly larger, higher-contrast size directly under the headline offer, not small gray text.
- 4. Add a specific candle name or scent count to subject/preheader to differentiate from prior BOGO candle sends (e.g., "80+ candles including Pumpkin Pecan Waffles") since candle BOGO is a recurring beat for this brand.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pumpkin Pecan Waffles and more — lowest-priced candle is free, today only.`

# Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "B1G1 Candles" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud email (AMPscript comments, `click.e2`/`image.e2` tracking domains) with standard table-based HTML for legacy client support; automated QA shows a 57% pass rate driven by missing authentication/unsubscribe headers and several alt-text gaps.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel present and correctly sized (1×1): `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?65PJ4XWUPCSUVOQIUNPX5LD36M.70252&d=70252&bmt=0`.
- 34 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated prober (expected behavior for SFMC-wrapped `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects) — destination URLs could not be verified for validity or 404s from this audit; flag as unverified, not confirmed-broken.
- One image element has an empty `src` attribute (QA: `Image missing alt text: : src:`), which will render as a broken image icon in clients that don't suppress it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Stylesheet uses `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` twice (once in `<head>`, once inline in a `stylingblock-content-wrapper`) — `@import` is unsupported/stripped by Outlook (Word rendering engine) and many webmail clients, so this will silently fail rather than break rendering; redundant duplicate import is dead weight.
- Legacy DOCTYPE (`HTML 4.01 Transitional`) and MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) confirm Outlook-targeted table layout — consistent with intended multi-client support, no issues found there.
- Preheader hidden text block uses a long repeated sequence of `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` character padding to control preview-text length. This is functional (not a design issue) but the volume of zero-width/invisible characters increases HTML weight and can be read aloud character-by-character by some screen readers if `aria-hidden` isn't applied — no `aria-hidden="true"` attribute is present on this `<div>`.
- 4 images flagged missing alt text:
  - `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` — no alt attribute.
  - Empty `src` image — no alt attribute (compounds the broken-image issue above).
  - A second empty `src`/empty-alt image.
  - Image whose `alt` attribute appears to contain the tracking/campaign string (`https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=0809...NRDM_B1G1ALLCDL_AM_BAL_FULL_V1`) rather than descriptive text — likely a template merge field that failed to resolve into human-readable alt copy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- AMPscript/dynamic-content comments present but not resolved in this rendered source (`<!--Set variables to support dynamic template attributes-->`, `<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript...-->`, `<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N"...-->`) — these are template scaffolding comments left in the sent HTML; they don't affect rendering but indicate the AMPscript logic layer is exposed as HTML comments in the delivered payload (minor payload bloat, not a functional bug).
- The `everestengagement.com` alt-text string (`NRDM_B1G1ALLCDL_AM_BAL_FULL_V1`) looks like an unresolved internal campaign/creative code merged into a customer-facing attribute — evidence a merge field mapped to the wrong field (alt vs. internal tracking ID).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers (WARN). Cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support from headers alone; may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence — flag for header-source verification.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header** (RFC 8058): not found — if genuinely absent, this email does not support one-click (Gmail/Yahoo) unsubscribe, which is required for bulk senders under Google/Yahoo's 2024 sender guidelines.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture. No conclusion can be drawn on authentication compliance without this header.
- No visible physical-address or unsubscribe-link markup in the truncated source provided; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance from available HTML — recommend reviewing the untruncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated HTML (all 34 outbound links are wrapped in `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects and were skipped by the prober), so landing-page/UTM alignment cannot be verified from this data.
- No issues found regarding domain consistency — all tracking/image assets consistently use `bathandbodyworks.com` subdomains (`e2.`, `image.`, `click.`), no mismatched or third-party lookalike domains detected.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify with the sending platform whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (may be an AgentMail relay capture gap) — if genuinely missing, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
2. Confirm `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) via a direct-inbox header capture, since relay-based capture appears unreliable here.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 flagged images; fix the image with an `alt` value that currently contains a tracking/campaign string instead of copy.
4. Remove the empty-`src` `<img>` tag(s) or supply valid asset URLs to avoid broken-image glyphs.
5. Drop the redundant duplicate `@import` Google Fonts declaration; since Outlook ignores `@import` anyway, add a web-safe fallback font in the `font-family` stack rather than relying solely on the import.
6. Add `aria-hidden="true"` to the hidden preheader-padding `<div>` to prevent screen readers from vocalizing the invisible filler characters.
7. Re-run link validation with tracking-redirect resolution enabled (follow redirects) to confirm the 34 skipped links resolve to valid, UTM-tagged landing pages.
