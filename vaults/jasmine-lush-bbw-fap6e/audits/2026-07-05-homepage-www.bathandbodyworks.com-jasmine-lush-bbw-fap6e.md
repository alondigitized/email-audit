# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I literally cannot even see the homepage right now because Bath & Body Works hit me with a "Press & Hold" bot-check wall the second I landed. What little is peeking behind the modal looks warm and candle-adjacent, which is very on-brand, and I did catch a "Today only! $5.95 Wallflowers® 2-packs" flash at the top — which, as a wallflower gifter, would normally have me hyped. But that modal is doing real damage.

## What's working

- **The Wallflowers flash-deal banner** — "$5.95 Wallflowers® 2-packs, Today only, Online only" is literally the product I buy to give as gifts. The right offer, in the right place, for the right person. Even through a bot wall I clocked it.
- **Navigation bar is clean** — hamburger, search, logo, account, bag — all present, no clutter. I could probably navigate out of this mess if I could tap anything.
- **Warm hero image peeking through** — what little I can see behind the modal reads like a cozy lifestyle/candle vibe. That's the B&BW aesthetic I'm here for.

## What's weak

- **Bot verification modal is the entire experience** — "Before we continue... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" covers the whole homepage. Nothing below the banner is shoppable or readable. This is a full stop.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — I have My Bath & Body Works Rewards and I came here hoping to see my points or a member offer. Nothing.
- **No CTA I can actually tap** — the only interactive element I see below the top bar is the bot-check button. No "Shop Now," no "See the Deal," nothing.
- **Hero is completely hidden** — I cannot tell what the main campaign is. Summer? Sale? New launch? No idea.
- **Reference ID footer** — `97afda25-785a-11f1-a80b-eda48d237d0e` is technically diagnostic info that shipped to my screen. Not a great look.

## Recommendations

- **Move bot verification to a less hostile pattern** — a lightweight invisible CAPTCHA (like hCaptcha passive) or challenge-on-checkout instead of a full page block would keep the homepage usable while still catching bots.
- **Pin the Wallflowers flash-deal to a sticky banner with a countdown** — "3 hours left" next to $5.95 would turn that banner from a passive notice into a genuine urgency driver for someone like me who responds to candle/wallflower deals.
- **Surface a rewards-balance CTA above the fold** — even a small "Hi Jasmine, you have 450 points" strip for logged-in members would immediately differentiate this from Amazon and make me feel like I belong here.
- **If the bot wall must exist, show offer context behind it** — let the hero breathe visually so users know what they're about to unlock; a blurred but legible "BOGO 50% Sale — Shop Now" creates pull instead of a cold wall.
