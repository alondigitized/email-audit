# look what’s here early...our Black Friday buy 3, get 4 FREE* deal. 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest of BBW's Black Friday sends so far, because it finally states the offer in full. The red block says "ENTIRE STORE BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in huge white type, with four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) right under it. The eye lands on the offer first. A second beat, "4 gifts to choose from plus, a free tote for $40," adds a gift-with-purchase hook.
- The problem is cadence and contradiction. Earlier today BBW sent "it's like dessert before dinner - this HUGE Black Friday deal starts tomorrow," and now it says "look what's here early." The email reads as a second launch announcement on the same day. After the 11/21 to 11/24 run of $1 sanitizers, $2.95 soaps and the $19.95 build-a-gift, a subscriber is on roughly their sixth promo in five days.
- The bottom third is weaker. Below the three-product row there is a huge empty white gap before the footer. The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text over a pale image and is nearly unreadable. Overall it's a good hero on a messy, over-frequent send.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: giant "BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" on solid red, with "Limited time only | In-store & online" above it.
- Four category buttons route the click to Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers or Hand Soaps, instead of dumping everyone on the homepage.
- The "FIRST TIME EVER 4 gifts to choose from plus, a free tote for $40" module has a clear "$40" price and an "Over $110 value*" badge, which add a second reason to buy.
- The "Black Friday 2026" banners and the shiny gift-bag hero feel seasonal and on-brand.
- The top strip shows Rewards and free shipping at $50, and the "More to love" links add loyalty and pickup options.

## What's weak

- A large blank white gap (about 500px) sits between the footer links and the bottom pattern strip. It looks like a broken or unloaded section.
- "Our feel good formula" has white text on a pale cream image, which is hard to read, and "Learn more" is a weak CTA in that spot.
- The fine print is thin. "Free items are the 4 lowest priced items in each set of 7 eligible items*" is tiny grey text under the headline. It is disclosed, but it is easy to miss.
- The three-product row (Winter Candy Apple soap, Cocoa Shea Rich hand cream, Snowflakes & Cashmere candle) shows no prices and no buttons, so it is dead weight.
- The messaging is muddled. The "Black Friday 2026" copy says "It's here. In stores & online Wednesday and Friday. Online only Thursday (stores closed for Thanksgiving)." That is a lot of calendar logic for a quick skim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cadence. Send one "it's live" email, not a "starts tomorrow" tease and a "here early" follow-up on the same day. If both must go out, make the second a clear reminder with a deadline.
- 2. Remove the blank gap and fix the low-contrast "feel good formula" banner, or cut the banner entirely.
- 3. Add a worked example near the hero, such as "Buy 3 soaps, get 4 free = 7 for $X". It makes the math concrete and reduces the asterisk anxiety.
- 4. Put prices and "Add to bag" buttons on the three products, or drop the row.
- 5. Simplify the date line to "Online now. In stores Wed + Fri. Closed Thursday."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday came early: buy 3, get 4 free 🛍️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix any 7 soaps, candles, wallflowers or body care. The 4 lowest-priced are on us.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free tote with $40, in stores Wed + Fri, online all week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based layout with dark-mode CSS and MSO conditionals. Eight of the 48 tracked links were skipped by the QA probe, and five images lack alt text. The authentication and unsubscribe header checks could not be completed (see §5). The visible HTML source is truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 48 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified. The link example in the QA data is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B3G4_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_F…`. This is an Everest redirector, not a direct `bathandbodyworks.com` URL.
- **Identifier in the URL:** The `e=` parameter (`200511831233`) looks like a per-recipient or per-send identifier. It is exposed in the query string of every tracked link, including the one flagged under images.
- **Mixed hosts:** Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which is a first-party subdomain. Click tracking goes through a separate third-party domain (`everestengagement.com`). This is not a defect, but it means the sender-aligned domain and the click domain differ.
- **Unconfirmed destinations:** I can't confirm the number of broken links or HTTP status codes from the data provided.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images, confirmed by QA).** The files are:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`

  All are on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`. If any of these carry promotional text, that content is unavailable to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Tracking element flagged as an image:** The sixth item is flagged under the name `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=2005118…`. That means the image has no alt text and its `src` or name is the tracking URL. It is probably a tracking pixel or a linked image, and it should have `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Table placed in `<head>`:** In the visible source, `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no `</head>` or `<body>` before it. This may be a truncation or ESP-template artifact. If it's real, it's invalid markup that some clients will repair differently.
- **Doctype and charset:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but `<meta charset="utf-8">` is an HTML5 construct. `Content-Type` is also declared twice (`http-equiv` and `charset`). Both say UTF-8, so there is no functional conflict.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't host. The request will fail or return nothing, and `@import` in `<style>` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) keeps text readable.
- **Dark mode:** The email supports dark mode through `prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`, and `color-scheme` meta tags. No defects are visible in the portion shown.
- **Other elements:** `role="presentation"` is present on the wrapper table, and `lang="en"` is set. The `<title>` is `Bath & Body Works ` with a trailing space and no campaign context. This is minor.
