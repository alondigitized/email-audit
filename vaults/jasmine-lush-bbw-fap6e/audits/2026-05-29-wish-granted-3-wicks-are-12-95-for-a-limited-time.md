# 💫 wish granted: 3-wicks are $12.95 for a limited time. 🌟
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Day 2 of B&BW's $12.95 3-Wick Candle arc — the creative brief is nearly identical to yesterday's May 28 "starts today—$12.95 steals" send, which earned a 9/10. The gold price badge still anchors the hero cleanly, and "Sunshine Scents" is the right seasonal play for late May. But the "wish granted" subject line pivot is decoration masquerading as narrative — it trades the directness that made yesterday's opener strong for a vague whimsy that the two star emojis can't rescue.
- Below the fold, the email stacks BUY 3 GET 3 FREE on body butters/hair care, a "Kitchen Care is Finally Here" category launch, a TOP OFFERS thumbnail grid, and a MORE TO LOVE strip. That's four distinct modules competing with a hero that should own the whole send. The "LAST FEW DEALS" urgency banner at the top contradicts the timeline — this arc is only 24 hours old.
- The $12.95 badge still converts. Everything else in this email is friction.

## What's working

- Gold "$12.95" price badge dominates the hero and is immediately scannable — same visual anchor that worked on May 28
- "Sunshine Scents" seasonal framing is appropriate and consistent with the ongoing summer arc
- BUY 3 GET 3 FREE secondary offer gives basket-builders a real reason to click beyond the candle deal
- "Kitchen Care is Finally Here" adds a genuine novelty signal that partially offsets the Day-2 re-run feel
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breaks

## What's weak

- "Wish granted" subject framing is whimsical but unmemorable — the star emojis substitute style for specificity
- "LAST FEW DEALS" urgency banner is misleading — the arc launched yesterday; "last few" feels manufactured rather than earned
- No hard deadline anywhere in the email — "for a limited time" is doing all urgency work unsupported by any date
- Generic "SHOP" CTA when "Shop 3-Wicks" would cost zero characters and add real clarity
- Multi-module layout (4+ sections) dilutes the hero offer that should own the full send

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or a specific date. B&BW's own closing-bell sends (May 27: "ends today," May 25: "final hours") are consistently stronger performers — the urgency mechanism is proven; use it
- 2. **Sharpen the CTA copy** — replace "SHOP" with "Shop 3-Wicks" or "See All Scents" — verb + noun, zero ambiguity
- 3. **Kill two of the four below-the-fold modules** — Kitchen Care deserves its own send; burying a new category launch inside a candle arc email underserves both
- 4. **Rewrite the subject** — cut the "wish granted" wrapper and let the price lead
- **Subject Alt A:** `$12.95 3-Wick Candles — ends this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3-wicks at $12.95 — pick your summer scent now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Over 300 scents. One price. Grab yours before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Summer candles for $12.95 — your cart is waiting.`
