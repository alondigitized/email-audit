# 40% off EVERYTHING* is still ON, but not for long...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- The top of this email is strong. A red "Black Friday week starts now" block carries a huge **40% off Entire store** over a shelf of Bath & Body Works products. A "2 days left" banner sits above it and four category buttons sit below it: Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps. The offer and the urgency are clear in about two seconds. The subject's "not for long" is backed by that "2 days left" strip.
- The bottom half is weaker. A tall blank gap follows "More wonder is waiting…", and the product row of three items has no visible prices or buttons. Another large empty stretch sits near the footer, with only short stray lines of text and no readable legal copy. This is the same pattern as the 11/18 "Black Friday week starts now" send, which also had a broken lower half.
- This is the fourth 40%-off send in two days. The creative is a recycled hero, so subscribers will see it as repetition. The urgency is real, but the email adds nothing new beyond the "2 days left" strip.

## What's working

- The 40% hero is unmistakable and sits above the fold.
- "2 days left" adds a credible deadline directly over the offer.
- Four category buttons let a shopper jump straight to their interest.
- The alternating image-and-text gift blocks are easy to scan.
- The Rewards, texts and pickup links give useful secondary paths.

## What's weak

- The large blank gap below "More wonder is waiting…" reads as missing content.
- The three-product row (Champagne Toast, Fresh Balsam, Winter Candy Apple) shows no prices or buttons.
- A blank band near the bottom has only stray short lines, so the footer and fine print are unreadable. The "*" in the subject has no visible exclusions.
- The gift blocks ("A gift wish," "Luxurious, trendy and oh-so giftable") have no CTA buttons, so the clicks they could drive are unclear.
- The "Our feel good formula" text sits over a pale image with low contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank regions. Fill the gap under "More wonder" with real product tiles, and make the footer render legibly with the exclusions.
- 2. Give each gift block a "Shop [category]" button, and add prices to the three-product row (for example "Now $X, was $Y").
- 3. Make the deadline specific, for example "Ends Friday 11:59 PM ET," and don't send another near-identical 40% email before then.
- 4. Add a line near the hero that says what the asterisk excludes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Friday: 40% off your whole cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days left for 40% off Wallflowers + candles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gift sets, Wallflowers and hand soaps, all 40% off through Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on holiday scents before the sale ends. Free shipping at $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source has several structural and accessibility problems: five images lack alt text, and a `<table>` appears to sit inside `<head>`. Compliance, authentication and link-destination checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured and the links are redirect-wrapped. QA reports a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

- **No evidence of broken links.** None can be confirmed from the data provided.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** All are hosted at `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  
  If these are content images, such as the offer, they need descriptive `alt`. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`. Screen readers may read out filenames or URLs, and blocked-image clients show nothing.
- **A tracking image has no alt.** QA flagged an image whose `src` is `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/...`, which is most likely the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Invalid document structure.** In the truncated source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags directly. I see no `</head>` or `<body>` in between, so the table is in the head. This may be an artifact of truncation, so verify it against the full source. If it is real, clients will handle the layout inconsistently.
- **Legacy doctype with HTML5 meta.** The page uses the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with `<meta charset="utf-8">`. It also declares charset twice, once via `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and once via `meta charset`. This is redundant and low risk.
- **Font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that isn't served by Google Fonts. The request probably fails or returns nothing. `@import` is also stripped or unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. The stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial, so the impact is a wasted request rather than a visible break.
- **Dark mode is handled.** The source includes `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas, `prefers-color-scheme` rules and `[data-ogsc]` selectors. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible portion. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and body copy weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, one-click unsubscribe fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` should be checked directly with a DNS lookup.
- **Footer content is unverified.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section. The `.footer-disclaimer` class exists, but its content wasn't visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs and destinations can't be assessed.** Click URLs go through `everestengagement.com` redirects, and the final landing URLs weren't resolved because those links were skipped. The campaign is identified only by the `c=` code `111926_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. Whether landing pages honor the "40% off EVERYTHING*" promo is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to the five listed images: descriptive text for content images, `alt=""` for decorative ones.
2. Give the tracking pixel `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.
3. Confirm in the full source that the `<table>` is inside `<head>`. If so, move it into `<body>`.
4. Re-run the audit with raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. The relay, not the sender, may be dropping them.
5. Resolve the redirect chains on a sample of the 49 skipped links to check the final URLs, HTTP status and UTM parameters.
6. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue". Rely on the system font stack.
