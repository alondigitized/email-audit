# $5.95 single wicks = instant (early) holiday cheer. 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- The first screen works. A "2 days only" bar sits over "Holiday head start" and a huge **$5.95** price. Behind it is the green Vanilla Bean Noel candle with a gift bow. "All Single Wick Candles" names the offer, and "Shop now" is clear. This is the same $5.95 single-wick sale that this morning's "oh, what fun - sleigh your faves tomorrow. 🛷" teaser set up, so the payoff is on message. It is also much the same price-led creative Bath & Body Works has run all week, such as the 10/17 $6.95 body care send and the 10/16 $5.95 men's send.
- The body weakens after the hero. A second "Today only" block pushes $1.95 hand cream with code GETREADY, which competes with the candle offer and clashes with the "2 days only" bar above it. The Platinum mist block has a headline and an award badge but no visible button. Large empty gaps follow, and the footer area is blank apart from a few stray underlines. It reads as a strong top third on a half-broken render.
- Verdict: a good price-led hero, undercut by a split offer, a CTA-less third block and visible layout gaps. Call it a solid 6.

## What's working

- The $5.95 price is the largest element on the screen, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- "All Single Wick Candles" is a clear, broad scope, and the exclusions and limit ("Excludes boxed candles. Up to 24 per customer.") are stated right under the price.
- The Vanilla Bean Noel candle with its bow looks seasonal and ties in with "Holiday head start".
- The "2 days only" bar gives a credible deadline.
- The hand cream block has a clear price, a code and its own "Shop now" button.

## What's weak

- Two urgency labels clash. "2 days only" sits above "Today only", so the reader can't tell what ends when.
- The $1.95 hand cream and code GETREADY split attention from the candle offer. Candles need no code, hand cream does.
- The "Stronger scent, better you" Platinum block has no CTA or price. The award badge is nice, but it is a dead end.
- There are big blank gaps below the Platinum image and across the lower third. The footer text isn't visible, only stray underlines.
- The "Our feel good formula" text is pale on a cream background and nearly unreadable. The circular "New" badge floats awkwardly over the candle area.
- The barcode graphic with "GETREADY" is clutter and does nothing for the reader.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the candle sale the only offer above the fold, and move hand cream into a clearly secondary "also today" row. Stop mixing "2 days only" and "Today only".
- 2. Give every block a button. Add "Shop candles" or "Shop Platinum" to the mist section, or cut the section.
- 3. Fix the empty regions and the invisible footer text so it renders legibly.
- 4. Raise the contrast on "Our feel good formula" and drop the barcode graphic.
- 5. Make the CTA say what it does, for example "Shop $5.95 candles".
- **Subject Alt A:** `$5.95 candles, 2 days only. 🕯️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Every single wick is $5.95 (yes, all of them)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Vanilla Bean Noel and more, up to 24 per customer. Excludes boxed candles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before the holiday rush. Plus $1.95 hand cream with code GETREADY.`
