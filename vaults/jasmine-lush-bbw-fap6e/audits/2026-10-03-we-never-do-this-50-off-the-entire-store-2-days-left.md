# 🗣️we NEVER do this: 50% off the entire* store. 👏 🤗 😍2 days left!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first send. "Half off. Full spree. 50% off Entire store" sits in a burnt-orange block above a lineup of fall products, and nothing competes with the number. It's nearly the same hero as the 2026-10-02 "50% off the entire* store" send, so it works as a reminder. Because it repeats that creative, it adds little new.
- The body is where it loses steam. Below the hero are four "New" editorial tiles (fragrance mist, Home Sweet Holidays, a cleanser, Midnight Rouge). None of them says 50% off or has a price or a button. A reader who wants to act on the deal has to go back up to the four category pills. The "2 days left" urgency lives only in the subject line. The hero says "Limited time" and the body never gives a date. The fine print near the bottom is very long, and the asterisk on "Entire store" invites distrust.
- Overall it's a solid, readable send with a strong hero and a weak middle. It's a day-two reminder, not a campaign.

## What's working

- The 50% number is enormous and sits first in the eye path.
- The product lineup under the hero is seasonal (Frosted Cranberry, fall leaf candle) and fits the moment.
- Four category pills (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) give quick entry points.
- The free-shipping bar at the top gives a second incentive without clutter.
- The "You might like" row shows named products, which adds some product detail.

## What's weak

- "2 days left" appears only in the subject. The hero says "Limited time" with no date, so the urgency doesn't carry into the body.
- The four "New" editorial tiles have no prices, no 50% reminder and no buttons, so they dilute the offer.
- The offer is asterisked ("Entire store*") and the exclusions are in a dense fine-print block at the bottom.
- There is no single hero CTA button. The only clickable options are category pills and small text links.
- The creative nearly duplicates the 2026-10-02 send, so cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a dated deadline in the hero ("Ends Oct 5") and add a real "Shop 50% off" button under the headline.
- 2. Cut the four "New" tiles to one or two, and add the "50% off" price tag to each tile that stays.
- 3. Put a short exclusions line right under the hero so the asterisk doesn't feel buried.
- 4. Replace the 2026-10-02 hero image with a fresh one so a subscriber who saw yesterday's email sees something new.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Bath & Body Works, "50% off the entire* store"

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) build with markup validity problems: tables sit inside `<head>` and before `<body>`, and charset is declared twice. Six images have missing alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay didn't capture the headers and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking links on the `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirect domain were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and status are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Second tracker with no alt:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFFSHOP_AM_BAL-NTF-O` is a third-party engagement domain. It is separate from the SFMC open pixel (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?...&bmt=0`). The QA check flagged it as an image missing alt text.
- **Hidden open pixel:** The open pixel is wrapped in `<table width="0" height="0" style="mso-hide:all; line-height:0; font-size:0;">`. This is standard practice, but the `1x1` `<img alt="">` is fine for accessibility.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements are placed inside `<head>`, which implicitly closes the head early.
  - More wrapper tables sit between `</head>` and `<body>`.
  - Parsers will hoist or reorder these, which makes the `<style>` blocks inside them unreliable.
- **Duplicate charset declarations:** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
- **Mixed doctype and markup:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with HTML5 `<meta charset>` and XHTML-style self-closing `/>`.
- **Missing alt text on 6 images:**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - The Everest-hosted image above
- **Bad web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't serve, so it is a wasted request.
- **Unsupported `@import`:** `@import` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The Source Sans Pro `@import` is affected too, and the declared stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial.
- **Text zoom disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `<body>` blocks user text resizing on iOS, an accessibility issue.
- **Responsive rules likely don't apply:**
  - The visible wrapper tables use an inline `style="width: 600px;"` with no `email-container` class.
  - The `.email-container { width:100% !important }` rule therefore wouldn't match them.
  - The inline fixed width would force horizontal scroll on narrow viewports. This is based on the visible portion only, since the source is truncated.
- **Dark mode coverage:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared, but the dark-mode overrides in the visible CSS are gated behind `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` and `(-webkit-hyphens: none)`, so they only cover Apple clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The truncated remainder couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** The QA check reports `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) as missing. The check itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them against the raw original message.
- **Authentication unknown:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment couldn't be checked.
- **Footer not seen:** The visible-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so CAN-SPAM footer elements are **unverified**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable:** All visible links route through the SFMC redirect, and the probe skipped all 50, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID only in the third-party URL:** The only campaign identifier visible is `c=100326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFFSHOP_AM_BAL-NTF-O` in the Everest URL.
- **Landing-page alignment not testable:** It can't be assessed without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML plus raw headers. That would confirm the footer unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Resolve a sample of the 50 `click.e2` links through the redirect chain. Confirm each lands on a valid bathandbodyworks.com URL with consistent `utm_*` parameters.
3. Add alt text to the 5 content images. Add `alt=""` to the Everest tracking image if it is a pixel, or meaningful alt text if it is visible content.
4. Move the wrapper tables and `<style>` blocks into a valid `<body>` structure. Remove the duplicate charset meta.
5. Replace the `@import` font loading with `<link>`-free system font stacks, and drop the nonexistent "Helvetica Neue" Google Fonts import.
6. Remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, or replace it with `100%`.
7. Apply the `email-container` class, or `max-width:600px; width:100%`, to the main wrapper table so the mobile media query takes effect.
8. Fix the QA summary inconsistency: it reports "3 warnings" but lists 9. The checker's tally appears to group them by category.
