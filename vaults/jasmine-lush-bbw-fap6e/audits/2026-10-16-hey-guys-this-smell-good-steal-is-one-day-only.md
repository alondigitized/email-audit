# hey guys...this smell-good steal is one day only. 🧔🏻‍♂️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- The hero does its job. "Just added: your new routine" sits over a cologne bottle and a men's body care tube. The offer is concrete: $5.95 men's full-size body care and $35 full-size cologne, with the FRESHPICKS code. It matches the "hey guys" subject, and it's a rare Bath & Body Works send that speaks to men. The "Today only" tag adds credible urgency.
- The rest of the email undercuts it. Below the hero it turns into a recycled catch-all. The BOGO candles module is the same "Cozy now. Merry later." creative from yesterday's 10/15 send. The 75% off Fall sale strip is last week's 10/13 headline. A one-day men's deal ends up sharing space with three other promos, a "You might like" row of candles and a mist, and a wall of fine print. A recipient who wanted the cologne deal has to scroll past a lot of noise, and the candle and fall-sale modules pull attention away from the men's offer.
- My judgement: a solid hero on a bloated, repetitive body. This brand sends a near-identical stack every day, so the men's angle is the only thing here that feels new.

## What's working

- The hero pairs a clear price ($5.95 / $35) with a "Today only" tag and a visible "Shop now" button.
- The product photography is clean and large, and the cologne and body care are easy to tell apart.
- The "hey guys" subject lines up with a men's-specific hero, which is real segmentation.
- The FRESHPICKS code and the 24-per-customer cap are right under the offer, so there are no surprises at checkout.
- The Rewards banner at the top is a light, non-intrusive loyalty touch.

## What's weak

- The BOGO candle module repeats 10/15's creative, and the 75% off strip repeats 10/13's headline. It reads like a template stack, not a men's send.
- There's a large empty gap between the "New Everyday Luxuries" module and the Fall sale strip. It looks like a broken or missing image.
- The "New formula for 2X the concentration" callout box sits awkwardly on top of the floral image.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale, low-contrast text on a cream background that is hard to read.
- The fine print is a huge block of dense text at the bottom, with overlapping promos (BOGO, 75% off, $5.95, $35). That weakens the "honest offer" feel.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now" or "Learn more". None names a verb plus a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the men's offer. Keep the hero and a men's-only product row (cologne, body wash, deodorant), and drop the candles, Everyday Luxuries and Fall sale modules. The segmentation is the one thing worth protecting.
- 2. Fix the empty gap and the overlapping callout, and darken the "feel good formula" text, or remove that module.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with specific CTAs like "Shop men's body care $5.95" and "Shop $35 cologne".
- 4. Show the price in the subject. "Smell-good steal" is vague, and the $5.95 and $35 numbers are the hook.
- 5. Collapse the fine print into a short summary with a "Details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `guys: $5.95 body care + $35 cologne, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `new routine, old price: men's body care $5.95 🧴`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Full-size cologne is $35 and body care is $5.95. Use code FRESHPICKS before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limit 24 per customer. Stock up on men's favorites while the price lasts.`
