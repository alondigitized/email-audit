# ⏰ set your alarms - EVERYTHING* is buy 3 get 3 free starting tomorrow.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the biggest, cleanest storewide offer this brand has run all week — Buy 3 Get 3 Free across Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, and Hand Soaps blows past the $1.95 car scents and $3.75 hand soap promos from earlier sends. The "STARTS TOMORROW" framing plus a diffuser-and-candle hero shot sells "Fall Scent Event" as a seasonal reset, not just another flash discount, and the orange Buy 3 Get 3 block is impossible to miss even on a fast scroll.
- The tradeoff is length: after the offer block, the email sprawls into six sequential product-collection blurbs (Preserve Collection, Scent as Design, Iconic Fragrances, Blend Bar, Santal Ridge) before repeating the offer and finally landing a real CTA button ("Start your list"). That's a lot of scroll for a reader to get to an actionable button, and the primary CTA is essentially buried mid-email rather than living with the hero.
- Solid hero, strong offer, but the CTA placement and mid-email padding cost it clicks it should be getting for free.

## What's working

- Storewide Buy 3 Get 3 Free is the strongest, clearest offer signal seen in this brand's recent sends — bigger than the $1.95/$3.75 category promos.
- "STARTS TOMORROW" banner + "Fall Scent Event" gives the send a clear seasonal narrative instead of another generic discount blast.
- Product categories eligible for the deal (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) are spelled out as buttons right under the offer, reducing ambiguity about what qualifies.
- Free shipping banner at $50 is visible above the fold, adding a second concrete incentive.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, all product photography loads correctly.

## What's weak

- No clickable CTA button sits with the hero offer block itself — a reader has to scroll through five collection blurbs before hitting "Start your list."
- The offer literally starts tomorrow, not today, which softens the urgency compared to this brand's usual "today only" plays.
- The middle section (Preserve Collection, Scent as Design, Iconic Fragrances, Blend Bar, Santal Ridge) reads as generic brand storytelling with no prices or specific offers, diluting the one strong hook.
- The asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" in the subject isn't resolved anywhere visible in the render — no fine print or exclusion note is shown before the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Duplicate the "Start your list" button directly under the orange Buy 3 Get 3 hero block — don't make readers scroll past five collection teasers to find the only CTA.
- 2. Cut the mid-email collection gallery to 2-3 items max, or move it below a second, earlier CTA — right now it's the longest, least offer-focused stretch of the email.
- 3. Since the deal doesn't start until tomorrow, add a specific start time/date to the hero copy ("Starts 8/29 at 12am ET") to make the countdown feel real rather than vague.
- 4. Resolve the "*" from the subject line visibly near the hero — even a one-line "*exclusions apply" builds trust and matches rubric expectations for offer honesty.
