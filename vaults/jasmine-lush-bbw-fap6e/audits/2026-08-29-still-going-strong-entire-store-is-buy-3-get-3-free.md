# still going strong 💪 - ENTIRE STORE* is buy 3 get 3 FREE.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Buy 3 Get 3 Free email in two days from Bath & Body Works, and it's dressed up as a seasonal launch — "The Fall Scent Event" hero — before pivoting to the same storewide offer that's been running since yesterday's "⏰ set your alarms" send. The subject line "still going strong 💪" is honest about the fatigue: it's explicitly signaling this is a continuation, not news, which undercuts urgency rather than building it. Visually the email is clean and on-brand, with a genuinely nice fall product lineup (Preserve Collection, Santal Ridge, Blend Bar), but the offer itself is now old news dressed in new packaging.
- The execution is solid — no render bugs, clear hierarchy, good product photography — but the cadence problem is the real story here. A subscriber who opened yesterday's two Buy 3 Get 3 Free emails has already seen this exact deal twice; this is essentially a re-skin with fall-collection imagery bolted onto an unchanged promo block ("Limited Time Only! Buy 3 get 3 FREE | Entire store | Lowest-priced items are free").

## What's working

- Clear, unmissable hero offer — "ENTIRE STORE Buy 3 get 3 FREE" in a bold orange block with category CTAs (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) directly beneath it.
- Strong product photography throughout — the diffuser/candle flatlay, the Santal Ridge bottle shot, and the "We think you'll love" row all look premium and on-brand.
- No layout bugs — everything renders cleanly, buttons are visible, no broken images or overlapping text.
- Fall repositioning ("The Fall Scent Event," "New season stock-up starts now") gives this send a distinct narrative from the two identical Buy 3 Get 3 Free emails sent the day before.

## What's weak

- The subject line "still going strong 💪" openly admits this is a repeat of an offer subscribers have already seen twice — it reads as filler rather than a reason to open.
- The offer block itself is pixel-identical in substance to yesterday's two sends: "Buy 3 get 3 FREE, Entire store, Lowest-priced items are free" — zero escalation or variation.
- Long scroll before any secondary content — six stacked product sections before reaching the second CTA, which is a lot of scrolling for a subscriber who's already seen the deal.
- Cadence: this is the 4th near-identical Buy 3 Get 3 Free email in roughly 24 hours, which risks unsubscribes more than it risks missed sales at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-announcing the same offer — if it's genuinely "still going," lead with what's new (the fall collection) and treat the discount as secondary reinforcement, not the headline block it currently is.
- 2. Add a real deadline. "Limited Time Only" with no date/time is doing nothing after three prior sends with the same vague urgency — a countdown or explicit end time would be more credible than repeating "limited" for the fourth time.
- 3. Trim the scroll — six product spotlights before the second CTA is excessive; cut to 2-3 hero fall items and get to "Shop" faster.
- 4. Vary the creative meaningfully from yesterday's sends, not just retitle it — same orange offer block, same button style, same layout skeleton.
