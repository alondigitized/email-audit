# body care is $5.95. 💖 so much to love, so little price.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in two days pushing $5.95 body care. It follows the 2026-11-12 teaser ("tomorrow's about to be BIG for your routine. 🤫") and the "body care. $5.95. it's happening. 🤩" payoff. The hero is still strong. "Select body care" sits over a huge magenta $5.95, with a "Shop now" button right under it. The "Up to 24 per customer" cap is small but visible.
- The email then tries to carry three offers at once. It has the $5.95 hero, an "Ends today" candle BOGO that repeats the 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-11 sends, and a $40 Festive Reset Gift Set. The eye lands on the hero first, but the second and third modules pull attention away from it. The "Ends today" label on the BOGO is the only urgency in the email, and it competes with the "Limited time only" labels on the other two offers.
- The render also has visible gaps. The product row has no usable imagery and no prices. A large empty white block sits between the footer module and the bottom pattern. The result is a decent promo with weak structure, and it's the same story told for the third day running.

## What's working

- The $5.95 price is huge and magenta, and it dominates the hero. It's impossible to miss.
- The "Shop now" button is visible and sits close to the offer.
- The "Ends today" bar on the candle BOGO gives that offer a credible deadline.
- "Dermatologist approved, feel good formulas" adds trust copy in the hero.
- The free-shipping bar at the top ("Free shipping with any $50 purchase") adds a second reason to buy.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete in one email: $5.95 body care, candle BOGO, and the $40 gift set. There is no single clear priority after the hero.
- The candle BOGO has run in several recent sends (2026-11-09 and 2026-11-11). It reads as recycled.
- The product row (Snowflakes & Cashmere, Ocean) shows thin, washed-out images and no prices or buy buttons. It looks unfinished.
- A large blank white block fills the lower third of the render, below the "Find a store" bar. It looks like a layout bug.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has white text on a pale background. It's hard to read.
- The "Up to 24 per customer" limit is tiny, and the $5.95 offer has no deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the $5.95 hero and add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight." Move the candle BOGO and the gift set to their own sends. This lifts the email most.
- 2. Fix the blank space and the weak product row. Either fill them with real product tiles that carry prices and buttons, or remove them.
- 3. Make the "feel good formula" banner readable by using dark text or a darker background.
- 4. Put the purchase limit next to the price, in larger type, so it doesn't read as buried fine print.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 2026-11-12 sends. Use a different hero product or a new angle, so it doesn't feel like day three of the same email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on the Salesforce Marketing Cloud (stylingblock) / Everest tracking stack. QA found 5 images missing alt text, and the unsubscribe headers and authentication results could not be verified. The visible markup also has structural problems: a malformed head and an unusable web-font `@import`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 45 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed.
- **Tracking domain:** Click tracking runs through `bbwus.everestengagement.com`. That is a third-party domain, separate from the sender domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which is on the sender domain.
- **Image used as a tracked link:** The fifth missing-alt finding is labeled with a tracking URL rather than a file name: `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_595TBC_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. This suggests an `<img>` whose `src` is an Everest endpoint, probably an open-tracking pixel. It has no alt text and was not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (4 images from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`):**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - Counting the Everest tracking image above, 5 images in total lack alt. If any of these are content or CTA images, they will be blank when images are blocked, and screen readers will read the file name or nothing.
- **Malformed document structure:** In the supplied source, `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the `<meta>` tags inside `<head>`. No `</head>` or `<body>` is visible before it. This may be a truncation artifact, but if it is real, clients will handle the invalid nesting inconsistently.
- **Web-font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. "Helvetica Neue" is not a Google Fonts family, so the request will not return a usable font. `@import` is also stripped or ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The `.font-main` Helvetica/Arial fallback stack still applies.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the head uses HTML5 `<meta charset>` alongside `http-equiv` Content-Type, so the charset is declared twice.
  - The `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` block is duplicated in the `[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]` conditionals.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags are present, along with `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` overrides. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so tokens in the body or footer cannot be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** QA reports it not found. QA notes it may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** QA reports it not found, so RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for senders at this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is unknown. This is probably a relay artifact.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Click tracking:** The only tracking-parameter evidence is the Everest URL's `e=200511831233` and `c=111326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_595TBC_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters are visible.
- **Destinations:** Redirect targets were not resolved (see section 2). Whether UTM or campaign parameters survive to the landing pages cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 listed images. Give the Everest tracking image `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, or confirm what it is.
2. Fix the document structure so `<head>` is closed and `<body>` opens before the first `<table>`. Check whether the source is malformed or only truncated.
3. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue". Rely on the `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallback stack.
4. Remove the duplicate charset declaration and the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.
5. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. Resolve the 45 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe. Confirm they return 200 and check that campaign or UTM parameters reach the landing pages.
