- **Open-pixel or linked image missing alt.** QA flagged `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_295HS_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL_...`. If it's a tracking pixel, it should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **5 content images have no alt text.** All are on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`

  With images blocked, the screen-reader and image-off experience is empty. The offer ("ALL hand soaps. $2.95") may be carried only by images. I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Markup structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the `<meta>` tags and before any `</head>`/`<body>` in the visible source. Content in `<head>` is invalid. Clients may handle it inconsistently, and some strip or reorder it. The source is truncated, so I can't rule out a later `</head>`.
- **Redundant declarations.** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" ...>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. The `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` XML is duplicated across the `[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]` blocks. This is harmless but adds weight.
- **Web-font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` references a family I don't believe Google Fonts serves. It's likely a failed request. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (Helvetica, Arial) covers it.
- **Doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is fine for email, but `lang="en"` on a 4.01 doc is non-standard (it should be `xml:lang` under XHTML). This is low impact.
- **Title.** `<title>Bath &amp; Body Works </title>` has a trailing space and carries no campaign information. This is minor.
- Dark-mode handling (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`, `color-scheme` meta) is present and coherent.
