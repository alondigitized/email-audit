# 📣 we NEVER do this. buy 3, get 4 FREE on EVERYTHING*. 💸💸💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in two days that pushes the same "Buy 3, Get 4 FREE" Black Friday offer. The subject line is the sharpest of them, because "we NEVER do this" adds a believable scarcity hook. The hero still works: "ONLY ONCE A YEAR" over a gift-bag photo, then a huge red block with "BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" and four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps).
- The body goes soft after that. A second promo block, "4 gifts to choose from plus a free tote for $40 with any $40 purchase", competes with the hero, and the "Over $110 value" badge adds more clutter. The email also has a timing contradiction. The top banners say "In stores tomorrow" while the lower banner says "Online only today (stores closed for Thanksgiving)". The bottom third is largely empty white space with floating underlines, which looks like broken or missing content.
- Judgement: a strong offer and a clear hero, weakened by a muddled second offer, a thin product section and an unfinished-looking footer. It is a solid 6-7 send, and it is repetitive for a subscriber who has already seen this deal twice.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "ENTIRE STORE BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in giant white type on red, readable in a second.
- The "ONLY ONCE A YEAR" line over the shiny gift-bag photo sets the seasonal, event feel.
- Four category buttons let the reader jump straight to the scent type they care about.
- The "Free items are the 4 lowest priced items in each set of 7 eligible items*" line sits right under the headline, so the fine print isn't buried.
- A free-shipping bar and a Rewards sign-up are visible at the top.

## What's weak

- The "$40 / free tote / choose 4 gifts" block splits attention from the main offer and adds a second, differently worded threshold.
- "In stores tomorrow" and "Online only today (stores closed for Thanksgiving)" appear in the same email, which is confusing.
- The lower half of the email is a large blank white gap with stray underlined fragments, and these look like broken images or missing text.
- The three product tiles (Champagne Toast, Winter Candy Apple, Mahogany Teakwood Intense) have no prices, no CTA and no deal tie-in. They are tiny and weakly linked to the offer.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text on a pale photo, so it is hard to read and off-message for a doorbuster send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the $40 gift/tote block, or move it below the product row, so the one offer is the Buy 3, Get 4 FREE deal.
- 2. Fix the timing copy so the email says one thing, for example "Online today, stores open early Friday".
- 3. Replace the product tiles with 3-4 bestsellers that show a "buy 3" price example and a "Shop now" button.
- 4. Remove the empty space and the stray footer fragments, and fix the footer render.
- 5. Drop the "feel good formula" banner, or use dark text on a solid background.
- 6. Vary the creative from the 11/25 sends ("you buy 3. you get 4 FREE*", "look what's here early...") so it doesn't read as a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `We never do this: buy 3, get 4 FREE (today only)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Buy 3, get 4 FREE on everything. Online today.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candles, soaps, body care and more. Your 4 lowest-priced items are free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stores are closed today, but the entire store is open online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`, `camarker-inner`) with 46 click-tracked links through `bbwus.everestengagement.com`. Confirmed defects are limited to missing image alt text and some markup and CSS hygiene. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains. Their final destinations, redirect health and any expired-link risk are unverified.
- **Tracking URL format:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112626_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B3G4_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. The `e=` value looks like a per-recipient identifier. The `c=` campaign code reads as promo `B3G4`, which matches the subject.
- **Mismatched hosts:** images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and clicks go through `everestengagement.com`. This is a normal ESP setup, but the click domain is not on the sender's branded domain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). Deliverability or link-reputation risk is possible, but nothing in the data confirms it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA on 5 images.** Four are hosted at `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/…`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  The fifth is the `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` image. It is most likely a tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. If any of the other four are promotional or navigation images, alt-text-less rendering with images blocked will lose the offer content.
- **Google Fonts `@import` for "Helvetica Neue":** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. Helvetica Neue is not a Google Fonts family, so this request will likely fail. Gmail and several other clients also ignore `@import`. The `font-main` fallback stack covers rendering, so the impact is a wasted request.
- **Markup structure:** in the visible source, a `<table>` follows the `<meta>` tags directly, with no `</head>` or `<body>` shown before it. The source is truncated, so this may be a truncation artifact. If it is real, it is invalid markup that clients may reflow differently.
- **Redundant MSO blocks:** the `OfficeDocumentSettings` XML appears twice, once under `<!--[if mso]>` and once under `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`. This is harmless duplication.
- **Doctype and meta mismatch:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with both `<meta charset="utf-8">` and the `http-equiv` Content-Type meta. This is redundant and not a rendering defect.
- **Dark-mode handling is present:** `color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes`, `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are all in place. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The footer and later body were truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, not evidence of a defect:** the QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and no `Authentication-Results`. Its own notes say the AgentMail relay may strip or fail to capture these. The SPF, DKIM and DMARC status and the one-click unsubscribe support can't be determined from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** these would be in the footer, which was truncated, so they could not be verified.
- The `From` address (`bathandbodyworks@e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) uses a branded subdomain, which is a good sign for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible.** Attribution runs through the tracking redirect (`c=…_B3G4_…`) instead. The final landing URLs and any UTM params were not resolved because the 46 tracking links were skipped. Continuity with the landing page therefore can't be verified.
- The campaign code `B3G4` matches the subject's "buy 3, get 4 FREE". This is consistent but unverified at the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` images listed in section 3. Set `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixel.
2. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the system font stack already defined in `.font-main`.
3. Confirm the `<head>`/`<body>` structure in the full source. If the table really sits inside `<head>`, move it into `<body>`.
4. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve a sample of the tracking links through their redirects to confirm landing URLs, HTTP status, and any UTM params.
6. Check the footer in the untruncated HTML for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
