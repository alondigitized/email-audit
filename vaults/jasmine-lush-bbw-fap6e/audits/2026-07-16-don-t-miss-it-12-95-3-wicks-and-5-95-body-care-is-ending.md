# don't miss it: $12.95 3-wicks and $5.95 body care is ending. 🤑
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the expected closing-day push on the Fall Favorites Event — the direct bookend to yesterday's "starting now" launch email (7/15, 7.00/10). The subject nails the formula: exact prices, both categories named, and "is ending" as the urgency closer. For a B&BW subscriber who saw yesterday's open, this functions exactly as intended — a deadline reminder with nothing to decode.
- The body, however, tries to carry too many promotions at once. Fall Favorites gets the hero correctly, but a "Summer Sale — Select Items 75% off" banner appears mid-scroll, directly competing for attention. That's two separate promotional frames in one closing-day email, and they fight each other. The Wallflowers section (dark, carnival-themed, occupying a full content block) further dilutes the "this deal ends today" urgency that should be driving every pixel.
- The "Picked just for you" section with named products (A Thousand Wishes, Champagne Toast, Into the Night) is the email's best move — specific product names give a real reason to click beyond a category page. But that strength gets buried under the weight of everything else. Net result: a competent closer that over-stuffs the scroll and blurs its own call to action.

## What's working

- Subject line does the core job — $12.95 and $5.95 prices plus "is ending" in one breath, no preamble needed.
- "ENDS TODAY! ONLINE ONLY!" header banner immediately communicates the deadline before a single scroll.
- Hero section cleanly pairs the two offer tiers (body care and candles) with autumnal visuals that match "Fall Favorites Event."
- "Picked just for you" product grid names specific SKUs and adds three distinct click targets with product names a subscriber would recognize.
- Wallflowers section introduces a valid secondary offer (fragrance refills) with its own CTA, cleanly separated from the hero.

## What's weak

- A "Summer Sale — Select Items 75% off" banner fires mid-scroll directly underneath the Fall Favorites hero — two competing promotional narratives in a single closing-day email, no priority signal between them.
- Every CTA in the email reads "SHOP" — no object, no destination hint; "SHOP CANDLES" or "SHOP BODY CARE" would've cost nothing.
- The Wallflowers section's large dark/carnival creative consumes the prime second-screen position that should've reinforced the closing urgency.
- Subject line clocks in around 62 characters (plus emoji), well past the ~50-char mobile threshold — "don't miss it:" alone is 15 chars of preamble before the actual offer.
- No loyalty or member-tier pricing visible anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Summer Sale banner or move it to a thin footer note.** "Fall Favorites is ending" and "Summer Sale 75% off" are incompatible hero messages. The 75%-off liquidation deserves its own send tomorrow — don't cannibalize today's urgency with a competing frame.
- 2. **Verb + noun on every CTA.** "SHOP CANDLES," "SHOP BODY CARE," "SHOP REFILLS" — one word added per button, measurable lift in click confidence.
- 3. **Compress the Wallflowers section to a single banner row.** Full-bleed dark creative is wasted on a closing reminder; a one-line callout ("+ Wallflowers Refills — also ending today") keeps the offer without stealing the scroll.
- 4. **Drop "don't miss it:" from the subject.** The prices and "is ending" are the hook; the preamble is padding that costs mobile characters.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "3-Wick / Body Care Sale Ending" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) template with Everest Engagement tracking. Core rendering and compliance infrastructure is functional, but three categories of issues warrant attention: missing alt text on multiple images, unconfirmed authentication headers, and absent List-Unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 tracking links skipped** — all CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` (ExactTarget click-redirect domain). No broken destination URLs were confirmed, but destination health is unverified because the QA probe skipped the redirect chain. This is expected behavior for tracked links; no action required unless destinations are in question.

**Open pixel present** — `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?TL53HP2Z6XCEBFVK2SFWYZB7UE.70248` correctly uses a 1×1 img inside a zero-dimension table (`width="0" height="1"`). No issue.

**Everest Engagement tracking beacon** — `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=0716...` uses a secondary engagement tracking domain in addition to ExactTarget. The `e=` parameter is **empty**, suggesting a merge token (`%%emailaddr%%` or equivalent) failed to populate at send time. This is the same image flagged for missing alt text below.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**6 images missing `alt` text (confirmed by QA):**

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| `a04fef77a8e834c2.png` | `https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/a04fef77a8e834c2.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=...` |
| `418d0db7...fd2.png` | `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/418d0db7...png` |
| *(2 images with empty `src` — likely conditionally loaded or render-blocked)* | `src=""` |
| Everest beacon img | `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=...` |

Alt text is absent on content images and tracking pixels alike. For the content images this is a WCAG 2.1 / accessibility issue; screen readers will announce nothing or a raw URL. For tracking pixels, `alt=""` (empty string) is the correct pattern — these appear to be omitting the attribute entirely rather than using `alt=""`.

**Duplicate `@import` for Google Fonts** — `Source+Sans+Pro` is imported twice: once in `<head>` and once inside a `<style>` block within the template body. This is harmless in most clients but adds unnecessary payload and can cause FOUC in Gmail's CSS stripping behavior.

**`<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` placement** — declared after the opening `<body>` comment block, not inside `<head>` before `<style>`. Most clients handle this gracefully but it is malformed per HTML spec.

**Fixed 600px width outer table** — the outermost centering table uses `width="600"` as an attribute, not a `max-width` style. On clients that don't support `@media` queries this will overflow on very narrow viewports. The inner content correctly uses `width="100%"` with media query overrides; the outer shell should match.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=` parameter is unpopulated** in the Movable Ink beacon URL:
`https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/a04fef77a8e834c2.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=071626_...`

`mi_u` is the Movable Ink unique subscriber identifier. If this is empty at render time, Movable Ink cannot personalize the image and will likely serve a fallback creative. This may be intentional for non-personalized blocks, but should be confirmed.

**`e=` parameter empty in Everest beacon** — `?e=&c=0716...` — `e` is the subscriber email token. If populated at send this would have resolved; the empty value in the captured source suggests the token was either not mapped or the relay captured the email pre-personalization.

**AMPscript/NTF comments visible in source** — Several AMPscript processing comments were not stripped before delivery:
```html
<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
```
These expose internal campaign logic (APT = Abandonment Propensity Tier, NTF = New-to-File flag). Not a security risk, but these should be stripped in the final send template.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — the QA relay did not capture this header. CAN-SPAM requires a functional unsubscribe mechanism; this header is also required for Google/Yahoo bulk sender compliance (≥5K/day). The header is almost certainly present at the MTA layer (ExactTarget auto-injects it) but was not visible through the AgentMail relay. **Confirm at the raw SMTP header level before treating this as a true gap.**

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 is now required by Google Postmaster for bulk senders. Same caveat: likely injected by ExactTarget's MTA but unconfirmed via relay.

**Authentication-Results not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay capture. Sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is an ExactTarget subdomain; standard BBW/SFMC deployments have SPF and DKIM configured. **Verify via MX Toolbox or a raw header capture that DKIM passes for `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.**

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters** — the HTML source is truncated, so a complete link audit was not possible. The `mi_ecmp` parameter (`071626_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W595BC_AM_NTH`) is consistent across observed tracking URLs, indicating campaign-level tagging is applied. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) exist on destination URLs behind the click-redirect is unverified due to the 24 skipped probes.

**`mi_ecmp` campaign code is present** on both the Movable Ink beacon and the Everest beacon — consistent, no mismatch.
