# get a first look at a ✨big deal✨ starting tomorrow…😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- B&BW is using this send to preview their Fall Sale before it launches — a teaser mechanic they rarely run compared to their usual "ends tonight" closers. The reveal inside the email is genuinely good: $5.95 body care and $12.95 three-wick candles are among the sharpest price points they've promoted this week, and the "STARTS TOMORROW! ONLINE ONLY!" banner gives the email a distinct purpose in the subscriber's inbox.
- The problem is the subject wastes the reveal. "Big deal" tells you nothing; the whole point of a teaser email is to build enough anticipation that someone opens tomorrow's email too — but vague copy generates vague curiosity. Compare this to their July 12 candle BOGO closer (scored 9/10), which led with the specific mechanic and seasonal story. This one buries it.
- The bigger structural flaw: the body simultaneously teases tomorrow's Fall Sale *and* shows a "TODAY'S DEALS!" grid of candles, bath gels, and Wallflowers. That mixed message forces the subscriber to decide between two competing actions. The click intent leaks.

## What's working

- **"STARTS TOMORROW! ONLINE ONLY!" banner is prominent** — it immediately frames the email's purpose as a preview, which is the right context-setter.
- **Hero prices are specific and legible** — $5.95 body care / $12.95 3-wick candles, stacked cleanly with a "START YOUR CART" CTA.
- **"Picked just for you!" section adds a personal texture** — three hand soap SKUs with individual SHOP buttons is a reasonable personalization module above the fold.
- **Seasonal pivot is timely** — pivoting from the Fruit Fusion / summer flash-sale run to a Fall Sale preview on July 15 is a natural cadence move.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is maximally vague** — "✨big deal✨" after five days of specific price-point subjects ($7.99, $3.50, BOGO) is a step backward; the subscriber has no signal of what to expect.
- **"TODAY'S DEALS!" grid conflicts with the "starting tomorrow" hero** — it splits attention and muddles whether to act now or wait.
- **The Vampire Blood product in the hero area is contextually jarring** — next to the $5.95 body care pitch, it reads as cross-sell noise rather than a fall seasonal reinforcement.
- **No end date for the Fall Sale** — "starts tomorrow" without a close date undercuts FOMO; the deal feels open-ended rather than urgent.
- **Cadence fatigue risk** — this is the fifth distinct promo email in four days; the "Picked just for you!" personalization module can't fully compensate for that volume.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the price in the subject** — drop "big deal" and lead with the number. The Fall Sale at $12.95 three-wicks is the news; treat it like news.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall Sale preview: 3-Wick Candles $12.95 tomorrow ✨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First look: $5.95 body care drops tomorrow 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only — add to your cart now, deal unlocks tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save your favorites before the Fall Sale goes live.`
- 2. **Remove or relocate the "TODAY'S DEALS!" grid** — if the hero is a tomorrow-focused teaser, anything that invites a same-day competing click works against that. Move the daily-deals module below the fold or cut it entirely from this specific send.
- 3. **Add an end date to the Fall Sale** — even "through the weekend" gives subscribers a reason to act on open day rather than assuming the deal is evergreen.
- 4. **Lean into the fall seasonal story** — the Vampire Blood product teases something specific; either hero it as a fall launch or cut it. As-is it's a distraction.
- 5. **"START YOUR CART" → "Shop the Fall Sale"** — the CTA should echo the seasonal framing in the hero, not just trigger generic cart behavior.

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "Big Deal" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) using standard HTML 4.01 Transitional email structure. Key concerns are malformed HTML (content tables rendered outside the `<body>` tag), multiple missing alt attributes on content and tracking images, two unprocessed shell-command placeholder comments in the markup, and compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) not captured by the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 25 click links unverified | All wrapped in `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects — redirect targets and UTM pass-through not confirmed |
| Open pixel, no alt | `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?24HRHNAEY3HUZHNUVZB244OZJY.70253&d=70253&bmt=0` — 1×1 img, `alt=""` missing |
| Third-party engagement tracker | `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=071426_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_...` (Validity/Return Path) — no alt text |
| Secondary analytics pixel | `https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/16a27679680c20ed.png?mi_u=...` — "mi" subdomain indicates Message Intelligence tracker — no alt text |

All three trackers fire on open; combined, they expose subscriber activity to two separate third-party analytics platforms (Everest/Validity + BBW MI).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed HTML — content tables outside `<body>`:**
The preheader `<table>` and the 1×1 open-pixel `<table>` both appear in the document before the `<body style="-webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ...">` tag. Any content rendered before `<body>` is technically in an implied body context, but strict parsers and some webmail clients may handle this inconsistently.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import`:**
- `<head>` import: `Source+Sans+Pro` — default weight only
- Body table import (inside `<style>` in a `<td>`): `Source+Sans+Pro:wght@400;700`

The first import will not load bold weight, creating a fallback mismatch. The duplicate HTTP request is wasteful and the `<head>` version should be removed or consolidated.

**Images missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**

| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` (image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com) | Content image — descriptive alt required |
| `16a27679680c20ed.png` (mi.bathandbodyworks.com) | Tracking pixel — should have `alt=""` |
| Everest tracker img | Tracking pixel — should have `alt=""` |
| 2 images with empty `src` attribute | Will generate failed network requests; no alt |

**`<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` placement:** Placed after `<style>` in `<head>` — should precede all other meta/style declarations for consistent charset handling in legacy clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unprocessed shell command placeholders:**
```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```
This comment appears **twice** in the source. The backtick syntax is bash command substitution — it was never evaluated. If the intent was to inject a date string (e.g., `ET07.26`), the mechanism failed silently. AMPscript equivalent would be `%%=FORMAT(NOW(), "MM.yy")=%%`.

**AMPscript variable comments:** References to `@NTF_Flag` and `@APT` are in comment blocks only — no variable bleed into rendered output. These are internal logic flags for New-to-File campaign routing.

**Empty Content Builder slot:** A `data-type="slot"` block (`data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9"`, label: "Preheader") is present but appears to have rendered nothing. The actual preheader text ("hint: it's never too early. 🍂") is set via a hardcoded hidden `<div>` rather than through the slot — the slot appears unused.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by relay — one-click compliance for Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements unconfirmed |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Not found — required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe compliance |
| `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) | **WARN** | Not captured — relay may be stripping headers; deliverability status unknown |
| Sending domain alignment | Unverified | `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is an ESP subdomain; DMARC alignment depends on subdomain policy configured at BBW's DNS |
| CAN-SPAM unsubscribe + physical address | Not verifiable | HTML is truncated — footer not present in provided source |

The absence of `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is the highest-priority compliance gap if this email reaches Gmail/Yahoo at volume; both providers now require it for bulk senders and may route to spam without it.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All 25 CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects — final destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be verified without resolving the redirects (QA skipped all 25).
- Campaign identifier visible in tracker URLs (`071426_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W`) follows BBW's internal naming convention. Whether this maps to `utm_campaign` values on landing pages is unknown.
- No UTM parameters are visible in the pre-redirect URLs themselves — if UTM injection happens at redirect resolution (common in SFMC), it needs to be spot-checked by resolving at least one sample link.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix HTML structure** — Move both the preheader table and the open-pixel table inside the `<body>` tag to eliminate the malformed document flow.
2. **Consolidate Google Fonts** — Remove the `<head>` `@import` (weight-incomplete); keep only the body-level import with `wght@400;700`.
3. **Fix alt attributes** — Add `alt=""` to all three tracking pixels; add descriptive alt text to the content image (`418d0db7...`); remove or fix the two empty-`src` image tags.
4. **Resolve the date placeholders** — Replace `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` with AMPscript `%%=FORMAT(NOW(), "MM.yy")=%%` or remove if purely a dev artifact.
5. **Verify List-Unsubscribe headers at SFMC level** — Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are configured in the SFMC send classification; re-inspect raw headers on a delivered copy, not through the relay.
6. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC** — Pull raw headers from a delivered copy (not relay-intercepted) to verify authentication chain on `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.
7. **Spot-check UTM pass-through** — Manually resolve 2–3 click links from different CTA positions to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are appended at redirect resolution.
