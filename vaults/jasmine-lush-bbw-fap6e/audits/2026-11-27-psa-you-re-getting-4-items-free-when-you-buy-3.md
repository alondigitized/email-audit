- The hero offer is unmistakable: "BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in giant white type on red.

# Technical Audit: Bath & Body Works, "PSA - you're getting 4 items FREE when you buy 3*"

The HTML source is truncated and the header-based QA checks are inconclusive, so the findings below are limited to what the visible source and QA data confirm.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) infrastructure (`image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, `bbwus.everestengagement.com`) with dark-mode handling. The confirmed issues are missing alt text on 5 images, malformed document structure in the head, and a font `@import` that is unlikely to work. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing was skipped for all 46 tracking links.** They are click-redirect domains, so none were resolved and destination validity is unconfirmed.
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The QA run reports 0 errors.
- **The QA tool misreported the tracking-pixel entry.** The flagged "image" `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112626_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B3G4_ICYMI_BAL_G6M_V4` is a tracker. Its `<img>` has no alt attribute, and the QA output uses the URL as its label.
- **The tracker URL carries `e=200511831233`.** This looks like a recipient-level identifier. It is standard for ESP open pixels, so I'm only noting it, not flagging it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five `<img>` tags have no alt text.** Four are content images under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`: `4851c40c-…93aa.png`, `d6327aef-…8306641.png`, `00a9ac12-…bb7b9c.png` and `bbebdf75-…de93f7.png`. The fifth is the tracking pixel. Missing alt on content images makes them silently disappear with images blocked and gives screen readers nothing to announce. The tracker should carry `alt=""`.
- **A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` sits directly after the `<meta>` tags.** The visible source shows no closing `</head>` or opening `<body>` before it. Parsers will auto-close the head, but this is invalid structure and can behave inconsistently across clients. I can't confirm whether `</head><body>` appears later in the truncated part.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` is unlikely to work.** "Helvetica Neue" is not a Google Fonts family, so the request will likely fail. Gmail and several other clients also strip `@import`. The `font-main` fallback stack (Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif) covers the visual result. The cost is a wasted external request and a possible render-blocking fetch in clients that honor it.
- **There is a redundant charset declaration.** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=UTF-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. This is harmless.
- **The title is `Bath & Body Works ` with a trailing space.** It is not campaign-specific.
