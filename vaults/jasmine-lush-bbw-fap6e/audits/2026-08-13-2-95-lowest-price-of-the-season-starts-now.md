# 📣 $2.95 - lowest price of the season starts now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, hero-first send: "Lowest price of the season" with a clean, concrete $2.95 hand soap offer, an actual product shot (Woodland Evening foaming soap), and a "STARTS TODAY!" banner that gives credible urgency. It's a step up from yesterday's 08-12 café-fragrance send that buried the "head-to-toe" subject promise under a mismatched hero — here the subject and hero are in lockstep.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold. The email piles on a second full offer ("Fall semester fresh scents," $4.95 body wash/lotion with a coupon code and barcode), then a third ("Buy 3 Get 3 Free"), then a "Top Offers" grid, then a "More to Love" block. That's four distinct offers competing for one click, which dilutes the clean single-offer opening BBW nailed on 2026-08-11 ("today only — fall steals from $3.95," 8/10). This reads like three merged campaigns rather than one coherent send.
- Verdict: great top-of-funnel hook, undercut by offer sprawl — trim it down to the $2.95 hero plus one secondary CTA and this would be an 8+.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unmissable: large "$2.95" price, clear product (foaming hand soap), "Lowest price of the season" framing.
- "STARTS TODAY!" banner adds real urgency without being spammy (no ALL CAPS shouting, no exclamation stacking).
- Free shipping banner ($50+) sits above the fold and is a genuine, honest incentive.
- Product photography is clean and on-brand — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the send.
- "Just dropped" callout on the soap gives a novelty hook beyond price.

## What's weak

- Offer overload: hand soap $2.95, body wash/lotion $4.95 w/ coupon code, then "Buy 3 Get 3 Free" — three competing hero-weight offers in one email fatigues the eye and splits intent.
- The in-store barcode/coupon block ("YESMOISTURE") is a jarring visual break between two very different offer styles (email hero vs. in-store redemption) — reads like two teams' emails stitched together.
- "TOP OFFERS" grid and "MORE TO LOVE" section add more low-priority CTAs (texts signup, BOPIS, store finder) that further dilute the primary $2.95 ask.
- No mention of loyalty/rewards member pricing despite BBW having a known loyalty program — a missed personalization signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one primary offer (the $2.95 soap hero) plus at most one secondary CTA — move the $4.95 coupon and Buy 3 Get 3 into a separate send or a smaller "also today" strip, not a full second/third hero block.
- 2. Drop or shrink the barcode/coupon section — it visually competes with the digital hero and adds friction for an email-only shopper.
- 3. Move "Top Offers" and "More to Love" further down or cut for this send — they're template filler that doesn't earn placement above the fold's momentum.
- 4. Add a loyalty angle ("Rewards members save more") if applicable — free personalization lift.

# Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "$2.95" Promo Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / ExactTarget) HTML email with functional tracking pixels and mobile media queries, but it fails baseline compliance-header capture, ships one HTTP (non-TLS) image, and has widespread missing `alt` text — 57% automated pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel present and well-formed: `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?7GNXDSWPJZ6EFNCQ4DNTUNKE3M.70246&d=70246&bmt=0` (1x1, served over HTTPS).
- 40 outbound links route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` click-tracking redirects — expected ESP behavior; QA skipped HTTP probing on these by design (redirect/tracking domains), so final-destination validity is unconfirmed from this data.
- One link uses a third-party engagement-tracking domain rather than the primary click-tracking domain: `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=081326_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295FOAMHS_AM_BAL-NTF-OP` — worth confirming this is an intentional secondary tracking vendor and not a broken/mismatched link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Legacy DOCTYPE (`HTML 4.01 Transitional`) with table-based layout and conditional MSO comments — standard for Outlook compatibility, no issues.
- Mobile media query block (`@media (max-width:599px)`) present and scoped correctly to `.mobile-body-padding`, `.mobile-lr-padding`, `.wrap`.
- **Missing `alt` attributes on 5 images**, confirmed by QA:
  - `LiveContent.aspx` dynamic image (`cl.S7.exct.net`)
  - `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png`
  - Two images with empty `src=""` (broken image references — see below)
  - The `everestengagement.com` tracking image
  This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and will read as blank/unlabeled to screen readers.
- **Two `<img>` tags have empty `src=""`** — these will render as broken images in all clients; needs source-code investigation (likely a stripped AMPscript/personalization variable that didn't resolve in this static capture).
- **Non-HTTPS image source**: `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...` — mixed content; many mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) will block or flag non-TLS image loads, causing that image to fail to render for a subset of recipients.
- Large block of hidden spacer entities (`&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` repeated) used for preview-text padding — functions correctly as an invisible spacer, no rendering issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved AMPscript (`%%...%%`) or merge-tag placeholders visible in the truncated source.
- Comments indicate AMPscript-driven logic was stripped/executed server-side before capture (e.g., `<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript-->`, `<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N"...-->`) — cannot verify personalization correctness from static HTML alone; flag as **not fully auditable** from this artifact rather than "no issues."
- The two empty `src=""` images are the most likely symptom of a personalization token that failed to resolve — recommend checking the pre-render AMPscript for these image blocks specifically.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo per their 2024 bulk-sender requirements. QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence; needs confirmation against raw headers at the MTA, not just what AgentMail captured.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — same caveat; without both headers, one-click unsubscribe is not functional even if a List-Unsubscribe header exists.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture. Given BBW is a high-volume commercial sender on a dedicated ExactTarget subdomain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`), authentication is almost certainly configured at the sending MTA; absence here is most likely a relay/capture gap, not a live auth failure — recommend verifying via raw header pull rather than treating as a confirmed compliance issue.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer in the truncated HTML — truncation limits this; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) presence or absence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `everestengagement.com` link carries a structured campaign code: `c=081326_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295FOAMHS_AM_BAL-NTF-OP` — encodes date (08/13/26), channel (EM), segment (SU/BAU), promo (295FOAMHS), and audience (NTF-OP), consistent with standard SFMC campaign-tagging convention. No standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` query params observed on the sampled tracking links (BBW uses its own `c=`/`qs=` scheme instead of UTM) — this is a vendor convention, not necessarily a defect, but means GA/analytics attribution depends on server-side redirect mapping rather than URL-visible UTMs.
- Cannot confirm final landing-page URL alignment since all 40 links are wrapped in `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects that were skipped by the HTTP probe — destination/landing-page match is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for decorative) `alt` attributes to all 5 flagged images, particularly the personalization-driven `LiveContent.aspx` and product images.
2. Fix the two empty `src=""` `<img>` tags — trace back to the AMPscript/content-block variable that isn't resolving.
3. Change `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers directly at the sending MTA (not just via the AgentMail relay capture) to confirm whether this is a real compliance gap or a relay visibility limitation.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 40 skipped tracking-redirect links by following them manually to confirm they resolve to live, correctly-tagged landing pages.
6. Confirm the `everestengagement.com` link is an intentional secondary tracking integration rather than a stray/misconfigured link.
