# final hours ⏳ to shop $5.95 single wicks.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight re-run of the $5.95 single-wick play B&BW already sent on 7/24 ("🙋‍♂️ heads up guys, final hours for $5.95 picks," 8/10) — same price point, same "final hours" urgency, just with the male-targeted framing stripped out. The hero itself is tight: "Small Flicker. Big Scent." is a genuinely good line, the $5.95 price and CANDLEWIN code are impossible to miss, and the candle photography (Mahogany Vanilla, Cinnamon Spiced Vanilla, Leaves) sells the product on sight.
- The problem is everything after the fold. The subject sets up one urgent offer, but the body immediately stacks three more — $12.95 3-wicks, Buy 3 Get 3 body care, and a 75% off sitewide sale — none of which get any urgency framing of their own. That's a lot of competing asks for an email whose subject promised a single, ending-soon deal, and it dilutes the "final hours" pressure that's supposed to be the whole point of opening this email.
- Solid top module, cluttered execution below it. Call it a 7 — better than an average promo, short of the 8-9s this brand hit when it held a single focused offer end-to-end.

## What's working

- "FINAL HOURS!" banner + "$5.95" price sit in the very first screen with zero scrolling required.
- CANDLEWIN code and "Limit 24" terms are printed directly on the hero, not buried below.
- Candle photography is specific and appetizing — named scents (Mahogany Vanilla, Cinnamon Spiced Vanilla, Leaves), not stock imagery.
- Free-shipping banner at the very top adds a second reason to click before the reader even reaches the hero.
- SHOP button is clean, high-contrast, and repeated after every offer block.

## What's weak

- Three unrelated offers (3-wick $12.95, Buy3Get3, 75% off) crowd in right after the hero, competing with the "final hours" single-wick message the subject promised.
- No countdown clock or explicit cutoff time anywhere despite "FINAL HOURS" — the urgency is asserted, not shown.
- This is functionally the same $5.95/$12.95 candle promo the brand ran on 7/24 and referenced across the week — creative fatigue is starting to show.
- The barcode/"show on phone or print" redemption block sits high in the email, using prime real estate for logistics instead of reinforcing the deal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one offer — the $5.95 single wicks — and drop the 3-wick, Buy3Get3, and 75%-off blocks into a separate send. A single-offer email is what earned this brand's 9/10s in this same audit run.
- 2. Add a real countdown timer or a specific end time (e.g., "ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") next to "FINAL HOURS" so the urgency is verifiable, not just asserted.
- 3. Move the barcode/redemption instructions below the SHOP button, not above it — it's operational detail, not part of the sell.
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific ("Shop $5.95 Wicks") instead of a bare "SHOP" repeated four times with no differentiation between offers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `small flicker, big scent — $5.95 wicks, final hours.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code CANDLEWIN before midnight — limit 24.`
