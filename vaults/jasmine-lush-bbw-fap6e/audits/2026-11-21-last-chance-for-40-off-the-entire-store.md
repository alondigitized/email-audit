# 🚨 LAST CHANCE 🚨 for 40% off the ENTIRE store.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- The top of this email is strong and the bottom is not. A red block reads "Black Friday week starts now" over a huge **40% off Entire store**, set on a shelf of Wallflower plugs, hand soaps and a candle. A lavender "Last chance" strip sits above it. Four category buttons sit below: Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers and Hand Soaps. It's clear, loud and urgent.
- After that the email loses focus. Four editorial blocks follow, each with a gift-themed headline. Then a "More wonder is waiting…" tile leads into a large empty gap. A "Starts tomorrow" hand-soap promo ($2.95) follows, then another blank stretch. The product row at the bottom is tiny, and the footer is mostly white space with what look like missing images. The marquee strip at the top is also cut off ("CK FRIDAY WEEK … BLACK FRIDAY W").
- This is roughly the fifth near-identical 40%-off send in four days. The 11-20 "FINAL HOURS" and "ends TODAY" sends already used the same creative. A subscriber has seen this hero repeatedly, so "last chance" has lost credibility. The hero is good and the urgency matches the calendar. The gaps and the fatigue hold the email back.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable. "40% off Entire store" in huge type on red is readable at thumbnail size.
- The "Last chance" strip above the hero ties the offer to a deadline.
- Four category buttons under the hero let a shopper jump straight to their interest.
- The free-shipping bar and the Rewards line sit at the very top, so the shipping threshold is visible immediately.
- The gifting copy ("A gift wish (or two) come true", "Gifts for sensitive skin") fits the season and has a clear voice.

## What's weak

- Large blank white gaps break the middle of the email, after "More wonder is waiting…" and below the hand-soap promo. They look like images that failed to load.
- The footer area is a long stretch of white space with only fragments of underlined text, which looks broken.
- The "Starts tomorrow" $2.95 hand-soap promo competes with the 40% hero and muddies the offer.
- The bottom product row has tiny images and tiny captions. Nothing there shows a price or review.
- The top marquee text is clipped on both edges.
- The subject shouts with two 🚨 emoji and "ENTIRE" in caps. It is the fifth near-identical "last chance" in a week, so the urgency feels manufactured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank gaps and the footer so nothing in the render looks like missing content. This matters most.
- 2. Cut the "Starts tomorrow" soap block, or move it below a clear 40% module. One offer per email.
- 3. Replace the tiny product row with 4 larger tiles that show a name, a price and a "Shop" button.
- 4. Add a deadline with a time ("Ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET") to the "Last chance" strip.
- 5. Vary the creative across the Black Friday run. Use a different hero or a countdown so the send doesn't look recycled.
