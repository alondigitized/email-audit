# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, BBW is coming in HOT right now — 40% off the entire store is genuinely a big deal and I felt it immediately. This is very much a "we know our core girls and we're giving them an excuse to spend" homepage, leaning hard into the Gift Mom Event. It reads warm and sale-forward, which is very on-brand for them.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **The $3.50 Wallflowers promo banner at the very top** — that's the first thing I see and I am immediately tapping "Details" because I gift Wallflowers to literally everyone. Putting that at the tippy top was smart.
- **"40% Off Entire Store" headline** — huge, bold, unmissable. I came here for deals and this confirms I'm in the right place. That copy makes me want to go straight to candles.
- **The subhead "feel the love (+ make Mom *and* your wallet happy)"** — the tone is playful and Gen Z-legible. It doesn't feel stiff or corporate. I actually smiled.
- **"Shop now" CTA** — simple white pill button, easy tap target on mobile, no friction.
- **The botanical floral product visual** — the wildflower aesthetic on the hero product feels very fresh and spring-coded, which tracks for May.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is blurry/out of focus** — I can barely read what's on that product. If it's a gift set or a candle, I want to see it clearly. Right now it just looks like a soft-focus blur with flowers.
- **No visible loyalty/rewards callout** — I'm a My Bath & Body Works Rewards member and there's zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. Not even a "members earn extra points today" hook.
- **"Exclusions apply" feels like a buzzkill immediately after the headline** — I know it has to be there, but on mobile it kills the momentum right after "your wallet happy."
- **The video controls (mute/pause) are visible** — they're floating over the hero on mobile and it looks unpolished, like I accidentally paused something.

## Recommendations

- **Make the hero image/video crisp** — if this is a video, the thumbnail frame should be a sharp, well-lit close-up of the actual hero product. Blurry botanical vibes are a Pinterest mood board, not a conversion driver.
- **Add a rewards callout to the top banner or just below the hero** — something like "Rewards members: stack your points on 40% off" would make me feel seen and push me deeper into the site faster.
- **Move the video controls off-center or hide them by default** — the floating pause/mute UI is distracting and makes the mobile layout feel janky.
- **Tease the scent categories** — even one line like "Candles · Body Care · Wallflowers · Gifts" below the CTA would help me orient and click faster instead of hunting through the menu.
