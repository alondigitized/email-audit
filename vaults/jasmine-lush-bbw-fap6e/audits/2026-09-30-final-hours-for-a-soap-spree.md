Compared with yesterday's "$3.95 holiday head start" send, which never showed its price, this one delivers the offer it advertises.
- The email then dilutes itself. Below the hero are three more modules: a 2-day $13.95 3-wick candle offer, a Platinum mist "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" offer, and a product trio. ## What's working

- The hero price block ($3.50 / $10.95) is huge and sits right under the product photo, so the offer lands immediately.
- "Final hours" in a dark banner at the top gives credible, visible urgency.
- The barcode and "use code SUDS" make the offer usable in store and online.
- The hero photo is clean: the soap bottle and refill carton are clearly visible, with a seasonal Frosted Cranberry scent.
- The "Shop now" buttons are obvious and repeated for each module.

## What's weak

- The subject's "soap spree" never mentions $3.50, the strongest number in the email.
- The body runs long, with candles, Platinum mist, a product trio, a "feel good formula" block and a footer, and none of it is about soap.
- The "Up to 24 per customer with code" note is small gray text, and "Shop now" sits off to the side of the prices.
- The 2-day candle offer and the "Limited time" body care offer compete with the soap deadline.
- The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" sections are filler with no offer, and the legal fine print is very long.

- 2. Cut the send to the soap hero plus one secondary offer, and drop the "feel good formula" block.
- 3. Put "Shop now" directly under the prices and make the code visible next to it.

The HTML has structural validity problems (content outside `<body>`, tables nested without rows) and several images lack alt text, including a linked image. Auth and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped the click-redirect domains, e.g. `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and `bbwus.everestengagement.com`. Destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure image source.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...` (Salesforce dynamic content) loads over HTTP. It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the dynamic block may not render.
- **Open pixel is fine.** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?...` is 1x1 with `alt=""`, hidden via `mso-hide:all`.
- **Possible dead font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family I don't believe Google Fonts hosts, so it likely adds a failed request with no effect. The second import, Source Sans Pro, is declared but `.font-main` and `<body>` use Helvetica Neue. I did not verify either against the live API.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements containing `<style>` and MSO conditionals sit inside `<head>`, before `</head>`.
  - More wrapper tables sit between `</head>` and `<body>`.
  - `<table ... style="width: 600px;">` directly contains another `<table>` (after `<!-- Header LOGO -->`) with no `<tr>/<td>` between them. Clients may repair this inconsistently.
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):

  - Any text baked into these images is invisible to screen readers and when images are blocked.
- **Dead CSS selector.** `u + #body a` targets a `#body` element, but `<body>` has no `id="body"`. The Gmail/iOS link-color reset never applies.
- **Fine:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta tag, and a mobile media query.
