# ⚡️ FLASH sale - shop quick, it ends tonight. 🌃
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a mystery-offer email — "Click to reveal" — and it withholds the one thing that would earn a click: the actual discount amount. There's no % off, no $ off, no product category visible anywhere in the hero. Against the brand's recent run of "$2.95 Wallflowers, lowest price of the season" sends, which scored well precisely because they led with a concrete number, this is a step backward into vague scattergun territory, minus even the scattergun's specificity.
- The visual craft is fine — a moody, well-lit net-bag-of-produce image, clean "Ends tonight | Online exclusive" urgency banner, consistent brand type — but craft can't compensate for a hero CTA that asks the reader to gamble a click on an unknown reward. Below the fold it reverts to generic merchandising (candle, body cream, body cream) with no visible tie to "Flash Sale" pricing.
- Judgement: skippable. The subject and hero commit to urgency without payoff, and a subscriber who's seen four "$2.95 Wallflowers" emails this month has no reason to click through on faith.

## What's working

- "Ends tonight | Online exclusive" banner gives credible, scannable urgency right above the hero.
- Free shipping $50 threshold is stated plainly near the top, no scrolling needed.
- Hero image (mesh bag of citrus/produce) is well-composed and on-brand, no render bugs.
- Clean single-column layout, no overlapping text or broken assets anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- No offer amount anywhere — "Click to reveal" hides the discount, which kills scannability and trust.
- "We think you'll love" product grid (candle, two body creams) has no visible price, discount badge, or connection to the Flash Sale headline.
- Hero product category is unclear — the bag-of-fruit imagery doesn't signal what's on sale (candles? body care? everything?).
- Fourth-plus "lowest price" / urgency-styled send in about ten days from this brand — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who's opened the recent Wallflowers run.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount in the hero — replace "Click to reveal" with the real number (e.g., "40% off sitewide, ends tonight"). Reveal-gating a flash sale trades away the strongest lever headline copy has.
- 2. Add price/discount badges under the three "We think you'll love" products so the sale is visible in the product grid, not just implied by the header.
- 3. Tie the hero image to a category — if this is candles/body care specific, show the product, not abstract fruit-in-a-bag styling.
- 4. Vary the visual template from the recent Wallflowers sends — same navy/cream palette and layout risk blur-together fatigue in an inbox that's already seen several near-identical structures this month.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off flash sale — ends tonight ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `last call: flash sale ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online only, no code needed — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites, marked down for a few hours only.`
