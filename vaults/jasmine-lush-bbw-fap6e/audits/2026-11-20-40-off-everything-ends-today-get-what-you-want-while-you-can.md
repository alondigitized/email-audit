# 40% off EVERYTHING* ends TODAY. get what you want while you can. 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- The top half is a strong Black Friday send. A red block carries a huge **40% off Entire store** over a shelf of snowman Wallflower plugs, a hand soap, a mist and a candle. A lavender "Ends today" strip sits above it, and the free-shipping-at-$50 bar and four category buttons make the offer easy to act on. A subscriber knows what this is within a second.
- The bottom half is badly broken. After the "More wonder is waiting…" tile there is a large white void, then a three-product row with captions but only one visible bottle image. Below that is another big blank stretch, and the footer links look cut off, with stray underlines floating alone. The 11-19 send ("2 days left") had a similar problem, and this one still hasn't been fixed. The gift-guide modules in the middle (A Thousand Wishes, Everyday Luxuries, Essentials) are decent but read as filler for a last-day push.
- My judgement: a good hero and a sloppy finish. It is the fourth near-identical 40% send in three days, and the "ends TODAY" urgency is the only new thing. It should convert on urgency alone, but the layout bugs undercut a brand that normally sends clean emails.

## What's working

- The 40% off Entire store hero is huge, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- The "Ends today" strip gives a credible deadline and is a real change from the "2 days left" sends earlier this week.
- Free shipping at $50 and the Rewards link sit right at the top, so the member and shipping benefits are visible before the hero.
- The four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) let people shop their own category quickly.
- The alternating image and copy tiles have a clean rhythm and warm seasonal photography.

## What's weak

- There is a large blank white gap below "More wonder is waiting…", where a product grid or images should be.
- The product row shows captions (Champagne Toast, Winter Candy Apple) but only one visible product image, which looks like missing content.
- The footer is a mess. Unlabeled underlines and fragments float in blank space, and the "Our feel good formula" banner text is washed out over a pale image.
- The "Ends today" strip is small and pale lavender, so the urgency sits below the fold of attention. The lavender strip is also the weakest part of the visual hierarchy.
- The gift tiles carry no prices, no product names and no clear CTA, so nothing is a specific product with a link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the rendering of the product grid and footer. The blank voids are the biggest drag on this email, so put real product images with prices ("$X, now 40% off") in the blank band.
- 2. Make "Ends today" part of the hero, for example a "Ends tonight at midnight" ribbon on the red block, and add a countdown if possible.
- 3. Cut the middle tiles from five to two, and give each a button ("Shop gift sets", "Shop Essentials").
- 4. Add a top-sellers row with real prices and review stars.
- 5. Put the "Find a store" and Rewards benefits above the footer, and clean up the stray link fragments.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with role="presentation", dark-mode handling and an Outlook (MSO) fallback. The confirmed issues are 5 content images with no `alt` attribute, a non-functional Google Fonts `@import`, and markup in `<head>`. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified from this capture, and the 50 tracked links were not probed. The QA pass rate was 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on all 50 links because they use click-redirect domains. Link health, redirect chains and landing-page status are therefore unknown.
- **Redirect domain differs from the sending domain:** Links go through `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/...`. Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, and the From domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. A third-party click domain that doesn't match the From domain is a minor alignment concern for some spam filters, but it is not confirmed as a defect.
- **Likely tracking pixel flagged for alt text:** The sixth alt-text warning is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. Its `/ea/` path and the lack of an image file extension suggest an open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images**, all hosted at `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  If these images carry the offer or CTA, the message is lost when images are blocked or a screen reader is used. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`.
- **Google Fonts `@import` for a font that isn't on Google Fonts:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family I don't believe Google Fonts serves, so it will likely fail. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (`"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) is fine, so the only cost is a wasted request.
- **Table markup in `<head>`:** In the source, `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` directly follows the `<meta>` tags with no `</head><body>` between them, and `<style>` and MSO conditional blocks sit inside the table cell. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>`, but some clients may drop or mis-scope the `<style>` block. The MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` XML is also duplicated (`mso` and `gte mso 9`), which is redundant.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with HTML5 `<meta charset>` and a duplicate `http-equiv` Content-Type charset declaration. This is harmless but redundant.
- **`<title>`:** It is a generic `Bath & Body Works ` with a trailing space, not tied to the campaign.
- **Good practices confirmed:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta tag, `color-scheme` meta tags, and dark-mode rules for both `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message in a direct-delivery mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Verify with a direct-to-inbox test or by querying the DNS records for `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.
- **Footer elements:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed. The `.footer-disclaimer` class exists in the CSS.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible:** Attribution relies on the redirect's own `c=112026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` and `e=` IDs. Final landing URLs and any UTMs added after the redirect couldn't be checked because the probe was skipped.
- **Landing-page alignment:** Not verifiable from the capture.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 flagged images, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Remove the `@import` for Helvetica Neue, which isn't a Google font. Rely on the existing system font stack.
3. Move layout markup out of `<head>` into a proper `<body>`. Remove the duplicate MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw delivered message, not the relay copy.
5. Follow a sample of the 50 redirect links, using a browser or a probe that follows tracker redirects, to confirm the final URLs, status codes and any UTM handoff.
6. Set a campaign-specific `<title>` without trailing whitespace.
