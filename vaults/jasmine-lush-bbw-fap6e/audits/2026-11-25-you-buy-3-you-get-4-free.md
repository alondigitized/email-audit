## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has a few structural problems: a `<table>` sits inside `<head>`, there is a non-functional Google Fonts `@import`, and 5 images lack alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay not exposing headers, so they can't be confirmed. The source was truncated, so footer and body content were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Click and image domains differ.** Links and a pixel use `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, while images use `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Both are normal for ESP setups, but the click domain differs from the From domain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). That may affect link-reputation alignment.
- **A subscriber identifier is in the URL.** The tracking URL `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B3G4_ICYMI_BAL_G6M_V2` carries `e=200511831233`, which looks like a per-recipient ID. That is expected for click tracking, but it means forwarded emails expose the ID.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** The source goes from `<meta name="format-detection"…>` and `<meta name="color-scheme"…>` straight into `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` with no `</head>` or `<body>` visible. A table inside `<head>` is invalid, and some clients may relocate or drop it.
- **Google Fonts `@import` does nothing useful.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host. Most email clients ignore `@import` anyway, and Gmail strips it. It adds a failing or wasted request, and the fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) is what actually renders.
- **Redundant markup.** There are two identical `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` blocks, under `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`. There is also both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … UTF-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`. Both are harmless but unnecessary.
- **Images missing alt text (QA-confirmed).** These are all hosted at `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  
  If these carry promo content, screen-reader users and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **A sixth missing-alt hit is ambiguous.** It is reported against the `everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` URL. It is probably either a tracking pixel, which should use `alt=""`, or a linked image, which needs descriptive alt text. Confirm which.
- **Dark-mode handling is present.** The source has `prefers-color-scheme` rules, `[data-ogsc]` and `[data-ogsb]` selectors, and a `darkmode-dark` or `darkmode-light` swap. No defect is visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible source. Merge fields lower in the truncated body were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No confirmed issues.** All three QA warnings are caveats about relay capture, not defects. They are `List-Unsubscribe` not found, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found, and `Authentication-Results` not found. The QA text itself says AgentMail may strip these headers, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown.
- **Body-level items were not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link, physical postal address, and the disclosure behind the subject's `FREE*` asterisk are not in the truncated HTML. Check them against the full source.
- **Visible positives.** The email uses a dedicated sending subdomain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) and a proper `From` display name.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs can't be assessed.** Links are wrapped in `everestengagement.com` redirects, so final-URL UTM parameters and landing-page alignment aren't visible.
- **Campaign identifier is present.** The `c=` value (`…B3G4_ICYMI_BAL_G6M_V2`) encodes the offer, which fits the subject's "buy 3 get 4" promo. Whether the redirect appends UTMs is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Close `</head>`, open `<body>`, and move the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` table inside it.
2. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the system font stack.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 5 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and tracking pixels.
4. Remove the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` block and the duplicate charset declaration.
5. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` or the original sending-platform headers. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Consider treating relay-stripped headers as "unknown" rather than WARN in the QA tool.
6. Follow a sample of the 48 tracking links manually or with a redirect-following probe. Check that they resolve and carry UTMs.
