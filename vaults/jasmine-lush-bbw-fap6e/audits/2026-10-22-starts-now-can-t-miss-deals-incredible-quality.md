# starts now - can't-miss deals 🤝 incredible quality.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, single-offer hero: "Save on perfect gifts" with a big **$5.95** on full-size mists and body spray. The "Starts today" bar and the "Shop now" outline button make the message easy to read. It's the same $5.95 price point Bath & Body Works used for the single wicks on 10/20 and 10/21 ("last day for $5.95 single wicks"), applied to a new category. That makes this one more send in a heavy, repetitive cadence.
- The execution has problems. The subject is vague filler ("can't-miss deals 🤝 incredible quality") and never names the mist or body spray offer. The body then goes soft, with a low-contrast "Our feel good formula" banner and a generic "More to love" link list. There are also large blank white gaps below the hero and across the footer, with stray underlines where text appears to be missing.
- Judgement: a decent hero undermined by a generic subject, a weak second half and visible layout gaps. Mid-pack for this brand, below the 10/21 "last day" send.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is clean. The eye goes to the oversized **$5.95**, then "Full-size mists & body spray."
- The "Starts today" bar and "Free shipping with any $50 purchase" give a believable reason to act now.
- The product photography is attractive, and the Ocean and Snowflakes & Cashmere bottles show what is on offer.
- The brand voice and look are consistent with past sends, so the sender is instantly trusted.
- The three-product row (Champagne Toast candle, Night Balm, Cocoa Shea hand cream) gives easy add-on browsing.

## What's weak

- Big blank white gaps sit below the hero and between "Find a store" and the footer. Orphaned underlines with no visible text point to a render problem in the legal and footer links.
- "Our feel good formula" is white text on a pale cream image, so it is nearly unreadable. "Learn more" is a weak CTA.
- The product row has no prices or deals. It dilutes the $5.95 message and doesn't say whether those items are on offer.
- "Shop now" is a generic, low-emphasis outline button in pink on pink.
- The "More to love" and "Find a store" blocks add nothing for someone mid-promo.
- "Starts today" gives no end date, so urgency is soft.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the rendering gaps and footer so the email ends cleanly. Pulling the product row up under the hero is the cheapest quality lift.
- 2. Put the offer in the subject. A specific item and price beats "can't-miss deals."
- 3. Replace the low-contrast "feel good formula" banner with a dark-text version, or cut it. Add an end date to the hero bar.
- 4. Make the button a solid fill with specific copy such as "Shop $5.95 mists," and add prices to the product tiles.
- 5. Vary the creative. Three days of $5.95 sends trains subscribers to wait for the next one.
