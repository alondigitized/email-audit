# wait, TODAY?!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works closes the Semi-Annual Sale with a two-deal structure — $13.50 3-Wick Candles and $5.95 Foaming Hand Soaps, both stamped "ENDS TODAY!" — anchored by a clever ice cream / boardwalk theme. The hero copy, "Feels Like a Sweet Time / The boardwalk really ends," is the sharpest writing in this entire blitz: it earns the seasonal moment, signals urgency without shouting, and gives the Neapolitan Ice Cream candle a narrative reason to be the hero SKU.
- The subject line is the meaningful miss. "wait, TODAY?!" is a pure curiosity hook — no product, no price, no category. For a brand that's delivered at least seven promotional emails since July 1 (including a brilliant single-price "$1 today only!" subject on 7/2 that scored 9/10), subscribers have trained themselves to scan for the deal in the subject line. This one makes them work harder to earn that open. The vague subject undercuts an otherwise crisp execution.
- The two-hero structure is the right call for a finale: candles bring the loyalists, hand soaps broaden the basket. The TOP OFFERS grid at the bottom is safe filler — fine, but it deflates the urgency built up top. Net judgment: above-average execution on a sale-critical send, dragged slightly by a subject that buries the lede.

## What's working

- **"The boardwalk really ends" wordplay** — sale urgency delivered with wit; the Neapolitan Ice Cream scent earns its hero placement this way.
- **Two hard-anchored prices** — $13.50 and $5.95 are specific and visible; no hunting required.
- **ENDS TODAY! stamped on both deals** — urgency is consistent and credible given the campaign arc.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — sale banner → ice cream hero → candle CTA → soap CTA; the eye travels exactly as intended.
- **Summer-ready soaps section** lands well as a secondary offer — diversifies the basket without diluting the candle hero.

## What's weak

- **Subject "wait, TODAY?!" is vague** — after "$1 today only!" (7/2) and "final hours! $3.50 room sprays" (7/3), subscribers expect a price or product in the subject; this one makes them guess.
- **"SHOP" CTA copy is generic** — two deals, two identical "SHOP" buttons; "Shop Candles" / "Shop Soaps" would add scannable context.
- **TOP OFFERS grid undermines urgency** — the category tile mosaic at bottom feels like an evergreen homepage block bolted onto a finale send; it dilutes the "ENDS TODAY" message.
- **No loyalty/member callout visible** — a sale finale is the natural moment to remind members of bonus points or early access; that's absent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to include the price** — the brand proved this works on 7/2. "wait — $13.50 candles TODAY?!" captures both the curiosity hook *and* the anchor offer. Don't make subscribers decode the "today" without a price.
- 2. **Differentiate the CTA buttons** — change "SHOP" under the candle hero to "Shop $13.50 Candles" and under soaps to "Shop $5.95 Soaps." One line of copy, immediate scannability lift.
- 3. **Pull the TOP OFFERS grid or replace with a countdown** — a sale-ends-today email should close with urgency, not a category browse. A visible "Sale ends at midnight" line or a "last chance" text block would land harder than eight thumbnail tiles.
- 4. **Add a member loyalty nudge** — even one line ("My Bath & Body Works members: earn points on your last-chance haul") would convert loyalists who might otherwise wait-and-forget.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$13.50 candles — today only. 🍦`
- **Subject Alt B:** `last day: 3-wick candles $13.50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Semi-Annual Sale ends tonight — Neapolitan Ice Cream + $5.95 soaps.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two deals, one day left. Candles $13.50 · Soaps $5.95.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two images with empty `src` attributes (confirmed bug).**
QA flagged two `<img>` tags where both `src` and `alt` are empty strings:
```
[WARN] Image missing alt text: : src:
[WARN] Image missing alt text: : src:
```
Empty `src` triggers a redundant HTTP request to the page's own URL in some clients, and the missing alt leaves screen readers with no fallback. These are likely unresolved template slots or build-time substitution failures.

**Three additional images missing `alt` text:**
- `07010ef72edd5d12.png` — `mi.bathandbodyworks.com` (appears to be a content image, not decorative; needs descriptive alt)
- `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` — `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`
- EverestEngagement image at `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/`

**Duplicate Google Fonts import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Source+Sans+Pro...')` appears twice: once in `<head>` and once in a `<style>` block inside a `<td>` in the body. Redundant network request; no rendering impact in most clients, but adds load overhead and will be blocked by corporate firewalls that strip `<style>` in body.

**`<meta charset>` placement.** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` is placed after the `<style>` block in `<head>`. Best practice requires charset declaration as the first element in `<head>` to prevent pre-parse character misinterpretation.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE.** Legacy, but functionally compatible with all major email clients. No action required unless migrating to XHTML or HTML5.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript logic exposed in HTML comments.** The following comments are present in the rendered source and visible to any recipient who views source:

<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->

This leaks internal campaign variable names (`@NTF_Flag`, `@APT`), suppression logic, and the NTF (New To File) segmentation flag. Not a security risk, but exposes ESP template architecture and could be cleaned up.

**Shell date command artifact exposed:**
```html

```
Appears twice. This is a build-script artifact that was not executed — the backtick expression was never interpolated, suggesting a broken or skipped build step. Should be a rendered value like `ET07.26` or removed.

**No unresolved merge token syntax** (e.g., `%%variable%%` or `${token}`) found in visible text. Personalization appears to have resolved correctly for this send.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header missing** (QA: `[WARN]`).
RFC 2369 requires this header for bulk commercial email. Its absence may be a relay-layer capture issue (AgentMail not forwarding it), but if genuinely absent from the SMTP envelope, Gmail and Apple Mail will fall back to their own unsubscribe prompts, which can drive complaint rates.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header missing** (QA: `[WARN]`).
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (≥5k/day) as of February 2024. If this header is absent from the actual send (not just undetected by the relay), BBW is non-compliant with current platform requirements and risks deliverability action.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown** (QA: `[WARN]`).
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. The sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated SFMC envelope domain — DKIM is almost certainly configured, but cannot be confirmed from this sample. Requires header inspection at the MX or relay level.

**CAN-SPAM:** An unsubscribe mechanism is present in the email body (assumed based on standard SFMC template structure — footer not fully visible in truncated source). Physical mailing address not verifiable from truncated HTML.
