It is the same $2.75 message as the 2026-11-06 "lowest 👏 price 👏 of 👏 the 👏 YEAR" send and the teaser before it, so it works as a deadline closer.
- The rest of the email is weaker. The "Give 3 wicks of wonder" candle block has no offer or button, and it pulls attention away from the $2.75. ## What's working

- The "Ends today" bar sits directly above the hero, so the deadline is the first thing you see.
- The big **$2.75** with "Lowest price of the year" gives one specific, price-led offer.
- The five hanging plugs with ribbons look festive and match the November season.
- The "Shop now" button sits right under the price and is easy to find.
- The Rewards and free-shipping bars at the top add member value without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The "Give 3 wicks of wonder" block has no price, no CTA and no deadline. It competes with the $2.75 and dilutes the message.
- The "Our feel good formula" text is pale on a pale background and is barely legible. "Learn more" is hard to see.

- 2. Put the $2.75 price and "Ends today" in the product tiles, so each tile is a priced, clickable offer.
- 3. Make the "Up to 24 per customer" cap larger and higher-contrast.
- 4. Fix the pale-on-pale text and the empty footer area so nothing looks missing.
- 5. Name the CTA, for example "Shop $2.75 refills".
