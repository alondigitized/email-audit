# scents the saving: $5.95 starts today. 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the payoff to yesterday's teaser, "something special will be here tomorrow...🍁😍". The hero delivers cleanly: a "Starts today" banner, a big white **$5.95** on a deep crimson field, "Select full-size mists, body spray & body cream," and a visible "Shop now" button. The Platinum shimmer bottles are glossy and give the offer some appeal. The eye lands on the price first, which is right.
- The email loses focus after that. Below the hero it adds a candle tile with no price or CTA, then the 75% off fall sale strip, a "You might like" row of three products, a "feel good formula" block with low-contrast text, and a "More to love" utility list. That's about five competing messages after a single-offer hero. The candle tile and the 75% banner both dilute the $5.95 offer. The 75% strip echoes the 10/08 send ("75% off fall goodness - while it lasts.") that the brand ran only a day ago.
- My judgement: a strong hero in a cluttered body. It's a solid mid-tier send. The offer is clear, but the layout gives the reader too many other places to go. There is also a large dead white gap between the candle tile and the fall sale strip, and another before the legal copy.

## What's working

- The $5.95 price is the largest element in the hero. It reads instantly.
- "Starts today" closes the loop on yesterday's teaser and sets credible urgency.
- The hero names the product types (mists, body spray, body cream), so the offer is specific.
- The "Shop now" button is clear, and the sparkly Platinum bottles are strong product imagery.
- The free-shipping-over-$50 bar and the Rewards line at the top are a useful, quiet value-add.

## What's weak

- The candle tile ("Candles with a taste for entertaining") has no price or visible CTA. It's a pretty photo that goes nowhere.
- The 75% off banner competes with the $5.95 hero and repeats yesterday's campaign.
- There is a large empty white gap between the candle tile and the fall sale strip, which looks like a broken or missing module.
- The "Our feel good formula" block has pale white text on a washed-out image. It's hard to read, and "Learn more" is nearly invisible.
- The "You might like" products (hand soap, counter spray, Wallflowers refill) have no prices and don't match the $5.95 hero.
- The fine print is a long dense block, and there is a second large dead zone around it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the $5.95 hero the only offer above the fold. Remove the candle tile and the 75% strip, or move them to a clearly secondary row below product tiles.
- 2. Add 3-4 $5.95 product tiles (a mist, a body spray, a body cream) with scent names and a "Shop $5.95 mists" CTA. Replace the unrelated "You might like" row.
- 3. Cut the empty white gaps so the layout reads as one continuous email.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the "feel good formula" block, or drop it. Put the end date near the hero, since the legal copy shows the deal ends 10/12.
- 5. Move the Rewards, text signup and store-pickup items into a compact footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`, `camarker-inner`) with dark-mode and Outlook handling. The QA run found 0 errors and 3 warning categories, with a 57% pass rate. The confirmed defects are 5 images with no `alt` attribute, a Google Fonts `@import` for a font Google doesn't host, and markup placed in `<head>`. The header and tracking-link warnings are unverified. The HTML was truncated, so everything after the CSS block (body, footer, links) was not available to me.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and UTM params are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100926_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_595TBCFFMBC_AM_BAL_G6M` is on a third-party domain (`everestengagement.com`), separate from the sender's `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and image host `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. It carries `e=` (likely a recipient ID) and a `c=` campaign code. It is not a defect, but note that the open pixel depends on a different domain than the one the sender authenticates.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images, QA-confirmed):** all on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  If any of these carry content such as offer text, it is invisible to screen readers and to clients with images blocked. A missing attribute also differs from `alt=""`, which marks an image as decorative.
- **Pixel missing alt:** the `everestengagement.com` tracking pixel has no `alt`. It should have `alt=""`.
- **Google Fonts `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family I don't believe Google Fonts serves, so it likely returns an error and adds a wasted request. `@import` is also ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The font stack already falls back to `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`, so there is no visual dependency.
- **Markup in `<head>`:** in the visible portion, the `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and the `<style>` block appear after the `<meta>` tags with no `</head>` or `<body>` before them. Some clients repair this, but it is invalid. Confirm against the full source, since the HTML was truncated.
- **Minor redundancy:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are both present, and the `OfficeDocumentSettings` XML is duplicated under `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`. Both are harmless.
- **Dark mode:** `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` selectors are present, so Apple Mail and Outlook.com are covered. Nothing wrong is visible in the CSS shown.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the subject or the visible source. The footer was not in the truncated source, so tokens there are unchecked.

- **Authentication-Results:** missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay. This is unverified, not a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Link `href`s are not in the truncated source and the 48 tracking links were not resolved. The only campaign identifier visible is `c=100926_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_595TBCFFMBC_AM_BAL_G6M` in the pixel URL. No UTM parameters are visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 listed images (or `alt=""` if decorative), and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
2. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the existing system font stack.
3. Move the `<table>`/`<style>` content into a proper `<body>` and close `<head>`. Verify against the full HTML first.
4. Check the raw `.eml` from the origin (not the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Capture them upstream of the relay in the QA pipeline so these three warnings stop being ambiguous.
5. Resolve a sample of the 48 tracking links with a redirect-following check, for example by logging the final URL without fetching the page. This would make the continuity and UTM checks possible.
6. Pass the full, non-truncated HTML to the audit so the footer and unsubscribe link can be checked.
