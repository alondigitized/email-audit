# deals starting at $7.99? light us up. ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' June 23 send — at minimum the twelfth email in what has become a nine-plus-day Semi-Annual Sale blitz. The subject ("deals starting at $7.99? light us up. ✨") is the most candle-forward framing in a week, but the candle hero ($7.99–$12.99) is the same deal that first appeared in the June 17 and June 18 sends and has recurred in every email since. The creative is clean and the three-tier price architecture (candles → body care 75% off → soaps/sanitizers 50% off → Wallflowers $3.50) is legibly laid out, but there is nothing new here. The "once they're gone, they're gone" scarcity language is the hardest-working copy on the page, and it's buried below the hero.
- The brand has now sent this same candle + body care combo so many times that "light us up" reads less like wit and more like a Pavlovian trigger. A subscriber who has received every send this week has already made their decision. This email is working the fence-sitters, but the visual and structural sameness makes it easy to ignore.
- Judgement: a functional promotional email that does its job without doing anything memorable. Strong hierarchy, credible offers, but zero creative freshness at this point in the campaign.

## What's working

- **Three-tier price architecture is scannable** — candles → 75% off body care → 50% off soaps → $3.50 Wallflowers reads in a single downward glance.
- **"Once they're gone, they're gone" scarcity copy** is the most motivating line in the email and it's backed by 75% off body care — the strongest deal on the page.
- **Product photography is clean and on-brand** — the pink-and-red palette is consistent, no broken images.
- **"Top Offers" + "Today's Deals" tab row** adds a navigation layer that signals there's more to explore without cluttering the hero.
- **BOPIS prompt ("Buy Online, Pickup In Store")** is a smart friction reducer that was visible and unambiguous.

## What's weak

- **The candle hero ($7.99–$12.99) is now at least its sixth appearance** in this blitz — the "light us up" subject is the only new angle on a deal subscribers have seen daily since June 17.
- **"Today's Deals" tab is a dead button in this render** — there's no visible differentiation between "Top Offers" and "Today's Deals" content, making it feel like a navigation placeholder.
- **Footer is extremely dense** — fine print runs several paragraphs, making the email feel heavier than it is; the CTA-to-fine-print ratio tips toward compliance clutter at the bottom.
- **No urgency deadline visible** — "once they're gone" implies scarcity but there's no "sale ends Sunday" or specific end date anywhere in the render.
- **"Find A Store" button** sits awkwardly between BOPIS and social icons — it breaks visual flow before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Introduce one genuinely new offer or product** — the candle and body care deals have both been the hero in prior sends; even a single "new arrivals just added to sale" module would break the sameness and give fence-sitters a new reason to click.
- 2. **Add a specific deadline** — "Sale ends Sunday" or "Last chance: 72 hours left" would make "once they're gone" land harder. The scarcity copy is doing the heavy lifting with no concrete anchor.
- 3. **Collapse the "Today's Deals" tab or give it real content** — as rendered it looks like a ghost button; either populate it with something distinct or remove it so the hero breathes more.
- 4. **Tighten the subject line** — "light us up" is cute but the candle metaphor competes with the price specificity; lead with the scarcity angle that's doing real work in the body.
- 5. **Trim the footer fine print** — the legal block is disproportionately long relative to the email height; a collapsed "see full terms" approach would reduce scroll fatigue.

- **Subject Alt B:** `"last call: candles $7.99, body care 75% off"`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `"Scents are going fast — shop before your faves sell out."`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `"Sale ends soon. Candles from $7.99 + 75% off body care."`
