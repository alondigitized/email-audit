# oh, what fun - sleigh your faves tomorrow. 🛷
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser for tomorrow's $5.95 single-wick candle sale, and the hero does the work. A "Starts tomorrow" bar sits over "Holiday head start" and a huge **$5 95** above a green Vanilla Bean Noel candle. The price is the first thing the eye lands on, and the offer is concrete. That is a strong first screen.
- The rest of the email sags. The second block, "Ready-to-give joy," has only soft copy about 45 hours of fragrance and repeats the same "Start your list" button. The "You might like" row is hand soap, hand sanitizer and a snow globe holder. None of those is a candle, so the row dilutes the one thing the email is selling. The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks add more clutter. The send is also the fourth candle or body-care price push in about a week, after the $6.95 body care on 10/17, the BOGO candles on 10/18 and the flash sale on 10/18. A teaser fits that cadence, but it needs a stronger reason to act now.
- Verdict: a clear, well-built price hero, held back by a vague "Start your list" CTA and a body that drifts off the candle offer. I'd call it a 6.

## What's working

- The **$5.95** price is huge and sits on the first screen, so the offer reads instantly.
- "Starts tomorrow" gives a credible, specific time hook, and the subject echoes it.
- The hero candle is on-season and attractive, and the green and teal palette fits holiday.
- The "Excludes boxed candles. Up to 24 per customer." qualifier is right under the price, so it isn't buried.
- Free shipping at $50 and the Rewards line sit in a header strip at the top.

## What's weak

- "Start your list" is vague for a sale that hasn't started. It doesn't say what the shopper should do or save.
- The "You might like" row shows hand soap, sanitizer and a snow globe, not candles, and it pulls attention from the offer.
- The second block repeats the same CTA and adds no price or urgency. "Ready-to-give joy" is brand copy, not a reason to click.
- The "New" circle outline on the hero candle looks like a stray overlay and clashes with the price.
- No end date is visible in the body. The sale window only appears in the fine print, October 20 to 22.
- No ratings or reviews appear anywhere. The footer blocks add length without helping the sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "Start your list" with something concrete, like "Set a reminder" or "Shop candles tomorrow." Better still, add a visible date line, "Tomorrow–Thursday, Oct 20–22," under the price.
- 2. Swap the "You might like" row for three or four single-wick candles with scent names and a "$5.95" label on each.
- 3. Put social proof, such as star ratings or "bestselling scent," on the hero candle.
- 4. Cut the "New" ring and the "feel good formula" block so the email ends on the candle offer.
- 5. Add a one-line comparison, like "Reg. $26.95," to make the savings feel real.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Third-party tracking domain:** the tracking pixel is served from `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, not the sender domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Images use `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. This is normal ESP behavior, not a defect.
- **Identifier in the pixel URL:** `?e=200511831233&c=101926_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_595SW_PREV_BAL_G6M_V10` carries a recipient-level ID and a campaign code. This is expected for tracking.

It will fail or fall back, and `@import` is ignored by many email clients anyway. The `font-main` stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial, so impact is low.
