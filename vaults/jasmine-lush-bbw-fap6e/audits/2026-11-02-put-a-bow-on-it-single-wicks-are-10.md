# 🎀 put a bow on it: single wicks are $10.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a $10 single-wick send that gets buried under a general holiday catalog. The top hero is good: a glowing winter candle, "Merry wicks, right on time," and a big "$10" with "All Single Wick Candles" and a Shop now button. The eye lands on the price first. Below that, the email turns into a scroll of mood-board panels: "Classic wonder," "Create your own wonderland," "Our favorite holiday traditions," "The Home Sweet Holidays collection," and "Bring on the merry." None of these repeat the offer.
- Compared with the last five days, this is a step down in sharpness. The earlier $10.95 3-wick sends (11/1 "don't miss out," 10/30 "it's here") had a huge price and an "Ends today" or "Starts today" bar over every hero. This one has a "Starts today" bar and one price block, then drifts into brand storytelling. The subject promises one clean idea, single wicks at $10, but the body spends about 80% of its length on lifestyle imagery.
- There are also visible layout problems. Large blank gaps sit between the first and second modules and again at the bottom. The footer area has stray fragments of lines and text that look broken. Overall it's a decent hero with a weak body, and it's the kind of send that adds to the candle fatigue.

## What's working

- The "$10" is large and sits directly over the candle hero, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- "Starts today" on a dark bar adds a time cue, and "Excludes boxed candles" is stated openly next to the price.
- The winter candle hero image is on-season and glows, which suits the holiday timing.
- The "Shop now" button is visible and sits next to the price.
- The Rewards, texts and pickup links near the bottom give loyalty and convenience hooks.

## What's weak

- Only the top hero carries the offer. Every other panel is vague copy such as "Inspire wonder" and "The most wonderful scents of the year," with no price or product call to action.
- There is a big white gap below the "Classic wonder" panel, and a large empty area before the footer. They look like missing or unloaded modules.
- The bottom of the email shows stray line fragments and floating bits of text that look broken.
- The "Classic wonder" and "Our feel good formula" panels use small, low-contrast text over busy images and are hard to read.
- The email has no deadline. "Starts today" says nothing about when the price ends, which weakens urgency after a week of "ends today" sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lifestyle panels down to a short row of three or four $10 single-wick products with names and a "Shop $10 candles" button. That would lift the email the most.
- 2. Repeat the $10 offer, or a small "$10 single wicks" tag, in each remaining section, so the offer carries the whole scroll.
- 3. Add a clear end date, for example "Ends Sunday," so there's real urgency.
- 4. Fix the blank gaps and the broken footer area before sending.
- 5. Make the secondary text larger and put it on solid backgrounds.
