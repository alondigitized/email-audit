# $3.50 room sprays today only. 🎉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led room spray send. "Today only" sits over a hero reading "Instantly make every room merry," with a big **$3.50** and a visible "Shop now" button. The offer is concrete and the hero is easy to read. It also lands after four days of $5.95 body care and $2.95 minis, so it is a welcome change of category.
- The body of the email works against the hero. Four more offers follow: $5.95 body care, $9.95 body wash, a $40 gift set, and free shipping at $50. Every block has its own urgency label ("Limited time only," "2 days only"), so "today only" loses its force. The "Up to 24 per customer" fine print sits right under each price, and there is a promo code, JUMPFORJOY, with a barcode below the hero.
- The render also has visible bugs. Large empty white gaps sit above the three product tiles and again near the footer. The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text on a pale background and is hard to read. The footer shows stray lines with no readable text. Overall it is a decent hero wrapped in a cluttered, slightly broken body.

## What's working

- The $3.50 price in the hero is large and clear, and "Today only" sets a credible deadline.
- The "Shop now" button is visible and sits next to the offer.
- The festive product shot of three sprays fits the season.
- The free-shipping bar at $50 is a useful secondary incentive at the top.
- Switching to room sprays after days of body care and minis gives subscribers something new.

## What's weak

- Five offers compete for attention, and each has its own urgency label, which dilutes the "today only" hero.
- Large blank gaps appear above the three product tiles and in the footer.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has low-contrast text that is hard to read.
- The footer shows stray lines with no readable text.
- The "Up to 24 per customer" caps and the code requirement ("with code* JUMPFORJOY") weaken the sense of an honest offer.
- The barcode right under the hero is visual clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the room spray offer plus one backup, such as the $5.95 body care. Drop the $9.95 wash and the $40 gift set from this send.
- 2. Fix the blank gaps and the low-contrast banner, and tidy the footer.
- 3. Put the JUMPFORJOY code in the button or hero so there is no separate barcode block.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop $3.50 room sprays."
- 5. Show scent names or reviews on the sprays, such as Cranberry Snowflake and Winter Candy Apple.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style email (`cl.S7.exct.net`, `bbwus.everestengagement.com` tracking) with dark-mode handling. The confirmed issues are missing alt text on at least 5 images, one non-HTTPS image, and an unsupported font `@import`. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture pipeline and are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **54 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **An image carries a click-tracking URL as its identifier.** The QA entry is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_350CRS_AM_NON-TBC_FULL_…`. That URL is probably the `<a>` wrapping an alt-less image.
- **Mixed content.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=…` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy may rewrite it, but other clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning. It is also a dynamic-content image with no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (confirmed, 6 flagged):**
  - `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `…/d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `…/00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `…/7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `…/bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as nothing or as filenames. If any of them carry the "$3.50 room sprays" offer, that offer is lost.
- **Ineffective web-font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't host. `@import` in `<style>` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-family` stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial, so it degrades safely, but the request does nothing.
- **Duplicate charset declarations.** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` both appear. This is harmless, but redundant.
- **Structure.** In the visible source, the `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no visible `</head>` or `<body>`. The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm a malformed document.
- **Doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email, and `lang="en"` is present.
- **Dark mode.** It uses `prefers-color-scheme` plus `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` selectors, and `meta color-scheme` is set. I found no issues in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source. The footer and the rest of the body are truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed warnings:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA notes say these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. They can't be treated as sender defects without the raw headers.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.
- **From domain:** the sender is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with SFMC, but SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment remains unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are unverifiable.** All 54 links go through click-redirect domains, and the final landing URLs were not resolved. The only visible tracking is the vendor's `e=` and `c=` parameters. The `c=` value encodes the campaign name (`…_PROMO_0_RDM_350CRS_AM_NON-TBC_FULL_…`) rather than standard `utm_*` params.
- **Landing-page match:** the destinations were not probed, so I can't confirm the "$3.50 room sprays today only" offer matches the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five image-host PNGs listed above and to `LiveContent.aspx`. Use `alt=""` for purely decorative images.
2. Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS (`https://cl.S7.exct.net/…`), or confirm the SFMC tenant supports an HTTPS image domain.
3. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for "Helvetica Neue", or replace it with a real hosted font. Keep the system-font fallback stack.
4. Re-run QA with the raw `.eml` headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Follow the 54 tracking redirects to their final URLs. Then check that each landing page resolves, that `utm_*` params are carried through, and that the offer matches.
6. Capture the full HTML, not the truncated version. That would let you verify the document structure, the footer address, the unsubscribe link and any merge tokens.
