# 👋 only a few hours left to shop $1.95 hand creams.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear last-call send with one dominant offer. A "Final hours" bar sits over "Make it the softest season" and a big **$1.95** hand cream price, with a visible "Shop now" button and the in-store code GETREADY. The subject promises exactly that, so there's no gap between inbox and body. It works as a conversion email.
- It's also the second send from this brand today. Earlier on 10/20 was "$5.95 single wicks = instant (early) holiday cheer. 🎄", and the candle offer comes back here as a second module. Three more offers follow: the $5.95 candles, "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" on full-size body care, and a "You might like" row. A subscriber who sees a "few hours left" urgency email on the same day as another promo will start to discount the urgency. The page gets noisier as you scroll, and the hand cream offer is the only one that matches the subject.
- My judgement is a competent, urgent, price-led send that's undercut by cadence and by offer sprawl below the hero.

## What's working

- The hero price is huge. **$1.95** is the first thing the eye lands on, beside two hand cream products.
- The urgency is credible. "Final hours" in the top bar matches "only a few hours left" in the subject, and "2 days only" on the candle module makes the two deadlines distinct.
- The in-store barcode and "Online, use code GETREADY" block makes the offer usable in both channels without a separate click.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. The nav (New arrivals, Top offers, Gifts) is simple and the "Shop now" buttons are easy to find.
- The seasonal framing ("softest season", "Holiday head start") fits the date.

## What's weak

- This is the second promo from the brand today, after the $5.95 candle email. The "few hours left" urgency loses weight when the same offers keep showing up.
- The body has four competing offers: $1.95 hand cream, $5.95 candles, Buy 3 get 3 FREE body care, and the product row. The candle module is nearly as large as the hero, so the eye gets pulled away from hand cream.
- Every CTA says "Shop now". None names the product or the price.
- The "You might like" row is small and low-contrast. The three hand creams are shown without prices, so it doesn't reinforce the $1.95 deal.
- The qualifiers (up to 24 per customer, code required, excludes some items) are small, and the fine print at the bottom is a very long block.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one story. Drop the candle module and the B3G3 block, or push them well below the hero. Today's earlier candle send already covered that offer.
- 2. Make the CTA specific. Replace "Shop now" with "Get $1.95 hand creams" so the verb, product and price all appear on the button.
- 3. Put a live countdown or an explicit end time ("ends 5:59 AM ET") in the hero instead of the generic "Final hours".
- 4. Give the "You might like" row prices ("$1.95") and a "Shop hand cream" link, so it supports the offer rather than just showing product.
- 5. Add a one-line summary of the code and the limit (code GETREADY, up to 24) next to the hero button, so shoppers can see the conditions before they click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$1.95 hand creams end tonight 🧴`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: hand cream, $1.95 (limit 24)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code GETREADY ends at 5:59 AM ET. Stock up on Cocoa Shea, Snowflakes & Cashmere and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your scents now, since $1.95 won't be back after tonight.`
