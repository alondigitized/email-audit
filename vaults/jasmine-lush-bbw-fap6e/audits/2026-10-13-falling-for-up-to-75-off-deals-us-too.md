# falling for up to 75% off deals? us too! 😍 🍁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a big-number seasonal sale lead. The hero is a "Fall sale" headline over a huge **75% off** on a leaf-strewn cream background, with a "Shop now" button. The offer is easy to find, and the autumn imagery fits mid-October. The subject's "up to 75% off" and the hero's "75% off / Select items" agree, so the email doesn't bait and switch.
- Below the hero the email loses focus. Three more modules follow: a Wallflowers plug ("Set the bar high for hosting"), a Platinum body spray ("Stronger scent, better you") and a "Our feel good formula" banner. None shows a price, and only the hero has a visible CTA button. The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text on a pale background and is hard to read. Large blank gaps sit above "You might like" and above the legal block. The fine print is long and dense. It includes a separate $3.50 Wallflowers offer that the body never mentions.
- Compared with the last few days, this send is less sharp. The Oct 10–12 emails each led with one hard price ($3.50, $5.95, $1.95) and a "Final hours" tag. This one swaps that for a vague "up to 75%" on "select items" and has no urgency signal in the hero. It is a decent brand-awareness send but a weaker conversion send.

## What's working

- The 75% off numeral is huge and dominates the hero, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- A visible "Shop now" button sits directly under the offer.
- The autumn leaf imagery matches the "Fall sale" copy and the season.
- The Rewards bar at the top ("Join Rewards for free treats and more") puts a loyalty hook above the hero.
- The Platinum award-winner badge adds a little social proof.

## What's weak

- "Select items" is vague, and nothing in the hero or modules shows which products are 75% off.
- The Wallflowers and Platinum modules have no price, no CTA button and no clear click target.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has low-contrast text on a pale background and is hard to read.
- Large empty gaps above "You might like" and above the footer look like layout breakage or missing images.
- The "You might like" products are tiny and unpriced, with no sale prices to show the discount.
- The long fine print and the unmentioned $3.50 Wallflowers offer in the legal text muddy the honesty of the offer.
- There's no deadline anywhere in the hero, unlike the "Final hours" tags on recent sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show real sale prices. Replace the "Select items" hero with a named category, or add two or three hero products with their "was/now" prices, so the 75% claim becomes something concrete.
- 2. Add urgency to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", matching the "Final hours" tag that worked in the Oct 10–12 sends.
- 3. Add a CTA button to each module. Use "Shop Wallflowers" and "Shop Platinum", and put the $3.50 Wallflowers price on the first one.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the "Our feel good formula" banner, or cut it. Trim the empty gaps.
- 5. Add prices and ratings to the "You might like" tiles. Move the legal text to a collapsed or shorter block.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate comes from 3 header-related warnings, which are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay may have stripped the headers. The confirmed code-level defects are missing alt text on 6 images and some malformed `<head>` markup. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe link and body-link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 45 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Mixed domains:** Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, but click tracking goes through `bbwus.everestengagement.com`. Click links use a third-party domain, not the sending brand's subdomain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). This is a minor reputation and filtering risk, not a defect.
- **Exposed identifiers:** The tracking URL carries `e=200511831233` and `c=101326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_75OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. The `e=` value looks like a per-recipient identifier. I can't confirm whether it is a hashed or opaque token.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):** 6 images have no alt attribute.
  - Five are hosted at `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`, with filenames `4851c40c-…`, `d6327aef-…`, `00a9ac12-…`, `7b7c94ef-…` and `bbebdf75-…` (.png).
  - The sixth is an image wrapped in the link `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=2005118…`. If it is the primary CTA or hero, screen readers and images-off clients get no link text.
- **Malformed `<head>`:** After the `<meta>` tags, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears with no `</head><body>` boundary in the visible source. The table, its MSO conditional comments and its `<style>` block sit inside `<head>`. Clients parse this inconsistently.
- **Redundant markup:** The same `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` block appears twice, in an `[if mso]` and an `[if gte mso 9]` comment. There is also a duplicate charset declaration: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … UTF-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`. These are harmless but add weight.
- **Broken font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't host, so the request will likely fail. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and many other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack covers it, so impact is limited to a wasted request.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with `<html lang="en">`. `lang` is set, which is good.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Bath &amp; Body Works </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the campaign.
- **Dark mode:** Support exists (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`). `.darkmode-dark` forces `70px × 70px`, so any dark-mode image swap that isn't 70px will be distorted. The truncated source doesn't show which elements use it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The footer may contain tokens, but it was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed. Re-check against the raw `.eml`.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN, RFC 8058):** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify it on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay didn't expose the header, so no conclusion is possible from this data.
- **Footer content:** The postal address and visible unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No visible UTM parameters:** Links are wrapped in Everest redirects (`?e=…&c=…`). The `c=` string encodes the campaign (`75OFF`, `BAU_PROMO`, `G6M`), but UTM propagation to the landing page is unverified because the probe skipped all 45 links.
- **Landing page unverified:** I can't confirm whether the "up to 75% off" claim in the subject matches the destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 6 flagged images. For the linked image, use alt text that describes the link action (for example, "Shop up to 75% off").
2. Close `</head>` and open `<body>` before the first layout `<table>`. Move the `<style>` and MSO blocks into `<head>`.
3. Remove the `Helvetica+Neue` Google Fonts `@import`, since the family doesn't exist there and `@import` is stripped by many clients. Rely on the system font stack.
4. Re-run the compliance checks on the raw message source (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Resolve a sample of the Everest redirects with a browser-like client to confirm destination status and UTM or campaign parameter carry-through.
6. Remove the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` blocks and charset meta, and set a campaign-specific `<title>`.
