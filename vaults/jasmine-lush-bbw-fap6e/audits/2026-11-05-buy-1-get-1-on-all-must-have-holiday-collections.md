# buy 1 🎁 get 1 🎁 on all must-have holiday collections. 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear BOGO send with a real offer. The hero says "Buy 1 get 1 free" on select holiday items, with "Lowest-priced item is free" and a "Today only" bar above it. The code HOLLYJOLLY appears twice, once beside the CTA and once as a barcode. The Snowflakes & Cashmere product shot is on-brand and the headline is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The email gets messy below the hero. The "Magic happens when you choose to wish" banner is a second, unrelated message (A Thousand Wishes, a new launch). Under it is a large blank gap. The product row is mismatched: Champagne Toast car refill, Winter Candy Apple sanitizer and Winter Candy Apple soap are not the fragrance collections the hero promotes. The "Our feel good formula" block is low-contrast white text on a cream background and is nearly unreadable. The footer also has large white gaps with scattered underlines. Compared with 11/4's "$1.95 is driving off soon" send, this is less focused and less specific.
- Judgement: a decent offer, but the render is sloppy and the message is diluted. Expect solid opens and mediocre clicks.

## What's working

- "Buy 1 get 1 free" is large and dominant, and "Today only" adds credible urgency.
- The "Shop now" button is a clear outlined CTA next to the code and the "Up to 24 per customer" cap.
- The promo code appears twice, as text and as a barcode, which helps both online and in-store redemption.
- The hero product shot (Snowflakes & Cashmere mist, body cream and fragrance) matches the holiday collections the subject promises.
- The Rewards strip at the top and the "More to love" links give members a way in.

## What's weak

- A large blank white gap follows the Thousand Wishes banner, and more blank space runs through the lower half of the email. Both look like broken or missing content.
- The three-product row (Champagne Toast refill, Winter Candy Apple sanitizer and soap) has no prices and does not match the BOGO fragrance hero.
- The "Our feel good formula" text is white on cream, so it is nearly illegible.
- The second hero, "Magic happens when you choose to wish," pulls attention away from the offer. It has a vague "New" badge and no CTA.
- The footer is mostly empty white space with orphaned underlined fragments, so the legal and unsubscribe text isn't readable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or fix the blank gap and the empty footer so the email reads as finished.
- 2. Make the product row match the offer by showing the BOGO fragrance items with their prices, such as mist and cream at the actual price.
- 3. Move the Thousand Wishes launch to its own send, or give it a real CTA below the offer.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the "feel good formula" block, or cut it.
- 5. Put the HOLLYJOLLY code and the end time in the preheader, and tie the subject to the specific collections.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with solid dark-mode and Outlook handling. The QA data confirms several image accessibility and security gaps, a malformed `<head>`, and a broken font import. Compliance and authentication headers couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links unprobed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination, redirect chain and status are unverified. No broken links are confirmed.
- **Insecure tracking asset.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…` is a plain-HTTP dynamic-content image. Gmail's image proxy and some clients can block or warn on it.
- **Subscriber ID in a URL.** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=110526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_B1G1SSCEE_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` carries a subscriber-level `e=` value in a query string. That is typical of ESP click tracking, but the value is visible to anything that logs or forwards the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images.** Four are CDN assets under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - The fifth, `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`, is on the same CDN path.
  - The sixth is the `LiveContent.aspx` image. With images blocked, screen readers and image-off clients get nothing for these.
  - The `everestengagement.com` entry, flagged for missing alt, is probably a tracking beacon. Beacons should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Markup outside `<body>`.** In the visible source, the `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and its `<style>` block follow the `<meta>` tags with no `</head><body>` between them. That is invalid under the HTML 4.01 doctype and makes parsing client-dependent. The source is truncated, so check the full HTML before acting.
- **Broken font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't serve. The import is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, and elsewhere it adds a failing request. The fallback stack handles rendering, so remove the import.
- **Redundant meta.** Both `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are declared. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Dark mode.** The `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are in place. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `e=` parameter above is populated, not a raw token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, not failures.** The QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and no `Authentication-Results`. Its own note says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are therefore unknown, and one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Check the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox.
- **Footer not inspected.** The footer, with the unsubscribe link and postal address, falls outside the truncated HTML, so it was not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTMs visible.** The only full URL visible uses ESP-native tracking (`c=110526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_…`) rather than UTM parameters. Whether the landing pages receive `utm_*` values depends on the 42 unprobed redirects.
- **Landing alignment unchecked.** No landing page data was captured, so nothing can be confirmed here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content PNGs and the one on the same CDN path. Set `alt=""` on tracking beacons.
2. Serve the `LiveContent.aspx` image over HTTPS, or confirm that Marketing Cloud's HTTPS variant is available for that image.
3. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for Helvetica Neue and rely on the existing system font stack.
4. Wrap the content in a proper `</head><body>` structure after confirming the problem in the full HTML.
5. Re-run header checks against the raw message, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.
6. Follow a sample of the tracked links to verify final destinations and UTM or campaign parameter propagation.
