# $1.95 is still here for a few more hours. 🕒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a "final hours" urgency send stacking three separate offers — $1.95 hand cream, $5.95 body care, $13.95 candles — under a single "FINAL HOURS!" banner, but the urgency claim only actually applies to the top offer with a code (MAINSQUEEZE); the other two read as ongoing promotions bundled in for scale. The hero shot (hand cream tubes with apple slices and honeycomb) is clean and specific, with a real price and a real code, which is the strongest thing in this email.
- This is the second $1.95 hand cream send from this brand today alongside the "Feel-Good Fall Event" and "Candle picking season" beats already running — cadence is getting noisy. The email is functionally solid (no render bugs, clear pricing, clean visual hierarchy) but it's doing three jobs at once (clearance urgency + new fall launch + candle promo) and none of them gets the full spotlight.

## What's working

- Hero offer ($1.95 hand cream, code MAINSQUEEZE) is fully visible above the fold with clear pricing and a visible "Shop now" button.
- In-store barcode + "Show on phone or print" instructions add real utility for a walk-in shopper.
- Three-tier layout (hand cream → body care → candles) is visually distinct with color-coded banners (dark red, dark red, black) so sections don't blur together.
- "We think you'll love" product row adds relevant cross-sell without cluttering the top offer.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- "FINAL HOURS!" urgency is applied to the whole email but only the hand cream offer has a code/deadline feel — the body care and candle sections undercut the urgency by reading as evergreen.
- Three competing offers in one email dilutes the primary CTA; the eye has to work to figure out which is *the* offer.
- This is essentially the same $1.95/MAINSQUEEZE hand cream mechanic Bath & Body Works has been running, just re-wrapped in urgency language — feels like a deadline retrofitted onto stale inventory rather than a genuine limited-time event.
- "Feel-good fall event" and "Candle picking season" are both already running as their own standalone sends today/this week, so repeating them here reads as filler rather than a fresh reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a single-offer urgency email — just the $1.95 hand cream with a hard deadline (e.g., "ends tonight at midnight ET") — and let body care/candles run in their own sends as already happening. Combining three offers under one urgency banner is the single biggest drag on this email's effectiveness.
- 2. Add an actual countdown or specific cutoff time next to "FINAL HOURS!" — right now the urgency is a label, not a fact.
- 3. Move the barcode/in-store instructions higher or make them more visually prominent if in-store redemption is a priority channel.
- 4. Drop "Feel-good fall event" and "Candle picking season" from this send entirely to avoid the redundancy with today's other Bath & Body Works emails already logged.

- **Subject Alt B:** `last call — $1.95 hand cream, up to 24`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab it before MAINSQUEEZE expires tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sulfate-free, paraben-free — $1.95 while it lasts.`
