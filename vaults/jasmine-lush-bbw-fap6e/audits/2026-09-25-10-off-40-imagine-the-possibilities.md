- This one actually works. "This one's for the dreamers" plus a lavender butterfly-print Body Wash hero photo is genuinely pretty, and the $10 off $40 offer with code SOAR is stated clearly and immediately below the headline — no scavenger hunt like some of this brand's holiday-teaser sends (e.g. 09-22 "we've got merry on our minds" split personality). The subject line matches the hero exactly, which is rare for this brand's recent cadence.
- Below the fold it turns into a standard B&BW scroll-stack: a second $4.95 lotion/cream flash deal, a candle lifestyle shot, a Fruit Fusion "Cherry Crush" launch banner, then recs and footer boilerplate. Nothing there is broken, but stacking three separate offers (SOAR $10/$40, $4.95 lotion, Fruit Fusion launch) in one email dilutes the clean single-offer hero this brand nailed on 09-24 with the $3.50 room spray send.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy match exactly ("$10 off $40" / "This one's for the dreamers") — no bait-and-switch.
- Offer is concrete and visible without scrolling: "$10 off any $40 purchase, use code SOAR" sits directly under the headline.
- Clean single CTA button ("Shop now") right under the offer, high contrast against the purple hero.
- Hero art (butterflies, purple lighting, Butterfly Body Wash bottle) is on-brand, seasonal, and visually striking — no render bugs.
- Barcode graphic for in-store scanning is a nice touch for omnichannel shoppers.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers below the hero (SOAR, $4.95 lotion/cream, Fruit Fusion collection) with no visual signal for which one matters most — classic scattergun pattern this brand keeps repeating.
- "Dewy debut" section's actual offer float ($4.95, up to 24 per customer) is comparatively hard to spot vs. the hero's bold $10 off — inconsistent typographic hierarchy between sections.
- Candle section ("Candles with a taste for entertaining") has no visible price or code at all — pure branding with no conversion path beyond "View in store."
- Long footer/fine-print block eats a lot of vertical scroll relative to actual offer content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections max: SOAR hero + one supporting cross-sell (pick either the lotion deal or Fruit Fusion, not both) — this brand's best-scoring recent send (09-24 room sprays, 8/10) won by staying single-minded.
- 2. Add a deadline/time stamp near the SOAR offer in the hero itself ("Today only" is already in the top banner — repeat it near the CTA button so it's visible even if someone screenshots just the hero).
- 3. Give the candle section a price or CTA — right now it's a dead scroll with no shoppable hook.
- 4. Tighten the footer legal block or move soonest-expiring terms up near the offer instead of burying them at the bottom.
