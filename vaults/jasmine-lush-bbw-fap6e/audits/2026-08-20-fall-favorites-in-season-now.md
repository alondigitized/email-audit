# fall favorites in season now. 🍎🎃🫐
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is one of the better-constructed Bath & Body Works sends in the recent run — the subject "fall favorites in season now" actually matches what's in the hero for once, unlike the "fall for a new fragrance" / "Small flicker. Big scent." mismatch from 2026-08-18. "Candle picking season" with Afternoon Apple Picking and Sweet Cinnamon Pumpkin candles at $13.95 is a clean, on-season, on-brand open, and the second banner's Buy 3 Get 3 Free on full-size Body/Skin/Hair Care is a genuinely strong offer stated in plain numbers.
- Where it falls down is stacking: two hero offers back-to-back (candles, then B3G3), followed by an 8-tile "Top Offers" grid and four more secondary CTAs (texts, BOPIS, Find a Store, guarantee badge) before the footer. There's no single primary action — a reader has to choose between at least three competing CTAs before scrolling is even done.
- Net: honest, seasonally relevant copy and real pricing, dragged down by a diffuse, everything-but-the-kitchen-sink layout. Call it a solid mid-pack send — better than this brand's mismatched-subject emails, worse than the $1.95 hand cream send from 2026-08-19 which had one crystal-clear offer.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are aligned for once — "fall favorites in season now" delivers actual fall candles (apple picking, cinnamon pumpkin), fixing the mismatch problem flagged in 2026-08-16 through 2026-08-18 sends.
- Two concrete, specific offers are visible: $13.95 3-wick candles and Buy 3, Get 3 Free on full-size Body/Skin/Hair Care.
- "Dermatologist Approved" claim is sourced with a footnote ("Based on review of independent testing by board-certified dermatologist"), which reads more credible than a bare badge.
- Clean product photography — candles, body cream, and jam-jar-style lotion all render sharply with no broken images or overlapping text.
- "STARTS TODAY" banner gives real, credible urgency rather than vague "hurry" language.

## What's weak

- Two hero offers compete for attention right out of the gate (candles $13.95, then B3G3 on body/skin/hair) — no single primary CTA emerges.
- The 8-icon "Top Offers" grid (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Soaps, Men's Shop, Home Care, Gifts) plus a "Today's Deals" button fragments attention further and reads like a homepage, not a promotional email.
- Bottom third is padded with low-value asks (text sign-up, BOPIS, Find a Store, "We Just Want You To Love It" guarantee) that dilute rather than reinforce the offer.
- Preview text isn't visible/legible in this render — can't confirm it complements the subject rather than repeating it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and cut the other, or reorder so the stronger offer (Buy 3, Get 3 Free — objectively bigger value than $13.95 candles) leads above the fold instead of appearing second.
- 2. Trim "Top Offers" from 8 tiles to 3-4 category links max, or drop it entirely and let the two hero banners carry the email.
- 3. Move "MORE TO LOVE" (texts, BOPIS, Find a Store) below the footer social icons, or cut it — none of it drives incremental revenue this send.
- 4. Add a specific expiration date to "STARTS TODAY" in the hero copy itself (e.g., "through 8/24") so the urgency is scannable without reading fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `candles $13.95 + buy 3, get 3 free starts today 🍂`
- **Subject Alt B:** `fall candle picking is officially open. 🎃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Afternoon Apple Picking, Sweet Cinnamon Pumpkin & more — all 3-wicks $13.95.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus buy 3, get 3 free on full-size body, skin & hair care.`
