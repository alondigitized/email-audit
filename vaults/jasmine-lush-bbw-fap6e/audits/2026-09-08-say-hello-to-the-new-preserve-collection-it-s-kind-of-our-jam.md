# say hello to the new Preserve Collection. it's kind of our jam. 🍇🍎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a total departure from the brand's week of "$3.50 TODAY ONLY" urgency stacking — no discount, no countdown, no fine print, just a new collection launch styled like a farmhouse cookbook shoot. It's the most aesthetically cohesive send in this brand's recent history (2026-09-04 through 2026-09-07 all leaned on Wallflowers/body-care price callouts), and the golden-hour photography with blackberry jam, honey toast, and apple orchard product tie-ins actually lands as "kind of our jam."
- The trade-off is real, though: there is zero offer, zero urgency, and zero price anywhere in this email. For a subscriber trained by this brand's own cadence to expect a specific dollar or percent number, that's a jarring reset — pleasant to look at, but nothing pulls you to buy today versus next week.
- Judgement: a strong brand/seasonal-story send that will underperform on clicks relative to this brand's discount-driven sends, but it's a smart once-in-a-while palate cleanser after five straight "$X.XX today only" emails.

## What's working

- Consistent golden-hour, farmhouse-cottagecore art direction across every hero image — no stock-photo whiplash.
- Product shots (candle, body spray, honey jar, apple-orchard candle) are sharp, well-lit, and each has a "New" badge for clarity.
- Copy is tight and sensory ("Smells and looks like your homemade blackberry jam," "Crisp. Juicy. No trip to the orchard needed.") — genuinely on-brand and different from the flat "$X.XX" callouts of recent sends.
- Single clear "SHOP THE COLLECTION" button breaks up the scroll at a logical midpoint.
- "We think you'll love" cross-sell row adds a personalization touch without cluttering the main story.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or promo anywhere — a hard break from this brand's last five sends, all of which led with a specific dollar figure ($1.95, $3.50, $5.95, $13.95).
- No urgency device at all after a week of "TODAY ONLY" / "FINAL HOURS" — subscribers conditioned to act fast have nothing to act on here.
- Very long, image-heavy scroll (roughly 8-9 full-width sections) before reaching the single CTA button — high mobile scroll fatigue for a top-of-funnel launch.
- Secondary CTAs ("Sign up for texts," "Buy online, pick up in store," "Find a store") are low-commitment utility links, not product-forward next steps.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one soft, honest offer or incentive tied to the launch (e.g., "New, and 20% off through Sunday") — this brand's audience is discount-trained, and a zero-offer send risks lower conversion even with the best photography of the week.
- 2. Move the "SHOP THE COLLECTION" button up near the top, right after the hero, and repeat it after each product vignette — right now it's buried mid-scroll with three product beats before it.
- 3. Tighten the scroll — three near-identical "styled women in a field" photos (hero, mid, near-bottom) is redundant; cut one and let the product close-ups (candle, jam, honey, apples) carry more of the visual weight.
- 4. Give the "We think you'll love" row real CTAs — link each product name directly instead of a generic "SHOP" under each, so it reads less like filler.
- **Subject Alt A:** `new: Preserve Collection — blackberry jam, honey toast & orchard apple 🍇🍎`
- **Subject Alt B:** `just dropped: scents inspired by slow living 🍯`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wild Blackberry Jam, Cinnamon Honey Toast & more — new for fall.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Homemade-jam candles and honey-toast scents, straight from the orchard.`
