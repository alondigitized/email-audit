# 🚨 final hours: $5.95 scents for him. ⛰️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing reminder for the $5.95 men's body care and $35 cologne deal that the "one day only" send went out on earlier today. The hero is the same one. "Just added: your new routine" sits over a teal cologne bottle and a Santal Ridge tube, with $5.95 and $35 priced underneath and the FRESHPICKS code. A small "Final hours" tag is the only new element. The subject carries the urgency, but the body barely does.
- Below the hero the email drifts into a generic BBW catch-all. There is a BOGO candle block, a "New Everyday Luxuries" block with Buy 3 get 3 free, a "Fall sale 75% off" banner and a "You might like" row. That is four extra offers competing with the one the subject promised. A subscriber who just got this morning's send will feel the repeat. The email is competent but redundant, and it dilutes its own urgency.

## What's working

- The hero prices are large and easy to read: $5.95 men's body care and $35 full-size cologne, with the code beneath.
- The "Final hours" tag and the "Limited time" labels back up the subject's urgency.
- The product photography is clean. The bottle and tube are sharp and the layout is uncluttered.
- The "Shop now" buttons are clear and repeated for each block.
- The in-store barcode and "online, use code" line cover both purchase paths.

## What's weak

- This is the same creative as this morning's "this smell-good steal is one day only" send, with a small tag added.
- "Final hours" is a tiny label above a huge "Just added: your new routine" headline. The headline reads as a new-arrival message, which contradicts the urgency.
- The BOGO candles, Buy 3 get 3 free, 75% off and "You might like" sections pull attention away from the men's deal. The 75% banner is a recycled message from the 10/13 send.
- The offer needs a code, and the dense fine print runs to about eight paragraphs of disclaimers. That makes the $5.95 deal feel conditional.
- The "Ready for the feel good? Join Rewards" banner at the top is a weak opener for an urgency send. It adds friction before the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the deadline. Replace "Just added: your new routine" with something like "Ends tonight: $5.95 body care for him", and put a countdown or an "ends 11:59 PM ET" line next to the price.
- 2. Cut the body to one secondary offer, or none. A last-chance send should not re-pitch candles and a 75% sale.
- 3. Make the code visible and tappable. Put FRESHPICKS inside the CTA area or give it a copy-code button, and name the CTA "Shop $5.95 men's body care".
- 4. Move the Rewards banner out of the top slot and shrink the legal block.
- 5. Skip the send, or gate it to non-openers of this morning's email, so the cadence stops feeling like a repeat.
