# everyone’s shopping it. 👀 LAST DAY for buy 3 get 4 free EVERYTHING.*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day version of the same Buy 3, Get 4 FREE send BBW has run all week. "Ends today | In-store & online" sits under the Black Friday 2026 banner, so the deadline is credible. The hero is unchanged from the 11/25 "you buy 3. you get 4 FREE*" send: "ONLY ONCE A YEAR" over the gift-bag photo, then a red block with "ENTIRE STORE BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in huge white type. The eye lands on the offer immediately.
- The subject line does the work here. "LAST DAY" is the first time this week the urgency is real. It is also the only thing new, because the creative is recycled. The second offer is a secondary module: "FIRST TIME EVER 4 gifts to choose plus a free tote for $40 with any $50 purchase". It is in effect a gift-with-purchase and competes slightly with the hero. The body adds almost nothing for someone who has seen the earlier sends.
- Judgement: a solid, readable closer that earns its send through the deadline alone. It is competent but tired. After six or seven near-identical sends, it relies on subscribers who haven't bought yet.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable. "BUY 3, GET 4 FREE" in giant white type on red is the first thing you read.
- "Ends today | In-store & online" in a red bar above the hero gives the deadline credibility and covers both channels.
- Four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) give clear entry points into the offer.
- The "$40 for 4 gifts + free tote, over $110 value" module is a concrete second hook with a visible "Shop now" button.
- The free-shipping bar ($50 purchase) and the Rewards prompt sit above the hero and reinforce member value without cluttering it.

## What's weak

- The creative is essentially the same as the 11/25 and 11/27 sends. A "LAST DAY" subject on identical art undersells the urgency.
- The email has two competing offers (Buy 3 Get 4 and the $40 gift set). The second dilutes the focus of a last-day message.
- The Black Friday 2026 banner at the top is cropped at both edges ("ACK FRIDAY 2026"), which reads as a clipped ticker.
- The "You might like" products are small and low-contrast, with no prices or reviews, so the product detail is thin.
- The fine print is a very long block of tiny text, and the "free" items are only the lowest-priced four of seven. The asterisks make the offer feel less clean.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero reflect the deadline. Add a countdown or "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM" directly on the red block, since "LAST DAY" currently lives only in the subject.
- 2. Remove or demote the $40 gift module on a last-day send. One offer, one message.
- 3. Replace "You might like" with bestsellers that qualify for the deal, with prices and star ratings, so the shopper can fill a 7-item cart fast.
- 4. Fix the cropped banner so "BLACK FRIDAY 2026" is fully readable.
- 5. Add one line of plain-language terms near the CTAs: "Free items are the 4 lowest priced of 7."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 4 free items when you buy 3 🖤`
