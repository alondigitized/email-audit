# last day for prime-time savings. what'll you score? 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day version of the "prime time" run, and it is tighter than the sends before it. The 10/06 emails ("are you primed to prep for holiday?" and "tons of deals. 🤑 2 days.") used five red tiles. Today's send cuts that to three: $12.95 3-wick candles (online exclusive), $3.50 foaming hand soaps, and $3.50 Wallflowers refills. Each tile has a big price, an "Ends today" label and a "Shop now" button, so the offer is clear in about two seconds.
- The weak spot is everything below the tiles. A "Fall sale, up to 75% off" strip is left over from 10/05. It carries a different offer in a different season, and "Select items" is vague. After it come a generic "You might like" row of body mists and wash, a "feel good formula" brand block and a "More to love" link list. The urgency fades there, and none of it relates to the three deals above. The footer's fine print also shows the $12.95 candle is online only, while the other two deals run in store and online.
- Overall it is a solid last-call send. The deadline is real and the tiles are legible. But it is the fourth or fifth promo in about 72 hours with recycled creative, so it works as a reminder, not a new reason to click.

## What's working

- Each tile leads with a huge price, so the $12.95 and $3.50 offers register at a glance.
- "Ends today" appears on every tile. After the "2 days" send on 10/06, the deadline is credible.
- Cutting from five tiles to three makes the email shorter and easier to scan than the 10/06 sends.
- The tiles show real products (Fresh Balsam candle, the Nutcracker soap, Wallflowers refills) in rich holiday styling, with strong contrast and no broken images.
- The free-shipping bar at the top ("any $50 purchase") gives a clear incentive to add items to the order.

## What's weak

- The "Fall sale, up to 75% off, Select items" strip is stale 10/05 creative. It competes with the holiday tiles and muddies the "last day" message.
- The "You might like" row (A Thousand Wishes mists, Into The Night wash) has no price, no deadline and no link to the deals, so the urgency stops.
- The "Shop now" buttons are thin white outlines on busy photos, which is low-contrast for the main CTA. The solid navy "Find a store" bar at the bottom is the heaviest button in the email and carries the least important action.
- The candle deal is online only, while the other two are in store and online. That is only explained in the fine print.
- The email is long, and most of the length is footer and fine print. The brand-voice block ("Our feel good formula") adds nothing on deadline day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the 75% off strip and the "You might like" row, and replace them with one more deadline element, such as a countdown or a "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" bar. This would help most because it keeps the whole email on a single message.
- 2. Make the "Shop now" buttons solid and high-contrast (white fill with dark text, or solid red). Put a "Shop all deals" button under the third tile.
- 3. Name the scent or product in each CTA, for example "Shop $12.95 candles" and "Grab $3.50 soaps". "Shop now" is generic.
- 4. Say "Online only" on the candle tile itself, and put "In-store & online" on the other two.
- 5. Don't send the same three tiles again after today. If there's a follow-up, change the creative, for example the next Fresh Balsam or Black Friday teaser.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: $12.95 candles end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends today: $3.50 soaps + $12.95 candles 🕯️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `3-wick candles $12.95 (online only), hand soaps and Wallflowers $3.50. Gone at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $50 too. Prime-time prices end tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is sent from an ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, going by the `click.e2.` and `camarker-inner` markers) with 41 tracked links. The visible source has structural HTML problems: block elements inside `<head>` and content before `<body>`. Six images lack alt text. The relay-stripped headers mean unsubscribe and authentication cannot be confirmed from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA check skipped 41 tracking links (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects), so none of their destinations were probed. Redirect health and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?CCVNU24WFEPUVESX4O2TY6S7SY.70264&d=70264&bmt=0` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""`. It sits in a `width="0" height="0"` table with `mso-hide:all`, which is standard and not a defect.
- **Third-party tracker:** A second image is served from `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD`. It appears to be a pixel for a different vendor (Everest), and the `e=` parameter looks like a recipient-level identifier.
- **Font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host, so it will likely return a 400 error. The font stack still falls back to Helvetica/Arial. The `@import` is also ignored by Gmail and other clients that strip it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements containing `<style>` blocks are placed inside `<head>`, before `</head>`.
  - More `<table>` wrappers (`<!-- Begin variables -->`, `<!-- Begin Preheader and Header -->`) sit between `</head>` and `<body>`.
  - Parsers will hoist or relocate these elements inconsistently. Some clients may drop the `<style>` rules, including the dark-mode `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block.
- **Duplicate charset declarations:** The source has `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" ...>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`. It also uses an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with an HTML5 `charset` meta. This is harmless but inconsistent.
- **Missing alt text (QA warning):** Five images are hosted at `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  The sixth is the Everest tracker above. If the five content images carry promo or CTA content, screen-reader and images-off users get nothing, and some clients show the filename.
- **Preheader:** The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none !important; font-size:1px`). It is followed by a padding block of `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` entities to stop body text bleeding into the preview. No issue.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. ## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` images. Use `alt=""` explicitly for purely decorative ones. Add `alt=""` to the Everest pixel.
2. Move the `<style>` blocks into a single `<head>` section. Move the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables into `<body>`, and remove the duplicate charset meta.
3. Replace the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" with a valid font or drop it, since the fallback stack already covers it. Prefer `<link>` or a web-safe stack, because `@import` is stripped by Gmail.
4. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks on raw headers from a direct send. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass and align.
5. Resolve a sample of the 41 redirect links with a follow-redirects fetch. Confirm they return 200, carry UTM or campaign parameters, and land on pages matching the promo.
