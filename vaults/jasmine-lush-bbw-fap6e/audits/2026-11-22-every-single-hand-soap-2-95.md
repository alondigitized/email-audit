# every single hand soap = $2.95. 👏👏👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer send, and the hero carries it. A huge "$2.95" sits under "ALL Hand soaps" with a "Shop now" button, a "Limited time only" green tag above it, and three soap bottles on a pastel gift-box shelf. The eye lands on the price straight away. It is also much tighter than the 40%-off repeats from Nov 19–21, and it is close to the same hand-soap offer as the Nov 21 "$2.95 soaps start today" send.
- The middle and bottom of the render are weak. Under the "Give 3 wicks of wonder" candle banner there is a large blank white gap, about 130px at display scale. The three Winter Candy Apple products sit below it with no price, no visible button and no clear link to the hero offer. The bottom third of the email is also a long stretch of white space with only a few stray underline fragments. That looks like broken or missing content, and it undercuts an otherwise good top.
- My judgement: a strong hero on an offer subscribers have already seen. The candle banner pulls attention away from soap, and the render gaps make the lower half look unfinished. It is a good send with sloppy execution.

## What's working

- The "$2.95" hero is oversized and unambiguous, with "ALL Hand soaps" making clear it covers the whole category.
- A single "Shop now" button sits right under the price, with a "Limited time only" tag above it.
- The product shelf shows three recognizable bottles, a "New" badge and the dermatologist-approved callout, which adds trust.
- The sender is familiar, and the free-shipping bar and Rewards link are visible at the top.
- Winter Candy Apple, a seasonal scent, is shown in the product row.

## What's weak

- The "Give 3 wicks of wonder" banner is a big candle image with no visible offer or button, and it competes with the soap hero.
- There is a large blank white gap between the candle banner and the product row.
- The product row has no prices, no "$2.95" reminder and no visible CTAs.
- A very long empty white area at the bottom, with a few stray underline fragments, looks like missing content.
- The "Limited time only" tag has no deadline, so the urgency is vague.
- The red "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" ticker is cropped at both edges, which reads as slightly cut off.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank areas first. Close the gap under the candle banner and fill or remove the empty footer space. The email looks half-rendered right now.
- 2. Put "$2.95" on each product card in the row, with a "Shop soaps" button. The row is currently dead weight.
- 3. Remove or demote the candle banner, or give it its own offer and CTA. As it stands it dilutes the soap message.
- 4. Replace "Limited time only" with a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 5. Don't send the same hand-soap offer again within a day of the Nov 21 send. Vary the creative, for example by showing scents.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `stylingblock-content-wrapper` markup, with `everestengagement.com` click tracking). The one confirmed defect is missing image alt text on 5 images. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the captured data, and the HTML source was truncated before the body and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 42 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed.
- **Observed pattern:** links use `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112226_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_295HS_0_BAL_G6M_V1`. The `e=` value looks like a per-recipient ID, which is normal for ESP tracking. The `c=` value is a campaign code.
- **Image links:** at least one image link (the one above) has no alt text. See section 3.
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images, confirmed by QA):**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  All are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`. The sixth finding is the linked image at `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…`. Its link has no accessible name, because the QA output falls back to the href as the label.
- **Font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. To my knowledge Helvetica Neue isn't hosted on Google Fonts, so this request likely fails. `@import` in email is also stripped or ignored by many clients, and the `font-main` stack already falls back to Helvetica/Arial. Low severity and harmless, but it adds an unnecessary external request.
- **Document structure (low confidence):** in the truncated source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no visible `</head><body>`. This may be a truncation artifact, so it isn't flagged as a defect.
- **Dark mode:** the `prefers-color-scheme` rules and the `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` Outlook selectors are present and consistent. No issues found.
- **MSO conditionals:** `OfficeDocumentSettings` is declared twice, under `[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]`. This is redundant but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source or subject line. The footer wasn't included, so tokens there can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three QA findings are warnings, and none is confirmed as a real defect:
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found.** QA notes it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is an observability gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies, so one-click support is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, and the same domain serves the images.

The footer was truncated, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link can't be verified from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTM parameters** (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) appear on the visible links. Attribution relies on the `c=` campaign code (`112226_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_295HS_0_BAL_G6M_V1`), which encodes a date (11/22/26) and an offer code (`295HS`, matching the $2.95 hand soap subject line).
- Final destinations weren't resolved, because the redirect probe was skipped. Landing-page alignment with the "$2.95 hand soap" offer can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 5 flagged images. For the linked image, make the alt describe the link destination, or use `alt=""` only if adjacent link text covers it.
2. Re-run the audit with raw headers captured, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
3. Resolve the click-redirect chains in a follow-up probe, using a browser or `curl -L` with a real user agent. Verify that the final URLs return 200 and show the $2.95 hand-soap offer.
4. Remove or replace the `@import` for Helvetica Neue, since the font stack already falls back correctly.
5. Capture the full, untruncated HTML so the footer's unsubscribe link, postal address and any merge tokens can be audited.
