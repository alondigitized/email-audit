# final hours till $1.95 hits the road.🚦
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing send for today's $1.95 car fragrance refill deal. The hero does its job: a "Final hours" tag, a big **$1.95** next to **$2.95** hanging diffusers, a CARBUDDY code, and a visible "Shop now" button. The in-store barcode strip is a smart touch. It is the second send on this offer today, after "$1.95 cruisin' scents - today only. 🚗💨". The "final hours" urgency feels more automatic than earned.
- Below the hero, focus drops. The email stacks a $3.50 Wallflowers tile, an eau de parfum launch, a 75% off Fall sale strip, a "You might like" row and a brand-values block. The Wallflowers tile says "Starts today" in the same email that says "Final hours". That is the same $3.50 price that was billed as "almost over" on Oct 10, so the messaging reads as recycled.
- Judgement: a solid single-offer hero attached to a generic catch-all body. It will convert the people already in the car-scent mindset and dilute everyone else. It is competent, but it adds to the fatigue from a week of near-identical deadline sends.

## What's working

- The hero shows one dominant price, **$1.95**, with a clear secondary **$2.95**, so the offer is readable in about two seconds.
- The "Final hours" tag and the lowercase 🚦 subject line line up, so the urgency is consistent from inbox to hero.
- The barcode with "In store, show barcode on phone or printout / Online, use code CARBUDDY" covers both channels.
- The product photography is clean and seasonal, and the "Cruise into cozy season" headline ties the car scents to fall.
- The Rewards banner and the free-shipping-over-$50 bar sit at the top without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The Wallflowers tile says "Starts today" under a "Final hours" hero. The two timelines contradict each other.
- The fine print is long, and the "up to 24 per customer" limit and code requirement are small and tucked under the prices.
- The body piles on four unrelated promos (Wallflowers, eau de parfums, 75% off Fall sale, "You might like"). Nothing competes with the hero, but nothing supports it either.
- The "Shop now" CTA is generic, and the Fall sale "SHOP" link is tiny and easy to miss.
- It is the second $1.95 send today, after the 🚗💨 send this morning, and the third day of back-to-back deadline emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body down to the $1.95 hero plus one companion offer, and drop the eau de parfums and brand-values blocks from deadline sends. That would lift the email most.
- 2. Resolve the "Starts today" versus "Final hours" conflict. Either label the Wallflowers tile "Also today" or remove it.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with something specific like "Shop $1.95 car refills", and put the 24-per-customer limit next to the price in readable type.
- 4. Add a real countdown or exact cutoff ("ends 11:59 PM ET") instead of "final hours". Skip the second send on the same day when the first already said "today only".
- 5. Add a product-level link or a rating on the Tree Farm refill to give people a reason to click a specific scent.
