# score all your juicy, hydrating faves. 😉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is B&BW's third Fruit Fusion send in three days — the 7/19 teaser, the 7/20 launch, and now today's "score your faves" push. The meaningful upgrade here is that they finally attached a hard offer (BUY 3, GET 1 FREE on all full-size body, skin, and hair) to the Fruit Fusion creative, which the 7/20 send conspicuously lacked. The subject line also actually delivers on what's inside for once, after the bait-and-switch problems on 7/19's "back-to-school" send and the teaser/body mismatch pattern this brand keeps repeating.
- That said, the same structural problem persists: the email cannot pick a lane. Fruit Fusion gets the hero, then the email pivots to an All-Day Moisture body wash section, then a Wallflowers Fall Fragrance Plugs offer (fall plugs in mid-July is an odd reach), then the Summer Sale 75% banner — which at this point has appeared so many times in recent sends that it reads as a permanent footer, not a campaign. The scroll-depth required to hit that sale banner means most subscribers never get there.
- The "WE JUST WANT YOU TO LOVE IT! No guarantees" closer at the bottom is genuinely charming and I'd love to see it further up the email. The rest of the offer stack below the hero is wasted real estate on a mobile device.

## What's working

- **BUY 3, GET 1 FREE** is the clearest, most concrete offer B&BW has attached to the Fruit Fusion line across its three-send arc — finally gives subscribers a reason to act today vs. just browse.
- **Subject-to-hero alignment** is the tightest in this campaign cycle: "juicy, hydrating faves" maps directly to the Fruit Fusion hero copy ("Juice up your routine. Stay hydrated with benefits.").
- **Fruit Fusion product photography** is vivid and distinctive — purple, yellow, and blue bottles read as a cohesive new launch line, not just another generic body care shelf.
- **SHOP THE COLLECTION** button placement is immediate, above the fold on most mobile viewports, with good contrast.

## What's weak

- **Wallflowers Fall Fragrance Plugs mid-email, mid-July** is jarring — "Your Fall Escape Awaits" in summer reads as inventory push, not a campaign beat, and undercuts the seasonal freshness of Fruit Fusion.
- **Summer Sale 75% banner** (bottom-third) has appeared in every send this week; it has become visual noise, indistinguishable from a standing promo, not a closing urgency play.
- **"LIMITED TIME ONLY" on Wallflowers Refills at $15** has no deadline — day, time, or date — which makes the urgency language meaningless.
- **Top Offers / Today's Deals thumbnail row** is too small to be actionable on mobile and competes with every other module above it; it adds scroll depth without adding purchase motivation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one offer per send.** BUY 3, GET 1 FREE on Fruit Fusion is the news — let it run the full email. The Wallflowers and Summer Sale modules should be in a separate send, not buried under the primary campaign.
- 2. **Give "LIMITED TIME ONLY" an actual deadline.** "Ends Sunday at midnight" or "Today only" converts; "LIMITED TIME ONLY" does not.
- 3. **Pull the "No guarantees" footer voice into the hero.** The brand voice flash at the bottom is the most human moment in the email — something like "No miracles promised. Just softer skin." in the hero subhead would make this send memorable.
- 4. **Move Summer Sale 75% above the fold OR kill it.** If it keeps appearing it should be the hero, not a scroll-buried afterthought. Otherwise drop it from this send entirely so Fruit Fusion gets credit for driving conversions.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 3, get 1 free — Fruit Fusion is here.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new hydration routine: buy 3, get 1 free.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Full-size body, skin & hair care — mix, match, and save today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Fruit Fusion + BUY 3 GET 1 FREE. Ends [date].`

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "score all your juicy, hydrating faves" (2026-07-21)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional email sent via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget, `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). Three confirmed issue classes: unresolved personalization/merge tokens producing empty parameter values, missing `alt` attributes on six images, and unverifiable authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 tracking links skipped** — all routed through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`; destination URLs and UTM consistency could not be verified by probe.

**Empty subscriber token in Everest engagement pixel:**
```
https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=072126_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_TBCB3G1_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_FULL_V1
```
The `e=` parameter (likely subscriber email/ID) resolved to empty. Engagement attribution for this variant will be broken.

**Open pixel** (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?...`) is structurally correct; no issue there.

**Leaked build artifact — shell template comment (appears twice):**
```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```
A shell command that was never executed; exposes build tooling detail in production source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=` empty in two `mi.bathandbodyworks.com` image URLs:**
```
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/86f62dc2bf1d8cce.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=072126_...
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/df3ce8c3cd22b912.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=072126_...
```
`mi_u` is the per-subscriber identifier for Marketing Intelligence tracking. Empty value means these image impressions are not attributable to individual subscribers.

**`e=` empty in Everest pixel** — see §2 above; same class of failure.

**Two empty `src` images** — unresolved merge tags that should have produced image URLs.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN) — Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (≥5k/day). Not captured by AgentMail relay; must be verified at SMTP level directly. If absent from actual delivery headers, this is a deliverability risk under Gmail/Yahoo's February 2024 bulk sender policy.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe not confirmed. Same verification caveat applies.

**`Authentication-Results` not captured** — SPF/DKIM status unknown via relay. BBW's ESP infrastructure almost certainly signs outbound mail; verify raw headers on a delivered copy.

HTML is truncated — footer CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link) could not be confirmed from the provided source. Assumed present given sender scale but should be spot-checked.

`mi_ecmp` campaign code (`072126_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_TBCB3G1_AM_BAL-NTF`) is consistent across image URLs. Everest pixel uses a variant suffix (`-OPTD_FULL_V1`) — indicates A/B variant tracking is in place, which is correct.
