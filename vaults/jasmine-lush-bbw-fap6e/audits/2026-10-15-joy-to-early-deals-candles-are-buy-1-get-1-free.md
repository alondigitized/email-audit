## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images, confirmed by QA)**, all under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those blocks are blank or announced by filename.
- **Document structure:** `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the last `<meta>` in `<head>`, with no visible `</head><body>`. Parsers will usually hoist it, but it is invalid markup. Clients that don't repair it can drop styling.
- **Doctype and charset:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the markup uses `<meta charset>`. There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv Content-Type` and `meta charset="utf-8"`), which is redundant but harmless.
- **Duplicate Outlook blocks:** two identical `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` blocks (`<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`). This is redundant, and the first is read by all MSO versions.
- **Web font:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. Helvetica Neue isn't a Google Fonts family, so the request likely fails or does nothing. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and other clients, so the stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial. This costs an external request with no benefit.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta and `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are present. No defects are visible.
- **Title:** `<title>Bath &amp; Body Works </title>` has trailing whitespace and no campaign context. This is minor.
