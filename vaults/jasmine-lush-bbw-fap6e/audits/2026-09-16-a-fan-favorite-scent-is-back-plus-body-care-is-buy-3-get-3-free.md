# a fan-favorite scent is back. plus, body care is buy 3 get 3 free. ☀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Member Days-flight send in three days, and it's the most crowded one yet: "fan-favorite scent" callback, buy-3-get-3 body care, 3x points, a sweepstakes, and a $1 PocketBac deal, all stacked in one long scroll. The hero delivers on the subject — Sun-Ripened Raspberry mist and cream are shown clearly under "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" — but by the time you reach the sweepstakes and PocketBac blocks it feels like three emails glued together rather than one coherent pitch.
- Compared to yesterday's 09-15 closer ("final hours... free 3-wick"), this one buries its best asset. Buy-3-get-3-free on full-size body care is a genuinely strong offer, but it's competing on the page with a sweepstakes, a rewards multiplier, and a $1 hand sanitizer flash deal — none of which reinforces the others. It reads as a kitchen-sink send rather than a focused push.
- Verdict: solid product photography and a real offer, but the email tries to do too much in one scroll, diluting the buy-3-get-3 hero that should be the whole story.

## What's working

- The Sun-Ripened Raspberry mist + cream product shot is clean, well-lit, and matches the "fan-favorite scent is back" subject line promise.
- "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" is stated plainly with the clarifying "Lowest-priced items are free" — no bait-and-switch ambiguity.
- Section dividers (purple 3x Rewards block, orange sweepstakes band, yellow $1 PocketBac block) create clear visual chunks, so even though it's crowded, each offer is scannable on its own.
- "We think you'll love" product row adds relevant personalized-feeling cross-sells (hand sanitizer, body cream, soap) right below the fold.

## What's weak

- Too many competing CTAs (Join today & shop, Download the app, Shop now, Find a store) dilute which action matters most.
- The hero doesn't show a product-specific dollar/percent price — "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" only becomes concrete once you scroll to it, while the top banner just says "Free shipping on $50."
- The sweepstakes and 3x Rewards blocks add clutter without a unifying visual thread — they interrupt the buy-3-get-3 narrative rather than support it.
- "Ends today" appears at the top but isn't repeated near the actual buy-3-get-3 offer, so the urgency doesn't attach to the thing you're being asked to buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — lead with fan-favorite scent + buy-3-get-3, and either drop or move the sweepstakes/3x points/PocketBac blocks to a follow-up send. This is the single biggest lift: one clear offer converts better than four competing ones.
- 2. Move "Ends today" down next to the buy-3-get-3 CTA button so urgency attaches to the actual purchase decision, not just the free-shipping banner.
- 3. Add a price anchor near the hero product shot (e.g., "starting at $16.50, buy 3 get 3 free") so the value is visible without scrolling.
- 4. Consolidate CTAs — pick one primary button style/color for the main offer and demote secondary asks (app download, texts, store finder) to the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `members only: buy 3, get 3 free — ends today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your favorite raspberry scent just returned. Stock up before the offer ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full-size body care: buy 3, get 3 free. Members get free shipping too.`
