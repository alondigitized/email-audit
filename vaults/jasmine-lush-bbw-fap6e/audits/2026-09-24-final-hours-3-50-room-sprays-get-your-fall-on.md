# final hours⏰ $3.50 room sprays. get your fall on...🍂
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same $3.50 Concentrated Room Sprays offer this brand ran on 09-24 ("one day only: $3.50 room sprays"), just repackaged with a "Final hours" urgency banner instead of "one day only." The hero is clean and on-brand — pumpkin, lavender sprigs, autumn styling — and the offer is stated plainly with a visible "Shop now" button, so this repeats the strong execution from that earlier send rather than the muddled ones from 09-22/09-21.
- Where it loses ground versus the 09-24 send is stacking: right below the room-spray hero is a second, unrelated "Dewy debut" $4.95 body lotion/cream promo that starts today, followed by a candles lifestyle banner with no price or CTA at all. Three different offers in one scroll dilutes the "final hours, act now" urgency the subject promises — if this is genuinely the last chance on room sprays, the email's own layout undercuts that by immediately pivoting to a brand-new offer that "just dropped."
- Solid, on-brand send with a real, visible offer up top — but the urgency framing in the subject isn't matched by the body's structure, and the candle section is dead weight.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states "$3.50 All Concentrated Room Sprays, up to 24 per customer, with code AUTUMN" — concrete offer, no digging required.
- "Final hours" banner directly above the hero reinforces the subject line's urgency.
- Visible, high-contrast "Shop now" button right next to the offer copy.
- Seasonal styling (white pumpkin, lavender, dropper bottles) matches the fall theme and subject line ("get your fall on").
- Product recs ("We think you'll love") and footer utility links (Rewards, texts, store locator) are all present and functional-looking.

## What's weak

- The "Dewy debut" $4.95 lotion/cream section directly competes with the "final hours" room spray urgency — two hard offers back-to-back muddies the single-CTA focus.
- The candles banner ("Candles with a taste for entertaining") has no price, no code, no button — pure filler that adds scroll without a payoff.
- Long vertical scroll before reaching any secondary navigation or lifestyle payoff; mobile readers may not get past the second offer.
- No countdown or explicit deadline time/date near the hero — "final hours" is stated but not backed by a specific cutoff visible in the render (the small print at the bottom has dates, but that's easy to miss).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the candle banner — it has no offer and just adds scroll fatigue between two real promos.
- 2. Move a specific deadline (e.g., "ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") into the hero itself, not just the fine print, to make "final hours" credible.
- 3. Either lead with room sprays only and move "Dewy debut" to a follow-up send, or visually subordinate the second offer (smaller module, no competing button styling) so it doesn't fight the primary CTA.
- 4. Add a second CTA button near the bottom product recs to capture readers who scroll past the hero without clicking.
