# last day 🗓️ to score this 3-wick steal.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-bell email on B&BW's $12.95 3-Wick Candle arc — Day 3, and the third time this exact creative has run in the May 28–31 window. "Sunshine Scents" hero, gold price badge, "ENDS TODAY!" red banner — it's structurally identical to the May 29 send ("wish granted: 3-wicks are $12.95") and nearly identical to May 28's launch. The arc has worked: B&BW knows this deal converts, and a "last day" closer is the correct structural play. But by the third iteration, a real subscriber is in pure FOMO-closing mode, not discovery mode, and the email doesn't sharpen the knife — it just re-serves the same plate.
- Below the fold, a second offer surfaces: a Buy 3 Get 3 Free on Body, Skin & Hair Care — timely product expansion, but it's buried under a different brand identity ("A New Kind of Nautical") with no price anchor, which diffuses attention. The "TOP OFFERS" grid below that adds further noise.
- Net judgement: structurally sound, brand-consistent closing bell — but the creative fatigue is real. A subscriber who opened May 28 and May 29 has seen this render twice already. The "last day" hook is the only differentiating signal.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TODAY!" red banner lands instantly** — top-of-email urgency is unmissable, no scroll required.
- **Gold $12.95 price badge on the hero candle** is the clearest offer signal in the email; eye goes straight to it.
- **"Sunshine Scents" copy + summer candle imagery** is seasonally coherent for late May.
- **Second offer (Buy 3 Get 3 Free)** expands the basket opportunity beyond candles without abandoning the same visit.
- **Brand voice is clean and consistent** — no lorem ipsum, no render bugs, layout holds.

## What's weak

- **Creative is visually identical to May 28 and May 29** — a subscriber who opened either prior send will feel zero new information here.
- **The B3G3 Free offer has no price anchor** — "Buy 3, Get 3 Free" with no floor price is hard to value at a glance; it reads vague next to the crisp $12.95 badge.
- **"A New Kind of Nautical" secondary hero** is stylistically jarring — a completely different brand campaign dropped mid-email dilutes the $12.95 close.
- **"TOP OFFERS" product grid** is small-thumbnail and product-dense; on mobile this will feel like a catalog dump rather than a curated CTA.
- **No scent specificity in the hero** — "Sunshine Scents" is a category label, not a named candle. A single hero scent name would make the offer feel more tangible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero copy to make "last day" feel earned.** The subject already says "last day" — the email interior should echo that FOMO, not just re-run the launch creative. Add a visible countdown or "ends tonight at midnight" detail directly on the hero.
- 2. **Give the B3G3 offer a price floor.** "Buy 3, Get 3 Free — body care starting at $X" converts harder than the bare promotion mechanic. Drop the "Nautical" campaign framing entirely; it's a distraction.
- 3. **Cut or collapse the TOP OFFERS grid.** On a closing-bell email, fewer choices = faster click. The grid is for browse emails; this one should funnel to two CTAs max.
- 4. **Name one hero scent.** "Sunshine Scents" is abstract. "Mahogany Teakwood — $12.95" is a purchase.
