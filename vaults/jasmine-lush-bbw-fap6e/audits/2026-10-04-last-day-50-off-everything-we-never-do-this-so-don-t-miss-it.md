# last day. 🔴50% off everything*. 🔴(we NEVER do this, so don't miss it!) 🔴
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the third day of the same 50% off campaign, and it's a clean closer. The burnt-orange hero is the same one used on 10/02 and 10/03: "Half off. Full spree. 50% off Entire store." The only change is a black "Ends today" bar above it, and that is the right thing to add. The 50% number dominates, and four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) sit right under the product lineup.
- The weakness is everything below the hero. Once you scroll past it, the email turns into a generic new-arrivals feed. The "New" blocks (fragrance mist, Home Sweet Holidays, bigger cleanser, Glow & Gather, Midnight Rouge) have no prices, no sale callouts, and no buttons. The final-day urgency fades, and the reader is left with soft editorial copy. The "*" on "Entire store" also sends you to dense fine print at the bottom, and I can't tell from the render what the exclusions are.
- Verdict: a solid last-call send. Because the subscriber has now seen this hero three days running, the only new information is the deadline. The body should have kept pushing that deadline and the discount instead of drifting into new-product storytelling.

## What's working

- The "Ends today" bar sits directly above the 50% hero, so the deadline is the first thing you read.
- The 50% type is huge, and nothing competes with it.
- The four category buttons right under the product lineup let you go straight to your category.
- The product lineup (Frosted Cranberry, White Pumpkin, Vanilla Bean, the maple-leaf candle) fits the fall season.
- The top banner stacks free shipping on a $50 purchase on top of the sale, which is a second reason to buy.

## What's weak

- The five "New" feature blocks have no price, no sale tag, and no CTA button. The urgency drops off right after the hero.
- The "You might like" row shows three small items with no prices and no 50%-off markers. That is where a shopper decides to buy.
- Headings like "Now's the time to try our new collections" are soft and don't mention "today."
- The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" sections come after the main offer and read as filler on a last-day send.
- The asterisk on "Entire store" and the fine print at the bottom are long, and the exclusions aren't visible near the offer. That weakens the "honest offer" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Repeat the deadline in the body. Add an "Ends tonight" tag to the product modules and put a second CTA button after the first feature block.
- 2. Show sale prices in "You might like," for example "$7.50 (was $15)," with a "Shop" button on each item. Better still, make the grid into a "best sellers at half off" row.
- 3. Cut or compress the "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks on a final-day send. They dilute the ask.
- 4. Add one short line near the hero spelling out the main exclusion, so the asterisk doesn't feel like a trap.
- 5. Vary the hero slightly from the 10/02 and 10/03 sends, for example with a countdown or "Last chance" in the headline, so it reads as the final send and not a repeat.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) send with structurally invalid HTML (content outside `<body>`, nested tables with no cell wrapper). Six images lack `alt` text, and the QA run (57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings) could not verify the unsubscribe headers, authentication, or any of the 49 tracking links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed.** The HTTP probe was skipped because they are on tracking/click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, destination status codes, and broken-link checks are therefore **unverified**, not passed.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?62JB3R5PDUSEBLUZVHOWKLW4X4.70253&d=70253&bmt=0` is a 1×1 `<img>` with `alt=""`. It is wrapped in a `width="0" height="0"` table with `mso-hide:all`. This is standard and not a defect.
- **Second tracker:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` is an image/asset on a third-party domain (Everest) in addition to the SFMC pixel. It has no `alt`, and its purpose can't be confirmed from the truncated source. The QA check labelled it with the URL instead of a filename.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed). Five are hosted at `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  The sixth is the `everestengagement.com` image above. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these may render as filenames or nothing. If any carry the offer text, that content is lost.
- **Invalid document structure** (visible in the source):
  - `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements are placed inside `<head>`, before `</head>`.
  - More tables sit between `</head>` and `<body>`, so they are outside `<body>`.
  - `<table style="width:600px">` is followed directly by another `<table>` ("Header LOGO") with no `<tr><td>` wrapper. Parsers handle this inconsistently (Outlook/Word engine especially).
- **Doctype and meta tags:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `<meta charset="utf-8">`, and `http-equiv="Content-Type"` is also declared. This is redundant but harmless.
- **`@import` web fonts:**
  - `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts does not host, so it will not load.
  - `@import ...Source+Sans+Pro...` is a separate block. `@import` in `<style>` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so both fall back to Helvetica/Arial.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared, and the dark-mode CSS is limited to `.darkmode-white` / `.apple-dark-*` classes under `@supports`. The visible source defines no dark-mode image handling (for example, transparent-PNG logo variants). I can't confirm whether that is a real problem without the rest of the HTML.
- The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none`) and padded with `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp;` filler. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The HTML was truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**, not proven missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, caused by the relay. The sender domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** the footer was cut off in the truncated source, so they are not confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified.** All 49 links are wrapped in SFMC click-redirects and weren't resolved, so the final URLs and any `utm_*` parameters are unknown.
- The only campaign identifier visible is the Everest query string `c=100426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_50OFF_AM_BAL_G6M_V1`. It suggests an internal naming scheme but is not a UTM.
- Whether the destinations carry the "50% off" offer and the `*` terms can't be checked here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to the 6 flagged images. Use descriptive text for any offer-bearing image and `alt=""` for decorative ones.
2. **Fix the document structure.** Move the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. Wrap the nested "Header LOGO" table in a `<tr><td>`.
3. **Remove the broken font import.** `Helvetica Neue` isn't on Google Fonts, so drop it. Use `<link>` instead of `@import` if Source Sans Pro matters, and keep the Arial fallback.
4. **Re-run QA with raw headers** (or from the original MIME source) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't treat these warnings as sender defects until the relay is ruled out.
5. **Resolve the tracked links** with a redirect-following check (or a Playwright click-through) on a sample of the 49. Capture final URLs, `utm_*` parameters, and status codes.
6. **Identify the Everest asset** and add `alt=""` if it's a pixel.
7. **Re-run on the full, untruncated HTML** to check the footer (unsubscribe link and address) and any later merge tokens.
