# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened the site and immediately got hit with a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" challenge modal — that's the main thing dominating the screen. Behind it I can just barely make out what looks like a pink/purple candle or body care product hero and some botanical elements, which honestly looks right up my alley. But the bot-check wall is front and center, and the only real content I can clock above that is the flash sale banner at the very top. BBW is clearly pushing a BOGO right now, which is very me, but the experience of actually landing on this page is rough.

## What's working

- **The flash sale banner** — "Today online only! Flash Sale: buy 1, get 1 FREE" hits immediately at the top of the screen. As a Semi-Annual Sale stockpiler, BOGO language is basically catnip. I read it before anything else.
- **The nav bar** — Clean, minimal, recognizable BBW branding. Hamburger, search, account, and bag icons are all exactly where I expect them on mobile. Muscle memory kicks in.
- **The partial hero aesthetic** — What I CAN see of the hero behind the overlay looks like pink/purple product bottles with green botanical elements. That color palette and product category feel right for a candle/body-care person like me.

## What's weak

- **The bot detection modal** — This is the entire problem. Landing on the homepage and being greeted with "Before we continue... Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" is genuinely jarring. It covers nearly the full viewport. On mobile, this reads as sketchy or broken, not "security."
- **Zero loyalty hook visible** — I'm a My Bath & Body Works Rewards member. There's no sign of my account, my points balance, or any member-specific greeting. The modal wall means even the nav's account icon is unreachable.
- **No CTA for the actual sale** — The flash sale banner says "Details >" but that's a tiny link in a thin strip. The full viewport is consumed by a challenge, so there's no shop button, no product image I can tap, nothing that drives me toward purchase.
- **"Details" chevron is easy to miss** — Small, right-aligned, low contrast against the dark banner. If I'm on autopilot I might not register it as tappable.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the bot-detection modal for recognized/logged-in users** — If I'm already signed into My Bath & Body Works Rewards, I should not be hitting a CAPTCHA. Session tokens or device recognition should clear this for returning customers immediately.
- **Elevate the flash sale "Details" link to a pill button** — Make it `Shop BOGO Now` styled as a white button inside the dark banner strip. The current small "Details >" text is too easy to skip over on mobile.
- **Show a rewards/loyalty widget in the nav or sticky header** — Even a small "Hi Jasmine — 420 pts" or a points-balance pill next to the account icon would make me feel seen the second I land.
- **If the bot-check is unavoidable, keep it lightweight** — The current modal is large and feels alarming. A smaller, less intrusive inline banner or a one-tap (not press-and-hold) flow would reduce the perceived friction significantly.
