# $3.95 lip care today only. 💄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led send, and a better one than the recent Wallflowers and flash-sale emails. A "Today only" banner sits over a "Pucker up, winter" hero. It shows a big **$3.95** next to strawberry lip balm and a lip mask, with "Up to 24 per customer with code MOISTURIZE" and a visible "Shop now" button. The offer is concrete and the eye lands on the price. It matches the subject, which the 11/16 Wallflowers send did not.
- The weak part is everything below the hero. Two large Wallflowers/holiday scent panels ("Plug into that festive feeling" and "Classic wonder") have no price or CTA and dilute the lip care message. There are big blank gaps between modules, and a large empty stretch near the footer where images or text did not render. - Overall it's a solid, honest price-led send. It would be stronger if it stayed on lip care instead of drifting into holiday scents.

- The $3.95 price is the largest element in the hero, so the offer is clear on first look.
- "Today only" in a dark banner above the hero gives credible urgency.
- A visible "Shop now" button sits next to the price.
- The promo code MOISTURIZE and the 24-per-customer limit are stated up front, with no hidden terms.
- The subject, hero product and offer all agree.
- The Rewards strip at the top and the "More to love" links give members a clear path.

## What's weak

- The two holiday scent panels carry no price and no CTA, so they pull attention away from lip care.
- Large white gaps sit between the modules and above the footer. They look like blank or unloaded areas.
- The "Fragrance that lasts up to 30 days" badge and the body copy on "Classic wonder" are tiny and low-contrast.
- The three small product tiles (Fresh Balsam, Snowflakes & Cashmere) have no prices, and the images are tiny.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has low-contrast text on a pale image. It is a brand message, not a reason to click.
- The hero fine print ("*Based on review of independent testing...") is small and distracting.

- 2. Fix the blank gaps, or collapse the modules that failed to render.
- 3. Make the CTA more specific, such as "Shop $3.95 lip care", and repeat it after the product row.
- 4. Add a rating or review count to the hero. The email currently has no social proof.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Salesforce Marketing Cloud email (`cl.S7.exct.net` and `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) with a dark-mode-aware, responsive build. Automated QA found no hard failures, but there are 3 warnings, six images without alt text, one non-HTTPS image, and invalid markup in `<head>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** They use click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Non-HTTPS image.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjMs...` is loaded over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the dynamic-content block may not render in clients that enforce HTTPS.
- **Mixed tracking hosts.** The email uses `cl.S7.exct.net` (SFMC) and `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_395LIP_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_`. Each is a separate third-party dependency for click and open tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on six images.** Five are `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/` files: `4851c40c-…`, `d6327aef-…`, `00a9ac12-…`, `7b7c94ef-…` and `bbebdf75-…`. The sixth is the `LiveContent.aspx` image. With images blocked, these render empty, and screen readers announce the filename or nothing.
- **Alt-less `everestengagement.com` image.** QA also flagged an image from `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, with its URL as the label. If it is a tracking pixel, it should carry `alt=""` explicitly. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Table in `<head>`.** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and the MSO conditional blocks appear right after the `<meta>` tags with no `</head>` or `<body>` in the visible source. Parsers will close `<head>` implicitly, which is invalid markup and risks inconsistent behavior across clients. The source is truncated, so this should be confirmed against the full HTML.
- **Duplicate charset declarations.** The source has both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`. This is redundant but harmless.
- **HTML 4.01 doctype with HTML5 meta tags.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is mixed with an XHTML-style self-closing `<meta .../>`. It is low risk but inconsistent.
- **Ineffective web-font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host. The request likely returns an error, adds a pointless network call, and is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers the rendering impact.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, tables use `role="presentation"`, and dark-mode handling covers both `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with proper authentication, but it is not verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address not verified.** They are not visible in the truncated source or the QA output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing-page parameters unverifiable.** All 46 links go through redirect domains and were not resolved, so final URLs, UTM presence, and promo alignment with "$3.95 lip care" could not be checked.
- **Campaign ID is visible.** The `c=111726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_395LIP_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_` parameter on the Everest link encodes the campaign, so attribution is carried server-side. Whether it survives to the landing page is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` images and the `LiveContent.aspx` image. Use `alt=""` on any pure tracking pixel.
2. Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS (`https://cl.S7.exct.net/...`).
3. Move the `<table>` and MSO blocks out of `<head>` and add proper `</head><body>` structure. Check the full HTML, since the source was truncated.
4. Remove the `@import` of `Helvetica+Neue` from Google Fonts and rely on the system font stack.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message at the receiving mailbox, not through the relay. If they are missing at the source, add them.
6. Re-run QA with redirect-following enabled on the 46 tracked links to confirm final destinations, HTTP status and UTM or campaign parameters.
