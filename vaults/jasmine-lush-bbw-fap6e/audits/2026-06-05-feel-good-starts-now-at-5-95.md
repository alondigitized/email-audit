# feel good starts now at $5.95! 🤗
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works opens a new body care arc on June 5, pivoting cleanly from the room spray double-send that closed yesterday. The teal "STARTS TODAY!" banner, the large "$5.95" price badge, and "Smells Like a Good Day" headline execute this brand's launch playbook with the same precision that scored the June 1 single-wick open a 9/10. - Below the fold, the email tries to run three simultaneous arcs: the body care launch, "$16 LIMITED TIME ONLY" body candles, and "Feel-Good Gifts. For Him." That's three different buying intents in one scroll, and the "For Him" gift section specifically snaps the audience in half — the subscriber who opened for $5.95 body scrub isn't necessarily in a male-gift mindset right now.

## What's working

- **Hero executes the launch beat perfectly.** Teal "STARTS TODAY!" banner + "$5.95" price badge + named product ("All Full-Size White, Body Scrub & Cream") + SHOP button — nothing ambiguous.
- **Margaritaville collab stops the scroll.** "Let the Fruits Light the Way" with branded packaging is the most visually interesting second section B&BW has sent in this June run.
- **Subject line is concrete and mobile-friendly.** "$5.95" in the subject, lowercase tone, 🤗 emoji, ~35 chars — clean inbox presentation.
- **No render bugs.** Every image block loaded, no layout breaks, no orphaned whitespace.

## What's weak

- **"Feel-Good Gifts. For Him." fractures the audience.** Dropped into a personal care launch for a female-skewing subscriber, this section asks the same person to switch from shopping for herself to shopping for a man. It belongs in its own dedicated email.
- **"SHOP" is a dead CTA.** No noun, no specificity. "Shop Body Care" or "Shop $5.95 Deals" would reinforce the hero offer instead of pointing at nothing.
- **No closing deadline.** "STARTS TODAY!" is half the urgency equation. Unlike the June 4 "final hours ⏱️" close-arc email (8/10), this launch gives no end date — lowering the reason to act now vs. later.
- **TOP OFFERS grid competes with the hero.** Four competing visual sections before hitting a thumbnail grid diffuses the single-offer momentum the hero created.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Remove or defer the "For Him" section.** Let the body care launch breathe; gift-for-him content earns its own day and subject line.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the hero.** "Ends Sunday" or "3 days only" right below the price badge converts "starts now" from a soft launch into real urgency.
- 3. **Sharpen the CTA.** SHOP → "Shop Body Scrub & Cream" or "Shop $5.95 Deals" — a verb + noun that mirrors the hero copy.
- 4. **Cap to two below-fold stories.** Keep the Margaritaville collab (it earns its place), cut the $16 candles section (a separate arc, a separate day), and drop the TOP OFFERS grid or move it to a tighter placement.

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "feel good starts now at $5.95!"

### 1. Technical Summary

Structurally sound HTML4 email with standard ESP infrastructure (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / ExactTarget on `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). Primary technical concerns are missing authentication header visibility through the relay, absent one-click unsubscribe support, and multiple images lacking alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**25 tracking links skipped** — all click-through URLs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which is standard SFMC click-tracking. No broken destinations were detected, but destination URLs could not be probed.

**Open pixel present** — 1×1 tracking image at:
```
https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?HWQPU5BRUOCUVPFJJ63ZAD7RHM.70249&d=70249&bmt=0
```
Standard practice; no issue beyond the expected Apple MPP inflation.

**No issues** with link structure or redirect chains from what was testable.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**5 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA:

| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| Hero/promo image | `https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/33160019b06936af.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=...` |
| Static asset | `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/418d0db7...` |
| Engagement tracker | `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=060526_...` |
| Two additional images | empty `src` attributes — likely conditional/dynamic blocks that rendered empty |

Missing alt text breaks screen reader accessibility and degrades the experience in image-off rendering environments (common in corporate email clients and many mobile clients with aggressive data saving).

The two images with empty `src` attributes are a soft bug — either unfired AMPscript dynamic blocks or suppressed conditional content that left orphaned `<img>` tags.

**Preheader duplication risk** — two separate preheader blocks are present: one in a standard `<div class="preheader">` and a second in a `stylingblock` slot. The visible preheader text ("keep skin hydrated and happy all summer long.") is only in the first block. The slot-based block appears empty. This is not a rendering bug per se but is a fragility — if the slot fires, clients may concatenate both.

**Google Fonts import** — `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Source+Sans+Pro')` will be stripped by most email clients (Gmail, Outlook). Fallback stack is `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`, which is appropriate.

**Fixed 600px width** — the outer table uses `width="600"` with no fluid wrapper, relying on `@media (max-width:599px)` for mobile. The media query breakpoint at 599px vs. the 600px container is a 1px gap that could cause the mobile styles to not fire on exactly-600px viewports.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

AMPscript comments reference:
- `@NTF_Flag` — used to conditionally set APT (Audience Personalization Token)
- `@APT` — applied to links for personalized tracking

No unresolved merge token literals (`%%variable%%`) are visible in the truncated source, which is the expected production state. The `mi_u=` parameter in the hero image URL is empty:
```
?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=060526_NRDM_TBC_595TBCMISTS_NS_BAL_V1
```
`mi_u` is a subscriber identifier — if this is intentionally blank for anonymous/non-personalized sends it is acceptable, but if it should carry a subscriber ID it is silently dropped.

### 5. Compliance

|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — cannot confirm presence |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules for >5k/day senders |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is the highest-priority compliance item. Gmail and Yahoo now enforce one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders; absence can trigger inbox suppression or spam folder placement at scale.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The campaign code `060526_NRDM_TBC_595TBCMISTS_NS_BAL_V1` appears in the hero image `mi_ecmp` parameter and the Everest engagement tracker, indicating campaign tagging is applied at the image level.

However, the 25 click links were not probed, so it is unknown whether:

This is a gap — campaign attribution to GA4 or site analytics cannot be confirmed from available data.
