# just a little longer for $5.95 single wicks. 🕯️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in two days pushing the same $5.95 single-wick candle sale. Yesterday's "$5.95 single wicks = instant (early) holiday cheer" was the same offer, and earlier today's "📣 last day for $5.95 single wicks." was the last-call version. A subscriber who has already seen "last day" will find "just a little longer" confusing. It reads as the deadline slipping, or as filler.
- The render itself is clean. A "Final hours" bar, a giant **$5.95** and the green Vanilla Bean Noel candle make the hero obvious, with a clear "Shop now" button. The email then stacks $3.95 lip care, a barcode and code XOXO, a Midnight Rouge fragrance block and a Buy 3 Get 3 Free body care banner. That is four offers in one email, so the single-wick message is diluted just as the subject promised one thing.
- Verdict: well built, but redundant and over-stuffed. The subject's vague urgency and the heavy cadence hold it back more than the creative does.

## What's working

- The hero price is huge and unmissable: "$5.95 All Single Wick Candles" over the Vanilla Bean Noel candle.
- The "Final hours" bars sit above the first two modules and give credible urgency.
- The "Shop now" buttons are clear and well placed.
- The in-store barcode with "Online, use code XOXO" cleanly separates the two redemption paths.
- No broken images or layout problems are visible.

## What's weak

- The subject says "just a little longer", but the body says "Final hours". The messages don't match, and it follows "last day" from earlier today.
- The email carries four competing offers: $5.95 candles, $3.95 lip care, Buy 3 Get 3 Free body care and a fragrance block. That is a lot of price points for one "last call".
- The "Make a grand entrance" fragrance module has no price or deal in its headline, so it is a large block with little reason to click.
- Limits and exclusions ("Excludes boxed candles. Up to 24 per customer") and a wall of legal text sit under the hero and in the footer. The offer feels more restricted than the headline suggests.
- The top "Join Rewards" banner is easy to skip, and it doesn't speak to existing members.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a third near-identical wick email. If a last-call send is needed, make it one hero with one deadline and cut the lip care and fragrance modules, or fold them into a separate send.
- 2. Make the subject say what's new. A clock plus the specific hour beats "a little longer".
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with "Shop $5.95 candles" so the button names the product.
- 4. Put the deadline hour in the hero bar instead of just "Final hours".
- 5. Add a price or deal line to the fragrance module, or remove it.
