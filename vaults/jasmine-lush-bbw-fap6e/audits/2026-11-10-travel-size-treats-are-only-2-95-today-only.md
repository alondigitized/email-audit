# ✈️ travel-size treats are only $2.95, today only.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led travel-size send. The "Today only" bar sits over a hero reading "Bring joy along for the ride," with a big **$2.95** under the product lineup. The offer is specific, the "Shop now" button sits beside the price, and the "CUTIES" code is shown as a barcode. The eye lands on the price fast.
- It then loses focus. A second BOGO candle block ("ALL candles Buy 1 get 1 FREE") carries nearly the same weight as the hero. That is a repeat of yesterday's 2026-11-09 "ALL candles are buy 1 get 1 free" send. Below it is a row of three small product tiles with tiny captions, then a washed-out "Our feel good formula" banner. The email also has large empty white gaps and a footer whose text is blank. That reads as broken.
- Judgement: a solid price-led hero undermined by a competing offer, dead space and a thin footer. It is better than a generic send, but it won't reach the 8 the candle BOGO got.

## What's working

- The $2.95 price is large and sits right next to "Shop now," so the offer registers immediately.
- The "Today only" bar adds credible urgency, in the same pattern as the 2026-11-07 "just for today" room-spray send.
- The pastel hero with three travel-size products is seasonal and shows what you get for the price.
- The "CUTIES" code is shown as text and a barcode, which makes it easy to use in store.
- The "More to love" block puts Rewards, texts and buy-online-pick-up-in-store links in one clear place.

## What's weak

- The candle BOGO block competes with the hero for attention. It repeats yesterday's offer, so the email has two offers and no single focus.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text on a pale background, and the text is barely legible.
- Large blank white gaps sit above the product tiles and below "Find a store." The footer links appear as stray underlines with no readable text, which looks like broken rendering.
- The three product tiles don't match the hero offer. They show a Wallflowers refill, a candle and a hand soap, with no prices and tiny grey captions.
- The offer fine print ("Up to 24 per customer with code") is small, and the code is hidden in the text and a barcode.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it one offer. Drop the candle BOGO, or cut it to a thin strip, so the $2.95 travel-size deal is the only message.
- 2. Replace the three off-theme tiles with three travel-size products, each with a $2.95 tag and its own link.
- 3. Put the code in a visible button, such as "Use code CUTIES," and move the "up to 24" limit into a readable line.
- 4. Fix the blank gaps and the footer so the text shows.
- 5. Make the "feel good formula" banner readable, or cut it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based ESP template with dark-mode and Outlook handling. The QA run found two confirmed defects: 6 images with no alt text, and one image loaded over plain `http://`. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 44 links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains, so none were checked for status or destination. The one redirect visible in the QA data is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295TRAVEL_AM_BAL-NTF_FU`.
- **Insecure image/tracking URL:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTYs…` is loaded over HTTP. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent image. Mail clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content may not show it, and the QA check flagged it as "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked".
- Mixed tracking domains are in use: `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, `cl.S7.exct.net` and `bbwus.everestengagement.com`. This is not a defect on its own, but the unbranded `exct.net` host on an HTTP URL is the weakest link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 6 images):**
  - `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `…/d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `…/00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `…/7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `…/bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx` (the HTTP image above)
  - The `everestengagement.com` redirect URL was also flagged as an image without alt.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these slots are blank or announced by filename or URL. If they carry the offer or CTA content, that content is unavailable to those users.
- **Unnecessary font request:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. "Helvetica Neue" is not a Google Fonts family, so the request can't return a usable font. `@import` in `<style>` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so this adds a request for no benefit.
- **Document structure (from the truncated source only):** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and its MSO conditional comments appear directly after the `<meta>` tags with no visible `</head><body>` between them. This may be an artifact of truncation or of the ESP's content-block wrapping. Check the full source for a `<body>` element.
- **Minor:** duplicate `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` blocks (`<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`), and both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`. These are redundant but harmless.
- **No issues found:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - A viewport meta tag is present.
  - Dark-mode handling is in place (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`).
  - Mobile media queries are present (`max-width: 600px` and `380px`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance
- **Not verifiable:** the QA run did not find `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), or `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing the headers. These are warnings from the capture path, not confirmed defects in the sender's configuration.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be checked.
- To confirm SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe, inspect the raw message at the original recipient mailbox, or send a test to a mailbox that retains full headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable:** the visible tracking URL carries a campaign identifier in `c=` (`111026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295TRAVEL_AM_BAL-NTF_FU`), but no `utm_*` parameters are visible. Because all 44 click-redirect links were skipped, the final landing URLs, the redirect chain and the offer alignment ($2.95 travel-size) could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 6 flagged images, and `alt=""` to any that are purely decorative. Prioritize any that carry the offer message.
2. Serve the `LiveContent.aspx` image over HTTPS (`https://cl.S7.exct.net/…`), or move it to the `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` HTTPS host.
3. Remove the `@import` of "Helvetica Neue" from Google Fonts. The existing `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallback stack already covers it.
4. Confirm in the full source that the `<body>` wrapper exists and the template is not rendering table markup inside `<head>`.
5. Re-run QA with a direct mailbox capture, or a header-preserving path, so the unsubscribe and authentication headers can be validated.
6. Follow the redirect chains for the 44 tracking links in a separate pass to confirm landing pages, status codes and UTM or campaign carry-through.
