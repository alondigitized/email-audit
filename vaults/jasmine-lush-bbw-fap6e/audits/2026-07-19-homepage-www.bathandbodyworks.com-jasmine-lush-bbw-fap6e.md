# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I literally cannot see the homepage — I hit a "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" wall the second I landed. The only commerce-forward thing visible is a thin promo strip at the very top announcing a "Clean Hands Event: $3.50 hand soaps & more." Bath & Body Works clearly has bot-detection running hot enough to catch a real shopper on mobile, which is a rough first impression for a loyalty member like me who just wants to browse new scents.

## What's working

- **Promo strip at top ("Clean Hands Event: $3.50 hand soaps & more")** — that price point is legit for a hand soap and is exactly the kind of deal I'd tap "Details" on. Relevant to my buying habits (I stock up on shower/body stuff during events).
- **Clean nav bar** — hamburger, search, account, bag icons are all there and uncluttered; the moment the modal clears I'd know exactly how to navigate.
- **Background image (partially visible)** — soapy, lathered hands behind the modal at least sets a sensory/lifestyle mood consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- **Bot-gate modal ("Before we continue… Press & Hold")** — this is the entire above-the-fold experience. Any momentum from opening the app evaporates instantly. For a returning rewards member on mobile this is borderline insulting.
- **No loyalty hook visible** — as a My Bath & Body Works Rewards member, I expect to see my account, points balance, or a member-exclusive deal the moment I land. None of that is accessible.
- **Zero product or campaign hero visible** — the entire homepage value prop (new scents, Semi-Annual Sale, seasonal drops) is hidden behind the gate. I have no idea what's being pushed right now.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is awkward UX** — this interaction pattern is designed for desktop; on a phone it's a clunky gesture that can misfire, especially with one hand.

## Recommendations

- **Exempt authenticated/returning app users from the bot gate** — if I'm logged into my rewards account, skip the challenge entirely. My session cookie is proof enough.
- **Surface the promo strip offer as a tappable full-width banner even behind the gate** — let the "Details >" link resolve so I at least see the campaign while waiting; it gives the page purpose.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a tap-based or invisible hCaptcha/Turnstile** — mobile-native verification that doesn't require a two-second hold gesture.
- **Pin a loyalty points reminder to the modal header** — even "Hi Jasmine — you have 350 points" would make the gate feel less hostile and remind me why I'm here.
