# a 3-wick deal to brighten the sky. 🎇
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' July 4th send — and it's a thematic pivot back to candles after two days of impulse-price plays ($1 PocketBac on 7/2, $3.50 Room Sprays on 7/3). The "brighten the sky" subject with fireworks emoji is doing obvious but effective July 4th theming. The hero creative is a clever visual pun: a soft-serve ice cream cone that resolves into a Neapolitan Ice Cream scented 3-wick candle. The problem is that pun takes a beat too long — on mobile, a subscriber scrolling fast sees ice cream, not the candle deal they were promised in the subject.
- The $13 3-wick price is the real draw here, and it's a meaningful step up from the impulse-tier deals this week. Candles are BBW's hero category, so this is the brand returning to home turf. A second section for "New Summer-Ready Soaps" with foaming hand soaps at ~$5.50 is logical but undercuts focus — the July 4th creative momentum doesn't carry into soaps.
- The email earns its open on brand familiarity and a clear price promise, but the ice cream/candle hero confusion and vague "LIMITED TIME ONLY" deadline (no specific end time) leave conversion points on the table. Competent mid-campaign execution; not their sharpest send this week.

## What's working

- **Subject-to-hero alignment is solid**: "3-wick deal" in subject line pays off immediately with $13 3-Wick Candles front and center below the hero.
- **July 4th theming is on-brand and timely**: fireworks emoji, "brighten the sky" copy, and summer ice cream scent feel cohesive for the holiday.
- **Price clarity**: $13.00 is visible and unambiguous — no "starting at" hedging.
- **Candle detail present**: "Up to 45 hours" burn time is surfaced, which is the one product spec candle shoppers care about.
- **Semi-Annual Sale banner** at the top gives campaign context and reinforces the deal legitimacy.

## What's weak

- **Hero image confuses the category**: the ice cream cone reads as food first; subscribers scanning at inbox speed may not process "this is a candle" before scrolling past.
- **"LIMITED TIME ONLY" has no teeth**: no specific end date or time shown — after seven days of BBW's "today only" and "final hours" urgency, a vague deadline won't move anyone.
- **"SHOP" CTA is the weakest possible verb**: two "SHOP" buttons with no product noun give subscribers nothing to orient around.
- **Soap section dilutes the July 4th story**: New Summer-Ready Soaps at ~$5.50 is fine product but the creative drops the fireworks theme entirely — it feels like a different email stapled on.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Sharpen the CTA copy** — "Shop $13 Candles" and "Shop Summer Soaps" instead of two generic "SHOP" buttons. One word change, measurable lift.
- 2. **Add a specific deadline** — "Today only" or a countdown to midnight works here; BBW has proven this urgency pattern drives the urgency-close send later in the day. Leaning on "LIMITED TIME ONLY" without a clock is lazy.
- 3. **Lead with the candle, not the ice cream** — Put the candle jar front and center in the hero; use the ice cream cone as a secondary graphic element or background. The visual pun is charming but not worth the category confusion.
- 4. **Cut or collapse the soap section** — Either give soaps their own send or reduce it to a "Also on sale today" module. Two hero sections competing for attention is a cannibalizing pattern.
- 5. **Reconnect the soap section to July 4th** — If keeping both, something like "fresh-start the long weekend" ties it back to the campaign theme.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$13 3-wick candles. light 'em up. 🎇`
- **Subject Alt B:** `today only: 3-wick candles, $13.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Every scent, every size. Pick your holiday fave before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 45 hours of fragrance for $13. Limited time.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound (valid DOCTYPE, charset, Outlook conditional comments) but has two confirmed compliance gaps (missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, unverified SPF/DKIM via AgentMail relay) and at least 4 images missing `alt` attributes. All 24 click links route through a redirect domain that could not be probed.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 click links skipped** — all route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Not resolvable by the QA probe, so destination URLs, redirect chain integrity, and UTM parameter presence are unverifiable from this audit.

**Open pixel** — `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?EJKV7QMRRBEUFDJLM3EGT2E5PY.70249` is placed in a `width="0" height="0"` table with `mso-hide:all`. Functionally correct but worth confirming the pixel fires in non-Outlook clients where the wrapper table collapses.

**No issues** with link syntax or broken `href` values detected in the visible source.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**4 images confirmed missing `alt` text:**

| Image | src |
|---|---|
| `30b43c8d4500368e.png` | `https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/30b43c8d4500368e.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=070426_EM_JUNSAS_...` |

| *(empty src, empty alt — 2 instances)* | `src=""` |

The two empty-src images are the more serious issue: they will generate broken image requests (GET on current page URL) in some clients and may trigger spam filters.

**Preheader duplication** — two separate `display:none` preheader blocks exist: one in a top-level `<table>` and one inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper`. The visible preheader text "grab your faves before it fizzles out..." is in the first block; the second block contains only whitespace-padding characters. This is a common BBW pattern but the duplicate hidden `<div>` adds unnecessary DOM weight and could surface in certain parser-heavy clients.

**`<style>` in `<body>`** — `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` appears twice: once in `<head>` and again inside the body `stylingblock-content-wrapper`. Gmail strips `<style>` tags in `<body>`, so the second `@import` is a no-op in Gmail. This is benign but redundant.

**`lang="en"` present** on `<html>` — correct.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript variables referenced but not visible in source** — comments reference `@NTF_Flag` and `@APT` variables. The truncated source does not show any unresolved `%%variable%%` tokens in visible content, so no merge-token bleed is detectable from this excerpt.

**`mi_u=` parameter is blank** on the mission intelligence (MI) pixel URL:
`https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/30b43c8d4500368e.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=070426_...`
The `mi_u` field appears to be an unfilled subscriber ID merge token. If this is not populated at send time, the MI platform cannot attribute the open to a specific subscriber.
