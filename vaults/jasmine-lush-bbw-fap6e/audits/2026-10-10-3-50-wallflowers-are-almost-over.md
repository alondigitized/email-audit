# $3.50 Wallflowers are almost over.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer lead. The hero is a "Final hours" tag, then "Get a jump on merry" over a big white **$3.50** for Wallflowers Fragrance Refills, with a visible "Shop now" button. The red retro oven with a Wallflowers plug-in on its door is a charming, on-theme image. The eye lands on the price first, which is the right call.
- The problem is cadence. The brand already sent "get ready, $3.50 is today only..." earlier on 10/10 with the same hero offer. This is a near-duplicate with softer urgency, and the 10/07 "final hours for the good stuff." send ran the same last-call play. Below the hero, the $5.95 mists module (the 10/09 offer), the $15.95 3-wick candle and a "Fall sale up to 75% off" strip all compete for attention. That dilutes the "almost over" message.
- Verdict: a solid send in isolation, but it's the second ask for the same deal in one day. The fine print is heavy: the offer needs a code and has a per-customer limit.

## What's working

- The $3.50 price is the largest element and sits above the fold, so the offer is instantly readable.
- The "Final hours" tag in the hero adds credible urgency.
- The in-store barcode and the "Online, use code MERRYMOOD" line make redemption clear for both channels.
- The oven and plug-in photo is on-theme and has a distinct look.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The same $3.50 offer went out earlier today, so this reads as a repeat rather than a new reason to open.
- Three secondary modules and the fall-sale strip after the hero scatter attention and soften the closing-window message.
- The CTA is a generic "Shop now" that doesn't say what you're shopping for.
- The code, the 24-per-customer cap and a very long block of fine print add friction.
- The "You might like" row is small and low-contrast, and the Rewards prompt at the top is a join pitch, not a member benefit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the second same-day $3.50 send, or give it a genuinely new angle: a countdown, or a "last chance, ends at 11:59 PM ET" line. A near-duplicate hurts cadence more than it helps conversion.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero plus one secondary offer. Move the fall-sale strip and the feel-good-formula block out of a last-call email.
- 3. Change the CTA to "Shop $3.50 refills" and pre-apply the code through the link, so shoppers don't have to enter it.
- 4. Show the stock-up cap ("up to 24") as a bold benefit near the CTA, not in the small print.
- 5. Put a visible time ("ends 11:59 PM ET") in the hero instead of only "Final hours".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud infrastructure (`cl.S7.exct.net`, `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) with a dark-mode-aware, responsive structure. The confirmed problems are missing image alt text, one non-HTTPS image, and a font `@import` that looks invalid. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and HTTP status are unverified.
- **Insecure image:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMjV9...` is loaded over `http://`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop it. It is a dynamic-content image, so the module it feeds could fail to render.
- **Tracker image:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=101026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_350WFSBEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V4` is loaded as an `<img>` from a third-party domain. It has no alt text, and the domain differs from the sending and image domains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images):** the QA run flagged the following.
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`
  
  With images off, or in a screen reader, these are blank or read as the filename. If any of them carry the offer or a CTA, that content is lost. Beyond these six, the `everestengagement` tracker above is also flagged.
- **Font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that, as far as I know, isn't on Google Fonts. It likely adds a failing request with no benefit. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers the actual rendering.
- **Doctype/charset:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with HTML5 `<meta charset>`, and `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `charset`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Dark mode:** the `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]` overrides are present. No defect is evident in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest of the markup is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are missing from the captured headers (WARN). The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. Treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender defect, though one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) can't be verified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are outside the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only campaign identifier visible is the proprietary `c=101026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_350WFSBEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V4` parameter on the Everest tracker. No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source.
- The 50 links are wrapped redirects, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment with the "$3.50 Wallflowers" subject can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all six flagged images and the Everest tracker. Use `alt=""` for decorative images or tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `LiveContent.aspx` dynamic-content image over `https://`, or confirm the ESP rewrites it at send time.
3. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" from Google Fonts and rely on the system font stack.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message at the origin MTA, not the relay.
5. Check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from the raw headers.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually to confirm the final URLs, UTM tagging, and that they land on the Wallflowers offer.
7. Remove the duplicate charset declaration, and move the doctype to HTML5 if legacy-client constraints allow.
