# 🚨🚨🚨 EVERYTHING* is buy 3 get 3 free starting right now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Buy 3 Get 3 Free storewide event teased in this morning's "⏰ set your alarms" send (2026-08-28, 7.00/10) — now flipped from "starting tomorrow" to "starting right now," with the subject line escalated from a clock emoji to a triple siren 🚨🚨🚨. The creative itself is clean and on-brand: a big "Fall Scent Event" hero, an unmissable orange "ENTIRE STORE Buy 3 get 3 FREE" block with four clear category CTAs, and a tidy scroll of product callouts (Preserve Collection, Blend Bar, Santal Ridge) that reinforces the fall theme without overwhelming.
- The offer is the strongest one this brand has run all week and the layout sells it well — big type, high contrast, no render bugs, repeated CTA lower in the email so late scrollers don't lose it. The problem isn't the design, it's the cadence: this is the second email today pushing the identical promo, and the subject leans on ALL-CAPS + triple siren emoji to manufacture urgency for an offer that was already announced hours ago. That reads as spammy rather than urgent, and after a week of near-daily $1.95/$3.75 promo emails, a reader who already opened this morning's teaser has little reason to open this one too.
- Judgement: strong creative, weak send strategy — this should have been the only Buy 3 Get 3 email today, not a same-day double-tap.

## What's working

- "ENTIRE STORE / Buy 3 get 3 FREE" hero is immediately legible with strong color contrast (white-on-orange) and sits right under the fold.
- Four category CTA buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) give an unambiguous, specific next click instead of one generic "Shop Now."
- Consistent fall-scent visual theme (diffusers, warm wood tones, amber product shots) ties hero to body copy.
- Offer is repeated as a secondary CTA block ("Starts Today! Buy 3 get 3 FREE") further down for scrollers who didn't convert at the top.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or lorem-ipsum — clean render top to bottom.

## What's weak

- This is a same-day repeat of this morning's "starting tomorrow" teaser — two emails for one promo in one day is fatigue-inducing, echoing the exact rerun pattern flagged on 2026-08-27 ("only hours left" reprising "today only").
- Subject line uses 🚨🚨🚨 plus all-caps "EVERYTHING*" — stacks two spam signals (excessive emoji, shouting caps) the rubric explicitly penalizes.
- The asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" has no visible fine-print callout anywhere in the render — exclusions aren't disclosed in the body, which reads as bait-and-switch.
- "Lowest-priced items are free" is buried as small gray subtext under the big orange banner — the actual mechanic (3-for-3, cheapest free) is easy to miss on a skim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress this send for anyone who already opened/clicked the "starting tomorrow" teaser this morning — don't double-fire the same offer same-day; if urgency is the goal, wait until the event is actually mid-flight (afternoon/evening) rather than re-announcing "right now" hours later.
- 2. Drop the triple siren emoji and all-caps "EVERYTHING" — one clear signal (🚨 or caps, not both) reads as confident, not desperate.
- 3. Surface the asterisk disclosure or at least a "some exclusions apply" link near the hero so the offer doesn't feel like a bait-and-switch.
- 4. Promote "Lowest-priced items are free" mechanic closer to the hero type size — that's the actual math shoppers need before clicking.
