# 40% off EVERYTHING* - Black Friday week starts now. 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong hero on top of a broken bottom half. The red "Black Friday week starts now / 40% off Entire store" block is loud and easy to read, and it sits over a product shelf. Free shipping at $50 and the Rewards line sit above it. A subscriber knows the offer within a second.
- The email falls apart below the fold. A 100-bonus-points section pushes a second offer. The three product tiles show no prices. Then there is a huge empty white void of roughly 500px before the footer. The footer shows only stray underlines with no visible link text. This is the second send today with the same 40% hero. This morning's "early Black Friday deals start TOMORROW. 🖤" used the same red "Black Friday week starts now" block. Reaching 40% off everything is a real escalation, but the creative didn't change.
- Judgement: a good offer and a clear top, but the render bugs and thin product merchandising hold it back. I'd call it a solid 6.

## What's working

- The 40% hero is huge, high-contrast and unambiguous. "Entire store" is stated plainly.
- The "3 days only" tag above the hero adds credible urgency.
- Four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) give clear routes in.
- The free-shipping bar and the Rewards line give a loyalty hook up front.
- The "100 bonus points" block has a clear "Join today & shop" button and a stated value ($18.95).

## What's weak

- The bottom third is a blank white void. The footer shows stray underline fragments with no readable link text, so it looks broken.
- The "EVERYTHING*" asterisk and the "Exclusions apply" fine print undercut the "Entire store" claim.
- The three product tiles show names only, with no prices or sale callouts. Nothing says what 40% off means in dollars.
- The "Our feel good formula" block is low-contrast, with pale text on a cream image. It is hard to read and adds nothing to the offer.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" marquee is clipped at both edges. Together with the "3 days only" and "2 days only" tags, the urgency messaging is cluttered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the render so the footer shows real link text and the blank gap is gone. This is the biggest quality lift.
- 2. Put sale prices on the product tiles, for example "$X.95 after 40% off". Link each tile to its product rather than to a generic landing page.
- 3. Pick one urgency frame. "3 days only" and "2 days only" together dilute each other. Make the bonus-points offer a clearly separate member perk.
- 4. Make the hero CTA a real button ("Shop 40% off") instead of relying on category chips alone.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters, and don't send two near-identical Black Friday emails in one day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off the whole store starts now 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday week: 40% off everything*`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candles, Wallflowers & body care, plus 100 bonus points for members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3 days only. Free shipping at $50, and rewards members score extra.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with dark-mode and Outlook (MSO) handling, but the QA run could not confirm unsubscribe headers or authentication. It also found 5 images without alt text. Only part of the HTML was available, so the footer, the link destinations and the body markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links unverified.** The QA probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the 46 destinations or redirect chains were checked. A broken or expired destination would not show up in this audit.
- **Tracking pixel flagged as an image.** The flagged `src` is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111826_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_AM_BAL_FULL_V1`. It looks like an open-tracking pixel, not a content image. It still needs `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` so clients don't show a broken-image icon or alt text.
- **Unneeded font `@import`.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't serve. Helvetica Neue is a system font. The `@import` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients, and it adds a third-party request that can't succeed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** All are hosted at `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  With images blocked, or with a screen reader, these render as nothing or as the filename. If any are linked or carry offer content, that content is lost.
- **Markup structure.** In the visible source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags directly, with no `</head><body>` seen. This is probably a Salesforce Marketing Cloud wrapper or an artifact of truncation, and it can't be confirmed from the excerpt. If the table really sits in `<head>`, parsers will repair it inconsistently.
- **Redundant MSO blocks.** There are two identical `OfficeDocumentSettings` blocks, one under `<!--[if mso]>` and one under `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`. This is harmless but redundant.
- No issues found with `lang="en"`, the viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the `color-scheme` meta and dark-mode selectors (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the available source or the subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers. This is **unconfirmed**: it could be a capture limitation, not a sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the same relay limitation. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and the image/tracking hosts `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and `bbwus.everestengagement.com` are different domains. Check SPF/DKIM alignment against the original headers.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated part of the HTML, so neither was confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No landing-page URLs available.** The 46 links are click-redirects and were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked.
- **Campaign identifier visible in the tracking pixel only.** The `c=` value `111826_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_AM_BAL_FULL_V1` is the campaign code. It shows the send is tracked, but not that the destination URLs carry matching `utm_*` values.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images listed above, and `alt=""` to the `everestengagement.com` pixel.
2. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the existing `font-family` fallback stack.
3. Pull the raw `.eml` from the receiving mailbox. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and alignment. Add the same check to the QA tooling, because the relay hides them.
4. Run the audit against the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address, and whether the table really sits in `<head>`.
5. Resolve a sample of the 46 redirect links, following redirects with a browser user-agent. Check final status codes and `utm_*` parameters, and whether the landing page matches the 40% off offer.
6. Remove the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` block to reduce markup.
