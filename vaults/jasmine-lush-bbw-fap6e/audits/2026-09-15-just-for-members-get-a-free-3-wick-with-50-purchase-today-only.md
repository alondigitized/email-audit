# ✨just for members✨ get a FREE 3-wick with $50 purchase, today only.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Member Days" sale (Sept 14-16) email machine finally putting the free-candle offer front and center in the subject line, and the hero delivers on it: "FREE 3-Wick Candle with $50 purchase" in a big orange block, right under the fold, with a clear "Join today & shop" button. Compared to the 09-14 kickoff send that buried this exact offer under free shipping, buy-3-get-3, 3x points, and a tote, this version leads with it — that's a real improvement in hierarchy.
- But it's still the same kitchen-sink template underneath. After the candle hero, the email piles on: a decades-anniversary body-care block, buy-3-get-3, 3x rewards points, a $12.95 tote, a sweepstakes, and a "we think you'll love" product grid — six distinct offers competing for the same click. Anyone who read yesterday's 09-14 send has already seen four of these five blocks verbatim.
- Net: strongest single-offer hero of this Member Days run so far, dragged down by an email that's really five emails stapled together with growing promo fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero clearly states "FREE 3-Wick Candle with $50 purchase" with product shots, a limit callout ("Limit 1"), and a single CTA — no click-to-reveal games like the 09-13 flash sale.
- "Today only" urgency is visible both above the fold and in the hero banner.
- Product photography is clean and on-brand (fall candles, then bright body-care fruit imagery).
- Visual color-blocking (orange → purple → magenta → navy) gives each section a distinct identity, so scrolling doesn't feel monotonous even with six offers stacked.

## What's weak

- Six competing offers (candle, body care BOGO, 3x points, tote, sweepstakes, product grid) dilute the one genuinely good hero — there's no obvious "if you do one thing, do this."
- This is the fifth Member Days-adjacent send in four days reusing nearly identical blocks (buy-3-get-3, 3x points, $12.95 tote all appeared in the 09-14 email) — heavy repetition for anyone who opened that one.
- The candle hero doesn't show a fall scent name or new-collection framing the way the 09-13 "$11.95 all 3-wicks" closer did — it's a generic candle, not tied to the "Falling Leaves & Cashmere" / "Pumpkin Pecan Waffles" products shown right above it.
- No loyalty-tier distinction shown — "member exclusive" is repeated but there's no visible sign-in state or personalized point balance to make it feel individually earned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections: the free-candle hero and one secondary offer (BOGO body care), and move points/tote/sweepstakes to a follow-up send — stacking six CTAs is costing clicks, not adding them.
- 2. Tie the hero candle to the two products already pictured above it (Falling Leaves & Cashmere, Pumpkin Pecan Waffles) so the free candle feels like a real product choice, not a generic freebie.
- 3. Since this is the fifth similar send this week, vary the offer mechanic next time (e.g., a % off instead of free-with-purchase) to counter fatigue rather than reshuffling the same blocks.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or explicit end time next to "today only" — right now urgency is a label, not a deadline.
