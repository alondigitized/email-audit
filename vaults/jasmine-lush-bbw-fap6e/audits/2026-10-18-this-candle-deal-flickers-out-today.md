# this candle deal flickers out today…🕯️✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- The first screen is strong. An "Ends today" bar sits over a pink-lit stack of holiday candles with the headline "Cozy now. Merry later." and "Buy 1 get 1 free." A "Shop now" button and the qualifier "Lowest-priced item is free" sit beside the offer. It repeats the BOGO candle offer from the 10/15 "joy to early deals" send, but "Ends today" adds credible urgency.
- Below the hero the email gets weaker. A second hero, "Make a grand entrance" over a Midnight Rouge bottle, has no visible button or offer, so it works as a brand tile and competes with the BOGO message. Then a large blank gap, a small "Fall sale 75% off" strip left over from the 10/13 send, a three-product row with no prices, and a footer area that looks partly blank. The email reads as two campaigns stacked together.
- Overall it is a solid single-offer top with a messy bottom. The first 20% of the email does the work, and the rest dilutes it.

## What's working

- "Ends today" banner above the hero gives clear urgency.
- The hero is clean: candle imagery, "Buy 1 get 1 free candles" and a visible "Shop now" button.
- "Lowest-priced item is free" states the BOGO terms plainly.
- The rewards line at the very top ("Join Rewards for free treats and more") adds a loyalty hook without crowding the hero.
- The seasonal imagery (holiday candle scents) fits mid-October.

## What's weak

- The "Make a grand entrance" fragrance block has no offer, price or button, so it splits attention from the BOGO.
- A large blank gap sits between that block and the "Fall sale 75% off" strip, and more blank space sits near the footer, which looks like broken or unloaded content.
- The "Fall sale 75% off Select items" strip is tiny and vague, with an underlined "SHOP" link, and it conflicts with the BOGO offer.
- The "You might like" products (Pink Kiwi Berry, Mahogany Teakwood Intense, Warm Vanilla Sugar) show no prices or sale callouts. Only one is a candle, so they don't support the headline offer.
- The "Our feel good formula" block has low-contrast white text on a pale background, which is hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the candle story. Remove the Midnight Rouge block and the stray 75% strip, or give the strip real copy and a deadline. One offer, one CTA.
- 2. Make the "You might like" row candle-only, with BOGO-eligible prices and a "Buy 1 get 1" tag on each.
- 3. Fix the blank gaps so sections flow without dead space.
- 4. Make the "Our feel good formula" text dark or move it onto a solid background.
- 5. Add a second "Shop candles" CTA after the product row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses an ESP-generated (Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style) template with click-tracked links on `bbwus.everestengagement.com` and images on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Confirmed issues are 5 images with no `alt` attribute, plus markup problems in the `<head>`. Header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **45 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified. No link-level breakage is confirmed.
- **Per-recipient identifier in the tracking URL.** Example: `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=101826_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B1G1ALLCDL_AM_BAL_G6M_…`. This is a standard ESP pattern, so I am noting it rather than calling it a defect.
- **Likely dead font request.** The CSS includes `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. Helvetica Neue is not, to my knowledge, a Google Fonts family, so the request likely fails or returns nothing. It adds an external request and is stripped by Gmail and Outlook anyway. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers rendering.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images (QA-confirmed):**

  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`

- **Sixth image with a tracking-URL source.** It is on `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` and is also missing alt. It is likely the open-tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""`.
