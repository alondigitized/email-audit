# today only—hand sanitizers starting at $1. 🤲
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- B&BW pivots cleanly from the $12.95 candle arc into a fresh "today only" hand sanitizer deal — "Fresh Sanis for Summer" is a seasonally appropriate, timely hook and "TODAY ONLY!" as a top-of-email banner creates real decision pressure. The lead offer is right: a PocketBac price drop is high-frequency-purchase territory for the core B&BW subscriber, and the "today only" window is credible.
- But the email has a structural trust problem: the subject says "starting at $1" while the hero price badge clearly reads $2 for PocketBacs. If the CLEANUP promo code in the barcode block is what unlocks the $1 price, the email never makes that connection explicit — the subscriber sees $2, sees a barcode, and has to guess that the code produces a further discount. That's a failure of mechanics explanation that will cost conversions and may feel like bait-and-switch to anyone who pays attention.
- The second problem is fatigue. The $12.95 candles block appears here on Day 3 of a three-day arc (May 28, May 29, May 30). That creative brief is spent. It's visual noise sitting directly below the hero offer, diluting the urgency of a legitimately new deal.

## What's working

- "TODAY ONLY!" banner anchors the top; the single-day urgency framing is the strongest structural element.
- "Fresh Sanis for Summer" is clean, seasonal, and distinct from the three prior candle/body-care subjects.
- Price badges ($2 PocketBacs, $4.95 sprays) are legible and fast to parse in the hero.
- The coupon code CLEANUP with purchase limit signals membership value — the mechanics are there, just not explained.
- Email renders fully clean: no broken images, no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- Subject says "starting at $1," hero badge reads $2 — the gap is unexplained and reads as bait.
- No SHOP button on the lead hand sanitizer offer — the hero dead-ends at a coupon barcode with no direct click path.
- The $12.95 candle block is now a third appearance (May 28 launch, May 29 reminder, today) — it's invisible wallpaper at this point.
- Three stacked deals (sanis, candles, BOGO body care) dilute the single "today only" urgency into a menu.
- "SHOP" button copy on every secondary CTA is generic; none tie back to the lead offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Explain the $1 mechanic or fix the price** — If CLEANUP takes PocketBacs from $2 to $1, say it: "Use code CLEANUP and PocketBacs drop to $1." If $1 is a separate SKU (travel size, single-unit), show it in the hero. Right now this reads like a subject line overpromise, and that erodes trust with the segment most likely to notice.
- 2. **Add a direct SHOP button to the hand sani hero** — Every secondary offer has a button; the lead offer should too. The barcode alone is not a CTA.
- 3. **Cut the $12.95 candle block entirely** — Day 3 of the same creative with the same price adds nothing. Replace with a sani-specific product grid (top 6 PocketBac scents, price-badged) that earns the scroll.
- 4. **Consolidate to two offers** — Hand sanis (hero, today only) + BOGO body care (secondary, fresh). The candle space should be used for product depth, not a retreaded arc.
- 5. **Make the coupon mechanic front and center** — "Enter CLEANUP at checkout" should appear in bold copy directly above the barcode, not buried in fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `today only: use CLEANUP and PocketBacs drop to $1 🤲`
- **Subject Alt B:** `fresh sanis from $1—today only, while they last`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `PocketBacs at $1 with code CLEANUP + sprays at $4.95. Today only, limit 25 per member.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up for summer: hand sanitizers from $1 today only. Plus BOGO on full-size body care.`
