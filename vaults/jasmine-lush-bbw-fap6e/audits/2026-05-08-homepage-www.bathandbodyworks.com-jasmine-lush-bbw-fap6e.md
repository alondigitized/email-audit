# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally cannot shop right now — there's a "Press & Hold" bot challenge sitting on top of the entire homepage, blocking me from seeing anything below the nav. What I *can* see is a really pretty Vera Bradley collab hero (pink pump bottle, daisies, very cottagecore), a top banner calling out "Gifts for Her" and free shipping over $50, and... that's it. The brand aesthetic is totally my vibe, but the experience is currently broken.

## What's working

- **"Gifts for Her, Deals for You + Free ship w/ $50" banner** — the "Gifts for Her" framing is *exactly* how I think when I open this app. I'm always shopping to give someone a wallflower set or a shower gel trio.
- **Vera Bradley collab hero** — the daisy imagery + pink pump bottle is gorgeous, very Pinterest-core, and I'd 100% tap to see what the collab looks like. Vera Bradley + BBW is a smart pairing for my demographic.
- **Navigation bar** — hamburger menu, search, account, and bag are all visible and exactly where I expect them on mobile. No friction finding my way around (once the overlay goes away).
- **Spring/gifting season alignment** — "Gifts for Her" + daisies in May is on-calendar. Doesn't feel stale.

## What's weak

- **Bot verification overlay** — this is the biggest fail. I tapped the link, and the first thing I see is "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human." That is not the vibe. That kills momentum immediately, especially on mobile where I'm just casually scrolling.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — I'm a Rewards member. I should see something that acknowledges that (points balance, member offer) the moment I land, not buried somewhere below.
- **No CTA visible** — whatever button is on that Vera Bradley hero is completely hidden. I don't know if it says "Shop Now," "Shop the Collab," or nothing at all.
- **No urgency or time-bound offer** — "Free ship w/ $50" is useful but passive. There's no "ends Sunday" or "only 3 left" to give me a reason to act now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- **Move bot verification to a lighter touch** — use a passive Cloudflare/invisible challenge instead of an interruptive "Press & Hold" modal. This thing would make me close the tab. A friction gate on the homepage is a conversion killer.
- **Surface the Rewards welcome back message** — replace the generic account icon with a personalized chip ("Hi Jasmine · 420 pts") for logged-in members. I'd feel seen immediately.
- **Add a deadline to the shipping offer** — change "Free ship w/ $50" to "Free ship w/ $50 · Offer ends May 11" to create urgency without being manipulative.
- **Make the Vera Bradley CTA button visible and verbatim in the banner** — even if I can't see the full hero, a strip that says "Shop the Vera Bradley × BBW Collab →" would let me tap through without scrolling past the bot wall.
