# tomorrow’s about to be BIG for your routine. 🤫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser for tomorrow's Doorbuster: select body care at $5.95. The offer is concrete and the hero is big, with the price in huge magenta type under "Starts tomorrow." It lands on the same day as the Holiday Traditions Doorbuster module, which says "Starts tomorrow | Online exclusive." So the email is selling a deal you can't use yet.
- The subject, "tomorrow's about to be BIG for your routine," is vague and doesn't name a price or product. The body is a different story, because it leads with $5.95. After the 11/09 candle BOGO and the 11/10 "final hours" $2.95 minis sends, this is another high-frequency promo in a short window. Subscribers have been trained to expect a deadline, and "starts tomorrow" gives them nothing to act on today.
- The render also looks broken. A large blank gap sits between the Doorbuster module and the three product tiles. A second big blank area sits below the "Find a store" bar, with orphaned underlined link fragments floating in white space. The "Our feel good formula" text is also nearly illegible white on a pale cream photo. Overall it's a decent teaser with sloppy execution. I'd call it middling.

## What's working

- The hero price, "$5.95 select body care," is huge and immediately readable.
- "Starts tomorrow" makes the timing clear and gives a reason to come back.
- Rewards and free shipping over $50 sit in the top bars, so member value is visible.
- The Doorbuster band and the Holiday Traditions collage feel seasonal and festive.
- "Up to 24 per customer" sets expectations on limits.

## What's weak

- The offer isn't live, and nothing says what to do today. "Start your list" is a weak, ambiguous CTA.
- A large blank gap sits between the Doorbuster module and the product tiles.
- A huge empty area below the footer bar holds stray underlined link fragments, so it looks like missing content.
- The "Our feel good formula" copy is white on cream and almost unreadable.
- Three near-identical hero treatments repeat the same $5.95 message, and the dermatologist-approved claim shows up twice.
- The product tiles are tiny and use generic labels like "Travel Size Fine Fragrance Mist" with no price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the rendering first. Remove the blank gaps, fix the broken footer links, and make the "feel good formula" text legible. That's the biggest lift to click and trust.
- 2. Make the CTA specific and set a reminder. Replace "Start your list" with something like "Preview the $5.95 deals" or "Remind me tomorrow." Add a sign-up-for-texts prompt higher up.
- 3. Cut the duplicated hero and dermatologist modules. Keep one price-led hero and one product row.
- 4. Put prices and names on the product tiles, and link each tile to its product.
- 5. Put the price in the subject, since the email already leads with $5.95.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 content images** (all on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`):
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`

- **Malformed document structure.** After the `<meta>` tags, the source goes straight into `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` with no `</head>` or `<body>` visible. Table markup inside `<head>` can trigger quirks-mode parsing in some clients. The source is truncated, so confirm against the full HTML.
- **Doctype and meta.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the markup uses `<meta charset="utf-8">`. It also duplicates the Content-Type declaration (`http-equiv` plus `charset`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Redundant MSO blocks.** Two identical `OfficeDocumentSettings` blocks are present (`<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`).
- **Web font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't host. The import has no effect. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and other major clients. The fallback stack `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` handles rendering correctly.
- **Dark-mode hiding.** `.darkmode-dark` and `.darkmode-light` swap via `display:none` plus `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`. This is a standard pattern, and no defect was found in the visible CSS.
