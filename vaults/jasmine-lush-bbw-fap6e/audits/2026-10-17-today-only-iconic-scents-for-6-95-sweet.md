# today only - iconic scents for $6.95? sweet. 🤎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- The hero does its job. A "Today only" banner sits over a golden "In the Stars" body mist, with "Our fragrance icons" and a big **$6.95** for select full-size body care, up to 24 per customer, code WARM. The "Shop now" button is visible and the barcode tile reinforces the code. The offer is concrete and the urgency is credible.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a pile of competing promos: BOGO candles, a Platinum mist with no offer, Weekly Wonders, a 75% off fall sale, and an Ocean product row. It runs against the single-offer discipline of the Oct 15 "candles are buy 1 get 1 FREE" send. Sending this one day after the two "$5.95 scents for him" emails on Oct 16 also makes the cadence feel heavy. There are also large blank gaps in the lower third, which look like render breakage.
- Verdict: a strong top fold weighed down by clutter, and the stack of near-daily promos weakens it. It's a solid mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The hero pairs a big **$6.95** price with a clear "Today only" tag and a visible "Shop now" button.
- The WARM code is shown twice (in the hero and under the barcode), so it's easy to find.
- "Up to 24 per customer" gives the offer a concrete, believable limit.
- The BOGO candle block carries an honest "Lowest-priced item is free" qualifier, and the season-appropriate "Cozy now. Merry later." copy fits the date.
- The footer shows a loyalty prompt ("Become a Rewards member") and a "Find a store" bar.

## What's weak

- At least five competing offers dilute the $6.95 message. The subject promises one deal and the body gives six.
- The Platinum block has no price, no CTA text and no offer, so it reads as a large, empty brand ad.
- The lower third of the email has large empty white gaps with stray lines, which look like missing or broken content.
- The "Select full-size body care" copy doesn't say which products qualify, so the $6.95 deal feels vague.
- The 75% off fall sale strip is small and buried, and it contradicts the premium "iconic scents" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the $6.95 hero, one secondary offer (BOGO candles) and the product row. Drop Platinum and Weekly Wonders, or save them for a later send.
- 2. Name the qualifying products in the hero (for example "Fine Fragrance Mist, Body Cream, Shower Gel") and link each tile to its product.
- 3. Fix or remove the blank gaps at the bottom so the footer reads as finished.
- 4. Space out the daily promos. A quieter day after the Oct 16 "$5.95 scents for him" pair would help.
- 5. Make the Ocean row "You might like" a "Bestsellers at $6.95" row with prices and star ratings, so it ties back to the hero offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-built template. It has dark-mode and mobile CSS, but 6 images have no alt text and one image loads over `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, body markup and CTA hrefs weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links weren't probed.** 52 links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so none of their destinations were validated. Examples are `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=…&c=101726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_695ICONS_AM_BAL-NTF-OPT` and `cl.S7.exct.net`. The QA reports 0 broken links, but that only means nothing was tested.
- **Insecure image/tracking source.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=…` is an `<img>` served over HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS, or trigger mixed-content warnings. If it is a dynamic-content block, that content may fail to render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (all on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`):
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`, which also has no alt text.
  
  These images will be blank or unlabeled for screen readers and with images off. If they carry the offer content, such as the `$6.95` price, that content is lost.
- **Image labelled with its URL.** The `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` image has its own URL as its accessible name. That is probably a tracking pixel or a linked image with no real alt text. It should have `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text if it is a CTA.
- **Pointless font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family I don't believe Google Fonts hosts. That would add a failing network request and gain nothing. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so the stack falls back to `Helvetica, Arial` either way.
- **Possible head/body structure problem.** In the captured source, a `<table>` follows the `<meta>` tags with no visible `</head>` or `<body>`. This may be a truncation or sanitization artifact, so I haven't confirmed it.
- **Dark-mode CSS in the head.** The `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are present and consistent. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** weren't found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them. Treat these as unverified rather than confirmed defects. If the original headers lack them, the email would not meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058).
- **Authentication-Results** wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain. Alignment couldn't be checked.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address** are not visible in the truncated source and need to be checked in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTMs visible on tracked links.** Campaign attribution sits in an opaque redirect parameter, `c=101726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_695ICONS_AM_BAL-NTF-OPT`, so the click path goes through the redirector first. The final URL and any UTM parameters couldn't be checked because redirects weren't followed.
- **Landing page alignment is unverified.** Nothing confirms that the "$6.95 iconic scents" offer matches the destination page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones and tracking pixels. Give `LiveContent.aspx` a fallback alt as well.
2. Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS, or replace the `http://cl.S7.exct.net` source with its HTTPS equivalent.
3. Remove the `@import` for `Helvetica Neue`. The `font-family` fallback stack is enough.
4. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml`, or follow the redirects with a real GET. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the destination URLs and the UTMs.
