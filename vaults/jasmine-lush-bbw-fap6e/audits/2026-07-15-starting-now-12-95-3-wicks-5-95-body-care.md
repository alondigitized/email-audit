# starting now: $12.95 3-wicks + $5.95 body care. 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the delivery on yesterday's teaser — B&BW promised "a big deal starting tomorrow" and the subject line delivers the reveal cleanly: **$12.95 three-wicks, $5.95 body care, Fall Favorites Event, two days only**. As a launch email it does its job. The hero is on-brand, the "2 DAYS ONLY ONLINE ONLY" badge creates legitimate urgency, and the Vampire Blood seasonal creative signals this is a real seasonal moment, not a recycled promo.
- The problem is the same one that plagued the July 14 $7.99 send: B&BW can't resist the kitchen sink. Below the hero sit a Wallflowers deal, a "Picked just for you" carousel, a Summer Sale 75%-off banner, and a TOP OFFERS grid — five separate value propositions fighting for attention. The 75% off Summer Sale is buried three sections down and is objectively the most dramatic number in the email, but the subscriber has to scroll past two other offers to find it.
- The Wallflowers "campus" copy (your home is "the campus's newest attraction") is a back-to-school crossover angle that's mildly clever but feels tacked on. Net result: a strong launch email that dilutes itself with too many parallel narratives.

## What's working

- **Hero executes the teaser's promise perfectly** — subject and creative match exactly what was previewed, which rewards subscribers who opened yesterday's tease.
- **"2 DAYS ONLY ONLINE ONLY" badge** is immediately visible in the hero, creating hard time-bounded urgency without feeling manufactured.
- **Two concrete prices in the subject** ($12.95 and $5.95) pre-qualify the offer before the open — subscribers know exactly what they're walking into.
- **Vampire Blood candle creative** anchors the Fall transition visually; it's seasonal and distinctive, not generic leaf-pattern filler.
- **"Picked just for you"** carousel adds a personalization layer mid-email that breaks up the product grid monotony.

## What's weak

- **Summer Sale 75% off is buried** — the most aggressive discount in the email is the third section down, after the Wallflowers pitch. That number should be in the subject or at minimum in the hero.
- **Wallflowers "campus" framing is confusing** — mixing back-to-school language into a Fall Favorites Event email muddies the campaign's seasonal identity.
- **Five competing promo sections** kill focus; the Fall Favorites hero loses authority by the time you've scrolled past Wallflowers, carousel, Summer Sale, and TOP OFFERS.
- **"SHOP" CTA copy is generic throughout** — every button says SHOP, not "Shop 3-Wicks" or "Shop $5.95 Body Care," which leaves specificity on the table.
- **Second email on the same day** (following the morning teaser) accelerates list fatigue — this is the fourth B&BW email in two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the biggest number** — If 75% off is live, it belongs in the subject and the hero alongside the $12.95/$5.95. "Starting now: $5.95 body care, $12.95 candles + up to 75% off" is a materially stronger subject.
- 2. **Collapse to two sections max**: Fall Favorites hero + one secondary module (either Wallflowers or Summer Sale, not both). Cut the "Picked for you" to a footer or drop it.
- 3. **Specificity-up the CTAs**: Replace all generic SHOP buttons with "Shop 3-Wicks," "Shop $5.95 Body Care," "Shop Sale" — one verb, one noun per button.
- 4. **Cut the campus copy from Wallflowers** — the back-to-school angle doesn't earn its space inside a Fall Favorites send. Use B&BW's standard cozy-home framing.
- 5. **Delay the second send** — the teaser did its job; sending the launch email the same calendar day as the teaser burns the anticipation built overnight.

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works (2026-07-15)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional structure with Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript/ExactTarget) as the ESP. Several merge tokens failed to populate, two images have empty `src` attributes, and compliance headers are unverifiable through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 click-redirect links unprobed** — all route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. The QA harness skipped HTTP probing on these, so broken destination detection is absent for this send.

**Open pixel has empty alt** — `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?K7EOPYOMKL7EHFBI4JIZJDDV3I.70253&d=70253&bmt=0` uses `alt=""` on the 1×1 tracking pixel. Acceptable for tracking pixels, but worth noting it is rendered outside the `<body>` tag (see §3).

**Two images have empty `src` attributes** — QA flagged two images with `src: ` (blank). These will generate a 404 or browser-default broken-image request. Both also lack `alt` text, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed document structure** — content appears before the `<body>` tag. The preheader `<table>` (containing the hidden preheader `<div>`) and the tracking pixel `<table>` are placed in the `<head>` section before `<body>` is opened. This is invalid in HTML 4.01 and may cause Outlook/Gmail to enter quirks parsing.

**`<meta charset>` misplaced** — `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` is the last element in `<head>`, after `<style>` blocks. Per HTML spec it should precede all other elements so the parser knows the charset before encountering encoded content.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import`** — Source Sans Pro is imported twice: once in `<head>` (`wght@400`) and once inline in the body block (`wght@400;700`). Gmail strips `<style>` blocks in both locations anyway, so this is harmless but indicates a copy-paste artifact.

**5 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed):
- `5339ba5d83be23b3.png` — tracking image at `mi.bathandbodyworks.com` with unpopulated `mi_u=` token
- `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` — content image with no alt
- Two images with empty `src` — no alt, no src
- Everest Engagement image at `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/` — no alt

**Legacy doctype** — `HTML 4.01 Transitional` triggers quirks mode in some clients. No functional breakage, but it is an indicator of an aging template that may accumulate rendering debt.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=` not populated** — the Movable Ink tracking URL contains an empty subscriber token:
```
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/5339ba5d83be23b3.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=071526_...
```
`mi_u` is the per-recipient identifier for Movable Ink personalization. If this was supposed to carry a subscriber ID, personalized content rendering will fail silently (falls back to default image).

**Everest Engagement `e=` token empty** — the engagement tracking image URL:
```
https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=071526_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W595BC_AM_NTH_FULL_V2
```
`e=` is empty, which typically means the subscriber email/ID merge failed. Attribution data for this open will be unresolvable.

**Developer/operational comments left in rendered HTML** — the following are visible in the delivered source and should not be in production output:
- `<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->` (appears twice — looks like a shell substitution that was never evaluated)
- `<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->`
- `<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->`
- `<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->`
- `<!--Set variables to support dynamic template attributes-->`

These comments expose internal logic (APT = Audience Profile Targeting, NTF = New-to-File flag) and suggest the AMPscript rendering block is a stub that either didn't execute or was commented out rather than suppressed.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN) — Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the unsubscribe button in the UI. Its absence degrades the one-click unsubscribe experience and increases spam report risk. Likely stripped or not forwarded by the AgentMail relay, but cannot confirm the header was present at delivery.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` absent** (QA WARN) — RFC 8058 one-click machine unsubscribe is not surfaced. Gmail enforces this for bulk senders (>5k/day). Absence may flag this sender with Gmail's bulk sender requirements enforcement.

**`Authentication-Results` absent** (QA WARN) — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. The sending domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` (ExactTarget/SFMC subdomain). Cannot assess alignment without the relay forwarding the header.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM passthrough unverifiable** — all 24 destination links are wrapped in `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects that the QA harness did not probe. UTM parameter presence on landing pages cannot be confirmed.

**Campaign code format** — the `mi_ecmp` and `c=` parameters use an internal BBW format (`071526_EM_SU_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W595BC_AM_NTH`) rather than standard `utm_campaign`. If the site analytics stack expects UTM, these parameters will not be attributed correctly unless the redirect layer translates them.
