# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so BBW is clearly in full Mother's Day mode and I get it — gift season is my season — but this homepage is giving me an almost empty loading screen energy. The hero is so dark I genuinely cannot tell what the product or scene is supposed to be, and with zero offer copy, zero loyalty hook, and a "Shop now" button that tells me absolutely nothing about what I'm shopping for, this feels like a placeholder that accidentally went live. The brand is pushing gifting for Mom, which tracks for early May, but it's executing it in the laziest possible way.

## What's working

- **"Feel-Good Gifts. For Her." headline** — short, warm, emotionally pitched at exactly the occasion I'm shopping for right now. It's simple but it works for my gifting mindset.
- **Mother's Day subtext copy** — "Mom's given you so much. Return the favor with gifts made just for her." is actually kind of sweet and specific enough to feel intentional rather than filler.
- **Clean nav bar** — logo centered, account icon and cart visible, search icon accessible. The bones of the mobile navigation are solid and I can find the hamburger easily.
- **Chat bubble bottom right** — I actually like having that accessible if I want to ask about a scent or what's in a set. Feels like a nice touch for someone like me who uses the app.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is completely invisible** — I cannot tell what product, model, or lifestyle scene is supposed to be here. It's a gray-black void. If this is a render failure, it's a major one. If it's an intentional dark overlay, whoever approved this made a bad call.
- **Zero offer or deal above fold** — I'm a Semi-Annual Sale stockpiler. A coupon hunter. I opened this app half-expecting a members offer or a promo and there is nothing. Not even "Free shipping on $35+." That's a huge miss.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook** — I have a My Bath & Body Works Rewards account. I want to see my points or a members-only deal right away. There's nothing here acknowledging I'm a returning customer.
- **"Shop now" CTA is too vague** — Shop what? Candles? Gift sets? Body care? Even "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" would give me way more confidence about what I'm tapping into.
- **Above-fold content is 80% dark empty space** — the entire upper two-thirds of the viewport is taken up by whatever this hero image is supposed to be. It's wasted real estate on a screen where pixels matter.

## Recommendations

- **Fix or replace the hero image immediately** — if it's broken, escalate to eng right now. If it's intentional, swap it for a bright, warm lifestyle shot of a Mom receiving a candle set or wallflower starter kit — something that actually shows product.
- **Add a promo strip above the hero** — a slim bar at the very top ("Members: 20% off sitewide" or "Free shipping on $40+") would immediately signal value before I even engage with the main content.
- **Make the CTA specific** — change "Shop now" to "Shop Mother's Day Gift Sets" so I know exactly where I'm going before I tap.
- **Surface the rewards account widget** — a "Hi Jasmine — you have 150 points!" nudge or "Members save an extra 10% this week" badge above the fold would make returning shoppers feel seen immediately.
