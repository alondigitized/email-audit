# 👀 don't miss out - $10.95 3-wicks end today.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the last-chance send in a five-day candle run. The "Ends today" bars sit on top of both heroes. The $10.95 3-wick price is huge and pink, so it's the first thing the eye lands on. The $5.95 single-wick offer sits right below it, and each hero has its own "Shop now" button. As a closer, the layout does what it should.
- The problem is cadence and novelty. After "one more sleep," "it's here," "can't-miss" and "still going strong" (10/29–10/31), this is the fifth candle email in about four days. The creative is the same pink hero with a new "Ends today" bar. The subject is also a generic "don't miss out," which is the weakest copy in the run. A subscriber who ignored the last four has no new reason to open this one.
- It's a solid, honest, well-built promo. It's also a fatigued one.

## What's working

- The "Ends today" bars above both heroes give the deadline credibility and make it impossible to miss.
- The $10.95 hero has a clear hierarchy: the oversized price is the focal point and "Up to 24 per customer" is a real scarcity cue.
- The $5.95 single-wick tier gives a cheaper entry point in the same scroll.
- The two heroes use different seasonal products (Snowflakes & Cashmere, the nutcracker and Vanilla Bean Noel) and the holiday imagery fits the date.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The creative is a near-repeat of the three prior sends, so there's no new product or angle.
- "You might like" shows hand soap, sanitizer and a Wallflowers refill. These are off-topic for a candle deadline and dilute the focus.
- The "Our feel good formula" block and the "More to love" links (Rewards, texts, pickup) sit below the fold and compete with the urgency.
- The fine print is dense, with different date windows for each offer, and the "excludes boxed candles" note is tiny.
- The top Rewards and free-shipping banners are small and pull attention away from the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the deadline in a countdown format, such as hours left or "ends at 11:59 PM ET", instead of the "Ends today" bar alone. After four days of the same hero, a real clock is the one fresh element left.
- 2. Cut the secondary content. Drop "You might like," the formula block and "More to love," or replace them with top-selling candle scents tied to the sale.
- 3. Add social proof to the hero, such as a "bestselling scent" tag or star ratings.
- 4. Suppress this send for people who already purchased or clicked in the run, and consider consolidating the run into fewer emails.

The 57% pass rate is mostly caused by header-capture gaps rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Tracking pixel:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=110126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_10953W_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` appears to be the open-tracking pixel. It is on a different domain (`everestengagement.com`) from the sender domain (`e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). That is normal for an ESP, but it is a separate third-party host.
- **Broken links:** No confirmed ones.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images):** The QA found no `alt` attribute on these images, all hosted at `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  With images blocked, these render as blank space, and screen readers will read out the filename or skip the image. Where an image carries the offer or a CTA, the message is lost. If an image is decorative, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Pixel without alt:** The `everestengagement.com` pixel is also flagged. This is low severity if it is a 1×1 pixel, but it should still have `alt=""`.
- **Markup in `<head>`:** In the captured source, the content `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and the MSO conditional comments appear straight after the `<meta>` tags. I see no `</head>` or `<body>` in between. This may be a truncation artifact or an ESP wrapper, so confirm it against the full source. If it is real, the table sits inside `<head>`, which is invalid. Parsers will usually hoist it into `<body>`, but results vary.
- **Doctype and charset:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with HTML5 `<meta charset="utf-8">` and a duplicate `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>`. Two charset declarations are redundant and harmless.
- **Font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't serve, so it does nothing. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and many other clients. The `Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallback stack means nothing visibly breaks, so remove the import as dead code.
- **Dark mode:** The `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are present. The `.darkmode-dark` image swap (70×70) is hidden by default and relies on `display:none` plus `!important` overrides. I can't verify rendering from source alone.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the captured HTML. I saw no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. The source is truncated, so the body and preheader were not fully checked.

- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't reach the footer, so I can't confirm them. The `.footer-disclaimer` styles suggest a footer exists.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Campaign code:** The pixel's `c=110126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_10953W_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` suggests a date (110126) and a promo tag. This is an internal ID, not a UTM.

2. Add `alt` text to the 5 flagged images. Use descriptive text for offer or CTA images, and `alt=""` for decorative images and the tracking pixel.
3. Follow the click-tracking redirects for a sample of the 42 links (or all of them) to check the final status codes, UTM parameters and offer match.
