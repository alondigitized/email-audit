# 🔔 fresh savings are in session...starting tomorrow.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer hero send: $2.95 hand soaps, "lowest price of the season," starting tomorrow. It's a step down from the tightly-built $3.95/$5.95/$13.95 tiered coupon email BBW ran on 2026-08-11 ("today only - fall steals") — that one had three price points and multiple categories; this one leans on a single SKU category (foaming hand soaps) with a "starts tomorrow" pre-announcement, which is a softer, less urgent hook.
- The hero image is genuinely well shot — a dewy green Woodland Evening soap bottle with a "Just dropped" badge and a clear $2.95 price — and the "TOP OFFERS" grid below gives good category breadth (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Soaps, Men's Shop, Home Care, Gifts) for browsers who don't care about hand soap specifically. But the core mechanic — an offer that isn't live until tomorrow — undercuts the "shop now" impulse this format usually wants to create.
- Overall a solid, on-brand, bug-free send, but it's a lower-intensity offer than BBW's recent BOGO and steep-discount candle/soap pushes, and the "starts tomorrow" framing works against immediate clicks.

## What's working

- Hero has one unambiguous, specific offer ($2.95, "lowest price of the season") with a clear product shot and price tag.
- "Just dropped" badge plus visible product label (Woodland Evening) gives the hero real specificity, not a generic stock photo.
- Free shipping banner ($50+) at the very top adds a second, low-friction incentive before the fold.
- Category grid (TOP OFFERS) gives multiple entry points beyond hand soap, useful for non-target shoppers.
- No render bugs — clean image, no overlapping text, no broken assets.

## What's weak

- "STARTS TOMORROW!" directly kills urgency-driven clicking — a reader opening today has no reason to act now, and no reminder/save-for-later mechanism is offered.
- Primary CTA "START YOUR LIST" is vague — it doesn't say "shop" or reference the $2.95 price, so it under-sells the hero offer it sits next to.
- Preview/subject promise "fresh savings," but the hero is a single-category soap deal, not the broad "head-to-toe" assortment implied — same subject/content mismatch pattern flagged in the 2026-08-12 "save on head-to-toe faves" audit.
- No urgency deadline given for how long the $2.95 price lasts once it starts (fine print only, not in the visual hero).

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or soften "STARTS TOMORROW" as the dominant headline — if pre-announcing, pair it with a way to act now (e.g., "set a reminder" or let people shop today at the price early for loyalty members). As-is, the biggest visual beat in the email tells the reader not to click yet.
- 2. Change the CTA from "START YOUR LIST" to something offer-specific like "SHOP $2.95 SOAPS" so the button and the price claim reinforce each other.
- 3. Add a clear end date/window for the $2.95 price near the hero (not buried in the footer legal text) to create real urgency once the sale opens.
- 4. Tighten subject/preview to match the single-category hero rather than implying a broad "head-to-toe" savings event.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$2.95 hand soaps — lowest price of the season, starts tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `🔔 tomorrow: $2.95 foaming hand soaps (lowest price yet)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up before the price goes back up — 6 AM ET tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Woodland Evening and more, all $2.95 for one day only.`

  - Two images with **empty `src`** and no `alt` — these will render as broken image icons in clients that don't suppress broken images, and are inaccessible to screen readers regardless.

- Mobile responsiveness handled via a single `@media (max-width:599px)` block adjusting padding only (`.mobile-body-padding`, `.mobile-lr-padding`, `.wrap`) — no fluid image or font-scaling rules visible in the truncated source, but nothing broken given what's shown.
- Preheader uses a hidden-text spacer block (repeated `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` sequences) to pad out unwanted preview text after the intended preheader ("start the school year off clean. 🙌") — functions as intended, no rendering defect.
- Custom web font loaded via `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` **twice** (once in `<head>`, once again inline in the body `stylingblock-content-wrapper`) — redundant `@import`, and `@import` is unsupported in most email clients (Gmail, Outlook) so this will silently fail to load in the majority of render environments; not a breaking issue since fallback fonts (Arial/Helvetica) are specified inline, but the `@import` itself is dead weight.
