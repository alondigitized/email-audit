# ⏳ final hours to score a free 3-wick with $50 purchase - members only.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the closer on the "Member Days" arc (kickoff 09-14, mid-flight 09-15 sends earlier today), and it's the strongest version yet of that offer: "FREE 3-Wick Candle with $50 purchase," in a big orange block, right at the top of the fold, with "Member exclusive" and "Limited 1" framing it clearly. Unlike the 09-13 "click to reveal" flash sale, there's zero mystery here — you see the candles, the price threshold, and the CTA in one glance.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the hero. The email keeps going for six more offer blocks (buy-3-get-3, 3x points, $12.95 tote, sweepstakes, "we think you'll love," feel-good formula) before finally hitting the footer, which is the same kitchen-sink problem the 09-14 kickoff had. The "Final hours" urgency in the subject also doesn't show up anywhere prominently in the visible hero — it's a tiny label above the hero image, easy to miss.
- Net: strong hero, bloated body, urgency undersold visually. This lands mid-pack — better than the reveal-gimmick send, not as tight as the 09-13 closer.

## What's working

- The hero clearly states the offer, the threshold, and the limit ("FREE 3-Wick Candle with $50 purchase," "Limit 1") — no digging required.
- Product shot shows two actual candle SKUs (Falling Leaves & Cashmere, Pumpkin Pecan Waffle), which reinforces the current fall assortment.
- "Join today & shop" CTA button is repeated consistently at every offer block, so wherever the eye lands there's an obvious next step.
- Clean color-blocked layout separates each offer visually (orange, gingham, purple, plum) so sections don't blur together.

## What's weak

- "Final hours" urgency is a tiny gray label above the hero, easy to scroll past — it doesn't match the urgency implied by the subject line.
- Six distinct offers stacked in one email (free candle, buy-3-get-3, 3x points, $12.95 tote, sweepstakes, plus cross-sell) dilutes the free-candle hero instead of closing hard on it.
- The lifestyle hero image (person in a field) doesn't feature the product at all, wasting prime visual real estate that could reinforce the candle offer.
- No visible countdown clock or explicit end time/date for "final hours" — the urgency is asserted, not shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 2-3 blocks max: hero free-candle offer, one secondary offer (buy-3-get-3 makes sense since it's also full-size body care), and the footer. The tote, sweepstakes, and 3x points blocks are diluting the closing message.
- 2. Add a real countdown or explicit deadline ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") directly in or under the hero, not just "Final hours" in small text above it.
- 3. Replace or crop the lifestyle photo so the candle product is visible in the hero itself, not three blocks down.
- 4. Move the "Limited 1" fine print into the CTA button area so it doesn't read as risk-hedging buried text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `⏳ tonight only: free 3-wick with your $50, members only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `last call — free candle ends at midnight (members)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Falling Leaves & Cashmere or Pumpkin Pecan Waffle — your pick, on us.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$50 in the cart unlocks a free 3-wick. Ends tonight.`
