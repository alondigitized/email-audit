# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is... not great. The entire homepage is blocked by a bot-verification wall — a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" interstitial with a big outline button, just sitting there over basically nothing. I can't see any products, promos, or anything Bath & Body Works-y. This is giving me "website is scared of me" energy, not "Semi-Annual Sale is coming, stock up" energy.

## What's working

- **Brand logo is partially visible** — at least I know I landed on Bath & Body Works and didn't get routed somewhere sketchy.
- **The "Press & Hold" button is visually clean** — not broken, not overlapping, renders fine as a UI element in isolation.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is invisible** — no hero, no promo, no products, no Semi-Annual Sale teaser, nothing. I came to shop and I'm being interrogated first.
- **No reassurance this is worth completing** — there's zero context like "big sale on the other side" to motivate me to bother pressing and holding. Just blank white space above and below the button.
- **Loyalty/rewards completely absent** — as a My Bath & Body Works Rewards member, I expect to at least see a hint of my account or points before I even get in.
- **Reference ID at the bottom (0cfc45e2-55d4-11f1-be38-57add95a2547)** — cold, clinical, error-page energy. Not a vibe for a brand built on warm scented candles.
- **The logo is cropped/cut off on the right** — "Bath & Body Works" bleeds off-screen, which looks broken even if it's technically just overflow.

## Recommendations

- **Move the bot check to a less intrusive pattern** — use a silent invisible reCAPTCHA or honeypot in the background instead of a full-screen interstitial that replaces the entire homepage experience.
- **If a gate is unavoidable, show something behind it** — blur the hero and promo banner underneath the modal so I can see there's a Semi-Annual Sale waiting for me; give me a reason to press and hold.
- **Ensure the logo renders fully on mobile** — the "Bath & Body Works" wordmark is clipping off-screen right; a simple `max-width: 100%` or viewport-constrained font size would fix it.
- **Pipe in the rewards balance or a "Welcome back" message** — if I'm a known loyalty member, personalize the interstitial so it at least acknowledges me.
