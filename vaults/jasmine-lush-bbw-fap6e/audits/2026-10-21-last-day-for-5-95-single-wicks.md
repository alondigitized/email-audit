## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud build (`cl.S7.exct.net`, `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) with Everest click tracking. Its main confirmed defects are missing image alt text, one non-HTTPS dynamic-content image, and a malformed document head. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Mixed content.** The dynamic-content image is served over plain HTTP: `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwMzYs…`. Clients that block non-HTTPS images, or that proxy images, may render it blank. Switch it to `https://`.
- **Visible tracking URL pattern.** The visible tracking URL is `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=102126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_595SW_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL_V…`. The `e=` value looks like a per-recipient identifier embedded in the URL. That is expected for ESP tracking, but it means the links are not static.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`, and the `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/…` image
  
  The Everest and LiveContent entries may be tracking or dynamic images, which would call for `alt=""`. If any of the PNGs carry the offer text, such as "$5.95 single wicks", that text is lost when images are off or for screen readers.
- **Malformed document structure.** After the `<meta>` tags, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears with no `</head>` or `<body>` in the visible source. The source is truncated, so confirm against the full HTML. If it is accurate, parsers will auto-correct the structure inconsistently.
- **Ineffective web-font import.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue…')` points at a family Google Fonts doesn't serve, so it adds a blocked or failing request for no benefit. Most clients strip `@import` anyway, and the fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial`) does the work.
- **Doctype and meta mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with HTML5-style `<meta charset="utf-8">`. The `Content-Type` meta tag also duplicates it. This is harmless, but redundant.
- **Dark-mode handling is present.** The email has `color-scheme` meta, `prefers-color-scheme` rules and `[data-ogsc]` selectors. No issue found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The only dynamic element is the `LiveContent.aspx` call, which carries an encoded query string (`qs=…`). It renders server-side, and a failed fetch has no visible fallback in the captured source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the relay may not capture these headers. Treat this as unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, so alignment is plausible but untested.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source doesn't show either. They can't be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** All links route through `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, and the destinations weren't resolved. Attribution depends on the campaign code `c=102126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_595SW_AM_BAL-NTF_FULL_V…`, which is truncated here.
- **Landing-page alignment is unverified.** The subject promises "$5.95 single wicks" on "last day", and no probe confirmed the link target reflects that offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five PNGs. Use `alt=""` for the tracking and dynamic images if they are decorative.
2. Change `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` to HTTPS, and add a fallback for failed loads.
3. Ensure `<head>` is closed and `<body>` is opened before the first content table.
4. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for Helvetica Neue.
5. Get the raw message headers directly, not via the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. Follow a sample of the 44 tracked links with a redirect-following probe. Check final destinations, status codes, UTM/campaign parameters and offer match.
7. Add a QA step that scans the full, untruncated HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
