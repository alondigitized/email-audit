# ✨ALL room sprays are $3.50, just for today.✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led room-spray send. The hero is "Festive feels in two spritzes" over a big "$3.50" for all Concentrated Room Sprays, with a "Today only" bar above it and a "Shop now" button beside the price. The eye goes to the price first. The code ROOMSPRAY is shown for both online and in-store use, and a barcode is included for the store.
- The weak spot is the second module. It repeats yesterday's "Lowest price of the year" Wallflowers $2.75 message. The 11/6 send already said "$2.75 Wallflowers start today", and the disclaimer shows that deal ended 11/6. That makes this module look stale and muddies the single-offer story. The third block, a $14.95 3-wick candle, adds a third price point. The email is solid but a little cluttered for a one-day room-spray push.
- Judgement: a good, honest, urgent send that loses some focus to leftover Doorbuster creative. It's about a 7.

## What's working

- The $3.50 price is large and sits under a clear "Today only" bar.
- The hero shows the actual products (Snowflakes & Cashmere and others) in a festive setting.
- The code ROOMSPRAY and the barcode make the offer easy to redeem online or in store.
- "Up to 24 per customer" is stated beside the price, so the limit isn't buried.
- Free shipping at $50 and the Rewards prompt sit at the top without competing with the hero.

## What's weak

- The "Lowest price of the year" Wallflowers $2.75 block recycles the 11/6 Doorbuster message. The fine print says that offer ended 11/6.
- The "Limited time only" label on the Wallflowers block competes with "Today only" on the hero.
- The 3-wick candle at $14.95 is a third offer with a third CTA.
- The "You might like" tiles have tiny, hard-to-read names and no prices or CTAs.
- The legal footer is very long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or reframe the expired Wallflowers block. Replace it with room-spray variants or bundles, or a "Doorbuster ended, but sprays are still $3.50" message.
- 2. Show more than two or three spray scents, and add a scent-name caption and a rating line for social proof.
- 3. Move a countdown or "ends at midnight" line into the hero, and pick one urgency label.
- 4. Put prices on the "You might like" tiles so they work as product CTAs, and make the text larger.
- 5. Shorten the footer legal text, or collapse it behind a link.

The confirmed defects are missing `alt` text on 6 images and one non-HTTPS image. The header findings (unsubscribe, authentication) are unverified, because the relay may have stripped them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTMsInQiOjEwNTM4LCJzIjo3fQAH...` is a Marketing Cloud live-content image served over plain HTTP. Some clients can block or rewrite it (mixed content), and the dynamic block may not render.

It is most likely an open-tracking pixel. If so, it needs `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `display:none`/`border="0"`, so it doesn't show as a broken image or get read aloud.

It will likely return an error. It also adds a blocking external request in clients that honor `@import`, and many don't. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers this, so the impact is wasted requests only.
