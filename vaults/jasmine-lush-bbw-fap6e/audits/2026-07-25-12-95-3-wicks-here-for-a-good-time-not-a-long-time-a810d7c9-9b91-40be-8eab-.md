# $12.95 3-wicks: here for a good time, not a long time…❤️‍🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth touchpoint on the same $12.95 3-Wick Candle campaign in three days, and it shows. Where the 7/23 teaser ("set your alarms") and the 7/23 launch ("get glowing") earned 8-9/10 for holding a single, focused hook, this send buries that same $12.95 hero under a wall of additional offers — a $5.95 Today Only single-wick deal with an in-store barcode, a Buy 3 Get 3 body-care push, a "Why Pick Our 3-Wicks" educational block, a second $12.95 banner repeat, and a 75% off Summer Sale strip, before even reaching the Top Offers grid.
- The top of the email still does its job — "Fall for the Glow," the $12.95 badge, and a clean "SHOP" button are all visible without scrolling, and the candle product shot is well-lit and on-brand. But the "Fall for the Glow" framing sitting directly above a "Summer Sale" banner further down is a seasonal mismatch that a subscriber will register even subconsciously.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, diluted by offer-stacking and cadence fatigue. This reads like B&BW ran out of new angles on the 3-wick event and defaulted to "throw everything in one email."

## What's working

- Hero banner delivers the offer instantly — "$12.95 All 3-Wick Candles" is legible without scrolling, with a clear "SHOP" CTA.
- Product-detail callout ("up to 45 hours of room-filling fragrance," "Limit 24") gives a concrete reason to buy, not just a price.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- The $5.95 Today Only single-wick offer includes a barcode for in-store redemption, which is a genuinely useful cross-channel touch.

## What's weak

- Four distinct offers ($12.95 3-wicks, $5.95 single wick, Buy 3 Get 3 body care, 75% off summer sale) compete for attention in one email — no single hero survives to the bottom.
- "Fall for the Glow" hero copy sits above a "Summer Sale" banner later in the same email — a seasonal contradiction in one send.
- This is the same $12.95 3-Wick event covered on 7/23 (teaser), 7/23 (launch), and 7/24 (retention) — a 4th nearly-identical touch in 3 days risks fatigue rather than urgency.
- Primary CTA copy is just "SHOP" repeated on every banner — generic, gives no reason to click one section over another.
- The subject line's "here for a good time, not a long time" is playful but doesn't attach to a specific deadline anywhere visible in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer. Given the brand has already run teaser, launch, and retention emails on the $12.95 3-wicks, this send should either pivot to a genuinely new hook (the Violet Buy 3 Get 3 launch) or drop everything except the 3-wick close — not both plus a flash sale plus a summer clearance.
- 2. Fix the season clash — pull "Fall for the Glow" language or move the Summer Sale banner to a separate send; having both in one email undercuts urgency on either.
- 3. Give the CTAs actual verbs+nouns — "Shop 3-Wicks," "Grab the $5.95 Deal," "Claim Buy 3 Get 3" — instead of four identical "SHOP" buttons.
- 4. Attach a real deadline to the $12.95 offer in-body (a date or countdown), since "Limited Time Only" without a date reads as evergreen, not urgent.
- **Subject Alt A:** `last call: $12.95 3-wicks won't last the weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `today only: $5.95 candles + $12.95 3-wicks 🔥`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: Buy 3, Get 3 free on new Violet body care.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `45-hour burn, $12.95 — ends soon.`
