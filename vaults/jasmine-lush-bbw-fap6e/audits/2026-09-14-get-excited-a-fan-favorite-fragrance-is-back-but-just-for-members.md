# 😎 get excited - a fan-favorite fragrance is back, but just for members. 🔓
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' "Member Days" kickoff send (Sept 14-16), and it's stuffed with offers — free shipping, buy-3-get-3-free, 3x rewards points, a $12.95 mini tote, and a sweepstakes — but it buries the one thing the subject line promised. "A fan-favorite fragrance is back, but just for members" sets up a specific reveal; the actual hero delivers vague teaser copy ("Three decades. One legend. Members only.") and you have to infer from the product shot that this is Sun-Ripened Raspberry. That's a classic subject/hero mismatch.
- The design itself is clean and on-brand — bold purple/gold blocking, clear product photography, legible CTAs — and there's no render breakage. But the email tries to do five things at once (Member Days, the fragrance relaunch, buy-3-get-3, the tote, the sweepstakes) and none of them get the focused, single-offer clarity that converts. It reads like a landing page compressed into an email rather than one sharp pitch.
- Net: solid production values, muddled message. A subscriber opening for "the fragrance is back" scrolls past a generic loyalty-day banner before finding it, which is a real leak in the funnel this subject line built.

## What's working

- Purple "Member Days" banner up top is unmissable and states dates + free-shipping threshold clearly.
- Buy 3 Get 3 Free is a strong, unambiguous, high-value offer stated in large type with a clean product shot.
- Multiple distinct CTA buttons ("Join today & shop") are visually consistent and easy to spot throughout.
- "We think you'll love" product recs at the bottom add a low-friction secondary path to purchase.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a specific returning fragrance, but the hero section never names it — "Three decades. One legend." is mood copy, not the product reveal the subject set up.
- Five competing offers (Member Days, fragrance relaunch, B3G3, 3x points, $12.95 tote, sweepstakes) stack vertically with equal visual weight — nothing tells the eye where to land first.
- The actual fragrance identity (Sun-Ripened Raspberry) is only legible by reading the small product label in the photo, not from any headline.
- "3x Rewards Points" block gives no dollar/point value or deadline, making it feel like filler between two stronger offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero to match the subject line's promise: lead with the fragrance name and "back by popular demand" framing before anything about Member Days generally — the subject already earned the open, don't make the reader hunt for the payoff.
- 2. Cut the email from five offers to two: the fragrance relaunch (hero) and B3G3 (primary CTA), and move the tote/sweepstakes/3x points into a single "also happening this week" strip lower down.
- 3. Add a real deadline/value callout to the 3x Rewards Points block (e.g., "now through 9/16") so it doesn't read as generic filler.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sun-Ripened Raspberry is back — members get it first 🍓`
- **Subject Alt B:** `it's official: your favorite scent just returned`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Buy 3 Get 3 Free on full-size body care, member days only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $50+, 3x points, and a $12.95 mini tote — all week.`
