# $2.95 soaps start today. 🫧  new, skin-loving favorites just added.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a better send than the last week of 40%-off repeats. It leads with a single concrete offer: **ALL hand soaps $2.95**, with a "Starts today" tag. A second block adds **$1 PocketBac sanitizers** for "Today only" with code JOY. The pivot from storewide 40% to a category price point is a real change of beat, and the soap hero is clean and easy to read.
- The render has problems, though. There's a large blank gap under the "Give 3 wicks of wonder" candle block, then three small product tiles with tiny captions. The lower half is mostly empty white space, with orphaned underline fragments near the footer and a faint, low-contrast "Our feel good formula" block. A scrolling subscriber sees a strong top and a bottom that looks broken. A cropped, scrolling "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" ticker also clips at both edges.
- Verdict: a good hero and offer, undermined by missing content and a sloppy lower half. It's the best-targeted BBW send in the recent run, but it's not a clean one.

- The $2.95 hand soap price is huge, high-contrast and unmissable, with a "Shop now" button right under it.
- The "Today only" $1 PocketBac block adds a second, urgent, specific deal.
- The product shelf with the bottles shows the soaps directly, and the seasonal styling fits Black Friday week.
- The announcement bar keeps the free-shipping-at-$50 and Rewards messages visible without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- A big blank white band sits below the candle hero. It looks like missing images or modules.
- The three product tiles (Winter Candy Apple soap, sanitizer, A Thousand Wishes gift set) are small, with captions too tiny to read, and show no prices or buttons.
- The lower half of the email is mostly empty, with stray underline fragments near the bottom that look like broken links.
- The "Give 3 wicks of wonder" candle block has no visible offer or CTA, so it competes with the soap message.
- The "Our feel good formula" banner has pale text on a cream background that is hard to read.
- The clipped "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" ticker at the top looks cut off.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Salesforce Marketing Cloud-built, with Everest and `exct.net` tracking. It has no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). Alt-text gaps, one non-HTTPS image, and unverifiable authentication and unsubscribe headers are the main findings. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe as click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes, and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjcs...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites this, but clients that block mixed content may drop it. If this is a dynamic-content block, it won't render there.
- **Image URL with tracking parameters:** the image `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295HS_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_F...` carries a per-recipient `e=` value in the query string. The audit tool flagged it as an image lacking alt text. It is a tracking or dynamic endpoint, not a static asset.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 6 images have no `alt` attribute:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - the `LiveContent.aspx` image
  
  All are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as nothing or as a filename. Decorative images should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Document structure:** in the visible source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the `<meta>` tags, with no `</head>` or `<body>` before it. Parsers will auto-correct this, but it is invalid markup. It also puts `<table>` content, and `<style>` and conditional comments, inside the head.
- **Redundant MSO blocks:** `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` both declare identical `OfficeDocumentSettings` XML. This is harmless but duplicated.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. As far as I know, "Helvetica Neue" is not served by Google Fonts, so this request likely fails. `@import` is also ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-family` fallback stack handles it, so the effect is a wasted request.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags plus `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are present. No issues found from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The footer was truncated, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and the tracking domains look consistent, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** the footer was truncated, so these can't be confirmed from the provided source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing page destinations can't be verified because all 50 links were wrapped in click-tracking redirects and skipped by the probe. The `c=` value in the Everest URL (`112126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295HS_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_F...`) suggests campaign attribution is passed through the tracking layer. Whether it reaches the landing page is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 5 PNGs, using `alt=""` where decorative. Also fix the `LiveContent.aspx` and Everest images.
2. Serve the `cl.S7.exct.net` LiveContent image over HTTPS.
3. Fix the document structure by closing `</head>` and opening `<body>` before the first content table. Drop the duplicate MSO settings block and the ineffective Google Fonts `@import`.
4. Check the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. This separates a relay artifact from a real defect.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually or with a redirect-following probe. Confirm the final URLs, status codes, and UTM or campaign parameters.
6. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, and postal address can be checked.
