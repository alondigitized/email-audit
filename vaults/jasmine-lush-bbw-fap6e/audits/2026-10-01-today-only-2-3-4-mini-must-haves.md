# today only, $2, $3 & $4 mini must-haves. 🥰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong offer-led hero. "Today only" sits in a dark banner, then a tiered price ladder: **$2** hand cream & sanitizer sprays, **$3** mini body care, bar soaps & more, **$4** lip care. A "Shop now" button and the code LITTLES (up to 24 per customer) finish it. The eye lands on the big numerals first. Compared with the 2026-09-29 "$3.95 holiday head start" send, which never showed its price, this one delivers on what the subject promises.
- The body then loses focus. After the hero there are three more unrelated modules: festive 3-wick candles, New Everyday Luxuries, and a "We think you'll love" product row. The hero's urgency gets diluted, and the lower sections have no price or CTA button. Two big stretches of blank space (around the candle image and the Everyday Luxuries block) look like images that didn't load or hadn't rendered. The fine print is long and dense.
- Verdict: a good top third and a muddled rest. It's a solid 6-7 send that would be a clear 8 if it stopped after the hero.

## What's working

- The $2 / $3 / $4 ladder is concrete, scannable, and sits right under a "Today only" banner.
- Subject and hero match, with "$2, $3 & $4 mini" echoed by the price tiers.
- "Shop now" is a clear, visible button, and the promo code LITTLES is shown with a barcode.
- The product photography (hand cream, body mist, lip product) is attractive and sits on a clean cream background.
- The "up to 24 per customer" limit appears directly under the offer, not only in the footer.

## What's weak

- The lower modules (3-wick candles, Everyday Luxuries) have no prices, no buttons, and no link to the offer, so they pull attention from the "today only" deal.
- There are large blank gaps around the candle and Everyday Luxuries modules. They read as broken or missing images.
- The Everyday Luxuries image has a small text box ("New formula with 2X the concentration…") that is hard to read.
- The fine print is very long and dense, with several overlapping offers (the $13.50 candle deal and the Buy 3 Get 3 deal) that aren't in the hero.
- "Today only" is a small bar label, with no countdown or time of day in the visible body.

- 1. Cut the email down to the hero plus one supporting module. Move the candles and the new-arrivals content to a separate send so the "today only" ask isn't competing with them.
- 2. Add a "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" line under the price ladder. This matches the "Ends today" banner on the 09-27 hydration send, which scored 7.
- 3. Fix the empty image gaps, or make the candle and Everyday Luxuries modules full-bleed with a price and a button.
- 4. Add specific button copy, such as "Shop $2–$4 minis" instead of "Shop now". Link each tier ($2, $3, $4) to its own category.
- 5. Add a line of social proof or a bestseller tag to the product row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC-built template (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, `cl.S7.exct.net`) with structurally invalid HTML: content blocks sit inside `<head>` and between `</head>` and `<body>`. It also has missing alt text on 7 images and one non-HTTPS image. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and auth items below are unverified rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS image.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMTZ9…` is loaded over plain HTTP, which clients may block or flag as mixed content. It is also dynamic content (SFMC LiveContent), so it may render blank if the call fails.
- **Third-party image host.** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_SMALLSEE_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` is a separate vendor domain (Everest) serving an image with an identifier in the query string. If the vendor is down or blocked, that module breaks.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?...` is 1×1 with `alt=""` and is wrapped in a `mso-hide:all` table. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - Several `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` blocks sit inside `<head>`, before `</head>`.
  - Further tables (variables, preheader/header) sit between `</head>` and `<body>`.
  - Parsers hoist this content unpredictably, so some clients may drop or reposition it.
- **Duplicate charset declarations.** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …UTF-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are both present. This is redundant but harmless.
- **Ineffective web-font imports.**
  - `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't host.
  - `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients.
  - The `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallbacks cover the failure, so the impact is a wasted request.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 7 images).**
  - Five images have no alt: `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`, `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`, `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`, `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png` and `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`.
  - The other two are `LiveContent.aspx` and the `everestengagement.com` image.
  - Images are the likely carriers of the "$2, $3 & $4" offer, so with images off or a screen reader the offer is not conveyed.
  - Any that are intentionally decorative should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Fixed-width layout.** `style="width: 600px;"` is set on the nested tables, with responsive rules only in a `@media` block. Gmail app clients that strip `<style>` would fall back to a 600px fixed layout.
- **Preheader and hidden-text handling** looks standard: `.preheader` is `display:none`, with a `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp;` spacer block. No issue.
- **Dark mode.** `color-scheme` meta tags and `prefers-color-scheme` rules are present. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) are visible in the retrieved HTML. The `e=200511831233` parameter in the Everest image URL looks like a recipient or send identifier, so that module is personalized or dynamic. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both were reported missing, but the QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is unconfirmed.
  - Re-check the raw message source from a direct-to-Gmail send before treating it as a defect.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sending domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** These are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every click goes through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects. Final landing URLs and any `utm_*` parameters could not be inspected because the probe was skipped.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `c=100126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_SMALLSEE_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` on the Everest image URL. It encodes promo/segment info but is not a UTM.
- Landing page alignment with the "$2, $3 & $4 mini" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables and the variables/preheader blocks inside `<body>`, and keep only `<meta>`, `<title>` and `<style>` in `<head>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 PNGs and the Everest image, or `alt=""` where decorative. Make sure the offer text appears in alt or live text.
3. Serve the LiveContent image over HTTPS (`https://cl.S7.exct.net/...`) and give it a fallback, or confirm SFMC supports the HTTPS endpoint.
4. Remove the `Helvetica Neue` Google Fonts `@import`. It doesn't resolve and isn't needed given the fallback stack.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw headers from a non-relay delivery. Fix the pipeline's header capture if the headers are present upstream.
6. Follow the 46 redirect links in a browser or with a redirect-aware tracer to confirm destinations, status codes and UTM presence. Add an allowlist so the QA probe can resolve `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer unsubscribe link and physical address, and any merge tokens.
