# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally cannot see the homepage — what loaded is a bot-detection wall with a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" interstitial. The Bath & Body Works wordmark is partially visible at the top, and below it is just a big "Press & Hold" button and a reference ID. There is zero content, zero product, zero offer. From my perspective as a customer opening this on my phone, the brand just put a velvet rope in front of the front door.

## What's working

- The Bath & Body Works wordmark renders crisply and is immediately recognizable — at least I know I'm on the right site.
- The "Press & Hold" button is large and mobile-friendly in tap target size; if a real human lands here they can clear it in one gesture.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is gated — I see no product, no promo, no nav, nothing that makes me want to stay.
- The copy "Press & Hold to confirm you are not a bot" is clinical and cold for a brand that sells warmth and self-care vibes.
- No context is given for why this is appearing — a first-time visitor would have zero idea if this is a glitch or intentional.
- The reference ID at the bottom (`ede49310-550a-11f1-ae68-514221a5186a`) reads like an error screen and erodes brand polish instantly.
- The top of the wordmark is clipped / cut off on the right edge, suggesting a layout sizing issue even on this interstitial.

## Recommendations

- Remove or dramatically reduce bot-gate friction on organic mobile traffic — a "Press & Hold" interstitial before the homepage even loads is a conversion killer, especially on App Store / social referral traffic where my generation expects instant gratification.
- If the bot gate must stay, inject brand voice into the copy: something like "Just making sure it's really you — hold on a sec ✨" keeps the warm B&BW tone instead of reading like a Cloudflare error.
- Show a blurred or dimmed version of the homepage behind the gate so visitors can see product/offers while the check runs — reduces bounce intent.
- Add a brand-color background (B&BW's signature blue or a seasonal palette) instead of stark white — even the friction screen should feel on-brand.
