# oh noooo! final hours for $3.25 Wallflowers. 😭
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' closing bell on the $3.25 Wallflowers arc that launched this same morning ("hold on. ✋ they're only $3.25?! 😲", 8/10). The formula is identical to that send: red "FINAL HOURS!" banner at top, $3.25 hero price prominent, and then — same problem — three separate "LIMITED TIME ONLY!" sections stacked beneath it (Wallflowers, $5.95 Mists/Body Sprays, and 3-Wick Candles with a visible countdown timer). If the morning email couldn't commit to one hero, the closing bell should — it doesn't.
- The subject line is the best thing here. "oh noooo! final hours for $3.25 Wallflowers. 😭" breaks from B&BW's otherwise formulaic subject pattern and carries real personality. The "Shore Thing" creative is seasonally sharp for early June. But sending a second email the same day on the same arc, with the same three-section layout and the same "SHOP" CTAs, is fast-burning the list's patience.
- Judgement: a competent close that squanders the urgency setup by refusing to cut the Mists and Candle sections and let Wallflowers own the send.

## What's working

- **"FINAL HOURS!" red banner + $3.25 price anchor** land immediately above the fold — the urgency reads before a single word is parsed.
- **"oh noooo!" subject line** is genuinely differentiated; playful panic stands out in a sea of "TODAY ONLY" subject lines.
- **"It's a Shore Thing" hero creative** is summer-appropriate and visually cohesive with the Wallflowers product imagery.
- **Countdown timer visible** in the 3-wick section adds a real-time urgency layer that supports the "final hours" framing.
- **TOP OFFERS icon row** gives a fast-scan shortcut to four product categories without requiring a full scroll.

## What's weak

- **Second email of the same day on the same arc** — the morning send already introduced $3.25 Wallflowers; this close feels like a spam nudge, not a meaningful final call.
- **Three "LIMITED TIME ONLY!" sections dilute the Wallflowers urgency** — if everything is final hours, nothing is.
- **"SHOP" CTA is generic across all three sections** — no verb+noun specificity (e.g., "Shop $3.25 Refills" or "Grab a Refill"), which flattens click intent.
- **No visible social proof, reviews, or member-specific pricing** — nothing to tip an undecided subscriber who already saw the morning send.
- **Mists and Candles sections are identical in visual weight to the hero** — the eye has no reason to stay on Wallflowers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email to one section: Wallflowers only.** This is the closing bell. The Mists and Candles sections belong in a separate send (or not at all today). Let the $3.25 refill breathe.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA** from "SHOP" to something like "Shop $3.25 Refills — Final Hours" or "Grab Mine Before They're Gone."
- 3. **Lead with the countdown timer** in the hero, not buried in section three. A live clock in the hero + "FINAL HOURS!" reinforces each other perfectly.
- 4. **Add a short social proof line** — "Over 1M sold" or a star rating under the hero product image.
- 5. **Rewrite subject + preheader** to create a genuine inbox A/B:
- **Subject Alt A:** `last call: $3.25 Wallflowers end tonight 😭`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your Wallflowers clock is ticking. ⏱️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fragrance Refills at the lowest price of the season — ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `All Wallflowers® Refills $3.25. After tonight, they go back to full price.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for broad client compatibility but has three notable issues: two broken image `src` attributes, missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers (RFC 2369 + RFC 8058), and unprocessed template comments leaking internal infrastructure details into rendered HTML source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues found** with link structure itself, but coverage is limited:

- All 25 clickable links route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect). QA probes were skipped for all 25 — destination URL integrity and UTM parameter presence cannot be confirmed from this audit.
- Open pixel at `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?CMWMVQZBKP4UNOADKQ65I3H26M.70251...` — standard 1×1 tracking implementation, alt="" is acceptable here.
- Secondary engagement tracker present: `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=060626_RDMFH_WF_325WFEE_NS_BAL_V7` — dual-system tracking (SFMC + Everest). This image is also flagged for missing alt text (see §3).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Three missing alt text violations (QA-confirmed):**

| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` (hosted on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`) | Content image, `alt` attribute absent |
| Two images with empty `src=""` | Both `src` and `alt` absent — will render as broken image icons in all clients |
| Everest Engagement pixel (`bbwus.everestengagement.com`) | `alt` absent; tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly |

**Additional rendering notes:**
- `DOCTYPE HTML 4.01 Transitional` — archaic but standard for email; no action needed.
- No `<meta name="viewport">` tag. Mobile layout relies solely on the `@media (max-width:599px)` block. Clients that ignore media queries (some Outlook mobile versions) will render the fixed 600px table unscaled.
- `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Source+Sans+Pro...')` — blocked by Outlook (all versions) and some corporate mail proxies. Fallback stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` is defined and will engage, but this is worth noting if font consistency is a rendering requirement.
- `bgcolor="#ffffff"` HTML attribute used instead of `background-color` CSS — functional across all clients, no issue.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unprocessed / leaked template artifacts in production HTML source:**

```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```
This appears **twice** in the `<head>`/`<body>` boundary area. It is shell command syntax — either an unprocessed template variable or a leftover build artifact. It does not affect rendering but exposes internal naming conventions and should not be in production sends.

```html
<div data-type="slot" data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9" data-label="Preheader"></div>
```
Template slot metadata (`data-key`, `data-label`) is rendered into the live HTML. Email clients do not execute these attributes, but they expose SFMC content block keys to anyone who inspects source.

AMPscript logic comments are also present verbatim:
```html
<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
```
These are developer notes describing business logic rules (NTF flag handling, APT assignment). They have no deliverability impact but constitute an information disclosure issue.

No unrendered merge token syntax (e.g., `%%variable%%`) observed in the visible truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**Two header-level warnings (QA-confirmed):**

| Header | Status | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` (RFC 2369) | **Not found** | Gmail and Apple Mail use this to surface the unsubscribe button at the top of the email UI. Its absence forces users to the footer link, increasing spam complaints. |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **Not found** | One-click machine unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender policy for >5k/day senders) is not confirmed operative. |
| `Authentication-Results` | **Not found** | SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown — header not captured through AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment from this audit. |

**Note:** Both `List-Unsubscribe` headers are SMTP-level headers, not HTML-level. Their absence in QA findings likely reflects the AgentMail relay stripping or not forwarding them rather than the sender omitting them. However, the warning stands until confirmed against raw SMTP headers from the originating MTA at `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`.

The preheader div contains visual preview text (`"hurry, before this deal wilts away. 🥀"`) correctly hidden with `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden`. Filler zero-width character padding is present and technically correct.

Footer unsubscribe link is not visible in the truncated HTML provided — assumed present based on standard compliance practice, but cannot be confirmed.
