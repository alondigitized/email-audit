# $1 today only! tiny price, totally necessary. 🥰
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Bath & Body Works' July 2 send — call it email twenty-one or so in the Semi-Annual Sale blitz — and it pivots sharply away from yesterday's (July 1) "75% off body care" broadside by anchoring to the simplest possible hook: $1 PocketBac, today only. After the brand fired its biggest caliber yet yesterday, the $1 price point lands as a smart low-ticket impulse play — impossible to misread, requires zero math, and gives fatigued subscribers a reason to open without expecting another all-categories clearance event.
- The secondary blocks — 3-Wick Candles starting at $13 and new Foaming Hand Soaps, both labeled "STARTS TODAY!" — add double-news value without stepping on the hero. The "new price drop" framing for those secondary items gives this send a freshness signal that the last few carousel-style emails lacked.
- The single genuine weakness is that the supporting grid at the bottom (TOP OFFERS / TODAY'S DEALS thumbnails) adds visual noise and undercuts the clean "$1 in/out" message the subject line promises. This could have been the tightest, most decisive B&BW email of the blitz — it's almost there.

## What's working

- **$1 hero is a perfect subject-to-body match** — the price is the first thing the eye hits and it confirms the subject line promise instantly.
- **"TODAY ONLY!" + "STARTS TODAY!" labeling** creates two distinct urgency signals — one for the existing $1 deal, one for genuinely new price drops — rather than recycling the same stale urgency copy.
- **Secondary blocks have concrete prices and dedicated SHOP buttons** — $13 candles and foaming soap pricing are immediately readable without scrolling.
- **Summer theming lands correctly** — ice cream/dessert imagery in the candle block and the "Summer-Ready" soap headline feel earned for July 2, not forced.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, product images load, typography is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP" CTA copy is generic across all three hero blocks** — every block says the same word; none reinforces the specific offer (e.g., "Grab $1 PocketBacs" or "Shop $13 Candles").
- **No fragrance names or scent-specific copy in the hero** — BBW subscribers shop by scent, and a $1 offer with zero scent info means the click is a blind bet.
- **TOP OFFERS thumbnail grid at the bottom competes with the hero** — same pattern repeated blitz-wide now; it reads like visual clutter rather than added value.
- **No loyalty/member callout** — twenty emails into a blitz, the brand hasn't used member-only pricing or early-access language to reward loyal subscribers and create differentiation.
- **"Today only" credibility is wearing thin** — the blitz has used this frame on at least four distinct sends; subscribers may be starting to discount it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make every CTA earn its space:** Replace "SHOP" with offer-specific copy — "Grab $1 PocketBacs," "Shop $13 Candles," "See New Soaps." This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Surface 2-3 scent names in the hero block** — even just "Champagne Toast · Mahogany Teakwood · A Thousand Wishes, $1 each" would let subscribers pre-qualify and spike CTR.
- 3. **Cut the TOP OFFERS grid** or rename it "What's New Today" and fill it only with the day's actual new price drops — distinguish it from the standing sale inventory.
- 4. **Add a one-line member callout** above the footer: "Members: you unlocked early access to these new drops." Costs nothing, rewards loyalty, creates FOMO for non-members.
- 5. **Add a countdown or sell-out signal** on the $1 hero — "Only until midnight" or "While supplies last" — since $1 PocketBacs will genuinely sell fast and that scarcity is real and believable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$1 PocketBac — today only (yes, really)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `midnight deadline: grab a $1 hand sani 🧴`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New price drops just started: 3-wick candles from $13 + summer soaps.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus candles from $13 and brand-new foaming soaps — new drops today only.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Compliance headers were not captured by the relay, creating unresolvable uncertainty about List-Unsubscribe status.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 tracking links — health unverified**
All CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` (SFMC redirect). The QA probe correctly skipped HTTP checks on redirect domains. Destination URL health and UTM propagation cannot be confirmed from this audit without following the redirects.

**Open pixel**
`https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?AQUJMQEFLW7EFNXYC2CLT5BXV4.70252&d=70252&bmt=0`
Rendered in a 0×0 table before `<body>` opens (see §3). Open tracking may misfire in clients that halt rendering at the malformed DOM boundary.

**Movable Ink & Everest Engagement image URLs carry subscriber IDs**
- `mi_u=200511831233` in the Movable Ink image src
- `e=200511831233` in the Everest Engagement src

These appear correctly populated (not raw merge tokens), but both images are missing `alt` attributes (see §3).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CRITICAL — DOM elements rendered before `<body>` opens**

```html
</head>
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
<table ...>  <!-- open pixel table, BEFORE <body> -->
<body style="...">
```

Two `<table>` blocks (the open-pixel table and the centering wrapper) are emitted after `</head>` but before `<body>`. Browsers auto-repair this; many email clients do not. Outlook, Apple Mail, and Gmail all handle implicit body insertion differently — this is a confirmed rendering risk.

**5 images missing `alt` text (QA confirmed)**

| Image | src |
|---|---|
| Movable Ink hero | `https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/eb826c20b7cd4ecf.png?mi_u=…` |
| Static asset | `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/418d0db7….png` |
| Everest Engagement | `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=…` |
| 2 × empty `src` | `src=""` |

Missing alt on the hero (Movable Ink) means subscribers with images-off see no fallback text for the primary offer. The two empty-`src` images will render as broken image icons in most clients.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import`**
`Source+Sans+Pro` is imported once in `<head>` (weights 400 only) and again inline inside a table cell `<style>` block (weights 400 + 700). The second import also requests bold weight the first does not, meaning bold may load inconsistently depending on which `<style>` block the client evaluates first.

**Preheader implementation is correct** — hidden `<div class="preheader">` with `display:none; height:0; overflow:hidden; opacity:0; mso-hide:all` is properly constructed. Text reads "your pockets will be so happy. 👖🤗".

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unresolved shell command in HTML comments (appears twice)**

```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```

This raw backtick shell-command syntax appears at two locations in the source. It was never executed or replaced — either a build-step failure that left a template variable unrendered, or a stale developer artifact. It is inert to recipients but signals a broken templating step.

**AMPScript implementation comments not stripped before send**

```html
<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
<!--Set variables to support dynamic template attributes-->
```

Four internal logic comments are present in the delivered HTML. These expose segment flag names (`NTF_Flag`), internal campaign-targeting terminology (`APT`), and a conditional logic note. No functional breakage, but this is an operational hygiene failure — these should be stripped by the build/send process.

**Populated merge fields** — Subscriber ID (`200511831233`) and campaign code (`070226_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_1PB`) appear correctly resolved in Movable Ink and Everest Engagement URLs. No raw `%%variable%%` tokens observed in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe headers — unconfirmed (QA WARN)**
Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were captured by the AgentMail relay. The QA note correctly flags this as a relay-capture limitation rather than a confirmed absence. However, Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate both headers for senders at BBW's volume. If the headers are genuinely absent, this is a deliverability and compliance defect. **Must be verified against raw SMTP headers from the sending MTA.**

**SPF/DKIM/DMARC — unconfirmed (QA WARN)**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured via relay. Sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a SFMC subdomain — authentication records should be present at the DNS level, but this audit cannot confirm pass/fail from available data.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism** — The HTML source is truncated before the footer. Cannot confirm from this sample. These are standard elements in BBW's template; no finding made without the full source.

All 24 CTAs redirect through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. UTM parameters would be embedded in the destination URLs registered in SFMC, not visible in the HTML source. The campaign code `070226_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_1PB` is present in image tracking URLs and suggests a structured naming convention is in use, but UTM propagation to `bathandbodyworks.com` landing pages cannot be confirmed without following the redirects.
