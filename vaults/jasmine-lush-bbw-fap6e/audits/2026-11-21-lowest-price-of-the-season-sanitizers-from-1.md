# 🖐️ lowest price of the season - sanitizers from $1.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third BBW send today, and the sanitizer offer is the strongest of them. The hero is a huge "$1" under "Lowest price of the season" with a single "Shop now" button. A "Final hours" tag sits above it. The $1 price point is easy to grasp, and it undercuts the earlier "$1 PocketBac, today only" mention in this morning's $2.95 soaps send, which makes it feel like a real escalation.
- Below the hero the email runs two more offers: all hand soaps at $2.95, and $13.95 three-wick candles. It also repeats the in-store barcode and the online code JOY. That makes three price points in one send. The soap block repeats this morning's "$2.95 soaps start today" email almost exactly, so a subscriber who opened both will feel the repetition. The 40%-off sends earlier in the week had the same fatigue problem.
- Overall it is a solid, clear send, but it has too many offers for a single scroll. The fine print is very long and the exclusions are heavy. I'd call it a good hero attached to a crowded body.

## What's working

- The "$1" hero is oversized and legible, and it ties directly to the subject line.
- The "Final hours" and "Starts today" tags give each offer a time frame.
- The JOY code and barcode block spells out how to redeem in store and online.
- The product photography is bright and seasonal, and the pink mist and sanitizer sit cleanly in the hero.
- The candle block with the model is a warm, human break between the product shots.

## What's weak

- The "Black Friday week" marquee strip at the top is cropped mid-word ("CK FRIDAY WEEK"), so it reads as a rendering glitch.
- The sanitizer hero, the soap block and the candle block are three separate offers competing for the same click.
- The hero fine print ("Up to 24 per customer, use code JOY") is tiny and light, and the "Lowest price of the season" claim carries an asterisk.
- The footer legal copy runs very long, with many exclusions and dates, which undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- The "You might like" row shows three Winter Candy Apple items with no prices, so it adds little.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the $1 sanitizer the only offer in the send, or cut the soap block entirely, since it repeats this morning's email. One offer with one button would lift click rate the most.
- 2. Fix or remove the cropped marquee strip at the top.
- 3. Put the JOY code in a visible, tappable button in the hero instead of below the fold.
- 4. Add prices and ratings to the "You might like" row, or drop it.
- 5. Move the long legal copy behind a "See details" link and keep two lines of essentials visible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with 51 click-tracked links and a dark-mode CSS system. Its main code-level problems are missing alt text on 6 images, one non-HTTPS image, and body content that appears to sit inside `<head>`. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the relay likely stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The probe skipped all 51 links as click-redirect domains (`[WARN] 51 tracking link(s) skipped`). Link health, redirect chains, and final destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure image:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients may block it or show mixed-content warnings. It is a Marketing Cloud dynamic-content image, so a failed load leaves a blank or broken block.
- **Odd image `src`:** One image has `src=https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_1PBEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V3`. This is a second tracking and personalization vendor (Everest) beyond the Salesforce click domain. The URL carries a recipient-level `e=` ID in a request that fires on open or image load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 of the 6 images flagged in QA):**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  All are on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`. If any carry offer content, it is unavailable to screen readers and to image-blocked clients. The sixth flagged image is `LiveContent.aspx` (see section 2).
- **Alt-less Everest image:** The `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/...` `<img>` also has no alt text.
- **Structure:** In the visible source, `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no `</head>` or `<body>` between them. Browsers and some clients will auto-close `<head>`. The MSO conditional blocks and `<style>` that follow sit in an unexpected position. The source is truncated, so verify against the full HTML.
- **Dead font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` points to a family Google Fonts does not host. It adds a failing request and is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The fallback stack `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` covers it.
- **Doctype:** The email uses HTML 4.01 Transitional with a `<meta charset>` alongside the `http-equiv` charset. This is harmless duplication.
- **Duplicate MSO blocks:** `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` appears in both `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, so it is redundant.
- **Accessibility positives:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and `color-scheme` meta tags are present.
- **Dark-mode rule:** `.darkmode-dark` forces `width:70px; height:70px !important`. If a non-70px asset is swapped in, it will distort.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header** (`[WARN]`) and **no `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (RFC 8058). The check's own note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Treat both as unconfirmed.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` matches the image host `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which is consistent with a properly aligned sending domain. No alignment failure is evidenced.
- **Footer unchecked:** The footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) falls in the truncated portion, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links go through click-redirect domains, so the final URLs and any UTM parameters cannot be verified. The QA probe never resolved them.
- The only visible campaign identifier is the Everest `c=112126_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_1PBEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V3` token. No UTM parameters are visible in the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the 5 flagged `image.e2...` PNGs, the `LiveContent.aspx` image, and the Everest image. Use `alt=""` only for decorative images.
2. **Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS.** Switch `http://cl.S7.exct.net/...` to `https://` in the Marketing Cloud content block.
3. **Fix the document structure.** Close `</head>` and open `<body id="body">` before the content wrapper table. This also makes the `u + #body` Gmail selector work.
4. **Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue"** and the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` block.
5. **Re-run the audit on raw `.eml` or SMTP-level capture** to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. The relay is the likely cause of the current warnings.
6. **Resolve the redirect chains** with an allow-listed HEAD or GET follow of the 51 links. This would confirm the destinations and UTM carry-through.
