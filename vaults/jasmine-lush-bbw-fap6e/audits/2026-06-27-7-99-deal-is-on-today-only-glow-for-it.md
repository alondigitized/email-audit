# 🚨 $7.99 deal is ON. today only! glow for it...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works fires another "today only" salvo — this time hanging the frame on "Glow Time" with a $7.99 candle lead. The creative is tidier than most of this blitz: four distinct offer blocks with clear price points, a legible hierarchy, and product photography that actually supports the copy. The duck emoji from the Semi-Annual Sale header is back, but it doesn't distract from the hero.
- The real problem isn't what's inside — it's what subscribers see in their inbox. This is conservatively the seventeenth email in nine days. The June 26 "big price drop. big. HUGE." send already deployed aggressive urgency at a high creative peak; today's "today only! glow for it..." reads like the same pill in a different wrapper. The 🚨 emoji in the subject is a minor red flag after multiple days of low-grade alarm signals. Cadence fatigue is doing more damage than any creative misstep here.
- Inside the email, the four-block layout earns its keep: $7.99 candles anchors the hero, 75% off body care and 50% off hand soaps fill the middle, and $3.50 Wallflowers closes the stack. Clean render, no broken images, no layout bugs. A solid execution dragged down by volume.

## What's working

- **$7.99 hero is immediate** — the price and "Select Candles" read instantly; no scrolling required to get the deal.
- **Four distinct offer tiers** give the email legs beyond a single SKU — 75%, 50%, and $3.50 anchors all land in one scroll.
- **"TODAY ONLY!" treatment** is visually separated as a banner, not buried in copy — it genuinely reads as urgency, not noise.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, product photography is crisp and on-brand.
- **"Once they're gone, they're gone"** is the best headline in the email — concrete scarcity without hyperventilating.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP" CTA is four emails too generic** — every one of these Semi-Annual sends uses the same button copy; after seventeen emails it disappears.
- **No scent names, no SKUs, no product specifics** — "Select Candles" at $7.99 doesn't tell me which candles. A single named hero scent would triple click intent.
- **Cadence penalty is severe** — the June 26 send already hit a creative peak; this email reads like a re-skin of the same urgency beat with a different emoji.
- **🚨 subject emoji is escalating alarm fatigue** — the brand has cycled through ⌚️, 🏡, 🧖‍♀️, ✨, 💸, and now 🚨 in a week; each one dilutes the next.
- **No loyalty/member tier visible** — for a brand with a robust rewards program, there's no personalized "your points" hook or member-exclusive callout anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name one candle.** Lead the hero with a specific scent — "Mahogany Teakwood, $7.99 today only" — so subscribers have a reason to click that isn't generic. This single change lifts click intent more than any copy tweak.
- 2. **Retire "SHOP" as the CTA.** "Get it for $7.99" or "Grab the deal" is specific; "SHOP" is invisible after seventeen uses.
- 3. **Kill one of the four offer blocks.** The four-tier layout dilutes focus; two strong blocks with richer product detail beat four sparse ones every time.
- 4. **Add a loyalty hook.** Something like "members: extra 10% off your candles" in the hero would differentiate this from the identical offer every non-member sees.
- 5. **Back off the daily cadence.** One send tomorrow with a genuine last-day creative — not another "today only" — would hit harder than three "final hours" claims in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `your $7.99 candle is waiting — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `candles at $7.99. pick one before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mahogany Teakwood, Eucalyptus Spearmint, and more — select styles, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `75% off body care + your $7.99 hero. Semi-Annual Sale ends soon.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has two confirmed broken merge tokens (`mi_u=`, `e=`) that will cause subscriber-level personalization and image targeting to silently fail for all recipients. Nine images are missing alt text, and compliance headers are unverifiable through the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**23 click-tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all routed through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Destination URLs and redirect chain health are unverified; no broken-link confirmation is possible from this audit.

**Open pixel present and functional:**
```
https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?7DP4HU42QUEUXFY4G5QY7VEGKM.70260&d=70260&bmt=0
```
Standard 1×1 pixel, rendered as a hidden `<img>` with empty alt — expected.

**Empty `e=` parameter in Everest Engagement URL:**
```
https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=062726_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_799SELCDL_AM_BAL-NTF-OPTD_FULL
```
The `e=` param (email/subscriber identifier) is blank. If Everest uses this for attribution or suppression, it will be misfired or unattributed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**9 images missing alt text** (confirmed by QA). Affected images include hero/promotional assets served from `mi.bathandbodyworks.com` and `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Two additional entries have both empty `src` and empty `alt` — these are likely unfired dynamic content slots:

```
[WARN] Image missing alt text: src=""   (×2)
```
Empty-src images will produce broken image icons in some clients.

**Google Fonts `@import` will be stripped** by Gmail, Outlook (all versions), and Apple Mail in some configurations. The import (`Source+Sans+Pro`) has no explicit in-email fallback declaration; rendering will silently fall back to the client default sans-serif, not the declared `Arial, Helvetica`.

**Content placed before `<body>` tag** — the first preheader `<table>` and the MSO pixel `<table>` both appear in the source before the `<body>` element opens. Several email clients will handle this by auto-inserting a `<body>`, but behavior is undefined and may cause layout shifts in Outlook.

**Duplicate preheader pattern** — a hidden preheader `<div>` ("price drop. mic drop. 🎤") exists inside the body in addition to the pre-body slot. If both render, preview pane text may concatenate or show the wrong string.

**Unexecuted shell template comments (×2):**
```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```
These appear verbatim in the source, meaning the build pipeline did not interpolate the date. Not visible to end users but indicates a template compilation error upstream.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`mi_u=` is empty in all Movable Ink image URLs**, e.g.:
```
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/ff3ed908ea50da06.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=062726_...
```
`mi_u` is the Movable Ink subscriber identifier. When blank, Movable Ink cannot serve personalized or contextually targeted image variants — all recipients receive the default fallback image regardless of targeting rules.

**AMPscript variable block appears to have rendered empty:**
```html
<!--Set variables to support dynamic template attributes-->
</td>
```
The `<td>` that should contain AMPscript `SET` statements for `@APT` and `@NTF_Flag` is empty in the rendered output. If these variables govern send-time product targeting or audience rules, they silently defaulted or were omitted.

**Preheader content block slot unpopulated:**
```html
<!-- <div data-type="slot" data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9" data-label="Preheader"></div> -->
```
The slot is commented out rather than rendered. The fallback hidden `<div>` preheader carries the text, but the slot-driven system did not fire.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA warns this may be a relay capture gap, but if absent at delivery, this email is non-compliant with Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5k/day senders). BBW's volume almost certainly exceeds this threshold.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click machine unsubscribe is required for bulk senders under the same Google/Yahoo policy. Absence here exposes the sender to inbox placement penalties.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown** — `Authentication-Results` header was not captured through the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. Sending domain is `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain); alignment with `bathandbodyworks.com` DMARC policy cannot be verified from available data.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link** — not visible in the truncated HTML provided. Assumed present in the footer (standard for BBW); cannot confirm from this source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Campaign code present in image URLs but not as UTM params:**
```
mi_ecmp=062726_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_799SELCDL_AM_B
```
This is a Movable Ink internal campaign parameter, not a UTM parameter. It tracks image impressions, not clicks to site.

**UTM params on destination URLs are unverifiable** — all 23 click links go through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects that QA skipped. There is no way to confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` are appended to the final landing page URLs from this audit. This is a gap in the QA coverage, not a confirmed bug.
