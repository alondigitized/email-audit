- The subject, the hero copy ("ALL 3-Wick Candles") and the product photo (Snowflakes & Cashmere) all line up.
- "Up to 24 per customer" signals that stock is limited and invites stocking up.

## What's weak

- No end date appears in the body. The sale runs through 11/2, but that is only in the fine print, so there is no credible urgency.
- The $5.95 single-wick block uses the same visual weight as the hero. It splits attention, and its "Shop now" button is a plain outlined box that looks weaker than the hero's.

- 3. Replace "You might like" with candle fragrances: three or four top-sellers with the $10.95 price shown on each. Cut the "feel good formula" block.
- 4. Add a rewards angle, for example "Members get early access" or a stacked perk, so the loyalty links earn their space.
