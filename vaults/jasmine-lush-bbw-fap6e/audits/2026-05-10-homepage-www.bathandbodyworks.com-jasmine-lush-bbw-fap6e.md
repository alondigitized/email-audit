# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I went to open the site and immediately got hit with a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" bot-check modal that's covering almost the entire page. Behind it I can barely make out what looks like a candle with daisies — very my vibe — and a thin banner at the top saying "Gifts for Her, Deals for You." But honestly? I can't shop what I can't see. This is a brick wall as a first impression, and for a brand I visit specifically to hunt new candle drops and Semi-Annual Sale deals, it's a frustrating start.

## What's working

- **"Gifts for Her, Deals for You" announcement bar** — lands for me immediately. I'm always looking for gift sets and deal stacking, so this banner is doing the right thing in the right spot.
- **Candle + daisy hero image (partially visible)** — what little I can see of the product hero looks spring-fresh and on-category for me. The florals and warm packaging are exactly the aesthetic that makes me want to scroll.
- **Clean nav bar** — hamburger, search, account, and bag are all in the right spots and legible despite the chaos below them.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check modal is a full-page conversion killer** — "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" covers the entire shopping experience. As a high-tech-comfort Gen Z shopper, I know what this is, but it's still jarring and it tells me the site may be under stress or overly aggressive with bot protection, which erodes trust.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards visibility** — I'm a My Bath & Body Works Rewards member. There's no points balance, no member-only teaser, nothing to make me feel recognized as a returning customer before I even see the homepage.
- **No concrete offer above the fold** — "Deals for You" is a promise, not a deal. No "25% off" or "BOGO" or "$35 free shipping" is visible. The banner teases but doesn't deliver.
- **Primary CTA is completely absent** — the only button I can tap right now is to prove I'm human, not to shop anything.

## Recommendations

- **Fix or soften the bot-check UX** — if CAPTCHAs are necessary, use an invisible or passive check. A modal that blocks the entire homepage before a single product is seen is a direct bounce driver, especially on mobile.
- **Surface a loyalty balance or welcome-back message in the nav or announcement bar** — even "Hi Jasmine, you have 450 points" in the account icon area would immediately reward returning app users switching to mobile web.
- **Swap "Deals for You" for a real number in the banner** — "BOGO 50% on Body Care — Today Only" beats "Deals for You" every single time.
- **Make the hero CTA visible before the modal fires** — the candle imagery is compelling; if the bot check cleared faster (or passively), I'd be tapping into a scent collection immediately.
