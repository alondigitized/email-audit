# cheers to moisturizing body wash + a deal that brings joy. 🥂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This one actually breaks the mold of the brand's week-long "$3.50 TODAY ONLY" urgency loop — the subject promises "a deal that brings joy" but the hero is a genuinely relevant beat: the moisturizing body wash now in a larger pump size, with a real, clear "Buy 3 Get 1 FREE, lowest-priced item free" offer up top. That's the strongest hero this brand has sent all week — concrete, on-category, and immediately visible without scrolling.
- The problem is everything after the fold. The email pivots hard into The Reserve Collection — home fragrance, diffusers, candles — and just keeps going for six more scroll-screens with zero additional offer, zero urgency, and no second CTA tied to a deal. It reads like two unrelated emails stapled together: a real body-care promo up top, then a long lifestyle-brand homepage tour. The "We think you'll love" product strip near the bottom is the only other concrete, clickable offer, and it's buried after a wall of ambiance copy.
- Net: a strong subject-to-hero match drags down into bloat. Trim the home fragrance section to one screen or cut it, and this jumps from a decent mid-week send to one of the better ones in the recent run.

## What's working

- Hero image directly matches the subject line — moisturizing body wash, new larger pump size — so subject and creative are aligned.
- The offer is stated plainly and is genuinely good: "Buy 3 get 1 FREE | All full-size body care. Lowest-priced item is free." No hunting for fine print like the 2026-09-07 "click to reveal" flash sale.
- Clean, single "Shop now" CTA directly under the offer with clear visual hierarchy.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is intact throughout.
- The "We think you'll love" product carousel gives three specific, named, shoppable products with individual "SHOP" buttons — good secondary click surface.

## What's weak

- The email is enormously long relative to its single offer — six-plus screens of Reserve Collection lifestyle content with no discount, CTA urgency, or pricing attached to any of it.
- No second offer or urgency device reinforces the mid-email or bottom sections — after the top offer, it's pure brand storytelling until the product grid near the very end.
- Visual identity split: the top third feels like a promo email, the middle two-thirds feel like a candle/home-fragrance brand deck — jarring tonal whiplash for someone who opened for body wash.
- "Explore the Collection" and "Shop the Collection" CTAs in the middle are vague verbs+nouns that don't map to any visible price or deal, unlike the sharp top offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Reserve Collection section by at least half — one hero image + one CTA is enough; six screens of ambiance copy for a fragrance line buried mid-email will lose scrollers before they reach the product grid.
- 2. Move the "We think you'll love" product strip up, directly under the fold of the top offer, so there's a second concrete click opportunity while the buy-3-get-1-free hook is still fresh.
- 3. Add a small urgency or deadline element to the top offer ("ends [date]") — right now it reads honest but open-ended, no reason to act today versus next week.
- 4. Give the Reserve Collection section its own micro-offer or "new arrival" badge instead of pure lifestyle copy, so it doesn't feel like a non-sequitur from the promo above it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `buy 3, get 1 free — moisturizing body wash, bigger pump.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your body wash just got an upgrade (and a free one).`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New larger size, same easy-to-use pump. Buy 3, get the cheapest free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `More product, same great scent — plus a deal worth stocking up for.`
