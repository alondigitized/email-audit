# only a few more hours for $2.95. ⏱️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same $2.95 Woodland Evening foaming hand soap hero that Bath & Body Works has now run four times in as many days — 2026-08-13 ("lowest price of the season starts now!"), 2026-08-14 ("$2.95 for a limited time. go ahead and shop."), 2026-08-15/16 in this exact "final hours" framing. Identical product shot, identical price, identical "Just dropped" badge, identical "Lowest price of the season" copy. At this point it's not urgency, it's fatigue — a subscriber who opened even one of the last three sends has already seen this exact creative.
- The email itself is competently built: clear hero, unambiguous price, a real "FINAL HOURS!" banner, and a decent secondary stack (Buy 3 Get 3 Free full-size, then the Nightmare Before Christmas early-access teaser). But the layout buries the more interesting news — the Disney collab, which drove an 8.00/10 on 2026-08-16 as a standalone send — under a fourth rerun of a $2.95 soap that's stopped being novel.
- Judgment: mediocre send, dragged down entirely by repetition, not execution. If this were the first appearance of this creative it'd score meaningfully higher.

## What's working

- Hero price and product are unmistakable — $2.95, one SKU, one CTA button ("SHOP"), no ambiguity.
- "FINAL HOURS!" banner at top plus repeated "FINAL HOURS!" label on the BOGO block gives real, visible urgency.
- Free shipping bar at the very top sets an immediate, low-friction expectation.
- Clean three-tier structure (hero deal → BOGO body/hair → Nightmare Before Christmas teaser) with strong visual separation between blocks.
- Top Offers grid and footer trust signals (100% guaranteed, store locator, social icons) are all rendering cleanly with no visual bugs.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth near-identical $2.95 Woodland Evening hero in four days (2026-08-13, 08-14, 08-15, and now 08-16) — same photo, same badge, same price, same "lowest price of the season" line.
- The genuinely fresh content — Nightmare Before Christmas member-exclusive early access — is relegated to the third content block instead of leading, despite testing 8.00/10 as its own send just today.
- "Lowest price of the season*" carries an asterisk with no visible fine print nearby in the render, which reads slightly bait-y on a claim repeated four times running.
- Subject promises "only a few more hours" but the email offers no actual countdown/clock — just static "FINAL HOURS!" banner text, so the urgency is asserted rather than shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop rerunning the $2.95 soap hero as-is — rotate in a genuinely new hero image/angle if the price point must be repeated again, or retire it for at least a few sends. Repetition is the single biggest score drag here.
- 2. Promote the Nightmare Before Christmas collab to the top of the email — it's the one thing in the last several sends that tested well and hasn't gone stale.
- 3. Add a visible expiration time/countdown next to "FINAL HOURS!" so the subject's urgency has something concrete to anchor to.
- 4. Resolve or drop the "*" on "Lowest price of the season" — either show the qualifier inline or drop the asterisk.
