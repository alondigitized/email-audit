# $2.95 Wallflowers - lowest price of the season* starts today. 🙌
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "$2.95 Wallflowers, lowest price of the season" send already audited at 8.00/10 on 2026-09-17 — a clean, honey-toned hero with the price, the offer, the per-customer limit, and a "Shop now" button all stacked in the first screen. It's the standout of this week's Bath & Body Works run precisely because it commits to one number instead of stacking five offers like the 09-14 through 09-16 sends did.
- Below the fold it does start to sprawl again — a "New scents just dropped" module, a second "Today only" $1.95 car-fragrance/$2.95 diffuser deal with a ROADTRIP promo code and barcode, then a recommendation carousel and brand-values block. None of that undercuts the hero, but it does dilute the single-offer clarity that made the top of this email work.
- Verdict: strong hero, cluttered tail — ship the top half as-is, trim the bottom.

## What's working

- Hero leads with a specific price ($2.95), a superlative claim ("Lowest price of the season*"), and a concrete limit ("Up to 24 per customer") — no ambiguity about the deal.
- "Starts today" badge and a clear "Shop now" button sit right in the first screen, no scrolling required.
- Product shot (Whipped Honey & Vanilla Wallflowers refill) is sharp, on-brand, and matches the honey-drip background — no render bugs.
- A second concrete offer ("Today only," $1.95 car refills / $2.95 diffusers with code ROADTRIP) reinforces urgency without contradicting the hero.

## What's weak

- Two competing "today only" urgency claims (fall event hero + ROADTRIP block) dilute the singular sense of urgency the hero establishes.
- The barcode under the ROADTRIP deal is orphaned with no explanation of how/where to scan it — confusing for an email context.
- "New scents just dropped" module has no price or CTA distinct from the hero, so it reads as filler between two paid offers.
- Generic "We think you'll love" carousel and "Our feel good formula" brand block push actual footer/legal content far down, adding scroll with no incremental offer value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the ROADTRIP block — merge "New scents just dropped" into a single supporting module or drop it; two hero-strength offers plus a recommendation carousel is one section too many.
- 2. Remove or contextualize the orphaned barcode — add "show this barcode in-store" text or drop it if it's not actionable in this channel.
- 3. Differentiate the two "today only" claims — e.g. tag the ROADTRIP deal as "Also today" instead of repeating "Today only" verbatim, so they don't read as duplicate urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$2.95 Wallflowers refills — today's lowest price of the season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Wallflowers Event: $2.95 refills, up to 24 per customer`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $1.95 car fragrance refills with code ROADTRIP, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New fall scents just dropped — shop before the $2.95 deal sells out.`
