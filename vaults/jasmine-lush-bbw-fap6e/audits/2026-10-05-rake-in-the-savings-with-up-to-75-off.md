# 🍂rake in the savings with up to 75% off. 🍁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- The hero is strong. "Fall sale / Up to 75% off / Select items" sits in huge type over a fall-leaf photo, with a "Shop now" button, and the number is the first thing you see. The "Starts today" bar adds urgency. The email then drifts from that promise. The body is almost entirely Home Sweet Holidays content, with no Halloween, no fall scents and no discounted products shown. The subject sells autumn ("rake in the savings 🍂🍁") and the body sells Christmas.
- The mismatch weakens the click. The 75% offer is never tied to a product, price or scent, so "select items" stays vague. The product row at the bottom (White Flare, Champagne Toast, Frosted Cranberry) has no prices and no sale markers. Only the hero button is clearly tied to the sale. The editorial blocks ("A sparkle there, a sprig here", "Classics on hand") are charming but have no CTA.
- Judgement: a good open and an average click. It leads with the offer, then spends the rest of the email on holiday brand content that doesn't deliver on it. In the run of 50%-off sends from 10/02–10/04, this is a new beat with a bigger headline number. It's also a softer one, because "up to" and "select items" replace "50% off the entire store."

## What's working

- The hero puts "75% off" in oversized type and nothing competes with it.
- The "Starts today" banner above the hero gives credible, time-bound framing.
- The "Shop now" button is clear and sits right under the offer.
- The header nav (New arrivals, Top offers, Gifts) and the Rewards strip are tidy, and "Top offers" is a handy shortcut.
- The photography is warm and well styled, and it fits the season.

## What's weak

- The body switches to holiday content ("Merry, bright & early", "Home Sweet Holidays"), which undercuts the "fall sale" hero.
- There are no sale products with prices, so "up to 75% off, select items" never turns into a concrete deal.
- The editorial tiles (candle, soap, teddy) have no visible buttons. Only the final "SHOP THE COLLECTION" has one, and it's thin white text on a photo.
- The "You might like" row is small, shows no prices or discounts, and sits near the bottom.
- The legal block is long and dense, and the sale is "select items" with exclusions, so the offer feels less honest than the headline suggests.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the second block onward with a grid of actual 75%-off items, each with a was/now price and a "Shop" button. That would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Make the body match the hero. Either keep the sale content fall-themed, or move the holiday story below the deals and label it as a separate section.
- 3. Add a named category CTA under the hero, such as "Shop 3-wick candles up to 75% off", instead of generic "Shop now".
- 4. Add real buttons to each editorial tile, and use a solid button on the final holiday banner so it doesn't blend into the photo.
- 5. Move "You might like" up and add prices.
- 6. Name an end date for the sale near the top, since "Starts today" implies one but doesn't give it.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not probed:** 40 tracking links were skipped by QA because they are click-redirect domains, so none were checked for dead or expired redirects.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?KP6ZQSUYXERU7G5JRQJJHXS5UQ.70246&d=70246&bmt=0` is 1x1 with `alt=""`, wrapped in a `width="0" height="0"` table with `mso-hide:all`. This is correct.
- **Third-party redirect domain:** One asset uses `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_HOLIDAYS_AM_BAL_FULL_V…`. QA flagged it as an image missing alt text, but its identifier is a link URL, not an image filename. The truncated output doesn't show whether it is an `<img>` or an `<a>` wrapping one.
- **Domain mix:** The email uses `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which align with the From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. It also uses the unaligned `everestengagement.com`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 confirmed images):** All five are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  With images blocked, nothing renders in their place. If these carry promo content, screen readers get nothing.
- **Content outside `<body>`:** `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` elements, with `<style>` and MSO XML inside them, sit in `<head>`. More wrapper tables sit between `</head>` and `<body>`. Parsers will hoist or reorder this content, and Gmail and Outlook may handle it inconsistently.
- **Invalid table nesting:** `<table … style="width: 600px;"><!-- Header LOGO --><table …>` puts a `<table>` directly inside a `<table>`, with no `<tr><td>` between them. Browsers repair this, but Outlook's Word engine may not.
- **Font `@import` is ineffective:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a font Google Fonts doesn't host, so the request fails. `@import` is also stripped by Gmail and most webmail. The `font-family` stack does fall back to Helvetica/Arial. A second `@import` for Source Sans Pro sits in its own `<style>` block, so the email makes two external font requests.
- **Redundant declarations:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="…UTF-8">` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - The `OfficeDocumentSettings` XML appears twice, under `<!--[if mso]>` and `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`.
  - The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with HTML5 `<meta charset>`.
- **Title:** `<title>Bath &amp; Body Works </title>` is generic and has a trailing space.
- **Responsive classes:** The media queries target `.email-container`, `.mobile-padding` and `.logo-image`. The outer tables visible in the excerpt use inline `style="width: 600px;"` and don't carry `email-container`, so the responsive rule may not apply to them. This can't be confirmed because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA attributes this to AgentMail relay capture, so it is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Check the raw message headers to confirm.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender and click domains are aligned subdomains of `bathandbodyworks.com`, which is consistent with proper authentication, but this is not proof.
- The physical address and unsubscribe link in the body footer are in the truncated section, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links route through redirectors, and the final destinations and UTM parameters were not resolved. The only campaign identifier visible is the Everest tracking parameter `c=100526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_HOLIDAYS_AM_BAL_FULL_V…`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 flagged images. Use `alt=""` only if they are purely decorative.
2. Move the `<style>` blocks and MSO conditionals into a single `<head>`. Close `</head>` before any table, and put all tables inside `<body>`.
3. Wrap the nested `<table>` in `<tr><td>` to fix the invalid nesting.
4. Remove the `Helvetica+Neue` Google Fonts `@import`. Use `<link>` or `@font-face` for Source Sans Pro, with the existing system-font fallback.
5. Drop the duplicate `OfficeDocumentSettings` block and the redundant charset meta.
6. Verify the `bbwus.everestengagement.com` asset: whether it is an `<img>` or an `<a>`, and whether it needs alt text.
7. Capture the raw `.eml` headers directly (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
8. Resolve the redirect chains (a follow-redirects check) to confirm final landing pages and UTM values.
