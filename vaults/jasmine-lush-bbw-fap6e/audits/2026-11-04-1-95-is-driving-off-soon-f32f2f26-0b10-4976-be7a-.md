# 🚘 $1.95 is driving off soon.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led Bath & Body Works send. The $1.95 car fragrance refill and $2.95 hanging diffuser sit in a big "Sleigh the holiday road trip" hero, under a "Final hours" bar. The eye lands on the price first, and the subject's car emoji and "driving off" pun match the hero. It's the same car theme as yesterday's "🚗 get in, shop $1.95 today only."
- Below the hero it gets crowded. The $10 single-wick block repeats the 11/2 and 11/3 sends ("put a bow on it," "$10 single wicks"), so a regular reader has seen this offer three times this week. Then come a "Classic wonder" hero with no offer and a row of three small products. This is the third day in a row of "$1.95 / $10" promos with a fresh pun on top, and the subject's urgency wears thin.
- The render also has large dead white gaps: under "Classic wonder," above the footer, and in the lower footer. They look like missing content or images that didn't load. Overall it's a solid but repetitive 6-7 email.

## What's working

- The $1.95 price is huge and unmissable, and it sits next to a $2.95 second price point.
- "Final hours" bars on the first two heroes give a credible deadline.
- The subject pun lines up with the "road trip" hero, so subject and creative feel like one idea.
- The "Shop now" buttons are clearly outlined and the hero imagery is on-brand.
- The "Ready for the feel good? Join Rewards" banner and the "More to love" block give members a way in.

## What's weak

- Big blank white gaps in the body and footer read as broken or missing content.
- The $10 single-wick block is a repeat of the 11/2 and 11/3 sends, so it adds no news.
- "Classic wonder" has no price, no offer and no CTA. The tiny product row under it is hard to read.
- The fine print ("Up to 24 per customer with code MERRYMILES") is tiny and the promo code is easy to miss.
- The "Our feel good formula" and "Learn more" block has low-contrast text and adds nothing to the sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the blank gaps and make sure every image block loads. The empty space makes the whole email look unfinished.
- 2. Cut the repeated $10 wick block, or replace it with something new. After three days it adds nothing.
- 3. Put the promo code in a visible button or badge next to "Shop now," for example "Shop now · code MERRYMILES."
- 4. Give "Classic wonder" a price or a reason to click, or drop it.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop $1.95 refills," instead of "Shop now."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with dark-mode CSS and a Outlook (MSO) fallback. The QA data shows 6 images with no alt text and 1 image served over plain `http://`. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings can't be confirmed, because the relay may have stripped those headers. The HTML source was also truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 42 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure image request:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTAs...` is loaded over HTTP. This is Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content. Mail clients that block mixed content may not render it, and the request can be intercepted or modified in transit. It should be `https://`.
- **Third-party tracking domain:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=110426_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_195CD295HD_FH_BAL_G6M_V...` is flagged as an image without alt text. It looks like a tracking or dynamic-content asset, so it should at least carry `alt=""`.

- **`Authentication-Results`:** It wasn't captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be verified from this data.
