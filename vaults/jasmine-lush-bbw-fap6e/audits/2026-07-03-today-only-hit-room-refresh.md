# today only: hit room refresh. 🔄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works pivots off yesterday's $1 PocketBac blockbuster (scored 9/10) into a room-spray anchor today — $3.50 All Concentrated Room Sprays is a solid, giftable deal heading into July 4th weekend. The subject line is crisp, the price is real, and the seasonal palette reads summer. But the email can't decide what it's selling: room sprays, 3-wick candles, and foaming soaps each get a full hero treatment, and the result is three emails stapled together rather than one focused send. "Today only" has appeared in nearly every B&BW send for three-plus weeks; the urgency signal has real credibility erosion at this point.
- The room spray angle is the right call — it's a distinct category from yesterday's PocketBac and it maps cleanly to home-fragrance gifting before the holiday. The execution just buries the lead under competing offers. A buyer who came in for a $3.50 spray exits confused about whether to also grab candles at $13.50 or soaps at $5.
- Overall: the bones are there, the campaign logic is sound, but the email is structurally over-stuffed for a "today only" send.

## What's working

- Subject line is clean and specific — "today only: hit room refresh. 🔄" lands under 35 chars, zero spam signals, and the category is clear.
- $3.50 room spray price anchor is a genuinely strong value play and shows prominently in the hero.
- Summer palette (light blues, coral pinks, ice cream imagery) is seasonally on-point for July 4th weekend.
- Multiple price tiers ($3.50 / $13.50 / $5) give the email a range of entry points.
- Render is clean — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breakage visible.

## What's weak

- Three full hero sections (room sprays, candles, soaps) compete equally for attention — no single offer wins.
- "TODAY ONLY" and "LIMITED TIME ONLY" labels appear on nearly every module; after three-plus weeks of this cadence, the urgency is worn down.
- SHOP CTAs are single-word generic; no noun attached ("Shop $3.50 Sprays" would close the loop the subject opened).
- No specific scent names, count, or product breadth signal — a buyer doesn't know if their scent is included.
- No loyalty/Rewards member mention despite B&BW having an active rewards program.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to one hero.** Cut the candle and soap heroes to a secondary row below the fold. The subject promises "room refresh" — the email should deliver exactly that and one CTA, then upsell below.
- 2. **Strengthen the CTA copy.** "Shop $3.50 Sprays" over "SHOP" — verb + noun closes the loop.
- 3. **Add product depth in the hero.** "50+ scents, all concentrated, $3.50 each — today only" eliminates the "but is my scent available?" hesitation that kills conversion.
- 4. **Bring in a Rewards hook.** "Earn points on every spray" or "My Offers members: extra 10% off" differentiates subscribers from casual visitors.
- 5. **Rest the "today only" banner.** After 3+ weeks of daily urgency, one email using a soft deadline framing ("stock runs low, price goes back up Saturday") would feel more credible than the sixth "TODAY ONLY!" in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$3.50 room sprays: all-day deal, all the scents 🏠`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your whole home smells good for $3.50 — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All Concentrated Room Sprays, 50+ scents — grab yours before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fill every room for $3.50. Candles and soaps deals below too.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Duplicate `@import` for Google Fonts** — `Source Sans Pro` is imported twice: once in `<head>` (weight 400 only) and again inside an in-body `<style>` block (weights 400 and 700). The bold weight is only loaded in the second import, meaning `<head>`-only clients will render bold as fallback system font.

**Unresolved template artifact** — the comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` appears twice in the source (once before `</head>`, once immediately after). This is a shell command template expression that was never substituted during build. It renders as an HTML comment and is invisible to users, but indicates a template compilation gap.

**`<meta charset>` placement** — `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` is positioned at the end of `<head>` rather than as the first child. Some legacy parsers begin character detection before reaching it; charset declaration should be the first element in `<head>`.

**No `<meta name="viewport">` tag** — mobile layout relies entirely on `@media (max-width:599px)`. Adding a viewport meta is standard practice and required by some clients to activate media queries reliably.

**Five images missing alt text (QA confirmed):**

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

AMPscript APT (Abandonment/Promo Targeting) logic is referenced via HTML comments but not rendered in the truncated source — expected behavior for server-side AMPscript.

**Silent failure risk flagged in template comments:**
```html
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
```
For NTF (New-to-File) campaigns where the flag is `"N"`, APT is expected to come from the email template with no fallback. If the email-level APT variable is unset or misspelled, the personalization silently fails with no error surfacing to the sender.

No unresolved `%%variable%%` tokens visible in source. Subscriber ID `200511831233` is populated correctly in both tracking pixels.
