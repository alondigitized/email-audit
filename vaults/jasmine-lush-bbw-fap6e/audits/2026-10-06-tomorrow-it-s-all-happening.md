# tomorrow, it's 👏 all 👏happening.👏
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a "tomorrow" teaser, and a good one in places. Five full-bleed red holiday tiles each lead with a huge price: $12.95 3-wick candles, $4.95 body care, $5.95 single-wick candles, $3.50 foaming hand soaps, $3.50 Wallflowers refills. Nothing competes with the numbers, and the nutcracker and Fresh Balsam product shots make the season obvious. You know what's coming in about two seconds.
- The weakness is that it's a pre-announcement. Every button says "Start your list" and nothing is live yet. After the 10/02 to 10/04 "50% off the entire store" run and the 10/05 "up to 75% off" send, a recipient has already seen four days of "we NEVER do this" emails. A subject that says "it's all happening" with three clap emojis tells them nothing about what is happening. The email is strong, but the subject line wastes it.
- Overall: well-built, price-forward creative with a vague, repetitive subject and a five-tile scroll that has no single hero. Solid, but not a standout.

## What's working

- Price-first hierarchy: each tile leads with a big dollar figure, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The $12.95 3-wick candle tile is the top block, so the hero offer is visible without scrolling.
- The "Tomorrow only" and "Starts tomorrow" labels create credible, specific urgency. Each tile also marks "Online exclusive" or "In-store & online."
- Seasonal, on-brand photography (nutcracker and balsam packaging, red gift backdrop) fits the holiday moment and the brand voice.
- The free-shipping-at-$50 bar and the Rewards banner sit above the hero without crowding it.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical red tiles with the same "Start your list" button give no single hero and no clear priority. The scroll feels repetitive.
- "Start your list" is a soft, ambiguous CTA. It doesn't say what the list is or what you get, and it asks for commitment to a sale that isn't live yet.
- The outlined buttons sit on busy photos. In the single-wick candle tile the button crowds the product image and loses contrast.
- Tiles link to categories ("Select body care," "Single wick candles"), not to a specific product. No ratings or reviews appear anywhere.
- The fine print is a long block of dense text with item limits and exclusions. The offers read as honest at the top, but the conditions are buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject so it names the offer. The current one says nothing, and the offer is the best thing in the email.
- 2. Put one hero tile on top and shrink the other four to a compact grid. Lead with the single best price point, such as the $3.50 hand soaps or the $12.95 candles.
- 3. Change the CTA copy to a verb plus a payoff, such as "Shop tomorrow's deals" or "Remind me at 6 AM." Add a one-line countdown or a live-time cue.
- 4. Make the buttons solid-filled with a contrast color so they read on every photo.
- 5. Add a social-proof line under the hero, such as a star rating or "Fan-favorite scent." Link tiles to specific products, not categories.
- 6. Add a short "limit/exclusions" note near the offers so the honest-offer signal holds without scrolling to the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `starts tomorrow 6 AM: our best candle & soap prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Set your list now: 3-wick candles $12.95, body care $4.95, and more. Online exclusive.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh Balsam, Frosted Cranberry and more. Shop early, free shipping at $50.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Bath & Body Works, "tomorrow, it's 👏 all 👏happening.👏"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget-style) template with valid-looking tracking infrastructure on the `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` subdomain. It has malformed document structure, 6 images flagged for missing alt text, and an unverifiable header and authentication picture because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs were not inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (`click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`), so destinations, UTM parameters and redirect health are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- The open pixel is well-formed: `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?...&bmt=0`, 1×1, `alt=""`, wrapped in a `mso-hide:all` table.
- A third-party tracker/image is present at `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=100526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_12953W_PREV_BAL_G6M_V1`. It is a different domain from the sending and click domains and carries a per-recipient ID (`e=`). It also carries an internal campaign/segment code in the URL (`c=...PREV_BAL_G6M_V1`). That code is visible to any recipient who inspects the source.
- The `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` request almost certainly fails, because "Helvetica Neue" does not appear to be a Google Fonts family. It adds a render-blocking external request in clients that support `@import`. Many clients, including Gmail, ignore it.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** The QA check flagged these PNGs on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, any content in them is lost. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`.
- **A sixth image is also missing alt text:** the `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/...` asset above. If it is a tracking pixel, it needs `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - `<table>` elements, `<style>` blocks and MSO conditional comments sit inside `<head>`, before `</head>`.
  - More tables and comments (`<!-- Begin variables -->`, `<!-- Begin Preheader and Header -->`) sit between `</head>` and `<body>`.
  - `<body>` therefore opens after content has already started. Browsers and some clients will auto-correct this inconsistently, and Outlook-Word rendering is the highest risk.
- **Mixed doctype and charset declarations.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE is paired with both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" ...>` and the HTML5 `<meta charset="utf-8">`. This is redundant but harmless.
- **The `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` block is duplicated** in two conditional comments (`[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]`). This is redundant but harmless.
- The `lang="en"` attribute is present, tables use `role="presentation"`, and the preheader is hidden correctly with padding characters (`&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; ...`). No issues there.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The subject and preheader (`hint: prime time to save on gifts. 🤭`) render as plain text. The remainder of the body was truncated and could not be checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself notes the relay may be stripping them. It is **unconfirmed** whether the original message lacks them. Verify against the raw message at the origin MTA or in a direct-to-Gmail test.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected limitation of the AgentMail relay and not evidence of a failure.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the HTML is truncated.
- The From address is on the aligned subdomain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which matches the click and image domains. That is a positive signal for alignment.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page URLs **cannot be assessed.** All 42 tracked links resolve through redirect domains that were skipped.
- The only visible campaign identifier is the Everest `c=` code, not a UTM set.
- No issues are confirmable from the available data.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 5 content images, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. Add `alt=""` to the Everest asset.
2. Fix the document structure. Move the `<table>` wrappers, `<style>` blocks and comments that sit between `<head>`/`</head>` and `<body>` into the body, or into a valid head. Retest in Outlook (Word engine).
3. Remove or replace the `@import` for the non-existent "Helvetica Neue" Google Font. The existing `font-family` fallback stack is sufficient.
4. Re-run the QA with a probe that follows the redirects on a sample of the 42 `click.e2...` links, and record the final landing URLs and UTMs.
5. Capture the raw message headers at the origin, not only through the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass before treating compliance as clean.
6. Pull the untruncated HTML and re-audit the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Fix the QA summary. It reports "0 issue(s), 3 warning(s)" at a 57% pass rate, but the output lists 9 warnings, so the counts are inconsistent.
