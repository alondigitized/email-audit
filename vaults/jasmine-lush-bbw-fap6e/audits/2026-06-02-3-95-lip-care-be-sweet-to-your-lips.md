# $3.95 lip care—be sweet to your lips. 💋
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Bath & Body Works opens a new product arc on June 2 — pivoting cleanly from the single-wick candle launch (June 1, 9/10) and the hand-sanitizer run (May 30) into lip care. The $3.95 price point is sharp, the "TODAY ONLY!" urgency banner leads, and the in-store barcode with code SWEET is a practical, subscriber-friendly touch. This is a well-structured category pivot.
- The second section — Coastal Mood Lighting at $10 — is smart cross-sell real estate, carrying forward yesterday's candle arc into the same email. But it also dilutes focus: this is now a two-deal email with a grid of eight category tiles underneath, and all three layers compete for attention below the fold.
- Clear judgement: the lip care hero executes well, but the email packs too much in. A dedicated single-offer send would have been stronger.

## What's working

- **"TODAY ONLY!" red banner leads hard** — zero ambiguity about urgency, visible above the product imagery.
- **$3.95 price badge is prominent and legible** — the offer requires no decoding.
- **In-store barcode + code SWEET** — smart omnichannel execution; most brands skip this.
- **Coastal Mood Lighting secondary slot** — clean way to extend yesterday's $10 single-wick launch (June 1) without a separate send.
- **Fresh category** — after a week of candles, sanitizers, and flash sales, lip care reads as genuinely new and earns the open.

## What's weak

- **Two distinct deals below the fold** — Coastal Mood Lighting undercuts the lip care focus; the eye doesn't know which deal to commit to.
- **"SHOP" CTA is category-generic** — both primary and secondary CTAs say the same thing; no reinforcement of the specific offer.
- **Top Offers grid adds a third layer of noise** — eight product tiles immediately after two sale sections creates decision fatigue.
- **No loyalty or member pricing visible** — B&BW has a loyalty program; surfacing member-exclusive value here would reward repeat openers.
- **"Limit 24" is undersold** — a 24-item limit is actually generous for a $3.95 deal; the fine print buries a feature that should build confidence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Single-deal focus** — move the Coastal Mood Lighting section to a separate send (or a smaller footer callout). The lip care arc deserves a clean send.
- 2. **Sharpen the CTA** — replace "SHOP" with "Shop $3.95 Lip Care" on the primary button.
- 3. **Promote the limit** — reframe "Limit 24" as "Stock up — up to 24 per customer" in the hero copy to signal generosity, not restriction.
- 4. **Surface loyalty value** — add one line: "Members get early access — sign in to unlock."
- 5. **Collapse the grid** — remove or shrink the 8-tile TOP OFFERS block; it fragments the send into a catalog browse rather than a deals moment.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "$3.95 Lip Care" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

SFMC/ExactTarget deployment with malformed document structure (content rendered before the `<body>` tag), multiple template build artifacts leaked into production source, and 4 images with alt text violations. The AgentMail relay did not surface List-Unsubscribe or authentication headers, leaving RFC 8058 and deliverability posture unverifiable.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **25 click-wrapped links** via `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` — all destinations hidden behind redirect; UTM parameter presence on landing pages **cannot be verified from source alone**.
- **Open pixel** `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?E42VZZOT3CME3O4MYLJ6AWXL2Y.70252...` — present, 1×1, `alt=""` — acceptable.
- **Third-party engagement pixel** (`bbwus.everestengagement.com`) — `alt` attribute is incorrectly set to a truncated URL string (`https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=2005118`) rather than `""`. QA confirms this as a flagged image.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **No `<meta name="viewport">` tag** — mobile layout depends entirely on the `@media (max-width:599px)` fallback. Without the viewport meta, some mobile clients will not trigger the responsive breakpoint.
- **Content rendered before `<body>` tag** — the preheader `<table>` and open pixel `<table>` both appear in the DOM *before* the `<body>` tag opens. Invalid per HTML spec; most clients recover silently, but it is a structural defect.
- **Charset meta placement** — `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` appears mid-`<head>` after `<style>` and MSO XML blocks. Per spec it should be the **first child of `<head>`** to ensure encoding is declared before any content is parsed.
- **4 images with alt text violations** (QA confirmed):
  - `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` — `alt` is set to the raw filename, not a descriptive string.
  - **2 images with empty `src` attributes** — will render as broken image icons in all clients.
  - `bbwus.everestengagement.com` pixel — `alt` set to a truncated URL (see §2 above).
- **Google Fonts `@import`** (`Source+Sans+Pro`) — blocked by Gmail and Outlook; silently falls back to `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. Not a failure, but any layout dimension relying on the web font will render differently in the majority of clients.
- **Preheader padding volume** — the hidden `<div>` uses an extremely high density of `&#847;`, `&zwnj;`, `&#8199;`, and `&shy;` characters (60+ repetitions). Aggressive spam heuristics on some gateways may score this negatively.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Internal AMPscript variable comments exposed in sent source:**
  ```html
  <!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
  <!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->
  <!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
  ```
  Variable names `@NTF_Flag` and `@APT` and campaign logic are visible to any recipient who views source.
- **SFMC drag-and-drop builder slot artifact** left in production HTML:
  ```html
  <!-- <div data-type="slot" data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9" data-label="Preheader"></div> -->
  ```
- **Unexecuted shell command comment** appears **twice**:
  ```html
  <!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
  ```
  The literal backtick expression is present in the rendered source — a build-time substitution that was never executed. This exposes the template versioning scheme.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All 25 destination URLs are fully click-wrapped — there are **no visible inline UTM parameters** in any `href` before redirect. If the `click.e2` redirect layer drops parameters, there is no fallback.
- Campaign identifier in the Everest pixel URL: `c=060226_RDM_LIP_395LIPEE_NS_BAL_V1` — encodes date (06/02/26), segment (RDM), campaign (LIP), offer (395LIP), and variant (V1). UTM naming alignment against GA4 should be audited against this convention.
- Cannot confirm landing page offer alignment (e.g., $3.95 pricing surfaced on destination) without resolving at least one redirect chain.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix the 2 broken `src` attributes** — identify missing asset URLs and restore before next send; these render as broken image icons in all clients.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to content images; set `alt=""` explicitly on all tracking pixels.
3. **Verify List-Unsubscribe headers at MTA layer** via raw header dump (not relay) — RFC 2369 + RFC 8058 compliance is required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk senders.
4. **Strip all template artifacts** from the production send pipeline: remove `@NTF_Flag`/`@APT` logic comments, the SFMC slot comment, and the two unexecuted `` `date "+ET%m.%y"` `` comments.
5. **Add `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">`** as the first meta in `<head>`.
6. **Move the charset meta** to first position in `<head>`, before `<style>` and MSO XML.
7. **Resolve a sample redirect chain** from `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` to confirm UTM parameters survive and reach the landing page analytics layer.
8. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC** via direct MTA header inspection — the relay gap means authentication posture is currently unknown for this send.
