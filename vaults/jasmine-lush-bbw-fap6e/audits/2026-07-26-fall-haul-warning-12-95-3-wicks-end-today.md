# ⚠️ fall haul warning: $12.95 3-wicks end today.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the closer on B&BW's $12.95 3-Wick Candle push — the same event that opened with "set your alarms" on 7/23, launched with "get glowing" the same day, got a retention nudge on 7/24, then two more "final hours" sends on 7/24 and 7/25. By the time this one lands, it's at least the sixth touch on an identical price point in four days, and the fatigue shows even though the individual send is well-built.
- The render itself is clean and hierarchy is textbook: hero candle, $12.95 in giant type, single SHOP button, no scrolling required to get the offer. But the email doesn't stop there — it stacks a Buy 3 Get 3 Free body-care push, a second "Why Pick Our 3-Wicks" candle block, a 75% off summer sale banner, and a nine-tile "Top Offers" grid underneath. That's four distinct offers competing for one click, which dilutes the "ends today" urgency the subject line promised.
- There's also a seasonal mismatch worth flagging: the hero copy is "Fall for the Glow" with autumn leaves and a pumpkin-toned candle, sent July 26. That's a full campaign built around fall imagery running in the middle of summer, right next to a "Summer Sale 75% off" banner two sections down — the email is visually arguing with itself about what season it is.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unmissable: candle image, $12.95, single "SHOP" CTA, no scroll needed on mobile.
- "ENDS TODAY!" banner right under the top nav reinforces urgency immediately, matching the subject line.
- Buy 3 Get 3 Free block uses a distinct blue banner and product lineup, giving the email a genuine second reason to click beyond the candle.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- "We Just Want You To Love It! 100% Guaranteed" footer badge reinforces trust before the legal fine print.

## What's weak

- Fall-themed hero ("Fall for the Glow," autumn leaves, pumpkin candle) sent in late July directly contradicts the "Summer Sale 75% off" banner four sections later — the email doesn't know what season it's in.
- Four separate offers (3-Wicks, Buy 3 Get 3, Summer Sale 75%, Top Offers grid) crammed into one send dilutes the "ends today" urgency that's supposed to be the whole point.
- The "Why Pick Our 3-Wicks?" section repeats the same $12.95/candle message already delivered in the hero — redundant real estate that could've promoted something new.
- This is the sixth near-identical touch on the same $12.95 3-Wick event since 7/23 (per the brand history); a subscriber who's opened even two of these has seen this exact price and product before.
- No visible strikethrough or "was $16.50" reference next to $12.95, so the discount isn't demonstrated on-screen — it just asserts a price.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to one offer.** Kill the redundant second 3-Wick block and push Buy 3 Get 3 and the Summer Sale into separate sends — a true "ends today" email should have one hero, one CTA, one urgency beat, not four competing offers.
- 2. **Fix the seasonal clash.** Either commit to "Fall for the Glow" fully (drop the Summer Sale banner from this send) or drop the fall styling in July — don't run both in the same scroll.
- 3. **Show the discount, not just the price.** Add a struck-through reference price next to $12.95 so the "deal" is visually provable, not just asserted.
- 4. **Retire or reframe this campaign for the next send.** Six touches on the same $12.95 offer in four days is past the point of urgency and into fatigue — the next email should either be a genuinely new product or explicitly the "actually last call" with no more sequels.
- 5. **Give the CTA more teeth.** "SHOP" repeated 10+ times is generic — swap the hero CTA for "Shop $12.95 Candles" to reinforce the price at the point of action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `last call: $12.95 3-wicks gone tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `still $12.95? not for long — 3-wicks end today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 3, get 3 free on body care too — both deals end at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your fall candle stash starts here. Shop before the price goes back up.`
