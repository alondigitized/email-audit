# ✨ ✨ something really big starts tomorrow. ✨ ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest send in the brand's recent history — "Fall Candle Event, lowest price of the season, $11.95 all 3-wick candles" is a genuine, unambiguous, high-value offer, and it's built as a proper pre-launch teaser ("Starts tomorrow") rather than another "$3.50 TODAY ONLY" urgency-stack. The hero shot — a lit Leaves candle against a moody fall backdrop with scattered leaves — is the best-produced visual of the run, and the copy hierarchy (event name → price anchor → CTA) is completely clean, no clutter.
- Compared to 2026-09-06's pumpkin Wallflowers "$3.50, today only" (7.00/10) and 2026-09-07's flash-sale mystery-offer trick (5.00/10), this email trades gimmick for substance: a real category-wide price drop on a core product line, disclosed plainly with an asterisk and full terms at the bottom rather than buried in a "click to reveal" wall. The only real knock is that it's a teaser for tomorrow, not today — so the CTA ("Start your list") is soft-commitment rather than a direct buy, and today's click doesn't convert to today's purchase.
- Strong send, clear win for a candle-buying persona. Ship it as-is; the main opportunity is just tightening the subject/preview for inbox competitiveness.

## What's working

- Clear, singular offer stated in large type: "$11.95 All 3-Wick Candles, Lowest price of the season" — no ambiguity.
- "Starts tomorrow" framed as a countdown-style teaser banner, which builds anticipation without resorting to fake urgency.
- Hero image is genuinely well-art-directed — warm lighting, seasonal leaf styling, product label legible.
- "Up to 24 per customer" signals genuine stock-up value and reads as generous, not restrictive.
- Clean secondary "We think you'll love" cross-sell row (Wallflowers refill, hand soap, sanitizer) reinforces the fall scent line without overwhelming the primary offer.
- No render bugs — clean modular layout end to end.

## What's weak

- Primary CTA "Start your list" is a soft ask, not a purchase action, since the sale isn't live yet — likely to have a lower same-session conversion than a live "Shop now."
- No specific end date/time for the event visible in the hero (only in bottom fine print) — a visible deadline once live would add urgency.
- Sale is 3-wick candles only; single-wick or mini candle buyers get no callout here.
- "Our Feel Good Formula" ingredient-trust module and "More to love" block add length without advancing the offer — could be trimmed for a teaser email specifically.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero's specificity — right now it teases "something really big" but the email itself names the offer clearly; don't hide the ball in the inbox.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or exact start time ("6AM ET tomorrow") to the hero banner, not just in fine print, to convert teaser urgency into a calendar-worthy moment.
- 3. Consider a secondary CTA like "Set a reminder" alongside "Start your list" for users who want a nudge tomorrow rather than browsing today.
- 4. Trim the "Feel Good Formula" module for this specific send — it dilutes a teaser email whose only job is to get people to come back tomorrow.
