# last chance: buy one candle, get one free ends today. 🕯️🕯️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- B&BW lands a textbook "last chance" closer on their candle BOGO — and this is one of their cleaner hero executions this week. The "First Flicker of Fall" framing ties urgency to a seasonal story rather than just stamping "ENDS TODAY" on a blank background. The candle BOGO leads cleanly, the subject is specific, and the render holds. If you came into this email looking for a reason to buy a candle, it gives you one in the first two seconds.
- The problem is everything that follows the hero. A B3G3 full-size body care block and a 75% Summer Sale compete directly with the candle story, reproducing the same cluttered three-offer stack B&BW has deployed in every send since July 8. The Summer Sale block in particular creates a seasonal schizophrenia — "First Flicker of Fall" above the fold, "Summer Sale" two scrolls later. Neither offer can breathe.
- This is the seventh B&BW email in four days and the fourth with an "ends today / last chance" mechanism. The subject line is doing its job. The brand's send cadence is working against it.

## What's working

- **Hero block is focused**: "First Flicker of Fall" + "All Candles BUY 1, GET 1 FREE" + "SHOP" reads in a single glance; the autumn candle imagery reinforces the seasonal story.
- **Seasonal seeding is smart**: Pivoting to fall in mid-July is B&BW's established playbook, and "First Flicker of Fall" is more evocative than a plain discount stamp.
- **Subject is the strongest element**: "last chance: buy one candle, get one free ends today." — specific offer, clear deadline, no vagueness.
- **Both hero blocks carry "ENDS TODAY" stamps**: The deadline is never ambiguous for either the candle or body care offer.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breakage visible.

## What's weak

- **Three competing offers dilute the hero**: B3G3 body care and the 75% Summer Sale are immediate attention competitors — a subscriber might scroll past candles entirely and land on the wrong category.
- **"SHOP" is a limp CTA across all three blocks**: Generic, undifferentiated, and does zero work to reinforce the specific deal.
- **No scent hero**: The fall candle image is decorative, not persuasive — no scent name, no specific product, nothing to want. "All Candles" is a category, not a reason to act.
- **Summer Sale undercuts the fall story**: Pivoting to discounted summer inventory within the same scroll as "First Flicker of Fall" creates a seasonal identity conflict.
- **Cadence fatigue is real**: Four "ends today" emails in four days. This is the July 10 morning → July 10 evening → July 11 → July 12 urgency loop again, just on candles instead of Wallflowers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **One offer, full commitment**: Cut the B3G3 body care and Summer Sale from this send. The 75% Summer Sale has been in every email since July 8 — it can live in its own dedicated send or rest for a day. The candle BOGO deserves the full page.
- 2. **Replace "SHOP" with specific CTA copy**: "Get My Free Candle" or "Shop the BOGO" — something anchored to the action, not just a department link.
- 3. **Name a scent in the hero**: Show one specific fall scent with its name and a one-line descriptor. Give the recipient something to covet, not a category to browse.
- 4. **Rest the urgency cadence after this send**: If a new deal launches tomorrow (as it has every day since July 9), "last chance" loses all credibility. B&BW is spending down the trust it built with these words.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free candle expires tonight 🕯️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First Flicker of Fall — free candle, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy any candle, get one free. All scents, no exclusions — ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall's first candle drop: two for the price of one, today only.`

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works B1G1 Candle Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with no structural errors, but three areas need attention: missing authentication header visibility through the relay, unresolvable List-Unsubscribe headers, and several images lacking alt text including one with an empty `src`.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**24 click-redirect links not probed.** All CTAs route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, which the QA relay correctly skipped. No broken destination URLs can be confirmed or ruled out from this audit alone.

**Open pixel present and functional:**
```
https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?GUT3UBNAQB5U5OCOKZMC2IQPLA.70252&d=70252&bmt=0
```
Standard 1×1 ExactTarget open tracker — no issue, but alt text is empty (expected for tracking pixels).

**Everest engagement pixel also present:**
```
https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=&c=071226_EM_...
```
The `e=` parameter (likely subscriber ID) appears **unpopulated** — this would silently break Everest engagement attribution for all recipients if not resolved at send time by AMPscript substitution.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**5 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA):

| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| `e06e6d949b43db77.png` (via `mi.bathandbodyworks.com`) | No `alt` |
| `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` | No `alt` |
| Everest pixel `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/` | No `alt` (tracking pixel — acceptable but should be `alt=""`) |
| 2× images with empty `src` attributes | No `alt` **and** no `src` — these will produce broken image icons in most clients |

**Two images have empty `src`** — these are likely conditional content blocks that weren't properly suppressed. They'll render as broken image icons in Outlook and Gmail.

**`<meta>` charset tag placement:** The `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" charset=utf-8>` is placed after `</head>` opens but *after* two `<style>` blocks and conditional comments — technically valid for HTML 4.01 Transitional but best practice is first child of `<head>`.

**Dual `@import` for Google Fonts** — `Source+Sans+Pro` is imported twice (once in `<head>`, once inside a nested `<style>` tag in the body). Redundant but harmless; blocked by Gmail anyway.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript variables referenced in comments but not visible in truncated source:**
```html
<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email-->
```
The `@NTF_Flag` and `@APT` variables must be validated at send time. No unrendered `%%variable%%` tokens visible in the provided HTML.

**Everest pixel `e=` param is blank** (see §2) — if this is a merge token, it was not substituted in the captured source.

**`mi_u=` param on the MIA tracking URL is also empty:**
```
https://mi.bathandbodyworks.com/p/rp/e06e6d949b43db77.png?mi_u=&mi_ecmp=071226_...
```
`mi_u` is the subscriber identifier for MIA (Movable Ink Activator). An empty value means personalized/dynamic image content will not render correctly — the fallback static image will show for all recipients.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** by relay. This is likely present in the actual SMTP envelope (ExactTarget adds it) but was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance from this audit — should be verified via raw header inspection on a live send.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected.** One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) as of Feb 2024. If SFMC is not generating this header, it is a deliverability risk with major ISPs. Confirm in send logs.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown. Domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` should have both SPF and DKIM configured; this must be verified directly.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not visible in truncated source. Must be present in the email footer.

**Unsubscribe link** — not visible in truncated source. Must be present and functional.

**Campaign code `071226_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_B1G1ALLCDL_AM_BAL_FULL_V1`** is embedded in image tracking URLs, confirming campaign tagging is in place at the image level.

If UTM parameters are appended post-redirect by ExactTarget, they should be validated in a test send to confirm `utm_campaign`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_source` are present on landing pages.
