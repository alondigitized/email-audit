# get ready, $3.50 is today only...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean "today only" send, and the $3.50 Wallflowers refill is a strong lead. The hero is a dark banner reading "Today only", then "Get a jump on merry" over a big white **$3.50**. It has a "Shop now" button and a retro red oven photo. The eye lands on the price first. The code MERRYMOOD and the 24-per-customer limit sit in small type under the price.
- The body is crowded. Below the hero, the email stacks the $5.95 fragrance mist deal ("Limited time"), a festive 3-wick candle tile with no price, and a Fall sale strip with "Up to 75% off". That is four offers in one scroll. Yesterday's "scents the saving: $5.95 starts today" already carried the $5.95 mists. Here that deal is demoted to the second slot and the $3.50 refills take the lead. Compared with the 10/07 "final hours for the good stuff" send, this one is less focused.
- Overall it is a competent promo with a clear lead offer, weakened by clutter and a lot of dead space. I'd call it a 6 out of 10.

## What's working

- The hero price of $3.50 is large and high-contrast, and the "Today only" banner sets a credible deadline.
- The hero has one clear button, "Shop now", set against a seasonal photo.
- The $5.95 mist block has a bold price on a crimson field, and "Select full-size mists, body spray & body cream" tells you what it covers.
- The "You might like" row shows three named products with category labels, which gives some product detail.
- The "More to love" section surfaces Rewards, texts and store pickup.

## What's weak

- Four offers compete: $3.50 refills, $5.95 mists, festive candles with no price, and 75% off. The result is a promo pile, not a story.
- The candle tile has no price and no button, so it is a dead end.
- There is a large blank gap between the candle tile and the Fall sale strip, which looks like a broken or missing image.
- The Fall sale strip is small, with a tiny "SHOP" link, and it undercuts the "Today only" urgency.
- The disclaimers are a long wall of fine print, and the MERRYMOOD code is easy to miss in small type under the price.
- Several buttons say only "Shop now" and don't name a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one or two offers. Keep the $3.50 refills as the hero and the $5.95 mists as the secondary, and drop the candle tile and the 75% strip.
- 2. Put the code MERRYMOOD in a button or a visible chip. Add a countdown or "ends 11:59 PM ET".
- 3. Fix the empty gap, or put real content there such as refill scent swatches.
- 4. Give each button specific copy, such as "Get $3.50 refills".
- 5. Add scent names or a bestseller badge to the hero so there is something to click toward.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$3.50 Wallflowers refills. Today only.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code MERRYMOOD: $3.50 refills end tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 24 refills per customer. Use code MERRYMOOD at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $5.95 full-size mists, still going strong.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's source and headers are only partly verifiable. The QA run found 0 hard failures, but the HTML is truncated and 50 tracking links were not probed. The confirmed defects are 7 images flagged for missing alt text (one of them also loaded over `http://`) and a web-font `@import` that likely never loads. The header-based compliance checks are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unknown. This is an unverified area, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure image source:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjUwMjV9AAcAAAAAB7GLnc9pP3ZF7o-0S--RkcJ2LL4Vh6ER1kTx3izU-OXD79...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy will usually rewrite it, but other clients may block it as mixed content. It's a Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent image, so a blocked load leaves that content block empty. Switch the source to `https://`.
- **Tracking image:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=101026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_350WFSBEE_AM_BAL-NTF-OP` is an image with no alt text. It appears to be a tracking or engagement pixel from a third-party domain separate from the sender's `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. The `e=` value is a per-recipient identifier embedded in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (7 images).** All of these are flagged by QA:
  - `.../m/1/4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `.../m/1/d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `.../m/1/00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `.../m/1/7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `.../m/1/bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`
  - the `everestengagement.com` image

  The QA output reports the src as the identifier, so I can't tell whether these are content or decorative images. Content images need descriptive `alt`. Decorative ones and pixels should carry an explicit `alt=""`. If any of these carry the offer, the email is unreadable with images off.
- **Font `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that, as far as I know, Google Fonts doesn't serve. It likely returns an error and adds a blocked external request. `@import` in `<style>` is also stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The existing `font-family: "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` fallback stack covers rendering, so the import can be removed.
- **Document structure (from the truncated source):** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and the `<style>` block appear directly after the `<meta>` tags, with no `</head><body>` visible between them. If that holds in the full source, content sits inside `<head>`, which parsers auto-correct inconsistently. Confirm against the full HTML.
- **Redundant markup:** The charset is declared twice (`http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`). The `<!DOCTYPE>` is HTML 4.01 Transitional, yet the head uses HTML5 meta tags. Neither causes a failure.
- **Dark mode:** The dark-mode CSS covers `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]`/`[data-ogsb]`. No issues found in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so I can't check the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are absent in the real message, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules would be violated.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them. The `.footer-disclaimer` selector suggests a footer exists.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data. All 50 links are click-redirect URLs that were not followed, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible. The only campaign identifier observed is the `c=101026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_350WFSBEE_AM_BAL-NTF-OP` parameter on the Everest URL. No UTM mismatch can be claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all 7 flagged images (descriptive for content, `alt=""` for decorative or pixels). Verify that no offer text lives only in an image.
2. Change the `LiveContent.aspx` image source from `http://` to `https://`.
3. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the system font stack.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Confirm in the full HTML that content sits inside `<body>` and that the unsubscribe link and physical address are present.
6. Follow the redirect chains for a sample of the 50 skipped links, with a headless browser or by capturing the final URL. This checks UTM tagging and landing-page continuity.
