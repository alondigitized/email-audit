# final hours ⏰ for $2.95 minis.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "final hours / today only" send in four days, and it's a clean one. The "Final hours" bar sits over a hero reading "Bring joy along for the ride", with a big **$2.95** under the minis lineup of a body cream, a body mist and a hand sanitizer spray. The offer lands in two seconds. The "Shop now" button is visible without scrolling.
- The weak spot is that the code **CUTIES** is needed to get the price. The barcode and "Online, use code CUTIES" sit below the fold of the hero, and the button doesn't carry the code. The second block, "ALL candles Buy 1 get 1 free", repeats the 2026-11-09 candle send ("get glowing. 🌟 ALL candles are buy 1 get 1 free"). That splits attention between two offers when a "final hours" email should have one.
- Verdict: a competent, familiar send that is credible on urgency and weak on novelty. It's a solid 6-7 and unlikely to stand out from the last four days of $2.95, $2.75 and $3.50 promos.

## What's working

- The "Final hours" bar plus a large **$2.95** gives a clear, credible price and deadline.
- The hero is uncluttered, with three products on a seasonal snow backdrop and a visible "Shop now" button.
- The barcode and "use code CUTIES" block makes in-store and online redemption explicit.
- The candle BOGO block has strong contrast and a clear "Lowest-priced item is free" qualifier.
- The render has no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The code requirement is easy to miss and adds friction before checkout.
- The candle BOGO competes with the minis offer for the same "final hours" attention.
- Both "Shop now" buttons are generic, with no product or category named.
- The "You might like" row shows three small, low-contrast tiles that look unrelated to the minis offer.
- Below that, the "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks add length without adding reasons to click.
- The fine print is long and dense, with a limit of 24 per customer and exclusions. The hero's "Up to 24 per customer" line is small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code in the hero or button, for example "Shop minis with code CUTIES", or auto-apply it.
- 2. Make this a single-offer email. Drop the candle BOGO to a small strip, or send it separately.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop $2.95 minis" instead of "Shop now".
- 4. Replace the generic "You might like" row with the actual minis, each with its price and a one-tap link.
- 5. Vary the creative from the previous days' promos so the urgency doesn't read as repetitive.
- **Subject Alt A:** `last call: $2.95 minis end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your $2.95 minis code expires at 5:59 AM ET`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code CUTIES: body care, hand cream & sanitizer sprays. Up to 24.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus, all candles are buy 1 get 1 free through Saturday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML uses a conventional table-based ExactTarget/SFMC structure with dark-mode handling. QA found missing alt text on 7 images, one non-HTTPS image, and markup placed outside `<body>`. The compliance and authentication headers were not captured, so they could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations not verified:** 44 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (`bbwus.everestengagement.com`, `cl.S7.exct.net`). The final landing URLs and their status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…` is loaded over plain HTTP. Gmail proxies it, but other clients may block it as mixed content. This is the SFMC LiveContent/dynamic-content endpoint. If the block happens, that module won't render.
- **Image served from a redirect URL:** one `<img>` has a `src` of `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295TRAVEL_FH_BAL_G6M_V6`. This is the same tracking-redirect pattern as the links, so it is likely a tracking or open pixel, but this can't be confirmed from the truncated source. If it is a pixel, it needs `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (7 images):**
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - `LiveContent.aspx`
  - the Everest redirect image above
  
  Image-off clients and screen readers get nothing for these. If any are the $2.95 mini offer graphics, the offer is invisible to those users.
- **Invalid head structure:** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and its MSO conditional comments appear directly after the `<meta>` tags, before any `</head>` or `<body>`. Parsers will auto-close `<head>`, but this is invalid and can behave inconsistently in some clients.
- **Redundant charset declarations:** both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. This is harmless but unnecessary.
- **Unsupported `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. "Helvetica Neue" is also not a Google Fonts family, so this likely does nothing. The `font-main` stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta tag, `color-scheme` meta tags, and dark-mode overrides (`prefers-color-scheme` plus `[data-ogsc]`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible in the supplied HTML or subject. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip these headers. This is unverified, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message at the originating mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sender domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is normal.
- **Footer not checked:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated part of the source and couldn't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Click attribution goes through SFMC/Everest redirects, and the only campaign identifier seen is the `c=111026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_295TRAVEL_FH_BAL_G6M_V6` parameter on the Everest URL.
- Because the redirects were skipped, landing-page alignment with the "$2.95 minis" offer can't be confirmed. The campaign ID contains `295TRAVEL`, which is consistent with the offer.
- The subscriber identifier `e=200511831233` sits in the query string of a URL that appears in the HTML source. This is standard for ESP tracking, but any forwarded copy exposes it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 5 CMS-hosted `.png` images. Add `alt=""` to any tracking or decorative images.
2. Serve the LiveContent image over `https://`, or confirm the SFMC endpoint supports it.
3. Move the wrapper `<table>` and the MSO blocks into `<body>`. Drop the duplicate charset meta tag.
4. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" and rely on the system font stack.
5. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` or the originating mailbox, and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. Resolve a sample of the 44 tracking links with redirect-following (a follow-redirects probe) to check final status codes and landing pages. This is a QA-tool improvement.
