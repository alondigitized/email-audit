# FINAL HOURS. $2.95 soaps won’t wait. 👋
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the same $2.95 hand soap send BBW ran on 11/22 ("every single hand soap = $2.95") and earlier on 11/23 ("ALL hand soaps. $2.95. ending today."). The hero still works. A huge "$2.95" sits under "ALL Hand soaps", with a "Shop now" button and a "Final hours" bar above it. The eye lands on the price first.
- The email then dilutes itself. Below the hero it adds a $2 hand cream "Doorbuster Deal" and a $13.95 3-wick candle. The candle block has no brand headline, a tiny "Final hours" label and a small CTA. The hero also still says "Black Friday week starts now", which clashes with "Final hours" directly above it.
- Fine for a subscriber who wants soap, but it's the third or fourth near-identical push in one day. Urgency is spent, and the extra offers weaken the "$2.95 won't wait" message.

## What's working

- The "$2.95" price is huge and sits directly under "ALL Hand soaps", so the offer reads in a second.
- "Final hours" bars above the hero and above each offer block give consistent, credible urgency.
- The hero image shows three soaps on a festive gift shelf, with a "New" badge and a "Dermatologist approved" note. The product is clear and on-season.
- Each offer has its own "Shop now" button, so the CTAs are easy to find.
- The "You might like" row shows named soap and sanitizer products, which supports the soap theme.

## What's weak

- The hero says "Black Friday week starts now" under a "Final hours" bar. The two messages contradict each other.
- Three offers ($2.95 soap, $2 hand cream, $13.95 candles) compete for attention, and the soap isn't the clear single focus.
- The candle block is weak. "Give 3 wicks of wonder" is a vague headline, the model photo is off-topic for the soap offer, and the "Shop now" button is small.
- The scrolling "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" and "DOORBUSTER DEAL" marquee bands are cropped mid-word, so they look like a layout glitch.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is long and dense, and the "You might like" tiles have tiny text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the candle block and make the soap the only offer, or at least drop the hand cream. A $2.95 soap email shouldn't also ask for a $13.95 candle purchase.
- 2. Remove "Black Friday week starts now" from the hero and put "Ends tonight" there instead, so the headline matches the urgency.
- 3. Add a time to the deadline, such as "ends 11:59 PM ET", and a per-customer limit note near the button.
- 4. Add a rewards angle if the recipient is a member ("Rewards members: extra perk"). The email currently only asks people to join.
- 5. Fix or remove the cropped marquee bands.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: $2.95 soaps end tonight 🧼`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Every hand soap $2.95 — until midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $2 hand cream. Ends at 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our lowest soap price of the season won't last the night.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with dark-mode and Outlook (MSO) handling, and 44 tracked links. The QA data shows 5 images without alt text, a Google Fonts `@import` that can't resolve, and unverified authentication and unsubscribe headers. The HTML source is truncated, so link, footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** all 44 tracking links were skipped by QA as click-redirect domains, so none were probed for status, redirect chain or final destination.
- **Per-recipient identifier in a URL:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=112326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_295HS_FH_BAL_G6M_V3` carries `e=200511831233`. That is likely a recipient or send ID, which is normal for ESP tracking. The `c=` campaign code is readable, so the click-through URL exposes internal naming (`BAU_PROMO`, `295HS`).
- **Third-party tracking domain:** `everestengagement.com` is used alongside the first-party image host `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. A tracking domain that isn't on the sender's brand can lower click-through reputation, but the data here doesn't show whether it is aligned with the sender.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (5 images, from QA).** These are all on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these give no content. If they carry the promo copy, the offer is lost.
- **Missing alt on the `everestengagement.com` image:** the 6th flagged item is the `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/` URL. It is probably a 1×1 open-tracking pixel, which should have `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden`. The source is truncated, so I couldn't confirm this.
- **Broken font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family that Google Fonts doesn't serve. Helvetica Neue is a system font, so the request will fail. `@import` in `<style>` is also stripped or ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) still works, so this is wasted markup and a failed network request.
- **Document structure:** in the visible source the `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags with no `</head><body>` in between. The snippet is truncated, so this may be an artifact. If it is real, block content inside `<head>` is invalid and some parsers will hoist or drop it.
- **Duplicate charset declarations:** both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. This is harmless but redundant, and the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype doesn't define `<meta charset>`.
- **Dark mode (no defect):** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared, with `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` overrides. This looks correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source or subject. Footer and body personalization couldn't be checked because of truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (QA WARN):** QA notes this may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN):** this is the RFC 8058 one-click header, and it is required for bulk senders by Gmail and Yahoo. It has the same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment couldn't be assessed. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is the usual aligned setup, but that isn't proven here.
- **Footer not visible:** the visible source doesn't include the footer, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. This is a visibility gap, not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the truncated source. Links go through the `everestengagement.com` redirect, so attribution depends on the `c=` campaign code (`112326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_295HS_FH_BAL_G6M_V3`) rather than standard `utm_*` tags. Whether `utm_*` is appended at the redirect is unknown.
- **Landing pages:** destination URLs weren't resolved (all 44 tracking links skipped), so landing-page alignment with the "$2.95 soaps" subject couldn't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images listed above. Give the tracking pixel `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
2. Remove the `@import` for "Helvetica Neue" from Google Fonts and rely on the system stack.
3. Check that the sent HTML has `</head><body>` before the first `<table>`. If the real send also lacks it, fix the template.
4. Re-run header QA against the raw SMTP message rather than the AgentMail relay copy. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
5. Run a destination probe on a sample of the 44 tracked links, following redirects. Record final URLs and any `utm_*` parameters, then compare them to the offer in the subject line.
