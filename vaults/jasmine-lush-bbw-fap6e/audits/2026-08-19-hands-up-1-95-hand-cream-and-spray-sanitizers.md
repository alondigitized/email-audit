# 🙋 hands up - $1.95 hand cream and spray sanitizers.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the clearest, most honest offer Bath & Body Works has run in the string of emails I've seen this month: "$1.95 hand cream and spray sanitizers," a real price, a real product, front and center under "Treat your hands well." No mismatch between subject and hero — refreshing after the "fall for a new fragrance" and "something new" sends that buried unrelated candle offers up top.
- But it's still the same layout DNA as every recent send: hero deal, "SHOP" button, then a second full-bleed deal block (this time the $10 single-wick candles, "ends today") stacked right underneath, competing for attention. The candle block is visually louder (dark banner, glossy product shot) than the actual named offer, which dilutes an otherwise tight hero.
- Net: solid, honest hero offer with a specific price and a real product category match (body/bath care), let down by a second competing deal and a generic grid of category tiles that add little.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "$1.95," "Hand Cream & Sanitizer Sprays," visible product shot, "TODAY ONLY" banner, code HAPPYHANDS, all in one clean fold.
- Subject and hero copy match — no bait-and-switch like recent sends in this brand's history.
- Barcode + "show on phone or print" makes the in-store redemption path frictionless and visible.
- Clean, on-brand visual style (waffle/caramel styling ties into the "Pumpkin Pecan Waffles" and "Caramel Vanilla" scents shown).
- CTA button ("SHOP") is high-contrast and clearly clickable.

## What's weak

- Second deal block ($10 candles, "ENDS TODAY") is visually heavier than the primary $1.95 offer and pulls focus away from the subject line's actual promise.
- "TOP OFFERS" grid (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Soaps, Men's Shop, Home Care, Gifts) is generic catalog navigation, not a merchandising decision — it dilutes urgency built up top.
- No visible price/loyalty-member callout despite the brand having a rewards program mentioned lower ("Birthday Reward") — no member-exclusive pricing shown on the hero itself.
- Fine print at the bottom is dense (multiple paragraphs of exclusions) — undercuts the "simple $1.95 deal" framing established at the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or shrink the candle block — it's a second "ends today" urgency claim stacked on the first, and it's actively competing with the hero offer for the click. If it must stay, make it visually subordinate (smaller, no dark banner).
- 2. Move the barcode/redemption instructions directly under the hero CTA instead of after it — reduce steps between "I want this" and "how do I get it."
- 3. Trim the TOP OFFERS grid to 4 tiles max, or drop it — it doesn't reinforce the $1.95 offer, it distracts from it.
- 4. Surface the "Limit 24 / Use code HAPPYHANDS" text closer to the price so at-a-glance scanning still lands the full offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `today only: $1.95 hand cream + spray sanitizers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HAPPYHANDS in-store or online — ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab caramel vanilla, pumpkin pecan waffles & more for $1.95.`
