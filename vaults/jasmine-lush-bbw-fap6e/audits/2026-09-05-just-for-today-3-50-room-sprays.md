# just for today - $3.50 room sprays. 🏡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely new, well-executed hero — apples, waffle cones, and a fall-toned room-spray duo replace the recycled apple-orchard or Santal-body-wash shots this brand has leaned on all week. "TODAY ONLY!" plus a crisp $3.50 price and a visible barcode/promo code give it real urgency and real specificity, which is more than most of this week's sends managed.
- But it's the same three-tier stacking structure as the 2026-09-04 "$1.95 is still here" send: hero offer, then a second "LIMITED TIME ONLY!" $5.95 body-care banner, then a third $13.95 candle banner. Only the top tier carries the "today only" urgency; the other two dilute it. This brand keeps reaching for multi-offer stacking as a crutch instead of trusting one hero to carry the send.
- Net: strongest hero art of the week, undercut by the now-familiar stacked-offer format. A solid mid-pack send.

## What's working

- New, seasonally appropriate hero imagery (apples, waffle cones, warm lighting) — a genuine break from the repeated Santal/orchard shots logged 09-01 through 09-04.
- Clear single price point on the hero ($3.50) with an explicit usage cap ("Up to 24 per customer") and a printable barcode/promo code for in-store use — unusually complete offer mechanics.
- "TODAY ONLY!" banner plus "Fall is served. Spritz accordingly." gives concrete urgency and a light, on-brand voice.
- Clean visual hierarchy: banner → headline → hero image → price → CTA, no overlapping text or broken assets.
- "We think you'll love" cross-sell row adds product variety without cluttering the top of the email.

## What's weak

- Same three-stacked-offer format as 09-04's "$1.95" send ($3.50 → $5.95 → $13.95) — the "LIMITED TIME ONLY" banner is reused twice for offers with no visible deadline, which cheapens the "TODAY ONLY" claim up top.
- Long scroll before reaching "We think you'll love" and the brand-trust block — mobile readers have to pass three separate offer sections before any personalization.
- Free shipping banner threshold ($50) is disconnected from the $3.50 hero price, making the "free shipping" callout feel like boilerplate rather than a matched offer.
- No loyalty/rewards-member pricing or points callout visible despite this being a loyalty-heavy brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one hero offer with a real deadline. Drop the $5.95 and $13.95 banners into a simple "also on sale" row below the fold instead of full-width repeated "LIMITED TIME ONLY!" banners — that's the single biggest lift since it restores urgency credibility.
- 2. Tie the free-shipping banner to the actual hero price (e.g., "Stock up: 3 for $3.50 sprays ships free at $50") so it reads as connected, not boilerplate.
- 3. Surface a rewards/loyalty callout near the hero CTA — subscribers who are members should see points or member-price context.
- 4. Move "We think you'll love" higher, right after the hero, to shorten the scroll before personalization kicks in.

- **Subject Alt B:** `24 room sprays, $3.50 each — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up on fall scents before midnight — code SAVORFALL`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $5.95 body care and $13.95 candles, while they last`
