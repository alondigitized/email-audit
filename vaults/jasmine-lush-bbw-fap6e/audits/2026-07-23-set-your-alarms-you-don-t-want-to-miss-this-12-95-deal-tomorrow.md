# ⏰ set your alarms, you don't want to miss this $12.95 deal tomorrow. 🤭
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a classic B&BW advance-notice teaser for their signature 3-Wick Candle event — "Fall for the Glow" at $12.95, starting tomorrow. Unlike most B&BW teasers that bury the hook (see the Fruit Fusion launch on 7/20 that crammed four unrelated offers into one email), this one earns it: the subject's "$12.95 deal tomorrow" lands in a hero that delivers exactly that — $12.95, All 3-Wick Candles, Starts Tomorrow. The creative discipline is real and rare for this sender.
- The CTA "START YOUR LIST" is an unusually smart call for a pre-event send. Rather than a hollow "SHOP NOW" that can't convert today, it invites wishlist-building behavior — setting purchase intent before the deal goes live. The fall palette, warm product shot of "The Perfect Autumn" candle, and "Level 24" loyalty indicator all reinforce a coherent, personalized message.
- The main drag: the subject line runs ~70 characters and will truncate on most mobile inboxes before subscribers even see "$12.95." And this is B&BW's seventh email in six days; cadence fatigue is real. The price would hit harder with a crossed-out original beside it.

## What's working

- **Subject-hero alignment**: "$12.95 deal tomorrow" in the subject maps directly to the hero — B&BW finally did this again after the chaotic 7/20 Fruit Fusion multi-promo debacle.
- **"START YOUR LIST" CTA**: exactly right for a pre-event send; creates behavioral intent before the deal opens rather than a dead "shop" link.
- **$12.95 dominates the hero**: price is the largest typographic element — zero ambiguity about the offer.
- **Level 24 loyalty indicator**: quiet personalization that rewards members without screaming about it.
- **Thematic coherence**: "Fall for the Glow," warm autumn palette, and The Perfect Autumn candle are locked together; no creative mismatch.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is ~70 characters**: will truncate on most mobile clients before "tomorrow" appears; "you don't want to miss this" is pure padding.
- **No compare-at price**: $12.95 is only as powerful as the subscriber knows — "$27 → $12.95" or "Save 50%" anchors the value immediately and costs nothing to add.
- **Nothing to buy today**: the deal doesn't exist yet, softening conversion; "START YOUR LIST" helps but doesn't fully bridge the gap.
- **Generic category nav grid** (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers…) below the fold is boilerplate that doesn't serve the fall candle narrative.
- **Seventh email in six days**: cadence fatigue is compounding; a subscriber can scroll past this on muscle memory alone.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the subject to under 50 chars**: Drop "you don't want to miss this" entirely — it's the weakest part and eats the punchline on mobile.
- 2. **Add compare-at pricing**: Cross out $27.00 next to $12.95 in the hero. Perceived value doubles at zero creative cost.
- 3. **Name specific fall scents in the hero or preheader**: "The Perfect Autumn, Leaves, Sweater Weather" creates desire that "All 3-Wick Candles" doesn't — subscribers buy scents, not SKU types.
- 4. **Replace the generic nav grid with a fall collection preview strip**: four named candle thumbnails build tomorrow's urgency instead of scattering attention.
- 5. **Add a "Deal opens in X hours" micro-element** near the CTA — reinforces the pre-event window without feeling gimmicky.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow: $12.95 3-Wick Candles. Set your alarm. ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fall for the Glow — all 3-Wick Candles drop to $12.95 tomorrow. Add yours to your list now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Perfect Autumn, Leaves, Sweater Weather — $12.95 each, starting tomorrow only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Content placed between `</head>` and `<body>` tags.** A `<table>` containing the preheader `<div>` is injected in this gap — before the `<body>` tag opens. Behavior across email clients in this gap is undefined and can cause content to be dropped or double-rendered.

```html
</head>
<!-- slot comment -->
<table ...>  <!-- ← outside <body> -->
  <td><div class="preheader" style="display:none...">something bright is coming...</div></td>
</table>
<body ...>
```

**`<meta charset>` declared last in `<head>`.** The UTF-8 charset declaration appears at the bottom of `<head>` after all `<style>` blocks. It should be the first element in `<head>` to ensure correct character parsing before any styled content is read.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import`.** The `Source+Sans+Pro` font is imported twice:
- Once in the `<head>` `<style>` block
- Again in an inline `<style>` block inside the email body

Neither import will load in Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail — fallback stacks (Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif) are defined in body-level declarations and will apply, but the duplicate import adds unnecessary payload.

**3 images missing `alt` text (QA confirmed):**
| Image | Issue |
|---|---|
| `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` | No `alt` attribute |
| Two images with empty `src=""` | No `alt` attribute and broken source |
| Everest Engagement tracking pixel | No `alt` attribute |

Two images have empty `src` attributes entirely — these will generate broken image requests in some clients.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA, WARN).** This header is required for bulk senders under Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 sender requirements. Its absence (or non-capture by the AgentMail relay) is a deliverability risk for Gmail routing.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (QA, WARN).** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is absent. Required for Gmail bulk sender compliance. Without it, Gmail may downgrade inbox placement.

**Authentication-Results header not present (QA, WARN).** SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the captured headers. The sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is an ESP subdomain — DKIM alignment should be verified at the ESP level.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link are standard in Bath & Body Works sends; not visible in the truncated source but expected to be present in the footer.
