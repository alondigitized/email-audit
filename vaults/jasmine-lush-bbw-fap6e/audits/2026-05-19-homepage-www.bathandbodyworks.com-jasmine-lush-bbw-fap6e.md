# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally cannot see this homepage right now — there's a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" bot-check modal covering basically everything. What I *can* see is a promo banner at the top ("Today only! $1.95 car refills & $2.95 diffusers with code SAVE"), the B&BW nav bar, and a sliver of what looks like a blue product or body wash shot peeking out behind the modal. The brand is Bath & Body Works, and based on the banner alone they're pushing a car refill and diffuser deal — 100% relevant to me as a Wallflower gifter. But I cannot actually shop this page because I'm stuck at a bot gate.

## What's working

- **Top promo banner** — "Today only! $1.95 car refills & $2.95 diffusers with code SAVE" is exactly what I came to see. That's my Wallflower world. Single-day urgency plus a dollar amount. I'd tap "Details" immediately if I could.
- **Nav bar is clean** — hamburger, search, account, and bag icons are all recognizable and uncluttered. Getting to my account and rewards would be one tap once the modal clears.
- **Partial hero glimpse** — the blue product/water visual I can see behind the modal looks fresh and summery, which tracks for a May campaign.

## What's weak

- **Bot-check modal blocks 100% of the browsable content** — this is the single biggest UX failure on screen. I'm on my phone tapping through to check the sale, and I'm stopped cold by "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human." That's jarring and feels like a 2015 captcha experience.
- **No loyalty/rewards visibility above the fold** — my rewards balance and member perks are zero percent visible; the modal nukes any chance of a personalized hook.
- **Primary CTA is buried or invisible** — the only button I can see says "Press & Hold," which is a security gate, not a shop CTA. For a returning rewards member like me, there's no "hey, welcome back" energy whatsoever.
- **Hero is completely obscured** — I cannot evaluate the campaign messaging, seasonal relevance, or lifestyle imagery at all.

## Recommendations

- **Move bot verification to a less intrusive pattern** — a silent background check, or a lightweight banner challenge, instead of a full-screen modal that kills the entire above-the-fold experience on mobile.
- **Pin the rewards/loyalty hook in the nav or below the promo banner** — even a small "You have X points" chip next to the account icon would make returning shoppers feel seen before they hit any gate.
- **Ensure the promo banner "Details >" link bypasses or accelerates bot resolution** — if I tap "Details" before clearing the modal, I should land somewhere useful, not a dead state.
- **Test mobile bot-gate frequency for logged-in users** — rewards members shouldn't be getting hard-gated on their own account sessions; suppress the modal for authenticated sessions.
