# 👀 in the mood for something new?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Subject promises "something new" but the hero delivers the same $10 single-wick candle offer with a "Small flicker. Big scent." headline — a repackaged discount, not a new product. The Jack Skellington/Nightmare Before Christmas module is the actual new thing here, and it's stronger creative than the last five sends this brand has run, but it's buried second, below the candle discount block instead of leading the email.
- This is a clearer, better-organized send than most of Bath & Body Works' recent cadence — no lorem-ipsum, no overlapping text, clean grid of category tiles. But the subject/hero mismatch is a real problem again: "in the mood for something new?" over-promises against a $10 candle sale that's effectively the fourth candle-discount beat in a week.
- Net: solid execution, misleading subject-to-hero pairing, decent but not standout email.

## What's working

- Clean, legible hero with a specific offer ($10 single-wick candles) and a visible SHOP button.
- The Jack Skellington module is genuinely differentiated seasonal content — a licensed Disney tie-in, not another soap discount.
- "TOP OFFERS" grid with 8 clearly labeled category tiles (Fragrance, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Soaps, Men's Shop, Home Care, Gifts) gives multiple entry points.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is clean top to bottom.
- "STARTS TODAY" banner gives credible, time-bounded framing for the candle deal.

## What's weak

- Subject line "in the mood for something new?" is not matched by the hero — the hero is a $10 candle sale BBW has run repeatedly this week, not something new.
- Two competing heroes (candle sale, then Skellington) split attention with no single clear priority.
- "TOP OFFERS" header sits next to a "TODAY'S DEALS" button that doesn't visually connect to any specific new offer — filler CTA.
- This is at least the fourth candle-centric promo email in a week (2026-08-13 through -16 all leaned candles/soap); "something new" cadence-wise reads thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Jack Skellington module as the hero — it's the only actually-new content this week and matches the subject's promise; move the $10 candle offer below it as a secondary deal.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to name the Skellington collab directly instead of a vague "something new" tease that over-promises.
- 3. Cut the "TODAY'S DEALS" button next to "TOP OFFERS" — it's a redundant, unlabeled CTA competing with SHOP and SHOP NOW.
- 4. Add a specific date/time cutoff near "STARTS TODAY" (matching the fine print's Aug 17–20 window) so urgency is visible in the hero, not just the footer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Jack Skellington collection just landed — plus $10 single wick candles through 8/20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Creamy pumpkin, dark woods, and a $10 candle sale — all today.`
