# last day for new $4.95 hydration hits.💧
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This one lands the offer cleanly: "$4.95 Select body lotion & cream" sits directly under a "Dewy debut" headline with a real product photo (Platinum lotion + Midnight Rouge body cream) and an unmissable "Ends today" banner up top. It's a stronger open than the 09-26 "$5.95 candles" send in the same brand history — the visual is more premium (dark, moody still-life vs. the cozy cocoa shot) and the "Just dropped" tag gives it a reason to exist beyond pure discount.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the hero. The candle section ("Candles with a taste for entertaining") has zero CTA button, zero price, and no obvious link — it's a full-width lifestyle image that dead-ends. Below that there's a huge stretch of blank space before "We think you'll love," which reads like a broken/missing content module, not intentional whitespace.
- This is a 7-range send: strong top, sloppy middle, generic bottom. Fix the dead zone and the candle section and it's an 8.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unmissable: "$4.95," "Select body lotion & cream," "Up to 24 per customer" all stacked tightly above one "Shop now" button.
- "Ends today" urgency banner is credible and simple, no exclamation-point spam.
- Product photography is high-contrast and premium-feeling (dark backdrop, metallic Platinum bottle, deep red Midnight Rouge tube).
- "Just dropped" badge signals newness, differentiating this from a plain clearance push.

## What's weak

- The candle lifestyle image has no CTA, no price, no product names visible in that block — it's a scroll-stopper with nowhere to go.
- A large blank gap sits between the candle section and "We think you'll love" — looks like a broken/empty module, not a deliberate design choice.
- "We think you'll love" recommendation tiles have no prices and no CTA buttons, just product names under thumbnails.
- "Our feel good formula" block is pure brand-voice filler with a vague "Learn more" link — no offer, no urgency, dilutes the one clear message from the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or fix the blank gap between the candle image and "We think you'll love" — that's the single biggest visible defect and likely costs clicks from anyone who assumes the email ended there.
- 2. Add a "Shop candles" button and a price/offer line to the candle lifestyle section — right now it's a picture, not marketing.
- 3. Add prices or "Shop now" links to each "We think you'll love" tile — three products with no path to purchase is wasted real estate.
- 4. Move "Our feel good formula" (brand trust copy) to the very bottom near the footer — it has no offer and currently interrupts momentum between two product sections.
- 5. Consider trimming from two hero sections down to one strong hero + a tighter cross-sell strip, since the current layout dilutes the "$4.95, ends today" urgency with unrelated candle content.
