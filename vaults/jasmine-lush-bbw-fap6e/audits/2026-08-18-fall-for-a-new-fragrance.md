The actual "new fragrance" story — the Preserve Collection with Wild Blackberry Jam and Cinnamon Honey Toast — doesn't show up until several scrolls down, buried under a candle ad that has nothing to do with the subject line's promise.
- Once you get past that opener, the creative itself is genuinely nice: moody, editorial photography (girls in a field, blackberry jam jars, honey jars, apples) with a clear seasonal "Preserve Collection" identity and two products flagged "New." - Clear "Preserve Collection" seasonal branding with a coherent editorial photography style (field, jam, honey, apples).
- Two products are explicitly flagged "New," giving a real reason to look closer.
- "Smells and looks like your homemade blackberry jam" is a specific, sensory line of copy that stands out from generic promo language.
- Clean navigation footer (Top Offers grid, Find a Store, loyalty guarantee) with no rendering breakage.

## What's weak

- Subject says "fall for a new fragrance" but the hero is a $10 single-wick candle deal with "Small flicker. Big scent." — same mismatch pattern flagged on 2026-08-15.
- No single clear CTA button for the actual new-fragrance story — only a "Shop the Collection" link buried after four separate product beats.
- The email stacks three unrelated concepts (candle discount, Preserve Collection story, Top Offers grid) with no visual through-line tying them together.
