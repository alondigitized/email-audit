# early Black Friday deals start TOMORROW. 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the strongest part of this email. A red block reads "Black Friday week starts now, 40% off Entire store" over a shelf of Winter Candy Apple, Tis the Season and Twisted Peppermint products. The offer is clear and the eye lands on it first. The problem is that the subject says deals start TOMORROW, a thin strip says "Starts tomorrow", and the hero says "starts now". A subscriber can't tell whether to shop today.
- The body is weak after the hero. There are four category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps), then three small, unpriced product tiles, then a "feel good formula" brand block with low-contrast text. A long stretch of blank space follows the footer, with stray underlined fragments and no readable legal text. Compared with the 11/17 "$3.95 lip care" sends, which led with a big price and one product, this is a broader, less urgent teaser.
- Verdict: a decent announcement with a clear 40% hook, undermined by the tense conflict and a messy lower half.

## What's working

- The "40% off Entire store" hero is huge, red and unambiguous.
- Seasonal product photography (snowman Wallflower plug, peppermint mist, candle) fits the holiday moment.
- The category buttons let a shopper go straight to Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers or Hand Soaps.
- The "Free shipping with any $50 purchase" bar and the Rewards line carry loyalty and shipping perks near the top.
- The subject's 🖤 emoji and lowercase style match the brand's recent voice.

## What's weak

- "Starts tomorrow" conflicts with "starts now" in the hero, which muddies the urgency.
- The hero has no single primary CTA button. The four outlined category buttons compete and look secondary.
- The three product tiles have no prices, no sale callout and no stars or reviews, so they read as filler.
- The "Our feel good formula" block has pale text on a pale background and is hard to read.
- A large empty gap, scattered underlined fragments and no visible footer text appear at the bottom, which looks like a render bug.
- The scrolling "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" marquee is cut off at both edges.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the timing. Pick one message ("Starts tomorrow: 40% off everything") and make the hero, banner and subject match. Add a date and time.
- 2. Add one filled "Shop 40% off" button under the hero. Keep the category links smaller.
- 3. Show sale prices on the product tiles, for example "$X → $Y with 40% off", and add a star rating.
- 4. Raise the contrast on the brand block, or cut it from a pre-sale teaser.
- 5. Fix the footer gap so the legal and unsubscribe text renders.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off everything starts tomorrow 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `set your cart: Black Friday week begins tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Candles, Wallflowers and body care all 40% off, plus free shipping at $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members get first look. Here's what to add to your cart tonight.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 45 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains were checked.
- **Third-party click domain:** The tracking host is `bbwus.everestengagement.com`, not the sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`. Images are served from `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com`, so the image host matches the sender but the click host does not. This isn't a defect by itself, but it is a domain-alignment gap that some filters weigh.
- **Subscriber ID in URL:** The sample URL `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=111726_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_40OFF_PREV_BAL_G6M_V10` carries a per-recipient `e=` value in the query string. That is standard for redirect trackers, but it means any forwarded copy of the email carries the recipient's identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 elements):** Five images on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/` have no `alt`: `4851c40c-…png`, `d6327aef-…png`, `00a9ac12-…png`, `7b7c94ef-…png` and `bbebdf75-…png`. If these carry content or links, they are inaccessible with images blocked or in a screen reader. If they are decorative, they should have `alt=""`.
- **Sixth missing-alt element:** The sixth is the Everest URL above. It is probably a tracking pixel or a linked image, and it should also have `alt=""`.
- **Markup in `<head>`:** In the visible source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags directly. No `</head>` or `<body>` is visible before it, so the table appears to sit inside `<head>`. This is invalid HTML and parsers will auto-correct it inconsistently. The source is truncated, so confirm against the full HTML.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with HTML5 `<meta charset="utf-8">`. The document also declares charset twice (`http-equiv` Content-Type and `meta charset`). Both are harmless but redundant.
- **Ineffective font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts does not host. The request is wasted, and `@import` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-family` fallback stack to Helvetica/Arial covers the actual rendering.
- **Dark mode:** The source includes `prefers-color-scheme` and `[data-ogsc]` rules, with `.darkmode-dark` forced to 70×70px. I found no defect in what is visible.
