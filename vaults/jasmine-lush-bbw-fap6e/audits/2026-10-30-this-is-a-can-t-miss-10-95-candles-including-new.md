# this is a can't-miss: $10.95 candles, including ✨new.✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the same-day payoff to the "one more sleep" teaser and the "📢 it's here" send. It is the third candle email in 24 hours. The $10.95 3-wick hero is the strongest element. The price is huge and pink, "Starts today" sits on a dark bar above it, and there is a clear "Shop now" button. The $5.95 single-wick block below repeats the same pitch in a second format.
- The subject is the weak link. "Can't-miss" is filler, and "including ✨new.✨" promises something the body never shows. I can't see anything labeled new, and the hero product, Snowflakes & Cashmere, is not marked as new either. A subscriber who has seen two candle emails since yesterday will see this one as a repeat.
- Verdict: a competent, clear-offer send that suffers from cadence fatigue and a subject that doesn't match the body. It is on par with the rest of the run, not better.

## What's working

- The $10.95 hero is large and high-contrast, and the eye lands on it first.
- The "Starts today" bars give credible, specific urgency.
- A two-tier offer ($10.95 3-wick, $5.95 single wick) gives two price points.
- "Up to 24 per customer" signals scarcity and a real deal.
- The header strip shows Rewards and free shipping over $50.

## What's weak

- The "new" in the subject has no visible counterpart in the body.
- The second block's "Shop now" button sits below the image, away from the offer, and is easy to miss.
- The "You might like" row is thin and off-message. It includes a hand sanitizer and a body cream, and none of the three items carries a price.
- The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" sections dilute the focus on candles.
- The fine print is long and dense, and the "Excludes boxed candles" exclusion is small and easy to miss.
- This is the third candle send in about a day, with near-identical creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "new" real. Show a "New" badge on the actual new scent, or drop it from the subject.
- 2. Merge the day's candle sends into one, or vary the creative so the cadence doesn't feel like repetition.
- 3. Move the second "Shop now" button into the $5.95 hero, and name the CTA ("Shop 3-wick candles").
- 4. Put prices and "$10.95" tags on the "You might like" candles, and drop the non-candle items.
- 5. Cut the "feel good formula" and "More to love" modules, or push them below the fold.

## 1. Technical Summary

The automated QA flagged no hard failures, only 3 warnings. Those are missing header captures (likely a relay artifact) and missing image `alt` attributes (confirmed). The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destination URLs, redirect chains or HTTP statuses were verified.
- **Tracking host:** `bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=103026_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_10953W_ICYMI_BAL_G6M_V…`. It carries a per-recipient ID (`e=`) and a campaign code (`c=`). This is normal for ESP tracking, but it means the final landing URLs can't be audited from the email alone.
- **`@import` of a non-existent Google font:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`. As far as I know, Google Fonts doesn't host "Helvetica Neue", so this request likely fails. Many clients block `@import` anyway. The `font-main` stack falls back to Helvetica/Arial, so the visible impact is nil, but it's a wasted external request.

3. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and the `<body>` structure.
4. Follow the tracking redirects for a sample of the 42 links, or all of them, to confirm landing URLs, status codes and offer alignment.
