# have you shopped yet? 👀 the whole store* is buy 3 get 3 free!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Buy 3 Get 3 Free email of the day from Bath & Body Works — following "⏰ set your alarms" this morning and "🚨🚨🚨 EVERYTHING* is buy 3 get 3 free starting right now!" — and it's the most polished of the three, but that's a low bar given the cadence fatigue. The hero is genuinely strong: a warm, seasonal "Fall Scent Event" diffuser-and-candle shot leads straight into a bold orange "ENTIRE STORE Buy 3 get 3 FREE" block with four clear category buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps). That's a clean, unambiguous offer stated once, high up, without clutter.
- Below the fold it sprawls into six back-to-back product storytelling blocks (Preserve Collection, Blend Bar, Santal Ridge, etc.) before repeating the offer and CTA a second time near the bottom. That's a lot of scroll for a repeat subscriber who's already seen this exact promo twice today in other sends.
- Net: well-built creative, honest single offer, no render bugs — but this is the same event restated for the third time in one day, and the subject leans on "have you shopped yet?" guilt-bait rather than new information, which will read as noise to anyone who already opened this morning's version.

## What's working

- Single, clear, honest offer — "Buy 3 get 3 FREE, entire store" with "lowest-priced items are free" disclosed right under the headline, no buried fine print.
- Strong visual hierarchy — eye goes hero image → "STARTS TODAY" banner → orange offer block → category CTAs in a clean top-to-bottom line.
- Four category shortcut buttons (Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps) give the reader a direct path instead of one generic "Shop" button.
- Offer is repeated at the bottom with a second "Shop" CTA, so a reader who scrolls through the storytelling content still gets a clear close.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical Buy 3 Get 3 Free send today — same offer, same category set, just re-skinned creative. Subscribers who opened this morning's "set your alarms" or the "starting right now" blast get nothing new here.
- Subject "have you shopped yet? 👀" is guilt/nag framing rather than new information — it doesn't add urgency or a reason to open a third time.
- Six sequential product-story blocks (Preserve Collection, Blend Bar, Santal Ridge, etc.) between the top offer and the bottom CTA is a lot of scroll depth for what is fundamentally a single storewide promo.
- No visible countdown/deadline on when Buy 3 Get 3 Free ends — "STARTS TODAY" is there but there's no urgency anchor for when it stops.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or heavily trim the mid-email product-story blocks — collapse the six sections into 2-3 max, or a single grid, so the second CTA lands faster. Scroll depth is the biggest drag here.
- 2. Fix the subject to carry new information instead of a guilt nudge — reference the specific hour/day-count remaining since this is send #3 of the offer.
- 3. Add a visible end date/time near the top offer block so repeat openers have a reason to act now instead of assuming they can catch it tomorrow.
- 4. Space this campaign's re-sends out — three same-day promotions of the identical offer risks list fatigue; consider skipping this exact rebroadcast for anyone who already opened today's earlier B3G3 email.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers, Hand Soaps — all included, lowest-price items are free.`
