# $2.95 waves goodbye tonight. 👋
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth appearance of the exact same $2.95 Woodland Evening foaming hand soap hero that Bath & Body Works has now run under three different subject lines in four days (2026-08-13 "$2.95 - lowest price of the season starts now!", 2026-08-14 "$2.95 for a limited time," and now "$2.95 waves goodbye tonight 👋"). The product shot, the price, the "Lowest price of the season" copy, even the "Just dropped" badge are identical — only the urgency framing has been reshuffled. A subscriber who opened any of the prior three sends will recognize this instantly and the "waves goodbye tonight" urgency reads as manufactured rather than credible.
- Below the soap hero, the email stacks a second offer (Buy 3 Get 3 Free full-size body/skin/hair) and then a third, genuinely different beat — Rewards-member early access to the Nightmare Before Christmas x Disney collection, the same teaser that scored 8.00/10 as its own standalone send earlier today. Burying that exclusive, high-interest content as the third scroll-stop under two recycled soap offers wastes the best asset in the email.
- Verdict: reused hero art, a third-generation subject line on the same $2.95 offer, and a genuinely exciting collab buried below the fold. This is a cadence problem more than an execution problem — nothing is broken, but nothing here earns a second open from someone who's already seen the last two sends.

## What's working

- Clean top banner: free shipping threshold is stated clearly and separately from the main offer, no clutter.
- "ENDS TODAY!" band gives the $2.95 offer a real deadline, and the price is large, bold, and impossible to miss.
- Dermatologist-approved callout on the "slow living" block adds a credible trust signal not seen in prior sends.
- The Nightmare Before Christmas block is genuinely differentiated — licensed IP, moody lighting, clear "Member exclusive" badge — strong creative on its own.
- Top Offers grid with clean category icons gives a fallback path to browse if the hero offers don't land.

## What's weak

- The hero product photo, price, and "Lowest price of the season" claim are identical to the 2026-08-13 and 2026-08-14 sends — a repeat subscriber will feel this immediately.
- Three separate offers (soap $2.95, Buy 3 Get 3, NBC early access) compete for attention with no single clear "start here" — this is the same offer-stacking problem flagged in the 2026-08-15 "mood lighting" and 2026-08-13 "closing the book" sends.
- The best asset in the email (NBC collab) is positioned third, after two soap/body-care offers the subscriber has already seen twice this week.
- "Waves goodbye tonight" is a stretch of urgency copy for a soap that's been "ending" or at its "lowest price" for three straight sends — it undercuts credibility rather than building it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero: lead with the Nightmare Before Christmas early-access block since it's the only offer here that isn't a repeat, then follow with the $2.95 soap as a secondary "also ending today" strip lower in the email.
- 2. Retire the Woodland Evening $2.95 hero art for at least a week — three sends on the identical product shot in four days is fatigue, not persistence.
- 3. Pick one hero offer, not three. If soap, Buy 3 Get 3, and NBC all need to run, use a single unifying frame ("Today only: 3 ways to save") instead of three stacked full-width blocks.
- 4. Drop or soften "waves goodbye" — it's the third urgency variant on the same $2.95 price point and reads as recycled rather than real.

- **Subject Alt B:** `last call: $2.95 soap + early access to NBC`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: $2.95 soap and Buy 3 Get 3 Free — all ending tonight.`
