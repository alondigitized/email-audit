# we've got merry on our minds...🎁🎅🏻🎄
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This email has a split personality: the subject and hero promise holiday teaser content ("we've got merry on our minds"), but the actual concrete offer — $4.95 hand soaps with code HANDFUL — is a completely unrelated flash deal buried under a "Today only" banner two full scrolls down. Anyone opening for the Christmas teaser hits a soft, vague "sneak a peek at our holiday collections" hero with a "Cue the magic" CTA that doesn't even link to a shoppable holiday page (or if it does, it's not visually a product push) — then a jarring subject/hiring block in the middle for job recruiting, which has zero business belongs in a promotional retail send.
- Compared to the brand's recent run (09-17 through 09-21), this continues the scattergun pattern the account has been flagged for repeatedly — stacking a holiday teaser, a $4.95 soap flash sale, a "Now Hiring" recruitment pitch, and three unrelated product recommendations all in one email. It's visually clean and on-brand (soft holiday pastels, consistent BBW navy/white system), but strategically unfocused.
- Verdict: pretty, on-brand, but confused about what it's actually selling — this is the fifth+ consecutive send from this brand that buries or splits its real offer instead of leading with it.

## What's working

- Clean, cohesive visual design — soft pastel holiday imagery in the hero feels seasonally appropriate and premium.
- The $4.95 hand soap offer is crisp and specific once you reach it — clear price, clear code (HANDFUL), clear CTA ("Shop now").
- "Today only" and "Up to 24 per customer" add credible urgency/scarcity to the soap deal.
- Product recommendation tiles ("We think you'll love") are well-composed with clean product shots and names.

## What's weak

- Subject promises holiday content but the hero CTA ("Cue the magic") leads nowhere near a shoppable offer — it's a mood-board teaser, not a sale.
- The real, time-bound offer ($4.95 soaps) is invisible without scrolling past the entire hero — a mobile reader has to scroll 2+ screens to find any discount.
- A "Now Hiring / Apply Today" recruitment block sits in the middle of a promotional retail email — irrelevant to a shopper and disruptive to the funnel.
- The email tries to do four jobs at once (holiday tease, flash sale, recruiting, general product discovery) and none gets the visual priority it needs.
- No holiday hero product is actually shown or linked — "It's never too early for wonder" has no product, price, or category attached to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job for this send. If the subject is about holiday, lead with an actual holiday product/collection shot and a real CTA ("Shop the Holiday Collection"), not a mood tease. If the real offer is the $4.95 soap, make that the hero and hero subject line — it's the only concrete, time-bound deal in the email.
- 2. Move the $4.95 soap offer above the fold — it's Today Only and disappearing below a full-screen mood hero is losing the most actionable content of the whole email.
- 3. Cut the "Now Hiring" block from promotional sends entirely, or move recruiting to a separate dedicated email/channel — it breaks the shopping mindset and adds no value to a customer session.
- 4. Add a real holiday product tease (a candle, a specific scent, a gift set) to the hero if the subject is going to reference "merry" — right now the promise and the payoff don't match.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HANDFUL before midnight — stock up on your favorites.`
