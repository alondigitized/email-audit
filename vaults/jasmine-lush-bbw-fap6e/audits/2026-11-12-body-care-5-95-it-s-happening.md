**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the day-of payoff for yesterday's teaser, "tomorrow's about to be BIG for your routine. 🤫". The hero is strong: "Starts today," a huge magenta **$5.95**, and "Select body care," with a visible "Shop now" button. Anyone who saw the teaser gets the payoff at once.
- The email then tries to carry too much. After the hero come a BOGO candles block, a second Doorbuster block repeating $5.95 for Holiday Traditions, and a $40 Festive Reset gift set. That is four offers in one send. The Doorbuster hero is also repeated lower down, and its banner is cropped ("ORBUSTER DEAL … DOORBUSTE"). The candle BOGO is the same offer as the 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-11 sends.
- The bottom third has problems too. A large blank gap sits between the gift set and the product tiles. Another empty stretch sits near the footer, with stray underline fragments. The three product tiles look sparse and lack prices. Overall it is a good lead offer with a cluttered, partly broken body. Fine, not great.

## What's working

- The hero price is the loudest thing in the email. "$5.95" in big magenta type with "Select body care" reads in a second.
- "Starts today" pays off the 2026-11-12 teaser and gives the send a clear reason to exist.
- The "Shop now" button is visible and sits right under the price.
- The "Free shipping with any $50 purchase" bar and the Rewards link at the top add a secondary reason to buy.
- The dermatologist-approved note gives some trust and social proof.

## What's weak

- Four competing offers ($5.95 body care, candle BOGO, Holiday Traditions $5.95, $40 gift set) dilute the hero.
- The Doorbuster banner is visibly cropped ("ORBUSTER DEAL … DOORBUSTE"), a render bug.
- The $5.95 Doorbuster offer shows up twice in one email, which is redundant.
- A large empty gap sits above the three product tiles, and another near the footer, with stray line fragments. These look like missing images or content.
- The product tiles (Champagne Toast, Platinum, Ocean) show no prices and are small, so they don't support the offer.
- The candle BOGO repeats the 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-11 sends, which adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer. Drop the repeated Doorbuster block and put the $5.95 deal first and only. Keep at most one secondary offer, such as the gift set.
- 2. Fix the cropped Doorbuster banner and the empty areas. Either fill them or remove them.
- 3. Make the product tiles useful. Show the $5.95 price on each tile, add star ratings, and link each tile to its product page.
- 4. Give the candle BOGO a rest. It has appeared in three sends this week.
- 5. Add a clear deadline, such as "today only" or an end date, so urgency is credible.
