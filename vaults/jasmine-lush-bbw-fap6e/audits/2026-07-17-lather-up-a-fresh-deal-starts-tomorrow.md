# 🫧 lather up, a fresh deal starts tomorrow.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- B&BW is executing a clean category pivot here. After four consecutive emails flogging the $12.95/$5.95 candle-and-body-care cycle (July 14–16), they've rotated to hand care — and this teaser telegraphs it well. The $1/$2/$3.50 price stack in the hero is sharp; the numbers do the selling, and "The Clean Hands Event" is a clear, memorable event name.
- The structural weakness is the same one their July 15 teaser had: the deal isn't live yet, so the email can't convert a buyer — it can only build intent. The "START YOUR LIST" CTA is a clever acknowledgment of that, but it also means this send lives or dies on the subject line opening rate, and "a fresh deal starts tomorrow" is atmosphere copy, not offer copy. No price, no product, no reason to open over the dozen other promos in the tab.
- Bottom line: the body of this email is well-executed; the subject undersells it. If even half the value locked in the hero ("$1 PocketBacs") had leaked into the subject line, this would be a 9/10 across the board.

## What's working

- The $1/$2/$3.50 price ladder in the hero is immediately legible — three tiers, three products, no ambiguity.
- The bubble emoji 🫧 in the subject is thematically tight; it signals soap/lather without spelling it out, and it differentiates this send from the prior candle/body-care cycle.
- "STARTS TOMORROW!" badge sets expectation correctly and avoids the bait-and-switch feeling of a tease that never reveals the deal.
- "START YOUR LIST" is a smart pre-sale CTA — it captures intent without pretending you can buy today.
- Category freshness: after four hand-heavy "3-wick / body care" emails, pivoting to hand sanitizers and foaming soaps reads as genuinely new.

## What's weak

- Subject reveals nothing specific — no price, no product, no category. Compare to the July 15 teaser that at least had "big deal" hinting at scale; this one just says "fresh."
- "START YOUR LIST" is ambiguous copy — a list for what? Clicking doesn't deliver the offer today, and the CTA doesn't clarify the action.
- "Picked just for you!" section serves body sprays (A Thousand Wishes, Into the Night, Champagne Toast) — unrelated to the hand care event above it, making the transition feel like a template default, not genuine personalization.
- No end date visible anywhere — "STARTS TOMORROW!" names when it starts but not when it ends, reducing the urgency of setting a reminder.
- Loyalty/rewards program absent from the hero; B&BW's rewards members have reason to act first but nothing here calls them out.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Leak the price in the subject.** "$1 hand sanitizers? Starts tomorrow." is a three-second open decision vs. curiosity-bait that can be ignored. The July 15 teaser with "✨big deal✨" scored 8/10 and this is B&BW's second use of the mechanic — they need to give the subscriber more to act on, not less.
- 2. **Sharpen the CTA.** "Build Your List" or "Preview the Event" is clearer than "START YOUR LIST." Even "Shop When It's Live" sets better expectations and reinforces the time anchor.
- 3. **Tie "Picked for you" to the event.** Replace the body spray thumbnails with hand care products (PocketBac holders, foaming soap scents) so the section previews the sale rather than cross-selling a different category.
- 4. **Name the end date.** Add "Ends 7/18" or "48 hours only" under "STARTS TOMORROW!" — it creates pull-forward urgency without implying the deal is live now.
- 5. **Add a rewards callout.** A single line — "Rewards members, your points stack on $1 PocketBacs" — costs nothing and lifts click intent for the core subscriber base.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$1 hand sanitizers land tomorrow — lather up 🫧`
- **Subject Alt B:** `your next haul: $1 PocketBacs start tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Clean Hands Event: $1 sanitizers, $2 sprays, $3.50 soaps — starts 7/18`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `PocketBacs $1 · Foaming Soaps $3.50 · starts tomorrow, shop early`

The two entries with empty `src` are likely conditional blocks (AMPScript/SSJS) that resolved to nothing at render time. If image-blocking is on, screen readers will announce these as unlabeled images. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.

**Duplicate Google Fonts `@import`:**

- Inline `<style>` block inside the email body imports `Source+Sans+Pro:wght@400;700`

The first import is redundant and inconsistent (missing weight declarations). Most clients will honor the second, but the extra HTTP request is unnecessary.

**Content rendered before `<body>` tag:**
```html

<table ... class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">...</table>  ← outside <body>
<!-- date artifact -->

```
The SFMC preheader slot table is injected between `</head>` and `<body>`. Technically malformed per HTML spec; most email clients tolerate it via error recovery, but it is not guaranteed behavior.
