# Festive Fragrance Event - final hours. shop all your holiday faves. 🫧🎄❄️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, offer-led send. The "Final hours" bar sits above a Festive Fragrance Event hero that says "Buy 1 get 1 free," with "Lowest-priced item is free" beneath it. The Snowflakes & Cashmere products and the green-teal backdrop look good. The eye lands on the BOGO, and the HOLLYJOLLY code appears twice, with a barcode for in-store use.
- The email then dilutes itself. After the hero come a second offer (Buy 3 get 3 FREE on full-size body care), a perfume launch (A Thousand Wishes), a product grid, a "feel good formula" brand block and a store-finder band. The result is a catalog, not a single push.
- This is also the second BOGO send today. The 11/05 "buy 1 🎁 get 1 🎁" email carried the same HOLLYJOLLY offer and the same mechanic. A subscriber would see this one as a repeat with "final hours" added. The urgency is credible, but the cadence is heavy. Overall it's a solid 7 that is held back by clutter and repetition.

## What's working

- The hero puts "Buy 1 get 1 free" in large type with a clear "Shop now" button.
- The "Final hours" banner sits above the hero and adds visible urgency.
- The HOLLYJOLLY code appears beside the CTA and again under the barcode, with separate in-store and online instructions.
- Product photography is clean and seasonal, with Snowflakes & Cashmere in a cohesive winter palette.
- The Rewards strip at the top gives members a visible benefit.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers, BOGO and Buy 3 get 3 free, split attention. The second sits in small text under the perfume block.
- The hero doesn't say which items qualify beyond "select holiday items."
- The "You might like" grid has three small, low-contrast items with no prices and no CTAs, so they give the reader little reason to click.
- The "Our feel good formula" and "More to love" blocks add length without adding conversion value.
- The fine print is a very long wall of text, and the two promotions' terms run together.
- "Up to 24 per customer with code*" is tiny and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the BOGO hero, one "shop the BOGO" product row with prices, and the footer. Move Buy 3 get 3 and A Thousand Wishes to a separate send.
- 2. Name the qualifying categories in the hero, for example "Candles, body care, hand soap," so the reader knows what is eligible.
- 3. Replace the generic grid with BOGO-eligible products that show the sale price and an "Add to bag" style CTA.
- 4. Put the countdown or end time ("Ends 5:59 AM ET") in the hero, not just "Final hours."
- 5. Make the code a copy-friendly line at the top of the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: buy 1, get 1 free ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO holiday faves, gone at 5:59 AM ET 🎄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HOLLYJOLLY. Lowest-priced item is free on select holiday items.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Snowflakes & Cashmere, Winter Candy Apple and more. Offer ends tomorrow morning.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Bath & Body Works, "Festive Fragrance Event - final hours"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`cl.S7.exct.net`, `e2.bathandbodyworks.com`). Its main confirmed issues are 6 images with no `alt` text and one dynamic-content image served over plain `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the relay didn't expose the headers, not because they failed.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **The `http://` image is a confirmed issue.** `http://cl.S7.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTEs…` loads over plain HTTP. Clients that block non-HTTPS content may drop it.
- **An image is wrapped in a tracked link whose name is a URL.** The QA check names the `<img>` after `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=110526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_B1G1SSCEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V4`. That suggests the image is wrapped in a link, or that the URL is being used as its label. Either way it has no `alt`.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **6 images have no `alt` text.** If the images fail to load, or a screen reader reads them, there is no text equivalent. The `.png` files are all under `https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`
  - The sixth is the `LiveContent.aspx` image, plus the `everestengagement.com` image noted above. These were the 6 warnings, so check whether any is a tracking pixel that should carry `alt=""`.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')`.** Helvetica Neue isn't a Google Fonts family, so the request should fail. The `.font-main` fallback stack (`Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) covers rendering, but the import is a wasted request.
- **Duplicate charset declarations.** The source has both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8"/>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Possible structure problem (unconfirmed).** In the visible source, a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` follows the `<meta>` tags. I can't see a `</head>` or `<body>` before it, and the HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm it.
- No issues found with dark-mode handling (`prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`) or the MSO conditionals in the visible portion.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may not capture the header, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** The tool notes this is expected through the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` and the image/click domains look aligned, but this is not verified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so neither could be checked.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are wrapped in ESP redirects, and the 42 tracking links were skipped, so destination UTMs and landing pages are unchecked.
- **The visible campaign identifier is `c=110526_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_RDM_B1G1SSCEE_FH_BAL_G6M_V4`.** This is a vendor tracking code, not a standard `utm_*` parameter. Confirm analytics maps it to the right campaign.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 `.png` images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and tracking pixels.
2. Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over HTTPS, or switch the SFMC dynamic-content block to an HTTPS endpoint.
3. Remove the Google Fonts `@import` for Helvetica Neue and rely on the system font stack.
4. Inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Fix the relay capture if they are missing, so these checks stop returning "unknown".
5. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to verify landing pages, redirect depth and UTM or campaign parameters.
6. Check the full, untruncated source for a proper `</head><body>` structure and for the unsubscribe link and address.
