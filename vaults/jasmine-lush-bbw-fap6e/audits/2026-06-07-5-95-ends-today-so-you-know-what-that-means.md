# $5.95 ends today, so you know what that means...🏃‍♀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing bell on the $5.95 body care arc that launched June 5 ("feel good starts now at $5.95! 🤗", 9/10 in this audit). The red "ENDS TODAY!" banner, "$5.95" price badge, and "Smells Like a Good Day" headline are carried over nearly verbatim from that send — which is exactly the right playbook for a closing bell. The hero executes cleanly.
- Below the hero, however, the email stacks a second "ENDS TODAY" section (what appears to be a themed candle collab — "Let the Force Light the Way" — with 3-Wick pricing), then drops into a "TOP OFFERS" product grid with eight or more categories, a store-locator block, and a loyalty guarantee badge. That's a lot of asks. The June 4 and June 3 closing bells kept a tighter single-arc focus; this one reverts to the multi-arc stacking behavior last seen in the June 6 Wallflowers send.
- The result is still a solid, recognizable B&BW closing bell — the hero converts well — but the lower-half clutter diffuses urgency that the subject line and ENDS TODAY banner work hard to build up top.

## What's working

- Red "ENDS TODAY!" banner at the top of the fold is the first thing the eye hits — urgency is immediate.
- "$5.95" price badge is large, legible, and anchors the value prop without needing body copy to explain it.
- "Smells Like a Good Day" creative continuity with the June 5 launch reinforces recognition for anyone who opened that send.
- Subject line's trailing "...you know what that means...🏃‍♀️" adds personality and playfulness without spammy signals.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, text clean, layout intact.

## What's weak

- Stacking two simultaneous "ENDS TODAY" arcs (body care + the candle collab) splits urgency — you can't have two "most urgent things."
- "SHOP" is the CTA label on both hero buttons: a verb with no noun, no destination signal, indistinguishable between the two offers.
- The "TOP OFFERS" grid (eight product tiles below the two hero sections) reads like a catalog dump and has no clear hierarchy or urgency of its own.
- No reviews, social proof, or product-specific detail (scent, size, count) to close a hesitant opener.
- Cadence risk: seven emails in seven days from this brand — even well-executed closing bells start to blur at this frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one arc per closing bell.** The body care $5.95 is the subject-line hero — the candle collab should be its own dedicated send, not buried in section two.
- 2. **Specificity on the CTA buttons.** Replace "SHOP" with "Shop $5.95 Body Care" and "Shop 3-Wick Candles" so each button tells the subscriber where they're going.
- 3. **Collapse the TOP OFFERS grid** into a single tight "Also ending soon →" teaser row — three tiles max — rather than eight untargeted product thumbnails.
- 4. **Add a scent or hero product callout** under the price badge (e.g., "Including Warm Vanilla Sugar, Into the Night + more") to give closet fans a reason to click before the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last hours: $5.95 body care is done tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $5.95 faves expire at midnight 🕛`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All full-size mists, sprays & creams — the price drops back up tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Smells Like a Good Day, but only until tonight. Stock up now.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **5 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed):

### 5. Compliance

**Issues found:**

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** (QA WARN) — This header is typically injected at the MTA/relay layer (SFMC's ExactTarget). Gmail and Apple Mail use this header for native unsubscribe UI; absence degrades inbox placement signals.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected** (RFC 8058 / QA WARN) — One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender rules for >5k/day senders) cannot be confirmed. - **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from this capture. Sending domain `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` is a dedicated SFMC subdomain and is almost certainly DKIM-signed, but cannot be confirmed from the available data.
- **CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism** — not visible in the truncated source. Assumed present in the email footer (standard for BBW); cannot confirm from the provided HTML.
