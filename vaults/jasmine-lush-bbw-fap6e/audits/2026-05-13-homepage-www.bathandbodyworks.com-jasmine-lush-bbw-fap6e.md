# Homepage snapshot · www.bathandbodyworks.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I pulled up Bath & Body Works on my phone and immediately got hit with a bot gate — not the homepage. The entire screen is a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" challenge, with nothing else visible except a clipped wordmark and a reference ID at the bottom. I cannot see any products, promos, nav, or anything that says "shop here." This is a complete wall before the front door.

## What's working

- The "Press & Hold" button is clearly legible and centered — at least the friction mechanic itself is unambiguous
- The brand wordmark is recognizable even at partial crop, so I know where I landed

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is hidden** behind a bot challenge — I can see nothing: no hero, no candle imagery, no Semi-Annual Sale tease, no rewards hook, nothing
- **Logo is visibly clipped** — the "s" in "Works" bleeds off the right edge of the viewport, which is a render bug on mobile
- **Zero shopping context** — no nav, no category tiles, no promo strip, no indication of what's currently on sale
- **Blank white void** above and below the challenge — Bath & Body Works is a heavily visual, scent-forward brand and this screen communicates absolutely nothing about that
- **Reference ID at the bottom** (`a5676930-4f03-11f1-9344-e1f75d1076d9`) is visible to users, which feels unpolished and technical for a mass-market beauty brand

## Recommendations

- **Remove or reduce the bot gate for mobile organic traffic** — returning My Bath & Body Works Rewards members on mobile are being blocked before they ever see a candle; a logged-in cookie or behavioral signal should bypass this entirely
- **If a gate is required, brand it** — instead of a plain white challenge screen, use the BBW signature gingham or a simple product background so the brand experience starts immediately rather than feeling like a server error
- **Fix the wordmark viewport clip** — the logo should be fully visible at all mobile widths; this looks broken right now
- **At minimum, surface one static promo strip above the challenge** (e.g., "Semi-Annual Sale is on — sign in to shop") so the visit isn't a complete dead end before authentication
