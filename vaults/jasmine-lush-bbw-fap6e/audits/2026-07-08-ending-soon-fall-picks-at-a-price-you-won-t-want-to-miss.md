# ending soon: fall picks at a price you won't want to miss. 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a catch-all "ending soon" send that's pulling in too many directions at once. The hero pushes **Wallflowers Fall Fragrance Plugs** — a deliberate early-fall pivot — while the secondary block quietly continues the Fruit Fusion moisturizer campaign from July 6–7. Below the fold, there's a two-row "TOP OFFERS / TODAY'S DEALS" product grid, a "MORE TO LOVE" sign-up nudge, and a "Find a Store" button. That's five distinct content blocks competing for a single tap.
- The timing is the biggest conceptual problem. After a tight campaign arc — $1 PocketBac (July 2) → $3.50 Room Sprays (July 3) → July 4th candle send → Semi-Annual Sale close (July 5) → Flash Sale (July 5 PM) → two Fruit Fusion launches (July 6–7) — pivoting to "fall picks" on July 8 feels like an editorial reset that subscribers aren't ready for. The seasonal whiplash is real.
- That said, the price-led "ENDS TODAY! $3.95 Wallflowers Fragrance Refills" hook is the clearest signal in the whole email, and it does its job. If this were the only thing in the email, it would be a solid send. Instead it's buried under competing visual noise.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TODAY! $3.95"** is specific, urgent, and immediately visible — the strongest copy in the email.
- **Fruit Fusion continuity** in the secondary block is a smart editorial thread; it rewards subscribers who opened the July 6–7 sends without requiring context from newcomers.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean.
- **Brand identity** is consistent throughout: B&BW logo, color palette, and the "NOT guaranteed" satisfaction tagline are all present.

## What's weak

- **Fall in July** is jarring — especially landing just one day after the second Fruit Fusion summer launch. The creative tonal shift is abrupt.
- **The subject line is vague** — "fall picks at a price you won't want to miss" names no price, no product, no category. It's a tease that doesn't tease anything specific.
- **Too many CTAs** — "SHOP" (hero), "SHOP THE COLLECTION" (Fruit Fusion), a four-item grid, another four-item grid, two "MORE TO LOVE" links, "Find a Store." The click intent is scattered.
- **"TOP OFFERS / TODAY'S DEALS" grid** has tiny thumbnails (Gingham, Body Care, Candles, Wallflowers) that are too small to merchandise at this rendering size.
- **Cadence is at its limit** — this is email #7 in 8 days. The "ending soon" framing adds a low-grade Groundhog Day quality given the week's volume.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email to one story.** Either this is the Wallflowers Fall launch (with the $3.95 refill as the value hook) OR it's another Fruit Fusion send. Not both. Splitting attention across two hero campaigns reduces conversion on both.
- 2. **Put the price in the subject line.** "$3.95 Fragrance Refills" is the most concrete reason to open — say it.
- 3. **Collapse the two product grids** into one row of three items or remove them entirely. The tiny thumbnails aren't merchandising; they're padding.
- 4. **Anchor the fall timing** — add a single line of copy ("stock up before fall arrives") to address the July-in-fall cognitive gap. Right now the seasonal mismatch goes unexplained.
- 5. **Strengthen the hero CTA copy** — "SHOP" does nothing. "Shop $3.95 Refills" is specific and actionable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$3.95 Wallflowers Refills — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall fragrance for $3.95. Ends today. 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up before fall arrives — Fragrance Refills at their lowest price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends tonight: Wallflowers Refills $3.95 + All-Day Moisture just one shower away.`

## Technical Audit — Bath & Body Works "ending soon: fall picks" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) on the `e2.bathandbodyworks.com` subdomain using standard HTML4 Transitional email markup. Multiple template rendering failures left unprocessed build tokens and AMPscript developer comments in the delivered source; additionally, two critical compliance headers required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy are absent.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**25 click-redirect links** all route through `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` — QA skipped HTTP probing on these, so destination URLs and UTM parameter presence are **unverified**.

**Open pixel** at `https://click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/open.aspx?U5GXA3FOPLNUXAYWOQ7YJUKKSE.70251&d=70251&bmt=0` uses `alt=""` — acceptable for a 1×1 tracker but flagged here for completeness.

**Everest engagement pixel** (`https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=070826_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_395WF_AM_NTH_G6M_V`) is missing `alt` attribute — confirmed by QA.

**Two images have empty `src` attributes** and no `alt` text — these will fire 404 requests against the image host. Likely broken conditional content that wasn't stripped pre-send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Content outside `<body>` tag** — a `<table>` element and preheader `<div>` are placed between `</head>` and `<body>`. This is invalid HTML and behavior is client-dependent; some clients may drop or misplace this content.

```html
</head>
<table ...>  <!-- ← outside <body> -->
  <tr><td><div class="preheader" ...>shop now and pretend it's September...</div></td></tr>
</table>
<body ...>
```

**`<meta charset>` declared late** — `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` appears at the bottom of `<head>`, after `<style>` blocks. Should be the first element in `<head>` to ensure correct parser character interpretation before any content is consumed.

**Google Fonts imported twice** — `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Source+Sans+Pro...')` appears in `<head>` (weight 400 only) and again inline in the email body (weights 400+700). Double network request; both calls are blocked by Outlook and most corporate email clients, triggering the Arial/Helvetica fallback stack for the majority of desktop users.

**Missing alt text on content image** — `418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png` (`https://image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/418d0db7-5a65-43cb-b5c9-9c6c142e4fd2.png`) has no `alt` attribute — confirmed by QA.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unprocessed shell token (×2)** — `<!-- date "+ET%m.%y" -->` appears twice verbatim in the HTML source. This is a shell command fragment that was never evaluated. It is inside HTML comments so not subscriber-visible, but indicates a pre-send build step failure.

**AMPscript developer comments left in rendered output:**
```html
<!--Adds daily APT set via ampscript. Adds preset APT for emails flagged NTF-->
<!--Set APT for NTF campaigns, otherwise respect email-defined APT-->
<!--If @NTF_Flag is "N", @APT should be set in the email; no default fallback here-->
```
These are production template debugging notes that should be stripped or conditionally excluded before delivery.

**Unpopulated content slot** — `<div data-type="slot" data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9" data-label="Preheader">` is present in source as an empty placeholder tag; it was never populated or removed.

**Empty AMPscript variable block** — `<!--Set variables to support dynamic template attributes-->` is followed by an empty `<td>` with no rendered variable output. No fallback content is present.

### 5. Compliance

| Header | Status | Evidence |
|--------|--------|----------|
| `List-Unsubscribe` | **MISSING** | QA: "Header not found" |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` | **MISSING** | QA: "RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe may not be supported" |

`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are **required by Gmail and Yahoo Mail bulk sender policy** (enforced since February 2024 for senders exceeding 5,000/day). Absence risks inbox placement degradation and prevents Gmail's native one-click unsubscribe UI from functioning.

Physical mailing address and footer unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM compliance for those elements, but they are assumed present given the ESP template.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 25 CTAs are wrapped in `click.e2.bathandbodyworks.com` redirects — HTTP probing was skipped, so **UTM parameter propagation to landing pages is unverified**.

Campaign identifier is embedded in the Everest pixel URL: `070826_EM_JUNSAS_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_395WF_AM_NTH_G6M_V`. This confirms SFMC-side campaign tagging exists but does not confirm that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` parameters are appended to destination URLs. Manual spot-check of one decoded click URL is needed.

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Deliverability risk:**
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers to the SFMC send definition. Without these, Gmail/Yahoo filtering risk is active on every deployment.

**P1 — Template rendering failures:**
2. Investigate why `<!-- date "+ET%m.%y" -->` tokens are reaching delivered HTML — indicates a pre-send rendering pipeline step is failing or misconfigured.
3. Strip AMPscript developer comments from production templates (environment-conditional or build-time removal).
4. Populate or remove the empty `<div data-type="slot" data-key="gvhu7dvhmx9">` preheader slot.

**P2 — HTML validity and accessibility:**
5. Move the preheader `<table>` inside the `<body>` tag to correct the invalid structure.
6. Move `<meta charset>` to first position in `<head>`.
7. Add `alt` text to `418d0db7...png` and the Everest pixel `<img>`.
8. Remove or fix the two images with empty `src` attributes — these are generating unnecessary 404s.
9. Deduplicate Google Fonts `@import` to a single call with both weights (`wght@400;700`); evaluate inlining a character subset to improve Outlook fallback behavior.

**P3 — Verification needed:**
10. Probe a sample of decoded click-redirect URLs to confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=...`) are present on landing pages.
