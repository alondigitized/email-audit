# 🌟 $10 single wicks 🌟 for a short time.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same $10 single-wick offer as yesterday's "🎀 put a bow on it" send (11/2), re-sent with a new subject and the same "Merry wicks, right on time" hero. The hero still works. A glowing winter candle, a huge "$10", "All Single Wick Candles" and a Shop now button put the offer first. A subscriber who got both emails would find nothing new here.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a catalog. A Thousand Wishes gets two panels, one in purple and one in pink. There is also a "Buy 3 get 3 FREE" body care line, a three-item product row and a brand-values block. None of these push the candle offer. "Limited time only" appears twice for two different offers, which splits attention and weakens the urgency.
- Verdict: a solid hero attached to a diffuse body, and a repeat of the day before. It beats the mystery "FLASH sale" send (11/1), but it does not match the $10.95 3-wick run for clarity.

## What's working

- The $10 is large and sits next to "All Single Wick Candles", so the offer reads at a glance.
- The hero image is seasonal and looks good, and "Merry wicks" is on-brand wordplay.
- The Shop now button is clear and sits right beside the price.
- "Excludes boxed candles" is stated up front, so the offer feels honest.
- The Rewards strip at the top gives members a visible nudge.

## What's weak

- "Limited time only" appears twice with no date or deadline, so it isn't credible urgency.
- Two A Thousand Wishes panels and a body care BOGO compete with the $10 hero.
- The "You might like" row shows a car refill, hand soap and sanitizer. None of them is a candle, so none supports the hero offer.
- The pink panel has a stray empty outlined box with "More fragrance for long-lasting wear" floating in it, which looks like a layout oddity.
- Buy 3 get 3 FREE has only a small text link and a Shop now button, with no product image.

- 2. Cut the second Thousand Wishes panel and turn "You might like" into a row of $10 candles in popular scents.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`stylingblock-content-wrapper camarker-inner`) with solid responsive and dark-mode CSS. Confirmed defects are 5 content images missing `alt`, a `<table>` placed inside `<head>`, and an invalid `@import` font. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 42 links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of the destination URLs, status codes or redirect chains were verified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://bbwus.everestengagement.com/ea/fw0q51vctP/?e=200511831233&c=110326_EM_FA_BAU_PROMO_0_NRDM_10SW_AM_BAL_G6M_V1` was flagged as an image without alt text. It is almost certainly the open-tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""`. That is a QA misclassification rather than a content defect, but the missing attribute can't be ruled out from the data.
- **Identifier in URL:** the pixel URL carries a numeric `e=200511831233` parameter, apparently a per-recipient identifier. This is normal for ESP tracking, so I'm not flagging it as an issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 5 images)**, all on `image.e2.bathandbodyworks.com/lib/fe9213737561047a72/m/1/`:
  - `4851c40c-9928-4c38-88b8-02583dd793aa.png`
  - `d6327aef-bba4-4c90-8be7-77b3d8306641.png`
  - `00a9ac12-8940-4f37-9570-9b23c6fb7b9c.png`
  - `7b7c94ef-aaa3-42f7-8531-38bf7f901ae6.png`
  - `bbebdf75-1fc0-48a3-96d4-ad8652de93f7.png`

  If any of these carry offer text (such as "$10 single wicks"), images-off clients lose it entirely.
- **Invalid document structure:** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` appears directly after the `<meta>` tags inside `<head>`, with no `</head>` or `<body>` first. Browsers and some clients will auto-correct this, but the behavior varies by client. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm whether `<body>` appears later.
- **Font `@import` is ineffective:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Helvetica+Neue&display=swap')` requests a family Google Fonts doesn't serve, so the request will fail. Most clients (Gmail, Outlook) also strip `@import`. The `font-family` fallback stack to Helvetica/Arial covers the rendering impact.
- **Minor redundancy:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - The `OfficeDocumentSettings` block is duplicated under `[if mso]` and `[if gte mso 9]`.
  - The HTML 4.01 doctype is paired with HTML5-style `<meta charset>`.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, dark-mode handling (`color-scheme`, `prefers-color-scheme`, `[data-ogsc]`) and a mobile breakpoint at 600px/380px.
