# Homepage snapshot · ritual.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Ritual is doing almost everything right for someone like me — clean aesthetic, "traceable science and sourcing" is literally my love language, and the Pregnancy tab is sitting right there in the nav like they know exactly why I showed up. The hero is a little off-pitch ("Body Builders" is gym-bro energy, not prenatal-planner energy), but that subline — "for living life or creating it" — is a quiet wink that I actually felt. This homepage reads as a premium, ingredient-obsessed wellness brand running a holiday sale.

## What's working

- **"Traceable science and sourcing"** — that subhead is written for me. I genuinely read ingredient origin stories before subscribing anywhere, and this signals exactly the transparency I want from a prenatal brand.
- **"Pregnancy" tab in the nav** — it's the second or third visible option and I could tap it immediately. No hunting required to find my category.
- **The "creating it" subhead** — understated, not saccharine. Doesn't scream "baby bump incoming!" but signals they know their customer. I appreciate that register.
- **Hero image energy** — joyful, confident young woman holding the product against a blue sky. It's summer, it's a holiday weekend, and she looks like someone I'd aspire to feel like. Works.
- **"Shop the Sale" highlighted pill** — the yellow/gold highlight differentiates it from the other nav tabs; I noticed it instantly.

## What's weak

- **"For the Real Body Builders"** — the headline. That's not me. I'm not building muscle; I'm building a baby (soon). This is a missed opportunity to lead with a prenatal or women's health hook, especially since the brand has one.
- **"Shop All" CTA** — vague. I'd rather see "Shop Prenatal" or "Shop Pregnancy" as the primary button, given that the Pregnancy tab is already right there. "Shop All" makes me work harder.
- **No specific discount amount** — "Limited Time Savings" at the bottom edge of the screen is a tease with no payoff. What's the discount? 20%? $10 off? I compare Ritual with Care/of and Persona; without a number I can't benchmark.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no review count, no "4.8 stars from 50,000 subscribers," no press badge. Ritual has a strong rep but I don't see evidence of it here.
- **No loyalty or subscription hook** — Ritual's whole model is subscriptions, but there's no "pause anytime," "subscribe & save," or member pricing mentioned above the fold. That's what would actually differentiate them from a one-time-purchase competitor.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Body Builders" for a dual-message hero** that speaks to both fitness and family planning — something like "Built for What You're Building" or A/B test a Pregnancy-specific hero for users who land on the Pregnancy tab.
- **Change the CTA from "Shop All" to "Shop Prenatal"** (or make it dynamic based on the active nav state) — one tap closer to my product and far less friction.
- **Add a specific offer callout** to the "Limited Time Savings" strip — even "20% off sitewide this weekend" would let me compare and commit; vague savings feel like a bait click.
- **Pin one trust signal below the hero** — star rating + review count, or a "pause or cancel anytime" subscription badge — before the first scroll. That's the objection I need answered before I go any further.
