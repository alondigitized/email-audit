# Homepage snapshot · ritual.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Ritual doing what Ritual does — clean, minimal, yellow, unmistakably "we know you've already Googled our methylfolate sourcing." The homepage is pushing a sale event right now, which is actually useful because I've been eyeing their Essential Prenatal for a while. That said, the above-the-fold experience on mobile is almost entirely hero image, and I have to scroll before I can actually do anything.

## What's working

- **"Pregnancy" as a top nav tab** — one tap to my exact category without opening a mega-menu. This is the right call for mobile and it earns immediate goodwill.
- **The capsule imagery** — if you're already in the Ritual funnel, this image is a Pavlovian trigger. The yellow-on-yellow minimalism signals premium supplement, not CVS aisle.
- **"Shop the Sale" in the nav strip** — I notice this immediately and it raises my engagement because I've been on the fence about subscribing; a sale feels like the sign I needed.
- **Headline legibility** — "The future of health is *clear*" renders cleanly at mobile font sizes, the italic "clear" is a nice visual beat.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold** — on a phone, I see a beautiful photo and a headline that fades out. I don't know where to tap. The purchase intent this brand works hard to build in email gets stranded here.
- **Hero is product-only, no human** — I'm 32 and planning a pregnancy; I want to see someone in a version of my life stage, not a floating capsule still-life. Care/of does lifestyle imagery here; Ritual feels colder.
- **"Build your Ritual and save up to 30% this..." is cut off** — the offer line is amputated mid-sentence. I have to scroll to find out what the deadline is, what the mechanism is, whether it applies to subscriptions or one-time. That friction is real.
- **No ingredient/transparency hook above the fold** — Ritual's entire moat is "we tell you exactly where each nutrient comes from." None of that is visible here. The headline says "clear" but doesn't cash it out with anything I can verify.

## Recommendations

- **Add a floating CTA strip or anchor button** — "Shop Prenatal" pinned just above the scroll line so there's always one obvious action visible without fully scrolling past the hero.
- **Swap the still-life capsule hero for a lifestyle shot of a woman in the planning/early-pregnancy phase** — she doesn't need to be visibly pregnant, just early-30s, intentional, holding the bottle or taking her capsule in a real-kitchen setting.
- **Surface one ingredient callout in the sub-hero copy** — even a single line like "Chelated iron that won't upset your stomach — sourced from [origin]" does more brand differentiation work than the current offer snippet.
- **Complete the offer copy in-viewport** — restructure the hero module so "Save up to 30% — ends [date]" is fully visible; truncated offers read like dark patterns even when they aren't.
