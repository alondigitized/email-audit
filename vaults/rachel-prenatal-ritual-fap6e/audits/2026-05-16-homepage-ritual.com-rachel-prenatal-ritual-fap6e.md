# Homepage snapshot · ritual.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Ritual is clearly positioning itself as the thoughtful, science-first alternative to mystery-ingredient vitamins — and as someone who obsessively reads every ingredient-origin story before committing to a subscription, that message lands immediately. The hero pill shot on that yellow gradient is genuinely beautiful, and the subhead copy ("for living life or creating it") reads like someone at Ritual actually knows I exist. That said, the above-the-fold experience is almost entirely brand-building — I can see the vibe but I can't do anything yet.

## What's working

- **"For living life or creating it"** — this subhead is doing heavy lifting. It's inclusive of both active-lifestyle shoppers and family-planners without screaming "PRENATAL" in capital letters. Exactly how I want to be spoken to.
- **The clear-capsule hero image** — showing the actual pill construction is a genius transparency move. I can literally *see* the beadlets inside. For someone who reads ingredient sourcing, this is instant credibility before a single product page loads.
- **"Traceable science and sourcing"** — this phrase is almost verbatim what I'd type into Google when comparing Care/of vs. Ritual. It confirms they understand my decision criteria.
- **Clean mobile layout** — hamburger + wordmark + bag icon is uncluttered. No intrusive modal or pop-up over the hero on load (at least none visible in the screenshot).

## What's weak

- **No CTA button above the fold** — I'm on a phone, the brand message landed, and now I have nowhere to go without scrolling. That's a missed tap.
- **No offer visible** — I know Ritual runs subscription discounts and first-order deals. None of that is surfaced here. As someone comparing Ritual to Care/of and Persona, price is absolutely a factor I weigh before committing.
- **No social proof** — not a single review count, star rating, or "trusted by X subscribers" stat. The pill image is beautiful but I want to know that other people with my exact situation (TTC, first trimester) have validated this.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — one extra tap to even find "Essential Prenatal" feels like friction when I came here with specific intent. A sticky category strip would help.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CTA button directly below the subhead** — "Shop Essential Prenatal" with a subscription-first price shown inline. Don't make me scroll to act.
- **Surface a first-order offer above the fold** — even "20% off your first subscription" as a subtle persistent strip under the nav would stop me from immediately opening Care/of to compare.
- **Add a trust count in the hero zone** — "200,000+ subscribers" or "4.8 stars, 50K reviews" placed between the subhead and the CTA converts the brand message into social proof without redesigning the layout.
- **Test a lifestyle insert alongside the pill** — a secondary image of someone clearly in "creating it" mode (not a stock bump photo, but a real lifestyle moment like a morning ritual on a kitchen counter) would make the hero feel less clinical and more like my life.
