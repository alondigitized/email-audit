# Homepage snapshot · ritual.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Ritual is leading with brand clarity over commerce here — the hero is pure product photography of their signature yellow capsule with a tagline that's elegant but leaves me doing interpretive work. The "for living life or creating it" sub-copy is the only thing that even hints at my prenatal focus, and it's easy to miss. This feels like a fashion brand that also sells vitamins, which is a vibe I respect, but there's no hand-holding toward the product I actually came here for.

## What's working

- **"for living life or creating it" sub-copy** — that second clause lands for me. It's the one line that says *this brand knows I'm planning a pregnancy* without being clinical about it.
- **The capsule hero image** — showing the actual product in macro detail reinforces the "traceable, clear" positioning beautifully. I can see what I'm putting in my body, which is a core trust trigger for me.
- **"Traceable science and sourcing" copy** — this is exactly the language that makes me slow down. I read ingredient origin stories; a brand that leads with sourcing transparency earns a few more seconds of my attention.
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — no pop-up modals, no aggressive banners. I'm not immediately on defense.

## What's weak

- **Zero CTA above the fold** — I've watched a pretty capsule video and read a tagline, but there's nowhere to go. The pause button (⏸) near the hero is the only interactive element I can see, and it's for stopping the animation, not shopping.
- **No prenatal callout** — Ritual's prenatal line is their most known product, but nothing above the fold names it. "Creating it" is too subtle for a high-intent shopper who's comparing this against Care/of right now.
- **No offer or value hook** — no free shipping bar, no "first order" discount, no subscription intro rate. Care/of and Persona both hit me with a % off on first subscribe. Ritual is asking me to buy on vibes alone.
- **Hamburger menu kills category navigation** — tapping "Menu" to find Prenatal is two taps I shouldn't have to make.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sticky prenatal entry point** — a pill-shaped badge or sub-nav chip reading "Essential Prenatal" or "For You: Prenatal" that surfaces immediately for users landing during family-planning research.
- **Surface a first-subscribe offer above the fold** — even "free shipping on your first subscription" gives me a reason to act now instead of opening a new tab for Care/of.
- **Swap the sub-copy hierarchy** — move "for living life or creating it" *above* "Traceable science and sourcing" so the emotional hook lands before the functional one.
- **Add a visible CTA button** — something like `Shop Prenatal` or `Find My Ritual` directly below the headline so I don't have to scroll to find a path forward.
