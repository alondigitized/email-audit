# Homepage snapshot · ritual.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: this feels like a premium wellness brand that knows its aesthetic — the oversized capsule close-up on a saturated yellow background is stunning and unmistakably Ritual. The sub-copy "for living life or creating it" caught me immediately; that's a direct wink at someone in my exact headspace. But above the fold it's almost entirely brand theater — no CTA, no offer, no way to get to a product without hunting.

## What's working

- **"for living life or creating it"** — that phrase is doing enormous work. As someone actively in the "creating it" phase, this sub-copy stops my scroll cold. It's the most targeted thing on this screen.
- **The capsule close-up hero** — I read every ingredient-origin story; showing me the actual capsule in macro detail signals transparency before I've even tapped anything. It's visual proof of the "traceable" claim.
- **"Traceable science and sourcing"** — four words that map perfectly to how I shop. This isn't fluffy wellness language; it's a direct promise to the person who's going to Google every ingredient anyway.
- **Minimal nav chrome** — clean header doesn't compete with the message. High-tech comfort readers appreciate restraint.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button in the viewport** — I want to tap something. There's a pause button on the video but nothing that moves me toward prenatal vitamins. I'm motivated and there's nowhere to go.
- **No product specificity above the fold** — the hero signals "vitamins" broadly, but doesn't say "Essential Prenatal" or any SKU. I'm comparing with Care/of and Persona; I need a faster path to the specific product I'm researching.
- **No offer or subscription hook** — I subscribe and pause mid-trimester. Ritual's subscription model is a core differentiator, but it's completely invisible here. No "subscribe and save," no free-shipping threshold, nothing.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "clinically backed" badge, no third-party cert. For a brand competing on ingredient transparency, leaving these off the first screen is a missed reassurance beat.
- **"Menu" hamburger only** — one tap, sure, but I can't see at a glance whether prenatal is even a category. A visible category strip would cut my bounce risk significantly.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent below-hero CTA strip** — something like `Shop Essential Prenatal →` with a sub-line "subscribe & save 15%" directly under the headline. One week to ship, massive impact on scroll-to-click rate.
- **Swap the generic sub-copy for a trimester hook** — "Whether you're planning, pregnant, or postpartum — your formula, traced." Names where I am in the journey and signals the right product lineup.
- **Surface a trust badge inline with the hero copy** — a small "3rd-party tested" or "clinically studied ingredients" tag under the headline costs zero layout space and answers the question I'm already forming.
- **Add a promo banner above the hero** — even "Free shipping on subscriptions" in a slim sticky bar would keep me from bouncing to Care/of's homepage to comparison shop.
