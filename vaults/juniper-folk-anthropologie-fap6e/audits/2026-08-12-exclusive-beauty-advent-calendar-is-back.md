# Exclusive: Beauty Advent Calendar is BACK
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie running the Beauty Advent Calendar play for the second time in three days — August 10's "It's here: The Beauty Advent Calendar" (7.00/10) was a soft, waitlist-framed early-access tease; this one drops the waitlist and goes straight to a hard sell with "Back by popular demand" and a "SHOP BEFORE IT'S GONE" button. That's a sensible sequencing move (build anticipation, then convert), but sending the same product twice in 72 hours to the same list risks fatigue, and an "advent calendar" pitch in mid-August still reads as a strange seasonal mismatch no matter how it's framed.
- The hero itself is well-built: a dreamy illustrated calendar box on a deep-purple night-sky background, a "$300 VALUE!" starburst badge, and a clean CTA. It's the strongest single module in the email. Below the fold, though, the send sprawls into eight unrelated "MORE TO EXPLORE" modules (loungewear, denim, sweater dresses, shoes, lip products, a nine-photo product grid) that dilute the beauty hook entirely — by module four you'd never guess this was a "beauty" email.
- Verdict: a good hero, buried under a generic catalog dump, sent too soon after a near-identical predecessor. Solid open bait, mediocre follow-through.

## What's working

- The hero illustration (pink Parisian-style calendar box, twinkle lights, "$300 VALUE!" badge) is genuinely eye-catching and on-brand.
- CTA hierarchy is clean — one clear button ("SHOP BEFORE IT'S GONE") sits directly under the offer with no competing asks.
- "Featuring Boy Smells & more" gives one concrete brand name, adding credibility over a vague "top brands" claim.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, no overlapping elements.
- The PERKSFEST membership banner and inclusive-sizing/Klarna footer strip add real loyalty-program value beyond the hero offer.

## What's weak

- This is the second Beauty Advent Calendar send in three days (Aug 10 "It's here," now Aug 12 "Exclusive... is BACK") — near-identical hero art and message, which reads as repetitive rather than a fresh beat.
- "$300 VALUE!" is not a discount — there's no %/$ off, so the "exclusive" subject over-promises relative to what's actually inside.
- The beauty focus completely evaporates after the hero; eight subsequent modules (hoodies, denim, dresses, shoes, lip edit, product grid) have nothing to do with the advent calendar and crowd out any secondary beauty CTA.
- "SHOP BEFORE IT'S GONE" implies scarcity but gives no actual deadline or stock count, so the urgency doesn't feel fully credible.
- A beauty advent calendar pitched in mid-August, four months before December, is a seasonal mismatch that undercuts the "exclusive" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend the same product this fast — if a second touch is needed, change the creative angle (e.g., lead with "Boy Smells" as the star ingredient/brand rather than repeating the box art) or wait at least a week.
- 2. Add a real, specific offer element (e.g., "free gift with purchase" or a genuine %-off) rather than relying on "$300 value" alone — that's a value claim, not an incentive.
- 3. Trim the post-hero real estate — cut at least half the unrelated "MORE TO EXPLORE" modules, or replace them with beauty-adjacent cross-sells (skincare, fragrance) to keep the email on-theme.
- 4. Put a real deadline or unit count on the urgency line ("Only 500 left" / "Sells out by [date]") instead of the vague "before it's gone."
- 5. Move PERKSFEST and inclusive-sizing/Klarna up or keep as-is, but consider a mid-email beauty recap module before the catalog sprawl starts, to re-anchor to the subject line.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `24 days, one box, way under retail — while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boy Smells, cult favorites & more inside. Limited restock.`
