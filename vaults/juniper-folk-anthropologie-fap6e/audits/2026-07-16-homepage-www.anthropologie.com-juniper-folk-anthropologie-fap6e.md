# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so — I didn't actually get to see Anthropologie's homepage. What loaded instead is a bot-detection wall: "We detected unusual activity from your device or network. Complete this verification check to continue." There's a CAPTCHA slider and a list of reasons I might've been flagged. For a brand that's supposed to feel warm and invitation-only, this is a cold slap before I've even seen a product. I'm already annoyed.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark at the top is clean and legible — brand recognition still fires immediately even on a block screen.
- The cookie banner is present ("We use cookies. Learn more.") which at least signals the site has functional JS infrastructure somewhere.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is gated behind a CAPTCHA slider** — I never see a product, an offer, or a hero image. The shopping experience is dead on arrival.
- **The bot-detection messaging is clinical and cold** — "Unusual activity from your device or network" reads like a server error log, not a brand that knows how to make me feel like a welcomed regular.
- **My IP is exposed in the error text** (47.229.200.33) — that's a weird, privacy-adjacent detail to surface to a casual shopper. Slightly unsettling.
- **"Use of developer or inspection tools" listed as a reason** — any time a power-user shopper (and I do zoom / inspect images to check fabric textures on mobile) gets flagged, that's friction for exactly the customer you want.
- **"Submit Feedback About Experience" link** at the very bottom feels like an afterthought apology, not a real recovery path.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic bot-wall with a soft CAPTCHA that stays on-brand** — a gentle, illustrated challenge (Anthro's whimsical illustration style would work perfectly here) instead of a sterile enterprise security screen.
- **Pre-load the hero image behind the verification modal** so I can see the campaign while completing the check — give me a reason to complete it.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the visible error text** — no shopper needs to see that, and it erodes trust.
- **Add a one-tap "Text me a code instead" or AppleID-based bypass** for logged-in AnthroPerks members — returning loyalty customers should never hit this wall.
