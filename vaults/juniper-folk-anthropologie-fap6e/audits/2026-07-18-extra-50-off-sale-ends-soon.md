# Extra 50% off sale ends soon!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's follow-up to the July 15 "Extra 50% off sale + new styles added?" send, and the pivot is smart: that email led with newness, this one leads with closing urgency — "ends soon." The hero executes cleanly: the "NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge stacks on top of a clear "EXTRA 50% Off\* SALE" headline with a high-contrast SHOP NOW button. That's the right escalation arc.
- The serious problem is the secondary module. Below the 50% off hero, the brand serves the identical "30% off fashionably early fall event" botanical-olive creative that has now appeared in at least six of the last seven Anthropologie sends since July 6. Subscribers who've been in this cadence for twelve days are going to clock this as stale filler before they even notice the scarcity messaging at the bottom. Having two different active discount levels — 50% and 30% — in the same email with no clear delineation of what each applies to is also genuinely confusing.
- The "THERE ARE SO FEW LEFT..." section near the bottom is the most underutilized asset in this email. Scarcity language that specific deserves to be surfaced higher. Right now it's buried after five product category modules, where most recipients will never see it.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is clean** — "NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge → "EXTRA 50% Off\*" → SHOP NOW reads in one eye pass without confusion.
- **Category navigation row** — five distinct sale sub-categories (Clothing, Home, Outdoor, Shoes, Kitchen & Bath) let different buyer types self-select immediately instead of hunting.
- **"NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge** — directly resolves the reason to open this email vs. the July 15 version, giving the send a defensible reason to exist.
- **Scarcity copy is present** — "THERE ARE SO FEW LEFT..." is the strongest urgency signal in the email; it just needs to be higher.
- **AnthroPerks loyalty footer** — reinforces member identity at close.

## What's weak

- **Recycled fall event secondary module** — the same botanical-olive "fashionably early fall event 30% off" creative appearing for the sixth-plus consecutive send is visible creative fatigue; loyal subscribers will recognize it as filler.
- **Dual discount confusion** — 50% off sale and 30% off fall event coexist with no visible explanation of which applies to what, risking cart abandonment when customers hit checkout exclusions.
- **"Ends soon" has no date** — the urgency claim in the subject never resolves to a specific deadline anywhere in the body render, which defuses the credibility of the close.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — the primary button could be "Shop 50% Off Sale" and tell the user exactly what they're clicking into.
- **Scarcity buried** — "THERE ARE SO FEW LEFT..." appears below five editorial modules; most eyes won't reach it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the scarcity message** — move "THERE ARE SO FEW LEFT..." or a version of it into the hero subhead ("Only a few left at 50% off") or a tight strip immediately below the primary CTA. This is the strongest motivator in the email and it's invisible to scroll-shy recipients.
- 2. **Kill the recycled fall event module** — replace it with a single curated "Before it's gone" product grid of 3–4 bestsellers that are genuinely low-stock. Show inventory urgency, not creative inventory.
- 3. **Resolve the dual-discount confusion** — either retire the 30% off module entirely or add a single line: "Sale styles: extra 50% off. Fashionably Early Fall Event: 30% off new arrivals." Don't make readers guess.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA** — change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop 50% Off Sale" on the primary button.
- 5. **Add a deadline to the body** — "Sale ends Sunday at midnight" under the hero gives the urgency claim a spine.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off — last 48 hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New styles added + only a few left at this price`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Utility bottoms, florals, dresses — all 50% off, ends soon`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders with a malformed Outlook-conditional CSS block and contains an unfilled merge token in a third-party tracking pixel. Compliance header capture is incomplete through the AgentMail relay, and several content images lack alt text.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**19 tracking/redirect links not probed** — all click-wrap URLs were skipped by the HTTP checker. Final destination URLs, HTTP status codes, and redirect chain health are unverified for any CTA in this send.

**Plain-text body is 71.6% URLs** (7,374 of 10,303 chars) — exceeds the 70% heuristic threshold. Spam filters at major ISPs (Gmail, Outlook) apply additional scrutiny to plain-text parts dominated by URLs. This is a structural issue with how the plain-text alternate is generated.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Outlook CSS syntax error** in the `<!--[if mso]>` block:

```css
body, table, td {
  font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;
}
```

Two defects on the same line:
- Empty slot in the font stack (`Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'`) — the double comma is invalid; Outlook will stop parsing the font-family at the empty value.
- `!important` is placed *after* the semicolon, making it a CSS parse error. The declaration will be ignored in strict parsers; Outlook's rendering engine may or may not be lenient here.

**Duplicate `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule** — declared once under FREE PEOPLE and again under TERRAIN. The second declaration silently overrides the first; both map to Open Sans so there is no behavioral difference, but it indicates a copy-paste artifact in the shared template.

**`.yfix{}` declared three times** — dead CSS, no functional impact, but confirms the stylesheet is assembled by concatenation without dedup.

**7 images missing `alt` text:**

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** — the AgentMail relay did not surface this header. If it is present upstream but stripped by the relay, this is a relay configuration issue. If it is genuinely absent, the email is non-compliant with Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since Feb 2024 for senders >5k/day).

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe is not supported at the header level. Gmail and Yahoo enforce this for bulk senders; its absence increases the risk of the domain being flagged or placed behind a friction wall.

**Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain, so DKIM alignment is expected; the absence of the header in the relay capture means this audit cannot confirm it. Recommend checking raw headers at inbox (e.g., Gmail "Show original") to verify SPF=pass, DKIM=pass, DMARC=pass.
