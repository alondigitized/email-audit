# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- I opened the Anthropologie app on my phone and got hit with a digital bouncer — literally the first thing I see is a white screen with my IP address and a list of reasons I might be a bot. There is zero content, zero shopping, zero Anthro aesthetic. As a loyal AnthroPerks member who spends a not-insignificant chunk of my paycheck here, this is a deeply bad first impression.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark at the top is clean and renders correctly — that's it. That's literally the only brand element I can see.
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link at least gives me one escape valve instead of a pure dead end.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I see "Access is temporarily restricted" in place of any product, campaign, or offer. There is nothing to shop.
- **My IP address (75.140.9.0) is exposed on-screen.** That feels weirdly invasive and technical for a lifestyle brand that is supposed to make me feel like I'm browsing a curated boutique.
- **The bot-detection trigger list ("Rapid taps or clicks," "JavaScript disabled," "Automated activity")** is developer-speak that a real shopper should never have to read. It's anxiety-inducing, not welcoming.
- **There is no recovery path for a legitimate user** — no "Try again," no "Continue as guest," no login option. Just a feedback link and an opaque ID string.
- **Zero brand warmth.** Anthro's whole identity is warmth, texture, and discovery. This page is a cold, white void with serif forensics.

## Recommendations

- **Add a human-readable retry CTA** — something like "Tap here to continue to the site" that clears the block for real users, rather than stranding them on a dead-end page.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the customer-facing screen.** That's internal diagnostic info and it makes a shopper feel surveilled, not welcomed.
- **Replace the clinical numbered list with a single warm sentence** — e.g., "We hit a snag loading the page — tap below to try again." Keep the Anthro voice even in error states.
- **Log verified AnthroPerks members through automatically** — if I'm logged in, I should never see a bot wall. Session auth should bypass this check entirely.
