# EXTRA 50% off + up to 40% off home
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth consecutive Anthropologie sale email since June 26 — and the navy ornate badge hero is back, essentially unchanged for the seventh day running. The subject "EXTRA 50% off + up to 40% off home" is a near word-for-word repeat of the June 29 send ("EXTRA 50% off sale + up to 40% off home"), minus the word "sale." At this cadence and with this creative, the email risks becoming invisible to anyone who has opened any of the previous five sends. The offer is real, the layout is clean, and the "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" product grid is a genuine personalization layer — but the overall impression is fatigue, not urgency.
- The structural bones are solid: hero badge lands first, category nav buttons immediately below, content modules (Travel Edit, Utility Bottoms, Summer Dresses, Beach Day Dresses, Dining Bedding) give the scroll something to do, and the personalized grid anchors the lower half. No render bugs. The home expansion ("Dining Bedding") is the one genuinely new content beat, continuing what June 29 started when home was first layered in.
- Judgement: a technically competent email executing a campaign strategy that has outlived its creative shelf life. The offer is compelling; the delivery is stale.

## What's working

- **Hero badge is immediately legible** — "50% OFF" reads at a glance, no ambiguity about the primary offer.
- **Category nav buttons** give subscribers a direct path to their preferred department without hunting.
- **Home section ("Dining Bedding")** adds genuine cross-category breadth and keeps the home expansion thread alive.
- **"WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" grid** is the strongest personalization signal in the email — nine curated products adds specificity the hero lacks.
- **No visible render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping copy.

## What's weak

- **Hero creative is identical to at least five prior sends** — the navy ornate badge has appeared every day since June 26 with no variation; subscribers have seen it enough times to scroll past it on muscle memory.
- **Subject is a near-duplicate of June 29** — dropping "sale" from "EXTRA 50% off sale + up to 40% off home" is not differentiation; inbox-preview regulars will read it as the same email again.
- **No deadline or expiry anywhere visible** — seven days of "EXTRA 50% off" with no end date erodes urgency with each send.
- **CTA copy is category-label, not action-oriented** — "SALE CLOTHING" and "SALE DRESSES" describe destinations, not actions; "Shop Sale Dresses" would pull harder.
- **No loyalty/AnthroPerks callout** — the June 27 launch email used early-access framing effectively; by day seven that thread has fully disappeared.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire or significantly mutate the badge creative.** After seven days, the navy ornate hero is wallpaper. Swap to a flat-color product-forward hero (model in a specific dress, price tagged) or a time-limited countdown version. The offer is still strong — the vehicle is tired.
- 2. **Add an explicit end date.** "Ends Sunday" or "Last 48 hours" forces a decision and distinguishes this send from the six before it.
- 3. **Rewrite CTA buttons as action + category.** "Shop Dresses" > "SALE DRESSES". A verb costs nothing and lifts clicks.
- 4. **Lead with the personalized grid, not the badge.** The "WE PICKED THESE JUST FOR YOU" section is the most differentiated element — moving it up above the category nav would give the send a personal hook the hero can't provide.
- 5. **Subject and preheader refresh:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends soon: extra 50% off sale + 40% off home`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sale picks — extra 50% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `We pulled 9 things we think you'll actually want.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on your sale order. Shop before it ends.`

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — NOT FOUND** (QA WARN)
Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced Feb 2024+). Absence may cause the email to be routed to spam or have Gmail's native unsubscribe button point to a fallback.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header — NOT FOUND** (QA WARN)
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is mandatory for senders above 5K/day to Gmail. Without this header, Gmail cannot honor one-click unsubscribe, which increases spam complaint risk.

**Authentication-Results — UNKNOWN** (QA WARN)
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unverifiable because the AgentMail relay did not surface the `Authentication-Results` header. Cannot confirm the `s.anthropologie.com` envelope domain is properly aligned with DMARC policy. This should be verified against raw headers from a delivered copy.

**Plain-text fallback — MISSING** (QA FAIL)
Text version is 0 characters. A missing plain-text part increases spam score across multiple filters (SpamAssassin, Barracuda) and is a CAN-SPAM best practice. It also breaks accessibility for email clients that render plain-text only.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — could not be confirmed from the truncated HTML, but this is a hard requirement and should be verified in the full source footer.
