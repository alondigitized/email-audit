# omw to enjoy 30% off for BLACK FRIDAY! ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid Black Friday send, and it's the sixth Anthropologie email I've audited in about two days. The hero is the same gift-tag graphic with a huge "30% OFF" that ran in the 11/21 and 11/22 sends. The eye lands on the offer first. Three pink buttons (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts) sit under it, and a red "+ Extra 40% off** Sale" banner sits below them. The category grid beneath is polished and seasonal.
- The hero is the repeated creative. The "Extra 40% off sale" banner is a callback to the 11/20 "Extra 40% off sale + NEW sale styles" send. Having two stacked offers (30% off and an extra 40% off) works against the "one concrete offer" rule. The "excludes select items" and double-asterisk language also dilutes honesty, because the reader can't tell which discount applies to what.
- The sender is "Anthro Black Friday" and the subject is "omw to enjoy 30% off for BLACK FRIDAY! ✨". The tone is casual and the offer is concrete, but at this cadence the inbox is getting near-identical promos. I'd call it competent but fatigued, and a subscriber has little new reason to open it.

## What's working

- The hero's "30% OFF" is huge and unmistakable. The offer is clear within a second.
- Three category-specific CTA buttons (clothing, home & furniture, gifts) let the reader pick a lane.
- The 2-up and 1-up category tiles with navy labels (Bestselling Dresses, Holiday Party Shop, Shoes for the Season) are visually rich and seasonal.
- The "Might We Recommend…" product row with star ratings adds social proof.
- The footer nav (New Arrivals, E-Gift Card, TikTok, Pick Up In Store) is tidy and useful.

## What's weak

- The hero creative is recycled from the earlier 11/21 and 11/22 sends, so there's no novelty.
- Two stacked discounts (30% off plus an extra 40% off on sale) confuse the offer. The asterisks and "excludes select items" hedge it.
- The marquee strip at the top ("IDAY STARTS NOW! | BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW!") is clipped mid-word on both edges. It reads as a cropped banner.
- The email is very long, with nine category tiles before any product. Nothing links to a specific hero product.
- The tiles have no pricing or product-level detail, so the reader gets little reason to click one over another.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and lead with it. Either run "30% off" or "extra 40% off sale", not both. Put the exclusions in plain language right under the hero.
- 2. Change the creative. After two days of the same gift tag, swap in fresh imagery, or add a countdown or "last day" treatment.
- 3. Fix the clipped marquee so the text reads cleanly, or remove it.
- 4. Cut the tile count to 4-5 and add one hero product with a price.
- 5. Move the star-rated product row higher, since it's the strongest proof in the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off everything, ends Sunday night`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday dress edit: 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Party dresses, cozy knits & gifts, all marked down. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus an extra 40% off sale styles. Online and in stores.`

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie "omw to enjoy 30% off for BLACK FRIDAY!"

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`

- **Dotomi hash:** `dtm_email_hash=` is empty (see section 4).
- **Plain-text part:** 7,151 of 9,968 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs drive this, and it can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** inside `<!--[if mso]>`, the rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry (`Helvetica, ,`) is invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` ends the declaration, leaving a stray `!important;`, so the override is not applied.
  - The result is unpredictable font fallback in Outlook.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` are content images on `image.s.anthropologie.com`. Screen readers will announce the filename or nothing.
  - The two tracking pixels (`1_0` and `003a700000uTzaxAAC`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. Fallback stacks exist, so impact is limited.
- **Duplicate selector:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice, which is harmless redundancy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty email hash:** `dtm_email_hash=` has no value. This is either an unresolved or blank personalization field, or the QA copy was sanitized or sent to a seed address. It can't be confirmed from the data, but if it also happens in production sends, Dotomi can't match the recipient.
- **Truncation:** the `mon-trk` URL is cut off in the QA output, so I can't confirm its recipient identifier.
- **Unresolved tokens:** none are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found, which is a deliverability risk under Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** also not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` matches the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com`, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **Body footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** these can't be evaluated. The links are redirect-wrapped and the 37 tracked destinations were not resolved.
- **Campaign ID:** the pixel path includes `112226_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayInitialReminder2_PM`, which suggests a consistent campaign naming scheme. This couldn't be matched to landing-page parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject or preheader text.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the two pencilline banners and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Confirm that `dtm_email_hash` is populated in live sends, not just in this capture.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` from raw headers at the receiving mailbox, bypassing the relay.
7. Resolve the 37 redirect links with a follow-redirects check to verify destinations, status codes and UTM carry-through.
8. Trim the plain-text part, for example with shorter link URLs, to bring URL density below 70%.
