# 25% OFF designer denim for a limited time
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a real subject shift for Anthropologie after a full week of "Extra 50% Off" hammering — the top banner still shows a maroon "Extra 50% Off Sale" ribbon, but the hero itself leads with a clean blue block: "25% Off* Designer Denim" with a single gold "Shop Now" button. It's the same category-led beat as 2026-08-26's "25% OFF designer denim" send, and it works for the same reason: a named category with a specific percentage is more scannable than another undifferentiated "Extra 50% Off" banner.
- The problem is everything below the fold. After the hero and the "Sale on Sale" maroon block, the email dissolves into eight nearly-identical lifestyle/product-grid modules ("The Trend Hub," "Going Analog," "Suede Shop," "The Lip Edit," "Made With You In Mind") with zero pricing, zero denim-specific product shots, and no second CTA tied to the subject line's actual promise. A subscriber who opens for denim scrolls past shoes, lipstick, and books before hitting a wall of unlabeled outfit thumbnails.
- Net: a good subject/hero pairing wasted by a body that doesn't reinforce the offer at all. This scores in line with the rest of the week's sends — solid open, mediocre click.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states the offer: "25% Off* Designer Denim" with a legible gold "Shop Now" CTA, visible without much scrolling.
- "Limited time!" badge in the top-left corner adds credible urgency without shouting.
- Category-specific subject (denim) breaks the brand's week-long "Extra 50% Off" repetition — a genuine differentiator per the brand history.
- Clean two-tone color blocking (blue hero, maroon sale banner) creates clear visual separation between the two offers stacked on top of each other.

## What's weak

- No actual denim product images anywhere in the email — the hero has no product shot at all, just type on a color field.
- The "Sale on Sale / Extra 50% Off" maroon block competes directly with the denim hero for primary attention, diluting the subject line's promise.
- Eight generic "More to Explore" modules (Trend Hub, Going Analog, Suede Shop, Lip Edit, etc.) have nothing to do with denim and push the relevant offer entirely off-screen after one scroll.
- No second denim CTA anywhere in the body — once you scroll past the hero, the promo is effectively abandoned.
- Fine print ("Extra 50% off sale items...") is dense boilerplate stacked at the very bottom, consistent with this brand's pattern of burying exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the type-only hero with an actual pair of jeans/denim product shot — right now there's no product photography anywhere near the offer, which is a bigger miss than any copy issue.
- 2. Cut the module count in half and move a "Shop Denim" product grid (3-4 actual denim SKUs with prices) immediately after the hero, before Trend Hub/Going Analog/Suede Shop derail the narrative.
- 3. Decide which offer is primary — stacking "Extra 50% Off Sale" directly against "25% Off Designer Denim" in the first screen forces the eye to choose; pick denim (it's the subject line) and demote the sitewide sale to a smaller secondary banner.
- 4. Add a second CTA button lower in the email, after the lifestyle modules, so scrollers who skipped the top still have a way back to denim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your denim just dropped 25%`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Designer denim: 25% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New washes, straight-leg to wide-leg — all 25% off for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the denim edit before this one's gone.`
