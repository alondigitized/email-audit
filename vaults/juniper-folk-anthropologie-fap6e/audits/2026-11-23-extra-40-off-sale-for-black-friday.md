# Extra 40% off sale for BLACK FRIDAY ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about two days, and it recycles the same gift-tag hero with a huge "30% OFF" that ran in the 11/21 and 11/22 emails. The new element is an "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" banner under the tag. It's a different offer sitting directly beneath the 30% one, so two percentages compete for the same spot.
- The body is well made: a tidy grid of category tiles (sweaters, holiday decor, sneakers, dresses, sleepwear, furniture, jewelry, kitchen, beauty, wedding, Terrain), then a "Might We Recommend…" product row. The imagery is strong and the navy label bars read cleanly. But it is a catalog, and nothing is tied to the extra 40%. The sale banner has a single "Shop Clothing" button, with no sense of which items or how deep the discount goes.
- Judgment: competent and on-brand, but it comes after six near-identical sends, and the headline offer is muddled. A subscriber would see a repeat, not news. Roughly a 6.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF hero is huge and immediately readable, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The three pink CTAs (Shop Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts) are clear and visible above the fold.
- The category tile grid has good photography and a consistent navy label treatment, and it is easy to scan.
- The holiday styling (stockings, tree, cozy sleepwear) fits the season.
- The "Might We Recommend…" section adds product-level browsing.

## What's weak

- 30% off and "Extra 40% off* sale" appear together with no explanation of how they relate, and the asterisk and "excludes select items" hedging make the offer feel less clear.
- The hero is the same gift-tag creative as the previous six sends, which adds to fatigue.
- Almost every tile is a vague category label ("Treat Yourself to Beauty", "Ready-to-Ship Furniture") with no price or deal cue, so the discount isn't carried into the body.
- The recommended products have no visible prices, names, or ratings, and only a few carry a "Top Rated" tag. That is weak social proof.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and the exclusions (select items, online and in stores) are buried there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Choose one headline offer. Either lead with "Extra 40% off sale" and make it the hero, or fold it into the 30% as a clear tier such as "30% off everything + extra 40% off sale styles".
- 2. Refresh the creative for each send. Show sale items with real prices (was/now) instead of repeating the gift tag.
- 3. Put sale prices or "from $X" on a few tiles, and add names and prices to the recommendation row.
- 4. Pair each CTA with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop sale sweaters", rather than three category buttons.
- 5. Show a credible deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off sale ends tonight ⏳`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale styles just dropped to 40% off more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, boots & holiday decor, marked down again. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 30% off everything else. Black Friday pricing, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-templated build (Anthropologie/URBN template with Salesforce-style tracking domains). The visible source has a malformed Outlook font declaration, zoom-blocking viewport settings, and several images without alt text. The header-level compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay, and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unverified.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels** are present:
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112226_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayInitialLaunchReminder3_PM/003a...`
- No broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`.
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) and the stray `;` before `!important` invalidate the declaration.
  - The Outlook-specific font override is therefore likely ignored.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Missing alt text** (QA-confirmed) on three content images:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels** (`dotomi 1_0`, `mon-trk 003a700000uTzaxAAC`) is low severity, since they are 1x1 beacons. They should still use `alt=""`.
- **`@import` of Google Fonts** inside `<style>` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stacks cover it, so this is minor.
- **Duplicate rule:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice in the font CSS. This is cosmetic.
- **Plain-text part is URL-heavy:** 8,148 of 10,977 characters (about 74%) are URLs, mostly raw tracking links. This can hurt spam scoring and readability for text-only recipients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`** in the Dotomi pixel URL. The parameter is blank in the rendered output, so the hashed-email merge field did not populate.
  - This is either a failed substitution or a deliberate suppression in the test or seed send.
  - If it is a failure, the retargeting pixel cannot match the recipient.
- No other unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[%…%]`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **From domain:** `s.anthropologie.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain. This is normal, but alignment cannot be verified without the headers.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible on the links in the source provided. The tracking wrappers obscure the final URLs, and the 42 links were not resolved.
- The `PromoBlackFridayInitialLaunchReminder3_PM` campaign ID in the Monetate-style pixel suggests that campaign attribution lives in the redirect and pixel layer rather than in visible UTMs.
- Landing-page alignment with the "Extra 40% off" claim could not be checked technically.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO font declaration to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. Remove the empty entry, and put `!important` inside the declaration rather than after the `;`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three content images and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels. Add a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Check that `dtm_email_hash` populates in live sends, and confirm that the blank value here comes from the test or seed address.
5. Re-run the compliance checks against the raw `.eml` or the original ESP send to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Do not treat the current warnings as defects until then.
6. Resolve the 42 redirect links with a follow-redirects crawl to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
7. Trim raw URLs in the plain-text part, or shorten them, to reduce the URL ratio.
