# Added to sale: 1000+ styles to love
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals-to-sale send with a clear top-of-email hit. A red "JUST ADDED!" banner and a big "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" headline sit over a single SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands there first. The problem is that the hero has no discount. "New sale styles" tells me the items are marked down but not by how much. The only number is in the second block, "30% Off* Hosting, Throws, Gifts & More". That is a home and gift offer, not the apparel the hero promises.
- The rest is a catalog-style grid of seven shop links (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Winter Activities, Accessory & Shoe Gifts, Beauty Gifts, Holiday Entertaining) and nine "Made With You In Mind" products. It looks polished, but it splits attention and nothing says "click this one". It also arrives after a week of "Extra 50% off sale" (Nov 5 and 7) and "25% off dresses" (Nov 6–8). Against that, "1000+ styles" with no percentage reads as a step down. Judgement: good production, soft offer, and a hero that doesn't match the only price promise in the email.

## What's working

- The red "JUST ADDED!" banner and large red headline on cream give an instant, scannable hero.
- Hero and secondary CTAs both read clearly: "SHOP NOW" buttons in dark brown and green.
- The "30% Off*" block has a "Limited time!" badge and holiday styling that fits the season.
- Editorial photography is strong, especially the red cashmere sweater and the party-dress grid, and the models skew style-forward.
- The footer perks row (Five-Star Favorites, Pay Later w/ Klarna, Gift & Styling Services, AnthroPerks) adds reassurance, and I see no render bugs.

## What's weak

- The hero gives no discount depth. "New sale styles" with no %, price or category is vague.
- The hero and the "30% Off" banner promise different things (apparel vs. hosting/throws/gifts), which muddies the primary offer.
- Seven category tiles plus nine products make a long scroll with no clear priority. Only two products carry a "Top Rated" tag.
- It repeats the Nov 5–8 sale cadence, but "Added to sale" has less punch than "ENDS TONIGHT: 25% off dresses" or "Extra 50% off".
- The 30% fine print is tiny and sits at the very bottom. The asterisks are never explained near the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real number in the hero, for example "1000+ new sale styles, up to X% off". Then tie the 30% banner to the same category or drop it.
- 2. Cut the category grid to three or four tiles, and lead with the Holiday Party Shop and Cashmere, which are the strongest visuals.
- 3. Add prices or sale tags to the "Made With You In Mind" products, so the grid shows the markdown.
- 4. Add a deadline to the offer, since the "Limited time!" badge has no date.
- 5. Pull the asterisk terms up near the offer, in one short line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `1000+ new styles just hit sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns: sweaters, dresses & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off hosting, throws & gifts. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh sale finds in cashmere, party dresses and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's source has several confirmed code-level defects: malformed CSS, an empty `<title>`, zoom-blocking viewport settings and missing alt attributes. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't expose the headers. The QA pass rate was 50%, with 0 failures and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **34 tracking links were not probed.** The click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **The Dotomi pixel has an empty hash parameter.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. Either the merge value wasn't populated for this recipient, or the capture stripped it.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels are in the body:**
  - `login.dotomi.com/.../px/1_0`
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../111226_AN-US_Promo1000NewSaleStyles/003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  These aren't a defect, but they matter for privacy and blocking behavior. Images-off clients won't fire them.
- **Plain-text part is 75% URLs.** 6,550 of 8,777 characters are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The text part is mostly long tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes on 3 content images:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render with no text.
- **Missing alt attributes on 2 tracking pixels** (`1_0` and `003a700000uTzaxAAC`). They should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** The HTML title is blank.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid CSS in the MSO conditional block.** The rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`), and the `!important` sits after the semicolon as a stray token. The `!important` is dropped and the declaration is malformed.
- **Quoted generic font families.** The font stacks use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes. In CSS that names a font called "sans-serif" rather than using the generic keyword. The fallback fails when the preceding fonts are unavailable.
- **Web-font `@import` from Google Fonts.** It is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so those clients fall back to the stack above. Because of the quoted-generics bug, the fallback is weaker than intended.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some older Outlook and IE engines treat that as quirks mode.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved or empty value.** `dtm_email_hash=` is blank in the Dotomi pixel URL (see Section 2). No other unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated source. The rest of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers. This is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is likely a relay artifact. No defect is confirmed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified.** They fall outside the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters could not be assessed.** Links are wrapped in tracking redirects, and no destination URLs were resolved in the supplied data.
- **Campaign identifier visible in the pixel path.** The Monetate path has `111226_AN-US_Promo1000NewSaleStyles`, which matches the subject ("1000+ styles"). The landing pages themselves were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three banner and content images, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
2. Set a meaningful `<title>`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the MSO font rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
5. Remove the quotes around `serif` and `sans-serif` in the font stacks, and delete the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
6. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
7. Confirm in the raw `.eml` that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and check the Authentication-Results for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `s.anthropologie.com`.
8. Investigate why `dtm_email_hash` is empty. It may be expected for a seed address.
9. Resolve a sample of the 34 tracking links with a real browser or a redirect-following fetch, to verify that UTMs and landing pages carry through.
10. Shorten the plain-text part. Remove or consolidate the tracking URLs to bring the URL share under 70%.
