# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this feels immediately like home — the earthy tones, the outdoor Mediterranean light, the girl literally sitting on rocks in a flowy cream set. Anthropologie is clearly in Mother's Day mode right now, leaning hard on the gifting angle with a free express shipping push. The vibe is right, but the homepage is more of a mood board than a conversion machine.
- **8/10**
- + Hero imagery explicitly speaks to my persona — boho outdoor lifestyle, flowing silhouettes, natural textures
- − No returning-shopper hook — no AnthroPerks balance, no "welcome back" signal, no loyalty CTA
- + Concrete offer above the fold — "Free Express Shipping on $50+" is right there in the banner
- − No unmistakable primary CTA button — category tiles label destinations but don't use button copy ("Shop Dresses" vs. just "Dresses")
- + Visual hierarchy is clear — the 2×2 grid is easy to scan, eye lands on New! first
- + No render bugs — layout is clean; cookie banner is dismissible and doesn't break anything
- + Demographic signals match — models read late 20s to mid-30s, boho sensibility throughout
- + Current campaign — Mother's Day framing with urgency shipping copy, spring/outdoor aesthetic
- − Loyalty/membership benefits not visible — AnthroPerks gets zero screen real estate above the fold
- + Offer feels honest — the $50 threshold and "limited time" language is clear and not buried

## What's working

- **The 2×2 category grid** — landing me directly on "New!" and "Dresses" in the first scroll is chef's kiss. Those are the two tiles I would tap first anyway, and they're right there.
- **The banner urgency** — "limited time! Free Express Shipping on $50+ get it in time for Mother's Day!" is doing real work. I'm a registry shopper, so any excuse to buy something nice and get it fast lands.
- **The visual aesthetic** — the rocky outdoor landscape, warm golden tones, and relaxed silhouettes all say "this brand gets me" before I read a single word.
- **"Top Rated" tile** — that's a clever trust proxy that requires zero UI overhead. I'll always tap that.

## What's weak

- **Zero AnthroPerks acknowledgment** — I have a rewards balance and an Anthro Day discount and the homepage acts like that doesn't exist. A single "Your perks are waiting" nudge would pull me in faster.
- **Tile labels, not CTAs** — "Dresses" as a tile label feels passive. "Shop Dresses" or "New in Dresses" would give me more confidence about what I'm about to land on.
- **Cookie banner blocking bottom two tiles** — I can see enough to recognize what's there, but "Dresses" gets partially obscured by the cookie bar. Minor, but friction is friction.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — no star ratings, no "4,000+ reviews," nothing that says other people like me co-signed this stuff.

## Recommendations

- **Surface AnthroPerks above the fold** — a thin persistent bar or banner slot for signed-in users showing points balance or upcoming Anthro Day. One line. Huge pull for loyalty members like me.
- **Rewrite tile labels to active CTAs** — change "Dresses" → "Shop Dresses" and "New!" → "Shop What's New" to remove ambiguity about tap destination.
- **Add a review count badge to the Top Rated tile** — even "4.8★ from 12K reviews" as an overlay would reinforce the trust signal that the label implies but doesn't prove.
- **Offer a "Gift She'll Love" editorial module** — since the banner is all-in on Mother's Day, a curated gift guide tile below the category grid would extend the urgency into browse behavior.
