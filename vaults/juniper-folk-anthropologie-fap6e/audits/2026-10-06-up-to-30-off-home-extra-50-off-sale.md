# Up to 30% off home + extra 50% off sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first Anthropologie Home send, and a better-organized one than the recent maeve lookbooks. The hero is a boxed olive-and-cream panel with two stacked offers: "Extra 50% off Sale" and "Up to 30% off Home." Both are tappable and the numbers are huge. A grid of colorful category tiles follows (Kitchen & Dining, Bedding, Rugs & Curtains, Lighting, and so on), so a home shopper can reach their room quickly.
- The weakness is focus. Two offers share one hero, and one of them is general sale, which isn't home. The "Extra 50% off Sale" line sits above the "Home" line, so the subject and the Home wordmark don't quite agree on what the email is about. Below the tiles the email turns into a long, generic editorial stack (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets) and a 3x3 apparel grid with no product detail. That drifts away from the home promise. The fine print is also dense, with asterisks and exclusions, which hurts the honesty of the offer.
- My judgement: a solid 6. The top third is strong and clear, and everything after it dilutes it.

## What's working

- The hero offers are large and legible: "50% OFF" and "30% OFF" in big olive and maroon type on a cream box.
- The "Home" wordmark in the header signals the category right away.
- The color-blocked category tiles are scannable and give specific entry points (Rugs & Curtains, Lighting, Bath).
- "Online & in stores" adds a useful omnichannel cue.
- The footer has useful extras: 10% off for SMS signup, Afterpay, inclusive sizing, and styling services.

## What's weak

- The hero splits attention between sale and home. "Extra 50% off" is not a home offer, and it sits above the home offer.
- The Home subject line and hero don't state which items are included or what the "extra" is applied to.
- The mid-section modules (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets) are unrelated to home. Only Mantel Décor is on theme.
- The 3x3 "These are so you, too" grid shows no product names, prices or ratings, so there is nothing to click on with intent.
- The fine print is long and has multiple asterisks and exclusions. The "up to 30%" and "extra 50%" qualifiers feel hedged.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and no expiry date. "Limited time!" is vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with "Up to 30% off Home" and move the sale offer to a secondary strip. Or send a sale-only email.
- 2. Add a hard deadline, such as "Ends Sunday," next to the offers.
- 3. Replace the generic editorial modules with home products that show a price and a before/after. Put 4 to 6 featured home items right under the category tiles.
- 4. Put real product names, prices and ratings on the "so you" grid, or cut it.
- 5. Cut the fine print down to one line under each offer and link to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off home, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs, bedding, lighting: up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 50% off sale. Shop the room you've been planning.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kitchen, bath, bedding and more, online and in stores.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a multi-brand ESP template, and the visible source has no critical failures. The confirmed issues are missing alt text on 7 images, a malformed Outlook CSS rule, zoom-blocking viewport settings, and an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value in a tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication checks are unverified because AgentMail does not capture those headers, not because they failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 43 links on tracking or click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing pages are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter:** the Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` is empty. This is probably an unresolved or stripped merge value, which would make the pixel useless for identity matching. It could also be an artifact of the capture, so treat it as likely rather than certain.
- **Plain-text part:** 8,338 of 11,812 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This indicates long tracking URLs in the text/plain alternative, which can add spam-filter weight.
- **Subscriber identifier in a URL path:** the pixel `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../100626_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS30OffHomeLaunch/003a700000uTzaxAA...` carries what looks like a Salesforce contact or subscriber key (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`). That is normal for ESP tracking, but the ID is exposed in a third-party request URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (7 flagged, some listed twice):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0`
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAAC`
  - The banner images appear twice each, likely from separate desktop and mobile variants. The two tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Malformed Outlook rule:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`. It has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`) and a misplaced semicolon (`; !important;`). Parsers may drop the declaration, and `!important` is never applied.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Comment before doctype:** an HTML comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stacks cover this, so it is low impact.
- **Duplicate CSS:** the `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is defined twice. This is harmless redundancy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` is the only evidence of an unresolved token.
- No visible `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` leakage appears in the truncated source.
- The rest of the source was not visible, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** neither header was found. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed violation. Without both, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders, cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope uses the subdomain `s.anthropologie.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** the truncated source does not show them, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** not verifiable. Links route through tracking redirects, and the 43 links were skipped, so UTM presence and landing-page behavior are unknown.
- **Campaign identifier:** the pixel path `100626_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS30OffHomeLaunch` is consistent with the subject ("Up to 30% off home + extra 50% off sale"). It is dated 10/06/26 and labeled for the US.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Populate `dtm_email_hash` or remove the pixel if it cannot be filled. Confirm the merge runs at send time.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and the pencilline banners if they are decorative. Add descriptive alt text to `1e313de8-…png`.
3. Fix the Outlook CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add a non-empty `<title>` and move the comment after the doctype.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message at the source or in a direct inbox. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC the same way.
7. Probe the redirect destinations offline, or resolve the click-tracking URLs, to verify UTMs and landing pages.
8. Shorten the text/plain URLs, for example with cleaner or shorter links in the alternative part.
