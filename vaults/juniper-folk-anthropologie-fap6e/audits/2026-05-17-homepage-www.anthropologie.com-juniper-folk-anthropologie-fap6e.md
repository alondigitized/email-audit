# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is not a homepage — this is a bot-block wall. The Anthropologie logo appears at the top, and then it's just a cold error page telling me my access is "temporarily restricted" because of suspected bot activity. There is zero shopping experience here, zero product, zero vibe. As a loyal AnthroPerks member who literally opens this app all the time, getting locked out like a robot feels genuinely insulting.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark renders correctly at the top — at least I know I'm in the right place before the wall comes down.
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link is present, so there is technically an escape hatch.

## What's weak

- **The entire page is a bot-block error.** There is no homepage — no hero, no categories, no offers, nothing. I'm a real person on my phone and I got locked out.
- **The error copy is cold and clinical** — "unusual activity," "IP 75.140.9.0," error ID string. This is developer-facing language, not customer-facing language. It reads like a server log, not a brand I love.
- **No recovery path for legitimate shoppers.** There's no "try again," no link to the app, no human support option, no CAPTCHA to prove I'm human. "Submit Feedback" goes nowhere useful when I just want to browse dresses.
- **Massive blank white space** in the middle of the screen — the page feels broken and abandoned, not intentional.
- **Citing my IP address publicly** ("IP 75.140.9.0") feels weirdly exposing, even if it's just diagnostic. Not a vibe.

## Recommendations

- **Add a human verification step (CAPTCHA or tap-to-confirm) instead of a hard block.** If you think I'm a bot, let me prove I'm not in one tap — don't just slam the door.
- **Rewrite the error copy in brand voice** — something like "Hold on, we need a moment to verify it's you. Tap below to continue shopping." Not a raw server message with IP addresses.
- **Provide a real recovery CTA** — "Open in the Anthropologie App" or "Try Again" — so a blocked user doesn't just bounce to Free People instead.
- **Remove the error ID and raw IP from the visible page.** Keep that in logs. Showing it to customers feels broken and slightly creepy.
