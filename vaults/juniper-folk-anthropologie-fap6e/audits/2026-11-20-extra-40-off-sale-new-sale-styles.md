# Extra 40% off sale + NEW sale styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Extra 40% off" send in three days. The hero is the same brown panel with big pink "EXTRA 40% OFF*", "SALE", "NEW STYLES ADDED" and a pink SHOP NOW button, as in the 11/19 "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE" and 11/18 "Extra 40% off sale? Well, okay then. 👀" emails. The offer lands instantly, but a subscriber has now seen this creative three times.
- Below the hero the email turns into a gift-guide and holiday lookbook. "On My Way… with last-minute gifts" (Top-Rated Gifts, Stocking Stuffers, Buy Online & Pickup in Store, E-Gift Cards) sells gifting, not the sale. The "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" strip with a tiny SHOP NOW repeats the 11/14 "1000+ new sale styles" send. The result is competing messages: sale, gifts, and editorial.
- Verdict: a solid, readable promo, but it's repetitive and diluted. The only new element is the gifting pivot, and that pivot undercuts the sale CTA.

## What's working

- The hero is offer-first. Big pink "40% OFF" on brown, with a clear SHOP NOW button, gets the eye there first.
- The gifting block has a distinct 2x2 grid. Its four tiles are easy to scan and fit the Black Friday and holiday timing.
- The "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" strip is red and concrete, and ties back to the sale.
- The "Made With You in Mind" product grid shows real products with star ratings, which gives some social proof.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The creative is near-identical to the 11/18 and 11/19 sends. The "NEW STYLES ADDED" line is stale.
- The hero asterisk has no deadline or exclusions up top. The fine print is buried at the very bottom.
- The "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" button is tiny and easy to miss, and the strip repeats the hero's "new styles" claim.
- The editorial "More to Explore" tiles (Trend Hub, Cashmere Shop, Beauty Gifts, Holiday Entertaining) are mostly non-sale. They pull attention away from the 40% offer.
- The product grid shows stars but no prices or sale prices, so the discount isn't reinforced where people shop.
- The email is very long, with five or six stacked sections.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero new each day. Add a deadline ("Ends Sunday") or a specific category, and move "new styles added" into a product row.
- 2. Put sale products with sale prices directly under the hero, ahead of the gifting block.
- 3. Enlarge the "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" button, or fold it into the hero.
- 4. Cut the editorial tiles to two or three, and make the gift guide a secondary module.
- 5. Surface the exclusions and dates near the hero asterisk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off sale, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New to sale: 1000+ styles, extra 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus last-minute gifts you can still pick up in store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters, denim and party looks, all marked down further.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Template markup is mostly sound, but the visible source has a malformed Outlook CSS rule and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. QA flagged 5 images without alt text. Compliance and authentication results are inconclusive because the relay did not capture headers. The HTML is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 38 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter:** the pixel `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. If the template expected a hashed email there, the merge value did not populate. That could be a seed-inbox artifact or a real failure to populate the hash.
- **Third-party pixels:** two pixels are present, `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../112026_AN-US_Promo40OffSOSReminder2/003a700000uTzaxAAC`. The second carries what looks like a per-recipient ID (`003a7...` is a Salesforce Contact ID format). Both load only when images are enabled.
- **Plain-text part:** 7,388 of 9,908 characters (about 75%) are URLs. This ratio is a spam-filter signal and makes the text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>`. `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` disable pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** inside `<!--[if mso]>`, the rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry between `Helvetica,` and `'Font-Normal'` is invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` makes that token a stray declaration, so the `!important` is ignored.
  - Outlook font fallback is therefore unreliable.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Missing alt text** (QA data):
  - Content images:
    - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
    - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
    - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - Tracking pixels:
    - `1_0` (dotomi)
    - `003a700000uTzaxAAC` (mon-trk)
  - The pixels should use `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`. If the three content images carry text or links, they need descriptive alt.
- **Web font via `@import`:** the `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` inside `<style>` is not supported in Gmail or several other clients. Fallbacks are declared (`Helvetica, Arial`, `Georgia`), so this is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only evidence is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` noted above.
- No unrendered tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so presence is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. This is required for one-click unsubscribe under Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` aligns with the From address.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters on the 38 click-tracked links could not be inspected because the links were skipped.
- The campaign ID in the pixel path (`112026_AN-US_Promo40OffSOSReminder2`) suggests a November 2026 "Promo 40% Off SOS Reminder 2" send. The subject's "Extra 40% off" matches that ID.
- Landing-page alignment cannot be validated without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`, removing the empty entry and the stray semicolon.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport tag.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the three content images and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Fill in `<title>`.
5. Confirm `dtm_email_hash` populates on a real (non-seed) send.
6. Re-test with raw headers (EML or direct SMTP capture) to verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Resolve the 38 tracking links with a redirect-following probe to check destinations and UTMs.
8. Add more text content to the plain-text part to reduce the URL ratio, for example by trimming tracking-parameter length.
