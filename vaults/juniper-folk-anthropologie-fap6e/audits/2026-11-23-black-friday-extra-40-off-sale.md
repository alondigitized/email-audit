# Black Friday = EXTRA 40% OFF SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about three days, and it reuses the same gift-tag hero as the 11/21 to 11/23 emails. The giant "30% OFF" sits on a plaid-edged tag, and the only new element is the "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" banner underneath. The eye lands on the 30% first, and the extra 40% reads as a footnote on a footnote. That inverts the subject line, which leads with the extra 40%. Compared with this morning's "Extra 40% off sale for BLACK FRIDAY ✨" (7/10), this is a near-duplicate with a slightly blunter subject.
- The body is tidy. Teal-captioned tiles cover dresses, holiday decor, sweaters, sneakers, jewelry, denim, throws, beauty and wedding guest styles. Price anchors like "under $50" and "under $100" make the offer concrete. The "Might we recommend..." grid and the footer nav are standard. The scroll is long, with about a dozen tiles before any product-level specifics.
- The offer math is muddy. Three buttons sit under 30% (Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts), and a separate dark-green "Shop Clothing" button sits under the extra 40%. A shopper can't tell whether the two discounts stack or apply to different items. The "**" and the "excludes select items" line hint at fine print, and the small-print legal copy is only at the very bottom. Solid execution, but it's a fatigue-heavy send in a crowded cadence.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is huge and instantly readable, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The tiles pair clear category labels with price caps ("Sweaters under $100", "Jewelry & accessories under $50"), which makes the discount feel real.
- The gold and green palette fits the season and the Anthropologie brand.
- The category mix covers apparel, home, beauty, gifts and weddings, so most subscribers will find something relevant.
- The footer has a clean "Even more to love" block with new arrivals, e-gift card, TikTok and in-store pickup.

## What's weak

- The "+ EXTRA 40% OFF**" banner is small and buried below the hero. The subject's main hook is the weakest element in the body.
- The hero has four separate CTAs (three gold buttons plus a green one) with no clear primary, and it isn't obvious how the 30% and the extra 40% relate.
- The creative is almost identical to the previous 7 sends, which makes it feel like noise.
- The tile grid is long and uniform, with every caption in the same teal bar. Nothing signals a hero product or best seller, and I can't see reviews or ratings.
- The disclaimers are tiny and only at the bottom, while the "**" and "excludes select items" hints leave the offer feeling slightly hedged.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the stack explicit in the hero. Replace "30% OFF" plus a small extra-40% banner with one line, for example "30% off everything. Extra 40% off sale styles." Give the extra 40% equal visual weight, since the subject promises it.
- 2. Collapse the four hero buttons into one primary CTA ("Shop the sale") plus a quieter text link row.
- 3. Add a countdown or a dated deadline ("Ends Monday 11/24") to make the urgency credible instead of generic.
- 4. Cut the tile grid by a third and add one hero product with a star rating and a price.
- 5. Space out the sends. Consolidate the Black Friday cadence into fewer emails with a distinct angle each (for example, an extra-40% sale-only send, then a last-chance send).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off sale styles, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off + an extra 40% off sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim and holiday decor, with prices from $50. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The extra 40% stacks on sale styles. Here's what's worth grabbing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has several code-level defects: malformed MSO CSS, missing alt text on 3 content images, and a viewport tag that disables zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, and the HTML was truncated, so link, footer and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The links go through click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash` parameter.** The Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The value is blank. This is either an unresolved merge token or an intentional blank for the seed address. I can't tell which from the source.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8096 of 10929 characters (about 74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This can hurt spam scoring and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Two third-party pixels.** `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../112326_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedLaunch_AM/...` are external dependencies. They're flagged below only for their missing `alt` attributes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  All are hosted on `image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/fe3e11717164057c701470/m/...`. If they're decorative, they should have `alt=""`. If they carry meaning, they need descriptive text.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** `dotomi.com/.../1_0` and `mon-trk.com/.../003a700000u...`. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family entry, and `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it's a stray token. The declaration is invalid or partly ignored, so the Outlook font fallback is unreliable.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize issue.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and web-view versions use the title as the page name.
- **Comment before the doctype.** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Web-font `@import`.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` attribute selectors are also unreliable. The font-family fallbacks do cover this.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is defined twice (the Beholden and Terrain sections). This is harmless but redundant.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `${...}` appear in the visible portion.
- The only suspect is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` above. The HTML was truncated, so the rest of the body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Both may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. The QA note says as much, so I can't confirm a real defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain is `s.anthropologie.com`, and the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com` is on the same subdomain, which is consistent.
- **Footer not visible.** The visible HTML doesn't include the footer unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, so I can't confirm or refute those requirements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Can't be assessed.** The 42 links are click-tracking redirects that weren't resolved, so no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters are visible.
- **Campaign ID in the pixel.** The only campaign identifier is in the `mon-trk` pixel path (`PromoBlackFridayContinuedLaunch_AM`). It doesn't confirm that the landing pages carry matching UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive or empty `alt` text to the three banner/content images. Add `alt=""` and 1x1 dimensions to both tracking pixels.
2. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add a `<title>` and move the template comment after the doctype.
5. Verify whether `dtm_email_hash` is populated in a real (non-seed) send.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy. That would give real `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` values and the untruncated body.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 42 tracking links to confirm landing URLs and UTMs.
8. Trim URL density in the plain-text part below 70%.
