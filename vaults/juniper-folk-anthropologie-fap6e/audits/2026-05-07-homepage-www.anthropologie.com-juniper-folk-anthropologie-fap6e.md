# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Anthro is going full Mother's Day mode right now — the e-gift card push is front and center, and there's a "just for you" personalized offer banner that hits *candles, accessories, and sleepwear*, which is basically my whole personality. The overall vibe is very much the cozy, outdoorsy-woman Anthropologie we know and love — the imagery is doing the right thing, even if the navigation is making me work harder than I'd like.

## What's working

- **"CANDLES & MORE" in the offer copy** — as a candle stockpiler, this caught my eye immediately and made the 30% off feel personally relevant, not generic
- **"JUST FOR YOU" framing on the promo banner** — implies personalization/loyalty tier even if the explicit rewards balance isn't shown; it makes me feel like a known customer
- **"limited time!" badge on the promo** — the teal badge creates just enough urgency without being obnoxious
- **Mother's Day e-gift card banner** — timely, and the "What Mom really wants? Options." copy is a little witty; relevant right now in early May
- **The "New!" and "Top Rated!" lifestyle tiles** — the women in both shots look like people I'd aspire to dress like, and the split between discovery-mode ("New!") and social-proof-mode ("Top Rated!") is smart editorial architecture
- **A\|HOME tab toggle in the header** — I shop both sides of Anthro and love that I can hop between them without hunting

## What's weak

- **No category navigation visible above the fold** — everything is behind the hamburger; I can't get to "Dresses" or "Tops" in one tap, and that slows me down when I'm in app-scroll mode
- **No free-shipping mention** — I almost always spend enough to qualify, but not seeing the threshold here means I have no nudge to top up my cart
- **No reviews, ratings, or social proof** — the lifestyle tiles are beautiful but there's no "4.8 stars, 2,300 reviews" badge to give me a reason to trust a specific product
- **Asterisk on "30% OFF*"** — the exclusions aren't disclosed on this screen; that always makes me hesitate slightly before tapping
- **Cookie banner partially covers the bottom content** — it's covering what look like two more lifestyle modules, which means the page is showing me less than it should on first load

## Recommendations

- **Surface AnthroPerks balance or tier status in the promo banner** — swap "JUST FOR YOU" for "ANTHRO PERKS MEMBER: UP TO 30% OFF" so returning shoppers feel seen and the loyalty hook is unmistakable
- **Add a sticky or visible category strip below the header** — even a simple row of "Dresses / Tops / Home / Sale" chips would let me navigate in one tap instead of hunting through the hamburger
- **Include the free-shipping threshold inline** — a small line under the SHOP NOW button like "Free shipping on $50+" gives me a cart-size nudge for free
- **Replace the asterisk-only disclosure with one plain-language exclusion** — even "Excludes sale items" on the banner itself would make the offer feel cleaner and more honest without hurting conversion
