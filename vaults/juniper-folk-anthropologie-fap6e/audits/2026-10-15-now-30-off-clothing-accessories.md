# NOW: 30% off clothing & accessories
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Feels Like Fall promo Anthropologie has sent all week, now relabeled "Fashion Weekend." The hero is the strongest part. A big orange-red "30% OFF*" sits on a cream box, with "Clothing & Accessories" under it and a lime "Shop Now" button. The eye lands on the offer first. Compared with the Oct 8–12 sends, the creative has moved from a three-offer stack to a single-offer hero, which is cleaner.
- Below the hero it gets muddy. A second orange banner says "30% OFF**" for "Fall Entertaining, Candles & Decor" with different terms (online and in stores, with exclusions). Then a long run of category tiles follows: Trend Hub, Holiday Party, Cashmere, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets. After that comes a nine-item "Might we recommend" grid. Two 30% offers with different fine print dilute the message. The tiles are pure browse, with no prices, product names or deadlines.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send, but it is a late-week repeat. After the Oct 13 "unexpected combos" lookbook, a subscriber sees more of the same sale. It will get opened on habit and clicked by people who were already going to shop.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A giant "30% OFF*" in a contrasting color, with "Clothing & Accessories" beneath it, makes the offer unmissable.
- The lime "Shop Now" button stands out against the cream and is easy to spot.
- The subject matches the hero, so there is no mismatch between inbox and body.
- The tile imagery is strong. The leather jacket, fur coat, red cashmere sweater and mantel scene read as seasonal and editorial.
- The footer has useful extras: AnthroPerks, Klarna, an App Store badge and social icons.

## What's weak

- There are two different 30% offers with different terms. The hero is "online only." The orange banner is "online & in stores" and covers decor. Neither is clearly primary.
- "Limited time!" appears twice but with no end date. That is soft urgency, especially after six or seven near-identical sends this week.
- The category tiles are all labels with no prices, product names or reasons to click. The nine-item grid has no names or prices either.
- The CTA copy is generic. "Shop Now" does not say what you are shopping.
- The email is long. The key terms are in tiny fine print at the bottom, and "online only" under the button is faint.
- AnthroPerks is buried in a small footer tile. Members get no visible benefit up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the repetition. After a week of the same sale, send one clear message with a real end date, such as "Ends tonight" or "Ends Sunday." That date matters more than anything else on this list.
- 2. Drop the second orange 30% banner, or fold it into the hero as one line. One offer, one set of terms.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop 30% off clothing" or "Shop the cashmere edit."
- 4. Trim the six category tiles to three or four. Add price or "from $" copy and a short product hook to each.
- 5. Add names and prices to the recommended grid, or cut it. Add a review snippet, such as the "Five-Star Favorites" tile, higher up.
- 6. Move AnthroPerks near the hero as a member perk, such as early access or free shipping.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is structurally sound, but QA couldn't verify the compliance headers, authentication results, or any of the 35 click-tracking destinations. The confirmed code-level defects are missing `alt` attributes, malformed font CSS, and an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value in a third-party pixel. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 35 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` is a Conversant/Dotomi pixel. Its `dtm_email_hash` parameter is empty (see §4).
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../101526_AN-US_30OffFashionLaunch/003a700000uTzaxAAC` is an open/monitoring pixel. It is a separate tracking domain from the ESP's `image.s.anthropologie.com`, so it adds a second third-party dependency.
- **Duplicate assets:** `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` each appear twice. This is probably a repeated divider module and isn't a defect on its own.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (confirmed):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (×2)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (×2)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - Both tracking pixels
  - Decorative images and pixels should carry `alt=""`. A missing attribute lets screen readers announce the filename or URL. The `1e313de8…png` image needs a check on whether it carries content.
- **Invalid Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - There is an empty list entry (`, ,`).
  - `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it is ignored.
  - The Outlook font override is therefore not enforced as intended.
- **Quoted generic font families:** `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` are quoted throughout the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` rules. Quoted, they are treated as font names rather than generic keywords, so the final fallback doesn't resolve.
- **Fonts depend on `@import`:**
  - `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients.
  - The `[style*="Font-Normal-…"]` selectors only match if the token survives in inline styles.
  - Web fonts will fall back in those clients, and the broken fallback stack above makes that worse.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Comment before the doctype:** the `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. That can trigger quirks mode in some legacy engines (old IE-based Outlook paths).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the Dotomi pixel. Either the hash didn't resolve at send time, or the value is intentionally blank for test or seed sends. The truncated source doesn't show other unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`), so none are confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip headers, so this is unconfirmed. If it's real, it matters for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a subdomain, so alignment needs verification against raw headers.
- **Body footer:** the truncated HTML doesn't show whether it has the physical postal address or an unsubscribe link, so neither is confirmed present or absent.
- **Plain-text part:** 6,773 of 9,288 characters (73%) are URLs. This is a deliverability and spam-score concern, and the long tracking URLs are the cause.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. The 35 tracked links are redirects, so final URLs and UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, etc.) couldn't be inspected.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `101526_AN-US_30OffFashionLaunch` in the `mon-trk.com` pixel path.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to decorative images and both pixels, and add a descriptive `alt` to `1e313de8-….png` if it carries content.
2. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;` and unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in all font stacks.
3. Fill in `<title>`, and remove `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the template comment below the doctype.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers from the sender, bypassing the relay. Do the same for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve the 35 tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe to check final status codes and UTMs.
7. Check whether `dtm_email_hash` is populated on real sends.
8. Shorten the plain-text part's URLs, or reduce the number of links it carries.
