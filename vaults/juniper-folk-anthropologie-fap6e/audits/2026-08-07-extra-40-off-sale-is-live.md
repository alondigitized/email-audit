# Extra 40% Off Sale is LIVE
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's biggest markdown push yet this stretch — "Extra 40% Off Sale is LIVE" tops the July 29/31 "up to 30% off" sends by ten points and drops the soft-launch framing for flat urgency ("LIVE," "Limited time!"). The lilac hero with bold black "EXTRA 40% Off* SALE" type and a clean coral "SHOP NOW" button is the strongest, most legible hero this brand has run in the recent history — no ambiguity about the offer or the action.
- Where it gets messy is everything after the fold. A second "Get Autumn Ready — Up to 30% OFF furniture, décor & Halloween" banner appears almost immediately below the 40%-off category grid, creating two competing percent-off claims in the same email. From there it's a long, undifferentiated scroll of lifestyle collections (Trend Hub, Colored & Washed, Going Analog, Sweater Dresses, Suede Shop, Lip Edit) with no pricing or CTA on any of them — pure browse filler stapled to a sale email.
- Judgement: strong top-of-funnel execution, diluted by a bloated, unfocused body. This should have been two emails — a tight 40%-off sale blast and a separate autumn-editorial send (like Aug 3's "fall styles, before fall gets here").

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable: large "EXTRA 40% Off*" in bold black type on a lilac field, single clear "SHOP NOW" CTA button.
- Six-tile category grid (Clothing, Home, Accessories, Shoes, Dresses, Kitchen & Dining) gives an immediate, scannable path into the sale by department.
- "Limited time!" badge on the hero adds credible urgency without shouting (no all-caps, no exclamation stacking).
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent brand typography and color-blocking.
- Footer surfaces real incentives (10% off first app order, Klarna, inclusive sizing) rather than just legal boilerplate.

## What's weak

- A second "Up to 30% Off" banner (furniture/décor/Halloween) appears right under the 40%-off hero — two different percentages competing for attention dilutes the headline offer.
- The bottom two-thirds is six unpriced lifestyle collections with zero CTAs — no "shop the look," no linked product, just imagery and a caption.
- No deadline anywhere — "Limited time!" is repeated twice but never says when it ends, undercutting the urgency it's trying to create.
- No loyalty/AnthroPerks callout despite this being exactly the kind of sale where a member-early-access angle (used well on Aug 2) would add stakes.
- Asterisks on both offers (40%* and 30%**) signal exclusions that aren't resolved until small print at the very bottom — buried relative to the bold hero claims.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half: end it after the category grid. Move the "Get Autumn Ready" 30%-off banner and all six lifestyle collections into a separate send — they're competing offers and off-strategy content bolted onto a flash sale.
- 2. Add a hard deadline to the hero ("Ends tonight" or a date) — "Limited time!" alone isn't urgency, it's a placeholder for urgency.
- 3. Give at least the top 2-3 lifestyle tiles (Trend Hub, Sweater Dresses, Suede Shop) their own "Shop Now" links if they stay in — right now they're dead weight with no path to purchase.
- 4. Surface AnthroPerks member pricing/early-access alongside the 40% off, echoing the Aug 2 "Today ONLY Early Access" beat — stacking loyalty + discount is a stronger hook than discount alone.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off is here — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off sale access starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale styles, home, shoes & more — before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `AnthroPerks members: 40% off unlocks first`
