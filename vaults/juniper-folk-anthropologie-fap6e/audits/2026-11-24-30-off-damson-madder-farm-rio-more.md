# 30% OFF Damson Madder, Farm Rio & more 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about three days. It reuses the plaid-edged gift-tag hero with the giant "30% OFF" from the 11/21–11/24 emails, including "🚨30% OFF BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS LIVE 🚨" earlier today. The "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" strip sits on a green band under the tag, and the same three red CTA buttons carry over. The only new element is the subject's brand callout of Damson Madder and Farm Rio. The body doesn't back that up: I can't see either brand named anywhere in the render.
- The hierarchy is strong. The eye lands on the big 30% and then on the three red buttons. The category grid below is clean and shoppable. The offer stack is confusing, though. A 30% tag sits above an "extra 40%" strip, and the fine print is a wall of tiny text at the bottom, so the offer reads as less honest than it should.
- Judgement: a competent but redundant send. The subject promises brand-led specificity that the creative never delivers, and a subscriber on day four of near-identical promos has little reason to open it.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF" on the tag is the unmistakable focal point.
- Three stacked red CTA buttons (Shop Clothing / Home & Furniture / Gifts) are clear and tappable.
- The red-banner category grid (dresses, kitchen, bottoms, shoes, jewelry, holiday decor) is easy to scan and has strong editorial photography.
- The "Might We Recommend…" product row with star ratings adds social proof.
- The seasonal holiday styling (tree, party shop, gifting) fits the moment.

## What's weak

- The subject names Damson Madder and Farm Rio, but neither brand appears in the visible creative. This is a bait-and-switch feel.
- The hero is the same creative as the 11/21–11/24 sends, which adds to subscriber fatigue.
- The "30% off" and "extra 40% off" stack is confusing. It's unclear whether the discounts combine, and the asterisks and exclusions are buried in tiny footer text.
- The top banner marquee is cut off ("IDAY STARTS NOW! | BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! | BL"). It reads as repetitive and slightly clipped.
- The category tiles have only a bare red label and no price or offer, so the clicks go to broad categories.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the creative match the subject. Add a dedicated Damson Madder / Farm Rio module high in the email with real product shots, or change the subject to match what's actually there.
- 2. Collapse the offer into one clear line, such as "30% off everything, extra 40% off sale styles," and move the key exclusions out of the footer.
- 3. Refresh the hero daily. Vary the creative or the angle, such as a countdown or a "last chance" frame, so the email doesn't look like a repeat.
- 4. Put prices or "from $X" on the product tiles, and name the brands in the tile labels.
- 5. Fix the top marquee so it doesn't clip, or drop it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with Outlook/VML scaffolding. The visible source has a malformed Outlook CSS rule, a zoom-blocking viewport meta, and missing alt attributes on 5 images. Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party pixel with an empty parameter:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends in an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. If this was meant to be populated per recipient, the merge failed or the value is blank by design. I can't tell which from the source.
- **Recipient identifier in a pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112426_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder1_MID/003a700000uTzaxAAC` includes what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`). It is a per-recipient ID in a third-party URL, which is a privacy consideration. It is not a functional defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry (`, ,`) is invalid.
  - `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it is a stray declaration and not applied to `font-family`. The Outlook font override is therefore likely ineffective.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Images missing alt text (5 reported):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0` (tracking pixel)
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAAC` (tracking pixel)

  The pixels should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech. The three hosted images need explicit alt, either descriptive or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients. Low severity.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The fallback stacks (Helvetica, Arial, Georgia) cover this. It is informational only.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` in the Dotomi pixel is the only possible failed merge (see §2).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes it may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so it cannot be confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** This has the same capture caveat. If it really is absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `s.anthropologie.com`, and alignment can't be verified from the data provided.
- The footer, physical address and body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion and weren't assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. The 42 links are redirect-wrapped and unprobed, and the truncated source hides the final URLs.
- The only campaign identifier visible is the pixel path `112426_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder1_MID`.
- Landing page alignment with the subject ("30% OFF Damson Madder, Farm Rio & more") is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`, with no empty entry and `!important` inside the declaration.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add `alt` to all 5 flagged images: `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels, and descriptive or empty alt on the three hosted images. Also give `<title>` a value.
4. Check the raw MIME source or a direct-to-inbox copy for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The AgentMail relay may be stripping them, so don't treat them as confirmed failures yet.
5. Check whether the empty `dtm_email_hash=` is intentional, and review the exposure of the Contact ID in the third-party pixel path.
6. Resolve a sample of the 42 tracked links to confirm UTM retention and landing page match.
7. Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and address can be audited.
8. Move the comment below the DOCTYPE.
