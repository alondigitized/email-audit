# ENDS TONIGHT: 25% off denim, sweaters & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first category promo. A pale-blue "WINTER WARM UP" hero puts a big navy "25% OFF*" over "Denim, Sweaters & Sweater Dresses" with a SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer immediately. It is also the same creative as the Nov 13 "25% off denim, sweaters & sweater dresses" send and the Nov 14 "ENDS SOON" version. Only the orange "ends tonight!" sticker is new, which is the same pattern as the Nov 6-8 dress sequence.
- Below the hero, the email stacks a "1000+ New Sale Styles" block and a separate "30% Off" holiday banner (Hosting, Throws, Gifts & More). Together with the 25% offer, that gives three competing messages in the first screen and a half. Then it runs into a long "More to Explore" grid and a "Made with you in mind" product row with "Top Rated" labels. The top is strong, but the middle is a catalog dump.
- My judgement is that this is a competent last-call send. A subscriber who has seen this three days running will read "ends tonight" as a habit, not a deadline.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a huge "25% OFF*" over named categories, with one navy SHOP NOW button.
- The "ends tonight!" sticker in the hero corner adds urgency without shouting.
- The preheader ("Thankful for great outfits. Get yours in time for Thanksgiving") adds a seasonal angle that the subject doesn't have.
- The "Top Rated" tags on the product row give some social proof.
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The third near-identical 25% denim/sweaters send in three days (Nov 13, Nov 14, Nov 15) makes "ends tonight" feel less credible.
- Three offers compete: 25% off, 1000+ new sale styles, and 30% off holiday goods. The second SHOP NOW button, the "+" divider and the 30% banner dilute the single message.
- "Online only" and the asterisks on "25% OFF*" and "30% Off**" signal buried fine print, which costs trust.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". None names a product or category.
- The "More to Explore" grid has seven tiles of tiny captions ("The Trend Hub", "Beauty Gifts"). It's scattershot for a last-day push, and the product row has no prices or names.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the last-call real.** Drop the "1000+ New Sale Styles" block and the 30% banner from the top. Keep one offer and one button. Move the holiday-goods offer to its own send.
- 2. **Give the CTA a noun.** Use "Shop 25% off sweaters" or "Shop denim" instead of a bare SHOP NOW.
- 3. **Surface the fine print.** Put the "online only" and end-time details in one visible line under the CTA, for example "Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight."
- 4. **Cut the grid.** Replace the seven-tile "More to Explore" with 6-8 in-offer products that show names and sale prices.
- 5. **Add a reason it's different today.** Examples are a countdown timer or a "last chance for Thanksgiving delivery" line, so it doesn't read as a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off sweaters & denim`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: your Thanksgiving knit, 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends at 11:59 PM PT. Order today for Thanksgiving delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweater dresses, denim and cozy layers, all 25% off until midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template renders defensively with VML/MSO conditionals, but the Outlook font override is syntactically broken and several images lack alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) can't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML was also truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 40 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and status are unknown. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Empty hash parameter:** the Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` is empty. Either the hash is populated at send time and the test inbox stripped it, or the token failed to resolve. The source doesn't settle which.
- **Third-party pixels:** two tracking pixels load from `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com`. They are not first-party hosts, so they are subject to blocking and add external dependencies.
- **Plain-text URL ratio:** 7,768 of 10,409 plain-text characters (~75%) are URLs. This exceeds the 70% threshold and comes from long tracking URLs in the text part.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 6 images):**
  - Content images: `ES_04.jpg`, `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` and `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`, all on `image.s.anthropologie.com`.
  - Tracking pixels: `1_0` (Dotomi) and `003a700000uTzaxAAC` (mon-trk). These should carry `alt=""` explicitly, and ideally `width="1" height="1"` with `aria-hidden`.
- **Invalid MSO font rule:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has two defects:
  - The empty entry (`, ,`) is a malformed font list.
  - The `;` before `!important` makes the declaration invalid. Outlook's Arial override will not apply as intended.
- **Quoted generic keywords:** font stacks use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes (for example `'Francois One', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif'`). Quoted, these are treated as family names rather than generic fallbacks.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some engines.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Rendering relies on fallbacks, which the quoted-generic defect above weakens.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source, such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}`.
- Possible issue: the empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. The Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement for one-click unsubscribe can't be verified from this capture.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a subdomain. This is again a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer items:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML. They are unconfirmed, not absent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. The 40 tracking links were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no final landing URLs or UTM parameters. The mon-trk pixel path includes the campaign identifier `111526_AN-US_Promo25OffFashionAnd1000PlusNewSaleStylesCombo`, which matches the 25%-off subject. That is the only continuity signal available.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray `;`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images. Set `alt=""` on both tracking pixels.
3. Unquote generic keywords in the font stacks: `sans-serif` and `serif`.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add a `<title>` and `lang="en"`, and move the template comment below the DOCTYPE.
6. Confirm the `dtm_email_hash=` value is populated in a real send.
7. Capture raw headers for this sender, bypassing the relay or using a direct seed inbox. Verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results.
8. Resolve a sample of the 40 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe. Check final destinations and UTM parameters.
9. Trim the plain-text part. Use shorter links or less link-heavy text to reduce the 75% URL ratio.
