# BLACK FRIDAY SALE! 30% off Pistola, Frame & MORE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized Black Friday send, but it's the fifth Anthropologie email in about two days. The hero is the same festive gift-tag graphic with a big "30% OFF" that we saw in the 11/21 and 11/22 "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" emails. It has three pink buttons (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts) and an "Extra 40% off sale" line underneath. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The body is a tidy grid of category tiles: top-rated dresses, holiday decor under $50, sweaters under $100, sneakers, jewelry, denim, beauty gift sets, and wedding and guest styles. It reads well but dilutes the message. The subject promises "Pistola, Frame & MORE," yet I can't see Pistola or Frame called out anywhere in the render. The one visible denim tile says "Premium Denim" and doesn't name either brand.
- Verdict: a competent, shoppable recap with a subject that over-promises. The creative is stale for anyone who has been getting the previous four sends.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is huge and unmissable, and the tag graphic suits the season.
- Three separate CTA buttons route shoppers to clothing, home or gifts, so there's something for different intents.
- Price-anchored tiles such as "Under $50" and "Under $100" make the gift-shopping value concrete.
- The "Top-Rated" labels in the recommendation grid add light social proof.
- The layout is clean and consistent, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject names Pistola and Frame, but the render never shows those brands. That's a bait-and-switch feel.
- The hero repeats the creative from the earlier Black Friday sends, so it adds nothing new for this week's subscribers.
- The top banner marquee ("IDAY STARTS NOW! / BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW!") is clipped at the edges, so it reads as cut-off text. The message is also stale, since the sale has already started.
- The fine print on the 30% and the extra 40% is pushed to the very bottom, and the asterisks leave the exclusions unclear.
- There are about nine tiles plus a recommendations grid, with no single hero product. That scatters attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the named brands real. Add a Pistola and Frame denim and apparel tile right below the hero, or drop the brands from the subject.
- 2. Change the creative. After four near-identical sends, rotate the hero to a new angle such as "Final hours" or "Last call," with an actual deadline.
- 3. Fix the marquee so the text isn't truncated, and update its copy.
- 4. Cut the tile count to four or five, and lead with the strongest categories.
- 5. Put the exclusions in plain language near the offer, not only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pistola & Frame denim, 30% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off + extra 40% off sale, ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top-rated dresses, sweaters under $100 and gifts under $50, all in one place.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Denim from Pistola and Frame, plus gift picks under $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template has several confirmed head and CSS defects: invalid Outlook font rules, quoted generic font fallbacks, an empty `<title>`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The QA run also found missing alt text on 5 images and a plain-text part that is 74% URLs. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the headers are absent from the relay capture and the HTML is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 42 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 8054 of 10887 plain-text characters (about 74%) are URLs. This comes from long tracked URLs in the text part and can raise spam-filter scoring.
- **Empty pixel parameter:** the third-party pixel `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. This could be an unpopulated merge value or a test-send artifact, and I can't tell which from this capture.
- **Subscriber ID in a URL:** the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path includes `003a700000uTzaxAAC`, which looks like a Salesforce contact ID, and the message ID `112226_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayInitialReminder1_MID`. This is an identifier in a third-party request, not necessarily a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the `dotomi` and `mon-trk` tracking pixels

  The first three should carry `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text otherwise. The pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is in the head source.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. The empty list item (`, ,`) and the stray `; !important;` make the declaration invalid, so Outlook will likely ignore it.
- **Quoted generic fallbacks:** rules such as `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"]` use `'sans-serif' !important` and `'serif' !important` in quotes. Quoted, these are treated as font names, not generic families, so the generic fallback never applies.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/…')` is stripped or ignored by Gmail and several other clients, so those clients fall back to the stacks above. Combined with the quoted generics, fallback rendering is unreliable.
- **Minor:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated HTML or the QA data.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` above is the only possible personalization gap and is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` matches the `From:` address, which is consistent with alignment, but that is not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 42 tracked links are redirect URLs whose final destinations and UTM parameters were never resolved. The only URLs visible in the evidence are the image and pixel hosts.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO font rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`, with the `!important` inside the declaration and no empty list entry.
2. Use unquoted `sans-serif` and `serif` in all `Font-Normal-*` rules.
3. Fill in `<title>` and remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Add `alt=""` to the two banners and the pixels, and descriptive alt text to `1e313de8-…png` if it carries information.
5. Add a short human-readable plain-text part and stop mirroring the full tracked URLs in it.
6. Check the raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a direct-delivery test send rather than the relay copy.
7. Resolve a sample of the skipped tracking links manually to confirm destinations and UTM parameters, and confirm that `dtm_email_hash` populates in a real send.
