# NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF SALE for Black Friday!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourteenth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days. The only change from the 11/25 "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE: Black Friday is on 🖤" email is the headline. The plaid-edged gift-tag hero is the same, and "NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF SALE" has replaced the 40% line, with a red "Shop Now" button beneath it. The 30% OFF tier for everything else sits below with three more red buttons (Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts). The escalation from 40% to 50% in a single day is the one real news here, and the hero carries it.
- The body is clean and shoppable. It has a grid of red-banner category tiles (Top-Rated Dresses, Sweaters Under $100, Jewelry Under $50) and a "Might we recommend" product block. The red top ticker ("THE YEAR JUST GOT BETTER | THE BEST DEALS OF THE YE…") is cut off at the right edge. The email is long, and the two stacked discount tiers ("50% off sale" and "30% off everything else") make the offer harder to parse than it should be. The footer fine print is tiny and dense.
- Verdict: a solid, trustworthy send with a real escalation, but fatigue is the main risk. A fourteenth near-identical email in four days will feel like noise unless the subject sells the new 50%.

## What's working

- The "NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF" headline is the biggest, clearest element and signals escalation over the earlier 40% emails.
- The hero has a single prominent "Shop Now" button right under the 50% offer.
- Red-banner category tiles with price caps ("Under $50", "Under $100") make the body skimmable and concrete.
- Anthropologie's wordmark and the editorial photography keep the brand voice consistent.
- The "Might we recommend" block carries "Top Rated" labels, which add some social proof.

## What's weak

- Two discount tiers (50% off sale, 30% off everything else) stacked in the hero, with four buttons, split the eye and blur the primary CTA.
- The top ticker text is truncated on the right ("THE BEST DEALS OF THE YE…").
- The creative is the same plaid gift-tag as the 11/21–11/25 sends, so cadence fatigue is high.
- The asterisked exclusions ("excludes select items") and the footer fine print are tiny and buried.
- The product recommendations are uncaptioned, with no prices or names, so there is little detail to act on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the hero to one offer and one button. Lead with "50% off sale" and put the 30% tier in a single line of text, not three more buttons.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero (for example, "Ends tonight at midnight ET"), since the subject carries no urgency.
- 3. Fix the truncated ticker, or shorten the copy so it fits.
- 4. Add prices and product names to the recommendation tiles.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier sends, such as a fresh hero image, so the 50% escalation doesn't look like a repeat.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`image.s.anthropologie.com`, `login.dotomi.com`, `pxl.mon-trk.com`). It has a few real code defects: malformed CSS, a comment before the DOCTYPE, an empty `<title>`, and missing alt attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from the data given.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links weren't probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so link destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **`login.dotomi.com` pixel has an empty parameter.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The value is blank, which could mean an unresolved merge field or a hash stripped by the relay. Confirm against the original send.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels** (`login.dotomi.com`, `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../112526_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayDeeperDiscount_AM/003a700000uT...`) have no `alt` attribute. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - Content images: `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` and `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`. Screen readers may read out the filenames, and nothing shows if images are blocked.
  - Tracking pixels: `1_0` (dotomi) and `003a700000uTzaxAAC` (mon-trk). These should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` stops users from pinch-zooming, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. That triggers quirks mode in some older Outlook/IE-based renderers.
- **Malformed MSO CSS.** The rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family entry, and `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it's invalid and ignored.
- **Quoted generic font keywords.** Rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes, for example `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"] { font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif' }`. Quoted, they're read as font names, so the generic fallback never applies.
- **Duplicate selector.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is defined twice, which is harmless but adds bloat.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` in `<style>`.** Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip it, so the web fonts fall back to the stack. The fallbacks above are partly broken (see the quoted-keyword point).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8528 of 11364 characters (75%) are URLs, which hurts the text alternative's readability and can count against spam scoring.

- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` is the only suspect (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, so check the subdomain's alignment with a direct header inspection.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so they're unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- The pixel path contains a campaign identifier (`112526_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayDeeperDiscount_AM`) that suggests tracking is attributed on the sender side.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three content images. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to both tracking pixels.
2. Fill in `<title>` and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
4. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Unquote the generic keywords in the font rules, and remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
5. Verify the `dtm_email_hash=` value in the original send, since it should not be blank.
6. Pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, then re-run the compliance checks.
7. Resolve a sample of the 44 tracked links to confirm UTM parameters and that landing pages match the "Extra 50% off" offer.
8. Shorten the plain-text alternative by reducing raw tracking URLs.
