# 40% OFF everyone’s favorites: Aero Pant, Astrid Sweater & MORE
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the twenty-second Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days. It reuses the plaid-edged gift-tag hero from the 11/27 "🚨 ATTN: 40% OFF our most-loved styles: Rylan Pant, Mabel Shirt & MORE" email. The hero stacks three tiers: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE, 40% OFF our most-loved styles, and 30% OFF almost everything else. The tag-and-bow shape is distinctive and the hierarchy reads cleanly.
- The subject promises "40% OFF everyone's favorites: Aero Pant, Astrid Sweater & MORE." The body never shows an Aero Pant or an Astrid Sweater by name. The grid below the hero is generic category tiles like Top-Rated Dresses, Sweaters Under $100 and Premium Denim. The 40% tier is also the middle tier, not the headline, so the subject leads with the second-best offer. The named products are a promise the email doesn't visibly keep.
- Overall it's a competent, polished send with a clear offer. It is also a near-clone of the one sent hours earlier, with only the product names swapped in the subject. A subscriber on the 22nd Black Friday email will feel fatigue more than excitement.

## What's working

- The three-tier hero makes the offer ladder instantly scannable, with big percentages and one CTA per tier.
- The three CTAs (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts) are bright pink buttons that stand out against the maroon.
- The red-banner category tiles are consistent and easy to tap, with price anchors like "Under $50" and "Under $100."
- The photography is rich and seasonal, with holiday decor, a stocking and gift sets that match the time of year.
- "Might We Recommend…" product row, footer links and the App Store badge add useful secondary paths.

## What's weak

- The named hero products (Aero Pant, Astrid Sweater) don't appear as labeled products anywhere in the render, so the subject and body disconnect.
- The top banner text is cut off at both edges ("THE YEAR JUST GOT BETTER | THE BEST DEALS OF THE YE…"), which looks like a clipped marquee.
- The "Might We Recommend" tiles show no names or prices, only star dots, so they give little product detail to click on.
- The email is very long, and many of the tiles are low-intent browse categories.
- The hero shows multiple asterisks and "online & in stores" qualifiers, and the fine print is tiny and buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the body. Show the Aero Pant and Astrid Sweater in a hero-adjacent module with names and prices, or drop the names from the subject.
- 2. Lead with the strongest offer. "EXTRA 50% OFF" is the headline in the hero, but the subject sells 40%.
- 3. Fix the clipped marquee banner so it fits the width or scrolls cleanly.
- 4. Add product names and sale prices to the recommendation row.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Consolidate near-duplicate sends so each one has a distinct hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off sale + 40% off Aero Pant & Astrid Sweater`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Aero Pant is 40% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off almost everything else. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved styles, now 40% off. Shop before they sell out.`

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie Black Friday (`anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`)

## 1. Technical Summary
The Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template has malformed CSS in its Outlook block, five images without alt text, and a Dotomi tracking pixel with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers. The HTML supplied is truncated, so body-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **45 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The Dotomi pixel has an empty email-hash value.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The captured copy may not have populated this value, either because of how the test inbox was sent to or because of relay processing. If production sends look the same, the pixel can't match users.
- **The Monetate pixel (`pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-…/112726_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayReminder1_MID/003a700000uTzaxA…`) embeds an internal campaign ID and a Salesforce-style 18-character record ID in the path.** This is an ID leak, not a defect. The URL is truncated in the QA output, so the full value can't be confirmed.
- **The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (8718 of 11599 chars).** Long tracking URLs dominate the text alternative, which raises spam-filter risk and hurts readability for text-only clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five images are missing alt text.**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the Dotomi pixel (`1_0`)
  - the Monetate pixel (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`)
  
  The two tracking pixels should carry an explicit `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. The three `image.s.anthropologie.com` images should have alt text or `alt=""` if they are decorative.
- **The Outlook (MSO) font rule is malformed:** `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`
  - It contains an empty font-family entry (`, ,`).
  - It has a stray `;` before `!important`, so the `!important` is dropped as a separate invalid declaration and the font fallback chain is unreliable in Outlook.
- **Generic font keywords are quoted.** For example, `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` appear in the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` rules. Quoted, they are read as font names rather than generic fallbacks, so the fallback never triggers.
- **Web fonts are loaded with `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/…')` inside `<style>`.** Gmail and several other clients ignore this, so these clients rely on the fallback stacks, which are flawed as noted above.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is a minor accessibility and web-view issue.
- **The viewport meta tag is `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`.** It disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem in web-view and some mobile clients.
- **The duplicate `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is harmless.** I'm not counting it as an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **The empty `dtm_email_hash=` is the only token-shaped problem visible** (see section 2).
- **No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are evident in the visible source.** The HTML is truncated, so the full body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these. Treat them as unverified, not as confirmed failures. If a raw `.eml` from the sender has them, this resolves. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements need one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown.** This is likely a relay artifact. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` should be checked directly with a DNS or header-capture test.
- **The visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed.** They fall in the truncated portion, so no pass or fail is given.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page destinations cannot be assessed.** All 45 links were routed through tracking redirects and skipped by the probe. No landing-page alignment issues are reported, because there is no data to support one.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the MSO font rule.**
   - Change it to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
   - Remove the empty entry and the stray semicolon.
2. **Unquote the generic font keywords** (`sans-serif`, `serif`) in all `Font-Normal-*` rules.
3. **Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.** Add descriptive or empty alt text to the three Anthropologie CDN images.
4. **Populate `<title>`, and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag.**
5. **Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=`.** Check whether production sends populate it, and whether the hash is stripped for seed or relay addresses.
6. **Capture the raw message headers.** Use a direct seed inbox or the raw source to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
7. **Follow the redirects on a sample of the 45 tracking links.** Use a real HTTP trace to check UTMs and final destinations.
8. **Shorten URLs in the plain-text part.** Use fewer or cleaner links to reduce the URL-to-text ratio.
