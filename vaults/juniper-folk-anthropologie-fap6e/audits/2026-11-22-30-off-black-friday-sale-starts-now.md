# 🚨 30% OFF: BLACK FRIDAY SALE STARTS NOW 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent Black Friday send. It is also the fourth one from Anthropologie in two days, and the creative is the same festive gift-tag hero as the three 11/21 emails ("30% off: BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW with AnthroPerks", "30% off 1000s of styles", "Early Access ENDS TONIGHT"). The hero is strong. The gift tag carries a huge "30% OFF" and three category buttons (Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts). Below it sits a secondary "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" band with its own button.
- The subject is the weak point. It reuses the "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" line from the 11/21 send. That claim feels stale on 11/22, and the siren emojis and ALL CAPS make it read as spam. The body is a clean, well-organized grid of category tiles, but the email never gives one reason to act today.
- Judgement: good execution and weak differentiation. A subscriber who got three near-identical sends yesterday has little reason to open this one.

## What's working

- The gift-tag hero puts "30% OFF" front and center, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- Three category CTAs (Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts) let a shopper pick a lane right in the hero.
- The "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" band stacks a second offer without competing with the main one.
- The editorial tile grid (sweaters, decor, shoes, sleepwear, furniture, beauty) is easy to scan. The navy label bars are consistent and the imagery is strong.
- The footer has a clear "Even More to Love" block with New Arrivals, E-Gift Card, TikTok and Pick Up In Store.

## What's weak

- The "STARTS NOW" banner at the top repeats the 11/21 creative and now feels stale. The ticker strip at the top is also cut off mid-word ("IY STARTS NOW!"), which looks like a clipped marquee.
- The email has no urgency or deadline anywhere visible. The earlier sends at least had "ends tonight" and "today only" stickers.
- Two overlapping discounts (30% off and extra 40% off sale) with asterisks and fine print make it hard to tell what applies to what. Honesty suffers.
- The tiles link to broad categories rather than hero products. "Might We Recommend" shows tiny product images with no prices.
- There is no AnthroPerks or loyalty messaging, which was the hook in the 11/21 emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give this send a reason to exist. Lead with the day-specific angle (the weekend, the last day before Black Friday, or "Prices drop again Friday"), and put a real deadline sticker on the tag.
- 2. Clarify the offer architecture. Say "30% off full-price" and "extra 40% off sale" as two distinct lines with the exclusions in plain view, not behind asterisks.
- 3. Fix the clipped top banner so it doesn't cut off words.
- 4. Add prices to the "Might We Recommend" tiles, plus a "best seller" or rating count to make them clickable.
- 5. Bring back the AnthroPerks hook for members.
- 6. Drop the siren emojis and the caps.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off everything, plus extra 40% off sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday weekend, started: 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, shoes, furniture and gifts. Sale styles take an extra 40% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated sweaters and holiday decor are marked down. See what's in.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie "Black Friday Sale Starts Now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ESP template (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `image.s.` / `s.` subdomains) with several confirmed code-level defects: broken Outlook CSS, zoom-blocking viewport settings, empty `<title>`, and missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of how the email was captured, not because of confirmed failures. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** These are click-redirect domains, so the destinations, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels are present:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112226_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayInitialLaunch_AM/...`
- **`dtm_email_hash=` is empty.** It is either an unresolved personalization value or intentionally blank for this recipient. It can't be told apart from the data provided.
- **Render-time dependencies:**
  - The Google Fonts `@import` (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...`) adds an external render-time request.
  - `@font-face` loads `community.urbn.com/static/fonts/*.woff` from a different domain.
  - Both are ignored by many clients and fall back to the declared stacks.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid Outlook CSS (confirmed in source):** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`.
  - The empty font-family entry (`, ,`) is invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` orphans it, so the declaration is not important.
  - The font-stack fix is probably not applied in Outlook.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom in supporting clients. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this hurts accessibility and the browser-view / web-version context.
- **Missing alt text on content images (3):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - With images blocked, these render as blank or filename text. If the images are decorative, they should have `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (2):** the `dotomi` and `mon-trk` images. This is low severity, since they are 1x1 pixels and should carry `alt=""` and `aria-hidden`.
- **CSS cleanliness:**
  - `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (identical rules).
  - `.yfix{}` is repeated empty.
  - Custom `Font-Normal-*` targeting relies on `[style*=]` attribute selectors, which Gmail and some webmail clients don't support.
- **Plain-text part is link-heavy:** 8148 of 10977 characters (74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. That is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were visible in the truncated source.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` value in the Dotomi pixel is the only candidate, and it may be intentional (see section 2). Without the full source, the rest cannot be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
  - The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed.
  - If the headers are actually absent, one-click unsubscribe fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be verified.
  - The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is a normal setup.
  - The relay is the reason the result is unknown, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer not seen.** The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing parameters are unverifiable.** All 42 links go through click-redirect domains and were skipped, so the final URLs, UTM params and landing-page match to the "30% off" offer weren't checked.
- **The `mon-trk` pixel path carries a campaign ID** (`112226_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayInitialLaunch_AM`). This is consistent with the send date and campaign, with no mismatch evident.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the Outlook CSS:** remove the empty font-family entry and the stray `; !important`. Use `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Add alt text.** Use descriptive alt on the content banners, or `alt=""` if decorative. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. **Populate `<title>`** with the subject or campaign name.
5. **Rebalance the plain-text part.** Cut raw tracking URLs and add readable text, so URLs are under 70%.
6. **Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source with full headers.** That would settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. **Resolve the redirect chains** for a sample of the 42 tracked links, to check final URLs, UTM params and landing-page status.
8. **Deduplicate the CSS:** drop the repeated `Font-Normal-os` rule and the empty `.yfix{}` blocks.
