# Join AnthroPerks for 20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a two-offer email, and the two offers compete. The top hero is a pink "20% OFF*" on a cream card inside a chartreuse frame, with an "ends soon!" badge and a "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" button. Just below it is a lavender "EXTRA 50% OFF SALE" banner with a "limited time!" badge and a "SHOP NOW" button. The 20% hero is the headline, but the 50% banner is the better deal, and the two sit within a few hundred pixels of each other. The subject promotes only the 20%. Anyone who clicks through for that offer will see a bigger one right under it.
- The rest of the email is a long editorial scroll: Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Decor, Holiday Party Accessories and Holiday Glam. After that comes a 3x3 product grid, a perks strip, and a large block of fine print. It looks good and stays on brand. But it has no single product, no price and no reason to click one tile over another. This is the second AnthroPerks 20% send in three days, after "20% off, AnthroPerks exclusive" on Oct 22. A day after "Extra 50% off sale is live. Go." (Oct 23), the 50% banner here is a repeat. Cadence is the main problem.
- Verdict: the creative is clean and the brand is trusted, but this is a recycled mashup of two earlier sends. Fatigue and the muddled offer hierarchy hold it at a middling score.

## What's working

- The hero is large, readable and offer-first. "20% OFF*" is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The "ends soon!" badge adds urgency without shouting.
- The lifestyle photography is on-season (party dressing, cashmere, mantel decor) and consistent with the brand.
- The "Even more to love" strip packs in real perks: 25% off for 5+ reviews, Pay Later with Afterpay, free Home Design Services, and Find a Store Near You.
- The category tiles give clear paths into the holiday shops.

## What's weak

- The 50% off banner undercuts the 20% hero. The better deal sits in a smaller, lower-contrast block below it, so the email has no single clear offer.
- "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" reads as a membership sign-up CTA. It is unclear what a current member should do.
- The category tiles and the product grid have no prices, names or CTAs. The grid is just thumbnails under "You'll ♥ these, too."
- The fine print is a very long block and the asterisks are everywhere. The offer feels hedged: "sale and other exclusions apply," plus in-store and online qualifiers.
- This is a repeat of the Oct 22 AnthroPerks 20% send and the Oct 23 50% send. There is nothing new here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer. If the 50% sale is live, lead with it and put the AnthroPerks 20% in a small member line. Otherwise drop the sale banner from this send.
- 2. Rewrite the CTA as a verb plus a noun, such as "Get 20% off", and don't make it a sign-up ask for members.
- 3. Add prices, product names or a "shop the edit" button to the product grid, and link a few hero products rather than just category pages.
- 4. Cut the fine print to a short summary line with a link to the full terms.
- 5. Space out the AnthroPerks sends, or send a different angle: a holiday gift guide or restock news instead of the same 20% again.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Party dresses + 20% off, members only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 50% off sale styles, in stores and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cashmere, holiday party looks and mantel decor, all in one place.`

The 2 tracking pixels also lack `alt`; `alt=""` would be correct for them.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family entry, and the stray `; !important;` is a separate invalid declaration. The font override therefore likely doesn't apply in Outlook, and `'Font-Normal'` isn't a defined font.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on clients that honor it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** a minor accessibility and metadata gap.
- **Google Fonts `@import`:** the `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. Rendering falls back to the stacks in the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` rules, which depend on those inline style hooks being present.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** the `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in legacy IE and Outlook rendering paths.
- **Template bloat:** the `<style>` block carries CSS for UO, Free People, Beholden and Terrain brands, and `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice. This adds payload but isn't a functional defect.
