# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I didn't get Anthropologie — I got a wall. The page I landed on is a bot-block screen: logo at the top, the words "Access is temporarily restricted," and a list of reasons I'm being punished for just trying to shop. There is no hero, no offer, no product, no vibe — nothing that says "welcome, come browse our new dresses." This is a hard stop at the door.

## What's working

- **Brand logo:** The Anthropologie serif wordmark at the top is crisp and recognizable — at least I know whose wall I hit.
- **"Submit Feedback About Experience" link:** Technically a recovery path exists. It's not great, but it's there.

## What's weak

- **Complete content blackout:** No hero, no imagery, no copy, no navigation — I have zero reason to stay or believe I can shop here right now.
- **Cold, clinical error message:** "Access is temporarily restricted" and a numbered bullet list of reasons I'm being flagged reads like a security audit report, not a brand interaction. Anthropologie sells *magic*; this page sells anxiety.
- **Bot-flag reasons list is user-hostile:** Telling a real shopper that "automated (bot) activity on your network" or "use of developer tools" got them blocked is accusatory. I didn't do anything wrong.
- **No recovery CTA for legitimate users:** There's no "Try Again," no CAPTCHA to prove I'm human, no path back to the actual homepage — just a dead end.
- **No brand warmth whatsoever:** The entire page is black text on white. No Anthro color, no texture, no typography system. It could be any site's generic error page.
- **IP exposed publicly:** Showing `IP 75.140.9.0` to the end user is unnecessary and feels invasive — a real shopper doesn't need to see that.

## Recommendations

- **Add a human-verification path (CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" button):** Right now the block is a dead end. Give me one tap to prove I'm a person and drop me into the actual homepage.
- **Restyle the block page with Anthro brand DNA:** Even if access is restricted, use the brand's warm cream palette, editorial typography, and a soft illustration. "We're just making sure it's you — hang tight" lands very differently than a numbered list of accusations.
- **Replace the numbered blame-list with a single friendly line:** Something like "We noticed something a little unusual. Tap below to continue." No numbered reasons, no IP address, no blame.
- **Surface a "Go back to the homepage" link** that retries the request — at minimum give me a way to try again without copy-pasting the URL.
