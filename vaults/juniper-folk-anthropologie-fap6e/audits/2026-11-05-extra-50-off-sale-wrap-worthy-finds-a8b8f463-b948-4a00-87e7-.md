# Extra 50% off sale: wrap-worthy finds
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, offer-first sale send. A dark green hero carries "Instant Gift-ification: EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" with a SHOP GIFTS FOR YOU & THEM button. The orange "50% Off*" is the largest element and the eye lands on it first. Six color-blocked category buttons sit right below it, then an app-exclusive "25% OFF** select slouchy totes" strip with a "final hours!" badge.
- The lower two-thirds is a long editorial grid (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop and so on), followed by nine "we picked these just for you" product tiles. That is a lot of scroll with no prices and no per-tile CTAs. The extra 50% off is the same promo that ran Oct 22–25, so a regular subscriber has seen it before. The subject says "wrap-worthy finds" but the body is a generic sitewide sale, and that mismatch weakens the gifting hook.
- Judgement: solid, workmanlike promo. The hero is strong but it's a recycled offer, and the body dilutes it.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and high-contrast: orange "50% Off*" on dark green, with a clear button.
- Six color-coded sale category buttons make it easy to jump to the right area.
- The app-exclusive 25% slouchy totes strip adds a second, time-limited offer ("final hours!").
- The editorial tiles (Cashmere Shop, Holiday Party Shop, Winter Activities Shop) match the season.
- The photography is consistent and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The "wrap-worthy finds" subject isn't echoed in the hero. The gift angle is only partly there ("Gift-ification").
- The 9-tile "picked just for you" block has no prices, product names or sale callouts, only star ratings. Nothing shows the discount on actual items.
- The editorial grid is long and pushes the personalized picks far down the email.
- Heavy fine print behind two asterisks, with exclusions listed at the bottom.
- The extra 50% off repeats the Oct 22–25 promo, and the 25% totes strip is app-only, which splits the focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put sale prices and "was/now" on the picks grid, and add a "Shop all sale" button under it. This is the single change most likely to lift clicks.
- 2. Cut the editorial grid from six tiles to three and move the picks grid up.
- 3. Add a real end date to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday", rather than leaving urgency implicit.
- 4. Make the hero and subject agree. Either lead with gifts or lead with the 50% off, not a blend.
- 5. Put the key terms (exclusions, dates) in one line under the hero instead of only in the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links unprobed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unconfirmed.
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`** in the Dotomi pixel: `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. - **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 7,907 of 10,758 characters (~73%). The tracking-heavy text part raises spam-filter risk. It also reads poorly in text-only clients.
- No broken links were confirmed in the visible data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed MSO font rule:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`. It has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`), and the stray `;` before `!important` makes that declaration invalid. Outlook may ignore the Arial-first intent and fall back to its default font.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 for low-vision users.
- **No `lang` on `<html>`.** The tag has only the `xmlns`, `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o` attributes, so screen readers can't pick the pronunciation language.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Missing alt text on content images** (the first two are `emailbanner_pencilline_*` images):
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With no alt attribute, screen readers may read out the filename, and nothing shows when images are blocked.
- **Missing alt on two tracking pixels:** `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAAC`. These are low severity, but they should carry `alt=""` so they are skipped by assistive tech.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` in a `<style>` block is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stacks cover this, so it isn't a defect.
- **Duplicate rule:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice, which is harmless.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This is low risk in email clients.
