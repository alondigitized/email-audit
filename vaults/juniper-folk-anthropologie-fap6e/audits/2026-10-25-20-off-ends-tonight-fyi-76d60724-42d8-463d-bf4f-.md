# 20% off ends tonight, FYI
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the third day in a row of the same AnthroPerks 20% off and extra 50% off sale story. Oct 22 was "20% off, AnthroPerks exclusive," Oct 24 was "Join AnthroPerks for 20% off," and today is the "ends tonight" version. The offer stack at the top is clear: a pink "20% OFF*" hero on a cream card inside a chartreuse frame, with an orange "ends tonight!" badge and a "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" button. Right below it is a lavender "EXTRA 50% OFF** SALE" banner with a "SHOP NOW" button.
- The two offers compete, which is the same problem as the Oct 24 send. Two stacked banners with different buttons and different mechanics make the eye choose. The 20% is member-only, tied to a PERKS20 code, and excludes sale items. The 50% applies only to sale items. The page never says which one applies to what.
- Under the offers, the email turns into a long editorial grid: Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Mantel Decor, Holiday Party Accessories and Holiday Glam, followed by a nine-product grid. The photography is strong and the holiday framing is timely. But no tile or product shows a price, a discount or a sale label, so the urgency in the hero doesn't carry into the rest of the email. Verdict: a competent deadline send with a muddled offer and a body that works against the urgency. About a 6.

## What's working

- The hero offer is big and legible. A pink "20% OFF*" on cream sits inside the chartreuse frame, and it is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The "ends tonight!" badge appears on both the 20% card and the 50% banner, so the deadline reads fast.
- Holiday-themed categories (Holiday Party Shop, Mantel Decor, Holiday Glam, Cashmere) fit the season.
- The editorial photography is high quality, and the models and styling suit a style-led women's shopper.
- The footer has useful extras: SMS signup for 10% off, Afterpay, inclusive sizing and styling services.

## What's weak

- The email carries two offers (20% off and extra 50% off) with two different CTAs, and no clear primary action.
- The 20% banner says "YOUR PURCHASE" with an asterisk and "sale and other exclusions apply." The 50% banner has a double asterisk, and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense. The offer reads as less honest than the big type suggests.
- The six category tiles and nine products have no prices or sale callouts. Nothing connects the product grid to the discount.
- The email repeats the same content as Oct 22 and Oct 24 and uses the same hero art. Subscribers who have already seen it twice this week will find it stale.
- The email is very long. The product grid, "Even More to Love" and the legal block sit far below the offer, and the deadline is not repeated anywhere lower down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with the 20% (member) offer or the 50% (sale) offer, and make the other a small secondary line. Don't give both a full-width banner and a button.
- 2. Add a price or a "% off" tag to each product in the grid, so the discount continues into the product area.
- 3. Add a second "ends tonight" reminder with a countdown or a "11:59 PM PT" line after the category tiles.
- 4. Say who the offer is for, for example "AnthroPerks members: code PERKS20," and put the exclusions in plain words near the button rather than only in the footer.
- 5. Change the creative from one send to the next. Show a different hero or product story on the last day instead of reusing the Oct 22 art.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 20% off + extra 50% off sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code PERKS20 before 11:59 PM PT. Holiday party looks included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get 20% off. Sale gets an extra 50% off. Both end tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several code-level defects in the visible head and CSS: a malformed Outlook font rule, quoted generic font keywords, and a viewport that blocks zoom. Three content images lack alt text, and one tracking pixel carries an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, and the HTML source was truncated before the body and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **34 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Empty hash on the Dotomi pixel.** The pixel at `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has `dtm_email_hash=` with no value. Either the hash didn't resolve at send time (for example, for a seed or relay recipient) or the token is broken. If it's broken, the retargeting pixel can't match the recipient.
- **Third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../102526_AN-US_20OffAnthroPerksExclusiveEndsTonight_NonMember` and the Dotomi pixel are both loaded. The Dotomi pixel sits on a `login.` subdomain. Both are blocked or proxied by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and some clients, so they are unreliable as open or visit signals.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The rule `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has an empty list entry. The `;` before `!important` also leaves a stray token. The declaration is probably dropped or misparsed, so the Outlook font override may not apply.
- **Quoted generic keywords.** The font stacks use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes, for example `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"] { font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif' ... }`. Quoted, these are treated as family names rather than the generic keywords, so the final fallback does nothing.
- **Web fonts are fragile.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. Fallbacks are needed, and the quoted-keyword bug above weakens them.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom where clients honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Missing alt text, content images (3).** All three are on `image.s.anthropologie.com`:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these render as nothing.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (2).** The Dotomi and mon-trk pixels have no alt. They are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ... ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ... -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy renderers. It is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`** (see section 2) is the only evidence of an unresolved or empty dynamic value.
- **No visible unrendered merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the truncated HTML. Body content wasn't available, so personalization can't be fully cleared.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If it's genuinely absent, it conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). The same relay caveat applies, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, and alignment can't be checked from the data provided.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible because the source was truncated. Not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** Links go through click-redirect domains that QA skipped, and the visible HTML contains no resolved destination URLs. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "20% off ends tonight" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`, and remove the empty entry and the stray `;`.
2. Unquote the generic font keywords (`sans-serif`, `serif`) in all `[style*="Font-Normal-*"]` rules.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the three CDN images, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag, and add a `<title>`.
5. Investigate why `dtm_email_hash` is empty. Check whether it only happens for seed or relay recipients.
6. Capture the raw headers before relay (or fetch from the source MTA) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Re-run QA with redirect resolution on the 34 tracked links, and with the full, untruncated HTML. That would cover the UTM, destination and footer checks.
