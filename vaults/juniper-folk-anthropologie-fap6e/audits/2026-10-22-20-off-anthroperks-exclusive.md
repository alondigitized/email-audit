# 20% off, AnthroPerks exclusive
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first AnthroPerks member send. The hero is a pink "20% OFF*" on a cream card inside a chartreuse frame, with an orange "limited time!" badge. Below it sit "YOUR PURCHASE" and a "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" button. The eye lands on the offer first, which is right. It's a quieter, less aggressive follow-up to last week's five-send 30% Fashion Weekend run (Oct 15-18), and the Oct 19 holiday editorial send.
- The weakness is the offer and the CTA. A 20% discount is a step down from the 30% the brand just trained subscribers on. "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" reads as a loyalty-signup prompt, not a shopping prompt. The body below the hero is a pile of holiday category tiles (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Mantel Décor, Holiday Party Accessories, Holiday Glam) with no clear second offer or hero product. It feels like a catalog, not a campaign.
- Judgement: a competent loyalty promo with a clear top but a muddy middle and a lot of fine print. It's a mid-pack send, helped by brand recognition and a clean hero.

## What's working

- The hero is dominant and legible: large pink "20% OFF*" with "YOUR PURCHASE" in bold, on a cream card.
- The "AnthroPerks | EXCLUSIVE" header frames this as a member benefit right away.
- The "limited time!" badge adds urgency without shouting.
- The tile grid is visually rich. The editorial photos (party looks, cashmere, mantel décor, red nails) are on-season for holiday.
- The "Even More to Love" footer strip offers real extras: 25% off for 5+ reviews, Afterpay, free Home Design Services, and a store finder.

## What's weak

- "JOIN & SHOP TODAY" is ambiguous for existing members, and the button is small and muted orange against the cream card.
- "Limited time!" gives no deadline. The brand history shows real "ends tonight" urgency, so this feels weaker.
- 20% is below the 30% from earlier this week, and the asterisk fine print ("sale and other exclusions apply") sits in tiny grey text.
- The six category tiles give no price, product or sub-offer, and none carries a CTA beyond a label.
- The "You'll ♥ These, Too" grid shows nine unlabeled products with no prices or names. The footer legal copy is very long and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Change the CTA to "Shop 20% off now" (for members) and make the button larger and higher contrast. Put a real end date next to it, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 2. Add a short member-gated line under the hero explaining how the code or account activation works, so existing members don't hit "Join" friction.
- 3. Cut the six category tiles down to three or four. Tie each to the offer, for example "Holiday party dresses, 20% off."
- 4. Add names and prices to the "You'll love these, too" product grid, and put one clear best-seller above it.
- 5. Pull the review-for-25% perk higher up. It's the most interesting hook and it's buried at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source shows several code-level defects: malformed MSO CSS, an empty `<title>`, zoom-blocking viewport settings and unlabeled images. The QA run couldn't verify unsubscribe headers, authentication or any of the 36 tracked links, so compliance and continuity can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 36 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a QA gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Empty hash in pixel URL:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. The pixel either fired without a resolved hash (likely a test or seed address) or the merge value failed to populate. If it also happens for real sends, Dotomi/Conversant can't match the recipient.
- **Subscriber ID in a pixel URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../102226_AN-US_20OffAnthroPerksExclusiveLaunch_NonMembers/003a700000uTzaxAAC` carries what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`) in the path. It's a minor privacy and exposure point, since it's a persistent identifier in a third-party request.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 unique images, confirmed by QA):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`. Each appears twice, probably as desktop and mobile variants.
  - `Evergreen-Email-Module1_04.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The Dotomi and mon-trk tracking pixels. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `display:none` or `aria-hidden`, so screen readers skip them.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients and web views use it as the accessible document name.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` stops pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has two problems:
  - The empty entry between commas makes the font-family value invalid, so Outlook may drop the whole declaration.
  - The stray `;` before `!important` leaves a dangling token, so the `!important` is never applied.
- **Quoted generic families:** `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` are quoted throughout the `[style*="Font-Normal-*"]` rules. Quoted, they're read as font names, not generic keywords, so the generic fallback doesn't work when the web font fails to load.
- **Web font loading:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so those clients fall back to the system font stack. The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is declared twice.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** The `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Older IE-based engines such as Outlook 2007–2013 may switch to quirks mode, which can affect layout.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`:** This is the only unresolved or empty token visible (see Section 2). No literal `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear in the truncated source.
- **Segment mismatch:** The mon-trk path contains `..._NonMembers`, while the subject reads "AnthroPerks exclusive". If this send reached non-members, the segmentation and the pixel's campaign label disagree. I can't confirm that from the HTML.
- **Truncation:** The body is truncated, so the full body wasn't checked for stray tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** The QA run didn't find either header. It noted the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed** as a sender defect. If they really are missing, that conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a dedicated subdomain.
- **Footer:** The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so I can't check them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. The 36 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated HTML shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. The only campaign identifier visible is in the mon-trk pixel path, `102226_AN-US_20OffAnthroPerksExclusiveLaunch_NonMembers`. The landing page for the 20% offer and its UTMs need a separate check against the redirect targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images. Give the two tracking pixels `alt=""`, `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
2. Fix the MSO style to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
3. Unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in every font-family fallback.
4. Populate `<title>` with the subject line, and remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Check why `dtm_email_hash` is empty, and whether the pixel path should expose a Contact ID.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` with headers, or against a direct ESP capture, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
7. Resolve the 36 tracking links with a redirect-following probe, then check the final URLs for UTMs and for 200 responses on the offer landing page.
8. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE, and drop the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
