# 🔥 Now we’re cooking: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighteenth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days, and it reuses the same plaid-edged gift-tag hero as the 11/26 "EXTRA 50% OFF SALE! Black Friday. Easy as 🥧" email. The hero stacks two offers: "NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF* SALE" with a Shop Now button, then "+ 30% OFF** ALMOST EVERYTHING ELSE" with three buttons (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts). Both asterisks lead to fine print ("excludes select items"). The subject's "Now we're cooking" is a food pun that the hero never echoes.
- The body is a long scroll of two-up category tiles on green bands: bestselling dresses, kitchen and dining, bottoms, furniture, party shop, shoes, jewelry, decor, beauty, wedding and guest styles, and Terrain. It ends with a "Might we recommend…" product grid. The photography is strong and the brand voice is consistent. But the email is a catalog with no single focus. Nothing in the lower tiles carries a price, and the product grid shows star ratings but no names or prices.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send. Its main problem is cadence, because it is a near-duplicate of yesterday's creative, which makes it easy to skip. The "extra 50%" headline is the strongest thing in it and is clear enough to carry the email on its own.

## What's working

- The "NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF" headline is large and sits at the top of the visual hierarchy, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The red "Shop Now" button contrasts well against the burgundy tag.
- The three category buttons (clothing, home, gifts) let people self-select without scrolling.
- The editorial photography and the green label bands look cohesive and premium.
- The star ratings in the recommendation grid add some social proof.

## What's weak

- The subject's cooking pun doesn't connect to the hero, so the promise and the payoff feel disconnected.
- Two stacked offers (50% extra on sale, 30% on everything else) take a second read to understand, and the asterisks hint at exclusions.
- The hero is the same plaid-edged gift tag as the previous sends, so the creative feels stale.
- The lower tiles have no prices or discount callouts, so nothing in the body ties back to the offer.
- The recommendation grid shows only stars, with no product names or prices, which gives people little reason to click.
- The scroll is very long, with about 12 category tiles competing for attention.
- The green marquee strip at the top is cut off ("ANKSGIVING! | HAPPY THANK…"). It reads as a cropped banner rather than a clean header.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero. Lead with "Extra 50% off sale" and demote the 30% line to a secondary strip.
- 2. Change the creative between sends. Use a new hero image or a countdown tied to a real deadline, since the audience has seen this tag many times.
- 3. Put the discount on the tiles, for example "Dresses from $X" or "Up to 50% off", so each tile is a reason to click.
- 4. Cut the tile count from about 12 to 6 or 7.
- 5. Add product names and prices to the recommendation grid.
- 6. Fix the cropped top banner, or replace it with a static one-line message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off sale, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: extra 50% off sale styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off almost everything else. Dresses, decor and gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends soon. Shop the extra 50% off before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "🔥 Now we're cooking: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The template has no confirmed blocking defects. It does have several real rendering and accessibility flaws: malformed Outlook CSS, a viewport tag that disables zoom, an empty `<title>`, and 5 images without alt text. Compliance headers and UTM parameters couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels.** Two third-party pixels are embedded:
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112626_AN-US_PromoThanksgivingBlackFridayLaunch_AM/003a7000...`

  Both are subject to image blocking, and neither has alt text (see section 3).
- **Possible empty hash.** `dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the Dotomi pixel. If this is the delivered HTML rather than a capture artifact, the merge value didn't populate and the pixel can't tie the open to a recipient.
- **Contact ID in the pixel path.** The mon-trk path ends in `003a7000…`, which looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier exposed in a third-party URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 5 images):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The Dotomi pixel (`1_0`)
  - The mon-trk pixel (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`)

  The first three are content images hosted on `image.s.anthropologie.com`. Under image blocking or a screen reader they render as nothing or as filenames. The two pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** Inside `<!--[if mso]>`, the rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`:
  - It has an empty font-family list entry (`, ,`).
  - A stray `;` precedes `!important`, so the declaration is invalid and the `!important` is dropped.
- **Quoted generic font keywords.** `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` are quoted throughout the `[style*="Font-Normal-*"]` rules. Quoted, they are treated as font names rather than generic fallbacks, so the fallback chain doesn't work as intended.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (the Beholden and Terrain blocks).
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE --> <!DOCTYPE ...` can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Web font loading.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` in a `<style>` block is ignored by Gmail and other clients and adds a blocking external request in clients that honor it. The `@font-face` sources on `community.urbn.com` have the same limits. The fallbacks are what most recipients will see.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The one issue is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` value (section 2). No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear in the visible portion of the source. The HTML was truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `s.anthropologie.com`.
- **Footer not visible.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed either way.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8,528 of 11,371 characters (75%) are URLs, above the 70% QA threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and a poor plain-text experience.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable.** All 44 tracking links were skipped and the visible source includes no destination URLs. UTM presence, campaign naming consistency and landing-page alignment with the "EXTRA 50% OFF SALE" offer can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign label.** The pixel path contains `PromoThanksgivingBlackFridayLaunch_AM`, while the subject and sender say "Black Friday". That is consistent and not an issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three content images. Add `alt=""` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Populate `<title>`.
4. Fix the MSO rule: remove the empty `, ,` entry and the stray `;` before `!important`. Use unquoted `sans-serif` and `serif` in the font stacks. Delete the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` block.
5. Move the HTML comment after the DOCTYPE.
6. Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=` in the Dotomi pixel, and whether the Salesforce Contact ID in the mon-trk pixel path is acceptable to expose.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the raw original message, not the AgentMail relay copy.
8. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part.
9. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolved tracking links to check the footer, UTMs and destinations.
