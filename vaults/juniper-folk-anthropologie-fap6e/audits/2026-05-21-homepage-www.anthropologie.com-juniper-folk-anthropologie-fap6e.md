# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened the app excited to check what Anthro is pushing for spring and instead got hit with a wall that says "Access is temporarily restricted." There is no hero, no product, no promo — just a bot-block error page with a diagnostic list and an IP address. As a loyal AnthroPerks member who shops here regularly, this is a deeply frustrating first impression that has nothing to do with me and everything to do with their bot-detection misfiring.

## What's working

- **The logo renders.** The ANTHROPOLOGIE wordmark is legible and correctly positioned at the top — so I at least know I'm in the right place before the experience completely falls apart.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is inaccessible.** The message "Access is temporarily restricted" with a numbered list of bot-detection reasons (rapid taps, JavaScript issues, automated activity, developer tools) means I'm staring at a diagnostic page instead of dresses.
- **The "unusual activity" explanation is cold and technical.** Telling a real customer her IP (75.140.9.0) triggered a bot flag is not brand-appropriate — it reads like a server log, not Anthropologie.
- **Zero brand voice.** The copy is completely stripped of any warmth, seasonality, or style. This could be an error page for a utility company.
- **The only interactive element is a feedback link.** "Submit Feedback About Experience" is the loneliest CTA I've ever seen on a retail homepage. There's no "try again," no redirect to the app, no customer service link.
- **Massive blank white space** dominates the middle of the screen — the area between the logo and the error copy is completely empty, making the page feel broken and abandoned.

## Recommendations

- **Add a graceful fallback page** that keeps brand imagery and copy ("Something's off on our end — try refreshing or visit us in the app") instead of exposing raw bot-detection language to real users.
- **Surface a "Shop the App" or "Try Again" CTA** so a locked-out customer has somewhere to go rather than a dead end.
- **Never show a raw IP address** to a customer. Replace the diagnostic ID block with a simple error code and a customer service phone number or chat link.
- **Re-tune the bot-detection threshold** — an AnthroPerks member opening the app on a phone should not be flagging as a bot.
