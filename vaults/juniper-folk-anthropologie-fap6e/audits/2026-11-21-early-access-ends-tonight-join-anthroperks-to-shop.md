# 📣 Early Access ENDS TONIGHT. Join AnthroPerks to shop!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Black Friday send in one day from Anthropologie, and it's the same creative with a new deadline. The hero is the festive gift-tag graphic with a huge "30% OFF", a "final hours" sticker, and a "BLACK FRIDAY | EARLY ACCESS" banner. The eye lands on the offer immediately. It follows this morning's "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" and the midday "Join AnthroPerks for EARLY ACCESS" sends, so the novelty is gone.
- Below the hero, the body is a long, polished grid of category tiles under red label bars, followed by a "Might We Recommend…" product block. It looks good and is easy to scan. But the tiles carry no prices or promo detail, so the body doesn't reinforce the urgency in the hero.
- Verdict: a strong render with a credible deadline, but audience fatigue is a real risk. Success depends on whether "ends tonight" is believable, since the "final hours" sticker and the subject both say so while the brand has sent the same promo all day.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is huge, and the "final hours" sticker gives it credible urgency.
- The two CTAs (SHOP CLOTHING in gold, SHOP HOME & FURNITURE in gold) are clear and sit right under the offer.
- The "+ EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" row with a green SHOP CLOTHING button offers a second, deeper-discount path.
- The tile grid has clear labels with price anchors ("Under $100", "Under $50"). Images are strong and seasonal.
- The AnthroPerks header and "early access" banner frame the member benefit.

## What's weak

- It repeats the hero from the two earlier sends today, which is the third near-identical message in one day.
- Several CTAs sit in the hero: two gold buttons plus a green one. That splits attention.
- "Sign up & save first" in small red text is easy to miss. It is the gating action the subject tells you to take.
- The "excludes select items" and "online & in stores" fine print under the offer is tiny. The long legal block at the bottom is dense and small.
- The email is very long. Most tiles have no price, discount badge or product name, so the offer fades after the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the sign-up gate the primary action. Replace the competing buttons with a single "Join AnthroPerks free" button, then show shop buttons once the member is in.
- 2. Consolidate the hero into one CTA plus one text link, and put the exclusions in a legible line just below the offer.
- 3. Show a countdown timer or "Ends 11:59 PM" in the hero to make the deadline concrete.
- 4. Cut the tile grid to 4 or 6 modules and add a "30% off" badge on each.
- 5. Vary the creative between same-day sends so the third email feels new.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 30% off ends at midnight ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your AnthroPerks early access closes tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free, take 30% off, plus extra 40% off sale. Ends 11:59 PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated dresses, sweaters under $100, and gifts under $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup defects: a malformed Outlook font rule, quoted generic font keywords, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and missing alt text on banner images. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive. The headers weren't captured by the relay, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer and link markup.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter on the Dotomi pixel.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. Because `dtm_email_hash=` is empty, the pixel can't match the recipient. This may be an artifact of the test address or relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels are in the body.** They are `login.dotomi.com/.../px/1_0` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112126_AN-US_PromoPerksEarlyAccessBFEndsSoon_PM_Nonmember/0`. Both are `<img>` tags without alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook font rule.** The `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) makes the font-family declaration invalid.
  - The `!important` after the semicolon is orphaned, so the Outlook font override likely doesn't apply.
- **Generic fallbacks are quoted.** Examples are `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in the `[style*="Font-Normal-…"]` rules. Quoted, they are read as font names, not generic families, so the final fallback is lost.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** The tag has `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text failure on mobile.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Missing alt text on three content images.**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  All are hosted on `image.s.anthropologie.com`.
- **Tracking pixels lack `alt=""`.** The Dotomi pixel (`1_0`) and the mon-trk pixel (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`) were flagged. They should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Web fonts depend on `@import`.** The Google Fonts `@import` in `<style>` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The declared fallbacks apply there, which makes the quoted-generic issue above more visible.
- **Duplicate CSS rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (minor).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` are visible in the supplied source.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter (section 2) is the only possible personalization gap. The rest of the body was truncated and couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header was found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` matches the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com`, which is consistent with a properly configured sending subdomain, but this isn't proof.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address can't be verified.** The footer was cut off in the truncated HTML.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8430 of 11476 characters (73%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter and readability risk.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. All 44 tracking links went through click-redirect domains and weren't probed, so final landing URLs and UTM parameters are unknown.
- The pixel path `..._PromoPerksEarlyAccessBFEndsSoon_PM_Nonmember` suggests a segmented send for non-members. Whether the landing pages match that segment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty list item and the stray `;` before `!important`.
2. Use unquoted generic keywords (`sans-serif`, `serif`) in all `[style*="Font-Normal-…"]` rules.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the three content images. Add `alt=""` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
5. Populate `<title>`.
6. Re-run QA with raw `.eml` capture to confirm the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers, so the relay-stripping hypothesis can be ruled in or out.
7. Re-run with the full HTML and resolve redirects on the 44 tracked links. That would let you verify UTM parameters, landing URLs and the footer unsubscribe and address.
8. Trim the plain-text part's URL density, for example by replacing the long tracking URLs with short links or dropping non-essential links from the text version.
