# Last call: 20% off ends tonight
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer reminder. The "ends tonight" burst and the "You're the Best / 20% OFF / WITH CODE: APP20" box in the hero both signal urgency, and the maroon free-express-shipping banner above it supports the offer. It's the same APP20 creative from 2026-09-30 ("20% off exclusively in the app", 7/10) with a deadline sticker added. A subscriber who saw the first send already knows this offer.
- The hero does its job, but everything under it is a long, generic scroll of editorial tiles (September Collection, Trend Hub, Holiday Shop, Cashmere, Boots, Beauty Gift Sets) and a nine-product grid. That undercuts "last call." Nothing in the scroll is tied to the 20% code, and the grid items show no prices or discount markers.
- The fine print also does a lot of work. The offer is iOS app only, with an 11:59 PM PT deadline, and it excludes sale items, MOTHER Denim and more. The hero says "App Exclusive," but it never says the code only works in the iOS app. A mobile web shopper could click "Shop New Clothing" and hit a dead end. Verdict: solid, honest urgency, but a recycled offer in a bloated body. Expect a modest click rate.

## What's working

- The hero card has a clear hierarchy. The huge "20% OFF" in maroon serif is the first thing the eye lands on, with the code right below it.
- The "ENDS TONIGHT" circular badge adds urgency without shouting.
- Two clear CTAs ("Shop New Clothing" and "Shop New Home") are placed directly under the code, so there's no hunting.
- The free-express-shipping banner at the top stacks a second benefit on the app offer.
- The tone matches the season. Fall boots, cashmere and a Holiday Shop all feel current for early October.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or gaps.

## What's weak

- This is the same APP20 creative as 2026-09-30. Adding "ends tonight" to a repeat offer reads as manufactured urgency.
- "App exclusive" never says the code only works in the iOS app. That detail appears only in the footer fine print.
- The body is over-stuffed. Six editorial tiles and a nine-item grid dilute the one-offer message, and none of it carries a price or "20% off" marker.
- Both CTAs go to broad categories ("New Clothing", "New Home"). Neither points to a specific product or collection.
- There is no loyalty or member benefit, and no social proof such as reviews or bestseller flags.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the app requirement explicit in the hero, for example "Use code APP20 in the Anthropologie iOS app," and add a "Get the app" button. The App Store badge is currently buried at the bottom.
- 2. Cut the editorial stack to two or three tiles, or drop it. Keep the product grid but add "20% off with APP20" labels and prices.
- 3. Add a live countdown or an exact deadline ("ends 11:59 PM PT tonight") in the hero instead of only in the footer.
- 4. Point the CTAs at specific destinations, such as "Shop Cashmere" or "Shop Fall Boots," instead of "New Clothing".
- 5. Don't reuse the same creative three days later. Swap in a new hero image, or bundle the code with a product story.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code APP20 in the iOS app by 11:59 PM PT, plus free express shipping on $150+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance at 20% off new fall arrivals and the Holiday Shop.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Anthropologie, "Last call: 20% off ends tonight"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has real accessibility and CSS defects: 7 images without alt text, a malformed Outlook font rule, and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay may have stripped the headers. The QA run scored a 50% pass rate with 0 errors and 4 warnings.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **32 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 6248 of 8526 characters (73%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for the text/plain alternative, and it is probably caused by long tracked URLs.
- **Third-party tracking pixels are present.** The `login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?...` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../100326_AN-US_20OffRegPriceAppExclusiveEndsTonight_V1/...` pixels are both flagged for missing alt (see section 3).

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 `<img>` entries** (the QA output lists `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` twice each, so some are repeated placements):
  - `.../m/25/482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `.../m/25/482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `.../m/1/1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The two tracking pixels (dotomi `1_0` and mon-trk `003a700000uTzaxAAC`) should carry `alt=""`.
  - If the banners are content, a missing alt means screen readers announce the filename and image-blocked clients show nothing.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty font-family entry (`, ,`) invalidates the declaration.
  - The stray `;` before `!important` leaves a dangling token.
  - Outlook will likely ignore the intended font override.
- **Quoted generic families.** Several rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes, for example `font-family: 'Francois One', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif' !important`. Quoted, these are treated as named fonts rather than generic keywords, so the final fallback does nothing.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Comment before the doctype.** `<!-- *** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE *** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`, which can trigger quirks mode in older IE/Outlook rendering engines.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` inside `<style>`.** Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore it. It also adds a render-blocking request in clients that honor it.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (the Beholden and Terrain blocks).

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The dotomi pixel URL ends in `dtm_email_hash=` with an empty value. This is either a merge field that failed to resolve or a value the relay stripped. Either way, the visit pixel carries no identifier, so the retargeting or attribution it supports is likely broken for this copy.
- The source is truncated, so no other tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) can be assessed. No unresolved tokens are visible in the supplied portion.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed violation. If they are truly absent, the email misses the one-click unsubscribe that Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. Check the raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither appears in the truncated source, so neither can be verified here.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 32 links are tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are unknown. The mon-trk pixel path includes the campaign ID `100326_AN-US_20OffRegPriceAppExclusiveEndsTonight_V1`, which suggests the campaign is tracked at the ESP level. No evidence either way on UTM tagging.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three banner/content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
2. Fix the MSO rule: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray `;`, and make `'Font-Normal'` a real family or drop it.
3. Unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in all font stacks.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta, and add a title.
5. Move the template comment after the doctype.
6. Replace the `@import` with a `<link>` in a `@media screen` or progressive-enhancement context, or accept the system-font fallback.
7. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Capture them outside the relay.
8. Investigate why `dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the dotomi pixel.
9. Shorten the tracked URLs or improve the text/plain part, so URLs make up less than 70% of it.
10. Resolve a sample of the 32 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTMs.
