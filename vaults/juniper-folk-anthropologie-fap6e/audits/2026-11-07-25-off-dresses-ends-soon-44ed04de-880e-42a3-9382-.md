- This is a near-repeat of yesterday's "25% OFF 1000+ DRESSES" (Nov 6, 7/10). A "limited time" badge sits on top of the hero and a "ends soon!" badge was added. The subject now carries the urgency, and the body does the rest of the work.
- The hero has two offers with two SHOP NOW buttons. The large orange "25% OFF" and "50% OFF" numerals are easy to read, but the eye has no single place to land. A third offer follows straight away: "30% OFF Holiday Cheers: Hosting & Gifts." That makes three stacked percentages before any product appears. ## What's working

- The hero is offer-first, and the large orange "25% OFF" numerals are legible at a glance.
- The "ends soon!" and "limited time" badges add urgency at the top of the email.
- The category tiles, such as Holiday Party Shop and Cashmere Shop, tie the email to the current season.
- The editorial photography is on-brand and consistent with prior sends.
- The footer has useful secondary links: All-New Arrivals, E-Gift Card, TikTok and Pick Up in Store.

## What's weak

- The hero carries two competing offers and two buttons, and a third banner follows right after. That dilutes the dress message in the subject line.
- "Ends soon" has no date or time, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The grid under "We picked these just for you" shows no dresses. It shows jeans, sweaters and cardigans, so the dress offer is never backed by product.
- None of the products show prices, discounted prices or reviews beyond a small "Top Rated" tag.
- The fine print uses asterisks and is tiny. The "excludes select brands" and "online & in stores" caveats are buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one button. Put dresses in the hero and move the Extra 50% off sale and the 30% off hosting banner further down, or send them separately.
- 2. Replace the "Just for you" grid with 6 to 9 dresses showing sale prices and a "25% off" tag, so the offer has product behind it.
- 3. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59pm PT" or "Ends tonight," instead of "ends soon."
- 4. Reduce the cadence of near-identical sends. After Nov 5 and Nov 6, a third send in a row with the same hero needs a new angle, such as a styled dress lookbook or a size-run alert.
- 5. Show the exclusions near the hero, such as "Select dresses, online and in stores," so the offer reads as honest.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **40 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Blank `dtm_email_hash` in the Dotomi pixel.** The pixel URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. - **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 7,718 of 10,982 characters (70%) are URLs. This is the QA warning, and it is consistent with long tracking links in the text version.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images without alt text (5):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - Dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/…` pixel

  The two pixels should carry `alt=""` and be hidden. The three hosted images need meaningful alt text, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. I can't tell from the QA data whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Malformed Outlook rule.** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has an empty family entry. The stray `;` before `!important` also makes the declaration invalid, so Outlook may ignore the font override.
- **Quoted generic fallbacks.** Declarations like `font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif'` quote the generic keyword, so it is treated as a font named "sans-serif" and not the generic family. The same applies to `'serif'`.
- **`@import` for Google Fonts.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?…')` inside `<style>` is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. It can delay rendering in clients that do support it.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
  - A comment precedes the `<!DOCTYPE>`, which puts older Outlook and IE engines in quirks mode.
  - `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice.
  - `.yfix{}` is declared empty three times.
