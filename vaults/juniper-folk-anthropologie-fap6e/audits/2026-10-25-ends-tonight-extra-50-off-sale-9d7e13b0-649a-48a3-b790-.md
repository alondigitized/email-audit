# Ends tonight: extra 50% off sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the "ends tonight" payoff for a sale Anthropologie has been pushing all week. The lavender hero is clear: "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" with a SHOP NOW button and a small "ends tonight!" badge. Six color-blocked category tiles sit right under it, so the offer and the way in are obvious within one scroll. It's competent and on-brand.
- The weak spot is the fatigue. Subscribers got "Extra 50% off sale is live. Go." on Oct 23, "Join AnthroPerks for 20% off" on Oct 24, and "20% off ends tonight, FYI" earlier today. This is the second "ends tonight" for overlapping offers in one day. The AnthroPerks 20% strip below the hero competes with the 50% hero and dilutes the deadline. The editorial grid below is pleasant but pushes the sale out of focus.
- Verdict: a clean send with a credible deadline, but it lands in a crowded week and splits attention between two offers.

## What's working

- The lavender hero puts "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" front and center with one SHOP NOW button.
- The "ends tonight!" badge gives a clear deadline in the hero.
- The six color-blocked tiles (Clothing, Home, Accessories, Shoes, Dresses, Kitchen & Dining) give fast category entry points.
- The editorial "More to Explore" grid (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Decor, Holiday Glam) keeps the brand's lifestyle voice and suits the season.
- The "Even more to love" block surfaces reviews-for-25%-off, Afterpay and store locator.

## What's weak

- The AnthroPerks "20% OFF YOUR PURCHASE" strip competes with the 50% hero. It carries its own "ends tonight" badge, and the stacked discounts are confusing.
- The deadline has no hour or timezone. "Tonight" is vague.
- The page is very long. The sale links are buried under roughly six editorial tiles and a 9-image product grid.
- The "More with your name on it" grid has no visible prices or labels, so there's no product detail or social proof to click on.
- The dense asterisk fine print at the bottom hides the exclusions behind the 50% claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the 20% AnthroPerks strip from this send, or merge it into the hero as one line. A last-day email should have a single offer.
- 2. Put the exact deadline in the hero, such as "Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight," with a countdown if possible.
- 3. Add prices or "now $X" labels to the product grid, and a top-rated badge on a few items.
- 4. Cut the editorial tiles to two or three, and put the product grid higher.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific, e.g. "Shop 50% off dresses".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, sweaters and home picks, extra 50% off through 11:59 PM PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an ExactTarget-style template (Anthropologie/URBN) with no QA failures, but the source has malformed CSS, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, missing `alt` attributes on 6 images, and a subscriber ID in a tracking-pixel path. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML was truncated, so body-level findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 47 links were skipped as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter in a pixel URL:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. Either the value wasn't populated at send time, or it was stripped on capture. If it's not populated, the Dotomi (Epsilon) retargeting pixel can't match the recipient.
- **Subscriber identifier in a pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../102526_AN-US_50OffSOSEndsTonight/003a700000uTzaxAAC`. The trailing `003a7…AAC` follows the Salesforce Contact ID format (18 characters, `003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party request URL.
- **Third-party pixels:** the dotomi and mon-trk pixels are both `<img>` beacons from external domains.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 4 content images** (all at `image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/fe3e11717164057c701470/m/…`):
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `Evergreen-Email-Module1_04.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With images blocked, these render as blank space for screen-reader and image-off users.
- **Missing `alt` on 2 tracking pixels** (`dotomi …/px/1_0`, `mon-trk …/003a700000uTzaxAAC`). They should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none`, so they aren't announced.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has an empty list item (`, ,`) and a stray `; !important;`. The declaration is invalid and the Outlook font override may be dropped.
- **Quoted generic font keywords:** `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` appear in quotes in the `Font-Normal-*` rules (e.g. `font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif' !important`). Quoted, they are treated as a font family name, not the generic fallback.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. That can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **`<title></title>` is empty,** and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (only `xmlns` declarations).
- **Google Fonts `@import`:** Gmail and some other clients ignore it. This is acceptable only because fallbacks exist, and they are partly broken by the quoting issue above.
- **Minor CSS hygiene:** the `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is declared twice, and `.yfix{}` is declared empty three times.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved merge field (probable):** the empty `dtm_email_hash=` in the Dotomi pixel is the only token-like gap in the visible source.
- **No other issues in the visible HTML:** no raw `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` strings appeared. The rest of the body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note says the AgentMail relay may be dropping them. Neither presence nor absence is confirmed, so treat this as unverified, not a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain, so alignment would need checking from raw headers.
- **Body footer not verifiable:** the visible HTML doesn't reach the footer. The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable:** all 47 links route through click-tracking redirects and weren't resolved, so UTM presence and consistency are unknown.
- **Campaign naming is consistent:** the mon-trk path `102526_AN-US_50OffSOSEndsTonight` matches the subject, "Ends tonight: extra 50% off sale", and the send date (10/25/26).
- **Landing-page alignment not checked,** for the same reason (links skipped).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images. Set `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions or `display:none` on the two tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;` with no empty entry and no stray `!important;`. Unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in the `Font-Normal-*` rules.
4. Confirm that `dtm_email_hash` is populated at send time. If it's blank by design, remove the parameter.
5. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` path needs to be exposed, or whether an opaque token would do.
6. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE, fill in `<title>`, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the raw message in a mailbox that preserves headers (e.g. Gmail "Show original"), not through the relay.
8. Resolve a sample of the 47 tracking links with a redirect follower to confirm UTMs and landing-page status. Also capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and merge tokens.
