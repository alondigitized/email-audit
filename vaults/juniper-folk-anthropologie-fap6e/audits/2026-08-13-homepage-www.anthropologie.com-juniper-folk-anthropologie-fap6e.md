# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a denim-and-basics blitz, not the boho-dress wonderland I come to Anthro for — the hero is all cropped tanks and stacked jeans pockets, with a blanket "30% Off" banner up top. It's clean and pretty in that editorial Anthro way, but nothing here is whispering "patterned midi dress for a backyard wedding" at me, which is usually why I'm here.

## What's working

- The top banner's 30% Off is impossible to miss — big serif type, olive "Shop Now" button, good contrast.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $50+" sits right under the header where I'd actually notice it before scrolling.
- The nav bar has "Dresses" one tap away, which is the one thing keeping me from bouncing immediately.
- The "24 Days Of Beauty Advent Calendar" strip with the pink "Shop Fast (it sells out)" pill is a fun little urgency nudge — I'm a sucker for beauty gifting sets.

## What's weak

- Zero dresses anywhere above the fold — it's jeans, a cropped blouse, and a denim-on-denim moment. For someone who lives in patterned dresses, this hero just isn't for me.
- That cookie banner ("We use cookies. Learn more.") is sitting right on top of the "shop je[an]" label, clipping the text — feels sloppy for a brand this polished.
- No AnthroPerks or rewards callout anywhere I can see — just a plain "Sign In / Sign Up," so as a member I get zero acknowledgment.
- "24 Days Of Beauty Advent Calendar" reads like Christmas content showing up in mid-August — either stale or very oddly timed for the season.
- The 30% off has a little dagger symbol next to it with no visible fine print — makes me wonder what's excluded before I even click.

## Recommendations

- Swap the denim/crop-top hero for a lifestyle shot of someone in a flowy patterned dress — give me a reason to stop scrolling.
- Add an AnthroPerks member-pricing badge or points balance near the sign-in link so returning members feel recognized.
- Fix the cookie banner z-index so it doesn't clip the "shop jeans" label underneath it.
- Either pull the holiday advent calendar strip until closer to the season or clearly relabel it as an early-access preview.
