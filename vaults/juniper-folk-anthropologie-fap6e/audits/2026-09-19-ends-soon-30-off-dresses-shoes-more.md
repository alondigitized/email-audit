# Ends Soon: 30% OFF dresses, shoes & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Maeve — Anthropologie's younger sub-line — running the same "30% Off" discount play the parent brand has now run repeatedly (2026-09-17 "30% OFF Fall Looks," 6.00/10), except this time the urgency ("Ends Soon") lives only in the subject line and never shows up in the actual creative. The email opens with four full-screen lifestyle/editorial shots — bookstore, brownstone stoop, varsity-prep styling — before the offer banner finally appears roughly 40% down the scroll. That's a lot of scrolling before the thing the subject promised.
- The offer itself, when it arrives, is generic: "30% OFF Fall Looks · online only," not "30% off dresses & shoes" as the subject specifically calls out. So a reader who opens because of "dresses, shoes & more" hits four screens of sweaters and totes before finding a banner that doesn't even name those categories. The category grid below (Dresses, Pants, Heritage Prep, Shoes) is clean and browsable, but it's link-to-category, not link-to-product, and there's no countdown or date backing up "Ends Soon."
- Net: solid photography, on-brand voice, functional layout — but a subject/body mismatch and a buried CTA drag it down. This lands in the same 5-6 range as the brand's other discount sends this cycle.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent Maeve wordmark treatment.
- Editorial photography (bookstore hero, stoop shot) is well-art-directed and on-brand for the varsity/prep positioning.
- Category grid ("Maeve Dresses," "Maeve Pants," "Maeve Shoes") gives clear browse paths post-offer.
- "Handpicked for you" curated grid at the bottom is a genuine personalization touch.
- Single unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button on the offer banner.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "dresses, shoes & more" but the visible offer banner just says "30% OFF Fall Looks" — no dresses/shoes callout in the creative itself.
- "Ends Soon" urgency from the subject is completely absent from the body — no countdown, no date, no badge.
- The actual discount banner doesn't appear until well past the fold — four editorial images run before the offer, burying the hero.
- This is visually the same "30% OFF Fall Looks" banner and layout the brand used 2026-09-17, just two days later — repeat creative, not a fresh send.
- No loyalty/member pricing or perk shown despite this being a repeat-purchase-driving lifecycle brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off banner to the very top, above the editorial lead images — readers who open on "Ends Soon: 30% off" should see the offer immediately, not after four scrolls.
- 2. Make the banner copy match the subject: swap "30% OFF Fall Looks" for "30% OFF Dresses & Shoes" so the promise is kept end to end.
- 3. Add a visible deadline (date or countdown) to the banner if "Ends Soon" is going to be the subject hook — right now it's an unsupported claim.
- 4. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTAs ("Shop Dresses," "Shop Shoes") tied to the subject's named categories.
- 5. Since this creative is a near-repeat of 2026-09-17, either meaningfully refresh the imagery or space out discount sends further to avoid promo fatigue.
