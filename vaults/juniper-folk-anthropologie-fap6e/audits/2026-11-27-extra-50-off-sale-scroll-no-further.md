# EXTRA 50% OFF SALE. Scroll no further 🛑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the twenty-third Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days, and it is the same plaid-edged gift-tag hero as the 11/27 "40% OFF everyone's favorites: Aero Pant, Astrid Sweater & MORE" email. The hero stacks three tiers: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE with a "Shop Now" button, 40% OFF OUR MOST-LOVED STYLES, and 30% OFF ALMOST EVERYTHING ELSE with three shop buttons. The offer is clear and the brand is instantly recognizable. Stacking three tiers is also confusing, because the eye has to work out which discount applies to what.
- "Scroll no further 🛑" promises a single answer, but the body then runs a long scroll of eight category tiles and a "Might we recommend…" grid. The subject and the body disagree. After four days of near-identical sends, the creative is wallpaper, and the only fresh element is the subject's cheeky stop-sign.
- Judgement: a competent, well-built send that is hurt by cadence fatigue and a cluttered offer hierarchy. It's a solid 7. The hero does its job, but nothing here would make a subscriber who ignored the last twenty-two sends act on this one.

## What's working

- The hero shows three discounts in large serif type on a gift-tag shape. The brand look is unmistakable.
- A "Shop Now" button sits directly under the 50% headline, so the top offer has a visible CTA.
- The 30% tier has separate buttons for Clothing, Home & Furniture and Gifts. That gives category-level routing.
- The two-up category tiles (dresses, kitchen, shoes, jewelry, holiday decor) are well shot and tie into the holiday season.
- The red category labels under each image are consistent and scannable.

## What's weak

- The three stacked tiers (50/40/30) compete. "Extra 50% off sale" and "40% off most-loved" sit side by side with asterisks, and the exclusions only appear in small print ("excludes select items").
- The subject says "Scroll no further," but the email is very long, with eight tile rows, a recommendations grid and a footer.
- The top banner text reads cut off at both edges ("HE YEAR JUST GOT BETTER" / "THE BEST DEALS OF THE YE"). That looks like a render clip.
- The hero copy is almost identical to the previous sends. Repeating it for the twenty-third time gives no new urgency, and the email shows no deadline.
- The "Might we recommend…" grid has no prices or CTA copy, only "Top Rated" tags. The legal footer is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one dominant offer: the 50% off sale. Move the 40% and 30% tiers below as a secondary strip. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight 11:59 PT". Time-bounded urgency is missing despite the Black Friday context.
- 3. Make the subject honest: either shorten the email to match "scroll no further" or drop that line.
- 4. Fix the clipped marquee text at the top edge.
- 5. Add prices and "Shop [item]" copy in the recommendations grid, and use fewer category tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 50% off sale, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 50% off sale 🛑 ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off most-loved styles and 30% off almost everything else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dresses, home, gifts and more. Black Friday pricing ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie "EXTRA 50% OFF SALE. Scroll no further 🛑"

The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head/CSS and the automated QA output. The body, footer and link `href`s were not available to inspect.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email comes from a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/...`, `pxl.mon-trk.com`) with no QA errors. It has several confirmed code-level defects: an invalid Outlook font rule, a zoom-blocking viewport, missing alt text, and an empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter. Header-based checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be evaluated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **45 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chain and expiry are unverified.
- **Empty tracking parameter:** the Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` is blank, so the pixel cannot associate the open or visit with a subscriber. This may be an artifact of the test inbox, but it is not populated in this render.
- **Third-party pixels:** two are present, `login.dotomi.com` (Conversant) and `pxl.mon-trk.com`. The second carries a Salesforce contact ID in the path (`.../003a700000uTzaxAA...`). Both are extra third-party requests that Apple MPP and image-blocking clients will suppress or pre-fetch, which skews open data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) is a syntax error.
  - The stray `;` before `!important` invalidates the declaration, so Outlook will not get the intended Arial override.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on supporting clients.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Missing alt text, content images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - With images off or a screen reader, these render as nothing or as a filename.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the Dotomi and mon-trk images also lack `alt`. Use `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden`) so they are skipped by assistive tech.
- **Web font reliance:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` loads 8 font families.
  - Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import` and fall back to the listed stacks.
  - The shared CSS includes other brands' classes (UO, FP, Beholden, Terrain) that this email does not need.
  - The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is defined twice.
  - These add weight but cause no functional breakage.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 8663 of 11539 characters (75%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The text alternative is mostly raw tracking URLs and offers little readable content.

## 3a. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source or flagged by QA.
- `dtm_email_hash=` is empty (see §2). It is the only sign of a personalization value that failed to populate.
- The remaining body could not be checked for greeting or name tokens.

## 4. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume. Verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay and is not a finding.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source, so not assessed.
- **From domain:** `s.anthropologie.com` matches the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com`, so no mismatch is evident.

## 5. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or destination URLs were available. The 45 links were skipped and the source is truncated, so UTM completeness, landing-page alignment and promo-code carry-through cannot be assessed.
- The mon-trk pixel path includes a campaign slug (`112726_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayReminder2_PM`), which indicates a campaign ID is set.

## 6. Recommendations
1. **Fix the MSO CSS.** Use `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;` with no empty entry and no stray `;`.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.**
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the three content images. Add `alt=""` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
4. **Populate `<title>`.**
5. **Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=`** in the Dotomi pixel. Confirm whether it is populated in production sends or whether the merge is failing.
6. **Check the raw message source** for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay may have hidden them.
7. **Improve the plain-text part** with readable link labels instead of raw tracking URLs, to bring the URL share under 70%.
8. **Trim the shared CSS** to the Anthropologie fonts only, and remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
9. **Re-run QA against the full, untruncated HTML and a live link probe.** This is needed to check UTM parameters, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and landing-page continuity.
