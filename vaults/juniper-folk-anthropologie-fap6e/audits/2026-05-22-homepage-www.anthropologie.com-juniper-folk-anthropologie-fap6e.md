# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Anthropologie expecting dreamy editorial content, maybe a summer dress campaign or a registry push — and instead I got a bot-block wall. The entire homepage is a plain white access-restriction screen. There is literally nothing here for me to shop, browse, or feel anything about. This isn't a brand experience; it's a locked door.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark renders cleanly at the top — I at least know where I landed.
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link is present, so there is technically an escape hatch.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage content is blocked — I see zero product, zero imagery, zero offer, zero navigation.
- The block message is cold and clinical ("We detected unusual activity from your device or network") — completely off-brand for a label that sells warmth and aesthetic.
- The error lists "rapid taps or clicks" as a reason, which punishes normal mobile browsing behavior, especially for a phone shopper like me who scrolls fast.
- IP address 75.140.9.0 is exposed in plain text on the page — unnecessary friction and slightly unnerving for a casual shopper.
- No retry button, no redirect to a CAPTCHA, no graceful fallback — just a dead end.

## Recommendations

- Add a graceful CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" challenge instead of a hard block — don't lock out real mobile shoppers who scroll quickly.
- If the block must happen, skin the error page on-brand: soft background, Anthro-style serif copy, and a "Try again" button that leads back to the homepage.
- Remove the exposed IP address from the user-facing message — it adds friction and confusion without helping anyone.
- Add a direct link back to the homepage or a category shortcut so I'm not just stranded on a dead-end screen.
