# Ends soon! 30% off clothing & accessories
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Fashion Weekend / Feels Like Fall 30% off promo Anthropologie has sent all week, now with "Ends soon!" urgency in the subject. The hero is clear. A large orange-red "30% OFF*" sits over "Clothing & Accessories" with a lime "SHOP NOW" button, and a second blue "30% OFF**" block for Fall Entertaining, Candles & Decor sits below it. The offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog. Six editorial tiles (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets), a nine-product grid, and a footer link grid all compete for attention. Only the two hero buttons are explicit CTAs. The rest are captions with no price or reason to click.
- Verdict: a solid, familiar send, but "Ends soon!" has no date or time behind it. Coming after the Oct 16 "Enjoy 30% off dresses, sweaters, shoes" and Oct 15 "NOW: 30% off clothing & accessories" emails, the urgency isn't credible. It's a good email that is probably running into fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero offer is dominant: the big "30% OFF*" in orange-red with a lime button has strong contrast and clear hierarchy.
- The two offers are separated by category and by "online only" versus "online & in stores", so the scope is clear.
- The editorial tiles are on-season (Holiday Party, Cashmere, Mantel Décor, Beauty Gift Sets).
- The footer's "Pay Later w/ Klarna" and "Join AnthroPerks" give a loyalty and affordability nudge.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Ends soon!" gives no date or time, and the body never says when the sale ends.
- The "MORE TO EXPLORE" tiles have captions only. No tile carries a price, a discount or a verb.
- The nine-item "Might we recommend…" grid has no names, prices or sale markers, so the 30% off isn't reinforced at the product level.
- The asterisks and fine print sit at the very bottom in tiny type. Exclusions such as select brands and gift cards are buried.
- This is the same creative as earlier in the week, so it feels like a repeat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends tonight at midnight PT", and match the subject to it. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Add sale prices (for example "$89 $62") and a short name to each product in the grid.
- 3. Turn the editorial captions into verb-led CTAs, such as "Shop cashmere" or "Shop party looks".
- 4. Move a short exclusions line directly under the hero asterisk.
- 5. Cut the cadence, or swap in fresh creative on a last-chance send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 30% off, plus candles & décor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at midnight. Cashmere, party looks and mantel décor included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off fall fashion and home. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The Anthropologie "30% off" email is a shared URBN template with 36 click-tracked links and no hard errors. The gaps are missing alt text on 7 image instances, a malformed Outlook font stack, and List-Unsubscribe headers and authentication results that the AgentMail relay didn't capture, so they can't be confirmed either way.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 36 tracking links were skipped for HTTP probing because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and expiry are **unverified**, not shown to be broken.
- `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` is a Dotomi (Epsilon) tracking pixel. Its `dtm_email_hash=` parameter is **empty**, so the pixel carries no identifier. It is either an unresolved merge field or an intentional blank, and the truncated source doesn't show which.
- `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../101726_AN-US_30OffFashion30OffHomeComboEndsSoon/003a700000u...` is an open-tracking pixel (alt text `003a700000uTzaxAAC`). The `003a…` string looks like a Salesforce Contact ID, so the pixel URL carries a subscriber record ID.
- The plain-text part is 7008 of 9527 characters URLs (about 74%), which is above the 70% warning threshold. This is mostly caused by the long tracking URLs.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text** on 7 image instances, 5 of them distinct images:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the two tracking pixels above

  The tracking pixels should have `alt=""`. The banner images and the PNG may be decorative, but they still need an explicit `alt=""`. If any carry content, they need descriptive alt text. The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Invalid Outlook font stack:** `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` inside `<!--[if mso]>` has two defects.
  - The empty list item (`Helvetica, ,`) is invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` makes the declaration invalid, so the Outlook override is ignored.

  It appears to come from an unfilled template variable (see the next section).
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` leaves nothing for screen readers or clients that surface titles.
- **Web-font `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` loads 8 font families. Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore it, and the fallback stacks cover those clients. It is a weight and latency cost where it is supported.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Brand CSS in every send:** The stylesheet includes UO, Free People, Beholden and Terrain classes in an Anthropologie send. This is dead CSS and adds weight.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The empty slot in `Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'` (the template comment says the font should be injected there) and the empty `dtm_email_hash=` are signs of **unresolved template variables** that rendered as blank strings. No literal `%%token%%` or `{{ }}` strings appear in the truncated source, but the HTML was cut off, so personalization in the body wasn't checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were **not found**. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require both, so check them against the raw message.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a sending subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- The truncated HTML doesn't let me confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, which are required under CAN-SPAM. Check the footer.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Every destination sits behind a redirect, so UTM parameters, landing URLs and promo-code carryover couldn't be inspected.
- The tracking token `101726_AN-US_30OffFashion30OffHomeComboEndsSoon` carries a campaign name that mentions **"Home"** (`30OffHomeCombo`). The subject says only "clothing & accessories". That mismatch is in the campaign naming and doesn't prove a landing-page problem, but check the landing pages.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and the decorative banner and PNG images. Add descriptive alt text to any image that carries content.
2. Fix the MSO font declaration to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;` with no stray `;` before `!important`, and confirm the template variable is populated.
3. Fill in `<title>` and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0`.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the relay-caused warnings as defects until then.
5. Find out why `dtm_email_hash=` is empty. If it is meant to carry a hash, the Dotomi pixel is unusable for audience matching.
6. Follow a sample of the 36 tracking links manually to verify destinations, UTM or campaign parameters, and the offer's carryover.
7. Consider a shorter plain-text version, since the long tracking URLs push its URL ratio to about 74%.
