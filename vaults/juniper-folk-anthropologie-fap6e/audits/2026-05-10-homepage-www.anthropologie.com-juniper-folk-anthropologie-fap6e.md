# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Anthro's homepage immediately feels like mine — the boho-outdoor imagery, the earthy palette, the butterfly details. They're clearly in Mother's Day mode right now, pushing a "UP TO 30% OFF" moment with an "ends tonight" urgency nudge, and the categories they're discounting (accessories, sleepwear, candles) are basically a checklist of my cart. This homepage is doing a lot right for someone like me, but it's leaving conversion money on the table by hiding trust signals and the free-shipping threshold.

## What's working

- **"Accessories, Sleepwear, Candles & More"** — literally three of my favorite Anthro categories in one line. The fact that candles are called out specifically is almost comically on-target for me.
- **"Ends tonight!" circle badge** — creates real urgency without feeling manipulative. I believe it.
- **The two lifestyle image tiles ("New!" and "Top Rated")** — both feature women with wavy/curly hair in outdoor settings. The right tile especially (denim jacket, swing, water backdrop) looks like a mood board I'd save.
- **Mother's Day e-gift card banner** — soft, relevant, the copy "What Mom really wants? Options." is casual and smart. As a 33-year-old who's both a potential gift-giver and gift-receiver, that lands.
- **"JUST FOR YOU" framing** — even if it's not actually personalized, it reads like Anthro is talking to me, not shouting at the internet.

## What's weak

- **No AnthroPerks callout anywhere** — I'm a member and I earn points here constantly. Showing me my rewards balance or a "Members get extra 10%" nudge would tip me to buy immediately instead of maybe-later.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague** — shop what, exactly? The button links to the sale, but the copy doesn't tell me I'm about to browse candles or dresses. "Shop Candles + More" would keep me more excited.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always want to know if I'm close to free shipping before I start building a cart. That info is missing above the fold.
- **Cookie banner is blocking the bottom tiles** — the "We use cookies. Learn more." bar covers what looks like another lifestyle section. Minor, but it visually cuts off the scroll momentum.
- **No social proof visible** — no star ratings, no "X reviews," no "bestseller" badge on the products in the image tiles. For a brand with strong community, that's a missed signal.

## Recommendations

- **Add an AnthroPerks balance chip to the nav** — right next to the bag icon, show "Points: 320" or "Your discount: 15%" for logged-in members. One line of personalization that dramatically increases urgency.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Candles, Accessories + More"** — specificity reduces hesitation. I want to know where the tap is taking me before I commit.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in or below the promo block** — something like "Free shipping on orders $50+" would slot cleanly under the "SHOP NOW" button and increase AOV.
- **Add a star-rating overlay to the "Top Rated" tile** — if it's top-rated, show me the number (e.g., "4.8 ★ · 2,400 reviews"). That's the signal that makes me tap vs. scroll past.
