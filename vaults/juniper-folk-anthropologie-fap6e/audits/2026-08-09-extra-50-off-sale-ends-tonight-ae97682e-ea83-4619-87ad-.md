# Extra 50% OFF sale ends tonight!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's biggest markdown of the stretch — "Extra 50% Off Sale ends tonight!" — one-upping the August 7 "Extra 40% Off Sale is LIVE" by ten points of discount and swapping "LIVE" urgency for hard-deadline "ends tonight" urgency. The hero is clean and unmissable: a "ends tonight!" badge, "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" in oversized type, and a lavender-and-blush color block that reads instantly on open. The category-tile grid right below (Clothing, Home, Accessories, Shoes, Dresses, Kitchen & Dining) is a smart touch — it turns a generic sitewide sale into something that lets the eye jump straight to a category of interest.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email tacks on a second, unrelated "Up to 30% OFF furniture, décor & Halloween" promo directly under the 50%-off hero, then a long scroll of undifferentiated editorial tiles (Trend Hub, Colored & Washed Collection, Going Analog, Sweater Dresses, Suede Shop, Lip Edit, a 9-grid "more with your name on it") with zero connective tissue back to the sale. It's the same structural problem as the August 6 "Up to 30% OFF" send — a strong hero buried under scroll — except here the hero is genuinely the strongest of the recent history, so the waste is more costly. Verdict: excellent top-of-funnel, undisciplined everywhere else — an 8, capped by the bloated back half and the confusing dual-offer stacking.

## What's working

- Hero is instantly legible: "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" in huge type against a lavender block, no scrolling needed to get the offer.
- "ends tonight!" badge creates credible, specific urgency (paired with subject line, reinforcing not repeating).
- Bold "SHOP NOW" button is high-contrast and sits directly under the offer.
- Category tile grid (Sale Clothing/Home/Accessories/Shoes/Dresses/Kitchen & Dining) lets the reader self-select instead of hitting one generic CTA.
- No render bugs — all images load, text is crisp, no overlap.

## What's weak

- A second, competing sale ("Up to 30% OFF furniture, décor & Halloween") appears immediately below the 50% hero, which dilutes and confuses the primary offer.
- The email is extremely long — 8+ editorial sections (Trend Hub, Colored & Washed, Going Analog, Sweater Dresses, Suede Shop, Lip Edit, a 9-image grid) with no pricing or sale tie-in, well past where most subscribers stop scrolling.
- Section headers like "Going Analog," "The Trend Hub," and "More With Your Name On It" give no indication of what's inside — pure navigation friction.
- No countdown timer or specific end time next to "ends tonight," which is a missed chance to sharpen the deadline shown in the hero.
- The fine print at the very bottom ("*Extra 50% off sale...") is the only place exclusions live — nothing in the hero signals restrictions, which risks a bait-and-switch feel at checkout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the category tile grid and the fine print — move the 30%-off furniture sale and all editorial content to a separate send. One offer per email will roughly double effective CTR on the primary "SHOP NOW."
- 2. If the furniture sale must stay, move it to a clearly separate "Also On Sale" module further down, not directly under the hero where it competes visually.
- 3. Add a real countdown timer or explicit time ("Ends tonight at midnight ET") next to the badge — "tonight" alone is vague and easy to dismiss.
- 4. Rename vague section headers to outcome-driven copy, e.g. "Suede Shop" → "Suede Shoes, 50% Off," so the sale carries through the scroll instead of vanishing after the hero.
- 5. Surface a one-line exclusion note near the hero (e.g., "some exclusions apply") instead of only in bottom-of-email fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off — ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: Extra 50% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clothing, home, shoes & more — all included, tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Extra 50% off ends at midnight ET.`
