# It’s here: The Beauty Advent Calendar
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's holiday-adjacent beauty play — "24 Days of Beauty Advent Calendar is Back" — a strong seasonal hook in August, positioned as a "sign up for early access" waitlist rather than a straight sale. It's a genre-switch from the discount-heavy run this brand has been on (August 9's "Extra 50% Off," August 7's "Extra 40% Off"): no percent-off mechanic here, just a $300-value anchor and 48-hr AnthroPerks early access, which reads as a smart way to build waitlist urgency without discounting.
- The hero illustration (a pink dollhouse-style advent calendar in snow) is genuinely eye-catching and on-brand, and the deep plum/night-sky background makes the calendar pop. But the email quickly reverts to Anthropologie's generic "shop the grid" filler — nine near-identical product tiles under "More With Your Name On It," then six more single-image category blocks ("The Trend Hub," "Suede Shop," "The Lip Edit," etc.) with zero copy differentiation. It's a strong open, weak follow-through.
- Judgement: a good subject/hero combo dragged down by a long, undifferentiated scroll — solid but not a top-tier send for this brand.

## What's working

- Hero art (pink advent-calendar dollhouse) is distinctive, seasonal, and stands out from the brand's usual flat-lay/model photography.
- "$300 VALUE!" badge gives a concrete, quantified hook even without a percent-off.
- "48-hr Early Access" + AnthroPerks framing creates a clear reason to act now that isn't just "SALE."
- Single unambiguous CTA button ("SIGN UP & BE THE FIRST TO SHOP") sits right below the hero with no scrolling needed on mobile-height crops.
- No render bugs — every tile and image loads cleanly, text is legible against the dark hero background.

## What's weak

- The email is a beauty-calendar hook wrapped in nine unrelated apparel product photos and six unrelated category tiles — very little of the send actually reinforces the beauty story after the fold.
- "More With Your Name On It" and "More To Explore" sections have zero product names, prices, or copy — just image + tiny caption, so there's nothing to evaluate for fit/size/price before clicking.
- Featured brands line ("Featuring Phlur, L'Occitane & more") is small, plain text — buried instead of used as a second hook.
- No countdown or date for the calendar's actual on-sale day — "48-hr Early Access" to what, starting when, isn't stated anywhere visible.
- Bottom promo band ("Recycle Denim & Get $20 Off," "Pay Later w/ Klarna") competes with the primary beauty message and dilutes focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or drastically shrink the nine-tile apparel grid and six category blocks — replace with 3-4 tiles previewing actual beauty items/brands inside the calendar (Phlur, L'Occitane, etc.) with names and prices, so the body reinforces the hero instead of pivoting to unrelated apparel.
- 2. Add a specific date/time for early access ("Early access opens Aug 12, 10am ET") — right now "48-hr Early Access" has no anchor point, which kills urgency.
- 3. Promote "Featuring Phlur, L'Occitane & more" into a larger, bolded line right under the hero — brand names are a stronger click driver for beauty shoppers than the generic tagline above it.
- 4. Move the Klarna/denim-recycling band to a separate send — it's off-message here and competes with the calendar CTA.
- 5. Add a small "Notify me" / waitlist counter or social proof line ("Sold out in 3 days last year") to justify the early-access urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `24 Days of Beauty, $300 Value — Early Access Starts Now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Beauty Advent Calendar Is Back (Sign Up First)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Phlur, L'Occitane & more — 48-hr AnthroPerks early access before it sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last year's calendar sold out fast. Get first dibs, members only.`
