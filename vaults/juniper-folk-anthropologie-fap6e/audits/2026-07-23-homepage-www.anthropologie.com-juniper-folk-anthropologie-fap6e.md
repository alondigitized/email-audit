# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Anthropologie literally blocked me from seeing their homepage — I got a bot-detection wall instead. A slider CAPTCHA with "Slide right to secure your access" is the first thing I see. The entire shopping experience is dead on arrival. There's no hero, no product, no vibe — just a paranoid verification gate and a cookie banner.

## What's working

- The ANTHROPOLOGIE wordmark is clean and legible at the top — brand recognition is the one thing this page delivers.
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link at the bottom at least acknowledges there's a problem to report.

## What's weak

- **The CAPTCHA wall itself** — a slider verification gate blocks 100% of the shopping experience. I see zero product, zero campaign, zero reason to stay.
- **No fallback content** — even behind a bot check, there's nothing here: no product hint, no brand message, no navigation.
- **Cookie consent banner stacked on top of an already broken page** — two friction layers before I've seen a single dress.
- **Paranoid messaging** — listing reasons why my device was flagged ("Rapid taps or clicks," "Use of developer or inspection tools") is accusatory and alienating. I just tapped the app like a normal person.
- **No escape route** — no "Shop anyway," no nav, no search bar. Dead end.

## Recommendations

- **Tune bot-detection thresholds** — legitimate mobile app users on shared IPs shouldn't be hitting this wall; tighten the signal triggers so real shoppers aren't caught.
- **Add a grace-period landing strip** — if verification is required, show a thin brand banner with the current hero offer above the CAPTCHA so I at least know what I'm unlocking.
- **Replace the accusatory reasons list** with a single friendly line: "Just making sure you're you — this takes two seconds."
- **Surface a static category nav link** below the slider so I can at least tap into Dresses or New Arrivals without waiting for the full page load.
