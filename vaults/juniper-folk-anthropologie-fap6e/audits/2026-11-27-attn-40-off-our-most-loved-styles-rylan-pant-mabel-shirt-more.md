# 🚨 ATTN: 40% OFF our most-loved styles: Rylan Pant, Mabel Shirt & MORE
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the twenty-first Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days. It keeps the plaid-edged gift-tag hero from the 11/26 "30% OFF, just in time for dessert 🍰" email. This time the hero stacks three tiers: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE, 40% OFF "our most-loved styles," and 30% OFF almost everything else. The subject only sells the middle tier, so a recipient who clicks through finds a bigger 50% headline than the one that earned the open.
- The body is a clean, scannable grid of category tiles with red label bars: sweaters, holiday decor, shoes, furniture, beauty. It ends with a "Might we recommend" product grid. The visual rhythm is strong and the brand voice is steady. But the email asks the reader to decode three discount tiers with asterisks before choosing anything. Rylan Pant and Mabel Shirt are named in the subject and never appear by name in the render.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand holiday send. The subject and hero don't match, and by the twenty-first nearly identical promo the cadence is fatiguing.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and seasonal. The gift-tag motif, plaid edging and red, pink and green palette make it unmistakably Black Friday.
- The three-tier stack gives each offer its own colour block and its own buttons (Shop Now, Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts).
- The category tiles are well photographed, with consistent red label bars. They are easy to scan and cover home, apparel and beauty.
- "Might we recommend…" gives a product-level fallback for readers who scrolled past the tiles.
- The top banner, "The year just got better · The best deals of the year," frames the urgency.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "40% OFF our most-loved styles: Rylan Pant, Mabel Shirt & MORE," but the hero leads with EXTRA 50% OFF SALE. The 40% tier sits second, and the two named products aren't visible by name.
- Three stacked percentages (50/40/30), each with asterisks and "excludes select items," read as complicated. This is the weakest part of the offer's honesty.
- The top banner text is cut off at both edges ("THE YEAR JUST GOT BETTER … THE YE"). It's probably a marquee-style design, but it looks clipped in the render.
- The category tiles have no prices, discount callouts or product names, so there's no reason to click one tile over another.
- The 50% button says "Shop Now," which is generic. The email is long, with about ten category tiles plus a recommendation grid and no clear single hero product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. If the subject leads with 40% and Rylan Pant, the hero should show the Rylan Pant and Mabel Shirt large, with the 40% badge on them.
- 2. Collapse the tiers to one headline offer, or order them to match the subject. The fine print can carry the rest.
- 3. Add price or discount callouts and named products to the category tiles, for example "Top-rated sweaters, from $68."
- 4. Cut the tile count from about ten to five or six. Put the best-sellers first, since twenty-one near-identical sends has trained the reader to skim.
- 5. Replace "Shop Now" with a specific CTA such as "Shop the Rylan Pant."
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off Rylan Pant, Mabel Shirt + more favorites`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our most-loved styles are 40% off today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus extra 50% off sale and 30% off almost everything else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pants and shirts everyone's been waiting on, now 40% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`image.s.anthropologie.com`, sent from `s.anthropologie.com`). Its HTML has a malformed Outlook CSS rule, viewport settings that block zoom, an empty `<title>`, and several images without alt text. The automated "0 issues / 4 warnings" result mostly reflects headers the AgentMail relay didn't capture, so it doesn't confirm that those items are compliant.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **45 tracking links not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels are in the body.** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&...` and `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../003a700000uTzaxAAC` are both present. Both are cross-domain trackers, and the mon-trk path carries what looks like a Salesforce contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8,718 of 11,594 characters (about 75%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long tracked URLs inflate the text alternative and can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook rule.** The source has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. The empty family entry (`, ,`) is invalid. The stray `; !important;` is a standalone token, so the rule is not marked important and Outlook may ignore the font override.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** There is no document title for assistive tech or web-view rendering.
- **Missing alt text on 5 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The Dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - The mon-trk pixel `003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  The two pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The first three are content images, and that is the more meaningful failure.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE --> <!DOCTYPE html>` can push some older engines into quirks mode.
- **Web font via `@import`.** The Google Fonts `@import` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The `Helvetica, Arial` fallbacks cover this, so the impact is minor.
- **Duplicate CSS.** The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is declared twice, which is harmless bloat.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The Dotomi pixel URL ends in `dtm_email_hash=` with an empty value. If this is a merge field, it did not resolve and the retargeting pixel is firing without an identifier. It may be an artifact of the test recipient, but it is empty in the delivered source.
- No other unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible truncated source. The HTML was cut off, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed violation**. Check the raw headers at the originating MTA. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `s.anthropologie.com` are unknown. This is a relay limitation.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** they fall outside the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages can't be validated. All 45 click-tracking URLs were skipped, and the visible source doesn't expose the final destinations. The only parameters visible are on the third-party pixels (`dtm_cid=2466`, `cli_promo_id=99`), not on site links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`. Remove the empty family entry and the stray `;` before `!important`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `alt` text to the two banner images and the PNG. Add `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
4. Populate `<title>`, and move the comment after the DOCTYPE.
5. Check that `dtm_email_hash` resolves for real recipients.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC at the sending side, because the relay hides them.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links manually to verify UTMs and landing pages. Consider shortening tracking URLs to improve the text-to-URL ratio in the plain-text part.
