# 30% off clothing & accessories ends tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth send in a row for the same Fashion Weekend 30% off promo. It's the "ends tonight!" version, after "Today ONLY! AnthroPerks Early Access" earlier today and "Ends soon!" on Oct 17. The hero works. A large orange-red "30% OFF*" sits over "Clothing & Accessories" on a cream panel, with a lime "SHOP NOW" button and "ends tonight!" above it. The deadline is credible, and the lead sentence is the strongest read in the email.
- The email then dilutes itself. A second "30% OFF**" block for Fall Entertaining, Candles & Decor competes with the hero and carries its own button and asterisk. Below it come an AnthroPerks "ON MY WAY" holiday banner, six editorial category tiles and a nine-product grid. None of the tiles or products show a price or a discount. The 30% message disappears about a third of the way down the page.
- Judgement: a competent last-chance send with a clear top half and a messy bottom half. It will probably get opened, because it's a deadline on a brand you know. It will convert below its potential, because the offer is split in two, the deadline is repeated only in tiny copy, and the lower modules are generic.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. The orange-red "30% OFF*" is the first thing the eye lands on, with a lime "SHOP NOW" button beneath it.
- "ends tonight!" is in both the subject and the hero, so the urgency is honest and consistent.
- The "online only" and "online & in stores" labels under each button set expectations.
- The photography is strong and on-brand. The category tiles (Trend Hub, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Décor, Watches) feel editorial.
- The footer "Even more to love" row (Recycle Denim for $20 off, Klarna, Inclusive Sizing) gives secondary reasons to click without hurting the hero.

## What's weak

- The second "30% OFF**" block with its own button splits attention. The reader has to work out which 30% applies to what.
- The AnthroPerks "ON MY WAY" banner, with "today only!" in a tiny circle, adds a competing urgency and offer. Its copy ("The road to holiday starts here") is vague, and "JOIN NOW TO SHOP FIRST" gates the deal behind a signup.
- The category tiles and the "You'll ♥ these, too" grid show no prices, no sale marks and no product names. The 30% off message is lost once you scroll past the top.
- The tiles have captions but no buttons, so the hero's "SHOP NOW" is the only clear action in the first screen.
- The footer is long, and the asterisk fine print is tiny. The exclusions are hard to read, and "Excludes Terrain, select brands" is exactly what an honest-offer check would flag.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Fall Entertaining/Décor block or move it below the product grid. Keep one hero offer, with one deadline and one button.
- 2. Put a live countdown or a specific time ("ends 11:59 PM PT tonight") in the hero. "ends tonight!" is small and easy to miss.
- 3. Add a sale price or "30% off" tag to the product grid and the category tiles. Add "Shop dresses" or "Shop sweaters" style CTAs to each tile.
- 4. Fold the AnthroPerks banner into a single line, or drop it from the last-chance send. It reads as a different campaign.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific: "Shop 30% off clothing" rather than "SHOP NOW".
- 6. Put the full fine print behind a "See details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 30% off ends at 11:59 PM PT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: 30% off sweaters, dresses & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus fall décor and candles at 30% off — online and in stores, tonight only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's last chance: 30% off clothing and accessories ends at midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`image.s.anthropologie.com`, `pxl.mon-trk.com`). Its main technical problems are missing alt text, a malformed Outlook font rule, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value in a third-party pixel. The compliance headers could not be verified, because the relay may have stripped them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 38 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text version:** 7388 of 10415 characters (about 71%) are URLs. This is most likely the tracking-wrapped links, and it can raise spam scoring. It is a minor deliverability risk.
- **Blank pixel parameter:** The Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash` is empty (see section 4).
- **Pixel contents:** The `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../101826_AN-US_30OffFashion30OffHomeComboEndsTonight/003a7000...` pixel path contains what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`). This is a subscriber identifier in a third-party URL, and may be intended behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (4 instances, 3 distinct files):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (appears twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (appears twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  
  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these render as nothing or as filenames. If they are decorative, they need `alt=""`. The `alt` attribute should be present either way.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/...` have no alt. They are harmless, but adding `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` is cleaner.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** The `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - It has an empty font-family list entry (`, ,`).
  - The `;` before `!important` makes the `!important` invalid, so the Outlook override is not applied as intended.
  - It looks like an unresolved template variable.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) issue.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Web fonts:** Fonts come from `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` in a `<style>` block. Gmail and some other clients ignore or strip `@import`, so they fall back to the declared stacks. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Blank hash:** `dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the Dotomi pixel. This is either a merge field that resolved to nothing or a placeholder left unpopulated, so the retargeting pixel cannot match the subscriber. It may be an artifact of the QA test inbox, so confirm against a live send.
- **Font stack:** The empty font-family slot in the MSO block (section 3) suggests an unpopulated variable.
- No other unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the truncated source. The rest of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Neither was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified failure. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated HTML did not reach the footer, so these could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All 38 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source exposes no final destination URLs. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "30% off clothing & accessories ends tonight" offer could not be verified.
- The `mon-trk` pixel path names the campaign `30OffFashion30OffHomeComboEndsTonight`. That is a "Fashion + Home combo" label, while the subject line says "clothing & accessories". It is a naming inconsistency in internal tracking, with no confirmed user-facing impact.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two banner images and the `.png`. Use `alt=""` for decorative images and the tracking pixels.
2. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray `;`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport tag.
4. Populate `<title>`.
5. Check why `dtm_email_hash=` is blank. Verify it with a real send, not the relay copy.
6. Inspect the raw origin headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't rely on the relay copy.
7. Re-run the audit with full HTML and resolve the 38 tracking links through their redirects. That will confirm destination URLs, UTM params and the footer unsubscribe and address.
