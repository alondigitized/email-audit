# 25% off denim, sweaters & sweater dresses
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, clear category promo. A pale-blue "WINTER WARM UP" hero carries a big navy "25% OFF*" over "Denim, Sweaters & Sweater Dresses," with one SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first, and the subject matches the hero exactly. Compared with the Nov 6–8 dress sends, this one is more seasonal and covers three categories, which suits a cold-weather shopper better.
- The weak spot is the second promo. A "HOLIDAY CHEERS 30% Off**" block with its own SHOP NOW button sits directly under the hero. It is a higher discount for a different category (hosting, throws, gifts), so it competes with the lead offer and buries the subject's promise. Below that, the "More to Explore" grid is pure editorial with no prices and no product detail. After the Nov 12 "1000+ styles to sale" send, the cadence is daily and the offers keep rotating, so this reads as "another day, another percentage."
- Verdict: well executed and clear at the top, but diluted by two offers and a long, price-free scroll. It is a solid mid-to-high send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: large "25% OFF*" with the categories spelled out, one dark button, and a "limited time!" sticker.
- The subject mirrors the hero, so there is no mismatch between inbox and body.
- The seasonal framing ("Winter Warm Up," plus the Thanksgiving line in the header) feels current.
- The 3x3 "Made With You in Mind" grid shows real denim, knit and sweater product, with "Top Rated" labels as light social proof.
- The editorial tiles (Cashmere Shop, Trend Hub) have strong imagery that fits the brand voice.

## What's weak

- Two competing offers stack at the top (25% and 30%) with two identical SHOP NOW buttons. It is unclear which is the main event.
- The "limited time!" stickers on both blocks give no actual deadline, so the urgency is vague.
- The product grid has no prices, no sale prices and no product names. Only the "Top Rated" tags give any detail.
- The middle of the email is a long run of editorial tiles with no offer, which pushes the product grid far down.
- The asterisked fine print at the bottom is tiny, and it is the only place exclusions appear, so the offer is less transparent than it could be.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Move the 30% Holiday Cheers block below the product grid, or cut it from this send, so the 25% apparel offer owns the top.
- 2. Put the product grid right under the hero and cut the editorial tiles to two or three. Add sale prices to each product.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the sticker, such as "Ends Sunday," instead of "limited time!"
- 4. Make the CTA specific: "Shop 25% off denim & sweaters" instead of "Shop Now."
- 5. Since this follows the Nov 12 sale send, vary the creative. Lead with one hero product, not another percent-off banner.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has several code-level defects: malformed Outlook CSS, an empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport meta tag, and missing alt attributes. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the body, footer, and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **38 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains, and expiry are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text part is 74% URLs** (7302 of 9871 chars). Long tracked URLs dominate the text alternative, which can hurt spam scoring and readability in text-only clients.
- **Dotomi pixel has an empty parameter:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The empty `dtm_email_hash=` suggests the hash token didn't resolve, or the test recipient has no hash. If it fires empty for real sends, the retargeting match is lost.
- **Mixed tracking vendors:** `pxl.mon-trk.com` and `login.dotomi.com` are third-party pixels alongside the first-party `image.s.anthropologie.com`. They add extra DNS lookups and are commonly blocked by Apple MPP and image-blocking clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images**, per the QA check:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - the mon-trk pixel `003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  The two tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`). The three content images need descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This hurts the web-view and screen-reader experience.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`. It has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`) and a stray `;` before `!important`, so the declaration is invalid and the Outlook font override may not apply.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` inside `<style>`.** Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip it, so the web fonts won't load there. Fallback stacks exist, but the quoted generics (`'sans-serif'`, `'serif'`) are invalid, because generic family names must be unquoted. Those declarations fall through to the next valid family.
- **Duplicate selector:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is defined twice. This is harmless but redundant.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` (see above) is the only token-related finding. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are absent in the original message, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` is aligned with the From address, but this couldn't be verified.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and physical address:** not assessable, because the footer was cut off in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters could not be verified.** All 38 links go through click-redirect domains and were skipped, and the visible source shows no UTM strings.
- The campaign identifier `111326_AN-US_Promo25OffSelectApparelLaunch` appears in the mon-trk pixel and matches the subject's 25% promo. Landing-page offer alignment was not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and add descriptive alt text to the three content images.
2. Fill in `<title>` and remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the MSO CSS: remove the empty font-family entry and the stray `;` before `!important`.
4. Unquote the generic font families (`sans-serif`, `serif`), and move web-font loading to a `<link>` or `@font-face` fallback for clients that ignore `@import`.
5. Confirm in the original raw message (not the relay copy) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are present and pass.
6. Check why `dtm_email_hash` renders empty. If it also happens on real sends, fix the merge variable.
7. Trim the plain-text part, for example by using shorter or fewer tracked URLs.
8. Resolve the 38 redirect links to the final destination and check for UTM parameters, status codes, and offer match.
