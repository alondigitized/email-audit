# ENDS TONIGHT: Up to 30% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Anthropologie drops back into full discount mode with a clean but formula-driven sale email, just 24 hours after the second maeve "Hot List" editorial send. The creative is competent — the yellow-and-sage summer palette is on-brand, the 30% OFF hero reads fast, and the category nav does useful work. But "ENDS TONIGHT: Up to 30% off" is essentially the same playbook Anthropologie ran through the entire May barrage of "Extra 40% off" sends, just with a lower discount. The editorial momentum the brand spent the last three weeks building — maeve, off-duty utility, "Tiny tops 🤝 cool cargos" — gets flattened in a single send.
- The asterisk on the 30% is doing quiet damage. "Up to" already hedges the number; the asterisk piles on, signaling exclusions before the customer has clicked anything. Combined with a generic "SHOP NOW" CTA and no single hero product to anchor the eye, this email asks the reader to do a lot of work to discover what, exactly, is on sale.
- Salvageable, but it's a step backward for a brand that was finding its editorial voice.

## What's working

- **30% OFF hero is instantly readable** — large type, warm yellow button, clear visual hierarchy above the fold.
- **Category nav (DRESSES / TOPS / BOTTOMS / VACATION OUTFITS / DENIM / ACCESSORIES / BEAUTY)** does the segmentation job the subject line ignores — subscribers can self-select without hunting.
- **Summer palette is tight** — yellows, olives, naturals, and the "Summer Florals" / "Beach Bag Beauty" editorial sections all stay coherent with the season.
- **"ENDS TONIGHT" creates a real decision window** — the urgency is credible and short, not a manipulative fake countdown.
- **Multiple lifestyle editorial sections** (The Traveler, Utility Bottoms, Summer Arrivals) give the email editorial substance beyond a raw discount announcement.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 30% off" + asterisk undermines trust before the first click** — "up to" already tells you most items won't qualify; the asterisk doubles the hedge.
- **No single hero product or styled look is spotlighted** — the email is a wide-net catalog pass rather than one compelling reason to click right now.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the weakest possible CTA** — after three editorial maeve sends building category affinity, this button reads like a department store clearance flyer.
- **Cadence friction** — this is the third Anthropologie send in two days (after June 15 maeve and June 16 maeve Hot List), and the first hard discount in that run. Subscribers who opened the charming maeve editorial yesterday are being handed a blunt coupon today.
- **Preheader appears to be boilerplate shipping/header copy** rather than crafted complementary text — the inbox preview is doing nothing to support "ENDS TONIGHT."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "Up to 30% off" with a specific category hero** — pick the one category (e.g., dresses or vacation outfits) where the full 30% applies and lead with that. "30% off every dress, today only" beats "up to 30% off everything*" every time.
- 2. **Rewrite the primary CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to something category-specific: "Shop the Sale" or "See the Dresses" — anything with a noun.
- 3. **Drop the asterisk from the hero headline** — move the exclusion language to fine print at the bottom. Don't lead with a caveat.
- 4. **Write a real preheader** that extends the urgency narrative, not shipping boilerplate.
- 5. **Acknowledge the maeve editorial thread** — a single line ("The maeve pieces you loved? They're in the sale.") bridges the past two sends instead of orphaning them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 30% off window closes tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dresses, florals, beach bags — 30% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Every summer style. Full 30% off. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pieces from the Hot List — now 30% off, today only.`
