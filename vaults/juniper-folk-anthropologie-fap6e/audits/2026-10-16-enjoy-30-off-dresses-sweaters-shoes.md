# Enjoy 30% off dresses, sweaters, shoes
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Anthropologie Fashion Weekend" 30% off send the brand has run all week, now with a subject line that names product types. The hero is still the strongest part. A big orange-red "30% OFF*" sits on a cream box with "Clothing & Accessories" under it and a lime "SHOP NOW" button. Your eye lands on the offer first, and the CTA is easy to find.
- Below the hero the email dilutes itself. A second orange banner adds a different 30% offer, "Fall Entertaining, Candles & Decor." Six editorial tiles follow (Trend Hub, Holiday Party, Cashmere, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets), then a nine-product grid with no names or prices. The subject promises dresses, sweaters and shoes. The body shows a cashmere tile, a few sweaters and some dresses, but no shoe tile that I can see. Watches, candles and beauty gift sets pull away from a fashion-led offer.
- It's competent and on-brand, but it's more of the Oct 15 "NOW: 30% off clothing & accessories" email with holiday content added. By now the sale reads as always-on, so the urgency in "limited time!" feels thin. Judgement: a solid, fatigued send, mid-6 territory.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large, high-contrast, and readable at a glance: "30% OFF*" with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- The subject names concrete categories (dresses, sweaters, shoes), which is more specific than the Oct 15 "clothing & accessories" wording.
- The brand look is consistent: serif wordmark, cream palette, editorial photography with clean captions.
- The tiles move into holiday and cashmere themes, so the email feels seasonal rather than stale.
- The "Join AnthroPerks" link and the Klarna "Pay Later" option at the bottom add some loyalty and payment-friction relief.

## What's weak

- The subject promises dresses, sweaters and shoes, but the body has no shoe tile and no dress tile that I can see. The tiles are Trend Hub, Holiday Party, Cashmere and similar.
- A second 30% banner for decor sits right under the hero, and the asterisks differ (* vs **). That splits the offer, and the fine print is tiny.
- The "Might we recommend…" grid shows nine products with no names, prices, or visible links. There's nothing to click for a reason.
- Watches, mantel décor and beauty gift sets are off-category for a shopper responding to a clothing offer.
- Cadence is a problem. This is at least the seventh send of this promo in about ten days (Oct 6 to Oct 16), and the "limited time!" label has lost credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the subject. Add three tiles for dresses, sweaters and shoes right under the hero, and cut the watches, beauty and mantel tiles.
- 2. Give the product grid names and sale prices (for example, "$98 → $69"). Add one hero product above it.
- 3. Move the decor banner below the fashion content, or drop it, so there's one offer.
- 4. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday at midnight," in place of the generic "limited time!"
- 5. Space the sends out, or retire this creative for a few days. Seven near-identical emails train people to wait for the next one.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The cashmere sweater edit, now 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fashion Weekend: 30% off clothing & accessories online only. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has several CSS and accessibility defects (malformed `font-family` declarations, `user-scalable=0`, empty `<title>`, 7 `<img>` warnings). The QA data cannot confirm compliance or tracking behavior, because the headers were not captured, 35 links were not probed, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **35 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so final destinations and HTTP status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel with an empty parameter:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. Either the hash token did not resolve at send time, or the value is injected at click time. If the token failed, the retargeting pixel carries no identifier.
- **Second pixel:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../101626_AN-US_30OffFashionLaunchResend/003a700000uTzaxAAC` embeds a per-recipient ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`, a Salesforce Contact ID format) in the path. The campaign slug includes "Resend", so this send is a resend of an earlier campaign.
- **Plain-text part is 73% URLs** (6818 of 9333 chars). The long tracking URLs make the text alternative hard to read and can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 `<img>` warnings (5 unique images).** The pencil-line banners appear twice each in the source.
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (×2)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (×2)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The two tracking pixels above (`1_0`, `003a700000uTzaxAAC`) need `alt=""`.
  
  Purely decorative images should use an explicit `alt=""`. A missing attribute can make screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>`. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) on clients that honor it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and web-view renderers use it as the document label.
- **Malformed Outlook font CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`. It has an empty font-family list entry, and the `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it is parsed as a stray token. The Outlook font override is therefore likely ignored or inconsistently applied.
- **Quoted generic families:** `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` appear in quotes throughout the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` rules. Quoted, they are treated as literal font names, not generic fallbacks. If the web font and the named fallbacks are unavailable, the fallback does not resolve.
- **Web font loading:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` in a `<style>` block is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. It is also render-blocking where supported. The `@font-face` rules for `community.urbn.com/static/fonts/*.woff` are limited to `@media screen`. Both only work in a subset of clients.
- **Duplicate rule:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (UO/Terrain blocks). This is harmless but is leftover template debris.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in older Outlook/IE-engine renderers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` is the only visible unresolved-token candidate (see §2).
- No literal `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FirstName]` tokens appear in the visible source excerpt. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is also likely a relay limitation. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` and the image domain `image.s.anthropologie.com` are aligned, which is a positive signal but not proof of authentication.
- **Footer unverified.** The visible HTML is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **cannot be assessed**, because the 35 redirect-wrapped links were skipped and no destination URLs are exposed in the available source.
- The tracking IDs in the pixel paths (`101626_AN-US_30OffFashionLaunchResend`, `cli_promo_id=99`) suggest campaign naming exists. Whether it carries through to the site is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt=""`** to the two tracking pixels and any other decorative images. Add descriptive alt text to `pencilline_1`, `pencilline_2`, and `1e313de8-….png` if they carry content.
2. **Fix the viewport meta:** remove `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0`.
3. **Fix the Outlook CSS:** change it to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray `;` before `!important`.
4. **Unquote generic families:** use `sans-serif` and `serif`, not `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'`.
5. **Populate `<title>`** with the subject line or preheader.
6. **Verify the `dtm_email_hash` token** in the Dotomi pixel is populated at send. Confirm it is not blank in the delivered message.
7. **Re-run the audit against a raw `.eml`** that includes headers. This would settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the redirects to check UTMs and landing pages.
8. **Trim the plain-text part** by shortening or removing tracking URLs, and move the DOCTYPE above the template comment.
