# Take an extra 50% off sale styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Anthropologie Home send as this morning's "Up to 30% off home + extra 50% off sale", with the two offers swapped in priority. The hero is a boxed olive-and-cream panel. "EXTRA 50% OFF* SALE →" is on top, "UP TO 30% OFF** HOME →" sits below it, and "Feels like fall sale (limited time!)" is the kicker. The offer lands immediately. The colorful category tiles (Kitchen & Dining, Candles & Gifts, Bedding, Rugs & Curtains, Lighting and others) give clear shopping paths.
- The email stops being a home sale after the tiles. Below them is an editorial strip with a leather-jacket model, a holiday party shop, a cashmere sweater, mantel décor, watches and beauty gift sets. After that comes a nine-image apparel grid ("These are so you, too:") with no captions, prices or links I can identify. The subject says "sale styles", but the hero's "Sale" is an unspecified category. The email never shows which sale items get the extra 50%.
- A good offer-first hero is followed by a long, unfocused scroll. It is a slightly weaker repeat of this morning's send, and sending the same offer twice in one day feels like list fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero is offer-first. "EXTRA 50% OFF" is large and high-contrast in cream on olive.
- The two offers are stacked, each with its own arrow CTA, so the eye lands on them in order.
- The category tiles are color-blocked and easy to scan, and they cover the full home range.
- "Feels like fall sale (limited time!)" matches the season.
- The footer has useful extras: SMS 10% off, Afterpay, inclusive sizing and styling services.

## What's weak

- Nothing shows which items are on sale. There are no products, no prices and no before/after pricing.
- The "limited time!" line has no date or deadline, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The editorial block and the apparel grid pull attention away from the home offer. The lower grid is uncaptioned and has no visible prices, which makes it feel like filler.
- Both offers carry asterisks (* and **), and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense.
- It repeats this morning's offer ("Up to 30% off home + extra 50% off sale"), so the cadence feels spammy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put 4–6 real sale products with prices in a grid directly under the hero. Show was/now pricing so the 50% off reads as real.
- 2. Add a specific end date ("Ends Sunday") to replace "limited time!".
- 3. Cut the editorial strip and the apparel grid, or relabel them as clearly separate sections with captions and prices.
- 4. Stop sending the same offer twice in one day. If a second send is needed, change the angle or the audience.
- 5. Shorten the fine print and give the exclusions one clear line above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off sale, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale + home: the fall markdowns are live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 30% off home. Bedding, rugs, lighting and more, ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall sale on sale: lamps, throws and dinnerware from $12.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally functional, but it has a malformed Outlook font declaration, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and several images with no alt text. Compliance headers and link destinations could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **43 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations, redirect chains or final landing pages are validated.
- **Two third-party pixels are embedded:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../100626_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS30OffHomeLaunchResend/003a700000u...`
- **`dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the captured URL.** This could be an unresolved merge value or a capture artifact. Confirm that it populates in a live send.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs** (8,338 of 11,824 characters, over 70%). That bloats the text alternative and is a mild spam-filter signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The MSO conditional block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family list entry, and the `;` before `!important` detaches it from the declaration. Outlook will ignore the `!important` and may drop the declaration.
- **Missing alt text** on content images:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
- **Tracking pixels have no alt attribute** (`1_0` and `003a700000u...`). They should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1x1 sizing so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is blocked.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which stops pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**, which is a minor accessibility and metadata gap.
- **Google Fonts `@import`** is in a `<style>` block. Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore it, so the fallback stacks are what render. The `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` selectors only match if those strings are present in inline styles, which can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **The template comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`.** This can trigger quirks mode in older Outlook/IE-based renderers.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the captured source.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` above is the only possible personalization gap. It is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found** [WARN]. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this isn't confirmed as missing from the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found** [WARN]. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements expect it.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment couldn't be checked. The From domain is `s.anthropologie.com`.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and postal address can't be verified**, because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified.** All 43 links are click-tracking redirects, so the final destinations and any UTM parameters weren't inspected.
- **The `mon-trk` path contains the campaign ID** `100626_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS30OffHomeLaunchResend`, which suggests a resend. That is consistent with the subject, but it isn't evidence of a continuity problem.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two pencil-line banners and `1e313de8...png`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta, and populate `<title>`.
4. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
5. Check the raw message at the sending MTA for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, since the relay may be stripping them.
6. Resolve a sample of the 43 tracked links to confirm landing pages and UTM tagging. Confirm that `dtm_email_hash` populates in a live send.
7. Trim the plain-text alternative so it isn't dominated by raw tracking URLs.
