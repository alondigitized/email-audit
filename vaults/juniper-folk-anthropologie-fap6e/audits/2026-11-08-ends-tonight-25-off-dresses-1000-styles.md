# ENDS TONIGHT: 25% off dresses (1000+ styles)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the same burgundy dress hero Anthropologie sent on Nov 6 ("25% OFF 1000+ DRESSES") and Nov 7 ("25% off dresses ENDS SOON"). The only visible change is a new "ends tonight!" sticker. The top of the email is strong. "25% OFF DRESSES" and "Extra 50% OFF SALE" are stacked in orange on burgundy, and each has its own SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. A "Holiday Cheers: 30% OFF Hosting & Gifts" band adds a third offer. After that comes a long, editorial "More to Explore" grid and a nine-product "Made with you in mind" block. A subscriber who has seen this hero three days running gets a real deadline and not much else that's new.
- Judgement: a solid, clear send with credible urgency, but it's the third near-identical dress push in three days. The creative fatigue is the problem, not the execution.

## What's working

- The "ends tonight!" sticker adds a credible deadline to an offer that was previously just "ends soon."
- The hero has strong hierarchy. The oversized orange "25% OFF" and "50% OFF" lead the eye, and each has its own pink or white SHOP NOW button.
- The subject names the exact offer and the scale: 25% off, 1000+ styles.
- The "More to Explore" tiles (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop) are on-brand and seasonal.
- The "Made with you in mind" grid has star ratings and a personalized label, which adds social proof and a nod to segmentation.

## What's weak

- Three stacked offers compete: 25% dresses, extra 50% off sale and 30% off hosting and gifts. None gets the full focus.
- The hero is nearly identical to the Nov 6 and Nov 7 sends. The only difference is the sticker, which is too small to feel fresh.
- The hero has no product imagery, only type on burgundy. Nothing shows what a dress looks like until you scroll.
- The "More to Explore" tiles have no offers or CTAs beyond the labels, so the second half of the email dilutes the deadline.
- The asterisked fine print is dense at the bottom, and each offer carries different terms and dates. That undercuts how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one message for the last-day send: "Ends tonight: 25% off dresses." Show one or two dress photos above the fold and drop the 50% off block.
- 2. Add a countdown or a "11:59 PM PT tonight" line next to the CTA.
- 3. Drop the Holiday Cheers band, or move it below the product grid, so it stops competing with the deadline offer.
- 4. Replace the generic "More to Explore" tiles with dress sub-categories (party, midi, sweater dresses) so they support the offer.
- 5. Give the product grid price and offer tags, such as "25% off," on each tile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off every dress, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 1000+ dresses at 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Holiday party picks, midis and sweater dresses. Sale ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus an extra 50% off sale styles. Gone at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a heavily templated multi-brand Anthropologie send. The QA run found no hard failures but 3 warnings. The visible source has a malformed Outlook CSS rule and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer weren't audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 41 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Redirect destinations, expiry and status are unverified.
- The Dotomi pixel has an empty parameter: `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. If `dtm_email_hash` should carry a hashed address, the pixel is firing without its identifier. This may be intentional for the test inbox, but it can't be confirmed.
- Two third-party pixels are embedded: `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../110826_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS25OffDressesEndsTonight/003a70000…`. They are expected tracking, but they depend on image loading.
- The `mon-trk.com` path is URL-encoded with the campaign name. I can't verify from the truncated source whether it matches the subject offer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in the MSO block:** `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`) and a stray `;` before `!important`. The declaration is invalid, so Outlook probably ignores the `!important` and the font stack.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html xmlns=...>` tag has none.
- **Missing alt text on 3 content images** (`image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/fe3e11717164057c701470/m/...`):
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
- **Missing alt on 2 tracking pixels** (`dotomi` and `mon-trk`). This is low severity, but `alt=""` plus `width/height="1"` is still best practice.
- **Web font via `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stacks cover it, but the custom fonts won't render there.
- **Duplicate rule:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (Beholden and Terrain blocks).
- **Comment before the doctype:** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering paths.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` above is the only possible personalization gap.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** one-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.anthropologie.com`, which uses a subdomain alignment pattern that should be verified against raw headers.
- **Footer not audited:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed. All 41 click links are redirect-wrapped and weren't resolved, and the source was truncated.
- The only campaign identifier visible is the `mon-trk.com` path (`110826_AN-US_Combo50OffSOS25OffDressesEndsTonight`). It carries a date (11/08/26) and a "Combo50Off…25OffDresses" label that should be reconciled with the subject's "25% off dresses" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO font rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, populate `<title>`, and add `alt` text to the three banner and content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. Move the `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comment below the doctype.
5. Confirm the `dtm_email_hash` value is populated in production sends.
6. Resolve a sample of the 41 redirect links with a redirect-follow probe to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
7. Capture raw headers (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
8. Re-run the audit on the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be checked.
9. Check the QA scoring. A 57% pass rate with 0 issues and 3 warnings doesn't add up, and may mean the skipped checks are counted as failures.
