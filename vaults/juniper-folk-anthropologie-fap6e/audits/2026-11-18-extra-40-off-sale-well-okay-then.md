# Extra 40% off sale? Well, okay then. 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, offer-first send with a weak middle. The brown hero carries a big pink "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE" with "NEW STYLES ADDED" and a SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer immediately, and the subject's 40% matches the hero. It's also a real escalation from the 25% denim and sweater run of Nov 13–15.
- Below the hero, the email splits its message. A "ON MY WAY… with last-minute gifts" block pushes gift categories (Top-Rated Gifts, Stocking Stuffers, Buy Online & Pickup in Store, E-Gift Cards). A second "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" banner repeats the Nov 12 and Nov 14 creative. The long "More to Explore" editorial grid has no prices and no offer. The email sells a discount at the top and then wanders into gifting and mood.
- Overall it's a solid 6. The headline is strong and the lower half dilutes it. It's also the fifth or sixth promo in a week, so the escalation has to carry the open.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and high-contrast: pink "40% OFF" on dark brown, with one SHOP NOW button.
- The subject and hero agree on "Extra 40% off sale," so there's no bait-and-switch feeling.
- The "Thankful for great outfits. Get yours in time for Thanksgiving" top line adds seasonal timing.
- The star-rated product row ("Made with you in mind") adds some social proof and real product imagery.
- The editorial grid is polished and on-brand, with consistent voice and no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- The gifting block competes with the sale hero. It introduces a second theme before the shopper has acted on the first.
- "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" repeats the Nov 12 and Nov 14 creative. It reads as filler, with a tiny SHOP NOW button.
- The "More to Explore" grid has no pricing, discounts or CTAs beyond the tile labels, so the 40% disappears for most of the scroll.
- The hero has no deadline. The offer carries an asterisk and the fine print sits at the very bottom, so urgency is missing and exclusions feel buried.
- The email is very long, at roughly 5,800px, with a lot of scroll before the product row.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the gifting block, or move it below the product row. Let the 40% sale be the single story, and add a visible end date such as "Ends Sunday" in the hero.
- 2. Put sale products with strikethrough and sale prices directly under the hero, instead of the plain star-rated tiles.
- 3. Add a one-line exclusions summary near the hero in place of the fine print at the bottom.
- 4. Trim "More to Explore" to three or four tiles, each labelled with the discount.
- 5. Drop the repeated "1000+ new sale styles" banner or merge it into the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 40% off is here 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `1,000+ new styles added, plus last-minute gifts before Thanksgiving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters, dresses and gifts, all marked down again.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie, "Extra 40% off sale? Well, okay then. 👀"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has one confirmed CSS defect in the Outlook block, missing alt text on 3 content images, and an empty `<title>`. The header-based compliance checks and the link checks could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, and the relay does not capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations unverified:** 39 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are untested.
- **Empty hash in the Dotomi pixel:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. This is probably an unpopulated merge field in the captured copy, so the pixel would record no identity. It is not confirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** the email loads two third-party tracking pixels, `login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/...` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../111826_AN-US_40OffSOSLaunch/003a700000uTzaxAAC`. They are standard but add external dependencies and privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS (confirmed):** the `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry (`, ,`) makes the font-family value invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` orphans the flag, so the declaration doesn't apply.
  - Outlook falls back to its default font.
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  Screen readers will announce filenames, and nothing displays when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `1_0` and `003a700000uTzaxAAC` should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** minor, but it affects web-view and accessibility tooling.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web font import:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is stripped or ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Fallback stacks exist (Helvetica, Arial), so the impact is low.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** the `<!-- *** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE *** -->` comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some older engines.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- The only anomaly is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` noted above.
- The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable, not confirmed defects:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - The QA output attributes all three to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers.
  - Check them on the raw message in a seed inbox before treating them as failures.
- **Plain-text part:** 7,578 of 10,154 characters (about 75%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is a minor spam-filter risk.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID:** `111826_AN-US_40OffSOSLaunch` is consistent with the subject's "Extra 40% off".
- **Unverifiable:** UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. Links route through click-redirect domains that were not probed, and the visible hrefs are truncated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the Outlook font rule:** change it to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`, with no empty entry and no stray `;`.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the two `emailbanner_pencilline_*.jpg` images and to `1e313de8-...png`. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. **Fill in the `<title>`** and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
4. **Move the comment** below the DOCTYPE, or drop it.
5. **Check `dtm_email_hash` population** in a live send to confirm the Dotomi pixel is personalized.
6. **Verify headers on the raw message** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) with a seed-list test rather than via the relay.
7. **Resolve and probe the 39 redirect links** with a headless follow, to confirm landing pages and UTM carry-through.
8. **Trim the plain-text part** to reduce URL density.
