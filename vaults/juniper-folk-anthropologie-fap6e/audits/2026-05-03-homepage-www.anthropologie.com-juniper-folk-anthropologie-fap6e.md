## What's working

- **AnthroPerks banner at the very top** — I see my program name before I even get to the logo. That's an instant "I belong here" signal.
- **"ENDS TONIGHT!" badge** — that mustard-yellow circle on the promo card is physically hard to ignore. Urgency without being obnoxious.
- **"$150 off $500" specificity** — I know exactly what I'd have to spend and what I'd save. No hunting for details.
- **DRESSES as the first category tile** — they know who their customer is. First tile, biggest mental real estate. I'd tap it immediately.
- **Warm pink gradient on the promo card** — even without lifestyle photography, the color palette is distinctly Anthro. Doesn't feel like a generic sale page.

## What's weak

- **Zero lifestyle photography above the fold** — the entire hero zone is a promo card with dollar signs. No model in a flowy dress, no styled home vignette, nothing that makes me *feel* the brand.
- **"sign up for first dibs on summer styles"** — I'm already an AnthroPerks member. This reads like a new-customer acquisition message, not a reward for loyalty.
- **Category tiles are text-only** — the yellow grid is functional, but DRESSES as plain text on a yellow rectangle doesn't inspire. Even a small editorial thumbnail per tile would pull me in faster.
- **Cookie banner covers the fifth category tile** — "S & ACCESSORIES" is partially obscured. Minor, but it interrupts the grid's visual completeness.
- **No trust signals anywhere above fold** — no free returns badge, no review counts, no "free shipping over $X." For a $500 threshold offer, I'd want at least one reassurance before committing.

## Recommendations

- **Put a lifestyle hero behind the promo** — even as a background: a model in a linen maxi dress wearing the summer edit. The promo copy can float on top. Urgency + aspiration, not urgency alone.
- **Swap "sign up for first dibs" to a member-specific message** — if I'm logged in: "Your AnthroPerks access is live — shop summer styles first." Speaks to loyalty instead of acquisition.
- **Add editorial thumbnails to the category tiles** — tiny crop of a dress, a candle, a linen blouse. One image per tile keeps the grid fast to render and dramatically more scroll-worthy.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold on the promo card** — something like "Free shipping at $150" alongside the "$150 off $500" offer. Reduces friction at the exact moment I'm calculating whether the deal is worth it.
