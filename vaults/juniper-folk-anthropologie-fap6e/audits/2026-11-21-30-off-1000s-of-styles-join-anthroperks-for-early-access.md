# 30% off 1000s of styles 🛍️ Join AnthroPerks for EARLY ACCESS.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a second Black Friday send from Anthropologie on the same day. It follows this morning's "30% off: BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW with AnthroPerks" (8/10), and the creative is close to identical. The hero is a festive gift-tag graphic with a giant "30% OFF". Below it sit "SIGN UP & SAVE FIRST", two gold SHOP buttons and a green "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" strip. The eye lands on the offer right away, and the brand voice is strong.
- The body is a well-built grid of red-labelled category tiles: sweaters, holiday decor, shoes, dresses, sleepwear, furniture, jewelry, beauty, wedding and Terrain. It is easy to scan and fits the season. A "Might we recommend…" product row follows with star ratings. The weakness is stacked messaging. The email has three offers (30% off, early access, extra 40% off sale) and four or five CTAs competing in the hero. The footer carries a dense wall of fine print on exclusions.
- My verdict is a strong, polished send. It is likely redundant for anyone who already got this morning's version, and after the 40%-off barrage of 11/18–11/20 the cadence is heavy.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF" gift tag puts the offer first and signals Black Friday and the holidays.
- The "SIGN UP & SAVE FIRST" line and the "Early Access" header bar push AnthroPerks membership, so there's a reason to join.
- The red-labelled category tiles are easy to scan and cover many shopping intents (apparel, home, gifts, beauty).
- The "Might we recommend…" row with star ratings adds social proof below the grid.
- The photography is on-brand, warm and seasonal, with no visible broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks two gold SHOP buttons, a green SHOP CLOTHING button and the "extra 40%" sale line, so no single CTA wins.
- The offer reads as "30%" and "extra 40%" at once, with asterisks and "excludes select items". That muddies how honest the deal feels.
- The send looks near-identical to this morning's, so it adds little for a subscriber.
- The hero is tall, so the category grid starts well below the fold on mobile.
- The footer fine print is dense and tiny, and it buries the exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the hero to one CTA. Use one primary button, "Shop 30% Off", and move home and sale to the tile grid.
- 2. Explain the 30% versus extra 40% relationship in one clear line, for example "30% off everything, plus extra 40% off sale".
- 3. Differentiate the afternoon send from the morning one. Use a countdown or a "last chance for early access" angle so it doesn't read as a duplicate.
- 4. Put a real product in the hero, such as a sweater with its price, instead of only the graphic.
- 5. Pull the key exclusions up into a short line under the hero and shrink the footer legalese.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, decor, shoes and more, 30% off plus extra 40% off sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a consistent ESP-wrapped sending and image domain (`s.anthropologie.com`, `image.s.anthropologie.com`). The confirmed issues are malformed Outlook CSS, a zoom-disabling viewport meta, missing `alt` attributes, an empty `<title>`, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`** has an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value in the captured HTML.
  - It may be an unpopulated merge field, or the value may be blanked for the test inbox. The source can't tell which.
  - If it is unpopulated, the pixel can't match the recipient.
- **Plain-text part is 73.6% URLs** (8,474 of 11,520 chars). This is a deliverability and readability risk because the text alternative is mostly raw tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.**
  - The rule is `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`.
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) and the stray `;` before `!important` make the declaration invalid, so the Outlook font override is likely dropped.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 in clients that honor it.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This affects web-view and browser-tab accessibility.
- **Missing `alt` attributes (QA-confirmed).**
  - Content images: `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` and `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no text fallback.
  - Tracking pixels (`login.dotomi.com/.../1_0` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../112126_AN-US_PromoPerksEarlyAccessBFReminder_MID_NonMember/`) should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **`<!-- ... TEMPLATE ... -->` comment before `<!DOCTYPE>`.** Anything before the doctype can push older IE-based and Outlook renderers into quirks mode.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` inside `<style>`.** This is unsupported in many clients, and in some Outlook versions it can force a Times New Roman fallback. The font stacks include fallbacks, so the impact is limited.
- **Duplicate CSS rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (the Anthro/Beholden and Terrain blocks). This is harmless but is template debt.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source, which is truncated.
- The only suspect is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` above.
- The `_NonMember` segment in the `mon-trk.com` path matches the subject's "Join AnthroPerks" framing, so that part looks intentional.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Re-check against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address** are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible, because the links are wrapped in click-redirect domains and the HTML is truncated.
- Landing-page alignment and campaign tagging can't be verified. This needs a redirect-following check of the 44 skipped links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three banner images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
4. Populate `<title>` with the subject or campaign name, and move the template comment below the `<!DOCTYPE>`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part. Use fewer, cleaner URLs, or a text-optimized version of the content.
6. Verify the `dtm_email_hash` population using a seeded recipient that is not a non-member test address.
7. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw headers. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `s.anthropologie.com`.
8. Resolve the redirect chains of the 44 tracking links to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
