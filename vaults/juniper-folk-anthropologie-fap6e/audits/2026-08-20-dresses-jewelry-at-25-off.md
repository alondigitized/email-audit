# Dresses & jewelry at 25% OFF
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's third "Perksfest" send this week, and it's the same app-only, AnthroPerks-gated early-access play they ran on 8/16 ("Perksfest is here: Shop maeve first," 6.00/10) — except now the hero is a straight 25% off dresses & jewelry instead of the maeve sub-brand tease. The "24-HR app exclusive" starburst badge is doing a lot of work to manufacture urgency around what's functionally just another percentage-off promo, the fifth of its kind in ten days if you count the whole run since 8/12.
- The hero itself renders cleanly — purple confetti background, clear "25% OFF DRESSES & JEWELRY," visible "JOIN & SHOP NOW" button — but the CTA gates you behind joining AnthroPerks and downloading the app before you can even shop, which is real friction for a subscriber who just wants to click through to dresses. Below the fold it's the usual sprawling merchandising wall: eight lifestyle/category tiles, a "we picked these for you" grid, then a footer block. Competent execution, tired playbook.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "25% OFF DRESSES & JEWELRY" in large, high-contrast type against the purple confetti background.
- Single clear primary CTA button ("JOIN & SHOP NOW") sits directly under the offer with no scroll needed.
- No render bugs — text is crisp, images loaded, no overlap or broken assets anywhere in the frame.
- "PERKSFEST" branding ties back to the loyalty program consistently with the 8/16 send, giving the campaign some continuity.
- Good product variety below the fold (apparel, home, beauty, accessories) for browsers not sold on the hero offer.

## What's weak

- The offer is gated behind AnthroPerks membership and app download — that's two extra steps between "opened email" and "sees discount," which kills impulse clicks.
- "24-HR app exclusive" urgency badge feels bolted-on and generic — no countdown, no specific expiration time visible.
- Category is narrow (dresses & jewelry) but the CTA and hero make no visual distinction between the two, so it reads as one vague bucket rather than two shoppable categories.
- This is the third loyalty-gated or percent-off send in five days (8/16 Perksfest, 8/16 "30% Off ends TONIGHT," now this) — fatigue risk is real and nothing here signals it's a different campaign.
- Fine print for the offer ("25% off select dresses & jewelry") isn't visible anywhere near the hero — you'd need to scroll to the footer to learn it's not sitewide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the app-gate friction from the hero CTA — let "SHOP NOW" go straight to the dresses & jewelry category page, and handle the AnthroPerks upsell as a secondary link, not the only path to the discount.
- 2. Give the badge a real deadline instead of "24-HR app exclusive" — a literal countdown or "ends 8/21 at midnight" reads as credible urgency; a generic badge doesn't.
- 3. Split the hero into two visual halves (or two stacked CTAs) for dresses vs. jewelry so each category gets its own click target.
- 4. Since this is back-to-back with 8/16's Perksfest send, differentiate the creative — new photography or a different confetti/color treatment — so repeat subscribers don't mentally file it as a duplicate.
- 5. Surface the "select styles" qualifier near the hero, not buried in footer legal text, to keep the offer feeling honest.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Perksfest discount: 25% off dresses + jewelry`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join AnthroPerks free and unlock 25% off — no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New dresses, layered jewelry, all at a discount for members.`
