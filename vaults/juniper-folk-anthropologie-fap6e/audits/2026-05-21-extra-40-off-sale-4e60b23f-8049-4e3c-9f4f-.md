# Extra 40% off sale!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is now the fifth "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE" send from Anthropologie in roughly 72 hours, and the creative is essentially indistinguishable from the May 20th versions — same sailboat-and-confetti hero, same teal/cream category tile grid, same "30% off" secondary blocks underneath. The only thing the brand changed is the subject line punctuation. For a recipient who has now seen this exact promo land four times this week, fatigue is the dominant signal.
- That said, the email itself is well-built. The hero discount reads instantly, the category tiles (Dresses, Tops, Sale, Brands, Shoes, Bottoms) give clear entry points, and the lifestyle photography is on-brand summer. The "Summer Activities / Picnic Charms / Going Outing / Summer Beauty Edit" editorial sections give it more shopping surface than a pure promo.
- Judgement: still a solid 7-out-of-10 email creative — but the brand is asking it to do too much work. Four near-identical Memorial Day sends in one week means the ceiling is dropping each send. This one would land best as the genuine "tonight only" cap, not just another reminder.

## What's working

- Hero "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE" with sailboats and confetti reads in under a second — clear Memorial Day energy.
- Six teal category tiles below the hero are an excellent navigation pattern for a broad-assortment sale.
- Two "30% OFF" tiles add a second offer tier without confusing the primary message.
- Editorial sections (Summer Activities, Picnic Charms, Summer Beauty Edit) extend dwell time beyond the pure-promo block.
- App download + store locator + social row at the bottom is a clean utility footer.

## What's weak

- This is the 4th near-identical EXTRA 40% off creative in a week — the subscriber's eye now glides past it.
- Subject line is literally unchanged from the May 20th morning send except for the exclamation point — no escalation, no new hook.
- No visible end-time / countdown on the hero, which is what a "final hours" send needs to feel different.
- Category tiles all use the same teal block — no visual differentiation between "Dresses" (likely top-clicked) and "Brands."
- "Summer Activities," "Picnic Charms," "Going Outing" feel like filler editorial that dilutes the sale urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make this send feel like the close, not a repeat.** Stamp a visible "ENDS TONIGHT" or "FINAL HOURS" lockup on the hero — without it, this is indistinguishable from yesterday's email.
- 2. Cut the secondary "30% OFF" tiles. On the finale send they undercut the headline number; lead with one offer.
- 3. Drop two of the four editorial blocks — keep "Summer Beauty Edit" (it's the only one with distinct product), kill "Picnic Charms" and "Going Outing."
- 4. Reorder category tiles by past click data instead of a symmetric 2x3 grid — put Dresses and Sale at the top row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: extra 40% off sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day closes at midnight — extra 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One last scroll before the sale ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call on dresses, shoes, and summer styles under $40.`

# Technical Audit — Anthropologie "Extra 40% off sale!"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on the shared URBN multi-brand template with standard ESP infrastructure intact. Primary technical issues are missing alt text across all major images and unverified deliverability/unsubscribe headers in the captured payload.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 19 tracking/click-redirect links present (standard ESP wrapping); HTTP probe was skipped per audit policy, so destination validity is unverified.
- Third-party tracking pixels confirmed: `login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0` (Conversant/Epsilon) and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../052126_AN-US_MDWSOSLaunchResend/...` — note the "Resend" suffix in the campaign ID indicating this is a resend deployment.
- Evergage personalization endpoints present (`urbanoutfitters.us-1.evergage.com/api/dataset/anus_prod/campaign/aDihG/L6DU1/...`) — 9 summary.png variants suggest dynamic content slots.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **17 images missing `alt` attributes**, including the primary hero banners (`482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` — each appears twice, likely mobile/desktop variants) and module image `Evergreen-Email-ModuleBIGO_04.jpg`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will receive no fallback text.
- Tracking pixels (`summary.png`, `1_0`, `003a700000uTzaxAAC`) also lack `alt=""` — these should carry empty alt to be flagged decorative.
- CSS includes broken `font-family` declarations with empty entries: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal';` and `font-family: Helvetica, Arial, , 'Font-Normal';` — the double comma is invalid CSS and the `'Font-Normal'` family is undefined (only suffixed variants like `Font-Normal-ns` exist).
- `<title>` element is empty — some clients surface this in preview/accessibility tooling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Evergage userId (`003a700000uTza`) and Salesforce-style contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`) are populated in tracking pixels — merge tokens resolved correctly.
- Dotomi `dtm_email_hash=` parameter is **empty** in the captured source — should contain a hashed recipient identifier; suggests truncation or a broken merge variable.
- No visible unresolved `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` tokens in the captured HTML fragment.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** in the captured payload (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed; required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverifiable from this capture. From domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a standard ESP subdomain consistent with delegated DKIM.
- Physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer presence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Captured HTML is truncated before CTA destinations are visible; landing-page UTM parameters could not be evaluated.
- Click links are wrapped in ESP redirector domains (skipped from probe), so final UTM strings on `anthropologie.com` destinations are not inspectable from this capture.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text to all 17 flagged images**; use `alt=""` for the Evergage/Dotomi tracking pixels and decorative spacers, descriptive alt for hero banners and product modules.
2. **Fix the malformed `font-family` declarations** in the MSO conditional block and the comment example — remove the empty token between commas and either define the `'Font-Normal'` base family or remove it.
3. **Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter** in the Dotomi pixel; if the hash is meant to populate per-recipient, the merge variable is broken.
4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are actually emitted by the ESP — re-capture via direct MX rather than relay to rule out a capture-side gap. Critical for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
5. **Populate the `<title>` element** with the subject line or campaign name for accessibility.
6. Re-run audit against full (untruncated) source to evaluate CTA UTMs, CAN-SPAM footer, and the closing markup.
