# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I didn't even get to see the homepage — what loaded instead is a full bot-detection block. The Anthropologie wordmark is there, and then below it: "Access is temporarily restricted." That's it. No hero, no imagery, no navigation, no offers. For a brand I genuinely love and shop regularly, landing on this screen on my phone would have me immediately questioning whether the app is broken or if I've been flagged for something I didn't do.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark renders correctly and is immediately recognizable — at least I know I landed on the right domain.
- "Submit Feedback About Experience" is a link — marginally better than a dead end, though I would never use it.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is gated.** What I see is "Access is temporarily restricted" — not a single product, image, or offer loaded.
- **IP address exposed in the error copy** (`75.140.9.0`) — this is an unnecessary overshare that reads as cold and technical, not brand-appropriate at all.
- **Session ID exposed** (`ID: c3d79e33-78a1-e546-3e24-8c0c1073d1b2`) — same issue: surveillance-y, not the warm, textured Anthro aesthetic I expect.
- **Zero brand warmth.** The error message is dry IT-department copy on a white screen. If a friend opened this app in-store to show me a dress, this screen would make us both put the phone down.
- **No recovery path.** There's no "Tap here to continue," no CAPTCHA, no redirect to the app — just a list of reasons I might be a bot.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the block page with a soft challenge** — a simple CAPTCHA or "tap to confirm you're human" button in Anthro's visual style, with the logo, earthy palette, and brand-appropriate copy ("Let's make sure it's you — tap to continue shopping").
- **Remove the IP address and session ID from the visible error page.** Those belong in logs, not in front of shoppers. The current copy reads like a security warning, not a retail experience.
- **Add a deep-link fallback** — if the browser-based homepage is restricted, surface a "Open in the Anthropologie App" CTA so mobile users have somewhere to go.
- **Log this as a mobile funnel leak.** Any shopper hitting this on their phone while browsing casually is gone — they're not submitting feedback, they're just bouncing to Free People or Nordstrom.
