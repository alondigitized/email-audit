# 30% off: BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW with AnthroPerks
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, on-brand Black Friday send. The hero is a festive gift-tag graphic with a huge "30% OFF" and a "today only" sticker. The eye lands on the offer immediately, and the AnthroPerks and early-access framing tells members they get something others don't. Below it, the email is a long, polished grid of category tiles.
- The problem is a muddled offer stack. The hero says 30% off, then a second line says "+ EXTRA 40% OFF** SALE" with its own button. Both carry asterisks, and the fine print says the 30% excludes select items and is in-store and online. A shopper can't tell at a glance what 30% applies to or how it combines with 40%. The hero also has two stacked gold buttons (Clothing, Home & Furniture) plus a green one, so the primary action is split.
- Verdict: well-executed and visually premium, but it loses points for offer clarity and a scattered body. Solid mid-to-high performer for members, a bit confusing for everyone else.
- (The brand history supplied with this request describes a different brand's sends, with Skechers-style creative and promos. I haven't used it for cadence comparisons.)

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is enormous and unmissable, with a clear "Black Friday | Early Access" banner above it.
- The "today only" sticker adds credible urgency.
- Segmented CTAs ("Shop Clothing" and "Shop Home & Furniture") route shoppers by interest.
- The category tiles are clean and editorial, with consistent red label bars: dresses, kitchen, bottoms, holiday party, shoes, jewelry, holiday decor.
- The "Might we recommend…" product grid with "Top Rated" tags adds light social proof.
- The AnthroPerks membership framing makes the offer feel exclusive.

## What's weak

- Two stacked offers (30% off and extra 40% off sale) compete, and each has asterisks, so neither is clearly the headline.
- Three buttons in the first screen dilute the primary CTA.
- The fine print is dense and buried at the bottom, with exclusions, "online & in stores" caveats and sign-up requirements ("Sign up & save first").
- The body is very long, with about eight tiles and a product grid. The tiles have no prices or specific products, so the scroll rewards browsing, not buying.
- The "Top Rated" product tiles show no prices or product names, so nothing in them gives a reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the hero to one offer and one button, for example "30% off for AnthroPerks members, today only." Move the extra-40%-sale message to a thin secondary strip or drop it.
- 2. Add a short "what's included / what's excluded" line directly under the hero so the asterisks don't surprise people.
- 3. Cut the tile grid roughly in half and add price anchors to the product tiles, such as "from $48".
- 4. Show the member benefit visibly, with a "You're in: early access" line and a deadline time.
- 5. Make the subject and preheader work together. The subject carries the offer, and the preheader adds the deadline or the member perk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early access: 30% off for AnthroPerks members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday early access is open (today only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 30% on clothing, home & gifts. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign up free, shop early, and stack it with extra 40% off sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a few real code defects: malformed Outlook CSS, a viewport that blocks zoom, an empty `<title>`, and 5 images without alt text. Compliance headers and authentication results could not be verified, because the relay did not capture them and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect health and final URLs are unverified.
- **`dtm_email_hash=` is empty** in the Dotomi pixel: `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. This is either an unresolved or blank merge value, or a test-inbox artifact. The pixel can't match the recipient.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels** are present: `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAAC`. The second path embeds what looks like a CRM contact ID (`003…`), so a recipient identifier is exposed in a URL. Both pixels lack alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in the MSO conditional block:** `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family entry, and the stray `;` before `!important` makes the declaration invalid. Outlook will likely ignore the font override.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>`. This disables pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **5 images missing alt text:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - `1_0` (Dotomi pixel)
  - `003a700000uTzaxAAC` (mon-trk pixel)
  
  The first three are content or decorative images and need `alt=""` or descriptive text. The two pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Web fonts via `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')`.** Gmail and several other clients strip this, so those clients fall back to the declared stack. That is acceptable, but it adds a render-blocking external request in clients that honor it.
- **Plain-text part is >70% URLs** (8528 of 11574 chars). The plain-text alternative is dominated by raw tracking links and is poor for text-only readers. It is also a mild spam-filter signal.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear in the visible source.
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` value is the only possible personalization gap (see section 2).
- The source was truncated, so the body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found,** with the same caveat. Without it, one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain, but alignment can't be verified here.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible. The 44 links sit behind redirect domains and were skipped.
- Landing-page alignment and UTM persistence through the redirect could not be verified.
- The pixel path `112126_AN-US_PromoPerksEarlyAccessBFLaunch_AM_Nonmember` is the only campaign identifier visible in the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add alt text to the three content images, add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and populate `<title>`.
4. Check the Dotomi `dtm_email_hash` population with a real recipient, and confirm the CRM contact ID in the mon-trk pixel is intended.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against raw headers from a direct send, not the AgentMail relay, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 44 tracked links to confirm landing pages and UTM carry-through.
7. Rebuild the plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw URLs.
