# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Anthropologie is clearly in full summer-mode right now — "Summer Starts Here" with a 30% off sale on swim, sandals, and beauty anchors the whole above-fold experience. It feels like the brand I love doing what it always does in May: a clean sale banner, aspirational lifestyle imagery, and just enough Mediterranean-coastal energy to make me want to drop $200 on sandals I don't technically need. It's pretty but it's not doing much heavy lifting to keep *me* specifically.

## What's working

- **Top promo strip (Icon Juice Glasses, Buy 4 Get 20% off):** This is literally my category — I'm a registry shopper and a candle stockpiler. Home goods promos speak directly to me and this one's front and center.
- **"Summer Starts Here" hero timing:** It's May 13th, this is correct. The seasonal relevance is on point and the 30% off is a real number, not vague "savings."
- **"New!" image tile:** Two women in the New arrivals tile look like my people — boho-casual, Mediterranean backdrop, early 30s energy. This made me actually want to tap through.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** It's teal/dark, centered, very legible on mobile. No guessing what I'm supposed to do.
- **"Top Rated" tile:** Social proof baked into the tile label itself. I trust "Top Rated" as a shorthand for what the community loves.

## What's weak

- **No AnthroPerks hook anywhere:** I specifically shop with my AnthroPerks points and Anthro Day in mind. The fact that there's zero loyalty callout above the fold feels like a miss — this is their most loyal customer segment and they're not acknowledging me.
- **Hero is text-only, no model:** The blue hero banner has no person in it — it's a promotional text block. For a brand that sells on aesthetic and lifestyle aspiration, this feels like a missed opportunity to show me what summer at Anthropologie actually looks like on a woman.
- **The asterisk on 30% OFF*:** I can see the asterisk but I can't see the exclusions. As someone who's been burned by "up to X% off" asterisks that exclude my cart, this creates low-level friction right away.
- **Cookie consent bar blocks the image tiles:** The "We use cookies" banner at the bottom is covering the lower half of both "New!" and "Top Rated" tiles — I can't see what's in those images properly without dismissing it first. Friction.

## Recommendations

- **Put a lifestyle model in the hero:** Swap the text-only blue background for a shot of a woman in a patterned dress or boho summer look. The swim/sandals sale can stay, but show me the Anthro life, not just a blue rectangle.
- **Add an AnthroPerks surface above the fold:** Even a one-line "Perks members get an extra 10%" strip under the hero would make returning shoppers like me feel seen. Right now a first-time visitor and I see the exact same page.
- **Make the asterisk scannable:** Show "exclusions apply — see details" as a tappable link inline with the offer. Don't make me wonder what's excluded.
- **Dismiss-on-scroll or delay the cookie banner:** It's blocking the image tiles on load. A 2-second delay or auto-dismiss on scroll would let the hero tiles breathe.
