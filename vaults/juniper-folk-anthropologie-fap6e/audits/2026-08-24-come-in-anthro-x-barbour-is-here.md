# Come In: Anthro x Barbour is here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie stepping outside its usual "percent off" and "app-only Perksfest" playbook to run a genuine brand collaboration — Anthro x Barbour — and it's the most editorially confident send in this brand's recent stretch. No garish discount banner, no gated-access wall, just moody outdoor/countryside imagery (Land Rover, lake canoe, plaid dog bed) selling a lifestyle, not a coupon. Compared to 8/20's "Dresses & jewelry at 25% OFF" (5.00/10) or the broken banner on 8/16's "15% OFF" (4.00/10), this reads like a completely different, more premium brand voice.
- The catch: the only offer in the whole email is a "Friendly reminder: 15% off your full-price purchase of $150+" line buried in tiny gray text at the very top, and it's not even for this collection — it's a stale reminder banner. Everything below it is 100% lookbook, zero price anchors, zero urgency. For a persona who scans and clicks, that's a beautifully art-directed email with almost nothing pulling the eye toward a transaction. Strong brand email, weak conversion email — worth running occasionally, not as often as the promo blitzes that preceded it.

## What's working

- Clean collaboration framing — "Anthro x Barbour" lockup with FALL/2026 badges reads premium and immediately legible.
- Consistent olive/plaid/outdoor visual language carried through hero, lifestyle shots, and even the pet/home accessory section — no jarring creative shifts.
- No render bugs — every image loaded, no overlapping text, no broken banners (a real fix vs. 8/16's cut-off "15% OFF" send).
- Good category breadth without feeling cluttered — Barbour hero rolls into "More to Explore" (Trend Hub, Sweater Dresses, Suede Shop, Lip Edit) with clear section dividers.
- Multiple clear CTAs ("SHOP THE COLLECTION," "SHOP NEW STYLES," "SHOP EXCLUSIVE ARRIVALS," "SHOP NOW") each tied to a distinct visual, not one generic button repeated.

## What's weak

- The only discount in the email (15% off $150+) is a one-line gray "friendly reminder" above the logo — easy to miss and disconnected from the Barbour story it's sitting on top of.
- No price, no product name, no exclusivity framing on the actual Barbour pieces — "SHOP EXCLUSIVE ARRIVALS" doesn't say what's exclusive or why.
- Zero urgency anywhere — no deadline, no "ends tonight," nothing time-bound, which is a big swing from the cadence of the last two weeks.
- Very long scroll (11+ distinct sections) before reaching account/app footer — mobile readers will bail well before "The Lip Edit" or "Made With You In Mind."
- Subject and preview both undersell the collab — neither mentions Barbour is a recognizable heritage outerwear brand, which is the actual news hook here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Promote the Barbour name and one concrete product/price into the subject or hero — right now the value prop is "an exclusive collection" with no specifics; naming an item (e.g., a Barbour jacket) or a starting price would give readers a reason to open beyond aesthetics.
- 2. Move the 15% off $150+ callout down into the body as an actual banner near a CTA, not a gray afterthought above the logo — right now it's nearly invisible and disconnected from the collection it applies to.
- 3. Add one urgency element — a launch-week framing ("new this week") or limited-stock note for the collab, since Barbour collabs typically sell out and that's a legitimate, non-manufactured urgency signal.
- 4. Trim the email — cut 2-3 of the later "More to Explore" categories (Suede Shop, Lip Edit feel like filler unrelated to the Barbour theme) to keep focus on the collab and shorten the scroll.
- 5. Label at least one hero product with a name/price so the eye has something concrete to click toward, not just "shop the collection."

- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Barbour jackets, just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Waxed cotton, wool, and everything built for outside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus, still time to save 15% on $150+`
