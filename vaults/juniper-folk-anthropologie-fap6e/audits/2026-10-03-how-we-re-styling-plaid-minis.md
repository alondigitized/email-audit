# how we're styling plaid minis.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is an editorial lookbook with a promo bolted on partway down. The hero is strong: a woman under a clear "I ♥ NY" umbrella in plaid and a gray sweater, with a big lowercase "maeve" wordmark. The subject promises "how we're styling plaid minis," but the first screen shows one plaid look. The four-tile grid below it (split-toe flats, pink lip, red bold bag, holiday décor) is mostly accessories and props. The plaid-mini story is thin.
- The offer is the weak point. The "ENDS TONIGHT! | APP EXCLUSIVE / 20% OFF / WITH CODE: APP20" box sits about a third of the way down, after the editorial grid. It's the same APP20 offer as this morning's "Last call: 20% off ends tonight" send, so it reads as a second note on the same deadline. The subject mentions none of it. A subscriber who got the 10/03 "Last call" email and the 9/30 "20% off exclusively in the app" email has seen this offer three times in four days.
- Verdict: pretty and on-brand, but unfocused. It splits attention between a plaid-mini story, a 20% app code, and five category tiles with a 9-up product grid. It's a 5 at best.

## What's working

- The hero is striking and distinctive. The umbrella and "I ♥ NY" give it a street-style feel, and the large maeve wordmark makes the sender clear.
- Lowercase captions with a small logo bullet ("add split toe flats", "try a pink lip") keep the voice consistent and conversational.
- The 2x2 grid is visually varied and easy to scan.
- The APP20 box is high-contrast, with maroon "20% OFF" and a clear "ENDS TONIGHT!" tag. When you reach it, it's unambiguous.
- The lower modules ("More to Explore", "More with your name on it") give category entry points, and the "Even more to love" footer adds useful perks: $20 off a new pair, Klarna and inclusive sizing.

## What's weak

- The plaid-mini promise is only partly delivered. Only the hero and one tile show plaid minis, and the other tiles are shoes, lipstick, a bag and candles.
- The offer is buried below the editorial grid and is not in the subject or hero. The "ends tonight" urgency arrives late.
- The two links under the offer ("SHOP NEW CLOTHING" / "SHOP NEW HOME") are small text links, not buttons. There's no strong primary CTA anywhere.
- It's far too long at about 5,700px. Five category tiles plus a nine-product grid with no prices or names dilutes the message.
- The product grid has no prices, names or reviews, so there's no product detail to act on.
- The fine print is tiny and dense: app-only, iOS and Android, $150+ shipping conditions and exclusions. The "20% off" is only for app orders, which makes it feel less open than the headline suggests.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either make this a plaid-mini edit (4 to 6 plaid minis with prices, linked to a plaid collection) or lead with APP20. Don't do both in the same send.
- 2. If the offer stays, move the APP20 box directly under the hero and add a real button, such as "Shop plaid minis in the app."
- 3. Link each editorial tile to a specific product or collection, and add a price and name to the product grid.
- 4. Cut the five category tiles or the nine-up grid. Pick one.
- 5. Mention the deadline in the preheader. Today's earlier "Last call" send already used the urgency, so don't make this the third beat of the same message. Suppress or segment people who already got it.
- 6. Keep the app-only condition visible near the code, not only in the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `plaid minis, styled 4 ways (+ 20% off in the app)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Split-toe flats, a pink lip and a bold red bag. Use code APP20 tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our favorite plaid mini looks, plus 20% off app orders ends tonight.`

# Technical Audit: "how we're styling plaid minis." (maeve by Anthropologie)

## 1. Technical Summary
The Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`) has several code-level defects: an invalid Outlook font declaration, a viewport that blocks zoom, an empty `<title>`, and 7 images without alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is also truncated, so body-level checks were limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter in a pixel.** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends with `dtm_email_hash=` and no value. Either the hash is deliberately blank for this test inbox, or the merge failed. I can't tell which from the source.
- **Third-party tracking pixels.** There are two: `login.dotomi.com` (Dotomi/Conversant) and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../100326_AN-US_DressesMaeveCheckmatePlaidMini/003a700000uTzax…`. The second embeds what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003a…` prefix) in the path. That is a per-recipient identifier exposed in an image URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images (2 are tracking pixels).**
  - Content images:
    - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (appears twice)
    - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (appears twice)
    - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - Tracking pixels: dotomi `1_0` and mon-trk `003a700000uTzax…`. These should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
  - The banner images are repeated (a mobile/desktop pair or a duplicate), and neither copy has alt text.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` gives screen readers and web-view clients nothing to announce.
- **Invalid MSO font rule.** In `<!--[if mso]>`, `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has two problems:
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) makes the declaration invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` orphans it.
  - The Outlook-only font stack is therefore likely dropped.
- **Web-font loading.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?…')` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` fallbacks to Helvetica, Arial and Georgia are listed, so the impact is limited. The duplicate `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is harmless.
- **Stylesheet bloat.** Rules for other brands are inlined into this send: UO, Free People, Beholden, Terrain and Community. This adds weight and increases the risk of Gmail's ~102 KB clipping. Whether the message clips can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- *** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE *** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` value (see section 2) is the only possible unresolved-token indicator in the provided source.
- No visible `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` placeholders were found in the truncated source. The remainder of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA flagged this as possibly not captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click support can't be confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay doesn't expose it. Don't treat this as a failure.
- **Body-level items not verifiable.** The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 9,576 of 12,767 characters (75%) are URLs, mostly long tracking links. This can raise spam-filter scores and makes the text version hard to read.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** All 42 links use redirect domains and were skipped.
- **Campaign slug aligns with the subject.** The slug in the mon-trk pixel, `100326_AN-US_DressesMaeveCheckmatePlaidMini`, matches "plaid minis". Whether the landing pages match is not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three content images and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Populate `<title>` (for example, with the subject line).
4. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
5. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
6. Trim unused brand font and class CSS to reduce size and clipping risk.
7. Confirm the `dtm_email_hash` population with the sender or ESP.
8. Re-test headers against the raw `.eml` or a direct seed inbox to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
9. Resolve the redirect chains with a tracking-aware probe, so that destination URLs and UTM parameters can be audited.
10. Shorten the plain-text alternative's URLs or trim its link count.
