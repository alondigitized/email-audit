# Extra 50% off sale won’t be here for long
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Anthropologie Home sale send in two days. It reuses the same boxed olive-and-cream hero as the two Oct 6 emails ("Up to 30% off home + extra 50% off sale" and "Take an extra 50% off sale styles"). The subject's urgency is mild ("won't be here for long"), and the "limited time!" script in the hero isn't backed by a date. The hero is clear: a big olive "EXTRA 50% OFF* SALE →" block, a "+" divider, then "UP TO 30% OFF** HOME →".
- The body is weaker than the hero. A colorful category grid (Kitchen & Dining, Candles & Gifts, Rugs & Curtains and so on) follows the hero. After it come six editorial tiles that mix home and non-home: Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets. Then comes a "These are so you, too" grid of nine apparel photos. A home-focused subscriber gets little product here that matches the offer. The deal is easy to read, but nothing in the email tells me what to buy.
- Judgement: a solid offer-first hero followed by a diffuse body. Cadence is the main problem, since this is nearly the same email as yesterday's two.

## What's working

- The hero puts both offers at the top in large type, so the eye lands on the deal first.
- The two offers are separated cleanly by the "+" divider, and each has its own arrow CTA.
- The colored category tiles are scannable and give a direct route into home subcategories.
- Brand voice and typography are consistent and instantly recognizable as Anthropologie Home.
- The footer carries useful extras: SMS signup for 10% off, Afterpay, inclusive sizing and styling services. The legal fine print is complete.

## What's weak

- The cadence is stale. This is the third send in two days with the same hero and the same 50%/30% offers, only reordered.
- "Limited time!" has no deadline, so the urgency in the hero and subject feels empty.
- The editorial tiles are off-focus for a home shopper: Watches, Cashmere, Trend Hub and Beauty Gift Sets.
- The nine-product apparel grid at the bottom has no names, prices or CTAs, and it has nothing to do with home.
- The offer is wrapped in asterisks, with exclusions in small gray print at the bottom. The "extra 50% off" applies only to select sale items, which the hero doesn't make clear.
- The category grid and the content tiles have no product imagery, so nothing shows what the sale actually includes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the editorial and apparel tiles with 6–8 actual sale home products showing sale price and was-price. This is the biggest lift, because it gives the 50% offer something concrete to click.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown, in place of "limited time!".
- 3. Don't send three near-identical promos in two days. Vary the creative, or hold the send until there is a new reason to write.
- 4. Add a one-line qualifier under the hero, such as "On select sale styles", so the asterisk doesn't feel like a trap.
- 5. Cut the apparel grid or segment it so home subscribers don't see it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 30% off home. Sale picks for every room, through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has several code-level defects: malformed Outlook CSS, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, an empty `<title>`, and missing alt text on 7 images. The QA run couldn't verify authentication, unsubscribe headers or any of the 43 tracked links, so those areas are unconfirmed rather than clean.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 43 links were skipped because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. The QA run did not check whether any resolve, so link health is unknown.
- **Empty hash parameter:** the Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. It ends with an empty `dtm_email_hash=`, so the value was blank at render time. Either the hash wasn't populated for this recipient or the merge failed, and the pixel can't match the user.
- **Identifier in the URL:** the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path contains what looks like a CRM contact ID (`003a700000uTzaxAAC`). This is a recipient-level identifier in a third-party URL, which is worth a privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It contains an empty list item, and the stray `;` before `!important` leaves the priority flag orphaned, so the font override is likely invalid in Outlook.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` has no content.
- **Missing alt text (7 images):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (appears twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (appears twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the Dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - the mon-trk pixel `003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  The two pixels should have `alt=""`, and the content images need real alt text or an explicit empty alt.
- **Quoted generic families:** font stacks such as `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` are quoted. Quoted, they are read as font names rather than generic keywords, so the fallback never applies.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. That can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Font loading:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` loads 8 font families. `@import` in `<style>` is unsupported or stripped in several clients, and it adds blocking requests where it works.
- **Duplicate rule:** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Blank `dtm_email_hash=`:** see Section 2. This is the only evidence of an unresolved or empty merge value in the visible source.
- **Not checked:** the HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm whether any `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens remain in the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe header:** QA flagged it as not found. It may just not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **No List-Unsubscribe-Post header:** QA flagged it as not found (RFC 8058), so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **No Authentication-Results header:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `s.anthropologie.com` should be verified directly.
- **Footer not visible:** the visible-in-body unsubscribe link and postal address can't be confirmed because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** all 43 links are tracking redirects that weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Extra 50% off sale" offer can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO font-family declaration. Remove the empty entry and the stray `;`, giving `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add alt text to the 5 content images, and `alt=""` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
4. Populate `<title>` and move the template comment below the DOCTYPE.
5. Unquote the generic families (`sans-serif`, `serif`), remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule, and replace the `@import` with a `<link>` or a reduced font set.
6. Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=` for real sends, and review the CRM ID in the mon-trk pixel path.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw SMTP headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
8. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify UTMs and landing pages, and shorten the plain-text part (about 70% URLs) with a trimmed plain-text version.
