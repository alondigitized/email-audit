# 15% OFF b/c we’re into you, for real…
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie's fourth straight-percentage-off send in ten days, and it's the weakest execution of the stretch by a mile — not because the offer is bad, but because the hero banner is visibly broken. The giant "15% off — 15% of..." headline runs off the left and right edges of the frame, with duplicated/overlapping "5% off" text bleeding out of view. That's the first thing the eye hits, and it reads as a rendering failure before it reads as an offer.
- Underneath the broken hero, the email is competent but unremarkable: a $150+ spend threshold, a checkout code, two clean CTA bars, and the usual "get yourself something special" model grid. Compared to August 9's "Extra 50% Off Sale ends tonight!" (9/10) or even the August 12 "30% OFF" send (5/10), this is a smaller discount with a higher spend gate, wrapped in a template that visibly failed QA.
- Judgement: this shouldn't have shipped in this state. The offer itself is mediocre for the brand's own recent cadence, and the broken hero graphic actively undercuts whatever click intent the subject line generates.

## What's working

- Clear secondary CTA structure — "shop new clothing & accessories" and "shop new home" are both unambiguous, well-contrasted buttons.
- Promo code (MHG52HPAAD) and "online & in stores" redemption info are both visible without hunting.
- Lifestyle photography (three outfit shots) is on-brand and shows real product in context.
- Loyalty-adjacent modules (reviews-for-discount, gift/styling consult, curbside pickup) add utility below the fold for engaged subscribers.

## What's weak

- The hero discount graphic is broken: "15% off" text is cut off at both edges and appears to duplicate/overlap ("5% off — 15% of..."), which is a hard render bug on the single most important line in the email.
- $150+ spend threshold for only 15% off is a weak value prop next to this brand's own July 29–August 9 run of 30–50% off sends with lower or no minimums — steep cadence drop-off a subscriber will notice.
- No urgency or expiration is visible anywhere in the rendered body (the deadline is buried in fine print, not shown on screen), unlike the "ends tonight" framing that made the August 9 send strong.
- Headline copy ("The marker of a beautiful friendship... Well, benefits...") is vague filler that delays getting to the actual offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero banner rendering immediately — the "15% off" graphic must be fully contained in-frame with no clipping or duplicated text; this is the single highest-impact fix since it's the visual anchor of the whole email.
- 2. Put a visible deadline in the hero (e.g., "through 9/14") instead of burying it in the footnote — borrow the urgency mechanic from the August 9 "ends tonight" send that scored 9/10.
- 3. Either drop the $150 minimum or raise the discount to match recent cadence (20–30%) — 15% off with a $150 floor is a step down subscribers will feel after two weeks of deeper cuts.
- 4. Trim the top copy block to one line so the offer is legible in the first screen's-worth of scroll on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off $150+, ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your invite: 15% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code MHG52HPAAD — online & in stores through 9/14`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals just dropped, 15% off when you spend $150+`
