# Up to 30% OFF furniture, Halloween & décor
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Anthropologie Home running a straight percent-off sale — "Up to 30% OFF furniture, Halloween & décor" — with a bold, colorful hero (a big bubble-letter "30% OFF" in mustard/coral/plum) sitting above a "limited time" badge and a category-chip nav (Halloween, Furniture, Bath, Lighting, Mirrors, Rugs & Curtains, Bedding). It's functionally the same discount mechanic the brand has leaned on since July 29 and 31, just re-skinned for home goods and Halloween instead of apparel/getaway/beauty.
- The top third does the job: clear offer, clear CTA, clean category grid. Where it falls apart is the endless scroll after that — nine unlabeled product tiles, then five more full-width "explore" modules (Halloween Shop, Fall Shop, Drinkware, Free Design Services, a terrain plug) with zero pricing or specificity, turning a sale email into a full catalog browse. By the time you'd hit "terrain," the 30%-off hook is long forgotten.
- Solid top-of-funnel execution, weak follow-through — this reads like a template stuffed with every merchandising module the team had on hand rather than a curated sale send.

## What's working

- The hero is unmissable — large, high-contrast bubble typography makes "30% OFF" the first and only thing the eye lands on.
- "Limited time" badge plus asterisk on the offer signals urgency without being shouty.
- Category chips (Halloween, Furniture, Bath, Lighting, Mirrors, Rugs & Curtains, Bedding) let a shopper self-select fast — good for a home-goods assortment this wide.
- Single, clearly-labeled "SHOP NOW" button in a color that pops against the cream hero background.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The email is exhaustingly long — after the hero and category grid, it's five more full-bleed modules (Halloween Shop, Fall Shop, Drinkware, Free Design Services, terrain) with no discount tie-in, diluting the sale message.
- The nine-tile product grid has zero labels, prices, or discount call-outs — a candle, two mugs, a runner, curtains — nothing tells you what's on sale or by how much.
- "Free Design Services" and the terrain cross-promo module have nothing to do with the 30%-off headline and read as filler.
- No mention of AnthroPerks/loyalty tie-in despite the brand gating offers behind it as recently as Aug 2's "Today ONLY! AnthroPerks Early Access."
- Fine print at the bottom ("select category," "not valid on select brands," "excludes gift cards") isn't visible until you scroll all the way past the catalog — buried relative to the loud 30% claim up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email roughly in half.** Drop or consolidate the Fall Shop / Drinkware / Free Design Services / terrain modules into a single "more to explore" strip — right now the sale gets buried under five unrelated browse modules.
- 2. **Label the product grid.** Add product name + price (or "30% off" badge) under each of the nine tiles so it doubles as a shoppable sale grid instead of a mystery mood board.
- 3. **Repeat the CTA lower in the email.** After the category chips there's no second "Shop Now" until the reader has scrolled past a dozen images — add one after the product grid.
- 4. **Surface the exclusions near the offer, not at the footer.** A one-line "excludes select brands" under the hero builds trust versus burying it after ten scrolls.
- 5. **Tie Halloween in visually, not just as a category chip.** Given the subject leads with it, the Halloween Shop module (candy apples on a tray) is six scrolls down — move it up near the hero if it's meant to be a co-lead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off: Halloween decor is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Furniture & Halloween, up to 30% off (ends soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Trick-or-treat trays, cozy throws, statement lighting — all on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Halloween arrivals join the home sale, for a limited time.`
