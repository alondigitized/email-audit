# 30% OFF on Pilcro, Maeve & MORE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about three days. It reuses the plaid-edged gift-tag hero with the giant "30% OFF" from the 11/21 to 11/23 emails, and it keeps the "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" banner from today's earlier sends ("Black Friday = EXTRA 40% OFF SALE", "Extra 40% off sale for BLACK FRIDAY ✨"). The offer is instantly legible. The three gold buttons (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts) are an obvious next step.
- The body is a tidy, editorial category grid in the brand's green and cream palette, and it makes the email feel like a well-built catalog. Two offers stack at the top, though: 30% off and "extra 40%" on sale. Both carry asterisks, and the fine print sits at the very bottom. Past the fold the email turns into generic category tiles and a "Might we recommend…" row with no prices.
- My judgement is that this is solid and on brand, but it adds nothing new. The subject names brands (Pilcro, Maeve), and the body never shows those brands. That mismatch is the biggest miss.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF" on the tag is the first thing the eye lands on, so the offer hierarchy is clear.
- The three gold hero buttons split intent by category (clothing, home, gifts) and are easy to tap.
- The "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" banner with its own "Shop Clothing" button gives a second, stronger hook.
- The category tiles (dresses, furniture, party shop, shoes, jewelry, decor, beauty) use clean, consistent imagery and green label bars. This is easy to scan.
- The render shows no broken images or overlapping text, and the holiday styling fits the season.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Pilcro, Maeve & MORE", but I can't see either brand named or featured in the render. Subject and body don't match.
- Two overlapping offers (30% off and extra 40% off sale) with asterisks leave the actual deal unclear. The exclusions sit in tiny text at the bottom.
- The top marquee strip is clipped ("IDAY STARTS NOW! · BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! · BL"), and it says "starts now" on a send that is days into the sale.
- The "Might we recommend…" grid shows star ratings but no prices, product names or sale pricing. That wastes the strongest click moment.
- The creative is recycled from the earlier sends, so there is real fatigue and little reason to open this one over the others.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Feature Pilcro and Maeve in a dedicated hero tile with a "Shop Pilcro" and "Shop Maeve" button, or change the subject to match what the email shows.
- 2. Collapse the two offers into one clear statement. "30% off everything, extra 40% off sale" is easier to take in than two stacked tags.
- 3. Add prices and sale prices under the recommended products, and link each to its product page.
- 4. Swap the creative at least once across the sends, with a new hero or a countdown, so it doesn't read as a repeat.
- 5. Fix the marquee so it doesn't clip, and drop "starts now" language that is stale.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pilcro & Maeve are 30% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday picks: denim, dresses, 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 40% off sale styles. Ends Cyber Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh arrivals from Maeve and Pilcro, marked down for the weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based Anthropologie template. The QA run shows 0 errors and 4 warnings. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on 5 images, a malformed Outlook-only CSS rule, and viewport settings that block zoom. The compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed from the data captured. The source was truncated, so footer and link markup couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links were not verified.** 42 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so the status and final destination of every CTA are unconfirmed.
- **The Dotomi pixel has an empty email-hash parameter.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The empty value is either an unresolved merge field or a deliberately blank value for this recipient. If it is a merge failure, retargeting attribution for this send breaks.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8096 of 10929 characters (74%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. Long tracking URLs in the text alternative can hurt spam scoring and make the text part unreadable.
- **Two third-party tracking pixels load on open.** These are `login.dotomi.com` (Dotomi/Conversant) and `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112326_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder1_MID/003a700...`. Neither can be verified from the QA data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **5 images are missing alt text:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the Dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel `003a700000uTzaxAAC`
- The two tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`. The first three are on `image.s.anthropologie.com`. The `pencilline` banners are probably decorative and should also use `alt=""`. If they carry content, they need descriptive alt text. Without the attribute, screen readers may announce the filename.
- **The Outlook-only CSS rule is malformed.** The rule inside `<!--[if mso]>` is `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`.
  - It has an empty font-family list entry (`Helvetica, ,`).
  - The `!important` sits after the semicolon, which makes it an invalid declaration. The intended override doesn't take effect in Outlook.
  - `'Font-Normal'` isn't a defined font.
- **The viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This is minor, but it affects the web-view and accessibility title.
- **An HTML comment precedes the DOCTYPE.** The `<!-- ... ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ... -->` comment comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. Some older clients may switch to quirks mode.
- **Web fonts depend on `@import`.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` inside `<style>` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `font-family` fallbacks (Helvetica, Arial, Georgia) cover this, so it's low severity. The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` selector is also defined twice, which is harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only evidence is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` in the Dotomi pixel (see section 2). The truncated source shows no visible unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[%…%]` tokens. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed.
- **The footer wasn't visible.** The postal address and in-body unsubscribe link weren't in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and destinations can't be confirmed.** All 42 click links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated source shows no resolved destination URLs.
- The tracking pixel name `PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder1_MID` indicates a Black Friday reminder campaign. It doesn't prove anything about landing page alignment with the "30% OFF on Pilcro, Maeve & MORE!" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the decorative `pencilline` banners and the 1x1 tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to `1e313de8-….png` if it carries content.
2. Fix the Outlook rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray `!important;`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Fill in `<title>` and move the template comment below the DOCTYPE.
5. Check why `dtm_email_hash=` is empty in the Dotomi pixel. Confirm whether it's expected for this recipient or a merge failure.
6. Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part to bring URL content under 70%.
7. Re-run with raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) captured from the original message rather than the relay. Use a resolver that follows the 42 tracking redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
