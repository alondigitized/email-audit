# Up to 30% off Jeffrey Campbell, Gola, Maeve & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first Anthropologie sale blast. It is also the fifth send in five days built on the same promo. The boxed cream hero stacks three colour-blocked offers: 30% off fashion (online only), 30% off home, and an extra 50% off sale. The hierarchy is clear and you see the discount before anything else. It follows "Take up to 30% off fashion during the Feels Like Fall Sale" (Oct 9) and "Up to 30% off fashion is here!" (Oct 8) almost beat for beat.
- The subject promises a brand lineup (Jeffrey Campbell, Gola, Maeve) that the render never delivers. I can't find those names or any footwear-led creative in the body. The hero is generic "Feels Like Fall Sale" and the tiles are broad shops like Trend Hub, Cashmere Shop and Watches. A subscriber who opens for shoes or Maeve lands on a general sale email. That mismatch is the main problem.
- Verdict: competent execution, tired cadence, and a subject that overpromises. It will perform like the earlier sends, not better.

## What's working

- The three stacked offers (30% fashion, 30% home, extra 50% sale) each have a distinct colour block. The savings read in a second.
- The "Feels Like Fall" framing fits the season and the brand's voice.
- The editorial tile grid (Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets) gives several category routes without clutter.
- The "These are so you, too" product grid adds shoppable items below the fold.
- Nothing is broken. Images load and the layout is consistent.

## What's weak

- The subject names Jeffrey Campbell, Gola and Maeve, but none of them appears in the visible email. This is a bait-and-switch feel.
- This is the fifth near-identical sale send in five days (Oct 6–10). The hero is unchanged and the urgency has worn thin.
- "Limited time!" has no end date or deadline, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The offers link to broad categories ("Fashion →", "Home →", "Sale →"). There is no specific product and no clear button.
- Three tiers of asterisked fine print sit at the bottom. The "online only" vs. "online & in stores" split is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero match. If the subject names Jeffrey Campbell, Gola and Maeve, lead the hero with a shoe or brand-led image and link straight to those brand pages.
- 2. Cut the cadence or rotate the creative. Don't send the same hero again, and give this send a distinct reason to exist, such as a brand spotlight.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday" or "Ends Oct 12", and put the 50% off sale in the subject.
- 4. Add a solid CTA button in the hero, such as "Shop shoes", instead of arrow-only text links.
- 5. Put the fine print for the online-only fashion offer in the hero itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Jeffrey Campbell, Gola & Maeve: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall sale ends Sunday: extra 50% off sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boots, sneakers and Maeve dresses are marked down through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 30% off home and an extra 50% off sale. Online and in stores.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ESP template (`xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o` VML namespaces, MSO conditionals) from `s.anthropologie.com`. The QA run found no hard failures, but 7 image alt-text warnings and a few CSS/markup defects. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the relay (see section 5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links.** 37 links point to tracking/click-redirect domains, and the QA probe skipped all of them. Their destinations and status are unverified.
- **Empty hash parameter.** The Dotomi pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. It ends in an empty `dtm_email_hash=`, so either the hash merge field resolved to nothing or the test recipient had no hash. Confirm which, because an empty value breaks the audience match the pixel exists for.
- **Third-party pixels.** Two are present, `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../101026_AN-US_Combo30OffFashion30OffHome50OffSOSReminder...`. Both are outside the first-party domain and are used for retargeting. Neither has an `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (7 QA warnings, 5 unique images):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (each flagged twice, so they are repeated in the markup).
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`.
  - The two tracking pixels. These should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom blocked.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The MSO block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. The empty list item (`, ,`) and the stray `; !important;` mean the `!important` is dropped, so the Arial override doesn't apply as intended.
- **Quoted generic families.** The font stacks use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes (e.g. `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"]`). Quoted, they are treated as font names rather than generic keywords. The Helvetica/Arial entries before them limit the impact.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice.
- **Comment before the doctype.** The HTML comment `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients. It may be an artifact of how the source was captured.
- **Web fonts.** Google Fonts are loaded with `@import` in `<style>`, which Gmail and several other clients ignore. The fallback stacks cover this.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Probable unresolved token.** `dtm_email_hash=` is empty (see section 2).
- **Truncated source.** No other merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible, but the HTML was truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Inconclusive, not confirmed defects.**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. Verify against a raw `.eml` from a direct-delivery test before treating them as sender failures.
- **Body footer not checked.** The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Cannot verify.** All 37 links go through click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.
- **Campaign identifier.** The pixel path includes the campaign ID `101026_AN-US_Combo30OffFashion30OffHome50Off…`. It matches the offer in the subject ("Up to 30% off…"), which is a good sign but not proof of landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three content images (the two pencilline banners and `1e313de8-….png`) and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to both tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Drop the empty list item and the stray `;`.
4. Use unquoted `sans-serif` and `serif`, remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule, and fill in `<title>`.
5. Move the template comment after the doctype if the template really emits it first.
6. Investigate why `dtm_email_hash` is empty. Check whether the seed address lacks a hash or the merge is failing.
7. Re-test headers using a raw `.eml` captured without the relay, to settle `List-Unsubscribe`, one-click and `Authentication-Results`.
8. Resolve the 37 tracking links with a redirect-following check to confirm the destinations return 200 and carry the expected UTMs.
