# Extra 40% OFF sale!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is now the sixth "Extra 40% off sale" send from Anthropologie in about four days, and creatively it's the same sailboat-and-confetti hero we've already seen on May 20th and 21st. The only meaningful structural change is below the fold: this version pads out the body with a category exploration grid ("Ways to Explore" — The Trend Hub, The Color Shop, Summer Activities, Phone Charms, Going Analog, Summer Beauty Edit) and a "From and to you too" social-style product strip, which gives it more substance than yesterday's near-duplicate.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF" framing is a soft downgrade from prior sends that read "EXTRA 40% OFF" — "up to" always reads weaker because it implies most items aren't actually at the headline number. For a subscriber who has now seen this exact hero five times, fatigue is the dominant signal.
- Net: the lifestyle/editorial scroll is the most interesting part of the email, but it's buried under a hero that subscribers have already dismissed multiple times this week. Mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The "Ways to Explore" tile grid is genuinely fresh content — Color Shop, Phone Charms, Going Analog read like editorial curation, not a sale flyer.
- Category quick-links under the hero (Sale Tops, Sale Dresses, Sale Shoes, etc.) give immediate paths into the sale by department.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and seasonal — bright yellow swim look, watermelon scene, picnic table — on-brand summer mood.
- Footer includes app download CTAs (App Store/Google Play), which is a sensible monetization layer.

## What's weak

- This is the sixth nearly identical "40% off sale" hero in four days — fatigue is severe.
- "UP TO 40% OFF" is weaker than the prior "EXTRA 40% OFF" framing and feels like a quiet downgrade.
- Subject line is verbatim a repeat of the May 21st 6/10 send.
- No model-shot lead for the apparel sale itself — the hero is illustration, so the actual product the subject is selling is below the scroll.
- The "From $you to you too" strip feels like a UI label that didn't render cleanly — copy is ambiguous.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the recycled sailboat hero. After six sends, replace it with one of the editorial tiles ("Phone Charms" or "Color Shop") as the hero — that's the differentiated content here.
- 2. Lead with a category, not the percentage. "Sale dresses under $80" or "Swim sale, 40% off" gives a reason this send is different from the last five.
- 3. Drop "UP TO" — restore "EXTRA 40% OFF" if true, since you've been training subscribers on that exact phrase all week.
- 4. Cap the cadence. A sixth identical-hero send in four days is past the point of diminishing returns; the next promo should look visually distinct.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Color Shop is open (+ 40% off sale)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Phone charms, picnic-ready dresses, and a Memorial Day markdown.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Editorial picks now in the sale — ends Monday.`

- Multiple third-party tracking pixels detected (`urbanoutfitters.us-1.evergage.com`, `login.dotomi.com`, `pxl.mon-trk.com`) — all functional but lack alt text (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **17 images missing `alt` attributes**, including primary creative (`482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`, `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`) and 9 Evergage `summary.png` personalization tiles. Screen readers will announce nothing for the hero banners.
- Document `<title>` element is empty (`<title></title>`) — preview/tab context lost in webmail clients.
- Duplicate CSS selector: `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` declared twice in the `<style>` block (Open Sans). Harmless but indicates template hygiene issue.
- Malformed Outlook MSO font stack: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal';` contains an empty token and stray semicolon before `!important` (`'Font-Normal'; !important;`) — invalid CSS that Outlook may discard entirely.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) header not detected — Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirement may not be met. This is a hard requirement for senders >5k/day to Gmail since Feb 2024.

- Campaign ID `052226_AN-US_MDWComboLimitedTime_VersionB` suggests an A/B test ("VersionB") — confirm variant attribution flows through to site analytics.

1. **Fix deliverability blocker:** generate a real plain-text alternative (currently 0 chars) — Gmail/Outlook spam filters penalize text/plain-less multipart messages.

4. **Populate `<title>`** with the subject line or campaign name for webmail/preview pane context.

6. **Clean up MSO conditional CSS:** remove empty token and stray semicolon from `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
