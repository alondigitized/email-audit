# Extra 40% off sale & up to 30% off home
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth "Extra 40% off sale" send from Anthropologie in about a week, and the sailboat-and-confetti hero is once again front and center — virtually identical to the May 20th through 24th creatives. The only meaningful add is a sub-hero "Up to 30% off indoor & outdoor furniture, décor & more!" panel and a Memorial Day-adjacent home angle.
- The body does try harder than recent sends: a colorful umbrella-and-towel lifestyle shot, named shoppable modules ("The Travel Edit," "The Cure Shop," "Summer Activities," "Pienza Classics," "Going Boating," "Summer Beauty Edit"), and a "Most-recommended" carousel anchor the lower fold. Visually it's lively and on-brand, but the cadence problem dominates everything.
- Verdict: a passable creative buried under sale fatigue. For a Walker-persona subscriber, ten near-identical hero treatments in a week trains the eye to skip. The home angle is a smart hook for the Memorial Day weekend but it's stacked under the same 40% off banner everyone has already ignored four times.

## What's working

- Lifestyle umbrella/beach photo gives the email warmth and a clear summer-weekend mood.
- Named shoppable modules ("The Travel Edit," "Going Boating," "Summer Beauty Edit") give multiple click entry points instead of one generic CTA.
- The "Up to 30% off home" sub-hero is a real second offer, not a recycled coupon.
- "Most-recommended" social-proof strip at the bottom is a credible click target.
- Color palette (yellow + sailboat hero + soft pastel modules) is cohesive and brand-recognizable.

## What's weak

- Tenth near-identical "Extra 40% off sale" send in a week — banner blindness is severe by now.
- Hero confetti-and-sailboat illustration is the same one used May 20–24; no creative refresh.
- Sale CTA buttons ("SHOP NOW" twice stacked) compete with each other inside the same hero block.
- Category-row chips under the hero are visually cluttered and hard to scan at thumb-distance.
- Subject and preheader are essentially identical to multiple prior sends — nothing tells the subscriber "this one is different."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the home/Memorial Day angle, not the 40% off sale. After ten sale sends, the home offer is the only new news.
- 2. Replace the sailboat hero with a Memorial Day weekend outdoor-living photo — patio, umbrella, table set. Make the creative match the new lead offer.
- 3. Collapse the two stacked SHOP NOW buttons into one. Two CTAs inside one hero dilute the click.
- 4. Move "Most-recommended" higher — social proof above the module grid earns more clicks than buried at the bottom.
- 5. Cut category chips to four max. Six is too dense on mobile.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Patio season starts here — up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Indoor + outdoor furniture, just in time for the long weekend.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — Anthropologie "Extra 40% off sale & up to 30% off home"

## 1. Technical Summary
Production-grade ESP template (Salesforce/ExactTarget-style relay via `s.anthropologie.com`) with solid cross-client scaffolding (MSO conditionals, font-fallback stack), but failing on three concrete deliverability/accessibility items: missing plain-text part, missing List-Unsubscribe headers in captured copy, and ~15 images with no alt attribute.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 19 tracking/redirect links present and skipped from probing — expected for a Sailthru/ExactTarget-class send, but means broken destination URLs cannot be confirmed without unwrapping.
- Tracking pixels confirmed from three vendors stacked in one send: `image.s.anthropologie.com` (ESP), `urbanoutfitters.us-1.evergage.com` (Salesforce Personalization/Evergage), `login.dotomi.com` (Conversant/Epsilon), and `pxl.mon-trk.com` (Monetate). This is a high vendor count and increases pixel-block / privacy-blocker exposure.
- The Evergage `summary.png` pixel is fetched 9 times with sequential path segments `/1/` … `/9/` — likely intentional impression slicing per module, but worth confirming it isn't a duplicate-load bug.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>` — accessibility/screen-reader regression and bad for client previews.
- **15 images missing `alt` attributes**, including two hero banners (`482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`) and the personalization/tracking pixels. Hero banners are the priority fix; pixels should use `alt=""` (decorative) plus `width="1" height="1" border="0"`.
- Duplicate selector definition: `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (ANTHRO/Open Sans block and TERRAIN block) — last-write-wins, low impact but a template-hygiene bug.
- Malformed font stack inside MSO conditional: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` — stray empty token and a stray semicolon before `!important`. Outlook will likely ignore the rule.
- Same stray-comma pattern (`Helvetica, Arial, , 'sans-serif'`) repeated across multiple `@font-face`-driven selectors; Gmail/iOS will skip the malformed entry.
- `'sans-serif'` / `'serif'` are quoted as if they were family names — generic keywords should be unquoted to actually function as fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Evergage pixel exposes `userId=003a700000uTza` (Salesforce 18-char ID prefix) and Monetate pixel embeds `003a700000uTzaxAAC` in the path — confirms personalization is wiring through, but it also means the recipient's SFDC contact ID is leaking via third-party domains. Not a bug, but a privacy/PII consideration worth flagging.
- Dotomi pixel ends with `dtm_email_hash=` (empty value) — token did not resolve. Either upstream merge failed for this recipient or the hash is intentionally suppressed; verify with a known-good send.
- No unresolved `%%...%%` / `{{...}}` tokens visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** MIME multipart/alternative is effectively HTML-only — hurts spam scoring (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender guidelines) and accessibility clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** by the AgentMail relay. If the production send is using header-based unsubscribe, ensure it's preserved through the relay; otherwise this is a CAN-SPAM/RFC 2369 gap.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) not present** — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (Feb 2024) for senders >5k/day. Anthropologie clearly exceeds that threshold.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header missing** in capture — SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unverifiable from this artifact. Likely stripped by relay; confirm in a raw inbox capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Cannot verify — all destination URLs are wrapped in click-tracker redirects and were skipped by the probe. Unwrap a sample (e.g., the hero CTA) and confirm:
  - UTM params present and consistent (`utm_source=sfmc`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=052526_AN-US_MDWComboEndsTonight_VersionA` based on the Monetate path).
  - Landing pages are 200 OK (not redirect chains > 2 hops).
  - Sale/home category pages match the offer copy.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add plain-text alternative part** to the MIME message — highest-impact deliverability fix.
2. **Add `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** headers if not already present at the ESP level; verify they survive AgentMail relay capture.
3. **Populate `<title>`** and add descriptive `alt` text to the two hero banner images; set `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
4. **Fix the MSO/Outlook font stack** — remove the empty token and the semicolon before `!important`: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Apply the same cleanup to every `Helvetica, Arial, , 'sans-serif'` occurrence.
5. **Unquote generic font keywords** (`sans-serif`, `serif`) and **dedupe** the `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` selector.
6. **Audit the 9× Evergage `summary.png` calls** to confirm each segment is intentional, not a loop bug.
7. **Capture a raw .eml** through AgentMail to confirm whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`, and `DKIM-Signature` are actually missing on the wire vs. stripped by the relay — the current "missing" signals are ambiguous.
