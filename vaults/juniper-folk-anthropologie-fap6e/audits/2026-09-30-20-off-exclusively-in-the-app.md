# 20% off exclusively in the app
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer app-download push. The hero is a boxed "You're the Best / 20% OFF / WITH CODE: APP20" card, and a maroon "Free Express Shipping on App Orders $150+" banner sits above it. The offer is concrete and reads first. Below it the email turns into a long editorial catalog of six category tiles and a 3x3 product grid. That dilutes the app message the hero sets up.
- It follows the 9/28 "Romantic new arrivals (with an edge)" editorial send, and it's a change from the 50%-off hammering of 9/24 and 9/27. Two days after a pure-editorial send, a sub-full-price offer gated to the app is a sensible move. It also avoids repeating the "Extra 50% Off" creative a fourth time. But "exclusively in the app" adds friction. Nothing in the render tells me how the code gets applied, and the App Store badge is buried at the very bottom.
- Overall it's competent but generic. The holiday-shop tiles and a Christmas-tree hero image sit awkwardly beside "Fall is a Feeling: The September Collection" on September 30. Judgement: a solid mid-tier send that under-uses its own app angle.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: large maroon "20% OFF" with a visible code, APP20, and a "Limited Time" seal.
- The free-express-shipping banner adds a second, threshold-based incentive ($150+) without competing with the hero.
- The two hero CTAs, "Shop New Clothing" and "Shop New Home", split the brand's two main categories.
- The editorial tiles (cowboy boots, cashmere, the Trend Hub) look on-brand and well shot.
- The 3x3 "More with your name on it" grid gives a scannable product close, with diverse models.

## What's weak

- There is no app CTA in the hero. Nothing says "Open the app" or "Get the app", and the App Store badge only appears near the footer.
- The hero image shows a decorated holiday tree with ornaments on 9/30, while the tiles below say "September Collection" and "Fall Boots". The seasonal story is muddled.
- The email runs very long, with six large tiles and nine products, and buries the single offer under content.
- The product grid has no prices, names, or links that I can see, and there are no reviews or social proof.
- The fine print sits at the bottom in tiny type. It says the discount is a regular-price purchase, iOS only, not valid in stores, with exclusions (Mother, wedding, food, select brands). That sits awkwardly against the honest-offer signal, and Android users are shut out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the app in the hero. Replace the two category buttons with one "Get 20% off in the app" button that deep-links to the app or the App Store, and move the App Store badge up under the code.
- 2. Cut the body to two or three tiles plus a shorter grid. Make every tile reinforce the "APP20" offer.
- 3. Match the hero image to the season, or lean into the holiday early with a matching headline.
- 4. State the key exclusions and the iOS-only limit near the code, not in footer fine print.
- 5. Add prices or "20% off with APP20" labels to the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your APP20 code: 20% off, app only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off just for app users`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code APP20 at checkout + free express shipping on $150+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall boots, cashmere and new home finds, all 20% off in the app.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's Outlook-specific CSS and font stacks are malformed, the viewport meta blocks zoom, and 8 images have no alt attribute. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified (likely a relay artifact), and the truncated source and skipped tracking probes leave link and UTM alignment unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **33 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations, status codes, or landing pages are verified.
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash` on the Conversant pixel.** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has an empty value. This is either an unresolved merge/hash value or a truncation in the QA output. If it is unresolved, the retargeting pixel isn't matching users.
- **Possible subscriber ID in a third-party URL.** The `pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../093026_AN-US_20OffRegPriceAppExclusive_V2/003a700000uTzaxAA` path ends in a value with the Salesforce Contact ID prefix (`003`). If it is a per-recipient ID, it is exposed in a third-party request URL. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated data.
- **Duplicated banner images.** `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` each appear twice. This is probably a desktop/mobile pair, but that couldn't be verified from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid Outlook CSS.** In the `<!--[if mso]>` block: `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry (`, ,`) is malformed.
  - The stray `;` before `!important` orphans the declaration, so the MSO font override is likely invalid or ignored.
- **Quoted generic families.** The font stacks use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` (for example `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"]`, `[style*="Font-Normal-pd"]`). Quoted, these are read as font names rather than generic keywords, so the generic fallback never triggers.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is defined twice (under BEHOLDEN and TERRAIN).
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty title and no lang.** `<title></title>` is empty, and `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute in the visible source.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** The `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Missing alt text (8 images, per QA):**
  - Content images (5 distinct files):
    - `Oct26_PROMO_20OFFAPP_CROSSDIV_02.gif`, the promo banner that carries the offer.
    - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (twice).
    - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (twice).
    - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`.
  - Tracking pixels (should carry `alt=""`):
    - `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0`
    - `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAA`
  - With images blocked, the promo banner leaves nothing to read.
- **Web font limits.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is dropped by Gmail and Outlook. The fallback stacks are only reliable once the quoting issue above is fixed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The empty `dtm_email_hash=` (see section 2) is the only suspect value. The rest of the body was truncated and couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA flagged it as WARN and noted it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unverified rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** No RFC 8058 one-click evidence. Same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `s.anthropologie.com` is a dedicated subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **In-body footer not visible.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address weren't in the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.
- **Plain-text part is URL-heavy.** 5,999 of 8,234 characters (73%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and a poor text-only experience.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and click-through parameters can't be assessed.** All 33 tracked links go through redirect domains and weren't probed. The visible source has no UTM parameters.
- **Landing target unknown.** The subject says "20% off exclusively in the app," and the only campaign identifier visible is `093026_AN-US_20OffRegPriceAppExclusive_V2` in the `mon-trk` pixel. It's unknown whether the links resolve to an app-store or deep-link destination or to a web fallback, so alignment with the offer can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO block to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty entry and the stray semicolon.
2. Unquote the `serif` and `sans-serif` generics in all `[style*="Font-Normal-*"]` rules, and remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `Oct26_PROMO_20OFFAPP_CROSSDIV_02.gif` and the other content images. It must state the 20% app offer. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Populate `<title>` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
6. Check that `dtm_email_hash` resolves at send time, and confirm whether the `003…` ID in the `mon-trk` pixel is per-recipient.
7. Re-run QA against the raw MIME source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
8. Resolve a sample of the 33 tracked links to confirm that the destinations and UTM parameters match the "app exclusive" offer.
9. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part, or shorten the tracked URLs.
