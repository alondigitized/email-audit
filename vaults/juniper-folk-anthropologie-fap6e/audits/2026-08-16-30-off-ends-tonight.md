# 30% Off ends TONIGHT
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, correctly-rendered version of the same "30% off" hero Anthropologie has now run four times in ten days — and unlike August 14's "15% OFF b/c we're into you, for real…" (4.00/10), the banner here isn't broken. "Ends Tonight" is stamped at the top, "30% OFF" is huge and legible, and shop-now-by-category buttons (Dresses, Tops, Bottoms, Accessories, Shoes, Beauty) sit right under the CTA — genuinely useful navigation shortcuts. But the fundamentals of the send haven't changed: it's a blanket sitewide discount with no personalization, wedged between a Perksfest teaser above the fold that competes for attention and a very long scroll of "just for you" browse tiles below.
- The urgency framing ("Ends Tonight") is the strongest lever here and it's used well — bold, top-of-email, unambiguous. That's the one thing elevating this over the August 12 "30% OFF clothing, accessories & more" send (5.00/10), which had no deadline at all.
- Judgement: solid execution of a generic, fatigue-prone promo. The layout and urgency copy are good; the offer itself is stale by the fourth repeat this stretch.

## What's working

- "Ends Tonight" urgency banner is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss at the very top.
- 30% OFF hero is large, high-contrast, and reads instantly without scrolling.
- Category shortcut buttons (Dresses/Tops/Bottoms/Accessories/Shoes/Beauty) let a shopper skip straight to their category — smart use of space under the CTA.
- No render bugs — text, buttons, and images all display cleanly (a real contrast to the Aug 14 send).
- "SHOP NOW" button is clear, high-contrast, and sits directly beneath the offer.

## What's weak

- Perksfest banner sits directly below the main offer and pulls focus onto a second, unrelated CTA (Maeve early access) before the eye ever leaves the hero zone.
- Below the fold it's an undifferentiated wall of lifestyle tiles ("The Trend Hub," "Going Analog," "Suede Shop," "The Lip Edit") with no product-level detail, pricing, or reason to click any one over another.
- Fourth 30%-off-style send in ten days (three sitewide-percent sends plus the broken one) — no escalation, no new hook, just repeated discount language.
- No loyalty/AnthroPerks member pricing or benefit called out in the hero, despite Perksfest running as a parallel campaign in the same email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Perksfest banner from this email entirely — it's a competing CTA that dilutes the "Ends Tonight" urgency. Give Maeve/Perksfest its own send.
- 2. Trim the "we picked these for you" grid from nine tiles down to 3-4 curated picks with visible price or % off per item — a scroll of unlabeled outfit photos does no work toward the click.
- 3. Vary the promo mechanic next time (free shipping threshold, tiered spend-and-save, or a category-specific push) instead of a fifth straight percent-off — subscriber fatigue is compounding.
- 4. Add "AnthroPerks members" framing near the hero since the brand is actively running a loyalty campaign in parallel — tie the two together instead of running them as disconnected sections.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 30% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart's about to lose 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, shoes, beauty — all included before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A few hours left to stock up at 30% off`
