# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I am *immediately* at home here. Anthropologie knows exactly who it's talking to — the lavender promo block with a butterfly illustration, the "JUST FOR YOU" personalization framing, and literally the word "CANDLES" in the discount copy? They see me. Right now they're pushing a Mother's Day gifting moment with a 30% off accessories/candles/sleepwear offer, and honestly I don't hate being treated like both the gift-giver and the recipient.

## What's working

- **"CANDLES & MORE" in the discount copy** — I am a self-described candle stockpiler and they literally named my category. I am tapping this.
- **Butterfly/botanical illustration on the promo block** — tiny detail but it signals aesthetic alignment; this isn't a clinical discount banner, it *looks* like Anthro
- **Lifestyle photography tone** — cream linen on coastal rocks ("New!"), denim jacket by the water ("Top Rated") — both feel like pages from my actual camera roll
- **Mother's Day banner with "buy online & pickup in-store"** — I browse on my phone and convert in-store all the time; this BOPIS callout is exactly my flow
- **"JUST FOR YOU" personalization framing** — feels warm, not transactional; Millennial-coded in exactly the right way
- **Lavender color on the promo block** — on-brand and spring-appropriate; doesn't feel like a generic red SALE banner

## What's weak

- **No AnthroPerks / rewards balance visible** — I am a card-carrying Perks member and I want to see my points or a "member exclusive" badge without hunting for it; this is table stakes for a returning shopper
- **"UP TO 30% OFF*" asterisk** — the fine print is doing work I can't see yet, which creates minor hesitation before I tap
- **Cookie banner partially obscures the lower lifestyle modules** — I know it's legally required, but it cuts off what looks like a second row of content and creates a visual clutter moment
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for a $30+ candle purchase, knowing the shipping threshold upfront is a conversion driver I'd want above the fold
- **Zero reviews or ratings visible** — the "Top Rated" label on the photo is intriguing but also anonymous; a star count would make me trust it more

## Recommendations

- **Surface the AnthroPerks balance in the "JUST FOR YOU" module** — replace the generic copy with "You have 240 Perks points — shop 30% off to redeem" and watch loyalty members convert faster
- **Add free shipping threshold to the promo block** — one line under the offer copy: "Free shipping on orders $50+" removes a pre-checkout anxiety point
- **Dismiss the cookie banner to a bottom-strip instead of a center overlay** — it's blocking what looks like a seasonal editorial section and that's a wasted impression
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for a category-specific CTA** — "Shop Candles & Gifts" or "Shop the Sale" is more specific and reduces cognitive load for intent-driven shoppers like me
