# today ONLY. AnthroPerks Early Access.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a pretty, on-brand maeve send that never says what the early access is. The hero is strong: a woman in a leopard fur coat and sequin shorts, holding a vintage camera, under a big "maeve" wordmark and "shop 24 hours early, members only." But the only copy under it is a thin italic line, "sign up to shop our picks for party season." There's no percent off, no code and no button. The recipient has to work out what they're getting.
- "AnthroPerks" in the header and "members only" in the hero both promise loyalty value, but nothing delivers it. Compared with the clear 20% off / APP20 sends on 9/30 and 10/3 ("Last call: 20% off ends tonight," 7/10), this one is all mood and no mechanism. It's better than the 10/3 plaid-minis lookbook because it has a real time hook (24 hours early), but it's still a brand-story email wearing a member-perk subject line.
- The body is a stack of category tiles (Dresses, Pants, Heritage Prep, Shoes), then a nine-product grid with no prices or names. It feels like browsing a catalog, not acting on a perk. Verdict: good-looking, weak on conversion. It needs one concrete member benefit and a real button.

## What's working

- The hero is arresting and clearly maeve, with a seasonal party-season mood.
- "shop 24 hours early, members only" is a credible, time-bounded loyalty hook.
- The AnthroPerks header and the shipping banner ($50+ standard, $150+ express on iOS app) reinforce member and app benefits.
- The visual hierarchy is clean: hero first, then tiles, then the product grid.
- The footer's "Five-Star Favorites," "Pay Later w/ Klarna," "Gift & Styling Services" and "Join AnthroPerks" tiles are a tidy, useful finish.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer: "early access" has no stated discount, drop or product, so there's nothing to act on.
- No visible CTA button. The only direct ask is a small italic "sign up to shop" line, and a small glyph sits beside it that looks like a placeholder icon.
- "Sign up to shop" is confusing for someone who is already subscribed or an AnthroPerks member. It reads as a gate, not a perk.
- The nine-item "These are so you, too" grid has no prices, names or labels, so there's no product detail to click on.
- The send sits in a near-daily cadence (9/30, 10/1, two on 10/3, now 10/4). It mixes editorial and promo beats without a clear reason to open this one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real member benefit in the hero, such as "Members shop the new party edit 24 hrs early," and add a solid "Shop early access" button directly under the image. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Replace the "sign up to shop" line with a clear state-aware message, like "You're in. Early access ends tonight at midnight."
- 3. Add names and prices to the product grid and link each tile to its product page. Give the first row a "just dropped" label to justify the early access.
- 4. Cut the category tiles from five to three, or tie them to the party-season theme, so the email is shorter and the CTA isn't buried.
- 5. Drop the generic "More You. More Maeve." tile or give it a purpose.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "today ONLY. AnthroPerks Early Access." (Anthropologie / maeve)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a template-driven build with several real code defects: invalid MSO CSS, a pre-DOCTYPE comment, quoted generic font families, and a zoom-blocking viewport. Six images have no alt text. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **35 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels in the body:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The `dtm_email_hash=` parameter is empty in the captured copy. That may be intentional redaction or an unresolved or suppressed hash. It can't be confirmed from this evidence.
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../100426_AN-US_PerksPreshopMaeveZeroProof_NonMembers/003a7000...`. The path embeds the campaign and segment name plus what looks like a Salesforce contact ID (`003a7000…`).
- **Duplicate assets.** `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` each appear twice. This is likely a repeated module and harmless.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed, 6 images plus 2 pixels):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (×2)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (×2)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The Dotomi and mon-trk pixels, where the missing alt is less relevant.
  - Content images with no `alt` get announced by filename in screen readers. Decorative ones should use `alt=""`.
- **Invalid MSO CSS.** The rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - It has an empty font-family entry (`, ,`).
  - The stray `;` before `!important` makes that token invalid, so the declaration is not important in Outlook.
- **Quoted generic families.** The rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes (e.g. `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"]`). Quoted, they are treated as font names, not generic keywords, so the generic fallback doesn't work.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering engines.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Some clients and accessibility tools use the title.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. This is low severity because fallbacks exist, though the quoted generics above weaken them.
- **Duplicate CSS rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is defined twice. This is cosmetic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear in the truncated HTML. The only open item is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). QA notes the relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is not verified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for high-volume senders, but again the relay may have dropped it.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** They were not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.
- **Plain-text part is >70% URLs** (7008 of 9038 chars). This is a deliverability risk (spam-filter heuristic) from long tracking URLs in the text alternative.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. All 35 links go through tracking redirects that QA did not resolve, and the HTML was truncated. No UTM parameters or landing pages could be inspected, so no continuity issues are claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the pencilline banners and the PNG. Use `alt=""` if they are decorative. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels too.
2. Fix the MSO font rule: remove the empty font-family entry and write `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
3. Unquote the `sans-serif` and `serif` generics in the font-family stacks.
4. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE, or remove it.
5. Populate `<title>` and remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0`.
6. Confirm the raw `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results at the sending ESP or a seed inbox. QA couldn't see them through the relay.
7. Shorten or simplify the plain-text part, and check where the empty `dtm_email_hash=` value comes from.
8. Resolve a sample of the 35 tracking links manually to verify destinations and UTM preservation.
