# Up to 40% off home, just in time for guests
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first home send. A plaid-framed house cutout carries "UP TO 40% OFF* Bedding, decor, in-stock furniture & more!" with a SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands there first. The "Get ready for guests" banner and the "Need it by Thanksgiving? Order by 11/17" line tie it to the season. That is the right pivot after the Oct 26 "Holiday Party Shop" editorial send, and a clear step away from the week-long 20%/50% off drumbeat.
- The body is where it loses focus. After the hero there are seven category buttons, then a "More to Explore" grid, a Terrain banner, and nine apparel tiles under "More with your name on it." Those apparel tiles have nothing to do with a home promotion. The "up to" framing and the asterisk also leave the offer vague. "Up to 40%" tells me the best case and hides the typical discount.
- Verdict: a solid, seasonal, readable email. It is diluted by a long tail, and it asks a home-focused reader to scroll past fashion. It's a 6, in line with the brand's recent range.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a big "40% OFF" on the plaid frame, with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The "Need it by Thanksgiving? Order by 11/17, 1 p.m. EST" line gives a credible deadline with a real shipping cutoff.
- The seven color-blocked category buttons ("In-stock furniture", "Bedding, pillows & throws", "Rugs & curtains" and so on) are easy to scan and let readers go straight to what they want.
- The lifestyle imagery is warm and on-season: the faux fur throw with the dog, the holiday mugs, and the Thanksgiving table.
- The subject's "just in time for guests" matches the hero's "Get ready for guests" copy.

## What's weak

- The nine apparel tiles under "More with your name on it" are off-message for a home sale. They bury the footer and dilute the home offer.
- "Up to 40%*" is vague, and the exclusions only appear in small footer text.
- The hero's SHOP NOW button is small and low-contrast, a thin outline on cream.
- The email has too many exits: seven category buttons, six editorial tiles, a Terrain banner, nine apparel tiles and four footer links. No single hero product is linked.
- The "More to Explore" tiles ("The Gift Guide", "The Holiday Shop") have captions but no offers or prices. They read as filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the apparel grid, or replace it with 3 to 6 priced home products, such as the throw, the mugs and a rug, each showing a "was/now" price. This would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Make the offer more precise: "Up to 40% off" becomes a specific tier, for example "40% off bedding & decor, 25% off furniture."
- 3. Give SHOP NOW a filled, high-contrast button.
- 4. Move the 11/17 shipping cutoff into the hero so it sits next to the offer.
- 5. Add social proof, such as a "bestseller" tag or review stars, on the featured products.

The fallback stacks exist, but the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` selectors only match when the fonts load.
