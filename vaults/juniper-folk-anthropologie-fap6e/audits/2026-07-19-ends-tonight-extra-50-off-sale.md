# Ends TONIGHT: Extra 50% off sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Anthropologie has a strong, clean hero here — "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" with a "NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge lands clearly, and "Ends TONIGHT" on the subject line is the right urgency close to a sale cycle. For a shutting-down-the-sale send, the mechanics are mostly correct.
- The fatal flaw is in the scroll. Below the hero, the same botanical olive-green "30% off fashionably early fall event" module that has appeared in every send since July 6 resurfaces again. A shopper opening this email because "Extra 50% off ends TONIGHT" now sees a competing, lesser offer from a different campaign. Which is it? The dual-offer structure undermines the very urgency the subject sells. This is the same tension that appeared in the July 15 send — they didn't fix it then and it's worse now with 13 days of creative repetition.
- The second issue is cadence. This is the ninth or tenth promotional email in under two weeks. "Ends TONIGHT" has diminishing returns when subscribers have seen near-daily urgency from the same brand. The close still works, but the trust debt is accumulating.

## What's working

- **Hero is the clearest in the recent run.** "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" in large type with a green "NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge is well-executed; offer is impossible to miss.
- **Category nav strips are genuinely useful.** Six sale-category links (Clothing, Shoes, Beauty, Bags, Brands, Kitchen & Dining) below the hero help shoppers self-route without hunting.
- **"Ends TONIGHT" is maximum credible urgency.** Short, concrete, and directly tied to the Extra 50% off — no ambiguity about what's closing.
- **No render bugs.** Images load, layout is clean, no broken slots or overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- **The 30% off fall event module appears inside an "Extra 50% off ends tonight" email.** These are two different offers at two different discount depths. Subscribers who came in for the closing sale hit the fall event creative and now wonder what they're actually shopping.
- **"NEW STYLES ADDED!" on an ending-tonight email is contradictory.** You can't simultaneously close the sale and tell people new product just arrived — it dilutes the scarcity.
- **The same botanical-green fall event creative has now appeared in at least nine consecutive sends.** By this point it reads as decoration, not a message.
- **No clear single-product hero.** The email jumps to themed editorial collections (The Taos Road, Drifty Botanica, Garden Treasures, etc.) without surfacing one standout item — no anchor for a shopper who doesn't know what to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the 30% off fall event module entirely.** This is a closing-urgency send. One offer, one deadline. The fall event creative has run its cycle; its presence here actively undercuts the 50% off close.
- 2. **Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" hero button with something deadline-specific.** "Shop the Sale — Ends Tonight" ties the CTA directly to the urgency instead of a generic prompt.
- 3. **Lead the body with one high-conviction hero product or category** (e.g., the highest-engagement sale item from prior sends) before going editorial. Give people who don't know what to pick an obvious anchor.
- 4. **Drop the "NEW STYLES ADDED!" badge.** It contradicts the closing-sale narrative. If new styles are on sale, say "New markdowns added" — that's additive urgency, not contradictory newness.
- 5. **Tighten the editorial section titles.** "The Taos Road," "Drifty Botanica," "Going Home" are pleasant but give no purchase signal. Even adding price points or a "from $X" line would help.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off ends at midnight — final hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: Extra 50% off everything on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added — this is the lowest price of the season.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clothing, shoes, beauty, home — all at extra 50% off. Done at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Anthropologie "Ends TONIGHT: Extra 50% off sale"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a shared URBN multi-brand template with brand-specific CSS for Free People, Urban Outfitters, Beholden, and Terrain loaded alongside Anthropologie-specific rules. Three compliance-level header warnings and six accessibility failures were flagged by automated QA; one merge token is confirmed unfilled in the rendered source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**19 tracking/redirect links skipped** — all click-through destinations are wrapped in a click-tracking redirector. Destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be probed. This is expected behavior but means UTM coverage and final-destination health cannot be confirmed from this source alone.

**Dotomi retargeting pixel has an empty merge token:**
```
https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?...&dtm_email_hash=
```
`dtm_email_hash` is present in the URL but has no value. The hashed-email token was not substituted at send time. This breaks Dotomi's cross-device retargeting attribution for this send.

**Second tracking pixel (mon-trk.com) present:**
```
https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-9cf5-4254-8a2d-39742e856443/071926_AN-US_.../003a700000uTzaxAAC
```
Two separate open/impression tracking systems are firing (Dotomi + mon-trk). No functional bug, but worth auditing for redundancy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Viewport disables zoom:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>
```
`user-scalable=0` / `maximum-scale=1.0` violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) and is blocked by iOS 10+ regardless, so it provides no practical rendering benefit.

**Outlook conditional CSS syntax error:**
```css
body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }
```
Two issues: (a) double comma creates a blank/empty font-family entry; (b) `!important` is placed after the semicolon, outside the declaration value — syntactically invalid. Outlook may silently ignore the rule or fall back to its default font.

**6 images missing `alt` text** (QA confirmed):

| Image | URL fragment |
|---|---|
| `emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (×2 in DOM) | `.../m/25/482199364-...` |
| `emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (×2 in DOM) | `.../m/25/482199362-...` |
| `Evergreen-Email-ModuleBIGO_04.jpg` | `.../m/1/Evergreen-...` |
| `1e313de8-...png` | `.../m/1/1e313de8-...` |

The two banner images each appear duplicated in the DOM — likely from a desktop/mobile conditional rendering pattern without deduplication. Both instances are missing `alt`. The Dotomi and mon-trk pixels are also flagged for missing `alt` but `alt=""` (empty string) is the correct value for decorative/tracking pixels; those two are false positives from the QA checker.

**Multi-brand CSS bloat in `<head>`:**
The stylesheet contains active button and font rules for Urban Outfitters (`.link-uo-*`), Free People (`.link-fp-*`), Beholden, and Terrain alongside Anthropologie rules. These are inert in this email but inflate the `<head>` payload and represent a template hygiene gap. `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is also declared twice (ANTHRO section and TERRAIN section) — the second declaration overrides the first silently.

### 5. Compliance

The missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header means Gmail's one-click unsubscribe button (required by Google for bulk senders since Feb 2024) may not be present. Needs verification against raw SMTP headers.

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**
1. **Dotomi `dtm_email_hash` token** — identify the ESP merge tag (`%%EMAIL_HASH%%` or equivalent) that should populate this parameter and verify it is mapped in the template. An empty value silently breaks retargeting for the entire list.

**P1 — Fix in next template revision:**
2. **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`** — confirm RFC 8058 header is present in raw SMTP headers (not just the AgentMail relay capture). If absent, add it to satisfy Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
3. **Alt text on the four content images** — add descriptive `alt` values; the two tracking pixels should get `alt=""`.
4. **Outlook CSS syntax** — fix `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` → `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`
5. **Remove `user-scalable=0`** — no functional benefit post-iOS 10; causes WCAG non-compliance.

**P2 — Template hygiene:**
6. Strip or gate the `.link-uo-*`, `.link-fp-*`, Beholden, and Terrain CSS blocks from the Anthropologie template variant — dead rules add payload and complicate debugging.
7. Deduplicate the `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` declaration.
8. Add an SMTP header capture step to the monitoring pipeline so `Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, and `DKIM-Signature` are reliably recorded.
