# 🥧 Dig in! 30% OFF 1000s of styles, while they last
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the nineteenth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days, and it reuses the plaid-edged gift-tag hero from the 11/26 "🔥 Now we're cooking: EXTRA 50% OFF SALE" email. The hero leads with "NOW, EXTRA 50% OFF SALE" and then adds "30% OFF almost everything else" under a "+" divider. The subject only mentions the 30%. So the subject sells the smaller offer, and the 50% on sale items is the better hook. This is the same subject/hero mismatch as the 11/25 "30% OFF Pilcro, Maeve & MORE 🫶" send.
- The body is a clean grid of teal-captioned category tiles: sweaters, décor, shoes, dresses, sleepwear, furniture, jewelry, kitchen, beauty, wedding and holiday. It looks polished and on-brand, and I saw no render bugs. But it's the same grid I've now seen many times. Nothing in it is urgent or new beyond "Thanksgiving!" in the top ticker.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand and visually clear, but it's one of many near-identical sends. A subscriber gets fatigued by this cadence. It's a solid 6–7, and cadence and the mismatch keep it from going higher.

## What's working

- The hero is big and clear. The "50% OFF" and "30% OFF" numerals are the first thing the eye lands on.
- The stacked offer structure is easy to parse: extra 50% on sale, 30% on everything else.
- The red "Shop Now / Shop Clothing / Shop Home & Furniture / Shop Gifts" buttons stand out against the pink and cream background.
- The category tiles have consistent teal captions and strong photography, so a reader can skim to what interests them.
- The "Might we recommend…" product row adds some personalization and carries "Top Rated" labels, which are social proof.

## What's weak

- The subject promises only "30% OFF", but the hero's biggest number is "50% OFF". The headline offer is buried in the subject.
- The "Dig in!" and 🥧 pie angle is dropped after the subject. The hero has no pie or Thanksgiving theme beyond the ticker.
- The offer carries asterisks and "excludes select items" fine print. The exclusions are in small text, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The category tiles carry no prices or product names, and the tiles themselves have no visible CTA text. Every tile is a "browse" prompt, not a product pitch.
- The fatigue is plain to see. It's the same plaid hero and grid as the previous sends this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align the subject with the hero's biggest number. Lead with "Extra 50% off sale" and fold in the 30% as the second beat.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends tonight" or "Thanksgiving only". "While they last" is weak urgency.
- 3. Replace several tiles with specific hero products and prices. A few "was/now" prices would give people something to click.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Consolidate near-duplicate sends so each one has a distinct reason to exist.
- 5. Surface the exclusions in one plain line near the buttons, not in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off sale + 30% off the rest 🥧`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Thanksgiving only: 30% off nearly everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, sleepwear, home & gifts. Online and in stores today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 50% off sale styles, plus 30% off everything else.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie "Dig in! 30% OFF" (s.anthropologie.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has several confirmed code-level defects: an empty `<title>`, a zoom-blocking viewport, malformed Outlook CSS, missing `alt` attributes and a blank tracking-pixel parameter. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so footer and link-level checks were limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracked links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so the final landing URLs and their status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **The Dotomi pixel has an empty parameter.** The pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` is blank, so either the hash failed to resolve or the capture inbox has no hash. If it's the former, the pixel can't match the recipient.
- **A third-party pixel carries a Salesforce-style ID.** The `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path ends in `003a700000uTzaxAAC`, and the `003` prefix is the Salesforce Contact object. A CRM record ID is being sent to a third-party tracking domain. Confirm this is intentional and covered by the privacy policy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** The title element has no text.
- **The viewport blocks zooming.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` disables pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** In the `<!--[if mso]>` block, `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has an empty list item (`, ,`). The `;` before `!important` also detaches it from the declaration, so the declaration is invalid and the font override likely isn't applied in Outlook.
- **Images missing `alt` (5 flagged by QA).**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` and `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` are content-bearing banners. With images blocked, nothing renders.
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png` is also missing `alt`.
  - The `dotomi` and `mon-trk` tracking pixels have no `alt`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Web font loading.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` inside `<style>` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The declared fallbacks (`Helvetica, Arial`, `Georgia`) cover this, so the impact is low.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8,528 of 11,371 characters (75%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs make the text alternative hard to read and can contribute to spam scoring.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens are visible in the supplied HTML.
- The only personalization defect is the blank `dtm_email_hash=` value above.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery. If the headers really are absent, one-click unsubscribe fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `s.anthropologie.com` and the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com` share a subdomain, which is consistent with alignment, but this is not verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** They fall outside the truncated HTML, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible in the supplied source. The links are click-tracked redirects and were skipped, so UTM propagation and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- The pixel path carries the campaign ID `112626_AN-US_PromoThanksgivingBlackFridayReminder1_MID`. This is useful for cross-checking against analytics.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `<title>` and remove `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
2. Fix the Outlook CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two `pencilline` banners and the `.png`. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Investigate the empty `dtm_email_hash=`. Review whether the Salesforce Contact ID in the `mon-trk` pixel path should go to a third party.
5. Pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Shorten the tracking URLs or add more plain-text copy to bring the URL share of the text part under 70%.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and with redirect-following enabled, to cover the footer, the 44 tracked links and UTM continuity.
