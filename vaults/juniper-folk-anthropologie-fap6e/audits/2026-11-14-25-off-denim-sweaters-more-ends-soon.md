# 25% off denim, sweaters & more ENDS SOON
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, offer-first promo, but it's the third near-identical "25% off" send in three days. The hero is a pale-blue "WINTER WARM UP" panel with a big navy "25% OFF*" over "Denim, Sweaters & Sweater Dresses," a SHOP NOW button, and an orange "ends soon!" sticker. The eye lands on the offer immediately. The preheader ("Thankful for great outfits. Get yours in time for Thanksgiving") adds a seasonal hook.
- The body is less disciplined. The hero stacks a second block ("1000+ New Sale Styles") with its own button, and a third offer ("30% Off** Holiday Cheers") sits right below it. That makes three offers and three CTAs before the first scroll. After that comes a long editorial grid of seven category tiles and a "Made With You In Mind" product grid. The product grid has star rows but no prices or names.
- Judgement: a solid, readable 7. It's the Nov 13 "25% off denim, sweaters & sweater dresses" email with an urgency sticker, a stacked sale-styles block and a Thanksgiving preheader. The "ends soon" tag is vague, and there's no real deadline to back it.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: huge navy "25% OFF*" with the category named directly beneath it.
- The "ends soon!" sticker adds urgency without shouting.
- The Thanksgiving preheader gives seasonal context and doesn't repeat the subject.
- The "More to Explore" tiles use real photography and are easy to scan by category.
- The footer offers useful extras: denim recycling ($20 off), Klarna, and inclusive sizing.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks three offers and CTAs: 25% off, 1000+ sale styles and 30% off holiday. This dilutes the primary message.
- "Ends soon" gives no date or time, so the urgency isn't credible. It also repeats Nov 13's offer almost unchanged.
- The product grid has stars but no prices, product names or sale callouts, so there's nothing to click for.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are small and dark-on-pale, and the "online only" text beneath is tiny.
- The asterisked fine print is buried in the footer, and the two offers carry different asterisk levels (* and **), which is confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the "1000+ New Sale Styles" block from the hero, or move it below the fold. One offer and one CTA at the top would lift clicks most.
- 2. Put a real deadline on the urgency sticker, such as "ends Sunday 11:59pm". Vague "ends soon" has been used since Nov 7.
- 3. Add prices and sale tags (or "was/now") to the product grid, and name the items.
- 4. Make the SHOP NOW button larger and higher-contrast, and say what it buys: "Shop 25% off denim."
- 5. Vary the creative from Nov 13. Lead with a hero product photo instead of a flat-colour panel.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a heavily templated, multi-brand Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with 41 click-tracked links. Visible defects are malformed Outlook CSS, missing `alt` attributes on 6 images, a zoom-blocking viewport tag and an empty `dtm_email_hash` value in a retargeting pixel. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the relay copy.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations or redirect chains are verified.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has an empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter. Either the hash token resolved to blank at send time, or the relay stripped it. If it is blank for real recipients, the retargeting match is silently broken.
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../111426_AN-US_Promo25OffSelectApparelReminder/003a700000uTza...` has a Salesforce Contact ID (`003a7…` prefix) in the path. This is a per-recipient identifier exposed in a third-party URL.
- **Plain-text part:** 7958 of 10996 characters (72%) are URLs, which trips the >70% URL threshold. This is a minor spam-filter signal and also makes the text version hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - Content images: `ES_04.jpg`, `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`, `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`, `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`.
  - Tracking pixels: `dotomi.com/.../1_0` and `mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTza…`. These should carry `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions so screen readers skip them.
- **Malformed MSO CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }`.
  - The empty entry between the commas is an invalid font list.
  - The stray `; !important;` makes the `!important` a separate invalid declaration, so it is never applied.
  - The comment says Arial should be first for Outlook, but the rule is partly broken.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this affects accessibility and web-view/archive display.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** `<!-- *** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE *** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can push older Outlook/IE rendering engines into quirks mode.
- **Web-font `@import`:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. Fallback stacks exist, so the impact is low. The `@import` also loads 8 font families, which is heavy for the clients that do support it.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so the rest of the markup (preheader, button VML, image dimensions) is not audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible HTML.
- The only suspect is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` value (see section 2). It is probably an unpopulated personalization field, and it cannot be confirmed from the relay copy alone.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for high-volume senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `s.anthropologie.com` and the image host is `image.s.anthropologie.com`, which are consistent subdomains, but alignment cannot be verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** these are not visible in the truncated source and need checking in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Unverifiable. All 41 links go through click-tracking redirects that QA did not resolve. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "25% off denim, sweaters & more" offer cannot be confirmed.
- The `mon-trk.com` pixel path includes the campaign name `111426_AN-US_Promo25OffSelectApparelReminder`, which is consistent with the subject. No mismatch is evident.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content images. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the `dotomi.com` and `mon-trk.com` pixels.
2. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;` (remove the empty entry and the stray semicolon).
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE, or remove it, and populate `<title>`.
5. Check that `dtm_email_hash` is populated in a real-recipient send, not only the relay copy.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against raw headers from a direct inbox (not via AgentMail).
7. Trim the plain-text part (fewer inline URLs, more descriptive text) to get under the 70% URL ratio.
8. Resolve the tracking redirects in a browser session to confirm the final URLs and UTMs.
