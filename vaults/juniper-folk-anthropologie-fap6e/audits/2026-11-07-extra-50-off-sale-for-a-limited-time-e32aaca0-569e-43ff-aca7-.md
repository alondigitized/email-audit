# Extra 50% off sale, for a limited time
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in four days built on the same "Extra 50% off sale" idea. On Nov 5, the "Extra 50% off sale: wrap-worthy finds" email used the identical green "Instant Gift-ification: EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" hero. Nov 6 and Nov 7 ("25% off dresses ENDS SOON") carried that block lower down. A real subscriber would see a recycled creative with a weaker subject line.
- The render itself is clean and offer-first. The orange "50% Off*" is the largest thing on screen, and a cream "SHOP GIFTS FOR YOU & THEM" button sits right under it. A colored grid of six sale-category buttons (Clothing, Home, Accessories, Shoes, Dresses, Kitchen & Dining) gives fast routes in. After that the email loses focus. It adds a second offer ("30% OFF Hosting & Gifts"), a long "More to Explore" editorial stack, and nine products under "We picked these just for you."
- Verdict: solid and functional, but a retread. The competing 30% banner and the very long scroll dilute a strong hero. It is a middling send that works mainly because of the brand and the discount.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: the oversized orange "50% Off*" on dark green pulls the eye first, and "limited time!" adds urgency.
- The six color-coded category buttons let a shopper go straight to dresses, shoes, home and so on.
- The "More to Explore" tiles (Cashmere Shop, Holiday Party Shop, Beauty Gifts) are on-season for gifting.
- The "We picked these just for you" grid shows star ratings under the products, which adds social proof.
- The footer fine print spells out the exclusions, so the offer reads as honest.

## What's weak

- The hero is almost identical to Nov 5's, so it feels stale to anyone who has been receiving these.
- The "30% OFF Hosting & Gifts" banner sits right under the category buttons and competes with the 50% message. That muddies the one-offer story.
- The email is very long, with five editorial tiles and nine products. The "More to Explore" tiles have no offer or price, so they read as filler.
- The product grid shows no prices, no sale prices and no product names. The shopper can't see the discount applied to anything.
- The "just for you" row implies personalization, but the products look generic.
- The asterisks and "limited time" give no actual end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday." Open-ended "limited time" is the biggest urgency gap.
- 2. Remove the 30% hosting banner, or move it below the products. One headline offer per send.
- 3. Cut the editorial tile stack to two or three tiles, and add a "from $X" price to each.
- 4. Show sale prices with the original price crossed out under each product in the grid, so the 50% off is visible on real items.
- 5. Refresh the hero creative. Use a product or lifestyle image instead of repeating the Nov 5 type-only block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off sale ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 50% off: gifts, dresses & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, dresses and home gifts, all marked down. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the extra 50% off before the best sizes go.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie, "Extra 50% off sale, for a limited time"

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) email with Outlook conditionals, but it has a malformed Outlook CSS rule, an empty `<title>`, no `lang` attribute and a viewport that blocks zoom. The automated QA run reported 0 failures and 4 warnings. The header-related warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not sender defects. The HTML was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup were not available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Two tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../110626_AN-US_50OffSOSReminder/003a700000uTzaxAAC`
- **The Dotomi pixel's `dtm_email_hash=` is empty.** This may be an unresolved or intentionally blank personalization value. If it is meant to carry a hash, the Dotomi match is broken for this send.
- **The Monetate-style pixel path ends in `003a700000uTzaxAAC`.** That is a Salesforce-style contact ID exposed in a third-party URL. It is low severity but worth confirming it is intended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Three content images are missing `alt`:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  
  All are on `image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/fe3e11717164057c701470/m/...`. They need `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text if not.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** gives screen readers and some clients no document title.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`.
- **The viewport meta disables zoom:** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **The Outlook-only CSS rule is malformed:** `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - It has an empty font-family entry and a stray `;` before `!important`.
  - The `!important` becomes a dangling declaration, so the Outlook font override is likely ignored or parsed unpredictably.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is used for web fonts.** Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore it, so those clients fall back to the stacks. The Anthropologie font (`Font-Normal-ns`/`-sp`) fallbacks are present, so this is informational only.
- **An HTML comment sits before the DOCTYPE** (`<!-- ***** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ***** -->`). This can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering engines.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were visible in the truncated source.
- The only suspect is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support cannot be verified from this capture. It is required for bulk senders by Gmail and Yahoo.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain aligned with the brand, but alignment was not verified.
- **The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (8096 of 11002 chars). This is a spam-filter and readability risk because the plain-text alternative is mostly raw tracking URLs.
- **The footer's physical address and unsubscribe link** were outside the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** All 42 tracking links were skipped, so destination URLs, UTM or campaign parameters and landing-page alignment with "Extra 50% off sale" could not be checked.
- **The campaign identifier `110626_AN-US_50OffSOSReminder`** appears in the Monetate-style pixel path. It is consistent with the subject line, but nothing confirms it carries through to the click-through URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`, removing the empty entry and the stray semicolon.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels. Add an `alt` to the three banner and content images, using `alt=""` if they are decorative.
3. Add a `lang="en"` attribute to `<html>` and populate `<title>`.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
5. Check why `dtm_email_hash` is empty in the Dotomi pixel and whether personalization is resolving.
6. Re-capture the raw message, not through the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Resolve the 42 tracking links through a browser-like follow to confirm final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
8. Trim the plain-text part with shorter, clean URLs, or drop link lists from it, to bring the URL ratio down.
