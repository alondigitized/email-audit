# Ends tonight: up to 40% off home
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the Oct 31 "Up to 40% off home ends SOON" send with a new "ends tonight" badge. The plaid-framed house cutout carries "UP TO 40% OFF* Bedding, decor, in-stock furniture & more!" and a SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first. Nine color-blocked category tiles follow, and the structure is clean and scannable.
- The urgency is real, but the creative is almost identical to Oct 27 and Oct 31. The only change is a small "ends tonight" sticker on the house. A subscriber has now seen this hero three times in a week. The lower half dilutes the message with the Gift Guide, Holiday Shop, Thanksgiving Shop, a Terrain banner and a "More with your name on it" women's apparel grid. The apparel grid has nothing to do with the home offer.
- Judgement: a solid, honest deadline send that works because the offer is clear. It's repetitive, and the sticker is too small to carry the "tonight" message. It isn't a standout.

## What's working

- The hero is offer-first: a huge "40% OFF*" with a visible SHOP NOW button, above the fold.
- The "ends tonight" badge and the "Today only! Free express shipping†" pre-header strip add a credible deadline and a second incentive.
- Nine color-blocked tiles (in-stock furniture, rugs & curtains, kitchen & dining, lighting and more) give clear category entry points.
- The "APP EXCLUSIVE: 25% OFF** LIGHTING" band gives a second, specific offer and a reason to open the app.
- The seasonal visuals (plaid, holiday houses, a dog in a pink faux-fur throw) fit the Nov 1 timing and the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The "ends tonight" badge is a tiny peach circle tucked into the house corner. It is the most important message of the send and it's the easiest thing to miss.
- The hero is nearly a copy of the Oct 27 and Oct 31 sends, which creates fatigue. The "GET READY FOR GUESTS" banner also feels stale by Nov 1.
- The asterisked offer ("*Up to 40% off select bedding, decor, furniture") is only explained in dense fine print at the bottom. "Up to" hides the real discount depth.
- The "More with your name on it" apparel grid has no tie to the home offer. The Terrain banner and the "The Gift Guide / Holiday Shop / Thanksgiving Shop" tiles pull attention away from the deadline.
- The free-shipping and app-exclusive offers compete with the headline 40%, so there is no single focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make urgency the hero. Replace the small badge with a large "ENDS TONIGHT" headline or a countdown above the 40% OFF, and drop the "Get ready for guests" banner.
- 2. Cut the lower content to the home categories and one cross-sell. Remove the apparel grid and the Terrain banner, or push them below the footer links.
- 3. Add proof of depth. Show two or three hero products with "now $X" prices so "up to 40%" feels concrete.
- 4. Surface the free express shipping next to the SHOP NOW button, not only in the pre-header strip.
- 5. Vary the creative from the Oct 31 send, or skip a send that repeats it with a changed sticker.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Midnight deadline: bedding, rugs & lighting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free express shipping through 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, decor & kitchen finds, plus 25% off lighting in the app.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/...`) from an aligned subdomain (`s.anthropologie.com`). The QA run found no hard failures, but its checks were limited: the relay stripped the auth and unsubscribe headers, 46 links couldn't be probed, and the HTML was truncated. There are CSS defects in the head and five images without alt attributes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not verified.** The QA probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expired-offer behavior are unconfirmed. This matters for an "Ends tonight" send.
- **Tracking pixels sit in the body.** Two third-party pixels are present:
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. The `dtm_email_hash` value is empty in the captured source. That is either a seed-address artifact or an unresolved merge value. If it is the latter, the pixel does no useful matching.
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../110126_AN-US_GetReadyForGuestsUpTo40OffEndsTonight/003a7000...`. The path carries what looks like a CRM contact ID (`003a7000...`, the Salesforce Contact prefix) in a third-party URL.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** The QA check reports 8806 of 12145 characters (about 72%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs are the likely cause.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes (5 images):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - the mon-trk pixel `003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  The two pixels should carry `alt=""`. The three `image.s.anthropologie.com` images need `alt=""` if they are decorative, or descriptive alt text if not. The "pencilline" names suggest decorative dividers.
- **Invalid Outlook font stack.** The MSO conditional style reads `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty list entry and the `!important` sits after the semicolon, so the declaration is malformed. Outlook may drop the font-family rule or ignore the `!important`.
- **Quoted generic families.** The font rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` as quoted strings, for example `font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif' !important`. Quoted, these are treated as family names rather than the generic keywords, so the generic fallback never applies.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Web fonts depend on an `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`.** Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, and the `@font-face` sources are hosted on `community.urbn.com`. The Helvetica/Arial fallbacks cover this, so it is low severity.
- **Template bloat.** The inline CSS includes the UO, Free People, Beholden and Terrain button/font classes, which an Anthropologie send doesn't use. This adds weight toward Gmail's ~102 KB clipping limit. The source was truncated, so I couldn't measure the final size.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source.
- **`dtm_email_hash=` is empty** in the dotomi pixel (see §2). Confirm whether this is populated for real recipients.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check this on the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` and the image host `image.s.anthropologie.com` are on the same organizational domain, which is consistent with alignment, but I can't verify it.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing-page checks weren't possible.** All 46 links went through click-tracking redirects and none were resolved. I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing-page offer alignment ("up to 40% off home"), or that the offer is still live.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw message headers directly and verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. This resolves the three header warnings.
2. Resolve a sample of the 46 tracking links with redirect following. Check the final URLs for UTM parameters, 200 status, and the "up to 40% off home" landing page.
3. Add `alt=""` to the two pixels and to the decorative banners, or descriptive alt text if the banners carry content.
4. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`, and unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in the font fallbacks.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta, and populate `<title>`.
6. Check whether `dtm_email_hash` populates for real recipients. Also review whether the contact ID in the mon-trk URL path is an intended identifier.
7. Check the final HTML size against Gmail's ~102 KB clip limit, and trim unused brand CSS (UO, FP, Terrain).
