# Up to 40% off home ends SOON
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first home send. A plaid-framed house cutout carries "UP TO 40% OFF* Bedding, decor, in-stock furniture & more!" with a SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands there first. Nine color-blocked category tiles below it make it easy to jump to rugs, lighting, kitchen and so on.
- It is nearly a repeat of the Oct 27 send, "Up to 40% off home, just in time for guests," which used the same offer and nearly the same "Get ready for guests" framing. The only new beat is "ends SOON," and the email never says when. The only date in the render is 11/17, which is a shipping cutoff for Thanksgiving delivery, not a sale deadline. The urgency is a badge, not a fact.
- Verdict: solid, tidy execution that adds almost nothing new for a subscriber who saw the Oct 27 email. It would be stronger with a real end date or a fresh hook.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: huge "40% OFF" on a cream house shape against dark plaid, with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The category tiles are well labeled and color-coded, so a shopper can go straight to Lighting or Rugs & Curtains.
- The shipping line, "Need it by Thanksgiving? Order by 11/17," ties the send to the season and gives a practical reason to act.
- The "App exclusive: 25% off** lighting" band adds a second, specific incentive and pushes app installs.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Ends SOON" in the subject, and the "ends soon!" badge on the hero, give no date. That reads as manufactured urgency.
- The offer is a near-duplicate of the Oct 27 "Up to 40% off home" send, so it will feel repetitive.
- The hero is "up to" with an asterisk, and the exclusions only appear in the fine print at the bottom.
- The lower half spreads out into editorial tiles (Gift Guide, Holiday Shop, Thanksgiving Shop, Terrain) and an unrelated "More with your name on it" apparel grid. That dilutes the home focus.
- No individual product, price or review is shown in the home section, so there is nothing concrete to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real sale end date in the hero badge and subject, for example "Ends Sunday." Credible urgency would lift this more than anything else.
- 2. Replace the category-only layout with three or four hero products showing sale prices, such as a throw, a lamp and a rug.
- 3. Cut the apparel grid, or move it below the footer links, so the email stays about home.
- 4. Lead the subject with something new rather than repeating Oct 27, such as the app-only lighting deal or the Thanksgiving hosting angle.
- 5. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop home deals," instead of "Shop now."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracking and header checks are inconclusive: 46 tracking links were not probed, and the relay hid the auth and unsubscribe headers. Confirmed defects in the source are malformed Outlook CSS, quoted generic font fallbacks, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, no `lang` attribute, and five images without `alt`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not verified.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unconfirmed.
- **Empty hashed-email parameter on the Dotomi pixel.** The URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` has no value. This may be because the test inbox is unpopulated, or the merge failed. The source alone can't tell which.
- **Tracking pixels.** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../103126_AN-US_GetReadyForGuestsUpTo40OffEndsSoon/003a700000u...` is a third-party open pixel. It is also flagged for missing `alt`.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8,852 of 11,512 characters (about 77%) are URLs. That is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 5 images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the Dotomi pixel `1_0`
  - the mon-trk pixel `003a700000u...`

  The pixels need `alt=""`. The content images need `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text if not.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. The empty list item invalidates the declaration, and the stray `;` before `!important` detaches the flag. Outlook will ignore the intended font override.
- **Quoted generic families.** Declarations such as `'Francois One', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif'` and `'Times New Roman', 'serif'` quote the generic keyword. A quoted `'sans-serif'` is read as a font name, not the generic family, so the final fallback does nothing.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** Screen readers can't pick a language.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. Some legacy engines (Outlook desktop, old IE-based clients) may then render in quirks mode.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice, once under Anthro and once under Terrain. This is harmless, but it is template debt.
- **Web fonts depend on `@import`.** The Google Fonts `@import` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The fallback stacks above then apply, and their generic fallbacks are broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only evidence is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` above. No visible `%%token%%`, `{{...}}` or `[[...]]` leaks appear in the truncated source or the QA output.
- The rest of the HTML was truncated, so greeting and name tokens can't be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unconfirmed, not a sender defect. Check the raw original message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay artifact and gives no evidence of failure. Verify on the raw message at the receiving MTA.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated source. They can't be confirmed or faulted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** All 46 tracking links were skipped and the visible source has no resolved destination URLs, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- The pixel path contains the campaign slug `GetReadyForGuestsUpTo40OffEndsSoon`. A landing-page check should confirm that it matches the "Up to 40% off home" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MSO CSS to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`. Remove the empty list item and the stray `;`.
2. Unquote `sans-serif` and `serif` in all font stacks.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport tag.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
5. Add `alt` to the 3 content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the 2 tracking pixels.
6. Resolve a sample of the 46 tracking links manually to confirm their destinations and UTM parameters. Check that `dtm_email_hash` populates for a real subscriber.
7. Inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC to settle the relay-related warnings.
8. Add more text content to the plain-text part, or shorten its URLs, to bring the URL ratio below 70%.
