# EXTRA 40% OFF SALE: Black Friday is on 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the thirteenth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about four days, and it reuses the same plaid-edged gift-tag hero. The giant "30% OFF" sits over three red buttons (Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture, Shop Gifts). The green "+ EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" strip with a Shop Clothing button sits underneath. Compared with the 11/24 "30% OFF Damson Madder, Farm Rio & more" and "🚨30% OFF BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS LIVE 🚨" sends, almost nothing is new.
- The offer is clear and the hero lands first. Below it is a tidy grid of red-banner category tiles: sweaters, holiday decor, sneakers, dresses, sleepwear, furniture, jewelry, kitchen, beauty, wedding and Terrain. The tiles are well shot and easy to scan.
- The email is competent but fatigued. Subscribers have seen this hero on nearly every send since 11/21, and the only change is the subject line. The top banner ticker is also cropped ("IDAY STARTS NOW!" at the left edge), which looks sloppy. It's a solid 6–7 send that adds little to the last dozen.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF" is unmistakable and sits at the top of the visual hierarchy.
- The green "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" strip adds a second, stackable-feeling offer in a contrasting colour.
- Three separate red hero buttons route shoppers by category (clothing, home, gifts).
- The red-banner category tiles are consistent, well photographed and easy to scan.
- The "Might we recommend" product row adds product-level options and a "Top Rated" proof signal.

## What's weak

- The creative is a near-exact repeat of the 11/21–11/24 sends, so it blends into the pile in the inbox.
- The top ticker is clipped at both edges ("IDAY STARTS NOW!" and a cut-off "BL…"), which reads as a render flaw.
- The asterisks on "30% OFF*" and "40% OFF*" push the exclusions into fine print at the bottom. The hero's "excludes select items" line is small.
- The category tiles carry no prices, discounts or product names, so there is no specific hook beyond the hero.
- The recommended products have no prices or names, only "Top Rated" labels, so they give little reason to click.
- With roughly 20 links, the email has no single dominant CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the creative rut. Swap the gift-tag hero for a fresh visual or a clear "last-call" or "new today" angle, so subscribers can tell this send apart from the last dozen.
- 2. Fix the clipped banner ticker so the text fits the width.
- 3. Show real prices or "from $X after discount" on the recommended products and a few category tiles.
- 4. Consolidate the hero into one primary CTA ("Shop 30% Off") and demote the others.
- 5. Put the exclusions in plain sight near the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off everything + extra 40% off sale, today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday picks, up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, sneakers and holiday decor are all marked down. Sale styles take an extra 40% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated finds are going fast. Shop before sizes sell out.`

# Technical Audit: "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE: Black Friday is on 🖤"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP template (Anthropologie/URBN) with several CSS and accessibility defects in the visible head. It also has missing alt text on 5 images and incomplete header capture, so SPF/DKIM and unsubscribe status can't be confirmed. The HTML was truncated, so body-level checks are limited to the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Possible empty merge value in a tracking pixel.** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` ends in an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. The QA output may have cut the string, so confirm against the full source. If it is really empty, the Dotomi pixel is sent without its hashed-email identifier.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8148 of 10982 characters (74%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold, because of long tracked links.
- No other link problems are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has an empty list item (`Helvetica, ,`). The stray `;` before `!important` also makes that declaration invalid, so the Outlook font override may not apply.
- **Quoted generic font families.** Fallbacks are written as `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` (e.g. `[style*="Font-Normal-ns"]`). Quoted, they are read as font names rather than the generic keywords, so the generic fallback never applies when the web font fails to load.
- **Web fonts load through `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` inside `<style>`.** Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip `@import`, so those clients fall back to the fonts that follow in each stack.
- **Duplicate rule.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice in the same block (harmless but redundant).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Screen readers and some clients get no document title.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Five images lack alt text:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - `login.dotomi.com/.../1_0`
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a7000...`

  The first three are content or decorative images and need `alt=""` or a description. The last two are tracking pixels and should have `alt=""`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or QA output.
- The possible empty `dtm_email_hash=` on the Dotomi pixel is the only personalization concern (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may not capture it, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found,** so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can only be checked against raw headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither is visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. All 42 links are wrapped in redirect domains and were not resolved.
- The tracking-pixel URL contains `cli_promo_id=99`, and the pixel URL `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../112426_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder2_PM/...` names the campaign. This suggests campaign identifiers are present, but the destination parameters are not observable.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Outlook CSS: remove the empty font entry and the stray `;`, giving `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'Font-Normal' !important;`.
2. Use unquoted generic fallbacks (`sans-serif`, `serif`), and move the font load to a `<link>` or an `@font-face` block that clients can handle.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and fill in `<title>`.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels and add real alt text to the three content or banner images.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. Capture headers directly if the relay strips them.
7. Resolve the 42 redirect links in a follow-up pass to confirm final URLs, UTMs and HTTP status.
8. Confirm whether `dtm_email_hash=` is populated in the real send.
