# today ONLY. AnthroPerks Early Access.

- This is a brand-collab send dressed up as an AnthroPerks early-access email. The hero is a plaid-headscarf model with "shop 24 hours early, just for members." and a huge "maeve" wordmark. The subject promises "AnthroPerks Early Access," and the hero delivers on that. The problem is what comes after it. A small "sign up to shop plaid every way" line follows, then a thin "Get ready for guests / up to 40% OFF" strip from the home campaign, which carries an "ends tonight" badge. - Compared with the Oct 31 "Up to 40% off home ends SOON" send, which led with one clear offer and a SHOP NOW button, this one is prettier but weaker. Judgement: strong brand and voice, soft on conversion. It gets 5/10.

## What's working

- The hero is striking, and the "shop 24 hours early, just for members" line gives real exclusivity.
- The subject's "today ONLY" matches the "Today only! Free express shipping" top banner, so the urgency is consistent.
- The category tiles are clean and well photographed, and they cover dresses, pants, shoes and fragrance.
- The "We picked these just for you" grid signals personalization and gives a lot of product to browse.
- The top nav (Clothing, Shoes, Dresses, New Home, Weddings) is easy to use.

## What's weak

- No discount or price is attached to the maeve content. "Early access" is the only hook, and it has no stated benefit.
- The only visible CTA is the tiny "+ MORE TO EXPLORE ▸" link. There is no clear button for the main hero.
- The 40% off home strip is small and sits under the maeve hero. It is a different campaign with its own "ends tonight" badge, and it muddies the message.
- The product grid has no product names, prices, or ratings.
- The email is very long, with five large editorial tiles before the product grid.

- 2. Move the 40% home strip out of this send, or move it below the product grid.
- 3. Cut the five editorial tiles to two or three, and put the product grid higher.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings under the product grid items.
- 5. Add a countdown or a specific end time to match "today ONLY."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures: the QA run shows 0 issues and 4 warnings. The confirmed problems are an invalid Outlook font declaration, 7 images flagged for missing alt text, an empty `dtm_email_hash=` token in a tracking pixel, and a viewport meta tag that disables zoom. Header-based checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be evaluated because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so body links and UTMs were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 37 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no landing-page status or redirect chain was checked.
- **Empty token in a pixel:** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has an empty `dtm_email_hash=`. The pixel fires without the identity value it expects. This may be a test-inbox artifact, so I can't confirm it is a production bug.
- **Possible campaign-label mismatch:** The `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path is `110126_AN-US_NewArrivalsPreshopMaevePlaidTheory_NonMembers`. The subject is "AnthroPerks Early Access" and the sender is "maeve by Anthropologie". The pixel's campaign name and audience tag (`NonMembers`) may not match this send. That is worth checking, but the data doesn't prove it is wrong.
- **Plain-text version:** 7151 of 10191 characters (about 70%) are URLs, so the plain-text part is mostly long tracking links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid Outlook CSS:** The MSO conditional block has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. The empty list item (`, ,`) makes the declaration invalid. The stray `;` before `!important` means the `!important` is not applied either. As a result, Outlook may ignore the Arial override. There is no space between `Helvetica,` and `'Font-Normal'`, only an empty slot.
- **Missing alt text (7 images):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The `dotomi` pixel
  - The `mon-trk` pixel

  The three content images need descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. The two tracking pixels should have `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 sizing.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Comment before the doctype:** `<!-- ... ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ... -->` sits before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy engines.
- **Web font reliance:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is stripped by Gmail and other clients. The fallbacks (Helvetica, Arial, Georgia) are present, so this is low severity.
- **Duplicate CSS:** The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is defined twice. It is harmless but redundant.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The only evidence is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` above. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appeared in the visible source, but the source was truncated.

- **Authentication-Results:** Not present. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown from this data. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` should be checked directly.
- **Footer and physical address:** The visible source is truncated, so I can't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations

8. Shorten the plain-text version by trimming or condensing the tracking URLs.
