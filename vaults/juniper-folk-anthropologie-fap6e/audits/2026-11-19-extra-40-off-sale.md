# EXTRA 40% OFF SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first send with a hero that does its job. A brown panel carries a big pink "EXTRA 40% OFF" over "SALE / NEW STYLES ADDED" and a pink SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer immediately. The subject, "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE", is the same message as the Nov 18 send ("Extra 40% off sale? Well, okay then. 👀"), just flatter.
- Below the hero, the email falls apart into a holiday gift-guide catalog. "On My Way… with last-minute gifts" is a 2x2 grid of Gifts, Stuffers, Buy Online & Pickup, and E-Gift Cards. A "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" strip follows with a tiny SHOP NOW button, and then a long run of editorial tiles. None of these tiles reinforce the 40% offer. The sale promise in the hero is quickly diluted.
- Judgement: a competent but repetitive send. The hero is strong, but there's no deadline, no named product, and nothing new since yesterday. The subscriber has now seen "25% off" three days running, then "Extra 40%" twice. Cadence is the real problem.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: large pink "40% OFF" on dark brown, with a high-contrast SHOP NOW button.
- "NEW STYLES ADDED" gives a reason to look again, even though the sale itself is a repeat.
- The "On My Way… last-minute gifts" block ties into the season and gives gift shoppers fast routes: Top-Rated Gifts, Stocking Stuffers, E-Gift Cards.
- The "Made With You in Mind" row shows star ratings, which is useful social proof.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images, overlapping text or layout glitches.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline or end date anywhere in the hero, so the offer has no urgency.
- The offer is diluted. Most of the email is gift-guide and editorial tiles ("Trend Hub", "Holiday Entertaining", "Beauty Gifts") that have nothing to do with the 40% off.
- The "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" strip repeats the hero's message with a much smaller button, so there are two competing sale CTAs.
- Tile CTAs are vague ("The Cashmere Shop", "Winter Activities Shop"). No specific product or price is shown.
- The asterisk on "40% OFF*" is explained only in tiny fine print at the very bottom. Exclusions are buried.
- The subject is generic and nearly identical to yesterday's send, with no hook or emoji and no personality.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle. Put the 40% hero, then one row of 3-4 priced sale products with ratings, then a single repeat CTA. Move the gift guide to its own send.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday", plus a countdown or date line.
- 3. Name what the 40% applies to ("Extra 40% off sale sweaters, dresses & boots") so the offer is concrete.
- 4. Merge the two sale CTAs into one. Change the button copy to something specific, like "Shop Sale Styles".
- 5. Move the key exclusions from the footer to just under the hero, in a legible size.
- 6. Vary the subject across the week. Don't send the same line two days in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off sale styles — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New to sale: 1000+ styles, now 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, dresses & boots just added. Plus last-minute gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take an extra 40% off before Sunday. Online & in store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`image.s.anthropologie.com`, sender `s.anthropologie.com`). The HTML has some malformed CSS and accessibility gaps. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the data supplied: the headers weren't captured, and the source is truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **38 tracking links not verified.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels.** Two pixels load from outside the sender's domain:
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../111926_AN-US_40OffSOSReminder1/003a700000uTzaxAAC`
- **Empty hash parameter.** `dtm_email_hash=` has no value, which suggests an unresolved or empty merge value. The Dotomi call is probably not matching a user (see §4).
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 7,302 of 9,822 characters (about 74%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is a deliverability and readability risk for the text alternative.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** None has an `alt` attribute:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the Dotomi pixel
  - the mon-trk pixel

  Decorative images and pixels should use `alt=""`. The content images need real alt text or `alt=""`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** The tag is `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>`. `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` prevent pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** It gives no document title for assistive tech or web-view rendering.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Malformed MSO CSS.** The rule `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has an empty list entry and a stray `; !important`. The `!important` is dropped, so the Outlook font override may not apply.
- **Comment before the doctype.** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` comes before `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy engines.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** The Google Fonts `@import` is stripped or unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. Fallback stacks exist, so the impact is low. The `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` rule is also declared twice, which is harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`.** This is the only suspect token in the visible source. It is either an unresolved ESP variable or a test send without a hashed address. The data doesn't show which.
- **Contact ID in the pixel path.** `003a700000uTzaxAAC` looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix) and resolved correctly. It identifies the subscriber to a third-party tracking domain, which is worth confirming against the privacy policy.
- **No visible unresolved tokens.** No `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). Without it, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Caveat on all three.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. They are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects, and checking the raw message at the origin would settle it.
- **Footer not assessed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Can't assess.** All 38 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show landing URLs or UTM parameters.
- **Campaign naming is consistent.** The pixel campaign ID `111926_AN-US_40OffSOSReminder1` matches the subject "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE".

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Headers:** Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw origin message, not the relay copy.
2. **Alt text:** Add `alt=""` to the two banners, the `.png` and both tracking pixels, or real alt text where the image carries content.
3. **Viewport:** Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0`.
4. **Document head:** Add a non-empty `<title>` and `lang="en"` on `<html>`, and move the template comment below the doctype.
5. **MSO CSS:** Change the rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
6. **Dotomi pixel:** Confirm that `dtm_email_hash` populates in production sends.
7. **Plain-text part:** Reduce URL density, for example by dropping raw tracking URLs from the text alternative.
8. **QA coverage:** Resolve and probe the 38 skipped tracking links through a redirect-following check, and capture UTM parameters on the final landing pages.
