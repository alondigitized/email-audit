# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Anthropologie doing its thing — soft, sun-drenched, coastal-boho energy with women who look like they just got back from a trip to Portugal. Right now it's all about Mother's Day urgency with a side of "Summer new arrivals," which is exactly the seasonal pivot I'd expect in early May. It feels like home, but it's not showing me *my* homepage — it's showing me the gift-giver's homepage.

## What's working

- **"Summer says be in the moment / Spend it in new arrivals"** — that top carousel copy is genuinely on-brand for how I think about shopping here. It's the right register: unhurried but aspirational.
- **The coastal lifestyle imagery in the category tiles** — the woman in the cream linen set on the rocks and the one in the striped skirt by the water look *exactly* like the mood board I'm always building. I want to know what they're wearing immediately.
- **"New!" and "Top Rated" tiles** — simple, unambiguous labels that get me where I want to go in one tap. No buried navigation required.
- **The Mother's Day banner aesthetics** — the watercolor/paint-splash decoration around "GET YOUR MOTHER'S DAY GIFTS ASAP!" is genuinely pretty and on-brand. It doesn't feel bolted on.

## What's weak

- **No AnthroPerks hook anywhere above fold** — I have a rewards balance and this is supposed to be my ecosystem. The fact that there's no "You have X points" or even a subtle loyalty mention is a miss for a returning member.
- **"SEND AN E-GIFT CARD" as a primary CTA** — this is a Mother's Day tool, not a shopping journey starter. I'm here to browse dresses, not send e-cards.
- **No price signal whatsoever** — no free shipping threshold, no member discount, no sale callout. For a millennial who budgets with her Anthro Day discount in mind, this feels incomplete.
- **Cookie banner obscures the bottom tile row** — it's clipping what looks like a third lifestyle image starting to render, so my first impression of the product range is cut off.
- **No direct category navigation visible** — the hamburger menu is there but nothing surfaces "Dresses" or "New Arrivals" without an extra tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add an AnthroPerks balance chip to the nav bar** — even a subtle "Hi, Juniper — 320 pts" next to the heart icon would make me feel seen as a returning member immediately.
- **Swap the "SEND AN E-GIFT CARD" button for a "Shop New Arrivals" CTA** — or at minimum add it as a third option. Mother's Day is valid but the page currently only serves gifters, not shoppers.
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold to the top banner** — something like "Free shipping over $150 for members" in the carousel copy would remove the main checkout friction I think about before committing.
- **Surface one featured product tile with a price + rating** — even a single card below the "New!" and "Top Rated" tiles showing a dress at $148 with 4.7 stars would dramatically increase my tap-to-product confidence.
