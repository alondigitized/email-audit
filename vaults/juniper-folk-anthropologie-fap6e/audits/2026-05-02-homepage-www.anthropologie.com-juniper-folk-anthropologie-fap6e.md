# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Anthropologie is doing its thing — Mother's Day urgency front and center, and a pretty aggressive "buy more, save more" push that honestly makes me want to do math. It reads as a brand currently in promotional mode, which is a little at odds with the dreamy, editorial vibe I usually associate with them. The category grid below the hero is clean and functional, though — Dresses and Home in the same breath is very me.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **"Nine days until Mother's Day" banner** — the countdown framing is soft but effective, and "get your gifts shipped on time" removes my anxiety about ordering late. I'm immediately thinking registry + gifting.
- **$150 off $500 hero** — as an AnthroPerks member who already overspends here, this is genuinely compelling. $500 at Anthro isn't hard for me to hit, especially mixing apparel and home.
- **"LIMITED TIME!" badge on the hero** — small but it creates urgency without screaming. It feels on-brand, not clearance-rack.
- **Category grid layout** — "Dresses," "Home & Furniture," and "Gifts & Seasonal" all on the same screen is exactly my shopping brain. I can go boho dress → candle haul in one session.
- **Clean search bar** — I app-scroll a lot and the search is right there, large and tappable. No friction.

## What's weak

- **Hero is purely transactional** — the coral gradient with a discount offer strips away all the lifestyle magic. Where's the editorial imagery? Where's the patterned dress on a sun-drenched model? This could be a Kohl's promo.
- **No visible editorial or lifestyle moment above the fold** — zero photography in the hero. For a brand whose whole identity is visual storytelling, that's a miss.
- **"A | HOME" tab in the nav** — it's there, but easy to miss. I actively shop Anthro Home and I almost skimmed past it.
- **Cookie banner is covering the bottom of the grid** — can't fully read "BOTTOMS" or "SHOES & ACCESSORIES" without dismissing it first. Minor but annoying on mobile.
- **No loyalty / AnthroPerks visibility above the fold** — this promo feels generic. A subtle "your Perks bonus applies" nudge would make it feel personal to me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the flat gradient hero for a lifestyle photo** — a model in a flowy patterned dress holding flowers or a candle, with the "$150 off $500" overlaid as a subtle ribbon. Keep the offer, add the aspiration.
- **Add an AnthroPerks callout to the hero** — something like "Perks members: stack your discount" makes the deal feel exclusive rather than mass-market.
- **Pin the "A | HOME" tab more prominently** — or add a "Shop Home" shortcut in the category grid. Home & gifts is a huge driver for me in the Mother's Day window.
- **Auto-dismiss or minimize the cookie banner** — or move it to a smaller bottom sheet so the full category grid is visible on load.
