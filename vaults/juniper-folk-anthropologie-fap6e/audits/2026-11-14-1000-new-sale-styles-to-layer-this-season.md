# 1000+ new sale styles to layer this season
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a maeve-branded "new to sale" send. The top is clear: a red "JUST ADDED!" tag over "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" and a small SHOP NOW button. Below that it becomes a long editorial lookbook with lowercase captions like "classic not predictable" and "style it preppy, style it grungy, style it in your way." The creative is attractive and the brand voice is consistent.
- The problem is cadence and focus. On Nov 12 Anthropologie sent "Added to sale: 1000+ styles to love," and this is nearly the same message with "layer this season" attached. The Nov 13 "25% off denim, sweaters & sweater dresses" promo also appears here as a mid-email block, so there are two offers in one email. Offers like these are most useful when one is at the top, and here the 25% off sits in a small band about a third of the way down.
- My judgement is a well-made but repetitive send. It looks good and it is easy to read. It doesn't give a reader a new reason to act beyond what Thursday and Friday already gave them.

## What's working

- The red "JUST ADDED!" tag over a bold "1000+ NEW SALE STYLES" headline puts the offer at the top.
- The lookbook photography is cohesive and on-trend (plaid, faux leather, trench coats), and it fits the "layer this season" angle.
- The top line, "Thankful for great outfits / Get yours in time for Thanksgiving," adds seasonal context.
- The category navigation (Clothing, Shoes, Dresses, New Home, Weddings) is clean and makes browsing easy.
- The footer has useful loyalty and service blocks (Sign Up For SMS, Pay Later w/ Afterpay, Inclusive Sizing, Free Styling Services).

## What's weak

- The "1000+ new sale styles" hook is nearly the same as the Nov 12 "1000+ styles to love," so it reads as a rerun.
- The hero button is a tiny, generic "SHOP NOW" under a headline with no discount depth, so it's unclear how much the sale saves.
- The 25% off denim and sweaters block sits mid-email and competes with the headline. It also repeats Nov 13's message.
- The email is very long, with about eight image rows and a six-tile "Made with you in mind" grid. The product tiles have tiny labels, no visible prices, and no clear link between them and the sale.
- The hero image is mostly the maeve logo and a model. It sells the brand, not the sale items the headline promises.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero. Either state the sale depth ("Up to 50% off new sale styles") or lead with the layering story. Don't use both.
- 2. Replace the generic button with a specific one, such as "Shop Layering Pieces" or "Shop New Sale Coats & Knits," and make it larger.
- 3. Show 4–6 priced sale products near the top, with strike-through prices, instead of lookbook photos with no prices.
- 4. Move the 25% off band up or drop it. Two offers in one email dilute both.
- 5. Shorten the email by cutting the lookbook rows and the bottom grid to one module each.
- 6. Vary the creative from Nov 12 so the cadence feels fresh.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Layer up before Thanksgiving: new sale styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plaid, faux leather & trenches, now marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard ESP-built (`s.anthropologie.com`, click-redirect tracking) HTML email. The visible source and QA data show a few code-level defects: missing alt text, a malformed Outlook CSS rule, an empty `<title>`, and zoom-disabling viewport settings. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP check because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **The Dotomi pixel has an empty parameter.** The pixel URL is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. `dtm_email_hash=` is blank, which suggests an unresolved or unpopulated hash. This could be an artifact of the relay or test inbox, so it is not confirmed as a send-side bug.
- **Two third-party pixels are present.** They are `login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0` and `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003a700000uTzaxAAC`. The second embeds what looks like a Salesforce contact ID (`003a…`, 18 characters) in the path. That is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five image instances lack alt text.** The affected images are:
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With images blocked, these render as nothing, or as the filename.
- **The two tracking pixels have no `alt` attribute.** They should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive tech (`width="1" height="1"`, or `aria-hidden`).
- **`<title></title>` is empty.** This affects the web-view and screen-reader document title.
- **The viewport meta disables pinch-zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` blocks zoom on mobile and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **The MSO conditional CSS is malformed.** The rule is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty font-family list entry, and `!important` sits after the semicolon, so it is invalid and ignored. Outlook may not apply the intended font stack. The same empty-entry pattern appears in the comment documenting module usage.
- **The Google Fonts `@import` sits inside a `<style>` block.** Many clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore `@import` and fall back. The `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` selectors only work where inline styles carry those tokens, which I couldn't verify in the truncated source.
- **An HTML comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`.** It is harmless in modern clients, but it can trigger quirks mode in some older engines.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source.**
- **The only evidence of an unpopulated field is `dtm_email_hash=` (empty).** See section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed as a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found (WARN).** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders, can't be confirmed. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `s.anthropologie.com`.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,285 of 13,504 characters (about 76%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold. This is a minor spam-filter signal.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified.** They are likely in the truncated footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.** All visible links go through redirect tracking domains, the probe was skipped, and the destination URLs are absent from the truncated source. No continuity defect is evidenced.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two pencil-line banners and the PNG. Use `alt=""` on the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
2. Fill in `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the MSO rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in the original message. The relay may be stripping them, so re-test against a direct inbox.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), merge tokens, and UTM parameters after following the redirects.
7. Trim the plain-text part's URL density, for example by dropping tracking-heavy links or adding more readable text.
