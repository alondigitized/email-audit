# Take up to 30% off fashion during the Feels Like Fall Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth version of the same Anthropologie sale in four days. It follows "Up to 30% off fashion is here!" (Oct 8), "Extra 50% off sale won't be here for long" (Oct 7) and the two Oct 6 sends. The creative is the same boxed hero with three colour-blocked offers. The only real change is the "Feels Like Fall Sale" name and a "limited time!" tag. The hero is clean and offer-first: 30% off fashion on maroon, 30% off home on pink, and an extra 50% off sale on olive. A reader gets the offer in about two seconds.
- The weak spot is the body below the hero. Six editorial tiles (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere Shop, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets) are captioned with no copy and no tie to the discount. A 3x3 product grid follows with no names, prices or reviews. The email has no deadline and no product detail. "Limited time!" has no date behind it, so the urgency isn't credible, especially after four days of the same promo.
- Verdict: a competent, readable send that lands as fatigue. The subject is the most specific of the week, but the creative has stopped being news.

## What's working

- The hero stacks three distinct offers in bold colour blocks, and the eye lands on the percentages first.
- Each offer row has its own arrow CTA ("FASHION →", "HOME →", "SALE →") with a clear channel note (online only, or online & in stores).
- The subject names the offer, the category and the campaign, "Feels Like Fall Sale", which fits the season.
- The six editorial tiles use strong photography, with clear category names and good variety.
- The footer has a clean row of utility links (Recycle Denim, Klarna, A-List, Inclusive Sizing).

## What's weak

- Nothing shows when the sale ends. "limited time!" is the only urgency cue.
- The creative is nearly identical to the Oct 6, 7 and 8 sends, so the cadence feels repetitive.
- The 3x3 product grid has no names, prices or sale markers, so nothing says these items are discounted.
- Three asterisks in the hero point to dense footer fine print. The "online only" exclusion for fashion is small italic text.
- The body is a long scroll of unlabeled lifestyle tiles. The offer drops out of view after the hero, and there's no second offer reminder or CTA button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Collapse the sale emails into one send per offer change. Don't send the same promo four days running.
- 3. Put sale prices on the product grid, for example "$68 $98", with a "30% off" tag and review stars on a few items.
- 4. Add a one-line benefit to each editorial tile, such as "Cashmere, 30% off".
- 5. Add a solid "Shop the sale" button under the hero, plus a repeat before the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off fashion ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall sweaters, boots + an extra 50% off sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fashion, home and sale styles marked down. Online only on fashion.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cashmere, party dresses and mantel décor are all in. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie, "Take up to 30% off fashion during the Feels Like Fall Sale"

The HTML source was truncated early in the `<style>` block, so body markup, links and footer were not available. Findings below are limited to what the visible source and QA data confirm.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a multi-brand UO/URBN template with several CSS defects, 7 images missing `alt` attributes, and a third-party tracking pixel with an empty `dtm_email_hash=` value. Compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **37 tracking links were not probed.** The click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unverified.
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash` on the Dotomi pixel.** `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` has no value, so the hash was never populated. Either the merge value failed to resolve or the capture stripped it. If it failed, Dotomi cannot match the visit to a profile.
- **Third-party pixels.** `login.dotomi.com` and `pxl.mon-trk.com` are both embedded, and neither has `alt`. The `mon-trk` path includes `003a700000uTzaxAAC`, which looks like a CRM contact ID. It is a resolved token, but it is a subscriber identifier in a third-party URL.
- **Overall QA result.** The pass rate is 57% with 0 issues and 3 warnings. The rate is low mainly because of the skipped probes and unverifiable header checks, not confirmed breakage.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Seven images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (×2)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (×2)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - the two tracking pixels above

  The pencil-line banners look decorative and should carry `alt=""`. `1e313de8…png` needs a decision based on its content. Pixels should be `alt="" width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Screen readers and some webmail tab or preview contexts get no document title.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** In the `<!--[if mso]>` block, `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;` has two problems:
  - An empty family between the commas, which looks like an unresolved template variable.
  - A stray `;` before `!important`, which makes the declaration invalid, so Outlook gets no forced font.
- **Quoted generic font keywords.** The `[style*="Font-Normal-*"]` rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` as quoted strings. Quoted, they are treated as family names, not generic fallbacks. Examples:
  - `'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif'`
  - `'Playfair Display','Times New Roman', 'serif'`
- **Comment before DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering engines.
- **Duplicate selector.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice in the same stylesheet (minor).
- **Web font loading.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so those clients use the fallback stacks. The fallback stacks have the quoting defect above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash=`** is the only confirmed unresolved value (see section 2).
- **Empty font-family slot** (`Helvetica, Arial, , 'Font-Normal'`) suggests a template variable that did not render.
- Greeting and body tokens could not be checked because the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain, so alignment needs checking against the raw message.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed.** All 37 links go through tracking redirects that were not resolved.
- **Possible template carry-over.** The pixel path `100926_AN-US_Combo30OffFashion30OffHome50OffSOSReminder` names a combined Fashion 30% / Home 50% / SOS reminder campaign. The subject mentions only fashion. This is an unconfirmed observation about the campaign naming and needs a check against the resolved landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-run QA with the full raw message.** This covers headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) and the untruncated body. Resolve the tracking redirects to check landing URLs and UTMs.
2. **Fix `dtm_email_hash`.** Confirm it populates per recipient in the original send. If it is genuinely empty, the Dotomi pixel is doing nothing.
3. **Add `alt` text:** `alt=""` on the decorative pencil-line banners and tracking pixels, and descriptive `alt` on `1e313de8…png` if it carries content.
4. **Set a `<title>`** (for example, the subject line).
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0`** from the viewport meta.
6. **Repair the Outlook CSS:** remove the empty font-family entry and the stray `;` before `!important`.
7. **Unquote generic families:** use `sans-serif` and `serif`, not `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'`.
8. **Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE** and remove the duplicate `Font-Normal-os` rule.
