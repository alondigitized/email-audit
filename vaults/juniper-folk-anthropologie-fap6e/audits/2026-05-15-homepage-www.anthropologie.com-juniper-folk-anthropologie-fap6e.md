# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Anthropologie and got a wall. Not a hero shot, not a flowy dress, not a candle — just "Access is temporarily restricted" and a bot-detection error page. Whatever the brand is pushing right now, I have zero idea, because their anti-bot layer blocked me before I ever saw a pixel of their actual homepage.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark renders clearly and recognizably at the top — at least I know I'm at the right brand
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link is functional — there IS a recovery path, however buried

## What's weak

- The page served me a bot-block error instead of the homepage — I'm a loyal AnthroPerks member and this is my welcome mat
- The error copy is clinical and cold: "unusual activity from your device or network" reads like an IT helpdesk ticket, not a brand that sells $200 boho dresses
- Four possible causes listed (rapid taps, JS disabled, bot activity from IP 75.140.9.0, dev tools) — none of them are how a normal shopper opens an app
- No fallback, no redirect, no "tap here to continue" button — just a dead end and an ID string
- Zero brand voice, zero warmth, zero product imagery — a complete abandonment of the Anthropologie aesthetic the second something goes wrong

## Recommendations

- Add a human-friendly fallback for false-positive bot blocks: a CAPTCHA or "tap to confirm you're human" button so real customers aren't stranded
- Replace the clinical error copy with on-brand language — even "Something's off on our end, let's get you back to browsing" is infinitely better
- Surface an email/SMS option on the block page so captured users can get a link to return — don't just eject them
- Run a mobile QA sweep for sessions coming from new IPs or headless environments; this block is likely burning real customers during first-opens
