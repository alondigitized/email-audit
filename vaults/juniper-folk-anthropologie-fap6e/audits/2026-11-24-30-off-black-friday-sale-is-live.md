# 🚨30% OFF BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS LIVE 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about three days, and it is the same creative as the 11/21–11/24 emails. The plaid-edged gift-tag hero carries a giant "30% OFF", with a green "+ EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" strip under it. The subject adds a siren-emoji "IS LIVE" framing, but the body doesn't change. A subscriber who got "EXTRA 40% OFF SALE FOR YOU 👏" earlier today has no new reason to open this one.
- The layout is clean. The hero offer is the first thing the eye lands on, with three red buttons below it: Shop Clothing, Shop Home & Furniture and Shop Gifts. Below that, a grid of themed tiles (Top-Rated Dresses, Holiday Decor Under $50, Sweaters Under $100, Sneakers Under $100, Premium Denim, Beauty Gift Sets) gives clear category entry points. The bottom is a standard "Might We Recommend" product grid and footer.
- Two things hurt it. The "30% OFF" and "Extra 40% OFF*" pair is confusing, because it isn't clear whether the discounts stack or apply to different items. The marquee banner at the very top is also clipped, so it reads "IDAY STARTS NOW! · BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! · BL". The send is competent but redundant, and the volume is the main problem.

## What's working

- The hero "30% OFF" is huge and instantly readable, and the gift-tag graphic is festive and on-season.
- Three distinct red shop buttons (clothing, home, gifts) give clear, category-specific CTAs.
- The themed tiles use concrete price caps ("Under $50", "Under $100") that make the offers feel tangible.
- The "Top Rated" labels under the recommended products add a light social-proof signal.
- The footer has useful utility links (E-Gift Card, Pick Up In Store, App Store).

## What's weak

- The creative is essentially a repeat of the last several sends, so cadence fatigue is the biggest issue.
- The "30% OFF" and "EXTRA 40% OFF*" messaging stacks two percentages with no visible explanation of which applies to what.
- The top marquee banner is cut off at both edges, which looks like a render glitch.
- The "Anthro Black Friday" sender name and the all-caps, siren-emoji subject read as spammy next to the brand's usually refined voice.
- The footer's fine print is very small and dense, and it buries the exclusions behind the asterisks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop sending near-identical emails back to back.** Consolidate the cadence, or give each send a distinct hook such as a category spotlight, a last-chance timer or a free-shipping threshold.
- 2. Clarify the offer in one line under the hero, for example "30% off most styles. Extra 40% off sale items."
- 3. Fix the marquee so the text isn't clipped, or drop it.
- 4. Add a visible deadline, such as "Ends Monday at midnight", to make the urgency credible.
- 5. Restore the brand's normal sender name.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: 30% off + extra 40% off sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday picks: dresses, decor, gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters and sneakers under $100, plus gifts under $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off most styles. Extra 40% off sale. Ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Anthropologie "30% OFF BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS LIVE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, so the audit covers the head, CSS and the QA data. The template has several CSS defects and an accessibility problem in the viewport meta tag. Five images lack alt text, including two third-party tracking pixels. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destination URLs or redirect chains were checked. Their health is unknown, not confirmed good.
- **Third-party pixels are present.**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=` is a Dotomi/Conversant pixel.
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../112426_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedLaunch_AM/003a700000u...` is a second tracking pixel.
  - The `dtm_email_hash=` parameter on the Dotomi URL is **empty**. Either the hash wasn't populated for this recipient or it was stripped. If it should carry a hashed email, the pixel can't attribute the visit.
- **Plain-text version is more than 70% URLs.** QA counted 8,096 of 10,929 characters as URLs, which is the wrapped tracking links. This can add spam-filter weight and makes the text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 images.** The first three are content images and the last two are pixels.
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - Dotomi `1_0`
  - mon-trk `003a700000uTzaxAAC`

  Pixels should carry `alt=""` with explicit 1×1 dimensions so screen readers skip them. Without alt text, clients may announce the filename or URL.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom.** The source has `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>`. `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0` disable pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This gives assistive tech and web-view rendering no document title.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The source has `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`.
  - The empty list entry (`, ,`) is invalid.
  - The `;` before `!important` ends the declaration, so `!important` is a stray token and never applies.
  - Outlook may drop or mis-parse the font stack.
- **Quoted generic font keywords.** The rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes, for example `font-family: 'Nunito Sans', Helvetica, Arial, 'sans-serif'`. Quoted, these are treated as family names rather than generic fallbacks, so the final fallback does nothing.
- **Duplicate selector.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice (under ANTHRO and again under TERRAIN). This is harmless but redundant, and the shared multi-brand CSS adds weight.
- **Web-font `@import`.** The `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` in the `<style>` block is unsupported or stripped by Gmail and several other clients. Rendering depends on the fallback stacks, which are affected by the quoting issue above.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`, which can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering engines.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` were visible in the truncated source. The only personalization concern is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` value noted in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so it may exist at the sender. Unconfirmed either way.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** There is no evidence of RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, but the header may have been stripped in transit.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender domain `s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain, so alignment should be checked from raw headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** These were not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 42 tracking links were skipped, and the final destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source. The only campaign identifiers seen are in the pixel paths (`112426_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedLaunch_AM`, `cli_promo_id=99`). Landing page alignment with the "30% off" claim could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three content images and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the Dotomi and mon-trk pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1.0` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the Outlook `font-family` rule by removing the empty entry and the stray `; !important;`, and unquote the generic keywords (`sans-serif`, `serif`).
4. Populate a `<title>` and move the template comment after the DOCTYPE.
5. Confirm `dtm_email_hash` is populated in the live send.
6. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly rather than through the relay, to resolve the three compliance warnings.
7. Re-run link validation by following the redirects, to confirm destinations, UTMs and landing page status.
8. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part, for example by using shorter or fewer links.
