# Extra 50% off sale is live. Go.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first sale blast. A lavender hero reads "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" with a "SHOP NOW" button. Directly below are six color-blocked category tiles: Clothing, Home, Accessories, Shoes, Dresses and Kitchen & Dining. The big number and the category chips make the send easy to scan. After Oct 22's "20% off, AnthroPerks exclusive" and the week of Fashion Weekend 30% sends, this is the escalation a subscriber would expect.
- The email loses focus after the chips. The AnthroPerks 20% strip repeats the Oct 22 offer, and then the editorial "More to Explore" grid and a nine-tile "You'll ♥ These, Too" block take over. None of those tiles shows a sale price, so the 50% promise never reaches a product. The pale-lavender "SHOP NOW" button on a lavender hero is also weak. Overall it's a solid, brand-consistent send, but the sale is stated and never demonstrated.

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 50% Off* SALE" hero is large and easy to read, with "online & in stores" adding reach.
- Six color-coded category tiles let the reader jump straight to what they want, including Dresses and Shoes.
- The subject "Extra 50% off sale is live. Go." is short and punchy, and it is a step up from the 30% sends.
- The AnthroPerks banner keeps member value visible with a stacked 20% off, a "limited time!" badge and "Join & Shop Today".
- Footer extras (10% off first app order with code ANTHROBSESSED, Klarna, inclusive sizing) add useful reasons to buy.

## What's weak

- The "SHOP NOW" button is pale on a lavender background, so its contrast is low and the CTA is easy to miss.
- No product in the email shows a sale price or a "50% off" marker. The "You'll love these" tiles are unlabeled, with no names, prices or reviews.
- The mid-section "More to Explore" (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Mantel Decor, Holiday Glam) is full-price editorial that dilutes the sale message.
- The email carries two offers, 50% off and 20% off, and the "*" and "**" asterisks suggest exclusions the reader can't see. Fine print sits at the very bottom, so it is buried.
- The nail-art "Holiday Glam" tile with a Celebrity Pink-style product sits oddly in a sale email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic "You'll love these" grid with six to nine sale products showing was/now prices, such as "$198 → $99". This would add the most click intent, because it proves the discount is real.
- 2. Make the CTA a dark or high-contrast button with a specific label like "Shop sale dresses" or "Shop the extra 50% off".
- 3. Put the key exclusions in a one-line note near the hero, such as "Extra 50% off sale items, ends [date]", instead of only in the footer.
- 4. Cut the editorial grid down to one or two sale-adjacent tiles, such as "Holiday party dresses, now 50% off".
- 5. Add a deadline, since "Go." implies urgency but gives no end date.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Third-party pixels:** Two pixels are embedded.
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`

- **Pixel alt attributes:** Both pixels lack an `alt` attribute. This is a minor issue, since tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- The HTML provided is truncated, so the remaining links are not assessable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (content images):** Three images flagged by QA have no `alt` attribute.
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  
  All are on `image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/fe3e11717164057c701470/m/...`. If they carry content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing. If they are decorative, they should have `alt=""`.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** `body, table, td { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important; }` has two defects.
  - The empty list item (`, ,`) makes the whole `font-family` declaration invalid.
  - The semicolon before `!important` leaves a stray token, so the intended override is dropped.
  
  Outlook may ignore the Arial fallback entirely.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** `<!-- ** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy Outlook/IE rendering engines.
- **Web fonts:** The Google Fonts `@import` inside `<style>` is not supported in Gmail or many other clients. Fallback stacks exist (Helvetica/Arial/Georgia), so this is low risk.

- No other unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated HTML.
