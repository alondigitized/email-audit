# Homepage snapshot · www.anthropologie.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- I didn't even make it into the store — I opened Anthropologie on my phone and got a "we detected unusual activity" verification wall with a slide-to-confirm slider instead of the homepage. No hero, no dresses, no candles, nothing. Whatever brand impression this leaves, it's not a shopping one.

## What's working

- The Anthropologie wordmark at the top at least confirms I'm on the right site and it's not a phishing dupe
- The "Submit Feedback About Experience" link at the bottom is a small mercy if I wanted to complain
- The cookie banner rendered fine, for what little that's worth

## What's weak

- The entire above-the-fold (and below-the-fold, from what's visible) is a "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" gate with a slide-to-verify puzzle — this is the whole experience
- It lists reasons I might be flagged (rapid taps, JS disabled, bot activity on my IP, dev tools open) which reads like a wall of text aimed at engineers, not at someone in a boho maxi dress browsing on her lunch break
- My actual IP address is printed right there in the copy, which feels invasive to show a regular shopper
- There's a stray cookie-consent banner overlapping the bottom of the verification box, so even the error page has a layout hiccup
- Zero brand warmth — no product, no styling, no candles, no Anthro Day mention, nothing that tells me this is the store I love

## Recommendations

- Fix whatever is triggering this bot check for normal mobile visitors — I'm not doing anything unusual, I just opened the app
- If a verification step is unavoidable, restyle it to match the brand (their typography is doing a little work here, but the rest is generic security-vendor UI) instead of dumping raw diagnostic text like my IP and "developer tools" language at a casual shopper
- Fix the overlapping cookie banner that's clipping into the verification module
- Add a lightweight "having trouble? try refreshing" path that doesn't require me to understand what "automated bot activity" means
