# Today ONLY! AnthroPerks Early Access
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 30% off Fashion Weekend promo Anthropologie has run since Oct 15, now framed as "Today ONLY! AnthroPerks Early Access." The Oct 17 send ("Ends soon! 30% off clothing & accessories") had the same offer. The "ends tonight | online only" banner is the third urgency escalation in a row, so it reads as stale.
- The layout is polished. A full-bleed editorial hero fills the first screen, with a muted "ON MY WAY" panel under it. The yellow "ends tonight" strip and the orange "30% OFF" block sit below that. The rest is a clean grid of category tiles and a nine-product "You'll ♥ these, too" section. The problem is order: the actual offer is pushed well below the hero photo, and the loyalty angle in the subject gets almost no payoff in the body.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send that is quiet about what it promises. Early access and 30% off are two different hooks, and neither lands hard. The fatigue from this week's near-identical sends will hurt more than the creative helps.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" block is large, orange-red and unambiguous, and it carries a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The yellow "ends tonight | online only" strip gives a credible, specific deadline.
- The editorial hero is strong brand imagery, and the sequin top matches the holiday-party theme.
- The category tiles (Trend Hub, Holiday Party Shop, Cashmere, Mantel Décor, Watches, Beauty Gift Sets) give many entry points. Each has a short, clear label.
- The AnthroPerks tie-in appears in the header banner and the "Join AnthroPerks for Exclusive Benefits" tile. There are no visible render bugs or broken images.

## What's weak

- The offer is below the hero photo, so the first screen sells mood, not a discount.
- The subject promises "Early Access," but the body never explains what early access means. The "Join now to shop first" line doesn't match a sale that has run all week.
- The CTA is a small "Shop Now" pill, and the hero photo itself has no visible button. The headline "ON MY WAY" is vague.
- The 30% claim has an asterisk, and the fine print only appears at the very bottom, in tiny text.
- The email is very long, with around 15 image tiles. The six category tiles and nine products have no prices, reviews or product names, so there's no reason to click one over another.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "30% OFF Clothing & Accessories — ends tonight" block to the top, directly under the header, and shrink the hero photo. The offer should be visible on the first mobile screen.
- 2. Say what "early access" means: early to what? If there's no real perk, drop that framing. If there is, state it ("Members shop first"). Don't use the same hook that has run all week.
- 3. Make the hero image clickable and add a visible "Shop party dresses" or "Shop 30% off" button to it. Replace "Join now to shop first" with a specific action.
- 4. Cut the tile count, or add price and "30% off" tags on products. Lead with one or two best-sellers and put the fine print closer to the offer.
- 5. Vary the creative day to day. This is the sixth consecutive send with the same offer, so a fresh hero or a different product focus would help.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day for 30% off holiday dresses & knits`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `AnthroPerks members shop the 30% off sale before it ends at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Party dresses, cashmere & gift sets, all 30% off through tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template (`s.anthropologie.com`, `image.s.anthropologie.com/lib/...`). Its main confirmed issues are malformed font-stack CSS, a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom, missing `alt` on several images, and a link-heavy plain-text part. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **39 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations or HTTP statuses are verified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels are present:**
  - `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/95bb6f26-.../101826_AN-US_PerksPreshopOnMyWay_NonMembers/003a700000uTzax...`
- **The dotomi URL ends in `dtm_email_hash=` with no value.** That could be an unresolved merge field or a QA display truncation. The data doesn't settle which.
- **The mon-trk path appears to contain a Salesforce Contact ID** (`003a700000uTzax…`, truncated). That sends a CRM identifier to a third-party domain, which is worth a privacy review.
- **Plain-text part is link-dominated:** 7578 of 9760 characters (~78%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. Long tracked URLs in the text alternative can hurt readability and spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (confirmed in the QA output):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg` (listed twice)
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg` (listed twice)
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`

  With images blocked, these render as nothing or as a raw filename. If they are decorative, they need an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** the `1_0` (dotomi) and `003a700000uTzaxAAC` (mon-trk) images are not content. They should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS:** the `<!--[if mso]>` block contains `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`. It has an empty list entry and a stray `;` before `!important`, so the declaration is invalid and Outlook ignores the intended override.
- **Quoted generic font families:** the `[style*="Font-Normal-xx"]` rules use `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'` in quotes. Quoted, these are treated as font names, not generic fallbacks, so the stacks have no real generic fallback.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` prevents pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Document-level gaps:**
  - `<title></title>` is empty.
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - An HTML comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`, which can trigger quirks mode in older IE and Outlook rendering engines.
- **Web font loading:** the CSS `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/...')` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients, so those clients fall back to the (flawed) stacks above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear in the truncated source or the QA data. The only possible exception is the empty `dtm_email_hash=` parameter above, which is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. If the headers are really absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is a relay artifact, not a confirmed failure.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither is visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm or deny them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The QA data can't support a finding here. All 39 click-tracked links were skipped, so their final landing URLs and any UTM parameters weren't resolved. The only campaign identifiers visible are in the pixel URLs (`cli_promo_id=99`, `101826_AN-US_PerksPreshopOnMyWay_NonMembers`), not in destination links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three content images, and `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels and any decorative banners.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Fix the MSO font rule to `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif !important;` (no empty entry, no stray `;`). Unquote `serif` and `sans-serif` in the web-font stacks.
4. Add `lang="en"` and a non-empty `<title>`, and move the `<!DOCTYPE>` above the comment.
5. Check whether `dtm_email_hash=` is populated in a real send and not just in the QA copy.
6. Review whether a CRM Contact ID belongs in the `mon-trk.com` pixel path.
7. Shorten or simplify the plain-text part to get the URL ratio under ~70%.
8. Re-run QA against raw headers, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
9. Resolve the redirect chains on a sample of the 39 tracked links to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
