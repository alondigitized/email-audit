# EXTRA 40% OFF SALE FOR YOU 👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth Anthropologie Black Friday send I've audited in about three days. It reuses the plaid-edged gift-tag hero with the giant "30% OFF" from the 11/21 to 11/23 emails. The "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" strip sits underneath, as it did in "Black Friday = EXTRA 40% OFF SALE" earlier today. The hero is clear and the eye lands on the offer first. Nothing about it is new.
- The body is a tidy category grid: sweaters, decor, shoes, dresses, sleepwear, furniture, jewelry, kitchen, beauty, wedding, Terrain. A "Might we recommend" product block follows. The layout is well organized and on-brand. It is also a long scroll with no single product pushed, and it has too many equal-weight CTAs.
- The offer is stacked and hard to parse: 30% off, plus an extra 40% off sale, each with an asterisk and exclusions. Anyone who got the last nine emails will find this one adds nothing. The only differences are a subject line with a clap emoji and a "for you" framing that the email doesn't deliver.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" tag hero is large, high-contrast and festive, and it dominates the top of the email.
- Three clearly labeled gold buttons (Clothing, Home & Furniture, Gifts) let you choose a path right away.
- The green "EXTRA 40% OFF* SALE" band with its own "Shop Clothing" button stands out.
- The category tiles use editorial photography and short labels, so they are easy to scan.
- The "Might we recommend" block carries "Top Rated" tags, which add some social proof.

## What's weak

- The creative is a near-duplicate of the last several sends, so the cadence feels like fatigue.
- Two overlapping discounts ("30% OFF" and "EXTRA 40% OFF*") are confusing about which applies to what.
- The hero is cropped at the top. The banner reads "…RIDAY STARTS NOW!" and looks like a cut-off marquee.
- The tile grid has no prices, offer detail or urgency, and the rows are all the same size, so nothing leads.
- The recommended products have no names or prices, and the images are small. The "Top Rated" tags don't show a rating.
- The fine print is dense, and the exclusions are buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the headline. Say what the extra 40% applies to, with a visible "ends Monday" deadline, and keep the tag hero only if it carries new information.
- 2. Replace the equal-weight tile grid with one hero product or category, then a smaller grid. Add price points or "from $X" to each tile.
- 3. Fix the cropped top banner so it reads fully, or remove it.
- 4. Add product names, prices and star ratings to the recommended block, and link each to its product page.
- 5. Vary the creative day to day. Use a "last chance" or "ends tonight" treatment instead of re-sending the same tag.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 40% off sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 40% off: sweaters, shoes & decor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top-rated sweaters, cozy sleepwear and holiday decor, all included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off sitewide plus an extra 40% off sale styles. Last chance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud template (`image.s.anthropologie.com`, `pxl.mon-trk.com`) with 42 click-tracked links. The QA run found no hard failures. The confirmed source issues are malformed CSS, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, missing alt text, and a tracking pixel with an empty `dtm_email_hash`. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so the destinations and HTTP status are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Empty `dtm_email_hash` in the Dotomi pixel.** The pixel is `https://login.dotomi.com/profile/visit/px/1_0?dtm_cid=2466&dtm_cmagic=7b41bf&dtm_fid=103&cli_promo_id=99&dtm_email_hash=`. If this reflects the delivered message, the hash did not resolve and the pixel cannot match the recipient. The test inbox may have no hash, so confirm against a real-subscriber send.
- **Subscriber identifier in a third-party pixel.** The `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel path ends in `003a7000…`. The `003` prefix is the Salesforce Contact ID format, and the ID is passed to a third-party domain. This is an observation, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `482199364-emailbanner_pencilline_1.jpg`
  - `482199362-emailbanner_pencilline_2.jpg`
  - `1e313de8-0c8e-41c2-97a7-2d33a8cff42e.png`
  - The two tracking pixels (`dotomi 1_0`, `mon-trk 003a7000…`) also lack alt text. Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=0"/>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 on mobile.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This affects web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Malformed Outlook CSS.** The block is `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;`:
  - The empty entry between the commas looks like an unresolved template variable.
  - The stray `; !important;` is invalid, so the declaration's `!important` never applies.
  - Because it sits inside `<!--[if mso]>`, only Outlook desktop is affected.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- ****** ANTHROPOLOGIE TEMPLATE ****** -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE html>`. That can trigger quirks mode in some legacy IE and Outlook engines.
- **Invalid generic font names.** The font stacks quote the generic family, as in `'sans-serif'` and `'serif'`. Quoted, it is read as a font name rather than the generic keyword, so the fallback never applies.
- **Duplicate or redundant CSS.** `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` is declared twice, and `local('Clearface'), local('Clearface')` is repeated. These are harmless.
- **Google Fonts `@import`.** It is stripped by Gmail and some other clients, so it only works as progressive enhancement. The Arial/Helvetica fallbacks are present.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so body markup, tables, and button structure could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The empty `dtm_email_hash=` is the only sign of an unresolved dynamic value, and the empty font-family entry above looks like another. See sections 2 and 3.
- No visible `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[First Name]` tokens appear in the truncated source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, but it is a risk under the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements. Check the raw `.eml` at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown. The sender `anthropologie@s.anthropologie.com` is a subdomain, so alignment cannot be assessed.
- **The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs.** 8,148 of 10,982 characters are URLs, from long tracking links. This is a mild spam-filter signal.
- The physical address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** All 42 links go through a click-tracking redirect and were skipped, so destination UTMs, landing-page status, and promo-code carryover are unknown.
- **The pixel URL implies the campaign name.** `112326_AN-US_PromoBlackFridayContinuedReminder2_PM` is the campaign identifier. Confirm the landing page matches the "EXTRA 40% OFF" offer in the subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the three banner images, and `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1.0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the Outlook CSS: remove the empty font-family entry and the stray `; !important;`, and unquote the `serif` and `sans-serif` generics.
4. Move the template comment after the DOCTYPE, and give `<title>` a value.
5. Confirm `dtm_email_hash` resolves in a real-subscriber send.
6. Check the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay.
7. Resolve a sample of the tracked links in a browser to confirm UTMs and landing-page alignment.
8. Shorten the plain-text part, or add more descriptive text to it.
