# allow us to introduce our GREATEST HITS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Anthropologie leads with a G.O.A.T. ("Greatest Of All Time") concept — a curated "greatest hits" product showcase presented in their signature editorial, magazine-panel style. The creative is clean and on-brand: mixed lifestyle photography, tight grid layouts, and the slightly whimsical script-on-caps typographic combo that Anthropologie does consistently well. The concept is smart; positioning specific SKUs as cult bestsellers gives the email a reason to exist beyond routine promotion.
- The problem is what this email is *not*: it carries no concrete offer. There's no percent-off, no free shipping threshold, no flash sale — just products. Coming one day after the ANTHRO+ loyalty launch ("We've been waiting for you," May 16), this send feels rushed. A subscriber who just received an emotional welcome into the loyalty program yesterday is now getting a product catalog push the very next morning. That cadence mismatch undermines both emails.
- The G.O.A.T. framing is charming in isolation, but without a hook — a discount, a member-exclusive, a limited restock — it's style over substance. This is a discovery email that asks for a click without giving you a reason to click *now*.

## What's working

- **G.O.A.T. concept is genuinely clever** — "Greatest Of All Time" applied to product bestsellers is memorable and differentiating; it gives editorial permission to be bold.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — the hero lands first, product grid follows logically; eye doesn't get lost.
- **Brand voice is consistent** — lowercase subject line, script-meets-serif typographic identity, warm lifestyle imagery all read unmistakably Anthropologie.
- **Product variety is evident** — apparel, home, and accessories are all represented, broadening click surface.
- **No render bugs** — the email renders cleanly; no broken images, no obvious layout collapse.

## What's weak

- **No offer** — a "greatest hits" showcase with no price reduction, member discount, or urgency signal is a hard ask for a click; gives subscribers no reason to act today vs. any other day.
- **Day-after cadence undercuts the loyalty launch** — sending a hard product push 24 hours after a sentimental ANTHRO+ welcome ("We've been waiting for you") feels transactional, not relational.
- **Hero copy is vague** — the G.O.A.T. subhead doesn't communicate anything about what makes these items the "greatest" (bestsellers? Editor picks? Most reviewed?).
- **No social proof** — this email is purpose-built for bestsellers; reviews, star ratings, or "X sold this week" callouts are entirely absent.
- **GREATEST HITS in ALL CAPS in the subject** — borderline flag in some inbox filters; stylistically inconsistent with their usual sentence-case brand voice.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a member hook tied to ANTHRO+** — since the loyalty launch just went out yesterday, this email is a missed opportunity to say "As an ANTHRO+ member, you get early access to our G.O.A.T. picks." That bridges the two sends and makes the cadence feel intentional.
- 2. **Anchor one "greatest" claim with proof** — add one visible data point: "Over 10,000 5-star reviews" or "Back by popular demand" or "Selling out fast." Best-seller framing needs receipts.
- 3. **Add urgency** — even a soft deadline ("through Sunday") or a low-inventory signal ("almost gone") gives subscribers a reason to click today.
- 4. **Reframe the hero subhead** — instead of a vague "greatest hits," specify: "Our most-loved, most-reordered pieces — all in one place."
- 5. **Restructure send cadence** — this email would land harder as a standalone mid-week send, not the morning after an emotional loyalty onboarding.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The styles our customers reorder again and again — shop the full G.O.A.T. edit.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Anthropologie "GREATEST HITS" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a shared URBN multi-brand template with several infrastructure gaps: compliance headers are absent from the relayed copy, 8 images lack alt text, and a syntax error in the Outlook-only MSO stylesheet may silently break font rendering for Outlook clients. Deliverability is flagged by the URL-heavy plain-text alternative.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**21 tracking links skipped** by the QA probe — all route through a click-redirect domain and could not be validated for destination or redirect chain integrity. No broken links were confirmed, but destinations are unverified.

**Plain-text body is 70.4% URLs** (9,466 of 13,444 chars). Spam filters treat URL-saturated plain-text as a signal; the plain-text part should be a human-readable summary, not a dump of raw redirect URLs.

**Tracking pixel domain** (`pxl.mon-trk.com`) is a third-party domain not under `anthropologie.com` or `s.anthropologie.com`. This is normal for ESP open-tracking but worth confirming it is the contracted vendor and not a stale/misconfigured pixel endpoint.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS syntax error in MSO (Outlook) stylesheet:**
```css
/* line in <!--[if mso]> block */
font-family: Arial, Helvetica, , 'Font-Normal'; !important;
```
Two bugs on the same declaration:
- Double comma produces an empty string as a font-family entry, which is invalid.
- The semicolon before `!important` terminates the property early, making `!important` an orphaned token. The declaration will not apply with `!important` priority in Outlook.

**Duplicate CSS rule** — `[style*="Font-Normal-os"]` (Open Sans) is declared twice in the same `<style>` block (once under "ANTHRO", once under "TERRAIN"). The second declaration silently overrides the first; no functional difference here, but indicates the shared template is not deduplicated.

**`.yfix{}`** is an empty rule defined three times. Dead code; harmless but contributes to template bloat alongside 9 brand-specific font stacks loaded for a single-brand send.

**8 images missing `alt` text** (from QA findings):

| Image | Domain | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `summary.png` (×3) | `urbanoutfitters.us-1.evergage.com` | No `alt` |
| `e6a722ed-...png` (×2) | `image.s.anthropologie.com` | No `alt` |
| `1e313de8-...png` | `image.s.anthropologie.com` | No `alt` |
| Tracking pixel | `pxl.mon-trk.com` | No `alt` |

Missing `alt` on content images breaks rendering in image-blocked clients (common on desktop Outlook with default settings) and fails WCAG 2.1 AA. The tracking pixel should have `alt=""` explicitly set.

**Multi-brand font import** — the `@import` call loads 8 Google Font families (Playfair Display, François One, Nunito Sans, Spectral, Marcellus, Karla, Cormorant Garamond, Open Sans) plus two WOFF files from `community.urbn.com`. Only Nunito Sans / Spectral are Anthropologie fonts. The rest inflate load time and are never applied in this send.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Evergage userId appears truncated.** The three Evergage recommendation image URLs use:
```
userId=003a700000uTza
```
The tracking pixel for the same subscriber uses the full 18-character Salesforce ID:
```
003a700000uTzaxAAC
```
The Evergage URLs are 4 characters short. If `userId` is used server-side to personalize the recommendation images, a truncated ID may cause fallback content or a lookup miss. Needs verification against the Evergage dataset configuration.

No unrendered merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) were visible in the truncated HTML — personalization appears to have fired before send.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header: not found** (QA WARN). This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (≥5k/day) since February 2024. Its absence from the relayed copy may be an AgentMail capture artifact, but it must be confirmed present in the live SMTP envelope.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found** (QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe is now enforced by Gmail/Yahoo for bulk senders. If this header is genuinely absent, the email is out of compliance with current inbox provider requirements.

**`Authentication-Results` header: not found** (QA WARN). SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the relayed copy. The sending domain `s.anthropologie.com` should have SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured; the relay must preserve or synthesize this header for auditing.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated HTML — these must be present in the rendered footer and were not flagged absent by QA, suggesting they exist below the truncation point.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**21 tracked links** route through a click-redirect domain — UTM parameter carriage cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain. If UTMs are appended at the redirect layer rather than in the raw href, they will not appear in the HTML source and are invisible to pre-send QA tooling. Recommend a spot-check of 2–3 resolved destinations to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are intact on landing.

No issues found with the Evergage recommendation endpoint domain (`urbanoutfitters.us-1.evergage.com`) — this is the expected URBN personalization CDN.
