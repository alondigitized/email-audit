# ⏱️ CLEAROUT ⏱️ ENDS ⏱️ TOMORROW ⏱️ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- The subject says "CLEAROUT ENDS TOMORROW," and the banner says "72-HR CLEAROUT / 60% OFF." Those two don't agree. A 72-hour clock that "ends tomorrow" is plausible, but the email never shows a date or a countdown. The subject's urgency is real, but nothing in the body backs it up. This is also the fourth or fifth Wayfair send in a row with the same red clearout banner on top.
- The body is dominated by pergolas. Twelve or so gazebo and pergola tiles take up most of the "picked just for you" block and the second product block. The category grid below them (rugs, mattresses, bedding, curtains, appliances, fitness, fireplaces, faucets) shows clear "up to X% off" numbers and is the most scannable part. Yesterday's browse-abandonment send, "Your pergola search is over!", used the same pergola inventory, so it's a repeat of what the recipient already saw.
- My judgment is that this is a competent but generic promo. The offer is clear, but the hero is a thin red strip and the first screen is a wall of near-identical pergola photos. The urgency in the subject is stronger than anything in the creative.

## What's working

- The red "72-HR CLEAROUT / 60% OFF" banner is a clear, specific offer at the top, with a deadline.
- The category tiles pair large percentages with labeled purple CTAs ("area rugs →", "mattresses & more →"). That is easy to scan.
- Many product tiles show "Was / NOW" pricing and star ratings, which gives price proof and social proof.
- The purple "shop all sales" bar gives a full-width fallback CTA.
- The page renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The first screen after the banner is a grid of near-identical pergolas. No single hero product stands out.
- The banner is small, and "60% OFF" is tiny against the headline. The offer isn't the first thing the eye lands on.
- The subject's "ENDS TOMORROW" has no matching countdown, date, or time in the body.
- The pergola block repeats a second time lower down, plus "10' W x 10' D pergolas" in "Your Home, These Picks." The email is heavily redundant.
- The product tiles have no captions or titles, only prices and stars. It's hard to tell what you're clicking.
- The footer block (Rewards, Pay Over Time, Professional, Exclusive Brands) has no member-specific benefit and no personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real countdown or "Ends Mon 11:59 PM" in the banner, and make the deadline the hero. That makes the subject's promise visible in the render.
- 2. Cut the pergola grid to two or three tiles with titles and savings, and move the category grid up. Don't send the same pergola set the day after the browse-abandonment email.
- 3. Make the "60% OFF" figure much larger and give it its own CTA button instead of a bare banner.
- 4. Add short product captions (name, % off) under every tile.
- 5. Vary the subject structure. The emoji sandwich around each word is hard to read and looks spammy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearout ends tomorrow: up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 60% off rugs, bedding & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 60% off rugs, bedding, curtains & more. Sale ends Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your pergola picks are still marked down, plus 55% off curtains.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Wayfair ESP-generated XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a full tracking and personalization layer. QA found no hard failures, only 3 warnings. The main issues are missing alt text on many images, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and unverifiable compliance and authentication headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Probing was incomplete.** 12 material links were probed and 61 skipped. 4 more were unprobed because the 70s time budget ran out. They are neither pass nor fail:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/-Aluminum-Pergola-with-Adjustable-Louvered-Roof-Waterpro…`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-Aluminum-Pergola-With-Adjustabl…`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-Adjustable-Aluminum-Gazebo-with…`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/All-Weather-Pergola-With-Curtains-and-Netting-Dark-Gray-…`
- **8 probed links returned HTTP 429.** The affected links are:
  - the app link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`, twice)
  - `/daily-sales/filters/find-the-perfect-pergolas-se73975.html`
  - `/daily-sales`
  - `/` (the logo link)
  - `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout` (the hero)
  - the two product links, `…~ETRS1219.html` and `…~VVE10006.html`

  This is bot rate limiting, so the links are likely valid. Their status is unverified, not broken.
- **Per-recipient tracking parameters:** `_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `maiid`, `emlid` and `refid=MKTEML_140873` are present on every link. This is expected for an ESP. The `_eml` value is unique per link (`800f88ad…` vs `197f9a1c…` on the two app links), so per-link click attribution is working.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3BlbiZ…` has no alt text.
- **Impression pixels:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` pixels are served over plain HTTP.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - Hero `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`. This is also the link text for the hero's click-through, so the link has no accessible name when images are blocked.
  - 12 `stars_9/10/11.png` rating images. Star ratings are unreadable to screen readers and with images off.
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`. This is a button image, so the CTA is invisible when images are blocked.
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels:** 21 pixels in total, the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` ones plus `t.wayfair.com/b.php`. Use `alt=""` on these. A missing attribute is worse than an empty one for accessibility.
- **Insecure image sources:** The 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. If blocked, impression counts are undercounted.
- **Reused-asset signals:** Filenames such as `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024…` and `0125_48hourclearance…` suggest assets reused from earlier campaigns. This is not a defect on its own.
- **Web font:** `@font-face` loads `Sofia` from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com` with a woff-only source and `mso-font-alt: Arial`. Fallback is declared, so no issue.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied truncated source or the QA data. The source is truncated, so the body was not fully checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip it, so this may be a capture artifact.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the result isn't listed. The footer is outside the truncated source, so it can't be confirmed here.

None of the three header findings is confirmed as a real defect. They are unverifiable with this capture method.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use Wayfair's proprietary scheme (`source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=10`, `refid=MKTEML_140873`, `wfcs=cs7`) instead. This is fine for first-party analytics but won't populate GA campaign reports without mapping.
- **Landing alignment:** The hero goes to `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, which matches the "CLEAROUT" subject. This could not be verified because of the 429 response.
- **App link (`onelink.me`):** It uses `c=Triggered` on a batch campaign (`source=batch`). That is a mismatch that may misattribute the campaign in AppsFlyer. It also carries `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, an HTTP fallback URL.
- **Subject vs. content:** The hero asset is named `72HR_Spring2026…` and the product links are pergolas. The subject says "ENDS TOMORROW". Whether the page content matches could not be confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, the CTA button image and the content images. Add `alt=""` to all 21 tracking pixels.
2. Give the star-rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or render ratings as text.
3. Move `li.wayfair.com/imp` to HTTPS to avoid blocking and mixed-content issues.
4. Change the AppsFlyer `c=Triggered` to a batch or campaign value, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw SMTP headers. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original message, not the relay copy.
6. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with a longer budget and backoff.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that the proprietary tracking is mapped into the analytics stack.
