# Price just dropped on Pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Price just dropped on Pergolas," and the render breaks that promise right at the top. The hero is a blank white box with one tiny broken-image icon. The first thing a subscriber sees is a hole, with nothing about pergolas or a price drop in it. This is the same failure as the 2026-10-15 "Just dropped: Pergolas on sale now" send, which also opened on an empty hero.
- Below the hero, "on sale: from your browsing" delivers four pergola and gazebo tiles with Sale badges, was/now pricing and star ratings. That is the real payoff, and it works. Then the email drifts into a generic catch-all: cleaning, financing, rattan, garden, sleek decor, "all under $29.99," and a "but wait, there's more" grid. Only one more pergola tile appears in the "we saw these catch your eye…" block.
- Judgement: the browse-abandonment idea is good, and the pricing tiles are credible. But the broken hero and the long, unfocused scroll undercut it. It is also the fifth or sixth pergola send in four days, so fatigue is likely. It's an average send that fixing one image would improve.

## What's working

- The "on sale: from your browsing" block shows four pergola and gazebo tiles with Sale badges, was/now prices and star ratings.
- The subject names the product and says the price dropped, which suits a browse-abandonment send.
- Dense Wayfair product photography is clean, and the section headings are readable.
- Each module ends in a clear purple "Shop … →" pill button, so the CTAs are easy to spot.
- The "On sale now: pergolas" link in the closing grid and the "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner near the bottom keep the seasonal promo present.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank white box with a small broken-image icon. It takes the prime above-the-fold space and shows no offer, no pergola and no headline.
- The only pergola content with pricing is four small tiles below the hero. The prices are tiny, and the discount depth isn't stated.
- After the pergola block the email turns into a catalog: cleaning, a credit-card promo, rattan, garden, sleek decor, and "under $29.99." This dilutes the subject's promise.
- The "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner sits at the very bottom, where few readers will see it. In earlier sends it was at the top.
- The cadence is heavy. This is the same pergola story as 10/13, 10/14 and 10/15, with no new angle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Replace the broken image with a pergola hero that carries a specific drop, such as "Pergolas now up to 40% off." Add a fallback background color and alt text so that if the image fails, a headline still shows.
- 2. Cut the email to the pergola story plus one or two adjacent modules, such as outdoor and garden. Drop the cleaning, rattan, sleek decor and "under $29.99" blocks.
- 3. Move the "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner to the top, under the header, and show the actual savings on each tile.
- 4. Add a single primary CTA right under the hero: "Shop pergola price drops."
- 5. Space out the pergola sends and rotate the angle, for example low stock, shipping dates, or a different category from the browse history.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your pergola just dropped to $[price]`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pergolas you viewed: now up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Price drops on the gazebos and pergolas you browsed. Ships before the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale prices on 4 styles you looked at, while stock lasts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Wayfair batch email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. Most flags are alt-text gaps and about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication can't be assessed because the headers weren't captured, and the product link behind the subject line was never probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing was inconclusive.** Of 12 probed links, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate limited), and none came back as a clean 200. Examples are `/appliances/cat/vacuums-deep-cleaners-c1862812.html`, `/daily-sales/rattan-%26-raffia-picks~e318772.html` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`. A 429 is not a broken link, so no dead links are confirmed.
- **The subject-line link was never checked.** The "Pergolas" product URL (`/LeanTo-WallMounted-Pergolas-With-Curtains-And-Netting-L7…`) was among the 4 links skipped when the 70s time budget ran out. The two `/daily-sales` links and the homepage link were skipped too.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** There are 20 impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `…019&sz=2x1`) served over `http://`. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The OneLink URL's `af_web_dp` parameter is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback is also non-HTTPS. This is from the URL string only, and I haven't confirmed that it redirects.
- **Very long URLs.** Each tracked URL carries about 15 query parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `batchid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `mdlid` and others). That adds weight but is not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing `alt`:**
  - `stars_9.png` (×3) and `stars_10.png` (×2) are review-rating graphics. They carry real information, so screen readers and images-off views lose the ratings.
  - `WFCC2%402x.png` and `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png` (Wayfair credit-card promo and disclosure) both lack alt text. A missing disclosure alt is also a legibility problem when images are blocked.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png` and `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-general.jpg` are banners with no alt.
  - The banner `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty` has no alt. Its filename contains a raw apostrophe, which is an unescaped character in a URL path. Its "0824_LaborDay" name also suggests a reused August asset, though I can't confirm it's stale from the source shown.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` (open pixel) and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, which is low severity.
- **Inline CSS:** The `u ~ div`, `u + .Gmail` and `body[data-outlook-cycle]` selectors are deliberate client hacks and not defects. The HTML was truncated, so I can't verify the body markup, `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"`, or preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The per-recipient IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `p=`) are populated. The source was truncated, so the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** Not found. This is likely because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture it, so it is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign but proves nothing.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** The QA run reported that an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but its status isn't shown in the output. The postal address wasn't visible in the truncated HTML. Neither is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own parameters instead, which are consistent across links: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `refid=MKTEML_121641`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16438`. This is fine for first-party analytics but won't map to GA4 channel grouping automatically.
- **Landing alignment is unverified for the subject-line product.** The "Pergolas" URL was never probed (see section 2), so I can't confirm the price-drop claim matches the destination. The category links (vacuums, rattan, garden, bedding) point to topically matching paths. The email's name-based link text, such as "Shop cleaning picks →", can't be checked against the destination page content.
- **App deep link:** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` with `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`. The campaign label `Triggered` on a `graymatter_batch` send is inconsistent, and attribution may be misclassified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the unprobed links with backoff or a longer budget, especially the Pergolas product URL and the homepage link. Re-run the 8 links that returned 429.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the review-star images, the credit-card promo and disclosure images, and the banners. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to `https://` and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
4. Escape or rename the asset `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als…` so the filename has no raw apostrophe.
5. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct inbox copy or a header-preserving relay. Until then, treat the compliance and authentication status as unknown.
6. Fix the OneLink `c=Triggered` campaign label to match the batch send, or confirm the mismatch is intentional.
