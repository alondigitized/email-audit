# BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS 🔎 Find one that's all you →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is one of the more disciplined Wayfair sends in the recent run — the hero's first tile literally reads "Must-Have Black Outdoor Privacy Screens," which is a direct, word-for-word payoff of the subject line. That's a real improvement over the 8/1 "CAT TREES" send, where the hero completely ignored the subject, and over 7/31, where the hero image was simply broken.
- But the follow-through is weak. "New Price Cuts" is the headline, yet no actual price or percentage appears anywhere near the top — you have to scroll past two full product grids before hitting any dollar figures ("Grilling accessories from $25," "Knife Sets Under $99"). The CTA is buried at the bottom in a generic "Snag 'Em ASAP" banner that isn't tied to any specific product or discount.
- Net: subject/hero alignment is genuinely good this time, but the email is long, price-light at the top, and leans on the same recycled tile-grid template Wayfair runs almost every day. Solid, not great.

## What's working

- The hero's top-left tile — "Must-Have Black Outdoor Privacy Screens" — matches the subject line exactly, unlike the 8/1 cat-tree/outdoor-clearance mismatch.
- Every image renders cleanly; no broken-image icons like the 7/31 privacy-screens send had.
- "Must-Grab Deal" tiles carry star ratings and "___ Recently Purchased" counts, giving light social proof.
- Loyalty and membership perks (Rewards, Flexible Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) are clearly laid out near the footer.

## What's weak

- No visible discount number or price near the top — "New Price Cuts" is a promise with no evidence until well into the email.
- The primary CTA ("Snag 'Em ASAP") sits in a generic red banner at the very bottom, disconnected from any product or offer.
- The four-tile hero grid splits attention across privacy screens, pergolas, pendant lights, and outdoor seating instead of committing to one dominant image.
- The email runs three near-identical product grids (12+ tiles) before any real call to action — a lot of scrolling for a Wayfair email that already sends daily.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero copy — replace "New Price Cuts" with something like "Black Privacy Screens — up to 40% off" so the subject's promise is paid off immediately with a figure, not just a label.
- 2. Make the privacy-screens tile the single dominant hero image instead of 1-of-4 equal tiles, so the eye lands exactly where the subject line sent it.
- 3. Move a real, product-specific CTA button up near the hero rather than burying a generic banner at the bottom.
- 4. Cut the "Scores in Your Style" grid — it largely duplicates "These Top Steals Are Going Fast" and just adds scroll length without new information.

# Technical Audit: Wayfair Marketing Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Wayfair promotional email built on a legacy MJML/Unbounce-style responsive template (`u-*` class system) with heavy tracking-pixel instrumentation. Automated QA scored a 57% pass rate driven primarily by missing header-level compliance signals and widespread missing `alt` text, not by broken links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 73 total links were probed (material/high-click-likelihood links); 61 footer/social/utility links were deprioritized by design.
- 7 probed links returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** during automated probing, including the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Wayfair" (homepage), "On sale now: pergolas," "On Sale Now: Pendant Lights," "Outdoor seating," and the app deep link "Get Exclusive Deals on the App." These are inconclusive (server-side throttling of the QA prober), not confirmed dead links — recommend a manual re-check outside the automated tool's rate limit window.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget cutoff, including a `/daily-sales` variant and a `/pdp/vvenace-standing-desk-max-adjustable-height-home-off...` product link — coverage gap, not a failure.
- All probed marketing links carry a consistent tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `brcid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `sm`, `refid=MKTEML_121815`, `emlid`, `maiid`) — internally consistent, no malformed or truncated query strings observed.
- App deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`) correctly pairs `af_dp` (native scheme) with `af_web_dp` (web fallback) for non-app users.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 open/tracking-pixel images** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. Mail clients that enforce mixed-content/TLS policies (Outlook, some Gmail configurations) may block these, undercounting opens.
- All 20 of the above pixels are also **missing `alt` text** (blank alt is actually correct/expected for pure tracking pixels, so this is low-severity, but flagged since QA reported it).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization placeholders were visible in the available HTML. Note: source was truncated, so this cannot be fully confirmed beyond the captured portion — no issues found in what was reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers. CAN-SPAM does not strictly require this header (a visible unsubscribe link suffices), but its absence means the message won't get client-native one-click unsubscribe treatment in Gmail/Yahoo/Outlook.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe (POST) is not supported, which is now a de facto requirement for bulk senders under Google/Yahoo's 2024 sender guidelines.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found, so SPF/DKIM pass/fail status could not be verified from this capture.
- Caveat: both warnings are flagged as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving/forwarding original headers rather than Wayfair's actual sending configuration — recommend verifying against raw headers at the original MTA before treating as a confirmed deliverability defect.
- A footer unsubscribe link is present in the template per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), satisfying the CAN-SPAM body-level requirement even if header-level one-click support is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Category links map correctly to subject-matter-relevant landing pages: "On sale now: pergolas" → `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now:-pergolas--se82161.html`, "On Sale Now: Pendant Lights" → `se44432.html`, "Outdoor seating" → `/daily-sales/outdoor-seating~e378306.html` — `eid` values are distinct per category and consistent with link text.

- No mismatched or generic fallback URLs (e.g., links resolving to unrelated categories or bare homepage where a product link was expected) observed among probed links.

1. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) links and 4 time-budget-skipped links outside of automated throttling to confirm they resolve (200) before next send.
2. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent open-tracking loss in TLS-enforcing clients.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 creative banner images and the star-rating images (`stars_9/10/11.png`) for accessibility and broken-image fallback.
