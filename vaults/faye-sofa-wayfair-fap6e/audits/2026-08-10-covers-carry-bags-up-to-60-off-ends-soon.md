- Subject promises "COVERS & CARRY BAGS" but the hero and product grid deliver neither — this is a hard content mismatch, not a soft one.
- No carry bags visible anywhere in the entire render.
- The "picked just for you" grid is generic and unfocused (cookware, mugs, a pool, a mini fridge) rather than curated around any stated theme.

Fix the subject-to-hero mismatch first — if the send is themed "Covers & Carry Bags," the hero banner and at least the first 4-6 product tiles need to actually be covers and bags, not a generic clearout grid. This is the single biggest lever; a subscriber who clicks in expecting bags and lands on cookware will bounce and trust the next subject line less.

- 3. If there's no true covers/bags inventory to feature, retitle the subject to match what's actually in the email (e.g., a generic clearout subject) rather than promising a category that isn't there.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Wayfair "COVERS & CARRY BAGS" Promotional Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard Unyer/BEE-style responsive markup with heavy inline CSS; core send infrastructure is functional but several tracking pixels, compliance headers, and accessibility attributes fail baseline checks. QA run completed at 57% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 category warnings, though header-level compliance data could not be captured at all.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 8 of 12 probed material links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) during automated probing, including the primary CTA (`Shop Sale` → `wayfair.com/daily-sales`), the homepage logo link, both app-download links (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`), and 4 product/review-star links. A 429 indicates the link may be valid but the target server throttled the crawler — these need manual verification rather than being treated as broken.
- 4 additional links were left unprobed due to a 70-second time budget cutoff: `Le-Creuset-Toughened-Nonstick-Fry-Pan-L785-K~LEC4744.htm`, `wayfair-product-L509-K~W111163909.html`, `wayfair-product-L1047-K~W003394870.html`, and `daily-sales/filters/must-have-low-pile-rugs-se64938.html`. Status unconfirmed either way.
- 20 tracking/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. Non-HTTPS resources are frequently blocked by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) enforcing mixed-content policies, which would silently drop open/impression tracking for this send.
- Every tracked link correctly carries a consistent click-tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_138742`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15941`, `batchid=3`, `varid=6`), suggesting the ESP-side tracking wrapper itself is intact even where the destination returned 429.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 30 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including the primary hero creative (`72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`), the secondary lifestyle banner (`wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`), a CTA button image (`0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`), and all 20 HTTP tracking pixels plus the `b.php` open-tracking pixel. Missing alt text on the hero/CTA images degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for any client that blocks images by default (subject line/preheader become the only content shown).
- The 20 HTTP-loaded tracking pixels (Section 2) are also an accessibility/rendering risk beyond tracking: if blocked as mixed content, they simply fail silently with no visible impact, but it confirms the template is mixing `http://` and `https://` asset schemes rather than using `https://` uniformly.
- Media queries are scoped correctly (max-width 480px / 320px breakpoints) with `!important` overrides for Outlook/Gmail-specific hooks (`u ~ div`, `u + .Gmail`), consistent with standard multi-client responsive patterns — no structural rendering defects found in the visible markup.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags, `{{...}}` placeholders, or broken personalization tokens found in the visible/truncated HTML. Tracking IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) are populated with real UUIDs rather than left as literal placeholders, indicating personalization/merge resolved correctly at send time.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication Headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers. Could not confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level — QA notes this may be a relay-side capture gap (AgentMail) rather than confirmation the header is absent from the original send.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules as of Feb 2024) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status is unknown from this capture. Given Wayfair is a high-volume commercial sender, this is very likely a relay/capture limitation rather than an actual absence of authentication, but it cannot be verified from available data.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA probing notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in the probing methodology), though its specific URL was deprioritized from probing and not independently validated.
- **Recommendation**: these three header gaps should be treated as relay/instrumentation issues to fix in the audit pipeline (verify AgentMail is preserving/forwarding raw headers) before concluding anything about Wayfair's actual compliance posture.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)
- Wayfair does not use standard UTM parameters; instead uses proprietary tracking params (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_138742`, `emlid`, `maiid`) consistently across all sampled links — internally consistent scheme, just not UTM-standard, so no continuity gap from a UTM-alignment perspective.
- Primary CTA (`Shop Sale`) and hero image link both point to `/daily-sales`, and product-specific links carry matching `sku`/`mplid` params to their respective PDP URLs (e.g., `sku=MTGL1000` → `Monument-Grills-Cover-4-Burner-GrillFits...MTGL1000.html`) — link text/target/SKU are aligned where checked.
- App-download links use AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with both deep-link (`af_dp`) and web-fallback (`af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`) params correctly set — note the fallback URL is `http://`, not `https://`, which is a minor inconsistency worth flagging for correction even though it's not a tracking pixel.
- Cannot fully confirm landing-page alignment for the CTA/hero/product links due to the 429s and time-budget skips in Section 2 — recommend re-running probing with backoff/retry against `wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me`.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels (and the app fallback URL) from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent tracking loss from mixed-content blocking.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, CTA button, and lifestyle banner images at minimum (tracking pixels can reasonably stay empty-alt/`alt=""` by convention, but decorative-vs-informational distinction should be made deliberately, not by omission).
3. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 time-budget-skipped links with retry/backoff before drawing conclusions on link health — current data is inconclusive, not failing.
4. Investigate why `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers weren't captured — confirm whether this is an AgentMail relay stripping/not-forwarding headers versus an actual sender-side gap, since the latter would be a real CAN-SPAM/bulk-sender-requirements risk for Wayfair and the former is a pipeline fix on our end.
