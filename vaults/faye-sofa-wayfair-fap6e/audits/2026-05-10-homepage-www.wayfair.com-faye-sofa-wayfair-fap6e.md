# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Wayfair clearly knows I'm here for the sale — the Memorial Day push is loud and immediate, all red-white-blue plaid with a big "up to 70% OFF" badge and a members-only hook front and center. This homepage is doing one thing: screaming that the clock is running out on deals I need to unlock by joining Rewards. The energy is right for someone like me who literally refreshes on sale days, but the lifestyle execution feels thin — I'm seeing a tufted brown leather office chair below the fold when I'm here for a walnut console or a boucle accent chair.

## What's working

- **The "up to 70% OFF" badge** — that number stops my thumb. I came here for Way Day energy and this delivers it.
- **"MEMBERS-ONLY EARLY DEALS"** — I love a VIP layer. If I'm already a Rewards member, this makes me feel like I got early access.
- **"FINAL HOURS" headline** — even without a countdown clock, the urgency registers immediately. I'm a little annoyed, which means it's working.
- **The "JOIN TO SHOP MEMORIAL DAY NOW" CTA** — clear ask, unambiguous action. No guessing what happens when I tap.
- **The top sticky banner** ("MEMORIAL DAY | MEMBERS-ONLY DEALS | JOIN NOW →") — smart use of that slim strip to reinforce the hook before the hero even loads.
- **Search bar ("Find anything home...")** — I know exactly what I want (nightstands, sideboard, maybe a rug) and this gets me there in one tap.

## What's weak

- **Zero lifestyle photography** — no styled room, no person living in a home. Just product shots. For someone who browses photos for hours, this is a miss. I shop with my eyes first.
- **The leather tufted office chair below the fold** — brown, traditional, button-tufted. That's not my aesthetic and it makes me question whether the algorithm even knows who I am.
- **No free-shipping mention** — for a starter-home buyer adding a sofa or credenza to cart, the shipping threshold is a make-or-break number. It's not visible anywhere in this viewport.
- **No reviews or ratings** on the product cards below — I read reviews end-to-end before I buy anything big. Showing me a dish set with no star rating doesn't move me toward the cart.
- **"FINAL HOURS" with no clock** — tell me it ends at midnight or 11:59 PM ET. Otherwise it feels like the same urgency they ran yesterday and will run tomorrow.
- **Membership gate as primary CTA** — if I'm already a Rewards member, does "Join to Shop" still apply to me? The copy is slightly confusing for returning members.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the product row imagery for modern-mid-century room scenes** — show me a styled living room with a walnut media console and a linen sofa, not a leather office chair. Use the hero carousel (dots are visible, there are 3 slides) to merchandise style categories, not just a static promo.
- **Add a countdown timer with hours:minutes** under "FINAL HOURS" — even a simple "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET" line makes the urgency credible instead of performative.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the promo strip or below the CTA** — something like "Free shipping on orders $35+" would be the one line that keeps me from bouncing to Amazon for the dish set.
- **Split the CTA copy for members vs. non-members** — if I'm logged in, show "Shop Your Early Deals" instead of "Join to Shop." The current copy creates friction for the customers already in the loyalty program.
