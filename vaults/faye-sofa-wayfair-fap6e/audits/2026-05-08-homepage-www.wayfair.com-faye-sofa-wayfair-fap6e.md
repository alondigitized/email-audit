# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- OK so Wayfair is clearly in full clearout mode right now — the whole above-the-fold experience is one big screaming "the sale ends tonight, come get it." As someone who literally refreshes for Way Day flash sales, the urgency messaging hits me in the gut in a good way, but the page is all discount energy and zero style energy — I can't tell whether this clearout is for the mid-century pieces I actually want or if it's just a pile of random stuff. It reads less like a curated home brand and more like a Tuesday morning flash-sale email blown up to full screen.

## What's working

- **The "ENDS TONIGHT" headline** — as someone who has missed Way Day sales by sleeping in, this creates real action bias in me. The urgency is visceral.
- **The 60% off number in giant red type** — I can read it from across the room. The visual hierarchy is doing everything right here.
- **The "72-Hour Clearout" sub-link below the hero** — that phrase tells me this isn't a random promo, it's a structured event with a name, which makes it feel more legitimate.
- **The search bar prominence** — "Find anything home…" is right at the top and big enough to use with one thumb. I know where to go if the hero doesn't hit what I want.
- **Product thumbnails below the fold** — I can see the beginning of a product rail (blinds, storage shelving) which tells me real items are right there, not buried.

## What's weak

- **Zero style signal in the hero** — the whole thing is bold type on a lavender background. No room shot, no mid-century sofa, no "this is what your living room could look like." For someone who browses photos for hours, this gives me nothing to dream about.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the laziest CTA copy possible** — shop what? The whole site? A category? I'd convert faster on "Shop Clearout" or "Shop Furniture Deals."
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — this is huge for me. I'm about to drop $400-800 on a piece; I need to know if I'm covered. It's not on screen.
- **No reviews or ratings** — even just star counts on the thumbnail products would give me a reason to tap instead of scroll past.
- **No loyalty hook** — I have a Wayfair account and I don't know if I'm logged in, if I have rewards, or if members get an extra percentage. That's a missed pull.
- **Product thumbnails feel random** — blinds and a storage rack aren't the gateway products for someone thinking "sofa, accent chair, coffee table."

## Recommendations

- **Swap the typographic hero for a styled room photo with the offer overlaid** — show a mid-century living room with a "UP TO 60% OFF — Ends Tonight" badge. I need the aspiration AND the urgency, not just the urgency.
- **Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Furniture Deals"** — or at minimum split the CTA into two: Furniture and Decor. Give me a lane.
- **Add a free-shipping line to the banner** — something like "UP TO 60% OFF ENDS TONIGHT | FREE SHIPPING OVER $35 | CLEAROUT →" takes zero space and removes a conversion blocker.
- **Surface the rewards balance or a member badge** — even a small "You have 240 rewards points" chip near the cart icon would make me feel seen as a returning shopper.
