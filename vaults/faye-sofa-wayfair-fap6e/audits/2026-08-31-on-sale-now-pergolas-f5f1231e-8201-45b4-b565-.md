## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair marketing/promotional template (VP/graymatter batch send) built on a responsive MSO/Outlook-conditional table layout; core functionality (links, tracking pixels) appears intact, but the send fails several deliverability, compliance, and accessibility checks.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed; all returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) rather than a clean 200/30x, including the primary hero CTA (`Shop Sale` → wayfair.com/daily-sales) and the subject-line-matching CTA (`On sale now: pergolas` → wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now...se82161.html). 429s are consistent with anti-bot throttling on wayfair.com rather than broken links, but they cannot be confirmed valid from this check.
- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (70s cap), including 3 planter category filters and the `wayfair-rewards` link — status unknown, neither pass nor fail.
- Tracking pixel/impression beacons (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=...`) load over plain HTTP (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...`), not HTTPS — flagged 20 times (s=124126000 through 124126019, sequential creative/slot IDs). Mixed-content HTTP resources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail proxies images over HTTPS automatically, but a direct `http://` src can still fail to load in some clients), which would suppress open/impression tracking for those slots.
- Redirect/beacon URL `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` used for open tracking is otherwise well-formed base64.
