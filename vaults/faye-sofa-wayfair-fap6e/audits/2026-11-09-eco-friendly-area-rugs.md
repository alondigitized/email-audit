- The first tile matches the subject and is a large, attractive, well-styled rug photo.
- The photography is large and clean, and each tile has a clear caption.
- The lower section ("we think you'll love these") adds a 2-column grid with a "40% off" nursery and seasonal-decor row for broader appeal.

## What's weak

- The "eco-friendly" angle is not explained. There is no material, certification or price in the rug tile.
- Only 1 of the 6 hero tiles is eco-friendly rugs. Two tiles are pergolas and one is a gazebo, with kitchen mats in between.
- No tile shows a price or star rating, so the shopper has no reason to click a specific product.
- The 70% claim is vague. It does not say which products qualify.
- The email is a very long single column of tiles, with no clear secondary hierarchy after the hero.
- The deadline ("ends tonight") is repeated from earlier sends and loses credibility.

- 3. Cut the pergola and gazebo tiles from a rug-focused send, or move them below the fold.
- 4. Show a price or "from $X" on every tile.
- 5. Use a credible deadline with a date and time, and vary the promo language from day to day.

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "ECO-FRIENDLY AREA RUGS."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML uses a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with mobile media queries. The confirmed defects are 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, missing alt text on five content or tracking images (two of them are linked images), and a `c=Triggered` campaign label on a batch send. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results are inconclusive, not broken.** All 9 listed probe warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits, for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/eco-friendly-area-rugs-se78333.html?eid=554378&...`. No 4xx or 5xx errors were reported.
- **3 links were unprobed** because the 70s time budget ran out. Their URLs look malformed, though they may only be truncated in the QA output:
  - `.../must-have-pet-friendly-rugs-se78321.` ends in a trailing period with no `.html`.
  - `.../pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=` has an empty `eid` value.
  - `.../best-gazebos%2c-pergolas-%26-trellis` has no `-seNNNNN.html` suffix.
  - Check these against the full HTML.
- **20 ad impression pixels use plain `http://`:**
  - They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`.
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS images will suppress these impressions, which undercounts ad measurement.
  - The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS and is unaffected.
- **The app deep link has an insecure fallback.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me` URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, which is HTTP.
- **Every link carries a unique `_eml` token.** This is expected per-link tracking, and it also explains why each probe was treated as a distinct URL.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge syntax appears in the supplied data. The `_emr` (recipient) and `_eml` (link) tokens are populated with UUIDs on all probed URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.**
  - The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, so it's a capture limitation and not a confirmed sender defect.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers before concluding.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` can't be validated from this data.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address** are not visible in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but it isn't listed in the results, so I can't confirm it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.**
  - Wayfair uses its own scheme: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121727`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16708`, `mdlid`.
  - This is consistent and sufficient for first-party attribution. It is not interoperable with GA-style UTM reporting.
- **Campaign label mismatch.** The app link has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send is labeled as triggered in AppsFlyer, which skews attribution. Check that this is intended.
- **Landing alignment is partly confirmed.**
  - The "Eco-Friendly Area Rugs" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/eco-friendly-area-rugs-se78333.html?eid=554378`, with a matching `unifiedeventid=554378`.
  - The header image, "Wayfair" and "Shop Sale" links go to `/` or `/daily-sales`, not the subject's theme.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the two linked banner images, plus the `sku_top_2` and `blankBanner` images. Use `alt=""` on the pixels.
2. Move `li.wayfair.com/imp` to HTTPS, and change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
3. Inspect the full HTML for the 3 unprobed URLs, especially the empty `eid=` and the trailing-period URL.
4. Re-run the probe with throttling or retry on 429 to get real status codes.
5. Capture the raw message headers directly to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Confirm whether `c=Triggered` is intended for the batch send.
