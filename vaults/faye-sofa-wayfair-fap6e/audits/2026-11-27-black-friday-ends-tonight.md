# 🚩 🚩 BLACK FRIDAY 🚩 ENDS TONIGHT 🚩 🚩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's standard Black Friday template with a "ends tonight" subject on top. Unlike the recent sends ("Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS", "KITCHEN SINKS"), the subject isn't tied to one product. That makes it a better fit for the generic "up to 80% OFF" hero, so the subject and body agree. The urgency is the strongest part of the send. It is the last-day Black Friday email and the render backs it up with a "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block.
- The body is very long and repetitive. Pergolas fill the "picked just for you" grid and the "doorbuster deals" grid, six identical-looking tiles in each. They carry star ratings and "Sale" tags but no prices or names. A reader sees the same product category twice and no price anywhere. The category tiles at the top (bedroom furniture 60%, area rugs 80%, bedding 70%, curtains 70%) are the clearest offers.
- Judgement: a credible urgency send held back by a bloated layout and the pergola repetition. It sits in the usual 6 range for this brand.

## What's working

- The subject's "ENDS TONIGHT" gives a real deadline, and the "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block repeats it.
- The "up to 80% OFF" hero banner with the Sealy feature is clear and sits at the top.
- The category tiles show specific discounts, such as "area rugs up to 80% OFF" and "bedding up to 70% OFF".
- "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" is a visible benefit that lowers friction.
- The "deals on big-name brands" list (Moen: spend $350, save $75; Sealy up to 60% off) gives concrete offers for shoppers who scroll that far.

## What's weak

- The pergola images fill both the "picked just for you" and "doorbuster deals" grids, with no prices or product names. The "just for you" label feels untrue when the same item repeats.
- The email is extremely long, and the footer, loyalty icons and app promo sit under many screens of product.
- The flash-deal tiles (mattresses, Christmas trees, fireplaces) aren't clearly tied to the "ends tonight" urgency.
- The "EXCLUSIVE doorbuster deals" header promises something special, but the tiles show no deal details.
- The red-flag emoji subject reads a bit spammy, and the footer fine print is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the duplicate pergola grids. Replace them with priced hero products from different categories, showing "was/now" prices, so the "doorbuster" label means something.
- 2. Add a countdown timer or "ends at 11:59 PM" line in the header to make the deadline credible and visible.
- 3. Add one clear CTA button, such as "Shop the last-day deals", under the hero instead of relying on tile taps.
- 4. Move the flash deals above "picked just for you" so the urgent content comes first.
- 5. Cut the length by about a third and move the loyalty and app blocks up.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: rugs, beds & more up to 80% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on everything, plus 24-hour flash deals on mattresses and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters close tonight. Shop before they're gone.`

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TONIGHT" email

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The real defects are missing alt text on about 45 images and 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels. Link health and compliance headers could not be confirmed. The HTML was truncated, and 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified, not broken.** All 8 probed material links returned 429 (rate limiting). They include `/daily-sales`, the `bedroom-furniture-clearance~e408852.html`, `area-rug-clearance~e408846.html` and `bedding-clearance~e408850.html` pages, and the app link. 58 links were skipped by design, and 4 more were not probed because of the 70s budget. These are the `curtain-%26-drape-clearance` page and three pergola product URLs. Re-probe with throttling or a different UA before drawing conclusions.
- **App deep link has inconsistent attribution params.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&c=Triggered&...&source=batch&batchid=2` labels a batch send as `c=Triggered`. That can misattribute installs in AppsFlyer.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback.** The app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`. Desktop or no-app users take an extra redirect hop.
- **20 impression pixels use `http://`.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`. They are mixed content, and clients or proxies that upgrade or block insecure requests will undercount ad impressions. Serve them over `https://`.
- **Tracking tokens look correct.** `_emr` (recipient) is constant across links, `_eml` is unique per link, and `refid=MKTEML_145366` is consistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 45 images have no alt text** (QA data):
  - Hero and banner images: `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, `...Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`, `...Skinny-BF-Doorbusters`, `...Skinny-EverythingShips`, `...Skinny-BigBrandNames`.
  - Linked tiles: `1119_BFCM_PHASE2-Email-1xButton-35` through `-39-WFUS.jpg` and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - **The banner and tile images are linked, so the links have no accessible name.** The probe recorded the top banner link text as `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jp]`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no usable label for these CTAs.
  - **About 12 `stars_9/10/11.png` rating images** from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/` also lack alt text, so ratings are not conveyed.
- **Tracking pixels lack `alt=""`.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` images have no alt attribute. They should carry an empty `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Mobile CSS risk.** `@media (max-width:480px) { .ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; ... height: 48px } }` can split product names mid-word and clip them at a fixed height.
- The rest of the source was truncated, so layout and client-compat checks beyond the `<style>` block are not possible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or probed URLs. Per-recipient `_emr`/`_eml` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Presence is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message before treating it as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the same relay caveat applies. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so these CAN-SPAM elements are unverified. The probe reports it always includes an unsubscribe link, but none appears in the findings output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own params (`wfcs=cs7`, `csnid`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `refid=MKTEML_145366`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`). This is consistent across links and fine if the analytics stack reads them. It will not appear in GA channel reports by default.
- Landing-page alignment could not be verified because of the 429s. The URL structure is sound: `/daily-sales/<event>~e<id>.html?eid=<id>&unifiedeventid=<id>`, with matching event IDs, such as `e408852` with `eid=635988`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all linked banner and tile images. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels. Give the `stars_*.png` images text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
2. Switch the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
3. Fix `c=Triggered` on the batch-sent AppsFlyer link to a campaign value that matches `source=batch`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message, not the relay copy.
5. Capture the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with backoff.
