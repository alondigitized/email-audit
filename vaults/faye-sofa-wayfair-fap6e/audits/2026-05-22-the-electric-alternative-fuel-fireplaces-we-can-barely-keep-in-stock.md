# The ELECTRIC & ALTERNATIVE FUEL FIREPLACES we can barely keep in stock
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- The subject line — "The ELECTRIC & ALTERNATIVE FUEL FIREPLACES we can barely keep in stock" — is the longest, most specific Wayfair has attempted this entire Memorial Day run, and it raises expectations hard. A subscriber curious about fireplaces opens expecting a focused, maybe inventory-urgent category send. What they get is the same multi-department clearance template Wayfair has been rotating all week: a Memorial Day Clearance hero, then "upgrading your bed?", then "spotless solves" (cleaning), then "small space, big style" storage/furniture. Fireplaces may appear in the hero frame but the email abandons the premise within one scroll.
- This is the seventh or eighth iteration of the same kitchen-sink pattern documented in the brand history — the formula is now completely predictable. The "we can barely keep in stock" urgency hook is genuinely interesting copy, but it's sabotaged when the body reveals no actual stock-count, no "X left" callout, and no fireplace-dominant layout to back it up.
- The net judgment: a good subject wasted on the wrong body. The open is earned, the click is lost.

## What's working

- **Subject urgency hook** is the strongest Wayfair has written this entire run — "we can barely keep in stock" implies scarcity without screaming "LIMITED TIME!!!"
- **Memorial Day Clearance banner** at the top of the email is seasonally timely and visually prominent.
- **Email renders cleanly** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds together.
- **Wayfair sender brand** is fully recognizable; no deliverability friction in the header.

## What's weak

- **Subject-body mismatch is total.** The email promises fireplaces; the dominant in-body sections are bedding ("upgrading your bed?"), cleaning ("spotless solves"), and compact furniture ("small space, big style"). Same bait-and-switch as the May 20 CHANDELIERS send.
- **No specific discount percentage visible in the hero** — "Memorial Day Clearance" is a label, not an offer. No "up to 70% off" or dollar callout in the first visible frame.
- **Multiple competing CTAs with no primary.** "Find Duvet Sets," "spotless solves," "small space, big style" all split attention; there's no single fireplace CTA the eye can land on.
- **"We can barely keep in stock" is unverified** — no stock counter, no "only 14 left" badge, no proof anywhere in the email body to support the scarcity claim. It reads as marketing filler on arrival.
- **Subject line is 68 characters** — will truncate hard on mobile, cutting off "...we can barely keep in stock" which is the only interesting part.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Match the body to the subject.** Open with a fireplace-only hero section — at least three fireplace hero tiles with "X in stock" or "ships by Memorial Day" callouts. If the body isn't fireplaces, kill this subject line and revert to the clearance template.
- 2. **Add proof to the scarcity claim.** Even a simple "Selling fast — 200 sold this week" badge under the hero fireplace image would give "can barely keep in stock" legs.
- 3. **Shorten and front-load the subject** — move the product noun and scarcity signal earlier so mobile doesn't truncate.
- 4. **Surface one concrete discount** — "up to X% off" or a dollar floor in the hero band, not just "Clearance."
- 5. **Consolidate to one CTA** in the first scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Electric Fireplaces: nearly sold out`
- **Subject Alt B:** `These fireplaces keep selling out — here's why`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop before Memorial Day inventory runs dry — electric & alternative fuel styles now in stock`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to [X]% off electric fireplaces this Memorial Day weekend`
