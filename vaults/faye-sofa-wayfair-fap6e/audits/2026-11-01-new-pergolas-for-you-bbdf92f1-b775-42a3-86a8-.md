# New Pergolas for YOU
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises pergolas, but the render doesn't deliver them. The Black Friday Pop-Up banner ("up to 70% OFF", "Shop Now") is a real hero. Under it, most of the product grid is empty boxes with tiny broken-image icons. Only two pergola photos load, the gray and the dark-curtained ones. The large block below the grid is also empty, and so is the large block above the footer.
- The category tiles at the bottom are mostly blank too. Only the first tile, "On sale now: pergolas", shows an image. The rest are bare alt-text links in default blue serif, such as "Pergolas for less" and "Top-Rated Kitchen Mats". The tiles also mix pergolas with kitchen mats, nightstands, bedding and curtains, so the "New Pergolas for YOU" promise gets diluted. This is the same Wayfair catch-all with a topic subject on top, and the 11/01 "Area rugs – prices are ⬇️ ⬇️ ⬇️" send had the same blank-hero problem. Judgement: a decent concept that breaks on delivery.

## What's working

- The purple Black Friday Pop-Up banner with "up to 70% OFF" and a "Shop Now" button is a clear, seasonal offer at the top.
- The two pergola photos that load (gray hardtop and dark-curtained) are attractive and match the subject.
- Star ratings under the product tiles add social proof.
- The "On sale now: pergolas" tile is the one category tile that renders an image, so it does show a relevant product.
- The footer perks (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) and the app-download QR block are tidy and clear.

## What's weak

- Four of the six product tiles are empty boxes with broken-image icons, so the subject's promise is mostly unmet.
- There are two huge blank white blocks, one mid-email and one before the footer. They look broken and force a long scroll over nothing.
- Almost all the category tiles are bare alt-text links in default blue serif, which looks unstyled and untrustworthy.
- The category mix drifts into kitchen mats, nightstands, bedding and sinks, which weakens the pergola focus.
- "inspo for your space." and "score styles you ❤️" are vague section headers with no specific product or price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the image rendering. Ship fallback images or a width-safe image host, because blank tiles are the biggest conversion killer here.
- 2. Make the email truly pergola-themed: a single pergola hero with a price ("Pergolas from $X"), then 4-6 pergola and outdoor-living products.
- 3. Cut the off-topic category tiles (kitchen mats, sinks, curtains) or move them lower under a clearly labeled section.
- 4. Replace the vague headers with specific ones such as "Pergolas up to 70% off".
- 5. Give each product tile a price and a reason to click, such as a sale price, free shipping or delivery date.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a mobile media-query block, sent as a batch (`source=graymatter_batch`). The main problems are about 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, missing alt text, and a malformed image URL. Link health and compliance headers could not be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed material links returned **429**, which is rate limiting and not a failure. These include `/daily-sales`, the homepage, the Erommy pergola PDP and the `onelink.me` app link. The remaining probed links show no error.
- **4 PDP links were not probed.** The time budget (70s) ran out, so they are neither pass nor fail:
  - `/-Aluminum-Pergola-with-Adjustable-Louvered-Roof-Waterpro…` (×2)
  - `/12-ft.-W-x-10-ft.-D-HeavyDuty-Outdoor-Metal-Pergola-AllS…` (×2)
- **Campaign label mismatch.** The app link carries `c=Triggered`, while every link carries `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=1`. A batch send is labelled as a triggered one, which will skew attribution.
- **Insecure fallback in the deep link.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP and costs a redirect hop for users without the app.
- **Duplicate links per product.** Image, title and `stars_10.png` all link to the same Erommy PDP. Each has a different `_eml` value, so click-level tracking is intact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **~20 insecure tracking pixels.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `…019` (`sz=2x1`) are flagged "Image uses http://" and may be blocked, so impressions could be undercounted. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text on all of these:**
  - Linked images (hero, header and product images), where it makes the link's accessible name empty or just the filename. The probe reported the link text as `[image: WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale-Skinny]` and `[image: email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg]`.
  - Review-star images `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`, which carry the rating information.
  - Tracking pixels (`imp`, `b.php`). These are decorative, so `alt=""` is enough, and the QA tool can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Malformed image URL.** `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not valid percent-encoding. Strict parsers or proxies may fail to load it.
- **Stale and test asset names.** `0728_OutdoorClearance…` and `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` are in production HTML. The first suggests leftover July template content, and the second is a test-named file.
- **Truncated source.** The CSS in the provided HTML is cut off, so rendering couldn't be fully checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the provided source or subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` header. The tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- An unsubscribe link was always included in probing, and no failure was reported for it.
- The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe text couldn't be checked.
- The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain consistent with the brand.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses its own scheme (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121277`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). That is internally consistent, but it won't map to standard analytics without custom parsing.
- **Mixed destinations.** Product links go to PDPs (`…~ETRS1219.html`, `sku=ETRS1219`), which fits the "Pergolas" subject. The hero banner "WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale", "Shop Sale" and the logo go to `/daily-sales` or `/`, not a pergola category page.
- **Hero asset vs. subject.** The `_1101_` hero is a different promotion from the pergola subject. Whether that matters depends on the content review.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
2. Add `alt` text to linked images, rating images (e.g. "4.5 out of 5 stars") and hero banners, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels.
3. Fix the `%o` in `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` and replace the stale and test-named assets.
4. Set `c=` to match the batch send, or document why it says `Triggered`.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed PDP links and the 8 rate-limited links with throttling.
6. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct send instead of the relay, to confirm compliance and authentication.
