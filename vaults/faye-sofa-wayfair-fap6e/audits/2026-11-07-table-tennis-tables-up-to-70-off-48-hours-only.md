## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "TABLE TENNIS TABLES ⭐ UP TO 70% OFF" (editor@members.wayfair.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed broken links, but it has real accessibility gaps (missing alt text on content images, no `lang` attribute) and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, because the relay may have stripped the headers and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Every probed link that failed returned HTTP 429 (rate limited), including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...` and the product links. This is the probe being throttled, not a dead destination. The QA tool itself says "link may be valid."
- **Incomplete coverage.** 12 links were probed and 67 skipped. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget: L168-K~id-AV22046375, L305-K~id-AV55060895, the 4-PCS wall-paneling product, and `/daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e408298.html`. These are neither pass nor fail.
- **Non-HTTPS hrefs.** The hero banner links to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, and the app deep-link carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. Both depend on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 open or impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019`, `sz=2x1`) use `http://`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, which undercounts impressions. The open pixel (`https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`) uses HTTPS.
- **Recipient identifier in URLs.** Every URL carries `_emr=1e128c59-35f4-4dd7-bd92-8c1bc373e14c`, and the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels carry the same value as `p=`. It is a persistent per-recipient ID, so forwarding the email exposes it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing alt text:**
  - The hero banner `WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale-SkinnyHero-Gene…` is wrapped in a link. Without alt text, screen readers and image-blocked clients expose only the filename, and the QA tool reports the link text as "[image: WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale-Skinny]".
  - `WFUS_1029_FallClearance-Skinny-BAU_Financing.png`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…` also lack alt text.
- **Star ratings:** `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear about 14 times. They sit inside product links, so the links have no accessible name, and the rating is not available to non-visual users.
- **Pixels with no alt:** `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels have none. This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct fix for decorative or tracking images.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="…">` with no `lang` or `xml:lang`.
- **Not an issue:** the CSS has `@media (max-width: 480px)` breakpoints, a `u ~ div` Gmail fix, and `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` on the Sofia `@font-face`.
- **Not verifiable:** the source is truncated, so the viewport meta, preheader and `<body>` structure could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The dynamic values (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) are all resolved. `_eml` is a per-link token, so it differs across URLs, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, the email lacks one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses a dedicated subdomain (`members.wayfair.com`), which is good practice, but alignment cannot be confirmed.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so these could not be checked. No violation is claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=1`, `varid=9`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `wfcs=cs7`, `refid=MKTEML_143028`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16693`. This is consistent across links, but a standard analytics tool will not read it as a campaign without custom mapping.
- **Landing pages match their link text.** "Shop Sale" and the banner go to `/daily-sales`, and product links use `~id-<SKU>` URLs with a matching `mplid` and `sku`. No mismatch was found, though the 429s mean the landing pages were not actually loaded.
- **App link:** the same link, "Get Exclusive Deals on the App", appears twice with different `_eml` values. It uses a OneLink deep link (`af_dp=wayfairapp://home`) with a web fallback, so the deep link depends on the app being installed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero banner and promo images. Add alt text or `aria-label` to the star-rating images, such as "4.5 out of 5 stars". Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change the `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` href to HTTPS.
3. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. The relay may be hiding them, so check against the original message rather than the relayed copy.
5. Re-probe the 4 unprobed and 429'd URLs with backoff to confirm they resolve, and re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML.
6. Consider adding `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the proprietary parameters to make attribution portable.
