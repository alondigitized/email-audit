# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is pure Fourth of July clearance mode — Wayfair came in loud with the red, white, and blue flag backdrop and a fat "UP TO 70% OFF" splashed across my entire screen. As a starter-home buyer who literally lives for Way Day-level deals, the offer grabs me instantly. But the hero feels like it's selling patriotism first and home goods second — I have to mentally translate "July 4th clearance" into "sofa and bar stool money."

## What's working

- **"UP TO 70% OFF" hero copy** — as someone who tolerates flat-pack assembly to save 30%, 70% off makes me stop scrolling Instagram and open a new tab
- **"FOURTH OF JULY CLEARANCE FEAT. SEALY" banner** — Sealy is a real mattress brand and my guest bedroom needs a bed; the brand name makes the clearance feel legit, not vague
- **Search bar pre-populated with "Search for bar stools"** — this is either personalized (I've literally been looking at bar stools) or a weirdly accurate default for starter-home buyers; either way it's useful framing
- **Carousel with 5 slides** — signals there's more beneath the hero so I'll swipe before I scroll
- **Holiday deadline is built-in** — "July 4th" is a real date, so I feel urgency without a manufactured countdown timer

## What's weak

- **Flag/patriotic hero imagery** — a red-and-white striped background is on-brand for the holiday but does nothing for home decor inspiration; I came to picture a sectional in my living room, not a fireworks show
- **No category entry points above the fold** — no furniture, living room, bedroom, or dining nav tiles visible; I have to tap the hamburger or search from scratch
- **Product thumbnails below the fold are mixed** — I can see what looks like a weight bench (gym equipment!) and a ceiling fan; the gym gear immediately reads as "not my department," which dilutes focus
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings on the thumbnails, no "free shipping over $X," no "easy returns" badge — all the things I'd need to actually pull the trigger
- **No loyalty hook** — Wayfair has a rewards credit card and a loyalty program; none of that is surfaced, which means I'm not reminded to use my points

## Recommendations

- **Swap the flag graphic for a lifestyle hero** — show a real living room or dining setup styled in modern-mid-century with a "Fourth of July Clearance" overlay; the deal message stays, but the mood shifts from patriotic billboard to home inspo
- **Add category entry tiles directly below the hero** — "Sofas," "Dining," "Bedroom," "Lighting" — four tappable tiles get me into my category in one tap instead of opening the hamburger
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold strip** — something like "Free shipping on orders over $35" under the nav bar; for a big-ticket purchase it reduces my hesitation at checkout
- **Surface star ratings on the product thumbnails** — even small "4.6 ★ (2,341)" lines under each tile dramatically increase my trust before I tap through
