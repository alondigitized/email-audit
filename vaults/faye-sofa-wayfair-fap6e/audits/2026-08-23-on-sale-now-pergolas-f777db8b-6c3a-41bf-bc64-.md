# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This one delivers on its subject and mostly on nothing else. "On sale now: pergolas" — a pergola/gazebo tile sits right at the top of the "must-shop steals" grid, second row down, on-theme and no scroll required on desktop. That's the good news, and it's the same good news from 2026-08-22's send: the specific tile promised in the subject actually shows up.
- The bad news is everything below and around it. There are at least five broken-image placeholders (small blue-square icons) scattered through "fresh-picked favorites," "cool down," and "Picks you'll love," and the top banner is the now-familiar generic "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" strip that has appeared verbatim across the last several sends (2026-08-22, 2026-08-23). The email is also a kitchen-sink grid — pergolas, ceiling fans, recliners, air conditioners, lanterns, a fountain, wall clocks, patio dining — with no single hierarchy pulling the eye anywhere specific after the hero banner.
- Net: subject-to-content match works, but render bugs and an unfocused, repeated-banner layout drag this down to an average Wayfair send.

## What's working

- The "On sale now: pergolas" tile is the second item in the top grid — subject and hero content line up with no scroll needed.
- "Get It Now, Pay Later" with Affirm/Klarna logos is a clear, distinct value-add block.
- Star ratings are visible on several product tiles ("fresh-picked favorites"), adding social proof.
- Footer loyalty tiles (Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional) are visible and legible.

## What's weak

- At least five product tiles render as blank white boxes with a small blue-square broken-image icon (in "fresh-picked favorites," "cool down," and "Picks you'll love") — a real, visible render bug.
- The top banner "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" is the identical generic strip used in the 2026-08-22 and 2026-08-23 sends — stale creative, no differentiation for this specific email.
- The email sprawls across a dozen unrelated categories (fans, recliners, AC units, lanterns, fountains, clocks, dining sets) with no visual hierarchy after the hero — nothing tells the eye where to go next.
- The "pergolas" tile itself is small and captioned only "Our best gazebo deals »" — it's present but not treated as a hero moment despite being the subject line's whole premise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken-image tiles before send — five dead placeholders in a promotional email is a QA miss that actively erodes trust regardless of copy quality.
- 2. Give the pergola/gazebo tile actual hero treatment (larger image, dedicated headline) since it's the entire subject line's promise — right now it's buried as one of six equal-weight grid tiles.
- 3. Retire the "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" banner or refresh its creative — it's run unchanged across at least two prior sends this week and reads as filler, not campaign-specific.
- 4. Cut the email's scope — pick 2-3 categories max instead of a dozen; a pergola-themed send should stay outdoor/patio-focused (gazebos, fountains, patio dining, outdoor lighting) rather than mixing in recliners and wall clocks.

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "On sale now: pergolas" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email built on a standard MJML/Unlayer-style responsive template with heavy tracking-parameter usage; QA passed 57% of probed links (rate-limited, not failed) but flagged systemic missing `alt` text and several `http://` (non-HTTPS) tracking pixels, plus absent authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 73 total links were probed (61 skipped per prioritization); of those probed, 7 returned **HTTP 429 (rate-limited)** rather than confirmed failures — includes the primary CTA `Shop Sale` (`wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`), the hero image link, and the app-download link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed broken.
- 4 links were unprobed due to a 70s time budget, including a PDP link (`/pdp/gibson-home-oslo-16-piece-dinnerware-set-gbsn2410.ht...`) and a BNPL link (`/buy-now-pay-later~b547.html`) — status unknown.

- 20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) load over **`http://` instead of `https://`** — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently degrade open/impression tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **28 images missing `alt` text**, including all functional/informational images: hero banner (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), header logo (`wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`), BNPL banner, SKU header images, product category images (air conditioners, fountains), and 8 review-star rating images (`stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png`). Screen readers will announce these as blank or fall back to filename, harming accessibility (WCAG 1.1.1) and rendering in blocked-image clients (Outlook default) as empty space with no context.
- The 20 `http://` tracking pixels are also missing `alt` — low-impact since they're 1x1 trackers, but consistent with a template-wide omission of `alt` attributes rather than isolated oversights.
- Media query block is a standard mobile-responsive framework (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidth`, etc.) — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization syntax found in the visible/truncated source. No issues found.
