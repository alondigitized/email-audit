# Your exclusive offer is waiting – don't miss out!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is an app-download email wearing an "exclusive offer" subject line. The hero is a purple block with "Get 10% OFF your first purchase in the app," a visible promo code (0VP294BNYB3GS), and a "Download Now" button. Below it is a phone mockup showing an accent chair at $199, 50% off. The offer is clear and the hierarchy is clean. But it is only for first app purchases, so it is narrow, and the fine print caps it at $5,000 and one use per customer.
- Compared with this week's sends, this one is a reset. The Oct. 24 "WAY DAY STARTS NOW" kickoff and the four pergola teasers sold up to 80% off. This one drops that and pushes the app. The pergola, rug and outdoor tiles at the bottom echo the earlier creative. Nothing here mentions Way Day, even though it is live today. The subject is the weakest part: it is generic, a little spammy ("don't miss out!"), and says nothing about 10% or the app.
- Verdict: a competent transactional-style promo with a weak subject line and a narrow offer. It would probably be opened less often than the Way Day send and clicked at a middling rate.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "Get 10% OFF" in very large white type on purple, with the code shown right below it.
- The "Download Now" button appears twice, as a white pill on purple and again as an outlined pill, so the CTA is clear.
- The phone mockup with a real product, price, strikethrough and review count shows what the app experience is like.
- The "Offer Expires: November 23, 2026" line gives a clear deadline.
- The 2x4 product grid carries category CTAs such as "Best-Selling Pergolas »" and "On Sale Now: Bar Stools »".

## What's weak

- The subject says nothing concrete. It doesn't mention 10%, the app, or a product.
- The offer is app-only and first-purchase-only, and this is not clear until you are inside the email.
- The deadline is a month away, so it creates no urgency, and the email ignores the live Way Day sale.
- The code is shown in a monospace box that looks like a form field and is hard to copy on mobile. It is unclear whether the button applies it automatically.
- The grid captions are small and generic ("Popular Picks »"), and no prices or discounts appear on the products.
- The app-download push sits above the product grid, so someone who wants to shop has to scroll past it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to name the real offer: the 10% off and that it is for the app.
- 2. Add Way Day pricing to the grid tiles, for example "up to 80% off," to bring back urgency.
- 3. Say "app-only, first order" near the top of the hero so there are no surprises.
- 4. Tell people the code applies automatically, or add a "Copy code" button.
- 5. Put the product grid above the second download block, or make the grid tiles link straight to the app.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses Wayfair's first-party tracking and has consistent per-send link parameters. The automated checks confirm one cluster of real issues: 20 non-HTTPS, alt-less tracking pixels, plus a linked promo image with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings are unverified because of the capture path, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Eight probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale") and `https://www.wayfair.com/` ("Wayfair"). A 429 is neither a pass nor a failure, so these are unverified.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 39 links were probed (27 skipped by design). Four more were not checked because the 70s budget ran out:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas-se60631.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/popular-picks-se48775.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-pergolas-se73973.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/all-season-outdoor-coverings-se35939`
- **Mixed content.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients that block non-HTTPS images won't fire them, so impression counts will be under-reported. Some proxies may also warn about insecure content.
- **Likely over-tracking.** `li.wayfair.com/imp` appears to be an ad-impression beacon, and 20 of them fire per open. That is a lot of extra requests for one email. This is an inference from the URL pattern, not something the QA data confirms.
- **Open pixel is fine.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?...eventType=EmailOpen` uses HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked promo image has no alt text.** `wfna_0331_app-install_2024_email_promo-code.jpg` is the content of the link to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app?...`. The link text resolves to `[image: wfna_0331_app-install_2024_email_promo-c...]`, which is the filename. With images blocked or in a screen reader, the link has no meaningful name. This is the only content image flagged.
- **Tracking pixels lack alt.** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels have no `alt`. This is low impact for decorative pixels, but `alt=""` is the correct attribute, and the checker flags its absence.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">` with no `lang`. This affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Responsive CSS looks sound.** It has 480px and 320px media queries, `u + .Gmail` and `body[data-outlook-cycle]` client fixes, and a `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for the Sofia web font. Nothing is flagged.
- **Not checkable.** The HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the body structure (tables, `role="presentation"`, preheader text).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) in the visible source or the QA output. Per-recipient IDs (`csnid`, `_emr`, `_eml`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so I can't confirm a real defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders, so check them on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it appears in the output. I can't verify it or the physical postal address, because the footer was truncated.
- **Sender domain.** `editor@service.wayfair.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with brand-aligned DKIM and SPF. This is unconfirmed without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme, applied consistently across links:
  - `refid=EML_84564`
  - `source=graymatter`
  - `treatmentGuid=1a4ca9d1-f5a2-4671-8d4e-576923ee4059`
  - `wfcs=cs7`
  - `csnid` and `_emr`
- This is not a defect, but a third-party analytics tool won't see campaign, source or medium without remapping.
- **App links** add `c=AppEmail_HVHA`, `af_sub5=AppEmail_HVHA` and `pid=Email`. The OneLink URL carries `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and a web fallback `af_web_dp=https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, so deep-link and fallback handling look correct.
- **`csnid` is a persistent recipient identifier in every URL.** It is also in the open pixel URL. Forwarded emails will carry it, so this is a privacy consideration.
- **Landing-page alignment is unverified.** Every probed link returned 429, so I can't confirm that destinations match link text.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""`. Consider consolidating the 20 beacons.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the app-install promo image, e.g. "Download the Wayfair App", since it is the sole content of its link.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) before treating these as defects.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling, retry and backoff, or a longer time budget, to confirm landing-page status.
6. Capture the full HTML so the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and body structure can be audited.
