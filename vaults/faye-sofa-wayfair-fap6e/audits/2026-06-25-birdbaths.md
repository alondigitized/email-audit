## What's weak

- **The subject delivers zero birdbath payoff.** The hero image is a generic outdoor garden scene; no birdbath is identifiable at scroll-zero. The "BIRDBATHS." subject is functionally misleading.
- **Scatter layout dilutes everything.** Four distinct sections (Open Box Deals → product grid → ceiling fans → picked for you) with no editorial throughline means no section lands with full force.
