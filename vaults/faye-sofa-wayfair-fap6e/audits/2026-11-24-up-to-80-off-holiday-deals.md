# 🚩 UP TO 80% OFF 🚩 HOLIDAY DEALS 🚩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the standard Wayfair Black Friday template with a holiday-deals subject line on top. The subject promises "UP TO 80% OFF" and "HOLIDAY DEALS," and the hero banner backs up the 80% claim. The rest is a long, undifferentiated scroll. The "picked just for you" grid is all pergolas and gazebos, which is an odd pick for late November. A holiday shopper sees one Christmas-tree tile near the top. Compared with the 11/24 "GARDEN STATUES" and "HOT TUBS" sends, this one is more honest about being generic, but it is just as undifferentiated.
- The 80% hero and the category tiles (bedding up to 70%, area rugs up to 60%, Christmas trees up to 45%, mattresses up to 60%) give a clear offer. They also compete with each other. About a dozen tiles carry different percentages, so there's no single anchor. The email also carries a credit card promo ($40 off), a Sealy sub-brand block, and an app download pitch.

## What's working

- The "BLACK FRIDAY up to 80% off" hero sits at the top with a high-contrast purple and white treatment.
- The category tiles use bright red-orange percentage labels, which are easy to scan.
- The "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" banner adds a second concrete benefit.
- The "deals on big-name brands" list (Moen: spend $350, save $75; Sealy; Chris Loves Julia) gives specific savings.
- Christmas trees at up to 45% off is the one tile that matches the holiday framing.

## What's weak

- The "picked just for you" grid, and a second grid lower down, are almost entirely near-identical pergola and gazebo photos. That's repetitive and off-season.
- The subject says "HOLIDAY DEALS," but only one tile (Christmas trees) is holiday-themed.
- The tiles carry no product names, prices or CTAs, so a click leads to a category page.
- Too many competing percentages (70, 60, 45, 55, 65, 50) and several sub-offers dilute the hero.
- The footer legal block is dense and small, and the credit card offer has fine print ("Offer ends 2/1/2027").

- 1. Lead with one holiday hero, such as Christmas trees and decor, with a single price point and a "Shop trees" button. Cut the grid to one category.
- 2. Deduplicate the product grids. Show varied products with prices instead of six pergolas.
- 3. Move the credit card and app promos below the main offer, or drop them from promo sends.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with responsive `@media (max-width:480px)` rules and per-recipient tracking parameters. The main code-level problems are about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, widespread missing `alt` text, and a campaign-parameter mismatch in the app deep link. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** 12 links were probed. 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), which is a probe artifact and not a defect. Examples are `/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/bedding-clearance~e408850.html`, `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`, `/daily-sales/christmas-tree-clearance~e408906.html` and the `onelink.me` app link. Their status is **unverified**.
- **4 links were not probed** because the 70s time budget ran out. They are `/daily-sales/mattresses-%26-more-on-clearance~e408876.htm` and three pergola product URLs (`-Aluminum-Pergola-…`, `Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-…`, `EROMMY-Louvered-Pergola-…`). They are neither passing nor failing.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, `sz=2x1`) use `http://`. Clients and proxies may block or rewrite them, which would undercount impressions. The open pixel (`https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…`) is HTTPS.
- **Insecure fallback URL:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is `http://`. That adds a redirect hop for users who fall back to the web.
- **Campaign parameter mismatch:** the `onelink.me` URL has `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3&varid=7`. A batch send is labelled as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Deep link target:** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` sends app users to the app home screen. The link text is "Get Exclusive Deals on the App".

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing `alt`:** the QA checks flagged about 43 images.
  - 10 are content-bearing banners and buttons: `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, `…Skinny-EverythingShips`, `…Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `…Skinny-BigBrandNames`, `1119_BFCM_PHASE2-Email-1xButton-35` through `-39-WFUS.jpg`, and `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - 12 are `stars_9/10/11.png` rating images, which carry rating information.
  - 21 are tracking pixels (1 `b.php` open pixel and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels). These are acceptable with `alt=""`.
- **Empty link text:** the first banner link (`/daily-sales/?_emr=…`) has no text alternative. With images off or in a screen reader, the link is announced by URL or filename only.
- **Mobile CSS:** `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all }` at ≤480px can split product names mid-word.
- **Not assessable:** the source is truncated, so I can't check `lang`, `role="presentation"`, or dark-mode CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible URLs contain populated per-recipient identifiers (`_emr=20f7cf55-…`, `_eml=<unique per link>`), and no unrendered tokens appear in the data provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is **unknown**. This is also a likely relay artifact.
- The From address is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a first-party subdomain.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme: `refid=MKTEML_143954`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16792`, `csnid`, `batchid`, `varid`, `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`. This is consistent and internal, but it won't map into third-party analytics without custom channel rules.
- **Landing alignment is unverified.** Subject "UP TO 80% OFF HOLIDAY DEALS" maps to `/daily-sales` and the `/daily-sales/*-clearance~e…` events, which matches the clearance framing. The 429 responses prevented checking that the pages load or that the offers are live.
- **Minor inconsistency:** the top banner links to `/daily-sales/` (trailing slash) and the "Shop Sale" CTA links to `/daily-sales`. Both likely resolve to the same page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 banner and button images and to the `stars_*.png` images (for example, "4.5 out of 5 stars"). This also fixes the empty link text on the top banner.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://` and correct `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign value in the AppsFlyer link.
4. Check the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy, before treating them as defects.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited URLs and the 4 unprobed URLs with throttling, or from a different IP, to confirm they resolve.
6. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name` with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, or truncate with an ellipsis.
