# Technical Email Audit — Wayfair "Black Outdoor Privacy Screens"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Wayfair batch marketing email (Unbounce/graymatter template) with heavy tracking-parameter usage across all links; automated QA passed 57% of checks with 3 warning categories and no hard failures, driven mainly by rate-limited link probes and missing image alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/59 links probed, 47 skipped** — probing prioritizes high-click-likelihood CTAs/images; footer and social links deprioritized by design, not a defect.
- **7 links returned HTTP 429 (rate limited)** during probing, including the primary app-download CTA (`Get Exclusive Deals on the App` → `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`), the hero "Shop Sale" and homepage logo links, and category links (air conditioners, pergolas). QA notes these are likely valid (server-side rate limiting, not broken links) but could not be confirmed as 200 OK.
- **4 links unprobed due to time budget** (70s cutoff), including `cat-trees`, `hot-tub-deals`, and `small-kitchen-appliances` category links — status unverified either way.
- All probed links carry consistent Wayfair tracking payloads (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs=cs7`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid=`, `sltid=`, `refid=MKTEML_121679`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15886`, `mdlid=`) — internally consistent across links, no malformed or truncated tracking IDs observed in the evidence provided.
- **20 first-party tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp`) served over plain HTTP**, not HTTPS (see §3) — these are impression-tracking beacons, not click links, but still a mixed-content/blocking risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **~11 content images missing `alt` text**, including the hero (`0728_OutdoorClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), header art, and multiple product-category images (air conditioners, small appliances). Screen readers and blocked-image fallback will render these as blank.
- **20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) missing `alt` text** — low impact since these are 1x1-style beacons, but they inflate the alt-text warning count.
- **20 tracking pixels loaded over `http://` instead of `https://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=...&sz=2x1&m=false&p=3e8f70f2-...`) — many mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) block or proxy non-HTTPS image requests, which can silently break open-tracking accuracy.
- Responsive CSS (media queries for ≤480px, `u-FullWidth`/`u-HalfWidth`/`u-Block` grid classes, Outlook/Gmail-specific hacks) is present and structured normally for an ESP-generated template; no structural rendering defects found in the visible markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens, personalization placeholders (e.g., `{{first_name}}`), or unresolved template variables are present in the visible HTML or QA output. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay.
- QA confirms an in-body unsubscribe link is always included in its probing methodology, but its specific URL/status was not surfaced in the findings above.
- **Recommendation caveat**: these three findings are relay-visibility gaps, not confirmed violations — verify against raw SMTP headers at the source (not the AgentMail-relayed copy) before treating as compliance failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses its own proprietary tracking schema rather than standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` are absent); instead: `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121679`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This is consistent across all sampled links, so internal attribution should resolve correctly even without UTM.
- Landing pages are topically aligned with their link text/creative: "Shop Sale" → `/daily-sales`, air conditioner product image → `/home-improvement/sb0/air-conditioners-c420527.html`, "On sale now: pergolas" → `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now:-pergolas--se82161.html`. No mismatches found between CTA copy and destination path in the evidence available.
- App-install CTA correctly deep-links via OneLink (`af_dp=wayfairapp://home`) with a web fallback (`af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`) for non-app users.
