## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Wayfair promo with a clean, tokenized link structure and a large tracking-pixel footprint. QA flagged missing alt text and non-HTTPS image sources. The compliance headers could not be verified, which is probably a capture limit of the AgentMail relay and not a sender fault.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- No broken links were confirmed. All 8 probed warnings were HTTP 429 rate limits, which means the server throttled the probe and the link is likely valid. This applies to:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-dressers-se44425.html?eid=343602...`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?af_dp=wayfairapp%3A%2F%2Fhome...`
- 4 links were not probed because the 70s time budget ran out. They are neither pass nor fail:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/comforters-%26-sets-for-less-se30625`
  - `/daily-sales/outdoor-furniture-clearance~e390732.html`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/just-for-you%3a-dining-chairs-se5586`
  - one more `/daily-sales?_emr=...` URL
- 58 of 70 links were skipped by design (footer, utility and social links).
- 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=1241260xx&sz=2x1&m=false&p=aa3ede72-...` impression pixels (`s=124126000` through `124126019`) load over plain HTTP.
  - Mail clients may block them or rewrite them through an image proxy.
  - If blocked, ad impressions go unreported.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) is in place. It has no alt text.
- The onelink.me deep link carries `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. The web fallback uses `http://`, so users without the app take an extra redirect hop.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 6 images are missing alt text. All 6 are content images:
  - `wfus_0331_twbdpfy_2024_email_banner-gif.gif`, a linked banner, so the link has no accessible name when images are off. QA also reports it as link text `[image: ...]`.
  - `..._sku-rec-callouts-01.jpg` and `..._sku-rec-callouts-02.jpg`
  - `wfus_0523_twbdpfy_2024_email_shop-all-picks-for-yo...`
  - `wfus_0522_twbdpfy_2024_email_sales-you-almost-miss...`
  - the `[image: ..._sku-rec-cal...]` image that serves as the sole content of a `/daily-sales` link
- 6 `stars_10.png` rating images have no alt text. Screen readers get no rating value.
- 20 `imp` pixels have no alt text. They are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""`. A missing attribute is read out by some screen readers.
- The `@font-face` for "Sofia" (`sofia_reg.woff`) has `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so Outlook falls back correctly. No issue.
- The markup uses an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with media queries at 480px and 320px, plus a `u ~ div` Gmail fix. This is standard. The HTML was truncated, so I can't verify the rest of the structure.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source or QA data. The truncated HTML limits this check.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed defect.
  - If they're really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024) require both.
- `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. QA says this is expected through the relay.
- The footer and unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported. The physical address and unsubscribe link in the body can't be confirmed.
- The From address is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain separate from `www.wayfair.com`. That's normal for ESP segmentation, and I found nothing to flag.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Wayfair uses its own parameters, not UTM: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=2`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_122338`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16484`, `wfcs=cs7`. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appear.
  - Third-party analytics such as GA that expect UTMs may need mapping rules.
  - This is a design choice, not a defect.
- Each link has a unique `_eml=<uuid>` and a shared `_emr=aa3ede72-...`. That gives per-link attribution, but it makes the URLs uncacheable and long.
- The subject says "markdowns on DRESSERS", and the CTA "On Sale Now: Dressers" goes to `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-dressers-se44425.html?eid=343602`. That matches on the surface.
  - The landing page could not be probed (429), so I can't confirm its content.
- The link text "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values. This is probably intentional per-placement tracking.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS to avoid blocking and mixed-content problems.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the banner and SKU callout images, especially the linked ones. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and `imp` beacons.
3. Add alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars" to the `stars_10.png` ratings, in line with the actual rating.
4. Change `af_web_dp` from `http://` to `https://`.
5. Check the raw `.eml` headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay before treating compliance as a problem.
6. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff to clear the 429s and cover the 4 unprobed links.
