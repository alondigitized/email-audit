# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is another Wayfair catch-all. The subject promises pergolas, and the render gives pergolas two small tiles in the first grid. The hero is now "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" with a Sealy "featuring" badge. That is a new frame after the "72-HR CLEAROUT" banner of the last three sends. The 70% figure is bigger than the 60% they were running on 11/11, so the offer escalated.
- The email is a long scroll of about 25 tiles. Pergolas show up twice, as "On sale now: pergolas" and "Pergolas and gazebos from $50 ›". Everything else is beds, curtains, stair treads, mats and stockings. It is a Wayfair template doing what it always does. A pergola shopper gets a fast, concrete price anchor, and nobody else gets a reason to care.
- Judgement: a competent but unfocused send, about a 5. It repeats the "pergola" thread from 11/10 ("10' W x 10' D pergolas") and 11/12 ("Pergolas for less"). The subject is mildly relevant to the persona and under-delivered on in the body.

## What's working

- The "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" banner sits at the top and is readable at a glance.
- "Pergolas and gazebos from $50 ›" gives a concrete price anchor, which is rare in this template.
- The purple section headers ("the must-shop steals", "top picks. low prices.") make the long scroll easy to scan.
- The "Your counter, completed." bar stool block is the one clean, single-product moment, with a clear "Tap here for bar stools" button.
- The Wayfair Rewards and financing icon row near the footer adds light loyalty and trust cues.

## What's weak

- Pergolas get two small tiles in a 25-tile grid, so the subject is only half-delivered.
- The "Early Black Friday" and "70% off" banner has no end date and no clear condition on what the discount covers. "Up to 70%" is vague.
- There is no single primary CTA. The only real buttons are "Apply Now" for financing and the bar stool button.
- Many tiles use the same low-contrast "Just for You / On Sale Now" label, so no product has a price or a reason to click.
- The two pergola tiles use nearly the same photo, which looks like padding.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Use a full-width pergola image with "Pergolas from $50" and a "Shop pergolas" button, and push the other categories below it.
- 2. Put a visible deadline on the Black Friday banner. This week's urgency ("ends tonight", "two days left") has disappeared and nothing replaces it.
- 3. Cut the tile count by about half and add a price to each tile ("Beds from $129").
- 4. Use a different photo for the second pergola tile, or drop it.
- 5. Give the financing block a clear value statement (for example "0% APR for 12 months") or move it lower.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure and link tagging look sound, with a media-query responsive layout and consistent tracking parameters. The QA run found 3 warnings. The largest problems are accessibility and delivery: 20 non-HTTPS `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, and about 30 images with no alt text. The header-based compliance checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited probes (8 of 12), inconclusive.** Wayfair returned 429 on the onelink.me app link (twice), `/`, `/daily-sales` (twice), the pergolas event and the pet-friendly rugs event. These are probe throttling, not confirmed breakage. The 4 other probed links produced no failure.
- **4 links unprobed (70s budget).** None has a verified status:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-and-gazebos-from-%2450-se81…`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/our-best-kitchen-mats-for-less-se205…`
  - `/wayfaircard?…`
  - `/?_emr=…&wfcs=cs7&_eml…`
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…&af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://` for the web fallback, which adds a redirect hop to HTTPS.
- **Conflicting campaign labels on the same link.** The app link has `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labelled as triggered could skew AppsFlyer campaign attribution.
- **Impression pixels over HTTP.** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=e8adbfbf-…` beacons are flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block mixed content or proxy images will drop them, so impression counts will be undercounted. The incrementing `s=` values suggest per-slot ad impression tracking, but this is inferred, not confirmed.
- **Recipient ID in every URL.** `_emr=e8adbfbf-db93-4eaa-a6a1-ca575400007a` is the same across all links and is also the `p=` value in the pixels. Forwarding the email exposes the recipient's tracking identity. This is informational.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (about 30).**
  - Content images:
    - `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-General.jpg`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_3_purple.png`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_1_purple.png`
    - 4× `wfus_jt800_minibahn_singleclass_barstools_style_la…`
    - `WFCC2%402x.png`
    - `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png`
    - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
  - Tracking pixels: `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Linked images with no alt.** The "SkinnyTop-General" and "event_top_3_purple" images are links, and their link text resolves to the filename (`[image: WFUS_1112_BFCM_…]`). Screen readers and image-blocking clients get no usable link label.
- **Placeholder asset.** `blankBanner (1).png` suggests an unused or placeholder module was shipped.
- **Responsive code.** XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, a 480px breakpoint and a Gmail `u ~ div` fix are present. The HTML is truncated, so I can't verify the viewport meta tag, `lang`, or the Outlook conditional comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens appear in the available HTML or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe and no List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Neither header was captured. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules are not met.
- **Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, which is expected through the relay. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address fall outside the truncated source. The probe reportedly always includes the unsubscribe link, but its result isn't listed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121686`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. These work with Wayfair's own analytics but not with GA or third-party tools that expect UTMs.
- **Subject and landing page align.** The "On sale now: pergolas" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.html?eid=603456&unifiedeventid=603456`, so the event IDs match.
- **Event header image links to the homepage.** `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple` links to `https://www.wayfair.com/?…sltid=5…` rather than an event page. It shares `sltid=5` and `mdlid=9614f7ff…` with the pergolas and rugs event links, so clicks on it are attributed to the module but land on the homepage.
- **Same slot ID on different destinations.** `sltid=5` appears on the homepage, pergolas and rugs links, so slot-level click attribution is ambiguous.
- **Skinny-top banner.** It links to `/daily-sales` with `sltid=4` and has no alt text, which is a minor mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked banners and product images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`).
2. Change the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons to `https://` to avoid blocking and undercounted impressions.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, and align `c=Triggered` with the batch send.
4. Check the raw message at the origin MTA for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, to confirm the relay is responsible for the missing headers.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or retry-after backoff to get real status codes.
6. Point the event header image at its event URL, or give the homepage link its own `sltid`.
7. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that downstream analytics ingest `source`, `batchid` and `refid`.
8. Remove or populate the `blankBanner (1).png` placeholder.
