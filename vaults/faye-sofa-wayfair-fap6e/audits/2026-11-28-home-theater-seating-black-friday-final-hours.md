## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Wayfair "HOME THEATER SEATING ❗️ BLACK FRIDAY ❗️ FINAL HOURS ❗️"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with first-party tracking, and I found no broken links or unresolved merge tokens. The real issues are 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, widespread missing alt text (including on image-only links), no `utm_*` parameters, and a `c=Triggered` / `source=batch` mismatch on the app link. The HTML is truncated and the header QA checks were inconclusive, so compliance can't be fully verified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 7 probed-link warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits (e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...`). These are not failures.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 of 84 links were probed, 72 were skipped, and 4 more timed out at the 70s budget (`/Primed-Pantry-Door-Slab-...`, `...L54-K~id-BY39013812.html`, `...L6114-K~id-BK81042267.html`, `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`). Treat these as unverified, not passing.
- **Campaign-type mismatch.** The onelink.me URL carries `c=Triggered`, but every link carries `source=batch&batchid=2`. Attribution in AppsFlyer will classify a batch promo as a triggered message.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The onelink `af_web_dp` is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL and depend on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Tracking parameters are per-link and consistent.** `_emr`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_145418`, `emlid=101` and `maiid=16824` are the same across links. `_eml` differs per link, which is expected.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image-only links have no accessible name.** The QA tool's "link text" for several product links is `[image: stars_10.png]`. The star-rating images have no alt text, and they are the link content for product URLs such as `/wayfair-product-L509-K~id-AV63005405.html?...` and `/BCANHOME-Smart-Bidet-Toilet-...-AD15036979.html?...`. Screen readers will announce these links without a name. The hero link `1113-US-BF-Email-ETn.gif` → `/daily-sales` has the same problem.
- **Alt text is missing on 12 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` images.** It is also missing on 5 content or banner images:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_Email_Skinny-BF-Doorbusters`
  - `...Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`
  - `...Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`

  These banners carry promotional text, so images-off clients will show blank space.
- **Tracking images have no alt.** This covers the open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels (`s=124126000` through `s=124126019`). These should use `alt=""` and ideally `role="presentation"`.
- **Mixed content.** All 20 `imp` pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...&sz=2x1`. Clients that block non-HTTPS images won't load them, so those impressions go unrecorded.
- **Template notes.** The `<html>` tag has `style="width:100% !important;..."`. The Sofia `@font-face` includes `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so Outlook has a fallback. The visible CSS uses 480px and 320px media queries. The HTML is truncated, so I can't assess the body structure, `lang`, or `role="presentation"` on layout tables.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and URLs contain no unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). I couldn't check the truncated portion of the HTML.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the raw message in a seed inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. This is not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unverified.** The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't in the findings. The truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address or the unsubscribe link, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer elements.
- **Sender alignment.** The From domain is `members.wayfair.com` and the tracking domains are `t.wayfair.com` and `li.wayfair.com`. All are same-organization, so there is no obvious domain mismatch.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters (`refid=MKTEML_145418`, `source=batch`, `emlid`, `maiid`). That works with Wayfair's own analytics but won't populate GA4 source/medium automatically.
- **Subject and landing pages don't line up.** The subject says "HOME THEATER SEATING", but the probed links go to `/daily-sales` (generic), `/` and an area-rug clearance page. The visible product links include a bidet toilet (`BCANHOME-Smart-Bidet-Toilet...`). No probed link goes to a home-theater-seating category or product. Link coverage is partial, so some of the 72 skipped links may.
- **Recipient identifier in URLs.** `_emr=87e1a2da-cf8d-4212-b1db-fe93294b75b6` appears in every link and in the `imp` pixel `p=` parameter. If it identifies the recipient, it is exposed in referrers and forwarded links.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS (20 instances) and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels, including `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add alt text to the star-rating images, for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`. Give every image-only link a meaningful alt or `aria-label`, starting with the product-card and `1113-US-BF-Email-ETn.gif` links.
3. Add alt text to the banner images so the promotional message survives images-off rendering.
4. Fix the onelink `c=Triggered` value to match the batch send, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
5. Check the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) in a direct seed inbox rather than through the AgentMail relay. Then confirm the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the existing parameters if GA4 attribution matters.
7. Re-probe the 4 timed-out links and the 429s with throttling, so link coverage isn't capped at 12 of 84.
