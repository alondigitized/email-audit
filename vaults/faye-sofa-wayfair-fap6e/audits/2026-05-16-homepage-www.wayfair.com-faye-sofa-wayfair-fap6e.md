# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair is in full Memorial Day Clearance mode — red, white, and loud about it. The homepage is essentially one big sale announcement: "up to 70% off" is the only message above the fold. As someone who literally refreshes the app waiting for Way Day-level deals, this is not a bad place to land, but it's selling the discount before it sells me anything I actually want.

## What's working

- **The "up to 70% OFF" number in massive red type** — I came here for a deal, and this confirms something is happening. Way Day energy, even if the branding says Memorial Day.
- **The persistent banner strip** at the top ("UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA →") gives me a secondary entry point and hints there's a featured brand involved, which feels curated.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** is purple and impossible to miss — no ambiguity about what the CTA is asking me to do.
- **Product tiles starting below the fold** — I can see the beginning of a "Memorial Day Deal" rail with furniture (cushioned pieces, a white desk), which is exactly the category I care about.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I'm looking for, so "Find anything home…" placeholder is exactly the shortcut I want.

## What's weak

- **Zero furniture category navigation above the fold** — if I'm not here for the sale specifically, there's no fast path to Sofas, Living Room, or Mid-Century.
- **No returning-shopper hook** — I have a wishlist, a cart probably sitting half-full from last time, and zero acknowledgment of that. A "Welcome back" or rewards chip would've cost nothing.
- **"FEAT. DELTA" is mysterious** — Delta who? Delta what? I'm being teased with a brand I don't recognize and no context. Feels like a missed editorial moment.
- **The gingham/plaid hero background** is patriotic but feels retail-generic — it doesn't say "modern-mid-century furniture" at all. It could be a barbecue supply store.
- **No free shipping signal** — for big furniture, that's the number I need to see to justify the purchase. It's nowhere in this viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Add a category strip below the hero** ("Sofas • Dining • Bedroom • Lighting") — one horizontal scroll rail gets me from sale mode to browsing mode in a single tap, reducing friction immediately.
- **Swap the gingham pattern for a lifestyle shot of a real room** — a living room with a mid-century sofa tagged "up to 70% off" converts way harder than a patriotic graphic for furniture buyers.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the persistent banner** — change it to "UP TO 70% OFF + FREE SHIPPING OVER $35 | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE →" so the deal feels more complete.
- **Add a "Your Wishlist Has Deals" returning-shopper nudge** — even a small badge on the cart icon or a one-line personalization chip would make me feel like Wayfair knows I was already in the funnel.
