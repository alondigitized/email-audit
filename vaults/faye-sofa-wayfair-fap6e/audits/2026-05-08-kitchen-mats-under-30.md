## What's weak

- **Hero-to-subject mismatch.** "Five must-shop steals" is a generic headline that wastes the specificity of the subject line. A recipient primed for kitchen mats under $30 gets a bait-and-switch feel immediately.
- **Price anchor disappears.** The "$30" limit is the strongest signal in the subject but is nowhere visibly reinforced in the hero section.
- **Category dilution.** The product grid shows rugs, accent furniture, lamps, and other home goods alongside mats. The kitchen mat promise is buried or missing entirely.
- **App-push feels bolted on.** "FIND AMAZING DEALS IN THE APP" is a separate agenda that competes with the already-weak primary offer — an unnecessary friction point for web/email readers.
