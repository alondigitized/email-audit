# CEILING FANS — our top picks.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "CEILING FANS — our top picks," and the render delivers almost none of that. The hero is a huge blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon, the same defect as the 10/7 "FLOORING, HARDWARE & MORE" and 10/6 "PATIO BENCHES." sends. It takes up the whole first screen. The one ceiling fan in the email is a small tile in the "inspo for your space" grid, with no label and no offer.
- The rest is a generic Wayfair catch-all. It has a furniture grid, a financing banner, and a "score styles you" block of pergolas, hot tubs, sinks, grills, coffee makers and trash cans. A thin "60% OFF" Delta strip sits near the bottom. Nothing in the body is about ceiling fans, so the subject gets a click and the body doesn't follow through. Wayfair has now sent several broken-hero emails in a row, and that pattern hurts trust more than any single send does.
- Judgement: a weak send. The subject is specific, but the body is off-topic and the hero is broken.

## What's working

- The subject is concrete and category-specific ("CEILING FANS"), and it stays under 50 characters.
- Below the hero, the product tiles load cleanly with star ratings, so there is some social proof.
- The financing module ("up to 60 month financing") has a clear purple "Apply Now" button.
- The footer has Rewards, Financing, Professional and Exclusive Brands, which gives members some value cues.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank white box with a broken-image icon, and it fills the first screen with no offer or CTA.
- The body doesn't match the subject. Only one small fan image appears, unlabeled and unlinked to any visible fan collection.
- The email has no single clear offer. The "60% OFF" Delta strip is buried near the bottom, and the top has no price or percentage.
- It is too long and scattershot: pergolas, hot tubs, sinks, grills, coffee and trash cans compete for attention, and no visible CTA is tied to the subject.
- The cadence feels repetitive. It is the third or fourth send in a week with broken or generic template content, alongside the 10/5 "5 Days of Deals" blast and the 10/7 patio heater send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero so it renders, and make it a ceiling fan image with the offer, such as "Ceiling fans up to 60% off," plus a "Shop ceiling fans" button. Add alt text and a background color as fallbacks so a failed image doesn't leave a blank box.
- 2. Build the body around fans: a 6-tile grid of top-rated fans with prices, star ratings and sub-category labels (outdoor, with lights, low-profile).
- 3. Cut the unrelated modules (hot tubs, sinks, grills, trash cans) or move them well below the fold.
- 4. Move the 5 Days of Deals / 60% off strip to the top and tie it to the subject.
- 5. Put the financing module after the product grid, not in the middle.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quiet, energy-saving picks with lights. Free shipping over $35.`

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "CEILING FANS — our top picks."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source was truncated, so findings come from the QA output and the visible URLs. The main problems are about 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels with no alt text, many linked images with no accessible name, a stale or test-named asset in production, and a ceiling-fan header image that links to the homepage. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** Eight probed links returned 429 (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157…`, `/daily-sales`, `/`, and the `L230-K~W009861949` product page). That is rate limiting from the probe, not a dead destination. Four product URLs were not probed because of the 70s time budget (`L168-K~W111651355` ×2, `Yamazaki-Home-Plain-Freestanding-Coat-Hanger-Rack…` ×2). Only 12 of 75 links were probed.
- **Non-HTTPS impression pixels.** 20 `<img>` tags use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (`sz=2x1`). All are flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block mixed content or proxy images may drop them, which undercounts impressions.
- **Missing alt on the open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` has no alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS app fallback.** The OneLink URL's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`, so desktop fallback goes through a redirect.
- **Inconsistent campaign labelling.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is labelled as triggered in the attribution campaign field, which will skew AppsFlyer reporting.
- **Stale or test asset names.** The image `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty…` carries a Labor Day (0824) name in an October send. Its filename also contains an unencoded apostrophe (`De4'als`) in the `src` path. `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` is a test-named asset in production.
- **Per-recipient `_eml` tokens.** These are unique per link, so link-level dedup and caching are not possible. This is expected for tracking and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt:** 10 content images in the QA list: `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-…`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `WFCC1%402x.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `5daysofdeals_fall2026_skinnyhero-vp.png`, and `image%20%283%29.png`. Image-blocking clients show nothing, and screen readers read nothing or a filename.
- **Star-rating images:** `stars_10.png` ×4 and `stars_11.png` ×2 have no alt, so the rating value is lost to screen readers and to clients with images off.
- **Linked images with no accessible name.** The probe recorded link text as `[image: stars_10.png]`, `[image: email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg]`, and the Labor Day filename. The `stars_10.png` link points to the same product URL as the "Kemp 52" W LED Standard Ceiling Fan" text link, so it adds a redundant, unnamed tab stop.
- No viewport or media-query problems are visible in the truncated source. The `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and the `u ~ div` Gmail fix look intact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible URLs contain no unresolved tokens (no `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]`). `_emr=f7d96494-…` is a populated recipient ID and is consistent across links. This is limited to the visible portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`** were not found. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. These are unverified, not confirmed failures. The raw message headers are needed to settle them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution uses proprietary params (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121356`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16430`, `mdlid`). - **Header image links to the homepage.** `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` (`sltid=5`) links to `https://www.wayfair.com/?…`, not to a ceiling-fan category. The product links in the same module (`mdlid=9e3fbd3b…`) go to PDPs with `sku=IVYX7542`, so only the header breaks the match with the subject.
- **Generic destinations elsewhere.** The "Wayfair" logo link goes to `/`. The "Shop Sale" link and the Labor Day-named banner go to `/daily-sales`.
- **Parameter hygiene.** Product links carry `mplid=IVYX7542` and `sku=IVYX7542`, which are redundant but harmless.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`).
2. Add descriptive alt text to the 10 content images. Give the star images alt text like "4.5 out of 5 stars", or put the rating in live text.
3. Remove duplicate linked star images, or give them `alt=""` with the link name carried by the adjacent text link.
4. Retarget the `sltid=5` header link to a ceiling-fan category URL instead of `/`.
5. Rename the test and stale assets (`email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-…`). Remove the apostrophe from the filename or percent-encode it.
6. Change the OneLink `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign value, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
7. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Re-run link probing with throttling or retries to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed product links.
