# STRING LIGHTS 🚩 📆 LESS THAN 24 HOURS 🚩 WAY DAY 📆 🚩 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is another competent Way Day closer, and it has the same flaw as the earlier ones. The subject leads with STRING LIGHTS, and the render has one string-lights tile, the top-left product. The rest is a generic mix of showers, patio screens, rugs, mattresses, fireplaces, bar fridges and pergolas. It is the same bait-and-switch as the Oct. 25 "DINING CHAIRS", "SECTIONALS" and "POPCORN MACHINES" sends.
- The execution is clean. A purple "Way Day ends tonight" banner leads, then a "picked just for you" product grid with was/now prices and star ratings, then category tiles with big "up to 80% off" numbers. A yellow "doorbuster deals" band, an Affirm 0% APR block and a free-shipping strip follow. Nothing is broken, which is a step up from the Oct. 25 pergola send with the blank hero.
- This is the eighth or so Way Day send in a week, so it lands as wallpaper. Yesterday's "WAY DAY ENDING TOMORROW" email already carried the same urgency. The "less than 24 hours" line is credible but not new. Judgement: solid layout, tired and mismatched subject, and it works only as a reminder.

## What's working

- The banner says "Way Day ends tonight" with "up to 80% off", so the deadline and the headline offer are visible immediately.
- Product tiles show was/now prices and star ratings, which gives concrete price proof.
- The string-lights tile is the first product, so the subject's promise is at least partly kept above the fold.
- The category tiles ("wall art up to 80% off", "area rugs up to 80% off") are scannable.
- Every image loads, with no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- The subject promises string lights, but the body is a generic everything-sale with one string-lights tile.
- Four emoji and ALL CAPS read as spam, and the subject runs to about 51 characters.
- The email is very long, with at least four stacked sections of offers. Free shipping, Affirm and the app promo all compete with the main CTA.
- The only button, "shop all sales", is generic and sits far down the page.
- The fine print is dense and small. The free-shipping asterisk and the Affirm 0% APR terms weaken the "honest offer" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Either put a full string-lights and outdoor-lighting module at the top, or drop the category from the subject. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the body to one hero, one category row and one button. Remove the app promo and the Affirm block from this send.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "shop string lights" or "shop deals ending tonight", and repeat it near the top.
- 4. Reduce the emoji and the ALL CAPS.
- 5. Vary the cadence. Don't send a near-identical urgency email the day after "ending tomorrow".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with consistent per-link tracking parameters. The main code-level defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 36 images) and about 20 `http://` tracking pixels. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because of relay capture limits.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.**
  - 12 links were probed and 69 skipped.
  - 8 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...` and the 3 product links. Rate limiting is not a defect, but these links are unverified.
  - 4 links were not probed because of the 70s time budget (e.g. `.../Metal-72-x-47-Privacy-Metal-Screen-Fence-Panel-L7552-K~D`, `.../wall-art-%26-wallpaper-clearance~e403402.htm`).
  - Neither probed nor unprobed links should be counted as pass or fail.
- **Mixed protocols in click URLs.**
  - The hero GIF link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, while the "Shop Sale" link uses `https://`.
  - The app deep-link fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
  - Both `http://` URLs depend on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Onelink parameters conflict.** `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&c=Triggered&...&source=batch&batchid=1` labels a batch send with the campaign name "Triggered". This looks like a mislabel that would skew AppsFlyer attribution. It is inferred from the parameters, not confirmed.
- **Duplicate CTA.** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values (`efd1f95d-...` and `163af80d-...`), which is consistent with per-placement tracking.
- **Tracking pixels.**
  - `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (the open pixel) has no `alt`.
  - 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=86693ccc-...` impression pixels use plaintext HTTP.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.**
  - Links use proprietary parameters instead: `refid=MKTEML_142305`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16612`, `wfcs=cs7`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `varid=13`, `batchid=1`, `source=batch`.
