# 📩 FAUX PLANTS AND TREES available just for you 📩
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a broken send. The subject promises "FAUX PLANTS AND TREES available just for you," but the rendered email opens with a large blank white block where the hero should be — no headline, no plant imagery, no offer copy, nothing. The first visible content is a generic "fresh-picked favorites" grid that happens to include one faux tree tile, but it's surrounded by a dresser, a mattress, a bathroom mirror, and a poolside lounger — nothing that reads as a coherent "plants and trees" campaign.
- This is the same pattern flagged repeatedly in Wayfair's recent history (2026-08-17 ottomans email, 2026-08-18 dining chairs email): subject line promises a specific category, email delivers an unrelated generic grid. This send is worse than those because the hero itself appears to have failed to render entirely, not just mismatched category emphasis.
- Judgement: this email fails on delivery before it even gets to targeting. Broken hero + off-category grid = don't ship as-is.

## What's working

- Rewards banner ("get 5% rewards... on every item, always") is clean and renders correctly with a clear CTA link.
- Star ratings are present on every product tile, giving light social proof.
- Footer trust badges (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) render cleanly with icons.
- One faux tree/plant tile does appear in the top grid, so the category isn't 100% absent — just badly buried.

## What's weak

- The hero section is a large empty white block — no image, no headline, no offer, no CTA. This is a visible render failure at the most important real estate in the email.
- Subject says "faux plants and trees" but the first content module is "fresh-picked favorites" with a dresser, mattress, bathroom mirror, and outdoor lounger — barely any plants.
- The "let's complete your space" and "shop before they're gone" sections continue the pattern: pergolas, hot tubs, bar stools, privacy screens — none of it plants/trees.
- No specific offer or price point visible anywhere in the render (no "%," no "$," no BOGO) despite subject implying availability/exclusivity.
- Visual hierarchy has nothing to land on — with the hero blank, the eye has no obvious entry point into the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken hero render first.** This is a blocking bug — an empty white block at the top of the email is going to read as broken/spam to any recipient and should stop this send from going out as-is.
- 2. **Put actual faux plant/tree product tiles in the first grid module**, matching the subject line — right now the category is a single tile buried among furniture.
- 3. **Add a visible offer** (specific % off or price anchor like "faux trees from $25") — currently there's no concrete hook anywhere in the body.
- 4. **Reorder modules so plant/tree content leads**, then furniture cross-sell after — right now the structure is generic-Wayfair-grid first, category second.
