# Pergolas for less
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a Wayfair catch-all whose subject, "Pergolas for less," is only half-delivered. The render opens with the red "72-HR CLEAROUT, ENDS TONIGHT, 60% OFF" banner, which is a different promise from the subject. Pergolas get one tile in the first grid, labelled "Pergolas for less »", and a second, "Pergolas and gazebos from $50 »". They don't return until the middle of the long scroll, where four more outdoor structure photos appear.
- The email has a strong offer and a credible deadline. It then buries both under a financing block, bedroom furniture, recliners, bedding, slipcovers and stair treads. This is the same pattern as the 11/11 "UP TO 60% OFF… LAST CHANCE" send and the 11/10 "10' W x 10' D pergolas" send. Wayfair is sending near-identical clearout templates three days running and rotating the subject.
- Judgement: a competent template with a mismatched subject and no single clear CTA. The "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency is the best thing in it.

## What's working

- The "ENDS TONIGHT" banner with the "60% OFF" badge is the first thing the eye lands on. It is a specific offer with a credible deadline.
- The purple "the must-shop steals" header and the 2x2 grid give a clean first-screen hierarchy.
- Star ratings under the "fresh-picked favorites" products add social proof.
- Product photography is bright and consistent, and the dark-frame pergola images are strong.
- The footer has Rewards, Financing, Professional and Exclusive Brands icons, plus an app promo.

## What's weak

- The subject promises pergolas, but the "Pergolas for less" tile is one of four in the first grid and sits next to rugs and kitchen mats.
- The "ends tonight" urgency isn't in the subject, so it can't help the open.
- The financing block ("up to 60 month financing") competes with the clearout offer and adds fine-print weight. The legal disclaimers at the bottom are dense and tiny.
- The email is very long, with 20+ product tiles. The second pergola run is far down, past recliners and bedding, which dilutes the pergola promise.
- No tile shows a price or discount, so "less" is never quantified. The only price in the first grid is "from $50".

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: a full-width pergola image with "Pergolas from $50 · up to 60% off · ends tonight" and one button, "Shop pergolas."
- 2. Cut the send to one theme (outdoor structures), with a short product grid showing real prices. Move the recliners, bedding and slipcovers to another send.
- 3. Put the financing block below the product grid, or drop it from time-bound clearout sends.
- 4. Add prices or "% off" badges to every tile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pergolas from $50, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: up to 60% off pergolas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `60% off clearout closes at midnight. Gazebos, patio sets and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shade for less: top-rated pergolas and gazebos, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main findings are 37 images with no `alt` attribute, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and unconfirmed unsubscribe and authentication headers. HTML visibility was limited because the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 59 skipped by design. 4 more were left unprobed when the 70s budget ran out:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-and-gazebos-from-%2450-se81…`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/our-best-kitchen-mats-for-less-se205…`
  - `/wayfaircard?_emr=…`
  - `/?_emr=…`
- **429 responses:** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429, which is rate limiting rather than a confirmed break. They are unverified, not failed. The affected links are:
  - The "Pergolas for less" link, which matches the subject line.
  - "Shop Sale".
  - "Wayfair".
  - Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links.
  - The two header image links.
  - "Must-Have Pet-Friendly Rugs".
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Each is flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block mixed content will drop these impressions, which undercounts ad measurement.
- **Insecure app fallback:** The AppsFlyer link `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…` sets `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Its web fallback uses `http://`, which forces a redirect hop.
- **Campaign label:** The same link carries `c=Triggered` while the other parameters say `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labelled "Triggered" is likely to pollute attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 37 images have no `alt` attribute:
  - 20 `imp` pixels.
  - 1 open pixel, `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…`.
  - 7 `stars_9.png` or `stars_10.png` review-rating images, so the rating is not conveyed to screen readers or when images are blocked.
  - 3 `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_recliners_*` product images.
  - Banner and header images: `72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, `WFCC1%402x.png`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `blankBanner%20%281%29.png` and `image%20%284%29.png`.
- **Image-only links:** The `72HR…` banner and the header image are wrapped in `<a>` tags, and the QA text for those links shows only `[image: filename]`. Those links have no accessible name, and the sale deadline in the banner is only in the image.
- **Tracking pixels:** The pixels should use `alt=""` and explicit `width`/`height`. The QA data doesn't show whether they do.
- **Asset filenames:** `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `blankBanner (1).png` and `image (4).png` look like test or placeholder assets in production. This is an asset hygiene issue only.
- **CSS:** The only CSS visible is the mobile media-query block. `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` and the `u + .Gmail` selectors are standard Gmail workarounds. No rendering defects were found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the QA data or the visible source. Per-recipient identifiers (`_emr=27e37e41-…`, `_eml=<per-link uuid>`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Neither header was found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping it, so the result is inconclusive rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely relay capture, and the sender (`editor@members.wayfair.com`) can't be validated from this data.
- **Footer:** The QA text says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but its URL and result are not shown. The physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** No `utm_*` parameters appear on any link. Attribution uses proprietary parameters instead (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121690`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). That works only if Wayfair's own analytics ingests them. Third-party analytics such as GA won't see a campaign or medium.
- **Subject-to-landing match:** The "Pergolas for less" link points to `/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=603460&unifiedeventid=603460`. The `eid` and `unifiedeventid` values match, which is consistent. The 429 means the landing page's actual content and status code were not confirmed.
- **Duplicate `_eml` token:** The "Pergolas for less" and "Must-Have Pet-Friendly Rugs" links share `_eml=dbcb71af-0c0c-48df-9578-d4981f6fcdbd` and `mdlid=9614f7ff-…`. This is probably module-level tracking rather than a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all 37 flagged images, with `alt=""` for pixels and a descriptive value for the rating stars, banners and products. Give the image-only anchors accessible names.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or retries, prioritizing the "Pergolas for less" and "Shop Sale" links.
4. Check the raw MIME headers from the original sender. The aim is to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
5. Check the unsubscribe URL in the footer and confirm the postal address is present.
6. Reconcile `c=Triggered` with the batch source label, and consider adding `utm_*` parameters if third-party analytics is expected.
7. Remove test or placeholder asset names (`*_test-09.jpg`, `blankBanner (1).png`, `image (4).png`) from production sends.
