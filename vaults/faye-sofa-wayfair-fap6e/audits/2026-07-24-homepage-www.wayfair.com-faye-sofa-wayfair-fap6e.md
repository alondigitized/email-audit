# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I immediately know what's happening here — Wayfair is going full Black Friday in July and they are NOT being subtle about it. The 80% off in giant red type is the entire pitch, and honestly? For me, right now, in my starter home that still has a folding table for a nightstand? This is the page I've been refreshing all week. It reads like a big-event retailer that knows its core customer shops by deal calendar, not impulse.

## What's working

- **The 80% numeral in oversized red type** is pure deal-brain dopamine. I knew within one second this was a sale event I should care about.
- **"EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE\*"** in the hero is huge for furniture — free shipping on big items is usually the question I have before I even look at price, so having it in the hero is smart.
- **The banner strip "BLACK FRIDAY IN JULY FEAT. BEDSURE →"** ties the event to a specific brand/category and gives me a one-tap entry point, which I appreciate.
- **The product images starting to appear below the fold** — that black fluted cabinet with gold hardware is giving very much the mid-century-meets-modern vibe I'm shopping for. Visual hook before I even scroll.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty or rewards hook anywhere visible** — I have a Wayfair account, I've spent money here, and the homepage treats me like a first-time visitor. That feels like a missed retention moment on a high-intent event day.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is too generic** — it doesn't tell me what I'm shopping. "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Black Friday in July" would create a cleaner event narrative without adding visual weight.
- **No countdown or end date** — "Black Friday in July" implies urgency but there's zero timer or "ends Sunday" copy visible. For a Way Day repeat shopper who lives by the deal window, not knowing when this ends actually reduces my urgency to act now.
- **No category shortcuts above the fold on mobile** — if I want to go straight to sofas or dining tables, I have to either tap the hamburger or search. On a sale event day, quick category tiles ("Bedroom," "Living Room," "Dining") would cut the path to conversion.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer to the hero** ("Ends Sunday at midnight") — even a simple one makes the "UP TO 80%" feel perishable, and for an event-driven shopper like me, that's the difference between "I'll come back later" and "I'm tapping now."
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Black Friday in July"** — ties the CTA to the named event, creates a stronger event-page feel without changing the layout.
- **Add a 3-tile category row directly below the hero** (Sofas, Bedroom, Dining) — one-tap paths to the categories where the big-ticket furniture lives. On mobile, those taps matter.
- **Surface a rewards balance or loyalty hook** in the top-right or directly below the hero — a line like "Your rewards: $X available" would make returning customers feel seen and increase cart attachment.
