# Your roundup of pergolas
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a roundup of pergolas, but the render mostly fails to deliver one. The hero banner at the top is a blank white box with one broken-image icon. The purple "picked just for you" block has five of its six tiles empty, so only one pergola photo (a dark louvered pergola over a patio dining set) and its star rating show. A second blank block about 400px tall sits between that module and "shop before they're gone." A third sits at the bottom.
- The lower grid is a mix of unrelated categories: rugs, bedding, chicken coops, cookware, makeup vanities, curtains, closet doors and bath mats. Only two or three tiles load: a pergola ("On sale now: pergolas »") and a cookware set. The rest fall back to blue serif alt-text links in a default font. The footer (Wayfair Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands, app promo) renders cleanly, which makes the broken body stand out more.
- Judgement: a good relevance idea, a browse-abandonment angle like the 2026-10-11 "Your pergola search is over!" send, with execution that looks broken. Whether the images are blocked or genuinely failed, the recipient sees no offer, no clear CTA and no hero. This is also another near-duplicate "picked for you" template in a week of them, after "BOOKCASES" and the 72-hour clearout sends.

## What's working

- The subject is concrete and ties to a product category the recipient has shown interest in.
- The one loaded pergola image in the purple block is a strong, aspirational lifestyle shot with a star rating.
- Star ratings under the tiles add some social proof.
- The "On sale now: pergolas »" tile gives a clear category link in the lower grid.
- The footer is tidy and on-brand, with Rewards, Financing, Professional and Exclusive Brands icons, plus an app-download block with a QR code.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is a blank box, so there is no offer, headline or CTA above the fold.
- Five of the six tiles in the "picked just for you" block are empty purple squares, so the personalized module delivers almost nothing.
- Large empty white gaps in the middle and at the bottom make the email feel broken and very long.
- The lower grid dilutes the pergola promise with rugs, chicken coops, makeup vanities, curtains and bath mats.
- Unloaded tiles show default blue serif alt-text links like "King & queen bedding best sellers" and "Quilt Sets You'll Love," which clash with the brand and read as spam-like.
- No price or discount is visible on any pergola. The only price is "Curtains from $9.99," which is the wrong category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix image delivery and fallback. Set bulletproof alt text with brand-styled fonts and background colors, and make the hero a live-text headline such as "Pergolas, from $X" with a real button, so the email works with images off.
- 2. Make the whole email about pergolas. Use a pergola hero with a specific price or sale, then a grid of 4-6 pergolas with prices and ratings. Drop rugs, chicken coops and makeup vanities.
- 3. Remove or collapse empty modules. If a dynamic tile has no product, suppress it rather than rendering a blank square.
- 4. Add one clear CTA button, such as "Shop pergolas under $X," and a credible deadline if one exists.
- 5. Add a pergola buying aid, such as size guide, "ships by" date or "free shipping over $35," near the top.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Louvered, hardtop and aluminum styles from $X, with free shipping on most.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Compare sizes and reviews before your patio season plans start.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair's "Your roundup of pergolas" email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a mobile media-query block. The main technical issues are 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing alt text, and one image URL with malformed percent-encoding. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are not confirmed defects. The pass rate was 57%, with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 "Rate limited (429)" results are probe artifacts, and the QA output says "link may be valid". Affected links include `/daily-sales`, the `/` homepage, `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, the bath-mat filter page, the `Outdoor-Louvered-Aluminum-Pergola-L7053-K~JLYD1476.html` PDP, and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 links were probed and 63 were skipped. Four pergola PDP links were not checked because the 70s time budget ran out. Their status is unknown, not passing.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not a valid percent-escape. Strict URL parsers or CDNs may reject it or fail to load the image.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link.** The OneLink URL's `af_web_dp` parameter is `http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Per-link recipient tokens.** Every link carries a unique `_eml=<uuid>` and `mdlid=<uuid>` alongside the shared `_emr=5865f2b4-…`. This is normal for click attribution, but it is why the URLs can't be cached or deduplicated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **HTTP pixels.** There are 20 `<img>` tracking pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients may block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, which would undercount impressions. Gmail's image proxy can also flag the mixed content.
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - Hero and banner images: `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_2_purple.gif` and `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`.
  - Credit-card images: `WFCC2%402x.png` and `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png`.
  - Rating images: six `stars_9/10/11.png` files from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`. Ratings are conveyed only visually, so screen readers and images-off clients get no rating.
- **Linked hero images.** The 72-hour hero and the SKU header are wrapped in `<a>` tags, so the link text is the image placeholder (`[image: 72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png]`). Without alt text, the link has no accessible name.
- **Tracking pixels.** `b.php` (the open pixel, `t.wayfair.com`) and the `imp` pixels lack alt text. Alt text is low-priority on them, but `alt=""` is the correct pattern.
- **Mobile CSS.** The `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` and `u + .Gmail` hacks are present, which is expected. I can't assess the rest because the HTML was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` were visible in the provided source. The source was truncated, so the body copy past the head was not fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not confirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing them, so it can't tell whether Wayfair sends them. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body checks not verifiable.** The provided source was truncated before the footer, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link could not be checked. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it is shown.
- **Sender.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain consistent with the link and image domains. Nothing suggests a mismatch.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses a proprietary scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121268`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16434`. This is consistent across links, but standard analytics (GA `utm_source/medium/campaign`) would not attribute these sessions without custom mapping.
- **Parameters are consistent.** The shared `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid` and `refid` are the same on every link, and `sltid` varies per slot.
- **Destinations don't match the subject.** The subject promises pergolas, but several probed links go elsewhere: the homepage (twice, `sltid=2` and `sltid=6`), `/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, the bath-mat filter and the app download. Only the `JLYD1476` PDP and three other pergola PDPs are on-topic. The PDP link includes `sku=JLYD1476` and `mplid`, so product-level continuity is intact there.
- **Creative asset names.** The assets are named `72HR_Spring2026` and `0728_OutdoorClearance`, which suggests reused creative. I can't confirm from the HTML whether it still matches the live promotion.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`: the `%oo` sequence should be `%6F` or a plain `o`, or the file renamed.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS and add `alt=""` and `width`/`height` to them and to `b.php`.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the hero and banner images and the credit-card images. Give the star-rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars". Give the hero images alt text that describes their link destination.
4. Fix the `af_web_dp` OneLink fallback to `https://`.
5. Re-probe the 4 unchecked pergola PDP links with a rate-limit-aware retry or a slower crawl to confirm they resolve.
6. In the QA pipeline, fetch the raw `.eml` or the original headers from the relay, if available. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Also pass the full HTML so footer compliance can be checked.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters or document the Wayfair-specific parameter mapping so analytics can attribute site sessions to this email.
