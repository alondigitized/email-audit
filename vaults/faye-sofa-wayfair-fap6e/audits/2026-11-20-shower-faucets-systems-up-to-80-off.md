## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send (`source=batch&batchid=3&varid=7`) with consistent per-link tracking, but its markup has widespread missing `alt` attributes and about 20 impression pixels loaded over plain `http://`. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 probed links that failed returned 429 (rate limiting), which means the server throttled the probe. Examples: `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...`, and the product URLs for `AS83018705`, `BF15011912` and `AV22046375`. These are inconclusive, not failures.
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 68 skipped. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget, including `.../wayfair-product-L167-K~id-AD55024870.html` and `.../daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985`. Those 4 are neither pass nor fail.
- **Non-HTTPS link.** The skinny banner link (alt text `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-Gener…`) points to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`. Every other CTA uses `https://`, so this one depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Non-HTTPS app fallback.** The OneLink URL `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback is also `http://`.
- **Tracking pixels over HTTP.** 20 impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false`. They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, which would undercount impressions. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS.
- **Inconsistent campaign parameter.** The app link has `c=Triggered`, but every link in the email has `source=batch`. A batch send tagged as a triggered campaign can mis-attribute app installs in AppsFlyer reporting.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** The QA run flagged the following:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`, `..._Email_Skinny-EverythingShip...`, `..._Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg` and `..._Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`
  - 14 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` rating images at `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`
- **Linked images fall back to filenames.** Several of these images sit inside `<a>` tags, so the link text becomes the filename. The banner and `stars_10.png` links are shown as `[image: ...]`. Screen readers and images-off clients get no meaningful link name.
- **Rating images carry no value.** The star images have no alt such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", so the rating is lost to assistive tech.
- **Tracking pixels lack empty alt.** `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels have no `alt`. An explicit `alt=""` is the usual practice for decorative or tracking images.
- **Custom font.** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads a `.woff` with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so there is a fallback. No issue.
- **Limited source.** The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the rest of the markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The `_eml`, `_emr`, `csnid`, `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid` values are populated UUIDs. The `_eml` value differs per link, which suggests per-link tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address not seen.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for one. The footer is outside the truncated HTML, so I cannot confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use Wayfair's own scheme instead: `refid=MKTEML_143900`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16778`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7` and `csnid=e15faabd-...`. This works only if Wayfair's analytics reads these parameters. Third-party analytics, such as GA channel grouping, may see these visits as direct or unattributed.
- **Product parameters are consistent.** `mplid` and `sku` match for each product URL (`EEBS1007`, `KDWX1428`, `FBYA1823`).
- **Subject and landing alignment** is not assessable. Landing pages were not captured, only 429s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and spacers. Give the star images the actual rating value.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Change the banner link `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` and the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
4. Fix the OneLink `c=Triggered` value to match the batch send, or confirm that it is intentional.
5. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside `refid` if third-party analytics need them.
6. Re-check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` using raw headers from a direct inbox, not the relay.
7. Re-probe the 429 and 4 unprobed links with throttling or a longer time budget, and capture the footer to verify the unsubscribe link and postal address.
