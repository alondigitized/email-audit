# FLOOR & WALL TILE ⏱️ UP TO 70% OFF ENDS TONIGHT ⏱️ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a mostly well-rendered Wayfair catch-all with a subject that doesn't match its contents. The subject leads with "FLOOR & WALL TILE", but I can't find tile anywhere in the render. The closest are a wall-panel product at the top left and a "wall art & wallpaper" tile. The Black Friday Pop-Up banner ("up to 70% OFF · ENDS TONIGHT") is the real hero.
- Unlike the 11/01 sends ("New Pergolas for YOU", "Area rugs – prices are ⬇️"), the images load here. The product grid shows prices, strike-throughs and star ratings, and the category tiles are clean. The email is easy to scan, but it sells "everything" rather than the thing in the subject.
- "Ends tonight" is also weak. Wayfair used "Ends Tonight" on 10/31 ("Last Call for Fall Clearance"), and the pop-up launched 11/01, so a subscriber has seen this urgency before. Verdict: a competent template send, mislabeled and low on credibility.

## What's working

- The top banner puts "70% OFF" and "ENDS TONIGHT" in large type above everything else.
- The product images all render, with was/now pricing and star ratings. That is a clear improvement on the blank boxes in the 10/31 and 11/01 sends.
- The category tiles ("area rugs", "bedding", "mirrors & decor", "mattresses & more") pair a big percentage with a clear arrow CTA.
- The purple "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" and "shop all sales" blocks are a strong, unambiguous closing CTA.
- The Affirm financing strip adds a concrete value prop for big-ticket buyers.

## What's weak

- No tile appears anywhere, so the subject's lead category is unfulfilled. A tile shopper bounces.
- "Ends tonight" is stale urgency, and the asterisked "Everything ships free*" adds fine print.
- The "Shop Now" button in the banner is tiny and low-contrast against the purple.
- Many tiles have no product detail beyond price and stars: no titles or fit/size info.
- The bottom block (pergolas, kitchen mats, area rugs) repeats categories already shown above. This feels like filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero match. Either put actual tile in the first product slots and a "Floor & wall tile" category tile at the top, or drop tile from the subject.
- 2. Enlarge the banner CTA and name the category, for example "Shop tile deals".
- 3. Make the deadline specific and one-time ("Ends 11:59 PM ET tonight"), and don't reuse it across the week.
- 4. Replace the repeated bottom tiles with items from the category you're promoting.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (QA reports 0 issues, 3 warnings). The real defects are missing `alt` text on about 35 images and 20 impression pixels loaded over plain `http://`. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited probes (not confirmed defects):** 7 of the 12 probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=…`, the three product links, and two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. 429 means the probe was throttled, so these are neither pass nor fail.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were not checked because the 70s time budget ran out, for example `/wayfair-product-L74-K~W011294897.html` and `/daily-sales/area-rug-sale~e408298.html?eid=635711`. 68 links were skipped by design, so overall link health is unverified.
- **Insecure impression pixels:** 20 tracking images load over `http://`, from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, so ad impressions will be undercounted. Each also lacks `alt`.
- **Insecure link:** the banner link target is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…` (not HTTPS), so it depends on a server redirect. The app deep-link fallback is also `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS, but it has no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images without alt text:** the QA reports these.
  - 12 review-star images (`stars_10.png` ×9, `stars_11.png` ×3) at `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`.
  - 3 banners: `WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale_SkinnyHero-Ends…`, `…Skinny-Affirm.jpg` and `…Skinny-FreeShip…`.
  - 2 further images: `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`.
  - 1 open pixel (`b.php`) and 20 `imp` pixels.
- **Unnamed links:** several `<a>` elements wrap only an image with no `alt`. The probe fell back to the filename as link text (`[image: WFUS_1101_48HBlackFridayPopupSale_Skinny]` and `[image: stars_11.png]`). The banner link and the product links with star images therefore have no accessible name for screen readers, and nothing shows when images are blocked.
- **Tracking pixels:** these should carry `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative.
- **Other markup:** the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, `@font-face` with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, and the 480px media queries look correct. The HTML source is truncated, so I can't assess the rest.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the provided HTML or QA data. Per-recipient IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `emlid`) are resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed rather than a proven absence.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** one-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the provided source is truncated, so I can't verify either. The QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it is listed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** none appear on any URL. Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters (`source=batch`, `refid=MKTEML_142890`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `wfcs=cs7`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16672`). This only matters if GA/UTM-based reporting is expected.
- **Generic landing pages:** the probed CTAs ("Shop Sale", the banner) go to `/daily-sales`, not a tile-specific URL. The subject is "FLOOR & WALL TILE", and the only tile product URL probed is `Bond-Tile-Auburn-Ribbon-24-in.-x-48-in…`. The sink and freestanding-cooler product links aren't tile. Landing-page relevance can't be fully verified because the source is truncated.
- **Deep-link parameters:** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` sends app opens to the home screen, not the sale. The app link also carries `c=Triggered` on what is a batch send (`source=batch`), which could mislabel the campaign type in AppsFlyer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `/daily-sales` banner link over HTTPS.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the banners and star-rating images (e.g. "4.5 out of 5 stars"). Add `alt=""` to the 21 tracking pixels. Make sure every image-only link has an accessible name.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original message, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC from raw headers rather than the relay copy.
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or backoff to clear the 7 429s and the 4 unprobed links.
5. Review `c=Triggered` in the onelink URL, and add `utm_*` parameters if cross-tool attribution is needed.
