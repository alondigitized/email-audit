# 🔴 CLEARANCE ⚪ MEMORIAL DAY 🔵
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's most stripped-down Memorial Day subject line of the entire run — "🔴 CLEARANCE ⚪ MEMORIAL DAY 🔵" drops even the category noun that previous sends at least attempted. Compare to May 20's "CHANDELIERS ✨ Up to 70% OFF for MEMORIAL DAY ✨": wrong product inside, but at least there was a hook. Here, the patriotic emoji is doing all the creative work, and it's not enough.
- Inside, the email is structurally the most organized of the run: discount tile rows, a genuine "picked just for you" personalization grid, a big-name-brands credibility section, and a dedicated "MEMORIAL DAY EXCLUSIVE doorbuster deals" tier. The doorbuster section is the strongest card in the deck — exclusive, urgency-coded, visually distinct. It's buried at the bottom.
- The net read: competent chassis, weak subject, wrong sequencing. The most compelling offer is the last thing you see.

## What's working

- **"Picked just for you" grid** — product-level personalization is visible and real, the strongest relevance signal in the email
- **Tiered discount structure** — the layout distinguishes sitewide (40–70% off tiles), brand deals, and exclusive doorbusters; it has a logic to it
- **Doorbuster section with "MEMORIAL DAY EXCLUSIVE" label** — the word "exclusive" and the dedicated section create a sense of scarcity that most other Wayfair sends this week skipped
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout collapse, no overlapping text throughout a long scroll

## What's weak

- **Subject is the weakest in the Memorial Day run** — no category, no %, just two nouns and three flag emojis; gives zero inbox differentiation from yesterday's email
- **Best offer (doorbusters) is buried last** — the exclusive section should be the hero, not the fourth content block
- **No single visual anchor** — the opening tile grid has four competing discount offers; the eye has nowhere to land
- **Cadence fatigue is a real ceiling here** — this is at minimum the eighth Memorial Day send in four days; open rates for this email are structurally capped regardless of execution
- **"Shop all sales" CTA is as generic as the subject** — the one button visible at scroll-end earns nothing

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the doorbuster section as the hero** — the "MEMORIAL DAY EXCLUSIVE" framing is the only differentiated claim in this email; it should be above the fold, not at pixel 900
- 2. **Put a % in the subject** — the email has 70% off deals; using that number costs nothing and signals value instantly
- 3. **Collapse the four-tile opener into one hero offer** — pick the single best clearance category and own it, same lesson from the May 19 LOW-PILE RUGS send
- 4. **Write a specific CTA** — "Shop doorbuster deals" outperforms "shop all sales" in every scenario

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `word-break: break-all` on product names | `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all }` — splits words at any character boundary, not word boundaries. Use `overflow-wrap: break-word` instead. |
| Fixed-height clipping on dynamic content | `.ProductRec-name { height: 48px }`, `.ProductRec-stars { height: 16px }`, `.ProductRec-price { height: 40px }`, `.ProductRec-salesCopy { height: 28px }` — long product names or multi-line prices will be silently clipped with no ellipsis. |
| `.EventBlock-name { height: 48px }` on half-width blocks | Same clipping risk for event/category labels. |
| Web font blocked in most clients | `@font-face` Sofia font renders only in Apple Mail and a few others; `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback is correctly set, but no CSS `font-family` stack fallback is visible for non-Outlook non-Apple clients. |
| Duplicate `@media` blocks | The `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)` rule is split into ~25 separate blocks instead of being consolidated. Increases parse time and file size; no functional breakage but is a maintenance risk. |
