# Technical Audit: Wayfair "KITCHEN MATS under $30" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is a standard MSO/Litmus-pattern responsive template (Outlook conditional classes, `u-*` mobile breakpoint classes) with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA passed 57% of checks with 3 warning categories and no hard failures, driven mainly by missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 72 total links were probed (footer/utility/social links deprioritized by design); of those, **8 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited)** rather than a confirmed pass — includes the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Kitchen Mats Under $30," and the header logo link. QA notes these are likely valid (server-side rate limiting of the prober, not necessarily broken), but they are unconfirmed.
- **4 links were never probed** due to time budget: `wall-art-clearance-se37038`, `top-rated-area-rugs-se73989`, `pool-tables-under-%24800-se76741`, `small-kitchen-appliances-c419242`.
- One tracking-pixel image beacon uses **plain HTTP**: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=503e60c3-...` (and 19 sequential variants, `s=124126000`–`124126019`). Non-HTTPS resources are blocked or stripped by many mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), which will silently break open/impression tracking for this batch.
- All material links carry a consistent tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_121692`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15853`, `mdlid`) — internally consistent, no malformed or truncated query strings observed in probed links.

Screen reader users get no fallback text for any product imagery or star ratings in this send.
- One `<img>` has both **empty `src` and missing `alt`** — a broken/placeholder image node.
- The 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons are also missing `alt` (low impact — 1×1 tracking pixels — but flagged for completeness alongside their HTTP issue above).
- Template itself uses standard Outlook/Gmail conditional-CSS hacks (`u ~ div`, `body[data-outlook-cycle]`, `u + .Gmail`) correctly scoped; no malformed media query or selector syntax observed in the supplied CSS block.

No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens observed in the visible HTML or QA output. Note: HTML source was truncated, so body content beyond the header/style block was not fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header: not found.** QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/passing the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm true absence from header data alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header: not found** — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header: not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable via this relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA methodology ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in probe scope), but it was not one of the 12 probed links, so its destination status (200 vs. error) is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses proprietary tracking params instead of standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` (e.g., `refid=MKTEML_121692`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15853`, `source=graymatter_batch`) — internally consistent across all links, not a defect, just non-standard UTM taxonomy (relevant if cross-referencing against GA/GA4-style attribution).

2. Add `alt` text to all content images (product shots, star-rating graphics) for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
3. Remove or populate the `<img>` node with empty `src`.
4. Re-probe the 4 time-budget-skipped links and the 8 rate-limited (429) links directly to confirm they resolve with 200s.
5. Confirm with the sending ESP/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are genuinely absent at the SMTP layer or only dropped by the AgentMail capture — this determines CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058 compliance risk vs. a monitoring gap.
