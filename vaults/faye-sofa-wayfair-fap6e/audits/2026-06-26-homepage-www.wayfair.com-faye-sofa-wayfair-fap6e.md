# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair in pure flash-sale mode — everything is purple urgency, "ENDS TONIGHT," and a 70% off badge demanding my attention. As someone who literally has the Wayfair app on rotation during Way Day weekends, I feel this on one level: there's a real sale happening right now. But the hero is almost entirely typographic — there's no sofa, no mid-century credenza, no "here's the room you want" moment that would tell me where to actually tap.

## What's working

- **"ENDS TONIGHT" double-hit** — The sticky banner at the very top AND the hero card both say the sale ends tonight. That redundancy works. It's the kind of deadline that makes me close Instagram and open Wayfair.
- **The 70% off circular badge** — Sits in the upper-left corner of the hero, impossible to miss. Specific enough to feel real rather than a generic "BIG SALE" tease.
- **The SHOP NOW button** — Purple, centered, no ambiguity. Even half-asleep at 8am I know what to tap.
- **Clean mobile render** — No jank, no layout pop, nothing broken. On a phone screen that alone keeps me from bouncing.
- **Outdoor furniture thumbnail below fold** — That circular woven table and chair scene is warm and inviting. Not explicitly mid-century, but it fits my vibe enough to make me want to scroll.

## What's weak

- **Hero is all type, no product** — The background just tiles "STYLE" in lavender letterforms over and over. That's a missed opportunity. Show me the sofa. Show me the walnut sideboard. The abstract background gives me nothing to want.
- **No category quick-links visible** — There's a hamburger menu, but no surface-level tiles for Living Room / Bedroom / Dining. Way Day is overwhelming; I need a one-tap path to my section, not a nav hunt.
- **The crib** — Below the fold, one of only two visible product photos is a white nursery crib. Maybe that's me someday, but right now I'm buying a starter home and hunting for a sofa. That slot feels wasted.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I always check whether I clear the free-shipping threshold before I start adding things. It's nowhere on this screen.
- **No trust signals** — No "free returns," no star ratings, no review counts on the product cards. For a purchase that might run $500+, I need that reassurance before I commit.
- **No returning-shopper recognition** — I've bought from Wayfair multiple times. There's no rewards balance chip, no "welcome back," nothing that makes me feel like anything other than a cold visitor.

## Recommendations

- **Make the hero product-forward:** Swap the abstract "STYLE" letterform background for a lifestyle shot — a real living room with a modern-mid-century sofa, warm wood tones, a sale badge overlaid. Give me something to want, then tell me it's 70% off.
- **Add flash-category tiles below the hero:** Four tiles in a 2×2 grid — Living Room, Bedroom, Outdoor, Dining — so I can land in my section in one tap instead of tunneling through the hamburger nav.
- **Surface free-shipping in the sticky banner:** Something like "UP TO 70% OFF + Free shipping over $35 | ENDS TONIGHT" — that single addition would push me toward filling a cart rather than browsing passively.
- **Add a returning-shopper hook near search:** A small "Welcome back — $X in rewards" chip would make this feel personal. I've given Wayfair years of purchase history and browsing patterns; use them.
