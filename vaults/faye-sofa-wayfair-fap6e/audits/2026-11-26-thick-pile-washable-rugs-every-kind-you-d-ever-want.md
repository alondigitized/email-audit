# THICK-PILE WASHABLE RUGS » EVERY kind you’d ever want.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Wayfair Black Friday template with a rug subject line on top, and it follows the 11/26 ICE MAKERS and 11/25 CHANDELIERS pattern. The subject promises "THICK-PILE WASHABLE RUGS," and the render delivers it in the first tile. A black shag rug sits at the top under "the must-shop steals," captioned "Cozy, Thick-Pile Washable Rugs ›." A second rug tile, "Our Best 8' x 10' Area Rugs," follows further down. That is better follow-through than most recent sends, but nothing in the render supports "EVERY kind you'd ever want."
- The "80% OFF BLACK FRIDAY" banner is the only offer, and it is a ceiling, not a rug-specific price. Below it is a long, undifferentiated scroll of pergolas, garden statues, bedding, table lamps, slipcovers and bread machines. The rug tiles carry no price, no sale badge and no reviews. ## What's working

- The rug hero is the first and largest image, so subject and body match immediately.
- The "80% OFF BLACK FRIDAY" banner at the top gives one clear, seasonal offer.
- There are two rug tiles (washable thick-pile and 8' x 10' area rugs), so rugs get more than token coverage.
- The imagery is clean and consistent, and I see no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer carries Rewards, Financing and the app QR code, which is a useful loyalty and app push.

## What's weak

- No tile shows a price, percent off or sale badge, so "80% OFF" is never tied to a rug.
- The tile captions ("Cozy, Thick-Pile Washable Rugs ›") are soft text links, and there is no button anywhere. The primary CTA is only the small "Shop Sale" in the header.
- Below the rug tiles the email drifts into pergolas twice, garden statues, bedding and kitchen items. Pergolas already led the 11/25 "On sale now: pergolas" send.
- Nothing shows the washable claim: no machine-wash icon, no sizes, no reviews.
- "we think you'll ❤ these" is generic and only weakly personalized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email rug-led. Use a rug hero with a real price ("Washable rugs from $39") and a visible button, then a grid of four to six rug tiles with sizes and star ratings. Drop the pergolas and statues.
- 2. Put the sale price and a "Sale" badge on every tile, so the 80% claim is tied to products.
- 3. Add a washable proof point: a "Machine washable" badge on tiles, and a reviews or ratings line.
- 4. Turn the tile captions into bold verb-plus-noun CTAs such as "Shop washable rugs."
- 5. Don't repeat creative across sends. Rotate the secondary tiles instead of reusing pergolas.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 480px responsive breakpoint. The automated QA found 0 hard failures. The warnings are mostly missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and header data the relay didn't capture. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the QA output and the visible `<head>` show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** All 8 flagged probes returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting by Wayfair), which is not a defect. Affected URLs include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale") and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link.
- **Coverage gap:** 12 links were probed and 49 skipped. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget (e.g. `/daily-sales/filters/our-favorite-garden-statues-se73878.`, `.../king-size-bedding-you%27ll-love-se33`). Their status is unknown. The truncated URLs in the QA output are a display artifact, not evidence of malformed hrefs.
- **Insecure fallback:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP. It depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Inconsistent campaign label:** the same app link has `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is labeled as triggered, which likely skews AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Tracking pixels:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?...` is the open pixel over HTTPS. 20 impression pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019&sz=2x1` are plain HTTP. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Linked images without alt text.** These are the real accessibility issues, because the link has no accessible name:
  - `1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif` links to `/daily-sales`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` links to `/`.
  - The QA output shows both as `[image: …]` link text.
- **Other images without alt text:**
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_2_purple.png`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
- **Tracking pixels without alt text:** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels. This is low impact if they are 1×1 or hidden, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **HTTP image sources:** all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels use `http://`. Some clients block mixed content, which would under-count impressions. Gmail's image proxy usually rewrites these, so the impact varies by client.
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`.
- The head has Gmail (`u ~ div`, `u + .Gmail`) and Outlook-app (`body[data-outlook-cycle]`) fixes. No other rendering defects are evident, but the body was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible. The `_emr` (recipient) and `_eml` (per-link) parameters are populated with UUIDs on every probed URL.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain, so check alignment there.
- **Body-level items could not be verified.** The unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was reported for it. The physical address could not be verified because of the truncation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses proprietary parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121290`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. These are consistent across all links. The gap matters only if analytics outside Wayfair's own stack needs UTMs.
- **Landing pages match the content:** the "Cozy, Thick-Pile Washable Rugs" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/cozy%2c-thick-pile-washable-rugs-se78341.html?eid=554386&unifiedeventid=554386`. The `eid` and `unifiedeventid` values agree.
- The `c=Triggered` mismatch is covered in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the linked images `1113-US-BF-Email-General.gif` and `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`. Add `alt=""` to the pixels and the decorative `blankBanner` and `sku_top_2` images.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Correct the `c=Triggered` label on the batch app link.
4. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` element.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` in the raw message source, not through the relay copy.
6. Re-probe the 4 unprobed links with a longer time budget or a backoff for 429s. Also capture the full HTML to check the footer address and unsubscribe markup.
