# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- OK, Wayfair clearly knows Memorial Day is their moment — the whole homepage is a red-white-and-blue clearance push with a massive "UP TO 70% OFF" hero front and center. As someone who literally refreshes for Way Day deals, this hits the right nerve immediately. That said, it's a blast-to-everyone clearance event, not a curated "here's what a millennial starter-home buyer actually needs right now" experience — the difference matters.

## What's working

- **The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero copy** — that number is big enough to stop my scroll. Way Day energy, right on time for Memorial Day weekend.
- **The "SHOP NOW" button** — it's visible, unambiguous, and in a contrasting dark pill. I know exactly where to tap.
- **The promo strip at top** — "MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA" is a second reinforcement before I even hit the hero. Doubles down on urgency.
- **Product thumbnails starting to appear** — the chairs visible in the early scroll look like exactly the warm-wood, cream-upholstery mid-century pieces I browse for hours. That's a good hook.
- **Carousel dots (5 slides)** — tells me there's more to explore; I'm already mentally swiping.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping callout above the fold** — for big furniture purchases, that threshold matters enormously to me. I need to know if a $400 chair ships free before I commit headspace.
- **"UP TO 70% OFF" with no floor** — "up to" could mean one clearance mug is 70% off and everything else is 10%. No anchor price or example item erodes trust.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards hook** — I have a Wayfair account; showing me my rewards balance or a member-exclusive price would make me feel seen, not just mass-marketed.
- **No style language for me** — nothing says "modern mid-century," "starter home," or anything millennial-coded. It's patriotic bunting and a big number, which works for anyone aged 18–80.
- **No countdown or "ends Sunday" urgency** — Memorial Day is a known deadline but the page doesn't say it. An explicit date would sharpen the urgency considerably.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Free shipping on orders $X+" badge directly in the hero** — even a small line under the CTA button. That one piece of info dramatically changes my calculation on big-ticket furniture.
- **Swap one carousel slide for a style-led shot**: a styled mid-century living room or dining nook with a "Shop the Look" overlay — this is what I actually browse, and it would break up the sale-banner monotony.
- **Surface a "Membership Perks" or "Rewards Balance" chip** in the header or promo strip for logged-in users — even a "You have 500 points" nudge creates stickiness before I bounce to a competitor.
- **Add explicit deadline copy** to the promo strip or hero sub-copy: "Ends Monday, May 26" would flip this from a vague clearance to a genuine Way Day-style countdown that keeps me glued.
