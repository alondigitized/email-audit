# CHINA CABINETS ⭐️ LABOR DAY CLEARANCE ⭐️ SHOP NOW ⭐️ 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Wayfair send this week chasing a hyper-specific subject line, and it's the same bait-and-switch that's dragged down four of the last five audits. Subject says "CHINA CABINETS ⭐️ LABOR DAY CLEARANCE ⭐️ SHOP NOW ⭐️" but the hero banner is the now-familiar generic "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" strip with no product photography at all — same banner that's shown up in the 08-28 and 08-26 sends. The one saving grace: the very first tile in the "picked just for you" grid is a tall glass-front lit display cabinet that could plausibly read as a "china cabinet" to a shopper, so this isn't a total whiff like the ceiling-fans or furniture-cushions sends — but it's buried below the fold as tile #1 of a generic 12-item grid, not called out or labeled.
- Below the fold this is a competent, well-organized Wayfair template — clean percentage-off tiles (30/70/55/50/50/50/40% across categories), an Afterpay financing callout, a doorbuster section, and a "Your Home, These Picks" personalization block. No render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text. It's just not a china-cabinet email, and a subscriber who opened specifically for china cabinets will bounce immediately.
- Judgment: mid-pack. Clean execution drags it up from the worst sends this week, but the subject-to-hero mismatch is the exact recurring flaw that's now shown up in the majority of this brand's recent sends, and it should be an easy fix.

## What's working

- No visual bugs — layout renders cleanly end to end, images load, pricing/star-rating overlays are legible.
- The lead grid item (lit glass display cabinet, "Was $299.99 Now $179.99") is a genuine plausible china-cabinet match, even if unlabeled.
- Percentage-off tiles are scannable and organized by category (decor, wall art, mirrors, bedding, curtains, mattresses, vanities, outdoor).
- Afterpay 0% APR callout adds a real financing incentive beyond the discount.
- "Your Home, These Picks" section attempts personalization with named categories (Kitchen Mats, Wall Art Clearance, Coastal rugs).

## What's weak

- Subject promises "CHINA CABINETS" as the headline product; the hero banner is a generic clearance strip with zero product imagery — same gap flagged in the 08-28 and 08-26 sends.
- No dedicated china-cabinet section, category link, or even a "Dining & China Cabinets" tile anywhere in the visible layout.
- The one relevant product (lit display cabinet) is unlabeled and indistinguishable from the surrounding random "picked for you" grid — a shopper scanning for cabinets could easily miss it.
- Star ratings appear on some tiles but not others (top two items in first row have none), inconsistent trust signaling.
- This is the third clearance-themed subject in five days using nearly identical "up to X% OFF" banner language — cadence is repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic clearance banner with an actual photo of a china cabinet and a "Dining Room Clearance" or "China Cabinets: Up to X% OFF" headline tile — match hero to subject, the single highest-leverage fix given this is now a five-email pattern.
- 2. Move the lit display cabinet (or a better china-cabinet SKU) into the very first hero position with an explicit label, not buried unlabeled in a generic grid.
- 3. Add a dedicated "Shop China Cabinets" CTA button near the top instead of forcing scroll through unrelated categories (decor, mattresses, outdoor fireplaces) before any dining furniture appears.
- 4. Standardize star ratings across all product tiles for consistent trust signaling.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glass-front cabinets, buffets & more — Labor Day markdowns end soon`
