# RECLINERS you'll love!!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's worst render of the recent batch. The email opens on nearly a full screen of blank white space — no logo lockup that reads as intentional, no hero banner, just a stray broken-image icon floating in empty space before anything real appears. That's a dead cold-open even worse than the gazebo/bidet disasters referenced in this brand's history.
- Once content starts, it's a mixed bag: the "must-shop steals" grid actually loads (recliners, pergolas, black outdoor privacy screens, shed deals), which is good news for the "RECLINERS you'll love!!" subject line since a recliner image is genuinely one of the first things a reader sees. But past that point the email collapses into a long scroll of broken-image placeholders — flatware, LED lanterns, espresso machines, toilets, mattress toppers, area rugs, tub doors, coffee makers, mini fridges, kitchen islands — dozens of blue broken-icon squares with only category text links and stray star ratings surviving.
- Judgement: the subject-to-hero connection works, but this send is unshippable as rendered. More than half the email is broken imagery, which tanks trust and buries any real offer.

## What's working

- The "Top-Rated Recliners" tile in the must-shop grid actually loads and pays off the subject line directly.
- Pergola, privacy screen, and shed-deal images in that same top module render cleanly with visible star ratings.
- The ceiling fan and dresser images under "fresh-picked favorites" load fine, keeping mid-email from being a total loss.
- Purple/white Wayfair brand color scheme stays consistent everywhere the layout does render.
- Footer keeps recognizable trust elements (Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, app QR code) intact.

## What's weak

- The email cold-opens on a large blank white block with a single stray broken-image icon — no hero, no offer, no branding for a full screen's worth of scroll.
- From roughly the "fresh-picked favorites" wreath tile onward, the majority of product images are broken (blue placeholder icons) — flatware, LED lanterns, espresso machines, toilets, mattress toppers, rugs, tub doors, coffee makers, mini fridges, kitchen islands all fail to load.
- No concrete offer (no %/$ discount) anywhere visible — just vague "Big Help. Small Price Tags." copy, which is weak compared to this brand's usual "UP TO 70% OFF" banners.
- Star ratings appear orphaned under broken image tiles, which reads as glitchy rather than intentional.
- Subject line uses ALL-CAPS + double exclamation ("RECLINERS you'll love!!"), a spam-adjacent pattern this brand should avoid given deliverability risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken-image pipeline before this ever sends again** — this is the single biggest lift available; a send that's 50%+ broken icons will tank clicks regardless of copy.
- 2. **Kill the blank white cold-open** — move the recliner hero tile (which does load) to the very top so the subject line pays off in the first screen, not several hundred pixels down.
- 3. **Replace vague copy with a real number** — "Big Help. Small Price Tags." should become an actual %/$ off callout tied to recliners specifically, matching the subject.
- 4. **Tone down the subject line** — drop the double exclamation and all-caps product name; it reads as spammy next to competitive furniture-brand subjects.
- 5. **Give the recliner CTA its own button**, not a generic "Shop Now" that routes to an undefined destination.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new favorite chair is on sale`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Uxbridge/Wayfair "Graymatter" batch template with standard tracking/personalization tokens; QA shows no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured, widespread missing alt text, and several HTTP (non-HTTPS) tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed material links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16287`, `mdlid`) — structurally consistent, no malformed params observed.
- 12 of 71 total links were probed (59 skipped by design/deprioritization); of those probed, 7 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by target server, not confirmed broken) and 4 were left unprobed due to time budget. No confirmed-broken (4xx/5xx non-rate-limit) links in the probed set.
- Tracking pixel `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` (20 instances, `s=124126000` through `s=124126019`) is served over plain HTTP, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently break open-tracking for this batch.
- Open-tracking beacon `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` present and uses HTTPS — no issue.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 20 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text: the `b.php` tracking pixel, hero images (`0915_epicfallsale_skinnyhero-general.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`), `small_appliances_01_gif.gif`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `wfus_jt800_batch10_livingroom_chaiselounges_general...`, `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, 8× `stars_10.png` review-star icons, and all 20 `imp` tracking pixels. Missing alt on content/hero images degrades screen-reader experience and image-blocked fallback rendering.
- Note: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` filename contains a literal `%` character (unencoded) — verify this doesn't cause an encoding issue in the actual `src` attribute (URL-decoded `%o` is not a valid escape sequence).
- Media queries use standard Uxbridge/BEE-style responsive classes (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidth`, etc.) targeting ≤480px — no structural issues found in the truncated markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the truncated source or QA output.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance status from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same relay caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — same relay caveat; sending domain's actual auth posture is unverified from this capture.

- **Recommendation-level flag**: these three header absences are most likely an artifact of the capture pipeline, not proof of non-compliance — should be verified against raw SMTP headers before treating as a real finding.

- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, etc.) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — not a defect, but means GA/UTM-based attribution tooling won't pick these up without a separate mapping.
