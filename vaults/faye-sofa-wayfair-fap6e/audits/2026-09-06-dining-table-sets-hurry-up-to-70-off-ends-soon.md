# DINING TABLE SETS 🚩 Hurry – UP TO 70% OFF ends soon 🚩 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a half-match, which for this brand is practically restraint. The subject promises "DINING TABLE SETS ... UP TO 70% OFF," and the first two tiles under "picked just for you" actually are dining sets — a wood table-and-chairs set and a poolside dining table with place settings. That's more than the last several Wayfair sends managed ("Furniture Cushions" that showed no cushions, "pergolas" that showed none). But it's still just two tiles before the email face-plants into the usual unrelated grid: furniture covers, a dresser, a wall clock, gym equipment, a playset, a sink, ceiling tiles, a sewing machine.
- The top banner is the same recycled "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE UP TO 70% OFF" strip seen in the 09-04 clearance send — no dining table framing at all in the hero. So the subject line's specific promise is fulfilled only in miniature, buried below a generic banner, then abandoned entirely by the second screen of the email.
- Verdict: better than the recent bait-and-switch run, but still not an email built around its own subject line — it's a generic catalog blast with two on-theme tiles bolted to the top to justify the subject.

## What's working

- The subject's core promise (dining table sets) is visible in the first "picked just for you" tile — a real wood dining table and chairs.
- A second dining-adjacent tile (outdoor dining table with place settings, "Now $560.11") reinforces the theme immediately after.
- Pricing is concrete and visible (was/now prices, star ratings) on multiple products, not just vague "% off" banners.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text isn't overlapping, layout is intact.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" and "shop all sales" CTAs are clear, high-contrast bars.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is generic "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE UP TO 70% OFF," not a dining-table hero — a shopper clicking from the subject line lands on a banner about nothing in particular.
- After the first two tiles, the email pivots entirely away from dining tables into furniture covers, a clock, gym equipment, a playset, a sink, and a sewing machine — no sustained category focus.
- "doorbuster deals" and "Your Home, These Picks" sections have zero connection to dining furniture, diluting the subject's promise.
- Repeats the same clearance banner and category-link block (area rugs/outdoor furniture/mattresses/tableware) seen in the 09-04 "CLEARANCE" send — low creative differentiation week over week.
- No single hero CTA tied to "dining table sets" specifically — clicking "shop now" goes to the generic clearance banner, not a dining category page.

- 1. Replace the generic clearance hero banner with a dining-table-specific hero (lifestyle shot of a set dining table, "Dining Sets Up to 70% Off," linking straight to the dining category) — right now the banner and subject don't agree with each other.
- 2. Move the two dining tiles that already exist to the very top, above the banner, and cut the unrelated doorbuster/gym/playset/sink grid or push it well below a dedicated dining section.
- 3. Add a "Shop Dining Table Sets" CTA button directly under the first two product tiles instead of a generic "shop now."
