## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The Wayfair email is structurally sound: it is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a mobile `@media (max-width: 480px)` block, and every sampled link carries tracking params. Its main problems are missing alt text on many images, about 20 tracking pixels served over plain `http://`, and no visible unsubscribe or authentication data in the captured headers. Most of the compliance and link results are inconclusive because of how the email was captured and probed, not confirmed defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 material links were probed and 37 skipped. The probed links returned 429 (rate limited), which means the server throttled the probe. It does not mean the link is invalid. Affected links include:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale")
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?...` ("Wayfair")
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/wayfair-product-L441-K~W004535054.html?...` ("Justine Basilico Coffee Table with Storage")
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App")
- **Probing was incomplete.** 4 links were not checked because the 70s time budget ran out, so they are neither pass nor fail:
  - `/daily-sales/ds/50%2B-dining-tables-verified-for-quality-`
  - `/furniture/sb0/recliners-c413904.html?_emr=deb723a6-81b3-`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/perfect-teen-dressers-se30603.html?e`
  
  The recliners and teen-dressers URLs are cut off mid-parameter. This is probably truncation in the QA report rather than the email, but the QA output can't confirm that.
- **Malformed image URL.** `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains a bare `%oo`, which is an invalid percent-escape. Some clients may fail to load it. The name also begins with `0728`, which suggests a stale or reused asset.
- **Test-looking asset names.** `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` has "test" in its name and is wrapped in a link to `https://www.wayfair.com/` (`sltid=5`). That is a minor hygiene issue and suggests a pre-production asset shipped.
- **Tracking structure is consistent.** Each link has a per-link `_eml` token and shared campaign IDs (`batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`). The `onelink.me` links include `af_dp` and `af_web_dp` deep-link fallbacks.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, content images:**
  - `48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`
  - `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`
  - `stars_10.png`
  - `verified-listicles-pingpong-pricecopy-email-miniba…`
  - `image.jpg` (`/im/54539322/resize-w1280…`)
  - `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_recliners_general_lay…`
  - two `wfus_jt800_batch9_livingroom_recliners_everystyle_…` images
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
  
  The linked images are the real problem. With images blocked, the link text for "Shop Sale" and the hero links falls back to a filename. The probe also reported these images as link text, e.g. `[image: 48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.]`. The `stars_10.png` rating image has no alt text, so the product rating is unavailable to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (the open pixel) and 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` images have no alt text. For 1×1-style beacons, an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix. A missing attribute is still a validation gap, and some screen readers announce the filename.
- **Plain `http://` images.** All 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels use `http://`. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may rewrite or block them. If they are blocked, the impression counts are lost. This also triggers mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Responsive support is present.** The visible CSS includes the `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` Gmail fix, `u + .Gmail` selectors, and an Outlook app stacking fix (`body[data-outlook-cycle]`). Because the HTML is truncated, I can't check the body markup, such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables or the preheader.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied source or QA findings. The `_emr`, `_eml` and `mdlid` values are rendered per-recipient identifiers, not leftover merge tags. The truncated HTML means I can't confirm this for the full body.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (RFC 8058 one-click) is also missing. The QA tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may be stripping or not capturing these headers. The sender is a large retailer, so the headers are likely present at origin. **Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed defect.** Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the truncated source doesn't show it or a postal address. I can't verify either one.
- **From domain.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain of the sender's domain. No alignment problem is visible from the data supplied.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No standard UTM parameters.** None of the sampled URLs has `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `wfcs=cs7`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16386`, `sltid` (slot ID). That works if their analytics reads these. A third-party analytics tool such as GA would not attribute the sessions to the email channel. The `onelink.me` links use `pid=Email` and `c=Triggered`.
- **Possible campaign mislabel.** The app link has `c=Triggered`, but the other links have `source=graymatter_batch`, which looks like a batch send. If this is a batch campaign, the "Triggered" tag will skew AppsFlyer attribution. It could also be a template default.
- **Landing-page alignment.** The subject is about coffee tables. Of the probed links, only the "Justine Basilico Coffee Table with Storage" product link (`sku=MSAP1000`) matches. The other probed links go to `/daily-sales` (twice) and the homepage `/` (twice, including the `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` header image, `sltid=5`). The unprobed links point at dining tables, recliners, pergolas and teen dressers. The hero and header links are generic, and the rest of the email's destinations don't match the subject line. The sampled data doesn't show a coffee-table category page.
- **Fallback behavior.** The `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` fallback uses `http://`, so it depends on a redirect to HTTPS.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`t.wayfair.com/b.php`, `li.wayfair.com/imp?…`) and descriptive alt text to the linked content images, including the hero banners and `stars_10.png` (e.g. `alt="5 out of 5 stars"`).
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `af_web_dp` fallback over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `%oo` escape in `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, and rename the asset to something that isn't date-stamped or a test file. Replace `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` or confirm it is intended.
4. Retest the 12 material links with throttling or a longer budget so the 429s and the 4 unprobed URLs are resolved. Verify that the truncated URLs are only cut off in the report.
5. Inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as defects. Fix the QA tool if the relay is dropping them.
6. Check the visible unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML. The truncated source doesn't show them.
7. If third-party analytics matter, add UTM parameters. Confirm that `c=Triggered` on the app links is intended.
8. Point the hero and header links at a coffee-table landing page so the subject line and destination match.
