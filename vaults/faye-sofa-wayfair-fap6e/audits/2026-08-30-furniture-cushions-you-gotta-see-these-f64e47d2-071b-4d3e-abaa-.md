# FURNITURE CUSHIONS. You *gotta* see these.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "FURNITURE CUSHIONS. You *gotta* see these," and this time the hero image actually delivers — a real photo of stacked cushions sits right at the top, with a star rating underneath. That's a genuine improvement over the last "furniture cushions" send from 2026-08-28, which scored 3.00/10 for the exact same subject/hero disconnect. Credit where due: Wayfair fixed the thing I flagged twice in a row.
- But the email falls apart almost immediately after that hero. It pivots to "the warmest welcome — doormats at every price," then "but wait, there's more" into a kitchen-sink grid of privacy screens, pergolas, dressers, shoe cabinets, air conditioners, pendant lights, bedding, curtains, hot tub accessories, bar stools, filing cabinets, sheets, mini fridges, shoe racks, and outdoor sheds. And several of those grid tiles are outright broken — plain white boxes with a small blue "missing image" icon where a product photo should be, at minimum four times ("White Curtains & Drapes," "Our Favorite Hot Tub Accessories," "Top-Rated Outdoor Privacy Screens," "In-Stock Filing Cabinets").
- Net take: the hero/subject alignment problem is fixed, but a new, more damaging problem — visibly broken images shipped to inboxes — replaces it. This is not a send I'd forward internally as a template; it's a send I'd flag to whoever QA's the ESP render.

## What's working

- Hero image genuinely matches the subject line — a real photo of stacked furniture cushions, unlike the 2026-08-28 "Furniture Cushions" send that broke this exact link.
- The "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" top banner is legible and consistent with brand's current campaign.
- Star rating under the hero product adds a light social-proof signal.
- The app-download CTA footer with QR code is clean and doesn't clutter the main offer stack.

## What's weak

- At least four grid tiles render as broken images (blank white boxes with a placeholder icon) — this is a visible, embarrassing render bug, not a copy problem.
- Immediately after the on-subject hero, the email pivots to doormats, then to a dozen unrelated categories — the cushions promise is abandoned after one screen.
- No visible discount, price, or urgency tied to the cushions themselves — the one "on-subject" product has no offer attached to it.
- The email is extremely long and unfocused — 15+ product categories crammed into one send dilutes any single CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken image tiles before send** — this is the single biggest lift available; four dead placeholder boxes actively erode trust and make the email look unfinished, worse than any copy mismatch.
- 2. Cut the email down to 3-4 categories max, anchored on furniture cushions, and drop the unrelated doormat/pergola/mini-fridge/filing-cabinet sprawl — a focused send converts better than a catalog dump.
- 3. Put a specific discount or price callout directly under the cushions hero photo (e.g., "Starting at $XX" or "Up to 40% off cushions") since right now the on-subject hero has zero offer attached.
- 4. Move the "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" banner content to actually reflect what's below it, or drop it if it's not the featured category — it's become a boilerplate banner across sends regardless of subject.

# Technical Audit — Wayfair "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses a standard Uber-style responsive MJML/Zeplin-generated table layout with heavy inline media queries; core rendering markup is sound, but several tracking pixels use insecure HTTP, most images lack alt text, and message-level authentication/compliance headers were not observable in the captured source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 58 total links were probed (46 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); 8 probed links returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** rather than a definitive pass — includes the primary CTA `Shop Sale` (`wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`), the masthead logo link, and a PDP link. These are marked "may be valid" but are unconfirmed.
- 4 links were left **entirely unprobed** due to a 70s time budget cutoff, including a PDP link and two category links (`doormats-c416566`, `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas`) — status unknown.
- All probed/queued links carry consistent tracking parameters (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`), indicating a coherent internal tracking schema — no malformed or truncated tracking params observed in the sampled links.
- 20 first-party open/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are loaded over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. Modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) commonly block or proxy-rewrite non-HTTPS image requests, which can suppress open tracking and/or trigger mixed-content warnings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **21 images missing `alt` text**, including primary hero creative (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), SKU header art, review-star ratings (`stars_9.png`), and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels. Missing alt on content images (hero, SKU headers) degrades the experience for image-blocked/screen-reader clients since no fallback text is rendered.
- One image filename contains a raw `%` character not URL-encoded: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — likely a typo in the source filename (`%oor` vs `door`), which may cause a 404 on some CDNs/clients that don't lenify the stray `%`.
- Markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with an extensive Uber-style mobile media-query block (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, Outlook `u-OutlookAppStackingFix`, Gmail-specific `u + .Gmail` hooks) — indicates deliberate cross-client targeting (Outlook, Gmail, iOS `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout)`); no structural defects found in the visible fragment.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.

4. Re-run link probing with a longer time budget or backoff/retry on 429s to get a definitive pass/fail on the 12 rate-limited + 4 unprobed links, particularly the primary "Shop Sale" CTA.
