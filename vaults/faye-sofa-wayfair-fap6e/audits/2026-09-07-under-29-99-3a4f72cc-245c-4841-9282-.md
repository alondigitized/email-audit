# under $29.99
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- For once, the subject actually matches the render — "under $29.99" opens on a hero banner reading "all under $29.99" with a punch bowl, lamps, a rug, and a console table underneath. That's a rare win for this brand's history of subject/render mismatches. But the honesty ends there: the email immediately buries its own promise under eight more unrelated modules — Halloween decor, chef knives, vintage revival, storage, bedding, mattresses, and outdoor/office furniture — none of which carry the under-$29.99 framing.
- This is the classic Wayfair firehose format: one honest hero, then a wall of disconnected categories with no price anchor. It reads less like a targeted "under $29.99" sale and more like a generic catalog dump that happened to use that line as clickbait for the top slot.
- Verdict: better than this week's average Wayfair send because the subject and top-of-fold actually agree, but it collapses into the same kitchen-sink pattern by the second scroll.

## What's working

- Subject "under $29.99" is honest — the hero banner literally says "all under $29.99" with matching product tiles.
- Clean red/white banner design at top gives clear visual hierarchy for the primary offer.
- Distinct color-coded section banners (red, purple, blue) help visually segment categories during scroll.
- Each module has a clear, specific CTA button ("Shop under $29.99", "Shop scary-season faves", "Shop bedding essentials", etc.).
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text or broken tiles.

## What's weak

- The under-$29.99 promise only applies to the first module; everything after (Halloween, vintage, storage, bedding, mattresses, recliners) has no visible price tie-in, so the subject stops being true halfway through the email.
- No urgency or deadline on the core offer itself — "70% off ends tonight" appears only in the top strip banner, disconnected from the "under $29.99" hero.
- Nine separate category modules in one email is overwhelming — no single hero product or clear "click here first" moment beyond the top fold.
- "But wait, there's more..." section crams privacy screens, massage chairs, recliners, and office chairs with no thematic connection to anything above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 3-4 modules max, or make every module honor the "under $29.99" promise so the subject stays true for the whole scroll — right now it's a bait-and-switch after module one.
- 2. Add a countdown or explicit deadline directly under the "all under $29.99" hero, since urgency currently only lives in the disconnected top strip.
- 3. Drop or split off the unrelated modules (Halloween, vintage, storage, mattresses, recliners) into a separate send — a $29.99-and-under email shouldn't end on massage chairs and privacy screens.
- 4. Lead with a single hero product shot (e.g. the punch bowl or rug) at larger size instead of four small tiles, to give the eye one clear landing point.
