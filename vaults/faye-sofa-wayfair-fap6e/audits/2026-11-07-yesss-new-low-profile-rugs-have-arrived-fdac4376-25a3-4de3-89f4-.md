- The render is clean. Every image loads and there are no gaps. The one real urgency element, "70% OFF Holiday Flash Sale ENDS TONIGHT," is buried near the bottom, after about eight screens of product. The email's best hook sits where few people will scroll.
- It fits the recent Wayfair run. The 11/06 "New pergolas available just for you" send had a hero grid that matched its subject. The 11/05 "Pergolas for less" send drifted into catch-all content, and this one does the same. It's a competent grid-of-everything email wearing a rug subject line.

## What's working

- All images render cleanly with no broken tiles or overlapping text.
- The "must-shop steals" header and the first tile are on-topic for the subject, since the rug tile is first.
- The 7% back credit card block is a clear loyalty offer, with a visible "Apply Now" button.
- Star ratings under the "fresh-picked favorites" tiles add social proof.
- The "70% OFF ENDS TONIGHT" banner is a credible, time-bounded deadline that matches the season.

## What's weak

- The subject promises rugs, but only one tile out of dozens is a rug. The first row also holds pergolas, mats and gazebos.
- The "fresh-picked favorites" section is four near-identical pergola tiles, with no rugs and no variety.
- The strongest urgency, "ends tonight," is near the bottom, and there is no deadline up top.
- There is no single dominant CTA. Dozens of small tile links compete with the card ad and the flash-sale banner.
- The tiles have no prices, so nothing tells you what a rug costs or what the offer is on the first screen.
- "Yesss!" plus an all-caps "LOW-PROFILE RUGS" reads as shouty. It is also the fourth or more Wayfair send in a few days, and the pergola repetition shows.

- 3. Put prices or "from $X" on the tiles, and use a single clear button such as "Shop low-profile rugs."
- 4. Move the credit card block lower, below the product content.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

That includes `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale") and `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` ("Wayfair"). The 60 skipped and 4 unprobed links (time budget) are unverified, not passing.
