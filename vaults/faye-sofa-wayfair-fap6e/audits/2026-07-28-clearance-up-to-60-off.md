# 👉 Clearance 👉 UP TO 60% OFF 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is one of Wayfair's more coherent sends this week — the hero banner actually says "60% OFF OUTDOOR CLEARANCE," and the subject says "Clearance UP TO 60% OFF." That's real alignment, unlike the "BAKING DISHES" (2026-07-27) or "WALL DÉCOR" (2026-07-27) sends where the subject named a category the hero never mentioned. The whole body stays on-theme too — rugs, planters, lighting, pet essentials, fire pits, grills, patio furniture — all outdoor, all clearance.
- Where it falls down is differentiation and fatigue. This is at minimum the sixth near-identical "X% OFF" Wayfair send audited in three days, and the subject is the same three-emoji-arrow, no-product-named formula ("👉 ... 👉 ... 👉") the brand has run all week. There's no deadline, no single hero product, and no personalization — just a wall of category tiles with percentage badges.
- Net: clean execution, honest categorization, zero novelty. A subscriber who's seen the last five Wayfair emails will skim and archive.

## What's working

- Hero banner copy ("60% OFF OUTDOOR CLEARANCE") matches the subject line almost verbatim — no bait-and-switch.
- Every category tile below the hero stays on the outdoor theme (rugs, planters, lighting, pet essentials, fire pits, storage, grills, dining) — no orphaned generic banner.
- Clean render: no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum, star ratings display correctly on every product thumbnail.
- Financing block (Affirm/Afterpay/Klarna) and "free & easy delivery over $35" are legible, well-placed trust signals.

## What's weak

- Subject is the same recycled "👉/❌/⏰ + UP TO X% OFF" emoji-bracket formula seen in five of the last six audited sends — cadence fatigue is real.
- No named product or category in the subject line itself ("Clearance" is as generic as it gets) — compare to "AWNINGS ❌ ENDS TONIGHT" or "KITCHEN MATS under $30," which at least anchor to a product.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in subject or hero — "clearance" implies limited stock but nothing here says when it ends.
- "Picked just for you" section isn't actually personalized — it's a generic mix (lantern, grill, storage bench, patio chair) with no visible signal tying it to a specific shopper.
- Primary CTAs are mostly bare category links ("outdoor planters →") rather than action-driving copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Anchor the subject to one specific category or hero product the way the "KITCHEN MATS under $30" and "AWNINGS" sends did — "Clearance" alone has zero curiosity or specificity. Even "Outdoor Clearance: rugs, planters, grills up to 60% off" would beat the emoji-arrow filler.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Sunday") — this send has an honest hero/subject match but leaves urgency on the table entirely.
- 3. Make "picked just for you" genuinely personalized (browsing history, past purchase category) or rename it to avoid overpromising — right now it reads as a generic merchandising block wearing a personalization label.
- 4. Tighten CTA copy on category tiles to verb+noun ("Shop Rugs Now") instead of bare category names with an arrow.

- **Subject Alt B:** `👉 60% Off Outdoor — Ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, fire pits, grills, and more — all outdoor, all clearance.`

# Technical Email Audit: Wayfair "Clearance Up to 60% Off"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard Wayfair batch marketing template (Unbounce/ESP-style responsive HTML) with heavy tracking pixel usage and multiple accessibility/compliance gaps in the automated scan; 57% of automated checks passed with 3 warning categories and 0 hard failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 67 total links were probed (55 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 6 returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** rather than a confirmed pass/fail, including primary CTAs: `Shop Sale` → `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, the hero image link, and both app-download links (`https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed broken.
- **4 links were never probed** due to the 70s time budget, including a product link (`wayfair-product-L6114-K~W100203863.html`) and a grill product page — status unknown.
- All probed/tracked links carry consistent campaign params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs=cs7`, `refid=MKTEML_138186`, `maiid=15872`, `emlid=101`), suggesting a coherent single-send tracking scheme with no mismatched IDs observed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 tracking pixels** (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) are served over **plain `http://`**, not `https://` — these will likely be blocked or stripped by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies (Gmail, Apple Mail), silently degrading open/impression tracking.
- **~24 images missing `alt` text**, including the primary hero banner (`0728_OutdoorClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), all star-rating icons (`stars_9.png`/`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`, appearing ~13×), promo banners, and the beacon/pixel images. This degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for anyone with images blocked (a common default in Outlook/Gmail), since no fallback text renders.
- Responsive CSS is present and reasonably thorough (`@media max-width: 480px` rules for buttons, titles, product rec grid, footer links), including a `min-width: 100vw` Gmail/Outlook mobile fix — no structural rendering issues found.
- Uses a custom `@font-face` (Sofia, served via HTTPS from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`) with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for Outlook — correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens were found in the visible source or flagged by QA. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** in the captured headers — QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence, but as observed it cannot be confirmed present.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 cannot be confirmed supported from available headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture (again potentially a relay artifact rather than a true auth failure).
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in the footer/utility link set), satisfying the baseline CAN-SPAM body requirement even though the header-level mechanism is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All primary CTA and product links resolve to `wayfair.com` (e.g. `/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/indoor%2foutdoor-rug-clearance~e369270.html`) — consistent with the sending domain (`members.wayfair.com`), no domain mismatch.
- Landing URLs consistently carry the campaign's tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `refid=MKTEML_138186`), enabling attribution continuity from email click to site session.
- App-deep-link CTAs use AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with matching `_emr`/`_eml`/`refid` params mirrored into `af_sub5`/`c=Triggered` for cross-platform attribution — structurally consistent, though unverified live due to 429s (see §2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels to `https://` to prevent silent tracking loss in TLS-enforcing clients.
2. Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for purely decorative) alt attributes to the hero image, promo banners, and star-rating icons.
3. Re-verify the 6 rate-limited links and 4 unprobed links via a slower/staggered probe pass before treating them as confirmed-good.
4. Confirm with the sending infrastructure/relay team whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are genuinely absent or simply not captured by the AgentMail relay — if genuinely absent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers and verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
