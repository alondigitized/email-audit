# Our best gazebo deals
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the worst-rendering Wayfair send in the recent run. Nearly every product image in the body — the gazebo photo, the pergola shot, the AC unit, the birdbaths, the cat trees, literally all of them — is a broken blue placeholder icon sitting in a sea of empty white space. The subject ("Our best gazebo deals") does technically match the first tile text ("Our best gazebo deals »"), which continues the streak of subject/hero alignment seen in 08-08's "Clearout: 72 HOURS ONLY" and "On sale now: pergolas," but that coherence is undermined by an email that looks fundamentally broken on open.
- The layout itself — purple section banners for "score styles you ❤️," "wayfair REWARDS," "cool down... on any budget," "let's complete your space 🏡" — is the same modular grid Wayfair always runs, just stuffed with more image slots than usual and none of them loading. Duplicate text links (e.g., "Our best gazebo deals »" appearing twice) stacked next to broken image icons make the whole thing read like a QA failure, not a finished campaign email.
- Judgement: this shouldn't have shipped. Even ignoring subject/hero mismatch patterns flagged in past audits, a recipient opening this sees a wall of broken images — that alone tanks trust and click-through regardless of copy quality.

## What's working

- Subject line "Our best gazebo deals" matches the first content tile in the hero grid, avoiding the bait-and-switch pattern flagged repeatedly in past sends (08-09 slipcovers/bookcases, 08-04, 08-01–03).
- Section structure is scannable — purple banners clearly divide "score styles," "rewards," "cool down," and "complete your space" segments.
- Footer includes the standard trust signals: Rewards, Financing, Professional program, Exclusive Brands, social links, and app download CTA.
- The 5% Rewards banner is a clean, simple callout with a clear "Take a look →" CTA.

## What's weak

- Nearly every product/lifestyle image in the body is a broken blue placeholder icon — this is a severe, visible render bug, not a minor cosmetic issue.
- Massive dead white space above the fold and between broken image blocks makes the email feel unfinished and untrustworthy.
- Duplicate text links appear stacked (e.g., "Our best gazebo deals »" shown twice, "On Sale Now: Shoe Storage Benches »" repeated) next to broken images, compounding the broken feel.
- No visible discrete offer (%/$ off, BOGO) anywhere in the readable copy — just category links like "Vanity Lighting for Less" and "Best-Selling Cat Trees" with no price anchor.
- With images broken, there's no visual hierarchy at all — the eye has nothing to land on except purple banner text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix image delivery before anything else — this is a full render failure, not a design nit. No copy or CTA change matters if every product photo is a broken icon.
- 2. Cut the duplicate text links stacked under broken images — clean up the template so each product tile shows one link, one image, one label.
- 3. Add a concrete offer to the hero (e.g., "Gazebos from $199" or "Up to 40% off outdoor structures") — right now there's no price anchor anywhere, unlike the stronger "72-HR CLEAROUT — UP TO 60% OFF" sends.
- 4. Trim the email length — this send has an enormous number of category tiles (birdbaths, cat trees, dining chairs, ottomans, wallpaper, planters, bedding); consolidate to the top 4-5 categories tied to the gazebo/outdoor theme in the subject.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Email renders on a legacy Unbounce/BEE-style responsive framework with standard `u-` mobile breakpoint classes. Automated QA returned a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and non-HTTPS tracking pixels; no broken links were confirmed (all failures were rate-limit responses, not 404s/dead links).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 60 links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); all 12 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by Wayfair's edge, not confirmed broken) — includes the primary hero CTA `daily-sales/filters/our-best-gazebo-deals--se82152.html` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me` deep link.
- 4 links were left unprobed on time budget, including `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` and `our-best-narrow-nightstands-se68199.` — note the trailing `.` and `%3a` (URL-encoded colon) in these slugs, which look like malformed href construction rather than probe artifacts and are worth a manual check.
- 20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "Image uses http://: non-HTTPS source may be blocked." In a TLS-enforcing mail client these impression pixels will be silently dropped, undercounting opens/attribution for this send.
- Standard `_emr`/`_eml`/`refid`/`maiid` click-tracking params are present and consistently applied across probed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- 15 images are missing `alt` text, including content-bearing creative (`0728_Outdo%orClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, three `wfus_jt800_batch10_majorappliances_airconditioners*` assets, `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`, `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`) as well as all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels and the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open-tracking pixel.
- Tracking/beacon pixels lacking `alt` is standard practice (decorative, zero-dimension), but the content images lacking `alt` (hero banners, product/appliance imagery, rewards disclaimer) will read as blank to screen readers and show broken-image placeholders with filenames when images are blocked — a real accessibility gap.
- One asset filename is truncated/malformed in the source: `0728_Outdo%orClearance_SkinnyHero-General.jpg` contains a stray `%` (likely an encoding artifact from `%23` or similar being partially decoded) — worth verifying the actual asset resolves correctly.
- Mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media max-width:480px`) is present and comprehensive (width/display overrides, font-size scaling, button padding) — no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `*|...|*`) or personalization placeholders found in the visible HTML/link set. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by the automated check. QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM/one-click compliance status from this data alone; needs verification against raw SMTP headers at the source MTA.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — same caveat as above; if genuinely absent, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements would not be met.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — status unknown, same relay-visibility caveat. Cannot confirm authentication posture from available data.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in-body (QA note: "an unsubscribe link is always included" in the probe set), satisfying the visible CAN-SPAM opt-out requirement at the content level.

**Recommendation**: these three header findings should be re-verified against raw headers captured before the AgentMail relay, since relay stripping vs. genuine absence changes the severity substantially.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Wayfair uses proprietary tracking params (`_emr`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121679`, `maiid=15926`, `emlid=101`, `batchid=15`, `source=graymatter_batch`) rather than standard UTM (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — consistent across all sampled links, so no internal mismatch, but note this if any downstream analytics expects `utm_*` params specifically.
- Subject line "Our best gazebo deals" aligns with the hero CTA link text/slug `our-best-gazebo-deals--se82152.html` — subject-to-landing-page continuity confirmed for the primary link.
- Cannot confirm final landing-page destinations render correctly (all probes 429'd before reaching content), so full parity between promoted category (gazebos/pergolas/outdoor) and landing page content is unverified this run.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Re-run link probing after the 429 rate-limit window clears (or with delay/backoff) to get pass/fail confidence on the 12 rate-limited + 4 unprobed links, particularly the hero CTA and app deep link.
2. Fix the malformed slugs `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` (raw `%3a`) and `our-best-narrow-nightstands-se68199.` (trailing dot) — likely href-generation bugs.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images currently missing it; leave tracking-pixel `alt` empty/omitted as-is (standard practice).
4. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to avoid silent drops in TLS-enforcing clients.
5. Verify List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and Authentication-Results directly against source SMTP headers (pre-relay) to rule out a relay-visibility false negative before treating this as a compliance gap.
