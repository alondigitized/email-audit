## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

Standard Wayfair Bronto/graymatter-batch transactional template; tracking, redirects, and image delivery are functional, but authentication headers weren't captured, several tracking pixels use plain HTTP, and all images lack alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 72 total links were probed (rate-limited to CTA/high-click links); 60 were deprioritized (footer/utility/social), per QA policy — no coverage gap here, by design.
- All probed material links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited), not confirmed dead — e.g. `wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`. Evidence is inconclusive, not a failure.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to the 70s time budget (e.g. `wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/blue-curtains-%26-drapes-you%27ll-lo...`), including one with a truncated/malformed-looking URL — worth a manual check that the encoding wasn't cut off mid-string.
- Standard `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid` tracking params present and consistent across sampled links — no missing-token issues found.
- 20 sequential open-tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) all resolve over `http://`, not `https://` (see item 4).

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- All content and tracking images are missing `alt` text (16+ instances flagged), including hero/promo images (`0922_SemiAnnual_SkinnyHero-General-1.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_...`) and review-star icons (`stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png`). Screen readers will announce nothing for these images if load fails.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000-019`) load over unencrypted HTTP. Mixed-content blocking in Gmail/Outlook webmail can silently drop these, undercounting opens; this is a deliverability/measurement risk, not a rendering risk to the recipient.
- One image `src` contains a literal `%o` inside the filename (`0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`) — malformed URL-encoding that may 404 depending on server-side path decoding; worth verifying the asset resolves.
- Media queries and MSO/Outlook conditional CSS present (`u-OutlookAppStackingFix`, Gmail-specific padding fix) — standard responsive scaffolding, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens or personalization placeholders visible in the truncated source (no unresolved `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` patterns). No issues found based on available evidence.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) also not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) header not found — cannot verify sender authentication passed; flagged as unknown, not failed.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per QA methodology note ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), so the visible CAN-SPAM footer requirement appears met even though header-level one-click support is unverified.
- Recommend re-running the audit against the raw SMTP/MIME headers (not just the relayed copy) to close out the three header-level warnings.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Wayfair uses its own attribution schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) rather than standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`). This is consistent internally but won't populate `utm_*` fields in third-party analytics tools expecting that convention — not a defect, just worth noting if cross-platform attribution is a requirement.
- Landing page slugs align with email content/CTA text (e.g. `daily-sales/deals-on-bins-%26-baskets~e322596.html` matches "deals on bins & baskets" copy; `daily-sales/filters/gas-grills-for-less-se24840.html` matches "Gas Grills for Less"). No mismatch found.
- App deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) includes both `af_dp` (native deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback) — correct OneLink fallback pattern.

7. Recommendations

- Re-run link probing outside the 429 rate-limit window (or with backoff/staggered requests) to get a real pass/fail on the 12 probed + 4 unprobed links.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (hero, promo, review stars) for accessibility and to preserve message context when images are blocked.
- Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in webmail clients.
- Verify the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` asset path resolves correctly given the embedded `%o` encoding artifact.
- Capture raw SMTP headers (not relayed) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present and passing.
