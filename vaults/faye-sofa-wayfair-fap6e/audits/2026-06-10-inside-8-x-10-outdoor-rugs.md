- This is the **seventh consecutive delivery** of "Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS" — it ran May 31, June 1, 4, 5, 6, 8, and now June 10. At seven sends with no creative change, this has crossed well past remarketing into subscriber abuse. A real inbox will have trained its owner to ignore or delete on sight before it even opens.

## What's working

- **"JUNE 11-13 HOT SUMMER SALE"** calendar block is the strongest element: a specific three-day window gives a real reason to act now rather than the stale "savings too good to pass up" headline.
- **Wayfair Rewards / 5-Star Financing / Exclusive Brands** trifecta in the footer is a clean, low-friction trust layer for anyone on the fence.
- **No render bugs** — product tiles, price callout, and QR code all appear intact and well-spaced.
- **"$1.49/ft²"** is a specific price anchor, even if buried; it outperforms every vague percentage claim in Wayfair's recent sends.

## What's weak

- **Subject/body bait** is the defining failure: "8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS" is never paid off. The hero says "score styles you ♥" and the body is a general product grid with rugs mixed alongside other home furnishings.
- **Seven identical sends** of the same subject line in 11 days. By now, any engaged subscriber's pattern recognition has filed this into the skip pile. This iteration cannot recover that ground.
- **No specific CTA button** visible in the above-the-fold hero — the main navigation has "Shop Sale" but the body's product tiles don't show clear, labeled buttons.
- **"savings too good to pass up"** as a body headline is pure filler: no category, no number, no deadline tied to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this subject line immediately.** Seven sends is three too many. The creative is dead; the signal you're collecting is non-response, not performance.
- 2. **Deliver the subject's promise.** If the subject says 8'×10' outdoor rugs, the hero should show at least one 8×10 rug with the actual price. One hero product, one price, one CTA.
- 3. **Lead the body with the sale date.** "JUNE 11-13 HOT SUMMER SALE" is the most newsworthy element in this email — it belongs in the hero, not at the bottom third.
- 4. **Replace "savings too good to pass up"** with the actual discount or a category anchor: "Up to X% off outdoor rugs, June 11–13 only."
- 5. **Add a visible CTA button** below the hero with specific copy like "Shop 8×10 Outdoor Rugs."
