# ICE MAKERS ⏱️ 🔔 48-HOUR CLEARANCE STARTS NOW 🔔 ⏱️ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises ICE MAKERS and a 48-hour clearance. The email delivers the clearance and mostly drops the ice makers. The hero has a big "48 HR" graphic with "70% OFF" and "48-hour clearance" in the middle. The first product under "picked just for you" is a countertop ice maker, so the promise is kept for one tile. After that the email turns into a general Wayfair sale: electric fireplaces, a sink, rugs, bar stools, mattresses, curtains, bedding, mirrors, nursery, faucets, a pergola and a patio shade.
- The offers are loud and specific. Rugs are up to 70% off, and mattresses, curtains, bedding, mirrors, nursery and faucets are 40-50% off. The layout is clean, and Affirm financing and free delivery over $35 add to the pitch. The problem is focus. About a dozen categories compete, with no single hero product and no single deadline tied to the ice maker the subject promised. The subject says one thing and the body says everything.
- This lands at an average promotional send. It also repeats the pergola thread from the Oct 15-17 sends ("FOUND: Pergolas," "Price just dropped on Pergolas"). That is a lot of pergola for someone who just wanted an ice maker.

## What's working

- The hero reads fast. "48 HR" plus "70% OFF" and the "48-hour clearance" label make the urgency obvious.
- Category tiles are consistent: a big "up to X% OFF" number with a purple button beneath. They are easy to scan.
- The ice maker is the first product tile, with was/now pricing and star ratings, so the subject's promise is met at the top.
- The Affirm banner ("as low as 0% APR") and "free & easy delivery over $35" lower purchase friction on big-ticket items.
- The purple and white palette is on-brand and consistent. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Only one ice maker appears. The subject promises a category the body barely covers.
- The products below the first tile (fireplaces, sink, shower, pergola) have no connection to ice makers or to each other. The grid feels like a catch-all.
- The product tiles have no visible labels or CTAs. They show only tiny prices and stars, so the viewer has to guess what each is and where it goes.
- The email is very long, about a dozen blocks before the footer. The "48 HR" urgency gets lost, and the tiles carry no deadline.
- Pergola is back in the "Your Home, These Picks" row ("On sale now: pergolas"), following the pergola-heavy sends of the last three days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the ice maker the hero. Show 3-4 ice makers with prices and a "Shop ice makers" button, then cut the body to 2-3 adjacent categories (mini fridges, kitchen appliances).
- 2. Show an explicit deadline such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM ET," with a countdown if possible, so the 48-hour claim is credible.
- 3. Add a caption (product name, discount) to each product tile and a clear button on the primary ones.
- 4. Trim the category tiles to the 4-6 strongest. Drop the pergola repeat or space it out from the earlier pergola sends.
- 5. Lead with one stated offer: either "up to 70% off" or a specific ice maker price.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ice makers up to 70% off — 48 hrs only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ice maker deal ends Sunday night`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Countertop ice makers, mini fridges and kitchen picks, marked down until Sunday 11:59 PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 70% off rugs and 50% off bedding, mirrors and more. Free delivery over $35.`
