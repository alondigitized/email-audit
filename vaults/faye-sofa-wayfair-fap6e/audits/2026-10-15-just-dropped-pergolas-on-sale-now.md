# Just dropped: Pergolas on sale now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a pergola send that opens on a blank white box. The hero is empty apart from one tiny broken-image icon. Below it, "on sale: from your browsing" delivers four pergola and gazebo tiles with Sale badges, was/now prices and star ratings. After that, the promise in "Just dropped: Pergolas on sale now" thins out fast.
- The rest is Wayfair's standard everything-template: cleaning, a 7% back credit card, rattan, garden, "sleek & chic", "all under $29.99" and a 70% OFF Holiday Deal Week banner at the very bottom. Only one more pergola appears mid-email under "we saw these catch your eye…", plus a "10' W x 10' D pergolas" link in the closing grid. A pergola shopper gets about 20% of the email, and the strongest offer sits at the very bottom.
- After the 10/12 "Your roundup of pergolas" send, which had a blank hero, the 10/13 pergola emails and the 10/14 "You were eyeing these pergolas" send, this is the fifth pergola-themed email in four days. "Just dropped" also feels inaccurate against that run. The blank hero repeats the 10/12 bug. Verdict: right product, wrong framing, broken top, and a cadence that reads as nagging.

## What's working

- The four pergola tiles sit right under the hero gap, each with a Sale badge, was/now pricing and star ratings. That is the right product at the right depth.
- The "we saw these catch your eye…" block is a single-pergola callout, which feels like real browse-based retargeting.
- Section headers like "the cleaning edit" and "get the garden ready" are skimmable. Purple "Shop … picks" buttons give clear CTAs in those blocks.
- The "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner and the Wayfair Rewards and Credit Card modules keep the email seasonal and loyalty-aware.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is a blank white box with a broken-image icon. It is the first and largest element, and it pushes the pergola tiles below the first screen.
- The subject promises pergolas, but most of the email is unrelated categories: cleaning, bedroom, rattan, desks and bath mats.
- The best offer, 70% OFF, is buried at the bottom instead of leading. The pergola tiles also have no explicit "Shop pergolas" button.
- There is no deadline or reason for "just dropped" beyond the label. After four days of pergola sends, urgency and novelty both feel manufactured.
- The email is very long, with many competing modules and mixed headline styles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Serve a fallback image or a live-text pergola headline with the discount, so the first screen is never blank.
- 2. Cut the page to the pergola story: one tile grid, one "Shop all pergolas" button, and one clear discount line. Drop cleaning, rattan, sleek & chic and bath mats from this send.
- 3. Move the 70% OFF Holiday Deal Week banner to the top and tie it to pergolas, with an end date.
- 4. Add a cadence guard. Suppress a fifth near-identical pergola send in four days, or fold it into a single weekly digest.
- 5. Make "Just dropped" true by featuring the newly added models, or use a price-drop angle, since the tiles already show was/now pricing.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's tracked links all carry consistent attribution parameters, but the QA run couldn't confirm compliance headers or authentication. The HTML has a systematic alt-text gap, about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and several asset/URL hygiene problems. The pass rate was 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **No confirmed broken links.** Eight probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), which the QA tool itself flags as "link may be valid". Examples: "Shop cleaning picks →" (`/appliances/cat/vacuums-deep-cleaners-c1862812.html`), "Shop garden picks →" (`/daily-sales/get-the-garden-ready~e308968.html`), and "Shop under $29.99 →" (`/daily-sales/ds/shop-under-%2429.99-se79155.html`). These are unverified, not failed.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 80 links were probed (68 skipped). 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget ran out, including the pergola product link (`/Aurora-10%E2%80%98-X-12-Louvered-Pergola-With-Adjustable…`) that matches the subject line. The headline offer's destination is therefore unverified.
- **Malformed product URL.** The pergola URL path contains `%E2%80%98` (a left single quote ‘) in the slug, from "10‘ X 12". It may resolve, but it's a fragile slug and should be confirmed.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Bl…` is the open pixel (it decodes to `eventType=EmailOpen`). It has no alt text.
- **Parameter consistency.** Every link carries `_emr`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121641`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16437`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7` and `csnid`, so attribution is uniform. `_eml` and `mdlid` are unique per link, which is expected.
- **App link.** Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links go to `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` and share `mdlid=b0a8235f-…` but have different `_eml` values. That's fine, but the fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, which is non-HTTPS.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, content images.** `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty-…`, `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-vp.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`, `WFCC3%402x.png` and `WFCC%203%20Disclosure.png` all lack alt text. If images are blocked, the header banner and disclosure render as blank space.
- **Missing alt text, ratings.** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (×3) and `stars_11.png` have no alt, so screen readers get no rating information.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` ad-impression pixels (2x1) are served over HTTP and have no alt text. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **Stale asset.** The image `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty` is a Labor Day asset from August in an October 14 send. It also has an apostrophe in the filename, which can break URL handling.
- **Responsive CSS.** The `<style>` block uses `@media (max-width: 480px)` with `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hacks, which is standard practice. I found no problems in the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible, truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncation means the remainder is unchecked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post are not found.** QA says the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed: it may be a capture limitation rather than a sender fault. Without them, there's no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`. The relay is the likely cause, so treat this as a QA blind spot.
- **Unsubscribe link.** QA states one is always probed, but no result is listed for it. It's neither confirmed present nor confirmed failing, and the physical address in the footer also wasn't visible in the truncated source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No standard UTM parameters.** There's no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Wayfair uses its own scheme (`source=graymatter_batch`, `refid`, `emlid`, `wfcs=cs7`). That's an internal convention, not a defect, but external analytics will see no UTM attribution.
- **Subject-to-landing mismatch risk.** The subject is "Pergolas on sale now". Visible link text covers cleaning picks, rattan & raffia, garden, "modern sophistication", under $29.99 and bedding sets. The pergola product link is the only one that matches, and it's unprobed. I can't confirm the landing page matches the subject.
- **`fromNEM=true`** appears on the daily-sales links but not on the vacuum category link or the app link. This is probably deliberate and I can't judge it from the data.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-probe with throttling or retries to resolve the eight 429s. Prioritize the pergola link, which is unprobed.
2. Re-run header capture against the raw message, not the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Add alt text to the five content images and the rating stars. Use `alt=""` for decorative pixels and spacers.
4. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, and the `af_web_dp` fallback too.
5. Rename the asset with the apostrophe, replace the stale Labor Day creative, and check the `%E2%80%98` pergola slug.
6. Confirm the unsubscribe URL and the postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
