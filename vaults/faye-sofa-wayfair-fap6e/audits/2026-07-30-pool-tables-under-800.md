- Below the fold this collapses into the usual Wayfair kitchen-sink scroll: ironing centers, shutters, bath mats, beach chairs, benches, birdbaths, bread pans, and — almost comically on-theme — "Regulation-Sized Ping-Pong Tables," which is the closest thing to a real cross-sell in the whole email. ## What's weak

- The top hero banner ("60% OFF OUTDOOR CLEARANCE") has zero connection to the subject line's pool table promise — the first thing a reader sees isn't what they opened the email for.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email despite past Wayfair sends ("ENDS TONIGHT," "LAST CHANCE") establishing that pattern — this one just fades into genericness.
- Cadence problem: this is at least the sixth "[Category] under $X" or "UP TO N% OFF" structured send this week from the same brand, per the audit history — fatigue risk is real.
- No specific product detail (dimensions, table size, felt color, reviews) shown for the pool table itself — just a category thumbnail and link.
- The email sprawls into a dozen unrelated categories (bath mats, birdbaths, bread pans) that dilute the pool-table hook the subject sold.

- 1. Swap the top hero banner to actually feature the pool table (image + "Pool Tables Under $800") instead of the recycled outdoor-clearance banner — this is the single biggest lift since it fixes the subject/hero mismatch that's cost this brand points on nearly every recent send.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the pool table offer ("Ends Sunday") — this brand has proven urgency copy works ("AWNINGS ❌ ENDS TONIGHT" scored 9/10) and this send has none.
- 3. Trim the tail-end grid or re-sequence it so game-room-adjacent items (the ping-pong tables tile) sit near the pool table content instead of scattered 15 tiles down.
