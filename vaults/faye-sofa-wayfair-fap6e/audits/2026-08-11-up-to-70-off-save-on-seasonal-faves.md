# 😎 UP TO 70% OFF 👉 Save on seasonal faves 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-grid send, and it's honest about what it is — the subject promises "seasonal faves" and "up to 70% off," and the hero banner delivers exactly that: "70% OFF SUMMER MARKDOWNS." No bait-and-switch here, which puts it ahead of the "COVERS & CARRY BAGS" (2026-08-10, 4.00/10) and "CORNER BOOKCASES" (2026-08-09, 5.00/10) sends that promised one thing and opened on the recycled 72-hour clearout banner.
- The problem is this email is pure grid — eight category tiles with %-off ranges (bedding, mattresses, curtains, rugs, fireplaces, dining, mirrors, storage, wall art, office furniture, vanities, grills) stacked under a purple "picked just for you" product carousel, then another purple "free & easy delivery" carousel. Nothing here is personalized to a persona; it's a full-catalog dump dressed as "picked just for you." There's no single hero product or category to click into — it's a choose-your-own-adventure of a dozen roughly equal CTAs, which dilutes intent rather than directing it.
- Rendering is clean throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum, unlike the gazebo send (2026-08-10, 3.00/10) where nearly every image was a broken blue placeholder. Solid, unremarkable execution: 6/10 territory.

## What's working

- Subject and hero banner agree — "70% off" and "seasonal faves" both show up immediately in the "70% OFF SUMMER MARKDOWNS" banner.
- Zero render bugs — every product photo, icon, and badge loads cleanly across a long scroll.
- Clear repeated CTA pattern (orange "up to X% off" buttons) makes the categories scannable even without reading closely.
- Financing options (Affirm, Afterpay, Klarna) and free-delivery threshold ($35+) are both surfaced, adding real purchase-decision value.
- Star ratings and "Sale" badges appear on the "picked just for you" carousel items, giving light social proof.

## What's weak

- "Picked just for you" is a lie the layout gives away — it's a generic living-room/kitchen/decor carousel with no visible personalization signal (no name, no browsing-history framing).
- No single hero product or dominant CTA — a dozen near-equal category tiles compete for the same click, so the eye has nowhere obvious to land after the banner.
- Preview text is missing/not shown — nothing complements the subject, which is a wasted second line of inbox real estate.
- Subject line stacks two emoji + two arrow glyphs ("😎 ... 👉 ... 👉"), pushing toward spam-pattern territory even though it's not ALL CAPS.
- Cadence fatigue risk — this is at least the 6th Wayfair promo in the recent run touching sitewide %-off framing; nothing here differentiates it from 08-08's "72-HR CLEAROUT" (8.00/10) beyond swapping "summer markdowns" copy in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero category (the highest-margin or best-performing one — likely bedding or rugs given they lead the grid) and build the whole top fold around it, pushing the other 10 categories below the fold as a secondary "more ways to save" grid. A/B this against the current all-categories-equal layout.
- 2. Replace "picked just for you" with a headline that matches what's actually shown — either genuinely personalize the carousel or relabel it "trending in your home" / "best sellers this week" to avoid the credibility gap.
- 3. Add real preview text tied to the offer, e.g. "Bedding, rugs, and mattresses all discounted — free delivery over $35."
- 4. Trim the subject to one emoji max and lead with the strongest single number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off summer markdowns — ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your seasonal refresh: 50-70% off bedding, rugs & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, mattresses, rugs & more — free delivery over $35`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 0% APR financing with Affirm, Afterpay & Klarna`

# Wayfair — Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MyEmma/Wayfair transactional-marketing template; core CTAs and unsubscribe are structurally present, but several tracking pixels use plaintext HTTP and most images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 67 links were probed (deprioritized by click-likelihood ranking); 8 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited), not confirmed broken — includes the primary CTA `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...&refid=MKTEML_138848&emlid=101&maiid=15958` and app deep link `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`.
- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (70s), including a product link (`wayfair-product-L128-K~W003575117.html`) and a category link (`Door-Knobs-with-Round-Rosette-Keyless-Lock...`) — status unknown, neither pass nor fail.
- All probed/unprobed links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `csnid`, `event_rec_txid`) — no malformed or missing tracking IDs observed.
- 20 open/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 22 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including the hero (`0811_SummerMarkdowns_SkinnyHero-General.jpg`), the financing banner, the Memorial Day skinny banner, product rating stars (`stars_9/10/11.png`, 8 instances), a promo button image (`0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`), and the open-tracking pixel `b.php`. Screen readers will announce these as blank/filename, or skip silently.
- 20 tracking-pixel `<img>` elements load over `http://` (non-TLS) — flagged as potentially blocked by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading (Gmail proxy, Outlook), which would also break open tracking for those events.
- Extensive `@media` mobile-responsive rules and MSO/Outlook-specific conditional CSS present (`u-FullWidthBlock`, `data-outlook-cycle` fixes, Gmail-specific padding hacks) — indicates deliberate multi-client rendering support; no structural rendering defects identified from the available source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the visible/truncated HTML. No issues found in the reviewed portion — note the source was truncated, so tokens later in the document could not be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay.
- QA notes an in-body unsubscribe link is always included/probed, consistent with CAN-SPAM's footer-unsubscribe requirement, but it was not directly evidenced in the truncated source provided.
- **Caveat**: all three header warnings are relay-side visibility gaps per the QA tool notes, not confirmed violations — should not be treated as compliance failures without re-verification against raw SMTP headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_138848`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`) rather than standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — internal analytics stack, not a defect, but not UTM-compatible if cross-referenced against GA-based reporting.
- Primary CTA (`Shop Sale`) and hero image both point to `/daily-sales` — landing page target is consistent with email subject/content ("UP TO 70% OFF ... seasonal faves").
- Category deep links (`bedding-clearance`, `mattresses-%26-more-on-clearance`, `curtain-%26-drape-clearance`) map logically to their anchor text — no mismatch found.
- App-install CTA correctly routes through OneLink deep-link SDK (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with `af_web_dp` fallback to `wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` — proper deferred-deep-linking pattern.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from HTTP to HTTPS to prevent silent open-tracking loss in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
2. Add `alt` text to all 22 flagged images, prioritizing the hero banner and financing/promo images (accessibility + fallback context when images are blocked).
3. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers outside the AgentMail relay — current absence is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation.
4. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and 4 time-budget-skipped links (including the unprobed product and door-knob category links) in a follow-up pass to confirm they resolve correctly.
