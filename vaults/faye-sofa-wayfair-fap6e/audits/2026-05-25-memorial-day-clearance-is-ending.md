# 🔴 MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE IS ENDING 🔴
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair closes out the Memorial Day run with the most honest subject line of the week: "🔴 MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE IS ENDING 🔴". After six straight days of category-bait subjects ("WALL MOUNTED SHELVES," "POTTING BENCHES," "OUTDOOR WALL DECOR") that delivered the same generic clearance template, this one finally matches what's actually in the email — a sitewide clearance grid. The bar is on the floor, but they cleared it.
- The creative is the same hot-pink/red clearance template we've seen all week, just with the deadline urgency now front-loaded: "ENDS TONIGHT" banner at top, a "48 HRS ONLY flash deals" mid-section, and stacked 40%/50%/70% off tiles. There is no single hero product or category — this is a "shop everything before the door closes" send, which is at least a coherent strategy for a final-day email.
- Subject-to-content alignment is finally honest, the urgency is real (last day), and the discount tiers are specific. This is still a wall of pink tiles, but it's the right wall of pink tiles for day six. Best Wayfair email of the run.

## What's working

- Subject finally matches the content — clearance ending, clearance shown.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" framing creates credible, time-bounded urgency on the actual last day.
- Specific discount tiers visible immediately: 40% / 50% / 70% off blocks.
- "48 HRS ONLY flash deals" middle section reinforces the deadline with concrete numbers.
- "picked just for you" personalization band adds a segmentation signal mid-scroll.

## What's weak

- Still no single hero product — eye bounces across a grid of small thumbnails with no anchor.
- Six identical-looking pink/red tiles stacked vertically create visual fatigue before the user reaches the bottom.
- No category entry point — a recipient who wants rugs or outdoor furniture has to scroll and squint.
- "picked just for you" promises personalization but the four items below it look like the same template tiles as everything else.
- Subject leads with red-circle emoji 🔴 on both ends — borderline spammy stacking for a brand that doesn't need it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero deal for the final-day send.** "70% off [specific product] — ends midnight" beats a grid of 12 tiles every time on a deadline email. Right now the 70% OFF tile is just one square among many.
- 2. **Drop one of the red emoji** in the subject. Single 🔴 + "ENDS TONIGHT" is sharper than the bookend treatment.
- 3. **Add a real countdown** ("Ends 11:59 PM ET tonight") instead of leaving "ENDING" vague.
- 4. **Trim the tile grid by half.** Three discount bands max — 70% / 50% / 40% — each anchored to one category and one image.
- 5. **Make "picked just for you" actually feel picked** — name the category ("for your outdoor refresh") or pull in a recently-browsed item.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off ends at midnight 🔴`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `48 hours left on clearance — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours: 70% off select clearance, ends 11:59 PM.`
