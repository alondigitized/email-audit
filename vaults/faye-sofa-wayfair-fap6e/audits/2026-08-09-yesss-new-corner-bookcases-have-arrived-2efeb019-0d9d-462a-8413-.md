# Yesss! New CORNER BOOKCASES have arrived →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "New CORNER BOOKCASES," but the inbox opens on the same recycled "72-HR CLEAROUT — UP TO 60% OFF" purple banner that's been anchoring nearly every Wayfair send this run (echoed in 08-08's "72 HOURS ONLY" clearout and the 08-04/08-03 "48-HOUR CLEARANCE" sends). The bookcase content shows up only as the first tile under "score styles you ❤" — a real photo with real copy, "Corner Bookcases You'll Love" — so the subject isn't a total lie, but it's not what greets you either.
- The bigger problem is execution: this render is riddled with broken images. At least six product tiles — Shoe Storage Benches, Cocktail Glasses, Canopies & Gazebos, Metal Patio Benches, Slipcovers, Blackout Curtains — are blue broken-image icons on blank white boxes, and "Must-Have Short Bookcases" is missing its photo entirely. That's roughly a third of the grid failing to render.
- This is a clear step down from the 08-08 and 08-07 sends, which at least held together visually even when the subject/hero mismatch was the main complaint. Here the mismatch persists AND the build is broken. Judgment: skip.

## What's working

- The first product tile does deliver on the subject — "Corner Bookcases You'll Love" with a real, on-theme lifestyle photo.
- Section headers ("picked just for you," "your future (faves) are here") give the email some structure and personality.
- The "pace your space" financing module and the rewards/financing/professional/app footer icons render cleanly with no bugs.
- Clear single CTA ("SHOP NOW") on the hero band, easy to spot.

## What's weak

- At least six product tiles show broken-image placeholders instead of photos (Shoe Storage Benches, Cocktail Glasses, Canopies & Gazebos, Metal Patio Benches, Slipcovers, Blackout Curtains) — a visible render failure, not a subjective miss.
- The "Must-Have Short Bookcases" tile — directly relevant to the subject line — has no image at all, just text on white.
- Hero banner is the same generic "72-HR CLEAROUT" purple block reused across multiple recent sends, with zero bookcase visual despite the subject promising exactly that.
- Grid sprawls into dog beds, gazebos, curtains, cocktail glasses, dressers — the email abandons the bookcase premise almost immediately and becomes an undifferentiated catalog dump.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken images before anything else** — six-plus dead tiles is a deliverability/trust problem, not a copy problem; no subject line rewrite fixes a broken render.
- 2. **Make the hero match the subject** — swap the recycled clearout banner for an actual corner-bookcase hero shot; if clearance is the real driver, lead with clearance in the subject instead of promising a new-arrival category you don't show.
- 3. **Cut the grid down to bookcases + 2-3 adjacent categories** (storage, shelving) instead of sprawling into gazebos, cocktail glasses, and dog beds — a "new arrivals" subject sets an expectation of focus.

# Technical Email Audit — Wayfair "New CORNER BOOKCASES have arrived"

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard MailChimp/ESP-style transactional-marketing template with heavy click-tracking redirect wrapping; core structural issues are missing unsubscribe/authentication headers in the captured relay data and widespread missing `alt` attributes on tracking pixels and content images.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 12 probed material links route through `_emr`/`_eml`/`refid=MKTEML_121344` click-tracking redirects (e.g., `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=2efeb019-...&refid=MKTEML_121344&emlid=101&maiid=15925`). Consistent tracking params across links; no malformed URLs observed.
- 7 of 12 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) during automated probing, including the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Wayfair" (logo), "Corner Bookcases You'll Love," and "Top-Rated Bookcases for Less." These are flagged as inconclusive, not confirmed broken — real click activity from an ESP domain frequently triggers destination-side rate limiting and is not itself evidence of a dead link.
- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (70s), including `/wayfaircard` and category filter links for dog beds, bookcases, and pergolas — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- 20 open/impression-tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) all served over `http://` rather than `https://` — see Rendering & Accessibility below.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 20 open-tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=...`) are served over plain HTTP. Many mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) block or proxy-rewrite non-HTTPS image requests, which can silently suppress open tracking for a portion of the list.
- 24 images missing `alt` text, including content-bearing images, not just spacers: `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `WFCC2@2x.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_3_purple.png`, `0728_OutdoorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, `WFCC 2 Disclosure.png`, plus the `b.php` open-tracking pixel and all 20 `imp` pixels. This affects screen-reader users and clients with images disabled (alt text is the only fallback content shown).
- Template uses standard Outlook/mobile responsive conditional CSS (`u-FullWidth`, `u-MobileHide`, MSO stacking fixes) — no structural rendering issues detected in the CSS itself.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- No issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected in the captured message — one-click unsubscribe from the mail client's UI (Gmail/Outlook "Unsubscribe" button) may not be available. QA notes this may be a relay-side capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a confirmed absence from Wayfair's actual send — flag as unverified rather than a confirmed CAN-SPAM violation.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click POST unsubscribe) also not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) not present in the captured headers — authentication status cannot be verified from this data; recommend checking headers at the receiving MTA directly rather than via the relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in probing scope), consistent with CAN-SPAM's minimum requirement of a working opt-out link in the body.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid`, `maiid`, `emlid`, `sltid`, `csnid`, `mdlid`) rather than standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`). This is internal/consistent, not a defect, but means standard UTM-based analytics tooling won't parse these — worth noting if cross-checking against a non-Wayfair-native analytics stack.
- Content-to-destination alignment looks correct: "Corner Bookcases You'll Love" → `/daily-sales/filters/corner-bookcases-you'll-love-se68226.html`, "Top-Rated Bookcases for Less" → `/daily-sales/filters/top-rated-bookcases-for-less-se44961.html` — subject line ("New CORNER BOOKCASES") matches CTA/landing destinations.
- App-install CTA ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App") correctly routes through a OneLink deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with `af_web_dp` fallback to `wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` — appropriate deferred-deep-linking pattern.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Migrate the 20 `imp` open-tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to avoid client-side blocking of open tracking.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images (hero banners, event/SKU top images, disclosure image) for accessibility and images-disabled fallback; tracking pixels can use `alt=""` (decorative) rather than nothing.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers from the sending MTA (not via the AgentMail relay capture) to confirm whether these are true gaps or a capture-layer limitation before treating as a compliance finding.
4. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) and 4 unprobed links with a longer time budget / backoff to get a definitive pass/fail on primary CTAs before signing off on link health.
