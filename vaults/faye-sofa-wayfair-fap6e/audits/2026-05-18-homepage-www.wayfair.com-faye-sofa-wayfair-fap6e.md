# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Memorial Day Clearance with up to 70% off — Wayfair knows exactly why I have this app on my phone. The homepage is screaming sale event right now, which is honestly the only reason I'd open Wayfair on a random Sunday anyway. That said, the red-and-white gingham vibe is very "backyard BBQ" and not very "I'm furnishing my first living room with a Joybird-adjacent sofa," so the aesthetic doesn't land as a lifestyle match even if the deal does.

## What's working

- **The 70% off number in giant red type** — as someone who tolerates flat-pack assembly to save 30%, seeing *70%* stops my thumb immediately
- **"SHOP NOW" button placement** — it's right under the offer, purple, impossible to miss, no hunting required
- **The top sticky banner calling out "MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA"** — flagging a specific brand (Delta) gives me a reason to click through, feels like a real curated event not just a generic promo
- **Clearance section heading below the hero** — "up to 70% OFF: Memorial Day Clearance →" with product tiles starting to load means I can see product immediately without extra taps, which is how I actually shop

## What's weak

- **Gingham/Americana hero pattern** — this reads "Fourth of July party supplies," not "modern-mid-century living room refresh." Nothing about this visual says *furniture* to me
- **Bunk bed as the lead visible product tile** — I'm not furnishing a kids' room right now; that's a jarring first product to see
- **No returning-shopper signals at all** — I've bought from Wayfair before, but I see zero "welcome back," no wishlist count, no rewards balance. The cart icon is empty. Missed warm-up opportunity
- **No urgency signal** — "Memorial Day Clearance" implies a deadline but there's no countdown, no "ends Monday," no "2 days left." For someone who waits for the right moment to pull the trigger, I need that push
- **Free shipping threshold not visible** — I always calculate whether I hit free shipping; it's nowhere on screen

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham background for a lifestyle room shot** — show a real living room with a sectional or accent chair in a warm, modern-mid-century space. Keep the "70% OFF" overlay but earn it with imagery that says *furniture, actually*
- **Add a "Sale ends Monday" countdown timer under the hero CTA** — a credible deadline is the single biggest conversion lever for a shopper like me who keeps things in my cart for days
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the sticky banner** — replace the blank space after the offer copy with "Free shipping over $35" or whatever the actual threshold is; that's the quiet purchase enabler for AOV math
- **Pull in a mid-century / living room category tile above the bunk bed** — reorder the clearance rail so sofas, accent chairs, or coffee tables appear first for the most common adult shopper intent
