# Are these the SLIPCOVERS you wanted?
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- The hero at the very top of this email is a broken image render — a large blank white block with just a tiny blue placeholder icon where the lead visual should be. That's the worst possible way to open an email whose subject asks "Are these the SLIPCOVERS you wanted?" — there's no slipcover hero to answer the question, just dead space before the recipient scrolls to a category grid.
- Once past the broken hero, slipcovers do show up — "Best-Selling Slipcovers" and "Must-Have Stretch Fit Slipcovers" are both present in the "score styles you ❤" and second grid sections — but they're two tiles among sixteen, sandwiched between privacy screens, pergolas, hot tubs, birdbaths, and wall clocks. This is the un-targeted grab-bag format Wayfair leans on constantly (see 2026-08-14 "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS," 2026-08-13 "OUTDOOR FOUNTAIN GOALS"), and here it's compounded by an actual render bug, not just a mismatch.
- Judgement: broken hero image plus a subject line that barely earns its keyword-stuffing makes this one of the weaker sends in this run — worse than the 8/10 recliner or Halloween emails because there's a genuine visual defect, not just thematic drift.

## What's working

- Slipcovers are genuinely present in the body (two separate tiles: "Best-Selling Slipcovers" and "Must-Have Stretch Fit Slipcovers"), so the subject isn't a total bait-and-switch.
- The purple "score styles you ❤" and "savings too good to pass up" section banners give the scroll clear visual breakpoints.
- Grid layout is clean and consistent, with legible captions under every product tile.
- Footer trust badges (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) and app-download CTA are present and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The hero image at the top is broken/blank — a large empty white rectangle with only a tiny blue icon, no product shot, no headline, no CTA.
- Because the hero is broken, there is zero visual payoff for the subject line above the fold — a reader has to scroll past dead space to find any slipcover content.
- The email reverts to Wayfair's generic everything-grid pattern (pergolas, hot tubs, birdbaths, wall clocks, barn doors) that dilutes the subject's promise, same pattern flagged in the 08-14 and 08-13 fountain/privacy-screen sends.
- A second blank block with the same broken placeholder icon appears again near the bottom of the email, suggesting this isn't a one-off glitch but a recurring template asset failure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken hero image asset first — this is a rendering defect, not a targeting issue, and it's costing the email its entire above-the-fold impact. Nothing else matters until this renders.
- 2. Once fixed, make the hero an actual slipcover product shot so the subject's question gets an immediate visual answer.
- 3. Move the two slipcover tiles to the top of the grid, directly under the (fixed) hero, instead of burying "Must-Have Stretch Fit Slipcovers" deep in the second section.
- 4. Trim the grid — 16 unrelated tiles is overwhelming; a slipcover-focused email should feature slipcovers plus 3-4 adjacent living-room categories (throw pillows, furniture protectors), not gazebos and hot tubs.
- 5. Fix the second broken placeholder block near the footer — same asset issue recurring.
