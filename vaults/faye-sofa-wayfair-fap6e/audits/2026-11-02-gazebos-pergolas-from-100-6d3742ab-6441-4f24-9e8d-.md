# Gazebos & pergolas from $100
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises gazebos and pergolas from $100, and the render doesn't deliver them. The hero slot has the headline "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" and a purple "Shop Now" button. The image beside it is a blank box with a tiny broken-image icon. The "ENDS TONIGHT" Black Friday Pop-Up banner at the top is the only strong visual.
- The rest is the usual Wayfair catch-all grid: kitchen mats, nightstands, bedding, benches, curtains, wall art and dressers. Two more slots are broken. The "Top Brands: Kitchen Sinks" tile is empty, and a huge blank block of about 250px sits between the grid and the footer. - Verdict: a decent subject with a concrete price anchor, undermined by an empty hero and a generic body. The "from $100" price point is the best part of the send, and the images fail to back it up.

## What's working

- The subject is concrete and short. It names a category and a price floor.
- The top banner ("up to 70% OFF, ENDS TONIGHT") gives credible urgency and a Black Friday frame.
- The "Shop Now" button is clearly visible and sits directly under the headline.
- The Rewards "MEMBER DEALS" block adds a loyalty angle, with "Plus 5% in rewards".
- The product tiles that load are clean and consistently styled.

## What's weak

- The hero image is a blank box with a broken-image icon, so no gazebo or pergola is visible.
- No pergola or gazebo product appears anywhere else. The body is kitchen mats, bedding, sinks and dressers, so the subject promise is lost.
- The "Top Brands: Kitchen Sinks" tile is broken, and a large blank white block sits above the footer.
- The "up to 70% OFF" banner competes with the "from $100" headline. Two different offers are on screen at once.
- The grid is long and undifferentiated, with no prices on most tiles and no single product to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the image delivery for the hero and the dynamic slots. A gazebo or pergola hero photo with a price tag would lift this email more than any copy change.
- 2. Make the body match the subject. Put 4 to 6 pergola and gazebo products with prices directly under the hero, ahead of the generic grid.
- 3. Pick one offer. Either lead with "from $100" or with the 70%-off banner, not both at the same weight.
- 4. Cut the grid by half, add price points to the tiles, and remove empty dynamic modules instead of letting them render blank.
- 5. Use the rewards block as a secondary offer, ideally tied to outdoor products rather than kitchen staples.

- **4 links were not checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out:

  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-nightstands-se44429.h…`

  - `/daily-sales/filters/quilt-sets-you%27ll-love-se56866.htm`

6. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the source or in a direct inbox. Don't rely on the relay copy.
7. Re-probe the 4 unchecked links and the 429 links with throttling or a longer budget before judging link health. The 57% pass rate is driven mainly by 429s and unprobed links, not confirmed breakage.
