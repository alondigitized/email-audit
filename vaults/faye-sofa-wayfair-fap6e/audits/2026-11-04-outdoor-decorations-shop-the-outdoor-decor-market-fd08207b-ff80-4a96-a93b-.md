# OUTDOOR DECORATIONS >>> SHOP THE OUTDOOR DECOR MARKET
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, fully rendered Wayfair send, and a clear improvement on the 11/01 and 11/02 emails that had blank hero boxes. The hero is a giant nutcracker in front of a lit house, with "the outdoor decor market" over it and a purple "Be the merriest house on the block" bar. All product images load, and the subject matches the content. The 11/02 tile and gazebo sends did not.
- The weak spot is the offer. The only discount is "70% OFF Holiday Flash Sale," and it sits near the bottom as a small strip. The top of the email has no price or deadline. The only prices are in the pill links ("wreaths & swag from $25", "inflatables from $30"). The lower half drifts into pergolas, area rugs and kitchen mats, which are the same catch-all filler seen on 11/01 and 11/02. It reads as a good themed email stapled to a generic one.
- Judgement: a good creative that is pleasant to scan and has no real reason to click today. It's about a 6, level with the 11/03 Holiday Flash Sale send.

## What's working

- The hero is seasonal and instantly readable: the nutcracker and the lit house say outdoor holiday decor at once.
- The 2x3 product grid renders fully, with star ratings under each tile.
- Four pill CTAs give specific category and price entry points ("wreaths & swag from $25", "welcome mats from $25").
- The subject matches the content, which fixes the mismatch problem from the 11/02 tile send and the 11/01 pergola send.
- The "holiday catalog" block adds a second browsing path with its own arrow button.

## What's weak

- The "70% OFF" Flash Sale banner sits near the bottom, and a reader who doesn't scroll never sees the discount.
- The hero has no price, percentage or deadline. "Be the merriest house on the block" is charming but gives no reason to act now.
- The "more styles to scoop up" section brings back pergolas, area rugs and kitchen mats, which dilutes the outdoor decor focus and repeats the 11/01 and 11/02 creative.
- Product tiles have no names or prices, so I'm clicking blind. The star ratings are the only proof and they are tiny.
- The "holiday catalog" preview image is a tiny, illegible page spread, so the block gives no real preview.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 70% off Flash Sale strip into the hero or directly under it, and add an end date. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put a price and product name under each of the six grid tiles.
- 3. Cut the pergola, rug and mat block, or send it only to people who have shown interest in those categories. Replace it with more holiday outdoor items such as lights, inflatables and wreaths.
- 4. Make the catalog preview legible, or replace it with a single hero product.
- 5. Use a lower-case, plainer subject. The current one has ">>>" and all caps, which looks shouty.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code-level problems are 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels, missing `alt` on 30 images (6 of them are linked star-rating images), and no `lang` attribute on `<html>`. The QA run couldn't confirm unsubscribe or authentication headers, and the HTML source is truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited links (not confirmed broken):** 9 of the 12 probed links returned 429, including:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale")
  - `https://wayfair.com/curated/the-outdoor-decor-market~ev632074.html?...`
  - the 4 PDP links
  - the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app link
  - the Wayfair logo link

  This is bot throttling, so link health is unverified rather than failed.
- **Coverage gap:** 46 links were skipped and 3 were unprobed after the 70s budget, e.g. `/pdp/the-holiday-aisle-48-pre-lit-wreath-w110020384.html` and `/curated/wreaths-%26-garlands-from-%2425~ev632076.html`. The percent-encoding in the last one looks correct.
- **App deep link:** the `onelink.me` URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the web fallback starts on plain HTTP and depends on a redirect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is served over HTTPS but has no `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** 20 images at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1` use `http://`. They look like ad-impression pixels, and clients that block insecure content will drop them.
- **Missing `alt`:**
  - The `imp` pixels, `b.php`, and the hero/banner images: `WFNA_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Holiday_Ph3_Minibahn_Sen…`, `wfna_winter2026_gtm_email_holiday_ph3_minibahn_dig…`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_1_purple.png` and `WFUS_1105_HolidayFlashSale-Skinny-General.png`.
  - `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (×4) and `stars_11.png`, all on `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`. These sit inside PDP links, so the link text becomes the filename (`[image: stars_10.png]`), and the rating is not available to screen readers.
  - The pixels can take `alt=""`. The hero and star images need real `alt` text.
- **Missing `lang`:** the visible root tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang` attribute.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens appear in the source or the probed URLs.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unverified headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and neither was `Authentication-Results`. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so these are unconfirmed rather than proven missing. Check them against the raw message headers.
- **Not assessable:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking scheme:** there are no `utm_*` parameters. Every link instead carries Wayfair's own set: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=10`, `csnid=e15faabd-…`, `refid=MKTEML_143045`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16697`, `mdlid=…`, `sltid=<slot>` and `wfcs=cs7`. It is consistent across all links.
- **GA attribution:** non-UTM tagging only attributes in third-party analytics if it is mapped.
- **Recipient identifier:** `_emr=fd08207b-ff80-4a96-a93b-5a4dcc673c55` is identical on every link. A forwarded email would pass that recipient ID to whoever clicks.
- **Landing alignment:** the subject's "Outdoor Decor Market" matches the `/curated/the-outdoor-decor-market~ev632074.html` link.
- **Host inconsistency:** that curated link uses the bare `wayfair.com` host, while the others use `www.wayfair.com`. A redirect is likely, but it wasn't verified because of the 429.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, or confirm they are intentionally best-effort.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels, and descriptive `alt` text to the star ratings (e.g. "4.5 out of 5 stars") and the hero images.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Re-probe the 429 and unprobed links with throttling, or follow up manually.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message headers rather than the relay copy.
6. Change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
7. Confirm that the `graymatter_batch` parameters are mapped into the analytics reports.
