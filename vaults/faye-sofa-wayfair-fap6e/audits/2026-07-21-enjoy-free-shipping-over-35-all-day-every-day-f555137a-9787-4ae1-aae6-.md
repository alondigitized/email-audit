# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth time Wayfair has sent "Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." as a subject line in four days — July 18 (twice), July 19, and now July 21 again. Familiarity has curdled into invisibility. Open it and the body doesn't mention free shipping once; instead you get four competing sections: a "New Price Cuts" hero, an Open Box Deals module, a cryptic "Scores in Your Style" teaser, and a self-aware red-tag block that literally says "SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES — Now that we got your attention…" That last line is the tell: Wayfair knows this send is noise and is winking about it rather than fixing it.
- No specific discounts are visible — no percentages, no dollar figures, no "up to X% off." "New Price Cuts" and "Going Fast" are placeholders that carry zero weight without numbers. The benefits strip (Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional, Exclusive Brands) is fine infrastructure copy, but placing it mid-email as a loyalty reminder assumes the reader made it this far, which at this cadence they haven't.
- Bottom line: recycled subject, subject-body mismatch, four competing CTAs, and no concrete offer. This is the worst execution in a July batch that already has several weak entries.

## What's working

- **Open Box Deals module is the strongest unit.** Dark navy background, clear headline, specific product rationale ("returned, like-new"), and a clean "Shop Now" CTA — this section alone could carry an email.
- **Wayfair brand marks render cleanly.** No broken images, no layout breaks, sub-brand logos (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss & Main, Perigold) render correctly in the footer.
- **"Going Fast" urgency is placed near the hero.** Scarcity language is at least above the fold before the clutter accumulates below.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is on its fifth outing in four days.** A standing perk is not a campaign — repeating it verbatim this many times trains subscribers to ignore it.
- **The body has nothing to do with free shipping.** The subject promises a benefit story; the body is a multi-section promo dump. A subscriber who opened expecting a free-shipping angle will feel misled.
- **"SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES — Now that we got your attention…" is self-defeating.** Acknowledging you're sending noise is not clever; it confirms the subscriber's instinct to scroll past.
- **No concrete discount numbers anywhere.** "New Price Cuts" means nothing without a percentage or price comparison. The entire email asks for trust it hasn't earned.
- **Four competing sections fragment the eye.** New Price Cuts → Open Box → Scores in Your Style → Generic Sales → App Download. There's no hero; there's just scroll fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this subject line permanently.** Free shipping over $35 is a standing program — it belongs in a footer note or a welcome series, not repeated as the lead subject five days running. Any category-specific subject outperforms this (see July 20's "NIGHTSTANDS ⚫ JULY 23–27: BLACK FRIDAY IN JULY ⚫" as proof).
- 2. **Make Open Box Deals the single hero.** It's the only section with a real premise (returned, like-new, limited inventory). Build the whole email around it — one subject, one hero image, one CTA, one urgency mechanism.
- 3. **Add a number.** "Up to 60% off" or "starting at $29" — any specific figure outperforms "New Price Cuts" every time.
- 4. **Delete the "SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES" block entirely.** Self-deprecating meta-copy is a failure mode, not a personality.
- 5. **Move the app download to a footer strip.** It's a retention play, not a conversion play — burying it saves above-fold real estate for the actual offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open Box Deals: Like-new finds, Wayfair prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New price cuts just dropped — going fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Returned and restocked. Shop before they're claimed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `These won't restock. Open box + price cuts inside.`
