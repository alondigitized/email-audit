# INTERIOR DOORS ⌛ UP TO 70% OFF won’t last long ⌛ 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises interior doors at up to 70% off, and the render barely delivers on it. Only the top-left tile in the "picked just for you" grid looks like a door: a white paneled interior door marked "Sale," with a Was/Now price. The next tile is a metal grid or screen that may be a door or room divider. After that the email drifts into a pool table, a coffee table, a laundry shelf, a trash can, area rugs, curtains, bedding, Christmas trees, mattresses, fireplaces and mirrors. This is the same Wayfair catch-all pattern as the 11-15 "END TABLES" and 11-14 "TABLE LAMPS" sends. The subject names one category and the body is a general Early Black Friday blast.
- The offer reads clearly: the "70% OFF" Early Black Friday banner appears twice, and there are category tiles at 70%, 50%, 40% and 55% off, a 0% APR Affirm strip, and free delivery over $35. The hierarchy is fine. The weakness is that the hero is a generic sale banner, not a door, so the subject's promise is not the first thing you see. The email is also very long, and the "limited-time" hourglass urgency is not backed by any visible deadline. The only date shown is the Affirm financing window ending 11/18.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand Wayfair send with a mismatched subject. It lands at the same mid-5 level as the other catch-all sends this week. It is stronger than "Popular Picks," but weaker than the 11-16 "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" send, which delivered its subject in the hero.

## What's working

- The "Early Black Friday up to 70% off" banner is bold and appears at both the top and the middle of the email, so the offer is easy to find.
- The category tiles ("70% OFF area rugs," "50% OFF curtains & drapes") use big numerals and orange buttons, so they scan fast.
- The Affirm 0% APR strip, dated through 11/18/2026, adds a financing angle for big-ticket items.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" removes a common objection.
- The Was/Now pricing with star ratings under the product tiles gives social proof and shows the discount.

## What's weak

- Interior doors, the only thing the subject names, appear in at most one or two tiles. The subject over-promises.
- The hero is a generic sale banner with no door imagery, so the subject's promise has no visual payoff at the top.
- The grid mixes pool tables, trash cans, Christmas trees, pergolas and a chicken-coop-style enclosure, so there is no single clear shopping intent.
- The "won't last long" hourglass urgency has no visible deadline. The only date shown is the Affirm financing window.
- The email is very long, with two repeated "70% off" banners and a "Your Home, These Picks" section that repeats pergolas and rugs seen earlier. Product tiles also lack names, so shoppers can't tell what they're looking at.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Put a full-width interior doors banner with a price anchor ("Interior doors from $X") first, and follow it with a door-only grid of 4 to 6 products, such as barn doors, French doors and pocket doors.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11/17 at midnight," so the urgency in the subject is credible.
- 3. Label every product tile with a name and price tier. Several tiles have only a price and stars.
- 4. Cut the repeated banner and the pergola/rug tiles that reappear below the fold. Shorten the email by about a third.
- 5. Segment by browse behavior. Send the doors version to people who viewed doors or home-improvement items and the general Black Friday version to everyone else.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Interior doors up to 70% off — ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Barn, French & pocket doors from $X`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early Black Friday pricing on doors, plus free delivery over $35.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Door prices drop through Monday. Finance with 0% APR through 11/18.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based and has responsive CSS, but it carries 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, about 28 images with no alt text, and no `lang` on `<html>`. The automated checks also couldn't confirm any authentication or unsubscribe headers. Of the 12 links probed, none returned a hard failure: 7 were rate-limited (429), and 4 more were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The seven 429 responses (`/daily-sales`, the accent-pillows event, the homepage, two product pages, and `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`) are rate limiting from the probe, not dead links.
- **Unprobed links are not failures.** Four product links were not checked because of the 70s time budget. The URLs shown for them are truncated in the QA output, which is probably a display limit, so treat them as unverified.
- **Mixed HTTP/HTTPS.**
  - The top banner links to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, not https.
  - The app link's fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Both depend on a server redirect to https.
- **Redundant app links.** The same OneLink "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" link appears at least twice, differing only in `_eml`.
- **Stale creative reference.** One image is `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-...` (May 2026 naming), in an email sent 2026-11-16. This is a filename only. I can't confirm from the truncated HTML whether it renders, so treat it as a possible leftover module to check.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 28 images.**
  - **Content images:** the BFCM skinny-top banner (`WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP.jpg`), the Affirm financing banner, `Skinny-VP.jpg`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`, the `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...` image, and the Memorial Day banner.
  - **Star ratings:** 12 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` images, which are meaningful ratings. Without alt text, a screen reader can't announce the rating.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels. These should have `alt=""`.
- **The banner links have no accessible name.** The QA link-text output shows `[image: WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP]` and `[image: stars_10.png]` as the link text, which confirms the linked images have no alt text.
- **Insecure image sources.** All 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` pixels are non-HTTPS. These may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients.
- **Missing `lang`.** The opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" style="...">` with no `lang` attribute. The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check `<title>`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or preheader markup.
- **Positives.**
  - The email has a viewport-based `@media (max-width:480px)` block.
  - `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hacks handle Gmail quirks.
  - `mso-font-alt` is set on the `@font-face`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` appear in the visible source or QA output. `_emr` (`1234e516-777b-43a0-aa66-8faed295caa0`) is constant across links, and `_eml` differs per link, as expected for per-link tracking.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message headers in the receiving inbox.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (also relay-related), so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `editor@members.wayfair.com` is unknown.
- **Footer not visible.** The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the physical postal address or the in-body unsubscribe link. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result for it is listed.
- **Recipient ID over plain HTTP.** The `p=` parameter in each `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...&p=1234e516-...` pixel is the same recipient ID as `_emr`. Anything on the network path can read it, since it is sent unencrypted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_143522`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16750`, `wfcs=cs7`. This is consistent across links, but it won't be picked up by GA-style UTM reporting without mapping.
- **Subject vs. landing pages.** The subject is "INTERIOR DOORS … UP TO 70% OFF". Among the probed links, the CTAs go to the generic `/daily-sales` and an accent-pillows event (`e410010`). The one interior-door link is a product page (`Primed-Pantry-Door-Slab…`). I couldn't verify a door-category landing page because of the 429s and the truncated HTML.
- **Product links appear well formed.** They carry `sku` / `mplid` params, such as `sku=EMEW1011`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the content banners and the star-rating images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`). Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp` and `t.wayfair.com` pixels over HTTPS, and use https for the `/daily-sales` and `/the-wayfair-app` links.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Confirm `<title>` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables in the full HTML.
4. Check the raw `.eml` headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, since the relay may have stripped them.
5. Check whether the `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay…` banner is a stale module.
6. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling, and confirm the unsubscribe link and footer address in the full HTML.
7. Map Wayfair's `refid` / `csnid` / `source` params to the UTM or analytics scheme you use for email-to-site attribution.
