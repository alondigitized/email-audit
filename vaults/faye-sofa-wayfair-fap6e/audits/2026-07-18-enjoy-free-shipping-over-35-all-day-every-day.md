# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- This email is a chandelier category send wrapped in the same 72-HR CLEAROUT banner that Wayfair fired on July 17 — but the subject line has nothing to do with either chandeliers or a clearout. "Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." reads like a transactional notification or a footer benefit, not a promotional hook. The most compelling offer in the email — up to 65% off in a time-limited window — is invisible at the inbox stage. A subscriber who got yesterday's alarm-bell clearout email ("⏱️ ❗ 72 HOURS ONLY ❗ ⏱️") and sees today's "free shipping everday" subject has no reason to think the clock is still ticking.
- Inside, the email is clean and well-executed. The chandelier hero photo is attractive, the "Shop Now" CTA is prominent, and a customer review (Katherine, NC) adds real social proof. The Wayfair Rewards icon row and Affirm 0% APR callout signal considered value layering. But there are three competing messages — free shipping (subject), clearout urgency (banner), chandeliers (hero) — and none of them reinforce the others. The eye doesn't know where to land.
- The email earns its keep on creative execution and offer depth but squanders open rate by burying the strongest signals. The subject is this email's single biggest liability.

## What's working

- **Chandelier hero is visually strong** — high-quality room shot, clear product in context, aspirational without being cold.
- **72-HR CLEAROUT banner with "UP TO 65% OFF" is bold and immediately legible** at the top of the body.
- **Customer review (Katherine, NC) is specific and credible** — "easy to install and looks fantastic" is the kind of real-world detail that moves purchase intent.
- **Benefit icon row (Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional)** surfaces multiple reasons to trust the brand without cluttering the hero section.
- **App QR code + phone mockup** at the bottom is a clean, modern placement for a secondary CTA that doesn't compete with the main "Shop Now."

## What's weak

- **Subject-body mismatch is severe** — "FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." is a baseline benefit reminder, not a promotional hook, and it shares zero language with the 72-HR CLEAROUT offer or chandeliers inside. Most recipients won't open.
- **The clearout banner recycles yesterday's creative verbatim** — subscribers who saw the July 17 "⏱️ ❗ 72 HOURS ONLY ❗ ⏱️" email are now seeing the same countdown inside a different-subject email on July 18, which erodes urgency rather than amplifying it.
- **Three competing messages** (free shipping → clearout urgency → chandeliers) break the visual hierarchy; there's no single narrative thread.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — chandeliers are a considered, higher-AOV purchase; "Shop Chandeliers" or "See Sale Chandeliers" would have been more specific and clickable.
- **Affirm fine print is very long** — the disclaimer block at the bottom dwarfs the rest of the footer and visually signals complexity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the clearout + category** — the 65% off, 72-hour window, and chandelier focus are all inside the email; the subject should surface at least two of three. The current subject is the most damaging single element.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Chandeliers: up to 65% off — 72 hours left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `72-HR SALE: Chandeliers from $X`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping over $35 on every order. Offer ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-rated styles, clearout prices — shop before the clock runs out.`
- 2. **Merge the clearout urgency with the chandelier category in the hero headline** — "Let's Talk: Chandeliers" is soft for a clearance send. "Clearout: Chandeliers Up to 65% Off" does both jobs.
- 3. **Change the CTA from "Shop Now" to "Shop Sale Chandeliers"** — specificity converts better on a considered purchase category.
- 4. **Don't resend the same 72-HR CLEAROUT banner two days running under an unrelated subject** — if July 18 is a category-specific send within the clearout window, call that out explicitly ("Day 2 of our clearout: Chandeliers"). Identical creative under a different subject line reads as a broken campaign.
- 5. **Trim the Affirm disclaimer** — link out to the full terms rather than printing a 5-line wall of text that visually collapses the footer.
