- Another Wayfair cold open lands on a blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon before anything useful renders — the same failure mode as the "Our best gazebo deals" and "Yesss! ## What's weak

- 2. Commit to the rug premise: build the "if you were an area rug" quiz payoff into actual content (e.g., 3-4 rug styles as "quiz results") instead of abandoning it after one hero block.
- 3. Cut the unrelated categories (kitchen appliances, outdoor pergolas/gazebos, recliners) from this send — they read as filler and undercut the personality-quiz hook.
