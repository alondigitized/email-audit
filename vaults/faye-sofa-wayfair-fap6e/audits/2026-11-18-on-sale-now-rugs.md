# On sale now: rugs
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the best subject-to-hero match Wayfair has sent this week. "On sale now: rugs" gets a large, attractive rug photo as the first image, captioned "On sale now: rugs »". The persian-style rug under a tufted sofa looks good. After that the email drifts into its usual catch-all format. Pergolas appear three times (the hero, the gazebo and "Pergolas for less"), and kitchen mats, a generic "Popular Picks" tile and a 16-tile grid of unrelated categories follow. A second rug tile, the bath rugs and mats tile, shows up far down the grid.
- The deeper problem is that nothing here has an offer. "On sale now" has no percentage, no dollar figure and no deadline. The only number in the email is "Kitchen Mats Under $30" and "Gazebos & pergolas from $100". The "Early Black Friday up to 70% off" banner sits near the bottom, below all the content, where few people will scroll to. It is a generic catalogue send dressed up as a rug email.
- Wayfair has sent this "must-shop steals" template repeatedly, as in the 11-15 "Popular Picks" send and the 11-16 and 11-17 sends. Cadence fatigue is real. On rugs, this is a modest improvement. As a promotion, it is the same weak one.

## What's working

- The subject and the hero agree, and the first image is a rug.
- The hero photo is large, aspirational and clean, and it draws the eye first.
- Wayfair is a recognizable sender, and the logo header is clear.
- The Black Friday banner is on-season, and it names Sealy and "up to 70% off".
- The footer has useful trust items (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) and an app download QR code.

## What's weak

- "On sale now" gives no discount, price or deadline, so the offer is vague.
- Pergolas and gazebos take three of the first six hero slots, which dilutes the rug message.
- The strongest offer, "up to 70% off Early Black Friday", is buried near the bottom.
- The captions are tiny, low-contrast text under the images, and there are no visible buttons.
- The 16-tile "we think you'll love these" grid is scattered, with no clear primary action.
- There is no visible social proof, such as reviews or ratings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer on the rug hero, for example "Area rugs up to 60% off, ends Sunday", with a visible button, "Shop rugs".
- 2. Make the first screen rugs only: a rug hero plus a 2x2 of rug sub-types (runner, 5x8, washable, outdoor). Remove the pergolas from the top.
- 3. Move the Early Black Friday "up to 70% off" banner to directly under the header.
- 4. Add ratings, prices and "free shipping" cues to the tiles.
- 5. Cut the grid to 6-8 tiles and sort them by rug-adjacent categories (bath rugs, mats, runners).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs up to 60% off, today through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Area rugs from $49 | early Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `5x8s, runners and washable styles, all marked down. Free shipping on most.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing starts now. Find your rug before sizes sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup includes responsive media queries and a Gmail-specific fix. Its main technical issues are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt text on linked images, and compliance headers that the QA run could not verify. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the QA data and visible markup show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 probe failures are HTTP 429 (rate limiting), not 404 or 5xx. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale") and `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App"). Treat these as unverified, not broken.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were skipped when the 70s time budget ran out, so they are neither pass nor fail. They include `/daily-sales/filters/gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8`, `/kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm` and `/popular-picks-se49538.html?eid=35623`. The QA output shows two of these URLs cut off, for example `...se8` and `...?eid=`. This is probably a truncation in the report, so check it against the raw HTML.
- **Plain-HTTP fallback in the app deep link:** the OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL and rely on a redirect to reach HTTPS.
- **Tracking pixels:** there are 20 impression pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019` (`sz=2x1`). There is also an open pixel at `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`. Twenty pixel requests per open add weight and extra points of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA data does not show whether the unsubscribe link was probed or whether it passed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png` is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales?...sltid=4`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` is wrapped in a link to `/?...sltid=5`.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_2_purple.png` and `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP.jpg` also have no alt text.
  - The QA link-text extractor reported the first two as `[image: blankBanner...]` and `[image: wfus_batchheader...]`. Those links have no accessible name, and with images blocked they render as blank.
- **Tracking pixels without alt text:** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels have no alt attribute. Adding `alt=""` is the correct fix for decorative pixels.
- **Mixed content:** the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels may be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS, which would silently drop impression tracking.
- **Responsive CSS:** the `<style>` block has `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and a `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` Gmail fix. No rendering problems are visible in the portion provided.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible URLs have resolved values: `_emr=0547edbf-...` is the same on every link and `_eml` is unique per link. No unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FirstName]` appear in the provided source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the original message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The sender domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe body link:** the truncated source and QA data don't confirm either one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121290`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16758`, `mdlid` and `pid=Email` on the app link. This is consistent across links and works with its own analytics. Any third-party analytics that expects UTM tags would not attribute this traffic.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment:** the subject "On sale now: rugs" matches the link `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-rugs-se81989.html?eid=602737`, and `unifiedeventid=602737` matches `eid`. Alignment is good.
- **Parameter length:** URLs are long (about 400 characters or more), but the QA data shows no problems from that.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked banner and header images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Switch the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, and the `af_web_dp` fallback, to HTTPS.
3. Check the original message source (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. Re-probe the 429 and unprobed links with throttling and a longer time budget. Confirm the unsubscribe link and physical address are present.
5. Consider consolidating the 20 impression pixels, or adding `utm_*` mappings if the downstream analytics need them.
