# TABLE LAMPS ⌛ UP TO 60% OFF ends June 3 ⌛ Be quick! 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- The subject line is the best thing Wayfair has written all week, and the body immediately betrays it. "TABLE LAMPS ⌛ UP TO 60% OFF ends June 3" is a genuinely useful promise — category-specific, price-specific, deadline-specific — and then the email delivers the identical WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT grid that has shipped every day since May 24: TV stands, bedroom furniture, kids furniture, mattresses, fitness gear. There is no dedicated lamps section, no lamps hero, no "Shop Lamps" CTA. It's the same five-category tile row with a fresh subject line pasted on top.
- The body itself isn't broken. Category deal tiles are scannable, star ratings in the product grid add passive social proof, and the layout is clean. But none of that saves you when the lamps shopper who opened specifically for lamps hits scroll and finds a bathtub and outdoor chairs.
- This is the second category-bait send from Wayfair today — May 30 already ran "On sale now: rugs" (scored 4/10 for the same reason) — and roughly the sixth clearout variant in eight days. The pattern is burning list credibility faster than any single good subject line can rebuild it.

## What's working

- Specific hard deadline in subject ("ends June 3") is credible and time-bounded — not the usual "limited time" filler.
- Category deal tiles (TV stands, mattresses, fitness) are cleanly laid out and quick to scan.
- Star ratings visible in the product grid add implicit social proof without adding visual clutter.
- Wayfair Rewards and flexible financing callouts in the footer reinforce repeat-purchase value.
- Clean render — no broken images, no layout breaks, no lorem-ipsum.

## What's weak

- Subject promises TABLE LAMPS; the body has zero dedicated lamp section or lamp tile — the same bait-and-switch as May 30 rugs and May 29 mattress foundations.
- Hero banner reads "WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" — same creative vehicle Wayfair has run all week — not the lamp offer the subject line sold.
- Five category tiles (TV stands, bedroom, kids furniture, mattresses, fitness) with no lamps entry confirms the disconnect is structural, not accidental.
- ALL CAPS subject + dual ⌛ emojis + exclamation tip into spam-adjacent formatting, especially after five consecutive similar sends.
- "Picked just for you" header sits above an obviously algorithmic multi-category grid; the personalization claim reads as hollow.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Follow through on the subject.** If TABLE LAMPS is the hook, open the body with a lamps-first section: 3–4 specific products, visible prices, the June 3 deadline, a "Shop Lamps" CTA — then flow into the broader clearout grid. The subject line is genuinely good; give the body a chance to earn it.
- 2. **Drop one emoji tier and the ALL CAPS.** "Table Lamps — Up to 60% off · Ends June 3" is just as urgent without tripping spam heuristics or reading as shouted.
- 3. **Replace "picked just for you" with deadline copy.** "These close June 3" or "Ends Tuesday: your clearout picks" are scannable, specific, and honest.
- 4. **Rotate the category tiles.** TV stands → bedroom → kids furniture → mattresses → fitness has appeared in every send this week. A lamps-first tile layout would actually differentiate this email and honor the subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lamp picks, up to 60% off through Tuesday`
