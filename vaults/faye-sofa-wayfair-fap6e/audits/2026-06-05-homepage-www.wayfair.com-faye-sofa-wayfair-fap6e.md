# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open Wayfair and immediately I'm staring at pool chairs and striped loungers — which, cute, but I came here to find a sofa for my living room? There's a live countdown for a 24-hour flash deal which genuinely made my thumb pause, and the "pillows from $15" badge is the kind of thing I'd screenshot to send my sister. But the whole above-the-fold is pushing the outdoor shop hard, and I'm a starter-home-interior person first — I'll get to a patio eventually, but my couch situation is urgent.

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** — "24 HOURS ONLY | FLASH DEALS | 11:51:26" is crack for someone who refreshes during Way Day. My dopamine spiked.
- **"PILLOWS FROM $15" badge** — a concrete dollar number on a product I might actually impulse-buy. That format (FROM $X in a circle) reads instantly.
- **"Wayfair Verified: outdoor furniture →"** rail below the hero — those checkmark badges signal curation and quality, which matters when I'm reading reviews end-to-end anyway.
- **"Peak summer living" vertical type** — playful, feels like a magazine, matches the lifestyle-browse mode I'm already in.
- **Search bar placement** — "Find anything home..." sits right at the top. When the hero doesn't match what I want, that search bar is my escape hatch.

## What's weak

- **No indoor furniture anywhere above the fold** — the entire hero is outdoor. If I'm here for a sectional or a credenza, nothing is speaking to me.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — Wayfair has a loyalty program but I'd never know it from this screen. A returning high-intent buyer like me should see my points balance or a member-exclusive teaser.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always want to know if I'm close to free shipping before I go deep into browsing. It's not here.
- **Hero imagery is too boho-tropical for my taste** — orange polka dots and yellow stripes are more Airbnb patio than modern-mid-century. It doesn't visually cue my aesthetic at all.
- **Three hero slides, zero indication of what's next** — two empty dots below the hero. I'd have to swipe to find out if there's something more relevant. That's friction I might not bother with.

## Recommendations

- **Swap hero slide 2 or 3 for an indoor living-room lifestyle shot** — a moody, warm-toned sofa vignette with a "shop living room" CTA would catch the massive segment of buyers who are furnishing interiors, not patios.
- **Add a rewards/loyalty strip directly below the search bar** — even a thin one-liner like "You have 240 Wayfair Rewards points → Shop now" would hook returning customers before the hero even loads.
- **Surface free-shipping info in the flash-deal banner** — swap the arrow on the right for something like "Free shipping on orders $35+" so price-sensitive browsers know the deal before they tap into a product.
- **Add star-rating count to the Wayfair Verified cards** — those product thumbnails are cut off at the bottom of the screenshot; putting "4.6 ★ (1,240)" under the image removes one review-reading step for people like me.
