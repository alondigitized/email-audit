# Open Box steals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest Wayfair render in the recent run. Every element loads, and the hero is a big, bold "open box deals" lockup with a Le Creuset-style Dutch oven sitting in the "o" of "box". It's a clever visual. After the blank-hero sends on 10/8 ("CEILING FANS — our top picks.") and 10/7 ("FLOORING, HARDWARE & MORE"), it's a relief that nothing is broken.
- The body is a stack of 12 identical purple bars, each with a category and an "up to X% off" figure. Nothing in it is a product. The only imagery is the single cookware shot, and the eye gets no second anchor. For a persona shopping sofas and living-room furniture, only bar stools, rugs and dining sets are close, and even those are just text rows. Without product photos, "open box" has nothing to prove: no condition, no price, no item. The hero arrow is also a small red circle, so the main click target isn't obvious.
- Verdict: a tidy, functional category directory with a memorable hero. It's low on desire and low on specificity. It will get a few category clicks but won't convert like a send that shows real products.

## What's working

- The hero typography is huge and legible, and the cookware in the "o" is a fun, on-brand touch.
- Everything renders. There are no blank boxes or broken-image icons, unlike the 10/7 and 10/8 sends.
- Every row pairs a category with a concrete "up to X%" figure, and the 70% tier on rugs, lighting and bar stools is a real hook.
- "SHOP ALL SALES" is a dark, full-width button that stands apart from the lighter rows.
- The footer is orderly: Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands, and an app QR code.

## What's weak

- No product imagery below the hero. Twelve text bars read like a menu and give nothing to react to.
- The hero says "open box deals" but never explains what open box means, such as a condition grade or a guarantee. It also shows no price.
- The only hero click target is a small red arrow. It's not labeled and it's easy to miss.
- Several rows share "up to 45% off", so the discounts blur together. Only a few categories (rugs, lighting, bar stools, dining sets) feel relevant to a furniture shopper.
- The Insider Sale "20% OFF" and the Rewards benefit are tiny. The app banner's phone mock-up is cropped and unreadable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the 12 bars with a 2x3 grid of real open-box products. Show the original price, the open-box price and a condition badge, such as "Like new, full warranty".
- 2. Turn the hero arrow into a labeled button, such as "Shop open box deals".
- 3. Add one honest line that defines open box, for example "Opened, inspected, priced to move."
- 4. Rank categories by the recipient's browsing, so a sofa shopper sees seating, rugs and tables first. Keep the 70% tiers up top.
- 5. Surface the Insider 20% offer as a real banner, not a screenshot thumbnail.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open box furniture, up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Like-new rugs, lighting & stools — marked down`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Inspected, ready to ship, and priced to go. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, lighting and bar stools at up to 70% off, while open-box stock lasts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a Gmail/mobile media-query block, and the 12 probed links showed no hard failures. Most of the 57% pass rate comes from about 20 tracking pixels that lack alt text and use `http://`, plus a few content images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** The eight 429 responses (`/daily-sales`, `/daily-sales/clearance`, the area rugs, wall art and shower curtain filter pages, the homepage, and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me` app link) are rate limiting from Wayfair's edge. They are not evidence of dead links.
- **Four links were never checked.** They are the `filters-masterClID~151`, `~41`, `~1191` and `~6260` clearance URLs, skipped when the 70s probe budget ran out. The QA output also shows them cut off mid-parameter, so they could not be verified.
- **Inconsistent campaign attribution on the app link.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` carries `c=Triggered` while the same email is tagged `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled as a triggered campaign can mis-attribute AppsFlyer installs.
- **Open pixel and impression beacons.**
  - There is one open pixel at `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=<base64>`, which decodes to `eventType=EmailOpen`.
  - There are 20 impression beacons, `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Their placement in the DOM is not visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Hero image has no alt text.** `wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif` is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales/clearance`. Its accessible name falls back to the filename (the QA link text shows `[image: wfus-openbox-2025-minibahn-02.gif]`), so screen reader users and image-blocked clients get no description of the main CTA.
- **Second image has no alt text.** `new_shop_all_sale_2024.jpg` has none either.
- **Tracking pixels have no alt text.** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` images lack `alt`. Use `alt=""` on them, because they are decorative.
- **Mixed content.** All 20 `imp` beacons use `http://li.wayfair.com`. Clients may block them or show insecure-content warnings, and the impression counts will be under-reported. Because the `imp` beacons are 2x1 and `b.php` is the `EmailOpen` event, this affects impression reporting. The open pixel itself is HTTPS.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`.
- The mobile CSS is in a `max-width: 480px` media query with a `u ~ div` Gmail fix. No rendering problems are visible in the retrievable source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. Per-recipient identifiers (`_emr`, per-link `_eml`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed failure**. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a defect.
- `Authentication-Results` was missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The cause is the relay, not the sender.
- The truncated source does not show the footer, so the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link could not be checked. The QA probe did not flag the unsubscribe link, which suggests it resolved.
- The sender `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain, consistent with a dedicated sending subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution uses proprietary parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_141511`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This works inside Wayfair's stack but won't show up in standard GA campaign reports.
- **Landing pages match the links.** Link text maps to `/daily-sales/clearance` filter pages, and each carries an `eid=` ID. The 429s prevented confirming the landing page content, so this was not verified.
- **Tracking is consistent.** `varid=2` appears on all links, and `sltid` increments by module position (1 header, 3 hero, 4 category tiles, 5 app).
- **Non-HTTPS fallback on the app link.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` points to `http://`, which depends on a redirect to HTTPS.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF and to `new_shop_all_sale_2024.jpg`. Add `alt=""` to `b.php` and the `imp` pixels.
2. Move `li.wayfair.com/imp` to HTTPS. This also applies to the `af_web_dp` fallback URL.
3. Set `<html lang="en">`.
4. Align the AppsFlyer campaign tag. Change `c=Triggered` to a batch-appropriate name, or fix the `source` value.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the raw MIME rather than via the relay.
6. Add `utm_*` parameters if GA reporting is expected.
7. For the QA harness, retry 429s with backoff and increase the probe budget past 70s so the four skipped clearance links get checked. The skipped links should also display their full URLs.
