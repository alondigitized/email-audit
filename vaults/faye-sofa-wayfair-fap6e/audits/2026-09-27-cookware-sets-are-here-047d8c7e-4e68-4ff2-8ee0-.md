- The hero "72-HR CLEAROUT — UP TO 60% OFF" banner is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.

## What's weak

- At least three broken/blank image placeholders are visible (birdbath tile, an unlabeled tile near the awnings section, and a large blank block near the bottom) — a real rendering failure, not a copy issue.
- Subject promises cookware specifically, but body buries it in a 16-tile grid spanning grills, coffee makers, birdbaths, chandeliers, cat trees, and garden statues — no dedicated cookware section or sizing/material detail.

- Visual hierarchy is muddled: after the hero banner, everything is same-size tiles with no clear second focal point.

- 3. **Add a concrete cookware offer** — "Up to 60% off" is sitewide boilerplate; pull a cookware-specific number (e.g., "$39 nonstick sets") to match subject specificity.
- 4. **Trim the grid** — 16 unrelated category tiles dilutes the cookware promise; cut to 6-8 tiles or split into a separate campaign for non-kitchen categories.
