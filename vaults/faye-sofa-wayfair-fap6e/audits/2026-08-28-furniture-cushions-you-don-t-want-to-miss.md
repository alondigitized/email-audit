# FURNITURE CUSHIONS you don’t want to miss
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the second "furniture cushions" send in the Wayfair run this week, and it's broken the exact same way as the 2026-08-25 version ("Bag one of these furniture cushions before they're all gone!" — 4.00/10): the subject promises cushions, and the body never shows a single cushion, doesn't even use the word. Instead the eye lands on a red-white-blue "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" banner, then a scattershot tour through window nooks, AC units, pergolas, recliners, sconces, bar stools, cat trees, mats, and sheds.
- Worse than the 08-25 send, this one has a hard render bug: roughly a third of the product tiles in the lower grid (wine glasses, best-selling bar stools, budget cat trees, welcome mats, slipcovers, accent chairs, rug pads, ice buckets, sheds) show only a blue placeholder icon where a product photo should be. That's not a stylistic miss, that's broken creative going out to subscribers.
- Given the subject makes a specific promise and the render can't even deliver working images for the grid it substitutes instead, this is a bottom-tier send — on par with or worse than 08-25's cushion failure.

## What's working

- The hero "inspo for your space" window-nook photo is a clean, real, in-focus lifestyle image with a 5-star rating shown.
- The AC unit section ("cool down... on any budget") has clear, well-lit product photography and two legible CTA links.
- The pergola/gazebo/recliner 2x2 grid at top uses real photos with visible pricing context ("on sale now," "top-rated").
- The purple "but wait, there's more..." and "your counter, completed." section headers create some visual pacing breaks in an otherwise long scroll.

## What's weak

- Zero cushions anywhere in the email — subject is entirely unpaid-off, repeating the exact failure from the 08-25 send.
- Multiple broken image tiles (blue square placeholder icons) in the lower grid — this is a visible, undeniable render bug, not a matter of interpretation.
- No coherent hero offer — the top banner says "70% OFF Labor Day Clearance" but everything below it is unrelated to a single sale event (AC units, sconces, bar stools, sheds).
- Email is extremely long with a disjointed category dump (wine glasses → bar stools → cat trees → mats → accent chairs → ice buckets → sheds) that reads like an unsegmented catalog blast, not a targeted campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before send — QA the render in a real inbox client; a third of the lower grid is non-functional and actively damages trust.
- 2. Either rename the subject to match the actual content (e.g., a Labor Day multi-category clearance) or build a real cushion-anchored hero — stop reusing "furniture cushions" copy on emails that don't feature cushions.
- 3. Cut the lower grid from 10+ disconnected categories down to 3-4 with real thematic coherence (e.g., "outdoor living: pergolas, sconces, recliners" only).
- 4. Lead with one unambiguous hero offer and CTA instead of three competing purple section banners.

# Technical Audit — Wayfair "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair batch-template email (graymatter_batch/batchid=15) with heavy tracking-pixel usage and template-generated markup; automated probing hit rate-limiting on most CTAs, and multiple images lack alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 of 58 links probed, 46 skipped** (deprioritized footer/utility/social) — coverage is partial by design, not a defect.
- **9 material links returned HTTP 429 (rate limited)** during probing, including primary CTAs ("Shop Sale," "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," hero image links, PDP link for the bench cushion). These are flagged as "may be valid" — server-side rate limiting on the prober, not confirmed broken links. Recommend re-probing at lower concurrency/slower rate before treating as pass/fail.
- **4 links unprobed** due to 70s time budget exhaustion (pergola/gazebo filter pages, homepage, one PDP) — status unknown, not failures.
- **20 first-party tracking-pixel requests to `li.wayfair.com/imp`** (sequential `s=124126000`–`124126019`) served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — see Rendering & Accessibility below.
- Link query strings carry a consistent, well-formed tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16067`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `csnid`) — no malformed or truncated tracking parameters observed in the sampled links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **21 images missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`, `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, review-stars asset `stars_9.png`, multiple product/category images) and 20 tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=...`). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or skip them; product/hero images should carry descriptive alt text at minimum.
- **20 tracking pixels loaded via `http://` (not `https://`)** — e.g. `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000&sz=2x1&m=false&p=f3b16466-...`. Mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients/webmail may prevent these from firing, undercounting opens; also inconsistent with the rest of the template's HTTPS assets (`assets.wfcdn.com`, `secure.img1-fg.wfcdn.com`).
- One image filename contains a literal `%` character un-encoded in the path segment (`0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`) — likely a typo in the source filename (`Outd%oor` instead of `Outdoor`) rather than encoding corruption, but worth flagging to the sender since it could 404 depending on CDN handling.
- Media-query CSS block is standard Unroll/BEE-style boilerplate (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidth`, `Text--fontSize*`, etc.) — no malformed rules or unclosed braces observed in the truncated source provided.
