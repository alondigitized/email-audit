## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair promotional email built on the Bee/Unbounce-style ESP template with heavy tracking pixel usage; core CTAs resolve but automated probing was rate-limited (429) on several links, and multiple images lack alt text with one tracking pixel served over plain HTTP.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 7 of 12 probed material links returned **429 (rate limited)** rather than a clean pass, including primary CTAs: "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," "Find the Perfect Bed," "Shop Sale," "Wayfair" (logo), and both banner images. These are flagged as "may be valid" but not confirmed reachable — evidence: `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...&refid=MKTEML_101362&emlid=101&maiid=16092`.
- 4 links were **unprobed due to time budget** (70s cap exceeded), including `outdoor-furniture-clearance~e371638.html` and `our-best-gazebo-deals--se82152.html` — pass/fail unknown, not confirmed working.
- 48 of 60 total links were deprioritized/skipped by design (footer/utility/social), consistent with probe methodology — no issue.
- 20 tracking beacons (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) load over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — see Rendering & Accessibility below for mixed-content risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 `<img>` tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`** (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`). Non-HTTPS image requests are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients on HTTPS-only policies, silently degrading open/impression tracking.
- **26 images missing `alt` text**, including content-bearing creative (`wfus_0331_twbdpfy_2024_email_banner-gif.gif`, `...sku-rec-callouts-01/02`, `...shop-all-picks-for-yo`, `...sales-you-almost-miss`) and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels, plus the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open-tracking pixel. Missing alt on creative images degrades the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
- Template uses XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with extensive `!important`-laden inline/media-query CSS typical of ESP-generated markup (Bee-style `.u-*` classes) — no structural rendering issues identified in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens found in the visible HTML/QA output. No issues found.
