## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's table layout, responsive CSS (`@media (max-width: 480px)`) and per-link tracking parameters look sound. The main defects are 20+ tracking pixels served over `http://`, missing `alt` text on linked banner images, and unverifiable compliance and authentication headers. The 57% QA pass rate is mostly an artifact of probe limits, not link failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited links aren't defects.** 8 links returned 429 (e.g. `wayfair.com/daily-sales`, `wayfair.com/?_emr=…`, `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`). The QA tool notes these "may be valid", so they are unverified rather than broken.
- **Coverage is thin.** Only 12 of 69 links were probed, and 4 were left unprobed when the 70s budget ran out. One of the unprobed links is the subject-line CTA, `/daily-sales/filters/5x8-shag-rugs-under-%2475-se81971.ht…`. The other three are the pergolas, kitchen mats and king bedding links. The QA output truncates these URLs, so I can't tell whether the source URLs are cut off or only the report display.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1`. Clients or proxies that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop them, which undercounts impressions. They also have no `alt` attribute.
- **Open pixel with no `alt`.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is the open-tracking pixel. It is HTTPS, but it should carry `alt=""`.
- **App deep link fallback is HTTP.** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. This adds an avoidable redirect hop.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png` links to `wayfair.com/`.

- **Legal copy in an image.** `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` has no `alt`, so any terms text it holds is invisible when images are blocked.
- **Rewards banner.** `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…` has no `alt`. The filename suggests a mobile-sized asset.
- The viewport and `u ~ div` Gmail fixes, the `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout)` iOS patch and the Outlook-app `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix are present. I found no rendering defects in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so I can't check the rest.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens were visible in the truncated source or the probed URLs. `_emr=8b93d97a-…` is consistent across links and matches the `p=` value in the impression pixels. `_eml` and `mdlid` are unique per link, as expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe headers could not be confirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool says the relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements would be violated.
- **Authentication is unverified.** `Authentication-Results` was missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer not reviewable.** The unsubscribe link, physical address and preference-centre link are not in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none is listed in the results, so I can't confirm it works.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters.** All links use Wayfair's proprietary scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=5`, `csnid=…`, `sltid=<slot>`, `refid=MKTEML_121686`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16803`. That works if their analytics consumes it. A third-party analytics tool expecting `utm_*` will see these visits as direct or unattributed.
- **Campaign label mismatch.** The OneLink app link carries `c=Triggered`, while the same email's links say `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send labelled "Triggered" will skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **Subject and CTA slugs align.** "5x8 shag rugs under $75" maps to `/daily-sales/filters/5x8-shag-rugs-under-%2475-se81971…`. I couldn't verify the destination because that link wasn't probed.
- **Recipient ID in every URL.** `_emr` appears in each link and in the pixels. This is a persistent recipient identifier in query strings that may be logged or forwarded.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp` over HTTPS and add `alt=""` to all pixel images.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to linked banners, product tiles and `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`. Put the disclaimer in live text.
3. Re-run the probe with throttling or retry-after handling, and prioritise the four unprobed links, especially the `5x8-shag-rugs` CTA.
4. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message before relay, then confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
5. Fix `af_web_dp` to `https://`, and reconcile `c=Triggered` with the batch send.
6. If attribution outside Wayfair's stack matters, map `source`, `batchid` and `refid` to `utm_*` values.
7. Make the QA tool's pass-rate scoring ignore 429s and unprobed links. Otherwise its 57% overstates how broken the email is.
