# Just in: PATIO TABLES.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair sends a multi-module digest that leads with a clearance offer and a bedroom/nature module, while the subject line promises patio tables. The subject-to-body disconnect is the single biggest failure here — the email never delivers on its own premise in the visible hero. The render is clean and on-brand, but the scatter-shot module stack (bedroom, rugs, bed & bath, sheet-size guide) dilutes any purchase intent the patio hook might have built.

## What's working

- **Clearance anchor is immediately visible.** The "75-OFF CLEARANCE" callout at the top is the strongest commercial hook in the email — it's hard to miss.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no overlapping text, consistent brand colors throughout.
- **Short, scannable modules.** Each section has a headline + one CTA, making the email quick to scroll.
- **Seasonal hook in subject.** "Just in: PATIO TABLES." is timely for a spring send and will resonate with anyone actively shopping outdoor furniture.

## What's weak

- **Subject-body mismatch is glaring.** The subject promises patio tables; the hero module delivers "Bring Nature to Your Bedroom." That's a broken promise in the first visible content block.
- **Four unrelated content pillars.** Bedroom, rugs, bed & bath, and a sheet-size guide are all competing in one send. None of them gets enough real estate to convert.
- **Generic CTA copy.** Every button says "Shop Now" — there's no destination specificity that tells you what clicking will actually show you.
- **No urgency on the clearance offer.** "75-OFF CLEARANCE" without an end date or inventory scarcity signal lets the reader defer.
- **Educational module ("Tips to Pick the Right Sheet Set Size") inside a promo email** feels out of place and slows the commercial momentum.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Honor the subject line.** If the subject says "PATIO TABLES," the hero image and first CTA should be patio tables — not bedroom decor. Send split sends if you need to serve both audiences.
- 2. **Cap modules at two per email.** Pick one hero category and one secondary. Four modules create decision paralysis and bury your best offer.
- 3. **Add a deadline to the clearance.** "Ends Sunday" or "While supplies last" would convert the banner from a discount signal into an urgency driver.
- 4. **Specifify CTA copy.** "Shop Patio Tables" and "See Clearance Deals" outperform "Shop Now" because they set expectations for the landing experience.
- 5. **Move or remove the sheet-size guide.** Educational content works in a dedicated nurture email; inside a clearance promo it kills click momentum.
