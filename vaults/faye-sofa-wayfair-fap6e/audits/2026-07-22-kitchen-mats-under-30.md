# KITCHEN MATS under $30 ✨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair does something right with the subject — "KITCHEN MATS under $30 ✨" is specific, price-anchored, and short. A shopper glancing at their inbox has an instant mental model: small purchase, functional item, browsable. That's exactly the promise that earns an open.
- The email doesn't keep it. The body leads into a "score styles you" grid that might have mats buried in it, but what the eye actually lands on — hard — is "Open Box Deals" featuring a white storage cabinet, then a giant **"store-it-all sheds"** module with a full-width outdoor shed photo and a purple Shop Now button. Kitchen mats are nowhere in the hero zone. This is the same bait-and-switch Wayfair ran on July 20 with "Inside: CORDLESS VACUUMS," which opened into a category salad. They have not fixed the pattern.
- The broader Black Friday in July campaign is live in the header, but the subject doesn't mention it — the opposite of what worked on July 20's NIGHTSTANDS email ("NIGHTSTANDS ⚫ JULY 23–27: BLACK FRIDAY IN JULY ⚫"), which tied a specific product to the named event and earned an 8. Here, the subject and the campaign are running in parallel universes.

## What's working

- Subject line is genuinely good: category-specific, price-capped, under 30 characters, zero ALL-CAPS spam feel.
- "★★★★★" social proof block is visible mid-email — helps credibility in an otherwise ad-heavy layout.
- No visible render bugs; layout is clean and images loaded.
- Black Friday in July banner is present and ties the email to a live, time-bounded campaign.

## What's weak

- **Bait-and-switch at hero level**: "store-it-all sheds" is the visual anchor of this email, not kitchen mats. The subject-body contract is broken immediately.
- Kitchen mat pricing ("under $30") is promised in the subject but no visible price callout appears in the body to confirm it.
- "Open Box Deals" section — a fine module — is wedged between the mat teaser and the sheds feature, creating three competing narratives in one scroll.
- The Black Friday in July dates are in the header but absent from the subject; the July 20 NIGHTSTANDS email proved that putting the event in the subject line works — this one abandons that lesson.
- "let's complete your space" grid at the bottom is generic catch-all inventory dumping, diluting whatever kitchen-mat focus the subject created.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the hero match the subject.** Show a grid of kitchen mat options with prices visibly under $30 — that's the entire implied promise. Sheds should not exist in this email.
- 2. **Surface the price proof in the body.** "Under $30" in the subject earns the click; a visible "Starting at $19.99" or a price-tag callout in the mat grid seals the purchase.
- 3. **Tie the subject to Black Friday in July.** The July 20 NIGHTSTANDS email showed how — merge the product specificity with the campaign name.
- 4. **Cut the "store-it-all sheds" module entirely** from this send, or move it to a dedicated email with a matched subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday in July: Kitchen mats under $30`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen mats under $30 — grab one before July 27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Anti-fatigue, washable, and runner styles — all under $30`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Price-drop rugs for every kitchen style, shipped free over $35`
