## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Wayfair, "📩 New glassware available just for you 📩"

### 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile media-query block. The confirmed code-level defects are missing `alt` attributes on content images, 20 plain-`http://` impression pixels, and test or stale asset names in production image URLs. The header warnings and the 429 results come from the audit environment, not from the email.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited links aren't broken.** Eight probed links returned 429, for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` ("Wayfair") and the Jax Glass Highball PDP (`...RXK10071.html`). That is Wayfair's bot throttling, so there is no evidence of a broken link.
- **Four links were never checked.** The 70s probe budget ran out before two `W009403826` product URLs and two `3Piece-LED-Plastic-Lighted-Trees-Set-L1004-K~DNYJ6864` URLs were tested. Their status is unknown.
- **The app deep link carries a campaign label that conflicts with the send type.** The OneLink URL (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`) has `c=Triggered`, but the same URL has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" will skew AppsFlyer attribution. The `af_web_dp` fallback is `http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, which is non-HTTPS and depends on a redirect.
- **The tracking pixels are plain HTTP.** Twenty `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=d8e0f234-…` images have no `alt` attribute. Clients that block or upgrade mixed content will drop these impressions.
- **The open pixel also has no `alt`.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Blbi…` is HTTPS, but it has no `alt` attribute.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images have no `alt`:**
  - The `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty…` banner.
  - `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`.
  - The `WFUS_Fall2026_GTM_Email_FHR_FallRefreshShop_Miniba…` banner.
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`.
  - `48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`.
- **Linked images fall back to filenames.** Two of the linked images (the Labor Day banner and `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`) have no `alt`. The prober therefore reported their link text as `[image: …]`, which is what a screen reader or image-blocked client would use.
- **Star ratings have no text equivalent.** Eight `stars_9/10/11.png` images (`secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`) have no `alt`, so the rating is lost when images are off. A value like "4.5 out of 5 stars" would fix this.
- **The apostrophe in a banner filename is unsafe.** `WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-…` contains a literal `'`, which can break unquoted or single-quoted attribute contexts in some pipelines.
- **The responsive CSS is in good shape.** The `<style>` block uses `max-width: 480px` queries and the `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` Gmail/iOS hacks. The truncated source shows no rendering defect.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the visible source. Each link carries a distinct `_eml` GUID, so recipient-level tracking resolved. The source is truncated, so the body and footer could not be checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sender `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is normal.
- **The footer was not reviewed.** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed and none appears among the failures. The physical address and unsubscribe link sit in the truncated footer, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid=…`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121671`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16441`, `mdlid`). Nothing is missing within that scheme, but third-party analytics expecting `utm_source/medium/campaign` will not attribute these clicks.
- **The glassware subject has no glassware landing page among the probed links.** The two probed banner links and "Shop Sale" go to `/daily-sales`, and the "Wayfair" logo link and `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` link to the homepage (`/`). Only the Jax Glass Highball product links go to a glassware PDP.
- **Production image URLs show test and stale assets.** These include `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` (a test asset), `LaborDay` (August, `0824`) and `Spring2026`. They suggest template assets were not refreshed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and rating images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS.
3. Correct `c=Triggered` to match the batch send, and use an HTTPS `af_web_dp`.
4. Replace the test and stale creative (`email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`, `LaborDay`, `Spring2026`) and remove the apostrophe from the banner filename.
5. Re-probe the four unchecked URLs with a longer budget or throttled requests.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the raw MIME of a direct inbox copy, not the relay copy.
7. Ignore the 429 warnings until the probe is retried with a different user agent or rate.
