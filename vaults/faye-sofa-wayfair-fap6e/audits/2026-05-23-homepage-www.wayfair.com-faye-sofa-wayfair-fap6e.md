# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing I see: big, bold Memorial Day Clearance with "up to 70% OFF" plastered front and center — and honestly, as someone who literally refreshes apps waiting for Way Day, this is speaking my language. The page is clean, the offer is loud, and the timing is perfect for a long weekend shopping binge. That said, the hero is more of a sale announcement than an invitation to actually browse furniture, and I'm already itching to see whether "up to 70%" means the sectional I've been eyeing or just clearance kitchen bowls.

## What's working

- **The "70% OFF" numeral** — it's huge, red-on-white, impossible to scroll past. As someone waiting for a sale moment, this is exactly the hook that stops me mid-coffee.
- **The top announcement strip** ("UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA →") doubles the urgency before I even hit the hero; two bites at the same apple.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — purple, centered, clear. No "Explore the Journey" nonsense. I know exactly what it wants me to do.
- **Search bar at the top** — "Find anything home…" placeholder is confident and broad. If the hero doesn't have what I want, one tap and I'm searching sectionals.
- **Carousel dots** — I can see there are five slides, so I know there's more to explore without committing to a full scroll.
- **Timing** — Memorial Day is *this weekend*. The urgency is real and credible, not manufactured.

## What's weak

- **No furniture in sight above the fold** — the first product thumbnails I can see are stacked colorful bowls. As a sofa-and-console shopper, that's not pulling me deeper.
- **Hero is a graphic, not a lifestyle photo** — gingham pattern is festive but it tells me nothing about what I could actually buy. Show me a living room, not a sale flyer.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment** — zero mention of rewards, past orders, or member perks. I've probably shopped here before; the page treats me like I just wandered in off the street.
- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — what am I shopping? Bowls? Sofas? Rugs? After clicking I might land anywhere.
- **No trust signals visible** — no free-shipping threshold, no return policy callout, no review counts. I'm being asked to commit before I know the ground rules.
- **No time deadline beyond the holiday** — "Memorial Day" implies a deadline but there's no "Ends Monday midnight" or countdown. The urgency is soft.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham sale-poster hero for a lifestyle room shot** with a clearance badge overlaid — show me a mid-century living room at 40% off and I will not close this tab.
- **Add a personalized strip below the hero** ("Pick up where you left off" or "Items in your Wishlist on sale") — for a returning shopper waiting for Way Day, that is a conversion slam dunk.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** in the announcement strip or just below the CTA — "Free shipping on orders $35+" is a friction-remover I need to see before I start adding to cart.
- **Segment the "SHOP NOW" carousel** — make each of the five slides a different category (Sofas, Bedroom, Rugs, etc.) with matching copy so I can tap into the category I actually came for.
