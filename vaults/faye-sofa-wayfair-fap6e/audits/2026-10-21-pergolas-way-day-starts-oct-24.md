# PERGOLAS 🟣 🟣 WAY DAY STARTS OCT. 24 🟣 🟣 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Wayfair send in recent history where the subject, the hero and the body all agree. The subject says "PERGOLAS," and the first two-thirds of the render is a wall of pergolas with Was/Now prices. That includes a "Way Day deals of the day" block, also all pergolas. Unlike the Oct. 21 "Christmas trees" email, with its blank hero and broken image, nothing here is broken.
- The email is still weak on offer and CTA. The only visible CTA is a tiny "SHOP EARLY" pill in the banner. The pergola tiles carry small price text. The "up to 80% off" and "Oct. 24–26" banner is the same Way Day creative as the Oct. 19 and Oct. 20 sends, so cadence fatigue is real. The long "but wait, there's more…" grid drifts into bar stools, rugs and filing cabinets, which dilutes the focus.
- Judgment: a coherent, on-subject send with real prices, but it has no strong CTA and it repeats the same Way Day push. It's better than the recent blank-hero sends, but only a mid-tier email.

## What's working

- The subject's PERGOLAS promise is delivered. About 12 pergola tiles fill the top 40% of the email.
- Strikethrough "Was / NOW" prices on each tile make the discounts concrete. Examples are $1,692.57 and $945.00.
- The purple Way Day banner with "up to 80% off" and Oct. 24–26 sits at the very top, and the dates are credible.
- Every image rendered, with no layout breaks. That is a clear improvement over the Oct. 21 and Oct. 19 sends.
- The "Exclusive Offer: $40 off your first order of $100+" credit-card block gives a second, concrete incentive.

## What's weak

- The only visible CTA is a tiny "SHOP EARLY" pill. There is no large button, and the tiles don't read as clickable.
- The email is about 5,000px tall and mixes in bar stools, rugs, filing cabinets and vanities after the pergolas. The pergola focus is diluted.
- The Way Day banner is the same one Wayfair has sent all week (Oct. 19 and 20). It reads as repetitive.
- The pergola grid has no product names, sizes or reviews. Price is the only detail.
- The credit-card offer and the Way Day offer compete, and the fine print is tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one large, clear CTA under the banner, such as "Shop pergolas up to 80% off."
- 2. Cut the pergola grid to the best 6 tiles. Add star ratings and a size or material line to each.
- 3. Move the "deals of the day" block up or remove it. It repeats the pergola grid.
- 4. Trim the "but wait" grid to 4 tiles, or tie it to the outdoor theme. Examples are patio sets and outdoor rugs.
- 5. Add a countdown such as "Starts in 3 days" for credible urgency, and vary the Way Day banner across sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pergolas up to 80% off, early access`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your patio upgrade: pergolas from $600`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Louvered, canopy and cedar styles. Way Day pricing starts Oct. 24.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop early before Way Day sells them out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair batch send (`source=batch`, `refid=MKTEML_142103`) with consistent first-party tracking, but link validity could not be confirmed (every probed link returned 429). The source also has 5+ content images with no alt text and 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be assessed because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified, not broken.** All 10 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limiting), including:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/curated/24-hour-deals~ev641886.html`
  - `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157`
  
  That means 0 links were confirmed 200. 60 links were skipped by design and 2 were unprobed after the 70s budget (`our-favorite-bar-stools-se73977.html`, `large-capacity-shoe-racks-se73165.ht…`). A re-probe with throttling is needed.
- **Insecure link:** the Wayfair Card image link uses `http://wayfair.com/wayfaircard?...`. It relies on a redirect to HTTPS. The OneLink web fallback is also `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the OneLink URL sets `c=Triggered`, but the send is `source=batch&batchid=3`. The app-install attribution will be bucketed as a triggered campaign.
- **Shared `_eml` token:** the "Pergolas for less" and "On Sale Now: Makeup Vanities" links both carry `_eml=2f17152a-c9e4-42ff-97f2-0a6be7dde0aa`. This is minor, but per-link click attribution may be coarser than the other links, which each have a unique `_eml`.
- **Duplicate destinations:** `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` is the target of at least 3 separate links ("Shop Sale", the GIF, and the header image), each with its own `_eml`. This is fine for tracking, but it is redundant.

  - `US-WD26-Preview-Email.gif` (links to `/daily-sales`)

- **20 impression pixels over plain `http://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=…&sz=2x1`). Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content will drop them, so impression counts will be under-reported. Some clients may also show mixed-content warnings.
