## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a consistent, per-recipient tracking scheme, and no unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible data. The main technical problems are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing alt text, and a malformed image URL. Several QA checks (headers, unsubscribe, 12 of 72 links) could not confirm anything either way. The HTML source is truncated, and the QA run probed only 12 of 72 links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 flagged links returned HTTP 429, which means Wayfair rate-limited the probe. They are unverified, not failed. These include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `.../daily-sales/72-hour-clearout?...`, the pickleball category link, the two `wayfairapp.onelink.me` links and the `Arhab Corner` product link.
- **Coverage is thin.** 60 links were skipped by design. 4 more were unprobed because the 70s time budget ran out, including the product links `...W113542819.html` (listed twice) and `...W011294897.html`.
- **Non-HTTPS impression pixels.** 20 tracking pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, each with `sz=2x1`. Clients that block or proxy mixed content can drop them, which undercounts impressions. They also lack alt text.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS but has no alt attribute.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not valid percent-encoding. Strict parsers or proxies (such as Gmail's image proxy) may fail to load it. I can't confirm from the QA data that it fails.
- **App deep-link fallback is HTTP.** The onelink URLs carry `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, a non-HTTPS web fallback.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **26 images have no alt text.** The QA run lists:
  - The hero `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`.
  - The SKU header `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg`.
  - Three pickleball images (`wfus_jt800_batch10_outdoor_pickleballequipment_*`).
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`.
  - The two Halloween banners.
  - The outdoor clearance hero.
  - The tracking pixels.
- **Star ratings are inaccessible.** `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear 8 times with no alt, so the rating value is not exposed to screen readers or image-blocked clients.
- **Image links have no accessible name.** When images are blocked or read by assistive tech, these links fall back to filenames. Examples are the pickleball link ("`[image: wfus_jt800_batch10_outdoor_pickleballequ]`"), the 72-hour clearout hero and the SKU header link.
- **Test and stale asset names in production.** `email-sku-headers_test-06.jpg` has "test" in its name, and `72HR_Spring2026_...` is used in an October send. This is an asset-hygiene concern only and doesn't break rendering.
- **No other rendering issues found.** The visible source has responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and a Gmail/Outlook fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. Every probed URL carries resolved IDs: a shared `_emr=f8826f76-...` and a unique `_eml` per link. No literal `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` tokens appear in the visible data. The truncated HTML means the footer and body copy are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation.
- **Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for it. The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link can't be checked because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any URL.** Wayfair uses a proprietary scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121675`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This is consistent, but it won't map to standard analytics without a custom mapping.
- **Campaign label mismatch.** The onelink app links set `c=Triggered` while `source=graymatter_batch` marks a batch send. That can misattribute app installs and opens as triggered traffic.
- **Landing pages don't match the subject in the visible links.** The subject is "BOOKCASES", but none of the links visible in the QA data point to a bookcase category. They go to pickleball/outdoor, daily-sales, 72-hour-clearout, the homepage, and the `Arhab Corner` product. I couldn't verify what that product is. The truncated HTML means bookcase links may exist elsewhere.
- **Redirect and landing behavior not verified.** The 429s blocked checks of the final URLs and parameter preservation.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or a retry and backoff, and probe the unsubscribe link explicitly.
2. Check the raw message to see whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` exist upstream of AgentMail. If so, mark these as relay artifacts in QA.
3. Move the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and descriptive alt text (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars") to the star images and content images.
4. Fix or re-host `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` with a valid URL-encoded name.
5. Confirm the `c=Triggered` versus batch labeling with the sender's attribution mapping, and change the `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.
6. Reduce the QA tool's probing noise by capturing the full HTML so footer, address and token checks can run.
