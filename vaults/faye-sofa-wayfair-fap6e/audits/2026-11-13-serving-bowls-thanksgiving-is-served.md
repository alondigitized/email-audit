## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules and per-module tracking parameters. The QA run found no hard failures. The warnings are an unverified unsubscribe header and authentication headers, 20 non-HTTPS impression pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and 9 of 12 probed links returning 429, so they are unconfirmed rather than broken.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation is inconclusive.** Of 56 links, 12 were probed and 44 skipped. Nine of the 12 returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/m/thanksgiving?...` and three `/pdp/...` product URLs. Wayfair is rate-limiting the prober. This is not evidence of broken links, but no destination was confirmed.
- **Four links were not probed** because of the 70s time budget:
  - `.../red-barrel-studio-odelia-fall-foliage-20-cotton-napk...`
  - three `https://wayfair.com/curated/...~ev635682 / ev635669 / ev635685` URLs
  
  The QA output cuts these URLs off, so I can't tell whether the source URLs are truncated too. The curated URLs use the apex domain `wayfair.com` instead of `www.wayfair.com`, which may add a redirect hop (unverified).
- **Impression pixels use plain HTTP.** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=71b585db-...` pixels are flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked". Clients that block or rewrite mixed content will drop them, so ad impressions will be undercounted. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS and fine.
- **Image-wrapped product links have no accessible name.** The three `/pdp/...` links (`arthur-court-designs-game-birds...`, `beachcrest-home-fierro-tripura...`, `godinger-silver-art-co-lattice-whiskey-decanter...`) use `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` as their anchor content. The probe reported them as "[image: stars_11.png]".

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes.** QA flagged these images, and the same pixel count and list apply to all of them:
  - Content images:
    - `WFUS_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Thanksgiving_Minibahn_S...`
    - `WFUS_Winter2026_GTM_Email_ReviewModule_Thanksgivi...`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_1_purple.png`
    - `whiteBanner.jpg`
    - the star-rating images `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png`, which carry the rating information
  - Tracking pixels: `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels.

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the content images convey nothing. The pixels should have `alt=""` so they are skipped explicitly. The link text `[image: WFUS_Winter2026_GTM_Email_Thanksgiving_M]` for the `/m/thanksgiving` hero link shows the same gap.
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` with no `lang`. The `<head>` content visible in the truncated source also shows no `<title>`.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off inside the `<style>` block, so the body markup (role attributes, preheader, `bgcolor` fallbacks, dark-mode rules) was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the URLs or text provided. `_emr=71b585db-...` is constant across links, and `_eml` varies per module, which looks intentional.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be checked. The sender domain is `members.wayfair.com`, and the From address is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **The body unsubscribe link and physical address were not verified.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is shown, and the footer is outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=10`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_143484`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16738`. This is consistent across links, but it won't feed standard UTM-based analytics without mapping.
- **Inconsistent app-link attribution.** Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links go to `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...&c=Triggered&...&source=graymatter_batch&batchid=3`. The AppsFlyer campaign is `c=Triggered`, but the other parameters describe a batch send, so campaign attribution is likely mislabeled.
- **Insecure web fallback.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` uses `http://`, which adds a redirect for users without the app.
- **Landing pages not verified.** The 429 responses prevented any check that the pages match the subject ("SERVING BOWLS >>> THANKSGIVING"). The hero link points to `/m/thanksgiving`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to the star images (for example, "4.5 out of 5 stars"). Add `alt=""` to the `b.php` and `imp` pixels. Add descriptive `alt` to the banner, header and review-module images.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Add `lang="en"` and a `<title>`.
4. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present on the original message. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` from raw headers.
5. Change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`. Correct `c=Triggered` to match the batch campaign.
6. Re-probe the 9 rate-limited links and 4 unprobed links with throttling or a longer budget. Check the apex-domain `wayfair.com/curated/...` redirects.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the body and footer.
