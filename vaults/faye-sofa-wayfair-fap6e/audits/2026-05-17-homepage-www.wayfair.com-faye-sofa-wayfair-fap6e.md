# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is giving me Way Day energy and I am not mad about it — 70% off for Memorial Day Clearance is exactly the kind of sale I set phone alerts for. Wayfair is clearly pushing one message right now: big discount event, shop now, don't wait. It's a blunt instrument, not a curated experience, but honestly? For me, it works.

## What's working

- **The 70% OFF number in the hero** — big, red, impossible to miss. For someone who waits for sales like this, it's an immediate attention-grab.
- **The banner strip** ("UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA →") reinforces the offer above the hero, so the deal registers twice before I even read the hero.
- **The mid-century desk in the product rail** — I see walnut tones, clean lines, a modern desk lamp. That's my living room. That thumbnail alone makes me want to tap.
- **Memorial Day timing** — this sale makes complete cultural sense right now. It doesn't feel stale or out of season.
- **Search bar is front and center** — as someone who browses with purpose ("mid century sofa under $600"), I appreciate that it's the first interactive element.

## What's weak

- **A ladder is the first product tile** — an aluminum step ladder is not why I'm on Wayfair. It kills the vibe of a furniture clearance event and wastes prime real estate.
- **No category navigation above the fold** — there's a hamburger menu but no quick-tap rails for Living Room, Bedroom, or Dining. I have to dig.
- **"SHOP NOW" is vague** — it takes me to clearance broadly. I'd rather see "Shop Living Room Deals" or "Shop Sofas" as a secondary CTA.
- **No free-shipping callout visible** — Wayfair's free shipping over $35 is a big trust signal for me and it's completely absent above the fold.
- **No personalization at all** — I'm a returning Wayfair user. No "Pick up where you left off," no recently viewed, no rewards balance. The homepage treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the ladder tile with a sofa or sectional** — furniture is Wayfair's identity and the first product I see should scream home, not hardware store.
- **Add a "Free shipping on orders over $35" strip** or badge beneath the search bar — it's a huge conversion signal for my AOV and it's invisible right now.
- **Split the hero CTA by room/category** — below "SHOP NOW" add 3 quick-tap pills: Living Room · Bedroom · Dining. One extra row, massive improvement in path-to-product.
- **Surface a returning-user hook** — even a simple "Welcome back, Faye — your wishlist has items on sale" banner would make me feel seen and push me straight into my saved items.
