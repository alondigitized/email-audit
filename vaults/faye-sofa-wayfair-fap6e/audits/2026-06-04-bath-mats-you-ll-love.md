# BATH MATS You'll Love!!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair is running "BATH MATS You'll Love!!" again. Per the brand history log, this exact subject line ran on June 2 and scored 4/10 — in that version, the body hero was a Buy Now Pay Later pitch with no bath mats in sight. This June 4 version swaps the hero to a "Talk of the Town" editorial favorites section, but the core misfire is identical: the subject promises bath mats, and the email opens with a mixed carousel of outdoor seating, bedroom dressers, and ambiguous "faves" that could drop any Tuesday of the year. At most, one flat textile product near the top of the Talk of the Town section might be a bath mat — but it's not called out and it's not the hero.
- The layout itself is clean. Consistent Wayfair purple-on-white palette, visible star ratings in the product grid, legible product cards with no visible render bugs. But visual polish can't fix a subject/body mismatch, and it certainly can't fix the cadence problem: resending the same subject line two days after it underperformed — with a body swap but no subject change — reads as pipeline misconfiguration, not intentional A/B testing.
- The "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" section header is genuinely good naming, the kind of friendly editorial voice that Wayfair should lean into more. But the products shown include what looks like a mattress — continuing the cross-category bleed that's been a recurring Wayfair problem across this audit week.

## What's working

- Wayfair's brand palette is consistent end-to-end — purple accents, clean white background, no visual noise
- Star ratings are visible in the product grid, providing passive social proof without a hard sell
- "Talk of the Town" editorial framing is warmer and less blast-y than a pure promo header
- "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" is a strong section headline that implies curation and trust
- "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" at the footer gives a clear secondary conversion path

## What's weak

- Subject promises bath mats; the hero delivers "Talk of the Town" with outdoor chairs and mixed furniture — the June 2 version had the same subject/body mismatch with a BNPL hero
- Reusing an underperforming subject line two days later without changing it is a cadence signal that trains subscribers to filter Wayfair out
- "Shop All Our Favorites" is among the weakest CTAs in retail — no category, no urgency, no specificity
- No visible price discount, percentage off, or anchor price anywhere in the email
- "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" promises deals but the products shown appear to include a mattress, not bath mats or bath category items

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the hero actually bath mats.** One full-width hero image of a specific bath mat, with product name, star count, and a from-price. The subject line earned a click — don't squander it on a generic favorites digest.
- 2. **Add a concrete price anchor in the hero block.** "From $12.99 · Free shipping on orders $35+" beats "Talk of the Town" for click intent on every segment.
- 3. **Replace "Shop All Our Favorites" with "Shop Bath Mats."** Takes 10 seconds to change; immediately aligns CTA with the subject promise.
- 4. **Don't resend an underperforming subject line without a subject change.** If the June 2 send underperformed, change the subject. If it performed, note that and resend intentionally with identical creative — not a body swap with the same subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bath mats from $12 — 4.5 stars and up`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The one bath mat your bathroom is missing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top-rated picks in every size, color, and material.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on orders $35+ — refresh your bathroom floor today.`
