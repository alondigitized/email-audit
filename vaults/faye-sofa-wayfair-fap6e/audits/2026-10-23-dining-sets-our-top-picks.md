# DINING SETS — our top picks.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "DINING SETS — our top picks," and the render barely delivers. The top "fresh-picked favorites" grid has one dark dining table with a bench at upper left. The rest is flatware, a glowing Halloween cauldron, an orange armchair and amber glasses. Below that the email drifts to pergolas, comforters, rugs and shoe racks. A shopper who opens for dining sets finds one on-theme tile, with no price and no named offer.
- The strongest assets are the purple Wayfair branding and the "Members-only Early Deals" banner (Way Day, up to 80% off). Both are generic, and neither is tied to dining. Mid-email there is a large "Wayfair Rewards: get 5% rewards… on every item, always" block. Below the "shop before they're gone" tiles is a large blank white box with one tiny broken-image icon. That is a visible render bug.
- Judgement: a mediocre, catalog-style send whose subject over-promises. It repeats the pergola-heavy pattern from the 10/22 "Best-selling pergolas" and 10/21 "PERGOLAS" sends, and the 10/20 comforter and 10/21 Christmas-tree emails also ended up as mixed grids. A subscriber would see this as a fourth near-identical Wayfair send in four days.

## What's working

- The "Members-only Early Deals" banner at the top gives a concrete "up to 80% off" and "earn 5% back" hook, with a Way Day tag for seasonal timing.
- Wayfair's purple branding is instantly recognizable, and the section headers ("fresh-picked favorites," "let's complete your space") keep the layout scannable.
- The star ratings under each top tile add some social proof.
- The Wayfair Rewards block states one clear benefit (5% back on every item) with a "Take a look →" link.

## What's weak

- The subject says dining sets, but only one of six hero tiles is a dining table. Flatware, a cauldron and an armchair dilute it, and the second half is pergolas.
- A large blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon sits near the bottom, about 12% of the email height.
- No tile shows a price, a discount or a CTA. Every tile is just a photo, which makes it hard to say what the offer is.
- The pergola section repeats the previous days' theme and has nothing to do with dining. "Comforters & Sets for Less" also reuses the 10/20 creative.
- The Rewards promo and the "Insider Sale" app banner compete with the product grid, so the email has no single primary CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the whole email about dining sets: a hero dining set at the top, then a 2x3 grid of sets with Was/Now prices, and no pergolas or cauldrons.
- 2. Add a single bold CTA button, such as "Shop dining sets," directly under the hero.
- 3. Fix or remove the blank, broken module.
- 4. Put one specific offer above the fold, such as "Dining sets up to 60% off" or "Up to 80% off for members," with a Way Day deadline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The HTML uses a table-based, XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a 480px mobile breakpoint. The main issues are 20 non-HTTPS `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons, widespread missing `alt` attributes, no link verification (every probed link returned 429), and no `utm_*` parameters. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and headers are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Link validation was inconclusive.** All 8 listed probes returned 429 (rate limited), including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/wayfair-product-L145-K~W112458011.html?...` (the Delija dining set) and `https://www.wayfair.com/wayfair-rewards?...`. A 429 is neither a pass nor a fail. 4 more links (the Jax Glass Highball and Pfaltzgraff flatware product URLs) were unprobed after the 70s budget, and 52 links were skipped.
- **Non-HTTPS impression beacons.** There are 20 sequential `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=ab693217-…` images. Some clients block or rewrite plain-HTTP images, so these beacons may not fire and impression counts may be undercounted.
- **Malformed asset URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` has an invalid percent-escape (`%oo`). It may fail to load or decode in strict clients.
- **Test-named assets in production.** `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` and `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…` appear in the send. This suggests test creative or paths were promoted unchanged. It is not a confirmed defect on its own.
- **App deep link `campaign` mismatch.** The OneLink URL (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`) has `c=Triggered`, but the other links carry `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" would skew AppsFlyer campaign attribution.
- **Mixed-protocol fallback.** The same OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`, an HTTP web fallback.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing `alt` on content images.** These include:
  - `WFUS_261024_WayDay-MEMBERSONLY_Email_SkinnyBanner-…`
  - `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_2_purple.gif`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
  - `wfus_rewardscontenttest_onsite_lhpbanner_mobile_24…`
  - `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg` (the disclaimer is image-only, so it is inaccessible with images off)
- **Linked images with no accessible name.** Two linked images have the filename as their only text, so screen readers and image-blocked views have no usable link label:
  - `WFUS_261024_WayDay-MEMBERSONLY_Email_Ski…` links to `/wayfair-rewards`.
  - `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` links to `/`.
- **Star ratings with no `alt`.** The `stars_9/10/11.png` images (about 15 instances) carry rating data with no text alternative. Their `<a>` wrappers (for example `[image: stars_9.png]` → the Delija product URL) have no accessible name.
- **Tracking images with no `alt`.** The `b.php` open pixel and the 20 `imp` beacons lack `alt`. They should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so assistive tech skips them.
- Responsive CSS (`@media (max-width: 480px)`, `u ~ div`, `.u-FullWidthBlock`) is present. The viewport meta, `lang` attribute and preheader were not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. Per-recipient IDs resolve (`_emr=ab693217-…`, unique `_eml` per link) and no unrendered tokens appear in the data provided. The source was truncated, so the full body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **Unconfirmed, not confirmed defects.** The QA output says the following headers were not found and attributes this to the AgentMail relay:
  - `List-Unsubscribe`
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)
  - `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)
- Verify these against the raw message at the sending source before treating them as failures.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the footer, which was truncated, so they were not verified. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was listed.
- The From address `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a consistent subdomain of the brand domain, with no mismatch evident.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Attribution relies on proprietary parameters (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121271`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). This is consistent across links, but GA-style campaign reporting would not populate without a mapping.
- **Header image does not match the subject.** The subject is "DINING SETS — our top picks", but the header image (`email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, `sltid=6`) links to the homepage: `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=…&sltid=6&mdlid=a1abfda6-…`. Product links in the same module (`mdlid=a1abfda6-…`) go to specific SKUs (`sku=FTDH2083`). The landing page for the hero click is likely broader than the email promise. The landing page itself was not fetched.
- **Stale-looking creative.** The asset `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` carries a July-dated outdoor clearance name in an October send. This could be a leftover module.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Serve all `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 sizing to the `b.php` and `imp` pixels.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the banner, header, rewards and disclaimer images, and to the star-rating images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`). Linked images need descriptive `alt` text so the links have names.
3. Fix or rename `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` so the percent-escape is valid. Remove `_test` assets from production templates.
4. Point the dining header image at a dining-sets category URL instead of `/`.
5. Align the OneLink `c=Triggered` value with the batch campaign, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
6. Add `utm_source/medium/campaign` parameters, or confirm that the `source`/`refid` parameters map into analytics.
7. Re-run link probing with throttling or retry-after handling to clear the 429s and cover the 4 unprobed and 52 skipped links.
8. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the original raw message headers, outside the relay.
