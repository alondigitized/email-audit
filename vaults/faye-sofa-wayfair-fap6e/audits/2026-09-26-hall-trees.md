- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (70s cap), including `top-rated-awnings-se28214.html` and `wayfair-rewards`. Not pass/fail — unverified.

- All observed links carry consistent tracking parameters (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) — internally consistent tagging scheme, no malformed params observed.
