# TRASH CANS & RECYCLING 📢 ❗ LAST CALL FOR FALL CLEARANCE 📢 ❗ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a standard Wayfair catch-all with a trash-can subject on top. The top banner reads "Fall Clearance: Ends Tonight," and the body is a 12-tile "picked just for you" grid. The grid is followed by category tiles for area rugs, bedding, kitchen furniture, mattresses, Christmas trees and curtains. Only one tile near the top is a silver trash can, with a Was/Now price of about $49.91. There is no recycling content anywhere, so the subject's first half is barely delivered.
- The urgency is real, since "Ends Tonight" matches "LAST CALL." The email also renders cleanly this time. Unlike the 10/31 sectionals send and the 10/30 bookcases send, there is no blank hero box with a broken-image icon. But the offers are scattered: up to 60% off rugs, 50% off bedding, 0% APR Affirm, free delivery over $35 and a gift card pitch. Nothing stands out as the one thing to click.
- Judgement: a competent, on-season clearance blast that doesn't deliver on its subject. It also keeps up Wayfair's near-daily cadence of one-category subjects over a generic body.

## What's working

- The "Fall Clearance: Ends Tonight" banner is a credible deadline, and it matches the "LAST CALL" subject.
- The category tiles carry big, readable percentages ("60% OFF area rugs," "50% OFF bedding") with arrow CTAs.
- Product tiles show Was/Now prices and star ratings, which gives real price and social-proof signals.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- Free delivery over $35 and the Affirm 0% APR option give practical reasons to buy.

## What's weak

- The subject promises trash cans and recycling, but only one trash can tile appears, with no label and no dedicated section.
- There is no single hero. The banner, product grid, six category tiles, Affirm strip, delivery strip and gift card strip all compete.
- The subject has two repeated 📢 ❗ emoji pairs and an all-caps lead, which reads as spammy. It runs about 60 characters, so mobile will truncate it.
- The lower "Your Home, These Picks" block (pergolas, rugs) repeats earlier items and adds length without adding value.
- The category tile CTAs are only nouns ("area rugs →"), and none of the offers is about the subject's category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject's category the hero. If the subject says trash cans, lead with a trash-can and recycling-bin block with a specific price or percentage, then follow with the general clearance content.
- 2. Collapse the stacked promo strips (Affirm, free delivery, gift card) into one slim footer band so the clearance offer stays the focus.
- 3. Cut the repeated lower product block, and drop the duplicate pergola and rug items.
- 4. Use verb-led CTA copy, such as "Shop rugs up to 60% off."
- 5. Drop the doubled emoji and keep the subject under 50 characters.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive media queries. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The findings are missing alt text on many images, about 20 tracking pixels served over `http://`, and compliance headers that could not be verified. The source was truncated, so the footer and the rest of the body weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** Of 12 material links probed, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate limited). That is a probe artifact, not a dead link. The affected links include:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...`
  - three product URLs: `YC10003`, `W112033310`, `AKDE1835`
  - the two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links
- **Not verified:** 4 links were unprobed (time budget hit at 70s) and 67 were skipped by design. These include `W112606519`, `W100650235`, `BOWI1005` and `daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e403624.html`. The unsubscribe link should have been probed, but no result was reported for it.
- **Non-HTTPS link:** the banner image link (`1029_FallClearance_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`) points to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`. That forces a redirect hop and exposes the per-recipient `_emr` and `_eml` tokens in cleartext on the first request. The `af_web_dp` fallback in the onelink URLs is also `http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` uses HTTPS. Twenty impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019&sz=2x1`) use plain HTTP, so clients may block or rewrite them and those impressions can go uncounted.
- **Per-link tokens:** each link has its own `_eml` value (for example `03513e71...` and `44ff0dcf...`), which is expected link-level tracking, not a defect.

- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels:** `b.php` and all 20 `imp` pixels. For 1×1 or 2×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix, but they have no `alt` attribute at all.
- **Stale asset filenames:** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner`, `0125_48hourclearance...` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024...` don't match this Oct 29 fall-clearance send. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether they render visibly or are hidden or fallback modules. If visible, they show off-season creative.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the available source or the subject line. The `_emr` and `_eml` values are resolved GUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are absent from the captured headers. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe aren't met.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=9`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_142519`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16647`, plus `skurectxid` and `event_rec_txid`. This works for first-party attribution, but GA-style UTM reporting wouldn't pick it up. This is an observation, not a defect.
- **Product links:** they carry `mplid` and `sku` (for example `sku=YC10003`), which gives consistent SKU-level tracking.
- **Landing page alignment:** it couldn't be checked, because the product and sale pages returned 429.
- **Mismatch with the subject:** the subject leads with "TRASH CANS & RECYCLING", but the only product link verified in the probe set that matches it is `NineStars-13.2-Gallon-...Trash-Can-L500-K~YC10003`. The other probed products are a patio door (`AKDE1835`) and `DOEL1061`. The primary CTAs go to the generic `/daily-sales` page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all content images: descriptive alt for the star ratings (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars") and the linked banners. Add `alt=""` to all 21 tracking pixels.
2. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` banner link to HTTPS.
3. Check that the Memorial Day, `0125_48hourclearance` and `2024_dailysales` assets are not shown in this send.
4. Re-capture the raw message headers directly (bypassing the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Re-probe with a slower rate or a longer time budget to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed product and sale links. Make sure the unsubscribe URL is actually tested.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, postal address, `lang` attribute and preheader.
