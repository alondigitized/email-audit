# OUTDOOR FOUNTAIN GOALS = DONE ✅
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a satisfying, specific payoff — "Outdoor Fountain Goals = Done" — and the hero delivers on it for exactly two product tiles before the email forgets what it's about. After that it's the same 25-tile grab-bag grid Wayfair has been running all week (privacy screens, trash cans, recliners, filing cabinets, hot tubs, gazebos), capped with the identical "EPIC FALL SALE 70% OFF" banner flagged in the 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-16 sends.
- There's also a visible render bug: the top of the email is a large blank white block with what looks like a stray loading/broken-image icon before the fountain photos even appear. That pushes the one piece of content that actually matches the subject line further down the scroll, which defeats the point of a tightly-themed subject.
- This is a subject line that over-promises and a body that under-delivers — the fountain hook gets you to open, then dumps you into the same undifferentiated catalog blast as every other recent Wayfair send. Judgement: mediocre, consistent with the 5-6/10 pattern this brand has been running.

## What's working

- The hero pairs two distinct fountain styles (stone tiered, blue ceramic) that directly match the subject's promise.
- "Wayfair Rewards," "Flexible Financing," and app-download CTAs are present for loyalty/engaged subscribers.
- Star ratings appear on several product tiles in the "your home, these picks" section, adding light social proof.
- Section banners ("the must-shop steals," "shop before they're gone") give the long grid at least nominal structure.

## What's weak

- A blank white gap with a stray loading/broken-image icon sits at the very top of the email before any content renders — a real visual bug on the first screen.
- Only 2 of roughly 30 product tiles relate to the subject's "fountain" promise; everything after the hero is unrelated catalog filler (trash cans, filing cabinets, hot tubs).
- No specific price or discount tied to the fountains themselves — the only hard number (70% OFF) is buried at the very bottom in a recycled generic banner.
- This is the same "EPIC FALL SALE — Up to 70% OFF" footer banner already flagged in the 2026-09-17, 2026-09-16, and 2026-09-15 sends — cadence fatigue is real at this point.
- No single clear primary CTA — the email is 25+ competing links with no visual hierarchy pointing to one action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank/loading-icon gap at the top — that's the first thing a recipient sees and it currently looks broken, not intentional.
- 2. Actually build the fountain email the subject promises: expand the fountain section to 6-8 SKUs with prices, and cut the unrelated grid categories (filing cabinets, trash cans, hot tubs don't belong here).
- 3. Move a real number into the hero — "Fountains from $89" or "Up to 40% off outdoor fountains" — instead of only using the generic 70% banner at the bottom.
- 4. Retire the "EPIC FALL SALE 70% OFF" footer banner for a few sends; it's now appeared in at least four consecutive emails and is starting to read as wallpaper, not urgency.
- 5. Give the email one unmistakable primary CTA button near the hero fountains, not 25 same-weight text links.
