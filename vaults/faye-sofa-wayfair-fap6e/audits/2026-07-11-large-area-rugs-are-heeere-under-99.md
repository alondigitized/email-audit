# LARGE AREA RUGS are heeere! under $99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair's "LARGE AREA RUGS are heeere! under $99" is a modest step up from the July 8 bath mats bait-and-switch (which opened on rugs anyway) and the July 9–10 repetitive "Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS" double-send. At least here, the subject promise — large rugs, under $99 — is basically honored: the hero image is a large, colorful rug in an outdoor patio setting and the product grid is rug-heavy. That's the floor.
- The ceiling is low, though. The hero copy abandons the "$99" hook entirely and reverts to the generic "The must-shop deals" line Wayfair has leaned on throughout this July batch. That's a missed opportunity: the subject's price anchor is the most concrete thing this email has going for it, and it doesn't land anywhere in the body. The copper decorative tray/bowl nestled in the product grid is an off-category distraction, echoing the bath-mats-pivoting-to-area-rugs problem from July 8.
- Bottom line: delivers on the subject, disappoints on the body. An adequate promotional send that does nothing to break Wayfair's July pattern of high-volume, low-ambition blasts.

## What's working

- **Category consistency** — Unlike the July 8 bath mats send, the email actually shows large area rugs. No bait-and-switch on the hero.
- **Price signal in the subject** — "under $99" is a concrete, scannable hook that stands out in an inbox of vague "BIG DEALS" lines.
- **Clean render** — No overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage visible. The email loads and reads without friction.
- **"We think you'll ♥ these" block** — Personalization section adds perceived relevance and breaks the pure catalog-grid monotony.
- **Hero image quality** — The large patio/outdoor rug photo is visually appealing and sets a lifestyle context.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy throws away the subject's best asset** — "The must-shop deals" says nothing. The $99 price anchor from the subject is completely absent in the body hero.
- **Off-category product in the grid** — A copper decorative tray/bowl appears in the product grid. This is not a large area rug, and its presence muddies the category promise.
- **No urgency anywhere in the body** — No deadline, no "ends tonight," no countdown. Given that Wayfair's July 9–10 "OUTDOOR RUGS" sends explicitly used urgency cues in prior emails, the omission here makes the offer feel stale.
- **App CTA competes for attention at the bottom** — "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" with its prominent purple buttons redirects conversion energy away from the rug purchase flow.
- **No price tags in hero** — The "$99" hook from the subject should be visible on at least one prominently placed product in the body, not left implicit via yellow price circles deep in the grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Carry "$99" into the hero copy** — Replace "The must-shop deals" with something like "Large rugs, all under $99" or "Summer refresh: rugs from $49." The subject sets up the best copy moment in the email; the body kills it.
- 2. **Pull one specific product to a full-width hero with price** — A single 8×10 rug at $89 or $79 with visible sizing is more clickable than a 9-item grid where nothing is highlighted.
- 3. **Add a soft deadline** — Even "Prices this low through Sunday" or "Limited summer pricing" would convert fence-sitters without the credibility issues of a vague "ends tonight."
- 4. **Remove the decorative tray from the grid** — It's not a rug; it breaks the category promise and signals editorial laziness.
- 5. **Defer the app CTA** — Move it to the footer, not a standalone section above it. Let the rug purchase be the conversion goal of this email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5 large rugs under $99 — grab yours before they're gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Under $99: the large area rugs worth buying this summer`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes up to 9×12. Free shipping. Prices this low don't last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Styles for every room — starting at $49, up to 9×12.`
