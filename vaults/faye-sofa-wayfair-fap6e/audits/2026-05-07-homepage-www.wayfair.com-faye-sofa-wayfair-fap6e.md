# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- I opened this and immediately felt that little jolt — Wayfair is in full clearout mode and wants me to know it in the first half-second. The whole above-the-fold experience is one message: there's a 72-hour sale and you need to move. For where I am in life — starter home, always hunting the next Way Day window — this landing hits different than a brand-story homepage would.

## What's working

- **72-HR CLEAROUT double-punch (banner strip + hero):** The strip at the top *and* the full hero both repeat the same message — it registers immediately even on a quick glance. That's the right move for someone who might be rapidly tab-checking between sites.
- **"SHOP NOW" button:** Dark purple, centered, clear label. No "Explore the Journey" copywriting nonsense. I know exactly what tapping it does.
- **Rattan/cane dresser in the product tiles:** That partially-visible piece at the bottom right is *exactly* my aesthetic — cane front, warm wood tone, mid-century silhouette. It tells me my category is in this sale without me having to search.
- **Hero color palette:** The lavender-purple feels fresh and editorial, not the aggressive red-sticker clearance look that makes a site feel like a discount bin.

## What's weak

- **Zero personalization:** No "welcome back," no recently-viewed row, no wishlist items flagged on sale. As a repeat shopper who's had items saved for months waiting for a clearout, this is a clear miss.
- **"Up to 60% off" hedge:** I've been burned enough times to know this means three items are 60% off and the rest are 18%. I'll still click, but it erodes trust right at the top.
- **No free shipping signal anywhere:** Furniture shipping costs can add $50–$150 to a cart. The absence of a "free shipping over $X" line above the fold is a real friction point for big-ticket items.
- **Silverware as featured product:** I came for furniture and soft furnishings. Leading the product grid with flatware feels like a category mismatch — if I'm in a 72-hour clearout mindset, I'm not thinking about forks.
- **No countdown timer:** "72-HR CLEAROUT" tells me there's urgency but not how much time is left. A real countdown — "47:23:04 remaining" — would activate my Way Day brain hard.

## Recommendations

- **Add free shipping threshold directly to the banner strip:** Replace "UP TO 60% OFF | 72-HR CLEAROUT →" with "72-HR CLEAROUT | Free shipping on orders $35+ →" — for furniture buyers, this single line removes the #1 friction point.
- **Drop a countdown timer below the hero CTA:** A small "47 hrs 12 min left" beneath the SHOP NOW button costs nothing to ship and would absolutely increase tap-through for urgency shoppers like me.
- **Reorder the product grid: furniture first, flatware last:** Put the rattan dresser and similar home-decor items in the lead tiles. Reserve flatware and kitchen for later in the scroll.
- **Surface one personalized hook above the fold for logged-in users:** Even "3 items from your wishlist are in the Clearout" in a thin strip would make me feel like Wayfair knows why I'm here.
