# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a cleanly rendered "Talk of the Team" favorites roundup, and its structure works better than the 10/6 "PATIO BENCHES." send, which was mostly blank boxes. Every image loads. The top four numbered picks are easy to scan, and the 60% and 50% off tiles give the eye something to land on. The purple "We Love These for You" block, with star ratings and "Recently Purchased" counts, adds real social proof.
- The subject doesn't match the body, though. "On sale now: pergolas" promises one product, but the pergola is only tile #1 of four. It sits among dining chairs, rugs and dressers, with dozens more tiles below. The pergola tile has no price, no discount and no reason to buy. The "On sale" claim rests on a small caption that reads "On sale now: pergolas »".
- Overall it's a competent, generic catalog email with a subject that overpromises. It earns an average score and nothing higher. It also lands as one more Wayfair send in a daily run (10/6 patio benches, 10/5 Five Days of Deals, 10/4 coffee tables).

## What's working

- Every image renders, with no broken-image gaps, unlike the 10/6 patio benches and 10/5 free-shipping sends.
- Pergolas lead the numbered "Talk of the Team" grid, so the subject topic is the first thing you see.
- The pink "up to 60% off" and "up to 50% off" tiles make the discounts concrete and easy to spot.
- Star ratings and "Recently Purchased" counts (for example 1356 on the amber glasses) give credible social proof.
- The "Shop All Our Favorites" pill button is a clear primary CTA, placed right after the first grid.

## What's weak

- The subject promises pergolas, but the body is a broad mix and the pergola is one small tile with no price or discount.
- No product tile shows a price, sale price or percent off. The only numbers are the "up to" category banners.
- Tile captions like "On sale now: Dining Chairs »" and "Dressers »" are interchangeable and say nothing about the product.
- The email has no urgency or deadline anywhere, so "On sale now" has nothing to back it up.
- It is long, with about 20 tiles and no single hero. The strongest offers (50% outdoor furniture clearance) are buried near the bottom.
- The loyalty and financing icons sit in the footer as bare labels, with no benefit or offer attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Use a full-width pergola hero with a visible price or percent off and a "Shop Pergolas" button. Move the outdoor furniture 50% off tile directly under it.
- 2. Put a price on the product tiles, such as "From $X" or "Up to 40% off", and show the deadline.
- 3. Replace the "Shop All Our Favorites" CTA with a verb-plus-category label such as "Shop Outdoor Sale".
- 4. Cut the tile count by half so the offers aren't diluted.
- 5. Add one line about Rewards or Financing next to the CTA, not only in the footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is sound, and the 57% QA pass rate comes mostly from warnings rather than hard failures. The main issues are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels with no alt text, about 15 content images with no alt text, several images that are the only content of a link, and unverified compliance headers. The link probe was also incomplete: 8 links returned 429 and 4 were never probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is incomplete.** 12 links were probed and 55 were skipped. Eight probed links returned 429 (e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`, `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…`). A 429 is rate limiting, so these are neither confirmed good nor confirmed broken.
- **The pergola link was not checked.** Four links were unprobed after the 70s budget. They include `…/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`, the link that matches the subject line, and `…/filters/just-for-you%3a-dining-chairs-se5586`. Two other unprobed URLs are cut off at `…&wf`.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback.** The OneLink URL sets `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. The web fallback is plain `http://`, so it depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Campaign labels conflict.** The same OneLink URL carries `c=Triggered` and `source=batch&batchid=3`. A triggered campaign label on a batch send will misattribute reporting in AppsFlyer.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=…` images. Each is flagged "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked", so impression tracking will under-report in clients that block mixed content or rewrite image URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images with no alt text:**
  - `wfus_0331_tott_2024_email_banner-01.jpg`
  - `…number-1.jpg` through `…number-4.jpg`
  - `…button-shop-all-our-favo…`
  - `…we-love-these-for-you-01…`
  - `…sales-we-tell-our-friend…`
- **Images that are the only content of a link.** The QA link-text output shows image filenames as the link text, for example `[image: wfus_0331_tott_2024_email_button-shop-al]` and `[image: wfus_0331_tott_2024_email_banner-01.jpg]`. Both point to `/daily-sales`. With images off or in a screen reader, these links have no usable label. The rating images `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` (6 instances) have no alt text, so the ratings are lost as well.
- **Tracking pixels with no alt.** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` images lack alt attributes. They should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **No document language.** The `<html>` tag shown has only `xmlns` and `style` attributes, with no `lang` or `xml:lang`.
- **Mobile CSS risks.** In the 480px media query, `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; overflow: hidden; display: block; height: 48px !important }` can split words mid-word. It also clips longer product names, because the height is fixed and overflow is hidden.
- **Rest of the CSS.** The `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` hacks, the `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for the Sofia `@font-face`, and the Outlook app fix are all in place.
- **Limit of the audit.** The HTML source is truncated, so the body markup was not inspected.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Per-recipient IDs resolved: `_emr=6ffcf7bc-…`, and a different `_eml` value on each link.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. The same applies to the missing `Authentication-Results` header, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The probe is documented as always including one, but no result is shown, so it is not verified.
- **Physical address.** It is not visible in the truncated source and could not be checked.
- **Sender.** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain, which is normal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme.** No `utm_*` parameters are present. Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_122465`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16480`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`. It is consistent across links. If the team relies on GA-style UTMs, nothing is passed.
- **Subject vs. landing pages.** Subject is "On sale now: pergolas". The links seen in the QA output (`Shop Sale`, the banner, the number images, `Wayfair`) all go to generic `/daily-sales` or the homepage. Only the unprobed `…on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` link is pergola-specific.
- **Possibly reused creative.** Asset names carry `0331_tott_2024`, and one link is "Find a gift for mom" → `…/find-a-gift-for-mom~e348522.html?eid=602182`. Both suggest reused creative. That event page returned 429, so I couldn't confirm it is live and relevant in October.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the linked banner, button and number images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and star images, and give the ratings real text such as `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`.
2. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp` over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Fix the `c=Triggered` vs `source=batch` mismatch in the OneLink parameters.
4. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name` with `overflow-wrap: break-word`, and use `min-height` or a line clamp instead of a fixed height.
6. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and 4 unprobed links with throttling, especially the pergola and "Find a gift for mom" URLs.
7. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be dropping them.
