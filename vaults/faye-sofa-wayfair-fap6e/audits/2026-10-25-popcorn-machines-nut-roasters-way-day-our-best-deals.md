# POPCORN MACHINES / NUT ROASTERS 🙌 WAY DAY 🎉 Our BEST deals 🎉 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Way Day-cycle send in about six days, and the subject overpromises again. It leads with "POPCORN MACHINES / NUT ROASTERS." The render has one red popcorn cart in the top-left tile. I can't see a nut roaster anywhere. The rest is a generic grid of planters, a rug, a range hood, turf tiles and shower fixtures. It's another case of a niche subject sitting on top of a broad Way Day catalog. The Oct. 24 "BAR STOOLS" send did the same.
- The structure is solid. The purple Way Day banner with "up to 80% off, Oct. 24–26" sets the offer immediately. A "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block adds urgency, and a free-shipping banner sits above one big "shop all sales" button. It is skimmable, but it is a long scroll with many competing links and no single hero. The "picked just for you" label doesn't match a grid that mostly looks like patio and garden stock.
- Verdict: a competent Way Day template with a mismatched, gimmicky subject line. A subscriber who got six similar sends this week will probably skim it. The offer is real, but the personalization and cadence are weak.

## What's working

- The Way Day banner states "up to 80% off" and "Oct. 24–26" at the top, so the event and dates are clear.
- Product tiles show "Was / NOW" pricing, such as $79.64 to $63.99 and $716.04 to $352.08, along with star ratings. That gives price and social proof.
- The category bars ("wall art & wallpaper up to 80% off", "curtains & drapes up to 70% off", and so on) put the discount depth up front.
- The "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block gives credible, time-bounded urgency.
- "Everything ships free*" and the single "shop all sales" button remove friction and give a clear primary action.

## What's weak

- The subject promises popcorn machines and nut roasters, but only one popcorn cart appears, in a small top-left tile. There's no nut roaster at all.
- "picked just for you" doesn't fit a generic mix of shower fixtures, a ping-pong table and turf tiles.
- The email is very long and has many competing sections: flash deals, gift card, "Your Home, These Picks", rewards badges and an app promo. It has no single hero.
- The "Your Home, These Picks" block repeats the pergola, patio and trellis content from the Oct. 21–24 sends, so it feels stale.
- The product tiles have no product names, only prices. It's hard to tell what some items are, or why they were picked.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero. If popcorn machines are the story, give the popcorn cart a full-width hero with price and a "Shop popcorn machines" button. Otherwise, drop the product-specific subject and lead with the Way Day offer.
- 2. Cut the page by about half. Keep the banner, one hero, one flash-deals block and one CTA, and drop the repeated patio and pergola grid.
- 3. Name the products or categories on the tiles and link each tile to its own page.
- 4. Add an end-of-event deadline, such as "Ends Oct. 26 at midnight", in the preheader and near the top.
- 5. Reduce the cadence, or vary the creative, so the sends feel different from each other.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Way Day, day 2: popcorn machines from $X`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 80% off ends Sunday. Our best deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flash deals refresh in 24 hours. Free shipping on everything.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Popcorn carts, patio sets and rugs. Way Day ends Oct. 26.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Wayfair "WAY DAY" email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: 0 QA issues, and no probed link returned a 4xx/5xx other than 429 rate-limiting. The real problems are widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels, and a coverage gap on authentication and unsubscribe headers that the QA data can't resolve.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 82 links were probed, 70 were skipped by design, and 4 more went unprobed when the 70s budget ran out. The unprobed ones are `/wayfair-product-L145-K~W005271090.html`, `/VIKIO-30-500CFM-…`, `/12-In.W-X-12-In.-L-Outdoor-Striped-…` and `/daily-sales/wall-art-%26-wallpaper-clearance~e403402.htm`. They are neither pass nor fail.
- **8 probed links returned 429.** These include `/daily-sales`, the `wayfair.com/` homepage link, three product links and two `wayfairapp.onelink.me` links. This is the sender's bot protection reacting to the probe. It is not a confirmed defect, but it means those links are unverified.
- **The OneLink app-download URL has a non-HTTPS web fallback.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` forces a redirect hop for desktop and non-app users.
- **The OneLink campaign label conflicts with the send type.** It carries `c=Triggered`, while every other link in the email has `source=batch&batchid=1`. This will misattribute app installs in AppsFlyer reporting.
- **20 ad-impression pixels use `http://`.** They run from `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, with `sz=2x1`. Non-HTTPS sources may be blocked or rewritten by clients. Gmail's image proxy fetches over HTTPS, so impressions are likely under-counted. The open-tracking pixel `t.wayfair.com/b.php` is HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on most images:**
  - Roughly 14 `stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png` rating images from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`. These carry real information (the rating) that screen readers can't read, and the linked star images also generate empty link names in the probe output (`link text: "[image: stars_10.png]"`).
  - The hero `US-WD26-Launch-ETm-Email-Desktop.gif`, which is also a linked CTA to `/daily-sales`, so that link has no accessible name.
  - Three `…SkinnyBanner-*` images (`-Fl…`, `-ev…`, `-Gi…`).
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`.
- **The 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` pixel have no `alt`.** They should have `alt=""` and be hidden.
- **The hero is an animated GIF** (`...Desktop.gif`). I can't confirm a static first-frame fallback for Outlook desktop, which shows only frame 1. The truncated HTML doesn't show the frame content.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** `lang`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the dark-mode handling.
- **Confirmed from the visible CSS:** the `@font-face "Sofia"` rule has an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback, and the mobile `max-width: 480px` media queries are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source or URLs. Recipient and campaign IDs are populated: `_emr=6ead9c24-…` is identical on every link, and `_eml` is a populated per-link or per-section ID. `skurectxid`/`event_rec_txid` are present on all links, so the product recommendations resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool attributes this to header capture by the AgentMail relay. It is unconfirmed, so I'm treating it as an unverified gap rather than a violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` is unknown. The same relay limitation applies.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but the footer is cut off in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters are used on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `refid=MKTEML_142251`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16594`, `source=batch`, `batchid=1`, `varid=13`, `csnid=…`. This is consistent across links, so it is intentional first-party tracking. It does mean the campaign won't appear in standard GA source/medium reports without custom mapping.
- **Product links carry `mplid` and `sku`** (e.g. `NST1031`, `PPTI1482`, `DOEL1061`), which supports landing-page continuity.
- **Two destination slugs don't match their `mplid`.** `wayfair-product-L349-K~W112033310.html` carries `mplid=DOEL1061`, and the slug is a generic placeholder. This is likely fine because the redirect resolves on `sku`, but it was not verified because the link returned 429.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF and the banners. Give the rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or merge them into one text rating. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Fix the OneLink `c=Triggered` label to match the batch send.
4. Pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If they are present, change the QA check to read them from the original message instead of the relay copy.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed links with a throttled or browser-based check, so they move from "unverified" to pass or fail.
6. Run the QA check against the full HTML to confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
