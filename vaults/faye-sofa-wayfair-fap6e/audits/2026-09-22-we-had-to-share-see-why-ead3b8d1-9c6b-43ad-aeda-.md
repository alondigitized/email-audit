- The urgency banner ("EPIC FALL SALE ENDS TONIGHT," 70% off) is bold and legible at the top.
- Wayfair Rewards section (5% rewards on every item) is a clean, well-isolated module with clear copy.
- Star ratings are consistently shown under each intact product tile, adding light social proof.
- Footer app-download callout and brand family strip are tidy and on-brand.

## What's weak

- At least 4 product tiles render as broken-image placeholders (blue dot icon on lavender/white), including two in "let's complete your space."
- A large blank white block near the bottom of the email also shows a broken-image icon with no caption or context.
- Subject line ("we HAD to share. See why →") gives no hint of category, product, or offer — pure curiosity bait with nothing to redeem it visually.
- No single hero/lead offer — the email opens straight into a dense "fresh-picked favorites" grid with no explanatory headline tying it to the subject.
- The Delta-branded sale banner ("EPIC FALL SALE") competes visually with "fresh-picked favorites" right below it and feels like a mismatched insert.

- 1. Fix the broken image tiles before send — this alone would meaningfully lift trust and click-through; a third of the product grid currently looks defective.
- 2. Replace the vague curiosity subject with a concrete category/offer callout so the eye and the inbox promise match.
- 3. Give the email one clear hero above the fold (a single product or sale moment) instead of opening cold into a 6-tile grid.
- 4. Cut or clarify the blank white block at the bottom — either populate it with a real CTA/image or remove it.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

This is a standard Wayfair batch marketing email (MKTEML_121355) built on a legacy Unyer/table-based ESP template. QA probing hit heavy rate-limiting (429s) on Wayfair's own domains, and the source shows widespread missing `alt` attributes plus several insecure tracking pixel URLs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 64 total links were probed (52 skipped by design — footer/utility/social deprioritized); of the probed set, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited), not confirmed failures. Affected: app deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157...`), `Shop Sale` (`/daily-sales`), homepage link, product PDP (`wayfair-product-L167-K~W002999900.html`), and associated image links.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to the 70s time budget, all pointing to `/Tornillo-1Light-Outdoor-Wall-Lantern-L6114-K~LATD7633.ht` and `/wayfair-product-L54-K~W112490693.html` (each duplicated) — status unknown, neither pass nor fail.
- 20 first-party tracking-pixel requests use plain `http://` instead of `https://` (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000...` through `...019`, sequential `s=` params). Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients and browsers enforcing mixed-content policies, which would silently break this impression-tracking batch.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Extensive use of Outlook/Gmail conditional CSS classes (`u-MobileHide`, `u-FullWidthBlock`, etc.) consistent with a standard responsive email build; no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.
- 25 images across the QA scan are missing `alt` text, including content-bearing images (`0915_epicfallsale_skinny-endstonight.jpg`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, product/rating graphics `stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_*` banners, `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`) and tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`). Missing alt text on content images degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for any client that blocks images by default (subject line/preheader become the only fallback context).
- One asset filename contains a literal `%` character unencoded in context: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — likely a typo/corruption in the filename itself (`Outd%oorClearance` instead of `OutdoorClearance`), which risks a 404 depending on how the CDN handles the stray `%`.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) found in the truncated source or QA output.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA flags this as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm true absence from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat; if genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of their 2024 sending guidelines.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured headers — status of sender authentication cannot be verified from this data; flag as unknown, not failing.
- QA confirms an unsubscribe link is present in the footer (deprioritized from probing but always included per tool methodology). No visible CAN-SPAM postal-address check possible from the truncated HTML provided.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121355`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `csnid`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — internal attribution system, not a defect.
- Landing targets (`/daily-sales`, `/`, `/wayfair-product-L167-K~W002999900.html`, app onelink) are topically consistent with the email's sale/product content; cannot confirm actual page content/availability due to 429s blocking verification.
- App deep link correctly uses AppsFlyer OneLink (`af_dp`, `af_web_dp` fallback to `www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`) — standard mobile deep-link fallback pattern, no issue found.

7. Recommendations

- Migrate the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking of open/impression tracking.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 25 flagged images, particularly the two hero/sale banners and the loyalty disclaimer image (compliance-adjacent content) — tracking pixels can keep empty `alt=""`.
- Fix or verify the malformed filename `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` on the CDN.
- Re-run link probing outside of the current rate-limit window (or with delay/backoff) to get a real pass/fail read on the 8 throttled links and 4 unprobed links before treating this batch as fully link-clean.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers outside the AgentMail relay, since the current absence may be a capture artifact rather than a true compliance gap.
