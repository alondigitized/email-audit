# Which TV STAND ARE YOU?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Subject asks "Which TV STAND ARE YOU?" — a quiz-style hook — but the email delivers zero quiz. The hero just shows a TV stand with "TV time, in style" / "SHOWSTOPPING STANDS HERE" copy, no personality-quiz mechanic, no options to click through. That's a subject-to-content mismatch right out of the gate.
- Below the hero it's the now-familiar Wayfair grid dump: a Rewards banner, then a "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" wall of 16 unrelated category tiles (deck boxes, dinnerware, wall sconces, mattresses, cookware...). None of it reinforces the TV-stand premise beyond the hero and one "Blue Sideboards & Buffets" tile.
- This is the same bait-and-switch structure flagged repeatedly in this brand's history (changing table pads 9/23, farmhouse decor 9/23, open box 9/24) — a clickbait subject, one relevant hero tile, then an unrelated grab-bag. Score: mediocre, consistent with brand norm.

## What's working

- Hero image is a real, attractive product shot (rattan-front TV console) with clean overlay text.
- "SHOWSTOPPING STANDS HERE" links directly to the hero product category — no dead-end.
- Wayfair Rewards banner is clear and visually distinct (5% back messaging, purple contrast block).
- No visible render bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, clean grid throughout.

## What's weak

- Subject promises an interactive "which TV stand are you" quiz; there's no quiz anywhere in the email.
- The 16-tile grid is a scattershot of unrelated categories (deck boxes, bath towels, mattresses) — no thematic thread to the TV-stand subject.
- Visual hierarchy fights itself: the Rewards banner competes with the hero for top billing before the reader ever reaches TV stand content.
- Section header "Sales We Tell Our Friends to Shop" is generic filler copy that doesn't connect to the subject or hero at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Either build the promised quiz (even a simple "3 styles, pick yours" visual) or drop the quiz framing from the subject — the mismatch is the single biggest drag on this send.
- 2. Move the Rewards banner below the product grid so the hero offer isn't interrupted immediately after the fold.
- 3. Trim the grid to TV-room-adjacent categories (sideboards, media consoles, lighting) instead of 16 random departments — reinforces the subject instead of undercutting it.
- 4. Rename the grid header to something tied to the room, e.g. "More Ways to Style Your Living Room."
- **Subject Alt A:** `TV stand quiz: find your exact style`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New TV stands, styled 4 ways`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rattan, wood, or sleek metal — see which fits your space`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 5% rewards on everything, always`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Wayfair template renders with standard MSO/Gmail responsive CSS; QA pass rate 57%, with issues concentrated in tracking-pixel HTTPS/alt-text hygiene and unconfirmed unsubscribe/authentication headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links probed by CTA likelihood; several returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited) rather than confirmed failures, including the primary CTA "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" (wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157) and "Shop Sale" (wayfair.com/daily-sales). These are marked as "may be valid" — not confirmed broken, but unverified.
- 4 filter/category links went unprobed due to a 70s time budget (e.g. `/daily-sales/filters/all-weather-deck-boxes-se16398.html`, `/daily-sales/filters/dinnerware-sets-from-%2425-se35945.h`) — status unknown.
- 50 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed.
- All sampled links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_140172`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) — tracking parameter structure is intact and consistent across CTAs.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 20 tracking/impression pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000`–`124126019`) are served over plain HTTP, not HTTPS — likely to be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would break impression tracking silently.
- Same 20 pixels plus 4 content images (banner, TV stand product image, rewards banner, "sales-we-tell-our-friends" image) and the loyalty disclaimer image are all missing `alt` text — screen reader users get no fallback content, and no context is shown if images fail to load.
- No layout/DOM-breakage issues detected in the truncated source; media-query scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, etc.) is standard and intact.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens detected in the source or QA output.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may simply be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than its absence at origin; cannot confirm compliance status either way.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) also not found — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — deliverability/authentication status cannot be verified from this relay capture.
- An in-body unsubscribe link was included per QA (always probed) but was among the deprioritized/unprobed set, so its destination status is unconfirmed.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Wayfair uses its own proprietary tracking schema instead of standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — e.g. `source=graymatter_batch&refid=MKTEML_140172&sltid=8&brcid=1`. This is consistent site-wide practice, not a defect, but standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't pick these up.
- CTA-to-landing-page mapping is logically consistent with subject line/content: TV stand CTA → `/furniture/sb1/tv-stands-entertainment-centers-c1868409-a8965~4.html` (matches "Which TV STAND ARE YOU?" theme); app CTA → onelink.me deep link to app home; sale CTA → `/daily-sales`. No mismatch detected.

7. Recommendations
- Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent tracking loss in TLS-enforcing clients.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 content images and the loyalty disclaimer image for accessibility/broken-image fallback.
- Re-probe the 429-rate-limited links (onelink.me app CTA, daily-sales, homepage) and the 4 unprobed filter links outside the rate-limit window to confirm CTA validity, given the primary CTA is among the unconfirmed set.
- Re-run header capture closer to origin (bypassing/adjusting the AgentMail relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present and RFC 8058-compliant, since relay capture gaps currently make compliance status indeterminate rather than confirmed-absent.
