# Open Box steals
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- "Open Box steals" promises deep discounts on discontinued/open-box inventory — a specific, high-intent subject — and the hero delivers a big bold "open box deals" wordmark with a Le Creuset dutch oven nested into the "o." Then the whole thing falls apart: there is not a single open-box item, price, or "steal" anywhere in the body. What follows is a wall of 18 identical purple category-name bars (Accent Pillows, Cabinet Pulls, Wall Art, Bar Stools...) with generic "up to X% OFF" copy that could be lifted from any Wayfair send on any day.
- This is the same failure mode as 2026-09-23's "CHANGING TABLE PADS under $30" and "FARMHOUSE & RUSTIC DECOR" sends — a themed subject and hero that never gets substantiated below the fold. Here it's worse because "open box" is a distinct inventory category (single units, clearance pricing, "as-is" condition) and none of that language, imagery, or pricing appears once past the hero.
- Judgment: this is a subject-line bait email dressed up with a nice hero graphic. The hero is visually strong, but the body is a generic category directory that has nothing to do with "open box" or "steals."

## What's working

- Bold, on-brand hero typography ("open box deals") with a real product photo (green dutch oven) integrated into the wordmark — genuinely eye-catching.
- Clear red circular CTA arrow directly under the hero, inviting a scroll/tap.
- No render bugs — clean purple bars, consistent spacing, images load, no overlapping text.
- "SHOP ALL SALES" gives a clear fallback CTA for someone who doesn't find their category in the list.

## What's weak

- Zero open-box products, discontinued-item framing, or "as-is" pricing anywhere in the body — the subject and hero are entirely unpaid off.
- 18 near-identical purple bars with no imagery, no differentiation, no visual hierarchy — the eye has nowhere specific to land after the hero.
- No urgency or end-date on any of these percentages, despite "steals" implying limited/one-off inventory.
- Category list is Wayfair's standard full-catalog sweep (bedroom furniture, dining sets, desks) — not curated for "open box," undermining the premise that this is a distinct, exciting inventory tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the 18 generic bars with actual open-box product tiles — photo, "Open Box," original vs. clearance price, and a "1 left" / quantity-limited badge. This is the single highest-leverage fix since it's the entire premise of the subject line.
- 2. Add a credible urgency device ("while supplies last" or a countdown) since open-box inventory is inherently limited — right now nothing here signals scarcity.
- 3. Cut the bar list to the 6-8 categories most likely to actually carry open-box stock (furniture, appliances, lighting) rather than the full 18-category sweep.
- 4. Move "SHOP ALL SALES" or an "Open Box only" filter link higher, near the hero CTA arrow, so a genuinely curious clicker doesn't have to scroll 18 bars to find nothing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open Box: dutch ovens, sofas & more up to 70% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One-of-a-kind Open Box steals — while they last`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Discontinued, returned & display pieces at clearance prices`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited quantities. Once it's gone, it's gone.`
