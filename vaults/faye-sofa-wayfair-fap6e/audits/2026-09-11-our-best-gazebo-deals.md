# Our best gazebo deals
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Another broken-image cold open for Wayfair — the subject promises "Our best gazebo deals," and the top of the email is a blank white box with a tiny broken-image placeholder where the gazebo hero should be. This is the exact same failure mode flagged in the 2026-09-10 "Yesss! New BIDETS have arrived" send (3.00/10) and the 2026-09-09 dining chairs send (4.00/10) — a real pattern now, not a one-off glitch.
- Once the eye gets past the blank hero, the email actually recovers reasonably well: "the must-shop steals" banner leads into a tidy 2x2 tile grid where the top-left tile literally reads "Our best gazebo deals" with a matching pergola/gazebo photo — so the subject-to-content match exists, it's just buried below a broken block instead of leading with it. From there the email sprawls into an unfocused mixed-category catalog dump (fans, recliners, doormats, cat trees, playground sets, patio bar stools) that has nothing to do with gazebos specifically.
- Net: the one asset that should have been bulletproof — the hero matching the subject line — is broken, again, and the rest of the email is generic Wayfair catalog filler wearing a gazebo-themed subject line. Weak send.

## What's working

- The subject line "Our best gazebo deals" does eventually find a matching tile ("Our best gazebo deals »" with a pergola image) in the first product grid.
- The "the must-shop steals" section banner is clean, legible, and correctly styled (purple background, white script-underline text).
- Star ratings are visible under several product tiles (recliner, sofa, tree), giving light social proof.
- The footer has clear brand-trust anchors: Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional, and the Family of Brands (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss & Main, Perigold).

## What's weak

- The hero image space at the very top is a blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon — no gazebo, no offer, no copy. This is the worst possible way to open a gazebo-themed email.
- After the broken hero, the email pivots almost immediately into unrelated categories (doormats, cat trees, recliners, playground sets) — very little of the body is actually gazebo/pergola-focused despite the subject promise.
- No visible discount percentage or dollar amount tied to gazebos specifically — "Open Box Deals" and "60% OFF WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" are generic Wayfair blanket promos, not gazebo offers.
- The footer "60% OFF WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" banner is visually cut off/cropped on the right edge, with stray text fragments ("gW...") bleeding past the image boundary.
- Layout is a long, undifferentiated catalog scroll with no single clear primary CTA — a reader can't tell what they're supposed to click first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken hero image before anything else — this is the third time in recent sends (bidets 09-10, dining chairs 09-09) that the top-of-email image has failed to render. This is a deliverability/QA blocker, not a design nitpick, and it's costing opens and clicks outright.
- 2. Lead the body with an actual gazebo hero and gazebo-specific offer (e.g., "Gazebos up to 40% off") instead of burying the one relevant tile inside a generic 2x2 grid.
- 3. Cut the unrelated categories (doormats, cat trees, playground sets, patio bar stools) from this send or move them below a clear "you may also like" divider — right now they dilute the gazebo promise the subject made.
- 4. Fix the cropped/cut-off "60% OFF WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" banner at the footer — visible text fragments bleeding off the image edge look broken, not intentional.
- 5. Add a specific gazebo discount callout near the top (percentage or dollar figure) so the offer is concrete, not just "best deals."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on 10'x12' gazebos, pergolas, and patio canopies.`
