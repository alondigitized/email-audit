# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Oh, they went full Memorial Day red-white-and-blue for this one — big, loud, can't-miss-it 70% off clearance moment. Very deal-forward, very "check this before you buy anything at full price," which honestly is exactly my energy right now. The hero is all promo, no product, which makes me curious enough to scroll but slightly impatient to actually *see* furniture.

## What's working

- **70% OFF graphic** — the size and boldness of that number is doing heavy lifting. I'm a clearance-event hunter and this is basically a flare gun aimed directly at me.
- **The "SHOP NOW" button** — purple on white, clear contrast, no ambiguity about what to do next.
- **Top banner strip** ("UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA") — doubles the signal without feeling cluttered; the "FEAT. DELTA" brand call-out tells me it's curated, not just a site-wide random markdown.
- **Rug tiles peeking below the fold** — glimpsing actual product (and one with what looks like an Open Box / verified badge) pulls my thumb downward. That's good.
- **Search bar placement** — "Find anything home…" right below the nav is immediately useful if I already know I want to look up sectionals.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% graphic, zero product** — I spend hours looking at photos before buying. A 70% off banner tells me *nothing* about whether the stuff I want (modern-mid-century sofas, accent chairs) is actually on sale.
- **Navigation is fully collapsed** — hamburger menu means I can't see categories at a glance. One extra tap to even know if my furniture category is part of this sale.
- **No loyalty or rewards signal** — I have Rewards points sitting around and Wayfair never reminds me at the right moment.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I always check this for big-ticket items. Not seeing it above the fold is a small but real annoyance.
- **"Up to" qualifier on the 70%** — technically honest but I've been burned enough to assume the 70% stuff is sold out and the remaining clearance is more like 15%.
- **No countdown timer** — Memorial Day implies urgency but I have no idea if this ends Sunday night, Monday, or Tuesday. That ambiguity actually *reduces* urgency for me.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the all-graphic hero for a lifestyle tile showing a real product** — e.g., a mid-century sofa tagged "Was $899 → $449" sitting in a living room that looks like a starter home. One concrete product image converts browsers like me faster than abstract percentages.
- **Add a countdown timer or end-date line** ("Sale ends Mon, May 26") directly on the hero — I'm more likely to act now if I believe the clock is real.
- **Surface at least one category chip row below the hero** ("Sofas · Rugs · Lighting · Outdoor") so I don't have to open the hamburger to know if my category is included in the 70% event.
- **Show my Rewards balance or a "member exclusive" badge** somewhere in the top strip — that's a retention play I'd respond to instantly.
