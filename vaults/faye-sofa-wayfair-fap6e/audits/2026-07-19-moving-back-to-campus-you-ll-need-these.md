# Moving back to campus? You’ll need these →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-aligned email Wayfair has sent in the July batch — subject line and body actually match for once. "Moving back to campus?" opens into a coherent dorm-themed campaign with three distinct product categories (storage, sorority styles, appliances), social proof, and visible discounts. That alignment alone puts it ahead of the July 17–19 streak of subject-body mismatches that plagued sends like "WALL ART CLEARANCE" dumping into a general bargains page.
- The execution is solid but the strategic premise is flawed: that question-mark subject line is a segmentation admission. If the list were actually filtered to college-aged subscribers or parents of incoming students, the format would be smart self-selection. Sent to the full Wayfair list, it's an explicit invitation for everyone over 25 to skip it. The discounts (40%, 45%, 30%) are real but buried in the lower scroll, and the primary CTA — "See deals in the hub" — introduces jargon where plain language would convert better.
- Good creative, undercut by list hygiene. If Wayfair has campus-relevant purchase signals (area rugs for small spaces, coffee makers, desk chairs in the last two years), this email earns its send. If not, it's wasted spend on a narrow creative ask.

## What's working

- **Subject-body alignment** — "Moving back to campus" → "designed for dorm life" hero. Finally. This is the first send in this July review window where the creative promise and body delivery match.
- **Three coherent category sections** — storage & organization, creator sorority styles, dorm-approved appliances. The structure tells a complete dorm move-in story.
- **Social proof section** — the star rating and review pull quote ("Makes the space feel like spring vacation..." — Casey, JD) is well-placed mid-scroll and adds credibility before the discount grid.
- **Discount callouts are concrete** — 40%, 45%, 30% off are visible numbers, not "up to X%" waffling.
- **Seasonal timing is right** — July 19 is prime back-to-campus shopping window, a full month before most August move-in dates.

## What's weak

- **Question-mark subject is a self-destruct mechanism on an unsegmented list** — "Moving back to campus?" tells 80%+ of subscribers this email isn't for them before they open it.
- **"See deals in the hub" is jargon** — "hub" is an internal Wayfair term; a shopper doesn't know what destination they're clicking to.
- **Discounts are below the fold** — the hero has zero price signal; a subscriber on mobile has to scroll through three sections before they see any %off figure.
- **"WHILE GOODS REMAIN" is the weakest possible urgency** — vague, unverifiable, and impossible to act on. It occupies real estate without creating any behavioral response.
- **"Creator sorority styles" label is opaque** — "creator" in this context is unclear; it reads as an influencer-partnership marker that doesn't tell the shopper what they're about to see.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment or reframe the subject** — if the list is unsegmented, drop the question mark and replace with a declaration that any home shopper can engage with. The question gates the audience you don't actually have a list for.
- 2. **Replace "See deals in the hub" → "Shop Dorm Essentials"** — eliminates jargon, sets destination expectation, cleaner verb-noun.
- 3. **Add a price signal to the hero** — "Up to 45% off" in the hero section would cut abandonment before anyone scrolls. The creative has the room.
- 4. **Swap "WHILE GOODS REMAIN" for a concrete deadline or social proof number** — "Move-in sale ends July 27" or "Trending with 3,200 dorm shoppers this week" both outperform the current urgency copy.
- 5. **Rename "creator sorority styles" to something shopper-readable** — "Influencer-picked dorm styles" or just "Sorority-approved looks" is clearer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your dorm checklist is missing these Wayfair picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Storage bins, desk chairs, mini fridges — everything before move-in day`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `40,000+ dorm-approved picks. Free shipping on orders over $35.`

<!-- Required -->
