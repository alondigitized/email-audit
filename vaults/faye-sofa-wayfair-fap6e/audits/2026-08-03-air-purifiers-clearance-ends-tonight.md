# AIR PURIFIERS ❗ CLEARANCE ❗ ENDS ❗ TONIGHT ❗ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Same recycled "48-HOUR CLEARANCE UP TO 70% OFF · ENDS TONIGHT" banner that's anchored nearly every Wayfair send in this run (08-02 "AREA RUGS," 08-02 "HEADBOARDS," 08-01 "Black Outdoor Privacy Screens," 08-01 "Cat Trees"). The subject screams "AIR PURIFIERS" with four stacked exclamation marks, but the hero — the very first thing your eye hits — never mentions air purifiers at all. It's the exact subject/hero disconnect flagged repeatedly in this brand's history.
- There's a small saving grace: the very first product tile under "picked just for you" (top-left, $479→$399) is a white cylindrical unit that reads as an air purifier. So the promise is technically kept — just buried in an 8-tile generic grid where 7 of 8 items are furniture and appliances that have nothing to do with air purifiers.
- Net read: this is a template clearance blast with an air-purifier subject line bolted on, not an air-purifier campaign. The subject's urgency/spam punctuation is worse than prior sends, and the "ends tonight" claim has now been recycled across enough emails that a repeat subscriber would stop believing it.

## What's working

- The top-left tile in "picked just for you" is a plausible air purifier match for the subject line ($479→$399, 5 stars).
- Percent-off badges and strikethrough pricing are visible and easy to scan across the grid.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" banner is a clear, low-friction secondary offer.
- Wayfair Rewards / Pay Over Time icons reinforce loyalty benefits for existing members.

## What's weak

- Hero banner has zero air-purifier imagery or copy — it's the same generic "48-HOUR CLEARANCE" wordmark reused across the last several sends per brand history.
- Subject stacks four "❗" emoji plus ALL CAPS — a harder spam signal than this brand's usual (already flagged) emoji habit.
- "Picked just for you" is not actually curated to the subject's category — 7 of 8 tiles are living-room/kitchen furniture, unrelated to air purifiers.
- Category promo bars below (area rugs, storage, curtains, mattresses) skip air purifiers/appliances entirely, reinforcing that the subject was an afterthought.
- "Ends tonight" urgency is losing credibility — it's been the claim in multiple recent sends without actually ending.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to actually show the air purifier with real specs (CADR, room size, price) and a "Shop Air Purifiers" CTA — this is the single biggest lift since it fixes the subject/hero mismatch that's now a repeat pattern across five-plus sends.
- 2. Curate "picked just for you" to air purifiers and adjacent home-air/appliance categories instead of a mixed furniture grid.
- 3. Drop two of the four "❗" from the subject — one is enough for urgency without tipping into spam filters.
- 4. Add an "Air Purifiers | up to X% off" bar to the category promo stack so the category is represented at least once outside the hero.
- 5. Retire "ends tonight" for a rotating, more specific deadline (e.g., a real countdown or day-of-week) so repeat subscribers don't discount it.
