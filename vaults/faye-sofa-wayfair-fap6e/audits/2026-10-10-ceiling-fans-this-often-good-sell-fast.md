# Ceiling fans *this* often good sell FAST
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises ceiling fans that "sell FAST." The render delivers one ceiling fan, in the first product tile, with no caption and no link label. After that it is the generic "5 Days of Deals / Ends Tonight" template with a product grid. The email also has several broken images: the second hero tile, a tile in row three, the first tile of "let's complete your space," and a very large empty white block under the cookware row. For a subscriber it reads as a half-loaded page.
- This continues the pattern in the brand history. The 10/9 "DRESSERS" and "BEAN BAGS" sends used the same template and barely paid off their subjects. The 10/8 "CEILING FANS — our top picks." send had a blank hero. Wayfair has now sent a ceiling-fan subject twice in three days, and this one is no stronger than the last. The subject's grammar is also garbled ("*this* often good sell FAST").
- Judgement: weak. The brand is recognizable and the "Ends Tonight" banner has real urgency, but the ceiling-fan promise isn't delivered and the broken images undercut trust.

## What's working

- The "5 DAYS OF DEALS · ENDS TONIGHT · presented by Delta" banner at the top gives credible urgency and a "60% off" badge.
- A modern black ceiling fan sits in the first tile, so there is at least a visual tie to the subject.
- The purple blocks and section headers ("fresh-picked favorites," "shop before they're gone") give a clear, scannable structure.
- Star ratings under each product add some social proof.
- The financing module ("up to 60 month financing" with an Apply Now button) is a clear, honest offer.

## What's weak

- Ceiling fans are not the focus. One fan appears with no "Shop Ceiling Fans" CTA, and nothing else in the email is a fan.
- Several images are broken: hero tile 2, the tile in row three, the first tile in "complete your space," and a huge blank white block near the bottom. That block makes the footer feel miles away.
- There is no single offer or primary CTA. The banner says 60% off, the financing module pushes a credit card, and the product tiles have no prices or discounts.
- Product tiles show no price or deal labels, so the "sell FAST" urgency has nothing behind it.
- The template is the same one used all week, so it feels repetitive (see 10/9 "DRESSERS" and "BEAN BAGS").

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero a ceiling-fan block with a clear offer, such as "Ceiling fans up to 50% off," and a button labelled "Shop ceiling fans." Put it above the generic grid.
- 2. Fix or suppress the broken image slots, and remove the blank block near the bottom. If an image fails, the layout should collapse rather than leave a gap.
- 3. Add price and discount badges to the product tiles, so the "sell FAST" claim is backed by numbers.
- 4. Move the financing module below the product content, since it distracts from the subject's offer.
- 5. Rewrite the subject so it reads cleanly and stays under 50 characters.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last day of 5 Days of Deals: fans, lighting and more from $X.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive media queries and per-recipient tracked links. The automated checks found no hard failures. The real issues are widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, one malformed image URL, and compliance headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 probe warnings are HTTP 429 (rate limiting from the probe), which is not a defect. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` and `https://www.wayfair.com/wayfair-product-L230-K~W111556454.html?...sku=IVYX8604`.
- **4 product links were not probed** because the 70s time budget ran out (`wayfair-product-L306-K~W112476058.html` ×2 and `wayfair-product-L310-K~W111781281.html` ×2). Their status is unknown.
- **Malformed image URL:** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`. `%oo` is not valid percent-encoding. Strict clients may fail to load the image, and the filename looks like a stale `0728` asset.
- **Non-HTTPS app deep-link fallback:** the onelink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. That is an `http://` destination, so it depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the onelink URL has `c=Triggered`, but every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is attributed as "Triggered" in AppsFlyer, which can skew reporting.
- **Test asset in production:** `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` is linked to `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=5`. A "test" filename suggests an A/B asset, which is not necessarily a defect.
- **Duplicated links:** the "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" onelink URL appears at least twice, each with a different `_eml` token. This is consistent per-link tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images** (QA-flagged):
  - `5daysofdeals_fall2026_skinny-endstonight.png`, which is a linked banner to `/daily-sales`
  - `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg`, which is linked to the homepage
  - `WFCC1%402x.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_2_purple.gif`
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
  - `image%20%283%29.png`
- **Linked images with no alt:** the link text falls back to the filename (for example `[image: 5daysofdeals_fall2026_skinny-endstonight]`). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no usable label.
- **Review-star images** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear 12 times with no alt. Ratings are conveyed only visually, and the product link at `wayfair-product-L230-K~W111556454.html` wraps `stars_11.png`.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:**
  - `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is the open pixel.
  - 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` impression pixels also have none.
  - These should carry `alt=""`.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels use `http://`. Clients and proxies may block them, which undercounts impressions, and some will show mixed-content warnings.
- **CSS:** the visible mobile CSS looks sound. It has `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` and Outlook/Gmail fixes. The source was truncated, so the rest of the markup, including any dark-mode CSS, could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found in the visible HTML. There are no unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FirstName]`.
- Personalization appears to be only per-recipient link IDs (`_emr=a73d8caf-...` is constant, and `_eml` is unique per link). Because the source is truncated, footer and body tokens could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unverified, not a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN), with the same caveat. Major mailbox providers expect one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Nothing in the evidence shows a failure.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA report says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown. The truncated HTML doesn't let me confirm the footer address.
- The From domain `members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain and looks consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** on any link. Attribution uses proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121355`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This works inside Wayfair's stack but won't populate standard GA `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` reports.
- **Link destinations are generic.** Both the banner ("5 days of deals… ends tonight") and "Shop Sale" go to `/daily-sales`. The `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` banner goes to the homepage `/`. The subject is about ceiling fans, but only the product links go to specific fan PDPs (`L230-K~W111556454`, `L306-K~W112476058`, `L310-K~W111781281`). I did not verify that the sale and homepage pages show fans.
- **Product links are consistent:** `sku=IVYX8604` matches `mplid=IVYX8604`, and `sltid=5` matches the header image's `sltid=5`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all content and banner images, and `alt=""` to the star ratings and the 21 tracking pixels. Where stars carry meaning, use alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Fix the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` URL (`%oo` → valid encoding or `o`) and check whether the asset is stale.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers, not the relay copy. Also confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com`.
5. Align the AppsFlyer `c=Triggered` value with the batch send, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
6. Add standard `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that downstream analytics map `source=graymatter_batch` and `refid`.
7. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retries to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed product links.
8. Replace the `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` filename with a production asset name.
