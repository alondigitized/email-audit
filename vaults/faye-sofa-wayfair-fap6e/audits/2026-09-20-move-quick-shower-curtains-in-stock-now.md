# Move. Quick. SHOWER CURTAINS, in stock now >>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- The subject line — "Move. Quick. SHOWER CURTAINS, in stock now >>" — sells urgency and a specific product, but the email opens on a blank, broken hero: just the nav bar, a stray blue dot, and a wall of white space before the content even starts. That's the first thing a recipient sees, and it reads as a rendering failure, not a hero image that didn't load in this capture — either way it's a bad first impression.
- Once the grid actually starts, "In-Stock Shower Curtains »" is present, but it's the first of four tiles in a "the must-shop steals" grid, sharing billing with Adirondack chairs, wallpaper, and a bathroom refresh deal. The subject's urgency ("Move. Quick.") is nowhere reflected in the body — no countdown, no low-stock messaging, nothing that explains why the recipient needs to move quickly. This is the same Wayfair pattern flagged repeatedly in the brand history (2026-09-19 pergolas, 2026-09-18 sectionals): a punchy, specific subject line that the body immediately dilutes into an undifferentiated catalog grid.
- Judgement: broken hero render plus a subject/body mismatch on urgency and product focus. This would score low even before the layout bug — with it, it's one of the weaker sends in this run.

## What's working

- "In-Stock Shower Curtains »" does appear as a named tile, honoring at least the product half of the subject promise.
- The purple "the must-shop steals" and "top picks. low prices." section headers give some visual rhythm/breaks in an otherwise long scroll.
- Wayfair Rewards banner ("get 5% rewards... on every item, always") is a clear, simple loyalty callout with its own visual break.
- Footer has clear brand-family logos (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss & Main, Perigold) and social icons, giving it a legitimate, trusted-brand feel.

## What's weak

- The hero section is broken/blank — just nav, whitespace, and a stray dot — with no headline, no shower curtain imagery, and no urgency cue at all.
- The "Move. Quick." urgency from the subject has zero visual representation anywhere in the body — no countdown, no "limited stock," no deadline.
- Shower curtains are buried as one of four equal-weight tiles instead of leading the email, undercutting the specific subject promise.
- The email is an extremely long, repetitive grid (nightstands, air fryers, grill covers, bird baths, dog crates) that has nothing to do with the subject and drowns out the named offer.
- The "70% OFF EPIC FALL SALE" banner near the bottom is a generic, recycled asset seen in prior sends (2026-09-16, 2026-09-15, 2026-09-14) and clashes tonally with the specific in-stock/urgency framing up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero render — this is the single biggest lift available: put an actual shower curtain lifestyle image with bold "In Stock Now" + urgency copy front and center, above the fold.
- 2. Make the hero and subject match: lead with shower curtains and an actual urgency mechanic (e.g., "only X left," "ships today if ordered by Y") rather than generic "steals."
- 3. Cut the grid down — this doesn't need 20+ unrelated tiles (bird baths, dog crates, air fryers); trim to a focused "in-stock, ships-fast" category set that supports the subject's promise.
- 4. Move the shower curtain tile (and 2-3 truly in-stock/fast-ship items) to the very top, before the generic "must-shop steals" grid.
- 5. Drop or reposition the recycled 70% off fall sale banner — it undercuts the "quick, in-stock" urgency story with a stale, unrelated discount play.
