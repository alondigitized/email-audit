# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth appearance of "Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." in Wayfair's recent sends — it went out on July 18 (twice), July 19, and now July 21. That subject line wasn't memorable the first time; by the fourth, it registers as filler. The body, meanwhile, has moved on entirely: there's a bold Black Friday in July banner (July 23–27, up to 80% off), an Open Box Deals hero, and a Recliners feature with a product grid. That's actually a stronger story than free shipping. It's being buried by a recycled subject that promises something the body doesn't deliver.
- The Recliners section is the best thing here. Six product thumbnails, a "Shop Now" button, and a headline with some personality ("It's like they were designed with you in mind"). If that were the hook, this would be a credible category send. Instead, a subscriber who opened for free shipping info arrives in a recliner sale, and the mismatch kills the momentum before it starts.
- Bottom line: real content, wrong wrapper — again. Same structural failure Wayfair repeated with July 18 and 19.

## What's working

- Black Friday in July banner is bold, date-bounded (July 23–27), and anchors the top of the email with a concrete savings claim (up to 80% off).
- Recliner product grid gives the reader six distinct options to browse — more scannable than a single hero image.
- "Shop Now" buttons are clean, high-contrast purple — no rendering issues.
- Open Box Deals section leads with a credible benefit statement ("returned, like-new products") that reduces purchase risk.
- Email renders without bugs — no broken images, no overlapping elements, no layout collapse.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth send with this exact subject line in four days — the free-shipping hook has been completely desensitized.
- Subject promises a free shipping reminder; body delivers a recliner sale and open box deals — a bait-and-switch at the inbox level.
- Two competing hero sections (Open Box Deals AND Recliners) split focus; neither wins the frame as the primary story.
- "Shop Now" is the only CTA copy across both sections — "Shop Recliners" or "Browse Open Box" would do actual directional work.
- No pricing visible on individual recliner products — the 80% off claim sits in the header but never connects to a specific product or anchor price.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this subject line permanently.** The BFJ + Recliners story is genuinely stronger; the free-shipping hook is dead weight at this send frequency.
- 2. **Pick one hero.** Open Box OR Recliners — not both. Two equals zero.
- 3. **Name the CTA.** "Shop Recliners" or "Browse Open Box Finds" beats "Shop Now" on every metric.
- 4. **Surface a price in the body.** "Was $649, now $299" on one recliner would validate the 80% off banner claim and make the click feel worth it.
- 5. **Move the BFJ countdown into the hero.** The July 23–27 window is the strongest urgency signal in the email and it's buried in a thin header banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Recliners: Black Friday prices, July 23–27`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Open box recliners — like new, up to 80% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop returned, like-new recliners before the sale closes July 27.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Black Friday pricing on recliners — no waiting until November.`
