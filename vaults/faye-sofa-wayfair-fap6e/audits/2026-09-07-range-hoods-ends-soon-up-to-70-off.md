# RANGE HOODS 🔴 ENDS SOON ⚪ UP TO 70% OFF 🔵 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Wayfair playbook the brand history has flagged all week: a screaming subject line ("RANGE HOODS 🔴 ENDS SOON ⚪ UP TO 70% OFF 🔵") that the render mostly fails to honor. The very first "picked just for you" tile does appear to be a ceiling-mount range hood (Was $2,699.00, Now $1,339.00), so there's a thin thread of truth here — better than the "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" sends that delivered zero cushions — but it's one tile buried among a chandelier, a kids-in-a-pool stock photo, a lunch box, dining chairs, and mugs before the eye ever gets a second range hood.
- The top banner is generic Labor Day clearance messaging ("LABOR DAY CLEARANCE ENDS TOMORROW"), not range-hood-specific, and the rest of the email is Wayfair's standard 30-tile firehouse: rugs, tableware, mattresses, appliances, bedding, Afterpay financing, and a "Your Home, These Picks" module with kitchen mats and rugs. Nobody who opens this for a range hood deal gets a range-hood shopping experience — they get the full-catalog blast with one relevant tile up top.
- Judgment: marginal improvement over the outright bait-and-switch sends (3-4/10 territory), but still a subject-line con relative to what's actually in the body. Score lands mid-pack.

## What's working

- The hero tile is genuinely a range hood with a real markdown (Was $2,699.00 → Now $1,339.00, ~50% off), so the subject isn't a total fabrication.
- The purple "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" banner creates a credible, time-boxed second offer layer distinct from the general clearance.
- "Free & easy delivery over $35" is a clean, unambiguous secondary incentive placed mid-scroll.
- Star ratings are visible on individual product tiles, giving light social proof.
- No broken images, overlapping text, or lorem-ipsum — the render is clean structurally.

## What's weak

- Only one of ~24 product tiles is a range hood; the subject promises a category-specific deal and the body is a generic sitewide clearance email.
- The hero banner reads "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE ENDS TOMORROW" — no range hood messaging in the one spot with guaranteed above-the-fold visibility.
- The "picked just for you" row mixes wildly unrelated categories (chandelier, kids in a pool, lunch box, dining chairs, mugs) right next to the one relevant product, diluting focus immediately.
- Repeats the exact bait-and-switch pattern flagged in five of the last six brand-history entries (cushions, dining tables, etc.) — subscribers who've been burned by this cadence will discount the subject line on sight.
- No dedicated "Range Hoods" category tile or CTA anywhere in the body — someone who clicks in expecting a hood-focused landing page won't find one link that says so explicitly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual range hood deal in the hero banner slot, replacing the generic "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE ENDS TOMORROW" strip — if the subject says range hoods, the first thing seen should be a range hood with its price cut, not sitewide clearance copy.
- 2. Group all kitchen-ventilation-adjacent tiles (range hoods, kitchen fixtures) into their own row directly under the hero, rather than scattering one hood tile among a chandelier and a pool photo.
- 3. Add a specific CTA button reading "Shop Range Hoods →" tied to a filtered category page, mirroring the "area rugs →" / "bedding →" pattern already used elsewhere in this template.
- 4. If inventory can't support a full range-hood section, don't lead the subject with that category — pick whatever category actually has the most tiles (e.g., area rugs, which has real presence in the render) to rebuild subject/body trust after this week's mismatches.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day Clearance: Up to 70% off, ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ventilate in style — plus rugs, bedding & more on sale`
