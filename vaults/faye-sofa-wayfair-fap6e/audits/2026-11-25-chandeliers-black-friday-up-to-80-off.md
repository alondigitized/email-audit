# CHANDELIERS 🟪 ⬛ 🟪  BLACK FRIDAY ⬛ UP TO 80% OFF 🟪 ⬛ 🟪  
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the standard Wayfair Black Friday template with a chandelier subject line on top. The subject promises "CHANDELIERS," and the render delivers exactly one chandelier: the top-left tile under "picked just for you," a crystal fixture marked Sale at $799.99. Everything after it is generic: a cooktop-style appliance, a pot, a vacuum, a sectional, rugs, bedding, Christmas trees and patio chairs.
- The purple "BLACK FRIDAY up to 80% OFF" banner is clear and sits at the top. The page is very long, and nothing connects the chandelier to the rest. Someone who opens for lighting lands on a mass-market sale page.
- Judgement: a competent but undifferentiated blast. The subject is more specific than the body. This is the sixth near-identical Wayfair send in three days.

## What's working

- The "up to 80% OFF" banner is the first thing the eye lands on, and it ties to the subject's offer.
- The first tile is a real chandelier with a sale badge, a was/now price and star ratings.
- "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" gives a clear second incentive in a strong purple band.
- The category tiles (area rugs 80%, bedding 70%, Christmas trees 45%) show specific discounts and are scannable.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Chandeliers get one tile out of the whole email. There is no lighting category block and no "shop chandeliers" button.
- The CTA is unclear. "shop all sales" is generic and sits well below the fold, and there is no hero button.
- The email is extremely long. It has a credit-card offer, a mattress promo, a second product grid and a "Your Home, These Picks" block, which dilutes the offer.
- The subject's emoji squares and caps are noisy. They repeat the pattern of the previous sends, so the cadence feels spammy.
- The "$40 off" card offer and the asterisked free shipping have fine print at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build a lighting-specific hero: a chandelier image, "Chandeliers up to X% off," and a "Shop chandeliers" button above the fold. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to one themed grid of 6 to 8 lighting products, and drop the unrelated category tiles.
- 3. Replace "shop all sales" with a specific verb and noun, and repeat the button after the product grid.
- 4. Cap emoji in the subject and vary the template between sends, so it doesn't look like the same email as the last five.
- 5. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Monday," so the urgency is credible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with responsive media queries. Its main confirmed defects are about 20 plain-`http://` tracking pixels with no alt text, and missing alt text on every content image. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of how the relay captures headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed. The 7 that came back with 429 are rate-limiting by wayfair.com and onelink.me, not dead links. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…` ("Shop Sale") and the product links with `mplid=JYLA1013`, `HBL10130` and `SRNZ1023`.
- **Unprobed links.** 4 links weren't checked because the 70s time budget ran out. They are neither passed nor failed, and include `…/Tramontina-Gourmet-Aluminum-Stock-Pot-with-Lid-L781-K~id…` and `…/daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e408846.html?eid=635985…`. 65 links were skipped as low priority.
- **Non-HTTPS links.**
  - The top banner link uses `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`, which depends on a redirect to HTTPS.
  - The app deep-link fallback is `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** There are 20 impression pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). Mail clients that block mixed content, or proxy-rewrite images, may drop these, which would undercount impressions.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (eventType=EmailOpen) has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed in source):**
  - Banners: `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jpg`, `…Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`, `…Email_Skinny-EverythingShip…`, `…Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`.
  - Promo graphics: `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…`.
  - Rating images: `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`, 12 or more instances.
- **Image-only links lose their label.** The banner link's accessible name is only the filename, as the QA output shows: `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-VP.jp]`. With images off or a screen reader, these links have no meaningful text. The star-rating links are affected the same way.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** Pixels with no alt text (20 × `imp`, plus `b.php`) add noise for assistive tech. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Web font fallback.** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads a `.woff` from `secure.img.wfrcdn.com`. It has `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so the fallback is handled.
- **Not assessed.** The HTML was truncated, so the body structure, `lang` attribute, and dark-mode handling couldn't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or URLs. Per-recipient identifiers are populated: `_emr=5284e296-…` (constant) and `_eml=…` (varies per link).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is **unverified rather than confirmed missing**. Check the raw `.eml` before treating it as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain is `members.wayfair.com`. Alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but it didn't report a result for it. This is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** All links use Wayfair's proprietary scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=…`, `refid=MKTEML_143967`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16796`, `brcid=1`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`. Attribution works inside Wayfair's stack, but standard GA/UTM reporting by campaign or medium would see nothing.
- **Parameters are consistent across links.** They are the same on the home, daily-sales, product and app links.
- **Landing-page alignment not verified.** The subject says "CHANDELIERS … UP TO 80% OFF", but no probed link targets a chandelier category. Probed destinations are `/daily-sales`, the homepage, and product pages for a mini fridge and a cordless vacuum. The top banner and "Shop Sale" go to `/daily-sales`. Chandelier links may be among the unprobed or truncated ones, so this is **unconfirmed**.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales` banner link to HTTPS.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the banners and star ratings, for example "4.5 out of 5 stars". Use `alt=""` on the pixels.
3. Re-run the header checks on the raw message (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Re-probe the 7 links that returned 429 and the 4 unprobed links with throttling or a longer time budget.
5. Add `utm_*` parameters, or map the proprietary parameters in analytics, if cross-tool attribution matters.
6. Confirm that the chandelier category links in the body match the subject line. The probed links don't show this.
