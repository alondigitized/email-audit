# BARS & BAR SETS ❄️ UP TO 80% OFF your winter refresh ❄️ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "BARS & BAR SETS" at up to 80% off. The render delivers that only in part. The first tile in the "picked just for you" grid is a rustic wood home bar, and the rest of the grid is unrelated: a beverage fridge, deck tiles, a farmhouse sink, a rocking chair, a stockpot. Below that is the standard Black Friday banner (Sealy co-brand), a "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block, and the usual category tiles. The subject sells a category, and the email is the generic Wayfair template.
- The layout is competent and the discounts are clear. The red "80% OFF," "45% OFF" and "70% OFF" tiles are easy to read, and a purple banner anchors the top. But there are no bar-specific CTAs, and nothing says "bars" except one product tile. This is the same pattern as the 11-21 "POPCORN MACHINES" and 11-20 "SHOWER FAUCETS" sends. The subject is keyword-matched to one product, and the body is the same Black Friday email.
- Judgement: a middling send, and the subject over-promises. A subscriber who got "KITCHEN MATS" on 11-21 and "5x8 shag rugs" earlier today will see this as one more templated blast.

## What's working

- The wood bar is the first product in the grid, so the subject has at least one match above the fold.
- The discount tiles (80%, 45%, 70%) are large, high-contrast, and easy to scan.
- The "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" banner adds urgency.
- Product tiles show "Was / NOW" pricing with star ratings, which gives price anchoring and social proof.
- The $40-off credit card offer and the "shop all sales" button give clear secondary paths.

## What's weak

- Only one of 12 grid items is actually a bar. The rest are unrelated, and the email never says "bar sets."
- The flash-deal tiles (area rugs, Christmas trees, bedding, curtains) have nothing to do with the subject.
- The email is very long (about 5,700px) with several competing modules: the grid, flash deals, mattress banner, second grid, credit card offer, and a "Your Home, These Picks" section. The hero offer gets diluted.
- The 80% in the subject is for "up to" deals. The visible bar and fridge tiles show a smaller discount, around 25% off on the fridge ($499.99 to $209.99 aside), so the headline is a stretch.
- One product tile near the bottom of the grid (the stockpot) shows no price. It is a minor gap but looks unfinished.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build a real bar-themed hero: a banner such as "Bars & bar sets: up to 80% off," with a "Shop bar sets" button, then bar carts, bar stools and wine fridges. This is the change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the generic modules (flash deals, mattress banner, second grid) in category-targeted sends, or move them below the themed content.
- 3. Show a real bar-set price in the hero, for example "Bar sets from $X."
- 4. Make the subject concrete, with one emoji at most, and match the discount to what the products show.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bar sets & home bars, up to 80% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your home bar, sorted: sets from $XX`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bar carts, stools and wine fridges, plus 24-hour flash deals on rugs and bedding.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Black Friday pricing on bar sets ends soon. Free shipping over $35.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive media queries and first-party tracking parameters. The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes (including on linked images) and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 12 links were probed and 72 were skipped by ranking. Every probe warning is a 429 (rate limit), not a 4xx/5xx failure, so those links are unverified rather than failing:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?_emr=…`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=…`
  - The three product links (`BQ94050868`, `BN66049547`, `BV64121650`).
  - Two `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links.
- **4 links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out. They are the DeerValley sink, `L6846-K~id-BL96081372`, the Tramontina stock pot, and `/flashdeals`. They are neither pass nor fail.
- **App deep-link campaign label looks inconsistent.** The OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labelled as a triggered campaign would skew AppsFlyer attribution. The `c=` value is confirmed in the URL, but the mislabelling is my inference.
- **The OneLink web fallback is plain HTTP.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` relies on a redirect to HTTPS.
- **Tracking pixels use `http://`.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` images (sequential IDs). They may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and they add 20 extra requests. `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (the open pixel) is HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Banner images have no `alt`:**
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`
  - `…Email_Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`
  - `…Skinny-BF-Sponsor.jpg`
  - `…Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi…jpg`
  
  The first is wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales`, so that link has no accessible name. Images-off clients get no fallback text for these.
- **Star-rating images have no `alt`.** The `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` images (~13 instances) are the only content of the product links, so the QA tool reports the link text as `[image: stars_10.png]`. Screen readers get no rating or an unusable link name. The same `stars_*.png` files are reused across products.
- **Tracking pixels (`b.php`, `imp`) lack `alt`.** Decorative pixels should have `alt=""`. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty, but the check flagged them.
- **Responsive CSS appears sound** in the truncated source: `max-width:480px` queries, a `u ~ div` Gmail fix, an MSO font fallback (`mso-font-alt: "Arial"`), and a custom `@font-face` (Sofia) with fallback. No rendering defects were confirmed. The HTML was truncated, so the body could not be fully audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. Recipient-level identifiers are present in the URLs: `_emr=4c987583-…` is constant across links, and `_eml` is unique per link. The truncated body can't confirm there are no tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. If they are absent from the original message, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `editor@members.wayfair.com` are unknown.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible** in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it was reported, so it is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses first-party parameters instead: `refid=MKTEML_143925`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16786`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=…`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=batch`. This is consistent across all links, but third-party analytics keyed to UTMs won't attribute the traffic.
- **Long, unique query strings** on every product URL (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `mplid`, `sku`) hurt link readability and cache hit rates. Some product slugs are very long (the beverage refrigerator URL, for example).
- **Landing alignment can't be assessed technically.** The links point to `/daily-sales`, `/flashdeals`, and PDPs, and none could be fetched because of the 429s. Whether the "Bars & Bar Sets" subject matches the landing destinations is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all banner images. Give the `stars_*.png` images something like `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`, or move the link onto text with the rating as a `title`/`aria-label`.
2. Set `alt=""` on the `b.php` and `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels.
3. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and consider consolidating them into fewer requests. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
4. Check the `c=Triggered` value on the OneLink URLs against the actual batch send type.
5. Re-run link probing with lower concurrency or a longer time budget to clear the 429s and the 4 unprobed links.
6. Capture raw headers (via direct SMTP or a different relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass before treating compliance as clean.
7. Re-audit with the untruncated HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the absence of stray merge tokens.
