# FOUND: Pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a pergola-heavy send that mostly delivers on "FOUND: Pergolas." Three separate product grids, 14 pergola photos in all, sit under a "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner. The star ratings are a nice trust signal. But the email is a catalog with no offer you can act on. There are no prices, no was/now numbers, and no product names. The 70% banner has no "up to" you can read, and the one visible "SHOP NOW" is tiny.
- The structure also falls apart in the middle and at the bottom. "Open Box Deals" is a big, loud block that has nothing to do with pergolas. A large blank white void opens up below the last grid, with one tiny broken-image icon in it. That is a render bug, and it looks like the same blank-box problem as the 10/15 and 10/16 pergola sends. This is the fourth near-identical pergola email in four days (10/14 "You were eyeing these pergolas", 10/15 "Just dropped: Pergolas on sale now", 10/16 "Price just dropped on Pergolas"), so the freshness is gone.
- Judgement: relevant but repetitive and sloppy. The creative is fine, the broken block drags it down, and a repeat subscriber would likely skim past it.

## What's working

- The pergola theme is consistent from the hero grid through "On sale now: pergolas" and "Top-Rated Pergolas."
- The star ratings under the tiles give social proof without any copy.
- The purple Wayfair branding and the "just for you" header read as personalized.
- The "Holiday Deal Week" banner at the top sets a clear seasonal frame.
- The footer has useful trust anchors: Rewards, Financing, Professional, and the app QR code.

## What's weak

- A large blank white block sits below the last product grid, with a single tiny broken-image icon. This is the biggest visual flaw.
- No prices, discounts, or product names appear on any pergola tile, so the "70% OFF" can't be tied to a specific product.
- The banner's "SHOP NOW" is tiny, and no clear primary CTA button appears anywhere in the body.
- "Open Box Deals" is the loudest block in the email and has nothing to do with the pergola subject.
- Fourteen near-identical pergola photos make the grids blur together. They all look like dark-frame pergolas with curtains.
- This is the 4th pergola email in 4 days, so the cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the broken block. A blank hole that big is the single biggest lift, since it makes the email look unfinished.
- 2. Put prices on the tiles, such as "Was $1,299, now $389," and add a "Shop Pergolas" button right under the hero.
- 3. Cut the grid from 14 pergolas to 4 to 6 and vary the price points, for example by size and style. Pair them with one clear offer line.
- 4. Move "Open Box Deals" below the pergola content, or drop it, so the email stays on one theme.
- 5. Space the pergola sends out, or rotate the angle. After four days in a row, a different category or a new hook would help.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a 480px mobile breakpoint and per-recipient tracked links. The QA run found no hard failures. The main problems are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, widespread missing alt text, and a malformed image URL. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 51 skipped. 8 of the probed links returned 429 and 4 more were unprobed after the 70s budget. This is bot rate-limiting by wayfair.com and onelink.me, not confirmed breakage. Link validity is **unverified**, not failed.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels.** There are 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=e5d762df-…` impression pixels (sequential `s=` IDs). Mail clients and image proxies may block non-HTTPS sources, which would undercount impressions. They are also missing alt text.
- **Malformed image URL.** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-…/0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not a valid percent-escape. Strict parsers or proxies may fail to load it.
- **App deep-link parameters are inconsistent.** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` has `c=Triggered` but also `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is being labeled as a triggered campaign in AppsFlyer attribution.
- **Insecure fallback URL.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP, so users without the app go through an extra redirect hop.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on linked images.** These images sit inside `<a>` tags, so the links have no accessible name. The probe reports their link text as `[image: …]`:
  - hero banner `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-vp.jpg` → `/daily-sales`
  - `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` rating images (about 12 instances) → product URLs
- **Other images missing alt text.**
  - `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png`
  - the `t.wayfair.com/b.php` open pixel
  - the 20 `imp` pixels
- **Review stars have no text equivalent.** Rating information is carried only by `stars_N.png` files. With images off or a screen reader, it is lost.
- The visible CSS shows no issues. It has a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and a `u ~ div` Gmail width fix. The HTML was truncated, so the rest of the structure could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the available HTML or URLs. One observation: `_emr=e5d762df-…` (recipient ID) and a per-link `_eml` are embedded in every URL, including the `p=` parameter on the impression pixels. Forwarded or shared links carry the recipient identifier.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified absence. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results was not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `editor@members.wayfair.com` could not be verified.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they were not assessed. The probe notes an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but its result is not shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Tracking uses proprietary parameters (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_121249`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`, `sltid`). This is consistent across all links, but it will not populate standard GA campaign dimensions without custom mapping.
- **Landing pages are aligned with the link text.**
  - Product links carry matching `mplid` and `sku` values (`ABOJ1079`, `PAUR1193`).
  - "Shop Sale" and the hero banner both go to `/daily-sales`.
- Landing-page content was not verified because of the 429s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels, and the `af_web_dp` fallback, to HTTPS.
2. Add alt text to the hero banner and the product-linked images. Give the rating images text such as `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`. Use `alt=""` on the decorative and tracking pixels.
3. Fix the `%oo` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` filename by re-uploading the asset with a clean name.
4. Correct the `c=Triggered` campaign label on the OneLink URLs so it matches the batch send.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original receipt instead of the relay copy.
6. Re-probe the 12 rate-limited and unprobed links with throttling or a different user agent to confirm they resolve.
7. If GA is used downstream, map `source` and `refid` to `utm_source` and `utm_campaign`, or append UTMs.
