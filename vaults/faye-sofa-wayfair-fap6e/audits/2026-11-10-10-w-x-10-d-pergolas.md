## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Wayfair promo batch (`batchid=15`, `varid=4`) with tracked links and tracking pixels. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on about 27 images and 20 tracking pixels served over plain `http://`. The header warnings and the 429 responses come from the capture and probing setup, so I haven't treated them as defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), which means the server blocked the probe, not that the link is dead. Examples:
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2`
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.html?eid=603462&...`
- **Coverage gap:** 52 links were skipped by design. 4 more were left unprobed when the 70s time budget ran out: `gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8...`, `deals-on-bedding-sets-se60120.html`, `on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` and `/wayfaircard?_emr=...`. These are unverified, not failures.
- **Insecure fallback in the app link:** the OneLink URL `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Desktop users and users without the app are sent to an `http://` URL, which adds a redirect hop and may trigger a mixed-content or insecure-redirect warning.
- **Duplicate CTA:** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values. That is normal per-link tracking and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 27 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - Content images: `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_3_purple.png`, `WFCC2%402x.png`, `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png`, `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`.
  - Tracking pixels: `b.php` and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels.
- **Linked images without alt:** the hero and header images are wrapped in links (`/daily-sales`, `/`). The link text falls back to the image filename, e.g. `[image: 72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png]`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, those links have no meaningful label.
- **20 tracking pixels over `http://`:** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, each `sz=2x1`. Some clients and proxies block non-HTTPS images. If that happens, impression data for these placements is lost.
- **Decorative pixels should use `alt=""`.** Missing `alt` is worse than an empty `alt`, because some clients announce the filename or URL.
- **`blankBanner (1).png`:** the filename suggests a placeholder asset. The truncated source doesn't show whether it renders as an empty block.
- The visible CSS looks sound. It includes a Gmail `u + .Gmail` fix, an Outlook-app `body[data-outlook-cycle]` fix and a `u ~ div { min-width: 100vw }` mobile fix. The rest of the HTML was truncated, so I can't audit structure or `<title>` and `lang`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the QA data or the visible source. The `_emr` and `_eml` parameters are populated with GUIDs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found:** QA says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, because the relay may strip or fail to expose headers. Check the raw `.eml` before calling it a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason, so there is no evidence of failure.
- The QA notes that an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no unsubscribe URL or physical address appears in the visible QA data. I can't confirm either from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** every link uses a proprietary scheme: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_121289`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16710`, `mdlid`, `wfcs=cs7`. This works if Wayfair attributes through its own system. It will not show up in GA channel reports.
- **Consistent attribution:** the parameters are identical across all links, with only `_eml` (per-link ID) and `mdlid` (per-module ID) varying. `unifiedeventid` matches `eid` on the event links.
- **Landing alignment:** the subject "10' W x 10' D pergolas" matches the link text and the `...10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.html` URL slug. Landing page content was not verified because the probe returned 429.
- **Stale asset name (low confidence):** the hero is named `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero`, and the email was sent in November. Check that the correct seasonal asset is in the template.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the 21 tracking and spacer pixels (`b.php` and the `imp` pixels).
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Change `af_web_dp` to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the raw message source. If the relay strips them, add a raw-header capture to the QA harness.
5. Re-probe the 429 and unprobed links with throttling or retry and backoff, and a longer time budget.
6. Confirm the hero image asset is current.
