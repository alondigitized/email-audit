# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Wayfair went full Fourth of July run-up for Memorial Day — red, white, and blue gingham everywhere, massive 70% OFF in my face the second I open the app. I respect the energy, and honestly "Memorial Day Clearance" is exactly the kind of sale event I refresh for. But past that giant number, the homepage is doing almost nothing to earn the next tap — no furniture, no mid-century vibes, no "here's what's on clearance in sofas."

## What's working

- **The 70% OFF hero number** — that giant typography stops the scroll. It's exactly the signal that makes me think "okay, Way Day energy, I should look around."
- **The top banner strip** — "UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA →" gives me a second confirmation of the event without eating more screen real estate.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — dark oval, high contrast against the white card, clearly tappable. No ambiguity.
- **Patriotic gingham background** — it's a bit loud but it codes *big sale event*, which primes me to think deal-hunting, not just casual browsing.
- **"up to 70% OFF: Memorial Day Clearance →" text link below the carousel** — gives me a second entry point if I want to see the full clearance collection.

## What's weak

- **Featured products are completely off-persona**: a matte black planter and what looks like a children's playhouse. I'm here for sofas, accent chairs, coffee tables — this doesn't tell me furniture is even in the sale.
- **No category shortcuts**: zero visible nav pills, no "Sofas," "Living Room," "Outdoor" tiles. The only paths are search, hamburger menu, or the generic SHOP NOW. That's two taps minimum to get to anything I actually want.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold**: no star ratings, no "X reviews," no "Best Seller" badge. For a clearance event, I want to know the stuff is still worth buying.
- **No free-shipping call-out**: I always check if I'll hit the free-shipping threshold before I commit. It's nowhere visible.
- **No urgency signal beyond the event name**: no countdown timer, no "Sale ends May 27" — Memorial Day clearance implies a deadline but never states one. That friction costs conversions.
- **No loyalty/rewards mention**: I don't even know if I'd earn points on clearance items.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the random planter/playhouse cards for clearance furniture thumbnails** — show me a sectional at 65% off or a mid-century media console and I'm tapping immediately. Product choices set category expectations.
- **Add a "Ends May 26" or countdown chip to the hero banner** — the Memorial Day implied deadline is soft. Make it explicit; I respond to hard deadlines.
- **Drop a horizontal category scroll beneath the hero** — four or five pills (Sofas, Dining, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting) so I can route to my category in one tap instead of hunting through the hamburger.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold inline** — even a one-line "Free shipping on orders over $35" strip would reduce hesitation before I commit.
