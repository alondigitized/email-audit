# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Wayfair is going full Memorial Day mode and I am NOT mad about it — "Warehouse Clearout up to 60% OFF" is literally the reason I have this app on my phone. This feels like a clearance event email that became a homepage: big sale energy, minimal browsing infrastructure. It's pushing the deal first, everything else second, which honestly? For me, on this particular weekend, that works.

## What's working

- **"Up to 60% OFF" badge on the hero** — that purple burst sits top-left of the white content box and hits immediately. I see the number before I finish processing the page. That's the hook.
- **Double-reinforcement of the offer** — the promo strip at the top AND the hero badge both say 60% OFF. Two impressions before the fold, neither feels redundant.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — it's the right color (Wayfair purple), it's below the headline, it's unambiguous. I know what happens if I tap it.
- **Product thumbnails already visible at the bottom of the viewport** — the clearout rail with bed and textile images starts loading into view without scrolling. I'm already seeing product before I do anything.
- **Search bar with "Find anything home…"** — dead simple, accessible right under the logo, one tap if I want to skip straight to "mid-century sofa."

## What's weak

- **Gingham aesthetic in the hero** — red and white checkered pattern reads "Fourth of July picnic" or "farmhouse kitchen," not "modern-mid-century starter home." I get that it's Memorial Day, but this visual doesn't match what I actually want to buy.
- **No loyalty hook** — I've ordered from Wayfair before, I have an account icon in the nav, but there's zero "hey, you have rewards" or "member pricing" callout. For someone who came specifically for the sale, that missed opportunity stings.
- **No countdown or deadline copy** — "Memorial Day" implies urgency but there's no timer, no "ends Monday," nothing that makes me feel like I need to tap now instead of in an hour.
- **No trust signals** — no "4.5 stars across 10M reviews," no "free shipping over $X," no returns mention. For a big-ticket furniture shopper, I want reassurance before I click into a clearout.
- **Product thumbnails at the bottom are unreadable at this viewport** — can't see product names or prices in the rail; they're cropped just enough that I need to scroll to get anything useful from them.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham background for a lifestyle shot of an actual furnished living room** — mid-century credenza, light sofa, warm tones. Keep the "60% OFF" badge. The sale number does the heavy lifting; the background should do style inspiration work, not patriotic pattern work.
- **Add a "Ends Monday" or countdown chip under the CTA button** — one line: "Sale ends Monday, June 2 at midnight." That's shippable in a day and would meaningfully move tap-through rate for people like me who are on the fence.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** — replace or extend "UP TO 60% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY WAREHOUSE CLEAROUT" to include "| Free shipping on orders over $35" or whatever the actual number is. I need to know before I build a cart.
- **Add a rewards/login nudge under the search bar** — "Sign in for member pricing" in small text costs almost nothing and would catch returning shoppers before they bounce to a competitor.
