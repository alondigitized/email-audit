# ⏰ 72 hours only: UP TO 60% OFF ⏰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's generic sitewide-sale template with a real deadline attached. A red "UP TO 60% OFF / 72-HR CLEAROUT" strip sits on top, and a purple "picked just for you" block follows. That block is almost entirely pergolas, six tiles in the first grid and six more after the Klarna band. Everything loads cleanly, and the category tiles (area rugs 60%, mattresses 30%, fireplaces 45%) are easy to scan. The deadline is credible: the Klarna band says the offer is available through 10/12/2026.
- The weakness is the hero. The "72-HR CLEAROUT" banner is a thin strip with a tiny "SHOP NOW" button, and it loses to the twelve pergola photos below it. The "picked just for you" label also overpromises. Twelve near-identical pergola tiles look like a merchandising loop, not personalization. The category tiles carry the real offers but sit below the fold, each labeled only "up to X% off" with a noun.
- Against the recent run (10/10 "Ceiling fans," 10/9 "Dressers," 10/8 "Open Box steals"), this is the first subject that doesn't promise a category, and it fits the body better than those did. That makes it the most honest match between subject and body this week. It is also the fifth near-identical "deals" send in five days, so the 72-hour urgency reads as routine. Verdict: average, with a tidy but forgettable execution.

## What's working

- The subject's "72 hours" and "UP TO 60% OFF" match the "72-HR CLEAROUT" banner and the 60% area-rugs tile. This is the first send this week where the subject and body agree.
- The category tiles are consistent and scannable. A big percentage, a category label and an arrow make each one a clear tap target.
- Star ratings under every pergola tile add social proof.
- The Klarna "0% APR up to 24 months, through 10/12/2026" band gives a second, dated reason to act.
- Every image renders. There are no broken-image blanks like the 10/8 "CEILING FANS" send.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is a thin strip with a tiny "SHOP NOW" button. The pergola photos dominate, so the offer isn't what the eye lands on.
- Twelve pergola tiles carry no price, no caption and no per-product discount. Only a few have a "Sale" badge, so the "picked just for you" claim feels hollow.
- The real offers (rugs 60%, mattresses 30%) don't appear until after the first photo grid. Most of those tiles show "up to" ranges, which is vague.
- The deadline is never stated at the top. "72-HR" is there, but the actual end date (10/12) only shows up in the Klarna fine print.
- The footer and app-download blocks add length without adding a reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around the deadline. Use a large "Ends Sunday 10/12" banner with a real price-anchored lead product, such as "Area rugs up to 60% off from $X," and a full-width button.
- 2. Cut the pergola tiles from twelve to four to six and add a price or % off to each. If they stay, label the block as what it is ("Patio season clearance").
- 3. Move the category grid above the first product block, and put the 60% tile first, since it matches the subject.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" and bare noun links with verb-plus-noun CTAs, for example "Shop rugs up to 60% off."
- 5. Cut the cadence. Five near-identical sends in five days trains people to ignore the "72 hours" claim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs up to 60% off — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `72-hr clearout: rugs, mattresses & patio`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Area rugs 60% off, mattresses 30% off, plus 0% APR through 10/12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest markdowns end Sunday night. See what's still in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's main technical weaknesses are 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels and widespread missing alt text, including on the hero and rating images. Most link probes returned 429 rate limits, which are inconclusive and not broken links. The List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Eight probed links returned 429, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...` and the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` app links. A 429 is Wayfair's rate limiting of the probe, so these are inconclusive.
- **Incomplete probing.** 12 links were probed and 49 skipped by priority. 4 more timed out at 70s and are unchecked: the three pergola product URLs (`Aurora-10…`, `Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-Adjustable-Aluminum-Gazebo-with`, `All-Weather-Pergola-With-Curtains…`) and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearout~e412400.html?eid=638026&_e…`. Link health for the product grid is unverified.
- **Insecure ad pixels.** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=3718fc63-…` tracking pixels load over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop them, which under-counts impressions. Every one is also missing `alt`.
- **Open pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00mZXZlbnRUeXBlPUVtYWlsT3Blbi…` (eventType=EmailOpen) is HTTPS but has no `alt`. Apple Mail Privacy Protection and image-blocking clients will make opens unreliable.
- **Deep-link fallback is not HTTPS.** The app links set `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so the fallback URL is HTTP and relies on a redirect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - Hero banner: `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`. It is a linked image to `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, so the link has no accessible name.
  - `WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale-Skinny-BAU_Financing.jp…`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - 12 review-star images (`stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png`, `stars_11.png`). Each is also the only content of a product link, e.g. the pergola `…~JLYD1804.html` link, whose accessible name is therefore empty. The rating value is not conveyed to screen readers.
- **Image-only links.** Because of the above, the hero and product-rating anchors have no text alternative. Image-blocking clients show nothing for them.
- **Mobile CSS.** I found no rendering defects in the visible portion. The HTML source was truncated, so the rest of the body could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the probed URLs. Per-recipient IDs (`_emr`, `_eml`, `skurectxid`) are populated. The body was truncated, so tokens deeper in the HTML can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both reported not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, with no evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it. I can't confirm its presence or status from the data provided.
- **Physical address:** the footer was not in the truncated source, so it can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** on any probed URL. Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme: `refid=MKTEML_140846`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16486`, `csnid`, `batchid=1`, `varid=10`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=batch`. This is consistent across links and likely intentional for their analytics, but it won't feed GA-style campaign reports.
- **Link destinations:** "Shop Sale" goes to `/daily-sales`, the hero to `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, and product links to PDPs with `sku=` and `mplid=` matching the URL slug. Landing pages could not be verified because of the 429s.
- **Possible stale creative:** two image filenames reference other campaigns (`WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale…Financing`, `0125_48hourclearance…`). The hero is named `Spring2026` and the email is dated October. This is inferred from filenames only, so confirm it against the rendered content.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` to them and to the open pixel `t.wayfair.com/b.php`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero image and the two financing and clearance banners.
3. Replace the star-image `alt` with the rating, e.g. "4.5 out of 5 stars", so the product links have accessible names.
4. Re-probe the 8 links that returned 429 and the 4 unprobed links with throttled requests.
5. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as findings.
6. Change `af_web_dp` to an HTTPS URL.
7. Optionally append `utm_*` parameters alongside the existing `refid` scheme if cross-platform attribution is needed.
