# The Drying Racks EVERYONE'S talking about 💬
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Wayfair email on July 23 — following the BFIJ launch, the outdoor organization drop, and the ornaments send — and it continues the same pattern that has defined this brand's recent history: a subject line that names a specific product category, and a body that contains none of it. The subject promises drying racks; the email delivers a ceiling fan hero, a credit card pitch, and the same scatter-grid of rugs, bed frames, outdoor furniture, and beverage fridges that keeps appearing across sends.
- The "keep your cool" fan section is actually a decent standalone piece — focused, one-product hero, clean CTA. It's dragged down by the credit card module above it and the usual bottomless product grid below. As a fan email, this would be a 6. As a drying rack email, it's a 0, because there are no drying racks.
- The bait-and-switch is now a pattern, not an accident. Wayfair did the same thing on July 22 with "END TABLES 🚩 UP TO 80% OFF" (body was rugs and kitchen) and on July 23 with "ORNAMENTS & TREE DÉCOR" (body had none of it). At four-emails-in-one-day, the brand has fully spent its goodwill on cadence alone, and a dishonest subject line on top of that is going to start training subscribers to ignore every subject as noise.

## What's working

- **Fan hero is the strongest single section** — "keep your cool" with a clear ceiling fan product image and a direct "shop fans" CTA is focused and mobile-legible.
- **BFIJ banner ties the send to the live campaign** — the top banner correctly signals that the event is running now (July 23–27), giving the email temporal relevance even if the body doesn't execute on it.
- **Layout is clean and renders without visible bugs** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- **"Get It Now, Pay Later" module is clearly presented** — the credit card section has its own visual block with a clear Apply CTA, which is appropriate placement above the fold.

## What's weak

- **Zero drying racks appear anywhere in the email** — the subject is a complete fabrication relative to the content. There is no section, no product tile, no secondary mention.
- **Product grid is the same recurring dump** — rugs, a bed frame, outdoor patio, beverage fridge, another rug. This combination has appeared in multiple July sends; it registers as the "misc overflow" section, not curated picks.
- **Four emails in one day is too many** — by send four, even a strong subject line gets skipped on cadence fatigue alone; the unsubscribe risk is real.
- **Credit card module above the fan hero dilutes focus** — it's a hard pivot in intent (apply for credit → shop fans) and buries the actual product offer.
- **Social proof in the subject goes nowhere** — "EVERYONE'S talking about" implies reviews, community signal, or bestseller copy inside; the body has none of that.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put drying racks in the email or change the subject** — this is the only change that matters. The body needs a drying rack hero, a price anchor, and a direct link to the category. If drying racks aren't on deal today, use any other honest subject for what's actually here.
- 2. **Lead with the fan hero, cut the credit card module to footer** — the Apply CTA is a conversion-path interruption before the subscriber even sees a product. Move it below the first product section or to a dedicated send.
- 3. **Collapse the product grid to 4 items max, with prices** — a six-product grid without prices reads as filler. Four items with anchored prices (even "from $X") gives the subscriber a reason to click.
- 4. **Add the social proof the subject promised** — if "everyone's talking about" is the hook, back it with a star rating badge, a review count, or a "bestseller" label on at least one product.
- 5. **Cut sends to 2/day max** — send four today is brand damage at this point.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Drying racks under $40 — our most-wanted pick`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Keep your cool: fans from $X + BFIJ deals live now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our #1-rated drying rack ships free — see why it sells out every week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Black Friday in July ends Sunday — fan deals inside`
