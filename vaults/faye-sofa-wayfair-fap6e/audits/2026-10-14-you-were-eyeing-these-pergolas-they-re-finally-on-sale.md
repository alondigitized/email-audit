# You were eyeing these pergolas — they’re finally on sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "these pergolas… finally on sale," and the top of the email delivers. Four pergola and gazebo tiles sit right under the "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner, each with a Sale badge, was/now pricing and star ratings. That's the best-targeted block Wayfair has sent this week. Compared with Oct 12's "Your roundup of pergolas" (the blank banner), this one is better.
- The rest of the email falls apart. Below the first fold, the "cleaning edit," "rattan & raffia," "sleek & chic" and "all under $29.99" modules are mostly lavender or red boxes holding a tiny broken-image icon, with product names floating over empty space. There's also a large blank white gap mid-email. It reads as a half-loaded catalog dump, and it dilutes the one personalized idea.
- Judgement: a strong first screen undone by a broken, unfocused body. It's a fifth pergola-adjacent send in four days (Oct 11 "Your pergola search is over!", Oct 12 roundup and clearout, Oct 13 "almost gone" and "HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK"), and the "finally on sale" claim sits next to a generic 70%-off banner.

## What's working

- The top four tiles are actual pergolas with Sale badges, was/now prices and star ratings, so the subject's promise is kept immediately.
- The "70% OFF HOLIDAY DEAL WEEK" banner is bold and seasonal, and it has a visible "Shop Now" button.
- "on sale: from your browsing" and "we saw these catch your eye..." feel like real behavioral personalization.
- Module-level CTAs are clear verb+noun buttons: "Shop cleaning picks," "Shop natural texture," "Shop garden picks."
- The Wayfair Rewards, Financing and Professional footer strip is present.

## What's weak

- Most modules render as empty tinted boxes with broken-image icons: cleaning, rattan, sleek & chic, and the under-$29.99 grid.
- A large blank white block mid-email, plus another near the bottom, breaks the flow and pushes content down.
- Only the first module matches the persona. Cleaning, rattan, planters and quilts dilute the pergola intent.
- "70% OFF" is an "up to" claim with no clear tie to the pergolas shown. The tiles show different discounts, which blurs the offer.
- There's no real deadline. "Holiday Deal Week" doesn't tell me when it ends, so "finally on sale" has no urgency behind it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the pergola module plus one adjacent module (outdoor lighting, curtains or anchors) and drop the rest. This would lift the score more than anything else.
- 2. Fix or suppress the broken tiles. Don't send modules whose images fail. Add alt-text fallbacks and background colors that look intentional, and pre-validate image URLs.
- 3. Make the top tile a single hero, with the price drop and a specific CTA like "Shop this pergola — now $X."
- 4. Say what changed: "Was $1,099, now $X — lowest in 30 days" is more credible than a generic holiday banner.
- 5. Add a real end date ("Deal ends Sunday") and pace the pergola sends, since this is the fifth in four days.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Pergolas you browsed: now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Price drops on the louvered and gazebo styles you viewed, through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your saved pergolas plus 3 patio picks, all marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. Its main code-level issues are 20 insecure, alt-less `http://` tracking pixels, a malformed percent-encoded image URL, and about 10 content images with no alt text. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts. The HTML was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed image URL:** `https://assets.wfcdn.com/dm/image/43bbe124-.../0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not a valid percent-escape. Strict parsers or proxies (Gmail image proxy, Outlook) may fail to load it. The filename prefix `0728_` also suggests a reused asset from a July campaign.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 `<img>` tags (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, `sz=2x1`) use plain `http://`. The QA data flags them as "may be blocked". Blocking would drop impression tracking for the product and ad slots they cover.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS and works as intended, but has no alt attribute.
- **Link probing is incomplete:**
  - Eight links returned 429 (rate limiting), so they are unverified rather than broken. These include "Shop Sale", "Shop cleaning picks →", and both onelink app links.
  - Four links were never probed: the homepage, `/daily-sales`, and the two pergola product URLs (`/111S-Aluminum-Pergola-...`, `/Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-...`).
  - The pergola PDPs are the links the subject line promises, and they remain unverified.
- **Redundant app link:** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values and the same `sltid=16`. This is expected per-slot tracking, not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (about 10):**
  - `wfus_1013_holidaydealweek_skinnytop-general.jpg`
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`
  - `WFCC1%402x.png`
  - `image%20%283%29.png`
  - `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (twice), `stars_11.png`
- **Effect of the missing alt text:**
  - With images blocked, the banners render as blank space.
  - Screen readers will read the filenames aloud.
  - The star-rating images carry meaning (the rating value) that is lost without alt text.
- **Tracking pixels:** the 20 `imp` pixels and the `b.php` pixel should use `alt=""` so assistive tech ignores them. Right now they are flagged as missing alt.
- **Responsive CSS:** the CSS includes mobile classes and a `u ~ div` Gmail fix. No rendering defects are confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The subject line ("You were eyeing these pergolas") implies behavioral targeting, and the two pergola product links are consistent with it. The truncated source does not let me verify the rest.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** missing, which is expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failed.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** the QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it is not listed in the output. The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical address.
- **From address:** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a branded subdomain. This is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No standard UTM parameters:** no `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` appear. Wayfair uses proprietary parameters instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121641`, `emlid=101`, `maiid`, `mdlid`. This is a consistent internal scheme, but third-party analytics such as GA won't attribute these clicks without a mapping.
- **Per-link identifiers:** `_emr` (recipient/message) is constant across links. `_eml` is unique per link and `sltid` is unique per slot, so link-level attribution is possible.
- **Possible campaign mismatch:** the subject implies a triggered or behavioral send, and the app deep link carries `c=Triggered`. But every link carries `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`, which marks a batch send. If this is meant to be triggered, the tagging is inconsistent.
- **Landing-page alignment:** the pergola product URLs are unverified (see §2). The visible CTAs go to vacuums, rattan, garden, modern, under-$29.99, and the generic `/daily-sales`. Only the unprobed PDP links match the subject's pergola promise.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `%oo` typo in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` URL (should be `%25oo` or `oo`). Confirm whether the asset is stale.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS and add `alt=""` to them and to `b.php`.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the banner images. Give the star-rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars".
4. Re-probe the eight 429 links and the four unprobed links, especially the two pergola PDPs, with throttling or a different user agent.
5. Check the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Make the send-type tagging consistent (`c=Triggered` versus `source=graymatter_batch`), or document why they differ.
7. Add a UTM mapping or a server-side translation if cross-tool attribution is needed.
