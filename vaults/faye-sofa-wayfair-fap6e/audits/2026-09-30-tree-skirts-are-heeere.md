# TREE SKIRTS are heeere!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "TREE SKIRTS are heeere!", and the email delivers that for about one screen. The top banner says "JUST DROPPED / New Price Cuts," and the hero is "Must-Have Tree Skirts" over a red Santa-sleigh skirt under a tree, with a "Shop Now" button. After that, it turns into the usual Wayfair catch-all. The rest is outdoor fireplaces, dog beds, pergolas, birdbaths, frying pans, peel-and-stick tile, refrigerators, rolling hampers and bidets, with no tree skirts in any of it. This is the same pattern as the 2026-09-30 "HOLIDAY ACCENTS — our top picks" send, which also promised seasonal décor and delivered a scattershot grid.
- The hero is clean, and the seasonal hook is fresh for the date. But "New Price Cuts" gives no number, and there's no percentage or dollar figure anywhere. The "Sales, Sales & Yes, More Sales" tag near the bottom adds no specifics. The 14-tile grid below dilutes the one seasonal idea, and the Wayfair credit card block interrupts the flow.
- Judgment: a decent subject and a strong-enough hero, undercut by a generic body with no concrete offer. Expect a fair open rate and weak click-through for anyone who came for holiday décor.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme. The red tree skirt under a decorated tree matches the subject.
- The purple "Shop Now" button is clear and high-contrast on the lavender hero band.
- The top of the email is well organized. A "Just Dropped" ribbon and a red "New Price Cuts" headline give it a clear focal point.
- The two-column tile grid is tidy, with consistent photography and no visible broken images.
- Wayfair's branding is consistent: the purple palette, the footer benefits row, and the app-download block with a QR code.

## What's weak

- The tree-skirt promise ends after the hero. No tile in the rest of the email is holiday-related.
- "New Price Cuts" names no discount, and no price is visible anywhere. The offer is vague.
- The tile links ("On Sale Now: Outdoor Fireplaces," "Bidets You'll Love") point in a dozen directions with no hierarchy, so no single product gets the eye.
- The red "SALES, SALES & YES, MORE SALES / Snag 'Em ASAP" tag is filler. It has no offer and no deadline, and it adds urgency without any substance.
- The financing card ("pace your space / Apply Now") pulls attention toward a credit product instead of tree skirts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email a real tree-skirt and holiday-décor send. Fill the grid with skirts, stockings, wreaths and tree toppers, and drop the pergolas, bidets and refrigerators.
- 2. Put a number in the hero, such as "Tree skirts from $29," or a percentage. Replace "New Price Cuts" with it.
- 3. Rewrite the CTA from "Shop Now" to "Shop tree skirts" so it names the category.
- 4. Move the financing block to the footer or remove it.
- 5. Replace the filler sale tag with a real deadline or a member-price callout.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus stockings, wreaths and toppers. New holiday price cuts inside.`

# Technical Audit: Wayfair, "TREE SKIRTS are heeere!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and per-recipient tracked links. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main gaps are missing alt text on many images, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed material links returned 429 (rate limited), which the probe flags as "may be valid". These cover the app CTA, both "Shop Now" and "Shop Sale" links, the logo, and the banner images. They are unverified, not failures.
- **4 links were not probed** (70s time budget):
  - `.../on-sale-now%3a-outdoor-fireplaces-se`
  - `.../popular-medium-sized-dog-products-se`
  - `.../10%27-w-x-10%27-d-pergolas--se82167.`
  - `.../steel-pergolas-you%27ll-love-se76757`
  - The first two end in `-se` with no numeric ID, unlike the `-se24347` and `-se76757` slugs. The pergolas URL ends in a trailing `.`. These may be truncated in the QA output, but if they are the real hrefs they would likely 404. Verify them against the raw HTML.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1` impression pixels. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS.
- **Non-HTTPS fallback:** the app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is plain HTTP.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the OneLink app URL has `c=Triggered`, while every other link has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled as triggered will misattribute in AppsFlyer.
