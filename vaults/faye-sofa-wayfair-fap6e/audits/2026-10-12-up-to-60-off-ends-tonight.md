# ⏰ UP TO 60% OFF ⏰ ENDS TONIGHT ⏰ 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Wayfair's usual 72-hour clearout template, and this time it renders cleanly. The red "72-HR CLEAROUT / ENDS TONIGHT" banner leads, and the pergola grid is a real match for someone who has been browsing pergolas all week. That is a step up from this morning's "Your roundup of pergolas" send, which had a broken hero and blank tiles. Every image loads here.
- The problem is that the email never states a price promise. The subject says "UP TO 60% OFF" and the banner repeats "up to 60%". Then the product tiles show was/now prices with no percent-off figure, and some tiles have no price at all. The category strips are ambiguous: mattresses are "up to 30% off" and curtains "up to 55% off". Pergolas take up 12 of 12 tiles plus the "Your Home, These Picks" links, yet no pergola-specific deal is mentioned. "ENDS TONIGHT" also sounds a lot like the "ends tomorrow" send from 10/11, so the urgency feels recycled.
- Verdict: a functional, well-targeted retargeting email with weak offer clarity and fatigue-level cadence. The pergola grid is the best part and the generic urgency is the worst.

## What's working

- The pergola grid is targeted at a browser who has seen pergolas in several recent sends, and it makes a clear case for continuing to shop that category.
- The banner is bold and readable: red field, white "ENDS TONIGHT", with an "up to 60% off" badge.
- Was/now pricing and star ratings on most tiles give some price anchoring and social proof.
- The red category strips (area rugs, mattresses, bedding, curtains) have clear, consistent copy and are easy to tap.
- The render is clean: no broken images or overlapping text, which fixes the problem from earlier today.

## What's weak

- The "SHOP NOW" button in the banner is tiny and low-contrast against the red.
- Several tiles show no price, and only some carry a "Sale" tag, so the grid looks inconsistent.
- The category strips mix 30%, 55% and 60% with no indication of which one applies to the pergolas the email is pushing.
- The bottom "Your Home, These Picks" block repeats pergolas, rugs and bedding with no clear hierarchy, and the Rewards and App blocks are generic footer filler.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" follows the 10/11 "ENDS TOMORROW" and the 10/10 "72 hours only" sends, so the urgency is stale and not credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero pergola at the top with its exact discount ("Was $1,688, now $1,134, 33% off") and a button that names the product, in place of the generic banner.
- 2. Cut the grid to six tiles and make every tile show a price and a discount badge.
- 3. Remove the strips with the weakest percent-off values, or label each strip with the actual maximum discount in that category.
- 4. Make the "SHOP NOW" button bigger and a contrasting colour.
- 5. Cap the cadence at one clearout email per day, and vary the creative after day two.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pergolas up to 60% off, last day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your pergola pick is $554 off tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `10x10 louvered pergolas from $745. Sale ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus rugs, bedding and curtains marked down for the last day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Wayfair's own tracking parameters on every link. The QA run found no hard failures: 0 issues and 3 warnings, mostly missing alt text and about 20 insecure `http://` tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture through the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed as sender-side defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** All 8 probe warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits. That is the target throttling the probe, not a dead link. These URLs were affected:
  - `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...` ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App", 2 instances)
  - `/daily-sales`
  - `/` ("Wayfair")
  - `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`
  - three pergola PDPs: `PAUR1193`, `JLYD1804`, `ABOJ1079`
- **Coverage gap:** 66 links were skipped by design. 4 more went unprobed after the 70s time budget, including 3 pergola PDPs and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearout~e412400.html?eid=638026`. Their status is unknown, neither pass nor fail.
- **Insecure pixels:** about 20 ad-impression pixels use plain HTTP (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=bf122e30-…`). Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop them. This undercounts ad impressions and may trigger "not secure" handling in some clients.
- **Open pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (eventType=EmailOpen) is HTTPS and has no alt text.
- **Deep-link fallback:** the OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Recipients without the app are sent to an HTTP URL before any redirect.
- **Campaign label mismatch:** the same OneLink URL has `c=Triggered` alongside `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labelled "Triggered" can skew AppsFlyer campaign attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, with the QA counts:**
  - The primary banner `72HR_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png` has none. It is also wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`, so that link has no accessible name when images are off or in a screen reader.
  - 11 `stars_9/10/11.png` rating images have none, so product ratings are unavailable to assistive tech. Each star image is also the sole content of a link to a PDP, so those links have no accessible name.
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wf…jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home…` have none.
  - The `imp` and `b.php` pixels have none. Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Asset filenames:** the banner is named `Spring2026`, and two other assets are named `0125_…` and `wfna_0331_…2024`. Reused or stale creative filenames suggest module assets were not refreshed. This is a hygiene note, since I can't tell from the source whether the rendered content is stale.
- **Custom font:** `@font-face "Sofia"` loads a `.woff` with `mso-font-alt: "Arial"`, so there is a fallback. Many clients ignore web fonts anyway.
- **Truncated HTML:** I could not verify the viewport meta, `lang`, preheader or dark-mode handling. The visible CSS has mobile breakpoints at 480px and 320px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied source or QA output. `_eml` differs per link while `_emr=bf122e30-…` is constant. That looks like a per-link ID plus a per-recipient ID, not a merge failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool notes the relay may strip them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so confirm against the raw message headers before treating this as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. From `editor@members.wayfair.com`, check alignment against the `members.` subdomain.
- **Body unsubscribe link:** the QA tool says one is always probed, but no result was shown for it. I could not confirm its presence or status from the data given.
- **Physical address:** the footer was truncated, so I could not verify it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** on any link. Attribution relies on Wayfair's own parameters: `refid=MKTEML_140918`, `emlid=101`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=10`, `csnid`, `wfcs=cs7`, `maiid=16490`. This is consistent across links and presumably intentional. It won't register in a standard GA UTM report without mapping.
- **Recipient IDs in URLs:** `_emr` and `_eml` identify the recipient in every URL. Forwarded or shared links carry that identity.
- **Landing alignment:** the banner (`/daily-sales/72-hour-clearout`) and the "Shop Sale" link (`/daily-sales`) match the "ENDS TONIGHT" subject. The PDP links are pergolas, and `area-rug-clearout` is a separate sale. Whether those fit the offer is a content question, not a technical one.
- **Product link params:** PDP links add `mplid`, `sku` and `brcid`, so product-level tracking is intact.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the banner (for example, "72-hour clearout: up to 60% off, ends tonight"). Give the star images alt text like "4.5 out of 5 stars", or move the rating text outside the image.
2. Set `alt=""` on all tracking and ad pixels, and serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS.
3. Change the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. Make the `c=` value match the send type (batch, not Triggered).
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message at the receiving mailbox, not through the relay.
5. Re-run link probing with throttling and retries for the 429 hosts and the 4 unprobed PDP and area-rug URLs. Add the unsubscribe URL result to the report.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that analytics maps `refid` and `emlid`.
7. Audit the banner and button assets (`Spring2026`, `0125_…`, `wfna_0331_…2024`) for stale reuse.
