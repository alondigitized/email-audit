# 🙌 BLACK FRIDAY 🙌 The sale of the SEASON 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a real Black Friday blast, and it's a competent one. A purple banner reads "up to 80% off / BLACK FRIDAY" with the Sealy co-brand. Below it, a 2x2 grid of red percentage tiles covers Christmas trees (45%), area rugs (55%), curtains (70%) and bedding (70%). The offers are legible and the hierarchy is clear. It's a better fit for the "BLACK FRIDAY" subject than the single-category sends of the past two days, such as "KITCHEN MATS for less" and "POPCORN MACHINES."
- The weak point is the middle. After "picked just for you," the email shows a wall of pergolas, six tiles in a row with no captions, prices or category labels. The pergola grid appears again lower down. That reads as a personalization algorithm stuck on one product, not as curation, and it pushes the "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" banner and the brand deals far down the page. The subject's emoji stacking and the repeated "sale of the season" language are also the same blast Wayfair has sent all week, since "BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE" on 11-19.
- Verdict: a solid event email with a clear offer, undermined by a repetitive, uncaptioned pergola block and a very long scroll. It's a 6 or 7, as on past sends.

## What's working

- The "up to 80% off BLACK FRIDAY" banner sits at the top with the Sealy co-brand, so the event is clear at a glance.
- The red "up to X% OFF" tiles are high-contrast and easy to scan in the opening 2x2 grid.
- The full-width purple "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" banner puts a concrete shipping offer in the middle of the page.
- The "deals on big-name brands & more" list is clean and specific: Moen spend $350 save $75, buy one get one 15% off dining chairs, and Sealy up to 60% off.
- The footer carries the rewards, Pay Over Time and app-download modules, which gives loyalty and convenience options.

## What's weak

- Six pergola tiles under "picked just for you" have no caption, price or category label, only star ratings and an occasional "Sale" tag. The same pergola block repeats later in the email.
- "Picked just for you" feels untrue when the content is one product type repeated.
- The email is very long, about 6,300px at full size. The free-shipping and brand-deal sections sit well below the first screen.
- There is no single primary button. The only CTAs are the tile images and a small "shop all sales" link near the bottom.
- The "$40 off your first order" credit card offer and its fine print cut against the honest-offer feel, and "*Some exclusions apply" adds a hedge on the shipping claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the repeated pergola grid with a mixed, captioned set of 4 to 6 products from different categories, each with a price or "% off" label. This one change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add one clear button under the hero, such as "Shop Black Friday deals," so the primary action is obvious.
- 3. Move the "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE*" banner and the brand deals above the product grid, and cut the length by a third.
- 4. Drop the second pergola block entirely. If the credit card offer stays, shrink it.
- 5. Tighten the subject: fewer emoji, and name a real offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: up to 80% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs, trees & bedding up to 70% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Everything ships free. Christmas trees up to 45% off, rugs 55% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Moen, Sealy and more brand deals are live. Shop before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a large, templated Wayfair promotion with per-recipient tracking on every link. The QA data shows no confirmed broken links. The real issues are missing alt text on content images, about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and no UTM parameters. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429, which is rate limiting, not a failure. These include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/?...` ("Wayfair"), and the three clearance event pages (`christmas-tree-clearance~e408906.html`, `area-rug-clearance~e408846.html`, `curtain-%26-drape-clearance~e408856.html`). Their status is unverified, not failed.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links hit the 70s time budget and were not checked. They are `bedding-clearance~e408850.html` and three pergola product URLs (`Outdoor-Louvered-Pergola-...`, `Erommy-Louvered-Pergola-...`, `-Aluminum-Pergola-with-Adjustable-Louvered-Roof-...`). 57 of 69 links were skipped by design, so coverage is about 17%.
- **Per-link tracking IDs:** Each link carries a unique `_eml=` value. The app link appears twice with different values (`_eml=2f2f1bee-...` and `_eml=a9397929-...`) for the same `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` destination. This is normal for click attribution, but it means link-level dedupe and caching won't work.
- **Insecure deep-link fallback:** The OneLink URL has `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Its web fallback is plain `http://`, so it depends on a server-side redirect to HTTPS.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) is served over HTTPS but has no alt attribute.
- **Insecure ad-impression pixels:** There are 20 pixels at `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=a8b04c89-...`. They load over `http://`, so mail clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them and impression counts will be undercounted. The `p=` value matches the `_emr=a8b04c89-...` recipient ID, so each pixel carries a persistent per-recipient identifier over cleartext.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing alt text:** These are the hero and promo banners and the button images:
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`. This one is also wrapped in a link to `/daily-sales/`, so the link has no accessible name beyond the filename, as the QA text shows: `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-Gener]`.
  - `..._Email_Skinny-EverythingShips`
  - `..._Email_Skinny-BigBrandNames.jpg`
  - `..._Email_Skinny-WFCC.jpg`
  - `1119_BFCM_PHASE2-Email-1xButton-35` through `-39-WFUS.jpg`
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`

  When images are blocked, these show nothing, and screen readers get no meaningful text.
- **Rating stars missing alt text:** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear about 15 times under `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`. The star rating is conveyed only by the image, so the product rating is lost to assistive tech and to image-blocked clients.
- **Tracking pixels:** The 20 `imp` pixels also lack alt text. Alt is not a meaningful concern for 2x1 tracking pixels, but `alt=""` is the correct markup so screen readers skip them.
- **Source markup:** The email uses an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with `@media (max-width: 480px)` responsive rules and an MSO font fallback (`mso-font-alt: "Arial"`). No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the full markup was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `*|...|*`). Tracking IDs are fully populated (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`). Only the truncated HTML was reviewed, so tokens in the rest of the body can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, so it can't be called a Wayfair defect. Verify against the raw message at the original receiving MTA.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** Not found. The QA note says this is expected through the relay, so SPF/DKIM status is unknown. No evidence of failure.
- **From address:** `editor@members.wayfair.com` is a sending subdomain of the brand domain. The tracking domains `t.wayfair.com` and `li.wayfair.com` are also on the brand domain, which is good for alignment.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The QA info says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported, and the footer is outside the truncated source. Not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters.** None of the links use `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Attribution relies on Wayfair's proprietary parameters: `source=batch`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_143902`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16782`, `wfcs=cs7`. This is consistent across links, but a standard analytics tool such as GA won't attribute the sessions by campaign without a custom mapping.
- **Parameter consistency:** All links share the same `csnid`, `varid=7`, `batchid=2` and `refid`. Event pages add `eid=` and `unifiedeventid=` with matching values (636015, 635985, 635990).
- **Landing page alignment:** The links point to `/daily-sales` and `/daily-sales/<event>~e<id>.html` for the clearance categories, which matches the sale and clearance framing. The destinations couldn't be fetched because of the 429s, so the landing content wasn't checked.
- **App link:** The OneLink URL uses `af_dp=wayfairapp://home`, which sends app users to the home screen, not to the sale. This is a deep-link specificity gap, not an error.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. At minimum cover the hero banner (also the link text for `/daily-sales/`), the three skinny banners, and the six `1xButton` images. Use `alt=""` on the 2x1 tracking pixels.
2. Give the star-rating images alt text such as "4.5 out of 5 stars", or render the rating as text.
3. Change `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` to `https://`. Confirm that the `af_web_dp` fallback also uses `https://`.
4. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that the proprietary `refid`/`csnid` parameters are mapped in the analytics stack.
5. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or a longer budget before calling them valid.
6. For compliance, inspect the raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) outside the AgentMail relay to settle the three WARNs. Also check the body footer for the unsubscribe link and physical address.
7. For QA tooling, treat 429 as "unverified" rather than as a warning that counts against the pass rate. The reported 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings) mostly reflects skipped and rate-limited links, not confirmed defects.
