# 🎉 BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE 🎉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a proper Black Friday blast, and the render sells the event well. A purple banner reads "up to 80% off / BLACK FRIDAY" with a Sealy co-brand. Below it sits a clean 2x2 grid of big red percentage tiles: 80% area rugs, 45% Christmas trees, 70% bedding, 70% curtains & drapes. The eye lands on the numbers first, which is the right call. It is much more offer-forward than the earlier "Pergolas for less" and "On sale now: rugs" sends.
- The email then loses focus. A "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" block opens a "picked just for you" grid, and every tile in it is a pergola or gazebo. The same pergola grid repeats lower down. That is roughly 12 near-identical pergola photos in a Black Friday email whose headline categories are rugs, trees and bedding. The "just for you" label feels false when it is one product category on loop. The page is also long, and it ends in a footer with a credit card offer and fine print.
- Verdict: strong top third, with a mid-section that wastes its space on repeated pergolas. It is a decent send, but not a targeted one.

## What's working

- The 2x2 tile grid with large red "80% OFF", "45% OFF" and "70% OFF" figures is instantly scannable, and each tile has its own button.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY" banner with "up to 80% off" gives the event clear context at the top.
- The "deals on big-name brands & more" list (Moen: spend $350, save $75; Sealy: up to 60% off) gives specific, concrete offers.
- The "24 HRS ONLY flash deals" header adds credible urgency.
- Star ratings under the product tiles add some social proof.

## What's weak

- The "picked just for you" grid is all pergolas and gazebos, and it repeats as a second pergola block. This is the same creative rut as the 11-19 "Pergolas for less" send.
- The tiles show "up to" percentages, so there is no actual price. The "Select items only" notes under every brand deal soften the offers.
- The $40-off credit card banner sits in the middle of the email and interrupts the deal flow. Its fine print is also tiny.
- The email is very long, with five or more distinct sections, so the hero offer is diluted before the footer.
- The flash deals header promises time-limited deals but gives no deadline or product to match.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the pergola grids to one short row. Replace them with products from the headline categories (rugs, Christmas trees, bedding) so the "picked just for you" label is true.
- 2. Put the flash deal in the first screen, with a real countdown or end time.
- 3. Show actual prices, such as "Rugs from $39", on the top tiles.
- 4. Move the credit card offer to the footer area.
- 5. Trim the email to about half its length.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: rugs up to 80% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hrs only: up to 80% off rugs, trees & bedding`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flash deals on rugs, bedding & more, ending tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 70% off bedding and curtains. Plus Sealy and Moen deals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email shows no confirmed hard failures: the QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings, and the 57% pass rate reflects warnings only. The real problems are widespread missing alt text, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and a campaign-parameter inconsistency on the app deep link. The compliance headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 56 skipped by design. 4 more went unprobed when the 70s budget ran out, including `/flashdeals?_emr=…` and both pergola product links (`Erommy-Louvered-Pergola…`, `111S-Aluminum-Pergola…`). Their status is unknown.
- **All 8 reported probe failures are HTTP 429, not 404 or 5xx.** They are the app link (twice), `/daily-sales`, `/`, `/daily-sales/`, and the area rug, Christmas tree and bedding clearance links. This is bot rate limiting by Wayfair, so the links are probably valid, but none were confirmed to resolve.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 `<img>` pixels use plain `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=bf7f4a40-…`. They have no alt text and are non-HTTPS, so clients that block mixed content may drop them and impression data will be undercounted. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` is HTTPS but also has no alt attribute.
- **Mixed campaign labels on the app link.** `https://wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?…&c=Triggered&…&source=batch&batchid=2` labels the campaign `Triggered` while the same URL says `source=batch`. This is a bulk send, so attribution in AppsFlyer will likely be misclassified.
- **Insecure web fallback.** The app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is `http://`, which forces a redirect hop.
- **Hero link has no accessible name.** The banner link's text resolves to `[image: WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-Gener…]`, which is a filename because the image has no alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (10):**
  - `WFUS_1119_BFCM_PHASE2_SkinnyTop-BF-General.jpg`
  - `…Email_Skinny-FlashDeals.jpg`
  - `…Skinny-WFCC.jpg`

  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  
  If these banners and buttons carry offer text or CTAs, they are invisible with images off or to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on 11 rating images:** `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` under `secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`. The star ratings are not conveyed as text.
- **Spacer and tracking pixels (21):** 20 `imp` pixels and `b.php` lack `alt=""`. This is minor but easy to fix.
- **Mobile name truncation.** In the visible `<style>` block, `.ProductRec-name { word-break: break-all; overflow: hidden; height: 48px !important }` at max-width 480px will break product names mid-word and clip them.
- **Stale asset.** `0125_48hourclearance-…` looks like a January 2025 asset reused in a November 2026 send. This is inferred from the filename only and is not confirmed.
- The source is truncated, so the rest of the body (including the footer) was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or URLs. The per-recipient IDs `_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid` and `skurectxid` are all populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` are unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer not verified.** The unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result is not shown. The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Tracking is proprietary (`_emr`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=batch`, `batchid=2`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_143879`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16768`). This is not a defect if Wayfair's own analytics consume these, but third-party analytics will not attribute the traffic as email.
- **Per-link `_eml` IDs on landing pages.** Each link carries a different `_eml`, for example `…4a9d4578…` on `/daily-sales` and `…9ad26eb8…` shared by the three clearance links. This makes click-level attribution possible.
- **Landing page alignment.** Landing alignment was not verified, because 429s blocked content checks. The `/daily-sales/` hero (trailing slash) and `/daily-sales` CTA resolve to the same path, but the redirect behavior is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check raw headers.** Pull the original message and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `members.wayfair.com`.
2. **Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS** and add `alt=""` to all pixels.
3. **Add descriptive alt text** to the 10 banner and button images and the 11 star images, for example "4.5 out of 5 stars".
4. **Fix the app link.** Change `c=Triggered` to a batch-appropriate value and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
5. **Re-probe the 4 unchecked links** and the 8 rate-limited ones with a slower request rate or a different user agent, to confirm the 429s are not hiding real failures.
6. **Replace `word-break: break-all` on `.ProductRec-name`** with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `-webkit-line-clamp`.
7. **Add `utm_source=email&utm_medium=…&utm_campaign=…`** if cross-platform analytics need to attribute this traffic.
