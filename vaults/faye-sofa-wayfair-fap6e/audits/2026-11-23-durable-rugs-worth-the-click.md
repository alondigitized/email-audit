# 👀 DURABLE RUGS worth the click
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is another Wayfair Black Friday blast with a rug-themed subject on top. The subject promises "DURABLE RUGS," but the email delivers one small tile. It's the top-left tile in the "score styles you ❤️" grid, captioned "Durable Rugs Just For You »". It shows a vintage-style rug under a sideboard. Three other tiles share that grid: pergolas, kitchen mats and garden statues.
- The rest is a long scroll of unrelated content. There's a full-width AllModern mid-century modern takeover, a bathroom vanity feature and a ten-tile "let's complete your space" grid. The grid mixes comforter sets, Christmas trees, bar stools, lamps, knives, shuffleboards and curtains. The only offer is the purple Sealy co-brand "BLACK FRIDAY up to 80% OFF" banner. The only real button is AllModern's "Shop This + More at allmodern.com."
- It's the same template as the 11/21 "KITCHEN MATS" and 11/22 "5x8 shag rugs under $75" sends, with the subject swapped. A subscriber who has seen the first tile of this grid change three days running will notice. I'd call it competent but generic, and it doesn't earn the "worth the click" promise.

## What's working

- The Sealy-branded "BLACK FRIDAY up to 80% OFF" banner is at the very top and states the offer plainly.
- The rug tile sits in the first position of the first grid, so it appears without scrolling.
- The render is clean: no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.
- Wayfair's branding is consistent and trusted, with purple section headers and clear category captions.
- The Rewards, Financing, Professional and Exclusive Brands icons and the app QR block give some retention hooks.

## What's weak

- The subject's "DURABLE RUGS" gets one tile out of about 14. Nothing in the email explains why these rugs are "durable."
- There are no prices, discounts or star ratings on any product tile. "Up to 80%" is the only number, and it isn't tied to rugs.
- The AllModern takeover is a huge dead zone for a rug shopper. It's a mid-century dining room with a "Shop This" button that leaves Wayfair.
- Primary CTAs are weak. Tiles are captioned category links with a "»", and there is no button for rugs.
- There's no deadline or urgency beyond the Black Friday label, and this is the fourth or fifth near-identical send in three days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make rugs the hero. Replace the 2x2 grid with a rug-only block of 4–6 tiles. Each tile should show a price, a "was" price and a star rating, with a "Shop rugs under $X" button above them. This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a durability angle that matches the subject. Use proof points such as "stain-resistant," "machine-washable" or "pet-proof," with review counts.
- 3. Cut or move the AllModern block below the fold. It's off-category and pushes the content that matters down the page.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Black Friday deals end Monday," and a real offer, such as "rugs up to 60% off."
- 5. Vary the first tile and grid across sends so the template doesn't read as the same email with a new subject.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a table-based template with responsive CSS, but the QA run found 20 non-HTTPS ad-impression pixels, many content images without alt text, and an inconsistent app deep-link. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** All 9 probe warnings are HTTP 429 rate limits (wayfair.com, allmodern.com, wayfairapp.onelink.me). The report says these "may be valid", so they are not failures.
- **3 links were never checked.** The probe hit its 70s budget, so these are neither pass nor fail:
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/our-best-kitchen-mats-for-less-se205`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-garden-statues-se73905`
  
  The report shows these URLs cut off mid-path, so they should be re-checked in full. Only 12 of 60 links were probed in total (48 skipped).
- **Mixed content: 20 tracking pixels use `http://`.** They are `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`, `p=d19b41d5-...`). Clients may block or proxy them, which would under-count impressions.
- **The app deep-link's web fallback is also non-HTTPS:** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` on the `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` link.
- **Campaign labeling is inconsistent on the OneLink URL.** It has `c=Triggered` but `source=graymatter_batch` and `batchid=15`. A batch send is labeled as a triggered campaign, which will skew attribution in AppsFlyer.
- **The open pixel is fine:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS. It is flagged only for missing alt text.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` is not found.** It is not confirmed missing, because the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` is not found.** The same caveat applies. Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The sender is `editor@members.wayfair.com`. Don't treat this as a failure.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical address could not be confirmed.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is listed. The footer was truncated from the supplied HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **There are no UTM parameters.** Links use proprietary tracking instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121678`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16804`, `mdlid`. This is consistent across links, but GA/UTM-based attribution won't see campaign data.
- **The subject-to-link match is good.** "DURABLE RUGS" maps to `/daily-sales/filters/durable-rugs-just-for-you-se78332.html?eid=554377&unifiedeventid=554377`.
- **The hero and header images link to generic destinations.** They go to `wayfair.com/` and `/daily-sales` rather than the rugs event, so the subject's promise doesn't carry through.
- **One link leaves the Wayfair domain.** `allmodern.com` (`end_banner.jpg`) is a sister brand, with `dcvrid=126941` added.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text to the 8 content images**, especially the 3 that act as link text. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. **Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.**
3. **Fix `c=Triggered` on the OneLink URL** to match the batch campaign.
4. **Verify the headers against the raw MIME message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. **Re-run link probing with throttling or a longer budget.** This covers the 429s and the 3 unchecked pergola, kitchen-mat and garden-statue links, and should include the unsubscribe link.
6. **Add UTM parameters, or map `source` and `batchid` into the analytics tool,** so email-to-site attribution works.
7. **Point the hero image links at the rugs event URL** instead of `/daily-sales`.
