# Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair runs its product-noun playbook again — but "8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS" is a notch above the usual formula because it buries a real dimension spec in the subject, which is the one signal a genuine outdoor-rug shopper would respond to. That specificity earns the open.
- The email doesn't honor it. The hero gesture toward outdoor rugs (the top banner does show rug imagery), but the body collapses almost immediately into "Mix, Cozy & Chill - for Less," a generic multi-category product grid labeled "top picks. low prices.," and then a "Cool down…" section pushing what appear to be fans or AC units at 60% off. None of that is 8x10 outdoor rugs. A shopper who opened expecting to see curated 8' × 10' options lands in the same undifferentiated dump Wayfair has sent every day this week.
- Call it what it is: a well-labeled box with the wrong contents inside. Better than the BAKING SHEETS bait-and-switch (July 4, 5/10) because the rug hero at least exists, but the multi-category sprawl still kills click intent for anyone who opened because of the dimensions.

## What's weak

- **Body doesn't deliver the 8' × 10' promise.** No rug with those dimensions is visibly featured or called out in the grid — opener gets another category dump.
- **"Cool down…" / AC section is completely off-subject.** An air-cooling section mid-email breaks any outdoor rug narrative Wayfair was building.

- 1. **Show an actual 8' × 10' rug in the hero with its price.** One hero product, one SKU, one click. If you're going to promise a dimension in the subject, the first image in the body should be that exact product with size and price labeled.
- 2. **Cut the "Cool down" section or move it to a secondary email.** AC units and fans have nothing to do with why someone opened this. It burns scroll real estate and signals Wayfair just reskinned a template.
- 3. **Replace "Mix, Cozy & Chill - for Less" with an outdoor rug size-guide CTA.** "Shop 8×10 | 5×8 | 4×6" — size-filtered landing pages convert better than category homepages for a shopper who came in on a specific dimension.
- 4. **Add a credible deadline.** Even "Summer pricing through July 8" is better than no urgency at all.
