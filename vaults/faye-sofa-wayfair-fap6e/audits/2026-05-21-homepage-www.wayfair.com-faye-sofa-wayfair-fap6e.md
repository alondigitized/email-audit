# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the second I opened this I saw "Memorial Day Clearance — up to 70% OFF" and my thumb was already moving toward it. This is very much a sale-first homepage — Wayfair knows their audience refreshes Way Day deals like a stock ticker, and Memorial Day is their warm-up lap. What's missing is anything that makes me feel like they *know* I'm looking for a Japandi-ish credenza and not just randomly hunting for Dutch ovens.

## What's working

- **The urgency banner ("UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA →")** — slim, punchy, scrolls me right into deal mode the second the page loads.
- **The hero carousel percentage treatment** — that oversized "70% OFF" in that color-block layout with the gingham red-white-blue background reads immediately. I know what's happening here before I've read a word.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — unambiguous, centered, no confusion about what to do next.
- **Product cards below the fold** — the partial bed card is catching my eye. Storage bed? Upholstered? I want to know more.
- **Search bar placement** — "Find anything home…" is right at the top. If the sale items aren't my thing, I can pivot in one tap.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty/rewards visibility** — I have a Wayfair account with order history. This page treats me like a cold stranger. A "your rewards balance" or "members get an extra X%" hook would have me in checkout in 30 seconds flat.
- **"Up to 70% off" is doing heavy lifting** — I've been burned enough times to know this usually means one throw pillow is 70% off and the sofa I want is 12%. A floor discount or median discount would feel more honest.
- **No style filtering signals above the fold** — I lean modern-mid-century. This page gives me zero indication whether the clearance skews farmhouse, ultra-modern, grandma-chic, or my style. That Dutch oven in the product card doesn't help.
- **No deadline** — "Memorial Day Clearance" implies urgency, but I see no countdown, no "ends May 26" — the FOMO isn't complete.
- **Zero social proof visible** — no review counts, no "X people bought this," no star ratings on the partial cards I can see.

## Recommendations

- **Add a style-filter strip below the hero** — four chips: Modern, Mid-Century, Farmhouse, Minimalist. Takes one tap to narrow from 2 million SKUs to the 40 I actually want. Shippable next sprint.
- **Show a logged-in rewards hook** — swap the thin promo banner for "Your rewards: $XX — apply at checkout" for returning users. Instant engagement bump.
- **Put a credible deadline on the hero** — "Clearance ends May 26" with a small countdown. The gingham pattern already screams patriotic holiday — close the loop on urgency.
- **Replace the Dutch oven product card with a seating or case goods card** — furniture is the high-AOV category. Lead with the thing that makes me spend $800, not $40.
