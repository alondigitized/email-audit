# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a rerun of the exact template Wayfair used the last two "pergolas" sends this week — generic "BIG-TIME BARGAINS" hero, then a 2x2 grid of unrelated deals (privacy screens, outdoor furniture, area rugs), followed by a long, undifferentiated scroll of clearance categories. The good news, again, is the subject-to-content match: "On sale now: pergolas" actually maps to the top-left tile, a real pergola photo with matching link copy. That's the one honest thread running through this send.
- Past that, it's the same soft-focus clearance blast the brand has run all week, just with dressers, mattresses, dog food storage, and wallpaper swapped in for canopies or china cabinets. There's no specific pergola price, no pergola category page depth, and no urgency — it's one photo doing all the work for the subject line, surrounded by 15+ other categories competing for attention.
- Judgement: functional but lazy. It clears the bar of "not a bait-and-switch" that plagued earlier sends, but it still doesn't reward someone who opened specifically for pergolas with any deeper pergola content or a real offer.

## What's working

- Subject line "pergolas" matches the actual top-left tile — a real pergola photo, correctly labeled "On sale now: pergolas »".
- Percentage-off badges (50%, 70%) are visually consistent and easy to scan across the grid.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- Loyalty/membership touchpoints (Wayfair Rewards, Flexible Financing, Wayfair Professional) are visible near the footer for repeat shoppers.

## What's weak

- No pergola-specific price, discount, or size/material detail anywhere — just a photo and a link, no reason to click beyond curiosity.
- Hero banner ("BIG-TIME BARGAINS") has zero connection to the subject line or the pergola category — it's generic seasonal filler.
- The email is enormous (15+ unrelated categories: dressers, mailboxes, dog food storage, wallpaper) which buries the one on-subject item after a single small tile.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email despite "on sale now" framing.
- Same visual template and structure as 2026-08-31 and 2026-08-30 sends — creative fatigue is visible if you're tracking the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the subject-line product its own dedicated hero section with a real price/discount, not just a shared grid tile — if you're going to promise "pergolas," lead with a full-width pergola hero and an actual % off.
- 2. Cut the number of secondary categories in half; a 15-category kitchen-sink email dilutes the one relevant reason someone opened.
- 3. Add a credible deadline ("Ends Monday," "48 hours only") to convert browsing into urgency.
- 4. Vary the template from the last two sends — same purple/lavender grid, same "Ways to Get More Bang for Your Buck" module, same footer stack, three days running.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair batch-triggered marketing template using the Unbounce/mcc.php-style link decoration and pixel-based tracking. QA scan shows 3 warnings (compliance/deliverability headers unconfirmed via relay) and widespread missing alt text plus insecure image sources on tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed CTA/body links carry consistent tracking params (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `brcid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`, `refid=MKTEML_121975`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16086`) — internally consistent decoration scheme, no malformed URLs observed.
- 8 links returned HTTP 429 (rate limited) during probing, including the primary App CTA (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157...`), the subject-line CTA (`daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now...`), and several PDP/category links. QA notes these are likely valid but unconfirmed — cannot verify destination validity from this scan.
- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (70s cap), including the "closeout" and "clearance" daily-sales links.
- 20 tracking-pixel requests to `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` (sequential `s=` values) all use `http://` — see Rendering & Accessibility for HTTPS issue.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 7 content images missing `alt` text: `wfus_0331_btb_2024_email_01_static.jpg`, `wfus_0331_btb_2024_email_03_static.jpg`, `stars_9.png`, `wfna_0331_btb_2024_email_05_static.jpg`, `wfus_0331_btb_2024_email_07/08/09_static.jpg`. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will render no fallback text for these.
- `b.php` open-tracking pixel and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?...` beacons also missing `alt` (expected for pixels, but flagged by scan).
- 20 `imp` tracking pixels load over `http://` (non-HTTPS) — will trigger mixed-content blocking in HTTPS mail clients (Gmail, Outlook.com), silently dropping open/impression data for that vendor.
- Extensive mobile-responsive CSS (`@media max-width:480px/320px`) present and structurally sound (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, Outlook-specific fixes via `mso-font-alt` and `data-outlook-cycle`).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) observed in the visible HTML/QA output — no issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat; if genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of their 2024 sender guidelines.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — cannot verify authentication status; recommend checking directly at the source MTA rather than via relay.
- QA confirms an unsubscribe link is present in the footer (always included per probing methodology), so a mailto/HTTP unsubscribe mechanism exists, just unconfirmed as one-click/header-based.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema, not standard UTM params (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) — internally consistent but not standard-UTM based, so any GA/UTM-based attribution tooling would need custom parsing for `refid`/`maiid`/`emlid`.
- Subject line "On sale now: pergolas" correctly maps to a matching CTA link (`daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.html`) — subject/landing alignment confirmed at the URL level (destination itself unconfirmed due to 429).

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images and `stars_9.png` (e.g., "5 out of 5 stars") for accessibility compliance.
- Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking and impression-data loss in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
- Re-probe the 8 rate-limited (429) links and 4 unprobed links directly against the sending domain (bypassing relay) to confirm destination validity before next send.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` directly at the originating MTA, since relay-based capture may be masking headers that are actually present.
