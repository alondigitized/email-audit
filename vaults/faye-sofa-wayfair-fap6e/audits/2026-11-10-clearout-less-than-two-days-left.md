# 📅 CLEAROUT 📅 Less than TWO DAYS LEFT 📅 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent Wayfair clearout send, but it never matches its subject. The subject says "Less than TWO DAYS LEFT," while the hero banner says "72-HR CLEAROUT, 60% OFF." Nothing in the render counts down or names a deadline, so the urgency is asserted rather than shown. The 60% headline is clear and sits at the top. It's the only thing that carries the email.
- Below the banner the email turns into a long product-and-category scroll. A "picked just for you" grid of priced products is followed by a purple tiled block of category discounts (60% rugs, 60% bedding, 50% seasonal decor, 30% mattresses, 55% curtains, 40% seating, 50% bedroom, 45% mirrors). A second product grid, a "shop all sales" bar, a "Your Home, These Picks" section and an app promo follow. The category tiles are the best-designed part, but the length dilutes the single offer. Judgment: solid, offer-forward, generic, and no sharper than the 11/09 "72 hours only: UP TO 60% OFF" send.

## What's working

- The red "72-HR CLEAROUT, 60% OFF" banner is bold and reads instantly.
- The purple category tiles pair a big "up to X% OFF" with a labeled arrow CTA ("area rugs →", "bedding →"), so each one is scannable and tappable.
- Product tiles show "Was / NOW" pricing and star ratings, which gives price anchoring and social proof.
- The "shop all sales" bar gives a clear catch-all CTA.
- The Wayfair Rewards and Pay Over Time icons surface member and financing benefits.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Less than TWO DAYS LEFT," but the render shows "72-HR," with no end date or timer. The two messages don't line up.
- The "picked just for you" product grids have no visible product names. The prices are tiny and hard to read at mobile width.
- The email runs too long and has many competing CTAs. The "Your Home, These Picks" block (pet-friendly rugs, pergolas, shoe racks) feels like leftover catch-all content and dilutes the clearout message.
- The discount ladder is inconsistent. "30% off mattresses & more" sits next to 60% tiles, and every figure is an "up to", so the real discount is vague.
- The footer is dense with fine print, and the app-download block is cramped.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the urgency concrete. Put an end date and time, or a countdown, in the banner, and keep the subject and hero language identical.
- 2. Cut the email to the banner, the category tiles and one product grid. Drop the "Your Home, These Picks" block.
- 3. Add product names and larger prices to the product tiles. Lead with the best-discounted items.
- 4. Add a single dominant CTA button under the banner, such as "Shop the clearout."
- 5. Move the Rewards and Pay Over Time perks above the fold or into a slim strip.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearout ends Wednesday: up to 60% off rugs & bedding`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ 48 hours left: up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, bedding, curtains and seating, marked down until Wednesday at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on most orders, plus deals up to 60% off. Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules. It has no confirmed broken links or unrendered merge tokens. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on content images, about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and a campaign-parameter mismatch on the app deep link. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** All 7 probe warnings are HTTP 429 (rate limiting), not 404/5xx. Examples are `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"), `https://www.wayfair.com/?_emr=...` ("Wayfair"), and the product URLs `...L6477-K~id-QC78979522.html`, `...L7499-K~id-BQ30004200.html` and `...L74-K~id-BA15002663.html`. Their status is unknown.
- **4 links were not probed** because of the 70s time budget: the wine and beverage cooler URL, `...L6-K~id-BY70005052.html`, the Tilefy subway tile URL, and `/daily-sales/area-rug-clearout~e417428.html?eid=640626`. These are neither pass nor fail.
- **Insecure link:** the hero image link is `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` (not HTTPS). It likely 301-redirects, but it adds an avoidable redirect hop.
- **Mixed protocol in the app link:** the Onelink URL embeds `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. That is an HTTP fallback.
- **Campaign parameter mismatch:** the Onelink URL has `c=Triggered`, but the other links carry `source=batch&batchid=3`. A batch send labeled "Triggered" will skew AppsFlyer campaign attribution.
- **Per-link IDs:** `_eml` differs on every link (for example `...2dac72d1...` versus `...46019e7b...`, both on the same "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" CTA). This is expected per-link tracking. The two are the same CTA, so it only matters if reporting aggregates by `_eml`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:**
  - The hero image `72HR_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png` is also the click target for `/daily-sales`. With images off, the link renders as an empty or filename-labeled link.
  - The promo banners `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...` have no alt text.
  - 12 `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` rating images (`secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../review_stars/`) have no alt text. Each is wrapped in the product link, so the QA probe labeled those links `[image: stars_10.png]`. A screen reader gets no rating or link purpose.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (low severity):** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (open pixel) and 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `124126019` pixels have no alt. These should carry `alt=""` as decorative.
- **HTTP image sources:** all 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...&sz=2x1` pixels use HTTP. Gmail's image proxy typically rewrites them, but other clients may block them as mixed content. The ad-impression beacons would then not fire.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** `lang` attribute, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the dark-mode meta tags.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `*|...|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Recipient-specific values are resolved in the URLs (`_eml`, `_emr`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`). The body after truncation could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself flags this as "may not be captured by AgentMail relay," so it is unconfirmed. It cannot be treated as a sender defect without the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain separate from the root brand domain, so alignment should be checked on the raw message.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it. The footer is cut off in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body elements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses a first-party scheme instead: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_143112`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16703`. This works for internal analytics but will not populate GA4 or other third-party tools unless they are mapped. This is a note, not a defect.
- **Parameters are consistent across links.** `_emr`, `csnid`, `refid`, `emlid` and `maiid` are identical on all probed links, so batch-level attribution is intact.
- **Landing-page alignment** (offer versus destination) could not be checked because the 429s blocked content verification.
- **App deep link:** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` lands on the app home, not on the sale. The web fallback is `/the-wayfair-app`, so this CTA sends users to a generic page, not the clearance offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the hero and promo banners. Give the star ratings meaningful alt (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars"). Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Change the hero link and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Correct `c=Triggered` on the Onelink URL to match the batch send, or confirm the value is intentional.
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or a longer budget to clear the 7 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed ones.
5. Re-verify the compliance and authentication items against the raw `.eml` (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before logging them as defects.
6. Consider adding `utm_*` mapping if downstream analytics outside Wayfair's own tooling need it.
