- This is the rare Wayfair send this week where the subject actually matches the hero — "BEDDING up to 60% OFF" opens directly on a tile reading "Bedding up to 60% off / bedding clearance" with a stack of folded linens. - Past that first row, the email sprawls into generic "Must-Grab Deal" tiles (a nightstand, mattress, sconce, tumblers, desk, cushion) with no bedding presence at all, then a "Scores in Your Style" grid of rugs, dressers, grills, nursery items, and gazebos. - No render bugs, no broken images, clean layout — better execution than several recent sends. But the subject-to-content ratio is thin: one tile out of fourteen actually delivers on the subject line.

## What's working

- Subject and hero tile agree for once — "BEDDING up to 60% OFF" opens on an actual bedding clearance tile with a visible discount and product photo.
- No broken images or layout glitches anywhere in the render — a genuine improvement over this week's aquarium and dining-chair sends.
- Clear percentage-off callouts on every category tile (60%, 40%, 35%, etc.) give concrete offer signals throughout.
- "Must-Grab Deal" tiles include star ratings and "recently purchased" counts, which read as light social proof.

## What's weak

- The bedding hero tile is one of four equally-weighted tiles in the top grid — outdoor screens, pergolas, and living room seating dilute the promised focus immediately.
- After the top row, bedding disappears entirely — six "Must-Grab" tiles and eight "Scores in Your Style" tiles cover furniture, kitchen, nursery, and outdoor with zero bedding follow-through.
- The email is a long, undifferentiated scroll of category tiles with no single strong CTA — the closest thing to a primary action is a generic "Snag 'Em ASAP" button near the bottom, disconnected from the bedding offer.
- Visual hierarchy doesn't prioritize the subject's promise — "These Top Steals Are Going Fast" and "Scores in Your Style" get equal or greater prominence than the bedding hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the render with a single full-width bedding hero (sheets/comforters/pillows), not a 4-up grid — if the subject says bedding, the first thing in view should be bedding, not privacy screens and pergolas.
- 2. Group at least 3-4 more bedding SKUs directly under the hero (comforter sets, sheets, pillows) before pivoting to other categories, so the email actually earns the subject line instead of using it as clickbait for a catalog dump.
- 3. Replace the generic "Snag 'Em ASAP" CTA with something bedding-specific like "Shop Bedding Deals" linking straight to the bedding clearance page.
- 4. Trim the "Scores in Your Style" grid or move it well below a proper bedding section — right now it out-competes the subject's category for screen real estate.
