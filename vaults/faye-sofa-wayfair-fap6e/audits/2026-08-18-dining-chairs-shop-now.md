# DINING CHAIRS | Shop Now >>
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair's subject reads "DINING CHAIRS | Shop Now >>" but the email opens with a generic "48HR $70% OFF" countdown banner and a "fresh-picked favorites" grid that's mostly not dining chairs — jars, a console table, a recliner, bar stools all appear before a proper dining set shows up. This is the same subject/hero disconnect this brand has run repeatedly (see 2026-08-15 "CHRISTMAS TREE SKIRTS," 2026-08-17 "OTTOMANS," 2026-08-14/17 "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS") — the subject over-promises a single category and the creative under-delivers on it.
- To be fair, the very first product tile does show a pair of dining chairs, and tile four is a round dining table with chairs, so there's at least a plausible link — better than the ottoman send that showed zero ottomans. But there's no dedicated "dining chairs" module or hero, just chairs buried in a mixed furniture grid. There's also a visible render bug near the bottom: a blank white block with a tiny placeholder icon sitting above the footer badges, echoing the broken-image issue seen in the 2026-08-16 "SLIPCOVERS" send.
- Net: mediocre, on-brand-for-Wayfair execution — a real but weak connection to the subject line, a countdown gimmick doing the actual attention-grabbing, and a broken image asset that shouldn't have shipped.

## What's working

- The lead product tile does show dining chairs, giving the subject line partial truth.
- A round dining table + chairs set appears in tile four, reinforcing the dining category.
- Clear countdown urgency ("48HR," "70% OFF") is visible immediately at the top without scrolling.
- Star ratings are shown under every product tile, adding light social proof.
- Purple section banners ("fresh-picked favorites," "let's complete your space," "shop before they're gone") give the long email clear visual structure.

## What's weak

- No dedicated dining-chairs hero or module — the subject's single-category promise dissolves into a mixed grid of jars, recliners, rugs, bar stools, and outdoor gear.
- A visible broken/blank image block sits just above the footer badges — a small placeholder icon on white, clearly an unrendered asset.
- The countdown banner ("48HR... 70% OFF") is generic clearance messaging disconnected from the dining chair narrative, so the top of the email doesn't reinforce the subject at all.
- The email is extremely long with 15+ unrelated product tiles (outdoor privacy screens, gazebos, recliners, chaise lounges), diluting the dining-chair focus promised in the subject.
- No explicit price or percent-off shown on the actual dining chair tile itself — the 70% off banner isn't tied to that specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic "48HR $70% OFF" hero with a dining-chair-specific hero image and price/percent-off callout — if the subject says dining chairs, the first thing in the email should be dining chairs at a stated discount.
- 2. Fix or remove the broken image block above the footer badges before this goes out again.
- 3. Group the dining chair and dining table tiles into their own labeled module (e.g., "Dining Chairs We Love") near the top instead of scattering them inside "fresh-picked favorites."
- 4. Trim the email — 15+ unrelated categories (gazebos, chaise lounges, privacy screens) undercut a subject that promised one specific product.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair dining chairs promo built on the Bee/Unbounce-style responsive email framework; core links resolve correctly where probed, but tracking-pixel delivery uses insecure HTTP and image accessibility is broadly non-compliant.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 64 links were probed (52 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); of those probed, 8 returned HTTP 429 (rate-limited by target server, not necessarily broken) — includes the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," and the "Vannie Upholstered Solid Wood Side Dining Chair" product link.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to a 70s time budget, including both `wayfair-product-L443-K~W113370810.html` and `Jax-Glass-Highball-14-oz-L1235-K~RXK10071.html` occurrences (2 instances each) — status unconfirmed, neither pass nor fail.
- 20 tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently degrade open/impression tracking.
- Consistent query-param structure (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `refid=MKTEML_138962`, `maiid=15996`, etc.) across all sampled links indicates functioning tracking-token injection at the platform level.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 22+ `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, spanning hero creative (`48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_SkinnyHero-VP.png`), SKU header art, star-rating icons (`stars_10.png`/`stars_11.png`, repeated 10+ times), the loyalty disclaimer image, and all 20 HTTP tracking pixels.
- One asset filename contains a literal `%` character (`0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`) — unencoded `%` in a URL path is invalid per RFC 3986 and may fail to load in strict clients.
- Responsive framework (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, etc.) and Outlook-specific fixes (`data-outlook-cycle`, `u-OutlookAppStackingFix`) are present, indicating standard cross-client scaffolding — no structural rendering defects observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization syntax found in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe): not found — same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results** header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status is unknown, not failed; likely stripped by the relay rather than absent at origin.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per QA probing notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), satisfying the baseline CAN-SPAM requirement at the content level, though header-level one-click compliance is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — internally consistent but not UTM-based, so cross-platform (e.g. GA) attribution depends on Wayfair's own resolution of this schema.

- App-download CTA correctly routes through an attribution deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with `af_web_dp` fallback to `wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app` — proper deep-link/fallback pairing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Convert the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels to HTTPS to prevent mixed-content blocking and tracking loss in strict mail clients.
2. Add `alt` attributes to all content and decorative images (use `alt=""` for pixels/decorative stars, descriptive `alt` for hero/product images) for accessibility and text-only rendering fallback.
3. Fix the unencoded `%` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` filename/URL.
4. Re-probe the 4 timed-out and 8 rate-limited links directly against wayfair.com (outside the QA tool's time/rate budget) to confirm they aren't genuinely broken.
