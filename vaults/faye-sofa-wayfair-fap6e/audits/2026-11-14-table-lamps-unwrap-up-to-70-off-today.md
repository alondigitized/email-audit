# TABLE LAMPS 🎁 UNWRAP UP TO 70% OFF TODAY 🎁 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is another Wayfair catch-all that only half-delivers on its subject. "TABLE LAMPS" is the first product in the first grid, and a pair of black-shade lamps sits top-left under "picked just for you." After that the email turns into a generic grid of rugs, pergolas, dressers, beds, a gym bench and a fireplace. The hero banner, "Early Black Friday, up to 70% off" with the Sealy badge, has nothing to do with lamps.
- The email is long and has no real hierarchy. The "picked just for you" label promises personalization, but the mix of pergolas, a gym bench and a dresser doesn't read as tailored to anyone. The offers are plentiful (70% off, Affirm 0% APR, free delivery over $35) but compete with each other. The only real conversion path is the "shop all sales" button, which goes to the sitewide sale.
- My judgement: a mid-tier send. The brand is recognizable and the discount is clear, but the lamp promise is dropped almost immediately. The subject's "UNWRAP" gift framing is also off, because the body is an Early Black Friday sale.

## What's working

- The "up to 70% off" banner is large and sits at the top, so the headline discount is clear.
- Strike-through "Was / NOW" prices with star ratings on many tiles give price and social proof.
- The lamp is the first tile, so the subject's product does appear at the top-left.
- The Affirm 0% APR band and the "free & easy delivery over $35" band add reasons to buy now.
- The purple CTA bands break up the long scroll and are easy to spot.

## What's weak

- Only one lamp tile appears. The subject promises table lamps, but the rest is rugs, pergolas, furniture and a gym bench.
- The gift/"unwrap" subject framing doesn't match the Early Black Friday banner. A gift-minded reader sees no gift content.
- Most tiles have no visible product name or CTA, and some have no price. A few tiles show only stars.
- The email is very long and repeats the same banner twice. The pergola and rug tiles recur across the grids, as in the 11-13 "pergolas" send.
- There is no deadline beyond the fine print, so the "TODAY" urgency isn't backed up in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email lamp-led: a lamp hero with a "Table lamps up to 70% off" banner, then a 6-tile lamp grid, then one adjacent-category row.
- 2. Cut the grid to 6-8 tiles and drop unrelated categories like the gym bench and pergolas.
- 3. Put a real product name and price on every tile, with "Shop lamps" as the primary CTA.
- 4. Match the subject's gift framing to the creative, or drop the gift emoji.
- 5. Add a real end date next to the 70% off offer, since "TODAY" isn't supported in the body.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Black-shade, brass and ceramic styles, plus free delivery over $35.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with responsive media queries. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt text on content images, and several assets whose filenames point to older campaigns. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated. The HTML source was truncated, so findings come from the QA data and the visible head and CSS.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** All 8 probe warnings were HTTP 429 rate limits (for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...`). These are inconclusive, not failures.
- **4 links unprobed.** The 70s probe budget ran out before these were checked, so their status is unknown:
  - `/wayfair-product-L1008-K~id-QC92689051.html`
  - `/BEDLORE%C2%AE-Dual-Layer-4-Memory-Foam-Mattress-Topper-w...`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/must-have-pet-friendly-rugs-se78321...`
  - `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
- **Only 12 of 79 links were probed.** Coverage is thin.
- **Insecure banner link.** The skinny-top banner links to `http://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` rather than `https://`. It depends on a redirect.
- **Insecure app fallback.** The app link `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` sets `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, which is also non-HTTPS.
- **20 tracking pixels over HTTP.** `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`) are all served over `http://`, and the QA check flags each one as "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked."
  - Clients that block mixed content, or proxies that rewrite images, may drop these impression pixels, which would cause under-counting.
  - Twenty separate pixel requests is also heavy.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` is HTTPS, but it has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing alt text.** The QA check flags these:
  - The hero and skinny banners: `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP.jpg`, `...Skinny-Financing-Affir...`, `...Skinny-VP.jpg`.
  - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`.
  - `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_your-home-these-pi...`.
  - `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner-...`.
- **Wrapped banners lose their link text.** The skinny-top banner is wrapped in a link, and the probe recorded its link text as `[image: WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-VP]`. With images off, the link has no accessible name.
- **Star-rating images.** 11 `stars_*.png` images (`stars_9`, `stars_10`, `stars_11`) are flagged for missing alt text. Each one is wrapped in a product link (`wayfair-product-L6449-K~id-BG53006353.html`, etc.), so the link has no text and the rating is not conveyed to screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels.** The `imp` and `b.php` pixels are flagged. If they carry `alt=""`, the QA tool may be over-reporting. A decorative pixel should have an empty `alt`, and that is acceptable.
- **No other rendering issues.** The visible CSS shows a custom `@font-face` (Sofia) with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback, a Gmail `u + .Gmail` fix, and an Outlook-app stacking fix.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and QA output contain no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%`. The per-recipient values in the URLs (`_eml=`, `_emr=`) are populated. The truncated source means the body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Not verifiable.** The QA run did not capture `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), or `Authentication-Results`. The tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. These are capture gaps, not confirmed failures.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address not confirmed.** The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed. The footer, including the physical address, is outside the truncated source.
- **No sending-domain evidence.** The From address is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, but SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be determined from the data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Links use Wayfair's own scheme: `source=batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid=`, `refid=MKTEML_143514`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16746`, `skurectxid=`, `event_rec_txid=`. The link parameters are consistent across links. This is a problem only if downstream analytics expect UTMs.
- **Conflicting campaign labels.** The app link carries `c=Triggered&pid=Email&source=batch&batchid=3`. `Triggered` conflicts with `source=batch`, so the campaign is mislabeled in AppsFlyer or the app link is reusing a template default.
- **Very long URLs.** Each link has 12 to 15 query parameters, which is roughly 300 or more characters. This adds weight, but it is not a failure.
- **Link targets don't match the subject.** The subject promises "TABLE LAMPS". Among the probed links, the CTAs go to the generic `/daily-sales` and the homepage `/`. The product links are individual SKUs (`mplid=LDKE1002`, `WIIO8168`, `ARQI1043`), and this check could not confirm they are lamps.
- **Stale asset names.** `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner`, `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg` and `wfna_0331_dailysales_2024_email_...` suggest legacy creative. This could be reused modules or leftover assets. It is low confidence because only the filenames are evidence. It is worth checking that no stale promo is visible or linked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels to HTTPS, or consolidate them into one pixel. Change the banner link and the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the banner images and to the star-rating images, for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retry-after handling to clear the 8 `429` warnings. Probe the 4 unprobed links separately.
4. Check the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers against the raw message from the original sender, not the relay copy, before treating compliance as pass or fail.
5. Fix the `c=Triggered` versus `source=batch` mismatch on the OneLink URL.
6. Confirm that the promo assets named `MemorialDay`, `0125_48hourclearance` and `2024_email` are intended in a November send.
