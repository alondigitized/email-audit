## What's working

- Every image renders, with no broken tiles and no overlapping text.
- The hero has a clear structure: a headline, a solid purple "Shop Now" pill, and a pergola photo that matches the subject.
- The 2x2 and 2x3 grids have consistent tile sizes and short, scannable captions.
- The brand is unmistakable, with the Wayfair wordmark at the top, a consistent purple palette, and a Rewards/Financing/App footer.
- "Closeout Deals →" and "Open Box Deals →" are clear, tappable bargain entry points.

## What's weak

- Only one tile is about pergolas. The subject's promise is dropped after the hero.
- There is no concrete offer: no percentage, dollar amount, deadline or code. "$9.99" is buried in a curtains caption.
- The "BIG-TIME BARGAINS" banner above the hero competes with the pergola headline for attention, so the eye has no single landing point.
- Captions such as "On Sale Now: Dressers »" don't say how much is off, and the two-line "Shop Appliances" block adds yet another unrelated category.
- A pergola is a spring or summer purchase and this send lands on 10/28. Nothing in the email makes it seasonal or urgent.

- 2. Replace the "BIG-TIME BARGAINS" banner with the pergola offer, so the first thing readers see matches the subject.
- 3. Put a credible deadline on the offer, such as "Ends Sunday". Offer a reason to buy now, like winter pricing or pre-season stock.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings under each tile. Add Rewards or financing language near the hero for big-ticket items.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile media query. Its main technical weaknesses are about 22 tracking pixels without alt text (20 of them loaded over plain `http://`), content images with missing alt text, and no visible UTM scheme. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** All 8 probe warnings are HTTP 429 (rate limiting) from `wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me`. These are probe throttling, not failures. They are unverified, not dead.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were skipped after the 70s budget (`budget-friendly-nightstands-se18327`, `top-brands%3a-kitchen-sinks-se16242`, `on-sale-now%3a-dressers-se44425`, and a `daily-sales?_emr=…` URL). The QA output shows them cut off mid-URL, so this may be a truncation artifact in the report.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 20 pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=1a1d2339-…`. Clients that block mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions. `li.wayfair.com` should be served over HTTPS.
- **Conflicting campaign tags on the app link:** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` carries `c=Triggered`, while every other link carries `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=3`. A batch send is being attributed as triggered in AppsFlyer.
- **Per-link `_eml` and `mdlid` values:** These are unique per link, so click-level attribution works. `_emr=1a1d2339-…` is repeated on every URL as the recipient identifier.
- **Duplicate app CTA:** "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" appears twice with different `_eml` values. The two instances can be tracked separately.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Tracking pixels without alt text:** `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (open pixel) and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels have no `alt`. They should have `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Reused creative filenames:** `wfus_0331_btb_2024_email_*` are the same filenames Wayfair has used in earlier sends. This is stale-asset naming, not a rendering defect.
- **Mobile CSS looks sound:** The `<style>` block has `@media (max-width: 480px)` and a `u ~ div` Gmail fix. The HTML is truncated, so I can't verify the `lang` attribute, a `<title>`, or `role="presentation"` on the layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the captured source or URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are absent in the capture. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay.
- **Sender:** From is `editor@members.wayfair.com`, a subdomain-aligned sender. This is not an issue in itself.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** The QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters on any link.** Wayfair uses its own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_140012`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16649`, `sltid`. This works for first-party analytics but won't map to GA4 source/medium without custom mapping.
- **Landing alignment:** The subject "Pergolas for less" matches the link `/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html` (`unifiedeventid=603460`). The probe was rate-limited, so the page's contents were not verified.
- **Deep-link fallback:** The app link sets `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp=http://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`. The web fallback uses `http://` instead of `https://`, which adds a redirect hop.
- **Query string length:** Some URLs run well over 300 characters. This is fine for modern clients.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the linked hero and tile images (e.g. "Pergolas for less") and `alt=""` to the 21 tracking pixels.
2. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and use `https://` in the `af_web_dp` fallback.
3. Correct the app link's `c=Triggered` to a batch campaign identifier, or confirm it is intentional.
4. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside the proprietary params, or document the mapping.
5. Re-run the probe for the 8 throttled and 4 unprobed links with slower pacing or a longer time budget before treating link health as passing.
6. Capture the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly, not via the relay, and check the untruncated footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
