# Bag one of these furniture cushions before they’re all gone!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This send has a real render bug and a subject that never gets paid off in copy. The subject promises "furniture cushions before they're all gone" — urgency, scarcity — but the body never once uses the word "cushion" or shows a price, a product name, or a countdown. The stacked-cushion product photo appears twice (identical image, "fresh-picked favorites" and "let's complete your space") with no label, no price, and a generic "Learn More" CTA that routes to buy-now-pay-later financing, not the product.
- Worse, there's a visibly broken module: a large empty white block with a small blue "?" placeholder icon sitting where an image clearly failed to load, right above the footer icons. That's the kind of visual bug that erodes trust regardless of offer quality.
- The top banner ("70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE") is a generic sitewide clearance strip that has nothing to do with cushions and is the same beat Wayfair has run repeatedly this week (2026-08-22 "LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" 7/10, 2026-08-23 duvet-fills send 6/10 that also had a subject/body mismatch). This is a weak send: subject over-promises, hero under-delivers, and there's a broken image. Judgment: skip.

## What's working

- The hero product shot (stacked green/rust/cream cushions on a bench) is a clean, well-lit lifestyle photo with a 5-star rating badge underneath.
- The "shop before they're gone" grid below is well-organized with clear thumbnail + label pairs (privacy screens, pergolas, gazebos, kettles, porch swings, slipcovers).
- Get It Now, Pay Later section clearly displays both Afterpay and Klarna badges with a specific "no interest" claim and a purple "Learn More" button.
- Footer benefit icons (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) are legible and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The subject says "furniture cushions" but no product in the visible render is labeled a cushion, priced, or linked with cushion-specific copy — the hero image is never captioned.
- Same cushion image is repeated twice ("fresh-picked favorites" and "let's complete your space") with no variation, price, or distinct CTA — reads like a placeholder duplicated by mistake.
- A large blank white section with a broken-image placeholder icon (blue "?") sits directly above the footer — a visible render bug.
- No urgency device anywhere (no countdown, no "X left," no date) despite the subject's "before they're all gone" scarcity hook.
- The "shop before they're gone" grid never includes a cushions tile — the closest is "Best-Selling Slipcovers," which is a different product category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image block above the footer — that's a hard blocker regardless of copy quality.
- 2. Put an actual cushion product tile with a price and a specific CTA ("Shop Cushions") in the hero, replacing one of the duplicate stacked-cushion images.
- 3. Add a scarcity element that matches the subject — a stock counter, style count ("12 styles left"), or a clearance-end date — since the subject is built entirely on urgency.
- 4. Add a cushions tile to the "shop before they're gone" grid so the body actually reflects the subject's product category.
- 5. Drop or relocate the Labor Day clearance banner — it's unrelated to cushions and has already run multiple times this week; competing headlines dilute the subject's promise.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: outdoor cushions, up to 50% off`

## 1. Technical Summary

Automated QA found no confirmed broken links, but flagged missing List-Unsubscribe/Authentication-Results headers, 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and pervasive missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 of 44 links were probed (32 deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 8 returned **HTTP 429 (rate-limited)** rather than a pass/fail — includes the primary CTAs "Get Exclusive Deals on the App," "Shop Sale," the hero image link, and the featured PDP link (`.../pdp/hokku-designs-18-h-customizable-bench-cushion-...w113526282.html`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed failures — recommend re-probing outside the batch rate limit.
- **4 links were unprobed** due to a 70s time budget cutoff, including a Buy-Now-Pay-Later link (`wayfair.com/buy-now-pay-later~b547.html`) and two PDP links — status unknown.
- All probed/tracked links carry consistent tracking params: `_emr`, `wfcs=cs7`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `sm`, `refid=MKTEML_121355`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16016`, `mdlid` — no malformed or missing tracking params observed in the sampled set.
- **20 open/impression tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`**: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019` (sz=2x1, m=false, p=c4951f73-...). - A separate open-tracking pixel via `t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (base64-encoded payload decoding to `mcch=ECM&eventType=EmailOpen&source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15...`) is HTTPS — no issue there.
