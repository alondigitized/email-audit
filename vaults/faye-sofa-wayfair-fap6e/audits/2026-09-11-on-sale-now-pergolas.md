# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a good render, by this brand history's low bar — no broken images, no blank white cold opens like the 2026-09-11 "Our best gazebo deals" or 2026-09-10 "Yesss! New BIDETS have arrived" sends. The hero pergola photo loads cleanly and the "This Week's Best Deals, Picked for You" purple banner up top gives clear context before the product grid kicks in.
- But the subject/render mismatch problem — the recurring flaw in this brand history — is back. The subject line promises "On sale now: pergolas," and while the hero image *is* a pergola, there's no price, no discount percentage, no urgency on it at all — just a bare caption "On sale now: pergolas »" under the photo. Everything with an actual number attached (60% off bedding, 40% off living room seating, 60% off area rugs) is a completely different category buried below the fold. A shopper who opens for pergola savings finds no pergola offer.
- Overall this is a mid-pack Wayfair send: technically clean, visually organized, but the subject is bait for a product that has no visible deal attached to it, which will hurt trust on click-through.

## What's working

- Hero image renders fully and matches the subject's product category (pergola) — no broken-image placeholder, unlike 3 of the last 6 audited sends.
- "LAST CALL" + "This Week's Best Deals, Picked for You" banner gives immediate visual anchor and urgency framing.
- Clear visual hierarchy in the deals grid — purple discount badges ("60% OFF," "40% OFF") pop against product photos.
- Single unambiguous CTA button ("Shop All Picks for You") is well-placed and easy to find.
- Social proof via "X Recently Purchased" counts under the middle product grid adds credibility.

## What's weak

- The pergola hero has zero visible discount, price, or offer — just a plain link caption, despite being the subject line's whole premise.
- The email is extremely long and repetitive — three separate near-identical grids ("Must-See Price," "Sales You *Almost* Missed," plus the recently-purchased grid) dilute the pergola focus entirely.
- No pergola-specific pricing or percentage-off anywhere in the visible render — a shopper scanning for the promised deal won't find one.
- Generic whole-catalog grab-bag (dart boards, foosball tables, ride-on toys, kids bookcases) undercuts the illusion of a "picked for you" pergola-interested audience.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or % off on the pergola hero itself — if pergolas are genuinely on sale, show the number; if they're not really discounted, don't lead the subject with "on sale now."
- 2. Cut the email length — three back-to-back grids of unrelated categories buries the one thing the subject promised. Trim to the top 2 sections.
- 3. Move a pergola/outdoor-living deal badge (matching the "MUST-SEE PRICE" style used elsewhere) directly onto the hero image.
- 4. Tighten "Picked for You" personalization — nothing here (dart boards, ride-on toys, kids bookcases) reads as picked for a pergola shopper.
