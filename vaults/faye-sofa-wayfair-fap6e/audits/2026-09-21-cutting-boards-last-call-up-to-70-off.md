- Subject promises "CUTTING BOARDS 🚩 LAST CALL 🚩 UP TO 70% OFF" — a specific product with credible urgency. - After that it's the same sprawling percent-off tile wall this brand runs every time — curtains, mattresses, cooling, storage, a purple "flash deals" band, outdoor fireplaces, cookware, seating, faucets — capped with a "Your Home, These Picks" module and the rewards/app footer. - Judgement: better than last week's rocking-chair and shower-curtain misses because the named product actually appears, but this is still a subject-writer overselling a generic sitewide sale blast.

## What's working

- The subject's product ("cutting boards") does appear as an actual tile with a visible discount price (Was $49.98 / Now $35.98).
- No render bugs — every tile has an image, price, and star rating; layout is clean and consistent with Wayfair's template.
- The 70% off banner and "ENDS TONIGHT" repeated four times across the top nav create real, legible urgency.
- Category CTAs (50% off curtains, 40% off cooling, 60% off living room seating) are specific with clear percentage callouts.
- Free & easy delivery over $35 banner is a concrete, low-friction trust signal.

## What's weak

- "LAST CALL" urgency from the subject isn't visually reinforced anywhere in the hero — the banner just says "EPIC FALL SALE," not last call or a countdown.
- The cutting board tile is buried second-tier under the hero banner, sharing space with an unrelated dog gate, console table, coat rack, wildflower canvas, and string lights — no thematic hero treatment for the named product.
- No dedicated "cutting boards" or even "kitchen" category CTA anywhere in the deal grid — kitchen only shows up generically under "cookware & bakeware."
- Visual hierarchy sends the eye to the purple 70%-off banner and Delta faucet cross-promo first, not to the subject's advertised product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero banner itself feature the cutting board (image + price) instead of a generic "EPIC FALL SALE" strip — if the subject says cutting boards, the first thing opened should be cutting boards, not a Delta faucet ad.
- 2. Add a "LAST CALL — ends tonight" countdown or badge directly on or beside the cutting board tile so the subject's urgency has a visual anchor.
- 3. Pull kitchen/cutting-board related tiles (cutting boards, knife sets, boards + bakeware) into one adjacent block instead of scattering kitchen among fireplaces and living room seating.
- 4. Drop or relocate the Delta faucet cross-sell out of the hero — it competes with and dilutes the subject's promised product.
