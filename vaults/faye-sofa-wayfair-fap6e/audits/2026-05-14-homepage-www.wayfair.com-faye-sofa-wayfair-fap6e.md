# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Wayfair is doing Memorial Day Clearance right now — big red, white, and blue energy, giant "70% OFF" plastered across the hero. As a starter-home buyer who literally refreshes for Way Day, I appreciate the urgency vibes, but I opened this looking for furniture and I'm immediately staring at baking sheets and a bathroom vanity. This reads like a home-goods department store running a holiday blowout, not a destination for someone trying to furnish a living room with intention.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 70% OFF" hero** — the number is enormous and the red/white/blue treatment makes it feel like a genuine event, not a manufactured sale. For someone who waits for Way Day, this triggers the same Pavlovian response.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — dark pill button, centered, clear. No mystery about what to do next.
- **Purple banner strip** — "UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA" anchors the deal at the top of the screen before the hero even loads. Belt-and-suspenders urgency.
- **Search bar placement** — "Find anything home..." is right there above the hero. If I know I want a sofa, I can skip the noise and go straight to search.

## What's weak

- **No furniture in sight** — the two product thumbnails I can see below the hero are baking sheets and a bathroom vanity (the Delta partnership explains this). I came to buy furniture and there's nothing pulling me toward my category.
- **Hero imagery is purely decorative** — the red-and-blue gingham pattern is patriotic, but it shows no product, no lifestyle, no room. It tells me nothing about what I'd actually get if I tapped through.
- **No free-shipping callout** — Wayfair famously has free shipping over a threshold and that is a major conversion signal for big-ticket furniture. It's nowhere visible.
- **No urgency clock** — "Memorial Day Clearance" implies a deadline but there's no countdown, no "ends May 26," nothing. For someone like me who's primed to act on scarcity, that's a missed push.
- **No loyalty or rewards mention** — zero. If Wayfair has a rewards program, this homepage isn't doing any work for it.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham pattern for a lifestyle hero** — show a real room: a walnut credenza, a boucle sofa, warm natural light. The sale number still lives on top but now there's something to covet behind it.
- **Add a countdown to Memorial Day** — "Sale ends May 26 at midnight" with a live timer would do exactly what Way Day hype does for me: create a credible deadline.
- **Surface a furniture/living room category shortcut** — one tappable chip row ("Sofas · Rugs · Lighting · Accent Chairs") between the hero and the product rail so I'm not hunting through a hamburger menu.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold** — a small "Free shipping on orders $35+" strip directly under the search bar is low-lift and would reduce my checkout hesitation on big-ticket items.
