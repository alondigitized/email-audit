# Yesss! New BIDETS have arrived →
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the worst kind of Wayfair send to open cold: the top of the email is a blank white box with a tiny broken-image icon where the hero should be. Whatever was supposed to render first — presumably a bidet hero justifying the subject line — never loaded, so the first thing a recipient sees on scroll is empty white space, not "new bidets have arrived."
- Once the render recovers, it's the usual everything-grid: a "score styles you 💗" tile wall mixing bidets with pergolas, privacy screens, wine fridges, and — bizarrely — a chicken coop with "people love this chicken coop." Bidets do appear twice in that grid ("Bidets You'll Love" ×2), so the subject isn't a total lie, but they're one of eight unrelated tiles, not a featured launch.
- Consistent with this week's brand history (broken images on 2026-09-09's dining chair send, subject mismatches nearly every day), this is another low performer — arguably worse than most because the broken asset sits at the very top of the email where it does the most damage.

## What's working

- Bidets genuinely appear in the grid twice ("Bidets You'll Love »"), so the subject isn't pure clickbait.
- Grid layout below the broken hero renders cleanly — no other visible image failures.
- "60% OFF" warehouse clearout banner near the footer is bold and legible.
- Nav bar (Shop Sale / Wayfair / New Arrivals) is clean and unbroken.

## What's weak

- The hero region is a blank white box with a visible broken-image placeholder icon — the first thing opened is empty space.
- No dedicated "new arrivals" bidet showcase — bidets are two small tiles buried in an eight-item unrelated grid (pergolas, privacy screens, wine fridge, chicken coop).
- "people love this chicken coop. See why" has nothing to do with bidets or the subject line at all.
- Visual hierarchy is scattered — eye has no clear landing point since the top slot is broken and the rest is a flat tile grid.
- Continues this week's exact pattern flagged in brand history (2026-09-09 dining chairs): subject promises one specific product, render buries or breaks it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken hero asset before send** — a blank white box with a broken-image icon at the top of the email is the single biggest problem; QA image rendering across clients before deployment.
- 2. **Build an actual bidet hero** — lead with one bidet product shot, a "New Arrival" badge, and a direct "Shop Bidets" CTA instead of burying it in a mixed grid.
- 3. **Cut the chicken coop and unrelated categories from the top fold** — if this is a "new arrivals" email, keep the first 2-3 rows on-theme (bathroom/bidets) and push pergolas/wine fridges/coops further down.
- 4. **Tighten subject-to-render alignment** — either rename the subject to match the actual grab-bag content ("Score styles you'll love") or make the render match the bidet promise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bidets, reimagined: shop the new arrivals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bathroom upgrade just landed →`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sleek new bidet seats, ready to ship this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus pergolas, privacy screens, and more new arrivals`

- 7 of the 12 probed links returned **429 (rate limited)** rather than a clean pass, including the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "Bidets You'll Love," and both app-deeplink CTAs ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App" → `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`).
