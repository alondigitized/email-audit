# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair is going all-in on Memorial Day Clearance — the whole above-the-fold real estate is one big red-white-and-blue sale announcement screaming "up to 70% off." As a Way Day devotee who literally refreshes for flash sales, I get why they do this, but the hero is pure discount energy with zero product or style inspiration. It looks like a flyer, not a furniture brand I want to spend a Sunday afternoon browsing.

## What's working

- **"Up to 70% OFF" with a specific brand mention (Delta)** — the banner strip at the top doubles down on the offer and names a featured brand, which makes it feel less generic
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — it's pill-shaped, purple, high-contrast against the white box; zero ambiguity about what to tap
- **The product cards below the hero** — I can already see an outdoor sectional set and what looks like a mattress, so categories are emerging before I even scroll
- **Scroll indicator dots** — I know there's more hero content rotating; it signals the page has depth
- **Search bar front and center** — as someone who browses with intent ("cream boucle sofa"), this is exactly where I want it

## What's weak

- **Zero lifestyle/aesthetic inspiration** — the hero is a gingham pattern with numbers. Nothing says "modern-mid-century starter home." I feel zero emotional pull toward a purchase
- **"Up to 70%" without a floor** — classic bait signal; I've been burned by "up to" sales where the 70%-off items are things I'd never buy
- **No personalization visible** — I've shopped Wayfair before. No "Welcome back," no recently viewed, no "Based on your style" hook anywhere above the fold
- **The product thumbnails are cut off** — I can see the tops of two cards but can't read the product names or prices yet, so I can't act on them
- **Cart and account icons only — no loyalty/rewards indicator** — no point balance, no "Registry" shortcut, nothing that rewards my brand loyalty

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham sale graphic for a lifestyle hero** — show a real living room in modern-mid-century style with "Up to 70% off this look" overlaid; same urgency, 10x more aspiration
- **Add a "Your clearance picks" row** — surface 3-4 products algorithmically matched to my browse history directly below the hero, not generic categories
- **Show free-shipping threshold in the banner strip** — something like "Free shipping on orders $35+" next to the offer would remove a checkout hesitation before it forms
- **Add a countdown timer for Memorial Day deadline** — "Sale ends Monday 11:59 PM" with a live clock would make Way Day energy feel real and drive me to act now rather than "come back later"
