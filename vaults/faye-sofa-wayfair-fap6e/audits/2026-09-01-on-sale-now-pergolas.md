# On sale now: pergolas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "pergolas," and for once the top-left grid tile actually shows a pergola — that's real progress over the run of bait-and-switch subjects this week (see 2026-08-30's "CANOPIES" and 2026-08-29's "CHINA CABINETS" sends, both of which buried their promised category). But the hero is still the same generic "70% OFF" clearance banner Wayfair has run every day this week, and the pergola tile is one of sixteen roughly equal-weight grid squares — bathroom vanities, dog beds, toilets, hall trees — with zero visual hierarchy pointing back to the subject line's promise.
- This is a mile-long, unfocused grid email that reads more like a category sitemap than a targeted promotion. There's a broken/empty content block near the bottom (a blank white area with a lone tiny icon) that looks like a render failure, and the email just trails off into footer boilerplate after that. For a subscriber who opened specifically hoping to see pergola deals, this delivers one small thumbnail and then seventeen unrelated categories — mildly better than bait-and-switch, still a scattershot send.

## What's working

- The subject-line category ("pergolas") is actually present as the first grid tile, unlike several recent sends this week.
- The "70% OFF" clearance offer is bold, legible, and impossible to miss at the top.
- Grid tiles have clear labels and visible CTA arrows ("On sale now: pergolas »", "Garden beds »"), so navigation intent is clear even if hierarchy isn't.
- The Wayfair Rewards "5% back on every item" section is a clean, distinct visual break with a clear CTA.
- No overlapping text or obviously broken product images in the main grid.

## What's weak

- There's a blank white block with a single stray icon roughly two-thirds down the email — looks like a broken/failed render, not intentional content.
- The pergola product photo is a small thumbnail, not the hero — someone who opened for pergolas has to scroll past the banner and scan a 4-tile grid to find it.
- The email is extremely long (16+ product grid tiles spanning air conditioners, toilets, dog beds, hall trees) with no organizing theme beyond "clearance," diluting any category-specific interest.
- Two identical stock "striped cushion" images appear twice in the email (once above "fresh-picked favorites," once again lower) — redundant, feels like a template placeholder repeated rather than curated content.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no "ends [date]") despite "Labor Day Clearance" branding at top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the pergola hero the actual hero: replace the generic "70% OFF" banner with a pergola lifestyle photo plus the discount overlaid, since the subject already committed to that category — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Fix or remove the blank broken block roughly two-thirds down the email before the next send.
- 3. Cut the grid from 16+ tiles to 6-8 curated, thematically related items (outdoor/patio, since that's what the subject promised) rather than sprawling into bathroom vanities and dog beds.
- 4. Remove the duplicate cushion-stack image — it reads as a filler/placeholder mistake.
- 5. Add a real deadline ("Labor Day Clearance ends Mon 9/7") to justify the urgency implied by the banner.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Wayfair promotional email built on the Bee/Unbounce-style responsive framework (`u-*` MSO/Gmail media-query classes); QA probing shows a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by rate-limited (429) link checks and widespread missing `alt` attributes, plus absent authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay level.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 9 of 12 probed material links returned **429 Rate Limited** during automated checks (e.g. `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`, `wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `wayfair.com/home-improvement/sb0/air-conditioners-c420527.html?...`). These are inconclusive (link may still be valid) but could not be confirmed reachable.
- 3 links were **unprobed due to a 70s time budget**, including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm` and `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/garden-beds-se81925.html?eid=602156&` — status unknown.
- All checked/tracking links carry a consistent parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16077`, `mdlid=...`) suggesting a unified tracking schema — no malformed or truncated tracking params observed in the sampled URLs.
- Open-tracking pixel (`t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`) is present and correctly base64-encoded.
- **20 impression-tracking pixels use `http://` instead of `https://`** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000...` through `...019`), flagged as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies. This is a real infrastructure defect — all impression pixels in this batch are non-secure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **21 images missing `alt` text**, including the primary hero (`0824_LaborDay-LAUNCH_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), header banner (`wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`), product images (`wfus_jt800_batch10_majorappliances_airconditioners` ×3), rating graphics (`stars_9.png` ×2), and all 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels (these last 20 are non-content and low-impact, but the hero, header, and product images lack alt text for screen readers/image-blocked clients).
- Media-query CSS uses standard Bee/Unbounce mobile scaffolding (`u-FullWidth`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, Outlook `data-outlook-cycle` fix, Gmail `u + .Gmail` hook) — no structural rendering defects identified in the truncated source provided.
- `@supports (-webkit-touch-callout: none)` fallback present for iOS Mail `u-HalfWidthBlock` image display — standard mitigation, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags, empty personalization tokens, or broken dynamic-content blocks found in the provided HTML/QA output.

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay.

- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `varid`, `brcid`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — internally consistent across all sampled links, no mismatched or missing IDs observed.
