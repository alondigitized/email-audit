## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with first-party Wayfair tracking on every link. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The real findings are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt text on 5 content images, one malformed image URL, and links that could not be verified because of 429s and the probe time budget.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified, not broken.** 8 of 12 probed links returned 429 (rate limiting), including these CTAs:
  - "Shop under $29.99 →" (`/daily-sales/ds/shop-under-%2429.99-se79155.html`)
  - "Shop cooking essentials →"
  - "Shop vintage looks →"
  - "Shop storage →"
  - "Shop joyful finds →"
  - "Shop today's deals →"
  - both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links

  A 429 usually means the sender's bot protection throttled the probe. It confirms nothing about whether the links work.
- **Coverage gaps:**
  - 4 links timed out unprobed after 70s: two `/daily-sales?_emr=…` links, the `/?_emr=…` homepage link, and the Moroccan Zellige tile product link.
  - 60 links were skipped by design.
- **Both "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links share `sltid=16`.** They differ only in `_eml` (`85b0010b…` vs `441c6455…`), so slot-level click attribution can't tell the two placements apart.
- **The OneLink app URL's campaign tag doesn't match the send type.** It carries `c=Triggered`, while the same URL has `source=graymatter_batch&batchid=15`. A batch send is being attributed as triggered in AppsFlyer.
- **The OneLink URL's web fallback is plain HTTP.** `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` makes non-app users go through an extra HTTP redirect hop.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - Open pixel: `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=…` (`eventType=EmailOpen`).
  - 20 ad-impression pixels: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019` (`sz=2x1`). All are plain HTTP. Impressions may be silently dropped by clients or proxies that block or upgrade insecure content, which undercounts impressions.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `1029_fallclearance_skinnyhero-vp.png`
  - `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purple.png`
  - `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`

  The first three are hosted on `assets.wfcdn.com` and `secure.img1-fg.wfcdn.com`. With images off, or for screen readers, these banners have no text equivalent.
- **Malformed percent-encoding in an image URL.** `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains `%oo`, which is not a valid hex escape (`o` isn't a hex digit). Strict URL parsers or proxies, such as Gmail's image proxy, may reject or mis-decode it, and the image may not load. This URL was not probed, so it is unconfirmed whether it resolves.
- **The 21 tracking pixels have no alt attribute** (`b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels). This is low severity, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct pattern for decorative pixels.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` only, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- The HTML is truncated after the `<style>` block, so the body markup (tables, `role="presentation"`, button markup) could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. Every URL carries resolved per-recipient identifiers (`_emr=cab3a0c1-…`, a unique `_eml` per link, and `mdlid`), and no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them in the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown.** This is likely a relay artifact. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` can't be verified from the available data.
- **The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.** The footer is outside the truncated HTML, and the QA output doesn't list the unsubscribe probe result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters are used.** Attribution runs on proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `refid=MKTEML_121660`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16624`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `varid=6`. This is consistent across all links and is not a defect. It does mean standard GA UTM reporting isn't populated unless Wayfair maps these server-side.
- **Landing page alignment couldn't be verified** because of the 429s. The URL slug `shop-under-%2429.99-se79155` matches the subject "All under $29.99", and the `$` is correctly encoded as `%24`.
- Slot numbering is sequential (`sltid=6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16`), and `fromNEM=true` is set on the daily-sales links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. This separates an AgentMail capture gap from a real sender gap.
2. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with a throttled, browser-like client to confirm the landing pages resolve. Treat 429 as "unknown", not a pass.
3. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the 4 banner and header images listed in section 3.
5. Fix the `%oo` in the `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` URL by re-encoding or renaming the asset.
6. Change `c=Triggered` to a batch or campaign value, use `https://` for `af_web_dp`, and give the two app links distinct `sltid` values.
7. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
8. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to cover the body markup and footer compliance elements.
