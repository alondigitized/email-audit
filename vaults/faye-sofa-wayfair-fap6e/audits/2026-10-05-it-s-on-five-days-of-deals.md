# 📣 It’s ON ✨ FIVE DAYS OF DEALS ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a working sale blast, and the cleanest Wayfair send in this run. After the broken-image sends on 10/5 (free shipping) and 10/2 (dinnerware), every image here loads. The structure is easy to scan: a "5 DAYS OF DEALS" banner, then big purple discount tiles (70% off area rugs, 50% off mattresses, 55% off mirrors), then product grids with star ratings.
- It's also a generic one. The subject promises an event, "FIVE DAYS OF DEALS," and the body delivers a general category sale with no hero product, no start or end date, and no prices on any product tile. The "picked just for you" grids show unlabeled photos with stars and nothing else. The body is also long: Klarna financing, a gift card strip, a Delta ad, rewards icons and an app QR code all compete with the deals. It's solid but forgettable.
- Compared with the 10/4 coffee-table send and the 10/3 free-shipping send, whose subjects and bodies didn't match, this subject and body are at least aligned. The risk is cadence. This is the 5th or 6th Wayfair send in about four days, and "up to 50% off" is the same offer as the 10/1 Adirondack email.

## What's working

- The discount tiles are big, high-contrast purple blocks with "up to 70% OFF" and a category label with an arrow. The offer is readable in about two seconds.
- The "Five Days of Deals" banner matches the subject, and the Christmas trees and indoor seasonal decor tiles fit early October.
- Nothing is broken in this render. The images load, with no blank hero boxes like the 10/2 dinnerware send.
- Star ratings under the product photos give some social proof, and "Sale" tags mark a couple of items.
- The Klarna block ("0% APR up to 24 months," available through 10/12/2026) is a clear financing offer with a real date.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is small. The "60% OFF" burst is a tiny circle on the left, and the real headline is 5 DAYS OF DEALS with no dates and no stated end time.
- The "picked just for you" product tiles have no names, prices or CTA text, just photos and stars. They look random (a shower head, a vanity, a pet bed), and nothing says why they were picked.
- Every offer is "up to X% off" with no floor, so the 70% rugs and 55% mirrors read as best-case claims.
- Many things compete for attention: gift cards, a Delta Faucet ad, Klarna, four icon links, an app banner and a QR code. The bottom third dilutes the sale.
- The tiles read as category names with arrows ("area rugs →"), not action copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one dated hero at the top: "5 Days of Deals: Oct 5–9," with a countdown or an end-time line. Lead with the single best offer, such as 70% off area rugs.
- 2. Show a price on every product tile, such as "$129, was $249." Without prices, the "picked just for you" grids are just photos. Label each tile with a product name.
- 3. Cut the lower third. Drop one of the gift card, Delta or rewards/icon blocks, and keep Klarna and the app banner.
- 4. Use verb CTAs on the tiles ("Shop rugs," "Get mattress deals") instead of bare category names.
- 5. Space out sends. Combine near-identical promos this week into one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off rugs, 50% off mattresses: 5 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Day 1 of 5: rugs, mattresses & mirrors up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deals run through Friday: rugs, mattresses, curtains & more. Plus 0% APR financing.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New deals each day, and the best prices go fast. Shop day one now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard table-based XHTML template with mobile media queries and tracking IDs that resolve on every link. The QA warnings are mostly missing alt text on images and 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because AgentMail did not expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe results are inconclusive, not failures.** 7 of the 12 probed links returned HTTP 429 (rate limited), including `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `https://www.wayfair.com/?...`, and the three `/daily-sales/*-clearance~e39xxxx.html` URLs. The probe was likely throttled by Wayfair's bot protection, so none of these is confirmed broken or confirmed working.
- **4 links were never checked.** Probing stopped after 70s, leaving `mirror-%26-decor-clearance~e390728.html`, the Zephyr refrigerator and Delta shower head product URLs, and `wayfair-product-L218-K~W114562030.html` unprobed.
- **Mixed-protocol tracking.** 20 impression pixels use plaintext HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`. Clients that block or upgrade non-HTTPS images (some privacy proxies) may drop them, which undercounts impressions. The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?...` is HTTPS.
- **App deep-link fallback is HTTP.** The onelink URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`, so desktop users get an HTTP URL that depends on a redirect.
- **Campaign parameter mismatch.** The onelink URL has `c=Triggered` but also `source=batch&batchid=5`. A batch promotional send labeled "Triggered" will skew attribution in AppsFlyer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.**
  - Banners:
    - `5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_SkinnyHero-General.png`
    - `wfus_5daysofdeals_fall2026-skinny-flashdeals.png`
    - `WFUS_5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`
    - `WFUS_0929_BigFurnitureSale-Skinny-BAU_Financing.jpg`
    - `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`
  - Star ratings: `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png`, 12 instances in total.
- **The hero banner is a linked image with no alt.** Its link goes to `/daily-sales/`, so the link's accessible name falls back to the filename. The probe reports it as `[image: 5DaysofDeals_Fall2026_SkinnyHero-General]`. Screen readers and images-off clients will see no usable CTA text.
- **The same applies to the other banner images.** Where a banner is the only link content, images-off clients lose the offer message entirely.
- **Rating images carry no value in text.** The stars images have no alt, so the rating is not conveyed to assistive tech.
- **Tracking pixels (`b.php`, 20× `imp`) have no alt.** This is low impact, but `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` are best practice for decorative or tracking images.
- **Positives.** The `<style>` block includes `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules, an Outlook-app fix (`body[data-outlook-cycle]`), a Gmail selector (`u + .Gmail`), and `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` for the web font. The truncated source gives no other rendering issues.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. Every per-recipient parameter (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`, `skurectxid`, `event_rec_txid`) is populated with a UUID, and no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. The HTML is truncated, so tokens in the footer or body below the cut cannot be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders above 5,000 messages a day, and Wayfair is well above that.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `members.wayfair.com` cannot be assessed from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address are unconfirmed.** The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but none appears in the results, and the footer is outside the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters appear on any link.** Attribution uses Wayfair's own scheme: `source=batch`, `batchid=5`, `varid=3`, `csnid=e15faabd-...`, `refid=MKTEML_141417`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16470`. This is consistent across links, but analytics tools that expect UTMs, such as default GA channel grouping, won't classify the traffic without custom mapping.
- **The offer and landing alignment looks sound by URL.** The hero and "Shop Sale" links go to `/daily-sales`, and the clearance tiles go to `/daily-sales/<event>~e39xxxx.html?eid=...`, with `unifiedeventid` matching `eid` (624753, 624780, 624768).
- **Redirect and destination behavior cannot be confirmed.** The 429s prevented checking whether the pages resolve.
- **Minor inconsistency.** The hero link uses `/daily-sales/` (trailing slash) and "Shop Sale" uses `/daily-sales`. Both likely resolve to the same page, but a redirect hop is possible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the linked hero and banner images (for example "Shop Five Days of Deals"). Give the star images alt text carrying the rating (for example "4.5 out of 5 stars").
2. Add `alt=""` to the `b.php` and `imp` tracking pixels.
3. Serve `li.wayfair.com/imp` over HTTPS, and change the `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.
4. Correct `c=Triggered` on the onelink URL to a batch or promotional campaign value so AppsFlyer attribution isn't skewed.
5. Re-run link probing with a lower request rate to clear the 7 rate-limited results and the 4 unprobed links.
6. Capture the raw message headers before relay, or from a direct-delivery copy. That will confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, which is the only way to settle the compliance and authentication warnings.
7. Consider adding standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` parameters alongside the proprietary ones, if downstream analytics needs them.
