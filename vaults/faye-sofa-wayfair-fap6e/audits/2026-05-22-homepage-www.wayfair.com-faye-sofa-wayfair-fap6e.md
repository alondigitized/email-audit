# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Wayfair is going all-in on Memorial Day — that "up to 70% OFF" hero is basically screaming at me the second I open the app, which honestly? I respect it. This feels exactly like the kind of sale I set a reminder for. The vibe is pure promotional energy, patriotic gingham and bold typography, and less about the furniture itself and more about the deal. It's a clearance-mode Wayfair, not a style-inspiration Wayfair.

## What's working

- **The 70% off hero:** This is exactly the number that makes me stop scrolling. Big, bold, impossible to miss — it earns the tap.
- **The announcement banner:** "UP TO 70% OFF | MEMORIAL DAY CLEARANCE FEAT. DELTA" is doing double duty — reinforces the deal and hints at a brand collab, which piques curiosity.
- **The timing:** Landing on Memorial Day weekend makes this feel urgent and earned, not manufactured. I know this sale is real.
- **The product thumbnails peeking below fold:** I can see what looks like an outdoor patio set and an armchair scene — immediately relevant to what I'm shopping, and they pull me into scrolling.
- **Search bar prominence:** "Find anything home…" is right there. If the hero doesn't match my exact vibe, I can pivot instantly.

## What's weak

- **Zero style personality:** The red-white-blue gingham is festive but it tells me nothing about what Wayfair's aesthetic actually is. I'm a modern-mid-century person — I need to see a walnut sideboard or a clean-lined sofa to feel like this store gets me.
- **No loyalty hook:** I've definitely ordered from Wayfair before. There's no "welcome back," no rewards balance, no saved items prompt. A returning-buyer nudge here would be easy wins.
- **No deadline or countdown:** "Memorial Day Clearance" implies urgency but there's no "ends Sunday" or timer. For someone like me who takes days to decide, I need a real deadline to stop procrastinating.
- **"Up to" without an anchor:** 70% off what? A $12 picture frame? I'd feel better if there was a callout like "sofas from $299" to give me a sense of actual savings on the stuff I buy.
- **No free-shipping callout:** Free shipping threshold is a huge purchase trigger for me — I know Wayfair has it, but it's not above the fold here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gingham for a lifestyle shot of a furnished living room** — show me a mid-century sofa styled in a small apartment and keep the 70% badge overlaid. I want the deal AND the aspiration.
- **Add "Sale ends Monday 11:59 PM" beneath the SHOP NOW button** — a real deadline converts browsers like me who love to "think about it."
- **Surface a returning-shopper bar** — a slim "Pick up where you left off: [last viewed item]" strip would instantly re-engage me and skip the cold-browse tax.
- **Drop a "Free shipping on orders $35+" line** right below the CTA — it's a trust unlock that costs nothing to display and is a genuine purchase catalyst for my AOV.
