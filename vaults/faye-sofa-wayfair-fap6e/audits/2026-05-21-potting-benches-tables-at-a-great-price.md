# POTTING BENCHES & TABLES at a great price.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- "POTTING BENCHES & TABLES at a great price." is Wayfair's most gardening-specific subject line in this entire Memorial Day run — and it's completely wasted. The email opens on a patio/outdoor furniture hero and immediately pivots to "Open Box Deals," a product grid of miscellaneous home goods, and a "Wayfair Welcome" mat section. Potting benches, if they exist at all in this scroll, are nowhere near the top of the email. The subject made a promise; the body ignores it.
- This is the eighth Wayfair email I've reviewed in four days, and the formula is identical every time: bolt a specific category noun onto the subject, send the same all-department kitchen-sink. That worked marginally on the chandelier send (May 20) and the bedding send (May 19) because those categories at least partially appeared in the hero. Here, the subject sets an expectation that the creative has zero interest in fulfilling.
- The "Open Box Deals" section is the most coherent unit in the email — it's a real offer with a clear "Shop Now" CTA — but it has nothing to do with potting benches. At this point in Wayfair's Memorial Day cadence, fatigue is compounding the mismatch penalty.

## What's working

- **"Open Box Deals" section is crisp** — a single action, one button, direct copy. Best-executed unit in the email.
- **Subject line is under 50 characters** (43 chars) and free of emoji clutter or excessive caps — cleaner than most of this run.
- **No visible render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping text or broken sections.
- **70% OFF badge is visible** and provides a concrete discount anchor late in the email.

## What's weak

- **Subject-to-body mismatch, again.** The hero is patio seating and an outdoor setting — no potting benches visible anywhere near the top.
- **"At a great price" is the weakest possible offer copy.** Every Wayfair email this week names a specific percent off; this one substitutes a vague phrase that signals nothing.
- **"The Wayfair Welcome" section is a non sequitur** — a welcome mat feature mid-scroll breaks whatever gardening theme the subject set up.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** Eight emails in four days; the inbox is numb. This send needed something to break the pattern and doesn't.
- **No urgency mechanism anywhere in the body** — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing tying this to Memorial Day despite being the stated campaign window.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show a potting bench in the first 300px.** If this category is in the subject, it must be the hero image. Put a styled potting bench with a price tag visible above the fold. Everything else is secondary.
- 2. **Replace "at a great price" with an actual number.** "Up to 40% off" or "Starts at $79" — any real signal is better than the filler phrase.
- 3. **Cut at least two mid-body sections.** "The Wayfair Welcome" and a generic product grid tile have no relationship to potting benches. Tighten to three sections: hero category, Open Box Deals, app footer.
- 4. **Add a deadline.** "Through Sunday" or "Memorial Day weekend only" gives a reason to act now that this email completely lacks.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Potting benches from $69 — Memorial Day pricing`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your garden's missing this. Potting benches on sale.`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Open-box deals + up to 40% off outdoor workstations this weekend only.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Build your garden workspace for less — potting benches and tables, priced to move.`
