# PERGOLAS we’re obsessed with(!)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth pergola-led Wayfair send in about four days. It follows Oct. 23 "PERGOLAS ❗ WAY DAY ❗ TOMORROW ❗", Oct. 22 "Best-selling pergolas" and Oct. 21 "PERGOLAS 🟣 🟣 WAY DAY STARTS OCT. 24". The creative is consistent with the subject: a "starts tomorrow, up to 80% off Way Day" banner, then two grids of pergola photos with star ratings. It's polished and on-brand, but it is nearly the same email subscribers already got yesterday.
- The weakness is that there are no prices anywhere. "Fresh-picked favorites" and "let's complete your space" are 12 lifestyle photos of near-identical black and white louvered pergolas. Nothing tells me what any of them costs or why one beats another. The only concrete money hooks are "up to 80% off", which is generic, and the "APP10" code far down the page. The bottom "shop before they're gone" grid drifts into comforters, rugs, sinks and shoe racks, which dilutes the pergola promise.
- Verdict: a competent, on-season Way Day teaser, but fatigued and price-free. It's a 6 on execution and lower on novelty.

## What's working

- The purple Way Day banner with "up to 80% off" and "starts tomorrow" sits at the very top and sets the urgency and the event.
- The subject and the body agree. The first two-thirds of the render is pergolas, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- Every product tile has a visible star rating, which gives quick social proof.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlap, and the purple palette makes the hierarchy easy to scan.
- The "Early Way Day TODAY ONLY / APP10 extra 10% off in the app" band is a credible time-boxed incentive, and the app-download block at the bottom reinforces it.

## What's weak

- No product shows a price, Was/Now figure or discount badge. The 12 pergola tiles carry no purchase reason, unlike the Oct. 21 send's Was/Now prices.
- The two pergola grids look almost the same. The photos are mostly black louvered or curtained units, so the second grid adds nothing.
- The only top-of-email CTA is a small yellow "Shop Early" pill. The "Apply Now" financing button is a second, competing action that isn't about the offer.
- The "pace your space" credit-card module interrupts the product flow right after the hero grid.
- The lower grid mixes in comforters, rugs, sinks and shoe racks, which weakens the pergola focus.
- The best incentive, the APP10 code, is buried near the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add Was/Now prices or "from $X" and a percent-off badge to every pergola tile. This is the biggest lift for click-through.
- 2. Cut the two 6-tile grids down to one tighter grid of 4 to 6 distinct styles (louvered, curtained, soft-top, gazebo), each with a price.
- 3. Move the APP10 code up into the hero and make the CTA specific, such as "Shop pergolas up to 80% off" in a large button.
- 4. Move the financing module below the products, or drop it on a Way Day teaser.
- 5. Cap the send cadence. Fold the pergola push into one or two sends and rotate to other categories so the subject line isn't repeating.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pergolas up to 80% off — Way Day starts tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pergolas from $X: your early Way Day preview`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Louvered, curtained and soft-top styles with extra 10% off in the app today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop early before Way Day deals go live. See top-rated picks with prices.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with a mobile `@media (max-width: 480px)` stylesheet and a per-recipient tracked link set. The QA data shows 22 images without alt text, 20 tracking pixels served over `http://`, and no verifiable compliance or authentication headers. Of the 12 probed links, 8 returned 429 and the others are not shown in the QA output. Another 4 product links were not probed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Unverified links.** Eight probed links returned 429 (rate-limited). These are not confirmed broken, and the QA output reports no 4xx or 5xx result.
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...sltid=2` ("Shop Sale")
  - `https://www.wayfair.com/?...sltid=2` ("Wayfair")
  - the `sltid=5` and `sltid=6` image links
  - the pergola product link (`...L7053-K~PAUR1264.html`) and its stars link
  - two `wayfairapp.onelink.me` "Get Exclusive Deals on the App" links
- **Unprobed links.** 4 product links were skipped on the 70s time budget: two `8088-Metal-Frame-Pergola...` URLs and two `-Aluminum-Pergola...Waterpro...` URLs. Their status is unknown. The QA output truncates these URLs.
- **Cleartext tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=1972795d-...`.
  - Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, so this is low risk there.
  - Some clients may block or warn on mixed content.
  - The recipient identifier `p=` is exposed in cleartext on any non-proxied fetch.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (`eventType=EmailOpen`) has no alt text. This is expected for a pixel, but it should carry `alt=""`.
- **Per-recipient `_eml` IDs.** Each link carries a different `_eml` value, for example `b456a570-...`, `d9544e50-...` and `17f53406-...`. This is normal for per-link tracking but breaks URL-level click aggregation if unnormalized.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Alt text.** 22 images have no alt attribute (the QA output flags them as "missing alt text").
  - The review-star images `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` and `stars_11.png` appear about 14 times. They carry the product rating, so screen-reader and images-off users get no rating information.
  - Linked images have no accessible name: the `WFUS_2026_1024_WayDay-PREVIEW_Email_Skin...` hero link and the `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` link. The QA output shows their link text as `[image: ...]`, meaning the filename is the only label.
  - Banner and header images are also missing alt: `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_2_purple.gif`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_2_purple.png` and `WFUS_261024_WayDay-APPEARLYACCESS_Email_SkinnyBann...`. So are `WFCC2%402x.png` and `WFCC%202%20Disclosure.png`. The Disclosure image may carry legal text.
- **Test artifact.** `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` has "test" in the filename. This is a production asset naming issue.
- **Structure.** The email uses a table layout with `u-*` responsive utility classes and a `u ~ div` Gmail fix. The mobile CSS is present. The truncated source gave no basis to assess `lang`, `role="presentation"` or the preheader.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible subject, links or QA output. The subject "PERGOLAS we're obsessed with(!)" renders cleanly. The truncated HTML limits this check.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe.** Not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it, so this is unconfirmed. For a bulk sender, Gmail and Yahoo require this header with `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058).
- **Authentication-Results.** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer.** The QA output confirms an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result is not shown. The physical address is not visible in the truncated source. Verify both.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No UTM parameters.** Links use Wayfair's internal tracking (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=6`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121271`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16555`, `mdlid`, `_emr`, `wfcs=cs7`). There is no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. This is fine if Wayfair uses its own attribution, but external analytics tools will see no campaign tagging.
- **Destination mismatch.** The subject is about pergolas, but several links go to generic destinations: `/daily-sales` ("Shop Sale", the hero image) and `/` (the "Wayfair" logo and the header image `sltid=6`). The product links, such as the pergola URL with `sku=PAUR1264`, do go to specific pages.
- **App link.** The OneLink URL has `af_dp=wayfairapp://home`, `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`. The campaign name `Triggered` does not match a batch send (`source=graymatter_batch`), so this looks like a mislabeled campaign in AppsFlyer attribution.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive alt text to the star images (for example `alt="4.5 out of 5 stars"`), the linked hero and banner images, and the disclosure image. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` impression pixels over HTTPS.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers rather than the relay copy. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC the same way.
4. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited links and the 4 unprobed pergola product links with backoff or a longer time budget.
5. Rename `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` to remove the test artifact.
6. Change the OneLink `c=Triggered` campaign value to match the batch send.
7. Consider adding UTM parameters if Wayfair needs cross-tool attribution.
8. Rerun the QA with the full HTML. The truncated source limited the checks for the preheader, `lang`, the footer address and the unsubscribe link.
