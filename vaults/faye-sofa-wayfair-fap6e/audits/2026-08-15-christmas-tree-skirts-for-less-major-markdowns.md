# CHRISTMAS TREE SKIRTS for less. *MAJOR* markdowns ➡
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "CHRISTMAS TREE SKIRTS for less. *MAJOR* markdowns" but the hero banner delivers a completely different, unrelated message: "SUMMER MARKDOWNS UP TO 70% OFF." Tree skirts don't appear until the second content row, as one small tile buried among wood deck boxes and vanities — no price, no rating, no urgency. This is the same subject/hero disconnect pattern Wayfair has run repeatedly this week (see the 2026-08-12/08-14 "BLACK OUTDOOR PRIVACY SCREENS" sends, both 4.00/10) — a specific, curiosity-driving subject line that the creative team never actually built a matching hero for.
- Below the fold it's the now-familiar Wayfair kitchen-sink scroll: window AC units, gazebos, swing sets, fountains, bird feeders, craft tables — a loosely-themed "summer clearance" catalog dump with almost no thread back to the subject line. It's competently produced (clean grid, consistent branding, visible star ratings, a Rewards callout) but it's not an email built around the promise in the subject.
- Also worth flagging: marketing "Christmas tree skirts" in mid-August is a strange seasonal beat regardless of clearance framing — it reads as inventory clearance copy repurposed into a subject line rather than an intentional seasonal moment.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand visual execution — consistent purple/orange Wayfair palette, no overlapping text or broken images.
- Hero banner has genuine visual hierarchy: bold "70% OFF" badge + orange "Shop Now" button is the first thing the eye hits.
- Wayfair Rewards module ("get 5% rewards on every item, always") is a clear, differentiated loyalty callout mid-email.
- Star ratings are visible on several product tiles, giving light social proof throughout the scroll.

## What's weak

- The subject's specific promise (Christmas tree skirts) is not honored by the hero — a classic bait-and-switch that this brand keeps repeating (same pattern as the 4.00/10 privacy-screen sends).
- Tree skirts appear only as a tiny, price-less, rating-less tile two screens down — nothing that rewards the click the subject earned.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email despite "*MAJOR* markdowns" language in the subject.
- The body is an undifferentiated grid of unrelated categories (AC units, gazebos, fountains, bird feeders) with no narrative thread — it reads like a generic weekly circular, not a targeted send.
- Subject uses ALL CAPS + asterisks (`*MAJOR*`), a mild spam signal, and runs slightly over the mobile-friendly length.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Build the hero around the subject's promise.** If the subject says tree skirts, the hero image and headline should be a tree skirt with its markdown price — not a generic "summer markdowns" banner. This single fix would resolve the biggest miss in the email.
- 2. Give the tree skirt tile a price, star rating, and larger placement — right now it's underselling the one thing the subject sold.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("through Sunday," "ends midnight") to back up "MAJOR markdowns" urgency language.
- 4. Drop the ALL CAPS + asterisk styling in the subject — it reads spammy without adding clarity.
- 5. Tighten the body to 2-3 categories that plausibly relate to a "for less" clearance theme instead of the full 10+ category grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tree skirts, marked way down for the season`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your tree skirt is up to 70% off right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy up your tree base before prices go back up`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: more clearance picks across the home`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This Wayfair MJML/Unbounce-style HTML email renders via table-based layout with standard MSO/Outlook fallbacks; automated QA flagged widespread missing `alt` attributes, several non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and no visible List-Unsubscribe/DKIM headers in the captured payload.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 of 73 total links were probed (61 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); several probed links returned `429 Rate limited` responses (e.g., `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`, `wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, `wayfair.com/?...`) — server-side rate limiting on the prober, not confirmed link breakage, but 4 additional links went unprobed due to a 70s time budget cutoff (e.g. `.../filters/popular-picks-se47289.html?eid=35094`, `.../wayfair-rewards?...`). These remain unverified.
- All probed links carry a consistent tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) — internally consistent across links, no malformed or duplicate params observed.
- 20 open/impression-tracking pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...`), not HTTPS. Mixed-content pixels like this are commonly blocked by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) and by browsers when the email is viewed in a webmail iframe, which can suppress open/attribution tracking for this batch.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text, including primary hero creative (`0811_SummerMarkdowns_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg`), the header logo image (`wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`), product/SKU header images, review star-rating icons (`stars_11.png`, `stars_10.png` ×5), and all 20 HTTP tracking pixels. Screen readers and clients with images blocked by default will render this content with no fallback text, and the hero/CTA image link (`"[image: 0811_SummerMarkdowns_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg]"`) has no visible link text alternative.
- One image filename contains a URL-encoded artifact suggesting a broken asset name: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (literal `%o` in the path, not a valid percent-encoding sequence) — worth confirming this resolves correctly rather than 404ing on some CDNs.
- Standard MSO/Outlook conditional CSS and `u-Mobile*`/`u-*Width` responsive classes are present and structured normally; no malformed media query syntax observed in the truncated `<style>` block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`, `[[token]]`) were found in the truncated HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected in the captured message — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this capture alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header also not detected, so one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured headers — sender authentication status is unknown from this data; this is flagged as a relay-capture limitation, not a confirmed authentication failure.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in the footer per the QA prober's "always included" note for footer/utility links, though its specific URL was not among the 12 probed links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Landing links point to matching Wayfair domains for their CTA context (e.g., "Shop Sale" → `/daily-sales`, "On Sale Now: Tree Skirts" → `/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-tree-skirts-se44808.html`, "Wood deck boxes" → `/daily-sales/filters/wood-deck-boxes-se82146.html`), so surface-level topical alignment looks correct.
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params are used; instead Wayfair uses a proprietary tracking schema (`refid=MKTEML_121694`, `maiid=15950`, `emlid=101`, `mdlid=...`, `eid=...`). This is consistent internally but means standard UTM-based attribution tooling won't parse this campaign without custom mapping.
- App deep-link CTA ("Get Exclusive Deals on the App") uses AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?af_dp=wayfairapp://home...`) with a proper `af_web_dp` HTTPS fallback (`wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`) — correct deep-link fallback pattern, though both instances returned 429 during probing and weren't confirmed live.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels over HTTPS to prevent mixed-content blocking and preserve open-tracking accuracy.
2. Add `alt` attributes to all 24 flagged images, prioritizing the hero banner, header logo, and SKU/product images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for the pure tracking pixels).
3. Fix the malformed filename/path `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` (stray `%o`) and verify the asset resolves.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) headers are actually present at the SMTP/MIME level — re-verify with a direct raw-header capture outside the AgentMail relay, since this tool's absence-of-evidence isn't conclusive.
5. Re-probe the 4 time-budget-skipped links and re-check the 6 rate-limited (429) links once the target server's rate limit window clears, to get a definitive pass/fail.
