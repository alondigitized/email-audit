**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**

- This is one of the more coherent Wayfair sends in this run — the subject ("On sale now: pergolas") actually matches the first tile in the hero grid, which is a real improvement over recent mismatches like the 2026-08-02 "AREA RUGS" send that opened on a generic clearance banner instead. The top module (50% off members-only pop-up sale) and the four-tile outdoor-structure grid (pergolas, privacy screens, gazebo deals, gazebos) form a tight, on-theme opener.
- It falls apart after that. The email sprawls into eight unrelated categories — futons, closets, hot tubs, sheds, massage chairs, dog beds — that have nothing to do with pergolas or even outdoor living broadly. Worse, there's a visible render defect: a blank white box with a small broken-image icon sits right before the footer, and the recycled "Open Box Deals" banner (same vague "shop big discounts," no percentage, no category) that was already called out in the 2026-08-07 "Open Box Steals" send is back verbatim.
- Net: solid subject-to-hero alignment drags down a bloated, unfocused body with an outright rendering bug. Middle of the pack for this brand's run — better than the mismatched sends, worse than a clean execution would be.

- The 50%-off members-only banner up top is a quantified, specific hook rather than vague "big sale" language.

## What's weak

- A blank white box with a small broken-image icon appears mid-email, just before the footer icon row — a visible render bug.
- The "Open Box Deals" banner repeats the same non-specific "shop big discounts" copy already flagged in the 2026-08-07 "Open Box Steals" send — still no percentage or category attached.
- After the pergola/gazebo grid, the email pivots into eight unrelated categories (futons, closets, hot tubs, sheds, massage chairs, dining sets, dog beds, wall lights), diluting the pergola focus the subject promised.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere, despite this brand's typical "ends tonight" clearance framing elsewhere in the run.

- 2. Give "Open Box Deals" an actual number or named category instead of "shop big discounts" — this exact vagueness was already flagged two sends ago and hasn't been addressed.
- 3. Cut the "but wait, there's more" section to 2-3 patio/outdoor-adjacent items instead of 8 unrelated home categories, so the body stays on the pergola/backyard theme the subject sets up.

# Wayfair — "On sale now: pergolas" — Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard BEE/Unbounce-style responsive HTML email (XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, `u-*` utility classes, mobile breakpoint at 480px) sent via a batch ESP (`graymatter_batch`) with heavy click-tracking redirection on every link. Automated QA returned a 57% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 warning categories (compliance headers, deliverability headers, and image/link hygiene).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 7 of 12 probed material links returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** during automated probing, including the primary CTAs "Shop Sale," "On sale now: pergolas," "Our best gazebo deals," and the app-install link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). QA notes these "may be valid" — the 429s indicate the destination is throttling the prober, not confirmed broken links, but they could not be verified as reachable.
- 4 additional links were **never probed** (time budget exceeded at 70s): `on-sale-now:-gazebos--se82150.html`, `daily-sales/clearance`, homepage root, and `futons-under-$200-se70954.html`.
- Every link carries a large tracking parameter set (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `sm`, `refid=MKTEML_121343`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`) — consistent and well-formed across all sampled URLs, no malformed or duplicate-param instances observed.
- Open/impression tracking pixel (`t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...`) present and correctly loads as an image tag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 tracking-pixel `<img>` tags** (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are missing `alt` attributes and served over **plain HTTP, not HTTPS** — non-HTTPS resources may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content policies, which would break impression tracking silently.
- **5 content images missing `alt` text**: `0804_MemebersOnlyPopUp_Skinny-NonMembers.png`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`, `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_middle_3_purpl[e]`, and `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` — all fail basic screen-reader accessibility.
- Notable filename defect: `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` contains a stray literal `%` character mid-word ("Outd**%**oorClearance"), suggesting a broken URL-encode/decode step in the asset pipeline (likely intended to be "OutdoorClearance"). Verify this doesn't indicate the underlying asset URL is malformed at the CDN.
- Mobile breakpoint CSS (`@media (max-width: 480px)`) is present and structurally standard (Outlook/Gmail-specific hacks for `u-P--5`, `data-outlook-cycle`, `-webkit-touch-callout` included) — no rendering-blocking issues found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders, or template variables (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. No issues found — note that the source was truncated, so full-document verification of personalization logic outside the visible portion was not possible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — inconclusive rather than confirmed-absent; QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — same caveat; if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) is not supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be determined via the relay used for capture.
- A footer/unsubscribe link is present in-body per QA (an unsubscribe link was included in the always-probed set), but its header-level RFC 8058 compliance could not be confirmed.
- **Recommendation caveat**: these three findings need to be re-verified against raw SMTP headers from the original delivery path (not the AgentMail relay) before treating them as sender-side defects.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All primary CTAs route to `wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/...` pages that thematically match the subject line ("On sale now: pergolas" → `on-sale-now:-pergolas--se82161.html`; companion "Our best gazebo deals" → `our-best-gazebo-deals--se82152.html`), indicating correct content-to-landing-page alignment.

- App-deep-link CTA uses AppsFlyer OneLink (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) with both `af_dp` (deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback) params correctly populated — fallback behavior appears correctly configured.

2. Add `alt` text to the 5 content images and, at minimum, empty `alt=""` (decorative) to the 20 tracking-pixel images for accessibility compliance.
3. Migrate the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
4. Investigate the malformed filename `0728_Outd%oorClearance_SkinnyHero-VP.jpg` at the asset/CDN level — likely an encoding bug in the image pipeline.
5. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers against raw sent-mail headers (not the relay capture) to close out the compliance warnings — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance if send volume qualifies.
