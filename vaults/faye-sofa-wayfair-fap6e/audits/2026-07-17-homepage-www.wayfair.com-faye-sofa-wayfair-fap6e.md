# Homepage snapshot · www.wayfair.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I refreshed the app and landed straight in a blaring red sale. Wayfair is screaming "72-HR CLEAROUT" at me right now — which, honestly, good. That's basically why I have push notifications on. But beyond the deal itself, there's almost no lifestyle context, no mid-century modern imagery, nothing that says "this is for your starter home" — just a big red coupon page.

## What's working

- **"72-HR CLEAROUT" urgency banner** — the pinned strip at the top *and* the hero together double-stamp the same deadline. For someone who refreshes for flash sales, this is catnip. I know exactly what the page is about in 0.5 seconds.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA placement** — dead-center on the hero, high contrast purple-on-red pill. I can't miss it even while scrolling fast.
- **Product thumbnails below the fold** — I can already see what looks like a white cubby bookshelf and a curved bench/ottoman, which are exactly the kinds of pieces I'm shopping. The wishlist hearts let me save without committing.
- **Search bar with "Find anything home..." placeholder** — I know I can type "mid-century sofa" and go directly there. That's a lifesaver when I'm hunting something specific.

## What's weak

- **Zero lifestyle imagery** — the entire hero is a red graphic with text. There's no styled room, no cozy living-room shot, no "this is what your first home could look like" moment. I browse photos for hours — this page gives me nothing to browse.
- **No returning-shopper hook** — I have a Wayfair account, I've bought things, I have a wishlist. None of that is surfaced. Where's "pick up where you left off" or my rewards balance?
- **"Up to 60% off" is vague** — I know it means the best item is 60% off and everything else is 20%. No free-shipping threshold is visible, which matters because shipping always sneaks up on me.
- **One active carousel dot out of five** — there are four more hero slides I'm not seeing. The dots are tiny; on a busy day I'll tap SHOP NOW without ever swiping.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "free returns," no review count on the featured products. For a clearout event I want to know I can return it if it arrives and looks nothing like the photo.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the pure-graphic hero for a styled-room lifestyle shot** with the sale badge overlaid — show a mid-century-adjacent living room in clearance, so I'm shopping a mood, not just a discount.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip** directly under the search bar ("Free shipping on orders $35+" or whatever the threshold is) — it's a conversion lubricant for anyone adding their second item.
- **Surface a "Your wishlist has X items on sale" or "Picked for you" module** below the hero for logged-in users — I will tap that immediately.
- **Show star ratings + review count on the product thumbnails** even at this small size — a "4.7 ★ (1,240)" badge would give me the confidence to tap through instead of hesitating.
