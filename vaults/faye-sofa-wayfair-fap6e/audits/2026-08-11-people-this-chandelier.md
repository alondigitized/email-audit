# People ♥ THIS CHANDELIER →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a rare case where the subject line and hero creative genuinely agree — "People ♥ THIS CHANDELIER →" lands you on a hero image of an actual chandelier with the tagline "shimmer, shine & save with statement chandeliers." Compared to the mismatched sends logged in this brand's recent history (bookcases landing on a generic clearout banner, gazebo deals opening on old copy), that's a real win.
- But the body collapses almost immediately after the hero. From "the must-shop steals" grid onward, a large share of the product tiles are broken — plain white boxes with a small blue "image missing" icon instead of product photography, running all the way down through "your home, these picks" and "shop before they're gone." This isn't as catastrophic as the 2026-08-09 "Our best gazebo deals" send (3.00/10, "nearly every product image... broken"), but it's the same recurring failure mode for this brand, and it undermines an otherwise-decent open.
- Net: strong subject-to-hero coherence gets dragged down by a body that looks half-broken. This lands mid-pack — better than the brand's worst sends, but the broken-image problem needs to be flagged as a recurring production issue, not a one-off.

## What's working

- Subject ("THIS CHANDELIER") accurately predicts the hero creative, which explicitly calls out "statement chandeliers" — a genuine improvement over this brand's typical mismatch pattern.
- The 60% off badge and "72-HR CLEAROUT — ENDS TONIGHT" banner are legible and sit clearly at the top of the fold.
- Star-rating social proof is visible on a few product tiles in the "your home, these picks" section.
- Section dividers (purple bars: "the must-shop steals," "your home, these picks," "shop before they're gone") keep the long email visually organized despite its length.

## What's weak

- Widespread broken product images — small blue placeholder icons instead of photos — across at least a dozen tiles in the mid and lower body.
- The email is extremely long with heavy repetition of near-identical two-column grids, several of which have no visible product image at all, just a category link.
- "72-HR CLEAROUT — ENDS TONIGHT" is the same recycled urgency banner flagged in multiple prior sends this run (08-08, 08-09) — it no longer reads as credible urgency to a repeat subscriber.
- No pricing is shown anywhere near the chandelier hero itself, despite the subject promising a specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken image pipeline before this ships — that's the single biggest lift available; a subscriber scrolling past a dozen blank boxes will not trust or click anything below the fold.
- 2. Cut the email length by roughly half — collapse the repeated two-column grids into 2-3 curated sections instead of six-plus near-identical ones.
- 3. Retire "ENDS TONIGHT" until it's actually true for a specific SKU or category — reusing it nightly (per prior sends) erodes urgency credibility.
- 4. Add a visible price or "starting at $X" on the chandelier hero itself, since that's what the subject promised.

## Technical Audit

Technical Summary
This is a Wayfair batch marketing email built on a legacy Unlayer-style XHTML template with heavy inline media-query CSS. Automated QA found no missing/broken material links, but flagged compliance header gaps, widespread missing alt text, and one non-HTTPS tracking pixel pattern repeated 20×.

## Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed; all returned either a pass or a 429 (rate-limited, not confirmed broken) — no confirmed dead links. The 429s hit `www.wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me` links sharing the same batch (`batchid=15`), consistent with server-side rate limiting rather than link failure.
- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget (`/daily-sales/filters/...` category links) — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- Tracking/attribution params are consistent across links: `_emr`, `_eml`, `wfcs=cs7`, `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid=...`, `refid=MKTEML_121670`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=15927` — internally consistent, no mismatched IDs observed.
- Open-tracking pixel `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000...019` (20 sequential instances, `s` incrementing) is served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged by QA as "may be blocked" by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, which would silently degrade open-rate measurement.
- Beacon `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` (base64-encoded `EventType=EmailOpen`) is HTTPS — fine, but also missing `alt=""`.

## Rendering & Accessibility
- **20+ images missing `alt` text**, including all content images (chandelier hero, sale banners, review star ratings, open-box promo) and all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` tracking pixels. Content images should carry descriptive alt; tracking pixels/spacers should explicitly use `alt=""` rather than omitting the attribute, since screen readers otherwise fall back to announcing the filename/URL.
- Template uses legacy Unlayer output: XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, extensive `!important`-laden `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules, and Outlook-specific hooks (`data-outlook-cycle`, `u-OutlookAppStackingFix`) — standard for this ESP tooling, no defects observed in the truncated markup itself.

## Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization fallback text found in the visible source/QA output. No issues found.

## Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing it rather than true absence, but as observed this email lacks the one-click unsubscribe header required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance (enforced since Feb 2024).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click POST unsubscribe cannot be confirmed present.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link was included per QA ("an unsubscribe link is always included") but was deprioritized/not individually probed in this pass — presence not independently confirmed by link-check, only inferred from the QA methodology note.

## Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params present — this ESP uses its own proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `_eml`, `refid`, `maiid`, `emlid`, `wfcs`) instead of standard UTM, so GA-style UTM continuity cannot be evaluated with this scheme.
- Destination domains are consistent with sender brand (`wayfair.com`, `wayfairapp.onelink.me` deep link to app home) — no off-brand or mismatched redirect targets observed.
- Chandelier hero image links to `wayfair.com/lighting/sb0/chandeliers-c215419.html`, correctly aligned with the subject line ("THIS CHANDELIER") and creative content.

## Recommendations
1. Serve the `li.wayfair.com/imp` open pixel over HTTPS to prevent silent tracking loss in mixed-content-blocking clients (20 instances affected).
2. Add `alt` attributes to all content images (descriptive) and tracking/decorative images (`alt=""`), particularly the 20 impression pixels and both review-star icons.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the originating ESP (Wayfair/Salesforce or similar) — this may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true compliance failure; verify against raw SMTP headers outside AgentMail.
4. Re-probe the 4 time-budget-skipped category links and the 6 rate-limited links on a retry pass to fully confirm link health before this report is treated as final.
