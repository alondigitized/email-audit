That's a low-trust pattern for a subscriber who's noticed the mismatch before.

## What's weak

- The hero is the only nursery/baby-relevant moment in the entire email — everything below (clocks, décor, birdbaths, office chairs, closeout deals, table lamps, propane fireplaces, toilets) is unrelated.
- "SHOP. SAVE. REPEAT." section has nothing to do with changing tables and reads like filler copy recycled across sends.

- Cadence concern: this is the same generic-grid template as the 09/23 farmhouse email and 09/21 rocking chairs email, just with a different hero swapped in — a subscriber will notice the repetition.
