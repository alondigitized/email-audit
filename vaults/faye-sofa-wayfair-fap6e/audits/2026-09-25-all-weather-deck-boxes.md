# ALL-WEATHER DECK BOXES.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This send is broken, and not in the "boring but fine" way this brand usually is — it's broken in the "the image server didn't load" way. The hero banner is a blank white rectangle with a tiny broken-image icon centered in it, and the very first product tile — the one meant to actually deliver on the subject line "ALL-WEATHER DECK BOXES." — is also a blank box with a broken-image glyph. There's a second broken image lower in the email too, right before the footer icons. Whatever this email was supposed to open with, it isn't what arrived.
- Ironically, once you scroll past the wreckage, the email recovers: the dinnerware tiles render cleanly, the rewards banner is legible, and the futon/small-space section is a genuinely nice, well-composed lifestyle shot. But none of that rescues the top of the email, which is where opens turn into reads or turn into deletes.
- This continues a pattern flagged in prior audits of this brand — subject promises one hero product, the email fails to deliver it cleanly (see 2026-09-23 "CHANGING TABLE PADS under $30" and "FARMHOUSE & RUSTIC DECOR"). Here it's worse: it's not a weak payoff, it's a technical failure. Judgement: this is a bottom-of-the-pack send, dragged down entirely by render bugs a QA pass should have caught before go-live.

## What's working

- The dinnerware tiles ("Dinnerware Sets From $25," "On-Budget Dinnerware Sets," "Oven Safe Dinnerware Sets") render with real, appealing lifestyle photography.
- The Wayfair Rewards banner ("get 5% rewards... on every item, always") is clean, on-brand, and easy to parse.
- The futon/"small-space all-stars" section has a strong, well-lit hero photo with clear overlaid CTAs ("Storage included," "Small-space faves").
- Footer iconography (Rewards, Financing, Professional, Exclusive Brands) and the app-download banner are intact and consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- The hero banner — the very top of the email — is a blank white box with a visible broken-image icon. No headline, no offer, nothing.
- The "All-Weather Deck Boxes" tile, the one product the subject line explicitly promises, is also a broken image — just a text link under an empty box.
- A third section (just before the footer icon grid) is also a blank box with a broken-image icon.
- Subject says "deck boxes," but the only working visual content in the entire email is dinnerware and furniture — the deck box payoff never actually appears.
- This is a more severe version of the bait-and-switch pattern already logged in this brand's history (2026-09-23 sends); here it's not just a weak match, it's a total non-delivery.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken images before anything else.** Three separate blank/broken-image blocks in one send is a QA failure, not a creative one — this should have been caught in a pre-send render check across clients. Nothing else in this email matters until this is resolved.
- 2. **Make the deck box hero actually deliver.** If deck boxes are the subject line's promise, the top of the email needs a real photo of a deck box in a real outdoor setting — this is currently the single biggest gap between promise and delivery.
- 3. **Don't let dinnerware outrank deck boxes.** Right now, once images load, dinnerware gets equal or greater visual weight than the subject's actual product. Reorder so the hero product visually leads.
- 4. **Add urgency or a real percentage-off callout to the deck box offer** — currently there's no visible discount tied to the named hero product, only "From $25" attached to dinnerware.
