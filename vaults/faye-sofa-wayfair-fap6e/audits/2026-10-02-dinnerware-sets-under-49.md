## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

The affected links include `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/dinnerware-sets-under-%2449-se16517.html?eid=220739&…` and `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?…&sltid=2`.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 48 skipped. 4 more were unprobed after the 70s time budget:
  - `…/dinnerware-set-deals-for-you-se18907`
  - `…/outdoor-fireplaces-by-real-flame-se7`
  - `…/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`
  - `…/wayfair-rewards?houseware_hva=…`
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA note says one is always probed, but no unsubscribe result appears in the output. It could not be confirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** 20 impression pixels use plain HTTP: `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=f2ae6175-…`. Clients that block mixed content or non-HTTPS images will drop them, which undercounts impressions.
- **Insecure fallback URL.** The app link's `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app` is also plain HTTP.
- **Unescaped apostrophe in an image URL.** `…/WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als-Skinny-Loyalty…` contains a literal `'`. It can break in attribute contexts or strict URL parsers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 25 `<img>` elements were flagged, including:
  - Linked images: the flush-mount hero (`wfus_jt800_batch9_homeimprovement_flushmounts_gene…`, 4 instances), `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_3_purple.png`, `email-sku-headers_test-09.jpg` and `0929_bigfurnituresale_skinnyhero-general.jpg`.

  - Disclaimer image: `loyaltyacquisitiondisclaimer.jpg`, which carries legal text.
- **Effect on linked images.** Where an image is the only content of an `<a>`, the link has no accessible name. The QA link labels fall back to `[image: <filename>]`, for example the flush-mount link.
- **Tracking pixels.** `b.php` and the 20 `imp` pixels are flagged for missing alt. If they carry `alt=""` the flag is a false positive for decorative pixels, and the truncated source doesn't show which. They should carry `alt=""` with explicit `width`/`height`.
- **Mobile CSS.** The `@media` block is well structured, with `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` Gmail and Outlook hooks. The truncated source gave no confirmed rendering defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` is not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) is not found, with the same caveat. This matters for the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` matches the link and pixel domains, but alignment was not tested.
- The footer (physical address, in-body unsubscribe) is outside the truncated source and was not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTM parameters.** Links use proprietary parameters: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=2`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121694`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16382`, `mdlid`. If the analytics setup depends on `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, attribution will not be picked up automatically. This is not an error if their internal tracking handles it.
- **Inconsistent campaign label on the app link.** The onelink URL has `c=Triggered` and `pid=Email`, while every other link says `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send labelled "Triggered" may skew reporting.
- **Deep link and fallback are present.** `af_dp=wayfairapp://home` and `af_web_dp` are set, but the fallback uses HTTP (see section 2).
- **Landing alignment.** The "Dinnerware Sets Under $49" link goes to `/daily-sales/filters/dinnerware-sets-under-%2449-se16517.html`, which matches the subject. The `%24` encoding is correct. Several other links go to the generic `/daily-sales` or the homepage `/`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels and the `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to all linked images and the disclaimer image. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels and the decorative star images, with a text rating alongside.
3. Rename or percent-encode the asset containing `'` (`WFUS_0824_LaborDay-WFREarlyDe4'als…`). Remove the test-named and old-campaign assets from the template.
4. Re-run link probing with throttling or retry backoff to clear the 429s. Cover the 4 unprobed links and the unsubscribe URL explicitly.
5. Check the raw `.eml` headers directly for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them.
6. Standardize the campaign label (`c=Triggered` versus `graymatter_batch`) and confirm that attribution works without `utm_*`.
