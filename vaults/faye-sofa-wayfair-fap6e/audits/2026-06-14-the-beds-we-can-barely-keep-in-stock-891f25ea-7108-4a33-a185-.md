# The BEDS we can barely keep in stock
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- The subject line — "The BEDS we can barely keep in stock" — is actually the best creative move Wayfair has made in this cadence since the June 12 "Open Box steals" email (8/10). No emoji pile-up, no ALL CAPS sledgehammer, just clean scarcity language on a specific category. It earns the open.
- The body does not honor that promise. What arrives is the same HOT SUMMER SALE multi-category template that has been cycling since June 11: a bedroom hero image at the top, then the Wayfair rewards loyalty strip, then a product grid that drifts into outdoor dining sets, patio umbrellas, and lounge seating. The subject creates a beds-focused frame; the email fills it with whatever is in the rotating inventory. This is the central failure.
- The loyalty section ("Earn 5% rewards... on every item, always!") is a genuine asset and is one of the better-placed trust signals Wayfair shows in this send cycle. But it can't rescue a category mismatch. Overall: a good subject wasted on the wrong template.

## What's working

- Subject line is the strongest in the recent cadence — scarcity framing without emoji noise, under 50 characters, specific category.
- Wayfair rewards strip is prominent and clearly worded; loyalty reinforcement mid-email reduces price friction.
- Hero bedroom image is coherent with the subject and sets correct expectations — at least for the first fold.
- Render is clean: no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds.

## What's weak

- Body betrays the subject: outdoor umbrellas, patio dining sets, and lounge chairs dominate the product grid — not the "beds we can barely keep in stock."
- No specific bed is featured with scarcity detail (limited qty, "only X left," style name) — the subject's urgency claim is never substantiated in the email.
- "Shop for your space" CTA is the same generic copy used in every HOT SUMMER SALE send; the subject deserved a "Shop these beds" button.
- Sixth-or-more HOT SUMMER SALE template in 72 hours — cadence fatigue is real, and the mixed grid reinforces the feeling that the category hook is just a subject-line costume.
- No visible price or discount inside the email; the HOT SUMMER SALE header is present but no specific markdown is legible at any product.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Deliver the beds:** Swap the multi-category grid for a curated set of 4–6 beds with actual inventory signals ("Only 12 left in queen," "Restocked today"). The scarcity subject creates a promise; honor it.
- 2. **Sharpen the CTA:** Change "Shop for your space" to "See the beds before they're gone" — verb + noun + urgency, matching the subject.
- 3. **Anchor a price:** Show at least one "Was $X / Now $Y" on the hero bed. A percentage banner buried in a header doesn't count.
- 4. **Cut the outdoor grid:** If it must stay, place it below a clear "Also on sale" divider so the beds section doesn't feel like bait.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `The 3 beds selling out this week`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Low stock alert: our best-selling bed frames`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `These styles restocked — but not for long.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 5% back on whichever one you grab.`
