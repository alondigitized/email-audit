# ORNAMENTS & TREE DÉCOR ✨ UP TO 80% OFF ✨ FREE SHIPPING ✨ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair sends its third email of July 23 — following the Black Friday in July launch and the outdoor organization drop — with a subject promising ornaments and tree décor at up to 80% off. The body delivers none of it. What's inside is the same catch-all promotional template: a "picked for you" product grid, category browse links spanning area rugs, bedding, TV stands, cooking gear, mattresses, outdoor furniture, lighting, and entryway pieces, plus a playground/patio product grid midway down. No holiday trim. No tree décor. No ornaments.
- This is the same bait-and-switch Wayfair ran on July 22 with the "END TABLES 🚩 UP TO 80% OFF starts TOMORROW 🚩" send — that email showed rugs and kitchen items instead. The pattern is now a documented habit: category-specific subjects are used as inbox hooks with zero intention of matching the body. That's a trust erosion problem with compounding damage as frequency rises.
- The email itself renders cleanly, the 80% off banner dominates the top fold, and the free shipping reinforcement appears in both the subject and the body. But structural competence doesn't offset a subject that lies. A subscriber who opened for holiday shopping bounces immediately; a general sale shopper stays but didn't need to be lured with Christmas ornaments in July to find their way to a rug grid.

- "EVERYTHING SHIPS FREE" in-body reinforcement echoes the subject-line promise cleanly.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "ORNAMENTS & TREE DÉCOR" — not one holiday product is visible anywhere in the email body. Same playbook as the July 22 end-tables send.
- ALL CAPS throughout the subject plus three ✨ emojis tips into spam-signal territory and truncates badly on mobile.
- No deadline anywhere: "UP TO 80% OFF" floats without a "ends Sunday" or any time anchor to force a decision.
- The email is extremely long with unrelated categories piled on top of each other — rugs, playground sets, mattresses, and entryway furniture in one scroll, no clear editorial throughline.
- Third Wayfair email today with another 80% off claim; cadence fatigue compounds the trust problem from the mismatched subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Ship a body that matches the subject.** If the subject says ornaments and tree décor, the hero image should show a styled holiday vignette and the product grid should feature actual holiday trim. A "Christmas in July" editorial with a "get ahead of the holidays" hook is genuinely differentiating — Wayfair just refuses to execute it.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "48-hour window" in the subject triples the urgency of anything currently present.
- 3. **Cut the subject to under 50 characters and drop the ALL CAPS.** Three ✨ and screaming caps read as discount-bin noise; a clean, confident sentence converts better.
- 4. **Throttle daily send volume.** Three emails in one day with overlapping 80% off claims is fast-tracking unsubscribes.
