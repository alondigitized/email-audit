# Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This email commits the same subject-body mismatch that defined the July 18 send with this exact subject line — and doing it twice makes it worse. "Enjoy FREE shipping over $35. All day, every day." promises a standing perk that is invisible in the body. What you actually get when you open: a 72-HR CLEAROUT 60% OFF hero banner, a Wayfair Rewards pitch ("get 5% rewards... on every item, always."), four loyalty feature tiles, and an app download block. Not one word about free shipping.
- The Rewards section is the real editorial spine of this email — it's large, central, and uses lifestyle imagery. That's a legitimate message. But Wayfair buried it under a clearout banner that contradicts the subject, then gave it only a weak "Take a look →" link instead of a real CTA button. The result is an email that doesn't know what it's trying to say.
- Repeating this exact subject line the day after the July 18 send makes this worse. Subscribers who opened yesterday get déjà vu with a different body. That erodes trust in the subject line as a signal of anything meaningful.

## What's working

- **60% OFF clearout banner is impossible to miss** — big, red, high contrast, first thing the eye lands on.
- **Wayfair Rewards value prop is clear** — "5% rewards on every item, always" is simple, concrete, and well-displayed with clean typography.
- **No visible render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text is legible throughout.
- **Brand family footer** (AllModern, Birch Lane, Joss&Main, Perigold) reinforces the platform breadth without clutter.

## What's weak

- **Free shipping is in the subject but nowhere in the email body** — the defining problem; it reads as bait-and-switch.
- **"Take a look →" is the only real CTA** — no button, no Shop Now, no direct link to the clearout or the rewards program landing page.
- **Two competing narratives (clearout sale + loyalty enrollment) dilute each other** — neither gets a proper close.
- **Same subject line as July 18** — repeating it the next day trains subscribers to ignore it.
- **App download block feels bolted on** — a third ask (after clearout and rewards) that lands below the fold with zero copy motivation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Ditch the free-shipping subject entirely or put free shipping in the email.** Pick one: either add a "Free shipping on orders $35+" banner above the fold and make that the thread, or write a subject that actually matches the Rewards pitch body.
- 2. **Replace "Take a look →" with a real CTA button** — "Start Earning 5% Back" or "Join Wayfair Rewards" with a visible button, not a text link buried under a lifestyle photo.
- 3. **Separate the clearout send from the rewards send** — these are two distinct motivations. Stacking them makes both weaker.
- 4. **Never reuse a subject line the next day.** The July 18 repeat is a credibility tax on every future open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 5% rewards — active on every order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearout — ends in 72 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack rewards on the clearout items you already want.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wayfair Rewards: earn on every item, every time. No catch.`
