# Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Wayfair has sent this exact subject line before — "Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS" ran on July 6 and scored 5/10 in brand history. Today's July 9 send is a near-identical resend, which is the single most damaging thing here: any subscriber who opened the July 6 version sees duplicate creative and a worn-out hook. Resend fatigue aside, the email at least opens with a legitimate rug hero — colorful, patterned outdoor rugs above the fold, which is a slight structural win over the July 8 "BATH MATS" bait-and-switch.
- But the body breaks the promise almost immediately. Below the rug hero and a ColorPlus loyalty callout, the "picked just for you" section features a bed. A bedroom bed. This is the same mismatch that got the July 8 bath-mat email docked: the subject sets an outdoor-rugs expectation and the body pivots to unrelated interior furniture. ## What's weak

- **Duplicate subject line.** July 6 already sent "Inside: 8' X 10' OUTDOOR RUGS." Running it again three days later signals list-management sloppiness and burns open rate among the segment that already saw it.
- **"Picked just for you" shows a bed.** This is the same bait-and-switch behavior that dinged the July 8 Bath Mats email — a bedroom recommendation has nothing to do with outdoor rugs.
