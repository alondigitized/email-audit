## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing email from Wayfair with standard link-tracking and skinny-banner impression pixels; automated QA surfaced compliance-header gaps, missing alt text across images, and mixed-content (HTTP) tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed; several returned HTTP 429 (rate limited) during automated checks, including the app deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`), `wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`, and product links. These are marked as likely valid (429 is a rate-limit response, not a broken-link signal), but link health could not be conclusively confirmed.
- 4 links were left unprobed due to time budget, including `wayfair.com/Circular-Single-Handle-Tub-Faucet-L6122-K~KIBI1162.html` and `wayfair.com/Smart-Toilet-with-Bidet-Foam-Shield-4Season-Temperature-` — status unknown, not pass/fail.
- All checked tracking/CTA links carry consistent parameter sets (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `source=batch`, `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid=MKTEML_139940`, `emlid`, `maiid`), indicating a coherent tracking scheme with no visible parameter corruption.
- 20 open-tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS — flagged as "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked" by mail clients (mixed-content blocking risk in HTTPS-only rendering contexts).

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 25+ images are missing `alt` text, including all `stars_10.png` / `stars_11.png` review-rating icons (repeated across multiple product blocks), the hero banner `48HR-Clearance_Spring2026_Skinny-EndsTonight.png`, `WFUS_0909_WarehouseClearout_Skinny-Affirm.png`, `WFUS_260511_MemorialDay-LAUNCH_Email_SkinnyBanner`, CTA button image `0125_48hourclearance-email-1xbutton-29-wfus.jpg`, and the open-tracking beacon `b.php`. With images blocked (default in most mail clients), screen-reader and no-image users get no fallback text for hero messaging, ratings, or CTAs.
- Extensive mobile media-query rules (`@media max-width: 480px`) are present for buttons, titles, headers, footer links, and Outlook/Gmail-specific hacks (`u ~ div`, `u + .Gmail`), consistent with standard Wayfair ESP templating — no structural rendering issues detected in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization placeholders found in the truncated source.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found by automated check — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than absence in the original send, but as captured, one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown from this capture.
- An unsubscribe link is noted as always included in QA probing scope, but its specific URL/status was not in the truncated findings provided.
- Recommend verifying these three header findings against a raw, non-relayed message capture before treating them as sender-side defects, since AgentMail relay handling is a known confound.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Wayfair's proprietary tracking schema (`_emr`, `wfcs`, `_eml`, `refid=MKTEML_139940`, `maiid=16179`, `emlid=101`) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — internally consistent, but not UTM-based, so cross-platform (GA4/UTM-based) attribution tooling would not parse these natively.
- Landing destinations (`/daily-sales`, individual product PDPs via `wayfair-product-*` and SKU-coded URLs, root domain) are topically aligned with email content (living room / clearance sale), based on URL slugs and link text ("Shop Sale", product image links).
- App deep-link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me`) includes both `af_dp` (deep link) and `af_web_dp` (web fallback to `wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`), which is correct practice for cross-platform continuity.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all content-bearing images (hero banners, CTA buttons, rating stars), even though tracking-pixel/impression images can reasonably stay empty (`alt=""`) rather than omitted.
- Migrate the 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...` tracking pixels to HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-enforcing mail clients.
- Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm whether these are true compliance gaps or capture artifacts.
- Re-probe the 4 time-budget-skipped links and the 429-rate-limited links in a follow-up pass to get conclusive pass/fail status.
