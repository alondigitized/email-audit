# FURNITURE CUSHIONS | Grab ’em ASAP >>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Another day, another Wayfair subject-line con. "FURNITURE CUSHIONS | Grab 'em ASAP >>" promises a specific, urgent cushion deal — the render delivers zero cushions, anywhere, in a 30+ tile firehose covering AC units, pergolas, headboards, sofas, bar stools, kitchen islands, hot tubs, and outdoor privacy screens. This is the exact same template-mismatch pattern flagged in "New score alert: Furniture Cushions" (2026-09-04) and "FURNITURE CUSHIONS we see selling out" (2026-09-03) — three cushion-branded subjects in four days, none of which have actually shown a cushion.
- The email itself is a competently built "everything catalog" blast: 70% off Labor Day banner up top, then category tile after category tile with no real narrative thread. It's not broken, just generic and mislabeled — someone is copy-pasting the same subject-line template onto whatever the merchandising team happened to stuff into the body that day.
- Judgement: this is a bait-and-switch send that will burn trust with anyone who actually wants cushions, and it's part of an established brand pattern, not a one-off mistake.

## What's working

- Top banner is unambiguous: "70% OFF LABOR DAY CLEARANCE" with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- Product tiles render cleanly — no broken images, no lorem ipsum, no overlapping text.
- Category breadth (AC units, sofas, bar stools, kitchen islands) is legible and consistently formatted with captions and price/deal labels.
- Star-rating social proof ("★★★★★ loved & lasting") appears on the sofa section, adding light credibility.

## What's weak

- Subject says "FURNITURE CUSHIONS" — there is no cushion product, section, or mention anywhere in the render.
- No coherent hierarchy: the eye has nowhere to land first beyond the top banner; after that it's an undifferentiated scroll of unrelated categories.
- "But wait, there's more..." framing implies a deal escalation but just introduces more unrelated categories (pergolas, privacy screens, headboards).
- Near-duplicate of the past two cushion-subject sends (2026-09-03, 2026-09-04) — this is the third bait-and-switch cushion email in four days, a real cadence problem.
- Preview/CTA structure gives no single primary action — dozens of equally-weighted "Shop" links dilute intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the subject to the actual body content — if cushions aren't in this send, don't say cushions. This is the single highest-impact fix; it's the third time this exact mismatch has shipped this week.
- 2. If cushions are meant to be the hero, put an actual cushion product/tile above the fold — right now there's nothing to click through to.
- 3. Cut the catalog sprawl — pick 2-3 categories and go deeper (more product, sizing/price) rather than 10+ shallow tiles.
- 4. Stop reusing "Labor Day Clearance" banner art across every send this week — it's now stale/repetitive per brand history.

- 4 links were unprobed due to time budget, including two identical `https://www.wayfair.com/pdp/hokku-designs-18-h-customizable-bench-cushion-for-in` product links and `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`.
