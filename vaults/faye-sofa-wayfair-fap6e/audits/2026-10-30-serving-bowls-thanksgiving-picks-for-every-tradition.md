## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: Wayfair, "SERVING BOWLS 🍁 🦃 THANKSGIVING PICKS FOR EVERY TRADITION"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a 480px mobile breakpoint. The QA run found 0 failures and 3 warnings. The real defects are about 26 images with no alt text, including six star-rating images that are the only content of product links, and 20 impression pixels served over plain `http://`. The missing authentication and unsubscribe headers are probably capture limits of the relay, not confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body markup were not inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No hard link failures.** 12 material links were probed and 46 were skipped. Eight returned 429, e.g. `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale") and `https://www.wayfair.com/m/thanksgiving?...`. A 429 means the server rate-limited the QA probe, so it says nothing about link validity.
- **Four links were not checked** because the 70s probe budget ran out:
  - `/pdp/taylor-connoisseur-digital-cooking-thermometer-with-...`
  - `/pdp/henckels-statement-2-piece-carving-set-jahi1007.html`
  - `/pdp/le-creuset-heritage-gravy-boat-kbgd1355.html`
  - `/curated/turkey-prep-from-$20~ev635670.html`
- **Cleartext impression pixels.** 20 pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=b1f9739e-ce8b-4585-ad4e-30726893ca15`) use `http://`.
  - Clients that block or upgrade non-HTTPS images may under-count impressions.
  - The recipient identifier `p=b1f9739e-...` is sent unencrypted. It is the same GUID as `_emr` in the link URLs.
- **App deep link fallback is non-HTTPS.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. Users without the app land on an `http://` URL and rely on a redirect.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 26 images:**
  - Content images:
    - `wfus_winter2026_gtm_email_thanksgiving_minibahn_s...`
    - `WFUS_Winter2026_GTM_Email_ReviewModule_Thanksgivi...`
    - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_middle_1_pur...`
    - `-wfna_fall2026_gtm_email_review_halloweenph2_send...`
  - Star ratings (6): `stars_9.png`, `stars_10.png` (x3), `stars_11.png` (x2).
  - Tracking pixels (21): `t.wayfair.com/b.php` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` beacons.
- **Unlabeled product links.** The star images wrap the PDP links, so the QA probe reports the link text as `[image: stars_10.png]`. Screen readers get no accessible name for those anchors, and the rating value (the star count) is not conveyed. The affected URLs are the joss-main serving bowl, gibson-elite dinnerware set, and august-grove table runner PDPs.
- **Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`**, with explicit `width`/`height` of 1 and `aria-hidden`, so assistive tech skips them.
- **Stale asset name.** The `-wfna_fall2026_gtm_email_review_halloweenph2_send1` image is a Halloween creative in a Thanksgiving send. Confirm that it is intended and not a leftover module.
- **Responsive CSS looks sound.** It uses a 480px media query, `u ~ div` and `u + .Gmail` Gmail fixes, and an Outlook-app stacking fix. No issues were found in the visible portion.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the supplied source or QA data. Per-link `_eml` values differ (e.g. `4455c459-...`, `22e01d5f-...`), so per-link tracking IDs are rendering correctly.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Inspect the raw message at the sender, because Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `editor@members.wayfair.com` are unverified. Check the original headers, not the relayed copy.
- **Footer not verified.** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the truncated source does not show the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Wayfair uses proprietary parameters on every link: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=15`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `brcid`, `refid=MKTEML_142681`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16651`, `mdlid`, `_emr` and `_eml`. Attribution depends on Wayfair's own tooling, not standard analytics.
- **Parameters are consistent across links.** `csnid`, `batchid`, `varid` and `refid` are identical on every probed link, and `sltid` varies by module position (1, 3, 4, 9).
- **Landing pages reach the intended content.** The hero goes to `/m/thanksgiving`, and the product links reach PDPs with `sku`/`mplid`. Those pages could not be fully validated, since the probes were rate-limited.
- **Long, user-unique query strings.** Every link carries `_emr` and `_eml`, which makes the URLs long and means no two recipients' URLs match.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and give them `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images. For the star images, use alt such as "4.5 out of 5 stars" or move the link onto text.
3. Fix the `af_web_dp` fallback to `https://www.wayfair.com/the-wayfair-app`.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the original message at the sender. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
5. Re-probe the four unchecked URLs, and re-probe the 429 links at a slower rate.
6. Confirm the Halloween-named asset is intentional.
