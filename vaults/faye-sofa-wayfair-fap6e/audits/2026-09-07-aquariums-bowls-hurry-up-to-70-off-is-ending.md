# Technical Audit — Wayfair "AQUARIUMS & BOWLS ⭕ UP TO 70% OFF" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Wayfair ESP (Uniform-style) HTML template with heavy tracking instrumentation; automated QA passed 57% of checks with 0 hard failures but flagged missing compliance headers, widespread missing `alt` text, and insecure (`http://`) tracking pixels.

- 12 of 82 total links were probed (rest deprioritized as footer/utility/social); of those probed, 7 returned **HTTP 429 (rate limited)** rather than a clean pass, including the primary CTAs "Shop Sale" (`wayfair.com/daily-sales?...`) and the app deep link (`wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157?...`). These are inconclusive, not confirmed broken — but they could not be validated as reachable.
- 4 product links were **unprobed** due to the 70s time budget (e.g., `wayfair-product-L397-K~W117038843.html`, `daily-sales/area-rug-clearance~e371602.html`) — status unknown.

- 20 open-tracking beacon pixels (`li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `...019`) are loaded, incrementing by campaign segment/store id — expected volume for a segmented send, not itself an issue.

- Custom `@font-face` (Sofia) references `https://secure.img.wfrcdn.com/.../sofia_reg.woff` with an `mso-font-alt: "Arial"` fallback for Outlook — correctly implemented.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all rating/promo images at minimum (`stars_10.png`→"4.5 out of 5 stars", banner images→campaign description); tracking pixels should use `alt=""` (empty, not missing) to be properly ignored by screen readers.

3. Re-probe the 7 rate-limited (429) links and 4 unprobed links with a longer time budget or backoff/retry logic to confirm CTA and product-link health before send.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` presence directly against raw SMTP headers (not just the AgentMail-relayed capture) and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass via a direct header check, since the relay may be stripping/not surfacing these.
