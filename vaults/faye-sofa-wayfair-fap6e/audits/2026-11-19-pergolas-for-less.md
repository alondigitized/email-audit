# Pergolas for less
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- "Pergolas for less" gets a real hero match. The first image is a large gray-roofed pergola, captioned "Pergolas for less »", and it sits directly under the purple "score styles you ❤️" banner. Two more pergola tiles follow in the first grid ("Gazebos & pergolas from $100" and "On sale now: pergolas"). That is more pergola coverage than most recent Wayfair sends gave their subject, and it's the strongest part of the email.
- After that the email falls into the usual Wayfair sprawl. There's a rugs tile, a kitchen mats tile, an "Open Box Deals" banner, accent pillows, quilts, Christmas trees, dining tables, curtains, ottomans, sinks and shoe racks. The "Early Black Friday ENDS TONIGHT, up to 70% off" strip at the top is the only urgency cue. It competes with the pergola hero and says nothing about pergolas. This is the same catalog-dump pattern as the 11/18 "On sale now: rugs" and 11/16 "Gazebos & pergolas from $100" sends. The 11/16 gazebo email did this better, with a bigger and clearer price anchor.
- Verdict: a good subject-to-hero match on a seasonally odd category in mid-November, followed by about 20 unrelated tiles. Fine for a pergola shopper, forgettable for everyone else. The "for less" promise has no number behind it.

## What's working

- The hero is a large, attractive pergola photo that matches the subject, with no bait-and-switch.
- Three of the first five tiles are pergola or gazebo, so the opening block is on-topic.
- The "ENDS TONIGHT" Black Friday strip at the top gives a credible deadline and a "70% off" anchor.
- Tile captions are short and clear, with a consistent "»" link convention.
- The footer is tidy, with Rewards, Financing and Professional value props and an app QR code.

## What's weak

- "Pergolas for less" has no price or percentage anywhere in the hero. The "$100" only appears in a smaller tile.
- The 70% off banner is generic and isn't tied to pergolas. It also has no clear CTA button besides a small arrow.
- The hero, "Pergolas for less »", and the "On sale now: pergolas »" tile are redundant. The caption is plain text and the hero has no button.
- Below the fold the email turns into a generic catalog: pillows, quilts, trees, sinks and shoe racks. There's no reason to scroll past the pergolas.
- The "Open Box Deals" banner is a large, loud block that pulls attention away from the hero, and it has a small visual glitch where the "a" in "Deals" is replaced by a lamp graphic.
- The "score styles you ❤️" banner is filler copy that takes the prime top spot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the hero. Overlay "Pergolas from $100" or "Up to 70% off pergolas" with a real button ("Shop pergolas"), and drop the duplicate pergola tile.
- 2. Build the whole email around outdoor. Use gazebos, patio sets and fire pits for the first screen, and cut the rugs, mats and sinks entirely.
- 3. Tie the "Ends tonight" strip to the featured category, so the deadline and the subject reinforce each other.
- 4. Cut the length. Keep one banner and about six tiles rather than 20+.
- 5. Remove or shrink "Open Box Deals" so it doesn't compete with the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pergolas from $100, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off pergolas & gazebos`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hardtop, steel and cedar styles, plus free shipping on most.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early Black Friday pricing ends at midnight. Shop shade for next summer.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a responsive `@media (max-width: 480px)` block and per-link tracking parameters. The confirmed issues are about 20 non-HTTPS tracking pixels (`http://li.wayfair.com/imp?...`) and missing alt text on both content and tracking images. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from this capture, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Rate-limited probes (not broken links):** 8 of the 12 probed links returned 429, for example `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales/filters/pergolas-for-less--se82165.html?eid=603460&...` ("Pergolas for less") and `https://www.wayfair.com/daily-sales?...` ("Shop Sale"). A 429 is not a failure, but these links are unverified, not passed.
- **Unprobed links:** 4 links were not checked because of the 70s time budget:
  - `.../on-sale-now%3a-area-rugs-se44339.htm`
  - `.../kitchen-mats-under-%2430-se10022.htm`
  - `.../gazebos-%26-pergolas-from-%24100-se8` (the URL looks truncated in the report)
  - `.../on-sale-now%3a-pergolas--se82161.htm`

  Their status is unknown, not passing.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels:** 20 pixels use `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000` through `s=124126019`, all with `sz=2x1&m=false&p=c993a8d6-...`. Mail clients that block or upgrade mixed content may drop them, which would undercount impressions.
- **App link fallback:** `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` sets `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. That fallback is HTTP, so it depends on a server redirect to HTTPS.
- **Per-link recipient IDs:** `_eml` differs on every link (for example `85b0010b-...` and `441c6455-...` on the two identical app CTAs), while `_emr` stays constant. This is normal per-link tracking, but it means identical CTAs can't be de-duplicated by URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing alt text (6):**
  - `WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-EndsTonight`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purple.png`
  - `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_1_purple.png`
  - `wfus_batchheaderrevamp_sku_top_3_purple.png`
  - `blankBanner%20%281%29.png`
- **Linked images with no usable name:** Several are wrapped in links, and the QA output uses the filename as link text (`[image: WFUS_1112_BFCM_PHASE1_Email_SkinnyTop-En]`, `[image: wfus_batchheaderrevamp_event_top_1_purpl]`). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get a filename, not a destination. The header image links to the homepage and the skinny banner to `/daily-sales`.
- **Tracking pixels lack alt:** The open pixel `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=...` and the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels have no alt attribute. These should use `alt=""` with explicit 1x1 or 2x1 dimensions and be hidden from assistive tech.
- **Placeholder image:** `blankBanner (1).png` appears to be a placeholder asset that shipped live.
- The responsive CSS (`u-FullWidthBlock`, `u-HalfWidthBlock`, `#Container`) is present. I can't see the body in the truncated source, so I'm not flagging any layout problems.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. I saw no unresolved tokens in the visible HTML or the QA data. The body was truncated, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm this as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this needs checking against raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `members.wayfair.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** The QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but the truncated source doesn't show it. The physical address is likewise unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** Links use proprietary parameters instead (`source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=15`, `varid=4`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_121289`, `emlid`, `maiid`, `mdlid`). They are consistent across all links. If downstream analytics expect UTMs, attribution depends on these parameters being parsed.
- **App link campaign label:** The app link has `c=Triggered` while the rest of the email has `source=graymatter_batch`, so a batch send is labeled as triggered in AppsFlyer. This likely skews campaign attribution.
- **Subject-to-landing alignment:** The subject ("Pergolas for less") matches the link slug `pergolas-for-less--se82165` with `unifiedeventid=603460`, so the alignment looks intact. The 429 means the landing page itself wasn't verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all tracking pixels over HTTPS (`https://li.wayfair.com/imp`) and add `alt=""` plus explicit dimensions.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the 6 content images, especially those wrapped in links. Remove or replace `blankBanner (1).png`.
3. Re-probe the 8 rate-limited and 4 unprobed links with throttling or retries to confirm the landing pages.
4. Inspect raw headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating these as defects.
5. Check why the app link uses `c=Triggered` on a batch send, and change its `af_web_dp` fallback to HTTPS.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link and merge tokens.
