## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a single 480px mobile breakpoint. Its main confirmed defects are about 29 missing-alt images (9 content images, the open-tracking pixel, and 20 impression pixels) and 20 impression pixels served over `http://`. The compliance and authentication gaps flagged by QA could not be confirmed, because the headers appear to be stripped by the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 material links were probed. Every failure was a **429**, which means rate limiting by `wayfair.com` and `wayfairapp.onelink.me`, not a dead link. The results are inconclusive for those 8 links, not failures.
- **4 links were not checked.** Probing hit its 70s budget before reaching the `must-have-backless-bar-stools-se6814`, `hybrid-mattresses-for-less-se24283`, and `just-for-you%3a-curtains-%26-drapes-` links, or the `daily-sales?_emr=…` link. The URLs in the QA output are cut off, which looks like display truncation, not malformed hrefs.
- **Insecure fallback in the app deep link.** The `wayfairapp.onelink.me/2420802157` URL carries `af_web_dp=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Fthe-wayfair-app`. That is an `http://` fallback for users without the app, and it depends on a server-side redirect to reach HTTPS.
- **Impression pixels over HTTP.** 20 `http://li.wayfair.com/imp?s=124126000…124126019&sz=2x1&m=false&p=302ee605-…` pixels are non-HTTPS. Clients or proxies that block mixed content will drop them, which undercounts impressions.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://t.wayfair.com/b.php?x=bWNjaD1FQ00…` is the open pixel, and it has no alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (9).**
  - `wfus_0331_btb_2024_email_01_static.jpg`, `_03_`, `_07_`, `_08_`, `_09_`, and `wfna_0331_btb_2024_email_05_static.jpg`
  - `Best-Selling+Washable+Runners.jpg`
  - `WFUS-OpenBox-2025-HVA-1.jpg`
  - Several of these are wrapped in links, e.g. `Best-Selling+Washable+Runners.jpg` links to `/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-washable-runners-se78340.html`. With images off, or in a screen reader, the link has no meaningful name and the probe falls back to the filename.
- **Missing alt on tracking images (21).** The `b.php` pixel and the 20 `imp` pixels have no `alt` attribute. A decorative image should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML source is truncated after the mobile `<style>` block, so I can't check `lang`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, dark-mode handling, or Outlook MSO conditionals.
- **No issues in the visible CSS.** The `u ~ div` Gmail fix and the 480px media queries are well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the QA data or the visible source. The per-recipient values (`_emr`, `_eml`, `csnid`) are resolved. Coverage is limited to the truncated portion of the HTML.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** QA reports both as not found. The checker itself notes the relay may not capture them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Wayfair is a bulk sender, and Gmail and Yahoo require both headers, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the same relay caveat applies. The From domain `members.wayfair.com` should be checked against the original message.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** The probe notes it always includes an unsubscribe link, but none appears in the QA output. The footer is beyond the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters.** Links use Wayfair's own scheme instead: `source=graymatter_batch`, `batchid=3`, `varid=7`, `csnid`, `sltid`, `refid=MKTEML_140011`, `emlid=101`, `maiid=16529`, `mdlid`, `wfcs=cs7`. If downstream analytics expects `utm_source/medium/campaign`, attribution depends on this mapping.
- **Consistent campaign IDs.** `batchid`, `varid`, `csnid`, `refid` and `maiid` are the same on every link, and `sltid` increments by slot position, which suggests the tracking is intact.
- **Landing pages.** The CTA targets (`/daily-sales/filters/best-selling-washable-runners-se78340.html`, `/daily-sales`, `/`) match the subject's "washable runners" theme. Their live status could not be confirmed because of the 429s.
- **Deep link.** The app CTA sets `c=Triggered` while `source=graymatter_batch`. A batch send labelled as a triggered campaign may skew attribution in AppsFlyer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images, especially those inside links, and `alt=""` to the 21 tracking pixels.
2. Serve the 20 `li.wayfair.com/imp` pixels over HTTPS, and change `af_web_dp` to `https://`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message before treating them as defects.
4. Re-probe with a lower request rate or a retry on 429, and include the 4 unchecked links.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to cover the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and `lang`/`role` attributes.
6. Check whether `c=Triggered` is correct for a batch send.
