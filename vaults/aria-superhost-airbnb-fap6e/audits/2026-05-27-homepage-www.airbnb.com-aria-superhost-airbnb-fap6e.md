# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and immediately got a cookie consent wall shoved in my face before I could see a single listing. The brand is Airbnb — I know this, obviously — but the homepage is doing zero selling right now because a modal is eating the entire viewport. I can see a search bar and three tabs (Homes, Experiences, Services), and that is essentially it. Not a great first impression even for someone who already has the app.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" bar** — it's front and center above the modal, so even mid-cookie-consent I can technically start typing a destination. That's muscle memory for me anyway.
- **Tab navigation (Homes / Experiences / Services)** — clean, icon-forward, and the "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services catch my eye. I notice they're leaning into expanding beyond just accommodations.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — persistent and accessible; I can bail to my Wishlists immediately without dismissing the modal.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal blocks 80% of the viewport** — there is no hero, no listing imagery, no pricing, no Superhost filter, no promotional content. I am staring at a legal notice instead of a beach house with 200 five-star reviews.
- **No Superhost or Guest Favorite signals anywhere** — as someone who literally filters to Superhost-only before anything else, I want to see that language on the homepage. It's not here.
- **No cleaning fee transparency** — one of my biggest pain points with Airbnb and there's nothing here addressing it. All-in pricing is apparently a thing now but I can't see it because of the modal.
- **"Accept all" button is the dominant black CTA** — the modal is actively training me to click the biggest button without reading. That's a dark pattern in a legal consent context.
- **Log in is buried in the bottom nav, not surfaced as a personalization hook** — I'm a returning user with wishlists and past trips. There's no "Welcome back, here's where you left off" energy here whatsoever.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal on first tap of "Start your search"** — let me act, then ask about cookies. Don't hold the product hostage to legal compliance. The search bar is right there; using it is implied consent to personalization.
- **Surface a "Superhost stays" or "Guest Favorites" hero card immediately post-modal** — I filter by this every single time. If that language is the first thing I see, I feel like Airbnb gets me.
- **Add an all-in pricing callout to the hero or a sticky banner** — something like "No surprise fees — see the full price upfront" would directly address the biggest reason I abandoned Airbnb for stretches and came back. This is a known trust issue with your core audience.
- **Replace the generic "Start your search" placeholder with a personalized or seasonal prompt** — e.g., "Where to this summer, Aria?" or "Superhosts near you for July" — pull from my wishlist or recent searches to make the homepage feel earned.
