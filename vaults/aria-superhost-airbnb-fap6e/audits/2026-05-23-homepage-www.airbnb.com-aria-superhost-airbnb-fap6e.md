# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and a cookie wall ate my entire homepage. Before I could see a single listing, price, or "Superhost only" filter, I'm staring at a consent modal. What's behind it? A search bar and the bare bones of a nav tab row. As someone who books with surgical precision — Superhost filter, 50+ reviews, all-in pricing checked before I even open photos — this is not a great opening move.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" bar** — prominent, pill-shaped, unmissable at the top. For a search-first product this is the right anchor.
- **Homes / Experiences / Services tab row** — "Homes" is pre-selected, which is correct for my default intent. The "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services at least signal the product is evolving.
- **Bottom navigation** — Explore, Wishlists, Log in are all one tap. Wishlists is exactly where I park trips I'm evaluating 2 months out.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** — it is the entire above-the-fold experience. Before I can see one listing thumbnail or price, I have to make a legal decision. This is not a welcome mat; it's a tollbooth.
- **No "All-in pricing" context visible** — I came here specifically primed about Airbnb's all-in pricing changes and there's no signal anywhere that the platform has addressed this. For a trust-sensitive booker like me, that's a miss.
- **Zero Superhost or Guest Favorite signals** — the tabs say "Homes" but nothing acknowledges that a segment of users filters immediately by quality tier. No "Superhost picks near you" or "Guest Favorite stays" hook anywhere.
- **No urgency or seasonality** — late May should mean Memorial Day weekend, summer inventory, something. The visible interface is completely season-neutral.
- **Log in is buried in the bottom nav** — returning users (me) have to go digging. There's no "Welcome back" shortcut above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal or at minimum auto-default to "Only necessary" with an opt-in toggle** — most users tap "Accept all" reflexively; making it a full-screen blocker harms first impression without meaningful consent benefit.
- **Surface a "Superhosts near you" or "Guest Favorites" module immediately below the search bar** — this single editorial rail signals to quality-focused bookers that the platform sees us.
- **Add all-in pricing language somewhere in the above-fold chrome** — even a small "Prices shown include all fees" badge near the search bar would directly address the trust gap Airbnb has been navigating publicly.
- **Inject a returning-user hook** — if I'm logged in (or recently visited), show my last search or a "Continue planning [destination]" nudge; I book 2 months out and return multiple times before committing.
