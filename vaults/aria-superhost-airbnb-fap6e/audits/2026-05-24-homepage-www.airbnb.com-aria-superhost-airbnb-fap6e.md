# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-24
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and immediately got a cookie wall shoved in my face. The entire homepage — whatever Airbnb is actually trying to show me — is buried behind a GDPR-style consent modal that takes up 80% of the screen. I can see a search bar, three tabs ("Homes," "Experiences," "Services"), and two very aggressively styled black buttons asking me to make a legal decision before I've seen a single listing. This is a brand that knows I'm already here and is still making me do paperwork at the door.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" pill** — clean, minimal, and immediately scannable above the modal. That's the right instinct even if I can't act on it yet.
- **Homes / Experiences / Services tab bar** — the "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services tell me the product is actively evolving. As someone who books experiences on trips, that's mildly interesting. One-tap navigation to categories is the right structure.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — persistent and clean. If I can dismiss the modal, I know exactly where to go next.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire above-the-fold experience.** I opened Airbnb to browse travel, and instead I'm reading GDPR legalese. The modal is not dismissable by tapping outside it. This is the single biggest conversion killer on the page.
- **"Accept all" and "Only necessary" are styled identically** — both full-width dark buttons, stacked. No visual hierarchy between them; no clear "recommended" action. That's lazy and mildly manipulative because it signals neither option is actually preferred, which makes me suspicious.
- **Zero personalization signals.** I'm presumably a returning user — Airbnb has my booking history — and I'm seeing a generic, logged-out state with "Log in" in the bottom nav. There's no "Welcome back, Aria" hook, no recently viewed listings, nothing.
- **No above-the-fold value proposition.** Behind the modal I can barely see the edge of a listing thumbnail and what looks like a partial price. Airbnb is spending my first 3 seconds on cookie compliance instead of "here's a stunning place in Lisbon."

## Recommendations

- **Soft-trigger the cookie consent** — show it as a slim bottom banner, not a full-screen modal. Let me see the homepage before you ask for legal sign-off. Every SaaS company has figured this out; Airbnb can too.
- **Prioritize logged-in state recognition above the fold** — if my session is active, kill the "Log in" tab and replace it with my profile. Surface "Continue planning your trip" or "Wishlists updated" instead of a cold-start flow.
- **Differentiate the consent button hierarchy** — make "Accept all" the primary CTA with a brand-colored fill and move "Only necessary" to a plain text link. The current twin-dark-button treatment reads as a dark pattern.
- **Pin a contextual hook behind or beside the modal** — even a single sentence like "Superhost stays in 100+ destinations" visible at the edges would remind me *why* I opened the app while I deal with the modal.
