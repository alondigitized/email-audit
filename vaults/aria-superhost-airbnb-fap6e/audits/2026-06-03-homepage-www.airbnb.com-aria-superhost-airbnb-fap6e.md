# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb on my phone and the first thing I got was a cookie consent wall blocking everything except a search bar and three tabs. That is the whole story above the fold. Whatever Airbnb is pushing right now — summer destinations, new Experiences, whatever — I cannot tell you, because a modal asking me to "help improve your experience" is currently helping itself to the entire screen. This feels like a brand that knows I'll power through it, and honestly they're not wrong, but it's still a bad first impression.

## What's working

- **Search bar persistence** — "Start your search" sits above the modal and is immediately tappable; muscle memory can get me past this screen in one second
- **Tab navigation with "NEW" badges** — Experiences and Services both have "NEW" labels, which as a native mobile user I clock instantly and find genuinely interesting
- **Cookie modal copy** — it's honest, links to the actual policy, and gives me a real "Only necessary" option instead of hiding it; I respect the choice architecture here
- **Bottom nav** — Explore, Wishlists, Log in is exactly the three things I need on a mobile homepage; clean and scannable

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I booked my last trip at 11pm after getting tipped off about all-in pricing; I came here ready to search, and I'm staring at GDPR compliance instead of listings
- **No returning-user signal** — I have a Superhost wishlist. I have saved searches. I have trip history. None of it is surfaced; the page treats me like a brand-new visitor
- **Zero destination or season content visible** — summer is here; I should be seeing Tuscany or a lake house, not a consent dialog
- **Blocking modal = zero engagement data** — I can't see what's beneath it, which also means I can't evaluate whether Airbnb's all-in pricing change is actually reflected in any messaging

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal** — Fire it after first scroll or first search interaction, not before the hero renders; returning users especially should not be cold-stopped at the door
- **Personalize the search bar placeholder** — For a logged-in or cookied returning user, swap "Start your search" for something like "Back to Italy? Search again" using last-search context
- **Surface a destination tile or season prompt behind/above the modal** — Even if the modal must fire first, the page beneath should be ready to show immediately on dismiss; right now dismissing it is a leap of faith into darkness
- **Add a "Skip to search" affordance** — One tap that bypasses the modal entirely for users who've already consented; native tech users will appreciate the escape hatch
