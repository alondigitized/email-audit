# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and immediately got hit with a cookie consent wall — the entire homepage is buried behind it. What I *can* see is the "Start your search" bar at the top, tabs for Homes / Experiences / Services (the latter two both have "NEW" badges), and the bottom nav. Whatever destination inspo or Superhost filtering I was hoping to see above the fold? Blocked. This feels like a compliance checkbox masquerading as a homepage.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" bar** — clean, centered, immediately readable. Muscle memory for any returning Airbnb user; I know exactly what to do with it.
- **Tab row (Homes / Experiences / Services)** — the "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services are small but noticeable. As someone who books full trips, I appreciate that Airbnb is surfacing non-accommodation options from the same entry point.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — Wishlists is one tap away, which matters for me since I maintain running lists 2 months ahead of a trip.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal detonated on top of everything** — I can't see a single property, any destination inspiration, any Superhost filter, or any pricing. This is the single biggest failure of this visit. A modal that blocks 90% of the viewport is not a "help us improve your experience" — it's a hostage situation.
- **No Superhost or filter signals above the fold** — even before the modal, nothing in the visible chrome hints at quality tiers. For me, Superhost status is table stakes. If that's three taps deep, I'm already annoyed.
- **No all-in pricing callout** — Airbnb made a big deal of showing total price including cleaning fees and service fees. I want to see that commitment front and center, not after I've already entered a destination. It's not here.
- **"Log in" instead of a recognized returning user state** — I likely have an account. Showing "Log in" cold feels like the app doesn't know me, which undermines any personalization promise the cookie modal just asked me to consent to.
- **"NEW" badges on Services with no context** — what is Services? Housekeeping? Experiences adjacent? I have no idea and no copy to tell me without tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or minimize the cookie modal for returning users / after first session** — or at minimum move it to a bottom sheet so the hero is still visible. Hiding the entire homepage is a conversion killer.
- **Surface "Superhost only" as a persistent toggle on the search bar**, not a buried filter — for a vocal segment of power users (me), this is a dealbreaker signal. Even a small "Superhost ✓" chip near the search input would resonate.
- **Add a "Total price shown" trust badge near the search bar** — lean into the all-in pricing change aggressively. Something like "Prices include all fees" in a small line under the search pill would immediately differentiate Airbnb and speak directly to the $300/night-becomes-$450 trauma we've all experienced.
- **Personalize the tab default or add a "Recent searches" row** — if I'm logged in, show me my last search or a saved wishlist destination. I book 2 months out; I'm almost always mid-research on something.
