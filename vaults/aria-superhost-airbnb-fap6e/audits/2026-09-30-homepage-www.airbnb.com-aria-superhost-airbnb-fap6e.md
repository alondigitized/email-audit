# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like Airbnb's logged-out homepage on a phone-sized viewport. There's no hero or campaign, just a search bar, an experiences rail and a stays rail. The only thing it pushes is the new AI search toggle in the black strip at the top. The cookie modal sits right on top of the stays I'd actually browse.

## What's working

- **The search pill (Where / When / Who):** It's big, centered and obvious. I know how to start a trip in one tap.
- **"Guest favorite" badge on the first card:** This is the filter I shop by. Seeing it right on the card is a real trust cue.
- **Price format "$230 for 2 nights · ★4.94":** Price and rating sit on one line. Showing a stay total fits the all-in pricing change I've been tracking.
- **Homes tab in the top nav (All / Homes / Experiences / Services):** My category is one tap away.
- **Experiences rail imagery:** The surf lesson, taco plate and pottery shots are bright and inviting.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal ("Help us improve your experience"):** It takes over the stays rail. It clips the "Available in Irvine this weekend" heading and hides the middle cards. I can't compare listings until I dismiss it.
- **Rail order:** "Explore experiences nearby" comes before stays. I'm here to book a place, and my category is pushed below the fold.
- **No review counts on cards:** I won't book under 50 reviews, and a 5.0 on the "Apartment in Tustin" card could mean 3 reviews or 300. I can't tell.
- **No cancellation or fee info on cards:** I read the policy before the photos. "$618 for 2 nights" says nothing about cleaning fees or flexibility.
- **AI search strip:** It's a black bar with a toggle defaulted to "Off", and it reads like an ad for a feature. It has no benefit copy.
- **No Superhost signal:** I can't filter for Superhost from here. There's only a "Guest favorite" badge on one card.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie prompt:** Make it a slim bottom bar, or show it after the first scroll, so the stays rail and its heading aren't covered.
- **Move "Available in [city] this weekend" above experiences:** Keep the stays rail on top, and add a Superhost / Guest favorite filter chip next to it.
- **Add review counts to cards:** For example "★4.94 (212)". It lets me vet a listing at a glance.
- **Put a "Free cancellation" or "Total price incl. fees" tag on cards:** The total is what I'm reading for, and it gives me a reason to tap.
