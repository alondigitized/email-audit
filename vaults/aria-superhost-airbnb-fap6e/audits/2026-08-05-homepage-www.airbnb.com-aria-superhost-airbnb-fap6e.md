# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This barely reads as a "homepage" — it's a search entry point with a cookie consent wall slapped over 80% of the screen. What I can see behind it (a search bar, category pills, a "Popular homes in San Diego" rail) looks like standard Airbnb, but there's nothing here yet that tells me this brand knows who I am or what I care about. Right now it's pushing "accept our cookies," not travel.

## What's working

- **The search bar up top** — "Start your search" is big, rounded, impossible to miss. Good first tap target.
- **Category pills (All / Homes / Experiences / 🔔 Services)** — clean, scannable, get me toward "Homes" in one tap once the modal's gone.
- **"Popular homes in San Diego" rail with an arrow** — at least it's trying to surface something location-relevant instead of a generic banner.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal eats the whole screen** — I can't see prices, reviews, Superhost badges, or even a full listing photo. It's the first and only thing I actually get to read.
- **Price is cut off mid-digit ("$4...")** — for someone who plans 2 months out and comparison-shops, an unreadable price is a dead end.
- **Zero trust signals** — no review counts, no "Guest favorite" tags, no Superhost badges anywhere visible. That's the first thing I filter by, and I can't even confirm it exists on this page.
- **No cancellation policy or fee transparency anywhere above the fold** — given the whole "all-in pricing" shakeup, I'd expect Airbnb to be leaning into that messaging right now, not hiding behind a GDPR banner.

## Recommendations

- Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom sheet or slim banner instead of a full-screen takeover — let "Popular homes in San Diego" actually be visible on load.
- Surface review counts and Superhost/Guest favorite badges directly on the listing cards in that rail, since that's the #1 filter for guests like me.
- Add a small all-in pricing callout near the search bar ("prices shown include all fees") — timely given the recent pricing change and exactly the kind of honesty signal that gets me to trust a listing.
- Once the modal clears, show a completed price, not a truncated one.
