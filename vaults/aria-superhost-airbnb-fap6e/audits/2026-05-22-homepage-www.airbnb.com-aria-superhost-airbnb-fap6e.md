# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and got a cookie consent wall before I could see a single listing. As someone who books on this app constantly — Superhost-filtered, all-in pricing checked, cancellation policy read before I even look at the photos — this is not the welcome I was hoping for. Whatever campaign or seasonal push they're running right now is completely invisible to me; the entire above-the-fold experience is a legal modal with two black buttons.

## What's working

- **Tab navigation (Homes / Experiences / Services):** Peeking above the modal, I can see the category tabs — Homes is selected by default which is exactly where I'd go. The "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services at least signal something is happening.
- **Search bar at top:** "Start your search" is right there in the pill — if I dismiss the modal, that's my first tap. Clean and familiar.
- **Bottom nav:** Explore, Wishlists, Log in are visible at the bottom. As a returning user I'd go straight to Wishlists.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire homepage experience:** I cannot see a hero image, no destination inspiration, no pricing, no Superhost-filtered entry point, no cleaning-fee transparency cue — nothing. This is a conversion killer on first visit and an annoyance on return.
- **"Accept all" is styled as the dominant action:** Bold black button, "Only necessary" gets the ghost treatment. Classic dark pattern. I notice this stuff.
- **No personalization visible:** I'm a logged-out state here (Log in is in the bottom nav), but even logged-out Airbnb could show destination categories, trending stays, or seasonal content above the fold. None of that is accessible.
- **No trust signal for new visitors:** Someone landing here cold has no idea what kind of inventory Airbnb has, what the Superhost filter looks like, or whether all-in pricing is actually on by default now. The modal eats all of that.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for recognized returning users** — if I've accepted cookies before on this device, don't make me do it again every session. Store that consent properly.
- **Move cookie consent to a slim bottom banner** rather than a full-screen takeover; let the hero breathe and give me something to look at while I decide.
- **Surface all-in pricing toggle or a "prices include fees" badge above the fold** — this is the single biggest trust signal for a power user like me right now, and it's completely absent.
- **Add a "returning guest" hook in the hero area** — a "Pick up where you left off" module with my recent search or saved list would get me past the search bar in one tap.
