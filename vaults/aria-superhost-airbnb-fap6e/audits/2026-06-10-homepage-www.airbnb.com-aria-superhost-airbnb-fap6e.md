# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and got stopped at the door by a cookie consent wall before I could see a single listing. The brand looks clean and minimal in the sliver I can see — familiar Airbnb pink at the bottom, the search bar up top, tabs for Homes / Experiences / Services — but a full-bleed modal asking me to manage my cookie preferences is my entire first impression. For someone who books 2 months out and reads the cancellation policy before the photos, this friction is immediately annoying. It says nothing about where I want to go.

## What's working

- **Search bar placement** — "Start your search" pill at the very top is exactly where I expect it on mobile. Muscle memory kicks in immediately.
- **Tab navigation** — Homes / Experiences / Services is clear, underlined "Homes" tells me where I'm defaulting to without thinking.
- **"NEW" badges on Experiences and Services** — catches my eye and signals the product is actively evolving. I'm aware Airbnb has been pushing non-stay bookings; this acknowledges it without being aggressive.
- **Modal design quality** — if I *have* to see a cookie modal, at least this one is clean: white card, legible type, two clear options, no fake X button that's 4px wide.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I cannot see a single listing, hero image, or destination. For a returning user who wants to browse Superhosts in Lisbon before my morning coffee, this is a full stop.
- **Zero personalization signal** — no "Welcome back, Aria," no recently viewed properties, no saved searches. Airbnb has all of this data on me; the homepage doesn't use any of it.
- **No price or fee transparency anywhere** — cleaning fees are my hot button and the all-in pricing rollout is something I've been tracking. The modal blocks any chance to see if the listing thumbnails (barely visible on the right edge) show total-price or per-night-only.
- **Bottom nav is thin** — Explore / Wishlists / Log in with no Trips or Filters shortcut means getting to "Superhost only, 50+ reviews" requires multiple taps past this wall.
- **No trust signal visible** — no Superhost badge counts, no "X million stays booked," nothing that reinforces why Airbnb over a hotel app.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for returning authenticated users** — I've already accepted. Show me a listing. If legal requires re-consent, do it inline, not full-bleed.
- **Surface a personalized hook above the fold** — even a simple "You have 3 saved places" or "Trips coming up" card would immediately tell me the app knows who I am and is working for me.
- **Show all-in pricing on thumbnail cards** — given Airbnb's public push on total price transparency, make that the hero message for savvy returning bookers like me: "Total price, always" as a trust anchor near the search bar.
- **Add a Superhost / Guest Favorite filter chip under the search bar** — one-tap to my filter defaults instead of burying them three screens deep in the search flow.
