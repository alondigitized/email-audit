# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Now I'll write the review based on what I can actually see in the screenshot.
- I opened Airbnb and immediately got a full-screen cookie wall. That's the experience. I can barely see anything behind it — a search bar at the top, three tabs (Homes, Experiences, Services), and what looks like some listing cards peeking around the edges. Airbnb is pushing tracking consent before it shows me a single listing, a single Superhost badge, or anything resembling the trip-planning tool I came here to use.

## What's working

- **Search bar is still visible at top** — the "Start your search" pill is accessible above the modal, so I technically could dismiss this and search. That's something.
- **Tab navigation (Homes / Experiences / Services)** — I can see all three tabs with "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services, which is a light signal that the product has evolved.
- **Bottom nav is intact** — Explore, Wishlists, Log in are visible; a returning user could navigate to Wishlists directly.
- **Modal renders cleanly** — "Accept all" / "Only necessary" / "Manage preferences" are legible and clearly differentiated.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire homepage experience** — I came to look at Superhosts in the mountains for August and I'm reading GDPR boilerplate. This is hostile.
- **Zero listing content visible** — Not a single property photo, not a price, not a star rating. The whole reason I opened the app is invisible.
- **No Superhost or Guest Favorite filters in sight** — My first filter after every search is Superhost. There's no hint that feature even exists on this screen.
- **"Start your search" CTA above the modal is basically inert** — Even if I tap it, I haven't dismissed the modal, so the interaction flow is broken.
- **Nothing about cleaning fees, all-in pricing, or cancellation policy** — These are the three things I actually care about before I look at photos. Zero signal.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or defer the cookie modal on return visits** — I've used Airbnb for years. Ambushing me with tracking consent on launch is not a welcome-back experience; bundle it into settings for logged-in users.
- **Swap the modal interrupt for a persistent bottom banner** — Let me see listings immediately. A thin "We use cookies — manage" strip at the bottom achieves compliance without nuking the entire above-the-fold experience.
- **Surface all-in pricing callout above the fold** — I specifically came because I heard Airbnb changed how they show pricing. That message should be on the homepage hero, not buried. "See total price before you book" would stop my scroll cold.
- **Add a "Superhost stays" or "Guest Favorites" quick-filter row below the search bar** — One tap to filter to my preference tier. It signals Airbnb knows why I'm here.
