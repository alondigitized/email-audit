# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and the very first thing I see is a cookie consent modal eating the entire screen. Not a great listing. Not a "Guest Favorite" badge. Not even a glimpse of that all-in pricing I've been hearing about — just a wall of legal text asking me to accept cookies before I can see anything. Airbnb looks like a clean, minimal travel platform from what's peeking through the edges, but this gate-first approach is a rough welcome.

## What's working

- **The search bar** — "Start your search" is right at the top, above the modal, exactly where I expect it. Muscle memory kicks in immediately.
- **"Homes" tab is active and underlined** — the default state correctly prioritizes what I'm here for. I don't have to tap anything to get to stays.
- **"Experiences" and "Services" now carry "NEW" badges** — I notice this. I'm already mentally tagging that Airbnb is expanding.
- **Cookie language is genuinely readable** — short sentences, plain English, link to the full policy. For a legal disclosure, this is as good as it gets.
- **Bottom nav is clean** — Explore, Wishlists, Log in are all I need, and they're right there.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I booked 40 nights on Airbnb last year and I'm still getting a full-screen cookie gate. That's a missed opportunity to make me feel like a known user.
- **Zero personalization visible** — no recently-viewed listings, no "pick up where you left off," nothing that signals Airbnb knows I'm a returning power user who filters hard.
- **No Superhost signal anywhere** — I came here specifically because I trust Superhost status. Nothing above the fold acknowledges that filter exists.
- **Completely obscured content** — whatever listing cards are behind this modal (I can barely make out photos and a price starting with "$") are wasted pixels until I dismiss the gate.
- **No all-in pricing call-out** — this was a big deal and it's nowhere visible. I specifically wanted to see if Airbnb is leading with that.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-screen modal on logged-in or returning sessions** — if I've used Airbnb before, serve the banner version at the bottom. Reserve the full-screen gate for true first-time visitors only.
- **Surface a "Welcome back" or recently-viewed rail above the cookie prompt** — even a partial glimpse of my last search destination would signal this app knows me.
- **Add a Superhost filter shortcut to the Homes tab strip** — a single chip that says "Superhost only" or "Guest Favorite" directly below the search bar would tell me immediately that my preferences are baked in.
- **Put the all-in pricing badge or a "total price displayed" toggle visible the moment the modal clears** — that's a trust signal I'd actually notice and appreciate before I even search.
