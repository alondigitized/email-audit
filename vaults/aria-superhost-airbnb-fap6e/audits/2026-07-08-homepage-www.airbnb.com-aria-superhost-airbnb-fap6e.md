# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and immediately got a cookie wall. That's the brand experience right now — before I can see a single listing, a modal is eating the entire screen asking me to help *them* improve *their* experience. Airbnb reads as a mature, familiar platform trying to introduce new verticals (I can see "Experiences" and "Services" both tagged NEW), but none of that matters because I literally cannot see the homepage.

## What's working

- **Search bar placement** — "Start your search" sits at the very top, above the modal, and stays accessible. As someone who always knows where I'm going, that's the right instinct even if I can't reach it yet.
- **Tab structure (Homes / Experiences / Services)** — I can see the category nav peeking above the modal. "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services tell me something is actually happening on this platform, which makes me mildly curious.
- **Modal copy is honest** — "Some cookies are required for the site to function, and cannot be turned off" is unusually straight-talk for a consent screen. I appreciate that they told me what's non-negotiable instead of hiding it.
- **"Only necessary" option is present and visible** — not buried, not greyed out. Respects that I might not want the full tracking suite.

## What's weak

- **Modal gate before any content** — I opened the app and got an internal survey form. I haven't seen a single destination photo, price, or property. For a platform whose entire brand is wanderlust and discovery, leading with a legal consent screen is a conversion-destroying choice.
- **No content visible at all** — I filter to Superhosts, check cancellation policies obsessively, and care about cleaning fees. I can't evaluate any of that because there's literally nothing to look at.
- **"Help us improve your experience" framing** — this headline makes it sound like I'm doing Airbnb a favor by accepting tracking cookies. I'm not. Don't gaslight me into thinking your data harvesting is a gift to me.
- **Cookie modal has no X / dismiss** — I can see "Accept all," "Only necessary," and "Manage preferences," but no quick close. I have to make a choice before I can use the app. That's a dark pattern wearing a polite coat.
- **NEW badges on Services/Experiences** — interesting signal, but completely wasted when the thing covering 90% of my screen is a cookie policy.

## Recommendations

- **Defer the cookie modal** — Let me see one screen of listings first. Fire the consent dialog after I've tapped a category or scrolled once. Every extra second of content I consume before seeing the modal means I'm more invested and more likely to accept and move on.
- **Add a visible dismiss / "X" to the modal** — Let me say "not now" without forcing a binary cookie decision. I'll make the choice later; don't hold the app hostage.
- **Keep the search bar above the modal overlay** — It's already there. Make it tappable through the overlay so power users like me can bypass the modal entirely by just searching.
- **Surface Superhost / Guest Favorite filters as persistent UI** — Once this modal is gone, the first thing I need to see is that Airbnb understands I only want vetted properties. A "Superhost picks" or "Guest Favorites" shelf above the fold would immediately signal that this platform gets me.
