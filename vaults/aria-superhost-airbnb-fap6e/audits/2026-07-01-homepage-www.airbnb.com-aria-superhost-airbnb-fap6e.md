# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Airbnb clearly wants to be the premium travel marketplace for stays *and* experiences, but on first load I'm not seeing the brand — I'm seeing a cookie consent wall that's eating 80% of my screen. What little I can make out underneath suggests geo-targeted "Popular homes in San Diego," which is a fine enough hook, but I have zero visibility into the Superhost filtering, Guest Favorite badges, or any of the signals I actually care about before I'll book anywhere.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" bar** — it's clean, immediate, and I know exactly what to do with it. That pill shape is familiar Airbnb UX and I'm not confused.
- **Category tabs (All / Homes / Experiences)** — the horizontal pill nav above the search results is visible and tappable without dismissing the modal. "Homes" is one tap, which is where I'm headed.
- **Geo-targeting header ("Popular homes in San Diego")** — even behind the modal I can read it, and location-contextual starting points are the right move for someone who books intentionally.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — clean, minimal, correct for mobile. My wishlist is one tap away once I deal with this consent wall.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is full-bleed and blocks all content** — on a phone this is the entire above-the-fold experience. I opened Airbnb to browse stays, not to manage data preferences. This is a hard bounce candidate.
- **Zero Superhost or Guest Favorite signals anywhere** — that's the first thing I filter for, and there's nothing to tell me this platform even surfaces those tiers.
- **No cleaning fee transparency** — Airbnb has been under fire for this specifically. As someone who has feelings about cleaning fees, I need to see all-in pricing messaging or I'm already suspicious.
- **No cancellation policy mention** — I read the cancellation policy before the photos. It's not here. The page doesn't even tease it.
- **No reviews or ratings visible** — behind the modal there appear to be listing cards, but no star counts or review totals are legible.
- **"Log in" in the bottom nav, not "My Trips"** — this suggests the page loaded me in a logged-out or unrecognized state, so there's no personalization whatsoever.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-screen cookie modal on mobile** — use a banner strip at the bottom (like Google Maps) or a compact top-of-screen notice. Full-bleed modals on first load are a known bounce driver and they're burying the actual product.
- **Surface Superhost and Guest Favorite filter chips immediately** below the search bar — these are my first two taps every single visit; baking them into the default view would reduce friction by two taps per session.
- **Add an all-in pricing callout** near the search bar or in the geo-section header (e.g., "Prices shown include all fees") — Airbnb has trained guests to distrust the displayed price, and addressing that anxiety above the fold would be a trust unlock, not just a feature.
- **If the user has a logged-in session, show recently-viewed or wishlisted properties** instead of purely geo-popular picks — I book 2 months out and I revisit listings repeatedly before committing; surfacing my shortlist immediately is a conversion win.
