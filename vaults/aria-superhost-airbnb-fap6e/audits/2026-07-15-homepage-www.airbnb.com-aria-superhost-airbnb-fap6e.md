# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and the first thing I see is a cookie consent wall blocking the entire homepage. What little I can make out behind it is a "Popular homes in San Diego" section — which, fine, geo-targeting is smart — but I cannot assess any offers, hero imagery, or product content because a legal modal ate the page. For a platform I use constantly, this feels like a stale, friction-heavy welcome.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" bar:** Immediately visible at the top, accessible, familiar — I know exactly what to do with it as a power user.
- **Tab navigation (All / Homes / Experiences):** Clean, one-tap access to Homes is right there. As someone who filters immediately to Homes → Superhost, this is a fast path.
- **"Popular homes in San Diego" section heading:** Geo-targeted copy is smart — it tells me Airbnb already knows roughly where I am or where I might be traveling. That's a relevant hook, even if the content beneath it is buried.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in):** Wishlists being one tap away is genuinely useful for someone like me who books 2 months out and stockpiles options.
- **Modal design itself:** Legible, three clear choices, no dark-pattern pre-checked boxes. "Only necessary" is prominently available — I appreciate not being buried to opt out.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal as full-screen interstitial:** This is the entire above-the-fold experience for a first render. On mobile this is especially brutal — the modal takes up 80% of my screen and I cannot see a single listing, price, or image.
- **No logged-in state / personalization surfaced:** The bottom nav says "Log in," which means this is a cold-start view. I'm a repeat Airbnb user — there's no "Welcome back" hook, no Wishlists badge count, no recently viewed. The platform knows me; the homepage doesn't act like it.
- **Zero pricing or Superhost signals visible:** My whole thing is filtering to Superhost + 50+ reviews. There's nothing in the visible real estate that speaks to quality or filtering — I can't tell if I'm about to see garbage listings or gems.
- **No offer or urgency cue:** Airbnb has been rolling out all-in pricing and I've been paying attention. There's no messaging about it here — no "prices now include all fees" trust signal, which would actually land for someone like me who has feelings about cleaning fees.
- **San Diego placement:** I'm not necessarily searching San Diego — if this is pure geo-IP targeting and I'm not traveling there, this section header is immediately noise.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal or replace it with a banner:** Move the cookie notice to a slim bottom banner so the actual product experience loads first. The modal-first pattern on mobile is a conversion killer.
- **Surface "all-in pricing" as a trust badge above the fold:** Given Airbnb's recent all-in pricing change, add a one-line callout — "Prices include all fees. No surprises." — near the search bar. I'd stop and read that.
- **Add a Superhost / Guest Favorite filter chip above the listing rail:** Right below the search bar or as a quick-filter row, a "Superhost" toggle would stop me in my tracks. That's the first thing I tap once I'm inside the app.
- **Logged-in users should see their Wishlists thumbnail, not "Log in":** Returning users should get a Wishlist count badge and a "Continue planning [Trip Name]" nudge in the bottom nav. The platform has this data — show it.
