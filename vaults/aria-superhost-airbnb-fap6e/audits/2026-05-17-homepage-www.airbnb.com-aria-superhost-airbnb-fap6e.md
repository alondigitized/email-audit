# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb and got a cookie consent wall shoved in my face before I could see a single listing. What's visible is almost entirely a modal — "Help us improve your experience" — with three button choices and a lot of legal copy. I can see a search bar up top, the Homes / Experiences / Services tab bar, and a sliver of what looks like a listing card peeking in at the edge. That's it. This is not a homepage experience. This is a bureaucratic intake form.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" search bar** — it's above the modal, immediately tappable, and for a native Airbnb user like me that's the one thing I actually need. Points for keeping that clear.
- **Tab bar: Homes / Experiences / Services** — I can see the "Homes" tab is selected and "Experiences" and "Services" have a "NEW" badge. That's genuinely useful signal; I didn't know Services was a thing.
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — clean, in the right place, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal occupies the entire viewport.** As someone who opens Airbnb regularly and already made my cookie choices at some point, re-hitting this is friction I did not expect. I came here to search, not to manage data preferences.
- **No above-the-fold hero at all.** I have no idea what Airbnb is featuring right now — no destination, no deal, no seasonal push. The homepage is invisible.
- **"Help us improve your experience" is the wrong headline for a cookie banner.** That's a dark pattern in tone — it frames legal compliance as a favor I'm doing them.
- **Zero Superhost or trust signals visible.** For me, the filter I apply first is Superhost. Nothing on this screen tells me Airbnb knows I care about that.
- **All-in pricing transparency — nowhere to be seen.** I've heard Airbnb updated pricing display. Can't tell because there's a modal in the way.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for returning authenticated or cookie-accepted users.** If I've been to Airbnb before on this device, don't reset my session to a cookie wall. This is the highest-priority fix.
- **Lead the hero with a Superhost or quality-filter value prop** — something like "Only Superhosts. Only you." would land directly for my use case as soon as the modal clears.
- **Surface all-in pricing messaging explicitly above the fold.** Since this is a known trust issue for power users, put "What you see is what you pay" front and center. That's a conversion differentiator right now.
- **Add a "recently viewed" or "pick up where you left off" module** immediately below the search bar for logged-in returning visitors — I'm booking 2 months out, I come back to the same search multiple times.
