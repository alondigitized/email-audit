# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened Airbnb and the *first* thing I get is a cookie consent wall that eats the entire screen. I can see a search bar, three tabs — Homes, Experiences (NEW), Services (NEW) — and then absolutely nothing useful because the modal is sitting on top of everything. This tells me the brand is prioritizing legal compliance theater over my actual booking experience, which, not great when I'm trying to plan a trip to Portugal two months out.

## What's working

- **"Start your search" pill bar** — clean, minimal, centered at the top; at least I know where to start typing if I can get past the wall
- **Homes / Experiences / Services tab bar** — the "NEW" badges on Experiences and Services are noticeable and make me mildly curious about what changed, especially the Services tab
- **Bottom nav (Explore / Wishlists / Log in)** — persistent and readable; I know where Wishlists is when I need it

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie consent modal** — this is the first thing I see. Every pixel of actual homepage content is buried. On my phone, this is a hard bounce moment if I weren't already motivated to book
- **Three-button modal layout with two black buttons** — "Accept all" and "Only necessary" look almost identical at a glance; the hierarchy is confusing and I have to actually read both options to differentiate them
- **No personalization signal** — I'm presumably a returning user (I have Wishlists in the nav) and nothing acknowledges that. No "Welcome back," no recently-viewed listing, no saved search
- **No Superhost or Guest Favorite filter visible** — my literal #1 filter and it's nowhere above the fold
- **Zero pricing transparency** — I'm specifically watching Airbnb's all-in pricing changes and there's nothing here that signals whether they've made progress on that

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal for logged-in / returning users** — or at minimum save the consent preference on first visit so it doesn't reappear every session; a modal eating 80% of the screen for repeat visitors is a conversion killer
- **Surface a "recently viewed" or "continue your search" module immediately below the search bar** — I'm a planner who books 2 months out, so I will have an active trip in progress; give me a re-entry point
- **Add a Superhost / Guest Favorite quick-filter chip row below the search bar** — these are table-stakes filters for me and many serious travelers; one tap to apply them from the homepage would reduce friction meaningfully
- **Show all-in pricing in any listing thumbnail that's visible** — given the public conversation about Airbnb's pricing transparency pivot, a visible callout like "All-in pricing on every listing" would land as trust-building, not just nice-to-have
