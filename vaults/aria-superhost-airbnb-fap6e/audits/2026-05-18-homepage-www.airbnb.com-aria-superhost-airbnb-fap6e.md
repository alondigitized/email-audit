# Homepage snapshot · www.airbnb.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened Airbnb's homepage and immediately got a cookie wall thrown at me — the entire above-the-fold experience is a "Help us improve your experience" modal with two buttons and a wall of policy text. I can see a search bar peeking out at the top and the Explore/Wishlists/Log in nav at the bottom, but literally everything else — hero, listings, any filter entry point — is buried. For a platform I already use constantly, this is a frustrating way to say hello. It tells me nothing about where I should book next or what's new.

## What's working

- **The search bar** — "Start your search" sits at the very top and is immediately tappable if I close the modal. That's the right instinct for a search-first product.
- **Bottom nav** — Explore, Wishlists, Log in. Clean, familiar, low friction. I know where I am even through the modal fog.
- **Cookie modal typography** — legible, not visually broken. If I have to see it, at least it's not garbled.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks 100% of the homepage content.** I cannot see a single listing, hero image, category, or promo. For a returning user who books on a regular cycle, this is pure friction.
- **"Accept all" is styled as the dominant CTA** — big black filled button — while "Only necessary" is a second outline button. That's a nudge pattern. As a tech-native who clocks these, it's annoying.
- **No Superhost filter entry point visible.** The one thing I do immediately on Airbnb is toggle to Superhost + Guest Favorite. None of that is reachable while this modal is up.
- **No trip-planning context.** I book 2 months out. There's no "Plan your summer" hook, no destination trending module, no urgency cue. The modal has killed any merchandising.
- **No cancellation policy signal.** I read that before the photos. There's zero trust copy visible — no "free cancellation" badge, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal for logged-in or cookied users.** If I've been on this app before, you have consent history — don't ask me again on every fresh session.
- **Surface one personalized search hook above the modal** — even a slim banner like "Ready for your next trip? Superhosts near you →" so I feel the brand before the legal friction hits.
- **Re-style the consent buttons** so "Only necessary" and "Accept all" are visually equal weight — that dark pattern undercuts trust with exactly the tech-native audience Airbnb targets.
- **Add a "Free cancellation" trust badge in the search bar area** — even that single line of copy signals that Airbnb's cancellation policy is worth reading, which is the first thing I look for anyway.
