# Last chance: Score extra savings during our Fall Style Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Fall Style Sale send in four days. The hero is clean: a blush panel, a big red "UP TO 30% OFF," and a black SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first. The subject, though, promises "extra savings" and the hero never delivers that. It says 30% off, the same as the Oct 25 send. The only "extra" is buried in the fine print, an additional 15% off sale items for up to 65% off.
- The "Last chance" framing is credible, since the fine print says the sale ends 10/29/26 at 10:00 PM CT. But the hero has no deadline, so the urgency lives only in the subject. A subscriber who saw Oct 25's "going fast: up to 40% off" subject over a 30% hero will see the same mismatch here. Both the "extra" and the 65% ceiling are buried.
- The body is a generic 3x3 grid of designer product with brand names and no prices. It's pretty and on-brand, but nothing is tied to the sale. The six category buttons at the bottom are a weak finish. Overall it's a solid, competent reminder with a deceptive-feeling subject and no urgency in the creative.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: red "30% OFF" on blush, with one black SHOP NOW button.
- The product grid is polished and varied: dresses, boots, bags, knitwear. The brand names carry luxury cues.
- The six category buttons make it easy to jump to a department.
- The fine print states the end date and time precisely, so the deadline is honest.
- The app download and personal stylist modules reinforce the brand's service angle.

## What's weak

- The subject says "extra savings," but the hero shows only "up to 30%." The extra 15% is buried in the fine print.
- The "last chance" urgency never appears in the body. There is no countdown, no "ends tomorrow," and no date near the button.
- The grid shows no prices, no sale prices, and no discount badges. Nothing proves the savings.
- All nine product tiles look equally weighted. There is no hero product, and the tiles aren't visibly clickable to specific items.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the button, which adds friction. The mix is also heavily women's despite the MEN nav.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tomorrow, 10 PM CT," ideally with a countdown. That makes "last chance" real.
- 2. Make the subject match the creative. Either surface the extra 15% in the hero ("Up to 65% off with extra markdowns") or drop the word "extra."
- 3. Add sale prices or "30% off" badges on the product tiles, with a few real before/after prices.
- 4. Pick one hero product or category, and cut the 3x3 grid to a tighter 2x3.
- 5. Move the exclusions to a lighter footnote link instead of putting them under the CTA.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: up to 65% off sale styles ends 10 PM CT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, boots and bags from Balmain to Ralph Lauren, last day to save.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 15% off sale items, ends tomorrow at 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The QA warnings (missing List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results, link probes) are mostly relay-capture limits and can't be confirmed as defects. The source is truncated, so the footer, links and body are not assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** QA skipped 29 tracking/click-redirect links (`[WARN] 29 tracking link(s) skipped`). Destinations and redirect health are untested, so no defect is confirmed.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 5488/6685 chars are URLs` (about 82%). The plain-text alternative is dominated by long tracking URLs. This is a mild spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Template comment left in the production HTML:** it sits before the DOCTYPE. It begins `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`, followed by `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`).
  - It shows the template was not stripped of authoring instructions.
  - A comment before the DOCTYPE can also push some renderers into quirks mode.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) on supporting clients.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation rules.
- **Weak `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is generic and has a trailing space. It doesn't describe the message.
- **Checkbox-hack mobile nav:** the CSS uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.mobileCont:checked`. This pattern doesn't work in Gmail (web/app) or Outlook desktop. The fallback isn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it fails.
- **Legacy and fragile CSS:**
  - Exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` stop matching if an element gets a second class.
  - `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s }` runs a permanent animation just to work around an iOS 9 bug.
  - These are low severity.
- **Alt text and image sizing:** not assessable. The `<img>` markup is cut off.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the retrieved portion. The AMPscript variables appear only inside the leaked comment (see section 3). The body is truncated, so token rendering in greetings or links can't be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing:** the QA note says it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". This is likely a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing (RFC 8058):** the same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so check it on the original send.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay stripped or didn't expose the header.
- **Footer not visible:** the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Links are tracking redirects (29 skipped) and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the retrieved source. The template variables (`@refid`, `@base_refid`, `@cmdid`, `@uEm`) suggest campaign attribution runs through refid and CRM params rather than UTMs. That is inference only. Landing-page alignment with the "Fall Style Sale" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip all authoring comments, including the AMPscript variable block and `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO`, before the send, and keep the DOCTYPE as the first node.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`. Replace the title with a descriptive one, such as the subject line, without the trailing space.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the original MTA message, not the relay copy.
5. Shorten the plain-text version, or use shorter redirect URLs, to reduce URL density.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, plus resolved redirect targets, to check destinations, UTM/refid params, alt text, the footer address and the mobile-nav fallback.
