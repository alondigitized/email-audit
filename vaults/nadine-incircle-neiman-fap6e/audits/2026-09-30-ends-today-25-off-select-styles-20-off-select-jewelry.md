# Ends today: 25% off select styles & 20% off select jewelry
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus's last-day push on the Friends & Family event. The subject, "Ends today," is the strongest asset because the urgency is real and the offer is stated up front. The green hero, 25% off, a single "Shop the event" button and the Double Points strip make a clear, well-ordered top third.
- The problem is fatigue. This is at least the third send on the same mechanic in four days. 09-26 was "Have you shopped 25% off?" and 09-27 was "Don't forget…". The creative is essentially unchanged, so a subscriber has seen this hero already. The 9-product grid has designer names but no prices or savings, and there are six category buttons plus a free-shipping banner competing for the click. The result is a solid but predictable closer that relies on the deadline.
- Judgement: a competent, deadline-driven send that will convert on urgency alone. It has no fresh creative and a visibly clipped headline.

## What's working

- "Ends today" in the subject gives credible urgency, and the hero repeats the offer in large type.
- The dark-green hero with a single cream "Shop the event" button is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The Double Points strip directly under the hero rewards InCircle members.
- The 3x3 grid mixes categories (denim, gown, leather jacket, boots, bag, menswear), so it covers several shopper types.
- The category buttons (Women's, Shoes, Handbags, Men's, Home) give clear paths into the sale.

## What's weak

- The "FRIENDS &FAMILY" headline is clipped at the right edge, and the "Y" is cut off. The ampersand also has no space before "FAMILY". This is a visible render flaw on the hero.
- The product grid shows no prices, no "was/now" and no discount badges, so 25% off is never attached to a real item.
- The email carries six category buttons and a separate free-shipping banner, so no single CTA dominates below the hero.
- The legal block is long, dense and small, and "Exclusions apply" appears twice above it. That undercuts the "honest offer" feel.
- It repeats the 09-26 and 09-27 sends almost exactly, with no new hook for the final day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero headline so it fits its container with proper spacing. Add a live countdown or "Ends tonight 10PM CT" line under "25% off" to make the last-day urgency visual.
- 2. Put prices in the grid, for example "$198 → $148.50", so the discount is tangible. Trim the grid to 6 items.
- 3. Cut the six buttons to two or three, and lead with the persona's category.
- 4. Move the free-shipping banner into the hero strip, next to Double Points, as one "perks" line.
- 5. Swap the hero imagery from the earlier sends, or make it a "last chance" edit.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 25% off ends tonight at 10PM CT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: 25% off + double InCircle points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jewelry is 20% off too. Plus free shipping on $150+ with code SHIPNM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Double InCircle points and 25% off select styles vanish at 10PM CT tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has one confirmed production-hygiene defect: an internal ESP template comment block that shipped in the sent HTML. It also has one accessibility defect, a viewport meta that disables zoom. The remaining QA warnings (missing `List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`, and 33 unprobed tracking links) are relay-capture or probe limitations, not confirmed sender defects. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified, not a confirmed issue:** the QA run skipped the HTTP probe on 33 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and link expiry are unverified.
- The truncated source exposes no `href` values, so I can't check for broken anchors, missing `target`, or mixed http/https.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled (confirmed):** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on iOS Safari and some Android clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern.
- **Missing language (confirmed):** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation profile.
- **Weak `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the email's content. This is minor.
- **Fragile selectors:** the mobile rules use exact-match attribute selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and `*[class=mobile]`. They fail if an element carries more than one class. Mixed with `*[class].abs` (a different, non-exact pattern), this is inconsistent and risks silent mobile-layout misses.
- **Hamburger nav depends on the checkbox hack:** the `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` rules only work in clients that support `:checked` and sibling combinators (Apple Mail, iOS Mail). Gmail and Outlook won't run it. Whether a fallback exists can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Legacy CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody` and `-webkit-animation: bugfix` (an iOS9 hack) are dead weight. This is low severity.
- Image alt text, image dimensions and the preheader are beyond the truncation point, so I can't assess them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template comment leaked into the sent HTML (confirmed):** the first node is a large HTML comment:
  `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) @cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...) CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`
  - It discloses internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), join keys, and identifier fields (`uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`).
  - The unresolved instruction "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and the commented-out `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` suggest a template scaffold that was not stripped before send. It also raises the question of whether the intended hashing scenario was applied.
  - The comments also add page weight, which matters for Gmail's ~102 KB clipping threshold.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens are visible in the rendered portion I have. Downstream tokens are unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is inconclusive. Treat it as unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed here.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are past the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. The 33 links are tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page targets are unknown.
- The comment block references `@refid` and `@base_refid` (Campaign Calendar), which implies campaign attribution goes through a refid parameter rather than standard UTMs. This is inferred from the comment, not verified on any link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the ESP scaffold comments before send.** Remove the entire `<!--REQUIRED variables ... -->` block and the `SET @user` comment from the production template, or use server-side comments (for example, AMPscript `/* */`) that aren't emitted. Also confirm which "scenario" was intended and that it executed.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1` is sufficient).
3. **Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`)** to `<html>`, and give the `<title>` a meaningful value without the trailing space.
4. **Convert `*[class=x]` selectors to `.x` or `[class~=x]`** for consistency and multi-class safety.
5. **Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`** or a direct-to-seed-inbox copy, not the AgentMail relay. That would validate `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. **Probe the 33 tracking links** through a redirect-following check that resolves the final URL, then verify the landing pages and their UTM or refid parameters.
7. **Check the sent size against the ~102 KB Gmail clipping limit.**
