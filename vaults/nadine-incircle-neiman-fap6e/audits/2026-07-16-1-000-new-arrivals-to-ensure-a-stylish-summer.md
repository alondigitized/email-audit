# 1,000+ new arrivals to ensure a stylish summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus's second "1,000+ new arrivals" broadcast in four days — the July 13 send ("Get into vacation mode with 1,000+ new designer arrivals," 5/10) used the same black-hero / split-gender / brand-grid template, and this is a near-pixel-identical rerun with slightly different hero copy. The formula is defensible for a department store, but sending it again this fast, on top of seven other NM emails since July 12, is self-cannibalistic. A subscriber who skipped the first one has no new reason to engage here.
- The render itself is clean: "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" in the hero, split "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" CTAs, and a twelve-brand grid (Alemais, Tory Burch, Dolce&Gabbana, Cult Gaia, Zimmermann, Alice + Olivia for women; Loewe, Christian Louboutin, Amiri, Ferragamo, Jacquemus for men) does genuine brand equity work. Those are strong labels. But there's no price, no offer, no urgency, and nothing to suggest the selection was chosen for you specifically rather than broadcast to all 5 million subscribers.
- The verdict: competent execution of a format NM has already exhausted this week. The brands could carry it — if the subject named even one of them.

## What's working

- The brand grid is legitimately curated — Loewe, Jacquemus, Cult Gaia, and Alemais signal editorial taste, not a clearance dump.
- Split "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" hero CTAs prevent irrelevant clicks before the subscriber even reaches the grid.
- Black hero block with bold white type creates immediate visual hierarchy; the eye lands on the headline before anything else.
- Email renders cleanly with no visible bugs, broken images, or placeholder text.
- "Watch with a personal style advisor" in the footer is a soft loyalty nudge that most retailers omit entirely.

## What's weak

- Zero offer or incentive — no discount, no complimentary shipping, no member perk. Two consecutive "just browse" broadcasts (July 13 + today) with nothing to close on.
- Subject line repeats the July 13 formula nearly word-for-word ("1,000+ new arrivals") with no brand specificity to differentiate.
- The twelve-brand grid gives each product too little real estate to create genuine desire — it functions as a catalog page, not a click trigger.
- This is at minimum the seventh NM email since July 12 (three gift-card sends, two Last Call sends, one Roundup, and now this). Cadence fatigue is compounding.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name a brand in the subject.** The July 14 Last Call send that called out A.L.C. by name scored 8/10; this generic "1,000+" version scores lower. Picking even one name from the grid — Jacquemus, Cult Gaia, Tory Burch — would separate this from the July 13 near-duplicate and give openers a reason to look.
- 2. **Hero one product above the grid.** Pull a single aspirational piece — the Cult Gaia sculptural silhouette or the Jacquemus short — and give it full-bleed editorial treatment before the brand grid. Force a click for the right reason rather than banking on browsing.
- 3. **Add a friction-reducing hook.** "Complimentary shipping on all new arrivals this week" or "Member early access" converts a browse email into a reason-to-act email, especially after a week of gift-card mechanics that trained the audience to expect a reward.
- 4. **Rest the new-arrivals format.** After July 13's send, this template needed at least a week's gap. Repeating it four days later at the same volume shows the template, not the merchandise.

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "1,000+ New Arrivals" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) template with standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional structure. The primary technical risks are a partially commented-out AMPscript personalization block with ambiguous active state, and two missing compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post) that affect one-click unsubscribe behavior in Gmail and Apple Mail.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped (QA):** All click-redirect links are routed through a tracking domain. No HTTP probe was performed, so broken destination URLs cannot be confirmed or denied. Manual spot-check of 2–3 destination URLs is recommended before send.

**Plain-text body is 87% URLs (5,289 / 6,096 chars):** This exceeds the 70% QA threshold. Plain-text parts dominated by URLs trigger Bayesian spam filters (SpamAssassin `MIME_BASE64_TEXT`, Postfix content filters). The plain-text version is likely auto-generated from link-heavy HTML without meaningful text stripping.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid, "")` (AMPscript block):** Concatenating with an empty string literal is a no-op. If this is a placeholder for a suffix that was never implemented, the refid value injected into tracking URLs will differ from what Campaign Calendar expects.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags in `<head>`:**
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are leftover template debugging artifacts. They do not affect rendering but they inflate raw source size and signal template hygiene issues. The active viewport tag is correct.

**`<title>` has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>`. Minor, but tab/window titles and screen reader announcements will include the extra space.

**CSS hamburger menu uses checkbox `:checked` pseudo-selector:** The pattern `input.mobileCont:checked ~ table *[class=contentInteractive]` has no support in Outlook (any version) and limited support in Yahoo Mail. If interactive content relies on this, it will silently not render in those clients. The template already uses `display: none !important` as the default state, so the fallback is hidden content — acceptable if the mobile toggle is purely progressive enhancement.

**`@-webkit-keyframes bugfix` animation targeting iOS 9:** This is a known fix for a hamburger menu rendering bug in iOS 9, which has effectively zero market share as of 2026. It's inert but adds noise.

**No `alt` attributes visible in truncated source:** Cannot fully verify from the provided excerpt, but given the image-heavy layout, missing `alt` text would be a WCAG 2.1 Level A failure and would degrade the plain-text experience in image-blocked clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` assignment is commented out inside an HTML comment:**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
This is inside `<!-- -->` (HTML comment), not a proper AMPscript comment block (`%%[ /* */ ]%%`). Whether SFMC's AMPscript engine evaluates this depends on context. The surrounding comment says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" — it is unclear from the source which scenario is actually active. If `@user` is undefined at send time, any URL parameter using it (e.g., `&user=%%=v(@user)=%%`) will render as an empty string or literal `%%=v(@user)=%%`, breaking personalization in tracking URLs and site targeting.

**`@cmdid` lookup missing closing parenthesis in source excerpt:**
```
@cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
```
The source shows `))` — one extra closing paren. If this is the actual template (not a transcription artifact), it will throw an AMPscript parse error and may suppress send or fall back to a blank value.

**`@uEm` from `NM_CRM_Feed` table:** This is a CRM-sourced value (likely hashed email variant) used in URLs. If a subscriber's record is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed`, the Lookup returns `NULL`, which will inject an empty parameter into click URLs — breaking CRM attribution for that cohort.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA WARN):** This header is required for Gmail's "Unsubscribe" button (visible next to the sender name). Its absence means Gmail renders no one-click unsubscribe affordance, increasing spam complaints. SFMC should be injecting this automatically on the sending MTA; its absence here suggests the AgentMail relay is stripping it, or it was never configured for this sending domain.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058, QA WARN):** Required for Gmail's POST-based one-click unsubscribe (enforced since February 2024 for bulk senders). Without it, one-click unsubscribe won't function even if List-Unsubscribe is present. This is a bulk sender compliance gap.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found (QA WARN):** SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from captured headers. If the sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is not aligned with DMARC policy on `neimanmarcus.com`, messages may be quarantined or rejected by strict DMARC receivers.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism:** Not verifiable from the truncated HTML. The footer is not included in the excerpt — this must be confirmed separately.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters:** Cannot be confirmed from the truncated source (links are redirect-wrapped by the tracking domain). The QA system skipped probing all 26 tracking links. Confirm that destination URLs include `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` parameters before send — absent UTMs will cause all traffic to be attributed as direct in GA4/Adobe Analytics.

**`@refid` and `@base_refid`:** These appear to feed into site URLs for campaign attribution. If `@base_refid` is not set in the Campaign Calendar sendable DE, `@refid` will be blank and campaign-level attribution will be lost for this send.
