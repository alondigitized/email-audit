# Ends soon: up to $250 off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth run of the exact same $400/$100–$1,000/$250 tiered gift-card mechanic since 8/26, now wearing its fourth different headline in six days ("Reminder," "Limited time left," and now "Ends soon"). The execution itself is clean — tiered spend/save table clearly laid out, nine well-shot products spanning apparel/shoes/bags across price points, sale-styled gold hero block, one unambiguous SHOP NOW CTA. Deadline is concrete: 9/3/26 10:00 PM CT, which finally gives the "Ends soon" framing real teeth instead of vague urgency.
- But subscriber fatigue is the story here. A recipient who's seen this table five times already isn't going to open faster because the word changed from "Limited time" to "Ends soon" — the offer, the code (SHOPFALL), and the layout are all identical to 9/1. This send earns credit for craft but loses points for contributing to what is now a week straight of the same promo.

## What's working

- Deadline is now stated in the hero copy region-adjacent fine print (9/3/26 10:00 PM CT) — the most concrete urgency signal of the whole run.
- Spend/save table is scannable at a glance — two tiers, no math required.
- Product grid mixes categories (trousers, gowns, handbags, shoes, denim) at varied price points, giving multiple entry points to click.
- Single black SHOP NOW button reads clearly against the gold hero.
- No render bugs — clean layout, all nine product images loaded, footer/nav intact.

## What's weak

- Sixth consecutive day of the identical $250-off mechanic (8/26–9/1, now 9/2) — this is promo fatigue, not a fresh send.
- Zero price visible anywhere in the product grid — can't tell if these nine items even clear the $400 spend threshold.
- "Ends soon" subject undercuts itself by being the third urgency-framing variant of the same static offer (after "Reminder" and "Limited time left") — a repeat subscriber will recognize the pattern.
- Product captions are designer names only (Akris punto, Jimmy Choo, Lanvin) with no price or product name — no way to judge fit against the discount tiers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the streak — either escalate the offer meaningfully (extra tier, bonus %) or switch to a genuinely different creative (like 9/1's Cleo Wade editorial) before running $250-off again. Six straight days on one mechanic trains subscribers to ignore the subject line entirely.
- 2. Add prices under each product so the grid actually helps decide whether to hit the $400 or $1,000 tier.
- 3. Move the 9/3 10:00 PM CT deadline into the hero itself (currently buried in footer fine print) — it's the single strongest asset this send has and it's underused.
- 4. Vary the CTA button copy from "SHOP NOW" (used identically across the whole run) to something tied to the deadline, e.g. "SHOP BEFORE IT ENDS."
