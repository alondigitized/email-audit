# New designers just added: The Designer Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Designer Sale send in roughly four days — NM launched it on 5/17, followed with urgency on 5/18, spotlighted specific designers (Givenchy, Ferragamo) on 5/19, and now comes back with "new designers just added" as the refresh hook on 5/21. That's a smart lifecycle structure: launch → urgency → editorial → expansion. The creative execution here is solid: a bold red hero with "UP TO 50% OFF" is impossible to miss, and the six-brand product grid below gives the email real browsing value.
- The weakest link is the middle section. A 3×3 product grid of flat bag/clothing shots with no prices attached turns what should be a shop-ready email into a lookbook. If someone sees a Burberry coat or a Cou Cou bag they like, there's no price to anchor the click decision. The InCircle 15% loyalty block at the bottom is a nice retention layer but it competes with the sale's own urgency.
- Clear, commercially confident send. Not as tight as the 5/19 Givenchy/Ferragamo email (9/10), but a solid mid-campaign reinforcement that earns its place in the sequence.

## What's working

- **Hero is instant**: red full-width banner, "UP TO 50% OFF," "SHOP SALE" button — the offer is captured in under a second.
- **Brand grid is scannable and real**: nine designer names with product shots (Jacquemus, Balmain, ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges, Cou Cou, Polo Ralph Lauren, Rag & Bone) — legitimate luxury breadth that validates the "new designers added" claim.
- **Category CTAs are segmented well**: separate buttons for Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Dresses — smart for a multi-category sale.
- **InCircle 15% loyalty block** adds incremental incentive for members without muddying the main sale message.
- **"Save on these styles before they're gone"** scarcity copy underneath the hero reinforces urgency without screaming.

## What's weak

- **No prices on any product** — flat product shots with no price anchors mean the grid functions as a teaser, not a close. Competitive luxury shoppers want to see $X marked down to $Y before they click.
- **Nine designers is too many for one grid** — the 3×3 layout at mobile width renders the product images very small; individual pieces are hard to evaluate.
- **"Limited Time" + "Exclusions Apply"** are visible but vague — no deadline date shown anywhere in the email makes the urgency feel hollow, especially on day four of this sale.
- **The InCircle sign-up block** ("CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF") adds a second, separate CTA that dilutes focus — the reader hasn't yet clicked the sale, and now they're being asked to sign up for a card.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or "Sale ends 5/25" below the "UP TO 50% OFF" line would transform the scarcity from felt to factual. This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Show one marquee product with a price** — pick the lead Balmain or Jacquemus piece, show $1,200 → $600, and link it directly. A concrete markdown converts better than a brand logo grid.
- 3. **Trim the grid to 4-6 designers** — fewer, larger product shots let the merchandise breathe at mobile widths. Featuring only the newly-added names also reinforces the subject line's "just added" hook.
- 4. **Move the InCircle block to a secondary send or footer callout** — it competes with the sale click. Members already know the benefit; non-members aren't ready to apply mid-sale-browse.
- 5. **Make the subject line do more work**: the current subject is solid but doesn't reference the 50% depth.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off: Burberry, Givenchy & more just added`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Designer Sale just got bigger — new names inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Jacquemus, ALAIA, Rag & Bone — before they sell out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off, plus your InCircle 15% on top`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "New designers just added: The Designer Sale"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is rendered on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) using an XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. QA surfaces four actionable warnings: missing one-click unsubscribe headers (a Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirement), unverifiable SPF/DKIM status via the AgentMail relay, and a plain-text body that is nearly entirely URLs — a material spam-filter risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text URL density — HIGH RISK**
QA reports 5,315 of 6,089 plain-text characters are URLs (87%). A machine-generated plain-text alternative rather than a human-readable version is a well-known spam signal for Gmail, Outlook, and SpamAssassin. This alone can suppress inbox placement.

**26 tracking links unprobed**
All click-redirect URLs were skipped by the HTTP probe. UTM completeness, destination validity, and redirect chain depth cannot be confirmed. No evidence of broken links, but also no confirmation they are healthy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants** remain in `<head>` — dead code from iterative testing that was never cleaned up:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
No functional impact, but signals a long-lived unmaintained template.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
The XHTML doctype is present but no `lang="en"` / `xml:lang="en"` is visible in the source. Screen readers default to OS language; omission degrades accessibility for AT users.

**`<title>` has a trailing space**: `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor cosmetic issue.

**Interactive hamburger nav** uses CSS `:checked` pseudo-class with `visibility`/`opacity` toggling. This only activates in Apple Mail / iOS Mail. It degrades gracefully (hidden by default via `.contentInteractive { display: none !important }`), so no rendering breakage in unsupported clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` variable definition is ambiguous — MEDIUM RISK**

The template comment reads `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` with two contradictory definitions:

```
// Active path:
@user = Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
// Commented-out path:
/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */
```

The active path sets `@user` to a numeric `EMAIL_ID`. The commented-out path indicates the intent was a SHA-256 hashed email. If any downstream system (analytics, identity resolution, onsite personalization) expects a SHA-256 value from `@user`, it silently receives an integer — wrong data type, no runtime error.

**`@emailid` dual-source logic**
Defined as "from sendable DE if available, otherwise Lookup to Subscriber_New." If the DE field is null for a segment of sends, the fallback lookup introduces a second code path that may return a different value format. No guard clause visible for the null case.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid`** are sourced from `NM_CRM_Feed` via lookup. If a subscriber is absent from that feed, both values silently render empty — risk depends on how they are used downstream (likely identity stitching and reporting).

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | QA: "Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay" |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | QA: "One-click unsubscribe may not be supported" |
| SPF / DKIM / DMARC | **UNKNOWN** | QA: `Authentication-Results` header not found via relay |
| CAN-SPAM physical address | **UNCONFIRMED** | Source truncated before footer; cannot verify |
| Unsubscribe mechanism in body | **UNCONFIRMED** | Footer not present in truncated source excerpt |

**`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` absence is a hard compliance gap.** Google and Yahoo have required RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024. Without it, Gmail may suppress delivery or auto-promote to Spam for users who previously marked similar mail as spam.

The missing `Authentication-Results` may be a relay capture artifact rather than a genuine auth failure, but it means deliverability monitoring has a blind spot — SPF/DKIM pass/fail cannot be confirmed from this data.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot verify UTM parameter presence or correctness — all 26 tracked links are behind click-redirect domains that were skipped by the HTTP probe. No broken destination URLs detected, but no confirmation of:
- UTM source/medium/campaign values
- Campaign ID alignment between email (`@base_refid`) and landing page parameters
- Whether the `@refid` concat (`CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` — concatenating with an empty string) produces the intended tracking value or is a stub left from template scaffolding

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Compliance / Deliverability**
1. **Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** in SFMC send classification or template header block. Without these, bulk sends to Gmail/Yahoo violate 2024 sender requirements.
2. **Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC** by inspecting raw headers on a test send — do not rely on the AgentMail relay for auth status. Confirm `e.neimanmarcus.com` has a valid DKIM selector and SPF include.

**P1 — Data Integrity**
3. **Resolve the `@user` ambiguity.** Determine whether downstream systems expect `EMAIL_ID` or `SHA-256`. Remove the losing branch and its comment. The current "CHOOSE ONE" instruction is a latent production bug.
4. **Add null guards for `@uEm` and `@cmdid`** lookups from `NM_CRM_Feed` — at minimum a `IIF(EMPTY(@uEm), "unknown", @uEm)` fallback to prevent silent empty values in analytics.

**P2 — Spam Signal**
5. **Rebuild the plain-text alternative** as human-readable copy, not auto-generated URLs. Target <20% URL density. This is a straightforward SFMC template change with measurable inbox placement impact.

**P3 — Template Hygiene**
6. Remove the three commented-out `<meta viewport>` tags — they are unreachable dead code.
7. Add `lang="en" xml:lang="en"` to the `<html>` tag.
8. Remove the trailing space in `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>`.
9. Verify `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is intentional — concatenating with an empty string is a no-op and may be a leftover template stub.
