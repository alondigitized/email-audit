# The Roundup: Tantalizing Totes
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a full-price editorial curation email — "The Roundup: Tantalizing Totes" — a designer bag roundup with zero discount language anywhere in the frame. The hero leopard-print YSL tote is genuinely striking and does the heavy lifting, then the email settles into a clean six-bag grid (Saint Laurent, Balenciaga, Christian Louboutin, Bottega Veneta, Zimmermann) with brand names as the only copy.
- This lands right in the middle of the full-price-editorial cycle Neiman Marcus has been running all week — same energy as "Pre-fall bags take shape" (8/16, 6.00) and "Pre-Fall's new standouts" (8/15, 5.00). It's a well-curated catalog page, not a persuasive marketing email: no price, no CTA copy beyond generic shop buttons, no reason to act today versus next week.
- Judgement: tasteful, on-brand, and completely inert. It's a lookbook, not a sell.

## What's working

- Hero image (leopard YSL tote) is genuinely eye-catching and on-trend — strongest visual asset in the send.
- Clean, editorial "Roundup" format signals curation/expertise rather than a hard sell, which fits Neiman Marcus's positioning.
- Two-button entry point ("Shop Saint Laurent" / "Shop Totes") gives readers a category vs. specific-brand choice.
- Grid layout is well-organized with consistent brand labels under each product — easy to scan.
- No render bugs — images, type, and spacing all render cleanly.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing visible anywhere — a $6,000 YSL tote sits next to unlabeled Balenciaga/Bottega bags with no price anchor at all.
- No urgency, no offer, no reason to click today versus bookmarking for later.
- Individual product tiles (Balenciaga, Bottega, Zimmermann) aren't clickable-CTA labeled — just image + brand name, no "Shop Now" per item.
- Repeats the same full-price-editorial beat as 8/15 and 8/16 sends this week — three near-identical "no discount, just pretty bags" emails in four days.
- Subject "Tantalizing Totes" is vague alliteration with no product or brand specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add price ranges under each bag (even "$1,290–$3,950") — high-intent shoppers browsing designer totes want to price-anchor before clicking, and its total absence is the biggest miss here.
- 2. Give the grid real CTAs — "Shop the Bottega Tote" per tile instead of relying on the two top-of-email buttons to carry all six products.
- 3. Break the cadence — this is the third full-price bag/editorial send in four days; alternate with the Last Call/Sale cycle instead of running them back to back.
- 4. Tighten the subject to name a brand or trend driver, since "Tantalizing Totes" carries zero information.
