# Veronica Beard Spring 2026
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, aspirational brand spotlight for Veronica Beard's Spring 2026 collection — a pure editorial send with no offer, no discount, and no urgency. It follows the exact same no-offer editorial playbook NM ran with the China Chow send on 5/24 and the Tempting Totes roundup on 5/23, both of which scored 4/10 for similar reasons. The hero photography is strong — a woman in a flowy pink dress on a chaise captures the "Relaxed Refinement" headline well — and the layout is clean.
- The problem is timing and intent. We're in late May, NM's inbox has been a non-stop promotional machine since mid-May (Designer Sale, Memorial Day, spend-to-save tiering), and this send arrives with zero financial reason to click. "Spring 2026" as a subject line in the last week of May is also mildly awkward — the season is practically over. The product grid is small and unpriced, and the CTA bank at the bottom ("Shop Men's," "Shop Kids") dilutes any Veronica Beard-specific momentum built up by the hero.
- Solid brand photography, weak conversion mechanics. Without an offer, this is window dressing for the database.

## What's working

- Hero lifestyle image is genuinely aspirational — the pink dress on the chaise is the best creative NM has used in this recent run.
- "Relaxed Refinement" headline is on-brand and actually descriptive of the aesthetic being shown.
- "Shop Veronica Beard" CTA is clear and unambiguous, sitting directly under the hero.
- Subject line is unusually specific — brand name + season is more ownable than most NM subjects.
- Email renders cleanly with no broken images, no overlapping text, no visible layout issues.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price point, no discount — there is zero financial trigger to click anything.
- The 6-product grid uses tiny thumbnails with no prices, making it feel like a preview page rather than a shoppable grid.
- The CTA battery at the bottom (Shop Clothing, Shop Shoes, Shop Handbags, Shop Men's, Shop Kids) completely scatters focus from the Veronica Beard feature.
- "Spring 2026" in the last week of May signals a slightly stale campaign moment — the runway window has nearly closed.
- No social proof, no "as seen in," no styling context beyond the hero shot.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price anchor or offer.** Even "New arrivals from $195" or "Members: complimentary shipping on Veronica Beard this week" gives the reader a reason to click now versus never.
- 2. **Cut the generic CTA bank.** Remove "Shop Men's," "Shop Kids," "Shop Shoes," "Shop Handbags." One CTA — "Shop Veronica Beard Spring 2026" — keeps the Veronica Beard story intact.
- 3. **Show prices in the product grid.** Thumbnails without prices feel like aspirational wallpaper. Even a starting price ("from $325") converts browsers to clickers.
- 4. **Sharpen the timing signal.** "New this week" or "just arrived" turns a season label into a reason to look today.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Hamburger nav CSS checkbox hack** — the mobile nav relies on the `:checked` pseudo-class pattern (`*[class].navbar-checkbox:checked ~ *[class].menu`). This is unsupported in Gmail (all clients), Outlook 2016–2021, and Yahoo Mail. Subscribers on those clients will receive a broken/invisible nav with no graceful fallback visible in the source.

**Three commented-out viewport `<meta>` tags** remain in `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Only `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1` is active. The dead markup is harmless but is template cruft that increases parse weight.

**`<title>` has a trailing space**: `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor; fix for cleanliness.

**`max-scale=1` on active viewport** blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility regression for low-vision users. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) recommends not disabling zoom.

Alt text coverage cannot be confirmed from the truncated source; verify all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` values.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical: entire AMPscript variable block is commented out.**

```html
<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @user ... @emailid ... @uEm ... @cmdid ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

Both the variable declarations and the `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` assignment are wrapped in HTML comments. If downstream `%%=v(@user)=%%`, `%%=v(@cmdid)=%%`, or `%%=v(@refid)=%%` substitutions exist anywhere in the body or tracked URLs, they will render as empty strings at send time. This is the most likely source of broken personalization or mis-attributed tracking.

**Action required**: confirm whether a live AMPscript block appears below the truncation point. If not, the variable initialization is entirely absent.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — QA warning. SFMC should inject this automatically via the sending domain; its absence as captured by the AgentMail relay suggests it may have been stripped or was never set on the job. Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements mandate this header for >5k/day senders; missing it risks inbox demotion.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected** — one-click unsubscribe is unavailable. Same bulk-sender policy risk as above.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from the captured message. Sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` should have both records; this warrants a raw-header check against a delivered copy.

The unsubscribe footer link is standard for NM templates but cannot be verified from the truncated source. Confirm it resolves correctly and is not suppressed by the missing `@emailid` variable.

All 31 outbound links were routed through click-redirect domains and skipped by the HTTP probe — UTM parameter presence and accuracy cannot be confirmed. At minimum, verify:
