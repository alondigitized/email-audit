# It’s here! The Designer Sale
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a textbook luxury sale-launch email — clean, fast, and confident. The red hero delivers the full offer in three lines ("THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF"), the product grid backs it up with nine recognizable designer names, and the category CTAs below route traffic efficiently. Nothing is wasted.
- The timing creates a small continuity puzzle. Yesterday's NM send was "Your exclusive access ends tonight!" — closing out what appeared to be a prior designer sale. Now, less than 24 hours later, a new designer sale opens. Subscribers paying attention will wonder if it's the same event reset, a sequel, or a misfire. The email does nothing to address that, and today NM has already sent a second email ("Experience services designed around you") on the same date.
- That said, this is a well-executed workhorse send. It does what a sale-launch email should: land the offer fast, show depth, and get out of the way.

## What's working

- **Hero communicates the value prop in one pass** — red field, "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" in large type, nothing competing
- **Nine-brand product grid signals real breadth** — Victoria Beckham, Christian Louboutin, Jacquemus, ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges, Colt Dala, Polo Ralph Lauren all in one view
- **Category CTA grid is practical** — five targeted shop buttons (Women's Clothing, Women's Shoes, Men's, Men's Shoes, Dresses) cut browsing friction immediately
- **App download + personal stylist mention** reinforces NM's premium service positioning without cluttering the offer area
- **Render is clean** — all nine product images load, no broken assets, no layout gaps

## What's weak

- **No deadline anywhere in the body** — "LIMITED TIME" is vague to the point of meaningless; yesterday's email had a hard end time, this one has nothing that manufactures real urgency
- **"It's here!" creates continuity confusion** — subscribers who got "Your exclusive access ends tonight!" 24 hours ago now get a new designer sale with no explanation of whether it's the same event or a fresh one
- **No loyalty differentiation** — NM runs a loyalty program (InCircle); zero mention of member pricing, early access, or card perks that would reward best customers
- **"SHOP SALE" CTA is generic** — for an email anchored on 50% off across nine named designers, the button copy underdelivers

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a specific deadline.** Even "Ends Sunday" converts "LIMITED TIME" from decoration to decision pressure.
- 2. **Address the cadence in the preheader** — a single line like "A new event, not an extension — new arrivals added" resolves subscriber confusion from yesterday's close without requiring body copy changes.
- 3. **Upgrade the CTA copy** — "Shop Up to 50% Off" carries the discount into the button, which is where it creates the most impulse.
- 4. **Pin one hero product with a visible price** — a single marquee item (e.g., Christian Louboutin pump, was $895 / now $447) makes the 50% feel concrete rather than aspirational.
- 5. **Add an InCircle / loyalty tier line** below the hero button — "Circle members: your exclusive window opens now" creates a high-value segment above the general offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Designer Sale: up to 50% off — now live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Designer Sale is here. Up to 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Victoria Beckham, Louboutin, Burberry — now up to half off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nine designers. One limited-time sale. Shop before it ends.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "It's Here! The Designer Sale"

**From:** NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com | **Date reviewed:** 2026-05-18

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) AMPscript-templated, table-based HTML send with six personalization lookups. QA returned a 43% pass rate driven by four warnings: missing compliance headers, unverifiable authentication, and a pathological plain-text ratio — all actionable before next deployment.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

| Severity | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| WARN | 26 click-tracking links could not be probed | QA: "26 tracking link(s) skipped — Tracking/click-redirect domains" |
| WARN | Plain-text body is 87% URLs | QA: 5,095 of 5,865 plain-text chars are URLs — spam filters penalize this |

**Details:**
- The plain-text part appears to be auto-generated from the HTML without stripping redirect wrappers. Spam filters (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_ONLY`, Gmail heuristics) penalize messages where the plain-text alternative is effectively a URL dump. This also renders unreadably in plain-text clients.
- The 26 skipped tracking links prevent confirming that all CTAs resolve to live landing pages. Manual spot-check of final destination URLs is required before send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Severity | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| INFO | Three conflicting `<meta viewport>` tags left in source as HTML comments | Lines in `<head>`: `<!--<meta name="viewport" ... user-scalable=no />-->`, `<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->`, `<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->` |
| INFO | `-webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s` applied to `body` on mobile | CSS block labeled "Hamburguer fix IOS9" — iOS 9 is EOL (2017); dead code |
| WARN | No `alt` text visible for any images in truncated source | Standard requirement for screen readers and image-blocked renders; unconfirmable without full source |

The commented-out viewport tags are inert but indicate accumulating template debt. The iOS 9 animation hack fires on every mobile render unnecessarily.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

| Severity | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH | `@user` variable resolution is ambiguous — scenario selector left as a comment | AMPscript block: `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->` followed by `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` |

**Details:**
The AMPscript header comment block shows `@user` was intended to be set via one of two scenarios, but the active assignment is commented out (`/* SET @user = HASHED_SHA256 */`). If `@user` is used downstream in any link parameter or personalization block without a fallback, it will render as an empty string or throw a runtime lookup error depending on SFMC's AMPscript null-handling configuration.

Variables in scope and their data sources:
- `@email` — sendable DE field `EMAIL_ADDRESS`
- `@base_refid` — Campaign Calendar DE
- `@refid` — `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` (no-op concat; confirm this is intentional)
- `@user` / `@emailid` — `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...)`
- `@uEm` — `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...)`
- `@cmdid` — `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...)`

The `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` no-op on `@refid` is suspicious — if `@base_refid` is numeric, this coerces it to string; if it's already a string, it's dead code. Either way, confirm intent.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Severity | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH | `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | QA: "List-Unsubscribe header not found" |
| HIGH | `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058) | QA: "One-click unsubscribe may not be supported" |
| HIGH | SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown | QA: "Authentication-Results header not found — SPF/DKIM status unknown via AgentMail relay" |

**Details:**
- Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for bulk senders (>5k/day) as of February 2024. Missing these headers will suppress deliverability to Gmail and trigger spam folder routing at Yahoo.
- The AgentMail relay is not forwarding `Authentication-Results`, making it impossible to confirm DKIM/SPF pass. If SFMC's sending domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is not aligned with a DMARC policy, inbox placement is at risk. Verify DKIM signing on the `e.neimanmarcus.com` subdomain directly via `dig TXT` or MXToolbox.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

| Severity | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| BLOCKED | Landing page alignment unverifiable | All 26 CTAs are behind redirect wrappers; HTTP probe was skipped |
| INFO | `@refid` / `@cmdid` used as presumed UTM/ref parameters | Variables visible in AMPscript header; downstream usage not confirmed in truncated source |

Manual verification needed: confirm that `@cmdid` and `@refid` are appended as query parameters on all sale landing page URLs, and that the receiving pages handle the `cmdid` parameter without error if the CRM lookup returns null.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**Before send (blocking):**
1. **Resolve `@user` AMPscript ambiguity.** Uncomment the correct scenario assignment and remove the dead branch. Add a null guard: `IIF(EMPTY(@user), "default_value", @user)`.
2. **Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** to the SFMC send classification. This is a deliverability blocker for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy.
3. **Regenerate plain-text part** by stripping redirect wrappers from URLs before building the plain-text MIME part, or use a summarized text version. Target <30% URL density.
4. **Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment** on `e.neimanmarcus.com` independently; do not rely on the relay's `Authentication-Results`.

**Before next template revision:**
5. Remove the three commented-out `<meta viewport>` tags and the iOS 9 animation keyframe workaround — both are dead code.
6. Audit the `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` no-op; if it was a type coercion intent, document it; if it's vestigial, remove it.
7. Establish a pre-send step that probes all CTA destination URLs after redirect resolution to catch broken links before deployment.
