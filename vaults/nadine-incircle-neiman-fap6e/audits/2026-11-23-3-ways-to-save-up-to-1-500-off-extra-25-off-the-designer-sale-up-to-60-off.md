# 3 ways to save: up to $1,500 off + extra 25% off + The Designer Sale: up to 60% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the Neiman Marcus Black Friday send stacked into one email: "up to $1,500 off," "extra 25% off," and "The Designer Sale: up to 60% off." The black hero with red frames is on brand and easy to scan. Three offers still means no single winner. The eye lands on "Black Friday" and "$1,500 off," but the $1,500 offer has real strings. It needs the code THANKFUL, a $250 minimum, and it excludes sale items.
- This is at least the fifth Black Friday-early send in four days. It follows the Nov 20 "Ends Sunday," the Nov 21 "40% off" exclusive and the Nov 22 "Last day" and "Reminder" sends. The sender name "Neiman's Black Friday" is fine, but the subject is an 83-character list of three offers, and it will truncate on mobile. The nine-item brand grid is generic. There are no prices, no urgency, and no link to a stated deadline. The real end dates (11/28 and 12/2) appear only in the footer.
- Verdict: a competent, tidy bundle that gives the subscriber no reason to act today. It leads with the offer menu and never says which one matters.

## What's working

- The black hero with red outlined boxes ranks the offers visually, and "$1,500 OFF" is the biggest type.
- The promo code THANKFUL is printed in the hero, so there is no hunting for it.
- The "Shop All" button is a clear white-on-black CTA, centered under the offers.
- Six category buttons (women's clothing, shoes, handbags, beauty, men's, men's shoes) route shoppers by interest.
- The render is clean: no broken images, overlapping text or layout glitches.

## What's weak

- Three stacked offers compete, and the subject's "3 ways to save" reads like a coupon sheet.
- The key terms (minimum spend, exclusions, end dates) sit in the footer in tiny type. "Exclusions Apply" under the hero is the only warning.
- The product grid shows brand names only, with no prices, no discounts and no hero product. It feels like filler.
- No deadline appears above the fold. "Time to check off your wish list—and gift list" is soft copy with no urgency.
- Cadence fatigue: the creative is nearly identical to the Nov 20–22 sends, with only the offer mix changed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one deadline in the hero, for example "THANKFUL code: up to $1,500 off, ends 11/28." Demote the other two to a single line.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero, not the footer.
- 3. Replace the brand-name grid with 3–4 priced products showing the discount, such as "Now $X, was $Y."
- 4. Change the "Shop All" button to a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop Designer Sale."
- 5. Suppress or slow the cadence for subscribers who haven't opened the prior sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 25% off + up to 60% off designer`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $250+, save up to $1,500 with code THANKFUL. Beauty included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off sale styles plus the Designer Sale. Ends 11/28.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** The file opens with a long `<!-- REQUIRED variables … -->` block before `<!DOCTYPE …>`. Content ahead of the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some legacy engines, including Outlook/IE-based renderers.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom where honored, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Most email clients ignore this tag, so it is a minor issue.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** Mobile rules such as `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]` and `*[class=f12]` only match when `class` is exactly that single value. Any element that gets a second class silently loses its responsive rule. I can't confirm this occurs because the body is truncated.
- **Checkbox hamburger nav:** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked ~ .menu` pattern is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. A fallback can't be verified from the truncated source.
- **`<title>`:** It is `Neiman Marcus ` with a trailing space and no campaign context. This is minor.
- **Alt text, `lang` and `role="presentation"`:** These can't be assessed from the truncated source.
