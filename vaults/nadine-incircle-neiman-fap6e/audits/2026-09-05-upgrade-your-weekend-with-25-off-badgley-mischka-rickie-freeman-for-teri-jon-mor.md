# Upgrade your weekend with 25% off Badgley Mischka, Rickie Freeman for Teri Jon & more
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-executed version of the same "25% off regular-price styles" mechanic Neiman Marcus already ran on 9/3 ("Surprise: Here's 25% off select regular-price styles from Canali, ALICE + OLIVIA & more," 6.00/10) — same offer structure, same fine-print exclusions, now wrapped in a bolder hero and a nine-item mixed grid instead of a banner-heavy layout. The giant negative-space "Sale" wordmark hero is genuinely striking and puts the 25% off, the "limited time" flag, and a single black SHOP NOW button all in one glance — no scrolling needed, no hunting for the CTA.
- Where it falls short is exactly where the subject promised something specific: Badgley Mischka and Rickie Freeman for Teri Jon are named in the subject line as the headline draws, but the grid actually leads with a Rickie Freeman gown (fine) and never surfaces Badgley Mischka at all in the visible render. That's a bait-and-switch gap between subject and body that a shopper opening specifically for those two names will notice immediately.
- Solid, on-brand, low-effort-to-scan email; docked half a point for the subject-to-grid mismatch and for repeating a mechanic subscribers have now seen at least twice in five days.

## What's working

- The hero's typographic "Sale" treatment is visually distinctive versus the brand's usual gift-card/banner sends — it stands out in a crowded inbox.
- Offer clarity is excellent: "LIMITED TIME," "25% OFF," "select regular-price styles," and "Exclusions Apply" are all visible in one frame with no scrolling.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits directly under the offer with strong contrast (black on gold).
- The nine-item grid spans a real range of categories (dresses, blouses, jeans, outerwear, menswear) rather than just one, giving multiple entry points to click.
- No render bugs — all nine product images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout.

## What's weak

- Badgley Mischka, named first in the subject line, is not visibly present anywhere in the nine-product grid — a clear subject/body mismatch.
- The grid gives no pricing at all (no "$X off" or "was/now"), so the shopper can't gauge savings without clicking through.
- No women's/men's labeling on the grid itself — it's a flat nine-up mix, requiring the two footer buttons (Shop Women's/Shop Men's) to sort by gender.
- This is the second sitewide "25% off regular-price" send in five days (9/3 and now 9/5) with nearly identical mechanics and fine print — cadence is starting to blur together for a subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/grid mismatch first — either swap in a visible Badgley Mischka piece as one of the nine grid tiles, or drop the name from the subject line entirely so the promise matches what's shown.
- 2. Add a price or "before/after" callout under at least the top-row items — right now the click has to happen blind on savings.
- 3. Differentiate this from the 9/3 send with a different creative device (e.g., the current hero type-treatment is good — keep leaning into that rather than reverting to gift-card banners) so back-to-back same-mechanic sends don't feel repetitive.
- 4. Label the grid by category or gender to reduce friction before the two bottom CTAs.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekend refresh: 25% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rickie Freeman, Theia, L'AGENCE and more — ends 9/7`
