# Last Call: Want an extra 25% off?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest send from Neiman Marcus this cycle by a wide margin. After a run of full-price editorial emails (8/19, 8/20, 8/21) and gimmicky mechanics (free-shipping threshold, single-brand points), "Last Call: Want an extra 25% off?" leads with a real, stackable discount — extra 25% off on top of sale styles already marked up to 70% off — in a bold red hero with unmissable type hierarchy.
- The product grid backs up the offer with recognizable, aspirational names (Balmain, Zimmermann, Manolo Blahnik, Dries Van Noten) rather than generic filler, and the layout is clean with no render bugs. The one drag is the deadline: "Ends 9/7/26" is over two weeks out, which undercuts the "Last Call" urgency framing baked into the subject and hero.
- Overall this earns its place near the top of the brand history — it's the honest discount email the InCircle-heavy sends this week were missing.

## What's working

- Hero is a single, unambiguous stacked offer: "EXTRA 25% OFF" + "up to 70% off" + "LAST CALL" badge, all readable at a glance.
- "SHOP SALE" CTA button is high-contrast (white on red) and impossible to miss.
- Product grid pairs real designer names (Balmain, Zimmermann, Manolo Blahnik, Stuart Weitzman) with clean product photography — no lorem ipsum, no broken images.
- Multiple category CTAs below the grid (Shop Women's, Shop Men's, Shop Handbags, Shop Shoes) give a clear next step regardless of what caught the eye.
- "Exclusions Apply" is stated up front in the hero rather than buried only in fine print.

## What's weak

- The "Last Call" urgency framing is undercut by an end date of 9/7/26 — over two weeks away, which reads as generic sale, not last-chance.
- No specific end-of-week or 24-48hr countdown despite "Last Call" branding — a missed urgency lever.
- Fine print discloses "interim markdowns may have been taken," a soft admission that the 70% isn't always off the original price — savvy shoppers will notice.
- This is a designer/mixed-category sale, not tied to any current season or event (compare 8/20's pre-fall arrivals push) — feels slightly generic for late August.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tighten the urgency: either shorten the actual promo window or, if it must run to 9/7, drop "Last Call" language (which implies imminent expiration) in favor of "Sale" framing, or add a nearer secondary deadline ("Extra 25% off ends this week").
- 2. Add a countdown or explicit day-count in the hero ("3 days left" style) if the real deadline is close — right now nothing in the visible copy signals urgency beyond the word "Last."
- 3. Consider trimming the grid to fewer, higher-price-anchor items with visible sale price crossed out — showing the actual discounted price next to strikethrough original would give concrete proof of savings instead of relying on the banner alone.
