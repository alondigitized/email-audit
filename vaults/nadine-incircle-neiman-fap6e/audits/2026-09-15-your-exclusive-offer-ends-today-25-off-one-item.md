# Your exclusive offer ends today: 25% off one item
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a rerun of the same "Exclusively for you: 25% off one item" send from 2026-09-14 (7.00/10), now compressed into an "ends today" urgency play — same mauve hero block, same "AN EXCLUSIVE OFFER JUST FOR YOU / 25% OFF" stack, same black "TREAT YOURSELF" button, same visible promo code banner, just a harder deadline in the subject and legal copy ("Ends 9/15/26 at 10:00 PM CT").
- The mechanic is genuinely strong — a real 25% off with a live unique code printed directly in the creative — and the layout is clean with no render bugs. But running the identical creative two days in a row with only the urgency dial turned up starts to read as the brand leaning on its last genuinely good offer rather than producing new work; a subscriber who opened yesterday's version will recognize this instantly and may feel manufactured urgency rather than real scarcity.
- Net: still a solid, offer-forward email on its own merits, but the back-to-back repeat and heavy exclusion list keep it from being a clean win.

## What's working

- Concrete, visible offer: "25% OFF one regular-price item" with a live unique code (SEPT25-K9K1-Q52FS6) shown right in the hero, no digging required.
- Clear single CTA ("TREAT YOURSELF") sitting directly under the offer with strong visual hierarchy — the eye goes straight to 25% OFF.
- Clean nine-item product grid below the fold gives immediate shopping options across categories (dresses, bags, shoes, outerwear) without forcing a click-through first.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text — everything renders cleanly.

## What's weak

- This is a near-exact repeat of 9/14's "Exclusively for you: 25% off one item" — same hero art, same copy, same code format — just with "ends today" bolted onto the subject line, which reads as recycled creative rather than a fresh send.
- Long, dense exclusion list at the bottom (fragrance, intimates, dozens of named designer brands) undercuts the "25% off one item" promise — the fine print does a lot of quiet excluding.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits in tiny gray text on the hero itself, which is honest but easy to miss against the mauve background at a glance.
- No visible loyalty/member-tier benefit even though Neiman Marcus has a rewards program — a missed chance to layer personalization on top of the generic code.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate the creative from yesterday's send.** If this really is a "last chance" reminder of the same offer, say so explicitly in the hero ("Last Chance: 25% Off Ends Tonight") rather than reusing identical hero art — right now it looks like a duplicate, not a deadline reminder.
- 2. **Move exclusions language out of tiny gray-on-mauve text** and into a slightly more legible placement so the offer doesn't feel like it's hiding its limits.
- 3. **Add a real countdown or time stamp near the CTA** ("Ends tonight at 10PM CT") instead of only in subject/footer — this is the one lever "ends today" urgency actually needs to land in the body.
- 4. **Swap in dynamic/personalized product recs** instead of the same generic nine-item grid used broadly, to make the "exclusive" framing feel earned.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: your 25% off code expires tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hours left — 25% off one item, code inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your code SEPT25-K9K1-Q52FS6 expires at 10PM CT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Don't let this one slip — 25% off, ends tonight.`
