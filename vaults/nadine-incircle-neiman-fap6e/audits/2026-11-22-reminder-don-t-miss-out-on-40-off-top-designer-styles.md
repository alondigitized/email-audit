# Reminder: Don't miss out on 40% off top designer styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, typographic reminder of the Nov 21 "40% off styles from Manolo Blahnik, Etro & more" send. The message is simple: "PRIVATE EVENT," a red "40% OFF," "Select designer styles | Limited time | Code NMACCESS," and a black "SHOP NOW" button. The eye goes straight to the red 40%, and nothing breaks.
- It is also the fifth Neiman Marcus promo in about three days. It follows the Nov 20 "up to 60% off" pair, the Nov 21 "Exclusive access" and Etro sends, and this morning's "Last day" email. A subscriber who got the Nov 21 version will see the same offer under a new code, NMACCESS instead of NMSALE. The send has no product, no image and no human touch, so it reads as a reminder with nothing new in it. The one real reason to click is the deadline, and that deadline is buried in the fine print.
- Verdict: a competent, low-effort reminder. It is fine as a nudge but weak as a standalone send, and it adds to the fatigue the last three days have built.

## What's working

- The red "40% OFF" in large type is the clear focal point, and the offer is legible in about one second.
- Only one offer and one button, so there is no stacking and no confusion, unlike the Nov 20 four-offer send.
- The promo code NMACCESS sits right in the hero, so there is no hunt for it.
- The layout is clean and on-brand: wordmark, four-item nav, and generous whitespace. Nothing overlaps or breaks.
- The footer has useful utility links (app, personal style advisor, find a store).

## What's weak

- The hero has no product, model or imagery. Nothing in it shows what is discounted, so it is generic and gives no reason to click now.
- The real deadline ("Ends 11/26/26 at 10:00 PM CT") appears only in the grey fine print. The hero says only "Limited time."
- The button says "SHOP NOW," a generic label that doesn't name a category or brand. It is also small relative to the headline.
- "Exclusions Apply" under the button and the long legal block (no stacking, interim markdowns, exclusions) undercut how generous the offer feels.
- This is the fifth near-identical promo in three days, and it shifts the code from NMSALE to NMACCESS without explaining why. That is confusing and breeds fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real date in the hero, such as "Ends Thursday 11/26, 10 PM CT," and add a brand or product row below it. A reminder should show what the discount buys, for example 3–4 designer tiles with before and after prices.
- 2. Change the button copy to something specific, like "Shop 40% off designer," and make it larger.
- 3. Cut the cadence. A single "ends tonight" send beats repeated "reminders" at the same discount.
- 4. Explain the code change, for example "Your new code: NMACCESS," so recipients who saved NMSALE don't think it's a different promo.
- 5. Move a short, plain version of the exclusions near the button and keep the legal text in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Thursday: 40% off designer, code NMACCESS`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off designer code expires 11/26`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Manolo Blahnik, Etro and more. Use NMACCESS at checkout before 10 PM CT Thursday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Private event pricing on select designer styles. Online only, exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Neiman Marcus, "Reminder: Don't miss out on 40% off top designer styles"

The HTML was truncated mid-stylesheet, so only the head, CSS and a leading comment block were inspected. The body, footer, links and unsubscribe markup were not visible. Anything outside that is marked unverified rather than assumed.

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML Transitional email with a mobile media-query layer. Two confirmed code-level issues are an exposed SFMC template comment and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and deliverability warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified.** 14 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking domains, so none were checked for 200 status or correct destination.
- **Plain-text part is link-heavy.** The QA data shows 2638 of 3579 characters (about 74%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. This is typical of long tracking URLs, but it can contribute to spam scoring on the text/plain alternative.
- No broken or malformed links were observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Dead code in the head.** There are several commented-out viewport variants (`width=320, initial-scale=0.29`, `width=1024`, `user-scalable=no`). Harmless, but it adds weight and suggests unreviewed template history.
- **Possible hamburger-nav fragility.** The mobile nav depends on the `.navbar-checkbox:checked ~ .menu` checkbox hack (`*[class].navbar-checkbox`, `*[class].hamburger`). Gmail and other clients that strip or ignore `:checked` selectors will not show the menu. I cannot confirm the fallback behavior without the body markup.
- **Class selectors may be brittle.** Selectors like `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=mobile]` use exact-match attribute selectors. They fail if an element has more than one class. I cannot confirm this without seeing the elements.
- **Alt text and `lang` attribute are unverified.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute. That is a minor accessibility gap for screen readers.
- `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space. This is cosmetic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **The SFMC and AMPscript variable-setup comment is in the delivered HTML.** The leading `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->` ships to recipients.
  - It exposes internal data-extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) and the `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` option.
  - It also contains unbalanced parentheses in the example `Lookup(...))`, which suggests it is copy-pasted template documentation.
  - It sits before the `<!DOCTYPE>`. A comment before the doctype puts some older rendering engines (IE) into quirks mode.
- No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens were observed outside that comment in the visible portion. The body was not visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **No confirmed defects.** The QA warnings are unverified:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` header not found.
  - The tool itself says these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so absence in the captured message is not evidence the sender omitted them.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source and are unverified.
- Sender domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable.** All 14 tracked links were skipped, so no final landing URLs or UTM parameters were resolved.
- The template references a `@refid` built from `@base_refid`, which implies a campaign reference parameter. Whether it appears on the links, and whether the landing pages match the "40% off designer styles" offer, was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC and AMPscript documentation comment** before send, or move it into a server-side `%%[ ]%%` block that is not output. It exposes internal DE and field names.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag (and avoid `user-scalable=no`) to allow zoom.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
4. **Delete the commented-out viewport variants** from the template.
5. **Re-run the audit against a source that preserves raw headers.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com`) from a direct inbox copy, such as the Gmail "Show original" view.
6. **Resolve the 14 skipped tracking links** with a redirect-following check, to confirm final destinations, UTM or `refid` parameters and offer alignment.
7. **Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part**, or add more descriptive plain text, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
8. **Provide the full, untruncated HTML** if you want the body, footer and image `alt` attributes audited.
