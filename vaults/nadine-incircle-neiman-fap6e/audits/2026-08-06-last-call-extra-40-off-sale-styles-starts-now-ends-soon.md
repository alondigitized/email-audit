# Last Call: *Extra* 40% off sale styles starts now (& ends soon)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus finally escalating the number instead of just re-running it — "Extra 40% Off" is a step up from the "Extra 25% Off Last Call" hero that's run at least four times since 7/29 (7/29, 8/1, 8/2, 8/3), and the "up to 75% off" total-savings framing adds real stakes. It's the same red-block-plus-white-button template the brand always defaults to, but the escalation from 25% to 40% is a legitimate reason to open if you've been ignoring the last few sends.
- Below the fold it's the standard nine-product merchandising grid (Zimmermann, Akris, Cinq à Sept, Veronica Beard, FRAME, PURPLE, Stella McCartney, Fendi, Vince) with zero prices shown, then category buttons, then a 15% text-signup pitch, then footer. Nothing here is broken, nothing is exciting — it's competent, over-familiar Last Call furniture.
- The real problem isn't this individual send, it's fatigue: this is the fifth-plus near-identical maroon Last Call hero in ten days. The percentage bump buys back some attention, but the deadline (8/9, 3 days out) and the total absence of pricing on any of the nine products keep this in solid-not-great territory.

## What's working

- The headline escalates the offer math clearly: "EXTRA 40% OFF" stacked over "SELECT SALE STYLES FOR UP TO 75% OFF" is legible and quantifies the total discount, not just the extra layer.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("SHOP SALE") sits directly under the offer with high contrast (white button on red).
- Nine-item product grid gives real browsing options across categories (dresses, knitwear, denim, accessories, outerwear) rather than one hero product.
- Fine print in the footer states a hard, specific deadline (8/9/26, 10 PM CT), which a scanning reader could still catch even though it's not in the hero.
- No render bugs — clean grid alignment, no overlapping text, no broken images.

## What's weak

- No prices or percent-off shown on any of the nine product tiles — a shopper has to click through blind to know if a $2,000 Akris dress or a $400 Vince shirt is what's on sale.
- Zero urgency in the hero itself — the actual deadline (8/9) is buried in 8-point footer legalese, not stated near the CTA where it would drive action.
- Same red block + serif "LAST CALL" wordmark this brand has run at minimum five times in the past ten days; visually indistinguishable from 8/3's send at a glance.
- No product is more prominent than any other — flat 3x3 grid with equal-weight images gives the eye nowhere obvious to land after the hero.
- No loyalty/InCircle member benefit called out despite this being exactly the kind of member who'd appreciate points-multiplier framing on a Last Call push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 8/9 deadline into the hero itself (e.g., "Extra 40% Off — Ends Sunday") — urgency drives Last Call opens more than the discount number alone, and right now it's invisible until the footer.
- 2. Add a price or "from $X" under at least the top row of products so the grid isn't a blind click-through.
- 3. Break the visual monotony vs. recent sends — swap the flat maroon block for a lifestyle image with the offer overlaid, since the brand's own history shows this exact template repeating too often.
- 4. Surface InCircle/loyalty point multipliers if this member has status — differentiate from the generic list.
- 5. Reorder the grid to lead with 1-2 stronger hero products (larger tile) before dropping into the uniform 3x3, giving the eye an anchor.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 75% off total. New markdowns just added — shop before 8/9.`
