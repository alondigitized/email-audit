# Reminder: Up to $500 off your purchase
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same "Up to $500 off" sitewide-discount play Neiman Marcus already ran on 7/25 (7.00/10 here) — spend/save ladder table, code NMJULY, black CTA button, blue hero block. The only structural difference is the word "Reminder:" bolted onto the subject line, which is a tell that this is a fatigue-send to non-clickers from the original blast, not a fresh creative. The merchandising grid below the hero is well-curated and legible — Mac Duggal, Alice + Olivia, Jimmy Choo, Rag & Bone, Amiri, STAUD, a nice mixed-category spread of dresses, denim, shoes — but it's the same offer mechanics as two days ago with zero new urgency beyond a vague "Limited Time."
- The spend/save table is the strongest element here: it's concrete, scannable, and lets a shopper self-select their tier instantly. But a reminder email with no new deadline, no incremental discount, and no "expires tonight" framing is a weak excuse to hit the inbox again — this reads as list-fatigue automation, not a deliberate campaign beat.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, no render issues — but it's a rerun with a prefix slapped on, and the footer fine print (excludes exclusives, beauty, fragrance, gift cards, Bergdorf Goodman, Saks Global employees) undercuts the "$500 off" headline hook.

## What's working

- Spend/save ladder table is genuinely useful — five clear tiers from $200/$50 to $2,000+/$500, no math required.
- Clean visual hierarchy: blue hero block draws the eye immediately to the offer and CTA before anything else.
- Product grid is well-photographed and diverse (dresses, denim, shirting, shoes) with recognizable designer names as social proof.
- No render bugs — every image loaded, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Promo code (NMJULY) is bold and impossible to miss.

## What's weak

- "Reminder:" subject prefix with identical creative to 7/25's send screams automated fatigue email, not a fresh campaign beat.
- No new urgency — "LIMITED TIME" is vague; the actual end date (7/27/26 10PM CT) is buried in 6-point footer legal text, not in the hero or subject.
- Heavy exclusions (exclusives, beauty, fragrance, gift registry, Bergdorf Goodman, Saks Global employees) sit far from the headline offer, which reads as a bit of a bait-and-switch on close inspection.
- No loyalty/InCircle member benefit shown despite NM's active InCircle program appearing in past sends — a missed personalization layer for a repeat/loyal shopper.
- Cadence: this is now the second identical-offer email in two days, which risks training subscribers to skip it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the hero, not the footer — "Ends 7/27 at 10PM CT" directly under "LIMITED TIME" would create actual urgency instead of vague filler text.
- 2. Since this is a reminder, differentiate it — bump the top tier, add a bonus gift-with-purchase, or lead with "Your $500 is about to expire" framing instead of re-running identical creative.
- 3. Surface InCircle points multiplier or member-exclusive early access if available, to reward the segment getting a second touch on the same offer.
- 4. Move 1-2 of the harshest exclusions (beauty/fragrance excluded, Bergdorf Goodman excluded) into a visible one-liner near the offer so it doesn't feel discovered only at checkout.
- 5. Retire "Ready, set, summer" — it's generic filler; replace with copy tied to the actual urgency ("Last chance for $500 off").
