# Get into vacation mode with 1,000+ new designer arrivals
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is NM's vacation-mode editorial new-arrivals send — a no-discount announcement built around a commanding black hero and a 12-product grid split across women's and men's categories. The luxury brand lineup is genuinely strong (Gucci sandals, Prada denim, Balmain bags, Balenciaga, McQueen) and signals real curation. But the email lands on the same day as the "Up to 60% Off Designer Sale" escalation (which scored 9/10 here), and that juxtaposition is brutal: a subscriber who already saw the sale email reads this and wonders whether to chase the discount or pay full price for new arrivals. That's a confused value proposition caused by poor send scheduling, not bad creative.
- The deeper problem: "1,000+ new designer arrivals" is the exact same count framing used in the June 15 "Summer arrivals: 1,000+ new looks" email just three days ago. For a subscriber opening this, it does not read as fresh. The number that was a specific, energetic signal last Tuesday is now a stale copy-paste.
- No price, no InCircle angle, no urgency, no preheader. This is an editorial browse email with high visual quality and low commercial pressure. It'll satisfy the window-shopper and leave the buyer with nothing to act on.

## What's working

- **Hero lands hard**: "BRAND NEW STYLES / JUST LANDED" on full black is unambiguous and visually dominant — eye goes there first, no confusion about the email's purpose.
- **Brand lineup earns attention**: Gucci, Prada, Balenciaga, Balmain, McQueen, Palm Angels in a single send signals genuine curation, not a clearance dump.
- **Women's/men's split is clean**: The section-level CTAs ("Shop All Women's New Arrivals" / "Shop All Men's New Arrivals") divide the grid cleanly and respect the dual-audience.
- **Render is flawless**: All product images loaded, typography consistent, no layout breaks visible.
- **Seasonal tie-in is timely**: "Vacation mode" + late-June new arrivals is the right calendar beat.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever on the same day as 60% off**: Two NM emails on June 18 — one screaming sale, one asking for full-price browse — split subscriber attention and let the sale email cannibalize this one's click pressure.
- **"1,000+ arrivals" is a recycled hook**: June 15 used the same count in the same context. Three days is too soon to repeat a headline number verbatim.
- **Zero price context in the product grid**: Every item shown is beautiful and unnamed except by brand — no price, no size indicator, no sale callout. Nothing converts a "nice" into an "add to bag."
- **No InCircle mention anywhere**: NM's best retention mechanic — the one they ran on June 15, 16, and 17 — is completely absent here.
- **No preheader visible**: The rendered email goes straight from header nav to hero with no explicit preheader text, so the inbox preview will pull navigation or hero copy rather than a purposeful inbox line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Price at least two products in the grid** — even one "Gucci Sandals · $695" label converts this from editorial to e-commerce. The June 16 spend-to-save send proved a price anchor materially changes click behavior.
- 2. **Don't send this on a sale day** — schedule this on a standalone Tuesday or Thursday when it isn't competing with 60% off messaging in the same inbox session.
- 3. **Retire "1,000+" until it's been at least two weeks** — June 15 already spent that number. Use a specific brand capsule count or name the hero brand in the subject instead.
- 4. **Add one InCircle line below the hero**: "InCircle members earn points on every new arrival" adds perceived member value with zero creative lift.
- 5. **Write an explicit preheader** to control the inbox preview line before it defaults to nav junk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New this week: Gucci, Prada, Balenciaga + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just arrived: 12 designers, zero compromises`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From sandals to denim — shop before your size goes`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `InCircle members earn points on everything new`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Vacation Mode / 1,000+ New Arrivals"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

SFMC AMPscript template with an unresolved variable-initialization fork and missing one-click unsubscribe infrastructure. QA returned 4 warnings across deliverability and compliance; 26 tracking links were unreachable for destination verification.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 26 tracking links unprobed | QA: click-redirect domains blocked HTTP probe; destination URLs and UTM integrity unverifiable |
| `@refid` is a no-op concat | `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` — empty second argument suggests a suffix token was intended but omitted; if used in tracking URLs the value may be incomplete |
| `@uEm` / `@cmdid` have no null-guard | Both looked up from `NM_CRM_Feed`; subscribers absent from that feed will produce empty strings, silently breaking any URL that embeds these values |

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Three conflicting viewport `<meta>` tags | Two are commented out (`width=device-width`, `width=320/initial-scale=0.29`, `width=1024`) but remain in source; maintenance hazard — wrong one could be uncommented accidentally |
| Fragile responsive class selectors | CSS uses `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]`, etc. — exact-match attribute selectors break if any element carries more than one class (e.g. `class="mobile hide"`) |
| CSS checkbox interactivity | `.mobileCont:checked~table *[class=contentInteractive]` pattern has near-zero client support outside Apple Mail; content in `contentInteractive` nodes is invisible to most subscribers |
| Layout tables lack `role="presentation"` | No evidence of `role="presentation"` on structural tables; screen readers will announce them as data tables |
| `<title>` has trailing whitespace | `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor but indicates template hygiene issues |

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Active variable scenario not selected | Header comment: `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` with no un-commented scenario block visible; `@user` assignment is fully commented out (`<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`) — if downstream blocks reference `@user`, it renders as empty or the raw token |
| `@emailid` has ambiguous source | Comment describes two resolution paths (sendable DE field vs. `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` lookup) with no visible conditional; double-lookup risk if both execute |
| No fallback values documented | `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, `@emailid` all depend on lookup tables with no `IIF()`/default guards in the visible source |

---

### 5. Compliance

| Finding | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header absent | High | QA WARN — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy (>5k/day); absence increases spam folder placement |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent | High | QA WARN — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe; Gmail/Yahoo have enforced this since Feb 2024 for bulk senders |
| Authentication-Results not captured | Medium | QA WARN — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown; likely an AgentMail relay gap but must be confirmed against raw MX delivery headers |
| Plain-text body is 90.4% URLs | Medium | QA: 5,214 of 5,767 plain-text chars are URLs — Spamassassin and similar filters score this heavily; plain-text should mirror readable prose |

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `@refid` attribution may be truncated | `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is functionally identical to `@base_refid` — if a campaign modifier suffix was meant to be appended, click attribution will be miscategorized in analytics |
| UTM coverage unverifiable | All 26 tracked links were skipped by the HTTP probe; cannot confirm UTM parameters are present or consistent across link blocks |

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Unsubscribe headers (blocking)** — Confirm SFMC send classification is configured to inject `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` at the MTA layer. If headers are present on actual delivery but missing from the AgentMail relay capture, add a raw-header check to the QA pipeline against a seed inbox.

2. **Resolve the `@user` scenario fork** — Remove all commented-out scenario blocks and leave exactly one active. If SHA256 hashing is the live path, uncomment that assignment; otherwise delete the dead comment to prevent accidental activation.

3. **Add null-guards to CRM lookups** — Wrap `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, and `@emailid` in `IIF(Empty(...), "", ...)` or equivalent so missing CRM records degrade gracefully rather than emitting empty tracking parameters.

4. **Fix `@refid` concat** — Either supply the intended suffix string or simplify to `SET @refid = @base_refid` to make intent explicit and searchable.

5. **Rebuild plain-text version** — Replace the URL-dump plain-text with a prose summary of the email. Target <20% URL density to avoid spam filter penalties.

6. **Validate authentication headers** — Probe a seed inbox delivery for raw `Authentication-Results` to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC are passing; do not rely solely on the AgentMail relay capture for this check.

7. **Remove dead viewport meta tags** — Delete the two commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants; one active tag is correct, the others are a maintenance trap.
