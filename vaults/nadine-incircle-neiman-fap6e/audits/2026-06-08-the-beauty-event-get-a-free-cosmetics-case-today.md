# The Beauty Event: Get a FREE cosmetics case today!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- The Beauty Event is NM's gift-with-purchase mechanic: spend $175 on beauty, take home a free cosmetics case. The hero is well-composed — two pouches (teal and pink) against a garden backdrop, followed by a product grid showing Creed, Chanel, La Mer, La Prairie, and Tom Ford. The InCircle triple-points callout is a smart loyalty kicker. On category pivot alone, this is a good call: after four consecutive gift card emails ending with yesterday's "LAST CHANCE" fatigue blast, switching to beauty is the right move.
- The critical flaw is visible in the first three seconds: the hero banner reads "JANUARY 2026" in clear type. This email is going out in June with five-month-old creative stamped on the front. For any subscriber who notices — and plenty will — trust evaporates. It reads like NM pulled a January campaign out of storage and hit send without updating the date. That's a significant operational failure for a brand that otherwise ran six carefully-timed sends in the past week.
- The offer itself is solid and the category pivot is correct. The stale date and a generic "SHOP NOW" CTA are the only meaningful execution misses.

## What's working

- Free cosmetics case GWP is unmissable in the hero — the offer reads instantly without scrolling
- Product grid (Creed, Chanel, La Mer, La Prairie, Tom Ford) correctly signals the luxury tier and targets the right shopper
- InCircle triple points callout is a smart fence-sitter hook for members already near the $175 threshold
- Six category-specific shop buttons (BEAUTY, FRAGRANCE, SKIN CARE, MAKEUP, HAIR & BODY, HAIR CARE) give easy deep navigation past the generic CTA
- Mobile app block (find a store, meet with a beauty advisor) adds a meaningful in-person service layer that most brands skip

## What's weak

- "JANUARY 2026" date stamp in the hero is plainly visible on a June send — looks like recycled creative and immediately undermines the campaign's credibility
- "FREE" in ALL CAPS in the subject is a spam-trigger signal for both filters and subscribers who scan for aggressive language
- "SHOP NOW" is a generic CTA that misses the chance to close the loop on the GWP promise ("Get Your Free Case" does the same job in four words)
- No visible offer deadline inside the email body — "today!" lives only in the subject line, not anchored by a date or countdown in the hero
- Six NM emails in seven days is aggressive; email frequency burn is starting to work against even well-targeted sends

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or remove the "JANUARY 2026" date in the hero.** This is the single most damaging visible element. Update it to the current month or replace with an evergreen seasonal label — this is a one-field edit that would meaningfully lift subscriber trust.
- 2. **Rewrite the primary CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to "Get Your Free Case" or "Claim Your Cosmetics Case" — mirrors the hero promise and removes ambiguity about what the click delivers.
- 3. **Add a visible deadline inside the email body.** "Offer ends June 10" or a "Today only" badge under the CTA makes the urgency credible without requiring subscribers to remember what the subject said.
- 4. **Lower-case "FREE" in the subject** to reduce spam signal exposure while keeping the same meaning.
- 5. **Write a real preheader** that extends the offer rather than leaking view-in-browser junk — lead with the brand names or spend threshold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty Event: Free cosmetics case with any $175 beauty purchase`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cosmetics case — spend $175 on Chanel, La Mer & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Creed, La Prairie, Tom Ford and more — free gift with every $175 spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: InCircle members earn Triple Points this week only.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus Beauty Event Email
**From:** NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com | **Date:** 2026-06-08

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The template carries two unresolved AMPscript variable assignment defects that risk rendering literal variable names to recipients, and the QA relay did not capture `List-Unsubscribe` headers, leaving one-click unsubscribe compliance unverified. The remaining issues are template hygiene and deprecated CSS.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped by HTTP probe.** All click-redirect domains were excluded from reachability checks, so broken destination URLs or redirect chain failures cannot be ruled out from this audit alone. A manual spot-check of at least the primary CTA and unsubscribe link destinations is warranted.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is a no-op.** Concatenating with an empty string literal adds no transformation. If downstream tracking logic depends on `@refid` being distinct from `@base_refid`, this is a silent passthrough that will produce identical values. Likely a template stub left unfinished.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants remain in `<head>`:**
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Dead weight in production source — indicates the template was forked from multiple versions without cleanup. No rendering impact, but signals template hygiene risk.

**`max-device-width` used in media queries:**
```css
@media only screen and (max-device-width: 480px), screen and (max-width: 480px)
```
`max-device-width` is deprecated in the CSS Media Queries Level 4 spec and is not reliable in modern WebKit. The redundant `max-width` fallback in the same rule mitigates breakage for current clients but the deprecated form should be removed.

**iOS 9 hamburger `@-webkit-keyframes bugfix` present:** Targets an OS version from 2015; adds parse overhead with no practical benefit for any supported client.

**`<title>` has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor, but may appear in some preview panes or accessibility tree readers with the extra whitespace.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Two AMPscript variable assignments are missing the `=` operator:**

```
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
@cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
```

Both lines are missing the assignment operator (`= Lookup(...)`). In Salesforce Marketing Cloud AMPscript, this means `@user` and `@cmdid` are never assigned — they evaluate to empty string. Any downstream personalization or tracking that references `@cmdid` (typically used for CRM attribution) will silently drop its value.

**Double-closing parenthesis on four lookups:** `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, and `@cmdid` all end with `))` instead of `)`. In SFMC this causes an AMPscript parse error at send time, which may suppress personalization silently or cause a fatal render failure depending on error handling configuration.

**`SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` is commented out in mixed syntax:**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```
This mixes HTML comment syntax with AMPscript block comment syntax. The comment says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW," implying the SHA256 path was intentionally disabled, but the alternative (`@user Lookup(...)`) has the missing `=` defect above, so `@user` is unset in both branches.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header: not detected** (QA warn). CAN-SPAM and Gmail/Yahoo sender requirements expect this header to be present. The AgentMail relay may be stripping it, or it may be absent at the MTA level. This must be verified against raw received headers from a test send — absence here is a deliverability and compliance risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header: not detected** (QA warn). Gmail and Yahoo have required one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) since February 2024 for bulk senders. If absent in the actual MTA output, this is a current deliverability policy violation.

**`Authentication-Results` header: not captured** (QA warn). SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status for `e.neimanmarcus.com` cannot be confirmed from this audit. The sending domain uses a subdomain ESP envelope, which requires its own DKIM key alignment. Verify via raw headers on a live test send.
