# Don't miss out on up to $250 off your purchase today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the tiered-dollar-off mechanic finally paired with an actual visual hierarchy: a clean, honest yellow hero block reading "LIMITED TIME / UP TO $250 OFF" with a legible spend/save table ($400→$100, $1,000→$250) and a promo code right there — no digging through fine print for the numbers. It's a step up from the gift-card gimmick this brand ran into the ground on 8/18, 8/21, and 8/25, and it's cleaner than the "Limited time: Get up to $250 off" send on 8/26, which used the same offer but buried the table lower and split the message across a longer scroll.
- Where it falls down is the same place Neiman Marcus always falls down: the nine-item product grid underneath is brand-name-only labeling (Mac Duggal, FRAME, Jimq Choo, Amiri, STAUD, rag & bone, Jacquemus) with zero prices, zero indication of which items are even eligible for the discount, and no size/availability signal. It's a scattershot mix of a $600 gown, denim, boots, sandals, and a bucket bag — nothing curated toward a specific shopper, which is consistent with the brand's recurring "flat six/nine-item grid" pattern seen across 8/24, 8/26, and 8/27 sends.
- Net: best offer clarity of this brand's recent run, but the same lazy, undifferentiated product grid drags it back to average.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unmissable above the fold: bold "$250 OFF," a literal spend/save table, and a visible promo code (SHOPFALL).
- Single unambiguous CTA — black "SHOP NOW" button with strong contrast against the yellow hero.
- "Exclusions Apply" is disclosed right in the hero, not hidden in a footer wall of text (though full terms are still down there too).
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout bugs.
- Category shortcut buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Accessories) give a secondary path if the grid doesn't land.

## What's weak

- Product grid shows nine random items with no prices — can't tell what's actually in the $100–$250 discount range or if any of it qualifies at all ("Sale and reduced-price merchandise do not qualify").
- No urgency signal in the visible body — the "Ends 9/3/26" deadline is buried in dense footer legal copy, not called out near the CTA.
- Grid has no gender/category cohesion (womenswear gown next to menswear shirt next to unisex bucket bag) — reads like a generic catalog dump, not a personalized pick.
- No loyalty/InCircle member benefit shown despite Neiman Marcus having a well-known loyalty program.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Ends 9/3/26" deadline out of the footer and into the hero — a date stamp next to "LIMITED TIME" would convert vague urgency into credible urgency and is the single highest-leverage fix here.
- 2. Add prices (or at least "eligible for SHOPFALL") to each grid item — shoppers won't click through nine unpriced products to find out if they qualify.
- 3. Split the grid into two focused categories (e.g., "Women's" and "Accessories") instead of one undifferentiated nine-item wall — matches the "Shop Women's/Men's" buttons already in the footer.
- 4. Surface InCircle points or member-exclusive framing if this segment is a loyalty member, consistent with the loyalty-benefit signal this brand keeps skipping.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $250 discount is waiting — SHOPFALL`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $1,000, save $250 — shop new arrivals before 9/3`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New fall styles from FRAME, Jimmy Choo, Amiri + more`
