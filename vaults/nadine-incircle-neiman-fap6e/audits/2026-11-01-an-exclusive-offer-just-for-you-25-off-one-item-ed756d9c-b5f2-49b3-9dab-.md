# An exclusive offer just for you: 25% off one item
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-built Neiman Marcus send in the recent history. After a week of Fall Style Sale repeats, the "Ends today" gift card email and several no-offer editorial sends (Oct 28 "A holiday filled with fantasy", Oct 30 "Things We Love"), it finally leads with a personal, single-item offer. A black hero carries a huge serif "25% OFF", a white SHOP NOW button, and a personal promo code in a cream band right below. The hierarchy is unmistakable.
- The weak points are the fine print and the deadline. Exclusions are extensive, and they cover Chanel, Dior, Gucci, Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Moncler, Canada Goose and many more. "Exclusions Apply" under the button is honest but small. The real deadline, 11/3/26 at 10:00 PM CT, is buried in the legal text, while the hero says only "for a limited time". A 3x3 product grid of eligible brands partly makes up for this, and six category buttons give shoppers clear next steps.
- My judgement: it's a strong, clear promotional send. It would be stronger if it told the reader the deadline up front and made it obvious what is actually excluded.

## What's working

- The hero is the clear first read. A big serif "25% OFF" on black, with the single CTA beneath it.
- The promo code sits in its own cream band directly below the hero, so there's no hunting for it.
- The "exclusive offer just for you" framing and the unique code make it feel personal, unlike the repeated mass Fall Style Sale sends.
- The 3x3 grid shows eligible, on-brand designers (Veronica Beard, Miu Miu, Chloe, Ferragamo, Zegna) with brand names labelled.
- Six category buttons cover women's, men's, shoes, jewelry, handbags and home, so many different shoppers have a route in.
- The layout is clean, with no visible broken images or render bugs.

## What's weak

- The deadline appears only in the fine print ("Ends 11/3/26 at 10:00 PM CT"). The hero says "limited time", which wastes the strongest urgency lever.
- The exclusions list is long and omits nothing important. It excludes many of the most desirable luxury brands, and "Exclusions Apply" is tiny, so the reader may feel baited.
- Every grid product has a brand name but no price, sale price or product name. The reader can't see what 25% off actually saves.
- The grid items don't carry a visible CTA, and the category buttons are generic. "SHOP NOW" gives no destination.
- The same grid look and the Veronica Beard zebra coat repeat the Oct 31 Veronica Beard send. That makes this feel recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tonight, 10 PM CT". It's the single biggest lift, because it is already true and already in the fine print.
- 2. Add a one-line exclusions summary near the code, such as "Excludes Chanel, Dior, Gucci, Hermès and more", with the full list kept below.
- 3. Show prices in the grid, with the 25%-off price next to the original price on a few items.
- 4. Change "SHOP NOW" to something specific, like "Shop 25% off", and add a "copy code" cue.
- 5. Vary the featured products from the Oct 31 Veronica Beard send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your personal code is inside. Valid through 10 PM CT on 11/3.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. It has leaked internal template comments and an accessibility-restricting viewport tag. The QA run was only partly conclusive: the header checks couldn't be verified through the AgentMail relay, all 30 tracked links were skipped, and the source was truncated before any body links or the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Not assessable from source:** the truncated HTML contains no `<a href>` values, so I can't confirm or flag specific URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables user zoom on iOS and some Android clients. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) concern.
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` (or `xml:lang`), which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Template comments before the DOCTYPE:** the document opens with a large HTML comment, then `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`, before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Content before the DOCTYPE can push some legacy engines into quirks mode.
- **Weak `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject. It shows up in web-view and some client tab or preview contexts.
- **Fragile mobile selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors. They stop matching if a second class is ever added to those elements.
- **Hamburger menu:** the nav uses a checkbox `:checked` hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`). Gmail and most Outlook clients don't support it, so the nav needs a non-interactive fallback. I can't verify one from the truncated source.
- **Stylesheet issues:**
  - `.show` declares `width` twice (`auto !important` and `278px`).
  - The second `<style>` block has no `type` attribute, which is harmless.
- **Alt text and image dimensions:** not assessable, because the truncated source ends before the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template internals exposed:** the leaked comment lists variable names (`@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`). It also lists internal Data Extension names and lookups: `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)` and `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...)`. The `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` instruction is also visible, so the template scaffolding shipped with the send.
- **Leftover template markup:** the comment's syntax has stray parentheses (`@user Lookup(...))`), and the "choose one scenario" block was left in place. This suggests the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **Rendered tokens:** no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens appear outside comments in the visible portion. The remainder is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (QA WARN):** the cause may be that the AgentMail relay didn't capture the header, so this is not confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (QA WARN):** this is the RFC 8058 one-click header. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, so check it on the original message. It's unverified here.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- **Footer not assessable:** the footer is cut off, so I can't confirm the physical postal address or the visible unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible source has no hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked. The 30 skipped tracking links also leave destinations unresolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip template comments at send:** remove or suppress the `REQUIRED variables…` and `SET @user` comments, and move them to AMPscript `/* */` blocks that don't render into output. This removes the DE and field-name exposure and puts the DOCTYPE first.
2. **Fix the viewport tag:** drop `maximum-scale=1`.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
4. **Check headers on a raw copy of the message** (not the relay copy): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` showing SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
5. **Resolve the tracked links:** follow the 30 redirects in a follow-up pass and check the final status codes, UTM parameters and landing-page match.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** so the footer, alt text and link checks can be completed.
7. **Housekeeping:** use class-contains selectors such as `[class~=mobile]`, remove the duplicate `width` in `.show`, and trim the `<title>`.
