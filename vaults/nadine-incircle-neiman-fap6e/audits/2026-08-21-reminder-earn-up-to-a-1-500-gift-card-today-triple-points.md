# Reminder: Earn up to a $1,500 gift card today + Triple Points
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a reminder of the exact offer Neiman Marcus already sent on 8/18 ("Shop new fall collections & earn a $75–$1,500 gift card," which scored 8/10) — same tiered spend-and-earn table, same mint-green hero, same product grid, just repackaged with "Reminder" in the subject and Triple Points for InCircle members bolted on top. The mechanic is still the strongest thing NM has run all week: a real, legible spend-tier table ($500→$75 up to $10,000+→$1,500) plus a genuine loyalty overlay for members, and the product grid mixes recognizable names (La Mer, Jimmy Choo, Burberry, Versace) with visible product imagery and no render bugs.
- Where it falls short of the 8/18 original is urgency and freshness — the deadline (8/25/26) is now closer, which is good, but nothing in the hero visually signals "reminder" or "3 days left," so a subscriber who already saw or acted on the 8/18 email gets no new reason to open this one over the last. It's a solid, honest offer wrapped in a lazy reuse of last week's creative.

## What's working

- The spend/earn table is genuinely useful and scannable — five tiers laid out in a clean grid, easy to mentally match against a shopping list.
- Triple Points for InCircle members is a real added incentive layered on top of the gift card, not a rehash.
- Product grid has strong brand recognition (La Mer, Burberry, Jimmy Choo, Versace, Dolce&Gabbana) with clean, consistent product photography.
- No render bugs — images load, layout is clean, text doesn't overlap.
- Clear single CTA ("SHOP & EARN") sits directly under the hero offer with high contrast against the mint background.

## What's weak

- Zero visual urgency in the hero — no countdown, no "3 days left," no red/orange accent — despite the subject line promising "today." The email doesn't look like a reminder at all.
- This is a near-total creative rerun of 8/18's "Shop new fall collections" send; a subscriber who opened that one will recognize the layout instantly and may skim past it.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the offer table in plain gray text with no link or expansion — same soft-fine-print pattern flagged in the 8/20 free-shipping send.
- The 9-product grid is generic new-arrivals merchandising, not curated around the gift-card threshold (e.g., no callout of "spend $500 on these picks to hit the first tier").

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero visually earn the word "Reminder" — add a deadline countdown or "Ends in 3 days" badge in a contrasting color (not mint-on-mint) so this doesn't read as a duplicate of last week's send.
- 2. Tie the product grid to the spend tiers — e.g., "Spend $500 on picks like these" under the first row — so browsing and the promo math connect.
- 3. Move Triple Points above or beside the gift-card table instead of in a separate black bar below, since for InCircle members it's arguably the stronger hook this time.
- 4. Vary the creative from 8/18 — different hero color, different crop, different product mix — so repeat opens don't feel like déjà vu.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 days left: up to $1,500 gift card + Triple Points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends 8/25: earn your gift card + Triple Points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $500+, earn a gift card — plus Triple Points for InCircle members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your gift card offer expires soon. Here's how the tiers work.`
