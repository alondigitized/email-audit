# Your $50–$500 gift card is here
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is NM's gift-card-earn email — spend $200–$2,500+ with code NM.JUNE and earn a $50–$500 gift card. The subject line ("Your $50–$500 gift card is here") is the best piece of copy NM has written in this review batch: personal, specific, and free of hype. The body delivers on that promise with a clean spend-tier table and a product grid of real luxury brands that gives purchase intent somewhere to land.
- The problem is urgency — or the complete absence of it. No deadline appears anywhere in the email body. A gift-card earn with no expiration reads like a standing promotion that can always wait until tomorrow. Add to that the cadence issue: this is the sixth NM email in roughly four days, following editorial sends, a sale launch, and escalation emails on June 21–23. Even a strong subject fights the inbox when the sender is becoming white noise.
- The "is here" framing in the subject is also a mild bait: the card isn't in the subscriber's account — they have to spend $200–$2,500 to earn it. Compared to the June 22 "Last chance for up to $200 off" send (scored 9/10), this one has comparable offer quality but lacks the deadline pressure that made that email work.

## What's working

- **Subject line is the standout.** "Your $50–$500 gift card is here" — 34 chars, personal, no fluff, concrete numbers.
- **Spend-tier table is upfront and legible.** $200→$50, $500→$105, $1,000→$225, $2,500+→$500 — the math is visible in the hero, not buried in fine print.
- **Product grid earns its space.** Nine named-brand thumbnails (Zimmermann, Jimmy Choo, FRAME, Veronica Beard) give specific reasons to click rather than pushing to a generic category page.
- **15% off text-signup block.** The dark-background "CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF" section adds a secondary hook for subscribers who won't hit the $200 minimum — zero-spend value for someone still on the fence.

## What's weak

- **No deadline anywhere in the body.** "ONLINE ONLY" is the only qualifier visible. A deadline-free earn mechanic has no urgency engine.
- **"Is here" oversells the premise.** The subject implies the card is already yours; it isn't — you have to earn it. Opens expecting a gift card to redeem will feel the gap.
- **Sixth email in ~four days.** After June 21 (twice), June 22 (twice), June 23 (twice), cadence fatigue is real. A good subject still has to beat inbox inertia from overexposure.
- **"ONLINE ONLY" restriction is undersold.** It sits in small caps above the hero headline. In-store NM regulars who open thinking the earn applies everywhere will bounce after checkout discovery.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an expiration date to the hero.** "Offer ends June 30" or even "this week only" converts open intent into action today. Highest-lift single change.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to match the earn mechanic honestly.** "Is here" implies it's already given; it should signal it's available to unlock.
- 3. **Surface "ONLINE ONLY" in the preheader.** In-store shoppers click, reach the exclusion, and churn. One preheader line saves them the trip.
- 4. **Suppress or consolidate cadence.** After five emails in three days, the next send needs to rest a day — or roll this into a product editorial with the earn reminder as a secondary block.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $200, earn a $50 gift card (code inside)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code NM.JUNE online. Offer ends June 30.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus Gift Card Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The template is built on a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) foundation with a table-based XHTML layout. No critical rendering blockers were found, but the AMPscript variable scaffolding contains a probable identity resolution mismatch that could expose EMAIL_ID in tracking parameters instead of the intended SHA256 hash, and both one-click unsubscribe headers are absent from the captured relay headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-redirect domains. No HTTP status verification was possible; broken redirect chains would not surface here.

**Plain-text part is 70% URLs** (5,787 of 8,244 chars). A plain-text body that is almost entirely raw redirect URLs provides no fallback reading experience and may trigger spam filters that penalize URL-dense plain-text parts (Gmail, Proofpoint).

**`@refid` is a no-op concat:**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string copies the value but adds no suffix. If `@refid` was meant to carry a campaign variant suffix, this is silently dropping it, meaning all click-through attribution will collapse onto the base campaign ID.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** Screen readers cannot infer document language. XHTML doctype is declared (`xhtml1-transitional`) but the opening tag only carries `xmlns`:
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">
```
Should be `<html xmlns="..." lang="en">`.

**`<title>` has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor but visible in some preview pane implementations.

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags** left in `<head>` alongside the active one. These are vestigial debugging artifacts and should be removed to reduce template payload and confusion:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```

**Dual breakpoint system** (600px and 480px/520px in separate `<style>` blocks) increases specificity conflict risk. The 480px block targets `max-device-width` (deprecated) alongside `max-width`. No functional breakage confirmed from truncated source, but consolidation is overdue.

**`body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }`** — iOS 9 hamburger menu workaround. Confirmed intentional (labeled in comment), but it fires a continuous animation on every render. No visual impact, but it registers as an active animation in accessibility tooling.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` identity mismatch — likely PII exposure in tracking URLs.**

The variable comment block defines `@user` as:
```
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
This resolves to a numeric **EMAIL_ID**, not a hash. The immediately following comment says:
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```
This SHA256 assignment is commented out. If `@user` is appended to click URLs or firing pixels as an identifier (common in NM's RCT/personalization stack), it would be passing the raw internal EMAIL_ID rather than the privacy-safe hash. This is a potential PII leak in query parameters.

**Internal schema exposed in HTML comments.** Table names and field names are visible in delivered HTML:
```
NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated, NM_CRM_Feed, EMAIL_ID, uEm, cmdid
```
These should not be in the rendered email source. Move variable documentation to AMPscript block comments (`%%[/* ... */]%%`) which are stripped at send time, not HTML comments which are delivered to the inbox.

**"CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" comment** — indicates conditional send logic (likely loyalty tier or gift card denomination routing) where only one code path should be active per send. No evidence the wrong branch was selected, but the comment surviving to production suggests template hygiene is loose.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header: NOT FOUND** (QA warn). Gmail and Yahoo enforce this header for bulk senders (>5k/day). Its absence from the AgentMail relay capture means either (a) it is present at the ESP layer but stripped by the relay, or (b) it was not set. Confirm directly against raw SMTP headers from a seed inbox — not just the relay capture.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header: NOT FOUND** (RFC 8058). Without this, Gmail's one-click unsubscribe button is disabled for this email. As of February 2024, Google mandates one-click for bulk senders. This is a deliverability compliance gap if the header is genuinely absent at SMTP.

**`Authentication-Results` header: NOT FOUND** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from relay capture. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. Requires raw header inspection from a seed inbox delivery.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism:** Cannot verify from truncated source, but standard NM templates include footer compliance blocks. No flag raised absent evidence of omission.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Cannot verify UTM parameters** — all 26 links were redirect-chain URLs that the QA probe skipped. No destination URL structure is available to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` presence or correct `@refid` / `@base_refid` population.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` no-op** (noted above) means if UTM values are dynamically built from `@refid`, they will match `@base_refid` exactly with no variant differentiation. Any A/B or tier-based variant tracking built on `@refid` suffix will be silently lost.
