# The Designer Sale: shop designer favorites now!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is NM's second Designer Sale push in three days — the June 9 send (10/10) launched the sale with specific brand callouts and a "New at" hook; this one arrives on June 11 as a straight reminder with zero new framing. The bones are solid: red hero, clean product grid (Zimmermann, Valentino Garavani, Amiri, Rag & Bone), category-organized CTA row. But where the June 9 email earned its perfect score by naming Arkis and Cinq à Sept in the subject and hero, this send retreats to the generic "shop designer favorites now!" — which is exactly the kind of copy that trains subscribers to tune out.
- The product selection is genuinely good — the Zimmermann florals and Valentino bags are the right bait for the persona — and the "up to 50% off" discount depth is real. The six-category CTA grid is also well-organized. But none of that upside is visible from the inbox; the subject and preheader carry nothing that distinguishes this from any other mid-sale blast. A luxury subscriber who opened the June 9 email has already seen the sale; this one needs a reason to go back.
- Verdict: competent execution of a weak brief. A subject line swap that names two or three of the brands in the grid would do most of the heavy lifting at zero production cost.

## What's working

- Red hero with "UP TO 50% OFF" and a clear "SHOP SALE" button — offer is legible at a glance.
- Product grid features genuine covetable names (Zimmermann, Valentino Garavani, Amiri, Rag & Bone) — no filler brands.
- Six-category CTA row (Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes ×2, Handbags, Dresses) is well-organized and scannable.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears immediately below the hero CTA — transparent, not buried.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: hero → scarcity line → product grid → CTAs flows naturally.

## What's weak

- Subject "shop designer favorites now!" is the blandest possible phrasing — the June 9 email proved named brands in the subject move the needle.
- "LIMITED TIME" without a close date provides no real urgency anchor; subscriber can defer indefinitely.
- No loyalty / InCircle callout — NM's member pricing is a genuine differentiator that's invisible here.
- Second Designer Sale email in 72 hours with no new hook (no "new additions," no "ending soon," no exclusive member window) — risks training the list to skip.
- Preheader "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" is soft; it adds scarcity language but no specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to name brands.** The June 9 send proved this works. "Zimmermann, Valentino & Amiri: up to 50% off" would open stronger with zero production change. The products are already in the grid — just pull the names up.
- 2. **Add a sale end date to the hero.** Replace "LIMITED TIME" with "Ends Sunday" or a specific date. Vague urgency is no urgency.
- 3. **Add an InCircle / member-price callout.** A banner strip or hero sub-line ("InCircle members: additional 10% off") leverages NM's loyalty advantage that competitors don't have.
- 4. **Freshen the subject framing.** If this is a second send to the same list, lead with "New additions to the Designer Sale" or "Last chance" — anything that reframes the recontact.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Zimmermann, Valentino & Amiri: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Designer Sale — new styles added, shop before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Zimmermann dresses, Valentino bags, Rag & Bone denim — limited sizes`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends soon — see which brands still have your size`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "The Designer Sale"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) build with a standard XHTML Transitional structure. Core rendering concerns are minor, but compliance gaps — particularly around List-Unsubscribe headers and SPF/DKIM authentication visibility — are the primary risks for deliverability and inbox placement.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped (click-redirect domains, unprobed)**
All outbound links appear to route through a click-tracking redirect layer. The QA system could not HTTP-probe these, so broken destination URLs cannot be ruled out. Manual spot-check of 3–5 CTAs against actual landing pages is required before send.

**Plain-text body is 70% URLs (5,140 of 5,910 chars)**
This is a strong signal that the plain-text alternative was auto-generated from the HTML rather than hand-authored. Spam filters (particularly Google and Microsoft) penalize plain-text parts that are near-entirely URL content with no surrounding prose. This can suppress inbox placement on Gmail.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Commented-out viewport meta tags (lines 3–5 of `<head>`)**
Three additional `<meta name="viewport">` variants are left in HTML comments:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are dead code from prior testing iterations and should be removed. They add payload weight and create confusion in future maintenance.

**`-webkit-animation: bugfix` hack present for iOS 9**
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
iOS 9 is effectively a zero-share client as of 2026. This adds unnecessary payload and causes an infinite animation event loop in any client that processes it. Safe to remove.

**`<title>` contains trailing whitespace**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Minor, but the trailing space will appear verbatim in browser-preview tab titles and some email preview panes. Strip it.

**No `alt` attributes confirmed on images from the truncated source**
The truncated source does not show image `alt` text. If product images lack `alt` attributes, rendering in image-blocked clients (Outlook default, many corporate environments) will produce blank content with no fallback text — a significant accessibility and content-blocking risk. Requires full-source verification.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` variable has a commented-out alternative assignment**
```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_SHA256 */ -->
```
The comment indicates a scenario choice was not resolved before the audit copy was captured. If this comment is present in the live template, the wrong user identifier may be sent to downstream personalization lookups (`NM_CRM_Feed` via `@uEm`, `@cmdid`). Confirm which scenario block is active in the deployed version.

**Multiple lookup chains with no explicit fallback handling visible**
```
@user = Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
@uEm  = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
@cmdid = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
If any lookup returns null (e.g., net-new subscriber not yet in CRM feed), tokens that depend on `@uEm` or `@cmdid` will render as empty strings or cause AMPscript errors depending on SFMC error-handling config. Verify `EMPTY()` / `ISNULL()` guards exist in the full template.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected (WARN)**
The QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to render the native unsubscribe prompt; its absence increases the likelihood of manual spam reports and degrades sender reputation. SFMC can inject this header via send-time configuration — confirm it is enabled on the sending job.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected (RFC 8058 WARN)**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail for bulk senders ≥5,000/day as of February 2024) depends on both `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Neither was observed. If Neiman Marcus sends at this volume to Gmail domains, this is a compliance requirement, not a best-practice suggestion.

**SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Without confirmed SPF pass + DKIM signature on `e.neimanmarcus.com`, DMARC alignment cannot be verified. This must be confirmed via a direct header inspection of a delivered copy (use mail-tester.com or equivalent with a seed address).

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism**
Cannot verify from truncated HTML. Full source must include a postal address and a functional unsubscribe link. Assumed present given the sender's scale, but must be confirmed in full-source review.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable due to tracking redirect layer**
All 26 links route through a click-tracking domain. The final destination UTMs (e.g., `utm_campaign`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`) cannot be inspected without resolving the redirect chain. Spot-probe 3–5 links manually to confirm:
- `utm_source=email` is present
- `utm_campaign` matches the campaign calendar `@base_refid` value
- Links do not land on a homepage or 404 instead of the Designer Sale collection page

**`@refid` constructed as `CONCAT(@base_refid, "")` — no suffix appended**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
The second argument is an empty string, making `@refid` functionally identical to `@base_refid`. If `@refid` is intended to carry a link-specific suffix (e.g., CTA position or module identifier), this logic is incomplete.
