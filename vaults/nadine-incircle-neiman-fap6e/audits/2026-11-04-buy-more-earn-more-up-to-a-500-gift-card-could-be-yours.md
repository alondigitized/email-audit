# Buy More, Earn More: up to a $500 gift card could be yours
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear spend-and-get tiered gift card promo. A mustard-gold hero reads "EARN A $50-$500 GIFT CARD", with a four-column SPEND/SAVE table ($200→$50, $500→$125, $1,000→$250, $2,000+→$500), a code strip (NOVTGC) and a black SHOP & EARN button. The offer is easy to grasp and the hierarchy is good. Below it sit nine designer product tiles and a six-button category grid.
- It is weaker than the Nov 1-3 "25% off one item" sends. The "Save" row is really a gift card you get later, not money off now, and the long terms block shows how many conditions apply. "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the table. After two days of the 25% off push, this reads as a slightly more complicated replacement. It is a solid promo, not a standout.
- Judgement: well built and honest enough, but the hero is a spreadsheet and the product grid is generic. That limits click appeal.

## What's working

- The tier table makes the whole offer scannable in two seconds, and 25% back at every tier is easy to compute.
- The hero is clean. The headline is large, the button is black on gold, and the code is visible.
- The urgency is real: the terms give an end date of 11/7/26 at 10 PM CT, though it is not in the hero.
- The nine-brand grid (Moncler, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Chloe, Tom Ford) is cleanly spaced and reads as luxury.
- The six-button category block gives easy routes by department, including Beauty.

## What's weak

- The deadline is only in the fine print. The hero has no "ends 11/7", so the urgency is wasted.
- The product grid has no prices and every tile links to a designer, not to an offer. The only copy above it, "Treat yourself (or someone special) to a new designer style," is generic.
- The gold category buttons are low contrast, with tan text on gold, and are weaker than the hero CTA.
- The terms block is long and dense. Gift card issued within 8 weeks, sale items excluded, and "Exclusions Apply" under the table all dilute the offer.
- The grid skews toward women's items, with only two of nine tiles for men.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends Nov 7" next to the code. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. State when the card arrives, e.g. "Card delivered within 8 weeks," so people don't feel misled. Or swap "SAVE" for "GET BACK".
- 3. Add prices or a "qualifies" tag on the product tiles, so the grid shows how a $500 spend could come together.
- 4. Make the category buttons black or outlined for contrast.
- 5. Compress the terms behind a single "Details" link, or at least bold the key exclusions.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code NOVTGC at checkout. Beauty and fragrance count.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $200, get $50. Spend $2,000, get $500. Three days left.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source has no confirmed breakage, but it ships internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) authoring comments in the delivered HTML, and it lacks `lang` and has a zoom-blocking viewport. The three QA warnings (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) are unverified. They are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed. The visible source is truncated and has no `href` values.
- QA skipped the HTTP probe on all 29 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains. Their resolution and destination status are unverified.
- The 62% pass rate with 0 issues reflects skipped or unverifiable checks, not failures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Non-standard DOCTYPE:** the system identifier is `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd`. The canonical URI is `http://`. This is harmless in practice, since email clients don't fetch it.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** the large SFMC comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some older engines treat content before the DOCTYPE as a quirks-mode trigger.
- **Title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` is generic and has a trailing space.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** rules like `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` only match elements whose `class` is exactly that value. Any element with multiple classes won't get the mobile rules. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Hamburger nav:** the checkbox-hack nav (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`) is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. Its fallback is in the truncated portion, so I can't verify it.
- **Alt text:** image `alt` attributes aren't in the visible source, so I can't assess them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens appear in the visible body.
- The header comment is not stripped by the send pipeline. It exposes the `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` variables, and the Data Extension (DE) names `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`.
- It also leaves in the unresolved authoring instructions `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. This is a template-hygiene and information-disclosure issue, not a rendering failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (QA WARN):** unverified. The QA note says the relay may not capture it. Check the raw message at the source.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (QA WARN):** this is the RFC 8058 one-click header. It is likewise unverified, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The `From` domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated footer, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The destination URLs and UTM parameters are in the truncated source, and the tracking links weren't probed. The `@refid` and `@base_refid` variables suggest campaign IDs are appended at send time, but that isn't confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip all SFMC authoring comments, including the `REQUIRED variables` block and the `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO` instruction, in the production render. This removes the DE and field name exposure.
2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw sent message, bypassing the relay. If they're absent, add them.
5. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` from the original headers, not the relay copy.
6. Re-run QA against the full, untruncated HTML. That would let it check the footer unsubscribe and address, the UTM parameters, the image `alt` text, and the hamburger nav fallback.
7. Probe a sample of the 29 skipped tracking links with redirect-following. This confirms they resolve to live landing pages.
8. Change `*[class=x]` selectors to `*[class~=x]` or `.x` so multi-class elements still get the mobile rules.
