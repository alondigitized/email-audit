# Don’t forget: Last Call is up to 70% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red "EXTRA 25% OFF / LAST CALL" hero block Neiman Marcus has now run at least four times in the past week — 7/29 ("Extra 25% off Last Call styles," 7.00/10), 8/1 (near-identical rerun, 7.00/10), and 8/2 ("But first, up to 70% off," 6.00/10). The subject line — "Don't forget: Last Call is up to 70% off" — is a nudge/reminder framing for a promo that's been in every inbox this week, and the creative underneath hasn't moved: same red block, same stack order, same "SHOP SALE" button, and a 9-product grid pulling from the same designer roster (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik have all appeared before).
- To its credit, this is a clean, bug-free execution — the offer is loud and unambiguous, the grid renders properly, and the six category buttons at the bottom give multiple paths to purchase. But cadence fatigue is real: this is a "don't forget" reminder for an offer the subscriber has already seen three times, with no new merchandising angle and no urgency beyond a stale-feeling end date buried in the fine print.
- Judgement: solid mechanics, zero creative or strategic freshness. This reads as an automated reminder blast rather than a considered send, and the "don't forget" subject only underscores that the brand is aware it's repeating itself.

## What's working

- Offer is immediately legible — "EXTRA 25% OFF" in huge type, "UP TO 70% OFF" as support, no scrolling needed to get the headline math.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("SHOP SALE") sits right under the offer stack with strong contrast (white button on red).
- Clean 3x3 product grid with visible brand names (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, etc.) gives the eye somewhere to land after the hero.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact top to bottom.
- Six category shortcut buttons (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Kids, etc.) reduce friction for shoppers who don't want the generic sale grid.

## What's weak

- This is a near-verbatim repeat of the 7/29 and 8/1 hero creative — same layout, same copy stack, same "SHOP SALE" button — with no new visual idea.
- Subject line explicitly signals repetition ("Don't forget") rather than disguising it, which primes a fatigued subscriber to skip rather than open.
- No real urgency: the only deadline is "Ends 8/31/26 at 10:00 PM CT" buried in small fine print at the very bottom — nothing in the hero communicates a ticking clock.
- Product grid has no prices, sale percentages, or "as low as" callouts on individual items — every item looks identical regardless of actual markdown depth.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA as the only qualifier visible above the fold, which reads as a soft caveat right where the eye lands after the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the creative pattern. Four sends of the same red hero block in under a week is the single biggest problem — swap in a lifestyle/editorial hero (like the 8/1 "Field Notes" send) or a different color treatment so this doesn't look like the same email recurring in the inbox.
- 2. Add a real countdown. Surface the 8/31 end date (or a nearer checkpoint) directly in the hero, not in 8pt fine print — "Ends Monday" beats "Don't forget."
- 3. Overlay prices or "Now $X" on at least the top row of grid products — an all-image, no-price grid gives the shopper nothing to react to before clicking.
- 4. Segment the reminder. If this is genuinely a "you didn't open our last one" resend, say so with more specific personalization (e.g., reference the item they viewed) rather than a generic repeat of the whole campaign.
- 5. Vary the subject after three uses of the same offer language — a reminder subject only works if the prior sends earned enough attention to make "don't forget" believable; three near-identical sends in a week undercuts that.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: your 25% off Last Call styles expire soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still 70% off — but not for much longer`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added to Last Call — shop before sizes sell out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off ends this week. Here's what's left.`
