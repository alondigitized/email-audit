# Spotlight on Shearling
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a quiet, editorial send from a luxury retailer with no offer. A model in a chocolate shearling-collar jacket and matching suede skirt fills the hero, with the headline "Shear Bliss" below it. The image is beautiful and the seasonal timing is right for mid-October.
- The weakness is what comes after it. The hero is not shoppable as shown, so the reader has to click to find out what's in it. The seven-piece grid underneath is a loose mix of earrings, a navy jacket, a bag, a red sweater, sunglasses, boots and a skirt. Only one of those pieces (the navy jacket with a dark collar) is shearling-adjacent. There are no prices, and the two top buttons are generic.
- It works as a brand mood piece and not much else. It follows a week of loud offers: the NMCIRCLE "$50–$200 off" run, the gift-card send on Oct 13, and "First Call: Up to 40% off" earlier today. Against those, it's a calm change of pace but gives a shopper little reason to act now.

## What's working

- The hero photo is strong. The shearling jacket is clearly the subject, and the warm beige set suits autumn.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space and a clear top-to-bottom path.
- Brand-name captions (Balmain, Saint Laurent, Givenchy, Fendi) signal a luxury assortment.
- The split "Shop Shearling" and "Shop Outerwear" buttons give two clear entry points.
- It's a calmer, editorial send after several promo-heavy days, which makes the cadence feel varied.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, or urgency anywhere in the email.
- The product grid doesn't back up the "Spotlight on Shearling" theme. Earrings, sunglasses, a red sweater and a skirt have no shearling in them.
- The designer credits are tiny, grey and numbered, so a reader has to match "01–07" to the items and can't tell what any piece costs.
- "Shop the Look" is a small underlined text link. It's weak compared with the boxed buttons higher up.
- The "Shear Bliss" headline is the only copy, and it doesn't tell you what's new, scarce or worth buying.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid match the theme. Show 6 to 8 real shearling pieces (coats, aviators, collars, boots) and tag each one with a visible price or "from $X".
- 2. Turn the hero into a clickable, labeled product call-out, such as "The Balmain shearling moto jacket, $X". Link it to that product page.
- 3. Add one line of reason-to-act copy, such as "New arrivals," "Limited sizes" or "Members get early access". The email currently has none.
- 4. Replace the underlined "Shop the Look" with a full-width button and make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop Shearling Coats".
- 5. Make each grid item tappable with its own name and price, instead of tiny numbered credits.
