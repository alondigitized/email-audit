# You get early access to The Designer Sale: up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, single-offer designer sale send. A black hero reads "THE DESIGNER SALE / EXCLUSIVE ACCESS / 40% OFF" with the 40% in red. Below it are "SELECT DESIGNER STYLES / LIMITED TIME | CODE NMSALE" and a white SHOP EARLY button. The eye lands on the offer in under a second. The hierarchy is the best thing about it.
- It reads as a standard sitewide-style promo with a VIP label. A tidy 3×3 grid of Balmain, Chloé, Dolce&Gabbana, Frame and others sits under the hero. The "early access" framing is the only thing that makes it feel special. There's no deadline in the hero, no prices, and no sale-price reveal, so the 40% stays abstract. The end date (11/15/26, 10:00 PM CT) is buried in 6pt-style fine print.
- It's stronger than the recent no-offer editorial sends, like the Nov 11 "Winter-Ready Boots" roundup and the Valentino piece. It's a real reason to click today. It's still weaker than it could be because it hides the urgency and the proof.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is excellent. The red "40% OFF" on black is unmissable and honest, and the code is right there.
- The offer is concrete: one percentage, one code (NMSALE), one button.
- The product grid is clean and consistent. Every cell has a designer name, and the mix covers dresses, denim, a bag, shoes and menswear.
- Category buttons (Women's, Men's, Handbags, Kids') give clear secondary paths.
- No render bugs. Images load, spacing is even, and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The real deadline (ends 11/15 at 10 PM CT) is only in the fine print. The hero says just "Limited time," which is vague.
- The grid shows no prices, no "was/now," and no discount badges, so the 40% is never made tangible.
- "Select designer styles" and "Exclusions apply" are vague. A shopper can't tell whether the grid items are even included.
- A 15% text-signup block competes with the 40% offer. It's a second, smaller offer that pulls attention away from the main one.
- No models or lifestyle imagery. The flat product shots are tidy but give little emotional pull.
- "SHOP EARLY" is a weak CTA. It has a verb, but nothing says what the shopper gets.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Replace "Limited time" with "Ends Sunday 11/15 · Code NMSALE." That's the cheapest, highest-lift fix.
- 2. Add price proof to the grid. Show "$X now, was $Y" or a "40% off" tag on at least the top row, so the discount is visible.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, for example "SHOP DESIGNER SALE" or "SHOP 40% OFF."
- 4. Name which categories are included in the hero, and soften the exclusions language.
- 5. Move the text-signup block below the footer links, or cut it from sale sends. It dilutes the single offer.
- 6. Add one aspirational hero image, such as a model in a sale piece, and make a few grid items live "bestseller" or "low stock" cues.
