# Prep for the cold months
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, image-led seasonal send with no offer. The hero is a woman in a shearling-trimmed leather vest and wide burgundy trousers, under the headline "Vested Interest." A second block shows a man in a green leather bomber with a shearling collar, captioned "Look cool, stay warm in coats featuring rich colors and stylish accents." The art direction is strong and on-brand, and nothing in the render looks broken.
- The email has no commercial hook. There's no percentage, no deadline, no member benefit and no named product. It also arrives on the same day as the "Up to $200 off + 3x InCircle points" send, which is the third NMCIRCLE push in three days. After that, a no-offer brand-story email reads as filler. It's comparable to the Oct 7 "Forecast calls for Burberry" and the Oct 5 Ralph Lauren sends, which also had no offer. It's a fine editorial moment, but it asks the reader to browse.
- My judgement: a polished mood board that depends on inspiration alone. It will do fine with outerwear shoppers and do little for anyone motivated by price.

## What's working

- The hero image is strong and seasonal, and the "Vested Interest" headline is a clever tie to the vest shown.
- The hierarchy is clean: logo, nav, hero, headline, two buttons. The eye lands on the model first.
- The outlined buttons are readable ("Shop Outerwear", "Shop Designer Collections") and sit right under the headline.
- Showing a women's and a men's look in one send widens the audience, and the green bomber is a distinct, shoppable-looking piece.
- The footer is tidy. It includes the app download, a style-advisor prompt, and store, contact and account links.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, urgency or member value, and no reason to click today.
- The second image's only link is a text link ("Shop Men's Outerwear") with no price, brand or product name, so it's easy to overlook.
- "Shop Designer Collections" is vague and sounds like a homepage link.
- The sale and InCircle messaging from earlier today is missing, so this send doesn't support the offer the brand is pushing that same day.
- The men's block drops off the women's story. The email never says who each half is for until the last link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook, such as an InCircle points callout or "Free shipping on outerwear." Even a small line beneath the headline would give the send a reason to exist.
- 2. Name the pieces and brands in the hero and the bomber ("Shearling-trim leather vest, Brand, $X") and link each image directly to its product.
- 3. Make the second block's CTA a button that matches the first pair, and label it "Shop Men's Leather Jackets" or similar.
- 4. Replace "Shop Designer Collections" with a specific category such as "Shop Women's Coats & Vests."
- 5. Space this send away from the promo sends. A third send in three days with another editorial piece will wear on the list.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible HTML source (truncated) is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile media-query block. The QA run found 0 hard issues and 4 warnings. All four are relay or capture limitations rather than confirmed defects in the email, and the truncation prevents checking links, images and footer.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped 17 tracking links (click-redirect domains) from HTTP probing, so their destinations are unverified.
- The "Plain-text is >70% URLs" warning (3199 of 3707 chars) points to tracked URLs in the text part. This is a deliverability signal, not a broken link.
- No broken or malformed links can be confirmed, because the truncated source doesn't show the `<a href>` values.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- The mobile CSS uses attribute selectors like `*[class=contenttable]`. These match only a class attribute that is exactly that value, so an element with extra classes won't match. This is fragile but not a confirmed failure.
- The hamburger nav depends on `:checked` checkbox and `~` sibling selectors (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`). Gmail app and Outlook strip or ignore these, so the nav likely won't work there. This is inferred from the CSS and not tested.
- `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and carries no subject context. It is minor.
- `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- Alt text, `lang` and `role="presentation"` can't be assessed because the source is truncated.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The top of the source has an HTML comment holding internal SFMC/AMPscript setup notes. It lists `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, the `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed` data extension names, and the instruction "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW". It ships to recipients and exposes internal data-model names. That is an information leak, though low severity.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- QA reports the `List-Unsubscribe` header not found and the `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found. Both warnings say the AgentMail relay may have stripped them. They are unconfirmed, and the sender may well include them. If they are really absent, that would conflict with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, so this is a gap in the test setup and not an email defect. The sender domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated footer, so they can't be verified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No UTM parameters or landing URLs can be confirmed from the visible source. The `@refid` and `@base_refid` variables suggest campaign tracking through a refid parameter and not UTMs.
- QA skipped probing of the 17 tracking links, so landing page alignment is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Remove the internal SFMC variable and data-extension comment block from the production template before send.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message at the source (not through the relay). If missing, add them.
3. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original delivery, or check DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` directly.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
5. Add a plain-text part with readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, or resolve the 17 tracking redirects, to check destinations, alt text and UTM or refid parameters.
7. Provide a fallback for the checkbox-based nav in clients that don't support `:checked`.
