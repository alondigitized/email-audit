# Don't miss out on a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same gift-card-with-purchase offer in six days. Oct 13 ("Treat yourself & earn a $100–$1,250 gift card"), Oct 15 ("Elevate your fall wardrobe & earn up to a $1,250 gift card") and now Oct 18 all use the same plum hero and spend ladder. The only real change is the subject, which now says "Don't miss out" and "today." The creative is clean and the offer is clear. A subscriber who has seen two of these already will skim this one.
- The hero does the work. "EARN A $100–$1,250 GIFT CARD" is large and high-contrast, with a cream "SHOP NOW" button right under it. The five-tier SPEND/EARN ladder is easy to read. Code OURGIFT is shown, and the fall product grid underneath is a polished luxury lineup.
- Two things weaken it. The subject promises urgency ("today"), but the body says the code runs until 10/20/26 at 10 PM CT, so nothing in the email backs up "today." The offer is also weaker than it looks. The fine print excludes sale items, excludes select designers, and pays out as a gift card within 8 weeks. Overall it's competent but stale, a solid mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A large "EARN A $100–$1,250 GIFT CARD" headline on solid plum, with a visible "SHOP NOW" button, puts the offer first.
- The SPEND/EARN ladder ($500→$100 through $5,000+→$1,250) makes the value easy to scan.
- Code OURGIFT sits in the hero, so the shopper doesn't have to hunt for it.
- The 3x3 designer grid (Moncler, Jimmy Choo, Valentino Garavani, Burberry) is consistent and on-brand for fall luxury.
- The category buttons (women's, men's, shoes, jewelry, handbags, home) give clear next steps.

## What's weak

- "Don't miss out… today" implies a same-day deadline. The only deadline in the email is 10/20, buried in the fine print.
- The hero shows "Limited Time" but no end date. The credible deadline is hidden in the legal copy at the bottom.
- The grid products show no prices and have no obvious individual links, so it reads as brand-name wallpaper. At a $500 minimum, the shopper can't tell what qualifies.
- This is the third near-identical send of this offer in six days, which makes it feel like fatigue rather than urgency.
- The "Exclusions Apply" line in the hero is tiny and low-contrast. The long exclusions block below it (sale items, select designers, an 8-week payout) makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Tuesday 10/20," and drop the false "today" framing. If the offer is the same as Oct 15, either change the creative or reduce the cadence.
- 2. Add a few products with prices, and a "qualifying" cue, so the grid supports the spend ladder (for example, "Spend $750, get $175: start with these").
- 3. Make "Exclusions Apply" more legible, and add a one-line summary of the key terms near the hero, such as "Sale items excluded, gift card sent within 8 weeks."
- 4. Vary the creative across sends: a countdown module, a "last chance" hero, or a different product lead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: gift card up to $1,250 ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $500, get $100 back: ends 10/20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code OURGIFT by Tuesday 10 PM CT. Beauty & fragrance count.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall coats, boots and bags qualify. See the full spend-and-earn ladder.`

## Technical Audit: Neiman Marcus, "Don't miss out on a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase today"

**1. Technical Summary**
The truncated source shows no broken markup, but it ships an internal ESP template comment and has a few accessibility and CSS defects. Tracking links and compliance headers could not be verified, so those results are inconclusive rather than clean.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The QA check skipped 30 tracking links (`[WARN] 30 tracking link(s) skipped`). They were not HTTP-probed, so redirect health and final destinations are unverified.
- The HTML was truncated before any `href` values, so I can't check per-link parameters or destination URLs.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`. Screen readers can't pick a pronunciation language.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid font stack:** `.disclaimerText { font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'; }`. Quoting `'sans-serif'` makes it a literal font name, not the generic fallback. If Arial and Helvetica are missing, the fallback fails.
- **Comment before DOCTYPE:** the `<!--REQUIRED variables…-->` block comes before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Older IE and some webmail engines can drop into quirks mode when a comment precedes the doctype.
- **Doctype URL:** the DTD URL uses `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/…`, not the canonical `http://` system identifier. This is harmless in email clients.
- **Exact-match class selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar only match when `class` is exactly that value. Any element with a second class silently loses the mobile rule. The truncated source doesn't show whether any element does.
- **Checkbox/`:checked` hamburger menu:** the mobile nav depends on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~…`. Gmail and Outlook clients don't support this, so those users likely get no working menu. I can't see the fallback markup in the truncated source.
- **Body animation:** `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }` runs forever and forces repaints on every client that honors it. A one-shot reflow hack would do the same job.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The leading comment lists SFMC variables (`@email`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`) and says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW". It also has a `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` line. This is template authoring text that shipped in the delivered HTML.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens are visible in the body of the truncated source.
- `uEm` and `cmdid` look like per-recipient identifiers that probably end up in link query strings. I can't confirm that without the `href` values.

**5. Compliance**
- `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found` and `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)`. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a defect. If the sender really omits them, it falls short of Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found`. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay.
- The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with alignment, but this can't be verified here.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the footer, which the truncated source doesn't include. Not assessed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. No `href` values are in the truncated source, and all 30 tracking links were skipped. Nothing was confirmed either way.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Strip the `<!--REQUIRED variables…-->` block from the production render. It discloses internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and hashing logic. If it must stay, move it below the doctype and out of the sent output.
2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Change `'sans-serif'` to the unquoted generic `sans-serif` in `.disclaimerText`.
5. Change `*[class=x]` to `*[class~=x]` or `[class*=x]` so multi-class elements still match. Provide a non-`:checked` fallback for the mobile nav.
6. Confirm that the sending MTA emits `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. Check the original message headers, not the relay copy.
7. Re-run the link audit with the tracking domain allowlisted or resolved. Check final landing URLs for UTM retention and that no recipient identifiers (`uEm`, `cmdid`) appear unhashed in query strings.
