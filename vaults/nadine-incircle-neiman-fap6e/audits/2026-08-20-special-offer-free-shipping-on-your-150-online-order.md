# Special offer: Free shipping on your $150+ online order
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a free-shipping mechanic, not a real discount — "FREE SHIPPING ON $150+" is a threshold gimmick that InCircle members already get for free on every order, per the fine print at the bottom. That's a meaningful gap: this brand's list is presumably heavy on higher-spend shoppers, and a chunk of them are getting a "special offer" that's already their status quo.
- The nine-product grid below the hero is generic catalog filler — Joe's Jeans, Rails, a Guerlain cream, a rag & bone shirt — with zero connective thread beyond "things we sell." No sale pricing, no curation logic, no seasonal framing. Compared to the 8/18 gift-card send (8.00/10, a real tiered spend-and-earn promo), this is a much weaker mechanic dressed up in the same lavender hero treatment the brand reuses constantly.
- Verdict: functional, clean, on-brand, but this is bottom-of-the-barrel promo inventory — a threshold nobody without InCircle would remember, wrapped around an unrelated product grid.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: offer, code, and CTA button all visible without scrolling.
- Clean 3x3 product grid with consistent image sizing and labeled brands — no render bugs.
- Category shortcut buttons (Women's, Men's, Shoes, Beauty, Handbags, Home) give multiple paths to browse post-hero.
- Fine print is present but tucked at the bottom, not obstructing the offer.

## What's weak

- The offer is functionally meaningless for InCircle members, who get free Standard shipping with no minimum — a segment this brand should be segmenting around, not blanketing.
- The nine products underneath have no unifying theme (denim, evening dress, wallet, skincare, menswear, shoes all mixed) — reads as randomly pulled inventory, not a curated "shop the offer" set.
- $150 free-shipping is a low-stakes, low-urgency mechanic with no deadline shown anywhere in the frame — nothing pushes a reader to act today versus next week.
- No price cut anywhere in the email — pairs a weak offer with a non-curated grid, the least compelling combination this brand has sent this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send — suppress or reword for InCircle members who already get free shipping unconditionally; for everyone else, pair the shipping offer with an actual percentage-off incentive to give it teeth.
- 2. Replace the random 9-product grid with a themed capsule (e.g., "under $150 to unlock free shipping" or a single category) so the grid reinforces the threshold instead of ignoring it.
- 3. Add a deadline to the hero — "ends Sunday" or similar — free shipping offers with no urgency read as evergreen and easy to defer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SHIPNM at checkout — ends Sunday`
