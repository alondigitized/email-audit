# Because you're family: Enjoy 25% off select styles & 20% off select jewelry
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time Neiman Marcus has hit inbox with this exact Friends & Family mechanic — 25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry — after 2026-09-17's original announce and 2026-09-20's "Friendly reminder" reissue. Nothing here is new creative or a new offer; it's the same green hero, same headline, same deadline (9/27/26 10:00PM CT) run a third time in nine days. That's aggressive cadence for an identical promo.
- That said, execution is clean: legible hero with a real, unambiguous discount, a nine-item designer product grid (MOTHER, Mac Duggal, Alice + Olivia, L'Agence, Balmain, Stuart Weitzman, Theory, Polo Ralph Lauren, Vince) with no render bugs, and a clear InCircle Double Points callout for loyalty members. It's a solid execution of a promo that's now stale from repetition — the brand is squeezing the same event for a third send rather than earning a fresh open.

## What's working

- Clear, high-contrast hero: "25% OFF select styles + 20% off jewelry" is legible instantly, no scrolling needed.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("SHOP THE EVENT") sits directly under the offer.
- InCircle Double Points banner gives loyalty members a visible reason to act beyond the discount.
- Nine-item product grid spans a real range of categories (denim, dresses, outerwear, handbags, boots, menswear) with clean product photography and no broken images or overlapping text.
- Multiple category CTAs below the grid (Women's, Shoes, Handbags, Men's, Home) reduce friction to a relevant landing page.

## What's weak

- This is the same Friends & Family event as 2026-09-17 and the 2026-09-20 "Friendly reminder" — three sends of the identical 25%/20% offer in under two weeks is fatigue-inducing, not urgency-building.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA in the hero — a soft trust hit, flagging fine print before the click.
- No preview/preheader text visible complementing the subject — inbox likely shows junk or nothing, wasting valuable real estate.
- Product grid has zero pricing or "% off applied" badges — shopper has to click through to see the actual discounted price.
- Subject line is generic "select styles" language identical in structure to prior sends — no new angle to justify a third open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical event a third time — if another touch is truly needed, change the angle entirely (e.g., lead with "48 hours left" urgency or spotlight one category like handbags) rather than reusing the same green hero and copy.
- 2. Add real preview text that surfaces something not in the subject — e.g., a specific category or the points multiplier — since none is visible here.
- 3. Overlay "25% off" price call-outs on 2-3 grid items to reduce a click-to-discover step.
- 4. Move "Exclusions Apply" smaller/lower, further from the primary CTA, so it doesn't undercut the offer at the moment of decision.
- 5. If sending again, segment: send the reminder only to non-openers of the 09-17 and 09-20 sends rather than blasting the full list again.
