# 25% off select regular-price styles ends soon
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 25%-off-regular-price mechanic Neiman Marcus already ran on 9/3 with "Surprise: Here's 25% off select regular-price styles from Canali, ALICE + OLIVIA & more" (6.00/10), just re-served three days later with an urgency wrapper ("ends soon") instead of the "surprise" framing. The hero is clean and the offer is unambiguous — big "25% OFF," bold "Sale" wordmark, one clear SHOP NOW button above the fold — and the nine-item grid spans women's dresses, denim, a menswear shirt (Canali), giving genuine cross-category breadth.
- The problem is cadence and content overlap: Canali also appeared as a promoted brand in the 9/3 send, and this is now the third discount-mechanic email in a week hitting "25% off regular price" language, following the same brand's Last Call "extra 25% off sale styles" on 9/4. A subscriber who opened 9/3's email will see this as a near-duplicate with a deadline slapped on top rather than a new reason to click.
- Solid, functional promo email, but it's derivative of its own recent sends and the subject leans on manufactured urgency without a visible date. Middle-of-the-pack score.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "25% OFF" in large type, "select regular-price styles" qualifier directly beneath it, single black SHOP NOW button.
- "Limited Time" and "Exclusions Apply" are visible in the hero itself, not buried only in fine print.
- Nine-product grid is genuinely diverse — evening gowns, florals, denim, a menswear shirt (Canali) — giving multiple entry points per shopper.
- Two clear secondary CTAs ("Shop Women's Clothing," "Shop Men's Clothing") let a men's shopper skip the women's-heavy grid.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout.

## What's weak

- No specific end date visible anywhere in the rendered creative — "ends soon" in the subject isn't backed by a countdown or date in the body; the actual deadline (9/7/26 10PM CT) is only in the tiny legal fine print at the bottom.
- This is the third near-identical "25% off regular price" discount beat from this brand in under a week (9/3, plus Last Call's extra-25%-off on 9/4) — no new products or angle to justify a repeat send.
- Canali reappears from the 9/3 grid, reinforcing the sense of recycled creative rather than a fresh merchandising pass.
- No loyalty/member pricing or InCircle callout, despite Neiman Marcus having a well-known loyalty program.
- The grid has zero pricing — not even a "from $X" or percent-off badge on individual items — so the shopper can't gauge value before clicking through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real countdown or explicit date/time in the hero (e.g., "Ends Sunday 9/7 at 10PM CT") instead of vague "Limited Time" — the urgency promised in the subject line isn't delivered visually until the fine print.
- 2. Rotate out repeated brands (Canali just ran 9/3) and vary the offer framing so this doesn't read as a rerun to anyone who opened the last one.
- 3. Add price or percent-off badges directly on product tiles — right now the grid is pure editorial with no value signal per item.
- 4. Surface an InCircle/loyalty line if applicable — "InCircle members earn double points" would differentiate this from the flat sitewide %-off pattern.
