# Don’t miss out on an extra 25% off Last Call styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-exact rerun of the 7/29 send ("Extra 25% off Last Call styles," 7.00/10) — same red hero block, same "EXTRA 25% OFF / UP TO 70% OFF / LAST CALL" stack, same "SHOP SALE" button, same 9-product grid (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Bugatchi, Stuart Weitzman, Theia, Manolo Blahnik, Cinq a Sept, AREA, Weekend Max Mara), same category-button block, same footer. The only change worth noting is the subject line, which bolts "Don't miss out on" onto the identical offer copy to manufacture urgency.
- The creative itself still works: the hero is unambiguous, the discount math is clear (25% off already-marked-down Last Call, up to 70% total), and the product grid is genuinely diverse across handbags, outerwear, shirting, dresses, and shoes with real designer names doing the credibility work. But sending the same sale event with the same grid three days after the 7/29 email, just with a more anxious subject line, reads as a brand squeezing a single promo for a second open rather than a fresh push — a subscriber who opened 7/29 has nothing new to see here.
- Judgement: solid execution, weak cadence. This would score well as a first touch; as a near-duplicate second touch inside 72 hours, it undercuts itself.

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable without scrolling: 25% off, up to 70% off, "LAST CALL," one white "SHOP SALE" button on red — zero ambiguity about the offer or the action.
- "Exclusions Apply" is disclosed right under the CTA rather than buried only in footer fine print.
- Product grid spans categories (bag, jacket, shirt, sandal, dress, mule, dress, top, blouse) with recognizable designer names, giving the sale real merchandising weight instead of a single stock photo.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no placeholder content.

## What's weak

- This is the same hero, same offer, same 9-SKU grid as the 7/29 "Extra 25% off Last Call styles" send — a subscriber who opened that one gets nothing new.
- No prices or discount amounts shown on individual product tiles — just designer name, so the shopper can't tell which items are the deep 70%-off ones without clicking through.
- No loyalty/InCircle member pricing or points messaging surfaced, despite Neiman Marcus having a loyalty program worth flagging.
- Subject line's added urgency ("Don't miss out on") isn't backed by a visible deadline in the subject or hero — the real end date (8/31/26) is buried in small footer text only.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't resend the identical creative within 72 hours.** If the goal is a second touch on the same sale, swap in a different hero product, a different subhead angle (e.g., lead with a specific category like shoes or dresses), or hold this send for shoppers who didn't open 7/29.
- 2. **Add a price or "was/now" callout to at least the top row of the grid** — right now the designer names carry the whole sell and shoppers can't gauge value without a click.
- 3. **Surface the 8/31 deadline in the hero**, not just the footer disclaimer — "Ends 8/31" under "SHOP SALE" would make the subject's urgency credible.
- 4. **Add an InCircle/loyalty line** if applicable — even a small "Members save more" would differentiate this from a generic promo and reward the CRM segment that's most likely to convert.
