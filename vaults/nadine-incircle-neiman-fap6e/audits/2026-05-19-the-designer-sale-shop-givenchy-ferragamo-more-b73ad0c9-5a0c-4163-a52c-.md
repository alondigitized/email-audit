# The Designer Sale: Shop Givenchy, Ferragamo & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a follow-on refresh to Monday's 10/10 Designer Sale launch email — nearly identical red hero, same grid format, same "UP TO 50% OFF" headline, same "SHOP SALE" button. The differentiation lives entirely in the subject line, which names Givenchy and Ferragamo to recapture non-openers from the launch send. That is a legitimate tactic and NM executes it cleanly.
- The problem: Ferragamo appears in the subject but not in the nine-brand product grid. Givenchy gets a slot, but a subscriber who clicked expecting Ferragamo — the brand that lured them — lands on a general sale page. That's a small but real bait-and-switch, and for a luxury brand it's a notable gap. It also doesn't add anything the launch email didn't already say: no new urgency, no deadline, no InCircle member tier — despite the InCircle recruitment email that landed in the same inbox just hours earlier today.
- The email is clean and functional. But compared to the 5/17 "access ends tonight" send (9/10) and the 5/18 launch (10/10), this third designer sale email in three days is running on fumes. It is doing maintenance work, not driving new desire.

## What's working

- Red hero lands the full offer in three lines — fast, no guessing required.
- Nine-brand grid earns the "& more" — ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges, Polo Ralph Lauren is a credible luxury roster.
- Gender-split CTA grid (Women's Clothing, Men's, Women's Shoes, Men's Shoes, Dresses) is smart merchandising — no dead-end for any segment.
- "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" is better scarcity copy than "limited time" alone.
- Zero render bugs — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Ferragamo is in the subject line and absent from the product grid — a promise not kept inside the email body.
- "LIMITED TIME" in the hero is vague; the 5/17 send had "access ends tonight," which was far more compelling. No deadline, no countdown.
- This is the third Designer Sale email in three days (5/17 early access, 5/18 launch, 5/19 here) — cadence is aggressive and the creative adds nothing new to justify the re-send.
- No InCircle callout — the email sent *earlier today* pitched InCircle membership; this send ignores it entirely, missing an obvious cross-thread hook ("Members see an extra 10%").
- The bottom CTA row reads "SHOP WOMEN'S | SHOP DRESSES" — "SHOP WOMEN'S" is orphaned without a category noun, inconsistent with the two rows above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Ferragamo in the grid.** If you name it in the subject, a Ferragamo product must appear in the email body. Swap one of the weaker fills (Cult Gaia) for a Ferragamo shoe or bag and fix the subject/body contract.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the hero.** Change "LIMITED TIME" to "Sale ends Sunday" or "3 days left." The 5/17 email proved deadline language works; the 5/18 launch could skip it (novelty carried it). This send needs the hook.
- 3. **Tie in InCircle.** Add a single line below the hero: "InCircle members: your extra 10% applies automatically." This links the two sends landing the same day and rewards the loyalty segment NM just pitched this morning.
- 4. **Fix "SHOP WOMEN'S" CTA** — give it a noun: "SHOP WOMEN'S BAGS" or "SHOP WOMEN'S READY-TO-WEAR."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on styles from 9 luxury brands before the sale ends.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus: "The Designer Sale" (2026-05-19)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) AMPscript with a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout. Two compliance-critical headers are missing from the relay-captured message, and a SHA256 hashing assignment for the `@user` variable appears to be commented out, with potential downstream effects on any personalized tracking URLs that depend on it.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links, zero probed**
All outbound links route through a click-redirect domain (likely `click.e.neimanmarcus.com` or equivalent SFMC redirect handler). QA skipped HTTP probing on all 26. No broken-link signal, but no confirmation of resolution either.

**`@user` SHA256 hash may be unset**
The AMPscript comment block contains:
```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```
This assignment is wrapped in an HTML comment (`<!-- -->`), which means AMPscript will not execute it. If downstream tracking URLs or retargeting pixels inject `%%=v(@user)=%%` expecting a SHA256-hashed email, those parameters will render as empty or the raw `@user` fallback — depending on whether the variable is assigned elsewhere in the non-truncated template.

**`@refid` construction visible in comment**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
This is a no-op concatenation — `@refid` equals `@base_refid` with no modifier appended. If campaign tracking differentiation was intended (e.g., `CONCAT(@base_refid,"_SALE")`), this is a bug. If it is intentional, the trailing `""` is dead code.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Link color forced to black**
```css
.appleLinks a { color: #000000 !important; text-decoration: none !important; }
```
Applied to suppress iOS auto-detection styling, but this removes all visual affordance for links in Apple Mail on mobile — links are indistinguishable from body text for sighted users.

**Overlapping breakpoints across two `<style>` blocks**
A second `<style>` block defines breakpoints at `max-width: 480px` and `max-width: 520px` for `.ImgSize` and `.ImgSize2x2`. The first block uses `max-width: 600px`. These are in separate stylesheet blocks, which can cause specificity conflicts in Gmail's CSS inliner — the second block may be ignored or stripped.

**Three commented-out viewport meta tags left in `<head>`**
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Dead code inflating payload. No functional impact, but adds ~200 bytes and signals stale template hygiene.

**`<title>` has trailing whitespace**
```html
<title>Neiman Marcus </title>
```
Minor. Renders as "Neiman Marcus " in browser tab if previewed via webmail.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript variable block exposed in HTML comment**
The full variable declaration block — including lookup table names, field names, and variable logic — is present in an HTML comment at the top of the `<body>`:
```html
<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) ...-->
```
SFMC strips AMPscript before send, but HTML comments are preserved in the delivered message. Recipients who view source can see internal data model names (`NM_CRM_Feed`, `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, field names `uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`). This is an information-disclosure issue at the ESP template layer.

**`@emailid` has dual-path comment but no active fallback guard visible**
The comment says "from sendable DE if available, otherwise Lookup to Subscriber_New." Whether a null-guard (`IIF(EMPTY(@emailid), ...)`) is implemented in the non-truncated template cannot be confirmed from the truncated source, but it should be verified.

`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are required for Gmail and Apple Mail to surface the native unsubscribe UI. Their absence increases spam complaint rate and risks bulk-sender policy violations under Google's 2024 sender requirements. This may be a relay-capture gap rather than a true header absence — confirm against raw SMTP headers at the MTA, not the AgentMail relay view.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Plain-text UTM saturation**
QA reports 87% of plain-text body characters are URLs (5,140 of 5,910 chars). This indicates the plain-text alternative was auto-generated from HTML without editorial reduction — it is essentially a dump of redirect URLs with minimal human-readable content. Major spam filters (SpamAssassin, Google Postmaster) penalize this ratio. It also provides zero value to plain-text readers.

**UTM parameters on outbound links**
Cannot verify UTM presence on the 26 tracked links without probing the redirect chain — all were skipped. Should be confirmed that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` survive the SFMC redirect and are not stripped by the click-handler before the destination URL.
