# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I picked up my phone, typed in neimanmarcus.com, and instead of the homepage I got a wall of text telling me I've been flagged as a bot. This is not a website — this is a bouncer telling me I'm not welcome. For a brand that built its entire identity on white-glove service, showing an InCircle member an access-restriction error page is a spectacular own goal.

## What's working

- The Neiman Marcus logo is recognizable and correctly rendered at top and bottom of the block card — brand identity is intact at least.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present, so there is technically a path to flag the problem.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is replaced by a bot-detection block — I see zero product, zero editorial, zero offers.
- The error message is clinical IT copy ("IP 75.140.9.0," "automated bot activity") — completely at odds with the NM brand voice. My stylist does not talk to me like a network administrator.
- The list of reasons I was blocked ("JavaScript disabled," "developer tools") reads like an accusation. I'm a 51-year-old InCircle member on my phone — not a script kiddie.
- No phone number, no store locator, no fallback — just "Submit feedback" buried in fine print. If I'm locked out mid-shopping trip I have no graceful exit.
- The background is barely visible — a pair of legs and a dark green texture bleed through — which makes the page look broken and uncurated rather than intentional.

## Recommendations

- **Soften the block page immediately** — replace the IP-address accusation copy with something like "We need a moment to verify your connection — please try again" and surface the 1-800 NM number prominently.
- **Add a loyalty bypass or CAPTCHA** — InCircle members should be able to authenticate with their NM account to bypass bot detection in one tap; treating loyal high-AOV customers as bots is a revenue leak.
- **Show a fallback editorial tile** behind the verification modal — the leaked background image proves assets are loading; use that moment to at least show the current campaign so I don't leave with nothing.
- **Instrument this error in your analytics funnel** — if this is happening to real customers on mobile, every one of those sessions is lost GMV from your highest-LTV segment.
