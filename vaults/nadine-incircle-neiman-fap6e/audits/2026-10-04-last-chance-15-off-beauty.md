# Last chance: 15% off beauty
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer beauty promo. It is almost the same send as the Oct 2 "15% off fragrance, beauty & more," with "Last chance" added to the subject. The pink hero leads with "15% OFF BEAUTY" in large serif type, followed by one black SHOP NOW button. Below it is a tidy 3x3 grid of brand-labeled tiles: Givenchy, La Mer, Dior, Guerlain, Chantecaille and others. The hierarchy is easy to read and nothing is broken.
- The subject promises urgency that the body doesn't deliver. The hero says "for a limited time," which is vague, and the real deadline (10/4/26, 10:00 PM CT) only appears in the fine print at the bottom. "Last chance" in the inbox followed by "limited time" in the hero weakens the claim. A second offer, a blue free-shipping block with code SHIPNM, competes with the beauty message near the bottom. It's a competent but repeat send. Anyone who saw the Oct 2 email has no new reason to act.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a big "15% OFF BEAUTY" with one black SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands on it first.
- The 3x3 grid of recognizable prestige brands (La Mer, Dior, Guerlain, Chantecaille) signals quality and gives several entry points.
- The category buttons (Shop Beauty, Fragrance, Makeup, Skincare) are specific and let a shopper go straight to their category.
- "Discount auto-applied at checkout" removes the need to remember a code.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images and no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The real deadline is buried in the footer, while the hero only says "for a limited time." That undercuts "Last chance."
- The product tiles show brand names only, with no product names or prices, so there is no sense of what 15% off actually means.
- The free-shipping block with a separate SHIPNM code and a second SHOP NOW competes with the main offer. The shipping terms are dense fine print.
- The top navigation (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) doesn't include Beauty, which is the point of the email.
- The monospace body type in the hero looks thin and typewriter-like next to the serif headline. "Exclusions apply" is tiny and low-contrast on the pink.
- The offer is essentially the same as Oct 2, and the Oct 3 sends were a $650 tiered promo and Chloé editorial, so this reads as recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tonight, 10 PM CT." This is the single change that would lift the email most, because it makes "Last chance" true on sight.
- 2. Replace brand-only tiles with hero products that show name and price, plus a "was/now" or "you save" line. Lead with the best sellers.
- 3. Drop the free-shipping block, or fold it into one line such as "Free shipping $150+ with SHIPNM." Don't give it a second large button.
- 4. Change the hero CTA from SHOP NOW to "Shop 15% off beauty."
- 5. Add Beauty to the top navigation.
- 6. Show any InCircle benefit (the fine print mentions free shipping with no minimum) in a visible line rather than the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 15% off beauty & fragrance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: 15% off La Mer, Dior & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Auto-applied at checkout. Ends 10 PM CT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skincare, makeup & scents from Dior, La Mer, Guerlain and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has no broken-link or unresolved-token evidence. It does have three confirmed source-level defects: a leaked ESP template comment, a viewport that blocks zoom, and a missing `lang` attribute. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay likely stripped the headers, not because of anything in the send.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped** by the QA probe (`[WARN] 29 tracking link(s) skipped`). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 5140 of 6849 characters (about 75%) are URLs. This usually means long tracked URLs are printed in the text alternative. It raises spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.
- Link targets and parameters beyond the truncation point could not be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` (or `xml:lang`), so screen readers can't pick the right language.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE:** the ESP comment block sits ahead of `<!DOCTYPE ...>`. Some clients and parsers treat anything before the DOCTYPE as a quirks-mode trigger.
- **Mobile selectors are exact-match:** the `@media` rules use `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=mobile]`, `*[class=hide]` and similar. Exact-match attribute selectors fail if an element carries more than one class, so those mobile rules would not apply to it.
- **Hamburger nav relies on a checkbox hack:** the `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` rules need `:checked` support. Gmail and several Outlook builds don't support it. I couldn't confirm a fallback because the HTML is truncated.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and no campaign context.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template comment leaked into the delivered HTML.** It exposes internal data-extension names and lookup logic:
  - `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)`
  - `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...)` and `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...)`
  - The text "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` are also in the output.
  - This is an authoring note that wasn't stripped. It reveals internal schema names, and the same pattern could leak real values elsewhere. The parentheses in the comment are also unbalanced (`@user Lookup(...))`).
- No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appear in the visible portion. Anything after the truncation point is unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** (`[WARN]`). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this isn't confirmed as missing from the send.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (`[WARN]`), with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`, so check authentication on the original message.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated part of the HTML and were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 29 tracked links were not resolved, and the visible HTML contains no destination URLs or UTM parameters. The `@refid` / `@base_refid` variables in the leaked comment suggest campaign tracking by refid, but that is not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the authoring comment block from the production template, and move it above or out of the send. It leaks internal DE names and sits before the DOCTYPE.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`.
4. Change the mobile selectors to `[class~=…]` or `[class*=…]`, or use single-class elements.
5. Confirm a no-`:checked` fallback for the nav in Gmail and Outlook.
6. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or use a custom click-tracking domain, to lower the URL ratio.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw original message, not the relay copy.
8. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and tracking-link resolution so UTM and landing-page continuity can be checked.
