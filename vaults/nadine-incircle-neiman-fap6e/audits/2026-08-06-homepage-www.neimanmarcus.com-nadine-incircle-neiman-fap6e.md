# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a bot-block page, not the actual homepage — no product content to review honestly.
- I tapped over expecting the Neiman's I know, and instead got a stark white box telling me my "device or network" looks suspicious. No dresses, no editorial, nothing — just a logo, an error message, and a mysterious ID number. If I didn't know better I'd think the site was down.

## What's working

- The Neiman Marcus script logo at the top is at least legible and recognizably theirs, so I know I didn't land on a phishing page.
- The tone of the error message is polite rather than alarming ("temporarily restricted" instead of "banned"), which keeps it from feeling hostile.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage experience is replaced by a bot-detection wall — "We detected unusual activity from your device or network" with a bulleted list about rapid taps, JavaScript, bot activity, and dev tools. None of that applies to me, I just opened the app on my phone.
- There's a raw IP address (47.229.200.33) and a long tracking ID displayed to me — that's engineer-facing debug info, not something a customer should ever see.
- No path forward besides "Submit feedback" — no retry button, no link back to the homepage, nothing.
- A stray pair of feet in white-and-black slingback heels is cropped awkwardly at the very top and bottom of the frame, like a template placeholder that never got its content.

## Recommendations

- Give the block page an actual retry/refresh button so a real shopper isn't stuck reading a bulleted list about bot detection.
- Strip the raw IP address and UUID from the customer-facing view — keep that in logs, not on screen.
- Soften the copy for legitimate mobile users — "rapid taps," "dev tools," and "JavaScript disabled" all read like I'm being accused of something.
- Fix whatever is triggering the false-positive bot flag for normal mobile browsing before this costs real Last Call traffic.
