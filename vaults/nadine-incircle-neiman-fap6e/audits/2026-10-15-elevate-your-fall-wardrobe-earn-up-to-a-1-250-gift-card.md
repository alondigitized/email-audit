# Elevate your fall wardrobe & earn up to a $1,250 gift card
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same gift-card-with-purchase offer as Oct 13's "Treat yourself & earn a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase". The creative is nearly identical: a plum hero, a spend-and-earn ladder and a "SHOP NOW" button. Only the subject and a new nine-product designer grid are different. The execution is clean and the offer is the first thing the eye lands on. The ladder is easy to scan.
- The nine-product grid (Mother, Moncler, L'AGENCE, Zimmermann, Louboutin, Jimmy Choo, Valentino Garavani, Burberry, Manolo Blahnik) is the main improvement over the Oct 13 send. It gives the recipient things to click and shows the brand's luxury positioning. It is still an undifferentiated catalog, though. There are no prices, no "gift card with this" tie-in and no reason to pick one item over another. The six category buttons below it are generic.
- For a subscriber, this is the second send of the same offer in three days. It follows the Oct 14 "First Call" markdown and "Shearling" editorial sends, and the earlier NMCIRCLE run. The send is competent but repetitive. The real value is the $1,250 ceiling, and that is hidden behind a $5,000 spend.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and the offer is clear: "EARN A $100–$1,250 GIFT CARD" in large white type on plum, with a high-contrast cream "SHOP NOW" button.
- The spend/earn ladder ($500→$100 up to $5,000+→$1,250) makes the mechanic understandable at a glance.
- The code "OURGIFT" and "Limited Time" sit right in the hero.
- The nine-product grid adds real merchandise, with consistent photography and bold designer labels.
- The deadline (10/20/26, 10:00 PM CT) and the exclusions are spelled out in the fine print.

## What's weak

- The hero has no visible deadline. "Limited Time" is vague, and the actual date is buried in the fine print at the bottom.
- The ladder shows the earn rate is about 20% at $500 and 25% at $5,000. The email never says so, which is a missed persuasion point.
- The product grid has no prices, no "earn with this" cue and no product-level links implied. It reads as a brand parade.
- The six category buttons are generic and use the same plum as the hero, so nothing signals a priority.
- The legal block is a long wall of small text. "Exclusions Apply" is tiny in the hero and "Sale and reduced-price merchandise do not qualify" is only in the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 10/20 · Code OURGIFT". It is the cheapest way to add credible urgency and differentiate this send from Oct 13.
- 2. Make the ladder a pitch. Add "Spend $500, get $100 back" as a worked example or show the effective % back.
- 3. Tie products to the offer. Add prices and a "counts toward your gift card" line under a few items.
- 4. Reduce the nine-brand parade to a hero product pair, or segment the grid by browsing history.
- 5. Move the key exclusions (sale items excluded) to a single line below the ladder to keep the offer honest.
- 6. Space out repeat sends of the same offer, or change the creative angle so subscribers do not see the same hero twice in three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $500, get a $100 gift card back`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gift card with purchase ends 10/20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to $1,250 back on fall favorites from Moncler, Burberry & more. Code OURGIFT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty & fragrance count too. Ends Oct 20 at 10 PM CT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The source is truncated, so the footer, body links and any unsubscribe markup could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 30 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- No broken or malformed links are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom in clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Checkbox-hack nav:** The mobile menu relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.hamburger`/`.close` toggles. Gmail and many Outlook builds don't support this, and the truncated source doesn't show a fallback. The hamburger-nav CSS, including the `top: 77px` hard-coded menu offset, is more fragile than a plain stacked nav.
- **Non-standard selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors. They fail if an element ever carries more than one class. The DTD URL is also `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/...` rather than the canonical `http://` form. Both are minor.
- **Alt text and image sizing:** Not assessable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template comment left in the live HTML:** The top of the document contains an unstripped SFMC/AMPscript instruction comment: `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", ...) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`.
  - It exposes internal data extension names and field names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) to every recipient.
  - It also shows an unresolved authoring prompt ("CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW") and a leftover `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It sits before the DOCTYPE, which can push some clients into quirks mode.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `@var` tokens appear in the visible rendered markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) can't be verified, for the same reason. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com`, a subdomain, so alignment needs checking on the raw message.
- **Not auditable:** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not auditable. The 30 tracking links weren't resolved, so UTM parameters, `refid` handling and landing-page alignment with the "$1,250 gift card" offer can't be confirmed. The comment block does reference a `@refid` built from `@base_refid`, but that only shows the intent to tag links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript instruction comment, and keep it out of the delivered HTML. Do this in the SFMC template or a pre-send step.
2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add a fallback for clients that don't support the checkbox-hack nav, such as a visible stacked link list.
5. Re-run the header checks against the raw message, not the AgentMail relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com`).
6. Resolve the tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe. Check final status codes and UTM/`refid` parameters, and re-audit the full, untruncated HTML for the footer and unsubscribe link.
