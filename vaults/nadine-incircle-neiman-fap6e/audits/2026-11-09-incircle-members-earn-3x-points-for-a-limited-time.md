# InCircle Members: Earn 3x Points for a limited time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident loyalty-multiplier send. A black hero with serif type says "Limited Time / EARN TRIPLE INCIRCLE POINTS on your designer purchase," with a white SHOP NOW button. The headline carries the whole message, and the eye lands on it first. The email feels like Neiman Marcus and shows no render bugs.
- The offer is a points multiplier, not a price cut, so it only appeals to someone who already thinks in InCircle terms. The hero never names the dates (11/8–11/14) or the points-to-dollars value. The exclusions line ("Excludes beauty, fragrance & jewelry") is small and grey on black. The body is a 3x3 grid of designer products with brand names but no prices, and no individual tile appears to link to a specific product. Compared with last week's "Limited time: up to $650 off" (11/08, 7/10) and the "Earn a $50–$500 gift card" send (11/07), this one is softer and less concrete.
- Verdict: well executed, but a mid-strength send. It would work for loyal cardholders and be forgettable for everyone else.

## What's working

- The hero headline is large and has strong contrast, so the offer is understood in about two seconds.
- "Limited Time" sits above the headline, which gives urgency without shouting.
- The product grid is tidy and consistent, with a mix of apparel, shoes and accessories.
- The six category buttons (women's, men's, shoes, handbags, accessories) give clear next steps for different shoppers.
- Nothing is broken in the render. Spacing is clean and the nav bar is clear.

## What's weak

- The deadline is not in the hero. The 11/14 end date appears only in the fine print at the bottom.
- "Triple points" is never turned into a value. There is no "that's 45 points per dollar" or "$X back," so the reward feels abstract.
- The exclusions line is low-contrast and sits next to the CTA, which feels slightly buried.
- The product tiles show no prices or reviews, and the brand names are the only copy.
- Most of the bottom third is dense legal text. The offer is also not tied to InCircle tiers, though the fine print mentions President's and Chairman's Circle.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date and a concrete value in the hero, for example "Ends 11/14 · 3x points on designer." Then show one worked example such as "$1,000 spend = 3,000 points."
- 2. Make the exclusions legible by raising their contrast, and keep them directly under the CTA.
- 3. Link each tile to a specific product or brand page, and add price points.
- 4. Add a tier-aware line for members ("You're a Circle member: your points apply automatically").
- 5. Move the app and stylist modules above the legal block, and trim the legal text.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a checkbox-hack mobile nav, and the HTML is truncated, so body links, images and the footer weren't available to inspect. Confirmed issues are an internal AMPscript/SFMC variable comment left in the sent HTML and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The four QA warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed as defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA skipped the HTTP probe for 31 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and expiry are **unverified**.
- Plain text is 5893 of 8146 chars URLs (about 72%, over the 70% threshold). This is consistent with long tracked URLs and can raise spam-filter scoring.
- The truncated source doesn't show the hrefs, so I can't confirm whether `@refid`, `@user` or `@emailid` are appended to links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) on iOS and Android.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE:** the HTML comment block precedes `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`. In older IE and Outlook-on-Word engines this can trigger quirks mode. Low severity.
- **Hamburger nav:** it uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` inside `@media (max-width: 600px)`. Gmail, Outlook and some Android clients don't support `:checked` or `~`, and the nav falls back to `display:none` on `.menu`. I can't confirm a fallback link set from the truncated source.
- **Class selectors:** `*[class=contenttable]` and similar are exact-match attribute selectors. They fail if an element carries multiple classes.
- **Alt text and `role="presentation"`:** unverified, as the body is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens are visible in the rendered portion. The only token references are in the leaked comment (see section 5), so the body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Internal template comment shipped to recipients:** the source begins with `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`. These comments expose internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`) and the hashing scheme. This is an information-disclosure and hygiene issue, and it also adds payload weight.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so the finding is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a subdomain, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- This can't be assessed. The tracking links are wrapped in click-redirect domains and weren't probed. No UTM parameters or landing URLs appear in the visible source.
- The template does reference `@base_refid` and `@refid` for campaign attribution, which suggests a ref-ID scheme rather than UTMs. Whether it is applied to links is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the `<!--REQUIRED variables ... -->` and `<!--/* SET @user ... */-->` comments at send time, or move them to SFMC-side comments (`%%[ /* */ ]%%`) so they aren't emitted.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line, ahead of any comments.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw MTA output, since the relay may strip them. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment at the sending-platform level too.
5. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Then check link hrefs, footer compliance, alt text, `@refid` propagation and the mobile-nav fallback.
6. Run a click-through test against a sample of the 31 redirect links to verify destinations and landing-page parity.
