# Friendly reminder: 25% off + 20% off jewelry
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Friends & Family event Neiman Marcus already sent on 2026-09-17 ("25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry starts now"), now reissued as a "Friendly reminder" with a hard deadline (ends 9/27/26 10:00PM CT). It's a genuine urgency send, not a rehash of vague campaign language — the countdown is real and the offer is unchanged and clearly stated. Visually it's clean: a strong green hero, bold "25% OFF" typography, and a nine-item product grid spanning dresses, denim, handbags, shoes, and menswear.
- The problem is redundancy fatigue, not execution. A subscriber who opened the 9/17 send already has this offer; this is the fourth Neiman Marcus email in this log's short window (9/17 event, 9/17 thin "Fresh takes on animal print," 9/18 gift card, 9/18 Chanel editorial), and this is a straight repeat of Sunday's creative with a reminder wrapper. Execution is solid, timing logic (reminder before deadline) is sound, but there's no new offer, no new product story, and cadence is starting to feel heavy.

## What's working

- Clear, single, unambiguous offer stated twice (hero + fine print): 25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry.
- Real deadline with exact date/time ("Ends 9/27/26 at 10:00PM CT") — credible urgency, not vague "limited time."
- InCircle Double Points callout adds a loyalty-member incentive on top of the discount.
- Nine-item product grid gives concrete shoppable variety (dresses, denim, handbags, shoes, menswear) rather than a single hero image.
- Clean visual hierarchy — eye lands on "FRIENDS & FAMILY / 25% OFF" immediately, CTA button is prominent and high-contrast.

## What's weak

- Subject leads with "Friendly reminder," which is inherently a lower-urgency, lower-curiosity opener than the original "starts now" send.
- This is a visual repeat of the 9/17 Friends & Family creative — same hero treatment, same layout — so a subscriber who opened that one gets zero new stimulus.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA in small type — a mild honesty flag, though not buried.
- No countdown timer or visual urgency device (e.g., "3 days left") in the hero itself — the urgency lives only in subject line and fine print.
- Coming on the heels of three other Neiman Marcus sends in under a week, this reminder reinforces a sense of over-mailing rather than standing out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the deadline visually present in the hero, not just the subject/fine print — a "3 DAYS LEFT" badge over the green banner would convert the reminder framing into visible urgency.
- 2. Swap "Friendly reminder" for something with more pull — reminders under-perform first announcements on open rate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 25% off + 20% off jewelry ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `48 hours left: Friends & Family savings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Event ends 9/27 at 10PM CT — plus InCircle members earn Double Points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Don't miss 25% off select styles before it's gone.`
- 3. Differentiate the product grid from the 9/17 send — rotate in different SKUs or highlight what's about to sell out, so repeat openers see something new.
- 4. Move "Exclusions Apply" or clarify it lightly (e.g., link to full exclusions) so it doesn't read as a hedge directly beneath the CTA.
- 5. Consider suppressing this send for anyone who already clicked the 9/17 email — a reminder should target non-openers/non-clickers, not blast the full list again.
