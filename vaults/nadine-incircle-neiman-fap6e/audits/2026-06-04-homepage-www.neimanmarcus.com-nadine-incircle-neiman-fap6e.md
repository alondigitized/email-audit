# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- I opened Neiman Marcus's website and was immediately locked out — not even a homepage, just a bot-block screen telling me my access is "temporarily restricted." This is not a shopping experience; it's a wall. For a brand that prides itself on white-glove service, greeting a loyal InCircle member with a CAPTCHA-adjacent error message is genuinely embarrassing.

## What's working

- The Neiman Marcus wordmark logo renders correctly — I at least know I'm at the right brand.
- The "Submit feedback" link is functional and gives me a theoretical way to report the problem, which is marginally better than a blank white screen.

## What's weak

- **The block page itself** — I see "Access is temporarily restricted" and a bot-detection explanation. I'm a 51-year-old woman browsing on my phone; I am not a bot.
- **No bypass or fallback** — there is no "click here to verify you're human," no CAPTCHA, no alternative path. I'm just stuck.
- **IP address exposed in error copy** — the message cites my IP (75.140.9.0) in plain text, which feels invasive and clinical for a luxury brand.
- **No grace messaging** — the tone is clinical and cold: "Rapid taps or clicks," "Automated (bot) activity." This is the language of a firewall log, not a luxury retailer.
- **Zero shopping content** — categories, editorial, new arrivals, promotions — all of it is invisible behind this wall.

## Recommendations

- **Add a soft CAPTCHA or human-verification step** instead of a hard block — let me prove I'm a real customer and continue shopping without calling customer service.
- **Strip the IP address from the end-user error message** — that detail belongs in a server log, not in my face on a phone screen. It reads as surveillance.
- **Replace the clinical bullet list with brand-appropriate language** — even the error state should sound like Neiman Marcus, not a server admin. Something like "We're having trouble verifying your connection — please try again or call our team at 1-888-888-4757."
- **Provide a direct phone or chat escape hatch** prominently on the error page — InCircle members expect a concierge path; a dead end is not acceptable.
