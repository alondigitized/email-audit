# Shop new beauty & earn a FREE gift
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- This is NM's June 17 beauty-category standalone — a one-day event ("THE BEAUTY EVENT / JUNE 17 ONLY") anchored by a free gift offer and Triple InCircle Points. After a week that cycled through spend-to-save, editorial arrivals, single-designer editorial (Chloé), and the strong June 15 triple-points-plus-$200-off send (9/10), pivoting to a beauty day-of-event format is sensible sequencing. It doesn't feel like inbox fatigue from the same creative.
- The architecture is clean: hero → loyalty callout → brand product grid → six category CTAs. But the central offer — the free gift — is never made specific at hero scale. What is the gift? What's the spend threshold? That ambiguity bleeds impact from the strongest hook in the subject line. The hero image (cosmetic pouches in pink and green) is lifestyle-adjacent but doesn't spotlight an actual product from the event, which is a missed sell.
- The six category buttons at the bottom (SHOP BEAUTY, SHOP FRAGRANCE, etc.) work as segmentation but feel like website navigation more than a closing persuasion moment. Overall: competent one-day category event, but the free gift is the lead and it's buried.

## What's working

- **One-day urgency is prominent and credible** — "JUNE 17 ONLY" in the hero reads immediately and doesn't require fine print to understand.
- **InCircle Triple Points callout** is surfaced early, which rewards loyalists and layers an additional incentive on top of the free gift.
- **Product grid brand depth** is good — Versace, La Mer, Tom Ford, Clé de Peau Absolue, Parfums de Marly signals premium positioning without feeling scattered.
- **No render bugs** — images load, layout holds, typography is clean throughout.
- **Six segmented category CTAs** give different-intent shoppers a direct path (skin care vs. fragrance vs. makeup).

## What's weak

- **Free gift is opaque** — neither the threshold nor the actual gift is visible in the hero; subscribers have to hunt for the terms.
- **Hero image undersells the event** — cosmetic bags/pouches are props, not products. A featured La Mer or Tom Ford shot would anchor the category better.
- **Triple Points fatigue risk** — this same mechanic ran in the June 15 send (the 9/10 email); running it again two days later for a different vertical softens the "limited time" credibility.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a weak primary CTA** — for a beauty event with named brands and a free gift, something like "Claim Your Gift" or "Shop the Beauty Event" would outperform.
- **No social proof or bestseller signal** — a "top-rated" or "most wished for" callout on even one product in the grid would lift click intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the free gift concrete in the hero.** Spell out the offer: "Spend $150+ and choose your free gift." The free gift is the headline hook and it's invisible at the moment of decision.
- 2. **Swap the hero image to a featured product.** A single aspirational La Mer moisturizer or Tom Ford fragrance shot would be more motivating than generic cosmetic bags.
- 3. **Upgrade the primary CTA copy.** "Claim Your Free Gift" beats "SHOP NOW" — it anchors the action to the offer.
- 4. **Move Triple Points into the hero block.** Right now it's below the fold in a smaller callout. For InCircle members it's a compelling stacking mechanic — give it hero-level visibility.
- 5. **Add one urgency signal to the subject line.** The body says June 17 only; the subject doesn't. A subscriber scanning at 7am who doesn't open immediately has no reminder the clock is ticking.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Beauty event ends tonight — free gift with purchase`
- **Subject Alt B:** `June 17 only: free gift + triple points in beauty`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop La Mer, Tom Ford, Clé de Peau — one day, one free gift`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your free gift is waiting. InCircle members earn triple points today only.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Shop new beauty & earn a FREE gift"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The send platform is Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript-based). Three confirmed warnings from QA indicate unverifiable unsubscribe headers and authentication results, likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay layer. One high-severity AMPscript variable conflict is visible in the HTML source: the SHA256 hash assignment is commented out in production code, creating a probable data-type mismatch in personalized tracking parameters.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links unverified**
All click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA: `[WARN] 26 tracking link(s) skipped`). Link health — including redirect chain integrity and 4xx/5xx errors — is unknown. Destination URLs could not be confirmed.

**`@refid` empty concatenation (AMPscript)**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string literal is a no-op. This pattern typically signals an unfinished template where a campaign suffix (e.g., a variant ID or source code) was intended but never populated. If `@refid` is appended to tracking URLs, all links will carry an incomplete reference ID.

**CRM lookup parameters carry silent-fail risk**
`@uEm` and `@cmdid` are populated via `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", ...)`. If the subscriber row is absent from `NM_CRM_Feed`, both variables resolve to empty string with no error — producing tracking URLs with blank parameter values and no way to detect the miss post-send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">
```
Disabling pinch-to-zoom fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Low-vision recipients cannot magnify content.

**Dead commented-out viewport tags**
Two additional viewport meta variants are present as HTML comments — development artifacts that add payload without function:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```

**Interactive hamburger nav — limited client support**
The CSS-only nav uses `:checked` pseudo-selector state toggling. This renders correctly only in Apple Mail. Gmail, Outlook (all versions), and Yahoo discard this interaction entirely — affected subscribers see no functional navigation in the majority of clients.

**iOS 9 `@-webkit-keyframes bugfix`**
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
This targets a no-op animation workaround for iOS 9 (released 2015, EOL 2017). Dead weight in every modern client.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` SHA256 assignment is commented out — high severity**

The comment block declares the intended assignment:
```
/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */
```
This line is inside a block comment and **does not execute**. The active assignment instead binds `@user` to `EMAIL_ID` via lookup:
```
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
`EMAIL_ID` is an integer surrogate key. If any tracking URL or pixel uses `@user` expecting a SHA-256 hex string (40 characters, alphanumeric), it will receive a short integer instead — corrupting identity resolution on the receiving analytics platform and potentially breaking audience matching for any downstream ad or CDP integration.

**Manual scenario selection is error-prone**
```
<!-- CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
```
This comment implies a human operator selects among template variants at send time. No conditional AMPscript logic enforces the selection — a misconfigured send would use the wrong scenario silently.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header unconfirmed**
QA: `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found`. CAN-SPAM and RFC 2369 require this header on commercial email. The AgentMail relay may have stripped or not forwarded it; it should be verified at the SMTP envelope level, not inferred from the captured headers.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**
QA: `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)`. Google's February 2024 bulk sender requirements mandate one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day to Gmail. Absence risks inbox demotion or bulk-folder placement for Gmail recipients.

**SPF/DKIM status unverifiable**
QA: `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found`. The relay did not preserve or surface authentication results. SPF and DKIM pass/fail status for `e.neimanmarcus.com` cannot be confirmed from available data.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot assess UTM parameter completeness or landing page alignment — all 26 tracking links were skipped by the probe. The following risks are present based on source inspection alone:

- If `@refid` is used as a campaign source token in landing-page URLs, the empty-concat issue (§2) means analytics will receive an incomplete campaign identifier.
- Blank `@uEm` / `@cmdid` on lookup miss will produce URLs like `...&uEm=&cmdid=` — GA4 / CDP will record these as legitimate empty-value sessions, polluting attribution data.
