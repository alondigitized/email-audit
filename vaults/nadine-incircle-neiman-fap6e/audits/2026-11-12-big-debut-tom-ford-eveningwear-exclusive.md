## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a checkbox-hack mobile nav. It carries leftover Marketing Cloud template documentation in an HTML comment, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The automated checks flagged missing header data (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) that is probably a relay-capture artifact, plus a URL-heavy plain-text part. The HTML source was truncated, so body links, images and the footer could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **17 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 3169 of 3661 characters (about 87%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter and readability risk for the text/plain alternative. The likely cause is long tracking URLs rendered in full instead of short link labels.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **The mobile nav depends on the checkbox hack.** It uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` and `.hamburger`/`.close`, and the whole block sits inside a `max-width: 600px` media query. This is unsupported or unreliable in Gmail and several Outlook clients, and a bare checkbox toggle is not reliably keyboard- or screen-reader-accessible. No fallback markup was visible in the truncated source.
- **Template comments precede the DOCTYPE.** The `<!--REQUIRED variables...-->` block comes before `<!DOCTYPE>`. A comment before the doctype can push older IE-engine clients into quirks mode.
- **Class selectors are exact-match.** `*[class=contenttable]`, `*[class=hide]`, `*[class=mobile]` and similar only match when the element's class attribute is exactly that string. They fail silently if any element carries a second class.
- **The title is generic.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject.
- Image alt text, lang attributes and table roles could not be assessed because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template notes shipped in production HTML.** The leading comment exposes the data-extension and field names `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, `EMAIL_ID`, `uEm` and `cmdid`. It also contains malformed pseudo-code: unbalanced `))` in the `@user` and `@emailid` lookups, and `@user Lookup(...)` with no `=`. It is only a comment, but it is a minor information-disclosure issue and a sign the template was not cleaned before send.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `@var`) were visible in rendered content within the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found.** QA reported this as WARN, with the note that the AgentMail relay may not capture the header. It is unconfirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The same capture caveat applies. It is required for one-click unsubscribe under Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify against the raw message from the sending platform.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but alignment cannot be confirmed here.
- The visible-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated footer and were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not evaluable. No destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page results were available. The 17 tracking links were skipped, so any UTM or refid parameters (the template references `@refid` and `@base_refid`) cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit on the raw `.eml` or full HTML to check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results, the footer and alt text, since the QA warnings may come from the relay.
2. Strip the template-documentation comment, or at least move it below the DOCTYPE. It leaks internal data-extension names and shouldn't ship.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Replace the checkbox-hack nav with a static link row or a fallback for Gmail and Outlook, and make sure it is reachable without the toggle.
5. Use `[class~=...]` or plain class selectors instead of `[class=...]` exact matches.
6. Shorten the plain-text version by using link labels or short redirect URLs instead of full tracking URLs.
7. Resolve the click-tracking redirects in a follow-up check and confirm the final landing pages and UTM or refid parameters.
