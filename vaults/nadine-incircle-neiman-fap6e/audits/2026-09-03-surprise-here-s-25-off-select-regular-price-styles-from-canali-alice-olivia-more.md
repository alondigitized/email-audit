# Surprise: Here's 25% off select regular-price styles from Canali, ALICE + OLIVIA & more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a legit break from the gift-card churn — a real sitewide discount (25% off regular-price, plus a $250-off-with-code SHOPFALL banner further down) with a clean nine-item product grid spanning women's, men's, and shoes categories. Compared to 9/1's "Limited time left to get up to $250 off" and the six-day gift-card mechanic run, this at least has a different offer mechanic and no card-purchase upsell attached to it.
- But two discount mechanics are stacked in one email (25% off up top, $250-off-with-code below) and they're never reconciled — a shopper has no idea which one actually applies to the L'AGENCE jeans or the Canali shirt in the grid, and that ambiguity undercuts the "surprise" framing in the subject line. The subject promises named designers (Canali, ALICE+OLIVIA) but neither appears prominently — Canali is a small product caption nine rows down, and ALICE+OLIVIA doesn't visually appear in the grid at all.
- Net: a solid, non-repetitive execution let down by a subject line that oversells specificity the email doesn't deliver, and by burying which discount applies to what.

## What's working

- Clean, editorial "Sale" hero with unmistakable 25% off callout and a clear SHOP NOW button.
- Nine-product grid gives real browsing variety across dresses, denim, and menswear, unlike recent no-discount roundup sends.
- Two category CTA buttons (Shop Women's/Men's Clothing) route beyond the homepage.
- Fine print is present with a clear end date (9/7/26), which is more transparent than some prior sends.

## What's weak

- Subject calls out Canali and ALICE+OLIVIA by name, but Canali is a tiny caption near the bottom and ALICE+OLIVIA is nowhere visible in the grid — a bait-and-switch on specificity.
- Two competing offers (25% off vs. up to $250 off with SHOPFALL) run in the same email with no guidance on which applies where — confusing, not "surprising."
- "Exclusions Apply" appears twice with no inline detail, pushing all substance into dense legal copy at the very bottom.
- No pricing shown anywhere in the grid — for a $250-off, $400-minimum-spend mechanic, showing actual price points would make the math land instead of requiring math in the shopper's head.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one primary offer for the send. If both must run, visually separate them as "Offer A: no code needed" vs. "Offer B: use code for bigger baskets" rather than stacking two banners that look like restatements of the same deal.
- 2. Feature Canali and ALICE+OLIVIA prominently in the hero or top row since they're named in the subject — right now the subject over-promises.
- 3. Add price/strike-through pricing under at least the hero products so the 25%-off math is visible without a click.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, stack up to $250 off with code SHOPFALL`
