# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened neimanmarcus.com on my phone and was greeted by a big white wall with the message "Access is temporarily restricted." No hero, no runway imagery, no personalized InCircle welcome — just a bot-detection gate. The only hint that this is NM is the signature script logo at the top and, oddly, a tiny crop of what looks like model legs in kitten-heel shoes peeking in from the bottom. This is not a homepage experience — it's a lockout screen.

## What's working

- **NM wordmark** renders correctly and is recognizable — at least I know I'm at the right brand.
- **"Submit feedback" link** is functional and present, which is a minimal grace for a frustrated shopper who wants to report the issue.
- **Faint shoe crop at the bottom** hints at editorial photography behind the gate — confirms there's a real page waiting if I could get past this.

## What's weak

- **The block message itself** — "Rapid taps or clicks," "Automated (bot) activity" — reads accusatory. I'm a 51-year-old InCircle member on my phone, not a hacker.
- **No bypass or alternative path** — no "try again," no phone number, no "sign in to continue." I'm completely stranded.
- **Zero InCircle acknowledgment** — if I were logged in, my loyalty status should carry weight. There's no sign-in prompt that might unlock the page.
- **IP address shown publicly** (75.140.9.0) — disconcerting; customers shouldn't see their own IP on a retail site.
- **Plain white layout with no NM visual language** — font choice and page design feel generic, not the luxe editorial standard I expect from this brand.
- **No customer service escalation path** — not even a phone number for a brand whose core customer is phone-comfortable.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the cold error copy** with warmer language: "We want to make sure it's you — please sign in to continue shopping" keeps the security intent without calling me a bot.
- **Surface an InCircle sign-in CTA prominently** so logged-in members can bypass the gate — rewards loyalty and reduces friction for the highest-value customers.
- **Add a client services phone number or chat link** ("Call us: 1.888.888.4757") — NM's core customer picks up the phone; let her.
- **Strip the raw IP address** from the customer-facing message — it adds zero value for the shopper and feels like a technical oversight, not a premium brand touch.
