# Ends today: 25% off select regular-price styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 25%-off-regular-price mechanic Neiman Marcus has been running on a loop — 9/3 ("Surprise: Here's 25% off..."), 9/5 ("Upgrade your weekend with 25% off Badgley Mischka..."), and now again on 9/7 with an "ends today" urgency wrapper slapped on. The deadline is real and stamped in the fine print (9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT), which is the one legitimately new element versus the earlier sends.
- Execution is clean: bold "Sale" hero graphic, unmissable "25% OFF" callout, single black SHOP NOW button, and a nine-item product grid spanning dresses, denim, and menswear shirting to hit multiple shopper types in one send. No render bugs, no broken images, no layout breakage.
- The problem is cadence fatigue, not craft. This is the third iteration of the identical discount mechanic within five days, and the subject leans on manufactured urgency ("ends today") for an offer that's already been seen twice this week. A subscriber who opened the 9/3 or 9/5 version will recognize this instantly as a repeat, not a fresh moment.

## What's working

- Hero graphic is unambiguous — "Sale," "25% OFF," and "SHOP NOW" all land in the first screen with no scrolling.
- Deadline is concrete and credible (9/7/26 10:00 PM CT), not vague "limited time" hand-waving.
- Product grid mixes categories (eveningwear, denim, menswear shirting) so multiple shopper intents are served from one email.
- Two category CTAs at the bottom (Shop Women's / Shop Men's Clothing) give a second path to convert beyond the single hero button.
- No visual defects — clean rendering throughout.

## What's weak

- Third near-identical "25% off regular-price styles" send in five days (9/3, 9/5, 9/7) — same mechanic, same visual template, just re-skinned urgency copy.
- "Ends today" urgency feels manufactured given the same discount was live all week under different subject lines.
- Hero has zero product imagery — an abstract "Sale" wordmark instead of the human/lifestyle photography this brand can lean on to differentiate from the last two sends.
- Exclusions are mentioned in the hero ("Exclusions Apply") but not specified until the dense legal block at the bottom — no hint at what's actually excluded.
- No loyalty/InCircle member perk surfaced anywhere despite this being exactly the kind of send where member-exclusive early access would justify the repeat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-running the same 25%-off mechanic on a 2-day cadence — if this is genuinely the last chance, differentiate it as a hard deadline event (countdown timer, "final hours" framing) rather than a copy-paste of 9/3 and 9/5.
- 2. Swap the abstract wordmark hero for a shoppable lifestyle image — three sends in a row with the same graphic template reads as a template, not a moment.
- 3. Move the exclusion categories (or a "shop eligible items" link) up near the hero CTA instead of burying them in the footer legal block.
- 4. Add InCircle member messaging (early access or bonus points) to justify sending a third variant of the same promo.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance today — 25% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop dresses, denim, and menswear before the discount disappears tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added — 25% off ends at 10PM CT.`
