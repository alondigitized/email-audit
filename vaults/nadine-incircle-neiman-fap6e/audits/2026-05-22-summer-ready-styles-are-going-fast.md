# Summer-ready styles are going fast!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the Designer Sale again — the fifth consecutive send in roughly five days (launched 5/17, urgency on 5/18, Givenchy/Ferragamo spotlight on 5/19, new designers added on 5/21, and now this). The creative is nearly identical to the launch email: red hero block, "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF," "SHOP SALE" button. The subject line — "Summer-ready styles are going fast!" — teases urgency and a seasonal angle, but the body delivers the same static sale banner the subscriber has seen four times already. That disconnect between subject promise and body reality is the central failure here.
- The product grid does some work: six individual item shots with brand labels (Rhode, Zimmermann, Nala paris, Loewe, Valentino Garavani, Alaïa, Rag & Bone) give the email tangible product energy. But there are no prices, no specific discounts per item, and no "X% off" callouts at the product level. For a subscriber who has been getting Designer Sale emails all week, there is nothing new to act on — no fresh hook, no countdown, no escalating offer.
- The cadence is the real problem. This is creative fatigue in action. By send five with no material change to the offer or creative, open and click rates will have cratered. A thoughtful "last chance" framing with a deadline or a price-drop escalation would have been the right move here.

## What's working

- Red hero is bold and brand-consistent — "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" is instantly readable.
- Product grid with named brands (Rhode, Zimmermann, Loewe, Alaïa, Valentino Garavani, Rag & Bone) is the best part of the email; it gives the sale credibility and product specificity.
- Six-button CTA grid (Women's Clothing, Men's, Women's Shoes, Men's Shoes, Handbags, Dresses) covers category entry points well.
- "Exclusions apply" and "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" are honest framing — no bait-and-switch language.

## What's weak

- Subject "Summer-ready styles are going fast!" implies new seasonal product, but the body is the same sale banner from 5/17 — a broken promise on open.
- Zero price callouts at the product level — no "was $X / now $Y" anywhere in the grid, which is the single strongest conversion lever for a sale email.
- No deadline or countdown visible — "limited time" in the hero is vague, and after five sends the urgency is completely hollow.
- Creative is nearly indistinguishable from the 5/17 launch send; by send five, a subscriber who hasn't acted yet needs a new reason to click, not a rebroadcast.
- The "Watch with a personal style advisor" callout at the bottom is tiny and visually buried — the most differentiated NM service feature gets no real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero.** "Ends Sunday" or "Sale ends at midnight" transforms "limited time" from vague to actionable. This is the single highest-impact change — it's the only thing that gives a fence-sitter a reason to act today instead of tomorrow.
- 2. **Show prices in the product grid.** Even just "from $X" or a strike-through original price next to two or three items would make the 50% off claim feel real and specific.
- 3. **Reframe the subject to match the body.** This is send five of the same sale — lean into that: "Final days: Designer Sale up to 50% off" is honest and urgent without a fake seasonal tease.
- 4. **Escalate the offer or the creative.** If the sale has truly been extended or deepened, say so explicitly ("New markdowns added"). If not, retire the send — the fifth identical email hurts deliverability reputation and trains subscribers to ignore the brand.
- 5. **Promote the "Watch with a personal style advisor" feature higher.** It's an NM differentiator; a small placement above the category CTAs would make this email stand out from any competitor's sale blast.

### 1. Technical Summary

The send infrastructure (AgentMail relay) is not forwarding authentication or unsubscribe headers, leaving SPF/DKIM status unverifiable and one-click unsubscribe non-functional. A live AMPscript template conflict creates a measurable risk of silent merge-token failure for the `@user` identifier.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links — destinations unverifiable**
All click-redirect URLs were skipped by the HTTP probe. No broken link confirmation is possible; destination UTM hygiene and 404 status are unknown.

**Plain-text part is 70%+ URLs**
QA measured 5,095 of 5,865 plain-text characters as URLs. Several spam filters (SpamAssassin `RATIO_OF_URLS_TO_WORDS`, Barracuda heuristics) score heavily against plain-text bodies where URLs dominate prose. The plain-text alternate likely needs substantive copy rather than a mirror-dump of the HTML link set.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Three vestigial `<meta viewport>` tags left commented in `<head>`**
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
Harmless today, but signals stale template maintenance hygiene and adds parse weight.

**Two overlapping breakpoints for the product grid**
`max-width: 600px` (main responsive block) and `max-device-width: 480px` / `max-width: 520px` (`.ImgSize`, `.ImgSize2x2`) are independent media queries with no clear override order. On a 520–600px viewport (common mid-size Android) `.ImgSize` rules won't fire but the 600px block will — column collapse behavior at that range is unconfirmed.

**CSS-only interactive carousel hidden by default**
`.contentInteractive { display: none !important; }` is the fallback. The checkbox-toggle pattern (`mobileCont:checked`) only works in iOS Mail and a handful of clients. Everywhere else the interactive content is permanently hidden — confirm a static fallback image or module is rendered in its place.

**`<title>` has a trailing space**
`<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor, but some preview-text scrapers pick this up.

**Alt text not verifiable** from the truncated source; should be confirmed separately given the image-heavy layout.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` has a conflicting, partially-commented definition — live template risk**

The comment block at the top of the HTML reads:
```
<!--REQUIRED variables: ...
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
...
CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```

The outer `<!-- -->` HTML comment does **not** suppress AMPscript. The inner `/* */` is an AMPscript block comment, but it is wrapped inside an HTML comment — whether Salesforce Marketing Cloud processes AMPscript inside HTML comments depends on the account setting `Suppress AMPscript in HTML comments`. If that setting is off, both the Lookup and the `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` execute, and the last assignment wins (`HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` as a literal string, not a function). If the setting is on, neither executes and `@user` is undefined.

**Impact:** any link or block conditioned on `@user` (typically personalized nav, loyalty tier, or click-tracking tokens) will either receive a literal string `HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` or empty, silently, with no send-time error.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid, "")`**
Concatenating with an empty string is a no-op. Either the second argument was stripped (e.g., a campaign suffix), or this is dead code. Confirm intent with the template owner.

**`@emailid` conditional** — the comment notes "if available, otherwise Lookup" but only one code path is visible in the truncated source. If the sendable DE doesn't include `EMAIL_ID`, the Lookup fallback must be confirmed present and syntactically valid.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN)
CAN-SPAM requires a functional opt-out mechanism, met by the in-body unsubscribe link. However, Gmail and Apple Mail use `List-Unsubscribe` to surface the one-tap unsubscribe UI. Without it, inbox providers may route to Promotions tab more aggressively or suppress the email entirely for users who previously marked similar mail as spam.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** (QA WARN)
Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements mandate one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`). Its absence puts the send outside Yahoo/Google compliance for bulk senders (≥5k/day threshold). This is likely a relay-stripping issue (see below) rather than a template issue, but the outcome is the same.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** (QA WARN)
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed through the AgentMail relay. If the relay is not forwarding these headers, downstream inbox providers are evaluating authentication on the relay's IP reputation, not NeimanMarcus's configured records. Verify relay forwarding config.
