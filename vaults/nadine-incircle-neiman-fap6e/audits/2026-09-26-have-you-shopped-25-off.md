# Have you shopped 25% off?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Friends & Family 25%-off mechanic Neiman Marcus has now run four times in a week — 09-23's "Hurry for 25% off select styles" (7.00/10), plus earlier reminders — with the biggest single upgrade being a real 3x3 product grid spanning womenswear, handbags, shoes, and menswear instead of a single moody editorial hero. The subject "Have you shopped 25% off?" is a soft, low-urgency nudge compared to 09-23's "Hurry for" framing, and it reads as the tail end of a fatigued campaign rather than a fresh push.
- The hero does its job cleanly: bold "FRIENDS & FAMILY," a legible "25% OFF select styles + 20% off jewelry" offer, a clear CTA button, and InCircle Double Points called out beneath. The grid below gives genuine cross-category browsing (Dolce&Gabbana, A.L.C., Theia, FRAME, Cult Gaia, Gianvito Rossi, Brunello Cucinelli, Emporio Armani, Versace) rather than a single product push, which is the strongest structural element I've seen from this campaign all week.
- Net judgement: solid execution, weak send-timing. It's the cleanest layout in this campaign's run, but as the fourth-plus touch on the identical offer with a question-mark subject that signals "you still haven't acted," it's going to underperform on opens even if it performs fine on clicks for anyone who does open it.

## What's working

- Clear, high-contrast hero: dark green background, large "FRIENDS & FAMILY" wordmark, unambiguous "25% OFF" plus jewelry sub-offer.
- Single unmissable CTA button ("SHOP THE EVENT") sits directly under the offer with no scroll needed.
- Genuine cross-category product grid (9 items: dresses, outerwear, gowns, denim, handbags, shoes, knitwear, tailoring, footwear) gives multiple entry points instead of one editorial image.
- InCircle Double Points benefit is called out in its own banner — good loyalty-member signal.
- Category CTA buttons below the grid (Shop Women's, Shop Handbags, Shop Men's, etc.) let a shopper jump straight to their category.

## What's weak

- Subject "Have you shopped 25% off?" is the weakest hook of the week — it's a guilt-nudge, not news, and openly signals this is a repeat send of an offer the recipient has already seen (and likely ignored) three times.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA in the hero — small but visible enough to plant doubt before the click.
- No pricing or percent-off shown on individual grid products — the shopper has to click through blind to see what 25% off actually looks like on the Cult Gaia bag or the Versace loafers.
- Zero urgency in the visible subject/hero copy despite the offer actually ending 9/30/26 — that deadline is buried in the fine print at the bottom, not surfaced where it would drive action.
- Fourth-plus near-identical send this week on the same mechanic — cadence fatigue is a real risk here regardless of creative quality.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 9/30 deadline in the subject line — urgency is the one lever this send is missing entirely, and it's sitting unused in the terms. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
- 2. Swap the guilt-nudge subject for a deadline-driven one — "have you shopped" implies staleness; a countdown implies news.
- 3. Add a small "Ends 9/30" or "3 days left" badge in the hero itself, not just buried in the footer legal text.
- 4. Consider trimming this to a true final-reminder send with day-count urgency rather than another neutral restate, given this is at least the fourth touch on the identical 25%/20% mechanic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 days left: 25% off Friends & Family`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — 25% off select styles ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off jewelry and Double Points for InCircle members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals from Theia, Cult Gaia, and Brunello Cucinelli — 25% off through 9/30.`
