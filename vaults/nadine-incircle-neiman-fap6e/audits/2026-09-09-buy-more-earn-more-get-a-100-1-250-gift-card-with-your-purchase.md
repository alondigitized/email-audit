# Buy More, Earn More: Get a $100–$1,250 gift card with your purchase
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the gift-card mechanic Neiman Marcus already ran on 9/8 ("Limited Time: Earn up to a $1,250 gift card today," 6.00/10), re-served the next day with a nearly identical tiered spend/earn table and the same pink hero. The mechanic is genuinely one of the stronger offers in this brand's recent rotation — it's concrete, tiered, and gives the shopper a reason to consolidate spend — and the render is clean: legible tier table, nine well-shot cross-category products, clear CTA buttons.
- But back-to-back sends with the same offer structure and nearly the same headline ("Earn up to a $1,250 gift card" → "Buy More, Earn More: Get a $100-$1,250 gift card") reads as a re-send rather than a new campaign beat, and a subscriber who opened yesterday's version will likely skim past this one. The fine print is dense (nine bullet exclusions plus a "what happens if I return merchandise" clawback clause), which undercuts the "earn a gift card" framing when read closely.
- Judgement: solid mechanic, weak execution on cadence — this is the second consecutive day running the same promo, which will fatigue the list faster than the offer's quality deserves.

## What's working

- The spend/earn tier table ($500→$100 through $5,000+→$1,250) is laid out cleanly and is immediately scannable.
- Nine diverse product tiles (women's, men's, shoes, handbags) give multiple entry points without cluttering the hero.
- "SHOP NOW" and the six category buttons below are unambiguous, high-contrast CTAs.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- This is a direct repeat of 9/8's "Limited Time: Earn up to a $1,250 gift card today" — same tier structure, same pink palette, same core offer served one day apart.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" reads like a fragment bolted onto the hero rather than an intentional subhead.
- The exclusions/clawback fine print block near the footer (returns can forfeit or reduce the earned card) is long and buried, which cuts against the "earn more" framing.
- No urgency deadline is visible in the hero itself — the 9/13/26 expiration only appears in small print far below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't run this offer two days in a row with near-identical creative — if the mechanic is repeating, at minimum vary the hero visual and headline angle so it doesn't read as a duplicate to anyone who opened yesterday's email.
- 2. Surface the 9/13 deadline in the hero (e.g., "Ends 9/13") to create real urgency instead of burying it in fine print.
- 3. Trim "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" into a proper second line or drop it — right now it floats awkwardly between the headline and the code.
- 4. Move a one-line plain-English summary of the return/exclusion policy up near the tier table so shoppers aren't surprised by the clawback clause later.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $500, get $100 back in gift cards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop fall arrivals from Chloé, Golden Goose, Jimmy Choo & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nine new pieces that qualify for your gift card`
