# Don't forget: up to $650 off your purchase ends soon
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer reminder with a clear tiered-spend mechanic. A teal hero reads "LIMITED TIME / UP TO $650 OFF / Code | NOVSAVE," with a white SHOP NOW button. Below it a spend/save table runs $250→$50, $500→$125, $1,000→$300, $2,000+→$650. The eye lands on the offer first, and the code is visible.
- The weakness is that the subject promises urgency ("ends soon") but the body never names a deadline. The hero says only "LIMITED TIME." The actual end date, 11/14/26 at 10:00 PM CT, is buried in the footer fine print. The product grid is a generic mix of ten-ish brands with no prices and no product-level hooks. It's a competent reminder, but it undersells the one thing that should drive a click: tomorrow night's deadline.
- Compared with the Nov 12 sends ("The Designer Sale exclusive access: 40% off" and the NMACCESS/NMSALE sends), this is the fourth promo-code send in about two days. The cadence is heavy, and a subscriber is likely to see this as more of the same.

## What's working

- The hero is a single, bold offer with a clear hierarchy: "UP TO $650 OFF" in large type, code beneath, button below.
- The spend/save table makes the tiers easy to scan and shows the value ladder at a glance.
- The "Don't forget" framing fits a reminder and ties back to earlier sends.
- The product grid is tidy and consistent, with luxury names (Jimmy Choo, Frame, Alice + Olivia) that suit the brand.
- Nothing is broken: images load, there are no overlaps, and the layout holds together.

## What's weak

- No deadline appears in the hero. "Limited time" is vague, and the real end date is only in the small footer print.
- The product grid shows no prices, no "after code" pricing, and no reason to pick one item over another.
- There are 6 near-identical teal category buttons ("SHOP WOMEN'S" and "SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING" overlap), which dilutes the CTA.
- "Exclusions Apply" is tiny, low-contrast text, and the long exclusions list in the footer undercuts the "up to" headline.
- The product tiles are unlabeled as to what they are, and there is no sizing or availability cue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tomorrow, 10 PM CT." Make it the second-largest element, since the subject already promises urgency.
- 2. Replace the six category buttons with 2-3 distinct ones (e.g. Women's, Shoes & Handbags, Men's).
- 3. Add a "Spend $500, save $125 = 25%" callout and highlight the best-value tier.
- 4. Add prices or "after code" prices under the product tiles, and cut the grid to 6 hero items.
- 5. Move a one-line exclusions summary next to the offer so it reads as honest rather than buried.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tomorrow: up to $650 off with NOVSAVE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day to save up to $650`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $500, save $125. Code NOVSAVE expires tomorrow at 10 PM CT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your tier savings are waiting: $50 to $650 off, today and tomorrow only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML leaks internal Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template comments and has accessibility gaps in the `<head>`. The QA run couldn't verify compliance headers, authentication or link destinations, so those items are unconfirmed rather than failed. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and links were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 5,488 of 6,919 characters (about 79%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The text alternative is probably raw tracking URLs rather than readable copy. This raises spam-filter risk and makes for a poor text-only experience.
- No malformed `href` values appear in the visible portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **No language attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` (or `xml:lang`), so screen readers can't select a pronunciation profile.
- **Weak `<title>`.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject line. It is minor but visible in web-view and some clients.
- **Checkbox-hack hamburger nav.** The `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` pattern depends on CSS `:checked` support, and `.menu` is `display:none` by default. Clients that strip `<input>` or don't support `:checked` (Gmail, some Outlook builds) may leave the nav inaccessible. This cannot be confirmed from the truncated source, so test it.
- **Duplicate `width` in `*[class].show`.** It declares `width: auto !important` and later `width: 278px`. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Legacy selectors.** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` are obsolete but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens or AMPscript appear in the rendered visible portion.
- The leading HTML comment **does** expose the template scaffolding:
  - `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid` appear in the comment.
  - It also shows `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", …)` and `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm"/"cmdid", …)`.
  - It ends with `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW`, so the scenario selection was never cleaned up.
- This is an information leak (internal data extension names, CRM feed field names) that ships to every recipient. See Recommendations.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. If it is truly absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The relay is the likely cause, but alignment of `e.neimanmarcus.com` is unverified.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address aren't in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unable to verify. The tracking links were skipped, and no UTM parameters, `refid` values or final landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. The comment references `@refid` and `@base_refid`, which suggests campaign attribution runs through a `refid` parameter rather than UTMs. That is plausible but unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the SFMC comment block** (`<!--REQUIRED variables … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`) at build or send time so internal DE and field names don't ship to recipients.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`.** Use `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`, and set a descriptive `<title>` with no trailing space.
4. **Generate a readable plain-text alternative** with short link labels rather than raw tracking URLs, so URLs are well under 70% of the text part.
5. **Confirm headers on the original send, not the relay copy.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via a seed-list or mail-tester check.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML.** Include the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and resolve the tracking links with a real GET to capture final URLs and UTM/`refid` parameters.
7. **Test the hamburger nav** in Gmail and Outlook to confirm a working fallback when `:checked` isn't supported.
