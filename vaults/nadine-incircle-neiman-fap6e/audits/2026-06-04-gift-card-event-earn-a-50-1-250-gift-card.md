# Gift Card Event: Earn a $50-$1,250 gift card
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-verbatim repeat of NM's June 3 "STARTS NOW" send — same olive-green hero, same JUN66C code, same five-tier spend-earn table ($500→$50 through $5,000+→$1,250), same "Exclusions Apply" caveat. The creative team changed almost nothing; the only substantive difference is the subject drops "STARTS NOW" and becomes "Gift Card Event." A subscriber who opened and ignored yesterday's email has been handed the identical brief with no new reason to act.
- That cadence problem aside, the underlying mechanics are solid. The spend-tier table is the most scannable version of a tiered gift-card mechanic I've seen from NM — five columns, clean two-row layout. The product grid beneath it (Oscar de la Renta, Jacquemus, Chloé, Jimmy Choo, Veronica Beard) reinforces the luxury positioning credibly.
- Verdict: the email itself is competent, but sending it 24 hours after a near-identical 8/10 send without a differentiated hook — no deadline surfaced, no new product angle, no urgency layer — turns persistence into noise.

## What's working

- **Spend-tier table is the clearest NM has made it** — five-column grid makes the earn thresholds instantly legible without reading a line of prose.
- **Promo code is front and center** — JUN66C appears in the hero at eye level; no digging required.
- **Category buttons cover real purchase intents** — SHOP WOMEN'S, SHOP SHOES, SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP HANDBAGS, SHOP NEW, SHOP JEWELRY give a shopper with any category in mind a landing point.
- **Product grid is brand-credible** — Oscar de la Renta, Jacquemus, Chloé, and Veronica Beard signal the right tier immediately.

## What's weak

- **Day-two repeat with zero creative differentiation** — yesterday's "STARTS NOW" launched this same promo; today's send brings nothing new, making it feel like a system re-fire rather than a deliberate touchpoint.
- **No deadline visible anywhere** — the tier table has no end date. The time pressure that anchored yesterday's 8/10 is completely absent.
- **Six CTA buttons fragment click energy** — the category spread disperses intent rather than channeling it toward the highest-converting path.
- **Fine print density undermines trust** — "Exclusions Apply" in the hero, combined with a wall-of-text footer, signals meaningful carve-outs without surfacing which categories are excluded until the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero tier table** — "Offer ends June 8" in the table header immediately restores the urgency this re-send is missing; it also gives the campaign a reason to exist as a follow-up rather than a duplicate.
- 2. **Give this send a differentiated lens** — reframe as "Most popular styles earning gift cards right now" with 3–4 specific product links instead of category pages; it makes the re-send feel like new editorial, not a reminder.
- 3. **Collapse to two primary CTAs** — SHOP WOMEN'S and SHOP SHOES are the highest-intent categories; the other four dilute both.
- 4. **Surface the minimum spend threshold in the hero** — "$500 minimum · new collections only" stated above the tier table pre-qualifies clickers, reduces bounce from shoppers who can't hit the tier, and is more transparent than a fine-print footnote.

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Gift Card Event" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) AMPscript deployment with standard table-based layout. Three header-level compliance gaps and exposed internal template scaffolding in the HTML comment block are the primary concerns.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links unprobed** — All click-redirect URLs were skipped by the QA relay. Link rot, broken redirect chains, or incorrect destination URLs cannot be confirmed from this audit.

**Evidence:** QA output: `26 tracking link(s) skipped: Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe`

**Action required:** Run a targeted link probe against the click-redirect domain (likely `click.e.neimanmarcus.com` or similar) from an authorized IP to validate all 26 links resolve correctly and land on intended pages.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Conflicting viewport meta tags left in source (commented-out but present)**

Three deprecated viewport configurations are present as HTML comments immediately above the active viewport tag:
```html
<!-- active -->
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">
<!-- dead code left in -->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are build artifacts and should be stripped from production sends.

**`maximum-scale=1` on active viewport tag** — Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Some email clients ignore this, but it is non-compliant where honored.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang="en"`, failing WCAG 3.1.1 and screen reader language detection.

**`<title>` has a trailing space** — `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>`. Minor but indicates the template was not linted before send.

**Truncated CSS in source** — `.TxtSize2x2` rule ends with `max-wid…`, indicating the HTML was either truncated during relay capture or the source template has a malformed property. If this is a relay artifact, verify the full template; if it shipped as-is, the responsive rule for 2×2 product grid cells is broken on ≤520px viewports.

**Fragmented media query breakpoints** — Responsive rules are split across two `<style>` blocks targeting different breakpoints (600px in the main block, 480px and 520px in a secondary block). This is functional but increases maintenance risk.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Internal AMPscript scaffolding and data extension names exposed in production HTML**

The HTML opens with a comment block that ships verbatim to recipients:
```html
<!--REQUIRED variables: @email (EMAIL_ADDRESS from sendable DE)
    @base_refid (from Campaign Calendar)
    ...
    @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
    @emailid ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", ...)
    @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
    @cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email))
    CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

This exposes: data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), internal field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`), hashing strategy (`HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`), and developer instructions. These should be stripped before deployment — they are visible to any recipient who views source.

**SHA-256 hash scenario commented out** — The `SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256` block is disabled. Verify the active `@user` assignment resolves correctly and is not emitting a null or literal string into tracking URLs.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

QA did not detect this header. Gmail and Yahoo Mail (since February 2024 bulk sender enforcement) require this header for senders above 5,000/day. Its absence risks inbox demotion or bulk folder placement.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe is not implemented at the header level. Required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy alongside `List-Unsubscribe`.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from the relay capture. The send domain `e.neimanmarcus.com` should have DKIM signed by SFMC and SPF aligned. This must be verified at the MTA level.

**Evidence:** QA output: all three are `[WARN]` with note `may not be captured by AgentMail relay`.

Note: These headers may exist at the MTA level and simply not have been forwarded by the relay. Confirm directly against raw headers on a delivered copy before treating as a confirmed send-side defect.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot fully evaluate — all 26 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe. UTM parameter presence, correct `utm_campaign` / `utm_medium` / `utm_source` values, and landing page alignment (offer parity, gift card threshold messaging) are unverified.

The `@base_refid` and `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid, "")` variables in the template comments suggest a campaign ID is being appended to tracking URLs. Verify `@base_refid` was populated from the Campaign Calendar DE at send time and is not null — a null refid would result in malformed or unattributed tracking parameters.
