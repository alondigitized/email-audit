# Last. Call. Up to 70% off L’Agence, Veronica Beard & more
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Extra 25% Off" Last Call hero that's been running since at least 7/29-8/7 — red slab, white "EXTRA 25% OFF," "LAST CALL" wordmark, single "SHOP SALE" button — with the subject line swapped to name-drop L'Agence and Veronica Beard. Except neither brand appears anywhere in the email. The nine-item product grid below the hero is Isabel Marant, Balmain, Bugatchi, Stuart Weitzman, Theia, Manolo Blahnik, Cinq a Sept, AREA, and Weekend Max Mara — a completely different designer roster than what's promised in the subject line.
- That's a real problem, not a nitpick: a subscriber who opens specifically for L'Agence or Veronica Beard scrolls the whole email and finds neither, which is the exact kind of subject-line bait this brand has mostly avoided in recent sends (8/7's "Extra 40% off" send at least matched its subject to its content). The fine print also walks back urgency — "Ends 8/31/26 at 10:00 PM CT" is three weeks out, a big step down from 8/9's "Ends tonight" real-deadline send.
- Execution is otherwise clean: product photography is crisp, grid is well-organized, CTAs are legible. But between the recycled hero creative and the subject/content mismatch, this reads as a lower-effort send propped up by a misleading subject line.

## What's working

- Product grid renders cleanly with nine clear, well-lit items and legible designer labels underneath each.
- "SHOP SALE" button is high-contrast and impossible to miss against the red hero.
- Secondary category buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Handbags, Shoes, Kids, Women's Dresses) give multiple clickable paths beyond the single hero CTA.
- No render bugs — images, text, and layout all display correctly.

## What's weak

- Subject promises L'Agence and Veronica Beard; neither brand appears anywhere in the hero or the nine-item grid — a direct subject/content mismatch.
- Hero creative ("Extra 25% Off... Last Call") is a near-exact repeat of the version that's run repeatedly since 7/29, with no new visual treatment.
- Deadline in the fine print ("Ends 8/31/26") undercuts any sense of urgency the "Last Call" framing is trying to create.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA, flagging fine print before the click rather than after.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-content mismatch first — either feature L'Agence and Veronica Beard product tiles in the grid, or rewrite the subject to match what's actually shown (the current mix of Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, etc.). This is the single biggest trust issue in the send.
- 2. Refresh the hero creative — this exact red slab has run enough times that repeat subscribers will recognize and skip it; swap in a lifestyle/model image like the 8/7 and 8/8 editorial sends that scored higher.
- 3. Surface a real, near-term deadline in the hero itself (not buried in the footer) if one exists — "Ends 8/31" doesn't support "Last Call" urgency; if there's no near-term expiration, drop the urgency framing.
