# Fendi’s 2026 eyewear collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a full-price designer collaboration send with zero discount, and it's the most editorially confident image I've seen in this batch — the two models back-to-back in denim against a saturated red backdrop, wrap-around Fendi frames catching the light, reads like an actual fashion campaign rather than an inbox promo. Compare it to 8/8's "The Cover Up" jacket drop (6/10) — same playbook of letting a strong hero image and zero discount carry the send — and it mostly works again here.
- Where it falls short is depth and specificity: one hero image, one paragraph of copy, two buttons, done. There's no price, no second product angle, no sense of how many styles are in the collection, and "Shop Fendi" vs. "Shop Sunglasses" is a confusing split when the whole email is already about Fendi eyewear. Strong top-of-funnel brand impression, weak conversion mechanics.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely striking — cinematic lighting, red backdrop, both models wearing statement wrap-around frames that clearly show the "geometric frames, house codes" language from the copy.
- Copy names the designer (Maria Grazia Chiuri) and specific product attributes (wrap-around styles, geometric frames, house codes), which reads as informed rather than generic.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — nothing competing with the hero image, no lorem ipsum, no broken renders.
- No discount/urgency bait — this is honest full-price brand storytelling, consistent with the "Fendi eyewear collection" subject.

## What's weak

- Only one product image — no close-up or detail shot of the frames themselves (hardware, temple detailing, lens shape), which matters for a $400+ eyewear purchase.
- No price shown anywhere, and no indication of collection size ("12 new styles" or similar) — reduces confidence before clicking.
- Two competing CTAs ("Shop Fendi" vs. "Shop Sunglasses") without a clear distinction is confusing when this is a single eyewear drop.
- No social proof, no loyalty/InCircle callout for a piece this premium.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a second image — a close crop on the frames/hardware — so the reader can evaluate fit and detail before clicking; this is the single highest-leverage fix for a luxury eyewear send.
- 2. Collapse to one CTA ("Shop Fendi Eyewear") or clearly differentiate the two buttons (e.g., "Shop Sunglasses" vs. "Shop Optical") so the split has a reason to exist.
- 3. Add a starting price ("Starting at $XXX") to set expectations before the click.
- 4. Add an InCircle/loyalty line if applicable — this is exactly the kind of designer drop members expect to hear about first.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Maria Grazia Chiuri's new Fendi eyewear is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Fendi's wrap-around eyewear collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Geometric frames, house codes, and statement shapes — shop the drop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Starting at $XXX, new Fendi sunglasses and optical are in.`
