# Have you shopped our Last Call Sale? Save up to 70%
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely strong Last Call send: a bold red hero with a real, legible discount stack ("Extra 25% Off" on top of "up to 70% off"), a clean white "SHOP SALE" button, and honest fine print ("Exclusions Apply") right in the hero instead of buried at the bottom. Compared to the brand's recent no-discount editorial sends (8/10, 8/11 countryside) and the flat "1,000+ arrivals" grid repeated on 8/10 and 8/13, this actually leads with a number and a deadline, which is what drives clicks.
- The 3x2 product grid below is well-labeled by designer (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, etc.) and the six-tile "SHOP" button grid at the bottom gives multiple clear paths in. The end-of-email fine print even specifies a real expiration ("Ends 9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT"), which is unusually transparent for this brand's recent cadence.
- The weak spot is that this is Last Call — Neiman's off-price/outlet sub-brand — not core Neiman Marcus, so if the recipient shops Neiman for full-price designer goods, this reads as a different (lower) tier than expected. Still, as an executed promo email it's one of the better ones in this brand's recent history.

## What's working

- The hero leads with a concrete, stacked offer (extra 25% off on top of up to 70% off) — much more compelling than the discount-free "1,000+ arrivals" or "countryside" editorial sends from earlier in the week.
- "SHOP SALE" is a clear, high-contrast white button against the red hero — unambiguous primary CTA.
- Fine print with the exact end date/time ("Ends 9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT") gives credible urgency without needing a countdown clock gimmick.
- Product grid is clean, correctly labeled by designer, and covers a real style range (handbag, jacket, dresses, sandals, shirt, mules).
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, layout holds together top to bottom.

## What's weak

- No models/lifestyle imagery — every product shot is a flat-lay/ghost-mannequin product photo, so there's no styling or fit context.
- The offer is Last Call (off-price outlet), not mainline Neiman Marcus, which isn't clarified beyond the logo lockup — could feel like a bait-and-switch to a full-price Neiman shopper expecting core inventory.
- Subject promises "Save up to 70%" but the hero's real headline offer is "Extra 25% Off" — the two numbers aren't reconciled until you read the small print, creating a beat of confusion.
- "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" is generic urgency copy — no actual low-stock or countdown signal to back it up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Reconcile the subject/hero math — lead with the same number in both places so there's no gap between what's promised and what's seen first (either subject says "Extra 25% off Last Call" or hero foregrounds "up to 70%").
- 2. Add one lifestyle/model shot near the top of the product grid to break up six flat product tiles and give a styling cue.
- 3. Make the deadline visible in the hero itself (not just bottom fine print) — "Ends 9/7" next to Shop Sale would sharpen urgency at the point of decision.
- 4. Clarify Last Call positioning briefly (e.g., "Last Call: our outlet edit") so recipients aren't surprised this isn't core Neiman inventory.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off Last Call — ends 9/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Last Call favorites, up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balmain, Manolo Blahnik & more — sale ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack an extra 25% off already-reduced styles`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- The provided HTML source is truncated before the `<body>` content, so individual CTA hrefs and redirect chains could not be inspected directly in this pass. No broken/malformed `href` values were observed in the reviewed head/style block.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- No issues found in the reviewed markup: standard Outlook/MSO conditional-comment scaffolding, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `.ExternalClass` line-height resets, and mobile breakpoints (`480px`, `520px`, `600px`) are all present and correctly structured.
- `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space before the closing tag — cosmetic only, no functional impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The AMPscript setup block declares `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, and `@cmdid`, sourced via `Lookup()` calls against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed` data extensions. All required variables are declared with a documented fallback scenario for `@user` (`HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`).
- Note: this AMPscript block is left in an HTML comment at the top of the compiled source rather than stripped before send. It's inert (browsers/clients ignore HTML comments) so it poses no rendering risk, but it does expose internal data-extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`) to anyone who views source on the delivered email — worth stripping in the SFMC send-classification/build step as a minor information-disclosure hygiene item.
- Personalized token resolution in the body (e.g., first-name merge, `@refid` interpolation into links) could not be verified — not present in the truncated source provided.

- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN) — the header may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, but if genuinely absent this is a CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058 gap; low-risk if a functional unsubscribe link exists in the footer body (not visible in truncated source).

- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN) — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable from this relay hop; recommend checking headers at the receiving MTA directly rather than via AgentMail.
- Footer/physical-address and unsubscribe-link presence (CAN-SPAM body requirements) could not be assessed — footer markup not included in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

Recommend re-running this check against the full HTML body to confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present and that the `@refid`-driven links resolve to a matching Last Call landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being set by SFMC send config, independent of AgentMail's capture — verify with raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
2. Investigate SPF/DKIM/DMARC status directly (e.g., via a `Received-SPF` / `Authentication-Results` check at the destination MTA) since the relay isn't surfacing it.
