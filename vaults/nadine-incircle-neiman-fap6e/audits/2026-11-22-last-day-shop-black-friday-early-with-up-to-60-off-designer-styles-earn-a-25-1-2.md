# Last day: Shop Black Friday early with up to 60% off designer styles + Earn a $25–$1,250 gift card (includes beauty & sale styles)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth near-identical Neiman Marcus Black Friday-early send in three days. It follows the Nov 20 morning send, the Nov 20 "Ends Sunday" send, and the Nov 21 "40% off" exclusive. The black hero with a red frame is the same as before. The "Last day" subject is the only new element, and it is a credible urgency hook because the fine print says the 30% offer ends 12/2, so the deadline is soft.
- The hero stacks three separate offers: up to 60% off the Designer Sale, a $25–$1,250 gift card, and up to 30% off regular-price styles. Nothing tells the eye which one matters. The gift card tiers are only explained in a dense fine-print block, and that block is the real friction. Below the hero, a 3x3 grid of brands (Parfums de Marly, Dolce&Gabbana, A.L.C., Jimmy Choo, Tom Ford, McQueen, Chloe) gives a decent browse, but it has no prices, no per-item discounts and no single hero product.
- Verdict: competent but fatigued. It is a reminder email that reads like a repeat, and the stacked offers dilute it. Cut the hero to one offer and the send would be much stronger.

## What's working

- "Last day" in the subject is a concrete urgency cue, and it is stronger than the earlier "Ends Sunday" version.
- The black hero with the red frame and the bold "UP TO 30% OFF" and "EARN A $25–$1,250 GIFT CARD" blocks is visually striking and on-brand.
- The 3x3 product grid is clean and uniform, and the brand names under each item signal luxury.
- The category buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Beauty) make the email easy to shop by segment.
- The navigation bar (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) is clear, and no images are broken.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete in the hero (60%, gift card, 30%), so there is no single focal point.
- The hero never says "last day" or shows a deadline. The urgency is only in the subject, and the body says "Limited time only," which is vague.
- The subject is about 130 characters long and will be truncated on mobile. It also repeats the near-identical Nov 20 subject.
- The fine print is a long wall of text, and it contains the real gift card terms and the actual end dates.
- The product grid has no prices, no markdown amounts, and no specific item to anchor on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero and put the deadline in the hero image, for example "Ends tonight," and show the real end time. Move the gift card and the 30% offer below it as secondary lines.
- 2. Replace the generic "Shop All" button with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop the Designer Sale."
- 3. Add "was/now" prices or "% off" tags to the grid items, and feature one hero product.
- 4. Collapse the gift card tiers into a visible one-line ladder (for example, spend $100, get $25) so the fine print is not carrying the offer.
- 5. Vary the subject and creative across sends. Four near-identical sends in three days trains people to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard table-based Salesforce Marketing Cloud template. The source is truncated, so the body, links and footer could not be inspected. The confirmed issues are leaked internal template comments, a zoom-restricting viewport tag, and a missing `lang` attribute. The three QA warnings are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No confirmed issues. 29 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (`[WARN] 29 tracking link(s) skipped`), so their resolution is unverified. This is an audit limitation, not evidence of breakage.
- The leaked comment shows `@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")`. If `@base_refid` is empty at send time, the `refid` parameter would be blank. This cannot be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE:** a large HTML comment precedes `<!DOCTYPE …>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy renderers.
- **Fragile media-query selectors:** the rules use exact-match selectors such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]`. They stop matching if an element carries more than one class. This is low severity.
- **Subject length:** the subject is about 130 characters and includes a non-ASCII en dash (`$25–$1,250`). Most inboxes will truncate it, and the offer detail after roughly 60–70 characters may not be shown.
- Image alt text and the hamburger-menu checkbox hack could not be assessed because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template instructions shipped in production HTML.** The top comment reads: `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->`. A second comment, `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`, follows it.
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`).
  - "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" suggests the scenario selection was not stripped from the template.
  - Whether any variables are unresolved in link parameters cannot be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` were not found.** The QA tool itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay, so they are not confirmed sender defects. The tool cannot tell whether the headers were stripped in transit or never sent.
- A visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the footer is in the truncated portion.
- The From domain (`e.neimanmarcus.com`) is a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with proper sender configuration. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment remain unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues found in the visible source. UTM parameters and landing URLs are not in the truncated portion. All 29 tracking links were skipped by the probe, so the final destinations were not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the internal AMPscript/SFMC documentation comments from the production template. This removes the exposed Data Extension names and the leftover "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" text, and the DOCTYPE then comes first.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Re-test with the original raw message headers, or a seed inbox, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. The relay-based result is inconclusive.
5. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Then check the unsubscribe link, postal address, alt text, UTM/`refid` population, and the final destinations of the 29 skipped tracking links, for example by following redirects with a headless client.
6. Optionally shorten the subject, putting the key offer within the first 60 characters.
