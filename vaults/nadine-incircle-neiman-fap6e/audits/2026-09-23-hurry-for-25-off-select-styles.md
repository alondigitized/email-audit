# Hurry for 25% off select styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send of the exact same Friends & Family mechanic in a week — 25% off select styles + 20% off jewelry, now with a subject line ("Hurry for 25% off select styles") that finally adds urgency but nothing else new. The creative itself is clean: a strong green hero, clear offer stack, InCircle Double Points callout, and a well-curated 9-item merchandise grid spanning jeans, gowns, leather jackets, handbags, and boots. Execution-wise this is one of the better-built Neiman Marcus sends — no render bugs, clear hierarchy, honest exclusions language.
- But the fatigue is real: 2026-09-17 "25% off select styles & 20% off select jewelry" → 2026-09-20 "Friendly reminder" → today's "Hurry" send, all pushing toward the same 9/27 10PM CT deadline. A subscriber who's seen this three times already will read "Hurry" as manufactured urgency, not real scarcity, and that undercuts the one lever this email is trying to pull.

## What's working

- Offer is immediately visible and unambiguous: "25% OFF select styles + 20% off jewelry" sits directly under the hero headline.
- Single clear CTA ("SHOP THE EVENT") is well-placed with good contrast against the green field.
- Product grid is diverse and well-photographed — denim, evening wear, outerwear, handbags, boots, menswear — giving multiple entry points regardless of shopper intent.
- InCircle Double Points banner adds a credible loyalty incentive layered on top of the discount.
- No layout or image-rendering issues anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is the third reissue of the identical Friends & Family mechanic in nine days (2026-09-17, 2026-09-20, now 2026-09-23) — cadence fatigue is the biggest problem, not creative quality.
- "Hurry" in the subject isn't backed up visually in the hero — no countdown, no date-specific banner in the image itself; the actual deadline (9/27 10PM CT) is buried in fine print at the bottom.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA in small gray text — technically transparent, but it's the first thing a skeptical shopper reads right after the button.
- Product grid has zero pricing or "% off" shown per item, so despite the 25%-off headline, the shopper can't tell which of these nine items actually qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Bring the deadline into the hero visually — a simple "Ends 9/27 at 10PM CT" line under the CTA would make "Hurry" credible instead of generic, and do more for urgency than the subject line alone.
- 2. Since this is the third touch on the same event, differentiate the angle — lead with "Last chance" framing explicitly, or swap the hero to spotlight a specific category (e.g., outerwear/boots given the season) rather than repeating the identical generic hero.
- 3. Add a price or "extra 25% off" flag directly on 2-3 product tiles to make the discount tangible at the grid level, not just in the header.
- 4. Move "Exclusions Apply" further from the CTA or shrink its visual prominence relative to "SHOP THE EVENT."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — 25% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% off jewelry and double InCircle points — ends 9/27.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Friends & Family picks are almost gone.`
