# Surprise Sale: 20% off select boots & booties
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer category sale, and it's the best-targeted send in recent memory. The hero is a tan block with an oversized black serif "S" and "Surprise Sale." Below it sit "LIMITED TIME," a clear "20% OFF," "select regular-price boots & booties," and a black "SHOP BOOTS" button. The offer, the category and the CTA are all in one glance. The oversized S clips the left edge, which looks intentional and editorial.
- Compared with the recent run, this is more commercial. After the 11/18 Dolce&Gabbana editorial and the 11/17 holiday gift guide, it is a tighter, more shoppable send. It is also the fourth discount message in about five days, after the 11/16 Designer Sale, the 11/17 gift-card tiers and the 11/18 Private Event 40% off. "Surprise" is hard to believe at that cadence. A 20% offer also looks weak next to the 40% and 50% offers from earlier this week.

But it has no prices or sale prices, and I can't see whether the tiles are linked to products. The send is competent and honest, with a real deadline in the fine print, but it buries that deadline and gives no member or price proof.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "20% OFF," "select regular-price boots & booties," and a black "SHOP BOOTS" button with strong contrast.
- The 3x3 grid has consistent, clean product photography and brand names in bold, so shoppers can scan by designer.
- The send reflects the season: boots in mid-November.
- The oversized serif "S" gives the hero a distinctive editorial look that fits the brand.
- The fine print states real terms, including an end date of 11/22/26 at 10:00 PM CT, and the nav bar (Women / Men / Shoes / Sale) is easy to use.

## What's weak

- The real deadline appears only in the small footer fine print. The hero says just "LIMITED TIME," which is vague.
- The product tiles show no prices or sale prices, so the 20% savings never turns into a number.
- "Exclusions Apply" in the hero is faint, and the footer has a long list of exclusions. Both undercut how honest the offer feels.
- The stylized "S" splits the headline into "urprise ale," which reads awkwardly at a glance.
- There is no loyalty or member benefit, no reviews, and no sizing or availability cue. The app and style-advisor block is generic filler.
- It is the fourth sale email in about five days, and 20% is the lowest discount among them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero: "Ends Sunday 11/22." It's the biggest lift for urgency and credibility.
- 2. Add sale prices (was/now) under each brand name, or at least "from $X" on the top row. Link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Fix the "S" headline so it reads as "Surprise Sale," for example by keeping the S but making the remaining letters larger and closer.
- 4. Replace the app and style-advisor block with a "Free shipping and returns" line or a member-benefit line.
- 5. Space out the sale sends, or tie this one to a different trigger (a weather or season hook) so "surprise" is earned.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a checkbox-hack mobile nav. The source has an internal ESP template comment, `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta, and no `lang` attribute. The automated checks raised 4 warnings (missing List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers, URL-heavy plain text, 23 unprobed tracking links). All four are limited by what the AgentMail relay and the truncated source exposed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **23 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them because they are tracking/click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 4,333 of 5,474 characters (about 79%) are URLs, above the 70% warning threshold. The text alternative is probably dominated by long tracking URLs, which hurts readability for text-only clients and can add to spam-filter scoring.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom in clients that honor it. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing `lang`.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation rules.
- **Hamburger nav depends on the checkbox hack.** The CSS uses `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, `.hamburger` and `.close`. Gmail and several other clients strip or ignore `:checked` selectors, and a hidden checkbox is generally not keyboard- or screen-reader-friendly. A fallback to visible links isn't evident in the truncated source.
- **Non-descriptive `<title>`.** The title is `Neiman Marcus ` (trailing space) and doesn't reflect the subject line.
- **Small text.** `.footerText` and `.disclaimerText` are 12px, which is small on mobile. The mobile override only raises `.f12` to 16px, and it isn't confirmed that these elements carry that class.
- **Alt text and image-blocking behavior can't be checked.** The source was truncated before the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens were confirmed in the visible body.
- The leading comment shows the template's required variables (`@email`, `@refid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, and others). Those are in a comment, so they don't render. The body wasn't visible, so I can't confirm those variables resolved in the link parameters.

## 5. Compliance
- **Source comment leaks internal ESP details.** The pre-DOCTYPE comment ships in the delivered HTML and exposes Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) and scenario notes (`SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256`). This is an information-disclosure and hygiene issue. The comment also has unbalanced parentheses (`@user Lookup(...))`), which suggests it was pasted from the template, not generated.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers, so I can't confirm it's a real sender-side gap. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` is unknown because the relay doesn't expose it.
- The visible source doesn't show the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link can't be confirmed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No issues found.** UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. All 23 links went through tracking redirects that QA skipped, and the visible source doesn't include the hrefs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip template and variable-documentation comments (including the pre-DOCTYPE block) at send time.
2. Change the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` and drop `maximum-scale=1`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Confirm the raw headers include `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align.
5. Shorten or consolidate the tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce how many it carries.
6. Provide a non-checkbox fallback for the mobile nav, such as plain visible links.
7. Resolve a sample of the 23 tracking links in a browser to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
8. Write a descriptive `<title>` and remove the trailing space.
