# Ends today: up to $650 off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer last-call send. A teal hero reads "LIMITED TIME / UP TO $650 OFF / Code | NOVSAVE" with a white SHOP NOW button. A spend/save table sits underneath: $250→$50, $500→$125, $1,000→$300, $2,000+→$650. The subject says "Ends today," and the fine print confirms it: "Ends 11/14/26 at 10:00 PM CT."
- It is nearly identical to yesterday's "Don't forget: up to $650 off" send. The creative is the same, only the urgency word changed, so the subject line has to do all the work. The body is a standard 3x3 grid of designer products (Alice + Olivia, Cinq à Sept, FRAME, Jimmy Choo, Sartorio and others). Six teal category buttons follow, and then a black "Designer Sale 40% off, code NMSALE" banner pulls attention to a second offer.
- My judgment is that it's competent and credible, but it's a rerun. The "ends today" deadline is the only new piece of information, and the render doesn't surface it anywhere. The hero says "Limited time," not "Ends tonight."

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable. Large "UP TO $650 OFF," the promo code and a white button on teal make the offer the first thing the eye lands on.
- The tiered spend table is honest and easy to scan. A shopper can see right away what spend gets what savings.
- The designer product grid is on-brand and consistent. Brand names are labeled, and the images are clean with no broken tiles.
- The fine print is complete, and the end date and time are stated explicitly. That makes the "ends today" claim credible.
- The nav (Women / Men / Shoes / Sale) and the six category buttons give clear paths for different shoppers.

## What's weak

- The "ends today" urgency isn't in the creative. The hero says "Limited time," and the actual deadline appears only in the fine print at the bottom.
- The black Designer Sale 40% banner competes with the primary offer. Its large red "40% OFF" pulls the eye away from the NOVSAVE code, and it needs a different code (NMSALE).
- The hero button is the only CTA above the fold. The product grid sits below it with no pricing, no "was/now" and no individual hero product.
- There are no reviews, social proof or product details. Nine products are shown with only a brand name each, so there is little reason to click any one of them.
- This repeats yesterday's "Don't forget: up to $650 off" almost exactly, and the Designer Sale was in the previous three sends. The cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Replace "LIMITED TIME" with "ENDS TONIGHT AT 10PM CT." This is the single change with the most lift, because it makes the creative match the subject.
- 2. Drop the Designer Sale banner from a last-call send, or move it below the footer links. One offer on a deadline send converts better than two.
- 3. Add price or "from" points and product names to the grid. Brand-only labels give shoppers nothing to act on.
- 4. Change the hero button copy to something specific, such as "Shop and save up to $650."
- 5. Keep the same-offer reminder to one send, not two on consecutive days, or give each send its own creative.
