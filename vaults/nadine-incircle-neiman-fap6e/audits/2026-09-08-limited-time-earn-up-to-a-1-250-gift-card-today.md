# Limited Time: Earn up to a $1,250 gift card today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus running its spend-tiered gift-card mechanic — a clean break from the 25%-off-regular-price loop it's been on since 9/3, and a step up from the pure-editorial sends on 9/5–9/7. The hero is unambiguous: pink block, "EARN A $100-$1,250 GIFT CARD," a five-tier spend/earn table, one black CTA. It's the most concrete offer this brand has shown all week — no guessing what you get.
- The catch is the offer's real audience: this only pays off at $500+ spend, and the top tier needs $5,000+. That's a promotion built for the existing high-spend NM shopper, not for pulling in a new one, and the nine-item merchandising grid underneath (Jimmy Choo, Golden Goose, Amiri, Chloé) confirms that — this is luxury-tier stock, not entry-price product.
- Execution is clean and render-bug-free, but this reads as a loyalty/incentive play more than an acquisition email — solid for the right subscriber, unremarkable for anyone below the spend threshold.

## What's working

- Single, unmistakable hero offer with an explicit dollar table (spend $X, earn $Y) — no ambiguity about mechanics.
- One clear CTA ("SHOP NOW") placed directly under the offer, above the fold.
- Nine-product grid gives immediate categories to shop into (apparel, bags, shoes, menswear) right after the hero.
- Fine print ("Exclusions Apply," full T&Cs at the bottom) is present but doesn't clutter the hero.
- No render bugs — clean grid, no overlapping text, no broken images.

## What's weak

- The offer only starts paying out at $500 spend — that's a high bar dressed up as a headline "$1,250" number, which reads as slightly inflated compared to what a typical shopper receives.
- No urgency/deadline visible in the hero itself — the expiration date only appears buried in the fine print (9/13/26), not in the hero or subject.
- The product grid is generic merchandising (same kind of luxury catalog carousel NM already ran on 9/7) rather than curated toward the gift-card mechanic specifically.
- Secondary CTA blocks ("SHOP WOMEN'S," "SHOP HANDBAGS," etc.) fragment attention right after the primary CTA — six competing buttons dilute the "SHOP NOW" push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero — "Ends 9/13" directly under "Limited Time" would convert vague urgency into a credible countdown; right now it's only in the legal footer.
- 2. Reduce the six-button "SHOP WOMEN'S/MEN'S/BEAUTY/etc." grid to two or three max — too many CTAs after the primary one dilutes intent.
- 3. Consider a lower entry tier or a "starting at $100 back" framing so the headline number isn't gated behind a $500 minimum that most subscribers won't hit.
- 4. Tie the product grid to the gift-card categories called out in the hero ("Includes Beauty & Fragrance") rather than a generic luxury mix — feature a beauty/fragrance item to reinforce that inclusion.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $500, earn a $100 gift card — ends 9/13`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $1,250 gift card is 5 days away`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The more you spend on fall favorites, the more you earn back.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals from FRAME, Chloé & Golden Goose — plus a gift card on us.`
