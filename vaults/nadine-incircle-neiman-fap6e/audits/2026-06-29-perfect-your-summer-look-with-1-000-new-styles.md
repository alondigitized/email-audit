# Perfect your summer look with 1,000+ new styles
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure new-arrivals broadcast — no offer, no price, no urgency, full stop. Following a dense run of Designer Sale emails at 60% off (June 23, 26, and earlier on June 29), this send is clearly meant to shift gears and signal "there's more to NM than clearance." The creative is competent: black hero with bold type, two clean 3×2 product grids (women's then men's), brand labels, section CTAs. The brand lineup does real work — Toteme, Valentino, Givenchy, A.L.C. on the women's side; Bottega, Frame, Acne Studios, Ralph Lauren on the men's — but the copy completely fails to frame any of it.
- "BRAND NEW STYLES / JUST LANDED" is the least interesting thing NM could write on a hero. The brands are better than the headline deserves. Combined with zero price signal, zero editorial narrative, and apparently a same-day send alongside the 60%-off Designer Sale email, this creates real positioning confusion: a subscriber seeing both emails today has to choose between deep discounts and full-price new arrivals, with no story to anchor the latter.
- The email is clean and functional. It is not particularly compelling. As a new-arrivals format, it needs either a price anchor or editorial muscle — it has neither.

## What's working

- Brand labels (Toteme, Valentino, Givenchy, A.L.C., Bottega, Frame, Acne Studios) are doing selling work the headline copy doesn't — any one of those names in the subject line would outperform the current generic.
- Clean visual hierarchy: black hero → women's 3×2 grid → section CTA → men's 3×2 grid → section CTA → utility footer. The eye moves predictably.
- Gender-split grids with independent CTAs ("Shop All Women's New Arrivals" / "Shop All Men's New Arrivals") let browsers self-select cleanly without crowding the landing page.
- "1,000+" in the subject is the one concrete signal in the entire send — a quantity number anchors perceived value even without a price.
- No render bugs — all 12 product images loaded, typography is clean throughout.

## What's weak

- Zero price signal anywhere in the body — not even "from $295" — leaves the email as a pure catalog browse with no financial hook.
- "BRAND NEW STYLES / JUST LANDED" is boilerplate; this headline could run on any retailer's new arrivals email any week of the year.
- Product thumbnails are small in a 3-column layout — on mobile these will be barely tappable and hard to evaluate, which suppresses click-through.
- No editorial framing whatsoever — brands drop in without a "why now," trend context, or any curation rationale beyond "they arrived."
- Appearing to be a same-day send alongside the Designer Sale 60%-off email is a self-defeating cadence; the full-price new arrivals email loses on every dimension against a deep discount offer sharing the same inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Feature one brand or one product in the hero copy.** "Toteme just landed. So did Valentino." is more compelling than "BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED" and costs nothing to write.
- 2. **Add a price anchor to the hero.** Even "From $195" or "Starting at $295" creates a commitment signal and separates this from the sale noise still in the inbox.
- 3. **Expand one hero product to a larger image.** The Valentino floral dress or Toteme bag could anchor a full-width hero rather than being buried in a 3-column thumbnail at postage-stamp scale.
- 4. **Add one editorial line per section.** "The week's sharpest women's arrivals" sets a curation frame; right now the grids read as a category dump, not a curated edit.
- 5. **Don't send new arrivals on the same day as a 60%-off sale.** Either hold this 24 hours or make the new arrivals *the* featured email and drop the sale to the next day.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh drops from the labels worth watching, now in stock`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Perfect your summer look" Email

**Sender:** NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com
**ESP:** Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript)
**QA pass rate:** 43% (4 warnings, 0 hard failures)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard SFMC AMPscript template with table-based layout and mobile hamburger nav. The primary technical risks are a disabled SHA-256 hashing path for the `@user` variable (potential PII leak into tracking URLs), missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers (deliverability and compliance), and unverifiable authentication headers via the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links unverifiable**
All click-redirect URLs were skipped by the HTTP probe because they resolve through a tracking domain. No broken-link confirmation is possible from this audit — these require a seed send or manual spot-check.

**Plain-text body is 90% URLs**
QA flagged 5,214 of 5,759 plain-text characters as URLs. This is a spam-filter signal; some filters penalize multipart/alternative emails where the plain-text version contains almost no readable prose. The plain-text body should be a human-readable summary of the email content, not a dump of raw tracking URLs.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` is a no-op**
The AMPscript header defines `@refid` as a concatenation of `@base_refid` with an empty string — functionally identical to a direct assignment. This is dead code that suggests an unfinished template edit (the second argument is likely meant to carry a campaign suffix).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Multiple commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags**
Three alternate viewport declarations are left as HTML comments in `<head>`:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are inert but are template cruft from A/B testing that was never cleaned up. Not a rendering bug, but they bloat `<head>` and create confusion for future maintainers.

**iOS 9 `@-webkit-keyframes bugfix` is stale**
The mobile stylesheet includes a known iOS 9 hamburger-nav workaround:
```css
body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }
@-webkit-keyframes bugfix { from { padding: 0; } to { padding: 0; } }
```
iOS 9 is end-of-life (2016). This animation fires on every render cycle for no benefit. Low priority but worth removing.

**No `alt` text visible in truncated source**
The HTML was truncated before image tags were fully visible. Alt text coverage on product images could not be confirmed — this should be verified against the full source.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**SHA-256 hashing path is commented out and inactive**

The AMPscript header defines two scenarios for populating `@user`, but the hashed-email path is doubly disabled — wrapped in both an HTML comment and a block comment:
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
The active path resolves `@user` to `EMAIL_ID` via a lookup against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`. If `@user` is passed into tracking URLs (the typical use), this means the **raw internal subscriber ID** is being transmitted in click-tracking parameters rather than a pseudonymized hash. Depending on how NM's CDP and analytics stack handle that value, this may expose a linkable identifier downstream.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` sourced from CRM feed**
Both are looked up from `NM_CRM_Feed` by email address. If either is surfaced in tracking parameters or forwarded to third-party analytics pixels, they represent additional PII-adjacent data points to audit.

**`@refid` identity collapse**
`CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` collapses to `@base_refid`. If the intent was `CONCAT(@base_refid, @some_suffix)`, the suffix variable is missing and campaign attribution may be incorrect.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected**
QA Warning: Header not captured by AgentMail relay.
This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender policy for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. Its absence (or relay-layer stripping) risks Gmail routing this to spam for affected recipients.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected**
QA Warning: One-click unsubscribe not confirmed.
RFC 8058 one-click support is a co-requirement alongside `List-Unsubscribe` under the Gmail/Yahoo policy. Missing this header means Gmail cannot surface the native one-click unsubscribe button in its UI.

**Authentication-Results not captured**
QA Warning: SPF/DKIM status is unknown because the AgentMail relay did not forward `Authentication-Results`. DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` cannot be confirmed from this audit. Must be verified via direct header inspection of a seed delivery.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism**
Not verifiable from truncated HTML — the footer was not included in the source excerpt. Assume present given NM's scale, but should be confirmed in full-source review.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 26 tracked links route through a click-redirect domain and were not probed. UTM parameter presence and consistency (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) cannot be confirmed. Given the `@refid`/`CONCAT` issue noted above, campaign-level attribution in analytics may be miscoded even if UTMs are present.

Not assessable without resolving the redirect chain. Requires manual spot-check of at least the hero CTA and nav links.
