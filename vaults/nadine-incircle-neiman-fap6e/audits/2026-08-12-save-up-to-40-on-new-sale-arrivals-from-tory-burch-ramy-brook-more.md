# Save up to 40% on new sale arrivals from Tory Burch, Ramy Brook & more
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same big-script-letter "Sale" hero Neiman Marcus already ran on 8/11 ("New. Sale. Arrivals. — up to 40% off," 7.00/10), just recolored from what was presumably a different palette into this dusty blue, and this time the 3x3 grid mixes real named designers (Tory Burch, Jimmy Choo, Ungaro, ALICE+OLIVIA) with sale-priced pieces instead of leaning on a countdown clock or a red slab. It's clean, legible, and the "UP TO 40% OFF" callout sits right next to a clear black "SHOP SALE" button — no render bugs, no overlapping text, nothing broken.
- The problem is this brand has now run two near-identical oversized-script-letter hero sales in 48 hours, and neither one is discount-honest at the top: "up to 40%" always means most items are far less, and "Exclusions Apply" is tucked in small gray type under the button. For a repeat subscriber, the visual freshness is already wearing thin even though the execution itself is solid.

## What's working

- The 3x3 product grid names real designers (Tory Burch denim bag, Jimmy Choo bucket bag, Ungaro gown) with visible product shots, not stock photography.
- Single unambiguous CTA — black "SHOP SALE" button reads clearly against the blue background.
- Clean secondary navigation (Shop Women's Clothing, Shop Men's, Shop Dresses, etc.) gives multiple entry points below the grid.
- No layout or image-render bugs — every product tile loads properly with visible labels.

## What's weak

- This is a recolor of the 8/11 "New. Sale. Arrivals." hero — same oversized script "S" treatment, same layout, just swapped to blue. Repeat creative within 24 hours is a cadence problem.
- "Exclusions Apply" and the real markdown range ("Savings of 25-40% off... Interim markdowns may have been taken") are buried in small legal type at the very bottom, while the hero shouts "UP TO 40% OFF."
- No urgency or end date anywhere in the email — unlike the Last Call sends this week that at least attach a deadline.
- Product grid has no prices visible, so there's no way to gauge the actual discount on any specific item before clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the giant-script-letter hero template for now — it ran 8/11 and is repeating almost verbatim; give the next send a genuinely different layout or at least a different headline treatment so the brand doesn't look like it's recycling assets.
- 2. Add a visible price or "was/now" strike-through on at least the hero product or grid tiles — "up to 40% off" with zero prices shown forces a click just to see the number.
- 3. Move "Exclusions Apply" or a plain-language markdown range closer to the hero, not buried in bottom legal copy — it's a trust issue when the biggest sale claim in size-72 font has no immediate qualifier nearby.
- 4. Add a credible deadline to the hero (this brand has real deadlines on other sends, e.g. "Ends 8/9/26 at 10:00 PM CT") — this send has none, which weakens urgency.
