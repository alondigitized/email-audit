# The Beauty Event: Receive a free tote & more with your $150 beauty purchase
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus's strongest send this stretch — it's the first email in the tracked history with a genuinely concrete, tiered gift-with-purchase offer instead of pure editorial or the repeated $100–$1,250 gift-card mechanic. "Free tote + beauty samples with a $150 purchase, free cosmetics case at $250" is specific, the hero image (the actual totes) sells the gift itself rather than an abstract promo, and the InCircle Triple Points banner adds a loyalty layer that rewards existing members. The nine-product beauty/fragrance grid below is well curated and on-theme.
- Where it falls down is precision and freshness of the deadline claim and category framing: this is a beauty-specific event, so anyone whose Neiman Marcus interest skews apparel/accessories (per prior sends in this history) will read it as off-category. The fine print is also doing a lot of heavy lifting — "one tote per customer," "while supplies last," "exclusions apply" are all buried in small gray/black-block text rather than near the hero. Solid, on-brand execution; not a top-decile send, but a clear step up from the recycled gift-card and pure-editorial emails logged on 9/8–9/10.

## What's working

- Concrete, tiered GWP offer stated in plain numbers ($150 → tote+samples, $250 → cosmetics case) — the clearest single offer of any recent NM send.
- Hero image shows the actual gift (colorful tote bags), which is more persuasive than a generic lifestyle shot.
- InCircle Triple Points callout gives loyalty members a second, distinct reason to shop this event.
- Clean, logical grid of 9 recognizable beauty/fragrance brands (Creed, La Mer, Dior, Guerlain) reinforces "beauty event" positioning without clutter.
- Category shortcut buttons (Shop Beauty, Skincare, Makeup, Fragrances, Hair Care, Bath & Body) give multiple clear paths to purchase.

## What's weak

- No visible end date near the hero or CTA — "Ends 9/20/26" only appears in the dense fine print at the very bottom.
- "One tote and one cosmetics case per customer, while supplies last" caveat sits directly under the CTA in tiny gray type — easy to miss, and the kind of restriction that can feel like bait-and-switch if not surfaced earlier.
- No percentage-off or dollar-off pricing — this is a pure GWP threshold offer, which is a smaller behavioral trigger for someone not already planning a $150+ beauty purchase.
- No social proof, ratings, or "bestseller" labeling on any of the nine products, so it reads as a catalog rather than curated picks.
- No visible personalization (no name, no "because you bought X" signal) despite InCircle member targeting being available.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Ends 9/20" deadline and the "one per customer, while supplies last" caveat up into or directly beside the hero copy — urgency and scarcity are the two biggest levers this email isn't using.
- 2. Add a small badge or ribbon on 2-3 products ("Bestseller," "New") to break up the flat grid and give the eye a hierarchy.
- 3. Consider swapping the pure GWP framing for a hybrid callout (e.g., "plus up to 20% off select beauty") if any percentage discount exists elsewhere in the sale — a stacked incentive tests better than GWP alone.
- 4. Segment version for InCircle members: lead with points multiplier + GWP as a stacked headline rather than a secondary black bar.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free tote at $150, free cosmetics case at $250 — ends 9/20`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your beauty haul just got a free tote (limited time)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, InCircle members earn Triple Points through 9/20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Creed, La Mer, Dior & more — gifts while supplies last.`
