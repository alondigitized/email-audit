# Cinq à Sept’s 10th Anniversary
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Neiman Marcus swaps back to designer-brand editorial, spotlighting Cinq à Sept's 10th anniversary — no price, no percentage, no code anywhere in the frame, continuing the pure-editorial thread from 9/5–9/8 rather than the gift-card mechanic that ran the last two days (9/8, 9/9). The hero is a single embellished black suit shot under "Wing It," a cute but generic headline that gives zero indication this is an anniversary milestone — nothing in the visible copy says "10 years" or "anniversary" anywhere in the render, which is a big miss given that's the entire subject line's hook.
- The six-product grid below is well-organized and consistently labeled "Cinq à Sept," and the two CTAs ("Shop Cinq à Sept" / "Shop Contemporary") are clear and unambiguous. But this is a straight catalog push dressed up as a milestone celebration that the visuals never actually celebrate — a shopper opening on the anniversary promise finds only "Wing It" and a suit. Judgement: competent execution, false-advertised occasion — the subject's strongest asset (the anniversary story) is completely absent from the body.

## What's working

- Clean single-hero-then-grid layout with no overlapping text or broken images.
- Two distinct, clearly labeled CTAs ("Shop Cinq à Sept," "Shop Contemporary") right under the hero.
- Six-product grid is well-cropped and consistently captioned, giving a real sense of the collection's range (blazer, gown, skirt, blouse, dress, bow-neck top).
- Standard NM footer utilities present (app download, style advisor, store finder, account) with no rendering issues.

## What's weak

- The "10th Anniversary" premise from the subject line is completely invisible in the body — no anniversary badge, milestone copy, or celebratory framing anywhere in the render.
- "Wing It" as a headline is vague and does nothing to explain why this email exists right now.
- No price, discount, or offer of any kind — for a "milestone" email, an anniversary-exclusive gift or discount would have been an obvious lever left unpulled.
- Product captions just repeat "Cinq à Sept" six times instead of naming the actual pieces (blazer, gown, etc.), wasting an easy specificity opportunity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the anniversary story in the hero: replace "Wing It" with copy that actually references the 10-year milestone (e.g., a "10 Years of Cinq à Sept" lockup) so the email delivers on its own subject line.
- 2. Add a milestone offer — a limited-time anniversary gift, gift-with-purchase, or discount tier gives shoppers a concrete reason to act now instead of just browsing.
- 3. Replace the six repeated "Cinq à Sept" captions with actual product names/descriptors to add scannable detail.
- 4. Consider a founder or brand-story pull-quote strip beneath the hero to make the "10th anniversary" feel earned rather than just a subject-line hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cinq à Sept turns 10: shop the anniversary edit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `A decade of Cinq à Sept — new arrivals to celebrate`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ten years of effortless tailoring, now in store`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Celebrate with the pieces that define the brand`

- No broken/malformed `href` attributes observed in the visible HTML source (truncated), but full-source link audit could not be completed due to the truncation.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Doctype is `XHTML 1.0 Transitional` — legacy but standard for SFMC email templates; no issue.
- `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` present — correct, prevents iOS auto-reformatting.
- Viewport meta present and unambiguous; two alternate viewport metas are commented out (dead code, no functional impact).
- Mobile media queries (`max-width: 600px`, `480px`, `520px`) are present and structured for responsive collapse (`ImgSize`, `TxtSize`, hamburger nav classes) — no issues found in the visible logic.
- No `alt` attributes visible in the truncated excerpt to confirm image accessibility — cannot verify from available source; flag as **unconfirmed**, not a defect.
- No ARIA roles or semantic landmarks present, typical/acceptable for table-based HTML email — no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The HTML source's leading comment block exposes raw AMPscript/SFMC lookup logic (`@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@user`, `@emailid`, `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated"...)`, `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed"...)`) as an HTML comment rather than executed server-side script — this is documentation/config left in the template. Since it's inside `<!-- -->`, it will not render or execute, but it does leak internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) into the email payload sent to end users, which is an information-disclosure hygiene issue (internal system/table names should not ship in production HTML).
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., literal `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) observed in the visible body content — personalization appears to resolve correctly where rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by QA — either absent or not captured by the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM requires a functioning unsubscribe mechanism (an in-body link may satisfy this, but header-level list-unsubscribe is best practice and increasingly required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed supported, a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement as of 2024.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this capture. This may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping the header rather than an actual authentication failure, but it cannot be verified from available data.
- No visible physical mailing address or footer disclaimer content in the truncated HTML to confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance — truncation prevents verification either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter presence/consistency or landing-page alignment — the 17 tracking links were skipped by the prober and the raw destination URLs are not visible in the truncated HTML excerpt.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript/Data-Extension-name comment block (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`, etc.) from the production-sent HTML — it should stay in the SFMC source template, not the rendered/sent payload.
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058) to comply with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements; confirm whether absence is a true sending-server gap or an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly at the sending MTA, since `Authentication-Results` could not be captured via this relay — do not treat the QA warning as a confirmed auth failure without checking at the source.
4. Re-run link/UTM validation against the full (untruncated) HTML source with tracking-domain redirects resolved, to confirm destination URLs and UTM continuity to the landing page.
5. Reduce plain-text-version URL density (86% of characters) if the plain-text alternative is user-facing, to improve spam-filter scoring.
