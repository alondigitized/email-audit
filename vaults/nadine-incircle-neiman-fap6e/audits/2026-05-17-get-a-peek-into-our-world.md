# Get a peek into our world... ​
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand nurture / onboarding email — a second send that arrived one day after the 15% off welcome email from 2026-05-16. Where yesterday's email had a clear value exchange ("join the list, get 15% off"), today's has none. It's a four-module orientation to what Neiman Marcus is and does: shopping services, editorial content, and the app. Beautifully designed, completely offer-free.
- The sequencing problem is hard to ignore. A subscriber who opened yesterday for the discount now gets a "Welcome to our world" brand tour with no callback to the offer still in their inbox. It reads like the welcome series was built in reverse — orientation before offer — or that these emails were loaded out of order.
- The email is elegant and on-brand, but it asks for multiple clicks (Shop Now, Explore Services, Discover Now, App Store, Google Play) without giving the reader a reason to take any of them. For a day-two touch, this needed either a reminder of the 15% off or a product hook. It delivers neither.

## What's working

- Clean, luxury-consistent visual design — the Neiman Marcus logo, serif typography, and editorial photography all read as premium without breaking the layout.
- No render bugs — text, images, and CTAs all display cleanly.
- The app section is a practical, differentiated CTA — the dual App Store / Google Play badges with a brief "personal touch" value prop is genuinely useful in an onboarding sequence.
- "Shop the Story" module adds editorial texture that distinguishes NM from a pure discounting retailer.

## What's weak

- No offer — the entire email is zero-discount brand content arriving one day after a 15% off hook. The 15% offer isn't referenced once.
- Subject line "Get a peek into our world..." is maximally vague — no product, no category, no benefit, no urgency.
- Five competing CTAs (Shop Now, Explore Services, Discover Now, App Store, Google Play) with no priority hierarchy dilute focus.
- No personalization — no name, no category signal, no reference to what the subscriber has browsed or purchased.
- "TRANSFORM YOUR SHOPPING EXPERIENCE" is brand filler — 110-year history copy in a second email is a missed opportunity to show rather than tell.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tie back to the 15% offer.** A single banner — "Your 15% welcome offer expires soon — use it now" — would make this send transactional and justify the open.
- 2. **Collapse to one primary CTA.** Pick Shop Now or Discover Now, kill the rest, and give readers one clear path.
- 3. **Make the subject line do work.** "Peek into our world" is a placeholder — replace it with a category or a designer name.
- 4. **Lead with a product moment.** One hero product or editorial drop (new arrivals, a designer launch, a trend) gives the eye something to want before the brand copy.
- 5. **Move the app module to email 3 or 4** in the welcome series — it's a retention mechanic, not an acquisition hook, and it competes with the conversion goal here.

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Get a peek into our world"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard SFMC AMPscript-driven send with a table-based XHTML layout. Two actionable issues require attention before the next send: an AMPscript variable that may render as a null/empty string, and a nested `@media` query block that silently drops CSS rules in all clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text body is 87% URLs by character count (3,311 / 3,813 chars).**
QA flagged this as a deliverability risk. The plain-text alternative appears to be auto-generated from the HTML and is essentially a URL dump with minimal readable content. Spam filters (SpamAssassin `URI_ONLY` ruleset, Gmail heuristics) penalize this pattern.

**35 click-redirect tracking links present.**
All 35 were skipped by the HTTP probe — expected, since they require a live SFMC click-tracking session to resolve. No broken destination URLs can be confirmed or ruled out from this scan alone. Manual spot-check of 2–3 representative links in a staging send is recommended.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical: Nested `@media` query — invalid CSS, silently ignored.**

In the second `<style>` block, a `@media (max-device-width: 480px)` rule contains a nested `@media (max-device-width: 520px)` block:

```css
@media only screen and (max-device-width: 480px), screen and (max-width: 480px) {
  .ImgSize { ... }
  @media only screen and (max-device-width: 520px), screen and (max-width: 520px) {
    /* ← INVALID: nested @media is not parsed */
    .ImgSize2x2 { ... }
    .TxtSize2x2 { ... }
  }
}
```

CSS does not allow nested `@media` blocks. Every rule inside the inner block is silently discarded by all major email clients. The 2×2 image grid layout rules (`ImgSize2x2`, `TxtSize2x2`) will not apply on narrow viewports, likely causing fixed-width overflow on phones below 520px wide.

**Minor: Multiple commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags.**
Three alternate viewport configs are left in `<head>` as comments. These are harmless but indicate unresolved historical debugging and should be pruned.

**Minor: `@-webkit-keyframes bugfix` for iOS 9 hamburger.**
This no-op animation hack targets a rendering bug in iOS 9 (2015). iOS 9 has <0.1% market share. The rule is dead code.

**Accessibility: Cannot confirm `alt` text coverage.**
Source is truncated; `alt` attributes on `<img>` tags cannot be verified from this excerpt. Should be checked in full source — especially the hero image and product images, which often carry empty `alt=""` in NM templates.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**High: `@user` variable state is ambiguous — may render as empty string.**

The template opens with this comment block:

```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

The AMPscript variable comment block defines `@user` via a `Lookup()` call against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, but then the comment says "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW" and the SHA256 override is wrapped in an HTML comment (`<!-- /* ... */ -->`). AMPscript inside HTML comments is **not executed** in SFMC. If the Lookup scenario was also not activated (i.e., the correct AMPscript block was never uncommented), `@user` will be undefined/empty at send time.

The downstream risk depends on how `@user` is used — if it populates a tracking parameter or personalization token in a link, affected subscribers will receive a broken or untracked URL. The template should be inspected to confirm exactly one scenario block is active and not wrapped in HTML comments.

**Low: `@refid` defined as `CONCAT(@base_refid, "")` — effectively a no-op concat.**
This is benign but suggests a placeholder for a suffix that was never implemented. Worth cleaning up to avoid confusion in future template edits.

---

### 5. Compliance

**High: `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected by QA relay.**
QA finding: `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found`. As of February 2024, Google and Yahoo enforce `List-Unsubscribe` (RFC 2369) for bulk senders (>5,000/day). If this header is genuinely absent at the MTA level — not just undetected by the AgentMail relay — the send is out of compliance with both provider requirements and CAN-SPAM best practices.

Action: Confirm via SFMC send logs or a raw header dump from a test send whether `List-Unsubscribe` is present at the envelope level. SFMC generates this automatically for triggered/standard sends but it can be suppressed by misconfigured SAP or custom header overrides.

**High: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058).**
One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` method variant) is now required by Google's bulk sender policy. Its absence means Gmail users see a two-step unsubscribe flow, increasing spam complaints. SFMC supports RFC 8058 via account settings — verify it is enabled for this sending domain.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown.**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured by the relay. The sending domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`. Cannot confirm pass/fail from this scan. A raw header from a delivered copy (via Gmail "Show original") should show:

```
dkim=pass header.d=e.neimanmarcus.com
spf=pass
```

If either fails, deliverability and domain reputation are at risk.

**CAN-SPAM physical address: Cannot verify from truncated source.**
Footer content was cut off. Physical mailing address is required. Assumed present based on NM's standard template — confirm in full source.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Cannot fully verify UTM parameters** without resolving the 35 click-redirect URLs. Based on standard NM email practice, UTMs are typically embedded in the redirect destination, not the tracking URL itself. Spot-check recommended on:
- Hero CTA
- At least one product link
- Footer nav links

Verify that UTM values include `utm_medium=email`, a campaign-specific `utm_campaign` value matching the `@base_refid` / `@refid` token, and `utm_source=neimanmarcus` (or equivalent). Mismatched or missing UTMs break attribution in GA4/Adobe Analytics.
