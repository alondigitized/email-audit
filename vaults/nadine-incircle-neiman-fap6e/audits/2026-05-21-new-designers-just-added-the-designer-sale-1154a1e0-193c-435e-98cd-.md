# New designers just added: The Designer Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Designer Sale send in the current NM campaign wave — following the 5/18 launch (10/10), the 5/17 urgency email (9/10), and the 5/19 brand-spotlight edition (9/10). This iteration has a clear job: announce freshly added designers to re-engage anyone who already opened the earlier sends. It does that job competently. The crimson hero with "UP TO 50% OFF" is direct, the 9-brand grid answers the "who's in it" question immediately, and the eight category CTAs below give the subscriber multiple entry points.
- Where it falls short is the loyalty layer. The "CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF" block buried at the bottom is the most personalized, highest-converting offer in the whole email — but it's positioned after eight red CTA buttons and can easily go unseen on mobile. For a subscriber who already knows the Designer Sale exists, the 15% InCircle sign-up incentive is the actual news; burying it undercuts the email's best hook.
- Overall: a solid mid-campaign refresh send — not as sharp as the launch, but functional and on-brand. The new-designer angle is a legitimate reason to re-mail; execution just needs the loyalty offer surfaced higher.

## What's working

- Crimson hero with "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" is visually dominant — offer lands in under one second.
- Nine-brand grid (Jacquemus, Balmain, ALAIA, Burberry, Givenchy, Courreges, Cole Haan, Polo Ralph Lauren, Rag & Bone) is specific and recognizable; gives the subscriber a reason to click beyond generic "sale."
- Eight segmented CTA buttons (Women's Clothing, Men's, Shoes, Handbags, Dresses) lower friction by routing to the right sub-category rather than dumping everyone on the sale homepage.
- "LIMITED TIME" + "Save on these designer styles before they're gone" sustains urgency without screaming.
- No broken images, no lorem ipsum, no layout breakage visible in the render.

## What's weak

- The 15% InCircle sign-up offer is buried below eight red buttons — the highest-value hook is the last thing you see.
- "New designers just added" in the subject isn't reflected in the email body — no callout badge or label on the grid tells you which designers are actually new.
- Eight near-identical red CTA buttons cause decision fatigue; they visually flatten into a wall of red rather than guiding the eye.
- No price anchoring or example products — "up to 50% off" is the only quantified signal; no "$800 → $400" style proof point to make the discount feel real.
- No deadline or end-date anywhere in the visible copy — "LIMITED TIME" is vague after four days of Designer Sale emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the 15% InCircle block immediately below the hero.** For subscribers who've seen three prior Designer Sale emails, the new-member 15% is the actual news. Move it to position two — before the brand grid, not after the CTA stack.
- 2. **Badge the new designers.** Add a small "NEW" chip on the Jacquemus, Courreges, etc. tiles that were actually added in this send. The subject promises it; the body doesn't deliver it.
- 3. **Add one deadline.** Even "Ends Sunday" in the hero subhead lifts urgency meaningfully after a four-email campaign wave.
- 4. **Collapse the CTA stack.** Eight buttons is too many. Reduce to two or three priority lanes (Women's, Men's, Shoes + Handbags) and trust the brand grid to carry specific intent clicks.
- 5. **Add one price-anchor example** in the hero or just below it — e.g., "Jacquemus from $195" — to make "50% off" concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Jacquemus, ALAIA & more — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Designer Sale just got bigger — 50% off + 15% for new members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save before they're gone — plus claim your exclusive 15% off today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Courreges, Rag & Bone, Polo Ralph Lauren just added. Shop now.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "New Designers Just Added: The Designer Sale"

**Sender:** NeimanMarcus@e.neimanmarcus.com | **ESP:** Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC/ExactTarget)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The template is a standard SFMC AMPscript build with no hard failures, but carries four QA warnings that together degrade deliverability: missing one-click unsubscribe headers (a Gmail/Yahoo compliance requirement since Feb 2024), unverifiable authentication status at relay, and a plain-text part that is 87% URLs — a known spam-filter signal.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all route through a click-redirect domain (SFMC click-tracking), which is standard. However, unresolved links mean broken-destination coverage is zero for this send.

**`@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")`** — visible in the AMPscript header comment. Concatenating with an empty string literal is a no-op; this pattern typically appears when a suffix token was removed but the `CONCAT` wrapper was left behind. If `@base_refid` is unpopulated at send time, every tracking URL receives an empty `refid` parameter, silently dropping campaign attribution.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` lookups from `NM_CRM_Feed`** — no default/fallback values are visible in the truncated source. Empty lookups will inject blank values directly into link query strings (e.g., `&cmdid=`), corrupting analytics joins downstream.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<html>` tag missing `lang` attribute** — `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang="en"`. Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) cannot infer document language; WCAG 2.1 § 3.1.1 failure.

**`<title>Neiman Marcus </title>`** — trailing whitespace. Minor, but some preview-pane renderers surface the title text; a clean value costs nothing.

**Three commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` variants remain in `<head>`** — no functional impact, but signals unresolved template debt and inflates rendered source size.

**Global `p { text-align: center; }` rule** — any paragraph-level text in the template is force-centered. This is intentional for a promotional layout but will misalign any plain-prose content (e.g., legal disclaimer paragraphs) added in future iterations without an override class.

**Hamburger nav uses CSS checkbox hack** (`navbar-checkbox:checked`) — works in Apple Mail and some Android clients; renders as a static broken layout in Outlook (all versions) and Gmail App (Android). If desktop Outlook is a material share of the subscriber list, the mobile nav graceful-degradation path should be confirmed.

**No `role` or `aria-` attributes on layout tables** — not blocking, but screen readers will announce every layout table as a data table.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` variable — ambiguous active code path.** The AMPscript header shows two mutually exclusive options:

```
@user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
```
and immediately below (commented HTML, not AMPscript comment):
```
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->
```

The HTML comment wrapper does not suppress AMPscript execution — only `%%[ /* ... */ ]%%` does. If both blocks are present in the live template (not just the displayed comment), the SHA-256 assignment would overwrite the lookup value. Verification against the live SFMC template is required to confirm which value is active.

**`@emailid` double-definition comment** — the header documents `@emailid` as coming from the sendable DE *or* a fallback lookup, but no error-handling branch (`IIF`, `Empty()`) is visible. An absent `emailid` in the DE row would silently pass a null into any link or suppression logic that references it.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CAN-SPAM physical address | Not verifiable (source truncated) | Expected in footer — confirm present |
| Unsubscribe link in body | Not verifiable (source truncated) | Standard for NM; confirm present |
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | QA: header not captured at AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | QA: one-click unsubscribe not supported |
| SPF/DKIM authentication | **UNKNOWN** | QA: `Authentication-Results` not found at relay |

**`List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` absence is the most actionable issue here.** Google and Yahoo have required both headers for bulk senders (>5k/day) since February 2024. Missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` specifically means Gmail will not display its native one-click unsubscribe button, increasing spam complaints as users who cannot easily unsubscribe mark as spam instead.

**Authentication gap** — the `Authentication-Results` header absence at the AgentMail relay means SPF/DKIM pass/fail cannot be confirmed from this capture. `e.neimanmarcus.com` as the sending domain should have a valid DKIM signature from SFMC; this needs verification at the MTA layer, not just the relay.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM coverage unverifiable** — 26 click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. No UTM parameter inspection was possible. `@refid` (the primary tracking token) is at risk of being empty per the `CONCAT` no-op finding in §2.

**Landing page alignment** — cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain. If the Designer Sale landing page requires a login or personalization token passed via `@uEm`/`@cmdid` and those lookups return empty for a subset of subscribers, those users will land on a generic page or get a broken personalization experience.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**

1. **Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** to the SFMC send classification or sender profile. Without these, inbox providers will increasingly filter or flag the campaign.

2. **Verify `@user` code path** in the live SFMC template. Confirm whether the SHA-256 block is actually suppressed or if it overwrites the lookup assignment.

**P1 — Fix in next template revision:**

3. **Add fallback defaults to `@uEm`, `@cmdid`, and `@emailid` lookups** using `IIF(Empty(...), "default", ...)` or equivalent AMPscript guard. Empty values silently corrupt link parameters.

4. **Replace `CONCAT(@base_refid,"")` with plain `@base_refid`** and audit whether the refid value is actually being populated at send-time via the sendable DE.

5. **Confirm SPF/DKIM authentication** by inspecting a raw received header at the final MTA, not the AgentMail relay capture.

**P2 — Template hygiene:**

6. Remove the three commented-out `<meta viewport>` tags from `<head>`.
7. Add `lang="en"` to the `<html>` element.
8. Add a plain-text part that is not URL-only — the 87% URL ratio (5,315/6,089 chars) is a deliverability risk with content-based spam filters.
