# Ready to earn a $50–$500 gift card?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus finally breaking from the two dominant plays it's been running all week — the four-times-rerun "Extra 25% Off Last Call" hero and the string of editorial-lookbook resets (8/1, 8/2, 8/3). Instead this is a "Buy More, Earn More" tiered gift-card mechanic: spend $200/$500/$1,000/$2,000+ and earn a $50/$125/$250/$500 gift card, code AUGUST. The maroon hero with the spend/earn table is genuinely clear and easy to parse at a glance — better information design than most retailer tier promos.
- The problem is what's underneath: the mechanic only qualifies on regular-price merchandise (sale and reduced-price items are explicitly excluded), and there's no urgency or deadline visible anywhere above the fold — the 8/9/26 expiration only shows up buried in the legal block at the very bottom. For a shopper skimming the hero, this reads as "spend money, maybe get something back," not "act now." The nine-product grid below is well laid out but shows zero prices, so there's no way to gauge whether any single item gets you into gift-card territory without clicking through.
- Net: a welcome change of pace creatively, competently executed, but the offer itself is under-sold and the fine print undercuts the headline appeal. Call it a middle-of-the-pack send — better than the fourth Last Call rerun, but not a standout.

## What's working

- The tiered spend/earn table (SPEND $200/$500/$1,000/$2,000+ → EARN $50/$125/$250/$500) is genuinely legible and easy to scan.
- This is a real departure from the Last Call hero NM has rerun four times since 7/29 and the editorial resets of the last two weeks — cadence variety is a win.
- Clean 3x3 product grid spans dresses, denim, footwear, and handbags, with a logical follow-up row of category shop buttons (Women's, Shoes, Handbags, Men's, Home).
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- "SHOP & EARN" CTA button has strong white-on-maroon contrast and sits directly under the offer table.

## What's weak

- No urgency anywhere above the fold — "Limited Time" appears in tiny hero subtext but the actual 8/9/26 deadline is buried in body copy far below the fold.
- Sale and reduced-price items don't qualify toward the spend threshold, a meaningful exclusion that isn't disclosed until the fine print — this undercuts the "just spend and earn" framing in the hero.
- Zero pricing shown anywhere in the product grid, so a shopper can't tell how close any item gets them to a spend tier without clicking through.
- No loyalty/InCircle tie-in despite this being exactly the kind of promo where member perks would compound naturally.
- The hero offer is generic (any category, any spend) rather than tied to a specific product or persona focus, so there's nothing here that feels tailored.

## Recommendations

- 1. Surface the deadline in the hero itself ("Ends 8/9") — right now urgency only exists in fine print, and that's the single biggest lever for a promo mechanic like this.
- 2. Add price points under at least the grid images, so shoppers can mentally map "this item + two more = $500 tier."
- 3. Disclose the sale-exclusion up front in the hero or directly under the table, not just in the legal block — it changes the math on the offer and burying it reads as a soft bait-and-switch.
- 4. Tie the mechanic to InCircle status (e.g., "InCircle members earn 2x") to give loyalty members a reason to open over the generic version.
- 5. Lead the subject/preheader with the deadline to create real urgency at the inbox level.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $200, earn $50 — ends 8/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your gift card offer expires soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn up to $500 back on regular-price orders through 8/9`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty, fragrance, fashion — all qualify. Code AUGUST.`
