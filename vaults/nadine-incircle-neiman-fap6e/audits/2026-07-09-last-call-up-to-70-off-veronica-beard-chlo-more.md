# Last. Call. Up to 70% off Veronica Beard, Chloé & more
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus's second Last Call send in consecutive days — yesterday's "Want up to 70% off? Last Call starts now" launched the identical offer (extra 25% on select sale styles, up to 70% total). Today's email swaps in brand names in the subject line but delivers the same red hero block, the same headline math, and the same "SHOP SALE" CTA. The creative is technically clean: the red hero reads immediately, the nine-item product grid is well-shot, and the category strip at the bottom addresses multiple shopping intents without forcing everyone to the same landing page.
- The single most glaring problem: the subject name-drops Veronica Beard and Chloé — two brands that do not appear anywhere in the product grid. What you get instead is Akris, Stella McCartney, L'Agence, Versace, FRAME, MOTHER, A.L.C., Oscar de la Renta, and Joe's Jeans. That disconnect is a mild bait — it works once, but a subscriber who opens expecting to see Chloé and doesn't is quietly trained not to trust the next subject line.
- Cadence is the deeper issue. NM sent six emails in four days (July 6–9), including two on July 7 alone. A subscriber who opened yesterday's Last Call launch has no new information or incentive to click today. The "Last. Call." period-punctuation trick is doing a lot of work to create urgency that the body doesn't substantiate — there's no deadline date anywhere in the email.

## What's working

- Red hero block dominates immediately; the offer hierarchy (EXTRA 25% OFF → UP TO 70% → LAST CALL®) is legible and unambiguous.
- Nine-brand product grid covers a useful range of categories (dresses, shoes, bags, denim, outerwear) without feeling chaotic.
- Category CTAs at the bottom (SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP HANDBAGS, SHOP SHOES, SHOP WOMEN'S DRESSES, SHOP MEN'S) route different intents cleanly.
- "Exclusions Apply" IS called out under the hero CTA — small but present and honest.
- No render bugs; images load cleanly, typography is tight.

## What's weak

- Veronica Beard and Chloé are the subject hook but appear nowhere in the nine product slots — subject-to-body brand mismatch erodes trust.
- "Last Call" implies finality but zero deadline date is visible anywhere — pure urgency theater.
- This is the second consecutive day with the same red hero + same offer math; for a subscriber who opened yesterday, there is no new reason to click.
- "SHOP SALE" is a generic CTA that doesn't carry the brand-name specificity the subject line promised.
- No InCircle/loyalty callout — a missed retention signal for NM's most valuable segment.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Veronica Beard and Chloé in the product grid** or remove them from the subject. Showing the brands you name-drop is table stakes; the gap is the biggest trust issue in this send.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "3 days left" underneath the hero CTA would convert the "Last Call" branding into actionable urgency.
- 3. **Differentiate from yesterday's send** — new creative angle, new hero brand, or a countdown unit. A same-red-block same-math email two days running reads as a scheduling mistake.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA** from "SHOP SALE" to "SHOP CHLOÉ ON SALE" or "SHOP VERONICA BEARD SALE" to close the subject-to-body gap.
- 5. **Add an InCircle/loyalty card** — even a one-liner "InCircle members: your points apply" would lift relevance for a high-LTV segment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Chloé + Veronica Beard are on Last Call — extra 25% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 25% off Chloé, FRAME & 7 more — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your extra 25% on designer sale prices. Up to 70% total.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Chloé. Veronica Beard. MOTHER. Extra 25% off — 3 days left.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Last Call" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send using AMPscript lookups against two data extensions (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`). QA capture via the AgentMail relay is incomplete — authentication headers and List-Unsubscribe headers were not relayed, leaving compliance and deliverability status unverifiable from captured headers alone.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped** — all click-redirect domains (likely `click.e.neimanmarcus.com` or similar). Destination URLs not probed; broken destinations cannot be confirmed or ruled out.

**Plain-text body is 86.2% URLs** (5,225 of 6,058 chars). A plain-text part consisting almost entirely of redirect URLs with no readable prose is a known spam-filter signal and violates the spirit of RFC 2822 multipart/alternative.

No UTM parameter structure is visible in the truncated source, but it cannot be audited without resolving the tracked redirects.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Hamburger nav via CSS `:checked` hack** — the interactive mobile nav relies on `<input type="checkbox">` + `:checked~` sibling selectors. This pattern has broken or inconsistent behavior in Gmail app (Android/iOS), Outlook mobile, and Samsung Mail. It silently degrades to a collapsed/invisible state rather than a visible fallback.

**Overlapping breakpoints** — three separate media query blocks target `max-width: 600px`, `max-width/max-device-width: 520px`, and `max-width/max-device-width: 480px` in separate `<style>` blocks. Specificity collisions between `.ImgSize`, `.ImgSize2x2`, and the `*[class=...]` pattern are likely on devices in the 480–520px range.

**`*[class=X]` attribute selector pattern** — used pervasively for responsive rules. This pattern is incompatible with Outlook desktop (all versions), which ignores `<style>` blocks entirely and will render the desktop/wide layout regardless of screen width.

**Legacy iOS 9 animation hack** present (`@-webkit-keyframes bugfix` with `from/to { padding: 0 }`). iOS 9 is end-of-life; this is dead code that adds noise.

**Three viewport `<meta>` tags** — one active, two commented out (including a `width=320, initial-scale=0.29` variant and a `width=1024` variant). Commented-out meta tags are harmless but indicate template hygiene debt.

**`<title>` tag is `"Neiman Marcus "` with a trailing space** — minor, but visible in some clients (e.g., tab title in webmail preview).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Commented-out SHA-256 hash substitution:**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
This line is disabled. The active `@user` variable is set via an `EMAIL_ID` lookup from `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`. If any downstream link, pixel, or CDP handoff expects `@user` to carry a hashed email (for identity resolution or audience matching), this code path is silently broken. Needs confirmation from the template owner which value `@user` is supposed to carry.

**No-op `@refid` concatenation:**
```
@refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"")
```
Concatenating with an empty string is a no-op. This is likely a vestigial placeholder for a campaign suffix that was removed. If `@refid` is appended to tracking URLs, it will match `@base_refid` exactly — not an error, but confirms the suffix was intentionally dropped (or accidentally omitted).

**Dual lookup for `@emailid`** — the comment documents two scenarios for resolving `@emailid` (from sendable DE vs. subscriber lookup fallback). If the fallback is triggered and the lookup returns empty, any personalization block referencing `@emailid` will render blank or throw a runtime error in AMPscript. No null-guard is visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — flagged by QA as potentially not relayed. If this header is absent from the live send (not just the relay capture), this is a violation of Gmail's February 2024 bulk sender requirements for senders >5,000 messages/day. Neiman Marcus at scale almost certainly crosses this threshold.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for qualifying bulk senders. Absence forces recipients to a multi-step unsubscribe flow, which increases spam complaints.

**Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from the captured headers. The sending domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`; DMARC alignment should be verified against `neimanmarcus.com`. Cannot confirm from this capture.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not visible in the truncated source; cannot confirm presence. Must be verified in the full footer.
