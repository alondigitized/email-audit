# Our top picks from the Fall Style Sale are going fast: up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the Fall Style Sale again, and the subject and body don't agree. The subject promises "up to 40% off" and "top picks going fast." The hero says "UP TO 30% OFF," and the fine print also says 30%. A subscriber who opens on the 40% promise lands on a smaller number, which looks like bait-and-switch. This is the third Fall Style Sale send in three days, after the Oct 23 "up to 30% off starts now" and the Oct 24 gift-card and editorial sends.
- The layout itself is clean. The mauve hero, the big red "30% OFF" and the black SHOP NOW button put the offer first. Below it, a 3x3 grid of named designers (Dolce&Gabbana, Balmain, Polo Ralph Lauren) makes the "top picks" claim believable. The Saint Laurent triple-points band adds a loyalty hook.
- The grid products aren't labeled with prices or discounts, and nothing says what's "going fast." The copy implies scarcity but shows no proof of it. Overall it's a competent repeat send, undermined by the 40/30 mismatch.

## What's working

- The hero puts "30% OFF" in large red type on mauve, with a high-contrast black SHOP NOW button, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The 3x3 grid of designer names (Balmain, Canali, Stuart Weitzman) reads as a curated set of picks and shows brand and category variety.
- The black Saint Laurent "TRIPLE POINTS" block breaks up the page and gives members a second reason to click.
- The six category buttons (Women's Clothing, Handbags, Shoes and others) give easy next steps for different shoppers.
- The "ends 10/29" deadline in the fine print is credible, and the layout renders cleanly with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The subject says "up to 40% off" but the hero and fine print say "up to 30%". That is a visible contradiction.
- The grid products have no prices, sale tags or "% off" labels, so the discount isn't tied to any item.
- Nothing in the body supports "going fast," such as low-stock or selling-fast badges.
- The deadline (ends 10/29) appears only in the small fine print, not in the hero.
- The hero says "select regular-price items" with "Exclusions Apply," and the fine print excludes beauty, fragrance and sale items. The offer feels hedged.
- The copy is nearly identical to the Oct 23 "Fall Style Sale: up to 30% off starts now" send, so cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero match. Either say 30% everywhere, or put "40%" in the hero if some items really reach it.
- 2. Add "Ends Wed 10/29" next to the 30% in the hero for credible urgency.
- 3. Put sale prices or "was/now" labels under each grid item, and add a "Selling fast" tag or low-stock cue to a few of them.
- 4. Link each grid tile to its product page, and make the hero CTA specific, such as "Shop top picks."
- 5. Move the Saint Laurent points block higher, or segment it to InCircle members.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Selling fast: 9 Fall Style Sale picks, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balmain, Canali, Dolce&Gabbana and more. Sale ends Wednesday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus triple points on Saint Laurent with a $500 purchase.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`. Some mobile clients treat this as disabling pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Hamburger nav uses the checkbox hack:** it relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`, `.hamburger` and `.close` inside the `max-width: 600px` media query. Gmail and some other clients strip or ignore this pattern. Whether the nav has a fallback isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Comment before the doctype:** a multi-line HTML comment sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Most clients tolerate this, but content before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some renderers.
- **Weak `<title>`:** it is `Neiman Marcus ` with a trailing space and no descriptive text.
- **Not assessable:** alt text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and heading structure are all beyond the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation left in the output:** the opening comment is still in the rendered email. It reads `REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email) … Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …) … CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`.
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`).
