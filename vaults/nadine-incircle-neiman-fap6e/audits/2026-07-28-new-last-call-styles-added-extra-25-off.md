# New Last Call styles added + Extra 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the first genuinely new-merchandise-plus-discount send from Neiman Marcus in the recent stretch — a real departure from the editorial "no price, no urgency" resets on 7/23-7/27 and the repetitive "$500 off" ladder that ran three times before that. The hero is clean: red block, big "EXTRA 25% OFF," "up to 70% off," Last Call branding, one white CTA button, fine print underneath. It's the clearest single offer this brand has sent in this audit window.
- Below the fold, the nine-item designer grid (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Bugatchi, Stuart Weitzman, Theia, Manolo Blahnik, Cinq a Sept, AREA, Weekend Max Mara) is a nice touch — it makes the "styles added" half of the subject line concrete instead of abstract. But it's a generic browse grid, not a curated "new arrivals today" moment, and the six shop-by-category buttons at the bottom dilute rather than reinforce the one CTA that matters.
- Net: strong hero, competent execution, but this is still a Last Call clearance email wearing a "new styles" hook — nothing here signals recency (no "just added" date, no "new" badges on the tiles) beyond the subject line itself.

## What's working

- Single unambiguous offer stated three ways (Extra 25% off / up to 70% off / Last Call) with one clean white "SHOP SALE" button on the hero.
- Product grid gives visual proof of "styles added" — nine distinct designer names/items, no repeated stock photos.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, layout holds together top to bottom.
- Deadline is present and specific in the fine print (Ends 8/31/26 at 10:00 PM CT), which past sends in this thread often buried or omitted.

## What's weak

- Nothing on the product grid signals "new" — no "Just Added" ribbon, no date stamp, so the "New Last Call styles added" half of the subject is invisible in the render.
- Six near-identical red "SHOP [category]" buttons stacked below the grid compete with the one hero CTA and add scroll without adding decision-making value.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits directly under the CTA in the hero — a soft trust ding right at the moment of highest intent.
- No social proof, no sizing/fit info, no loyalty/InCircle callout anywhere in the render despite this being a members-heavy brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a small "NEW" or "Just Added" badge to at least the top row of product tiles — right now the grid could be lifted from any Last Call email; nothing visually backs the subject's headline claim.
- 2. Cut the six category buttons to two or three (e.g., Women's, Shoes, Handbags) or drop them entirely — one email, one job: get the click to "Shop Sale."
- 3. Move "Exclusions Apply" below the fold with the other fine print instead of directly under the CTA button.
- 4. Add an InCircle/loyalty line near the CTA if applicable — this audience has been shown pure sitewide-discount plays three times running; a members' perk would differentiate this send.
