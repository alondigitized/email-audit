# Limited time: Earn up to a $500 gift card
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus running the exact same gift-card mechanic it sent yesterday, 8/4 ("Ready to earn a $50–$500 gift card?," 7.00/10) — same maroon hero, same "BUY MORE, EARN MORE" headline, same $200/$500/$1,000/$2,000+ tier table paying out $50/$125/$250/$500, same "SHOP & EARN" button, same nine-item designer grid (Mac Duggal, Theia, FRAME, Alice + Olivia, Ferragamo, Jimmy Choo, Burberry, Manolo Blahnik, Amiri). The only meaningful change is the subject line, swapped from a question ("Ready to earn...?") to an urgency framing ("Limited time: Earn up to a $500 gift card").
- The creative itself is clean and legible: bold hero, an easy-to-scan spend/earn table, no render bugs. But this is now a two-day-old repeat of the same promotion with cosmetic subject-line churn, and it sits in a week that's already been dominated by reruns of the "Extra 25% Off Last Call" block (4+ times) and a string of near-identical editorial resets. A subscriber opening this after yesterday's near-twin will register it as the same email again.
- Judgement: competent execution, weak cadence. The mechanic and grid deserve credit for clarity; the lack of new creative or new subject substance the day after an identical send drags this down.

## What's working

- The spend/earn tier table is the clearest version of this offer NM has run — four columns, no ambiguity about what you get at each spend level.
- "SHOP & EARN" is a bold, high-contrast, unmissable single CTA in the hero.
- The nine-brand product grid (Mac Duggal through Amiri) spans dresses, denim, shoes, bags, and a men's shirt — decent category breadth in one glance.
- No render issues: images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together top to bottom.
- Deadline (8/9/26, code AUGUST) is stated, giving the "Limited time" subject some backing, even if it's buried in fine print.

## What's weak

- This is a same-week repeat of 8/4's identical gift-card creative — hero copy, table, button, and grid are unchanged; only the subject line was reworded.
- No prices or product names on any of the nine grid items — just designer names on plain grey backgrounds, so there's nothing to click toward specifically.
- "Includes Beauty & Fragrance" and "Exclusions Apply" sit right in the hero, hinting at restrictions before you've even seen the offer's fine print.
- No loyalty/InCircle tie-in despite this being exactly the kind of spend-threshold promo where member status usually gets called out.
- Six-button link farm (Shop Women's, Shop Women's Clothing, Shop Shoes, Shop Men's Clothing, Shop Handbags, Shop Home) dilutes the single-CTA hero — it's a wall of near-duplicate options.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend the identical creative two days running — if the gift-card promo is still live, refresh the hero art or grid selection so it doesn't read as a copy-paste of 8/4.
- 2. Add a price or "From $X" under at least the hero product callouts so the grid isn't just designer-name wallpaper.
- 3. Collapse the six-button footer grid to 2-3 buttons max — one for Women's, one for Men's, one for Sale — the current six create decision paralysis.
- 4. Surface the 8/9 deadline in the hero itself (e.g., "Ends 8/9") instead of only in the fine print, to make the "Limited time" subject line actually true at a glance.
