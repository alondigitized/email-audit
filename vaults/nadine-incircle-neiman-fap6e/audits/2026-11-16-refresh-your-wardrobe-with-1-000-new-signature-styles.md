# Refresh your wardrobe with 1,000+ new signature styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, tasteful new-arrivals catalog send, and it has no offer. A black hero reads "BRAND NEW STYLES / JUST LANDED" with two small underlined text links, "Shop Women's" and "Shop Men's". Below it sit two 3x2 product grids, one for women and one for men. Each tile has a brand name caption and there are no prices.
- This is Neiman's at its quietest. After the 11/16 Designer Sale launch (up to 50% off), the 11/15 early-access last call (40% off) and the 11/14 "Ends today: up to $650 off", a no-price arrivals email feels like a palate cleanser. It works as brand-building, like the Balmain and Versace editorial sends on 11/13 and 11/14. It does little to drive a click today.
- The subject promises "1,000+ new signature styles", but the body shows twelve. The hero CTAs are the weakest part: tiny underlined text links on black, not buttons. The real buttons sit far below the fold. Verdict: polished and on-brand, but low-urgency and low-conversion.

## What's working

- The brand-name captions (Valentino Garavani, COACH, Saint Laurent, Theory, MOTHER) give a recognizable luxury signal at a glance.
- The women's and men's sections are clearly split, each with its own outline "Shop All…New Arrivals" button.
- The product shots are consistent: clean grey backgrounds, even sizing and a tidy grid.
- The mix is seasonal and fits mid-November: a suede jacket, a plaid coat, boots, gloves and a bomber.
- There are no render bugs. Images load, and the layout and nav are clean.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, or member benefit anywhere, so there is no reason to act today.
- The hero CTAs are small underlined text links on black, easy to miss and not button-styled.
- The hero is a lot of black space for a headline that says little. The "just landed" message could be shown with product.
- The subject's "1,000+" claim is never supported in the body. Twelve unpriced products with no deep links to a collection feel thin.
- It lands the same day as the Designer Sale launch with no mention of the sale. That is a missed cross-promotion and a mixed signal for subscribers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a hero product or model image in the hero and replace the text links with real buttons ("Shop Women's New Arrivals", "Shop Men's New Arrivals"). This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Add prices or "from $" cues under the product names. Pricing is the missing detail that drives clicks.
- 3. Add a one-line tie-in to the Designer Sale or a member benefit, such as free shipping, so the send has a reason to be opened today.
- 4. Make the "1,000+" claim real: add a tile or banner like "1,000+ new arrivals this week" that links to a collection.
- 5. Add a "New" badge or the product name on each tile so shoppers know what they are clicking.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style template with a responsive, hamburger-nav layout. It has no hard QA failures, but it has real code-level problems: an internal ESP comment ahead of the DOCTYPE, a zoom-blocking viewport, and no `lang` attribute. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **30 tracking links not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is 87% URLs** (5693 of 6527 chars). The text alternative is mostly raw tracking URLs, which raises spam-filter risk and makes it unreadable as plain text.
- **No hrefs appear in the truncated portion**, so I can't assess individual link formatting.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on supporting clients.
- **No document language.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** The large `<!--REQUIRED variables…-->` block precedes `<!DOCTYPE>`. Content before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients.
- **Brittle selectors.** The exact-match selectors `*[class=contentInteractive]`, `*[class=hide]` and `*[class=mobile]` fail on elements with more than one class.
- **Checkbox hamburger nav.** It relies on `.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu` (`:checked` sibling combinators). This is unsupported in Gmail and most Outlook clients. The truncated source doesn't show whether a non-interactive fallback exists, so I can't confirm this one.
- **Duplicate `width` declarations** inside `*[class].show` (`width: auto !important` and `width: 278px`) are harmless but sloppy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template metadata is shipped in the HTML.** The comment lists `@email`, `@base_refid`, `@refid`, `@emailid`, `@uEm` and `@cmdid`. It also names the data extensions `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated` and `NM_CRM_Feed`, and the fields `EMAIL_ID`, `EMAIL_ADDRESS`, `uEm` and `cmdid`. Anyone viewing source can read these, and it shows unresolved authoring notes ("CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW").
- **Unbalanced parentheses** appear in the `Lookup(...)` examples in that comment, which suggests it was copied in unedited.
- **No visible unrendered tokens.** I saw no `%%…%%` or `@var` leakage in the visible markup, but the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 can't be confirmed, and it is expected by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.neimanmarcus.com` are unknown.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical address and unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 30 tracking links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source. The comment suggests identifiers (`@refid`, `@cmdid`, `@uEm`) are carried in the links, but that's inferred, not confirmed.
