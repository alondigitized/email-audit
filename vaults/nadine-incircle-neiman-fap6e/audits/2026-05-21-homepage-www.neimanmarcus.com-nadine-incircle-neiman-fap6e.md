# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is embarrassing — and not for me. I opened Neiman Marcus on my phone and instead of their homepage I got a bot-block wall telling me my "activity" looks suspicious. I'm a 51-year-old InCircle member trying to browse before my stylist appointment and this is what greets me. That's a Neiman Marcus logo at the top and bottom of what is essentially a "go away" sign.

## What's working

- **Brand logo is present** — both top and bottom. At least I know whose wall I ran into.
- **"Submit feedback" link** — at least there's *some* path forward, even if it shouldn't be needed.

## What's weak

- **The entire page is a locked door.** An InCircle member who visits twice a season just got treated like a scraper bot. There is zero content, zero product, zero brand expression.
- **The IP callout is tin-eared.** Showing me my raw IP address (`75.140.9.0`) in the block message is technically transparent but completely off-brand for a luxury retailer. Chanel doesn't flash your network data at you.
- **No graceful recovery path.** The only option is "Submit feedback" — there's no "Prove you're human," no CAPTCHA, no "Try again," no redirect to the app. I'm just told to go away.
- **The tone is cold and clinical.** "Unusual activity from your device or network" with a bulleted list of reasons reads like a bank error, not a Neiman Marcus experience.
- **Both NM logos feel ironic** at this point — the brand is literally bookending a message that tells me I'm not welcome.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a CAPTCHA or light verification instead of a hard block.** If you must stop me, let me tap "I'm not a robot" and move on. A friction-check is forgivable; a wall is not.
- **Strip the IP address from the user-facing message.** Replace it with something warm: "We noticed something unusual. Please verify you're you — we'll have you back in a moment."
- **Add an app download or in-store stylist CTA to the block page.** If you're going to hold me here for even ten seconds, give me something to tap. "Open in the NM App" or "Call your stylist" would save the moment.
- **Route recognized InCircle accounts past bot-detection.** A logged-in loyalty member should not hit this wall — cookie or session data should signal that I'm a real customer.
