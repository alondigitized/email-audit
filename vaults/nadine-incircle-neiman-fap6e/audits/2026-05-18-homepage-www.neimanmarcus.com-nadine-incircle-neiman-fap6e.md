# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened NeimanMarcus.com on my phone and was immediately stopped cold — not by a campaign, not by a hero image, not by a sale banner, but by a security block wall telling me my device triggered a bot alert. I can see the Neiman Marcus wordmark, a white box with an error message, and absolutely nothing else. There is no homepage to review — only a dead end.

## What's working

- The Neiman Marcus wordmark is clean and centered — brand recognition at least registers before the block hits.
- The "Submit feedback" link is present, so there is at least one escape hatch for a real customer who got incorrectly flagged.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is absent — I am looking at a bot-detection interstitial, not a luxury retail experience. For an InCircle member spending in the five figures annually, this is a brand-damaging first impression.
- The error copy is clinical and cold: "Automated (bot) activity on your network" — accusing your customer of being a bot is not a luxury-brand tone.
- The IP address is exposed in plain text on-screen (`75.140.9.0`) — unnecessary and slightly alarming to a non-technical shopper.
- The "Reasons may include" bulleted list reads like a tech support doc, not anything close to the NM Daily editorial voice I'm used to.
- No soft-landing option: no "Try again" button, no alternate path to browse, no phone number for an InCircle concierge.

## Recommendations

- **Humanize the block page immediately** — replace "Automated (bot) activity" with "We had trouble loading your page" and offer a single "Try Again" button, styled in NM black-and-white, not a plain hyperlink.
- **Surface InCircle concierge contact on this page** — if a loyal member hits this wall, the fallback should be a white-glove option ("Call your stylist at 1-800-XXX-XXXX"), not a generic feedback form.
- **Remove the raw IP address from the visible UI** — it means nothing to a customer and reads as surveillance-adjacent, which is not the relationship NM wants with its top-tier members.
- **Suppress this block for authenticated InCircle members or known devices** — if I'm logged in with my InCircle credentials, the bot-detection threshold should be calibrated so a legitimate member browsing on mobile never sees this.
