# The Roundup: Christian Louboutin Shoes
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- This is NM's "The Roundup" editorial format — a clean, no-discount brand spotlight on Christian Louboutin shoes. Six product shots in a 2×3 grid, minimal copy, and two CTA buttons. It's competently assembled but arrives on June 19, the same day NM already fired the "Designer Sale upgrade: now up to 60% off" escalation (scored 9/10), which means this lands as a second send in one inbox on the same day — a cadence misfire.
- The editorial beats are fine: the hero sandal shot reads summer, the product selection spans styles from strappy kitten heels to platform wedges, and the NM visual language is intact. But there's not a single price, percentage, or urgency signal anywhere in the body. On a day when NM has already led with "up to 60% off," a zero-offer brand spotlight is a tough ask for a click.
- Verdict: a competent filler send that would work better if it appeared on a standalone day, or if it tied into the ongoing Designer Sale (these could be the 60% off Louboutins — the email doesn't say so).

## What's working

- **Brand specificity is the strongest card.** "Christian Louboutin Shoes" in the subject names one of the most recognizable luxury footwear brands — that alone is a qualified filter for the right subscriber.
- **Product variety in the grid.** Six distinct silhouettes — strappy kitten heel, classic pump, ankle-wrap flat, woven mule, stiletto, platform wedge — gives the scan a reason to linger.
- **Clean render, no layout breakage.** The 2×3 product grid is tight, white space is controlled, no broken images visible.
- **Two entry points at the top.** "Shop Christian Louboutin" and "Shop Shoes" let intent-matched visitors self-select; not redundant.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing or offer.** Not a single dollar, percentage, or "on sale" label anywhere. After the June 18–19 Designer Sale cadence, subscribers expect offers; this delivers none.
- **Second send, same day.** NM already sent the Designer Sale escalation on June 19. Two sends in one inbox slot dilutes both and trains unsubscribes.
- **Hero image doesn't tease the grid.** The hero shows one flat-laid sandal; the grid below has six very different products including pumps and platforms. The hero promises one story, the body delivers a different one.
- **"Shop Designer Collections"** at the bottom is too generic — it undermines the brand-specific framing set up by the headline.
- **No social proof or editorial hook.** No "our editors pick," no stylist note, no "best seller" badge — nothing to explain *why* this curation exists or why now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tie it to the ongoing sale.** If any of these Louboutins are in the Designer Sale, say so: "Starting at $X" or "Up to 60% off, including Louboutin." That one change aligns this email with the week's active campaign and gives subscribers a reason to act.
- 2. **Move this email to its own day.** The June 19 cadence (two sends) is the single most damaging problem. A brand-spotlight on a clear inbox day would score 1–2 points higher on every metric.
- 3. **Replace the bottom CTA.** Swap "Shop Designer Collections" for "Shop All Louboutin Styles" to stay brand-specific to the end.
- 4. **Add editorial framing in the hero.** One sentence — "NM editors' summer picks from Christian Louboutin" — gives the curation a reason to exist and makes this feel less like a catalog blast.
