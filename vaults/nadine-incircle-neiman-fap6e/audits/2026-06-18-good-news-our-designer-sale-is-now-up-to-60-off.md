# Good news: Our Designer Sale is now up to 60% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- The June 18 Designer Sale escalation is structurally confident: a bold red hero announces "UP TO 60% OFF," nine luxury-brand product shots fill the grid, and six category CTAs spread the click surface. After three consecutive days (June 15–17) where NM stepped away from the sale drumbeat — pivoting through spend-to-save, editorial Social Whirl, and the beauty event — this email returns to sale mode with a clear bid step: the Extra 20% Off framing that ran June 9–14 is now retired in favor of a headline discount. The "Good news:" opener earns its place precisely because it signals an escalation, not a repeat.
- The execution is clean. Red hero → product grid → CTAs → footer, in that order. The visual hierarchy holds on first glance and the six category buttons (clothing, shoes, handbags, dresses, men's) give shoppers an immediate path without forcing them through a generic sale page. No render bugs, no broken images, no clutter.
- The meaningful miss is the absent InCircle layer. The June 15 email that scored 9/10 combined a headline discount with triple points and felt like a member reward — this email is generic. No loyalty tie-in, no hard deadline, no price anchors on any of the nine products. "Before they're gone" isn't credible urgency when this sale has been running since June 9.

## What's working

- **Red hero is bold and immediately readable** — "THE DESIGNER SALE / UP TO 60% OFF / SHOP SALE" lands the offer in under a second.
- **Six category CTAs expand the click surface** — SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOES, HANDBAGS, DRESSES, MEN'S, MEN'S SHOES routes each shopper to the right lane rather than dumping everyone on a generic sale page.
- **Brand mix in the product grid signals genuine luxury** — Jacquemus, Chloé, Valentino Garavani, Stella McCartney, Polo Ralph Lauren — not off-brand names filling space.
- **"Exclusions Apply" is disclosed upfront** — right under the hero CTA, not buried in the fine print footer.
- **The "Good news:" opener reads like a genuine escalation** — it differentiates this send from the June 9–14 wave rather than repeating the same headline.

## What's weak

- **No InCircle benefits** — the June 15 send earned its 9 by layering triple points on top of the discount; this email leaves loyal members with nothing extra.
- **No credible deadline** — "before they're gone" is soft copy on a sale that has been running for nine days; it doesn't hold up.
- **SMS sign-up footer competes with the primary CTA** — "CLAIM YOUR 15% OFF" mid-sale-email is list-building noise at the wrong moment, diluting the send's urgency.
- **No price anchors on any product** — not one "Was $1,200 → $480" to make "up to 60% off" feel real rather than a headline hedge.
- **Flat product photography has no aspiration pull** — nine items on white backgrounds function as a grid, not a story; compare with the editorial atmosphere of the June 16 Social Whirl send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or a visible 48-hour window turns "before they're gone" into real urgency and gives fence-sitters a reason to act now vs. later.
- 2. **Bring back InCircle** — even one line ("InCircle members earn points on all sale purchases") reconnects the loyalty mechanic that made June 15 the strongest send of the month.
- 3. **Show one before/after price on the hero product** — anchoring even a single item at "Was $1,450 → $580" validates the 60% claim and stops "up to" from feeling like bait-and-switch.
- 4. **Cut or defer the SMS sign-up footer** — move it to a standalone list-building email; running it alongside a sale CTA bleeds the moment.
- 5. **Sharpen the subject line** — "Good news:" is warm but overused across retail; the actual news (sale just deepened) is strong enough to lead without the filler opener.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Designer Sale just hit 60% off — shop before it ends`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off Chloé, Valentino, Jacquemus and more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns added — your size may still be in.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Sunday. Shop 60% off designer before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit — Neiman Marcus "Designer Sale up to 60% off"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript) template with standard NM infrastructure. No critical rendering bugs detected in the partial source, but four QA warnings warrant attention — primarily around authentication header visibility via the AgentMail relay and one-click unsubscribe compliance.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**26 tracking links skipped by QA probe**
All click-tracking links routed through a redirect domain (likely `click.e.neimanmarcus.com`) were not HTTP-probed. Destination URLs and 404 status are unverified.
- *Risk:* Sale landing pages may point to incorrect category pages or non-sale URLs if the campaign calendar `@refid`/`@base_refid` variables weren't populated correctly before send.
- *Action:* Manually spot-check 3–5 tracked links against the live sale URL and confirm they resolve to the correct Designer Sale category with the expected discount depth (60% off).

**Plain-text version is 70%+ URLs (5,397 of 6,177 chars)**
The plain-text alternative is essentially a URL dump, not human-readable content. This degrades spam filter scoring and is a poor fallback for accessibility clients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Multiple commented-out `<meta name="viewport">` tags**
Three conflicting viewport meta declarations exist in the `<head>`, all commented out except one:
```html
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no" />-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=320, initial-scale=0.29">-->
<!--<meta name="viewport" content="width=1024">-->
```
These are harmless while commented, but indicate historical viewport thrashing. No active rendering issue, but dead code worth pruning.

**`<title>` has a trailing space:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` — minor, no functional impact.

**Media query breakpoint inconsistency**
Two separate `<style>` blocks define responsive breakpoints at different widths: `max-width: 600px` (hamburger nav, hide/show) and `max-width: 480px` / `max-width: 520px` (product grid). This is intentional for different layout zones but creates a 480–600px gap where product grid rules don't fire while nav rules do. Confirm 3-up product grid renders correctly at 500px on iOS.

**No `role="presentation"` on layout tables**
Standard table-based email layout; no accessibility roles. Screen readers will announce layout tables as data tables. Low severity for a promotional email but worth flagging for WCAG audit posture.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`@user` variable assignment inconsistency**
The AMPscript comment block documents two conflicting assignment methods:
```
@user = Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)
/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */  ← commented out but present
```
The hashed-email path is disabled via comment (`<!--/* SET @user = ... */-->`), meaning `@user` is the raw `EMAIL_ID` from the subscriber DE. If `@user` is passed to any URL parameter (e.g., for personalization or analytics identity), confirm the non-hashed value is intentional and complies with NM's data-sharing policy with analytics/ad pixels.

**`@uEm` and `@cmdid` sourced from `NM_CRM_Feed`**
Both fields use a Lookup against CRM feed. If a subscriber doesn't exist in `NM_CRM_Feed`, these will return empty strings. Verify downstream usage (likely loyalty personalization or suppression logic) handles null gracefully.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** *(QA WARN)*
The header may exist on the actual SMTP envelope but was not captured through the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM / Gmail/Yahoo sender compliance without direct header inspection.
- *Evidence:* `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found: Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay`

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** *(QA WARN)*
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) could not be confirmed.
- *Evidence:* `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)`
- *Action:* Pull raw headers from a seed inbox (e.g., Gmail) for this send and confirm both headers are present. SFMC should be injecting these automatically for commercial sends; absence in the relay capture is likely an instrumentation gap, not a true miss — but must be verified.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** *(QA WARN)*
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unverifiable from relay capture.
- *Evidence:* `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found: Expected via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown`
- *Action:* Same seed inbox check — confirm SPF and DKIM both pass for `e.neimanmarcus.com`. A DMARC failure here would suppress delivery at Yahoo/Google.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

No UTM parameters are directly visible in the truncated HTML source. With 26 redirect-tracked links, UTM attribution is presumably appended either by SFMC's click-tracking wrapper or hard-coded in destination URLs before redirect.

- **Risk:** If UTMs are embedded in the pre-redirect destination URL (inside the tracking link), they survive. If they're injected by the redirect service and the redirect service fails or misconfigures the campaign name, GA4 will attribute sessions as direct.
- **Action:** Decode one click-tracking URL and confirm the final destination includes `utm_campaign`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_source` values matching the campaign calendar `@refid` (`@base_refid`). Confirm the Designer Sale landing page is live and shows ≥60% off pricing (subject line claim).
