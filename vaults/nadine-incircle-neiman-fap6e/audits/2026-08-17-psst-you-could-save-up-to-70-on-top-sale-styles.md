# Psst... you could save up to 70% on top sale styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red Last Call hero Neiman Marcus has been running all week, now stacking an "Extra 25% Off" on top of "up to 70% off" — near-identical to the 8/13 send that scored 7.00/10. The "Psst..." subject is the one genuinely new beat here, a whisper-hook trying to make a fourth-plus outing of the same creative feel like an insider tip rather than a repeat.
- Execution-wise it's clean: bold hero, unambiguous white "SHOP SALE" button, a nine-item designer grid (Isabel Marant, Balmain, Manolo Blahnik, Theia, AREA) that gives real texture below the fold, and category CTA buttons for anyone who bounces off the grid. But this is cadence fatigue territory — the same red block, same "LAST CALL" wordmark, same "Exclusions Apply" fine print in tiny type under the button, just with the discount math shuffled. A subscriber who opened the 8/13 or 8/15 Last Call emails has seen this exact hero.
- Judgement: solid execution of a stale playbook. The subject line is doing more work than the creative to earn this open.

## What's working

- Clear, unmissable hero: "EXTRA 25% OFF" in huge white type on red, readable at a glance.
- Single unambiguous CTA — white "SHOP SALE" button pops against the red field.
- Nine-item merchandising grid below the fold gives concrete product variety (bags, dresses, shoes, menswear) rather than just the hero.
- Secondary category buttons (Shop Women's Clothing, Shop Handbags, Shop Kids, etc.) give a second path to purchase for browsers who don't want "Sale" broadly.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is crisp, layout holds together top to bottom.

## What's weak

- This is at minimum the third time this week the red Last Call/"Extra 25%" hero has run (8/13, 8/15, now 8/17) — visually indistinguishable from the prior sends.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits in tiny type directly under the CTA — the one piece of friction the brand chose to put closest to the button.
- No urgency signal in the hero itself; the real deadline ("Ends 9/7/26 at 10:00 PM CT") is buried in dense legal copy at the very bottom, not up top where it'd drive action.
- The nine-item grid has zero prices, sizes, or "shop this" links visible — it's inspiration, not a shoppable path, which undercuts the specific-offer momentum from the hero.
- "Psst..." as a subject device is a stretch — it doesn't attach to anything visible in the creative; the whisper conceit and the loud red sale banner are tonally mismatched.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire this exact hero for at least one cycle — same red block, wordmark, and layout have run 3+ times in under a week; even loyal openers will start pattern-matching it as "another Last Call" and skip.
- 2. Move the 9/7 deadline into the hero as a visible line ("Ends 9/7") — real urgency currently only lives in 8pt legal copy at the bottom.
- 3. Add prices or "Shop the Look" links under at least the top row of the product grid — right now it's a mood board, not a merchandising path.
- 4. Drop or soften "Psst..." — it promises something intimate/exclusive that the loud red banner doesn't deliver; align the whisper conceit with an actual insider mechanic (early access, code) or cut it.
