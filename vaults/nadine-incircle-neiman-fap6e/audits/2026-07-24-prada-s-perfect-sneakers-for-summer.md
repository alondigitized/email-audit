# Prada’s perfect sneakers for summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Neiman Marcus going full editorial — no offer, no markdown, just Prada sneakers in a clean brand spotlight. After a punishing promo stretch (Yesterday's "EXTRA 25% OFF" stack, the day-before Last Call Sale, and a five-day InCircle urgency ladder before that), the pivot to a product-category send makes sequencing sense. The execution is tighter than their last editorial attempt ("Fashion's power pieces," 7/22, 4/10 here) — a named luxury brand and a specific category replace the vague "power pieces" frame.
- That said, this is fundamentally a catalog email in editorial clothing. There is no price anchor, no reason to click today versus tomorrow, and no occasion hook that earns the feature. The garden-party dress email (7/21, 9/10) worked because it told you *when* and *why* to wear the thing. This one just says the thing exists.
- The email is competently made but unlikely to convert at the level NM needs after training their subscriber base on 25–70% off sends. The primary sin is asking for the click with no value on the table.

## What's working

- **Brand-name subject line lands clearly** — "Prada's perfect sneakers for summer" tells you exactly what's inside; no bait-and-switch.
- **Hero lifestyle image is strong** — white lug-sole sneakers with white socks, bright and season-appropriate; eye lands on the product.
- **Dual-gender split is smart** — separate Women's and Men's Prada sections broaden the send's addressable audience without cluttering the hero.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — logo → nav → hero image → "Make a Run for Them" → CTA → secondary modules; no confusion about where to look.
- **No render bugs** — images load, buttons render, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- **Zero price signal** — after a week of 25–70% off emails, there is no "from $X" anchor, no member pricing callout, nothing to justify opening your wallet today.
- **"Make a Run for Them" is cute but inert** — the pun lands, but it communicates no value. Compare to "Tick. Tock." (7/23, 9/10) which was also wordplay but with stakes attached.
- **Three CTAs dilute focus** — "Shop Prada," "Shop Men's Prada," and "Shop Women's Prada" split attention without hierarchy. One of these should be primary.
- **No InCircle / loyalty hook** — NM spent five days activating their loyalty base; this email ignores them entirely, breaking the continuity they built.
- **No occasion or need frame** — the garden-party dress email (7/21) earned its editorial status by telling you *when* to wear the product. Sneakers for "summer" is too broad to manufacture desire.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price anchor in the hero** — even "Women's styles from $650" beside the CTA gives subscribers a reason to click now versus closing the tab.
- 2. **Inject an occasion hook** — "The sneaker you'll wear from the airport to the aperitivo" is more evocative than "walk-for-miles comfort." Occasion frames convert; comfort copy doesn't.
- 3. **Consolidate to one hero CTA, split below the fold** — lead with the women's hero (larger subscriber segment), drop the men's module to the second scroll.
- 4. **Add an InCircle callout** — "InCircle members: earn points on Prada" is a free conversion lever sitting unused given the loyalty activation of the past week.
- 5. **Urgency signal** — even "Summer colors, limited availability" is better than the implicit "always here, click whenever."

### 1. Technical Summary

The send has two compliance gaps that violate Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements (missing `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers), and the HTML source exposes raw AMPscript scaffolding comments — including internal data extension names and a development instruction — that should not survive to production.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**32 tracking links — no probe coverage**
All click URLs resolve through a redirect domain (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / ExactTarget). The QA probe skipped all 32, so broken-destination detection has zero coverage for this send. Any dead product URLs would go undetected.

**Plain-text body is 85% URLs**
QA measured 2,792 of 3,281 plain-text characters as URLs. A plain-text alternative that is nearly all redirect strings is a spam-filter signal and renders unreadably in plain-text clients (Apple Mail plain-text mode, corporate mail readers that strip HTML).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPscript scaffolding comment block in production HTML source**
The full variable setup block survives as an HTML comment visible to anyone who views source:
```html
<!-- REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... @refid ... @user Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", ...) @emailid ... @uEm = Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) @cmdid Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "cmdid", ...) -->
```
This leaks internal data extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`) and field names (`uEm`, `cmdid`, `EMAIL_ID`). AMPscript comments (`%%[ /* ... */ ]%%`) are stripped by the ESP; plain HTML comments are not.

**Development instruction visible in sent HTML**
```html
<!-- CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW -->
```
This is a template author note that should be removed before send. Its presence confirms the AMPscript comment block was not reviewed prior to deployment.

**Commented-out identity assignment**
```html
<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */-->
```
An alternative user-identity strategy is commented out inline. The active path performs a lookup against `NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`. No functional defect, but the dead code and leakage of the SHA256 hashing approach should be cleaned.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** *(QA WARN)*
Google and Yahoo require this header for all bulk senders (≥5,000/day) as of February 2024. Its absence risks inbox demotion or rejection at Gmail/Yahoo.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** *(QA WARN — RFC 8058)*
One-click unsubscribe (`POST` method) is required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` under the same 2024 mandates. Without it, Gmail renders a less-prominent unsubscribe path and may enforce its own suppression mechanism.

**Note:** Both headers may be present at the MTA level and simply not captured by the AgentMail relay. Confirm against raw SMTP headers from a seed inbox (e.g., Gmail `Show original`) before treating this as a confirmed gap.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** *(QA WARN)*
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown through the relay. Verify from a seed send that `e.neimanmarcus.com` is aligned with the DKIM signing domain and that SPF includes the ESP's sending IP range. A DMARC failure here would compound the `List-Unsubscribe` risk.
