# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This still looks like my Neimans — clean, elegant, that unmistakable script logo — and they're leading with Last Call, which is exactly when I splurge. But the second I land, a "Would you like 15% Off?" pop-up plants itself right in the middle of the page and half-covers the actual 25% off banner underneath it, so my first instinct is to find the "X" or tap "NO THANKS" before I even see what they're actually selling.

## What's working

- **"LAST CALL®: EXTRA 25% OFF SALE STYLES"** ticker at the very top — this is the one event I actually plan my splurging around, so it grabs me immediately.
- **The red Last Call banner** with "select sale styles for up to 70% off" and the two clean buttons, SHOP WOMEN'S and SHOP MEN'S — no guessing where to tap.
- **The "Wide Appeal" handbag editorial** ("Presenting the season's most covetable carries") — that's the kind of NM Daily-style copy I'd actually stop and read, and the "Shop Designer Collections" link speaks directly to how I shop.

## What's weak

- **The "Would you like 15% Off?" pop-up** — it's sitting directly on top of the 25% off banner I already care about, and 15% is a worse deal than what's already running. It reads like a generic email-capture box, not something tailored to an InCircle member.
- **No InCircle presence anywhere visible** — I'm a member of the stylist program and I see twice a season, and there's nothing here — no rewards balance, no member badge, no acknowledgment I already have a relationship with this store.
- **The hero image is cropped at the neck** — just a torso holding a bag. I can't tell if this woman is dressed for someone my age or a 28-year-old's idea of "classic," which makes it hard to feel spoken to.

## Recommendations

- Kill the generic 15%-off pop-up or replace it with something InCircle-specific — a member sign-in prompt with my rewards balance would land far better than a discount lower than what's already advertised.
- Pull the hero image back to show more of the outfit and a face — right now it's cropped so tight it could be anyone.
- Surface an InCircle badge or "Member Benefits" strip above the fold — I want to see that this store still remembers I'm loyal, not just that there's a sale on.
- Swap "Wide Appeal" for copy that nods to the stylist program or personal styling — something like "Book Your Stylist" alongside the handbag shop links.
