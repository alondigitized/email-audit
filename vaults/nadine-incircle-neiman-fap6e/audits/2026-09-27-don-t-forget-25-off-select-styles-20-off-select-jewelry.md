# Don’t forget: 25% off select styles + 20% off select jewelry
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Friends & Family 25%-off mechanic Neiman Marcus has now run repeatedly this week — matching 09-26's "Have you shopped 25% off?" (7.00/10) — with a fresh headline tweak ("Don't forget") and the addition of a 20% jewelry callout. The hero is confident and on-brand: bold green field, oversized "FRIENDS & FAMILY" wordmark, a clean single CTA, and a legible InCircle double-points banner. Below the fold it's the standard nine-product grid spanning categories (dresses, outerwear, denim, handbags, shoes, menswear).
- The execution is clean — no render bugs, good visual hierarchy, a recognizable sender — but there's nothing here that separates it from the four-plus near-identical Friends & Family sends this week. "Don't forget" as a subject opener is a soft reminder play, not a hook, and the jewelry addition is buried as a small subhead rather than its own moment.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand, but fatigued creative running on its fourth-plus lap this week with diminishing novelty.

## What's working

- Hero is legible at a glance: offer (25% off), scope (select styles + jewelry), and CTA ("SHOP THE EVENT") are all visible without scrolling.
- InCircle Double Points banner gives loyalty members a clear added reason to act.
- Nine-product grid is well-organized by category with consistent image sizing and clean labels (brand names only, no visual clutter).
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact top to bottom.
- Secondary navigation buttons (Shop Women's, Handbags, Men's, Home) give multiple entry points for different shoppers.

## What's weak

- This is at least the fourth Friends & Family 25%-off send this week (09-22 through 09-26) with the same green field + wordmark treatment — visual fatigue is real by now.
- The new "20% off jewelry" hook barely registers — it's a small subhead line under the main headline, not its own visual beat, despite being the one genuinely new element in this send.
- "Don't forget" is a weak, low-urgency subject opener; it reads as filler rather than a reason to open today specifically.
- No visible end-date/urgency in the hero itself — "Exclusions Apply" is the only fine print shown above the fold, with the actual 9/30 deadline buried in the legal block at the bottom.
- Zero price anchoring — no "as low as" or example dollar savings, just a flat percentage repeated for the fourth time this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with urgency the reader can see without hunting: add "Ends 9/30" or a countdown directly in the hero, not just in the footer legal block — this is the easiest lift for a "don't forget" themed send.
- 2. Give jewelry its own visual moment (a small product image or icon) instead of a subhead line — it's the only new offer element this week and it's currently invisible.
- 3. Differentiate the subject from the rest of this week's run — stop repeating "25% off select styles" verbatim across sends.
- 4. Add one concrete price example in the grid captions (e.g., "Theia gown, was $895, now $671") to make the discount feel real rather than abstract.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Jewelry's in the mix: 20% off, plus 25% off select styles`

- **Preheader Alt B:** `New this round: 20% off fine jewelry, on top of 25% off select styles.`
