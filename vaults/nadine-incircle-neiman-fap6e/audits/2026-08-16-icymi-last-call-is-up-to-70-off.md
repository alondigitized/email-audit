## Executive summary

- This is the same red Last Call hero Neiman Marcus already ran on 8/13, just re-flagged as "ICYMI" in the subject to squeeze a second open out of the same creative. The hero itself is still the strongest thing this brand has shipped this week — a legible discount stack ("Extra 25% Off" on "up to 70% off"), a clean white "SHOP SALE" button, and honest fine print ("Exclusions Apply," plus a real markdown-and-end-date disclosure at the bottom). ## What's working

- Bold, high-contrast red hero with a clear discount hierarchy (Extra 25% off → up to 70% off → Last Call).
- Single unambiguous white "SHOP SALE" button sits right under the offer stack.
- Honest fine print visible in the hero ("Exclusions Apply") and full markdown/end-date disclosure in the footer.
- Nine real, named products with clean product photography and no render bugs.
- Category CTA grid (Women's Clothing, Men's, Handbags, Shoes, Kids, Women's Dresses) gives multiple entry points beyond the single hero click.

## What's weak

- This is a straight repeat of the 8/13 "Have you shopped our Last Call Sale?" creative — same hero art, same offer, same button — just relabeled "ICYMI."
- "ICYMI" as a subject device only works once; running it on the exact same creative reads as recycling, not re-engagement.
- No new urgency signal in the hero itself — the only real deadline (9/7/26) is buried in small footer legal text, not surfaced near the CTA.
- No loyalty/member pricing or personalization visible despite this being a repeat send to someone who (per the "ICYMI" framing) already saw it once.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend identical creative under an "ICYMI" wrapper — if re-sending to non-openers, at least swap the hero art or lead with a NEW product carousel so it doesn't look like the same email twice.
- 2. Surface the 9/7 deadline near the button ("Ends 9/7") instead of only in footer legal copy — gives the CTA real urgency instead of implied urgency from "ICYMI."
- 3. If this is genuinely a non-open resend, say so more concretely in-body ("You missed this: Extra 25% off ends soon") rather than relying on subject-line shorthand alone.
