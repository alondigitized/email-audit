# Just in: 1,000+ new pre-fall arrivals
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, zero-discount new-arrivals grid — "BRAND NEW STYLES, JUST LANDED" in a bold black hero, then a straightforward 12-product catalog split evenly between women's and men's. No fine print, no urgency, no stacked-discount gymnastics — a total departure from the Last Call/"S" Sale hero cycle Neiman Marcus has run all week (8/13, 8/15, 8/16). The merchandising is genuinely strong — McQueen, Prada, LOEWE, Ferragamo, Ralph Lauren Collection — which telegraphs "new season just dropped" credibly.
- The tradeoff is obvious: there's no price, no offer, no reason to act today. It's a browse email, not a convert email, and it reads as one of the better-executed browse sends from this brand this week precisely because the product photography and grid layout do the selling instead of a discount banner.

## What's working

- Hero copy ("BRAND NEW STYLES JUST LANDED") is immediately legible and on-message with the subject.
- Product grid is clean, evenly split Women's/Men's, with clear designer labels under every item (McQueen, Prada, LOEWE, Ferragamo, Ralph Lauren).
- Two clear CTA buttons ("Shop All Women's New Arrivals" / "Shop All Men's New Arrivals") anchor each section.
- No render bugs — all 12 product images and labels display cleanly, no broken images or overlapping text.
- Nav bar (Women/Men/Shoes/Sale) gives an easy escape hatch to the Sale section for price-sensitive browsers.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer anywhere in the email — nothing to click for if you're not already in a browsing mood.
- Hero has two competing links ("Shop Women's" / "Shop Men's") stacked as small underlined text, easy to miss against the bold headline above.
- No urgency or reason to act now — "just landed" is the only time signal, and it's soft.
- Product grid is undifferentiated — no "new" badges, no price, no quick-view, just image + brand name, so it reads more like a lookbook than a shoppable email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or price range under at least the hero products — even without a discount, seeing "$XXX" next to McQueen or LOEWE gives a browser a reason to click through rather than bounce.
- 2. Convert the hero's two small text links into one bold, high-contrast button ("Shop New Arrivals") — right now the CTA is buried under the headline.
- 3. Add a one-line "first look" or "before they sell out" urgency cue near the hero — new-arrival drops benefit from scarcity framing even without a discount.
- 4. Break the flat 6-item grid into a "Just In" featured row (2-3 hero pieces, larger) followed by the grid, to create visual hierarchy instead of 12 equal-weight tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `1,000+ new pieces just dropped for fall`
- **Subject Alt B:** `McQueen, Prada, LOEWE: new arrivals are in`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the first looks before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designer new arrivals for her and him, all in one drop`
