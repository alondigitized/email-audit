# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- I picked up my phone expecting to browse the new resort arrivals, and instead Neiman Marcus hit me with a bouncer. The page tells me my access is "temporarily restricted" due to "unusual activity" — no homepage, no hero, nothing to shop. For a brand that prides itself on white-glove service, greeting a loyal InCircle member with a bot-detection wall is a jarring contradiction.

## What's working

- The Neiman Marcus wordmark appears twice (top and bottom of the card) — brand identity is at least legible even on an error state.
- The "Submit feedback" link is a real mechanism; a customer who cares enough could report the friction.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is blocked — I am an InCircle member navigating from my personal phone and I cannot see a single product, offer, or editorial image.
- The error copy reads "Rapid taps or clicks" and "Automated (bot) activity" — accusing a loyal customer of behaving like a scraper is genuinely off-putting.
- "JavaScript disabled or not working" is listed as a cause; that framing makes me feel like the brand is blaming my phone rather than taking accountability.
- There is no graceful recovery path — no "continue anyway," no sign-in to verify identity, no human contact beyond a generic feedback link.
- The olive-green blurred background behind the modal appears to be a lifestyle shot that never fully loaded — the promise of a real homepage is visible, but I can't reach it.

## Recommendations

- **Implement session-based exemptions for authenticated InCircle members** so that known, logged-in customers are never caught in bot-detection blocks — my loyalty tier should be a pass, not a suspect flag.
- **Replace the generic bot-block copy** with a warmer recovery message: "Something went wrong on our end — tap below to reload" keeps the blame off the customer.
- **Add a direct phone or chat escalation on the error page** — a simple "Call your NM stylist" link would preserve the white-glove promise even in a broken state.
- **Log and alert on InCircle-member hits to this block** — if loyal members are triggering the gate at any meaningful rate, the detection threshold needs tuning before it damages retention.
