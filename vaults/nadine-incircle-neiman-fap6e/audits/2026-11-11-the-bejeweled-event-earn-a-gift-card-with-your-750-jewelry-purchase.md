# The Bejeweled Event: Earn a gift card with your $750+ jewelry purchase
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built spend-and-get send. A dusty-blue hero reads "THE BEJEWELED EVENT / EARN A GIFT CARD with your $750+ jewelry purchase." Under it, a tier table runs from Spend $750 / Earn $75 up to $10,000+ / $1,500+. A black SHOP & EARN button and the code BEJEWEL follow, then one large cushion-cut ring photo. The offer is easy to understand at a glance, and the tier table does the persuading.
- The send then stacks several things on top of each other. A black band adds Double InCircle Points. Next come a 3×3 grid of designer pieces, six category buttons, and a second black "Triple Points on David Yurman" block with another SHOP & EARN button. After that, a very long wall of terms closes the email. Three reward mechanics (gift card, double points, triple points) compete in one send. The footer fine print is dense and has all the usual exclusions. The offer is honest, but it is buried under a lot of legal text.
- Compared with the brand's recent run, this is closer to the 2026-11-08 "Limited time: up to $650 off" spend-and-save send than to the quiet editorial sends like "From dusk to dawn." That makes it a promo-heavy, high-consideration send. It is one of the stronger offers in the recent stream, but the cadence is getting crowded: a spend-and-save, a 15% sale-on-sale, a 3x points send and now this one, all within days. Overall it is a good, clear send that mixes too many incentives.

## What's working

- The tier table (Spend $750 → Earn $75 up to $10,000+ → $1,500+) makes the value concrete and shows a reason to spend more.
- The hero hierarchy is clean: the serif headline comes first, then the table, then the black SHOP & EARN button, then the ring photo.
- The code BEJEWEL sits right under the button, so there is no hunting for it.
- The designer grid (David Yurman, Roberto Coin, LANA, Chopard, Piaget) is well lit and consistent. The named brands suit a luxury jewelry shopper.
- The six category buttons (jewelry, bracelets, necklaces, rings, earrings, watches) give the reader a way to browse by type.

## What's weak

- Three incentives (gift card, Double Points, Triple Points on David Yurman) split the message. The black "Plus, earn Double InCircle Points" band reads as an afterthought.
- The Double Points band says "Limited time" but never gives the date. The real deadline, 11/21/26, appears only in the terms at the bottom.
- The terms block is very long and set in small type. The exclusions, including no sale items and the 8-week gift card delay, are all far from the offer.
- The 3×3 grid has no prices and no product-level links that I can see. Only the brand names are shown, so product detail is thin.
- The category buttons are pale blue-grey with light text, so they look washed out next to the black buttons above them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 11/21" beside the BEJEWEL code. Of all the changes, this one adds the most credible urgency.
- 2. Make Double Points a one-line add-on, and drop the Triple Points David Yurman block or move it to its own send. That keeps the email to one offer.
- 3. Add prices or a "from $" line to the grid items, and link each tile to a specific product.
- 4. Add a short plain-language summary of the key terms near the hero: "gift card arrives by email within 8 weeks, excludes sale items." Then shorten the legal block.
- 5. Increase the contrast on the category buttons so they match the primary CTA style.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $750 on jewelry, get a $75 gift card`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bejeweled Event: up to $1,500 back, ends 11/21`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BEJEWEL. Gift cards from $75, plus double InCircle Points through 11/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `David Yurman, Roberto Coin, Chopard and more. See your reward tier.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS. It has a leaked internal template comment, accessibility gaps in the `<head>`, and a CSS-only hamburger menu that may not render everywhere. The QA run found no hard failures. Its three warnings are all header-level, and they are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No issues confirmed. The QA run skipped HTTP probes on 35 tracking/click-redirect links, so their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- The source was truncated before any `href` values, so link structure can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`. Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Generic title:** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and says nothing about the content.
- **Checkbox-hack navigation:** The mobile CSS (`*[class].navbar-checkbox:checked~*[class].menu`, `.hamburger`, `.close`) relies on `:checked` sibling selectors. Gmail and many Outlook builds don't support these. I can't see the fallback markup in the truncated source, so the menu may be inaccessible in those clients.
- **Fragile selectors:** Rules such as `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=hide]` match the class attribute exactly. They fail if any element carries more than one class.
- **Minor CSS hygiene:**
  - `*[class].show` declares `width` twice, with `auto !important` and then `278px`.
  - `body { -webkit-animation: bugfix infinite 1s; }` runs an infinite animation to work around an iOS 9 bug.
  - The CSS includes legacy `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` hacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Template documentation leaked into production HTML:** The source opens with an unstripped comment: `<!--REQUIRED variables: @email ... @base_refid ... Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)) ... Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", ...) ... CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW --> <!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`
  - It exposes internal Data Extension names (`NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated`, `NM_CRM_Feed`), field names (`EMAIL_ID`, `uEm`, `cmdid`) and an unfinished "CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO" instruction.
  - The comment's own parentheses are unbalanced (`... @email))`).
  - This is an information-disclosure and template-QA problem. No unresolved visible merge tokens appear in the truncated body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (QA WARN):** The QA note says the relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed, but Gmail and Yahoo require the header for bulk senders.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN):** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't supported. This is also unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- **Footer not assessed:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are beyond the truncated source, so the footer is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not assessable. The truncated source contains no `href`s, and the 35 tracking links were not probed.
- The template references a `@refid` / `@base_refid` campaign variable, which suggests tracking is done through redirect IDs rather than visible UTM parameters. This is an inference only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Strip the AMPscript documentation comment before send, and add a pre-send lint that fails on `<!--REQUIRED variables` or `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO`.
2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Replace the generic `<title>` with the campaign name, and trim the trailing space.
5. Confirm a non-checkbox fallback for the mobile menu in Gmail and Outlook, for example by hiding it or using always-visible links.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw MIME from the origin ESP rather than the relay copy. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass in an unrelayed seed inbox.
7. Re-run the QA with the full HTML and un-skipped tracking links, so redirect chains, final landing URLs and the footer can be checked.
