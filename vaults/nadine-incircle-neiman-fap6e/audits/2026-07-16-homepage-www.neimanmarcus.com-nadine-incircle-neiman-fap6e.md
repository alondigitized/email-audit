# Homepage snapshot · www.neimanmarcus.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened the Neiman Marcus site on my phone and instead of a homepage, I got a wall of text telling me my access is "temporarily restricted." The logo is there — that elegant script I'd recognize anywhere — and I can just barely make out what looks like the tips of two pairs of heels at the very bottom of the screen, as if the site is taunting me with the shoes I came to see. This isn't a shopping experience; it's a bouncer blocking the door at my own club.

## What's working

- **Brand logo** — the Neiman Marcus script logotype renders correctly and looks polished, at least. That's the one thing I recognize as mine.
- **"Submit feedback" link** — there's at least a path forward rather than a dead end, even if I shouldn't need it.
- **Ghost of shoes at the bottom** — I can just see the silhouettes of what appear to be two pairs of pumps, which tells me the actual site content is loading behind the block. The merchandise is there. It just won't let me at it.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is an error page** — as an InCircle member who has shopped here for decades, being told I look like a bot is genuinely insulting. I tapped from my email newsletter link and this is what I get.
- **No InCircle recognition** — if anything should cut through an automated security check, it should be a logged-in loyalty account. There's no mention of my membership status, no graceful fallback like "Sign in to continue."
- **Error copy reads like IT jargon** — "Automated bot activity on your network (IP 47.229.200.33)" means nothing to me and everything to someone in a server room. This was written for a network administrator, not a 51-year-old woman who just wants to browse the new Valentino arrivals.
- **No offer, no navigation, no category tiles** — I have zero idea what the site is currently promoting or where I would even go if the restriction lifted.
- **Shoes at the bottom are cropped and inaccessible** — it's almost worse that I can see them. Like a velvet rope going up right as I reach the front.

## Recommendations

- **Implement a graceful signed-in bypass** — if a user has an active InCircle or NM account session cookie, skip the bot-detection gate entirely or fast-track it. I should never see this screen if I'm logged in.
- **Humanize the error copy** — replace the IP address and technical bullet list with something like: "We're having trouble confirming this is you. Try refreshing the page or signing in to your account." One tap to sign in, not a feedback form.
- **Add a persistent sign-in CTA to the block page** — at minimum, give me a button: "Sign in to your Neiman Marcus account" so I have a path back into the experience without abandoning the session.
- **Surface a campaign teaser behind the gate** — even a static fallback hero image with the current editorial theme ("Summer Runway," whatever it is) would keep the brand experience alive while the authentication resolves.
