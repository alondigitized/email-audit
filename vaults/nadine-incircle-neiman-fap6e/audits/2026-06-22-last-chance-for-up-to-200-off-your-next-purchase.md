# Last chance for up to $200 off your next purchase!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the "last chance" escalation of the spend-to-save offer first sent June 20 (scored 8/10), and it's a competent deadline follow-up. The same gold/cream hero box returns — spend tiers, code JUNE, SHOP THE SALE — but this version layers in an InCircle Triple Points angle and a 3×3 product grid section headlined "More Reasons to Love: Earn Triple Points on Top Styles." The dual message (spend-to-save + loyalty points) is logical but slightly dilutes focus: the headline is about $200 off, the editorial below pivots to Triple Points, and the two don't fully reinforce each other.
- The render is clean, hierarchy is clear, and the offer is honestly disclosed right in the hero ("select regular-price items," "Exclusions Apply," code visible). For a subscriber who saw the June 20 send and didn't act, this is the right move — urgency + a new loyalty hook. The problem is "last chance" without a visible expiration date, which blunts the urgency considerably.
- Solid execution of a standard deadline email. It's better than the June 21 editorial soft-sends that bracketed it, but it doesn't surpass the June 20 original that introduced the mechanic fresh.

## What's working

- **Hero retains the proven spend-tier structure** — SPEND/SAVE table, code JUNE, and SHOP THE SALE button all visible at a glance; no re-invention required.
- **Triple Points InCircle callout** adds a second conversion hook for members on the dark banner, immediately below the hero.
- **Product grid is clean and on-brand** — Stuart Weitzman, Jason Wu, Jimmy Choo, FRAME, STAUD are all credible luxury names; grid is well-spaced with no rendering issues.
- **Category CTA button grid at the bottom** (6 buttons: SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING, SHOP MEN'S, SHOES, JEWELRY, HANDBAGS, HOME) gives every shopper a category escape hatch.
- **No render bugs** — no broken images, overlapping text, or blank gaps visible anywhere.

## What's weak

- **"Last chance" with no deadline date** — "LIMITED TIME" appears in the hero but no end date or countdown. Urgency is claimed but not proved; a savvy subscriber knows NM promos roll over.
- **The two narratives fight for attention** — the hero sells "$200 off via spend tiers," the editorial sells "Triple Points on Top Styles." The product grid header pivots away from the discount, making the email feel like two sends stitched together.
- **Product grid is category navigation, not editorial curation** — nine brand thumbnails with no copy, no prices, no hero product. It doesn't sell anything specific; it just occupies space.
- **No price visibility in the product section** — after a hero built on dollar savings, the grid shows fashion photos with no price anchors or "starting at" context.
- **Heavy cadence week** — this follows the Designer Sale escalation series (June 18–21) and two same-day June 20 sends. By June 22, subscriber fatigue is real and "last chance" may read as noise rather than signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add the exact expiration date to the hero.** Change "LIMITED TIME" to "Offer Ends Tonight" or "Expires Sunday, June 22." Without a date, "last chance" is just a phrase.
- 2. **Unify the editorial hook under one CTA theme.** Either make the product grid "shop the sale picks" (surfacing discounted items) or drop the editorial section and shorten the email to hero + category buttons. The Triple Points angle competes with the savings angle.
- 3. **Surface one hero product at the $50-off entry tier** — a specific Stuart Weitzman flat or Jimmy Choo sandal priced so the math works out ($350 spend → $50 off → $300 effective). Concrete items convert better than tier tables.
- 4. **Shorten the CTA button copy below the fold** — "SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING" is fine but the six-button grid draws scroll-time away from the primary CTA above it. Cut to four or redirect traffic to the sale page filtered by category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Code JUNE expires tonight — up to $200 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $200 off is disappearing — last hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `InCircle members: earn Triple Points on every qualifying dollar`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $350, save $50. Spend $1,000, save $200. Code: JUNE`

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** These are required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5K/day). Their absence from relay capture does not confirm they are missing from actual delivery — the AgentMail relay may strip or not forward these headers. **This must be verified against a raw `.eml` received at a Gmail or Yahoo inbox**, not inferred from relay metadata.

**SPF/DKIM:** Sending domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`. Authentication status is unknown from relay capture. DMARC pass requires SPF or DKIM alignment on this subdomain. If NM uses SFMC's shared sending infrastructure without a custom DKIM domain, DKIM alignment may fail on strict DMARC policies.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from truncated source, but typically present in NM footer. Confirm it appears above the `</body>` tag.
