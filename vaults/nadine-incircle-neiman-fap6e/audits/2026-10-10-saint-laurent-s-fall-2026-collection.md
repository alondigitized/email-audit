# Saint Laurent’s Fall 2026 Collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, restrained Saint Laurent brand-story send. The hero is a woman in a black tailored suit against a brushed-metal wall, under the headline "Suited for Any Occasion." A second block shows the revived Mombasa hobo bag. It's well made and looks like the brand. It also has no offer, no deadline and no loyalty hook.
- It lands after five days of near-identical NMCIRCLE "$200 off + 3x points" sends. The most recent was "Don't miss out on up to $200 off..." earlier today. Against that run, a no-pressure editorial send is a welcome change, much like the Oct 9 gowns email and the Oct 7 Burberry send. A subscriber who isn't hunting for Saint Laurent has little reason to click. The email is pretty but passive.
- Judgement: good craft, weak commercial pull. It works as brand-building and cadence relief, not as a driver of sales.

## What's working

- The full-width suit image reads instantly, and the serif headline matches the luxury tone.
- "Shop Saint Laurent" is an outlined button with a clear verb and noun, placed directly under the hero.
- The Mombasa bag gets its own block with a short, specific story ("the return of the iconic Mombasa"). That gives a concrete product to click through to.
- The layout is simple, with no broken images or overlapping text. The nav (Women, Men, Shoes, Sale) sits cleanly at the top.
- After the Oct 6 to Oct 10 NMCIRCLE run, this feels like a pause rather than a fifth reminder.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, no incentive and no urgency, and nothing says why to act now.
- The hero headline never names Saint Laurent or the Fall 2026 collection. The brand only appears in the button, so the headline is vague.
- The Mombasa block has no price, no product name on the image and no member benefit. The only link is a small underlined text link ("Shop Saint Laurent Bags"), which is much weaker than the hero button.
- The outlined button is thin and low-contrast for the main CTA.
- The footer has a lot of utility links (app, style advisor, store finder) but nothing to push the click, and the loyalty program doesn't appear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to act, such as an InCircle points boost on Saint Laurent, early access, or a "just landed" tag. This would help most, because the brand has trained subscribers to expect an incentive.
- 2. Put "Saint Laurent Fall 2026" in or near the hero headline so the subject and body match.
- 3. Turn the Mombasa image into a clickable product card with a name, a price or "from" price, and a filled button such as "Shop the Mombasa."
- 4. Make the primary button a filled, high-contrast style.
- 5. Add a short row of 3-4 product thumbnails under the suit so the click has a clear target.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Mombasa bag is back at Saint Laurent`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Saint Laurent Fall 26: suits & the new It bag`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sharp tailoring and the return of the Mombasa. Shop now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn bonus points on Saint Laurent through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive media queries. It has no errors in the QA run (0 issues, 4 warnings, 50% pass rate). The source is truncated and 15 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer and compliance checks are incomplete. The confirmed problems are internal ESP template notes left in the shipped HTML and a few accessibility and CSS defects in the `<head>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not validated.** The QA run skipped HTTP probes on 15 tracking/click-redirect links, so redirect targets, status codes and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 2825 of 3310 characters (about 85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. Raw tracking URLs dominate the plain-text alternative, which can hurt spam scoring and readability. This is likely driven by the 15 tracking links.
- The visible HTML excerpt contains no `<a>` tags, so I cannot assess individual `href` values.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, with no `lang`. Screen readers cannot pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`. This blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid font fallback.** `.disclaimerText` uses `font-family: Arial, Helvetica, 'sans-serif'`. The quoted `'sans-serif'` is read as a font name, not the generic family, so the fallback chain has no generic family.
- **Weak `<title>`.** `<title>Neiman Marcus </title>` has a trailing space and doesn't reflect the subject ("Saint Laurent's Fall 2026 Collection").
- **Hamburger nav not confirmed.** The checkbox hack (`.navbar-checkbox:checked~.menu`) is only supported in some clients (Apple Mail, iOS Mail). Gmail and Outlook strip it. The fallback markup is cut off, so I can't confirm the nav degrades gracefully.
- **Leftover dead code.** Three commented-out viewport meta tags remain, including `width=1024` and `width=320, initial-scale=0.29`. They are harmless but add clutter.
- I could not check image `alt` text, because the body is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Internal template notes in production HTML.** The file opens with a comment block that includes:
  - `REQUIRED variables: @email … @base_refid … @refid = CONCAT(@base_refid,"") … @uEm … @cmdid`
  - `Lookup("NM_Subscriber_New_Consolidated", "EMAIL_ID", "EMAIL_ADDRESS", @email)`
  - `Lookup("NM_CRM_Feed", "uEm", …)`
  - `CHOOSE ONE SCENARIO BELOW` and `<!--/* SET @user = HASHED_EMAIL_SHA256 */ -->`

  This exposes internal Data Extension names, field names and authoring instructions to every recipient. It also shows the template wasn't stripped of its scenario-selection note before send. The comment sits before the `DOCTYPE`, which can trigger quirks-mode rendering in some clients.
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `@var`) appear in the visible rendered portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it isn't confirmed as a sender-side omission.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so check the original message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated footer, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** The visible source contains no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and all 15 tracking links were skipped. The template comment shows tracking is driven by `@refid` and `@base_refid`, which suggests a `refid`-style parameter instead of UTMs. This is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Strip the leading template comment.** Remove the `REQUIRED variables…` block and the `SET @user` note in the ESP's pre-send or minify step.
2. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
4. **Fix the font stack** in `.disclaimerText` to use unquoted `sans-serif`.
5. **Verify headers on the original message** (not the relay copy): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `e.neimanmarcus.com`.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML.** Resolve the 15 tracking links (following redirects) to check status codes, final URLs and UTM/`refid` parameters. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Cut its URL density, or use readable link labels instead of raw tracking URLs.
