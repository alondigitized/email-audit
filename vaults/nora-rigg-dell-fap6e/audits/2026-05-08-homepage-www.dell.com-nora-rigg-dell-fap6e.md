# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Dell has basically replaced its homepage with an AI chat portal — the entire above-the-fold experience is a conversational prompt box with four quick-action buttons underneath. No hero product, no Premier pricing callout, no XPS banner in sight. It's a bold, tech-forward call that fits my instincts as a high-comfort power user, but right now it feels like the landing page for a beta feature, not a homepage for the world's biggest business PC company.

## What's working

- **The "Find the best laptop for me" quick-link** — one tap and I'm in a configurator flow, which is exactly how I shop. That's the right shortcut for someone like me.
- **"Track my order" as a surface-level button** — appreciated. I don't want to dig through account menus for that.
- **The AI-first interface concept** — as a high-tech-comfort user, I'm not scared of a chat prompt. I'd actually try it. The conversational framing is appropriate for someone willing to spec out a machine.
- **Clean, distraction-free layout** — no cluttered promotional noise. The dark hero is calm and professional-feeling.
- **"Get technical support" visible** — as a repeat Dell buyer with a business account, having support surfaced immediately signals that Dell knows their audience isn't just first-time shoppers.

## What's weak

- **No Premier or business account signal anywhere** — I'm logged into my Premier account on desktop but on mobile I land here and there's zero "Welcome back, here's your pricing" hook. For a business account holder, this is a missed opportunity every single session.
- **The AI popup overlays the product imagery below the fold** — I can see two laptop silhouettes under the dialog, but the "New AI Assistant" card is covering whatever product content lives there. It's a modal-adjacent pattern that hides what I might actually want to browse.
- **"Show current deals" is a tease, not an offer** — there's no actual discount number, no threshold, nothing that makes me feel urgency. A button that says "deals" is marketing speak, not a deal.
- **No navigation categories above the fold** — there's a hamburger menu but zero visible category shortcuts (Laptops, Workstations, Monitors, etc.). On mobile, one tap to "Laptops > XPS" should be surfaced.
- **No social proof or product imagery** — no review counts, no star ratings, no product shots with specs. For a comparison-driven buyer, nothing here helps me evaluate anything.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Premier/business pricing immediately for recognized accounts** — a simple "Your Premier discount is active. Shop XPS" banner above the AI input would make this homepage feel built for me instead of everyone.
- **Replace the modal-style AI popup with an inline card** — the dismissible overlay covering product content is the wrong UX pattern for mobile. Embed it as a persistent bottom bar or collapsed card so the product below the fold is immediately visible.
- **Add one concrete offer with a real number** — swap the "Show current deals" button for a specific callout: "Dell Days: Up to 25% off XPS → Shop now." That's one line and it changes the entire conversion calculus.
- **Add category chips below the search bar** — Laptops | Monitors | Workstations | Accessories. Four tappable chips take me where I want to go in one tap without AI mediation.
