# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Dell on my phone expecting to land somewhere close to the XPS configurator or at least a business deals banner — instead I got what looks like a customer service chatbot is the entire homepage. The AI assistant interface is a bold swing, and honestly as someone who knows exactly what she wants, it feels like they're making me work harder than I should have to. It's a modern brand making a bet on AI-first navigation, but for a comparison-driven shopper like me it's a detour, not a shortcut.

## What's working

- **The AI quick-action button "Find the best laptop for me"** — as a comparison-driven buyer this is actually the right hook; I would tap this if I were starting a refresh cycle
- **"Track my order" button** — visible at-a-glance without digging into an account menu; useful for someone who's always mid-order
- **Clean dark hero** — the Dell Technologies brand reads as premium; no clutter, no pop-ups hijacking the viewport
- **"Transparency Statement" disclosure under the AI input** — signals that Dell knows this is a liability and is getting ahead of it; earns minor trust points from me

## What's weak

- **No deal surfaced above the fold** — "Show current deals" is a button, not a deal. If Dell Days or an XPS promotion is running right now, this is where it should be. I shouldn't have to tap to find out.
- **Michael Dell waving** — the image below the fold is the CEO at what looks like a conference. That is zero help to me trying to decide on a dual-monitor workstation purchase. Product or lifestyle, pick one.
- **No Premier/business account recognition** — I'm logged in to a Premier account in reality; this homepage treats me like a first-time visitor. No "Welcome back, Nora. Your account credit: $X" or similar.
- **AI-first navigation is a conversion bottleneck for configurator shoppers** — I already know I want XPS 15 or 16. Routing me through a chatbot adds a step that a product tile wouldn't.
- **No spec-level hooks** — no "Intel Core Ultra 9 / 32GB configs starting at $X" rail visible anywhere; the page is entirely brand-level, not product-level

## Recommendations

- **Replace or supplement the hero with an XPS / business configurator tile** — keep the AI input but add a "Build Your XPS" or "Shop Business Laptops" card alongside it so configurator shoppers don't have to ask the bot what they already know they want
- **Surface an active deal number in the hero** — change "Show current deals" to "Dell Days: up to 30% off XPS" (or whatever is live); the button label is doing zero persuasion work
- **Add a Premier member banner** for signed-in business accounts — even a thin strip that says "Premier pricing applied" or shows account credit would make me feel recognized and lower my bounce likelihood considerably
- **Swap Michael Dell's waving photo for a dual-monitor desk setup or a hybrid-work lifestyle shot** — something that reflects how their target business buyer actually uses the product
