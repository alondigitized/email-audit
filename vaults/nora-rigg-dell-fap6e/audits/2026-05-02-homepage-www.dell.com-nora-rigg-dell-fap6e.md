# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Dell is clearly betting hard on AI-first discovery right now — the entire above-the-fold experience on mobile is an AI chat prompt, not a product hero or a deals banner. As someone who lives in the configurator and comparison pages, I actually respect the intent here, but the execution feels like it was designed for someone who doesn't already know what they want. The brand reads as enterprise-adjacent but approachable, though the mobile viewport buries any actual products below the fold entirely.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action button** — this is genuinely useful shorthand and the kind of guided entry point that would work on a phone where I can't easily poke around the nav.
- **"Show current deals" button** — I always check Dell Days before committing; having it surfaced as a one-tap from the homepage saves me a step.
- **"Track my order" quick action** — practical utility right upfront; I order frequently enough that this saves me a login hunt.
- **AI Assistant popup with "New" badge** — I notice new tools, and as a high-tech-comfort user I'd at least tap it once; the positioning as a recommendation engine rather than a chatbot is the right framing.
- **Search bar is prominent and accessible** — for a power user, jumping straight to search is still my fastest path and it's not buried.

## What's weak

- **Zero products visible above the fold** — I opened the homepage on my phone and I cannot see a single SKU, category tile, or even a price. For a comparison-driven buyer like me, this is friction before I've even started.
- **No Premier account callout anywhere** — I have a business account with Dell. There's no sign of personalized pricing, my account-linked deals, or any signal that the homepage knows I'm a returning B2B customer. A generic consumer experience is a miss when you have a differentiated tier.
- **AI chat as the hero is a risky default on mobile** — I'm not going to type a freeform query on a small keyboard when I already know I want to configure an XPS. The chat-first layout front-loads effort.
- **"Please verify responses. Transparency Statement"** — this fine-print disclaimer right under the hero chat input immediately undercuts confidence in the tool before I've used it.
- **No visible pricing, promotional banner, or sale indicator above the fold** — "Show current deals" exists as a button, but there's no teaser of what the deals actually are (e.g., "Up to 30% off XPS this week"). I have to commit a tap blind.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a sticky Premier/business account badge or personalized greeting** ("Welcome back, Nora — your Premier pricing is active") above the AI chat block to immediately signal this isn't a generic storefront.
- **Add a thin promotional strip below the search bar** with a concrete teaser ("Dell Days: up to X% off XPS + Precision through May 9") so the deals CTA has context before I tap it.
- **Swap the full-viewport AI chat hero for a split layout** — keep the AI prompt in the upper half but show 2-3 category tiles (Laptops, Workstations, Accessories) in the lower half so the page feels like a store, not a chatbot landing page.
- **Move the "Transparency Statement" disclaimer** off the hero block entirely — drop it to the footer; having it immediately under the chat input reads as a confidence warning, not a legal nicety.
