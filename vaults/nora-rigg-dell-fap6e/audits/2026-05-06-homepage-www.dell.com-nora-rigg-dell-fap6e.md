# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Dell opened with an AI assistant takeover — the entire above-the-fold is a chatbot prompt, not product or deals. As someone who comes to dell.com with a specific config in mind and a Premier account ready to go, this feels like I've been handed a concierge when I wanted the floor plan. It's a bold UX bet, but it sidesteps everything I actually need on a first glance.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action button** — that's actually useful shorthand for a first-time or casual visitor; it shows Dell knows laptops are the primary intent driver
- **"Track my order" and "Get technical support" buttons** — practical post-purchase hooks that reduce support friction; I appreciate not having to hunt for these
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — no banner ads stacked on top of each other, no pop-unders; the page isn't fighting itself
- **AI input field as primary CTA** — for a high-tech-comfort user like me, the concept isn't off-putting; I could actually use this to spec a machine fast

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier / business account signal** — I have a company account and a rep. There's nothing here acknowledging I exist as a returning B2B buyer. Not a single badge, "Welcome back," or "Business pricing" callout
- **No actual offer above the fold** — "Show current deals" is a teaser, not an offer. I can't mentally benchmark value without a number
- **The AI modal pop-up ("New AI Assistant")** — there's already an AI assistant in the hero. A second floating modal saying the same thing is redundant and adds visual noise; it feels like the product team shipped two separate features without coordinating
- **No product imagery above the fold** — I came here thinking about an XPS or a Precision workstation. There are zero product shots until I start scrolling; the laptops I can see are clipped at the very bottom edge
- **No navigation exposed** — on mobile, a hamburger hides everything. I can't see categories, I can't shortcut to "Laptops" or "Monitors" without an extra tap

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier/business entry point in the nav bar or hero subtitle** — even "Business customers: sign in for your pricing" above the AI input field would stop me from feeling like I landed on the consumer homepage by mistake
- **Replace the floating "New AI Assistant" modal with a persistent deal strip** — something like "Dell Days: XPS 13 from $X, free shipping" at the top or bottom edge anchors the value prop without duplicating the chatbot that's already in the hero
- **Add one product thumbnail row below the AI section** — XPS, Inspiron, Alienware, Monitors — just the category tiles so I can bypass the AI if I know what I want
- **Pre-populate the AI input with a business-flavored placeholder** — swap "Ask me about Dell products and services" for "Configure a workstation, compare monitors, or check Premier pricing" to immediately signal this tool is for power users too
