# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like an enterprise hardware brand that has gone all-in on its AI assistant. The first thing I see is a big black "How can I help you today?" band. The XPS 13 hero sits underneath it, and I don't see a single price or deal anywhere. It's clean and familiar, but it feels like a support desk with a laptop ad attached, not a storefront.

## What's working

- **XPS 13 hero image:** Two laptops, one open and one closed showing the XPS logo, look crisp and premium. I'm already an XPS person, so this is the most relevant thing on the page.
- **"Dell Premier for Business" in the nav:** As a Premier account holder, I notice it right away. It tells me there's a business path.
- **Quick-prompt chips ("Show current deals", "Find the best laptop for me", "Track my order"):** They're a reasonable shortcut, and "Track my order" is useful for a returning buyer.
- **Nav labels:** "Computers & Accessories", "Deals" and "Financing" are plain and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- **The AI band dominates the fold.** I came to configure and compare, not chat. It pushes the product down, and the black-on-grey contrast makes the hero feel secondary.
- **No offer or price anywhere.** "Show current deals" is a chip, not a deal. I have no reason to act today.
- **"Study and work" copy.** I refresh my rig every 3 years for work. This reads as a college laptop pitch.
- **Floating widgets cover the carousel controls.** The counter and Play button are half-hidden, and two chat buttons crowd the corner.
- **"Shop Now" plus "Explore More."** Two similar CTAs make me pause, and "Explore More" is vague.
- **No specs or configurator hook.** I'm configurator-heavy, but I see no RAM, CPU, or "Customize" cue.

## Recommendations

- Shrink the AI band to a slim search bar, or move it below the hero. Let the XPS image and headline lead the screen.
- Add a visible offer line under the hero copy, such as "Starting at $X" or "Up to $X off XPS", with an end date. Show a Premier badge next to it, for example "Premier members: extra savings".
- Reword the hero for working professionals, for example "Two monitors' worth of power in one thin laptop". Change the CTAs to "Configure XPS 13" and "Compare models" so they match how I shop.
- Move the floating chat buttons so they don't cover the carousel counter and Play control, and replace the blank blue block on the Premier nav item with a proper logo or icon.
