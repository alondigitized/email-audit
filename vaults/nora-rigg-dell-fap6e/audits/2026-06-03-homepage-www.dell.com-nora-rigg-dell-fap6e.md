# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Dell has replaced its traditional homepage hero with an AI chat interface — "How can I help you today?" — which is a bold, tech-forward swing. It signals a company leaning into conversational commerce, and honestly, for a high-tech-comfort shopper like me, it's interesting enough to pause on. That said, the moment I look for actual deals, my Premier account benefits, or a fast path to the XPS configurator, the page gives me nothing — it's an empty lot with a helpful sign but no inventory.

## What's working

- **AI assistant as hero** — "Find the best laptop for me" and "Show current deals" are high-intent shortcut buttons that speak directly to how I actually shop. I'm already mentally typing "XPS 15 vs 16 for dual-monitor hybrid setup."
- **"Track my order" button** — Present in the primary CTA cluster, not buried. As someone who orders on a business account, this matters.
- **Clean render on mobile** — No layout jank, no broken tiles, no truncated copy. The dark background with white type and the ghost button styling is legible and sharp.
- **Monitor lifestyle image below the fold** — Two Dell displays with a colorful wallpaper render is literally my desk setup. It's not above the fold, but it's there.
- **AI transparency disclaimer** — "Please verify responses. Transparency Statement" is quietly trustworthy. A lot of brands would bury that.

## What's weak

- **No Premier account recognition** — I'm a returning business buyer. Not a single signal that Dell knows I exist — no "Welcome back," no account balance, no Premier-tier offer. The page treats me like a first-time anonymous visitor.
- **"Show current deals" is a button, not a deal** — There's no actual offer visible. I can't evaluate whether Dell Days is live, what's discounted, or whether there's a business buyer promo. I'm being asked to click before I know if it's worth my time.
- **Zero product imagery above the fold** — The AI interface is conceptually smart but visually barren. The monitors below the fold are partially cropped and easy to miss on scroll. I have no anchor product to react to.
- **No configurator entry point** — I'm a "configurator-heavy" shopper. The fastest path to converting me is a direct "Configure your XPS" or "Build a business system" link. It's not here.
- **Navigation is fully collapsed** — Hamburger menu only. On mobile, there's no visible category strip, no "Laptops | Monitors | Business" shortcut row. One extra tap minimum to get anywhere specific.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Premier account welcome strip** — Even a thin banner reading "Welcome back, Nora — see your exclusive pricing" or "Dell Days: Business buyers save an extra 10%" would immediately convert the page from generic to mine.
- **Surface one concrete deal number above the fold** — Replace or supplement the "Show current deals" ghost button with inline copy: "Dell Days — up to 40% off XPS & monitors. Ends June 6." That's one sentence and it changes the urgency calculus entirely.
- **Add a sticky category shortcut row below the search bar** — Laptops | Monitors | Business | Deals — four chips, one tap each. The AI assistant can stay as the centerpiece; this row just reduces friction for people who already know what category they want.
- **Swap the below-fold monitor crop for a full bleed lifestyle image** — Show the dual-monitor XPS setup at a real desk with a hybrid-worker context. It's one scroll away from sealing the deal for someone like me, and right now it's cut off at the bottom edge.
