# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is not what I expected — Dell has essentially replaced their traditional hero with an AI chat interface, front and center. It's a bold call, and honestly kind of fascinating as a tech person, but as someone who came here specifically to configure a laptop or check Dell Days pricing, it feels like it's making me work for what I already know I want. The brand reads as forward-thinking and enterprise-oriented, and the push right now is clearly AI-assisted shopping.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick action** — As someone who goes deep in the configurator, this AI shortcut is genuinely interesting. It's the right hook for how I actually shop Dell.
- **"Show current deals" + "Track my order" side by side** — Covers both the deal-hunter and the returning customer in one line. Efficient.
- **Clean, uncluttered mobile layout** — No aggressive modals, no banner stack, no popup. The page just loads and presents the tool. I appreciate the restraint.
- **Hamburger + cart + account icons** — Standard and immediately recognizable. Account icon suggests I could log into my Premier account quickly.
- **Professional woman at a desk** — The lifestyle shot barely peeking in below the fold at least gestures at a hybrid-worker context. Better than gaming rigs.

## What's weak

- **Zero pricing or offer specifics above the fold** — "Ask me about today's best deals" with no actual deal visible is a tease that doesn't pay off. I watch Dell Days specifically; show me the number.
- **No Premier/business account callout** — I have a Premier account. Dell knows this if I'm logged in. There's no personalized hook, no "Welcome back, Nora" or "Your Premier pricing is live." That's a miss.
- **"Please verify responses. Transparency Statement"** — Putting an AI disclaimer *directly under* the AI input field as the very first thing I read after the prompt is a confidence killer. It makes me second-guess using the tool before I've even tried it.
- **Nav is hamburger-only** — One tap to reach Business Laptops or XPS would put me into the funnel. Right now I'd have to either trust the AI or dig through a hamburger menu.
- **No trust signals** — No "4.5 stars from 12,000 reviews," no free returns badge, no financing mention. Nothing that closes the gap between browse and buy.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a concrete deal in the AI chat placeholder or directly beneath it** — "Today: XPS 15 starting at $1,199 — ask me to configure it" turns the chat from abstract to actionable in one line.
- **Add a Premier account recognition banner** — Even a slim "You're signed in with Premier pricing" strip would signal Dell knows who I am and make the AI feel personalized rather than generic.
- **Swap or reposition the AI disclaimer** — Move "Please verify responses" to a tooltip on an info icon rather than a standalone line. Right now it's the second thing I read on the page.
- **Add a sticky or visible quick-nav row** — "Laptops / Monitors / Accessories" below the search bar gives one-tap access for people who already know what category they want, without killing the AI-first experience for newcomers.
