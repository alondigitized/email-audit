# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This doesn't even read as a retail homepage to me — it's an AI concierge screen. Dell's led with "How can I help you today?" and a chat box before I see a single spec, price, or product name. As someone who's usually cross-referencing configs before I buy, I need to type or tap to even find out what's on sale, which is a strange first move for a site that used to hit me with deal banners immediately.

## What's working

- The "Find the best laptop for me" chip — as a comparison-driven shopper, a guided recommendation flow is actually useful to me if it works well.
- Clean, uncluttered layout. No banner soup, no popup begging for my email.
- The "Please verify responses / Transparency Statement" line — small thing, but it signals Dell isn't hiding what the AI is doing.

## What's weak

- Zero mention of my Premier business account or any loyalty/rewards status — I'm a repeat B2B-ish buyer and there's nothing here that recognizes that.
- No concrete offer. "Show current deals" is a button, not information — I have to tap to even learn if there's a deal worth my time.
- The laptop product image at the bottom is cropped mid-shot with no name, price, or spec attached — I can't tell what I'm even looking at yet.
- No visible path to a configurator or compare tool, which is exactly how I like to shop.

## Recommendations

- Put one real headline offer (with a number) above the AI chat box, not buried behind a "Show current deals" tap.
- Surface my Premier account status or business pricing badge near the account icon — I want to know instantly I'm logged into the right context.
- Add a "Compare laptops" or "Configure & price" quick-action chip alongside "Find the best laptop for me" — that's literally how I shop.
- Finish rendering the laptop product card above the fold with a name/price so the scroll payoff is obvious before I commit to scrolling.
