# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- Dell opened with an AI assistant front-and-center, which is a bold bet — "How can I help you today?" with quick-action buttons instead of a traditional hero banner. As a configurator-heavy buyer who lives in comparison mode, I appreciate the intent, but the execution trades away everything I actually need on a first load: what's on sale, what's new in XPS, and whether my Premier account is recognized. It reads like a product demo of Dell's AI more than a homepage designed to convert me.
- **5/10**
- + One unmistakable primary CTA is visible above the fold — "Show current deals" is a real, tappable button
- + Visual hierarchy is clear — the AI prompt is unambiguously the hero, the eye knows exactly where to go
- + No render bugs — page is clean, text is legible, no broken elements visible
- + Offer feels honest — "Please verify responses. Transparency Statement" is a rare self-aware disclosure
- − Hero copy does not explicitly speak to my persona — "How can I help you today?" is the same for a first-time buyer as it is for a Premier account holder refreshing a 3-year-old XPS rig
- − No returning-shopper hook — no "Welcome back," no Premier account badge, no recently-viewed
- − No concrete offer above the fold — "Show current deals" is a promise, not a deal; no % or $ figure visible
- − No demographic or lifestyle signals — nothing for a hybrid Millennial pro vs. a student or gamer
- − No loyalty/membership visibility — Premier account perks are invisible
- − No current campaign or seasonal relevance — zero seasonal or event-specific context (no Dell Days banner, no spring refresh)

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action button** — for a configurator-driven buyer like me, this is genuinely useful shorthand; it acknowledges I have a specific need, not a browsing mood
- **"Track my order" button** — surfaces a high-frequency post-purchase task right on the homepage; that's good service design
- **AI chat input field** — a tech-comfortable user appreciates being able to type a natural query ("show me XPS 13 deals under $1,200") rather than hunting through nav trees
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — no banner overload, no six-promo carousel; the page is focused and loads fast on mobile

## What's weak

- **No Premier/business account recognition** — I have a Premier business account and there is zero acknowledgment of that; the homepage treats me like an anonymous visitor
- **"Show current deals" button with no preview** — I can see a button but not a single price, percentage, or product name; it's a tease, not a hook
- **AI assistant popup obscures the product imagery** — the "New AI Assistant" overlay in the lower portion covers what appears to be a laptop image; it's the only product visual I can see, and it's half-blocked
- **No navigation shortcuts for my category** — no XPS, no business laptops, no workstations visible without hitting the hamburger menu; one extra tap is one too many on mobile
- **No Dell Days / deal cadence context** — I watch Dell Days specifically; if one is approaching or active, I'd need to already know that, because this page gives me nothing
- **Transparency disclaimer feels defensive** — "Please verify responses" is honest, but it undercuts confidence in the AI right as I'm about to use it

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier account banner or personalized greeting above the AI module** — even a thin "Hi Nora, your Premier pricing is active" strip would immediately differentiate me from a first-time visitor and increase my trust that the AI's recommendations will be account-aware
- **Replace the generic "How can I help you?" headline with a campaign-specific hook** — e.g., "Spring refresh is here — XPS 13 from $999, configured your way" so above-the-fold has at least one concrete signal
- **Add a "Deals" quick-badge or countdown to the "Show current deals" button** — something like "20+ deals live today" makes that button feel alive rather than hypothetical
- **Dismiss or auto-minimize the AI assistant popup by default** — let the product imagery breathe; keep the chat icon in the corner as a persistent but non-blocking entry point
