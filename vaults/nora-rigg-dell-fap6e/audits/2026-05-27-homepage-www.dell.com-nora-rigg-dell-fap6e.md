# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Dell's mobile homepage has gone full AI-first — the entire above-the-fold experience is an AI assistant prompt, which honestly reads as a bold, tech-forward bet that I kind of respect. It feels less like a retailer and more like a business tool, which tracks for the audience they're likely targeting. That said, I came here to check Dell Days deals and maybe price out a new XPS or a second monitor, and this page makes me work for it.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action chip** — this is genuinely smart for a configurator-heavy shopper like me; it acknowledges that I don't just want to browse, I want to narrow
- **"Show current deals" chip** — immediately surfaces the one reason I'm likely here on mobile; doesn't bury it in a mega-nav
- **Clean, low-noise layout** — no carousel chaos, no competing banners; the single-focus AI prompt reduces cognitive load
- **"Track my order" chip** — good returning-shopper shortcut; I have standing orders and B2B refresh cycles to monitor
- **Monitor teaser image below fold** — the dual-panel product shot (monitor + laptop) is the first visual that makes me feel like this is about *my* kind of setup

## What's weak

- **No Premier/business account hook above fold** — I have a Premier account. There is no acknowledgment that I exist as a business customer anywhere in this viewport. That's a miss.
- **AI hero is a dead end until you type** — the prompt field is wide open with no suggested context for someone like me; "Ask me about Dell products and services" is vague. I don't know if this thing can actually configure an XPS 15 for me or if it's going to give me FAQ answers.
- **Zero specific deals visible** — "Show current deals" is a button, not a deal. If Dell Days is happening, I can't tell from this screen.
- **No trust signals beyond a transparency disclaimer** — no review counts, no "as low as $X/mo," no free-shipping threshold, nothing that tells me buying here is safe and easy.
- **No imagery of actual people or work contexts** — the product shot below the fold is barely visible and shows no lifestyle or work-from-home context that would make me feel seen.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a "Premier account" or "Business" chip alongside the other quick-action buttons** — even one tap entry to my account segment would tell me this page knows who I am.
- **Replace the generic AI placeholder copy with a contextual suggestion** — something like "Try: Configure an XPS 15 for a hybrid team" gives a power user like me a concrete on-ramp and demonstrates the AI's actual capability.
- **Add a deal callout strip above the AI module** — even a thin banner that says "Dell Days: Up to X% off XPS and monitors" turns the hero from a blank prompt into an urgency moment.
- **Bring the monitor/laptop product visual above the fold** — it's the most relevant imagery on the page for me and it's getting cut off; crop it tighter or move the AI input field up to make room.
