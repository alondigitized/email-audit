# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Dell went all-in on an AI chatbot as the entire above-the-fold experience, and my first reaction is: interesting bet, but I came here to configure a laptop, not have a conversation. The page reads more like a support portal than a flagship e-commerce homepage. There is zero product, zero deal, zero Premier — just a dark modal asking me what I need.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action button** — for a configurator-heavy shopper, the promise of a guided path to a recommendation is genuinely useful, even if I'd rather just go straight to the configurator.
- **"Track my order" button** — a rare returning-shopper signal; it's buried among the quick actions but it's there.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — the dark background with centered AI chat creates a clear focal point, no visual noise competing for attention.
- **"Show current deals" button** — at least there's a path to offers even if no deal is surfaced immediately.

## What's weak

- **Zero product visibility above the fold** — I opened Dell.com and saw no laptops, no XPS, no Inspiron, nothing. For a comparison-driven shopper who wants to start configuring immediately, this is a full stop.
- **No Premier account recognition** — I have a Premier business account and there is no sign Dell knows who I am. No "Welcome back," no account balance, no Premier pricing callout.
- **The floating "New AI Assistant" popup** — there's already an AI chat prominently on the page, and then a secondary floating modal appears at the bottom saying the same thing. Two AI prompts at once reads as clutter, not confidence.
- **No concrete offer surfaced** — "Show current deals" requires a tap to see anything. My attention is gone before I make that extra step.
- **No category navigation visible** — the hamburger menu hides everything. I can't scan for XPS, Latitude, or workstation categories without an extra interaction.

## Recommendations

- **Surface one real deal in the hero** — swap the generic dark background for a "Dell Days — Up to X% off XPS" headline with a visible price or dollar amount. The AI input can stay as a secondary element below.
- **Add a Premier sign-in hook above the fold** — even a small "Sign in to see Premier pricing" badge near the top nav would acknowledge that business buyers exist and convert higher.
- **Collapse the duplicate AI modals** — the floating "New AI Assistant" popup at the bottom is redundant given the full AI chat already dominating the page. Kill one; the in-page version is stronger.
- **Add a horizontal category strip below the AI section** — Laptops / Monitors / Workstations / Accessories as tappable chips would let me self-navigate without needing the AI to guide me there.
