# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I open Dell on my phone expecting to hunt for an XPS upgrade or check Premier pricing, and I'm immediately staring at a Fortnite laptop on a black-and-purple gaming stage. This looks like the Alienware microsite hijacked the homepage. Dell is clearly pushing the new Alienware 15 hard right now, which is fine — just has nothing to do with why I'm here.

## What's working

- **Clean mobile layout** — nav bar, search field, hero, and CTA stack without clutter; on a phone screen this matters
- **"Shop Alienware 15" CTA** — unambiguous button copy, high-contrast purple, immediately tappable
- **Laptop-forward hero image** — the Alienware 15 is sharp and well-lit; if I were in the market for it I'd clock the product instantly
- **Search bar above the fold** — as a configurator-heavy shopper, I'd probably just search "XPS 15" anyway and that field is right there

## What's weak

- **Wrong persona, wrong product** — Alienware / Fortnite targets Gen Z gamers; I'm a Millennial professional watching Dell Days for an XPS refresh
- **Zero Premier account signal** — no "Welcome back, Nora" or Premier badge to tell me my business account is active and pricing is applied
- **No offer at all** — not even "Starting at $X" or "Free shipping on orders over $X"; I can't anchor a comparison
- **No category navigation visible** — the hamburger hides everything; as a comparison-driven shopper I want to see "Laptops / Desktops / Monitors" rails to orient me fast
- **No trust or credibility layer** — no review count on the featured product, no "free returns," nothing to confirm I'm on the right site for a business purchase

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier / business account strip** below the search bar — even a one-line "Premier pricing applied to your account" with an account icon gives me the confidence signal I need before I dig in
- **Add a "Dell for Work" or XPS quick-link tile** beneath the Alienware hero — a two-tile row (Gaming | Work & Creator) keeps the Alienware push while routing hybrid workers to the right funnel in one tap
- **Show a price anchor on the hero CTA** — change "Shop Alienware 15" to "Shop Alienware 15 — From $1,799" so even non-gaming visitors know what ballpark they're in and can decide quickly whether to explore
- **Add a compact promo strip** ("Dell Days: up to 40% off select XPS") above the hero — one line, links to the sale landing page, gives comparison shoppers a reason to stay rather than tab over to a competitor
