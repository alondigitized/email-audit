# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- Dell's mobile homepage has gone full AI-first — the entire above-the-fold experience is an AI assistant prompt, not a traditional hero. As someone who lives in the configurator and comparison-shops specs before touching anything else, I immediately notice that the traditional "Dell Days deals + product categories" entry point has been replaced by a conversational interface. It feels more like opening a chatbot than a retailer. That's a bold bet, and for me it's a mixed one.

## What's working

- **"Show current deals" quick-action button** — exactly where I'd tap first; doesn't require me to type anything to get to promotions
- **"Find the best laptop for me" shortcut** — surfaces the configurator intent without burying it in nav; even if the copy skews consumer, the function is what I want
- **"Track my order" button** — I reorder on the company plan constantly, so post-purchase access right from the home screen is genuinely useful
- **Clean layout and fast-loading appearance** — no cluttered banner stack, no layout shift; as a dual-monitor XPS person I appreciate that Dell isn't embarrassing itself visually on mobile
- **AI assistant with mic input** — voice-querying specs while comparing options on my laptop monitor is actually how I'd use this

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/business buyer recognition** — I have a business account and there is no "Welcome back, Nora" moment, no Premier pricing badge, no B2B entry point above the fold at all
- **No concrete deal visible** — "Show current deals" is a button, not a deal; I have no idea if Dell Days is active or what the lead offer is without tapping
- **"Find the best laptop for me" copy reads consumer, not pro** — feels like it's aimed at a first-time buyer; I want "Configure a system" or "XPS 15 configurator"
- **The laptop thumbnail barely visible at the bottom** — half-cropped, no product name, no price signal; it's decorative rather than functional
- **No category navigation visible** — Monitors, Accessories, Workstations — none visible without tapping the hamburger; one extra tap is friction when I'm in comparison mode
- **Transparency Statement disclaimer at the bottom** — understandable for AI but it's small, gray, and easy to miss; if there's a trust risk here, it should be more prominent

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier/business banner above the AI chat box** — even a one-line "Premier member? Sign in for business pricing" strip would give returning B2B buyers an immediate on-ramp and signal Dell knows who's coming
- **Replace the generic "Find the best laptop for me" button with "Configure an XPS or Precision"** — routes power buyers directly to the configurator flow instead of a consumer-quiz experience
- **Add a live deal strip between the AI panel and the product image** — one line: "Dell Days: XPS 15 from $1,199 | 15% off select monitors" gives me the deal signal without requiring a tap
- **Make the half-visible laptop at the bottom a tappable product card with model name and price** — right now it's a visual teaser with no payoff; convert it into a direct product entry point
