# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Dell's mobile homepage has gone full AI-first — the entire above-the-fold experience is a conversational interface with four quick-action buttons and essentially no traditional content. It reads like a product demo more than a homepage right now. For someone like me who lives in the configurator and has a Premier account, I appreciate the intent, but there's nothing here telling me what's on sale, what new XPS models dropped, or that Dell even knows I'm a returning business customer.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action button** — as someone who spends real time in the configurator, a guided finder on mobile actually makes sense; I'd tap this
- **"Show current deals" button** — gives me a direct path to deals without hunting through nav; that's efficient
- **"Track my order" button** — legitimately useful for a repeat buyer who just placed a new XPS order; no dig required
- **Clean mobile layout** — hamburger menu, search bar, and action buttons are all comfortably sized and well-spaced; no fat-finger frustration
- **AI disclaimer ("Please verify responses. Transparency Statement")** — I appreciate that they're being upfront; trust matters on a business purchase

## What's weak

- **Zero above-the-fold offers** — there's a "Show current deals" button but no actual deal. Even a banner line like "Dell Days: Up to 30% off XPS through May 9" would make this page feel alive
- **No Premier / business account context** — Dell's entire moat with me is the Premier program; not surfacing it at all on mobile is a missed layup
- **No product imagery worth noting** — I can see some faint laptop silhouettes at the very bottom of the screen, but they're cropped and tiny. There's no hero product shot, no spec callout, nothing that makes me want to stop and look
- **The AI Assistant popup layered on top of the AI homepage** — there's already an AI chat interface in the main content area, and then a "New AI Assistant" slide-in panel appears at the bottom too. That's two AI surfaces competing for attention on one screen
- **No category navigation exposed** — just a hamburger. For a comparison-driven buyer who wants to jump straight to Laptops > XPS > 15-inch, this adds unnecessary taps

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent Premier/business banner under the search bar**: one line — "Premier account: your saved configs and contract pricing →" — would immediately signal Dell knows who I am
- **Replace the generic hero headline with a campaign hook**: swap "How can I help you today?" for something like "Dell Days ends May 9 — up to 30% off XPS and Precision" with a price callout, even on mobile
- **Remove the duplicate AI panel at the bottom**: the quick-action AI interface is already the full page experience — the "New AI Assistant" popup just adds noise and obscures the product imagery below the fold
- **Surface at least one product tile above the fold**: even a single thumbnail of the current XPS hero SKU with a starting price would give my eye something to anchor to before I commit to the AI flow
