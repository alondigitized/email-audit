# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Dell is leading with an AI concierge right on the homepage — bold move, and honestly kind of interesting as a tech-first millennial who lives in product configurators. But I opened this expecting to jump straight to XPS or check my Premier pricing, and instead I'm staring at a chat prompt and a photo of Michael Dell. This reads less like a storefront and more like a tech demo.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" button** — as someone who lives in the configurator, I actually appreciate a shortcut into a guided flow. One tap to start comparing.
- **"Show current deals" quick-action** — surfaces deal-hunting intent immediately; I don't have to hunt through a nav menu.
- **Clean mobile layout** — hamburger menu, search bar, AI prompt, four clear action tiles. Nothing fighting for attention. The visual hierarchy is genuinely good on a small screen.
- **"Track my order" button** — useful functional utility right up front; doesn't make me dig.
- **AI transparency note** ("Please verify responses. Transparency Statement") — as someone who uses AI tools daily, I actually respect that they put that caveat right there.

## What's weak

- **No Premier account recognition** — I have a business account. The page has no idea I exist. No "Welcome back, Nora" or Premier pricing signal anywhere above the fold. This is a miss for retention.
- **No actual product visible** — not a single XPS chassis, monitor, or spec sheet. For a comparison-driven shopper, I need something to react to. A chat bot doesn't give me that anchor.
- **Michael Dell's photo** — it's aspirational/brand, fine. But it's not a product image or lifestyle shot that connects to my hybrid-work reality. It feels like a conference keynote, not a storefront.
- **No deal shown, only a button to find deals** — "Show current deals" is a promise, not a proof. I want to see the 15% off or the Dell Days banner right now.
- **No navigation categories visible** — there's a hamburger menu but no quick-access to Laptops, XPS, Business/SMB, or Monitors. I have to do work to get where I'm going.

## Recommendations

- **Surface one concrete deal above the fold** — replace the Michael Dell photo strip with a banner showing the actual Dell Days offer: "XPS 14 — $200 off, ends Sunday." That's the hook I'll click.
- **Add a Premier/business account badge or CTA** — even a small "Premier pricing available · Sign in" chip near the search bar would immediately signal that Dell knows this isn't my first visit.
- **Show one product image** — drop a hero tile of an XPS 13/14 next to the AI prompt. Give me something to tap that isn't a conversation starter.
- **Expand the quick-action row to include "Shop XPS" or "Business Laptops"** — four action tiles have room for category shortcuts. "Find the best laptop for me" via AI is fine, but a direct "Shop XPS" button closes the gap for returning buyers who already know what they want.
