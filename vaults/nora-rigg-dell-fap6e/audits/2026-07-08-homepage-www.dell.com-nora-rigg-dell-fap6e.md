# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Dell has replaced its traditional hero banner with what is essentially a front-page AI assistant — "How can I help you today?" with quick-action chips and a chat field. As someone who spends real time in the Dell configurator, I appreciate the ambition, but this feels like a product pivot that's still finding its footing. There's no deal front and center, no Premier recognition, and the whole thing reads like a chatbot landing page more than a shopping destination.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" chip** — this is basically the configurator on-ramp and it's one tap from the hero. For a comparison-heavy shopper like me, that's the right shortcut.
- **"Track my order" chip** — useful for repeat buyers; I don't have to hunt the nav or log in first.
- **AI-first interface** — the chat input with the spark icon reads as modern and premium, which fits Dell's XPS positioning.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — dark hero, white cards, clear chip buttons. On mobile the layout doesn't fight itself.
- **"Get technical support" chip** — I've had a Premier relationship for years; quick access to support matters to me more than it would to a first-timer.

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier/business acknowledgment** — I have a business account. This homepage treats me like I walked in off the street. No "Welcome back," no business pricing badge, nothing.
- **No Dell Days or seasonal deal above the fold** — "Show current deals" behind a button is not the same as showing me a deal. I watch Dell Days closely; if there's a sale running, put it where I can see it in the first second.
- **AI assistant disclaimer ("Please verify responses")** — I understand why it's there, but it slightly undercuts confidence right in the hero. If I'm asking it to help me find a laptop, am I supposed to fact-check it?
- **Lifestyle imagery below fold is cut off** — the desk-setup and blue-glow images are barely visible. On my phone I can't actually see the product or the context — it's just a sliver.
- **No price or spec signal anywhere** — for a high-tech, comparison-driven buyer, an $X starting price or "from $1,099 with business pricing" would do a lot of work here.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a Premier/business-account banner in the hero area** — even a slim "Hi, Nora — your Premier pricing is active" ribbon at the top would completely change how this page lands for returning business buyers.
- **Swap the generic hero chip "Show current deals" for a specific deal copy** — "Dell Days: Up to 40% off XPS + Business Laptops" is a one-liner that would make me stop scrolling.
- **Add a second chip or link for "Compare XPS models"** — "Find the best laptop for me" is good but leans casual; a comparison shortcut speaks directly to my shopping behavior.
- **Trim or reframe the AI disclaimer** — move "Please verify responses" to be inside the chat container, not below the hero. It's making the marquee feature look unreliable before I've even typed anything.
