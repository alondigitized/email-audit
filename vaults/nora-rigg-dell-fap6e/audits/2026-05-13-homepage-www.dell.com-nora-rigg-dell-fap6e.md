# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Dell opened the homepage with a full-screen AI chatbot interface — no hero product, no deal callout, no Premier account acknowledgment, just "How can I help you today?" and a set of quick-action buttons. For a brand that built its reputation on configurators and build-your-own power, this feels like a bold pivot toward conversational discovery. It's interesting, but on mobile it immediately surfaces an AI Assistant modal that chops off whatever is below the fold, which is friction I did not ask for.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" quick-action chip** — this is genuinely my mode. I'm configurator-driven, and an AI that acts like a guided configurator has real appeal for how I actually shop Dell.
- **"Track my order" button** — practical, immediate, no hunting through the nav. Good for a returning buyer.
- **Transparency Statement link** — as someone who uses AI tools daily at work, I notice and appreciate that Dell discloses the AI's limitations inline rather than burying it.
- **"Show current deals" chip** — it's not a deal itself, but having it front-and-center means I don't have to dig to find promotions.

## What's weak

- **No Premier / business account recognition**: I have a Premier account. Dell knows who I am at login, but this page gives me zero acknowledgment — no "Your account pricing," no "Business-only deal," nothing. That's a miss for the customer segment that drives Dell's margin.
- **The AI Assistant popup at the bottom**: it auto-launches, covers the partial product imagery visible at the very bottom of the screen, and creates a layer of UI I have to dismiss before I can assess what's below. That's a dark pattern adjacent behavior.
- **Zero product imagery above the fold**: the dark hero is entirely text and UI chrome. I can barely see two laptop silhouettes at the very bottom, clipped by the AI modal. A configurator-heavy shopper needs to see the hardware.
- **No Dell Days / campaign hook**: if Dell runs its famous sale events, they're not announced here. A Millennial who watches for Dell Days actively would have no idea one is live or coming.
- **Generic copy register**: "How can I help you today?" is fine for a chatbot, but it's not the voice of a brand that makes XPS machines I lust after. It's a help desk, not a product brand.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Premier pricing or a business-account badge in the header** — even a thin "Premier member | Your discount applied" strip would immediately signal this page knows who I am.
- **Replace the all-dark hero with a product-forward split**: keep the AI chat input on one side, put an XPS 16 or a dual-monitor workstation shot on the other — give me something to covet while the AI warms up.
- **Convert the AI Assistant modal from auto-launch to a persistent bottom tab**: let it be discoverable without commandeering the screen. I'll find it when I need it.
- **Add a Dell Days / active promo banner strip** between the hero and the quick-action chips — even "Dell Days — up to 40% off business PCs" would stop a returning shopper from bouncing.
