# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Dell went full AI concierge on mobile and I'm genuinely curious — but also a little frustrated. The homepage has basically replaced its traditional hero with a chatbot prompt, which is a bold move and speaks directly to someone like me who's comfortable with tech. What it doesn't do is surface any actual product, any deal, or any Premier account hook, so I'm walking in as a known Dell customer and the page is treating me like a stranger who needs hand-holding to find a laptop.

## What's working

- **AI concierge with pre-built prompts** — "Find the best laptop for me" and "Show current deals" are smart shortcuts that cut straight to what I actually want to do on a phone; I don't have to dig through nav
- **"Track my order" shortcut** — post-purchase access at the top of the chat options is genuinely useful; I'd use that
- **Clean mobile layout** — header is minimal, search is prominent, no clutter; the page loads looking polished
- **Partial monitor imagery below the fold** — colorful, high-quality product shot that makes the hardware look premium; I can see two Dell displays with vivid wallpaper and it makes me want to keep scrolling
- **Transparency Statement link on the AI** — appreciated the disclosure; feels grown-up and honest

## What's weak

- **Zero Premier account recognition** — I've been a Dell customer for years with a business account and the page greets me like I've never heard of Dell; no "Welcome back," no rewards balance, no member pricing badge
- **No deal visible above the fold** — "Show current deals" is a promise, not a deal; I still have to tap to find out if there's actually anything worth buying today
- **Four equal-weight buttons** — "Show current deals," "Find the best laptop for me," "Track my order," "Get technical support" all look the same; there's no hierarchy telling me what Dell wants me to do or what's most valuable right now
- **No product specs, pricing, or model names** — for a comparison-driven shopper who came here to check on XPS refresh cycles or Dell Days pricing, the homepage gives me nothing to anchor on
- **Hamburger-only nav on mobile** — every category is hidden; I can't scan what Dell sells at a glance, which forces me into the AI flow even if I already know what I want

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Premier/business account hook in the AI greeting** — if the user is logged in or browsing from a known B2B IP, swap the generic "How can I help you today?" for "Hi Nora — your next XPS refresh is eligible for Premier pricing. Want to see options?" One line, enormous relevance lift
- **Add a deal strip above the AI card** — a single-line banner showing the current Dell Days or promotional anchor ("Up to 30% off XPS + monitors · Dell Days ends May 26") costs no real estate and immediately gives comparison shoppers a reason to stay
- **Visually promote one CTA over the others** — fill "Find the best laptop for me" in Dell blue and leave the others as outlines; gives the page a clear primary intent without removing the utility shortcuts
- **Pull the monitor image up into the hero** — that colorful dual-monitor shot below the fold is doing premium brand work; crop it as a background behind the AI card so the page has visual interest above the fold, not just text on black
