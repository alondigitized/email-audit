# Homepage snapshot · www.dell.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Dell opened on me with an AI chat interface instead of a traditional homepage, and I'll be honest — that's either bold or baffling depending on how long I've been on my feet today. It reads like a tech-forward brand trying to modernize the shopping experience, but right now it's pushing me toward conversation rather than product. No deals, no XPS hero, no Premier account acknowledgment. Just a prompt field and some quick-action buttons.

## What's working

- **"Find the best laptop for me" button** — this is exactly how I shop. I want to start with constraints and narrow down, and one tap here gets me into a configuration flow. Good instinct.
- **AI assistant input field** — as someone who lives in dual-monitor setups and knows what I need, being able to ask "XPS 13 vs XPS 15 for hybrid work" in plain language is genuinely appealing.
- **Clean layout** — no clutter, no carousel wars, no 14 competing banners. The page has a clear visual center.
- **Transparency disclaimer** — "Please verify responses" with a linked Transparency Statement shows Dell isn't pretending the AI is infallible. I respect that as a skeptical comparison shopper.

## What's weak

- **Zero deal visibility above the fold** — "Show current deals" tells me deals exist but shows me nothing. My Dell Days radar goes dark immediately.
- **No Premier business account acknowledgment** — I have a Premier account and the page has no idea. No "Welcome back," no member pricing, no business-specific path.
- **Hamburger nav hides my categories** — one tap to my XPS configurator or business portal? Nowhere. Nav is completely collapsed on mobile, so my actual workflow is buried.
- **Lifestyle image is barely visible** — the photo of the woman at her desk is cropped to about 30% of a screen and partially obscured; it's atmospheric, not persuasive.
- **No product hero** — no XPS, no Precision, no "built for hybrid work" headline. It could be any tech brand's AI assistant page.

## Recommendations

- **Surface one active Dell Days deal directly in the hero** — swap "Ask me about today's best deals" placeholder copy for a live deal teaser, e.g., "XPS 13 — $200 off through July 18." Let the AI follow up.
- **Add a "Premier account" fast path button** — alongside "Find the best laptop for me," add "Access my Premier account" or "Business pricing" so B2B buyers aren't forced through a generic configurator.
- **Replace hamburger nav with 3–4 sticky category chips** — Laptops, Monitors, Accessories, Business — so I can reach my category in one tap without opening a full drawer.
- **Show the lifestyle image full-width** — the working-woman-at-desk shot is the strongest demographic signal on the page; let it breathe instead of treating it as decoration below the fold.
