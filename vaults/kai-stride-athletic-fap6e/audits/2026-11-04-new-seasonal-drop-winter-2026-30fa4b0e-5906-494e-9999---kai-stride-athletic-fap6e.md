# New seasonal drop: Winter 2026.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this one is half for me. The hero is a man and a woman running on a dirt road under a heavy sky, and that's the first runner imagery I've gotten from lululemon in a while. It follows the 11/03 "New running gear steps into the light" send, so lululemon is clearly leaning into running this week. After the studio-heavy 10/27 contoured sets and 10/28 Define Jacket emails, I'll take it.
- Then the email loses me. The hero is followed by a woman in a zip top, a woman lounging in a sweatshirt and sweats, and a model in a quarter-zip on a chair. There's no price, no product name and no run-specific detail anywhere. ## What's working

- The hero shows two people actually running on a gravel road in winter light. It reads like a real run, not a studio pose.
- The man in the gray shell is in my demo, and he gets a second appearance in the grid.
- The tight shot of the woman in a running vest with sunglasses has real kit detail, which tells me someone thought about running.
- The dark, muted palette and the red CTA pills stand out clearly, and the logo placement is clean.
- Dark footer links for the App, What's New and Find a Store are readable and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, price, product name or hero SKU. "Shop Winter 2026" is the only CTA and it goes to a broad collection.
- Most of the middle is lounge and studio content: the sweatsuit on the couch, the woman by the window and the man on the chair. That's the fashion-shopper-in-running-gear problem I care about.
- The copy has no run-specific information, such as the shell's weather rating, reflectivity or a vest's capacity. I can't tell if this is for my Sunday long run or a coffee shop.
- The "Give the feeling" gift block pulls me into holiday messaging with a woman sprinting, which is unrelated to the main message. The two identical "Shop Winter 2026" buttons do the same thing, so the email feels repetitive.
- The footer says benefits depend on Membership, but nothing in the body shows a member benefit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the hero. Put a caption on the gray shell, something like "The Fast and Free Shell: wind and water resistant, reflective trim, from $X". Runners shop weather and specifics.
- 2. Split the CTA. Give me "Shop men's running jackets" and "Shop women's running layers" instead of two identical "Shop Winter 2026" buttons.
- 3. Cut the lounge frames or move them down. Lead with run content, and lululemon would earn more clicks from me.
- 4. Add a sizing or availability cue, like "Sizes selling fast" or "Back in stock", so there's a reason to act now. Nike does this with drop hype, and adidas "Week of Deals" at least gave me an offer to react to.
- 5. Show a member perk if one applies, and add a club or group-run nod.
