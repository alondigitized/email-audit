## What's weak

- There is no offer at all. No price, no promo and no free shipping. Nike would at least tease a member drop, and adidas gave me a points giveaway last time.

- The brand voice is soft ("quiet edge") and there's no urgency. I can't tell if this is a launch or a restock. I also can't see sizing or availability, and there are no reviews.
- Three of the product tiles, coats, bags and joggers, are generic. "We think you'll also love" feels auto-filled, and the "See all" button doesn't tell me what I'd be shopping.

- 2. Give the hero one concrete product claim, such as a weight, temperature range or runner-tested detail. "Breathable" alone isn't a reason to click.
