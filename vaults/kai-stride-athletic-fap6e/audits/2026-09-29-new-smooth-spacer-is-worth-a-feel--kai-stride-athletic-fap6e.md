## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound: it declares `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and dark-mode handling. The QA run found four images with missing alt text, and it could not verify headers, authentication or the 42 tracked links. Most compliance and continuity checks are therefore inconclusive, not failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 42 links because they use tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chain depth and final landing URLs are unconfirmed.
- No broken or malformed links were confirmed in the available source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 4 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Masthead_Yogotype_PureWhite_FFFFFF` is the masthead logo. If it is linked, the link has no accessible name.
  - `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$` appears 3 times. These look decorative and should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Without the attribute, some screen readers announce the file URL.
- **Invalid CSS values:** `.guest-voice-lg`, `.guest-voice-md` and `.guest-voice-sm` use `letter-spacing: -2%` and `-1%`. The `letter-spacing` property does not accept percentages, so clients drop the declaration and the tracking is lost. Also, `font-weight:380`, `570` and `600` are variable-font values that most email clients ignore. These are low severity and degrade to the fallback fonts.
- **Good practice observed:**
  - `lang="en" dir="ltr"`
  - `<meta name="viewport" ... user-scalable=yes>`, so zoom is not blocked
  - `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags plus `:root` declarations
  - `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` overrides
  - `format-detection` meta tag

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]`. The body past the truncation point was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is a capture limitation, not confirmed sender non-compliance.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** Same caveat. RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain `e.lululemon.com` was not checked directly.
- **Footer elements:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 42 links were skipped by QA and the source is truncated. UTM parameters, landing-page match to "Smooth Spacer" and redirect behavior are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt="lululemon"` to the masthead image, or to its wrapping link. Add `alt=""` to the three `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images.
2. Replace `letter-spacing: -2%` and `-1%` with pixel or em values (for example `-1.12px` or `-0.02em`).
3. Verify the sender domain's `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers against the raw message. Don't rely on the relay-captured copy. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lululemon.com` the same way.
4. Resolve the tracking-domain redirects for a sample of the 42 links. Confirm final URLs and UTM values, and check that the hero CTA lands on the Smooth Spacer collection.
5. Capture the full HTML, without truncation, so the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and any merge tokens can be checked.
