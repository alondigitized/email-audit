# Summer Kickoff Event: Up to 40% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the third time in recent memory I've seen adidas push "up to 40% off" as the main message — there was a nearly identical send on June 3rd with the same headline energy. At this point the "Summer Kickoff Event" label is doing zero work; it could just as easily be "Winter Kickoff Event" and I wouldn't notice the difference. The hero is a big bold typographic treatment over what looks like an American flag-wave graphic, which reads more patriotic retail holiday than running-culture moment. Nothing here signals they know I run.
- The Strava x adiClub block is the one thing that actually stopped my scroll. That partnership is legitimately interesting to someone who tracks miles — linking a Strava subscription to my adiClub membership is a real benefit, not just a discount. But it's buried below the fold, almost treated as an afterthought, with a "Redeem Now" CTA that finally gives me something to act on.
- Would I click? Maybe — but only for the Strava block, and only because I already use Strava. I wouldn't forward this to anyone in the running club. The generic sale banner would have gone straight to archive if not for that one relevant hook.

## What's working

- **Strava x adiClub integration is genuinely useful.** For a running-club captain who logs every mile, a bundled Strava sub tied to loyalty points is a real reason to engage — not just a discount.
- **Clean typographic hero.** The "Summer Kickoff Event" lettering is bold and renders without any visual bugs — layout is solid.
- **Three segmented CTAs (Men / Women / Kids) below the hero.** Appreciated that they didn't just dump me on a generic sale page; at least there's a men's entry point.
- **No broken images.** Everything rendered. After seeing brands fumble this repeatedly, it counts.

## What's weak

- **The hero is generic retail, not running.** "Summer Kickoff Event" with a stars-and-stripes wave pattern reads July 4th department-store sale, not performance brand. Nike's personalized one-time code email at least felt addressed to me; this feels addressed to no one.
- **The sale is the same sale.** June 3rd was "Score big on the best gear, now up to 40% off." This is "Up to 40% off starts now." I'm seven adidas emails deep — this cadence is killing the urgency signal.
- **Strava block is buried.** The most differentiated and runner-relevant section is below two generic sale CTAs and a lifestyle image. It should be the hero.
- **"STORIES, STYLES AND SPORTSWEAR AT ADIDAS, SINCE 1949" footer tagline.** Reads like company boilerplate, not a brand moment. Lululemon's June 1 Father's Day guide at least felt like a human chose the editorial direction.
- **No performance product specificity.** No shoe name, no fabric callout, no trail vs. road distinction. The sale could be for anything in the catalog — shoes, bags, soccer jerseys (which I've been getting way too many of lately).

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with Strava x adiClub as the hero, not the sale.** Move the partnership block to position one. The discount becomes the secondary nudge ("unlock this + save on Ultraboosts") rather than the whole pitch.
- 2. **Name an actual product in the hero.** Something like "Adizero SL2 — now in the Summer Kickoff sale" gives me a reason to click that isn't just "maybe there's something good in there."
- 3. **Kill the flag-wave graphic or reframe it.** If the intent is a summer running event, show runners. A club start line, a track, anything that signals performance over retail.
- 4. **Break the sale-email cadence.** I've gotten this same 40%-off message twice in ten days. Send the Strava partnership as a standalone — it earns its own email and would open cleaner.
- 5. **Add a no-deadline fix.** "Up to 40% off starts now" with no visible end date kills urgency. If the sale ends June 15, say it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your adiClub membership now unlocks Strava`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Adizero SL2 just dropped into the Summer Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off across men's running — ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Track your miles, save on gear. New adiClub x Strava perk inside.`

## Technical Audit — adidas "Summer Kickoff Event" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken redirect renders a CTA non-functional; eight arrow-icon images are served over HTTP and will be blocked by major clients. Plain-text fallback is absent, and compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified due to AgentMail relay capture limitations.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken redirect — one CTA dead**

The click tracker at `https://dv.adidas.com/o/2e158291-c472-4490-a621-142b584c271f` responds with a redirect to a bare path:

```
/g/2e158291-c472-4490-a621-142b584c271f?txn=29c77200-6774-11f1-9fcb-3efb318f62ec
```

QA reports: `Link error: unknown url type`. A relative-path redirect that omits the scheme and host resolves correctly in browsers via the `Location` header base URL, but the path `/g/…` is not a recognized resolvable scheme for non-browser HTTP clients, and any non-standard redirect handler (email pre-fetchers, some mobile clients) will fail silently. Confirm the adidas redirect service at `dv.adidas.com` is returning a fully-qualified `https://` URL in its `Location` header for this UUID.

**[INFO] 36 tracking links not probed**

All click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com`, `dv.adidas.com`) were skipped. Destination URL integrity and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed for those links from this audit alone.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] 8 HTTP image sources — same asset, same file**

The arrow icon (`b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`) is referenced 8 times via HTTP:

```
src="http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg"
```

Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook 2019+ block mixed-content images. All 8 instances will render as broken images in those clients. Fix: replace `http://` with `https://` on `image.link.adidas.com`.

**[WARN] Additional HTTP image — tracking pixel served insecurely**

```
src="http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019ebd0141a8-b562954d-e51e-4225-a4a0-c5160094cac7-000000/Y1ikChWYuZD_5G0ksO-NCCzgt..."
```

Open-tracking pixel also on HTTP. Same blocking risk; also missing `alt` text (see below).

**[WARN] Missing alt text — 2 images**

- `https://dv.adidas.com/o/2e158291-c472-4490-a621-142b584c271f` — no `alt` attribute
- `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019ebd0141a8…` — no `alt` attribute

Screen readers and image-off rendering modes will produce blank gaps. CTA images should carry descriptive alt; tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string, not omitted).

**[INFO] Custom fonts fall back unconditionally**

`@font-face` declarations for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` reference only `local()` sources (Arial, Helvetica, Calibri). No remote font URL is loaded. These fonts will never render unless pre-installed on the recipient's device. This appears intentional but means brand typography is never delivered via email.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` block**

The font declarations appear twice — once in the main `<style>` block and once in a `<!-- For Yahoo Android App -->` comment-wrapped block. No functional impact, but adds unnecessary CSS weight.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header — not captured**

QA did not detect this header. This is flagged as a possible AgentMail relay artifact, not a confirmed absence. However, if the header is genuinely missing:

- Gmail and Yahoo (as of Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) will suppress the native unsubscribe UI for senders over 5,000/day.
- Deliverability scoring will be negatively affected.

Verify the raw SMTP headers on a direct delivery (outside the relay) to confirm presence of:
```
List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>, <https://...>
```

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not captured**

One-click machine unsubscribe is a Yahoo/Google bulk sender requirement for >5k/day. Same caveat as above re: relay capture — verify in raw headers.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` — not captured**

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be determined from this relay capture. The sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` should be covered by adidas's DMARC policy; confirm with a direct header inspection or a mail-tester.com run.

**[INFO] `ROBOTS: NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW` and `referrer: no-referrer` present**

Both meta tags are in the `<head>`. No issues.
