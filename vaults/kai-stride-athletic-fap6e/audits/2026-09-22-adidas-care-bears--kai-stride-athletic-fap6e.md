- Care Bears. Adidas sent me a Care Bears collab. I run a weekly long run with my club, and this week's email is a pastel lifestyle Superstar with a cartoon bear riding a shoelace through a cloud. This isn't drift anymore, it's a pattern: adidas has no idea I signed up as a runner.
- The photography's cute if you're eight, but there's nothing here for me — no mileage, no cushioning story, no drop date for anything I'd actually lace up for a tempo run. The kids' backpack and bucket hat at the bottom confirm this is a family/lifestyle blast, not a performance send. I'm not opening the app for this, and I'm definitely not forwarding it to the run club unless it's to joke about how far off-base adidas's targeting is.

- The hero banner has some genuine visual polish — the gradient sky, the little cloud and raindrop icons around the shoe are well produced.
- Pricing and star ratings are visible right under each product, so at least the merchandising layer is clean and scannable.
- "SHOP NOW" appears twice with a clear black button and arrow — no ambiguity about the CTA if I did care.
- The sale badges on the backpack (-20%) and bucket hat (-10%) are honest, specific discounts, not vague "up to" language.

## What's weak

- Every single product is a lifestyle item — a fashion Adistar, a Superstar (a shoe from the 70s, not a trainer), a plush backpack, a fuzzy bucket hat. Nothing here touches performance running.
- Care Bears is a nostalgia/kids IP. As a 33-year-old dude who cares about tempo runs and race PRs, this doesn't land as "cool collab," it lands as "wrong list."
- Subject line "adidas | Care Bears" gives me zero reason to open — no offer, no urgency, no running angle.
- This continues the exact complaint from last week's Z.N.E. email and the week before's Anthony Edwards drop — adidas keeps rotating lifestyle/collab content into a running-focused inbox segment.
- No community signal, no club angle, nothing that acknowledges I'm a repeat subscriber who's been getting six straight irrelevant sends.

- 2. If adidas wants to reach me at all this week, lead with the Adizero Evo SL or another actual trainer — like the 9/19 "fall savings" email at least tried to do — not a cartoon bear shoe.
- 3. Drop the collab/lifestyle sends into a clearly labeled "adidas Originals" stream so runners like me can filter it out ourselves.
