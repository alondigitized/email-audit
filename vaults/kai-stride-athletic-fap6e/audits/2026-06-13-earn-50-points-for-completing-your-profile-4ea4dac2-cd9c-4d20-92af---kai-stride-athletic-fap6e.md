# Earn 50 points for completing your profile
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is adidas asking me to complete my adiClub profile in exchange for 50 points. After a week of back-to-back promotional blasts — Summer Kickoff discounts, World Cup jerseys, FIFA rewards — this is a palate cleanser. It's a CRM trigger, not a sale push, and honestly that almost works in its favor: it's the one email this week where adidas isn't screaming "40% off" at me.
- That said, 50 points is a number with no context. I have no idea if that's one free shoe or a bottle of water. The hero image is three friends taking a selfie — fine lifestyle photography, but zero running signal. No Ultraboost. No runner on a track. Just... brunch vibes. I'm a running-club captain, and this email could have gone to literally anyone in their database.
- I'd probably click "Complete My Profile" in under five seconds because it's frictionless and I genuinely want adidas's recommendations to skew toward running gear instead of soccer kits. But I'm clicking despite the email, not because of it. Wouldn't forward this to anyone in the club.

## What's working

- **Single, unambiguous CTA.** "COMPLETE MY PROFILE →" in a solid black button — zero confusion about what they want me to do.
- **Clean layout, zero render bugs.** Nothing broken, no overlapping text, renders fast. Nike's transactional sends (the "here's your one-time code" from earlier this month) set the bar for clinical cleanliness; this matches it.
- **adiClub framing.** Tying the ask to the loyalty program is the right move — it's the one thing adidas does that earns me. Better than another "Shop Now" leading to a homepage.
- **Short, scannable body copy.** Three sentences. I didn't have to work. Good.

## What's weak

- **The hero image says nothing about me.** Three friends, selfie, indoor. I run 45 miles a week. Show me a runner or at least someone in Ultraboosts — lululemon's golf email at least showed golf gear on a golfer, which is more than adidas managed here.
- **"Hello," with no name.** They want me to complete my profile so they can personalize my experience, but they can't even pull my first name for the greeting. The irony is loud.
- **50 points — what does that buy me?** The value prop is naked. No context. Nike's loyalty emails at least gesture at what points unlock. This just hangs there.
- **No running signal anywhere.** Not in the hero, the copy, or the CTA destination. If I'm a fashion shopper who bought Sambas once, this email reads identically. Adidas knows I'm a runner — my purchase history says so. They chose not to use that.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** This is at minimum the sixth adidas email I've opened in roughly two weeks. The June 12th Summer Kickoff email already felt like the third repeat of the same beat. This one is different in type, but the inbox is still saturated.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pull the first name and one behavioral signal into the hero copy.** Something like "Let's build your running profile, Kai" + a line that mentions running or their latest footwear drop. The infrastructure for this clearly exists — they just didn't use it.
- 2. **Anchor the 50 points to real value.** "50 points = $5 toward your next purchase" or "50 points gets you closer to [specific reward tier]." Right now it floats.
- 3. **Swap the hero image for running context.** One athlete mid-stride or even a flat-lay of running shoes. Don't need to blow the budget — just signal you know who you're talking to.
- 4. **Add a soft deadline.** "Complete by [date] and we'll add 50 points to your account." Creates a reason to act now vs. "I'll do it later" (and never do).
- 5. **Throttle the send cadence.** This email would have landed harder if there weren't five others before it this month.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kai, we're missing a few details — earn 50 points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50 points waiting: help us personalize your run`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tell us your run style — we'll tailor everything to match`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Worth 5 minutes. Your adiClub rewards get smarter.`
