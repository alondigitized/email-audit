## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is an SFMC-built, table-based email with solid foundations: `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, and dark-mode meta tags. The QA run found 0 hard failures. Its three warnings are the missing unsubscribe and authentication headers, which are probably capture artifacts, plus four images with no alt attribute. The HTML source was truncated, so body, link and footer checks are limited to what the QA data reports.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 38 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and final landing URLs are therefore **unverified**.
- No broken links were reported.
- Nothing else can be confirmed from the available data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
**Confirmed issues: missing alt text on 4 images.**
- `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Masthead_Yogotype_PureWhite_FFFFFF`: the masthead logo has no alt text. This is the brand identifier, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$` appears 3 times. These look like decorative arrows next to footer links. They need an explicit `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. A missing attribute is not the same as an empty one, because some screen readers read out the filename.

**Observations (not defects):**
- The CSS uses `'Saans-Regular', Helvetica Neue, sans-serif` and `'KHGiga-Light', 'Times New Roman', serif`. The fallback stacks are present.
- `h1, h2, h3, h5` share one rule, while `h4` is styled separately (30px, `Saans-Light`, Arial fallback). This is inconsistent but harmless.
- `user-scalable=yes` in the viewport meta is correct for accessibility.
- The `\0` hacks (`.cta-white\0`, `.cta-white-fixed a\0`) are deliberate Outlook.com Android dark-mode targeting. They are valid but fragile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible portion. The truncated remainder is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture this header.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). Without it, one-click unsubscribe may not be supported. This is relevant to Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `hello@e.lululemon.com`, a subdomain, but alignment can't be verified.
- None of the three can be confirmed as real defects. They are most likely relay capture limits. Check the raw `.eml` headers before treating them as failures.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this can't be assessed. The links are click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the masthead logo, for example `alt="lululemon"`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the three `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images.
3. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. This tells you whether the three warnings are real or relay artifacts.
4. Make the QA tool follow the 38 tracking redirects to their final URLs. This would let it verify UTM parameters, HTTP status and landing page alignment.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover merge tokens, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
