# What’s new: Big Cozy’s comeback
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a lovely email for someone who isn't me. I run a club and buy for the Sunday long run. Every model here is a woman in a fleece, a quarter-zip, a puffer or wide-leg pants. Everything is shot in a cabin with warm wood and soft light. There's no run, no road, no pace and no men at all. It's a cozy lounge catalog.
- It's also a step back from the 11/03 "New running gear steps into the light" send, which was the first lululemon email in a while that felt aimed at me. The 11/04 Winter drop was half for me. This one drops the runner thread completely. Nike would have kept a cold-weather run layer somewhere in the stack, and adidas at least sent a code. Here I get no offer and no code, only a mood.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Maybe to my partner, but not to the club group chat. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and well lit, and the red "lululemon" wordmark makes the sender obvious right away.
- "Warmth that sparks curiosity" on the red block is a good visual break. It's the one moment with real contrast.
- The two stacked red buttons are easy to find and big enough to tap on a phone.
- The "Shop the latest drop" grid at the bottom is tidy and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- There is no runner content, and no man anywhere in the email. For a 33-year-old male club captain, that's a miss.
- There's no offer, no member benefit and no deadline. "Shop What's New" and "Shop Big Cozy" are vague, and the two buttons feel redundant.
- The copy is mostly poetry: "a sense of relief from the season's demands" and "sparks curiosity." It tells me nothing about fit, fabric or what the gear is for.
- There's no price, no sizing and no stock info anywhere. Some of the sub-links, like "Scuba Merino Wool-Blend →" and "Smooth Spacer →", are tiny text that's hard to tap.
- The email is very long, and the same cabin-and-fleece imagery repeats.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one cold-weather run layer for men near the top. A reflective vest or a thermal half-zip would do it, shown outdoors at dawn, and it should be linked to its own product page.
- 2. Split the send by segment. Men who clicked running gear should get a run-focused version, and the lounge fleece should go to the people who buy it.
- 3. Replace the two generic buttons with one specific CTA. Add a real hook like member pricing or a restock note.
- 4. Cut the poetic headlines and say what the product does, such as the fabric, the warmth and the fit.
- 5. Shorten the email and drop one of the repeated grids.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Big Cozy is back — shop before sizes go`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your post-run layer: Big Cozy fleece`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Barrel-leg pants, funnel-neck fleece and the warmest layers for cold mornings.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New cold-weather pieces, just in — sizes are moving.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were confirmed. 39 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains.
- Redirect resolution, final destinations and link health are unverified for all 39.
- No issues found in the evidence available.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** three `<img>` elements have no alt text. They all use `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`.
  - The filename suggests a decorative arrow icon.
  - Decorative images should carry an explicit `alt=""`. An absent `alt` attribute lets some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
  - If an arrow is the only content of a link, it needs descriptive alt text or an `aria-label` on the link.
- The head declares `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, `<meta name="viewport">`, `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes`, and VML/Office namespaces, with client-specific dark-mode border fixes. No issues found there.
- `font-weight: 380`, `570` and `-2%` letter-spacing are non-standard values. They are only used on custom fonts (`Saans`, `KHGiga`) with Helvetica Neue and Times New Roman fallbacks, so this is not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) are visible in the truncated source, and QA flagged none. The body was cut off, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not a confirmed defect:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. The sender (`hello@e.lululemon.com`) is on a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with an ESP sending with proper authentication.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked because the footer was truncated.

- No issues found in the evidence available.
