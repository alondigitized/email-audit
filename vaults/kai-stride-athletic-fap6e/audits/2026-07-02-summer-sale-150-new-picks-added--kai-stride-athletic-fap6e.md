# Summer Sale: 150+ new picks added
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Summer Sale email in ten days, and lululemon is back with the same orange heat-wave energy they've been running since June 22. To their credit, they actually put a "Shop Men's Summer Sale" button in the hero this time — that's a step up from the Groove Wide-Leg disaster on July 1, where I sat there wondering if I'd accidentally subscribed to a women's activewear newsletter. So the intent is there.
- But then I scroll two inches and the first product I see is the Align™ High-Rise Relaxed-fit Tank in lavender. A women's tank. First. The opener. That's the creative decision someone made here and it kills whatever goodwill the Men's CTA just bought. The Pace Breaker shorts are in the grid — which is actually the one thing I'd click on — but I have to visual-hunt for them past a baby tee and women's high-rise running shorts.
- "On track for a win" is doing a lot of lifting as runner-speak and it gets the job done in about three words too many. I'll mark this as read. I won't forward it. If the Pace Breaker restocked in my size I'd already know from the app.

## What's working

- **"Shop Men's Summer Sale" button in the hero** — acknowledging my gender in the primary CTA is the right call; adidas buried their equivalent in a sub-nav two scrolls down on their 30%-off send.
- **"150+ new picks added" framing** — at least it's a count, not just "Summer Sale" again; it creates a reason to re-open vs. the June 30 "hottest picks" send.
- **Pace Breaker Linerless Shorts in the grid** — one actual running product I might buy; that's more than the June 21 restock email delivered.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, images all loaded, text legible at phone scale.

## What's weak

- **Women's Align tank leads the product grid** — after placing a Men's CTA in the hero, the first product is a women's item; that's a sequencing error that erodes trust fast.
- **Zero performance specifics** — no fabric name, no "4-way stretch," no "reflective details for dawn runs" — nothing that proves lululemon knows runners vs. athleisure shoppers; Nike's OG email sends have more product credibility in a single line of body copy.
- **No discount percentage anywhere** — adidas showed "30% off" right in the subject and above the fold; this email says "Summer Sale" and expects me to go find out what that means.
- **Cadence fatigue** — this is the fourth Summer Sale treatment since June 22, same orange creative system; the "150+ new picks" hook is doing real work to justify reopening, but it's a band-aid on a frequency problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the men's grid with men's products** — if you give me a Men's CTA, deliver immediately on that promise; swap the Align tank out of position one and put the Pace Breaker or a men's running short first.
- 2. **Add a discount anchor** — "Up to 30% off" or even "From $39" in the hero; right now "Summer Sale" is doing zero price-conviction work and adidas is eating lululemon's lunch on this exact beat.
- 3. **One running-specific copy line** — "Pace Breaker. Surge Short. Built for your long run, now on sale." That's all it takes to signal you know who you're emailing.
- 4. **Tighten the CTA at the bottom** — "Don't miss the moment." is the vaguest possible urgency; give me a date or drop the line entirely.
- 5. **Reduce cadence or differentiate harder** — if you're going to send again next week, the creative has to evolve more than a headline swap.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to [X]% off running gear, shorts, and more — today only.`
