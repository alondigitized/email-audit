# What’s new: pillowy High Loft Puffers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a good-looking puffer lookbook that has nothing to do with running. I captain a run club, and I opened it hoping for a cold-weather long-run layer. What I got was a mood board of people standing in a field in High Loft jackets. "Turn the outdoors into an invitation" is poetry. I wanted to know whether I can run 16 miles in it. The answer is no, and the email doesn't even pretend otherwise.
- Since 11/12 ("New jackets Define the season") lululemon has sent me jackets, then loungewear, then two Black Friday sends. Today they're back on jackets and the Black Friday banner is stuck at the bottom. The 11/16 email at least had prices. This one has none, and the one real offer is a small "Shop Early Access" link buried under a red banner. The GORE-TEX "Silent Type" block is the most honest product story in the email, but it's still outerwear and not run gear.
- Verdict: I'd skim it, I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. Two models in puffers and one obvious "Shop What's New" button, so the eye lands in the right place.
- The UltraPeak block gives me real specs: "water-resistant and windproof glyde fabric." That's closer to the product detail I actually care about.
- The Silent Type GORE-TEX callout is specific. It says noiseless, it's available now, and it gives a date (November 20).
- Men's and Women's links are split out, so I can go straight to the men's rack.
- Black Friday early access is flagged as members-only and app-only. That's at least a clear loyalty hook.

## What's weak

- There isn't a single runner in the email. Everyone is posed in a field or a studio, so it reads fashion, not sport. Nike would show someone moving, and adidas would at least show a court or track.
- There's no price and no percent. For a Black Friday week email, the offer is hidden in a small link at the bottom.
- The "Shop What's New" button appears twice for the same destination, and the red pill buttons are small for a phone tap.
- I can't tell sizing, fit or availability. Nothing says whether these puffers are for warm-ups, commuting or standing around.
- Every segment is "Shop X," so I never get a reason to pick one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member offer, not the puffer. Put the Black Friday early access up top with a number, since that's the only reason to open this week.
- 2. Add one run-relevant product, such as a packable insulated layer for pre-run warm-up or post-run recovery. Show it on a real runner. Lululemon's seminar tone works when it says something concrete. Nike would hype it, and I'd rather have the facts.
- 3. Replace the duplicate "Shop What's New" button with specific copy like "Shop men's puffers."
- 4. Cut the number of image grids and give me one product with a price, a fit note and a review score.
