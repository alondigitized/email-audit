# Samba, reimagined just for you
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- The subject had me primed for something runner-relevant — "reimagined just for you" sounds like it could be about a new Adizero colorway or a performance update. Nope. It's a metallic Samba. A lifestyle sneaker I'd wear to brunch, not to the 6 AM long run. After the Adizero Evo SL ZIP email two days ago — which was genuinely excellent and actually remembered I run — this feels like adidas spun the targeting wheel and landed on "Kai" by accident.
- That said, the Strava x adiClub block saved this email from being a complete miss. Unlock a Strava subscription through my loyalty points? That's the one offer in here I actually care about. Strava is the app my entire running club lives on. But it's buried below a fashion-shoe hero, which is a strange place to hide the only runner-relevant value proposition in the email.
- Net verdict: I scroll past the Samba, tap REDEEM NOW on the Strava block, and close the tab. I wouldn't forward this to anyone in my club — not because it's bad, but because the running relevance is too buried to bother explaining.

## What's working

- **Strava x adiClub is genuine runner value.** A free Strava subscription through adiClub points is the kind of loyalty perk that earns real goodwill — my whole club would care about this.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** Hero loads first, body copy is tight, two CTAs are clearly separated. No five competing offers shouting at me.
- **No render bugs.** Everything loaded crisp — after some janky mobile layouts from lululemon recently, this is a non-trivial win.
- **Samba feels timely.** Whether I'm buying it or not, I know this is a cultural moment for the shoe. This isn't a clearance push dressed up as a launch.
- **adiClub benefit is clearly labeled.** "STRAVA X ADICLUB" as a section header signals loyalty member value at a glance.

## What's weak

- **"Reimagined just for you" is hollow.** The metallic Samba in my inbox is the same email that went to every adidas subscriber who ever clicked anything. There's nothing "for me" here — I run marathons, not runways.
- **Leading with a fashion shoe to a runner is a mismatch.** The Samba is not a running shoe. Sending this as my hero offer after the Adizero email is like Nike sending a dad-cap campaign to their Vaporfly list.
- **The only runner-relevant offer is below the fold.** Strava x adiClub should be leading. Right now the one thing I care about is the second thing I see.
- **No price, no urgency, no deadline.** "SHOP NOW" goes to... the Samba at full price, presumably. Nothing in the email gives me a reason to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the blocks.** Lead with Strava x adiClub — that's the runner hook. Drop Samba metallic below as a "while you're here" lifestyle add-on. This single structural change transforms the email for my persona.
- 2. **Segment this properly.** If adidas knows I clicked the Adizero email and zero lifestyle emails this year, send me a runner-first version. The Strava block was clearly built for performance customers — actually send it to performance customers first.
- 3. **Give the Samba a runner's angle, or skip me entirely.** "Your recovery shoe just got a glow-up" builds a bridge to my identity. Right now there's zero connection between metallic fashion and my running life.
- 4. **Add urgency or exclusivity to the Strava offer.** "Offer valid through July 31 for adiClub members" would make me tap immediately rather than park it for later.

## Technical Audit — adidas "Samba, reimagined just for you"

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken redirect in the click-tracking layer will deliver a dead experience for at least one CTA. Plain-text fallback is absent entirely, which creates deliverability and compliance exposure.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Broken redirect (dv.adidas.com click tracker)**

The QA prober resolved `https://dv.adidas.com/o/be067481-c63d-4d62-b64f-e57b346976de?cp_tp=...` and received an error: `unknown url type: '/g/be067481-c63d-4d62-b64f-e57b346976de?txn=f4f6c980-852e-11f1-bb66-8632512a7a78'`. The tracker's destination is a relative path (`/g/...`) instead of an absolute URL — the ESP campaign record for this asset UUID is missing the scheme+host. Clicks on any CTA pointing to this redirect will fail. The same UUID (`be067481-c63d-4d62-b64f-e57b346976de`) also appears as a tracked image source, so both the image load and the click are affected.

**INFO — 23 tracking links not probed**

All pass through `click.comms.adidas.com` or `dv.adidas.com` redirectors. Final destination health is unverified beyond the one broken link above.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**HTTP image sources (4 instances, same asset)**

`http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`

Used four times (alt: "arrow"). Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail all block mixed-content images by default; this arrow will render as a broken image on those clients. The CDN domain supports HTTPS — the `http://` scheme is the only issue.

**HTTP tracking pixel**

`http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f85d9c665-dadf4d98-41ae-484e-8a30-ea21f55bb1c1-000000/...`

Same mixed-content risk; open-tracking data will be lost on clients that block it.

**Missing alt text (2 images)**

- `be067481-c63d-4d62-b64f-e57b346976de` (hero/product image via `dv.adidas.com`)
- `aifbPZGsSGtNFGtias-hcVGf6CZjHxiuC_aYvG30DT8=452` (tracking pixel)

Empty or absent `alt` on the hero image means screen-reader users get no content description; images-off users get a blank.

**`@font-face` declarations resolve to local system fonts only**

Both `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared with `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica'), ...` — no remote font URL. The fonts will silently fall back to Arial/Helvetica everywhere. This is likely intentional for email-client compatibility but means the custom typeface is never loaded.

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`**

This suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on all clicks. Any server-side referrer-based attribution on the landing page will receive no referrer. Clicks are still tracked via the redirect chain, but direct referrer attribution is zeroed out.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML (no `{{`, `%`, or `*|` patterns). The subject line "just for you" implies audience segmentation, but that appears to be copy framing rather than a dynamic field. Cannot fully confirm for body content not included in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**FAIL — Plain-text fallback is 0 characters**

The MIME multipart message contains no `text/plain` part. CAN-SPAM does not strictly require one, but its complete absence increases spam-filter scoring on several major MTAs (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_ONLY` rule) and means the email is unreadable on plain-text-only clients and some corporate gateways.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**

The header was not present in the relay passthrough. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, Gmail and Apple Mail will not show the one-click unsubscribe UI. For bulk senders, Google and Yahoo's 2024 sender requirements mandate this header.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured (RFC 8058)**

Required for Gmail's one-click unsubscribe button. Without it, users must click through to a web form rather than unsubscribing in-client, which increases spam complaints.

**WARN — Authentication-Results not captured**

SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown through the AgentMail relay. Sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`; DMARC alignment cannot be confirmed from available data.

**Present and correct:** `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` prevents email archive indexing.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot confirm UTM parameters on final destination URLs because all 23 CTA links pass through the `dv.adidas.com`/`click.comms.adidas.com` redirect layer and were not probed to their terminals. The one broken redirect (section 2) confirms at least one destination URL is misconfigured.

The `no-referrer` meta (section 3) will suppress referrer on any direct href that bypasses the click tracker (e.g., footer plain-text links, if any).
