# Spezial is the must-have holiday gift
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- I'm a running-club captain, and this is a lifestyle sneaker email. A pair of pink suede Handball Spezials is held up against a teal door. It's pretty, but it has nothing to do with my Sunday long run. The countdown banner (3 days, 5 hours) got my attention for a second, then I realized it's a generic Black Friday timer, not a Spezial offer.
- This is the fifth adidas send I've audited in about a week. The 11/21 and 11/23 emails were Black Friday, the 11/23 one was Club América jerseys, and the 11/25 one was the adiClub newsletter. Still no running shoe, no Adizero, no Boston, no runner. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The countdown banner is easy to read at a glance. The black bar with white and gold numerals is clear.
- The hero photo is clean and the shoe is the only subject. It looks premium.
- "Up to 60% off" is stated plainly in the banner, so I know there's a deal.
- The buttons are big, high-contrast black blocks. They're easy to tap on a phone.
- The copy is short and says what the shoe is: a court-born shoe with a premium upper.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for runners. Spezial is a handball-heritage fashion shoe, and the copy says "street style" and "accents any look."
- The "up to 60% off" banner doesn't say whether it applies to Spezial. Spezial is a hyped lifestyle drop that almost never gets marked down, so the banner reads as a bait-and-switch.
- "Shop Men / Women / Kids" are three identical buttons. None of them names a product, a size run, or a price.
- There's no price, no sizing or availability info, and no reviews. I can't tell whether the shoe is in stock.
- The email is the fifth Black Friday-adjacent send in a week. The cadence is exhausting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by interest. Send me a running product, like a Boston or Adizero, with a mileage or pace angle. Send the Spezial email to people who browse lifestyle.
- 2. Tie the countdown to one real offer. State the Spezial price or the exact discount, and drop the generic "up to 60%".
- 3. Replace the three gender buttons with one specific CTA and a price: "Shop Spezial, $100".
- 4. Add a size-availability line and a review count under the hero.
- Compare this to Nike. Its one-time-code email did one job and I was done in under a minute. And lululemon's Align email was at least a single, clear product story, even though it wasn't for me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spezial: the gift that's actually in stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left: Spezial + Black Friday deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handball Spezial, now with Black Friday pricing. Sizes are moving fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your pair before the timer hits zero.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, responsive media queries, `mso-font-alt` fallbacks). The one hard QA failure is the missing plain-text part (0 chars). Smaller issues are insecure `http://` image and click URLs and missing alt attributes. The compliance headers could not be checked because the AgentMail relay may have stripped them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skips click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Click-tracking URL over plain HTTP.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a2159f258f-24f1e8ef-4699-4e82-8162-a2458577f62c-000000/...` is served unencrypted. The tracking token is exposed in transit and the request may be blocked or flagged by clients.
- **Likely open-tracking pixel.** The "image" at that same `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/` URL has no alt text and a non-HTTPS source. I can't confirm its dimensions from the truncated HTML.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image source (6 occurrences).** The `arrow` image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` uses `http://`. Clients may block it or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Non-descriptive alt text.** The alt text `"arrow"` on that image is decorative noise for screen readers. It should be `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/85cef444-492a-4e05-8b20-ad057e63a8ad?cp_tp=...`
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` (the likely pixel above)
- **Viewport meta lacks `width=device-width`.** The tag is `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">`. The media queries cover this in most clients, but the tag is incomplete.
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars.** This is the [FAIL] result. Screen-reader and text-only clients get no fallback, and spam filters may penalize HTML-only mail. It could be a relay artifact, since the AgentMail relay may only capture the HTML part.
- **Minor:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is acceptable for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The source was truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN].** The QA tool notes the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** One-click unsubscribe support can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is a branded subdomain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.
- **Page-level robots and referrer meta tags.** `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` is fine for email. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is harmless, but it can suppress referrer data on landing-page analytics in web-view contexts.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 22 links are wrapped in redirect trackers (`click.comms.adidas.com`), and the QA tool did not resolve them. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Spezial" subject can't be confirmed from the HTML or QA data. Resolving a few redirects manually would settle this.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative.** This fixes the one hard failure. First confirm with the raw `.eml` that it isn't just stripped by the relay.
2. **Move `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` assets and links to HTTPS.** This covers the 6 `arrow` instances and the click or pixel image.
3. **Fix alt attributes.**
   - Set `alt=""` on the decorative `arrow`.
   - Add alt text to `dv.adidas.com/o/85cef444-…`.
   - Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Set the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.**
5. **Verify headers against the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click), plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, since the relay hid them.
6. **Resolve a sample of the 22 tracked links** to check destinations and UTM parameters.
7. **Improve QA tooling.** Ingest the full raw MIME so header and plain-text checks stop being inconclusive.
