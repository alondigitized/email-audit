# Check out this month's adiClub benefits, here is your August Newsletter
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually the most legit thing adidas has sent me all month. The Strava integration block stopped my scroll cold — connect adiClub and Strava, earn points for logging runs. That's the first time in this whole barrage of Sambas and archive sales that adidas built something for me specifically, not just a discount code. It's buried as block two of four though, which feels backwards.
- The rest is a grab bag — win a signed World Cup ball, win a Penn State mascot tracksuit (I don't go to Penn State, not sure why I'd want a bear costume), then a generic "unlock savings" CTA. It reads like four different teams each got one slot in the newsletter and nobody edited it into a coherent story.
- I'd click Connect Accounts on Strava, skip everything else, and not forward this — not embarrassing, just not link-worthy. Compare to lululemon's "Meet the new Fast and Free" (6/10) which at least led with a real product shot of people running — adidas buries its one relevant hook halfway down a newsletter that opens with a soccer jersey giveaway.

## What's working

- The adidas x Strava block is genuinely useful to me — I already log every long run on Strava, so points-for-activity is a real reason to engage, unlike another "shop now" push.
- "Own a Piece of History" — signed World Cup ball — is a clear, specific prize with a clear CTA button, even if soccer isn't my sport.
- Newsletter format is honest about what it is — this isn't dressed up as a sale, it's dressed up as a benefits roundup, and it mostly delivers on that.
- Clean black CTA buttons throughout, easy to spot and tap on mobile.

## What's weak

- Penn State Nittany Lion tracksuit giveaway means nothing to me — I'm not a college football alum audience, and running-specific content would've served this slot better.
- Soccer jersey hero image up top has zero connection to a guy training for weekly long runs — this is the same demographic miss adidas made with the Samba lifestyle pushes on 8/16.
- "Unlock Savings on Your Faves" CTA is vague — "almost everything, from the latest Samba to your favorite running shoes" tells me nothing about an actual discount amount.
- No mention of running club / community angle at all, despite Strava being right there — a missed chance to speak directly to people like me who run in groups.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Strava integration, not the soccer ball giveaway — that's the one block built for a runner, put it first.
- 2. Cut or swap the Penn State tracksuit block for something running-specific — a shoe drop, a club challenge, anything performance-adjacent.
- 3. Put a real number on "Unlock Savings" — "$15 off Samba" beats "save on almost everything."
- 4. Add a Strava-club tie-in line — "connect your run club's leaderboard" — since community is a real hook for me and totally unused here.
