# adiClub x FIFA Rewards just got better 🔥
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one with cautious interest — adiClub is actually how adidas earns goodwill with me, and "rewards just got better" had enough vagueness to make me curious about whether something in my running gear spend was going to score me something. Then I saw the hero: adiClub x FIFA Rewards lockup on a dark background, partnership copy, Messi sweepstakes. Dead end. I run, not kick.
- Credit where it's due: the account-connection angle is a real loyalty mechanic. Linking programs to gate bonus rewards is smart — it's structurally the same move as lululemon's credit card perks, and adidas executes it cleanly here. But every single reward dangled in this email is downstream of "buying FIFA World Cup jerseys," and my jersey drawer is empty. The double points hook dissolves the instant I read "FIFA World Cup 26™ jersey purchases."
- Mark as read, don't click, don't forward. My run club captain group chat has zero use for a signed Messi jersey raffle.

## What's working

- **adiClub framing is front and center** — the newsletter header earns immediate recognition; this is the same trust signal that made the "Last day to redeem your voucher" send (the one with a personalized code) actually land.
- **Double points module is cleanly scannable** — the 2× offer is isolated in its own content block; I got the value prop in under two seconds without reading every word.
- **CONNECT ACCOUNTS CTA is unambiguous** — one clear button with directional arrow, high in the layout, no hunting.
- **Zero render issues** — dark hero, three content modules, footer all load clean; not always guaranteed in adidas HTML-heavy sends.
- **Timing is legitimate** — World Cup 26 is a real cultural moment, not the recycled "Summer Kickoff Event" headline I've seen copy-pasted since early June.

## What's weak

- **Total category miss** — every offer, every CTA, every sweepstake entry requires caring about soccer gear. I'm a runner. There's not one hook aimed at me.
- **Three separate asks in one email** — double points, signed Messi prizes, game-used match ball. That's three sweepstakes entry flows before I even get to the footer. Lululemon's Father's Day send last week had one message, one button, and it was sharper for it.
- **"Just got better" is vapor** — the subject teases an upgrade without quantifying it. Nike's sends, for all their flaws, at least tell me what I'm getting in the subject ("Here's your one-time code"). This one makes me work to find out "better" means... FIFA jersey points.
- **Preview text is auto-generated noise** — "This product has promotions and rewards for connected members" is the laziest possible preheader. It adds nothing the subject didn't already imply and wastes the one line of inbox real estate that could've triggered a running-specific hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment runners out of pure soccer pushes** — if I've bought running shoes in the last 90 days and zero soccer gear, this email should either skip me or swap in a "2× points on running purchases" hook alongside the FIFA content. One additional line of copy would completely change the relevance calculation for a segment that is probably 30–40% of adidas's active buyer base.
- 2. **Cap at two content modules** — double points + one sweepstake. The triple-offer layout scatters attention and reads like a kitchen-sink blast rather than a member communication.
- 3. **Make the hero copy earn its spot** — "CONNECT ACCOUNTS, GET MORE REWARDS" is functional but forgettable. Lead with the number: "Earn 2× Points on Every World Cup Purchase — and unlock raffles only connected members see." Give me a reason before asking for the action.
- 4. **Rewrite the preheader** — the current one is dead weight. Something like "Link accounts now — double points are limited time" at least adds urgency and specificity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 2× adiClub Points on World Cup Gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your adiClub rewards just leveled up — here's how`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Link accounts now. Double points on FIFA jersey purchases, limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Connected members earn more — three new perks inside.`
