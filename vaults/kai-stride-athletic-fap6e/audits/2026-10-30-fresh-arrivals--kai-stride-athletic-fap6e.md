That's five lifestyle shoes and zero running shoes. Last week's hybrid race shoe email (10/28) was the first one in a while that spoke to me, and this one walks it straight back.

But "Elevate your sneaker game" is generic, and the Pikachu shoes are a costume next to the 10/23 "adidas I Pokémon" send. That's the third time in about a week that I've seen the same Pokémon beat. ## What's working

- The 2x2 grid shows the price and a star rating on every shoe. I can see $110 against $180 without clicking, which is more honest than a lot of grids.
- The ratings (4.6 to 4.9) are social proof sitting right under the product.
- The hero shoe is shot cleanly on a plain background, and the eye lands there first.
- One black SHOP NOW button is unmissable. It's big and has a clear arrow.
- The "prime options available" line is useful. It tells me I can get these fast.

## What's weak

- There's no running anywhere. Nike would at least put a Pegasus in a "fresh arrivals" send, and lululemon's "Early Access: Bestsellers in new editions" at least leans on performance apparel. This reads as a fashion drop for someone who isn't me.
- The Pokémon shoes take up three of the four grid slots. That's the same creative as 10/23, and it's a novelty buy, not something I'd wear.
- The headline and body copy are filler. "Discover the latest and greatest" tells me nothing about what's new or why.
- There's no offer, no urgency, no drop date, no sizing or availability info, and no member perk. adidas had a loyalty email on my screen last week, but nothing here mentions it.
- The preheader at the very top is plain underlined text, and it reads like a leftover rather than something designed.
- "Shop Now" goes to who knows where. The email gives me nothing to tell me it's a specific collection.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by category. If I've clicked Adizero or Evo SL, lead with running, with the Adizero Boston or Adios Pro as the hero and a real "new this week" tag. That one change would get me to click.
- 2. Replace the filler copy with a specific hook, like a drop date, a colorway name, or "lands today in all sizes."
- 3. Link each product to its own page, and add a secondary CTA like "Shop running new arrivals."
- 4. Cut the Pokémon repetition. Send it once, to the people who care about it.
