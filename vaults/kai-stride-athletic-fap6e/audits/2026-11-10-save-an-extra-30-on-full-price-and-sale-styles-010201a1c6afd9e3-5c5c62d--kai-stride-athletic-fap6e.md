# Save an extra 30% on full price and sale styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a discount code with a dark photo stuck on top. I got a 30% off sale with code COZY, and nothing in the email says "runner." The hero is a close-up of the three stripes on dark fabric with "THE HOLIDAY SALE" in serif type. It looks like a luxury-hotel ad, not a brand that knows what I do on Sunday mornings.
- I'll admit the offer is clear, and the 11/11 end date is real. But this is the seventh or eighth adidas email I've gotten in about two weeks. Last Monday was the Week of Deals "last call," then Anthony Edwards, Metallica, and Samba. Not one has shown me a running shoe. Lululemon's Winter 2026 drop at least put two people running on a dirt road in the hero. adidas has a whole running line and keeps hiding it.
- I'd open it out of curiosity, then bounce. I wouldn't forward it. I might click Shop Men if I needed a new pair for the club's winter block, but I'd go to the site and search Adizero myself.

## What's working

- The offer is easy to read. "30% OFF HOLIDAY SAVINGS" is big and plain, and the code COZY sits right in the body copy.
- The CTA buttons are large black blocks with white text. They're easy to tap on a phone.
- The deadline is real. The fine print says it ends 11/11 at 11:59pm PST, so this isn't a fake countdown.
- The layout is clean and loads fast. It has one hero, one headline and three buttons, with no clutter.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is running. The hero is a crop of fabric, with no product and no athlete.
- The fine print excludes Samba, Gazelle, Campus, SL 72, Spezial, select Ultraboost, and more. Those are the shoes adidas has been pushing at me all month, so the "extra 30%" feels like bait. Nike's sale emails at least tell me what's on sale.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner above the hero competes with the main message. It's a second CTA that goes nowhere specific.
- Shop Men, Women and Kids is the laziest possible split. I want Shop Running. The email doesn't know I'm a runner, even though I'm an adiClub member.
- The preheader, "Start your holiday shopping early," shows up as a plain underlined text link, and it reads like filler.
- The legal block is longer than the actual pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the abstract hero with a real product. For a runner segment, show an Adizero or Supernova on a cold-morning road. Nike would lead with a Pegasus here, and it would work.
- 2. Replace Men/Women/Kids with category buttons, with "Shop Running" first for people like me.
- 3. Show the exclusions in the body. Say "30% off running, training and outerwear" instead of "tons of styles," so people know what the code covers.
- 4. Cut the "best sellers" banner. One email, one job.
- 5. Send fewer emails. Three days between sends would make the 30% feel like an event.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off running gear, ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code COZY: extra 30% off through 11/11`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Adizero, Supernova and winter layers are in. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your code works on running shoes and outerwear until 11:59pm PST tomorrow.`
