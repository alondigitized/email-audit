- No render bugs anywhere — images loaded, text is legible, tap targets are reasonable
- The 30% off is a real, unambiguous number with a clear "SHOP NOW" button — no math homework required

## What's weak

- This is the fourth FIFA/soccer-themed email in two weeks from adidas — the collab creative is wearing thin and I'm not the target
- The 30% off was already the *subject line hero* three days ago on June 30; recycling it as a secondary footer feel here makes it feel like a rerun rather than a reason to act
- That big gold/orange chunky lifestyle sneaker in the middle section is pure streetwear hype — it signals immediately that adidas is selling to a different customer
- Zero running relevance anywhere in the visible email — no Adizero, no Ultraboost, no training gear in sight
- The preheader "Explore the latest prices and sellers" looks like leaking comparison-widget copy — it does nothing to sell this email and made me do a double-take on who sent it
