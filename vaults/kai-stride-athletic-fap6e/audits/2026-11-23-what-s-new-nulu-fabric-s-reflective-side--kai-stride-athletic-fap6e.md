# What’s new: Nulu™ fabric’s reflective side
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday-season send from lululemon in about a week, and it's a yoga email. The subject says "reflective side," and I opened it hoping for night-run gear. What I got was people stretching on tatami mats in satin-panel black. "Reflective" here means "reflect on your holiday," not "be seen at 6 PM in November." That's a bait-and-switch for a run-club captain.
- It looks great. The photography is clean, and the "Hush the Holiday rush" hero is calm. But the Black Friday banner in the lower third is the only commercial content, and it gives no number. The 11/19 "Black Friday Sale is in motion" send at least had prices. This one is a mood board with a sale logo stapled on.
- I wouldn't click. I might forward the puffer to my partner, but nothing here is for the long run. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "Hush the Holiday rush" hero is a clear, calm hook, and the model and styling are cohesive.
- The two red "Shop What's New" and "Shop Yoga" buttons are visible and unambiguous.
- The Black Friday banner is a strong visual break that tells me there's a sale.
- The "We think you'll also love" grid has some layers that could double as cold-weather run gear.
- The footer is clean, with an app download link and a store finder.

## What's weak

- Nothing is run-specific. Yoga poses, a puffer and satin panels aren't what I buy for the weekly long run.
- "Reflective side" in the subject misled me. Nothing visible is reflective in the running-safety sense.
- There's no offer. "Hundreds of styles" under "Right here, right now" has no percentage, price or deadline.
- The cadence is tiring. The 11/17 puffers, 11/18 GORE-TEX, 11/19 sale and 11/21 "new contenders" emails all ran the same lookbook pattern.
- The "Shop Women's Sale" and "Shop Men's Sale" tiles show loungewear, not performance gear. Those labels are small, and the CTAs are text links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero, with a percentage or price and a deadline. Skip the mood copy.
- 2. Segment me. I'm a run-club buyer, so lead with a reflective or night-run piece, such as a Fast and Free or Swiftly layer with visible reflective detail.
- 3. Rewrite the Black Friday module with a concrete number and a clear button.
- 4. Cut the yoga repeat. Nike sends a running-specific hero to runners, and lululemon should too.
- 5. Make the sale tiles buttons, not tiny text links.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Reflective details, warm layers, and sale prices through Cyber Monday.`
