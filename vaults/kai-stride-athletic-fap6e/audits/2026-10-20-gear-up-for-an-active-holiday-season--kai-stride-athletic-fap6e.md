# Gear up for an active holiday season
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "this is a gift-guide filler send, and nothing in it is for me." I run a club and buy for the Sunday long run. This email shows two people on a bus bench in fleece and sneakers, then a gift card on a red background. There's no run, no shoe name, no price and no reason to act this week. The subject says "active" and the creative isn't active at all.
- lululemon's Pace Breaker email had a man mid-stride at dusk, and I could tell who it was for in about a second. adidas had the Messi kit and the Metallica tee in the last few days, and none of it was for a runner. This one has even less to hold onto. It doesn't tell me anything about the brand, either. A runner would at least see an Adizero or Adios in there.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, see that nothing is aimed at me, and mark it read. Three Shop buttons stacked under "Mix and Match" tell me they don't know who I am.

## What's working

- The gift card hero is clean and seasonal. The red background pops and the card art is nice, so I get the idea instantly.
- The Men / Women / Kids buttons are big, black and easy to tap with a thumb on a phone.
- The layout is simple. There's one image, one headline, one line of copy and one button per block, with no clutter or render bugs.
- The body copy is readable without zooming, and the adidas logo up top makes the sender obvious.

## What's weak

- There's no running anywhere. The models sit on a bus bench in lifestyle fleece, and "active holiday season" in the subject doesn't match what I see.
- There's no offer. It has no percent off, no free shipping and no deadline, so nothing makes me act today.
- "Shop Men," "Shop Women" and "Shop Kids" send me to broad category pages. No product is named, so I don't know what I'd be clicking into.
- The black "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner repeats the CTA before the hero. That's two "Shop Now" asks in the first screen, and the real hero sits below the banner.
- The preheader link at the top, "Explore endless gifting possibilities," is underlined and reads like leaked plain text. It looks a bit broken.
- The footer is bare: no member points and no mention of adiClub. My 50 adiClub points never come up, so adidas treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a concrete product and a deadline. For my segment that's the Adizero line or a cold-weather running layer, with a ship-by date for gifting.
- 2. Cut the black banner and the duplicate "Shop Now." One hero, one primary CTA.
- 3. Segment the send. I should see a running-first version with a shoe and a cold-weather layer, because I've bought running gear from adidas before.
- 4. Put a number on the gift card. "Gift cards from $25, delivered by email in minutes" gives me a reason to click.
- 5. Add a loyalty line, like "Members get early access to holiday drops."
- 6. Clean up the preheader so it stands alone and isn't a stray underlined link.
