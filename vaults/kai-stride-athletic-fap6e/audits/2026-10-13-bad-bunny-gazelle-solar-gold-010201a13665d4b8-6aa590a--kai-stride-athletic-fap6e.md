# Bad Bunny Gazelle Solar Gold
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "that's a nice shoe, and I'm not the buyer." It's a suede Gazelle in honey yellow with a Bad Bunny collab tag. I run a club, I buy for the Sunday long run, and this is a terrace sneaker. Nothing in it says miles, cushioning, or a drop. The photography is gorgeous, but it's aimed at sneaker-culture people, not runners.
- The bigger problem is the CTA. Both buttons say "SIGN UP," and the email never tells me what I'm signing up for. A raffle? A waitlist? A launch alert? There's no price, no date, and no sizing. That's a lot of ambiguity for a button I'm supposed to trust.
- This is also the eleventh adidas email in about two weeks. After Pikachu on 10/12 and the Apple TV points giveaway the same day, I'm not reading this one. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. If I have a sneakerhead friend, maybe, but the email doesn't give me much to pass along.

## What's working

- The hero shot is strong. Top-down on sand, outsole and upper side by side, and the honey suede reads well.
- The black-and-gold colorway is clear and consistent across both images.
- The layout is clean and I can read the body text on a phone without zooming.
- The Bad Bunny lockup next to the Originals trefoil makes the collab obvious right away.
- The buttons are big, black, and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- "SIGN UP" tells me nothing. There's no mention of a raffle, waitlist, or drop date anywhere in the email.
- There's no price, release date, or size information. The tag in the photo reads "06.23.24," which looks like an old date and just confuses things.
- The copy is mood-board language: "golden glow of a Caribbean sunset," "while the sun goes down." It never says what the shoe is like to wear.
- There's nothing for a runner. Nike would at least have put a performance shoe somewhere in the send, and lululemon would have tied the drop to a use case.
- The two CTAs are identical, and the second image is just a closer crop of the first, so the email says the same thing twice.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA say what happens. "Enter the draw" or "Get launch alert" beats "SIGN UP," and one line above the button should state the date and how the release works.
- 2. Add the practical details: price, release date, and size run. Sneaker-drop people want those first.
- 3. Cut the sunset poetry and give me two lines on the build, such as the suede, the gum outsole, and the fit.
- 4. Segment it. A running-club captain shouldn't be getting Originals lifestyle drops in a ten-day streak. Send me the Adizero side and this goes to the people who'd care.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive HTML 4.01 Transitional template with ESP click-tracking on every link. The QA run flagged one failure (empty plain-text part) and several warnings: `http://` image sources, missing alt text, and a viewport meta without `width=device-width`. The HTML supplied was truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s could not be fully inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Untested links:** 19 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing URLs are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** an image from `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a13665d4b8-6aa590a0-193d-4ea9-b998-3f788c3451e3-000000/ZkCQVDDCIUTqf2utkPZvenbtf…` is served over plain HTTP. It looks like the open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may not record opens.
- **Insecure image host:** the "arrow" image `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` is HTTP and is used four times. Switching to `https://` would fix all four.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/92045bc6-0515-42fd-9ccb-f0bf1e6d5696?cp_tp=…`
  - The tracking pixel above. For a pixel, `alt=""` is correct, but the attribute is absent.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` has no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale as intended. The responsive `@media` rules (`max-width:639px`, `.container{width:100%!important}`) partly compensate.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `<html lang="en">`. The `lang` attribute is present, which is good. The doctype is common in email and not a defect.
- **Fonts:** the `@font-face` rules for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` use only `local()` sources with `mso-font-alt: Arial`. This is a fallback to system fonts, not a defect.
- Alt text on the other images was not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The rest of the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text part:** the text version is 0 chars. A missing text/plain alternative is a deliverability and spam-filter risk. It may also be a capture artifact of the relay, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these. Confirm against the original message. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be verified. The sender is `adidas@us-news.comms.adidas.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be checked from this data.
- **Not verifiable:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they were not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 19 links are wrapped in ESP redirects and were not resolved, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. Destination URLs, campaign tagging and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a text/plain alternative. Check the raw message first to rule out a relay capture issue.
2. Change the HTTP image and pixel hosts (`image.link.adidas.com`, `click.comms.adidas.com`) to HTTPS.
3. Add `alt` to `dv.adidas.com/o/92045bc6-…` (descriptive) and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers. Capture `Authentication-Results` directly, not through the relay.
6. Resolve the redirects on the 19 tracking links and record their final URLs and UTM params. The audit pipeline should do this by following the redirects (a non-probing GET with redirect following) instead of skipping these domains.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, postal address, merge tokens and remaining alt text.
