# Align low-rise styles are here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a women's yoga and studio email, and I'm a 33-year-old guy who captains a running club. The hero is a woman's legs in dark Align leggings, followed by a grid of women's tanks, shorts and joggers. Nothing in it is aimed at me. It also lands as the sixth lululemon send in roughly ten days. After 11/24's "Out now: breathable winter running gear," where I finally saw a runner in a sage shell, this is a step backward. It's closer to the 11/23 Nulu yoga send, which also had nothing for the long run.
- The only relevant thing is the Black Friday banner and the "Shop Men's Sale" tile, and both sit past the halfway point. The banner has no percentage, no price and no end date. The 11/19 "Black Friday Sale is in motion" at least gave me real numbers. Here I'd have to click blind.
- Verdict: I'd skim it, not click, and not forward it. Mark as read. If I were buying for my partner I might tap Align, but I'm not that person in this inbox.

## What's working

- The hero copy, "Low rise, down to stretch," is clean, and the black Shop Align button is unambiguous.
- The product tiles are simple and each has a clear label, like "Align No Line Low-Rise Shorts." If you wanted Align, you'd know where to tap.
- The Black Friday banner is visually loud, so the season is obvious.
- Having "Shop Men's Sale" next to "Shop Women's Sale" is the one nod to someone like me.
- The footer shortcuts (Download the App, Find a Store) are tidy and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The whole top two-thirds is women's yoga product, and I see no runner, no run-specific copy and nothing about the club.
- The Black Friday banner has no offer in it. After five prior BF sends I want a number, and Nike and adidas lead with one.
- The Men's Sale tile is a single small image link buried mid-email, and I can't tell what's in it.
- The Wild Dot Multi print block pushes a women's yoga pose and a "first in stores" message, which has nothing to do with my weekly long run.
- It's a very long scroll with six near-identical product tiles, and there's no sizing, fit or availability info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Men on the running list should get a men's hero. Sending the women's Align hero to everyone wastes the slot.
- 2. Put a concrete offer into the Black Friday banner, a percentage or price plus a deadline.
- 3. Cut the tile grid from six to two or three, and add a men's run row (Fast and Free, Surge) for the right segment.
- 4. Move "Shop Men's Sale" above the fold for male subscribers.
