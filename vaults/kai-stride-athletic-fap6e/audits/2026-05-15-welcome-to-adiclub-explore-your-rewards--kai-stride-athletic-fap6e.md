# 🎁 Welcome to adiClub! Explore your rewards
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so adidas just welcomed me to adiClub and dropped a 15% voucher front and center — that part I respect. The hierarchy is clean, the promo code is right there, the expiry date is visible. Functionally, this email does its job as a transactional welcome send.
- But here's my problem: I'm a runner. I signed up for adiClub probably because I was buying Adizero SL2s or Boston 12s, and this email has zero idea about that. The hero image is straight lifestyle streetwear — no one is running anywhere. "Member Picks" drops me into Shoes, Accessories, Clothing, New Arrivals — generic as it gets. Compare that to Nike's triggered welcome flows which at least segment by what you browsed; adidas just shipped me the same email they'd send a Samba kid. Lululemon's recent mesh gear send (which I also reviewed) had the same casual-wear problem, but at least the copy spoke to movement. This doesn't even try.
- I'd click the REDEEM ONLINE button and use the 15% on my next race shoe purchase, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone in the club. It doesn't earn that.

## What's working

- **15% voucher is impossible to miss** — dedicated section, large headline, personalized promo code `WELCOME-DVLBTH`, and a hard expiry date. That's how you do a welcome offer.
- **"ADICLUB LEVEL 1 UNLOCKED"** at the very top is a smart gamification hook — it frames this as a progression system, which I notice.
- **Benefits list is scannable** — Free Shipping, Early Access, Members-Only Products. Four bullets, no fluff. I skimmed it in two seconds.
- **REDEEM ONLINE button copy is specific** — "REDEEM ONLINE" is an actual verb+noun. Nike often does vague "Discover" garbage; adidas wins here on CTA clarity.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, text is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is pure streetwear lifestyle** — not a single person running. For a welcome email sent to someone who just bought performance footwear, this is a miss. I feel like I'm looking at a Foot Locker flyer.
- **"Member Picks" is completely untargeted** — four generic category tiles (Shoes, Clothing, Accessories, New Arrivals). Nike's welcome flow ties back to what you actually bought or browsed. Adidas just showed me the homepage in email form.
- **Subject line buries the 15% off** — "Explore your rewards" is vague. The 15% voucher is the killer hook and it's not in the subject at all. That's a missed open-rate play.
- **No running-specific signal anywhere** — no "built for the road," no run-club nod, no performance language. Lululemon at least commits to movement even when it's casual-wear. This email could have been sent to someone who only buys slides.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the 15% off in the subject line** — "15% off your next order" beats "Explore your rewards" every day of the week. That's the concrete hook people actually open for.
- 2. **Segment Member Picks by purchase/browse history** — if I just bought running shoes, show me the Boston 12, the Adizero, the run tights. Not the Samba section.
- 3. **Swap the hero image for a runner** — one performance athlete, track or road, any gender, and this email suddenly speaks to half their subscriber base differently.
- 4. **Surface a running-specific benefit** — "Early access to shoe drops" hits different for someone in a run club than generic "Early Access to Sales."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Level 1 unlocked — here's 15% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem before May 29 · free shipping on every order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn points on every run, every purchase — here's how`

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024) for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. Its absence risks inbox placement degradation and bulk-folder routing.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` method) is also missing. This is now a hard requirement for Gmail bulk senders. Recipients cannot use the native Gmail unsubscribe button, which increases spam complaints.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header not captured**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown via the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm authentication is wired correctly. Sending domain is `us-news.adidas.com`; DMARC alignment for this subdomain against the `adidas.com` org domain should be verified independently.
