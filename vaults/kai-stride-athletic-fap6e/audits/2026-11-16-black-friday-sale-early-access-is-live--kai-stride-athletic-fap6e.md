## What's working

- Real prices are shown with strikethroughs ($78 to $59, $68 to $49). That's a big step up from yesterday's no-number hero.
- The Metal Vent Tech tee and Zeroed In shorts are men's pieces I'd actually wear on a run.
- The two-up Women's/Men's split under the hero lets me jump straight to my side.
- The two black "Shop Early Access" buttons are clear and sit where I'd expect them.
- The deadline is stated: Nov 18, 11:59 p.m. PCT. It's credible and time-bound.

## What's weak

- The hero is a giant red type-only block with no product and no discount, so I have to scroll to learn what the sale is.
- "Only on the app" with "Early Access is limited to purchases made in the lululemon app" is a gate. Nike's app-first drops at least pitch the app as a perk. Here it reads like a hoop.
- The fine print is a dense paragraph, and it says "Discounts vary by item." There's no headline "up to X% off," so I can't judge the offer.
- Nothing speaks to runners. There are no run-specific callouts, no fit or fabric detail, and no size availability. "Selection is limited to marked styles while supplies last" is the only stock hint, and it's buried.
- Half the grid is pink women's wear, and the men's side has only one tile in the male-run lane.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one specific number and one product in the hero, like "Up to 30% off Metal Vent, members only," instead of four lines of type.
- 2. Add a run lane. A tile row labeled "Run" would show whether lululemon knows runners. Nike has been better at this when it leans on Pegasus-style hero drops with specific product call-outs.
- 3. Move the app gate and the end date up near the CTA. Don't make me find them in the fine print.
- 4. Add a size or stock cue, like "Selling fast in M and L," to give me a reason to act now.
- 5. Make the product tiles tappable with a short fit note, like "Run-ready, anti-stink," so I'm not guessing.
