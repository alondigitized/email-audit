# Black Friday Sale: up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh lululemon Black Friday-season send in about two weeks, and it's the first one that actually states the deal in plain words. The hero is dark red and bold, and "time to lock in" is a decent line for a guy who's trying to stay consistent with training. Then I scroll and find the same generic grid I've seen all week: women's, men's, pants, shorts. Nothing about running.
- Compare that with the 11/24 "breathable winter running gear" send, which I scored 7 because it showed a guy actually running in a shell. This one has a man perched on a stool in a vest and shorts, and the shorts close-up could be any gym session. It reads as fashion-shopper-in-athletic-gear, not "this brand knows runners." Nike would at least put a shoe or a track in frame. adidas has sent me three near-identical Black Friday blasts, so I know this fatigue, but adidas at least leads with "up to 60%" in the subject and hero.
- Would I click? Probably once, to see whether the Men's shorts are really marked down, since I need long-run shorts. I wouldn't forward it. It's an okay catch-all for the last day of the sale, not a send that speaks to me.

## What's working

- "Up to 50% off select styles in-store and online" is the first concrete offer I've seen from lululemon all week. After the 11/26 "500+ new styles" email that said nothing, this is a relief.
- "Time to lock in" fits my mindset. It's motivational without being hype-machine, and it sits closer to the seminar tone than Nike's shouting.
- The Men's tile sits right next to Women's, so I don't have to hunt for my section the way I did on the 11/25 Align women's email.
- The layout is clean, with big white-on-dark type that's readable on my phone without zooming.
- The fine print states the end date and time: November 30, 11:59 pm PCT. That feels honest.

## What's weak

- There's no running anywhere. No shoe, no run, no reflective gear, and the one man shown is sitting on a stool.
- The urgency is buried. The deadline only shows up in the footer fine print, so the top of the email has no "ends Monday" cue.
- "Select styles" and "up to 50%" give me no hero product and no price. I can't tell if my shorts are 50% off or 15%.
- The two CTAs, "Shop Black Friday" and "Shop Best of Black Friday," are nearly identical and both generic. They're pill buttons on dark, but only the first is above the tile grid.
- The footer's gift guide, app, new, and store links are faint grey on near-black. They're hard to read and feel like leftovers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Monday") and show one real sale price on a real product. That would lift this more than anything else.
- 2. Segment by category. I buy running gear, so show me run shorts, a vest and a shell with a "run" tile, not a generic grid of four.
- 3. Cut the duplicate CTA, or make each CTA specific ("Shop men's run shorts").
- 4. Add a loyalty or member line if I'm a member, since Nike and adidas lean on member perks (adiClub in the 11/26 send).
- 5. Brighten the footer links so they're legible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The sampled source (`lululemon`, "Black Friday Sale: up to 50% off") is a mature SFMC template with dark-mode and Outlook handling. The only confirmed defect is four footer arrow images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this capture. The "57% pass rate" is mostly skipped or unverifiable checks, not failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified, not broken:** 28 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so there was no HTTP probe. Link validity, redirect chains and destination status are untested.
- No broken links were observed. Nothing in the data shows a malformed `href` or a mismatch between link text and destination.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** 4 instances of `<img>` missing alt text. All use the same source: `https://images.lululemon.com/is/image/lululemon/SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right?$pngstandard$`.
  - These are decorative arrows. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
  - If the attribute is absent entirely, some readers announce the filename.
- The `<head>` has `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes` for light and dark. It also includes VML and Office namespaces and Gmail, Outlook.com and Android dark-mode CSS hacks.
- Fonts are `Saans-Regular` and `KHGiga-Light` with fallbacks (`Helvetica Neue, sans-serif`, `Times New Roman, serif`). The fallbacks are reasonable. The font files themselves aren't visible in the truncated source.
- The rest of the HTML is truncated, so I can't assess the body structure, the `<title>`, or other images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. `Authentication-Results` was also absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The QA output itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers. I'm not treating them as sender defects.
- The From address `hello@e.lululemon.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign for authentication alignment, but that is inference, not evidence.
- The visible `<head>` has no unsubscribe or physical-address evidence. The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 28 links were skipped and the truncated source doesn't show any `href` query strings, so UTM presence and consistency, and landing page alignment, are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (or `alt="Right arrow"` if the arrows are meaningful) to the four `SFMC_Email_Footer_Arrow-Right` images. This is the only confirmed fix.
2. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` capture, or direct from the sender, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
3. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow without click-registering, or sample a few) to check final destinations and UTM parameters.
4. Raise the HTML truncation limit, or analyze the footer separately, to confirm the unsubscribe link, physical address and any merge-token leakage.
5. In the QA tooling, report skipped and unverifiable checks separately from passes, so the pass rate isn't misleading. Here it is 57% with 0 issues.
