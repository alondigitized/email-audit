# App exclusive sale: 25% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Ten days after the Dropset Pro email — the one I actually forwarded to the running club — adidas sends me this. An app-only sale with 25% off everything. No runners. No running shoes. No seasonal hook. Just a dark hero and a button that says "DOWNLOAD THE APP." I already have the app. I've been in adiClub long enough to have earned points on four or five purchases this year. Treating me like I need to be introduced to the adidas app in mid-July is a miss that lululemon and Nike both avoid — they at least gate their member emails behind "you're already in."
- The offer itself is clear and the 2x points callout for adiClub members is a genuine win. But the creative has zero running DNA. No Adizero. No Ultraboost. No "built for your long run" framing. If I'm being honest, this email could have been sent to someone who last bought adidas Originals slides in 2019. That's not who I am and not who this should be speaking to given what I know they can do — the Dropset Pro send proved they can write for runners when they want to.
- I'd mark this read, not click. I definitely wouldn't forward it. The offer is real but the execution feels like it was built for the broadest possible list, which is fine for volume but doesn't make me feel seen.

## What's working

- **25% off is clear and prominent** — "25% OFF IS LIVE" in large type is the first thing I read; no guessing what the offer is.
- **adiClub 2x points callout is legitimately useful** — acknowledging that I'm a member and giving me a reason beyond pure discount to use the app is the right instinct.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs** — the dark hero renders properly, button is visible, no broken images or overlapping text eating into the message.
- **Subject line does its job at the inbox level** — 28 characters, concrete offer, no emoji chaos. Short enough to read on my lock screen.

## What's weak

- **"DOWNLOAD THE APP" CTA is wrong for an existing subscriber** — I'm on this list because I'm an adiClub member who bought through adidas. Sending me a download prompt is like lululemon asking me to create an account on their newsletter.
- **Zero running signal anywhere in the email** — no shoe callout, no performance copy, no athlete. After the Dropset Pro email set the bar, this regression to category-agnostic sale mode is jarring.
- **"For a limited time" with no date** — that phrase has been in promotional emails since 2009. If the sale ends July 18, say July 18. Nike does this better even on their transactional sends.
- **Preheader is leaking what looks like a forward-to-friend code** — the tiny text at the top of the email reads "Did this find you? Use code: [something]" — that's garbage hitting the preview pane and it burned the one slot where you can add context to the subject line.
- **No product anchor** — a 25% off app sale with no featured shoe or category is just an invitation to browse. I'm a runner; point me at the running tab. The Dropset Pro email knew what I wanted. This one doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "DOWNLOAD THE APP" with "Shop 25% Off in the App"** — assume existing subscribers already have it; the CTA should sell the act of shopping, not the act of downloading.
- 2. **Add one hero product with running equity** — drop the Adizero Boston or Takumi Sen into the visual. Even a ghost image of a shoe makes this email feel like it was sent to a runner, not a demographic.
- 3. **Give the sale a deadline** — "Ends Sunday" or a specific date. The Dropset Pro email had race-day urgency; this one has none.
- 4. **Fix the preheader** — the forward-to-friend code is eating your preview text. Use that space.
- 5. **Lean into the adiClub angle harder** — if this is a member-only rate, make that the hero, not the app mechanic. "Member Price: 25% Off" would hit differently.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off in the app — adiClub members get 2x points too`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member sale is live: 25% off, app only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Sunday — shop running, training, and more at 25% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Logged-in adiClub members also earn double points this weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — adidas "App exclusive sale: 25% off"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a 38% automated pass rate driven by one broken CTA deep-link, a zero-byte plain-text fallback, mixed HTTP/HTTPS image loading, and unverifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers at the relay layer. Core rendering structure is sound.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken deep-link — primary CTA**
The click-redirect at `https://dv.adidas.com/o/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?cp_tp=v3.eJ...` resolves to an inner destination with an invalid URL scheme:
```
/g/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?txn=343a8d00-7ffa-11f1-89bb-9295eb1db778
```
`/g/...` is not a recognized URL scheme (not `https://`, not `adidas://`, not a valid universal link pattern). This will silently fail in most email clients — the user taps the CTA and nothing opens.

**[WARN] 16 tracking links unvalidatable**
All click links route through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects. Destination endpoint health and UTM integrity could not be probed.

**[WARN] Open/pixel tracker over HTTP**
`http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f63bb29a1-2deacd48-f2ae-47ff-8b27-e38150ed715d-000000/51cTD7Xi79jW1JbX3VxMt-ey5...` — tracking pixel served over HTTP; blocked by default in Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients, invalidating open-rate data for this send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two HTTP image sources — may be blocked**
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
This "arrow" asset appears twice and is served over HTTP. Gmail strips non-HTTPS images by default; the asset will render broken for a large share of recipients.

**[WARN] Missing `alt` text on two images**
- Dynamic Movable Ink image: `src https://dv.adidas.com/o/eb567f93-3591-4a29-9813-d49cec1c6cfe?cp_tp=...` — no `alt` attribute. If the Movable Ink render fails, recipients see nothing.
- Tracking pixel `51cTD7Xi79jW1JbX3VxMt-ey5...` — `alt=""` should be explicit even for decorative/tracking images.

**[INFO] Duplicate `@font-face` blocks**
`AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are declared twice — once in the main `<style>` block, once in the "Yahoo Android App" style block. Both fall back to local system fonts only (no remote `src: url()`). No rendering impact, minor HTML bloat.

**[INFO] HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Older but standard for broad email client compatibility; no issue.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `*|`, `%%`) visible in the source. Subject line carries no personalization — consistent with a broadcast promotional send.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback: 0 characters**
The MIME message has no text/plain part. Several spam filters (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) penalize HTML-only messages. Also a weak CAN-SPAM signal.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
QA relay did not see this header. If it's missing at the ESP level (not just a relay artifact), Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the native unsubscribe UI and this send violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (effective Feb 2024, enforced for >5k/day senders).

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click unsubscribe required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender policy. Absence here compounds the above risk.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown via relay. Sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`; DMARC alignment with `adidas.com` in the From display name should be confirmed independently.

**[INFO] `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`**
This instructs the browser not to send a `Referer` header when following email links. Does not affect CAN-SPAM directly but will suppress referrer-based attribution in GA4/Adobe Analytics for any recipient who opens in a browser.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[FAIL] Primary CTA broken at the link layer**
The broken `/g/...` scheme means the "app exclusive sale" CTA does not reach the app or a fallback web page. No UTM parameters can be attributed from this click.

**[WARN] UTM integrity on 16 tracked links unverified**
All links go through `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects, which could not be resolved. Cannot confirm that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended on final landing pages.

**[WARN] `no-referrer` meta suppresses organic referrer data**
Even for working links, browser-based opens will report `(direct)` instead of the email referrer in analytics.
