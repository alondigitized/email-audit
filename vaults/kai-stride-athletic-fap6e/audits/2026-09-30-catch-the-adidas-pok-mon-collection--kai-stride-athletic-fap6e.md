## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text alternative [FAIL].** The text part is 0 chars. This may be an artifact of how the relay captured the message. If it is real, it hurts deliverability and leaves text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks with nothing.
- **Insecure images.** The "arrow" image is `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`, and it appears twice. Non-HTTPS sources may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Missing alt text on two images.**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/7757405e-34e0-45b7-a188-b82cd6262834?cp_tp=...`
  - The tracking pixel above.
  
  The pixel should carry `alt=""`. If the dv.adidas.com image is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Viewport meta is incomplete.** It is `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">` with no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale the layout as intended. The media queries and `.container{width:100%!important}` rules partially compensate.
- **Web fonts are not actually loaded.** The `@font-face` rules for AdihausDIN and AdineuePRO map only to `local('Arial')`, `local('Helvetica')` and similar. Text will render in the system fallback in all clients.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is common for email and not a defect. `lang="en"` is present.
