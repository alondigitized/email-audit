## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed deliverability defect, an empty plain-text part (0 chars). It also has several insecure `http://` image and tracking sources and two images without alt text. Auth and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **21 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`), so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking asset.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1d8ab83c7-f5f34a02-ad68-4d41-83c9-031cfcfcb0f7-000000/UNDAZlGtdWYwg9T71fj41U4Rg...` is loaded as an `<img>` over plain HTTP. It is likely the open-tracking pixel. Clients may block it or flag mixed content, which would under-report opens.
- **Wrong sort of host for tracking.** The same `click.comms.adidas.com` host serves both click links and the image, so an HTTP-only pixel is inconsistent with the rest of the setup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing.** [FAIL] The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. Clients and spam filters that prefer or score the text part will see nothing.
- **Six `http://` "arrow" images.** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` appears 6 times. Non-HTTPS sources may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings. The same asset is probably reachable over HTTPS on the same host.
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/7a465e05-8e07-4089-855f-1cd407d71fe1?...`
  - the click pixel image `UNDAZlGtdWYwg9T71fj41U4RgHPA3njotGuqmUGasrQ=452`
  
  The first matters most. If it is a content image, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. The pixel should carry `alt=""`.
