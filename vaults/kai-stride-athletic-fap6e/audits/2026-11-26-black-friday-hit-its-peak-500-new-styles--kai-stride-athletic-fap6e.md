# Black Friday hit its peak: 500+ new styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth lululemon Black Friday-season send in about a week. The hero is big, bold and red, but it doesn't say anything. "New to black friday sale" with "500+ styles added" tells me there's more stuff, not what the stuff is or what it costs. There's no percentage, no price and no product I could picture wearing on a long run.
- It's also the second send in a row with no running in it. On 11/24, "Out now: breathable winter running gear" scored 7 because someone actually ran in it. The 11/25 Align email was women's studio wear, and today's is a studio-lit grid of people posing on the floor. The guy in the sweatshirt and dad cap looks like he's waiting for brunch. I'd skim it, not click, and not forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The hero is loud and readable, so the Black Friday message lands in about a second.
- The two identical "Shop Black Friday" buttons are dark, high-contrast and thumb-sized, so the next step is clear.
- The Women's, Men's, Leggings and Pants grid is simple to scan, and it has a men's option.
- The fine print states the deadline: offers end Nov 30 at 11:59 pm PST.

## What's weak

- There's no number anywhere above the fold, no "% off" and no price. Compare adidas's "up to 60% off," which at least gave me a reason to care.
- Nothing here is running. I see crop tops, a sweatshirt, leggings poses and wide-leg pants. For a club captain, that reads as a fashion brand, not a running brand.
- Four tiles with generic labels ("Shop Pants") send me to category pages, not a product I'd wear.
- The fine print is buried in the footer: "selection limited to marked styles," "discounts vary by item." That's the opposite of an honest offer.
- It's the sixth near-identical sale email in a week. After the 11/21 "new contenders" send, the cadence is fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero, either a price or a "% off marked styles" line, and name the deadline: "ends Nov 30."
- 2. Add a running row, such as a men's winter run jacket or half-zip, with the price shown. That would be a clear win over the 11/24 send.
- 3. Replace the category tiles with specific products and a visible price or "was/now."
- 4. Move the "discounts vary, marked styles only" qualifier up next to the offer.
- 5. Space out the sends, or at least segment so runners get run content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday: winter run gear, marked down`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Nov 30: run layers up to X% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Half-zips, shells and tights for cold long runs. Marked styles only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `500+ styles just added, including new run gear for the cold.`
