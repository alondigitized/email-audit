# Summer Sale's softest sets
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- I knew what was coming the moment I read "softest sets" — and yeah, the render confirmed it. Full screen of lilac matching sets, bras, tanks, and the Flow Y Ruched Bra front and center. Not a single men's piece in the grid. This is the second consecutive wrong-gender send lululemon has landed in my inbox this week — July 1 was the Groove Wide-Leg disaster, and now it's the Lilac Play capsule. At least on July 2, they tucked a "Shop Men's Summer Sale" button into the hero. This one doesn't even do that.
- The production quality is genuinely good — clean photography, consistent lilac palette, the orange Summer Sale header renders sharp — but that just makes the targeting miss more frustrating. I run in lululemon shorts every long run Sunday. I've spent real money with this brand. And they still can't figure out that I don't wear a bra.
- Mark as read. No click. No forward.

## What's working

- **Clean visual hierarchy** — the orange "SUMMER SALE" hero loads fast and the eye lands exactly where they want it; no layout chaos
- **Consistent brand campaign** — the coral-to-orange palette has been their Summer Sale signature since June 22, and it still looks polished
- **"Shop Summer Sale" CTA is unambiguous** — button copy is clear, placement is above the fold
- **Photography is excellent** — the lilac palette is cohesive and well-shot; this *would* be compelling for the right audience

## What's weak

- **Entire content block is women's** — every model, every SKU (Align Bra, Flow Y Ruched Bra, Nulu Rib Scoop-Neck Bra) is women's product; I'm a male subscriber and this is the second straight women's-only send
- **No men's entry point anywhere** — July 2 at least added a "Shop Men's Summer Sale" button; here there's no bridge at all
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — fifth Summer Sale email in roughly two weeks; the orange hero that felt bold on June 22 is now wallpaper
- **Zero performance/running signals** — "softest sets" and "Lilac Play" are lifestyle/yoga language; nothing here speaks to the 6 AM track club crowd I run with
- **No offer specifics** — "Summer Sale" is everywhere but no percentage off, no price-drop delta is visible; compare that to adidas who put "50 points" right in the subject line so I knew the exact value before I opened

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the list by gender** — this is the single highest-leverage fix. The July 2 send proved they have men's sale product; route it to male subscribers. Two wrong-gender sends in six days erodes trust faster than any cadence issue.
- 2. **Add a men's recovery row** — even one "While you're here, men's shorts are on sale" module at the bottom would give male subscribers a reason to scroll; costs nothing to template.
- 3. **Surface a concrete discount** — "up to 40% off" or "starting at $49" turns "Summer Sale" from a vague banner into an actual reason to click; Nike's transactional sends are sparse but at least they tell me the exact value exchange.
- 4. **Vary the hero creative** — the orange SUMMER SALE treatment has appeared five times in two weeks; even a subtle refresh (different crop, different color way) would reset the scroll-past muscle memory.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Men's summer essentials — now in sale`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your pace, your price: men's sale picks`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `ABC Shorts, Metal Vent tees, and more — up to 40% off this week only`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `The shorts you run in are in the sale. Shop before your size goes.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Font CDN — 5 broken asset requests (HTTP 403)**

All five custom fonts fail to load:
```
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Light.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Regular.woff
https://globalstaticassets.lululemon.com/fonts/Saans-Medium.woff

This affects `Saans-*` (body, headlines, CTAs) and `KHGiga-Light` (guest-voice serif display). **Severity: high** — affects every email client that supports web fonts.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not found**

The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5k/day) under their 2024 enforcement policy. Absence risks inbox placement degradation or bulk-folder routing.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header — not found**

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is absent from relay-captured headers. Gmail requires this for bulk senders. If the header exists at the MTA layer but was stripped by the AgentMail relay, this finding should be re-verified against raw MTA headers directly.

**Authentication-Results — not found**

SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from the captured headers. The sending domain `e.lululemon.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain) should have both SPF and DKIM configured — absence in captured headers is likely a relay artifact, but must be confirmed against the original SMTP envelope.

**CAN-SPAM** — footer content not visible in the truncated source, so physical address and unsubscribe mechanism presence cannot be confirmed from this review.
