# WORLD CHAMPIONS. SPAIN 🇪🇸⚽️🏆
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- I'll be straight: when "WORLD CHAMPIONS. SPAIN" hit my inbox this morning, I already knew the score. Yesterday was THE FINAL AWAITS - the cinematic buildup email - and now here's the celebration lap. The ESPAÑA hero in gold is genuinely striking creative. adidas clearly won the kit deal and they're wringing every last impression out of it. Good for them. Zero for me.
- I'm a running club captain. Yesterday adidas sent me the Adizero Evo SL ZIP launch - the email I actually forwarded to the club group chat - and the FIFA cinematic on the same day. Now today it's the victory sequel. Three adidas sends in roughly 24 hours, two of them soccer. My inbox is getting the FIFA broadcast package whether I want it or not. The running email yesterday was a 9. This one goes into the read-and-archive pile without a click.
- Would I forward it to someone? Yeah - the one friend who stayed up watching the final. Running club group chat? That's a hard no. If I forward that I lose credibility as a running-adjacent human.

## What's working

- The ESPAÑA hero image is premium and cinematic - gold letterforms over a packed stadium is a legitimately strong creative choice
- "STARS SHINE TOGETHER." is tight and clean; doesn't over-explain the moment
- "SHOP SPAIN WINNERS COLLECTION" is one of the more specific CTAs adidas has run lately compared to the app-sale email's generic dark hero with a "SHOP NOW"
- The adiClub match ball giveaway is smart loyalty architecture - gives members a reason to engage beyond just buying a jersey
- Layout is clean top to bottom: two distinct sections (buy the gear / enter to win the ball) don't step on each other

## What's weak

- I run, I don't play soccer - this email treats me like every other adidas subscriber with no segmentation signal whatsoever
- This is the second consecutive soccer email in under 24 hours; even if I watched the final last night, the cadence is aggressive
- lululemon's "Summer Sale, on its final run" was also off-persona for me but it had "Move fast" in the hero - at least a nod toward motion; this has zero running escape hatch anywhere in the scroll
- The top banner - "Explore the latest adidas best sellers | SHOP NOW" - is a lazy, generic cold-open for what should be a moment-marketing send; that banner copy kills the drama before the hero even loads
- "SPAIN MADE HISTORY WITH IT. NOW IT'S YOURS TO OWN." - the transition from jersey collection to match ball giveaway feels abrupt without a clear visual or copy bridge

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the list.** This email goes to soccer fans exclusively. Running subscribers get a version that bridges the same World Cup moment to the Adizero line - "The shoes of a champion. On your feet, not on a pitch." One hero swap, one copy change, massively higher relevance.
- 2. **Kill the top banner.** "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" does not belong on a World Cup win email. The moment should own 100% of the frame from pixel one.
- 3. **Tie the adiClub giveaway to the Adizero brand story.** adidas boots. adidas ball. adidas kits. There's a direct thread from the winning pitch to running shoe innovation - pull that thread instead of treating the giveaway as an isolated loyalty mechanic.
- 4. **Cap the send frequency.** Five adidas emails in one week - July 13 newsletter, July 15 app sale, two on July 18, now today - is too many. For runners getting two soccer emails back-to-back, this trains the unsubscribe reflex.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the official 2026 FIFA World Cup™ winners collection — plus adiClub members get a shot at the match ball.`
