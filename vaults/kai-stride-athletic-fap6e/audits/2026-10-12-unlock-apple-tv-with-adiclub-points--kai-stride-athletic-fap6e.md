# Unlock Apple TV with adiClub points
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth adidas email in about ten days. It's another one where the best thing in it is a giveaway, not a product. My gut reaction was "Apple TV for points? I have 50 of them." The newsletter's best moment for me is at the very bottom: an Evo SL Exo prize pack with actual running shoes in it. That's the first real runner content since the Chicago email on 10/3, and adidas buried it under a TV subscription, a Samba raffle and a football giveaway.
- I'd skim this on my commute and probably enter the Evo SL giveaway, because it's free and it's running gear. I wouldn't redeem points for Apple TV. I wouldn't forward it to the club either. Nike would lead with the shoe, and lululemon's "Just in" drop at least keeps one hero and one idea per send. This is an adiClub bulletin that happens to include a running prize.

## What's working

- The Evo SL Exo prize pack is a good hero. The shoes, cap, jacket and socks are laid out cleanly, and "showing off your team pride on the run" is the only line that spoke to me.
- The adiClub points-for-Apple-TV idea is concrete and easy to grasp. "2 or 3-month subscription" tells me exactly what I'd get.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan. Each block has one headline, a short blurb and one button.
- Black buttons with clear verbs (REDEEM NOW, ENTER NOW) are obvious and tappable on a phone.

## What's weak

- The runner content is the last block, so it's the one most readers will never scroll to.
- The Apple TV hero is MLS and TV-show posters. It's a media promo, not an adidas product.
- Two giveaways (Samba and rookie footballs) plus the Evo SL make three raffles in one email. That's too many, and it all reads as "enter to win" with nothing to buy.
- There's no price, size or availability anywhere, and no sign of what the points cost. The Apple TV offer says "Terms apply" and nothing more.
- The top "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner has a SHOP NOW button, but it's a generic best-seller push with no running angle.
- The Samba is the second one in this send. I already got the Samba gift email on 10/8 and the Club América jersey on 10/11.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Evo SL Exo block first. It's the only item aimed at runners, and a shoe hero converts better than a TV logo.
- 2. Say what the Apple TV deal costs in points. Showing the points price next to the offer makes it something I can act on.
- 3. Cut the giveaways to one per send, or fold them into a single "ways to win" strip.
- 4. Add a real running product row (Adizero, Evo SL) with sizes and a price, so there's something to buy.
- 5. Segment it. I've been sent football and lifestyle content for ten days. A runner tag would have put the Evo SL up top.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your adiClub points just got more useful`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter to win Evo SL Exo, apparel and a gift card. Members only.`

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
