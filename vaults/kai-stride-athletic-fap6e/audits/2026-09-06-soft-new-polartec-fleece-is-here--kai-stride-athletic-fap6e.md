- "Brisk mornings and cold starts" is exactly my seasonal use case — early fall long runs.
- The shoe cross-sell (Chargefeel 3, Wildfeel Trail) stays in athletic category, not sandals or handbags.
- Early Access fall 2026 teaser at the bottom flags member-only early access, which is a real loyalty signal if I'm a member.

## What's weak

- Eight-plus product shots and only one guy is actually wearing the fleece before the very bottom of the email — this reads as a women's fleece drop with a male afterthought.
- Random cinder-block guy shot adds nothing — no running context, no styling info, just a prop.
- Zero performance framing beyond the one spec line — no mention of moisture-wicking, layering-for-a-run, temp range, or wind resistance, which is what I actually need to decide if this replaces my current shell.
- "We think you'll also love" grid is almost entirely women's product again — feels like the algorithm doesn't know my gender, let alone that I run.
- No urgency or offer at all — no price, no % off, no deadline. It's a pure lookbook, which is fine for lifestyle browsing but gives me zero reason to act today.
