# Get up to 60% off gifts for Black Friday
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the third adidas Black Friday blast I've gotten in four days. It's the 11/23 "Up to 60% off for Black Friday is live" email again with a countdown timer added. The same couple is in the same red track jacket, and the same "UP TO 60% OFF" is stamped across them. I'm a running-club captain and nothing here is for me. I see a Badbo, Campus 00s, a quarter-zip, and a volleyball giveaway. There's no running shoe, no Adizero, no Ultraboost, no Supernova, and no runner anywhere.
- The offer itself is clear. The black countdown banner is the strongest thing in the email, and the urgency feels real because the timer is visible. But "up to 60%" on "some of our favorite styles" is vague. I don't know whether the discount covers anything I'd actually wear on a long run.
- I'd mark this read and move on. I wouldn't forward it. If the buttons said "Shop Running" I'd click, but I'd have to hunt for the category myself.

## What's working

- The countdown banner (02 days, 20 hours) makes the deadline concrete and easy to believe.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" is huge and readable, so the offer lands in about a second.
- The Shop Men / Women / Kids buttons are big, stacked, and easy to tap on a phone.
- The Top Sellers row links to specific products, not just the homepage.

## What's weak

- There is no running at all. Even the "Top Sellers" are lifestyle items, and I can't tell if running shoes are on sale.
- The creative is the same as the 11/23 Black Friday send, and I also got the 11/26 Spezial gift email today. The cadence feels like spam.
- The Harper Murray volleyball giveaway takes up half the email and is irrelevant to me. It reads like the 11/25 adiClub raffle newsletter again, and it buries the sale.
- "Up to 60%" with no product-level prices gives me nothing to anchor on. There are no sale prices or percentages on the Top Sellers.
- The gender-split buttons feel generic. They ignore what I've bought or browsed.

- 1. Segment by category. Send runners a hero and Top Sellers of running product, with real sale prices on each tile.
- 2. Cut the giveaway block from the sale email and send it separately to adiClub members.
- 3. Replace the Men/Women/Kids buttons with category buttons: "Shop running shoes," "Shop apparel."
- 4. Show price-off on each product tile, such as "$90 → $54".
