# Win the holidays with top gifts
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "another adidas gift email, and still nothing for a runner." It's the fifth or sixth adidas send in a week. The 11/10 "Gift iconic style with Samba" and the 11/09 Metallica email were already fighting for the same inbox space. This one has no offer, no code, and no shoe I'd run in. The hero shows a family walking past a green storefront with shopping bags, in fleece, track pants and Sambas.
- It looks clean, and nothing is broken. But "The Gifting Game Plan" is a headline with no plan behind it. The body copy says "comfort and style" and "sport-inspired," which is fashion language. Nike would at least put a Pegasus or a Vomero in the hero. lululemon's recent "Steady State" send put a guy in a hoodie and sweats, and that one at least felt like it was talking to me. This one talks to a gift-shopper who isn't me.
- I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read. If I were buying a gift for someone in my club, I'd go straight to the site.

## What's working

- The hero photo is well shot. The three-generation family with shopping bags reads as "gift" right away.
- Three buttons (Shop Men, Shop Women, Shop Kids) are big, black and easy to hit with a thumb.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and it's quick to read.
- The adidas logo and black-and-white styling are instantly recognizable, so I know who it's from.

## What's weak

- There's no offer. After the 11/10 "extra 30% off" send with code COZY, a no-discount email feels like a step backward.
- There's no running. The product is fleece, Sambas and a basketball, and nothing mentions miles, training or a long run. Nike and lululemon at least show up with movement in the frame.
- The "Explore the latest adidas best sellers" banner and the "Shop Now" button above the hero compete with the real message, and the button looks like a bare placeholder.
- The three Shop buttons all go to broad department pages, not specific products. "Trending gear" in the preheader is vague.
- No shipping cutoff dates or holiday deadlines, so there's no reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show one hero product with a price, like a gift under $100, and link it to that product. Right now every button goes to a department.
- 2. Add a "Shop Running" button. I'd drop one of the demographic buttons to make room.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery," to create urgency.
- 4. Cut the redundant best-sellers banner or make it carry the offer.
- 5. Space out the sends. Gifting content four days in a row trains me to skip.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with responsive media queries, but it has one failed QA check (no plain-text part) and several insecure `http://` image sources. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured by the AgentMail relay, not because the email is shown to be non-compliant.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **21 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so the QA check skipped the HTTP probe. Their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure image sources.** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1ce286b79-26440dac-17af-49be-870c-a0554db5212b-000000/81WO5MdO1XrJqRsJKZlZQwGOF…` is loaded over plain HTTP. It has no alt text, and its `=452` suffix suggests a 1×1 open-tracking pixel. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will not fire it, so open rates are undercounted.
- **Repeated `http://` arrow icon.** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` ("arrow") appears 6 times over HTTP. The same host on HTTPS should be tried, and the icon may be blocked or flagged as insecure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/65b3f1dc-b0d4-45c5-8a1a-a70d1fcdd7fc?cp_tp=…`. This is a content image, so it is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
  - The `click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…=452` tracking image.
- **Web fonts are not real web fonts.** All four `@font-face` blocks (`AdihausDIN` 400/700, `AdineuePRO` 400/600) use only `local('Arial'|'Helvetica'|…)` as `src`. No font files load, so the brand fonts never render, and every client falls back to Arial or Helvetica. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Legacy doctype.** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is paired with `lang="en"` and no `<title>` in the visible source. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the title.
- **Desktop image rule.** `@media (min-width:640px) { img {max-width:600px;} }` is not `!important`, so inline `width` attributes may override it. This isn't confirmed as a defect without the rest of the HTML.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and QA data show no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text alternative is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` part, which hurts spam scoring and accessibility. This is the one confirmed failure.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so verify against the raw message from the sending ESP.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` should be checked directly.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address were not verified.** The HTML is truncated, so the footer is not in the provided source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Link destinations are hidden behind click-tracking redirects (21 links skipped), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **cannot be confirmed** from the provided data. This is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed defect.
- `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is present. It has no effect in most email clients, but if the HTML is viewed in a browser it strips referrer data on outbound clicks.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` part with the offer copy and key links. This clears the only FAIL.
2. Serve the arrow icon and the tracking pixel over HTTPS. Switch `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com` references from `http://` to `https://`.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to `dv.adidas.com/o/65b3f1dc…`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, as decorative images should have it.
4. Re-run the audit against the raw RFC 822 message to verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) values.
5. Extend the QA pass to resolve tracking redirects, for example by following them with a HEAD or GET request. That would let it check final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status.
6. Store the full HTML and not a truncated copy, so the footer's unsubscribe link and address can be checked and token scanning covers the whole body.
