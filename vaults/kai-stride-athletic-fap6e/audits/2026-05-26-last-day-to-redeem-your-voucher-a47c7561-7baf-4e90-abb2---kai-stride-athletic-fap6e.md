# ⏳ Last day to redeem your voucher
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Ten emails from adidas in eight days, and this one lands with an actual hook — my voucher, my account, ticking clock. I'll give them that. The adiClub branding and personalized voucher code make this feel less like a blast-and-pray and more like something addressed to me specifically, which is more than I can say for the Anthony Edwards 2 drop yesterday morning or the Manchester United throwback last week.
- But here's the thing I noticed immediately: yesterday adidas sent me a sitewide 30% off with a promo code anyone could use, and now today they're reminding me that my personalized 15% voucher — valid on full-price items only — expires tonight. That's not a good look. You handed out the better deal publicly, and now you're urgency-drumming me toward the lesser one in my name. That's the kind of thing that makes a loyalty member feel like the program is theater.
- "Top Picks For You" is doing a lot of heavy lifting as a personalization claim. What I see in the product grid are lifestyle and casual sneakers — nothing that reads performance, nothing that reads running. Nike's recent one-time code email at least got me in and out efficiently; lululemon still doesn't know I run fifty miles a week either. adidas has the data — adiClub tracks my purchases — and they're still surfacing fashion-forward product over Ultraboost or Adizero. Mark as read. No forward.

## What's working

- **Voucher code surfaced clearly** — `WELCOME24L-BTH` is right there in the email body, no hunting required; the "REDEEM ONLINE" button is unambiguous
- **Deadline is explicit** — "Valid only on full-priced items until May 27, 2026" is printed right under the offer; no digging required
- **adiClub framing lands** — the loyalty tier wrapper makes this feel like a member communication, not a promo blast
- **No render bugs** — email is clean, images load, hierarchy reads top to bottom without breakage
- **Profile completion CTA is secondary** — they tucked the 50-points pitch below the fold; it doesn't compete with the primary voucher action

## What's weak

- **15% full-price is a step backward from yesterday's 30% sitewide** — any subscriber who opened both emails will clock this immediately; it undermines the loyalty pitch
- **"Top Picks For You" aren't running picks** — I see lifestyle sneakers in that product grid, not Ultraboost, not Adizero; adidas has my purchase history and still can't surface a running-relevant shelf
- **Tenth email in eight days** — cadence is broken; urgency fatigue is real and the "last day" hook lands soft when I've been hit daily
- **Preheader is wasted** — "UNLOCK MORE REWARDS | View this email online" is junk; it tells me nothing that the subject didn't and burns the inbox preview slot

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the product grid to performance running** — if adiClub has purchase data, serve me Ultraboost or Adizero picks, not whatever's trending for lifestyle shoppers; that single change would make "Top Picks For You" earn its headline
- 2. **Reconcile the offer against yesterday's 30% sitewide** — either frame 15% as loyalty-exclusive with a member-only benefit layer, or don't send the bigger public promo the day before; the juxtaposition kills trust
- 3. **Rewrite the preheader** — use the inbox preview slot to reinforce the urgency or the specific offer amount, not site nav copy
- 4. **Compress the email** — the app download and profile completion asks dilute the single-action urgency; one email, one job
- 5. **Lean into the runner segment** — even one line like "for your next long run" in the body copy costs nothing and makes this feel targeted
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 15% runs out tonight — shop before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: your adiClub voucher expires today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `15% off full-price — redeem before midnight tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your exclusive member discount ends in 24 hours`

## Technical Audit — adidas "Last day to redeem your voucher"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

All 8 custom font assets return HTTP 403 from the S3 origin, guaranteeing fallback rendering in every client. One CTA link contains unencoded whitespace in the URL, making it technically malformed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Malformed CTA URL (unencoded space)**
The "Complete my profile" link contains a literal space character in the URL path:
```
...tkn=wywsMJxPH%2Ff27N1I1awRPs0A9SrXTw2cQ2J9POo8tBup3Sw62gOkKEyTSIkpmo8%2Fb UkrsPo9CaLH2p3...
```
The space between `%2Fb` and `UkrsPo9Ca` is unencoded. RFC 3986 requires `%20`. Depending on the redirect handler at `go.adidas.com`, this will either break the token validation or truncate the URL at the space, producing a broken or incorrect destination.

**INFO — 22 tracking links skipped**
All click-redirect links via `go.adidas.com` were not probed. Destination integrity cannot be confirmed by this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — All 8 custom fonts returning 403**

Both `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` families (Regular + Bold, woff2/ttf/woff formats) are hosted on `s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com` and return 403. The bucket policy is blocking public access. All clients will render the `mso-font-alt: Arial` fallback. Affected assets:
- `AdihausDIN-Regular.woff2` / `.ttf`
- `AdihausDIN-Bold.woff2` / `.ttf`
- `adineuePRO-Regular.woff2` / `.woff`
- `adineuePRO-Bold.woff2` / `.woff`

**WARN — 14 images missing `alt` text**

Multiple content images have empty or absent `alt` attributes, including repeated instances of `2373748f...b.gif` (appears 7 times) and key content images `b9aa5d2a...jpg`, `5bf3fd66...jpg`. Screen readers will skip these silently; images-off clients will render blank space.

**WARN — 2 images served over HTTP (mixed content)**

Two assets use `http://` origins:
- `http://image.link.adidas.com/.../966ef092...png`
- `http://image.link.adidas.com/.../37231f63...gif`

HTTPS-strict clients (Gmail, Outlook.com) will block these. Switch to `https://image.link.adidas.com/...`.

**WARN — Image CDN subdomain is `image.staging.link.adidas.com`**

The majority of images (12+) are being served from `image.staging.link.adidas.com` — a staging subdomain — in what appears to be a production send. If this subdomain is rate-limited, firewalled, or decommissioned post-campaign, images will break for anyone who opens the email after deployment.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) are visible in the truncated source. Cannot fully confirm without the complete HTML.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The QA relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This is required by Google and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5K/day senders). If the header is present upstream but stripped by the relay, re-verify directly against raw headers.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo for bulk senders) depends on this header alongside a `List-Unsubscribe` mailto/https value. Absence means Gmail will not render the native unsubscribe button and may route to spam.

**WARN — SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Sending domain is `us-news.adidas.com`; DKIM alignment should be verified independently before treating deliverability as confirmed.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The "Complete my profile" CTA includes `cm_mmc` attribution params and an AppsFlyer deep link with `af_channel=Welcome`, `af_adset=MemOnb_Comms5`. UTM/attribution parameters are structurally present, but the malformed token (unencoded space, see §2) means the destination URL may not resolve correctly, breaking both the user experience and attribution tracking for this specific CTA.

Plain-text version is 72% URLs by character count (7,352 / 10,249 chars). This is a strong spam-filter signal; plain text should contain human-readable content, not URL dumps.
