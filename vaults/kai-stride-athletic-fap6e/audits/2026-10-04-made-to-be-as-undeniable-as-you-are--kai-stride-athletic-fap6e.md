# Made to be as undeniable as you are
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- Cool shoe, wrong inbox, again. I captain a running club, and adidas has now sent me a Pikachu collab on 9/30, a cow-print Samba on 10/2, a Chicago running email on 10/3, and now a rhinestone SL72. That's four sends in five days. The 10/3 Chicago email with the Adizero kit was the one that actually spoke to me, and this one undoes that goodwill.
- The SL72 is a retro terrace shoe, and the hero crops to a pair of baggy jeans and a sneaker. There's no runner in sight, no mileage, no cushioning story. "Enter a new era of expression with a bold rhinestone pattern" is fashion copy through and through. Lululemon's Smooth Spacer email was seminar-tone, but at least it knew who its customer was. This one reads like it went to everyone on the list.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to the one friend in the club who wears Sambas to brunch. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The SL72 hero is bold, clean, and unmistakable. The big white wordmark over the rust-brown wall makes the eye land on the product immediately.
- The layout is simple: one hero, one headline, one paragraph, one big black button. Nike's one-time-code email was just as lean, and this one is easy to scan on a phone.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is large and high-contrast, an easy tap target with no hunting.
- Nothing is hidden or gimmicky. There's no fake urgency and no fine print, so it feels honest, just not useful to me.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for runners. A rhinestone retro lifestyle shoe means nothing to someone buying for the weekly long run. adidas knows I'm a runner from the Adizero sends, and it ignored that.
- There's no offer, price, member perk, or reason to act today. Even adidas's own "We've got something for you" loyalty email gave me a hook. This one gives me nothing to do.
- The layout looks off. The black "best sellers" banner sits wider than the hero image, and the hero doesn't span the full width, so the right edge looks ragged.
- The hero shows only legs and jeans, so I can't tell who this is for or how the shoe looks on foot. There's no price, sizing, or fit info either.
- The cadence is exhausting. Pikachu, Samba, Chicago, SL72, all in one week, and only one of them was about running.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by category. Send runners like me the Adizero or Evo SL story, and keep SL72 for the lifestyle list. This one change would lift the email more than anything else.
- 2. Give me a reason to click: a price, a launch date, a "members get it first" line, or a sizing and availability note.
- 3. Fix the banner and hero alignment so both span the full width.
- 4. Show the shoe properly, on-foot with a full-body shot, and add the price.
- 5. Slow the cadence. Skip a day between fashion drops, as lululemon did with its quieter lounge sends.

# Technical Audit: "Made to be as undeniable as you are" (adidas)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text alternative (0 chars, the only QA FAIL). It also loads images over `http://`, including what looks like the click-tracker's open-tracking image, and some images have no alt attribute. Unsubscribe and authentication headers couldn't be checked because of the relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains and the HTTP probe skipped them. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure image sources.** Both the 2 `arrow` images and the tracking image are served over `http://`. Some clients may block or rewrite them, and Gmail's image proxy upgrades them while others won't.
  - `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a10439f956-2a3c5560-1f17-4b74-b7f2-e118fcabf907-000000/i1xMf87SRcQRtXv6Qtzp6FNE-pXIPcEDhRX5wvHhYfM=452`
- The click-tracking host serves its image over `http://` while the rest of the email uses `https://dv.adidas.com`. This is mixed protocol use. The `/CI0/…` path looks like an open-tracking image, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 2 images**:
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/d48059db-99f5-4abd-9956-e59302126a7c?cp_tp=…`: a content image, so screen readers get no description.
  - `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…=452`: probably the tracking image. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Plain-text fallback is missing** (0 chars). This is covered under Compliance and Recommendations.
- **Viewport meta lacks `width=device-width`.** The source has `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">`. It's minor, because the responsive rules key off `@media screen and (max-width:639px)`.
- **Custom fonts are not actually loaded.** Every `@font-face` for `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` uses `src: local('Arial'), local('Helvetica'), …`, so no web font file is fetched. This is harmless but redundant. The text renders in a fallback font.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible, truncated source. The rest of the HTML wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so it's unconfirmed whether the original message lacks them. Check the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`. This is a relay limitation and not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** The truncated HTML cuts off before the footer, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM's in-body unsubscribe link or postal address.
- **Plain-text part is missing**, which is a deliverability and accessibility problem (QA FAIL, 0 chars).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this couldn't be verified. The links are wrapped in click-redirect URLs and were skipped by the probe. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are only visible after resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` MIME part with the email's copy and links. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. Serve all images over `https://`: `image.link.adidas.com` (the `arrow` icon) and `click.comms.adidas.com` (the tracker, if the ESP supports an HTTPS tracking domain).
3. Add descriptive alt text to the `dv.adidas.com/o/d48059db-…` image, and `alt=""` to the tracking image.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml` outside AgentMail to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Treat the QA warnings as unconfirmed until then.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. That would confirm the footer unsubscribe and address, any merge tokens, and the resolved UTM and landing URLs for the 13 tracking links.
6. Optionally add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta and drop the no-op `local()` `@font-face` blocks.
