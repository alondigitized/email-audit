- My gut reaction: this is a raffle newsletter, and I'm a running-club captain. The hero is a pink Inter Miami jersey next to some sculptural trefoil pillows. That's fashion and fan gear, not anything I'd wear on a long run.
- It's also the second adiClub "enter to win" email I've gotten, and it comes right after the 11/23 Club América jersey send and the Black Friday emails. adidas still hasn't followed up on the 11/20 "Supercharge your training" email, the only one this month that treated me like a runner. Compare lululemon's "Out now: breathable winter running gear," where a guy in a shell was actually running. That one respected my category. This one doesn't.

## What's working

- The "Member Appreciation Weekend" countdown (15 days, 6 hours) is a clear, credible time hook, and it's the only loyalty-flavored beat that feels like a reward.
- The black "ENTER NOW" buttons are clean and high-contrast, and the CTAs are easy to find and tap on a phone.
- The hero giveaway has a concrete prize list: a jersey, a $200 gift card and 12 months of Apple TV. That's more specific than most promos.
- The layout is tidy, with a clear stack of modules and generous spacing. Nike's one-time-code email was this clean too.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for runners. A pink MLS jersey, a BW Army sneaker and Billie Jean King are lifestyle and fan content, with nothing about performance.
- The banner at the top reads "OUR adiclub NEWSLETTER • Y", so the headline is cut off. It looks like a broken marquee.
- Three giveaways in a row dilute each other. Three identical "ENTER NOW" CTAs don't tell me which one matters.
- The Toca Boca cartoon block takes up nearly a fifth of the email, and I have no idea what the "exclusive discount" actually is. It's vague, with no percent and no code.
- There's no price, no sizing and no product I could buy. The black "Shop Now" bar at the top is the only commerce hook, and it's generic ("best sellers").
- The giveaway for the BW Army shoes carries an asterisk, and the fine print is buried at the bottom.

- 1. Segment by sport. Send running-club members a version that leads with a running shoe or training drop, like the Hyperboost Edge, and put the giveaway below it.
- 2. Pick one hero giveaway and demote the rest to a single line each, so there's one "ENTER NOW" that matters.
- 3. Fix the clipped "OUR adiclub NEWSLETTER" banner so the full title shows.
- 4. Say what the Toca Boca discount is, with a number and a deadline.
