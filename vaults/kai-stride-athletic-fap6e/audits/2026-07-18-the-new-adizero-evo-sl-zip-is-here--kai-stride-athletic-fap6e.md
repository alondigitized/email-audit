# The new ADIZERO Evo SL ZIP is here
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Finally. An adidas email that remembers I run. After yesterday's FIFA cinematic with Spain versus Argentina, and the app-only sale the week before that had zero runners in it, and the July adiClub newsletter that gave me a signed soccer ball giveaway, this one lands like a breath of fresh air: a running shoe, a Strava tie-in, and copy that at least mentions "lockdown." I'll take it.
- That said, I'm torn. The "tongue-length zip for expressive design" framing is the tell — adidas is positioning this as fashion-forward runner-adjacent, not race-day. The Dropset Pro email from July 10th had race-day energy; this has sneakerhead-who-jogs energy. The Strava section below the fold is genuinely the stronger hook for someone like me, and it almost saves the email. I'd click "Redeem Now" on the Strava perk before I'd click "Shop Now" on the shoe.
- I would not forward this to the running club. The shoe isn't positioned clearly enough as a training tool versus a lifestyle drop. But I would not delete it either — I'd tap "Redeem Now," grab the Strava perk, and move on.

## What's working

- **Strava x adiClub is genuinely runner-targeted.** Strava is THE app for our club. Offering a subscription perk tied to loyalty is the first time in weeks adidas has treated me like a runner, not a FIFA fan.
- **"Enhanced lockdown" is the right vocabulary.** That's a runner's word, not a lifestyle brand's word. Someone in the copy room was paying attention.
- **Subject line names the exact product.** No clickbait, no mystery. I knew exactly what I was opening. Compared to lululemon's admittedly clever "Summer Sale, on its final run" last week, this is less creative but way more informative.
- **Two distinct CTAs for two different intents.** "Shop Now" for the shoe buyer, "Find Store Now" for the in-person crowd. That's clean segmentation.
- **Clean render, no visual breakage.** Considering adidas sent this same day as the "THE FINAL AWAITS" FIFA send, the production quality held.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is dark and detail-starved.** The Adizero Evo SL Zip is supposedly a design-forward shoe — the zip is the whole point — and I can barely see it. Nike would have shown an athlete mid-stride wearing the shoe. This gives me the shoe on a surface, in shadow.
- **"Expressive design" kills the performance signal.** That phrase positions this as a style piece, not a training tool. If I'm buying for Sunday's long run, I need to know outsole, stack height, weight — not that it looks good.
- **No price, no sizing, no availability context.** Is this a limited drop? Is it $120 or $220? I have no idea. The Dropset Pro email at least implied a clear product story.
- **Second email of the same day.** La Final was this morning. This is the same day. Even if the content is good, two pushes in one inbox rotation is starting to feel like Crocs running the same hero every week — erodes trust with every send.
- **Strava section is below the fold.** For a runner, the Strava perk is actually the stronger hook, but I have to scroll to find it. Bury the lead and you lose the click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Strava perk in the hero or a top banner.** Swap the order or add a secondary hero strip above the shoe copy — "adiClub Members: Strava on us" is a stronger runner-specific hook than a product launch on its own.
- 2. **Swap "expressive design" for a performance claim.** Tell me weight, drop, or what type of run this is built for. Even one sentence: "Built for tempo runs. Lighter than the SL." Done.
- 3. **Show the zip.** Feature a close-up detail shot that actually earns the product name. The zip is the differentiator — make it the hero.
- 4. **Add a single run-use-case line.** "Tempo. Track. Tuesday night."-style shorthand. That's what the Dropset Pro email felt like — someone who runs wrote it.
- 5. **Reduce same-day send conflicts.** The FIFA email and this one both hit July 18. Stagger by at least 24 hours or suppress the FIFA segment from the running segment.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lighter build, tongue-length zip, lockdown fit — and a Strava perk waiting for you.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Adidas ADIZERO Evo SL ZIP Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard failures: one broken click-redirect producing a non-routable destination URL, and a completely absent plain-text MIME part. Additionally, multiple images are served over HTTP and unsubscribe headers are missing from the relay capture, creating deliverability and compliance risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken click-redirect**
The QA link checker reports `unknown url type: '/g/4bfd09dd-a06b-4fc5-9789-2b81320f5354'` for the outer URL:
```
https://dv.adidas.com/o/4bfd09dd-a06b-4fc5-9789-2b81320f5354?cp_tp=v3...
```
The redirect resolver is returning a relative path (`/g/...`) instead of an absolute destination URL. Any subscriber clicking this CTA lands nowhere. Investigate the `dv.adidas.com` redirect service for this specific `txn=ff149000-82c2-11f1-8333-22bb2a244d04`.

**[INFO] 29 tracking links unprobed**
Click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP prober — destination URL correctness for the remaining CTAs is unverified.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] 6× repeated HTTP image source (will be blocked)**
The arrow decoration image is referenced six times over HTTP:
```
http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg
```
Gmail, Outlook.com, and most modern clients block mixed-content HTTP images. The arrow will render as a broken image in most environments.

**[WARN] Open/tracking pixel over HTTP**
```
http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/0102019f75f7a44b-3d507c21-ff74-41f3-b073-1f350e178491-000000/hDG_U...
```
Same mixed-content issue; open tracking may also be suppressed.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on two images**
- Tracking pixel: `src https://dv.adidas.com/o/4bfd09dd...` — no `alt` attribute
- Open pixel: `src http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` — no `alt` attribute

Even tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` to avoid accessibility scanner failures.

**[INFO] HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype**
`<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` — not a rendering failure, but some clients apply quirks-mode behavior. Not flagged as a bug given common ESP practice.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unfired merge token literals (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[NAME]`) visible in the truncated source.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from relay capture**
The AgentMail relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to display the one-click unsubscribe button; its absence increases spam complaint rate and risks inbox demotion under Gmail's February 2024 bulk sender requirements.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
Without this header, one-click machine unsubscribes (as opposed to redirect-based) are not supported. Gmail requires both headers for bulk senders.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown through the relay. Cannot confirm the sending domain `us-news.comms.adidas.com` is properly authenticated.

**[INFO] `ROBOTS: NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW` present** — correct; prevents web-view indexing.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**`<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` is set in `<head>`**
This suppresses the HTTP `Referer` header on all outbound clicks. UTM query parameters in link `href` values will still pass to landing pages, so GA4/campaign attribution should be unaffected — but any server-side referrer logging at adidas.com will see no origin. Confirm this is intentional; if not, remove or change to `origin-when-cross-origin`.

**Broken CTA has zero site continuity**
The `dv.adidas.com/o/4bfd09dd...` link failure (Section 2) means at least one CTA delivers no landing page at all. If this is a primary product CTA, it is a direct conversion loss.

**Destination alignment for 29 remaining links unverified**
No HTTP probes were run against click-redirect domains, so landing page / UTM alignment cannot be confirmed from this audit.
