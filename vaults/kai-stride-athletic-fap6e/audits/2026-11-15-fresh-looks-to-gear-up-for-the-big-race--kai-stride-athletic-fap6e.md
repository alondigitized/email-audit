# Fresh looks to gear up for the big race 🏁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened this because of the 🏁 in the subject. "Big race" sounded like it was for me. It isn't. There isn't a running shoe, a runner or a race anywhere in the email. I got a Las Vegas lifestyle capsule built around Formula 1 team partnerships, and then a soccer-boot giveaway. The subject promises a race and the body delivers a nightclub.
- The countdown banner is the one useful thing: "Black Friday First Access, up to 60% off for members." That's the same "up to 60%" adiClub pitch as the 11/14 send. It has a ticking clock, but there's no product under it. Lululemon's "Sets for a season of ease" email at least stayed in its own lane. Nike's one-time-code email did exactly one job and did it cleanly. This one tries three jobs and does none well.
- I'm not clicking. I'd skim it, shrug and archive it. I wouldn't forward it to the club chat, because nothing in it would make anyone there say "oh nice."

## What's working

- The countdown timer (4 days, 7 hours) is clear and feels like real urgency. It's the one thing that made me pause.
- "Up to 60% off for members" is a concrete number at the top. It tells me early access is worth something.
- The layout is clean and each block is easy to scan. The black "Shop Now" buttons are big enough to tap.
- The Predator and F50 boot image is the most striking visual in the email. It's the only one with actual product energy.

## What's weak

- The "big race" subject doesn't match the content. Neon Lights and After Hours capsules are fashion, not racing. For a run club captain, that reads as bait.
- There's no running anywhere. No road shoe, no Adizero, no Boston. Hoodies and wide-leg jeans on a rooftop don't tell me adidas knows runners.
- The F1 partner logos (Mercedes AMG Petronas, Audi Revolut) are the focus of the top two blocks. They crowd out the product.
- The giveaway is for soccer boots, and I only get in if I connect my adiClub and FIFA Rewards accounts. That's a lot of friction for a prize I can't use.
- The member 60% banner and the Las Vegas products are separate. I can't tell which items the discount covers.
- This is the same member-sale message I got on 11/14, so it's more repetition in a cadence that's already heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the content. Either lead with an actual race-day running product, or drop the 🏁 and the race language for a lifestyle send.
- 2. Put one product under the 60% banner and link to the eligible styles. A runner needs to see a shoe, not a countdown over a rooftop.
- 3. Segment by category. People who buy running shoes should get a running hero with an Adizero or Supernova, and a size-availability note.
- 4. Cut the three-job layout down to one. Either run the Vegas capsule or run the giveaway, not both plus a sale banner.
- 5. If the giveaway stays, send it to football buyers only and make entry one click.
