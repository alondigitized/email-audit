# New golf gear extends your season
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this actually got my attention — for the first time in weeks lululemon sent me an email where every single model is a man, and it's not another studio-pants fever dream. Connor Bedard and Roger Steele on a golf course, real athlete names attached to real gear. I don't golf every week but half my running club does weekend rounds, and "gear that plays well through fall" with a rugby shirt and a golf jacket is a genuinely different pitch than the fourth ballet-inspired lifestyle drop this month.
- That said, I clocked instantly that this isn't running gear — it's golf. Lululemon's been trying to stretch "athlete-endorsed" credibility across ice hockey and golf ambassadors, and neither Bedard nor Steele is a runner, so the sport-specific honesty I actually care about (does this brand know runners) is still a zero here. It's better than the pants email, but it's not for me in the way it thinks it is.
- I'd open it, skim it, not click. Wouldn't forward it — my running crew would ask why lululemon is emailing me about golf jackets.

## What's working

- Two named athletes (Connor Bedard, ice hockey; Roger Steele, golf advocate) — actual credibility signal instead of generic lifestyle models, first male-only lineup I've seen from them in a while.
- Clean hero shot, golf swing mid-motion, feels current for fall season transition rather than stale summer copy.
- Product grid ("Shop new styles this week") gives me options without forcing me to click through five separate CTAs.
- Amex tie-in at the bottom (up to $75 cash back) is a real, specific dollar offer — rare to see hard numbers in a lululemon email at all.

## What's weak

- It's golf, not running — my whole reason for staying subscribed is the long run, and there's zero running content here, same disconnect as always just flipped to a different sport.
- "Shop What's New" as the primary CTA twice — vague, could point to anything, no specific product or price attached to the hero offer.
- No mention of membership perks or loyalty pricing anywhere except buried in the Amex terms at the very bottom.
- Repeats the "Cotton-Blend Crew" from the 9/16 send almost verbatim ("Softness, doubled up" vs. that email's whole pitch) — feels like the same asset getting recycled two sends later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap "Shop What's New" for a specific product + price anchor — even golfers want to know what the rugby shirt costs before clicking three times to find out.
- 2. If you're going to use athlete names, put the sport in the subject line so a running subscriber can self-select out instead of opening on false hope.
- 3. Move the Amex $75 cash-back offer up near the hero — it's the only concrete number in the whole email and it's stuck in the footer.
- 4. Segment: I've now gotten five straight emails with zero running content. Ask me what I actually shop for, or stop assuming golf and running are interchangeable "male sport" content.
