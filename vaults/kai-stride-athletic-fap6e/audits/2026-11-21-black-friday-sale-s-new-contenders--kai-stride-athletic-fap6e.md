# Black Friday Sale's new contenders
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the fifth Black Friday send from lululemon in about a week, and it's the first one that tells me nothing. The 11/19 "Black Friday Sale is in motion" email at least gave me real prices. This one is a red "black friday" banner, "Our new starting lineup," and six product tiles with no percentage, no dollar amount and no price anywhere. The only date is buried in the fine print: offers end November 30, 11:59 PM PT.
- I captain a run club, and I'm the person who buys for the weekly long run. Pace Breaker shorts are the only thing I'd call a running product here. The rest is lifestyle: a jacket, an oversized tee, joggers, leggings and a backpack. The "starting lineup" line borrows sports language, but nothing in the email says anything about running. Nike would at least put a pace or a distance next to its hype, and this has none of that.
- Would I click? Probably one tile, Pace Breaker, and only out of curiosity. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the email that shows me a price.

## What's working

- The hero is loud and unmistakable. The red "black friday" banner tells me what this is in half a second.
- The 2x3 product grid is clean and easy to scan. Every tile has a one-line "Shop [name] →" label.
- Men's and women's product sit side by side, and the men's models look like they're in my age range. That reads as more inclusive than the puffer-only send on 11/17.
- The fine print is readable and states the end date, even though it's at the bottom. I'd rather have that than nothing.
- The pill buttons are big, dark and easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere. "Shop the Sale" with no %, $ or tier is a promise with nothing behind it. adidas at least leads with "up to 60%" in its Black Friday subject line.
- It's not a running email. Pace Breaker is the only run-adjacent item, and nothing says short length, lining or fit.
- No sizing or availability info. "Moving quick" is the only urgency copy, and it's vague. I'd rather see "sizes selling out" with the actual styles.
- Two near-identical CTAs, "Shop the Sale" and "Shop Best of Black Friday," point to what looks like the same place. The second one lands under a block of legal text.
- The accessories backpack block is huge and sits between me and the footer. It uses a lot of space on something I'm not shopping for.
- This is the same red banner as the 11/15 and 11/16 sends. It's repetitive by now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero, for example "Up to 40% off select styles" or a member price, and keep the end date next to it instead of in the fine print.
- 2. Add a run-specific row for people like me, with a shorts, tights or shell tile and a line like "Built for long runs."
- 3. Drop the second CTA or send it to a different, specific destination such as the men's sale.
- 4. Shrink the backpack block and use that space for a "low stock" or "last sizes" row.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Five Black Friday emails in a week is too many, and I'd trade three for one with real prices.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Pace Breaker shorts, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles through Nov 30. Sizes are moving fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shorts, tights and layers for the long run, all marked down.`
