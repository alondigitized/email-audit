# $80 and under shoes for class and beyond
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Back-to-school Sambas and Gazelles — cool, but I'm not in school and neither is anyone in my running club. This is the fourth adidas email in ten days and the second lifestyle-shoe hero after the metallic Samba rerun from the 7th/15th. Nothing here is a running shoe. The Adistar XLG 2.0 giveaway at the bottom is the closest thing to performance content, and even that's a giveaway, not a shop-now offer.
- I'm not clicking. This isn't my category and it isn't even framed as mine — "back to school fit" tells me immediately I'm the wrong recipient. I'm also not forwarding it; nobody in my running crew wants suede Gazelles for the long run. Straight to read-and-archive.
- Compare this to Nike's one-time-code email from earlier — that one did exactly one job (get me my code) and did it cleanly. This one is doing zero jobs for me: not selling me running gear, not building loyalty beyond a generic adiClub giveaway, not honoring that I'm a member who's been getting hammered with kids'-branded and lifestyle sends for weeks.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — no render bugs, no broken images, easy to scan on a scroll.
- The $80-and-under price point is a real, specific number, which is more honest than adidas's usual vague "up to X% off" banners.
- Shop Men / Shop Women split CTAs are clear and unambiguous — I know exactly where each button takes me.
- adiClub giveaway ties back to their loyalty program, which is at least a consistent brand thread across recent sends.

## What's weak

- Zero running content — no trainers, no mileage angle, no club/community hook. This is a lifestyle/back-to-school email, not built for someone who buys for the weekly long run.
- "Back to school" doesn't apply to me at all — I'm 33, not shopping for a semester, so the whole premise misses.
- Third lifestyle-shoe rerun in two weeks (Samba on the 7th, again on the 15th, now Gazelles/Sambas again here) — adidas is repeating the same shoe closet on me.
- The Adistar XLG 2.0 giveaway is buried at the very bottom after two rounds of non-running CTAs — if that's the one thing relevant to a runner, it's the last thing I see.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out of back-to-school lifestyle drops entirely — if adidas knows I'm a runner (adiClub should know this), lead with performance product, not Samba/Gazelle rotation number four.
- 2. Move the Adistar XLG 2.0 content to the top and make it an actual shoppable CTA, not just a giveaway entry — a real trainer launch beats a sweepstakes for engagement from someone like me.
- 3. If lifestyle content must go out, at least caption it honestly instead of "class and beyond" — that phrase has nothing for a non-student.
- 4. Stop rerunning the same Samba/Gazelle imagery — three sends in ten days with overlapping product is fatigue, not frequency.
