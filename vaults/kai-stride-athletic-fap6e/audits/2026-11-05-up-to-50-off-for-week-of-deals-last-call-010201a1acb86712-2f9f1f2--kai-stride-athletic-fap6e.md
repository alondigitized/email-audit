# Up to 50% off for Week of Deals last call
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is the sixth adidas email in about a week and none of them have had running in them. The countdown clock is the best thing here. It shows 14 hours and 33 minutes, so the urgency is believable. Then I scroll and get a pair of red Gazelles on wrapping paper, and I'm back to the gift catalog I saw on 10/31 ("Week of Deals: Save on Gazelle, Soft Lux, and more"). It's the same creative with a "last call" label on it.
- I'm a club captain and I buy for the Sunday long run. "Up to 50% off bestselling classics" tells me nothing about whether Adizero or Supernova is in the sale. Nike would at least put a Pegasus in the hero on a clearance day, and lululemon's winter drop gave me runners on a dirt road. This gives me three generic gender buttons and a "while supplies last" line.
- I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read. If one running shoe were discounted in the hero, I'd probably open the site.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (00 days, 14 hours, 33 minutes) makes the urgency feel real.
- "Up to 50% off" is stated clearly in the black banner, so I know the offer within a second.
- The three black buttons are large, high-contrast and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The layout is clean and readable on a phone with no render bugs, which is better than most of the clutter I get.

## What's weak

- There's no running anywhere. The hero is lifestyle Gazelles, which is the same beat as the 10/30 "Fresh arrivals" send.
- "Up to 50% off" with no named product or price means I can't tell if anything I'd buy is discounted.
- "WEEK OF DEALS" is spaced out in a wedding-invitation serif around the shoes, and it reads as decoration instead of a sale.
- Shop Men, Shop Women and Shop Kids is the laziest possible CTA set. It sends me to a category page, not a product, and there's no running option.
- Nothing mentions my adiClub membership or points, even though adidas knows I have 50 of them from the loyalty email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one discounted running shoe in the hero with a real price, like "Adizero Boston 13, $89 (was $140)". Specific beats "up to 50%".
- 2. Segment by category affinity. Someone who opened the 10/28 hybrid race shoe email should get a running deal, not Gazelles.
- 3. Replace the gender buttons with "Shop running deals" and "Shop all deals".
- 4. Add a line for members, such as "adiClub members get early access" or "extra 10% for members".
- 5. Don't send a fifth reminder for the same sale. A "last call" email should be the only one, with the clock as the hook.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`image.link.adidas.com`, `click.comms.adidas.com`) with a responsive table layout. The automated QA found one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several warnings: mixed-content `http://` images, missing alt text, and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so final destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is served over plain HTTP.**
  - Source: `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a1acb86712-2f9f1f2b-.../O7T8p4Alizr-ht-SOnLeRXChIqwv7RJ_cwAzjKMEWjU=452`
  - It also has no alt text.
  - Clients or proxies that block non-HTTPS content may drop it, which undercounts opens. It also exposes the request to a downgrade.
- **No broken or malformed links were confirmed.** The probe was skipped, so none could be verified either way.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images:**
  - `eebc2368-b3ae-43d8-9596-f1f95c7eb473.jpg` (`https://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe5c15707c63077e7013/m/1/...`)
  - `https://dv.adidas.com/o/0b804007-6a7f-4481-a971-0976bd8228c1?cp_tp=...`
  - the tracking pixel above

  The first two are content images and the pixel is a tracking image. If the content images are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If they carry information, they need descriptive alt text. With images off or a screen reader, the user gets nothing.
- **Six `http://` images (the "arrow" icon).** The same asset is referenced six times: `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`. This risks mixed-content blocking. The same asset is already served over HTTPS on the same host (`https://image.link.adidas.com/...`), so the HTTP references look unnecessary.
- **Viewport meta lacks `width=device-width`.** The source has `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1.0">`. This is a minor issue, since the layout also relies on `@media` queries.
- **Custom fonts only declare `local()` sources.** `AdihausDIN` and `AdineuePRO` are defined with `src: local('Arial')…`, so they resolve to system fonts. This is a stability choice rather than a bug.
- **`lang="en"` is present on `<html>`.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, which is acceptable for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The unseen remainder of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is missing (0 chars).** The message has no usable `text/plain` part. This hurts deliverability, since spam filters penalize HTML-only mail. It also breaks text-only and accessibility clients.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this may be a capture artifact. It is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `us-news.comms.adidas.com` could not be checked. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 22 links go through the `click.comms.adidas.com` redirect wrapper and were not resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Week of Deals last call / up to 50% off" subject could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` multipart alternative.** This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Change the six `http://image.link.adidas.com/...b0c011a2...jpg` references and the `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...` pixel to `https://`.**
3. **Add `alt` attributes** to `eebc2368-...jpg`, the `dv.adidas.com/o/0b804007-...` image and the pixel (`alt=""` where decorative).
4. **Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers.** If they are present there, the warnings are relay artifacts and can be ignored.
5. **Resolve the redirect chains** for a sample of the 22 tracked links to verify UTM parameters and landing pages.
6. **Optionally set the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`.**
