## What's working

- "Members, you're up first with Early Access" — actual loyalty signal, better than lululemon's tee email that had none.
- The guy against the wall with the crossed arms and medicine ball at least reads athletic-adjacent, more than last week's all-female tee lineup.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent grayscale/concrete aesthetic top to bottom.
- "full drop coming soon" teaser at the bottom sets an expectation without over-promising.

## What's weak

- Zero running content — no shoes, no motion, no mileage, no sweat. This is fall lookbook energy, not "know your runner" energy.
- The hero and majority of shots are women; men show up late in the scroll (three frames out of ten-plus), same imbalance I flagged in the tee email from 09/01.
- "Tap into the potential of the season" is vague marketing-speak — tells me nothing about the actual product or why I should care.
- No concrete offer — no %, no BOGO, no free shipping, just "Early Access to select styles," which could mean anything.
- CTA copy "Shop Early Access →" repeats three times with no product specificity — I don't know what I'm clicking into.

- 2. Balance the model lineup in the first three frames, not just at the tail end — right now it reads like a women's email with a men's afterthought.
- 3. Add a real offer signal — even just "shop before it sells out" with a date beats the vague "select styles."
