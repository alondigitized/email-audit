## What's working

- The Adizero Evo SL Woven Running Shoes card is an actual running shoe with a visible 40% cut ($150 → $90) — first real product-category match in five sends.
- Pricing is transparent per item — original price, percent off, and star rating with review count all sit right under each product, no hidden math.
- "No code needed" removes the usual friction point where I'd have to hunt for a promo code at checkout.
- The hero "FALL KICKOFF SALE" banner is clean and readable, no overlapping text or broken renders.

## What's weak

- The Adizero is one of four products crammed into a grid with two lifestyle Sambas and a track top — no callout, no "for your next long run" framing, just a price tag treatment identical to a sneaker collab.
- Zero running-specific content: no pace/mileage claim, no cushioning stack info, no mention of a race or training block — the stuff that would actually make me trust adidas "gets" runners.
- The email pivots hard into "Back To School Backpacks" at the bottom, which has nothing to do with anyone in my inbox segment who's here for run gear.
- The hero model shot (the guy in the track top) is styled entirely lifestyle/streetwear, reinforcing that this send is aimed at fashion-adjacent shoppers, not my club.
- Compare to Nike's "Here's your one-time code" send — that one was narrow and functional and did its one job in under a minute. This is the opposite: broad, unfocused, and asks me to do the filtering myself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the grid with the Adizero, not bury it third — put a performance shoe first if you're sending this to anyone flagged as a runner, and add one line of running-specific copy ("energy return for your long run days") instead of just a star rating.
- 2. Split this send by segment — a "Performance" version leading with running/training gear, and a separate "Lifestyle/Back to School" version with the backpacks and Sambas. One email trying to be both dilutes each.
- 3. Cut the backpack section entirely from a runner-segment version, or replace it with a gym bag / hydration vest that's actually training-adjacent.
- 4. Add a shipping/delivery deadline tied to Fall Kickoff urgency — right now "starts now" has no end date, so there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
