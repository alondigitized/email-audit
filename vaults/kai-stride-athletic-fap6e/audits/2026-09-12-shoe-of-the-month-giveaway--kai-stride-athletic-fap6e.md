- The green product shot is the best visual adidas has sent me this week — single shoe, no lifestyle clutter, actually lets the product breathe.
- "adiClub members" framing at the top at least acknowledges I'm a known customer, not a cold blast.
- One clear CTA ("ENTER NOW") with no competing buttons — no repeat of the three-button SHOP MEN/WOMEN/KIDS mess from the "Save 30%" email a couple days back.

- No indication this is a running shoe — Hyperboost sounds like it could be basketball or lifestyle, and there's zero performance copy (no drop, no cushioning stat, no terrain call-out) to tell me otherwise.
- It's a giveaway, not an offer — my odds of winning are basically nil, so there's no real incentive to act versus lululemon's actual fall drop email that had real new product and a real date to shop.

- Preview text just repeats the giveaway line instead of teasing anything additional — wasted real estate.

- 3. Add one sentence of actual product substance — drop weight, cushioning tech, terrain — so it reads like adidas knows runners, not just merchandisers.
