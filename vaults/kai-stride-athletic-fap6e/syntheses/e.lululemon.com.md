# ### Summary and Recommendations for "What's Trending Right Now" Email from lululemon (2026-05-21)

#### Overview:
The email lacks personalization and relevance, particularly for a subscriber who is an avid runner. The content predominantly features women’s tennis apparel with no mention of running-specific products or offers tailored to the recipient's interests.

#### What Worked:
- **Sender Name:** Recognizable brand name increases open rate.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Clear layout with hero image and product grid.
- **No Render Bugs:** Full-bleed images load, text is legible, and mobile responsiveness is good.

#### What Didn't Work:
- **Irrelevant Content:** No running-specific products or offers for a runner subscriber.
- **Generic Subject Line:** "What's trending right now" lacks specificity and urgency.
- **Weak Membership Tease:** Vague early access teaser with no clear product information.
- **No Offer:** Lack of any discount, promotion, or incentive to shop.

#### Recommendations:
1. **Segmentation by Interest:**
   - Segment the email list based on subscriber interests (e.g., running, yoga, tennis).
   - Use personalized content and offers relevant to each segment's preferences.

2. **Stronger Subject Line & Preheader:**
   - Example Alt A: `Men's top 10 this week — running, training & more`
   - Example Alt B: `New this week: performance styles built to move`

3. **Incorporate Running Products:**
   - Include a section dedicated to running-specific products (e.g., Surge shorts, Pace Breaker).
   - Use specific product names and benefits in the content.

4. **Clear Early Access Teaser:**
   - Provide clear information about early access products.
   - Example: "Get exclusive access to new colors for the Pace Breaker shorts."

5. **Offer Incentives:**
   - Include a discount or special offer (e.g., free shipping, member pricing).
   - Example: "Shop now and get 10% off your first purchase."

6. **Cadence Management:**
   - Avoid over-saturating the inbox with similar content.
   - Mix in different types of emails to maintain engagement.
