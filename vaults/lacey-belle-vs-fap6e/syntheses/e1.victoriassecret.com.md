# ### Technical Audit — Victoria's Secret "Scent Event Early Access" (2026-05-14)

---

#### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but carries two significant infrastructure risks: 12 third-party retargeting pixels load over plain HTTP (mixed content), and both one-click unsubscribe headers are absent from the captured message. Accessibility coverage is poor across tracking and content images.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text URL density — HIGH RISK**
- **QA reports:** 8,637 of 9,737 plain-text characters are URLs (88.7%).
- **Impact:** Spam filters penalize emails where the plain-text part is almost entirely link data.
- **Action:** Ensure that the plain-text alternative is not auto-generated and includes relevant content.

**20 click-redirect links skipped**
- **CTAs routing through:** `click.victoriassecret.com` (DV/MI redirect) and `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` (SFMC).
- **Verification needed:** Final redirect targets are unverified.
- **Action:** Verify the final destination URLs of all CTAs.

**Three separate open/impression pixels**
- **DV pixel:** `https://click.victoriassecret.com/o/715cb258-...` (HTTPS, correct)
- **SFMC pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?AZHAUNYKYBHENNRQTD5FCFJWNM.70251` (HTTPS, correct)
- **MI pixel:** `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` (**HTTP — see §3**)

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed-content HTTP images — HIGH RISK**
- **Count:** 12 instances
- **Details:**
  - MI open pixel: `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif`
  - Dotomi UCM: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...`
  - pub1.php – pub10.php: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub{1-10}.php?...`

**Alt text for images**
- **Missing alt attributes:** All 12 mixed-content HTTP images.
- **Content images missing alt text:** Two non-pixel images.

**Viewport blocks user zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
`user-scalable=0` / `maximum-scale=1` is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.

**DOCTYPE**
- **Used:** HTML 4.01 Transitional.
- **Impact:** Limits modern CSS support but does not introduce rendering bugs.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Subscriber-level tokens (`mi_u=200492586522`, campaign tag `DV_20260514_VS_5434_FILEBAL`) appear resolved in all captured tracking URLs.
- **Action:** Ensure that all merge tokens are properly resolved and included.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — MISSING**
- **Impact:** Required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders (>5K/day).
- **Action:** Add `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to comply with sender requirements.

**Authentication-Results — NOT CAPTURED**
- **Sending domain:** `e1.victoriassecret.com`.
- **Verification needed:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status.
- **Action:** Verify authentication results against raw received headers.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
- **Impact:** Required by CAN-SPAM regulations.
- **Action:** Ensure the physical mailing address is included in the email content.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs are wrapped in `click.victoriassecret.com` or `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects — final destination URLs and their UTM parameters are not visible.
- **Action:** Verify that all landing pages have proper UTM coverage.

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | 12 HTTP pixel assets | Migrate all Dotomi/MI pixels to HTTPS. Ensure all tracking and content images use secure protocols. |
| **High** | Missing List-Unsubscribe header | Add `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to comply with Gmail/Yahoo requirements. |
| **Medium** | Plain-text URL density | Improve the plain-text alternative by including relevant content, not just links. |
| **Low** | Viewport meta tag | Remove or modify the viewport meta tag to ensure user scalability and compliance with WCAG guidelines. |

---

### Summary

The email has several technical issues that need immediate attention:
- Migrate all HTTP images to HTTPS.
- Add `List-Unsubscribe` headers for compliance.
- Improve plain-text content.
- Ensure proper UTM coverage on landing pages.

Addressing these issues will improve deliverability, user experience, and regulatory compliance.
