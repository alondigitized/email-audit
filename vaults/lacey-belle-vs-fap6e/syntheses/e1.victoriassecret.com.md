The email from Victoria's Secret for the "Scent Event Early Access" campaign is well-structured with a clear and compelling offer. However, there are significant technical and compliance issues that need to be addressed to ensure optimal performance and compliance:

2. **Accessibility and Usability:**
   - **Alt Text:** Ensure all images, including tracking pixels, have proper `alt` attributes.

3. **Compliance:**
   - **CAN-SPAM:** Ensure the physical mailing address is included in the email.
   - **Authentication:** Verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for the sending domain.

By addressing these issues, Victoria's Secret can improve the email's performance, security, and compliance, leading to a better user experience and higher engagement rates.
