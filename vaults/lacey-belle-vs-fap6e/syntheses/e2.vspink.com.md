### Summary

The email campaign "Let's Get Tropical" from PINK has several technical issues that need immediate attention. The most critical are the HTTP pixel sources which should be updated to HTTPS for proper tracking and deliverability. Additionally, ensuring compliance with List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results headers is crucial for maintaining good sender reputation.

Medium-priority items such as `user-scalable=0` in viewport meta and missing alt text on images need addressing to improve accessibility and rendering consistency across different email clients. Lastly, refactoring the plain-text content can help reduce spam filter penalties.

By implementing these recommendations, PINK can enhance both technical performance and user experience for their subscribers.
