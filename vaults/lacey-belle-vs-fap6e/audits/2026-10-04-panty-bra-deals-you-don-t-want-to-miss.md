## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based layout with a 600px container and a mobile media query. It has malformed document structure, about 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels (Dotomi), and several images without alt text. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results warnings come from relay capture limits and can't be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most body links were not checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probes skipped:** 52 links went to tracking or click-redirect domains and were not probed (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** 11 images load over `http://` and are likely to be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Hashed identifier in query strings:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106...` is appended to the Dotomi pixel URLs (as the parameter name `a4edea106...=1`) and to the JSON-LD URL. Hashed email in third-party URLs is a privacy and data-leakage concern.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is 1x1 with `alt=""`. This is correct for a decorative image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Elements in `<head>`:** A `<table>`, `<script type="application/ld+json">` and `<meta>` tags sit inside `<head>` before the `<style>` blocks. This is invalid HTML, and parsers may close `<head>` early and drop the `<meta charset>` and viewport tags.
- **Misplaced `<title>`:** `<title style="display:none...">` is empty and sits inside a body table.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with self-closing `<meta ... />` syntax.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{...}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` matches no element, so both rules are dead.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text (QA, 14 images):**
  - `mi.victoriassecret.com/p/rp/a076cc7f15d20566.png`, probably a tracking pixel
  - `.../3505172c-7ae5-4640-adb1-053932dab3a3.gif` and `.../5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, which are on the content CDN and may be content images
  - the 11 Dotomi pixels above
  
  The Dotomi and `mi.` pixels should use `alt=""`. The CDN images need descriptive alt text if they carry content.
- **Outlook:** The `<!--[if mso]>` font fix is present, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values (`mi_u`, `customerID`, `dtm_email_hash`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** The QA check did not find either header. Its own note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders above 5,000 messages a day.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` should be checked directly.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not present in the truncated source, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Landing target:** The JSON-LD URL is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/panties/panties-offer?...`. It was not probed.
- **Campaign tagging:** The email uses proprietary parameters (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid=100426515`, `mi_ecmp=20261004_VS_6250_FULLFILE`) and no `utm_*` parameters. Attribution depends on the site's Coremetrics/mi-based analytics, so check that GA-style tools are not expected to read these.
- **Campaign ID consistency:** `20261004_VS_6250_FULLFILE` appears across the JSON-LD URL, the `mi_ecmp` parameter and the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category`.
- **JSON-LD block:** It uses `"@context": "http://schema.org/"` (non-HTTPS) and has only `image` and `url` in the visible portion. Required fields for Gmail promotion annotations may be absent, but the source is truncated so this can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check the raw headers:** Look for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating the compliance warnings as real.
2. **Fix the document structure:** Move the JSON-LD block and the `<table>` wrapper into `<body>`. Keep only `<meta>`, `<title>`, `<style>` and `<script>` in `<head>`, and put a real `<title>` there.
3. **Switch Dotomi pixels to `https://`:** If the vendor doesn't support it, drop the pixels. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. **Add descriptive alt text:** Cover the CDN `.gif` and `.png` images if they carry content.
5. **Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
6. **Delete or correct the dead CSS selectors:** Use `.ReadMsgBody` and remove `min-width{...}`.
7. **Review the hashed email in third-party pixel URLs** against the privacy policy and consent basis.
8. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML:** Include the footer, unsubscribe and address checks, and probe the 52 tracked links through a redirect-following check.
