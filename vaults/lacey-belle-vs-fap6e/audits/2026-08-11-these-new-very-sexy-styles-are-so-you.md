- Below the hero it's the usual sprawl: a second Very Sexy lifestyle shot with rhinestone-detail copy, a marble-statue "Shop All Bras" module, a "$6 Mists, Lotions & VS Body Care" clearance block, a "30% Off tomorrow" teaser, a category grid, and a Trending strip. - Net: strong subject/hero alignment drags this up from the brand's floor, but the two cannibalizing offers (Very Sexy collection vs. $6 clearance vs. 30% off tomorrow) and the credit-card banners eating the top fold keep it out of the 8+ range. Solid, not great — a 7.

## What's working

- Subject and hero genuinely match — "Very Sexy Styles" subject pays off immediately with "VERY SEXY COLLECTION" hero, unlike several recent sends in this run.
- Clear single hero CTA ("Shop All") sits right under the collection name with good visual weight.
- Rhinestone-bra detail module gives a concrete product reason to click, not just a vague lifestyle shot.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- Two nearly identical credit-card point banners (2x Tuesdays, 3x on Bras) stack at the very top, delaying the actual hero and offer.
- Three competing offers in one email — Very Sexy collection, $6 body care clearance, and 30% off tomorrow — split attention with no clear "if you only do one thing" hierarchy.
- The "$6 Mists, Lotions & VS Body Care" module is a jarring tonal and visual break from the Very Sexy lace/rhinestone story right above it.
- "Coming Tomorrow" 30% off is teased today but not actionable today, which is a soft ask sandwiched between two live offers.

- 2. Move the $6 body care clearance block to its own send or push it further down/out — it competes with, rather than supports, the Very Sexy bra story.
- 3. Give the 30% off teaser its own dedicated send tomorrow rather than previewing it today alongside two live offers.
- 4. Add a specific price or %-off callout near the hero itself — right now the hero has zero numeric offer, just the collection name.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may not be captured by the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown via this relay.
- Note: these are relay-visibility gaps, not confirmed failures — the sending domain (`e1.victoriassecret.com`, an ExactTarget/SFMC subdomain) typically implements these; absence in the captured headers reflects the AgentMail capture path rather than confirmed non-compliance.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**

**7. Recommendations**

- Migrate the `dotomi.com` tracking pixels (`ads.dotomi.com`, `login.dotomi.com`) from `http://` to `https://` — non-HTTPS resources are increasingly blocked/stripped by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook proxy).

- Remove the duplicate open-tracking pixel (same URL fires twice).
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers rather than the relay capture, since this is likely a capture-path gap, not necessarily a sender defect — confirm before treating as a compliance finding.
