# Not a Typo‼️ Limited Time: $15 Tees
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the first PINK send in a week where the subject isn't about panties or the Bra Event. "$15 Tees" is a clear, concrete price and a real change of beat after the 8/$38 panty run (10-01 to 10-06) and the Bra Event sends on 10-08 and 10-09. The hero is clean: a model in a pink PINK tee, "STEAL THE DEAL," then a big pink "$15 TEES" with three tee styles under it. The eye lands on the price.
- The email then buries its own lead. Below the tee block it keeps the full Bra Event module ("BUY 2, GET 1 FREE"). After that come a velvet-shorts feature, a "$10 full-sized beauty + 2/$10 minis" block, a holiday gift guide, a bling banner, a logo shop banner, and a bestsellers grid. That makes about five competing offers in one scroll. The Bra Event creative is a repeat for anyone who opened 10-08 or 10-09. The $15 tee is the new idea, and it gets roughly one screen before the email turns into a catalog.
- Verdict: a good subject and a good hero, undermined by a crowded body and a "Not a Typo" hook that the email never explains. It's a solid 6-7 send that could be an 8 with half the modules.

## What's working

- The "$15 TEES" block is large, high-contrast pink, and the strongest element in the email.
- Showing three different tee designs under the headline gives the offer some product variety.
- The subject names a specific price and product, so it's concrete and mobile-length.
- The hero model and styling fit PINK's young, casual audience.
- The "Straight from the vault" line gives the tee price a reason to exist.

## What's weak

- Five or more offers compete: tees, Buy 2 Get 1 Free bras, $10 full-sized beauty, gift guide, and bestsellers. Nothing is allowed to win.
- The Bra Event module is a near-repeat of 10-08 and 10-09, which makes this feel like a recycled template with a tee block bolted on.
- The tee CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" and there is no deadline beyond "Limited Time," so the urgency isn't credible.
- "Orig. up to $38.95" is small, and the fine print is dense and long. It undercuts the "Not a Typo" promise that $15 is a real steal.
- No reviews, sizing, or fit detail is shown for the tees.
- The "Free Panty Loading…" bar at the top is tiny and competes with the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to two modules: tees first, then one secondary offer, either the bras or the beauty block. Move everything else into a single "more to shop" strip.
- 2. Use a specific CTA such as "Shop $15 Tees" and add a real end date, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 3. Make the "Not a Typo" hook pay off in the email. Show a strikethrough price (~~$38.95~~ $15) so the surprise lands visually.
- 4. Rotate the Bra Event creative or retire it. Subscribers have seen it two days running.
- 5. Add a social proof line such as "Most-loved tee," or star ratings, under the tee row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The real problems are 12 third-party tracking pixels loaded over cleartext `http://`, some of which carry a hashed email address, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and missing `alt` attributes. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Cleartext pixels:** 12 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261010_PK_6276_FILEBALANCE`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Clients may block or rewrite these, and the requests are visible in transit. The first-party open pixel, `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?…`, is HTTPS.
- **Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels (`pub1`–`pub10`):** each is a separate third-party request on open, which adds load time. The QA data doesn't show whether any fail to load.
- **Click links not probed:** 48 tracking links were skipped as redirect domains, so click-through resolution and status codes are unverified.
- **JSON-LD URLs:** all three `PromotionCard` entries point to the same URL, `…/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms/offer?cm_mmc=…`. This is consistent, but it can't be confirmed to resolve.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing `alt`:** 13 images have no `alt` attribute (`o.gif`, the Dotomi UCM image, `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`, and `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`). For the 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` should be set explicitly. The `.png` is an HTTPS content-host image. If it carries content, the missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap.
- **Invalid document structure:** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will push them into the body, but it is invalid markup. It may also affect how Gmail reads the Promotion Card markup.
- **No `lang` or `<title>`:** the root tag is a bare `<html>`, and no `<title>` appears in the visible source.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both rules are ignored.
- **No `role="presentation"` on layout tables:** the main layout tables (`<table width="100%" border="0" …>`, `<table class="tb_properties border_style" …>`) lack it. Only the JSON-LD wrapper has it.
- **Mobile body font size:** the body has `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` inline, while the `<style>` block sets `100%`. The inline rule wins, so iOS text-size adjustment is disabled.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- **Hashed email in cleartext:** the Dotomi pixels pass the same SHA-256 hash (`4edea106b333e696…2e459aafc6fe`) as the query key, over `http://`. The same hash appears as `dtm_email_hash` in every JSON-LD URL, alongside `customerID=43cece97…`. A stable identifier sent unencrypted to a third-party ad network is a privacy and compliance concern.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed and shouldn't be treated as a sender defect without the raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while the tracking and image hosts are on `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment can't be verified from the data provided.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were cut off by truncation, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters:** links use the sender's own scheme, not `utm_*`: `cm_mmc=di-_-101026445-_-VSD_VSL_20261010_PK_6276_FILEBALANCE-_`, `d_cellid=101026445`, `mi_ecmp=20261010_PK_6276_FILEBALANCE`. The campaign ID is the same across the JSON-LD, the open pixel and the Dotomi pixels, so cross-system attribution is consistent. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is present in the visible URLs. That is only a problem if the destination analytics expect them.
- **Landing page:** the destination is `/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms/offer`. It was not probed, so whether it matches the subject's "$15 Tees" can't be confirmed.
- **Long URLs:** each URL carries a 64-character email hash and a 64-character `customerID` hash, which makes them long. The hashes identify the recipient on any forwarded or shared link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixels to `https://`, or drop the `pub1`–`pub10` cookie syncs. This addresses the largest QA warning cluster and the exposure of the hashed email in cleartext.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and a descriptive `alt` to `5bcec3ac-…png`.
4. Move the JSON-LD block and the wrapper table out of `<head>`, add `lang` and `<title>` to the `<html>`/`<head>`, and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
5. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector and remove the invalid `min-width{…}` rule.
6. Re-run the QA against raw headers (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e2.vspink.com`.
7. Re-run with the full HTML so the footer unsubscribe link and address can be checked, and probe the `/offer` landing page.
