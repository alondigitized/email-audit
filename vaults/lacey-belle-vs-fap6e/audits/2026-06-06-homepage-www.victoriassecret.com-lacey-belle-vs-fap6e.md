# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- OK so I open VS and immediately hit a big blank white void on the left side of my screen — the hero image is loading (or broken?) only on the right edge. What I *can* see is a curvy model in a gorgeous nude/pink bra with a "RATED W FOR WOW" badge, which honestly is right in my wheelhouse. The Rewards join module is front and center which I appreciate, and I can spot a couple of product cards peeking in at the bottom. But this homepage has a serious render problem that would have me second-guessing whether my connection dropped.

## What's working

- **VS & PINK Rewards module** — "Ready to get rewarded?" with Points Per $, $10 Reward, Birthday Gift icons, and a JOIN NOW button hits me right in the loyalty brain. As a Semi-Annual Sale girl, I want to know my points balance exists.
- **"Already a member? Sign In" link** — small but it's there; a returning shopper doesn't have to hunt.
- **Model casting in the hero (partial)** — the model visible on the right is curvy and wearing a pale pink/nude bra that's 100% my vibe. The "RATED W FOR WOW" badge is cheeky and on-brand for VS's current tone.
- **Product cards at bottom right** — "Lightly Lined Wireless Bra" at $21.99 (marked down from $34.95) and a panty at $4.99 / 5 for $26.99 give me real pricing signals I can act on.

## What's weak

- **Hero is half-rendered** — the left ~75% of the hero image is a white blank. On mobile this is catastrophic. I'd think the site is broken before I'd think "oh, cool layout."
- **Top promo banner is illegible** — there's a thin pink strip at the very top but the text is so small and low-contrast I can't read what it says. If there's a sale going on, I missed it entirely.
- **Hero copy is cut off** — all I can make out is "SCRE..." — whether that's "SCREAMING DEALS" or "SCREENTIME" or something else, I have no idea. Unfinished copy is a trust killer.
- **No bra-sizing or fit CTA above the fold** — I came here partly because I'm size-aware and bra fitting is part of my relationship with VS. Nothing says "Find Your Fit" or references sizing.
- **No Semi-Annual Sale indicator** — if SAL is coming or running, it's completely invisible here. That's the one event I calendar. Missing.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero render immediately** — whatever is causing the left-side white void (likely a failed image load or CSS width issue) needs to be resolved before anything else. A broken hero above the fold is a conversion killer on mobile.
- **Make the top promo banner legible** — bump the font size and contrast on that pink strip so I can read the offer in 2 seconds; if it's a sale callout, treat it like a sale callout.
- **Swap the JOIN NOW loyalty CTA for a SIGN IN + balance widget for logged-in users** — if I'm already a member, showing me a "join" prompt wastes prime real estate; show my points balance and what I can redeem instead.
- **Add a "Shop Bras by Size" shortcut tile** — even a single tappable tile linking to bra-by-size filtering would be a one-week ship and directly serves my primary shopping behavior.
