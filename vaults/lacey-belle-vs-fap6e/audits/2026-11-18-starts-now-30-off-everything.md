# STARTS NOW: 30% Off EVERYTHING🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's Black Friday kickoff, and the headline is clear: "30% OFF EVERYTHING" in big type, with category chips (Clothing, Sleep, Bras, Accessories, Activewear, Beauty) right under it. The subject and hero match, and the 30% sitewide offer is easy to read. The weakness is what follows. The email stacks eight or more modules, each with its own "Limited Time" tag and offer: Camo drop, 30% off sleep, 7/$35 panties, bras from $25, $8 beauty, and a free glitter tote with $125+. The hero offer gets diluted fast.
- For a subscriber it also feels repetitive. This follows the Nov 13–16 run of 10/$40 panty sends and the Nov 16–17 Stoney Clover Lane collab sends. Seeing 7/$35 panties back in the lineup, after 10/$40 and 7/$35 earlier in the month, makes the urgency feel less credible. The render is clean, with no broken images. It is a competent, loud promo that works on offer clarity and wears on cadence.
- The render is a very long, shrunken image, so I read only the large headlines. The fine print and the preheader were not legible.

## What's working

- The hero "30% OFF EVERYTHING" is the first thing the eye lands on, and the subject matches it.
- The category chips under the hero give quick routes into sleep, bras, beauty and so on.
- The "Shop now to beat the rush" banner at the top and the "Starts now" subject give a believable launch moment for Black Friday week.
- Each module has a bold headline and a single button, so the offers are easy to scan.
- The Rewards block with a "$25 off" member benefit near the bottom adds loyalty value.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers (7/$35, bras from $25, $8 beauty, free tote at $125+) dilute the 30% sitewide message.
- The 7/$35 panty deal is the same panty-party mechanic PINK ran Nov 9–16, so it feels recycled.
- The "everything" claim comes with visible fine print and exclusions, which undercuts it.
- The email is very long, so the lower modules (tote, beauty) will likely go unseen.
- The buttons say "Shop now" or "Shop the drop", which is generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the 30% hero, three category tiles and one bonus offer (the tote). Drop the repeated panty-party module, or fold it into a "bundle and save" tile.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday", and use a countdown if possible.
- 3. Make the buttons specific: "Shop 30% Off Sleep", "Shop Bras From $25".
- 4. Move the Rewards perk higher so members see the extra value before scrolling.
- 5. Give the Wednesday camo drop its own send instead of burying it mid-email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering/data bug: an unresolved `%%cellcode%%` merge token in the JSON-LD block. It also has invalid markup (a `<table>` and `<script>` inside `<head>`), invalid CSS selectors, and several plain-HTTP tracking pixels. Header-based compliance checks could not be confirmed from the relay data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Inconsistent tracking params in JSON-LD.** `offerPageUrl` has `d_cellid=111826415` and `mi_u=200492586522`. `merchantHomepageUrl` has `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%` and `mi_u=111826415`. The `mi_u` value (the user ID) differs between the two, and the cell ID is unresolved in one of them. Attribution for any click or crawl from the second URL will be wrong or empty.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels (mixed content).** These are `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif`, `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop them, which undercounts opens and breaks the Dotomi cookie sync.
- **Tracking links not probed.** 59 click-redirect links were skipped (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so link validity is unverified. This is a QA limitation, not a defect.
- **Hashed identifiers in query strings.** `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` and `?a4edea10…=1` appear in plaintext URLs, including the `pub*.php` pixel URLs. This is a privacy and forwarding exposure, not a functional bug.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will close `<head>` early at the `<table>`. The `<meta viewport>`, `<meta Content-Type>` and both `<style>` blocks that follow can then be pushed into `<body>` or ignored, depending on the client.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are no-ops.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing alt text on a content image.** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`.
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels.** `o.gif`, the Dotomi UCM image and `pub1.php`–`pub10.php` have no `alt`. They are 1×1 tracking pixels, so this is low severity, but `alt=""` should be set. The open pixel already does this correctly.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `role="presentation"` attributes, which is harmless but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%cellcode%%` is unresolved.** It appears in `merchantHomepageUrl` in the JSON-LD: `...&d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&dtm_email_hash=...`. The same field in `offerPageUrl` is resolved to `111826415`, so this is a template substitution gap and not a global failure. The sibling field `mi_u=111826415` in the same URL looks like the cell ID is in the wrong parameter.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe and authentication unconfirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The supplied HTML is truncated, so the body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.
- **Sender domain.** `From: PINK@e2.vspink.com` uses a different domain from the tracking host (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). This is common, but alignment can't be assessed without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking scheme.** Links use the vendor scheme `cm_mmc=di-_-111826415-_-VSD_VSL_20261118_PK_6495_FULFILE-_-` plus `mi_ecmp=20261118_PK_6495_FULFILE`. There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters, so GA-style attribution would need a separate mapping.
- **Cell ID mismatch.** The `d_cellid` mismatch described in section 4 means the second JSON-LD URL won't carry the same cell as the first.
- **Landing page.** `offerPageUrl` points to `/us/pink/new-arrivals`, but the subject says "30% Off EVERYTHING". Whether that page shows the offer can't be confirmed, because tracking links weren't probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `%%cellcode%%` substitution in the JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl`. Make `d_cellid` and `mi_u` match the values used in `offerPageUrl`, and add a pre-send merge-token check to catch unresolved `%%…%%` tokens.
2. Move the JSON-LD block into `<body>` (or a valid `<head>` position). Put `<meta>` and `<style>` inside `<head>` before any `<table>`.
3. Change all tracking pixels to `https://`. Add `alt=""` to the pixels and descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-….png`.
4. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, and remove or correct `min-width{}`). Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw message with full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Also check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Probe a sample of the 59 click-redirect links, and confirm that `/us/pink/new-arrivals` carries the "30% off everything" offer.
