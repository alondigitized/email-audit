# Your To-Do List: Buy 2, Get 2 FREE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "Buy 2, Get 2 Free" send in four days, after 10/21 "Buy 2, Get 2 FREE Starts Now" and 10/22 "Don't Miss Buy 2, Get 2 FREE". Only the subject framing is new. The "Your To-Do List" angle is a cute hook, but the body doesn't follow through on it. It reads as a standard catalog email with a Member Days banner on top.
- The top of the email is strong. A red "LIMITED TIME" block says "Buy 2, Get 2 Free Everything" and has category pills for Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty, Lingerie, Activewear and Shop All. A "JOIN FOR FREE" button sits right under it. The Member Days header and model photo above the block work against that offer, though. The eye splits between "Member Days" and the BOGO, and the only button in that block asks for a signup, not a purchase.
- Below that, the email is about 10,000px of unrelated modules. It runs through Velvet Angel Embroidery, the Perfumer's Vault and Rapture, $8 Mists & Lotions, Fashion Show, Bestselling Bras, the Gift Shop, a pajama grid, a rewards card, a $25 off card and a Now Trending row. The offer is clear, but the email gives no reason to act on it today. It is a solid 6, not better, because of fatigue and length.

## What's working

- The red "LIMITED TIME: Buy 2, Get 2 Free Everything" block is high-contrast and easy to read.
- Category pills let a shopper jump straight to Bras, Sleep, Beauty and other categories.
- The Velvet Angel Embroidery section is a clean, photogenic hero with one "SHOP NOW" button.
- The Rapture fragrance section is atmospheric and shows scent notes (Vintage Rose, Silken Amber, Sheer Musk). It also has its own "SHOP NOW" button.
- The Member Days and Rewards modules put loyalty benefits in plain view.

## What's weak

- This is the same offer for the fourth straight day, with no escalation or new reason to act.
- The only button in the offer block is "JOIN FOR FREE", which asks for a signup and not a purchase.
- The email is extremely long, with at least six competing modules after the offer. That dilutes the single message in the subject.
- The "Your To-Do List" subject promises a checklist, but no to-do structure appears in the body.
- "LIMITED TIME" has no visible deadline, so the urgency isn't credible after four days of the same message.
- The fine print under the offer block is dense and hard to read at inbox scale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Build a literal three-item checklist near the top (Pick 2 bras, Pick 2 panties, Add a gift) with a checkbox graphic and one button per item. That would deliver on the hook and replace the catalog scroll.
- 2. Add a real deadline. Use "Ends Sunday midnight" or a countdown, so "LIMITED TIME" becomes believable.
- 3. Turn the "JOIN FOR FREE" button into "SHOP BUY 2, GET 2". Move the membership pitch below the offer.
- 4. Cut the body to 3 modules (offer, one hero product, one fragrance or gift). Move the rest to the site.
- 5. Reduce repetition. After three days of the same offer, rotate to a different hook such as bestsellers, gift timing or fragrance.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based Marketing Cloud-style build (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com` tracking) with a 57% QA pass rate. The automated checks found 0 hard errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 10 non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking pixels with no alt text, one content image with no alt, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 56 tracking links on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` were skipped as click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** The following load over `http://`, so mail clients may block them or flag mixed content.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - That is 11 `http://` requests in total.
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** The Dotomi pixels are `cookieredir` cookie-sync calls (`pub1.php`–`pub10.php`). They add many extra requests and send data to a third party. They are not needed for rendering.
- **Duplicate open pixels:** There are two `open.aspx` 1×1 images with different `qs` values:
  - Immediately after `<body>`.
  - Inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper`, before `<div id="AMPScript">`.
  
  One of them is probably redundant and may double-count opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…` and `pub1.php`–`pub10.php` all have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and a `width`/`height` of 1.
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. If it carries content, it is invisible when images are blocked or read by a screen reader.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`. The duplicate `.ReadMsgBody` rule later in the source is correct.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a non-existent element.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
- **Misplaced `<title>`:** `<title style="display: none; …">` sits inside the body, inside a nested table cell. It is invalid placement and has no effect.
- **Legacy doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is used with `<style>` blocks inside the body. This is valid in practice but is a robustness risk.
- **Leftover comment:** `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` is an unprocessed shell snippet in a comment. It is harmless but sloppy.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appear in the visible source or the subject line. The `AMPScript` div contains only empty "Ampscript v0 START/END" comments.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** The QA check did not find it. The check notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** The QA check did not find it. The same relay caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so confirm on the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` should be checked against the original message headers.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source and need checking in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM evidence:** All visible links are wrapped in `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects. UTM parameters and the final landing URLs could not be inspected because the probe was skipped.
- **Campaign ID:** The Dotomi URL carries `dtmc_email_category=20261023_VS_6382_FILEBAL`. This is a campaign identifier and matches the send date of 2026-10-23.
- **Landing alignment:** Not verifiable from the available data.
