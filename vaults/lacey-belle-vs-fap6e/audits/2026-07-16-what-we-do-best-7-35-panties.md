# What We Do Best: 7/$35 Panties 😌
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- "What We Do Best" is PINK's most confident framing for the 7/$35 deal in recent memory — but the email immediately undermines that confidence by offering a bra, a BUY 2 GET 1 FREE EVERYTHING, a Brow Shop module, a Summerhouse lifestyle section, and a Foldover Flares callout in the same scroll. The subject promises the brand's signature move; the body delivers a department store clearance event.
- The hero executes cleanly: two models, colorful panties, large price overlay, one button. If the email had stopped there and gone deep into panty variety — styles, a color grid, a "pick your 7" visual — it would have matched yesterday's "The Girlies ❤️ A Panty Sale: 7/$35" (July 15, 8/10) in tightness. Instead it pivots hard, and the BUY 2 GET 1 FREE module is so large it visually competes with the hero offer.
- Back-to-back 7/$35 panty deals (July 15 → July 16) compress urgency into meaninglessness. A subscriber who saw yesterday's email and didn't buy has zero new reason to act today.

## What's working

- **"What We Do Best" framing** is brand confidence done right — positions the 7/$35 as a signature, not a desperation discount.
- **Hero image is clean and immediately legible** — price is the largest element, models are on-brand, hierarchy is correct.
- **Subject line character count (~34 chars)** is mobile-safe and concrete; the deal is in the subject itself.
- **BUY 2 GET 1 FREE headline** is a genuinely strong secondary offer, well-executed if it had been the hero instead of a detour.
- **No visible render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **Deal stacking kills message clarity** — 7/$35 panties + BUY 2 GET 1 FREE EVERYTHING + bra feature + four lower grid modules is the July 14 clutter problem (4/10) reasserting itself.
- **Consecutive-day panty deal at the same price** (July 15 was also 7/$35) removes any urgency signal — the deal exists forever in the subscriber's mind.
- **No deadline or urgency mechanism anywhere visible** — "What We Do Best" implies permanence, not "act now."
- **Bra section and BUY 2 GET 1 FREE section pull the eye away** from the hero offer before the subscriber has committed to clicking the panty CTA.
- **Lower discovery grid (Brow Shop, Summerhouse, Foldover Flares) is noise** — small images, hard-to-read labels, and no clear connection to the 7/$35 anchor.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Ship a single-offer email.** Hero section → panty style grid (show 6–8 colorways) → one CTA. Move the BUY 2 GET 1 FREE to its own send tomorrow. The multi-offer structure is this brand's recurring performance ceiling.
- 2. **Add an expiry to the panty deal.** "Today only" or "Ends Sunday" or even "While supplies last" — anything to break the infinite-deal perception consecutive sends have created.
- 3. **Lean into the "What We Do Best" confidence in the body copy**, not just the subject. Hero subhead could read: "7 pairs. $35. This is what we're known for." — give the subject line a visual echo.
- 4. **Drop the lower grid modules entirely** for this send, or compress to a single lifestyle strip that reinforces the panty hero rather than redirecting to brows and flares.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fill Your Drawer: 7/$35, All Styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match, and stock up — new styles just dropped in the sale.`

## Technical Audit — PINK "7/$35 Panties" (20260716_PK_5798_NONBRAB)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally functional but carries mixed-content violations across 12 HTTP-only tracking and retargeting pixels, a duplicate open pixel, and unconfirmable compliance headers due to relay capture gaps. No broken links were detected in probed assets; the 19 redirect-chain links were skipped and remain unverified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open pixel** — the exact same open-tracking URL fires twice in the rendered HTML:
```
https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?32R277E6BX6UBJFBK3436T5ZPI.70252&d=70252&bmt=0
```
First occurrence: `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">` at top of `<body>`. Second occurrence: inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` div. This will double-count opens for this send in ESP reporting.

**19 click links unverified** — all route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect domain). HTTP probing was skipped. UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs is unconfirmed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content — 12 HTTP image sources** will be blocked by clients enforcing HTTPS (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook.com):

| Asset | Domain | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `o.gif` open pixel | `http://mi.victoriassecret.com` | 1 |
| UCMController retargeting pixel | `http://login.dotomi.com` | 1 |
| `pub1.php` – `pub10.php` retargeting pixels | `http://ads.dotomi.com` | 10 |

**Missing alt text** on 12 images, including one visible product image (`5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` on `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Tracking pixel alt text omissions are low-severity, but blank alt on a content image degrades screen reader experience and images-off rendering.

**`user-scalable=0`** in the viewport meta tag (`maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`) prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure.

**`<title>` element placed inside `<body>`** with `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden` — this is a preheader/preview-text pattern but uses the wrong element; `<title>` is a `<head>` element and its behavior inside `<body>` is undefined across clients. A `<div>` or `<span>` with the same inline styles is the correct approach.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype** — legacy but standard for email; not flagged as a defect.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** — QA relay did not observe this header. If missing at the MTA level (not just a relay gap), Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI, and the send may fail Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post not captured** — RFC 8058 one-click POST mechanism absent or not relayed. Same risk as above for inbox placement.

**Authentication-Results not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown. The sending domain `e2.vspink.com` is a subdomain ESP envelope; alignment with `vspink.com` DMARC policy is unverifiable from available headers.

*All three compliance gaps may reflect AgentMail relay capture limitations rather than true header absence. Recommend verifying against raw MTA headers before actioning.*
