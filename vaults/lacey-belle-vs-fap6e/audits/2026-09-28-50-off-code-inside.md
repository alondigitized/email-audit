# $50 OFF 💰 Code Inside ⬇️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest opening PINK has sent in a while. It leads with a plain-dollar promo, "SAVE $50 when you spend $175+", with a tiered ladder underneath ($25 off $100, $15 off $75) and a code, SAVEBOW. The subject matches the hero, and the ladder gives a shopper a reason to add to the cart. After the 09-26 stack of "UP TO 50% OFF + EXTRA 25% OFF," a clean dollar-off headline is a welcome change.
- The email then undercuts itself. Below the hero it runs denim, $8/$25 panties, $17.99 in-store bras, the Big Fall Sale "Final Days" block (still "UP TO 50% OFF + EXTRA 25% OFF"), a Halftime Show app capsule, a category grid, a rewards module and a long legal footer. That is five or six competing offers in one scroll. The Big Fall Sale block is the same maroon/pink creative PINK has run since 09-17, so the "$50 off" promo lands beside the fatigue it was meant to break.
- Verdict: a good hero and subject on top of a bloated body. The promo-day idea is worth keeping, but the body should do far less.

## What's working

- The hero is a concrete, tiered dollar offer, "SAVE $50 WHEN YOU SPEND $175+", with a black SHOP NOW button. It sits at the top and is easy to read.
- The subject promises a code, and the code appears right under the hero.
- "LAST DAY" on the panties and "FINAL DAYS" on the sale block give credible, time-bounded urgency.
- The denim editorial ("DENIM DONE RIGHT") is the only section that feels curated rather than promotional, and it has a specific CTA, "SHOP DENIM BY PINK."
- The rewards and app modules are present, so members and app users get a nudge.
- I saw no broken images or layout breakage in the render.

## What's weak

- Too many offers fight each other: $50 off, 8/$25 panties, $17.99 bras and 50% + 25% off. Nothing after the hero has a clear priority.
- The "Big Fall Sale" wordmark block is the same creative PINK has repeated for eleven-plus days, so it reads as stale next to the fresh promo hero.
- The offer isn't fully honest at a glance. "$50 off $175+" also carries "Excludes… collaborations and clearance," and there is a wall of fine print at the bottom.
- The body is very long, with a tile grid and a rewards module that add little. Most people won't scroll to them.
- The top banner and preheader line are tiny and illegible at this render size, so the preview text adds nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero, one supporting module (denim) and the rewards line. Fold the panties and bras offers into one "add-ons" row, and drop the repeated Big Fall Sale block.
- 2. Put the code in a copy-friendly badge, "Use code SAVEBOW", next to the button. Add a visible end date.
- 3. Shorten the exclusions to one line under the hero and link the rest.
- 4. Write a real preheader that adds new information instead of leaving the inbox line to the header banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$50 off $175 ends tonight 💰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your code: $15, $25 or $50 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, $100 or $175 and save more. Code SAVEBOW, ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $8 panties, denim edit and your extra 25% off sale styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email ships a raw `%%cellcode%%` merge token inside its JSON-LD block, and its structure has problems: a `<table>` inside `<head>` and 11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, one of them missing alt text. The HTML source is truncated, so only the head and the start of the body were verifiable. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved token in a URL.** The JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl` contains `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%`. The `offerPageUrl` in the same block resolves the same parameter to `d_cellid=092826445`, so the substitution failed for this field only.
- **Inconsistent `mi_u` values in the same JSON-LD block.**
  - `offerPageUrl` has `mi_u=200492586522`.
  - `merchantHomepageUrl` has `mi_u=092826445`, which is the cell/campaign ID used in `d_cellid` and `cm_mmc`.
  - The two fields likely feed different attribution systems or are mis-templated.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels.** The QA scan flagged 11 images loaded over `http://`:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The 10 Dotomi `pubN.php` calls each carry the hashed email in the query string (`a4edea106b33...=1`).
- **Open pixel.** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is HTTPS with `alt=""`, which is fine.
- **Unprobed links.** 45 tracking links were skipped by the QA check (redirect domains), so destination health is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`. Tables are not valid in `<head>`, and parsers will close the head early and move the content into the body. The `<meta>` and `<style>` tags that follow the table then land in the body.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **Missing alt text.** All tracking pixels above lack `alt`. One content image also lacks it: `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
- **Document metadata.** The visible `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute and no `<title>` is visible in the source shown.
- **Outdated doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional; not a functional problem.
- **Outlook fallback.** `line-height:1` is set on `body, table, td` in the `mso` block, which may cause cramped text in Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** 1 raw token found: `%%cellcode%%` in the `merchantHomepageUrl` value (`d_cellid=%%cellcode%%`). It is in JSON-LD metadata, not visible copy, so recipients won't see it. It still produces a broken structured-data URL and possibly broken attribution.
- The other personalization fields (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`) resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found.**
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- All three warnings come from the AgentMail relay, which may strip or not capture these headers. They are unconfirmed, not proven defects.
- The physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion of the HTML and could not be verified.
- The sender domain `e2.vspink.com` differs from the tracking domain `e1.victoriassecret.com`. This is common, but alignment can't be validated without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The email uses Cheetah/Digital River-style parameters (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`) instead of `utm_*`. This is internally consistent, and the campaign ID `20260928_PK_6227_FILEBAL` is the same across links.
- The `%%cellcode%%` failure (Section 4) breaks `d_cellid` on the homepage URL.
- The `offerPageUrl` lands on `/us/vs/bras` for a PINK-brand send. Whether that matches the CTA destinations couldn't be verified because the body is truncated.
- The offer end date `2026-09-29T23:59:59-04:00` is well-formed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `%%cellcode%%` substitution in the JSON-LD template, and reconcile `mi_u` between `offerPageUrl` and `merchantHomepageUrl`.
2. Move the JSON-LD `<script>` into `<body>` (or directly into `<head>` without the table wrapper), and move the `<meta>`/`<style>` tags back into a valid `<head>`.
3. Switch all pixels to `https://`, or drop them. The 10 Dotomi `pubN.php` cookie-sync pixels are the biggest source of mixed-content and blocking risk.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-...png`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, and remove or correct `min-width{}`), and add `lang` to `<html>`.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and authentication headers from raw MIME or the sending ESP, since the relay capture is inconclusive.
