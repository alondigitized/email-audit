# PSST: Patch Some Zzzs 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a PINK x Stoney Clover Lane collab email that tries to do too much. The hero is strong: sunglasses model, "PINK x STONEY CLOVER LANE" lockup, and "PATCH SOME Z'S" over a pajama flat-lay, with a "SLEEP ON IT (& IN IT)" button. The subject promises sleepwear, and the top block delivers it. After that the email splits into a panty 7-pack, a Community photo contest, a "Vanilla Lane" product block, a $40 off $200 app offer, a category grid, a rewards band, and a feed of unrelated shoes and clothes.
- The email has no single offer. The top strip mentions panty deals, the middle pushes 500 bonus points for posting a photo in the app, and the lower part has "SAVE $40 when you spend $200+". A reader scanning for the deal finds three, and each needs an app download or a sign-in. That is friction. It also follows the Nov 12 collab send ("You're Gonna LOVE Our New Collab") and a week of 10/$40 panty sends, so the collab creative is a repeat, and the panty-focused middle section echoes the earlier deal.
- Verdict: good-looking, on-brand, and a solid hero, but it is a long scroll with competing messages. It will get opens on sender recognition, and clicks will split across too many CTAs.

## What's working

- The hero ties the collab lockup to a specific theme (sleep), and the subject echoes it.
- The "SLEEP ON IT (& IN IT)" button is clear and sits right under the hero copy.
- The pink and glitter art direction is consistent and matches the PINK brand.
- The "SAVE $40 when you spend $200+" block is the one concrete price offer, and its large red type stands out.
- The collab products are shown in lifestyle shots with models, which makes the products feel real.

## What's weak

- There are three or more competing offers and CTAs: bonus points, $40 off, and a panty promo. No one offer leads.
- Several CTAs require an app download or sign-in ("DOWNLOAD THE APP", "DOWNLOAD OR SIGN IN"), which adds friction.
- The "WHAT DAY IS IT?" 7-pack panty section drifts from the sleep theme set by the subject.
- The lower half (category grid, rewards, "all over your feed" shoes) is generic and pushes the email to a very long scroll.
- The fine print is dense and the "$40 off $200+" offer has an exclusions line, which weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story: the sleep collab. Lead with a single offer (the $40 off $200 or the points bonus), and move it into the hero.
- 2. Remove the Community photo contest and the app-download CTA from the main flow, or move them to a separate send.
- 3. Add specific product CTAs such as "Shop PJ Sets" and "Shop Sleep Shirts", with a price shown.
- 4. Drop the generic feed of shoes and clothes at the bottom.
- 5. Give a deadline for the $40 offer so it reads as credible urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sleep Sets + $40 Off $200 💫`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition pajamas and a gift-ready 7-pack, plus $40 off $200+.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "PSST: Patch Some Zzzs 💫" (PINK, 2026-11-16)

The HTML source was truncated before the footer, so the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address could not be inspected. Findings come from the visible source and the automated QA output.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally valid enough to render, but it has malformed `<head>` markup and 12 non-HTTPS tracking pixels, one of which carries a hashed email address. It also has a viewport meta that disables zoom. QA scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings, and all three warnings are header-capture gaps that cannot be confirmed from this relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 43 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). Destination integrity and redirect health are unverified.
- **Duplicate JSON-LD targets:** All three `PromotionCard` entries in the `application/ld+json` block point to the same URL, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/collabs/stoney-clover-lane?cm_mmc=di-_-111626415-_-VSD_VSL_20261116_PK_6490_FULFILE-_&d_cellid=111626415&…`. Only the images differ.
- **Cleartext hashed email to a third party:** The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` carry `a4edea106b33…fc6fe=1`. This is the same SHA-256 value as `dtm_email_hash` in the click URLs. It is sent over plain HTTP, so any network observer can see it, and it is a privacy exposure.
- **Identifier in URLs:** `customerID=43cece97…b1e2f` and `dtm_email_hash=…` appear in query strings, so they can leak through referrer headers, logs, and forwarded emails.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid head structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`, and the `<style>` blocks. HTML parsers implicitly close `<head>` at the table. The charset meta and styles then fall into `<body>`, which risks charset sniffing and style handling differences across clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Missing title:** There is no `<title>` in the `<head>`.
- **Layout tables:** Only the wrapper table has `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables (`<table width="100%" border="0" cellpadding="0" …>`, `<table … class="container">`) do not.
- **Missing alt text (QA):** These images have no `alt`:
  - the tracking pixels `o.gif`, `UCMController…`, and `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`;
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, which is on the content CDN and may be a content image.

  The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`. The PNG needs a review to decide whether it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS images (QA):** 12 images use `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/…/o.gif`
  - `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?…`
  - `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Invalid CSS:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` lack a leading `.`, so they are type selectors that match nothing. `max-height:auto` is not a valid value. `table{max-width:600px!important;}` applies to every table, including the 100% wrapper.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source, and the per-recipient hash values are populated. The footer is not visible, so coverage is partial.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (QA WARN):** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN):** One-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 could not be confirmed. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, and link and image hosts are `*.e1.victoriassecret.com` and `*.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment cannot be assessed without the headers.
- **Footer content:** The visible-body unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated section and could not be verified.
- **Third-party ad sync:** 10 Dotomi cookie-redirect pixels and the `UCMController` pixel fire on open for ad retargeting. If the footer has no opt-out disclosure, this is a privacy-policy exposure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Consistent campaign IDs:** `cm_mmc` (`di-_-111626415-_-VSD_VSL_20261116_PK_6490_FULFILE`), `d_cellid=111626415`, and `mi_ecmp=20261116_PK_6490_FULFILE` agree with each other and with the send date.
- **No standard UTMs:** The visible URLs carry no `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign`. Attribution depends on the proprietary `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp`, and `d_cellid` parameters. The 43 skipped links were not inspected, so this applies only to the visible JSON-LD URLs.
- **Landing page not probed:** The destination `/us/pink/collabs/stoney-clover-lane` was not checked, and QA skipped the tracking-domain links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the wrapper `<table>` and JSON-LD out of `<head>`. Put the `<meta>` tags and `<style>` blocks first, and add `<title>`.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Serve all pixels over HTTPS. Move Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` to `https://`, or drop the 10 cookie-sync pixels. Stop sending the hashed email in cleartext.
5. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels, and give the `5bcec3ac-…png` image appropriate alt text if it is a content image.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, from the raw message headers rather than the relay copy.
8. Add `utm_*` parameters alongside the proprietary tags if cross-tool analytics attribution is needed.
9. Fix the invalid CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`) and the `max-height:auto` values.
