# 10/$40 Panties—Get Them in a Bunch
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This one leads with the promised offer — "10/$40 PANTY PARTY," "100+ new choices," a size selector (XS–XXXL), and a "SHOP ALL" button — all above the fold, no ambiguity about what's on sale. It's the same beat as the 2026-09-02 "10/$40 Panties" send but this time the deal is genuinely the hero, not competing with a secondary offer for attention in the top section.
- The problem is everything after the first screen. This is one of the longest, most crowded VS emails in the recent history — a lace bra shot, a $25 tee bra offer, a full Scent Event fragrance module, a free tote GWP, a sweepstakes/ticket drop, then a nine-tile category nav, a rewards signup block, and a trending carousel. By the time a reader scrolls past three unrelated offers, the panty deal that justified opening the email is long forgotten. This is a "everything but the kitchen sink" send, not a focused promo.
- Verdict: strong hook, weak follow-through — the hero alone would score well, but the endless secondary-offer stacking dilutes it and this brand needs to trust one offer per send more.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: "10/$40 PANTY PARTY" sits directly under the header with no scroll needed.
- Size-inclusive selector (XS–XXXL) visible right in the hero, which is a real proof point for shoppers who need it.
- "SHOP ALL" CTA button is clear, high-contrast, and unmissable.
- Visual hierarchy in the top section is clean — pink/lace-colored panty spread draws the eye straight to the offer.

## What's weak

- The email buries the panty offer under four more unrelated promos (bras, fragrance, free tote, sweepstakes) before even reaching the nav grid — cadence fatigue for anyone who's opened the last several VS sends.
- Fine print under the hero ("Excludes third-party styles and clearance. Priced as marked") undercuts the clean "10/$40" headline the moment you read past it.
- No product photography of the actual panty styles included in the deal — the hero is a loose fabric/lace swatch shot, not garments, so there's nothing to click toward specifically.
- The mid-email offers (Scent Event, free tote, ticket giveaway) have nothing to do with the panty deal, making the email feel like three or four separate campaigns stapled together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the panty offer plus one supporting module (e.g., the $25 tee bra cross-sell) and move fragrance/tote/sweepstakes to separate sends — this single change would do the most to keep attention on the offer that got the open.
- 2. Replace the abstract fabric-swatch hero with an actual panty product shot or flat-lay of the styles included, so there's something concrete to click toward.
- 3. Move the "excludes third-party styles and clearance" fine print smaller/lower or soften it — right now it sits close enough to the headline to feel like a catch.
- 4. Add a real end date/countdown to "Limited Time" — it currently reads as boilerplate urgency rather than a credible deadline.
