# Our #1 Collection Just Got Even Better
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Victoria's Secret's fourth "#1 Collection" or FlexFactor-adjacent email in three days, and the seams are showing. Yesterday's "Better (& Sexier) Than Braless" (audited 9/10) had a memorable competitive hook; the day before that, "Attn: We Leveld-Up Our #1 Bra Collection" at least signaled a product upgrade. Today's "Our #1 Collection Just Got Even Better" is a creative retreat — a vague superlative with no new angle.
- The production is clean. The hero image (model in a sage/olive bra, airy background, clear product visibility) is one of the better-looking shots in this run. The email layers two real incentives: free shipping on a full-price purchase at the top, plus an End of Summer Sale with extra 25% off midway through. That's genuinely useful.
- The fatal flaw is structural: this email doesn't know what it is. It opens like a collection story, pivots to a sale, then sprawls into a Wedding Shop, a Travel Shop, and a rewards section — four separate missions in one send. The subscriber who opened to learn what's new about the #1 Collection has to wade through a catalog before reaching anything that confirms why they should click now.

## What's working

- Hero image is the strongest in the recent series — sage/olive tones, clean negative space, product is the clear focal point.
- Free shipping offer is surfaced immediately above the fold in a high-contrast banner.
- The End of Summer Sale "Extra 25% Off" mid-email gives a second reason to click beyond brand interest.
- Collection headline "More from Our #1 Collection" ties directly to the subject line — no bait-and-switch on open.
- Layout renders cleanly with no visible bugs.

## What's weak

- Fourth email in three days with the same core message; "Just Got Even Better" is a thinner creative advance than the prior two sends, not a new chapter.
- Lower half is a catalog explosion — Wedding Shop, Travel Shop, and Rewards each compete for attention without a unifying thread connecting them to the hero.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA is generic — doesn't name FlexFactor, a specific style, or a collection category.
- The End of Summer Sale insert mid-email cuts against the product-story tone of the hero, creating a mixed signal between "discover" and "discount now."
- No visible deadline on the sale, so the 25% off offer has no urgency teeth.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rest the collection story for 24 hours.** After four near-identical sends, the subscriber's "new" threshold has moved; coming back with a fresh angle (first-person reviews, a "wear test" story, a celebrity or influencer peg) would carry more weight than another "even better" claim.
- 2. **Name the specific product in every CTA.** "Shop FlexFactor Bras" or "Shop the Balconette" beats "SHOP NOW" every time — specificity drives the click.
- 3. **Kill the Wedding Shop and Travel Shop sections.** Either deserves its own send; crammed here they dilute the hero message and signal that VS is just batch-blasting a template.
- 4. **Put a date on the sale.** "Extra 25% Off Sale — Ends Sunday" is four words that turn a passive discount into a reason to act today.
- 5. **Tighten the subject to a product or a number.** "Just Got Even Better" is a consumer-review phrasing with no signal — lead with what's new.

## Technical Audit — Victoria's Secret "Our #1 Collection Just Got Even Better"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard HTML4-transitional bulk send using Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPscript blocks, `stylingblock-content-wrapper` markup) with Dotomi/Conversant retargeting pixels. The primary technical concerns are HTTP-only third-party pixel assets and missing relay-level authentication headers.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 click-redirect links skipped by QA probe** — all route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. Links could not be validated for destination health or redirect chain integrity. No broken links confirmed, but no clean bill of health either.

**Duplicate open-pixel fired** — `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?X5RME26LXFTU3AEOKUHKGAQ5N4.70252&d=70252&bmt=0` appears twice in the HTML source (once in `<body>` pre-header, once inside the AMPscript `<div id="AMPScript">` block). This double-fires the open event on load, inflating open-rate metrics.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**10 Dotomi retargeting pixels missing `alt` text** — `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` all have no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the raw URL or skip unpredictably depending on client.

**1 content image missing `alt` text** — `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. If this is a product or CTA image (likely, given the image CDN path), subscribers with images off see nothing.

**All 11 third-party pixels use `http://`** — `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` and `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub*.php` are plain HTTP. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block mixed-content images. These pixels will silently fail to fire for the majority of recipients on modern clients.

**`<meta>` tags placed after `<table>` in `<head>`** — The first element inside `<head>` is a table (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`), followed by `<meta name="viewport">` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`. Viewport meta after non-meta content is ignored by some parsers; charset declaration being late can cause initial parse as wrong encoding in edge cases.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility regression (WCAG 1.4.4) and is unnecessary for a marketing email.

**`<title>` tag inside `<body>`** — `<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>` appears inside the email body (within a `<td>`), not in `<head>`. This is a preheader suppression hack that works in some clients but is invalid HTML and may surface as literal text in others.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens detected in the truncated source. AMPscript blocks (`<!-- Ampscript v0 START -->` / `END`) are present but appear empty in the supplied HTML — either rendered server-side before capture or contain no active personalization. No issues confirmed from available source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — Not detected by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders (>5k/day); absence increases spam folder placement risk and blocks Gmail's native unsubscribe UI.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — One-click unsubscribe required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policy since February 2024. Without it, Gmail cannot offer the one-click flow and may suppress deliverability scoring.

These headers may exist upstream and be stripped by the AgentMail relay before capture — verify against raw SMTP headers at the MTA level before treating as a sender-side defect.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown from relay capture. Sending domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`; if DKIM signing is misconfigured on this subdomain, combined with missing `List-Unsubscribe`, inbox placement will degrade materially.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

20 click links were not probed (redirect domain skipped), so UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs cannot be confirmed. The click-tracking domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect that typically preserves UTM params appended at send time — but this cannot be verified from the available source alone.

No issues confirmed; recommend spot-checking 2–3 redirect chains manually to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` survive the redirect.
