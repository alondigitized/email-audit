- Countdown timer (19:09:09) on the 25%-off member exclusive adds credible, time-boxed urgency.

## What's weak

- After the hero, the email reverts to the same discount-stack + category-tile template that's run every single day this week (2026-08-09 through 2026-08-12) — the fleece story disappears entirely by section three.
- Five back-to-back category tiles (Bras, Halloween, Back to Campus, Denim) dilute the "campus fleece" focus the subject sold — there's no dedicated fleece product grid or sizing/fit callout anywhere in the body.
- The HydroJug free-shipping block and credit-card upsell feel bolted on and unrelated to the cozy/campus theme, adding clutter rather than reinforcing the hook.
- "Exclusions apply" language sits quietly under two of the three discount blocks with no visibility into what's excluded.

- 3. Move the countdown/discount stack after the fleece-specific content, not immediately after it, so the email doesn't collapse into last week's template by scroll three.
