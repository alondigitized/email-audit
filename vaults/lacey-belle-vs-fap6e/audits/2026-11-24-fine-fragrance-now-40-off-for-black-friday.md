# Fine Fragrance, NOW 40% Off for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday follow-up to the 11/24 "40% Off Everything" send, with the subject narrowed to fragrance. The top of the email delivers on that subject. A black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner sits over a pink gift-set image, with "LIMITED TIME 40% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets" and two buttons, "Shop Fine Fragrances" and "Shop Gift Sets". The offer is clear and on-subject.
- After that, the email becomes the same kitchen-sink template VS has run all week. The 7/$35 Panty Party, the 40% Off Everything category grid, a Free Tote, a second Free Tote & Blanket, Free Shipping, a rewards card and Trending tiles all follow. The fragrance promise appears once and is then buried under about eight competing modules. The email is about 8,800px tall.
- One caveat: the screenshot was downscaled heavily, so I can't read the preheader or the small print. I'm judging layout and hierarchy, not fine copy. Verdict: a solid, on-offer hero attached to an unfocused body. It's well executed, but it feels like the sixth VS send this week.

## What's working

- The hero matches the subject. "40% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets" has high contrast and a clear block of text.
- There are two specific CTAs near the top, "Shop Fine Fragrances" and "Shop Gift Sets".
- The Black Friday banner and "LIMITED TIME" label give seasonal context. The 40% number is also a step up from the 30% that VS ran 11/21–11/23.
- The gift-set imagery is a good holiday fit.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- The fragrance focus is lost after the first module. Panty Party, "40% Off Everything" and the category grid pull attention away from the subject's promise.
- There are two near-duplicate free-tote modules ("Free Tote with any $100+ purchase" and "FREE Tote & Blanket with any $150+ purchase"). The result is a pile of stacked offers.
- "LIMITED TIME" has no end date, so the urgency is generic.
- The fine print at the bottom is a very long wall of text. Exclusions are also stated under several modules, which makes the offer feel less clean.
- Nothing links to a specific fragrance. There's no hero scent, no bestseller and no reviews.
- The rewards and $25-off-first-purchase card sit near the bottom, where few people will see them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make this a true fragrance send. Keep the hero and add a row of 3–4 named bestsellers with prices and a rating or "Bestseller" tag. Cut the panty, tote and category modules, or collapse them into one slim banner.
- 2. Merge the two tote offers into one gift-with-purchase module.
- 3. Add a real deadline, for example "Ends Cyber Monday 11:59 PT", in the hero.
- 4. Move the rewards and first-purchase offer up, or drop it for non-members.
- 5. Shorten the legal block, or link it out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `stylingblock-content-wrapper`). Its main confirmed problems are about 20 insecure (`http://`) third-party Dotomi pixels, several images with no alt text, and invalid head markup. The QA run scored 57% with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** QA found 10 `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` to `pub10.php` images, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`. All use plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy and some clients may block them or flag mixed content. The cookie-sync pixels then fail silently.
- **Hashed email in every URL.** The `pubN.php` URLs carry the SHA-256 value `4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe`. The JSON-LD URL carries it as `dtm_email_hash=...` along with `customerID=43cece97...`. Sending a hashed identifier over unencrypted HTTP exposes it to network observers. HTTPS would fix this.
- **66 tracking links not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Open-pixel markup.** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""`, which is correct. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The QA tool flagged 11 Dotomi pixels (the `UCMController` image and `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`). It also flagged the content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, which is the only one that matters to users. Screen readers will read out the filename or the URL. The pixels should have `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid head structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will move `<head>` content into `<body>` unpredictably. A second `<title style="display:none">` and `<style>` also appear inside body tables, and the head `<title>` is empty.
- **Invalid or dead CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{ width:100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **Global `table{max-width:600px!important}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important}`.** This can clip nested layouts in clients that honor `!important`.
- **JSON-LD block.** Most clients strip it, and it is only useful for Gmail annotations. It is not an error, but the `<table>` wrapper around it is.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The HTML was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** These are not confirmed defects. The QA tool itself notes the relay may drop them. Check the raw `.eml` headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, not failed. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` aligns with the tracking domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`.
- **Footer not verified.** The footer, physical address and visible unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section, so they could not be confirmed.
- Because of the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules, verify one-click unsubscribe directly in the original message headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** The only visible destination URL, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/giftguide/shop-all?cm_mmc=di-_-112426525-_-VSD_VSL_20261124_VSB_6542_FULLFILE-_&d_cellid=112426525&...&mi_ecmp=20261124_VSB_6542_FULLFILE`, uses proprietary parameters instead (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`). This can be intentional, since Coremetrics and Dotomi do their own attribution. Analytics tools that expect `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` would not attribute the visit.
- **Destination doesn't match the subject.** The subject promotes a fragrance sale, but the JSON-LD promotion URL points to `/vs/giftguide/shop-all`. This is a landing-page alignment risk. Only the JSON-LD URL was visible, so the actual button destinations (in the 66 skipped links) weren't verified.
- **Long query string.** It contains a hashed email and a customer ID, which makes URLs long. This is minor.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all Dotomi pixels (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/...`) to HTTPS. Ask the vendor for HTTPS endpoints, or drop the pixels if they aren't needed.
2. Add `alt=""` to the 11 tracking pixels. Add meaningful alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<meta>` tags and JSON-LD out of the wrapper table so they sit in a valid `<head>`, and remove the empty and duplicate `<title>` tags.
5. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`) and delete the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the original message headers. Don't treat the relay warnings as proof of a defect.
7. Probe a sample of the 66 skipped tracking links by following redirects to the final destination. Confirm that the CTA destination matches the fragrance offer and that attribution parameters survive the redirects.
8. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that analytics reads the `mi_*`/`cm_mmc` parameters.
