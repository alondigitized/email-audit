# As Seen EVERYWHERE: New Super Flare
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a break from the panty-megasend loop that's run daily since 08-06 — for once the hero product isn't the 10/$40 panties, it's the new Super Flare pant. That's a real shift after weeks of near-identical "Ghosted/Boo/Nightmare" panty sends, and the "As Seen Literally Everywhere" hook with a bold editorial hero shot is the strongest top-of-funnel visual this brand has run recently.
- Past that hero, though, it's the same kitchen-sink scroll: 40% off activewear, $15 sleep, $10 beauty, a Fall Campus Tour invite, foldover faves, Halloween shop, VS Fashion Show teaser, then the full nav grid (new arrivals, clothing, bras, panties, sleep, swim, activewear, accessories) and a rewards/$25-off block at the bottom. ## What's working

- Clear hero break from the panty-fatigue cadence — Super Flare pants get the top slot with a strong editorial photo and visible "GET THE LOOK" CTA.
- Subject-to-hero match: "As Seen Everywhere" copy is echoed directly in the hero headline ("As Seen Literally Everywhere"), so open and landing feel connected.
- Diverse, well-lit product photography throughout (activewear, sleep, beauty) with consistent PINK pink/black/white palette.
- Individual section CTAs ("GET THE LOOK", "SHOP NOW") are visually distinct black buttons that stand out against the pink backgrounds.
- Bottom nav grid with category thumbnails gives browsers a fast path if none of the above offers hooked them.

## What's weak

- Nine separate offers (Super Flare, 40% off activewear, $15 sleep, $10 beauty, campus tour, foldover faves, Halloween shop, fashion show, rewards) in one email — no single clear "the" offer once you scroll past the hero.
- The Super Flare story that the subject promises ends after one screen; the rest of the email pivots away to unrelated categories, so subject-to-body follow-through breaks down fast.
- "$10 Full-Sized Beauty" section uses a dark Halloween-mood photo (candle, purple lighting) that clashes tonally with the bright, sporty Super Flare hero above it.
- Campus Tour block is a low-relevance insert for a broad email list — vague "Follow @VSPINK to find out where" CTA has no concrete action.
- Extremely long single-column scroll (a dozen+ stacked sections) — high bail-out risk before reaching rewards/loyalty content at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 3-4 sections max: Super Flare hero, one flanking offer (activewear or sleep, not both), then nav grid + rewards. Let the subject's promise carry through the whole email instead of one screen.
- 2. Move the $10 beauty and Campus Tour blocks to separate, purpose-built sends — they dilute a strong fashion-forward hero with unrelated categories.
- 3. Add a size/fit or "shop the exact look" detail link under the hero (e.g., "Shop the crop + Super Flare set") — right now the CTA is generic "GET THE LOOK" with no specific product link visible.
- 4. Tighten visual tone consistency — drop the dark Halloween-lit beauty photo or move it to the Halloween-themed section where it belongs.
