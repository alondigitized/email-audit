- Five or six offers dilute the 8/$38 message the subject sets up, and the deal is buried by the third scroll.
- Every module says "LIMITED TIME" with no date or deadline, so the urgency is not credible.
- The same 8/$38 hero ran on 10-01 and 10-02, so there is no new creative reason to click today.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Eight Reasons to Click: 8/$38 Panties"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has structural defects: body-level markup sits inside `<head>`, and the viewport meta blocks zoom. It also loads 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't expose the headers. The source was truncated, so footer and body links are unchecked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **50 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped HTTP probing on click-redirect domains, so link resolution and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels.** 11 `<img>` sources use `http://`, which may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261004_PK_6241_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs.** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` and `customerID=43cece97…1e2f` (SHA-256-length hex) are in the JSON-LD `url` values. The same email hash is the parameter name in the Dotomi cookie-sync URLs (`?a4edea106…=1`). This sends a hashed email to a third-party ad network through 10 plain-HTTP requests. It is a privacy exposure rather than a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Body-level markup in `<head>`.** `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` appear before `<meta name="viewport">` and the `<style>` blocks. This is invalid HTML. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the `<table>`, which pushes the viewport meta and both `<style>` blocks into `<body>`. Clients that drop or ignore `<style>` in `<body>` may lose the mobile media queries.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Invalid CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` (it targets an element, not a class).
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an element selector, not a property, and does nothing.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
- **Missing alt text.** The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. The 11 tracking and cookie-sync pixels also lack `alt`, but they are non-visual, so the impact is minimal.
- **Doctype.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is functional for email, but it is paired with `role="presentation"` attributes.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source. `mi_u`, `dtm_email_hash`, and `customerID` are all populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap, so I can't confirm a real defect. One-click unsubscribe is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer unverified.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters.** The destination `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/underwear/underwear-offer` uses a proprietary scheme: `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp`, and `mi_u`. It has no `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign`. This is acceptable if Coremetrics and Dotomi are the intended analytics, but it won't register in UTM-based tools.
- **Campaign IDs are consistent.** `20261004_PK_6241_FILEBAL` is identical across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp`, and `dtmc_email_category`.
- **Landing page unverified.** The landing page was not probed because tracking domains were skipped, so deep-link alignment and status are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD `<script>` and its wrapper `<table>` into `<body>`, so `<meta>` and `<style>` stay in `<head>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Serve all pixels over HTTPS. Reduce or remove the 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` cookie-sync calls, and review sending a hashed email to a third party.
4. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-…png`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Fix the CSS: `.ReadMsgBody`, and drop `min-width{…}` and `max-height:auto`.
6. Inspect the raw message headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e2.vspink.com`.
7. Re-run QA against the full HTML (not truncated), and probe the landing URL directly to verify the footer unsubscribe, postal address and destination status.
