# Attn Bomshell: Scent Event Won't Last
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- After four tight, fragrance-only Scent Event sends (May 14–17), this email makes the same mistake the May 17 "Let's Go Shopping" send did: the subject promises urgency around a specific campaign, but the body pivots halfway through to "The Lacy Panty Collection" — a completely different category with different audience intent. The subject creates a focused expectation; the email delivers a quarterly clearinghouse.
- The fragrance hero is genuinely good. Multiple Bombshell variants (Rouge, Bleu, and others) are named and visually distinct, the $39/$6.95 pricing from prior sends is visible early, and the hero composition is clean. If the email ended after the fragrance section, this would be a solid urgency send capping a week-long campaign. Instead, it concedes half its scroll depth to lingerie, draining the "Won't Last" tension completely.
- The hard-to-ignore production miss: the subject spells the flagship franchise name as "Bomshell" — one 'b' short of "Bombshell." After five polished consecutive sends building equity around that brand name, this is a notable QA failure that hands skeptical subscribers a reason to question the send's credibility.

## What's working

- Bombshell fragrance showcase hero is clean — multiple named variants (Rouge, Bleu, etc.) laid out clearly with good visual separation
- $39 EDP and $6.95 mini pricing is visible early and consistent with the five prior Scent Event sends, which rewards repeat openers
- "Won't Last" in the subject is credible because this campaign has been price-consistent all week — the trust is earned
- No render bugs — layout renders cleanly across the full scroll length

## What's weak

- "Bomshell" in the subject is a typo — the line is "Bombshell" and it's this campaign's entire brand anchor; misspelling it in the subject line is a material QA miss
- The pivot to "The Lacy Panty Collection" mid-email guts the urgency; a subscriber ready to act on fragrance sees lingerie content instead of a reinforcing CTA
- "Won't Last" is soft urgency — the May 14 "Just for Today" send was sharper because it committed to a window; no end date or time is visible here
- This is the sixth email in six days on this campaign; the kitchen-sink structure doesn't justify the send frequency the way a clean focused close-out email would

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the lingerie section entirely for this send.** "The Lacy Panty Collection" is its own campaign — mixing it here undermines both. The Scent Event close deserves a focused send.
- 2. **Fix the typo before the next send.** "Bomshell" → "Bombshell." Build a QA check for the brand's own franchise names in subject lines.
- 3. **Commit to a hard deadline.** "Ends Tonight at Midnight" or "Ends Sunday" converts "Won't Last" from vague threat to actionable window — the difference between a subscriber bookmarking it and buying it now.
- 4. **Elevate one hero SKU above the lineup.** Showing all Bombshell variants equally dilutes click intent. Lead with one (e.g., Rouge), grid the rest — gives subscribers a clear first click.

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional layout sent via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) with supplementary MessageIgnite (MI) and Dotomi retargeting pixels. The primary technical risks are 12 HTTP-only pixel sources that will be blocked by modern mail clients enforcing mixed-content rules, and absent authentication-results headers preventing SPF/DKIM verification at the relay layer.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure and a real usability regression for low-vision users.

**14 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA across all Dotomi pixels, the MI pixel, the DV pixel, and two content images (`eb81ca741916f4dd.png`, `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`). Decorative tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly; content images need descriptive alt text.

**Legacy doctype** — `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` is functional but dated; not a breakage risk in current clients.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — QA relay did not capture this header. If it is missing from the actual SMTP envelope (not just the relay capture), Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the one-click unsubscribe UI shown to subscribers, increasing spam-report rates. Requires header-level verification against a raw `.eml` capture.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — same caveat; one-click machine unsubscribe will not function for clients that support it (Gmail, Apple Mail 16+).

**Authentication-Results absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown at relay. The sending domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`; authentication alignment should be verified directly against SMTP headers in a raw message capture.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from the truncated HTML; assumed present in footer (standard for VS sends).

**UTM coverage unverifiable** — all 20 CTA links are behind `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects; QA correctly skipped probing them. UTM tagging on final landing pages (e.g., `victoriassecret.com`) cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects or sampling live clicks.

**Campaign ID is consistent** — `DV_20260518_VSB_5459_FULLFIL` appears in both MI pixel params and Dotomi UCM params, indicating the campaign tag propagates correctly across the tracking stack.
