# Attn: Buy One, Get One 50% off PJ Sets
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the PJ Event send, and it breaks the panty-party rut. After a week of near-identical 7/$35 emails, the brand opens with a different hero: "The PJ Event," a big "Buy One, Get One 50% Off Pajama Sets" and a SHOP NOW button, over a holiday-pajama group shot. The subject matches the hero. The top of the email delivers on it.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a catalog. It stacks panty packs from $20, bras from $25, $7 mists and lotions, a BOGO on beauty gift sets, and a Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free in-store offer. That makes six or seven competing offers and six or seven SHOP NOW buttons. The PJ hero is clear on its own, but it has to share the email with everything else. There is also a dense wall of legal fine print at the bottom.
- Verdict: a strong, seasonally right opener that is diluted by too many offers and by exclusion-heavy fine print. It beats the stale panty repeats on freshness, but it is still a brand-wide promo blast.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "The PJ Event" in script, then "Buy One, Get One 50% Off Pajama Sets," then one button.
- Holiday-pajama imagery fits the mid-November gifting season.
- It is a change of pace after the run of 7/$35 panty sends, such as 11/11 "Last Day: 7/$35 Panties & 40% Off."
- Each module has a bold, readable offer headline ("Panty Packs from $20", "Bras from $25", "$7 Mists").
- A rewards and credit-card banner near the bottom gives members a benefit.

## What's weak

- Too many offers and too many buttons. Pajamas, panties, bras, beauty, gift sets and an in-store bra offer all compete.
- The bras module has text laid over the busy photo, and it looks hard to read.
- The fine print is a huge block of legal text with exclusions. The offers say "Online only," "Excludes PINK" and "Excludes clearance," so they feel less honest than the headlines suggest.
- The panty module keeps pushing panties right after a week of panty sends, which risks fatigue.
- The in-store "Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free" offer has no clear online path.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the PJ hero plus two supporting modules, such as pajama sets by style and a sleepwear gifting guide. That would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Show actual pajama products with prices instead of only lifestyle shots.
- 3. Move the panty and bra offers into a separate send.
- 4. Show the real deadline in the hero. "Attn" implies urgency, but no end date is visible.
- 5. Shorten the legal block and put the key exclusions next to each offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `stylingblock` wrappers). It has structural HTML problems (body-level markup inside `<head>`) and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. It also loads 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi pixels with no alt text. The header-related QA warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **44 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**.
- **11 Dotomi pixels load over plain `http://`**, which can be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- Most of these are likely hidden 1x1 cookie-sync pixels, so the practical impact is wasted requests and blocked pixels, not broken visuals.
- The open pixel (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...`) is correctly HTTPS, 1x1 and `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Markup inside `<head>`:** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">` and the `<style>` blocks. Tables aren't valid in `<head>`, so parsers will close the head early. The viewport meta, charset meta and `<style>` blocks may then land in `<body>`, where behavior varies by client.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it never matches.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is a type selector for a non-existent element.
- **`<title>` in the body:** `<title style="display: none; ...">` is empty and sits inside the stylingblock wrapper, not in `<head>`.
- **Missing alt on a content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt` (flagged by QA).
- **Missing alt on pixels:** the 11 Dotomi `<img>` pixels also lack `alt`. This is lower impact, but `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Doctype and language:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, and no `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>` in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so this can't be confirmed for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **QA warnings, unconfirmed:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers, so there is no confirmed violation. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** neither is visible in the truncated source, so the footer is unverified.
- **Hashed PII in URLs:** SHA-256 email hashes are embedded in URLs, including the `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…` parameters in the JSON-LD URL. The same hash appears as a parameter name in all 10 `cookieredir` pixel URLs. This is a privacy and consent consideration for third-party ad-sync pixels, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Non-UTM tracking scheme:** the sample link uses `cm_mmc=di-_-111226525-_-VSD_VSL_20261112_VS_6476_FILEBAL`, `mi_ecmp=20261112_VS_6476_FILEBAL` and `d_cellid=111226525`. There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters, so analytics that depend on UTMs won't attribute this traffic unless the platform maps `cm_mmc`.
- **Landing mismatch in structured data:** the JSON-LD `PromotionCard` `url` is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/bras?...`, but the subject is "Buy One, Get One 50% off PJ Sets". The schema destination doesn't match the promoted category. Other CTA destinations couldn't be checked because the redirect links were skipped.
- **Campaign tag:** the campaign tag `FILEBAL` doesn't clearly relate to the PJ set offer. This is a naming observation only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD `<script>` and its wrapper table out of `<head>`. Put the `<meta>` and `<style>` blocks inside a valid `<head>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-…png`. Add `alt=""` to the Dotomi pixels.
4. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop the ones that fail. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector and remove `min-width{}`.
5. Point the JSON-LD `url` at the PJ sets landing page, or remove it if it no longer applies.
6. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that analytics maps `cm_mmc`/`mi_ecmp`.
7. Check the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) outside the relay, and verify the footer unsubscribe link and address in the full HTML.
8. Resolve the 44 skipped redirect links with a redirect-following probe to confirm their destinations.
