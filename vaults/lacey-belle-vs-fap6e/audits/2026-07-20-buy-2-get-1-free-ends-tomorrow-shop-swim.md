# Buy 2, Get 1 FREE Ends Tomorrow: Shop Swim
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Victoria's Secret's July 20 send — a promotional calendar pivot after five consecutive Panty Party emails (July 15–19). The subject leads with a Swim BOGO urgency play ("Buy 2, Get 1 FREE Ends Tomorrow: Shop Swim"), but the email body immediately undermines that focus by stacking three distinct promotional modules: the Swim BOGO, a still-prominent 7/$35 Panty Party section, and the $6 body care deal that first appeared July 18. That's offer overload, and the top hero doesn't clearly read as swimwear — the opening image skews beauty/fragrance, making "Shop Swim" feel like a broken promise on open.
- The Panty Party inclusion is logically defensible — yesterday's email called it "Ends Tomorrow," so July 20 is the true close-out day — but giving it near-equal visual weight to the swim BOGO splits subscriber attention at the exact moment VS is trying to shift the promotional narrative forward. Five days of Panty Party conditioning means subscribers will hunt for the familiar 7/$35 block and likely ignore the swim story VS wants to tell.
- The bottom line: competent, clean, and worth sending as a multi-offer wrap, but it lacks the single-minded clarity that made the July 16 "300+ New Choices" send score a 9. The swim BOGO is the right next campaign beat; the execution just doesn't commit to it.

## What's working

- **Urgency is earned, not manufactured**: "Ends Tomorrow" in the subject is credible because subscribers have watched the Panty Party build across five days — the deadline lands with weight.
- **Seasonally correct**: Summer swim content on July 20 is on-calendar; VS is timing the promotional shift before peak summer passes.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, overlapping text, or layout breakage visible — consistent VS production quality.
- **Multi-module logic has real conversion upside**: Subscribers who ignore swim may still click Panty Party or body care — the stacking isn't irrational, just unfocused.

## What's weak

- **Subject-to-hero mismatch**: The subject says "Shop Swim" but the top hero reads beauty/fragrance-adjacent, not swimwear — the visual promise and the email promise aren't aligned.
- **Three competing offers fragment attention**: Swim BOGO + Panty Party + $6 body care gives subscribers no clear answer to "what does VS want me to do right now?"
- **Panty Party module still carries full weight**: After calling it "Ends Tomorrow" in yesterday's send, featuring it as a prominent mid-email block with its own category links blurs the urgency signal on both offers.
- **No specific swim hero SKU**: The swim CTA links to a category page, not a featured piece — nothing to aspire to, nothing to anchor the click.
- **Sixth consecutive day of VS email**: Cadence fatigue is real at this frequency; open and click rates will be suppressed regardless of offer quality.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to swim in the hero**: Replace the opening with a hard lifestyle swim shot — one model, one standout suit, BOGO badge overlaid. If the subject says Shop Swim, the first pixel should prove it.
- 2. **Demote Panty Party to a last-call strip**: A slim banner ("Last chance: 7/$35 ends tonight") is enough — it doesn't need its own full module after five days of primary real estate.
- 3. **Feature one specific swim SKU**: Link the hero to a product page, not a category landing — give subscribers something concrete to click toward.
- 4. **Drop body care from this send**: Or save it for a standalone tomorrow. The $6 deal doesn't need to share space here; it already had its own email July 18.
- 5. **Simplify and sharpen the subject line**:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Swim BOGO ends tonight — buy 2, get 1 free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free swimsuit: buy 2, get 1 free today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop this season's hottest styles before the deal disappears.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick any 3 swim pieces — pay for just 2. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Victoria's Secret "Buy 2, Get 1 FREE Ends Tomorrow: Shop Swim"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) with a standard table-based layout. Two confirmed infrastructure bugs are present: a duplicate open-tracking pixel that will inflate open-rate metrics, and an unrendered build-script artifact in the HTML. Ten Dotomi retargeting pixels load over HTTP and will be silently blocked by most modern clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open pixel — metric inflation confirmed**

The 1×1 open-tracking pixel fires twice in the source:

- First instance: top of `<body>` in a `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">`
- Second instance: inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` content region

Both point to the identical URL:
```
https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?NMUIQPG2RMWUNEQ6D7LK6C33RM.70248&d=70248&bmt=0
```

Every open registers two beacon hits. Reported open rates for this send are overstated by approximately 2×.

**20 click links skipped by QA probe**

All CTA links route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` — standard SFMC click-wrapping. UTM parameter values could not be verified from the truncated source (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

Disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 2.1 §1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure; also violates iOS/Android accessibility guidelines.

**`<title>` element placed inside body**

```html
<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>
```

Located inside the table content stack, not in `<head>`. Some clients will surface this as visible text or treat it as a parsing error. Should be empty or moved to `<head>`.

**Ten Dotomi retargeting pixels load over HTTP**

```
http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...
http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?...
... (pub1–pub10)
```

Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail proxy/block non-HTTPS image sources. These pixels are almost certainly not firing for the majority of recipients — retargeting audience sync is broken.

**Hero image missing alt text**

```
src: https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png
```

No `alt` attribute. Image-blocking clients and screen readers receive no fallback. The dotomi pixel images also lack alt, but those are tracking-only so `alt=""` (empty string, not absent) is the correct fix there.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Unrendered build-script artifact**

```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->
```

This is a Bash date command left as an HTML comment — not an AMPScript expression. It appears to be a template tagging convention (e.g., `ET07.26`) that was never evaluated, pointing to a gap in the build/render pipeline. It is invisible to recipients but is present in every recipient's source and signals a broken pre-send templating step.

**AMPScript block present but empty**

```html
<!-- Ampscript v0 START -->
<!-- Ampscript v0 END -->
```

No unrendered `%%variable%%` tokens are visible in the truncated source. No personalization failures detectable from available HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent**

The QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail's bulk sender policy (enforced February 2024) requires one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, RFC 8058) for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. Victoria's Secret almost certainly exceeds this threshold on every send.

If the header is present at the MTA level but not relayed by AgentMail, this is a monitoring gap, not a sender error — but it cannot be confirmed from available data.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent**

SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. Sending domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com` (SFMC subdomain). If DKIM is misconfigured, Gmail and Yahoo will route to spam per their 2024 bulk sender requirements.

**Sender address alignment**

`From:` domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`. For DMARC alignment, the DKIM signing domain or SPF `Return-Path` domain must align with this. Cannot verify without `Authentication-Results`.

**Physical address / CAN-SPAM footer**

HTML is truncated; footer content with physical address could not be verified. Assumed present based on VS's standard template compliance history but not confirmed from this source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All destination URLs are wrapped behind `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects, which prevents direct inspection of landing page UTMs. The QA probe skipped all 20 click-tracked links.

**Cannot confirm:**
- Whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` are present on destination URLs
- Whether campaign ID `5821` or date `20260720` are carried through as UTM or query parameters
- Whether landing pages surface the promoted offer (Buy 2 Get 1 Free / Swim) without friction

This is a gap in QA coverage — the click-redirect probe must be followed through to the final destination URL to validate UTM carriage and landing page alignment.
