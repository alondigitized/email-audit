# Today Only: $25 Sweats When You Buy 3+
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine step up from PINK's recent daily-megasend cadence: the hero leads with a real, easy-to-parse tiered offer — $25 sweats/sweatshirts when you buy 3+ ($30 for 2, $35 for 1) — rather than another 10/$40 panty rerun or vague "buy 2 get 1" bra push. The pricing structure is unusual for the brand (reverse-tier pricing that rewards volume) but it's clearly laid out and honest about the math.
- The email is undercut by a visible rendering bug at the very top: garbled, nonsensical text ("FREE Party loading...JK, the app crashes an account to get yours.") sits above the hero, which is either broken preheader copy leaking into the render or a corrupted merge token. That's the first thing the eye hits before the "BUY MORE SAVE MORE" hero, and it actively damages trust and polish.
- Below the fold this reverts to the standard PINK megasend format — bra event, Super Flare denim, underwear grid, tees, free tote GWP, category nav, rewards block, and a UGC feed — all competently built but formulaic and now appearing for the eighth-plus time in five weeks.

## What's working

- The hero offer is concrete and specific: $25 each when you buy 3+, with the full tier breakdown shown up front.
- "TODAY ONLY" gives a credible, simple urgency hook without stacking exclamation points or ALL CAPS excess.
- Loyalty/rewards value is visible ("welcome back," $25 off first purchase) for repeat shoppers.
- Social proof is present via the "AS SEEN ON...EVERYONE" Super Flare callout and the "all over our feed" UGC grid at the bottom.
- Visual consistency (PINK pink/black palette, model photography, button styling) holds throughout the long scroll.

## What's weak

- There's a clear render bug at the very top of the email — garbled, off-brand text ("...JK, the app crashes an account to get yours.") appears before the hero, undermining the polish of the send.
- The hero CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" rather than something specific like "Shop Sweats" — a missed chance to reinforce the exact offer.
- This is effectively the 7th-8th near-identical PINK megasend structure in five weeks (per 09-07 through 09-12 history) — cadence fatigue is real for a daily subscriber.
- The tiered pricing ($35 for 1, $30 for 2, $25 for 3+) buries the single-item price as the least favorable option, which reads as a mild penalize-the-non-bulk-buyer tactic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the garbled top-of-email text immediately — this is the single biggest lift available; a broken/nonsensical first line reads as a bug to every recipient and costs more trust than any copy change gains.
- 2. Swap "SHOP NOW" for a specific CTA tied to the actual offer, e.g. "Shop $25 Sweats."
- 3. Lead the hero with the dollar amount instead of burying it under "BUY MORE SAVE MORE" — put "$25 SWEATS" in the largest type on the page.
- 4. Vary the send structure/cadence — five-plus near-identical megasends in a row risks list fatigue; consider a shorter, single-offer version for "Today Only" sends specifically, saving the full megasend for weekly moments.
