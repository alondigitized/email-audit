# Last Day: Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 FREE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send in six days pushing "The Bra Event" — same "Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free" offer that ran 2026-09-11, 2026-09-14, and 2026-09-15, now dressed up with "Last Day" urgency in the subject. The problem is identical to the 09-15 send: that urgency is nowhere in the actual hero. The top of the email reads "LIMITED TIME / Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free / A must-visit for every day, shine for nightlife too, and a bold color for everything else" — no deadline, no countdown, no "ends tonight." A subscriber scanning past the subject line gets zero reinforcement that this is their last chance.
- Below the fold this is the same kitchen-sink structure the brand always runs: Wicked Collection balconette bras, an "All Bras" push, a $45 panties multi-buy, and a Buy 2 Get 3 Free bath/body deal. It's a lot of real estate for one email, and the actual "Last Day" offer gets one shot at the top before the email moves on to unrelated categories.
- This lands as a mediocre, fatigued send — the offer is real and the render is clean, but the subject's urgency is a bait that the body doesn't deliver on, and the repetition since 09-11 is wearing thin.

## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout bugs anywhere in the email.
- Hero offer ("Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free") is immediately visible above the fold with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- Product photography (models, flatlays, bath/body grid) is well-lit and on-brand throughout.
- Rewards/loyalty tile ("$25 Off Your First Purchase") is present near the footer for prospective members.
- Multiple distinct product categories are organized into clearly separated visual blocks.

## What's weak

- "Last Day" urgency from the subject is completely absent from the hero copy — it just says "LIMITED TIME," which is generic and has appeared in prior sends too.
- No countdown, deadline time, or "ends tonight" language anywhere visible in the render.
- This is the same core Bra Event offer sent 09-11, 09-14, and 09-15 — a subscriber has now seen "Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free" four times in six days.
- Email is extremely long and kitchen-sink — bras, panties, bath/body all compete for attention, diluting the supposedly urgent bra offer.
- The panties and bath/body sections below have no urgency framing either, so if "Last Day" is meant to apply email-wide, nothing signals that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deadline in the hero — replace "LIMITED TIME" with "ENDS TONIGHT" or a specific cutoff time, matching what the subject line promises.
- 2. Cut the email down to just the bra offer for this final push — drop panties/bath-body to remove competing CTAs on the last day of a specific event.
- 3. Add a visual urgency device (countdown timer or bold "TODAY ONLY" banner) directly under "The Bra Event" logo.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 09-11/09-14/09-15 sends — reusing near-identical hero copy four times risks list fatigue and lower opens on the next campaign.
