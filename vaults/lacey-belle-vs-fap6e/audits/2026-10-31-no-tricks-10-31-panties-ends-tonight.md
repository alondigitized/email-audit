**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**

- This is the last-day close of the 10/$31 panty deal, and the top of it works. A "PANTY PARTY" banner, a large "10/$31", "Best Panty Deal of the Year", an XS–XXL size row and a "LAST DAY" tag make the offer clear straight away. "No Tricks" is a good Halloween-day hook for a 10/31 send.

## What's weak

- Six or more competing offers sit under the hero, so the eye has no single path after the panty banner.
- "LAST DAY" and "LIMITED TIME" are repeated on nearly every module, which cancels the urgency.
- The "SHOP ALL" button is generic and sends people to a category, not a hero product.

- 5. Vary the creative across the three days of this promo, for example by moving from "best deal" to "styles going fast" to "last call".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with malformed document structure, a viewport setting that blocks pinch-zoom, and about 11 third-party Dotomi pixels (10 `http://` cookie-sync images plus 1 UCM image). The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, not because they failed. The HTML we received is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 55 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixels:** Ten Dotomi cookie-sync images load over plain `http://`, which Gmail and Apple Mail may block or proxy:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?…` through `pub10.php`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** The JSON-LD `url` and the Dotomi pixels carry `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe`, `mi_u=200492586522` and `customerID=43cece97…1e2f`. The email hash is also the parameter name in `pub*.php?a4edea106…=1`. These are hashed rather than plaintext, but they are persistent user identifiers sent to third parties.
- **Open pixel:** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""`, which is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid structure:** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. A `<title style="display:none">` also appears inside `<body>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` early, so the `<meta>` viewport and charset tags that follow the table may be ignored.
- **Old doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used, which is valid for email but dated.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged these images:
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` is a content image and needs alt text.
  - The Dotomi pixels (`pub1–10.php`, `UCMController`) have none either. If they are tracking-only, `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` is the correct fix.
- **Invalid CSS rules:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector, so both are dead rules.
- **Global table cap:** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` is applied to all tables, which could cause clipping in nested layouts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source. The personalized parameters in the URLs are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both headers were not found. The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect**. Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules can't be verified.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `VictoriasSecret@e1.victoriassecret.com`, a subdomain. Alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer:** The visible unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign naming:** The campaign ID `20261031_VS_6420_FILEBAL` matches the send date and the "Ends Tonight" subject.
- **Tracking scheme:** The one landing URL visible (`/us/vs/panties/panties-offer?cm_mmc=di-_-103126545-_-VSD_VSL_20261031_VS_6420_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=…&mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=…`) uses Coremetrics `cm_mmc` and Epsilon parameters, with no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. This is fine if the site analytics is Coremetrics-based. If GA or another UTM-based tool is also used, those sessions will be attributed as direct or unattributed.
- **Not verified:** The landing page destinations were not probed, so "panties-offer" alignment with the 10/$31 offer can't be confirmed.

2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Move the `<table>`, JSON-LD `<script>` and `<title>` out of `<head>`/`<body>` to their proper places, and fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors.
4. Re-run the compliance check against the raw SMTP message, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
