# Last Day for SCENT EVENT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This send does what it promises and does it fast — "scent event," "LAST DAY," "$39.95 Eau de Parfum," "$6.95 travel sprays" all sit above the fold with a clean bottle-lineup hero. There's no bait-and-switch here, unlike the 2026-09-04 "Time to Smell GOOD" version — this one's subject and hero are in sync.
- But it's still the same Scent Event pricing ladder VS has run all week, now just with urgency stapled on. The "Tease for Every Flirt" fragrance-family module is a nice touch of differentiation, but everything below that — Ticket Drop giveaway, bras, PJs, category grid, Rewards, trending panties — is generic template filler that has nothing to do with the subject line, pushing the actual offer further from view once you scroll.
- Solid, on-brand, honest — but a fourth-in-a-row fragrance/urgency email this week means fatigue is setting in, and the long non-fragrance tail below the hero dilutes an otherwise sharp open.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the subject immediately: "scent event," "LAST DAY," "$39.95 1.7 Oz Eau de Parfum," "$6.95 Travel Sprays and Mini Mists" all visible without scrolling.
- Clear, single "SHOP NOW" button directly under the offer.
- "America's #1 Fragrance Brand" badge and sourced claim add credibility rather than just hype.
- "A Tease for Every Flirt" module gives four named scent families (Strawberry Bisou, Tease, Sugar Fleur, Crème Cloud) with real product imagery — good for someone actually comparing scents.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- After the fragrance section ends, the email pivots entirely away from scent — Ticket Drop giveaway, bras, PJs, lingerie grid, Rewards $25 off — none of it reinforces the "Last Day" urgency in the subject.
- "LAST DAY" urgency isn't reinforced with a countdown, deadline time, or repeated CTA further down — once you scroll past the hero, urgency evaporates.
- This is the third fragrance-themed email in five days (2026-09-04, 2026-09-05, now 2026-09-08) — cadence fatigue for anyone not currently shopping perfume.
- Fine print at the bottom ("Minimum purchase required," "Priced as marked, limit 10 per order") is easy to miss above the fold — the offer looks simpler in the hero than it is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or drastically shrink the non-fragrance modules (Ticket Drop, bras, PJs, category grid) — a "last day" email should stay focused on the expiring offer, not turn into a full-catalog send.
- 2. Add a visible deadline (e.g., "Ends tonight at midnight ET") near the hero CTA instead of just "LAST DAY" — makes the urgency concrete and credible.
- 3. Surface the limit-10/minimum-purchase fine print near the price callouts, not buried in the footer — protects trust.
- 4. Repeat a scent-specific CTA near the "Tease for Every Flirt" module (e.g., "Shop This Scent") so engaged scrollers don't have to scroll back up.
