# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this still very much reads like "old VS" — moody, black-and-white, artsy "Very Sexy Collection" hero that feels more like a perfume ad than a bra site. It's pretty, I'll give it that, but I opened this app wanting my usual bra restock or to see if the Semi-Annual Sale dates dropped, and instead I got a rewards pop-up shoved in my face before I even got to look around.

## What's working

- **The nav bar** — BRAS and PANTIES are right there as top-level tabs, so I know I'm one tap from my stuff.
- **"Our Roman Empire? Perfecting the Push-Up."** — cute, on-trend copy that actually made me smirk. That's the kind of meme-y millennial humor that keeps my attention for half a second longer.
- **Rewards modal contents** — Points Per $, $10 Reward, Birthday Gift are laid out clean and simple, no confusion about what I'd get for joining.

## What's weak

- **The pop-up ambushes the hero** — I land on the page and immediately a "Ready to get rewarded?" box covers the bottom right of the screen and chops off the sentence right below it. Feels intrusive before I've even looked around.
- **No actual offer** — "Discover Deals That Are Too Good to Miss" tells me nothing. What deals? What %? I'm not clicking through on vague banner text.
- **Hero isn't about bras at all** — "Very Sexy Collection" is a fragrance/lingerie line push, not the bra-fit content I actually come to VS for.
- **Nothing about Free Panty Wednesday** — that's literally an email hook they use to get me back, and it's nowhere on this homepage.

## Recommendations

- Delay or soften the rewards modal — let me see the hero and scroll once before it interrupts.
- Swap "Very Sexy Collection" hero for a bra-fit-focused hero shot (a real bra product on a real body, with a sizing CTA) since bras are my actual reason for being here.
- Put an actual number on the top banner — "20% Off Bras" beats "Deals Too Good to Miss" every time.
- Add a small Free Panty Wednesday or Semi-Annual Sale countdown strip since that's literally what pulls me back into the app on a schedule.
