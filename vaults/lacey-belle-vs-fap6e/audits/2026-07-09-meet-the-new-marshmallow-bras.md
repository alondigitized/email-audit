# Meet the New Marshmallow Bras 🥹
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday's "These New Marshmallow Bras Though 🤤" was PINK's cleanest single-product launch email in weeks — tight hero, one narrative, zero deal-stacking. Today's "Meet the New Marshmallow Bras" doubles down on the same product but buries the new-product moment under four stacked incentive layers: Free Shipping, Free Panty, 30% Off (shorts/bottoms), and a Free Pink Duffle. What was a confident product launch 24 hours ago now reads like a desperation bundle. The hero visual is still strong — model, pastel marshmallow colorway, clean crop — but the eye doesn't know where to land after it.
- This is also day-two of the same headline. A subscriber who opened yesterday's send will clock immediately that "Meet the New" is a second bite at "These New." The subject swap from punchy slang ("Though 🤤") to formal introduction ("Meet the") actually feels like a step backward — less personality, same product. The stacked offers aren't dishonest, but they undercut the luxury-adjacent positioning the Marshmallow Bra launch was setting up. You don't pile on a free duffle when you're trying to make a bra feel special.
- Judgment: solid execution on a strategically confused send. The creative team and the deals team appear to be pulling in different directions.

## What's working

- **Hero still earns attention** — pastel Marshmallow colorway pops immediately, model pose is approachable and on-brand.
- **Free Shipping banner is prominent** — top-of-email placement means even a quick scan captures the threshold benefit.
- **Product grid at the bottom** provides visual variety and multiple entry points for different sub-categories (bra styles, sets).
- **Subject line length** is clean at 31 chars — renders fully on mobile with room to spare.
- **Layout has no render bugs** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no Lorem Ipsum visible anywhere.

## What's weak

- **Four stacked offers dilute the new-product moment** — Free Panty + 30% Off + Free Duffle on top of Free Shipping turns a launch into a clearance pile.
- **Day-two fatigue** — "Meet the New Marshmallow Bras" subjects the same product as yesterday's "These New Marshmallow Bras Though 🤤"; engaged subscribers notice.
- **30% Off section is visually ambiguous** — from the render, it's unclear what category the discount applies to (shorts? activewear?), which weakens the CTA.
- **No urgency signal anywhere visible** — no "ends tonight," no date, no countdown. Multiple offers with no deadline = no reason to click now.
- **The Free Pink Duffle section competes for hero status** — a large black bag mid-email pulls focus away from the soft pastel bra story the top half was building.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop two of the four offers.** Keep Free Shipping (always) and one purchase incentive (Free Panty is the strongest brand-consistent choice). The Duffle and the 30% Off can live in a different send — probably tomorrow, when Marshmallow Bra click data tells you who engaged and who needs a nudge.
- 2. **Add a deadline to whatever offer remains.** Even "today only" or "this weekend" turns a passive scroll into a decision.
- 3. **Subject: lean into the continuation rather than pretending it's a fresh intro.** Yesterday's subject had voice. Today's sounds like a press release.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Marshmallow Bra is already selling out 🥹`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Grab it before it's gone — Marshmallow Bras`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free panty with your first pair. Today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New colors, new styles — shop the drop before it's gone.`
- 4. **Reorder the email body** so the 30% Off section comes *after* the Marshmallow Bra product detail, not alongside it. Right now the discount offer arrives before the customer has bonded with the product.
- 5. **Add a single social-proof signal** (star rating, "X sold this week") under the Marshmallow Bra hero. Yesterday's launch email had none; two days in, even a lightweight proof point lifts conversion.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — PINK "Meet the New Marshmallow Bras" (2026-07-09)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard table-based HTML4 email delivered via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (e1.victoriassecret.com). Multiple mixed-content HTTP assets, a duplicate open pixel, missing compliance headers, and 12+ images without alt text are the primary technical issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open pixel** — The open tracking pixel fires twice in the same message:
- Once at the top of `<body>`: `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?4TRS4WU5R3OEBPZBK46Y6VJHPE.70251&d=70251&bmt=0`
- A second time inside the AMPScript wrapper block

This inflates open-rate counts. One instance should be removed.

**19 click-tracking links skipped** — All click links route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects. UTM parameters on destination URLs cannot be verified from the truncated source; see Section 6.

**Dotomi retargeting pixels** — 10 HTTP pixel loads to `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1–10.php` plus one to `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`. All use `http://` (see Section 3). Dotomi/Criteo retargeting pixels this numerous in a single email are unusual and may cause render delays.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content — 12 HTTP image sources** confirmed by QA:
- `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` — MI open tracker
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...` — Dotomi UCM pixel
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`

Modern clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook.com) block HTTP assets or proxy them, breaking tracking. All should use `https://`.

**Alt text missing on 12+ images:**
- `o.gif` (MI tracker) — missing `alt=""`
- Dotomi UCM controller image — missing `alt=""`
- `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (Dotomi pixels) — all missing `alt=""`
- `bb0d0e58-6341-498f-a113-0b357d251787.gif` — missing `alt=""`
- `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` — missing `alt=""`

Tracking/pixel images should carry `alt=""` (empty, not absent) to satisfy accessibility requirements and prevent broken-image icons in image-blocked clients.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` disables pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

**Malformed CSS rule** — `min-width{width:auto!important;}` in the `<head>` `<style>` block has no valid selector prefix (missing `.`, `#`, or element name). This rule is likely dead code that has never applied; it should be removed.

**Empty `<title>` in `<body>`** — `<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>` is placed inside `<body>`, not `<head>`. The element is empty (no preview text content), making it a no-op placeholder that adds noise to the DOM.

**Template artifact left in source** — `` <!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` --> `` is a raw shell substitution comment that was never processed. It serves no function in the rendered email.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Empty AMPScript block** — The `<div id="AMPScript">` block contains only `<!-- Ampscript v0 START -->` / `<!-- Ampscript v0 END -->` with no logic between them. This is either a template placeholder with no active personalization, or a build step that failed to inject content. Confirm intentional.

**Hardcoded subscriber ID** in MI tracking pixel: `mi_u=200492586522`. If this should be a per-recipient merge token (`%%subscriberID%%` or equivalent), it is broken — every recipient's open event will be attributed to the same subscriber.

No unresolved merge token syntax (e.g., `%%` or `{{`) visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured** — QA relay did not observe `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers. Both are required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024) for senders at this volume tier. If headers are present upstream but stripped by the AgentMail relay, verify at the raw SMTP layer.

**Authentication-Results not captured** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. The sending domain `e2.vspink.com` (From header) differs from the ESP click domain `e1.victoriassecret.com`; confirm DKIM is signed under the correct `d=` alignment for the From domain.

**CAN-SPAM** — Unsubscribe link and physical address presence cannot be confirmed from truncated source, but are expected to be in the footer. No issues surfaced from available HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The campaign identifier in the MI pixel — `mi_ecmp=20260709_PK_5767_FILEBAL` — should map directly to UTM parameters on click destination URLs (e.g., `utm_campaign=20260709_PK_5767_FILEBAL`). This cannot be verified without resolving the `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects, which were skipped by QA.

**Action required:** Spot-check 2–3 CTA links by following the redirect chain manually to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are present and match the campaign code.
