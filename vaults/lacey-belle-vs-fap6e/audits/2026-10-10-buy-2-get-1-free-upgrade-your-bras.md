# Buy 2, Get 1 FREE—Upgrade Your Bras
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a sale email with one strong hero and then too many offers. The "Buy 2, Get 1 Free" headline sits over a model in a pale bra, with one "SHOP NOW" button, and it matches the subject line. It is nearly the same offer, creative and button as yesterday's "Our #1 Bras are Buy 2, Get 1 FREE" (10/9), so a subscriber who opened that one will see this as a repeat.
- Below the hero, the email stops being about bras. It adds "Fall's Best Bras" with a lace-bodysuit model, a "no-wire" support block, a "LIMITED TIME" 5/$45 Luxe Panties panel, and a "Buy 2, Get 3 FREE" Mists & Lotions panel. Then come the category tile grid, a Rewards and $25-off card, a "Now Trending" product row and a long wall of fine print. Each section is clean, but together they split attention across four offers. The subject promises bras and only the top third delivers.
- Verdict: good on-brand execution, weak focus and weak freshness. It would score higher if it dropped the panty and fragrance panels and sent a different bra angle than 10/9.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "Buy 2, Get 1 Free", is large and readable, and it matches the subject.
- The subhead copy ("a neutral for every day, sexy shine for nights out, and a bold color for everything else") gives a reason to buy three bras.
- Every content block has its own clear "SHOP NOW" button, so there is never a hunt for the next step.
- The "LIMITED TIME" tags on the panty and fragrance panels add some urgency.
- The rewards and $25-off-first-purchase card gives members and new sign-ups a reason to act.

## What's weak

- Four offers compete: B2G1 bras, 5/$45 panties, B2G3 mists and lotions, and a free-panty app promo in the top banner. The hero stops being the single thing to act on.
- Every button says "SHOP NOW". None names a product or benefit, and there is no deep link to a specific bra.
- The disclaimer block at the bottom is very long. The offer exclusions (PINK, third-party styles, clearance) are small print under the hero, which makes the offer feel less open than the headline.
- This is the same offer as the 10/9 send, the same B2G1 push after a week of 8/$38 panty sends. Fatigue is likely.
- The mists and lotions panel and the trending row pull away from the bra story the subject set up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story: bras. Keep the hero and the support block, and remove the panty and fragrance panels, or move them below a clear divider.
- 2. Make the hero specific. Name a bra ("Body by Victoria") and a benefit on the button, not just "SHOP NOW".
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday", to the hero, since only the secondary panels carry "LIMITED TIME".
- 4. Move the offer exclusions up to one short line right under the hero and cut the footer legal text where possible.
- 5. Don't repeat 10/9. Use a new angle such as fit, a bestseller or a size-range message.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Buy 2, Get 1 FREE—Upgrade Your Bras"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a legacy table-based ExactTarget/SFMC template (HTML 4.01 doctype) with no hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 3 warnings). It has several real code-level defects: ten insecure third-party pixels with no alt text, a duplicated open pixel, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and invalid CSS selectors. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Duplicate open pixel.** The same URL appears twice, as two 1x1 images: `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?CADK6CWTRN6UFEQPPNOL475IIE.70268&d=70268&bmt=0`. One is at the top of `<body>` and one is inside the `stylingblock-content-wrapper`. Opens may be double-counted.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** Ten `http://` Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) cookie-sync images, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`. These are non-HTTPS, so clients that block mixed content will drop them. They also add 11 third-party requests per open.
- **Unverified links.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 43 tracking/redirect links (click.e1.victoriassecret.com). Destination validity and redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **Template residue.** The HTML comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` is an unevaluated shell-style snippet. It is harmless to render but shows template debris in the delivered source.
- **Empty AMPscript block.** `<div id="AMPScript">` contains only empty "Ampscript v0 START/END" comments, so no logic is rendering here.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.** In the first `<style>` block, `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a nonexistent element. Both rules are dead. The second block has a correct `.ReadMsgBody`.
- **`<title>` inside `<body>`.** `<title style="display: none; …">` is invalid placement. There is no `<title>` in `<head>`.
- **Missing alt text.** QA flags 12 images without alt:
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` is a hosted content image, so this is a real accessibility gap.
  - `https://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/rp/a076cc7f15d20566.png?mi_u=…` is a tracking pixel.
  - The Dotomi pixels are also in the 12.
  - Pixels should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden`.
- **Inconsistent table roles.** Some wrapper tables have `role="presentation"`, but the main layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties`) do not. Screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Mobile CSS.** The `max-width:480px` and `max-width:640px` queries are present. `img { max-height:auto !important }` is invalid, since `auto` is not a valid `max-height` value, but it is harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appear. The tracking IDs (`mi_u=200492586522`, `CADK6CWTRN…70268`) resolved. The unresolved `date` comment (section 2) is not a visible merge token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The QA tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again due to the relay. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Footer not checked.** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID.** The campaign ID `20261010_VS_6283_FILEBALANCE` appears consistently in the `mi_ecmp` and `dtmc_email_category` parameters. This is a positive for attribution.
- **UTM parameters unverified.** No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source, and click links route through redirectors that were not followed. Landing page alignment with the "Buy 2, Get 1 FREE" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the duplicate open pixel, keeping one.
2. Serve or drop the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS. If they must stay, add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the content PNG `5bcec3ac-…png`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Fix or delete the broken CSS rules (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`, `max-height:auto`). Move `<title>` into `<head>`. Strip the leftover `date` comment from the template.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables.
7. For the next QA pass, capture raw headers (bypassing the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and the link UTMs, and follow the redirects on a sample of the 43 tracking links.
