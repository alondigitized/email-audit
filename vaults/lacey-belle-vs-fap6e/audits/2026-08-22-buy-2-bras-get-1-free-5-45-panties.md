# Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 FREE + 5/$45 Panties
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-branded execution of "The Bra Event" — Buy 2, Get 1 Free leads immediately with a clear black CTA button, no scrolling needed to see the offer. It's a step up in clarity from this week's run: unlike 8/21's "Inside: Your (New) Favorite Bra" (a five-way category sorter dressed as a hero bra) or 8/17's mismatched "Need a Lift?" send, this one's subject and hero actually agree — Buy 2 Get 1 Free is the first thing you see, full stop.
- Where it falls down is pacing and cadence. After the Bra Event hero, the email sprawls through four more push-up bra product shots ("Perfect Push-Ups," "Very Sexy," "Dream Angels," "Very Sexy Bombshell") before finally introducing the panties offer from the subject line — which doesn't appear until roughly a third of the way down. For a subject that promises two offers (bras AND panties), the panties deal is buried under a wall of near-identical red lingerie photography. This is the same kitchen-sink template this brand has run all week (per history, 8/17 through 8/21), just with a stronger opening hook this time.
- Net: strong hero, weak follow-through, and a subscriber who's seen five of these in six days is going to recognize the shape immediately.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "Buy 2, Get 1 Free" with a bold black "SHOP NOW" button, no bait-and-switch.
- Clean visual hierarchy at the top: brand nameplate → campaign title → offer → CTA, in that order.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlapping elements.
- Model imagery is on-brand and consistent with the persona (young adult women, red lingerie campaign).
- The $5/$45 panties section further down has its own clear CTA ("SHOP NOW") and a legible offer.

## What's weak

- Four consecutive near-duplicate push-up bra sections between the hero and the panties offer create fatigue — "The Perfect Push-Ups," "Very Sexy Shine Snap Push Up," "Dream Angels Push Up," "Very Sexy Bombshell Push Up" all show the same red-toned solo model shots.
- The panties offer from the subject line doesn't appear until deep in the scroll, undermining the two-offer subject promise.
- This is the same kitchen-sink long-scroll template the brand has run since 8/17 with only the top offer swapped — a subscriber who opened yesterday's bra email will recognize this instantly.
- The $5 Mists & Lotions section is "2 days only, in-store exclusive" — a third offer competing for attention in an already-dense email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the four repetitive push-up bra sections down to one or two — they add scroll length without adding information, and they're pushing the second half of the subject's promise (panties) too far down.
- 2. Move the 5/$45 panties block up to directly follow the bra hero, since the subject sold both offers as co-equal — right now it reads like an afterthought.
- 3. Differentiate the push-up sections visually (different color background, different framing) if they must all stay, so it doesn't read as one long red blur.
- 4. Consider dropping or relocating the in-store-only Mists & Lotions block — it's a third, unrelated offer diluting a two-offer subject line.
