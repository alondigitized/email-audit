## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-generated (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) send with heavy third-party tracking and several markup defects. Its automated QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified. This includes the open pixel at `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…`.
- **Ten plain-HTTP cookie-sync pixels.** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` all use `http://`, as does `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?…`. Each carries the SHA-256 email hash (`a4edea106b33…`=1) in the URL. This is a deliverability and privacy exposure, and clients that block mixed content will drop them.
- **Plain-HTTP open beacon.** `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261104_PK_6412_TRPFULL` is also non-HTTPS.
- **Identifiers in the structured-data URLs.** The JSON-LD `PromotionCard` URLs contain `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` (a 64-character hash). Both are subscriber-level identifiers inside a `<script>` block.
- **Redundant structured data.** All three `PromotionCard` entries point to the same URL, `/us/pink/new-arrivals?…`, so they add no value.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the JSON-LD `<script>` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will hoist it into `<body>` and may drop the preceding head content. Gmail and some other clients also strip `<script>`, so the JSON-LD is only useful where it is honoured.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`, so it matches nothing.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is a bare element selector and also matches nothing.
  - `max-height:auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - The content image `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`.
  - Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `pub1–10.php`, the UCMController image) have no `alt`. Several are 1×1, so the impact is low, but a missing `alt` is still flagged. Empty `alt=""` would be correct for the decorative ones.
- **Global `table{max-width:600px!important}`.** This applies to every table, including nested ones, and may clip layout in Outlook. It is also relevant to the `<!--[if mso]>` fallback, which sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The personalization values (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`) are all populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified, and QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link not verifiable.** They are in the truncated footer.
- **Domain mismatch (observation).** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while tracking, images and the click domain are on `e1.victoriassecret.com`. That is not a failure by itself, but brand-domain alignment should be confirmed in the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Non-UTM campaign tagging.** Links use `cm_mmc=di-_-110426425-_-VSD_VSL_20261104_PK_6412_TRPFULL-_`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp` and `mi_u`, with no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Attribution depends on the site's Coremetrics/Dotomi/mi_* tagging. Tools that expect UTMs will report this traffic as direct or unattributed.
- **Landing page alignment unverified.** The subject advertises "$20 Tees & $35 Flares", but the only visible destination, in the JSON-LD, is the generic `/us/pink/new-arrivals`. The body CTAs could not be checked because the source is truncated and the tracking links were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all tracking pixels over HTTPS. Switch `ads.dotomi.com`, `login.dotomi.com` and `mi.victoriassecret.com` to `https://`, or drop the ten `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels.
2. Move the JSON-LD block out of `<head>`/the wrapper table and into a valid `<head>` or `<body>`. Remove `customerID` and `dtm_email_hash` from the structured-data URLs. Deduplicate the three identical entries or point each at its actual destination.
3. Fix the CSS: `.ReadMsgBody`, remove the bare `min-width{}` rule, and drop `max-height:auto`.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated message and raw headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and would resolve the footer and body-link checks.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters alongside the `cm_mmc` tagging, and verify that the CTA destinations match the "$20 Tees / $35 Flares" offer.
