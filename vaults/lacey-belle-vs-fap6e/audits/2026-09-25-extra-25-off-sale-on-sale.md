# Extra 25% Off—Sale on Sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the sitewide "kitchen sink" sale template again — same torn-paper "SALE" hero, same red block, same category grid that's shown up repeatedly in the brand history (2026-09-22, 2026-09-18). The stacked layer of offers is the story here: "Up to 50% Off + Extra 25% Off Sale," then a panties callout, then a new Indulgent Crème launch, then $8 mists/lotions, then a nine-tile category grid, then rewards/SMS/credit-card blocks. It's comprehensive but exhausting — there's no single hero moment, just offer after offer stacked vertically with equal visual weight.
- The subject promises "Extra 25% Off—Sale on Sale," and the hero does deliver that exact stacked-discount math up top, which is a point in its favor over the 2026-09-20 bait-and-switch send. But by the time you scroll past the new Crème launch and the mists/lotions deal, the "sale on sale" thread has been diluted by two unrelated pushes. Serviceable but unfocused — a 6.

## What's working

- Hero immediately delivers the subject's math: "Up to 50% Off + Extra 25% Off Sale*" in bold red/white, matching the "sale on sale" premise.
- Clear black "SHOP NOW" buttons repeated after each offer block, so the CTA is never ambiguous.
- "FINAL DAYS" and the footer terms ("valid through 9/28... while supplies last") give the urgency a specific, credible deadline.
- The panty flat-lay art (8/$25 or $4.99 each) is clean, well-lit, and product-specific — no render bugs.
- Category grid at the bottom is well organized and gives multiple entry points for browsing.

## What's weak

- Four separate offers (sale+25%, panties, new Crème, $8 mists) compete for attention with no visual hierarchy prioritizing the headline "extra 25%" promise — everything after the hero reads like a different email got glued on.
- The Indulgent Crème launch and the $8 mists/lotions deal have nothing to do with "Sale on Sale" and break the narrative the subject line set up.
- This is visually near-identical to the 2026-09-22 "Up to 50% Off Sale" send — same torn-paper SALE treatment, same red field, same grid — so a frequent subscriber sees repetition, not a fresh moment.
- No mention of where or how the extra 25% applies (which categories, cart threshold) until the dense legal block at the very bottom — that's a lot of fine print for a "no code required" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two blocks: the sale-on-sale hero + panties offer (both on-theme), and move the Crème launch and mists/lotions deal to a separate send — this single change would sharpen the whole email around its own subject line.
- 2. Add a visible "no code needed, ends 9/28" line directly under the hero CTA instead of burying the exclusions in footer legal text.
- 3. Differentiate the hero art from the 2026-09-22 send — vary the torn-paper treatment or swap in a model shot to break the repeated-creative fatigue.
- 4. Move the rewards/SMS/credit-card acquisition blocks below the category grid (they already are) but tighten them into one row instead of three stacked panels — they're currently adding length without adding urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Final days: stack an extra 25% off already-reduced styles.`
