# 7/$35 Panties Starts *NOW*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the opener for a new panty deal, and the top of the email does the job. The "Panty Party" script, a big **7/$35**, a "LIMITED TIME" tag, an XS–XXL size row and a SHOP ALL button all sit in the first screen. The offer is clear, and it matches the subject.
- After that, the email drifts away from the deal. Below the panty hero it becomes a long catalog: "From the Runway to Your Vanity" fragrance (Bombshell, Base, Tease), a "Bras from $25" block, "The Shine Shop," a category tile grid, a Rewards module and a "Now Trending" row. The panty offer never comes back. A reader who wanted the deal gets one button near the top, then a lot of scrolling.
- Cadence is a problem. The brand ran 10/$31 on 10/31 ("No Tricks—10/$31 Panties Ends Tonight"). The next send pushed a different panty price, and 7/$35 is a worse per-unit price than 10/$31. Subscribers have been pulled through panty deals, BOGO fragrance and bras all week. Judgement: strong opening, diluted body, and a deal that feels less exciting than last week's.

## What's working

- The "7/$35" is large and easy to spot, with a script "Panty Party" header, so the offer reads in about a second.
- The XS–XXL size row near the top tells shoppers their size is in stock before they click.
- Subject and hero match. "7/$35 Panties" is exactly what the top module sells.
- The "LIMITED TIME" tag adds urgency without extra shouting.
- A Rewards/member module appears near the bottom, so loyalty benefits are visible.

## What's weak

- Only one panty CTA appears, near the top, and the rest of the email is fragrance, bras and general categories. The offer is not repeated.
- The "FROM THE RUNWAY TO YOUR VANITY" fragrance section takes the largest share of the scroll, so the email looks like a brand catalog, not a panty event.
- The deal has no specific end date, only "LIMITED TIME," which weakens urgency after the "Ends Tonight" sends of 10/31.
- "SHOP ALL" is generic copy, and the 7/$35 price is worse per unit than the 10/$31 deal from days earlier.
- The email is very long (about 12,000px tall), with many competing modules.

- 1. Cut the length and keep the panty deal as the spine. Repeat the 7/$35 button after the first product grid and again near the end, and move the fragrance and bra blocks below it.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") and show a few specific panty styles with prices, so the click goes to a product.
- 3. Change the CTA from "SHOP ALL" to "SHOP 7/$35 PANTIES."
- 4. Add a short line that frames the value against the 10/$31 sale, or hold the send until the offer is stronger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, AMPScript wrapper) that renders structurally but has markup defects, a viewport setting that blocks zoom, and about 11 Dotomi third-party pixels loaded over plain `http://`. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the relay, and the HTML source was truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **51 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations are validated.
- **Two open pixels fire.** There is one at the top of the body (`open.aspx?qs=ABl7ImQiOjUwNTAs…CNWlKwj1nU5…`) and a second inside the content wrapper (`…CNWlKwj2oe9K…`). Both are 1x1 with `alt=""`. The duplicate inflates open counts and adds a request.
- **Third-party Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels over `http://`.**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…&dtmc_email_category=20261104_VS_6433_FILEBAL`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These are cookie-sync calls to an ad network and are not first-party analytics. Clients that proxy or block insecure content will drop them, and the `dtmc_email_category` parameter appears to carry campaign metadata.
- **Leaked template comment.** `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` is left in the markup and exposes build tooling, which is minor.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text on 11 images.**
  - All 10 `pub*.php` pixels and the `UCMController` image have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` also has none. This one is a content image over HTTPS, so it is the only one a user could actually miss.
- **Mobile padding rule never applies.** The markup uses `class="content_padding"` (underscore), but the CSS defines `.content-padding` (hyphen), so the `padding:4px !important` mobile override never matches.
- **Invalid CSS selectors in the first `<style>` block.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{…}` has no leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element instead of `.ReadMsgBody`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid rule. It is dead code that targets a nonexistent `<min-width>` element.
- **Invalid `<title>` placement.** `<title style="display:none…">` sits inside a table cell in the body, and the `<head>` has no `<title>`.
- **Old doctype.** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is used with `role="presentation"` tables, which is inconsistent but not breaking.
- **Global `table{max-width:600px!important}`.** It can clip nested full-width tables in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[%…%]` tokens appear in the visible source. The AMPScript block is empty comments only (`<!-- Ampscript v0 START/END -->`). The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header.** QA reports it not found. This may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058).** The same relay caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` and the click domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` do align.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address fall in the truncated HTML, so they were not verified.
- **Privacy exposure.** The ten Dotomi cookie-sync calls are third-party tracking, and they run over an insecure channel.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 51 links are wrapped in `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects that were not resolved, so the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown. The only campaign identifier visible is `dtmc_email_category=20261104_VS_6433_FILEBAL`, which sits in a third-party pixel and not on a site link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `lang`, a proper `<title>` in `<head>`, and an `alt` on `5bcec3ac-….png`. Give the tracking pixels `alt=""`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the class mismatch (`content_padding` vs `.content-padding`) and the invalid selectors `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width`.
4. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them. Remove the duplicate open pixel.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message at the source, bypassing the relay, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC in the original headers.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML, and resolve the 51 redirect links to check landing pages and UTM parameters.
