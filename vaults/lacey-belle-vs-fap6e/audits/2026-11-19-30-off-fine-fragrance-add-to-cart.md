# 30% Off Fine Fragrance? ADD. TO. CART.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the fragrance-and-beauty-branded cut of the same 30% Off Everything campaign VS has sent all week. The hero leads with a pink gift-set image and "30% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets". It has two buttons, Shop Fine Fragrances and Shop Beauty Gift Sets, so the subject and the first screen match. That matches the subject, and it's the email's best moment.
- After the hero it turns into a kitchen sink. A second "30% Off Everything" block with five category buttons follows, then 7/$35 Panty Party, 5/$40 Mists & Lotions, a free quilted duffle with a $75 purchase, a "30% Off All Your Favorites" grid, a Rewards card, and a trending section. The fragrance message gets buried, and the "Beauty" sender is selling lingerie for most of the scroll. The footer carries several dense legal blocks, so the "No code required" line sits above a lot of fine print.
- This is also the latest near-duplicate in a run: "30% Off Everything Starts NOW" (11/18) and "30% Off is Happening NOW—Don't Miss Out" (11/19) came before it. The offer is clear and the brand is trusted, but the send is fatiguing and over-stuffed. Fine for a Black Friday-week blast, not a sharp one. (The render was heavily downscaled, so I'm reading layout and headline copy, not small print.)

## What's working

- The hero offer, "30% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets," matches the subject and is visible immediately.
- The offer is clear: "LIMITED TIME" sits over big script numerals. The "No code required" line removes a friction point.
- The "Shop Now to Beat the Rush" top banner adds seasonal urgency, and the gift-set imagery fits the holiday timing.
- The Rewards and "$25 Off Your First Purchase" block gives member and new-customer value.
- Layout is clean, with no visible broken images or overlapping text, and the pink palette is on-brand.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers (30% off fragrance, 30% off everything, 7/$35, 5/$40, free duffle) dilute the fragrance hero.
- The second "30% Off Everything" block repeats the hero's message with a lingerie-heavy model image, which makes the email feel redundant.
- The long scroll puts the Rewards card and trending products far below the fold.
- The "LIMITED TIME" label gives no end date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Heavy legal text stacked at the bottom, plus the duffle code and $75 threshold, makes the offer feel less clean.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a fragrance-only send: hero, 3 to 4 named fragrances or gift sets with prices, and one Rewards line. A Beauty-branded sender should deliver Beauty.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM ET".
- 3. Link individual products, such as a named best-selling fragrance with its price and gift-set contents, instead of only category buttons.
- 4. Drop the duplicate "30% Off Everything" block, or move it below the fragrance content as a single line.
- 5. Add a social-proof element, such as a star rating or "Best Seller" badges, on the fragrance products.
- 6. Soften the all-caps, punctuated subject so it reads as a confident offer instead of a spam signal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off fine fragrance, no code needed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gift sets + fine fragrance: 30% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gift sets, mists and best-sellers, all 30% off. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Plus 5/$40 Mists & Lotions while they last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ESP build (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, with Dotomi/Conversant retargeting). The source has invalid head markup, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and ten non-HTTPS third-party pixels that pass a hashed email address. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 64 tracking links on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their redirect targets and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 Dotomi cookie-sync images load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?a4edea106b33…=1` through `pub10.php`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, and they expose requests to interception.
- **Hashed email in URLs:** The `pub*.php` query strings embed the same 64-character hash that appears as `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` in the JSON-LD URL. The JSON-LD URL also carries `customerID=43cece97…`. Hashed identifiers are being passed to a third-party ad domain, and a hash of an email address is still personal data.
- **Open pixel:** The 1x1 pixel at `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is correctly formed, with `alt=""` and width/height set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Tables are not allowed in `<head>`, so parsers close the head and open the body at that point. The viewport meta and both `<style>` blocks then end up in `<body>`. The second `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` is also affected, which risks charset sniffing problems.
- **Zoom disabled:** The viewport meta contains `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing alt text:** The QA run flagged two content images:
  - `…/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg`
  - `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
  
  The ten Dotomi pixels also lack alt, but they are invisible tracking images.
- **Dead CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets an element rather than the class.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **Layout tables:** The outer layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. Only the `stylingblock` tables set it.
- **Empty title:** A `<title style="display:none…"></title>` appears in the body.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with self-closing `<meta … />` syntax. This is harmless but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear. Because the HTML is truncated, the check is limited to the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed and should not be treated as a sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact. The From domain, `e1.victoriassecret.com`, aligns with the click and image domains.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and postal address are outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** The one visible destination, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/beauty`, uses the retailer's own scheme instead of `utm_*`:
  - `cm_mmc=di-_-111926535-_-VSD_VSL_20261119_VSB_6501_FILEBAL`
  - `d_cellid=111926535`
  - `mi_ecmp=20261119_VSB_6501_FILEBAL`
  - `mi_u=200492586522`
- **Campaign code:** `20261119_VSB_6501_FILEBAL` is consistent across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and Dotomi's `dtmc_email_category`.
- **Missing `utm_*`:** There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters. Analytics tools that expect UTMs will not attribute this traffic unless the sites map the vendor parameters.
- **Landing page:** The URL points to the general `/beauty` category rather than a fragrance page. I could not check whether the page shows the 30% offer, because the destination was not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block out of `<head>` into `<body>`, or use a bare `<script>` in the head. Do not wrap it in a table.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the two flagged content images. Add `alt=""` to the pixels.
4. Switch all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them. Review whether hashed email and `customerID` values should go to third-party ad endpoints and the JSON-LD URL.
5. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector and remove the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables and remove the empty `<title>` from the body.
7. For the QA pipeline:
   - Capture the full raw message with headers, so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified.
   - Stop truncating the HTML, so the footer can be checked.
   - Resolve the click-tracking redirects for at least the hero CTA.
