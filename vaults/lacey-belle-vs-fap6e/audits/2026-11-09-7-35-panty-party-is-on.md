## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is well-formed on campaign tagging: `20261109_PK_6458_FILEBAL` is consistent across `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp`. Its main problems are about 12 non-HTTPS third-party tracking images with no alt text, a few invalid markup patterns, and header checks (unsubscribe, authentication) that couldn't be confirmed. Automated QA scored 57% pass, with 0 failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking links on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` were skipped, so destination status and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** These images load over `http://` and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261109_PK_6458_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** `pub1.php`–`pub10.php` are ten separate Dotomi requests. Each one carries the hashed email (`a4edea106b33…=1`). This adds load and third-party exposure of an identifier.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is HTTPS, 1x1, and has `alt=""`, which is appropriate.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `o.gif`, `UCMController…`, and `pub1.php`–`pub10.php` are tracking pixels with no `alt` attribute at all. Adding `alt=""` would mark them decorative.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` is a content-hosted image, so it likely needs real alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid structure:**
  - A `<table>` containing `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>` before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will relocate it.
  - An empty `<title style="display:none…">` sits inside a body table.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its `.` prefix and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` isn't a valid selector. Both are ignored.
- **Content wrapper:** `table{max-width:600px!important}` applies to every table. This is risky for nested layouts, but I can't confirm a failure from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The source was truncated, so the rest isn't covered.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both missing. The QA output itself says this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so it's unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, but the DKIM signing domain couldn't be checked.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address aren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.
- **Privacy:** Hashed identifiers (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`) are placed in URL query strings. This is not a CAN-SPAM issue, but it matters for forwarded or shared links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Landing URL:** `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/underwear/underwear-offer` is consistent with the "Panty Party" subject.
- **Parameters:** The email uses platform-specific parameters (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp`) and no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent internally, but standard analytics tools won't attribute it unless mapped.
- **Landing page:** I couldn't verify the page because the redirect probe was skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all tracking pixels over HTTPS, or drop the `ads.dotomi.com` `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync pixels.
2. Add `alt=""` to decorative pixels and meaningful alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
4. Move the JSON-LD block and the table out of `<head>`, remove the empty `<title>` from the body, and fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors.
5. Re-check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against raw headers from a direct-to-mailbox send, since the relay may be stripping them.
