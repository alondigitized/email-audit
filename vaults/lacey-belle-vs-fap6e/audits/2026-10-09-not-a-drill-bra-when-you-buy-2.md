# Not a Drill: 🆓 Bra When You Buy 2
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is yesterday's send with a new subject line. On 10-08 PINK ran "Get a ✨FREE BRA✨ When You Buy 2" with the hero "THE BRA EVENT / BUY 2, GET 1 FREE." Today's hero is the same: a model in a pink lace bra, "THE BRA EVENT," "LIMITED TIME," and "BUY 2, GET 1 FREE." A subscriber who saw yesterday's email has no reason to open this one. "Not a Drill" adds energy but no new information.
- The hero is clean and the offer is easy to read. Below it, though, the email turns into a long catalog. It has a $15 tees block, a velour pack, $10 full-size beauty, a holiday gift guide, a Logo Shop tile, a bestsellers grid and a rewards banner. The bra offer owns the top screen and then gets buried under seven other messages. It reads like a store circular, not a campaign.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send that repeats itself. It will perform about as well as yesterday's and will add to fatigue after nine days of near-daily promos.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. The pink bra shot sits over "THE BRA EVENT" and "BUY 2, GET 1 FREE" in large type, so the offer lands in a second.
- A single "SHOP NOW" button directly under the hero gives an unambiguous primary action.
- The "STEAL THE DEAL / $15 TEES" block uses a bold, high-contrast headline and a three-model strip, so it works as a second price anchor.
- The imagery fits the PINK demographic: young, casual, on-trend, with consistent brand colors.
- The rewards banner and the "$25 off" offer near the footer give loyalty members something to act on.

## What's weak

- The email repeats yesterday's offer and hero with no new reason to act. The 10-08 send already announced the free bra.
- "LIMITED TIME" has no date or deadline, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Too many competing sections follow the hero: tees, velour, beauty, gift guide, logo shop, bestsellers. The offer loses focus, and the email is very long.
- The fine-print block is small and dense, with exclusions and "lowest priced items are free" terms. The offer looks simpler than it is.
- The CTAs are generic ("SHOP NOW," "MORE & MORE VELOUR"). None names a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a deadline in the hero ("Ends Sunday") and carry it into the subject. This is the single biggest lift, because the offer is otherwise unchanged from yesterday.
- 2. Cut the body to the bra offer plus one secondary offer (the $15 tees). Move the beauty, gift guide and logo shop blocks to a different send.
- 3. Show specific bras (the Wear Everywhere and Push-Up styles, with names and prices) instead of one lifestyle shot. Add a "Shop Bras" button next to the hero image.
- 4. Pull the key terms ("lowest-priced item free, select styles") up near the hero in plain language.
- 5. Don't send the same offer on consecutive days. Add a new angle each time, such as a bestseller review or a size/fit tip.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lowest-priced bra is free when you buy 2. Plus $15 tees through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML has real structural problems. Markup is invalid: a `<table>` and `<script>` sit inside `<head>`. Ten or more tracking pixels load over plain `http://`, and several images have no alt text. The sending authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be checked because the AgentMail relay did not capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 49 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they point to click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified.
- Ten third-party Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php`. Each URL carries the same 64-character hash (`a4edea106b33...afc6fe`).
- Another `http://` pixel loads from `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`.
- An `http://` open-tracking pixel loads from `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261009_PK_6272_FILEBALANCE`.
- Clients that block or upgrade non-HTTPS content may fail to load these pixels. That would undercount opens and drop the cookie sync.
- The `http://` pixels and the 49 skipped links are the only link and tracking findings in this data. The three `ld+json` `url` values are identical (`/us/pink/bras?cm_mmc=...`) and show no other defect in the truncated source.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Invalid document structure.** The `<head>` contains `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and `<script type="application/ld+json">`. Per the HTML spec, a table in `<head>` makes parsers close the head early. The `<meta>` and `<style>` tags that follow, including the viewport meta and the responsive `@media` rules, may then be treated as body content or dropped by some clients. Scripts are stripped by most email clients, so the `ld+json` block only works in Gmail's schema-markup flow.
- **Broken CSS selector.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` has no leading `.` or `#`, so it matches nothing. The same applies to `min-width{width:auto!important;}`, which is not a valid selector for anything.
- **Missing alt text** on these images:
  - `o.gif` (the `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixel)
  - the `login.dotomi.com` UCMController image
  - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, a hosted content image at `image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/`
- The tracking pixels are not visible to users, so the missing alt text matters only for the content PNG. The 1x1 pixels should still carry `alt=""` for consistency. The first pixel in the source already does (`open.aspx` with `alt=""`).
- **Doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email, and no rendering problem is confirmed from it.
- **Possible mobile issue.** The CSS has a global `table{max-width:600px!important;}`. The `.container` class overrides to 100% on small screens, but only if the head parsing problem above doesn't discard the `<style>` blocks.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source.
- Personal identifiers are embedded in URLs: `dtm_email_hash=4edea106b333...`, `customerID=43cece97e6d8...`, and `mi_u=200492586522`. They are hashed, which is appropriate, but they appear in URLs and in the `ld+json` block. They are also sent to third-party domains (`ads.dotomi.com`) in the cookie-sync URLs. Whether that is acceptable is a privacy and compliance question for the sender, not a code defect.
- The source is truncated, so token checks cover only the visible portion.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA warning). The message may lack it, or the AgentMail relay may have stripped it. This can't be confirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA warning). One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (QA warning). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool notes this is expected via the relay.
- The sender domain `e2.vspink.com` differs from the image and click domains (`e1.victoriassecret.com`). That is normal for a brand's multi-domain setup, but DKIM and SPF alignment can't be verified here.
- The footer, with physical address and unsubscribe link, falls outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements.
- Treat the three header warnings as inconclusive, not as confirmed failures. The QA tool itself says the relay may not capture them.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The links use the retailer's own tracking scheme, not UTM: `cm_mmc=di-_-100926425-_-VSD_VSL_20261009_PK_6272_FILEBALANCE-_&d_cellid=100926425`, plus `mi_ecmp=20261009_PK_6272_FILEBALANCE`. `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` are absent from the `ld+json` URLs. This is not a defect if their analytics keys on `cm_mmc`.
- Campaign naming is consistent across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category` (`20261009_PK_6272_FILEBALA...`, truncated in the QA output).
- All three `PromotionCard` entries point to the same landing page, `/us/pink/bras`. The subject promises a free bra with a purchase of two. The page can't be checked against that offer because the link probe was skipped.
- The subject uses an emoji (🆓) and the preheader is not visible in the truncated source. Nothing technical is wrong there.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Move the `<table>` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. This protects the viewport meta and the responsive styles.
2. Serve all tracking pixels (`mi.victoriassecret.com/.../o.gif`, `login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/.../pub1-10.php`) over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to each.
3. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Fix the CSS selectors `ReadMsgBody` (should be `.ReadMsgBody`) and remove `min-width{...}`.
5. Check the raw headers outside AgentMail for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e2.vspink.com`. Our QA flags these as unknown, not failed.
6. Do a manual click-through of the redirect links to confirm the offer matches the `/us/pink/bras` landing page.
7. Consider reducing the 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels. They are a deliverability and privacy cost, and they widen the exposure of the hashed email identifier to third parties.
