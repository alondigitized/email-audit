# 30% Off Everything—Don't Miss Out
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the same kitchen-sink Black Friday-week send VS has run all week, with a new subject line on top. The hero is clear: "LIMITED TIME 30% Off Everything" on a pink banner with models in plaid and holiday pajamas, and a "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH" line above it. Six category pills sit under the headline. It's easy to read, but it doesn't differ from the 11/18 "30% Off Everything Starts NOW" and 11/19 "30% Off is Happening NOW—Don't Miss Out" sends.
- Below the hero the email stacks five more modules: 30% off bras, a 7/$35 Panty Party, $49.95 CozyFlannel pajama sets with $29.95 slippers, 30% off fine fragrances and gift sets, and a free quilted duffle with a $125+ purchase. Each module has its own "LIMITED TIME" tag. By the fourth one the tag means nothing, and the page runs very long. This is the third VS send in two days with the same structure (11/20 had two), so a subscriber sees it as wallpaper. The subject is a near-copy of 11/19's "Don't Miss Out" line.
- My judgement is that it's solid, brand-consistent promo execution with no creative fatigue management. It would convert if it were the first one a subscriber saw this week, but it isn't.
- I viewed the render at a heavily reduced size, so fine print and small text were not legible. My read is based on layout, hero copy and offer structure.

## What's working

- The "30% Off Everything" headline is large, with a clear "LIMITED TIME" tag, so the offer lands in a second.
- The category pills (Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear) give quick routes into the sale.
- Modules have a consistent pink visual system, and the product photography is strong, especially the gift-set wreath and the duffle.
- Specific price points ($49.95 pajama sets, $29.95 slippers, 7/$35 panties) give shoppers concrete value beyond the percent-off.
- The footer has a rewards block ("$25 Off") and a "Now Trending" row, which add member value.

## What's weak

- Five or six offers compete in one email: the 30% sitewide deal, bras, a panty bundle, pajamas, fragrance and a free duffle. No single one gets the focus.
- Every module carries "LIMITED TIME", which dilutes the urgency.
- The email is extremely long, and the hero CTA is a grid of category pills with no single dominant button.
- It repeats the same creative and structure as the 11/18, 11/19 and 11/20 sends.
- The duffle offer has a $125+ threshold that is buried beneath the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one secondary module and a footer. Move the rest to a separate send or dynamic content so the sale isn't the same wall of modules each day.
- 2. Make the hero button one big "Shop 30% Off" instead of six pills, and keep the pills lower down as navigation.
- 3. Rotate the creative. Day 4 of the same campaign needs a new hook, such as a "last chance" countdown, a gift angle, or a best-sellers edit.
- 4. Use the "LIMITED TIME" tag once. Give the other modules plain descriptors.
- 5. Put the duffle threshold ("with $125+ purchase") next to the headline in the same size.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% Off Ends Soon, Bras to Beauty`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free quilted duffle with $125+, plus 7/$35 panties and $49.95 pajama sets.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sitewide savings on sleep, bras and fragrance gifts. Ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has invalid document structure (a `<table>` and `<script>` inside `<head>`) and sends a hashed email address to third-party pixels over plain HTTP. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits, so they are inconclusive rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **65 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so destination validity is unverified.
- **Ten Dotomi/Conversant cookie-sync pixels use `http://`.** These are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`.
  - Clients may block them, and they are a mixed-content risk.
  - Each carries the hashed email as a query key: `a4edea106b33...afc6fe=1`. The same `dtm_email_hash=4edea106...` appears in the JSON-LD URL. Sending it unencrypted to an ad-tech domain is a privacy exposure.
- **The open-tracking pixel is fine.** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is HTTPS, 1x1, with `alt=""`.
- **The JSON-LD block has an extra identifier.** The `PromotionCard` URL includes `customerID=43cece97...` and `mi_u=200492586522`, which are per-recipient identifiers in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits before `<meta name="viewport">` and the `<style>` blocks, all inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table.
  - The viewport meta and the style blocks then land in `<body>`.
  - Client behavior will vary.
- **Malformed CSS selectors.** In the first `<style>` block, `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both rules are ignored.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). Most email clients ignore it, but some webmail and in-app views honor it.
- **Missing alt text on two content images.**
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg`
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixels.** The 11 Dotomi pixels also lack alt text. This is low impact if they are 1x1, but `alt=""` is still the correct form.
- **A hidden `<title>` sits mid-document.** `<title style="display: none; ...">` is empty and appears inside the body content.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with `role="presentation"` ARIA attributes. This is valid enough to render, but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment on the original.
- **The unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated HTML.** Neither was evaluated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTM parameters.** The one visible URL uses a proprietary scheme: `cm_mmc=di-_-112126515-_-VSD_VSL_20261121_VS_6524_FULFILE`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp=20261121_VS_6524_FULFILE`. This is consistent and campaign-identifiable, but `utm_source/medium/campaign` are absent, so GA-style attribution depends on the site mapping `cm_mmc`.
- **The JSON-LD URL goes to `/us/vs/newarrivals`.** The subject promises "30% Off Everything". Alignment of the CTAs cannot be checked because the 65 tracked links were not resolved and the HTML is truncated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD table and script out of `<head>`. Keep only `meta`, `style` and `title` in `<head>`.
2. Serve all pixels over HTTPS. Stop sending the SHA-256 email hash in third-party URL keys, or confirm consent and disclosure for the Dotomi sync.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to the two content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. Fix the CSS selectors to `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass`, and drop the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-run QA on the raw origin message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run with untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link and address.
7. Resolve the tracked click URLs in a follow-up pass to verify the landing pages match the "30% Off Everything" offer.
