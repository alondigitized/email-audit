# Last Day: Shop to Earn $15 Off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is VS's closing send on a "Shop to Earn $15 Off" loyalty mechanic — positioned as Last Day urgency. The hero does the job: the offer is right there above the fold with a clear CTA, model in lingerie, and the $15 savings threshold visible. It's a spend-to-earn mechanic (the "When You Spend $75+" qualifier is legible in the banner), which is meaningfully different from the flat discount drip VS ran June 22–25.
- Below the fold, VS pivots aggressively into fragrance — a "Find Your Summer Scent" section with multiple product shots, social-proof quotes, and named fragrances (Bare, Tease, Very Sexy). The fragrance section is well-executed standalone creative, but it competes directly with the bra/lingerie hero. The email is doing two jobs at once, and neither gets full commitment.
- The CoolBra™ send from June 29 — arguably VS's best recent email — had singular focus. This one dilutes that discipline by stacking fragrance editorial behind the loyalty offer. The $15 mechanic is legitimate and time-bounded; the fragrance section is filler padding a legitimate-but-thin offer.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unambiguous: "Save $15 / When You Spend $75+" with a CTA button — offer, threshold, and action in one glance
- "$29.50 Bras" price anchor below the hero is specific and scannable — gives the offer a product anchor
- Fragrance section includes named products (Bare, Tease, Very Sexy), social-proof pull quotes, and visual differentiation — it's real editorial, not filler imagery
- "Last Day" urgency in the subject is credible (email is dated June 30, which is a legitimate close date)
- No visible render bugs — images load, layout is intact, no overlapping text

## What's weak

- The fragrance mid-section is a sharp tonal break — a subscriber here for lingerie/bras gets a fragrance catalog they didn't opt into
- The social-proof quote placement is tiny and unreadable at thumbnail scale; the signal exists but won't land on mobile
- No loyalty or membership tier signal visible — for a spend-to-earn mechanic, this should be personalized ("You're $X away from earning $15")
- The bra grid below the hero is generic — no sizing, no fit callouts, no "bestseller" or "new" badge to drive urgency on individual units
- Two separate "Shop Now" CTAs for two different sections (bras and fragrance) creates decision paralysis — there's no single focal action

## Recommendations

- 1. **Personalize the spend threshold** — "You're $45 away from earning $15 off" converts dramatically better than a flat "spend $75." VS has the purchase data; this is table stakes for a spend-to-earn mechanic.
- 2. **Kill the fragrance section or make it subordinate** — Either cut it or explicitly tie it to the offer ("$75 gets you $15 off — a fragrance + bra hits that threshold easily"). Right now it's two separate emails stapled together.
- 3. **Add a countdown timer or specific expiry time** — "Last Day" is weaker than "Offer ends tonight at midnight ET." The June 24 "Ends Tomorrow" send was stronger on this exact point.
- 4. **Badge the bra grid** — "Bestseller," "New," or "Almost Gone" on individual units sharpens the click path from browse to buy.
- 5. **Consolidate the CTAs** — One primary CTA in the hero; fragrance gets a secondary text link, not its own full-width button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: spend $75, earn $15 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn $15 today — offer ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your $15 reward expires at midnight — shop bras, fragrance & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$29.50 bras count toward your $15 off. Today only.`
