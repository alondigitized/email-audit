# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is giving me major mixed feelings — VS clearly knows their brand, and that rewards pop-up came in hot, but the homepage itself is half-broken right now. The left side of the hero is just... white. Like, blank. The model imagery is cut off on the right edge of my screen, and a rewards modal is eating the whole middle. Not the first impression I expect from VS.

## What's working

- **VS & PINK Rewards modal** — the timing may be aggressive but the content is good: Points Per $, $15 Reward, and Birthday Gift are exactly the perks I care about as a frequent shopper. This is targeted at me.
- **"Already a member? Sign In"** link in the modal — good returning-customer hook. I don't have to dig for sign-in.
- **Partial bra imagery on the right** — even cropped, the tone and model styling feel on-brand for the VS woman I aspire to. The warm skin tones and lingerie aesthetic are correct.
- **Thin blush-pink top banner** — suggests there's a promo or announcement being surfaced at the very top, which is the right place for it.

## What's weak

- **Broken hero layout** — the left half of the screen is completely blank/white. This isn't intentional white space, this is a render failure. I'm staring at nothing.
- **Modal blocking everything** — I can't see the hero headline, the main CTA, or any product copy. The pop-up is covering the most valuable real estate on the page.
- **Zero shopping CTAs visible** — there's no "Shop Bras," no "PINK," no "Shop Now" anywhere on screen. The only button is "JOIN NOW" for rewards.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — on mobile I can't tell what categories exist without tapping the menu first. PINK, Bras, Sale — none of that is visible.
- **Top banner text unreadable** — the promo strip at top is too small/light to read. If there's a Free Panty Wednesday or Semi-Annual Sale message up there, I can't see it.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero render bug immediately** — half the above-the-fold area is white. Whatever is supposed to appear in the left panel (headline, CTA, offer) is invisible. This is a P0.
- **Delay the rewards modal** by 2–3 scroll events or set it on a 5-second timer so the hero gets a chance to land first — let me see what's on sale before asking me to sign up for points.
- **Add an always-visible category strip** — even a simple sticky row of "Bras | Panties | PINK | Sale" below the nav gives me one-tap access to my category without the hamburger detour.
- **Surface the top banner offer in larger text** — if this is Free Panty Wednesday or a Semi-Annual Sale tease, that copy should be legible at arm's length on a phone.
