# Stop Scrolling 🛑 $25 Bras Today Only
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's now-familiar kitchen-sink megasend, but the subject actually delivers again — "Stop Scrolling 🛑 $25 Bras Today Only" leads to a hero that opens on a model in a burgundy bra/panty set with a clean "$25 BRAS / TODAY ONLY" callout. That's a real story-to-hero match, the third day in a row this brand has managed it (2026-08-30's sweats, 2026-08-29's tees/shorts).
- Below the fold it's the standard PINK sprawl: panty packs from $20, a "Wild Rose" lace collection, a loungewear push, a pumpkin vanilla body-care section, then the mega nav grid and rewards footer. Nothing is broken, but the email is long and the bra offer — the entire reason for opening — gets buried under four more unrelated stories by the time you're two scrolls in.
- Cadence fatigue is the real issue here, not execution. This is essentially the same template running daily since 08-06 with the hero product swapped. It's a solid, honest single-offer subject on a reasonably well-rendered send — call it a 6.5, consistent with the brand's recent run.

## What's working

- Subject/hero alignment is tight: "$25 Bras Today Only" in the subject maps directly to "$25 BRAS / TODAY ONLY" as the first thing visible.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits directly under the offer with a pink button, unmissable.
- Clear price anchor is visible ("Orig. up to $49.95. Select styles.") giving the discount some credibility.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, product photography is sharp throughout.
- Visual hierarchy in the hero is clean: model image, price, CTA, in that order, no competing elements.

## What's weak

- Five distinct product stories (bras, panty packs, lace collection, sleepwear, body care) stacked in one email dilutes the "today only" urgency from the subject.
- "Today Only" urgency isn't reinforced anywhere below the hero — no countdown, no repeated deadline near the secondary CTA.
- Fine print ("Excludes Victoria's Secret, third-party styles and clearance") sits in tiny gray text easy to miss on mobile, which cuts against the "honest offer" signal.
- This is the same sprawling template structure PINK has run essentially unchanged since 08-06 — a subscriber who's opened a few of these will recognize the layout instantly and may skim past.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the bra offer plus one secondary story (panty packs), and move the rest into a follow-up send — a tighter email would make "today only" feel true instead of diluted by four other campaigns.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or explicit end time near the hero CTA to make the urgency concrete rather than just a subject-line claim.
- 3. Surface the exclusions line in slightly larger/darker text right under the price so it doesn't read as buried fine print.
- 4. Repeat the $25 bras CTA once more near the bottom of the email (before the nav grid) for people who scroll past without clicking.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call — $25 Bras Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New prints just dropped, and your top drawer needs them.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab your size before it's gone — today only.`
