# Shop Styles That Walked The Runway
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand runway-recap email, but it tries to do too much. The hero is strong: a confetti-covered finale shot with "Shop Looks *from the* RUNWAY" and a single black "SHOP THE SHOW" button. The subject, "Shop Styles That Walked The Runway," matches it. The next day after the show, this is the right job for the send.
- After the hero, the email loses focus. It runs a six-item runway-era list (Spring Awakening, Vintage Victoria, Summer Fever, Autumn Changes, Winter Melody, Winter Reprise), then the Very Sexy Shine Fantasy Bra, a Bombshell Runway fragrance block, a free Fashion Show Tote, the "A New Angel Gets Her Wings" Solange story, three category tiles, a Rewards/SMS/credit-card strip and "Now Trending." That is roughly eight modules with at least six different CTAs. The email is about 11,800px tall. The best offer, a free tote with $125+ and code RUNWAY125, is mid-email and the deadline sits only in fine print.
- Compared with the 10-18 sends ("TONIGHT: Tune In to Watch the Show" at 7/10, "Happening NOW: Shop The Show" at 6/10), this one fits the moment better than the "Happening NOW" catalog-style email. It still plays as a kitchen-sink catalog. Verdict: a solid 6–7, held back by the sprawl and the buried offer.

## What's working

- The confetti finale hero is emotional and instantly says "runway," and it gives the subject's promise a visual payoff.
- The "Shop Looks from the RUNWAY" serif headline is big and elegant, with a plain "SHOP THE SHOW" button directly under it.
- The Fantasy Bra module (jeweled bra, "Make it Yours," "Inspired by the Jacob & Co. runway design") gives a single hero product with a clear "SHOP ALL BRAS" button.
- Timing is right. The Solange copy ("walked the runway last night") and the preheader tease keep the email tied to the current show cycle.
- The Rewards, SMS and $25-off-first-purchase strip, plus the "Now Trending" grid, give members and browsers a clear next step.

## What's weak

- The free Fashion Show Tote with $125+ (code RUNWAY125, ends Oct 20) is the only real offer. It sits deep in the email, and the deadline and code are in tiny legal text at the bottom.
- Competing offers dilute the message: a free panty with purchase in the app (top banner), the free tote, and $25 off a first credit-card purchase. None of them gets top billing.
- The runway-era module is a long column of small, staggered images with tiny captions. It reads as filler and has no visible product detail or price.
- The Solange block has two stacked buttons, "SHOP NOW" and "WATCH IT NOW," with no clear priority. The category tiles below it are decorative, with no copy beyond the label.
- This is the fourth or fifth Fashion Show email in about four days (10-15 through 10-18). Even with a fresh angle, it will feel repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the tote offer in the hero. Add a slim banner or a line under the headline, such as "Free Fashion Show Tote with $125+ · ends 10/20 · code RUNWAY125," and make the button "Shop the Runway Collection."
- 2. Cut the layout to three modules: hero, Fantasy Bra, and one grid of runway looks with prices. Drop the era-by-era list or turn it into a compact 2×3 grid.
- 3. Pick one primary action in the Solange block, or move it to the footer for readers who want the story. Two equal buttons split attention.
- 4. Remove the free-panty app banner from this send. It competes with the tote and sends people to a different channel.
- 5. Carry a countdown or "Ends tomorrow" line on the tote offer, since the Oct 20 cutoff is real urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Show Tote + the looks from last night`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The runway looks you can shop now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Fashion Show Tote with $125+ — ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Jacob & Co. Fantasy Bra pre-order, plus the best of last night's show.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) build with malformed HTML structure and 11 third-party tracking pixels that use insecure `http://` and have no alt text. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. The missing-header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity is unverified.
- **10 `http://` cookie-sync pixels** point to `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. They carry `?a4edea106b33…=1`.
- **1 more `http://` pixel** at `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&…`.
- **Mixed content:** all 11 use plain HTTP, so clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop them. That includes Gmail's image proxy behavior and some privacy-focused clients.
- **Hashed email in third-party URLs:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` (SHA-256) appears in the JSON-LD `url`. The same hash is the query key on the dotomi pixel URLs. This shares a stable user identifier with a third party over unencrypted HTTP.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?…` is 1x1 with `alt=""`, which is correct for a pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** the SFMC wrapper `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">`, a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block and `<meta>` tags sit inside `<head>`. Tables are not valid in `<head>`, so parsers may close the head early and move the content. The viewport and content-type metas can then be ignored.
- **Empty title in the body:** `<title style="display:none…"></title>` is inside the body and is empty.
- **Missing alt text:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt. It is a content image, not a pixel, so this is a real accessibility defect. The 11 pixel images also lack alt, but that is low impact.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure in clients that honor it.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
  - `table{max-width:600px!important}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important}` apply globally and can constrain nested tables.
- **Outlook:** the MSO font fix sets `line-height:1`, which may clip text.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` are visible in the supplied source. `customerID`, `dtm_email_hash` and `mi_u` all resolved to values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that violates Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable from this data. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a subdomain of `victoriassecret.com`, so alignment should be checked directly.
- **Footer not checked:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated source. They were neither confirmed nor refuted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one visible destination is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/newarrivals/vs-icon-shoppe`. It is tagged with the proprietary params `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp=20261019_VS_6334_FULLFILE`, and has **no `utm_*` params**. GA-style attribution depends on the site mapping `cm_mmc`, which can't be verified here.
- The subject says "Styles That Walked The Runway". The only visible link goes to `/newarrivals/vs-icon-shoppe`, and the landing page was not probed, so alignment is unconfirmed.
- The JSON-LD `url` repeats the full tracked URL with the hashed email and customerID, so those identifiers are exposed in the markup.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 11 dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or remove them. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to each.
2. Stop putting `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` in the JSON-LD `url` and the third-party pixel query strings, or confirm this is covered by consent and the privacy policy.
3. Move the SFMC wrapper table, JSON-LD script and metas out of `<head>` and into the `<body>` or a proper head.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png`.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
6. Fix the CSS: `.ReadMsgBody`, delete `min-width{…}` and `max-height:auto`, and scope the `table{max-width}` rule.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and to read the footer.
8. Add `utm_*` params, or confirm that `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp` map into analytics.
