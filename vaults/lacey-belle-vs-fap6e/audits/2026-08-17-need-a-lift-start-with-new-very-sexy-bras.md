# Need a Lift? Start with NEW Very Sexy Bras
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the kitchen-sink template again, but the subject-hero match is weaker than the run's better sends. "Need a Lift? Start with NEW Very Sexy Bras" promises bras, and the hero does deliver "New Low-Cut Push-Up Bra" over a moody, well-shot lifestyle image — but the model's pose (bra held/draped rather than worn clearly) and the dim, shadow-heavy crop make the actual product harder to read at a glance than the 08-11 "VERY SEXY COLLECTION" send.
- The email then sprawls: a second full hero for "New Rose Lace Overlay Cheeky Panties," then a 25% off banner, then a full fragrance/body-care campaign (Lavender Cashmere Woods) that has nothing to do with the bra subject line, then a 2/$20 body care banner, a 9-tile category grid, rewards/credit card blocks, and a "Now Trending" strip. By the time a reader hits the offer and the trending panel, the original subject-line promise is long forgotten.
- Compared to 08-12's countdown-clock, offer-first send (7/10, "the most direct, offer-first email in this run"), this one buries a decent single offer (25% off $75+) three scrolls down under two unrelated hero campaigns. Judgment: on-brand, technically clean, but unfocused — a solid 6, not a 7.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed and on-brand, moody desert/editorial styling consistent with recent VS creative.
- The bra headline and subhead ("Perfect for plunge necklines and adds up to one cup size") give a concrete product benefit, not just a vague name.
- The 25% off banner is clean and legible — clear code, clear threshold, clear CTA button.
- Category grid (9 tiles: New Arrivals, Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty, Activewear, Swim, Lingerie, Loungewear) gives easy secondary navigation with no layout breakage.
- No visible render bugs — all images loaded, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- Subject says "bras," but the email pivots almost immediately to panties, then to a full body-care/fragrance sub-campaign (Lavender Cashmere Woods) — the promised product is one of four competing storylines.
- The bra product shot is dim and the garment itself is partially obscured by the model's pose/hand — harder to evaluate fit/style than a flat lay or clearer worn shot would allow.
- The 25% off offer is disconnected from the hero — it sits mid-scroll with no visual tie to the bra or panty product shown above it, so it reads like a bolted-on template block.
- This is a long email (roughly 10+ distinct content blocks) with no visual hierarchy pointing to a single primary action — the eye has too many equally-weighted "SHOP NOW" buttons to land on one.
- "Now Trending" strip at the bottom repeats the panty/bra/loungewear themes already shown above, adding length without adding new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two blocks max: bra hero (matching the subject) + the 25% off offer directly below it, tied visually (e.g., "25% off this bra" framing) — this is the single biggest lift, since right now the offer is orphaned three scrolls from the subject-line product.
- 2. Move or drop the Lavender Cashmere Woods fragrance campaign into its own dedicated send — it dilutes a bra-focused subject line and adds ~1/3 of the email's length with zero relevance to "Need a Lift."
- 3. Reshoot or recrop the hero so the push-up bra itself is clearly visible and well-lit, not just implied by pose — product clarity matters more than mood here.
- 4. Trim the category grid from 9 tiles to 3-4 relevant to intimates (Bras, Panties, Lingerie) since Beauty/Activewear/Swim are already off-message for this send.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email is a standard SFMC-templated Victoria's Secret promo with mobile-responsive CSS and open/tracking pixels; one broken SMS-signup link and several deprecated HTTP ad-tracking pixels were found, alongside missing compliance headers in the captured relay data.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (400):** SMS signup CTA ("Let's text. Sign up for SMS...") points to `https://vs.attn.tv/p/TaM/email` and returns HTTP 400.
- Primary open-tracking pixel: `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?HTPB3FWADODEPKLG7JWSD6BIAU.70251&d=70251&bmt=0` (appears twice in the head — duplicate open pixel, not a functional bug but redundant).
- 40 click-redirect/tracking links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for click-tracking domains, not itself an issue).
- 10 Dotomi ad-redirect pixels (`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) and one Dotomi UCM controller call (`http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`) are served over plain HTTP — see Rendering section below.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text: the 10 Dotomi `pubN.php` pixels, the Dotomi UCM controller image, and one content image (`5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`). The tracking pixels are non-visual and low-risk to leave without alt text, but the content image should carry descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""`) text for screen readers.
- 11 images use `http://` instead of `https://` (the Dotomi pixels/redirects listed above). Mixed-content images are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy), which will silently drop these ad-tracking calls.
- Head contains legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype and MSO/Outlook conditional font-fix — standard for this ESP template, no issues found beyond the above.

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Tracking/click-redirect links (40 total) were not probed, so destination landing pages and UTM parameter alignment cannot be verified from this data. The one confirmed-broken destination is the SMS opt-in landing page (`vs.attn.tv/p/TaM/email`, Attentive's platform) returning 400 — this breaks the email-to-site handoff for the SMS acquisition CTA specifically.

7. Recommendations

- Migrate the 11 Dotomi ad-tracking pixels from `http://` to `https://` to prevent silent blocking by mail clients on mixed content.

- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM) headers directly at the SMTP layer, since the AgentMail relay may be stripping or not surfacing them — current data is inconclusive, not a confirmed failure.
- Remove the duplicate open-tracking pixel call in the `<head>`/body (same `open.aspx` URL fires twice).
