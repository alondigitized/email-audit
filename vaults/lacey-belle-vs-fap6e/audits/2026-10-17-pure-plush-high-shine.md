## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has structural defects: a `<table>` inside `<head>`, two malformed CSS selectors, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. It also carries about 12 plain-HTTP third-party pixels, most with no alt text. The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and headers could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so link validity is unverified. This is a gap in coverage, not a confirmed defect.
- **11 pixels load over plain `http://`.** The QA run flagged them, and some clients will block or rewrite them:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **The 10 `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pubN.php` pixels are third-party cookie-sync calls.** They add 10 extra requests per open, and each carries the hashed email `a4edea106b33…` as a query key.
- **The primary open pixel is fine.** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?…` is served over HTTPS and has `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` wrapping the JSON-LD `<script>` sits directly inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table. The later `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<style>` blocks may then land in `<body>`, where support is inconsistent across clients.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Two CSS selectors are malformed and do nothing.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a non-existent element.
- **Outlook line height.** The `<!--[if mso]>` block sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td`. This can clip multi-line text in Outlook desktop.
- **Missing alt text:**
  - Pixels: `o.gif`, the `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` image, and `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`. These are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should carry `alt=""`.
  - Content image: `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt text. Its role can't be confirmed from the truncated source, but if it carries content, that content is invisible with images off or to a screen reader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge syntax (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) appears in the visible source. The per-recipient values `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` and `mi_u` are all populated. Coverage is limited to the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers are unverified, not confirmed missing.** The QA flagged `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` as absent. Its own notes attribute this to the AgentMail relay not capturing them, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown. The sender is `PINK@e2.vspink.com`; checking the raw message at the source would settle it.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The visible HTML ends before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.
- **Hashed email shared with third parties.** The email hash and `customerID` appear in the JSON-LD URLs and in the Dotomi pixel URLs. This is a privacy and disclosure concern, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID is consistent.** `20261017_PK_6321_FILEBAL` appears in `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and `dtmc_email_category`, so tracking aligns across the links and pixels.
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution relies on the retailer's own `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid` and `mi_ecmp` instead. This is a proprietary scheme, not a defect, but standard analytics tools won't pick it up without custom mapping.
- **All three JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries share one URL.** They point at `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/new-arrivals?cm_mmc=di-_-101726445-…` despite having three different images, so the cards cannot deep-link to distinct products.
- **Landing page not verified.** The tracked links were skipped, so whether the landing page matches the subject ("Pure Plush 🤝 High Shine") is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block out of `<head>`'s table wrapper, or place it in `<body>`, and keep only valid head elements (`meta`, `style`, `script`) inside `<head>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix or delete the broken selectors `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width`. Review `line-height:1` in the MSO block.
4. Serve every pixel over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png` if it is content.
5. Cut the 10 Dotomi cookie-redirect pixels to the ones actually needed.
6. Check the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay. Check the full footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Give each JSON-LD card its own destination URL where possible, and re-run the audit with the tracking links resolved to confirm landing-page alignment.
