## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Breaking News: The Trailer Just Dropped"

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `camarker-inner` wrappers) with structural HTML errors and about 11 non-HTTPS, alt-less third-party pixels. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 issues, 3 warnings). All three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed as sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 49 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations are verified. Only the HTTP probe was skipped, so this is a coverage gap rather than a defect.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 `http://` pixels are served from Dotomi (Epsilon):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Mixed-content or proxy image blocking (e.g. Gmail image proxy, strict clients) may stop these from firing, which would break cookie sync and retargeting attribution.
- **Identifier in URLs:** Each Dotomi cookie-sync URL carries `?a4edea106b333e69…=1`, and the JSON-LD `url` carries `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…`. These are hashed, not plaintext, but the same email hash is sent over unencrypted HTTP to a third party in the `http://` pixel URLs.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is 1x1 with `alt=""`. This is fine.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before `<meta>` and `<style>`. `<table>` is not valid in `<head>`, and parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at that point. The following `<meta viewport>`, `<meta charset>` and both `<style>` blocks then land in `<body>`. Support for styles there varies by client, and Gmail is the main risk for the media queries.
- **Empty title in body:** `<title style="display: none; …"></title>` appears inside the body and is empty.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Broken CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an invalid element selector.
  - Neither rule has any effect.
- **Missing alt text (11 images):**
  - `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…`
  - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Most of these are tracking pixels, so they should carry `alt=""` and be sized 1x1 or hidden.
- **Content image without alt:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. This one is on the brand CDN and is likely a real content image, so it is the only true accessibility miss.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is legacy but conventional for email. It is not flagged.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible source, and the `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` values are populated. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results:** All three headers were not captured. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, and the mail is from the SFMC domain `e1.victoriassecret.com`, so a missing header can't be treated as a sender failure. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and RFC 8058 one-click support remain unverified.
- **Footer:** The visible body is truncated before the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** It uses Victoria's Secret's proprietary parameters, not `utm_*`: `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid=093026515`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=20260930_VS_6165_FULLFILE` and `dtmc_email_category`. The campaign ID `20260930_VS_6165_FULLFILE` is consistent across the JSON-LD URL and the Dotomi pixels. Analytics that expect `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` won't attribute the visit without mapping.
- **Landing page:** The only landing URL visible in the truncated source is the JSON-LD `url`, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/newarrivals/vs-icon-shoppe`. The 49 click-tracked links weren't resolved, so landing-page alignment and redirect health are unverified.
- **Schema.org markup:** `@type: "PromotionCard"` is a Gmail Promotions-tab annotation. The JSON-LD sits in `<head>` inside a table, which is invalid placement (see section 3) and may prevent Gmail from parsing it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD `<script>` out of the table and into a valid `<head>`. Put the `<table>` wrapper in `<body>`, with `<meta>` and `<style>` directly in `<head>`.
2. Serve all Dotomi pixels over `https://`, and add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to them. Consider whether 10 cookie-sync pixels are needed.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Remove the empty `<title>` from the body.
5. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector and delete the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
6. On the QA side, capture the raw headers from the original message. This would verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC and List-Unsubscribe-Post, and stop these warnings from recurring as false positives. Also resolve click-tracked links with a follow-redirect probe to verify landing pages and the unsubscribe URL.
7. Confirm that analytics maps `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp` to the campaign, since there are no UTM tags.
