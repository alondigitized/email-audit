- 42 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com` domain) were skipped from HTTP probing per QA policy — not independently verified as functional.
- Open-tracking pixel present and duplicated: `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?LJCDQRQV7UREDGF67L7MPXKJJU.70252&d=70252&bmt=0` appears twice in the truncated source (once in `<body>`, once inside the nested content wrapper).

- Legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype and table-based layout — standard for ESP-rendered mass email, not itself a defect.
- Responsive/mobile media queries and MSO (Outlook) conditional font-fix block are present, indicating cross-client rendering support was addressed.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No merge tokens or AMPscript output visible in the truncated source — the `<div id="AMPScript">` block only contains empty `<!-- Ampscript v0 START/END -->` comments, suggesting either no personalization was applied to this version or the AMPscript payload was stripped before this render was captured. No broken tokens (e.g., unresolved `%%...%%`) observed.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — QA notes this may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — cannot confirm sender authentication passed; likely also a relay capture gap rather than a sending-domain failure, but unverifiable from available data.
- No visible unsubscribe link or physical address in the truncated HTML body — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance since the source was truncated before the footer.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All primary CTAs route through the `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` ExactTarget redirect domain rather than direct victoriassecret.com links — standard ESP click-tracking pattern, consistent redirect behavior not verifiable since these were skipped in the HTTP probe.
- SMS signup CTA breaks the email-to-site chain entirely (400 error on `vs.attn.tv`), meaning that specific conversion path is currently non-functional.
- Cannot assess UTM parameter alignment with landing pages since destination URLs beyond the redirect domain were not captured in this QA pass.

7. **Recommendations**
- Fix or remove the broken SMS signup link (`https://vs.attn.tv/p/TaM/email`, currently 400) before next send.
- Add `alt` attributes to all `<img>` tags, including third-party tracking pixels (empty `alt=""` is sufficient for purely decorative/tracking images).

- Confirm with the AgentMail relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are genuinely absent at the source or simply not being forwarded — this materially affects both CAN-SPAM one-click compliance and deliverability assessment.
- Re-run QA against the full (untruncated) HTML to verify footer-level CAN-SPAM elements (unsubscribe link, physical mailing address) and confirm the 42 skipped tracking links resolve correctly.
