# Buy 2, Get 1 FREE Ends Soon—Don't Miss It
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Victoria's Secret's July 22 continuation of the Buy 2, Get 1 Free push — but it carries a credibility problem right out of the gate. Yesterday's send declared "Ends Tonight," and today the same deal is back as "Ends Soon." Any subscriber who opened yesterday's email is immediately on notice that VS extended the deal, which makes the urgency language feel manufactured rather than real. That's the defining problem this email can't overcome regardless of execution quality.
- The creative itself is a patchwork: the hero image leads with what appears to be a fragrance/beauty product, not swim or lingerie — an odd mismatch for a headline promising "Buy 2, Get 1 Free Everything." The $6 body care block mid-email is a direct lift of the July 18 send's hero creative, reused wholesale here as a secondary module. The lingerie editorial image in the third block is the strongest visual in the email and arguably deserves hero placement.
- Three distinct offers compete for attention (BOGO Everything, $6 body care, "The Perfect Bra that Started it All") with no clear hierarchy. This is the weakest VS send in the current run.

## What's working

- BOGO headline copy is immediately readable — "Buy 2, Get 1 Free Everything" lands on first scan with no ambiguity.
- The dark editorial lingerie image (third block) is high-quality and models strong visual identity for the brand.
- Multiple CTA buttons throughout keep click surfaces accessible without scrolling far.
- The $6 body care module delivers a clean price anchor — a specific number helps conversion even as a secondary offer.
- Grid product block at the bottom gives variety and surfaces browseable SKUs.

## What's weak

- Leading with a fragrance/perfume-looking product image directly contradicts the "Everything" BOGO headline — the hero image and the offer feel like two different emails.
- "Ends Soon" after "Ends Tonight" (July 21) is an explicit deal extension that breaks subscriber trust in VS's urgency signals.
- The $6 body care block is visually near-identical to the July 18 send — zero refresh; repeat subscribers will feel like they're seeing recycled content.
- Three competing offers with no clear primary CTA hierarchy diffuse the click impulse — nothing is unambiguously THE reason to open your wallet right now.
- "The Perfect Bra that Started it All" has no size, fit, or specific style callout visible — vague lifestyle copy with no decision-driving detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the deal extension or own it.** If the deadline moved, say so — "We extended it one more day" is more honest than pretending "Ends Soon" means something new. Honesty here builds more long-term trust than manufacturing false urgency.
- 2. **Swap the hero image to match the BOGO Everything offer.** Lead with a lingerie or swim flatlay that visually telegraphs the breadth of the deal, not a beauty product.
- 3. **Pick one CTA lane.** Either lead with BOGO Everything OR $6 body care OR The Perfect Bra — not all three equally. The current email has no hierarchy.
- 4. **Refresh the body care creative.** The July 18 flatlay is showing its age; even a recolor or angle change would feel new to repeat openers.
- 5. **Add a specific bra name/style to the editorial block** — copy like "The Bombshell Bra, back in 12 new colors" gives the editorial image a reason to click.

## Technical Audit — Victoria's Secret "Buy 2, Get 1 FREE Ends Soon"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard HTML4 table-based build with responsive CSS media queries. The primary technical issues are a batch of plaintext-HTTP retargeting pixel calls from Dotomi/Epsilon that will be blocked by modern email clients, and missing authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open-pixel firing**
The open-tracking pixel `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?JAQV3LA2Z26EFHKRTKQMDZJIKI.70246&d=70246&bmt=0` appears **twice** in the HTML — once in the preheader `<div>` and again inside the `#AMPScript` content block. This will double-count opens for this send.

**20 click links skipped by HTTP probe**
All CTAs route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` — confirmed redirect/tracking domain, not probed. No broken destination URLs can be confirmed or ruled out from this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed-content HTTP images (10 instances)**
Ten Dotomi retargeting pixels use `http://` sources:
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&dtm_cmagic=f55cad&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id=2026&dtmc_email_category=20260721_VS_5832_FILEBAL`
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`

Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook all block HTTP image loads; these pixels will silently fail for the majority of recipients, invalidating retargeting audience signals for this send.

**Missing alt text (11 images)**
All 10 Dotomi tracking pixels have empty `alt=""` or no `alt` attribute. Additionally, `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (a content image served from `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`) has no alt text — this is the accessibility-relevant miss since tracking pixels with blank alt are acceptable.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Disabling pinch-to-zoom fails WCAG 1.4.4 and is blocked by iOS accessibility settings for users who require zoom.

**`<title>` inside `<body>`**
```html
<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>
```
A `<title>` element in the body (not `<head>`) is malformed HTML4. It renders as hidden text in some clients but may surface in accessibility trees or plain-text fallback renderers.

**Dead CSS comment artifact**
```html
<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` --> 
```
An unexecuted shell date command left as a comment — likely a build-script artifact. No functional impact but indicates an incomplete template substitution step.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the truncated source. The `#AMPScript` block is present but its content renders as empty (`<!-- Ampscript v0 START --> <!-- Ampscript v0 END -->`), suggesting either the personalization block was intentionally suppressed or the ESP did not inject dynamic content for this recipient. If personalization was expected here, it silently produced no output.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent at relay**
QA did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header via the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders (>5k/day) or they apply spam filtering. The header may exist on the originating MTA but is being stripped by the relay — this needs verification at the raw SMTP layer.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy since Feb 2024. Absence here means recipients must navigate to an external page to unsubscribe, which violates current mailbox provider policy and increases spam complaints.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com` (Epsilon ESP). If DKIM is aligned on this subdomain and DMARC passes, this is a relay capture gap, not a real auth failure — but it cannot be confirmed from available data.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters not verifiable**
All destination URLs pass through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirect chains; final landing page URLs and UTM parameters cannot be inspected without resolving the redirects. The QA checker correctly skipped these. No continuity issues confirmed, but none can be ruled out.
