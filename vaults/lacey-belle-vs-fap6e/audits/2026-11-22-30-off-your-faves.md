# 30% Off Your Faves‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's Black Friday catalog again, with the same 30% off the brand has pushed since the Nov 18 "STARTS NOW" send. It opens on a "SHOP NOW TO BEAT THE RUSH" banner and a lingerie hero for "LIMITED TIME BRAS FROM $25." Below that are 7/$35 panties, 30% off clothing, a 30% off everything chip block, $8 full-sized beauty, and a free glitter tote with a $25+ purchase. It is a long, eight-module scroll of stacked offers. The screenshot is very small, so I can't read the fine print or the top banner copy.
- The subject, "30% Off Your Faves‼️", promises one thing, but the hero leads with bras from $25. The 30% message only lands in the third and fourth modules. That mismatch is the same one that dragged the Nov 20 "Bras from $25 & 30% Off Everything Else" send. Each module is clean and branded, but with six offers competing, nothing wins.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but fatigued. It is the fifth near-identical 30% send in five days (Nov 18, 19, 20, 21, now 22). A subscriber has no reason to treat this one as new, and the vague subject does nothing to change that.

## What's working

- Every offer module has a "LIMITED TIME" label, a big offer headline, and a single "SHOP NOW" button, so each one is easy to scan.
- The gift-wrapped, holiday-styled photography fits the season, and the red/pink palette keeps the brand voice consistent.
- The "30% OFF EVERYTHING" block with category chips (Clothing, Sleep, Bras, Accessories, Activewear, Beauty) gives shoppers quick routes into their category.
- The Rewards module near the bottom shows a member benefit ($25 off).
- Nothing looks broken. Images load and there are no visible gaps or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The hero offer (Bras from $25) doesn't match the subject's 30% promise.
- Six or more offers (bras, panties, clothing, 30% off, beauty, tote) leave no single dominant message.
- The email is very long. The tote and the Rewards module sit deep in the scroll, where few will reach them.
- The subject has no deadline or new reason to open, and the "‼️" reads as noisy.
- The sections at the bottom (category tiles, Rewards, social, legal) are dense, and the fine print is a large block of small text. That undercuts the offer's honesty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Either lead with "30% OFF YOUR FAVES" or retitle the send around Bras from $25, and cut the modules that repeat the same message.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules: the lead offer, one product-specific hero (such as the tote), and the Rewards module.
- 3. Break the sameness. After four days of 30% off, give this send one new element, such as a countdown to Cyber Monday, a Sunday-only add-on, or the free tote as the lead.
- 4. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" buttons with specific ones, like "Shop Bras from $25" or "Get the Free Tote."
- 5. Move the Rewards module up for members, and shorten the legal block.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is malformed in several places: a `<table>` sits inside `<head>`, and the viewport meta blocks zoom. It also fires 11+ third-party tracking pixels over plain `http://`, some with hashed identifiers in the URL. Authentication and one-click unsubscribe headers could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Link validity is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?...` and ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels (`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) use `http://`. Mail clients and image proxies may block these as mixed content, so tracking and retargeting will silently under-report. `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` has the same problem.
- **The QA "alt" label for one Dotomi pixel is a parsing artifact.** It reads `2026&dtmc_email_category=20261122_PK_6522_FULFILE`, which suggests the `src` has an unescaped `&` or the parser split it. Check the raw attribute encoding.
- The open pixel at `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=...` is HTTPS with `alt=""`, which is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Invalid document structure.** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and the `<script type="application/ld+json">` are placed inside `<head>` before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will relocate or drop them, and Gmail's schema parser may ignore the PromotionCard markup.
- **Broken CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`, so it matches nothing.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an invalid selector.
  - `max-height:auto` is an invalid value in the mobile `img` rules.
- **`table{max-width:600px!important;}` applies globally.** It also caps the outer `width="100%"` wrapper tables, which can interfere with the fluid layout.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with `role="presentation"` ARIA attributes, which is inconsistent but not breaking.
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. This is a CDN-hosted `.png`, so it is likely a content image rather than a pixel. The other missing-alt hits (`o.gif`, `pub1–10.php`) are tracking pixels and low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. Recipient identifiers are embedded in URLs rather than the body:
- The JSON-LD `url` fields carry `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe`, `customerID=43cece97…1e2f` and `mi_u=200492586522`.
- The same email hash is used as a parameter name in all Dotomi pixels: `pub1.php?a4edea106b33…=1`.

This is a privacy exposure rather than a rendering bug, because a hashed email is shared with a third party (Dotomi) via query strings.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are absent from the captured headers.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed. If real, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Note that the From domain is `e2.vspink.com` while the links and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.** The HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking parameters are consistent.** The campaign ID `20261122_PK_6522_FULFILE` matches across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and the pixel URLs.
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present.** Attribution relies on the proprietary `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid` and `mi_*` parameters. This works only if the destination site's analytics reads them.
- **All three PromotionCard entries point to one URL.** Each is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/new-arrivals?...`, despite having three different images. The landing page was not fetched, so its alignment with the "30% Off" subject is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD script to `<body>` or a valid `<head>` position, and remove the `<table>` wrapper from `<head>`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Switch the `mi.victoriassecret.com` and Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels. Add `alt=""` to all remaining pixels.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
5. Fix the CSS selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`, `max-height:auto`) and scope `table{max-width}` to the `.container` table.
6. Re-capture the raw message, not the relay copy, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Review whether the hashed email and `customerID` need to appear in third-party-bound URLs.
8. Probe a sample of the 57 skipped tracking links for redirect health and final-destination parameters.
