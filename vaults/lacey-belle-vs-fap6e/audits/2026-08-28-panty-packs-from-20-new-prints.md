# Panty Packs from $20—NEW Prints
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Same kitchen-sink megasend PINK has run daily since 08-06 — this one just leads with panties instead of NFL bling. The subject "Panty Packs from $20—NEW Prints" actually delivers: the hero opens on "PANTY PACKS FROM $20" over a flat-lay of printed panties with a clean "SHOP NOW" button. That's more disciplined than most of this month's sprawl, but it's a fake-out — one scroll later you're into $25 tees, $35 sweats, $15 sleep, $10 bra offers, back-to-campus beauty BOGO, Little Words Project jewelry, and a "shop your feed" grid. It's the same seven-offer stack as every other August send, just with a different SKU up top.
- The panty-pack hero itself is genuinely strong — legible offer, one clear CTA, no render bugs. But by the time a reader hits "$35 SWEATSHIRTS & SWEATPANTS" three sections down, the subject's promise is long gone and this is indistinguishable from 08-22's "BLING" or 08-24's back-to-school send. Verdict: good hook, same bloated body — a 6 again.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "PANTY PACKS FROM $20" with matching flat-lay product shot and a single SHOP NOW button.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or lorem-ipsum anywhere in the render.
- Each section has a clear "LIMITED TIME" tag and price point, so individual offers are legible in isolation.
- Diverse model casting across sections (varied skin tones, body types) reads as authentic rather than tokenistic.

## What's weak

- Seven-plus distinct offers stacked in one email (panties, tees/shorts, sweats, sleep, bra, beauty BOGO, makeup bags, jewelry, feed grid) — the exact same megasend structure flagged on 08-06 through 08-24.
- Subject promises panties specifically, but panties are maybe 8% of the email's actual real estate — most readers scrolling past the hero get a completely different story.
- "TRY IT ON, THANK US LATER — $10 OFF A FULL-PRICED BRA" section has dense fine-print-style copy that's hard to parse against the busy background.
- No panty pack pricing detail beyond "$20" — no pack size, no how-many-included, so the click has to do all the work sizing scans can't.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a single-offer send built around the panty pack hero — that's the only section here with focused subject/creative alignment; if the megasend format must stay, put panties as the ONLY thing above the fold and demote everything else behind a "shop more deals" link.
- 2. Add pack contents to the hero copy ("3-Pack from $20") — shoppers deciding whether to click want to know quantity, not just price.
- 3. Break repeat offers (tees, sweats, sleep, bra, beauty, jewelry) into a rotating cadence across separate sends instead of one send — this is the same fatigue problem flagged in every megasend audit this month.
