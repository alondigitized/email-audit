# Don’t Ghost 10/$40 Panties, Boo 👻
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the **third** Halloween/spooky-panty email PINK has sent in roughly 24 hours — "So Good It's Scary" and "Too Cute to Spook" both ran July 2, and now "Don't Ghost...Boo" arrives July 3. On a Wednesday in early July. The creative conceit is burned out before the subscriber even opens. The 10/$40 panty deal is the same price point, the same Halloween aesthetic, the same hero format. At this send cadence, the inbox looks like PINK's automation got stuck.
- Inside the email, the structure is overloaded: hero 10/$40 panty block, then a bras-from-$25 module, then a colorful tees section, then a skincare/PINK product callout, then a Semi Annual Sale banner at 30% off. Five distinct offers competing for attention means none of them land with force. The Semi Annual Sale buried mid-scroll is arguably the strongest commercial hook in the email, yet it's treated as filler.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no layout collapse — but "no bugs" is the floor, not a win. This email is mechanically functional and strategically exhausted.

## What's working

- **Price point is front-and-center in the hero** — "10/$40 PANTY PARTY" is unambiguous; a subscriber knows the offer before scrolling.
- **Render quality is solid** — no broken images, clean layout, no overlapping text across all sections.
- **Subject length is mobile-friendly** — "Don't Ghost 10/$40 Panties, Boo 👻" is ~35 chars, well under the 50-char mobile clip threshold.
- **Semi Annual Sale module** adds a legitimate secondary hook (30% off) that breaks from the repeat 10/$40 framing.

## What's weak

- **Third Halloween send in 24 hours** — this creative conceit is completely spent by send #3; a real subscriber's reaction is annoyance, not engagement.
- **Halloween theme in early July** — the ghost/spooky framing was already forced yesterday; by today it reads like a scheduling error.
- **Five competing offer blocks** — 10/$40 panties, bras from $25, tees, PINK products, Semi Annual Sale all fighting for a click destroys hierarchy and conversion focus.
- **No urgency signal visible** — "Don't Ghost" implies scarcity but the body copy doesn't reinforce any credible deadline or limited-quantity hook.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — two emails yesterday and another today for the same item signals either inventory desperation or a broken send calendar.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop sending this email.** Three Halloween panty sends in 24 hours is table-stakes inbox noise. Rest the 10/$40 panty for at least 48–72 hours and lead with the Semi Annual Sale as the dedicated hero instead — it has fresher urgency and a cleaner story.
- 2. **Flatten the offer count.** One email, one hero offer, one CTA. The bras/$25 and tees modules can go in a secondary row, not above the fold as competing heroes.
- 3. **Give the Semi Annual Sale a deadline.** "30% Off" with no end date isn't urgent. Add "Ends Sunday" or a countdown-implied deadline in the subject or hero copy.
- 4. **Retire the Halloween creative until October.** Ghost/spooky puns in July feel tone-deaf. Lean into summer clearance framing instead ("Last Chance," "Summer Sweep") if the goal is the same inventory clearance.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus bras from $25 and tees in every color.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Semi Annual Sale is live — shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — PINK "Don't Ghost 10/$40 Panties" (20260703_PK_5719_FULFILE)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard SFMC/table-based send via `e1.victoriassecret.com` ESP. No structural blockers, but the email carries a duplicate open-tracking pixel, 10+ HTTP-only retargeting pixels that modern clients will block, and missing headers that prevent verifying SPF/DKIM status through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open pixel** — the same pixel fires twice:
```
https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?CAR3STPGPICEDLAM5JGKNXRZMY.70248&d=70248&bmt=0
```
Once at `<body>` open, again inside the `#AMPScript` div. This double-counts opens in reporting.

**19 click links skipped** — all route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`; destinations and UTM params could not be verified by the probe.

**Dotomi retargeting pixels (pub1–pub10)** — all 10 sync pixels use plain HTTP:
```
http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?...
```
Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail proxy/block HTTP image requests. These pixels will silently fail for a significant portion of the list.

**Unfilled shell variable in source** — HTML comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` is a raw bash substitution that was never resolved. Low impact (comment only), but indicates a template hygiene gap.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` placed inside `<body>`** — invalid HTML:
```html
<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>
```
This is a common Gmail preheader-suppression hack but is malformed; some parsers will surface it unexpectedly.

**15 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA. Includes:
- `o.gif` (MI open pixel, HTTP)
- `UCMController` Dotomi pixel (HTTP)
- `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` Dotomi pixels (HTTP)
- `5bcec3ac-...e3c2.png` (content image, HTTPS) — this one matters most for screen readers and image-off rendering

**`user-scalable=0`** in viewport meta — prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile; accessibility anti-pattern.

**DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional** — legacy. No immediate breakage, but limits use of modern CSS that improves rendering in newer clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**AMPScript block is empty** — the `#AMPScript` div contains only `<!-- Ampscript v0 START --> <!-- Ampscript v0 END -->` with no rendered variables. If personalization (first name, segment, dynamic content) was intended, it did not render.

**`mi_u` value appears hardcoded** — MI tracking pixel:
```
http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20260703_PK_5719_FULFILE
```
`mi_u=200492586522` is a static integer in the captured source. If this is not a personalization variable that rendered correctly for this recipient, it's a broken merge field affecting MI attribution for the full send.
