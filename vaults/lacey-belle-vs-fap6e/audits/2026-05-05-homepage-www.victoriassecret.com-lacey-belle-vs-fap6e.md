## What's working

- **REWARDS sign-up module** — "Ready to get rewarded?" with tier icons and a JOIN NOW button in that signature VS hot pink is placed prominently and speaks directly to me as a loyalty-oriented shopper. I have the app, I track my sale dates, I would absolutely tap this.
- **Pink lingerie imagery** — even cropped, the partial hero of a blonde woman in a pink lace bra is exactly the aesthetic I'm here for. It signals the brand is in their main lane.
- **BOGO-style promo (bottom right)** — there's a buy-more-save-more module for mists and lotions that's eye-catching with bright saturated color pops. Body care is an easy add-on for me during a VS visit.

## What's weak

- **Broken hero layout** — the left ~60% of the hero area is completely blank white. On mobile, that's a killer. It makes the page look half-loaded and I'd question whether to wait or bail.
- **No readable hero headline** — I cannot read the hero copy at all because it's either cut off or rendered off-screen. If VS is running a sale or a new launch, I have no idea.
- **Rewards module doesn't acknowledge I might already be a member** — the only secondary option is "Already a member? Sign in" in tiny text. As a returning customer, I should see my balance or a personalized hook, not a generic sign-up pitch.
- **No bra/lingerie navigation visible** — my primary category has zero visible entry points. No "Shop Bras," no size filter, nothing that connects to my reason for being here.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero rendering on mobile** — the white void on the left half is a hard stop. Verify the hero image CSS fills the full viewport width and doesn't overflow off-screen on smaller phones.
- **Surface a personalized greeting for returning members** — swap the generic REWARDS join prompt for a "Welcome back, Lacey — you have X points" module when cookies are present.
- **Add a visible bra-category shortcut above the fold** — one tappable chip ("Shop Bras," "Semi-Annual Sale," "Free Panty") anchored below the hero would drop me directly into my category without hunting the nav.
- **Make the hero headline readable** — whatever copy is attached to that pink lace bra image should be legible at mobile scale; crop or reposition so the text isn't bleeding off-screen.
