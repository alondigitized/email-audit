# 25% Off Is a Good Excuse to Shop 🙂‍↕️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink send dressed up as a single-offer email. The hero is clean — "LAST DAY / 25% OFF A $60+ PURCHASE" with a clear black SHOP NOW button — but it's followed by no fewer than five more distinct campaigns stacked underneath: Capri leggings, a backpack push, "$10 Full-Sized Beauty," the Rush Shop, Summerween Edit, Foldover Flares, and Wink of the Month, before finally landing on a full category nav grid and a Rewards/credit-card signup block. Compared to the tightly themed sends in this brand's recent run (the JanSport collab, the fragrance launch, even the Summerween panties pivot), this one lacks a single point of view.
- The subject line itself is the weak link: "25% Off Is a Good Excuse to Shop" is generic sitewide discount copy with no category hook, and it lands two days after the July 24 Summerween send which was also structured around 25% off — this reads like the second discount-flavored email in three sends, which dulls the urgency. The preview text is genuinely good, though — "Especially when there's a total Capri takeover going on rn" is real, specific copy that complements rather than repeats the subject.
- Net take: solid production values, an honest and clearly-presented top offer, but the email tries to be six emails at once and the subject line undersells the one thing (Last Day urgency) that should have been front and center.

## What's working

- Hero has clean visual hierarchy — "LAST DAY," the 25% off headline, and a single black SHOP NOW button all land above the fold with no scrolling.
- Preview text ("total Capri takeover going on rn") is distinct from the subject and gives a real reason to open, not boilerplate.
- The offer is disclosed reasonably transparently right under the CTA ("Code 25OFF60. Excludes select third-party styles. Details below.") rather than being hidden entirely.
- Secondary offers (backpacks from $35, $10 full-size beauty) are each individually well-produced with their own clear CTAs.
- Rewards/credit card module and category nav grid at the bottom give lower-intent scrollers something to land on.

## What's weak

- The subject line carries none of the urgency that's actually in the hero — "Last Day" never makes it into the subject or preview, so the strongest lever in this email is invisible from the inbox.
- Six-plus distinct campaigns in one send (Capris, backpacks, beauty, Rush Shop, Summerween Edit, Foldover Flares, Wink of the Month) dilutes the "25% off" premise the subject actually promised — a reader who opens for the discount has to scroll past a lot of unrelated pushes.
- The CTA copy is the generic "SHOP NOW" repeated across every module rather than category-specific copy, so nothing in the body differentiates one click from another.
- Coming two days after the July 24 "25% Off" Summerween send, back-to-back 25%-off framing makes this feel like a repeat rather than a fresh beat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the urgency in the subject — "Last Day" is the single strongest lever in this email and it's currently buried in the hero image only. Lead with it.
- 2. Trim the body to two offers max (25% off + one secondary), and move the rest to a "more ways to save" footer link instead of full modules — the current length dilutes the primary CTA.
- 3. Swap "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTA copy on each module (e.g. "Shop Capris," "Grab a Backpack") so clicks map to intent instead of all funneling generically.
- 4. Space this further from the July 24 Summerween 25%-off send, or differentiate the offer mechanic (BOGO, dollar-off) so two discount emails in three sends don't read as a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Day: 25% Off $60+ (Ends Tonight)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cart's About to Get 25% Cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $35 bags and $10 full-size beauty — today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Capri takeover is real. So is this discount.`
