# Attn: We Leveld-Up Our #1 Bra Collection
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- After eleven consecutive promotional emails in five days — most of them screaming 10/$40 panties or Semi-Annual Sale urgency — Victoria's Secret is making a second attempt at a product-led pivot. Yesterday's "NEW: FlexFactor Balconette" (July 8, 8/10) went completely cold on deals; today's send hedges by layering a product story hero ("More from OUR #1 COLLECTION") on top of three separate offer modules: 2x Points, Free Logo Tote, and Extra 25% Off Sale. That hedge is the email's central tension.
- The result is a kitchen-sink email that doesn't fully commit to anything. The hero is a credible product story with model photography, but the eye lands on "More from" — a trailing, passive construction — before it reaches the benefit. Then you scroll through a point-earn module, a GWP module, and a sale extension module that are each worth their own email. The brand is clearly trying to do too many things at once, and it shows.
- The typo in the subject line — "Leveld-Up" instead of "Leveled-Up" — is the kind of error that a premium brand this size should catch before send. For a list that's received twelve sends in under a week, a subject-line error signals sloppiness at exactly the wrong moment.

## What's working

- **Three distinct purchase motivations** (loyalty points, gift-with-purchase tote, extra % off sale) give different buyer types a reason to click — more than the typical single-offer send.
- **Product photography in the hero** is clean and on-brand; the "OUR #1 COLLECTION" label anchors VS's best-seller credibility.
- **Earn 2x Points module** is visually isolated in a contrasting band, which makes it scan well mid-scroll.
- **Free Logo Tote** is a smart GWP — the tote bag image is prominent and recognizable, giving the offer tangible appeal.
- **Breaking from the 10/$40 panty loop** is the right editorial call; this email at least looks different from the previous eight.

## What's weak

- **The subject line has a typo** — "Leveld-Up" is missing the second 'e'. For a brand spending this much on photography, that's a QA failure visible to everyone in the inbox before they open.
- **Kitchen-sink structure** means no single offer wins — 2x Points, Free Tote, and 25% Off are three distinct calls-to-action competing for the same scroll session.
- **"More from" hero headline is passive** — "More from OUR #1 COLLECTION" doesn't communicate the upgrade; it sounds like a catalog filler line.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTAs throughout** are generic and don't carry the product story forward; none says what you're shopping for or why now.
- **No visible deadline** on any of the offers in the email body, which undercuts urgency for the 25% Off Sale module — the one offer that would most benefit from a cutoff date.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the typo before anything else** — "Leveld-Up" → "Leveled-Up." Full stop. This should block the send.
- 2. **Commit to one hero story**: either "Our #1 Bra Just Got Better" (product upgrade, no deals) or "Upgrade your bra + earn 2x Points" (deal-led). Three competing stories dilute all three.
- 3. **Rewrite the hero headline** from the passive "More from OUR #1 COLLECTION" to an active benefit — "The Bra 10M Women Swear By, Now Even Better" or similar.
- 4. **Give the 25% Off Sale module a hard deadline** — a date or "48 hours only" so it functions as urgency rather than background noise.
- 5. **Upgrade the CTA copy** — "Shop the Upgrade" or "Find Your Fit" beats "SHOP NOW" for a product-story email.
- 6. **Subject Alt A:** `We leveled up our #1 bra. See what changed.`
- 7. **Subject Alt B:** `The bestselling bra just got better`
- 8. **Preheader Alt A:** `New fit tech, same iconic shape — plus earn 2x Points this week`
- 9. **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Logo Tote with qualifying purchase — while supplies last`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard HTML4 table-layout send via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (AMPScript markers present, `e1.victoriassecret.com` ESP domain). The primary technical concerns are a cluster of HTTP-only third-party retargeting pixels (Dotomi/Epsilon) that will be blocked by modern email clients, and authentication header gaps due to AgentMail relay interception.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 click links unprobed** — all route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirector. This is expected ESP behavior, but destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be confirmed without following the redirect chain.

**Duplicate open-pixel** — the 1×1 tracking pixel at `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?4GR7PRD4CJKEVIUGU47ITXTFEM.70249&d=70249&bmt=0` appears **twice** in the HTML source (once in the `<body>` preheader div and again inside the `#AMPScript` content block). This double-fires open events and inflates open rate metrics for this send.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**10 HTTP-only retargeting pixels blocked by default in Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook:**

All `ads.dotomi.com` cookie-sync pixels (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) use plain `http://`:
```
http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?a4edea...
```
Modern email clients either strip mixed-content resources or proxy them through image caches (Gmail strips HTTP, Apple Mail proxies — breaking the cookie-sync intent entirely). These pixels will fail silently in the majority of inbox environments.

**Companion Dotomi impression pixel also HTTP:**
```
http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...
```
Same failure mode.

**11 images missing `alt` text** — all Dotomi pixels plus one content image (`5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` on `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Decorative tracking pixels don't require alt text (empty `alt=""` is correct), but the `alt=""` attribute must be explicitly present; missing it entirely leaves screen readers to announce the full `src` URL.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — `<meta name="viewport" content="..., user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-to-zoom. This is an accessibility violation (WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4) and increasingly triggers spam filters on iOS.

**`<title>` inside `<body>`** — a `<title style="display:none">` element appears inside the body as a preheader/preview-text hack. This is structurally invalid HTML (title belongs in head) and will be ignored or rendered literally by some clients.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not found** — not captured by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) for bulk senders as of Feb 2024. If these headers are present at the SMTP layer but stripped by the relay, this is a relay configuration issue; if absent entirely, it's a sender compliance gap.

**Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown due to relay interception. `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a known Salesforce Marketing Cloud sending domain and is expected to be properly authenticated, but this cannot be confirmed from the captured headers.
