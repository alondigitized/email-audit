# Give Thanks: 40% Off Everything 🦃
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's Black Friday catalog again, now with a Thanksgiving subject line on top. It is the same "BLACK FRIDAY: THE HOLIDAYS START NOW" hero over "40% OFF EVERYTHING" that went out on 11/24 ("Black Friday's Here: 40% Off Everything") and 11/25 ("Why Wait? Black Friday's Here NOW"). Only the 🦃 and "Give Thanks" framing is new. Nothing in the body mentions Thanksgiving or gratitude.
- The stack under the hero is strong on offers but crowded: 40% off bras, 7/$35 panty party, $25 flannel pajama pants, a free XL bling tote with $100+, a tote and blanket with $150+, and free shipping on $50+. Each block has a pink/red image and a Shop Now button, so the email is easy to scan. But six near-equal offers dilute the 40% headline, and the long fine print block at the bottom is heavy. The render is only legible at a thumbnail scale in my view, so I'm judging structure, not small text.
- Verdict: a competent, reliable workhorse, but a fourth near-identical 40% send in a week. It reads as inventory, not a holiday moment.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "40% OFF EVERYTHING" is large type directly under the Black Friday header, with category chips right below it.
- Each offer sits in its own full-width block with a visible Shop Now button, so the CTAs are unambiguous.
- The free XL bling tote and tote-plus-blanket gifts-with-purchase add a tangible incentive beyond the percentage.
- The free-shipping-on-$50+ strip and a rewards "welcome back" block ($25 off) give loyalty members a reason to act.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- The "Give Thanks" and turkey framing is only in the subject. The body is unchanged Black Friday creative, so the promise and the payoff don't match.
- It is the fourth 40%-off send in four days, after 11/24 and 11/25. Subscribers will see no escalation and little urgency.
- Six offers compete, which buries the single strongest message. The two tote blocks look near-duplicate.
- "Limited time" appears on every block but no deadline is shown, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is a very long block of dense text, which undercuts the "honest offer" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Thanksgiving angle real, or drop it. Add a hero line like "Today only" or "Ends tonight", and show a countdown or deadline, since this is the last day before Black Friday.
- 2. Cut the stack to three offers: the 40% hero, one hero product (bras), and one gift-with-purchase. Merge the two tote blocks into a single tiered offer ("Free tote $100+, add a blanket at $150+").
- 3. Move the free-shipping and rewards strips up near the hero, so the benefits show before the scroll.
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" on each block with specific verbs and nouns ("Shop 40% Off Bras", "Get Flannel PJ Pants $25").
- 5. Shorten the fine print to the essentials and link out to full terms.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Thanksgiving only: 40% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bras, PJs and panties, plus a free XL bling tote with $100+. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed code defect: an unresolved `%%cellcode%%` merge token in the JSON-LD block. It also has structural HTML problems and a pile of insecure, third-party tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 57 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Their destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Cookie-sync pixels over HTTP:** 10 third-party Dotomi pixels (`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a4edea10…=1`) load over plain `http://`. A 1×1 `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` pixel and `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` do too. These can be blocked or rewritten by image proxies and mixed-content filters. Because the pixels carry a hashed email identifier (`a4edea10…=1`), a blocked load means lost attribution.
- **Pixel count:** The 10 sequential `pubN.php` pixels are a fan-out of ad-network cookie syncs. They add 12 extra third-party requests per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">` and both `<style>` blocks. HTML parsers close `<head>` at the first `<table>`. The viewport meta and the `<style>` blocks, including the `@media (max-width:480px)` responsive rules, are then parsed as body content. Clients that strip or reorder `<head>` could lose the responsive CSS.
- **Doctype:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html>` as shown). The source is truncated, so I can't confirm whether a `<title>` exists.
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. This is the only flagged image that is plausibly real content, so images-off and screen-reader users get nothing from it.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `o.gif` and the Dotomi `pub1–10.php` and UCMController pixels also lack `alt`. These are tracking pixels, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` is still correct.
- **Open pixel:** The open pixel at `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` correctly has `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Unresolved token `%%cellcode%%`:** It appears in the JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl`: `...victoriassecret.com/us/?cm_mmc=di-_-112626415-_-...&d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&dtm_email_hash=...`
- **Parameter conflict, `d_cellid`:** `offerPageUrl` in the same block resolves it correctly as `d_cellid=112626415`. So the personalization works elsewhere and this one URL was missed.
- **Parameter conflict, `mi_u`:** `mi_u` is `200492586522` in `offerPageUrl` but `112626415` in `merchantHomepageUrl`. The second value is the cell ID, so the merge field is probably mis-mapped. That would misattribute the user in downstream analytics.
- **Impact:** The token sits in structured data, so it isn't visible in the rendered email. Any annotation consumer (e.g. Gmail promotions markup) gets a malformed URL, and the attribution parameter is corrupt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** The QA flagged both as not found (RFC 8058). The QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so I can't confirm a real defect. Verify against the raw sending-side headers, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't assess them.
- **Domain mismatch:** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while links and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. This is a different subdomain and organizational domain, which is common for brand-family sends. It is only relevant if DMARC alignment fails, and I have no evidence of that.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** It uses `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp` and `mi_u` instead of `utm_*` parameters. Campaign ID `20261126_PK_6549_FULFILE` is consistent across both URLs, and `cm_mmc` embeds cell `112626415` consistently.
- **Landing page:** `offerPageUrl` points to `/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms`, a category page. I can't verify that the "40% Off" offer is shown there, since link probing was skipped.
- **Query-string identifiers:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…` (both SHA-256-length hashes) are appended to destination URLs. They are hashed rather than plaintext, but they still persist in browser history, referrers and server logs.
- **Inconsistent parameters:** The `mi_u` and `d_cellid` mismatch described in section 4 is also a continuity defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `%%cellcode%%` substitution in the JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl`. Resolve it to `112626415` and correct `mi_u`, which currently duplicates the cell ID instead of the subscriber ID (`200492586522`).
2. Move the JSON-LD wrapper table and script into `<body>`, so `<meta viewport>` and the `<style>` blocks stay in `<head>`.
3. Serve the Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixels over HTTPS, and consolidate the 10 `pubN.php` pixels or load them server-side.
4. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers from the sending ESP. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the original message.
6. Add `lang` to `<html>`. Consider moving to an HTML5 doctype if the ESP allows it.
7. Run an HTTP probe of a sample of the 57 skipped tracking links, to confirm redirects reach the intended `victoriassecret.com/us/pink/...` destinations.
