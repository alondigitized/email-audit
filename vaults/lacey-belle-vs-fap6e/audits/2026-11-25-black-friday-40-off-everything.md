# Black Friday = 40% Off EVERYTHING
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the full Black Friday kitchen-sink send. The "40% Off Everything" headline sits in a stack of offers: a free tote and blanket, a free tote, 7/$35 panties, 40% off bras, 5/$40 mists, and free shipping at $50. The 11/24 send "40% Off Everything—Black Friday is Finally HERE" already made the 40% move. This subject is a near-duplicate of it, sent a day later with the equals sign swapped in for the em dash.
- The email opens with a free tote and blanket gift-with-purchase, not the 40% discount. The two "Limited Time" blocks above the fold (the free tote and blanket, then the free tote) push "40% Off Everything" to second place. After that the email runs on the same module pattern as the week's earlier sends, with big headlines, black "SHOP NOW" buttons and a category row.
- Verdict: it's competent and on-season, but it's the third near-identical 40% message in about 24 hours. It reads as inbox noise, not a new reason to open. Caveat: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so the fine print and small copy were not legible to me. This review covers layout, hierarchy and headline copy only.

## What's working

- The "BLACK FRIDAY" banner over a pink gift-set image makes the season obvious right away.
- "40% Off Everything" is set large, with category pills (Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear) beneath it.
- Every module has a visible, consistent black "SHOP NOW" button.
- The 7/$35 panty block has a size selector (XS to XXL), which gets the shopper closer to a purchase.
- The rewards and $25 off module near the bottom gives members something to act on.
- The render looks clean, with no broken images or overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- The free tote and blanket gift-with-purchase sits above the 40% offer, so the most-promoted number in the subject isn't the first thing you see.
- There are too many competing offers: free tote, free tote and blanket, 40% off, 7/$35, bras, 5/$40 mists, and free shipping. The eye has nowhere to settle.
- The subject repeats the 11/24 "40% Off Everything" send almost word for word. There's no new hook for someone who already saw it.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". None names a product or category.
- The email is very long, and a lot of fine print and exclusion language sits at the bottom.

- 1. Lead with one hero. Put "40% Off Everything" first and move the tote gift-with-purchase to a slim banner below it. Give the free-shipping and rewards perks a single strip.
- 2. Give the subject a new angle: a countdown, a gift-with-purchase hook, or a "last chance" frame.
- 3. Make CTAs specific, such as "Shop 40% Off Bras" and "Get the Free Tote".
- 4. Cut the module count roughly in half, and suppress or segment sends to people who already clicked 11/24.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Victoria's Secret, "Black Friday = 40% Off EVERYTHING"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based layout, a 1x1 open pixel and heavy third-party cookie-sync pixels. Confirmed problems are invalid document structure, a CSS class mismatch, about 11 insecure and unlabeled Dotomi pixels, and a SHA-256 email hash in third-party URLs. Compliance and authentication can't be verified from the supplied data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 64 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the QA probe, so link validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 pixels load over `http://`. Clients that block mixed content may drop them.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Hashed email in the pixel URLs:** Each `pub*.php` URL carries `?a4edea106b33...6fe=1`. That value is the same 64-hex hash as `dtm_email_hash=4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe` in the JSON-LD URL. A hashed identifier is sent over cleartext HTTP to an ad-tech domain, and it is persistent and linkable. This is a privacy and compliance exposure.
- **Open pixel:** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is a standard 1x1 pixel with `alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid structure:** A `<table>` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and the `<style>` blocks. Parsers may auto-close `<head>` early, which can drop the meta and style rules that follow. A `<title style="display:none">` with empty content also appears inside `<body>`.
- **Class name mismatch:** The HTML uses `class="content_padding"` (underscore), but the mobile CSS defines `.content-padding` (hyphen). The `padding:4px !important` mobile rule never applies.
- **Invalid CSS selectors and values:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{...}` has no leading dot, so it targets a non-existent element. The `.ExternalClass` rules below it are correct.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an element selector that matches nothing.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
- **Missing alt text:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. The 11 Dotomi pixels also lack `alt`. These are invisible tracking images, so they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch zoom (WCAG 1.4.4) in clients that honor it.
- **Layout tables:** Only some tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties`) don't, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Missing attributes:** No `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`. The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values are rendered: `customerID=`, `mi_u=200492586522` and `dtm_email_hash=`. The source is truncated, so the body, preheader and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so these are probably capture artifacts, not sender defects. SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe are unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **Unverified in-body requirements:** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.
- **Privacy:** See section 2. The email-hash disclosure over HTTP to third parties is the one compliance-relevant finding the HTML confirms.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No standard UTM parameters:** Links use a proprietary scheme (`cm_mmc=di-_-112526515-_-VSD_VSL_20261125_VS_6546_FULLFILE-_-`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`, `customerID`). Attribution is consistent within VS's own system, but it won't populate GA `utm_source/medium/campaign` unless the site maps the `cm_mmc` params.
- **Landing page:** The only destination visible is the JSON-LD URL, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/bras`. It is a category page, and the subject line says "EVERYTHING." That page wasn't fetched, so its alignment with the offer is unverified.
- **Long URLs:** The `customerID` and `dtm_email_hash` parameters make each URL very long, but that is not a defect on its own.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the `http://` ones. Stop putting the raw email hash in third-party query strings.
2. Move `<meta>`, `<style>` and the JSON-LD into a valid `<head>`, and keep the layout tables in `<body>`. Remove the stray `<title>` from the body.
3. Change `.content-padding` to `.content_padding`, or the reverse, so they match. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector and remove `min-width{}` and `max-height:auto`.
4. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-...png`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Add `role="presentation"` to the outer layout tables and `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Re-run it on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer.
7. Map `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp` to UTM parameters if cross-tool attribution is needed.
