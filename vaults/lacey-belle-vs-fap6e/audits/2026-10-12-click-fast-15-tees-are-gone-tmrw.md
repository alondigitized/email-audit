# Click Fast ⚡ $15 Tees Are Gone Tmrw
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the third $15 Tees send in three days, after 10-10 ("Not a Typo‼️ Limited Time: $15 Tees") and 10-11 ("OMG, $15 Tees? Yes, Please"). It is a straight deadline play. The hero reads "LAST DAY / $15 TEES", and the subject says they're "Gone Tmrw". The offer is clear and the urgency is real, but a subscriber who has seen the same tee creative since Saturday has little new to react to.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. It stacks $35 sweatshirts, the Bra Event (Buy 2, Get 1 Free), $10 full-size beauty and a six-tile category grid. The "STEAL THE DEAL" banner at the top also carries a "FREE Panty loading… download the app" line, which competes with the tee message. The "last day" signal is spread across too many offers. Once the reader scrolls past the first two blocks, nothing else says "ending today".
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand deadline send, and a 7. Its weakness is fatigue and a diluted hierarchy, not execution. There are no render bugs, but there is a lot of fine print.

## What's working

- "LAST DAY" over "$15 TEES" in the hero puts the price and the deadline in one glance.
- The subject's "Gone Tmrw" matches the hero's "Last Day", so the urgency is consistent and credible.
- The first "SHOP NOW" button is clear and sits right under the tee strip.
- The three-model tee strip shows several styles, which suggests variety within the $15 price.
- The pink palette and model photography are on-brand, and the render has no broken images or layout breaks.

## What's weak

- Tees have led since 10-10, so the creative feels recycled. The hero doesn't change from the earlier sends in a way that rewards a returning subscriber.
- The top banner mixes in a "FREE Panty loading… download the app and create an account" line. That is a second offer and an app-signup ask before the reader reaches the tees.
- The $35 sweatshirts block repeats the same "Shop Now" CTA, and the Bra Event and $10 beauty blocks follow it. Four offers sit at similar visual weight, and none of the lower ones carry a deadline.
- The CTAs read "Shop Now" or "Don't Foam-o!". They aren't specific about what the reader gets.
- Every offer has "Orig. up to…" and exclusion fine print, and the legal block at the bottom is very long. That undercuts the honest-offer feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the tee hero and one supporting block (sweatshirts), and move the Bra Event, beauty and category grid to the next send. That removes the dilution.
- 2. Make the CTA specific: "Shop $15 Tees" instead of "Shop Now". Add a countdown or an "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" line beside it.
- 3. Remove the app-signup/"FREE Panty loading" line from the top banner, or move it below the fold, so the first screen carries one ask.
- 4. Vary the creative against 10-10 and 10-11. Use a different model or colorway, or add a "best-sellers" or "sizes selling out" hook, so the third send in a row isn't a repeat.
- 5. Put the price cap in plain words near the hero ("Select styles, orig. up to $39") instead of leaving it only in the footnote.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup is mostly standard ESP output (600px container, mobile media queries, MSO fix). Its main problems are a malformed `<head>`, about 10 insecure, alt-less tracking pixels, and compliance headers that the QA run could not capture. Because of that capture gap, the compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed from this data. The QA pass rate was 57% (3 warnings).

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **45 tracking links were not probed.** The HTTP probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified. The one that was visible, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms/offer?cm_mmc=di-_-101226445-_-VSD_VSL_20261012_PK_6297_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=101226445&...`, resolves to a single "offer" URL.
- **The same URL is repeated for all three JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries.** Each one has a different `image` but the identical `url`. If these are meant to be distinct promos, the URLs are wrong. If not, the structured data adds no value.
- **Insecure (`http://`) tracking pixels:**
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261012_PK_6297_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`

  Clients that block mixed content or proxy images may drop these, which would skew open and retargeting data.
- **Dotomi `cookieredir` calls (`pub1`–`pub10`) are third-party cookie-sync pixels.** They add 10 extra requests, and the `a4edea106b…=1` query string carries the recipient's hash.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text on 13 images:**
  - `o.gif`
  - the `login.dotomi.com` UCMController pixel
  - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`

  Of these, only the `.png` is likely a visible content image, so it is the only one where missing alt matters for accessibility. The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The `open.aspx` pixel already has `alt=""`, so this is inconsistent.
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table>` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block sits inside `<head>`, ahead of the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. A table is not valid in `<head>`, and browsers will usually move it into `<body>`. Some email clients may handle this inconsistently. The JSON-LD `<script>` is also commonly stripped, so it is unlikely to work outside Gmail.
- **The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with XHTML-style self-closing tags** (`<meta ... />`). This is minor.
- **Malformed CSS in the first `<style>` block.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both rules are ignored.
- **Viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`.** This prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Mobile `font-size: 16px !important` is set on `body`, and `max-height:auto` is invalid CSS.**

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **PII in URLs.** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` are 64-character hex strings (SHA-256-style) and appear in plain query strings on every link. They are hashed rather than raw emails, but they are persistent identifiers that can leak through referrers and shared links.
- The source is truncated, so tokens further down the body can't be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, and it should not be treated as a sender defect without the raw headers.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** The caveat is the same. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while the tracking and image hosts are on `victoriassecret.com`. That is a normal ESP setup, but it can't be verified here.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated section.** CAN-SPAM compliance can't be confirmed from the data provided.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **Tracking uses Cheetah-style parameters rather than UTMs.** The parameters are `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp`. There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` values. That is a deliberate platform choice, but it means the standard analytics attribution won't work without mapping.
- **Campaign ID consistency.** `20261012_PK_6297_FILEBAL` is consistent across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and the pixels.
- **Landing page alignment.** The landing URL path is `/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms/offer`. It matches the "$15 Tees" subject at the category level. The destination page was not probed, so offer validity and expiry can't be verified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Move the JSON-LD block into `<body>` or remove it from `<head>`. Make each `PromotionCard` `url` distinct, or drop the duplicates.
2. Switch all tracking pixels to `https://`. Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` both support it.
3. Add `alt=""` to the 12 tracking pixels, and a descriptive `alt` to `5bcec3ac-…png` if it is content.
4. Fix the malformed selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`), and drop `max-height:auto`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy, before calling these compliance failures.
7. Consider reducing the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels from 10 to those actually needed.
