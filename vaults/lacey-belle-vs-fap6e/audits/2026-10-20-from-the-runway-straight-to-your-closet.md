## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table layout with a 1x1 open pixel, but it has malformed document structure, about 11 insecure (`http://`) third-party Dotomi pixels with no alt text, and email-hash identifiers in URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`, with no `alt` attribute.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a4edea10…=1`
  - Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop them, so cookie syncing fails silently. Apple Mail Privacy Protection and Gmail's image proxy may also prefetch them.
- **Hashed email in URLs:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` and `customerID=43cece97…1e2f` appear in the JSON-LD `url`. The same hash appears as the parameter name in each `pub*.php` pixel URL (`?a4edea10…=1`). The hashed address is sent to a third-party ad-sync domain on every open, and it persists in any forwarded copy of the message. This is a privacy and data-sharing exposure.
- **Unprobed links:** 57 tracking links were skipped, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified. This is not a confirmed defect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?…` is HTTPS with `width="1" height="1" alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>`:** A `<table>…<script type="application/ld+json">…</table>` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta name="viewport">` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`. Parsers will end `<head>` at the `<table>`, so those meta tags land in `<body>` and may be ignored. The charset declaration may therefore not take effect, and the same applies to the viewport tag. Gmail's JSON-LD parsing may also be affected by the relocated markup.
- **Elements in the wrong place:** `<title style="display:none">` (empty) and a second `<style>` block are inside the body table.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{` has no leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are dead rules.
- **Zoom disabled:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt:** `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (`image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/…`) has no alt text. It is a content image, not a tracking pixel.
- **Doctype and layout:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is acceptable for email. Layout tables in the visible markup use `role="presentation"` on the Salesforce Marketing Cloud blocks, but the outer container tables do not.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is unconfirmed and not a verified sender defect. Verify against raw headers, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` and the click domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` share a subdomain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- The visible, truncated HTML has no footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The tracked URL is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/fashion-show/shop-the-show?cm_mmc=di-_-102026525-_-VSD_VSL_20261020_VS_6350_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=102026525&mi_ecmp=20261020_VS_6350_FILEBAL…`.
- It uses a proprietary scheme (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`) and no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent across the campaign ID `20261020_VS_6350_FILEBAL` and is not a defect, but it won't attribute in GA unless the destination maps these parameters.
- The path `/us/pink/fashion-show/shop-the-show` matches the Fashion Show subject theme. The landing page was not probed, so its status is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block, meta tags and `<title>` into a proper `<head>` and remove the `<table>` from it.
2. Serve all Dotomi pixels over `https://` and add `alt=""`. Consider whether the 11-pixel cookie-sync chain is worth the privacy cost.
3. Remove the email hash and `customerID` from URL query strings where possible, or confirm a data-sharing disclosure covers them.
4. Fix `.ReadMsgBody` and delete the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport tag.
6. Add alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
7. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw headers (direct SMTP capture or the ESP's seed-test tool). Also check the full HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
