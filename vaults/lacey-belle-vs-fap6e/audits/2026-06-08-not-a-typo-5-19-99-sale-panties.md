# Not a Typo: 5/$19.99 Sale Panties
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- This is VS's third pass at the 5/$19.99 panties price point in three days — after June 6's "5/$19.99 Panties—We Repeat, 5/$19.99" (7/10) and the broader SAS drumbeat running since June 3. The "Not a Typo" subject hook is the freshest creative decision here: it signals a legitimately surprising price rather than just re-announcing it. But the email body doesn't capitalize on that energy — it falls back into the same multi-offer stack (panties → activewear → $15 off coupon → panties again → bra fit → wedding → fragrance → category grid) that VS has been deploying all week.
- The panties hero is clean and the price is impossible to miss. The problem is what follows: a half-dozen competing modules dilute the message. A subscriber who has opened every SAS email this week already knows the deal; they need a reason to act *today*, and this email doesn't supply one.
- The $15 off $100+ coupon block appearing mid-email is a holdover from yesterday's coupon sends. Stacking it here without a clear expiration creates confusion — is it still live? Did it expire at midnight? That ambiguity actively works against click-through.

## What's working

- **"Not a Typo" subject framing** is the standout creative beat — implying the price is almost too good to be real is a smarter hook than the literal repeat-announcement from June 6.
- **5/$19.99 hero is immediately legible** — leopard panty image, large price text, and SHOP NOW button stack correctly; zero guesswork about the offer.
- **Activewear module** adds category breadth without muddying the lead; the Up to 60% Off SALE badge is consistent with the rest of the SAS visual system.
- **Consistent SAS visual identity** — hot pink SALE badges, clean white backgrounds, and the VS logo treatment are all on-brand and render without breakage.

## What's weak

- **No deadline anywhere in the email** — four modules, zero expiration dates. For the third reprise of the same price point, "ends tonight" or "48 hours left" is the only lever left to pull.
- **$15 off $100+ coupon mid-email is confusing** — it appeared in yesterday's sends; a subscriber doesn't know if it's still redeemable or stale, and there's no visible expiry.
- **Too many competing offers**: 5/$19.99 panties → 60% off activewear → $15 off $100 → another 60% off panties → bra fit guide → wedding shop → fragrance. The email tries to close everyone in one send; it closes no one well.
- **SHOP NOW CTAs are generic** — five or six instances of "SHOP NOW" with no differentiation. Even "Shop 5 for $19.99" on the hero button would sharpen the click intent.
- **No social proof or urgency signal** — after three days of the same price, a single "X,000 sold this week" stat or a low-stock badge would do real work.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or a countdown badge above the CTA. This is the only thing that differentiates today's email from Wednesday's.
- 2. **Kill the mid-email $15 off coupon block or update it** — if it's still live, give it an expiry; if it expired yesterday, remove it entirely. Stale coupons destroy trust.
- 3. **Rename the hero CTA** — change the first SHOP NOW button to "Shop 5 for $19.99" so it mirrors the offer language and removes ambiguity about where the click leads.
- 4. **Cut the email in half** — drop the bra fit guide, wedding shop, and fragrance modules to a single row teaser. The SAS message is strongest when focused; omnibus emails feel like a clearance dump.
- 5. **Add a low-stock or popularity signal** — "Selling fast" or "X styles left" next to the hero panty image is free urgency that doesn't require a deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale Panties Under $4 Each — Today Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 for $19.99: Panties Sale Ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix & match styles — as low as $3.99 each while they last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Every style, every color — 5 for $19.99 through the weekend`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open pixel**
The 1×1 tracking pixel at `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?SPBRLWJCRT5E5KXYHVZWA7PROI.70251&d=70251&bmt=0` fires **twice** in the HTML — once in the `<body>` preheader div and again inside the `#AMPScript` content block. This will double-count opens for this send.

**20 click-redirect links not probed**
All CTA links route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. QA skipped HTTP probing on all 20; destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be verified programmatically.

**10 Dotomi retargeting pixels — HTTP only**
`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` use plain HTTP. Major email clients (Gmail, Apple Mail) block mixed-content image loads by default. These pixels will silently fail for a significant portion of recipients.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 failure; blocks low-vision users from enlarging content.

**`<title>` element inside `<td>`**
```html
<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>
```
Placed mid-body inside a table cell — invalid HTML placement. Some parsers will surface this as visible text or ignore it unpredictably.

**Missing alt text — 11 images**
- `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (content image, `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`) — no `alt` attribute
- All 10 Dotomi `pub1–pub10.php` pixels — no `alt=""` (should be empty string, not absent)

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
```html
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>
```
Legacy doctype. Not a rendering blocker in practice given the Outlook conditional comments are present, but flags as non-standard.

**Empty AMPScript block**

Appears to be an unpopulated dynamic content slot. Not rendered, but confirms a template variable region that contributed no output — worth confirming intent vs. omission.
