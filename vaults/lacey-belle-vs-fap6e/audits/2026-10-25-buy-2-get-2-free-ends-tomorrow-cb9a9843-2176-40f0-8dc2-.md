# Buy 2, Get 2 Free Ends Tomorrow
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "Buy 2, Get 2 Free" send in six days, and it is the first one with real urgency. A red "LAST DAY" panel at the top reads "Buy 2, Get 2 Free Everything," with "Just in time for holiday shopping." Category pills sit underneath for Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty, Lingerie and Activewear, plus a "Shop All" button and a "Join for Free" button. The offer is clear and the deadline is credible. Compared with 10/24's "Buy 2, Get 2 Free Right Now," "Ends Tomorrow" is a better subject.
- Below the hero, the email drifts into a long catalog. It has a "Holiday Approved Bras" block, a "Set(s) for the Season" block, an "$8 Mists & Lotions" LIMITED TIME block, and then a stack of tiles for Fashion Show, Bestselling Bras, Gift Shop and Pajama Sets. A rewards module, a "Now Trending" grid and a very long legal block follow. The $8 Mists offer competes with the main promo, and the page is so long that the "last day" message is forgotten by the middle.
- Verdict: the top screen is strong and the subject is the best of the week. The rest is the same recycled catalog the brand has sent for six days, so I'd expect modest clicks and some fatigue.

## What's working

- The red "LAST DAY" panel gives a clear offer with a credible deadline. It is the first thing the eye lands on after the model hero.
- The category pills under the offer let a shopper jump to bras, panties, sleep, beauty and other categories.
- The subject now carries real urgency ("Ends Tomorrow"), where the earlier sends only said "Right Now" or "Starts Now."
- The photography is polished, and the Holiday Approved Bras and Set(s) for the Season blocks fit the season.
- The "Member Days" hero badge and the rewards module reinforce loyalty benefits.

## What's weak

- This is the sixth send of the same offer in six days, going back to 10/21 "Buy 2, Get 2 FREE Starts Now." The fatigue is obvious, and "Ends Tomorrow" is also the day-before wording rather than "ends tonight."
- The "LAST DAY" panel says "Ends Tomorrow" in the subject, but the in-email label is "LAST DAY," which is slightly inconsistent.
- The $8 Mists & Lotions "LIMITED TIME" block is a second headline offer, and it dilutes the main one.
- The email is extremely long, with many stacked tiles, a Fashion Show section and a trending grid. Nothing after the first screen reinforces the deadline.
- The legal copy at the bottom is large and dense. The first-screen offer says "Excludes select third-party dates and gift cards" in small text, and that exclusion is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to about three blocks: the LAST DAY hero, one best-seller grid of Buy 2, Get 2 items, and the rewards module. Remove the Fashion Show, Gift Shop and Pajama tiles.
- 2. Repeat the deadline with a countdown or "Ends tonight at 11:59 PT" on the product blocks, and move the $8 Mists offer out of this send.
- 3. Make the CTA specific. Replace "Shop All" with "Shop Buy 2, Get 2 Free," and use product-level links on the bra tiles.
- 4. Give the series an arc. After six sends of the same offer, vary the lead creative, or suppress people who clicked earlier in the week.
- 5. State the exclusions near the hero, not only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: Buy 2, Get 2 Free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight: your free 2 bras end`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick any 4 bras, panties or sleep. You pay for 2. Ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free items vanish after the holiday sale ends. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with 56 click-tracked links, a Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixel block, and malformed document structure. The automated QA pass rate is 57%. The warnings are mostly non-HTTPS and alt-less tracking pixels, plus header checks that the AgentMail relay likely prevented.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 56 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations or HTTP status codes were verified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - These may be blocked or rewritten by mail clients, and they can trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail.
  - Because they are third-party cookie-sync pixels, a blocked load silently breaks retargeting attribution.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` is a SHA-256 email hash. It appears in the JSON-LD `url` and in the `pub*.php` pixel query strings (`?a4edea106…=1`). `customerID=43cece97…` also appears in the JSON-LD `url`.
  - Any link-forwarding or screenshot exposes a stable user identifier.
  - The hashed email is sent to a third-party ad domain on image load.
- **Open pixel:** The `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` 1×1 image is correctly sized with `alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 11 images have no `alt` attribute: the Dotomi `UCMController` pixel, `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, and `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
  - The pixels are non-visual and should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
  - The `.png` is a content-host image, so it needs a real `alt` unless it is decorative.
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table>` with a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<style>`. Browsers and clients may force-close `<head>` early and drop the meta and style tags that follow. The `<html>` element also has no `lang` attribute.
- **Title in body:** `<title style="display:none…"></title>` is empty and sits inside the body.
- **Broken CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches no element.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a nonexistent element.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize concern.
- **Legacy doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional. This works in email clients but is outdated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear in the truncated HTML. The personalization parameters in the URLs (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header (RFC 8058) was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The sender domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` and the click domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` are aligned in the visible source.
- **Footer not verifiable:** The HTML was truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** The one visible destination is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/giftguide/shine`. It carries proprietary parameters instead: `cm_mmc=di-_-102526515-_-VSD_VSL_20261025_VS_6389_FILEBAL-_`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp=20261025_VS_6389_FILEBAL` and `dtmc_email_category`. This is consistent with an Adobe/Coremetrics setup, but it will not appear in GA4 channel reports without mapping.
- **Landing path unchecked:** The subject promises "Buy 2, Get 2 Free", but the only visible URL is a `/giftguide/shine` path. The other 56 click-tracked destinations were not resolved, so I can't confirm any link lands on a page that matches the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to all Dotomi pixels. Switch them to `https://`, or confirm Dotomi supports it.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
3. Move the `<table>` and JSON-LD `<script>` out of `<head>`. Place the meta and style tags first, and add `<html lang="en">`.
4. Fix or remove the dotless `ReadMsgBody{}` and `min-width{}` selectors. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Review whether the SHA-256 email hash and `customerID` in URLs are needed for the third-party pixels.
6. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from the raw `.eml`. If the relay strips them, add a capture path to the monitor.
7. Resolve the redirect chains of the click-tracked links with a follow-redirect probe. This confirms the destinations and checks that the campaign parameters survive to the landing page.
