# 10/$40—Perfect Pairs to Wear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This one delivers exactly what the subject promises, right away: "10/$40 PANTY PARTY," "100+ new choices," a size selector (XS–XXXL), and a "SHOP ALL" button sit above the fold with a colorful lace/mesh panty spread. That's the correct playbook — same one that worked in the 2026-09-03 "10/$40 Panties" sends (7.00/10 both times).
- Where it falls down is everything after the fold. The email is a kitchen-sink send: after the panty hero it pivots to jewel-tone loungewear, then bras with a "buy one get $10 off" in-store exclusive, then $25 tee bras, then a full Scent Event block ($39.95 EDP, $6.95 travel sprays) — the same fragrance pitch from 2026-09-04 and 2026-09-05 — then a category nav grid, then a rewards/trending footer. The subject's promise gets diluted fast; by the time a reader hits Scent Event they've forgotten why they opened the email.
- Net: strong open-to-first-click setup, weak follow-through. This is a slightly-worse cousin of the two 09-03 panty sends — same hero mechanics, but more clutter competing underneath.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("10/$40 PANTY PARTY") is visible immediately with no scrolling, matching the subject exactly.
- Size-inclusive selector (XS–XXXL) is shown right under the hero, addressing a real product-fit question before the click.
- "SHOP ALL" is a clear, unambiguous single CTA on the hero panel.
- Fine print (orig. price, exclusions) is present but small and unobtrusive rather than buried in a wall of legal text.
- No render bugs — all images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The email tries to sell five different categories (panties, loungewear, bras, tee bras, fragrance) in one send — the hero offer gets buried under unrelated pitches by the second scroll.
- Scent Event reappears for the third straight day (09-04, 09-05, now 09-06) with identical $39.95/$6.95 pricing — this is fatigue territory, not a fresh beat.
- Visual hierarchy competes with itself: the purple bra model, the pink Scent Event banner, and the panty spread all have similar visual weight, so the eye doesn't land cleanly on one primary offer after the hero.
- "$25 Sexy Tee Bras" and "Get One For $10 Off" bra offers are introduced without any bridge from the panty theme, making the email feel like a stitched-together catalog rather than a "Panty Party" campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the panty offer plus one complementary upsell (e.g., bras) and move loungewear/fragrance to separate sends — this send is doing the job of three emails.
- 2. Drop or heavily de-prioritize the Scent Event block; it's run three days in a row and is diluting a panty-specific campaign that has nothing to do with fragrance.
- 3. If multi-category is a business requirement, add a subhead like "More to Complete the Look" before the second/third product blocks so the pivot doesn't feel jarring.
- 4. Move the rewards/loyalty block ("Join Now (For Free)") higher — it's currently buried near the footer where most readers won't scroll.
