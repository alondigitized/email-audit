# Fall's Best Bras—Find Yours Today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- "Fall's Best Bras—Find Yours Today" opens on a genuinely strong hero — a floral-lace Lacie Balconette bra on a model against a saturated blue backdrop, with a clean "SHOP NOW" button right below "Falling for LACIE BRAS." That's a better subject-to-hero match than most of the recent sends in this brand history, which have mostly been "kitchen sink" torn-paper sale templates (2026-09-25, 2026-09-22, 2026-09-18) with the offer stapled on top.
- But it doesn't stay disciplined. Right after the bra hero, the email pivots hard into "Just Dropped: Backstage Fashion Show Merch" (an App Exclusive, so email readers can't even act on it without leaving to the app), then a nine-tile category grid, a rewards/SMS signup block, and a six-tile "Now Trending" grid. By the third scroll, this is functionally the same long, undifferentiated catalog dump as 2026-09-28's panty sale email — the subject promised bras, but bras are maybe 15% of the actual content.
- Net: good hero, weak follow-through, and a subject line that oversells focus the body doesn't deliver on.

## What's working

- Hero image is sharp, on-brand, and directly visualizes the subject line and product name (Lacie Balconette).
- Single clear CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits immediately under the hero with no scroll needed.
- "Two Days Only / Save $50 When You Spend $175+" banner up top gives a concrete, specific offer with a real deadline.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- The "Backstage Fashion Show Merch" section is App Exclusive — email readers get a CTA ("Shop Early in the App") they can't fulfill in the channel they're already in.
- Subject promises bras; body delivers one bra section then a 9-tile full-catalog grid (sleep, beauty, activewear, swim, loungewear) that has nothing to do with bras.
- "Now Trending" repeats the same sprawl pattern seen across recent sends — six more products stacked at the bottom with no new offer.
- The top offer banner (Save $50/$175+) is disconnected from the bra content below it — no bra-specific pricing or bundle tie-in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the App Exclusive Fashion Show Merch section entirely from this send, or gate it behind its own dedicated email — it actively works against email conversion here.
- 2. Follow the bra hero with 2-3 more bra styles/colors before pivoting to anything else, so the body matches the subject line's promise.
- 3. Tie the $50-off banner directly to bras (e.g., "Mix & match 2 Lacie Bras, save $X") instead of a generic sitewide spend threshold.
- 4. Trim the category grid down to bra-adjacent categories only (Bras, Lingerie, Sleep) rather than the full 9-tile sitewide grid.
