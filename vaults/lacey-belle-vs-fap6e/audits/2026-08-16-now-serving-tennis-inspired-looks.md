# Now Serving: Tennis-Inspired Looks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "tennis-inspired looks," and the hero delivers — four models in coordinated tennis-court activewear (racquets in hand, matching skirt/bra sets) under "Serving in Style," with a "Shop New Arrivals" CTA. That's a genuine, on-theme creative moment, a rarity in this brand's recent run of interchangeable "% off" sends. But it's buried: the actual top of the email is the same generic 25%-off-$75+ banner this brand has run daily since 08-10, and it eats the first screen before the tennis story even starts.
- Below the tennis section it reverts fully to kitchen-sink mode — a Whipped Mists & Lotions push, a new boyshort panty launch, a nine-tile category grid, rewards footer, trending strip. None of that is wrong, but it dilutes what could've been a tight, seasonal, differentiated send. This is a top-of-funnel creative win wrapped in the same bottom-of-funnel filler as every other VS email this week.
- Verdict: better creative than the 08-10 through 08-15 run, but the offer banner and category dump still crowd out the one genuinely fresh idea in the email.

## What's working

- The tennis hero is a real seasonal/occasion tie-in — matching court-ready sets, racquets as props, cohesive color story (lime, green, lavender) — not stock lifestyle filler.
- "Serving in Style" is a decent, on-brand pun that ties directly to the subject line.
- The $8 Whipped Mists & Lotions module has a clear, specific price point and a visible "SHOP NOW" button.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- The subject is about tennis, but the very first thing in the inbox render is a generic "Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" banner — same offer language as 08-15, 08-13, 08-12. Subject and hero don't match on open.
- The tennis section has no visible CTA copy tied to "tennis" specifically — "Shop New Arrivals" is generic and undersells the occasion angle.
- After the tennis moment, the email sprawls into mists/lotions, boyshort panties, and a nine-category grid — none of it reinforces "tennis," so the theme evaporates fast.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the sixth similarly-structured "hero + modules + grid + rewards footer" send in a week, and the top offer banner is copy-pasted from three prior sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the tennis hero, not the 25%-off banner — if the offer must stay, shrink it to a thin top strip so the tennis creative is the first full-width thing a reader sees. This is the single biggest lift since the subject line's promise should be delivered immediately.
- 2. Give the tennis section its own specific CTA — "Shop Tennis Looks" instead of "Shop New Arrivals" — so click intent matches the subject.
- 3. Cut or shrink the mists/lotions and boyshort modules in this specific send, or move them to a "you may also like" strip below the fold, so the email reads as a themed tennis edit rather than the usual kitchen sink.
- 4. Rotate the top offer banner copy — five straight days of "25% off $75+ / Limited Time" is training subscribers to skim past it.
