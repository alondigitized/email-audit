- **No render bugs.** Clean layout, no overlapping text, no empty image slots, consistent margins.
- **Seasonal timing.** Late-May jersey collection launch for summer wear is on-calendar.

## What's weak

- **The 😴 emoji contradicts the jersey pitch.** A sleep emoji against "New Jersey Collection" creates a mixed signal — is this activewear or sleepwear? The connection to the "Sleep From $35" section below is too buried to justify it.
- **Multi-section format dilutes the collection launch.** Beauty cross-sell and "Bottoms Up" mid-email treat this like a promotional digest, not a collection debut. The launch deserves a single-hero email.

## Technical Audit — PINK "Light as Air Jersey" Drop (2026-05-20)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email uses a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with table-based layout and inline CSS — standard for broad ESP compatibility. The primary technical concerns are a cluster of HTTP (non-HTTPS) retargeting pixels, missing alt attributes on tracking images, and unverifiable authentication state due to AgentMail relay header stripping.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**HTTP mixed-content pixels — 11 images affected**

All Dotomi/Criteo retargeting pixels and the MI open-tracking pixel load over HTTP:

- `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` — open pixel
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…` — retargeting signal
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` — 10 audience sync pixels

Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients enforce HTTPS-only image loading. These pixels will silently fail to fire on a significant share of recipients, undercounting opens and poisoning retargeting audiences.

**20 click-tracking links** route through `click.victoriassecret.com` and `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` — normal for Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Not flagged as errors; skipped by HTTP probe as expected.

**Plain-text body is URL-saturated**: 9,373 of 10,333 characters (91%) are URLs. This is characteristic of auto-generated plain-text from HTML and will trip spam heuristics in Barracuda, SpamAssassin, and some Gmail filters.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 15 images confirmed**

Tracking and decorative images universally omit `alt=""`:
- Open pixel: `a1cac855-81dd-4123-935f-16f9fddf9307` (no alt)
- MI pixel: `o.gif` (no alt)
- Dotomi pixels: `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (no alt on any)
- Content images: `824a57aaa9e48e40.png`, `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (no alt)

For tracking pixels, `alt=""` with `aria-hidden="true"` is the correct pattern (the MI pixel in the HTML does include `aria-hidden="true"` but omits the `alt` attribute — both are required). For content images, descriptive alt text is required for screen reader users.

**Doctype**: `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is intentional for Outlook compatibility but means no HTML5 semantic elements. Acceptable for email; noted for context.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**: Disables user zoom on mobile. Accessibility failure for low-vision users — WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%first_name%%`) visible in the truncated source. The campaign ID `DV_20260520_PK_5475_FILEBAL` is consistently present across all pixel URLs, indicating merge was applied before send. No issues found.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header — not confirmed**

`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) headers were not detected. These may be stripped by the AgentMail relay rather than absent from the original send, but their absence in the received message means:

- Gmail will not render the native one-click unsubscribe UI
- Yahoo/Google bulk sender requirements (enforced for >5k/day senders) mandate `List-Unsubscribe-Post`

**Authentication headers — unverifiable**

`Authentication-Results` header absent from the relay capture. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status for `e2.vspink.com` cannot be confirmed. Victoria's Secret is a high-volume sender and almost certainly has these configured, but the audit cannot attest to it from available data.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**: Not visible in truncated source — cannot confirm. Typically appears in footer; assumed present but not verified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be verified without the full link set (20 links skipped by probe). The campaign identifier `DV_20260520_PK_5475_FILEBAL` appears in pixel URLs as `mi_ecmp` — this is an internal ESP tracking token, not a UTM parameter.
