## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`). It is a table-based 600px layout with a mobile `@media` fallback. The confirmed problems are 10 insecure, alt-less third-party Dotomi pixels, one content image without alt text, and several invalid markup and CSS patterns. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed. The QA run scored 57% pass, with 0 issues and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** The click-redirect domain was skipped by the QA check, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page 200s are unverified.
- **Two open pixels fire.** Both are `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=70264&bmt=0`, with different `qs` values. One is in the top `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">` and one is inside `.stylingblock-content-wrapper`. This is redundant and may double-count opens.
- **10 third-party cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`.**
  - They are `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…&dtmc_email_category=20261102_VS_6427_FILEBAL` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - They are non-HTTPS, so clients or proxies that enforce HTTPS may block them. Image proxies such as Gmail's may also stop the cookie-sync from working.
  - They also load a third-party ad-tech vendor's tracking from inside the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 11 images.** The 10 Dotomi pixels above have no `alt`. Content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` also has none. The content image is the one that matters: screen readers and image-blocked clients will get nothing for it. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html>` has none.
- **Invalid markup in `<body>`.** `<title style="display:none…"></title>` is empty and sits in the body. A second `<style>` block is also inside the body. Both are invalid placement, and Outlook and some webmail clients strip or ignore them.
- **Broken CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches a nonexistent element. The correct `.ReadMsgBody` appears only in the later in-body `<style>`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector and does nothing.
- **Layout tables.** Most outer layout `<table>` elements lack `role="presentation"`. Only the `.stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables have it.
- **Doctype.** The email uses HTML 4.01 Transitional, which is legacy but compatible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or AMPscript output appears in the visible source. The `#AMPScript` div renders empty comments only. A leftover template expression is visible in an HTML comment (`<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->`). It is harmless but shows an unprocessed build step. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) is unverified for the same reason. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`, and alignment with the From domain could not be checked.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable from the data.** All 46 links go through the `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirect domain and were skipped, so no UTM parameters or destination URLs are visible.
- **Campaign ID.** The Dotomi pixel parameter `dtmc_email_category=20261102_VS_6427_FILEBAL` suggests a 2026-11-02 send. This is the only campaign identifier visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, and set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS endpoints, or remove them. Confirm whether the cookie-sync is needed.
3. Drop the duplicate `open.aspx` pixel.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
6. Move the `<title>` and `<style>` blocks out of `<body>`. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and remove the invalid `min-width{}` rule.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw MIME source. This would verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. It would also let the footer unsubscribe link and address be checked.
8. Resolve the 46 click-tracking redirects. This would confirm UTM parameters, landing-page status, and offer consistency.
