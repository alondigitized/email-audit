# Last Day: Buy One, Get One 50% Off PJs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day version of the PJ Event. The hero is unambiguous: a "LAST DAY" tag, a red "The PJ Event" banner, "Buy One, Get One 50% Off Pajama Sets" and one black SHOP NOW button. It matches the 11/15 and 11/16 sends ("BOGO 50% Off PJ Sets? Absolutely", "BOGO 50% Off PJs Ends Tomorrow"), and the subject now carries the deadline.
- Below the hero the email turns into a kitchen sink. It adds a Tuesday Panty Edit ("90s Brief"), a second "LAST DAY" for Bras from $25, a BOGO-free beauty gift set, a category tile grid, a rewards and credit card strip, and a "Now Trending" row. That gives three or four competing offers and several CTAs after a single clean hero. Because it is the fourth PJ-led send in six days, the "Last Day" urgency is believable but feels worn.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand closer with a strong top third. It is diluted by clutter and a long fine-print block.

## What's working

- The hero offer is clear and visible at the top. It has a red banner, a large "50% Off" and one SHOP NOW button.
- The "LAST DAY" tag in the hero adds credible urgency that matches the subject.
- The five-model plaid and holiday PJ photo is seasonal and gift-oriented.
- The Welcome back strip shows loyalty (Rewards) and a $25 off credit card offer.
- The "Limited Time" label on the beauty BOGO gives that module its own deadline.

## What's weak

- Three further promos (Panty Edit, Bras from $25, Beauty BOGO) follow the hero. Two more carry "LAST DAY"-style urgency, so urgency is spread thin.
- Small white text over the photo in the bras module sits close to the SHOP NOW button and is hard to read at this size.
- Nearly every module's CTA is a generic SHOP NOW or SHOP ALL PANTIES. None names a product.
- The legal block at the bottom is a long wall of dense fine print. The hero's own caveat, "Only up to $14.99 each, no code required. Excludes clearance", is tiny.
- The cadence is the issue. This is the fourth PJ BOGO send in six days (11/12, 11/15, 11/16, 11/17), and the creative is nearly identical.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the PJ hero plus one supporting module. Drop the beauty and bras blocks on a last-day send so the deadline is the only message.
- 2. Add a specific deadline, such as "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT", and a countdown element.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop PJ Sets BOGO 50% Off" and link individual PJ products.
- 4. Move the fine print into a short summary above the footer and put the full terms behind a link.
- 5. Space out PJ sends. After three in a row, use a different hero such as bras or gifting.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a legacy, table-based Salesforce Marketing Cloud template with a few invalid CSS selectors and no zoom allowed on mobile. The QA run found 10+ non-HTTPS, alt-less third-party Dotomi tracking pixels. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure third-party pixels (confirmed):**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…&dtmc_email_category=20261117_VS_6498_FULFILE`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These load over `http://`, so clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, and the cookie-sync and ID-matching calls will fail silently.
  - That is 11 extra third-party requests in one email, all with no `alt`.
- **Skipped probes:** 43 click-tracking links on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` weren't HTTP-probed. Their redirect targets and status codes are unverified.
- **Duplicate open pixels:** Two 1×1 open-tracking `<img>` tags with different `qs=` values appear in the source: one in the first `<div style="font-size:0">` and one after the `<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->` comment. Both have `alt=""`, so the open count may be inflated or inconsistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`, plus all the Dotomi images above.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS in the first `<style>` block:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets an element type and does nothing. The second `<style>` block has the correct `.ReadMsgBody`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an invalid selector.
  - `max-height:auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title style="display:none…">` is placed inside `<body>`, nested in a table cell, and is empty.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
  - The global `table{max-width:600px!important}` caps every nested table, which could constrain full-width wrappers.
- **Layout tables:** Only some have `role="presentation"`; the outer wrappers (for example the first `<table width="100%" … align="center">`) don't.
- **Template residue:** Leftover `<div id="AMPScript">` with empty `<!-- Ampscript v0 START/END -->` blocks, and a literal `<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->` comment. These are harmless, but they show unrendered template code shipping in production.
