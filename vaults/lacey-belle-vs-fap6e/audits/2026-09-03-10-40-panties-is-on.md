# 10/$40 Panties is *ON*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This one lands the offer immediately: "10/$40 Panty Party" sits right under the hero, with a colorful spread of panties, size selector (XS-XXXL), and a "SHOP ALL" button — no ambiguity about what's on sale. It's a strong, clean open.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email keeps stacking unrelated offers — $25 tee bras, a whole Scent Event fragrance push, a free tote GWP — before finally getting to the category grid. By the time a reader hits "Now Trending," this has stopped being a panty email and turned into a full catalog dump. That's consistent with the brand's recent pattern (see 2026-08-28's "Our #1 Collection" note about losing the thread after a strong hero).
- Compared to 2026-08-30's "Panty Packs from $20," which had a tighter, more focused build after its hero, this send has a good top and a bloated middle. Net: solid open, muddied click path.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: "10/$40 Panty Party" with a concrete price anchor, no scroll needed.
- Size chips (XS–XXXL) are visible right under the hero, reducing friction for anyone unsure of fit/availability.
- "SHOP ALL" CTA button is clear and high-contrast against the white background.
- Colorful, varied panty flat-lay photography gives visual energy and matches a "fall refresh" vibe.
- Category grid near the bottom (Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty, Swim, etc.) is clean and well-organized for browse intent.

## What's weak

- The email pivots to $25 tee bras, then a full Scent Event (fragrance), then a free tote GWP — three unrelated offers competing with the panty hero.
- By the time the eye reaches "Now Trending," the subject's promise (panties) is buried under four other campaigns.
- The rewards/text-signup/25% off block ("Welcome back") feels bolted on, breaking the visual flow between the tote offer and the category grid.
- No urgency/deadline attached to the panty offer itself (the tote has "while quantities last," the fragrance has "price ends Friday," but the headline panty deal has no time pressure).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — move Scent Event and the free tote GWP to their own dedicated sends (or a secondary block far below), so the panty offer owns the top two-thirds of the email uninterrupted.
- 2. Add a deadline to the panty hero itself ("Ends Sunday" or similar) — right now it's the one offer without urgency, which is backwards given it's the subject line.
- 3. Move the category grid up, right after the panty hero/tee bra block, so browsers reach it before fatigue sets in.
- 4. Tighten the "Welcome back" rewards module — it currently reads as a fourth competing offer; fold it into a persistent footer instead.
