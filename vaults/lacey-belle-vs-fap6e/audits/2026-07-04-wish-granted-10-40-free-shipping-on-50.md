# Wish Granted: 10/$40 + FREE Shipping on $50+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- This is VS's fifth Panty Party send in three days — the 9/10 launch, the punny sustain, the bra bundle crossell, yesterday's 7/10 sustain, and now "Wish Granted." The free shipping addition is a genuine new sweetener, and the subject delivers on the narrative payoff the phrase promises. That's the good news.
- The bad news: VS did what VS always does late in a campaign — stuffed every secondary initiative into the email before the window closes. The Panty Party hero holds its spot at top, then you get a Float Actor Balconette spotlight, then $25 Tee Bras, then a Bombshell Sale module, then a Save $15 tier offer. Five distinct selling moments in one scroll, with no single directional pull after the hero. The Bombshell Sale section mid-email appears to carry a CTA that looks out-of-season for July 4th weekend, which shatters whatever summery mood the panty-and-blue-sky hero tries to set.
- Verdict: The primary offer is airtight and the free shipping is a real lever — but the kitchen-sink body and cadence saturation (send five of five in 72 hours) will suppress performance below what this subject line deserves.

## What's working

- **Free shipping is a genuine incremental offer** — not recycled copy from the prior four sends; the subscriber who's been watching this series has actual new reason to act.
- **Panty Party hero holds its visual position** — 10/$40 is front and center with a colorful style-grid below it, communicating abundance at a glance.
- **Float Actor Balconette product spotlight** — a named, single-product feature block is smarter than a generic category push; the pink/gold colorway is seasonally appropriate.
- **No render bugs visible** — layout intact, images loaded, text legible at rendered width.

## What's weak

- **Five competing offers in one scroll** — Panty Party, Balconette feature, Tee Bras, Bombshell Sale, Save $15 — the email loses its directional argument after the fold and never reclaims it.
- **Bombshell Sale module appears seasonally off** — the style and CTA copy in that mid-email section feel disconnected from the summer/July 4th moment; it reads like a module pulled from a different campaign window.
- **No July 4th hook** — this lands on Independence Day weekend, a natural copy anchor ("Celebrate in style," "Stars, stripes, and 10/$40 panties"), and VS ignores it entirely.
- **Cadence fatigue** — "Wish Granted" is doing minimal differentiation work; a subscriber who ignored sends two through four has no new urgency signal to act on now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or seasonalize the Bombshell Sale module** — either cut it from this send or re-skin the copy/CTA to be summer/July 4th-coherent. One off-season module in the middle of the scroll punches above its weight in breaking the mood.
- 2. **Make free shipping the single secondary story** — a threshold progress mechanic ("$12 away from free shipping") below the hero would drive higher AOV and give the email a single second act instead of four competing ones.
- 3. **Add an end-date to the hero** — "Ends July 5" or "Ends tonight" gives the subscriber a specific reason to act on send five rather than waiting for send six.
- 4. **Lead with the free shipping angle in hero subhead** — the 10/$40 is known; "now with free shipping on $50+" is the new information; bury it less.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Shipping Unlocked — 10/$40 Panty Party`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10/$40 Panties + Your Wish: Free Shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends July 5 — grab 10 for $40 and ship free over $50`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping is yours at $50. The Panty Party's still on.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**10 Dotomi retargeting pixels use HTTP** (`http://login.dotomi.com/...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`). Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail) will block mixed-content HTTP resources or proxy/strip them, silently breaking retargeting audience sync.

Evidence:
```
src: http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...
src: http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php?a4edea...
```

**12 images missing `alt` text** — includes the Dotomi pixels (decorative, `alt=""` is acceptable there) but also two content images hosted on `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`:
- `25010789-c609-4b1a-acb4-754ce139a208.jpg`
- `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`

These are likely hero/product images. Missing `alt` degrades screen-reader accessibility and renders as broken in image-off environments (common in corporate/Outlook).

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE** — legacy but intentional for Outlook compatibility; not a defect.
