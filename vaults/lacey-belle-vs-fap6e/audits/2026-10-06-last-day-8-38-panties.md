## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template and has valid tracking infrastructure. It has several markup defects: block elements and a `<script>` inside `<head>`, broken CSS selectors, and a viewport that blocks zoom. It also loads about 11 plain-HTTP third-party ad-tech pixels. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the data provided (see section 5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, so the final destinations are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels over HTTP.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` all load unencrypted.
  - Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them.
  - Each URL carries the identifier `a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1` in clear text.
  - Ten sequential cookie-redirect pixels add request overhead and a privacy surface.
- **Open pixel is fine.** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?…&d=70252&bmt=0` is 1×1 with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will force-close `<head>` at that point.
  - As a result, the viewport meta, the Content-Type meta, and both `<style>` blocks effectively move into `<body>`.
  - A `<title style="display:none">` is also inside `<body>`.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the markup uses `role="presentation"`.
- **Broken CSS selectors in the first `<style>` block.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets nothing.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an element selector for a nonexistent tag.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on clients that honor it.
- **Missing alt attributes.** The QA scan flagged:
  - `.../m/1/589083ae-42bf-438a-9070-cea44800735c.jpg`
  - `https://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/rp/a076cc7f15d20566.png?mi_u=…`
  - `.../m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
  - The Dotomi pixels (`pub1`–`pub10`, UCM) also have no alt. For those, a missing alt only matters if they render visibly.
- The heading, CTA and text structure are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't evaluate alt text on content images or text/image balance.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Personal identifiers in URLs are hashed (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` is a 64-hex string), and `mi_u=200492586522` is an opaque ID. No raw email address is exposed in query strings.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw `.eml` or the original message headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed through the relay. The From domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`; the click and image domains are on the same `e1.` subdomain, which is consistent.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only landing URL visible is in the JSON-LD: `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/panties/panties-offer`.
  - It uses `cm_mmc=di-_-100626525-_-VSD_VSL_20261006_VS_6259_FILEBALOPT-_`, `d_cellid=100626525` and `mi_ecmp=20261006_VS_6259_FILEBALOPT`.
  - These IDs are consistent with the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category=20261006_VS_6259_FILEBALO…` parameter. That one is truncated in the QA output.
- It uses no `utm_*` parameters, only proprietary Coremetrics (`cm_mmc`), Dotomi and mi_* parameters. Standard GA-style attribution depends on server-side mapping, which can't be checked here.
- The JSON-LD `url` points straight to the landing page and doesn't go through the click-tracking redirect. If the schema is used for Gmail promotion cards, those clicks bypass SFMC click tracking.
- Landing page alignment with the 8/$38 offer can't be verified because the redirects weren't followed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw source headers. Have the monitor capture the original headers, not the relayed ones.
2. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the original message, not the relay copy.
3. Move the JSON-LD block and the wrapper table out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. Keep only `<meta>`, `<title>`, `<style>` and the MSO conditional in `<head>`.
4. Fix the `.ReadMsgBody` selector and delete `min-width{…}`.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
6. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS or drop them, and consolidate the 10 `pub*.php` redirects if possible. Add `alt=""` to decorative and tracking images.
7. Add descriptive alt text to the three flagged content images.
8. Extend the QA tool to follow `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects, ideally for a sample of links, so landing pages and parameter handoff can be checked.
