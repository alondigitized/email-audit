# Up to 60% Off Bras—Refresh Your Collection
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**

Then it falls apart. The VS Candy Shoppe body care section appears mid-email, followed by a loungewear block, followed by a product grid of what looks like 15+ thumbnails. The subject says bras; the email says "we ran out of things to cut."

That's its third appearance since June 7 — subscribers who've received every send in this sequence know the mechanic by heart, and the urgency has completely drained out of it. ## Technical Audit — Victoria's Secret "Up to 60% Off Bras" (2026-06-14)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a structurally invalid `<head>` (table and JSON-LD rendered before charset meta), a duplicate open-tracking pixel, 11 images missing alt text, and 10 retargeting pixels served over plain HTTP. Compliance headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) are absent, putting the send at risk under 2024 Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Duplicate open-tracking pixel**
`https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?CTZYHMAQVFIU5HJ2LDCQ22LPZE.70246&d=70246&bmt=0` appears twice in the truncated source — once in the `<div>` directly after `<body>` opens, and again inside the nested table block. Double-firing will inflate open-rate metrics.

**18 click-tracking links unprobed**
All CTAs route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. QA skipped HTTP probes for these, so destination URLs and UTM coverage are unverifiable from this review.

**Dotomi retargeting pixels over HTTP**
10 pixel images (`http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) and 1 sync pixel (`http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`) use plain HTTP. Gmail and Outlook.com proxy all images over HTTPS; insecure sources are blocked or silently fail, breaking the retargeting sync.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<table>` and `<script>` rendered inside `<head>` before charset meta**
The JSON-LD `<script>` block and a full `<table role="presentation">` appear in `<head>` before the `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" charset=UTF-8>` declaration. Per HTML spec the charset meta must be the first child of `<head>`; anything before it may be parsed with the wrong encoding. Browsers and email clients that reflow the DOM will move the table to `<body>`, causing unpredictable layout shifts.

**`<title>` inside `<body>`**
`<title style="display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden;">` is placed inside a `<td>`, not in `<head>`. This is invalid; the tag has no effect on preheader text in this position.

**Malformed JSON-LD schema**
```json
[{ "@context": "http://schema.org/",
   "@type": "DiscountOffer",
   "@type": "PromotionCard",
   "image": "",
   "url": "" }]
```
Three problems: (a) duplicate `@type` key — only the last value (`PromotionCard`) is retained by JSON parsers; (b) `image` and `url` are empty strings — Gmail Promotions Tab annotation requires populated values to render the deal badge; (c) `PromotionCard` is not a recognized schema.org type.

**11 images missing `alt` text**
- `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (VS content image, HTTPS)
- All 10 `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub*.php` pixels

Decorative pixels should use `alt=""` explicitly; content images require descriptive alt text for screen readers and image-off rendering.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />
```
Blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA).

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
`<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>` triggers quirks mode in some clients. HTML5 `<!DOCTYPE html>` is the current email standard.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
QA found no `List-Unsubscribe` header. Per Gmail/Yahoo February 2024 bulk-sender policy, senders at >5k/day volume must include this header. Absence risks deliverability penalties or inbox demotion.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is required alongside `List-Unsubscribe` for compliant one-click handling. Not present.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. DKIM signing by `e1.victoriassecret.com` is expected but unverifiable from this data.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism**
Not auditable from the truncated HTML — needs full-source verification. Flag for follow-up.

All 18 CTAs pass through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` click-redirects; QA could not resolve final destinations or confirm UTM parameter presence. UTM coverage (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) is unverifiable without expanding the click-tracking links.
