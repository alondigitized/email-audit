# Up to $50 Off Your Game-Day Looks 🏉
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the football-season kickoff send, and it commits the same sin as the July 28 "Buttery Soft, Wireless Bras" miss: the subject makes a specific promise the render never pays off. "Up to $50 Off Your Game-Day Looks" is the headline claim, but scrolling the entire body, I never see a "$50" anywhere — the actual offers are $35 sweats, $20 tees, $30 bras, $20 panties, and $25 off $125+. None of those individually or combined reads as an obvious "$50 off" moment, so the subject is either aggregational math nobody will do in their head or an outright stretch.
- The hero itself is well-shot — "GO (WHATEVER) TEAM!" with a football-field backdrop and a clean "SHOP THE NEW KICKOFF CAPSULE" CTA — but it's a collection launch, not a discount, which makes the subject/hero mismatch worse than usual. Below the fold this reverts to the same kitchen-sink structure flagged repeatedly in the brand history (07-25 through 07-27): five stacked "LAST DAY" campaigns, a PINK Friday teaser, a rewards nudge, and a UGC grid.
- Given this brand is sending near-daily and reusing "LAST DAY" across multiple consecutive days (07-25, 26, 27, 30, and now here), the urgency language is losing credibility. Solid production values, weak offer honesty — call it a middling send.

## What's working

- The hero art direction (football field, red/burgundy athleisure, bold "GO (WHATEVER) TEAM!" headline) is seasonally on-point for early August back-to-school/game-day timing.
- The hero CTA is specific and collection-named ("SHOP THE NEW KICKOFF CAPSULE"), not a generic "Shop Now."
- Section dividers are visually clean — each offer block has its own color-coded badge ($35, $20, $30, $20, $25) making the stack easy to scan even if there are many of them.
- Rewards/loyalty module ("welcome back," $10 off) is present near the footer for repeat customers.
- No render bugs — no broken images, overlapping text, or placeholder copy visible anywhere in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- The subject's headline number — "$50 Off" — never appears anywhere in the rendered body. The closest is "$25 off $125+," which isn't the same claim.
- The hero has no visible price or discount at all; it's a new-arrival capsule push riding on a subject line that promises savings.
- Six distinct campaigns are stacked in one email (capsule launch, sweats, tees, bras, panties, PINK Friday teaser, spend-and-save) — no single "primary" CTA for the eye to land on after the hero.
- "LAST DAY" is used on multiple offer blocks in this same email and has now appeared on at least four of the last six audited sends from this brand, which erodes urgency credibility.
- No sizing, fit, or availability information is visible on any product block, despite bras and sweats being fit-sensitive categories.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject/body mismatch first.** Either add a real $50-off mechanic to the hero (e.g., a tiered "spend $50, get $50 off" or bundle math shown explicitly) or rewrite the subject to match what's actually in the email — don't promise a number the render can't back up.
- 2. **Cut the stack from six campaigns to two or three.** Lead with the kickoff capsule (new arrivals) OR the discount ladder (sweats/tees/bras/panties) as the single email; move the rest to a follow-up send. This also fixes the "no primary CTA" problem.
- 3. **Retire "LAST DAY" until it's actually true.** Reusing it across four-plus consecutive sends trains subscribers to ignore it; swap in a real countdown or drop the urgency tag from evergreen-feeling offers.
- 4. **Add a sizing/fit line under the bra and sweats blocks** ("XS–XXL available") — high-return categories benefit from setting expectations before the click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Game-Day Squad Fit, Starting at $20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New kickoff capsule just dropped — plus this week's last-day deals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweats, tees, bras, and panties — all under $35 today`
