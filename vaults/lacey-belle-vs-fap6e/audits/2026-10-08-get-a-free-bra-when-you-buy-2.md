# Get a ✨FREE BRA✨ When You Buy 2
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's biggest-offer send in a week, and it ends the offer-free velour detour of 10-07 ("The Velour Allure Is Sooo Back"). The hero says "THE BRA EVENT / LIMITED TIME / BUY 2, GET 1 FREE." That is concrete, easy to read, and matches the subject. After six days of 8/$38 panties, moving to bras is a real change of beat.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. It stacks a holiday gift guide, $35 sweatshirts, $10 full-size beauty, a gift guide banner, a logo shop, bestsellers, category tiles and a rewards block. That's five or six offers competing with the one in the subject. The fine print at the bottom is dense and long.
- Judgement: a strong hero in a bloated body. The subject and hero do the work, and everything after the first screen dilutes it. One offer would have beaten six. Note: the screenshot is small, so I couldn't read the small text exactly.

## What's working

- The hero offer ("BUY 2, GET 1 FREE") is large and sits right under "THE BRA EVENT", so the offer is clear immediately.
- The subject and hero match, so there is no gap between what the inbox promised and what the email delivers.
- The email changes category. It moves from panties to bras, so it doesn't feel like a repeat of the 10-02 to 10-06 sends.
- The pink palette, model photography and bold type look like PINK. I saw no broken images or overlapping text.
- The holiday gift guide module makes the email feel seasonal for early October.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers: the $35 sweatshirts, $10 beauty and gift guide all pull attention away from the bra deal.
- Every button reads "SHOP NOW" or similar, not "Shop bras" or "Build my free-bra bundle."
- "Limited time" has no visible end date in the hero, so the urgency isn't believable.
- The footer fine print is long and heavy, and the hero only says "no code required" and "lowest priced items free." Exclusions are tucked into small type.
- The email is very long, so the second half (category tiles, rewards, "all over your feed") will mostly go unread.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the bra offer. Follow the hero with 3–4 best-selling bras at their pre-discount prices, and move the sweatshirts, beauty and gift guide to a later send.
- 2. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Bras: Get 1 Free" and put a date next to "limited time" in the hero.
- 3. Add a one-line "how it works" under the hero, such as "Add 3 bras, cheapest is free," so the mechanic is clear before the fine print.
- 4. Show sizing or fit cues (band and cup range, push-up or wireless) on the product tiles.
- 5. Move the rewards block higher, or drop it, since it's easy to miss at the bottom.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email uses a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) template. It has a valid JSON-LD block and a 600px responsive container. It also loads about 10 plain-HTTP third-party tracking pixels, and the head markup is invalid. The QA run's 57% pass rate comes from missing-header warnings, which are probably capture artifacts, and from the image warnings.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status (200/redirect/404) is unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** All of these load over `http://`, so they may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261008_PK_6267_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The ten `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pubN.php?a4edea10...=1` calls carry the hashed email in the query string. They are third-party ad-network syncs (Dotomi/Epsilon) fired from the email body. Many clients block them, and they add load time.
- **Sensitive identifiers in URLs.** The JSON-LD `url` values and the pixel URLs include `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` and `customerID=43cece97…1e2f`. These are hashed rather than plaintext, but they are persistent identifiers in the query string. A forwarded email or a shared link would pass them on.
- **Redundant JSON-LD.** All three `PromotionCard` entries point to the same `url` (`/us/pink/bras?cm_mmc=...`) with different images.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Invalid head markup.** A `<table>…<script type="application/ld+json">` block sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta name="viewport">` and `<style>` tags. Tables are not valid in `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early and push the later `<meta>` and `<style>` tags into the body. Gmail and Outlook may drop or mis-order the responsive CSS as a result.
- **Missing alt text.** `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (`image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/.../m/179/`) has no alt attribute. The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `pub1–10.php`, the Dotomi UCM image) also lack alt text. For hidden 1×1 pixels this has low impact, but the visible PNG is a real accessibility gap. The open pixel `open.aspx` has `alt=""`, which is correct.
- **Viewport meta.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Doctype.** The email uses HTML 4.01 Transitional, which is acceptable for email but dated.
- **CSS issues.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` has no `.` or `#` selector, and neither does `min-width{width:auto!important;}`. Both are invalid and ignored. `max-height:auto` is not a valid value. `table{max-width:600px!important;}` is applied to every table, which could constrain nested layouts.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks such as alt text on hero images, a text-based CTA and the preheader were not verified.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the visible source. The hashed identifiers are populated, not left as raw tokens.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** QA flagged both. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, the email would not meet the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay capture limitation. The sender domain `e2.vspink.com` and the click domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` are different domains, so DMARC alignment could not be checked.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical postal address and the unsubscribe link sit in the truncated portion, so they are unverified.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **Parameter scheme.** The links use `cm_mmc=di-_-100826425-_-VSD_VSL_20261008_PK_6267_FILEBAL`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp=20261008_PK_6267_FILEBAL`. These are vendor-specific parameters. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is present. This works for their own analytics stack but will not populate standard GA reports.
- **Landing page.** The JSON-LD target is `/us/pink/bras`, a category page. That fits a "Free Bra When You Buy 2" offer, but the offer terms could not be checked against the landing page. The 48 tracked links were not resolved, so continuity across the whole email is unverified.
- **Destination domain mismatch.** The sender is `vspink.com`, the click domain is `victoriassecret.com` and the landing page is `victoriassecret.com`. This is expected for the brand.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Move the JSON-LD `<script>` out of the `<table>` wrapper in `<head>`. Put the script directly in `<head>` or in `<body>` so the viewport meta and `<style>` are not pushed out of the head.
2. Serve the Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixels over HTTPS, or remove the ten `pub1–10.php` cookie-sync pixels.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels, and add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png` if it is visible.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the invalid CSS selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`) and the `max-height:auto` value.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message from the sending MTA, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment outside the AgentMail relay.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to cover the footer, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the 48 skipped tracking links.
