## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional template with invalid head structure. It also fires 10 plaintext-HTTP third-party (Dotomi) pixels that carry a hashed email identifier. The automated QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass). The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely capture artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 46 tracking links were skipped, all on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Destination resolution and link health are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixels:** Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. There is also `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`. Clients may block them as mixed or non-HTTPS content, and the requests are unencrypted.
- **Identifier exposure:** Each `pubN.php` URL carries `a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. That is the same value as `dtm_email_hash` in the JSON-LD URL, so a hashed recipient email goes to a third party in plaintext HTTP query strings.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is present at 1×1 with `alt=""`. That is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** All 10 `pubN.php` pixels and the `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` image have no `alt`. The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` also has no `alt`. The tracking pixels should have `alt=""`. The content image needs descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid head structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the JSON-LD `<script>` sits inside `<head>` before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table, so the viewport and charset `<meta>` tags may land in `<body>` and be ignored.
- **Stray title:** `<title style="display:none…">` is empty and sits inside `<body>`.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are dead rules.
- **Layout tables:** The main layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties`) lack `role="presentation"`. Only the styling-block wrappers have it.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `role` attributes and a viewport meta, which is mismatched but non-fatal.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated source. The remaining HTML could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers are missing from the captured data. QA attributes this to the relay not preserving headers, so it can't be confirmed as a sender defect. Check against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is aligned with the tracking domains, which is consistent with proper setup, but that is not verified.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters present:** The JSON-LD URL (`https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/beauty`) carries `cm_mmc=di-_-102826515-_-VSD_VSL_20261028_VSB_6400_FULLFIL-_&d_cellid=102826515&mi_ecmp=20261028_VSB_6400_FULLFIL`. The campaign ID is consistent across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and `dtmc_email_category`.
- **No standard UTMs:** There is no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Attribution relies on proprietary parameters (Coremetrics `cm_mmc`, `mi_*`). This is only a gap if GA-style analytics is expected.
- **Identifier in URL:** `customerID=43cece97…` and `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` (SHA-256 values) are in the landing URL. They will appear in site logs, referrers and analytics.
- **Landing alignment:** The URL is `/us/vs/beauty`, a generic beauty landing page. Whether it matches the BOGO Eau de Parfum offer can't be verified because the links were not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or remove the cookie-sync pixels. Add `alt=""` to every tracking pixel.
2. Stop appending the hashed email to third-party pixel URLs, or confirm there is a documented privacy basis for it.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Move the `<table>`/JSON-LD block out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. Remove the empty `<title>` from the body and fix the two invalid CSS selectors.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
8. Resolve the 46 redirect links in a follow-up check to confirm landing pages and offer alignment.
