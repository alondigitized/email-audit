# Patch Parties > Plans 🪩🌈🍀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third PINK x Stoney Clover Lane send in a week (after Nov 12 "You're Gonna LOVE Our New Collab" and Nov 16 "PSST: Patch Some Zzzs"). It runs a collab story, a UGC points promo, an app-only $40 offer and a credit-card bonus in one scroll. The collab hero is the strongest part: a collage of creators holding patch bags, with a "PATCH PARTIES > PLANS" lockup and a "LIMITED EDITION" sticker. It looks good and on-brand.
- Below the hero, four offers compete: "FREE Panty loading… DL the app" in the top bar, "EARN 500 BONUS POINTS", "SAVE $40 when you spend $200+" and "$25 OFF A $75+ PURCHASE". The first CTA, "GET INSPIRED," has no price or product attached. The best money offer, $40 off, sits well below the fold and requires the app. That gating adds friction, and the dense legal block at the bottom hurts the "honest offer" read.
- Judgement: a decent collab-awareness email that wastes its best offer. It is a fine brand beat but a mediocre conversion email.

## What's working

- The hero collage of real creators with patch bags is on-trend and social-first for a Gen Z audience.
- The "PATCH PARTIES > PLANS" and PINK x STONEY CLOVER LN lockup is a clear, branded collab signal.
- "SAVE $40 when you spend $200+" is a big, red, high-contrast block with a clear "DOWNLOAD OR SIGN IN" button.
- The category grid (Panties, Sleep, Bras, Activewear, Beauty and others) gives shoppers an easy way into the catalog.
- The holiday gift guide and "SPECIAL OFFER" block fit the season.

## What's weak

- Four competing offers (free panty, 500 points, $40 off, $25 off) dilute the message.
- The top hero CTA, "GET INSPIRED," is vague and has no offer, product or deadline.
- The email is long, and the best offer is far below the fold.
- Much of the money is app-gated or member-gated ("DL the app", "App Exclusive", Rewards sign-up), so many readers face friction.
- The legal fine print is a long wall of text. The "Limited Time · Member Exclusive" points promo also needs a photo post and a purchase, which feels buried.
- The collab story repeats the Nov 12 and Nov 16 sends with little new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and move "SAVE $40 when you spend $200+" directly under the hero. Cut the points promo and credit-card block into a single footer strip.
- 2. Replace "GET INSPIRED" with a specific CTA such as "Shop Patch Bags" or "Shop the Collab", and link it to the collab collection.
- 3. Add product shots with prices or patch-bag names so readers can see what the collab includes.
- 4. Make the app-only terms clearer at the top, such as "App-only: $40 off $200+".
- 5. Shorten the legal copy and put the key condition (the $200 minimum) in the visible offer text.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `stylingblock-content-wrapper`) with a structurally invalid `<head>`, 11 insecure `http://` pixels, and several images missing alt text. The automated QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings). The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **40 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as tracking/redirect domains, so click-through destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **11 tracking pixels load over cleartext HTTP.** These can be blocked as mixed content or intercepted:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261117_PK_6496_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **A hashed email identifier is sent over HTTP.** Each `pub{1-10}.php` URL carries `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. This is the same value as `dtm_email_hash` in the product URLs, so it is exposed in transit to a third-party ad network.
- **The JSON-LD links are redundant.** All three `PromotionCard` entries point to the identical URL, `/us/pink/collabs/stoney-clover-lane?cm_mmc=…`, while using three different images. The markup gives no per-card destination.
- **The JSON-LD links embed identifiers.** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` (SHA-256-length values) appear as query parameters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits directly inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will close `<head>` at the `<table>`, which can push the viewport meta and styles into `<body>`. JSON-LD is also unlikely to be honored in this position by most clients, though Gmail does read it for promotion cards.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a non-existent element. Both rules are dead.
- **Overly broad table rule.** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important;}` apply to every nested table and cell, which can break nested layouts.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure in clients that honor it.
- **No document language.** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute in the visible source.
- **Missing alt text, 12 images.** The `alt` attribute is absent on all of these:
  - `o.gif`
  - the `login.dotomi.com` pixel
  - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Missing alt on a content image.** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt, and it is the only one of these that is not a tracking pixel.
- The primary open pixel correctly uses `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML is truncated, so this is not confirmed for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed rather than a verified defect**. Check the raw message from the sender side.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC are unknown.** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, again attributed to the relay. The From address is `PINK@e2.vspink.com`, a sending subdomain. Verify alignment against the raw headers.
- **The footer is not visible.** The physical address and body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section, so they cannot be confirmed.
- **Third-party data sharing.** The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels pass a hashed email to an ad network. Verify this is covered by the privacy disclosure and honors opt-outs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Non-standard campaign parameters.** Links use `cm_mmc=di-_-111726425-_-VSD_VSL_20261117_PK_6496_FILEBAL-_`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp`. There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` values. Attribution in GA4 depends on `cm_mmc` being mapped server-side.
- **Landing page.** The only verifiable destination is `/us/pink/collabs/stoney-clover-lane`. The subject line references "Patch Parties", and the page was not fetched. The 40 tracked links were skipped, so subject-to-landing alignment cannot be confirmed technically.
- **Campaign ID is consistent.** `20261117_PK_6496_FILEBAL` matches across `mi_ecmp`, the pixel URLs and `dtmc_email_category`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block out of `<head>` into `<body>`, and put the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags directly inside `<head>`.
2. Serve all pixels over HTTPS (`mi.victoriassecret.com`, `login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com`), or drop the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels. At minimum, stop sending the hashed email in a cleartext URL.
3. Add `alt=""` to all decorative and tracking images. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix or delete the invalid CSS selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`), and scope the `table{max-width:600px!important}` rule.
6. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
7. Give each JSON-LD `PromotionCard` its own destination URL.
8. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment against the raw message. The relay-stripping explanation is plausible but unverified.
9. Add standard `utm_*` parameters alongside `cm_mmc`, or confirm the mapping in analytics.
