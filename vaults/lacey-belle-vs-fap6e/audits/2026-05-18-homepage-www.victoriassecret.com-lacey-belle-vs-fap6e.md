# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I opened VS and the first thing that hit me was... a lot of nothing on the left side and a fragrance campaign on the right. Like, I came here for bras — possibly to check if the Semi-Annual Sale dates dropped — and I'm getting perfume bottles and a Rewards signup widget shoved in my face. It's a pretty brand, don't get me wrong, but the homepage feels scattered right now, not focused.

## What's working

- **VS Rewards widget** — "Ready to get rewarded?" with the earn-points icons is the single strongest element on this page for me. I'm already a member so I'd tap "Sign in" immediately, and for anyone not enrolled, it's a clear pitch.
- **Summer/warm-season imagery** — the fragrance campaign has a gorgeous golden-hour coastal vibe and the swimwear lifestyle shots (right column) feel current for May. It's not stale.
- **Brand aesthetic** — the pink-tone palette at the very top and the warm photography are unmistakably VS. Feels premium.

## What's weak

- **Blank left-side void** — there's a massive white/empty area on the left half of the above-the-fold zone. That is a render bug or a missing hero asset, and it's the first thing I see. Not a great look.
- **Hero is fragrance, not bras** — I'm a bra and PINK shopper. Leading with perfume bottles is fine for some shoppers, but it does nothing to pull me toward what I actually came to buy.
- **No offer anywhere above the fold** — no Semi-Annual Sale teaser, no Free Panty Wednesday call-out, no "free shipping at $X." My biggest purchase trigger is a deal and there's nothing here to trigger it.
- **"JOIN NOW" is the dominant CTA** — my eyes land on a Rewards sign-up button, not a "Shop Bras" or "Shop PINK" button. Acquisition conversion is being prioritized over existing-customer shopping, which is backwards for someone like me who already has the app.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the left-side blank zone first** — whatever hero image or text block is supposed to load there, it's not rendering. This should be a same-day bug fix; it's killing first impressions.
- **Add a bra/PINK category CTA in the hero** — swap or supplement the fragrance lead with a "Shop Bras" or "Shop PINK" quick-link tile above the fold so core-category shoppers aren't starting from scratch.
- **Surface the Semi-Annual Sale countdown or teaser** — even a banner strip that says "Semi-Annual Sale coming [date] — mark your calendar" would spike my engagement immediately; I calendar it every year and I'm actively looking for the signal.
- **Move the Rewards sign-in option higher** — instead of a full widget mid-page, a slim "Member? Sign in for your perks" link near the top nav serves returning shoppers without dominating the layout.
