## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure and tracking stack are mostly sound. It has 46 tracking links, a 1x1 open pixel, and a 600px container. The markup has real defects: invalid document structure, broken CSS selectors, and accessibility gaps. It also loads about 12 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. Compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 46 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect health are unverified.
- **Cookie-sync pixels over HTTP:** `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, and `mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` all use `http://`. Clients that proxy or block mixed content will drop them.
- **Third-party exposure:** The ten Dotomi (Conversant) pixels add third-party calls on open, and each pixel URL carries the hashed recipient email (`a4edea106b33…=1`). They are extra deliverability and privacy surface.
- **JSON-LD annotations:** All three `PromotionCard` entries point to the same URL, `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/gifts?...`, and differ only by image. Most clients ignore this block.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early, which pushes the viewport meta, charset meta and `<style>` blocks into `<body>`. The `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` also falls well past the first 1024 bytes because of the long JSON-LD URLs.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both rules do nothing. The global `table{max-width:600px!important;}` also caps every nested table.
- **Zoom disabled:** The viewport meta `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 failure on supporting clients.
- **Missing document metadata:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute and the `<head>` has no `<title>`.
- **Layout tables:** Tables such as `<table width="100%" border="0" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" align="center">` and `table.tb_properties` lack `role="presentation"`. Only the wrapper table has it.
- **Missing alt text:** The content image `image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `pub1`–`pub10.php`, `UCMController`) also have no `alt`. Pixels should carry `alt=""`, as the open pixel already does.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens appear in the captured source. Recipient identifiers are embedded in URLs: `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` and `mi_u=200492586522`. The `customerID` value is a 64-hex-character hash, and all of these are rendered rather than raw tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not captured. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Also not found, with the same relay caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify CAN-SPAM footer elements.
- **Domain alignment:** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while images and click tracking use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. This is common for ESP setups. Check the DKIM `d=` domain against the From domain once raw headers are available.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking parameters:** Links use `cm_mmc=di-_-101626435-_-VSD_VSL_20261016_PK_6311_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=101626435&mi_ecmp=20261016_PK_6311_FILEBAL`. There are no standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters. That is fine if attribution runs through Coremetrics/Mi, but GA-based attribution would show this traffic as direct or unattributed.
- **Landing page alignment:** The only visible destination in the captured source is `/us/pink/gifts`. The subject is "Fleece Out ☮️ NEW Holiday 'Uniform'", which points to a fleece or apparel collection. If the main CTAs also land on the gifts page, the subject and landing page don't match. This can't be confirmed because the body is truncated and the redirect links weren't probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `<table>` and JSON-LD `<script>` out of `<head>` into `<body>`, and put the charset meta first in `<head>`.
2. Fix or remove the invalid selectors (`ReadMsgBody` needs a `.`, and `min-width{}` should go). Scope the `table{max-width}` rule.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`. Add `<html lang="en">` and a `<title>`.
4. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables. Add `alt` to `5bcec3ac-…png` and `alt=""` to every tracking pixel.
5. Serve `mi.victoriassecret.com` and `*.dotomi.com` pixels over HTTPS, and audit whether all ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are needed.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results. Then verify the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Probe the redirect chains for the 46 skipped links, confirm they land on the intended product pages, and check that campaign tagging reaches site analytics.
