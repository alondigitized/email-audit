# 40% Off EVERYTHING—Shop Accordingly
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- I could only see the email at about 126px wide, so I'm reading layout and large headlines, not fine print. Sixth Black Friday send in four days. The hero is clean: a black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner, then "HAPPENING NOW 40% Off Everything" with category links (Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear) and "Shop All". The rest is the usual kitchen-sink stack. It runs through 40% off bras, a 7/$35 panty party, 40% off fragrance, $35 pajama sets, a free tote, a free tote and blanket, free shipping at $50, and a favorites grid. A Rewards $25-off block and "Now Trending" tiles close it out.
- The subject is the weak link. "Shop Accordingly" is a wink that gives no reason to open today instead of yesterday. After "40% Off Everything—Black Friday is Finally HERE" on 11/24 and "40% Off Everything—Our Thanks to You" on 11/26, this is the same offer again with no new information. It is Black Friday itself, so the day is credible, but the email gives no deadline.
- Judgement: solid execution, no news, and heavy fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer unmistakably: "40% Off Everything" is the largest type on the page.
- The black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner makes the campaign instantly recognizable.
- Each module has its own offer headline and a "Shop Now" button, so the layout scans easily.
- The Rewards $25-off block gives members something extra.
- No broken images or overlapping text are visible.

## What's weak

- Six or seven stacked offers (bras, panties, fragrance, pajamas, two free-gift variants, shipping) dilute the single 40% message.
- "Limited Time" and "Happening Now" are vague, with no deadline or countdown on Black Friday itself.
- The email is very long, and the Rewards block sits near the bottom where most people won't reach it.
- The subject adds nothing new compared with the last two days' sends.
- Dense legal fine print appears below the offers, which makes "40% off everything" feel qualified.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the subject and hero, such as "Ends tonight at midnight" with a countdown. This would lift opens and clicks more than any other change.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules (the hero, bras, and one gift offer). Move the Rewards callout up to sit right under the hero.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs such as "Shop 40% Off Bras".
- 4. Skip sends that repeat the same offer with no new angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Call: 40% Off Everything Ends at Midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday Ends Tonight—40% Off Bras, Panties & More`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free tote with $100+ and free shipping at $50. Ends 11:59 PM.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: your extra $25 off is inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally invalid (body-level elements inside `<head>`). It loads 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels with no alt text, and it puts SHA-256 hashes of the recipient's email in URLs. The HTML source was truncated and the 68 tracking links were not probed, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs were not audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 68 tracking links on the click-redirect domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` were skipped by the QA probe. Destination validity and redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 `http://` (non-HTTPS) images from Dotomi, which may be blocked by clients and trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:**
  - The hash `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe` appears in the JSON-LD `url`.
  - The same hash is the query key in every `pub*.php` pixel: `?a4edea106b33…=1`.
  - `customerID=43cece97…` is also in the JSON-LD `url`.
  - The `pub*.php` pixels send this hash to a third party (`ads.dotomi.com`) on open. Anyone who has the recipient's email can recompute the hash.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is 1x1 with `alt=""`, which is correct. It will not fire where images are blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** The source has a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. A `<title style="display:none…">` is also inside body content, and it is empty. Parsers will hoist or break the head early, so the `<meta viewport>` and `<style>` blocks may be dropped or misparsed in strict clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Neither rule has any effect.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged an image without alt: `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. The Dotomi pixels also lack alt, but they are invisible tracking images.
- **Missing language attribute:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (accessibility).
- **Legacy DOCTYPE:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with `role="presentation"` only on some tables. The layout tables at `table.container` and the nested tables have none.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** not found.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.

QA notes that all three headers may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay. These are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects. The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** The only visible destination URL (JSON-LD `PromotionCard`) uses a proprietary scheme: `cm_mmc=di-_-112726515-_-VSD_VSL_20261127_VS_6566_FULFILE`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`. This is likely fine for their own analytics, but standard `utm_source/medium/campaign` are absent, so GA-style attribution depends on the `cm_mmc` mapping.
- **Landing page mismatch (structured data only):** The `PromotionCard` URL is `/us/vs/bras`, while the subject line promotes "40% Off EVERYTHING". The destination fits the category rather than a sitewide offer. The visible CTA hrefs were not available to compare.
- **Unverified destinations:** All 68 tracked CTA destinations are unprobed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block and wrapper table out of `<head>`, and put `<title>`, `<meta>` and `<style>` in a valid head. Add `lang` to `<html>`.
2. Replace all `http://` Dotomi pixels with `https://` equivalents, or drop them. Confirm that sending hashed emails to `ads.dotomi.com` matches the privacy policy and consent basis.
3. Remove hashed email and customer ID from the structured-data URL.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Add descriptive alt to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
6. Fix the invalid CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`) and remove `min-width{…}`.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the DKIM/SPF/DMARC results against the raw message headers, not the relay copy.
8. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML, and probe the real CTA destinations (follow the redirects from `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`) to confirm the landing page matches the "everything" offer.
