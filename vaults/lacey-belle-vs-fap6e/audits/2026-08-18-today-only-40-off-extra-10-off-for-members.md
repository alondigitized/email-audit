# Today Only: 40% Off + Extra 10% Off for Members
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the biggest offer of the run — 40% off everything plus an extra 10% for members, with a live countdown timer — and the render backs it up cleanly. The stack is dense (countdown → 40% off hero → category nav → 7/$35 panty deal → free tote → shop-by-category grid → rewards → trending), but each block is legible and the hero is unmistakable within the first screen: "40% Off Everything" in giant type with "Free Shipping on $35+ Orders" underneath.
- Compared to 08-12's "One Day Only: Members Shop 30% Off," this is a stronger offer (40% vs 30%) delivered with the same countdown-clock urgency mechanic, so the brand is escalating rather than repeating. It reads as the strongest single offer in the brand history log to date.
- Where it loses points is stacking — this is the kitchen-sink template again (three separate offers: 40% off, 7/$35 panties, free tote), which dilutes the single clear CTA the rubric rewards, and by the time a reader scrolls to "Welcome back" and "Now Trending" the urgency has faded. Still, the top-of-email execution is tight and the offer is honest and unburied. Solid, above-average send.

## What's working

- Live countdown clock ("16 hours 32 mins 48 secs") at the very top creates credible, immediate urgency.
- "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE Extra 10% Off" is called out separately from the 40% off, layering value without confusing the primary offer.
- Hero image (three models in varied swim/lingerie styles) is well-composed and matches the "Everything" breadth of the offer.
- Clean category nav (Bras, Panties, Sleep, Lingerie, Activewear, Beauty) gives multiple clickable entry points right under the hero.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, buttons are visible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers (40% off everything, 7/$35 panty party, free tote with $100+) stacked in one email splits attention instead of driving one action.
- "JOIN FREE FOR EXTRA 10% OFF" CTA is a text link, not a button — it's the weakest-looking CTA on the page despite gating a real discount.
- By the "Welcome back" / rewards / trending sections near the bottom, the countdown urgency has completely evaporated — feels like two emails glued together.
- Preview text ("Shop the app now for $30 off...") doesn't appear to reflect the actual lead offer in the render (40% off), a mismatch a subscriber would notice.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one offer per send, or clearly subordinate the panty party and free tote as "also today" strips below a single dominant CTA button — the 40% off hero should own the whole first screen with one big SHOP NOW button, not a five-cell category grid competing for the click.
- 2. Make "JOIN FREE FOR EXTRA 10% OFF" a real button, not a text link — it's gating an extra 10% and currently looks like fine print.
- 3. Repeat the countdown timer (or at least "Ends tonight") near the free tote and panty party sections so urgency carries through the whole email, not just the top.
- 4. Align preview text to the actual hero offer so open expectations match what's inside.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today Only: 40% Off Everything (+10% More for Members)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: 40% Off Sitewide Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get an extra 10% off — plus a free tote with $100+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends at midnight: 40% off everything, no exclusions on doorbusters.`
