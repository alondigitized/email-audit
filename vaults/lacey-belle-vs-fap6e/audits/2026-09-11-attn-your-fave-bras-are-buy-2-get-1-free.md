# Attn: Your Fave Bras Are Buy 2, Get 1 FREE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- "The Bra Event" delivers exactly what the subject promises — "Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free" sits directly under the hero in bold serif type, above a clean "SHOP NOW" button, with a lingerie-flatlay hero image and zero ambiguity about the offer. This is a straightforward, well-executed BOGO send that doesn't bury the lede.
- Where it loses points is length and repetition: this email keeps going for a very long scroll — Lounge, Work, Workout, Hitting the Town, Going Glam category blocks, then a second unrelated "$6 Mists & Body Care" promo, then a third "Free Cinched Tote" promo, then a rewards/trending grid. Three stacked offers in one email dilutes the primary Buy 2 Get 1 hook, and by the time a reader hits the tote offer they've forgotten the bra deal. Compared to 2026-09-10's "Glisten Up" send, which stayed tight on one collection, this one sprawls.
- Verdict: strong top-of-funnel execution, weak email architecture — should be trimmed to the bra event plus maybe one cross-sell, not three competing offers.

## What's working

- Primary offer ("Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 Free") is legible instantly, above the fold, with a bold "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Hero image (models holding/wearing bras against a lavender-to-black gradient) is high production value with no render bugs visible.
- Category breakdown (Lounge, Work, Workout, Hitting the Town, Going Glam) gives the shopper a clear occasion-based path into the bra assortment.
- "The World's #1 Bra Brand" section adds a credible trust/social-proof beat mid-email.
- Bottom grid (Welcome Back / Rewards / $25 Off / trending products) gives loyalty members a relevant reason to keep scrolling.

## What's weak

- Three distinct offers compete in one email — Buy 2 Get 1 Free bras, $6 Mists/Body Care, and Free Cinched Tote w/ $100+ — which splits attention and weakens the headline promo.
- The email is extremely long; a mobile reader has to scroll through 5 category sections plus 2 more promos before reaching the loyalty/rewards footer.
- "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" fine print ("Only up to $76.65 each. Excludes Pink. Pink pieces and clearance. Lowest-priced items will be provided at discount") is small and easy to miss relative to the bold headline claim — borderline bait-and-switch on "FREE."
- No visible urgency/deadline on the main bra offer itself (just "Limited Time" tag, no date), despite the brand's recent cadence of hard deadlines ("Last Day," "Ends Tonight").

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer. Lead with Buy 2 Get 1 Free bras and the category grid, move the Mists/Body Care and Free Tote offers to separate sends — this is the single biggest lift available, since right now the CTA gets diluted three times over.
- 2. Add a hard deadline to the bra event ("Ends Sunday" or similar) — the brand has trained readers on urgency all week (09-08, 09-07 sends), and this email's "Limited Time" tag is vague by comparison.
- 3. Surface the fine print earlier or bump its size slightly — "up to $76.65 each, excludes Pink" materially changes the offer and currently reads as an afterthought.
- 4. Shorten the middle category run from five sections to two or three strongest performers to reduce scroll fatigue.

- **Subject Alt B:** `2 Bras + 1 Free: The Bra Event Is Here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match, and save on your favorites — before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: shop by Lounge, Work, Workout & more.`
