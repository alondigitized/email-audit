# Today: 40% Off Bras, Sleep & More
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the best send in the run. After seven days of "Buy 2, Get 2 Free," Victoria's Secret changes the offer to "TODAY ONLY 40% OFF Everything." The hero is a big serif headline on black, with a free-shipping line and a row of black category buttons for Bras, Panties, Sleep, Lingerie, Activewear and Beauty. The eye lands on the offer first, and the one-day deadline is more believable than the stretched "Ends Today" wording on 10/26.
- Below the hero it turns into a catalog email. There is a $25 Eau de Parfum tile with a 6PM-5AM ET window, a Tuesday Panty Edit with 5/$35 panties, and a Free Tote with a $100+ purchase. After those come a category grid, a Rewards and credit card strip, and a Now Trending row. That is four or five competing offers under a clear lead. The fine print at the bottom is very long, with several exclusion paragraphs. Even so, the lead promo is clear and honest at a glance.
- Verdict: a strong, urgent, persona-relevant send, and a welcome change from the Buy 2 Get 2 fatigue. It loses points for clutter and dense legal text.

## What's working

- The hero "TODAY ONLY 40% OFF Everything" is large, high-contrast and readable in a second.
- The new offer breaks the Buy 2 Get 2 Free pattern from 10/21–10/26, so it feels fresh.
- The category buttons (Bras, Sleep, Lingerie and so on) match the subject and give a direct path to shop.
- Free shipping on $35+ is stated plainly under the headline.
- The $25 perfume tile has a specific price and a tight time window, and it adds a real urgency beat.
- The Free Tote tile has a clear threshold, and the product image is clean.

## What's weak

- Four or five offers (40% off, $25 perfume, 5/$35 panties, tote, rewards card) compete after the hero and dilute the message.
- The legal block at the bottom is very long and dense, and it lists many exclusions. That undercuts "Everything."
- The hero says "Everything," but the small print under the buttons mentions exclusions, collaborations and promotions. This is a mild honesty gap.
- The tote and perfume tiles have large empty areas that push content down. The email is about 6,500px tall.
- The rewards and credit card strip is small and low contrast. The "$25 Off Your First Purchase" benefit is buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle to the hero plus one secondary offer, such as the perfume. Move the tote and panty edit into a short row of tiles.
- 2. Put the exclusions in one short line right under the hero and link out to the full terms.
- 3. Add a countdown or an "Ends midnight PT" line under the headline so the urgency is explicit.
- 4. Make the member and credit card benefit a visible button near the hero for loyal members.
- 5. Link the category grid to specific best sellers with price points.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud send. The 50 tracked links couldn't be verified, and the QA run flagged missing or unconfirmed compliance headers. The confirmed code-level problems are 10+ plain-HTTP third-party Dotomi pixels, missing alt text, and zoom-blocking viewport settings. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and body links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** QA skipped 50 links on tracking and click-redirect domains (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com/...`) without probing them. Final landing URLs, redirect status and dead links are unconfirmed.
- **Duplicate open pixels:** Two `open.aspx` 1x1 pixels with the same `qs` prefix and `d=70265` appear. One is in the top `<div style="font-size:0">`. The other sits inside the header wrapper. This probably double-counts opens.
- **Third-party Dotomi pixels over HTTP:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. These are 11 cookie-sync pixels fired from an email. Because they use `http://`, clients and proxies may block them (QA flagged all of them). Privacy and consent exposure is also a concern.
- **Campaign tag in the UCM pixel:** `dtmc_email_category=20261027_VS_6397_FULFILE`. The pixel URL is truncated, so I can't confirm the full parameter set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. This is the one image flagged that is not a tracking pixel. Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no text.
- **Missing alt text, pixels:** The 11 Dotomi pixels have no alt. They are decorative, so `alt=""` would be correct. The current markup has no alt attribute at all.
- **Mixed content:** All 11 Dotomi images use `http://`, as above.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid or dead CSS:**
  - The first `<style>` block has `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` without the leading `.`, so the selector never matches.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title style="display:none...">` sits inside `<body>`, nested in a table cell.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
  - There is no `lang` attribute on `<html>`.
  - Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. Only some wrappers have it.
- **Leaked template code in a comment:** `<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->`. This is harmless, but it shows unprocessed template syntax.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The `#AMPScript` div is an empty wrapper with only `<!-- Ampscript v0 START/END -->` comments, and no unresolved `%%...%%` or `{{ }}` tokens appear in the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers. Treat both as unconfirmed, not as confirmed failures. If they are really absent, that's a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement miss.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer not inspected:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section. CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmable issues. Links go through SFMC click-wrapping, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be seen in the source. The only visible campaign identifier is `20261027_VS_6397_FULFILE` in the Dotomi pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a meaningful `alt` to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop them from the email body. Confirm they are consent-covered.
3. Remove the duplicate `open.aspx` pixel.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`) and move `<title>` into `<head>`. Add `lang` and `role="presentation"`.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, or the full HTML plus headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and let you inspect the footer.
7. Resolve the 50 click-tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe. That would verify final URLs and UTM carry-through.
