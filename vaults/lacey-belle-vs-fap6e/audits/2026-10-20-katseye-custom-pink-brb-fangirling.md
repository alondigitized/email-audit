# KATSEYE? Custom PINK? Brb, Fangirling 👙
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a pop-culture hero stacked on top of a promo pile. The KATSEYE group shot in custom PINK is the best asset, and it's a smart tie-in to the Fashion Show broadcast the brand pushed on 10/18 ("Shh! My Show's About to Start"). The subject promises that fangirl moment. But the hero offers only "GET THE LOOK" with no product or price, so it pays off the subject with a vibe and nothing to buy.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catch-all. It runs a free tote with $125+, a $10 reward for downloading the app and enabling push, a Member Days teaser, a $25 bonus card on $75+, an eight-tile category grid, an SMS signup and a $25 first-purchase coupon. Each offer is clear on its own, but together they compete and none is the obvious primary. The app-download and push-notification steps ask a lot of someone who just wanted the KATSEYE content. The fine print runs on for several screens.
- Judgement: a strong top third and a cluttered rest. It's a typical 7 for this brand. It matches the "catch-all PINK" pattern of the 10/15 to 10/19 sends, with a better hero than most.

## What's working

- The KATSEYE hero is on-brand, young and energetic, and it matches the audience. The "SING. DANCE. STRUT." copy is fun.
- It follows up on the 10/18 Fashion Show send, so the timing feels current.
- The tote module is clean and legible. "FREE TOTE WITH ANY $125+ PURCHASE" is a concrete offer with a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- The member modules are clear. The $10 reward is a steps layout, and "Coming tomorrow: Member Days" builds anticipation.
- The pink and black blocks create strong visual rhythm and break up a long email.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA, "GET THE LOOK," has no product, price or destination cue. The subject's curiosity doesn't turn into a reason to click.
- Too many competing offers (tote, $10 reward, $25 bonus card, $25 first purchase). None is clearly primary, and the $125+ and $75+ thresholds conflict.
- The $10 reward needs an app download and push opt-in. This is friction for a reward the email presents as free.
- The legal copy is a wall of tiny text at the bottom. It makes offers like the $25 holiday bonus card feel like they hide conditions.
- The generic category grid and "all over your feed" section feel like filler after the hero, and they dilute the KATSEYE story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one hero offer. Either tie the KATSEYE look to shoppable products (a "Shop the KATSEYE x PINK custom pieces" grid with prices) or lead with the tote. Move the app-download and $10 reward block lower.
- 2. Make the CTA specific: "Shop the KATSEYE Looks" instead of "Get the Look."
- 3. Cut the bonus card, first-purchase coupon and category grid, or fold them into one footer strip. Fewer than four modules would help.
- 4. Shorten the legal copy, and show the key conditions (dates, thresholds) next to each offer.
- 5. Reconsider the daily cadence. This is the 7th straight catch-all in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free tote + the KATSEYE x PINK looks 🖤`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get the Fashion Show looks, plus a free tote with $125+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: earn a free $10 reward and shop KATSEYE's custom PINK.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has structural defects: a `<table>` and JSON-LD block inside `<head>`, and malformed CSS selectors. It also loads 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels (Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com`) that lack alt attributes. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because the relay did not capture the headers. The source was also truncated, so the body, footer and most links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 43 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images use `http://`:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261020_PK_6348_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Mixed content:** Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or block mixed content, may drop these pixels.
- **Cookie-sync redirects:** `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1–10.php` are ten cookie-sync pixels. Each carries `a4edea106b33…=1`, which matches the `dtm_email_hash` value in the page URLs. This is the same hashed email identifier sent to a third-party ad-tech domain.
- **Repeated URL:** All three JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries use the identical `url` (`…/us/pink/fashion-show/shop-the-show?cm_mmc=di-_-102026435-_-VSD_VSL_20261020_PK_6348_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=102026435…`), so the cards are not differentiated.
- **Open pixel:** The 1×1 open pixel `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is present, with `alt=""`. This is fine.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** `<head>` contains `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">`, `<td>` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. A table is not valid in `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table, which can push the following `<meta name="viewport">` and both `<style>` blocks into `<body>`. That behavior varies by client.
- **Broken CSS selectors in the first `<style>` block:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an element selector, not a property, so it has no effect.
  - `max-height:auto !important` (in the `img` and `.header img` rules) is not a valid value.
- **Overbroad table rules:** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td {max-width:600px!important;}` apply to every table and cell, including nested ones.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` disables pinch-zoom where honored (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Outlook line-height:** The MSO conditional sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td`, which can clip wrapped text in Outlook desktop.
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt attribute. This is the only non-tracking image flagged. If it carries content, it is inaccessible with images off.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `o.gif`, `UCMController` and `pub1–10.php` also lack alt. For these, `alt=""` is the correct value, and the current attribute is absent.
- **Pass rate:** QA reports 57%, with 0 issues and 3 warnings.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) appear in the visible source. `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp` are all populated. The remainder of the body was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe` header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this cannot be confirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found:** There is no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058). The same relay caveat applies. One-click support is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** The header was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while links and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment cannot be assessed without the headers.
- **Footer not inspected:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated source.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` appear in every PromotionCard URL, and the same hash goes to Dotomi (see Section 2). This is a privacy and disclosure consideration. It is not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Parameter scheme:** The links use `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp`. There are no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters. Attribution relies on the vendor scheme, which is consistent across all three cards and carries the campaign ID `20261020_PK_6348_FILEBAL`.
- **Landing path:** The JSON-LD links go to `/us/pink/fashion-show/shop-the-show`. The 43 tracked links were not probed, so I could not check whether the landing page matches the subject (KATSEYE and custom PINK). The visible JSON-LD cards all point to the same fashion-show page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block and its wrapper table out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. Keep only `<meta>`, `<style>` and `<title>` in `<head>`.
2. Fix the selectors: change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{…}`, and remove `max-height:auto`.
3. Scope the `table{max-width}` rules to the `.container` class.
4. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`, without `maximum-scale` or `user-scalable=0`.
5. Switch `mi.victoriassecret.com` and the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
6. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` if it conveys content.
7. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers. That would resolve `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
8. Give each JSON-LD `PromotionCard` its own destination URL, or reduce the set to one.
9. Re-run on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and the other 43 links. Resolve the tracking redirects to confirm the landing pages.
