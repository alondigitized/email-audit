# 10/$40 Panties Is Almost Over 😭
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's fourth consecutive day leading with the 10/$40 panty formula — and the subject line finally admits it: "Almost Over." That last-call framing is the right move after three days of stock-up messaging, and the orange hero with the giant "10/$40 PANTY PARTY" lockup hits the eye before anything else. It's a confident, well-paced last-call send.
- The body does a lot more than the subject promises though — there's a 50% off bra block, a $25 Pink Bra promo, an Anywear collection moment, a "FINAL HOURS $25" beach hero, a $30 savings panel, and a value-tier strip at the bottom. That's six distinct offers stacked on top of the panty headline. For a subscriber, the deadline framing earns the open; for a marketer, the email is doing too much.
- Net: strong subject, strong hero, but the scroll dilutes the urgency the subject set up.

## What's working

- Subject finally adds urgency ("Almost Over 😭") after three days of flat repetition — earns the open.
- Hero lockup is unmistakable: orange, oversized "10/$40," beach-party energy on-brand for Memorial Day weekend.
- "FINAL HOURS $25" mid-email beach hero reinforces the deadline visually.
- Up to 50% off bra block and $25 Pink Bra add real secondary value for non-panty shoppers.
- Clean product grid at the bottom gives entry points across bras, undies, and loungewear.

## What's weak

- Email is six offers long — panty hero, 50% off bras, $25 bra, Anywear, $25 final hours, $30 savings, value strip. Urgency dies by scroll three.
- Two different "final hours" anchors ($25 and 10/$40) compete and confuse the deadline.
- No countdown / specific end time despite the "Almost Over" promise.
- "SAVE $30" panel mid-scroll is generic and breaks the panty-party thread.
- Crying-face emoji in the subject is melodramatic for a recurring weekly promo subscribers have seen four days in a row.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** Lead with panty hero → one credible deadline ("Ends tonight 11:59 PT") → single secondary block (the $25 bra) → footer. The "Almost Over" subject demands a tight, urgent body.
- 2. **Put a real clock on it.** Replace vague "Almost Over" with an actual end time in both hero and subject — last-call emails live or die on credibility.
- 3. **Kill the duplicate $25 hero.** Either the panties are ending or the $25 bra is ending — pick one ending for this send.
- 4. **Swap the 😭 for time language.** The face emoji is whining; "Ends Tonight" or a ⏰ does the same work with more authority.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 10/$40 panties (ends 11:59 PT)`
