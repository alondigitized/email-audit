## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The email has no hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). Technical debt is concentrated in 12 insecure `http://` tracking pixels with no alt text, markup placed in `<head>`, a zoom-disabling viewport meta, and email-hash identifiers in URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Insecure pixels:** 12 images load over `http://`, which risks blocking or mixed-content handling:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?...`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party sync pixels:** The ten `pub1–pub10.php` pixels are Dotomi cookie-redirect calls. Each is an extra third-party request on open, and each carries the hashed email (`a4edea10…c6fe=1`).
- **Unprobed links:** 51 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so their resolution and final status are unverified.
- **Destination:** The four JSON-LD `PromotionCard` URLs all point to `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/underwear/underwear-offer?...`, which is consistent. I could not probe the destination.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Alt text:** QA flagged these images without alt text:
  - `o.gif`, `UCMController` and `pub1–pub10.php`: tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""` and be hidden.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`: a content image, which needs descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid `<head>` structure:** The `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the JSON-LD `<script>` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Tables are not valid in `<head>`, and parsers will typically close `<head>` early. That can push the viewport/charset meta and styles into `<body>`.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches nothing. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an invalid selector.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with `role="presentation"` and self-closed `<meta ... />`. This is a minor inconsistency, and clients tolerate it.
- **JSON-LD:** `PromotionCard` is not a recognized schema.org type for Gmail email markup. The block likely has no effect, and it is only valid if registered with Google. Four cards share one URL.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the body is unchecked.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** Several parameters carry recipient identifiers:
  - `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe`, a 64-hex-character value that looks like SHA-256.
  - `customerID=43cece97…1e2f`
  - `mi_u=200492586522`
  - The `a4edea10…` hash is also sent to third-party `ads.dotomi.com` over plain HTTP, so it can be observed in transit.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. QA notes AgentMail may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay.
- **Body footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.
- **Sending domain:** The From domain `e2.vspink.com` was not independently verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **Tracking scheme:** The email uses Cheetah/Dotomi-style parameters rather than `utm_*`: `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid=100526425`, `mi_ecmp=20261005_PK_6244_FILBLNC`. The campaign ID is consistent across the JSON-LD URLs and pixels.
- **No UTM parameters:** None appear in the visible source. Attribution depends on the proprietary parameters surviving the redirect, which I could not confirm.
- **Landing page:** I could not verify that `/us/pink/underwear/underwear-offer` matches the "8/$38" subject offer.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Move all tracking pixels to HTTPS, or drop the 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` sync pixels. Add `alt=""` to each pixel and to decorative images.
2. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the JSON-LD wrapper table out of `<head>`. Place the `<script>` in `<head>` on its own, or move the markup into `<body>`.
5. Fix the invalid CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`).
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message at the source ESP, not the AgentMail relay.
7. Review sending hashed email and `customerID` identifiers to third-party hosts over HTTP.
8. Probe the 51 skipped tracking links with a redirect-follow check to verify final landing and parameter preservation.
