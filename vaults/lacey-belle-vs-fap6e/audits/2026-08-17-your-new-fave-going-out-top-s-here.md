- Three different discount mechanics stacked in one email (25% off $75+, $25 tees, $35 sweats) dilutes the single-offer clarity the hero promised.

- Fine print under the 25% banner ("Excludes select third-party styles") is a mild honesty ding, though not buried.

- 2. Drop from three offers to one: lead with 25% off $75+ as the umbrella discount and fold tees/sweats into "also eligible" rather than separate anchor prices — reduces cognitive load.
