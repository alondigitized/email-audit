# Happening NOW: Shop The Show
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Happening NOW" email for the Fashion Show that is built like a regular catalog email. It never commits to one job. The subject sells the live show, and the hero says "SHOP THE SHOW" with a model in a Fashion Show robe. Then the email turns into a Bombshell Runway fragrance module, a black-and-white tune-in block, a "Free Fashion Show Tote" offer, a category grid and a "Trending" product grid. Everything is on brand, but the offer, the tune-in info and the products all compete.
- The best parts are the "LIVE TONIGHT" block, with its Pink Carpet, Live Show and TikTok After Party times, and the tote offer (free with a $125+ purchase, code RUNWAY125). The weakest part is the order. Tune-in info should come first, because "Happening NOW" promises it. The "see it, buy it" idea in the hero is strong, but the fragrance module sits between the hero and the show details and dilutes it. The footer is also long, with dense fine print.
- Compared with this morning's "TONIGHT: Tune In to Watch the Show" (7/10), this send is a weaker version of the same beat. That earlier email had a countdown and a single focus. Two Fashion Show sends in one day is a heavy cadence, and this one gives a reader less reason to open it.

## What's working

- The hero "SHOP THE SHOW" with a black "SHOP LOOKS FROM THE RUNWAY" button is clear and matches the subject.
- The "LIVE TONIGHT" block uses big black type and lists specific times (4:30PM PT, 5:45PM PT after party), so the urgency is credible.
- The tote module has a concrete offer, a visible "SHOP NOW" button and a code, RUNWAY125.
- The category tiles (Fashion Show Merch, Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty and so on) give shoppers an easy way to browse.
- The app-download strip and the "$25 Off First Purchase" rewards card are visible in the render.

## What's weak

- The email has four or five competing modules (hero, fragrance, live show, tote, grid), so there is no single focus.
- The fragrance module sits above the show info and pushes the "happening now" content down the page.
- The tune-in CTA, "WATCH NOW", is below the first screen on mobile, so the "NOW" in the subject isn't backed up near the top.
- The tote is described as "a $59.95 value" and has a $125+ threshold, plus a code, a one-per-customer limit and "while quantities last". That is a lot of conditions for a "free" gift.
- There is a lot of fine print at the bottom, and the "Trending" grid only shows "Just Dropped / Trending" labels with no prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "LIVE TONIGHT" block to the top, with a "WATCH LIVE" button. Put "Shop The Show" below it as the second action.
- 2. Cut the fragrance module, or move it below the tote. One shopping offer is enough for a tune-in email.
- 3. Put the tote offer in the hero so the subject's "Shop" has a concrete reward, and use "Get the Free Tote" as the button.
- 4. Add prices or "from $" to the trending grid and shorten the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Live now: watch, then shop the runway`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Show is LIVE — free tote inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pink Carpet is on. Stream free and shop the looks as they walk.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Fashion Show tote with $125+, code RUNWAY125, tonight only.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has a malformed document structure (a `<table>` and `<script>` inside `<head>`) and about 11 third-party tracking pixels that load over plain HTTP without alt text. The compliance and authentication findings come from headers the AgentMail relay didn't capture, so they can't be confirmed from this data. QA pass rate is 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 45 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). Their destinations are unverified.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, each with `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`.
- An eleventh `http://` pixel loads from `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...`.
- Mail clients will block the `http://` images or flag them as mixed content. Cookie syncing will then fail silently, and any client that does load them leaks the recipient's hashed email to a third party.
- The open pixel `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?APGT5LHH635UTLGNSH5KYETADY.70263&d=70263&bmt=0` is HTTPS and correctly sized (1x1, `alt=""`). No issue.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Invalid document structure:** The `<head>` contains a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` with a JSON-LD `<script>`. Table content belongs in `<body>`, and parsers will auto-close `<head>` at the table. The `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<style>` blocks that follow may then be dropped or misplaced. The mobile media queries and the viewport meta are at risk.
- **Misplaced `<title>`:** `<title style="display: none; ...">` sits inside the body, inside a table cell. It is invalid there, and the empty title does nothing.
- **Malformed CSS:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it is a type selector that matches nothing. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an invalid selector.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text:** 11 tracking pixels (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php` and the `UCMController` image) have none. A 12th image, `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, also has none. It is the only content image flagged, so it needs a meaningful `alt` or `alt=""` if decorative. Screen readers will read out the file name or URL.
- **Global table CSS:** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` is applied to every table, which can clip nested layout tables.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with self-closing `<meta ... />` syntax, which is a minor inconsistency.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the truncated source. The truncation means the rest of the body is unchecked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN). The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a sender omission.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found (WARN). Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this is worth confirming on the raw message.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this data.
- The visible source is truncated, so the footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address couldn't be verified.
- The sender domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with good practice, but alignment is unconfirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The JSON-LD `url` for the promo card is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/newarrivals/vs-icon-shoppe?cm_mmc=di-_-101826525-_-VSD_VSL_20261018_VS_6337_FULLFILEOPTDOWNS-_&d_cellid=101826525&...`.
- It uses the proprietary `cm_mmc` (Coremetrics-style) parameter plus `d_cellid` and `mi_ecmp`. It has no standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, so GA-based attribution depends on the retailer's own tagging.
- The URL carries `dtm_email_hash`, `mi_u` and `customerID` (a hashed identifier). Hashed PII in a landing-page URL gets logged by analytics, referrers and proxies.
- That landing page is `/newarrivals/vs-icon-shoppe`, not an obvious "Shop The Show" page. The subject says "Shop The Show", so this may be a continuity gap. The destinations of the main CTAs are hidden behind skipped tracking redirects and can't be verified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Move the `<table>`/JSON-LD block out of `<head>`. Put the schema script in `<body>`, or in `<head>` as a bare `<script>`. Move `<title>` to `<head>`.
2. Serve all Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop them. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to each.
3. Add `alt` to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message, since the relay may have stripped them. Also confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e1.victoriassecret.com`.
6. Add standard `utm_*` parameters, or confirm the retailer's own scheme feeds the analytics stack. Stop putting `customerID` and `dtm_email_hash` in URLs where possible.
7. Resolve the 45 skipped tracking links in a follow-up probe. Check that the primary CTA lands on a "Shop The Show" page.
