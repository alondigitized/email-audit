# Today Only: $25 Sweats If You Buy 3
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK's same kitchen-sink megasend, running since 08-06, but for once the subject actually earns its "Today Only" urgency — the hero opens on "SWEATSHIRTS & SWEATPANTS $25 EACH / When You Buy 3+" and matches the promised offer almost word-for-word, unlike recent sends that buried the subject's promo below three other things. The buy-3 tiered pricing structure ("$25 each, $30 Each For 2, $35 For 1") is genuinely a strong offer worth surfacing clearly.
- But the execution has a real defect this time: scrolling past the beauty section lands on a section literally labeled "SOMETHING" over an empty pink gradient blob with no product, no copy, and no CTA beyond a generic app-download button. That reads as a broken or unfinished template block, not intentional design — it's the kind of visible bug that erodes trust in a "polished" retail brand.
- Everything else is the familiar PINK sprawl: sweats hero, then tees/shorts, then bra promo, then back-to-campus beauty, then the broken block, then app download, then a nine-tile category grid, then loyalty, then "all over your feed" UGC. It's long and undifferentiated from the last three weeks of sends, but the top-of-email hero-to-subject match is the best I've seen from this brand recently.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy match tightly: "$25 Sweats If You Buy 3" → "$25 EACH When You Buy 3+" is visible without much scrolling.
- The tiered pricing table (3+/2/1) is legible and makes the "buy more, save more" banner at the very top feel earned rather than vague.
- Clear, high-contrast "SHOP NOW" CTA buttons repeated after each offer block, so the eye always has a next action.
- Model demographics (young, athletic, campus-styled) match the loyalty/back-to-school positioning later in the email.

## What's weak

- The "SOMETHING" section is a visible render bug/placeholder — empty gradient, no product, no real copy, sitting mid-email where a hero product shot should be.
- This is the fourth+ nearly identical kitchen-sink structure in a row (VS Fashion Show 08-25 through today) — same length, same stacked-offer format, just re-ordered.
- Bra promo ("$10 OFF A FULL-PRICED BRA") is in-store only, buried mid-email after two online offers — easy to miss and creates a mixed online/in-store message in one send.
- The email is extremely long (9+ distinct offer blocks before the footer) with no way to jump to the "today only" deal without scrolling past unrelated beauty and bra content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or cut the "SOMETHING" placeholder block — either it's a shipped bug (highest priority fix) or unfinished creative that should never have gone out; either way it's actively damaging to brand perception.
- 2. Move the buy-3 sweats tiered pricing table higher/bigger — it's the strongest, most concrete offer in the email and currently competes visually with the hero image instead of leading it.
- 3. Split the in-store-only bra offer into its own section header or remove it from this online-focused send to avoid confusing "shop now" fatigue.
- 4. Vary the template structure — five sends running the identical stacked-block layout is cadence fatigue; even reordering isn't enough anymore.
