# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I land on the VS homepage and immediately get a rewards pop-up in my face before I even see what's new — classic VS move, honestly. The imagery I can see is very on-brand: close-up lace bra, a lifestyle shot of someone who looks like she's having a great beach day. But the left half of the hero is completely blank white, which is jarring, and the modal is eating most of my screen. Right now this feels less like a destination and more like a speed bump.

## What's working

- **The lace bra close-up** is gorgeous and immediately tells me I'm in the right place — I came here for bra content and that's exactly what I see.
- **The VS & PINK Rewards modal** is actually useful for me as a non-member: it shows me three specific, concrete benefits (Points Per $, $10 reward, Birthday Gift) rather than vague "exclusive access" language.
- **"Already a member? Sign In"** link inside the modal is a nice touch — it doesn't force me to sit through a rewards pitch if I'm already enrolled.
- **The lifestyle shot** (woman in blue swimwear/lingerie with a yellow bag outdoors) has a warm, summery energy that fits the VS brand and feels current.

## What's weak

- **Blank left-hero column** — there's a huge chunk of white where headline copy should be. This looks like a render failure on mobile and it's disorienting. I don't know what I'm shopping for.
- **The modal fires immediately** before I've seen anything, which is a dark pattern. I haven't even had a chance to want something yet, and VS is already asking me to join a program.
- **No visible offer in the hero** — no Semi-Annual Sale teaser, no percentage off, no free-panty promo. As someone who has those emails calendared, this doesn't match the deal-forward experience I expect from VS.
- **Navigation is just a hamburger** — I can't get to Bras or PINK in one tap. I'm stuck either closing this modal or hitting a menu I have to dig through.
- **The top banner** is pink and present but the text is completely illegible at this viewport — I can see there's something there, but I can't read it.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the mobile hero rendering** — the blank left column needs to display the headline copy. Whatever split-layout is being used is breaking on this viewport; swap to a stacked full-width layout for mobile if the two-column approach isn't loading correctly.
- **Delay or reduce the rewards modal** — show it after one scroll or user interaction, not on landing. Or trigger it only for non-logged-in, non-cookie'd visitors. Right now it's the first thing I see, which is a conversion killer.
- **Surface a deal above the fold** — even a banner strip saying "Free Panty with any purchase" or "Bras from $35" would match what I'm already primed for from my emails.
- **Add a sticky nav strip** with 2–3 tappable category pills (Bras, PINK, Sale) so I don't have to open the hamburger just to get to my category.
