# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- OK so I love VS and this is literally my brand, but opening the app to a broken homepage with a giant white void where the hero should be is not a vibe. What I *can* see tells me VS is pushing bras + rewards right now — the "Buy 2, Get 1 Free Bras" deal on the product rail and a rewards signup modal are both front and center — but the hero image failed to load on my phone and that's a pretty bad first impression even for a loyalist like me.

## What's working

- **VS & PINK Rewards modal** — "Points Per $, $10 Reward, Birthday Gift" icons are exactly the kind of loyalty loop that keeps me coming back. The "Already a member? Sign In" link is a warm returning-shopper hook.
- **"Buy 2, Get 1 Free Bras" deal** — shown right on the product cards with price ($74.95, $59.95). As someone who stocks up on bras, this is genuinely compelling.
- **"Push-Ups That Lift" module** — partially visible behind the modal, that label is targeted directly at what I shop.
- **Product rail imagery** — the Dream Angels Butterflies Embroidery Balconette and the Invisibra Push-Up Strapless both look beautiful; the lace shot especially catches my eye.
- **FREE Beach Tote GWP** — $150 threshold for a free tote is reachable for me in a single bra haul and feels like a legitimately fun summer perk.

## What's weak

- **Broken hero** — the entire left portion of the hero (majority of the viewport) is blank white. This is the single biggest above-the-fold real estate on the page and it's just... nothing. The right side shows a solid teal rectangle with no readable copy.
- **No shopping CTA in the hero** — even if the hero had loaded, all I see is a rewards JOIN NOW button. There's no "Shop Bras," "Shop PINK," or "See the Sale" button for someone who already knows what they want.
- **Nav is collapsed behind a hamburger** — I can only see the menu icon in the top right. One-tap access to Bras, PINK, or Semi-Annual Sale is completely hidden.
- **Zero trust or social proof** — no star ratings on the product cards, no "Free Returns" callout, nothing at checkout depth.
- **Rewards modal blocks content** — it's covering at least one module I can half-see ("Push-Ups That Lift"). If I dismiss it, will I even know that content was there?

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero render on mobile** — whatever the lazy-load or image CDN issue is, a blank hero for bra shoppers opening the app on iPhone is a conversion killer. Run a synthetic mobile test on every deploy.
- **Add a persistent "Shop Bras" or "Shop PINK" sticky CTA** below the modal so the rewards push doesn't cannibalize shopping intent — let me do both.
- **Put the Buy 2 Get 1 Free deal in a sticky banner strip at the top**, not just on product cards. That's the kind of offer that should be the first thing I read, not something I scroll to discover.
- **Surface at least one navigation chip** (Bras, PINK, Sale) above the fold so I don't have to open the hamburger — on mobile, one tap to my category is the difference between browsing and bouncing.
