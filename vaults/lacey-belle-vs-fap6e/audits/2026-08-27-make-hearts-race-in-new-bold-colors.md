# Make Hearts Race in NEW Bold Colors
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a car-themed catalog lap, not a "Bold Colors" launch — the subject promises new bold colors, but the hero is "Styles to Start Your Engines," a racy roadster shoot with bright prints, then it just funnels into the same Bras → Panties → Lingerie category trio the brand runs constantly. There's no single hero product in a new colorway anywhere near the top; it's a general spring/racing-motif refresh dressed up with subject-line urgency it doesn't deliver on.
- Past that, the email sprawls: Limited Time panty packs, a Tease fragrance cross-sell, a Fashion Show 2026 Watch Party Guide, an app-exclusive sweepstakes, and a "Welcome back" rewards/trending strip — five distinct offers stacked in one send. It's comprehensive to the point of diluting itself; nothing gets the singular focus "Make Hearts Race in NEW Bold Colors" implies.
- Compared to 8/26's "Push-Ups, Perfected" (7/10, single clear hero+CTA) and 8/24's Leonisa launch (7/10, genuine new-product story), this one regresses toward the unfocused "five-way sorter" pattern from 8/21. Judgment: mid-pack execution, subject-to-content mismatch drags it down.

## What's working

- Hero image is bold and on-brand — sports car, saturated prints, clear "SHOP NOW" button with no scroll needed.
- Clean black-button CTA pattern repeats consistently across every section (Bras, Panties, Lingerie, bundle, watch guide), so the eye always knows where to click.
- The "Limited Time" panty pack callout gives a concrete price anchor ("FROM $20").
- Good category segmentation with distinct hero images per section (Bras/Panties/Lingerie), so a shopper can visually jump to what she wants.
- Footer personalization touch — "Welcome back," loyalty status, and "$25 OFF" reward tile for returning/member shoppers.

## What's weak

- Subject says "NEW Bold Colors" but nothing in the render calls out a specific new colorway — the hero is a racing/print motif, not a color launch.
- Too many competing offers in one send (bras/panties/lingerie, panty packs, Tease bundle, Fashion Show watch party, app sweepstakes, rewards) — no clear single priority.
- The Fashion Show "Watch Party Guide" and Tease fragrance blocks are unrelated tangents that push the actual bra/panty offer further down and add scroll fatigue.
- "Trending Now" model grid at the very bottom is generic filler that doesn't map to anything above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero to actually deliver on the subject — lead with one new bold-colorway product shot and drop the racing-car metaphor, or change the subject to match the racing/roadster theme that's actually there.
- 2. Cut the email down to one primary offer above the fold (bra/panty/lingerie trio) and move Tease fragrance, Fashion Show guide, and the app sweepstakes to a separate send — this email is doing the job of three.
- 3. Move the Limited Time panty pack pricing ("FROM $20") higher, right under the hero, since it's the strongest concrete offer in the whole email.
- 4. Replace the generic "Trending Now" grid with a repeat CTA back to the hero offer, since by that point the shopper has scrolled past five unrelated pitches.
