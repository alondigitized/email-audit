# 30% Off Bras—Shop to Beat the Rush
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the bra-led cut of Victoria's Secret's week-long 30% Off Everything push, and it's the same kitchen-sink template with a new headline on top. The hero is strong. A top banner says "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH," and a model in pink lingerie sits over a big "30% Off All Bras" with a "SHOP NOW" button. The eye lands on the offer first.
- After the hero, the email stops being about bras. It stacks a 7/$35 Panty Party with a size selector, a second 30% Off Everything block with category links, a FREE Quilted Duffle with a $125+ purchase, a category tile grid, a rewards/credit card block and a Trending row. A subscriber who got "30% Off Fine Fragrances" on 11/20 and "7/$35 Panties + 30% Off" on 11/21 has seen nearly all of this. The subject promises bras, but only the first screen delivers on it.
- Verdict: a solid top fold with a diluted body. The subject is concrete and the offer is clear, but the email repeats the week's creative, so it feels like send #6 of the same campaign.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: "30% Off All Bras" in large type with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- "LIMITED TIME" labels and the "Beat the Rush" banner add urgency that fits Black Friday week.
- The panty grid with a XS–XXL size selector is a useful shortcut. It lets the shopper start browsing by size.
- Category buttons (Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear) under the second 30% block help shoppers who don't want bras.
- The pink palette and models in pajamas keep the look on-brand and seasonal.

## What's weak

- Four competing offers (bras, 7/$35 panties, 30% everything, free duffle) split attention after a clear hero.
- The second "30% Off Everything" block largely repeats the first one. It makes the email feel padded.
- The fine print under the duffle, and the legal block at the bottom, are dense and tiny. They bury the exclusions and the code GIFT25.
- The bra hero has no product detail or social proof, such as a specific style, price or reviews, so there is little to click on beyond the generic offer.
- Panty and bra tiles in the "Trending" row show "Just Dropped" and "Trending" labels with no visible prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email actually about bras. Replace the panty and duffle blocks with a 3–4 product bra grid showing price, a best-seller or review tag, and a size/style selector.
- 2. Move the duffle and panty offers to a single slim banner below the hero, or cut them for this send.
- 3. Cut the duplicate 30% Off Everything block. Keep one set of category links.
- 4. Pull the key exclusions and the GIFT25 code into readable text near the offer instead of the fine print.
- 5. Add credible urgency with a specific end date or time instead of "Limited Time."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is structurally invalid (body content and `<script>` placed inside `<head>`) and carries about 10 third-party `http://` cookie-sync pixels from Dotomi. The QA run passed 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **60 tracking links were not probed** (click-redirect domains), so their destinations are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Mixed-content or non-HTTPS images may be blocked by clients. The sync calls then fail silently, which degrades retargeting match rates.
  - Each URL carries `a4edea106b33…=1`, the SHA-256 email hash, in a plaintext HTTP request. The hash is exposed to anyone on the network path.
- **The open-tracking pixel is fine:** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is HTTPS, 1×1, with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. The `<title style="display:none">` is empty and sits inside the body. Parsers will auto-close `<head>` early, so the viewport and charset meta tags may land in the body.
  - The JSON-LD `PromotionCard` is Gmail-specific markup, but its placement is invalid.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). Most email clients ignore this tag, so the impact is limited to webview contexts.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `…/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg`
  - `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
  - If these are content images, they need alt text. The 10 Dotomi pixels also lack alt, but those are tracking pixels where `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Malformed CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{` is missing its leading dot, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are no-ops.
- **Conflicting width rules:** `table{max-width:600px!important}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important}` apply to every table and cell. This can clip nested fluid layouts.
- The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the markup uses `role="presentation"`, which is a mismatch with no practical effect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the provided source. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, the email fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.** The sender domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked** because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **The tracking parameters are not UTM.** The JSON-LD URL `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/bras?cm_mmc=di-_-112226535-_-VSD_VSL_20261122_VS_6529_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=112226535&mi_ecmp=20261122_VS_6529_FILEBAL&…` uses Coremetrics (`cm_mmc`) and mi_/d_cellid parameters. There is no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. GA-based attribution depends on these being mapped server-side.
- **Hashed identifiers are passed in the query string:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…` are both SHA-256. They persist in browser history, server logs and referrers.
- **The landing path matches the subject.** `/us/vs/bras` fits "30% Off Bras". Whether the 30% offer is reflected on that page was not tested.
- Only one destination URL was visible. The other 59+ tracked links were not resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw message headers and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Treat this as a relay-capture gap until confirmed.
2. Move the Dotomi sync pixels to HTTPS and add `alt=""`. Review whether sending the email hash over HTTP is acceptable.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the two image assets listed in section 3.
4. Move the `<script type="application/ld+json">` and its wrapper table out of `<head>`. Put the `<title>` and `<meta>` tags in the head with the correct order.
5. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Fix or delete the malformed selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`).
