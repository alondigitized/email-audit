# New NFL Collection 💅 Yay Sports!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- The subject "New NFL Collection 💅 Yay Sports!" promises one clean story, and the hero actually delivers it — "YAY SPORTS, GAME DAY JUST GOT CUTER" on a football-field backdrop with a model in a red touchdown jersey and pink shorts. That's a real improvement over the last several PINK sends I've reviewed, which buried the lead under a kitchen-sink template (08-20 cherry collection, 08-21 stripe hype, 08-22 bling, 08-25 VS Fashion Show all scored 6/10 for exactly this problem).
- But it doesn't stay disciplined. By section three you're already into "HAUNT GIRL ENERGY" (a Halloween lingerie drop, unrelated to football), then "PANTY PACKS FROM $20," then Halloween/Back to Campus trend blocks, then a massive New Arrivals grid. This is the same structural sprawl as every other recent PINK send — NFL just gets the first fold instead of getting buried. A subscriber who opens for the football merch scrolls into an entirely different campaign within two screens.
- Net: strongest subject/hero match I've seen from this brand recently, undercut by the usual mid-email whiplash. Call it a 6/10 — better hero discipline than peers, same bloated body.

## What's working

- Hero visual and copy directly match the subject — jersey-and-shorts model, football field texture, "GAME DAY JUST GOT CUTER" — no bait-and-switch on open.
- Clear single CTA button ("GO FOR THE WIN") sits right under the hero with obvious contrast.
- "LIMITED TIME — FREE SHIPPING WITH AN NFL COLLECTION PURCHASE" is a concrete, specific offer tied to the featured product.
- Loyalty section near the bottom ("welcome back," Rewards, $25 Off notification signup) is visible for returning/member subscribers.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlapping elements or broken layout.

## What's weak

- The email abandons the NFL theme almost immediately — Halloween lingerie ("HAUNT GIRL ENERGY"), panty packs, and a generic "TREND COLLECTION" grid all compete for attention within the same send.
- Second CTA "BUY IT" for the "#1 FAN" jersey block is vague — doesn't specify jersey, team option, or price.
- No visible price or % discount on the NFL collection itself — only a fine-print free-shipping offer, so there's no anchor number to react to.
- The email is extremely long (multiple unrelated campaigns stacked), which dilutes urgency and makes the football hook feel like a header slapped on a standard megasend.
- No social proof, reviews, or fit/sizing info anywhere near the NFL products, despite jerseys/shorts being fit-sensitive items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the NFL hero + "#1 Fan" jersey block, or clearly demarcate an "NFL Collection" mini-section (2-3 products max) before pivoting to Halloween/panty content — right now the theme dies after one scroll.
- 2. Add a real number to the NFL offer — a %/$ off or a price point on the jersey — free shipping alone is a weak hook for a "new collection" subject line.
- 3. Replace "BUY IT" with a specific CTA like "SHOP NFL JERSEYS" so it's unambiguous what happens on tap.
- 4. If Halloween and panty-pack promos must run same-week, send them as separate campaigns — bundling erodes the football story this send otherwise nails.
