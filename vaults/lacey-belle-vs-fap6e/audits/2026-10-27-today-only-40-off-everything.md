# Today Only: 40% Off EVERYTHING 🏃‍♀️‍➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, well-built last-day promo, and it follows five straight days of Buy 2, Get 2 Free. The render is 680x8954 and was only legible at a small scale, so I could not read the fine print. The hero is a hot-pink "40% OFF EVERYTHING" lockup with a "TOO GOOD TO LAST" countdown (about 11:19:13) and "+ free shipping on $35+ orders". Six black category buttons sit directly under it: Clothing, Bras, Panty Packs, Sleep, Beauty, Shop All.
- The email then stacks four "TODAY ONLY" modules, each with a hard price point and a SHOP NOW button. They are $30 sweatshirts and sweatpants, $20 tees and leggings, $8 full-size beauty plus 2 for $8 minis, and a free canvas tote with a $100+ purchase. It is easy to scan, and the new percent-off framing is a real change after Buy 2, Get 2 Free, which had run since 10/21.
- The weakness is offer sprawl. There is a headline percentage, four more price points, a tote threshold, a $25-off welcome-back coupon and a free-panty app-download bar. The page ends in a very long block of fine print. This is a good send, but it is cluttered.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: giant "40% OFF EVERYTHING" in brand pink, with the free-shipping threshold directly under it.
- The countdown timer and "TODAY ONLY" labels make the urgency feel credible.
- Each module pairs one product image with one price and one button ($30, $20, $8), so a skimmer can see what's on offer.
- The percent-off message breaks the Buy 2, Get 2 Free pattern from 10/21 to 10/26, which gives subscribers a reason to look again.
- The rewards and $25-off welcome-back block gives members a visible benefit near the bottom.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: 40% off, $30, $20, $8, the free tote, $25 off, and the free-panty app bar at the very top.
- The "FREE Panty loading…" app-download bar above the hero adds friction and pulls attention from the sale.
- The email is very long. The category grid, welcome-back block and "all your feed" section sit far below the offer modules.
- The legal block at the bottom is huge. It undercuts the "EVERYTHING" claim, because the module captions suggest exclusions.
- The send follows days of near-identical promos, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top bar and the bottom-of-email modules. Keep the hero, one or two price-point modules and the tote.
- 2. Put a single "exclusions apply" line with a link under the hero, and move the long fine print to a landing page.
- 3. Make the CTA copy more specific, for example "Shop 40% Off Sweats" instead of a generic "Shop Now".
- 4. Add a ratings or "best seller" cue to the product modules for social proof.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Everything Ends Tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: 40% Off + Free Tote`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $35+, plus $30 sweats and $20 leggings. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart just got cheaper. Everything's 40% off until tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several markup-structure problems (a `<table>` and `<script>` placed inside `<head>`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom) plus a heavy third-party pixel load over plain HTTP. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from the data captured, and the HTML provided is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **53 tracking links weren't probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** All of these load over `http://` and may be blocked or produce mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?...`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync load.** The ten `pub1`–`pub10.php` Dotomi cookie-redirect pixels plus the UCM call add a lot of third-party requests. They are a deliverability and privacy-filter risk, and they slow rendering when images are enabled.
- **Hashed email in URLs.** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…` are SHA-256-style identifiers in every `ld+json` URL and in the Dotomi pixel query string (`...pub1.php?a4edea106…=1`). Forwarded or shared messages leak a stable per-recipient identifier.
- **Duplicate promo URLs.** All three `PromotionCard` entries in the JSON-LD point to the same URL, `/us/pink/new-arrivals?cm_mmc=...`. That is a probable template-slot issue, and the cards have no distinct destinations.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` structure.** `<head>` opens directly with `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `<script type="application/ld+json">`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table, so the `<meta viewport>`, `<meta Content-Type>` and `<style>` blocks that follow may be processed in `<body>`. The charset declaration can then be ignored in some clients.
- **JSON-LD placement.** Gmail promotion annotations are expected in `<head>`. In the body or table they may not be parsed.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
  - Both rules are dead code.
- **Global width cap.** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important;}` apply to every table, which can clip nested layouts.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used alongside `role="presentation"` tables.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt. This is the only content-image alt failure confirmed.
  - The tracking pixels `o.gif` and `pub1–10.php` are also flagged, but they are non-visual, so `alt=""` is sufficient there.
  - The open pixel (`open.aspx`) correctly has `alt=""`.
- **Emoji in subject.** `🏃‍♀️‍➡️` is a ZWJ sequence (Unicode 15.1). Older clients will show it as separate glyphs, for example 🏃‍♀️ followed by ➡️.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`). The per-recipient values present (`mi_u`, `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`) are all populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** The QA tool didn't find either header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed either way. Verify against the raw message source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from the captured data.
- **Footer.** The footer, unsubscribe link and physical address are past the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be verified.
- **Sender domain alignment.** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while the tracking and image hosts are on `e1.victoriassecret.com` (`click.e1`, `image.e1`). Check that SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment holds across these subdomains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No standard UTM parameters.** Links use proprietary params only (`cm_mmc=di-_-102726415-_-VSD_VSL_20261027_PK_6373_FULLFILE-_`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp`, `mi_u`). That is consistent within the email, but it isn't visible to GA-style UTM reporting unless the site maps it.
- **Landing page.** The JSON-LD `url` values go to `/us/pink/new-arrivals`, while the subject promises "40% Off EVERYTHING". From the evidence available, a sitewide offer is landing on a new-arrivals listing. Confirm the page shows the offer. Body link destinations are truncated, so I can't verify them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `ld+json` block into `<head>`. Keep the `<table>` out of `<head>` so the meta tags and styles are parsed correctly.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Switch the `o.gif` and Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the 10-pixel cookie-sync chain.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
5. Fix or delete the invalid selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`).
6. Pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer and body links.
7. Give each JSON-LD `PromotionCard` its own destination URL, and confirm the landing page matches the subject-line offer.
