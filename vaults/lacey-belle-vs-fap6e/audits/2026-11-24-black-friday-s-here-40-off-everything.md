# Black Friday's Here: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the escalation send: PINK moves from the 30% off it has run since the Nov 18 "STARTS NOW" email to **40% off everything**. The hero handles it well. "BLACK FRIDAY: THE HOLIDAYS START NOW" sits over a large "40% OFF EVERYTHING" block, with six category chips right under it. The offer is the first thing you read.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long stack of single-offer modules, each with its own "SHOP NOW" button: $25 flannel pajama pants, 7/$35 panties, a free XL bling tote with $100+, a free tote plus blanket, $8 full-size beauty, and free shipping on $50+. The bigger problem is cadence. This is roughly the seventh PINK Black Friday catalog in a week, after "Did Someone Say 30% Off Everything?" and "Woke Up & Chose 30% Off PINK" on 11/23, and "30% Off Your Faves‼️" on 11/22. The jump from 30% to 40% is the only fresh news, and the subject carries it. The body gives it no more weight than the other seven offers.
- Note: the screenshot is very long and was shown to me downscaled, so I could read headlines and layout but not the small print or the preheader. I've scored accordingly.

## What's working

- The 40% headline is the largest element, with the "LIMITED TIME" tag above it, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The subject and hero say the same thing, so the click matches the promise.
- Category chips under the hero (Clothing, Sleep, Bras, Accessories, Activewear, Beauty) let different shoppers jump straight to their section.
- Concrete, specific secondary offers ($25 pajama pants, 7/$35 panties, $8 beauty, free shipping at $50) give shoppers reasons to browse.
- The rewards card with "$25 off" near the bottom surfaces member benefits.

## What's weak

- Seven or more stacked offer modules dilute the new 40% news, and the scroll is very long.
- The "FREE XL bling tote" appears twice, once alone and once with a blanket, which reads as repetitive filler.
- Every button says "SHOP NOW". None names a category or product.
- "LIMITED TIME" repeats on every module with no actual end date or countdown, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Dense legal fine print fills the bottom and the hero's own exclusions, which undercuts "everything".

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the whole email. Show 40% off plus one proof point, such as a single best-seller grid, and cut the body to 2–3 modules. Trim the duplicate tote blocks.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Monday 11:59 PM PT") instead of generic "LIMITED TIME".
- 3. Call out the change explicitly: "Up from 30%" or "Our biggest offer yet", so this send reads differently from the previous six.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs: "Shop Bras 40% Off", "Get the $25 Pajama Pants".
- 5. Move the fine print into a single "Details" link and keep exclusions short and near the offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Now 40% Off: PINK's Biggest Black Friday Deal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free XL bling tote with $100+. Deal ends Monday at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bras, sleep & pajama pants from $25. Free shipping over $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering and data defect: an unresolved `%%cellcode%%` merge token in the JSON-LD block. It also has several structural and accessibility problems and a heavy third-party pixel load. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 57 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, and link health is unknown.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a4edea10…=1`. A further `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` pixel and `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` are also `http://`. Clients and proxies that block non-HTTPS content will drop these, which breaks attribution and retargeting.
- **Pixel load:** About 12 third-party pixels in one send adds weight and a privacy surface. The `a4edea10…` value is the same `dtm_email_hash` that appears in the landing URLs, so the hashed email is also sent to Dotomi.
- **Open pixel:** The open pixel is correct (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...`, 1x1, `alt=""`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Parsers will auto-close `<head>` at the `<table>`. The `<meta viewport>` and `<style>` blocks then land in `<body>`, and some clients may ignore them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing alt text on content:** `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (`image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/`) has no alt attribute. It is the only non-tracking image flagged, so its content is invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
- **Missing alt text on pixels:** `o.gif`, the Dotomi `UCMController` pixel and `pub1–10.php` are flagged for missing alt. These are tracking pixels, so this is low severity. Adding `alt=""` would still stop clients from reading out file names.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also invalid because it targets a nonexistent element. Neither rule applies.
- **Missing language and table roles:** No `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`. Outer layout tables (for example `<table width="100%" border="0" cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" align="center">`) lack `role="presentation"`, while only some inner tables have it.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is acceptable for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed failure:** QA flagged `%%cellcode%%` as `[FAIL]`. It appears in the JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl`: `…/us/?cm_mmc=di-_-112426415-_-…&d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&dtm_email_hash=…`. The ESP did not resolve it.
- **Inconsistent parameters:** `offerPageUrl` has `d_cellid=112426415&…&mi_u=200492586522`. `merchantHomepageUrl` has `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&…&mi_u=112426415`. The `mi_u` value is the cell ID instead of the user ID, so the two objects were templated inconsistently.
- **Visible impact:** The token is in structured data, not visible body copy, so subscribers won't see it. Gmail schema parsing or any crawler of that block will get a malformed URL, and attribution for that URL will break.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA could not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The check notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **inconclusive, not a confirmed defect**. Verify against the raw headers of the original send.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The check attributes this to the relay. The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while the tracking domain is `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. The sending and click domains differ, but no alignment failure is demonstrated.
- **Body requirements:** The HTML was truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** URLs use the proprietary `cm_mmc=di-_-112426415-_-VSD_VSL_20261124_PK_6533_FULLFILE-_`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp` and `mi_u` parameters. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appear in the captured URLs, so GA-style attribution depends on site-side mapping of `cm_mmc`.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` (64-hex hashes) are in the query string. The hashed email goes to the destination and to any intermediary that logs URLs.
- **Landing page:** The offer URL lands on `/us/pink/tops-and-bottoms`, a category page. The subject line promises "40% Off Everything". The landing page cannot be checked because the redirects were not probed.
- **Consistency:** `mi_ecmp=20261124_PK_6533_FULLFILE` is the same in both URLs, so campaign ID tagging is consistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the template so `d_cellid` resolves in `merchantHomepageUrl`, and correct `mi_u` so it holds the user ID rather than the cell ID. Add a send-time check for any `%%…%%` token left in the output.
2. Move the JSON-LD block out of `<head>`'s invalid `<table>` wrapper into `<body>`, or place the `<script>` directly in `<head>`. Put `<meta>` and `<style>` first.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png`. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Move the Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixels to HTTPS, and reduce the 10-pixel cookie-sync set if possible.
6. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors, add `lang` to `<html>`, and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
7. Capture the full raw message (headers and untruncated HTML) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
8. Confirm that the 57 redirect links resolve to `victoriassecret.com` with `cm_mmc` intact, and consider adding `utm_*` parameters or documenting the `cm_mmc` mapping.
