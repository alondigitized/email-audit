# 25% Off—What're We Buying Today❓
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the kitchen-sink template again — the same one that dominated every send from 2026-08-06 through 2026-08-11, just with a fresh headline offer. Hero leads with "GET 25% OFF WHEN YOU SPEND $75+" over a camo tee, then cascades through six more "LIMITED TIME" price-point blocks ($25 tees, $35 sweats, free shipping with a HydroJug purchase, $15 sleep, $25 backpacks, $10 beauty) before dumping into a nav grid and rewards footer. It's comprehensive but shapeless — nothing says "today" beyond the subject line's rhetorical question, and there's no single hero product story the way 2026-08-13's Campus Fleece or 2026-08-15's Disco Capsule had.
- The subject itself is doing more work than the email delivers on — "What're We Buying Today❓" promises a curated pick, but the body is seven stacked offers with no editorial voice tying them together. It reads like a spend-threshold promo wearing a conversational subject line as a costume.
- Visually clean, no render bugs, offer ladder is legible — but this is a 6, not higher, because it's the same multi-offer template PINK has leaned on all month and the subject over-promises relative to what's actually inside.

## What's working

- Hero offer (25% off $75+) is bold, high-contrast, and unmissable at the top.
- Each subsequent block uses a consistent "LIMITED TIME" tag + big price callout, so scanning is easy even if you don't read every line.
- HydroJug collab block is a genuine novelty beat — a co-branded product angle we haven't seen in recent sends.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or lorem ipsum — the render is clean throughout.
- Bottom nav grid (new arrivals, clothing, bras, sleep, etc.) gives a fast escape hatch to category browsing.

## What's weak

- Subject asks "what're we buying today" but the body never answers that with a single recommendation — it's seven parallel offers, no hierarchy of "the" pick.
- This is the same multi-tier-discount kitchen-sink structure recycled from the entire PINK Friday run (2026-08-06–2026-08-11); nothing distinguishes this send creatively.
- No single strong hero product/model moment — camo tee, sweats, HydroJug girl, sleepwear, backpacks, beauty all get equal visual weight, so nothing anchors the eye.
- $10 beauty block and backpack block feel like filler tacked on to hit a "something for everyone" quota rather than a coherent seasonal story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero (the 25% off threshold offer or the HydroJug collab, not both) and cut the email to 3-4 blocks max — the seven-offer stack dilutes urgency rather than building it.
- 2. Make the subject match the body: either commit to a "today's pick" narrative and show one product, or drop the rhetorical-question subject in favor of a straightforward offer-led line.
- 3. Differentiate this from the PINK Friday sends by leading with the HydroJug collab as the real news — it's the only genuinely new asset in this send.
- 4. Tighten preview text so it adds new information instead of restating the subject's offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% Off $75+ Starts Now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus $25 tees, $15 sleep, and $10 beauty — all limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's about to get a 25% discount.`
