# 5/$19.99 Panties—We Repeat, 5/$19.99
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- VS is leaning into the "We Repeat" mechanic explicitly — the subject line admits this is a callback to yesterday's 5/$19.99 panties send ("Panties from $4.99 or 5/$19.99 is *ON*," scored 9/10), and that self-awareness is either charming or exhausting depending on how deep you are in their inbox. The email opens correctly: animal-print bikini bottom model with a SALE badge, price point front and center, followed immediately by a bra block. That's the right two-punch for SAS.
- What breaks the momentum is everything below the fold. After two tight sale heroes, the email sprawls into a Semi-Annual Sale category grid, a Bra Fit Guide callout, a Wedding Shop section, and a full product thumbnail grid. The creative team appears to have stuffed three separate campaigns into one send. For subscribers who've already received the panties price point yesterday, this diluted version offers no new hook below the hero.
- Verdict: a competent repeat send propped up by a price point that still has legs, but the email loses discipline fast after the second scroll. It will drive clicks from the hero — and then bleed out.

## What's working

- **"We Repeat" subject line self-awareness** — VS acknowledges the repeat instead of pretending it's fresh; that's a personality choice that earns a little goodwill.
- **Dual hero, dual category** — panties hero first, bras hero second covers the two biggest SAS revenue drivers efficiently before the fold.
- **SALE badges on both hero images** — the sale signal is visual, not just text-dependent.
- **"Up to 60% Off Bras" with a SHOP NOW button** — the secondary CTA is unambiguous and specific.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs** — no broken images, no overlapping copy visible in the render.

## What's weak

- **Email sprawl kills focus** — Bra Fit Guide and Wedding Shop sections beneath the sale heroes are topic whiplash; they belong in standalone sends, not buried here.
- **Product thumbnail grid at the bottom is low-resolution noise** — eight or more small thumbnails with no visible pricing or copy won't drive incremental clicks.
- **No deadline visible anywhere** — "Semi-Annual Sale" implies urgency but nowhere in the visible render does a hard end-date appear, making the "We Repeat" feel like convenience rather than genuine last-chance.
- **Category grid in the SAS block duplicates navigation** — the shop-by-category boxes (bras, panties, etc.) repeat what the hero blocks already did; this is filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut everything below the bra hero block.** Send a tightly focused two-hero email — panties + bras — and kill the Bra Fit Guide, Wedding Shop, and thumbnail grid entirely. Save those for their own sends.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the subject or the hero copy.** "Ends Sunday" or "2 Days Left" transforms "We Repeat" from a cute callback into a credible last-call.
- 3. **Give the panties hero a SHOP NOW button** — the bra block has one, the panties hero appears to not have a clear standalone button in the visible render.
- 4. **The product grid, if kept, needs prices visible** — no-context thumbnails don't sell.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard HTML 4.01 Transitional table-based layout with Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget/AMPscript) infrastructure. The primary technical concerns are mixed-content HTTP image requests from a third-party ad retargeting vendor and missing compliance headers at the relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 click-tracking links skipped** — all routed through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. QA probing was skipped for these; live destination validation was not performed. No broken link evidence in source, but destinations are unverified.

**Duplicate open-pixel** — the 1×1 tracking pixel at `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?22IENINSYYTUDENAETLTUNLINI.70253&d=70253&bmt=0` appears **twice** in the HTML: once in the outer `<body>` wrapper div and again inside the AMPScript content block. This double-fires open tracking and inflates open-rate metrics.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content — HTTP image sources (high severity):** 11 images served over plain HTTP from two Dotomi (Epsilon) retargeting domains:

- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&dtm_cmagic=f55cad&...`
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`

Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail) block mixed-content requests. These retargeting pixels will silently fail to fire in most environments.

**Missing `alt` text — 12 images confirmed:** All 11 Dotomi HTTP images plus `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (a content image hosted on `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`) have no `alt` attribute. The content image missing alt text is the more impactful issue — screen readers will skip it, and image-off rendering (common in Outlook) will show a blank space with no label.

**Empty `<title>` tag inside body:** `<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>` appears inline in the body content flow. This is a preheader suppression hack; it is benign but technically invalid HTML and may cause unexpected behavior in some parsers.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` — disabling user scaling fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA) and is flagged by accessibility auditors.

**HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE:** The document uses a legacy doctype. Not a rendering bug in practice for email, but worth noting for toolchain hygiene.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** at the AgentMail relay layer. This header is required for Google and Yahoo bulk sender compliance (enforced February 2024). Its absence at the relay may indicate the header was stripped in transit rather than never set — VS's ESP (Salesforce MC) typically injects this — but it cannot be confirmed from available data. One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) is also undetected for the same reason.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status for `e1.victoriassecret.com` is unknown from the relay capture. The sending domain uses a dedicated subdomain ESP envelope, which typically has valid DKIM. Cannot confirm or deny without raw header access.
