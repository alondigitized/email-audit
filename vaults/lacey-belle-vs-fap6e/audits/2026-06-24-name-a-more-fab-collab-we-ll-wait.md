# Name a More Fab Collab (We'll Wait) 🙇‍♀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Day 3 of the PINK × Elle collab campaign — following June 23's launch ("The Most Fab Collab (Ever) Is Hereee") and June 24's creative refresh ("TFW the Collab Is Legally Gorj"). The subject line pivots from declarative hype to a confident dare format ("Name a More Fab Collab — We'll Wait"), which is the right instinct on Day 3: you've announced, you've celebrated, now you strut. The hero "MAY CONTAIN EXCESSIVE CHARM" is the strongest copy in the email and earns the swagger.
- The problem is structural: this email is doing too many jobs at once. The collab campaign, a $10 full-sized beauty offer, free shipping, and a visible Semi-Annual Sale module all compete for attention. The visual hierarchy drags — the eye has to work to find the primary CTA, then wanders into the sale grid, then finds beauty products, then social icons. For a campaign in its third day, that dilution is costly.
- Honest verdict: better creative voice than execution. The "legally perfect" and "excessive charm" copy lines are genuinely good; the layout buries them.

## What's working

- **"MAY CONTAIN EXCESSIVE CHARM" is a strong headline** — witty, brand-voice-consistent, earns the collab's confidence without screaming.
- **$10 full-sized beauty is a concrete, visible offer** — specific price point breaks through the clutter and gives non-apparel shoppers a low-barrier entry.
- **Sender name "PINK | Elle 💕" stays consistent** with the collab creative — the co-brand reads immediately from the inbox.
- **REWARDS section visible** — reinforces the loyalty hook for existing members mid-funnel.
- **Multi-product grid** gives a wide catalog sweep that works for browse-mode openers.

## What's weak

- **Visual hierarchy collapses below the fold** — the Semi-Annual Sale module, beauty section, and product grid feel equally weighted; there's no clear "this is the primary path."
- **Subject line is wit-first, offer-second** — "Name a More Fab Collab (We'll Wait)" is charming but tells you nothing about what's in the email; no price, no product, no reason to open *today*.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "SHOP NOW" with no specificity (shop what? the collab? the sale? the beauty deal?) doesn't push toward a single conversion.
- **Day 3 fatigue risk is real** — same co-brand sender, same collab energy, three days running; without a new mechanic (like a limited drop or a deeper discount), it reads as a resend.
- **Free shipping doesn't feel differentiated** — it's presented as a feature, not an offer; no minimum threshold or end date makes it feel permanent and therefore skippable.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero CTA and own it** — the $10 full-sized beauty offer is the freshest mechanic in this email; lead the hero with it instead of burying it below the fold. "Get $10 Beauty Now" beats "Shop Now" for click velocity.
- 2. **Add a time peg to create urgency** — Day 3 of a collab launch needs "ends Sunday" or "last day" language to stop the scroll; without it, readers defer indefinitely.
- 3. **Collapse the layout into two sections max** — Collab + Beauty Offer, then Sale/Rewards footer. The current four-zone structure dilutes every offer.
- 4. **Put the offer in the subject line** — the dare format is clever but it's doing nothing to drive the open for people who weren't already warm on the collab.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$10 Beauty + the Collab You've Heard About`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Excessive Charm. $10 Beauty. Today Only.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping + the PINK × Elle collab — your move.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet the full-sized beauty deal you didn't see coming.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — PINK | Elle "Name a More Fab Collab" (2026-06-24)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a malformed `<head>` structure (table and script rendered before meta tags), a duplicated open-pixel, 12 HTTP-only tracking/retargeting images vulnerable to mixed-content blocking, and missing compliance headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) that put one-click unsubscribe conformance at risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel duplicated**
`https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?HYC3B4CRWK7UDMWVQ4LM5W2MPQ.70261&d=70261&bmt=0` appears twice in the truncated source (once in `<head>`, once in the `#AMPScript` div). Double-fires will inflate open-rate metrics.

**19 click-redirect links unprobed**
All CTA hrefs route through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. The QA probe correctly skipped them (redirect domain), so destination URLs and UTM coverage are unverified — see §6.

**Dotomi retargeting pixels — HTTP only**
12 pixels across two domains load over plain HTTP:
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…` (1 pixel)
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 pixels)

Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block HTTP image loads; these pixels will silently fail for a significant share of recipients.

**JSON-LD schema — duplicate `@type` field**
```json
{ "@type": "DiscountOffer", "@type": "PromotionCard", "image": "", "url": "" }
```
The second `@type` silently overrides the first (invalid JSON-LD per RFC 8259 — duplicate keys undefined behavior). Both `image` and `url` fields are empty strings, so even if parsed the rich-result annotation provides no actionable data.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed `<head>` structure**
A `<table>` block (with the JSON-LD `<script>`) is emitted as the first child of `<head>`, before `<meta charset>` and `<meta viewport>`. Parsers that encounter table tags in `<head>` may trigger quirks mode or drop the meta tags, breaking viewport scaling on mobile.

**`<title>` rendered inside `<body>`**
```html
<title style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;"></title>
```
The title element appears inside a `<div id="AMPScript">` in the body, not in `<head>`. It is also empty, so it provides no preview-text benefit and is semantically invalid.

**Invalid CSS values**
- `max-height:auto !important` (mobile media query on `img`) — `auto` is not a valid value for `max-height`; renders as though the property were absent.
- `min-width{width:auto!important;}` — missing class/element selector prefix; rule is discarded by all parsers.

**`user-scalable=0`**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="... user-scalable=0" />
```
`0` is not a valid value; the correct keyword is `no`. Behavior is browser-dependent and may not prevent zoom as intended.

**Missing alt text — 13 images flagged**
- `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/…/o.gif` — tracking pixel, no `alt`
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…` — retargeting pixel, no `alt`
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` — retargeting pixels, no `alt` on any
- `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/…/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` — appears to be a content image, no `alt`

Tracking/retargeting pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty, not absent) to satisfy accessibility validators without implying content.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge token syntax (e.g. `%%`, `{{`, `[[`) is visible in the truncated source. The `<title>` element used as a preview-text carrier is empty — if it was intended to hold personalized preview text, the value was never populated.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header absent**
QA confirms no `List-Unsubscribe` header was captured via the AgentMail relay. CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliance depends on an in-body unsubscribe link being present and functional (not verifiable from truncated source alone). Gmail and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender requirements mandate the header for >5k/day senders.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058)**
No one-click unsubscribe support detected. Gmail's bulk-sender policy requires this for commercial email at scale.

**Authentication-Results header absent**
SPF and DKIM verification status is unknown — the AgentMail relay did not surface the header. If DKIM is failing upstream, this email is at elevated spam-folder risk.

**Sending domain mismatch (observable)**
From address uses `@e2.vspink.com`; open-pixel domain is `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`; image CDN is `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`. DKIM alignment requires the `d=` tag in the DKIM signature to match the `From:` domain or its organizational domain — cannot confirm without the raw headers.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 19 CTAs pass through `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` click-redirect infrastructure. Because the probe skipped these (redirect domain), UTM parameter presence and correct `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign` values on landing pages are unverified. No UTM fragments are visible in the raw HTML hrefs before redirection.

No issues with destination domain plausibility (all links consistent with vspink.com / victoriassecret.com brand infrastructure).
