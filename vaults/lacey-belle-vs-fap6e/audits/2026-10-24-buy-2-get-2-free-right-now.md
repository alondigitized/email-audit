## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has structural HTML problems and 12 `http://` tracking pixels that lack alt text. The QA pass rate is 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path (AgentMail relay), so they aren't confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 58 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were validated.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** the QA data lists 11 `http://` images on Dotomi (Epsilon), all with no alt text and no HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a4edea10...=1`
- **Risks of these pixels:**
  - They can be blocked by clients that rewrite or block mixed content or proxy images.
  - They are cookie-sync calls that go to a third party on open.
  - Each one carries the 64-character hash `a4edea106b33...` in its query string.
- **Open pixel:** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is present as a 1x1 image with `alt=""`, which is correct for a tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`. This is invalid, and parsers will move it into `<body>`.
  - `<title style="display:none...">` appears inside `<body>` and is empty.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the markup mixes in `role="presentation"` and XHTML-style self-closing `<meta ... />`.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it never matches.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Missing alt text:** two images hosted by the sender have no alt:
  - `image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/1/3505172c-7ae5-4640-adb1-053932dab3a3.gif`
  - `.../m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
  
  If these are content images, they leave no fallback when images are blocked.
- **Layout and font CSS:**
  - `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important;}` are applied globally and can clip nested tables.
  - The Outlook fix sets `line-height:1`, which may cause tight text in MSO.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay.
- **Footer:** the physical address and the visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters:** the JSON-LD URL uses proprietary params instead:
  - `cm_mmc=di-_-102426515-_-VSD_VSL_20261024_VS_6386_FILEBAL`
  - `mi_ecmp=20261024_VS_6386_FILEBAL`
  - `d_cellid=102426515`
  - Standard analytics like GA would not attribute these without mapping.
- **Identifiers in the URL:** `dtm_email_hash=4edea106...` and `customerID=43cece97...` are hashed identifiers in the query string. They go into logs, referrers and analytics on the destination.
- **Possible landing mismatch:** the subject is "Buy 2, Get 2 Free", but the structured-data URL is `/us/vs/giftguide/shine`. This is the only destination visible in the truncated source. The CTA hrefs weren't visible, so it is unconfirmed.
- **Unvalidated destinations:** none of the 58 skipped tracking links were resolved.
