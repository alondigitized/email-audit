# Last Day: 25% Off When You Spend $75+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Attn: Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" template running one more lap, now rebranded "Last Day" — same offer, same structure, escalating only the urgency framing. The subject promises 25% off, but the hero doesn't deliver an offer at all: it's "Take a Joyride / Make Hearts Race," a lace-bra-and-lingerie campaign shot with a woman on a convertible hood, and you have to scroll past the entire hero, a full lingerie carousel, and a panties module before the 25%-off banner even resurfaces implicitly through nav. The top banner strip does carry "Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" in small text, but it's easy to miss against the dark maroon background.
- The kitchen-sink stacking continues below the fold — lingerie shots, a 5/$35 panties push, a Tease fragrance launch, a "Build Your Tease Bundle" cross-sell, then a nine-tile category nav and a Rewards/loyalty block. It's a lot of email for a "last day" urgency send, and the urgency itself is barely dressed up beyond the word "Last Day" in the subject — no countdown clock like the 8/18 sends used, no on-image badge reinforcing scarcity.
- Verdict: functional but unfocused. The subject's promise and the hero's payload don't match, which is the same gap this brand has repeated across multiple sends this week (8/15, 8/17). This one, at least, doesn't render buggy — clean images, no overlap — but it needs the offer pulled forward visually.

## What's working

- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Top banner does state the concrete offer ("Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+") even if visually understated.
- Model diversity is present across the scroll (multiple body types and skin tones shown in bra/lingerie shots).
- Loyalty section near the footer clearly shows Rewards sign-up plus a "$25 Off" incentive, giving members a visible reason to engage.
- Secondary offers (5/$35 panties, Tease bundle pricing) are concrete and specific, not vague "shop now" language.

## What's weak

- Hero says "Take a Joyride / Make Hearts Race" — a lingerie mood campaign with zero mention of the 25%-off/$75 offer that's literally the subject line.
- "Last Day" urgency isn't visually reinforced anywhere in the body — no countdown, no badge, nothing pulling the eye to "act now."
- The offer banner at top is small, low-contrast text on a dark background — easy to scan past on mobile.
- The email is extremely long (9+ modules: hero, bras, panties, lingerie, panties bundle, fragrance launch, fragrance bundle, category nav, loyalty, trending) — the actual $75+ offer gets buried under unrelated product pushes before you'd ever click through on it.
- This is essentially the same "25% off $75+" template as the 8/15 send ("Attn: Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+") with a new hero swapped in — no meaningful evolution beyond urgency copy in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 25%-off-$75+ offer directly in or immediately below the hero image, in large type with a "Last Day" badge — right now the hero completely ignores the promoted offer.
- 2. Add a countdown timer or explicit end-time (the brand already knows how to do this — see 8/18's "Final Hours" send) to make "Last Day" feel real instead of decorative.
- 3. Trim the body — six-plus product modules before the loyalty block dilutes urgency; a "last day" email should be short and offer-forward, not a full catalog scroll.
- 4. Increase contrast/size on the top banner offer text so it doesn't get lost against the dark background.
- 5. Move the Rewards/$25-off loyalty callout higher, near the primary offer, so member-eligible shoppers see stacked value immediately instead of at the very bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Day: 25% Off Ends Tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hours Left: 25% Off $75+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, save 25% — before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your lace-and-lingerie faves, 25% off through tonight only.`
