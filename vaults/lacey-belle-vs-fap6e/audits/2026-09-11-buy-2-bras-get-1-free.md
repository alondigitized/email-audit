# Buy 2 Bras, Get 1 FREE 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from the panty-megasend — the hero leads with "THE BRA EVENT: BUY 2, GET 1 FREE," a real, easy-to-understand bundle offer, not another 10/$40 panty rerun. The megasend structure is still fully intact underneath it though: after the bra hero it cascades into Foldover leggings, $25 tees, $35 sweatshirts, a free tote GWP, then a full new-arrivals grid and a "welcome back" rewards module — the same kitchen-sink stacking this brand has run since 08-06, just with a different lead offer this time.
- The bra offer itself is strong and clear on paper (buy 2 get 1 free is one of PINK's more generous, easy-to-grasp mechanics), but the hero model image is cropped tight and off-center, and the fine print immediately undercuts the "FREE" headline with "Excludes PINK Marshmallow collection and third-party styles." That's the honesty tax this brand keeps paying — a big bold claim followed by exclusions almost every send.
- Net: better than the last five panty-repeat sends because the hero offer is genuinely different and concrete, but it's buried in the same overlong stack that's been getting fatigued scores all month.

## What's working

- Hero offer is immediately legible: "BUY 2, GET 1 FREE" in large bold type with a clear "SHOP NOW" button right below it.
- "LIMITED TIME" tag creates urgency without shouting in all-caps exclamation marks.
- Clean section-by-section structure (Bras → Foldovers → Tees → Sweats → Tote GWP → shop-by-category grid) makes it easy to skim even though it's long.
- Model diversity is visible across the send (plus-size hero model up top, varied models further down).
- Rewards/loyalty module near the bottom ("welcome back," $25 off signup) gives a real reason for non-members to convert.

## What's weak

- The exclusion line ("Excludes PINK Marshmallow collection and third-party styles... Lowest priced item will be free") sits right under the "FREE" headline, which reads as a bait-and-switch signal per the brand's now-familiar pattern.
- Six separate "LIMITED TIME" callouts across one email (bras, tees, sweats, tote) dilutes urgency — if everything is limited time, nothing is.
- The email is extremely long (roughly a dozen stacked promo sections) — most subscribers won't scroll past the third or fourth block, so the tote GWP, new-arrivals grid, and rewards module are likely never seen.
- The hero model shot is cropped awkwardly tight at the top of the frame, cutting off the top of her head — a minor but noticeable polish issue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to 3-4 sections max (Bra Event, Foldovers, one more) and move tees/sweatshirts/tote/new-arrivals to a follow-up send — this brand has proven multi-week cadence works, no need to cram it all into one email.
- 2. Move the exclusion fine print further from the hero headline, or soften it — right now "FREE" and "excludes...third-party styles" sit inches apart, which reads as a walk-back.
- 3. Pick one urgency device (a real countdown timer, like prior sends have used) instead of repeating "LIMITED TIME" as a static tag six times.
- 4. Reframe the hero image crop so the model's full face/head is in frame — a small production fix but it's the first thing the eye hits.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Bra Event: Buy 2, Get 1 Free`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Your 3rd Bra Is On Us 😍`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match, and stack styles — the lowest-priced bra is free.`

- Legacy DOCTYPE (HTML 4.01 Transitional) and conditional MSO/Outlook font-fix block are present — standard for this ESP template, no issues.
