# Cozy Up to 30% Off Sleep (& Everything Else)

## Executive summary

- This is the sleep-and-lingerie cut of VS's week-long 30% Off Everything push, and it is another kitchen-sink send. The top of the email is strong: a "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH" banner, then a large "30% Off All Sleep & Lingerie" with two buttons. The subject's "Cozy Up to 30% Off Sleep" matches that opening.
- Below the hero, the email keeps adding offers: 7/$35 Panty Party, a second "30% Off Everything" block, a free quilted duffle with a $125+ purchase, an Angels Among Us show promo, a category grid, a rewards block and a "Now Trending" row. ## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "30% Off All Sleep & Lingerie", with "Shop Sleep" and "Shop Lingerie" buttons directly under it.
- The subject and hero match, so a sleep-focused subscriber lands on what the subject promised.
- The 7/$35 Panty Party module has a size selector (XS to XXL) and a product grid, which are useful for a click.
- "Limited time" framing and the "beat the rush" banner suit Black Friday week.
- A rewards block and a $25 off offer give members something extra.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: 30% off sleep, 30% off everything, 7/$35 panties, a free duffle, and a TV-style show promo. There is no single winner.
- The "30% Off Everything" block repeats the hero's offer almost word for word, which wastes a full screen of scroll.
- "Limited time" has no actual deadline or end date, so the urgency is weak.
- The fine print is dense and long, with several exclusion blocks. It makes the "everything" claim feel less honest.
- The "Angels Among Us" show promo sits mid-email and pulls attention away from shopping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make this a true sleep send. Lead with pajamas and robes, then add one secondary module (panties or the duffle). Cut the repeated 30% Off Everything block and the show promo to one small footer link.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM ET", in the hero.
- 3. Link the hero to one specific product or collection, such as "Shop Flannel Pajama Sets".
- 4. Shorten the fine print and collapse the exclusions into one line plus a link.
- 5. Move the rewards and $25 off module up if the recipient is a member.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flannel sets, robes & more — plus 7/$35 panties. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has structural defects (a `<table>` placed before `<head>` content, an empty `<title>`, invalid CSS selectors) and loads 11 third-party Dotomi pixels over plain `http://`. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers weren't captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** QA flagged 11 images loaded over `http://`, all with no alt text:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Mail clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, so cookie-sync and retargeting will fail silently.
- **Hashed email sent in cleartext:** The `pubN.php` URLs carry the query `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. This is the same value as `dtm_email_hash` in the JSON-LD URL. It is a SHA-256 email hash sent to a third party over unencrypted HTTP, which makes it observable in transit and linkable across contexts.
- **Hashes in the query string:** The `PromotionCard` URL also includes `customerID=43cece97...` and `mi_u=200492586522`. Anyone who forwards the email or copies the link carries those identifiers with it.
- **Unprobed links:** 64 tracking links (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects) were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health and landing status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed document structure:**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, where `<table>` is invalid. Parsers will implicitly close the head, which can push the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags into the body and cause inconsistent rendering.
  - `<title style="display:none...">` is empty and sits in the body.
  - The DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional, and `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid CSS:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. `max-height:auto !important` is not a valid value. All three are ignored.
- **Missing alt on content images:**
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg`
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
  
  If either carries offer or product content, it disappears for screen-reader users and with images off.
- **Layout tables:** Most layout tables lack `role="presentation"`. Only the styling-block wrappers have it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge syntax (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appears in the visible source, and the `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` values resolved. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA reports both missing. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them, so it is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that breaks the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement for one-click unsubscribe.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so alignment is unknown. The From domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com` and the tracking domain is `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTMs:** The visible URL uses proprietary parameters (`cm_mmc=di-_-112226515-_-VSD_VSL_20261122_VS_6527_FULFILE`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp=20261122_VS_6527_FULFILE`) and no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. This works only if the site's analytics maps `cm_mmc`, and generic analytics tools won't attribute the traffic.
- **Landing page vs. subject line:** The only destination visible in the source is `/us/vs/newarrivals`, while the subject promises "30% Off Sleep". Other CTAs are in the truncated part, so I can't confirm a mismatch. Check that the sleep and sitewide-offer CTAs land on matching collections.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change all Dotomi pixel URLs to `https://`, or remove them if the vendor doesn't support TLS. Stop sending the raw email hash in plain HTTP query strings.
2. Move the JSON-LD block into a valid `<head>` position or into the body in a valid container. Remove the `<table>` from `<head>`, and give `<title>` content or remove it.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add alt text to the two flagged content images. Add `alt=""` to the 11 tracking pixels, or drop them.
5. Fix the invalid CSS (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}` and `max-height:auto`). Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Inspect the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) outside the relay.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that analytics maps `cm_mmc`/`mi_ecmp`. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the CTA destinations and the footer unsubscribe link and address.
