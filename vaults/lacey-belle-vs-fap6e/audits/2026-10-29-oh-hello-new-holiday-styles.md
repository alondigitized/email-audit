# Oh Hello, New Holiday Styles 👋
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, well-organized holiday gifting send, and it's too busy. The render is 680x11124 and I could only read it at a small scale, so the headline text is legible but the fine print is not. The strongest read is the stack of distinct, price-led blocks: "$35 Sweatshirts & Sweatpants", "5/$31 Full-Sized Beauty", "Free Cozy Tote with a $50+ purchase" and "Free Cozy Tote & Blanket with a $150+ purchase". Each has a black or pink hero and a single button.
- It follows the 10/28 "PINK Mini Bar" send and the five-day Buy 2, Get 2 Free run. The brand has moved from a single mega-offer to a catalog of five or six smaller ones, which is more varied but dilutes the message. The subject, "New Holiday Styles", promises newness, but the body is mostly deals and gifting. The "SHOP EARLY, STRESS LESS" opener is the best framing in the email, because it ties the subject to the offers. Judgement: solid and on-brand, but long and unfocused, and the subject undersells the offers.

## What's working

- The "SHOP EARLY, STRESS LESS" opener sets a clear holiday-gifting theme and puts the gift-wrap imagery above the fold.
- Each offer block has one big number or claim ("$35", "5/$31", "FREE COZY TOTE") with a single button underneath.
- The tote offers use tiers ($50+ and $150+) that push order value up, and the product shots show what the freebie is.
- The "SPECIAL OFFER: Shop to earn a $25 bonus card" block and the Rewards module give members a visible reason to buy now.
- The pink/black alternation keeps the long scroll rhythmic, and I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is about 11,000px tall with six or seven competing offers, so no single one owns the email.
- The subject says "New Holiday Styles", but the first offer is a sweatshirt price promo, and "new" is only covered by a small "New Arrivals" tile near the bottom.
- The two tote blocks are near-duplicates ($50+ and $150+), which reads as repetition and splits attention.
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is a long wall of small text, and the free-tote and bonus-card terms look buried in it.
- It lands the day after the "PINK Mini Bar" send and the day after the "$25 Tees" push, so the cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer, either the $35 sweatshirts or the free tote, and move the rest into a compact grid. Cut the length by about half.
- 2. Merge the two tote blocks into one tiered module: "Free tote $50+, add the blanket at $150+".
- 3. Put the offer in the subject. The current one names a feeling, not a deal.
- 4. Move "New Arrivals" up near the top if the email is going to say "new".
- 5. Add a deadline near the hero, since the offers read as open-ended.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is structurally malformed: a `<table>` and JSON-LD script sit inside `<head>`, and several CSS selectors are invalid. It also loads about 12 non-HTTPS third-party pixels, and several images lack `alt` text. The source is truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. Automated QA reports 57% pass, 0 failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 50 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so destination status codes are unverified.
- **Cleartext third-party pixels:** The QA data lists 12 `http://` image sources.
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261029_PK_6410_FBCTRL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - These may be blocked by clients or proxies, and they send the recipient's hashed email in the clear.
- **Open pixel:** The pixel at `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is HTTPS, 1x1 and uses `alt=""`. No issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the `application/ld+json` script comes before the `<meta>` tags, inside `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early or relocate the content, so the viewport and charset meta tags may land in `<body>`. The JSON-LD itself may not be parsed reliably.
- **Empty title:** `<title style="display: none; ...">` is empty and sits in the body inside a table. A `<title>` is not valid there.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector for anything.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text (QA):** The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`.
  - Image sources `o.gif` and `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus the Dotomi `UCMController` image, also lack `alt`. These are tracking pixels, so they are lower priority, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Old doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with a global `table{max-width:600px!important;}`. This is workable, but it forces the 600px cap onto every nested table.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source.
- The URLs carry identifiers: `dtm_email_hash=4edea106...c6fe`, `customerID=43cece97...1e2f` and `mi_u=200492586522`. The hashed email is repeated in the Dotomi pixel URLs (`pub1.php?a4edea10...=1`). A hash is still a stable personal identifier, and it is sent to a third party over plain HTTP.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe:** QA found neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip the headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender failure.
- **No Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain `e2.vspink.com` differs from the link domains `victoriassecret.com`, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Unchecked footer:** The unsubscribe link and postal address were not in the truncated HTML, so they couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The campaign ID `20261029_PK_6410_FBCTRL` is consistent across the JSON-LD URL (`cm_mmc=di-_-102926425-_-VSD_VSL_20261029_PK_6410_FBCTRL`, `d_cellid=102926425`) and the pixel URLs (`mi_ecmp=`, `dtmc_email_category=`). Its date matches the send date.
- No `utm_*` parameters are used. Tracking relies on the proprietary `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and `d_cellid` parameters.
- The JSON-LD destination is `/us/pink/gifts`. With the click redirects unprobed, landing-page alignment with the "Holiday Styles" subject is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block into a valid `<head>` or `<body>` position, and move the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags above any content in `<head>`.
2. Fix or remove the invalid selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`), and populate or remove the empty `<title>`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Serve the `mi.victoriassecret.com` and `dotomi.com` pixels over HTTPS, or drop the ten `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels.
6. Check the raw message, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Then confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address in the full HTML.
7. Follow the redirects on the 50 skipped tracking links to confirm final destinations and status codes.
