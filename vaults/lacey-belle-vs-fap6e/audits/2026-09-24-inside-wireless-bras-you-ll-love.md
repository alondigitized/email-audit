# Inside: Wireless Bras You'll Love
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a "kitchen sink" send wearing a wireless-bra subject line it barely bothers to deliver on. The hero does the job — a sheer lace bralette on a model, "Spotlight On Wireless Bras," clean "SHOP NOW" button — but that's the entire wireless bra content. Everything below it pivots hard into panty sale, pajamas, sleepwear, body care, and a nine-tile category grid, none of which have anything to do with the subject line.
- Compare this to the brand's stronger recent sends — 9/21's "Up to 50% Off Bras" or 9/19's "7/$35 Panties Starts NOW" — both of which kept the subject-to-hero promise tight and singular. This one reads like three separate campaigns stapled together under a bra-themed subject, which is a step backward in focus even if the individual visuals are clean and on-brand.
- Judgement: solid production values, muddled strategy. Fine for a "just open and skim" subscriber, weak for anyone who opened specifically for wireless bras.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed — no overlapping text, model and product both clearly visible, immediate "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Subject-to-hero match at the very top is honest: "Wireless Bras" subject leads directly into "Spotlight On Wireless Bras."
- Panty sale section has a specific, legible offer ("8/$25 or from $4.99 each") with a torn-paper "SALE" graphic consistent with brand history.
- Category grid near the bottom (Sale, Bras, Panties, Sleep, Beauty, Activewear, Swim, Lingerie, Loungewear) gives multiple easy entry points if the hero doesn't land.
- Rewards/credit card module is clearly separated and not confused with the main offer.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "wireless bras" but roughly 80% of the email (panties, pajamas, sleepwear, body care) has nothing to do with that category — bait-and-switch by omission, similar to the 9/20 "Angels Among Us" miss.
- No second wireless-bra product shown after the hero — no styles, colors, or pricing for the actual subject-line item, so anyone who opened for bras hits a dead end fast.
- Email is extremely long with five distinct promotional blocks before the footer — cadence/fatigue risk, especially stacked against the near-daily sends already logged this week.
- The $11 VS Body Care block and pajama block have no visible urgency or differentiation from a generic recurring promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Either rename the subject to reflect the full mixed content ("Bras, Panties, PJs & More") or cut the email down to actually be about wireless bras — add 2-3 more product shots, prices, and a fit/support callout since that's what the subject sold.
- 2. Move the panty sale and pajama sections to a shorter, single "Shop the sale" recap block instead of two nearly full-screen features — this email is doing the job of three sends.
- 3. Add a specific price or "starting at $X" under the wireless bra hero — right now there's a CTA but no visible offer, which is the weakest part of the strongest section.
- 4. Trim the $11 Body Care block or give it real urgency (end date) since "Limited Time" with no deadline reads as filler.
