# Don't Let Your $50 OFF Expire 😰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the "last day" send for the SAVEBOW promo PINK launched on 09-28 ("$50 OFF 💰 Code Inside ⬇️"). It leads with the strongest element, a hot-pink hero: "LAST DAY / SAVE $50 when you spend $175+". Below it sits the tier ladder ($25 off $100, $15 off $75) and a "SHOP NOW" button. The subject's urgency matches the hero, so the promise and the payoff line up.
- After the hero, the email turns into a stack of competing offers. There is a boxy pajama shorts product story, a second "LAST DAY" block for THE BIG FALL SALE (up to 50% off plus an extra 25% off), a "LIMITED TIME 30% OFF CLOTHING" block, an app-exclusive Halftime Show capsule, a College Collection tile row, category tiles and a rewards module. That's four or five distinct offers in one send. The 09-26 emails already showed this Big Fall Sale machine, and it's back again as a second "last day" that competes with the $50 hero. The email is on-brand and credible on urgency, but it has no focus after the first screen.
- Verdict: a solid, above-average promo whose top third is effective. The rest waters down the "$50 expires" message and looks like an all-offers-at-once send. The render was tiny (143px wide), so I can't verify fine print or small copy. I saw no obvious broken images or layout breakage.

## What's working

- The hero puts "SAVE $50 when you spend $175+" on a saturated pink field, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- "LAST DAY" appears twice, and it fits the subject's "Don't let it expire" framing.
- The tier ladder ($25 off $100, $15 off $75) gives lower-spend shoppers an entry point.
- The promo code is stated right under the hero, so there's no hunting for it.
- The pajama shorts feature has three short customer-quote style callouts, which adds a bit of social proof.

## What's weak

- The pink "LAST DAY" Big Fall Sale block is a second headline offer. The 50% off plus extra 25% off message undercuts the $50 promo.
- The 30% off clothing block adds a third percentage-off message, so the shopper has to work out which discount applies.
- The lower half is a long, generic tile grid: new arrivals, categories, College Collection, Halftime Show, Fashion Show, plus an app-download block. It reads like a catalog, and the scroll is very long.
- The exclusions and fine print sit under the hero and in a dense legal block. "Excludes third-party styles" and similar terms appear near the offers, which hurts the honest-offer feel.
- The Big Fall Sale has run since 09-17, so a "last day" for it feels less credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body down to the $50 story: the hero, one product row (the pajama shorts are fine), a "shop the code" row, and a second CTA. Drop the Big Fall Sale, 30% off and app blocks from this send, or push them well below the fold.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET", and ideally a countdown. "Last day" alone is soft.
- 3. Make the CTA more specific than "SHOP NOW", for example "Use SAVEBOW – Shop $50 Off".
- 4. Put the code in a copyable pill or button so it isn't buried in small text.
- 5. Move the key exclusions into a single visible line under the ladder.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: $50 off $175 🩷`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your SAVEBOW code expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVEBOW: $50 off $175+, $25 off $100, $15 off $75. Last chance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tonight only. Shorts, sleep & bras count toward your total.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect. An unresolved `%%cellcode%%` merge token sits in the JSON-LD block. The HTML also has structural problems (a `<table>` and `<script>` inside `<head>`, invalid CSS selectors, a zoom-blocking viewport meta). It loads about 11 plain-HTTP third-party tracking pixels, and several of those carry a hashed email address. Unsubscribe and authentication compliance could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Inconsistent tracking parameters in one JSON-LD block.** `offerPageUrl` has `d_cellid=092926425&…&mi_u=200492586522`. `merchantHomepageUrl` has `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&…&mi_u=092926425`. The `mi_u` value differs between the two URLs. The `d_cellid` in `merchantHomepageUrl` is unresolved (see section 4).
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels (QA WARN).**
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20260929_PK_6229_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Hashed email over unencrypted HTTP.** Each `pubN.php` URL carries `a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. This matches the `dtm_email_hash` value in the JSON-LD.
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped these click-redirect links, so link validity and redirect destinations are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will close `<head>` at the table, which pushes the viewport and charset `<meta>` tags into `<body>`. The declared doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
- **Structured data is unlikely to be honored.** The `DiscountOffer` JSON-LD is in an email, where most clients strip `<script>`.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` (`.ReadMsgBody`). `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector and matches nothing.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). It is honored by some mobile clients.
- **Outlook line height.** The MSO conditional block sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td`. This can clip multi-line text in Outlook.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN).** These images have no alt attribute:
  - `o.gif`
  - `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…`
  - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
- **Only the last item is a content image.** The rest are tracking pixels, where empty alt (`alt=""`) is the appropriate fix. The `5bcec3ac…png` file is served over HTTPS and needs descriptive alt text if it is meaningful.
- **Open-tracking pixel is fine.** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx…` has `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%cellcode%%`** in `merchantHomepageUrl` (JSON-LD): `…/us/?cm_mmc=…&d_cellid=%%cellcode%%&dtm_email_hash=…`.
- **The token is resolved elsewhere.** `offerPageUrl` has the same parameter resolved (`d_cellid=092926425`), so this is a substitution miss in that template block.
- **Visible impact is limited.** The token is only in the JSON-LD, but the campaign cell ID is lost for any consumer of that URL.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. For bulk-sender requirements (Gmail/Yahoo), one-click support should be verified in the raw message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture.
- **Sender and tracking domains differ.** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`. Tracking and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. This is not a violation, but DMARC and DKIM alignment should be confirmed.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The provided HTML is truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.
- **Hashed email in URLs.** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` (a 64-hex hash) appear in JSON-LD URLs and in the HTTP pixel URLs. This is a privacy and disclosure concern rather than a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTM parameters** on the URLs visible in the truncated HTML. Attribution uses proprietary parameters: `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=20260929_PK_6229_FILEBAL`, `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`.
- **Campaign ID is consistent** across both URLs: `20260929_PK_6229_FILEBAL` appears in `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp`.
- **Landing page.** `offerPageUrl` points to `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/bras` for a "Save $50 When You Spend $175+" offer. I can't confirm from the source whether that page shows the offer.
- **Offer expiry matches.** `availabilityEnds` is `2026-09-29T23:59:59-04:00`, the same day as the send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the template substitution so `%%cellcode%%` resolves in `merchantHomepageUrl`. Add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%…%%` token.
2. Make `mi_u` consistent across URLs. The two JSON-LD URLs currently carry different values.
3. Move to HTTPS for `mi.victoriassecret.com` and the Dotomi pixels. Consider removing the ten `pubN.php` cookie-sync pixels, or at least not passing the hashed email over HTTP.
4. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png`.
5. Move the JSON-LD out of `<head>` and out of the table wrapper, or drop it. Correct the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`).
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta. Review the MSO `line-height:1`.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the raw message headers. Then check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
8. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that the `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp` parameters feed the analytics platform.
