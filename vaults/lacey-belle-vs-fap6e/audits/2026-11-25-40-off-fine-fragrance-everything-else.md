# 40% Off Fine Fragrance (& Everything Else)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Black Friday fragrance-and-everything send in 48 hours. It follows 11/24's "Fine Fragrance, NOW 40% Off for Black Friday" and this morning's "Black Friday = 40% Off EVERYTHING". The subject promises fragrance, and the hero delivers a black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner over a pink gift-set image. Beneath it is "40% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets" with a "Shop Fine Fragrances" button.
- After that it turns into the same kitchen-sink stack VS has run all week. A second "40% Off Everything" block comes next, then 5/$40 mists, 7/$35 panties, a free tote, a free tote and blanket, and free shipping at $50. A long legal footer closes it out. The rendering is clean, but the email argues with itself: two separate 40% headlines, then five more offers.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send with good top-of-email focus and little new to say. Subscribers who got the 11/24 fragrance send and today's earlier email will see recycled creative.

## What's working

- The hero matches the subject. "40% Off All Fine Fragrances & Beauty Gift Sets" sits right under the Black Friday banner, with a clear shop button.
- Offers are specific and numeric (40%, 5/$40, 7/$35, free tote, free shipping at $50), so there is no vague "savings" language.
- Section blocks are clearly separated, with large offer type and a "Limited Time" tag on each, so the email scans quickly.
- The Rewards/member block and the category grid ("40% Off All Your Favorites") give a second path for people who don't want fragrance.
- The render looks clean: no broken images, overlaps or visible gaps.

## What's weak

- The hero is narrower than the subject suggests. "Everything Else" is a second full 40% hero, so the email has two competing headlines.
- Seven or more offers stack down about 10,000px of scroll. The mists, panties, tote and tote-and-blanket blocks dilute the fragrance story.
- "Limited Time" labels have no visible date or countdown, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Exclusion fine print sits under several offers, and a long legal block dominates the footer. That reads as hedged, not honest.
- It's the third near-identical Black Friday 40% send in two days, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the fragrance hero, the 40% everything block and one add-on offer (the free tote). Drop the panties and mists blocks, or send them in their own category emails.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Cyber Monday midnight" or a countdown, in place of the generic "Limited Time".
- 3. Make the fragrance button specific, for example "Shop Fragrance Gift Sets", and show prices or best-sellers under the hero.
- 4. Move the exclusions out of each block into one short "details" link, so the offers feel cleaner.
- 5. Differentiate this send from this morning's: lead with gifting ("Gift sets 40% off") and say what's new since 11/24.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`) with malformed document structure, 10+ insecure third-party tracking pixels, and viewport settings that disable zoom. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs could not be inspected. Header checks were inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure cookie-sync pixels:** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, load over plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, and the requests themselves are visible in transit.
- **Email hash exposed in URLs:** each Dotomi pixel carries `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. This is the same value as `dtm_email_hash` in the CTA URL. A hashed email identifier is sent to a third party over unencrypted HTTP.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is HTTPS and 1x1 with `alt=""`, which is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - All Dotomi pixels (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php`, `UCMController`) have no `alt`. They are tracking images, so `alt=""` would be the correct value.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` is a hosted content image with no `alt`. This is a real accessibility gap if the image conveys content.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - A `<table>` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will push the table into `<body>`, and the viewport and charset metas may end up misplaced.
  - An empty `<title style="display:none">` sits in the body.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the markup mixes in HTML5 attributes such as `role="presentation"`.
- **Broken CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width:100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. A later `.ReadMsgBody` rule partly offsets the first.
- **Global table rules:** `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important;}` apply to every table, including nested ones.
- **Stylesheet:** the main `<style>` blocks are in `<head>` without inlining guarantees, so clients that strip `<style>` (some Gmail account types) will lose the responsive rules. This could not be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens. Visible personalization is limited to URL parameters (`customerID`, `mi_u`, `dtm_email_hash`) that rendered as values. The rest of the body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are absent from the captured headers. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** both fall in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.
- **Privacy:** the SHA-256 email hash and `customerID` hash are passed to Dotomi over HTTP (see section 2). This is worth a privacy review.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Visible URL:** the JSON-LD URL is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/beauty?cm_mmc=di-_-112526535-_-VSD_VSL_20261125_VSB_6547_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=112526535&dtm_email_hash=…&mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261125_VSB_6547_FILEBAL&customerID=…`.
- **Campaign tagging:** it uses proprietary parameters (`cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp`, `d_cellid`) and has no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Analytics tools that rely on UTM parameters will not attribute the visit unless they are mapped elsewhere.
- **Identifiers in the query string:** the URL carries `mi_u`, `customerID` and `dtm_email_hash`. These will land in site logs, referrers and analytics.
- **Landing alignment:** `/us/vs/beauty` is consistent with the "Fine Fragrance" subject. The other CTA destinations could not be checked because the tracking links were not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. Add `alt=""` to every tracking image. This resolves 20 of the QA warnings.
2. Stop sending the hashed email in third-party pixel URLs, or at least do it over TLS only.
3. Add a descriptive `alt` to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the `<head>` structure: move the table and JSON-LD script into `<body>` or `<head>` properly, and place the `<meta>` tags first. Remove the in-body `<title>`. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors.
6. Add `utm_*` parameters alongside the proprietary ones, or confirm they are mapped in analytics.
7. Re-run QA against raw headers, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Re-check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address on the full HTML.
