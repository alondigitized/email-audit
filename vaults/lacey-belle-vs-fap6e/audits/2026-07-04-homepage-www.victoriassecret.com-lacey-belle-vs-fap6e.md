# Homepage snapshot · www.victoriassecret.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: something went seriously wrong here. The hero is almost completely broken — the left half is a blank white void and the right side is just a black-and-red color block with no copy, no model, nothing. Below that, the VS & PINK Rewards module shows up which I actually appreciate, and way down at the bottom I can start to see some panty tiles with sale pricing. But for a brand I trust to know me, this is a rough welcome.

## What's working

- **VS & PINK Rewards module** — the three-benefit layout (Points Per $, $10 Reward, Birthday Gift) is clean and directly relevant to me. I'm already a member so the "Sign In" link is exactly what I want.
- **Sale-priced panty tiles** — No-Show Cheeky Panty and Exploded Logo Cotton Panty, both showing $12.50 with a strikethrough original price of $16/$40 Panties. That discount math catches my eye fast.
- **Light pink promo strip at the top** — I can see the strip is there, signaling a live promotion, even if I can't read the copy in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Hero is completely dead** — Left half is blank white; right half is a solid black slab and a crimson rectangle. No headline, no model, no campaign. This is the single most important piece of real estate on the page and it's a ghost.
- **JOIN NOW as primary CTA** — If I'm already a member (and I am), this button is noise. The page doesn't know I'm me, and it's leading with acquisition instead of engagement.
- **No nav visible** — I can't see any category links, which means I can't quickly jump to Bras or PINK without extra taps. For a bra-focused shopper, that's friction.
- **No bra content anywhere above the scroll** — Bras are my main reason for coming here; not a single hint of bra, sizing, or fit above the rewards module.
- **Confetti/colorful balls image** (right side, mid-page) — I can tell it's a July 4th or celebration vibe but it's cropped off-screen and feels disconnected without context.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero render immediately** — This is not a design note, it's a production fire. A blank hero on mobile is losing real conversions from people like me who bounce when the first screen looks broken.
- **Swap the JOIN NOW default for a "Welcome Back" signed-in state** — If the app knows I have the VS app, show my rewards balance and a "Shop Your Offers" CTA instead of acquisition messaging.
- **Add a bra-focused category tile above the rewards module** — Even a single "Shop Bras + New Arrivals" button would anchor me to why I came here.
- **Surface the Free Panty Wednesday or Semi-Annual Sale countdown** in the promo strip with legible copy — if there's a deadline, I want to see it the second I land.
