# 30% Off Everything Starts NOW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink Black Friday-week send, and the headline offer is clear. "30% Off Everything" sits under a "LIMITED TIME" tag, with a pink hero of five models in plaid and holiday pajamas. A "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH" banner sits above it. After a week of narrow, single-product sends (the 11/17 "Last Day" PJ BOGO, the 11/14 Panty Packs, the 11/13 Bras from $25), a storewide percentage is a big escalation, and the subject matches it.
- The body then dilutes it. After the hero come 7/$35 panties, 5/$40 Mists & Lotions, 30% off bras again, a free quilted duffle with $125+ purchase, a "30% Off All Your Favorites" tile grid, a rewards pitch and a Trending row. That makes six or more competing offers, several with their own SHOP NOW buttons, and the page is very long. The legal fine print at the bottom is dense.
- Judgment: a strong subject and hero lead into a bloated scroll. It will open well and get clicks, but it's not a sharp piece of creative. The 30% hero is the one thing a reader needs to see.

## What's working

- The subject and the hero both say "30% Off Everything," so the promise is unmistakable.
- The hero is clean, with a large script "30% Off" and category chips (Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear) that let people jump to what they want.
- Showing the category chips instead of one button gets people shopping in their own category.
- The 30% offer is repeated on the bra block and the favorites grid, which reinforces it.
- The free quilted duffle with $125+ gives people a reason to spend more, and the black "FREE Quilted Duffle" block stands out in the scroll.

## What's weak

- Too many offers fight for attention: 30% off, 7/$35, 5/$40, the free duffle, $25 off a first purchase. Nothing wins after the hero.
- The 30% off hero is repeated in the bra block and again in the favorites grid, so the email feels repetitive.
- The email is extremely long, and the fine print block at the bottom is dense and hard to read.
- "No code required. Excludes products subject to other promotions…" sits in small gray text under the hero. The "everything" claim is qualified right below it, which weakens the honesty of the offer.
- The rewards and credit card pitch at the bottom adds clutter and reads as a different campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle. Keep the 30% hero, the free duffle and one category grid, and drop the repeated bra block and the 7/$35 and 5/$40 blocks, or at least collapse them into the grid.
- 2. Move the duffle gift-with-purchase higher, right after the hero, because it's a concrete reason to buy now.
- 3. Put a real end date near the hero. "LIMITED TIME" isn't credible urgency.
- 4. Make the exclusions line darker and larger, or put it behind a clear "details" link.
- 5. Pull the rewards and card block out of this send. Send it separately.

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is a kitchen-sink Black Friday-week send, and the headline offer is clear. "30% Off Everything" sits under a "LIMITED TIME" tag, with a pink hero of five models in plaid and holiday pajamas. A "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH" banner sits above it. ## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally invalid (a `<table>` and `<script>` sit inside `<head>`) and carries 13 images with no alt text. Eleven of those are `http://` Dotomi pixels. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm several compliance items.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains such as `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`. Their destinations are unverified, not broken.
- **11 third-party Dotomi pixels use plain `http://`.** The first is `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`. The other ten are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading may block them.
  - The ten `cookieredir` pixels look like cookie-sync calls, and each carries `a4edea106b33...=1`, which appears to be a hashed email identifier.
- **Hashed identifiers sit in link query strings.** The JSON-LD `url` contains `dtm_email_hash=4edea106b333...` and `customerID=43cece97...`. Those values travel with any forwarded or shared link, and a hashed email is still a stable identifier.
- **The open-tracking pixel is fine.** It is `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` with `width="1" height="1" alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 13 images.**
  - Two content images: `.../m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg` and `.../m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. These are the ones that matter, because screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing from them.
  - Eleven Dotomi pixels. These are not visible content, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **The viewport meta disables zoom.** It reads `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` appears inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Browsers and clients will hoist or drop it unpredictably.
- **Empty `<title>`.** `<title style="display: none; ...">` has no content.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is common in email, but it is not a defect by itself.
- **CSS typo.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so the rule does nothing. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an invalid selector.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`. The personalization values present, `mi_u`, `customerID` and `dtm_email_hash`, are populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** `Authentication-Results` was not found either. QA notes that AgentMail may strip these headers, so this cannot be confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw `.eml` before acting on it.
- **The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML.** I can't confirm them either way.
- **SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown** for the same reason. `From: VictoriasSecret@e1.victoriassecret.com` is on a dedicated subdomain, which is the correct pattern.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign attribution parameters are present.** The only link visible in the truncated source uses `cm_mmc=di-_-111826515-_-VSD_VSL_20261118_VS_6499_FULFILE-_&d_cellid=111826515&mi_ecmp=20261118_VS_6499_FULFILE`. The campaign ID is consistent across the `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp` parameters.
- **No `utm_*` parameters are present.** Attribution relies on the proprietary `cm_mmc` and `mi_*` parameters, so GA-style analytics would see no campaign data.
- **Possible landing mismatch.** The JSON-LD promotion URL points to `/us/vs/newarrivals`, while the subject promises "30% Off Everything." I can't verify whether the other links land on a sitewide sale page, because the HTTP probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the two content images, and `alt=""` to the Dotomi pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com`, `https://ads.dotomi.com`), or drop the cookie-sync calls. Review whether the hashed email in these URLs is needed.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<table>` and JSON-LD `<script>` out of `<head>` into `<body>`. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors, and give `<title>` real content.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool's relay may be what drops them.
6. Probe a sample of the 61 click-tracking redirects to confirm their final landing pages match the "30% off everything" promise.
7. Consider adding `utm_*` parameters alongside the `cm_mmc` parameters.
