# 30% Off Bras (& MORE)—Don't Wait
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth cut of Victoria's Secret's week-long 30% Off Everything push. The creative hasn't moved since 11/20. The new part is a bra-first hero ("30% Off All Bras") and the subject "30% Off Bras (& MORE)—Don't Wait". The hero is clean: a pink banner, a big offer headline, a model shot and a single SHOP NOW button. The 30% number is the first thing you see.
- Below the hero it is the usual stack: 30% off everything with category pills, the 7/$35 Panty Party with a size selector, $49.95 CozyFlannel pajama sets and $29.95 slippers, 30% off fragrances, a category tile grid, a rewards block and a trending row. The email carries about six offers at once. For a Black Friday-week reader, that makes the bra headline feel like a relabel of the same send.
- The screenshot is very small, so I can't read the fine print or the preheader. Judged on structure, it's competent and on-brand, but it is a repeat. I'd rate it as strong execution of a message the subscriber has seen several times. That's the same 7 out of 10 as the 11/22 "30% Off Bras—Shop to Beat the Rush" send.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous. "30% Off All Bras" is large, with one SHOP NOW button.
- The subject and the hero headline match, so there's no mismatch after the open.
- "Limited Time" labels sit on each module and keep the urgency consistent.
- The 7/$35 Panty Party keeps its product grid and size selector. It's the most shoppable module, as it was in the 11/21 panty-led send.
- The rewards block is visible, so members see their benefit.

## What's weak

- The bra hero is followed straight away by a second "30% Off Everything" hero. The two offers compete for the same click.
- It's the same body template as 11/20–11/22, with only the top changed. Cadence fatigue is the main risk.
- The email stacks six or more offers (bras, everything, panties, pajamas, fragrance, favorites) and is very long. The focus the subject promised is lost.
- "(& MORE)" and "Don't Wait" are vague. "Don't Wait" adds pressure but no deadline.
- The "no code required, excludes select third-party styles" disclaimers appear under multiple heroes, so the offer reads as hedged.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email actually bra-led. Cut the second 30%-off-everything hero and the fragrance module, and put a bra product grid under the hero (bestsellers with fit or size cues). That would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Put a real deadline on the offer, such as "Ends tonight" or "Ends Cyber Monday", and put it in both the subject and the hero.
- 3. Vary the creative against the last five sends. Use a new hero image or a bra-fit angle instead of the same pajama and panty blocks.
- 4. Use one specific CTA, such as "Shop Bras", instead of a generic "Shop Now".
- 5. Move the disclaimers into one footer line.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has structural problems: block elements sit inside `<head>`, and the viewport meta tag blocks pinch-zoom. It also loads 11 third-party Dotomi pixels over plaintext `http://`, and those pixel URLs carry a hashed email address. The QA run flagged several header checks, but they can't be confirmed from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 63 tracking links were skipped for HTTP probing (click-redirect domains such as `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Link health is unknown.
- **Plaintext tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop them, and the requests are readable on the network.
- **Hashed email in plaintext requests:** each `pubN.php` URL includes `a4edea106b33…c6fe=1`. The same value appears as `dtm_email_hash` in the landing URL, so a hashed email identifier travels unencrypted.
- **Long tracking URLs:** the `url` value in the JSON-LD block (`…newarrivals?cm_mmc=…&dtm_email_hash=…&customerID=…`) carries several per-recipient identifiers. This is fine for function, but it raises the privacy exposure if the link is forwarded.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid `<head>` content:** a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing the JSON-LD `<script>` sits inside `<head>`. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>` at the table. The `<meta name="viewport">`, `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and both `<style>` blocks that follow then land in `<body>`. Support for them is inconsistent across clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` targets a nonexistent element. Neither rule applies.
- **Stray `<title>`:** an empty `<title style="display:none…">` sits in the body.
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg`
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`
- **Missing alt text on pixels:** the 11 Dotomi pixels also have no alt text. Because they are tracking pixels, this is low severity.
- **Legacy doctype and no `lang`:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional and no `lang` attribute is visible on `<html>`. The main layout tables don't consistently use `role="presentation"`. Only the stylingblock wrappers do.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source. The personalization values are all rendered hashes and IDs (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u=200492586522`). The source is truncated, so the rest of the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and neither was `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool notes these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so they are not confirmed defects. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- The footer is cut off in the provided source, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be checked.
- The From address uses `e1.victoriassecret.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with normal ESP setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters:** the only landing URL visible has none. It uses ESP/vendor parameters instead (`cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp=20261123_VS_6537_FULFILE`, `dtm_email_hash`). This is acceptable if the analytics are keyed on `cm_mmc`, but it is not UTM-compatible.
- **Possible subject/landing mismatch:** the JSON-LD `PromotionCard` URL is `/us/vs/newarrivals`, while the subject is "30% Off Bras (& MORE)". This is only one URL from a truncated source, so the mismatch isn't confirmed for the CTAs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD wrapper table out of `<head>`. Put the `<script>` in `<head>` on its own, and keep the metas and styles inside `<head>`.
2. Change the 11 Dotomi pixel URLs to `https://`. If the vendor doesn't support HTTPS, drop the pixels. Avoid sending hashed emails over HTTP.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
5. Fix the invalid selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`) and add `lang` and `role="presentation"` on layout tables.
6. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also check the footer unsubscribe link and the physical address.
7. Confirm the CTA destinations match the "30% off bras" offer, and that campaign IDs reach analytics, since there are no UTMs.
