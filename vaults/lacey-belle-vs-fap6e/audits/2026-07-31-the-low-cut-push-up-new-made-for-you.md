# The Low-Cut Push-Up—NEW & Made for You
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same New Low-Cut Push-Up Bra that anchored the 2026-07-28 send ("NEW Low-Cut Push-Up Bra + FREE Shipping," 8.00/10) — just three days later, with the free-shipping hook dropped and the subject softened to "NEW & Made for You." The product story itself is still fine: a clean fit callout ("Adds up to one cup size and has a plunge neckline, perfect for low-cut tops") sits right under the headline, and the swatch grid gives shoppers real color options before they click.
- Where this send loses ground versus its predecessor is focus. The hero image is a leopard-print swim/lifestyle shot on a beach — not the bra — so the very first thing the eye lands on doesn't match either the subject line or the product actually being sold. From there the email sprawls into the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged in the 07-27 send: Panty Packs, 30% Off Pajama Sets, Whipped Mists & Lotions (Buy 2 Get 3 Free), and a $25-off-$125 credit card offer, all stacked before you reach the category nav and rewards footer. Five distinct offers in one email dilutes whatever the subject promised.
- Net: a recognizable product with decent supporting copy, undercut by a hero/subject mismatch and an unfocused body. This reads as a lower-effort repeat of a bra push that already ran this week, not a fresh, single-minded send.

## What's working

- Subject and headline both name the exact product ("Low-Cut Push-Up"), so there's no confusion once you reach the bra section.
- The fit/benefit line under the bra headline ("Adds up to one cup size... perfect for low-cut tops") answers a real shopping question before the click.
- Color swatch grid gives a concrete preview of options, not just a single studio shot.
- "LAST DAY" urgency banners on Panty Packs and Pajama Sets are visually distinct (dark bar, all-caps) and easy to spot while scanning.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and typography all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- The hero image is a leopard-print swim/beach shot, not the push-up bra — it doesn't match the subject line or deliver the promised product on first glance.
- Five separate offers (bra, panties, pajamas, mists/lotions, $25-off-$125) compete for attention in one email, the same unfocused pattern the 07-27 send got dinged for.
- This is a near-repeat of the 07-28 push-up bra email just three days later, with the free-shipping incentive removed — reads as reheated inventory, not a new push.
- CTA copy is the same generic "SHOP NOW" on every single section, giving no product-specific reason to click any one of them over another.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere near the hero product to build purchase confidence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero image for the actual push-up bra (the leopard set already used further down works) so the first thing readers see matches the subject and headline — this is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut the email to two offers max: the push-up bra as hero, and one secondary offer (Pajama Sets or the $25-off-$125 credit-card deal), moving the rest to a "shop more" strip instead of full modules.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTA copy on the hero module, e.g. "Shop the Push-Up Bra," so the click intent is unambiguous.
- 4. Bring back the free-shipping or an explicit discount on the bra itself — right now the hero product is the only offer in the email without its own price incentive.
- 5. Space this send further from the 07-28 push-up bra email, or use it to introduce something genuinely new (a color drop, a bundle) rather than repeating the same product three days apart.
