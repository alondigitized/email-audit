## Executive summary

- This is another PINK kitchen-sink send — four distinct campaigns stacked (Relay Refresh activewear, backpacks/totes from $35, the "When Icons Unite" fragrance/lotion collection, and a generic 25%-off-$60+ sitewide code) under a subject that cherry-picks pieces from two of them ("25% Off" + "Relay Refresh") while the actual inbox preview text promotes a *fifth* angle entirely: backpacks. Subject, preview, and hero visual all point at three different offers — nothing lines up.
- The Relay Refresh hero itself is clean, well-cast creative (two models, leopard-print bra tops and leggings, one "GET MOVING" CTA), but it carries zero visible discount. So a shopper who opens because the subject promised "25% Off" scrolls past two unrelated promo blocks (backpacks, fragrance) before reaching the actual code. Compare to 7/23's fragrance launch or 7/22's PINK x JanSport drop, both of which scored 9.00 by keeping subject, hero, and offer as one thread — this send fragments that thread three ways.
- Solid individual creative blocks, undercut by a subject/preview/hero mismatch that will frustrate anyone who clicks in expecting what the subject line sold them.

- No render bugs — layout, product photography, and buttons are all clean throughout

## What's weak

- Preview text ("We'll take these to go, please: Backpacks & Totes from $35") has nothing to do with the subject's "25% Off" or "Relay Refresh" — a third, unrelated hook
- The 25%-off-$60+ offer named in the subject only appears in the fourth content block, after two unrelated campaigns
- The Relay Refresh hero, what the subject actually spotlights, shows no discount at all
- Four separate "LAST DAY" pressure points stacked in one email dilutes urgency instead of building it

- 2. Rewrite the preview text to reinforce the subject's actual angle instead of introducing backpacks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Relay Refresh Drop + 25% Off $60+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sparkly, Sporty, 25% Off Today Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet Relay Refresh — plus 25% off your $60+ cart`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New activewear prints just landed. Code inside.`
- 3. Move the true hero offer (25%-off-$60+) up ahead of backpacks and fragrance so the promised discount isn't three scrolls deep.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

Mixed-content HTTP images are commonly blocked or flagged by modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), which can break tracking attribution even if it doesn't break rendering.
