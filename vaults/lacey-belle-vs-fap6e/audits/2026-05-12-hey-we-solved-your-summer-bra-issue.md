# Hey, We Solved Your Summer Bra Issue
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-12**
## Executive summary

- This PINK send is a summer swim push built around a 30% off offer, stacked with a beauty giveaway and a "Follower Faves" product grid to fill out the scroll. The subject—"Hey, We Solved Your Summer Bra Issue"—is a clever problem-solution hook that earns the open. Once inside, the email delivers on the promise with a clear swim discount and several supporting modules. The visual execution is bright, on-brand, and free of obvious rendering issues.
- The problem is length and scatter. By the time you've scrolled through the swim offer, the beauty giveaway, the "Hotter Than a Heatwave" lifestyle block, and the Follower Faves grid, the original "bra issue" hook has completely dissolved. The email can't decide whether it's a bra email, a swim email, or a beauty email. That diffusion costs click-through. A persona oriented toward athletic wear will feel mildly addressed by swim but largely ignored by the beauty giveaway and lifestyle framing.
- Solid execution on awareness and brand reinforcement; weaker on focused conversion.

## What's working

- **30% OFF PINK SWIM** is rendered in large, high-contrast type — the offer is impossible to miss mid-scroll.
- The "Hey, We Solved Your Summer Bra Issue" subject is one of the better problem-framing hooks in this category; it manufactures curiosity without being deceptive.
- Colorful, on-brand visuals are cohesive throughout — the summer palette and model styling match the PINK brand identity precisely.
- "Follower Faves" product grid adds social-proof framing to bottom-of-funnel browsing without requiring extra copy.
- The "Big Full Sized Beauty Giveaway" module adds a secondary value prop that can pull in segments not primarily motivated by the swim discount.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a bra solution but the hero content leads with swim — misalignment between the hook and the landing experience will cause scroll drop-off immediately.
- Four distinct content modules (swim, beauty giveaway, lifestyle, follower faves) make this email feel like four campaigns stitched together; there's no single focal action.
- "Hotter Than a Heatwave" is a lifestyle headline with no clear offer attached — it adds visual clutter without adding a reason to click.
- No visible urgency mechanism — "30% off" without a deadline or inventory signal removes the push to act now vs. later.
- Athletic/activewear dimension of PINK is entirely absent, meaning customers from that segment get a mismatch between their interest and the email content.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Tighten the email to one hero offer.** Kill either the swim module or the beauty giveaway as the primary — pick one, make it the whole email, use the other as a footer teaser. Scatter is the single biggest conversion killer here.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the 30% off.** "Ends Sunday" or "48 hours only" immediately makes the offer actionable instead of bookmark-able.
- 3. **Bridge the subject promise to the hero copy.** If the subject is "We Solved Your Summer Bra Issue," the hero H1 should echo that language — something like "Meet Your New Summer Bra" — not pivot directly to swim.
- 4. **Give "Hotter Than a Heatwave" a clickable offer.** Right now it reads as a mood board. Add a CTA with a specific category or product, or cut it.
- 5. **Surface sizing/fit detail in the hero.** The "summer bra issue" angle implies comfort/fit — a single line about sweat-proof, no-dig, or wireless would reinforce the value proposition without adding visual weight.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Summer Bra Fix Is 30% Off Right Now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Bra Built for Summer Heat (30% Off Today)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free full-size beauty haul with swim — this weekend only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Swim and bras built for heat. 30% off, this weekend only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed content (HIGH):** 12 images served over `http://` will be silently blocked by Gmail (since 2019), Apple Mail, and Outlook 365. The 10 Dotomi pixels and the MI o.gif pixel are all affected. This silently suppresses retargeting audience data and open measurement.

**Missing alt text (16 images):** Every tracking pixel and the two content images flagged (`824a57aaa9e48e40.png`, `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`) lack `alt` attributes. Decorative pixels should at minimum carry `alt=""` — content images with no alt fail WCAG 1.1.1.

**DOCTYPE:** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` is used. Not a breaking issue in practice (most ESPs use this for compatibility), but it triggers legacy rendering paths in Outlook.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta:** Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile — WCAG 1.4.4 violation. Should be removed or changed to `user-scalable=yes`.

**`max-scale=1` in viewport meta:** Same effect as above — prevents zoom for low-vision users.

---
