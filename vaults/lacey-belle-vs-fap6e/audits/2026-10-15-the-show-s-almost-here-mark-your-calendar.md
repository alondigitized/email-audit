# The Show's Almost Here—Mark Your Calendar
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This email sells a TV event. The countdown clock (94:34:52) and the "Sun, Oct 18, live from Los Angeles, 4:30PT" block tell you the show is coming within seconds. The big black-and-white hero and the pink event banner are the strongest part. Then it turns into a standard Victoria's Secret catalog: merch, a gift-with-purchase tote, a category grid and a trending-products grid.
- The subject says "Mark Your Calendar," but the email has no add-to-calendar button. The only event CTA is "Get the Inside Scoop," which is vague. The money offer, the Free Fashion Show Tote with a $125+ purchase, sits well below the fold. The top banner offers a free panty with purchase, so the email carries two competing offers. It's a good brand-and-hype send, and it does less than it could to convert.

## What's working

- The live countdown timer at the top creates credible urgency and matches the subject.
- The full-bleed hero and pink "Fashion Show 2026" banner make the date and time easy to read (Sun, Oct 18, 4:30PT/7:30ET).
- The tote offer is a clear block: "Limited Time," "Free Fashion Show Tote with any $125+ purchase," a $59.95 value and a "Shop Now" button.
- The Rewards and $25-off card strip gives members a reason to engage.
- It's visually consistent with the event: the merch hero ("Ombré Styles") connects to the show.

## What's weak

- "Mark Your Calendar" has no matching action. There is no calendar link or reminder button.
- "Get the Inside Scoop" is a weak CTA for an event with a specific stream time and platform.
- The hero and merch images are fashion-forward but show no product detail or link-worthy specifics.
- Several things compete in the body: the free-panty banner, merch, the tote, a category grid and Rewards. Only the tote has a clear price condition.
- The fine print under the tote is a dense wall of text, and the offer details are buried far down the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an "Add to Calendar" button next to the countdown. It would pay off the subject's promise and give a utility click.
- 2. Rename the CTA to something specific like "Watch Live Oct 18 on YouTube."
- 3. Move the free tote higher, directly after the event banner, and drop or shrink the free-panty line so there is one offer.
- 4. Trim the category grid and trending rows. Keep the event, the tote and one merch drop.
- 5. Cut the legal block or turn it into collapsed or linked terms.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stream it live from LA on YouTube, plus score a free Fashion Show Tote.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based ExactTarget/SFMC template with a duplicated open pixel, ten insecure (`http://`) third-party Dotomi pixels with no alt text, and one content image missing alt text. The header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the QA relay did not capture those headers. Automated QA: 57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Duplicate open pixel.** The same tracker is embedded twice: once in the top `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">` and again later in the body. Both use `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?VDDS7CVP3NPEJMDXELHTAK3EXM.70269&d=70269&bmt=0`. This can double-count opens in clients that load both.
- **Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels over plaintext HTTP.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, each with `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…&dtmc_email_category=20261014_VS_6308_FILEBAL`.
  - Image proxies and mail clients commonly block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, so these will likely fail silently.
  - The 64-character hex query key looks like a hashed identifier. If so, it is being sent over an unencrypted request to a third-party ad network.
- **Click links not probed.** 45 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 11 images:**
  - All ten Dotomi pixels (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php`) and the `login.dotomi.com` pixel, which have no `alt` attribute at all.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. This is a hosted content image, so a missing alt is a real screen-reader and images-off problem.
  - The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, or be moved out of the body.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid or no-op CSS in the first `<style>` block:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element. The later `.ReadMsgBody` copy is correct.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector for anything.
  - Mobile rule `max-height:auto !important` is not a valid value.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title style="display: none; …">` sits inside `<body>` inside nested tables, and `<style>` blocks are also embedded in the body. Gmail and some Outlook builds strip or ignore these.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables have no `role="presentation"`. Only the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables have it.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional. It works but is dated.
- **Leftover template debris:**
  - The comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->`.
  - The empty `<div id="AMPScript">` with "Ampscript v0 START/END" comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[%…%]` tokens appear in the truncated source, but the remainder was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so this is unconfirmed. Bulk senders to Gmail and Yahoo need both, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `e1.victoriassecret.com`; check alignment in the raw headers.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are wrapped in `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects and were skipped by the probe. UTM parameters, final landing URLs and landing-page status could not be checked. The one campaign identifier visible is `dtmc_email_category=20261014_VS_6308_FILEBAL`, and that appears only on the Dotomi pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the duplicate open pixel, keeping one instance.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. They are blocked or rewritten by proxies and send an identifier over plaintext.
3. Add `alt` to every `<img>`. Use descriptive text for `5bcec3ac-…png` and `alt=""` for the pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix or delete the broken CSS: add the dot to `ReadMsgBody`, drop `min-width{…}`, and replace `max-height:auto`.
6. Move `<title>` and `<style>` into `<head>`, add `role="presentation"` to layout tables, and clear the leftover comments and the empty `AMPScript` div.
7. Check the raw `.eml`, not the relayed copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
8. Resolve a sample of the 45 click-tracked links to confirm UTM tagging and landing-page parity.
