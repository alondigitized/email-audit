## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud template with mobile media queries. The main problems are 12 plain-`http://` tracking images, missing alt text on a content image, and a viewport meta that disables zoom. The truncated source and AgentMail relay stripped headers, so compliance and authentication can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so QA couldn't check destinations or status codes. Whether any are broken is unknown.
- **12 images load over `http://`, which can be blocked or mixed-content flagged:**
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261107_PK_6440_TRIPLE`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The ten Dotomi `pub1–pub10.php` calls are cookie-redirect pixels. They add ten extra third-party requests per open.
- **Two open-tracking pixels.** There are two 1x1 `open.aspx` images, one right after `<body>` and one inside the content wrapper. They carry different `qs` values, so they are not exact duplicates, but they are redundant.
- **Per-recipient ID in the URL.** The `mi_u=200492586522` parameter is in the plain-HTTP `o.gif` URL, so it is exposed unencrypted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`. It is hosted in the content image library, so it is likely a content image, not a pixel.
  - The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`) also lack `alt`. This is low impact, but it fails validation.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Invalid or ineffective CSS:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it targets a nonexistent element. A correct `.ReadMsgBody` appears later in the body.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
- **Markup validity:** `<title style="display:none…">` sits inside `<body>` and is empty. A `<style>` block is also embedded in the body. Many clients tolerate this, but Gmail may strip it.
- **Layout tables:** the outer layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, while the inner `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables have it. The inconsistency affects screen-reader output.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email clients.
- **Template residue:** `<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->` is an unrendered shell-style snippet left in a comment. There is also an empty `<div id="AMPScript">` with empty `Ampscript v0 START/END` comments. Neither is visible, but both are template artifacts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens or `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` placeholders appear in the visible source. The only residue is the comment noted above. The HTML is truncated, so the body copy could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo make these headers mandatory. Check the raw `.eml` or a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `PINK@e2.vspink.com` and the links use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. The mismatch between `e2.vspink.com` and `victoriassecret.com` is normal for ESP setups but can't be validated here.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTMs not verifiable.** All CTAs are wrapped in opaque `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` redirects with encoded `qs` values, and QA didn't resolve them. I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing page alignment or the promo state on the landing page.
- **Campaign ID:** the visible `mi_ecmp=20261107_PK_6440_TRIPLE` and `dtmc_email_category=20261107_PK_6440_TRIPLE` parameters are consistent with each other and with the send date (2026-11-07).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Switch the 12 `http://` tracking pixels to `https://`.** Check that `mi.victoriassecret.com` and the Dotomi hosts serve valid TLS. Consider dropping the ten Dotomi `pub1–10.php` pixels or loading them server-side.
2. **Add a descriptive `alt` to `5bcec3ac-…png`.** Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.**
4. **Fix the CSS typos** (`.ReadMsgBody`, `min-width{}`, `max-height:auto`). Move the empty `<title>` into `<head>` or remove it.
5. **Strip the template residue:** the `date "+ET%m.%y"` comment and the empty AMPScript div.
6. **Re-verify compliance on the raw message.** Check for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have dropped them. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. **Resolve the 63 redirect links** with a follow-redirects probe. That would confirm landing URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
