# 40% Off Bras (& Everything Else) is HERE​
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday-week send from Victoria's Secret in about 72 hours. The subject finally puts bras first: "40% Off Bras (& Everything Else) is HERE". The hero delivers on it with a black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner over a "LIMITED TIME 40% Off All Bras" block and a SHOP NOW button. Today's earlier "40% Off Everything—Our Thanks to You" was the gratitude angle. This send drops it and returns to a bra-led hero.
- The body is the same kitchen-sink template as the last few days. Below the hero it stacks "40% Off All Sleep & Lingerie", "40% Off Everything", "7/$35 Panty Party", "Free Tote", "FREE Tote & Blanket" and free shipping at $50+. Each block has its own giant headline and CTA, so the eye has no single winner after the first screen. The long legal block at the bottom is dense and gray.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand Black Friday send that is almost indistinguishable from the three before it. The bra-first subject is the only real change, and the body doesn't follow through on it.

## What's working

- The hero matches the subject. "40% Off All Bras" is the first thing under the banner, with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- The black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner gives instant seasonal context.
- The "40% Off" headline type is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is readable even in a thumbnail.
- The "40% Off Your Favorites" category tile grid (Bras, Panties, Sleep, Activewear, Lingerie, Loungewear) gives good second-chance navigation.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Seven or more offers stack in sequence (40% bras, 40% sleep, 40% everything, 7/$35, free tote, free tote and blanket, free shipping), so no one offer lands.
- "40% Off" appears three times as a headline, and the repetition reads as filler.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW", and a few are category buttons like "SLEEP" and "LINGERIE". None names a product or benefit.
- The "limited time" urgency has no visible end date or deadline.
- The email is very long, and the exclusions and promo-code fine print at the bottom are dense and easy to ignore.
- The cadence is heavy. A subscriber who got "40% Off Everything—Our Thanks to You" earlier today gets the same offer again, and the "(& Everything Else)" in the subject adds nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three blocks: bras hero, one free-gift offer, and shipping. Collapse the duplicate "40% Off" headers into one. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight", to the hero.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific copy such as "Shop 40% Off Bras", and link a hero product (a specific bra style) instead of a category.
- 4. Show one social-proof element, such as a best-seller rating or "most-loved bra" callout, to give the click a reason.
- 5. Pace the sends. A fifth near-identical email in three days trains people to skip. Segment by purchase category (bra buyers vs. fragrance buyers) so the subject and hero are different for each.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) template with 67 tracked links and a set of third-party Dotomi retargeting pixels. The confirmed problems are insecure `http://` pixel requests, a missing alt on a content image, malformed head/body structure, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 67 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains, e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`), so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi requests use `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`

  Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or enforce mixed-content rules, may block or rewrite these.
- **Cookie-sync fan-out:** `pub1.php` to `pub10.php` is a 10-request cookie-sync chain on every open. It adds load and privacy-filter exposure, and these requests are likely stripped by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and similar features.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** The JSON-LD `url` and the `pub*.php` query strings carry `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…aafc6fe` and `customerID=43cece97…b1e2f`. These are SHA-256 hashes, and the same email hash appears in the pixel URLs sent to a third party.
- The open-tracking pixel (`open.aspx?qs=…`, 1×1, `alt=""`) is implemented correctly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on a content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt attribute. It is hosted in the content image library, so it is probably a content image.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** The `dotomi.com` pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1`–`pub10`) have no alt. They should carry `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, or be hidden.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid structure:**
  - A `<table>` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will implicitly close the head there, so the viewport and charset metas may end up in the body.
  - An empty `<title style="display:none…">` appears inside the body.
- **Broken CSS selectors:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element rather than the class.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector.
- **JSON-LD:** `PromotionCard` schema is Gmail-specific markup, and the `url` is not a clean landing URL. It appears to be valid JSON.
- **Subject line:** it ends with an invisible zero-width character (`HERE​`, U+200B). This can cause display or dedupe quirks.
- **Not assessed:** the HTML is truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the markup, such as `role` attributes, heading structure or dark-mode handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are flagged as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay. The From domain (`e1.victoriassecret.com`) is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer:** the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) is outside the truncated HTML, so it is **not verified**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameter scheme:** the email uses the retailer's own parameters rather than `utm_*`: `cm_mmc=di-_-112626525-_-VSD_VSL_20261126_VS_6553_FILEBAL`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp=20261126_VS_6553_FILEBAL`. There is no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, so analytics tools expecting UTMs, such as GA, will not classify the traffic without custom mapping.
- **Landing page:** `/us/vs/bras` matches the "40% Off Bras" subject. Other link destinations weren't visible in the truncated source, and the site-side promo was not checked.
- **Identifier exposure:** hashed customer and email identifiers are placed in the landing URL query string, so they persist in browser history, referrer logs and site analytics.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all pixels over HTTPS (Dotomi supports `https://`), and consider reducing or consolidating the `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync fan-out.
2. Add an alt to `5bcec3ac-…png`, and add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0, maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<script type="application/ld+json">` and its wrapper table out of `<head>`, and place the `<meta>` tags before any content so the head isn't closed early.
5. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, remove `min-width{}`), and remove the empty `<title>` in the body.
6. Strip the trailing U+200B from the subject line.
7. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer on the raw message, since the relay may be dropping the headers. Re-run the link probe against the final redirect destinations.
8. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that analytics maps `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp`.
