# 40% Off Everything—Black Friday is Finally HERE
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the real Black Friday send, and it finally changes the number: "40% Off Everything" replaces the 30% that VS ran all week, including 11/23's "30% Off Bras (& MORE)—Don't Wait". The hero is strong. A "BLACK FRIDAY" banner sits over a group of models, a "HAPPENING NOW" tag leads into a big "40% Off Everything," and a row of category pills sits underneath. The eye goes straight to the offer.
- Below the hero, though, it's the same kitchen-sink template. I count seven or more stacked offers: 7/$35 panties, 5/$40 mists & lotions, a free tote with $100+, a free tote & blanket with $150+, free shipping on $50+, a second "40% Off All Your Favorites" category grid, and a rewards/credit-card strip. The length is overwhelming on mobile. The "Happening Now" and "Limited Time" tags repeat so often that they stop meaning anything, and the stacked gift-with-purchase tiers could confuse readers about which threshold applies.
- Verdict: a solid, high-converting hero on a bloated body. The escalation to 40% is the first real news of the week, and the subject captures it. Fewer offers would sharpen the email.

## What's working

- The 40% escalation is the first genuinely new offer after a week of 30%, so the subject and hero have real news.
- The hero is clear: a "BLACK FRIDAY" banner, a large "40% Off Everything," and category pills for Sleep, Bras, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie and Activewear.
- The offer is a single percentage, easy to grasp at a glance.
- The "7/$35 Panty Party" tile has a size selector (XS to XXL), which lets the reader start shopping straight from the email.
- Free shipping on $50+ and the free tote give a low-threshold sweetener.
- The pink and black palette looks on-brand and reads as a premium holiday send.

## What's weak

- The body has too many stacked offers (panties, mists, two free-gift tiers, shipping, a second 40% grid), which dilutes the "Everything" message.
- The 40% offer is repeated in a second category grid near the bottom. That's redundant, and the grid is another long scroll.
- The two free-gift tiers ($100+ tote, $150+ tote & blanket) look alike and ask the reader to work out the thresholds.
- Fine print is dense. A long legal block sits at the bottom, plus smaller disclaimers under each module, which cuts against the "honest offer" signal.
- "Limited Time" labels appear on several modules, and the week of near-identical sends makes the urgency feel less credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three modules: the 40% hero, one hero product or category grid, and a single gift-with-purchase. Move the panty and mist deals to a follow-up send or collapse them into one row.
- 2. Merge the two free-gift tiers into one clear ladder, such as "Free tote $100+ / Free tote & blanket $150+," in one module.
- 3. Drop the duplicate 40% category grid, or make it the only category navigation instead of repeating the hero pills.
- 4. Put the member and credit-card perks higher up. Right now they're buried near the footer.
- 5. Add one concrete deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11:59 PM," instead of repeating "Limited Time."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Its markup is structurally invalid: a `<table>` sits inside `<head>`, and there are broken CSS selectors. It also loads ten non-HTTPS retargeting pixels. The source was truncated, so the footer and most links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status are unverified.
- **Ten retargeting pixels load over plaintext HTTP.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content (Apple Mail Privacy Protection, Gmail's image proxy) will drop them. They also leak the recipient hash over an unencrypted channel.
- **Every one of those pixels is missing `alt`.** Each URL carries `a4edea106b33…=1`, which matches the `dtm_email_hash` value in the JSON-LD URL. That is a SHA-256 email hash sent to a third-party ad network.
- **Open-tracking pixel is fine.** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is 1x1 with `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** `<head>` contains `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">…<script type="application/ld+json">` before `<meta name="viewport">` and `<style>`. Tables aren't valid in `<head>`, so parsers will close the head early. The viewport meta and the `<style>` blocks that follow may then be treated as body content, and some clients ignore them.
- **`<title>` is in the body and empty.** It appears as `<title style="display: none; …"></title>` inside a content table.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Broken CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing the leading `.`, so it matches no element. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an invalid selector. Both are dead rules.
- **Global `table{max-width:600px!important;}`** also applies to nested layout tables and may constrain inner elements unintentionally.
- **Content image missing alt.** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no `alt`, so it is invisible to screen readers and gives no fallback when images are off.
- **Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional** while the markup uses `role="presentation"`. This is harmless but inconsistent.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient values are rendered as hashes: `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`. The source was truncated, so the body and footer could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header was not found.** The QA tool flags that it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, and it is probably a capture artifact, since Salesforce Marketing Cloud normally sets it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` matches the tracking and image domains, which is consistent with a properly delegated sending subdomain.
- **Footer not inspected.** The visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address required by CAN-SPAM were cut off by truncation, so they are unverified.
- **Email hash shared with an ad network.** Passing the hashed email to `dotomi.com` with no visible consent mechanism in the captured source is a privacy exposure under CCPA/GDPR.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking uses vendor parameters, not UTM.** The JSON-LD URL carries `cm_mmc=di-_-112426515-_-VSD_VSL_20261124_VS_6540_FULLFILE-_`, `d_cellid=112426515`, `mi_ecmp=20261124_VS_6540_FULLFILE` and `dtm_email_hash`. There are no `utm_*` parameters, so GA-based attribution depends on the `cm_mmc` mapping.
- **Personal identifiers sit in a landing URL.** `customerID=43cece97…` and `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` are in the URL query string, which can end up in server logs, referrers and analytics.
- **Landing page may not match the offer.** The JSON-LD promotion URL is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/bras`, a category page. The subject promises "40% Off Everything". Whether the visible CTAs go sitewide could not be checked, because those links were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block, viewport meta and `<style>` into a valid `<head>`. Keep tables in `<body>` only, and put the `<title>` in the head.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix or delete the broken selectors `ReadMsgBody` (should be `.ReadMsgBody`) and `min-width{}`.
4. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS (`https://`) and add `alt=""` to them. Reconsider whether the email-hash retargeting is justified and covered by consent.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
6. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Also confirm the footer has a visible unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Add `utm_*` parameters, or confirm that `cm_mmc` maps into analytics. Remove `customerID` and the email hash from URL query strings where possible.
8. Confirm that the hero CTA destination matches the "40% Off Everything" offer.
