# Panty Packs from $20—Start Shopping
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- The top of this email does its job. A tight lace-panty close-up, "LIMITED TIME," italic "Panty Packs from $20," and a white "SHOP NOW" button make a clear, single-offer hero that matches the subject. The panty promo is the strongest part of the send.
- The rest of the email dilutes it. Below the hero it becomes a catalog: a red holiday-bra grid ("Bras for Every Occasion") with four category tiles (Cozying Up, Going Glam, Stepping Out, Staying Active), then an $8 Mists & Lotions block, a Fashion Show 2026 countdown, a "Free Fashion Show Tote" block, a category grid, a rewards strip and a "Now Trending" panty row. That is five or six competing messages. The email also runs about 10,000px tall, so it is a long scroll for a $20 pack promo.
- It lands as a competent but over-stuffed VS send. Compare the 10/15 "The PJ Shop is *OPEN*" and 10/14 "A Bestselling Bra, Now with Bling" sends: this is the same everything-in-one-email pattern, now with a fourth category added.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: "Panty Packs from $20" appears in large italic type over a vivid pink and black lace close-up.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is a high-contrast white pill, centered under the headline, and impossible to miss.
- The "LIMITED TIME" eyebrow adds urgency without shouting.
- The black strip above the hero, "Download the app & create an account for a Free Panty with Purchase," gives a second incentive that fits the panty theme.
- The red bra grid on the pale blue background with string lights looks seasonal and holiday-ready.
- Pricing and exclusions are stated under the hero: "Online only. Orig. up to $40. Select styles. Excludes third-party styles and clearance."

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: panty packs, free panty with app signup, $8 mists and lotions (buy five or more), the free tote, and the Fashion Show countdown. The panty message loses focus after the first screen.
- The hero fine print sits at the bottom of the image, separated from the headline by a large gap of imagery. It reads as an afterthought.
- The preheader "And did we mention the season's best Bras?" pulls attention to bras, not the panty pack subject, and is paired with a "View on browser" link.
- The hero is all product flat-lay with no model, and the second block is a bra grid. Bras take the next screen even though the subject is panties, so the follow-through on the promised category is weak.
- The Fashion Show block (countdown, "Get the Tote Now," "Free Fashion Show Tote") feels like a separate email stapled on. It repeats the 10/15 "The Show's Almost Here" send from the day before.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the panty pack story. Keep the hero, add a "Now Trending" panty row with prices directly under it, and move the bra grid and Mists & Lotions to a separate send or a footer module.
- 2. Put the price detail in the hero: "Pick any 3, from $20" or similar, with the fine print tight under the CTA.
- 3. Move the Fashion Show module to its own event email, or collapse it to a single slim banner.
- 4. Make the preheader about the pack offer, not bras.
- 5. Add one product tile with a visible price and a size or style cue (lace, cotton, seamless).

- **Subject Alt B:** `Lace, cotton, seamless: packs from $20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free panty when you order in the app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited time. Online only. Select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "Panty Packs from $20—Start Shopping"

### 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). It has 11 third-party Dotomi pixels loaded over plain `http://` with no alt text, a duplicated open pixel, and invalid CSS and markup in the head. The QA pass rate is 57%. The header-based warnings can't be confirmed because AgentMail relayed the message and the HTML source was truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure third-party pixels (11 total).** All are `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&...&dtmc_email_category=20261016_VS_6330_FILEBAL`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a4edea10...=1`
- These are Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync/redirect calls. They add third-party requests and a data-sharing surface, and none are loaded securely.
- **Duplicate open pixel.** The same `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?KQZJIG6RE2ZUXPQC2LM5QZ6EH4.70267&d=70267&bmt=0` appears twice:
  - once in the `<div style="font-size:0;line-height:0;">` at the top of the body;
  - once again after the `<title>` block.
  This can double-fire and inflate open counts.
- **Unverified click links.** 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The Dotomi pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`) have none, and neither does `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. The pixels are decorative and should have `alt=""`. The `.png` is a content image and needs descriptive alt text or an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize failure.
- **Invalid or dead CSS in the first `<style>` block:**
  - `ReadMsgBody{...}` is missing its leading `.`, so it never matches.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an element selector for a non-existent `<min-width>` tag.
- **Misplaced `<title>`.** `<title style="display: none; ...">` sits inside `<body>` and is empty. That is invalid placement, and the document has no valid `<head>` title.
- **Leaked template artifact.** The comment `<!-- \`date "+ET%m.%y"\` -->` is a shell-style expression left in the production source. It's harmless but sloppy.
- **Empty `<div id="AMPScript">`.** It wraps empty "Ampscript v0 START/END" comments.
- **Table semantics.** Several layout tables lack `role="presentation"`, for example `table.tb_properties` and the nested `align="left"` tables. Some do have it (`stylingblock-content-wrapper`), so it's inconsistent.
- **Legacy doctype.** HTML 4.01 Transitional is acceptable for email but limits modern CSS support.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `%…%` tokens appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** All three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay, which may strip these headers:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) was not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Not verifiable.** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be checked.
- **Sender alignment.** `From` is `VictoriasSecret@e1.victoriassecret.com` and tracking is on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, so the sending and tracking subdomains are aligned.
- **Privacy.** The Dotomi cookie-sync calls are third-party tracking that may need disclosure, depending on jurisdiction.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The QA data reports no landing-page resolution and the visible source shows no UTM parameters. All 51 links are wrapped in SFMC redirects and were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be verified.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `dtmc_email_category=20261016_VS_6330_FILEBAL`, which is passed to Dotomi, not to the site.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or remove the 11 `http://` calls, which are cookie-sync pixels with no user value in the email.
2. Remove the duplicate `open.aspx` pixel so each send fires one open beacon.
3. Add `alt=""` to the decorative pixels and meaningful alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Fix the CSS (`.ReadMsgBody`, drop `min-width{}`), move or remove the `<title>` from `<body>`, and strip the `date "+ET%m.%y"` comment and the empty `#AMPScript` div.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
7. Re-run the audit against the raw MIME from the original mailbox, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Probe the redirect destinations to check UTMs and landing-page status, and review the full footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
