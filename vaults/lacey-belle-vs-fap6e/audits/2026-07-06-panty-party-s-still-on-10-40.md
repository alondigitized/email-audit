This pixel appears once in the `<body>` pre-header div and again inside `#AMPScript` → inflates open-rate counts for this send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed-content images — 11 total:**
- `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif` (MI pixel)
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` (1 pixel)
- `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1–10.php` (10 pixels)

All will be blocked or proxied by Gmail image proxy (which strips cookies anyway, defeating the retargeting purpose).

**Missing `alt` text — 14 images confirmed by QA:**
- MI tracking gif (`o.gif`)
- Dotomi UCM pixel
- All 10 `ads.dotomi.com/pub*.php` pixels
- `6863ddd4703556dd.png` (appears to be a rendering/spacer image via `mi.victoriassecret.com`)
- `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` (content image via `image.e1.victoriassecret.com`)

The last two are notable — tracking pixels not needing alt is acceptable practice, but a content image (`5bcec3ac...png`) without alt text fails WCAG 2.1 AA and will render as a broken image icon in image-off environments.

**DOCTYPE:** `HTML 4.01 Transitional` — triggers quirks mode in some renderers. Non-blocking but suboptimal; most modern retail senders use `<!DOCTYPE html>`.

**`user-scalable=0` in viewport meta** — disables pinch-zoom on mobile. Accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4).
