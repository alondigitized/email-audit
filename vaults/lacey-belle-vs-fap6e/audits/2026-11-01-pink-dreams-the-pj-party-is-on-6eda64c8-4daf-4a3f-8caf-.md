## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional layout, and its `<head>` is malformed. It also carries about 12 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The automated QA found no hard failures. Compliance headers and authentication results could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains such as `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Redirect chains and destination status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels:** 12 images load over `http://`.
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261101_PK_6421_FULFILE`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, which would silently drop tracking and cookie-sync calls.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** The JSON-LD `url` and the dotomi cookie-redirect URLs carry `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…`. The `pub*.php` URLs also use the hash as a query-parameter name (`?a4edea106b33…=1`). Hashed email and customer ID are being passed to a third-party ad network (dotomi) over unencrypted HTTP.
- **Open pixel:** The 1x1 `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` image is HTTPS and has `alt=""`, so it's fine.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table>` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`. Parsers will close `<head>` early. The charset and viewport declarations then land in `<body>`, where they may be ignored. An empty `<title style="display:none">` also appears inside the body.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Missing alt attributes (14 images):**
  - Tracking pixels with no `alt` attribute at all: `o.gif`, `UCMController`, `pub1.php`–`pub10.php`. They should carry `alt=""`.
  - **Content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt text. This is the one that matters if the image carries a message or CTA. The truncated source doesn't show its role.
- **Layout tables:** Only the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties`, and the nested header tables) don't, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **CSS:** The `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` rule has no leading dot, so it matches nothing. Likewise, `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is an invalid selector. Both are dead rules.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` are visible in the provided source, although the source is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool says this may be a relay capture artifact, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while links and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment can't be checked without the raw headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking scheme:** The email uses a proprietary scheme instead of `utm_*`: `cm_mmc=di-_-110126415-_-VSD_VSL_20261101_PK_6421_FULFILE`, `d_cellid`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp=20261101_PK_6421_FULFILE`. The campaign ID is consistent across the JSON-LD URL and the pixels. This is only a gap if downstream analytics expect `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`.
- **Landing page:** The only destination visible is the JSON-LD `url` (`/us/pink/gifts`). The subject is "PJ Party", so the gifts landing page may not match the subject. This is unverified, because the 50 CTA links weren't probed and the body is truncated.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Move tracking pixels to HTTPS:** Switch `mi.victoriassecret.com` and the dotomi `UCMController` and `pub1`–`pub10` pixels to `https://`, or drop them. Add `alt=""` to all of them.
2. **Fix the document structure:** Move the JSON-LD script and the `<meta>` tags into a valid `<head>`, put the `<title>` there too, and add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
3. **Allow zoom:** Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
4. **Add alt text to the content image:** Do this for `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` if it carries information.
5. **Get the raw headers:** Capture them from a direct send, not the AgentMail relay. Then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e2.vspink.com`.
6. **Probe the CTA destinations:** Follow the 50 skipped click-tracking redirects to their final URLs. Check for 200 status, preserved campaign params, and landing-page relevance to the "PJ Party" theme.
