## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Woke Up & Chose 30% Off PINK 💁‍♀️"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `camarker-inner`) with a heavy third-party tracking footprint: 57 skipped tracking links and 11 `http://` pixels. The QA run found no hard failures, but there are a few markup defects and accessibility gaps. The compliance headers could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing is incomplete.** 57 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination validity is unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (11).** `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, load over plain HTTP.
  - Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, but strict clients may block them or warn about mixed content.
  - Because they are blocked in some clients, tracking and cookie sync will be inconsistent.
- **Third-party cookie sync.** The ten `pub*.php` calls carry the same identifier, `a4edea106b33…=1`. That is the same SHA-256 value as `dtm_email_hash` in the JSON-LD URLs.
- **Identifiers in URLs.** `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` (64-hex), and `mi_u=200492586522` appear in plain query strings. That is a privacy and data-leakage concern if links are forwarded or logged.
- **Same destination for all three cards.** All three JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries point to `.../us/pink/new-arrivals?cm_mmc=...6530_FULLFILE`. Their images differ (`b143ae38…`, `81894145…`, `8efdb262…`), so the three cards aren't differentiated in tracking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** A `<table>` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. Browsers and some clients will auto-close the head early, which can drop the viewport meta and styles in strict parsers. The JSON-LD also needs Gmail schema whitelisting to take effect.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` matches no element. Both are dead rules.
- **Viewport zoom disabled.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing alt text.** The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt text. Other `alt` flags for `o.gif` and `pub1`–`pub10.php` are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while `<meta ... />` uses XHTML self-closing syntax. It is harmless, but inconsistent.
- **Text size.** The mobile style is `font-size: 16px !important`, which is fine. The Outlook MSO block sets `line-height:1`, which may cause cramped text in Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. `customerID`, `dtm_email_hash`, and `mi_u` are all populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. Treat it as a gap in the audit, not a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain `e2.vspink.com` differs from the tracking and image domains (`e1.victoriassecret.com`), so alignment should be checked.
- **Body footer not verifiable.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link or postal address, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer elements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Non-standard tagging.** Campaign tagging uses `cm_mmc=di-_-112326415-_-VSD_VSL_20261123_PK_6530_FULLFILE-_`, `d_cellid`, and `mi_ecmp=20261123_PK_6530_FULLFILE`. There are no `utm_*` parameters, so GA-based attribution depends on the sender's custom mapping.
- **Consistent IDs.** The campaign code `20261123_PK_6530_FULLFILE` is identical across the Dotomi, `mi.` pixel, and link parameters.
- **Landing page unverified.** All links are on tracking domains, so I couldn't confirm that the landing page (`/us/pink/new-arrivals`) matches the 30%-off promise in the subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the Dotomi and `mi.victoriassecret.com` pixels to HTTPS, or remove them. Add `alt=""`, `width`, and `height` to all tracking pixels.
2. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-…png`.
3. Place the JSON-LD `<script>` in `<body>`, not in a `<table>` inside `<head>`. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from the raw message, not the relay copy.
6. Reduce the plaintext-hashed identifiers in URLs and consider server-side lookup tokens.
7. Verify the landing page offer against the subject line with an authenticated click-through, since the HTTP probe was skipped.
