- Subject and preview are backed up literally: "$25 TEES & SHORTS" and "$35 SWEATSHIRTS & SWEATPANTS" are the first two content blocks, in that order.

- No render bugs — text is crisp, images loaded, buttons visible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Still a kitchen-sink megasend — by the time a reader hits the fragrance quiz and rewards banner, the subject's actual promise is six scrolls behind them.
- The camo-capsule hero at the very top isn't priced or CTA'd as clearly as the two blocks below it, so the strongest visual (top-of-email) isn't the strongest offer.

- This is functionally the same template/cadence as 08-24 through 08-28 — a subscriber opening daily will feel the repetition even if today's subject is more literal.

- 3. Make the in-store bra offer either shippable or clearly labeled "in-store only" in bigger type near the top of that block — right now it reads like an online deal until you look closely.
