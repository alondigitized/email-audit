# You're Gonna LOVE Our New Collab 👀🌈
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a collab launch for PINK x Stoney Clover Lane. The subject promises something new, and the hero delivers a branded "PINK x STONEY CLOVER LANE" lockup over pink, glittery product shots. The email is a long scroll with three separate offers: a customizable-patch story ("HEY, BESTIE"), a bonus-points app promo ("EARN 500 BONUS POINTS"), and two gift-with-purchase tiers (a free cozy tote with $100+, a free tote and blanket with $150+). The collab is the best idea here, but the email buries it under promos.
- The hero is strong. After the 7/$35 Panty Party run on Nov 9–11, this is a refreshing change of beat. Even so, the lower half drifts back into the usual PINK catch-all: category tiles, a rewards banner and dense legal fine print.
- Judgement: a good open-driver and a mid-strength click-driver. The collab reads as new and giftable, but the CTAs are generic ("Get Creative", "Shop Now") and the offer stack is confusing.

## What's working

- The hero lockup makes the collab instantly legible. A recognizable co-brand over a pink, textured product scene sets the hierarchy well.
- It breaks the 7/$35 Panty Party pattern from Nov 9–11, which should feel fresh to a subscriber.
- The gift-with-purchase offers are concrete: "FREE COZY TOTE with $100+ purchase" and "FREE COZY TOTE & BLANKET with $150+ purchase." The product shots make the freebie desirable.
- The "HEY, BESTIE" personalization block (patches, charms, "make a gift for your friends") ties the collab to holiday gifting.
- The 500-bonus-points app promo gives members a reason to engage beyond the sale.

## What's weak

- Too many asks stack up: shop the collab, customize, download the app, and two gift tiers. Nothing clearly owns the primary CTA.
- CTA copy is weak. "Shop Now" on both GWP blocks and "Get Creative" are generic verb-plus-nothing buttons.
- The second GWP block largely repeats the first, with a higher threshold. It reads as a repeated block and adds scroll length.
- The footer carries a wall of tiny fine print covering the offer terms and codes. The "free" tote with a $100+ minimum and promo codes feels less honest than the headline suggests.
- The email is about 10,000px tall at full size. The collab hero is at the top, but the price points a shopper cares about don't appear until deep in the scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one job: the collab. Put a single "Shop the Collab" button on the hero, and demote the app promo and the second GWP tier to a tighter strip.
- 2. Merge the two GWP blocks into one tiered module: "Spend $100 = free tote, $150 = tote + blanket," with a single CTA.
- 3. Replace generic CTAs with specific ones, such as "Shop Stoney Clover Lane" and "Claim Your Free Tote."
- 4. Move the key terms (minimums, dates) into a short visible line under the offer instead of only the footer.
- 5. Add a clear deadline or limited-stock cue to the collab, if one is real.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering/personalization defect (an empty greeting slot, `Hi ,`). The source also has invalid head markup, a viewport that blocks zoom, and a dozen third-party `http://` tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers weren't captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed** (QA INFO). The click domain `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` and others were skipped, so redirect chains and final destinations are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels (`http://`):**
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261112_PK_6474_FULLFIL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These can be blocked or rewritten by clients as mixed content, which would silently drop the open and retargeting signals.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** 10 Dotomi `cookieredir/2482/pub1–10.php` pixels fire per open, each carrying `a4edea106b33…=1` (a hashed identifier). This adds third-party requests and a privacy exposure.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** every destination URL, including the JSON-LD `url` fields, carries `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…` and `customerID=43cece97…`. These are SHA-256 hashes of identifiers. They are copied into referrers, browser history and any forwarded email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing `alt` (QA):**
  - The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt text. This is a real accessibility gap.
  - The other flagged images (`o.gif`, `pub1–10.php`, the Dotomi UCM pixel) are tracking pixels. Missing alt there is low impact. The `<img>` inside the leading `font-size:0` div does have `alt=""`.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid `<head>` content:**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` appear inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will close the head early, so the `<meta>` and `<style>` blocks may end up in the body.
  - The `<meta>` and `<style>` tags then follow the table.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are no-ops.
- **Presentation roles and language:** only the wrapper table has `role="presentation"`. The visible `<table class="container">` and the inner layout tables have none in the captured source. The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. The source is truncated, so this is partial evidence.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** the greeting renders as `Hi ,` with an empty name field. The merge field resolved to an empty string with no fallback, so recipients with no first name see a malformed greeting.
- No raw unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were observed in the captured source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed absent**. Check the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain (`e2.vspink.com`) differs from the link and image domains (`*.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Alignment cannot be confirmed from the data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit in the footer, which is cut off by the truncated source. No determination is possible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Landing page:** all three JSON-LD cards point to `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/collabs/stoney-clover-lane`. This matches the subject ("New Collab").
- **Tracking parameters:** the URLs use the vendor's `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid=111226415`, `mi_u` and `mi_ecmp=20261112_PK_6474_FULLFIL`. There are no `utm_*` parameters, so GA-style attribution depends on the `cm_mmc` mapping. That is consistent with the platform's own scheme, but it is not a defect.
- **Campaign ID:** it matches across the links and pixels (`20261112_PK_6474_FULLFIL`).
- **Unverified:** the destinations were not probed (49 links skipped), so status codes, redirects and landing-page rendering are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a fallback to the greeting merge (for example `Hi there,`), or suppress the greeting when the name is empty. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Move the `<script type="application/ld+json">` and its wrapper table out of `<head>`, and put the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags first.
3. Fix the two invalid selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, `.min-width`), or remove them.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Add descriptive `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-…png`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Serve the `mi.victoriassecret.com` and Dotomi pixels over HTTPS. Reconsider the 10 cookie-sync pixels.
7. Add `lang` to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to the layout tables.
8. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the raw origin message rather than the relay copy.
9. Re-run the link probe against the destination URLs, for example through a redirect-following resolver for the `click.e1…` links.
