## What's working

- **Rewards module placement** — "Ready to get rewarded? REWARDS" with tier icons and a "JOIN NOW" button is exactly the kind of hook that brings me back. I have a VS card, I watch my points. This speaks to me.
- **Bra model in the hero fragment** — Even though the hero is partially rendered, the visible slice is a woman in a pink lace bra. That's my actual reason for being on this site.
- **Pink palette throughout** — Consistent brand color, feels fresh not dated. The beauty gift module at the bottom right also feels on-theme for spring.
- **"40% OFF VS Beauty Gift Sets" callout** — Even partially cut off, that's a real dollar-off signal that would make me scroll down.

## What's weak

- **Hero rendering is broken** — There is a massive white space on the left side of the viewport where the hero image should be. As a high-tech-comfort Millennial, a broken page makes me question whether to trust the checkout.
- **No bra-specific CTA above the fold** — The only visible CTA is "JOIN NOW" for rewards. I came for a bra — I want "Shop Bras" or "Find My Fit" somewhere obvious.
- **No price or offer on the bra/apparel content** — The beauty promo has "40% off" but the lingerie hero has zero offer copy. That disconnect feels weird.
- **Rewards benefit copy is too small** — The tier icons are cute but the text describing what you actually get is unreadably small on mobile.
- **No navigation bar visible** — I can't see a nav strip or category row, which means I don't know how to get to PINK or Bras in one tap from this viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero image render** — Whatever is causing that white-space split needs a hotfix. Even a solid pink background behind the cropped model would be better than a blank half-page on mobile.
- **Add a bra-fit CTA to the hero fragment** — Overlay "Shop New Bras" or "Find Your Fit" as a button on the visible model image so the hero earns its real estate.
- **Surface free shipping threshold in a sticky banner** — Even just "Free shipping on orders $50+" pinned at the top would give me a reason to keep adding items.
- **Make rewards tier benefits scannable** — Replace the icon row with a quick 3-column summary ("Free Panty | Birthday Gift | Early Access") so I know what I'm joining without squinting.
