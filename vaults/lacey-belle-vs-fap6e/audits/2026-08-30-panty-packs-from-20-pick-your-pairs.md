# Panty Packs from $20—Pick Your Pairs
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This one delivers exactly what the subject promises and then keeps going. "Panty Packs from $20" opens with a clean hero — three panty colorways draped over the models, a concrete price anchor, a "SHOP NOW" button, and honest fine print about exclusions right below the fold. It's a tighter, more disciplined open than 2026-08-27's "Bold Colors" bait-and-switch, and it's on par with 2026-08-28's Body by Victoria send for hero clarity.
- Where it loses points is length and thread-following: after the panty hero, it pivots hard into "Luxe Leopard Prints" (a different product, different message), then into the Nood adhesive bra brand boutique, then a full nine-category grid, a Rewards/SMS/credit-card block, and a "Now Trending" carousel. That's four distinct campaigns stapled into one email. Panty Packs is a legitimate, well-executed hero — but the subscriber has to scroll through a leopard-print teaser and a shapewear-brand launch to get back to anything panty-related, which dilutes the specific offer that got them to open.
- Solid single-offer hero, undermined by scope creep below the fold. Worth sending, but the back half should have been its own campaign.

## What's working

- Hero image directly matches subject: three panty pairs in fall colorways with a "$20" price front and center.
- CTA is a real black "SHOP NOW" button, high contrast, unambiguous.
- Fine print ("up to $40, select styles, excludes third-party styles and clearance") is visible and not buried.
- "Limited Time" banner sets credible urgency without shouting.
- Membership/loyalty and credit card offer ($25 off first purchase) are surfaced lower in the email for engaged scrollers.

## What's weak

- Subject is about panty packs, but the second content block ("Luxe Leopard Prints") is a completely different product story — bras/lingerie, not panties — breaking the promise before the reader can act on the hero.
- The Nood "Brand Boutique" section is a third unrelated campaign (adhesive/strapless bras), pushing the actual panty offer further from memory.
- Email is very long for a single-offer subject — nine-category grid plus "Now Trending" carousel reads like a catalog dump appended to a promo.
- No second, reinforcing panty CTA anywhere after the hero — once the reader scrolls past the top, the $20 offer is never mentioned again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the panty hero + one supporting panty/loungewear cross-sell block; move Leopard Prints and the Nood launch to their own sends — this is the single biggest lift since it stops competing subjects from diluting the $20 offer.
- 2. Add a second "Shop Panty Packs" CTA further down (e.g. right before the category grid) so scrollers who skipped the hero still get a way back to the offer.
- 3. Replace the generic "Online only. Orig. up to $40..." fine print styling with slightly more visual separation so it doesn't look like legal boilerplate blending into the page background.
- 4. Trim the nine-category grid to categories relevant to the panty/loungewear offer, or move the full grid to a footer-nav role rather than mid-email real estate.
