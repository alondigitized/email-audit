# 30% Off is Happening NOW—Don't Miss Out
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-duplicate of the 11/18 send ("30% Off Everything Starts NOW"). The hero is a pink banner with two models in pajamas under "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH". Below it is "LIMITED TIME 30% Off Everything" and a category grid (Sleep, Bra, Beauty, Panties, Lingerie, Activewear, Shop All). The offer is clear and honest up top. It then turns into a long kitchen-sink scroll.
- The email stacks 7/$35 Panty Party, a "Last Day" BOGO 50% beauty gift set, 5/$40 Mists & Lotions, a free quilted duffle with $120+ purchase, a "30% Off All Your Favorites" grid, a rewards banner, a "Trending" strip and a wall of fine print. After the hero, nothing owns the page. Layered offers dilute the 30% message, and the fine print runs very long.
- Verdict: solid brand and clear lead offer, but this is the second 30% send in two days and the body is cluttered. I can't see any rendering bugs in the image. The weakness is repetition and offer overload.

## What's working

- "30% Off Everything" is large and sits under a "LIMITED TIME" tag, so the offer is readable at once.
- The category buttons right under the hero give shoppers a quick way into their section.
- Urgency is layered: "SHOP NOW to BEAT the RUSH" and "LAST DAY" on the beauty BOGO.
- The product photography is strong and on-brand, and the free duffle offer adds a clear incentive.
- The rewards and $25 off first-purchase module is visible near the bottom.

## What's weak

- This is a near-repeat of the 11/18 "30% Off Everything Starts NOW", so subscribers will feel fatigue.
- Seven or more competing offers (30%, 7/$35, BOGO 50%, 5/$40, free duffle, $25 off, rewards) blur the focus.
- The email is very long, and the middle sections push the 30% message out of view.
- The exclusions note under the hero ("No code required. Excludes products subject to other promotions…") and the dense legal copy at the bottom undercut the "Everything" claim.
- The "30% Off All Your Favorites" grid repeats the category buttons from the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the 30% hero, one secondary offer (the duffle, as a gift-with-purchase hook), and the category grid. Drop the repeated 30% grid at the bottom.
- 2. Differentiate from 11/18. Lead with the free duffle, or use a real countdown, so it doesn't read as a resend.
- 3. Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Pajamas 30% Off", and move the exclusions closer to the offer in plain language.
- 4. Put a single hero per category with a product-level link instead of the category menu.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% Off Ends Soon — Pajamas, Bras & More`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 7/$35 panties and a free quilted duffle with $120+. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sleep, lingerie, beauty and more — 30% off sitewide while it lasts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with correctly aligned sending and tracking subdomains (`e1.victoriassecret.com`, `click.e1`, `image.e1`). Its source has structural HTML and CSS defects, and it loads ten plaintext-HTTP third-party pixels that carry a hashed email identifier. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified from the supplied data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were never probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page parity are unverified.
- **Ten Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`. Clients that enforce HTTPS-only images may block them, and some will show broken-image placeholders.
- **A hashed email travels in cleartext.** Each `pubN.php` URL carries `?a4edea106b333e696054ee7efded08d13b357adf450709a7b066a2e459aafc6fe=1`. That value is the same `dtm_email_hash` used elsewhere in the email, so a SHA-256 email identifier is sent over unencrypted HTTP to a third party.
- **Open pixel looks fine.** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is 1×1, uses `alt=""`, and sits in a `font-size:0` wrapper.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`. That is invalid markup and is likely to be foster-parented into `<body>` by parsers.
  - `<title style="display:none…">` appears inside `<body>`, empty.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional.
- **JSON-LD `PromotionCard` is of little use.** Gmail annotations require a registered sender, and most other clients strip `<script>`.
- **Malformed CSS selectors in the first `<style>` block.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{` is missing its leading `.`.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector, so the rule is dead.
  - `max-height:auto` is not a valid value.
  - The global `table{max-width:600px!important;}` and `table td{max-width:600px!important;}` can clip nested tables.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom.** `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` prevents pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. It is also placed after the body-level markup rather than properly in `<head>`.
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Two images, `…/m/1/a514db59-fe67-4f07-8a48-9250d1598b0a.jpg` and `…/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, have no `alt` attribute. If they are linked or carry the offer, the message is unavailable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on the Dotomi pixels.** The 11 pixel `<img>` tags have no `alt`. This is lower severity, but they should be `alt=""`.
- **Outlook fix is questionable.** The MSO block sets `line-height:1` and `color:#808080` on `body, table, td`. That can collapse line spacing and lower contrast in Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear. The personalization parameters (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`, `mi_u`) are populated.

The HTML was truncated, so tokens in the body and footer can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverifiable, not confirmed defects.** The QA run reported `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` as not found. The report itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay, so I can't confirm whether they are present on the original message. For a bulk sender at this scale, the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers directly.
- **Footer not in the sample.** The physical address and visible unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section, so they are unverified.
- **Sender alignment looks good.** The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` matches the click and image subdomains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Non-standard tracking only.** The only visible destination is the JSON-LD URL `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/newarrivals?cm_mmc=di-_-111926525-_-VSD_VSL_20261119_VS_6502_FILEBAL-_&d_cellid=111926525&…&mi_ecmp=20261119_VS_6502_FILEBAL`. It uses Coremetrics (`cm_mmc`), `d_cellid` and `mi_ecmp` parameters, with no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Campaign ID `20261119_VS_6502_FILEBAL` is consistent across `cm_mmc` and `mi_ecmp`.
- **Landing page may not match the offer.** The subject advertises "30% Off", but this URL points to `/newarrivals`. Because the primary CTAs are click-wrapped and unprobed, I can't confirm where they actually land.
- **Personal identifiers in a plain URL.** Hashed email and customer IDs are appended to the destination URL. They will surface in referrer logs, analytics and browser history.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop the ten cookie-sync pixels. Stop passing the hashed email in plaintext query strings.
2. Add `alt` text to the two content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block and its wrapper table out of `<head>`, or remove the block unless Gmail Promotions Annotations is registered.
5. Fix the CSS: change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, delete the `min-width{}` rule and `max-height:auto`, and scope the global `table` and `table td` `max-width` rules to the container.
6. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) headers on the raw message, not the relay copy. Also confirm the footer address and unsubscribe link.
7. Rerun the link probe against the resolved click-through destinations to confirm status codes and that the 30%-off CTA lands on the matching page.
