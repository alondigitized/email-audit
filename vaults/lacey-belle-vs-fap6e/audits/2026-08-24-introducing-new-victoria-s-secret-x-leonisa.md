# Introducing NEW Victoria's Secret x Leonisa
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is an actual new-product launch, not another lap around the Bra Event carousel — the hero leads with "Victoria's Secret x Leonisa," a genuine collaboration announcement with a nude bodysuit and clear "Shapewear technology meets a signature look" positioning statement. That's a real point of differentiation after a week of near-identical bra sends (8/18 through 8/23 all scored 6.00-7.00 running the same bra-carousel template). Unfortunately, after the strong collab hero and three clean shapewear product shots, the email collapses right back into that exact template — the Bra Event, Make It a Match, 5/$45 Panties, 25% Off Eau de Parfum, tease fragrance — all stacked below the fold with no further mention of Leonisa.
- The top third is genuinely well done: one collab hero, one clear "Shop Now," then three focused product cards (bodysuit, high-waist short, legging) each with a short bullet feature. That's disciplined for this brand. But the email is doing two jobs at once — new launch announcement, then kitchen-sink weekly promo — and the second job outweighs the first in sheer length. A subscriber who opens for the Leonisa news scrolls past it in 15 seconds and lands in the same "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" / "5/$45" / "25% Off" stack seen four times this week.
- Verdict: good hero, undermined by an email that can't resist tacking on the entire weekly offer ladder. This should have been a standalone launch email or led much harder into Leonisa-specific cross-sell before pivoting to bras/panties/fragrance.

## What's working

- Clear, on-brand collab lockup ("VICTORIA'S SECRET x LEONISA") with a real explanatory subhead, not just a product shot.
- Three shapewear product tiles each carry a distinct benefit callout (smoothing, sculpting, high-waist) — more informative than the brand's usual bra sends.
- No render bugs — clean images, no overlapping text, no broken tiles.
- "LIMITED TIME" badges are used consistently and legibly across the Bra Event, panties, and EDP sections.

## What's weak

- The Leonisa story ends after three product tiles (roughly top 25% of the email) and is never referenced again — no styling tips, no "wear it under this," no additional SKUs.
- This is the fifth bra-forward promo stack this week (8/18–8/23 all scored 6-7 with the same beats); "Bra Event," "5/$45 Panties," and "25% Off EDP" are copy/paste from prior sends.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled by length — a launch email shouldn't be scroll-competing with four unrelated promos for attention.
- No loyalty/Rewards messaging until the very bottom footer strip, easy to miss if the reader doesn't scroll that far.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half: end after the Leonisa shapewear section (or add one more Leonisa-specific module — "Shop the Full Collection") and send the Bra Event/panties/fragrance content as a separate, focused send. Trying to be a launch email and a weekly circular in one send dilutes both.
- 2. If the promo stack must stay, re-order so Leonisa gets a second placement lower in the email (e.g., "Also New" recap card) instead of vanishing after three tiles.
- 3. Move Rewards/loyalty messaging up near the top or right after the hero — right now it's buried at the very bottom.
- 4. Tighten subject to promise what's actually being delivered up top, since most of the email content is unrelated to Leonisa.
