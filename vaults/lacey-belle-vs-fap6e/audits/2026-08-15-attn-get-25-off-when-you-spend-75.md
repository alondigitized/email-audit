# Attn: Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the kitchen-sink template again, but the hero at least matches the subject: "Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" sits right under a "Limited Time" tag with a clean SHOP NOW button. It's the same structure the brand has run at 6-7/10 all week — hero offer, then six-plus bra-style modules, a whipped-mists-and-lotions push, a $25 rewards teaser, category grid, and social footer. Nothing here is broken, but nothing distinguishes it from the 08-13 or 08-10 sends either, just a swapped percentage and a spend threshold instead of a flat markdown.
- The subject line is also a problem before the email even opens: "Attn: Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" — leading with "Attn:" is a dated, spam-adjacent trick that this brand hasn't used in the prior five sends I've seen, and it undercuts an otherwise decent, concrete offer.
- Judgement: a mid-pack send, on par with 08-13 and 08-14, held back by subject-line noise and a $75 spend threshold buried where a shopper won't see it until they're already in checkout math.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the exact subject-line offer immediately: "Get 25% Off When You Spend $75+" in large type with a "Limited Time" flag.
- Single unambiguous CTA button ("SHOP NOW") directly under the hero offer, no scavenger hunt.
- "World's #1 Bra Brand" trust line plus a clean model shot gives the hero visual hierarchy — eye lands on offer text first, then face/product.
- Product modules (Very Sexy, Dream Angels, Body by VS, Signature, Invisible, VSX) are well-labeled with individual "SHOP NOW" CTAs, so category-specific intent is easy to act on.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent with the brand's known template.

## What's weak

- Subject line opens with "Attn:" — reads like a spam trick, not a Victoria's Secret voice; nothing in the brand history shows this pattern before.
- The $75+ spend threshold is the actual condition of the offer but is set in noticeably smaller type than "25% Off" — someone scanning fast will think it's an unconditional 25% off.
- This is at minimum the fourth percent-off/member-exclusive promo email this week (08-08, 08-10, 08-12, 08-13, 08-14) — cadence fatigue is real and this send does nothing to differentiate itself creatively.
- Six-plus near-identical bra-style modules stacked below the fold dilute the hero offer rather than reinforcing it — by the time a reader reaches "Whipped Mists & Lotions" the original 25%-off hook has faded.
- No urgency deadline visible anywhere in the hero (just "Limited Time" with no date/countdown), which is weaker than the countdown clock used in the 08-12 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop "Attn:" from the subject entirely — it's the single biggest drag on open rate this week; lead with the concrete offer instead.
- 2. Make the "$75+" spend threshold the same visual weight as "25% Off" in the hero — right now it reads as fine print on a headline offer.
- 3. Add a real countdown or explicit end date/time to the hero to create credible urgency, matching what worked in the 08-12 "One Day Only" send.
- 4. Trim the bra-style module stack from six to three — let the hero offer breathe instead of drowning it in near-duplicate product blocks.
- 5. Rotate off percent-off framing for at least one send this week — the brand has run 20%, 25%, 30%, 25% offers back to back; a non-discount hook (new arrivals, loyalty perk) would break the fatigue pattern.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Bra Upgrade Just Got 25% Cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, save 25% — new Very Sexy and Dream Angels styles are in.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25% off ends tonight. Shop bras, lounge, and new whipped lotions.`

- Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually present at the SMTP/header level (outside this HTML-only capture) to verify RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
