# Go On, Glisten Up in NEW Bras
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely well-built product email — "Go On, Glisten Up" delivers exactly what it promises: a shimmery new "Balconette" bra from "The Wicked Collection," styled with a moody purple/wine palette and a clean hero shot right up top. Unlike the last few Victoria's Secret sends I've seen (the repetitive "10/$40 Panty Party" run from 09-03 through 09-08), this one leads with new-product energy rather than recycled discount copy — a nice change of pace.
- But it doesn't stop at one offer, and that's the problem. After the bra hero, it stacks "The Bra Event" (Buy 2, Get 1 Free), then "5/$45 Luxe Panties," then a "Free Cinched Tote" GWP, then a rewards/new-trending footer. Four distinct offers in one email dilutes the "NEW" story the subject line sold me on — by the time I hit the tote GWP, I've forgotten this was supposed to be about new bras.
- Solid, on-brand execution, but it's an everything-bagel email dressed up as a launch email. Judgment: 6.5/10 — good creative, unfocused offer strategy.

## What's working

- The hero delivers on the subject immediately — "NEW FROM THE WICKED COLLECTION" and "Go On, GLISTEN UP" sit right at the top with a clear "SHOP BALCONETTE BRAS" button.
- Clean, moody visual identity (deep purple/wine tones, shimmer fabric) that feels distinct from VS's usual pink-heavy palette — reads as a real "new collection" moment, not a repaint of an old promo.
- The Bra Event section ("Buy 2, Get 1 Free") is a genuinely strong, clearly stated offer with visible product swatches in multiple colors.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, buttons are legible, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the scroll.
- Footer nav (rewards, new arrivals icons, "New Trending" carousel) is tidy and gives an easy secondary path if the hero doesn't land.

## What's weak

- Too many competing offers: new bra launch, Buy2Get1 bras, 5/$45 panties, and a free tote GWP all in one email — no single clear "the" offer.
- The $25 rewards banner near the bottom is visually flat and easy to miss/scroll past — feels like an afterthought bolted onto a launch email.
- "Free Cinched Tote" section uses a stock product shot on a plain background that looks noticeably cheaper than the moody photography used earlier — a visual quality dip mid-scroll.
- Fine print for the tote GWP ("$450 value... Excludes clearance... Priced as marked") is the kind of qualifier-heavy language the rubric flags — not a clean, honest freebie.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one offer per email. Split into two sends: "New Wicked Collection Balconette" (pure launch, no other CTAs) and a separate "Buy 2 Get 1 + Panties + Tote GWP" bundle email. This single change would lift Business Impact and Click-Through the most — right now the hero's momentum gets diluted by three more offers stacked below it.
- 2. Move the Buy2Get1 event higher or drop it — it's the strongest, clearest offer in the whole email and it's buried in section three.
- 3. Reshoot or restyle the tote GWP tile to match the moody photography used for the bras — the flat product-on-white shot breaks visual consistency.
- 4. Simplify the tote's fine print to one line ("with $100+ purchase") instead of stacking value/exclusion/pricing caveats.
