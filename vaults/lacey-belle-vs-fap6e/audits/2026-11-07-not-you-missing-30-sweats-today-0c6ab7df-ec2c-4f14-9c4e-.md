# Not You Missing $30 Sweats Today 🙂‍↔️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same PINK Friendsgiving catch-all the brand has sent all week, with a new subject on top. The subject promises $30 sweats, and the hero delivers. It shows a model in cream sweats, a "DEAL OF THE DAY" badge, "$30 Sweatshirts & Sweatpants" in bold white on hot pink, and a "Shop Now" button. That matches yesterday's "Your Dream Sweats Are $30 Today 👀" almost exactly.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a long stack of unrelated offers: 7/$35 panties, a free mini with purchase, bras from $25, $25 pajama pants, $25 tees, $25 eau de parfum and $8 lip gloss with beauty minis. Every block is clear, but nothing ranks above anything else, so the eye has no reason to prefer the sweats. I could only view the render at about 113px wide, so I read headlines, prices and layout, not the fine print.
- My judgement: the top is strong and the middle is generic. The "Not You Missing…" subject is a guilt-trip line on the same deal the brand sent yesterday. A subscriber would read it as a third repeat of the sweats offer in a week.

## What's working

- The hero matches the subject: the $30 sweats offer sits in the first block, with a clear badge and button.
- The "DEAL OF THE DAY" badge adds credible urgency.
- Hot pink with large white price headlines makes every offer easy to scan.
- Each block has one price and one "Shop Now" button, so the layout is predictable.
- Prices are concrete throughout ($25 bras, $25 tees, 7/$35 panties).

## What's weak

- The email is a very long catch-all with about nine offer blocks, and the sweats deal gets no more weight than the rest.
- The "Limited-edition, bestie-approved" block and the product-thumbnail grid add clutter and no clear price or CTA at this size.
- The sweats offer repeats yesterday's "$30 Sweatshirts & Sweatpants" send. The only change is a guilt-trip subject line.
- Nearly every block carries a "LIMITED TIME" tag, which dilutes the real urgency of the deal of the day.
- The disclaimer text under each block is tiny and hard to read, so conditions like the "free mini with purchase" qualifier feel buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus two or three supporting offers, and move the rest to a "more deals" link. Right now the email works against its own subject.
- 2. Add something new to the sweats block, such as colors, sizes, a "sold out last time" signal or a countdown. That would justify a second send of the same offer.
- 3. Use "LIMITED TIME" only on the real deal of the day and give the other blocks distinct labels.
- 4. Make the fine print readable and put the free-gift condition next to the headline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source and QA data show no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The confirmed problems are invalid head markup, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, many insecure `http://` tracking pixels, and a missing `alt` on one content image. The 59 tracked links and the compliance/auth headers could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified redirects:** 59 links on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their final destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixels over `http://`** (QA-flagged, may be blocked or cause mixed-content warnings):
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=200492586522&mi_ecmp=20261106_PK_6437_TRIPLE`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie-sync load:** Ten Dotomi `pubN.php` pixels plus `UCMController` add 11 extra third-party requests. Any that fail or are blocked are silent.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** The JSON-LD block and links carry `dtm_email_hash=4edea106…c6fe`, `customerID=43cece97…1e2f` and `mi_u=200492586522`. These are persistent per-recipient identifiers. They also appear in the `ads.dotomi.com` pixel query strings (`pub1.php?a4edea106…=1`), so they are sent to a third party on image load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure:** A `<table>` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before `<meta name="viewport">` and the `<style>` blocks. Parsers implicitly close `<head>` at the table, so the viewport meta and both `<style>` blocks may be moved into `<body>` or ignored. The responsive `@media` rules may then not apply in some clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.` or `#`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both rules are inert.
- **Missing alt text:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` is a hosted image, so it is likely content, and it has no `alt`. The other flagged images (`o.gif`, `UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`) are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` for clean screen-reader output. The `open.aspx` pixel already has `alt=""`.
- **Legacy doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used, with `<meta>` tags outside `<head>`. This is not breaking but is non-standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[…]` tokens appeared. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Domain alignment:** The From domain is `e2.vspink.com`, while links and images use `e1.victoriassecret.com`. Alignment can't be verified without headers.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated portion, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No standard UTMs:** The visible URLs use the proprietary `cm_mmc`, `d_cellid`, `mi_ecmp` and `dtm_*` parameters instead of `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`. The campaign ID `20261106_PK_6437_TRIPLE` is present and consistent across the JSON-LD URLs, `o.gif` and the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category`. Analytics that rely on UTMs would not attribute this traffic.
- **Landing page:** The only destination confirmed in the source is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/new-arrivals` (both JSON-LD cards). Both cards point to the same URL. The 59 body links are unprobed, so their landing alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD table and script into `<body>`, and keep `<meta>` and `<style>` inside `<head>`.
2. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1`.
3. Switch the `mi.victoriassecret.com` and `*.dotomi.com` pixels to `https://`, or drop them if the endpoints don't support it.
4. Add `alt` text to `5bcec3ac-…png` and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Fix or remove the `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` CSS rules.
6. Check the raw message headers (outside the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
7. Probe a sample of the 59 `click.e1` links with a redirect-following check, to confirm destinations and that none dead-end.
8. Confirm whether `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` in URLs and third-party pixels match the sender's privacy disclosures.
