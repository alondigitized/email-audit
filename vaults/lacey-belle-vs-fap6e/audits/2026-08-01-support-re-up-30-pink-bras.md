# Support Re-Up: $30 PINK Bras
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same $30 PINK Bras hero the brand ran on July 30 ("We Support You: $30 PINK Bras," 8.00/10) — same "TOP-DRAWER TAKEOVER" bra grid, same pink polka-dot "LAST DAY / $30 PINK BRAS" banner, same "SHOP NOW" button — just re-sent two days later under a new subject line. Calling it "Support Re-Up" is at least honest framing, but reusing "LAST DAY" urgency for a second time in three days quietly tells a subscriber who opened both that the deadline wasn't real the first time.
- Below the fold this reverts to the brand's default kitchen-sink structure: $35 sweats, 5/$35 panties, a denim "crowd goes blue" push, a PINK Friday app-exclusive teaser, a sitewide $25-off-$125 code, a rewards module, and a social proof grid — six-plus distinct campaigns in one send, which dilutes the single clean offer the subject promised.
- Net: strong subject/hero match (a real strength this brand has struggled with recently per the July 28 and July 31 misses), undercut by a stale repeat and a visibly broken teaser image. Solid, not great — 7/10 territory.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are in sync for once — "$30 PINK Bras" is the first thing you see, no bait-and-switch like the July 28 or July 31 sends.
- Clean top-of-funnel visual hierarchy: bra grid → price → CTA, all above the fold.
- Multiple concrete, specific offers (not vague "shop the sale" language): $30 bras, $35 sweats, 5/$35 panties, $25 off $125+.
- Rewards/loyalty module is present and visible near the bottom, with a clear join CTA.
- Social proof grid ("all over your feed") at the very bottom reinforces trust.

## What's weak

- This is a near-identical rerun of the July 30 "We Support You: $30 PINK Bras" hero — same art, same "LAST DAY" claim, sent 48 hours later. That's a credibility problem, not just a cadence one.
- The PINK Friday "REVEAL #1" teaser image is visibly pixelated/blurred to the point it reads as a broken or low-res asset rather than an intentional reveal mechanic.
- Six-plus unrelated campaigns stacked in one email (bras, sweats, panties, denim, app teaser, sitewide code, rewards, UGC) — the clean single-offer subject line doesn't match the sprawling body.
- Fine print exclusions ("Excludes Marshmallow Bras," "select styles") sit right under the sweatshirt and panty offers, which chips away at the "honest offer" read.
- Generic "SHOP NOW" CTA copy repeated on nearly every module — no differentiation by category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the "LAST DAY" banner for this send — reusing an already-expired deadline is the single biggest trust-killer here. Either let the bra offer stand on price alone or use a genuinely new deadline.
- 2. Fix or replace the pixelated PINK Friday teaser image — a blurry circle undercuts a "collectible" hype mechanic instead of building it.
- 3. Cut the email down to 2-3 modules (bras, panties, sitewide code) and move sweats/denim/app-teaser to a follow-up send — this protects the clean subject-to-hero match instead of burying it.
- 4. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTA copy ("Shop $30 Bras," "Grab 5 for $35").
