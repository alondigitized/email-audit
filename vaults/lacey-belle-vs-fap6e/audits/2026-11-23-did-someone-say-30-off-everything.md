# Did Someone Say 30% Off Everything? 🙂‍↕️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth near-identical PINK Black Friday catalog in six days. It opens on a "SHOP NOW TO BEAT THE RUSH" banner, then a "LIMITED TIME 30% OFF ALL SLEEP" block. The headline "30% OFF EVERYTHING" doesn't appear until the second module, and the subject line promises it first. After that the email stacks four more offers: bras from $25 (marked "LAST DAY"), a Marshmallow bra and beauty pairing, a 7/$35 panty party, and a category grid.
- The offer is clear, and the creative is bold, saturated pink and red with strong seasonal energy. But the email is too long, and the lead module (sleep) contradicts the subject line. A recipient who read "Did Someone Say 30% Off Everything?" and sees "ALL SLEEP" first has to scroll to find the promised offer. The "LAST DAY" tag on bras is the only credible urgency.
- Verdict: competent but fatigued. It's the same 30% promo repeated daily with a new emoji in the subject. It works as a reminder but does nothing to separate itself from the previous five sends.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF EVERYTHING" hero is huge, high-contrast and unmistakable once you reach it.
- "LAST DAY" on Bras from $25 is the only real time pressure, and it's believable.
- The 7/$35 panty party gives a concrete multi-buy deal with its own CTA.
- The category grid and "SHOP NOW" buttons make it easy to jump to a product area.
- The pink/red holiday styling with candy-cane and gift props fits the season and the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The first module is 30% off sleep, not everything, so the subject and the lead creative don't match.
- The email is very long, with at least six offer modules, which dilutes the hero.
- Fine print under each block is dense, and the legal footer is a wall of small text.
- It repeats the brand's 30% message from Nov 18–23, so it reads as the Nov 23 "Woke Up & Chose 30% Off PINK" send again.
- The "Did Someone Say…" subject is a generic meme-style opener with no product or deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "30% OFF EVERYTHING" in the first module and move the sleep block below it, so the hero matches the subject.
- 2. Cut the email to three modules: everything, bras from $25 (last day), and 7/$35 panties. Drop the repeated banner and the duplicate category grid.
- 3. Lead with the deadline. Today's "LAST DAY" is a better hook than a meme opener.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day, or segment by category affinity (bras vs. sleep), so subscribers don't see the same send.
- 5. Move the fine print into a collapsed "details" link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off everything, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bras from $25 end today. Plus 7/$35 panties and free pickup.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's wrapper code is malformed. It puts block elements in `<head>`, uses invalid CSS selectors, and sets a viewport that blocks zoom. It also embeds about 12 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The 3 automated warnings are about missing headers and can't be confirmed from the source. The pass rate was 57%, and the QA run found 0 hard issues.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were not probed.** The click-redirect domains were skipped (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com` style). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels.** 12 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://mi.victoriassecret.com/p/up/bc3d99b3ec2e075f/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261123_PK_6531_FILEBAL`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Clients may block these, so they could fail silently. They also send the hashed email in cleartext over HTTP, in the form `?a4edea106b33…c6fe=1`. This is the same value as `dtm_email_hash` in the JSON-LD URL.
- **Cookie-sync fan-out.** The 10 sequential Dotomi `pub1–pub10.php` pixels add third-party requests that slow rendering and may be blocked by privacy proxies.
- **Primary open pixel is fine.** `click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?...` is HTTPS, 1x1, with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid document structure.** The first thing inside `<head>` is a `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">`, followed by `<meta>` and `<style>`. Tables are not allowed in `<head>`. Parsers will close `<head>` early, so the viewport meta and the first `<style>` block land in the body.
- **Empty title in body.** `<title style="display: none; …"></title>` sits inside a body table.
- **Invalid CSS selectors.**
  - `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it matches a nonexistent element.
  - `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also not a valid selector for its intent.
  - `max-height:auto !important` is an invalid value.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0`. This blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing alt text, flagged by QA.**
  - The content image `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt attribute.
  - The tracking pixels `o.gif`, the Dotomi `UCMController` pixel, and `pub1–pub10.php` also lack alt. They are invisible, so this is lower priority, but `alt=""` is still advisable.
- **Emoji in subject.** The subject uses 🙂‍↕️ (head shaking vertically, Unicode 15.1, a ZWJ sequence). Older clients may render it as two glyphs, a face plus an arrow.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied excerpt. The source is truncated, so the rest is unverified.
- Recipient identifiers are resolved and embedded in URLs: `dtm_email_hash`, `customerID` (a 64-hex hash), `mi_u=200492586522`. They are populated and not broken.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes they may have been stripped by the AgentMail relay. This can't be confirmed from the source. If the sender omits them, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements would be at risk.
- **Authentication-Results is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the likely cause. The From domain `e2.vspink.com` does not match the image or click domains (`e1.victoriassecret.com`). This isn't a defect on its own, but it should be checked against the DKIM `d=` value.
- The visible footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Campaign attribution uses `cm_mmc=di-_-112326425-_-VSD_VSL_20261123_PK_6531_FILEBAL`, `d_cellid`, and `mi_ecmp=20261123_PK_6531_FILEBAL`. These are consistent with each other, but a GA-style UTM-based setup would not pick them up.
- The JSON-LD `url` points to `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/pink/gifts`. The `/pink/gifts` landing page was not probed because tracking links were skipped, so alignment with the "30% Off Everything" subject is unverified.
- The `PromotionCard` JSON-LD sits inside the HTML body wrapper. Its URL carries per-recipient hashes (`dtm_email_hash`, `customerID`), which makes the structured data user-specific.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD wrapper table out of `<head>`. Put `<meta>` and `<style>` directly in `<head>` and the title in `<head>`.
2. Serve all pixels over HTTPS, or drop the Dotomi `pub1–pub10` sync chain. At a minimum, stop sending the hashed email in `http://` URLs.
3. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`) and remove `max-height:auto`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
5. Add `alt` to `5bcec3ac-….png` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
6. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have stripped them. Also confirm the in-body unsubscribe link.
7. Re-run link probing against the final redirect destinations, including `/us/pink/gifts`, to check status and landing alignment.
