# 40% Off Everything—Our Thanks to You
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Victoria's Secret's fourth Black Friday-week send in about 72 hours, and the first one to switch to a gratitude angle. The body is the usual kitchen-sink template. A black "BLACK FRIDAY" banner sits over a hero reading "40% Off All Sleep & Lingerie." Below it come a 7/$35 Panty Party, $35 pajama sets, a second "40% Off Everything" block, a free tote, a free tote and blanket, and free shipping on $50+. The hierarchy is clean, and each block has a bold offer and a button.
- The subject promises thanks, but the body doesn't deliver it. Nothing in the render reads as a thank-you, such as a bonus, a surprise or early access. It's the same 40% as 11/24's "40% Off Everything—Black Friday is Finally HERE" and 11/25's "Black Friday = 40% Off EVERYTHING." A subscriber who has seen those three has little reason to open this one.
- Verdict: competent and repetitive. The creative is fine, but the send adds no new news and the subject's promise goes unfulfilled.
- (Caveat: the screenshot is very long and was shown at a small size, so I'm reading layout and large headlines, not fine print.)

## What's working

- The hero states a specific offer, "40% Off All Sleep & Lingerie," with a clear "Limited Time" label and two buttons (Shop Sleep, Shop Lingerie).
- Each block has one bold headline, one offer and one CTA, so the long scroll stays easy to skim.
- The black and white typography with photography keeps the brand look consistent with the earlier Black Friday sends.
- Value is layered. A percentage-off block, a price-point block ($35 pajama sets, 7/$35 panties) and free gifts at a threshold give several reasons to click.
- The Rewards block near the bottom shows the $25-off member perk.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Our Thanks to You" is never paid off in the body. There's no thank-you moment and no exclusive.
- The email is extremely long, with at least seven offer blocks, so no single message wins.
- The offers repeat. "40% Off" shows up in the hero, again as "40% Off Everything," and again as "40% Off All Your Favorites."
- "Limited Time" labels appear everywhere, but no deadline or countdown is visible, so the urgency is generic.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of text, which works against the offer feeling clean and honest.
- Cadence is saturated. This is the third 40% Black Friday email in about two days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the thank-you real. Either add a small gift or bonus for Thanksgiving day, or drop the gratitude framing and lead with the deadline.
- 2. Cut the body to three blocks: the 40% hero, one free-gift block and Rewards. Move the rest to a "More deals" grid.
- 3. Add a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends tonight at midnight," plus a countdown.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 11/24 and 11/25 sends. Spotlight one hero product, such as the $35 pajama set, instead of repeating category buttons.
- 5. Move the Rewards perk up and tie it to the thank-you theme.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 7/$35 panties and $35 pajama sets. Ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally malformed (body content inside `<head>`) and its viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom. It also loads 10 insecure `http://` Dotomi pixels without alt text. 67 tracking links, the compliance headers and SPF/DKIM could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.e1.victoriassecret.com`). Link validity and destinations are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 Dotomi images load over plain `http://`. They may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling in some clients.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - The `pub*.php` URLs carry the same hashed-email query key (`a4edea10…aafc6fe=1`).
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is 1×1 with `alt=""`, which is correct for a pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flagged the 11 images below.
  - The 10 Dotomi pixels (above), which are likely 1×1 tracking images. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`, a content image that needs real alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0" />` blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - A `<table class="stylingblock-content-wrapper">` containing a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sits inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` tags. Parsers will implicitly close `<head>`, so the viewport and charset metas end up in the body.
  - `<title style="display:none…">` is empty and sits inside the body, not the head.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, while the markup uses self-closing `<meta … />` XHTML syntax.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading dot, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Neither rule has any effect.
- **Outlook fallback:** the `mso` block sets `line-height:1` on `body, table, td`, which may cause cramped text in Outlook desktop.
- **JSON-LD (Gmail promo annotation):** the `PromotionCard` script is wrapped in a table inside `<head>`, so Gmail may not parse it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body was not available.
- Recipient identifiers are embedded in URLs: `dtm_email_hash` (SHA-256), `customerID` (SHA-256) and `mi_u=200492586522`. These are hashed rather than plaintext, but they are persistent per-recipient identifiers that are also passed to a third-party ad domain (`ads.dotomi.com`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, the email falls short of Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is not evidence of failure. The From domain (`e1.victoriassecret.com`) is consistent with the click and image subdomains.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameter scheme:** the only visible destination is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/giftguide/shine?cm_mmc=di-_-112626515-_-VSD_VSL_20261126_VS_6552_FULFILE-_&d_cellid=112626515&mi_ecmp=20261126_VS_6552_FULFILE…`. It uses `cm_mmc`/`mi_ecmp` (Coremetrics/Marketing Cloud) and has no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. This is consistent campaign tagging but may not be picked up by GA-style tools.
- **Landing page:** the JSON-LD promo card points to `/giftguide/shine`, while the subject line promises "40% Off Everything". Whether the other 67 links land on a sitewide-offer page could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the JSON-LD block and table into `<body>`, with the `<meta>` tags, `<title>` and `<style>` inside a valid `<head>`. Give the title real text.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta.
3. Serve the Dotomi pixels over `https://`, or drop them. Add `alt=""` to the pixels and a descriptive alt to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Fix or remove the invalid CSS selectors (`ReadMsgBody`, `min-width`).
5. Re-run the QA against raw SMTP headers, not the AgentMail relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve a sample of the 67 tracking links through the redirect to confirm final destinations and that the promo landing matches the "40% Off Everything" subject.
7. Confirm that the full HTML (not just the truncated part) contains an unsubscribe link and a postal address.
