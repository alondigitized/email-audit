# 7/$35 🩲 Two Days to Restock Your Drawer
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is PINK doing another "LAST DAY" panty megasend, and it's the same underlying THE BIG FALL SALE creative system this brand has run all week (2026-09-17, 2026-09-18) — same maroon/pink hero backdrop, same category grid, same footer. The one real change is the hero offer: "7/$35 PANTY PARTY" leads clean and specific with a size-pill selector visible right in the hero, which is a genuine step up from vaguer "up to 50% off" framing. But by the third scroll it's the identical pattern — a $25 bra push, the "THREE WORDS: THIS WON'T LAST / UP TO 50% OFF" banner, and the nine-tile category grid — that's shown up nearly unchanged for days.
- The panty offer itself is legible and single-focus at the top, which should help open/click intent for anyone in-market for panties specifically. But "LAST DAY" urgency is getting worn thin — this brand has used "last day" and "don't forget" framing on multiple sends this week, so a repeat subscriber has good reason to distrust it now.
- Net: solid hero, tired shell. 6/10 territory — better than the 09-16/09-15 sends, not as sharp as 09-13's tiered sweats offer.

## What's working

- Hero offer "7/$35 PANTY PARTY" is a real, specific price point, not a vague percentage.
- Size selector (XS–XXL) visible directly in the hero adds useful pre-click information.
- Single clear "SHOP ALL" CTA button under the offer, high contrast on the pink background.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.
- Secondary $25 bra offer is also concrete and specific.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth+ "LAST DAY" / "don't forget" urgency send in about a week — the credibility of "last day" is shot.
- The "THE BIG FALL SALE... UP TO 50% OFF" banner and category grid are near-identical to the 09-17 and 09-18 sends — zero creative refresh.
- Two competing offers (7/$35 panties, then $25 bras) before you even reach the sale banner dilutes which is the actual hero.
- The nine-tile category grid at the bottom is generic mall-catalog filler, not offer-specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or retire "LAST DAY" language for a few sends — it's been used too often this week and reads as false urgency now; replace with a real countdown or just lead with the offer itself.
- 2. Cut the email to one hero offer (panties) instead of stacking panties → bras → sitewide banner; the multi-offer stacking undercuts the specific, punchy 7/$35 lead.
- 3. Refresh the "BIG FALL SALE" banner creative — same copy/layout three sends running is fatigue risk for a real subscriber.
- 4. Move the size-selector interaction closer to the CTA button so it feels part of the same action.
- **Subject Alt A:** `7/$35 Panties — 2 Days Left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Restock Alert: 7 Panties for $35`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall prints just dropped. Grab 7 for $35 before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes XS–XXL in stock now — panty party ends soon.`
