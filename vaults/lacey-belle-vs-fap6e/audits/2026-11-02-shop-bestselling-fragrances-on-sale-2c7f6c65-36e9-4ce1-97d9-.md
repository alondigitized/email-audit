# Shop Bestselling Fragrances On Sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- The subject and the email don't match. "Shop Bestselling Fragrances On Sale" sells a sale. The hero is a "LAST DAY" Buy One, Get One FREE Eau de Parfum under a "scentevent" banner. That is a better offer than the subject conveys, and it goes unmentioned there. The hero is strong: a bottle lineup, a huge "FREE" and a single "SHOP NOW" button. It also notes "Only up to $6000 each" style fine print directly below.
- Below the hero the email becomes a pajama catalog. It has six modules: Pajama Sets, CozyFlannel, Waffle Knit, Signature Satin, SoSoft Modal and 100% Cotton. None of them are fragrance. A reader who opens for perfume scrolls past roughly 70% of the email without seeing any. This is the same BOGO that ran on 10/28, 10/29 and 11/01 ("NEW Holiday Styles Just Dropped"). By now the offer is a cadence problem, not news.
- Verdict: strong top 15%, weak and mismatched everything else. The subject undersells the offer, and the body drifts off topic.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. A giant "FREE" under "Buy One, Get One" puts the offer first.
- A "LAST DAY" tag adds credible urgency.
- A single black "SHOP NOW" button sits directly under the offer.
- The fragrance bottle lineup is attractive and on-brand.
- The Rewards and $25 Off First Purchase module near the bottom gives some loyalty value.

## What's weak

- The subject says "On Sale" and never mentions BOGO or "Last Day". It is generic and misses the strongest hooks.
- Most of the email is pajamas. The fragrance offer appears once and the rest is off-message.
- The pajama modules are long and repetitive, with six similar "SHOP NOW" links and no prices or offers.
- The fine print is a dense wall of legal text, and the offer exclusions ("up to $6000 each") sit directly under the CTA.
- This is the fourth send on the same fragrance BOGO in a week, so the cadence is fatiguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the real offer and the deadline. "Last Day" is the best hook.
- 2. Cut the pajama section to one module, or move it to a separate send. Add two or three bestselling fragrances with names, prices and a "Buy 1, Get 1" tag.
- 3. Show scent names and review stars under the hero. Right now there is no product detail or social proof.
- 4. Shorten the legal copy and link it out.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier BOGO sends so it doesn't read as a repeat.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.e1.victoriassecret.com`, `stylingblock-content-wrapper`) with 50 click-tracked links. Most of the automated warnings come from 10 plain-HTTP Dotomi cookie-sync pixels and one content image with no alt text. The source was truncated, so the footer and body links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 50 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe (click-redirect domains), so destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 `<img>` tags load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Clients that proxy or upgrade images (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may block or fail them.
  - Some clients flag mixed content.
  - Each `pub*.php` URL carries the hashed email (`?a4edea106b33…=1`) in cleartext over HTTP.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?qs=…` is a 1×1 image with `alt=""`, which is correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on a content image:** `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png` has no alt attribute. If it is meaningful, it will be unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on the Dotomi pixels:** the same issue appears on the 10 tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid document structure:**
  - A `<table>` and `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`.
  - `<title>` appears in the body with `display:none`.
  - Parsers will relocate these, so rendering is probably unaffected, but the markup is non-conformant.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** `ReadMsgBody{…}` is missing its leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Both are no-ops. The valid `.ExternalClass` rules do work.
- **Missing table roles:** only the `stylingblock-content-wrapper` tables have `role="presentation"`. The main layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties`) do not, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Schema markup:** the `PromotionCard` JSON-LD is only honored by Gmail for registered senders. It is harmless otherwise.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The truncated source limits this check.
- The JSON-LD URL carries per-recipient identifiers: `dtm_email_hash` (SHA-256), `customerID` (SHA-256), and `mi_u=200492586522`. They are hashed, but the same hash values are repeated in the plain-HTTP pixel URLs (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Check the raw headers at the original recipient (Gmail "Show original") before acting on it.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is missing, so status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is normal.
- **Footer:** the visible source is truncated before the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking scheme:** links use the SFMC/Monetate/Dotomi convention instead of `utm_*`:
  - `cm_mmc=di-_-110126525-_-VSD_VSL_20261101_VSB_6424_NONBEAU`
  - `d_cellid=110126525`
  - `mi_ecmp=20261101_VSB_6424_NONBEAU`
  - No `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` is present in the visible links. This is only a problem if the site's analytics expects UTMs.
- **Landing page:** the only destination visible is `https://www.victoriassecret.com/us/vs/beauty`, which is consistent with the fragrance subject line. The other 49 links could not be checked.
- **Campaign code:** `NONBEAU` in the campaign code looks like a segment label, not a landing-page mismatch. I can't confirm either way from the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the 10 Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or remove them. Stop passing the email hash in plaintext HTTP query strings.
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels, and a descriptive alt to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
3. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the CSS selectors (`.ReadMsgBody`, drop `min-width{}`). Add `role="presentation"` to the layout tables. Move the table and JSON-LD out of `<head>`.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw headers from a direct inbox. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and the 50 skipped tracking links.
