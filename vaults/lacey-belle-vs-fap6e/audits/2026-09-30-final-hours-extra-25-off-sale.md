# Final Hours: Extra 25% Off Sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the sitewide "kitchen sink" sale email again, and the "Final Hours" urgency is the only new thing in it. The red torn-paper "SALE" hero, "LAST DAY / Up to 50% Off + Extra 25% Off Sale*" and a single SHOP NOW button are clear and land first. It's the same template as 2026-09-25 ("Extra 25% Off—Sale on Sale") and 2026-09-28 ("Hurry—8/$25 Sale Panties Ends Tomorrow"), so a subscriber has seen this hero three times in five days.
- Below the hero the email dilutes itself. It stacks a bra story, a panty edit, a fragrance launch, $8 mists and lotions, a $50-off-$175 threshold offer, a nine-tile category grid, a credit card offer and a trending strip. The "Body by Victoria Bra Sold Every Five Seconds" block, with its 5,300+ five-star reviews, is the best editorial moment. It's a real social-proof hook, but it sits below a sale email that's mostly shouting.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but forgettable. Its main job, closing out a sale, is being done by a template that has stopped surprising anyone. It would earn its place if it were half as long and had one offer.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: red block, "LAST DAY" tag, a big "Up to 50% Off + Extra 25% Off" and a visible SHOP NOW button, all above the fold.
- The "LAST DAY" tag is repeated on the $50-off-$175 block, so the deadline is easy to see.
- The bra block uses a concrete social-proof stat ("5,300+ 5-star reviews") with three models in real product, which is more persuasive than a generic banner.
- The "Tuesday Panty Edit" block has a specific CTA ("SHOP 5/$35 PANTIES") tied to a price point.
- The category tile grid gives a fast escape route to Sleep, Beauty, Activewear, Swim, Lingerie or Loungewear for anyone the hero misses.
- Nothing looks broken: no clipped text or missing images in the render.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with at least six competing offers (25% extra, 5/$35 panties, $8 mists, $50 off $175, a credit card $25, and a free panty with the app).
- The top banner ("Download the app… Free Panty with Purchase") pushes an app install ahead of the sale, then a separate panty deal appears further down.
- The subject says "Final Hours" while the body says "LAST DAY" twice, a small mismatch on the one message the email is built around.
- Nearly every block ends in a generic SHOP NOW, and there is no single hero product to click.
- The sale creative and cadence are stale (see 2026-09-25 and 2026-09-28), and the footer is a wall of fine print behind several asterisks ("Sale*", "Sold Every Five Seconds*").

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one editorial block (the bra stat, which is the strongest) and the category grid. Drop the $8 mists, the fragrance launch and the credit card block from a last-day send.
- 2. Move the app banner out of the top slot. Put the deadline and a countdown-style line ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT") at the top instead.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific to the product: "Shop 25% Off Bras" and "Shop 5/$35 Panties" instead of repeating SHOP NOW.
- 4. Align the wording: use "Final Hours" or "Last Day" everywhere, and state the actual end time.
- 5. Refresh the hero creative so it isn't the third identical torn-paper "SALE" in a week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 25% off sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: bras + panties, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5/$35 panties and $50 off $175, all gone at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The bra with 5,300+ five-star reviews is still on sale. Not for long.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering-blocking bug, an unresolved `%%cellcode%%` merge token inside the JSON-LD block. It also has structural HTML problems, about 10 third-party pixels with no alt text served over plain HTTP, and no visible authentication or unsubscribe headers. Those header gaps come from the relay, so they are unconfirmed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Unresolved token in a URL:** `merchantHomepageUrl` in the JSON-LD contains `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%`. The `offerPageUrl` in the same block has a resolved value (`d_cellid=092926545`). The `%%cellcode%%` value is the only raw token in the QA data.
- **Inconsistent `mi_u` parameter:** `offerPageUrl` has `mi_u=200492586522`. `merchantHomepageUrl` has `mi_u=092926545`, which is the same value as `d_cellid` and the `cm_mmc` cell segment. One of the two is likely wrong, which would break attribution.
- **Unverified links:** 44 tracking links were skipped (click-redirect domains, no HTTP probe). Their destinations and status are unverified.
- **Third-party HTTP pixels:** `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` and `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` are cookie-sync pixels served over `http://`. The `pub*` URLs carry a hashed email (`a4edea106b33...=1`) as a query key. This is a hashed identifier sent to a third party in clear-text HTTP requests. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will not fire them.
- **Hashed identifiers in URLs:** `dtm_email_hash` and `customerID` (both 64-hex SHA-256 values) are in the offer URLs. They are a privacy consideration, not a functional defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Invalid document structure:** a `<table>` and a `<script type="application/ld+json">` sit inside `<head>`, before the `<meta>` and `<style>` tags. HTML parsers close `<head>` and start `<body>` at the first `<table>`. The `<meta viewport>`, `<meta charset>` and both `<style>` blocks then end up in the body. Clients that strip or ignore body-level `<style>` will lose the responsive rules.
- **Wrong DOCTYPE for the markup:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is used with self-closing `<meta ... />` tags, which is an XHTML-style syntax.
- **Missing alt text:** 11 images have no alt text. They are the Dotomi `UCMController` pixel, `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, and `image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`. The pixels are decorative. The `.png` is a content image on the sender's own CDN and needs alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 accessibility concern.
- **Malformed CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody{ width: 100%;}` is missing its leading `.`, so it never matches. `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is also an invalid selector. Both are dead rules.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML shown is cut off, so I can't check anything after the header block.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL] `%%cellcode%%`:** in the `merchantHomepageUrl` value inside the JSON-LD block. It is an unrendered Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) AMPscript-style variable, which points to a personalization-resolution failure. It is in a JSON-LD script, not visible body copy, so subscribers won't see it. Google's email markup parser or a crawler will read it. If the same token is used in `<a href>` links further down (past the truncation), those would break for real users.
- No other raw tokens were detected.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify on the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. The From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment at the source.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source. This can't be assessed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **UTM convention:** the URLs use the retailer's own `cm_mmc` (Coremetrics-style) parameter, not `utm_*`. The value is `di-_-092926545-_-VSD_VSL_20260929_VS_6233_FILEBAL-_`. Campaign ID `20260929_VS_6233_FILEBAL` is consistent across `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category`.
- **Broken cell attribution:** on the homepage URL `d_cellid=%%cellcode%%` will not match the real cell ID. Any click on that URL would lose the cell dimension.
- **Offer/landing mismatch (data only):** the JSON-LD says "Save $50 When You Spend $175+" and links to `/us/vs/bras`. The subject line is "Extra 25% Off Sale". The structured data offer and the subject offer differ. This is a data consistency check for the sender to confirm, not a copy critique.
- **Offer expiry:** `availabilityEnds` is `2026-09-29T23:59:59-04:00`. It matches the "Final Hours" subject and today's date.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the `cellcode` token in the JSON-LD `merchantHomepageUrl` (`%%cellcode%%` → the resolved cell ID). Add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%...%%` left in the rendered output.
2. Reconcile `mi_u` between `offerPageUrl` (`200492586522`) and `merchantHomepageUrl` (`092926545`).
3. Move the `<table>` and JSON-LD block out of `<head>` and into `<body>`. Keep `<meta>` and `<style>` in `<head>`.
4. Serve all Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop them. Reconsider sending a hashed email address in HTTP query strings.
5. Add `alt=""` to the decorative pixels and a descriptive `alt` to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
6. Remove `user-scalable=0` and `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Fix the invalid `ReadMsgBody` and `min-width` selectors.
7. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results on the original message headers, not the AgentMail copy. Update the QA checks to fetch the raw headers, so these WARNs can become PASS or FAIL.
8. Probe a sample of the 44 skipped tracking links, following redirects, to check destinations and status codes.
