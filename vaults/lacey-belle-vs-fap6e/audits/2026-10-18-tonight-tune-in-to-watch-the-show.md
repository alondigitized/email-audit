# TONIGHT: Tune In to Watch the Show
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a tune-in email for the VS Fashion Show, and it does that job well. A black masthead with a pink "FASHION SHOW 2026" logo and a live countdown (07:44:48) sits at the top. Below it is a huge "LIVE TONIGHT" headline with a clear schedule: Pink Carpet 4PM PT, Live Show 4:30PM PT, TikTok After Party 5:45PM PT. The urgency is real and the subject matches the hero.
- It loses focus after that. Two CTAs sit side by side in the hero ("Get the Details" and "Register for the After Party"). Then come merch (Ombré styles), a free tote offer, a category grid, a rewards strip and a trending grid. The tote promo (free with $125+, code RUNWAY125) is the only concrete offer, and it is buried halfway down. The black-and-white silhouette hero is moody but tells you nothing about what to watch for. Nothing in the email shows where to watch, or a link to the stream itself.
- Verdict: a strong event announcement with a diluted body. The first screen works. By the end it has turned into a standard shopping catalog.

## What's working

- The countdown timer makes the "tonight" urgency real and visible.
- The "LIVE TONIGHT" headline is large, with a clean three-line schedule below it.
- The subject and hero agree, so the open pays off immediately.
- The free Fashion Show Tote offer has a clear value ($59.95) and a code.
- It follows the Oct 15 "Mark Your Calendar" send logically as the final reminder.

## What's weak

- Two equal-weight CTAs in the hero compete, and "Get the Details" is vague.
- The silhouette hero image is dark and anonymous, with no model or product.
- The tote offer and the Ombré merch module are below the first screen, with the tote the lower-priority one.
- The lower half (category grid, rewards, trending) pulls attention away from the show.
- The fine print is dense and long, and the tote's exclusions are hard to read.
- It is the fourth show-related send in a week, so there is cadence fatigue (Oct 15 reminder, Oct 17 PJ and panty sends).

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "Get the Details" and "Register" with one primary CTA: "Watch Live on YouTube". Make the after-party a text link.
- 2. Move the stream link and time into the top bar, with the time shown in the reader's time zone.
- 3. Use a recognizable model or runway image in the hero instead of the silhouette.
- 4. Cut the email to the show, the tote offer and one merch module. Drop the category and trending grids.
- 5. Put the tote offer directly under the schedule, and name the code in the headline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight: The Show + a Free Tote`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pink Carpet starts at 4PT. Stream free on YouTube and TikTok.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with a 600px table layout and a mobile media query. The main code-level problems are 10+ non-HTTPS Dotomi cookie-sync pixels with no alt text, a duplicated open-tracking pixel, and a viewport meta tag that disables zoom. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Duplicate open pixel:** the same URL is embedded twice, once in the top `<div style="font-size:0; line-height:0;">` and again before `<div id="AMPScript">`. Both are `https://click.e1.victoriassecret.com/open.aspx?2QIVRL3BYSFEZIK24P7XVAQEZU.70269&d=70269&bmt=0`. This can double-count opens.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2482...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2482/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` all load over plain HTTP. Mail clients and Gmail's image proxy may block them, and they are mixed-content and privacy exposure.
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** the Dotomi pixels are ad-network syncs placed in an email body. The UCM URL carries `dtmc_email_category=20261018_VS_6336_FULLFILE`. They add 11 extra third-party requests per open.
- **Unverified links:** 46 tracking links on `click.e1.victoriassecret.com` were skipped, so the QA data doesn't confirm whether any destinations are broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 12 images:** the Dotomi UCM pixel, `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, and `https://image.e1.victoriassecret.com/lib/fe92137274640d7b71/m/179/5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
  - The tracking pixels are not meaningful content, but they should still carry `alt=""`.
  - The `.png` is hosted on the brand's image domain and is probably real content, so it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid CSS selectors:** the first style block has `ReadMsgBody{...}` without the leading `.`, and `min-width{width:auto!important;}` is not a valid selector. Neither rule does anything.
- **Malformed `<title>`:** an empty `<title style="display: none...">` sits inside `<body>` and is invalid there.
- **Layout tables:** only some tables have `role="presentation"`. The outer layout tables (`class="container"`, `tb_properties border_style`) do not, so screen readers may announce them as data tables.
- **Doctype:** the doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional. That is functional but dated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unrendered `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens. Two leftovers are harmless but sloppy: a literal template comment (`<!-- `date "+ET%m.%y"` -->`) and an empty `AMPScript` block. The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. The QA tool says this is likely an AgentMail relay limitation, so this is unconfirmed. The raw message should be checked, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer:** the truncated HTML doesn't reach the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) are unverified.
- **Sender domain:** the From domain `e1.victoriassecret.com` matches the click and image subdomains, so there is no domain mismatch.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- None of the 46 tracked links were resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is the Dotomi `dtmc_email_category=20261018_VS_6336_FULLFILE`, which sits in a third-party pixel rather than a site link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the duplicate open pixel so only one remains.
2. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`, or drop the cookie-sync pixels from the email body.
3. Add descriptive alt text to `5bcec3ac-502d-4d8f-b48c-0a3d20e6e3c2.png`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `user-scalable=0` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Fix or delete the invalid `ReadMsgBody{}` and `min-width{}` rules, and move `<title>` into `<head>`.
6. Add `role="presentation"` to all layout tables.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message, not the relay copy.
8. Resolve the click-tracked redirects to check destination status codes and UTM parameters.
