# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- What I'm seeing isn't actually a homepage — it's an entry interstitial that ate the entire viewport before I could see a single product. The modal offers two paths: "Continue" into the main site, or "START PLAYING" in the Play Zone, which is clearly aimed at kids. As a 38-year-old hunting double-VIP weekend deals and set retirement dates, I have already been sorted into the wrong bucket before the page even loads.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button** is large, centered, and immediately tappable — no hunting required. It's the one piece of friction-reduction on this screen.
- **Cookie banner** is well-behaved — sits at the bottom without a full-screen takeover, doesn't block the main interaction.
- **Clean modal rendering** — the LEGO branding (red logo, blue/yellow palette) is crisp and on-brand. No visual glitches.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a gate, not content.** I came to check if the Icons Bouquet is on sale before the retirement date — instead I'm being asked whether I want to "watch videos and more." That's a miss.
- **Play Zone is the second most prominent element on the page** — "START PLAYING" in big type with minifig art. This makes lego.com feel like a children's portal, not a platform I spend hundreds of dollars on per year.
- **Zero VIP / loyalty signal.** My VIP points balance, a double-points reminder, anything — none of it. I'm a logged-in member who knows my number; acknowledge me.
- **No product, no offer, no category** is visible anywhere. The interstitial completely erases the shopping intent I arrived with.
- **The three icons in the header** (looks like bag, cart, clock) are tiny and unidentifiable at mobile scale — I can't even confirm I'm in the right region without squinting.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the interstitial for return visitors / VIP members.** If I have a cookie or I'm logged in, drop me directly on the homepage. This gate is friction for zero security benefit.
- **Demote the Play Zone below the fold** or route it through a dedicated kids sub-domain. It should not compete visually with the shopping CTA on the main www.lego.com entry.
- **Add a VIP status strip** to the top of this modal (or the header) — even just "You have 1,240 VIP points" turns a cold gate into a warm welcome and sets up conversion.
- **Surface one time-sensitive hook here** — "Double VIP points this weekend" or "Icons Botanical Collection retiring soon" would make me sprint past "Continue" instead of sighing through it.
