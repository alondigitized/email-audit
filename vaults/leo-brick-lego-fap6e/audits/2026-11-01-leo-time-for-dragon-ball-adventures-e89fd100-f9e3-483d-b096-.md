# Leo, time for Dragon Ball adventures
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a LEGO Insiders early-access launch for the first-ever LEGO Dragon Ball set (Shenron with Kid Goku), and the hero is strong. The dark teal diamond frame puts the green dragon front and center. The "Ready to gather the 7 Dragon Balls?" headline is playful and on-theme. The three detail tiles below the CTA (dragon head, Dragon Ball pile, Goku on the Kintoun) give the set some depth.
- The email then loses focus. After the hero it adds an arts & crafts gift-with-purchase ($130 threshold), a "win your wish list" sweepstakes and an app download block. That is four jobs in one send. The Dragon Ball launch is the only thing with real urgency, and the rest dilutes it. The hero never shows a price or set name, and "Shop now" appears twice with generic copy. Compared with the 10/24 Downton Abbey send (8/10), which let one product own the email, this is more scattered but still better than the 10/31 "latest and greatest sets" send (4/10).
- Verdict: a good hero wrapped in a cluttered body. A decent 6.

## What's working

- The hero is a single, striking product image inside the brand diamond frame, so the eye lands on the dragon first.
- The early-access framing ("As a member, you have early access… 11/1 to 11/3") gives a credible, dated reason to act.
- The three-tile strip shows the set from several angles and adds the Dragon Ball motif.
- The Dragon Ball logo lockup is a strong licensed-IP cue for fans.
- The Insiders card at the bottom shows Leo's name and 0 points, so it reads as member-specific.

## What's weak

- The hero never names the set or gives a price, so there is no concrete offer.
- Both main CTAs say "Shop now", which is generic and not a verb plus a noun.
- The arts & crafts GWP, the sweepstakes and the app promo compete with the launch, and the arts & crafts section is off-persona for a Dragon Ball buyer.
- The gift-with-purchase and sweepstakes both carry asterisked fine print ("while supplies last.*", "wish list.*").
- Despite "Leo" in the subject, the body has no personalization beyond the member card at the very bottom.
- There are no reviews, ratings or piece-count details visible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the Dragon Ball launch plus one secondary module. Move the arts & crafts GWP and the sweepstakes to another send, or reduce them to a single footer line.
- 2. Name the set and give the facts in the hero: set name, piece count, price and "Early access ends 11/3". This is the biggest lift.
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Get early access" or "Build Shenron".
- 4. Reframe the GWP as a threshold incentive tied to the set, for example "Add the set to a $130 order and get a free gift".
- 5. Add a countdown or "Ends 11/3" badge above the CTA to make the urgency visible.
