# Tick tock, time is flying, Leo!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a countdown-urgency send for the final hours of Back to Hogwarts, and it's built almost identically to the 2026-08-27 "countdown has begun" email — live clock, gift-with-purchase blocks, a product grid — except this time the countdown reads "0 days," which actually sells the "it's nearly over" urgency better than the earlier teaser did. The hero (woman unboxing the Ministry of Magic set inside a gold diamond-pattern frame) is warm and well-composed, and the flow from countdown → hero → CTA → GWP → product grid → loyalty/points → app download → digital membership card is logical.
- The execution drags it down. The four-item "Add these to your collection" grid has large, unexplained blank white space in every card — two show the product jammed to one side with an empty half, which reads as broken or unfinished rather than intentional design. There's also a lot of email here for one send: countdown, hero, two GWP tiers, four products, a "25 years of magic" loyalty pitch, ornament collectibles, app download badges, and a digital card — it's trying to be five emails at once, diluting the actual urgency message.
- Verdict: solid urgency mechanic wrapped in a bloated, visually unpolished send. 5/10 — better than the flat 2026-08-25 catalog dump, worse than the clean 2026-09-01 Enterprise Bridge single-product send.

## What's working

- The live countdown clock (0 days / 15 hrs / 51 min / 46 sec) is the strongest urgency signal LEGO has used in this stretch — sharper than the vaguer "countdown has begun" version.
- Hero image is warm and on-brand: a real person genuinely engaging with the product, not just a product-on-white shot.
- Clear single primary CTA ("Shop now") directly under the hero and urgency copy, with a hard deadline ("Ends 09/06").
- Gift-with-purchase tiers are laid out cleanly with product thumbnail, name, and their own "Shop now" buttons.

## What's weak

- The four product cards under "Add these to your collection" have large, unbalanced blank space — products crammed to one side, blank on the other — looks like missing/broken content, not a deliberate layout.
- The email tries to do too much: countdown urgency, two GWP tiers, a four-product grid, a loyalty-points pitch, collectible ornaments, and app download badges all stacked in one send, burying the actual "it's ending" message.
- The "Celebrate 25 years of magic" loyalty section and collectible ornaments feel bolted on and unrelated to the Back to Hogwarts deadline — it dilutes rather than reinforces urgency.
- Points/value both show "0 / $0.00" twice in the email, which for a loyalty member reads as broken personalization, not a null state.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product grid — either fill those cards properly or move to a consistent two-column layout; half-blank cards actively look like a rendering bug and undercut trust.
- 2. Cut the email down to countdown + hero + GWP tiers + one CTA. Move the loyalty/ornament/app-download content to a separate, non-urgent send — mixing "last hours" urgency with a leisurely loyalty pitch is contradictory.
- 3. Fix or suppress the "0 points / $0.00 value" display for logged-in Insiders members; showing zero everywhere for a 25-year loyalty program looks like a data bug.
- 4. Add a second CTA lower in the email (near the product grid) so users who scroll past the hero still have a clear path to purchase before time runs out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15 hours left, Leo — Back to Hogwarts ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call, Leo: Hogwarts offers gone tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free gifts and collector sets — but not for much longer.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Ministry of Magic phone booth gift disappears at midnight.`
