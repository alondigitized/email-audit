# LEGO® Ideas Godzilla is coming...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a LEGO Insiders teaser for the Ideas Godzilla set, which arrives 11/27. The hero is the strongest part. The grey-and-blue Godzilla sits inside a red-and-black diamond frame, and "Run. Build. Scream." is a good headline. A second Godzilla banner and a gift-with-purchase promo follow it. The gift is a Godzilla logo and footprint, available 11/27-11/30 while supplies last.
- The email is too busy for a teaser. It has two Godzilla heroes, a livestream box, a four-product grid and an app promo. A Black Friday sticker floats at the bottom with no offer attached. The only CTA on the hero is "Add to wish list", which is soft because the set isn't buyable yet. The gift-with-purchase is the most compelling hook, but it sits mid-email as small body text. No price or piece count is shown for Godzilla. The subject says "is coming...", but the date is only in the body copy.
- Compared with the 11/2 Venice send (7/10) and the Downton Abbey launch (8/10), this is a similar template with a weaker call to action. It is a decent fan-service send. It would convert better if it led with the date and the gift.

## What's working

- The red-and-black diamond hero puts Godzilla front and center, and the eye lands on it first.
- "Run. Build. Scream." is punchy, and "The gift that screams 'Godzilla was here!'" is a fun, on-brand line.
- The gift-with-purchase gives people a reason to buy on launch day.
- The Insiders points header and per-product "+points" badges reinforce the member benefit.
- The Godzilla and Monsterverse logos add franchise credibility.

## What's weak

- Two stacked Godzilla sections repeat the same message, so the gift copy is pushed below the fold.
- "Add to wish list" is a low-commitment CTA, and the Godzilla set has no price, piece count or points.
- The livestream box has a tiny "Learn more" button and vague copy ("an exciting new build"). It competes with the main story.
- The product grid (Dragon Ball, Santa, Venice, Cozy Plants) is unrelated to Godzilla, and the Black Friday sticker has no offer behind it.
- The 11/27 date is buried in body text, and the gift terms carry an asterisk with fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Merge the two Godzilla sections into one. Put the 11/27 date and the gift-with-purchase directly under the headline, and make the button "Get early access 11/27" or "Set my reminder".
- 2. Show the Godzilla set's price, piece count and Insiders points, as the grid products already do.
- 3. Cut the grid to one or two items that relate to the story, or drop it. Remove the Black Friday sticker unless it carries a real offer.
- 4. Give the livestream a date, a time and a clear "Add to calendar" button, or move it to its own send.
- 5. Put the gift terms in plain language near the CTA, not behind an asterisk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Godzilla lands 11/27, with a free gift`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, the Godzilla set has a free gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy the set 11/27-11/30 and get the Godzilla logo and footprint, while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Insiders get first look at the new Monsterverse build. Mark 11/27.`
