# You didn’t forget, did you, Leo?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- The subject "You didn't forget, did you, Leo?" promises a reminder, but the body is a generic Black Friday loyalty roundup. There is no forgotten item, cart or deadline. Nothing in the email answers the question the subject asks.
- The body is a long scroll with five competing offers. They are a "Take a look at our Black Friday sale" hero, 4x loyalty points, three gifts with purchase, two "sale items we think you'll love" and a sweepstakes for one million points. Every module has its own button. The sale hero never states a discount, and the points balance is a visible 0 / $0.00, which sits awkwardly next to the loyalty pitch.
- This is the same Insiders template as the earlier Godzilla and "counting down to Black Friday" sends today. The subject is clever, but the email underneath is the weakest kind of Black Friday message: lots of modules and no concrete deal. I'd call it a 5-6 send.

## What's working

- The red-and-black BLACK FRIDAY hero is on-season and is the first thing your eye lands on.
- The Insiders header shows the points balance, and the 4x points banner has a distinct green block that stands out.
- The gift-with-purchase thresholds are concrete: the Jukebox at $250+ and the Snow Globe at $170+.
- Product cards show piece counts (1161, 973), which gives some detail for shoppers.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject is a guilt-trip hook with no payoff. Nothing in the body is something Leo "forgot."
- There is no stated discount. "Save on selected sets" is vague for a Black Friday email.
- The hero button says "Explore our sale," which is a generic verb and noun.
- The header shows Points 0 / Value $0.00 twice. That deflates the "4x points" and "loyalty points are waiting" copy.
- The sweepstakes and the Retro M-Tron redemption push toward Insider rewards instead of the sale. There are about nine CTAs, and the long scroll dilutes the hero.
- The email repeats today's other Black Friday and Godzilla sends, so cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one promise and lead with it. Either state the real Black Friday offer in the hero (percent off, or the best gift with purchase), or deliver on the subject with a "your doorbuster ends tonight" message.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules: hero, one gift-with-purchase, and four sale products with prices.
- 3. Give the hero button specific copy, such as "Shop Black Friday deals."
- 4. Drop the points-balance header and the sweepstakes block for members with zero points, or show them something relevant to that.
- 5. Space out sends. Today's Godzilla and countdown emails already used the same template.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, Black Friday deals end Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, don't miss 4x Insiders points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free gifts with purchase plus 4x points on every set, today through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders perks are live: gifts, 4x points and sale sets inside.`
