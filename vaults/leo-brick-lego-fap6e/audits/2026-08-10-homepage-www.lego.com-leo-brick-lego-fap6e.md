# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- First thing LEGO.com shows me isn't a set, a deal, or my VIP status — it's a "pick a door" splash screen asking if I'm here to shop or to play games in "THE PLAY ZONE," stacked on top of a cookie consent banner. I can see just enough of the blurred page behind it — a yellow promo strip up top, a row of product tiles with price badges down low — to know there's an actual storefront back there, but it's locked behind a gate. For a VIP member who already knows exactly why he's here, this reads like a kids'-brand experience bolted onto a hobbyist storefront.

## What's working

- **The modal itself is well-built** — clean blue/yellow split, legible copy, no visual glitches. Whoever built this gate did it competently.
- **Faint hints of a real storefront behind it** — I can make out product tiles with yellow price badges at the bottom edge, which at least tells me there's a shop back there worth clicking into.

## What's weak

- **The "LEGO.com vs. Play Zone" gate itself** — I'm a 38-year-old VIP member who builds UCS Star Wars sets, not a kid picking between shopping and a game portal. This choice screen adds a tap of friction for zero payoff on my end.
- **Cookie banner stacked directly under the modal** — two consent/gate layers before I see a single set. That's two taps just to get to the homepage I actually asked for.
- **Zero VIP/loyalty signal anywhere visible** — as a double-VIP-weekend hunter, this is the first thing I look for, and the gate buries it completely.
- **No visible offer or hero message** — can't tell if there's a sale, a new Botanical Collection drop, or anything retirement-date-relevant, because it's all blurred out behind the popup.

## Recommendations

- Kill the "LEGO.com vs. Play Zone" split-screen gate for logged-in/returning adult shoppers — route AFOL and VIP accounts straight to the storefront.
- Collapse the cookie banner into a single slim bottom bar instead of a second full-width blocker stacked under the modal.
- If a kid-mode gate is legally/brand-necessary, remember it via cookie so VIP members past a certain age/segment never see it twice.
- Surface VIP status or points balance the instant the real homepage loads — don't make me hunt for it after clearing two popups.
