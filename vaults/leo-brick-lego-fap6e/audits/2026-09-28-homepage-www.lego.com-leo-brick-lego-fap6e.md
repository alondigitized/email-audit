# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- I typed in lego.com to check on Botanical Collection restocks and got stopped cold by a "which door do you want" gate — LEGO.com vs. "The Play Zone" — stacked on top of a cookie banner. I can't even see the actual homepage; it's blurred out behind this modal. Whatever campaign or set they're pushing right now is completely invisible to me.

## What's working

- The blue/yellow gate is at least visually clean — clear typography, no glitching, LEGO logo crisp and centered
- "Continue" is an obvious enough label that I know which side gets me to actual shopping vs. kid content

## What's weak

- **The full-page gate itself** — I'm a 38-year-old VIP member who wants to check set retirements and restocks, not choose between "shop" and "play games, watch videos." This screen should not exist for someone with an account/cookie history.
- **Cookie banner stacked underneath** — now I'm clicking through two overlays before I see a single product or price.
- **Zero personalization** — nothing here acknowledges VIP status, purchase history, or double-VIP weekend timing, and I can't even see if one's running because the homepage is blurred out behind this gate.
- **"THE PLAY ZONE" panel is pure kid-bait** — minifigures with a tablet, "play games, watch videos" — completely irrelevant to someone building UCS Star Wars sets.

## Recommendations

- Skip this gate entirely for logged-in or returning VIP members with cookies already set — remember my choice
- Collapse the cookie banner into a single, smaller footer strip instead of a second full-width overlay
- If a gate has to exist for kids vs. adults, make the "LEGO.com" side lead with something an AFOL actually cares about (new set drop, VIP double-points weekend) instead of generic "shop, get support & more"
- Surface the current promo/campaign banner behind the modal so returning shoppers aren't staring at a blurred wall
