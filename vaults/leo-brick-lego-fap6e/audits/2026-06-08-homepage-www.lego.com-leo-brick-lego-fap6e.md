# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- I opened LEGO.com and got a gatekeeper screen — not a homepage. The entire above-the-fold experience is a region/age interstitial asking me to hit "Continue" or detour to a kids' Play Zone, with a full-width cookie banner stacked on top of that. There is zero product, zero campaign content, zero anything that tells me why I bothered opening the app. For a VIP member who came here to check if there's a double-VIP event running, this is a dead end before the race even starts.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button** — large, high-contrast, hard to miss. One tap to get past the gate. Good button sizing for mobile.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout jank. The page paints quickly and predictably.
- **Cookie notice** — appears below the fold of the gate modal rather than covering the primary CTA, which is at least better than a full-screen modal stack.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold** is a dead-end gate, not a homepage. I can't see a single product, deal, or reason to stay.
- **"THE PLAY ZONE"** with cartoon minifig runners is the only brand content visible — it's pitched at kids, not the adult collector segment that represents the highest-AOV VIP tier.
- **No VIP/loyalty signal anywhere** — I came here as a member and this page has no idea who I am.
- **Cookie banner + interstitial simultaneously** — two friction layers before I've seen one product image. Classic CRO anti-pattern.
- **"You are about to enter LEGO.com"** — this phrasing sounds like a parental gate warning, not a welcome. As an adult this reads weird and slightly condescending.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the standalone age/region gate on mobile** — detect my geolocation and LEGO Account session silently and drop me straight onto the homepage. VIP-logged-in users especially should never see this screen.
- **Swap "THE PLAY ZONE" secondary panel for a VIP or seasonal offer card** — if you must show two paths, the second path should be "Shop New Releases" or "VIP Double Points Weekend," not a kids' game hub.
- **Merge cookie consent into the main gate modal** as a single inline checkbox + Continue — eliminates one full friction layer without reducing compliance.
- **Show one hero product image in the background behind the gate modal** — even a blurred Botanical Collection or UCS set behind the dialog gives me a reason to push "Continue" instead of bouncing.
