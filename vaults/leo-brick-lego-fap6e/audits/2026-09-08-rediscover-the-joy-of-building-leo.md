# Rediscover the joy of building, Leo
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a rerun of the 2026-08-19 "Rediscover the joy of building, Leo" send — same subject line verbatim, same Botanicals hero plant, same "If you love it, build it" / "Awaken your Joy" copy. That's not a new campaign, it's a repeat, and after LEGO's recent stretch of countdown clocks, Insiders early access, and the Star Trek Enterprise Bridge drop, sending this identical generic-catalog email again reads as a scheduling miss rather than a deliberate touch.
- Worse, the "Here's something just for you" personalization block — supposedly the one section tailored to Leo — is visibly broken: there's a gift icon and a coin icon floating in an otherwise empty white box, no product name, no price, no CTA text next to the product image. That block is the entire reason this email exists (personalized recommendation) and it's rendering empty.
- The rest of the email is competent but unfocused — a Botanicals hero, then a Pokémon competition, then a four-icon theme nav (Star Wars, Harry Potter, LEGO Icons, Ideas), then an app download pitch. Nothing here is wrong on its own, but stacked together with a broken personalization module and zero discount, this is a low-effort repeat send. Judgement: skip.

## What's working

- Clean black/white/red LEGO brand system carries through consistently — logo, Insiders points card top and bottom, barcode loyalty card at the footer.
- The Botanicals plant hero is a strong, distinctive product shot that stands out from typical set-box photography.
- Loyalty context (0 points, $0.00 value) is visible twice, reinforcing the Insiders membership frame even though the member has no balance to show.
- The competition block ("BUILD A BERRY AND YOU COULD WIN AWESOME PRIZES!") is visually distinct with its own yellow background, so it doesn't blend into the rest of the scroll.

## What's weak

- The "Here's something just for you" personalized-product block is rendering broken — icons with no accompanying text, no product name, no price, next to an unlabeled product image. This is the one section meant to feel tailored to Leo and it's empty.
- This is a verbatim repeat of the 2026-08-19 "Rediscover the joy of building, Leo" email — same subject, same hero, same copy — with no new offer or context added.
- No discount, no price, no urgency anywhere in the email — just "Shop now" and "Enter now," both generic CTAs.
- The email tries to do four things at once (Botanicals hero, personalized pick, competition, theme browse, app download) with no single clear priority, unlike the cleaner single-hero Star Trek send from 2026-09-01.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken personalization block before this ships again — a floating gift icon and coin icon with no product name or price is a rendering failure, not a design choice. Verify the merge/render pipeline for that module.
- 2. Don't resend an identical subject + hero within three weeks — differentiate this send from 2026-08-19 with a new product focus or drop it from the calendar entirely.
- 3. Add one concrete offer (% off, bundle deal, free gift threshold) — every recent LEGO send scoring above 5 has had a countdown, early access, or gift-with-purchase hook; this one has none.
- 4. Cut the email down to two sections: hero + one clear CTA, rather than hero, personalized pick, competition, theme nav, and app download all competing for attention.
