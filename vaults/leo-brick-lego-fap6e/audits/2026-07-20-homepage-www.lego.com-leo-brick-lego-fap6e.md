# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get a homepage — I got a gate. LEGO.com is showing me a country/access interstitial before I can see a single product, deal, or VIP point balance. The secondary panel is pitching "The Play Zone" (games and videos for kids), which is the exact wrong thing to lead with for an AFOL who opened the site to check double-VIP weekend offers. The brand looks fine graphically — bold yellow and blue, clean LEGO logo — but functionally this is a wall, not a welcome.

## What's working

- **Clean interstitial layout** — the "Continue" button is unambiguous and centered; even annoyed, I know exactly what to tap
- **LEGO brand identity is solid** — the logo, yellow/blue palette, and minifig artwork are on-brand and render crisply
- **Cookie disclosure is upfront** — it's visible, not buried; I can see exactly what's being set before I proceed

## What's weak

- **The interstitial itself** — a returning VIP member who opens lego.com on their phone should not hit a friction wall before seeing any content; this kills momentum instantly
- **"The Play Zone" panel is completely wrong audience** — kids' gaming/video content as the secondary CTA on a shared entry screen signals LEGO doesn't know who's visiting; I'm here for Botanical Collection retirement dates, not Ninjago videos
- **Zero commerce above the fold** — no sale, no new release, no VIP callout, nothing that justifies me tapping "Continue" faster than I otherwise would
- **Three icons above the "Continue" text** (what look like a bag, cart, and clock) are too small and unlabeled to communicate anything useful at a glance

## Recommendations

- **Skip or shorten the interstitial for known returning users** — if I'm logged in or have a VIP cookie, drop me straight onto the homepage with my points balance visible; the gate is pure friction for repeat shoppers
- **If the gate must exist, inject the current top offer into it** — a single line like "Double VIP Points this weekend" on the interstitial turns a wall into a teaser and increases tap-through intent
- **Replace the Play Zone secondary panel with an age-segment fork** — a "Build for yourself" path (Botanical, UCS, Icons) alongside the kids path would immediately signal that LEGO sees adult fans
- **Label those three icons** with text underneath so a first-time or low-vision visitor understands what they're tapping before hitting Continue
