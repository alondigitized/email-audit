# Don't miss epic LEGO® Insiders Weekend promotions
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Insiders Weekend send, and it arrives a day after the weekend ended. Subject and hero both say "Final call" and the timer shows 0 days, 2 hours, 32 minutes. But the gift copy says "Ends 11/22," and the prior "last chance" send on 11/22 already used that deadline. The urgency is real, but the email feels like a rerun of "Leo, last chance for member offers."
- The body is better structured than the hero. It has a member product grid, two gifts with purchase (Retro Jukebox at $250, Winter Snow Globe at $175), a rewards redemption block, and a 1 million points sweepstakes. The execution has visible problems. The hero CTA is an empty white pill with no label. All four product tiles show no names, prices, or buttons. The points balance reads 0 and $0.00. A member with nothing to spend sees a "members, these are just for you" section with no concrete offer.
- Verdict: a competent template carrying a stale, over-repeated campaign. The offer is buried, the hero button is broken, and the product grid is anonymous. It is a 5 at best.

## What's working

- The countdown timer under "Final call" gives credible, visible urgency.
- The gift-with-purchase section states thresholds ($250 and $175) with Shop now buttons.
- The green Insiders Weekend hero is on-brand and matches the earlier sends in the sequence.
- The app download block and the Enter now sweepstakes CTA give secondary paths for engaged members.
- The Redeem Reward button on the Retro M-Tron Explorer is clear and shows its points cost (2,400).

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is a blank white pill, so the primary action has no copy.
- The four "Members, these are just for you" tiles have no titles, prices, or CTAs. Only tiny icons sit at the bottom of each tile.
- The points balance shows 0 and $0.00 twice. For a member that is a dead-end message, and it undercuts the loyalty angle.
- The hero copy is vague ("explore all the benefits") and never states the offer. The gift-with-purchase deal sits far below the fold.
- The deadline "Ends 11/22" appears in a send dated 11/23, which reads as expired. Fine print like "While supplies last. T&Cs apply" adds to that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero CTA so it carries a label such as "Shop member gifts." Then move the two gifts with purchase directly under the timer as the lead offer.
- 2. Fill in the product tiles with names, member prices, and a Shop button. If product data is missing, suppress the module instead of sending blank tiles.
- 3. Hide or replace the 0-point balance block for members with no points. Show "Earn double points" instead.
- 4. Check the send date against the offer end date so nothing ships after the deadline. The timer and the "Ends 11/22" copy contradict each other.
- 5. Stop sending near-identical "last chance" emails. One final send is enough.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, your free LEGO® gift expires tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 gifts with purchase, ending soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $175 and unlock the Winter Snow Globe. Members only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a shot at 1 million bonus points. Enter before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

The real defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes and one non-HTTPS image. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 60 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing, so their destination validity and redirect health are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 10,962 of 15,055 characters URLs (about 73%, above the 70% threshold). Long tracked URLs dominate the text alternative, which can raise spam-filter scrutiny and reads poorly.
- No broken links were confirmed.

  - Decorative images: `GreyBrick.png` (about 8 instances), `Email+Angle+Divider+VI+aihscia.png`, `iconpath.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. A missing attribute is read out as the filename by some assistive tech.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a proven failure. Check the raw message at the sender, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. The sender is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`, and the HTML shows images served from `image.e.crm.lego.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud). Verify alignment of the e.crm.lego.com subdomain at the source.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. The truncated HTML can't confirm or rule it out, since footers usually sit at the end. Treat it as a possible issue, not a confirmed one.
- The From address is `Noreply@`, which is a deliverability and reply-handling consideration rather than a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The 60 links were tracking redirects and were not resolved, and the HTML was truncated. Product images do point to the `lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/` CDN with consistent `fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400` params. No issues found within the observable data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the barcode image `src` from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx...` to `https://`. This is the highest priority because it is a real functional risk.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, product, reward, GWP and footer-logo images. Set `alt=""` on spacers and dividers (`GreyBrick.png`, `Email+Angle+Divider…`, `iconpath.png`, footer separators).
3. Confirm at the sender (not through the relay) that the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and the footer physical address are present.
4. Trim the plain-text part, for example by using shorter or untracked URLs, to bring it under 70% URLs.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. Probe a sample of the 60 tracked links to verify destinations and UTMs, and scan the full body for merge tokens.
6. Make the `<title>` specific to the email, such as the subject line. This is optional.
