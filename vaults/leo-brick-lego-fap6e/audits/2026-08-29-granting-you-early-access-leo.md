# Granting you early access, Leo...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's Insiders early-access send for the new Harry Potter Ministry of Magic Collector's set, and it follows directly on the heels of the 2026-08-27 "countdown has begun" teaser and the 2026-08-12 "favorite week is nearly here" send — this is the third Back to Hogwarts beat in under three weeks. The good news: it finally delivers the payoff (early access is live, not just teased), stacks two gift-with-purchase offers, and surfaces Insiders member pricing with points values on two hero sets. That's more concrete value than most of the recent LEGO sends in this stretch.
- The bad news is the hero itself is a bust — the top banner is a huge navy block with only a thin gold diagonal brick pattern hugging the left edge; there's no product shot, no set name, no visual payoff for "early access granted" until you scroll past a mostly-empty screen. For a $130+ collector's set drop, leading with empty navy space is a real miss. Loyalty personalization is also visibly broken — the points balance in the header and the digital card both show "0 points, $0.00 value" for a named member (Leo), which reads as either a rendering glitch or a genuinely empty account, neither of which sells "the magic of membership."
- Net: strong offer architecture (gift w/ purchase + member pricing + early access), undercut by a wasted hero and a personalization module that looks broken. This scores mid-pack — better than the generic browse sends, weaker than the 08-12 early-access teaser that had a real diorama hero.

## What's working

- Clear value stack: early access + two gift-with-purchase tiers + Insiders member pricing, all in one send.
- Points and value are shown on both hero sets (Diagon Alley +1300, The Burrow +1690), which is concrete and useful for a loyalty-driven persona.
- "Shop now" CTAs are present and repeated at every offer block (hero, both gifts, both product cards) — no ambiguity about what to click.
- Gift-with-purchase cards include real deadlines ("Available until 09/06, while stocks last") — credible urgency without shouting.
- Digital Insiders card at the footer is a nice loyalty touch, reinforcing membership even after the CTAs are done.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is nearly empty navy space with just a gold diagonal stripe on the left edge — no product image, no set name, no reason to care before scrolling.
- Both the header ("0 Points, Value $0.00") and the footer digital card show zero balance for Leo, which either looks broken or undercuts the "exclusive member" pitch entirely.
- No actual set image appears until deep in the "LEGO Insiders Price" block — for a set this visually rich (Ministry of Magic), that's a wasted asset.
- The two gift-with-purchase product thumbnails are tiny and low-contrast against the dark navy card background, easy to skim past.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the third Harry Potter/Back to Hogwarts email since 08-12, and the subject doesn't distinguish itself from "Every wizard's favorite week is nearly here."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual product shot of the Ministry of Magic Collector's set in the hero, not empty navy space — this is the single biggest lever here since "early access granted" needs a payoff image immediately.
- 2. Fix or hide the $0/0-points display for members — either show real accrued points or drop the module until it renders correctly; a visibly broken loyalty number actively undermines trust.
- 3. Enlarge and brighten the gift-with-purchase thumbnails so the free items read at a glance, not just the copy.
- 4. Differentiate this subject from the prior two Hogwarts sends so subscribers don't mentally file it as the same email again.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, your Ministry of Magic set just unlocked`
- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hours early: Ministry of Magic + free gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop before everyone else — plus two free gifts with purchase.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Insiders get first pick of the new Wizarding World collection.`
- 5. Move the member-pricing product grid (Diagon Alley, The Burrow) higher, ahead of the gift blocks, since those are the higher-intent purchase drivers.
