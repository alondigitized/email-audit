# Every wizard’s favorite week is nearly here, Leo
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's Harry Potter "Back to Hogwarts" early-access teaser, and it's one of the stronger sends in this recent stretch — the Ministry of Magic diorama hero is genuinely striking, the copy is specific ("enter code 62442 in the iconic telephone box"), and there's a real, dated member perk (free gift 08/29–09/06) stacked with a wishlist CTA. It reads as a cohesive Harry Potter drop rather than the generic "trending bricks" grab-bag format LEGO has leaned on lately (8/6, 8/12 Member Day sends).
- Where it stumbles is value clarity: the points/value module shows "0 points, $0.00" twice, which either means Leo genuinely has zero Insiders points or the personalization token is rendering as a hard zero for everyone — either way it kills the "25 years of magic, turned into savings" pitch it's trying to make right above it. The primary CTA is also "Add to wish list," not "Shop now" or "Pre-order" — a soft ask for what should be a hard-launch moment.
- Net: strong visual and thematic execution, undercut by a weak CTA verb and a loyalty module that reads as broken or unflattering. This lands solidly above LEGO's recent Member Day filler but below the X-Files/Klimt early-access sends that had a harder, single-minded CTA.

## What's working

- Hero image (Ministry of Magic set) is high-detail, on-theme, and immediately legible as a premium collector's set.
- Headline "Magical on so many levels" plus a concrete redemption mechanic (code 62442 in the telephone box) gives real, specific instructions, not vague hype.
- Dated member gift window (08/29–09/06) creates credible urgency without screaming about it.
- Clear section rhythm: hero → member gift → loyalty → "add to collection" cross-sell → app download → barcode member card. Easy to scan on mobile.
- Digital membership barcode at the footer is a nice, on-brand utility touch for in-store use.

## What's weak

- Points/Value module shows "0 / $0.00" twice (once mid-email, once on the footer card) — reads as broken personalization or a demoralizing reminder Leo has no points, right when the copy is trying to sell loyalty value.
- Primary CTA is "Add to wish list," a low-commitment verb for what's framed as an early-access launch — no "Shop now," "Pre-order," or "Get early access" button anywhere.
- Two Harry Potter product tiles ("Add these to your collection") have no name, price, or points value visible under them — just icons, so there's no product detail to click into confidently.
- The App Store/Google Play badges sit oddly mid-scroll between products and the Harry Potter logo lockup, breaking the flow right before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or hide the points/value module — a real balance builds urgency, a hard zero undercuts the whole "25 years of savings" section; if it's a broken merge token, that's the highest-priority fix.
- 2. Swap the primary CTA from "Add to wish list" to something with intent, e.g. "Shop Early Access" or "Reserve Yours" — wishlisting is a weak ask for a dated collector's-item drop.
- 3. Add product names/prices under the two Harry Potter tiles in "Add these to your collection" so the click has a specific target.
- 4. Move the App Store/Google Play badges to the very footer, after the Harry Potter branding, so the product story isn't interrupted mid-scroll.
