# Leo, we're counting down to Black Friday
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday countdown teaser with a visible 0 days, 2 hours, 58 minutes, 36 seconds timer and a big red-and-black BLACK FRIDAY graphic. The Godzilla set is the real hero, and it is a strong one. It is a large, dramatic product image with "comes to life tomorrow" and a gift with purchase, so there is a reason to care. The email leads with urgency but gives almost no offer. The sale is "coming" and the discount is never stated.
- The render also has visible bugs. The pill-shaped CTA buttons under the hero and under the Godzilla block are empty white ovals with no label. The two big white cards (Godzilla box, gift card) are mostly empty space. The "Your exclusive gift" card shows only a tiny thumbnail with no readable copy. A small jagged black artifact sits at the bottom of the red/black section. Points and value read "0" and "$0.00" twice, which is a dead loyalty hook for this member.
- Judgement: a good concept, weakened by an unlabeled CTA and no concrete offer. It is the sixth Insiders-flavored send in a week, after "Leo, discover the perks of being a LEGO® Insider" on 11/24, which also teased Black Friday without an offer. It is more visual than that one but still a tease.

## What's working

- The countdown timer is a clear, credible urgency device that sits at the top.
- The Godzilla hero is dramatic, on-brand and impossible to miss, with a gift-with-purchase hook.
- The black, red and green palette is consistent and reads as Black Friday immediately.
- The "We think you'll love these sets" row, with Holiday House and Millennium Falcon, shows piece counts and Insiders points, which are useful details.
- The app-download block and the Insiders card at the bottom reinforce membership.

## What's weak

- Both main CTA buttons render as blank white ovals with no label, so the primary action is ambiguous.
- There is no stated discount, percentage or dollar amount anywhere. "Our Black Friday sale" is vague.
- The Godzilla and gift card tiles are large white boxes with a lot of empty space, and the "Your exclusive gift" card shows only a tiny thumbnail with no readable copy.
- "0 points / $0.00" is shown twice, which highlights an empty balance and gives no member benefit.
- There is a small black jagged artifact near the bottom of the dark section, and the layout is very long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the CTA labels. Use specific copy like "Get early access" or "Shop Godzilla tomorrow". Add one visible, labeled button above the fold.
- 2. State the offer. Put the actual Black Friday terms, such as "Up to X% off + free gift with $Y", in the hero.
- 3. Shrink the empty white product and gift tiles. Add captions with the gift name, the spend threshold and the dates.
- 4. Replace the $0.00 points block for members with no balance with a "Earn double points" or "Members get early access" line.
- 5. Cut the length and keep one hero, one gift and one product row.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Godzilla lands tomorrow, free gift inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member early access, a free gift with purchase and the Godzilla set arrive first.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders perks unlock before the sale opens. See what's in.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is MJML-style table markup with a responsive media-query block, dark-mode meta tags and a full-width preheader. QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). The findings are mostly missing alt text on about 13 unique images, one non-HTTPS personalized image, and header checks that could not be verified through the relay. The HTML source is truncated, so body links, the footer and the unsubscribe block were not visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **46 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped HTTP checks on the click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and link validity are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is heavily URL-based.** 10,528 of 14,357 characters (about 73%) are URLs, above the 70% threshold. The likely cause is long tracking URLs in the text alternative, which can raise spam-filter scoring.
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 13 unique images.** The QA list repeats each image because desktop and mobile variants share a source. QA cannot tell a missing `alt` attribute from a deliberate `alt=""`, so the decorative ones may be fine.
  - Content images that need real alt text:
    - `01_BFCM_Countdown_2611-Hero-Banner-Short.png`
    - `02_BFCM_Countdown_2611-Hero-Banner-XL.png.png`
    - `04_BFCM_Countdown_2611-Reward+1.png`
    - `04_BFCM_Countdown_2611-Reward+2.png`
    - `05_BFCM_Countdown_2611-Reward.png`
    - `32c1h6ssjeb9m68s32dpp.gif` (from `gifcdn.com`, a third-party host)
    - Product images `11387_Prod.png` and `75192_Prod.png` (`www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...`)
  - Likely decorative, which should carry `alt=""`:
    - `GreyBrick.png`
    - `iconpath.png`
    - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png`
    - `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`
    - `Logo+Container+logo+white.png`
- **Image served over `http://`.** The "Insider Barcode" image (`http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...`) is non-HTTPS and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients. It does have alt text.
- **Web fonts depend on Google Fonts.** Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import` tags are in the head. Many clients strip them, but the font stack falls back to `'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`, so this is low risk.
- **The GIF is hosted on `gifcdn.com`**, a third-party domain with no sender control over availability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders "Leo," and no unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The barcode `LiveContent.aspx` image is dynamic content that resolves at open time. The truncated source leaves the body unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From domain is `e.crm.lego.com`, with the sender address `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters could not be verified.** All 46 links go through click-tracking redirects that were not resolved.
- Product images load from `www.lego.com` CDN URLs, which indicates the same product catalog (sets 11387 and 75192) as the site.
- Landing page alignment with the Black Friday countdown could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero banners, the three reward images, the GIF and the two product images. Set `alt=""` on the decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, `iconpath.png`, the footer separator and the logo container).
2. Serve the Insider Barcode image over HTTPS. Change `cl.s50.exct.net` from `http://` to `https://`, or confirm that Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`exct.net`) supports it.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. The relay may be dropping them.
4. Shorten the plain-text part, or reduce long tracking URLs in it, to bring URL share below 70%.
5. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to check final destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Host the GIF on the sender's own image domain (`image.e.crm.lego.com`) instead of `gifcdn.com`.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
