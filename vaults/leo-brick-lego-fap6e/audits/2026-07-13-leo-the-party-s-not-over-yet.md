# Leo, the party’s not over yet 🎁
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday LEGO fired the curtain call on Insiders Days with a last-chance countdown email (July 12, 8/10). Today they pivot cleanly to a birthday lifecycle trigger — the right instinct the day after a five-email promotional run. The hero lands well: a party-themed minifig banner, "Keep the fun going, Leo," and warm birthday copy. The emotional register is appropriate and the personalization is genuine.
- The problem is structural. The email's most valuable moment — an exclusive birthday gift promo code (4NXVWURMKA) with a 30-day window — is buried below two product CTAs *and* a social-sharing prompt. Most mobile readers will bail before they ever see it. Meanwhile, the two featured products (Arcade Pinball Machine, Koenigsegg Megacar) are straight retreads from the July 1 and July 2 sends, both promoted here with the same generic "Shop now" buttons and no birthday-specific reframe.
- The bones of a genuinely good birthday send are here. The promo code section is honest, time-bound, and exclusive. But the email's structure actively works against its best idea.

## What's working

- "Leo" in subject and hero creates a real lifecycle feel — this reads like a personal send, not a broadcast.
- Clean cadence break: coming one day after the Insiders Days finale, a birthday email is the right gear shift.
- Promo code with a 30-day window and clear purchase condition (4+ items) is honest and specific.
- Render is clean throughout — no broken images, no layout breakage, no overlapping text.
- Subject line is mobile-friendly at ~31 characters and emoji is restrained (one, on-theme).

## What's weak

- The birthday gift promo — the best offer in the email — is buried below two product CTAs and a social sharing section; most readers won't scroll to it.
- "Shop now" on both product CTAs is generic and fails to reframe the Pinball Machine and Koenigsegg as birthday-worthy purchases.
- The "Share the pictures and tag LEGO®" section interrupts purchase momentum to serve LEGO's UGC pipeline, not the subscriber.
- Neither the subject line nor (apparently) the preview text hints at the promo code, so the inbox entry point leaves the offer's value invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Promote the birthday gift section above the product CTAs.** The promo code is the sharpest conversion hook in the email; putting it below two shop prompts and a social share ask is a structural own-goal. Move it directly below the hero.
- 2. **Reframe the product CTAs around the birthday occasion.** "Treat yourself" or "Add it to your birthday wishlist" does more work than "Shop now" when your thesis is "you deserve to keep celebrating."
- 3. **Cut or relocate the social sharing prompt.** It breaks flow at exactly the wrong moment. Push it to the footer or cut it entirely from this send.
- 4. **Tease the promo in the subject.** The birthday gift concept is exclusive and time-bound — that's inbox-level urgency that's currently invisible until deep scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, there's a birthday gift waiting for you 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your birthday bonus: 30 days, one promo code`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Claim your special birthday gift with any purchase of 4+ LEGO sets — code inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A birthday gift from us: use your exclusive code before it expires`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — LEGO Birthday Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for a table-based HTML email but has two confirmed compliance gaps (missing one-click unsubscribe headers) and a high URL density in the plain-text part that poses a deliverability risk. The two "broken link" failures in QA are false positives against preconnect hints, not navigable URLs.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**False positives — not real failures:**
The QA checker flagged `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` as broken (404). These are `<link rel="preconnect">` hints in `<head>`, not navigable URLs. Probing them with an HTTP GET produces a non-200 response by design. Exclude preconnect/prefetch targets from link-checking logic.

**Duplicate Google Fonts requests:**
Two separate stylesheet `<link>` tags load Noto Sans Display:
```
/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700
/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@400;700
```
The first is a strict subset of the second — redundant request, adds latency.

**28 tracking links unverified:**
All click-redirect URLs (Salesforce MC / `e.crm.lego.com` click domain) were skipped. Destination URLs and UTM integrity cannot be confirmed from this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text (confirmed, 2 instances):**
```
src: https://image.e.crm.lego.com/lib/fe3b117175640479761271/m/1/
     02_Lifecycle_Birthday-Journey_1_50x50_Offer_v2.png
```
Same image appears twice with no `alt` attribute — likely the desktop/mobile image swap pair (`.mj-image-swap-mobile` / `.mj-image-swap-desktop`). Both instances need descriptive alt text. Screen readers will announce the raw filename.

**Three duplicate `@media (max-width: 599px)` blocks:**
The stylesheet declares the same breakpoint three times. Functionally harmless in most clients but bloats the `<style>` block and creates maintenance risk if rules diverge.

**Good signals:**
- `lang="en"` on `<html>`
- `meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark"` + `supported-color-schemes` — dark mode declared
- `<meta name="viewport">` and IE Edge compat meta present
- `.preheader` class correctly suppressed with `visibility:hidden; opacity:0; height:0`

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Subject line uses `Leo` (recipient first name) — merge token resolved correctly pre-send. No unresolved `{{token}}` or `%%token%%` placeholders visible in the truncated HTML source.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from captured message:**
The AgentMail relay did not surface this header. If it is genuinely missing from the envelope, Gmail/Yahoo's bulk sender requirements (enforced since Feb 2024 for >5K/day senders) are violated. LEGO at this send volume almost certainly includes it at the MTA layer — but the relay should be capturing and surfacing it for verification.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent:**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders. If absent from the actual envelope (not just uncaptured by relay), this is a compliance gap that can affect inbox placement.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured:**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status is unknown through the relay. The sending domain `e.crm.lego.com` should align with a published DMARC policy — cannot confirm without headers.

**Plain-text URL density:**
5,229 of 7,363 plain-text characters (71%) are URLs. This is above the threshold commonly associated with spam scoring in Spamassassin and similar filters. The plain-text part is almost certainly auto-generated from the HTML and not curated.

**`Noreply@` sender address:**
CAN-SPAM requires a valid reply-to mechanism; a `Noreply` address is technically compliant if an unsubscribe path exists, but some ESP policies flag it. Not a hard violation given LEGO's infrastructure.
