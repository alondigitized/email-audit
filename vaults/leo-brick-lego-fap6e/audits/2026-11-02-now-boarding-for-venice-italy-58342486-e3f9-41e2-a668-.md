# Now boarding for Venice, Italy
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a LEGO Insiders early-access send for the new Icons Venetian Neighborhood set. The teal diamond hero with the canal buildings is strong, and "The canals open early for members" is a good headline. The member-exclusive angle is the best idea in the email. The Venice theme also holds together from the subject through the hero to the water taxi gift.
- The offer is buried. The gift-with-purchase (the Venetian Water Taxi, 11/01–11/08) sits in the second block, below a "Shop now" button for the hero. The hero never says what the set costs or which set it is. "Venice is calling, Leo" is warm but vague. The email then adds three modular-building cross-sells, a "revisit the builds" block, two more gift promos and an app banner. That dilutes a send that should be about one set and one gift. The 11/01 start date also makes it feel stale. Leo gets this on 11/02 or later, and the 11/01 early-access start has already passed.
- My judgement is a good hero and a clear member angle, but a diffuse body with two competing CTAs near the top. It is in line with the recent Insiders sends: polished, and not very concrete.

## What's working

- The teal hero with the Venetian buildings in the diamond frame puts the product first and matches the Insiders look from the Dragon Ball send on 11/01.
- "The canals open early for members" and the "Early Access" tag give a clear member benefit.
- The Venetian Water Taxi gift is shown with a date range, 11/01–11/08, so there is a deadline.
- The three modular cards show piece counts and Insiders points (+1495, +1625, +1950). Those are useful details for an AFOL.
- The layout is clean, with no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero and the water taxi block each have their own "Shop now" or "Get yours" button, and neither tells the reader what to do first. The gift mechanic is unclear.
- The hero set is never named in a headline. "The new LEGO Icons Venetian Neighborhood set" is only in the small body copy, and there is no price or piece count.
- The gift fine print is tiny and sits at the bottom. There is no spend threshold or "with purchase of" qualifier near the water taxi.
- Tudor Corner, Shopping Street and Italian Riviera have no price and no link to the Venice story. They read as a generic catalog.
- The "Revisit the builds" block, the two small gift promos and the app banner pull attention away from the Venice launch.
- The start date of 11/01 is already past when this arrives.

## Recommendations

- 1. Merge the hero and the gift into one story: "Venetian Neighborhood is here — get the Water Taxi free." Use one primary CTA, "Get the set + free taxi", and show the price and spend threshold beside it.
- 2. Name the set and give its price and piece count in the hero. Replace the past-tense start date with "Early access ends 11/08."
- 3. Cut the modular cross-sells to one row, or drop them, and move them below a "Complete the city" label.
- 4. Remove the two small gift promos and the app banner from this send, or move them to the footer.
- 5. Show the gift mechanic as a visual: set plus taxi, with "Free with purchase" and the end date.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Venice early access: free Water Taxi, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Venetian set + free boat ends 11/08`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get the new Icons Venetian Neighborhood first, plus a free Water Taxi through 11/08.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access is live for Insiders. See the set, the gift and the points you'll earn.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive MJML-style build with dark-mode meta tags and Google Fonts. The QA data shows widespread missing `alt` text (about 25 flagged instances), an insecure `http://` personalized barcode image, and a plain-text part that is 77% URLs. The compliance warnings are mostly unconfirmed, because the HTML source was truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **51 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The plain-text part is link-heavy.** 10,505 of 13,564 characters (77%) are URLs, which points to long wrapped tracking URLs in the text alternative. This can hurt text-part readability and spam scoring.
- **Insecure image source:**

  - The mixed-content request may be blocked or proxy-rewritten, so the member barcode may not render.
  - The query string `qs` carries an encoded personalization payload, which is exposed in plaintext over HTTP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 25 `<img>` instances.** They fall into two groups:
  - **Content images:** the hero banners `01_112026_Carnival_InsidersEarlyAccess-Hero-Banner-Standard.*` and `04_…-Hero-Banner-Short.png`, plus the `05_…-50x50-ContentCard+3.p…` content cards. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, any message carried by the hero is lost.

If these are decorative, they should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The QA tool can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one, so confirm in the source.
- **`@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans…')` inside `<style>`, plus `<link>` font tags.** Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so the fallback stack (`'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) applies. This is expected behavior, not a defect.
