# Unlock 500 loyalty points with Member Day
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's Insiders "Member Day" send — a loyalty-points bonus event, distinct from the product-launch and themed drops that have dominated recent sends (Klimt 8/2, X-Files 8/2, Pokémon SMART Play 8/1). The hero is clean and legible: a green-on-green diamond pattern, a gift-card graphic stamped "500," "It's here!," and one clear promise — an extra 500 loyalty points on today's order. Compared to 8/7's "Enter if you dare" Halloween teaser, which oversold its hook and underdelivered, this one is refreshingly honest: the offer is stated plainly, has a real deadline ("ends today"), and the CTA does exactly what it says.
- Where it drags is the back half. The "sets are waiting to be built" grid recycles the exact same four products as the Klimt/X-Files/Boba Fett cross-promotion pattern seen in nearly every recent send — Gustav Klimt shows up here for the third time in two weeks. There's no personalization despite the account clearly being logged in (0 points, $0.00 value shown twice), which undercuts the "just for members like you" copy. It reads as competent, on-brand, and low-effort in the personalization layer.
- **What worked:**
- Single, unambiguous hero offer ("EXTRA 500 loyalty points") with a real deadline ("ends today")
- Points/value ledger shown at both top and bottom reinforces this is a loyalty-account email, not a generic promo
- Product grid shows piece count and points-earned per set, which is genuinely useful purchase-decision info for this persona
- Clean visual hierarchy — eye goes hero card → "It's here!" → Shop now button, no competing elements
- **What didn't:**
- Personalization is broken/absent: "Leo" only appears in the tiny footer wallet card, and points shown as 0 twice reads as either accurate (deflating for a "special perk to celebrate your membership") or a data placeholder — either way it undercuts the pitch
- Gustav Klimt – The Kiss is the third appearance in recent sends (8/2 dedicated Insiders drop, and now here) — heavy repetition for a subscriber
- The "Your favorite themes" section (Star Wars, Ideas, Icons, City) has zero copy explaining what clicking it does — generic filler
- No indication of how many points 500 actually equals in dollar value, despite the email showing "$0.00" value right above — a missed chance to make the offer concrete

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or hide the 0 points / $0.00 display — showing a zeroed-out balance directly under "celebrate your membership" copy actively works against the offer; if this is real user data, suppress it when zero, or replace with "earn your first 500 points today."
- 2. Swap Gustav Klimt out of the four-set grid — it's had its own dedicated send 8/2 and repeating it this soon reads as inventory filler, not curation.
- 3. Add a dollar-equivalent to "500 points" in the hero (e.g., "500 points ≈ $5 to spend") — makes an abstract loyalty number concrete and raises perceived value.
- 4. Give "Your favorite themes" a purpose — either link to a real personalized rec ("because you build Star Wars") or cut the section; as-is it's dead weight before the app-download CTA.
- 5. Tighten "ends today" with a specific time (e.g., "ends today at 11:59pm ET") — casual urgency, no shipping deadline pressure.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, your 500 bonus points are live today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Member Day: +500 points on any order, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One order today earns you 500 extra LEGO Insiders points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Member Day perk expires at midnight — here's how it works.`
