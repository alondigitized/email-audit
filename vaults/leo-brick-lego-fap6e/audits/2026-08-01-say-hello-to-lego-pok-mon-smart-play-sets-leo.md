# Say hello to LEGO® Pokémon™ SMART Play™ sets, Leo
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's launch email for the new Pokémon SMART Play line — interactive brick sets that react to movement, positioned as a tech-forward evolution of the standard Pokémon builds. Compared to the SpongeBob Day launch (7/22, 8/10) and the algorithmic "trending sets" send (7/24, 8/10), this is a bigger production: a genuinely new sub-line, not just a seasonal IP tie-in, and the email treats it that way with a dramatic diamond-framed hero shot of a Charizard vs. Jolteon battle set.
- The email stacks a lot into one send — hero launch, two-product carousel, a $130+ gift-with-purchase, a 2x loyalty points push, a livestream replay, and an app cross-promo. That's more blocks than the prior sends in the brand history, and it starts to feel like LEGO tried to consolidate several campaigns into one email rather than sequencing them.
- The execution is clean and on-brand, with the Insiders points strip up top and the membership card at the bottom doing real personalization work. My main knock: no price is shown anywhere, and the movie-night gift comes with a "brick not included" caveat that undercuts the GWP's appeal on a quick read. Solid send, held back from the very top tier by an overloaded body and a slightly foggy secondary offer.

## What's working

- The hero diamond-cutout shot of the Charizard vs. Jolteon SMART Play battle is genuinely eye-catching and communicates "interactive/tech" without needing copy to explain it.
- Insiders personalization is present at both the top (points/value bar) and bottom (barcode membership card with "Leo" printed on it) — a nice bookend.
- Multiple concrete, distinct offers are visible: a $130+ gift-with-purchase (Ditto as Squirtle), 2x loyalty points on select sets, and a livestream replay — this isn't just a bare product push.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, buttons are legible, yellow/black LEGO brand palette is consistent throughout.
- CTA buttons are plentiful and clearly styled ("Shop now," "Learn more," "Find out more," "Watch now") so wherever the reader stops scrolling, there's an obvious next action.

## What's weak

- No price appears anywhere — not on the hero, not on either product tile (only piece count and points are shown). For a purchase-intent email this is a real gap.
- The movie-night GWP has a "SMART Play brick not included" caveat sitting in small print — that's the kind of asterisk that quietly deflates an offer once a reader actually reads it.
- The email runs seven-plus distinct content blocks before the footer; that's a lot of competing CTAs (Shop now ×3, Learn more, Find out more, Watch now, app store badges) with no single dominant next step.
- Subject line names the product line but skips the two things that would actually pull a click — no specific product name (Charizard, Jolteon, Pikachu) and no offer/urgency hook.
- No people or kids in any of the imagery — for a toy brand this is a missed chance to signal "for someone like your family" versus pure product photography.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add price or a "from $X" anchor** to at least the hero and the two product tiles — piece count and points alone don't tell a shopper what they're committing to.
- 2. **Clarify the GWP mechanic in the headline copy itself**, not just fine print — e.g., "free Ditto as Squirtle with $130+" should be the subhead, with the "brick not included" caveat made either clearer or removed if it's genuinely misleading.
- 3. **Trim the email** — cut the livestream and app cross-promo blocks (or move them to a separate send) so the loyalty-points and GWP offers get more visual room to breathe.
- 4. **Sharpen the subject line** to name the actual hero product and add a hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Charizard vs. Jolteon just got interactive, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New LEGO® Pokémon™ SMART Play™ sets are here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: a free Ditto gift with $130+ SMART Play purchases`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Watch it move. Shop the new interactive battle sets.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
