# Be the first to build LEGO® Art Gustav Klimt – The Kiss 💛
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's Insiders early-access email for LEGO Art Gustav Klimt "The Kiss" — a 4,000-piece brick recreation of the famous painting, done in partnership with Vienna's Belvedere Museum. It's a hard pivot from the run of sends in the brand history: X-Files (8/2), Pokémon SMART Play (8/1), and the trending-sets send (7/24) were all franchise/IP-driven, and this is a fine-art adult-collector piece instead. The execution itself is clean — no render bugs, a striking hero image, one clear CTA up top — but it lands the same day as the X-Files Insiders drop per the brand history, which is an aggressive cadence for a subscriber's inbox.
- The hero section does its job: the gold Klimt mosaic against black with a diamond backdrop is genuinely eye-catching and the "Shop now" button is unmissable. Where it loses points is what follows — a coloring-sheet reward and two unrelated cross-sell sets (Tower Bridge, La Catrina) crowd out the "be the first" urgency the subject promises, and there's no actual deadline anywhere in the email to back that urgency up.
- Solid craft, softer targeting. This reads like a content/merchandising send more than a true early-access moment.

## What's working

- The brick-mosaic hero render of "The Kiss" is a strong, on-brand visual hook that communicates the product instantly.
- Single, high-contrast "Shop now" CTA sits directly under the headline with no scroll needed.
- The "belvedere" museum badge adds a credible authenticity/partnership signal for an art crossover product.
- Personalized Insiders membership card at the bottom (Leo's name, barcode, points) reinforces the loyalty program.
- Bonus "Redeem Reward" coloring sheet is a nice zero-cost value-add for members.

## What's weak

- Subject and hero copy pitch a fine-art collector set — a sharp departure from the franchise/IP sends (X-Files, Pokémon, SpongeBob) that have defined recent cadence, so it doesn't clearly hit an established interest area.
- No visible deadline or countdown anywhere — "be the first" and "Early Access" have no actual time-bound to create urgency.
- Below the fold, Tower Bridge and La Catrina cross-sells compete with and dilute the Klimt launch instead of reinforcing it.
- This lands the same day as the X-Files Insiders early-access send per brand history — back-to-back sends risk fatigue.
- The Insiders card shows "0 points / $0.00" — a flat, unexciting personalization moment to close on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real early-access window ("Insiders access ends Aug 9") under the headline — the biggest lever, since urgency is claimed but not shown.
- 2. Drop or heavily de-emphasize the Tower Bridge/La Catrina cross-sell in this send so the Klimt launch owns the email.
- 3. Surface price and the 4,000-piece scale near the hero CTA — currently that detail is buried in body copy, and it's the single best differentiator for this set.
- 4. Replace "Shop now" with a CTA that matches the exclusivity angle, e.g. "Get early access."
- 5. Space this out from the X-Files send — two Insiders "be the first" sends in one day undercuts both.

## Technical Audit: LEGO® Art Gustav Klimt – The Kiss (Early Access Email)

**1. Technical Summary**
Email is a standard MJML/table-based HTML build from Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`e.crm.lego.com` / `cl.s50.exct.net`) with responsive media queries; automated QA flagged missing authentication/unsubscribe headers, widespread missing alt attributes, and one non-HTTPS image resource.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 35 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for ESP click-tracking domains — not itself a defect, but means link-destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Plain-text alternative is 6,265/8,391 characters (~75%) raw URLs, which increases spam-filter risk and degrades the plain-text fallback experience for clients that render it.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 13 `<img>` instances across the template are missing `alt` text, including the header logo (`LEGOInsiderLogowhitelog.png`), the hero banner (`Project-Gold-...-Hero-Banner-Standard.png`, appears twice), a reward content block image (appears twice), two product thumbnails (`bltdc033ed672251563-21067_Prod_en-gb.png`, `bltcfca1e7974173b84-21372_Prod_en-gb.png`), decorative footer elements (`GreyBrick.png` ×3, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` ×3), footer separator graphics (`Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` ×3), and the mobile footer logo (`Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`). This affects screen-reader users and clients that block images (alt text would otherwise convey content).
- One image ("Insider Barcode," a `LiveContent.aspx` dynamic image at `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...`) is served over plain HTTP rather than HTTPS, risking mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-only mail clients.
- Head includes Google Fonts `@import` and `<link>` calls (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) — most major mail clients (Outlook, Gmail) strip external font loading, so these will silently fall back to the `LEGO Typewell, Noto Sans, Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif` stack; not a bug but worth confirming the fallback stack renders acceptably.
- MSO conditional comments and mobile media queries (`max-width: 599px`) are present, indicating standard Outlook/mobile handling — no structural issues found in the truncated markup.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g., AMPscript `%%=...=%%` or `{{...}}`) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. No issues found — note this section is limited by source truncation and should be re-checked against the full payload if personalization is expected.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than an actual absence; needs verification against raw SMTP headers at the source.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected, meaning Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe compliance (required as of 2024 bulk-sender rules) cannot be confirmed from this capture.
- No physical mailing address detected in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM §7704(a)(5) requires one; confirm whether it exists in the untruncated footer (LEGO emails typically include this) since the HTML was cut off before the footer's address block was reached.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture — this is most likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than an authentication failure, but should be verified independently if deliverability is in question.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Cannot be verified: all 35 outbound links use ESP click-tracking redirects (`cl.s50.exct.net`/similar) that were skipped by the prober, so destination URLs, UTM parameters, and final landing-page alignment are not available in this dataset. Recommend resolving a sample of tracked links to confirm they land on the intended LEGO Art / Klimt product page with consistent UTM tagging (source/medium/campaign).

**7. Recommendations**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 13 flagged images, particularly the hero banner and reward content block, which carry primary campaign messaging.
- Replace the `http://` `LiveContent.aspx` barcode image with an HTTPS source to avoid mixed-content blocking.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist in raw SMTP headers (outside the relay capture) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
