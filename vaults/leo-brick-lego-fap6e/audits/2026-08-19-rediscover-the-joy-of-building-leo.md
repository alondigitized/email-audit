- This is LEGO's "adults who build" pitch — Botanicals hero, "Awaken your Joy," then a four-up product grid spanning Halloween, Star Wars-adjacent arcade, McLaren-tier supercar, and florals. It's the widest net LEGO has cast in this recent stretch, and that's the problem: unlike 8/12's Harry Potter teaser or 8/7's Halloween send, there's no single theme or urgency hook, just "here's stuff, some of it might be yours." The member-gift banner (Restaurants of the World: Greece, spend $180+, 08/11–08/20) is the only concrete, time-bound offer on the page, and it's buried two-thirds down after four product cards.

## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage anywhere in the scroll.
- Botanicals hero image is well-composed and visually distinct from the typical minifig/set-box hero LEGO usually runs.
- Member-gift banner has a real, dated offer (spend $180+, 08/11–08/20, while supplies last) with a visible product image.
- Loyalty card and barcode at the footer is a nice utility touch for a physical-store visit.
- Personalization present ("Leo" on the loyalty card, member point balance shown).

## What's weak

- No actual discount or promo anywhere — "Shop now" x5 with zero price incentive, same weakness as 8/18's send.
- Product grid is thematically scattered (Halloween pumpkin, sci-fi pinball, supercar, flower bouquet) — no organizing idea ties them together, so nothing signals "this is for you."
- The one dated, member-gift offer is the least visually prominent block on the page — smaller and lower than the plain product cards above it.
- "Here's something just for you" claims personalization but the four sets share no obvious link to a single adult-builder profile.
- Points balance shown as 0 / $0.00 with no path forward (no "earn X points on your next order") — a missed nudge for a loyalty-forward brand.

- 2. Cut the product grid to one theme (or explicitly label sections by builder interest — "Botanicals," "Vehicles," "Seasonal") so "just for you" earns its claim.
- 3. Give the 0-point Insiders member a reason to act — e.g., "Earn 500 points toward your next reward" tied to the CTA.
