# Check out our latest and greatest sets, Leo
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic "browse our catalog" send with no offer, no discount, no urgency — just a product-recommendation deck stitched to a loyalty-points explainer, a wish-list nudge, and a co-build cross-sell for Stitch & Scrump. It's structurally similar to 2026-08-18's "Room for a new build in your life?" (also a 5.00/10, also offer-less), and the brand is now running this exact "no deal, just discovery" pattern for the second time in a week.
- The bigger problem is execution, not just strategy: both product cards in the hero carousel are missing their product names and prices, and every CTA pill in the email (the one under the product cards, the one under the Membership Tip photo) renders as a blank white oval with no visible label. That's not a soft-sell choice, that's a broken send — a recipient can't even tell what they'd be clicking on.
- Points/Value both showing "0 / $0.00" twice in the render (header and barcode footer) reads as a real account state, not a mock, which undercuts the "member" framing this email leans on. Between the missing offer, the blank buttons, and the flat personalization, this lands at the bottom of the recent LEGO stretch — well below the Hogwarts teaser (7/10) and even below the other no-offer send.

## What's working

- The hero photo (a kid mid-build, sunlit, genuine) is warm and on-brand, consistent with LEGO's "adults/kids who build" visual language.
- The green diamond-brick pattern framing the hero photo carries the brand's visual identity cleanly.
- The "Reward yourself in a few simple steps" module clearly numbers out how points redemption works — good use-case education for a member who hasn't redeemed yet.
- Theme icon row (Star Wars, Icons, City, Ninjago) at the bottom gives a scannable way to jump to a category of interest.
- Membership Tip and Stitch & Scrump sections are visually distinct blocks (dark green vs. light blue), so the email doesn't blur into one long scroll.

## What's weak

- Both hero product cards are missing product names and prices — just a plant/topiary set and a Winter Village-style set floating with stray icon glyphs (coin purse, vase) and no text.
- The primary CTA pill directly under the product cards is blank — no visible button copy at all.
- The CTA pill under the Membership Tip photo is also blank.
- No offer anywhere in the email — no %, no $, no BOGO, no free shipping; every other module is either educational or a soft nudge to browse.
- "Points 0 / Value $0.00" appears twice (top banner and barcode footer), which for a "member" makes this feel like it's addressing an account, not a person with actual reward status.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the blank-button render bug first — every CTA pill needs visible label text before anything else about this email matters. This alone is likely suppressing clicks across the whole send.
- 2. Add product name + price to both hero cards. Right now there's no way to know what's being sold or what it costs.
- 3. Anchor the send to an actual offer or moment — a %-off, a bundle price, or at minimum a "new this week" framing — rather than an unprompted browse nudge, especially two sends in a row with the same non-offer structure.
- 4. If this account genuinely has 0 points, don't lead with a "member" points banner that highlights $0.00 value — either suppress that module for zero-balance members or replace it with a "start earning" CTA tied to a specific first purchase incentive.
- 5. Trim the email — five distinct modules (products, rewards steps, membership tip, Stitch & Scrump, app download) with no unifying offer makes this seven scrolls of soft-touch marketing with no clear single action.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MJML/SFMC-generated LEGO promo email with responsive media queries and MSO conditional fixes; several compliance and accessibility gaps surfaced by automated QA (missing unsubscribe headers, no physical address, unlabeled images, one non-HTTPS asset).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 55 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for redirect domains — not itself a defect, but means link destinations are unverified in this pass).
- One image is served over plain HTTP: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5Nzl9...` ("Insider Barcode" dynamic content pixel). Mixed-content HTTP resources are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Outlook, Gmail proxy) and should be served via HTTPS.
- No broken/malformed href values found in the truncated source provided.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images across the email are missing `alt` attributes, including the hero (`01-Trending-Now-August-Email-Hero.png`), product cards (`02-Trending-Now-August-Email-Card1/3/4.jpg`), and structural/footer images (`GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`, `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`). This degrades the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail), leaving no fallback text.
- Markup uses legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if lte mso 11]>`) and standard responsive breakpoint at 599px — consistent with typical Outlook/webmail-safe patterns, no structural issues found.
- `<style>` block references Google Fonts via both `<link>` and `@import` — redundant loading of the same font family (`Noto Sans Display`) via two different `<link>` tags with overlapping weight sets (`wght@700` and `wght@400;700`); not a functional bug but unnecessary duplicate network requests.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No issues found in the visible HTML fragment; full body not available to confirm personalization elsewhere.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — either absent from the message or not captured by the relay. Without it, mailbox providers cannot surface a native one-click unsubscribe UI.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe (POST) is not confirmed supported; Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (effective 2024) mandate this for high-volume senders.
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the truncated content — CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. This may exist in the untruncated footer; recommend confirming against the full source.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay; this is a QA-tooling visibility gap rather than a confirmed authentication failure, but should be verified independently against raw headers at the receiving MTA.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable from the truncated source/QA data — the 55 tracking links were skipped by the prober, so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable only for purely decorative assets like spacer/separator PNGs; content images like the hero and product cards need meaningful alt text).
2. Replace the HTTP `LiveContent.aspx` barcode image reference with an HTTPS URL.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in raw SMTP headers (may be relay-stripped rather than genuinely absent) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
4. Confirm a physical mailing address is present in the full footer (not visible in truncated source) and retest with complete HTML.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against raw headers from the sending MTA rather than relying on the AgentMail relay capture.
6. Remove the duplicate/redundant Google Fonts `<link>` declaration for `Noto Sans Display` to reduce unnecessary requests.
7. Re-run link/UTM validation against the untruncated HTML with tracking-redirect resolution enabled to confirm destination and UTM continuity.
