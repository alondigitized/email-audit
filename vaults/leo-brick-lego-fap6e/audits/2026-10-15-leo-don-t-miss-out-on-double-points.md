# Leo, don’t miss out on double points
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty reminder that repeats the double-points offer from 10/13 ("Earn double points on every set, Leo"). Only the deadline phrasing has changed. The hero is strong: a green diamond frame, a "2x" badge, the headline "Ready to earn double?", a clear "Only until 10/19" deadline and a "Shop now" button.
- The body of the email is broken. Four white product cards sit under the hero. Each one shows a small broken-image icon, a LEGO build pushed to one side, and no product name, price or link text. The email promises a reason to shop and then gives no specific set to click. This is the same broken-render pattern as 10/08 and 09/23.
- Below the cards is a generic "Membership Tip" block with icons and category tiles, then an app-download panel and a loyalty card. The Insiders header and loyalty card show 0 points and $0.00. For a member with no balance, "double points" has little pull. Verdict: a good hero and a weak, broken middle. It is a repeat of a send from two days ago.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. The "2x" badge, the question headline and the green frame make the offer clear at a glance.
- The deadline is explicit: "Only until 10/19", with the "next four days" framing.
- "Shop now" is a visible outlined button directly under the offer copy.
- The Insiders header and the loyalty card at the bottom make the member benefit obvious.
- The green brand frame and the LEGO Insiders branding are consistent with the earlier sends.

## What's weak

- The four product cards have large empty white space and tiny broken-image icons. The builds (Batmobile, SpongeBob pineapple, a modular set, the Klimt-style art piece) float off to one side. There is no name, price or points-earned label on any of them.
- Nothing in the product section tells you what to click. There is no per-product CTA and no visible product detail.
- This repeats the 10/13 send two days later. A subscriber sees the same offer again, and the 10/11 "latest sets" send was also in the same week.
- The points balance reads 0 / $0.00 in two places. This undercuts the "earn double" pitch.
- The lower half is generic: the Membership Tip icons, the category tiles ("Retiring Soon", "Star Wars™", "LEGO® Icons", "New") and the app badges dilute the single offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product modules. Give each card a centered image, a set name, a price and "Earn X points" text, with the whole card linked. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a personalized hook. For a 0-point member, use "Start your balance with double points" instead of "Ready to earn double?".
- 3. Cut the Membership Tip and the app-download block. Keep the hero, a fixed product grid and one closing CTA.
- 4. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Shop sets, earn 2x".
- 5. Don't resend the same offer within two days. Either use this as the reminder and suppress it for people who already purchased, or change the creative.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next set earns double, Leo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four days left to earn 2x Insiders points on any set.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Batmobile, SpongeBob and more all count. Ends 10/19.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound: it has a `lang="en"` attribute, a viewport meta tag, responsive media queries, and a preheader class. QA flagged missing alt text on many images and an insecure `http://` personalized barcode image. The compliance and authentication warnings may come from the AgentMail relay rather than the sender, so they are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- **Plain-text part is 12,480 of 14,855 characters URLs (about 84%).** This is consistent with long tracking-wrapped links. It is a mild deliverability and readability risk, not a defect on its own.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image source (2 occurrences):** the "Insider Barcode" image loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwMzAs...`. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may hide it. It is a dynamic LiveContent URL, so the barcode may fail to render. It does have alt text.
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - Hero: `261015_Q4_2_PointsOctober_Reminder_Hero-Banner-Standard.jpg` (2 occurrences).
  - Content cards: `20261015_Q4_2_PointsOctober_Reminder_3xGrid-ContentCard+1.png`, `+2.png` and `+3.png` (2 occurrences each). If these are the main content or CTA images, images-off and screen-reader users get nothing.
- **Missing alt text on likely-decorative images:** `GreyBrick.png` (8 occurrences), `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (3 occurrences) and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`. If decorative, these should carry an explicit `alt=""`. An absent attribute makes some screen readers read out the filename.
- **Web fonts:** the `<link>` tags and `@import` to `fonts.googleapis.com` are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stack falls back to `'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`, so there is no functional break.
- No other rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject line renders the first name ("Leo, …"). No unresolved tokens or placeholders appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may not capture these headers, so treat them as **unconfirmed**. Re-check against the raw message headers. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `e.crm.lego.com`.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **could not be verified**. All 61 tracking links were skipped and the destination URLs are hidden behind redirect domains. Nothing in the source shows a continuity defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero banner and the three `3xGrid-ContentCard` images.
2. Set `alt=""` on the decorative images: `GreyBrick.png`, the footer separators and the footer logo.
3. Serve the Insider Barcode LiveContent image over `https://`, or confirm the ExactTarget/SFMC endpoint supports it, so it isn't blocked.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Fix them only if they are actually absent.
5. Resolve a sample of the tracking links manually to confirm the redirect destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. Optionally trim the plain-text alternative or add readable link labels to improve its text-to-URL ratio.
