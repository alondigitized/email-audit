# Last day for double points, Leo
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the third double-points push in seven days: "Earn double points on every set, Leo" (10/13), "Leo, don't miss out on double points" (10/15), and now "Last day for double points, Leo". The subject and hero do the job. The green diamond frame, the 2x badge and "Don't miss out on double." make the offer clear, and "Last day" is credible because the promo ends 10/19.
- The lower half of the render is broken. All four product cards are almost entirely blank white boxes. I can see a small placeholder icon in each, and the product images (Star Destroyer, Elf, Poké Ball, Catrina) sit stranded at the edges. No product names, prices or CTAs are visible. This is the same failure pattern as the 10/08 Halloween send (4/10). The email has nothing between the generic "Shop now" and the footer to turn urgency into a specific purchase.
- Verdict: a good reminder idea that's let down by the render. The subject would earn an open, but the body sends people to the homepage, so clicks stay modest.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The diamond-framed photo, the 2x badge and "Don't miss out on double." put the offer first.
- "Last day" is a credible, specific deadline that matches the 10/13–10/19 window.
- "Leo" personalization in the subject, plus the Insiders header with a points and value counter, reinforces the loyalty angle.
- The "Shop now" outlined button is clearly visible, and the green and white palette is consistent with earlier sends.
- The category tiles (Retiring Soon, Star Wars™, LEGO® Icons, New) and the app download block give secondary paths.

## What's weak

- The four product cards are blank white boxes with placeholder icons. No names, prices or CTAs show, and the images are misaligned.
- The only CTA is a generic "Shop now" with no product or collection target.
- This is the third near-identical double-points message in a week, which is cadence fatigue.
- The header and footer show "Points 0 / Value $0.00". That undercuts the "earn double" motivation when the member has nothing banked.
- The offer copy never says what double points is worth. The "*For full terms" link in the footer has no matching asterisk in the body.
- The category tiles are tiny and the labels are weak. "New" is vague, and nothing says the 2x applies to them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product cards. Make sure each card renders a name, a "2x points" tag and a CTA, and add a fallback for when images or text fail to load. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Make "Shop now" specific, for example "Shop sets, earn 2x", and link each product card to its own page.
- 3. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM") and spell out the value ("2 points per $1 = $X back on a $100 set").
- 4. Add real points context for members with a zero balance, such as "Your first order earns X points, worth $Y".
- 5. Collapse the sequence. Send one launch email and one last-chance email, not three.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 2x points on every set, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, your double points expire at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn twice the Insiders points on any set, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Star Wars, Icons and Pokémon sets are all 2x until midnight.`

The QA run found missing alt text on 20 images, an HTTP image source, and no captured compliance or auth headers. The 38% pass rate shows 5 warnings and 0 hard failures. The header warnings may come from the AgentMail relay rather than from the sender.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 55 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Their destinations are unverified.
- Plain text is 15,280 of 18,146 characters URLs (about 84%). Long tracking URLs are inflating the text part, which can raise spam-filter scores. Short or unwrapped links would help.
- Two instances of `<img>` "Insider Barcode" use `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=…`. This is a non-HTTPS source, which some clients block or flag as mixed content, so the barcode may not render. The domain is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) dynamic-content endpoint.
