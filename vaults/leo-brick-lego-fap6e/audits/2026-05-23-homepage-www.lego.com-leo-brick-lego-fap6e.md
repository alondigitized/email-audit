# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened lego.com on my phone and got hit with an entry modal before I even saw a single product — a dual-path gate asking me to either continue into the main shop or veer off to "The Play Zone" for games and videos. This instantly signals the brand is straddling kids and AFOLs without committing to either. As a VIP member hunting double-VIP weekends, I'm already annoyed: my first tap has to be a formality, not a product discovery moment.

## What's working

- **Clean modal layout:** The two-path structure (shop vs. Play Zone) is visually distinct. I know exactly what the "Continue" button does without guessing.
- **No visual clutter:** The blue/yellow color blocking is classic LEGO brand — no broken images, no lorem ipsum, no layout chaos. Renders fine on mobile.
- **Cookie notice is unobtrusive:** It sits below the modal at readable font size without covering the CTA — that's better than a full-screen consent wall.

## What's weak

- **Entry gate adds zero value for a returning VIP:** I'm already on the site. This modal delays my path to the Botanical Collection or any current VIP offer by one mandatory tap. LEGO knows who I am from my account — why am I being routed through a generic "you're about to enter" screen?
- **Play Zone pitch is completely off-target for me:** The "Play games, watch videos and more!" section with its kid-coded minifig characters is noise. I'm 38 and here to check if the Icons Orchid finally has a VIP event.
- **Zero commercial intent above the fold:** No set, no offer, no price, no "Double VIP points this weekend" — nothing that would make me move faster. The homepage's job is to convert interest into a tap; this modal does none of that.
- **"START PLAYING" CTA competes visually with "Continue":** Two CTAs at nearly equal visual weight creates a micro-moment of decision fatigue for no upside (for my use case).

## Recommendations

- **Auto-skip this modal for logged-in VIP members** and route them directly to a personalized landing page showing their points balance + current VIP promotions — zero extra tap, immediate signal that LEGO knows them.
- **Replace the generic modal copy** ("You can shop, get support & more") with something campaign-specific: "Double VIP points this weekend — shop the Botanical Collection" would have me tapping Continue in under a second.
- **Push the Play Zone to a secondary nav tab**, not as a peer CTA to the main shop path. The current layout implies both paths are equally relevant — they're not for adult shoppers.
- **Inject a single loyalty hook into the modal** for recognized users — even a small "Welcome back, Leo — 2,450 VIP points" banner under the logo would be enough to confirm I'm in the right place.
