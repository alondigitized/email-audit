# Leo, the latest LEGO® sets are here for you to discover!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic monthly "new sets" roundup. It is clean and on-brand, but it gives a recipient little reason to act. The green Insiders frame, the four hero builds (PlayStation, Downton Abbey, Holiday winter house, Poké Ball) and the points widget at the top all render properly. Nothing is broken, which is an improvement on the broken-render run from 9/23 and 9/25.
- The product grid is the weak point. All four tiles are white boxes with a picture and two small icons, and no product name, price or set number. A recipient can't tell what any of these cost or what they're called. The same four builds were sold as Early Access hero sends on 10/02 ("Leo, celebrate the new LEGO® PlayStation™", "Leo, we've been expecting you") and 10/03 ("Build festive magic early!"). Those sends had a focused story and this one doesn't. The "Coming soon: 2x loyalty points" offer is the only concrete incentive, and it's buried below the fold, with an asterisk and a future start date.
- Verdict: a safe, forgettable digest. It's a mid-to-low 4–5 send, well below the 10/02 high of 8.

## What's working

- The render is intact, with no broken images, overlapping text or lorem ipsum.
- The Insiders header with Points and Value (0 / $0.00) personalizes the email and signals membership.
- The product photography is strong. The PlayStation and Poké Ball are recognizable, aspirational builds.
- The "Coming soon: 2x loyalty points! … October 13–19" band gives a dated, specific incentive.
- The app-download block and the membership card with barcode are tidy and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The product tiles have no names, prices or per-tile CTAs. Two small unlabeled icons (a box and a points bag) carry no information.
- There is one generic "Shop now" under the grid and a second one in the membership section. Neither names a category or set.
- The hero copy, "Explore the latest sets this month," is generic, and the body copy "exciting new sets in store for you" is filler.
- The 2x points offer is below the fold and starts October 13, so it can't drive action today. It is also marked with an asterisk.
- The category row (Star Wars, Icons, Ideas, City) is small and unlinked-looking. The tiles are low-contrast labels, not clear CTAs.
- There is no urgency or reason why these four sets were picked for this recipient.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 2x points offer in the hero, e.g. "Double points start Oct 13," and tie the four sets to it. That gives the email one concrete offer.
- 2. Add a set name, a price and a points-earned line to each tile, and make every tile its own deep link.
- 3. Replace the two "Shop now" buttons with specific CTAs such as "Build the PlayStation" or "Shop Icons sets."
- 4. Cut the "Get the most out of your membership" block, or shrink it, since the stock photo and filler copy add nothing.
- 5. Segment the sets by past behavior, such as Icons vs. Star Wars, instead of sending everyone the same four.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, MJML-style HTML 4.01 Transitional build with a resolved personalized subject ("Leo,"). The QA run flagged widespread missing image alt text, an insecure `http://` personalized barcode image, and compliance and authentication headers that could not be verified. The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text weight:** 11,520 of 14,624 characters (about 79%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This exceeds the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the text version and can hurt spam scoring and readability for text-only clients.
- **Mixed content:** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over plain HTTP: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwMjYs…`. The QA data confirms this twice, so it probably appears in both the desktop and mobile variants. Clients and proxies that enforce HTTPS may block it, which would leave a blank space where the member barcode should be.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** Alt text is missing on about 35 image instances, including these:
  - Product images, e.g. `72306_Prod.png`, `21373_Prod.png`, `11387_Prod.png` and `blt3435be8c14df0276-72154_Prod_en-gb.png`, all on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…`. These carry content, so missing alt text is a real accessibility defect.
  - Content and banner images: `20261011_Q4_Novelties-50x50-ContentCard+1.png` and `20261231_Q4_ComingSoon1HYNovelties-Slim-Banner.png`. A banner like this usually carries meaning that image-blocked clients will not show.
  - Footer images: `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark`.
  - `GreyBrick.png` and `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, repeated six times each. These look decorative and need `alt=""` to be explicitly marked as such.
- **Duplicated modules:** Several images repeat exactly (`72306_Prod`, `21373_Prod`, `11387_Prod`, `72154_Prod`). This is consistent with separate desktop and mobile blocks toggled by `.show-on-desktop`, `.hide-on-desktop`, `.show_on_mobile` and `.hide_on_mobile`. That increases HTML weight, and screen readers may announce content twice if the hidden variants rely only on CSS.
- **Web fonts:** The `@import` and `<link>` references to `fonts.googleapis.com` (Noto Sans Display) are stripped or ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stack (`LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) is present, so degradation is acceptable.
- **Title:** `<title>LEGO</title>` is generic. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders "Leo," with no unresolved tokens, and none are visible in the truncated source. The personalized barcode is served from `cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx`, a dynamic content endpoint. Its HTTP delivery is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found, so RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping headers. This is therefore not a confirmed sender defect, and the raw message headers should be checked.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay, as above.
- **Footer content:** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated source, so they cannot be assessed.
- **From address:** The From address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`. The sending subdomain is aligned, but a no-reply address offers no reply path. That is not a legal issue, though.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The source is truncated and all 61 links were redirect-wrapped and not probed. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed. Product images point to `www.lego.com` CDN assets, so the destinations are presumably on-domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product, banner and content images. This covers the `*_Prod.png` set, `20261011_Q4_Novelties-50x50-ContentCard+1.png` and `20261231_Q4_ComingSoon1HYNovelties-Slim-Banner.png`. Set `alt=""` on decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, the footer separators and the points icon).
2. Serve the Insider Barcode image over `https://`. Check that the `cl.s50.exct.net` LiveContent endpoint supports TLS, and update the `src`.
3. Capture the raw message headers outside the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass).
4. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 61 tracking links. Confirm landing pages return 200 and that UTM or campaign parameters survive to the destination.
5. Reduce the plain-text URL ratio below 70%. Shorten tracking links or trim the link-heavy modules in the text part.
6. Add `aria-hidden` or `role="presentation"` handling to the duplicated desktop and mobile variants, so assistive tech does not read the content twice.
