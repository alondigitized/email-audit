# Here comes Santa Claus...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal LEGO Insiders catalogue send. The Santa hero in the green and yellow diamond frame is the strongest element and fits "Here comes Santa Claus..." well. But nothing here gives Leo a reason to act now. There is no discount, no deadline and no points multiplier. After the 10/13, 10/15 and 10/19 double-points sends, this one swings to pure merchandising.
- The structure is tidy: hero, "Bricks of the season", "Bet you'll love these", "Reward yourself", the app promo and the Insiders card. It also repeats the same blocks. The Santa hero build reappears as "Santa's Holiday Countdown", and there are five "Shop now" buttons with identical copy. The Insiders points badge shows 0 points and $0.00 twice. For a member that reads as empty, and it undercuts the loyalty angle. The render is intact, which is a relief after the broken sends on 10/08, 09/23 and 09/25.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand holiday browse email that will get opened on brand trust and little else. It is a 5 out of 10.

## What's working

- The Santa hero in the diamond frame is eye-catching, and the eye lands on it first.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or layout bugs.
- Product cards show piece counts and "+390 / +260 / +780 Insiders Points" earn values, which ties purchases to the loyalty program.
- The "Reward yourself" section gives redeemable items with point costs (1700, 500), a real loyalty hook.
- The seasonal timing is right, with Santa, Elf and Holiday House picks in late October.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer or deadline. The copy is "New ways to bring festive cheer" with no price, percent or date.
- Five identical "Shop now" CTAs don't say which product or action each one leads to.
- The points widget reads "Points 0 / Value $0.00" twice, which makes the member look inactive. "Redeem reward" buttons for 1700 and 500 points are unreachable at 0 points.
- The Santa hero and "Santa's Holiday Countdown" look like the same build shown twice, so the content feels padded.
- The subject doesn't name a product or offer, and it has no personalization, unlike the "Leo" sends earlier this month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real hook. Use a dated early-access window, a gift-with-purchase threshold, or a "points on holiday sets" multiplier, and put it in the hero headline.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop the Holiday House", "Get Mrs. Claus", "Redeem 500 points".
- 3. Hide the points and rewards blocks when the member balance is 0, or swap them for "Earn your first points on these sets."
- 4. Cut the duplicate Santa tile and add a different build.
- 5. Personalize the subject with the recipient's name, as the last four "Leo" sends did.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, Santa's Holiday Countdown is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Santa, Elf and Mrs. Claus: earn bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet the not-so-mini minifigures, plus 780 Insiders Points on the Holiday House.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Festive builds are in. Earn points on every set this season.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (responsive media queries, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). Automated QA scored a 38% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 5 warnings. The main code-level gaps are missing `alt` attributes on about 35 image instances, an `http://` image source, and compliance signals that could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 55 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- Plain-text part is 15744/20023 characters (~79%) URLs, above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter and readability risk, and it is consistent with long tracked URLs.
- No broken links were reported, but the skipped links mean that is not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on images.** The QA data lists these (many repeat because of mobile/desktop duplicate blocks):
  - Hero banner: `01_Holiday_Email-2_Holiday-SKUs_Hero-Banner-Standard.gif` (2x). It is the primary content image and has no text alternative.
  - Product images: `bltf1df1459ba3b1736-40875_Prod_en-gb.png`, `blt2b3eb4df7154d352-40865_Prod_en-gb.png`, `11387_Prod.png`, `blt6caf97af32ad6473-40874_Prod_en-gb.png`.
  - Footer and decorative images: `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` (many repeats), `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png`, `Logo+Container+logo+white.png`.
  - Decorative images should use `alt=""` explicitly. Hero and product images need descriptive alt text. With images blocked, the hero and products render with no fallback text.
- **Non-HTTPS image.** The "Insider Barcode" image loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...` (listed 2x). Mixed-content or secure-proxy clients may block it, which would leave the loyalty barcode missing.
- **Doctype.** The source uses HTML 4.01 Transitional, and a comment (`<!-- -->`) precedes it. A comment before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients. This is a minor risk and not confirmed to cause problems.
- **Web fonts.** Fonts load through `@import` and `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, but the stack falls back to `'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`, so no defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens. The "Insider Barcode" is a dynamic LiveContent image, and its `alt` text is present.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements are not demonstrably met.
- **No physical mailing address detected.** The HTML is truncated, so the address may be in the footer. It is unconfirmed, and the footer should be checked in the full source.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. The sender is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`, a `noreply` address, and the domain's alignment could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing page alignment cannot be verified. The 55 links are click-tracking redirects that were skipped, so final destinations and campaign parameters are not visible. Product images do load from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...` (set IDs 40875, 40865, 11387, 40874). Without the landing URLs, that cannot be matched to destination pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF and the four product images. Set `alt=""` on `GreyBrick.png`, the footer separator and the other decorative images.
2. Serve the Insider Barcode LiveContent image over `https://`.
3. Reduce plain-text URL density by shortening tracked URLs or adding more descriptive text to the plain-text part.
4. Check the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). If the relay strips them, capture headers another way before treating them as defects.
5. Confirm that the full HTML footer includes a physical address.
6. Resolve the tracked redirects, for example with a manual or header-only follow, to verify UTM parameters and landing pages.
