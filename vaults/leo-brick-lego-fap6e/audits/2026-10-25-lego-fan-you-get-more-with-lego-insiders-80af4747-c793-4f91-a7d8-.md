# LEGO® Fan, you get more with LEGO® Insiders
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic Insiders membership reminder, and it undersells itself. The green hero with the woman carrying a stack of LEGO boxes is warm and on-brand. "MMMOOOOOORE..." is a fun brand-voice moment. But the email never says what "more" is. There is no number, no date and no product. The whole send runs on "a whole lot more" and "stacks of rewards."
- The persona is a member with 0 points and $0.00 value, shown twice in the header and the footer card. The email tells a member "you're already a member" and then shows them an empty balance. That's a missed chance. An email to a zero-balance member should say how to earn the first points, not repeat the value prop. The three benefit tiles are also weak. Each is a flat icon with one small line of text, and none has a link or a button. The one real CTA, "Explore now," is vague and sits above the fold with nothing specific behind it.
- Compared with the recent sends, this is the weakest. The 10/24 Downton Abbey launch (8/10) had a hero product and a clear reason to act. The 10/13–10/19 double-points run had a concrete offer and a deadline. This one has neither, so it reads as filler between launches. Judgement: pleasant, safe and low-value. It will get some opens on brand trust, but few clicks.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and human. The smiling woman holding a stack of sets in the green diamond frame matches the Insiders look from 10/13 and 10/15.
- The headline "Shopping with LEGO® Insiders gets you MMMOOOOOORE..." has personality and fits the brand voice.
- The layout is clean and scannable. The green hero, the white tiles and the dark footer separate clearly, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The points and value header, with the barcode card in the footer, reinforces membership and makes the program feel tangible.
- The app download block with App Store and Google Play badges is clear and gives a usable secondary action.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer. The email has no %, no $, no points multiplier and no deadline. "More rewards" and "stacks of one-of-a-kind rewards" are vague.
- The primary CTA "Explore now" is generic and doesn't say what it opens.
- The three benefit tiles are large and mostly empty. The icons are big, the text is tiny and none of them is clickable. One of them, "Win your wishlist** when you enter our holiday draw," is the only real hook, and it carries an asterisk with no visible terms near it.
- The balance shows 0 points and $0.00, twice, which undercuts "you get more." The body also tells the recipient they're already a member, so nothing is aimed at getting them to earn.
- The mobile subject uses "LEGO® Fan" as a stand-in for a name, which reads as a failed merge. The 10/24 and 10/22 subjects used "Leo."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the one real hook, the holiday wishlist draw. Put the entry deadline in the hero and make the CTA "Enter the holiday draw."
- 2. Make the tiles useful. Give each a short headline, a link and a number, such as "Earn X points per $1" or "Redeem 500 points for a free set."
- 3. Use the empty balance. For a 0-point member, add a "Earn your first points" module with the quickest way to start, such as a purchase, a review or the app.
- 4. Replace "Explore now" with a verb plus a noun that names the destination, such as "See my rewards" or "Enter the draw."
- 5. Fix the greeting. Use the first name, or drop the fallback entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, win your wishlist this holiday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Enter the LEGO® holiday draw, Leo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter the holiday draw and see what your points can unlock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders perks, rewards and a chance to win your wishlist.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Mostly clean, responsive MJML-style markup from an Exact Target / Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`exct.net`) send. The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes on several images and a non-HTTPS dynamic barcode image. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header and footer data this audit couldn't see, so they aren't confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 19 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Insecure image URL:** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over plain HTTP: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNDAs…`. It appears twice, likely as desktop and mobile variants. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop it. That image is functional (a member barcode), not decorative.
- No other broken or malformed links are evidenced.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images** (all on `image.e.crm.lego.com/lib/fe3b…/m/1/`):
  - `Static+Hero.png` (2×)
  - `M3+icon+1.png`, `M3+icon+2.png`, `M3+icon+3.png` (2× each)
  - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (3×)
  - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
  - The repeats are probably desktop/mobile swap pairs (`.mj-image-swap-mobile` / `.mj-image-swap-desktop` in the CSS). Each variant still needs `alt`. If the images are decorative, they need an explicit `alt=""`. The hero and icons likely carry meaning, so they need descriptive text.
- **Web font loading:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans…')` and the `<link>` tags are ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The font stack falls back to `Arial, sans-serif`, so this is low risk.
- **Pre-DOCTYPE comment:** The source starts with `<!-- -->` before `<!DOCTYPE …>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some older Outlook/IE-based renderers. Low severity.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared, but the truncated source shows no `prefers-color-scheme` rules. The `_Dark` footer image naming suggests it may be handled elsewhere. Unverified.
- **Accessibility:** `<html lang="en">` is present, but `<title>` is a generic "LEGO".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. The subject's "LEGO® Fan" looks like a generic fallback and not a broken token. The barcode relies on a personalized `LiveContent.aspx?qs=…` call. Whether it renders for a non-member test inbox is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unconfirmed, and check against the raw original message.
- **Physical address:** The check found none. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer wasn't visible. Verify this against the full source before treating it as a violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** Absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. This is a relay limitation.
- **From domain:** `e.crm.lego.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment can't be confirmed without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 19 tracked links were not resolved, and the visible source doesn't show destination URLs or UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the hero and the three M3 icons (descriptive). Add `alt=""` to the footer separator and logo images if they are decorative.
2. Serve the `LiveContent.aspx` barcode image over HTTPS, or confirm that `cl.s50.exct.net` supports it. Add a fallback for when it is blocked.
3. Re-run the audit against the full raw message. That would confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the `Authentication-Results` values.
4. Resolve the tracking-link redirects, for example with a follow-redirect probe on a sample. That would verify the landing destinations and UTM parameters.
5. Remove the comment before the DOCTYPE, and give `<title>` a meaningful value.
