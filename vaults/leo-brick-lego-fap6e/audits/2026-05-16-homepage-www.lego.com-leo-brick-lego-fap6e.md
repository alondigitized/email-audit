# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened LEGO.com on my phone and what I got was a country-gate modal stacked on top of a cookie consent banner — so I'm looking at two friction walls before I've seen a single set. Behind the modal I can make out "THE PLAY ZONE" and "START PLAYING," which tells me the actual homepage underneath is aimed at kids, not at a 38-year-old VIP chasing a double-points weekend. Not a great start.

## What's working

- **The entry modal is visually coherent** — blue/yellow LEGO brand palette, recognizable logo, clean "Continue" button. At least it's not broken.
- **Cookie notice is below the main modal** rather than overlapping it, so there's a readable reading order even if the sequence is annoying.

## What's weak

- **Double-friction gate** — a country selector modal stacked with a cookie consent bar means I hit two walls before seeing any product. That's one too many interruptions on mobile.
- **"THE PLAY ZONE / Start Playing"** is the only homepage content visible, and it's squarely for kids. I'm a VIP member with a Botanical backlog — there is nothing here for me.
- **No VIP/loyalty hook anywhere** — as a returning member, I expect to see my points balance or a member offer within seconds. Completely absent.
- **No offer, no urgency, no product** — the modal hides everything. Even a banner glimpse of "Double VIP Points this weekend" in the background would have been enough to keep me moving.
- **"Start Playing" below the modal** implies the page underneath is a games/entertainment hub, not a shop. Wrong destination impression before I've even tapped Continue.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the country-gate on return visits** — I'm a logged-in VIP member. If you know who I am, don't make me re-confirm my region. Detect locale from my account and drop me on the real homepage.
- **Replace the background hero with an AFOL-targeting module** — something like the current Botanical Collection drop or an Icons/UCS teaser so that the gate feels like I'm about to enter somewhere worth going.
- **Surface VIP points balance in the modal header** — one line like "Welcome back, Leo — 2,450 VIP points" would immediately signal this visit has value.
- **Move cookie consent to a bottom bar, not a separate interrupt** — the GDPR notice doesn't need to live underneath a modal; a slim persistent bottom bar is industry standard and far less aggressive on mobile.
