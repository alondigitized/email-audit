# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at isn't actually the LEGO homepage — it's a region/entry gate that intercepts me before I can even get to the store. I'm presented with a binary choice: enter the main shopping site via "Continue," or get shunted into "The Play Zone" for games and videos. As an AFOL who showed up to check VIP deals or snag a Botanical Collection set before it retires, this gate adds friction before I've seen a single product.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button** is large, legible, and unambiguous — I know exactly one tap gets me to the actual site.
- **Clean render:** No visual bugs, text doesn't overlap, cookie notice is discrete (bottom of screen, not a full-screen takeover modal).
- **LEGO logo** is prominent and recognizable — brand identity isn't in question.

## What's weak

- **The Play Zone bifurcation** is actively hostile to adult shoppers. I came to check if the Orchid is still in stock. Being offered "games and videos" as a co-equal path is a miss — this is a kids-first design that makes me feel like an afterthought.
- **Zero VIP/loyalty presence:** I'm a VIP member hunting double-VIP weekends. If my member status had been detected, this gate would be the perfect moment to say "Welcome back — you have 4,200 points." Instead: nothing.
- **No current offer or urgency:** This gate is a dead zone commercially. If there's a double-VIP weekend or a set retiring next week, I have no idea from this screen.
- **Competing CTAs create ambiguity:** "Continue" (shopping) vs. "Start Playing" (kids zone) are visually almost equal weight. A child or casual visitor could tap the wrong one and land somewhere irrelevant.
- **Cookie notice timing:** The cookie wall fires here, at the gate, before I've even seen product — maximum friction at the worst possible moment.

## Recommendations

- **Collapse The Play Zone into a footer link.** Make "Continue to Shop" the single dominant CTA. The Play Zone should live in the nav under a "Kids" or "Play" tab, not as a co-equal entry point competing with the main store.
- **Surface VIP/member state at the gate.** If a cookie or account session is detected, swap generic copy for "You have X VIP points — tap to shop" to immediately reward returning members like me.
- **Add a single above-the-fold offer line.** Even "Free shipping on orders over $35 · VIP members earn 5% back" would convert this dead gate into a conversion touchpoint.
- **Defer the cookie banner** until after the user has made the Continue/PlayZone choice and is loading the actual page — stacking the cookie wall on top of the gate compounds the friction.
