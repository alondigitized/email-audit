# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a homepage — it's a gateway interstitial, and honestly as a 38-year-old AFOL who's been on LEGO.com hundreds of times hunting VIP double-point weekends, this is the digital equivalent of someone stopping me at the door and asking if I'd like to go to the ball pit instead. The page splits visitors into two lanes: grown-up shoppers via "Continue" or kids via "THE PLAY ZONE." It's a functional routing screen, not a shopping experience.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button** — clean, prominent white pill on blue. I know exactly which tap gets me past this screen and into the actual store.
- **Page renders quickly and cleanly** — no layout shift, no broken images, no spinner of doom. Technically solid.
- **Two-path routing concept** — the intent of separating adult shoppers from kids is smart UX thinking, even if the execution is awkward here.

## What's weak

- **"THE PLAY ZONE" takes up nearly half the above-fold real estate** — I'm here to shop the Wildflower Bouquet before it retires, not watch YouTube. This split-screen treatment forces adult shoppers to consciously opt out of kids content.
- **No VIP hook whatsoever** — I'm a logged-in VIP member. My balance, my current tier, a "Welcome back, Leo" — none of it. LEGO knows who I am; this screen acts like I'm a stranger.
- **Cookie consent banner eating the bottom third** — combined with the two-CTA split above, the actual usable screen real estate is cramped. On a phone this feels congested.
- **Zero merchandising signal** — no hero product, no "new this week," no campaign banner. As an AFOL tracking retirement dates obsessively, I want to see if there's a VIP event running the moment I land.
- **Minifigure art reads as kids content** — two minifigures with a game controller is a visual cue that signals "this section is not for you" to any adult shopper.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a thin VIP status bar above the "Continue" CTA** — even "Welcome back — your VIP balance: 2,400 points" moves me toward the store feeling rewarded, not routed.
- **Replace the Play Zone section with a slim active-promotion strip** — something like "Double VIP Points this weekend · Ends Sunday" gives adult shoppers a reason to push past the gate fast.
- **Relocate the cookie consent to a bottom toast, not a full-bleed banner** — it's squeezing already-limited real estate on mobile; a slim dismissible bar preserves the two-CTA layout without the claustrophobic feel.
- **Suppress the Play Zone split for recognized returning adult accounts** — if LEGO.com already knows I'm a VIP member, skip the two-lane routing entirely and drop me straight onto the homepage.
