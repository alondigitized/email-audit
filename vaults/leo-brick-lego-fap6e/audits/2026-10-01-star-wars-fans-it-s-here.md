# Star Wars™ fans, it’s here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong concept with broken execution. Early Access to the Executor Super Star Destroyer, double points on Star Wars sets, and a free Darth Vader lightsaber gift with purchase is a real campaign. The dramatic black hero with the Star Destroyer is the best visual in the send. But every button is a blank white pill with no label. The four product tiles under "Explore the galaxy" show a Vader minifig, a gingerbread AT-AT, C-3PO and Boba Fett, each beside a tiny gift icon and no name or price. The "2x" banner and the lightsaber gift tile are also unlabeled.
- It looks like the same missing-text failure I flagged on 2026-09-23 ("these won't be around for much longer") and 2026-09-25 ("Room for a new build in your life?"). Both were 3/10. Here the headline and body copy do render, so the email is more usable than those two. But a reader still can't tell what to click or what anything costs.
- Verdict: a good offer and a good hero, undercut by a body that is empty where the labels, prices and CTAs should be. The membership card also shows 0 points and $0.00, which makes the "double points" promise feel hollow.

## What's working

- The Star Destroyer hero on black with diagonal chevron art is striking and clearly Star Wars.
- "Now is the time to strike, Leo" is on-theme and personalized, and it carries the "before general release" exclusivity.
- The red "Double points granted" banner with a 2x icon puts the loyalty hook at the top, with real dates (Oct 01 – Oct 07).
- The Darth Vader lightsaber gift-with-purchase is a concrete, desirable incentive.
- The app download block and the Insiders card keep the member framing consistent.

## What's weak

- Every CTA is an empty white pill: under the banner, under the hero copy, and under the lightsaber copy. There is no visible button text anywhere.
- The four "Explore the galaxy" tiles have no product names, prices or points values, only a stray gift icon each. The tiles are mostly white space, and the product images sit off to one side.
- The gift-box icon overlaps the lightsaber image as a pink square. It reads as a broken-asset placeholder.
- The offer is hard to parse. Double points, early access and the lightsaber gift all compete, and no price for the Executor is shown.
- The membership card shows 0 points and $0.00, which undercuts the loyalty pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make sure every button renders its label, for example "Shop the Executor" and "Get the lightsaber gift". This is the biggest lift, because without labels there is nothing to click.
- 2. Restore product names, prices and points on the four tiles, and center the images.
- 3. Lead with one offer. Make the Executor early access the hero, and put the lightsaber GWP and double points in a single line beneath it.
- 4. State the Executor's price and the spend threshold for the gift, so the offer reads as honest.
- 5. Replace the pink gift placeholder with clean art or remove it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `2x points on Star Wars sets through Oct 7, plus Vader's lightsaber with purchase.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get it before general release. Gift available while supplies last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive SFMC-style build with a valid doctype, `lang="en"` and a viewport meta. QA scored it 38% with 5 warnings. The main issues are 20 images flagged for missing alt text, 2 HTTP-sourced images, and compliance headers or footer data that could not be confirmed in the captured material.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and broken links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is over 70% URLs.** 10,017 of 12,267 characters are URLs, which comes from long tracking redirects. This can hurt spam scoring and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Two images load over `http://`.** The "Insider Barcode" `LiveContent.aspx` image comes from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…`, and it appears twice. Clients that block mixed content, or proxies that rewrite it, may not show it. The barcode is functional content, so a failure to load matters more than for a decorative image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 image warnings for missing alt text.** Examples:
  - `LEGOInsiderLogowhitelog.png` and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
  - The four hero and slim banners: `01_…Slim-Banner`, `02_…Hero-Banner-X…` (×2), `03_…Hero-Banner-S…` (×2)
  - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (×3)
  - `GreyBrick.png` (×8)
- **Decorative images may be intended.** If `GreyBrick.png` and the separator are decorative, they should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The QA check cannot tell a missing attribute from an empty one, so I can't confirm which case this is.
- **The banner images and logos are content images.** If they have no alt text, the message is unreadable with images blocked.
- **Web fonts are loaded two ways.** The head has `<link>` tags to Google Fonts and a CSS `@import` (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans`). Gmail and several other clients strip both. The stack `LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif` does fall back to Arial, so this is low risk.
- **A global `input { display: none !important; }` rule** is present. It is harmless unless the template uses form elements.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the captured source. The only personalized element visible is the dynamic Insider barcode image (`LiveContent.aspx`), and the HTML is truncated, so the rest could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **No physical address was detected.** The captured HTML is truncated, so the footer was probably not scanned. Confirm against the full source before treating this as a violation.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `e.crm.lego.com` with sender `Noreply@`, and the relay did not expose the results.
- **The From address is `Noreply@`.** This is a no-reply address, which is a minor deliverability and engagement signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 49 tracked links were skipped, and the visible source shows no resolved destination URLs, so I can't check UTM parameters or landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero and banner images and to both logos. Set `alt=""` explicitly on decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, the footer separators).
2. Serve the barcode `LiveContent.aspx` image over HTTPS, or confirm that the SFMC endpoint supports it.
3. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for the footer address. Capture them upstream of the AgentMail relay so the QA check is reliable.
4. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original delivery to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. Resolve the tracking redirects in the pipeline (follow them and record final URLs) so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
6. Make sure the plain-text part has readable copy, not just redirect URLs.
