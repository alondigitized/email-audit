# Leo, check out this month’s membership wrap-up
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO Insiders' monthly wrap-up for Leo — a four-job email that tries to promote a sweepstakes, display member points, tease early-access products, and solicit survey feedback simultaneously. The sweepstakes hook is the strongest element by a significant margin: a trip to Hobbiton plus a four-set Lord of the Rings bundle is a genuinely aspirational offer for the adult collector segment, and the LOTR/Hobbit hero imagery earns its top placement. The LOTR campaign tie-in is timely after June 1's "best sets for adults" send, which scored a 9/10 on this same ledger — that email led with a tight curatorial premise; this one dilutes its premise with four competing asks.
- The email stumbles hardest in the rewards section. Leo's points total displays as "0." Whether Leo genuinely has zero points (a new member with no purchases) or whether this is a data-pull failure, the effect is identical: the "this month's top rewards" framing rings hollow when the featured stat is a goose egg. Compounding the problem, a smiling woman appears in the same rewards block with no caption, no name, no context — she reads as unrelated stock art dropped into a partially-built template.
- The sweepstakes is worth sending for. The monthly wrap-up chassis around it needs work.

## What's working

- The LOTR/Hobbit hero shot displays multiple sets together — shelf-worthy photography that signals depth of the collection.
- The sweepstakes prize (Hobbiton trip + 4-set bundle) is a strong aspirational offer, clearly explained in the body copy.
- First-name personalization in the subject ("Leo") works for this loyalty program context.
- Early-access framing — "As a member, you can be the first to build..." — creates genuine exclusivity signal for a collector persona.
- Clean render: no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent LEGO brand palette throughout.

## What's weak

- Points total renders as "0" — demoralizing for a new member, alarming data-pull signal for an established one; either reading undermines the wrap-up premise.
- Two separate "Enter now" CTAs (sweepstakes + rewards section) with different implied destinations create genuine confusion.
- The woman's photo in the rewards block has no caption, name, or context — reads as unexplained filler.
- The sweepstakes closing date appears truncated or set in type too small to read in the render.
- Four distinct action requests (sweepstakes entry, leaderboard check, wish list adds, survey) dilute focus and suppress click confidence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or suppress the 0-points display.** If Leo genuinely has zero points, substitute "You're just getting started — earn points with your next purchase" rather than surfacing a bare zero. Showing "0" in a rewards wrap-up actively signals disengagement at the moment you're trying to build loyalty investment.
- 2. **Consolidate to one above-fold CTA.** The sweepstakes is the single highest-value action in this email. Relegate the leaderboard link, wish list adds, and survey to plain-text secondary links below. Competing CTAs split attention and cost clicks on the best offer.
- 3. **Caption or replace the rewards section photo.** Give the woman a name, a points total, a member-spotlight label — anything. Without context she undermines rather than reinforces the social proof she's presumably there to provide.
- 4. **Make the sweepstakes deadline legible and prominent.** "Closes June 30 — one free entry per member" as readable copy above or below the CTA is both a clear urgency signal and an honesty signal. Truncated fine print does neither.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the sweepstakes.** "Wrap-up" signals routine. The Hobbiton trip is the news.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, you could win a trip to the Shire this month`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your June rewards are here — plus a shot at Hobbiton`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One free entry per member: the LEGO® LOTR 4-set bundle is on the line.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders points, early-access drops, and a sweepstakes — all inside.`

**Real concern:**

**Duplicate Google Fonts requests:**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700&display=swap">
```

### 5. Compliance

```

```
These are missing from what the AgentMail relay surfaced. This is a **high-priority gap**: Gmail and Apple Mail require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for bulk senders to pass one-click unsubscribe requirements (Google/Yahoo 2024 sender requirements). The headers may exist upstream but are being stripped or not forwarded by the relay. Verify at the raw SMTP layer, not via relay inspection.

**SPF/DKIM status unknown:**

`e.crm.lego.com` is a third-party ESP subdomain (Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern). SPF/DKIM alignment cannot be confirmed from the captured relay data. This should be verified directly against the sending domain's DNS records and DMARC policy — not just inferred from the relay.
