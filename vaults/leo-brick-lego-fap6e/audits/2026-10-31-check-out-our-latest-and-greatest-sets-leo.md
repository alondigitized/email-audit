# Check out our latest and greatest sets, Leo
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a generic "latest sets" Insiders send, and the render makes it weaker than the subject promises. The two hero product cards, a winter cabin set and the PlayStation console, show only an image. The names, prices and buttons around them are blank. Two tiny icons sit where product details should be, and a white pill sits empty below the cards where a CTA should be. A subscriber can't tell what the sets are, what they cost, or where to click.
- The lower half is better. The "Reward yourself in a few simple steps" block explains redemption, and the two Gift with Purchase rows have dates and "Shop now" buttons ("Ends 11/09/2026"). But the rewards graphic is an oversized yellow shape with no clear meaning. The membership card shows 0 points and $0.00. After a week of Insiders mail (10/21 Santa, 10/22 PlayStation, 10/24 Downton Abbey, 10/27 Winter Village), this feels like a recycled catalogue of sets Leo has already seen.
- Verdict: a weak, cadence-fatigued send with visible gaps in the main modules. It's competent brand chrome around missing content.

## What's working

- The green Insiders header with the points and value strip is on brand and instantly recognisable.
- The two Gift with Purchase rows give concrete offers: spend over $130, and a Harry Potter office gift. Both have an "Ends 11/09/2026" deadline and a "Shop now" button.
- The numbered rewards steps are clear, and "Redeem now" is a real, visible button.
- The app-download module with both store badges is tidy.
- The category tiles (Star Wars, Icons, Ideas, City) give easy browse paths.

## What's weak

- The product cards look broken. The text and price areas next to the cabin and the PlayStation set are empty, and the only visible elements are two small icons.
- A blank white pill below the cards looks like a CTA with no label.
- Neither hero product has a name, price, or visible link.
- The yellow shape in the rewards box is an unexplained graphic that takes up a lot of space.
- The points strip shows "0 / $0.00", which tells a member they have no benefit.
- The email repeats the PlayStation and Winter Village sets from sends on 10/22 and 10/27, so it adds nothing new.
- The headline "Check out these incredible builds" has no offer, no urgency and no hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the product modules so each card shows the set name, price, a one-line hook and a labelled "Shop now" button. This is the biggest lift, because right now nothing in the hero can be clicked with any confidence.
- 2. Lead with one new set and one dated offer, such as the Gift with Purchase ending 11/09. Move it above the product grid.
- 3. Cut or shrink the rewards-steps block, and replace the yellow graphic with an actual reward.
- 4. Show the points balance only when it's above zero. Otherwise swap the strip for "Earn double points on your next order".
- 5. Don't re-send sets Leo has already been shown this week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Harry Potter™ gift when you shop before 11/09.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The LEGO® email (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, based on the `image.e.crm.lego.com` and `cl.s50.exct.net` hosts) has valid structure and a resolved first-name token in the subject. The confirmed technical problems are repeated missing `alt` attributes, two HTTP-sourced images, and a plain-text part made mostly of tracking URLs. The compliance warnings come from relay capture limits and a truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking can't be verified.** The QA check skipped 54 tracking links as click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect chain or 404 was checked.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 12,288 of 15,458 characters (about 80%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is a spam-filter risk and makes the plain-text version hard to read. Likely cause: long tracking URLs repeated for every link.
- **Insecure image source.** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over plain HTTP: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNDYs…`. It appears twice, and clients that block mixed or insecure content may not render it. It is a dynamic, per-recipient image, so a missing barcode would break a functional element.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text** on at least 10 distinct image sources, many repeated across the mobile and desktop variants. All are on `https://image.e.crm.lego.com/lib/fe3b117175640479761271/m/1/`:
  - `GreyBrick.png` (4 occurrences)
  - `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` (4)
  - `rewards_icon_tt.png` (2)
  - `iconpath.png` (2)
  - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (3)
  - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
- **Alt text may be decorative.** Some of these (`GreyBrick.png`, the separator, `iconpath.png`) may be decorative. If so, they should carry an explicit `alt=""` instead of omitting the attribute. `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` and the rewards icon are likely informative.
- **Barcode alt text exists but is weak.** The Insider Barcode image has alt text ("Insider Barcode") but no scannable-code equivalent.
- **No other rendering defects are confirmed** in the visible source. It declares `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes`, Outlook MSO conditionals, and a 599px mobile breakpoint. The source was truncated, so the rest of the markup was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders as "…, Leo" with no unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens visible. The barcode URL looks like a per-recipient dynamic image, but that can't be checked in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three findings below are unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so they may be present in the original message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support, so check the raw headers.
- **No physical address detected.** The HTML was truncated and the footer wasn't visible, so this may be a false positive. Confirm against the full source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `e.crm.lego.com` is a sending subdomain, so verify alignment on the original message.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 54 links went through click-redirect domains and were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no landing URLs or UTM parameters. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the Insider Barcode image over HTTPS (`cl.s50.exct.net` supports it) and re-test rendering in Gmail and Apple Mail.
2. Add `alt` text to every flagged image: descriptive text for the Insiders logo and rewards icon, and `alt=""` for decorative bricks and separators.
3. Shorten the plain-text version by using short link aliases, or by listing fewer links, so URLs fall below 70% of the characters.
4. Pull the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and the footer's physical address. Treat these as open items until then.
5. Resolve the 54 tracking links through a redirect-following probe to verify destinations and UTM consistency.
