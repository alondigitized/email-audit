# LEGO® Insiders Days are back, Leo!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's fourth Insiders Days email in four days — following the campaign launch on July 7, a re-engagement send on July 9, and two earlier premium-drop emails — and it commits the single most damaging mistake a mid-campaign email can make: it sounds like it's launching something that already launched. "LEGO® Insiders Days are back, Leo!" is the subject line you send on Day 1. On Day 4 of a six-day campaign, the correct message is countdown urgency — *two days left* — not re-introduction. The body copy ("Time to celebrate LEGO® Insiders days!") doubles down on that error.
- Visually the email is well-assembled. The green hero is on-brand, the four-product grid is aspirational and relevant (Pinball Machine, Koenigsegg Megacar, Woodland Mushrooms, F&F Supra), and the "3 Ways to Celebrate" iconography breaks down the value proposition cleanly. The renders are clean, the CTA buttons are legible, and the Insiders app row at the bottom adds a loyalty depth layer. The bones are good.
- The judgment: this email behaves like the 7/7 launch email got cloned, lightened slightly, and re-sent. A genuinely engaged Insiders subscriber who has already opened the prior two campaign emails will feel no forward motion here. Without a deadline nudge and with the same "back!" energy as Day 1, this send is likely to be skimmed and ignored by the very members it most wants to convert.

## What's working

- **Product grid is targeted and premium** — the Koenigsegg and Pinball Machine are exactly the aspirational, high-piece-count sets adult Insiders want to see; both have prior dedicated emails building desire.
- **"3 Ways to Celebrate" section** is clear and well-structured — icons, concise labels (Double the Fun / LEGO® Insiders Price / Gifts with Purchase), and individual CTAs make the offer trifecta scannable.
- **Visual hierarchy holds** — the eye lands on the hero offer first, flows to products, then to benefits; nothing fights for attention.
- **Personalization is present** — "Leo" appears in subject and membership display header, reinforcing the 1:1 feel.
- **App upsell row** at the bottom is a natural loyalty extension and not intrusive.

## What's weak

- **"Are back" language fires on the wrong day** — this subject framing belonged on July 7; sending it on Day 4 makes it read as a duplicate launch or a targeting error for the subscriber who already opened two prior Insiders Days sends.
- **Zero urgency around the closing window** — the campaign ends July 12 (two days away from send date), but nothing in the subject, hero copy, or CTA creates a countdown; the phrase "7/7 to 7/12" is buried in the hero sub-copy.
- **Hero CTA is too soft** — "Explore more" is a discovery prompt, not a conversion prompt. On Day 4 of a sale, "Shop now — 2 days left" outperforms it.
- **0 Points / $0.00 member header is deflating** — the membership rail shows no accrued balance, which undercuts the entire double-points value proposition for any member in this state.
- **Cadence risk is real** — four emails about Insiders Days in four days from the same sender trains subscribers to deprioritize LEGO in their inbox; the July 9 "10/10" send was the right mid-campaign note, and this one doesn't advance the story.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject and hero around the closing deadline.** The single highest-lift change. "Insiders Days end Sunday, Leo" or "2 days left: double points, Insiders pricing" converts the same audience without making them feel like they're reading a launch email they already received.
- 2. **Swap "Explore more" for a deadline CTA.** Change to "Shop before July 12" or "Shop now — offer ends Sunday." Verb + noun + urgency beats passive discovery language every time at this stage of a campaign.
- 3. **Add a countdown visual or bold date callout in the hero.** Even a simple "Ends July 12" badge over the hero image signals timeline without a redesign. The 7/7 launch email didn't need this; Day 4 does.
- 4. **Pull the 0-balance header** or replace it with "Your exclusive access" copy if the member's balance is zero — showing a $0.00 value display in a rewards email is counterproductive.
- 5. **Tighten the product grid to one featured "urgency hero" set** rather than four equal tiles. Pick the set closest to selling out or most on-brand for the week (Pinball Machine is the strongest candidate given its own dedicated launch email) and give it a scarcity nudge.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Insiders Days end Sunday, Leo — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days left: double points + Insider pricing`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Koenigsegg, Pinball Machine & more — but only until July 12.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your double-points window closes Sunday. Don't leave them on the table.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — LEGO Insiders Days Reminder

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has no broken navigable links; the two FAIL-flagged URLs are preconnect hints, not pages. The primary issues are a pervasive missing-alt-text problem across ~35 images, redundant font loading, and compliance headers that are not visible to the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**False positives in QA output:** `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` are flagged as broken (404), but both appear as `<link rel="preconnect">` resource hints — not navigable hrefs. A direct HTTP GET to these hosts returns non-200, which is expected. Not real broken links.

**31 tracking links skipped:** All CTA and product links pass through a click-redirect domain and were not probed. UTM coverage and destination URL correctness cannot be confirmed from this data (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — 35 images missing `alt` text.** Every content and footer image in the payload has no `alt` attribute, including the hero banner (`01_20260709_Q3_InsidersDays_Reminder_Hero-Banner-Standard.gi…`), all three product card images (`04_20260707_Q3_InsidersDay_Launch_Card-[1-3].png`), the rewards/sale image, and all footer images. With images disabled (Outlook default, Gmail cached-proxy), the email renders as a blank white page.

**Several images appear to load the same src twice.** The QA findings list identical warnings for the same filenames (`GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, product set images). This suggests each image block is duplicated in the HTML — likely a desktop/mobile swap pattern that is not hidden correctly, or a copy-paste template error.

**Redundant Google Fonts loading:** Three separate font requests are fired for overlapping families:
- `<link>` → `Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700`
- `<link>` → `Noto+Sans+Display:wght@400;700` (supersedes the first)
- `@import` inside `<style>` → `Noto Sans` (no Display variant)

The first `<link>` is fully redundant. The `@import` fires after the `<link>` tags parse, delaying render.

**Dead CSS — `NotoB` mapping commented out:**
```css
/* [style*="NotoB"] { font-family: 'Noto Sans Display', sans-serif; } */
```
Any element using `style="font-family: NotoB"` to trigger bold display font falls through to the system font stack. If bold headlines depend on this selector, they are silently degraded.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**No issues found.** The first-name token rendered correctly as "Leo!" in the subject line. No unresolved `{{token}}` or `[FNAME]` syntax is visible in the HTML source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** by the AgentMail relay. If absent at the MTA level (not merely invisible to the relay), Gmail and Apple Mail will not display their native unsubscribe UI, and bulk-sender compliance (Google/Yahoo 2024 requirements) is at risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not captured** (RFC 8058). One-click machine unsubscribe is required for senders above Gmail's bulk threshold. Missing this header disqualifies the send from one-click unsubscribe.

**`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact, but cannot be ruled out as an actual auth gap without checking the raw headers at the receiving MTA.

**Note:** These three may be relay-capture gaps rather than genuine absences. They should be verified against raw headers received at the inbox.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Cannot confirm UTM coverage** — all 31 CTAs pass through a click-redirect and were skipped by the probe. UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) on destination landing pages are unverifiable from this data.

**Plain-text alternative is 81.5% URLs** (14,337 / 17,596 chars). A machine-generated plain-text part that is almost entirely redirect URLs is a known spam-filter signal and provides no readable fallback for plain-text clients.

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | 35+ images missing `alt` | Add descriptive `alt` to every content image; use `alt=""` for purely decorative images (spacers, separators) |
| **P0** | List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers | Verify raw inbox headers; if absent, configure ESP to inject both per RFC 2369 and RFC 8058 |
| **P1** | Plain-text body is 81.5% URLs | Generate a human-readable plain-text version; strip or replace redirect URLs with readable anchor text |
| **P1** | Duplicate image loads | Audit desktop/mobile swap blocks; ensure hidden variants use `display:none` with `max-height:0; overflow:hidden` on the correct breakpoint |
| **P2** | Redundant first `<link>` for `Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700` | Remove; the second `<link>` with `wght@400;700` covers it |
| **P2** | `NotoB` CSS rule commented out | Either restore the rule or remove `style="font-family: NotoB"` from elements that rely on it |
| **P3** | Authentication-Results not captured | Confirm SPF/DKIM pass status via raw headers or ESP delivery report |
