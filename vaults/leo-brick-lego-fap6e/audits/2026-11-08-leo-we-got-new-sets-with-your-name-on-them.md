# Leo, we got new sets with your name on them!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a monthly "what's new" Insiders roundup. The subject promises sets "with your name on them", but the body is a generic four-product grid. Only "Leo" in the subhead personalizes it. Nothing in the grid is tailored to a person.
- The structure is clean. Four product cards each show piece count and Insiders points, so a shopper can compare quickly. The gift-with-purchase block lower down is the strongest conversion lever. The hero is weak, though. "See what's new this month!" is a headline with no hook, and there is no deadline or offer above the fold. Compared with the 11/02 Venice and 11/03 Godzilla sends, which had a single hero set and member-early-access framing, this one is a catalog.
- The email works as a browse-and-shop utility, but it is not compelling. Its strongest elements, the gift-with-purchase offers ending 11/09, are buried below the fold.

## What's working

- Each product card shows piece count and Insiders points, which gives concrete value at a glance.
- The four-card grid is tidy and consistent, with clear "Shop now" buttons on every card.
- The Points/Value header ties the email to loyalty status.
- The gift-with-purchase cards state spend thresholds and end dates (11/09, 11/21).
- The seasonal mix of Holiday House and Mrs. Claus fits the November timing.

## What's weak

- The subject's "your name on them" promise isn't delivered. The sets are the same for everyone.
- Every CTA is a bare "Shop now". Six identical buttons plus a yellow one give no specificity and no hierarchy.
- The gift-with-purchase offers sit below roughly 1,300px, so the time-sensitive 11/09 deadlines are easy to miss.
- The member's Points show 0 and $0.00 in both the header and the footer card. This highlights an empty balance and gives no reason to engage.
- The theme row (Star Wars, Icons, Architecture, City) has tiny images and no clear link affordance. The App Store block adds more clutter.
- The hero has no image, so the top of the email is a text block on green.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift-with-purchase offers up under the hero and lead with the deadline: "Free gift ends tomorrow."
- 2. Replace the six identical "Shop now" buttons with product-specific CTAs, such as "Get the Holiday House" or "Build Cozy Plants".
- 3. Make the personalization real by ordering or labeling the cards from Leo's browsing or purchase themes. Otherwise drop the "your name" claim.
- 4. Cut the 0-points header, or reframe it as "You're 0 points away from your first reward".
- 5. Collapse the app promo and theme row into one slim footer strip.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`cl.s50.exct.net`, `image.e.crm.lego.com`) with 66 click-tracking links. The QA data shows widespread missing image alt text, an HTTP-sourced image, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay capture gaps, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 66 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Insecure image:** `<img alt="Insider Barcode">` loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…`. This is mixed content, and clients or proxies may block it. If it is blocked, the Insider barcode will not render. It appears twice, which suggests separate member and non-member blocks or desktop and mobile variants.
- **Plain-text bloat:** The plain-text part is 12,032 of 15,085 characters URLs (about 80%). Long tracking URLs are inflating it, which is a mild spam-scoring and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - Product images: `10371_Prod.png`, `72306_Prod.png`, `11387_Prod.png` and `bltf1df1459ba3b1736-40875_Prod_en-gb.png` (all from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...?fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400`). With images blocked, the product modules have no text equivalent.
  - Footer brand image: `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`.
- **Missing alt text on likely decorative images:** `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, `iconpath.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`. These should carry an explicit `alt=""` if they are decorative. The QA check flags them, so the attribute is absent or empty, and it cannot tell which.
- **Duplicate flags:** Most of the 31 alt warnings are repeats of the same few assets, caused by duplicated desktop and mobile blocks. There are about 10 unique images.
- **Web font import:** The `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans...')` in `<style>` and the `<link>` tags are unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The fallback stack `LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif` handles this, so it is a low-severity cosmetic issue.
- **Stray comment before the doctype:** A leading `<!-- -->` sits before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Some clients can fall into quirks mode when anything precedes the doctype. Low severity and unconfirmed.
- **Good practices present:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and MSO conditional comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
The subject renders "Leo," with no unresolved tokens. The truncated HTML shows no visible `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens, but the body is cut off, so personalization in the rest of the body cannot be verified. The Insider barcode is dynamically rendered through `LiveContent.aspx`, so it depends on the HTTP image loading correctly (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. QA attributes this to AgentMail relay stripping, so it is not confirmed as a sender defect. If they are genuinely absent, the sender would not meet Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown because of the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be confirmed.
- **From address:** `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com` is a no-reply sender. Reply handling is not evidenced, and this is a minor deliverability and engagement consideration.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 66 links are wrapped in tracking redirects that were not probed, so final URLs and UTM parameters are unknown. Product images load from `www.lego.com/cdn/...` and are consistent with the brand domain.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the Insider Barcode source from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx…` to HTTPS, and add descriptive alt text.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images and the Insiders logo. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the decorative assets (`GreyBrick.png`, `iconpath.png`, separators, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`).
3. Confirm at the source that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC using a direct-to-inbox test, not the relay.
4. Resolve and audit the 66 tracking links with a click-through tool to verify destinations and UTM consistency.
5. Shorten tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce link count, to bring the URL ratio down from about 80%.
6. Remove the stray comment before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Optionally inline the critical font fallback so Gmail does not depend on `@import`.
