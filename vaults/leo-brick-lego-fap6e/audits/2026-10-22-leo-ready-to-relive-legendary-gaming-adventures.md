## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML is structurally sound (lang="en", viewport meta, dark-mode meta, MSO fixes). The QA run shows a 38% pass rate, driven mainly by widespread missing `alt` attributes, two non-HTTPS images, and compliance headers that couldn't be confirmed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 37 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, and this is not a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 11,656 of 15,230 characters URLs (about 77%, above the 70% threshold). Tracking-wrapped links inflate the text part and can raise spam-filter scores.
- The "Insider Barcode" image loads over `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. It appears twice. Mixed content can be blocked or downgraded by clients and proxies.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Many `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute, so screen readers will read file names or skip the images. Affected sources:
  - `01_Project-P26-Sustain-2810-Hero-Banner-Tall.png` (hero, 2 instances)
  - `72051_Prod.png` and `blte7ecfac1aa09d7c5-11374_Prod_en-gb.png` (product images, each repeated)
  - `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png` and `Logo+Container+logo+white.png` (repeated 3–7 times each, including footer assets)
- The repetition suggests desktop and mobile variants are both in the markup, since the source uses `.show-on-desktop`/`.hide-on-desktop` and `.mj-image-swap-*` classes. The alt omissions are therefore a template-level problem.
- Web fonts load through `<link>` and `@import` from `fonts.googleapis.com`. Many clients ignore them, and the `font-family` stack (`LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) falls back to Arial. This is acceptable, so it is not flagged as a defect.
- The `.preheader` class uses `display:none`, `height:0` and `width:0`. This is standard, but the HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the preheader text exists.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The subject renders "Leo" with no unresolved tokens visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The HTML was cut off, so tokens later in the body couldn't be checked.

**5. Compliance**
- The QA tool reports no `List-Unsubscribe` header and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header. Its own note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders of this volume. Check the raw message headers.
- No physical mailing address was detected (CAN-SPAM). The footer is image-heavy and the source is truncated, so the address may be in the unseen portion or baked into an image. This needs manual confirmation.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed through this relay. The From domain `e.crm.lego.com` and the image host `image.e.crm.lego.com` are consistent.
- The From address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`. A no-reply sender discourages replies and is a minor deliverability and trust signal.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- The 37 links are wrapped in tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages couldn't be verified.
- Product images load directly from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...`, not from the email image host. If those assets change or are removed, the images will break after send. This is a minor risk.
- No other continuity issues can be confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones (`GreyBrick.png`, separators, logo containers).
2. Serve the "Insider Barcode" image over HTTPS. Ask the ESP (the `exct.net` domain indicates Salesforce Marketing Cloud) for an HTTPS `LiveContent` endpoint.
3. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. If they are missing at the source, add them.
4. Confirm a physical postal address appears in the footer as live text. Add it if it's missing or image-only.
5. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original message, not the relayed copy.
6. Reduce the URL share of the plain-text part by trimming redundant links or using shorter tracking links.
7. Host product images on the email image CDN or pin their URLs, so they don't depend on website asset paths.
8. Resolve a sample of the skipped tracking links manually to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
