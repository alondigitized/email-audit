# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get a homepage — I got a gate. LEGO.com is showing me a two-panel interstitial: one half is a country/shop portal ("You are about to enter LEGO.com"), the other half is a kids' Play Zone pitch with minifigure characters inviting me to play games and watch videos. The brand looks like it's primarily trying to route children to entertainment content while also acknowledging that adults might want to, you know, actually buy something. As a VIP member who came here to check retirement dates and hunt deals, this is friction before I've even seen a product.

## What's working

- **Clean rendering:** The two-panel layout and cookie notice are all pixel-perfect — no layout breakage, no overlapping text.
- **"Continue" button is legible:** At least the path to the actual site is one tap, not buried. Big, centered, clear enough label.

## What's weak

- **It's a gate, not a homepage:** I came to shop UCS sets and check VIP points. Instead I'm looking at a country-selector interstitial before I've seen a single product or offer.
- **Play Zone panel is 100% kid-targeted:** "Play games, watch videos and more!" with two child minifigures and a "START PLAYING" CTA tells me nothing about the Botanical Collection drop I'm hunting or the double-VIP weekend I'm trying to catch.
- **Zero loyalty/VIP signals:** No sign that LEGO knows I'm a returning VIP member. No rewards balance, no personalized hook, nothing that says "welcome back."
- **Cookie banner compounds the friction:** A full-paragraph cookie notice sits below an already-gated entry screen — that's two layers of friction before I've seen a single product image.
- **No pricing, no offer, no urgency:** This is a homepage hit that leaves me with zero commercial information.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the interstitial for recognized sessions:** If I'm a logged-in VIP member or have a cookie, route me directly to the homepage. Gate-pages should be for first-ever visitors only.
- **Replace the Play Zone panel with an adult collector hook:** Even a static "New: Icons & Botanical Collection" tile with a "Shop Now" button would tell returning AFOLs they're in the right place.
- **Surface VIP status and current promotion in the entry screen:** If this gate must exist, at minimum show "Double VIP Points — this weekend only" so there's a reason to tap Continue with urgency.
- **Collapse the cookie notice into a slim banner:** Move it to a one-line strip at the bottom so it doesn't visually compete with the primary CTA.
