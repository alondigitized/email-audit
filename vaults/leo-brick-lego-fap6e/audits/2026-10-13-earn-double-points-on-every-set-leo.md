# Earn double points on every set, Leo
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built loyalty send with a clear offer: double Insiders points on any set from today (10/13) to 10/19. The green frame, the "2x" badge and the "Build up your loyalty points quicker" headline all land the offer quickly. The four product cards show the exact points each set earns (+1170, +780, +2275, +1300), which makes the offer concrete.
- The offer itself is weak, though. The recipient's balance reads 0 points and $0.00, both in the header and in the member card at the bottom. Double points on a zero balance is a thin hook. The "2x" is also pitched as points, not money. The email never says what 1,170 points are worth, so the number means little. Two extra offers (a gift with purchase at $180 and a "selected kids sets" sale) split attention from the main message. The hero is a generic family photo. Every CTA is a bare "Shop now", and nothing here goes beyond the same four sets used in the 10/11 roundup.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand, and clearer than the broken renders from 9/23 and 10/8. It is still a mid-tier promo, and it keeps pushing the same hero sets for the fourth or fifth send in two weeks.

## What's working

- The offer is stated plainly with a hard deadline: "twice the points… from today until 10/19".
- The "2x" badge sits over the hero and is easy to spot.
- Each product card shows piece count and points earned, which gives a reason to compare sets.
- The render is intact. There are no broken images or overlapping text.
- The Insiders header and member card reinforce that this is a member perk.

## What's weak

- The 0 points / $0.00 balance in the header and the card undercuts the "earn double" pitch. The email never says what a point is worth.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop now", including all four product cards.
- The email has four separate offers: double points, a gift with purchase, a kids-sets sale and the app. The gift-with-purchase and kids-sale blocks are small and compete with the main message.
- The hero is a stock family photo with no product in it. The PlayStation, Holiday House and Downton Abbey have all been featured in recent sends (10/2, 10/3, 10/11).
- The fine print ("*For full Terms & Conditions") is buried at the very bottom, and the "selected kids sets" sale names no specific sets or discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the points concrete. Say "2x points = $X back on this set" on each card, and treat the zero balance as an opening line: "Start your balance with double points."
- 2. Put one product in the hero in place of the family photo, using the best-value set, with the points figure on it.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Earn +2,275 points" or "Build Downton Abbey".
- 4. Cut or demote the kids-sale and gift-with-purchase blocks so double points is the only headline offer.
- 5. Move the terms line up beside the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2x Insiders points through 10/19, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, your first points just doubled`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn up to +2,275 points on a single set. Ends 10/19.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Double points on every set, plus a free Italy gift at $180.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype, `lang="en"`, viewport and MSO fixes, responsive media queries). The QA run flagged missing alt text on about 33 `<img>` instances, a plain-text part that is mostly URLs, and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers. The headers could not be checked because of the relay, so they are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 63 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Plain-text alternative:** 11,651 of 14,404 characters (about 81%) are URLs. This usually comes from long tracking URLs in the text part. It raises spam-filter risk and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Product image hosts:** Product images load from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...?fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400`, while campaign assets load from `image.e.crm.lego.com`. This isn't a defect, but it adds a dependency on the production site CDN.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 33 `<img>` instances.
  - **Content images:**
    - The hero banner `20261013_Q4_2_PointsOctober_Launch_Hero-Banner-Standard.jpg` has no alt text.
    - The GWP banner `..._GWP-Banner+1.jpg` and the slim banner `..._Slim-Banner.png` have none either.
    - Product images `72306_Prod.png`, `11387_Prod.png`, `21373_Prod.png` and `21369_Prod.png` have none, so product names are not available to screen readers or with images blocked.
    - If these banners carry the double-points offer as text baked into the image, it will be lost whenever images are blocked.
  - **Decorative images:** `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`, `iconpath.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` should carry an explicit `alt=""`. If it is already there, the QA check is a false positive for these. The logo image needs a real alt, such as "LEGO Insiders".
- **Repeated image instances:** Several images appear two to four times, for example the hero banner twice and `GreyBrick.png` about seven times. This is probably a desktop/mobile swap (`.mj-image-swap-*`, `.hide-on-desktop`), so each instance needs its own alt attribute.
- **Web fonts:** The source uses both `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">` and a CSS `@import`. Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore both. Fallbacks are defined (`LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`), so the risk is low.
- **`[style*="NotoR"]` selector:** It relies on an attribute-substring match on inline styles. It is unsupported in some clients and degrades to the fallback stack.
- **Not assessable:** The source was truncated, so dark-mode CSS under `color-scheme: light dark` and the full markup could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders "Leo" with no unresolved tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source. The truncated body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** It was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain `e.crm.lego.com` is aligned with the image host `image.e.crm.lego.com`.
- **Footer:** The footer physical address and unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They cannot be checked because the links are wrapped in tracking redirects and none were resolved.
- **Product thumbnails:** The product CDN images (`72306`, `11387`, `21373`, `21369`) have no confirmed click-through destination, so their landing-page match is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, GWP and slim banners, with the offer copy ("Earn double LEGO® Insiders points") rather than the filename. Add the set names to the four product images.
2. Add `alt=""` to the decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, separators, `iconpath.png`, footer product icon). Give `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` a real alt.
3. Reduce URL density in the plain-text part, using shorter tracking links or fewer links.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay hides them.
5. Resolve a sample of the 63 tracking links manually, or with a browser-based check, to confirm the destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to check the footer address, unsubscribe link and dark-mode styles.
