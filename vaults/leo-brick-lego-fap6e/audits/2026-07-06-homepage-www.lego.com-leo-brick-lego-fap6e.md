# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- What I'm actually seeing isn't the homepage — it's an entry gate, a fork in the road between the shopping experience ("You can shop, get support & more") and a kids' Play Zone. LEGO is showing me a decision prompt before I even get to browse. As a 38-year-old AFOL who came here to check double-VIP events and scope Botanical Collection retirement dates, being intercepted by a minifig-illustrated gateway split between "adult shopper" and "kids' games" is mildly annoying — though at least the shopping path is the first option.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button:** Large, centered, high-contrast white pill button — easy to tap on mobile and unambiguous for getting past the gate.
- **Dual-path clarity:** The page does at least make clear there are two destinations. I know not to tap "START PLAYING."
- **Cookie notice at bottom:** Actually positioned below the action area so it doesn't block the CTAs — better than a full-screen modal takeover.

## What's weak

- **Interstitial gate before ANY product content:** As a returning VIP member who already knows the site, being stopped at a "You are about to enter LEGO.com" screen feels like a toll booth. Zero personalization, zero recognition.
- **Play Zone section competes with the shopping path:** The yellow "THE PLAY ZONE / START PLAYING" block is visually louder (bright yellow, LEGO logo) than the "Continue" button above it. Wrong visual hierarchy for an adult shopper.
- **No loyalty or VIP signal anywhere:** I have VIP points. I'm here to spend them or check a double-VIP event. Nothing on screen acknowledges that I exist as a member.
- **Zero product imagery:** Not a single set, not a single Botanical leaf. Complete brand content desert.
- **Minifig characters skew young:** The two minifigures illustrated in the Play Zone section look like kid-targeted characters. Alienating for AFOL.

## Recommendations

- **Personalize the gate for logged-in VIP members:** If I'm already signed in, skip the gate entirely — or at minimum show my VIP points balance in the header before I click "Continue."
- **Visually subordinate the Play Zone:** Drop it below the fold or reduce it to a text link — the kids section should not visually compete with (or outshine) the adult shopping CTA.
- **Add one above-the-fold content hook:** Even a single text line like "Double VIP Points: July 4–13" above the Continue button would tell me there's a reason to push through the gate now rather than later.
- **Replace the generic icons with a campaign image:** The three thin icons (what look like a store, a bag, and a circle) communicate nothing. A thumbnail of a recent LEGO Botanical or Icons set would anchor adult collectors immediately.
