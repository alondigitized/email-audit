# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened LEGO.com and hit a wall — not a homepage, but a bifurcation gate asking me to choose between "Continue" (shop) and "START PLAYING" (the Play Zone). Before I can even see a product, I'm also staring at a cookie consent banner eating the bottom third of my screen. This is a kid-routing interstitial masquerading as a landing experience, and for a VIP member hunting a double-points weekend, it is an immediately deflating first impression.

## What's working

- **Clean bifurcation logic:** The two-path design (Continue / Start Playing) is visually unambiguous — I know "Continue" gets me to the shop without guessing.
- **No render bugs:** Everything loads clean. No broken assets, no placeholder text, no layout shift visible in the capture.
- **Cookie notice is readable:** Text is legible, links to Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy are visible — not buried in tiny gray type.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a gate, not a homepage:** I came to shop and I'm seeing zero products, zero offers, zero sets. This is friction before value.
- **"THE PLAY ZONE" and minifigure imagery are clearly child-targeted:** As a 38-year-old AFOL spending real money on UCS Millennium Falcon-tier sets, this opening frame is not for me. It signals I'm an afterthought.
- **No VIP/loyalty hook anywhere:** LEGO's VIP program is genuinely good — double-point weekends are a real purchase trigger for me — and it's completely invisible here.
- **Cookie banner stacks with the interstitial:** Two pieces of friction at once. I have to dismiss the cookie notice AND click Continue before I see anything useful.
- **"You can shop, get support & more" is the weakest possible copy:** That "& more" is doing zero work. It reads like a placeholder.

## Recommendations

- **Swap this interstitial for a true mobile homepage:** Route adults directly to the shop experience and handle Play Zone discovery from within the nav. The bifurcation screen serves the org chart, not the customer.
- **Surface the VIP entry point immediately:** A logged-in VIP member should see their points balance and any active bonus-point events above the fold — that is the single most powerful hook LEGO has over Amazon for AFOLs.
- **If the gate must stay, make "Continue" work harder:** Replace generic copy with something like "Shop Sets, Track Retirements & Earn VIP Points" — three words that would stop me from bouncing.
- **Consolidate friction:** Merge the cookie acknowledgment into the Continue flow with a single checkbox rather than running two consent steps before the user sees a single product.
