# LEGO® Insiders Weekend is coming!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a save-the-date teaser for LEGO Insiders Weekend (11/21–11/22). It is pitched on "double loyalty points and incredible gifts with purchases." The offer is vague, and the rendered hero is the weakest part. The green diamond frame is empty: a big blank dark-green block sits where the hero image should be. That is the one visible render bug, and it sits right where the eye lands first.
- The rest is competent but diffuse. There are two competing hero CTAs ("Build a wish list" and "Learn more"), a four-product grid, a sweepstakes ("win your wish list", ends 11/22) and an app promo. The email never says what the double points are worth or what the gifts are. The sweepstakes is the most concrete hook, but it's buried below the fold. It's also the fourth Insiders send in a row built around products rather than a clear offer, after 11/14 "Room for one more set, Leo?" and 11/11 "Leo, forgotten someone on your gift list?".
- Verdict: a mediocre teaser with a broken-looking hero. Three themes compete in the body, with no price, no gift reveal and no single focus.

## What's working

- "Leo" personalization in the hero subhead and the member card at the bottom.
- The points balance and value (0 / $0.00) in the header is a nice loyalty touch.
- The dates (11/21–11/22) are specific and give credible urgency.
- Each product tile shows piece count and Insiders points earned, which is useful at-a-glance detail.
- The "Win your wish list" block has a clear deadline (Ends 11/22) and a single "Enter now" button.

## What's weak

- The hero is a large empty dark-green diamond with no image. It looks like a failed image load and wastes the prime screen space.
- The offer is vague: "special promotions", "incredible gifts" and "double points" with no thresholds, products or amounts.
- Two stacked hero CTAs ("Build a wish list" and "Learn more") split intent, and neither says what you get.
- The product grid is generic and the same on every Insiders send. It has no tie to Leo's history, and "Add to wish list" is a low-value action for a teaser.
- Too many modules compete: wish list, sweepstakes and app download. Nothing is the clear lead.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put real imagery there (a gift-with-purchase set or a "2X points" graphic) with the dates in the headline.
- 2. State the offer: "2X points + free gift on $X+, Nov 21–22."
- 3. Cut to one primary CTA, such as "Set your reminder" or "Preview the gifts."
- 4. Move the sweepstakes up, or drop the product grid. Don't run both.
- 5. Personalize the grid from the member's wish list or browse history.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Insiders Weekend: double points + gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus enter to win your whole wish list. Ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mark Nov 21–22: double points and free gifts with purchase.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "LEGO® Insiders Weekend is coming!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a responsive table layout with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and a color-scheme declaration. Confirmed defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a personalized barcode image served over plain `http://`. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **49 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, per the `cl.s50.exct.net` host). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, so this is a coverage gap rather than a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure image source:** The "Insider Barcode" image loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjMs…`. It is flagged twice, once for each desktop/mobile variant.
  - Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or rewritten by clients, which would break the barcode.
  - The `qs` token is a per-recipient personalization parameter sent unencrypted. Its contents are opaque, so I can't tell whether it exposes anything sensitive.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 8836 of 11717 characters (about 75%) are URLs. This is consistent with long tracking links and can hurt spam scoring and text-only readability.
- **UTM parameters:** The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm any UTM parameters on destination links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 36 image warnings for missing alt text.** The QA tool can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from an empty one.
  - **Content images:**
    - Product images `11387_Prod.png`, `10371_Prod.png`, `72306_Prod.png` and `bltf1df1459ba3b1736-40875_Prod_en-gb.png`, all on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…`
    - Hero GIF `01_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Reveal-1711-hero.gif`
    - `03_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Reveal-1711-WYWL_Content-card.png`
    - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
  - **Likely decorative:** These need an explicit `alt=""` if they don't already have one.
    - `02_…-Divider.png`
    - `GreyBrick.png`
    - `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png`
    - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`
- **Duplicated assets:** Most images appear two or more times. This points to desktop/mobile swap blocks built with `.show-on-desktop`, `.hide-on-desktop` and `.mj-image-swap-*`. If a client ignores the media queries, both versions render, and screen readers will read both.
- **Generic title:** `<title>LEGO</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Legacy doctype and external fonts:**
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, which is acceptable for email.
  - Fonts load through Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import`. Clients that block external CSS fall back to the stack `LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`, so this is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were reported. The dynamic "Insider Barcode" `LiveContent` image is a personalization element, and its `http://` source is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. The cause is relay capture, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** These were not in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.
- **Sender:** `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com` is a no-reply address, so recipients can't reply. This is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but it is worth noting.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 49 tracked links were skipped and the source was truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified. Product images pull directly from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…` with `fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400`. That is fine functionally, but the images are hot-linked from the main site's CDN.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the barcode image:** Serve `LiveContent.aspx` over `https://`, and confirm the barcode renders in clients that block mixed or insecure content.
2. **Add alt text:** Give every content image descriptive `alt` text, including the product names, the hero and the content card. Mark decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, dividers, separators) with `alt=""`.
3. **Confirm the unsubscribe headers:** Check the raw message at the receiving MTA for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Also check `Authentication-Results` for SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. The relay may be dropping them.
4. **Trim the plain-text part:** Shorten tracking URLs in the plain-text alternative, or use a cleaner text rendering with fewer inline links.
5. **Set a descriptive `<title>`:** For example, "LEGO® Insiders Weekend is coming!"
6. **Re-run with the full HTML:** Probe a sample of the redirect links, verify UTM and landing-page continuity, and check the footer for the unsubscribe link and address.
