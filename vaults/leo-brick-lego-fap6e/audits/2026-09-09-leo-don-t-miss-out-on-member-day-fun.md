# Leo, don't miss out on Member Day fun
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the same-day follow-up to today's own "Leo, it's LEGO® Insiders Member Day!" send — same green diamond hero, same 2x points bag icon, same digital membership card footer — except now it's framed as "last chance," with a visible render bug in the headline ("Don't miss out **ondouble** loyalty points!" — missing space). Sending two near-identical emails on the same calendar day for the same perk is aggressive cadence, and the second one arrives with a copy defect that undercuts the urgency it's trying to sell.
- The offer itself — 2x loyalty points on selected items, ending today — is clear and single-minded, and the "Dive into your favorites" personalized category row (Star Wars, Harry Potter, LEGO Icons, Ideas) is a nice touch. But a loyalty-points perk is inherently a soft ask; it doesn't reduce price and won't move someone who isn't already deep in the points ecosystem. Combined with the typo and the fact this is a re-send of the morning's exact template, this lands as a weak, slightly sloppy urgency nudge rather than a compelling reason to act.

## What's working

- Single, clear CTA ("Go! Go! Go!") with strong visual contrast against the green background.
- "Quick, Leo – it's your last chance to claim" personalizes the urgency with the recipient's name.
- Personalized "Dive into your favorites" row (Star Wars, Harry Potter, LEGO Icons, Ideas) reflects actual browsing/purchase categories.
- Digital membership card at the footer with barcode is a genuinely useful loyalty touchpoint, not just filler.
- App download CTAs are clearly presented for members wanting mobile access to perks.

## What's weak

- Render bug in the hero headline: "Don't miss out **ondouble** loyalty points!" — missing space, reads as a typo.
- This is a near-duplicate of the same-day "Member Day" send (2026-09-09) — same hero art, same 2x icon, same layout, just re-labeled "last chance." That's cadence fatigue within hours, not days.
- Points balance shown is 0 / $0.00 — for a member with no points, "double loyalty points" has zero perceived value, undermining the entire premise of the email.
- No specific products or set names are tied to the offer — "selected items" is vague and gives no reason to click into a particular category.
- No visible deadline time (just "ends today") — real but not sharply urgent since there's no clock or hour count.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline typo immediately — "ondouble" reads as broken/sloppy and actively hurts trust in an urgency-driven send.
- 2. Don't send this as a second same-day email chasing the morning's Member Day send — fold both into one send, or clearly differentiate the follow-up as a reminder with new information (e.g., "6 hours left") rather than a re-skin.
- 3. Suppress or reframe this offer for zero-point members — either show what double points would be worth on a specific item ("Double points = enough for $X off your next Star Wars set") or route zero-balance users to a different message entirely.
- 4. Name 2-3 actual qualifying products in the hero instead of "selected items" to give the personalized favorites row a direct tie-in to the offer.
- 5. Add a real countdown (hours remaining) rather than "ends today" to sharpen urgency credibility.
