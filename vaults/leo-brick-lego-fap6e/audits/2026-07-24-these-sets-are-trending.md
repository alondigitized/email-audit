# These sets are trending
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's product-recommendation email — the algorithmic "here's what's trending" format that appears between event-driven campaign sends. Compared to the SpongeBob Day launch (7/22) and the Insiders Days blitz before it, this email has no event hook, no IP centerpiece, and no urgency lever. It's a holding pattern between real sends.
- The "we saw these and thought of you" framing tries to simulate curation but undercuts itself immediately by pairing Water Lilies (adult botanical, 18+) with the Fast & Furious Toyota Supra MK4 (automotive/movie IP) — two product lines that rarely live in the same collector's wishlist. The algorithm is showing and the editorial mask slips.
- The rewards-unlock section at the bottom is the most interesting element here — a Space Arcade Poster redeemable reward — but it's buried below the product recs rather than leading. This is the one signal that could have driven a click and it's treated as an afterthought.

## What's working

- **Rewards unlock is a genuine hook.** A redeemable Space Arcade Poster reward unlocked is a concrete, member-only benefit — more compelling than a generic "Shop now."
- **Product detail signals are present.** Piece counts and age ratings are visible under each product, which is useful for a builder audience making a purchase decision.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean.** White-background product section → dark-green rewards → light-green app promo flows logically with no cluttered panels.
- **Insiders card footer with name and balance** grounds the email in loyalty context and makes it feel personalized at the close.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is a ghost.** "These sets are trending" is 22 characters of nothing — no product, no offer, no name. Compare to "Leo, the party's not over yet 🎁" (7/13) which put name + hook in the subject.
- **Thematic mismatch in product recs.** Water Lilies (meditative, adult-botanical) next to Fast & Furious (car-license, nostalgia-IP) reads as algo output, not curation. The "thought of you" claim doesn't survive the pairing.
- **No pricing visible anywhere.** Not a single set shows a price or a discount — making "Shop now" a click with no rational basis.
- **Points balance (9 pts / $0.00) is prominent and deflating.** Showing Leo he has almost zero loyalty value in the header doesn't motivate engagement — it's a reminder that he hasn't earned anything yet.
- **Orphaned "Shop now" button.** There is a third CTA button visible below the Fast & Furious section with no product image or title associated with it in the render — the product block above it appears missing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the reward, not the products.** "You've unlocked a reward" as the hero headline with the Space Arcade Poster as the visual would give this email a reason to exist. Move product recs below the redemption CTA.
- 2. **Put the name and an offer in the subject.** The 7/13 birthday email proved LEGO knows how to write name-personalized subjects — use it here.
- 3. **Show prices.** Even without a discount, showing "$59.99" anchors the decision and turns "Shop now" into "add to cart."
- 4. **Fix or remove the orphaned third CTA.** A "Shop now" button with no product context is a broken experience; either surface the third product or cut it.
- 5. **Edit the header points display.** Hide or de-emphasize a $0.00 points balance — show points only when the number is motivating.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, your reward is ready`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Water Lilies, Fast & Furious, and a free poster`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem your Space Arcade Poster — plus two sets we think you'll love.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders reward unlocked. Shop the sets trending right now.`

## 1. Overview

## Technical Audit — LEGO "These sets are trending"

**From:** `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com` | **Date:** 2026-07-24

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has one confirmed broken-experience issue (HTTP image source), a cluster of missing alt text across repeated footer blocks, and compliance gaps in unsubscribe headers and physical address detection. Two QA "broken link" failures are false positives from the checker probing `<link rel="preconnect">` tags as navigable URLs.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**False positives — not real issues:**
- `[FAIL] https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` flagged as 404 broken links. These are `<link rel="preconnect">` hints in `<head>` — they are not navigable URLs and should not be probed by the link checker. No action needed; update the checker to skip `rel="preconnect"` elements.

**Real issue:**
- **HTTP image source:** The Insider Barcode image is served over HTTP:
  ```
  http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NDd9...
  ```
  This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent dynamic image. Modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook 2019+) block mixed-content HTTP images. The barcode will fail to render for a significant portion of recipients.

**Tracking links:** 28 click-redirect links were skipped by the checker — destination integrity unverified. Manual spot-check of product CTAs recommended.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text (12 flagged instances across repeated footer blocks):**

| Image | Instances |
|---|---|
| `GreyBrick.png` | 4 |
| `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` | 6 |
| `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` | 3 |
| `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` | 1 |

Decorative images should carry `alt=""` explicitly; functional images (e.g., the Insider logo) need descriptive alt text. Screen readers will announce the filename or skip unpredictably.

**Insider Barcode has no alt text:** If the LiveContent HTTP image is blocked, recipients see a broken image with no fallback label — compounding the HTTP issue above.

**Custom font not loaded:** `body { font-family: LEGO Typewell, ... }` references a proprietary typeface not loaded via `@font-face` or any Google Fonts link. Silent fallback to Noto Sans/Arial occurs everywhere — expected behavior for email, but worth noting if brand rendering is a concern.

**Redundant Google Fonts requests:** Noto Sans Display is loaded twice — once via two `<link>` tags in `<head>`, and once via `@import` inside `<style>`. This causes 2–3 redundant HTTP round-trips on load.

**Duplicate media query blocks with conflicting rules:** Two `@media screen and (max-width: 599px)` blocks both target `.zigzag__mobile-x-padding` with different values (`width: 20px` vs `width: 40px`). The last declaration wins in CSS cascade, but this is a latent bug that could break zigzag layout on mobile if block order changes.

**Dark mode declared, no overrides present:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` opts into dark mode, but no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules exist. Apple Mail and Samsung Mail may invert or shift background colors unpredictably.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The Insider Barcode uses a Salesforce LiveContent URL with encoded subscriber context:
```
http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYiOjEsImQiOjQ5NDd9...
```
If this resolves per-subscriber at send time and the subscriber is not an Insider member, the image will fail silently (no alt text, no fallback). No merge token syntax (`%%...%%` AMPscript or similar) is visible in the truncated source — full HTML should be audited to confirm no unresolved tokens remain in production sends.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **Not detected** | Not captured through AgentMail relay — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy (2024) |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **Not detected** | One-click machine unsubscribe not verifiable |
| Physical mailing address | **Not detected** | CAN-SPAM §7 requires valid postal address in body — may be in truncated HTML, needs full-source confirmation |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | **Unknown** | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture; sending domain `e.crm.lego.com` status unverifiable |

The missing `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are the highest-risk compliance gap — Gmail and Yahoo now enforce these for bulk senders and may route to spam or surface their own unsubscribe UI instead of LEGO's.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The truncated source prevents full UTM audit of the 28 tracked links. The presence of click-redirect domains confirms tracking infrastructure is in place. Recommend spot-checking 3–5 product CTA links to verify:
- UTM parameters present (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` at minimum)
- Landing pages are in-stock and price-consistent with email copy
- No redirect chains longer than 2 hops (deliverability risk)

---

### 7. Recommendations

**Priority 1 — Fix before next send:**
1. **Upgrade Insider Barcode to HTTPS.** Replace `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` with its HTTPS equivalent, or route through an HTTPS proxy. This is a broken-experience issue for Gmail and Outlook users.
2. **Add alt text to all images.** Decorative images: `alt=""`. Functional images (Insider logo, barcode): descriptive text. Eliminates 12+ accessibility warnings.

**Priority 2 — Fix in next template revision:**
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are set at the Salesforce Marketing Cloud send configuration level — they will not appear in the HTML body and may simply not be passed through the relay.
4. **Confirm physical mailing address** is present in full HTML source (not visible in truncated version).
5. **Deduplicate Google Fonts requests.** Remove the two `<link>` tags and rely solely on the `@import`, or vice versa — not both.
6. **Resolve duplicate media query conflict** for `.zigzag__mobile-x-padding` (`20px` vs `40px`) — consolidate into a single block with a single value.

**Priority 3 — QA tooling fix:**
7. **Update link checker to skip `<link rel="preconnect">` elements.** The two `[FAIL]` results for Google Fonts are false positives that suppress the real 22% pass rate signal.
