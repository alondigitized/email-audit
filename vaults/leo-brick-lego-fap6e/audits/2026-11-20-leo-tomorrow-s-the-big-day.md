# Leo, tomorrow’s the big day!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a countdown teaser for LEGO Insiders Weekend (11/21–11/22). The timer reads 0 days, 8 hours, 25 minutes, 34 seconds, which makes the "tomorrow's the big day" subject honest. The deadline is credible and the sender is trusted. The body undercuts it, though. The hero below the timer is a huge empty green diamond pattern that looks like a missing image. It pushes the offer about 400px down the page.
- The real offer is buried in the middle. "Two gifts with purchases" (Retro Jukebox at $230, Winter Snow Globe at $170) is the only concrete incentive, and it sits below a four-product wish-list grid. The top-of-email copy ("double loyalty points, amazing gifts… and more") is vague. The reader sees "Build a wish list" and "Learn more" before any dollar figure.
- This is a competent loyalty reminder with a good timer and a wasted hero. It repeats the Nov 17 "Insiders Weekend is coming!" teaser almost beat for beat. It's about a 5, with its best content hidden.

## What's working

- The live-style countdown timer is the strongest element. It gives credible urgency and matches the subject.
- The subject names the recipient ("Leo") and the timing, so it's personal and time-bound.
- The gift-with-purchase block names specific thresholds ($230 and $170) and the end date, 11/22.
- The product grid is clean and shows piece counts. Dragon Ball, Executor and Gingerbread AT-AT suit an adult-fan audience.
- The points and value badge (0 pts, $0.00) and the member barcode at the bottom reinforce the loyalty context.

## What's weak

- The hero area is a large blank green diamond pattern with no product, offer or image. It reads as a broken or missing image.
- The promised "double loyalty points" is never quantified, and there is no clear headline offer above the fold.
- The primary CTAs, "Build a wish list" and "Learn more", are weak. They don't send the reader to a purchase.
- Both "Add to wish list" buttons and the product grid ask for a low-commitment action rather than a purchase. The wish list buttons carry no urgency or price.
- The gift-with-purchase block is small and low-contrast. Its fine print ("$250 this weekend" in the intro versus $230 and $170 on the cards) is confusing.
- The sweepstakes block ("enter for your chance to win") uses a generic heart graphic and dilutes the message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the empty hero with the gift-with-purchase offer: show the Jukebox and Snow Globe, with "Double points + 2 free gifts, tomorrow only". Put the offer above the fold, directly under the timer.
- 2. State the double-points mechanic as a number, such as "2x points on every set, 11/21–11/22".
- 3. Swap "Build a wish list" for "Shop Insiders Weekend", and make the primary button solid and singular.
- 4. Fix the threshold inconsistency. Either use the $250 intro or the $230/$170 cards, not both.
- 5. Move the sweepstakes block below the app promo or cut it.
- 6. Mention something personal, such as the points balance or a wish-listed set, since Leo has 0 points.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $170 and get a free Winter Snow Globe. Ends 11/22.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard table layout with responsive media queries, dark-mode meta tags and a hidden preheader. The confirmed defects are missing alt text on roughly 15 unique images and one non-HTTPS image (the Insider barcode). The header and tracking-link warnings are inconclusive because of how the email was captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 51 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no HTTP status, redirect chain or destination was checked. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text version:** 9,261 of 12,758 characters (about 72.6%) are URLs. This exceeds the 70% threshold, which points to long, unshortened tracking URLs in the text part. It is a minor deliverability and readability concern.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over `http://`: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjYs…`. Gmail's image proxy upgrades http images, but some clients may block or warn on them. A member barcode that fails to load is functionally important.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged 15 unique images, each reported twice, likely because of desktop and mobile variants.
  - **Content images that need descriptive alt text:**
    - `11390_Prod.png`, `11387_Prod.png`, `40806_Prod.png`
    - `blta6d4a31cdd14d65d-75457_Prod_en-gb.png`
    - `01_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Countdown-2011-hero.gif`
    - `32c1h6ssjeb9m64o3id9g.gif` (gifcdn.com)
    - `04_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Countdown-2011-GWP_01.png`
    - `04_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Countdown-2011-GWP_02.png`
    - `03_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Countdown-2011-WYWL_Content-card.png`
    - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
  - **Likely decorative:** `GreyBrick.png`, `iconpath.png`, the `0x_…-Divider.png` files and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_…png`. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The checker cannot tell an empty alt from a missing one.
- **Third-party hosts:** One GIF is hosted on `gifcdn.com`, outside LEGO's own `image.e.crm.lego.com` domain. This adds a dependency on a third-party host.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML was cut off, so MSO/Outlook conditional comments, link text and the body markup could not be inspected. The visible head has no confirmed defects. `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag and the `color-scheme` meta tags are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders the first name ("Leo,") and no unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are absent from the captured headers, but the QA note says AgentMail may strip them. This cannot be confirmed as a sender defect. The `From` address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay, not the sender.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address were not verifiable because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. All 51 tracked links were skipped, and the visible product image URLs point to `www.lego.com` CDN assets, not click destinations. No confirmed issues.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the Insider barcode over HTTPS (`https://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx…`).
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the product, hero, GWP and WYWL images. Set `alt=""` explicitly on dividers and spacer images such as `GreyBrick.png` and `iconpath.png`.
3. Rehost the `gifcdn.com` GIF on the LEGO image CDN.
4. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part, or accept the 72.6% URL ratio as a known trade-off of tracked links.
5. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve and sample the redirect chains for the 51 tracked links to check UTM parameters and landing-page destinations.
