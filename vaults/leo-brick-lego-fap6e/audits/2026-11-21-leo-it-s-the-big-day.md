# Leo, it's the big day
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the payoff send for the LEGO Insiders Weekend campaign, and it is the weakest of the three. The subject says "it's the big day", but the hero says "The fun has begun! Have you checked out our big membership weekend yet, Leo?" That is a soft, rhetorical question, not a deal. Nowhere in the render is there a concrete number such as "2x points ends Sunday" or "$X off". The 11/20 countdown email had a real deadline, and this one drops it for "will be over before you know it".
- The body is a stack of modules. There is a hero with a family photo, a "You deserve a reward" block, four sets under "Member offer, alert!", a perks strip, an app-download box and a membership card. The perks strip lists "2x Loyalty Points", "4x Loyalty Points", "LEGO® Insiders Price" and "Gift with Purchase". But it is four icons with no mapping to products and no explanation of what earns which. The recipient has to guess which set qualifies for 4x.
- The email is clean, on-brand and has no visible render bugs. But it is generic, and it undersells the weekend it spent four sends building up. Judgement: a polished, lukewarm send that wastes its biggest day.

## What's working

- Clean green LEGO Insiders branding with a clear hero and a high-contrast "Shop now" button.
- The points and value tracker at the top and in the footer card ("Points 0, Value $0.00") signals the member context.
- The four-set grid (Venusaur/Charizard/Blastoise, E.T., Razor Crest, Maersk) shows piece counts and gives each product its own "Shop now" button.
- No broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage.
- The subject's "big day" honestly matches the weekend timing and continues the 11/17 and 11/20 sequence.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer is stated. "Special promotions, just for members" is the only offer language, with no %, $ or points multiplier.
- The hero question "Have you checked out our big membership weekend yet?" reads as a nudge, not as urgency, and "will be over before you know it" gives no deadline.
- The perks strip ("2x", "4x", "Insiders Price", "Gift with Purchase") isn't tied to any product or threshold, so it can't drive a decision.
- Every set in the grid is shown without a price or discount. "Bring home sets you love for less" never says how much less.
- "You deserve a reward" with 0 points offers a 2,400-point Retro M-Tron Explorer. For a recipient with 0 points that is a tease, not a reward.
- Six "Shop now" buttons are identical, and the hero CTA goes nowhere specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual offer in the hero. For example, "Double points on every set, through Sunday 11:59 PM", with the deadline on the page.
- 2. Map the perks to products. Badge each set with its own "2x points" or "4x points" tag, and state the Gift with Purchase threshold.
- 3. Show member price against regular price on each set, or at least "Save $X".
- 4. Replace the 2,400-point reward block for a 0-point member. Show points-to-go, or swap in a "Spend $X, earn a free set" message.
- 5. Make CTAs specific: "Shop 4x points sets", "Claim your gift", "See Insiders prices".
- 6. Cut the app-download box below the fold on a last-chance send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, 2x points ends Sunday night`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Insiders Weekend: your gift is waiting, Leo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double points, member prices and a free gift with purchase. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop Charizard, E.T. and Razor Crest at Insiders prices before the weekend ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based, MJML-derived HTML from Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`image.e.crm.lego.com`, `cl.s50.exct.net`) with dark-mode and responsive handling. The main confirmed issues are missing `alt` attributes on many images, one insecure `http://` image, and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified in this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **59 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure image source.** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over plain HTTP: `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNjcs…`. Mail clients and image proxies may block mixed content, and the barcode is a functional element. It appears twice, so the desktop and mobile variants are both affected.
- **Plain-text part is dominated by URLs.** 10,773 of 13,664 characters (about 79%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. This points to long tracking URLs inlined in the text alternative, which is a spam-filter risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on many images.** Each of these is flagged, usually twice (desktop and mobile duplicates):
  - Hero GIF: `01_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Launch2-2111-hero.gif`.
  - Reward graphic: `04_BFCM_Launch2_2711-Reward+1.png`.
  - Other content images: `Edjc9jd9ajc.png`, `Email+Angle+Divider+VI+aihscia.png`.
  - Product images: `72153_Prod.png`, `75447_Prod.png`, `…21370_Prod_en-gb.png` and `…40955_Prod_en-gb.png` on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/`.
  - Footer images: `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`.
  - Spacer or decorative images such as `GreyBrick.png` and the dividers should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute. The hero, reward and product images carry content and need descriptive text. With images blocked, the hero and reward content would be lost.
- **Web font via `@import`.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans…')` is stripped by Gmail and others. The `font-family` stack falls back to `Arial, sans-serif`, so this is low impact.
- **Generic `<title>`.** The title is `LEGO` and does not describe the email.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"`, `viewport`, `color-scheme` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present, and the preheader is hidden correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders the first name ("Leo, it's the big day"), and the captured source shows no unresolved merge tokens. Coverage is limited because the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects.** The QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` header. It attributes this to the AgentMail relay, which may strip them. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated HTML does not show either, so they could not be checked.
- **Sender.** The From address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`, a no-reply address on a dedicated sending subdomain. That is acceptable, but it gives recipients no reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** Destination links are wrapped in SFMC click-tracking redirects, and the probes were skipped. No UTM values or landing-page alignment could be confirmed from the captured data.
- **Product images are hotlinked.** They load from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…` with `fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400`, which is consistent with the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF, the reward graphic and the product images, and set `alt=""` on `GreyBrick.png` and the dividers.
2. Serve the Insider Barcode over HTTPS (`https://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx…`).
3. Shorten the plain-text part by removing inline tracking URLs, or reduce the number of links shown there.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the original message, because the relay hides them. Also confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
5. Resolve a sample of the 59 tracking links to confirm UTM tagging and landing pages.
6. Replace the generic `<title>` with a campaign-specific one.
