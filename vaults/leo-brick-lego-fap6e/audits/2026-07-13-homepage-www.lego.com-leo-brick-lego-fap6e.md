# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened lego.com and hit a wall — literally. What I'm looking at is not a homepage, it's a country/region entry gate with a cookie consent banner stacked on top of it. The screen is split: blue "main site" entry up top, yellow "Play Zone" kids section below. Right now LEGO is presenting itself as a brand primarily worried about legal compliance and routing children to games — which, fair, but I'm 38 and I came here to check if the Botanicals are on double-VIP this week.

## What's working

- **Clean two-zone split:** Blue = adult site, Yellow = kid zone. The color coding is instant and legible on mobile — I know which button to tap without reading.
- **No layout breakage:** Icons render cleanly, cookie banner doesn't obscure the CTAs. At least nothing is broken.
- **Cookie notice is present and readable:** Short, plain English, links to policy — better than the dark-pattern banners I usually see.

## What's weak

- **"THE PLAY ZONE" is the dominant visual:** Yellow is the warmer, louder half of this screen. My eye goes there first — to a section designed for children. That's backwards for a platform where AFOLs drive a huge chunk of D2C revenue.
- **"Continue" is the weakest possible CTA copy:** No product signal, no promise, no reason to feel anything. "Shop Sets" or "Browse VIP Deals" would be directional and relevant.
- **Zero VIP hook:** LEGO VIP is one of the better loyalty programs in retail. There's no "Sign in to see your points" or "Double VIP this weekend" — nothing. I have to get through this gate first and then go hunt for it.
- **Cookie banner eats ~25% of screen real estate:** It doesn't auto-dismiss, it doesn't have a single-tap "OK," and it competes with the two CTAs for my attention.
- **No product, set, or offer imagery at all:** I'm a visual shopper. The only imagery is two cartoon minifigures playing video games. Not a Botanical Collection flower, not a UCS Millennium Falcon, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "Continue" with a VIP-aware CTA:** "Shop VIP Deals" or "Explore New Sets" — something that activates purchase intent before I even land on the homepage.
- **Kill or minimize the Play Zone half on mobile:** On desktop, side-by-side makes sense. On mobile, stack it below the fold or collapse it into a secondary link. Let the adult experience own the first screen.
- **Surface a live campaign signal on this gate:** Even a one-line banner — "Double VIP Points ends Sunday" — would make me tap faster.
- **Consolidate the cookie banner into a single-tap dismiss:** Slim it down to one line + "Got it" button. Right now it's three lines of legalese fighting my CTAs for attention.
