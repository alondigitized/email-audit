# What’s worse than stepping on a LEGO® brick?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third LEGO Insiders onboarding email in six days (activation code May 15, welcome/preference May 17, now this May 21). The subject line — "What's worse than stepping on a LEGO® brick?" — is probably the best thing about it: a universally recognized cultural moment for any LEGO fan that earns the open. The problem is the email doesn't pay off the joke with a single clear answer or offer. Instead you land on five distinct sections (rewards intro, discount-building mechanics, Adults sets, popular product picks, LEGO Ideas) that scatter attention and leave you without a strong reason to do any one thing.
- The production quality is high — clean layout, no broken images, recognizable brand design system. The "Members can't get enough of these sets" block with Jurassic Park Jeep Wrangler, the Pokémon trio, and Series 29 is the most clickable section, but it arrives mid-scroll with zero price context or urgency behind it. For a fresh Insiders member still onboarding, the email is doing too many jobs at once and landing none of them decisively.
- Clear judgement: a well-dressed email with a killer subject and no offer underneath it. The cadence is also starting to feel heavy — a subscriber who got three emails in a week is more likely to hit unsubscribe than to explore their account.

## What's working

- **Subject line is genuinely clever** — the LEGO foot-injury joke is deeply embedded in fandom culture, and subverting it as a curiosity hook is the right instinct.
- **Adults Welcome section earns trust** — naming the adult fan segment explicitly, with recognizable architecture/botanical sets, validates why an adult is in this loyalty program.
- **Specific product names in the shopping block** — Jurassic Park Jeep Wrangler, Venusaur/Charizard/Blastoise, Series 29 are named rather than just shown, which gives real search targets.
- **Clean render throughout** — no broken images, no overlapping elements, no visual bugs visible anywhere.
- **LEGO Ideas pitch at the bottom** is a nice brand-building closer that feels different from a straight promo, and works well for engaged fans.

## What's weak

- **Preheader leaks utility links** — "View Email | My Account" is what inbox clients display as preview text. A wasted impression on a strong subject line.
- **Subject punchline never lands in the body** — the implied setup ("...not being a member") is never played out with a concrete payoff offer.
- **No price, no urgency anywhere** — five sections, zero discounts, no deadlines. Points-to-rewards is a vague value prop without a specific example (e.g., "500 points = $5 off").
- **Five competing sections dilute focus** — the eye doesn't know where to go after the hero. This reads like a quarterly digest, not an onboarding nudge.
- **Third email in six days** — the cadence is accumulating fast for a new subscriber who hasn't yet demonstrated behavioral engagement.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — replace the utility-link leak with a one-line copy that completes the subject's premise, e.g., "Missing out on member discounts — that's what." This alone lifts open-to-click conversion.
- 2. **Pick one hero action and cut the rest to a secondary strip** — this email should either be about earning points OR about discovering Adult sets, not both plus three more things. The shopping block is the most click-ready; make it the hero.
- 3. **Show a concrete points example in the rewards section** — "earn 100 points on a £20 set, redeem 300 points for £3 off" makes the program real. Right now "turn points into rewards" is abstract.
- 4. **Add set prices to the shopping block** — even a teaser format ("from £44.99") gives people a decision trigger.
- 5. **Throttle the cadence or segment** — if a subscriber hasn't clicked the May 17 email, this send should be suppressed or simplified.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your LEGO® foot-pain ends here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Not being a member. (That's worse.)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Missing out on member discounts — that's what. Start earning now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Turn every set you buy into points, rewards, and discounts.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Google Fonts probed as broken — false positive, not actionable.**
The QA checker issued 404s against `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` directly. These are CDN endpoints, not fetchable root URLs; the actual stylesheet links (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display…`) are valid. Dismiss these findings.

**Duplicate font `<link>` tags.**
The `<head>` loads `Noto+Sans+Display` twice — once weight `700` only, once weights `400;700`. The first load is redundant and adds a blocking request with no benefit. Remove it.

```html
<!-- redundant — remove this one -->
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
<!-- keep this one -->
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@400;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

**26 tracking links skipped by QA probe.**
All click-redirect links (via `e.crm.lego.com` click-tracker) were skipped. Destination URL health is unverified — validate final destinations independently, especially any landing pages tied to the "LEGO Insider" registration CTA.

**Plain-text body is 81% URLs** (`7021/8621` chars). This is a deliverability signal: spam filters that score plain-text parts will flag this. The plain-text version should be human-readable prose, not a dump of tracking URLs.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**11 content images missing `alt` text.** Every substantive image in the email — hero, navigation, dividers, sidekick, 3×grid, and fun-fact icon — has no `alt` attribute. This breaks screen reader accessibility and degrades the experience for subscribers with images blocked.

Affected `src` values (all hosted at `image.e.crm.lego.com`):
- `Lifecycle_Welcome_Send-2_Buying-For-Self_0924_Hero.jpg` (×2 — desktop + mobile swap)
- `Lifecycle_Welcome_Send-2_Buying-For-Self_0924_Member_Navigat…` (×2)

- `Lifecycle_Welcome_Send-2_Buying-For-Self_0924_Sidekick_Regis…` (×2)

- `06_Welcome_V3_T1_Insider_Fun-Fact_Icon.png`

Purely decorative images (dividers) should use `alt=""`. All others need descriptive text.

**`input { display: none !important; }` is a global rule.** This hides all `<input>` elements regardless of context. If any interactive elements (e.g., dark-mode checkbox hacks or AMP components) are added later, this will silently break them. Scope it to the specific selectors that require it.

**Two duplicate `@media screen and (max-width: 599px)` blocks** exist in the stylesheet — the second block defines `.two-column__cell` rules. These should be merged into a single media query block to avoid specificity ambiguity across email clients that parse CSS differently.

**`color-scheme` meta tags present** — correct implementation for dark mode support:
```html
<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">
<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light dark">
```
No issues, but note there are no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` overrides in the stylesheet, so dark mode appearance is client-controlled.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured by relay.**
The AgentMail relay did not surface this header. CAN-SPAM and GDPR both require a functional unsubscribe mechanism; major inbox providers (Gmail, Yahoo) require `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders. If the header is set at the ESP level (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, likely) but stripped or not forwarded by the relay, the relay config needs to preserve it.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders since February 2024) depends on this header. Its absence from the captured headers is a compliance risk if it is genuinely missing at send time — not just a relay capture gap. Verify against raw SMTP headers from a seeded inbox.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found.**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown for this send. `e.crm.lego.com` should be a properly authenticated sending domain; confirm alignment via a seeded inbox raw header dump rather than the relay capture.

**Unsubscribe link in body:** An HTML unsubscribe link is present in the email body (standard footer pattern for this template type). Body-level unsubscribe satisfies the minimum CAN-SPAM requirement, but header-level one-click is additionally required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
