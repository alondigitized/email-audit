# It’s a great time to be a member, Leo
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a Day-3 campaign-continuation send for LEGO Insiders Days (running 7/7–7/12), arriving two days after the launch email ("LEGO® Insiders Days are here, Leo!" — 8/10 on July 7th). Its job is to re-engage members who opened but haven't yet shopped. The creative restates the same three pillars — double points, Insiders Price, gifts with purchase — while adding two featured adult-collector sets (Jurassic Park Jeep Wrangler, X-Men: The X-Mansion) and a high-value points redemption option (Retro Magnets Bundle, 5,000 points).
- The body is genuinely well-executed: clean hierarchy, relevant products, the Instant Reward hook is a nice addition not present in the launch email. The problem is entirely at the inbox layer. "It's a great time to be a member, Leo" is a warm, personalized greeting that communicates zero about what's inside. There's no urgency signal, no offer teaser, no reason-to-open beyond goodwill. For a campaign with a ticking clock, that's a self-inflicted wound.
- The email earns strong post-open engagement — you're likely to click once you're in — but it leaves points on the table at the open stage, particularly given Leo's balance sits at zero (0 points / $0.00), meaning he's never redeemed and may be disengaged.

## What's working

- **Retro Magnets Bundle Instant Reward** is a smart addition — a specific high-value reward (5,000 points) that wasn't foregrounded in the July 7th launch email, giving this send a genuine new hook in the body.
- **Product selection is sharply targeted** — Jurassic Park Jeep and X-Mansion are premium adult collector sets with fan-base gravity; not generic fillers.
- **Three-benefit summary ("3 ways to celebrate")** is visually scannable and gives each pillar its own CTA, reducing cognitive friction for different shopper intents.
- **The campaign deadline (7/7–7/12) is visible in the body text**, anchoring urgency even if the subject fails to carry it.
- **App download module** at the bottom is a light, non-intrusive loyalty deepening mechanism that fits naturally at the end.

## What's weak

- **Subject line carries no offer and no urgency** — "It's a great time to be a member" could have been sent any day of the year. The campaign has a hard deadline (7/12, three days from now) that goes completely unmentioned in the subject.
- **"Explore now" hero CTA is generic** — it's visually clear as a button but says nothing about the destination or action; "Shop Insiders Days" or "Claim your rewards" would be immediately more actionable.
- **No acknowledgment of Leo's zero points balance** — a personalized nudge ("You haven't earned any points yet — today's the day") would convert a disengaged member better than a generic "great time to be a member" frame.
- **Featured products show no pricing** — for sets at this price tier, the absence of any price anchor (even just "From $X") makes it harder to act without leaving the email.
- **Cadence risk** — this is the second Insiders Days email in two days; without a new, differentiated hook (beyond restating the same benefits), it risks training Leo to treat the follow-ups as noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to include the deadline and a specific offer** — the July 1st pinball email and July 2nd Koenigsegg email hit 9/10 precisely because their subjects named the product and the window. This subject wastes the most expensive real estate in email marketing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 days left: double points + gifts with purchase, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, Insiders Days end Sunday — earn 2x on Jurassic Park, X-Mansion`
- 2. **Lead the preheader with the Retro Magnets Instant Reward** — it's the one genuinely new offer in this send and it belongs above the fold in inbox view, not buried mid-scroll.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem 5,000 points for the Retro Magnets Bundle — or earn them by Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insiders Days window closes July 12. 2x points + an Instant Reward waiting.`
- 3. **Upgrade "Explore now" to a specific CTA** — "Shop Insiders Days deals" or "Claim 2× points" gives the thumb a reason to move.
- 4. **Add a personalized 0-points nudge** — a single line above the hero ("You haven't earned any Insider points yet — there are 3 days left") would address the specific disengagement signal Leo's balance reveals. This is a data-driven personalization the brand already has.
- 5. **Surface at least one price on the featured products** — even a "From $X" anchor creates purchase confidence and reduces clicks that bounce from the product page once the price is seen.

## Technical Audit — LEGO Insiders Day Reminder (2026-07-09)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound but has systemic accessibility failures (21 images missing alt text) and unverifiable compliance headers due to AgentMail relay stripping. The broken-link findings for Google Fonts domains are false positives from the QA probe and not real deliverability issues.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**False positives — not actionable:**
- `[FAIL] https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` flagged as 404. These are `<link rel="preconnect">` and CSS `@import` endpoints — they don't respond to HEAD/GET probes directly. Not broken.

**Real concern:**
- 31 click-tracking links skipped by QA probe (redirect domain). These cannot be verified for destination integrity. No confirmed breakage, but landing page continuity (Section 6) is unverifiable without manual spot-checks.

**Duplicate Google Fonts requests:**
- Three separate font load calls in `<head>` for Noto Sans / Noto Sans Display, plus an `@import` in `<style>`. This causes redundant HTTP requests on every open.

  ```html
  <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700&display=swap">
  <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@400;700&display=swap">
  @import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans&display=swap');
  ```
  These should be consolidated into a single `<link>` with all weights.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — 21 images missing `alt` text:**

All decorative and content images omit `alt` attributes. Affected images include:

| Image | Role |
|---|---|
| `LEGOInsiderLogowhitelog.png` | Brand logo — should have `alt="LEGO Insiders"` |
| `01_20260709_Q3_InsidersDays_Reminder_Hero-Banner-Standard.gi` | Hero banner — content-bearing, needs descriptive alt |
| `04_20260709_Q3_InsidersDay_Reminder_Card-[1,2,3].png` (×2 each) | Promo cards — content-bearing |
| `05_20260709_Q3_InsidersDay_Reminder_Magnets.png` (×2) | Product image — content-bearing |
| `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (×3) | Decorative — should have `alt=""` |
| `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` (×2) | Logo — needs alt text |
| `GreyBrick.png` (×4) | Decorative spacer — should have `alt=""` |

Missing `alt` on content images breaks screen reader parity. Missing `alt=""` on decorative images causes screen readers to announce filenames. Both are WCAG 2.1 Level A failures.

**Minor — commented-out CSS:**
```css
/* @import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700&display=swap'); */
/* [style*="NotoB"] { font-family: 'Noto Sans Display', sans-serif; } */
```
Dead code in production send. Suggests the NotoB bold variant is loaded via `<link>` but not applied via CSS — bold text may be falling back to system fonts in some clients.

**List-Unsubscribe:**
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured by AgentMail relay. RFC 2369 compliance unverifiable.
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) not captured. Major inbox providers (Gmail, Apple Mail) use this for native unsubscribe UI. If missing in actual delivery headers, this will suppress the one-click unsubscribe button and increase spam complaints.
