# Leo, it’s LEGO® Insiders Member Day!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely new beat for the LEGO stretch I've been tracking — not another set drop, not another countdown clock, not a rerun. It's a "Member Day" perk email built entirely around 2x loyalty points, complete with the actual digital membership card (points balance, barcode, name "Leo") reproduced at the bottom. That's the strongest personalization signal LEGO has shown in weeks of sends.
- But the offer itself is thin gruel dressed up in confident design. "2x points on selected items" with no named category, no dollar equivalent, and a "T&Cs apply, ends today" that reads as an afterthought is the same pattern as the 2026-08-25 "latest and greatest sets" send — big visual polish, small actual incentive. And Leo's points balance is $0.00, so "double zero" is a slightly awkward thing to be celebrating a membership around.
- Net: cleaner and more differentiated than most of this brand's recent output, but the core offer won't move a zero-balance member to click today.

## What's working

- Single, uncluttered hero message — one offer (2x points), one CTA ("Shop now"), no competing blocks fighting for attention.
- The membership card reproduction (name, points, barcode) at the footer is a nice touch of real personalization, distinct from the generic "Leo" first-name token LEGO usually leans on.
- "Your favorite themes" row (Star Wars, Harry Potter, LEGO Icons, Ideas) is a real personalization signal tied to browsing/purchase history, not generic merchandising.
- Visually clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, on-brand green/white LEGO palette throughout.

## What's weak

- The offer has no teeth for this recipient: Leo's balance is 0 points / $0.00 value, so "double your points" has nothing to double — the email doesn't address that gap at all.
- "Selected items" is never specified anywhere in the visible copy — no category, no example product, no link preview of what qualifies.
- No product imagery or hero SKU at all — just an icon graphic (moneybag/points symbol), which undercuts click intent for someone who wants to see something they'd buy.
- "Ends today" urgency is buried in small italic text under the body copy rather than being a visual focal point (no countdown, no bold banner), weaker than LEGO's own countdown-clock sends (e.g. 2026-08-27, 2026-09-06).

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the qualifying categories or show 2-3 product thumbnails from Leo's favorite themes (Star Wars/Harry Potter) that qualify for 2x points — turns an abstract point multiplier into a concrete "buy this today" nudge.
- 2. Address the zero-balance reality directly — e.g. "Start earning double today" framing rather than implying Leo already has points to leverage.
- 3. Elevate the "ends today" urgency into a visual element (bold banner or simple countdown) instead of small italic fine print — consistent with what worked in the countdown sends.
- 4. Add a rewards-value translation ("2x points ≈ $X back on a $50 set") so the abstract point currency becomes tangible.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 2x points on Star Wars, Harry Potter & more — today only.`
