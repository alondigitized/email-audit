# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at isn't the LEGO homepage — it's a gatekeeper interstitial that splits me into "shop/support" vs "THE PLAY ZONE" before I've seen a single set, a single deal, or my VIP balance. As an AFOL who opens LEGO.com specifically to check for double-VIP weekends and set retirement news, this is the digital equivalent of someone blocking the store entrance and asking me if I'd rather shop or go to the ball pit. The brand looks like it's built for a seven-year-old's first visit, not a returning VIP who spends four figures a year on Botanicals and UCS ships.

## What's working

- **Clean visual split** — two distinct paths (Continue vs. Start Playing) are immediately legible; no confusion about which button does what.
- **"Continue" CTA** — pill-shaped, white on blue, high contrast; it's the obvious next step for a returning shopper.
- **Cookie notice** — present and readable; it's a legal box-tick but at least it doesn't cover the whole screen with a dark overlay.

## What's weak

- **"THE PLAY ZONE" section** — placing a kid-coded section with game/video CTAs alongside the shopping path immediately signals this site isn't thinking about me. I'm 38. I don't want to watch videos.
- **Zero VIP/member hook** — I opened this app because I suspected a double-VIP weekend. There is nothing here — not a banner, not a points balance, not a "VIP event live now" strip. That's a miss.
- **No product content above the fold** — no set imagery, no featured collection, no retirement countdown for a retiring set. The interstitial is 100% navigation friction, 0% desire creation.
- **Minifig characters** — the two characters in the Play Zone illustration are clearly aimed at kids. No Botanical. No UCS. No AFOL signal whatsoever.
- **"You are about to enter LEGO.com"** — this copy reads like a terms-of-service gate. It's cold, transactional, and does nothing to prime a collector.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or fast-track this interstitial for logged-in VIP users** — if I'm already signed in, skip the gate entirely and drop me on a personalized homepage showing my points balance, active promotions, and items in my saved list.
- **Surface a VIP/double-VIP banner before the gate if the session must exist** — a strip that says "Double VIP Points this weekend — earn 2x on every order" would make me rush through "Continue" instead of sighing through it.
- **Separate the adult and kids experiences with a recognizable signal** — even a small "AFOL" or "Adults Welcome" badge on the shopping path would tell me this store knows I exist.
- **Replace the generic "Play games, watch videos" copy with something that at least nods to adult fans** — e.g. "Watch set reviews, speed builds & more" to make the content lane feel relevant beyond age 10.
