# Ready for Halloween, Leo?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is finally a real seasonal hook after a stretch of member-day reruns and countdown clones — "Brick or Treat" is a genuinely new campaign beat, not a rerun, and the pumpkin-with-ghost hero art is the best visual moment in this whole tracked stretch. The purple-to-green Halloween palette is confidently different from LEGO's usual red/yellow, and the layout gives you a clear path: hero offer, product grid, loyalty reward, gift-with-purchase, community challenge, app cross-sell.
- The problem is depth, not concept. The hero CTA is a generic "Shop now" with no set name or price attached, and the two product tiles below (La Catrina, Halloween Skull Candle) show piece count and points but never a price — for a purchase decision email, that's a real gap. It's also a lot of email: five distinct CTAs stacked vertically (Shop now, Shop now, Redeem reward, Shop now, Enter now) before you even hit the app download block, which dilutes urgency rather than building it.
- Compared to 2026-09-15's Batman Day send (the current high-water mark at 6/10), this has stronger, more original art direction but a weaker single offer — Batman Day at least had an anniversary hook and a clear "20 years" narrative. This lands a shade below that, call it a 5.5, rounding to 5.

## What's working

- The pumpkin-jack-o'-lantern-plus-ghost hero image is distinctive, on-theme, and doesn't reuse a stale creative asset from prior sends.
- Purple/green Halloween palette is a real departure from the brand's usual red/yellow/blue, giving this send visual identity.
- Loyalty points balance and barcode membership card are both present and consistent with the "Leo" personalization pattern seen in prior Insiders sends.
- The Gift-with-Purchase banner (Scary Haunted Tree, spend threshold, expiry date) is a legible, complete offer with a real deadline.
- The "Haunted Halloween Challenge" block adds a community/UGC angle rather than just another product push, a genuine change of pace from the grid-of-products format in 2026-09-13.

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere on La Catrina or Halloween Skull Candle — only piece count and points, which stalls a purchase decision.
- Five separate CTA buttons (Shop now ×3, Redeem reward, Enter now) compete for attention with no clear primary action.
- Hero CTA "Shop now" doesn't name a set or price — it's the weakest, least specific CTA copy in the email despite sitting in the most valuable real estate.
- The gift-with-purchase fine print ("spend $100+ on selected sets*... while stocks last") is genuinely small and easy to skim past, bordering on buried-condition territory.
- Visual pacing is heavy — six content blocks before the footer is more than a single sitting justifies, especially with three of them ending in near-identical black "Shop now" pills.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "from $X" under at least the hero pumpkin set and the two grid products — right now there's no way to gauge affordability without clicking.
- 2. Cut the CTA count from five to two or three: merge the two grid "Shop now" buttons into a single "Shop the collection" CTA under the grid header rather than one per tile.
- 3. Make the hero CTA specific: "Shop the Jack-o'-Lantern set" instead of generic "Shop now" — ties the eye-catching art directly to a purchasable product.
- 4. Surface the gift-with-purchase deadline (09/21–09/30) more prominently — right now it's in small print inside an already-secondary banner.
