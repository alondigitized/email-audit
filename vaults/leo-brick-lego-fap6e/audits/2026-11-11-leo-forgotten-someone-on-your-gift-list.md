# Leo, forgotten someone on your gift list?

The "Don't forget the grownups!" hero, a minifigure in a reindeer costume hugging a wrapped present inside a green-and-yellow diamond frame, tells adult fans to treat themselves. Below it is a mostly empty white card with a tiny boat image and nothing else. ## What's working

- The reindeer-minifig hero is festive, seasonal and the first thing the eye lands on.
- "Don't forget the grownups!" is a clear, warm angle for adult LEGO fans.
- The "Double loyalty points" SpongeBob block is a concrete member benefit with a visible "Shop now" button.
- Each product tile shows piece count and Insiders points, which suits collectors.
- The Insiders header with the points balance and the member card at the bottom reinforce the loyalty framing.

## What's weak

- The white card under the grid is nearly empty, with a tiny boat image and no title, price or button, so it reads as a broken module.
- The "Last chance to gift these" card has an empty white pill where the CTA label should be, and the product has no name.

- 4. Name the "last chance" set and give it a date, or cut the claim.
