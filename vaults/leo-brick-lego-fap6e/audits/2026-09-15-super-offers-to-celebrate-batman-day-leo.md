# Super offers to celebrate Batman™ Day, Leo
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest LEGO send in the recent stretch, and it's not close. "Celebrate 20 years of LEGO Batman" gives a real anniversary hook, the Bat-Signal yellow-and-black hero art is genuinely eye-catching, and the email leans into a specific IP moment (Batman Day, 9/19) instead of another generic "new sets" grid or countdown clock rerun.
- The execution has one clear flaw that undercuts everything else: both primary CTA buttons — under the hero and under "Your bat-gift" — render as blank white pills with no visible label. That's the exact click path a subscriber would use, and it's broken. Everything above and below it (product tiles, gift-with-purchase, Mayhem Mode video game tie-in, app download) is well organized, but a shopper who scrolls past two dead buttons may just bail before reaching the fourth vehicle tile.
- Verdict: best creative concept of this LEGO run, dragged down by a render bug on the two buttons that matter most.

## What's working

- Batman Day + 20th-anniversary framing is a genuinely fresh beat, distinct from the countdown/new-set-drop formula seen on 9/1, 9/6, and 9/13.
- Hero art (Bat-Signal diamond, cowl silhouette, caution-tape border) is the most visually distinctive design in this LEGO stretch.
- Clear content stack: loyalty 2x-points banner → hero offer → product grid → GWP → video game tie-in → app cross-sell → membership card footer.
- Four distinct Batman vehicle/set tiles are visible without any lorem-ipsum or missing-image gaps.
- GWP block ("Get the Batman: Bruce Wayne and the Batsuit with $40+ purchase") is a concrete, specific incentive.

## What's weak

- Both primary CTA buttons (hero and bat-gift section) render as blank white pills — no visible button copy, a genuine click-blocking bug.
- Subject leads with "Super offers" — vague filler language that undersells the much stronger "20 years of Batman" hook actually used in the hero.
- Product tiles have no names, prices, or piece counts — just images on white cards, so there's no way to gauge cost or scale before clicking.
- "Ready for Mayhem?" video game section competes with the LEGO set offer for attention and dilutes the Batman Day set-shopping focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the CTA buttons first — this is a hard blocker on conversion; ship button copy like "Shop Batman Day" and "Claim your gift" before anything else.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to use the anniversary hook that's already in the hero copy instead of generic "Super offers."
- 3. Add price and set name under each of the four product tiles so shoppers can pre-qualify before clicking through.
- 4. Move or shrink the Mayhem Mode video game block below the fold entirely, or cut it — it's not a purchase driver for the Batman Day sets email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20 years of LEGO Batman starts now, Leo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Batman Day is coming — get your bat-gift, Leo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Batsuit gift with $40+, plus 2x points through 9/19`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Batmobiles, Batcave sets, and a free Bruce Wayne minifig`
