# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- What I'm seeing isn't a homepage — it's a routing interstitial, a fork in the road asking me to go to LEGO.com proper or the kids' "Play Zone." As a 38-year-old VIP member who came here to check whether that Botanical Collection set is about to retire, this is pure friction. There's zero merchandising, zero loyalty signal, and the dominant visual on screen is a child-oriented Play Zone pitch I have no business clicking.

## What's working

- **"Continue" CTA** — large, white, pill-shaped, impossible to miss. I know exactly how to get past this screen.
- **Clean render** — no layout shift, no broken assets, cookie notice is readable and non-obstructive.
- **Two-path routing logic** — in principle, separating the adult shop from the kids' Play Zone is sound UX; the execution just buries the adult path below the more visually aggressive yellow Play Zone block.

## What's weak

- **Play Zone visual dominates** — the yellow slab with LEGO logo, minifigures, and "START PLAYING" is louder than the adult "Continue" path. A returning VIP has to consciously suppress the wrong CTA.
- **Zero personalization or loyalty signal** — I'm logged into my VIP account on this phone. There's no "Welcome back, Leo" or VIP points balance anywhere. This screen treats me like a stranger.
- **No merchandising whatsoever** — I came here for a reason (Botanical, UCS, double-VIP event). The page gives me nothing — no teaser, no urgency, no hook.
- **Cookie banner stacks below the Play Zone CTA** — on a small phone screen the cookie notice competes for vertical space with the already-busy routing UI.
- **Interstitial exists at all** — high-tech, logged-in adult VIP should not see this gate. My account context should route me directly to the adult shop.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the interstitial for logged-in VIP members** — if I'm authenticated, the site knows I'm an adult. Route me directly to the homepage and save the fork for anonymous visitors.
- **Flip the visual weight** — if the interstitial must exist, make "Continue to LEGO.com" the dominant blue block and demote the Play Zone to a smaller secondary option below it. Right now the hierarchy is inverted.
- **Surface one loyalty hook on this screen** — even a single line like "Your VIP points: 1,240 — see what's new" would make this dead-end screen feel like a welcome mat instead of a speed bump.
- **Collapse the cookie banner into a dismissible strip** rather than a full-height overlay competing with the routing UI on mobile.
