# Room for a new build in your life?
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is another broken send from the same stretch I flagged on 2026-09-23 ("these won't be around for much longer," 3.00/10) — same failure mode, different products. Both hero product tiles (the jack-o'-lantern build and the haunted-house set) show a tiny gift-box icon and a green points-pouch icon sitting in empty space with no price, no set name, and no point value attached to either. The category strip at the bottom (Star Wars, Icons, Ideas, City) renders icons with invisible/missing text labels underneath.
- The "We can see a new set in your future... 🔮" hook is a decent seasonal tie-in to the Halloween stretch this brand has been running since "Ready for Halloween, Leo?" (2026-09-21), and the crystal-ball framing is at least a fresh angle rather than a straight countdown clock. But a subscriber can't tell what either product costs, what it's called, or why they should click — the email is asking for a click on faith alone.
- Judgement: this is a content-complete failure, not a design nitpick. Score accordingly.

## What's working

- Loyalty header (points/value module) at top renders cleanly and correctly shows the member's real balance.
- The crystal-ball "we can see a new build in your future" hook is a genuinely different creative beat than the last few countdown/member-day sends.
- The two hero product images (jack-o'-lantern LEGO build, haunted house) are high-quality and on-theme for the Halloween stretch.
- Digital membership card footer is consistent with prior sends and renders without error.

## What's weak

- Both product tiles are missing price and point-value text next to their icons — just a bare gift box and pouch icon floating in white space.
- Neither product has a visible name/label, so there's no way to know what's being sold.
- The bottom category row (Star Wars, Icons, Ideas, City) has invisible or missing labels under each icon.
- No visible price or offer anywhere in the email — this is worse than a discount-less email, it's a data-less one.
- Primary CTA button (the white pill under the products) has no visible text at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the data binding first** — this is the same root defect as the 09-23 send two days ago. Whatever token is supposed to populate price/point-value/product-name/CTA-copy is not resolving. This should block the send entirely until QA confirms real values render, not just icons.
- 2. Add explicit set names and prices under each hero tile ("Haunted House — $999.99 · 4,010 pcs") so the click has a reason behind it.
- 3. Give the CTA button real copy — "Shop New Sets" or "See the Builds" — an empty pill converts nobody.
- 4. Restore category labels under the bottom icon row so segmentation is legible.
- 5. Once content renders, lean harder into the crystal-ball hook in the subject line — it's underused there.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, your next build is already picked out`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We saw this LEGO set in your future 🔮`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two new builds, hand-picked for your collection.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Halloween sets just landed — see what's next.`
