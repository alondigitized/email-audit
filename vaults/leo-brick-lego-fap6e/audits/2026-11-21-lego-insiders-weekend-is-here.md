# LEGO® Insiders Weekend is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the payoff send for the LEGO Insiders Weekend campaign that began with "LEGO® Insiders Weekend is coming!" on 11/17 and "Leo, tomorrow's the big day!" on 11/20. The structure is solid: a green hero, a member-offer product grid, and a clear two-gift-with-purchase section. The "Ends 11/22" deadline lines up with the whole teaser run.
- The render has real problems, though. The primary CTA is a blank white pill with no label under "Ready to explore offers?", so the main action reads as broken. All four product cards (Pikachu, pirate ship, Tower Bridge, Downton Abbey) show only a tiny gift-box icon where the name, price and discount should be. The "Save on a dream set with member offers" header promises savings, but no number appears anywhere in the grid. The only concrete offers are the two gifts at $250 and $170.
- Verdict: good campaign continuity, weak execution on the page. The CTA and product-card gaps cost it clicks. I'd put it around a 5, in line with most of the recent sends.

## What's working

- The "Two gifts with purchases*" block gives clear spend thresholds ($250 and $170), a deadline (Ends 11/22) and "Shop now" buttons.
- The hero is on-brand: a diamond-framed photo of two adults building, with the Insiders Weekend badge.
- The points and value tracker at the top and the member card at the bottom reinforce the loyalty angle, and "Party hat on, Leo!" adds some personalization.
- It delivers on the 11/17 and 11/20 teasers, so a subscriber sees the same deadline and a credible "it's here" moment.
- The app download block (App Store and Google Play) is tidy and easy to spot.

## What's weak

- The main CTA under the hero copy is an empty white pill with no text. It is the first thing a reader would tap, and it looks broken.
- The four product cards have no title, price or discount, only a small gift icon. "Save on a dream set" shows no savings.
- The offer is split: member promotions are mentioned but never quantified, and the real offer sits far down the page in the gift section.
- Points show 0 and $0.00 twice, which makes the "Insiders" status feel hollow.
- The page is long and the offer is buried. The only deadline appears in the gift section, below a long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero CTA with real copy such as "Shop member offers", and add the product name, price and savings to each card.
- 2. Move the two-gifts offer into the hero, since it is the clearest value in the email.
- 3. Add a visible deadline, for example "Ends Sunday 11/22", near the top.
- 4. Link each product card to its own product page, and add a member-price line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Insiders Weekend: 2 free gifts with your order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, your Insiders Weekend gifts are waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $170 or $250 and pick up a free set. Ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Member-only deals on Pikachu, Tower Bridge and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`cl.s50.exct.net`, `image.e.crm.lego.com`) with responsive CSS, `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag. The confirmed technical problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and one non-HTTPS image. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay probably stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 51 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect behavior are **unverified**. This is a QA limitation, not a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 9,261 of 12,263 characters URLs (about 75%, above the 70% threshold). The long tracking URLs dominate the text alternative, which can raise spam-filter scores.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA on about 30 `<img>` instances.** Because the same images are listed twice, these look like desktop and mobile variants.
  - **Content images:**
    - `HeroLaunch1.png`
    - Product images from `lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…`: `72152_Prod_en-gb.png`, `21363_Prod_en-gb.png`, `bltdc033ed672251563-21067_Prod_en-gb.png` and `21373_Prod.png`
    - `JukeboxGWP.png`
    - `GlobeGWP.png`
    - `Edjc9jd9ajc.png`
    - `iconpath.png`
    - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
  - **Likely decorative:** `GreyBrick.png`, `Email+Angle+Divider+VI+aihscia.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`. These need an explicit `alt=""`. Without it, screen readers may announce the filename. With images blocked, the hero and product imagery has no text fallback.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** the "Insider Barcode" `<img>` loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=…`. Mixed-content handling or image proxies may block it, so the Insiders barcode may not render. It does have alt text.
- **Font loading:** the `<head>` has two `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans+Display…">` tags plus an `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans…')` inside `<style>`. `@import` is unsupported or stripped in several clients. The `font-family` stack falls back to `Arial, sans-serif`, so the impact is cosmetic.
- **Title:** `<title>LEGO</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Minor:** `.preheader` is hidden with `display:none !important; height:0; width:0`. This is standard practice, but I can't confirm the preheader text from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens. The source is truncated, so the body and footer weren't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither was found. QA says the AgentMail relay may not preserve them, so this is a **possible** gap. Check the raw message headers at the origin before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay and not the sender.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these would be in the footer, which falls in the truncated portion. I can't confirm either from the data provided.
- The From domain, `e.crm.lego.com`, is a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with proper authentication but doesn't prove it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Click-tracking wrappers (`cl.s50.exct.net`) were skipped, and the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters weren't resolved. Product images point directly to the `lego.com` CDN, which is fine for asset hosting.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, product, `JukeboxGWP`, `GlobeGWP` and logo images. Add `alt=""` to the divider, `GreyBrick` and separator images.
2. Serve the Insider Barcode LiveContent image over `https://`. Check that the SFMC link-wrapping or image domain supports TLS.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the original headers, bypassing the relay.
4. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the raw message source.
5. Shorten the plain-text version, or use cleaner tracking URLs, to reduce its URL ratio.
6. Remove the redundant `@import` and keep a single font `<link>`. Set a `<title>` that matches the subject.
7. Re-run QA with a probe that follows the 51 tracking redirects, to check the final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status codes.
