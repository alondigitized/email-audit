# It’s never too early, Leo
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal Insiders send that tries to do three jobs: sell the Winter Village set, promote a 11/03 livestream, and push a four-product gift grid. The yellow-and-green diamond hero is on brand, and a woman building a snowy cottage fits "holiday traditions" well. The email loses its way once the hero is done.
- The product grid is the big problem. Four product cards (Downton Abbey, an elf, Mrs. Claus, a Santa fireplace) show images but no names, no prices and no points values. Only two tiny icons sit in empty white space under each one. The grid looks unfinished, and there is nothing to click for. Compared with the 10/24 Downton Abbey send, which gave one set the whole stage, this one spreads attention thin. Verdict: a pleasant, on-brand holiday nudge with a mid-email gap in the content.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and seasonal. The smiling builder with the Winter Village set sells the "family tradition" idea quickly.
- The black "Shop now" button on yellow is clear and high-contrast.
- The livestream block ("Save the date… 11/03 … could win a signed set!") gives a concrete reason to come back.
- The Insiders header shows the points balance and value up top, and the member card with barcode at the bottom reinforces membership.
- The black gift section with the candy-cane and gift confetti strip makes the holiday mood obvious.

## What's weak

- The four product cards have no product names, prices or points. The white space below each image is empty apart from two small unlabeled icons.
- The hero names no set and no price, and its copy is generic ("this cozy new addition"). The email has no offer at all.
- The "Points 0 / Value $0.00" header, shown twice, reminds Leo that they have nothing to spend.
- There is no deadline or urgency. "Never too early" is soft, and the only date is the 11/03 livestream.
- The bare email address at the very bottom is a stray, unstyled line that looks like a leftover.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill in the product cards with a set name, price and points earned. Every card should have a visible "Shop" link. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Name the hero set and attach a concrete hook, such as an early-access date, a gift-with-purchase threshold or bonus points. Rename the CTA "Shop Winter Village".
- 3. Cut the grid to two products, or make it clearly themed (for example "under $50"), instead of mixing a very large castle set with small figures.
- 4. Hide the "0 points" balance for members with no points, or replace it with a way to earn some.
- 5. Add a real date to the subject, such as the livestream or an early-shopping window.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, responsive Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`cl.s50.exct.net`, `image.e.crm.lego.com`). Its main technical problems are widespread missing alt text, an `http://` image source, and an unverifiable header and authentication status because of the relay. The source is truncated, so findings are limited to what the HTML excerpt and the QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **45 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure image source.** The "Insider Barcode" image loads from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNDIs…`. It is flagged twice, so it appears in at least two places. Some clients and proxies will block or rewrite it, and it can trigger mixed-content warnings. The barcode is functional content, so a blocked load means a broken in-store or member feature.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 8,256 of 10,657 characters (about 77%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This is typical of long tracking URLs, but it is a spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text is widespread.** QA flagged about 30 instances. These are the likely meaningful ones:
  - Hero banners: `01_Holiday_Email-3_Project-Cheer-Sustain_Hero-Banner-Standar…` (flagged twice)
  - `05_BFCM_Reveal-0311-Slim-Banner.png`
  - Product images: `21373_Prod.png`, `blt2b3eb4df7154d352-40865_Prod_en-gb.png`, `bltf1df1459ba3b1736-40875_Prod_en-gb.png`, `blt6caf97af32ad6473-40874_Prod_en-gb.png`
  - `Logo+Container+logo+white.png`
- **Likely decorative images** are `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png`. They should carry an explicit `alt=""`, not a missing attribute. With images off, or in a screen reader, the banners and products have no text equivalent. The blocked-image state also exposes filenames or nothing.
- **Duplicated content blocks.** The same images repeat (`21373_Prod.png` twice, each product twice, `GreyBrick` and footer points about 6 times). The source is likely duplicating desktop and mobile variants through `.show-on-desktop` / `.hide-on-desktop` and `.mj-image-swap-*` classes. If both variants are in the DOM, screen readers may read the content twice unless the hidden variant has `aria-hidden` or `mso-hide`. Only `.hidden` carries `mso-hide:all`.
- **Web fonts.** There are two `<link>` requests for Noto Sans Display plus an `@import` for Noto Sans. `@import` and `<link>` fonts are ignored by Gmail and Outlook, which fall back to the declared stack (`'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`). The duplicate font links are redundant.
- **Comment before the DOCTYPE.** `<!-- -->` precedes `<!DOCTYPE …>`. This can push older IE-engine clients into quirks mode, but modern clients are unaffected. Low severity.
- **Other markup that looks fine:** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, `color-scheme` / `supported-color-schemes`, a hidden preheader class, and media queries at 599px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders as "It's never too early, Leo" with the first name resolved, and no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `${…}` tokens appear in the visible excerpt. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a proven violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe headers, so verify on the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain (`e.crm.lego.com`) is aligned with the image host, which is consistent but not proof.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the footer body.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are wrapped in ExactTarget click tracking and weren't probed, so destination URLs and campaign tags are not visible.
- **Product imagery** is served from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…?fit=bounds&canvas=1:1&width=400&height=400` rather than the email CDN. It is consistent with the site, but it depends on site CDN availability and the asset names are not stable.
- **Landing-page alignment** was not testable with this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, BFCM banner, logo and product images. Set `alt=""` on `GreyBrick`, the footer points graphics and the separators.
2. Serve the Insider Barcode over `https://`. Confirm in the SFMC LiveContent configuration that it supports it.
3. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the relay's omission as a sending defect until then.
4. Confirm that the desktop and mobile duplicate blocks are hidden from assistive tech (`aria-hidden`, `mso-hide`).
5. Shorten tracking URLs or add a short, meaningful plain-text alternative to bring the plain-text URL ratio below 70%.
6. Probe a sample of the 45 tracking redirects for final destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
7. Remove the duplicate Google Fonts `<link>` and the comment before the DOCTYPE.
