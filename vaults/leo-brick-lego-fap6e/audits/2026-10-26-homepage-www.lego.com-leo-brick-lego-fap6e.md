# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- I opened lego.com and got a gate before I saw any LEGO. A two-panel modal splits "LEGO.com" (shop, support) from "THE PLAY ZONE" (games and videos), with a cookie banner stacked underneath. It's very on-brand, but it's aimed at kids, and it hides the homepage, so I can't tell what the brand is pushing right now.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button:** it's the only filled, focus-ringed element in the blue panel, so I know which tap gets me to the store. "You can shop, get support & more." is plain and quick to read.
- **Blue-vs-yellow split:** it separates "shop" from "play" at a glance. I'm not a Play Zone guy, but I'm never confused about which side I'm on.
- **Branding:** the red LEGO logo tab and the primary colors are unmistakable and render sharply.
- **Cookie notice:** it's brief and names its Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy links, and it doesn't add a second blocking dialog.

## What's weak

- **Forced interstitial on arrival:** as a VIP who visits constantly, I don't want to click through a "which LEGO are you?" screen every time. It's a speed bump before I see a single set.
- **The "THE PLAY ZONE" panel:** the minifig-with-controller art and "START PLAYING" button are for kids. They give me nothing for Botanical or UCS builds.
- **Everything behind the modal is a dark blur:** I can see a yellow header strip and a row of dark tiles near the bottom, but no text, set names or offers. I can't tell if there's a VIP or double-points banner, and that's what I came for.
- **Modal plus cookie bar:** the stacked pair covers nearly the whole screen. I have to dismiss them before I can interact with anything.
- **No loyalty or adult-builder signal:** nothing says "Insiders," "VIP" or "18+."

## Recommendations

- Remember the "Continue" choice (or detect a signed-in account) so returning shoppers skip the Shop/Play Zone gate. Default everyone to the shop and put a small "Play Zone" link in the header instead.
- If the gate stays, add a third option to the blue panel, such as "Shop Icons & Botanical" or "Sign in to VIP." That gets an adult builder one tap closer to the sets.
- Fold the cookie notice into a slim bottom bar with an accept button, so it doesn't stack under the modal and block the screen.
- Put a visible VIP/double-points strip in the first view, so loyalty is on screen the moment the gate is dismissed.
