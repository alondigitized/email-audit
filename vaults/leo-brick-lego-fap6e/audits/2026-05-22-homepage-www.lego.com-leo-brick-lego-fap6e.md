# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I land on LEGO.com and immediately hit a gatekeeping modal asking me to confirm I want to enter the site — before I see a single product, deal, or VIP hook. The modal drops me into what looks like a kids' "Play Zone" pitch rather than anything resembling the Botanical Collection or UCS lineup I came here for. As a 38-year-old VIP member hunting double-VIP weekends, this is a frustrating cold shower before I've even loaded the actual shop.

## What's working

- **"Continue" button** is clean and clear — at least it's not a dark-pattern cookie wall with buried "reject all." One tap and I'm through.
- **Cookie notice** is legible and matter-of-fact at the bottom — not a forced full-screen blocker demanding action before anything else loads.
- **LEGO logo** is crisp and bright — brand recognition is instant, which is table stakes but at least it's present.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks everything.** I know there's a homepage behind this overlay — I can vaguely see a dark background — but I have no idea what's live. My VIP status, any active promo, any set launch — all invisible.
- **"THE PLAY ZONE" is the wrong hook for me.** The entire above-the-fold non-modal content targets children. "Play games, watch videos" is not why I opened LEGO.com at 11pm looking for Icons pricing.
- **Two competing CTAs with no hierarchy.** "Continue" and "START PLAYING" are stacked, similar weight — the page doesn't make it obvious which path is the main event.
- **No geo/personalization context.** Three small icons (store, bag, clock?) appear in the modal but give no information — no "Hi Leo, your VIP points are…" or even a country flag. A logged-in user gets zero acknowledgment.
- **Zero urgency or offer signal.** As a VIP member who times purchases around double-VIP windows, I'm getting nothing — not even a whisper of what's on promotion this week.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the active promotion in the modal itself.** If there's a double-VIP event or Botanical restock happening, one line of copy here — "VIP Double Points this weekend" — would stop me from bouncing before I even Continue.
- **Add a "Shop Now" path alongside "Continue"** that routes directly to the main catalog, so power users aren't forced through a generic entry screen.
- **Replace the Play Zone as the background content with adult-targeted creative.** The Orchid, the Wildflower Bouquet, the Millennium Falcon — something that signals "adult collector money is welcome here" even while the modal is up.
- **Detect login state and reflect it.** If I'm a VIP member, greet me by name or surface my points balance in the modal header — turning a friction moment into a loyalty touch.
