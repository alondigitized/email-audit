# Leo, a gigantic new set is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong product launch wrapped in a bloated template. The hero is a big Godzilla build with a clear "Bring the iconic Titan home" headline and the line "the biggest LEGO® Ideas set yet." That is a real hook, and it is stronger than the generic Black Friday countdown LEGO sent earlier today. The gift-with-purchase strip and the 4x points banner right under it add real offers.
- The email then loses focus. After the hero it stacks four unrelated sets (Grogu, STEM, Christmas Table Decoration, Bluey's house) and two points-redemption cards. It also adds an app-download block and a membership card with a 0 points / $0.00 balance. The Godzilla story is strong enough to carry the whole send, but it gets diluted into a catalog. The hero CTA is a small "Shop now" with no price and no set name.
- Judgement: a good idea at the top, a template everywhere else. It is a solid 6. It scores higher than the "counting down" teaser because the product is concrete and the offers are visible.

## What's working

- The Godzilla hero image is dramatic and instantly readable, with a clear headline.
- "The biggest LEGO® Ideas set yet" gives a concrete, shareable reason to care.
- The gift-with-purchase strip (Godzilla Logo and Footprint) adds a real incentive tied to the hero.
- The green "4x loyalty points, 4x the fun" banner makes member value visible and stands out against the black.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero has no set name, price, or piece count, so the click is a leap of faith.
- The gift strip's fine print is tiny and the "while supplies last" line is nearly unreadable, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The four "hot right now" sets (Grogu, Bluey, Christmas decoration) have nothing to do with the Godzilla story or each other.
- The membership header and footer card both show 0 points and $0.00. For this recipient that is a dead loyalty prompt, and the rewards cards (3,000 and 2,400 points) are out of reach.
- The email is very long, about 4,500px, with an app-download block and a Black Friday graphic near the bottom that add nothing.
- Today's earlier countdown email also pushed Godzilla, so this is a second Godzilla push in one day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email about Godzilla only. Add the set name, price, and piece count to the hero, and use a button that says "Build Godzilla — Shop the set."
- 2. Cut the unrelated sets and the app block. Keep one row of Godzilla-adjacent products (the poster, other Ideas sets) at most.
- 3. Enlarge the gift-with-purchase and 4x points offers so they read as the reasons to buy now. State the end date and the spend threshold in plain type.
- 4. Hide the points-redemption cards when the balance is 0. Replace them with a prompt like "Earn 4x points on this set."
- 5. Don't send a second Godzilla email on the same day as the countdown send. Merge the two, or space them out.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, build the King of the Monsters`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free gift with purchase and 4x Insiders points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The biggest LEGO® Ideas set ever, now in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, MJML-style template with a responsive media-query block, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and a resolved first-name token in the subject ("Leo,"). The QA data confirms two main problems: widespread missing `alt` attributes and an insecure `http://` image source. Several compliance and tracking checks couldn't be confirmed from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 60 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, status codes, and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 11,340 of 15,097 plain-text characters (about 75%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can add spam-filter risk. This comes from the QA check only, since the text part isn't visible in the truncated HTML.
- **Insecure image:** `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNzMs…` ("Insider Barcode") is served over plain HTTP. It appears twice. Clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning, and the member barcode may not render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 32 `<img>` warnings, covering these assets:
  - Hero and promo images: `01_BFCM_Launch3_2711-Hero-Banner-XL.png`, `02_BFCM_Launch3_2711-GWP.png.png`, `03_BFCM_Launch3_2711-4xPoints.png.png`, `05_BFCM_Launch3_2711-Reward+1(-1).png`
  - Logos and icons: `02_BFCM_Launch2_2711-Logo.png.png`, `iconpath.png`, `Logo+Container+logo+white.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png`
  - Product images from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…`: `75446_Prod_en-gb.png`, `21355_Prod.png`, `40743_Prod.png`, `10459_Prod.png`
  - Repeated `GreyBrick.png` (about 10 instances)

  Several look like spacers or separators (`GreyBrick.png`, `Footer_TopSeparator…`). If so, they need an explicit `alt=""`. The hero, GWP, points, reward, and product images carry content and need descriptive alt text. Most images appear in duplicate, which fits the desktop/mobile swap pattern (`.mj-image-swap-mobile` / `.mj-image-swap-desktop`). Both variants need alt text.
- **Image-only hero:** With the hero and GWP images missing alt text, image-blocked clients would show no content for those sections.
- **Web fonts:** Google Fonts are loaded through both `<link>` tags and a CSS `@import` (`Noto+Sans`). `Noto+Sans+Display` is linked twice with different weight sets. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so the fallback stack (`LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) applies there. This is expected, but the duplicate font requests add weight.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is acceptable for email. The Outlook conditional comments are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders as "Leo, a gigantic new set is here" with no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `${…}`. The truncated HTML doesn't allow checking the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both were not found (RFC 8058 one-click). The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Check against raw headers from the sending ESP.
- **Physical address:** The check didn't detect one. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected, and this can't be confirmed as missing.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, which is expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown.
- **Sender domain:** `e.crm.lego.com` matches the image host `image.e.crm.lego.com`, which supports alignment. This is not a defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 60 tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing destinations couldn't be checked. Product images do reference `www.lego.com` CDN assets with set IDs (75446, 21355, 40743, 10459), so verifying that the click targets match those sets is worthwhile.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the Insider Barcode LiveContent image over `https://`. Confirm the ESP supports it, and test rendering with images blocked.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, GWP, 4x points, reward, and product images. Use `alt=""` on decorative spacers (`GreyBrick.png`, footer separators). Apply this to both the desktop and mobile variants.
3. Resolve the 60 tracking links in a follow-up pass to confirm status codes, redirect depth, and UTM or campaign parameters.
4. Check raw headers from the original send for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. Also confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass, and check that a physical address is in the footer.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, or reduce its tracking-URL length, to bring it under the 70% URL ratio.
6. Remove the duplicate `Noto Sans Display` `<link>` and the redundant `@import` to reduce font requests.
