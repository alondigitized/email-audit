**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday teaser wearing a generic "perks of being an Insider" subject. The hero says "Black Friday is coming soon, Leo!" with a red-and-black BLACK FRIDAY graphic, but it promises "even more special promotions" without naming a single one. It arrives the same day as the "VIP 30% off starts now" and "Members 15% off Aero Series" sends in the vault, which makes it feel like filler.
- The bigger problem is the render. Both main CTA buttons are empty. The white pill under the hero copy has no label, and the pill at the bottom of the white rewards card is blank too. The rewards card is mostly dead white space around a green "1 000 000" points card. A reader gets no clear action from the two most prominent blocks.
- Verdict: weak and a little confusing. The member-gift module and the perk icons are the only useful content, and they sit below the broken part. It is a step down from the Insiders Weekend sequence (11/20–11/23), which at least had a real deadline.

- The hero is on theme: the red-and-black Black Friday art is bold and seasonal.
- The "Your crafty member gift*" module has a real offer: a Craft Set gift with a $75 purchase, available until 11/26.
- The four-icon perk row (Insiders Price, 4x Loyalty Points, Gift with Purchase, Rewards) is quick to scan.
- The app-download block with App Store and Google Play badges is a clear secondary action.
- The points and value display (0 points, $0.00) and the membership barcode reinforce the loyalty framing.

## What's weak

- Both main CTA buttons are blank. The one under the hero copy and the one in the rewards card have no visible label.
- The rewards card is mostly empty white space. The "1 000 000" points graphic floats with nothing explaining it.
- "You deserve a reward" sits next to a balance of 0 points and $0.00, which undercuts the message. The points widget appears three times, each showing zero.
- The Black Friday copy is vague, with no percentage, dollar amount or date.
- The only concrete offer, the gift with purchase, is buried halfway down in tiny type. Its "Shop now" button is small and low-contrast against the white card.
- The subject promises "perks" while the hero leads with Black Friday, so the message is split.

- 2. Put one concrete offer in the hero, such as the date Black Friday perks unlock or the exact member discount.
- 3. Remove the zero-points widget, or swap it for a message that fits new members, such as "Earn 4x points this weekend."
- 4. Move the Craft Set gift above the fold and make the "Shop now" button full-width.
- 5. Cut the oversized white rewards card down to the points graphic and one button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built from a MJML-style template and sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`cl.s50.exct.net`). It has no confirmed hard failures. The confirmed problems are missing image alt text and an insecure `http://` source for the personalized barcode image. Header-based checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified from the AgentMail capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 39 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 8127 of 10704 characters (about 76%) are URLs. The text alternative is dominated by long tracking links, which can raise spam-filter scores and is hard to read. This is not a link failure, but it is worth fixing.
- No broken or malformed links were identified in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
**Confirmed issues:**
- **Images without alt text** (the QA output lists several twice, which probably means desktop and mobile variants):
  - `01_LI-Weekend_Campaign-LastChanceContingency-2411-Hero.png`
  - `02_LI-Weekend_Campaign-LastChanceContingency-2411-Content-ca…`
  - `05_BFCM_Countdown_2611Reward1.png`
  - `iconpath.png`
  - `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (3 instances)
  - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`
- **Insecure image source:** The "Insider Barcode" image loads over plain HTTP (2 instances): `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNzAs…`. Gmail's image proxy and some clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, so the barcode, the one functional image, may not render. It does have alt text.
- **Image-heavy hero:** The hero and content-card images are likely carrying the offer. Without alt text, image-blocked and screen-reader users get nothing there. This follows from the alt issues above.

**Observed, not defects:**
- `lang="en"`, the viewport meta and `color-scheme` meta are present.
- Web fonts use `@import` and `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com`. Many clients ignore these, but the font stack has `Noto Sans, Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif` fallbacks.
- `<title>LEGO</title>` is generic. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject renders the first name ("Leo,") and no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The barcode comes from per-recipient dynamic content (`LiveContent.aspx?qs=…`). The truncated source and skipped probe mean I couldn't confirm it resolves to a valid image.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. Verify against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **Sender address:** The From address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`. This is a deliverability and reply-ability concern, not a CAN-SPAM violation.
- The footer unsubscribe link and the physical address fall outside the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unverifiable from the data provided. Click URLs are wrapped in the `exct.net` tracker, and the final landing URLs and UTM parameters were not probed or visible in the truncated source. No misalignment can be asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Serve the barcode over HTTPS.** Change `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx…` to `https://`, or use the Marketing Cloud secure tracking and image domain. This is the highest-impact fix.
2. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to the Hero, Content-card and BFCM Reward images. Use `alt=""` on purely decorative ones (`iconpath.png`, footer separators, footer logo).
3. **Check the raw delivered headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. This confirms one-click unsubscribe and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. **Rework the plain-text part** so it uses fewer or shorter URLs and has readable text between them.
5. **Run a full tracking-link resolution check** (follow redirects with a real client) to confirm the 39 destinations, their UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
