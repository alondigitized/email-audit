# LEGO® Insiders Weekend is underway!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third and final beat of the LEGO Insiders Weekend sequence, sent on the last day (11/22). It is the most honest of the four sends, but it is still a generic template. The hero reads "Maximize your LEGO® Insiders Weekend… There's still time, Leo!" on a green background, and the member-points widget sits above it. That widget shows "Points 0, Value $0.00", which is a weak first impression for a loyalty email.
- The body does have a concrete offer. "Two gifts with purchases*" (spend $250 for the Retro Jukebox, spend $170 for the Winter Snow Globe) is the real hook, but it is buried below a four-product grid. The grid (Nike Air Force 1 × LEGO set, Venusaur/Charizard/Blastoise, Downton Abbey, Maersk container ship) has no prices, no member discount, and no tie to the weekend. After that come a reward tile, a sweepstakes and an app promo. That is five or six competing asks in one email.
- Verdict: it is a competent but diffuse last-chance reminder. The urgency is credible, since the weekend ends today. The email does not use that urgency, and the best offer sits below the fold. It sits at the bottom of the 6/10 range set by the recent sends.

## What's working

- The sender is a recognized brand, and "LEGO® News" with Insiders branding reads as trusted.
- The deadline is credible. "Ends 11/22. While supplies last" makes sense as a final-day reminder.
- The two gift-with-purchase thresholds ($250 and $170) are concrete and have visible Shop now buttons.
- The product grid is clean: four tiles, each with piece count and a consistent button.
- The sweepstakes ("1 million loyalty points") adds some excitement and a reason to click for people who won't buy.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. "Maximize your… Insiders Weekend" is vague and does not mention double points or the gifts. The hero CTA is a single generic "Shop now".
- The points widget shows "0 points / $0.00" twice (top and in the "You deserve a reward" section). The email pushes a 2,400-point reward to someone with zero points.
- The product tiles have no prices, no member pricing and no points-earned badge. That makes the "member offers" heading hollow.
- The gift-with-purchase module is small, low-contrast text on grey, and it is the real offer. Several asterisks and "T&Cs apply" lines add fine-print friction.
- It is the fourth Insiders Weekend send in a row (11/17, 11/20, two on 11/21), so the cadence is heavy. The creative is largely the same green template.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the two gifts to the hero. "Spend $250 → free Retro Jukebox. Ends tonight." should be the headline, with one button to the qualifying sets.
- 2. Cut the module count. Drop the reward tile for a 0-point member, and either drop the sweepstakes or fold it into the footer.
- 3. Add price and points-earned to each product tile, such as "2x points today", so the grid shows the member benefit.
- 4. Make the gift modules full width with a larger product image and one clear threshold line each.
- 5. Add real urgency, such as an hours-left timer like the one in the 11/20 send, instead of "there's still time".
- 6. Suppress the points widget when the balance is zero, or replace it with a "Start earning" message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, last day: free Jukebox with $250`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: 2 free gifts + 2x points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $250 or $170 today and a gift is on us. Insiders Weekend closes at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Double points and gifts with purchase end tonight. Plus, enter to win 1M points.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, responsive media queries at 599px). Automated QA gave a 50% pass rate with 0 errors and 4 warning categories. The main problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, an insecure `http://` personalized image, and compliance headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 59 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unverified. Nothing was confirmed broken.
- The "Insider Barcode" image loads over `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…` (flagged twice, which suggests desktop and mobile variants). It is a plain-HTTP request to a tracking-domain host.
- The plain-text part is more than 70% URLs (10,716 of 14,678 chars). That is consistent with long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative, and it can hurt spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** is the main finding. About 17 unique images are flagged, each repeated (likely desktop/mobile duplicates):
  - Informative images need descriptive alt: `HeroLaunch1.png`, product images `43026_Prod.png`, `72153_Prod.png`, `21373_Prod.png`, `bltc4e81aefa63718a7-40955_Prod_en-gb.png`, and the reward banners `04_BFCM_Launch2_2711-Reward+1.png` and `01_LI-Weekend_Campaign-Sustain-2211-Reward.png`.
  - `JukeboxGWP.png`, `GlobeGWP.png`, `Edjc9jd9ajc.png` and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` are probably informative too.
  - Probably decorative: `GreyBrick.png`, `Email+Angle+Divider+VI+aihscia.png`, `iconpath.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png`. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The checker can't tell whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- `<title>LEGO</title>` is generic, which is minor.
- The stylesheet loads web fonts through `<link>` and `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/…')`. Gmail and Outlook ignore these, so the email falls back to `Arial, sans-serif`. The stack is `LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial`, so rendering will vary by client.
- `<!-- -->` precedes the DOCTYPE. This can trigger quirks mode in some legacy clients and is low severity.
- The source is truncated, so table nesting, MSO conditionals beyond `lte mso 11`, and dark-mode CSS couldn't be fully assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source or the QA output. The "Insider Barcode" image is dynamic Salesforce Marketing Cloud LiveContent (`LiveContent.aspx?qs=…`). It is member-specific, so it is likely a fixed-size render with no fallback if the request is blocked. See the `http://` issue above.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** not found (WARN). The QA tool notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so authentication status is unknown. The From domain `e.crm.lego.com` is aligned with the image and tracking subdomains.
- The visible footer unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated source or QA output, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters can't be verified. The 59 tracking links are redirect-wrapped (exct.net click domain) and weren't resolved, so landing URLs and campaign parameters are unseen.
- Product images are served directly from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/…` with `width=400&height=400`. This is consistent with the site's asset pipeline, with no mismatch evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every informative image (hero, products, reward banners, GWP images, Insiders logo). Set `alt=""` on dividers, icons and spacers.
2. Change the "Insider Barcode" `src` from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/…` to `https://`, and add fallback alt text such as "Your LEGO Insiders barcode".
3. Re-check the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message headers. The AgentMail relay may be stripping them.
4. Resolve a sample of the tracking links to confirm destinations return 200 and carry the expected UTM parameters.
5. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line, and make the `<title>` more descriptive, for example the subject line.
6. Trim the plain-text part, using shorter links or less repeated URL content, to lower the URL ratio.
