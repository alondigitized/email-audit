# New bricks, new builds
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's monthly "trending bricks" round-up format again — the same shape as the 7/24 "These sets are trending" send, but this time folded together with a Star Wars teaser and a Member Day reminder into one longer scroll. The headline hook ("Roll up, roll up!") is fun and on-brand, but the email never tells you what any of these four featured builds actually are — no names, no piece counts, no prices, just images and two tiny icon glyphs per card. That's a real step back from the product-forward creative LEGO ran on 8/1 and 8/2.
- The bigger problem is visible on the screen: both primary CTA buttons — the one under the trending grid and the one under the Member Day banner — render as blank white pills with no copy on them. That's a hard, observable bug, not a guess about markup; it kills the two clearest paths to a click in this email.
- Subject line is also the weakest of the recent run. Every prior send in the brand history used the recipient's name or a concrete hook ("Leo, you get to encounter it first," "Say hello to LEGO® Pokémon™ SMART Play™ sets, Leo"). "New bricks, new builds" drops both the personalization and the specificity, right as this brand is sending at a pace (six emails in roughly two weeks) where a generic subject is going to get buried. Net: decent brand trust carries this one, but the blank CTAs and vague subject pull it well below the 8-9s LEGO has been landing lately.

## What's working

- Loyalty bar (Points/Value) and member hub callout are front-and-center, reinforcing this is a benefits-bearing Insiders email.
- Product grid mixes categories well — art (Klimt), architecture (Tower Bridge), IP (E.T.), and space — giving a broad "something for everyone" read.
- Member Day teaser gives a specific, credible date ("Set a reminder for 08/11!") with a clear single-day urgency hook.
- Footer membership card with barcode, points, and value reinforces the loyalty-app tie-in cleanly.
- Visual system (green background, white cards, consistent iconography) stays on-brand and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- Both primary CTA buttons — after the trending grid and after the Member Day banner — render completely blank, no visible label. This is the single biggest miss in the email.
- No product names, piece counts, or prices anywhere in the four "trending" cards or the two Star Wars cards — pure image-only tiles with unclear tiny icons next to them.
- Subject line drops the personalization and specificity every recent send in this brand's history has used ("Leo, ..." / named IP).
- No offer of any kind — no discount, no free shipping, nothing to click toward beyond vague discovery.
- This is the second algorithmic "trending" round-up in under two weeks (following 7/24's "These sets are trending"), and it's landing in a six-email, two-week stretch — cadence fatigue risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the blank CTA buttons first.** Both primary buttons need visible copy ("Shop trending sets," "Set my reminder") — right now there's no clickable path forward that a recipient can even read.
- 2. **Name the products.** Add product name + price under each of the four trending cards and the two Star Wars cards; a blind grid of thumbnails doesn't sell anything on its own.
- 3. **Restore personalization to the subject line.** Every recent LEGO send used the recipient's name or a specific IP callout — this one didn't, and it's the outlier for a reason.
- 4. **Lead with the Star Wars section instead of the anonymous "trending" grid**, since that's the section actually tied to a franchise this persona has responded to before (per 8/2 X-Files send).
- 5. **Watch the send cadence** — six sends in under two weeks risks list fatigue; a "trending" round-up this soon after the 7/24 trending email reads like filler between bigger launches.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Star Wars sets are almost here, Leo`
