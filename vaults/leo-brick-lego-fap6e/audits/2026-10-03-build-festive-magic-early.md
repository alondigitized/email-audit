# Build festive magic early!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Early Access send for the LEGO Icons Holiday House. The yellow and green diamond hero is strong, and "Enjoy Early Access to festive joy!" gives a clear reason to open. The render is intact this time. The product tiles show prices and points, which they didn't on 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-25.
- It's a catalog email, though, not a campaign. The hero sells one set, then the email spreads across the winter village, four seasonal sets and two rewards. The Early Access window ("10/01 until 10/03") is already ending on the day this arrived, so the urgency is stale. The reward tiles are the weakest part. The member card shows "Points 0 / Value $0.00", which tells a zero-balance member they have nothing to redeem.
- Verdict: well-rendered and seasonal, but it arrived late and gives the reader no price or offer. About 6/10.

## What's working

- The hero is bold, with a diamond pattern and the Holiday House centered. The eye lands on it first.
- The Early Access key badge and the "build it first" member framing give a clear reason to act.
- Every product tile has a piece count, a points value and its own "Shop now" button.
- The black "Expand your winter village" block sets up a good second beat that cross-sells a train, a post office and a lodge.
- The tiles render cleanly, unlike the broken sends on 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-25.
- The app-download block and member card are tidy and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The date line "10/01 until 10/03" means the window closes on the day this arrived. The deadline is almost over and the copy never says so.
- There is no price, discount or free gift. Points are the only incentive, and they are small ("+390").
- The hero set shows no piece count, price or points, which is the opposite of the smaller tiles.
- The rewards section shows "Points 0 / Value $0.00", and the member card repeats it. That reads as an empty account, not a perk.
- The "charm is in the detail" strip has three unlabeled, uncaptioned images and no link cue.
- The email is long, with roughly eight CTAs and no single dominant action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around a deadline: "Early Access ends tonight." Show the price and points on the Holiday House itself, and make "Shop now" bigger.
- 2. Cut the email to the hero, one village block and four tiles. Drop the three-image strip and one reward.
- 3. Hide the rewards block, or replace it with a "you're 1 purchase away" message, for members with 0 points.
- 4. Add a sub-line of social proof or scarcity, such as "Members-only until 10/03."
- 5. Put the Early Access dates at the top of the hero, not in the body copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Holiday House Early Access`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Leo, build the Holiday House first`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get it 3 days early. Ends 10/03, plus 2x points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Candy canes, garlands and a new Icons set, yours before anyone else.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) template with responsive CSS and dark-mode meta tags. The confirmed defects are widespread missing image alt text and a cleartext `http://` dynamic image. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 60 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 12,864 of 16,734 characters URLs (about 77%), above the 70% threshold. This is likely long SFMC tracking URLs repeated in the text alternative, and it can hurt spam scoring.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, about 40 flagged instances):**
  - The hero banner `01_Holiday_Email-1_EA_Project-Cheer_Hero-Banner-Standard.jpg` has no alt text.
  - The 3-up grid image `03_Holiday_Email-1_EA_Project-Cheer_3xGrid_Detail-Hero.png` has none.
  - The 50x50 content images `05_..._Winter-SKUS_50x50-Conten…` have none.
  - The product images on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...` (`40875`, `40874`, `40865`, `40958` `_Prod_en-gb.png`) have none.
  - `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` and `Logo+Container+logo+white.png` have none.
  - The decorative `GreyBrick.png`, `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` and `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Light.png` are also flagged. If these are decorative, they should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
  - Most entries appear twice, probably from desktop and mobile variants. With images blocked, the hero and product content would be unreadable.
- **Insecure image source:** `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwMTgs...` ("Insider Barcode") is loaded over HTTP, flagged twice. Clients may block it as mixed content, so the barcode may not render. The `qs` token is also sent in cleartext.
- **Font loading:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans...')` and the `<link>` tags are ignored by Gmail and some Outlook clients. The fallback stack (`'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) covers this, so it is minor.
- **Generic title:** `<title>LEGO</title>` is not descriptive.
- Present and fine: `lang="en"`, viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `color-scheme` and the MSO conditional comments.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the truncated source. The barcode is dynamic via `LiveContent.aspx`, and its HTTP delivery is covered in section 3. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]**. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed, so verify against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]**. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Sender is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the footer, which was truncated out of the source. They could not be verified.
- Also note the `Noreply@` from address cannot receive replies.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters can't be verified. The 60 links go through SFMC click tracking and the destinations were not probed.
- Product images are served directly from `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/` (`en-gb` assets). That is a locale signal, and the landing locale was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero banner, the 3-grid image, the Winter-SKUS images and the four product images. Set `alt=""` on the decorative images (`GreyBrick.png`, the separators and the points icons).
2. Serve the Insider Barcode `LiveContent.aspx` image over HTTPS (`https://cl.s50.exct.net/...`). Add a text fallback for the member ID.
3. Check the raw headers in the source mailbox for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Don't treat the relay warnings as sender defects until then.
4. Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links repeated there.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, including the footer, to verify the unsubscribe link, the postal address and the UTM parameters. Resolve a sample of the tracking links.
6. Replace `<title>LEGO</title>` with a descriptive title (for example, the subject line).
