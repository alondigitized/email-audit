# Leo, these won't be around for much longer
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a broken render, not just a mediocre one. Every product tile is missing its price and its "get it before it's gone" urgency data — instead of dollar amounts and point values, the tiles show a tiny gift-box icon and a coin-pouch icon with nothing next to them. The CTA button at the bottom of the tile stack is a blank white pill with no label at all. On top of that, none of the four sets are named, so a "these won't be around for much longer" urgency email gives Leo zero information about what's disappearing or why he should care.
- Compared to the 09-21 "Ready for Halloween, Leo?" send and the 09-15 Batman Day email, both of which at least had a clean seasonal hook and legible hero art, this one regresses hard on basic execution. The "Get them before they're gone" retiring-sets concept is actually a decent lever — LEGO retirement FOMO is a real, proven driver — but it's undermined by a render that looks unfinished.
- Judgement: this email should not have gone out in this state. The concept has merit; the execution is broken enough to tank both trust and conversion.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand green/white LEGO Insiders header with points/value ($0.00) is visible and consistent with prior sends.
- "Get them before they're gone" is a strong, specific urgency hook — distinct from prior countdown/rerun beats.
- Four-product grid structure is easy to scan, alternating image left/right for visual rhythm.
- Category footer icons (Star Wars, Icons, Ideas, City) and the membership card / barcode footer render correctly.

## What's weak

- Every product tile is missing its price and points value — only a gift icon and an empty coin-pouch icon show, no numbers.
- No product names are shown anywhere in the four tiles — Leo can't tell what he's about to lose.
- The main CTA button is a blank white pill with no text — completely ambiguous, no verb, no destination.
- "App Store" / "Google Play" badges are the only fully legible CTAs in the whole email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the broken tile template first** — this is a data-binding/render failure, not a design problem. Product name, price, and points-value text must appear next to (or under) each set image before this email is usable at all.
- 2. **Label the CTA button** — "Shop Before They're Gone" or "Shop Retiring Sets" instead of a blank pill.
- 3. **Add a retirement date or "while supplies last" framing per set** to make the urgency concrete instead of generic.
- 4. **Lead with the product name in the subject or preview**, since the hero copy alone gives no hint what's retiring.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, 4 LEGO sets are retiring soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: these sets won't be restocked`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Starry Night, Hogwarts Castle, and 2 more — once they're gone, they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Retiring soon: grab these before they leave the shelves for good.`
