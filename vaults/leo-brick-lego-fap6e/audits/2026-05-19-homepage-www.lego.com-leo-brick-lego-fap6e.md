# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I didn't even make it to the homepage — what loaded on my phone was a full-screen modal forcing me to choose between the LEGO.com shopping experience and "The Play Zone" (games and videos for kids). The brand is clearly bifurcating its audience at the door, which makes sense in theory, but in practice it means zero product, zero VIP hook, and zero reason to feel excited before I've even seen a set. Right now LEGO.com reads like a bouncer at the rope, not a store.

## What's working

- **Clean modal render:** The gate itself is visually tidy — LEGO brand colors (blue/yellow), recognizable logo, legible type on mobile. No broken images or overlapping text.
- **Bifurcation intent is logical:** Separating adult shoppers from kids content is the right instinct at a brand level — LEGO does have a massive kids audience and it makes sense not to dump a 6-year-old into the VIP checkout funnel.
- **"Continue" is above the fold:** If I know I want the store, the path forward is one tap. Low friction once I understand the modal.

## What's weak

- **This is a wall, not a welcome:** Showing zero product before asking me to self-select kills any browsing momentum. I opened the app to feel the pull of a new set, not to answer a routing question.
- **No VIP acknowledgment at the gate:** If LEGO knows I'm a returning member (logged in on device), this modal should greet me by name or at least show my VIP tier — instead it treats me like a stranger.
- **The Play Zone gets equal visual weight:** Yellow section with minifigures and "START PLAYING" is visually louder than "Continue." An AFOL's eye lands on the toy characters before the shopping path.
- **Cookie banner competes with the modal:** The privacy notice occupies the bottom third of the viewport simultaneously with the routing modal — that's two competing things demanding attention before I've seen a single set.
- **No current campaign/offer signal:** Double-VIP weekends, new set reveals, retirement alerts — any of those would make me tap "Continue" faster. None are here.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the routing modal for authenticated VIP members** — if I'm logged in, LEGO knows I'm not a 7-year-old; skip straight to the homepage with my VIP balance in the nav.
- **Add a single editorial hook to the "Continue" card** — even one line like "New: LEGO Icons Wildflower Bouquet — shop now" turns the gate into a teaser and gives me a reason to rush through it.
- **Reduce the Play Zone visual weight for adult-detected sessions** — make it a small text link at the bottom rather than a full yellow module that competes for attention.
- **Stagger the cookie banner** — delay it by 2–3 seconds or collapse it to a small footer strip so it doesn't land simultaneously with the routing modal on first load.
