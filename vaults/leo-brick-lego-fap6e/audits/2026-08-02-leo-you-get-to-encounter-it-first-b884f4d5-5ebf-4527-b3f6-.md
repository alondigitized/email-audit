# Leo, you get to encounter it first
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's Insiders early-access drop for the new LEGO Ideas The X-Files set — a UFO-abduction/agency-office diorama stacked with eight minifigures (Mulder, Scully, Cigarette Smoking Man, and friends). The dark, moody art direction is the right call for this license, and it's the same "Classified" mystery-box framing that made the SpongeBob and Pokémon launches work. The gift-with-purchase (Scully's Lab, 8/1–8/10) and the Insiders loyalty card at the footer both reinforce that this is a members-first perk, not a generic blast.
- Where it falls down is timing and the subject line. This lands just one day after the 8/1 Pokémon SMART Play launch — LEGO is now sending big campaign emails on consecutive days, which risks the inbox fatigue the brand has mostly avoided so far. And the subject line never names the product, which is a bigger miss than usual since "The X-Files" is exactly the kind of specific, nostalgia-triggering noun that should be in front of the fold.
- Solid, on-brand creative execution undercut by a vague subject and compressed cadence — a 7-8, not a 9.

## What's working

- The hero product shot (UFO abduction scene) is the first thing the eye lands on — strong, single focal point.
- "Classified for LEGO® Insiders only" and the "not out there...yet" copy play directly into X-Files mythology — genuinely clever license tie-in.
- The GWP (Scully's Lab) has explicit dates (8/1–8/10) and "while supplies last," giving real urgency.
- The minifigure cast lineup functions as a mini reveal/collectible showcase, distinct from the two hero product shots.
- The Insiders loyalty card at the footer visibly grounds this as a member benefit, consistent with the "Insiders only" framing up top.

## What's weak

- The subject line never names "X-Files" or "LEGO Ideas" — a licensed IP launch buries its biggest hook.
- Both CTAs say "Shop now" — generic, doesn't differentiate the early-access buy from the GWP claim.
- Sent one day after the 8/1 Pokémon SMART Play email — back-to-back major launches risk cadence fatigue.
- The GWP offer relies on an asterisk + separate "Terms & Conditions" link rather than stating restrictions inline.
- The Insiders card shows 0 points / $0 value, which reads a little deflating right under a "member benefits" showcase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the product name in the subject line — this is the single biggest lever here. X-Files fans need to see the franchise to open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, X-Files LEGO Insiders Early Access is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The truth is in here, Leo — early access starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get The X-Files LEGO Ideas set before anyone else — plus a free Scully's Lab gift`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Insiders-only: shop The X-Files set first and claim a free mini build`
- 2. Differentiate the two CTAs: "Get Early Access" on the hero set, "Claim Your Gift" on the Scully's Lab block, instead of two identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 3. Space this out from the 8/1 Pokémon send — stagger major IP launches by a few more days to avoid two-in-a-row fatigue.
- 4. Put an explicit end-date/countdown on the early-access window itself (right now only the GWP has dates) to give the main offer the same urgency.
- 5. Surface the GWP restriction inline in one short line rather than only via the asterisk/terms link.

## 1. Overview

This is LEGO's Insiders early-access drop for the new LEGO Ideas The X-Files set — a UFO-abduction/agency-office diorama stacked with eight minifigures (Mulder, Scully, Cigarette Smoking Man, and friends). And the subject line never names the product, which is a bigger miss than usual since "The X-Files" is exactly the kind of specific, nostalgia-triggering noun that should be in front of the fold.
