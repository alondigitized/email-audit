# Homepage snapshot · www.lego.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- What I'm actually looking at isn't the homepage — it's the entry gate LEGO throws up before you even get in the door. The screen splits the world in two: adult shoppers hit "Continue," kids (or their parents) hit "START PLAYING" to reach the Play Zone. As an AFOL who VIP-hunts double-points weekends, I'm already mildly annoyed that I have to click through a splash screen just to reach the catalog. There's zero merchandising here, no offers, and no loyalty hooks — just friction.

## What's working

- **"Continue" CTA** is cleanly labeled and positioned — no ambiguity about which path leads to the store.
- **No render bugs** — the layout is crisp, fonts load, LEGO logo renders correctly. For a mobile splash this is table stakes, but at least it's not broken.
- **Cookie consent** is readable and non-blocking — it sits at the bottom without hijacking the whole screen.

## What's weak

- **The gate itself is the problem** — making me click "Continue" before I see a single product, offer, or category is pure friction with no value exchange.
- **Play Zone prominence over-indexes on kids** — the yellow "START PLAYING" block with minifigures is visually louder than the "Continue" path, which is the one a VIP collector actually wants.
- **Zero loyalty hook** — I'm a VIP member. There's no "Welcome back, check your points" or "Double VIP this weekend" anywhere. LEGO has my account data; they could personalize this splash and don't.
- **No merchandising whatsoever** — not a single Botanical Collection leaf, not a UCS set corner, nothing to remind me why I opened the app.
- **Cookie notice competes for real estate** — on a small phone screen it pushes both CTAs up and adds visual noise.

## Recommendations

- **Skip the gate for logged-in VIP users** — if I'm already authenticated, drop me straight onto the homepage with my points balance in the header. The entry screen is a new-visitor pattern; don't punish return shoppers.
- **If you keep the gate, swap the visual hierarchy** — make "Continue to Shop" the dominant yellow block and demote Play Zone to a secondary option. Right now the page is optimized for kids.
- **Surface one timely hook on the splash** — even "Double VIP points this weekend" in a small banner strip above the CTAs would justify the gate and give me a reason to rush through.
- **Merge the cookie consent into the splash UX** — a single "Continue & accept cookies" button eliminates the double-click and the visual clutter at the bottom.
