# All set for Halloween, Leo?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a Halloween catalogue send that fails on execution, and it follows the broken-render pattern from 2026-09-23 ("Leo, these won't be around for much longer", 3/10) and 2026-09-25 ("Room for a new build in your life?", 3/10). The four product tiles are missing their names, prices and point values. In their place are a small gift-box icon and a green money-bag icon with no numbers. The hero button and the "Create your own Black bouquet" button are blank white pills with no label.
- The creative idea is good. The purple-to-black spooky skyline, the skull hero and the "Go on, we dare you" headline set a playful tone. The skull is dark purple on purple, though, so it barely registers. The reader's eye lands on the headline and then on an empty button.
- My judgement is a mediocre send that looks half-finished. The Insiders points header and the footer membership card (Points 0, Value $0.00) make it feel like a rewards-program email with nothing to spend. The Builder app and LEGOLAND blocks pad the email without helping the Halloween push.

## What's working

- The purple-to-black-to-lime colour blocking with a bat silhouette skyline is festive and on-brand for the season.
- "Go on, we dare you" is a playful headline that suits the Halloween mood.
- The four product tiles are clean, with well-lit set photos (haunted house, carousel, skeleton figure, gothic castle) on white cards.
- The Insiders header and footer card keep the loyalty programme visible.
- The Builder app and app-store badges give a secondary reason to engage.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is a blank white pill. The main action has no label, so the top of the email has no clear next step.
- None of the four product tiles shows a name, price or points value. The tiles carry only two unlabelled icons and a generic "Shop now".
- The skull hero is dark purple on purple and nearly invisible, so the email has no strong first image.
- The "Create your own Black bouquet" block has a second blank white button under the copy.
- The loyalty card shows Points 0 / $0.00, which is a dead-end signal for the reader. There is no offer, deadline or urgency anywhere, and the lower half drifts into Builder app and LEGOLAND promotion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make every button render with a visible label. Fix the hero and bouquet CTAs first, and use "Shop Halloween sets" and "Get the Spider Pack". Use dark text on a light pill, or a solid brand-colour button with white text.
- 2. Restore the product data on each tile: set name, price and Insiders points (for example "2,614 pcs · $229.99 · 230 pts"). If the data can't load, hide the icons instead of showing empty ones.
- 3. Replace the faded skull with a brighter, higher-contrast hero or lighten the skull so it pops against the purple.
- 4. Add one real reason to act: a points multiplier, a free gift with purchase, or a ship-by date for Halloween delivery. Right now the email has none.
- 5. Cut the LEGOLAND and Builder app blocks, or move them below a single "Insiders perks" strip. Keep the email focused on Halloween.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, 4 spooky builds before Oct 31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dare to build? Halloween sets inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Haunted houses, skeletons and gothic castles, plus Insiders points on every set.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Oct 24 to build them in time for Halloween night.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based MJML-style template with a language attribute, a viewport meta tag and fallback fonts. QA scored it 38% with 0 errors and 5 warnings. The findings are mostly missing alt text, an HTTP-sourced image, and compliance headers or a footer address that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **54 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 10,123 of 12,488 characters (about 81%) are URLs, which points to long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative. This can hurt text-part readability and spam scoring.
- **One image loads over plain HTTP.** The "Insider Barcode" image comes from `http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi...`. It can be blocked as mixed content, and it carries a per-recipient `qs` token in an unencrypted request. Because it is a personalized member barcode, the plaintext exposure matters. It appears twice, so it is probably duplicated across desktop and mobile variants.
- **Link and font hygiene.** The `<head>` links the same Google font family (`Noto+Sans+Display`) twice, once with `wght@700` and once with `wght@400;700`. It also uses `@import` for `Noto+Sans`. That is redundant, and Gmail and some clients ignore these. The fallback stack (`LEGO Typewell, 'Noto Sans', Cera Pro, Arial, sans-serif`) mitigates this.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on many images.** Unique offenders, repeated across what look like desktop and mobile copies:
  - **Hero:** `01_20261007_Halloween_REMIND_Hero-Banner-Standard.gif`. This is an animated GIF carrying the main message.
  - **Product images:** `blt21fb8295f9caaf81-11383_Prod_en-gb.png`, `21369_Prod.png`, `bltcfca1e7974173b84-21372_Prod_en-gb.png` and `76788_Prod.png`. These are on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...`. If they sit inside links, the links have no accessible name.
  - **Content cards:** `05_..._ContentCard-BuilderApp.png`, `06_..._ContentCard-PAB.png` and `08_..._ContentCard-LEGOLand.png`.
  - **Footer images:** `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` and `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png`. `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` and `GreyBrick.png` are probably decorative. They should carry `alt=""` rather than no attribute, but QA can't tell whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **The hero is an animated GIF.** Without alt text, any key message in it is inaccessible, and clients that show only the first frame (Outlook desktop) lose it.
- **Responsive switching relies on class toggles.** The template uses `.show-on-desktop`/`.hide-on-desktop` and `.hide_on_mobile`/`.show_on_mobile`, which explains the duplicated images. If any copy is hidden with only `max-height` rather than `display:none`, screen readers can read the duplicate. The visible CSS uses `display:none !important` for the hidden variants, so this looks mitigated.
- **The HTML source was truncated.** Table roles, `<a>` text and `mso-hide` coverage beyond the CSS can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the evidence provided. The subject ("All set for Halloween, Leo?") resolved a first name. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}`. The personalized barcode image (`LiveContent.aspx`) resolved, but is the HTTP concern noted in section 2.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture issue, so it is unconfirmed. The header is still expected for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified, with the same relay caveat.
- **No physical address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one. The HTML was truncated and the footer isn't visible, so this is unconfirmed. It is likely present in the footer in practice for a sender of this size.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips or doesn't capture it. The From domain (`e.crm.lego.com`) and image host (`image.e.crm.lego.com`) are consistent, which is a good sign.
- **Sender address.** The From address is `Noreply@e.crm.lego.com`, so replies are not accepted. This is not a violation, but it is poor for deliverability engagement signals.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects and none of the 54 were resolved, so final destination URLs and UTM or campaign parameters are unknown.
- **Product images are hosted on `www.lego.com/cdn/cs/set/assets/...`.** The names (`11383`, `21369`, `21372`, `76788`) are set IDs, so product-to-landing-page alignment could be checked once the redirects are resolved.
- **Campaign naming is consistent.** Assets are named `20261007_Halloween_REMIND`, matching the subject's Halloween reminder.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero GIF, the four product images and the three content cards. For the hero, put the key message (Halloween offer or CTA) in the alt text and make the first frame self-sufficient.
2. Add `alt=""` to the decorative images: `GreyBrick.png`, the points icon and the footer separators.
3. Serve the "Insider Barcode" image over HTTPS (`https://cl.s50.exct.net/...`) to avoid mixed-content blocking and plaintext exposure of the member token.
4. Verify the headers on the original message at the MTA level, bypassing the AgentMail relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Confirm the footer's physical postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
6. Resolve and probe the 54 tracking links to check redirect depth, status codes and final UTM parameters.
7. Shorten the text part: use fewer or shorter tracked URLs, or add real prose between links.
8. Merge the duplicate Google Fonts `<link>` and `@import` declarations.
