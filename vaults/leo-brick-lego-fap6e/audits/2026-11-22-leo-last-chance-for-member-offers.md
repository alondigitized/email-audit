# Leo, last chance for member offers
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send of the Insiders Weekend sequence, and the first one to carry a real deadline. "Amazing member offers end tonight…" is an honest last-call hero. It is stronger than this morning's "LEGO® Insiders Weekend is underway!" (6/10), which was a generic template. The green hero, family photo and "Shop now" button read cleanly. Below that, the 2x2 product grid (Tower Bridge, Hubble Space Telescope, La Catrina, Aston Martin F1 car) is clear and tidy.
- The email undercuts its own urgency. The hero says "final hours" and "offers just for members", but never states what the offer is. "Double points" appears once, in small text under "Unmissable member offers". The gift-with-purchase tiers ($250 for the Jukebox, $170 for the Snow Globe) sit far down the page in tiny type. A reader who is deciding in the final hours has to scroll roughly two-thirds of the way down to find the actual deal. The header also shows Points 0 / Value $0.00, which makes the "boost your balance" pitch feel empty.
- Verdict: a solid last-call frame with the offer buried. Subject and hero agree on urgency, but the product grid is generic and the money details come too late. About a 6.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree: "last chance" and "end tonight" are consistent, so the urgency is credible.
- The green Insiders hero with the family photo is a strong, on-brand focal point, and "Shop now" is clearly visible.
- The 2x2 product grid is clean, with consistent cards, piece counts and a button on each.
- The gift-with-purchase section names real thresholds ($250 and $170) and a date (ends 11/22, while supplies last).
- The sweepstakes block and the app-download block add secondary reasons to engage, and the footer is complete and tidy.

## What's weak

- The hero never states the offer. "Explore amazing LEGO® Insiders benefits" is vague, and "double points" is only a small line under the grid heading.
- The gift tiers and the "ends 11/22" deadline are small and sit below the fold.
- The header shows Points 0 and $0.00, which undercuts the "boost your balance" message.
- Every product card has its own "Shop now", so six buttons look identical and nothing sets the main action apart. Prices are also missing from the cards.
- This is the third Insiders email in two days, and it repeats the creative of "Leo, it's the big day" (11/21) and "LEGO® Insiders Weekend is underway!" (11/22). The Retro Jukebox and Snow Globe gifts feel like a second pitch bolted on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero: "Double points + free gift ends tonight" with the $250 and $170 thresholds, and put the countdown or a clear "Ends tonight" line beside the button.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, for example "Earn double points", and cut the product cards' buttons to a single "Shop all member offers".
- 3. Move the gift-with-purchase block above the product grid and enlarge it, since it is the actual incentive.
- 4. Add prices or points-earned to each product card, such as "Earn 2x points: 7,490 pts".
- 5. For a member with 0 points, replace the empty points header with a sign-up or first-order message.
- 6. Suppress or consolidate this send if the recipient already got two Insiders emails today.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, double points + free gift end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: 2x points on LEGO® sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $170 for the Winter Snow Globe, $250 for the Retro Jukebox. Ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Double points on Tower Bridge, Hubble and more, until midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, ESP-rendered (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) email with a mobile media-query set and a font fallback stack. The confirmed problems are missing alt text on product and content images, an HTTP-sourced barcode image, and a very high URL-to-text ratio in the plain-text part. The authentication and unsubscribe header warnings are likely capture limitations, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 9964 of 13484 characters (about 74%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate the plain-text part and can raise spam-filter scores.
- **No broken links were confirmed.** The QA run reports 0 errors.
