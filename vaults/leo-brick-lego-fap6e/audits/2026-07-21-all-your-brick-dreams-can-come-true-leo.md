# All your brick dreams can come true, Leo
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is LEGO's monthly Insiders wrap-up email — a format switch after the Insiders Days campaign blitz that ended July 12. Coming nine days after that six-email sprint, the timing is right, and the wrap-up concept is genuinely useful for an engaged member. But the email tries to do too much at once: sweepstakes entry, reward redemption, three product recommendations, a teaser for the next event, and an app download push are all competing for attention in a single send. The result is a newsletter when it should be a campaign.
- The sweepstakes — "Wish it, then win it" (up to $1,000 in LEGO products, ending 07/31) — is the most compelling element, but it's buried behind the soft "monthly wrap-up" framing. That opportunity is wasted in the subject line, which swaps the sharp, direct tone LEGO used throughout the Insiders Days run ("The clock is ticking...", "It's a great time to be a member, Leo") for tagline copy that could live on a LEGO Store banner.
- The Leo points balance showing 0 / $0.00 throughout is either correct and a mild personalization hazard (reminding the member they have no earned points) or a data glitch — either way, it weakens the "This month's top rewards" section.

## What's working

- **Sweepstakes mechanic is solid**: add-to-wishlist to enter is a frictionless, product-engagement action with a real dollar prize and a hard 07/31 deadline.
- **Bluey Tea Party reward is well-timed**: Bluey remains culturally hot and a family-friendly reward headline helps broaden the audience beyond the adult collector niche.
- **"Member days are coming!" tease**: a smart forward hook that reactivates Insiders Days anticipation without burning another full campaign.
- **Name personalization is consistent** throughout: header, points bar, and footer all carry "Leo" correctly.
- **No render bugs**: all images load, no overlapping text, the green background sections contrast cleanly with card content.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is a brand tagline, not an email offer**: "All your brick dreams can come true" conveys nothing actionable — compare to the Insiders Days run where every subject named either the event or the benefit.
- **Five competing CTAs dilute intent**: Enter Now, Redeem reward, three Shop Now buttons, Update Now, App Store, Google Play — the email never decides what it wants the reader to do.
- **Zero points balance looks like a bug**: Leo's Insiders balance reads 0 / $0.00 in two places, which makes the "This month's top rewards" section feel awkward for a member who has apparently earned nothing.
- **"Member days are coming!" section is vague to the point of being inert**: no date, no benefit teaser, no reason to click "Update now" over just waiting for the actual campaign email.
- **Product recs are eclectic without curation logic**: Arcade Pinball, Koenigsegg Megacar, and Woodland Mushrooms in the same "make room for these" carousel signals no personalization — it reads like a trending-products widget, not a member recommendation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the sweepstakes**: the $1,000 wish-list giveaway ending 07/31 is the most ownable news in this send. Put it front and center instead of hiding it behind the wrap-up frame.
- 2. **Pick one primary CTA per email section** and demote the rest. The sweepstakes "Enter Now" should own above the fold; everything else should stack below as secondary actions.
- 3. **Either fix the 0-points display or suppress it**: show the member's actual balance, or if Leo is genuinely at zero, acknowledge it ("Start earning — here's how") rather than showing empty fields next to reward content.
- 4. **Give the "Member days are coming" tease a date or benefit**: "Next Insiders Days: August — add your preferences to unlock early access" converts far better than a blank teaser.
- 5. **Personalize the product recs by theme/history**, not by trending. The July 9 email (10/10) earned top marks in part because product recs felt curated; this carousel undoes that trust.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Leo, win up to $1,000 in LEGO — add to your wish list by 7/31`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your July rewards + a wish-list giveaway, Leo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Enter this month's sweepstakes, redeem your Bluey reward, and get a sneak peek at what's next.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wish list sweepstakes, Bluey Tea Party reward, and Member Days is coming back.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — LEGO® News: "All your brick dreams can come true, Leo"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a well-structured MJML-derived HTML 4.01 template with solid cross-client compatibility scaffolding, but has two confirmed infrastructure issues — an HTTP mixed-content barcode image and missing authentication visibility — alongside widespread missing alt text across footer assets.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**False positives from QA probe:**
- `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` flagged as 404s — these are `<link rel="preconnect">` hints, not navigable URLs. The HTTP probe correctly gets a non-200 response; not actionable.

**Real issue — 32 tracking links skipped:**
- All CTAs route through click-redirect domains and were not probed. Destination link health is unverified. If any product or offer URLs have changed since template authoring, broken destinations would go undetected here.

**No issues found** with the font CDN `<link href>` loads or image CDN (`image.e.crm.lego.com`) URLs.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Mixed-content HTTP image (FAIL):**
The LEGO Insiders barcode is served over HTTP:
```
src: http://cl.s50.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7InYi...
```
Appears twice (desktop + mobile swap). Modern clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail) block HTTP images by default — the barcode will render as a broken image for most recipients. This is a functional breakage for any member-facing reward/scan flow.

**Missing alt text (18 instances):**
The following images have no `alt` attribute or empty `alt=""`:
- `01_20260720_Q3_Member_Moments_Hero-Banner-Short.png` (×2 — desktop/mobile)
- `Footer-Products_GenericPoints_Colour.png` (×8)
- `GreyBrick.png` (×6)
- `Footer_TopSeparator_Desktop_Member-Non-Member_Dark.png` (×3)
- `Footer_Mobile_LegoInsidersLogo.png` (×1)

The hero banner missing alt text is the highest priority — it's the primary visual content. Footer decorative assets (GreyBrick, separators) could reasonably use `alt=""` explicitly, but should be confirmed rather than omitted.

**Duplicate Google Fonts requests:**
Two `<link>` tags load the same `Noto Sans Display` family:
```html
<link href="…Noto+Sans+Display:wght@700…">
<link href="…Noto+Sans+Display:wght@400;700…">
```
Plus a third `@import` for plain `Noto Sans` in `<style>`. Three separate font requests for overlapping subsets — minor load overhead but no rendering failure.

**Commented-out CSS:**
The `NotoB` font mapping rule is commented out while `NotoR` is active. If any elements rely on the `NotoB` class, they'll fall back silently with no error.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Subject line uses `Leo` — consistent with first-name personalization. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{FIRST_NAME}}` or `%%first_name%%` literals) are visible in the truncated HTML. No issues found in the provided source.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**List-Unsubscribe header not captured (WARN):**
Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were detected via the AgentMail relay. This may be a relay capture gap rather than a true absence — LEGO's ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, based on `e.crm.lego.com` + `cl.s50.exct.net`) virtually always injects these. Confirm by inspecting raw headers from a delivered copy.

If headers are genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo will not surface the one-click unsubscribe button, which violates their 2024 bulk sender requirements for >5K/day senders.

**Authentication-Results not captured (WARN):**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unknown. Same likely-relay-gap caveat applies. `e.crm.lego.com` is a dedicated SFMC sending domain and would typically be DKIM-signed; verify from raw message headers in a mail client.

**Plain-text URL density (WARN):**
79% of the plain-text part is URLs (13,246 / 16,750 chars). Spam filters treat high URL-to-text ratios as a signal. This is common in auto-generated plain-text from rich HTML but worth flagging to the ESP team to ensure the plain-text version is human-readable rather than a raw href dump.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

32 tracking links were skipped — UTM parameter presence and correctness on destination URLs cannot be confirmed from this audit. The click-redirect domain (`e.crm.lego.com`) is standard SFMC behavior. No issues confirmed; manual spot-check of 2–3 CTAs recommended to verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are populated correctly for this send.
