# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- This is AG1 in full celebrity-acquisition mode — Hugh Jackman is apparently their new face, and the pun headline is doing its best. As someone who's been blending this stuff cold every morning since 2019, the homepage reads like it's trying to get *new* people in the door, not acknowledge me as an annual subscriber who already knows every ingredient. Clean, premium, but zero personalization for someone who's already converted.

## What's working

- **The NSF Certified for Sport® trust badge** — visible immediately below the hero fold. That's the one third-party signal I actually research before recommending anything to friends. Shows AG1 knows their audience cares about this.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" + "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — these land for me because I will literally go look up the citations. The credentialing is front and center.
- **The "Better Mornings, No Matter Hugh You Are" pun** — corny but it works. It's confident, not desperate. High-quality brand voice that says they're not chasing trends.
- **The green CTA button** — high contrast, full width on mobile. Attention is not split. You know exactly where to tap.

## What's weak

- **"Get Started" CTA for a returning annual subscriber** — I'm already started. This CTA is entirely acquisition-framed. There's no account entry point, no subscription dashboard shortcut, no "Manage my subscription" anywhere visible.
- **Hero image is nearly illegible** — the photo behind the headline is very dark and blurred. I can barely make out that it's Hugh Jackman. The brand is spending money on a celebrity partnership but the asset itself reads as a render artifact.
- **Hugh Jackman as the identity hook** — I don't identify with a 56-year-old action star. I identify with founders and researchers optimizing longevity protocols. Peter Attia would land harder for me. Bryan Johnson obviously. Even Andrew Huberman's crowd.
- **Massive blank below-the-fold section** — the bottom two-thirds of the viewport is completely empty off-white. Whether that's a lazy-load failure or a content rendering issue, it looks broken on first load and kills any momentum to scroll.
- **Zero pricing transparency** — no subscription price, no free trial info, no "starting at" signal. For acquisition, this is a missed opportunity. For retention, it's not even trying.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Get Started" for a split CTA** — show "Get Started" to unrecognized visitors and "My Subscription" or "Reorder" to logged-in users. I'm an annual buyer; the homepage should know that.
- **Fix or brighten the hero image** — if Hugh Jackman is the campaign, I need to actually *see* him. A blurry dark rectangle is not a celebrity endorsement, it's a placeholder.
- **Replace the celebrity anchor with a scientist or longevity-track persona** — show me a 35-42 year old doing a morning stack in a minimalist kitchen, not an action hero. The biohacker cohort is your most loyal segment; speak directly to us.
- **Add a visible ingredient/science hook above the fold** — even a single line like "75 vitamins, minerals & whole-food-sourced ingredients" below the hero subhead would give the ingredient-panel readers like me a reason to keep going.
