# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-09-27
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Opened drinkag1.com expecting the usual clean, clinical AG1 aesthetic and that's basically what I got — minimalist, product-forward, one clear offer. Right now it's pushing a 20% subscription discount ("BACK2ROUTINE") ahead of a September 30 deadline, which is exactly the kind of stockpile-before-it-ends nudge that gets my attention as an annual subscriber. The one thing that threw me: the actual "shop now" button on screen is just a blank green bar with no visible label.

## What's working

- **"ENDS SEPTEMBER 30" ticker** — small, top-left, but it's a real deadline tied to today's date, not a stale evergreen banner.
- **Trust badge stack** (NSF Certified for Sport, "backed by industry-leading research," "trusted by leading scientists and athletes," 90-day guarantee) — this is the block that actually sells me. I read every ingredient panel twice; third-party validation language like this is what makes me trust a greens powder enough to resubscribe.
- **Subscription-specific offer** — "20% on your first AG1 Pro, AG1 Next Gen or AG1 Essentials Gummies subscription order" speaks directly to how I already buy (annual subscription, not one-off).

## What's weak

- **Blank CTA button** — the big green bar under the offer copy has zero visible text. I can't tell if it says "Shop Now," "Redeem Offer," or something else. That's a real hesitation point for someone who reads labels twice before committing.
- **No visible navigation** — there's a small "‹" arrow mid-hero (looks like a carousel control) but no hamburger menu, no product links, nothing else on screen. If I wanted to jump straight to AG1 Pro vs. Next Gen vs. travel packs, I have no visible path to do it.
- **Huge dead white space** below the trust badges — the page just goes blank for a long stretch before hitting the footer logo. Feels like content didn't load or the fold is misjudged.
- **No mention of travel packs specifically** — I stockpile single-serve packets before trips, and that product isn't called out anywhere visible here.

## Recommendations

- Fix or re-render the CTA button so the label ("Shop Now" / "Redeem 20%") is actually visible — right now it looks broken, not just minimal.
- Add a persistent nav bar (or at least a visible hamburger icon) so returning subscribers can jump straight to their product line instead of scrolling and hoping.
- Surface the travel-pack SKU in the hero rotation, especially heading into fall travel season — it's a natural stockpile trigger for buyers like me.
- Trim or fill that dead space below the trust badges with a "why 75 ingredients" or "what's in your scoop" module — feeds the label-reader instinct instead of leaving a blank scroll.
