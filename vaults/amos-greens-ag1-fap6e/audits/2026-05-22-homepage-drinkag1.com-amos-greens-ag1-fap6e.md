# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is AG1's own turf and I'm literally a daily subscriber, so I came in with high expectations — and got a slick, confident brand page that still feels like it's talking to someone discovering AG1 for the first time, not someone who's already blending a scoop every morning. The Hugh Jackman headline is clever wordplay and the trust signals are exactly what I'd point to when I'm defending my supplement spend to skeptical friends. But the whole above-fold experience is new-customer acquisition mode, and there's zero acknowledgment that a returning subscriber even exists.

## What's working

- **"Better Mornings, No Matter Hugh You Are."** — The wordplay is actually good. As someone who reads every ingredient panel twice and sends AG1 articles to group chats, this is the kind of clever that makes me feel like the brand gets the audience it's built.
- **Green CTA button on dark hero** — High contrast, impossible to miss, properly sized for a thumb tap on mobile. No hunting required.
- **NSF Certified for Sport® trust bullet** — This is the one that silences skeptics at dinner parties. Seeing it immediately below the fold without scrolling is the right call for a supplement brand.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" + "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — Both land for a Bryan-Johnson-curious buyer. I'm not buying vibes; I'm buying peer-reviewed credibility.
- **Lifestyle grid below fold** — The 2×2 image grid of active people outdoors signals real-life use, not a sterile lab or a shaker bottle on a white background.

## What's weak

- **"Get Started →" CTA for a returning subscriber** — I already started. This CTA is acquisition-mode and completely ignores that I'm probably here to manage my subscription, order travel packs, or check a shipment. It's a dead end for me.
- **No personalization or subscriber hook** — Zero "welcome back" energy. No loyalty program callout, no member pricing, no subscription portal link. For a brand built on annual recurring revenue, this is a real miss.
- **Hugh Jackman hero reads celebrity-forward, not science-forward** — The copy leans hard into the Hugh pun but buries the actual product story. I scan for efficacy signals; this gives me entertainment first.
- **No visible pricing or subscription terms above fold** — For a supplement with a reputation for being expensive, the absence of any pricing context before the CTA creates a small but real friction point.
- **Navigation not visible** — I can't see a nav bar in the viewport. If I want to jump to ingredients, bundles, or travel packs, I have no visible path.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Manage My Subscription" or "Member Dashboard" secondary CTA** next to "Get Started" — even a small text link would stop existing subscribers from bouncing immediately.
- **Swap Hugh Jackman's hero subhead for a one-line efficacy hook** ("75 vitamins, minerals, and whole-food sourced ingredients — in one daily ritual") beneath the wordplay headline so the celebrity draw and the product claim coexist.
- **Surface a visible subscription/savings badge** ("Subscribe & Save 15% — free shipping on every order") in the hero area — this directly rewards the behavior that drives their business model.
- **Show a sticky nav or at minimum a hamburger icon** clearly visible in the viewport so mobile users can navigate to ingredients, shop, or account without guessing.
