# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a dead-simple Hugh Jackman celebrity-acquisition play — clean, dark, masculine, built entirely for someone who hasn't tried AG1 yet. As a daily subscriber who's been blending this thing cold every morning for the better part of three years, I feel invisible here. The brand knows its aesthetic, but the homepage reads like I've never heard of it.

## What's working

- **Hugh Jackman headline pun** — "No Matter Hugh You Are" is the kind of wordplay that lands for someone who's already in the AG1 orbit and appreciates a brand that doesn't take itself too seriously
- **Three trust bullets** — "Validated by NSF Certified for Sport®," "Backed by industry-leading research," "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes" are the exact reassurance levers a biohacker scans for; NSF certification is a genuine differentiator, not fluff
- **Green CTA on dark hero** — color contrast is hard to miss; single focal action, no competing buttons stealing attention
- **Lifestyle image grid below the fold** — the warm-toned 4-photo grid showing active, real-ish people doing morning routines earns the "daily ritual" promise

## What's weak

- **"Get Started" is an acquisition-only CTA** — as an existing subscriber I'm essentially locked out of the homepage; there's no "Manage Subscription," "Reorder," or member portal hook
- **Hero image is too dark/grey** — the photo behind the headline is muted and low-contrast; I can barely read the scene; a biohacker expects precision, not moody ambiguity
- **Zero ingredient/stack depth above the fold** — "75 high-quality ingredients" is the most compelling thing about AG1 and it's nowhere; one quick callout would separate this from a generic wellness ad
- **No pricing or subscription framing visible** — new or returning, I still don't know if this is $79/mo or $99/mo without digging; the omission feels evasive
- **Subhead is vague** — "Since 2010, AG1 has been the morning ritual that keeps Hugh Jackman at the top of his game" is fine for brand building but adds zero informational density for someone who reads every ingredient panel twice

## Recommendations

- **Add a subscriber-recognition banner** — even a sticky "Already a member? Log in →" pill in the header removes the alienation for returning customers who land here from email or a referral link
- **Swap vague subhead for one hard data point** — replace the Jackman sentence with something like "75 bioavailable ingredients, one daily drink — formulated for people who don't cut corners on input quality" to give the biohacker brain something to latch onto
- **Brighten or crop the hero image** — bring the Hugh Jackman visual to at least 70% brightness so the scene reads clearly; the current wash makes it feel like a placeholder
- **Surface a subscription price anchor** — even "Starting at $X/mo for subscribers" above the fold kills the hesitation that makes analytical buyers click away before converting
