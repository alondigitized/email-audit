# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is my homepage — I drink AG1 daily and I recognize a brand that knows exactly who it's talking to. The Hugh Jackman campaign is smart and I feel it: "Better Mornings. No Matter Hugh You Are" hits the wordplay right, the NSF certification badge is the first thing I look for as someone who checks every label twice, and the overall aesthetic is clean biohacker minimalism. Right now AG1 is pushing the Jackman endorsement hard, clearly leaning into the performance-optimized male health angle — which, for me personally, lands.

## What's working

- **"Better Mornings. No Matter Hugh You Are."** — The headline pun is exactly the right register for me. Clever without trying too hard. I read it twice on purpose.
- **NSF Certified for Sport® badge** — This is the first thing I look for. Seeing it right under the CTA without having to dig into the FAQ builds immediate trust.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" + "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — Three credential bullets stacked vertically reads like a quick ingredient-panel scan. Perfect for how I consume info.
- **Green CTA button** — High-contrast, sits on a neutral/dark hero, completely unambiguous as the primary action.
- **Lifestyle image grid at the bottom** — The four-photo mosaic of people using the product in active/outdoor settings humanizes the brand beyond the celebrity push.

## What's weak

- **No subscription or returning-member hook visible** — I'm already a subscriber. The homepage doesn't acknowledge I exist. "Get Started" is new-customer language — I don't need to get started, I need to manage my plan or explore travel packs.
- **Hero image is muted and dark** — The photo reads more like a moody fashion campaign than a performance nutrition brand. If Hugh Jackman is the face, I want energy, not brooding.
- **Zero pricing or offer visibility** — No free-shipping threshold, no trial offer, no subscription discount. For something I'm already paying for monthly, the absence of value signaling makes the page feel like it's not working hard enough.
- **"Get Started →" CTA copy is vague** — Start what? A subscription? A free trial? A quiz? This is my homepage and I still don't know what the next click gets me.
- **No review count or social proof number** — I want to see "100,000+ daily subscribers" or a star rating above the fold. The credential badges are good but they're institutional, not peer-based.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-member/subscriber split** — Detect logged-in users and swap "Get Started" for "Manage Your Subscription" or surface a travel-pack promo. I came here for a reason — make it easy.
- **Make Hugh Jackman more kinetic** — Swap the moody still for a shot of him actively preparing or drinking AG1, ideally in a morning routine context. "Better Mornings" needs a morning visual.
- **Surface one concrete number above the fold** — "4.7 stars · 28,000 reviews" or "trusted by 1M+ people" stacked right under the credential bullets would close the trust loop that the badges open.
- **Sharpen the CTA copy** — "Start Your Daily Ritual" or "Try AG1 for 30 Days" gives me a clear picture of what I'm committing to. "Get Started" is doing too little work for the highest-traffic button on the page.
