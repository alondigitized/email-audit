# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is my own homepage — I drink AG1 every morning — and the experience still reads like it was built exclusively to convert someone who's never heard of the product. The Hugh Jackman partnership is front and center with a clever wordplay headline, which lands for a performance-minded guy like me, but there is zero acknowledgment that I might already be a subscriber. Below the trust badges the page is a blank cream void, which reads either as a render failure or content that never loaded.

## What's working

- **"Better Mornings, No Matter Hugh You Are."** — I actually smiled at this. The pun works, and anchoring on Hugh Jackman's morning ritual is the right frequency for a biohacker audience. I read ingredient panels twice; I care that he's been using this since 2021.
- **NSF Certified for Sport® badge** — That's the credentialing signal I look for before I trust any stack. It's the first thing I see below the fold break, and it reads immediately.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research"** and **"Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — Two of three trust bullets hit my research-before-purchase instinct. These are exactly the words I respond to.
- **Green CTA button** — Full-width, high-contrast lime green against a dark hero. It doesn't compete with anything. Hard to miss.

## What's weak

- **"Get Started →" is a new-user CTA** — I've been a subscriber for two years. There is nothing on this screen for me: no "Log In," no "Manage Subscription," no "Reorder." I feel invisible.
- **Blurred carousel right panel** — The right edge of the hero shows a ghost of another slide at roughly 50% opacity. It's not inviting me to swipe; it just looks broken or half-loaded.
- **The entire lower two-thirds of the page is empty** — Below the three trust bullets there is a vast cream void. Either content didn't load or the screenshot caught a catastrophic blank section. That's a bounce trigger.
- **No offer, no price anchor, no subscription value prop** — As someone who stockpiles travel packs and buys annual subscriptions, I want to see "subscribe and save X%" above the fold. There's nothing.
- **No navigation bar on mobile** — I can't tap to find travel packs, gift subscriptions, or ingredient details without hunting. The only exit is the CTA or the back button.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Get Started" for a split CTA**: "Get Started" for new visitors, "My Subscription" or "Log In" for returning users — detect session state and personalize above the fold.
- **Surface the subscribe-and-save rate in the hero subhead** — "Since 2021, AG1 has been the morning ritual... starting at $X/month with annual subscription." One number closes a lot of hesitation for annual buyers like me.
- **Fix or investigate the blank lower section** — Whatever module is supposed to render below the trust badges (product selector, ingredients rail, testimonials) needs to load. A blank page is the fastest way to lose a returning subscriber.
- **Add a swipeable carousel indicator** — If the right-bleed is intentional to hint at more slides, add dots or a progress bar; right now it reads as a glitch, not an invitation.
