# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I already blend this stuff every morning, so landing on this homepage is like walking into your own gym — familiar, but I'm immediately clocking whether they're talking to me or to someone who's never heard of AG1. Right now they're leading with Hugh Jackman as a lifestyle hook, which is aspirational but definitely new-customer energy. The page is clean, the trust signals are there, but there's zero retention infrastructure above the fold — no subscription dashboard link, no subscriber welcome, no "hey, you're one of us."

## What's working

- **"Better Mornings, No Matter Hugh You Are."** — The Hugh Jackman pun headline is clever and immediately signals performance-oriented morning routine, which is exactly my framing for why I take this.
- **Three-bullet trust stack** ("Validated by NSF Certified for Sport®", "Backed by industry-leading research", "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes") — these are my language. NSF Certified for Sport is the signal I actually care about when I'm reading ingredient panels. Seeing it front and center confirms I'm in the right place.
- **Green "Get Started →" CTA** — high contrast against the muted hero background, impossible to miss.
- **Clean render** — no jank, no layout shift, no broken assets. The page performs.

## What's weak

- **"Get Started" is new-customer copy** — I'm an annual subscriber who just wants to reorder travel packs before a trip. There's no "My Account", "Shop Pouches", or subscriber-specific entry point visible. The whole above-the-fold experience ignores that I exist.
- **No price signal anywhere** — I read every ingredient-panel email twice. I want to see if there's a subscriber discount, a bundle, or a trial offer. Blank on that front.
- **Hugh Jackman is 57** — Aspirational, yes, but he's not exactly "38-year-old biohacker tracking his biomarkers with Bryan Johnson's Blueprint." The lifestyle signal is one generational remove away from feeling like it's about me specifically.
- **No ingredient transparency above the fold** — The thing that differentiates AG1 for people like me is the formulation depth. Zero mention of it here. The trust bullets are good but vague.
- **Bottom lifestyle grid** — Those thumbnails at the bottom feel low-res and generic in the viewport. I can see figures but can't read any context.

## Recommendations

- **Add a subscriber re-entry CTA alongside "Get Started"** — a secondary "Manage My Subscription" or "Shop Travel Packs" button would tell returning customers like me that the brand knows we exist; this is a one-line copy change and a button style.
- **Swap the sub-headline from celebrity narrative to a specific formulation hook** — something like "75 vitamins, minerals, and whole-food sourced nutrients. One daily scoop." gives the biohacker brain something to anchor on without losing the morning-ritual framing.
- **Add a free-shipping or trial-offer strip** below the hero — even a slim banner ("Free shipping on orders over $X · Cancel any subscription anytime") reduces friction for first-time trial and reassures existing customers on renewal economics.
- **Pull one specific product image above the fold** — show the canister, the travel pouches, or the blender cup setup. Right now the hero is a man, not a product. For ingredient-obsessed buyers, the product IS the imagery.
