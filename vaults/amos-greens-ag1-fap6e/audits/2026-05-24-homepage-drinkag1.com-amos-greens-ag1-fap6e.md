# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-24
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is AG1 doing what AG1 does — clean, minimal, and aspirational. The Hugh Jackman partnership is front and center, leaning hard into the "elite performance ritual" angle that got me into this stuff in the first place. It's a brand homepage, not a store — there's zero commerce energy here, no subscription upsell visible, no returning-customer acknowledgment whatsoever.

## What's working

- **"No Matter Hugh You Are" pun** — I groaned and smiled. Millennial wordplay that doesn't try too hard. I respect it.
- **NSF Certified for Sport® badge** — the single most important trust signal for someone like me who reads every ingredient panel. This is load-bearing.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" + "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — these three trust bullets are exactly what a Bryan-Johnson-adjacent buyer needs to see within 3 seconds.
- **Green CTA button** — brand-consistent, visible, doesn't compete with anything else on the page. The hierarchy just works.
- **Bottom lifestyle photo grid** — real people in outdoor/active settings, not sterile studio shots. Grounds the product in a life I recognize.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-subscriber acknowledgment** — I've been subscribed for years and this page treats me like a stranger. No "Welcome back," no loyalty perks callout, no "Manage your subscription" entry point. That's a miss.
- **"Get Started" CTA is acquisition-framed** — I'm already started. As an existing customer landing here, this CTA has nothing for me. There's no secondary CTA for the subscriber path.
- **No offer, no price, no subscription hook above the fold** — the value prop is vibes-and-Hugh, not "here's what you get and what it costs." For new visitors who aren't already believers, this is vague.
- **Hugh Jackman is 56, not 38** — not fatal, but the generational fit is slightly off for a Millennial-coded audience. I relate to his performance goals, not his life stage.
- **No product shot visible in the hero** — I know what the pouch looks like, but a first-timer has no idea what they're even being sold here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a returning-subscriber CTA** — a secondary button or nav link ("Manage subscription / Reorder") so existing customers don't have to fight the acquisition funnel to do anything useful.
- **Drop the product pouch into the hero** — even a small inset of the AG1 canister next to Hugh anchors what you're selling. Right now the hero could be for a gym, a supplement stack, or a protein bar.
- **Surface one concrete offer** — even "Free bottle of D3+K2 with your first subscription" counts. There is zero price signal above the fold for new visitors.
- **Swap "Get Started" for split CTAs** — "Start Your Ritual" for new visitors, "Reorder / My Account" for returning ones. One line of personalization, massive lift for subscriber LTV.
