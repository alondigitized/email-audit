# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the Hugh Jackman campaign in full force — clean, confident, and premium. As a daily AG1 drinker I recognize the brand immediately, and the "Better Mornings. No Matter Hugh You Are" pun lands better than it should. What's missing is anything for me, the guy who already buys this stuff: no subscription portal, no loyalty hook, no subscriber-only offer. This homepage is pure top-of-funnel acquisition, which makes sense commercially but leaves me with nothing actionable.

## What's working

- **"Better Mornings. No Matter Hugh You Are."** — The pun is genuinely clever and as a Millennial who reads every ingredient panel, I appreciate a brand that's confident enough to play wordplay at the hero level. It stopped me.
- **Green "Get Started →" CTA button** — Color contrast pops hard against the dark hero background. Unmissable, even on mobile.
- **NSF Certified for Sport® badge** — This is the first thing I actually look for with any supplement. Seeing it right below the fold without hunting is a trust win.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" + "Trusted by leading scientists and athletes"** — These three trust signals stacked together are exactly what I need to forward the product link to skeptical friends. Short, credible, no overreach.
- **Minimal nav** — No distracting mega-menu. The clean header respects the hero and keeps focus on the CTA.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-subscriber hooks** — I already have a subscription. There's no "Log in," no "My Account," no "Your next shipment" teaser. The whole page assumes I've never heard of AG1, which is tone-deaf for a brand whose entire model is retention.
- **Hugh Jackman is not my demographic mirror** — He's aspirational, yes, but he's mid-50s. I'd engage harder with a 35-40 male biohacker type — someone doing zone-2 cardio at 6am, not walking the red carpet.
- **No price, no subscription offer, no incentive** — As someone who stockpiles travel packs before international trips, a bundle offer or "first-month discount" would have me tapping through immediately. Nothing here triggers that.
- **Lifestyle image grid at the bottom is cropped and unreadable** — Four images appear in a 2×2 grid that's cut off. I can see people in outdoor contexts but can't tell what they're doing or why it matters to me.
- **"Get Started" is new-customer language** — If I'm already subscribed, this CTA sends me nowhere useful. No "Manage Subscription" or "Shop Add-ons" option visible anywhere.

## Recommendations

- **Add a subscriber/returning-customer banner** at the very top ("Already a member? Log in →") — even a one-line slim bar would catch me and send me somewhere meaningful instead of bouncing.
- **Swap the hero image for a 35-40 male biohacker lifestyle shot** — someone blending their AG1 into an ice water shaker at 5:45am, not a celebrity athlete. The copy can stay; the face needs to be mine.
- **Surface one concrete subscriber perk above the fold** — "Subscribe & Save 15%" or "Free Travel Packs with annual plan" printed near or on the CTA button. Converts both new and returning audiences.
- **Make the lifestyle grid tappable with visible product labels** — right now it's decorative. If those images link to specific use cases (travel, training, recovery), add a caption or overlay so I know where they go.
