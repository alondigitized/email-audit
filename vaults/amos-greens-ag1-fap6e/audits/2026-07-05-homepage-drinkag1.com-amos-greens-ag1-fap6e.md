# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident, premium health brand that knows exactly what it sells — and apparently right now it's selling Hugh Jackman. The celeb-anchored hero is doing the heavy lifting, and the credentialing strip below it (NSF Certified, research-backed, scientists + athletes) signals this brand takes its positioning seriously. As someone who has been auto-shipping AG1 for years, this homepage reads like it's pitching me on a product I already own — which is both reassuring and a missed opportunity.

## What's working

- **NSF Certified for Sport® badge** — this is the first thing I check on any supplement. Seeing it right there below the hero without having to dig is exactly right.
- **"Backed by industry-leading research" credentialing strip** — I read every ingredient panel twice; this kind of signal matters to me and it's front and center.
- **Full-width green CTA button** — impossible to miss, good contrast, clear action. The brand's signature green color is doing real brand-equity work here.
- **Minimal, premium aesthetic** — no cluttered banners, no screaming discount popups. Matches the premium $79/month product positioning I expect.

## What's weak

- **Hugh Jackman is the wrong aspirational figure for me** — I'm 38, I follow Bryan Johnson and Peter Attia, not Wolverine. The celebrity hook feels off-persona for serious biohackers in my age bracket.
- **Zero subscriber recognition** — I've been a customer since before 2021 and this page treats me like a stranger. "Get Started" is new-customer language; there's no "Manage subscription" or "Reorder" anywhere visible.
- **Massive blank beige real estate below the fold** — the bottom two-thirds of the screenshot is entirely empty. Either content failed to load, or this is catastrophic lazy-load timing. Either way it reads as a broken page.
- **No concrete offer** — no travel pack bundle, no referral credit, no subscription discount. The brand knows subscription customers stockpile travel packs (I do) and there's nothing here targeting that behavior.
- **Hero subtext buries the functional claim** — "the morning ritual that keeps Hugh Jackman at the top of his game" is celebrity-benefit framing, not ingredient/efficacy framing. I want to see "75 vitamins, minerals, and whole-food sourced nutrients" — that's what I told my friends about when I evangelized this product.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Hugh Jackman hero for a Bryan-Johnson-adjacent optimization frame** — something like "Your morning stack, optimized. 75 nutrients. One drink." with a dark-mode aesthetic or lab-clean visual. Jackman works for mass appeal but loses the biohacker core who is already converting.
- **Add a returning-customer banner or sticky header state** — if I'm logged in (or even if not, use a cookie), show "Welcome back, Amos — your next shipment ships in 12 days. Need travel packs?" That's one personalization that converts repeat loyalty into upsell.
- **Surface the travel pack above the fold during Q3/Q4** — I stock up before international trips; a "Heading somewhere? Travel packs in stock →" callout would fire directly on my behavior.
- **Fix or accelerate the below-fold content load** — the lower 70% of the visible viewport is blank. Whatever modules sit below (best-sellers, ingredient highlights, reviews) need to render before a mobile user's thumb loses patience.
