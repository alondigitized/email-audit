# Homepage snapshot · drinkag1.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I literally blend this stuff every morning — cold, in a Vitamix, no compromise — so landing on drinkag1.com feels like showing up to your own house party. The site is running a full Hugh Jackman push right now, leaning hard into celebrity-as-social-proof rather than the ingredient-depth and protocol-nerd content I actually came here for when I need to justify restocking. It reads premium and clean, but it's clearly built to convert first-timers, not reward the subscriber who's already in deep.

## What's working

- **The Hugh Jackman headline pun** — "Better Mornings, No Matter Hugh You Are." — is genuinely clever and I smiled. The wordplay signals the brand has wit, which lands for me.
- **Three trust badges** (NSF Certified for Sport®, industry-leading research, leading scientists and athletes) stacked right below the fold line are the most biohacker-relevant copy on the page. Those three bullets do more for my trust than any celebrity could.
- **The green CTA button** is the exact green of the canister — brand coherent, high contrast, impossible to miss.
- **Lifestyle mosaic at the bottom**: outdoor, active, multiple people — signals this is a whole-life product, not just a gym supplement.

## What's weak

- **"Get Started →" for a returning subscriber is tone-deaf** — I'm already started. I'm three canisters deep. The CTA is pure acquisition copy and completely ignores me.
- **No ingredient or science hook above the fold** — as someone who reads every label twice, I want to see 75-ingredient count, adaptogens, or some protocol-nerd signal. The Hugh Jackman angle is entertainment, not evidence.
- **Dark hero image kills product visibility** — I can barely make out the product or the scene. The dramatic low-light aesthetic prioritizes mood over information.
- **No price, offer, or subscription pricing visible** — the most common conversion driver for a premium supplement ($79+/month) is completely absent.
- **No personalization for existing subscribers** — no "welcome back," no subscription status nudge, nothing that acknowledges loyalty.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Get Started" for a split CTA** — a secondary "Manage My Subscription" or "Reorder" link next to the primary button would instantly acknowledge the retention segment without hurting acquisition flow.
- **Add a hero sub-badge with the ingredient count** — something like "75 vitamins, minerals & whole-food ingredients" directly under the headline; it takes one line and immediately feeds the label-reader in me.
- **Brighten the hero image or add a product shot overlay** — the dark aesthetic is cinematic but buries the canister. I should be able to identify the product in under a second.
- **Run the trust badges as live-linked items** — "NSF Certified for Sport® — see the certificate" would do a lot of work for anyone doing due diligence on a $79 monthly commitment.
