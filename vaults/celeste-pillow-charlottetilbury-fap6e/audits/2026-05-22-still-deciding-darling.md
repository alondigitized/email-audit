# Still Deciding, Darling?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a browse/consideration-nurture email — the subject "Still Deciding, Darling?" signals Charlotte Tilbury knows the recipient browsed but didn't buy. The strategic idea is sound: catch a warm lead with tools (quiz, virtual try-on, consultation booking) and a discount. The execution, however, scatters attention across five competing CTAs with no single conversion anchor. The fatal flaw is offer coherence: the sitewide 20% off sale was live as of yesterday's blast (scored 9/10), yet this email presents the older 15% welcome code. A fence-sitter opening "Still Deciding" expecting the push to buy will find a *weaker* deal than what's allegedly on the site right now.
- As the seventh email in seven days, list fatigue is real. The brand has earned credibility points in this sequence, but it's burning them with cadence. The subject is actually one of Charlotte Tilbury's better behavioral triggers — lean, on-brand, under 50 chars — but the body doesn't cash the check.

## What's working

- **Subject line is behaviorally sharp** — "Still Deciding, Darling?" reads as a genuine cart/browse-abandonment trigger, not a generic blast, and it fits the brand voice perfectly.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent brand aesthetic throughout.
- **15% off + free shipping is visible** at banner and footer — two placements for the offer reinforces recall.
- **Multiple service differentiators on display** — consultation booking, virtual try-on, and easy returns address real pre-purchase objections in one email.
- **Short subject under 50 chars** — mobile-friendly, no spam signals, no excessive punctuation.

## What's weak

- **Offers a worse deal than the live sale** — the active sitewide 20% off (confirmed in the 5/21 and 5/22 send) makes the 15% DARLING15 code here feel like a step backward for any subscriber who's been paying attention.
- **Five competing CTAs with no hierarchy** — "SEE OUR CHARLOTTTES," "QUIZ NOW," "TRY NOW," "BOOK NOW," "DISCOVER MORE" — all roughly equal weight; the eye doesn't know where to go and the finger doesn't either.
- **No specific product shown in context** — the icons grid exists but no single hero product is featured with urgency, price, or social proof attached.
- **Returns and Refunds section kills momentum** — surfacing return policy mid-funnel in a "still deciding" send introduces doubt rather than confidence; it belongs in a post-purchase flow.
- **Seventh email in seven days** — even a well-executed nurture will feel like pressure at this cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Sync the offer to the active sale** — replace DARLING15/15% with the live 20% off before this goes out. Undercutting your own promotion in a conversion-focused email is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. **Pick one CTA and build the email around it** — hero a single best-seller (Pillow Talk lipstick, Hollywood Flawless Filter) with price, a review pull-quote, and one prominent "Shop Now — 20% Off" button. Strip the quiz, consultation, and returns blocks to a slim footer.
- 3. **Add a credible deadline** — "Sale ends Sunday" or "offer expires at midnight" turns a passive nurture into an action moment.
- 4. **Move virtual try-on above the fold as a functional CTA** — it's a meaningful objection-reducer but it's buried; lead with "Try it on before you buy" directly tied to the hero product.
- 5. **Cut the Returns section entirely** — it's a friction cue, not a conversion cue, in this context.

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Braze-sent table-based email with MSO VML conditionals and a responsive breakpoint at 620px. No catastrophic rendering failures apparent; several accessibility and tracking hygiene issues warrant attention.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Confirmed from source:**
- All links route through `clicks.charlottetilbury.com` (Braze click-tracking proxy). This is expected, but the logo anchor uses a long opaque Braze redirect URL — no evidence of UTM parameters being appended downstream without inspecting the final redirect destination.
- Logo `<a>` has `tabindex="-1"` applied: `<a href="https://clicks.charlottetilbury.com/..." target="_blank" style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">`. This removes the link from keyboard tab order entirely, breaking keyboard navigation.

**Requires verification (not determinable from truncated source):**
- Whether destination URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` after Braze redirect resolves.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|

| Web font loaded only for non-MSO clients | `<!--[if !mso]><!--><link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css"><!--<![endif]-->` — Outlook renders system fallback font; verify fallback stack is declared in inline CSS |

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- Image assets served from `cdn.braze.eu` — EU-origin CDN. Latency acceptable for EU recipients; US/APAC recipients may see slower image load. Not a blocker but worth noting if open rates are geographically skewed.
- UTM continuity unverifiable without resolving the Braze redirect chain. Must confirm `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` are present on landing page URLs, especially for the primary CTA buttons (rows 5–8 per CSS selectors `.row-5 .column-1 .block-5.button_block`).

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Add `alt` text to all images** — confirm logo `img` has descriptive alt; product images should have product name as alt text.
2. **Remove `tabindex="-1"` from the logo anchor** — there is no technical justification for pulling the primary brand link out of tab order.
3. **Replace `outline:none` with a visible focus style** — or remove the property entirely and let clients apply default focus rings.
4. **Add `<title>` content** — e.g., `<title>Charlotte Tilbury — Still Deciding, Darling?</title>`.
5. **Verify UTM params survive Braze redirect** — spot-check at least the primary CTA URL by clicking from a test send and inspecting the final URL in browser devtools.
6. **Confirm List-Unsubscribe-Post header** — required for Gmail bulk sender compliance (>5k/day threshold).
7. **Audit DKIM alignment** — ensure signing domain matches envelope `From` subdomain to avoid deliverability penalties.
