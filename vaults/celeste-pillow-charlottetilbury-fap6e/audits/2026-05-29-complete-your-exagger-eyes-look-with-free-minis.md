# Complete Your Exagger-Eyes Look With FREE Minis 🪄
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury closes the Exagger-Eyes trilogy (May 27 launch, May 28 eyeshadow sticks, today's completion email) with a GWP mechanic — 2 free minis when you hit a spend threshold. That's a smart pivot from pure product-push: it gives a transactional reason to convert now rather than just browse. The 3-step tutorial structure (Prime + Moisturize → Sculpt + Highlight → Volumize + Maximize) is the best content architecture of the three sends — it justifies showing multiple products while keeping the narrative coherent around a single "complete look."
- The execution stumbles in two places. The spend threshold — the whole reason the offer exists — is buried in fine-print-sized body copy below the hero headline. That's a trust problem. And the dual CTA buttons ("SHOP NEW! EYESHADOW STICKS" vs. "GET YOUR FREE GIFTS") split attention and dilute click intent. There's no deadline on the free gift, so urgency collapses.
- This is the better creative of the three Exagger-Eyes sends, but it's arriving on day three of the same product thread. Inbox fatigue is real. If the threshold were surfaced and the CTA unified, this would be a strong performer.

## What's working

- **GWP mechanic is fresh.** After two straight product-introduction emails, adding a "free minis" incentive gives a new reason to act rather than just re-announcing the same launch.
- **3-step tutorial layout.** "FREE" badges on Steps 1 and 3 anchor the eye and make the value proposition scannable — this is the clearest product storytelling CT has done in this campaign.
- **Hero image quality.** The close-up mascara application shot is high-impact and on-brand, no render issues or broken images.
- **Clean render.** No overlapping text, no broken image gaps, no lorem ipsum — the email displays correctly.
- **Narrative continuity.** Tying this to the May 27–28 Exagger-Eyes thread gives subscribers who opened those sends a coherent story arc.

## What's weak

- **Spend threshold is hidden.** The amount you need to spend to unlock the 2 free minis appears in small gray body copy, not the hero — the most important number in the email is also the hardest to read.
- **Split CTA dilutes intent.** "SHOP NEW! EYESHADOW STICKS" and "GET YOUR FREE GIFTS" are two different asks. Most recipients will pick one or neither; they don't reinforce each other.
- **No urgency on the free gift.** No "offer ends Sunday," no countdown — the GWP has no deadline, so "I'll come back later" wins.
- **Third Exagger-Eyes email in three days.** The cadence math is catching up; the open and click rates on this send will almost certainly trail May 27–28 purely from subscriber exhaustion.
- **App/savings footer feels tacked on.** The dark footer with "Magical savings — up to 20% off" disrupts the "premium gift" narrative established above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the threshold in the hero, large.** The current hero says "2 Free Mini Gifts!" but doesn't show what you have to spend. Rewrite the sub-headline as: "Spend $X, unlock 2 free minis — Charlotte's Magic Cream + Exagger-Eyes Volume Mascara." That single change is the highest-impact edit in this email.
- 2. **Unify the CTA to one button.** Drop "SHOP NEW! EYESHADOW STICKS" and make "GET YOUR FREE GIFTS" the single primary CTA. The gift is the hook; the eyeshadow sticks are the means to get there.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** "Free minis through Sunday" or a simple "Limited time" line in the hero would recover some urgency.
- 4. **Strip or move the app footer.** The "Magical savings — up to 20% off" messaging undermines the premium GWP frame. Move it to a dedicated app-promotion email.
- 5. **Rest the Exagger-Eyes thread for 2–3 days.** Let this convert before sending a fourth send in the same campaign arc.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free Minis With Your Exagger-Eyes Order 🪄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Charlotte's Magic Cream + Exagger-Eyes Volume Mascara — free with any $X order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complete your look. Get 2 minis free when you spend $X — limited time.`
