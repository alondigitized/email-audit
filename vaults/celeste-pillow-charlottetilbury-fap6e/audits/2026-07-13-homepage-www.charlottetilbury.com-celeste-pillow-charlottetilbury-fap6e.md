# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the Charlotte Tilbury I know and love — that warm peach-and-gold world landing hard on a Summer Sale push. The brand is doing what it does best: making even a discount page feel expensive. Right now it's all about moving Iconic Kits at up to 50% off, and honestly? As someone who waits for exactly these moments to stock up on backup Pillow Talks, this is speaking directly to me.

## What's working

- **The product cluster in the hero** — that warm coral grouping of complexion and lip products reads like my bathroom counter. I can already see the Flawless foundation and what looks like a compact in there. Very "this is your routine, on sale."
- **"Up To 50% Off Iconic Kits + Unlock Free Gifts"** in the top banner is a strong double hook — the kits are exactly how I buy CT at Christmas, and a summer sale on them is a real pull.
- **Visual hierarchy on mobile** is excellent. The banner, the SUMMER SALE badge, the 50% OFF in big bold type, then SHOP NOW — I don't have to hunt for anything.
- **The NEW "Ask" button** next to the search bar is a nice touch — if I'm hunting for a specific shade of Pillow Talk, being able to ask feels useful.
- **Summer Sale feels timely** — it's July, this is credible, and I don't feel like I stumbled onto stale content.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook visible** — I've definitely bought from CT before. Show me my rewards balance or a "member exclusive" badge. That returning-customer recognition is missing entirely above the fold.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — for a brand with this much product specificity (I know *exactly* which Magic Cream variant I want), the CTA could be "Shop the Sale" or "Shop Kits & Save" and do so much more work.
- **No people in the hero** — CT's editorial photography with models is usually aspirational and age-spanning. A flat product shot on a gradient is fine but doesn't give me someone to project onto.
- **"Minimum spend applies" in the free-gift footer** — that asterisk on free gifts is small enough to feel like a gotcha after a big "FREE GIFTS AS YOU SHOP" promise.
- **No social proof visible** — no review count, no "4.8 stars" badge, nothing that would nudge a new-to-CT visitor. I don't need it, but a lot of people do.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic product cluster for a curated Iconic Kits shot** — show the actual kit boxes with names ("Pillow Talk Set," "Magic Skin Elixir Duo"). I want to see what I'm buying, not a loose product pile.
- **Add a returning-customer strip** — even a slim "Welcome back — your Loyalty points: X" bar or a "Restock your favourites" shelf would make me feel seen and speed me to cart.
- **Make the free-gift threshold explicit in the banner** — change "Unlock Free Gifts! T&Cs apply" to "Spend £50, unlock your free gift" so the deal feels concrete and not like fine print bait.
- **Add a countdown or end-date** — "Sale ends July 20" would push me to act now vs. saving the tab to revisit in three days and forgetting about it.
