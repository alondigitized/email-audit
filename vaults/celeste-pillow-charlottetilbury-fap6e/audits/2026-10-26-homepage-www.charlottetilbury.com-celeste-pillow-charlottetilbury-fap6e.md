# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Charlotte: glittery red-velvet holiday glam, with Charlotte herself in the hero next to Christina Aguilera and Jasmine Tookes. The message is "Gift more beauty," with the Holiday shop pushed hard through the top banner, the nav and the single SHOP GIFTS button. It feels like a magazine cover. It's gorgeous, but it sells a mood and not a deal.

## What's working

- **The hero shot:** the eyeshadow palette in the foreground, the compact, the foundation sticks and the holiday sets lined up at the bottom are exactly what I buy at Christmas. It makes me want a bigger set.
- **"Gift more beauty" in script:** it's elegant and on-brand. "More love, more magic, more joy!" reads warm, not salesy.
- **Clean nav:** MAKEUP, SKINCARE, BEST SELLERS, GIFTS, FRAGRANCE and SHADE MATCH TOOLS are one tap each. NEW! HOLIDAY GIFT SHOP with the sparkle icon is easy to spot.
- **The "Ask" avatar and the chat popup:** "Speak to a makeup artist for free!" with Charlotte's face feels like real service. I'd actually use it for shade questions.
- **Category tiles starting below:** the Magic Cream jars and foundation bottles on red velvet carry the same mood.

## What's weak

- **No offer anywhere above the fold:** for someone who buys the big Christmas sets, I want to see "save X on the set" or "free gift with purchase" right away. Nothing is visible.
- **The "Loyalty" link is tiny:** it sits in the header in small grey text. There's no points, tier or perk message, so a returning shopper like me gets no reward cue.
- **The top banner is only an announcement:** "Holiday shop is now open" has no CTA, no deadline and no shipping or delivery cutoff.
- **The chat popup covers the category tiles:** it sits over the right side of "Shop By Category" and fights for attention just as I start scrolling.
- **No Pillow Talk or hero-product moment:** I can't see a single named product, price or star rating. I'd have to dig for my shades.
- **No trust signals:** no review counts, returns info or free-shipping line in view.

## Recommendations

- Replace the thin banner with a real offer strip, for example "Free delivery over $X + free gift on holiday sets," with a "Ships in time for Christmas by [date]" line.
- Add a one-line loyalty message in the header or under the hero, for example "Members earn double points on holiday sets," with a visible button.
- Add a "Holiday bestsellers" rail right under the hero, with Pillow Talk, Magic Cream and Hollywood Flawless Filter. Give each a price, star rating and "Add to bag."
- Delay the chat popup until after a scroll or a few seconds, or shrink it to the existing "Ask" bubble, so it doesn't cover the category tiles.
