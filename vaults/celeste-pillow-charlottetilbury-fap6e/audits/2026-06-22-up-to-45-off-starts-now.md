# Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW! ☀️🌴⛱️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury drops their summer sale with the strongest discount number in this recent cluster — 45% off is a concrete, legible anchor that outperforms anything in the UNREAL summer run to date. The "Take it ALL OFF!" pun is genuinely sharp copy, and the GWP tier ladder is a proven mechanic that rewards trading up. The visual execution is clean, the hierarchy is tight, and the warm golden palette stays on-brand.
- The problem is the gap between what the subject promises and what the email delivers. "Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW!" implies click-and-shop; every primary CTA in the body says "DOWNLOAD THE APP." For any subscriber who shops on desktop or hasn't installed the CT app, this is a wall, not a funnel. The June 16 send pulled this exact same app-gating move — and here it's back, with higher discount stakes and higher friction.
- Cadence is the other problem. This arrives 24 hours after the Summer Solstice send, which scored an 8/10 — CT's strongest send in the recent run. Two promotional emails in two days from a luxury brand erodes the premium feel that makes their 45% off feel like an event rather than a clearance fire sale.

## What's working

- **"Take it ALL OFF!" is the best line CT has sent in this cluster** — the double meaning (makeup removal + the discount) is clever, memorable, and brand-right.
- **45% is the clearest, most concrete offer number** in recent CT sends; it dominates the hero and reads instantly.
- **GWP tier ladder** (1 gift → 2 gifts → 3 gifts with spend thresholds) is cleanly laid out and incentivizes trading up.
- **"App Early Access" ticker at the very top** does at least flag the app requirement before the CTA hits, even if the subject telegraphs nothing.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean and uncluttered** — the eye lands on the discount first, then the secondary feature, then the benefits block.

## What's weak

- **Subject-to-CTA mismatch creates a bait-and-switch moment** — "Starts NOW!" implies frictionless access; "DOWNLOAD THE APP" is not that.
- **No end date anywhere** — "Starts NOW!" builds entry urgency but there's no close date, making "I'll get to it later" the path of least resistance.
- **Day-after cadence is too aggressive** — arriving 24 hours after the Solstice send (an 8/10), this send competes with rather than builds on the goodwill of that email.
- **Three emojis + "NOW!" in the subject** reads cluttered and slightly frantic for a luxury brand; undermines the elevated tone the body achieves.
- **No web fallback in the hero flow** — the "CHARLOTTETILBURY.COM + APP" mention is buried in the lower benefits block, not surfaced as a CTA for non-app users.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a web CTA alongside the app CTA** — a secondary text link ("or shop charlottetilbury.com") beneath each app button costs nothing and rescues every desktop shopper who won't download an app for one sale. Highest single-impact change.
- 2. **Put a hard end date on everything** — "sale ends Sunday" or "through June 25" on both the 45% off and the GWP tiers turns ambient urgency into a real deadline.
- 3. **Trim the subject to one emoji and flag the exclusivity** so the inbox expectation matches what's inside:
- **Subject Alt A:** `App Early Access: Up to 45% Off Iconic Kits ☀️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `45% Off Starts Now — App-Exclusive Preview`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free gifts from $69. Shop the summer kits before anyone else.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Golden Goddess Glow + Iconic Kits up to 45% off — open now in the app.`
- 4. **Rest the cadence** — the Solstice send deserved 48–72 hours of inbox shelf space; let it breathe before firing the next promo.
- 5. **Add a hard close to the GWP tiers** — "through June 25 only" on the gift ladder makes the tier mechanic feel urgent, not evergreen.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (Gmail web, Outlook 365) surface the title tag in UI chrome. Should contain a meaningful value matching the subject line.

**Linked logo has empty `alt` attribute:**
```html
<img ... alt="" title="">
```
The logo is wrapped in an `<a>` tag. For screen readers, a linked image with `alt=""` will be announced as the raw URL. WCAG 2.1 (SC 1.1.1) requires a text alternative; minimum fix is `alt="Charlotte Tilbury"`.

**External font CSS with no load fallback:**
```html
<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css" ...>
```
Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>` (correct). However, if the origin is unreachable at render time, custom fonts silently drop to client defaults — acceptable only if the font stack inside `fonts.css` includes proper system-font fallbacks.

**MSO/VML conditional comments:** present and correctly structured. Responsive breakpoint at `620px` is standard.
