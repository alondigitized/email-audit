# NEW! Exagger-Eyes Easy Eyeshadow Sticks Have Landed 🌟
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury is on a roll. After pivoting off the exhausted 20%-off treadmill with yesterday's Met Gala Exagger-Eyes liner launch (scored 8/10), today's send picks up the thread with the Eyeshadow Sticks variant and teaches you how to actually use the product family. The "1, 2, 3, DONE!" / SCRIBBLE–SMUDGE–SWIPE tutorial structure is the right editorial move: it reduces intimidation, showcases the formula's transfer-proof differentiation, and builds desire through demonstration rather than discount.
- The execution is mostly clean. The hero model shot is glamorous, the swatch section is well-lit and shoppable-looking, and the "Tilbury Tip" + signature quote at the bottom closes the email with brand warmth. Where it falls down is conversion architecture: the one tangible incentive ("2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts") is buried so deep it might as well be in the footer, and there is no clear shop-now CTA button above the scroll fold. You get the full education and none of the nudge.
- Judgement: a strong product-education email that trusts the creative too much and the offer mechanics too little. It earns its open; it doesn't fully earn the click.

## What's working

- **Transfer-proof claim leads prominently** — "TRANSFER-PROOF!" banner + model application shot immediately answers the "why buy this" question before you read a word of copy.
- **SCRIBBLE / SMUDGE / SWIPE tutorial** — three-step structure is memorable, democratizes eye makeup, and doubles as product differentiation.
- **Shade swatch grid** — professional swatch photography with labeled shades gives beauty consumers the colour-range context they need to commit.
- **"Everyone can be an icon" quote + Charlotte signature** — aspirational, on-brand voice closes the body before the footer and avoids ending on a generic CTA.
- **Cadence reset is real** — after eight consecutive 20%-off sends (May 14–26), two consecutive product-launch emails feel like news, not noise.

## What's weak

- **GWP buried in the footer** — "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts" is the strongest conversion hook in the email and it appears below the social icons. Swapping it up to the second scroll position would materially lift clicks.
- **No visible CTA button in the body** — the only shop prompt is "CHARLOTTETILBURY.COM + APP" in the footer-adjacent block; there is no dedicated button above the fold or mid-email.
- **Second straight "NEW! Exagger-Eyes…" subject opener** — yesterday's subject was "NEW! Exagger-Eyes Spotted At The Met Gala 👀"; today's is "NEW! Exagger-Eyes Easy Eyeshadow Sticks Have Landed 🌟". Back-to-back template subjects train the eye to skim past them.
- **No price or urgency signal** — pure education is fine for Day 1 of a launch; by Day 2 a subscriber reasonably wants to know what the sticks cost or whether there's an introductory offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pull the GWP up** — move "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts with your order" into a band immediately below the hero, then add a "SHOP EXAGGER-EYES STICKS" button. That single repositioning turns a buried afterthought into a real conversion driver.
- 2. **Add a mid-email CTA button** — place a solid CT button (dark/branded, label: "SHOP EXAGGER-EYES STICKS") between the shade grid and the Tilbury Tip. One anchor, one action.
- 3. **Break the "NEW! Exagger-Eyes…" subject template** — two identical openers in a row teaches subscribers to gloss over the lead word. Vary the entry angle (see alts below).
- 4. **Add a soft price anchor** — even "from £XX" under the shade grid removes decision friction without forcing a discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 steps to transfer-proof eyes — meet the Sticks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Eyeshadow Sticks the Met Gala inspired 🌟`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Scribble. Smudge. Swipe. Done. + 2 free minis with your first order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pro artist-approved formula now in a beginner-friendly stick — shop the launch.`
