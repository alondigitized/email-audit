# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Now I have what I need to write the review.
- This is Charlotte Tilbury doing what she does best — wrapping a blunt commercial offer in that signature warm, rosy, luxe aesthetic. The whole above-the-fold is one big "20% OFF EVERYTHING" moment flanked by a loyalty-exclusive gift I immediately want. It reads like a brand that knows its returning customer is already half-converted before the page even loads.

## What's working

- **The loyalty banner at the very top** — "Unlock A Free Collagen Lip Bath In Pillow Talk Fair When You Spend $115" is exactly the kind of GWP that gets me opening my Notes app to calculate what I need to add to hit threshold
- **"20% OFF EVERYTHING!*"** — unambiguous, huge, impossible to miss; as someone who waits for the big CT events to stock up, this is the signal I came to see
- **The product flatlay selection** — a palette, what I'm reading as a Beautiful Skin product, a pencil, and what looks like a Magic Cream jar are all products in my actual rotation, which makes the sale feel directly relevant to me
- **Brand restraint on clutter** — the above-the-fold is focused; they didn't shove six banners at me and let the offer breathe

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is a text link, not a button** — for a 20% sale this significant, that CTA deserves a proper pill button. The underline link almost disappears against the warmth of the hero
- **No model or person in frame** — I love CT's runway-beauty positioning but a lifestyle shot of someone actually wearing Pillow Talk while the sale runs would pull me in emotionally, not just rationally
- **Zero time urgency** — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing. I know from experience that CT's % off events don't last forever, but nothing on screen tells me that. I could easily think "I'll come back later" and then miss it
- **No free shipping callout** — the loyalty GWP threshold is $115 but I can't see what ships free. That's a piece of information I want before I build a cart

## Recommendations

- **Replace the text-link "Shop Now" with a solid CTA button** — burgundy background, white text, standard pill shape — it should match the weight of the offer it sits under
- **Add a thin urgency strip below the hero** ("Sale ends May 26 · Shop before it's gone") — even a fabricated-feeling deadline converts better than silence
- **Swap one product in the flatlay for an explicit Magic Cream jar with a label**, since that's the product I personally check for every CT sale — naming the hero SKU increases relevance for loyalists like me
- **Add a free shipping threshold line** somewhere in the top zone — even "Free shipping on orders over $50" set in small text below the search bar would reduce cart abandonment from people like me doing mental math
