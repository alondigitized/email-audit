# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is quintessential Charlotte Tilbury — the romantic, blush-drenched fantasy I fully buy into every time I open the app. Right now they're leading with what looks like a Pillow Talk-coded hero campaign: three women in a floral dreamscape, that unmistakable dusty-pink lip. It reads spring romance, and honestly? For me that lands immediately.

## What's working

- **The hero image** is doing serious heavy lifting — three women in a blush floral scene holding what looks like a Pillow Talk lipstick. I saw this and felt seen immediately.
- **"Unlock Mini Magic Gifts When You Spend $95!"** — the banner offer is specific and achievable. I routinely spend more than that here, so this reads as a reward, not a stretch.
- **The search bar** ("Search product, shade, colour") is smart — "shade" and "colour" signal that this is a beauty search, not a generic product lookup. That tells me the UX understands how I actually shop.
- **Clean, high-contrast logo placement** — the Charlotte Tilbury script is immediately recognizable and the nav doesn't fight the hero.
- **Diversity in the hero** without it feeling forced — the three models feel like a genuine campaign cast, not a token gesture.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is a missed opportunity** — I'm already primed by the hero imagery, and a button that says "Shop Pillow Talk" or "Shop the Collection" would close that loop. "SHOP NOW" sends me somewhere generic.
- **No hero headline copy** — the entire above-the-fold experience is image + CTA. Beautiful, but I don't know what this campaign is called, what product it's centered on, or why I should care today versus yesterday.
- **No loyalty callout** — I have a Charlotte Tilbury account. I know they have a Beauty Unites rewards program (or equivalent), but nothing here acknowledges me as a returning customer. A "Welcome back" or rewards balance nudge would make me feel known.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — the $95 gift threshold is front and center, but I don't know if I get free shipping at that spend level. That's a common decision point for me.
- **The hamburger menu means one more tap** to reach any category. No quick-links to Lips, Skincare, or Magic Cream visible without expanding nav.

## Recommendations

- **Add a campaign headline over the hero** — even three words like "The Pillow Talk Edit" or "Spring's Most-Wanted Lip" would anchor the image and give me a reason to tap beyond aesthetic attraction.
- **Change "SHOP NOW" to a product-specific CTA** — "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Pillow Talk" tells me what I'm buying into and improves conversion for visitors who aren't already devotees.
- **Swap the generic account icon for a personalized loyalty nudge** — show returning members their points balance or a "Your Rewards" chip in the nav. I'd tap that instantly.
- **Add a secondary text strip below the hero** surfacing free-shipping information alongside the $95 GWP — two incentives, stacked, make the case for me to load up my cart right now.
