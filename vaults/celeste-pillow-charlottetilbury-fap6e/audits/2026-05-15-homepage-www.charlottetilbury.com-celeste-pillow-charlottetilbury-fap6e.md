# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Charlotte Tilbury doing what Charlotte Tilbury does best — leading with complexion and making it feel like a beauty event, not just a product push. The "IT'S BACK!" hook on the Airbrush Flawless Blur Concealer tells me there's been some drama around this formula, and I'm already curious. Mobile-first execution looks clean and elevated, very on-brand for the house.

## What's working

- **"IT'S BACK!" badge on the hero** — this is smart. It creates instant intrigue for anyone who knows the brand. I immediately want to know what happened to it and whether they changed the formula.
- **Product-only hero shot** — rows of concealers in every shade against that warm sandy backdrop is genuinely beautiful. It reads editorial without being pretentious.
- **GWP banner at top** — "Unlock Mini Magic Gifts When You Spend $95" is exactly the kind of low-friction incentive that tips me from browsing to buying. It's at the very top, can't miss it.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no pop-ups eating my screen on load, search bar right there. The navigation feels respectful of my time.
- **"Full Coverage With Elastic Stretch" subline** — gives me a real functional reason to care beyond just the brand name. That's a complexion claim that means something.

## What's weak

- **No face, no skin** — I'm looking at products floating in a void. For a concealer — a product about what your skin looks like when you wear it — showing zero skin is a miss. I want to see what "elastic stretch" actually does on a real complexion.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment** — I am a returning customer. I have bought Pillow Talks here. There's nothing on this page that knows that, or rewards it, or even hints that membership exists.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — for a relaunch moment, I'd want something like "Shop the Return" or "Get Yours Back." The CTA copy doesn't match the energy of "IT'S BACK!"
- **No price or shade count visible** — even a "30 shades" callout or the price point would help me calibrate whether I'm window-shopping or ready to act.

## Recommendations

- **Add a before/after or skin-close crop to the hero** — swap or layer one of the product beauty shots with a tight skin shot showing the elastic-stretch finish; concealer sells when you can see what it does to skin, not just what it looks like in the tube.
- **Upgrade the CTA copy to match the moment** — change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop the Return" or "Find My Shade" to mirror the "IT'S BACK!" energy and drive shade-matching behavior.
- **Drop a loyalty micro-badge near the GWP banner** — a small "Earn Beauty Points on this purchase" line costs nothing and turns a GWP banner into a double incentive for loyal shoppers like me.
- **Add shade count to the subheadline** — "Full Coverage With Elastic Stretch · 44 Shades" gives me a reason to tap before I even think about it; the shade range is presumably a selling point, use it.
