# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Oh she came out swinging — Charlotte knows I check her sale like a second job. The Summer Sale hero is warm, glowy, and immediately recognizable as everything I love about this brand: the bronzey complexion palette, what looks like a lip product front and center, that signature peachy-gold gradient. This is a sale homepage, full stop — it's not trying to be editorial or educational, it's trying to get me into the sale funnel fast.

## What's working

- **"SUMMER SALE — UP TO 50% OFF + FREE GIFTS AS YOU SHOP"** is the kind of headline that makes me stop scrolling Instagram mid-thumb. The offer is specific enough to be believable.
- **The product grouping in the hero** — I can see what looks like a foundation/face compact, a complexion product, and a lip product all styled together. That's my routine in one shot. Instant recognition.
- **The warm peach-to-coral gradient** is very on-brand and summer-coded without feeling cheap. Charlotte's visual identity is strong here.
- **The top-of-page banner** ("Up To 50% Off Iconic Kits + Unlock Free Gifts! T&Cs apply") reinforces the offer before I even hit the hero — double tap, I see it twice.
- **The "NEW! Ask" AI chat button** is a nice touch — high-tech comfort is my whole thing, and a shade-matching AI assistant is genuinely useful to me.
- **Search bar is prominent** — I can get to "Pillow Talk" or "Magic Cream" in one tap without hunting through a menu.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty callout above fold** — I've been shopping Charlotte Tilbury for years. I know there's a loyalty program. Why isn't there a "Member exclusive" badge or a "You've earned X points" hook for returning shoppers? This reads like a new-visitor sale page, not a returning-devotee page.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the weakest CTA Charlotte could write** — I have no idea if I'm shopping skincare, lips, or the full "Iconic Kits" mentioned in the banner. The disconnect between "Iconic Kits" at the top and "SHOP NOW" as the button is friction.
- **No model or lifestyle imagery** — it's all flatlay product against a gradient. For a brand that sells a *look* and a *fantasy*, no face in the hero feels like a missed opportunity. I want to see someone wearing the Pillow Talk.
- **Fine print is immediately visible but small** — the asterisks on 50% and Free Gifts are doing a lot of work below the CTA. "Minimum spend applies. While stocks last." That's three conditions in six words. It makes the offer feel a little more cautious than Charlotte's brand voice usually is.
- **No navigation categories visible** — the hamburger is collapsed. If I don't already know my way around (and I do, barely), I have zero visual cue about what categories exist.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA for category-specific CTAs below the hero** — "Shop Skincare Deals" / "Shop Lip Deals" / "Shop Face Deals" as three quick-tap pills would let me self-select in one motion and remove the "where am I even going" ambiguity.
- **Add a returning-shopper hook** — even a small "Welcome back — your loyalty points are waiting" banner or a persistent loyalty badge in the header would make regulars feel seen. CT's loyalty program is genuinely good; show it to me above the fold.
- **Put a face in the hero** — even a beauty close-up of someone in the 35-45 age range wearing a Pillow Talk-toned look would activate the aspirational hook. This sale is warm and luxurious but it needs a human anchor.
- **Clarify the free-gift mechanic upfront** — if there's a minimum spend, tell me the number in the hero: "Free gift with every $75+ order." A number converts better than asterisks.
