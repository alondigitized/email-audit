# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I open this and my first thought is: *this is home.* The romantic floral hero with three women in blush pink, one clearly holding what looks like a Pillow Talk bullet, is pure Charlotte Tilbury DNA — soft, aspirational, and genuinely beautiful. Right now the brand is pushing a GWP spend-threshold offer and leaning hard into that timeless, garden-party romanticism that made me fall for CT in the first place. No bells, no chaos — just the aesthetic doing the heavy lifting.

## What's working

- **The hero image itself** — three diverse women, lace and florals, that unmistakable CT blush palette. I recognize the world immediately, and one woman is clearly holding a pink lipstick tube. I'm in.
- **The GWP banner** — "Mini Magic Gifts When You Spend $95" is the exact kind of offer that pushes me from browsing to adding. I've got Pillow Talk backups to stock; I will spend $95 to unlock gifts.
- **The search bar prominence** — it lives right below the nav on mobile, front and center. I know exactly what I want half the time ("Pillow Talk Matte Revolution"), and this gets me there in seconds.
- **Clean, distraction-free mobile layout** — no pop-up blocking the hero, no spinning banners, no cluttered icon row. The page breathes.
- **The Charlotte Tilbury wordmark** — elegant, centered, immediately signals prestige. The font choice is consistently on-brand.

## What's weak

- **Zero hero headline copy** — there is no text over that beautiful hero image. I have no idea what this campaign is called or what the hero is selling. Is this a new collection? A lifestyle shoot? A Pillow Talk campaign? I genuinely cannot tell.
- **"SHOP NOW" is doing nothing for me** — that button copy could be for a furniture store. It doesn't say "Shop Pillow Talk," "Shop The Look," or "Explore the Collection." It's generic to the point of being meaningless on a brand this distinctive.
- **No loyalty hook whatsoever** — I am a CT Beauty Rewards member. My points balance, a "member exclusive" badge, a "you're X points from your next reward" nudge — none of that is visible. For a returning customer who came here specifically, this feels like a miss.
- **No product identity in the hero** — the lipstick being held is not named or labeled. If this IS a Pillow Talk moment, say so. I would tap immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Add hero headline copy** — even just "Pillow Talk Season" or the campaign name overlaid on the hero in CT's signature serif. The image is gorgeous; one line of copy would convert it from art into commerce.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for specific CTA copy** — "Shop Pillow Talk" or "Shop The Look" matches what the hero actually shows and tells me exactly what I'm tapping into.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards nudge in the banner or just below the hero** — something as simple as "Beauty Rewards members: $95 spend = double gifts" turns a generic offer into a loyalty payoff.
- **Name the product in the hero** — a subtle product callout ("Pillow Talk Matte Revolution, £29") overlaid or in a small module below the fold-break would eliminate zero-to-PDP friction entirely.
