# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This still reads like the Charlotte Tilbury I know and love — that plush, glam red hero shot and Charlotte's own face right there ready to help — but honestly the top of the page is trying to do too much at once. Between the holiday banner, the "Ask Charlotte" quiz, and a chat popup shoving itself over everything, it feels a little chaotic for a brand that's usually so polished. Clearly the push right now is the Holiday Shop and driving me into that AI-quiz-meets-live-chat funnel rather than showing me an actual product.

## What's working

- The **"Ask Charlotte" quiz block** ("5-min every day look," "Find my perfect foundation shade") — this is exactly the kind of personalized guidance that keeps me coming back, very on-brand.
- **Charlotte's own face** in both the round avatar and the chat popup — that founder-as-face-of-brand trust signal always lands with me, I feel like I'm shopping with her, not a faceless retailer.
- The **rich burgundy feather hero image** — moody, luxe, très Pillow Talk color family even without a product in frame.
- **"Holiday shop is now open!!!"** banner up top tells me exactly what's new right now, which I always check first.

## What's weak

- The **chat popup literally covers "Not sure where to start, darling?" and cuts off the third quiz button** ("I'm not sur...") — that's sloppy for a brand this premium.
- **No actual product on screen** — the hero is texture/mood only, no lipstick, no Magic Cream, nothing I can tap straight into.
- **No concrete offer** — just "Holiday shop is now open," no percentage off, no gift-with-purchase teaser, nothing that gives me a reason to act today.
- **Loyalty is just a word in the nav** — no points balance, no member perk teaser, feels like an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- Swap the abstract feather hero for a lifestyle or product shot — a hand holding the new holiday Pillow Talk set would convert me instantly.
- Move or auto-dismiss the "Speak to a Makeup Artist" popup so it doesn't overlap the quiz CTAs — let me read "Not sure where to start, darling?" in full.
- Add a real offer chip above the fold — "Free deluxe gift over $75" or similar — holiday shoppers like me want to see the incentive immediately.
- Give "Loyalty" a visible perk teaser in the header (e.g., "Earn double points this week") instead of a bare label.
