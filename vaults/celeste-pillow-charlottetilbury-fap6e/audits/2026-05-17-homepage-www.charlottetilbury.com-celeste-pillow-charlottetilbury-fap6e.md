# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury is doing what it does best — leading with a hero product, clean gold-accented packaging shot, and that unmistakable warm cream palette that reads luxury without screaming it. Right now the homepage is all about the Airbrush Flawless Blur Concealer being back, and for a complexion-first devotee like me, that's immediately relevant. It's targeted, it's clean, and it feels like CT knows exactly who opens this app.

## What's working

- **"IT'S BACK!" relaunch hook** — as someone who tracks product restocks, that two-word tease makes me stop scrolling immediately. I know the Blur Concealer has a cult following and this signals urgency without a countdown timer.
- **Full product family shot** — showing the shade range in one image answers the "do they have my shade?" question before I even tap. That row of bottles speaks to the full-coverage concealers I actually buy.
- **"Full Coverage With Elastic Stretch" descriptor** — for a complexion buyer who knows what she wants, this sub-copy is genuinely informative, not fluffy.
- **Top strip offer** — "Unlock Mini Magic Gifts When You Spend $95" is on-brand for CT's GWP model; I know from years of buying that the Minis are actually good, so this registers as a real incentive.
- **Clean mobile layout** — logo centered, hamburger left, wishlist heart and bag right — the nav chrome is exactly where I expect it and not fighting the hero.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty/rewards hook above the fold** — I'm a repeat CT customer. There's no "Welcome back, Celeste" tier acknowledgment, no rewards balance, nothing that says CT knows I've spent here before.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — for a product this specific, "Shop Blur Concealer" or "Find My Shade" would convert better. The copy does the heavy lifting in the hero but then hands off to the most vanilla CTA possible.
- **No model/skin application shot** — the product packaging is beautiful, but I want to see the concealer *on* a complexion. A split of the bottles + a close-up skin-finish shot would close the last gap.
- **Top strip is tiny and easy to miss** — the GWP offer is the only transactional hook above the hero and it's in 10px text at the very top. I almost skipped it.
- **No new-arrivals or best-seller rail visible** — I'm a multiproduct shopper; a "Also loving right now" strip would pull me deeper into the site instead of leaving me with one binary choice.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Find My Shade"** — shade anxiety is the #1 concealer barrier; a shade-finder CTA converts better and is completely shippable with existing CT infrastructure.
- **Add a thin loyalty bar just above the hero** — "You're 200 points from your next reward" or even a generic "Join Beauty Unlocked — earn on every order" strips in under a designer hour and addresses the biggest missing signal for returning shoppers.
- **Composite a skin-application panel into the hero image** — one half bottles, one half a close-up of the concealer blended on warm medium skin. This is a one-day asset swap, not a redesign.
- **Make the GWP strip scannable** — bold the "$95" and "Mini Magic Gifts" so the offer reads in a glance; right now the whole strip blurs into a single line of text.
