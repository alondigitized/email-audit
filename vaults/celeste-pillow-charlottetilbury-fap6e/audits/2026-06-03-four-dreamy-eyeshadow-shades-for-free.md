# FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades... For FREE! 🤩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury is back with a loyalty-exclusive GWP send, and it's their most generous offer in this recent run — a full Pillow Talk Dreams quad versus the "2 free minis" from the May 29 email (scored 8/10). "FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades" is smarter subject framing than "free palette" because it trades category language for sensory specificity — you can almost picture four distinct pans. The loyalty-exclusive banner lands immediately at the top, the hero model is on-brand, and the Pillow Talk franchise is CT's strongest equity anchor.
- The execution stumbles in one consequential place: the CTA reads "JOIN + SHOP NOW" on an email explicitly addressed to loyalty members. If you already belong, "JOIN" plants a small seed of doubt — do I need to do something first? The spend threshold to unlock the palette is also invisible in the body, which makes the "For FREE!" subject feel like fine print is being withheld. Charlotte Tilbury ran countdown timers and "Ends Tonight" urgency throughout May; this send has none of that, so the offer feels open-ended rather than pressing.
- Net verdict: a strong, well-targeted loyalty send with a legitimately compelling reward. The creative is clean, the brand voice is consistent, and the offer is the best in the recent sequence. The CTA copy and the hidden threshold are the only two things standing between this and a sharper result.

## What's working

- The "EXCLUSIVE TO LOYALTY MEMBERS!" banner is the very first thing you see — signals relevance before the hero even registers.
- "FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades" framing counts the product rather than categorizing it; more visceral and more generous-feeling than "free palette."
- The Pillow Talk Dreams quad is shown clearly as a physical product alongside the hero model, so the reward is tangible rather than abstract.
- "COLLECT YOUR FREE PALETTE" as a secondary CTA below the hero is a smart re-engagement hook for scrollers who miss the first button.
- The escalation from May 29's 2 free minis to a full quad is a meaningful step up — the loyalty program feels like it's actually rewarding members more over time.

## What's weak

- "JOIN + SHOP NOW" is a confusing CTA on a loyalty-exclusive email — "JOIN" implies you might not qualify yet, which creates friction for the audience this was sent to.
- The spend threshold to unlock the palette is not disclosed anywhere in the visible body — "For FREE!" in the subject overpromises relative to what's shown.
- No urgency signal — no deadline, no "while supplies last," no countdown — leaving the offer feel available indefinitely, which kills impulse.
- The app download section below the fold competes with the single-offer focus and reads like footer filler rather than a meaningful secondary action.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the CTA.** "JOIN + SHOP NOW" → "CLAIM YOUR FREE PALETTE" — existing members claim, they don't join. This removes the single biggest friction point.
- 2. **Show the threshold in the hero.** Add "Spend $X, unlock 4 free shades" as a sub-line under the headline. Transparency here increases AOV rather than suppressing clicks — subscribers who've been caught by CT's GWP conditions before won't click unless they know the deal upfront.
- 3. **Add one urgency lever.** A simple "Available through June 7, loyalty members only" line, or a low-inventory signal, would convert fence-sitters without needing a full countdown timer.
- 4. **Cut the app section.** This send has one job. The app module dilutes it with no measurable payoff for a GWP redemption email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free Pillow Talk palette is waiting — loyalty members only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members get 4 free shades — claim yours before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your loyalty reward: the Pillow Talk Dreams quad, on us. Spend $X to unlock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four dreamy shades, completely free — here's how to claim yours.`
