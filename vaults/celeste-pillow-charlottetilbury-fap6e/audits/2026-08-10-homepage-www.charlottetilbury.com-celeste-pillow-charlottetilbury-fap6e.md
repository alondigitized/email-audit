# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This still looks and feels exactly like Charlotte Tilbury to me — that warm gold-and-shell aesthetic never gets old. Right now they're pushing the "Unreal Collection" summer glow-up with new Mini Unreal Blush Sticks and a Highlighter Stick, plus a 15%-off-first-order banner up top. It's pretty, on-brand, and easy on the eyes, but as someone who already has three Pillow Talk lipsticks in rotation, nothing here is really talking to me specifically.

## What's working

- The top banner ("15% off + FREE delivery on your first order with code DARLING15") is impossible to miss and gives me an instant reason to poke around.
- "Not sure where to start, darling?" module with quick buttons ("Find my perfect foundation shade," "5-min every day look") and the "ASK CHARLOTTE" button feel very on-brand — that's the voice I associate with their emails.
- The Unreal Collection hero shot with the blush sticks, highlighter, and that little strawberry-print one is genuinely eye-catching — I'd stop scrolling on that.
- "Shop By Category" tiles right below the fold get me moving fast toward makeup/skincare without hunting through the nav.

## What's weak

- The hero pushes an entirely new sub-line (Unreal Blush/Highlighter Sticks) instead of anything tied to Pillow Talk or Magic Cream — as a repeat buyer of both, I'd expect at least a nod to "your favorites are back in stock" or a Pillow Talk callout.
- That 15% off code is explicitly "first order" — as someone who's bought here for years, that offer does nothing for me and low-key feels like it's not for loyal customers.
- The "Loyalty" nav item is just a word with no visible perks, points balance, or member pricing — I have no idea what's in it for me without clicking in.
- No product images include an actual person, so there's nothing here signaling "someone like me" is shopping this — it's all flatlay product shots.

## Recommendations

- Swap or add a hero panel that speaks to returning shoppers — "Restock your Pillow Talk favorites" with the lipstick front and center.
- Turn the "Loyalty" nav link into a small badge or teaser (e.g., "You have 240 points") for logged-in/returning visitors instead of a plain label.
- Add a repeat-customer offer alongside the first-order code — something like free shipping over $50 for everyone, not just new customers.
- Drop in one lifestyle shot of a woman actually wearing the Pillow Talk lip or Magic Cream glow in the hero rotation — pure product flatlays start to feel a little cold.
