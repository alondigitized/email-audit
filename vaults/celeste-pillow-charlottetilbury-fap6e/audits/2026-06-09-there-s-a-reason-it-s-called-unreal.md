# There’s A Reason It’s Called Unreal 👀✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury pivots cleanly from the Exagger-Eyes eye-product run (May 28 through June 7) into complexion territory with what appears to be a new "Unreal Skin" foundation or serum-foundation. The structural bet here — three sequenced benefit modules (15-second application → 60-second shade match → unreal glow finish) before landing on a GWP — is sound product education. The specific claims are the email's best asset: "15 seconds" and "60 seconds" are memorable, credible, and differentiated in a category where "effortless" is overused noise.
- The problem is the offer is buried. The Free Mini Magic Gift lands at the very bottom of a long email, app-gated, with a spend threshold that's illegible at render size. Compared to the June 3 send — "FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades... For FREE!" which led with the deal — this one asks you to read five modules before it tells you there's a reason to buy *today*.
- The subject line's wordplay is clever, but it sacrifices category clarity. Someone who's been getting Charlotte Tilbury daily for two weeks and sees "There's A Reason It's Called Unreal" has no idea whether this is skincare, foundation, mascara, or fragrance. That ambiguity costs opens.

## What's working

- **"15 Second Application" and "60 Second Shade Match"** — precise, testable claims that stand out in a commoditized category. These are the email's strongest differentiators and are visually prominent.
- **Diverse model casting** — showing multiple skin tones across the complexion modules is exactly right for a shade-range story; it earns credibility rather than just claiming it.
- **Clean visual hierarchy in the hero** — the headline "THERE'S A REASON IT'S CALLED UNREAL SKIN!" is large, legible, and immediately communicates the product focus.
- **Category pivot** — after a heavy eye-product run through early June, shifting to complexion feels fresh and non-repetitive. CT avoids the trap its May/June sends sometimes fall into.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, no overlapping text or broken modules.

## What's weak

- **GWP buried at the bottom** — the Free Mini Magic Gift is the most action-driving element, yet it's the second-to-last module. Subscribers who skim won't reach it.
- **App-exclusive GWP creates channel friction** — limiting the offer to the app punishes the web-only majority of subscribers; the June 3 Pillow Talk Quad GWP worked because it was channel-agnostic.
- **Spend threshold is illegible** — the GWP mechanics text is too small to read in the render; a subscriber can't quickly assess whether they qualify.
- **Subject line doesn't signal category** — "Unreal" is the product name, not a descriptor of what kind of product it is. After daily emails, a subscriber needs more signal to justify the open.
- **No urgency signal anywhere** — no "for a limited time," no deadline, no countdown. The email has no reason-to-act-now beyond general newness.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the GWP into the hero or module two.** The product education is good, but "learn how it works *and* get a free mini" is a stronger combined hook than "learn how it works, then we'll mention there's a free gift later." Drop a GWP badge into the hero image and repeat it after the shade module.
- 2. **Make the spend threshold large and scannable.** Whatever the threshold is (appears to be £/$/€70+), it should appear in at least 18px text with a bold anchor — "Spend $75, Get Your Free Mini." Illegible fine print erodes trust.
- 3. **Add a subtle deadline or limited-quantity signal** to the GWP module. Even "While supplies last" converts better than silence.
- 4. **Rethink the app exclusivity or add a web fallback.** App-only offers alienate the segment that hasn't downloaded. Consider "App gets it free, web gets it with code UNREAL."
- 5. **Open with a tighter subject line that names the product type:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Foundation: Unreal Skin in 15 Seconds`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Foundation They're Calling Unreal 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free mini gift when you try it — app + site`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `60-sec shade match. 15-sec application. See why.`
