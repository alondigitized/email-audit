# Did You Miss This Magic? 💌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a digest/highlights format — Charlotte Tilbury titled it "Charlotte's Magic Highlights" and built it as a five-module roundup recapping product launches from the last three sends: the Exagger-Eyes Eyeshadow Sticks (May 28), the GWP completion mechanic with 2 free minis (May 29), plus returning product "It's Back Darling!", a new "Meet Glow + Go!" bronzer section, a summer icons module, and a lip product teaser at the bottom.
- The format is a legitimate play for subscribers who didn't open the last few sends. But for anyone who did open all three Exagger-Eyes emails this week, this is nearly 100% recycled content — same products, same GWP offer, same CTAs. The digest structure also creates a classic multi-CTA dilution problem: five sections with five "SHOP NOW" buttons means no single product wins the eye or earns the click.
- The subject "Did You Miss This Magic?" does the bare minimum — FOMO hook without telling the subscriber what they missed. Paired with a likely generic preheader, the open-decision moment is weak relative to how much content sits inside.

## What's working

- The Exagger-Eyes trilogy is cleanly preserved at the top with the 3-step system walkthrough, giving first-time openers useful product education.
- The GWP mechanic (2 free minis) is visually prominent in the hero section and acts as the email's strongest conversion anchor.
- Brand visual consistency is flawless across all five modules — fonts, product photography, and color palette are cohesive.
- Summer theming on the lower modules feels timely and seasonal without feeling forced.

## What's weak

- Subject line tells the subscriber nothing specific — "this magic" could mean anything.
- The digest format drowns the GWP offer, which was the May 29 centerpiece, by surrounding it with four other product pushes at equal visual weight.
- Every single CTA reads "SHOP NOW" — no differentiation across five distinct products and offers.
- No urgency visible anywhere: the GWP, "It's Back Darling," Glow + Go — none carry a deadline.
- For the engaged subscriber who opened May 27–29, this is the third or fourth time seeing the Exagger-Eyes pitch with zero new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor the subject on the actual offer, not the concept.** "Did You Miss This Magic?" burns the open rate on abstraction. Tell them what they missed — free minis, new eyeshadow, the returning product — and the open does the job.
- 2. **Put a deadline on the GWP.** If the 2-free-minis offer expires, say so here. An expiring GWP is the best reason to make this a standalone send instead of a digest.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs.** "Shop Eyeshadow Sticks," "Claim Your Free Minis," "Shop Glow + Go" each beat "SHOP NOW" and help the eye navigate a multi-module layout.
- 4. **Cut to three modules maximum.** Five sections with equal hierarchy is five hero slots — which means no hero. Lead with GWP, support with the summer icons, close with the returning product.
- 5. **Reference the send history explicitly.** A short line like "In case you missed our biggest launch this week…" acknowledges the subscriber's inbox and justifies the digest framing.
