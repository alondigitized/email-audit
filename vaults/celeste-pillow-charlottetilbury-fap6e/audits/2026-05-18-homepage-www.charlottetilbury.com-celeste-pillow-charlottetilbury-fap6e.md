# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Oh, she knows what she's doing. Charlotte Tilbury's mobile homepage is landing squarely on a hero product I've had in my bathroom cabinet for two years — and the "IT'S BACK!" framing feels like a personal text from Charlotte herself. This is a brand that operates like a luxury house with a cult following, and right now it's leaning hard into a complexion relaunch with a gift-with-purchase hook layered on top.

## What's working

- **"IT'S BACK!" opener** — for a devotee who watched the Airbrush Flawless line evolve, this headline hits like nostalgia and newness at the same time. I'm already curious what changed.
- **Product arrangement shot** — that fanned array of concealers across every undertone reads like "we made a shade for you specifically," which is genuinely reassuring for complexion shopping.
- **$95 spend threshold banner** — I'm probably spending $95 anyway. Framing it as a gift unlock rather than a discount feels premium and not desperate.
- **Search bar placement** — "Search product, shade, colour" right below the nav is exactly the right on-ramp for someone like me who already knows what she wants and just needs to confirm it's in stock.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook visible** — I buy Charlotte Tilbury at Sephora partly for the Insider points. If charlottetilbury.com has a direct loyalty program or a reason to buy here instead of at a retailer, I can't see it.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — after such a specific product call-out, "Shop Now" undersells the next step. "Shop Blur Concealer" or "Find Your Shade" would keep the momentum going.
- **No model/skin context** — the product-only hero is gorgeous but it gives me no sense of the finish on actual skin. For a concealer relaunch, a before/after or a close-up skin shot would close the loop.
- **No trust signals in this viewport** — no star ratings, no "4.8 stars from 12,000 reviews," no free-returns mention. For a complexion product where shade fit is everything, that absence creates friction.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Find Your Shade"** — keeps the shade-matching intent front and center and signals there's a helpful tool waiting, not just a product page.
- **Add a skin-result inset** — a small secondary image showing the concealer on a range of skin tones (even thumbnail-sized) alongside the product shot would answer the "will this work on me?" question before I have to go find it.
- **Surface the loyalty or direct-purchase benefit in the top banner** — something like "Buy direct for free shipping + mini gift on $95" stacks two reasons to buy here vs. Sephora in one line.
- **Add social proof micro-copy near the CTA** — even "Loved by 50,000+ customers" or a star-rating snippet under the "SHOP NOW" button would reduce the hesitation gap for new shoppers and reaffirm it for returning ones.
