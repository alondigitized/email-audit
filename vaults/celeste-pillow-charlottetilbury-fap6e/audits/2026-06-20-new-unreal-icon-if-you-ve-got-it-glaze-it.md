# NEW! Unreal Icon! If You've Got It... Glaze It!  💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury is launching what appears to be a new glaze product — "Hydrated Glaze" — as the next entry in its UNREAL franchise. The email deploys the now-familiar summer playbook: warm golden-hour hero shot, campaign copy introducing the product's credentials, multiple editorial-style sections working through the product story, and a secondary GWP buried at the bottom. The tone and aesthetic are on-brand and summer-appropriate.
- The problem is structural: this is the seventh Charlotte Tilbury email in eight days (June 12–20) and the fourth using the UNREAL framework. The email is doing a lot of explaining — four distinct editorial beats, each with its own headline — in what should be a tight product launch. By the time a subscriber reaches the "Gorgeously Flattering Tailored Golden Shade" section, attention has long since collapsed.
- The subject pun ("If You've Got It... Glaze It!") is charming, but it front-loads the campaign name and buries the actual news. This is a new product launch; the email buries the lede.

## What's working

- **Warm golden-hour hero is visually strong** — the hero shot earns attention immediately and reads as summer-specific without feeling generic.
- **The UNREAL brand frame lands** — "What Makes It UNREAL?" gives the product a credibility hook and continues the campaign coherently.
- **Skin-inclusive shade messaging** — the "Tailored Golden Shade" section with a darker-complexioned model signals broad demographic intent.
- **Secondary GWP offer visible** — the "Get 2 FREE" panel at the bottom gives a reason to act beyond just learning about the product.

## What's weak

- **Four editorial sections is too many for a single product launch** — "Game-Changing Glaze," "Revive Skin With A Glazed Glow," and "Gorgeously Flattering" are three ways of saying the same thing.
- **No visible discount or urgency mechanism in the primary offer zone** — a product launch with no price anchor, intro offer, or deadline gives the reader permission to come back later (and not).
- **GWP is buried at the bottom** — the secondary offer (the clearest reason to click now) appears after multiple scroll lengths, too late to rescue the email.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is the seventh email in eight days from Charlotte Tilbury; the June 17 sticker send and June 19 summer line-up send are still recent enough that this glaze launch risks feeling like noise rather than news.
- **Subject length exceeds mobile threshold** — "NEW! Unreal Icon! If You've Got It... Glaze It! 💫" reads long in an inbox and the "NEW!" opener is the weakest possible first word.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the GWP as the call to action** — move the "Get 2 FREE" offer into the hero zone. Product-only launches without a deal mechanism have a lower click floor; the GWP is the conversion hook.
- 2. **Collapse the editorial sections to one** — keep the strongest single product story beat ("Revive Skin With A Glazed Glow" is the most evocative) and cut the others. Three headlines all selling the same glaze dilute each other.
- 3. **Add a deadline or exclusivity signal** — even a soft "limited launch shades" line creates closure urgency a pure launch email currently lacks.
- 4. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the news, not the campaign name** — the UNREAL name is earning diminishing returns as the frame for every send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your new summer glow: UNREAL Glaze is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Glazed skin, golden summer — shop the new UNREAL Icon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hydrated, radiant, all-day glazed finish — plus 2 free gifts with your order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet the newest UNREAL Icon. Shop now and unlock 2 free gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Charlotte Tilbury "Unreal Icon" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built with a standard table-based layout delivered via Braze (EU region), with VML Outlook shims and a Beefree-style multi-row structure. Several structural HTML violations and a truncated dark-mode CSS block introduce parsing risk across clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Braze redirect domain confirmed:** All links route through `clicks.charlottetilbury.com` (Braze click-tracking). This is expected, but:

- **Destination URL unverifiable** — the `href` in the visible CTA is a Braze-encoded opaque token. UTM parameters on the *final* landing page cannot be confirmed from the source (see §6).
- **Image CDN:** `cdn.braze.eu` (EU Braze CDN) — consistent with EU data residency; no issue on its own, but image blocking will hit users with images disabled since no `alt` is visible in the truncated `<img>` tag.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Empty `<title>` | `<title></title>` in `<head>` | Low — some clients surface this in tab/notification text |
| `<meta>` tags inside `<div>` | `<meta name="color-scheme" ...>` placed in a `<td>` HTML block, not `<head>` | Medium — these are silently ignored; dark mode color-scheme hint never applies |
| Unclosed dark-mode `@media` block | `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { ... }` in inline `<style>` inside `<div>` is missing closing `}` in source | High — malformed CSS can cause downstream rule bleed in WebKit-based clients |
| Raw `<tr><td>` inside `<div>` | HTML block wraps table rows in a `<div>` (invalid HTML) | Medium — Outlook and older clients may mis-render |
| External font stylesheet | `<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css">` | Medium — stripped by Gmail, Yahoo, and most webmail; fallbacks (`Helvetica, Arial, Sans-serif`) kick in but may affect visual fidelity |
| No visible `alt` text | `<img src="https://cdn.braze.eu/...` truncated with no `alt=` in scope | High — accessibility failure; images-off rendering shows blank |

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Preheader is fully static:** `"Unlock Limited-Edition Stickers When You Shop Charlotte's Unreal Icons!"` — no recipient-level token (e.g., `{{first_name}}` or Braze `{{${first_name}}}`).
- No merge tokens are visible anywhere in the truncated source. If the full HTML contains none, the send is fully un-personalized, which is a missed Braze capability.
- No conditional content blocks (Braze `{% if %}` or Connected Content) detected.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`noreply@mail.charlottetilbury.com` sender address** — CAN-SPAM and GDPR best practice requires a functional reply-to or visible contact path. A `noreply` From with no Reply-To header set is technically non-blocking for CAN-SPAM but degrades trust signals and can trigger spam filters.
- **Footer not visible** in truncated source — CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link cannot be confirmed. Must be verified in the full render. Row `row-41` exists in the CSS (social block), suggesting a footer is present but unconfirmed.
- **Authentication headers** not available in the HTML source — DKIM/SPF/DMARC must be verified at the MX/header level on delivery.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All CTAs pass through Braze's opaque redirect (`clicks.charlottetilbury.com`). Braze appends UTM parameters at redirect time if configured in the campaign settings — **this cannot be confirmed from the HTML alone**. Verify in Braze campaign settings that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are set on the link-tracking configuration.
- If UTMs are not configured at the Braze level, all traffic lands on `charlottetilbury.com` as direct, breaking attribution.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix the unclosed dark-mode `@media` block** — add the missing `}` before this ships; CSS bleed in Apple Mail is a real risk.
2. **Move `<meta name="color-scheme">` to `<head>`** — it does nothing inside a `<td>`.
3. **Add `alt` text to all images** — required for accessibility compliance and images-off rendering.
4. **Audit Braze campaign link settings** to confirm UTM parameters are injected at redirect time, not missing entirely.
5. **Remove raw `<tr><td>` from inside the HTML `<div>` block** or restructure as a proper nested table — the current structure is invalid HTML.
6. **Set a Reply-To header** to a monitored inbox if the From must remain `noreply@`.
7. **Confirm footer** contains physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe — verify in full HTML render, not just the truncated source.
