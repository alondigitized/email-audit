# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Oh, they got me at the banner. A free Collagen Lip Bath in *Pillow Talk Fair* unlocked at $115? That is not a coincidence — that is Charlotte knowing exactly who she's talking to. The homepage is running a sitewide 20% off event with a clean flat-lay hero showcasing the Magic Cream jar and a palette, and the whole thing feels like a very on-brand, very me sale moment. This is a brand that doesn't need to explain itself; it just leads with the discount and the product I already love.

## What's working

- **The loyalty banner** — "Unlock A Free Collagen Lip Bath In Pillow Talk Fair When You Spend $115" is the most personally targeted line I've read on a homepage this week. That shade name alone stopped my scroll.
- **20% OFF EVERYTHING hero** — bold, hierarchically dominant, instantly legible at thumb-scroll speed on mobile. I don't have to hunt for the deal.
- **Magic Cream jar in the flat-lay** — I have a sixth sense for that gold lid. Seeing it in the hero confirms this sale covers skincare, not just makeup, and I'm immediately calculating whether to grab the Magic Cream Light I've been eyeing.
- **Peachy, blush-toned background** — it is so unapologetically Charlotte Tilbury. The brand identity is intact even at maximum promotional mode. The faint "MAGIC" watermark behind the products is a nice atmospheric detail.
- **Search bar prominently placed** — "Search product, shade, colour" tells me I can go straight for my shade without digging through nav. That matters when I already know what I want.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is doing too much heavy lifting** — with 20% off everything, I want a faster path to my category. One tap to "Shop Skincare" or "Shop Lips" would cut friction significantly.
- **No model or lifestyle imagery** — the flat-lay is beautiful but there's no face, no skin, no woman wearing this. For a brand whose entire world is about transformation and glamour, a faceless product shot on a blush background feels slightly cold at sale time.
- **No deadline or urgency signal** — "20% off everything" begs the question: for how long? No countdown, no "ends Sunday," nothing. A sale without an end date is a sale I'll come back to "later" — and later means never.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no star ratings, no "as seen in," no badge. I already trust Charlotte, but a first-timer would have nothing to anchor trust in before tapping Shop Now.
- **T&Cs asterisk with no expansion** — the 20% off reads "EVERYTHING!*" and the only footnote is "*T&Cs apply" in fine print at the bottom. I want to know if the value sets are included before I get to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Add a soft urgency line** — something like "Sale ends Monday" or a simple countdown pill beneath the "Shop Now" CTA. Charlotte doesn't need to shout; even a quiet deadline nudges me from "I'll check later" to "I'm tapping now."
- **Split the CTA into two tracks** — a "Shop Skincare" and "Shop Lips & Colour" button pair beneath the main "Shop Now" would route me to what I actually want in one tap and reduce pogo-sticking through the nav.
- **Swap or supplement the flat-lay with a face shot** — even a single model shot with finished skin showing the Magic Cream glow would emotionally anchor the skincare story. The products alone don't show me what they'll do for my skin.
- **Expand the loyalty banner copy slightly** — "Loyalty-Exclusive Gift: Pillow Talk Fair Collagen Lip Bath free when you spend $115" is compelling but tiny. Make it one notch larger or add an arrow-link so I can immediately verify my loyalty status.
