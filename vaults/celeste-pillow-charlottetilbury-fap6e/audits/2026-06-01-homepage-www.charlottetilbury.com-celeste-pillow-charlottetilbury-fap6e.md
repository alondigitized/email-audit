# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- This is immediately and unmistakably Charlotte Tilbury — the typography, the close-up skin shot, the confident product naming. Right now the brand is pushing a new eye product launch, Exagger-Eyes, hard. It reads like a brand that knows its aesthetic completely, but the above-the-fold story is one product deep and doesn't reach out to a returning loyalist the way it could.

## What's working

- **The offer banner at top** — "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts When You Spend $100" is the right hook for me. I will hit $100 at CT without blinking. The placement as a sticky top strip means I can't miss it.
- **"NEW! EXAGGER-EYES" headline** — the bold weight, the tight tracking, the "NEW!" tag above it — this is exactly how CT communicates a launch. It reads the way their emails read, and I trust that consistency.
- **The hero image** — that close-up of a warm bronze eye being applied is genuinely gorgeous. It sells the product AND the brand's POV in one frame. Very CT editorial.
- **The "12 Universally-Flattering Shades!" chip** — this is doing work. It tells me there's depth in the range without making me go hunt for it.
- **Search bar prominence** — right below the nav, I can immediately type "Pillow Talk" and find my thing. That matters for a loyalist like me who knows exactly what she wants.

## What's weak

- **No returning-customer signal** — I've ordered from CT multiple times. There's no "welcome back," no loyalty balance, no "your shade match" prompt. The experience is identical for me and a first-time visitor.
- **"Shop Now" is doing zero heavy lifting** — this CTA could say "Shop Exagger-Eyes," "Find Your Shade," anything. "Shop Now" is the most generic two words in e-commerce.
- **No free shipping mention** — the offer is about free gifts, but for a $100+ basket I'd also want to know if shipping is covered. That information is absent above the fold.
- **No social proof anywhere** — not a star rating, not "bestseller," not "as seen in Vogue." CT has an enormous press and review footprint and none of it is working here.
- **One product is the entire story** — above the fold there's nothing that acknowledges the rest of the range. If I'm coming for skincare, I'm getting eye shadow and a "Shop Now" button with no breadcrumb to my category.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for a shade-led CTA** — "Find Your Shade" or "Shop All 12 Shades" directly addresses the "universally-flattering" claim and moves me toward a decision faster.
- **Add a "Back for more?" loyalty strip** below the hero — even something lightweight like "You have X points waiting" or a rewards program badge would make me feel seen as a repeat buyer.
- **Surface a trust/review signal on the hero product** — a "★★★★★ 2,400+ reviews" line near the headline would close the loop between editorial confidence and real-world validation.
- **Add free shipping threshold to the top banner or directly beneath the offer copy** — "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts When You Spend $100 + Free Shipping" consolidates the value story in one line.
