# A Free Gift, NEW! Bridal Boxes + So Much More! 💌
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is another "Charlotte's Magic Highlights" digest — the same recurring format that appeared on June 6 (scored 7/10) and previously in May. The email is doing five things at once: a free gift, the Unreal Glam push, Exagger-Eyes with 10% off, new Bridal Boxes, and a 20% off complexion block. Each section is reasonably well-produced individually, but the digest structure means the email has no single center of gravity. A real subscriber opening this faces a scroll-and-hunt experience, not a clear call to action.
- The bridal boxes are the only genuinely new beat here — and for June, that timing is defensible (peak wedding season). But the Exagger-Eyes block is a near-repeat of the June 6 send, and the "So Much More!" subject tail is the lazy catchall that erodes trust in this format over time.
- The send is competent but fatiguing. Charlotte Tilbury has run this digest pattern at least three times in the past two weeks, and the subject line pattern — "free gift + new thing + vague catchall + 💌" — is becoming brand noise rather than brand recognition.

## What's working

- **Bridal Boxes are genuinely timely.** June is peak wedding-adjacent shopping; the new product hook earns its placement.
- **Multiple price signals are visible.** 10% off Exagger-Eyes and 20% off in the complexion section mean subscribers who scroll will find concrete value.
- **The "Charlotte's Magic Highlights" banner is instantly recognizable.** Brand consistency at the top is solid.
- **Product photography quality is high throughout.** The amber/gold bottle and eye product renders cleanly with no broken images.
- **Free gift is surfaced near the top.** The "FREE MR MAGIC GIFT WITH…" block appears early enough to reward openers.

## What's weak

- **"So Much More!" is doing no work.** It inflates the subject without adding signal — a recurring problem in this format (same complaint on June 6).
- **Exagger-Eyes is a near-repeat from the June 6 send.** A subscriber who got that email will recognize this as a retread, which hurts both perceived value and the 10% offer's urgency.
- **No single hero CTA.** Six discrete sections each with their own "Shop Now"-style button means zero visual hierarchy — the eye has nowhere to land.
- **No deadline or scarcity language is visible.** The free gift and percentage offers feel permanently available, which reduces urgency to zero.
- **The bridal section is buried mid-email.** The most genuinely new product is not the first thing a reader sees.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Bridal Boxes, not the digest header.** The only genuinely new product deserves hero placement. A single-hero email centered on the bridal launch would outperform this digest on both click and conversion.
- 2. **Kill "So Much More!" from the subject.** Every character it occupies could be specific product copy.
- 3. **Add a deadline to the free gift and percentage offers.** Even "this weekend" or "while stocks last" creates urgency the current email completely lacks.
- 4. **Retire or rest the Exagger-Eyes block.** It ran June 6. Repeating it 7 days later with a modest 10% off signals the brand is stuffing the digest to fill space.
- 5. **Consolidate to 2-3 sections maximum.** Free gift + Bridal launch + one complexion offer would be a tighter, cleaner email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Charlotte's Bridal Beauty Boxes Are Here 💌`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free Gift + New Bridal Boxes — This Weekend Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your something new: three curated bridal looks, ready to gift.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free Mr. Magic gift — no code needed.`
