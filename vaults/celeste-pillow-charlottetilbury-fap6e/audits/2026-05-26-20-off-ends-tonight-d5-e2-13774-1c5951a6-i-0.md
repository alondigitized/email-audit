# 20% OFF Ends Tonight 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- "Last chance" is a credible hook — until you see the countdown sitting at **00 HRS 00 MINS 00 SECS**. The timer has expired (or never loaded), and paired with "Ends Tonight" in the subject, that zero-zero-zero timestamp actively destroys the urgency the send is built around. A subscriber who clocked the same 20% off on May 21, May 22, May 22 again, May 24, and May 25 is already skeptical; showing them a broken clock is the final straw.
- Outside the timer disaster, the bones are solid. Charlotte Tilbury's visual identity is unmistakable — the rose-gold palette, the "Darling" voice, the aspirational model spread all land cleanly. The loyalty block (free Pillow Talk mini-gift for members) is the one element that adds something new to a campaign the list has seen nine times.
- The honest verdict: this email should have been the last send of a well-run 20%-off campaign, but the broken timer turns its strongest asset — deadline urgency — into a liability. A subscriber seeing 00:00:00 is more likely to conclude the sale is *over* than to click.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is clean** — 20% off lands above the fold, the "SHOP WITH 20% OFF" button is visually distinct, and the eye reads top-to-bottom without confusion.
- **Loyalty block adds real value** — "Exclusive to Loyalty Members: Unlock a Free Pillow Talk Mini-Gift" is the one piece of new information this email introduces vs. prior sends; it gives members a reason to click beyond pure discount.
- **"LAST CHANCE, DARLING!" copy is on-brand** — CT's signature voice is consistent and familiar to subscribers; it doesn't feel generic.
- **Visual quality is high** — model imagery, product shots, and rose-gold palette are polished; no broken images or layout breaks visible.

## What's weak

- **Countdown timer reads 00:00:00** — this is the most damaging element; it signals the offer has expired or the send is technically broken, directly contradicting the "Ends Tonight" urgency premise.
- **Ninth 20%-off email in ten days** — every prior send in this window has led with 20% off; this send adds nothing new to the offer itself and the list knows it.
- **No product anchoring** — the hero is sitewide ("everything"), with no single product or category to pull a specific desire; compare this to the May 24 send which productized the AIRbrush Setting Spray and gave subscribers something concrete to want.
- **The "Discover the Magic of charlottetilbury.com + APP" section feels like filler** — it adds length without adding urgency or offer specificity at the moment a subscriber needs one final reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or remove the countdown timer before send** — a live countdown that reaches zero mid-send period is worse than no countdown at all. Either suppress it once it hits zero or replace it with static "ends midnight tonight" copy.
- 2. **Hero one specific product as the "final chance" object** — pick the top-selling item from this 20%-off period (likely the Flawless Filter or a Pillow Talk SKU) and make the hero about *that*, not sitewide. Specificity is what this cadence has been missing.
- 3. **Elevate the loyalty gift above the fold** — the free Pillow Talk mini-gift is genuinely new information; it belongs in the hero block, not buried below the main offer section.
- 4. **Pull back on frequency after this send** — nine emails in ten days with the same offer will cost the list. A 3–5 day gap with a "thank you for shopping the sale" post-purchase touchpoint would rebuild goodwill.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Pillow Talk gift expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, loyalty members unlock a free Pillow Talk mini — claim it before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything at 20% off — but the offer closes at midnight.`

Apple Mail and some webmail clients render the `<title>` in the window/tab label. An empty value is a minor but solvable gap — should contain the subject line or campaign name.

**External font `<link>` stripped by Gmail:**

Gmail strips all `<link>` tags from `<head>`. The conditional comment `<!--[if !mso]><!-->...<![endif]-->` correctly excludes this from Outlook, but Gmail fallback font behavior is uncontrolled. ### 5. Compliance

**`noreply@` sender address:**
```

```
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) requires the From address be a valid address recipients can reply to for opt-out requests. A `noreply@` address does not meet this requirement on its own — it's compliant only if a functional reply-to header or a clear unsubscribe mechanism compensates. Cannot verify the reply-to header or footer from the truncated source.

**Unsubscribe link and physical mailing address** — both are required by CAN-SPAM and GDPR and are typically in the footer. The source is truncated before the footer (row-29 is referenced in CSS but not present in the provided HTML). These must be verified in the full source. If either is missing, this is a hard compliance failure.

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) cannot be assessed from HTML — requires raw email headers. Braze sends from `mail.charlottetilbury.com`; DKIM signing and DMARC alignment for that subdomain should be confirmed in mail headers.
