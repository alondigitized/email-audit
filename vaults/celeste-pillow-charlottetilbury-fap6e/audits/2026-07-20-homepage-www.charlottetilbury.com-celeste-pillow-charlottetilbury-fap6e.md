# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Oh, this is Charlotte doing what Charlotte does — wrapping a sale in so much glamour you forget you're being sold to. The SUMMER SALE hero is warm, saturated, unmistakably CT with those golden compacts and Pillow Talk-coded lip products front and center. Right now they're pushing urgency — the whole above-the-fold is one big ticking clock pointing at 50% off.

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** ("00 DAYS 18 HRS 55 MINS 11 SECS") is genuinely effective — paired with "ENDS TONIGHT, DARLING!" it creates real urgency without feeling cheap
- **"ENDS TONIGHT, DARLING!"** — the brand voice is doing heavy lifting; that "darling" is so distinctly CT it makes me feel like I'm already in the relationship
- **Product imagery in the hero** — I can see what looks like a CT compact, Pillow Talk-coded lip products, and that iconic gold packaging; this isn't generic beauty, it's MY beauty
- **The warm sunset gradient** behind the products is lush and premium — it reads as gift-worthy, which is how I think about CT
- **The "NEW! Ask" AI feature badge** on the search bar — as a high-tech-comfort user I notice this and it's a genuine differentiator that makes me trust the shopping experience
- **The search bar placeholder** "Search product, shade, col..." — the "shade" and "col[or]" shorthand tells me this is built for someone who shops by product knowledge, not by browsing

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — Charlotte Tilbury has a Beauty Rewards program and I have points. There's zero acknowledgment that I'm a returning customer or that this sale might stack with my rewards
- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — 50% off what? All categories? Pillow Talk specifically? Complexion? The CTA doesn't tell me where I'll land, and for a sale this broad that matters
- **No free-shipping info** — the "FREE GIFTS AS YOU SHOP" has a minimum spend catch in tiny print, but I don't know the threshold. That asterisk-chain (**) makes me slightly suspicious before I even tap
- **No model or person in the hero** — it's a beautiful product flatlay but there's no aspiration anchor, no "this is who wears this" — for a brand that's built on Charlotte's own glamour persona, that's a miss at the highest-stakes moment
- **"While stocks last"** under the free gifts — this is slightly unsettling; if I wait two hours before buying, are the free gifts gone? It adds vague anxiety rather than clean urgency

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty badge to the top banner** — "Rewards members: redeem points during Summer Sale" takes one line and turns a generic sale into a reason for returning customers to act now rather than later
- **Split the CTA by bestseller category** — below "SHOP NOW," add two secondary pills: "Shop Lips" and "Shop Complexion" — lets someone like me skip the browse and go straight to Pillow Talk or Magic Cream without wading through the full sale
- **Replace the product flatlay with a lifestyle shot** — a woman (my age range, my style) actually wearing the Pillow Talk lip and the glowy complexion, in a warm summer setting; the products are beautiful but they don't make me feel anything the way a face does
- **Surface the free gift minimum spend threshold** prominently — "Free gift with £40+ spend" right under "FREE GIFTS AS YOU SHOP" removes the asterisk anxiety and actually motivates me to hit the threshold
