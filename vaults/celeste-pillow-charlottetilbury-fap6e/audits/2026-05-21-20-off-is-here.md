# 20% Off Is HERE!  ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury turns the dial up from the 15%-off welcome offer on May 16 to a sitewide 20%-off blast today — the fourth email in six days. The offer is their strongest yet in this sequence, and the layered loyalty incentive (free Collagen Lip Bath with $115+ spend) gives members a genuine reason to push their cart higher. The hierarchy is clean: 20% hero dominates, loyalty section sits mid-email, app discovery closes it out.
- The weakness is cadence and the subject line. Four emails in six days makes this feel like fatigue-induced escalation rather than a special moment. "20% Off Is HERE!" is direct but low-intrigue — no credible deadline, no Memorial Day hook despite landing on the start of the holiday weekend, and "EVERYTHING!" followed by visible fine print at the bottom is the brand's recurring honesty risk.
- Overall: a structurally solid discount email — but over-messaged this week and missing the most obvious urgency peg available on this exact date.

## What's working

- **20% hero is immediately dominant** — pink type, gold sparkle background, and CTA button lock the eye on the offer within a second.
- **Loyalty exclusive mid-section adds spend-up mechanics** — free Collagen Lip Bath in Pillow Talk hue for $115+ is a visible, specific loyalty reward, not a vague "member perk."
- **CTA copy is specific and action-oriented** — "SHOP WITH 20% OFF" is a verb + value, not "Explore" or "Discover."
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, no overlapping text visible in the render.
- **Brand voice holds** — Charlotte's signature, "darling" editorial tone, and the glamour aesthetic are all consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- **No Memorial Day anchor** — sending on May 21 (Memorial Day weekend) with no seasonal callout wastes the most credible urgency peg available; a deadline tied to Monday would have been effortless.
- **"EVERYTHING!" overpromises** — lengthy fine print is visible at the bottom; exclusions almost certainly make "everything" misleading, which erodes trust on repeat exposure.
- **Cadence is exhausting** — this is email #4 from Charlotte Tilbury in six days; the "special offer" framing is actively diluted by the May 18 GWP and May 16 15%-off sends that came before it.
- **No end date visible anywhere** — "for a limited time only" in the body reads as boilerplate, not real urgency, without a specific date.
- **Preheader almost certainly leaks** — no dedicated preheader text is visible; inbox clients will surface "20% OFF EVERYTHING!" from the hero, repeating the subject verbatim.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a Memorial Day deadline explicitly** — "20% off everything, through Monday May 26" anchors urgency to a real moment and justifies why this email is different from the May 18 send.
- 2. **Acknowledge the escalation from 15% → 20%** — one line like "We upgraded your welcome offer — this is 20%, sitewide" rewards engaged subscribers and justifies the inbox hit.
- 3. **Replace "EVERYTHING!" with an honest scope** — "20% Off Hundreds of Products" or "20% Off Almost Everything" actually reads as more trustworthy and reduces fine-print blowback.
- 4. **Surface a deadline on the loyalty GWP section** — "Spend $115+ by May 26" motivates the spend-up without requiring the reader to hunt for terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% Off — This Weekend Only, Darling`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We Upgraded You: 20% Off Sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Loyalty members also unlock a free Collagen Lip Bath with $115+ — ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off ends May 26 · plus your free member gift with $115`
