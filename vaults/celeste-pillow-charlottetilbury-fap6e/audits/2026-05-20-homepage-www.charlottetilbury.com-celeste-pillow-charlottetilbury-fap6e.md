# Homepage snapshot · www.charlottetilbury.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the moment I land on this page I immediately know what Charlotte wants me to do: buy the Blur Concealer that they're clearly relaunching. The "IT'S BACK!" framing, the stunning product flat lay, the warm beige gold palette — this is peak Charlotte Tilbury visual identity, no confusion about that. It's a single-minded concealer push, which honestly works for me because I've been watching this one since it dropped.

## What's working

- **"IT'S BACK!" header** — scarcity framing on a returning product is smart; if you missed it before, this creates urgency without a fake countdown
- **Product flat lay photography** — the spread of shades against that warm beige background is gorgeous; I stopped scrolling the moment this loaded
- **The spend-$95 GWP banner** — Mini Magic Gifts is a classic CT hook and $95 is exactly my impulse threshold at checkout
- **"Full Coverage With Elastic Stretch"** — this benefit copy is the right level of specific; I know exactly what problem this solves (under-eye creping, movement) and it reads like Charlotte actually wants me to understand the product
- **Search bar with "shade, colour" placeholder** — surfacing shade-matching directly in the search UI is smart for a foundation/concealer push

## What's weak

- **No human in the hero** — I'm 38 and I want to see how this sits on skin that looks like mine, not just a lineup of tubes. The product flat lay is beautiful but it doesn't show me how the finish looks on a real face
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — on a page shouting "IT'S BACK!" the button could be "Shop the Concealer" or "Find Your Shade." "Shop Now" could be from any brand on the internet
- **No loyalty callout** — I have a Charlotte Tilbury account and I know they have some kind of rewards/loyalty structure. Not a whisper of it above the fold feels like leaving money on the table for a repeat buyer like me
- **No shade count or shade-finder prompt** — concealer is an intimidating buy; even a "26 shades" badge or a "Find Your Match" link would dramatically lower the friction from consideration to cart
- **No urgency signal tied to the relaunch** — "IT'S BACK!" implies it went away before, but I have no idea how long this window lasts. A "limited relaunch" or "while stocks last" line would make me move faster

## Recommendations

- **Add a face to the hero** — swap or layer in a tight lifestyle crop of the concealer on real, 35–45 year-old skin; show the finish, not just the packaging
- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Find Your Shade"** — shade anxiety is the number-one drop-off point for complexion, and a more specific CTA doubles as a nudge toward the shade finder
- **Add a relaunch scarcity line** — something like "Back for a limited time — 26 shades, while stocks last" directly under "IT'S BACK!" would turn curiosity into urgency
- **Surface the loyalty / GWP hook more prominently** — the Mini Magic Gifts banner is great but easy to skim; repeat buyers should see a member-specific prompt ("You're $X away from a Mini Magic Gift") to make the return visit feel rewarded
