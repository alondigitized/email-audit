# Early Access To SALE Ends Soon! 🧡✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Charlotte Tilbury's fourth email in three days as the summer sale clock winds down, and the creative team has layered a GWP ("Golden Goddess Glow — 3 Free Full-Size Gifts") on top of the 45% off Iconic Kits in the app that's been running since June 22. The result is an email with two competing primary offers: the tiered gifting mechanic (spend $118/$160/$270 to unlock 1/2/3 gifts) gets the hero slot, while the 45% off that drove the last two sends is now a secondary section below the fold. That hierarchy inversion will confuse subscribers who have been trained on "45% off" as the campaign anchor.
- The Golden Goddess Glow GWP hero is genuinely arresting — a warm, close-up golden-skin model shot with good product staging — but "Ends Soon" in both the subject and preheader is repeated vagueness. Seven emails in eight days from this brand is aggressive cadence, and the lack of a hard deadline on "Early Access ends soon" makes the urgency feel manufactured rather than credible.
- The email renders cleanly and the spend-threshold tiers ($118 / $160 / $270) are readable. But it's doing too much: GWP tiers + 45% off + app download push = three asks, one send. The June 22 "Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW!" was sharper precisely because it committed to one offer.

## What's working

- **GWP hero is strong**: "3 Free Full-Size Gifts" with a golden-skin close-up is a visually arresting entry point.
- **Spend tiers are legible**: $118 / $160 / $270 thresholds are clearly labeled with distinct product shots and individual CTAs per tier.
- **Brand palette is consistent**: The warm peach/coral/orange tones are on-model for Charlotte Tilbury and signal luxury without screaming discount.
- **No render bugs**: Clean layout, no overlapping text, images load correctly throughout.

## What's weak

- **Two competing primary offers**: GWP hero + 45% off below the fold means neither owns the email. The June 23 send had the same problem.
- **"Ends Soon" is empty urgency**: No hard deadline visible anywhere in the render — the sale end date is never stated.
- **45% off is app-only again**: Subscribers who prefer desktop are asked to download the app to redeem the biggest discount, adding friction.
- **Three separate asks in one email**: Tier gifts, 45% app discount, and an app-feature explainer column are each pulling the reader in a different direction.
- **Cadence fatigue**: This is the seventh CT email in eight days; "early access ends soon" was also the framing on June 23, making this feel like a near-duplicate push.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one offer and own it.** If the GWP is the lead, cut the 45% off section or demote it to a single line in the footer. If 45% off is the lead, put it in the hero and let GWP be the "sweetener" callout.
- 2. **Replace "Ends Soon" with the actual deadline.** "Sale ends Sunday midnight" or "Gifts valid through June 25" changes perceived credibility entirely.
- 3. **Collapse the app push into a single sentence.** The three-column "Discover the Magic" feature grid mid-email is a distraction; move it to the footer or cut it.
- 4. **Surface the gift tier value.** Saying "3 FREE FULL-SIZE GIFTS" is good; saying "3 Full-Size Gifts — £120 Value Free" is compelling. The financial value of the free gifts is never shown.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend £118 and pick your first gift — 3 tiers, 3 looks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free full-size gifts with your summer picks — offer ends tonight.`
