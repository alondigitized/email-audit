## What's working

- **The loyalty banner ("Loyalty-Exclusive Gift! Unlock A Free Luxury Palette In Pillow Talk Dreams When You Spend $170. T&Cs Apply.")** — this is surgical. It surfaces immediately, names the exact reward, gives the exact spend trigger, and it's Pillow Talk. The offer could not be better aimed at me if it had my name on it.
- **The hero eye shot** — tight close-up of a warm bronze/copper smoky eye being applied with fingers is stunning. It communicates the product's payoff without a word. That shade is exactly what I'd put on the inside of my lids on a Saturday.
- **"12 Universally-Flattering Shades!" badge** — inclusive copy that removes the "but will it work on my skin tone?" hesitation. Smart for a new format from a brand that could easily default to aspirational-only messaging.
- **"NEW!" label** — for regulars like me, this flag is a specific piece of information: it's not something already in my drawer. I need to look.
- **Clean nav with search bar** — I know the brand, I know what I want, and the search field is right there front and center.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA** — the most generic button in beauty retail. For a brand as specific and editorial as Charlotte Tilbury, this feels like a placeholder. "Shop Exagger-Eyes" or "Try 12 Shades" would have 10x the pull.
- **Tight eye crop on the hero** — beautiful, but context-free. I can't see whether this pairs with a Pillow Talk lip or a bold red. I can't see the full look, the skin, the vibe. A little runway-moment framing would sell this harder.
- **Zero trust signals in view** — no review count on the new product, no star rating, no "free returns" badge. When I'm deciding whether to try a new format (sticks, not pans), I want to see that someone else already loved it.
- **No free-shipping info visible** — I can see the spend threshold for the loyalty gift, but I don't know when shipping gets free. For a brand whose loyal customers often stack purchases to hit thresholds, that number matters.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Exagger-Eyes"** — one-word change, immediately more specific. It costs nothing to ship and would meaningfully improve click intent.
- **Add a shade cross-sell note under the badge**: "Pairs beautifully with Pillow Talk lips →" — tie the new eye product back to the lip product I already own, close the cross-sell loop while I'm already looking.
- **Add a micro trust bar under the CTA**: something like "★ 4.8 from 1,200 reviews · Free returns" — kills hesitation on a brand-new product format without cluttering the hero.
- **Loosen the crop on the hero** — give me a half-face shot showing the full look, including the lip. Keep the bronze flame energy but let me see the whole picture, not just the lid.

## 3. What's Working

- **The loyalty banner ("Loyalty-Exclusive Gift! Unlock A Free Luxury Palette In Pillow Talk Dreams When You Spend $170. T&Cs Apply.")** — this is surgical. It surfaces immediately, names the exact reward, gives the exact spend trigger, and it's Pillow Talk. The offer could not be better aimed at me if it had my name on it.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Add a shade cross-sell note under the badge**: "Pairs beautifully with Pillow Talk lips →" — tie the new eye product back to the lip product I already own, close the cross-sell loop while I'm already looking.
- **Add a micro trust bar under the CTA**: something like "★ 4.8 from 1,200 reviews · Free returns" — kills hesitation on a brand-new product format without cluttering the hero.
- **Loosen the crop on the hero** — give me a half-face shot showing the full look, including the lip. Keep the bronze flame energy but let me see the whole picture, not just the lid.

## 6. Bottom Line

I'm already tapping "Shop Now" — that Pillow Talk Dreams offer closed me before I even processed the new product.
