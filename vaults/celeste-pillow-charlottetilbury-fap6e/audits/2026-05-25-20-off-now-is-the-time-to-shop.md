# 20% OFF!! Now Is The Time To Shop! 🛍️✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury's eighth 20%-off email in ten days. The hero pivots once more — this time to "Trending Beauty Secrets" — but the underlying offer hasn't budged since May 21. The creative is well-organized: bold hero percentage, a clean grid of six trending products with "Shop Now" rails, a loyalty unlock ($85 Collagen Lip Bath gift at $115+), and the usual app/loyalty footer reinforcement.
- The problem is sequencing, not craft. As a subscriber I've seen this same 20% banner four times this week with different hero framings (sitewide → best-sellers → setting spray → trending secrets). It's starting to read as inventory rotation rather than fresh news. The "Trending Beauty Secrets" angle is a soft frame — it doesn't actually tell me what's trending or why I should care today vs. yesterday.
- Net: a competent template send that would have scored higher in isolation, but cadence-fatigue is dragging this down. Mid-pack.

## What's working

- "20% OFF" stamped enormous in the hero — no ambiguity about the offer.
- Six-product grid gives concrete entry points (Setting Spray, Powder, Lip Cheat, Lipstick, Flawless Foundation, Healthy Glow) rather than a vague homepage push.
- Loyalty unlock is clearly framed — free $85 Collagen Lip Bath at $115+ spend, with a "Get Your Free Gift" CTA.
- Footer reinforces the four pillars subscribers respond to: free shipping, free returns, magical savings, exclusive launches.
- "Discover the magic of CharlotteTilbury.com + App" badge is on-brand and reinforces app install.

## What's weak

- Eighth email in ten days with the same 20% offer — fatigue is now the dominant signal.
- "Trending Beauty Secrets" hero is filler — it doesn't say *what* is trending or *why now*.
- Subject double-exclamation "20% OFF!!" tips toward spam-shouty, especially after a week of identical promos.
- Product grid lacks any badge or callout for "best-seller," "new," "low-stock" — every tile reads identically.
- No countdown or end-date visible — the urgency the subject claims ("Now Is The Time") is not reinforced in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a credible deadline.** A visible "Ends Tuesday 11:59pm" countdown is the single biggest miss — the subject promises urgency, the body delivers none.
- 2. **Differentiate this send from the prior four.** Anchor it to a single editorial idea (e.g., "Pillow Talk Week" or "Charlotte's 5 Most-Saved") instead of yet another grid.
- 3. **Tag the product grid.** "Best-Seller," "New," "Almost Sold Out" labels give the eye a reason to land on a specific tile.
- 4. **Cool the cadence.** Skip a day or drop to a non-promo (tutorial, founder note) to reset the inbox.
