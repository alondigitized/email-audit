# 20% Off! Including AIRbrush Flawless Setting Spray 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Charlotte Tilbury's seventh email in nine days, and the fatigue is starting to show. The 20% off campaign continues but now narrows its hero to a single product — AIRbrush Flawless Setting Spray — while keeping the sitewide offer available below. It's a smart pivot to keep the discount fresh, but it lands as yet another red-burgundy block in an inbox that already has five of them this week.
- The hero treatment is clean: oversized "20% OFF" headline, the spray bottle photographed in two angles, and a clinical claim ("up to 16 hours") to justify the buy. The loyalty GWP — a free Collagen Lip Bath in Pillow Talk when you spend $115 — is the strongest hook of the email and it's buried in the middle.
- Judgement: solid execution, derivative within the brand's own week. A standalone 7; downgraded one point for cadence exhaustion against the May 21 / May 22 / May 22 / May 22 series.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("20% OFF AIRBRUSH FLAWLESS SETTING SPRAY") is unmissable above the fold.
- Two clear CTAs split the offer: shop the spray vs. shop the sitewide 20%.
- The loyalty GWP module (free Collagen Lip Bath, worth $30) is a genuine member benefit.
- Product photography is on-brand — pink-toned, hero shots well-lit, packaging legible.
- "Discover the magic of charlottetilbury.com + APP" four-tile module reinforces brand utility.

## What's weak

- Subject line repeats "20% Off" — the fifth consecutive email leading with this exact phrasing.
- The clinical "up to 16 hours" claim is buried in tiny text under the hero, not in the headline.
- Burgundy-on-burgundy CTA buttons have low contrast; "Shop 20% Setting Spray" almost disappears.
- Heavy fine-print block at the bottom (exclusions, loyalty terms) signals a complicated offer.
- No price anchor — what does the spray cost before/after 20%? The shopper has to do the math.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the product hero, not the discount.** AIRbrush is the actual differentiator; "20% off" is week-five wallpaper. Try "Your 16-hour setting spray, now 20% off."
- 2. **Surface a price anchor in the hero** — "$32 (was $40)" makes the offer concrete instead of abstract.
- 3. **Pull the free Collagen Lip Bath GWP higher** — that $30 freebie is a stronger pull than another setting-spray banner.
- 4. **Increase button contrast** — white pill buttons on burgundy, or a darker outline. Current pills blur into the field.
- 5. **Cool the cadence for two days** — this is the seventh hit in nine days; the next one will feel like spam by default.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 16-hour setting spray — 20% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `AIRbrush Flawless: 20% off + a free Pillow Talk gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The clinically-proven waterproof finish, now $8 off. Plus a free lip gift at $115.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Setting spray that lasts 16 hours — 20% off through Sunday.`
