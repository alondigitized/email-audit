# Early Access: Up To 45% Off Beauty Kits In The App! 🤩💫
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a dual-purpose send: push the same 45% summer sale as yesterday's email (June 22, "Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW!") but route everything through the app with an "Early Access" wrapper. The tension is immediately apparent — the sale publicly launched yesterday, so the "Early Access" framing on June 23 reads as marketing spin rather than genuine exclusivity. Subscribers who opened yesterday's email know the sale is already live on the site; calling today's app version "Early Access" can erode trust.
- That said, the email itself is well-constructed. The hero is clean, the 45% anchor is visible immediately, the GWP tier (3 Free Full-Size Gifts) adds genuine layered value, and the coral palette is on-brand and seasonally coherent. The product grid gives real browsable inventory rather than a vague "shop the sale" gesture.
- The single biggest drag here is cadence fatigue. This is the seventh Charlotte Tilbury email audited in the last eight days, and the third in a row leaning on the app-download push (June 16, June 22, now June 23). Even a loyal subscriber is feeling volume pressure, and "Early Access" on a sale that's already started is unlikely to overcome it.

## What's working

- **45% hero is immediately legible** — the number anchors the eye in the first screen-height, with no visual competition.
- **GWP tier graphic earns its space** — "3 Free Full-Size Gifts" with a stacked 1/2/3 gift unlock is a genuine value-add that the June 22 email didn't lead with.
- **Product grid with named SKUs** — four specific products with "Shop Now" CTAs rather than a generic "explore the collection" blob.
- **Consistent campaign identity** — "TAKE IT ALL OFF! / POWER YOUR GOLDEN GODDESS GLOW" locks into the UNREAL summer visual system cleanly; warm coral palette is coherent end to end.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, buttons are visible.

## What's weak

- **"Early Access" is undercut by the timeline** — the June 22 send already declared the 45% sale live; framing the next day as "Early Access" is a credibility gap a loyal subscriber will notice.
- **App-gate is genuine friction** — every single CTA routes to "SHOP ON THE APP," which requires an install and login step that the website doesn't. Conversion will leak here.
- **Discover The Magic Of The App section is filler** — this same app-features block (video tutorials, personalized recommendations, in-brush try-on) has appeared in at least three of the last six sends. It no longer earns its vertical space.
- **No visible deadline or time anchor** — "Early Access" implies urgency but there's no date, countdown, or "ends Sunday" in the hero to make it credible.
- **Subject line is over 50 chars** — "Early Access: Up To 45% Off Beauty Kits In The App! 🤩💫" runs ~56 characters before emojis; likely truncated on most mobile inboxes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Either genuinely gate the app or drop "Early Access"** — if the sale is already live on the site, rename this "App Exclusive: Extra 5% Off Kits" or similar to give the app-click a real reason to exist. "Early Access" for something that launched yesterday is the highest-trust risk in this email.
- 2. **Add an expiry to the hero** — "App Early Access ends midnight Sunday" or a visible countdown turns a vague urgency claim into a decision driver.
- 3. **Retire the Discover The Magic Of The App module** — replace it with one editorial GWP explanation ("Spend £X, unlock Gift 1 → 2 → 3") since the tier graphic needs a sentence to be truly scannable.
- 4. **Trim the subject to under 50 chars** — strip the emojis or shorten the body copy to avoid mobile truncation.
- 5. **Surface one hero kit by name** — the subject says "Beauty Kits" plural; naming the marquee kit (e.g., "Pillow Talk Icon Kit") in the hero would sharpen desire over the current generic "ICONIC KITS" label.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App Early Access: 45% Off Iconic Kits — Ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kit Sale: Up To 45% Off + 3 Free Gifts In The App`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your golden summer kit — Beauty Kits up to 45% off, plus unlock up to 3 free full-size gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `App-exclusive: 45% off Pillow Talk, Glowing Body & more kits. 3 free gifts waiting.`
