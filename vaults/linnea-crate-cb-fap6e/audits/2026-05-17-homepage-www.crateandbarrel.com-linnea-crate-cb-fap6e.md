# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is clearly a Memorial Day push, and Crate & Barrel is leaning hard on their Crate Card rewards angle — "earn 20% back" is front and center in the top bar AND the hero. As someone who just registered here, the outdoor furniture focus in the hero doesn't immediately speak to me (I'm here for walnut dining tables and textured stoneware, not a coastal sofa set), but the bones are right — clean photography, aspirational lifestyle, and a rewards story that actually makes me want to apply for the card. The Breville Barista Touch showing up in the product grid? That's the first thing that made me stop scrolling.

## What's working

- **Crate Card Double Rewards story** — the "earn 20% back" hook is everywhere and it genuinely moves me. I'm full-price tolerant, but earning $500 in rewards on furniture I was already planning to buy? That's math I can do.
- **"Shop Kitchen & Entertaining" tab** just below the hero — even though the hero is outdoor furniture, this tab is right there and one tap to my actual category.
- **Breville Barista Touch in the rewards grid** — an espresso machine surfacing in a Memorial Day sale feels exactly right for someone setting up a kitchen. That product earns attention fast.
- **Photography quality** — the coastal outdoor scene is beautiful. The lifestyle shots in the "bestselling furniture" section below feel warm and livable, not sterile.
- **"Up to 30% off bestselling furniture"** lower section with soft, bright room imagery — this feels closer to my aesthetic and gives me a browsable entry point.

## What's weak

- **Hero category mismatch for me** — I'm here for registry: stoneware, walnut furniture, kitchen. The hero is a coastal outdoor sofa scene. That's a conversion miss on the first thing I see.
- **Four CTAs in the hero block** — "Shop Outdoor Furniture," "Shop Sofa Collections," "Shop Memorial Day Event," AND "Apply Now" compete simultaneously. I don't know where to look first.
- **No registry integration above the fold** — "Wedding Registry" is in the secondary nav but there's no contextual hook for someone in active registry mode. A first-time registrant returning to shop off their own list gets zero personalization.
- **The rewards math is slightly confusing** — "$475 $500 in Reward Dollars" crossed out next to each other reads as a deal, but I had to look twice to understand what was being struck through and why.
- **No trust signals at surface level** — no review counts, no "free returns," no "free shipping over $X" visible above the fold. For someone dropping $800 on a sofa, these matter.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a registry/home-setup lifestyle shot** during peak wedding season (May–June) — a beautifully set dining table with walnut and stoneware would convert newly registered customers like me far harder than an outdoor sofa.
- **Collapse the hero to one primary CTA** — "Shop the Memorial Day Sale" with a secondary link ("or apply for the Crate Card") would stop the decision paralysis from four competing buttons.
- **Add a persistent "Free shipping on orders over $X" line** in the promo strip — it's the one piece of conversion info I'm actively looking for and it's completely absent.
- **Surface a "Your Registry" or "Shop Your List" module** in the secondary nav bar for logged-in registrants — a returning registrant should be one tap from their own registry, not hunting through menus.
