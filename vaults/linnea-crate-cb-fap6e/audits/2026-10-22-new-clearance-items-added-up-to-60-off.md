# NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance send whose body doesn't back up its subject. The subject promises "NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!", but the hero is an autumn-leaves photo with "up to 60% off clearance" in white type over busy foliage. No discounted product appears above the fold. The mustard category band below it helps, with links to Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting and a 40–60% off Final Sale link. But those are plain text links, not products.
- After the hero, the email turns into a general-purpose catch-all. It has free shipping on toys over $99 and a TheDesignDesk services block. Below those come a Halloween shop banner, a four-item "we love these for you" grid, a Crate Card promo and an app download block at the bottom with a "chance at $500" teaser, then a second TheDesignDesk block. Only the Halloween treat bag shows a markdown ($34.95 to $19.97). The strollers, glider and dresser show full price. A reader who clicked for "new clearance" finds one marked-down item. It's a competent template send, but not a sharp one.
- Cadence is also a problem. This is at least the sixth send in three days (Oct 20–22), and it repeats the TheDesignDesk pitch from the Oct 21 "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic" email. Compared with the Oct 21 "ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!" send, which had real product and a real deadline, this one is weaker.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off" is a concrete, scannable offer, and it appears in both the subject and the hero.
- The single "Shop Clearance" button is clear and sits right under the headline.
- The six-link category band (Furniture, Rugs, Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) gives people a quick way to jump to what they want.
- The Halloween treat bag shows a strike-through price ($34.95 to $19.97), which is the only real proof of savings in the email.
- The Halloween shop banner fits the Oct 22 calendar.

## What's weak

- No clearance product is visible above the fold. The leaf photo is atmosphere, not merchandise, and the white headline competes with the foliage.
- "Selling fast!" is a small tagline with no deadline or stock signal, so the urgency isn't believable.
- The "we love these for you" grid mostly shows full-price strollers, a glider and a dresser. That contradicts the clearance promise.
- Too many competing blocks: free toy shipping, two TheDesignDesk blocks, Halloween, the Crate Card, and the app. No single offer wins.
- The long legal footer is dense, and the "up to 60%" disclaimers are buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the leaf hero with a 3–4 product clearance grid showing was/now prices and "% off" badges. This is the biggest lift, because the subject's promise would be visible at once.
- 2. Cut the second TheDesignDesk block and the Crate Card promo. Reduce the email to one story: clearance.
- 3. Make the "we love these for you" grid all clearance items, each with a markdown price and a "Shop now" link.
- 4. Use a darker overlay or a solid band behind the hero headline so it reads at a glance.
- 5. Add a real deadline or inventory cue, such as "While supplies last, ends Sunday," instead of "Selling fast!".
- 6. Reduce send frequency or vary the creative. Three or more near-identical promos in a day trains people to ignore the sender.

    - the hero `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FHERO.gif`

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the product and hero images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. This is the main confirmed defect.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. **Escape the title:** Change `<title>` to `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. **Unsubscribe headers:** Check the raw message source or the sending platform for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture rather than reporting a sender issue.
5. **Authentication:** Read SPF, DKIM and DMARC results from the original delivery headers, not the relay copy.
6. **Redirect check:** Resolve a sample of the 71 tracking links with a redirect-following check to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
7. **Source capture:** Capture the full HTML, so the footer, unsubscribe link and merge tokens can be audited.
8. **Duplicate pixels (sender side):** Consider consolidating the five identical `rlcdn` beacons and removing the leftover template comment.
