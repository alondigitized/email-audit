# STARTS NOW: Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a mixed-message send. The subject promises free shipping on $99+, but the hero is "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" with a sofa photo. Free shipping gets its own block only below the fold, under "starts today: free shipping on orders over $99!*". A reader who opens for shipping lands on a furniture sale. A reader who came for sofas wonders why the subject said shipping.
- The rest is a standard Crate & Barrel module stack: a rugs block, a Caraway coffee maker preorder, the sofa offer again, a glassware grid, a rewards card, an app download and a Design Desk pitch. It renders cleanly with no broken images. But there are about seven competing CTAs and no single story. Sofas appear twice, and the shipping offer is buried behind a 6-link category grid.
- My judgement: clean execution, weak focus. It's the fourth or fifth Crate & Barrel family send in about two days, following the 10/08 clearance and "up to 60% off NEW markdowns" emails. The offer ladder keeps shifting between 30%, 50%, 60%, 20% and now free shipping.

## What's working

- The hero is legible: a large "up to 20% off" headline, a muted lifestyle photo and a white "Shop Now" button.
- The free-shipping block has a clear threshold ($99) and a "Shop All Free Shipping" button.
- The category grid (Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) fits the season.
- The rewards card block adds a loyalty angle, "Get 10% back in Reward Dollars".
- The glassware grid shows prices, which gives a quick sense of value.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with shipping, but the hero leads with sofas, so the two don't match.
- Sofas are promoted twice (hero and mid-email), while the shipping offer sits in the second block.
- The "STARTS NOW" urgency has no end date, so it isn't credible.
- Dense asterisked fine print sits in the footer, and "standard shipping; exclusions apply" under the shipping headline hedges the offer.
- Seven or more CTAs (rugs, Caraway, sofas, rewards card, app, Design Desk, store finder) dilute the click.
- The 10/08 sofa and bedroom sends hit the same themes, so it feels like cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Put free shipping in the hero, with the $99 threshold, an end date and one button. Move sofas to a secondary slot.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules at most: hero offer, one bestseller grid, one loyalty or app block. Drop the duplicate sofa block.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("ends Sunday 10/12") to replace the open-ended "starts now".
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" with a specific button such as "Shop sofas & sectionals" or "Get free shipping".
- 5. Reduce the send frequency or give each send a distinct offer.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup is a standard Marigold/Cheetah-style XHTML table layout with a responsive `@media (max-width:460px)` block. Two problems stand out. About 29 images lack alt text, and the header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed. The QA run passed 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` (LiveRamp, five instances of the same pixel)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/938b7052-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- The five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels differ only by `n=`. This is redundant third-party tracking that adds requests, and it's a privacy consideration. It isn't broken.
- Several `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, and others) carry a per-recipient `mi_u` token. These are dynamically generated and personalized, so they depend on the render server being available.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- The QA run flagged 29 images with no alt text:
  - 12 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...` (for example `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `d8b928f0-...gif`, `3c9b2911-...jpg`).
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` x5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/938b7052-...`).
- The tracking pixels are low priority. The content and dynamic images are the real concern. With images blocked, the email's offer content may not be conveyed, and screen readers get no description.
- `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom. That's an accessibility issue.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. It's minor, but invalid under the XHTML doctype.
- `<meta charset>` is duplicated (`http-equiv` plus `charset`), and `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values. These are harmless but sloppy.
- The CSS has `*{... line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` global resets. Combined with `img {max-width: 600px}` / `640px` at desktop breakpoints, this could affect layout in unexpected ways. I can't confirm any actual breakage from the truncated source.
- The template contains an unresolved authoring placeholder comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It's visible only in source.
- The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the body for `role="presentation"`, preheader text, or `lang`/`dir` on content blocks. `<html lang="en">` is present.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. There are no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are resolved per-recipient values. The only leftover template text is the `IMPUT HERE` comment above.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe is not found.** The QA warning notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. I can't confirm whether the sender omitted it.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for senders above the volume thresholds.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, as expected via the relay. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I couldn't confirm the physical postal address or the visible unsubscribe link.

These three items are relay-capture limitations rather than confirmed sender failures. Treat them as unverified, not as violations.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Unverified. The 59 tracking links were not probed, and the truncated source doesn't expose final destination URLs. I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "Free shipping on orders $99+" offer. Click tracking appears to go through Marigold redirect domains, so any UTM parameters would be applied downstream.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 12 content images and 7 dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta so users can zoom.
3. Consolidate the five repeated `rlcdn.com` pixels into one if they serve the same purpose.
4. Escape the `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;`, and remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags.
5. Delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` placeholder comment from the template.
6. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct inbox copy to confirm compliance. AgentMail's relay is the likely reason they're missing.
7. Follow the redirect chains on the 59 tracking links with a tracker-aware probe to confirm that landing pages and UTM parameters match the free-shipping offer.
