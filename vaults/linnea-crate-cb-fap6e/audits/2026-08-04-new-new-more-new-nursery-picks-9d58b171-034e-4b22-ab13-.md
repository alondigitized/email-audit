# New, new & more new nursery picks →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a real step up from the back-to-school reskins Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel have been running all week — the subject promises "new nursery picks" and the hero delivers exactly that, with a genuine first-look at new dresser, seating, and crib arrivals. Compare that to 2026-08-03's "Going fast: grab school gear at 20% off," which was just yesterday's promo re-skinned with urgency copy; this send at least has distinct, on-topic content behind the subject line.
- The problem is the top banner: "back-to-school sale up to 20% off" is the literal first thing a reader sees, and it has nothing to do with nursery furniture. It's a leftover module from the other campaign bolted onto an otherwise well-organized new-arrivals email, and it muddies what should be an easy, single-minded pitch.
- It's a well-built, on-brand email let down by an unrelated header banner, no visible pricing, and length that will lose scroll-weary readers before they reach the lower CTAs. Solid content, sloppy assembly.

## What's working

- Subject line ("New, new & more new nursery picks") is matched almost word-for-word by the hero headline ("first look: new nursery furniture") — a real, honest connection between promise and payoff.
- Clean categorical structure — seating, cribs, dressers — each with "New Color"/"New Arrival" badges that signal genuinely fresh inventory, not a recycled push.
- Every section has its own clear "Shop X" button, so there's no ambiguity about where to click.
- Consistent sage-green/cream palette and clean product photography throughout — no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- The "we love these for you" grid near the bottom adds a personalized-feeling curated touch.

## What's weak

- The top banner ("back-to-school sale up to 20% off") is completely unrelated to the nursery content below it and steals the first-impression real estate from the actual hero message.
- No pricing or specific discount is shown on any individual product — just two vague, competing offers (top banner "20% off," mid-email "up to 50% off summer sale") that never tie back to the nursery furniture being sold.
- The email is very long — seating, cribs, dressers, a "personalize it" module, a second sale banner, a "we love these" grid, a credit card pitch, and an app pitch — most readers will drop off before reaching the lower CTAs.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email, so there's no reason to act today versus next week.
- CTAs like "Shop All Nursery Furniture" link to category pages, not the specific hero product shown, adding a browse step before purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace or remove the back-to-school banner and put a nursery-specific offer (or none) at the top so the very first thing readers see matches the subject line.
- 2. Add a visible price or % badge directly on the hero product image — "up to 20% off" floating with no anchor product feels abstract.
- 3. Cut the email down — combine seating/cribs/dressers into one scrollable carousel instead of three full sections, and drop either the credit card or app-download block.
- 4. Add a credible deadline to the "up to 50% off summer sale" line (e.g., "ends Sunday") to give a reason to click now.
- 5. Link the hero CTA to the specific featured dresser, not just the category page.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh arrivals in every finish — see what's new before it sells out`
