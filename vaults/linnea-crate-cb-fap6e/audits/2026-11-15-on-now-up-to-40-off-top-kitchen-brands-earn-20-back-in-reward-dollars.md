Each block has its own CTA, so the reader can go straight to the category they care about.

After the kitchen run it turns into a general Early Black Friday and Rewards email, with a Thanksgiving-menu block, an entertaining/furniture/holiday-decor grid, a second Double Rewards banner, an app promo, and a TheDesignDesk pitch. It follows the pattern of the recent Early Black Friday sends (11/13 "up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards", 11/14 "Double Rewards + Up to 50% off"), so it risks reading as one more near-identical promo.

- Each category block has a clear headline offer and its own Shop Now button.
- Named brands (Breville, KitchenAid, Caraway) add credibility and give a reason to click.

- The headline offers are small, plain text under the images, so the percentages are not dominant.
- The subject's "20% back in Reward Dollars" is easy to miss in the body, and I can't read the terms in this render. It could be confusing next to "Double Rewards".
- Several product blocks have tiny caption text overlaid on photos that I can't read at this size.

- 2. Make the percentage-off figures large and bold in each block, and lead the top strip with one clear offer instead of two.
- 3. Reconcile the Rewards language so "20% back" and "Double Rewards" read as one program.
- 4. Add a short "top picks" product row near the top, with prices, so the reader can buy without scrolling.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) layer, and the QA run passed 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The substantive findings are widespread missing image alt text, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and several third-party tracking pixels. The header warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 89 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of the destinations, redirect chains or 404s were validated.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** the Movable Ink pixels (`https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=dbe8da71…`, `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e432d240-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`) carry a recipient-level hash in the `mi_u` parameter. The same hash appears in `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…`.
- **Duplicate third-party pixels:** five LiveRamp pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `n=1` through `n=5`) fire on a single open. This adds third-party requests and a possible privacy and deliverability footprint.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 48 images:** the QA output is truncated, so the true count may be higher. The images fall into these groups:
  - **Content images** (about 41), for example `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/bf3053a9-…jpg` and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images.
  - **Tracking pixels** (`o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`, 5× `rlcdn.com/448796.gif`). These should carry `alt=""`. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or just empty.
  - **Impact:** if the content images are absent and not empty, screen readers will announce filenames or nothing. With images blocked, the email will render as blank space.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed markup:**
  - The doc declares an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, but `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" …>` are not self-closed.
  - The `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags appear three times.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - Most clients tolerate these, but they are template hygiene defects.
- **Template placeholder left in source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, because both match at ≥768px and the later rule wins.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source, and the `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are populated. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the document is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers (unconfirmed):** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing. The QA tool itself attributes these to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. I can't confirm that they are absent in the original message, so I'm not treating them as sender defects. The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` should be checked directly (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) from raw headers.
- **Footer not verified:** the truncated HTML doesn't include the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 89 links are click-tracked redirects that were skipped, so final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible. The sender's own tracking relies on Movable Ink and `mi_*` parameters rather than visible `utm_*` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (offer and product images, the `mi/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Resolve a sample of the 89 tracked links in a browser or with a redirect-following tool, to confirm landing pages and UTMs.
4. Re-run the audit with raw headers (not via the relay) to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
5. Clean up template hygiene: remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, drop the duplicate meta tags, escape `&` in `<title>`, and remove the dead `max-width:600px` rule.
6. Review whether five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels per send are intended.
