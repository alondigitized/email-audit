# Hello, beautiful: Our new Denver store just opened!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a store-opening announcement that quickly turns into a general holiday catalog. The top is the strongest part. It has the Crate & Barrel logo, a hero image of a holiday-set tablescape, and the headline "Hello, beautiful: Our new Denver store just opened!" Two buttons follow, "Shop All Holiday" and "Store Details". The subject and hero match, and the Denver angle gives the send a local reason to exist.
- After that, the email drifts. It runs through garlands and wreaths, ornaments, stockings, décor, a Denver store walk-through video, a DesignDesk appointment, a Thanksgiving dining room event at up to 30% off, a product grid, an app promo ("including your chance at $500") and a second DesignDesk banner. The store news is only the opening beat. A subscriber who isn't near Denver gets a long, undifferentiated holiday catalog.
- My judgement is a pretty email with a good opening and weak focus. The subject promises one story and the body delivers about eight, so the Denver news doesn't get the room it needs. It scores a solid middle, in line with this brand's other recent sends at 6–7/10.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject, with a warm, seasonal tablescape and the "Hello, beautiful" headline.
- "Shop All Holiday" is a clear, visible primary button, and "Store Details" gives a sensible secondary path.
- The holiday modules (garlands, ornaments, stockings) are seasonal and well photographed. Each has its own specific CTA, such as "Shop Garlands & Wreaths".
- The "take a walk through our winter wonderland in Denver" video module adds a distinctive, store-specific element.
- The brand voice and styling stay consistent from top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, about 10,700px tall, and mixes too many messages. Store opening, holiday décor, DesignDesk, the dining event and the app promo all compete.
- There is no single credible, dated offer in the hero. The only percentage, "up to 30% off", is buried mid-email in the Thanksgiving dining event, and no deadline is visible.
- DesignDesk appears twice with near-identical "Book a Free Appointment" CTAs, which is redundant.
- Several image captions and overlay labels, such as the "Cherry Creek Store" tags, are tiny and hard to read at this render size.
- The Denver news doesn't help subscribers outside Colorado, and nothing visible in the email addresses them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the send. Give Denver-area subscribers a focused store-opening email with an event, a grand-opening offer and an address. Send everyone else the holiday catalog under a different subject.
- 2. In the Denver version, put the store address, opening hours and any grand-opening perk above the fold.
- 3. Cut the modules to three or four, for example holiday hero, dining event, DesignDesk once, and a product grid.
- 4. Put one dated offer in the hero, such as the dining event's 30% off with its end date.
- 5. Make caption and overlay text larger and higher contrast for mobile.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found 3 warnings and no hard failures. The recurring problem is missing `alt` attributes on about 30 images, and the HTML source is truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and final status codes are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` is a LiveRamp pixel fired five times, differing only in `n`. The hashed ID in `s=` is the same value as the `mi_u=` parameter on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. That means one subscriber identifier is sent to a third-party domain on open.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c83ed945-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` serve the same purpose. This is normal, but neither has an alt attribute (see section 3).
- **Hero image host.** The hero (`20261027_CB_NSO_CherryCreek_PostOpening…`) loads from `s7d5.scene7.com`, while the rest load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. This is a second image host to keep available.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images.** This is the main confirmed issue.
  - Content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg` and `.gif`) and the Scene7 hero have no alt. With images blocked, the email shows empty boxes, and screen readers read out filenames or URLs.
  - Dynamic recommendation images `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 of them) also have no alt.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. An absent attribute is treated differently from an empty one.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure for low-vision users.
- **Invalid XHTML.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" …>` are unclosed under an XHTML doctype.
  - The `<title>` is the brand name, not the subject.
- **Redundant or conflicting CSS and meta.**
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, as `telephone=no,address=no,…` plus separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is set to both `100%` and `none`.
  - `img {max-width:600px}` at ≥768px is overridden by the later `img {max-width:640px}` at ≥640px, so the 600px rule is dead.
  - Empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }` inside the MSO conditional.
  - Global `p{text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` affect every element.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is shipped in the source, with a typo.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The truncated source means the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **QA warnings, unconfirmed.**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. They are not confirmed defects. Verify against the raw message headers from the original delivery. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **Footer not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated portion of the source, so they are neither confirmed nor flagged.
- **From address.** `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with the image and tracking hosts.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 63 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show final URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity problems were found, but nothing was verified either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to all content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Escape the `&`** in `<title>`, self-close the `<meta>` tags, and set a meaningful title.
4. **Consolidate the `format-detection` tags and the `img` max-width media queries**, and remove the empty CSS rule and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. **Re-run QA with the full raw message**, including headers and the complete HTML, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe link and the address.
6. **Resolve the redirect chains** on the 63 skipped tracking links, then check landing-page status codes and UTMs.
7. **Review the LiveRamp pixel.** Check whether firing five copies is intentional and whether the shared hashed ID is covered by the brand's privacy disclosures.
