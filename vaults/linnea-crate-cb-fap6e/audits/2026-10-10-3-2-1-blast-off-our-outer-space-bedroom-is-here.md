- 2. Cut the email to the theme story, one offer block and the product grid. Drop the sweepstakes, Affirm, card and app modules from this send, or move them to a separate send.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "3, 2, 1…blast off! Our outer-space bedroom is here"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, sent through Marigold/MI (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) with images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Automated QA gave a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The header and authentication results could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **84 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so QA skipped the HTTP check. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Repeated third-party pixel.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fires five times. These are LiveRamp (rlcdn) beacons carrying a 64-character hex `s=` value, probably a hashed recipient ID, though the source doesn't confirm that. Five identical calls add requests and third-party data sharing without apparent benefit.
- **Repeated tracking image.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` appears 6 times.
- **Open pixel and dynamic image.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is the open pixel. `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c3986fc4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a dynamic-content image.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=` value is repeated in every pixel URL. If it is a persistent recipient ID, it travels in URLs that proxies may log.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 48 images have no alt text, per QA.** These include:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, for example `ebaf6d5a-…jpg`, `58e57623-…gif`, `636f6921-…gif` and `790368cc-…gif`.
  - The spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - The tracking pixels listed above.

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the content images have no text equivalent. The animated GIFs also have no text fallback. Pure tracking pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`. Whether those attributes are absent or just empty can't be confirmed from the QA output.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed, and charset is declared twice. `format-detection` is declared four separate times with overlapping values. These are low-impact but add noise.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px) img{max-width:600px}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px) img{max-width:640px}`, because both match at 768px and above. The 600px rule never takes effect.
- **Global `p` styling.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are global resets. They can cause unintended centering and tight line height wherever the template omits inline overrides. This is a risk, not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens, and QA flagged none. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN: `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it.
- **WARN: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **WARN: `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` are unknown.
- **Not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the part of the HTML that was truncated, so they can't be confirmed.

These are verification gaps, not confirmed violations. The relay may be stripping the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 84 tracked links were skipped, so landing URLs, `utm_*` parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked. The `mi_u`/`mi_cid` parameters show the MI click-tracking layer is in use, but the final destinations are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images. Give pixels and spacers `alt=""`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Remove the duplicate `rlcdn` and `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels, keeping one of each.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Dedupe the `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and remove the dead 600px `img` rule.
5. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy, then re-run the compliance checks.
6. Resolve the tracking links through the redirect chain and verify the `utm_*` parameters and landing pages in a follow-up pass.
7. Feed the untruncated HTML to the audit so the unsubscribe link and address can be checked.
