# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is leaning hard into Memorial Day right now — the whole page is basically one giant sale event with a rewards amplifier on top. It reads as a premium furniture brand running a seasonal clearance, which is fine, but the first thing I actually see is a modal asking for my email and phone number before I've even gotten to look around. As someone who came here for modern tabletop and walnut-finish furniture, I'm getting a lot of outdoor patio sectionals and not much else above the fold.

## What's working

- **Rewards amplifier banner** — "Earn 10% 20% Back in Reward Dollars, Ends 5/18/2026" is exactly the hook I want as a new registrant. That double-points window makes me want to act before Monday.
- **"Up to 60% off the memorial day event" strip** — it's bold, it's seasonal, it's in the right moment. Memorial Day furniture shopping is a real thing and this lands.
- **Nav completeness** — "Tabletop & Bar" is right there in the primary nav, one tap to my category. The taxonomy is clean.
- **Visible CTAs below the fold** — the product tile rows ("Arrange Artfully," "Breathe Serene Room") with reward dollar callouts give me editorial + transactional context in one module.
- **"Up to 30% off bestselling furniture" section** — the walnut-forward bedroom/living imagery in that lower rail is much closer to my aesthetic than the outdoor push above it.

## What's weak

- **Email + phone modal firing immediately** — I haven't seen a single product yet and I'm being asked for my mobile number. The "by signing up" fine print includes recurring marketing texts and arbitration language. This is a hard bounce trigger for me.
- **Outdoor furniture dominates the hero** — patio lounge chairs and sectionals are not what I came to C&B's .com for. I want to see walnut dining tables, modern stoneware, the fall registry must-haves. This hero misses my use case entirely.
- **No tabletop or kitchen content above or near the fold** — my primary reason for being on this site isn't visible until I scroll past the modal and the outdoor push.
- **"Up to 60% off" with no qualifying detail** — which categories? All furniture? Tabletop too? The vagueness makes me less excited about the offer.
- **Reward dollar callouts on product tiles are confusing** — "Earn $599 $499 in Reward Dollars*" reads like a price, not a reward. I had to look twice.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the immediate phone-capture modal** — or at minimum trigger it on exit-intent, not on load. Let me see the homepage for 30 seconds first; I'm already a registered user.
- **Rotate the hero to tabletop/dining for non-outdoor segments** — a shot of a walnut table set with modern stoneware + a "Complete your registry" CTA would convert me in one tap. Outdoor can live as a secondary rail.
- **Surface a registry-specific module above the fold** — something like "Your Registry · 12 items · 3 still needed" would be a killer returning-shopper hook for a recently registered Millennial.
- **Clarify which categories qualify for the Memorial Day 60% off** — one line, "including tabletop, bedding & outdoor" would make the offer feel actionable instead of theoretical.
