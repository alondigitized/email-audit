# Toys they’ll love—now up to 30% off for Black Friday!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the toy-focused Black Friday send, and it's well built. The hero says "Black Friday is on! Up to 30% off toys*". Below it are four large, single-product blocks: a ball pit, a wooden train table, an art easel and a play tent. Each has a sale and regular price, a short customer quote and its own "Shop Now" button. Using real products with real quotes is stronger than the category tiles in the omnibus sends.
- It's also the third or fourth Black Friday blast this week. The 11/22 "Black Friday: Up to 30% off toys that top their list!" send used nearly the same hero and offer, and the 11/22 omnibus "Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →" shares the "Black Friday is on" headline. A subscriber has seen this promise already. The bottom half drifts into a generic "up to 50% off" furniture and bedding grid, a stocking promo and a recommendations block. That dilutes the toy message.
- Verdict: a solid, honest, well-organized send that doesn't stand out. The product blocks do the work. The repeated creative and the long tail hold it back.

## What's working

- The hero names the category and the discount ("Up to 30% off toys") and has a single "Shop the Sale" button.
- Each hero product shows sale and regular price, which makes the saving concrete.
- The customer quotes under each product add social proof.
- Each product block has its own "Shop Now" button, so clicks go to specific items.
- The photography is warm and lifestyle-led, and the layout shows no broken images or overlap.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 11/22 toy send's offer and hero framing almost exactly, so it feels like a resend.
- The second half switches to generic "up to 50% off" furniture, bedding and stockings. That undercuts the toy focus.
- The email is very long, and the hero sits small at the top under a shipping banner.
- The asterisk on "30% off toys*" and the "up to" language leave the real discount unclear, and no deadline is visible.
- "Shop the Sale" is generic. The headline "Black Friday is on!" is also recycled from the omnibus send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the five toy products and the one toy CTA. Move the furniture, stockings and recommendations to other sends.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Cyber Monday", to the hero and subject.
- 3. Lead with a different angle from the 11/22 toy send, for example "Gifts under $100" or "Ships by Dec 24 guarantee".
- 4. Replace "Shop the Sale" with "Shop toys up to 30% off".
- 5. Use the best-priced item as the hero product so the offer reads as a deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Toy deals end Monday: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `A wooden train table, 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ball pits, easels and play tents, with holiday delivery still on the table.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved toys, marked down through Cyber Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main issues are about 49 images with no alt text and a stack of third-party tracking pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication headers couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, UTM and merge-token checks are only partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **99 tracking links were skipped.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on these click-redirect URLs, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp beacons.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` are 1x1 pixels and all lack alt text. Their `s=` value starts with the same hash prefix as `mi_u=dbe8da718e29…` on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. That suggests a per-recipient identifier is being passed to a third-party data broker. Confirm this is covered in the privacy disclosure.
- **Other pixels.** There is an open-tracking pixel at `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and another at `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c5494bba-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are image-based, so they won't fire in clients that block images, and neither has alt text.
- **`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`** appears six times with the same `mi_u` value. It may be a real-time personalized image or a pixel. The same asset is requested six times, which adds avoidable requests.
- **Two spacer images are hosted on a different domain.** `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite` and the `40px` variant come from Scene7 rather than `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Both also lack alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 49 images have no alt attribute.** These are 34 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` or `/m/47/` (`.jpg`, `.png` and `.gif`), 2 spacers, 6 `f3c0d22992a01946.png` images and 7 tracking pixels.
  - Alt text is missing on content images such as `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `c1cafb52-714b-4aa2-98ef-fb9aa59481cb.gif`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show as nothing or as filenames.
  - Spacers and pixels should use `alt=""`. Content images need descriptive alt text.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which prevents pinch-zoom and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** There are three `format-detection` metas (one combined, two split) and two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Template placeholder left in.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and the `<title>` is generic.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, and there are MSO/Outlook fallbacks and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- **The truncated HTML limits this check.** Only the head and styles were visible, so the body can't be fully confirmed.
- **Per-recipient IDs.** `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` are populated in the pixel URLs, so the personalization hash does resolve.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** [WARN]. The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. I can't confirm it is actually missing from the sent message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** [WARN]. The same capture caveat applies, but one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** [WARN]. This is expected through the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, which is normal.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** These weren't in the truncated source, so I can't confirm or flag them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM or landing-page data was available. Click URLs are redirect-wrapped and weren't probed, and the QA output contains no UTM findings.
- I can't verify that destination pages match the "up to 30% off" subject line. This needs a resolved-redirect check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text.**
   - Give each of the 34 content images descriptive alt text (product name or offer).
   - Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
   - This clears about 49 of the QA warnings.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Escape the title** (`&amp;`), set a meaningful `<title>`, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. **Check the headers on the raw message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay may be stripping them.
5. **Resolve the 99 redirect links** in a follow-up run to confirm they return 200 and carry the expected UTM or campaign parameters.
6. **Review the LiveRamp pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com`).** Check the third-party identifier sharing against the privacy policy, and consider dropping the repeated `n=1` to `n=5` beacons, since one would do.
7. **Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML** so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and merge tokens can be checked.
