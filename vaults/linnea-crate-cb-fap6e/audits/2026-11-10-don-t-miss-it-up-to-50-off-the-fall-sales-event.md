# Don't miss it: Up to 50% off the Fall Sales Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the Fall Sales Event send, and the offer is clear. A deep red hero says "don't miss out! The Fall Sales Event up to 50% off" with a single "Shop Now" button. Directly above it, a "last Double Rewards this year!" card gives the real deadline, 11/10. The subject does its job: it names the event and the discount, and it matches the hero.
- The body is the problem. After the hero it runs through a 4-tile row (Host & Toast, Last Double Rewards, guest-ready, clearance), an in-stock sofa block, a Gift Shop banner, kitchen deals, a Thanksgiving Hosting Guide, a TheDesignDesk $5,000 sweepstakes, a product grid, a Crate Card promo, an app promo, and a second TheDesignDesk pitch. It is about 10,000px tall, and everything after the first two screens competes for attention.
- This is also the third or fourth Fall Sales send in three days. It follows 11/09's "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards →" and 11/08's "Double Rewards. Up to 50% off. The Fall Sales Event is on!" Only the "Don't miss it" framing and the last-day Double Rewards deadline are new. It's a solid last-day reminder, but it is bloated and repetitive.

## What's working

- The hero is a bold red block with one headline and one "Shop Now" button, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- "Last Double Rewards this year!" with an 11/10 end date is a credible, time-bounded loyalty hook.
- The subject matches the hero, naming the event and "up to 50% off", so there's no mismatch between inbox and open.
- The kitchen section gives concrete pricing ("Sale $149.95 Reg. $199.95", "up to 30% off select All-Clad cookware") and its CTA names a destination, "Shop All Kitchen Deals".
- The top banner ("order by 11/16… in time for Thanksgiving") adds a second, seasonal reason to act.
- I saw no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long, with at least eight distinct content modules, so the 11/10 deadline gets buried.
- The Gift Shop banner, Thanksgiving Hosting Guide, TheDesignDesk sweepstakes (twice) and app promo each pull attention away from the sale.
- Several CTAs are generic: "Shop Now", "Learn More", "Download Now".
- Asterisks on "Double Rewards*" and "30% off*" signal fine print, and a dense legal footer appears at the very bottom.
- It repeats 11/08 and 11/09 creative with little new reason to open again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the four offer tiles, one product row and the footer. Move TheDesignDesk, the app promo and the Gift Shop to a different send.
- 2. Put the deadline in the headline, for example "Ends tonight", so the urgency doesn't depend on a small banner.
- 3. Replace the generic CTAs with specific ones, like "Shop sofas ready in days" or "Shop cookware 30% off".
- 4. Add a real hero product or room scene so the first screen shows what's on sale, not just a text block.
- 5. Suppress or dial down repeat sends to people who were mailed the same event in the last 48 hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 2x Rewards + up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Double Rewards end today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 60% off clearance, plus 20% back on your Crate Card. Today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11/16 and it arrives before Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional build. It has proper `lang="en"` and responsive CSS, but about 28 images lack alt text, the viewport blocks pinch-zoom, and the header-level compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay. The QA run scored 57% pass with 0 errors and 3 warnings, and the source was truncated, so footer and link-level checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking not verified:** 77 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical LiveRamp beacons load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. They are the same pixel repeated with only the `n` counter changing, so they add requests with no extra data.
- **Shared identifier:** the `s=` value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`) begins with the same hash as the Movable Ink `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. The same per-recipient identifier appears to be passed to a third-party domain. Forwarding the email would also expose it in image URLs.
- **Mixed image hosts:** one content image is served from `s7d5.scene7.com` (`2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary…`). The rest come from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`. That adds another domain whose availability and blocking behavior can differ.
- **Stale asset name:** the scene7 path contains `2026_0920` (a September 20 asset) in an email sent in November. This may be a reused module and is worth verifying.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (about 16):**
  - Every `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif` reported by QA, e.g. `6100bbef-…jpg`, `c917a887-…jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-…png` (`/m/47/`), and the animated `70061691-…gif`.
  - The scene7 `Thanksgiving Guide` image.
  - If these are the promotional or hero images, the offer is invisible with images off and to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on Movable Ink dynamic images (7):** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e…`, `789e7381…`, `5ec241ce…`, `00423cc9…`, `f5ca9094…`, `45e8c84f…`, `18f12621…`). These are live-rendered content and need alt text.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `rlcdn` ×5, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8ef2495c…`. These are not real accessibility failures, but they should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on iOS and Android.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `meta charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and HTML5).
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in production.
  - Global `p{text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` rules are broad and can override inline styles in some clients.
- **Dark mode:** the email declares `color-scheme: light` only, so there is no dark-mode handling. That is a deliberate choice rather than a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML or subject. The `mi_u` hash parameters are resolved. The body past the truncation point was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found (WARN). The QA note says this may be a relay capture artifact, so it is **unconfirmed**. Verify against raw headers from the original MTA.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found (WARN). Same caveat. One-click support is required for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent (WARN), which is expected through the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is **unknown**, not failed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not assessable because the source is truncated before the footer. Do not treat these as present or absent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 77 links were skipped by the probe, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, so campaign tagging and landing-page alignment are unverified. The Thanksgiving Guide asset path (`2026_0920`) is the only continuity signal, and it hints at a possible stale module.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the ~16 content images (`image.mail…/m/1/*`, scene7) and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*` dynamic images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Collapse the five `rlcdn` beacons into one, and review whether sharing the `mi_u`-matching hash with LiveRamp fits the privacy policy.
4. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection`, and remove the leftover template placeholder comment.
5. Re-run the audit with full HTML and raw headers, or fetch directly from the sender, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer address and unsubscribe link, UTM parameters, and redirect resolution of the 77 tracked links.
6. Raise the truncation limit in the audit pipeline so footer and compliance checks are not skipped on long emails.
