## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate is driven mostly by about 60 images flagged for missing alt text, plus missing header data that is probably a relay artifact. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 105 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Duplicate tracking pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same URL. It adds requests without adding data.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are 5 pixel requests to rlcdn.com. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed identifier.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a684505b-…` are the open pixel and a view-in-browser or tracking endpoint.
- **Image hosting:** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is a spacer image fetched from a separate CDN host. This adds a DNS lookup and request for no content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 60 `<img>` elements were flagged, mostly under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg` and `e5d44713-…gif`. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, content images give no fallback. The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from a deliberate `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels:** the pixels listed in section 2 should carry `alt=""`. Without it, screen readers may announce the filename.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - There is a duplicate charset declaration (`http-equiv="Content-Type"` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values in the combined and split forms.
  - An unreplaced comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, remains in the source.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. This is harmless in practice but invalid XHTML.
- **Line height:** `*{… line-height:100%}` sets a global 100% line height. It can crush wrapped text in clients that apply `*` rules.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both headers were not found. The QA tool itself notes these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. Raw headers from the original delivery are needed to verify.
- **Footer:** the truncated HTML cut off the visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address. Neither can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No `href` values appear in the truncated source, and the 105 tracking links were not resolved. The wrapped click-tracking domains hide any UTM parameters until redirect, so the QA data confirms neither presence nor absence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, using the `…/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif` URLs flagged. Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels, including the scene7 spacer.
2. Remove the duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel instances and keep one.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Clean up the template: remove the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags and the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and escape the `&` in `<title>`.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw original message, not the relay copy. This would confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracking links, or capture the full HTML, to verify UTM parameters, landing pages and the footer unsubscribe link.
