# The easiest update: new bedding!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure new-arrivals catalog send — no discount, no sale, no urgency anywhere in the entire 12,000px scroll. "Layer a cozy bed," "new quilts & duvets," "new sheet sets," "new & bestselling baby bedding," "bestselling blankets," "make it extra special" (personalization), "new! the snooze sleep collection" — it's section after section of plaid/gingham sheet sets and quilts with only "Ships Free‡" as the value prop. Compare that to Crate & Barrel's 8/13 sibling send, "Up to 25% off bedroom furniture, bedding & more," which led with a specific sale price ($1,899 vs $2,299) — this Crate & Kids send has nothing like that.
- The visuals are genuinely strong: warm, editorial product photography, a coherent plaid/gingham color story (rust, gold, navy, blush), a nice baby-bedding embroidery close-up, and a personalization module showing pillows monogrammed "Noah," "Cameron," "Remi." But strong photography without a hook is a browse email, not a conversion email — this is closer to a lookbook than a promotional push, and at this length (I counted at least 9 full product sections before hitting the app download and "$500" sweepstakes teaser) it asks a lot of scroll for zero stated incentive.
- Judgment: well-produced, on-brand, but commercially weak. This is a "new collection awareness" send, not a click-driver, and should be scored and treated as such.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent hero structure — each section has a colored banner headline, one hero lifestyle image, then a 3-up product grid with "Ships Free‡" labels.
- The personalization section ("make it extra special" with monogrammed "Noah/Cameron/Remi" pillows) is a genuinely differentiated, non-generic hook for a baby/kids brand.
- Photography is warm and cohesive — the plaid/gingham palette (rust, mustard, navy, blush) reads as a real seasonal color story, not stock imagery.
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum across the whole scroll.
- Footer app-download module ("It's all on the app, including your chance at $500") and "TheDesignDesk" free design consultation add legitimate secondary value.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, dollar amount, or % off anywhere in the email — the only tangible perk repeated is "Ships Free," which is table stakes, not an offer.
- Subject line ("The easiest update: new bedding!") promises ease/simplicity but the email itself is a marathon — 9+ stacked sections before you hit any secondary CTA (design consult, app, store locator).
- No urgency signal at all — no "new arrivals" date-stamp, no limited stock, no season cutoff — nothing that creates a reason to act today vs. next week.
- The one real incentive in the whole email — "your chance at $500" — is buried near the very bottom, gated behind an app download, not surfaced anywhere near the top.
- No pricing shown on any individual product tile (quilts, sheet sets, blankets all show name + "Ships Free‡" only) — a shopper can't gauge cost without clicking through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real offer to the hero — even "new arrivals, 15% off your first bedding set" or a specific quilt price point would convert this from browse-mode to buy-mode; right now there's no reason to act now versus bookmarking for later.
- 2. Cut the email by half — 9 near-identical hero+grid sections (quilts, sheet sets, baby bedding, blankets, throw pillows, personalization, sleep collection, app, design desk) is scroll fatigue; consolidate into 3-4 sections max and push the rest to a "Shop All Bedding" landing page.
- 3. Surface the "$500" sweepstakes hook near the top, not buried after the 9th section — that's the only urgency-adjacent asset in the whole email.
- 4. Show at least one price or "from $XX" on hero products so the click has a concrete payoff.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh sheet sets just landed — plus a chance at $500`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plaid, gingham & stripe sets — all ship free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New quilts, duvets & sheet sets for every kid's room.`
