# Custom furniture made easy →

- This is a custom-upholstery education send, and it looks good. Large editorial room photography, then a run of collection blocks (Lounge, Retreat, Serenity, Cosygrove, Axis). Each block has a short blurb and its own "Shop" button. Below those are a swatch-ordering block, The DesignSpace trade-style appointment block, and a Fall Sales Event footer section. The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can't read the small body copy reliably.
- It's a lot of email for one idea. The hero, "the exact right sofa made easy" with a "Start Customizing" button, is clear. The Double Rewards and "up to 50% off" messages sit in a thin top strip and in a module near the bottom, so the savings never lead. This is also another Crate & Barrel send within roughly 48 hours of the 2026-11-06 "We're counting down to Black Friday with deals!" and 2026-11-05 "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" emails. Compared with those, this one is a calmer, brand-building piece. The discount-fatigued subscriber gets something different here, but the deal is underplayed.
- Verdict: strong creative and a good fit for a furniture shopper, but too long, with a muddled hierarchy between "custom" and "sale". It's a solid send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero has a single clear promise ("the exact right sofa made easy") and a single "Start Customizing" button.
- The photography is large, consistent and aspirational, with a repeating image, label and button rhythm that makes the long scroll easy to follow.
- The send follows the useful-content angle: swatch ordering and a design appointment address the main hesitation about buying custom furniture.
- The top strip repeats Double Rewards and the Fall Sales Event, so members see the loyalty benefit.
- It's a change of pace from the previous days' discount-heavy sends.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with about five near-identical collection blocks, which dilutes the hero.
- The offer is buried. The savings sit in a small top strip and a footer module, not the hero.
- Several collection headings are overlaid on photos in small type, and they look hard to read at the rendered size.
- The "Shop [Collection]" buttons are all alike, with no price, lead time or fabric detail to differentiate them.
- This is yet another send in a very tight cadence, and the "countdown" messaging at the top conflicts with the "made easy" theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the collection blocks from five to two or three, and put a single savings line directly under the hero, e.g. "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards on custom upholstery".
- 2. Make the custom promise concrete in the hero with a delivery window, a fabric count or a swatch offer.
- 3. Put product names, "from $" pricing or lead times in the collection blocks, and use larger type for headings over images.
- 4. Move the swatch and design-appointment blocks up, since they remove the main friction.
- 5. Merge the footer sale module into one clear module, and space this send further from the others.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a template-built email (Movable Ink `mi.` and `dv.` hosts, XHTML 1.0 Transitional). The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings), and the main confirmed problem is widespread missing `alt` text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the relay didn't capture headers, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified redirects:** 78 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` (LiveRamp) are five separate pixel requests, all missing `alt`.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71...` (7 images), `.../p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/624ceacf-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` carry a per-recipient hash. This is expected for Movable Ink. These images fail if the MI host is slow or blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (confirmed, 42 images listed by QA):**
  - 28 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/...` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `6f9ddb32-...jpg`, `a2041dbe-...gif`, `971aa10f-...jpg`). With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show no text or read the filename.
  - 7 Movable Ink `rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, etc.).
  - 7 tracking pixels (5 `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, the `dv.` image). These should carry `alt=""`.
  - The QA output appears cut off at the `dv.` entry, so the real count may be higher.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a bare `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype, so the markup is invalid. Browsers and email clients tolerate it.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px or wider, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers (cosmetic):**
  - A placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv` Content-Type tags.
  - `format-detection` declared three times.
  - An empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. I saw no unresolved `%%token%%`, `{{ }}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` patterns. The source is truncated, so the body was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA WARN):** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN):** one-click unsubscribe support can't be verified. The same relay caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be validated from this data.
- **Footer not verified:** the truncated HTML didn't show the visible unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, so I can't confirm either. Both are required under CAN-SPAM.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 78 links are wrapped in click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unknown. The truncated source didn't expose any destination URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 28 content images and the 7 MI recommendation images, and `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and delete the duplicate meta tags and the leftover placeholder comment.
4. Fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries, or drop the dead 768px rule.
5. Verify the headers on the raw `.eml` rather than the relay copy: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and aligned).
6. Resolve the redirect links in a follow-up crawl, using a headless browser or a redirect follower on a sample, to check destinations and UTM parameters.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and look for merge tokens.
