# Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Crate & Barrel Kids send with a credible urgency hook. A live countdown timer (03 : 15 : 46 : 32) sits above a Halloween pumpkin photo. Directly below it, "ends tomorrow! free shipping on orders $99+" gets its own banner and a "Shop All Free Shipping" button. The subject and body match, which is rare in this brand's recent run.
- The email then loses focus. After the shipping banner it stacks a second "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event: Up to 50% off" block, a "500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29!" block, a TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo, and a design-pro appointment. That is six or seven competing asks in one scroll. The shipping deadline is the sharpest message, and it gets diluted.
- The send is competent but bloated. Compared with the 2026-10-10 "Our outer-space bedroom is here" send, the creative is stronger here. It still reads as a catch-all, and for a furniture-focused reader the kids and nursery framing is a partial fit.
- Note: I reviewed the render at a heavily reduced size, so small copy and the preheader are not legible.

## What's working

- The countdown timer above the hero gives time pressure you can see at a glance.
- The "ends tomorrow! free shipping on orders $99+" banner has a low threshold and a clear button.
- The subject line states the same deadline and offer as the body.
- The Halloween hero fits the date (Oct 11) and the "shop halloween" tile.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and consistent branding throughout.

## What's weak

- The headline "up to 50% off" sits above the fold, but the free-shipping offer is a second banner below the hero. Two offers compete for the top spot.
- The lower half is a pile of unrelated modules: sweepstakes, credit card, app download and design appointment. They dilute the deadline.
- The shipping offer is tied to the Semi-Annual Event, and a fine-print asterisk adds exclusions. That undercuts the "simple" message.
- The product grid shows strollers, a recliner, a dresser, a crib mattress and a play gym. They are scattered across categories with small, hard-to-read pricing.
- Seven or more CTAs make it unclear what the one action is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the shipping deadline, the 50% event and one product grid. Move the sweepstakes, app promo and credit card to a separate send.
- 2. Put the free-shipping bar and countdown directly over the hero, and move the 50% event to a single follow-up module.
- 3. Give the main button a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop Nursery Deals", and link it to one category page.
- 4. Segment the product grid by browse behavior so a furniture shopper sees dressers and recliners first.
- 5. Show the price and original price on each product tile at a readable size.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with a mobile media query, `lang="en"` and Movable Ink dynamic content. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass, 0 issues, 3 warnings), but about 30 images lack alt text and the authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified:** 72 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party identity pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` to `n=5` requests fire per open. The `s=` value is a 64-hex hash, and its leading characters match the `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` value on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. The same per-recipient identifier appears to be passed to a third party (LiveRamp, `rlcdn.com`) across five sync calls. This is not a rendering bug, but it adds requests and sends a stable ID to an external domain.
- **Per-recipient URLs:** each recipient gets unique `mi_u` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` values, so the image URLs can't be cached or compared across sends.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 30 images, per QA):**
  - 16 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg`/`.gif`) and one on `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, which look like Movable Ink dynamic-content images.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/242731d4-…`, which is a dynamic image.
  - Images blocked by default (Outlook, some Gmail modes) will show as blank space with no fallback text.
- **Probably harmless pixels:** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` ×5 and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`), which the QA tool may be flagging only because the attribute is absent.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It also doesn't describe this message.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). Only one is needed.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template, and "IMPUT" is misspelled.
- **Conflicting CSS:**
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` conflicts with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`.
  - Two `img{max-width}` rules (600px at ≥768px, 640px at ≥640px) overlap at desktop widths, so the later 640px rule wins.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the truncated portion. The body wasn't visible, so token rendering there is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** QA notes the relay may not capture it. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect it, so confirm against the raw message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** one-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 can't be confirmed, for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Not verified:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated part of the source.

These three header warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed defects. The data can't confirm or rule out a problem.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 72 links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the visible source. The "Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+" offer can't be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the ~17 content images. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the pixel and tracking images. Set a fallback `alt` on the Movable Ink dynamic images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`) and make it descriptive. Collapse the four `format-detection` tags into one.
4. Review the five `rlcdn.com` calls and the hashed-ID parameter. Reduce them to one or drop them if they're not needed.
5. Re-run the audit with the full raw message (headers plus untruncated HTML) to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, UTM parameters and the footer.
6. Make the QA pipeline resolve the redirect chains on the skipped tracking links, at least for the primary CTAs, so landing-page continuity can be checked.
