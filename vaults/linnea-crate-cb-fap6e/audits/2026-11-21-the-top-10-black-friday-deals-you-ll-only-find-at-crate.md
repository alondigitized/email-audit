# The top 10: Black Friday deals you’ll ONLY find at Crate →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's "top 10" curation play, and it's the best structural idea in a week of near-identical Black Friday sends. A numbered list of ten items gives the scroll a rhythm. The hero reads "the top 10: Black Friday deals you'll only find here," under a red banner promising up to 50% off plus free shipping. It then walks through KitchenAid, a Breville oven, cookware, bedding, a rug and more.
- Caveat on this review: the screenshot is 680x12000, and the copy is small at the size I could view it. I'm confident about the layout, section order and headline offers. I'm less sure of fine print and some item names.
- The email is still long and kitchen-heavy. Items 01 to 05 and the closing blocks are mostly cookware and appliances, so "top 10" reads more like a kitchen catalog with a few home pieces mixed in. After the list there's a second "up to 40% off kitchen deals" block, a cookware tile row, a Design Desk $5000 sweepstakes and a "we love these for you" grid. That's the 9th or 10th Black Friday send I've audited this week, following the 11/20 "Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!" and "IT'S ON!" sends. The curation helps, but the cadence and the repeated "up to 50% + free shipping" banner are the same message again.
- **Judgement:** strong concept, decent execution, and weak differentiation from the rest of the week's sends. It's a solid mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The numbered "top 10" framing gives a clear reason to scroll and a scannable structure.
- The top banner states the offer plainly (up to 50% off + free shipping), with a "Shop Now" button right under it.
- Named hero deals are specific. The KitchenAid mixers are "up to 30% off" and the Breville oven is "up to $100 off."
- Sale and regular prices sit next to the products, which makes the savings concrete.
- A shipping-deadline line at the very top ("get it by Thanksgiving with 2-day shipping…") adds credible urgency.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. The list is a satisfying 10 items, but the kitchen block, cookware tiles, sweepstakes and recommendation grid keep going after it.
- The list is skewed to kitchen. Furniture, rugs and bedding feel like afterthoughts, and the "top 10" promise isn't fully diverse.
- CTAs are generic "Shop Now" buttons repeated throughout, rather than verb-plus-product copy.
- The "ONLY find at Crate" exclusivity claim isn't backed by visible proof beyond "Exclusive" tags on a few items.
- The Design Desk $5000 sweepstakes block competes with the commerce focus right at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut everything after item 10.** Drop the duplicate kitchen block, the cookware tile row and the recommendation grid. Keep the list and one footer CTA.
- 2. **Diversify the 10.** Aim for a mix across kitchen, bedroom, rugs and decor so the "top 10" claim holds up.
- 3. **Make CTAs specific.** Use "Shop the KitchenAid deal" and "Shop the Breville oven" instead of "Shop Now."
- 4. **Put a visible "Exclusive to Crate" badge on every list item,** to back up the subject claim.
- 5. **Move the sweepstakes to its own send** or to a minimal footer link.
- 6. **Shorten the subject** to fit mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `10 Black Friday deals only at Crate`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Top 10: our best Black Friday deals →`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `KitchenAid up to 30% off, Breville $100 off, and 8 more. Free shipping included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11/24 to get it by Thanksgiving. Start with #1.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass rate, 0 issues, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problems are about 48 images without alt text and a few markup defects. The header checks and link checks were inconclusive because of the relay and the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **99 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` differ only in `n`. The `s=` value has the same prefix as the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images, so a per-recipient hash appears to be passed to a third-party domain (rlcdn.com, a LiveRamp domain).
- **Other tracking assets:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is the open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a25fbe80-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a second open or measurement beacon, so two open-tracking mechanisms are present.
- No broken or malformed links were detected, but that is limited to what could be checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 48 images are missing alt text** (QA WARNs, list possibly truncated).
  - **Content images (about 41):** 33 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `f0a7c357-…gif`. Seven are on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, and one is `971aa10f-…jpg`. With images blocked, these render blank.
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** the five `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`; the attribute is currently missing, not empty.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup defects:**
  - The XHTML doctype is used with a raw `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`, which is invalid XHTML.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under the XHTML doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared twice or more, and charset is declared twice.
  - The title does not reflect the subject line.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is shipped in production source.
- Most of the body source was truncated, so alt text and structure there could not be reviewed beyond the QA list.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid=` parameters are resolved per-recipient values, not broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (WARN)** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** QA notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, and it needs raw-header verification, since Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be validated here.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 99 links were skipped as tracking-redirect domains, and no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters are in the provided data. The only parameters visible are the ESP's `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. Landing-page alignment with the subject ("Black Friday deals you'll ONLY find at Crate") was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the roughly 41 content images, and `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title (`&amp;`), close or remove the duplicate meta tags, and strip the `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.
4. Capture the raw headers, bypassing the relay if possible. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` / click-domain chain) and check final URLs for status codes and UTM presence.
6. Review whether passing the per-recipient hash to `sr.rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy policy, and whether five duplicate pixels are needed.
