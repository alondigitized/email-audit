# Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, busy Crate & Barrel Kids Early Black Friday send. The image was heavily downscaled (680x7783 shown at 175x2000), so I'm judging layout, hierarchy and headline copy, not fine print or exact prices. The hero is a dark nursery photo with "early black friday is here! up to 50% off" and a "Shop the Sale" button. A red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner sits above it. The offer is clear and the hero is strong.
- The email then repeats the offer. A four-tile category grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs, each "up to 50% off") is followed by a second "Shop the Sale" button. After that come a Jenny Lind bed promo, a second red Double Rewards banner with two buttons, a Feeding America block, a product grid, a Crate Card promo, an app download block, a Design Desk block, and a store locator. That is roughly nine content modules and at least eight CTAs. The primary message is buried by the end, and the Double Rewards banner appears twice.
- Judgement: a solid hero and a clear deal, but the email is bloated and repeats earlier Kids sends. It reads as the same Early Black Friday blast as the Nov 11 "Early Black Friday Starts TODAY!" Kids email with a new subject line.

## What's working

- The hero ties "early black friday," "up to 50% off" and the nursery photo into one message, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The red Double Rewards banner at the top gives loyalty members a second reason to act.
- The four-tile category grid with "up to 50% off" on each tile makes the deal easy to scan.
- The Jenny Lind callout ("up to 20% off our iconic Jenny Lind beds") gives a specific, named hero product with a single CTA.
- The product grid (Nuna strollers and car seats, Babyletto chair, Jenny Lind bed) shows real products with visible prices.

## What's weak

- Double Rewards appears twice, and the "Shop the Sale" button repeats too, which makes the email feel padded.
- The hero text overlaps a busy photo, and the headline "up to 50% off" looks hard to read against the crib and wall.
- Feeding America, the Crate Card, the app download, Design Desk and the store locator all sit in the last 40% of the email. They compete with the offer and dilute the click path.
- The legal fine print is a dense block in two places. It makes the offer feel less honest.
- This is the second "Early Black Friday starts now" Kids send in two days, after the Nov 11 version. Cadence is fatiguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to hero, category grid, one product row and one rewards banner. Move the Feeding America, Crate Card, app, Design Desk and store modules to separate sends. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the hero headline on a solid colour block or add a darker overlay so "up to 50% off" is legible at mobile size.
- 3. Merge the two Double Rewards banners into one, and give it a deadline ("ends Nov 17").
- 4. Use one primary CTA style and verb throughout, such as "Shop Nursery Deals", instead of repeating "Shop the Sale".
- 5. Add star ratings to the product grid for social proof.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, and 74 tracking links were not probed, so link destinations, the footer and the unsubscribe link could not be verified. The confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 33 images) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 74 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so there is no HTTP status or final-destination data for any CTA.
- **Third-party beacons:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` pixels load from LiveRamp. They carry a hashed per-recipient identifier in `s=`.
- **First-party beacons:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ff8d12b6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and tracking pixels. Neither is an error, but both need `alt=""` (see §3).
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, about 33 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `26171910-…jpg`, `af1c5ddd-…jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif`) have no alt. Image blocking or screen readers will show nothing for them.
  - `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png` and `971aa10f-….jpg` are also missing alt.
  - Spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` has no alt.
  - Pixels and beacons (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4. Combined with `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`, text cannot be resized.
- **Conflicting media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - The `<title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated, so personalization IDs are resolving. The truncated source means the footer and body copy could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check them against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` should be checked directly against its DNS and the raw headers.
- **Footer not verifiable:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are beyond the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 74 links are click-redirect URLs with no resolved destination, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Early Black Friday / Double Rewards" offer are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Add `alt=""` to the spacer, pixels and beacons.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Remove the conflicting `max-width:600px` or `640px` `img` rule so a single desktop cap applies.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from the raw message. Rerun QA with full source if the relay is dropping headers.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links in a follow-up run to check UTMs and landing pages.
6. Clean up the duplicate `format-detection` metas, the unescaped `&` in the title, and the stray "IMPUT HERE" template comment.
