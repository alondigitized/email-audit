# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, busy Crate & Barrel Early Black Friday send. It does the main job: a deep red hero says "starts today! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" with a single "Shop Now" button, and a "last Double Rewards this year!" card sits above it. The offer is clear and the deadline (Nov. 17) is credible. The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I'm judging layout, hierarchy and headline copy, not the fine print.
- The problem is repetition and cadence. This is at least the fifth sale-and-Rewards send in three days. Subscribers saw "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" on 11-10, then "Dinnerware, serveware & more up to 30% off + 2x Rewards" on 11-09. The "last Double Rewards this year!" card appears twice in this email, near the top and again mid-page. Subject-line urgency has also run out of room. "Early Black Friday" is a new framing, but the offer under it is the same one.
- The body sprawls through a category grid, a kitchen-deals block, a Thanksgiving menu, recommended products, a credit card promo, an app download and a design appointment. The first screen is strong. After it, the eye has no clear next stop. Verdict: a solid hero wrapped in an overstuffed email. It is serviceable but fatigued.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 30% off Early Black Friday" with one "Shop Now" button.
- The "last Double Rewards this year!" card above the hero gives loyalty members a clear reason to act, with the Nov. 17 end date.
- The four-tile category grid (entertaining, furniture, kitchen brands, holiday decor) is easy to scan and each tile carries its own "up to 30% off".
- The kitchen block shows specific products with sale and regular prices (e.g. "Sale $179.95 Reg. $219.95"), which makes the discount concrete.
- The footer navigation (Furniture, Bedding, Decor & Pillows, Tableware & Bar, Kitchen, Rugs, New Arrivals, Sale) lets a reader jump straight to a category.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards message appears twice, and the credit card, app and design appointment sections all compete for the same click.
- Two tiles in the category grid ("up to 30% off furniture" and "up to 30% off entertaining") have light text over busy photos, which makes them hard to read.
- The email is very long, with at least ten distinct blocks, and the hierarchy flattens after the kitchen section.
- The Thanksgiving menu block has a "Get the Recipes" CTA that is unrelated to the sale and pulls attention from the offer.
- It continues a high cadence of near-identical promos, so "Early Black Friday" feels like a relabel of the Fall Sales Event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid, one product block and one rewards reminder. Move the credit card, app and design-appointment blocks to a separate send.
- 2. Show Double Rewards once, in the hero, rather than as a card above it and again halfway down.
- 3. Fix the legibility of the light text on the busy category tiles, using a scrim or darker type.
- 4. Make the subject lead with what is different, which is the new "Early Black Friday" event, and drop the stacked offers.
- 5. Give each section one specific CTA, such as "Shop Cookware Deals" rather than "Get the Recipes" next to "Shop All Kitchen Deals".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday came early: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday is on. Last 2x Rewards ends Nov. 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 30% off cookware, furniture and decor, plus earn 2x Rewards through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11/19 by 11:59 PM PT to get it home for Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`<!--V2-->`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`) with 70 redirect-wrapped links. The automated QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problems are about 30 images with no `alt` attribute, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and header checks that could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 70 links were skipped because they are tracking or click-redirect domains, so none of the final landing URLs were checked for 404s or redirect chains. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) load in the body. They carry a per-recipient `s=` hash.
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d498b6dc-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are the open pixels. Several `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images are per-recipient dynamic content.
- **Per-recipient parameters:** `mi_u` and `s=` appear in image URLs. If the email is forwarded, those identifiers go with it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 30 `<img>` elements with no `alt`.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` include `6100bbef-…jpg`, `d8683d75-…jpg`, `84b30380-…jpg` and `24335f96-…gif`.
  - Dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…` include `560d850e7e517ede.png` and `789e7381ff44be74.png`.
  - Blocked-image clients and screen readers will get nothing for these.
  - The pixel images (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so the empty alt is deliberate.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `body`. This prevents user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times, and both `http-equiv` and `charset` content-type declarations are present. This is redundant but harmless.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source (typo in the original).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are resolved. The remainder of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed** as a sender defect. Check the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.
- **From domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image domain `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. This looks consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be verified. The visible source has no `utm_*` parameters, and the 70 redirect links were not resolved. The only identifiers seen are `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on image URLs. Resolve a sample of the click-tracking links and check that they land on the sale pages with campaign parameters preserved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Change `&` to `&amp;` in `<title>`, and remove the leftover template comment and duplicate meta tags.
4. Inspect the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Follow redirects for a sample of the 70 tracking links to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM or campaign parameters.
6. Fix the QA pipeline so it records the original authentication headers when the relay strips them.
