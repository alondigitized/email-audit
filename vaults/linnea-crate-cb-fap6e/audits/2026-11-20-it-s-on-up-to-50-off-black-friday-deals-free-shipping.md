# IT’S ON! Up to 50% off Black Friday deals + free shipping →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth Crate & Barrel Black Friday send I've audited this week, and it's the omnibus Kids edition. The hero reads "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" over a muted, textured photo, with a single "Shop the Sale" button. It reads as a clear promise. The offer lands before anything else.
- Below the hero is a long scroll of two-up category tiles: kids furniture, baby furniture, holiday decor, nod chairs, bedding, toys, playroom furniture and baby gear. Each tile carries its own "up to X% off" line, and some add "Ships Free!". After that come a holiday travel gear block, a wool rugs block, an UPPAbaby Remi playard feature, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design services block. It's a catalog, not a campaign. It repeats the 11/20 "Black Friday deals are here! Shop up to 50% off" send almost beat for beat, and the 11/19 sends before it covered the same ground.
- Judgement: competent and honest, but fatigue is the real problem. A subscriber who already got the 11/18 "STARTS NOW" kickoff and several category sends today has little reason to open another "it's on!" email. The hero text on a busy photo also has weak contrast.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer directly: "up to 50% off + free shipping," with a visible "Shop the Sale" button.
- The category tiles each show a specific discount, so the reader can find their category quickly.
- "Ships Free!" tags on bedding and furniture tiles reinforce the free-shipping claim.
- The product grid with prices ($79, $1,099 and so on) gives concrete detail for people who scroll that far.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero headline sits on a textured photo with a pale button, so contrast is weak and the text is hard to read at small size.
- The tiles run at least 8 deep, each with a different percentage (20%, 30%, 50%). That dilutes the "50% off" headline.
- The email repeats the earlier 11/20 omnibus send's structure, including the rugs block and the app and design-desk promos, so it feels recycled.
- Several secondary CTAs ("Shop Wool Rugs," "Shop All Gear," "Learn More") are small and muted.
- The credit card, app download and design services blocks near the bottom pull attention away from the sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the textured hero image with a bright, high-contrast product shot, and put one deadline in the headline, such as "Ends Monday." The 11/20 sends already hit "ends today," so a real end date is the differentiator.
- 2. Cut the tile grid to 4 to 6 tiles, led by the highest-discount categories.
- 3. Drop the credit card, app and design desk blocks from sale sends and keep them for non-promo days.
- 4. Make the "Ships Free!" tag consistent, or state the free-shipping threshold once above the grid.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11/20 omnibus send, or space the two sends further apart.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping + 50% off nursery & playroom`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding, toys and gear. Free shipping on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest kids sale: cribs to playroom, now through Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard (VML/MSO fallbacks, media queries, a preheader-safe structure), but about 40 images are flagged without alt text and the viewport meta blocks user zoom. Header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed, because the relay didn't expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **83 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **A third-party pixel appears in the source.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` are five LiveRamp pixels. The `s=` token looks identical to the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the Movable Ink URLs (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `/p/up/…/o.gif`). If so, a per-recipient hashed identifier is being passed to a third party. I can't confirm what the hash encodes from the truncated HTML.
- **Tracking pixels are duplicated.** The same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` URL appears six times, and the `rlcdn` pixel five times. This is redundant and adds requests.
- There is also an open-tracking pixel at `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/f2a3a63d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 40 images, confirmed by the QA check.** The content images are:
  - About 20 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, for example `a0fbd386-…jpg` and `abd6141d-…jpg`.
  - The Scene7 hero `2026%5F1122%5FCBK%5FBF%5FDealsGrid%5FREMIX%5FHero…`.
  - `…WoolRugs…gif` and `…UppababyLaunch%5Fanim`.
  - If these are linked images, images-off clients and screen readers get no accessible link name.
  - The remaining flagged items are spacers and tracking pixels (`25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`). These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The check doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4 and is ignored by some clients.
- **The `<title>` is invalid and generic.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype (it should be `&amp;`). It also doesn't match the "Crate & Barrel Kids" sender.
- **Template residue and duplicates in the head.**
  - A placeholder comment is left in: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping, redundant values.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `<!--V2-->` sits before `<html>`, which can trigger quirks in some parsers.
- **A global `*{ -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100% }` rule** overrides the later `body` text-size-adjust of 100%. The two conflict.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated source. The `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are Movable Ink dynamic-content parameters, not broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA flags both as WARN, with the note that the relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.** This is likely a relay artifact. Verify it on the original message.
- **The footer isn't in the truncated source.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address can't be verified.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking hosts, which is a positive signal for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 83 links went through click-redirect domains and were skipped, so no UTM parameters or landing-page URLs were observed. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and linked images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), or use a descriptive title such as the campaign subject.
4. Remove the template placeholder comment and the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags. Resolve the conflicting `text-size-adjust` rules.
5. Deduplicate the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `rlcdn` pixels. Confirm what the `rlcdn` `s=` token contains and whether it is disclosed in the privacy policy.
6. Re-run QA against the raw MIME source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC).
7. Resolve the redirect chains out of band, with a headless fetch or an ESP click-map. This checks the landing-page URLs and UTM parameters.
