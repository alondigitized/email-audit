## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's source is a standard table-based, Marigold/Cheetah-style template. The QA run (50% pass, 0 errors, 4 warnings) found missing alt text on about 50 images, no captured List-Unsubscribe headers, and several third-party tracking pixels. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links unverified:** 92 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped for HTTP probing, so none were checked for destination status or redirect chains.
- **Repeated pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with identical src. These are likely per-module recipient-tracking pixels, and they add repeated requests.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e…&n=1` through `n=5` are 5 LiveRamp (rlcdn) sync pixels. The `s=` value is a 64-hex string. It starts with the same prefix as the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e…` identifier, so one recipient identifier appears to be shared with a third-party domain. This is a privacy and compliance concern, and some clients and privacy proxies will block these pixels.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is the open pixel. `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/65b94b79-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a second tracker.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA run flagged about 50 `<img>` elements, including:
  - ~29 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`
  - the 6 `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels
  - the 5 `448796.gif` pixels
  - `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel
- **Alt triage:** The checker can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from an intentional `alt=""`. It's acceptable for the tracking pixels to have an empty `alt=""`. It isn't acceptable for the content images, where an absent `alt` makes screen readers announce the filename (e.g. `5aa908ba-02d3-…jpg`) and gives nothing to read if images are blocked. This is a real issue for the content images.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Markup validity (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` isn't self-closed and duplicates `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times: once combined, then `date`, `address` and `telephone` separately.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and "IMPUT" is a typo.
- **Global CSS resets:** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element. This can override inline defaults and force centered paragraphs where that isn't intended.
- **Single mobile breakpoint:** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports from 461px to 639px get the desktop layout with `img{max-width:…}` unset, which may cause overflow on larger phones.
- **Plain text:** 18,408 of 25,853 characters (about 71%) of the plain-text part are URLs, above the 70% threshold. This is a deliverability and readability risk. The cause is probably long tracking URLs.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (e.g. `{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing List-Unsubscribe headers:** The QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe` and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The checker notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this isn't confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so the headers should be checked on the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication unverified:** `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image and tracking subdomains.
- **Footer not inspected:** The HTML provided is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues could be confirmed. All 92 click links are redirect-tracked and weren't probed, so the destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed. The only identifiers visible are `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on image URLs, which are the platform's tracking identifiers. They aren't UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and use explicit `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw origin message. Also verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment, since the relay hides them.
4. Review the 5 `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels for privacy and compliance. They share a recipient-level identifier with a third party. Consider dropping the 6 repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels in favor of one.
5. Shorten the plain-text part's URLs, or provide a cleaner text alternative, to bring the URL share below 70%.
6. Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and delete the leftover template comment.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML. Probe a sample of the redirect links and resolve their final URLs, to check UTM parameters and landing page continuity.
