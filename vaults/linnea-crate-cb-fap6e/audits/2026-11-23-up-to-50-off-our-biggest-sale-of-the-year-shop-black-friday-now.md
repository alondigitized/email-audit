# Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year! Shop Black Friday now →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's main-brand Black Friday omnibus: "Black Friday is here! Up to 50% off + free shipping" over a Breville espresso machine, then a long scroll of category tiles. It follows the same template as the Black Friday sends audited on 11/22 and 11/23 ("Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →" and "It's on! Black Friday deals & free shipping inside"). The one new element is a "Big news: New items just hit our Black Friday sale" band under the hero.
- The email is well built. The offer is clear, the hero is clean, and the Thanksgiving-delivery cutoff at the very top is a credible urgency line. But it's a long, 11,000px catalog dump with about a dozen competing CTAs. The espresso hero and the "up to 50% off" headline don't line up, because nothing in the hero shows what is half off. A subscriber who has already seen three near-identical sends this week has little reason to open another.
- Caveat: the render was downscaled heavily, so small print and per-product prices were hard to read. My read is based on layout, headlines and structure.

## What's working

- The "HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving" strip at the top gives a real, credible deadline.
- The hero states one offer plainly: up to 50% off plus free shipping, with a single "Shop Now" button.
- The category tiles show their discount depth ("up to 40% off", "up to 50% off", "up to 30% off") and are easy to scan.
- Sections are broken up with clear sub-headers ("shop 1000s of Black Friday deals", "up to 40% off top Black Friday kitchen deals").
- Sale-price and regular-price callouts on kitchen products add specific, concrete value.

## What's weak

- It's the fourth or fifth near-identical Black Friday blast in two days, so the cadence is fatigued.
- The hero image is an espresso machine, but the headline is a store-wide discount. Image and offer are disconnected.
- It's far too long, with many sections and CTAs (clearance, kitchen, Design Desk, holiday decor, app, credit card). The offer gets diluted by the time you reach the bottom.
- The lower half shifts to the Design Desk consultation, the app download and the credit-card promo. These are brand-service pitches that don't belong in a sale email.
- The "We love these for you" product row seems generic rather than personalised.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by roughly 40%. Keep the hero, the top 6 category tiles, one kitchen feature and one footer CTA. Move the Design Desk, app and credit-card promos to separate sends.
- 2. Make the hero image match the offer, for example a collage of the best-selling discounted items instead of a single espresso machine. Change the button from "Shop Now" to "Shop Up to 50% Off".
- 3. Lead with the Thanksgiving delivery deadline in the subject or preheader, since that's the most concrete urgency you have.
- 4. Give each category tile its own clear action ("Shop sofas") instead of leaving the tile as the only click target.
- 5. Segment sends by category (kitchen, furniture, decor) so repeat Black Friday blasts aren't identical.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Order by 11:59 PM for Thanksgiving delivery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off + free shipping, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, kitchen and decor deals, plus free shipping on everything you need for the holidays.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added. Order with 2-day shipping to get it by Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template. The visible issues are about 39 images flagged for missing alt text, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and several leftover template defects. The QA warnings about unsubscribe and authentication headers can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, and all 83 links were redirect-wrapped, so the body, footer and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** 83 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so landing-page status and redirect chains could not be checked.
- **Third-party pixel repeated 5 times.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` appear. The `n` values differ and the `s` value is identical. The domain is a LiveRamp ad-sync endpoint. The 64-hex `s` value looks like a SHA-256 hash, possibly of the recipient's address, but the data doesn't show what it hashes. The repeated calls add load and third-party exposure for no clear benefit.
- **Recipient-keyed identifiers in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…`, `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/aec03822-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party tracking and open pixels. Proxies that pre-fetch images will inflate opens.
- **Other tracking domains.** Images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is a first-party subdomain. No broken or mixed-content (http) assets were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 39 images.** Examples: `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, 23 other `…/m/1/*.jpg|gif` assets, 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` files, and `…/m/1/971aa10f-…jpg`. The QA list ends mid-URL, so the count may be incomplete. Most of these are content images, and any that carry the offers or product names are unreadable with images off or in a screen reader. The `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels need `alt=""` rather than nothing. It's also unclear from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) in clients that honor it.
- **Text-size handling.** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rules disable iOS text scaling. The global `p{text-align:center}` forces centering on every paragraph unless it is overridden.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Template leftovers.** There is a duplicate `<meta charset>` alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type, and `format-detection` is declared three times. The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head, and there are empty `<!---->` and `<!--[if !mso]><!-->…<!--<![endif]-->` blocks.
- **Dead CSS.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both have the same specificity and both match at 768px and wider, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, and the VML and MSO conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect. Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are unmet if the headers are truly absent.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown, again probably because of the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host, which is consistent but proves nothing.
- **Footer unchecked.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 83 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so UTM parameters, final URLs and any sale-page alignment with "Up to 50% off… Black Friday" couldn't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Collapse the five identical `rlcdn` pixel calls to one, or remove them.
4. Escape the title (`&amp;`), and clean up the duplicate meta tags, the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder and the dead `max-width:600px` rule.
5. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` or the original headers, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links (follow redirects, don't probe the tracker) to check UTMs and landing-page status.
