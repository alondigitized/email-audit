# Up to 30% off holiday hosting essentials + Double Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a hosting-themed send that tries to be four emails at once. The top of the email works. The hero reads "host & toast: up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more" over a styled table setting, with a single Shop Now button and a six-tile grid of specific categories (dinnerware, wine glasses, serveware, flatware). It matches the subject and the season. It is also the most on-topic Crate & Barrel send I've seen in this run.
- Below that, the email loses focus. It runs from Thanksgiving table styles into Double Rewards kitchen deals (All-Clad, espresso, blenders), then "up to 35% off cookware & bakeware," then an "it's here! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" block, then a second "ends today: last Double Rewards this year" banner. After that come a product carousel, a credit-card-style "it all adds up" module, an app-download and $500 chance module, and a TheDesignDesk services strip. The screenshot is about 11,000px tall. The hosting message is buried under repeated discount tiers (30%, 35%) and repeated Rewards messaging.
- The caveat is that the screenshot was downscaled heavily, so I can't read fine print or small captions. The structure and headline copy are legible, and the verdict rests on those. Overall it is a solid first screen on a bloated scroll. It's near the middle of this brand's range, and it is another near-duplicate of today's Rewards-deadline sends.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: "host & toast: up to 30% off" over a table setting.
- The top strip carries the Double Rewards "ends today" deadline and an Early Black Friday note, so urgency is visible before the first scroll.
- The six-tile grid gives specific categories (dinnerware, wine glasses, serveware, drinkware, flatware) with their own offers.
- The "5 styles for your Thanksgiving table" module is a real editorial hook that fits the season.
- The photography is warm and cohesive, with autumn tones and tabletop styling.

## What's weak

- The email makes too many separate offers: 30% hosting, 30% Early Black Friday, 35% cookware and Double Rewards. A skimmer won't know which one is "the" deal.
- The Rewards deadline appears twice, once in the top strip and again as a full banner mid-email. Today's "ENDS TODAY: Last 2x Rewards this year!" and "Last Double Rewards of the year!" sends already hit it.
- The email repeats the same message with different layouts. The cookware and bakeware tiles, the kitchen-deals block and the Early Black Friday block overlap.
- The bottom third (app download, $500 chance, TheDesignDesk, credit-card-style module) is unrelated to hosting and dilutes the message.
- There are many CTAs: Shop Now, Shop The Table, Shop All Tabletop & Bar, Shop The Styles, Shop All Kitchen Deals and more. Several tiles are small and hard to tell apart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hosting story: hero, six tiles, Thanksgiving styles, and one Rewards line. Move Early Black Friday, the app and the TheDesignDesk strip to separate sends. That alone would sharpen both clicks and brand perception.
- 2. Keep one deadline. Use a single sticky Rewards line at the top and drop the mid-email repeat.
- 3. Merge the "kitchen deals" and "cookware & bakeware" modules into one product-led block with 3-4 priced hero items.
- 4. Replace the generic "shop now" buttons with verb and noun copy such as "Shop dinnerware sets" or "Set the table."
- 5. Add an order-by shipping cutoff for Thanksgiving delivery next to the hero. The top strip hints at one, but I can't read it at this size.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Set the Thanksgiving table: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hosting this year? Dinnerware up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 2x Rewards through tonight on tabletop, bar & cookware.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11/18 to get it before Thanksgiving. Serveware, glassware & more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The captured source is truncated, so the footer, link `href`s and headers couldn't be inspected. Of what was visible, the confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` text (about 55 images in the QA list) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings are unverified, because they may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 95 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. No click-through result, redirect chain or broken-link status is available.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels (`n` increments from 1 to 5). They are LiveRamp (rlcdn) beacons, each repeating the same `s=` hash. That hash is identical to the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images, so one recipient identifier is being passed to a third party.
- **Open/tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b500e0e6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry no `alt`. They are likely 1×1 pixels, so this is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (confirmed):** the QA check flags about 55 images. Most are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`, plus `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. Seven more are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. With images blocked, the email shows no fallback text. Because the QA output only reports a missing `alt`, I can't tell whether the attribute is absent or empty (`alt=""`). If any of these are decorative, `alt=""` is correct, and the check may be over-reporting.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility (WCAG 1.4.4) failure.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` plus separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses a bare `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **CSS conflicts:**
  - `img {max-width:600px}` (≥768px) and `img {max-width:640px}` (≥640px) overlap. Both apply at ≥768px, and the later 640px rule wins.
  - The 600px rule is effectively dead code.
- **Lang attribute:** `<html lang="en">` is present, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are populated recipient hashes. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** the QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. The header's presence is unverified, so this is not a confirmed violation.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN]:** the same caveat applies. If it's really absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't supported, which Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Not visible in the truncated source:**
  - The footer unsubscribe link.
  - The physical postal address.
  - Any sender-identification text.
  - These are not confirmed missing, only unchecked.
- **Third-party sharing:** the `rlcdn.com` pixels pass a per-recipient hash to a data-onboarding vendor. This is a privacy-disclosure point and not itself a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Link destinations and UTM parameters weren't captured because all 95 tracked links were skipped. No landing-page alignment issues can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image in `/m/1/` and `/m/47/`, and use `alt=""` explicitly on decorative images and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.…/o/…`, `rlcdn`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Clean up the template: drop the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags, escape `&` in `<title>`, and remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Remove the redundant 600px `img` rule or scope the breakpoints so they don't overlap.
5. Re-check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the raw message headers (not the relay copy) before treating them as defects.
6. Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be verified.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for the 95 tracked links and record the final URLs and UTM parameters. This would let the continuity check run.
