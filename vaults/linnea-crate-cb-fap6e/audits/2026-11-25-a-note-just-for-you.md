# A note just for you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a text-first "personal note" Black Friday send from Crate & Barrel Kids. It reads like a letter from a person, with a "Hi," and "Happy Shopping, The Crate & Barrel Kids Team". It's a deliberate break from the banner-and-tile blasts of the past three days, such as "NEW ITEMS ADDED! Up to 50% off Black Friday deals!" and the 11/24 furniture and decor sends. The format is a good idea. The execution is weak.
- The "note" is a five-item numbered list of underlined links. It has no hero image, no product photo and no single offer. The discounts are scattered across 30%, 40%, 50% and 20%, and every one carries an asterisk. The eye has nowhere to land, and the only button is a muted "Shop the Sale" that sits below a wall of copy. The personal tone is also fake. The subject says "just for you", but nothing in the body is personalized.
- Below the fold, the email turns into the standard footer stack: an app download with a $500 sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk, the Crate Card, store locator and category links. They compete with the one message the note is trying to deliver. Bold idea, muddled follow-through.

## What's working

- The plain-letter format stands out from this brand's repetitive Black Friday template run.
- The numbered gift ideas are scannable and tie to real categories: easel and toys, bedroom makeover, personalized chair, baby gear, Babyletto.
- The "Personalize a Nod or Lounge Chair (our #1 gift)" line adds a specific, credible hook.
- Free shipping on $79+ is stated in the copy, and the category nav at the bottom gives easy exits.
- The Crate&BarrelKids header is clean and the render has no broken images or overlap.

## What's weak

- There's no hero image or product photography in the main section, so the top half is a wall of grey-backed text.
- The offers are fragmented and asterisked: up to 50%, up to 40%, up to 30% and up to 20%, each with a different footnote.
- The single "Shop the Sale" button is small, muted maroon and sits at the bottom. It goes to a generic sale page rather than a specific product.
- The "Black Friday" framing is easy to miss. "Shop Black Friday now before it's over" is buried in the last line, with no deadline.
- Five secondary modules below dilute the message, and the app and sweepstakes block is the most visually dominant element in the email.
- The "just for you" claim is empty. There's no name, no browsing-based content and no segmentation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero image and one headline offer. Lead with "Up to 50% off bedroom and playspace + free shipping $79+", with a visible button above the fold.
- 2. Cut the list to three items with a small product image each, and give each item its own link and button.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop Gifts Up to 40% Off" rather than "Shop the Sale". Make the button larger and higher contrast.
- 4. Put a real deadline in the body (the 11/26 Thanksgiving cutoff or the end of Black Friday).
- 5. Drop the app, Crate Card and TheDesignDesk modules from this send, or move them to a single slim row. A note-style email should stay focused.
- 6. If the subject says "for you", back it up. Reference the recipient's browsing, name or kids' age range.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5 kids' gifts up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our #1 gift (personalized!) is on sale today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wooden easels, bedroom makeovers and baby gear: our Black Friday picks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $79+ ends soon. Here's where we'd start.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: an empty first-name merge field ("Hi ,") and a 403 on the "Official Rules" link. Several images lack alt text. The HTML source was truncated, and the relay didn't capture the authentication or unsubscribe headers, so compliance and authentication can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken link (403):** The "Official Rules" link points to `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/app-sweepstakes`. This is a legal-disclosure link, so a failure matters more than usual. A 403 from an automated probe can be bot protection rather than a real outage, so verify it in a browser.
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking or click-redirect links skipped the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Duplicate tracking pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` is requested 5 times (`n=1` to `n=5`) with the same `s=` value. That is redundant third-party calls. Other pixels are `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://s3enpwpv.emltrk.com/v2/s3enpwpv?i=99278410`.
- **Identifier in URLs:** The `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels and the `mi_u=` value on the `o.gif` pixel share the prefix `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f…`. This looks like one hashed recipient identifier shared with a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`). Confirm this matches the privacy disclosures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** Three images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` have no alt:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `2072d6b9-0ff6-4d6b-9743-38ba0fce3744.png`
  - `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`

  If these carry content, they are blank to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** The rlcdn, `o.gif` and emltrk pixels also lack alt. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.tPad-auto {padding-top:auto!important;}` is not a valid value for `padding-top`. It is harmless but dead code.
- **Redundant head markup:** There are three charset declarations (`http-equiv`, plus `<meta charset="utf-8">` twice) and three `format-detection` metas. This is harmless.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta and `color-scheme` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty name field (confirmed):** The QA check found the greeting renders as "Hi ," with an empty name slot. This is a merge-field failure with no fallback value. It matters more because the subject line "A note just for you!" promises personalization.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found:** The header is missing from the captured message. QA notes AgentMail may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be validated here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** These are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.
- **Official Rules link:** The 403 on this link is also a compliance risk if the email runs a sweepstakes.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment:** The truncated HTML and the skipped tracking links show no destination URLs or UTM parameters, so this can't be assessed. The one probed destination, `/app-sweepstakes`, returned 403.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the "Hi ," greeting by adding a merge fallback (for example "Hi there,") or suppressing the greeting when the name is empty.
2. Check `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/app-sweepstakes` in a real browser. If it is bot-blocked only, no action is needed. If it is truly 403, restore it before send.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the three content images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. Remove the duplicate `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls.
5. Re-capture the raw message with full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't treat these as failures until then.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Then resolve the 52 tracking links and check UTM parameters and landing alignment.
