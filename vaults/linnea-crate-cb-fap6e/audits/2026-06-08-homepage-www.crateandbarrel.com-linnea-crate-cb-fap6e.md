# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: Crate & Barrel looks like the brand it is — warm, aspirational, modern without trying too hard. The aesthetic is immediately right for me, all rattan and warm wood tones and styled rooms I want to live in. But I literally cannot see the homepage right now because a 10%-off email modal is blocking the whole thing, which is such a buzzkill for someone who is literally already on the site trying to shop.

## What's working

- **The lifestyle room hero** (visible behind the modal): rattan pendant light, walnut-adjacent tones, marble table surface — this is exactly the mood I'm building my registry around. I feel seen.
- **"Tabletop & Bar" visible in the main nav** — one tap away from my primary shopping focus. No hunting.
- **Promo strip** with "Today's 20% off Living Accessories" is genuinely useful — accessories is a huge chunk of my registry.
- **"everyone's five-star favorite" headline** — casual, confident, reads like a friend who has good taste recommending something. Very millennial tone.
- **Rewards banner** in the top bar is sticky and clear; as someone who just registered, I want to know I'm earning something.

## What's weak

- **The email modal fires immediately** on page load and covers the entire above-the-fold experience — I came here to shop, not to negotiate. It's the first thing I interact with and it's a form.
- **Modal fine print is a wall of dense legalese** — "By signing up via text, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing text messages (e.g. 10 coupon) not necessarily from Crate and Barrel..." I'm tech-literate but this feels aggressive for a brand I already have a registry with.
- **No registry hook anywhere visible** — I *just registered* with Crate & Barrel. There's nothing on this page acknowledging that relationship: no "complete your registry," no "items on your wishlist," nothing. That's a missed conversion.
- **The hero is all sofas** — "Shop All Sofas & Sectionals" and "Shop In-Store Sofas." I care about dinnerware and accessories. The navigation gets me there, but the hero is pushing a category I'm less in-market for right now.
- **No trust signals**: no visible free-returns mention, no review counts on featured products, no "free shipping over $X" above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for known users / registry owners** — if I'm logged in or have an active registry, don't hit me with a sign-up form I can't use. Replace it with a registry progress bar or a "You have 14 items on your registry — shop your picks" CTA.
- **Add a "Registry Picks" or "Trending on Registries" rail** above the fold — this is a Registry-focused shopping moment for me and the page has no signal that CB even knows I'm in that mindset.
- **Surface free shipping threshold** in the promo strip — swap one of the outdoor furniture promos for a "Free shipping on orders $99+" line. It's the question I always have before I start filling a cart.
- **Move "Tabletop & Bar" or "Dinnerware" into a visual module** (not just a nav link) — a lifestyle shot of a laid table with the walnut-handled flatware would stop me mid-scroll.
