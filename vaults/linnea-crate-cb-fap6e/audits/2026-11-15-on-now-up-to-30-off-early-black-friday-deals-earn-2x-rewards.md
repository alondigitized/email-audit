# ON NOW! Up to 30% off Early Black Friday deals + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Early Black Friday send I've seen from Crate & Barrel in about two days. It follows "Ends soon: Early Black Friday deals + 2x Rewards" and "ON NOW! Up to 40% off top kitchen brands + Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars!", both from the same day. The structure is familiar. A "last Double Rewards this year" strip leads, then an "it's on! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" hero with a single "Shop Now" button. A 2x2 tile grid follows (entertaining, furniture, kitchen brands, holiday decor), then a kitchen deals block with priced products.
- The kitchen block is the strongest part of the email. It shows real sale prices against regular prices (espresso machine $1,099.95 vs. $1,999.95, cutlery $249.95 vs. $449.95) and carries a "Ships Free" flag. Below it the email loses focus. It adds a stockings promo, a Gift Shop banner, a repeated Double Rewards banner, a "we love these for you" product row, a credit card block, an app download and a design desk. That is eight or more competing asks in one scroll.
- The offer also got weaker. Earlier today the headline was "up to 40% off" kitchen brands and "up to 50% off", and now it is "up to 30%". A reader who has been following the run will notice the drop. Judgment: a competent, clear promo, but it is repetitive and bloated, and the subject line leans on a lower discount than the last three sends.

## What's working

- The "last Double Rewards this year" strip at the top gives a loyalty angle, with the Nov. 17 deadline visible.
- The kitchen deals block shows concrete sale prices against reg. prices, which makes the value easy to see.
- "Ships Free" is flagged on the kitchen and stocking sections.
- The 2x2 category grid (entertaining, furniture, kitchen brands, holiday decor) lets a reader self-segment quickly.
- The "Shop Early Black Friday Deals" and "Shop All Kitchen Deals" buttons use clear verb-plus-noun copy.

## What's weak

- Too many sections and CTAs after the kitchen block (stockings, Gift Shop, Rewards, Top Categories, credit card, app, design desk) dilute the main offer.
- The hero's "up to 30% off" is a smaller number than the "up to 40%" and "up to 50%" in the earlier sends. It also sits on a lifestyle photo with no product focus.
- The Double Rewards message appears twice, at the top and again mid-email, and it is the same message as the earlier sends.
- The dense grey fine print at the bottom and the small price text in "we love these for you" are hard to read.
- Some category tile labels sit on busy photography, and the label on the top-right tile has weak contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the kitchen block. Keep the hero, the grid, the kitchen deals and one Rewards banner. Move the app, credit card and design desk into the footer.
- 2. Pick one hero product with a price in the first screen, such as the espresso machine at $1,099.95 vs. $1,999.95, instead of a lifestyle room shot with "up to 30%".
- 3. Change the offer by day, not just the wording. Today's send could name the Nov. 17 deadline in the hero as well as the top strip.
- 4. Increase contrast on the tile labels and enlarge the price text in the product rows.
- 5. Suppress sends of this type if the subscriber got one in the last 12 hours, or merge them into a single daily send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Espresso machines $900 off + 2x Rewards ends Nov. 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: kitchen deals ship free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last Double Rewards of the year: earn 2x back on everything through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cookware, cutlery and espresso machines, all with free shipping.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with VML/MSO fallbacks. The main confirmed issues are about 30 images with missing alt text, a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom, and several third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay capture and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and link `href`s were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 73 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are five identical LiveRamp pixel requests, differing only in the `n` counter.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** the `s=` value `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8` matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` token on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. A recipient-level ID is being passed to a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`).
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7ec6e46e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are two separate first-party open beacons.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:**
  - 15 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (jpg, png, gif), for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6100bbef-…jpg` and the animated `70061691-…gif` and `24335f96-…gif`.
  - 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png` through `18f126218cc222ec.png`) look like dynamic product or recommendation tiles. If any are linked, they have no accessible name.
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`, also on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Pixels without alt:** the beacons (`o.gif`, `dv…/o/7ec6e46e…`, `448796.gif` ×5) should carry `alt=""`. The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` turn off iOS text resizing. The `body` rule sets it to `100%`, but the later `body { … -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` overrides it.
- **Conflicting duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` appears three or more times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags). `charset` is declared twice, in the `http-equiv` Content-Type tag and in `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
- **Contradictory media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, which also matches at 768px and wider. The 600px rule is dead code. The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, so viewports of 461–639px get no responsive rules.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. It also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient-level `mi_u` token is a tracking ID, not a visible merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing:** QA flagged it as not found.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing:** QA flagged this RFC 8058 header as not found too.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Likely a capture artifact:** QA itself notes these headers "may not be captured by AgentMail relay." The sender's behavior is therefore unconfirmed. The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) was cut off by truncation, so CAN-SPAM body requirements weren't assessed.
- **From domain:** the From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and images and links are on aligned subdomains of `crateandbarrel.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 73 tracking links went through click-redirect domains and were not resolved. No UTM parameters or final landing URLs were observed, and the links use `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` tracking parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 22 content images (15 + 7) and `alt=""` to the pixels and decorative images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` and `charset` tags and the dead 600px `img` rule. Fix the `&` in the title, delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, and consider a breakpoint between 461px and 639px.
4. Review the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls and the recipient-ID sharing for consent and privacy.
5. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw MIME message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Then re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML for the footer.
6. Resolve the redirect chains in a follow-up pass to confirm final landing URLs and any UTM parameters.
