# School starts SOON! Up to 20% off essentials 🎒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- The hero nails the assignment — "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off," backpacks, lunch boxes, duffel bags, pencil cases, food storage, water bottles, all logically grouped and legible at a glance. If Crate & Kids had stopped after the "focus zone" desk/chair module, this would be one of their tighter sends.
- It doesn't stop there. Past the desk section it pivots into a $5,000 TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, then a "50% off summer sale" nursery banner (crib, dresser — nothing school-related), then a "we love these for you" grid dominated by baby car seats, a glider chair, and a stroller, then a new-credit-card pitch, then an app-download banner dangling a separate "$500" chance, then a second TheDesignDesk CTA. This is the same failure mode flagged in the 2026-08-01 "Shop furniture you'll love now & forever" send and the 2026-07-31 sofa email — multiple unrelated campaigns stapled into one email under a single subject line.
- The back-to-school promise in the subject only covers the first third of the email. A parent opening for school-essentials deals scrolls into nursery furniture and sweepstakes copy that has nothing to do with why they clicked. Solid top, diluted bottom — 7/10.

## What's working

- Hero headline and subhead directly match the subject line ("back-to-school sale: up to 20% off") with a clear "Shop the Sale" button.
- The essentials grid (backpacks, lunch boxes, duffel bags, pencil cases, food storage, water bottles) is clean, evenly gridded, and exactly what a back-to-school shopper wants to see.
- One product shows an actual struck-through sale price ($54.99 → $42.25), giving the offer some concrete proof instead of just a banner claim.
- The "create a focus zone" desk/chair module is a smart adjacent upsell — still tied to school prep.

## What's weak

- The very top banner ("up to 50% off beds, storage & more") competes with the hero for attention before the reader even sees the back-to-school offer.
- The desk section stacks two CTAs back to back — "Shop the Sale" and "Shop All Gear" — with no visual distinction on which one to click.
- More than half the email (sweepstakes, nursery "summer sale," baby car seats/glider/stroller in "we love these for you," credit card pitch, app-download "$500" teaser) has zero connection to back-to-school shopping.
- No end date anywhere for the 20% off offer, so the "SOON" urgency in the subject isn't backed up by a visible deadline in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "focus zone" module. Everything from the sweepstakes banner onward belongs in separate sends — this is at least three campaigns (back-to-school, nursery/summer sale, sweepstakes/card acquisition) merged into one.
- 2. Drop or relocate the top "50% off beds, storage" banner — it steals the first-glance moment from the back-to-school hero the subject promised.
- 3. Fix the double-CTA in the desk module — pick one action ("Shop Desks & Chairs") instead of two stacked buttons.
- 4. Add a visible deadline near the hero offer ("Ends 8/6") to justify the "SOON" urgency in the subject.
- 5. If "we love these for you" is personalized, make sure the back-to-school shopper's grid actually shows school-adjacent items, not baby gear — right now it undercuts the whole premise of the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Backpacks, lunch boxes & more — 20% off before the bell rings`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, lunch boxes, desks & more — sale ends soon`
