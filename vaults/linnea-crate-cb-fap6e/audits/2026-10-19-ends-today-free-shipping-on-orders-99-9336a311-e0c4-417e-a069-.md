# ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear last-day free-shipping reminder with a weak top half. The subject promises "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+", but the first screen is a gray "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 50% off" banner over a large jack-o'-lantern pumpkin hero. The free-shipping headline only appears below that image. Two offers compete, and the urgent one sits in small type under a Halloween photo.
- Below the headline the email works. Six category tiles (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, halloween) and a "we love these for you" grid of priced products give a reader plenty to click. After that it drifts into a wallcovering launch, a "cozy winter edit," a credit-card pitch, an app download, TheDesignDesk and a store locator. It also repeats the free-shipping promo that ran twice on 10/18 ("Free shipping starts today!" and "2 days only!"). That is the third send of the same promo in three days, with a last-day label added.
- Judgment: right message, wrong order. The deadline should lead the email, and everything after the product grid should go.

## What's working

- The "ends today" headline plus "Shop All Free Shipping" gives a credible deadline and a clear action.
- The six-tile category grid is easy to scan and lets the reader pick a category.
- The product grid shows real names and prices, such as the $34.95 pumpkin treat bag, the $1,499 Jenny Lind dresser and the $295.20 Naturepedic mattress. That helps the click decision.
- Nothing is broken. Images render cleanly and the layout holds together.
- The Halloween imagery and the winter/holiday bedding edit fit the date.

## What's weak

- The hero image, a pumpkin with no offer or CTA, takes the most space while the deadline sits below it in modest type. This is the main miss.
- "Up to 50% off" and "free shipping $99+" split attention, and "Standard shipping, exclusions apply" plus the dense fine print make the offer feel hedged.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is a pale gray-blue with low contrast, and it appears twice.
- The email is very long. The wallcovering collection, credit card, app, DesignDesk and store locator dilute a deadline send.
- There is no personalization, and no visible loyalty or member benefit beyond the credit-card pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: a bold "ENDS TODAY: free shipping on $99+" banner over the image, with a dark, high-contrast button right there. Cut the nursery-event banner or move it below.
- 2. Cut everything after the product grid except the footer. A last-day email should end at the products.
- 3. Add a visible countdown or "ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" line, since the 10/18 send already used a timer.
- 4. Make the free-shipping terms a one-line summary above the fold with a "details" link, and move the long legal block lower.
- 5. Suppress or space this send from the 10/18 duplicates. Three near-identical promos in three days trains people to ignore the brand.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping on $99+ ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, storage and nursery picks ship free until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by tonight and skip shipping on $99+, plus up to 50% off the Bedroom & Nursery Event.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 header warnings, plus about 30 alt-text warnings. The HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s could not be inspected. The compliance and continuity findings below are therefore limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click-tracking links:** 71 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking/redirect domains. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` requests are in the body. This is a LiveRamp pixel. The same `s=` hash is fired five times, and only the `n` counter differs. This is redundant, and it adds third-party requests and a persistent identifier to every open.
- **First-party tracking:** Open and tracking pixels are at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/471f6eb6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are normal for the ESP, but they carry a per-recipient `mi_u` hash in the URL.
- **Repeated image:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times, probably as recommendation placeholders.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 30 `<img>` tags have no alt attribute. The QA lists them all as warnings.
  - **Content images (real accessibility issue):** These are the 14 or so `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` images. They also include the Scene7 `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FCozyWinterEdit%5FTertiaryVideo` hero/video still. If these carry the offer or product content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - **Spacer and pixel images:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `o.gif`, `448796.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute. Without it, some screen readers read out the filename.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype should be `&amp;`. Most clients tolerate it.
- **Template leftovers and duplicates:** The `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` placeholder comment shipped. There are redundant `format-detection` metas (`telephone`, `address` and `date` are each declared twice) and both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>`. None of these cause errors.
- **Conflicting image rules:** Two `img {max-width}` rules apply at 768px and above (`600px` and `640px`). The later one wins, so the `600px` rule is dead code.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` is declared and no dark-mode styles are visible in the excerpt. This is a deliberate choice, not a defect.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set, and VML/Outlook (MSO) conditional fixes are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the excerpt. The remainder of the body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers are reported as not found. The QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. If the relay does strip them, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirement cannot be verified from this capture.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender and tracking domains are `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`. These are consistent subdomains of one organizational domain, which is favorable for alignment, but this cannot be confirmed without headers.
- **Footer:** The physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. The 71 tracking links were not resolved, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the excerpt. Nothing here shows a continuity problem, and nothing confirms continuity either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive alt text to every content image from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the Scene7 video still. Add `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Duplicate pixels:** Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls to one.
4. **Title and comments:** Escape `&` in the `<title>`, and strip template-placeholder comments and duplicate metas from the build.
5. **Capture gaps:** Capture the raw `.eml` with full headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also re-run the audit on untruncated HTML, and resolve a sample of the 71 tracking links to verify landing pages and UTM parameters.
