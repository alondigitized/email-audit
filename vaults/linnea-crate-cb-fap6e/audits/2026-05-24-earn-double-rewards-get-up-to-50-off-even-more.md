# Earn Double Rewards + get up to 50% off even more!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids is closing out the Memorial Day push with another "ends today" wrapper on the exact same 2x Rewards + up to 50% off creative they've been recycling since 5/21. The hero is a styled nursery dresser scene, followed by the headline event copy, a tile grid of rugs/bedding, a secondary "Double Rewards is the best time to shop" callout with a crib, then a "we love these for you" product row and an app/Designcrew footer. It renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, the layout holds together.
- The fundamental problem: this is now at least the fourth send carrying this exact promo in five days, and it lands the day AFTER the same brand family sent "FINAL WEEKEND" on 5/23. "Ends today" after "Final weekend" reads as urgency theater. For a nursery-focused persona the creative is on-target — cribs, dressers, bedding — but the offer is identical to what I've already been told is ending three different times this week.
- This is a competent send executing a tired playbook. Solid creative on a stale beat.

## What's working

- Nursery-relevant hero (dresser styled with crib/decor) lands the category fast for a baby-focused subscriber.
- Clean visual hierarchy: logo → hero → headline → tile grid → secondary offer → product row.
- Product tiles show actual category breadth (rugs, bedding, furniture) rather than one SKU.
- "ends today" badge gives a real deadline, not vague "shop now."
- No render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Fourth near-identical 2x Rewards + 50% off send in five days — fatigue is real.
- "Ends today" lands the day AFTER "FINAL WEEKEND" from the same brand — credibility hit.
- Subject ("up to 50% off even more!") is vague — "even more" than what?
- No specific hero product price or featured SKU — just a generic event banner.
- Two stacked "Double Rewards" callouts feel redundant within one email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the duplicate "Double Rewards" callout block.** The hero already says it. Replace the second one with a specific nursery bundle (crib + dresser + glider at X% off) so the email has at least one concrete hook.
- 2. **Lead with a hero SKU and a price.** "Hampshire 3-Drawer Dresser, $XXX (was $YYY)" beats another event banner — especially on the final day.
- 3. **Drop "even more" from the subject.** It's filler that signals "we've sent this before."
- 4. **Make the "ends today" feel earned** — add a cutoff time ("ends tonight 11:59 PT") rather than a generic badge, since subscribers have heard "final" twice already this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 2x Rewards on the crib + dresser`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — 50% off nursery + Double Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Memorial Day savings close at midnight. Shop cribs, dressers, bedding.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 2x Rewards with up to 50% off — last call on nursery essentials.`
