# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is in full Memorial Day mode — the entire above-the-fold experience is pushing outdoor furniture and Reward Dollars. As someone who just registered and is deep in the tabletop/walnut rabbit hole, this feels like I walked into the right store but got handed a patio catalog. The bones are right — Tabletop & Bar is in the nav, Reward Dollars are loud, and the sale is real — but a modal interrupted me before I even saw the hero.

## What's working

- **Reward Dollars banner (top strip):** "Earn 10% → 20% Back in Reward Dollars! Ends 5/26/2026" is immediately actionable for me — I want to bank points on registry buys.
- **"Tabletop & Bar" in the nav:** One tap to my exact category, no hunting.
- **Memorial Day timing:** The sale is real and current — 60% off outdoor and 30% off furniture are numbers worth clicking for.
- **Modal offer (10% off full-price items):** Honestly, as a full-price-tolerant registry shopper, 10% off full-price is the one promo that moves me. The hook is right even if the delivery is annoying.
- **Clean typographic hierarchy below the modal:** The three-column module (Memorial Day Event / Lounge Sofa / Breathe Towel) reads fast and gives me category signals.

## What's weak

- **Modal on arrival:** The SMS/email capture popup covers the entire hero. I haven't seen the product yet and I'm already being asked for my phone number. "Maybe Later" is small. This is friction before value.
- **Hero is 100% outdoor furniture:** My registry has zero patio items. The walnut side tables and minimal dinnerware I actually want are nowhere above the fold.
- **No registry entry point visible:** There's no "Complete Your Registry" nudge, no "Back In Stock" on my saved items, no "Registry" shortcut above the fold beyond a tiny icon in the top bar.
- **Reward Dollars tiers are vague in the featured cards:** "Earn $400+ in Reward Dollars" on the Lounge Sofa — what does that mean per dollar? The mechanic isn't clear to a newer member.
- **No trust signals or social proof:** No review counts, no "as seen in," no "free shipping on orders over $X" above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the modal for a banner-style opt-in** — slide a 10%-off bar in from the bottom or anchor it to the nav. Stop blocking the hero on first load.
- **Add a registry quick-entry module below the hero** — "Pick up where you left off → Your Registry" with a CTA and 2–3 recently-viewed items; this is table stakes for a registry-registered shopper.
- **Run a tabletop/dinnerware lane in the featured module row** — one of the three below-hero columns should be pulling my actual category (Tabletop & Bar, Serveware, or a "Walnut Accents" editorial), not just outdoor + towels.
- **Make the Reward Dollars mechanic legible** — add one line: "Earn $1 for every $10 spent" so a new Rewards member (me) immediately understands the value without clicking through.
