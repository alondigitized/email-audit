# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I'd expect Crate & Barrel to look like — elevated, warm, and very much in that "natural materials + earth tones" moment I'm obsessed with right now. The hero is all about a sofa system, which isn't my primary mission (I'm here for dinnerware and registry pieces), but the overall aesthetic is completely on-brand for the lifestyle I'm building. Right now the site is pushing furniture hard, with a secondary nudge toward kitchen and entertaining lower on the page.

## What's working

- **"Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" topbar strip** — catches my eye immediately as a recently registered shopper who wants to maximize every purchase
- **Free shipping urgency banner ("3 days left!")** — the countdown language actually makes me want to act now rather than tab-switch and forget
- **"in stock & ready to ship" module** — this is brilliant for me; I'm building a registry and need to know things actually exist and can be gifted. Coffee tables starting at $699 and dining tables at $799 are visible price anchors
- **Hero lifestyle photography** — walnut side tables, linen sofa, pendant lighting — this is my exact mood board. Even if I'm not buying a sofa today, the vibe keeps me on the page
- **Sub-nav category row** — Tabletop, Kitchen, Bath, Decor, Bedding all visible in one tap. I can get to my category instantly

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% furniture, zero tabletop/dinnerware** — I came for registry pieces and the hero sends me the wrong direction. "Everyone's five-star favorite" refers to a sofa I'm not buying
- **Wedding Registry CTA is nav-buried** — it's a text link in the top nav, not a hero feature. For someone actively building a registry, this should be louder
- **"Shop Furniture & Decor" and "Shop Kitchen & Entertaining" CTAs are nearly the same visual weight** — Kitchen/Entertaining is what I actually want but it reads as secondary
- **Loyalty program detail is nearly invisible** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars. Terms Apply." is tiny gray text at the very top. I'm full-price tolerant so rewards matter more to me than discounts, and this treatment undersells it
- **No product-level imagery in my category above the fold** — the "in stock & ready to ship" grid shows sofas, coffee tables, and dining tables. No dinnerware, bowls, or serving pieces in view

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Registry hub in the hero or a dedicated hero slot** — a split hero rotating between "furnish your home" and "build your registry" would catch me the second I land, not after I hunt through the nav
- **Promote the Tabletop / Kitchen category rail above the "in stock" module** — swap one of those furniture tiles for a ceramic bowl or place setting so shoppers like me see our category immediately
- **Give the Rewards program a proper callout block** — a "10% back on every purchase — join free" badge near the header or as a sticky strip would make the value proposition land for full-price shoppers like me
- **Add a free-shipping threshold number to the urgency strip** — "3 days left! free ship on orders $35+" (the fine print says exclusions apply but I can't read the threshold) would make me calculate my cart instead of hesitating
