## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks. It has broad alt-text gaps (about 30 images flagged), and every link goes through a click-redirect domain. The compliance and authentication QA warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped 62 tracking links as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were probed. Landing-page validity and UTM presence are unverified.
- Several tracking pixels are embedded:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2c92bf82-5fa3-49d5-9156-68f469593eee?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` calls. These are LiveRamp third-party sync pixels, and the `s=` value is a hashed identifier.
- The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier is repeated across the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. These are dynamic recommendation images, and each carries the per-recipient token in its URL.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm link-level issues such as empty hrefs or `http://` links in the body.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged about 30 images. Most are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`. They include `a2041dbe-…gif`, `23a06901-…gif` and `123cb01b-…gif`, which may be animated. Seven are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product-recommendation images. If these are product tiles with no alt text, screen-reader and images-off users get no product information.
- The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `rlcdn 448796.gif` ×5, `dv…/o/2c92bf82-…`) also lack alt. For 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix and is low severity.
- `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared repeatedly, with conflicting forms (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`). This is redundant markup, not a functional break.
- The preheader template contains the leftover comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. This is an unfilled template placeholder (a typo for "INPUT") and means no web font is loaded.
- The global rule `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` forces centered text on all paragraphs unless overridden.
- The global rule `*{… line-height:100%}` applies a 100% line-height to every element. This can cause text to clip or crowd for users who enlarge text.
- `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` conflict. Because the 640px rule comes later, it wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap never applies.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.
- `mi_u=` hashed IDs and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated, so personalization resolved. The HTML was truncated, so the body copy was not checked for tokens.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found, and `Authentication-Results` is absent. QA itself says the relay may strip these headers, so these are unconfirmed rather than proven defects. Check the raw message headers before reporting a violation.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion of the HTML. I can't confirm or deny them from this data.
- The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a branded subdomain. This is consistent with a dedicated authenticated sending domain, but SPF, DKIM and DMARC can't be verified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Not verifiable. All 62 links are wrapped in redirects that were not resolved, so the final URLs and any UTM parameters are unknown.
- The subject ("On the sofa hunt?") can't be matched to a landing page without the resolved destination of the primary CTA.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` to every product, hero and recommendation image (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/1/*`). Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta so users can zoom.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE …]` template comment.
4. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and resolve the conflicting `img{max-width}` media queries.
5. Replace the global `*{line-height:100%}` with scoped rules.
6. Re-run QA with raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, rather than treating the relay-related warnings as failures.
7. Run the link probe against the redirect chains (follow the 62 tracking links) to verify final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status, and review the full, untruncated HTML for the footer and unsubscribe link.
