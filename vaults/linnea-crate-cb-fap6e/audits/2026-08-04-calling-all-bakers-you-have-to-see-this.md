# Calling all bakers: you have to see this 🍪
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday's "The best collab since 🍪 & 🥛 JUST hit our app" (2026-08-03) already announced this exact Levain Bakery collab and scored 8.00. Today's send is the same launch, re-run a day later — the founders' story, the cookie jar hero, the "bring the famous bakery home" line are all functionally the same beat, just packaged into a longer email with a recipe guide bolted on.
- Judged purely on production quality, this is genuinely strong work: moody hero photography, a numbered "guide to cookie perfection," a named product grid, and a brownie recipe as a low-pressure secondary CTA. But the email doesn't know when to stop — after the collab story wraps, it tacks on a Dutch oven discount, an Aspen collection sale, a new credit card pitch, an app download push, and a DesignDesk consult ask, none of which have anything to do with "bakers."
- Net judgement: high-craft creative undercut by same-day repetition and scope creep. The subject promises bakers something to see; the email delivers that and then five other unrelated offers.

## What's working

- Hero shot is genuinely appetizing — cookies, milk glasses, and the branded jar staged like an actual bakery counter, with "Shop the Collab" front and center.
- "Levain Bakery's guide to cookie perfection" (cold butter, supersize to 6 oz, etc.) gives real content value instead of a pure sell.
- The 3x2 "more from our new collab" grid names each SKU (Spatula, Bench Scraper, Cookie Cutting Board, Cake Stand, Bread Basket, Cookie Plate), making browsing effortless.
- Brownie recipe tease with its own "Get the Recipe" CTA is a smart secondary path for readers not ready to buy.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breaks across the full length.

## What's weak

- This is a same-collab repeat of yesterday's 2026-08-03 send — no new news, no restock/urgency angle, just the identical launch resurfaced 24 hours later.
- The hero CTA ("Shop the Collab") carries zero price incentive, while the real discounts in the email ($135 off Le Creuset Dutch ovens, 15% off Aspen) belong to completely unrelated product lines.
- The email sprawls to over 11,000px, pivoting from "bakers" into cookware sales, a new credit card, app download, and a design consultation — diluting the subject's promise.
- Subject line ("you have to see this 🍪") is vague clickbait with no concrete offer, product, or urgency to justify the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend the identical collab launch two days running — if a follow-up send is warranted, lead with something new (first-week reviews, a restock alert, a launch bundle) instead of repeating the same hero.
- 2. Give the collab itself an incentive (e.g., "free cookie jar with $75+ collab purchase") so the hero CTA isn't purely aspirational — right now the only real discounts in the email are for unrelated products.
- 3. Cut the email after the brownie recipe CTA; move Dutch oven/Aspen/credit-card/app content to a separate send so the subject and body both stay on-promise for bakers.
- 4. Replace the vague subject with something concrete:
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Levain Bakery tools: shop cookie jars, spatulas & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Levain x Crate&Barrel collab is finally here 🍪`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Founder-designed bakeware, plus our guide to Levain's perfect cookie`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `6-oz cookies, cold butter, and 4 more secrets from Levain's bakers`
- 5. Add a proof point (a rating, a review quote, "sold out in 24 hours") near the hero grid — nothing in the email substantiates "famous bakery" beyond the brand name itself.
