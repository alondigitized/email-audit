# Black Friday just got even BETTER! Shop up to 50% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh or eighth Crate & Barrel Black Friday blast in about three days, and the email itself is solid. The hero ("Biggest sale of the year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping") sits on a deep red background with a single "Shop Now" button. A grid of category tiles follows, each with its own percent-off tag. It's clean and easy to scan.
- The problem is the subject line, "Black Friday just got even BETTER!" It promises news but delivers the same "up to 50% off" as the 11/25 "NEW ITEMS ADDED!" send. It also repeats the "Biggest Sale of the Year!" hero. The only visible new content is a Shun knife and Breville/Philips kitchen block, a gift-shop banner and a "We love these for you" strip. Nothing in the render explains what is "better."
- Judgement: a competent catch-all that adds to the fatigue. It will get opens from sheer brand familiarity, but the subject overpromises and the body has no new hook.
- Caveat: the screenshot is 680x10421 and I viewed it heavily downscaled. I can judge layout, hierarchy and module structure, but not fine print or small product copy.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a large offer headline, "free shipping" stacked under it and one button.
- The category grid ("up to 40% off sofas," "up to 60% off kitchen brands," and so on) gives lots of entry points without making you read much.
- The kitchen block ("up to 40% off top Black Friday kitchen deals") names recognizable products, including Shun cutlery, Ninja, Philips and Le Creuset-style cookware. That is more concrete than the generic tiles.
- The Gift Shop banner and the "best kitchen gifts from brands they love" module fit the season.
- Several "Shop Black Friday Sale" buttons repeat down the page, so there is always a CTA nearby.

## What's weak

- The subject says things got "even BETTER," but the hero offer is the same up-to-50% as the earlier sends. Nothing in the render supports the claim.
- Everything is "up to" with no floor, so the real discount is unclear. The dense legal block at the bottom adds to that.
- The page is very long, with 8+ modules. The kitchen deals, gift shop, "we love these for you," a Crate Card promo, an app download and a design-appointment promo all compete.
- The offer has no deadline, so there's no urgency beyond the generic "Black Friday."
- The sender name, "Crate & Barrel BLACK FRIDAY," uses all caps, and the subject adds "BETTER!" and an arrow. Together that reads more like a shouty promo than a trusted brand.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match one concrete new thing. If kitchen is the new content, lead with that offer. If nothing is new, drop "even BETTER."
- 2. Cut the body to three or four modules: hero, the best category grid, one hero product block, and a footer. The Crate Card, app and design-appointment promos belong in a different send.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "ends tonight at midnight" or a countdown, since this is the last Black Friday weekend send.
- 4. State the realistic range in the hero, for example "30-50% off" instead of "up to 50%."
- 5. Vary the creative. This send looks nearly identical to the earlier ones, so a subscriber has little reason to treat it as different.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Shun, Ninja & Philips: Black Friday prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on everything through midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Knives, espresso, cookware and gift sets, all marked down.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "Black Friday just got even BETTER!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has structurally sound XHTML with `lang="en"` and VML/MSO fallbacks, but at least 38 images have no alt text and the viewport meta blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the QA relay did not capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **79 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and landing-page status are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5`. They share one recipient-hash `s=` value and differ only in `n`. Each is an extra third-party request, and none has alt text.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and the open pixel `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`. This is standard tracking, but it means the images are recipient-specific and not cacheable.
- **Further tracking pixel:** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a268ceb3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (at least 38 images, the QA list appears cut off):**
  - 23 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `61581a40-…gif`.
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - 5 `rlcdn` pixels, the `o.gif` pixel, and the `dv.` pixel.

  The 23 content images are the real accessibility problem. With images blocked, the email will render as empty blocks and its links will have no accessible name.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Image-width CSS conflicts:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` shipped to production.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: one combined and two separate.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header found** (QA WARN).
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header found** (RFC 8058 one-click, QA WARN).
- **No `Authentication-Results` header found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- All three warnings are flagged by QA as likely relay artifacts of AgentMail, so they are unconfirmed rather than confirmed sender defects. A direct-delivery test is needed to settle them.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the footer, which was truncated, so they could not be checked.

The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and image and tracking hosts are branded subdomains. That is consistent with aligned sending infrastructure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The click-redirect domains were not resolved, and no final destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the available source or QA data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 23 content images and the 7 `mi.../rp/*.png` images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries so the intended 600px or 640px cap applies.
4. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, and strip the template placeholder comments and duplicate meta tags.
5. Consolidate the five identical `rlcdn` pixels into one, unless the `n=` variants are intentional.
6. Re-test through direct delivery, for example a seed list, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
7. Follow the 79 redirect links with a redirect-aware probe to verify final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
