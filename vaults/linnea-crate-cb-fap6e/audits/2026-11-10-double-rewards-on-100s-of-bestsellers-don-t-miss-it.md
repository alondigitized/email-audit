# Double Rewards on 100s of bestsellers. Don’t miss it!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, well-built Crate & Barrel Kids promo, but it repeats the offer more than once. Note that the screenshot was heavily downscaled, so I'm judging layout, hierarchy and headline copy. I can't read the fine print or small product text. A banner reads "don't miss out: the fall sales event: up to 50% off". A "last Double Rewards this year!" block with Shop Now / Apply Now buttons sits under a moody nursery hero. Below that are three themed product grids (bedroom, nursery, playroom), each ending in a single category CTA.
- The Double Rewards message appears twice (top and mid-email), and the sale banner is a third pass at the same ask. That reads as emphasis, but it also makes a deadline-driven email feel padded. After the product grids come a nursery chairs feature, a gifting block, a "we love these for you" strip, a card promo, an app promo with a $500 hook and a design-services plug. It's a catalog, not a campaign.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand send with a clear core offer. It's diluted by length and by the other Crate & Barrel sends of the past two days. The 11/10 Fall Sales Event emails, and the 11/9 "Dinnerware… + 2x Rewards" email, hit nearly the same beats.

## What's working

- The banner and hero state the offer up top: up to 50% off, plus Double Rewards.
- The product grids are cleanly themed (bedroom, nursery, playroom), each with its own CTA, such as "Shop Bedroom Furniture".
- The seasonal, lifestyle photography is consistent and feels premium.
- The red "last Double Rewards this year!" block stands out from the neutral imagery, so the loyalty angle is easy to find.
- Product tiles carry rewards callouts ("Earn X% back…"), which ties the loyalty offer to specific items.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards message is repeated, and the sale banner makes a third pass at the same ask. The email feels redundant.
- The length is excessive. Nursery chairs, gifting, the app, the card and design services all compete after the main offer.
- The two top buttons (Shop Now, Apply Now) split attention between shopping and credit-card sign-up.
- The offer is "up to" 50% and rewards-based, and the rewards math appears only in small type. That makes the value hard to judge at a glance.
- This is another near-identical send from the same brand, after the 11/10 Fall Sales Event emails and the 11/9 Double Rewards mention. Cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer story: sale + Double Rewards, shown once up top and once at the bottom. Drop the app, design-service and card-promo modules, or move them to a separate send.
- 2. Make the primary CTA a single "Shop the Sale". Move "Apply Now" lower, or into the footer area.
- 3. Put a plain-language rewards example in the hero ("Spend $500, earn $X back") so the value is obvious.
- 4. Space out sends. Merge the Fall Sales Event and Double Rewards messages into one communication per day.
- 5. Add a real deadline in the hero (Nov. 17) rather than relying on the generic "last Double Rewards this year".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double back on cribs, bunk beds and playroom picks through Nov. 17.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook (MSO) conditionals and 112 click-tracking links. The provided evidence shows widespread missing `alt` text and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **112 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Five repeated third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are the same LiveRamp-domain pixel with an `s=` hash parameter, fired five times. It is likely one per template section, which is redundant.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` URLs carry `mi_u=`. The same value is reused in the `rlcdn.com` `s=` parameter and in the `o.gif` open pixel. Anyone who forwards or archives the email carries that identifier with it.
- **Open pixels.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3a3da172-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open-tracking beacons. Neither has an `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Dynamic image reuse.** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times with the same `mi_u` value. Either the images are identical, or the response varies on parameters that were truncated from the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged about 55 images, and the content images are all hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`. Examples are `a13bcc00-…jpg`, `ad301dcd-…gif`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif` and `f9868e75-…gif`.
  - The scene7 GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif` is also flagged.
  - The Movable Ink dynamic images (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6) are also flagged.
  - These are the likely product and promo images. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the content is invisible.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text) on mobile.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` are applied via `*{…}` and in `body`. This stops user font scaling in some clients.
- **Duplicate and redundant head tags.**
  - Two charset declarations (`<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, and the first declaration already covers the other two.
  - `<style>` blocks carry the same `.ReadMsgBody` and `table td` rules twice.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid XHTML. It should be `&amp;`. The title also does not match the subject line.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and the empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }` remain in the shipped HTML.
- **Large images.** The `img {max-width:640px}` rule, the 460px mobile breakpoint, and `.showmobile{width:414px}` may produce inconsistent scaling on viewports between 460px and 640px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible portion. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values appear to be resolved. The truncated remainder was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are absent at the sender, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 112 tracked links were not resolved, and no UTM parameters are visible in the provided source. Landing-page alignment with "Double Rewards" is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to the pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`, `rlcdn.com/448796.gif`) and to the dynamic Movable Ink images where appropriate.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` where possible.
3. Collapse the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixel calls into one. Review the third-party identifier sharing against the privacy policy.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and align it with the subject line. Remove the duplicate meta and style rules and the leftover placeholder comment.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay, and cover the footer address and unsubscribe link. Also follow the redirect chains on a sample of the 112 tracked links to check UTMs and landing pages.
