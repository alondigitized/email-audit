# Up to 25% off bedroom furniture, bedding & more →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's bedroom furniture sale — "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" with a genuinely handsome canopy bed hero and priced product (Coveteur Upholstered Canopy Bed, $1,599 reg $2,299). The category grid underneath is clean and legible: beds, dressers, bedding, decor & lighting, each with its own discount tier. Then it does what every recent Crate & Barrel send does — cascades into Design Desk sweepstakes, a clearance banner, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a store-locator footer.
- The top third is the strongest bedroom-category email from this brand in the recent run — better than the Lupita Design Desk send (2026-08-22) because the hero offer is unambiguous and on-topic instead of buried under editorial content. But the kitchen-sink problem persists: by the time a reader hits the KitchenAid mixer and beverage tub in "we love these for you," this has stopped being a bedroom email and started being everything-in-the-catalog again, same complaint as the clearance send from earlier today.
- Verdict: strong top-of-funnel, diluted by mid-email sprawl. Worth opening, worth clicking the hero, but the CTA doesn't stay focused long enough to earn a second click deeper in.

## What's working

- Hero offer is immediate and specific: "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" with a real price and strikethrough ($1,599 vs $2,299) directly under the image.
- Category grid (beds/dressers/bedding/decor) is well-organized with a discount tier per category, so a shopper can jump straight to what they need.
- Two clear stacked CTAs — "Shop Bedroom Sale" and "Shop All Bedroom" — both sit right after the offer grid, no scrolling required to find them.
- No render bugs: images loaded cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- "we love these for you" grid includes a KitchenAid stand mixer and a beverage tub — neither is bedroom, breaking the subject line's promise and diluting focus.
- Design Desk sweepstakes and clearance banner sit between the bedroom offer and the "recommended" grid, meaning a reader has to scroll past two unrelated campaigns to get back to the category the subject sold them on.
- Credit card pitch and app download blocks add more non-bedroom asks before the footer — same sprawl pattern flagged in the 2026-08-22 Design Desk audit.
- "up to 60% off bedding" and "up to 60% off decor & lighting" tiles use a bigger discount than the 25% headline, which undercuts the subject line's number as the best deal in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bedroom" or replace the Design Desk/clearance/mixer detour with more bedroom-specific cross-sells (nightstands, headboards, throw pillows) — keep the whole send on-category to match the subject's promise.
- 2. Lead the subject/hero with the 60%-off bedding and decor numbers instead of 25%, since that's the stronger offer visible in the grid and currently undersells itself.
- 3. Move the credit card and app pitches to the very bottom, after any remaining product content, so they don't compete with the shopping CTAs.

- Template uses standard MSO conditional comments, `max-width` media queries, and Outlook/webmail resets — no structural rendering defects detected in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) detected in the visible source. Cannot confirm absence in the truncated portion of the HTML.

- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
