## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` dynamic images) with about 47 images that have no `alt` attribute, plus 85 click-tracking links that couldn't be probed. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay not exposing headers, so they're unverified rather than confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so only the `<head>` and QA output could be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 85 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so none were checked for status, redirect chain or destination.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` are the same LiveRamp pixel repeated five times. The `s=` value is 64 hex characters, which looks like a hashed identifier. That's inferred from the format, not confirmed.
- **Personalised URLs:** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` is on every Movable Ink URL (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`) and on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/efe9e66e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
- **Plain-text part:** 17,416 of 24,430 characters (71.3%) are URLs, which is just over the 70% threshold. That is a mild spam-filter signal and follows from the long tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 47 images):**
  - 33 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `b3b8db9b-…gif`, `d077ef7e-…jpg`). Screen readers and image-blocking clients get no text for these.
  - 7 dynamic images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png` and others).
  - The remaining 7 are tracking pixels (5 × `rlcdn` `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…/o/efe9e66e-…`). These should carry `alt=""`.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid markup in `<title>`:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Leftover placeholder:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production source.
- **Duplicate `<meta>` tags:** `format-detection` appears three times, and both `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>` are present. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Overly broad CSS:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply globally, so any block that doesn't override them inherits centred text with `line-height:100%`.
- **Conflicting `img` rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and above. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: there are no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%` or `[[ ]]` tokens, and the `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The body past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. QA notes the relay may strip these headers, so they can't be called missing. Check them against the raw `.eml` or the original delivery headers.
- **Not confirmed:** the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated part of the HTML. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign but not proof of authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 85 links are click-redirect URLs, so the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are hidden behind the redirects. Resolving them with a real GET (not the HTTP probe the QA run skipped) would show whether the landing pages match the "bedroom refresh" subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all 40 content and dynamic images. Use `alt=""` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>` and remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags.
4. Re-run the QA against raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Follow the 85 redirects to extract final URLs and UTM parameters, and add that to the QA pipeline.
6. Consider this a low-priority item: the five identical `rlcdn` pixels are redundant, and the plain-text part could be trimmed to bring it under 70% URLs.
