# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is pushing Memorial Day hard — outdoor furniture, sofas, sectionals — and there's a rewards program front and center that I actually care about. But I barely got to see any of it, because the second the page loaded I got hit with a full-screen "Save 10% off full-price items" email capture modal that ate the entire above-the-fold experience. As someone who literally just registered here, the page is pitching me on the brand when it should already know I'm sold.

## What's working

- **Reward Dollars announcement bar** — the "Earn 10%, 20% Back" tiered headline is the clearest loyalty hook I've seen on a C&B visit. As someone who's about to drop serious registry money, that math matters to me.
- **Memorial Day event framing** — "Ends 5/26/2026" gives the sale a credible deadline. I know this weekend is real.
- **Category nav** — "Tabletop & Bar" and "Kitchen" are visible without scrolling. One tap to where I live on this site.
- **Product tiles at the bottom** — even through the modal clutter, I can see the Memorial Day event tiles with merchandise imagery. They look clean and proportional, which is on-brand for the minimal aesthetic I'm here for.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the whole first impression** — "Save 10% off full-price items*" with a mandatory email field and an optional phone field launches before I've seen a single product. I'm a new registrant — I already gave C&B my email. This is friction, not welcome.
- **Outdoor sofa focus doesn't speak to my registry** — the lifestyle imagery behind the modal is all outdoor lounge seating. My list is dinnerware, bar tools, and the occasional walnut accent piece. I feel like I landed on the wrong department's homepage.
- **No registry-specific hook anywhere above the fold** — C&B has a killer registry program. There's no "Complete your registry" CTA, no "Your registry has X items on sale this weekend" message. That's a miss for someone in my exact lifecycle moment.
- **"Shop Furniture Sale" and "Shop All Memorial Day" CTAs are competing without a clear winner** — two soft-blue buttons side by side with nearly identical hierarchy. Neither one pulls me decisively.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the email modal by 30 seconds / one scroll** — or, better, suppress it entirely for logged-in users and known registrants. I came here to browse, not to re-subscribe.
- **Add a "Your Registry — 12 items on sale this weekend" banner** for recognized accounts — this is a Memorial Day sale and registrants are the highest-intent shoppers on the site right now.
- **Swap the above-the-fold hero from outdoor sofas to a mixed-use tabletop + living room lifestyle shot** — show the walnut dining table laid with minimal white stoneware. That image sells to me *and* the furniture shopper simultaneously.
- **Elevate the Reward Dollars announcement bar copy** — make the tier structure scannable in two seconds: "Spend $250 → earn 10% back. Spend $500 → earn 20%." Right now the bar teases but doesn't close.
