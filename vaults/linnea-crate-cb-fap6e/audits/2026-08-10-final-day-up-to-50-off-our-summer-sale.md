# FINAL DAY: Up to 50% off our summer sale→
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' final-day push on the summer sale, and it's a workhorse email, not a showcase one. The top banner does the actual work — "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off" stacked against "up to 50% off summer sale" in the hero — cramming two offers into the first fold, which is more confusing than urgent. There's no countdown clock, no "ends tonight" language beyond the subject line, and the nursery hero image, while pretty, doesn't communicate urgency at all.
- Below the fold it turns into a standard catalog scroll: category tiles, a rug grid, fall arrivals, personalization, a "we love these for you" product block, a credit card pitch, and an app download CTA. That's a lot of asks stacked into one email — sale, fall preview, personalization, credit card, app, showroom visit — and it dilutes the "final day" urgency the subject line promised. Compare this to the 2026-08-09 "up to 60% off clearance" send, which stayed disciplined with one hero offer and a clean category grid; this one sprawls.
- Judgement: functional but unfocused. The final-day urgency lives only in the subject line and never shows up visually in the body, which is the single biggest miss.

## What's working

- Clear "up to 50% off summer sale" hero headline with legible sans-serif type against a clean lifestyle nursery photo.
- Two stacked CTA buttons (Shop the Sale / Shop New Arrivals) give the reader an immediate choice without scrolling.
- Category tile grid (furniture, kids bedding, baby bedding, decor, rugs, clearance) is well-organized with consistent image sizing and "Ships free" callouts where relevant.
- "We love these for you" section shows real product photography with prices and star-rating placeholders, which supports late-funnel clicks.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, spacing is consistent throughout.

## What's weak

- No visual urgency anywhere in the body — no countdown, no "ends tonight," no red/orange urgency color — despite "FINAL DAY" in the subject line.
- Two competing offers up top (20% off back-to-school banner vs. 50% off summer sale hero) muddy the primary message in the first fold.
- Email tries to do too much: sale, fall arrivals, personalization upsell, credit card pitch, and app download all compete for attention in one send.
- The credit card and app sections push the actual sale CTA far down the page — by the time a reader gets there, the "final day" framing has been forgotten.
- "up to 50%" and "up to 20%" are vague ceiling offers with no visible product-level proof of the discount depth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the urgency where the eye actually lands — add a "Final Day — Ends Tonight" banner directly under/over the hero headline, ideally with a countdown or bold red treatment, so the promise in the subject line shows up immediately in the body.
- 2. Cut the top banner's competing 20% off callout or merge it with the hero — one offer, one number, one headline.
- 3. Move the credit card and app-download blocks to the bottom, after "we love these for you," so the sale CTAs stay closer to the top of the scroll.
- 4. Add a specific price or percentage on at least one hero product ("Cribs from $XXX," "up to 50% off cribs & dressers") to make the discount concrete rather than an abstract ceiling.
- 5. Trim the number of distinct asks — this send has six different CTAs (sale, new arrivals, fall arrivals, personalize, credit card, app); cut to three max for a final-day email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — summer sale drops off at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, dressers & bedding — before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 500+ new fall arrivals starting at $29`
