# It’s here! Earn DOUBLE REWARDS + Shop up to 60% off The Labor Day Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Labor Day kickoff send from the Crate & Barrel family in three days, and it's basically the same email as the 8/25 "STARTS NOW!" send with a rewritten subject that leads with Double Rewards instead of the discount. The hero is clean — sandy beach texture, bold "It's here! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" headline, single "Shop Now" CTA — and the category cascade underneath (sofas, kitchen, entertaining, decor, rugs, kids furniture) is well organized with real lifestyle photography throughout.
- The problem is fatigue, not execution. This brand has now sent near-identical Labor Day mechanics (double Rewards + up to 60% off) three times in a 48-hour window across C&B, Crate & Kids, and now this variant. The Dyson vacuum and Sonos speaker deals lower in the email are a nice concrete anchor, but they're buried past a very long scroll of category tiles.
- Judgment: solid execution, weak cadence. This is a duplicate-effort send that should have been an A/B subject test on the existing campaign rather than a whole new email in the inbox.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: headline, discount %, and single "Shop Now" button all visible without confusion.
- Category grid is well-photographed with real room settings, no placeholder or broken images.
- Dyson vacuum deal shows real strikethrough pricing ($849.99 vs $979.95) — concrete, credible savings.
- "Limited Time Rewards" banner reinforces the loyalty angle a second time for members who skimmed past the hero.

## What's weak

- This is the third variant of the same Labor Day promo this brand has sent in ~48 hours (Crate & Barrel 8/25, Crate & Kids 8/25, Crate & Kids 8/24) — recipients will recognize the reused hero mechanic instantly.
- Double Rewards is the subject's lead hook but only appears as small text at the very top and a repeated banner mid-email — it's not visually the hero the subject promises.
- The email is extremely long (over a dozen sections) before reaching the strongest concrete deals (Dyson, Sonos) — most readers won't scroll that far.
- "we love these for you" personalized section has no visible personalization cue (no name, no browsing history callout) despite implying tailored picks.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop sending near-duplicate Labor Day emails within 48 hours** — consolidate this and the 8/25 "STARTS NOW" send into one campaign with subject-line A/B testing instead of two separate sends to the same list.
- 2. Move the Dyson/Sonos priced deals up near the hero — concrete dollar savings outperform category tiles for click-through.
- 3. Make Double Rewards visually match the subject's emphasis — right now the subject oversells a benefit the body treats as secondary.
- 4. Trim the category cascade — 8 category tiles before reaching hero products is excessive scroll for diminishing returns.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double Rewards + up to 60% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day deal: Dyson $849.99 + 2x Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 10-20% back in Reward Dollars now through Sept 8`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: up to 25% off select Dyson and Sonos`
