# Top draft pick: NFL Nod Chairs!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a niche hero wrapped in a generic Early Black Friday blast. The top third is focused: three NFL-themed nod chairs and a "Shop Nod Chairs" button, with the "Top draft pick" subject echoing the copy. Below that, the email turns into the same long Crate & Barrel Kids template as the sends earlier this week, with the Double Rewards banner, "up to 50% off" tiles, Jenny Lind beds, and a "we love these for you" product grid.
- A subscriber who has just seen "Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!" and "New! Exclusive designs for Hanukkah" today will see this as the third send in one day. The NFL hook is a fun, specific idea and the best subject of the three. But the offer doesn't appear until you scroll past the hero, so the subject promises a product story and the body drifts into a sale roundup.
- Verdict: a good concept, diluted by a template that keeps adding sections. It would have landed better as a short 3-section send. Note that the image was downscaled (680x9164 shown at 148x2000), so I'm judging layout and headline copy, not fine print or exact prices.

## What's working

- The "Top draft pick: NFL Nod Chairs!" subject is concrete and playful, and it matches the hero.
- The hero is clean: three team-branded chairs on a checkered backdrop, with a single "Shop Nod Chairs" button.
- The chair row below the hero names each variant, so the product is easy to scan.
- The "Last Double Rewards this year" band in red repeats the loyalty benefit and puts the Nov. 17 deadline in front of members.
- The Game Day pint-glass block extends the NFL theme into gifting, so the theme isn't a one-product idea.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, and the NFL focus is gone after the second block.
- The Early Black Friday section ("up to 50% off furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs") is the same stack as the 11/11 and 11/12 sends.
- There is no price or percent-off on the NFL hero itself. The offer only shows up lower down, so the top of the email doesn't say why to buy now.
- Several competing CTAs follow the hero (Shop Top Categories, Learn More, Shop the Sale, Shop All Baby Gear, Shop Now). No single button stands out.
- Teams aren't named clearly in the hero. A fan can't tell at a glance which franchises are covered, which is the main reason to click.
- The footer is dense with fine print, and it sits right after the app and TheDesignDesk promos, so the ending feels cluttered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the NFL hero, the chair row, the Game Day glassware, and a single Double Rewards banner. Drop the generic Early Black Friday tile grid and the baby-gear and bed sections for this send.
- 2. Put the offer in the hero. Add a line such as "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars through Nov. 17" directly under the headline.
- 3. Name the teams in the copy, or add a team selector ("Find your team"), so fans see their franchise immediately.
- 4. Space the sends out. This is the third Kids email today, so move the NFL send to a different day or fold it into a segment for fans or gift buyers.
- 5. Make "Shop Nod Chairs" the only high-contrast button and style the later ones as text links.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template, and the automated QA passed 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 40 `<img>` tags) and a cluster of third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture relay and aren't confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and link `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed:** 79 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped (QA INFO). Redirect resolution and destination status codes are **unverified**.
- **Third-party identity pixels:** five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…1015f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` requests (LiveRamp). They carry the same 64-character hashed recipient identifier, incrementing only `n`. Five sequential sync calls per open add load and third-party data sharing. Disclosure in the privacy policy can't be checked from this source.
- **Recipient-identifying parameters:** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` is appended to `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs (`/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`). The same `mi_u` value appears in the rlcdn `s=` parameter, so one identifier is shared across the first-party and third-party pixels.
- **Beacon image:** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/348df755-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` has no alt text. It is likely an open-tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 23 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` have no `alt`. Examples are `3930ebb6-…jpg`, `bafa6654-…gif`, `e16121d7-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`, and the `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png` image. The remaining ones are:

  With images off or in a screen reader, the product content is unavailable.
- **Pixels without alt:** the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif` ×5, `dv.…`) are missing `alt`. For decorative or tracking images, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **Text resizing disabled:** the universal selector `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` declared on `body`.
