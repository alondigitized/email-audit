# Hours left for free shipping! Don’t miss out
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the third free-shipping push in one day. It follows "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" and the earlier furniture-sale send. The top half is clean. A stack of pastel Dutch ovens sits over a plain headline, "hours left: free shipping on orders over $99!", then a six-link category grid and a "Shop All Free Shipping" button. The offer reads at a glance.
- Below that the email loses focus. It runs through a holiday decorating hero, a Thanksgiving dining event at up to 30% off, a glassware grid, a credit card pitch, an app download, a design appointment, and a store locator. Each block is competent, but together they compete with the free-shipping message the subject sold. The Dutch oven hero also has no product link or name, so it doesn't tie to anything in the offer.
- Verdict: a decent urgency reminder with a bloated body. The first screen works, and everything after it dilutes the deadline. On a day with three near-identical sends, "hours left" is the right escalation, but the mail adds little the earlier ones didn't already say.

## What's working

- The headline repeats the subject and states the offer plainly: free shipping on orders over $99.
- The six category links (Tableware, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) let a shopper jump straight to what they want.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is clearly visible and sits right under the headline.
- The Thanksgiving dining event shows a sale price on the table, a specific "up to 30% off" offer, and a seasonal tie-in.
- The brand look is consistent and uncluttered: large photography, lowercase headlines, clean white space.

## What's weak

- The hero is a Dutch oven stack with no price, no product name and no link. It doesn't say why it appears under a shipping promo.
- The body introduces three more offers (new holiday collection, dining event, Reward Dollars card) that pull attention from the countdown.
- "Hours left" gives no clock, deadline time or timer. The exact cutoff is buried in the fine print, and the earlier send had a countdown.
- The cadence is heavy. A third free-shipping email in one day, after "ENDS TODAY!", makes the urgency feel manufactured.
- The email is very long. The app, TheDesignDesk, and store locator blocks add little for someone deciding on shipping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT", with a countdown timer. This is the single biggest lift because it makes the urgency credible.
- 2. Cut the body down to the free-shipping offer plus one secondary offer. Drop the app, TheDesignDesk and locator blocks from this send.
- 3. Make the hero a shoppable product. Label the Dutch oven with name and price, and link it.
- 4. Add a threshold nudge near the glassware, such as "add a set to reach $99", so the product grid supports the free-shipping offer.
- 5. Suppress this send for anyone who already got the earlier free-shipping emails today, or merge the three into one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: free shipping on $99+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dutch ovens, dinnerware and holiday decor ship free when you spend $99. Offer ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart is $99 away from free shipping. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Hours left for free shipping! Don't miss out"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and Movable Ink dynamic content. It has no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). Two things stand out: 26 images flagged for missing alt text, and compliance/authentication headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **59 tracking links skipped.** They went to click-redirect domains and weren't probed, so destination status (404s, redirect chains) is unverified.
- **5 duplicate LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` use the same hash and differ only in `n`. Five identical third-party beacons add requests and cross-site ID syncing with no functional benefit visible in the source.
- **Hashed identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f…` param on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images and the `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels share the same prefix. This looks like a per-recipient hash, which is a privacy point, not a defect.
- **Tracking pixels without alt.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b5e0fd92-…` are open and impression pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 26 images.** Counts by source:
  - 11 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `fc2adc28-….gif`, `1c9c4d87-….jpg`, `971aa10f-….jpg`.
  - 1 on Scene7: `2026%5F1011%5FCB%5FHolidayDecorating%5FSecondary…`.
  - 7 Movable Ink `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, and others), likely dynamic product tiles.
  - 7 tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""`.
  
  With images off or a screen reader, the content images have no text fallback. Any that carry the offer message or are inside links are the most serious cases.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, because both match at ≥768px and the later one wins.
- **Head hygiene:**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (three declarations).
  - Duplicate charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`).
  - A leftover template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - An unescaped ampersand in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
- **Global `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}`** is a broad reset that can cause unexpected line-height behavior in some clients. This is a risk, not a confirmed bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. Per-recipient values (`mi_u`) are populated.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message headers from a direct-delivery copy. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain, `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, should be checked via DNS or a direct-delivery test.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** Both fall in the truncated portion of the source, so they can't be confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. All 59 links are tracking-wrapped and were not resolved, so the final destinations and any UTM parameters are unknown. The link inventory also doesn't show whether the free-shipping offer in the subject is carried through to the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 content images (11 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, 1 on Scene7). Add `alt=""` to the 7 tracking pixels and review the 7 Movable Ink tiles.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from raw headers. Don't treat the relay warnings as defects until then.
3. Resolve the 59 tracked links with a redirect-following probe. Verify each destination returns 200 and check for UTM parameters.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Deduplicate the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels to one.
6. Clean up the head: remove duplicate meta tags and the placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, and delete the dead 768px `img` rule.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
