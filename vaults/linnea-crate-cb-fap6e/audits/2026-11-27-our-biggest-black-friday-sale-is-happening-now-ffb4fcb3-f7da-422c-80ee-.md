# OUR BIGGEST BLACK FRIDAY SALE IS HAPPENING NOW!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday catalog Crate & Barrel has sent all week, now with an all-caps subject and "1-day-only deals" language. The hero is a red ribbon reading "Biggest Sale of the Year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + 1-day-only deals + free shipping!" with a Shop All Deals button. After that comes a long stack of single-category tiles, each with its own "up to X% off" line and a Shop Now button.
- The email is clean and well built, but it is very long and gives the eye no clear next step. Ten or more near-identical tiles (kitchen, sofas, wreaths, furniture, holiday decor, hosting, decor, rugs, Kids, clearance) carry equal weight. A kitchen-deals block with product cards comes after them. Compared with the 11/27 "Hot Black Friday Kitchen deals" send, which led with one hero, this one dilutes its own message. The "1-day-only" urgency is the best new element, but it is mixed into a list of other offers.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send that is fatigue-prone. A subscriber who has already had several of these this week has little reason to open another.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: up to 50% off, free shipping, and "1-day-only deals."
- Each tile has one lifestyle image, one discount line and one button, so every module is easy to scan.
- Offers differ by category, from 30% to 60% off, so there is a real price anchor for each.
- The "today only: 40% off wreaths, garlands, Monochrome fragrances" tile adds credible, time-bounded urgency.
- The imagery is seasonal (garlands, stockings, fireplaces) and fits the holiday moment.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with roughly 10 category tiles before the kitchen block. Nothing stands out as the hero deal.
- The only urgency is "1-day-only" in the hero and one tile. There is no countdown or deadline hour.
- This is the latest in a run of near-identical sends: "Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!" and "Our biggest Black Friday sale is happening now!" the same day, plus 11/26 "Happy Thanksgiving!" and "Black Friday just got even BETTER!"
- The kitchen section at the bottom repeats the kitchen tile at the top, and its small product cards have tiny, hard-to-read pricing text.
- The "Black Friday" ribbon badges repeat on every tile, which adds clutter without information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tile list to the 3–4 strongest "today only" deals and lead with one hero product. A shorter email would lift clicks more than any other change.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "ends tonight at 11:59 PM CT."
- 3. Segment by category affinity. Send kitchen buyers the kitchen block and holiday-decor buyers the decor block.
- 4. Show prices on product cards at a legible size, with ratings or a bestseller flag as social proof.
- 5. Space out the sends. Two same-day Black Friday blasts with near-identical subjects train people to ignore them.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `1-day-only deals on kitchen, holiday decor and furniture. Free shipping on $79+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters end at midnight. See what's still in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has several markup and accessibility defects, mainly about 46 images with no `alt` attribute and a viewport that disables zoom. The QA run flagged missing `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` headers, but all three look like relay capture gaps rather than confirmed sender faults. The source is truncated, so footer, UTM and link-level checks could not be completed. The 57% pass rate reflects warnings only, with 0 hard issues.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not HTTP-probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM survival are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are third-party tracking pixels that add requests and a third-party data-sharing surface.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4210b1c8-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are expected for dynamic image personalization, but the recipient hash is exposed in every image URL.
- Tracking pixels `o.gif` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4210b1c8-…` are also flagged for missing alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 46 `<img>` elements have no `alt` attribute.**
  - About 29 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` (e.g. `3a6e0878-…jpg`, `77b4f73e-…gif`) and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Seven are Movable Ink dynamic images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/560d850e7e517ede.png` and others).
  - Eight are pixels or trackers.
  - If these are the main promotional images, the email is unreadable with images blocked and for screen-reader users.
  - Pure pixels should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The QA check can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype should be `&amp;`. It is a minor validity error.
- **Duplicate head tags:**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,…`, `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (also a typo) was never removed. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Redundant CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are defined twice.
- `lang="en"` is present on `<html>`, which is correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. Personalization is handled by Movable Ink `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters. The truncation prevents checking the body and footer.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip them. This is unconfirmed, so check the raw headers on a direct-delivery copy. If they are genuinely absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image hosts (`image.`, `mi.`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`), which is good, but alignment is not proof of DKIM.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the HTML is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 81 links go through tracking redirects that were not probed, and the truncated HTML doesn't expose final destination URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content and dynamic image, and `alt=""` to all pixels (`rlcdn.com/448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC (`Authentication-Results`) to confirm whether the gaps are only relay artifacts.
5. Consolidate the five identical LiveRamp pixels into one, or confirm the repetition is intentional.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve the tracking redirects. That would let you verify UTM parameters, landing-page status and the footer unsubscribe and address.
