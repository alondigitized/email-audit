# Don't miss: Up to 60% off more clearance items!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance email whose body is mostly something else. The hero is strong: "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off*" with a single "Shop Clearance" button and a row of category links (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, "Shop 40%–60% Off Final Sale"). The subject's promise is delivered at the top. Below that, the email turns into a pile of competing messages.
- The same-day app promo (20% off play furniture) appears twice, once in the top banner and once in a full section. A Design Desk $5,000 sweepstakes and a free design appointment each get their own block. A credit-card promo and a second app download block follow. Before the reader reaches the products, they've seen about five different asks. It also repeats the creative from earlier this week: the Nov 1 "Today only: 20% off top play furniture!" and "100% free design help" sends. A subscriber who got both has now seen the same two offers three times.
- The product grid is the weak spot. It's labeled "we love these for you" and mixes a pumpkin treat bag, strollers, a car seat, a glider, a dresser and an activity chair. The one product with a visible markdown, the pumpkin bag, is a seasonal novelty. Prices are visible, but the strollers show a strikethrough while no discount percentage is called out on them. Verdict: a good hero wrapped in a cluttered body. It would be a 7 if it stopped after the clearance block.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 60% off" with a single "Shop Clearance" button, and it matches the subject.
- The category link row gives quick routes into clearance by room or product type.
- The product grid shows prices, and a couple of items show the strikethrough original price.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and consistent brand styling.
- The footer nav and legal text are complete, so there's no friction in finding other categories.

## What's weak

- The clearance offer is competing with the app-only 20% play furniture banner, which appears twice and uses the "today only" urgency instead.
- The Design Desk sweepstakes and the free design appointment send readers off to a service, away from clearance.
- The "we love these for you" grid has no clear link to clearance and mixes categories, from Halloween decor to a car seat. Little of it looks like a "more clearance items" pick.
- The "Don't miss" subject has no deadline, and the body doesn't say when the markdowns end beyond "while quantities last."
- Several full-width promo blocks, including the credit card and app download, push the content to roughly 6,300px tall.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the clearance story. Remove the second app block and the Design Desk repeats, and keep one secondary promo at most.
- 2. Fill the product grid with actual clearance items that show a percent-off badge and was/now prices, ideally in the persona's focus (nursery and baby).
- 3. Give the offer a real deadline or inventory cue, such as "ends Sunday" or "final sale, while supplies last", in the hero.
- 4. Suppress sweepstakes and design-service blocks for people who got them earlier in the week.
- 5. Make the hero button more specific, such as "Shop 60% Off Clearance".

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Don't miss: Up to 60% off more clearance items!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO conditional comments and mobile breakpoints. The main confirmed issue is widespread missing `alt` attributes on images. The header-based compliance checks could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 68 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** these image beacons are present:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` (five LiveRamp pixels)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e7e3e52c-ea00-4324-928d-ecde26658353?mi_cid=…` (a second beacon)
  - six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` images (repeated per-recipient tags)
- **Third-party identifier:** the `rlcdn.com` pixels send a 64-hex value (`s=dbe8da71…`) to a third party. It looks like a hashed identifier, but the source doesn't confirm that.
- **Redundancy:** five identical LiveRamp calls (`n=1..5`) and six identical `rp/f3c0…png` requests add load without any visible benefit.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flagged about 28 `<img>` elements, including these content images:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `.../038b0feb-5641-4d84-9f6b-529ab47352b9.jpg`
  - `s7d5.scene7.com/.../20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_100s`
  - `.../f1bdc9e3…jpg`, `67c84df5…jpg`, `16d802ed…jpg`, `5b49ac67…jpg`, `59d3a675…jpg`, `07e15d43…jpg`, `eb8bcaba…jpg`, `907f5ca9…jpg`, `971aa10f…jpg`, `f7fce13f…jpg`
- **Decorative images:** the spacer `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` and the pixels above should carry `alt=""`. The check can't tell an absent `alt` from an empty one, so some of these may be fine.
- **Content images:** with images blocked, any content image without `alt` renders as nothing, and screen readers read the filename or skip it.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** there are two `Content-Type`/charset declarations (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`). `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, and `address=no`.
- **Leftover template text:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe missing:** `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found`. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is not confirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post missing:** `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)`. One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not checked:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. All 68 tracked links are redirect URLs that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Fall Clearance" content can't be checked from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Start with the Fall Clearance hero (`20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_100s`).
2. Fix the `<title>` (`&amp;`, and make it match the subject) and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Merge the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and remove the leftover "IMPUT HERE" comment.
4. Re-capture the raw message with full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't treat the current warnings as sender defects until then.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify destinations and UTM parameters, and re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer.
6. Consolidate the duplicate LiveRamp (`n=1..5`) and `rp/f3c0…png` pixel calls, and review the privacy disclosure for the third-party identifier.
