## What's working

- **"Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" top banner** — I noticed it immediately and it makes me feel like a regular here, which I want to be after just building my registry
- **"Wedding Registry" link in sub-nav** — it's right there in the utility bar, which is exactly the shortcut I need at this stage of my life
- **Promo strip with "Up to 20% off Tabletop & Bar"** — I can just barely see this in the horizontal deal strip below the main nav, and it is doing a lot of work for me specifically
- **"Up to 40% off Dining"** — the category tile visible below the fold confirms they have deals in my lane right now
- **"built-to-last bestsellers" section** — the framing hits exactly right: I'm making purchases I want to keep forever, not churn in two years
- **"The BFC Collection" sub-nav link** — for a rewards-aware shopper, this signals a loyalty tier that makes me curious

## What's weak

- **Email-capture modal fires immediately** — I just landed, I haven't seen anything yet, and I'm being asked for my email *and* mobile number before the page loads. Millennial dark-pattern reflex kicks in and my thumb goes to "Maybe Later"
- **Hero is 100% outdoor furniture** — beautiful, but I'm registering for dinnerware and walnut serving boards, not patio sectionals. The mismatch doesn't make me leave, but it doesn't pull me in either
- **No registry-specific messaging above the fold** — I recently registered at Crate and Barrel specifically; there's no "welcome back, your registry is live" hook or registry-completion prompt anywhere visible
- **"Shop All Outdoor" as a secondary CTA feels redundant** — when the entire hero is already outdoor, having two outdoor CTAs side-by-side dilutes both of them
- **No visible reviews or social proof** — the bestsellers section at the bottom has product imagery but no star ratings or review counts visible in the screenshot

## Recommendations

- **Delay the email modal by 30 seconds or one scroll** — firing it before I've even seen the homepage creates friction before I've decided I like the brand; give me a reason to want the emails first
- **Add a registry-completion banner to the sub-nav strip** — something like "Your registry has 12 items — see what's on sale" would be an instant tap for me right now
- **Swap one of the two outdoor CTAs for a "Tabletop & Bar Sale" CTA** — the deal is already in the promo strip; surfacing it with a dedicated button at hero level would catch registry shoppers immediately
- **Surface product ratings on the bestsellers rail** — even just "4.8 ★ (1,240 reviews)" under a piece makes the "built-to-last" claim feel earned rather than claimed
