# 💗Our most loved furniture collection is…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The render is heavily downscaled, so I'm reading structure and large headlines, not small print. This is a Jenny Lind furniture-collection feature, and the hero says "our #1 furniture collection: meet jenny lind." It then walks through three rooms (bedroom, nursery, playroom) with product pairs and GREENGUARD Gold callouts. The merchandising idea is good: one hero collection, three use cases.
- The offer is the problem. "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" sits in a thin banner at the very top and returns later as a mid-email block. The hero itself leads with the brand story, not the savings. The email is also very long. After the Jenny Lind rooms it adds a toys free-shipping block, a Venus Williams collab, the event block again, a "we love these for you" grid, an app push and a Design Desk teaser. The Jenny Lind message gets diluted.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand catalog send, but a tidy 6. It's the sixth Crate & Barrel Kids email in the audited history in three days, and it follows the 10-03 "Heritage vibes" new-arrivals send. That's a lot of cadence for one promo.

## What's working

- A single hero collection (Jenny Lind) gives the email a clear spine.
- Room-by-room sections ("for their bedroom / nursery / playroom") make browsing easy.
- Each section has its own button, such as "Shop All Jenny Lind" and "Shop All Furniture Collections."
- GREENGUARD Gold Certified labels are a real trust signal for parents.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices and products, which gives shoppers something concrete to click.

## What's weak

- The up-to-50% event is buried in the top banner, so the hero has no price hook.
- The email scrolls very long and splits attention among toys, the Venus Williams collab, the app and the Design Desk.
- No visible deadline, so there is no credible urgency.
- The event block appears again mid-email, repeating the message. The Oct 2 holiday-bedding send ran the same event banner.
- The subject trails off ("collection is…"), which reads as a gimmick and hides the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "Jenny Lind: up to 50% off," with an end date.
- 2. Cut the email to the Jenny Lind story plus a single product grid. Move toys, the collab and the app to other sends.
- 3. Add social proof next to the hero, such as review counts or the bestseller claim.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Don't send this within hours of the new-arrivals email.
- 5. Finish the subject line and make it concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our #1 crib collection is up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Meet Jenny Lind: kids' furniture, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `GREENGUARD Gold certified cribs, beds and play tables. Semi-Annual Event pricing ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One collection, three rooms: nursery, bedroom and playroom, all on sale.`
