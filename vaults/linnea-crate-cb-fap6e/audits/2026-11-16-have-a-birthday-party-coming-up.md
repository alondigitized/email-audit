# Have a birthday party coming up?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- The email's subject promises a birthday-party gift guide, but about two-thirds of the render is a Black Friday and Double Rewards email. The first screen delivers on the subject. A "the birthday shop" hero with a Shop Now button sits under two thin top strips. Below it are age-tiered gift modules for kids, toddlers and babies, each with its own button. After that the email turns into the same stack Crate & Barrel has sent all weekend: early Black Friday furniture, bedding and rug tiles, the $2,700+ giveaway, a red "last Double Rewards this year" block, a card-signup promo and an app download.
- The birthday angle is a good segmentation hook. Gifting by age is more useful than another generic sale blast. But it runs about 11,700px long and keeps changing topic, and the first screen gets diluted. This is also at least the fourth Crate & Barrel send in about two days (compare "ENDS TOMORROW: Shop quick-ship faves with 2x Rewards!" earlier today), so a reader sees familiar modules.
- Verdict: a good concept buried in a recycled template. It's competent and has no visible render bugs, but it gives the reader no single reason to act now.

## What's working

- The hero reads "the birthday shop", so it matches the subject.
- The kids, toddlers and babies sections ("what kids are wishing for…") let a parent jump to the right age.
- Each gift module has its own button instead of one generic CTA.
- The product photography is warm and consistent: toy kitchen, dollhouse, nursery pieces.
- Double Rewards and the Black Friday discounts give price-sensitive shoppers a reason to buy this week.
- Nothing looks broken. There are no missing images, overlaps or odd gaps.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a birthday party, but the middle and lower half is furniture, bedding, rugs, a giveaway and a card promo.
- The module order is cluttered. The giveaway, the red Rewards block, the "shop Black Friday deals first" signup and "we love these for you" all compete after the gift sections.
- The birthday-shop hero carries no price, deadline or Rewards cue. The offer is only in the thin top strips, so the eye lands on a category with no incentive.
- The email is extremely long, with an app download, a design-service promo and a dense legal footer at the bottom.
- The cadence is heavy. It repeats the Double Rewards and Early Black Friday beats from the 11-15 and 11-16 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the send actually about birthdays. Cut it to the hero and the three age groups, with one deal line (for example "2x Rewards on gifts, ends tomorrow") and one Rewards strip. Drop the furniture, giveaway, card and app modules.
- 2. Put a concrete offer or deadline in the hero. "Birthday gifts + 2x Rewards" is stronger than a bare "the birthday shop".
- 3. Put real products with names and prices in each age group, not only a category button. Add one best-seller or review cue.
- 4. Change the buttons from "Shop Now" and "Shop Kids Gifts" to "Shop gifts for ages 3-5" and similar.
- 5. Suppress this send for anyone who already got a Double Rewards email today, or merge the two.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: it declares `lang="en"` and has a table layout with MSO fallbacks. It has two real defects: roughly 50 images lack alt attributes, and several third-party tracking pixels carry what looks like a per-recipient hash. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated before the body and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 93 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were probed. Broken or expired redirects cannot be ruled out.
- **Pixels without alt text:** several pixels have no `alt`:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, repeated 6 times
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/fef14703-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e…&n=1` through `n=5`. This is five hits to a LiveRamp domain.
  - The `s=` value starts with the same hex string as the first-party `mi_u=` value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f…`). This suggests the same recipient identifier is being passed to a third party. This is an inference from the matching prefix. Confirm the hash content and consent basis.
  - Five near-identical pixel calls are redundant and add load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 50 images are flagged, including:
  - nearly all content images under `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `3930ebb6-…jpg`, `1cce28bd-…jpg`)
  - `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`
  - two GIFs (`8c4ce5d9-….gif`, `9c5d35f4-….gif`)
  - the spacer `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`

  With images blocked, screen readers and image-blocking clients see nothing. Informational images need descriptive alt text. Spacers and pixels need `alt=""`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled template placeholder.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times, with conflicting values.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Global CSS:** `*{…line-height:100%}` and `p{text-align:center}` are blanket rules. They can override intended line-height and alignment, and `line-height:100%` on text is tight for readability.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact. Treat it as unconfirmed, not as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The sending domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is visible, but no result is available. This is also likely relay-related.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so neither can be confirmed or flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 93 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source shows no final destinations or UTM parameters. The platform appears to be Movable Ink / Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), which wraps links in redirects. Nothing here confirms that UTMs survive the redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels (about 50 instances).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Review the `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels. Confirm what the `s=` hash encodes, whether consent covers sharing it, and why the pixel fires 5 times.
4. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder, dedupe the `format-detection` and `charset` metas, and escape `&` in the title.
5. Check the raw headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating the compliance warnings as real.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a real GET, following redirects, to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
