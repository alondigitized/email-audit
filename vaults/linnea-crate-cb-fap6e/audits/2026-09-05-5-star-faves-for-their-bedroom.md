# 5-star faves for their bedroom →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel Labor Day marathon-scroll template I've now flagged repeatedly this week — but this time it opens on a genuinely on-topic hero: a real bed with a 5-star review row and "bestsellers for their bedroom," not a generic kitchen-sink banner. The top-of-fold reads more curated than the prior sends, with reviewer quotes woven between product blocks, which is a nicer trust signal than anything I've seen from this family of emails so far.
- That said, it collapses into the exact same fatigue pattern by the third scroll: bedding, rugs, wall art, storage, then the full "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" grid dump repeats categories already shown above, followed by yet another Double Rewards banner and a whole second "500+ new fall arrivals starting at $29" section. It's structurally two or three emails stapled together.
- Net: the top third is the best hook this brand has sent all week, but it drowns itself by not stopping there. Judgement: decent open, weak click-through — the good hero gets buried under redundant re-selling.

## What's working

- Subject and hero both explicitly reference "bedroom," and the top section actually delivers bedroom bestsellers with a visible 5-star rating row.
- Customer review quotes ("so cute and comfortable," "well made and beautiful") are interspersed between product shots — social proof this brand hasn't shown in prior sends I've reviewed.
- Clear repeated CTA buttons ("Shop Now," "Shop Bedroom Furniture," "Shop All Bedding") give obvious next steps at each section.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together across the long scroll.

## What's weak

- The email re-runs the identical Labor Day kitchen-sink grid (furniture, bedding, rugs, decor, lighting) that's already appeared in this brand's last 4-5 sends this week — zero new creative in the back half.
- Two separate offer banners (Double Rewards top and bottom, plus a full-price Labor Day grid) dilute the one focused "bedroom" hook the subject promised.
- By the time the reader hits "500+ new fall arrivals starting at $29" and a fireplace/living-room shot, the email has fully drifted off the bedroom premise from the subject line.
- Extremely long single-column scroll with no clear stopping point risks most readers never reaching the actual Labor Day pricing details.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep the top bedroom bestsellers + reviews section, drop the redundant full kitchen-sink Labor Day grid that's already been sent all week, and end on one strong CTA.
- 2. Move the Double Rewards banner to a single placement (top OR bottom, not both) to avoid the redundant-offer feel.
- 3. If cross-selling fall arrivals, spin that into its own send rather than tacking it onto a "bedroom" themed email — it breaks the promise set by the subject.
- 4. Add a specific dollar or percentage number to the hero (e.g., "up to 50% off bedroom furniture") rather than making shoppers scroll to find pricing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `5-star bedroom bestsellers, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real reviews, real comfort — shop the bestsellers`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Double Rewards through Labor Day`
