# New Black Friday deals added! Open to see…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is another Crate & Barrel Kids Black Friday catch-all, and it's the sixth or so in three days. The hero says "new Black Friday deals added!" with "free shipping is on" above it. That's a fair match for the subject, but the subject's "Open to see…" is a teaser that the body never pays off. There is no single new deal to point to. The email is a long scroll of eight sale-category links, a rugs module, a personalization shop, stockings, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design appointment.
- The strongest read is that it's competent but diluted. "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" is clear, and the eight-link category grid is easy to scan. But I can't see a dollar-off headline or a single hero product above the fold. The rugs and personalization modules look like leftovers from the 11/24 furniture and decor sends. A subscriber who got the 11/24 "New Black Friday deals added!" and the 11/25 "NEW ITEMS ADDED!" would see the same template again.
- Judgment: a middling promotional send. It will get opens on brand recognition and holiday timing, but it won't drive urgency or a specific product click.

## What's working

- The "up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" headline is a concrete, honest offer, with "Exclusions apply. Orders $79+" disclosed right beneath it.
- The two-column category grid (Furniture, Toy, Baby Bedding & Bath, Rugs, Finishing Touches, Holiday and others) makes it easy to jump to the right sale.
- The "Up to 50% off rugs" module is a specific category offer with a clear "Shop the Sale" button.
- The product grid under "we love these for you" shows prices and strikethrough sale prices on strollers, a rocker and a dresser, which gives real purchase intent.
- The stocking module ("$29.95 and ship free!") ties a concrete price to a seasonal hook.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing modules. The sale grid, rugs, personalization shop, stockings, product grid, credit card, app and design desk all fight for attention, and there's no single hero.
- The subject's "Open to see…" promises a reveal, but the body only repeats "new Black Friday deals added!" with no named new deal.
- The same template and message have run all week, with no escalation in offer or urgency. There is no deadline, countdown or "ends tonight" language in the visible creative.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is very long. The $500 app giveaway and the credit card promo are off-message and pull away from the sale.
- The hero is soft: a muted lifestyle photo with small "new Black Friday deals added!" text and a modest "Shop Now" button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one dominant offer the hero, such as "Up to 50% off + free shipping, ends tonight," with a countdown or a hard deadline. This is the biggest lift, because urgency is missing.
- 2. Make the subject pay off its teaser. Name the actual new deals in the subject and hero.
- 3. Cut the module count roughly in half. Drop the credit card, app and design desk blocks from sale sends.
- 4. Move the product grid with sale prices higher, right after the hero and category links.
- 5. Vary the creative across the week so the sixth send doesn't look like the first.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New today: 50% off rugs, strollers & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $79+, plus new markdowns on furniture, rugs and baby gear.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a standard ESP template with 79 click-tracked links, but about 37 images lack `alt` attributes and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified.** 79 links point to tracking or click-redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and landing-page status are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party identifier pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e29…&n=1` through `n=5` requests carry what looks like a 64-hex hashed recipient identifier (`s=`). The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. The five-fold repetition of the rlcdn pixel adds load and third-party requests without added signal. The repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel also looks like redundant tracking.
- **Another open pixel.** `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1cc80cf1-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is an extra open-tracking image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (about 24).** These include `…/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `d3119bec-…jpg`, `5d4ea9f0-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`), `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`, `m/1/f8c48ff1-…gif`, and the Scene7 assets `2026_1124_CBK_BF_NewDealsAdded_02.gif` and `2026_1105_CBK_PersonalizedGiftShop_Seconda…`. Image-blocking clients and screen readers will get no text for these.
- **Missing alt text, spacers and pixels (about 13).** These are `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`, the `rlcdn` and `mi.` pixels, and the `dv.` pixel. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly. A missing attribute makes some screen readers announce the filename.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` remain in the head.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** There are four `format-detection` metas and two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype, and the unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">` is also invalid XHTML. The title does not match the subject line either.
