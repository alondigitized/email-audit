## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template, with 89 tracked links and roughly 50 images. The QA run flagged 3 warnings and 0 errors, but the 57% pass rate is mostly driven by missing alt attributes. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **89 tracking links were not probed.** The HTTP probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` differ only in `n`. Each is a separate third-party request, and none has alt text.
- **No other link defects are confirmed.** The visible HTML was truncated before the body links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 50 images.** The QA check flagged all of these:
  - **Content images (36):** `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png` (e.g. `61c7fb62-…jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`). If these are linked product or hero images, they carry no accessible name, and the email is unreadable with images off.
  - **Dynamic images (7):** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5×), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/acff58b7-…`. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The QA output doesn't show whether the attribute is absent or empty, so confirm this one.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is blocked.** Both `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` are set. They conflict with `body { -webkit-text-size-adjust:100% }`, and the `none` value prevents text scaling in WebKit.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The template also repeats `<meta name="format-detection">` three times with conflicting values.
- **Template debris.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.
- **A per-recipient identifier is in the image URLs.** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` and the `s=dbe8da71…` LiveRamp parameter share the same hash prefix. This is expected for Movable Ink, but it means the identifier goes to a third party (`rlcdn.com`) on every open.
- **Dynamic image rendering is unverified.** The 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` images are generated per recipient, and their fallback behavior was not tested.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may have dropped it, so this may be a capture limitation. It is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click support is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status could not be assessed. The sender is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be checked against raw headers.
- **The footer was not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are beyond the truncated HTML, so neither is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 89 links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't show their `href` values. UTM presence, landing-page match to the subject ("Smaller dining space? … designer hacks"), and redirect-chain length are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to the 36 content images and the 7 dynamic images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*`).
2. **Add `alt=""`** to all tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
4. **Escape the title** as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, dedupe the `format-detection` meta tags, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. **Reduce the LiveRamp calls** to a single pixel instead of five.
6. **Verify the headers against the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, bypassing the AgentMail relay if possible.
7. **Re-run the QA with the full, untruncated HTML**, and resolve the tracked links to their final URLs. That lets the UTM and landing-page checks run and shows whether the footer unsubscribe link and postal address are present.
