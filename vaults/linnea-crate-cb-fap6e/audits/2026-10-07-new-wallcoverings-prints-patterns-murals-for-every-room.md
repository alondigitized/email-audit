# NEW wallcoverings: Prints, patterns & murals for every room →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a wallcovering launch email, and it's well built for what it is. The hero is "introducing our first-ever wallcovering collection" over a moody, designer-style room. Below it come texture, print and pattern sections, and each has a "before" and "after" room shot. The subject delivers on the hero.
- The render is heavily downscaled (680x12000 shown at 113x2000). I can read structure, headlines and button labels, but not fine print. The slate banner at the top reads "it's on up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals", which has nothing to do with wallpaper. The same banner ran on the Oct 6 sofa, bestsellers and top-picks sends. Past the Crate & Barrel Kids wallcovering block, the email turns into a grab bag. It runs through TheDesignDesk, a repeat sofa promo, a "pumpkin spice… bagels?!" video tile, a "we love these for you" product grid, and an app download. The strong launch story gets diluted.
- Verdict: a solid, on-brand launch with good visual storytelling. It's too long and too many offers compete with it, so the click path is muddy. This is the second wallpaper-themed send today, after "It's not done-done without NEW wallpaper!", so the news value is already blunted.

## What's working

- The hero is a single clear idea: "introducing our first-ever wallcovering collection" with a "Shop Now" button right under it.
- The before/after room shots for classic prints and bold patterns show the transformation instead of describing it.
- The section headlines ("timeless texture", "generations of quality", "classic prints", "bold patterns") are short and scannable.
- The Crate & Barrel Kids nursery wallcovering block speaks to a distinct audience and has its own CTA.
- The sofa banner at the top gives a concrete percentage offer (20% off).

## What's weak

- The 20% off sofas banner contradicts the wallcovering hero, so the first thing a reader sees is two different messages.
- The email runs very long, and the bagel/pumpkin spice video tile and the app-download block are off-topic tail content.
- There are about seven near-identical "Shop Now" buttons with no product-specific copy.
- There's no price, offer or deadline on the wallcovering itself, so nothing gives a reason to act today.
- The product grid at the bottom ("we love these for you") is decor objects. It has nothing to do with wallpaper, and the product text is too small to read in this render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or swap the sofa banner on launch sends. Put a wallcovering-specific hook there, such as free samples or a design appointment, so the hero and the top bar agree.
- 2. Cut the email after the Kids wallcovering block and TheDesignDesk. Drop the bagel video, the repeat sofa promo and the product grid, or move them to a separate send.
- 3. Give each section a specific CTA, such as "Shop textures", "Shop classic prints", "Shop bold patterns" and "Shop kids' wallcoverings", in place of repeated "Shop Now".
- 4. Add a free-sample or peel-and-stick install callout. It lowers the commitment of a big, scary purchase.
- 5. Space out the wallpaper sends. Two on the same day teaches subscribers to ignore the second.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email, built on a Movable Ink (`mi.`/`dv.crateandbarrel.com`) and Salesforce-style template. QA passed at 50% with 0 errors and 4 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing image alt text and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 77 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Plain text is mostly URLs:** 15,744 of 22,430 characters (about 70%) are URLs, which is at the warning threshold. This suggests long tracked URLs are printed inline in the text part. Spam filters can penalize that, and it reads poorly for text-only clients.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1…5` beacons are present, differing only in `n=`. `rlcdn.com` is a third-party ad-sync domain. Repeating the same beacon five times looks redundant.
- **Dynamic content:** The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` image appears 6 times. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4d0c37ad-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and dynamic-content calls. If Movable Ink's service is slow or down, these slots fail.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 45 `<img>` elements are flagged. Content images include:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - ~25 `…/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif` files on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`
  - `s7d5.scene7.com/…Ooni_Social…`
  
  Alt-less images are announced by file name in screen readers. They also leave blank gaps when images are blocked, which matters for an image-driven wallcovering email.
- **Tracking pixels:** The rlcdn, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels need `alt=""` to be correct, not descriptive alt text. QA can't tell whether they have the attribute set empty or omitted.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **CSS cascade:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Source hygiene (minor):**
  - A leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times and `charset` twice.
  - `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are global rules that can have unintended effects.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` are visible in the truncated HTML. The `[IMPUT HERE…]` text is a leftover comment, not a token.
- Every pixel URL carries the same 64-hex-character `mi_u=` or `s=` value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`). This looks like a SHA-256 hash of the recipient identifier, though that is inferred, not confirmed. It is passed to a third party (`rlcdn.com`). That is a privacy and consent point to check, not a rendering bug.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so absence is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly missing, this fails Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the images are on a matching subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending but not proof.
- **Footer:** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified. The 77 skipped tracking links hide their final destinations, and the truncated source doesn't show the URLs. The subject ("NEW wallcoverings") can't be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, using the product or room name. Set `alt=""` on every tracking pixel and spacer.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Verify with the raw `.eml` that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present. Also check the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. QA couldn't see these through the relay.
4. Shorten or remove the raw tracked URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio well under 70%.
5. Resolve and probe the final destinations of a sample of the 77 tracking links to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page match.
6. Remove the duplicate `rlcdn.com` beacons, fix the overlapping `img` max-width media queries, and escape `&` in `<title>`.
7. Review what the hashed `mi_u`/`s` identifier sent to `rlcdn.com` is derived from and whether consent covers it.
