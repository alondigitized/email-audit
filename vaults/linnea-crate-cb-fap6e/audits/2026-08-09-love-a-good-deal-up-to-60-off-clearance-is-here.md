# Love a good deal? Up to 60% off clearance is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-executed clearance send — a beach hero with "up to 60% off clearance," a clear category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding), and a strong "Shop All Clearance" CTA right up top. Visually it's the cleanest Crate & Barrel send in this brand's recent history — no clutter, no lorem ipsum, good hierarchy.
- The problem is everything after the fold: the email piles on the Levain bakery collab, TheDesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes, "we love these for you" product recs, a credit card pitch, and an app download push — five unrelated CTAs competing with the actual sale. It's the classic Crate & Barrel kitchen-sink structure this brand keeps repeating (see 2026-08-08 bestsellers, 2026-08-08 bedding sweep). The hero is genuinely good; the rest dilutes it.
- Verdict: solid subject and hero, but message dilution below the fold caps this well below what a focused clearance email could score.

## What's working

- Hero is immediate and legible: "up to 60% off clearance" over a clean beach image, with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- Category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding) gives clear category-level entry points with real product photography.
- "Shop All Clearance" CTA reinforces the hero offer a second time before the email pivots elsewhere.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlap, no placeholder content.
- "Ships Free" badge on bedding & bath adds a concrete secondary incentive.

## What's weak

- The email abandons its own subject line halfway down — Levain bakery collab, TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, credit card offer, and app download all crowd out the clearance message.
- No specific discount tiers or product-level pricing shown for clearance items — "up to 60%" is vague with no anchor price example.
- "While quantities last" is the only urgency cue and it's generic, not time-bound.
- The "we love these for you" product recs show full prices with no clearance/sale marking, which contradicts the clearance framing right above them.
- Five distinct offers (clearance, bakery collab, sweepstakes, credit card, app) compete for one click — no single dominant CTA after the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Clearance" — or move clearance to be the only offer and push bakery collab/sweepstakes/credit card/app to separate sends. This one change would lift open-to-click follow-through the most.
- 2. Add one concrete price example in the hero or subhead (e.g., "sofas now from $999") to make "up to 60%" feel real, not generic.
- 3. Replace "While quantities last" with a dated urgency cue if one exists (e.g., "Ends Sunday").
- 4. Mark the "we love these for you" section as sale-eligible or remove it — full-price recs undercut the clearance pitch directly below the fold.
- 5. If cross-promotion is required, cap it to one secondary block, not four.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on furniture, rugs, bedding & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the categories before they sell out`
