# Everyone has a perfect KitchenAid match—find yours
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a KitchenAid-themed send that is also a Black Friday kickoff. The subject promises a "perfect match" quiz-style hook, and the body delivers a long product-led scroll: stand mixer hero, Artisan Plus, Artisan, Bowl-Lift, "more to love" accessories, then a generic Black Friday grid. The KitchenAid section is the best part. It is editorial, product-forward and clearly organized.
- The strategy is muddled, though. A thin banner at the top ("Black Friday starts now! Up to 50% off + free shipping") has its own Shop Now button, and the same offer repeats in the lower modules. That leaves two stories competing: the mixer match and the sitewide sale. The subject mentions neither the sale nor a price. The scroll is also very long, with several near-identical "Shop" buttons. This is a good content email that would be stronger with one job.
- Judged against today's cadence, this is the Crate & Barrel main-brand sibling of the three Kids Black Friday kickoffs sent earlier on 11/18. A subscriber who got "STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!" will see the same banner again, and that dulls the urgency. The KitchenAid angle is the one thing that makes this send different.

## What's working

- The KitchenAid hero is strong. The mixer is photographed beautifully, and the "your KitchenAid stand mixer matchmaker is here" headline is a distinct, playful concept.
- Product sections are well segmented: Artisan Plus, Artisan, and Bowl-Lift each get their own block with color swatches and a dedicated button.
- The "pasta night is on" lifestyle module ties the attachments to a real use case instead of just selling specs.
- The Black Friday banner at the very top carries the deal and free shipping clearly, so the offer is visible immediately.
- The imagery is polished and consistent with Crate & Barrel's premium brand voice. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The top banner and the KitchenAid hero compete. The email opens with a sale and then switches to a product-match story.
- The KitchenAid modules show no prices or discounts. The Black Friday savings only appear lower down in other categories, so the hero never pays off the sale.
- The email scrolls through many modules and CTAs, and the pasta and accessory sections dilute the "match" idea. The "matchmaker" promise isn't followed by an actual quiz or selector.
- Body copy and product captions are small and dense, which hurts mobile readability.
- It is a repeat of the same-day Black Friday kickoff messaging, which adds to cadence fatigue. The 11/17 send, "ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards," has also just been followed by another promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the KitchenAid discount in the hero, for example "KitchenAid stand mixers: up to $X off," and make the sale the reason to click. Right now the hero doesn't tell you the savings.
- 2. Make the "matchmaker" real. Link to a short guide or quiz, with a button like "Find my mixer." Then the subject's promise is kept.
- 3. Trim the body to the hero, three mixer tiers and one accessory block. Move the generic Black Friday grid into a single footer link.
- 4. Remove or shrink the top Black Friday banner so it doesn't compete with the hero.
- 5. Use buttons with specific copy ("Shop Artisan mixers," "Shop Bowl-Lift") instead of repeated generic "Shop Now."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Which KitchenAid fits you? Black Friday prices inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Artisan, Artisan Plus or Bowl-Lift? Compare colors and sizes, free shipping on all.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style template (`<!--V2-->`, "MI styling") with about 66 images missing alt text and no capturable authentication or unsubscribe headers. The HTML is truncated and 107 tracked links weren't probed, so link, footer and UTM checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **107 links unverified.** They all sit on tracking or click-redirect domains, so the QA probe skipped them. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Repeated third-party pixel.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` is the same LiveRamp pixel fired five times with the same `s=` hash. Only `n` changes.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `…/o.gif?mi_u=…` carry a per-recipient `mi_u` value in the query string. Any forward or "view in browser" copy will leak it.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cf1e4f62-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are tracking pixels. These are expected, but the `dv.` pixel has no file extension.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 66 images.**
  - 52 content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `71f9885c-…gif` and `971aa10f-….jpg`.
  - 7 images from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - The tracking pixels `rlcdn` (5), `o.gif` and `dv.` (7 in total).
  - The QA check can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`. The pixels should carry `alt=""` and the content images descriptive text. If images are blocked, the product and CTA imagery has no text fallback.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Responsive gap.** All responsive rules sit in `@media (max-width: 460px)`. Viewports of 461–639px get no mobile rules.
- **Conflicting `img` rules.** Both `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Source hygiene.**
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - A leftover placeholder comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, is generic, and doesn't match the subject.
  - Global resets (`*{line-height:100%}`, `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`) are broad.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The `mi_u` tokens are resolved and no unrendered `{{ }}` or `%%` tokens appear. The truncated body can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this may be a capture gap rather than a sender defect. It is unconfirmed, and one-click unsubscribe support can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so check its DNS records directly.
- **Footer content unverified.** The visible HTML doesn't include the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 107 links are redirect-wrapped and unprobed, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. Whether the Marketo-style redirects append UTMs at the destination, or whether the KitchenAid landing pages match the subject line, is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 52 content images and the 7 `mi.../rp/` images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Resolve the duplicate `img` max-width rules and add a breakpoint between 461 and 639px.
4. Consolidate the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags, escape `&` in `<title>` (or use the subject line), and strip the leftover placeholder comment.
5. Review whether five identical `rlcdn` pixels are needed, since they look like a duplicate-render bug.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also resolve the tracked links to final URLs to check UTMs and landing pages.
