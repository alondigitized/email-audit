# Hear about our Black Friday deals first when you sign up →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a text-signup acquisition email dressed up as a Black Friday teaser. The hero reads "get Black Friday deals first" over a blurry, bokeh-lit Christmas tree and gifts. The offer is "Sign up for texts now & take 10% off your next order—including holiday gifts!" The subject promises early access, but the body's real hook is the 10% off. That is the more persuasive line, and it only appears below the fold.
- After the hero, the email turns into a catalog and cross-promo sprawl. There is a holiday module, six products, a credit-card promo, an app download with a "$500" chance, a TheDesignDesk appointment and a store locator. The two buttons at the top, "Sign Up Now" and "Shop Now", work against each other. The send competes with itself, and the Black Friday teaser has no date and no real deals.
- Judgement: a decent list-growth play with a clear first CTA, but too diluted to feel like an event. The same brand sent six other mid-quality emails in the last two days, including today's mirror and kids' chair sends. Cadence fatigue works against it.

## What's working

- The hero headline is large and legible, and the "get Black Friday deals first" idea is clear.
- The sign-up copy attaches a concrete incentive, 10% off the next order.
- "Sign Up Now" is a solid dark button directly under the hero.
- The product grid shows real items with prices, such as the $14.97 Halloween treat bag marked down from $34.95. It also includes baby gear from Nuna and Babyletto.
- The footer category navigation is clean and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The 10% offer is buried in small grey body text under the hero instead of being the headline.
- The hero image is blurry and generic. It shows no product and no kids, and nothing says "Kids."
- There are two competing buttons ("Sign Up Now" and "Shop Now") right next to each other, and no hierarchy beyond fill versus outline.
- The email stacks too many unrelated modules: credit card, app with a $500 sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk and a store locator. The Black Friday message gets lost.
- The product grid sells a stale Halloween bag on October 28 beside strollers and a dresser. It has no theme, and no holiday gift angle despite the headline.
- The legal fine print is long and dense, and it hides the 10% terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the hero. Put "Text us, get 10% off + first access to Black Friday" in the headline, and drop the second "Shop Now" button so there is one clear action.
- 2. Cut the module count by at least half. Keep the sign-up, one holiday gift row and the app. Drop the credit card, the design service and the store locator from this send.
- 3. Replace the blurry tree photo with a crisp kid-and-gift image or a hero product.
- 4. Remove the Halloween item and curate the grid around holiday gifts for kids.
- 5. Add a date, such as "Black Friday starts Nov 24", so the early-access promise has weight.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Hear about our Black Friday deals first when you sign up →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a conventional XHTML table layout with a 460px mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed problems are missing `alt` attributes on 22 images (7 of them content images, including the SMS sign-up CTA) and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated before the footer. QA reports 57% pass, 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **62 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain). They carry a 64-hex `s=` value that matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` value on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels, so it looks like a shared recipient identifier. The pixel count is the five `n=` requests, and the 64-hex length is consistent with a SHA-256 hash. What it hashes can't be confirmed from this source.
- **Duplicate pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` loads 6 times with an identical `mi_u` value. Besides the `o.gif` open pixel and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/de6107f9…`, that makes about 13 tracking requests per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (7):**
  - `2026%5F1028%5FCBK%5FSMS%5FSignUp%5FPM.gif` (scene7). This is the image matching the subject-line CTA, so its sign-up message is unavailable with images off.
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `a55e00cd-0dab-4be0-898b-2774b9a04b33.jpg`
  - `480e84b4-9e90-4785-bf94-73277cc7da30.jpg`
  - `505438f2-5fee-4d03-89da-d510903ea9f7.jpg`
  - `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`
  - `cd2cef02-53e0-421d-8405-3fab2d942e82.gif`
- **Missing alt on spacers and pixels (15):** `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, plus all tracking pixels. These need `alt=""`, not descriptive text. Without the attribute, some screen readers announce the filename.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Redundant head tags:** `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type, and three separate `format-detection` metas conflict (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` versus individual tags). This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Dead CSS:** at ≥768px, both `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` and the later `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }` match. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It has no rendering impact.
- **Unverified:** the source was truncated, so the body layout, Outlook/VML fallbacks and dark-mode behavior (`color-scheme: light` is declared) could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear, though the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. QA's own note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit in the truncated portion and are unverified.
- **Privacy note:** the LiveRamp (`rlcdn.com`) pixels carrying a recipient-derived identifier are third-party data sharing. Whether the privacy policy and consent cover it is outside what the source shows.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. The 62 links were skipped and the truncated source exposes no `href` values, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment are unverified. The SMS sign-up destination (the CTA the subject line promotes) should be checked manually.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images, starting with the SMS sign-up GIF. Add `alt=""` to the spacers and the 12 or so tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and consider aligning it with the campaign.
4. Remove the 5 duplicate `rlcdn.com` pixel requests and the 6 duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests if they aren't intentional.
5. Check the raw `.eml` headers directly for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and follow the redirects on the tracking links. That covers the footer, unsubscribe, address, UTMs and landing page checks.
7. Remove the stale `IMPUT HERE` template comment and the dead 600px `max-width` rule.
