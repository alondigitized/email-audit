# Black Friday: Up to 30% off toys that top their list!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led toy send. It is the sixth Crate & Barrel Kids Black Friday email I've audited this week, and it is the first aimed squarely at toys. The hero reads "Black Friday starts now! Up to 30% off toys," with a single "Shop the Sale" button over a lifestyle photo of a child in a ball pit. Below it, a repeating module shows one toy per block: photo, sale price against regular price, a customer quote and a "Shop Now" button.
- The subject promises "toys that top their list," and the body delivers it with real products, prices and reviews. The weakness is volume. The email runs very long, and it repeats the "up to 50% off" furniture and bedding grid and the Christmas collection and Oilo blocks that earlier sends already used. After the "Starts now! Up to 20% off Babyletto & Namesake" send and "ON NOW! Up to 40% off top kitchen brands" earlier today, this reads like another near-identical promo in a pile.
- Verdict: a solid, honest, well-organised toy email, held back by cadence fatigue and a long scroll after the toy modules. At the render's displayed size I can't read every line of copy, so the details below stick to what is clearly visible.

## What's working

- The hero names the offer ("Up to 30% off toys") and has one obvious "Shop the Sale" button.
- Each toy block shows sale and regular price, which makes the discount concrete.
- Customer quotes under the products add credible social proof.
- Each product has its own "Shop Now" button, so clicks go to specific items rather than a generic page.
- The photography is warm and consistent, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long. The toy story ends around the sixth product, then furniture, bedding, Christmas, Oilo, a sweepstakes-style block and a recommendations grid follow.
- The identical "Shop Now" label on every product, plus "Shop the Sale," gives the reader no reason to pick one.
- The "starts now" language repeats what this brand has sent several times today and on 11/18. The urgency has lost credibility.
- The "up to 50% off" furniture grid contradicts the "30% off toys" headline, so the offer feels diluted.
- The free-shipping banner at the top carries an asterisk and exclusions in small print (furniture, rugs and select items). That blunts an otherwise honest offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the toy modules and the "shop all toys" CTA, and drop the furniture, bedding and Oilo blocks. This is the biggest lift because it keeps the toy focus and shortens the scroll.
- 2. Make CTAs specific, for example "Shop the ball pit," "Shop train sets" and "Shop 30% off toys."
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Toy deals end Sunday at midnight," in place of repeated "starts now" language.
- 4. Lead with a small product grid of the top three toys above the fold, with prices, instead of one lifestyle image.
- 5. Move the free-shipping exclusions out of the top banner and into the footer, or state the minimum plainly.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard Movable Ink/ESP table layout with no hard failures: the automated QA found 0 errors and 3 warnings, with a 57% pass rate. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing alt attributes, accessibility-hostile viewport and text-size meta settings, and an unescaped `&` in the `<title>`. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 97 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Link health and final destinations are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `&n=5` beacons (LiveRamp) are embedded. Each carries the same hashed identifier in the `s=` parameter. Five identical calls per open is redundant.
- **Per-recipient tokens in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` appears six times. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/6928c3cd-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are the open and tracking pixels. They resolve as designed, but the images depend on live per-recipient rendering.
- **Image hosts:** assets load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com`. The probe did not test them, so availability is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 `<img>` warnings. Content images such as `…/m/1/f9756180-d90b-4a43-ba8b-f03ddd5d87b8.jpg` and `CK_H26_KidsHolidayMagic_24x13_Edit3_Tert` carry no alt. With images blocked, the email's product and offer content is unreadable to screen readers.
- **Decorative images:** the scene7 spacers (`25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_50px_White`, `…_40px_White`) and the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry an explicit `alt=""`. Some of the warnings may be false positives if `alt=""` is already present, since the checker doesn't distinguish.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling in WebKit. The same stylesheet also sets `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`, so the rules conflict.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template hygiene:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, remains. This is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - The mobile breakpoint is only `max-width:460px`, so mid-size viewports get the desktop layout.
  - `img{max-width:600px}` (≥768px) and `img{max-width:640px}` (≥640px) overlap, and the later rule wins.
- **Title:** `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel". It does not match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters are populated. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. `Authentication-Results` is also missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so don't treat it as confirmed non-compliance. It needs a check against raw headers.
- **Sender domain:** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment can't be confirmed without the headers.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. They were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 97 click links are redirect-wrapped and were skipped, and no UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. Landing page alignment with the "Up to 30% off" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, preferably with subject-aligned text.
4. Deduplicate the `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons to one call, and remove the duplicate meta tags and the placeholder comment.
5. Inspect raw headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. Resolve the redirect chains of a sample of the 97 tracked links to confirm destinations and UTM parameters. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
