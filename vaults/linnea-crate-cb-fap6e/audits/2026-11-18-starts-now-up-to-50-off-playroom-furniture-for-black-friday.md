# Starts now: Up to 50% off playroom furniture for Black Friday →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Black Friday kickoff Crate & Barrel Kids has sent today. It follows "Our biggest sale of the year starts now!" and "STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!" earlier on 11/18. What sets it apart is the playroom furniture angle. The hero is a styled shot of nod chairs under "up to 50% off play furniture, nod chairs & more", with one Shop Now button. For the first screen, that's a clean read.
- The middle of the email is where it works best. Four single-product blocks (Jenny Lind play table, spruce green nod chair, olive play chair, sesame faux fur barrel chair) each show a sale price with the regular price struck through and their own button. That is more concrete than the generic category modules the brand usually leans on. After that it sprawls into a category grid, a rug banner, "we love these for you" recommendations, a credit card pitch, an app promo, a design-service promo and a dense legal footer.
- My judgement: this is a good hero and product spine on a bloated, repetitive send. The top strip also seems to undercut the pitch, because the free-shipping line appears to carry an exclusion for furniture, and furniture is the whole subject. The text is very small at this render size, so I'm reading that exclusion with moderate confidence.

## What's working

- The hero is aspirational and on-theme: cozy nod chairs and a wreath, with a single "up to 50% off" headline and one button.
- The four featured furniture blocks show sale and regular prices, so the discount is visible rather than implied.
- Every product block has its own Shop Now button, and the heavy dark-green and wood photography keeps the visual rhythm consistent.
- The seasonal framing ("Black Friday starts now!") fits the date and matches the brand's other sends this week.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- The top strip appears to say "Excluded furniture, rugs & select items" next to "free shipping starts now". If I'm reading that right, the email promotes furniture while excluding it from the free-shipping offer, which feels like bait-and-switch.
- The email is extremely long. After the furniture section it adds a category grid, rugs, recommendations, a credit card, the app, DesignDesk and footer links, and the original offer gets buried.
- Every button says "Shop Now", and the generic copy makes the many CTAs hard to tell apart.
- Nothing has a deadline. "Starts now" gives no end date, so there's no urgency beyond the first impression.
- It's the third near-identical Black Friday launch today, so a subscriber sees the same message again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the free-shipping and furniture contradiction. Either say plainly in the top strip that furniture ships on its own terms, or lead with a furniture-specific delivery offer. This is the single biggest trust fix.
- 2. Cut everything after the product blocks and the category grid (rugs, recommendations, credit card, app, DesignDesk). That gives a short email focused on one story.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop nod chairs" and "Shop play tables".
- 4. Add an end date or a tiered early-access window so the offer has a real deadline.
- 5. Add star ratings or a "bestseller" tag to the four featured products.
- 6. Consolidate the day's sends so one email carries the Black Friday launch, and segment the rest by category interest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nod chairs & play tables up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday playroom deals: up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jenny Lind play table from $X, plus nod chairs in 6 colors. Early deals end Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The playroom pieces everyone waits for are marked down. Shop before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout from a Marigold/Mapp-style ESP (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`), with 89 redirect-wrapped links. The confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes, a viewport that blocks zoom, and header checks that could not be verified. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** All 89 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so link health, redirect chains and final landing pages are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel repeated 5 times:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5`. This is a LiveRamp (rlcdn) beacon. Five fires per open adds requests and a third-party dependency, and it is also flagged for missing alt text.
- **Other pixels:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` looks like the open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/f233873b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` also looks like a tracking pixel.
- **Repeated image request:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with the same filename. This is probably a dynamic recommendation image, so it should be checked for rendering when images are blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 40 images flagged):**
  - Most are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `f9756180-…jpg`, `b2cb2160-…jpg`).
  - Two are on scene7: `2026%5F17%5FCBK%5FTertiary%5FWoolRugs%5FImg.gif` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - If these are product or promo images wrapped in links, blocked-image clients and screen readers get no link label.
  - For the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`), `alt=""` is the correct fix.
  - The QA check can't tell a missing `alt` from `alt=""`, so confirm which it is.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` also prevents iOS text scaling.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and does not reflect the subject line.
- **Template hygiene:**
  - A leftover placeholder comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - There are duplicate `format-detection` meta tags: one combined, then separate `date`, `address` and `telephone`.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set, and the VML and MSO conditionals are present for Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source, but the HTML was cut off, so the full body is unverified.
- A subscriber-level identifier is embedded in image URLs via `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`.
- The same 64-hex value appears as `s=` in the `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons, so a hashed subscriber ID is being sent to a third party. This is a privacy and disclosure observation. I can't confirm what the hash encodes.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers. Treat both as unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown, again likely stripped by the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is good for alignment.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** These could not be confirmed because the source is truncated. Verify them in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing pages unverified:** Every link is wrapped in an ESP redirect and was skipped, so no destination or UTM parameters could be checked. No continuity issues are confirmed.
- **Image host split:** Images load from three hosts: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. A failure on any one would break part of the layout independently.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially linked ones. Use `alt=""` on the pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` rule.
3. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` (or better, use the campaign subject), and remove the placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags.
4. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` source to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be dropping them.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links (HEAD or GET through the redirect chain) to check final URLs, UTMs and HTTP status. Raising the skip threshold for the ESP domain would close the 89-link gap.
6. Reduce the `rlcdn` beacon from 5 fires to 1, and confirm the hashed-ID sharing is covered in the privacy policy.
