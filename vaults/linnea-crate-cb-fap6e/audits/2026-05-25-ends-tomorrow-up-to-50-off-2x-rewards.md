# ENDS TOMORROW: Up to 50% off & 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' "ends tomorrow" countdown wrapper for the final stretch of the Memorial Day push — the same Double Rewards + up to 50% off creative that has been running since 5/21 with a fresh urgency label slapped on top. The countdown timer and bold offer headline are the clearest things on screen, so the hierarchy works technically. But the brand committed a meaningful copy error: the email body reads **"ends today!"** above the timer while the subject line says "ENDS TOMORROW." That's a recycled-creative tell — someone flipped the subject but didn't update the body — and any subscriber who notices it immediately loses trust in both the deadline and the brand.
- The nursery and furniture product grids are well-shot and the Double Rewards section is clearly laid out, but after five nearly identical sends in four days the email is screaming into the void. By the time you've gotten "final weekend," "ends today," and now "ends tomorrow" from the same brand in 72 hours, urgency has no meaning.
- Judgment: technically functional, creatively exhausted, operationally sloppy. The "ends today" / "ends tomorrow" mismatch alone would cause me to disengage.

## What's working

- Countdown timer is prominent at the top — gives the deadline physical weight.
- Product grids are clean and on-brand; nursery furniture, rugs, and plush items are well-shot with consistent styling.
- Double Rewards section is clearly delineated with its own CTA — loyalty benefit is not buried.
- Visual hierarchy is functional: timer → hero offer → product grid → rewards CTA flows logically.
- Star ratings are visible in at least one product section — rare in this brand's sends and adds credibility.

## What's weak

- **"Ends today!" in the body vs. "ENDS TOMORROW" in the subject** — a direct contradiction caused by recycled creative. This is the most damaging issue in the send.
- Offer is identical to at least four prior sends; no new hook, no specific product category callout, no "here's what's left."
- CTA copy is generic "SHOP NOW" throughout — no product-type specificity (contrast with the 5/25 Crate & Kids furniture send that named beds and cribs in the subject).
- No personalization of any kind — the prior Crate & Kids send that scored highest this week used segmented category framing; this one is entirely undifferentiated.
- The app promo block near the bottom competes with the sale CTAs and dilutes urgency near close.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the "ends today" vs "ends tomorrow" mismatch** — update the body banner to match the subject deadline. This is table stakes; ship nothing until this is reconciled.
- 2. **Category-segment the subject** — follow the playbook from the 5/25 furniture send ("Beds, cribs & more!") which was the best-performing subject in this window. Name a specific product type.
- 3. **Replace "SHOP NOW" with action+category copy** — "Shop Cribs & Beds," "Shop Nursery Rugs," etc. One click per category section.
- 4. **Suppress to anyone who already opened/clicked any of the four prior sends** — the subscriber most likely to convert already engaged; the rest are now fatigued.
- 5. Remove the app promo block from urgency emails — it splits attention at the wrong moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 50% off cribs, beds & nursery furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Memorial Day savings end tonight — 50% off + 2x Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double Rewards on every order through midnight — shop by category`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nursery furniture, rugs, and decor — up to half off before it's gone`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Conflicting `img` max-width media queries — wrong value wins on desktop**

```css
/* Query 1 */
@media only screen and (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }
/* Query 2 — declared after, so wins at 768px+ */
@media only screen and (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }
```

Both queries match screens ≥ 768px. Because query 2 is declared later in the stylesheet, `max-width: 640px` overrides `max-width: 600px` on all screens ≥ 640px. The 600px cap is unreachable.

**[MEDIUM] `<title>` brand mismatch**

```html
<title>Crate & Barrel</title>
```

Sender identity is "Crate & Kids SALE"; the title reflects the parent brand. Some email clients surface `<title>` in accessibility trees and screen reader announcements.

**[MEDIUM] `*` selector overrides are overly broad**

```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; max-height:1000000px; line-height: 100%; }
```

`line-height: 100%` applied globally collapses line height on multi-line text blocks across clients that honor it. `max-height:1000000px` on `*` is a Yahoo Mail hack that can cause overflow side effects in modern webmail.

**[LOW] Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags**

`format-detection` appears four times — once as a combined value and three times individually. Redundant but harmless; lint noise in template review.
