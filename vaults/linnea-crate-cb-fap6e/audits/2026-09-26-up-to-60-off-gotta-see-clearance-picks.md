# Up to 60% off gotta-see clearance picks →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Crate & Barrel Kids email that's genuinely trying to do too much, but at least leads with the thing the subject promises — "up to 60% off clearance" sits right at the top as a bold headline over a warm autumn leaves photo, with a "Shop Clearance" button immediately visible. That's the correct move. But then it sprawls: a top banner promoting a *different* 20% off baby essentials deal, a Venus Williams collab announcement, a bestsellers section, a Nuna travel system push, a full product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download plug, and a design consult offer — all stacked into one scroll.
- The clearance hero itself is clean and on-brand — moody, seasonal, matches the "gotta-see" energy of the subject line. But by the time a reader hits the fourth or fifth distinct offer, the "60% off clearance" promise from the subject has been diluted into background noise. This is the same structural problem flagged in the 2026-09-25 "Your no-stress holiday season starts…now!" send — Crate & Barrel Kids keeps building emails that are six campaigns wearing one trenchcoat.
- Judgement: solid hero, weak follow-through. Above-average open appeal on subject alone, but the body doesn't reward the click with a focused clearance shopping experience.

## What's working

- The hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — "up to 60% off clearance" as a large, legible headline over an autumn-toned lifestyle photo, immediately followed by a "Shop Clearance" CTA.
- Clear category sub-navigation right under the hero (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) gives shoppers a fast path into the sale without scrolling further.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- The Venus Williams collab section is visually distinct (green background, star treatment) so it doesn't get confused with the clearance offer.

## What's weak

- The top banner ("ending soon! up to 20% off the best baby essentials") competes directly with the hero headline for attention and creates offer confusion in the first screen.
- Too many unrelated CTAs after the fold — Venus Williams collab, bestsellers, Nuna travel system, credit card, app download, design consult — bury the clearance offer the subject sold.
- The product grid ("we love these for you") shows full prices, not clearance pricing, so it's disconnected from the "60% off" promise and reads like a generic catalog insert.
- No urgency/deadline language anywhere in the clearance section itself — "ending soon" only applies to the unrelated baby essentials banner up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill or relocate the top baby-essentials banner — it should not sit above the clearance hero when the subject line is 100% about clearance. If both offers must run, give clearance the top slot and demote baby essentials to the footer nav.
- 2. Cut the email in half. Move Venus Williams collab, Nuna travel system, and the credit card/app blocks to a separate send — this email should be "clearance, and only clearance" to match subject-to-body promise.
- 3. Add clearance pricing (strikethrough + sale price) to the "we love these for you" product grid so it visually reinforces the 60% off claim instead of looking like an unrelated catalog.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the clearance section itself (e.g., "through Sunday") — right now the only urgency language on the page is attached to a different offer entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance alert: up to 60% off kids furniture & decor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off clearance — ends this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nursery furniture, bedding & decor — final markdowns before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New styles just marked down up to 60% off`
