# It’s never too early to shop Christmas decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's "first look at holiday" seasonal-launch email, and it's a strong visual open — a genuine wreath wall hero shot immediately establishes the Christmas theme, backed by a clean six-category nav grid (decor, ornaments, stockings, garlands & wreaths, tabletop, bedding) and a single unambiguous "Shop All Christmas" CTA. That's exactly the kind of category-first structure that worked well in the 2026-09-13 "back-to-school" send I scored 7/10.
- Where it falls apart is focus: after the strong hero, the email pivots hard into two separate "TheDesignDesk" free-design-service pitches, an app-collab teaser with a mystery "her" (celebrity?) styled shot, another app/rewards card push, and only then a "we love these for you" product grid. By the time you reach actual sellable ornaments and garlands, you've scrolled past four unrelated CTAs competing for attention.
- It's a beautiful top module riding on top of an unfocused, over-stuffed body. Trim the mid-funnel modules and this jumps from a 5 to a 7.

## What's working

- The wreath-wall hero is genuinely on-theme, high production value, and instantly reads "Christmas."
- "first look! holiday 2026" + the category grid gives a clear, scannable next step for browsers who don't know what they want yet.
- "Shop All Christmas" is a specific, unambiguous primary CTA button.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real priced items (advent calendar, ornaments, gift wrap) with visible pricing — concrete and clickable.
- No render bugs: images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Two nearly identical "TheDesignDesk" modules appear back-to-back-ish (mid-email and near the footer) — redundant and dilutes both.
- The "our latest collab" module shows an unidentified silhouette/legs and cryptic copy ("this star's unmatched style") with zero product or name shown — confusing, not enticing.
- The core Christmas offer has no discount, price anchor, or urgency — it's all browse/inspiration, no reason to buy now.
- Priority is muddled: five distinct CTAs (Shop Christmas, Book Appointment x2, Download App x2, Learn More) all fight for the same click before the reader reaches actual products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut one of the two TheDesignDesk modules entirely — keep the free-design pitch once, ideally near the bottom as a secondary CTA, not competing with the hero.
- 2. Move the "we love these for you" product grid up, directly under the hero/category nav, so shoppers see real sellable items before hitting the app/design-service detours.
- 3. Fix the "latest collab" module — either name the collaborator/product or cut it; an unlabeled silhouette teaser reads as filler, not intrigue.
- 4. Add a concrete hook to the hero (e.g., "shop early access before it sells out" is implied but never quantified — add a date, % off, or "limited quantities" callout).
