# Get ready for Week 1 with the NFL Game Day Collection!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's NFL licensing tie-in, and it's a competent lifestyle-collection send that leans on team merch (branded beer glasses, monogrammed can sleeves, team pennant aprons, an actual Raiders/team candle collection) rather than a hard discount. There's no visible price cut anywhere in this email — it's positioned as a "new collection" launch, same playbook as the recent dining-room and Crate & Kids sends in this brand's history, just with a football skin on it.
- The top third does its job: "introducing the NFL game day collection" with a clean hero of branded pilsner glasses and a "Shop Now" button reads clearly and on-brand. But the email then sprawls into a long, undifferentiated scroll — kitchen, candles, robes, kids' chairs, cookware sets — loosely tied to "game day" by proximity rather than by a tight offer, which is the same structural weakness flagged in prior Crate & Barrel/Crate & Kids audits (2026-08-11 dining room send, 5.00/10).
- No sports fan is going to mistake this for a jersey or team-gear drop; it's home goods with team logos slapped on, and that's a fine niche play but it needed a sharper hook (a specific NFL matchup date, kickoff timing, "shop by team") that never shows up.

## What's working

- Hero section is unambiguous: "introducing the NFL game day collection" + visible beer glasses + "Shop Now" button lands the concept in one glance.
- Team-branded products (Packers pilsners, Raiders candle collection, 49ers monogram robe, team pennant aprons) are genuinely novel and differentiate this from generic tailgate copy.
- Section headers ("elevate every football Sunday," "cheers! may the best team win," "cook like a pro") give a light narrative thread through the scroll.
- No visible render bugs — images are crisp, buttons are consistent, no overlapping text or broken assets.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere — no %, no free shipping, no bundle price — this is pure catalog browsing dressed as urgency-free "game day" content.
- Excessively long scroll (11+ sections) dilutes the NFL hook; by the time you hit "clean kitchen" and "we love these for you" it's a generic Crate & Barrel catalog, not a football email.
- No specific team selector or "shop your team" CTA despite showing multiple teams (Packers, Raiders, 49ers) — a fan wants to filter to their team, not browse random logos.
- No kickoff date, Week 1 game date, or any real urgency despite the subject line promising "Week 1" — that specificity never appears in the body.
- Kids' chair and robe/candle sections feel like filler unrelated to "game day," diluting the football theme the subject promised.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "Shop by Team" grid near the top (logos/helmets as filter tiles) — this is the single biggest lift since the email displays 3+ teams but gives no way to jump straight to your team's gear.
- 2. Cut the scroll roughly in half — pull out kids' chairs, robes, and generic kitchen sets; keep only sections that carry visible NFL branding.
- 3. Insert a real deadline tied to the subject's "Week 1" promise (e.g., "order by [date] for kickoff delivery") — right now the urgency is only in the subject line, not in the body.
- 4. Add at least one price signal — even "starting at $X" on the hero glass set would give the eye something concrete to act on.
