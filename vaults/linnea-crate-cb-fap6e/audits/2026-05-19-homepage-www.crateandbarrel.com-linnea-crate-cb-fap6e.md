# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Crate & Barrel is very much in Memorial Day sale mode — outdoor furniture, sofas, the whole warm-weather push. As a brand I love and trust, it reads immediately as the right place, but the first thing I actually see is an email-capture modal that blocks pretty much everything behind it. They're pitching a rewards-driven, sale-heavy event, but I'm coming here registry-first, and nothing above the fold is speaking to that.

## What's working

- **Rewards banner at the very top** — "Earn 10%, 20% Back in Rewards Dollars" with a deadline (5/30/2026) is the right loyalty hook for a full-price-tolerant shopper like me; I actually track my rewards balance.
- **Memorial Day offer is legible** — "up to 60% off" reads clearly even with the modal overlay; the discount depth is real.
- **Navigation bar is well-structured** — "Tabletop & Bar" is right there in the main nav, one tap from my actual category, and I don't have to hunt.
- **Aesthetic is on-brand** — what little I can see of the hero imagery (warm patio tones, clean whites) feels like the CB I know and trust.

## What's weak

- **The email modal is the entire page** — it fires before I've seen anything, forces email + phone, and the "10% off" has an asterisk leading to wall-of-text fine print. This is the first thing I see and it makes me want to close-and-bounce.
- **Hero is outdoor furniture, not my category** — I'm here for dinnerware, a registry, or walnut pieces. The whole Memorial Day push skews patio/sofa. I have to work to find my section.
- **No registry-entry point visible** — Crate & Barrel is *the* registry destination for my life stage, and there is zero above-the-fold signal pointing me toward it.
- **"Apply Now" CTA for rewards card is awkward** — sitting next to "Shop Outdoor Furniture" it reads as a credit card ad, not a loyalty benefit. Feels misplaced.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the immediate modal or delay it to exit-intent** — fire it on scroll-out or after 30 seconds; right now it's the first thing I see and I'm closing it before I've formed a positive impression.
- **Add a registry CTA in the hero or above-the-fold nav strip** — even a sticky "Building your registry? Start here →" chip would convert someone in my exact life stage at a disproportionate rate.
- **Swap one hero column to Tabletop & Bar / Kitchen** — Memorial Day for dishes and entertaining ware is a legitimate pitch; "Up to 40% off registry essentials" with a walnut-finish serving board lifestyle shot would catch my eye immediately.
- **Replace the asterisk-heavy modal copy with something cleaner** — "Sign up, get 10% off your next order" in plain language, no footnote wall, no phone-number field on the first ask.
