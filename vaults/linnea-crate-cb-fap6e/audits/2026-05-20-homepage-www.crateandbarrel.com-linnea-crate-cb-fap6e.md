# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is leading hard with Memorial Day right now — "up to 60% off" is plastered everywhere and the Reward Dollars earn rates are bumped up. The vibe is premium home with a seasonal urgency push, which honestly fits my taste level, but I barely got to see any of it because a full-screen email-capture modal slammed into my face the second the page loaded. The bones are good; the first experience is annoying.

## What's working

- **"up to 60% off the memorial day event" banner** — Memorial Day is landing right now and the offer is concrete, not vague. As someone who's building out a full apartment, this actually matters to me.
- **"Tabletop & Bar" in the nav** — I can see it clearly in the top category strip. One tap to my category. That's exactly what I want.
- **Reward Dollars earn rate bump** — "Earn 10% → 20% back" is a meaningful loyalty signal and it's above the fold. I'm already enrolled, so this is a reason to buy now.
- **"Wedding + Registry" link in the secondary nav** — visible in the utility bar up top. Directly relevant to my situation.
- **Lifestyle imagery** — the outdoor/lounge scene coming through behind the modal looks clean and aspirational, not cluttered.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen email-capture modal on first load** — I have not opted into anything yet and I'm already being asked for my phone number. This is the worst possible first impression. I immediately want to hit "Maybe Later" and feel annoyed.
- **Zero dinnerware or tabletop above the fold** — I'm a registry shopper specifically hunting modern minimal plates and glasses. The entire above-fold experience is about sofas and outdoor furniture. Relevant to me eventually, but not my entry point today.
- **No registry-specific hook** — "Wedding + Registry" is one small text link in the utility nav. Given that registry customers are among the highest-intent, highest-AOV shoppers on the site, there's no hero, no "complete your registry" CTA, nothing.
- **Modal SMS fine print** — the text in the modal body is tiny and dense, covering FCC autodialer disclosures and cancellation terms. Feels like a legal trap rather than a brand conversation.
- **"Breathe Instantly Event" module** — partially visible below the modal and I have no idea what that is. Confusing name, zero context.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the modal for a slim bottom-of-screen banner** — "Get 10% off + be first to see new arrivals" in a slide-up strip respects the browse session and converts better for Millennial shoppers who immediately dismiss overlays.
- **Add a registry-entry hero tile** — a second carousel slide targeting "Recently registered? Finish your list while Memorial Day prices are live" would convert me specifically right now.
- **Bring tabletop imagery above the fold** — even a small editorial tile in the "Lounge Sofa / Breathe Instantly" row showing a walnut serving board or a modern place setting would stop my scroll. Right now there's nothing for me until I dig into nav.
- **Name "Breathe Instantly" more clearly** — if it's bedding or bath, say "up to 40% off bedding." The clever name loses me on mobile where I can't see what it is.
