# 🚨Ends tonight: Earn 2x Rewards & get up up 60% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Crate & Kids kitchen-sink marathon-scroll template I've now audited well over a dozen times this week — countdown clock up top ("00:07:20:26"), "earn 10%-20% back in Reward Dollars" banner, then the full room-by-room grid: nursery furniture, bedding, rugs, decor/lighting, clearance, a sweepstakes, "we love these for you" product carousel, app download, and footer. Nothing here is broken, but nothing here is new either — subject line typo ("get up up 60% off") is the one distinguishing feature, and not in a good way.
- The offer itself is real and the template executes cleanly (no image breaks, clear pricing tiers per section), but this is the identical creative I've now seen with a countdown clock reading down from roughly 16 hours to 7 hours across sends this week. A subscriber opening five of these in six days learns to stop opening them.
- Judgement: competent execution of a fatigued campaign, undercut by a sloppy subject line typo that actively signals low quality-control on this particular send.

## What's working

- Countdown clock creates real urgency and is legible at the top of the render.
- Clear tiered structure — each section pairs a specific category (bedding, rugs, decor, clearance) with its own reward/discount callout.
- "Ships free" and "100s of items clearance" call out concrete, scannable offers.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject line contains a visible typo: "get up up 60% off" — this is the most glaring, avoidable flaw in the entire send.
- This is the same template/offer structure as at least a dozen prior sends this week (2026-09-08, 2026-09-08, 2026-09-08, 2026-09-08 all near-identical) — zero creative refresh.
- Subject uses an emoji + urgency stack ("🚨Ends tonight") that reads as spammy after this many repeats.
- Extremely long scroll (11,227px) buries the sweepstakes and product carousel far below the fold where most recipients on mobile will never reach.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line typo before anything else ships — "get up up 60% off" undermines trust in every other polished thing in this email.
- 2. Break the cadence: this is at minimum the 13th near-identical Labor Day send this week per brand history — either consolidate into fewer, higher-quality sends or clearly differentiate this one (final-hours-only single CTA, not the full room grid).
- 3. Move the sweepstakes and "TheDesignDesk" service higher — those are actual differentiators buried at the bottom of an 11k-pixel scroll.
- 4. Trim the countdown language variety across sends — the fact that seven+ sends this week all lead with a similar clock but different subject phrasing looks like spray-and-pray, not urgency.

## Technical Audit

Standard MessageGears/Cheetah-style HTML email with heavy tracking pixel load and no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured; core rendering markup (MSO conditionals, media queries) is well-formed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Multiple open/redirect tracking pixels present from distinct vendors: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/...` (fired 6x with identical `mi_u` param), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/...` (open pixel), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp/RampID, fired 5x sequentially with `n=1`–`n=5`), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...` (Movable Ink/dynamic content pixel). The repeated firing of the same pixel URL 5-6 times is typical for multi-zone dynamic content but inflates per-open network calls.

- Client-side coding is legacy/table-based with standard Outlook (`mso`) and Apple Mail (`ExternalClass`) fixes in place; `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` meta tags are correctly set.
- `<style>` block includes both a general 460px mobile breakpoint and separate 640px/768px desktop image max-width rules — no conflicts observed in the truncated source.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` are hardcoded to `light` only — no dark-mode-specific handling, which can cause contrast/inversion issues in Outlook/Apple Mail dark mode (not flagged by QA but visible in source).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
