# The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event: Up to 30% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, on-brand Thanksgiving dining event send. The offer is up to 30% off dining tables, seating, storage and select lighting, and the subject and hero both say so. The hero is a styled dining room with the headline "the Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off" and a Shop Now button. For someone shopping for a table or chairs ahead of the holiday, it lands.
- The email is very long, though. After the dining hero and the four-tile category grid, it moves through a Hosting Guide banner, two stacked buttons, a rug and accent-furniture promo, a Design Desk sweepstakes, Affirm financing, a product carousel, a credit card ad, an app download and another Design Desk push. Only the top third supports the subject. This repeats the pattern in the brand's recent sends. On 10/21 the "ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!" email was a tight single-message send. "Starts now! Up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs" and "Let's nail your holiday aesthetic" both mixed in unrelated modules. The 10/21 design-services email also used the same "Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30%" banner, so this send is the main event after a teaser.
- Judgement: a solid mid-pack promo with a clear hero. It buries its best offer under too many secondary modules and has no deadline.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. "Up to 30% off" appears in both, and the email delivers on the promise.
- The four-tile grid (dining tables, seating, storage, lighting) with a "% off" label on each gives shoppers an easy way into their category.
- The hero is a styled, aspirational room that fits Crate & Barrel and the season.
- The featured products show sale and regular prices, which makes the discount concrete.
- The Shop Now and "Shop The Dining Furniture Event" buttons are clearly labeled.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline. "Up to 30% off" with "select styles" never says when it ends, so there is no reason to click today.
- The email has too many competing modules: sweepstakes, Affirm, credit card, app, Design Desk twice and a store locator. They dilute the dining message.
- The Thanksgiving Hosting Guide banner puts its title over a busy photo, which makes it hard to read. Two stacked buttons below it ("Shop The Dining Furniture Event" and "Shop All Dining Furniture") are near-duplicates.
- The offer is less clear than it looks. "Up to 30%" is applied to "select styles", and the lighting tile shows a lower 20% off.
- The sweepstakes ("chance to win $5000") pulls attention from the sale and sends people to a service appointment instead of a purchase.
- The footer fine print is long, which weakens the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the dining event: hero, category grid, 4-6 products and one footer. Move the sweepstakes, financing, credit card and app promos into other sends or a slim footer strip. This is the change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a credible deadline to the hero and subject, for example "Ends Sunday" or "Through 10/27", and a countdown or date in the first banner.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop Now" with "Shop dining tables" and give each category tile its own button.
- 4. Merge the two stacked dining buttons into one. Put the Hosting Guide in the footer area or drop it.
- 5. Make the discount depth clearer, e.g. "30% off dining tables, 20% off lighting", instead of a blanket "up to".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Thanksgiving table, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, chairs & storage on sale. Free design help to get your room ready by Thursday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Seat everyone in style. Shop dining sets before the sale ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a Movable Ink personalization layer. The truncated source shows no broken markup or unrendered tokens, but I could not confirm the unsubscribe link, the UTM parameters, or the authentication headers. The main confirmed issues are about 29 `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag. The header warnings (below) are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verified:** 64 tracking links were skipped because they go through click-redirect domains. The truncated HTML shows none of the `href` values, so I can't confirm any destination, redirect chain, or link health.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (LiveRamp) fire from the email body. Each is a separate request to an external domain, and the `n=` counter suggests the same call is repeated.
- **First-party tracking:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7d28d0a6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression pixels. Both are expected.
- **Identifier sharing:** the hash in `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` has the same prefix as the `s=` value sent to `rlcdn.com`. A per-recipient identifier appears to be passed to a third-party ad-data domain. I can't tell what it hashes, but it is a privacy consideration.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** the QA scan lists at least 29 `<img>` tags without `alt`, and its last entry is cut off, so the true count may be higher.
  - About 22 are content images:
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg, png and gif)
    - `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/09%5FCore/…Thanksgiving…`
    - Seven Movable Ink product images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`
  - The other seven are tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). They need `alt=""`.
  - With images blocked, the content images render as blank space for screen-reader and image-blocking users.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` turns off text-size adjustment everywhere.
- **Dead CSS rule:** two `img` max-width rules overlap. `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule never applies.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder comment (and misspelled).
  - The `<meta name="format-detection">` tag appears three times with differing values.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype and is generic.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `charset=utf-8`, and Outlook VML/Office namespaces and `mso` conditionals.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. Personalization looks like Movable Ink dynamic images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…?mi_u=…`). I could not verify that those image URLs resolve, because they were not probed and the body is truncated. No other issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found.
- `Authentication-Results`: not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The QA tool itself says these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. I am not treating them as confirmed defects at the sender. Check the raw `.eml` before concluding anything.
- The visible From is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`. It is a subdomain of the brand domain and matches the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` asset host.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 64 click-tracking links were skipped by the probe, and the `href` values are outside the truncated source. I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing-page status codes, or whether the redirects resolve to the Thanksgiving dining event page. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text:** give every content image a meaningful `alt` (the `image.mail…` and `s7d5.scene7.com` images and the seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` product images). Give the seven tracking pixels `alt=""`.
2. **Fix the viewport tag:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Fix text-size rules:** drop `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and keep the `100%` setting on `body` and `html`.
4. **Re-audit with the full source:**
   - Re-run with the full raw `.eml` and the untruncated HTML.
   - Check the three headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`).
   - Confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address.
   - Resolve a sample of the 64 tracking links and check the UTMs on the final URLs.
5. **Review third-party tracking:** have someone review the five repeated `rlcdn.com` pixel calls and the hashed `mi_u` identifier shared with LiveRamp. They are a minor performance cost and a privacy and consent consideration.
6. **Clean up the template:** remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment, the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and the dead 600px `img` rule. Escape the `&` in `<title>`.
