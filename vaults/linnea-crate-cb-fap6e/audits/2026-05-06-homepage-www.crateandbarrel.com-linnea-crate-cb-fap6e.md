# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Opening Crate & Barrel right now means getting immediately swallowed by an email-capture modal before I can see anything. Once I dismiss it, the brand looks exactly like what I expect — warm, modern, slightly aspirational — but the whole above-the-fold story is about outdoor furniture, which has nothing to do with why I registered here. This is a full-spring outdoor push, and I am very much a tabletop-and-entertaining person.

## What's working

- **"Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" top banner** — I'm registry-shopping and full-price tolerant, but a 10% rewards loop is genuinely useful over the course of building out a whole kitchen.
- **"Registry" link in utility nav** — tiny but it's there; as a recently registered person, this matters to me instantly.
- **Aesthetic of the hero** — warm sand tones, organic shapes, clean lines. Even though it's outdoor furniture, the visual language matches the brand I trust for my registry picks.
- **"built-to-last bestsellers" module** — the headline framing is exactly the quality signal I want when I'm registering for things I'll keep for 10 years.
- **Category nav breadth** — "Tabletop & Bar" and "Kitchen" are both visible in one tap, so I know my categories exist even if they're not the hero.

## What's weak

- **Modal popup asking for both email AND phone** — before I've seen a single product, C&B is demanding two contact methods plus a TCPA consent block. I almost always hit "Maybe Later" and feel slightly annoyed.
- **Hero is outdoor furniture, not my world** — I'm here to build a dinnerware registry. The 30% off patio sets is irrelevant to me.
- **No registry-specific hook above the fold** — if you know I'm a recently registered person (or even just that it's wedding season), show me something. Not a patio.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for a high-AOV registry shopper, this matters. It's nowhere on the first screen.
- **No product imagery that matches my aesthetic focus** — walnut, ceramic, minimal table settings. None of that is in the viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a registry moment** — it's spring, prime wedding season; a "Build Your Registry" editorial hero with a styled minimal tablescape would convert people like me far better than patio chairs.
- **Add a free-shipping callout to the rewards banner** — one line: "Free shipping on orders $X+" alongside the rewards message turns a passive banner into an active nudge.
- **Make the modal single-field** — email only, lead with the 10% offer prominently, ditch the phone requirement and TCPA paragraph. I'll give my email for 10% off; I won't give my phone number before I've seen a product.
- **Surface a "Registry Picks" or "New in Tabletop" module above the bestsellers section** — even a slim editorial strip that acknowledges the registry journey would make me feel seen.
