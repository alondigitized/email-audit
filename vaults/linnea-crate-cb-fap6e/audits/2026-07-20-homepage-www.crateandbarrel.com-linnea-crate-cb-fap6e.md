# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is showing me a cozy, editorial living room hero — dark wood, warm lighting, elevated layering — which honestly hits close to my aesthetic. But the first thing I actually see is a full-screen email capture modal blocking the entire page, and *that's* the brand experience right now. Underneath it, they're pushing furniture hard, with a sofa as the lead story and category tiles for rugs, lighting, and accent chairs.

## What's working

- **Living room hero imagery** — the dark wood sideboard, layered rugs, pendant lighting, and warm-toned sofa is basically my Pinterest board. Whoever art-directed this knew the customer.
- **"The best sofa you'll ever own" headline** — confident, not over-designed. It doesn't hedge. I respect that.
- **"Shop New Furniture" + "Shop Sofas & Sectionals" dual CTAs** — giving me two entry points immediately is smart; I might want to browse broadly or go straight to a specific category.
- **Category tiles below the modal** — rugs, lighting, accent chairs, accent tables, pillows, botanicals, media consoles, dining furniture — these are exactly the categories I'm building a registry from.
- **Top banner offer** — I can see "Save 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" in the header strip, which is a loyalty signal even if it's small.

## What's weak

- **The email modal is the first interaction** — I'm here to browse my registry or explore the new furniture collection and the site greets me with a lightbox demanding my email and phone number. "Maybe Later" in light gray against white is the lowest-priority button they could've designed.
- **The modal copy is misleading** — "Save 10% off full-price items\*" has an asterisk in the headline, and the fine print is a dense paragraph of legal text. That asterisk kills the momentum.
- **Registry navigation is buried** — "Wedding Registry" appears in the nav but I have to hunt for it through a crowded top bar. For someone who is actively managing a registry, this is not front and center.
- **No product pricing in the hero** — I can see category tiles starting at $3.65(?), but nothing in the hero tells me what the sofa costs. I have no anchor.
- **Returning customer friction** — there's no "Welcome back, Linnea" energy. The site treats me like a stranger every time.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for logged-in or cookied users** — if I've been on the site before or have an account, don't make me dismiss a signup form. Recognize me.
- **Pin a registry quick-link in the hero or top nav** — "Managing your registry? Pick up where you left off →" as a personalized banner would feel like the brand actually knows me.
- **Show starting prices or a specific anchor product in the hero** — "Starts at $1,299" next to the sofa CTA reduces friction and sets expectations before I click through.
- **Replace "Maybe Later" with a stronger dismiss** — make the X button obvious and the opt-out copy at least neutral-tone ("No thanks"). Burying the exit is a dark pattern that makes me distrust the brand.
