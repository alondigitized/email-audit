## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO/VML fallbacks. It has about 53 images with missing alt text, 86 click-tracked links that couldn't be resolved, and several header-level compliance checks that couldn't be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most link `href`s weren't available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 86 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking or click-redirect domains. Final destinations, redirect health, and UTM carry-through can't be confirmed from the data.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire on one open. Each carries the same 64-character recipient-keyed `s=` value, so the repetition adds nothing and exposes a recipient identifier to a third party.
- **Open/click beacons:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/fcc9fd8f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` also carry per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) in the URL.
- **Plain-text part:** the plain-text part is 17,524 of 23,928 characters URLs (about 73%). This is above the 70% warning threshold and is a mild spam-filter signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 53 images:**
  - 38 are content images under `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` or `/m/47/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `63834636-…gif`, `caa9f5d4-…jpg`, `ec9ea9bb-…jpg`.
  - 8 more are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - The rest are tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`). For these, alt should be present and empty (`alt=""`).
- **Content images:** for images that carry content, no alt text means screen readers have nothing to read. It also means the message degrades to blank blocks when images are blocked.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **XHTML validity:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Markup cleanup:** `format-detection` is declared three times, once combined (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) and twice split (`date=no`, then `address=no`, then `telephone=no`). There is also a leftover template placeholder comment (`<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`) and empty `<!---->` comments. These are harmless but are template debris.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this should be checked against the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer:** the footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 86 click links route through tracking domains and were not resolved, and the truncated HTML doesn't show the `href` query strings. No UTM parameters were observed, so their presence or absence is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, especially the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/…` assets. Set `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Drop the four duplicate `rlcdn` pixels (`n=2` to `n=5`) and review whether the LiveRamp pixel and its recipient-keyed identifier are needed.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
5. Inspect the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The relay is the likely reason these weren't captured.
6. Trim the plain-text part, which is 73% URLs, by shortening tracked URLs or adding more descriptive text.
7. Rerun the audit with the full, untruncated HTML and a redirect-following probe, so the footer compliance, final URLs, and UTM parameters can be checked.
