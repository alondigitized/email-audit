# Starts now! Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a free-shipping email from Crate & Barrel Kids with too many competing messages. The subject leads with "Starts now! Free shipping on orders $99+". The top banner, though, is "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off", and the hero is a child playing with a toy. Free shipping only appears below that, as a headline with a pale "Shop All Free Shipping" button. The strongest offer in the render (up to 50% off) isn't the one the subject promises.
- After the shipping block there is a six-tile category grid (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, holiday), a second shipping button, a baby gear block, a Halloween shop, "we love these for you" product picks, a credit card promo, an app download and a Design Desk appointment. That's eight or nine different asks in one scroll. The product picks with prices ($764.15, $314.95 and similar) are the most useful part for a parent shopping nursery. The email also repeats the free-shipping $99+ message from the "STARTS NOW: Free shipping on orders $99+" send earlier today, so a subscriber sees the same promo twice in one day.
- Verdict: a competent catalog email that is diluted by clutter and by the mismatch between subject and hero. The offer is real but the creative doesn't commit to it.

## What's working

- The "we love these for you" block shows named products with visible prices, including sale prices, so there is concrete detail to click on.
- The category grid (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, holiday) is clean, with consistent photography and easy tap targets.
- The top banner states a clear, time-relevant offer: "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off."
- The baby gear block on a dark green background gives the eye a clear break and a visible "Shop All Baby Gear" button.
- The seasonal Halloween shop block fits the date.

## What's weak

- The subject promises free shipping, but the hero is a toy photo under a 50%-off event banner. The first screen doesn't deliver the subject's offer.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is a pale grey-blue with low contrast, so the main CTA is hard to spot. It also doesn't say what the shopper will find.
- The email has too many stacked asks: shipping, categories, baby gear, Halloween, products, credit card, app, Design Desk and store finder. The primary CTA gets lost.
- Free shipping is stated twice (once above and once below the grid) while the 50% event banner and the free-shipping headline compete with each other.
- The long fine-print block at the bottom is dense and tiny, and it covers several overlapping promotions.
- This is the same free-shipping promo that went out earlier today, and the hero image barely differs in intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Put "Free shipping on $99+, starts now" over the hero image with a high-contrast button, and move the 50%-off event into a secondary strip.
- 2. Darken the CTA buttons so they stand out against the white background, and use verb-plus-noun copy such as "Shop nursery with free shipping."
- 3. Cut the secondary modules. Drop the credit card, app and Design Desk blocks from a promo send, or keep at most one.
- 4. Remove the duplicate free-shipping button below the category grid.
- 5. Space out the free-shipping sends. A second one on the same day should lead with something different, such as the baby gear or the Halloween shop.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Nursery event: up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off bedroom & nursery. Ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with MSO conditionals, sent through a Marigold/Mobile Inbox-style (MI) stack. The QA run found missing alt text on many images, 69 tracking links that weren't probed, and no captured compliance or authentication headers. The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the footer, the unsubscribe link, or the physical address.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- QA skipped the HTTP probe on 69 click-redirect links, so no destination, redirect-chain or status-code validation was done. This is an unverified area, not a confirmed defect.
- Tracking and pixel resources found:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2522e321-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (open/view tracker)
  - 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` pixels. These are third-party LiveRamp (rlcdn.com) syncs, which means third-party data sharing from the email body. The `s=` parameter is a 64-hex-character hash that looks like a hashed identifier.
  - 6 `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` repeated pixels.
- The `mi_u` value (`dbe8da718e…`) is the same in both the `mi.` and `rlcdn` URLs. That is consistent with one recipient identifier being passed to a third party.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Confirmed by QA: about 30 `<img>` elements have no alt text.
  - Content images: 15 or more on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `3e71a7cd-…gif`. Without alt text, image-blocked clients show blank space and screen readers read out the filename. If these images carry the offer ("Free shipping on orders $99+") or the CTAs, that information is lost.
  - Spacer images: `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, twice. These should have `alt=""`.
  - Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `rlcdn 448796.gif` ×5, `f3c0d229…png` ×6, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should also have `alt=""`. QA cannot tell a missing attribute from an intentionally empty one, so some of these may be false positives.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. It is invalid XHTML under the declared DOCTYPE, but it is low impact.
- The HTML has duplicate and conflicting meta tags: `<meta charset>` alongside `http-equiv Content-Type`, and `format-detection` declared 4 times. This is harmless but redundant.
- The `*{… line-height:100%}` universal selector and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` disable text zoom on iOS. The source also has leftover template placeholders (`<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, "IMPUT" typo).
- `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both rules match at 768px and above, so the later 640px rule overrides the first, which makes the 600px rule dead code.
- The viewport has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- The template includes `<!--V2-->` before `<html>`, which puts a comment before the root element. Some parsers may handle this differently, but this is minor.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the truncated source or QA output.
- The `mi_u` and `s=` values are per-recipient hashed identifiers. They are present and populated.
- The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- WARN: `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. QA says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. The header cannot be confirmed or ruled out from this capture. If it is really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (5,000+ messages/day) require it.
- WARN: `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) was not found. The same capture caveat applies, and the same bulk-sender requirement applies.
- WARN: `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Alignment could not be verified from this data.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the postal address are in the truncated portion. They were not verified.
- The 57% pass rate is mostly driven by the alt-text warnings, not by hard failures (0 issues).

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links are wrapped in click-redirect domains, and QA skipped probing them. UTM parameters and final landing pages therefore could not be checked.
- The visible URLs are not exposed in the truncated source, so I can't tell whether UTM parameters are applied before the redirect or after it.
- The subject "Free shipping on orders $99+" cannot be matched to a landing page from this data.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially any that carry the offer or CTA text (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*`). Use `alt=""` on spacers and pixels (`25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`, `o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d229….png`).
2. Check the raw message headers outside the AgentMail relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and a passing SPF/DKIM/DMARC result. Re-run QA with the original headers preserved.
3. Reduce the 6 repeated `f3c0d229….png` pixels and the 5 `rlcdn` pixels to one each, if the duplication is not needed, and review the third-party `rlcdn.com` sharing for privacy and consent disclosure.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom accessibility.
5. Escape the `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;`, and clean up the duplicated `format-detection` metas, the dead `max-width:600px` rule, and the leftover "IMPUT HERE" template comment.
6. Probe the redirect chains with a real GET, following redirects, to confirm UTM params and the landing page. The 69 links were skipped by QA.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and the tokens past the truncation point can be checked.
