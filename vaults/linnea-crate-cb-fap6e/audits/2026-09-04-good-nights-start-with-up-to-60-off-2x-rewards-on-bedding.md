# Good nights start with up to 60% off & 2x Rewards on bedding→
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' bedding-focused cut of the same Labor Day template the whole Crate & Barrel family has run all week — but unlike the kitchen-sink versions I've flagged repeatedly (2026-09-03, 2026-09-04's "New furniture added" send), this one actually stays on-message with its subject: bedding is the hero, and the top of the email is a clean, cozy lifestyle shot with "up to 60% off bedding + ships free." That's a real improvement in focus.
- Where it falls apart is length and repetition of the offer. After the bedding hero and a full crib/toddler bedding module, the email pivots to Jellycat stuffed animals, then repeats the Double Rewards banner a second time, then pivots again to a $5,000 sweepstakes for The Design Desk, then a "we love these for you" product grid, then a credit card pitch, then an app download pitch. It's the same problem as the other sends in this family this week: one good hero idea diluted by five unrelated modules stacked below it.
- Net: better subject-to-hero alignment than the sibling sends this week, but still a kitchen-sink body that will lose most scrollers well before the design pro sweepstakes or credit card pitch.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are aligned — "up to 60% off bedding + ships free" matches "Good nights start with..." and the top visual is an actual bed with bedding.
- Clear, specific pricing shown on individual SKUs (sale price vs. reg price) for both kids and baby bedding modules.
- "Back in stock: bestselling Jellycat stuffed animals" is a legitimate, specific restock callout, not vague filler.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact end to end.

## What's weak

- Double Rewards banner appears twice (top and again mid-email as "Limited Time Offer") — redundant within a single send.
- Five distinct offers competing for attention: bedding sale, Jellycat restock, Double Rewards, $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, credit card signup — no single CTA dominates.
- The $5,000 sweepstakes and credit card modules have nothing to do with "good nights" / bedding and dilute the theme the subject promised.
- This is the fourth-plus near-identical Labor Day template from this brand family in two days (per prior audits on 2026-09-03 and 2026-09-04) — cadence fatigue is real for a repeat subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the baby bedding module. Everything from Jellycat onward (restock, second rewards banner, sweepstakes, cross-sell grid, credit card, app) should be a separate send or dropped — this alone would double effective engagement with the actual bedding offer.
- 2. Keep only one Double Rewards mention, ideally folded into the hero banner rather than repeated as its own module.
- 3. If Jellycat and the sweepstakes must stay, move them to a "you might also like" footer, not full-width modules with their own CTAs.
- 4. Test subject lines that lean harder into the specific bedding percentage vs. the generic "good nights" framing:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off bedding, ships free — Labor Day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bedding refresh: 60% off + 2x Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cotton quilts, comforters & shams from $34.99 — free shipping ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: Jellycat is back in stock`
