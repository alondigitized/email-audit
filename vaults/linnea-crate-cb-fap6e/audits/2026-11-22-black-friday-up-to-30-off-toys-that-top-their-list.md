# Black Friday: Up to 30% off toys that top their list!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids' toy-focused Black Friday send, and it's the best-targeted of the week's near-identical blasts. The hero reads "Black Friday is on! Up to 30% off toys*" with a single "Shop the Sale" button. Below it, six hero products each get a big lifestyle photo, a sale-vs-regular price, a one-line customer quote and a "Shop Now" button. They are a pop-up ball pit, a 45-piece wooden train set, an art easel, a canvas play tent, a workbench and a rocking horse.
- The product-per-block structure beats the category-tile grids in the 11-22 omnibus send ("Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →") and the kitchen send. A parent can see an exact item, a price and social proof without clicking. The weakness is length. After the six products it keeps going with a 2x4 category grid, a Babyletto/Namesake promo, a sign-up offer, a play-furniture banner, a Food Bank/Crate & Kids Giving block and a "we love these for you" row. By the end the toy message is diluted, and this reads as the fifth or sixth Black Friday send in three days.
- Judgement: a solid, clearly targeted toy send. The product-and-quote format is a real strength, but the long tail of competing modules and the sixth-send fatigue hold it at the upper-middle of the range.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "Up to 30% off toys*" with one button, and the toy focus matches the subject line.
- Each product block is repeatable and scannable: big photo, sale and regular price, a short quote, a button.
- The customer quotes ("Best train set! Fantastic gift for my grandchildren") add social proof that the other recent sends lacked.
- The images are warm and holiday-ready, with kids playing, a wreath and a tree.
- A free-shipping bar sits on top ("on orders $99+"), so the shipping incentive is visible before the hero.

## What's weak

- After the toys, the email adds furniture, Babyletto and Namesake, bedding, a sign-up offer and a charity block. That is too many competing asks.
- The asterisk on "30% off toys*" and the tiny fine print leave the exclusions unclear.
- The mid-email category grid has small text and tiny tiles, and it is hard to read on mobile.
- The sign-up block ("save 10%") sits mid-email and interrupts the shopping flow for people who are already subscribers.
- The full-width product photos make the email very tall, so the hero offer and the first product are about all that is visible without scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the toy grid except one module, such as the "we love these for you" row, or move it above the category grid. Keep the email about toys.
- 2. Make the deadline concrete in the hero, for example "Ends Monday", rather than a general Black Friday message. Also spell out the exclusions in the visible text.
- 3. Pair the six products into a 2x3 grid for scanning. Keep the quote on each one. Add a "Shop all toys" button at the bottom of the grid.
- 4. Remove the sign-up block for existing subscribers, or put it at the very bottom.
- 5. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with item-specific copy, such as "Get the train set".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a standard table-based XHTML template, but about 51 images lack alt text, and the viewport meta tag disables zoom. Compliance headers and link destinations couldn't be verified: the QA relay didn't capture the headers, and all 100 tracked links were skipped. The HTML source was also truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **100 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination resolution, redirect chains and dead-link status are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…5a8&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixel requests. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, probably a hashed subscriber identifier, sent to a third party on each open. Five pixels in one send add load and extra requests. Confirm the privacy-policy disclosure covers this.
- **First-party tracking pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel), `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d24d5580-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` repeated 6 times. The `mi_u` per-recipient ID is embedded in image URLs, so it will be exposed if the email is forwarded.
- **Image hosting:** images load from `scene7.com`, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `mi.…`, `dv.…` and `rlcdn.com`. This is four or more domains. Each is a separate DNS lookup and a possible blocking point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 51 images** (QA warnings). The content images are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (.jpg and .gif) and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these are announced by filename (for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`) or skipped. This is a WCAG 1.1.1 failure on the content images.
- **Decorative and tracking images should use `alt=""`.** These are `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F50px%5FWhite` (scene7), `o.gif`, the `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, the `448796.gif` ×5 and the `dv.…` pixel. An absent attribute reads differently from an empty one in assistive tech.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user scaling (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize is blocked:** the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` rule prevents iOS text resizing.
- **Invalid HTML under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and should be `&amp;`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed, and it duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type meta.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately.
- **Template residue:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` remain in the head. These are harmless but add bytes and show an unfinished template.
- **Generic title:** `<title>` is "Crate & Barrel", not the subject line or the Kids brand.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer were not fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them. This is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (5,000+ per day), so verify against raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. It is a subdomain, so check that its DKIM `d=` aligns with it.
- **The footer was not visible in the truncated source.** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address (CAN-SPAM) could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 100 links are click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to 30% off toys" offer are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~37 content images (product and promo tiles). Set `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` wildcard with `100%`.
3. Consolidate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, or remove the duplicates. Check that the data-sharing disclosure covers them.
4. Fix the markup: `&amp;` in the title, a single charset meta, one `format-detection` meta, and the leftover template comments removed.
5. Re-run QA on the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve the redirect chains with a follow-redirects probe, and check that UTMs and the landing page match the offer.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and merge tokens.
