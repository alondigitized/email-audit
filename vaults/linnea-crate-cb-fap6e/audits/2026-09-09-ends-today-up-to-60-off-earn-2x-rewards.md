# ENDS TODAY: Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the Crate & Kids sibling of the exact same Labor Day marathon-scroll template I've now audited well over a dozen times this week — a beach-toned hero ("Ends Today! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event"), a grid of category tiles (kids furniture, nursery bedding, baby bedding, nursery chairs, rugs, lighting), then a full second act for TheDesignDesk, a Jellycat restock module, a "we love these for you" personalized-looking product grid, a Crate Card upsell, and an app-download pitch before the footer. It's clean, on-brand, and functionally identical to the Crate & Barrel version — just re-skinned for the kids/nursery audience.
- The "Ends Today" urgency is the one genuinely different beat from the rest of this week's cadence (which has mostly been "ends tomorrow" or countdown clocks), so at least the timing signal is credible and distinct. But subscribers who also get Crate & Barrel mail are seeing near-duplicate creative for the third+ time this week, and the email is long enough that the actual offer/CTA is buried under six other modules by the time you'd scroll to the bottom.
- Solid, on-brand execution that will convert existing high-intent shoppers, but it's creatively identical to sibling-brand sends and offers nothing to differentiate "today" from any other day in this campaign.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: headline, percentage, single red "Shop the Sale" CTA above the fold.
- Category tiles below the hero each carry their own specific offer copy (e.g., "up to 60% off nursery furniture," "up to 50% off rugs") rather than a generic "shop now."
- Jellycat restock module ("back in stock: bestselling Jellycat stuffed animals") is a nice merchandising hook distinct from the sale push.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- "we love these for you" section reads as personalized with prices and product names visible.

## What's weak

- Email is extremely long (7+ screens) — the core Labor Day offer competes with TheDesignDesk, Jellycat, personalized picks, Crate Card, and app-download pitches, diluting the single CTA.
- Creative is a near-copy of the Crate & Barrel Labor Day template this same week — same layout, same red CTA, same "Ends Today/Tomorrow" framing — so repeat subscribers to both lists will feel the redundancy.
- "Ends Today" urgency isn't reinforced anywhere below the hero (no countdown clock, no repeated deadline near the second CTA), so it loses force by the time a reader scrolls further.
- Fine print asterisk on the hero offer ("up to 60% off... *") isn't legible/visible at a glance — no visible callout of exclusions near the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half — move TheDesignDesk, Jellycat, Crate Card, and app-download pitches to separate sends; a "60% off ends today" email should be a single-purpose urgency push, not a six-module catalog.
- 2. Repeat the deadline visually lower in the email (a mini countdown or "offer ends tonight" banner above the second CTA) so urgency survives the scroll.
- 3. Differentiate from the Crate & Barrel sibling send with kids/nursery-specific hero imagery instead of the generic beach photo, which reads as a stock lifestyle asset the brand reuses across sub-brands.
- 4. Surface the fine-print exclusions inline near the hero, not buried in the footer legal block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 60% off nursery furniture ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only — up to 60% off Crate & Kids Labor Day Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nursery furniture, bedding, rugs and more — all on sale until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus, earn 2x Reward Dollars when you shop today.`
