# Our latest collab is serving this star’s unmatched style
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Venus Williams email from Crate & Barrel in two days — after "Shop it first" (9/14, 6.00/10) and the same-day repeat "Early on our app" (9/14, 5.00/10) — and this time it finally delivers what the first two only teased: an actual product showcase. The hero repeats the now-familiar "Venus x Crate & Barrel" phone-lockup styling, but this send scrolls into a full room-by-room collection reveal (Lawn Club sectional, Meadows bed, Monte Carlo dining table, Legacy sideboard) plus a personal Venus quote ("I hope this collection makes you think... This is the lifestyle I want.") that adds real celebrity authenticity missing from the prior two sends.
- The execution is clean — consistent dark-green/warm-wood palette, real lifestyle photography, no render bugs — but it's buried under repetition fatigue. A subscriber who opened Sunday's teaser and Sunday's "early access" repeat is now getting a third variation of the same collab in 48 hours, which reads as the brand squeezing one collab across three sends rather than three genuinely new occasions to open.
- Net: solid content, poor cadence. If this had been the only Venus email of the week, it'd score well above the brand's recent average; landing third in the sequence caps it.

## What's working

- The Venus Williams quote/testimonial block adds a personal, credible layer the two prior teaser emails lacked.
- Multiple concrete new products are shown (sectional, bed, dining table, sideboard, lighting) rather than just the collab hook.
- Visual consistency (green tones, botanical/tennis motifs, consistent typography) carries through the whole scroll.
- "new!" product labels and repeated "Shop the Collab" CTAs give clear, unambiguous next steps at every section.
- The Crate&Kids sub-collab and "we love these for you" recommendation strip at the bottom extend relevance beyond just the flagship line.

## What's weak

- This is the third Venus Williams-branded send in ~48 hours (9/14 x2, now 9/15) — severe cadence fatigue for anyone who opened the earlier two.
- The hero image is nearly identical in composition/tone to the prior day's hero, so at a glance it doesn't read as new content.
- It's a long, undifferentiated scroll of near-identical product cards (image, tag, CTA) with little hierarchy — nothing signals "this is the must-see item."
- The "TheDesignDesk" free design-help CTA near the bottom feels like an unrelated afterthought bolted onto a collab email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a third variant of the same collab within 48 hours — consolidate into one strong send with all the product reveal, quote, and CTA in it; save follow-ups for after a multi-day gap.
- 2. Lead the body with the Venus quote higher up (right after hero) — it's the most differentiated, human element in the email and it's currently buried mid-scroll.
- 3. Cut the product list from five+ full-width sections to a tighter 3-item "shop the edit" carousel to reduce scroll fatigue.
- 4. Drop or relocate the TheDesignDesk CTA to a separate email — it dilutes the single-collab focus.
- 5. Vary the hero visual from the prior day's send so repeat openers immediately see something new.
