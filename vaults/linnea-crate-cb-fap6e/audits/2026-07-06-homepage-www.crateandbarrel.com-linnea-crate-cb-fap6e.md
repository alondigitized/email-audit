# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is leading with a warehouse clearance push — "ends tomorrow, up to 60% off" — which is big and time-pressured, but not exactly where my head is at as someone who just set up a registry and is more focused on discovering what's new than clearing out old stock. The brand aesthetic is unmistakably right for me (the living room and dining vignettes are peak modern minimal), but the first thing I actually experience is a full-screen email/SMS capture modal asking for my phone number before I've even seen the homepage. That's a rough start.

## What's working

- **"Wedding Registry" in the secondary nav** — it's right there, second item after Sale. As someone who just registered, that's the exact signpost I want.
- **Rewards banner at the very top** — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" before anything else signals this is a loyalty-forward brand that values repeat shoppers like me.
- **Warehouse sale urgency** — "ends tomorrow!" with a hard deadline is genuinely motivating even for a full-price shopper; I notice it.
- **Dining vignette (partially visible below modal)** — the round dark table + upholstered chairs glimpse is exactly my taste. If the modal weren't there I'd be tapping into that immediately.
- **Category nav breadth** — Tabletop & Bar, Kitchen, Bedding all visible at a glance; one tap to my core categories.

## What's weak

- **The email/SMS modal as the first experience** — requiring a phone number on the very first page load, before I've seen a single product, is aggressive. The "Maybe Later" dismiss link is small and low-contrast. This is the definition of an interruption dark pattern.
- **Warehouse sale hero is off-brief for me** — I'm registry-shopping, not clearance-hunting. The hero prioritizes the sale customer, not the new-registrant or design-curious browser.
- **No explicit registry discovery moment above the fold** — the nav link exists, but there's no hero module, no "complete your registry" hook, no "new arrivals for your registry" rail visible anywhere in this screenshot.
- **Outdoor furniture at the bottom feels seasonal/irrelevant** — "up to 60% off outdoor furniture" is fine but it's not where I am mentally in July as a recently registered couple furnishing a home interior.
- **Modal fine print is dense and off-putting** — the SMS opt-in legalese takes up more visual real estate than the actual offer copy, which undermines the 10% pitch.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the modal with an inline registry/loyalty nudge** — swap the interstitial for a sticky footer bar: "Already registered? Sign in for your registry perks + 10% member pricing." Keeps the offer without ambushing me on arrival.
- **Add a "New to the Registry" editorial module in the hero rotation** — a lifestyle shot of a walnut dining table set for two with a "Build Your Registry" CTA would speak directly to someone like me who just registered and is still discovering the catalog.
- **Make the dining vignette (round dark table scene) the hero** — that image is doing more for my engagement than the warehouse sale hero. Lead with aspirational product, merchandise the sale secondary.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold or member-pricing strip** — I can't see anywhere what it costs to ship or whether my Rewards status gets me anything beyond the 10% intro offer. One line in the nav strip would close that gap.
