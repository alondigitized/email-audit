# New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, no-nonsense clearance email that leads with exactly what the subject promises — "up to 60% off clearance" sits as a big, bold hero headline over a moody sale-branded image, with a "Shop Now" button visible with zero scrolling. It's not pretty (this is clearance, so the creative is intentionally minimal/dark rather than aspirational), but it's honest and fast to parse — category chips (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) let a shopper self-route in seconds.
- Where it loses points is bloat: after the clearance block, the email pivots hard into an unrelated Halloween decor push, then a generic "we love these for you" product grid, then app download, then credit card, then TheDesignDesk — five different asks stacked on top of one clearance offer. Compare to the tighter "We use these glasses every single day" send (2026-09-24, 8/10) which stayed focused. This one dilutes its own hero offer by burying it under four more asks below the fold.
- Verdict: strong open/click on the strength of the hero alone, but this is a kitchen-sink email that would perform better if it stopped after the clearance CTAs.

## What's working

- Hero headline delivers the exact subject-line promise ("up to 60% off clearance") with zero scroll required.
- Clear, unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop Now") sitting directly under the offer.
- Category chip grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) gives immediate self-routing for a broad clearance sale.
- Curated product grid ("we love these for you") shows real prices and real products (dresser, sofas, dining table, chair) — no lorem-ipsum or broken images.
- No render bugs — everything loads cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- Subject line ("New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed") undersells the actual offer — "up to 60% off" never makes it into the subject, which is a much stronger hook.
- Email tries to do five jobs at once (clearance → Halloween → product picks → app/$500 → credit card → DesignDesk), diluting the one offer that actually earns the send.
- "so scary, it's chic" Halloween section directly competes with the clearance hero for attention right after it, with no visual break signaling "new topic."
- Hero image is a dark, abstract textured background — moody but doesn't show any actual clearance product, so there's no product proof point up top.
- Fine print in the footer ("Final sale items are void of returns... except the following categories") is the kind of buried exclusion language the rubric flags — real subscribers won't read it but it undercuts "honest offer" if scrutinized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the number — "up to 60% off" is a much stronger hook than "new markdowns just landed" and is already the hero headline; don't bury your best asset in the body only.
- 2. Cut the email after the clearance CTA block (categories + Shop All Clearance). Move Halloween, the product grid, app/$500, credit card, and DesignDesk into separate sends — this email is currently five campaigns wearing one subject line.
- 3. Swap the abstract dark hero image for a shot of an actual clearance product (a marked-down sofa or chair with a strikethrough price) to give the "60% off" claim visual proof.
- 4. If keeping the multi-section format, add a visual divider or section label before "so scary, it's chic" so it reads as a deliberate second story, not scroll-fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped: up to 60% off furniture & decor`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off is here (while it lasts)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns across furniture, rugs, bedding & more — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Select styles, steep discounts, limited quantities.`
