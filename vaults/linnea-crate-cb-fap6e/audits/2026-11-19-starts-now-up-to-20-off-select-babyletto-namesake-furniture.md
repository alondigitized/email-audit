# Starts now! Up to 20% off select Babyletto & Namesake furniture!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Crate & Barrel Kids send of the Black Friday run, and the first to go narrow on brand and category. The hero reads "Black Friday starts now: up to 20% off Babyletto & Namesake favorites." Below it sit three large product modules (kiwi stripe glider, abigail gold crib, kiwi ivory bouclé glider), each with a sale price, a struck-through regular price, a customer quote and its own Shop Now button. That is specific and useful for a nursery shopper.
- The email then undercuts itself. A banner above the hero says "free shipping' starts now!" and offers "up to 50% off Black Friday deals" in a long grid. Up to 20% off in the hero competes with up to 50% off right below it, and the "starts now" language repeats the 11/18 kickoffs ("STARTS NOW: Black Friday deals + free shipping!"). The tail also runs long, with a credit-card promo, an app promo, a design-services promo and a dense legal block. The top third is strong and the rest is a catch-all.
- Judgement: a solid, product-led nursery send with credible pricing. It is held back by a weak 20% headline, a muddled offer stack and a repeated kickoff message. It earns a 6.

## What's working

- The hero names the brands, Babyletto and Namesake, so a nursery shopper knows at once what this is.
- Each hero product shows a sale price against a struck-through regular price, which makes the saving concrete.
- Customer quotes under each product add social proof, and each product has its own Shop Now button.
- Large, clean lifestyle photography makes the products the focus.
- The "shop up to 50% off Black Friday deals" grid gives a way into other categories without leaving the email.

## What's weak

- The 20% headline offer is weak next to the 50% grid below it. The email undersells itself and sends mixed signals.
- "Starts now" repeats the 11/18 kickoff emails, so the cadence feels like the same message again.
- The free-shipping banner at the top has odd punctuation ("free shipping' starts now!") and a small threshold line ("on orders $75+"). It reads as a footnote to the hero.
- The bottom third has too many unrelated modules (credit card, app download, design appointment) after the product grid. This dilutes the primary CTA.
- The legal and fine-print block is very dense, and the discount exclusions are not clear up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Either make the hero "up to 50% off" with Babyletto and Namesake as the feature, or keep the brand focus and drop the 50% grid from this send. Don't stack two discount levels.
- 2. Fix the free-shipping banner: correct the stray apostrophe and state the threshold in the headline line.
- 3. Drop "starts now". By the fifth send of the run it is stale, so use a deadline or a remaining-inventory cue instead.
- 4. Cut the credit card, app and design modules from this send, or move them into a compact footer row.
- 5. Put a visible "Shop all Babyletto" and "Shop all Namesake" button directly under the hero, so the hero isn't just a single "Shop Now".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The sampled source (truncated) is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO/VML fallbacks and a 460px mobile breakpoint. The QA run found about 34 images with no alt attribute and an unverifiable compliance and authentication posture. Link destinations and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not validated.** 80 links were skipped as "Tracking/click-redirect domains", so no destination status codes or redirect chains were checked. Landing-page health is unconfirmed.
- **Several third-party and tracking pixels, none with alt text:**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`: five LiveRamp pixels carrying a per-recipient hash.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`: the same URL repeated 6 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`: open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/53f493d9-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`: another tracker.
- **Redundant requests.** The repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` calls add load and a privacy surface. Image proxies and Apple Mail Privacy Protection also make them unreliable for measurement.
- The per-recipient `mi_u` hash is passed in image URLs, so it is exposed to any third-party host.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The 21 images hosted at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`) have no alt attribute. If these are the product and hero images, the email is unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader. Examples: `f9756180-…jpg`, `86df1569-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`.
- **Tracking pixels also lack `alt=""`.** About 13 of them. Decorative images should carry an explicit empty alt.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` values and prevent iOS text scaling.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&`.
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags appear three times, plus duplicate charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`).
- **Leftover template comments.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, `<!--V2-->` and an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule remain in the source. This is cosmetic, but it shows the template wasn't cleaned.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FirstName]` appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The rest of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Without it, one-click unsubscribe isn't supported. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address not verifiable.** The footer was cut off in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 80 tracked links were skipped, and no final destination URLs or UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in the supplied HTML. Tracking relies on opaque `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 21 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
3. Deduplicate the pixels: one `f3c0d22992a01946.png` instead of six, and consolidate or justify the five `rlcdn.com` calls.
4. Capture raw headers directly (not through the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Resolve a sample of redirect chains manually or with a tracked-link resolver, to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate meta tags, and clean up the template comments.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and personalization tokens can be checked.
