# Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, polished Kids send that opens with the right message, "last Double Rewards this year!" It follows with a short line about earning double Reward Dollars on the Crate Card and two buttons, "Shop Now" and "Apply Now". The subject promises "Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →", and the hero delivers on it. Below it, the email is organized by room: bedroom, nursery and playroom. Each section has a lifestyle photo and a four-product grid. Every product shows its reward as a struck-through amount next to a doubled one, for example "Earn ~~$35~~ $70 in Reward Dollars" on the Rosalind play table. That is a concrete, per-item version of the offer.
- The email then buries its own message. After the room sections it adds a "fall sales event: up to 50% off" banner, a gifting block, a $500 sweepstakes-style tile, a stroller and Crate Card promo, a Crate Card info block, an app download and a TheDesignDesk pitch. That is at least six competing asks stacked under a single-offer subject. It is also the third Crate & Barrel send on 11/5, after "The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY!" and "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅". A real subscriber would see the same offer a third time, now in a longer and less focused form.
- Verdict: a strong top third and a mid-section that is competent but repetitive. The email is too long for its job, and the Double Rewards message is weakened by the sale banner and the later blocks. It is a solid 6.
- Note: the full image is 680×12000, so I could read the hero text only at low resolution. I read the product-grid text directly, at full resolution.

## What's working

- The hero text matches the subject's Double Rewards promise, so the open delivers what the inbox line promised.
- The per-item "Earn $35 → $70" treatment shows the offer on each product instead of only in a banner.
- The room-based structure (bedroom, nursery, playroom) is easy to scan and suits parents shopping a room at a time.
- The layout is clean: consistent two-by-two grids, readable product names and no visible broken images or overlapping text.
- Section CTAs such as "Shop Playroom" and "Shop All Furniture" are clear outlined buttons, and each names a destination.

## What's weak

- The "fall sales event: up to 50% off" banner brings a second offer into an email about Double Rewards, which dilutes the message.
- Late-email blocks (the $500 tile, stroller promo, Crate Card explainer, app promo and TheDesignDesk) add more asks than a rewards email needs.
- Rewards are expressed as "Reward Dollars" amounts with a § footnote. Shoppers cannot tell what the underlying earn rate or the real value is.
- The hero offers "Shop Now" and "Apply Now" side by side, so the primary action is split between shopping and credit-card sign-up.
- The send repeats the day's two earlier Crate & Barrel emails, and the first Kids-branded one adds little new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero and the three room sections, and drop everything after the playroom block. Move the stroller, app, sweepstakes and Crate Card education to a different send.
- 2. Remove the "up to 50% off" banner, or make it the only offer. Do not run two competing events in one email.
- 3. State the deadline and the earn rate in the hero in plain words, such as "2x Rewards on kids' furniture through Nov 17", with the § fine print in the footer.
- 4. Make "Shop Now" the single primary button and demote "Apply Now" to a text link.
- 5. Suppress this send for anyone who already received that day's two main promo emails.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Double Rewards of the year starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kids' rooms, 2x Rewards: ends Nov 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double Reward Dollars on nursery, bedroom and playroom picks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cribs, beds and play tables all earn 2x with your Crate Card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link targets could not be checked. The confirmed issues are about 50 `<img>` elements without alt text and a few head-level accessibility and cleanliness problems. The three header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) are unverified, because the QA tool says the relay may be stripping those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 113 tracking links, which are click-redirect domains. Redirect chains, 404s and final landing URLs could not be confirmed.
- **Repeated third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times in one message. This is a LiveRamp domain. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed identifier. `mi_u=dbe8da71…` on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels carries the same value.
- **Duplicate first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears six times with identical src.
- **Other tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d4029a0b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open or tracking pixels.
- **Image hosts:** Content images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `s7d5.scene7.com`. Scene7 is a separate host from the sending domain and could not be probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 50 `<img>`, flagged by QA):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, for example `20815667-626a-48ff-a000-53d635c39432.jpg`, `7a8a3e3d-…jpg`, and `bbf6f9d4-…png` under `/m/47/`.
  - The Scene7 GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - With images blocked, or for a screen reader, those sections would be empty or announce a filename.
  - The tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv…/o/…`) should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. I can't tell from QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty, so treat those as lower priority.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{…}` and in `body`.
- **Page title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and is generic, with no subject-specific text.
- **Leftover template markup:**
  - The placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still present.
  - `meta format-detection` is declared four times and charset twice (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - `*{… line-height:100%}` is a universal selector that can override per-element line-heights in some clients.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider, both match and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appeared in the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is **not confirmed as a sender issue**. It is a real concern for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements if the headers are actually absent.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Check the raw message headers.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link, physical address and sender identification are in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.
- **Data sharing:** The hashed identifier sent to `sr.rlcdn.com` is a third-party data-sharing surface. It is worth checking against the sender's privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages could not be assessed. All 113 tracked links are behind redirects and were not resolved, and no direct `crateandbarrel.com` hrefs appeared in the visible source. The subject promises "Double Rewards", and nothing confirms the landing pages carry that offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the `/m/1/*.jpg` set and the Scene7 GIF. Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Dedupe the pixels: `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6 and `448796.gif` ×5.
4. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment and the duplicate meta tags. Write the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it specific.
5. Fix the image `max-width` ordering so the media queries do not conflict.
6. Re-run QA with raw message headers and the full, untruncated HTML. That will confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer, and the resolved redirect destinations with their UTM parameters.
