# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel greets me with an email-capture modal before I can even see the homepage — that's the first impression. Behind it, the brand is clearly in Memorial Day mode: outdoor furniture, sofas, sectionals. For me, someone who opened this site to think about dinnerware and registry pieces, the page is doing a lot of selling that isn't aimed at me specifically, even if the aesthetic is exactly right.

## What's working

- **Reward Dollars banner** — The "Earn 10% 20% back" callout is immediately visible at the top and speaks to my full-price tolerance; I'd actually use this.
- **Memorial Day sale framing** — Seasonally accurate, gives urgency, and the outdoor content at least tells me a sale is happening sitewide.
- **Category navigation** — Even behind the modal I can see "Tabletop & Bar" and "Kitchen" in the nav, which is exactly where I'd head.
- **Clean aesthetic** — The design language — white space, neutral imagery, minimal typography — is very on-brand for how I think about Crate & Barrel versus, say, Pottery Barn.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I landed on the homepage and immediately got a 10%-off email gate. As someone already in the purchase consideration phase for registry, this is annoying friction, not a helpful hook.
- **Outdoor furniture is front-and-center; I'm here for tabletop** — The hero content I can see (sofas, sectionals, outdoor dining) doesn't reflect why I came. Registry-focused content is nowhere.
- **No registry CTA visible** — For someone recently registered, I'd expect at least a "Manage your registry" or "Registry perks" touch point. Nothing visible.
- **"Save 10% off full-price items*" asterisk** — The asterisk on the primary offer makes me suspicious before I've even engaged.
- **No product imagery for my category** — Not a single dinnerware shot, walnut serving board, or minimal tableware piece visible anywhere in the viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Move the email capture to a scroll-triggered or exit-intent trigger** — Blocking the homepage on load for someone who is mid-registry is unnecessary friction and likely tanking time-on-site for high-intent visitors.
- **Add a persistent "Registry" nav tab or badge** — One tap to "My Registry" should be available above the fold for recently registered shoppers; it's a massive retention hook this brand is leaving on the table.
- **Swap one editorial module for a tabletop / dinnerware lifestyle shot** — Even a single "New in Tabletop" rail with walnut-toned flatware would pull someone like me to scroll; the all-outdoor-furniture push reads as seasonal myopia.
- **Show the Memorial Day offer with a specific deadline date, prominent** — "Thru 05/26" or similar is apparently present but buried; putting a clear countdown would make the urgency honest and actionable.
