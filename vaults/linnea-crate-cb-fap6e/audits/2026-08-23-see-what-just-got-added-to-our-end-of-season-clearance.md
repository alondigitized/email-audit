# See what just got added to our end-of-season clearance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's end-of-season clearance blast, and it's the clearest, most honest offer I've seen from this brand in the recent run — "up to 60% off clearance" sits right in the hero over a calm beach photo, with a clean category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding) right underneath. It's a legitimate, well-organized clearance email for someone actually shopping markdowns.
- The problem is what comes after: the clearance section wraps up around a third of the way down, and then the email pivots hard into an NFL game-day collection, a dining room storytelling section, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a Design Desk consult offer — none of which have anything to do with clearance. This is the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged in the 2026-08-22 Design Desk email (7.00/10) and the Fall Arrivals send (8.00/10): a strong, focused opening third followed by unrelated cross-sell modules stacked to the bottom. For a clearance-motivated subscriber, everything past "Shop All Clearance" is dead weight.
- Judgement: strong subject-to-hero match, real percentage-off offer, clean grid — but the email doesn't stay on message, and that hurts an otherwise sharp send.

## What's working

- Hero delivers a concrete, credible offer ("up to 60% off clearance") that matches the subject exactly.
- The 8-tile category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) makes browsing by department effortless and is the strongest visual section in the email.
- "Shop All Clearance" button is clearly placed as the section's closing CTA.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- The email abandons clearance after the grid and pivots to NFL game day, dining room, credit card, app download, and Design Desk — five unrelated modules stacked below a clearance-titled email.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no "ends Sunday") despite "just got added" implying newness/limited availability.
- No specific product callouts within clearance — it's category tiles only, no actual marked-down item with a price shown.
- "Shop All Clearance" button styling (pale blue on white) is low-contrast and easy to miss against the surrounding grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Clearance" — end the send there, or replace the NFL/dining/card/app modules with 4-6 actual clearance products with strikethrough pricing to give the eye something to click beyond a category label.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Monday" / "While supplies last") near the hero to justify the "just got added" urgency implied by the subject.
- 3. Increase contrast on the "Shop All Clearance" CTA button so it doesn't get lost against the white background.
- 4. Show at least one hero clearance item with before/after pricing to prove the 60% claim concretely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New clearance drops: up to 60% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fresh markdowns just landed in clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, decor and more — newly added, steep discounts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the latest clearance arrivals before they're gone`
