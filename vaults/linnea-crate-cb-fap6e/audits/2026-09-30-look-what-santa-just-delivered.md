# 🎅🏼Look what Santa just delivered…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises Santa, and the hero delivers a holiday advent calendar. Under it, the email turns into a general Crate & Barrel Kids clearing-house. It carries a baby-essentials sale, 60% clearance, a product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download, a design appointment and a store locator. That is seven or eight jobs in one send. The holiday "first look" is a fine hook, but it gets two lines of copy and one button before the email pivots.
- Both the top banner and the first product module repeat "ending soon! up to 20% off the best baby essentials". That is the same offer and phrasing as earlier today ("ENDS TODAY! Up to 20% off the best baby essentials →") and as 09-28 ("ENDS SOON…"). A subscriber sees a Santa subject, then a banner that contradicts it, then the same baby sale they were already sent. The brand is trusted and the layout is clean. The email has no single story, though, and it reads as a recycled send with a holiday teaser on top.
- Verdict: a competent but unfocused send. The Santa subject on Sept 30 is a curiosity gamble that the body doesn't pay off.

## What's working

- The hero is a warm, tactile image (a child with a personalized advent calendar) that signals the holiday season.
- The "first look! the 2026 holiday kids collection" block has a clean category nav (bedding, decor, stockings, toys and gifts) and a clear "Shop All Holiday" button.
- The baby essentials and clearance modules pair a headline, an offer and a button ("Shop the Sale", "Shop Clearance") in a consistent layout.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows named products with prices, including struck-through originals on the Nuna strollers.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. Sender branding is strong.

## What's weak

- The subject ("Look what Santa just delivered…") and the body don't match. The first offer a reader sees is a baby sale with a deadline.
- The top banner has a stray apostrophe: "essentials' →".
- The offer stack is scattered: 20% off baby, up to 60% off clearance, credit card rewards, a $500 app giveaway and a design appointment, all with equal weight. There is no single hero offer.
- The clearance banner puts white text directly over busy orange autumn leaves, so "Selling fast. Shop now." is hard to read.
- "Ending soon" gives no date, and the heavy fine print at the bottom ("up to", exclusions) works against the honesty signal. The advent calendar hero also has no button of its own.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. If the subject is Santa, make the holiday collection the whole email: hero, three to four holiday products, one "Shop holiday" button. Move the baby sale to its own send, or cut it, since it already went out today.
- 2. Remove the repeated baby-essentials banner from the top of the email, or fix it if it stays. A deadline banner above a holiday hero dilutes both messages.
- 3. Fix the stray apostrophe, and put a real date on the deadline ("Ends Sun 10/4") instead of "ending soon".
- 4. Make the hero clickable, or add a button directly on it, and put the product grid on holiday items rather than strollers and gliders.
- 5. Move the credit card, app and design-desk modules into a slimmer footer strip. Put a solid panel behind the white text on the clearance banner.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Look what Santa just delivered…"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a responsive `@media` setup. It has a large number of images with no `alt` text, and it carries five third-party tracking pixels. The HTML was truncated and the relay dropped the headers, so the footer, unsubscribe and authentication checks are unverified. The QA pass rate was 57%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **67 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so I can't confirm that any destination resolves or that redirects land on the intended pages.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels (third party):** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`.
  - The `s=` value, `dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`, starts with the same hex as the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`.
  - That suggests the same per-recipient identifier is shared with a third-party domain, which is a privacy and compliance consideration.
- **First-party tracking pixels:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3b043c53-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - Six instances of `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`
- **Redundant pixel calls:** five identical rlcdn calls and six identical `rp/` calls add requests without adding information.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images.** These are real images, not pixels, and are flagged by QA:
  - 11 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, including `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `51d4c5f9-…jpg` and `859e7782-…gif`.
  - `…/971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.
  - Two `s7d5.scene7.com` assets: `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - If these carry offer or product content, image-blocking clients will show nothing. Screen readers will announce the file name or skip the image.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** the `rlcdn`, `rp/`, `o.gif` and `dv.` images. `alt=""` is acceptable for these, but a missing attribute is not.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup for the declared doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. The `<title>` also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate and conflicting declarations:**
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, once combined and twice separately.
  - `img {max-width:600px}` (min-width 768px) is followed by `img {max-width:640px}` (min-width 640px). Same specificity and later source order means the 640px rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is harmless but shows the template placeholder was never cleaned up.
- **Animated GIFs:** `859e7782-…gif`, `cdab6b90-…gif` and `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif` have no alt text. Outlook desktop shows only the first frame, so any message carried by later frames is lost.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The source was truncated, so the remainder is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so I can't say the sender omitted it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify this against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender and image domains `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` are consistent subdomains, but that isn't proof of alignment.
- **Footer unverified.** The physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link sit beyond the truncation point, so I can't confirm either.
- **Third-party identifier sharing.** The `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels carry a per-recipient hash. If the privacy policy doesn't disclose this, it is a compliance exposure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. All 67 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated HTML shows no final destination URLs or UTM parameters. The visible `mi_cid`/`mi_mid`/`mi_u` parameters belong to the ESP tracking layer, not campaign attribution. No issues are confirmed, and no problems are claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels and spacers, including the scene7 `Bottom_Spacer`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the ampersand in the title (`&amp;`) and deduplicate the `format-detection` tags and the conflicting `img max-width` rules.
4. Review the five `rlcdn.com` pixels for identifier sharing, and consolidate the duplicated `rp/` pixels.
5. Re-run the audit with the full raw message headers and untruncated HTML to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, the footer address and the final link destinations and UTMs.
6. Resolve the tracking redirects with a follow-redirects probe to validate landing pages and UTM parameters.
