# Just got BIGGER: Earn 2x Rewards + up to 50% off The Memorial Day Event
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' "refresh" play for the Memorial Day campaign — the same structural move the Crate & Barrel side ran earlier today with "400+ NEW DEALS ADDED." The "Just Got BIGGER" hook is the Crate & Kids version of that same re-engagement signal, and it earns its existence: 50% off is a genuine step up from the 40% off that anchored the furniture and rugs emails sent two days ago (5/20). The product mix is wide — bedding, rugs, lighting, baby gear, storage — and the visual quality is solid throughout, with room-scene lifestyle photography that suits the brand.
- The problem is cadence. This is somewhere around the sixth or seventh Crate & Barrel family email in roughly 72 hours. Even with a stronger offer, the subscriber who has been tracking this campaign is running out of reasons to open. The "just got bigger" framing is credible once; after multiple "bigger/more/added" messages, it reads as table-stakes promotional noise.
- Verdict: technically sound execution of a creatively exhausted campaign. The 50% figure is the best offer this brand has put forward all week — it deserved a cleaner, shorter subject to let that number breathe.

## What's working

- **50% off is the strongest discount of the series** — a real step up from the 40% that ran on 5/20, which makes the "just got BIGGER" headline factually defensible.
- **2x Rewards is visually integrated** into the hero, not buried in footer copy, which rewards members immediately on open.
- **Multi-category product grid** (bedding → rugs → lighting → baby → storage) gives every parent persona at least one entry point without needing to scroll to a separate email.
- **No visible render bugs** — room scenes are sharp, text is clean, layout holds on the narrow mobile crop.
- **App download block at the bottom** is a smart low-friction loyalty nudge for a subscriber already deep in the email.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is 71 characters** — it runs long on mobile and the "Just got BIGGER:" preamble eats half the available preview before the actual offer appears.
- **"BIGGER" in all-caps** in the subject reads slightly aggressive alongside "SALE" already in the sender name — two caps signals before the inbox even opens.
- **CTA copy is generic across the board** — every button says "SHOP NOW" with no category specificity; a parent interested in bedding vs. baby furniture gets the same button.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is the brand's 6th+ Memorial Day send this week; the refresh tactic works once, not three times.
- **Hero lacks a human presence** — the room scene is beautiful but impersonal; none of the visible imagery shows a child or family in the space, which is the emotional hook that Crate & Kids' own brand promise usually delivers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead subject with the number, not the hype word.** "Up to 50% off" is the strongest signal this brand has sent all week — it should be the first four words, not buried after "Just got BIGGER:".
- 2. **Add category-specific CTAs** — "Shop Bedding," "Shop Baby," "Shop Storage" would drive deeper engagement than a uniform "SHOP NOW" across six product blocks.
- 3. **Put a child in the hero.** One lifestyle shot of a kid in the room scene would anchor the Crate & Kids brand promise that the room-only photography doesn't deliver.
- 4. **Trim or skip the app block** — it's a reasonable upsell, but on the 7th email in three days it adds scroll length without sale urgency.
- 5. **Consider a hard deadline** — "Ends Monday" or "Last day: Monday" would turn the time-bound implication of "Memorial Day" into an explicit countdown that justifies another open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off — The Memorial Day Event just got bigger`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off + Double Rewards: Memorial Day ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `More deals added — shop bedding, baby, storage & more before it ends`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on everything your kids' spaces need — ends Memorial Day`
