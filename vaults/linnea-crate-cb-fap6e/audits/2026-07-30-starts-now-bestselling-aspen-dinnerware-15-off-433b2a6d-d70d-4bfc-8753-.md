# Starts now! Bestselling Aspen dinnerware 15% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is one of Crate & Barrel's cleaner sends: the subject ("Starts now! Bestselling Aspen dinnerware 15% off") lands on a hero that says almost word-for-word the same thing — "bestselling Aspen collection 15% off" — with a Shop Now button right beneath it. That kind of subject/hero agreement is the exact pattern behind the brand's two recent 8.00/10 sends (7/30 Beatrix collection, 7/30 Summer Sale), and this email earns the same mark for the same reason.
- Where it loses points is the same structural tic that's dogged this brand's last several sends: a top ribbon promoting "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" sits directly above a hero promoting a specific, smaller 15% off. A subscriber has to do math to figure out which number actually applies to what's on screen — is Aspen part of the 60% sale, or is 15% the real ceiling? Below the fold, the email recovers nicely with real per-SKU pricing, a customer quote, and a well-organized "we love these for you" cross-sell block.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel alignment, real prices, no render bugs — dragged down by the recurring ribbon-vs-hero discount mismatch and a generic "Shop Now" CTA that could be doing more work.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy match almost exactly — "Aspen dinnerware 15% off" in the subject, "bestselling Aspen collection 15% off" in the hero.
- Every product tile shows actual sale vs. regular pricing ($5.05 Reg $5.95, $31.40 Reg $36.95, etc.) — no vague "up to X% off" hand-waving at the item level.
- A customer testimonial ("A classic design and offers great quality for the price") adds real social proof mid-scroll.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- Good cross-sell logic underneath the sale block (seasonal produce content, then complementary cookware/kitchen tools) instead of just stopping at the discount.

## What's weak

- The "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" ribbon at the very top contradicts the 15% off Aspen hero directly beneath it — same discount-stacking confusion flagged in the 7/26 and 7/28 sends.
- Primary CTA copy ("Shop Now," "Shop the Sale") is generic — it doesn't name the product, missing a chance to reinforce "Aspen" at the click moment.
- No credible deadline anywhere ("Starts now!" implies urgency but never says when it ends), so the offer doesn't feel genuinely time-boxed.
- The Crate Card / rewards teaser and the "It's all on the app" chance-at-$500 block both compete for attention late in the email, diluting what should be a single-offer send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop or reword the top ribbon so it doesn't contradict the hero — either make it "15% off Aspen, plus up to 60% off Summer Sale storewide" so the two numbers aren't in tension, or move the ribbon below the Aspen block entirely.
- 2. Swap "Shop Now" for a product-specific CTA like "Shop Aspen Dinnerware" to reinforce the offer at the click.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("ends Sunday," "48 hours only") next to "Starts now!" so the urgency has teeth.
- 4. Trim the back-half cross-sell (Crate Card + app + design service) to one supporting block max — right now three separate asks compete after the main offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Aspen dinnerware: 15% off starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your favorite Aspen collection, 15% off for 48 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Coupe plates, salad bowls, mugs — all in the sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mix, match, and save on the tableware you keep reaching for.`
