# Is your tween asking for a new room? Get inspired →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is an editorial, content-led send. The "TheDesignDesk" feature profiles twin kids and their dream bedrooms (Rumi's "mystical garden cat bedroom" and Juno's "city by the sea bedroom"). It's a long scroll of room photography with shop buttons tucked underneath. The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event banner (up to 50% off) sits at the very top, but it's a thin strip above a soft design-service pitch. The subject promises "tween room inspiration", and the body is closer to a magazine spread than a sale.
- The render is 12,000px tall and I could only view it at a small scale, so small copy and the preheader weren't legible. The structure is clear: lots of editorial imagery, a duplicated "Introducing a new kids collab" block, a "we love these for you" product grid, a card-perk promo, an app-download pitch and a second "Book a Free Appointment" pitch. That's six or seven competing asks, which dilutes the one thing a reader could act on, the 50% event.
- Judgement: pleasant to look at, but unfocused and long. It follows the same Semi-Annual Event beat as the last six sends. It would work better as a shorter room-inspiration email with one hero CTA.

## What's working

- The Semi-Annual Event banner with "up to 50% off" is at the top, so the offer is visible without scrolling.
- Real-kid room stories (Rumi and Juno) are a credible, shareable way to sell furniture.
- Each room story ends with a "Shop [name]'s Bedroom" button and shoppable product tiles. A short testimonial-style quote ("so soft, like a marshmallow") adds social proof.
- The photography is consistent and aspirational, and the brand voice is clear.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long, with six or seven separate asks: shop the rooms, book an appointment, new collab, product grid, card perk, app download and a second appointment pitch.
- The collab "Introducing a new kids collab…" block appears twice back to back, which looks like a duplicated module.
- The sale is buried. The 50% banner is small and the hero is editorial, so the eye lands on the design feature rather than the offer.
- The subject asks about a "tween", but the body is about twins' bedrooms and doesn't clearly show tween-aged products.
- "Book a Free Appointment" appears as a secondary button multiple times, which competes with the shop CTAs.
- This is the sixth Crate & Barrel send in two days (Oct 1–2), all pushing the same event. Compare "New! Holiday bedding is here" earlier today. That cadence is fatiguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a single story, one hero CTA ("Shop Tween Bedrooms") and one product grid. Move the app, card and appointment modules to a footer strip or drop them.
- 2. Make the 50% event the hero headline or a bold band, not a thin banner above the editorial hero.
- 3. Delete the duplicated collab block.
- 4. Add a deadline to the event, such as "Ends [date]", to create credible urgency.
- 5. Show tween-specific products (desks, loft beds, lighting) to match the subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Two kids, two dream rooms (shop the looks)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off beds, nightstands & rugs in the Semi-Annual Event.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "Is your tween asking for a new room? Get inspired →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard Marigold/Mediaplus-style build with an Outlook (MSO) fallback and a mobile breakpoint, but it has widespread missing alt text and some markup hygiene problems. The source was truncated, so the body links, footer, and unsubscribe block could not be inspected. The header warnings are most likely relay artifacts, not sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 84 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains and never probed. The destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1..5` requests are LiveRamp pixels. Each carries a hashed identifier (`s=`) and sits in an email that already has first-party pixels:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel)
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/63c96a33-…`
  - six repeats of `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`
- **Duplicate pixels:** The repeated `rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` requests are redundant weight and extra tracking calls.
- **Stale asset paths:** Scene7 images such as `Rumi%5FVideo%5F2up.gif` and `Juno%5FVideo%5F2up.gif` sit under `/2026/04%5FKids/2026%5F0428…`, an April campaign folder. They are reused in an October send. This is a possible stale-asset risk, though I can't confirm it from the excerpt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flagged about 50 `<img>` tags. Roughly 33 are content images, for example:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`
  - `Venus%5Ftertiary%5F640%5FFnl%5FNewLogo.gif` (appears twice)
  - `Rumi%5FVideo%5F2up.gif`
  - `Juno%5FVideo%5F2up.gif`

  If these images are blocked, the email shows no text equivalent. Screen readers will announce filenames or nothing.
- **Pixels and spacers:** The remaining flagged images are tracking pixels and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly, and I can't tell from the QA output whether they do.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables user zoom.
- **Text resize disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;…}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. The universal `*` selector applies `line-height:100%` globally, which tightens line spacing for all text.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type. `format-detection` is declared three separate times, and the later tags are redundant or conflicting.
- **Template placeholder left in:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still present, and "IMPUT" is a typo. It is harmless but unfinished.
- **Overlapping media queries:** The `min-width:768px` rule sets `img{max-width:600px}`. The `min-width:640px` rule comes later and sets `max-width:640px`. At 768px and wider, the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` is declared, so dark mode is intentionally unsupported.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject and visible head source contain no unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both were not found. The QA output itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers. Treat it as unconfirmed, not a sender defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses the `mail.crateandbarrel.com` subdomain, and the image hosts are consistent (`image.mail.…`, `mi.…`, `dv.…`). Authentication cannot be verified from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but little could be checked. All 84 click links were tracking-redirect URLs that were skipped, so final landing URLs and UTM parameters were not inspected.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` to pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%` and drop the `*{line-height:100%}` rule.
3. Escape the title as `&amp;`. Remove the duplicate `meta` tags and the leftover template comment.
4. Consolidate the duplicate `rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `448796.gif` pixels, and confirm the LiveRamp pixels are disclosed in the privacy policy.
5. Fix the overlapping `img max-width` media queries so one rule governs ≥640px.
6. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to capture the `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers. Resolve the 84 tracking redirects to verify landing pages and UTMs. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer and unsubscribe link.
