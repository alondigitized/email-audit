# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is clearly in full Memorial Day sale mode — everything is pushing the event, outdoor furniture, and the rewards program. As someone who came here to browse the registry or pick up some walnut-accented tableware, the hero is more "weekend patio refresh" than "modern kitchen obsession." The brand still looks gorgeous and trustworthy, but the homepage is optimized for furniture shoppers right now, not dinnerware people.

## What's working

- **Rewards program placement** — "Earn 10%, 20% back in Reward Dollars" appears in both the sticky top banner and the hero. As someone who just registered, the "Double Rewards for a limited time for new members" line is actually designed for me.
- **Memorial Day urgency** — "Ends 5/30/2026" is a real, specific date. Not vague, not buried.
- **Navigation bar** — I can see "Tabletop & Bar" and "Kitchen" in the nav, which means I'm one tap away from my actual shopping focus even if the hero doesn't take me there.
- **"up to 60% off" banner** — clean pill-style label above the hero, easy to read at a glance.
- **"up to 30% off bestselling furniture"** module below fold — the callout is specific, not generic.

## What's weak

- **Forced modal on landing** — asking for email AND mobile phone number simultaneously the second I land? On a phone? I'm bouncing that instantly. The "10% off full-price items" hook is reasonable but the double-ask with SMS legal copy is a lot.
- **Hero is entirely outdoor furniture** — I came here for walnut serving boards and minimal stoneware. The patio scene is pretty but zero of my registry items live there.
- **No above-fold path to dinnerware/kitchen/tabletop** — the visible CTAs are "Shop Outdoor Furniture" and "Shop Memorial Day Event," neither of which routes me to what I actually want.
- **Below-fold product rail** — partially visible cards show sofas and furniture (Lounge Sofa, Amalfi Tower). Still no dinnerware, no Kitchen, no Tabletop in the visible product stream.
- **"Apply Now" button** — placed alongside shopping CTAs, it's competing for visual attention at a weird moment in the flow.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal on mobile after one field** — ask for email or phone, not both, on first visit. A new registrant isn't going to hand over SMS access before she's seen anything.
- **Swap the hero for a registry or "new arrival" lifestyle shot** — it's May; a Memorial Day table setting (walnut board, linen napkins, minimal stoneware) is still seasonal AND maps to my reason for being here.
- **Add a "Registry picks on sale" callout strip** — even a single row above the outdoor furniture section that says "Registry favorites in the sale" with three tabletop images would catch me immediately.
- **Surface a "Tabletop & Bar" featured module above the furniture rails** — I can get to it via nav, but not seeing it in the content stream means I might not stay long enough to get there.
