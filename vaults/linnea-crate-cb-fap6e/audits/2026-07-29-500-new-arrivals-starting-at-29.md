# 500+ new arrivals, starting at $29→
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running its now-familiar kitchen-sink playbook — again. A "back-to-school sale up to 20% off" ribbon sits at the very top, the hero pivots to "500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29," and by the time you scroll to the mid-body there's a THIRD sales message: "up to 50% off summer sale." Three separate seasonal narratives (back-to-school, fall, summer) are stacked in one send, which is the exact same structural flaw flagged in this brand's 7/28 "Our Back-to-School Sale is on!" email, where a "up to 50% off summer sale" ribbon undercut the stated headline offer.
- Below the fold it's the standard sprawl: bedroom, nursery, playroom category blocks each with their own CTA, a sweepstakes entry, the summer sale banner, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a design service ad — six or seven distinct campaigns crammed into a single scroll, mirroring the 7/25–7/28 pattern already called out repeatedly.
- The hero itself is clean and the $29 price point is a genuinely strong hook for a nursery-furnishing parent. But the offer stacking undermines trust — a subscriber can't tell if the real deal is 20% off, 50% off, or the flat $29 starting price, and that confusion is now a recurring brand habit, not a one-off.

## What's working

- Hero headline and CTA ("500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29") are legible and land first without competing visual noise.
- The $29 starting price is a concrete, specific hook that's easy to act on.
- Category sections (bedroom/nursery/playroom) are cleanly organized with clear sub-headers and dedicated CTAs.
- No visible render bugs — images load, buttons are intact, no overlapping text.
- Certification badges (GREENGUARD Certified, Moisture-Free, FSC Certified) on product tiles add credible, category-relevant detail.

## What's weak

- Three conflicting sale claims in one email (20% off ribbon at top, $29 starting price in hero, 50% off summer sale mid-body) — the exact issue flagged in the 7/28 send.
- Subject promises "new arrivals," but the top ribbon leads with back-to-school and the mid-body pivots to summer clearance — none of the three match each other.
- Six-plus unrelated campaigns crammed into one email (furniture, sweepstakes, summer sale, credit card, app download, design service) continues the kitchen-sink pattern flagged on 7/25, 7/26, 7/27, and 7/28.
- No social proof (star ratings, review counts) anywhere in the product grid despite this being a considered-purchase category (nursery furniture) where trust signals matter.
- This is at least the fifth near-identical structural send in five days — cadence fatigue is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE offer and lead with it everywhere in the email — kill the 20% back-to-school ribbon and the 50% summer sale banner if the real headline is "$29 new arrivals." Consistency here would resolve the top complaint across this brand's last three audits.
- 2. Cut the email to two sections max: new arrivals (with room-type sub-nav as text links, not full stacked CTAs) and one secondary push (sweepstakes OR credit card OR app OR design service — not all four).
- 3. Add a star rating or "X reviews" line to at least the top product row — this is a furniture buy, and zero social proof is a real gap for the category.
- 4. Move or drop the credit card and app-download blocks entirely from a "new arrivals" themed send — they read as filler that dilutes the arrivals message.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Nursery, bedroom & playroom: 500+ new pieces just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, dressers, rugs & more — all under one sale`

# Technical Audit — Crate & Kids "500+ new arrivals" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Bronto/Cheetah-style HTML email (table-based, MSO-conditional layout) with heavy tracking instrumentation but incomplete compliance headers and near-total absence of image alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 81 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (tracking/redirect domains), so final destination URLs could not be verified for this audit.
- Multiple distinct tracking/pixel endpoints fire on open, including:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` — appears **6 times** in the source (likely one per content module rather than a single global pixel).
  - `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=...&n=1` through `n=5` — sequential LiveRamp/RampID identity-sync pixels (5 instances).
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` — additional open-tracking pixel.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7ca67cd7-620c-49d4-915a-b1030ae86a26?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` — separate delivery/open verification callout (Validity/Everest "dv" domain).
- No broken or malformed `href` syntax observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **30 of ~34 images are missing `alt` text**, spanning both content images (hero banner, product shots) and tracking pixels. For content images (e.g., `2025_0728_CBK_WholeHomeNewArrivals_HERO`, multiple `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/.../m/1/*.jpg`), missing alt text degrades the experience for screen-reader users and for any client that blocks images by default — no fallback text renders.
- Doctype is legacy `XHTML 1.0 Transitional`, paired with `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">`, `<meta name="format-detection" ...>` (declared redundantly 4 times), and full MSO conditional-comment blocks (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso]>`) — consistent with an Outlook/desktop-safe table build.
- Media queries target `max-width:460px`, `min-width:640px`, and `min-width:768px` for responsive breakpoints; structure appears intact in the truncated sample (no unclosed tags observed).
- Tracking-pixel `<img>` tags lack `alt=""` (empty alt), which is the correct pattern for decorative/invisible pixels — but since alt is entirely absent rather than empty, screen readers may announce the raw filename/URL for these as well.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders (e.g., `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`, `[[...]]`), or dynamic-content blocks were observed in the visible HTML. Note the source was truncated, so this cannot be confirmed as exhaustive — recommend re-running the check against the full payload if personalization is expected in this campaign.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header: not found.** Not captured by the relay, or not sent — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058): not found.** Without this, mailbox providers (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements) cannot present true one-click unsubscribe, which is now a compliance risk for high-volume senders as of the 2024 Gmail/Yahoo mandates.
- **Authentication-Results header: not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay — this is a QA/telemetry gap on the receiving side (AgentMail relay) as much as a sender issue, but it means authentication cannot be confirmed either way from available data.
- No footer/unsubscribe link content was present in the truncated HTML sample to independently verify a CAN-SPAM-compliant physical address or unsubscribe link — recommend checking the untruncated source directly.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Standard UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) were not visible in the sampled links; the visible tracking links instead use platform-specific params (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`, `cp_tp=v`) consistent with a Bronto/Cheetah Digital ESP rather than GA-style UTM tagging.
- Because all 81 click-through links were skipped from HTTP probing, landing-page alignment (i.e., whether links resolve to matching live product/category pages on crateandbarrel.com) could not be verified in this audit.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (hero, product shots); use `alt=""` explicitly for the tracking/pixel images to avoid screen readers announcing raw filenames.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are being sent by the ESP and correctly passed through the AgentMail relay — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication is passing at the sending MTA; the missing `Authentication-Results` header here is inconclusive and should be checked against raw SMTP headers rather than relay output alone.
4. Spot-check a sample of the 81 redirect links manually to confirm they resolve to live, matching landing pages before next send.
5. Consider consolidating the 6 duplicate `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/...` open-tracking pixels if they are firing redundantly per module — no functional benefit beyond the first, and it adds page weight.
