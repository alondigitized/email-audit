# Our bedroom bestsellers, up to 25% off for a limited time!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's bedroom bestsellers roundup, and it's actually one of the cleaner category emails I've seen from this brand this cycle. The hero — a canopy bed at "Sale $1,899 Reg. $2,299" — gives you a real anchor price immediately, which beats the vague "up to X% off" framing that dominated the 08/14 and 08/15 sends (living room, dining room, bunk beds). The four-tile subcategory grid (beds, dressers, bedding, lighting) is a smart way to let a shopper self-select instead of scrolling a flat product wall.
- Where it falls apart is scope creep: after the bedroom sale wraps, the email keeps going — a 60% off clearance banner, a $5000 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" grid that randomly includes an air fryer and dinner plates, a credit card pitch, and an app download push. That's five different asks stacked under one subject line about bedroom bestsellers. Compare to the 08/14 "Up to 60% off The Summer Sale" send, which stayed disciplined around one offer — this one dilutes a genuinely strong hero with unrelated tack-ons.
- Net: strong top, cluttered bottom. A subscriber scanning on mobile gets the offer instantly, but anyone who scrolls past the fold is pulled off the bedroom narrative into four unrelated pitches.

## What's working

- Hero has a real anchor price ($1,899 vs $2,299), not just a vague percentage — rare and effective for this brand's recent cadence.
- Four-tile subcategory breakdown (beds/dressers/bedding/lighting) with specific % off each gives shoppers a clear self-select path.
- Two clear CTAs stacked right after the grid ("Shop Bedroom Sale" / "Shop All Bedroom") keep the primary action unambiguous.
- Clean, on-brand product photography throughout — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "bedroom bestsellers" but the email pivots into clearance, a design sweepstakes, a credit card pitch, and an app download — four separate off-topic asks diluting the core offer.
- The "we love these for you" grid includes an air fryer and dinner plates under a bedroom-themed email — a jarring, seemingly random cross-sell.
- No visible loyalty/member pricing callout despite this being exactly the kind of email where a rewards member would expect to see it.
- "up to 25%/30%/60% off" language is repeated across four tiles without ever anchoring a second real price — only the hero has a concrete number.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the bedroom CTAs. Move clearance, the sweepstakes, the credit card, and the app pitch into separate sends — this single email is doing five jobs at once and none of them land as hard as they could standalone.
- 2. Add a second anchor price (e.g., on the dresser or bedding tile) so the "up to X% off" tiles feel as concrete as the hero.
- 3. If space allows, add one loyalty-member benefit line near the top CTA (e.g., "members save an extra X%") since Crate & Barrel has a rewards program and this shopper would look for it.
- 4. Trim the "we love these for you" grid to bedroom-adjacent items only — the air fryer and dinner plates break the theme and cost focus.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Canopy beds, dressers & bedding — now from $1,899`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New season, new bedroom. Shop the sale before it ends.`
