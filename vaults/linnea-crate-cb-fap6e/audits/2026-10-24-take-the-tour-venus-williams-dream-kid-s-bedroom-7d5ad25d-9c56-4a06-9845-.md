## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, a mobile breakpoint at 460px, and 73 tracked links. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and some template hygiene problems. The header warnings (List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results) are capture limitations, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing was not possible.** All 73 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destination status codes, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Several third-party pixels sit in the body.** The `<img>` tags involved are:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are five separate LiveRamp pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`, the open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ca68c3e4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, repeated 6 times.
- **The same hashed identifier is reused across domains.** The `s=` value on rlcdn.com starts with the same hex as `mi_u=` on mi.crateandbarrel.com (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`). This looks like a recipient hash shared with a third party, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **One asset is hosted separately and looks reused.** `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` is served from `s7d5.scene7.com`, while the other creative comes from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. The filename carries a 2026-09-26 date, a month before this send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA).** About 35 `<img>` tags are flagged. They fall into two groups:
  - **Content images.** There are about 17 of these on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`), plus the scene7 GIF. With images blocked, these render as blank space.
  - **Tracking pixels.** The rlcdn, `mi.`, `dv.` and `o.gif` pixels need `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure.
- **Invalid markup under the declared doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML 1.0 Transitional. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Redundant or leftover head markup:**
  - `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are both present.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times, with duplicate `telephone`, `address` and `date` entries.
  - The unfilled template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **CSS conflicts and breadth:**
  - `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap. At 768px and wider, the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule has no effect.
  - The global `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are broad and can cause unintended inheritance.
  - `.mobile{width:460px!important}` is a fixed width on narrow viewports.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The only personalization evidence is the `mi_u` / `mi_mid` / `mi_cid` tracking parameters, which resolved normally. Because the HTML was truncated, the body and footer weren't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, so verify against the raw message. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.** Authentication-Results was not captured, so nothing can be concluded.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** The footer was cut off by truncation.
- **Possible third-party data sharing.** If the `sr.rlcdn.com` `s=` value is a hashed recipient ID, it may warrant privacy-policy review. This is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source, and all 73 links went through click-tracking redirects that weren't resolved. UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be assessed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` text** to the ~17 content images and the scene7 GIF. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels and give them explicit 1×1 dimensions.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Escape the title** to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it descriptive. Remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. **Resolve the overlapping `img max-width` media queries.** Keep one rule, or use ranges (`min-width:640px and max-width:767px`).
5. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Don't rely on the relay-captured copy.
6. **Re-run QA** on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirects to check destination status and UTM parameters, and confirm the footer's unsubscribe link and address.
