# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the brand I registered with, and the aesthetic checks out — the glimpse I get behind the modal is exactly the kind of neutral, elevated lifestyle I associate with C&B. But the very first thing I see is a full-screen email-and-SMS capture popup, which tells me the brand is more interested in my contact info than in showing me what's new right now. It's Memorial Day weekend, there's clearly a sale happening, and I had to squint past legal fine print to figure that out.

## What's working

- **"Earn 10% 20% back in Reward Dollars*" banner** — the stacked reward callout at the very top immediately signals this is a brand that rewards loyalty. As someone who registered here, that's a reason to keep shopping.
- **"Final Weekend" urgency copy** — the Memorial Day deadline framing creates a real reason to act today rather than come back next week.
- **"Tabletop & Bar" in the top nav** — my exact category is one tap away without any digging. That's thoughtful taxonomy.
- **Modal offer ("Save 10% off full-price items*")** — full-price tolerance is my default, so a genuine 10% on full-price merchandise actually moves the needle for me more than a clearance event would.
- **Clean aesthetic behind the modal** — the neutral palette and furniture imagery I can see are visually consistent with the brand I know and like.

## What's weak

- **The forced modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I came to browse dinnerware and instead I'm staring at an email form with SMS legal disclaimers. For a returning registrant, this feels like being IDed at a store I've shopped at for years.
- **Hero is outdoor furniture, not tabletop** — the scene behind the modal shows a sofa and outdoor lounge setup. That's not wrong for C&B, but it doesn't speak to me specifically.
- **Free-shipping threshold is nowhere visible** — as someone buying registry items (higher AOV), I'd want to know the shipping calculus immediately.
- **No product-level trust signals** — no review counts, no star ratings, nothing to validate a specific purchase without tapping deeper into the site.
- **"Mobile Later" as a dismissal option** — that label implies my only path out is "later," not "never." Mildly manipulative and I notice it.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal or replace it with a slim banner** — a "Join Registry Perks — save 10%" persistent strip at the top preserves the offer without blocking the homepage I came to see.
- **Swap the hero for a tabletop or dining lifestyle scene** — outdoor furniture is seasonal, but Memorial Day is also when people entertain; a walnut dining table set with modern dinnerware would hit my exact use case.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** — one line like "Free shipping on orders $99+" placed next to the Reward Dollars callout would close a common hesitation before I even get to a PDP.
- **Add a "New in Tabletop" or "Registry Favorites" quick-link chip** — a horizontal pill row beneath the nav ("Outdoor · Sofas · Tabletop · New Arrivals") would let me tap directly to my category without parsing the full nav bar.
