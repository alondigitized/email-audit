## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with roughly 35 images flagged as missing alt text, including content images, third-party pixels and open-tracking pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers. The source is also truncated and all 75 links are click-tracked, so link, footer and landing-page behavior can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 75 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) are five identical pixel calls that differ only in `n`. Together with `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2277c0de-…`, these are five or more tracking requests per open.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** The `s=` and `mi_u=` values are 64-character hex strings, which look like hashed recipient identifiers. I can't confirm what they hash.
- **Duplicate image:** `f3c0d22992a01946.png` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`) appears 6 times with the same `mi_u` value. It may be a dynamic recommendation image or a repeated pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 35 `<img>` elements are flagged. The list contains these types:
  - 22 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg` and 8 `.gif` files. With images blocked or for screen readers, these show no text.
  - 6 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`.
  - 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, 1 `o.gif` and 1 `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2277c0de-…`. These are tracking pixels that should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Template markup problems:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
  - There are duplicate `<meta charset>` and `Content-Type` declarations.
  - Three `format-detection` meta tags overlap. One combined tag plus two more is redundant.
  - The leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Zoom and text sizing:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` can block pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
  - `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{}`, `html` and `body`, which disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Breakpoint:** The only responsive breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports from 461px to 600px get the desktop layout.
- **Language:** `<html lang="en">` is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers:** The QA check found no `List-Unsubscribe` and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The QA output itself says this may be relay capture loss, so I can't confirm the headers are actually missing from the sent message.
- **Authentication:** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sender and tracking domains (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`) are consistent subdomains of the same organizational domain, which is good for alignment, but that is not verified.
- **Footer:** The footer, with its unsubscribe link and postal address, is beyond the truncation point and unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't assess. All 75 links are tracking redirects that weren't probed, and the HTML was truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~22 content images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`).
2. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy. That will settle the three compliance and deliverability warnings.
3. Resolve a sample of the tracking redirects with a redirect-following probe. That will check destinations and UTM parameters.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
6. Confirm that the 5 repeated `rlcdn` pixels are intentional, and review the hashed identifiers they expose in URLs.
7. Capture the full, untruncated HTML so the footer and unsubscribe link can be audited.
