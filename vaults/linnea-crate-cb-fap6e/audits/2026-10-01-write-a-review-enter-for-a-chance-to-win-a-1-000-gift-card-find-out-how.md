# Write a review. Enter for a chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card. Find out how →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-solicitation email dressed up as a sweepstakes. The hero reads "want to win a $1,000 gift card?" over a Jenny Lind crib, with "Write a Review" and "Shop Bestsellers" buttons below. The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event banner ("Up to 50% off") sits above the hero. The email can't decide whether it wants reviews or sales. It also promotes washable rugs, Jellycat, a product grid, the Crate Card, the app and a design consult.
- The $1,000 hook is a strong, concrete prize and the hero is clean. But the ask (write a review) is lower-value to the recipient than the sale. The prize copy "Rate and review your fave product for a chance to win" is easy to skim past. Contest rules are buried in the dense legal block at the bottom. After three sale sends today, this one is a pivot, but the lower half reads as a generic catch-all.
- Verdict: a decent hook with a muddled body. A recipient with no recent purchase has nothing to review, so the main CTA doesn't fit them.

## What's working

- The $1,000 gift card is a specific, high-value hook in the hero headline.
- The hero is uncluttered, with a large crib image and a clear headline.
- "Write a Review" is the primary button, with "Shop Bestsellers" as a secondary option.
- The "Contest ends October 31" deadline is stated in the hero copy.
- The product grid shows prices, with strikethrough sale prices on some items, which gives people a reason to browse.

## What's weak

- The sale banner competes with the contest for attention at the top. Two offers are stacked in the first screen.
- The star graphic, with two of five stars filled, is a mixed signal for a review ask.
- The body is a long catch-all (rugs, Jellycat, products, credit card, app, design desk, store locator). The eye has no clear path after the hero.
- Contest eligibility and odds are not visible near the CTA. They are in the fine print at the bottom.
- The "Write a Review" button is small and low-contrast, a muted mauve on a muted background.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Either make the contest the sole hero and drop the sale banner, or run the sale email and put the review ask in a slim strip. Today the email tries both.
- 2. Send it to recent purchasers with their purchased item pre-loaded in the review CTA. A generic "write a review" has no concrete product behind it.
- 3. Make the "Write a Review" button larger and higher-contrast, and add a one-line "No purchase necessary / 1 entry per review" note under it.
- 4. Cut the lower half to one or two modules (the product grid and the app). Remove the credit card, design desk and store locator blocks.
- 5. Put the star rating widget in the email so recipients can click a star to start a review.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your review could win $1,000 to Crate & Barrel`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rate one product you own. Takes 2 minutes. Contest ends Oct 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One review, one entry. Plus up to 50% off the Bedroom & Nursery Event.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, a zoom-blocking viewport meta, and leftover template cruft. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits, so they can't be confirmed as sender defects. The HTML is truncated and the 65 tracking links were not probed, so link, UTM and footer checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified redirects:** 65 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) pass a long hashed identifier in the query string. Other pixels include `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5c8cb637-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. They add many third-party requests, and the `s=` and `mi_u=` values look like the same recipient hash.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA check flagged 24 `<img>` elements.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (the QA output also lists one under `/m/47/`): `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `82e1c4fe-…gif`, `74344650-…jpg`, `d7ea9922-…jpg`, `ea0160ba-…jpg`, `f28e7f40-…jpg`, `ed972f76-…gif`, `8727260f-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - The scene7 `202505_CBK_WashablePerformance_Rugs` image
  - Tracking pixels: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6×), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5×), `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5c8cb637-…`
  - **Impact:** with images blocked or in a screen reader, the content images have no fallback text. Pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` override user text-size preferences in iOS/WebKit clients.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` doesn't match the subject line. The `&` is also unescaped, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (3 variants).
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shipped in production.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centered text on every paragraph unless overridden per element. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.

- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Verify against the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay. The `From` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images and the stale scene7 rugs image. Set `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the `rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.` and `o.gif` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favour of `100%`.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay strips them, capture headers upstream or test with a direct seed inbox.
4. Resolve the 65 tracking links with a follow-redirect probe. Confirm final URLs return 200, keep UTMs and land on the review/sweepstakes page.
5. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, and set the title to something descriptive.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML so the footer unsubscribe and postal address can be checked.
