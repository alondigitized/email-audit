# GOING FAST: Up to 60% off the Summer Sale!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a summer-sale email pretending to be a summer-sale email — the "up to 60% off" hero is there, and the category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, decor, rug, bed & bath, clearance) actually does the job of getting a browsing shopper somewhere useful fast. But then the email immediately pivots to a $23,000+ honeymoon sweepstakes with The Knot/Wedding Registry, which has nothing to do with a sale email and buries the actual offer under scroll-fatigue territory before you even get to it.
- By the time you scroll past the honeymoon contest, in-stock shipping speed, "the new fall table," and a "we love these for you" product grid, the email has stopped being a sale email and turned into a full site catalog with five different CTAs competing for attention. Compare this to 2026-08-10's "FINAL DAY: Up to 50% off summer sale" — that one at least kept the discount messaging as the dominant thread throughout. This one leads strong then dilutes itself.
- Judgment: a decent top-of-funnel hero undercut by scope creep. The subject promises urgency and a clear discount; the body delivers that for about 15% of its length, then wanders.

## What's working

- Clean, unambiguous hero: "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" with a single "Shop Now" button directly under it — no scroll needed to see the offer.
- Category grid (furniture/outdoor/kitchen/entertaining/decor/rug/bed&bath/clearance) gives a shopper multiple concrete entry points instead of one generic link.
- Product image quality is strong throughout — no broken images, no layout breakage, no lorem ipsum.
- Price/comp pricing shown on the hero dresser ("Sale $1,499 Reg. $1,999") gives a tangible sense of savings.

## What's weak

- The $23,000+ honeymoon sweepstakes (with a co-brand, TheKnot/WeddingRegistry) is a jarring non-sequitur directly under the sale hero — it dilutes the "GOING FAST" urgency from the subject line almost immediately.
- Too many CTAs stacked back to back (Shop Now, Enter Now, Shop Now, Shop Now, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment) — no clear secondary priority, everything competes equally.
- "the new fall table" section mixes season signals with a "summer sale" subject — sends a confusing seasonal message in the same email.
- Product grid at the bottom ("we love these for you") has no visible personalization logic — reads as generic catalog filler, not a "for you" curation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the honeymoon sweepstakes block from this send entirely (or make it its own dedicated email) — it actively competes with and undercuts the sale urgency the subject line promises.
- 2. Move the category grid up or keep it directly under the hero, then end the email shortly after "Shop All Sale" — collapse fall-table/in-stock-shipping/credit-card content into a shorter, secondary block or a separate campaign.
- 3. Pick one dominant CTA style and de-emphasize the rest (outline buttons for secondary actions like "Learn More"/"Download Now" vs. solid for the primary sale CTA) so the eye has a clear hierarchy.
- 4. Add a real countdown or explicit end-date near the hero to back up "GOING FAST" — right now the urgency is only in the subject line, not in the body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends soon: up to 60% off summer sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off is going fast — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, decor, and outdoor picks up to 60% off — while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale prices drop as low as 60% off, but not for long.`
