# TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "TODAY ONLY: 20% off lighting!" and the email's first banner delivers it: "now in our app: 20% off lighting, today only!" The problem is that the deal is app-only. The only button in the hero is "Download Our App," so a subscriber who wants lamps today has to install something first. That is a lot of friction for a one-day sale.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. It runs through a holiday mantel, a rug and accent-chair promo ("up to 20% off"), a six-lamp grid, a Reward Dollars card pitch, a second app download block, a Design Desk booking and a store locator. The lighting grid is the best part. It has real prices with strikethroughs, for example the Amaryllis lamp at $76.99 down to $53.98. But it sits below two off-topic sections. The rug and chair promo is the same offer I saw in this brand's earlier "Starts now! Up to 20% off accent chairs, tables & rugs" send today.
- This is the fourth Crate & Barrel send I've audited today. The urgency is credible and the offer is concrete, but the email fights its own message. I'd call it an average send, in line with the 6/10 scores of today's other sends.

## What's working

- The subject and the hero banner match, with "20% off lighting, today only!" in both. That is a clear, specific offer with a deadline.
- The lighting grid shows prices with strikethroughs, so the savings are visible in dollars.
- The product photography is clean and consistent, and nothing in the render is broken.
- The footer navigation (Furniture, Bedding, Rugs, Sale and so on) and the legal text are complete and tidy.

## What's weak

- The hero's only CTA is "Download Our App," not "Shop lighting." The offer is gated behind an install.
- The second section is a holiday mantel scene with "5 holiday-ready styles you'll love," which has nothing to do with lighting. It pushes the lighting products far down the page.
- The rug and chair block with "up to 20% off" repeats today's earlier send and muddies the "today only" lighting message.
- There are too many competing CTAs: Shop Now, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment, plus the app badge. The Thanksgiving dining banner at the very top adds another message before the hero.
- The grid has no ratings or reviews, and it is not clear whether the lamp prices already include the 20% discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero CTA "Shop 20% Off Lighting" and link it to the lighting category. Offer the app as a secondary option, or drop the app-only gate if the deal allows it.
- 2. Move the six-product lighting grid up directly under the hero and cut the mantel and rug sections. A one-day lighting sale should be a one-topic email.
- 3. Add a countdown or an "ends tonight at midnight" line near the hero to back up "today only."
- 4. Move the Thanksgiving dining banner out of the top slot, or drop it for this send.
- 5. Show the sale price and the percentage off on each product, and add star ratings.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Floor lamps, chandeliers and pendants, 20% off until midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (QA: 0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The main findings are about 20 images flagged for missing alt text, an unverified unsubscribe and authentication state, and some template hygiene problems in the `<head>`. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **52 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, destination status and UTM handling are therefore unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are the same pixel with only the counter changed. This adds third-party requests and load time with no apparent benefit.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs.** Many image URLs carry `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…`. This looks like a hashed subscriber ID. The same hash prefix appears as `s=` in the `rlcdn.com` pixels, so the identifier is shared with a third-party domain.
- **Open-tracking pixels.**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/df1f12ff-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images with no alt text (QA warning).** The check can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`.
  - **Likely content images (about 14):**
    - 6 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` (`bbf6f9d4-…png`, `56be7a63-…jpg`, `18887a19-…jpg`, `91daf28b-…gif`, `5c7b78d8-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`).
    - 7 dynamic product-recommendation images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede`, `789e7381ff44be74`, `5ec241ce0b852c66`, `00423cc9007b550c`, `f5ca909404423879`, `45e8c84f5a9185ff`, `18f126218cc222ec`).
    - If these are the promo or product imagery, the image-blocked experience is empty.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel. They should carry `alt=""`.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` stops zooming on some mobile clients.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `*` and `body`, which blocks user text-size scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Conflicting image sizing.** `img {max-width:600px}` (min-width 768px) is overridden by `img {max-width:640px}` (min-width 640px), so the 600px rule is dead.
