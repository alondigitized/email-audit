# Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand last-day push for upholstery. The top of the email carries a Lounge 3-Piece Sectional hero, then "hours left: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals*" and a single "Shop Now" button. Yesterday's "The bestseller edit" and "A sofa that's soft, cozy" sends set this up. This one adds a real deadline and a blunt subject line, which is the right escalation.
- The hero is strong, but the email runs about 8,600px long and dilutes itself. After the sofa and sectional tiles it drifts into rugs, lighting, free swatches, performance fabrics, The Design Desk, custom furniture, a product grid, the Crate Card and the app. A "final hours" send should push one action. This one reads like the general monthly newsletter with a sale banner on top.
- Verdict: a solid, credible send that is let down by cadence (the fourth near-identical sofa message in two days) and a bloated body. It is above average but not sharp.

## What's working

- The hero shows the sectional with a price caption and "Shop Now" directly under the offer, so the deal and the action sit together.
- "hours left" in the hero matches the "Ends today!" subject, so the urgency is consistent and believable.
- The two-up "up to 20% off sectionals / sofas" tiles with "Shop Upholstery Sale" give a specific, category-matched CTA.
- The performance-fabrics module, with a stain on the cushion, answers the real objection to buying a sofa (durability) with a visual.
- Photography is warm and consistent, and the brand voice stays on-brand throughout.

## What's weak

- The sale gets one hero and one tile row. Everything after is a different pitch, including swatches, the Design Desk appointment, custom furniture, the Crate Card and the app download. That weakens the "ends today" pressure.
- The Design Desk "Book a Free Appointment" CTA appears twice and competes with the sale CTA.
- The offer is "up to 20% off" on "select styles" with an asterisk. It's modest, and the real terms sit in tiny footer text.
- The subject is 56 characters, so it likely truncates on mobile before "sectionals".
- Sofas have been the lead in every send since 2026-10-13, so a subscriber sees the same message again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the two sale tiles, one best-seller row with prices and a deadline footer. Drop the rugs/lighting/decor/mirrors tiles, the Design Desk and the app block for a final-day send.
- 2. Put a live countdown or a "today 11:59 PM PT" line in the hero and make the headline the first thing under the image.
- 3. Show 3-4 specific sofas with sale prices and star ratings so there is a product to click, not just a category.
- 4. Collapse the two Design Desk blocks into one, below the sale content.
- 5. Suppress or space this send against yesterday's sofa sends for people who already clicked.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 20% off our best-selling sofas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: sofas & sectionals up to 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Axis, Lounge, Oceanside and more. Sale prices end at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $99+ and stain-resistant fabrics, today only.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a Crate & Barrel sofa-sale send through the Movable Ink/Salesforce-style stack (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). Its HTML has several accessibility and tracking-hygiene problems: many images have no alt text, and there are third-party pixels. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits, not from confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level findings are limited to what the QA output shows.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 64 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and validity are unverified.
- Five identical LiveRamp pixels fire from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. This is repeated third-party tracking, which adds requests and privacy exposure. All five carry no alt text.
- The Movable Ink assets (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…`) and the open pixel (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`) put a user-identifying hash (`mi_u`) in the URL. Dynamic images that fail to load will leave blank slots.
- `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9bf6d4fb-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a further tracking or open-pixel call with no alt text.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**

No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. The `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment is a template note, not a token. The truncated source means I can't rule out tokens further down.

5. **Compliance**

- The visible source didn't include the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not verifiable. All 64 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so no landing URLs, UTM parameters or destination pages were resolved. The sale claim ("Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals") can't be matched to a landing page from this data.

4. Escape the `&` in `<title>` and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.
