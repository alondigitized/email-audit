# Just for you: a holiday sneak peek, only on our app!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a genre shift for Crate & Kids: no countdown clock, no "60% off," no Labor Day urgency — just an app-download push wrapped in a "holiday sneak peek" tease, followed by a kitchen-sink scroll of unrelated modules (design service sweepstakes, Jellycat restock, a "we love these for you" product grid, and a new co-brand credit card pitch). The hero itself is well-styled — moody green nursery, warm lighting — but the actual holiday sneak peek is nowhere to be seen; you're asked to download the app to see it, which is a real ask for zero payoff in the email itself.
- After the hero, the email loses focus fast. A $5000 sweepstakes, a stuffed-animal restock, six unrelated nursery furniture pieces, and a credit card offer are all crammed into one send with no throughline back to "holiday." Anyone opening for a holiday preview gets six other pitches before they can leave.
- Compared to the 2026-09-09 "Something festive just landed on our app!" send (5.00/10), this is the same playbook — hide the actual content behind an app download — but with more clutter bolted on around it. Judgment: a decent hero, undermined by a bait-and-switch CTA and a scattershot body. **5/10.**

## What's working

- Hero image is genuinely appealing — warm, styled, on-brand holiday nursery scene.
- "holiday sneak peek, only on our app" headline is concrete and creates a plausible reason to open the app.
- Product grid ("we love these for you") shows real SKUs with prices, no placeholder or broken-image gaps.
- Clean section dividers and consistent brand typography/logo throughout — no render bugs.

## What's weak

- The "sneak peek" is not actually shown — you download the app to see it, which is a bait-and-switch on the subject line's promise.
- Body sprawls into unrelated territory (sweepstakes, Jellycat restock, furniture grid, credit card) with no holiday thread connecting any of it.
- Two separate app-download CTAs ("Download Our App" and "Download Now" / "chance at $500") repeat the same ask without adding new information.
- No actual holiday product, image, or date is shown anywhere in the email — just the word "holiday" in copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show 2-3 actual holiday products or a real preview image in the email itself — don't make the entire payoff app-exclusive. If the app-exclusive angle is required, show a teaser thumbnail behind a blur/lock icon so there's a visual reason to click.
- 2. Cut the email down to one focused module (holiday preview + app CTA) and move the sweepstakes/Jellycat/credit-card pitches to separate sends — this reads like four emails stapled together.
- 3. Move the design-service sweepstakes and Jellycat restock either earlier (if they're the real priority) or drop them from this send entirely.
- **Subject Alt A:** `See our holiday collection first — app only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Holiday favorites just dropped (app exclusive)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toys, decor, and cozy bedding — download the app to shop first`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Be first in for holiday. Plus: enter to win $5000.`
