# Beyond bins & baskets: clever storage ideas!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- The render is 10,587px tall and I only saw it scaled down, so I'm reading layout and headline copy, not fine print. This is a solid, on-brand storage-ideas email. It runs a clean editorial rhythm of alternating lifestyle shots, one-line captions and "Shop…" buttons. It works through bedroom storage, bunk beds, bookcases and bins & baskets, then a nursery storage block. The subject promise ("clever storage ideas") is delivered by the body.
- The main flaw is a mismatched opener. The first thing the reader sees is an "ending soon! up to 20% off the best baby essentials" banner. That is a different campaign from the one in the subject line, and it pushes the storage hero ("a room they can clean up in 5 minutes flat") below it. The same baby-sale module repeats near the bottom, along with an app promo and a design-services promo. The email tries to do too many jobs.
- Overall it's competent and consistent with the brand's recent 7/10 sends, but it's diluted. Storage is the story the subject sells, and the email keeps interrupting it with the baby sale.

## What's working

- The "a room they can clean up in 5 minutes flat" hero gives storage a benefit-led promise.
- Modules are clear and repeatable: image, caption, then a specific button such as "Shop Kids Bedroom Furniture" or "Shop Bins & Baskets".
- Named hero products (Jenny Lind storage bed, Bridgeport bunk bed, Oliver and Cade bookcases) give the reader something concrete to click.
- Detail like "made with FSC-certified oak" and "deep-drawer storage" adds reasons to buy.
- A "we love these for you" grid with visible prices adds a browsing path at the end.

## What's weak

- The top banner sells a baby-essentials sale while the subject and hero are about kids' storage, so the opening is confusing.
- The baby-sale module appears twice (top banner and mid-lower block), and the app and design-desk promos are also stacked in. The email is very long and the ending is scattered.
- The only offer, up to 20% off baby essentials, has nothing to do with storage furniture. A reader shopping for storage sees no reason to buy today.
- Many buttons share similar "Shop…" wording, so no single primary CTA stands out.
- This is another near-daily send. It follows the 9/28 "ENDS SOON: Up to 20% off the BEST baby essentials" email, and the baby-sale banner here feels recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the baby-sale banner from the top and either drop it or move it to a small footer strip. Let the storage hero open the email.
- 2. If a promo is needed, attach a storage-relevant offer, such as "Free shipping on storage" or a percentage off bookcases and bins.
- 3. Cut the repeated baby-sale block and the app and design-desk promos to shorten the email.
- 4. Pick one hero product, such as the bunk bed with drawers, and make it the single dominant CTA.
- 5. Add reviews or ratings to the product tiles.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a template-built (Movable Ink/"MI") XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with 77 click-redirect links and about 37 `<img>` tags missing `alt`. Header, authentication and UTM checks are inconclusive: the QA warnings come from relay capture gaps, and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 77 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM handling could not be checked. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Dynamic image hosts:** Several images depend on personalization or tracking hosts (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, 6 instances; `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ab1e2420-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`). If these hosts are slow or blocked, those slots render empty.
- **Third-party pixels:** `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` fire five times with the same `s=` value. That looks redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (confirmed by QA):** About 37 images have no `alt` attribute.
  - 23 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `51d4c5f9-25a0-4018-b814-7eed720076fa.jpg`, `cdab6b90-….gif`). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no text equivalent, and if these are linked, the link has no accessible name.
  - The rest are a spacer (`s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`) and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`). For those, an explicit `alt=""` is the right fix, not descriptive text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Leftover template text:** The source still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (typo included). It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed for XHTML and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type meta.
  - The `format-detection` meta appears three times.
  - The `img {max-width:600px}` rule in the `min-width:768px` query is overridden by the later `min-width:640px` rule (`640px`).
- **Mixed in the source:** `lang="en"`, `color-scheme` metas and MSO conditional comments are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. Nothing unrendered was visible, since the `mi_u=` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` values are resolved. The truncated HTML means the body and footer were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both missing. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. A sender of this size very likely sets them, but the capture can't show it.
- **Authentication-Results:** Missing for the same reason. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, though the From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not verifiable, because the HTML was cut off before the footer.
- **Privacy note:** The `s=dbe8da71…` values in the LiveRamp pixels are 64-hex identifiers (consistent with a SHA-256 hash). They are sent to a third party on each open. It is worth confirming this is disclosed and consent-appropriate.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All links are behind click-redirect domains and were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to all 23 content images. Use `alt=""` on the spacer and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Fix the `&` in the title (`&amp;`), close or remove the duplicate metas, and strip the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls to one if the duplication isn't intentional.
5. Improve the QA tool:
   - Resolve redirects, capturing the final URL and UTMs, instead of skipping the probe.
   - Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message instead of the relay copy.
   - Analyze the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and address can be checked.
   - Treat tracking pixels separately from content images in the alt-text check.
