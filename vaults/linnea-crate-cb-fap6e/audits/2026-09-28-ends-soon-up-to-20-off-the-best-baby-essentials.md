# ENDS SOON: Up to 20% off the BEST baby essentials →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely well-organized brand-wide sale email that leads exactly where the subject promises: "ends soon! up to 20% off baby essentials" sits right at the top over a warm, lifestyle baby-wearing shot, followed by an immediate "Shop the Sale" button. From there it turns into a long, brand-by-brand scroll — Nuna, Stokke, BabyBjörn, Lalo, Mushie, WonderFold, BEABA, Maxi-Cosi, Thule, Cybex — each with its own discrete discount tile, which is a smart way to let a shopper self-select by brand loyalty rather than forcing one generic hero product.
- The weakness is length and repetition: by the time you hit "more ways to save," then a Crate&Kids x Properties collab, then clearance, then a Nuna travel system exclusive, then "we love these for you," the email has essentially become five emails stapled together. The urgency promise ("ends soon") in the subject is never reinforced with a date or countdown anywhere in the body, so the scarcity feels like copy dressing rather than a real deadline.
- Judgement: solid top section, but it dilutes itself with scope creep below the fold — consistent with this brand's pattern (see 2026-09-26 "60% off clearance" send, which also drifted into a grab-bag).

## What's working

- Hero directly mirrors the subject line — "up to 20% off the best baby essentials" appears verbatim under "ends soon!" with a soft, on-persona baby-wearing photo.
- Clear, consistent visual system: each brand section uses the same badge-plus-photo-plus-CTA pattern, making the long scroll easy to skim even if not easy to finish.
- Percentage-off badges are specific and visible per product (20% off Bugaboo, 15% off Maxi-Cosi, etc.), not just one vague top-line offer.
- No render bugs — images all loaded, text is legible, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- No visible deadline or date — "ends soon" is never backed by a specific end date/time anywhere in the body, so the urgency is unearned.
- The email is extremely long and keeps introducing new campaigns (clearance, Crate&Kids x Properties, Nuna exclusive, "we love these for you," credit card, app, TheDesignDesk) well past the baby-essentials sale promised in the subject — classic scope creep.
- Primary CTA gets diluted — by the sixth "Shop All [Brand]" button, there's no single obvious next action, hurting decisiveness.
- The clearance and Nuna exclusive sections near the bottom compete with the top-line sale rather than reinforcing it, muddying which offer is "the" offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Ends Sunday 11:59pm ET") — this single fix would make the "ends soon" urgency credible and testable.
- 2. Cut the email after the core essentials brand grid (Nuna → Cybex) and move clearance/Properties collab/app promo/credit card into a separate send — reduces scroll fatigue and keeps the CTA singular.
- 3. Add a mini nav/anchor bar right under the hero linking to each brand section, so long-scrollers can jump straight to their brand.
- 4. Replace at least one generic "Shop All [Brand]" CTA per section with a named hero product ("Shop the Bugaboo Fox5") to lift specificity.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — up to 20% off strollers, car seats & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nuna, Stokke, Cybex, Maxi-Cosi — 15-20% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your nursery wishlist, marked down through Sunday`
