## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate comes from missing alt text on about 40 images and three header warnings. The warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so link targets, footer content and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 79 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels with identifiers:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp) carry what appears to be a per-recipient hashed identifier in `s=`. The same value appears in the `mi_u=` parameter on the first-party pixels `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/775e4367-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another open or tracking beacon. Sending a recipient-derived identifier to a third-party ad-data domain is worth reviewing for privacy disclosure. It isn't a rendering defect.
- **Redundant pixels:** `rlcdn.com` is requested five times with only `n` varying, and `f3c0d22992a01946.png` six times. This adds requests without apparent benefit.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (about 25):**
  - 19 are hosted at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, for example `ec997ddd-48db-4d16-bffd-48b5b834d0f5.jpg` and `b551a04f-…gif`.
  - 1 is at `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026_1104_CBK_QualityGifting_SecondaryStor…`.
  - 4 more are `4341f12e-…`, `43537ec0-…`, `913b427e-…jpg` and `d24f24c6-…gif`.
  - The last is `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - If these images carry the product or offer content, an image-blocked or screen-reader view is likely empty. The QA check can't tell whether `alt` is absent or `alt=""`, so confirm in the full source.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (about 13):** The `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels and `o.gif` should carry `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later, equal-specificity `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider, images cap at 640px, not 600px.
- **Template residue:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the head. It is harmless but misspelled and unremoved.
- **Minor:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - The title is generic and doesn't match the "Kids" sender.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, redundantly.

No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Personalization appears only as the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` tracking parameters. The truncation limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are WARN, not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not a confirmed sender defect. The headers need to be checked on a raw copy of the message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, which the QA note also attributes to the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent but not proof.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so neither could be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw message, or fix the relay capture, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Then retest.
2. Resolve a sample of the 79 tracking links, with redirect-following, to verify destinations and UTMs. This is the largest unaudited area.
