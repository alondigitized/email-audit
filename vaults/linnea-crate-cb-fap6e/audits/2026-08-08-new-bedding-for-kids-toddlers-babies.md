# New bedding for kids, toddlers & babies →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing the "everything bedding, everywhere" sweep — kids bedding, baby bedding, toddler bedding, stroller blankets, the Avocado mattress collab, a summer sale banner, a TheDesignDesk tween bedroom pitch, and a Nuna stroller cross-promo, all stacked into one very long scroll. It's structurally close to the 2026-08-08 "storage picks" grab-bag (6.00) rather than the disciplined single-category sends that scored 9.00 and 8.00 earlier this week.
- The subject line "New bedding for kids, toddlers & babies" is at least honest about the scope — it tells you up front this is a catalog dump, not a focused launch. That's a point in its favor versus a subject that overpromises and under-delivers. But by the time a reader hits the Nuna stroller section and the TheDesignDesk tween makeover pitch, they've scrolled past four or five distinct "shop now" asks that have nothing to do with what got them to open.
- Net: solid, on-brand product photography and a clean modular grid structure, but this send tries to be four emails at once. A subscriber who wants toddler bedding has to scroll past kids, baby, stroller blankets, and two other brands' promos to find it.

## What's working

- Clear section dividers by age group (kids / baby / toddler) each with its own hero shot and "Shop New X Bedding" CTA button — easy to scan even in a long email.
- Product grids consistently label "Personalized," "New Arrival," and "Bestseller" tags, giving quick differentiation between similar-looking quilts.
- The nursery/newborn interstitial ("the newborn shop") with a sleeping-baby photo is a nice emotional beat that breaks up the product-grid monotony.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together across the full length.
- Consistent muted, neutral color palette (sage, blush, cream) that reads as cohesive brand identity throughout.

## What's weak

- The email sprawls across at least seven distinct promotions (kids/baby/toddler bedding, stroller blankets, Avocado collection, summer sale, TheDesignDesk tween makeovers, Nuna strollers) — no single clear primary offer.
- The subject promises "kids, toddlers & babies" bedding but the email keeps going well past that into unrelated categories (strollers, financing app pitch), which isn't reflected in the subject/preview at all.
- "Up to 50% off summer sale" — the one concrete discount in the whole email — is buried roughly two-thirds of the way down, well past where most readers on mobile will have stopped scrolling.
- Cross-brand inserts (Nuna, TheDesignDesk) dilute the Crate & Kids identity and read as ad space rather than curated recommendations.
- No urgency or dates anywhere except the vague "sale" banner — nothing tells the reader why they should act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two or three focused sends (kids bedding / baby+toddler bedding / cross-sell strollers+partners) — the 08-07 disciplined nursery-bestsellers send scored 9.00 precisely because it stayed in one lane; this one dilutes that formula.
- 2. Move the "up to 50% off summer sale" banner to the top, above the fold — it's the only hard offer in the email and it's currently invisible without heavy scrolling.
- 3. Cut or relocate the Nuna and TheDesignDesk sections to a separate partner-spotlight email; they break the category promise made in the subject line.
- 4. Add a concrete deadline to the summer sale banner (e.g., "ends Sunday") to create real urgency instead of an open-ended "sale."
- 5. Tighten the subject to reflect the actual scope or narrow the email to match the subject.
