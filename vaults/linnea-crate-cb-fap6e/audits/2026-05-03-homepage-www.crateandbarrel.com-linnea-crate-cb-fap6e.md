# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**

- This is clearly a high-design, aspirational home brand — the outdoor living hero is warm, editorial, and looks like a Kinfolk spread come to life. Right now it's pushing an outdoor furniture campaign pretty hard, with an email capture modal that fires immediately on load. For someone like me who's actively building out a registry and obsessing over every piece, there's a lot here that resonates — but that modal is the first thing I actually see, which is a friction problem.
- **6/10**
- + hero copy and imagery speak to my aesthetic (minimal, warm, outdoor living) — matches the modern-minimal sensibility I bring to everything I buy
- + one concrete offer is visible ("Save 10% off full-price items*" in the modal)

## What's working

- **The hero lifestyle shot** — the large patio umbrella with warm terracotta tones and natural materials is exactly the kind of aspirational content I stop scrolling for. It hits the walnut-and-linen corner of my brain immediately.
- **"Weather-ready quality, exclusive design"** — the hero headline is tight and confident. It doesn't over-explain; it trusts the visual.
- **The "Shop All Outdoor" CTA** — visible beneath the headline, clear destination, no mystery about where I'll land.
- **The 10% off modal** — even though the timing is intrusive, the offer itself is solid. Full-price items, email + SMS opt-in, clean form layout.
- **"Built-to-last bestsellers" section** — glimpsed below the fold, this kind of social proof / editorial naming is exactly the kind of thing that slows me down.

## What's weak

- **The modal fires immediately** — I haven't even seen the homepage yet and I'm being asked for my email. This is the single biggest bounce risk for a high-intent returning shopper like me.
- **Registry is invisible** — I literally registered here. There is no "Manage Your Registry," no returning-registrant hook, no "welcome back" signal anywhere above the fold. This feels like a missed layup.
- **The top nav discount bar** ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars. Terms Apply →") is tiny and easy to miss — this is the loyalty hook but it's buried in a thin banner at the very top.
- **"Maybe Later" dismissal copy** — feels passive-aggressive. Just say "No thanks."
- **The asterisk on "full-price items*"** — the fine print is right there in the modal and it's dense legal boilerplate. Not egregious, but the asterisk signals exclusions before I've even agreed.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by 10–15 seconds or trigger on exit intent** — let me actually see the homepage before asking for my email. I'm a registered customer; this friction is unnecessary.
- **Add a returning-registrant banner** — a slim persistent bar ("You have a registry · View it →") for logged-in or cookied users would immediately surface the use case I'm actually here for.
- **Swap the nav loyalty text for a visible chip or badge** — pull the "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" out of the tiny top strip and give it a pill badge near the account icon so loyalty feels like a feature, not fine print.
- **Add a registry or gifting editorial module** — a lifestyle shot of a table set for two with a "Built your registry around this" caption would hit me and everyone else in bridal/new-home setup mode directly.
