# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-03

The bones are great; the entrance is friction.
- **8/10**
- + Hero imagery aligns with modern minimal aesthetic — outdoor terrace, clean lines, neutral palette fits the sensibility I shop for
- + Loyalty hook above the fold — "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars" is the very first banner I see
- + Concrete offer visible above the fold — "Up to 40% off Outdoor" CTAs in the hero
- + Unmistakable primary CTA — "Shop All Outdoor" button clearly rendered

- + Page reflects current campaign/season — outdoor content in early May is exactly right

## What's working

- **"weather-ready quality, exclusive design" hero copy** — quality-led messaging is exactly right for the brand and for me; I'm full-price tolerant and here for the craft, not the clearance
- **Rewards banner ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars")** — instantly reminds me there's a loyalty benefit; enough to keep me signed in
- **Outdoor lifestyle photography** — the terrace scene with the large umbrella and modern seating is genuinely aspirational and matches my post-registry patio wishlist
- **Seasonal accuracy** — leaning into outdoor in May is the right call; I'm actively thinking about summer entertaining
- **Category tiles with specific discount callouts** — "Up to 50% off Lounge Furniture," "Up to 50% off Dining Furniture" are directly actionable, not vague
- **"Built-to-last bestsellers" module** — confirms the brand's quality positioning; the modern interior shot reinforces that this isn't a trend-chasing brand

## What's weak

- **Forced modal is the first thing I experience** — before I can register any merchandising, I'm asked for email AND phone number; requiring both before I've seen a single product is aggressive
- **Hero is outdoor-only** — I came in with registry things on my mind: dinnerware, a walnut tray, maybe a duvet; there's no pathway to that world above the fold
- **Zero returning-shopper recognition** — I'm a registered shopper at this store; no "Welcome back," no "View your registry," no recently-viewed hook anywhere visible
- **"Shop All Outdoor" CTA is too generic** — the hero names the Bellacor Collection; the button should connect to it directly rather than dumping me into the full outdoor catalog

## Recommendations

- **Make the phone number optional in the modal** — or replace the double-field capture with email-only; requiring both before a first meaningful interaction is a conversion killer and sets a sour tone
- **Add a registry re-entry CTA** — even a pinned "View Your Registry →" in the top nav or a thin personalized banner would dramatically improve session value for someone like me who is actively registered
- **Tighten the hero CTA** — swap `Shop All Outdoor` for `Shop the Bellacor Collection` to create a direct through-line from hero headline to product destination
- **Add a tabletop or indoor editorial strip below the outdoor tiles** — a "Set the table for summer" module would bridge the outdoor mood to the registry categories that drive my actual purchasing
