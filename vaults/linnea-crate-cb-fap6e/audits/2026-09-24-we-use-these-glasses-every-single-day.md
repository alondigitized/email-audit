# We use these glasses every single day
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is one of the stronger Crate & Barrel sends in the recent batch — it actually opens with the thing the subject promises. "We use these glasses every single day" leads straight into a moody pour-shot of a glass mug and "refresh your go-to glassware" with a "Shop Drinkware" button, no scrolling required. That's a real improvement over the pattern flagged in prior audits (2026-09-23 "the one that checks every box" sofa email, 2026-09-24 nursery chair email) where the subject's promise dissolved into a sprawling catalog.
- But it doesn't hold the line. By the second screen it's already pivoted to "tabletop essentials start at $1.95" (six unrelated subcategories: dinnerware, flatware, table linens, outdoor tabletop), then a smoothie recipe, then a full bedroom-furniture pitch, then a generic "we love these for you" product grid, then the Crate Card, the app, and TheDesignDesk. The glassware promise gets about 15% of the real estate before the email becomes the same everything-store scroll as the rest of the brand's recent sends.
- Net: good hook, weak follow-through. It earns credit for nailing the top of the email but loses it for abandoning its own premise almost immediately.

## What's working

- Hero image (milk/coffee pouring into a glass mug) is a strong, specific visual match for "glasses every single day" — no bait-and-switch at the very top.
- "Shop Drinkware" is a clear, unambiguous, verb+noun CTA right under the hero.
- The "we love these for you" product grid at the bottom shows real prices ($10.95–$65.95) and product names, giving concrete detail for someone actually shopping glassware/tableware.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The email abandons the glassware premise almost immediately, pivoting to tabletop essentials, a smoothie recipe, bedroom furniture, and a credit card pitch — the subject earns maybe one screen out of roughly a dozen.
- No actual discount or promo tied to the glassware hero itself — "$1.95" is a starting price for an unrelated tabletop category, not an offer on "these glasses."
- Bedroom furniture mid-email is a jarring non-sequitur with zero connection to the glassware/tabletop theme.
- No urgency or time-bound element anywhere in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the bedroom-furniture module entirely (or move it to a separate send) — it has no thematic tie to a glassware/tabletop email and dilutes an otherwise-coherent top half.
- 2. Give the hero an actual offer — a %-off or "buy 4 get 1 free" on glassware would convert a mood shot into a real reason to click, versus just "refresh."
- 3. Move the "we love these for you" glassware/dinnerware grid up, right after the hero, so the email's back half reinforces the subject instead of drifting into recipes and furniture.
- 4. Tie the Crate Card 10% back callout to the glassware purchase specifically ("get rewarded on today's pick") rather than leaving it as a generic module at the bottom.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New drinkware, 20% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Simple, sturdy, and built for daily use — see the set.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus tabletop essentials from $1.95.`
