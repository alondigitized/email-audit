## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (Marigold/"MI" platform) with VML/MSO fallbacks. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 48 images flagged) and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. QA scored 57% pass, with 0 hard issues and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **81 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the click-redirect domains, so destination status codes, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Repeated tracking pixels.**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` fires 5 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ef032a69-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further pixels.
  - None of these has alt text, and they add 13 or more extra requests per open.
- **Third-party pixel receives a recipient identifier.** The `s=` value on `sr.rlcdn.com` (a LiveRamp domain) matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` token on the first-party pixels. A persistent per-recipient hash is therefore passed to an external domain.
- **Template leftovers in the head.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the typo) is still present.
  - There is an empty `<!---->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 48 images flagged:**
  - Content images: about 28 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|.gif|.png`, for example `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `e54a93c8-7d2a-47db-a056-2da30d46377c.gif` and `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.
  - Scene7 assets: `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and the `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` spacer.
  - Pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""` explicitly. If the content images are linked tiles, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no label for them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`** disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`** appears in `html`, `body` and `*{…}`, which blocks text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Duplicate and redundant `<meta>` tags.**
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - The unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">` is inconsistent with the XHTML doctype.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Generic title.** It doesn't match the subject ("Spotlight on Nuna…").
- **Global `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}`** is a broad reset that can override intended line-heights, though `.f10`/`.f14`/`.f20` partly compensate on mobile.
- **Image width rules conflict.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap, and the later rule wins in the 768px+ range, so the 600px cap is overridden. The `max-width` cap also applies to every `img`, including the pixels.
- The HTML was truncated, so the body markup (headings, link text, tap-target sizes) could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations

3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and set it to the campaign subject. Delete the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas and the `IMPUT HERE` leftover comment.
4. Collapse the 6 duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels and 5 `448796.gif` pixels to one each. Review whether passing the `mi_u` hash to `rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy policy.
5. Re-test with raw headers from the original SMTP message, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the `Authentication-Results` pass/fail.
