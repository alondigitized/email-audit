# Up to 30% off holiday decor! Let’s deck your halls 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday-decor email on top of a Black Friday catch-all. The subject promises "up to 30% off holiday decor," but the top of the email opens with the generic Black Friday banner ("HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving"). Then comes a "new deals added! up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" hero, and only after that the decor hero. The image was heavily downscaled, so I can read layout and headlines but not fine print.
- The subject names one offer and the body leads with two different ones (50% and 30%). The decor message is real, with a "for all your holiday traditions" grid of garlands, stockings, ornaments and Hanukkah, but it sits second. After that the email turns into the long omnibus scroll: kitchen deals, a Thanksgiving table section, a "shop up to 50% off" tile grid, clearance, the Design Desk, product picks and the app promo.
- It's competent and on-brand, but it's the same long template as the 11/24 "NEW DEALS just added" and "New Black Friday deals added!" sends, with a decor hero swapped in. A subscriber who has gotten six of these in three days will skim it. Judgement: mid-pack. It would work better if it committed to decor.

## What's working

- The "for all your holiday traditions" grid is a good decor-specific block. It covers garlands, stockings, ornaments and Hanukkah, which is inclusive and easy to scan.
- The "HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving" banner adds credible, time-bound urgency.
- Every section has a visible button, so nothing is hard to find.
- The imagery is warm and seasonal (fireplace, garlands, red stockings) and matches the subject's 🎄 tone.
- I see no broken images or layout breakage in the render.

## What's weak

- Two hero offers sit stacked at the top (50% Black Friday, then 30% decor), so the eye has no single landing point. The subject's 30% offer isn't the first thing you see.
- The email is extremely long. Decor is about the top fifth, and the rest drifts into kitchen, Thanksgiving tables, furniture and clearance.
- Most CTAs read as generic "Shop Now" buttons, with no verb plus noun.
- It repeats the catch-all Black Friday template from earlier in the week (11/23 "It's on!", 11/24 "NEW DEALS just added"), so it adds to fatigue.
- Almost every offer is "up to," and there are no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the catch-all Black Friday hero and lead with the decor hero, so the subject and the first screen make the same promise.
- 2. Trim the body to decor and holiday entertaining, and move kitchen, furniture and clearance to a separate send or a short "more deals" strip.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop Garlands & Wreaths" and "Shop Stockings."
- 4. Show one or two hero products with price and a rating instead of only category tiles.
- 5. Use a deadline tied to something concrete, such as "Order by 12/__ for Christmas delivery."

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Movable Ink-personalized template with no hard QA failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The warnings are missing alt text on about 51 images and three unverifiable compliance and authentication headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks are incomplete.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All 93 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- Five identical pixels load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `n=5`. This is the LiveRamp third-party sync, fired five times. The `n=1..5` suffix suggests they are redundant.
- The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. The same per-recipient ID therefore goes to a third-party domain, which you may want to check against the privacy disclosures.
- Open and tracking pixels are `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1500e4f2-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are expected for Movable Ink.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 51 images were flagged.
  - 37 are hosted content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which look like dynamic recommendation images.
  - The rest are tracking pixels, where empty alt is acceptable.
  - If the content images are image-only blocks, the offer and CTA text will be invisible with images blocked or to screen readers.
  - The QA tool can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`. Confirm which it is.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` (in `*{}` and `body`) disables iOS text resizing.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The title is also generic.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** there are two `charset` declarations, and `format-detection` appears four times with overlapping values.
- **Conflicting `img` rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at desktop widths. The later rule wins, so 600px is dead code.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated HTML.

**5. Compliance**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was absent. The QA tool attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. This is unconfirmed, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown, not failed.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not assessable. Link probing was skipped for all 93 links, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs appear in the visible source or QA output.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers (not relay-processed) to verify the unsubscribe link, postal address, `List-Unsubscribe*` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
2. Resolve the 93 redirect links with a HEAD/GET follow-through to check final URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 37 content images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com` recommendation images. Add `alt=""` to the pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
5. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and deduplicate the `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags.
6. Reduce the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels to one, and confirm the third-party ID sharing is covered in the privacy policy.
