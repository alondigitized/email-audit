# Can you guess Caraway’s latest color? (Hint: 🌊🧘🌴)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, kitchen-sink product-marathon email dressed up around a single new-color teaser for Caraway. The subject promises a guessing game ("Can you guess Caraway's latest color?") but the render never actually shows what the color is — the Caraway module just says "a calming new color from Caraway" with no swatch or clear product shot of the color itself, so the hook in the subject line doesn't pay off visually. After that it sprawls into blenders, cookware, toaster ovens, ice cream makers, a "clean kitchen" section, and an app-download pitch — no single hero, no urgency, no discount.
- Compared to the Labor Day marathon-scroll sends I've audited all week (countdown clocks, "60% off," Double Rewards banners), this is a calmer, non-promotional template — closer to the "holiday sneak peek" app-push send from earlier today. That's a nice change of pace from the discount fatigue, but it trades urgency for a wall of undifferentiated modules with no clear reason to click any one of them.
- Net: pleasant browsing content, weak conversion mechanics. This reads more like a catalog digest than a targeted send.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual style — consistent photography, whitespace, and typography throughout.
- Caraway module is positioned early (second block) and tied to the subject line's teaser, so the thematic link is at least structurally present.
- "even more new arrivals that ship free" section clearly surfaces a real value signal (free shipping) with specific product thumbnails.
- No render bugs — no broken images, overlapping text, or placeholder content visible.

## What's weak

- The subject line's central hook — "guess Caraway's latest color" — is never resolved in the visible render; no swatch, no color name, no reveal.
- No discernible hero or single primary CTA — a dozen+ "Shop Now" buttons compete equally, with zero visual hierarchy pointing to one offer.
- No offer at all (no %, no $ off, no free-shipping headline) — every rubric offer-clarity signal fails.
- Email is extremely long (12,000px) with 10+ product categories crammed in — feels like a full catalog dump rather than a curated send.
- No urgency, no personalization, no loyalty/rewards messaging despite this brand's history of leaning hard on Rewards banners in recent sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Actually show the new Caraway color in the hero — a clear product swatch or named color ("Introducing: Lagoon") right under the subject's teaser, since that's the entire premise of the open.
- 2. Cut the email to 3-4 modules max and pick one hero (the Caraway color reveal) with a single dominant CTA — the rest can move to a "shop more" footer grid.
- 3. Add a concrete offer or reward hook (this brand has member Rewards infrastructure it uses elsewhere — bring at least a "member pricing" callout here) to give the click a reason beyond curiosity.
- 4. Tighten the subject to promise something that's actually delivered, or drop the tease format entirely.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New from Caraway: a color made for your kitchen`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on new arrivals across the kitchen`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The calming color everyone's asking about — shop it first`
