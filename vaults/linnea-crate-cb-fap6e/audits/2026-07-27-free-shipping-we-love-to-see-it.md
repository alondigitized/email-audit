# FREE SHIPPING. We love to see it 👀
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running the same kitchen-sink playbook flagged across this brand's last several sends (7/25, 7/26 x3, 7/27's "Can't wait to get home"), just with free shipping as the new headline hook. The subject promises one clean thing — "FREE SHIPPING" — but the very top of the render opens with a competing "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off" ribbon sitting above the hero photo, and by mid-scroll there's a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, then a second "up to 50% off summer sale" banner, then a credit-card acquisition push, then an app download pitch. Four distinct offers/asks in one email.
- The free-shipping section itself is well executed: a genuinely nice monogrammed-backpack hero, a clear "it. all. ships. FREE!" headline, and a tidy 8-tile category grid ("kids bedding ships free," "bath ships free," etc.) that actually earns the subject line. If the email stopped there it would be a strong, focused send.
- It doesn't stop there, and that's the recurring problem with this brand's cadence — a good single-offer idea gets buried under three more asks by the time you've scrolled halfway. Judgement: solid top module, undermined by the same bolt-on sprawl this brand keeps shipping.

## What's working

- Hero image (parent with a personalized yellow quilted backpack) is warm, on-brand, and immediately readable as back-to-school/kids content.
- "it. all. ships. FREE!" is a clean, unambiguous headline directly matching the subject line's promise.
- The free-shipping category grid (bedding, gear, cuddlies, bath, curtains, throw pillows) is well organized and reinforces the offer across the whole catalog rather than one category.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.
- "we love these for you" product grid shows real SKUs with visible prices (backpack $40.20, dresser, glider recliner, stroller travel system), not just generic lifestyle shots.

## What's weak

- The very first thing visible is a "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off" ribbon — a different offer than the one in the subject line, creating a mismatched first impression.
- A $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes and a second "up to 50% off summer sale" banner are both stacked into the same email, diluting the free-shipping message the subject sold.
- A "new Crate & Barrel card" cross-sell and an app-download pitch are also crammed in near the bottom — neither belongs in an email whose subject is about shipping cost.
- No urgency or deadline is visibly legible anywhere in the render, so the free-shipping offer reads as evergreen rather than something to act on today.
- No reviews or social proof on any product tile, despite several tiles showing higher-ticket items (a $1,730 stroller system, a dresser) where trust signals would help conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to just the free-shipping module (hero + "it. all. ships. FREE!" + category grid) and send the sweepstakes, summer sale, and credit-card pitch as separate, focused sends — this is the single highest-impact fix given the brand's repeated kitchen-sink pattern.
- 2. Remove or demote the back-to-school ribbon above the hero — it competes with the subject line's promise in the first 2 seconds of open.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the free-shipping offer ("ends Sunday," "today only") to convert the evergreen-feeling message into something with urgency.
- 4. Add a star-rating or review-count strap to at least the higher-ticket product tiles (stroller system, dresser) to build trust before the click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping, no minimum — starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `👀 Everything ships free (yes, everything)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — free shipping is already in your cart`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedding, gear, bath, and more — all shipping free today`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-templated (Movable Ink "MI" markup + Outlook MSO conditionals) responsive HTML build from Crate & Barrel's `mail.crateandbarrel.com` domain; it passed the majority of automated checks (57% pass rate, 0 hard issues) but carries unresolved template scaffolding, missing alt text across all images, and unverifiable authentication/unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 71 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (tracking-domain wrapped), so final destination URLs could not be verified.
- The click/open pixel `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101` fires **6 times** in the same document (identical `src`, no differentiating params) — likely per-content-block engagement tracking, but worth confirming this is intentional and not a duplicate-insertion bug, since each duplicate adds a redundant network request.
- The LiveRamp/RLCDN pixel `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` fires 5 times sequentially (only the `n` param increments) — consistent with LiveRamp identity/data-onboarding sync tags, not a defect.
- A DoubleVerify-style view pixel is present: `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2b15e0de-f9fc-449b-b110-6b57e489c488?mi_mid=019fa2cd-6e80-7000-a3c5-0dcba8ab310e&cp_tp=v`.
- A leftover ESP template placeholder is present in `<head>`: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (note the typo "IMPUT"). This indicates the font-import injection step in the build pipeline was either skipped or never cleaned up before send — not a rendering blocker on its own, but a sign of incomplete template QA.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **29 of 29 images in the QA scan are missing `alt` text**, including all content images served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `3ff87ae4-8e01-4f3a-8d96-c4313c2bd770.jpg`, and 12 others). This affects screen-reader users and anyone with images blocked by default (a common enterprise/webmail default), who will see a fully blank email with no fallback text.
- Tracking/beacon pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) also lack `alt=""`; for purely decorative/invisible pixels this is low-severity but should still use `alt=""` (not omitted) per best practice.
- The document uses XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE with MSO conditional blocks and `@media` breakpoints at 460px/640px/768px — standard multi-client scaffolding, no structural rendering defects observed in the truncated source.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` does not match the sending persona "Crate & Kids" (from header). This won't affect inbox rendering but is a code-level inconsistency in the template's `<title>` tag versus the From name.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, AMPscript, Handlebars, or unresolved `%%...%%` tokens) were observed in the visible/truncated HTML. No issues found based on available source — note that the source was truncated, so tokens later in the document could not be fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found in the captured headers.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe compliance): not found.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC status): not found — pass/fail status is unknown from this capture.
- QA flags these as WARN-level with a caveat that the AgentMail relay may simply not be capturing/forwarding these headers, rather than the sender omitting them. This should be verified against the raw MTA headers before treating it as a sender-side compliance gap, particularly given Gmail/Yahoo's Feb 2024 bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be verified: all outbound links route through tracking/redirect domains that were skipped by the HTTP prober, so final landing-page URLs and UTM parameters are not present in the QA data or visible HTML. No landing-page alignment can be confirmed from available evidence.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 14 unique content images; set `alt=""` explicitly on the 3 tracking/beacon pixel types (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. Remove or resolve the unfilled template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` before send.
3. Verify whether the 6x-duplicated `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel is intentional (per-block tracking) or a build duplication bug.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist on the raw MTA send (outside the AgentMail relay capture) to validate RFC 8058 / Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status via raw headers, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
