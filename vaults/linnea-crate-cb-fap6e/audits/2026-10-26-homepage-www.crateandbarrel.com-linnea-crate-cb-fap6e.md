# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see is a big "Save 10% off full-price items*" popup sitting on top of what looks like a walnut dining room sale. Behind it, Crate & Barrel is pushing "3 days left! up to 30% off the dining room e…" and "Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars." The brand feels right for me, but I have to get past the popup before I can shop.

## What's working

- **The hero photo:** dark walnut table, spindle-back chairs, and linen drapes. It's the exact look I register for, and it feels warm rather than trendy.
- **"Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars":** as a registry shopper I'll be spending a lot, so a returning-buyer reward at the top of the page matters.
- **"3 days left! up to 30% off":** a specific discount with a real deadline.
- **Nav row:** "Wedding Registry" is front and center, and the brand switcher (Crate&Barrel, Kids, CB2, Hudson Grace) lets me jump to CB2 for the bolder pieces.
- **Sale CTAs:** "Shop Dining Tables on Sale" and "Shop Dining Chairs on Sale" say exactly what I'll get.

## What's weak

- **The popup fires before I've seen anything.** It blocks the hero headline, most of the nav ("Free D… Serv…", "Tabletop &…"), and the promo strip.
- **The popup asks for too much.** Both email and mobile number are marked required for 10%.
- **The "Send me news about Crate & Barrel Kids too" box is pre-checked.** I'm not shopping for kids, and it feels sneaky.
- **The SMS legal paragraph is dense.** It mentions "AI content, cart reminders," data-sharing, and arbitration, which is a lot to read for a coupon.
- **The 10% comes with an asterisk and exclusions.** "Full-price items" is also strange next to a sale on dining, so it's unclear whether the two stack.
- **"Maybe Later" is the only way out besides the small X.** It's underlined text rather than a clear "No thanks."
- **No people or room-scale lifestyle shot.** It's just chairs, which is fine, but I can't picture my place.
- **The "0% Interest on a…" text in the top bar is clipped,** so the financing offer is unreadable.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the popup.** Trigger it after a scroll or on exit intent, or at least after the hero has been visible for a few seconds, so the sale banner and "Shop Dining Tables on Sale" land first.
- **Cut the popup to email only.** Make SMS an optional second step, uncheck the Kids box by default, and shorten the legal copy to one line with a link.
- **Say what the 10% covers.** Add a line like "Stacks with sale prices" or "Not valid on sale items" right under the headline, so there's no asterisk surprise.
- **Add a trust strip under the hero.** Free shipping threshold, returns window, and a registry perk ("Registry completion discount") would give me reasons to convert.
