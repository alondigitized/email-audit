# “But what’s my holiday aesthetic??” 🎄😵‍💫
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a five-look holiday style guide from Crate & Barrel. The subject asks "what's my holiday aesthetic??" and the body answers with five named styles: modern heritage, timeless evergreen, classic cheer, frosted sage and winter harvest. The idea is sound because it turns a catalog into a quiz-like decision. The dark-green and cream photography is cohesive and warm.
- The execution is long and repetitive. The render is about 12,000px tall, and all five looks use the same template: a big fireplace or tree hero, a two-image grid, a caption and a "Shop the Look" button. Nothing tells me which look is best for me, so I scroll through five equal blocks. A "hours left" free-shipping banner opens the email, but that is the same $99+ promo from the 10/18 sends ("2 days only! free shipping on orders over $99*"). The subject's personality is stronger than the body's.
- The tail then adds three more pitches: TheDesignDesk, an "in-stock furniture" block and an app download. It reads as a finished campaign with a clear theme, but too long and too many asks.
- My caveat: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can read headlines and button labels but not fine print or prices.

## What's working

- The subject's "But what's my holiday aesthetic??" is conversational and names a real shopper dilemma.
- The free-shipping banner at the top, "hours left… orders over $99*", gives a concrete, time-bounded offer before the hero.
- The five numbered, named looks ("1. modern heritage" through "5. winter harvest") make the email scannable and give each section a point of view.
- The photography is cohesive, with stockings, garland and candlelit mantels in a consistent palette. It looks premium and on-brand.
- Every look ends with a clear "Shop the Look" button, so the CTA copy is specific and consistent.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical blocks have no ranking or quiz mechanic, so the "find your aesthetic" promise is never paid off with a recommendation.
- The email is extremely long, and the tail stacks TheDesignDesk, furniture, a product grid and an app promo. The ask dilutes after the first two looks.
- The free-shipping banner repeats the 10/18 promo and carries an asterisk, so the offer is not fresh or special.
- "Shop the Look" buttons sit below each look, so the buyer can't tell which products or prices are in it. The "we love these for you" grid at the bottom is small and low-impact.
- The dark TheDesignDesk section breaks the visual rhythm and gives a second primary-looking CTA, "Book Your Free Appointment".

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a quick "pick your look" nav near the top, with five small tiles linking down or out. This is the single biggest lift because it delivers on the subject.
- 2. Cut the body to three looks plus one service block. Drop the app promo and the generic product grid.
- 3. Show two or three priced hero items per look instead of a decorative grid, so shoppers can judge value.
- 4. Give the shipping banner a fresh angle, or a real deadline time if it is down to hours.
- 5. Move TheDesignDesk to a single compact strip at the bottom.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five looks, one free-shipping deadline. Ends tonight on orders over $99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick a mantel, tree and table look. We've done the styling for you.`

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "But what's my holiday aesthetic??"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source (truncated) has widespread missing `alt` attributes (27 images flagged in the QA output) and several template-hygiene defects. Authentication and unsubscribe-header status could not be verified because the AgentMail relay did not expose those headers, so those QA warnings are unconfirmed. QA result: 57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 61 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party pixel (LiveRamp):** 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` are the same beacon fired five times with only `n` varying. The `s=` value is 64 hex characters, which is consistent with a hashed identifier. The source doesn't show what it hashes.
- **Movable Ink and open pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c48c3e55-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression trackers. The `dv.` URL has no file extension and no `alt`. Neither is a defect on its own, but they add to a heavy tracking footprint.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (27 images listed; the last QA entry is cut off, so the count may be higher):**
  - 13 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. Examples: `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `f8e57f8e-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`. These are probably content images, so with images blocked or a screen reader they have no text equivalent.
  - 7 on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, and others). These look like Movable Ink dynamic content images (`mi_u=` param), so dynamic content may be inaccessible.
  - 5 `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons, plus `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel. These are tracking pixels, and `alt=""` is the correct fix for them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS from scaling text.
- **Invalid markup:** the `<title>` contains a raw `&` (`Crate & Barrel`) under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting tags:**
  - `<meta charset>` appears alongside `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Global resets:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` apply to every element, which can override intended styling. I didn't confirm a rendering failure from them.
- **Positive:** `<html lang="en">` is set and MSO/VML fallbacks are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The `mi_u=` and `s=` values are populated. The body past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which suggests consistent sending infrastructure, but this proves nothing about authentication.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these fall past the truncation point and could not be verified.
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** the 5 `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons carry a 64-char hex identifier to a third-party data provider (LiveRamp). This is worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures. It is not a CAN-SPAM violation by itself.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 61 links were on tracking domains and were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show final destination URLs or UTM parameters. No mismatches were confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the 13 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` dynamic images. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the `rlcdn.gif` beacons, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `text-size-adjust:none` where it isn't needed.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `meta charset` and `format-detection` tags, delete the dead `max-width:600px` rule and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message at the origin, not through the relay, and capture `Authentication-Results` there as well.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, physical address, token rendering and UTM parameters. Resolve the tracking redirects to audit the final URLs.
6. Ask why the same `rlcdn` beacon is fired 5 times and confirm it is intentional.
