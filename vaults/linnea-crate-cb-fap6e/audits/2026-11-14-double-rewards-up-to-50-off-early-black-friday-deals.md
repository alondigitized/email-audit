# Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a full-funnel Early Black Friday send from Crate & Barrel Kids, and it's trying to do too much. The top is solid. The red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner and the "early black friday is here! up to 50% off" hero give a clear offer and a clear "Shop the Sale" button. After that it sprawls: category tiles, a second Double Rewards banner, a clearance block, a gifts block, a sweepstakes, a product grid, a credit card ad, an app ad and a store-locator, all above a wall of legal copy.
- The render is downscaled (680x8351 shown at 163x2000), so I can judge structure and hierarchy but not fine print or exact prices. Even at that size the hero headline looks low-contrast over the dark nursery photo. The "up to 60% off clearance" overlay on the autumn-leaf image has the same problem.
- Compared with the 11/14 "custom furniture look in 5 weeks" send, this is the less focused of the two. The 11/13 "Early Black Friday deals are on!" email already ran the same Double Rewards + 30% off story, so this is the second or third pass in two days. It's a competent promo, but it reads as a catalog dump.

## What's working

- The red Double Rewards banner at the top is high-contrast and states a deadline (Nov. 17).
- The hero names a specific discount, "up to 50% off", with a visible "Shop the Sale" button.
- The 2x2 tile grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs) gives clear category entry points with a percent off on each.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real products with prices, which helps shoppers who scroll.
- The seasonal framing (Early Black Friday, holiday gifts, wish lists) fits the date.

## What's weak

- Hero and clearance headlines sit on busy photos and look hard to read at this size.
- The Double Rewards message appears twice, and the card promo appears again lower down. This repetition dilutes the offer.
- There are too many modules (sweepstakes, DesignDesk, credit card, app download, store finder) and too many competing CTAs. The sale-intent shopper gets lost.
- The offers are all "up to" and vary by category (50%, 25%, 60%), so there's no single clean number.
- A dense block of legal text closes the email, and it's visually heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer story: Double Rewards + up to 50% off. Remove the sweepstakes, app promo, credit-card ad and store finder, or move them to a separate send.
- 2. Put the hero headline on a solid or gradient band rather than over the photo, so the offer reads instantly.
- 3. State the Double Rewards deadline once, near the top, and drop the duplicate banner.
- 4. Use one anchor hero product with price and review stars instead of a wide mix of categories.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and make the preheader add new information (deadline, shipping, or a specific category).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + Double Rewards ends Nov. 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: up to 50% off kids' rooms`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nursery, bedding and baby gear deals, plus 20% back in Reward Dollars. Ends Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on select bedding. Shop the best deals before they're gone.`
