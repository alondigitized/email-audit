# The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY! Shop up to 50% off + earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a big, well-built event send. A red hero reads "The Fall Sales Event up to 50% off" with a single "Shop Now" button. It leads with the offer and the 2x Rewards hook, and the render shows no broken images or overlaps. The module below the hero is the strongest: in-stock furniture, with an "Earn $X Reward Dollars" figure under each sofa and table.
- The body then stops being a sale email and becomes a catalog. After the sale content come a TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, a Thanksgiving hosting guide, a Gift Shop banner, a glassware grid, a credit card promo, an app download and a second TheDesignDesk block. That is 8 or more competing CTAs below one clear offer, and the eye has nowhere to settle after the hero. The fine print is also long and dense.
- Judgement: a solid 6-7 event email. The offer is real and the urgency is credible, but the brand has already sent four emails today (11-04), and this one dilutes its own message by trying to be everything.
- Note: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can't read small copy reliably. I'm basing this on layout and the legible headlines.

## What's working

- The hero puts "up to 50% off" and a single "Shop Now" button first, on a bold red background.
- The 2x Rewards hook is visible near the top ("last Double Rewards this year!") with a stated end date.
- The "in-stock furniture by next week & earn 2x Rewards" module pairs a delivery promise with loyalty value, which is a useful differentiator.
- The glassware grid shows prices, so the "we love these for you" products have concrete detail.
- The brand look is consistent, and nothing is visibly broken in the render.

## What's weak

- Roughly 8 distinct CTAs and campaigns compete: the sweepstakes, Thanksgiving, Gift Shop, credit card, app and a repeated TheDesignDesk. Only the first one matters for this send.
- TheDesignDesk appears twice, and its $5000 sweepstakes pulls attention from the sale.
- The sale tiles ("Host & Toast," "Last Double Rewards this Year!," "guest ready," "clearance") repeat the "up to X%" line without making any single product the hero.
- The legal text is long and dense at the bottom and mid-email. The "up to" discount and the rewards terms are buried in it.
- The email is very long, about 8,400px tall. The sale content is only the top third.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the sale. Keep the hero, the 2x Rewards strip, the in-stock furniture module and one product grid. Move the sweepstakes, Gift Shop, credit card and app blocks to other sends.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or end date to the hero. The Nov. 17 Rewards date is easy to miss.
- 3. Put the 2x Rewards offer in the hero itself, not in a separate banner above it.
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with a specific verb and noun, like "Shop Fall Sale Furniture."
- 5. Show before/after or sale prices on the hero products, so "up to 50% off" is concrete.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off furniture & décor, starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-stock sofas, tables & rugs arrive by next week. Earn 2x Rewards through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year: earn on every sale piece.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout, with mobile CSS at a 460px breakpoint and `lang="en"`. About 28 images flagged by QA have no alt attribute, and the markup has some leftover template cruft. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, because the relay likely stripped the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so anything past the `<style>` block is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **67 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5` carry the same 64-hex identifier, which looks like a hashed recipient ID. They add five third-party requests and pass an identifier to a third-party domain.
- **Movable Ink and dv-domain pixels.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0c78760c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party-domain tracking pixels carrying per-recipient parameters.
- **Not confirmed:** no broken links were found, but none could be tested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 28 images, which is the main confirmed defect.**
  - ~14 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`, for example `6100bbef-…jpg`, `7c2caaea-…jpg` and `70061691-….gif`.
  - 1 Scene7 image: `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary…`.
  - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which look like dynamic recommendation content.
  - The `448796.gif` and `o.gif` pixels are tracking pixels. They should still carry `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions.
  - Content images without alt text appear as blanks with images off, and screen readers read out the filename or URL.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set on `*` and `body`, and `*{line-height:100%}` is applied globally. Both hurt text scaling.
- **Duplicate and conflicting `format-detection` metas.** There are three: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, then `address=no` and `telephone=no`. There is also a redundant `<meta charset>` alongside `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under an XHTML doctype.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
- **Empty rule.** The MSO conditional style `td,th,div,p,a { }` is empty.
- **Global `p` rule.** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` applies to every paragraph and may cause unintended centering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unrendered merge tokens** were found in the visible source or the QA data.
- **Per-recipient identifiers** appear in image URLs (`mi_u=dbe8da71…`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). The hash is stable across the Movable Ink and LiveRamp URLs, so one identifier is shared across vendors.
- **Not verified:** the source is truncated, so body-level personalization can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- These are probably capture limitations rather than sender defects. They need raw-header confirmation before anyone treats them as real. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a dedicated mail subdomain, which is normal practice.
- **Not verified:** the footer unsubscribe link and postal address fall in the truncated portion of the HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM or landing-page data was available.** All 67 destinations sit behind redirect domains that were not probed, so UTM presence, redirect targets and sale-page alignment can't be assessed. No issues are asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels, including the five `rlcdn.com` pixels.
2. Collapse the five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls to one, or confirm the `n=1..5` variants are intentional.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. Clean up the head: merge the `format-detection` metas into one, drop the duplicate charset meta, escape `&` in the title, and delete the placeholder comment and empty CSS rule.
5. Pull raw headers from the original MX delivery, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 67 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to check final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
7. Fix the QA summary. It reports "0 issue(s), 3 warning(s)" while listing about 30 warnings, and it appears to count only the compliance and deliverability categories. Alt-text failures should be counted.
