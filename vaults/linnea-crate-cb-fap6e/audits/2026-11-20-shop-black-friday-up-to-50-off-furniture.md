## What's working

- The subject names one category and one offer ("up to 50% off furniture"), which is concrete and short.
- The hero has a visible "Shop Now" button and a free-shipping callout above the fold.
- The furniture section has a dedicated "Shop Furniture Sale" button, plus a "save on this bestseller" tile with a sale and regular price, which is a real product anchor.
- The photography is consistently on-brand, with holiday styling that fits the season.
- No broken images or layout glitches are visible in the render.

## What's weak

- The hero is generic Black Friday, not furniture, so it doesn't match the subject.
- The scroll is very long, with kitchen, decor, clearance, a sweepstakes and an app promo diluting the furniture message.
- Several sections each carry their own competing "up to X% off" tier (50%, 40%, 30%), so there's no single dominant offer.
- Ninth Black Friday send this week, and it repeats the 11/20 "Shop up to 50% off" omnibus creative nearly word for word.
- The $5000 design sweepstakes and app-download blocks are off-message for a furniture buyer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: a sectional or dining-set photo with "Up to 50% off furniture" as the headline, and drop the generic Black Friday banner.
- 2. Cut the email to the furniture story plus one bestseller block. Move kitchen, decor, clearance and the sweepstakes to other sends.
- 3. Use one featured offer with a real deadline, such as the delivery cutoff, instead of stacking tiers.
- 4. Add social proof (star ratings or review counts) on the bestseller tile.
- 5. Stagger the cadence. Skip a send, or segment so furniture browsers get this one and everyone else gets kitchen.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with a mobile media query and Outlook conditional comments. Its main verifiable defect is about 54 images with no `alt` attribute. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the relay appears to strip headers, and the truncated HTML hides the footer and link targets.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified.** 98 links were skipped as click-tracking redirects, so none were probed. Redirect resolution, broken links and link-to-landing-page parity are unconfirmed.
- **Five duplicate LiveRamp pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `&n=5` carry the same recipient hash `s=`. They are third-party tracking calls with no `alt`.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=dbe8da71…` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` carry `mi_u`. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e7e509df-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is a further tracking image. This is expected for dynamic-content and open tracking, but it means rendering depends on the Movable Ink hosts being reachable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text on about 54 images (the main finding).**
  - About 40 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, probably dynamic product images.
  - If these are the sale and product imagery, blocked-image and screen-reader users get no content, which is the likely cause of the 57% pass rate. The tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`) should carry `alt=""`, but the check flags them anyway.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on some clients.
- **Text-size override.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `*{}` rule and in `html{}` and `body{}`, which stops iOS from scaling text.
- **Invalid XHTML.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype and should be `&amp;`. The title also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Redundant markup.**
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type` tags.
  - Four `format-detection` metas, two of which duplicate the first.
  - Overlapping `img{max-width}` rules at `min-width:768px` and `min-width:640px`. The later 640px rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Positive.** `lang="en"` is set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible portion. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only that portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may be dropping them. This is unconfirmed, but if the headers are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay rather than the sender.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible HTML does not include the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, so I can't confirm either.
- **Sender domain.** The From address is on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain consistent with the image hosts. Alignment is not verifiable from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be checked. Any claim here would be speculation.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`). This is the quickest fix and would raise the 57% pass rate.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, make the title match the campaign, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the duplicate meta tags.
4. Cut the five identical `rlcdn` pixels to one, if each isn't needed for a separate purpose.
5. On our side, capture the raw `.eml` or the original headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` instead of inferring them from the relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirected links in the QA pipeline, following the redirects to the final URL. That would let us check UTMs and landing-page parity for the 98 skipped links.
7. Run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to confirm the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and merge-token checks.
