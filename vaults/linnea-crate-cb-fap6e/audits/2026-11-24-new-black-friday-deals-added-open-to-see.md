# New Black Friday deals added! Open to see…
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids' fifth or sixth Black Friday catch-all in three days. It follows the same template as the 11/23 "It's on! Black Friday deals & free shipping inside" send. The hero is "new Black Friday deals added!" over a gift-wrapping photo with a single "Shop Now" button. A "free shipping is on" banner sits above it. "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" follows in text, then a text-link grid of ten categories.
- The hook is "new deals added," but nothing in the body shows what is new. The same hook ran in the 11/24 main-brand send ("NEW DEALS just added") and the 11/22 send ("NEW ITEMS hit our Black Friday sale"). A subscriber who has seen those will see this as a recycled urgency claim.
- The body then drifts away from the sale. It has a gift-shop block, a travel baby-gear block, four products (two strollers at $1,350–$1,450 and a $1,499 dresser), a credit card promo, an app promo, a design-desk promo and a store locator. It reads as a long omnibus. The offer is real and the free-shipping banner is clear, but the email has no single point of focus.

## What's working

- The "free shipping is on" banner is the first thing seen and states the offer clearly, with the $79+ threshold.
- The hero headline and "Shop Now" button are large and readable on the photo.
- The two-column category link grid (Toy Sale, Furniture Sale, Baby Gear Sale) is quick to scan and lets a parent jump to their need.
- The "we love these for you" block shows real products with prices. The Busy Baby Activity Chair shows a strikethrough from $159.00 to $129.95, and the Babyletto recliner shows $999.00 to $799.20.
- The brand look is clean and consistent, and no render bugs are visible.

## What's weak

- "New deals added" is not backed by any visible new product or deal. The hero image is a generic wrapped-gift photo.
- The hero button says "Shop Now," a vague CTA that doesn't name a category or product.
- The email is far too long. After the sale grid there are about eight more modules, and the credit card, app, design desk and store locator blocks dilute the Black Friday message.
- Only two of the four featured products show a sale price. The strollers at $1,350–$1,450 and the $1,499 dresser show no discount, so the "up to 50% off" claim isn't demonstrated.
- The sender name "Crate & Barrel Kids BLACK FRIDAY" is all caps. The subject ends with a truncated "Open to see…", which reads as a gimmick.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero show the actual new deals. Use a three-product row with real percentage badges (for example, "Toy sale: up to 30% off") instead of a lifestyle gift photo.
- 2. Cut the card, app, design desk and store locator modules from the middle of the email. Keep one secondary block and move the rest to the footer.
- 3. Change the CTA to a specific one, such as "Shop toy deals" or "Shop 50% off furniture."
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm," and a "Get it by Thanksgiving" shipping cutoff. The 11/24 main-brand send used one, and it is the only credible urgency lever.
- 5. Show sale prices on every featured product, or swap in the items that actually have a discount.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: 50% off kids' furniture + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on toys, nursery and baby gear. Free shipping on $79+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Thursday for delivery before Thanksgiving. Ends Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template with a valid `lang="en"` and a responsive `<style>` block. The QA run flagged missing alt text on about 30 images, five duplicate third-party pixels, and several template-hygiene problems. It also could not verify the unsubscribe headers or authentication, because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source is truncated, so everything below the `<head>` is unchecked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 73 links on tracking and click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their destinations were validated.
- **Repeated third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` through `&n=5`. The same pixel fires five times, with only the `n` value changing.
- **Recipient-derived ID sent to a third party:** the `s=` value in the `rlcdn.com` (LiveRamp) pixels starts with the same hex string as the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. This looks like a recipient identifier, probably a hash, being passed to a third party. The truncated HTML can't confirm what it is, so confirm it against the full source.
- **Repeated first-party pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. There is also an open pixel at `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and a pixel at `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/87bec1ef-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. That is three or more tracking beacons plus the five LiveRamp ones, which adds request weight and gives several chances for images to be blocked.
- **Stale asset:** `…/10%5FKids/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide/…SecondaryStory.gif` is an October creative inside a 2026-11-24 send. It may be intentional, but the asset path doesn't match the campaign.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 30 `<img>` elements have no alt attribute. The affected sources include:
  - the content images `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` (about 16 of them);
  - the animated GIFs `2026%5F1124%5FCBK%5FBF%5FNewDealsAdded%5F02.gif` and `91ea068d-…gif`.
  
  If these images are blocked, the content and links they carry disappear. A GIF that carries the offer message, such as `NewDealsAdded_02.gif`, needs meaningful alt text.
- **Decorative pixels:** the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`) should have `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The QA tool counts their missing alt as a warning, but it's a low-severity one.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` stops users from zooming, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the global rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent text resizing in WebKit.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** there are three `format-detection` tags, and both `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>` are declared. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Template leftovers:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` block were left in the production source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The body is truncated, so this is not fully verified.
- The `mi_u`, `mi_mid` and `mi_cid` parameters are per-recipient and per-campaign identifiers, and they are populated. That is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** QA could not find it. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed and not a verified failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** also not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be verified. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body checks:** the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be checked against CAN-SPAM.
- **LiveRamp pixel:** passing a recipient-derived identifier to a third-party pixel (see section 2) may need to be covered in the privacy disclosure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The 73 skipped tracking links mean no UTM parameters or landing destinations were verified, so continuity can't be assessed. The sampled image URLs use `mi_*` parameters rather than `utm_*`, which suggests attribution runs through the redirector.
- Only a full-body redirect-chain resolution can check that the Black Friday links land on matching pages. No evidence of a mismatch was found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to every content image. Put the full offer text in the alt for the GIFs that carry it. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the ampersand in `<title>`, de-duplicate the meta tags, and strip the template comments, including the `IMPUT` placeholder.
4. Drop the duplicate pixels: 5× `rlcdn` and 6× `f3c0d22992a01946.png`. One of each is enough.
5. Confirm the `rlcdn` `s=` value isn't a plain email hash, and that it's disclosed if it is.
6. Re-run QA against the raw origin headers, bypassing the relay. That should confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
7. Resolve the 73 redirect links to check the destinations and UTM parameters. Also verify that the October `GiftGuide` GIF is intended in this send.
