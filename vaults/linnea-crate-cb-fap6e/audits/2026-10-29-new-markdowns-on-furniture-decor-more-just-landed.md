# New markdowns on furniture, decor & more just landed 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance-led send that tries to be five emails at once. The hero says "ends soon! the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off" and then, in the same block, "New Markdowns Added! up to 60% off clearance." Two offers with two different percentages sit in one hero, and a "Shop Now" button sits under them. The 60% clearance number does the real work and matches the subject. The 30% line above it competes with it, and the dark, muted background makes the pairing feel murky.
- Below the hero, the email drifts. A six-link category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tablescape & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) gives decent navigation. Then comes a JURA E8 espresso machine feature, a second kitchen-appliance block, a "we love these for you" product grid, the credit card, the app, TheDesignDesk, the store locator, and social. The subject promises furniture and decor markdowns, but the espresso machine takes up the largest image space after the hero. The last stretch below the products reads like a standard footer stack.
- Compared with the 10/28 sends ("Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event!" and "TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!"), this one is the third or fourth sale message in two days. It adds a bigger number, 60%, but with no new urgency beyond "ends soon!" and "while quantities last." Verdict: a decent clearance hook, diluted by an off-topic coffee feature and too many modules.

## What's working

- "up to 60% off clearance" is a big, specific number and delivers on the subject's "new markdowns."
- The six-category link grid right under the hero lets a shopper jump straight to furniture or decor.
- The "Shop All Clearance" button gives a clear primary action.
- The product grid shows real prices, including markdowns with strike-through (e.g. the dining table and dining chair).
- The sender and branding are consistent and easy to recognize, with the Crate & Barrel logo at the top.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks "up to 30% off" (the Thanksgiving dining room event) on top of "up to 60% off" (clearance). Two offers dilute each other.
- The hero is low-contrast: pale text over a dark, murky leaf photo with no product in view. Nothing in it shows furniture or decor.
- The espresso machine feature and the "beautiful bestsellers" block are full-width and sit above the actual markdown products. They're off-subject and push furniture below the fold.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows full prices on most items. Only a couple show a visible markdown, so it doesn't prove the "new markdowns" claim.
- Below the products come the credit card, app, design service, store finder, and social. That's too many closing asks for one send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one offer: "up to 60% off clearance, new markdowns added." Remove the 30% Thanksgiving line, or put it in a thin banner. Swap the foliage image for a furniture or decor photo.
- 2. Move the product grid directly under the category links, and show only items that are actually marked down, with was/now prices and the percent saved.
- 3. Delete the JURA espresso block, or send it as its own email. It's off the subject's promise.
- 4. Move the app, credit card, and DesignDesk modules into one slim row, or drop two of them.
- 5. Make the CTA more specific: "Shop Clearance Furniture" instead of a generic "Shop Now".

- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance furniture, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, tables, rugs and decor just dropped. Select styles, while quantities last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML skeleton is sound: it has a `lang="en"` attribute, a table layout, and an MSO conditional fallback. The main technical gaps are about 29 `<img>` tags with no `alt`, a viewport that blocks zoom, and header-level compliance and authentication checks that the QA run couldn't verify. The source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 4xx/5xx status are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) are embedded. Only the `n` parameter differs, so the repeats add requests without adding data.
- **Same identifier shared with a third party:** The `s=` value begins `dbe8da718e2997cc…`, the same prefix as the Movable Ink `mi_u=` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. This appears to be one recipient-level identifier going to both the first-party Movable Ink host and the third-party `rlcdn.com` host. It is worth confirming this is intended and disclosed.
- **Open pixel:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is present (open tracking via Movable Ink).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (29 flagged images).** Content images are affected, and none are labelled:
  - 15 hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `578aa77d-…jpg`).
  - 7 Movable Ink dynamic images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - 1 dynamic image at `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1ab03f69-…`.
  - The 5 `rlcdn.com` pixels and `o.gif` are tracking pixels, so an empty `alt=""` is acceptable there. The QA tool can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** The universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` turns off iOS text-size adjustment for every element.
- **Markup validity:**
  - The `<title>` is `Crate & Barrel` with a raw `&`, which should be `&amp;` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are declared.
  - `format-detection` is declared several times with conflicting content (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`).
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in the production source.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` is locked to `light`, so there is no dark-mode adaptation. This is a deliberate choice, not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.
- The dynamic modules (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) rendered with `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters. Their rendered content and fallback behaviour can't be assessed from this data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **WARN, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify against the raw original message.
- **WARN, `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The visible alignment is good: the From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the images are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not visible:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. All 61 click-tracking URLs were redirect domains and weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 `image.mail…` content images, and give the Movable Ink dynamic images (`mi…/p/rp/*`, `dv…/o/*`) an alt template. Set `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rule from `*`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, deduplicate the charset and `format-detection` metas, and strip the template placeholder comment.
4. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, and confirm that sharing the recipient hash with a third party is intentional.
5. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Follow the redirects with a resolver that supports the tracking domains to check final URLs and UTM parameters.
