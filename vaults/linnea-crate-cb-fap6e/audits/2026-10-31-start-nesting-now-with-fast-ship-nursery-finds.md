# Start nesting now with fast-ship nursery finds!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, retailer-standard nursery send. It leads with a soft, aspirational crib-room hero ("Delivers as fast as 7 days / get their nursery ready now"). A 2x4 category grid follows, then a Doona car-seat feature, a holiday block, a clearance block, product tiles and an app and Design Desk footer. The subject's "fast-ship" promise is carried by the hero kicker and by "Ships free*" tags on some tiles. The brand is trusted, the layout is clean, and nothing looks broken.
- It's also too long and too diffuse for a message whose subject promises one thing. The "up to 60% off" clearance banner is buried near the middle, and the "$500" app prize sits near the bottom. The body mixes nesting, holiday decor, loyalty card, app and Design Desk in one scroll. After the Oct 30 sends (Venus Williams collab, birthday gifts, Keane anniversary) and the Oct 31 dining-room last-call, it reads like another everything-email.
- Judgement: a solid 6. It's well made but not targeted, and the fast-ship hook never becomes a real offer.

## What's working

- The hero ties directly to the subject: "Delivers as fast as 7 days" is the kicker over a clear Shop Now button.
- The category grid (chairs, cribs, bedding, dressers, changing tables, blankets, car seats, strollers) is easy to scan, and the "Ships free*" labels sit right on the tiles.
- The Doona car-seat and stroller feature gives a concrete high-consideration product a spotlight.
- The product tiles show real names and prices, which gives the click something specific to land on.
- The Design Desk and "find a store" blocks add service value in a familiar brand voice.

## What's weak

- No headline offer. Fast ship and free shipping are the only hooks, and the one real discount ("100s of new markdowns up to 60% off") is a mid-email banner.
- Too many jobs in one email: holiday decor, a credit card promo, the app, the Design Desk, clearance and nursery. The nursery theme gets diluted.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now" or "Learn More", and the only product-specific CTAs sit in the tile grid.
- The "Ships free*" asterisks and the dense legal block at the bottom make the shipping promise feel qualified.
- The length is punishing on mobile. The key stroller and car-seat content sits deep in the scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one concrete offer, such as free shipping with a dated cutoff, or move the markdown banner into the hero. A "fast-ship" subject needs a number.
- 2. Cut the email to the nursery story: hero, category grid, Doona feature, product tiles. Move holiday, the credit card and the app to a separate send.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs such as "Shop ready-to-ship cribs" and "Shop strollers."
- 4. Add social proof, such as ratings or a "top-rated" badge, on the product tiles.
- 5. State the shipping terms in the hero ("Free shipping on nursery orders $X+ — ships in 7 days") so the asterisks aren't needed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nursery ready in 7 days or less`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cribs, chairs & car seats that ship fast`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on cribs, bedding and dressers — plus up to 60% off clearance.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doona car seat, rockers and more, delivered in as little as 7 days.`

- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8&n=1..5` beacons (LiveRamp) are present.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** About 16 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `50378ef5-…jpg`, `7efc990d-…jpg`, `57ed4bf7-…jpg`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. With images blocked or a screen reader, these are invisible or announced by filename. The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels missing alt.** The `rlcdn`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly. They are low impact but are inflating the warning count.

- **Text scaling disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent iOS text resizing. They also conflict with the earlier `100%` declarations in the same `<style>` blocks.
- **Markup validity.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type declaration.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - The global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` forces centered text on every paragraph.
