# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Our 15 top gifts for everyone on your list →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a Movable Ink/ESP-style XHTML template with VML/MSO fallbacks. It has widespread missing image alt text (QA pass rate 57%), several template and CSS defects, and third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **106 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains). Destination validity, redirect chains and link-to-image matching are **unverified**.
- **Duplicate third-party pixels:** the same LiveRamp pixel fires five times, differing only in `n=1…5`. Example: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1`. Each one is an extra third-party request.
- **Identifier passed to a third party:** the `s=` value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`) shares its prefix with the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. This looks like the same 64-hex subscriber identifier going to `rlcdn.com`. I can't confirm it is a hashed email, but it should be covered in the privacy disclosures.
- **Tracking and personalization pixels** (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` ×7, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8bca5247-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`) are visible in the source. The `p/rp/` pixels have no file extension variation and no alt text, so they may be mistaken for content images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on roughly 60 images**, per the QA list. This includes the hero (`…2026_1104_CB_GiftGuide_hero_V2-ezgif.c…`, apparently an animated GIF) and the product images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`. These images are likely linked product tiles, so with images blocked or a screen reader the links have no accessible name. Two `.gif` files (`407078dc-…`, `c65ab55e-…`) are also missing alt text. The QA tool can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`. Pure tracking pixels should have `alt=""`.
- **Text-resize blocking:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` setting. This disables iOS text scaling for the whole document.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on clients that honor it.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at ≥768px. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces center alignment on every paragraph unless it is overridden inline.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype should be `&amp;`.
- **Leftover template placeholder** in the head: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times, and both `http-equiv Content-Type` and `meta charset` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated HTML. The per-recipient `mi_u` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `[WARN]` **List-Unsubscribe header not found.**
- `[WARN]` **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.**
- `[WARN]` **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.

The QA tool attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. I can't confirm they are actually missing from the sent message. For a bulk sender, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo, so this should be verified on a direct-delivery copy. The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were cut off by truncation and are unverified. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is consistent with the image and tracking subdomains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 106 links go through click-tracking redirects and were not probed, so UTM parameters and final landing pages could not be checked. No UTM parameters appear in the visible image URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every product and hero image, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.`).
2. Remove the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` override and `maximum-scale=1`.
3. Reduce the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels to one, and confirm the shared identifier is covered in the privacy notice.
4. Fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries. Escape `&` in `<title>`, and remove the placeholder comment and duplicate meta tags.
5. Re-run the header checks against a direct-delivery copy to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve a sample of the click-tracking links to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
