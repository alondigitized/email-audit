# Write a review. Enter for a chance to win $1,000. Find out how →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate&Kids' third kids/nursery-adjacent send logged this week and lands the same day as "New, new & more new nursery picks →" (2026-08-04, 8.00/10) — two emails from the same brand on the same day is aggressive cadence, and this one is the weaker of the pair. The subject promises a focused ask ("write a review, win $1,000") but the render buries that ask under back-to-school 20% off, fall arrivals, a Nuna car seat push, a 50% nursery sale, a six-item product grid, a credit card pitch, an app-download plug, and a design-service CTA — nine distinct asks competing for one click.
- The hero itself is clean and well-built: a clear headline, a named product (Beatrix bed), two legible buttons. The problem is everything below it. A subscriber who opens for the contest gets a wall of unrelated shopping modules instead, and ironically, an email about writing reviews shows zero actual customer reviews or star ratings anywhere in the body.
- Verdict: solid top-of-funnel design dragged down by scope creep and same-day cadence overlap with the brand's own nursery send.

## What's working

- Hero is single-focus and legible: bold headline, named product (Beatrix Dyed Raksam Green Wood Kids Twin Bed), two clearly labeled buttons ("Write a Review," "Shop Bestsellers").
- No visible render bugs — clean image loads, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the long scroll.
- Seasonally coherent for early August (back-to-school banner, fall arrivals, tail-end summer sale) without stale leftover promo language.
- Consistent brand styling (typography, color palette, spacing) end to end, which reads as trustworthy.

## What's weak

- Subject (~64 characters) never mentions nursery/kids furniture — the brand's core hook — and is too long for mobile truncation.
- Nine separate CTAs (Write a Review, Shop Bestsellers, Shop Now, Shop the Kids, Shop all Nuna, Shop the Sale, Learn More, Download Now, Book a Free Appointment) bury the one action the subject actually promised.
- Zero social proof — no star ratings or review snippets anywhere, which is a strange miss for an email whose entire premise is "write a review."
- Same-day duplication with "New, new & more new nursery picks →" (also 2026-08-04) risks inbox fatigue from this exact sender.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut scope: keep the contest hero + one shopping module (bestsellers or fall arrivals), move Nuna, nursery sale, credit card, app download, and design desk into a separate send — this single change would do the most to raise open-to-click conversion.
- 2. Shorten and sharpen the subject so it's under 50 characters and ties the contest to the category.
- 3. Add a real preheader that complements rather than repeats the subject.
- 4. Insert 2-3 actual review snippets/star ratings near the "Write a Review" CTA to make the ask feel social rather than purely transactional.
- 5. Space this out from the same-day nursery-picks send — stagger by at least a day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rate your new nursery pick, win $1,000 →`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your review = a shot at $1,000 (2 min)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tell us what you think — contest ends 8/31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: fall arrivals from $29 while you're here.`
