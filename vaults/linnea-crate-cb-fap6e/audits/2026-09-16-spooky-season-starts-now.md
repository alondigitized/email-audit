# Spooky season starts NOW 👻→
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids opens its Halloween shop with a full, well-organized catalog email — trick-or-treat baskets, costumes, decor, bedding — and it's a genuine seasonal kickoff, not a repeat of the Venus Williams collab churn from the last few days. The "now open: the halloween shop" hero with the string-lit spider-web illustration is a strong, on-brand seasonal hook, and the categorized sections (baskets → costumes → decor → bedding) give the email real structure instead of an undifferentiated scroll.
- Where it falls apart is scope creep: by the midpoint the email pivots into a Crate & Barrel cross-sell ("everything you need for spooky season"), then into unrelated bedroom/lion-plush/living-room inspo content, then a credit card pitch and app download blurb. What starts as a focused kids' Halloween launch turns into the brand's usual kitchen-sink send, diluting the one thing that would have made this a great email — a tight, single-purpose Halloween shop reveal.
- Solid seasonal timing (mid-September for a Halloween shop launch is right on schedule) and clean visual execution keep this in the same range as the brand's better recent sends, but the unrelated back-half content caps it below the 8/10 "fall baking" email from yesterday.

## What's working

- Clear, on-theme hero illustration (glowing pumpkin, spiderweb string lights) that immediately signals Halloween without being generic.
- Logical category breakdown — baskets, costumes, decor, bedding — each with its own subhead and product grid, easier to scan than a flat list.
- Personalization badges ("Personalize It") on trick-or-treat baskets are a genuine differentiator for a kids' brand and a real reason to click.
- "In stock now: bestselling Jellycat!" callout adds urgency/scarcity tied to a recognizable, desirable brand.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text, grid alignment is consistent throughout.

## What's weak

- The email drifts into a full Crate & Barrel (adult) cross-promo and unrelated bedroom/living-room "shop the look" content that has nothing to do with kids' Halloween — this is scope creep that dilutes the Crate & Kids-specific offer.
- No visible discount, % off, or promo code anywhere — every CTA is just "Shop All," which is a missed opportunity given the brand has run outright offers in other recent sends.
- Credit card pitch ("new: the card you just got better") and app-download blurb tacked on at the bottom feel like unrelated filler bolted onto a seasonal launch email.
- Very long single-scroll layout with 6+ distinct sections risks losing mobile readers well before the bedding/inspo content near the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email at "boo-tiful bedding & more" — drop the Crate & Barrel cross-sell, bedroom inspo, credit card pitch, and app blurb into a separate send. A focused kids' Halloween launch converts better than a diluted catalog scroll.
- 2. Add a concrete offer (e.g., "20% off Halloween shop" or "free shipping $75+") to at least one CTA — right now nothing distinguishes this from a plain catalog browse.
- 3. Move the "Personalize It" baskets higher or call them out in the subject line — personalization is the strongest, most differentiated hook in this email and it's buried in section two.
- 4. Tighten the CTA copy — "Shop All Halloween" repeats five times; vary it per section ("Shop Costumes," "Shop Decor") to reinforce what's actually being clicked.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Halloween Shop is open 🎃 personalized baskets inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: scary-cute costumes + trick-or-treat baskets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Personalized baskets, costumes for every kid, and decor that doesn't scream "haunted house"`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Jellycat's back in stock — plus everything for trick-or-treat night`
