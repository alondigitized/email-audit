# Pinning down your vibe for the holidays? Try these looks →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's "holiday looks" send, and it's one of the better-structured emails in a week of near-identical Black Friday blasts. Five numbered style stories run down the page: modern heritage, timeless evergreen, classic cheer, frosted sage and winter harvest. Each has a big lifestyle photo, a two-up product crop and a "Shop the Look" button. The rhythm is clear and the photography is strong. It is more editorial than the "Santa's fave deal" toys send or the "top 10" list from earlier today.
- The weak point is the top. A red Black Friday banner ("Up to 50% off + free shipping", "Shop Now") sits above the styling story, so two ideas compete for the hero. The subject promises vibe-finding, but the first thing the eye lands on is a discount. After the five looks the email keeps going: a TheDesignDesk appointment block, a kitchen deals module, a personalized gifts banner and another "Shop up to 50% off Black Friday deals" tile. That is a 12,000px scroll, and by the end the styling story has turned into a generic promo catalog.
- Verdict: strong creative concept, diluted by a bolted-on Black Friday omnibus tail. It is a good send that could have been a great one if it had committed to the looks.

## What's working

- The five numbered looks give the scroll a clear cadence and let a reader self-select by style.
- The photography is rich and seasonal, with trees, mantels, garlands and stockings that feel current for late November.
- Each look has its own "Shop the Look" CTA, so the click goes to a themed collection rather than the homepage.
- The subject matches the body. The "looks" framing is carried through the numbered sections.
- The TheDesignDesk block offers a free design appointment, a useful service angle.

## What's weak

- The red Black Friday banner at the top competes with the "5 holiday-ready styles" hero and muddies the message.
- The offer is generic ("up to 50% off + free shipping") and isn't tied to any of the five looks, so there are no prices on the styled products.
- The email runs very long. The kitchen deals, personalized gifts and second BF banner at the bottom repeat the omnibus sends from earlier this week.
- All five "Shop the Look" buttons look identical and small, with no product names or prices, so each look says little about what you'd actually buy.
- The body copy under each look is small, light text that is hard to read at inbox size.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the looks and move the Black Friday offer into a slim top bar, or tie it to the looks ("Shop the look, up to 50% off"). One idea per hero.
- 2. Cut everything after look #5 except one closing CTA, or move the design service and kitchen deals into a separate send. This email would be stronger at about half the length.
- 3. Add 2-3 named hero products with prices to each look, e.g. "Faux Fir Tree, from $149".
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific: "Shop Frosted Sage" instead of the repeated "Shop the Look".
- 5. Add a deadline for the offer ("Ends Monday") to create credible urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Modern heritage to winter harvest, plus free shipping on Black Friday deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your style, then shop trees, garlands and table settings up to 50% off.`
