# TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the strongest part: "only in the app: 20% off mirrors, today only!" sits over a clear mirror photo, with a "Download Our App" button below it. The urgency is credible and the offer is specific. The catch is that the CTA asks for an app download, not a mirror purchase, so the click path is longer than the subject suggests.
- Below the hero the email turns into the usual Crate & Barrel catalog. There is an unrelated rug and accent-chairs module at "up to 30% off", a Halloween module ("so scary, it's chic"), a product grid of candles and vases, a credit-card promo, a second app pitch and a DesignDesk block. The mirror offer appears only in the hero. None of the modules after it show a mirror.
- Verdict: a good subject and hero held back by a diluted body and an app-gated CTA. Compared with the 10/27 "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on toys $99+" send, which kept its message consistent from subject to button, this one loses focus. It is another 6-ish send.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match word for word: "20% off mirrors" with "today only" appears in both.
- The mirror photo is clean and clearly on-category.
- The offer is one concrete percentage, and the deadline is credible.
- The brand logo is prominent and the brown-and-cream palette is consistent.
- The product grid shows names and prices, so it is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The only hero CTA is "Download Our App". It says nothing about mirrors, and it adds friction for anyone who isn't already in the app.
- The body drifts to rugs and chairs at "up to 30% off", Halloween, candles and vases, a credit card, and DesignDesk. The mirror deal is not reinforced anywhere below the hero.
- The Halloween module's headline and text sit over a busy image. "so scary, it's chic" and the small copy are hard to read.
- The app is pitched twice (the hero and "It's all on the app, including your chance at $500"). The repeat dilutes the message.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows no mirrors, and the products shown have no obvious link to the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero button with "Shop 20% Off Mirrors", and put the app as a secondary line such as "Use the app for the code". A mirror CTA is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Fill the second module with 3-4 specific mirrors showing the sale price and the original price, instead of the rug module.
- 3. Cut or move the Halloween, credit card and DesignDesk modules. A single-offer send with a countdown would do better.
- 4. Add a visible "ends tonight" line and the app promo code, if there is one, near the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is a table-based XHTML Transitional build with several markup defects, and 12 content images have no alt text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers weren't captured. The source was truncated and 51 tracked links were not probed, so link, footer and landing-page checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** all 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Final landing URLs, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…5a8&n=1` to `n=5` requests fire per open. Each carries a 64-hex `s=` value that looks like a hashed recipient identifier. Five calls to one endpoint add load time, and each is another place the recipient identifier is exposed.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** `mi_u=dbe8da71…` appears on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` appear on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b310734f-…`. These are normal for dynamic content, but they make the images uncacheable per recipient.
- **Third-party opens pixel:** the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` open-tracking pixel is the one first-party item in this group.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (12):**
  - Five from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`: `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `4eb578f1-…jpg`, `f3246943-…gif`, `78fef553-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - Seven from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`: `560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, `5ec241ce0b852c66.png`, `00423cc9007b550c.png`, `f5ca909404423879.png`, `45e8c84f5a9185ff.png`, `18f126218cc222ec.png`.
  - I'm assuming these are content images from their hosts and paths. If any is a spacer, it should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - With images blocked, the email may render as blank space.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (7):** the five `rlcdn.com` GIFs, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b310…` are not user-facing. They should still carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed, and the `Content-Type` and `charset` metas are duplicated.
- **Conflicting `format-detection` metas:** there are four. The first sets `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, and three single-value tags follow it. Clients may take either set.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, with a typo. An empty `<!---->` and an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` MSO rule are also present.
- **Text resizing blocked:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is applied via `*{}` and `body`, which prevents user text scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Layout and dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` is set with no dark-mode fallback. That is a deliberate choice, but it is still a rendering constraint.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) were visible in the truncated source.
- The per-recipient `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values are populated, so dynamic-content tokens did resolve.
- Only part of the HTML was available, so body and footer personalization is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing [WARN]:** the QA note says the relay may not capture it, so this isn't confirmed as a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing [WARN]:** one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender and image domains, `mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, are consistent, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer not inspected:** the truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address or the visible unsubscribe link. I'm not asserting either is missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be assessed. Links go through tracking redirects, so the final landing URLs weren't resolved or probed.
- Whether the "20% off mirrors" landing page matches the offer is unverified.
- Running the audit with a link-resolving step (following redirects, with a browser UA) would close this gap.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 12 content images, listed in section 3. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*{}` and `body`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Self-close the metas and merge the duplicate `format-detection` and `charset` tags into one each.
4. Fix the `img` `max-width` media-query order so the 600px and 640px caps behave as intended.
5. Reduce the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixel calls to one if the vendor allows it. Confirm the `s=` value isn't a reversible email hash.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the 51 redirected links to check landing URLs and UTMs, and inspect the full footer.
7. Remove the template placeholder comment and the empty style rules.
