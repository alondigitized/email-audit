# New! Chandeliers & lamps for fall
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel doing a clean, single-category sweep of its lighting assortment — chandeliers, floor lamps, table lamps, sconces — organized almost like a catalog rather than a single hero offer. It's well shot (moody, aspirational room photography throughout) and easy to scan, but it buries its own headline: "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" sits in a thin band above the hero and is never reinforced with a specific price cut until the "we love these for you" grid near the bottom, where discounts (e.g., $1099→$879.20) finally show up.
- There's also a real content bug: the second hero module is captioned "New! Everton Chandelier / 8 Shade Options Available" but the image itself is overlaid with "Etienne Chandelier" — and the grid at the bottom sells an "Etienne Glass Chandelier Light 27.7" for $799, so the caption name is simply wrong for the pictured product.
- Compared to this brand's other 2026-08-05 send that scored 9.00 ("New backpacks in every size, now starting at $39!"), this one is weaker on execution focus — that email nailed one category with one price anchor; this one sprawls across six lighting subcategories with the discount detached from the products until deep in the scroll. It's a solid, on-brand catalog email — not a standout.

## What's working

- Consistent premium photography — every hero image is a styled room shot, not a product-on-white cutout, which reinforces aspirational browsing.
- Clear section architecture: pendants & chandeliers → floor lamps → table lamps → sconces, each with its own "Shop [category]" CTA, so a shopper can jump straight to what they want.
- "Pro tip" copy under floor lamps and pendants adds real styling value (hanging height, placement) rather than pure sales copy.
- The bottom product grid finally shows concrete strikethrough pricing ($1099→$879.20, $79.95→$63.96), which is the strongest purchase signal in the whole email.
- Sale banner and "new" framing align with a real seasonal launch (fall lighting refresh), not stale copy.

## What's weak

- The top-level "up to 60% off" offer is never tied to an actual chandelier/lamp price until the very bottom — most of the email shows full-price items with no visible discount.
- Product name mismatch: caption reads "New! Everton Chandelier" over an image labeled "Etienne Chandelier" (confirmed as the real product name later in the grid) — a sloppy proofing error in a hero module.
- No urgency anywhere — no end date on "The Summer Sale," so there's no reason to act today versus next week.
- Six-plus product categories in one send dilutes focus; there's no single "best deal" the eye is pushed toward.
- Generic CTA copy repeats "Shop Now" / "Shop [Category]" throughout — functional but not compelling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move real numbers into the hero: replace "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" with the actual best chandelier deal (e.g., "Chandeliers up to 60% off — from $XXX"), so the discount is concrete on open, not just implied.
- 2. Fix the "Everton"/"Etienne" naming mismatch before this ships again — small errors like this erode trust in a $800+ purchase decision.
- 3. Add a deadline ("Sale ends Sunday") near the top banner to convert browsing into urgency.
- 4. Trim to 3-4 categories per send and rotate the rest next week — this reads like two emails merged into one.
- 5. Vary CTA copy per section ("Light Up Your Entryway," "Find Your Floor Lamp") instead of repeating "Shop [Category]."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall lighting, up to 60% off — new chandeliers just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your entryway needs this chandelier (60% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `8 new shade options, plus floor & table lamps from $63.96`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Statement chandeliers, sconces & lamps — sale ends soon`
