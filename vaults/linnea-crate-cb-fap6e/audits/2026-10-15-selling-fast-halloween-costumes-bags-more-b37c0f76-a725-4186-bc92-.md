# 🎃 Selling fast: Halloween costumes, bags & more  →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal merchandising send. The preview is downscaled to about 113px wide, so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print. The hero says "selling fast! the halloween shop," and the dark, moody photography is on-season. Below it, a long scroll walks through trick-or-treat baskets, bedding, costumes and decor, each with its own "Shop All" button.
- The structure is tidy and the creative is strong, but the email is far too long. Eight or more category blocks, a clearance banner, a Jellycat band and a "we love these for you" grid all compete. The only price hook is a free-shipping strip at the top and an "up to 50% off clearance" banner that sits two-thirds of the way down. ## What's working

- The hero is on-season and branded, with a clear "Shop All Halloween" button.
- Each block has a headline, a one-line description and a product row with names and prices.
- The Halloween photography is moody and consistent, so the email feels premium for a kids' brand.
- A free-shipping banner sits at the very top.
- The "everything you need for spooky season" and "up to 50% off clearance" banners give a second chance to convert.

## What's weak

- The strongest price hook, up to 50% off clearance, is buried far below the hero.
- Every section ends in a generic "Shop All" button instead of a hero product or specific offer.
- The scroll is about 12,000px, with the Jellycat, instashop and "we love these for you" blocks diluting the Halloween focus.
- The subject says "selling fast," but nothing in the body shows stock levels or a deadline.
- The email came after three other sends the same day, which is cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "up to 50% off" clearance or a single real offer into the hero, and cut the body to four sections: baskets, costumes, bedding and decor.
- 2. Drop the Jellycat, "we love these for you" and the app-promo blocks from a Halloween send.
- 3. Back up "selling fast" with evidence, such as a "only a few left" badge on the costumes or a "ships by Oct 25" deadline.
- 4. Replace "Shop All" with specific CTAs such as "Shop costumes" and "Shop trick-or-treat baskets."
- 5. Space sends out so this doesn't land on top of three other Crate sends the same day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketing Cloud-style (MI) template with about 98 click-tracked links, hosted on three image domains and several third-party pixels. QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, plus about 60 images flagged for missing alt text. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe markup, and link `href` destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations are unverified.** 98 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Redirect targets, 404s, and loops are unknown.
- **A third-party pixel carries a recipient identifier.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` is five LiveRamp pixels. The `s=` value begins with the same string as the first-party `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. This looks like one per-recipient hash shared with a third party. That is a privacy and disclosure concern, and it raises load count and blocklist exposure.
- **Pixels are duplicated.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times, `rlcdn` 5 times, plus `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/aed9429b-…`. Some are probably intentional per-module tracking, but the duplicates add requests and inflate open counts.
- **Images come from three domains:** `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com`, and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. Mixed hosting means more DNS lookups, and a scene7 outage would break images independently of the others.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 60 images are flagged for missing alt text (QA).** The report can't tell a missing attribute from an empty one.
  - *Content images:* the main images (e.g. `…/m/1/6cda691d-b295-47d6-aa7c-8c52d1eec48a.jpg`, `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`, and the `.gif` banners such as `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`) need descriptive alt text. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those modules are blank.
  - *Spacers and pixels:* `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`, and the tracking pixels should have an explicit `alt=""`. Without it, some readers announce the filename.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Source hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times and `charset` twice (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - A template placeholder comment is left in the source: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - The global `*{… line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` rules are broad and may override module styles.
- **MSO fallbacks are present** (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mso-table-lspace`). No issues there.
- **Pass rate is 57%**, driven mostly by the alt-text warnings.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). Per-recipient IDs appear only in tracking URLs (`mi_u=`, `mi_cid=`, `mi_mid=`), and those are rendered. The truncated source couldn't be checked for tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the sender doesn't actually set them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status can't be assessed. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **The footer is not visible** in the truncated HTML, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address couldn't be checked.
- **The From domain** `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and matches the image host (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`). That alignment is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. Tracking links were not resolved, and no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source or QA output. The only identifiers seen are the vendor's `mi_*` parameters. Landing pages, UTM consistency, and offer-to-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve a sample of the 98 tracking links (follow redirects) to confirm final URLs, status codes, and UTM or `mi_*` retention.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the content images and `alt=""` to spacers and pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fetch the raw `.eml` with the original headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC without the relay in the way.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, and any merge tokens.
6. Review whether the `rlcdn.com` LiveRamp pixel needs to appear 5 times, and whether the shared per-recipient hash is disclosed in the privacy policy.
7. Clean up the template: escape `&` in the title, drop the duplicate meta tags, and remove the leftover placeholder comment.
