# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Makeover reveal!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a MessageGears-style template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) with about 40 images flagged for missing alt text and duplicate or leftover template markup. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings look like relay capture gaps, not confirmed defects. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 67 links use click-redirect domains and were skipped, so destination status and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Third-party identifier sharing:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f8fd1015f5a8&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp) carry an `s=` value that matches the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` identifier on Crate & Barrel's own `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels. That suggests the same recipient-level hashed ID is passed to a third party.
- **Redundant pixels:** the `n=1` to `n=5` calls are identical apart from the counter, so they add requests without adding information.
- **Extra tracking pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/acbba079-…` are open or verification beacons. They are expected, and their missing alt is not a defect (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 40 images are flagged.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` include `17050965-…jpg`, `a353bd30-…jpg` and `dceaca6f-…jpg`.
  - The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e…png`, `789e7381…png` and others) are also flagged.
  - The `s7d5.scene7.com/…HolidayAwareness_Anthem…` image is flagged as well.
  - The check can't tell a missing `alt` attribute from `alt=""`. That matters for the `rlcdn.com`, `o.gif` and `dv.` tracking pixels, which should use `alt=""` and are fine if they do.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` rule also prevents text resizing. It sets 100% line-height on every element and is overridden only in places.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype declared.
- **Redundant head tags:** `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type. There are three separate `format-detection` metas, and the first already covers `date`, `address` and `telephone`.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and `IMPUT` is a typo. It's harmless but sloppy.
- **Odd breakpoint:** the mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, which is narrower than typical phone-width breakpoints. Wider phones and small tablets fall through to desktop CSS. This is an inference from the CSS, not a rendered test.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, and the MSO conditional comments and VML namespaces are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` parameters resolved correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports neither header (RFC 8058) was found. The QA note says this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap, so I can't confirm it as a sender defect. Verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with an aligned sending domain.
- **Footer unverified:** the body-level unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 67 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated source doesn't expose the final URLs. No UTM parameters, redirect chains or landing-page status could be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text:** add descriptive `alt` to the content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. **Allow zoom:** remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Fix the head markup:** escape the title as `&amp;`, drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` metas, and delete the placeholder comment.
4. **Deduplicate the LiveRamp pixels:** reduce the five `rlcdn.com` calls to one. Confirm the hashed `s=` identifier is disclosed in the privacy policy.
5. **Recheck compliance items:** capture raw headers outside the relay to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the `Authentication-Results` pass. Also review the untruncated footer.
6. **Probe the tracked links:** follow redirects on a sample of the 67 links to check final URLs, UTM parameters and status codes.
