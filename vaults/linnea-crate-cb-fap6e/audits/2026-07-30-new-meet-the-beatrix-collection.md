# NEW! Meet the Beatrix collection
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is one of the rare Crate & Kids sends where the subject and hero actually agree with each other — "NEW! Meet the Beatrix collection" opens straight into "meet the beatrix bedroom collection" with a Shop Now button, no bait-and-switch. That's a real improvement over sends like 7/29's "That modern cozy vibe you love?" where subject and hero talked past each other.
- But the collection reveal is the only disciplined part of this email. After the bedroom and nursery Beatrix sections wrap up, it collapses into the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged repeatedly in this brand's history (7/28 "top sofas," 7/26/7/28 back-to-school repeats): fall arrivals grid, a 50%-off summer sale banner, a fall lookbook, a $500 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product rail, a Crate & Barrel credit card pitch, an app download push, and a free design consultation offer — all stacked below the actual "new collection" the subject promised. By the time a reader scrolls past the fold, the Beatrix story is old news.
- Net: strong open hook, weak follow-through. This is a send that would get credit for a good subject/hero pairing and lose it on fatigue and dilution.

## What's working

- Hero visual and copy match the subject line exactly — "meet the beatrix bedroom collection" — no mismatch penalty.
- Two clearly separated collection stories (bedroom, nursery) each get a hero image, product grid, and a callout about material/hardware detail (green finish, drawer knob hardware).
- Product photography is clean and consistent — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout breaks anywhere in the scroll.
- Detail shots (the drawer-pull closeup) do real work showing the "vintage vibe" hardware the copy talks about.

## What's weak

- The email is enormous — the Beatrix reveal is maybe 20% of the total scroll before pivoting into unrelated fall arrivals, a sale, a lookbook, a sweepstakes, a credit card ad, and an app pitch.
- No visible price, sizing, or availability on any Beatrix piece — a reader interested in the actual collection gets zero purchase-decision info.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now" — generic, repeated identically across the whole email, no product-specific copy.
- The "up to 50% off summer sale" banner sitting mid-email directly undercuts the "NEW" framing of the subject — it tells the reader the new stuff isn't the deal, the old stuff is.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** End it after the Beatrix nursery section with one strong CTA. Move fall arrivals, the summer sale, the sweepstakes, and the credit card pitch into their own sends — this brand already over-sends near-identical promos per the history log, so this doesn't need to be one more kitchen-sink email.
- 2. **Add price and material info** directly under the Beatrix hero and product grid shots — right now there's no purchase-decision detail on the actual featured product.
- 3. **Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs** — "Shop the Beatrix Bed," "Shop the Nursery Collection" — so each button tells the reader what they're clicking into.
- 4. **Drop or relocate the sale banner** — pairing "NEW collection" messaging with "up to 50% off" mid-scroll splits reader attention between two competing narratives.
- 5. Subject/preheader alternatives for testing:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Introducing Beatrix: vintage-inspired kids' furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Beatrix Collection is here — new bedroom & nursery finds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Timeless hardware, vibrant green tones — shop the full collection`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New bedroom and nursery pieces with vintage-inspired details`
