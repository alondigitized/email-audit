# Up to 50% off top dressers, beds, gliders & more!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-led furniture send, and the best of the recent Crate & Kids batch. The "limited time only! up to 50% off furniture" headline sits right under a nursery hero. Four hero products follow in a steady rhythm, each with its sale price against its regular price: the Abridged bunk bed, Little Bear toddler bed, Redondo 6-drawer dresser and Babyletto Kiwi glider. The subject promises dressers, beds and gliders, and the body delivers exactly those, which is a sharper match than the Samsung Frame TV subject on 10/17, whose body was a general living-room and Thanksgiving dining event.

After the four products it keeps going: a 60%-off clearance block, a wallcovering launch, baby gear, a holiday gifts block, a "we love these for you" grid, a Crate & Kids credit card promo, an app download with a $500 sweepstakes, and a Design Desk pitch. ## What's working

- The headline "limited time only! up to 50% off furniture" is the clear first read, with a visible "Shop the Sale" button right under it.
- Each product has its own block with a sale-vs-reg price line, a short description and a "Shop Now" button. That makes it easy to click straight to an item.
- The Babyletto glider block carries a customer quote, which is rare social proof in a promo send.
- Product choice matches the subject. Dressers, beds and gliders all appear in order, so the subject doesn't over-promise.
- The thin sage banner with free shipping on $99+ lowers the friction of buying bulky furniture.

## What's weak

- The email is roughly 10,000px tall and runs through many unrelated modules after the furniture. The sale gets buried.
- Each product has two CTAs ("Shop Now" plus a "Shop the Furniture Sale" text link). The repetition adds clutter without adding choice.
- The "limited time only" line has no end date, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The hero image has no overlaid offer text. The percentage only appears below the image, so the lifestyle photo does no selling work.
- The bottom is dense with legal fine print, and the app and card promos pull attention away from furniture.
- Cadence is too tight given the other Crate sends this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the "we love these for you" grid. Move the card, app and Design Desk modules to a separate send. Keep clearance only if it is the same category.
- 2. Put the "up to 50% off" and an end date ("ends Sunday") on the hero image itself.
- 3. Use one CTA per product and drop the secondary text link.
- 4. Put the glider's customer quote or star rating on every product, not just one.
- 5. Space this send out from the other Crate promos, or consolidate the week's topics into fewer emails.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a large, table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (Crate & Barrel Kids, sent via an ESP on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`) with 88 tracked links. About 45 images lack alt text, and the QA data can't confirm the unsubscribe headers or SPF/DKIM. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 88 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains, and expiry are unverified.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm individual `href` values, broken anchors, or UTM presence.
- Tracking pixels are present and are also flagged as missing alt:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b3993086-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - 5× `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` (LiveRamp/RampID third-party pixels)
  - 6× `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`
- The `rlcdn.com` pixels carry a hashed identifier (`s=dbe8da71…`) to a third-party domain, and five of them fire in one send. This adds third-party requests and a privacy consideration, though it isn't a functional defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 `<img>` elements are flagged. The count is approximate because the QA output may be truncated.
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, roughly 25 of them. Examples: `6cda691d-b295-47d6-aa7c-8c52d1eec48a.jpg` and `f1db6dd8-071b-4d4b-ab5d-5549f0cf3f58.gif`.
  - One banner on `m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`.
  - Scene7 story banners: `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif` and `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif`. Their filenames carry dates of 09/26 and 10/02, so they look reused from earlier sends.
  - Two `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` spacers.
  - If these images are blocked, as is common in Outlook, the content is unreadable and screen readers get no description.
  - Pure tracking pixels and spacers should use `alt=""` rather than no attribute, so screen readers skip them.
- **Doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional with `<meta charset>` declared twice (`http-equiv` and `charset`). This is harmless but redundant.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, and the title doesn't describe the email.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the head, which shows an unfinished template.
- **Responsive CSS:** the stylesheet has conflicting `img {max-width: …}` rules at 640px and 768px, and the mobile breakpoint is 460px. The source is truncated, so I can't verify further.
- **Plus side:** `lang="en"` is set, and MSO/Outlook conditional blocks are present.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible source. I saw no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. The truncated HTML means the footer and body weren't fully inspected.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this may be an artifact of the relay, not a real gap.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and alignment can't be verified from this data.
- The physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be checked, because the footer was cut off in the truncated source.

All three header findings are unconfirmed, not confirmed failures. They should be checked against the raw message headers.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links can't be evaluated. All 88 are behind tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and the truncated HTML doesn't show the final URLs.
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "Up to 50% off top dressers, beds, gliders & more!" are unverified.
- Stale assets suggest possible content reuse. Banners named `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance` and `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide` appear in an email sent on 10/17, so the linked destinations may be stale.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Pull the full raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the AgentMail relay copy, and confirm one-click unsubscribe and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
3. Resolve the 88 tracking links with a redirect follower to check final URLs, HTTP status, and UTM parameters, and confirm the sale pages match the subject line.
4. Check whether the 09/26 and 10/02 Scene7 banners are stale or expired.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and escape the `&` in `<title>` (`&amp;`).
6. Review the third-party `rlcdn.com` pixels for privacy and consent compliance.
7. Pass the full, untruncated HTML to the next audit run so the footer, links, and merge tokens can be checked.
