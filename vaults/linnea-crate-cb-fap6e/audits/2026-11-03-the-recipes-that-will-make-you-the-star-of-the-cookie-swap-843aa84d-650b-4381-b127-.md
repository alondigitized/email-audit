# The recipes that will make you the star of the cookie swap
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a content-led baking email, and it delivers on the subject. A hero reads "the best baking season is here!" over a kitchen flat-lay. Seven recipe cards follow, each with a large cookie photo, a short blurb and a "Shop & Get the Recipe" button. Seasonal timing is good, and recipes make a better hook than another discount, which has been most of this brand's recent cadence (Nov 2 kitchen brands "up to 40% off", Nov 2 "100s of things that ship for FREE!").
- The email then dilutes itself. A top banner ("ends today! accent chairs & tables up to 30% off") promotes furniture, which has nothing to do with cookies. After the recipes come a category block, a Kids in the Kitchen section, a Black Friday signup, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app download with a $500 sweepstakes and a design service. That is roughly eight different asks. The email also runs very long, and the screenshot was too small for me to read the fine print.
- Judgement: a strong subject and creative concept, weakened by a mismatched offer banner and a bloated second half. It lands around the middle of the range.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match, and the recipe-as-content angle fits the cookie-swap season.
- Photography is large and appetizing, and each recipe card follows the same pattern: image, name, blurb, button.
- The "Shop & Get the Recipe" button copy ties content to commerce.
- The "for your sweetest traditions" tile block (bakeware, kitchen linens, tools, appliances & electric) gives a clear route into the shopping categories.
- The Black Friday signup captures intent ahead of the biggest sale period.

## What's weak

- The "ends today! accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" banner sits above a baking hero. It is the only visible offer and has nothing to do with the subject.
- The recipe cards repeat seven times with the same button, so the scroll is long and the middle drags.
- The tail stacks a Black Friday signup, product grid, credit card, app download and DesignDesk promo, which competes with the main call to action.
- The hero has no clear price or offer for the baking category, so the click rationale rests on inspiration alone.
- The recipe titles and blurbs are small and gray, and hard to read at inbox scale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or relocate the furniture banner. If an offer has to lead, make it a bakeware or appliance one, for example "up to 30% off bakeware."
- 2. Cut the recipes from seven to three or four, and put a "Get all the recipes" button after them.
- 3. Cut the footer promos to one. The Black Friday signup is the best fit, so drop the credit card, app and DesignDesk blocks.
- 4. Add a product link under each recipe, such as the cookie cutters or a stand mixer, so the content leads to a specific item.
- 5. Add a social proof element, such as ratings on the product grid.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. The automated QA scored it 57% (0 failures, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problems are 37 images without alt attributes and a viewport/zoom restriction. The header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f8fd1015f5a8&n=1` through `n=5` requests go to a LiveRamp domain. The `s=` value is a 64-character hex string. Its prefix matches the `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cc…` token on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, so one subscriber identifier appears to be shared across the first-party and third-party pixels.
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4849dba4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (a second beacon).
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 37 images** (QA listing, which was itself truncated):
  - 23 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, e.g. `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.
  - 7 images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - 5 `rlcdn` pixels.
  - `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` beacon.
- **Tracking pixels without alt or `role="presentation"`/`aria-hidden`.** Screen readers may announce them.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Text-size overrides.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` disable text scaling. They also conflict with the later `body {…:100%}`.
- **Unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`.** This is invalid in an XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate and redundant markup:**
  - Two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`).
  - `format-detection` declared four times with conflicting values.
  - Empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }`.
- **Placeholder left in source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap, and the later rule wins at ≥768px (640px instead of 600px).
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centered paragraphs across clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images. Add `alt=""` plus `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the duplicate meta tags, the empty CSS rule and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. Reconcile the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
5. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the redirect chains on a sample of the 70 links to check destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Review whether sharing the hashed subscriber ID with `rlcdn.com` is covered by the sender's privacy disclosures.
