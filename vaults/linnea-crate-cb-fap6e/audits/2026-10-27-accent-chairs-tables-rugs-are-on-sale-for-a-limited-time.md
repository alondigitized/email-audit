# Accent chairs, tables & rugs are on SALE for a limited time
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, multi-module sale email whose subject doesn't match its hero. The subject promises "Accent chairs, tables & rugs on SALE." The top banner says "new items added! the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off," and the accent-furniture block only starts below that. A reader who clicks for accent chairs lands on a dining-table rug photo first.
- The body does deliver on the subject. It shows tiered offers: up to 30% off accent tables, up to 30% off accent chairs, and up to 60% off rugs. There are three "Shop All Sale" and "Shop Now" buttons, a quick-ship module, and a "we love these for you" product grid with prices. The sweepstakes, DesignDesk, credit card, app and store-locator modules then pile on and dilute the focus.
- Judgement: a competent but overstuffed send. The offers are real and the product imagery is good, but the email asks for too many things. It is also the second DesignDesk push in two days, after the 10/26 "free design help" send. Solid mid-pack.

## What's working

- The subject's categories (chairs, tables, rugs) map one-to-one onto the first body module, each with its own discount tier.
- The rug at "up to 60% off" is a strong, specific hook with a visible price on the hero (Brighton Wool Ivory Rug, "Sale $2,579.20 Reg. $3,699").
- Product imagery is large and editorial, so the home-furnishing feel is clear.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows named products with prices, which helps a shopper who wants specifics.
- The Crate&Barrel wordmark sits at the top and the footer links are clean, so the brand is instantly recognizable.

## What's weak

- The hero conflicts with the subject. It leads with the "Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off" and a "*Select styles" asterisk before the accent-furniture offer.
- Too many competing CTAs: Shop The Sale, Shop All Sale, Shop Now, Book a Free Appointment (twice), Learn More, Download Now. There is no single clear action.
- The sweepstakes ("chance to win up to $5000"), the Reward Dollars credit card and the app promo bury the sale in the lower half.
- The accent tile labels ("up to 30% off accent tables" and "accent chairs") are small text over busy photos, and the headline discount is hard to read.
- Dense fine print at the bottom hides the exclusions, which hurts how honest the offer feels. The DesignDesk module also repeats from the previous day's send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Open with "Accent chairs, tables & rugs: up to 60% off" and move the dining-room event lower or drop it.
- 2. Cut the lower half to one sale CTA and one secondary service (DesignDesk). Remove the credit card and app modules from this send.
- 3. Put the discount tiers in bold text on solid blocks, not over photos.
- 4. Space out the DesignDesk promo so it doesn't repeat daily.
- 5. Surface the sale end date, for example "Ends Sunday," instead of "limited time."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Refresh your living room: accent sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Accent tables and chairs up to 30% off, rugs up to 60% off. Select styles, through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best rug prices of the season, plus quick-ship chairs for the holidays.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`<!--V2-->`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` personalization) with no hard failures: the QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings, at a 57% pass rate. The real problems are about 30 images with no alt text, viewport and text-size settings that block zoom, and header-level compliance checks that couldn't be verified because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **64 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are LiveRamp (rlcdn) syncs. They add third-party requests and carry the same hashed identifier `s=dbe8da718e29…`.
- **Identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a…` value is repeated across the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images and the `o.gif` open pixel. It is a per-recipient token embedded in every image request, so forwarding the email or caching its images exposes it.
- **Open pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/679d81f6-d247-432f-8e8d-c67700da20fb?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are standard, with no defects found.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 30 images (QA WARN).**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`: 17 images with no alt, such as `2d3d8b72-….jpg` and `cd56cdc7-….gif`. With images blocked or a screen reader, these are blank or announced by filename.
  - Dynamic images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images, e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`). These look like personalized recommendation tiles, so they likely need real alt text.
  - Tracking and sync pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…/679d81f6-…`). These should carry `alt=""` and are lower priority.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text scaling. The later `body` rule sets `100%`, so the rules conflict.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template residue:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled placeholder comment.
  - There are two `<meta charset>` declarations (the `http-equiv` one and `<meta charset="utf-8">`).
  - The `format-detection` meta tag is repeated three times.
  - These are harmless but add weight.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` because the later rule wins. The 600px cap never applies.
- **Present and fine:** `lang="en"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `color-scheme: light`, and MSO conditional comments with `PixelsPerInch`.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source, and `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` are populated. The truncated portion could not be checked.
