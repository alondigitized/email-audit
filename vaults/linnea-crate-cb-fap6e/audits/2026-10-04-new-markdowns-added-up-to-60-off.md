# New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance blast with a clean hero: "New Markdowns Added! up to 60% off clearance" over a muted slate-green image, with a white "Shop Now" button. A row of category links sits right under it (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tablecloth & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor). The 60% number is the strongest hook, and it matches the subject. For a home-furniture reader, "Shop Furniture" in the first screen is a real way in.
- The email then undercuts itself. A banner above the hero pushes "up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals," which was yesterday's Oct 3 subject, "Sofas, sectionals and more up to 20% off." Below the hero are a second sale ("up to 25% off storage & dining event"), TheDesignDesk twice, a product grid, a credit-card pitch and an app download. That is at least five competing asks and three different discount numbers, 20%, 25% and 60%, in one scroll. The hero image is also murky and low-contrast, so it reads as a texture rather than a product.
- Judgement: a competent, high-volume retail send with a good headline offer and no focus. It would land harder with one hero and a furniture-led clearance story. It is the same 6/10 as the rest of the brand's sends this weekend.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% off clearance" is a big, specific number, and it is the largest type in the email.
- The white "Shop Now" pill on the dark hero is unmistakable.
- The category link row gives a furniture shopper a direct path (Shop Furniture, Shop Rugs, Shop Decor).
- "New Markdowns Added!" and "Select styles. While quantities last." give the clearance some freshness and scarcity.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices, with strike-through on some, and the Lounge 93" Sofa is relevant to a furniture reader.
- The Crate Card block mentions 10% back in Reward Dollars, which is a real member-style benefit.

## What's weak

- The top banner (20% off sofas) conflicts with the 60% hero. Two sale numbers sit within one screen of each other.
- The hero image is a dark, blurry botanical texture with no product, so the 60% offer has nothing to attach to.
- Further down the email stacks a 25% storage event, a free design appointment (twice), a credit card, an app promo and a store locator. It is a long scroll with no priority.
- Only 2 of the 6 grid products look discounted, so the "up to 60% off" promise isn't supported where the products are shown.
- Several CTAs are generic ("Shop Now", "Learn More", "Download Now"), and the fine print is small and dense.
- This is a third or fourth send in two days, and the sofa offer repeats Oct 3's. It also sits alongside Oct 4's "Up to 35% off top kitchen brands!", so the discount ladder keeps shifting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the 20% sofa banner and make the 60% clearance the only hero offer. Two offers in one screen dilutes both.
- 2. Replace the textured hero with a real clearance product (a sofa, a dining table), with the price drop shown on the image.
- 3. Fill the product grid with the deepest markdowns, and show was/now prices on every item.
- 4. Cut the email to the hero, categories, a markdown grid and one secondary block. Drop the app promo and the second TheDesignDesk block.
- 5. Give CTAs verbs and nouns, such as "Shop furniture clearance."
- 6. Give the sale an end date. "While quantities last" is not a deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furniture clearance: up to 60% off, new today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns on sofas, tables & rugs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select sofas, dining and rugs just dropped. Prices end when stock does.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh clearance across furniture, rugs and decor, plus 10% back with your Crate Card.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main problems are about 28 images with no `alt` attribute and zoom and text-resize restrictions in the head. Compliance and tracking checks are mostly inconclusive. The source was truncated, and the QA run couldn't see headers or probe the 61 tracking links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 61 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were checked for resolution, redirect depth or final destination. This is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** five identical LiveRamp beacons load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5`. The `s=` value looks like a hashed subscriber identifier. Each one is an extra third-party request with no `alt`.
- **Other tracking images:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a1d69e1b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are Movable Ink open or tracking beacons. The `mi_u` identifier is carried in image URLs, so forwarding the email forwards that identifier.
- **Broken links:** none confirmed. No link-level failures were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** QA flagged 28 images.
  - 14 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe90…/m/1/` and `/m/47/`. These appear to be content or banner images. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-….png`, `0350abec-….gif` and `6c00bab9-….jpg`.
  - 7 are Movable Ink dynamic images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png` and others). They are likely product tiles, so the missing text matters most here. They will be blank or announced by filename when images are blocked.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (the 5 `rlcdn` beacons, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon). They need `alt=""`, and a missing attribute is invalid HTML.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks user zoom. This fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resize disabled:** `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }`, `body { … -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent iOS text scaling.
- **Dark mode:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">` and `supported-color-schemes: light` opt out of dark-mode adaptation. This is deliberate, but dark-mode clients get no adaptation.
- **Markup hygiene (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset.
  - There are three redundant `format-detection` metas, plus an empty `td,th,div,p,a { }` rule in the MSO block.
  - `img {max-width:600px}` at `min-width:768px` conflicts with `img {max-width:640px}` at `min-width:640px`. The later rule wins at 768px and above, so the 600px rule is dead.
  - A template placeholder comment leaked into production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values in the Movable Ink URLs are rendered, not literal tokens. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. The check notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** and not a verified defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the same relay caveat applies.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be confirmed or denied.
- **Third-party pixels:** the `rlcdn` beacons may need disclosure in the privacy policy, but nothing in the HTML shows a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 61 links go through tracking redirects and none were probed, so the UTM parameters and landing pages can't be seen. The source doesn't show whether the "Up to 60% off" CTA lands on a matching sale page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 14 hosted content images and the 7 Movable Ink tiles. Give Movable Ink dynamic alt text where the platform allows it. Use `alt=""` on all 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `html`, `body` and `*` rules, keeping `100%` where a fix is needed.
3. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox, not the relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and DKIM alignment. Treat the three relay warnings as unresolved until then.
4. Re-run the QA with untruncated HTML and a redirect-following probe on a sample of the 61 tracking links. That would confirm destinations, status codes and UTM carry-through.
5. Fix the template hygiene: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate meta tags, the empty MSO rule and the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and reconcile the two `img max-width` media queries.
6. Consider cutting the five duplicate `rlcdn` beacons down to one, or confirming they're needed. That would reduce third-party requests and identifier exposure.
