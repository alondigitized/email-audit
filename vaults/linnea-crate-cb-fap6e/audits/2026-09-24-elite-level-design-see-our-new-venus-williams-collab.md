# Elite-level design: See our NEW Venus Williams collab
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This actually delivers on its subject line, for once — "VENUS WILLIAMS x Crate&Barrel Kids" is the hero image at the very top, no scrolling required, with a clear "inspired by the game, designed by the goat" tagline. That's a real departure from the last five Crate & Barrel sends I've flagged, which all promised one specific thing and buried it under a mega-catalog.
- But it doesn't stay disciplined. After the strong open it collapses into the exact sprawl pattern from the 09-23 and 09-22 sends: bed, chair, lamp, dresser, bedding, throw blanket, Design Desk pitch, registry plug, "we love these for you" grid, app download, financing offer. The Venus Williams collab — the entire reason for the subject line — gets maybe two screens before the email forgets about her and becomes a generic kids-furniture catalog again.
- Net: best subject-to-hero match I've seen from this brand recently, but it still can't resist tacking on six unrelated modules after the payoff. Worth opening, weak on sustained relevance.

## What's working

- Hero image delivers exactly what the subject promises — Venus Williams, tennis-court styling, visible within the first screen.
- Clear green "Shop the Outfit" CTA button directly under the hero, well contrasted against the dark green background.
- Individual product shots (bed, chair, dresser) are clean, well-lit, no render bugs or overlapping text.
- Tennis-themed bedding call-outs (racket lamp, tennis-ball throw blanket) reinforce the collab concept visually, not just in the hero.
- Star ratings and "quality pieces your little one can grow into" trust copy appear on the Design Desk crib block — real social proof.

## What's weak

- The Venus Williams collab thread disappears after ~2 screens; the rest is a generic bed/chair/lamp/dresser catalog indistinguishable from prior C&B sends.
- Design Desk crib pitch, wedding/baby registry block, and financing/app-download footer are three unrelated asks crammed into one email — same "everything but the kitchen sink" pattern flagged in the 09-23 and 09-22 audits.
- No visible pricing or discount anywhere in the render — every CTA is "Shop Now" / "Shop the Outfit" with zero offer, so there's nothing concrete pulling a click.
- The registry section ("nicole brydon bloom & justin theroux's registry faves") is a jarring non-sequitur dropped in the middle of a tennis-collab email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the tennis/Venus-themed products (hero → bed → chair → lamp → dresser → bedding/throw). Everything past that — Design Desk, registry, "we love these for you," app/financing footer — should be its own send. This is the single highest-impact fix; it's what's kept every recent C&B email at 5-6/10.
- 2. Add a concrete offer to the hero CTA — "Shop the Collab" with a visible %/$ off or "new arrival" badge. Right now there's no reason to click beyond curiosity.
- 3. Keep Venus Williams present visually throughout the product sections (small badge or her image reused), not just at the top, so the collab feels sustained rather than a header slapped on a generic catalog.
- 4. Drop or relocate the celebrity registry block entirely — it has nothing to do with a sports-collab kids furniture drop and breaks narrative continuity.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Court style, kid-sized: our Venus Williams collab is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Racket lamps, tennis-ball throws, and the bed she designed herself.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A collection built for game-day naps and playroom wins.`
