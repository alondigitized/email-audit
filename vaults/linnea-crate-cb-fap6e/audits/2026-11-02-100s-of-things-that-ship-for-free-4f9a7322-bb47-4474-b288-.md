# 100s of things that ship for FREE!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a free-shipping send from Crate & Barrel Kids, and the hero matches the subject. "it. all. ships. free!" sits over a textile photo with a single "Shop All Free Shipping" button. A two-column grid below it covers kids bedding, baby bedding, kids gear, baby gear, cuddlies, bath, curtains and pillows. Each tile is labeled "ships free". The promise is clear and the button repeats once after the grid.
- The weakness is that the email doesn't stay on one idea. Below the grid it adds a "sign up, save 10%" block, a "find your 1,000 hour chair" nursery seating hero, a "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off" clearance block, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app download, and The Design Desk. That's six or seven competing messages. The free-shipping offer also has no threshold or deadline visible in the body. The fine print is a dense block at the bottom, so "ships free" reads as unconditional but isn't clearly so.
- My judgment is a clear, on-subject hero that gets diluted by a long catalog scroll. This is the same pattern as the 2026-11-01 clearance and design-help sends. It's decent for a parent shopping for the nursery, but it's not a sharp send.

## What's working

- The hero copy "it. all. ships. free!" matches the subject, and the single button is obvious.
- The eight-tile grid with "ships free" captions makes the offer concrete across categories.
- The photography is warm and consistent, and the tiles are easy to scan.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button repeats after the grid, so the CTA is reachable without scrolling back.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows prices, which gives some product detail.

## What's weak

- Free shipping has no visible threshold, deadline or end date, so there's no credible urgency.
- The "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off" block sits mid-email and competes with the shipping offer.
- The sign-up 10% block, nursery seating hero, credit card, app and Design Desk modules dilute focus. That's about seven calls to action.
- The "ships free" tile captions are small and low-contrast, and each tile has no individual CTA or price.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny, which undercuts how honest "free" feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the grid and the repeat button. Move clearance, the credit card, the app and the Design Desk to other sends.
- 2. State the terms in the hero, such as "Free shipping on all kids & baby, through Sunday." Add a deadline.
- 3. Put prices or "from $X" on the grid tiles and add a "best seller" or review-count cue.
- 4. If a second offer stays, make it the 10% sign-up and put it above the fold only for non-subscribers.
- 5. Rewrite the tile captions so they name the product benefit rather than just "ships free."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout from a Marketing Cloud-style ESP. It has dark-mode and viewport meta handling, but the QA run reports a 57% pass rate, with many images missing `alt` and no confirmable authentication or unsubscribe-header data. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe link, UTM and merge-token checks could not be run.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable destinations:** 71 tracking links were skipped by QA as click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Redirect chains, 404s and destination match to the offers are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are five LiveRamp pixels. `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/df9a7f10-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party open and view pixels. Five identical third-party pixels in one send is redundant, and a blocked image load could affect tracking.
- **Repeated asset:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times with an identical `src`. If these are dynamic or recommendation images, the repeats suggest a template slot that isn't varying. If they're pixels, they're redundant. I can't tell which from the truncated source.
- **Possibly stale asset:** the hero `Hero%5FEdit%5F3%5FTypography%5F360.gif` sits under `/2025/08%5FKids/20250815%5FCBK%5F1000Hour%5FNurseryChair/`. That's a 2025 path in a 2026 send, probably reused from an old template. Confirm it's intentional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** QA flags about 31 `<img>` tags with no `alt`. The real content images are the scene7 and `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/.../m/1/*.jpg` assets (12 or more), the hero GIF, and the `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_Tert` banner. With images off, the email will be blank in those slots, and screen readers will read out filenames or URLs. The pixel and spacer images (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv.`, `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`) should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` is set in several places, which disables iOS text resizing.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared 3 times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). `charset` is declared twice (the `http-equiv` form and `<meta charset>`).
- **Template leftovers:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block remain in the head.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and doesn't reflect the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the portion captured. The rest of the body couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are absent from the captured headers (RFC 8058 one-click not evidenced). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check them in the raw message from a non-relay inbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a sending subdomain, but alignment can't be verified here.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.
- **Privacy:** the same 64-hex value (`dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8`) appears as `mi_u=` on first-party URLs and as `s=` on `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels. It looks like a hashed recipient identifier passed to a third party (LiveRamp). That is a privacy-disclosure consideration, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 71 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated HTML shows no resolved destinations or UTM parameters. The only campaign identifiers visible are asset names (`2026_0820_CBK_EverydayFreeShip`, `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM`). One is an August free-shipping asset and the other is a November clearance asset. This mix suggests the content blocks come from different campaigns, so the landing pages should be checked against the "100s of things that ship for FREE!" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Deduplicate the `format-detection` and `charset` meta tags, escape `&` in `<title>`, and strip the leftover template comments.
4. Capture raw headers from a direct inbox (not the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Resolve the redirect chains of a sample of the 71 tracked links in a follow-up pass to verify destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and merge tokens.
7. Check whether the 2025 hero GIF and the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` image are intentional.
