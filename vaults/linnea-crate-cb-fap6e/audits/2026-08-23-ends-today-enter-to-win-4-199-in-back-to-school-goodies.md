# ENDS TODAY: Enter to win $4,199 in back-to-school goodies!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' "$4,199 Back-to-School Giveaway" — a co-branded sweepstakes email with a grid of nine partner-brand gift-card prizes (Bumbleride, Oeuf, Gathre, Dough, Saranoni, Meri Meri, Bentgo) sitting under a monogrammed backpack hero. The sweepstakes hook is legitimate and the prize grid is genuinely well-organized, but the email is really a Trojan horse for a catalog blast — after "Enter Now," it cascades into backpack bundles, room themes, five product tiles, a credit-card pitch, an app-download push, and a Design Desk appointment CTA. That's a lot of asks stacked after the giveaway, which dilutes what should be a single, high-urgency entry mechanic.
- Compared to the brand's recent run — the Beatrix furniture launch (6.00), the clearance blast (7.00), the fall-arrivals catalog (8.00) — this lands in the middle: a fresh mechanic (first sweepstakes I've seen from them) but the same kitchen-sink cascade problem that dragged down the Lupita Nyong'o Design Desk send.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and on-brand: a monogrammed shark-print backpack against a soft nursery backdrop, clearly back-to-school.
- The $4,199 prize breakdown is genuinely well-designed — nine clear brand tiles with specific dollar values, easy to scan.
- "ENDS TODAY" urgency in the subject is backed by no visible contradicting date in the body.
- "Enter Now" appears twice (hero and post-grid) with consistent black-button styling, so the primary action is unambiguous.
- No render bugs — layout, images, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The email doesn't stop at the giveaway — it keeps going into bundles, room themes, five unrelated product tiles, a credit card ad, and an app download, burying the actual sweepstakes CTA under six more competing asks.
- No visible entry mechanic explained (is it a click-to-enter, a form, a purchase requirement?) — "Enter Now" is a leap of faith with no rules/terms visible near the fold.
- "Sponsored in part by MOTHER" is a stray, unexplained fragment sitting right under the prize grid — reads like an ad-network artifact.
- Nothing on the page reinforces the "ends today" urgency after the subject line — no countdown, no date stamp in the hero or near the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the second "Enter Now" button. A sweepstakes email should be a single-purpose send — the bundles/room-themes/product-grid/credit-card/app cascade competes with and buries the entry mechanic.
- 2. Put a visible deadline (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") directly under the hero headline, not just in the subject line — right now the urgency evaporates once you open the email.
- 3. Add one line explaining how entry works (e.g., "Enter with your email — no purchase necessary") right above the first "Enter Now" button to remove friction/hesitation.
- 4. Fix or remove the orphaned "Sponsored in part by MOTHER" line — it reads like a bug, not a credit.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `9 brands, one giveaway — backpacks, car seats, gift cards`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No purchase necessary. Enter before midnight.`

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MI (Message Intelligence)-templated HTML email with heavy MSO/Outlook conditional styling and multiple third-party tracking pixels; core structural markup is sound, but the QA pass rate (57%) is dragged down almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and unconfirmed email-authentication/compliance headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (redirect-wrapped domains), so final destination URLs are unverified — cannot confirm they resolve without errors.
- Multiple tracking pixels fire on open: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (loaded 6x with identical `mi_u` param — likely per-block MI opens tracking), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (primary open pixel), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp/RampID identity resolution pixel, fired 5x with sequential `n=1..5` params), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/46029b22...?cp_tp=v` (likely DemandVector or Dotdigital-style conversion/view pixel).
- No broken or malformed `href`/`src` syntax observed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

Tracking pixels lacking `alt=""` is low-priority, but the content/hero images missing `alt` fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades the experience for screen-reader users and anyone on images-off email clients.
- Template includes standard Outlook MSO conditional blocks, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and mobile media queries (`max-width:460px`) — indicates responsive/Outlook-safe design intent is in place.
