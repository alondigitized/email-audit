# Up to 30% off holiday hosting essentials + Double Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**

- The render is downscaled from 680x10475 to 130x2000. I can judge structure, hierarchy and headline copy, but not fine print or exact prices.
- This is a hosting-and-Thanksgiving send that tries to do four jobs at once. It sells hosting essentials, pushes the Early Black Friday offer, repeats Double Rewards, and promotes the app and The DesignDesk. The top is focused: a dark green hero reading "host & toast: up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more" with a single "Shop Now" button. A small banner above it carries the Double Rewards and order-by deadline. Below that the email loses discipline.
- It is the same Early Black Friday story from 11/11 ("EARLY BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW!") and 11/12 ("Double Rewards + Up to 50% off Early Black Friday deals!"). ## What's working

- The hero is clear: a green block, a hosting headline, a "30% off" claim and one button, all above the fold.
- "Host & toast" gives a concrete, seasonal reason to open instead of generic sale language.
- The category grid (dinnerware, wine glasses, serveware, drinking glasses, table linens, flatware) is easy to scan, and each tile carries its own discount.
- The Double Rewards "last of the year" block and the loyalty-card section reward members and add credible urgency.
- The Thanksgiving "styles for your table" editorial section adds helpful content between the promo blocks.

## What's weak

- The email is very long (about 10,000px). The core hosting message is buried under Early Black Friday, kitchen deals, rewards, app promo and DesignDesk.
- The offers are layered: 30% off, up to 40% off, up to 50% off, up to $500 off, up to $170 off and 30% off Le Creuset. A reader can't tell what the deal actually is.
- Many CTAs compete: Shop Now, Shop the Sale, Shop All Tabletop & Bar, Shop Thanksgiving, Shop Top Categories, Learn More and others. "Learn More" is vague.
- The order-by-11/18 shipping deadline is tiny text in the top banner, even though it is the most credible urgency in the email.
- The Early Black Friday section repeats the 30% off message already in the hero, so the email feels redundant.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hosting story: hero, category grid, one Double Rewards strip and one product row. Move app, DesignDesk and Early Black Friday duplication to other sends.
- 2. Promote the order-by-11/18 deadline into the hero as a visible line or badge.
- 3. Pick one headline offer. "Up to 30% off" works. Don't stack 40%, 50% and $500 off in the same email.
- 4. Replace "Learn More" and "Shop Now" with specific CTAs, such as "Shop Hosting Essentials" and "Earn Double Rewards".
- 5. Space out near-duplicate promos. This is the third Early Black Friday-adjacent send in two days.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dinnerware, glassware and flatware, plus Double Rewards ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). Its main defects are about 44 images with no alt attribute, and headers and tracking links that couldn't be verified. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and most link markup were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **86 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and broken links are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` calls (LiveRamp) fire per open.
  - There is also a first-party open pixel at `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`.
  - A further beacon sits at `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c9f2474c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
- **A recipient identifier is shared with a third party.** The 64-hex value in `rlcdn …?s=dbe8da71…` has the same prefix as the `mi_u=dbe8da71…` parameter on the first-party pixels. This is probably a hashed recipient ID, but the source doesn't say what it hashes.
- **One image is served from a different host.** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/09%5FCore/2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide…` is a September (`2026_0920`) Thanksgiving Guide asset in a November 12 send. It may be a stale reused asset, and its URL is truncated in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **44 images have no alt attribute.**
  - 29 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` (plus one `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`).
  - Seven are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images, which look like personalized or recommendation images.
  - One is `scene7.com`.
  - Seven are tracking pixels (5× `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). These should carry `alt=""`.
  - If those images are linked or carry the offer text, blocked-image or screen-reader users get no content.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Markup problems:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed.
  - There is a duplicate `format-detection` meta (three declarations, one combined and two split).
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, the MSO/VML namespaces and Outlook DPI settings, and mobile breakpoints at 460px are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear. The truncated remainder was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the tracking and image subdomains, which is consistent with aligned sending, but that isn't proof.
- **The footer could not be checked.** The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 86 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing-page matching for the "30% off holiday hosting" and "Double Rewards" offers were not checked. The tracking is carried in `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text.** Give descriptive alt text to the 30 content images and the seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. Add `alt=""` to the seven pixels.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Fix the markup.** Escape the title as `&amp;`, close the meta tags, dedupe `format-detection` and remove the template placeholder comment.
4. **Check the raw `.eml` headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, outside the AgentMail relay. Don't treat the current warnings as sender defects until then.
5. **Resolve the redirects.** Follow the 86 tracked links in a headless run to confirm final URLs, UTMs and offer alignment, and confirm the Scene7 Thanksgiving asset is intentional.
6. **Review the pixel stack.** Five repeated LiveRamp pixels carrying a shared hashed ID are worth a privacy and consent review.
