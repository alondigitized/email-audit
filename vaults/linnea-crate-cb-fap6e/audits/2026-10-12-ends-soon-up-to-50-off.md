# ENDS SOON: Up to 50% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, long-scroll sale send from a brand the recipient knows. The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event sits under a free-shipping banner, with up to 50% off as the headline offer. Each category tile (furniture, nursery seating, bedding, bath, lighting) has its own photo, discount line and button. The screenshot is a very small downscale, so I can read the layout and headlines but not fine print.
- The subject is the weak part. "ENDS SOON" gives no date or time, and it doesn't say what is on sale. By 2026-10-12 this brand had already sent "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" and "Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+", plus several urgency-led sends over the weekend. A subscriber has seen this "ends soon" move too many times for it to feel real.
- The body is competent but long, and it makes two offers compete: up to 50% off, and free shipping with a "final hours" banner partway down. Judgement: well built and on-brand, but it is another undifferentiated urgency send in a crowded cadence.

## What's working

- The hero names the event, "The Semi-Announual Bedroom & Nursery Event, Up to 50% off", and puts one "Shop" button directly beneath it.
- Each category module pairs a lifestyle photo with its own discount line (for example "up to 30% off nursery seating") and a labeled button, so shoppers can jump to the category they want.
- A free-shipping bar at the top and a "final hours" shipping banner mid-email repeat the threshold offer.
- The render looks clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the spacing is consistent.
- The "we love these for you" product grid with prices and the Affirm "pay later" module give lower-funnel options for shoppers who scroll.

## What's weak

- The subject says "ENDS SOON" with no date or hour, and it doesn't name bedroom, nursery or kids.
- The email is very long, about eight or more stacked modules. The strongest deals are buried in repetitive tiles, and the lower modules (Affirm, TheDesignDesk, app promo) dilute the sale.
- The offer language is hedged with "up to" and "select styles" on almost every tile, so no tile shows a hero product with a real was/now price.
- Free shipping and 50% off compete for urgency. Which one is actually ending is unclear.
- The campaign repeats this brand's other sends from the last few days (bedroom and nursery event, free shipping), so it adds little new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to name the category and state a real deadline, replacing the generic "ENDS SOON" with a date or time and "bedroom & nursery".
- 2. Add a live countdown or "ends tonight at midnight PT" line to the hero, so the urgency in the subject is backed up in the email.
- 3. Cut the body to the top three or four tiles, and put one hero product with a was/now price near the top.
- 4. Pick one primary offer per send. Either lead with up to 50% off and put shipping in a thin footer line, or the reverse.
- 5. Move Affirm, TheDesignDesk and the app promo below the product grid, or drop them from sale sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/MI-templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with `lang="en"` and MSO/VML fallbacks. Its main confirmed defects are about 38 `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication findings are unverified because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML provided is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 77 tracking links because they use click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Recipient-identifying hash in third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…15f5a8&n=1` to `&n=5` requests go to rlcdn.com (LiveRamp). The same hash appears as `mi_u=dbe8da71…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, so a per-recipient identifier is being passed to a third-party domain. Whether that is disclosed is a compliance question for the sender.
- **Redundant pixels.** The same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` image is requested six times. There is also an open pixel at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and one at `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e519ddf3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. That is a lot of beacon requests for one message.
- **Mixed image hosts.** Most images load from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Two come from `s7d5.scene7.com`: `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`. The second host adds a dependency, but nothing shows it is broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 38 images (confirmed by QA).**
  - About 24 are content images, e.g. `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `2dae08c2-…jpg` and the Scene7 `…CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif`. With images blocked, or for screen readers, these will be blank or read as a filename or URL.
  - The rest are tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, the `dv.` pixel). They should carry `alt=""`, not a missing attribute.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables user scaling, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which is an unfilled placeholder with a typo. It is harmless to rendering but shows no QA of the template.
- **Duplicate and conflicting meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared three times, once combined (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) and twice separately. Both `http-equiv` and `<meta charset>` are also declared.
- **Conflicting image max-width rules.**
  - `@media (min-width:768px) { img {max-width:600px} }` and `@media (min-width:640px) { img {max-width:640px} }` overlap.
  - At 768px and above the 640px rule loses only by source order, and it comes later, so it wins. The 600px rule is dead code, and the result is ambiguous across clients.
- **Over-broad reset.** `*{… line-height:100%}` plus `.ExternalClass * {line-height:100%}` forces 100% line-height everywhere, which can compress text in clients that honor the universal selector.
- **Unescaped ampersand in the title.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` in XHTML. The title also doesn't match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the truncated source. The only per-recipient values seen are the tracking hashes above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is a likely capture artifact, not a confirmed sender defect. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo expect both, so check the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified.** The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and image hosts share the `mail.` subdomain, which is consistent with aligned branding. No alignment failure is evidenced.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address not verifiable.** The HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable from this data. All 77 click links were skipped as tracking redirects, so UTM parameters, landing-page status and promo alignment with "Up to 50% off" were not checked. No evidence of a break, and none of continuity either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the first ones listed above. Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Collapse the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests to one, and review whether the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels need to fire. Check that the per-recipient hash going to a third party is covered by the privacy disclosure.
4. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder, merge the three `format-detection` metas, reconcile the two `img` `max-width` media queries, and write `&amp;` in the `<title>`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw message received directly, not through the relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the 77 tracked links with a follow-redirects probe to check final status and UTM parameters, and re-run the audit with the untruncated HTML to cover the footer.
