# Our Back-to-School Sale is on! Up to 20% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is functionally the same email Crate & Kids sent on 7/26 ("Up to 20% off our Back-to-School Sale!" — 7.00/10), rebuilt with the exact same structural flaw called out then: a "up to 50% off summer sale" ribbon sits directly above the "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off" hero, so the first thing your eye resolves is a bigger, unrelated discount that contradicts the subject line's promise. Two days later, that fix still hasn't shipped.
- Past the top fold this is a well-organized back-to-school catalog — clean category grid (backpacks, lunch boxes, duffels, pencil cases, storage, water bottles), a room-setup module, a $4,999 giveaway, new-arrivals teaser, and a product rail with real prices. The execution is competent, but it's also the same kitchen-sink playbook this brand runs on nearly every send: sale + giveaway + new arrivals + credit card pitch + app download + design service + footer, all stacked into one scroll.
- Net: solid category targeting undercut by a self-competing hero and a promo cadence that's starting to blur together. This one's a 7 — better organized than some recent sends, but not enough distinguishes it from 7/26's near-identical subject and layout to call it a step forward.

## What's working

- The back-to-school hero is clear and on-theme: personalized "STANFORD" backpack, lunch box, and desk accessories styled together.
- The category grid (backpacks, lunch boxes, duffel bags, pencil cases, food storage, water bottles) is a genuinely useful, skimmable back-to-school shopping index.
- "Shop the Sale" CTA button is high-contrast and easy to find.
- The "we love these for you" rail shows real product photography with visible prices, not placeholder content.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- The "up to 50% off summer sale" ribbon directly above the back-to-school hero undercuts the subject's "up to 20% off" — same issue flagged in the 7/26 audit of this brand, unresolved two sends later.
- No deadline or urgency anywhere in the sale messaging — "the sale is on" with no end date gives no reason to act today.
- Way too many competing CTAs (Shop the Sale, Shop Desks & Chairs, Shop All Gear, Enter Now, Shop Now, Learn More, Download, Book It) — nothing after the hero is prioritized.
- The credit card pitch and app-download block feel bolted on to a back-to-school email and dilute the category focus.
- Nearly identical subject/offer to the 7/26 send ("Up to 20% off our Back-to-School Sale!") — subscribers who saw that one have little reason to open this.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the "up to 50% off summer sale" ribbon from the top of this send entirely, or move it well below the back-to-school module — right now it's the first thing readers see and it contradicts the subject line.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the back-to-school offer ("ends Sunday," "through 8/15") — "the sale is on" has zero urgency.
- 3. Cut the send down to 2-3 modules max: hero + category grid + one secondary CTA (either the giveaway or new arrivals, not both plus the credit card plus the app plus the design service).
- 4. Differentiate this send from 7/26's near-identical subject/offer — vary the discount framing or lead with a specific category (e.g., backpacks) instead of repeating "up to 20% off."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Backpacks, lunch boxes & more — up to 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Everything on the back-to-school list, up to 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Personalized backpacks, lunch boxes, and desk sets for the whole list.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New fall arrivals from $29 — plus your shot at $4,999 in prizes.`
