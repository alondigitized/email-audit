# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is *my* brand — warm woods, minimal interiors, pendant lamps I've been saving to Pinterest for two years. Crate & Barrel clearly knows its customer and the editorial vibe is right. But the very first thing I'm greeted with is a full-screen email capture modal asking for both my address AND my phone number, which is a lot of commitment before I've even seen the homepage properly.

## What's working

- **"everyone's five-star favorite" hero**: social-proof framing hits the right note for a registry shopper who is doing research and wants validation before committing
- **Lifestyle photography behind the modal**: warm walnut tones, organic shapes, layered shelving — this is exactly the aesthetic I come here for; it immediately tells me I'm in the right place
- **Rewards banner ("Earn 10% Back in Rewards Dollars")**: visible at the very top without scrolling, which matters for a full-price-tolerant shopper like me — it reframes spending as earning
- **Navigation tabs**: "Dining & Bar" and "Kitchen" are right in the nav rail, making my category one tap away
- **Product cards with anchored price points**: "Coffee Tables Starting at $348" sets expectations before I commit a single click

## What's weak

- **Email + phone capture modal on first load**: asking for both email AND mobile number before I've seen a single product is aggressive; the "Maybe Later" microcopy makes it worse because it sounds like there's a penalty for declining
- **"Shop All Arhaus & Terracasa" CTA**: I had to squint to parse this — Arhaus is a competitor brand and this reads like I'm being sent to a collab I didn't ask for, not my core CB catalog
- **"Shop Five-Stars" CTA**: vague enough that I'm not sure what category or product type I'm being dropped into
- **No registry-specific hook above the fold**: I am a recently-registered shopper and there is zero "manage your registry / complete the look" nudge visible — a huge miss for someone who opened this site specifically to shop off a list
- **Outdoor sale module at bottom**: "up to 30% off outdoor furniture" is not my category at all; it fills visible real estate without delivering for me

## Recommendations

- **Kill the dual-field modal at entry or reduce it to email only**: the phone field is a conversion killer — either make it opt-in checkbox or remove it entirely; test a slide-up after first scroll instead
- **Add a "Continue Your Registry" or "Registry Hub" banner above the fold**: a recently-registered couple is one of the highest-LTV segments on this site; even a slim bar reading "Your Registry · 12 items added" would drive immediate deeper engagement
- **Rewrite the hero CTAs**: swap "Shop All Arhaus & Terracasa" for something direct like "Shop the Five-Star Edit" or "Shop Bestsellers"; keep it brand-owned language
- **Surface a dining/tabletop category tile or rail**: I came here for modern minimal dinnerware and walnut cutting boards; a single "Shop Dining" lifestyle card above the fold would cut my path to purchase by two taps
