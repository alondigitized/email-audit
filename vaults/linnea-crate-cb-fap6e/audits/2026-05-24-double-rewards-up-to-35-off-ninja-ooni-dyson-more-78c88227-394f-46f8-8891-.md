## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2`
  Most clients strip `@font-face` anyway, but webmail/Apple Mail users will fall back silently. CDN ACL likely blocks cross-origin font fetch — should be served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` or an open CDN path.
- **Missing alt text** — 45+ `<img>` elements lack `alt` attributes, including hero/product images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`. Image-blocking clients (Outlook default) will render empty rectangles. ## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header missing** in captured headers — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail). Verify at the SMTP source that both `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:…>, <https://…>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) are set. Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (Feb 2024) require one-click for senders >5k/day.
