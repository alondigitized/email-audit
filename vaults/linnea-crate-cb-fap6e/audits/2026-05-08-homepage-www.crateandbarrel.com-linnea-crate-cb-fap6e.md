# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is clearly a brand that knows its lane — warm naturals, editorial photography, modern-minimal sensibility all the way. Right now it's pushing a spring outdoor furniture sale hard, which makes sense for May, but I opened this app thinking about my registry and instead I'm staring at a full-screen email capture modal before I've even seen the homepage properly. The bones are there, the vibe is right, but the entry experience is doing too much.

## What's working

- **"Managing Registry" in the secondary nav** — I spotted it immediately. That's exactly the shortcut I want. One tap to my list.
- **"Save 30% Back in Rewards Dollars" top banner** — I'm full-price tolerant but I'm not going to turn down rewards. Seeing this before anything else sets the right tone.
- **Hero photography** — the outdoor seating scene is warm, editorial, aspirational. The earthy tones and natural textures are exactly the aesthetic I come to C&B for. It doesn't feel like a discount retailer.
- **"the spring refresh event: up to 30% off" promo strip** — timely, specific, believable for a spring push.
- **"built-to-last bestsellers" rail below the fold** — "built-to-last" is the kind of copy that resonates with me. I'm buying for keeps, not to replace in two years.

## What's weak

- **The email modal fires immediately** — I haven't seen a single product and I'm already being asked for my email AND my phone number. Asking for mobile number upfront feels aggressive. This is not a Sephora Beauty Insider situation where I know the value exchange.
- **"up to 30% off outdoor furniture" hero — wrong category for a registry shopper** — I care about Dining & Bar, Kitchen, Bedding. Outdoor furniture is a want-someday purchase, not a right-now one. The hero isn't speaking to me.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible anywhere** — for a registry purchase that might be $200–$400, I want to know shipping math before I tap into a product.
- **Promo strip fine print is unreadable at mobile size** — the text below "the spring refresh event" looks like it has exclusions but I literally cannot read it in this viewport.
- **No registry-progress or welcome-back moment** — since I'm a registered user, I'd love to see "Welcome back, Linnea" or "Your registry: 12 items remaining." Nothing like that on load.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the email capture modal by 30+ seconds or one scroll** — or replace the immediate interstitial with a sticky bottom bar ("Save 10% — enter your email"). Let me see the homepage first before you ask for my phone number.
- **Add a registry entry point to the hero or above-fold area** — something like "Back to your registry →" as a persistent banner for logged-in users would convert me instantly. The "Managing Registry" nav link is easy to miss.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the top banner** — swap or rotate "Save 30% Back in Rewards Dollars" to also show "Free shipping on orders over $X." That information actively moves me toward checkout.
- **Introduce a "New to Your Registry" module below the hero** — pull in recently-added or trending registry items. It turns a browse into a mission.
