# Going fast: grab school gear at 20% off →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "up to 20% off back-to-school" promo Crate & Kids already sent on 2026-08-02 ("School starts SOON! Up to 20% off essentials 🎒"), just re-skinned with fresh urgency copy ("Going fast," "Selling Fast!," "time is running out") and a slightly rearranged hero. The backpack-wall hero is genuinely strong — dozens of personalized backpacks (names embroidered right on them) is a great scroll-stopper and it's directly on-theme with the subject line.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email pivots hard from back-to-school kids gear into a "we love these for you" module stacked with full-price nursery furniture — a $1,499 dresser, a $1,300 stroller travel system, an $899 glider — none of which has anything to do with backpacks or school. A second competing offer ("up to 50% off summer sale") shows up mid-email too, muddying which discount actually applies to what. The hero does its job, but the body reads like three different campaigns stitched together.
- Given this is a near-repeat of a promo sent two days ago with the same discount tier and a similar hero concept, cadence is the real weak point here, not creative execution.

## What's working

- The hero backpack wall is personalized (names like "Hayden," "Benjamin," "Alice" stitched on) and immediately legible as back-to-school inventory.
- "Shop Kids Gear" CTA is a clear, specific, well-contrasted button directly under the hero copy.
- The backpacks/duffle bags module pairs category shots with concrete info: "Ships Free‡ & Starts at $39."
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images end to end.

## What's weak

- "We love these for you" recommends nursery furniture (dresser, stroller, glider) at full price — totally off-theme for a back-to-school send and a jarring demographic mismatch (infant gear vs. school-age kids).
- A second, larger discount ("up to 50% off summer sale") appears mid-email, competing with and undercutting the headline "up to 20% off" offer.
- Urgency copy ("Going fast," "Selling Fast!," "time is running out") has no actual deadline attached — no date, no countdown, so it reads as generic pressure rather than credible scarcity.
- The Crate Card module ("the card you love just got better") is a new-cardholder pitch, not an existing loyalty perk, but is dressed up like a benefit reminder.
- This is essentially a repeat of 2026-08-02's "School starts SOON! Up to 20% off essentials" — same discount tier, same backpack-wall concept, sent two days later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the nursery furniture from "we love these for you" and replace it with school-adjacent add-ons (lunch boxes, water bottles, folders) — the mismatch is the single biggest drag on this send.
- 2. Pick one offer and lead with it end to end — drop the "up to 50% off summer sale" banner from this email or move it to its own send so the 20% off back-to-school message doesn't get diluted.
- 3. Attach a real deadline to the urgency copy ("Sale ends Sunday 8/9") instead of vague "going fast" language.
- 4. Space this out from the 8/2 back-to-school send — two near-identical 20%-off/backpack-wall emails within 48 hours risks fatigue for subscribers who already saw this offer.
