# It's on! Black Friday deals & free shipping inside
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids' Black Friday catch-all, and it is the same send as the last several. The hero says "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" over a nursery photo with a single "Shop the Sale" button. Below it sits an eight-tile category grid (kids furniture, baby furniture, toys, nod chairs, bedding, playroom, holiday decor, baby gear), a second "Shop the Sale" button, a Babyletto and Namesake feature, and a "gifts made for wish lists" banner. The long tail then runs through a Feeding America block, a "we love these for you" product row, a credit-card promo, an app download, TheDesignDesk, and a heavy footer.
- The offer is clear and the hero is strong. But the render is about 8,200px tall and tries to do everything. The eye gets the 50% off, then is pulled into a grid where every tile carries a different "up to" number (50%, 30%, 20%). It is almost a copy of the 2026-11-22 "Black Friday is is on! Shop our deals now →" send. The brand has sent roughly ten near-identical blasts this week. Today it already sent two other Black Friday emails: the toy-focused one and the gift guide. A subscriber would see this as the fourth near-identical promo in two days.
- Verdict: competent and clean, but redundant and overstuffed. It is a 6-7 email that would lose to its own sibling sends in the inbox.

## What's working

- The hero gives one concrete offer, "Up to 50% off + free shipping", with a visible "Shop the Sale" button.
- The category grid is cleanly laid out, with captions under each tile and no visible broken images or overlaps.
- The seasonal imagery (stockings, holiday decor, a tree) fits the Black Friday window.
- The "we love these for you" row shows named products with prices and strike-through sale prices, such as the Babyletto dresser and the Nuna stroller.
- The "Ships Free" tags on some tiles reinforce the free-shipping offer.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long. Eight category tiles, then Babyletto, gifts, Feeding America, a credit card, the app, and TheDesignDesk all compete for attention.
- Tile discounts vary (50%, 30%, 20%) and every one says "up to", so the headline 50% reads as best case. The fine print is also dense at the bottom.
- The Feeding America block and the credit-card promo dilute the sale message in the middle of a conversion email.
- It is nearly a repeat of the 2026-11-22 omnibus send and the toy and gift sends from earlier today. There is no new reason to open it.
- The hero subcopy ("Select styles + free shipping on orders $79+") is small and low-contrast over the photo. The $79 threshold is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid, one featured product row, and the footer. Move the Feeding America, credit card, app, and TheDesignDesk blocks out of the send.
- 2. Put the free-shipping threshold ($79+) in the hero headline area at readable size.
- 3. Pick a single discount tier per tile, or label tiles "30% off" instead of "up to 30% off" where it's true.
- 4. Make the sends distinct. If this is the omnibus send, say what's new today, such as a countdown or a deal that ends tonight, rather than repeating the headline.
- 5. Add one credible deadline, such as "Ends Monday midnight", to the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + free shipping ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nursery & kids' furniture: up to 50% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, bedding, toys & more, plus free shipping on orders $79+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Black Friday prices are live. Shop top-rated baby gear first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–driven XHTML table layout with 78 click-tracked links. QA found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings), but about 35 images have no alt text and the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom. Header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 78 links were skipped by the QA probe because they use tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and link-to-image alignment are **unverified**, not confirmed good.
- **Third-party identifier pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1015f5a8&n=1…5` beacons (LiveRamp) are embedded. The `s=` value begins with the same hash as the Movable Ink `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd1…` parameter. The same per-recipient identifier appears to be passed to a third-party domain, which is a privacy and disclosure concern. I can't tell from the truncated source whether the identifier is a hashed email.
- **No other link defects:** The source shows no broken or malformed hrefs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA run flagged about 35 `<img>` elements, split into two groups:
  - **Content and layout images (about 17):** these are hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `9808d754-…jpg` and `b14e1268-…jpg`) and `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. They also include the `s7d5.scene7.com` deals-grid hero `2026%5F1122%5FCBK%5FBF%5FDealsGrid%5FREMIX%5FHero…` and the gift-guide GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, the deals grid and hero will have no text equivalent.
  - **Spacer and pixel images:** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and the tracking pixels `o.gif`, `448796.gif` (×5) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/82c8f4a4-…`. These should carry `alt=""` so they are explicitly decorative. A missing attribute is announced inconsistently.
  - **Movable Ink dynamic images:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times without alt. If it renders product or offer content, that content is inaccessible.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents user zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Source hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype (it should be `&amp;`).
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in production.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, redundantly.
  - The global `*{line-height:100%}` rule and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are blunt resets that can override intended spacing in some clients.
- **Outlook support:** MSO conditional blocks and `o:PixelsPerInch` are present, and `lang="en"` is set. Neither raises an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. The source is truncated, so the body copy and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Both are flagged as not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are truly absent, the message lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper setup, but that is not verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** The footer was cut off by truncation, so they were not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 78 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated source doesn't show final destination URLs or UTM parameters. The subject promises "Black Friday deals & free shipping". No landing page was checked to confirm it matches that offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix alt text.** Add descriptive alt to the deals-grid hero and content images (the `m/1/…` and `m/47/…` files), and `alt=""` to the spacer and tracking pixels. Make sure the Movable Ink `rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` blocks emit alt text.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Verify headers on a direct copy.** Check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results on a message received outside the relay before concluding there is a compliance problem.
4. **Review the `sr.rlcdn.com` beacons.** Confirm that the identifier is not a plain hash of the recipient's email and that it is disclosed in the privacy policy.
5. **Extend QA to tracking links.** Resolve the redirects (or follow them via the ESP) so UTM parameters and landing-page status can be checked.
6. **Clean up the template.** Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment and the duplicate `format-detection` metas.
