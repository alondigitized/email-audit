# ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, honest urgency send. "Ends today: up to 50% off furniture" lands on a nursery and kids furniture sale, backed by a bunk bed, a glider and a dresser, each with a Shop Now button. The deadline is credible because the sale really does end today. The top third works. The hero bed shows the sale price against the regular price, the headline sits right under it, and a single "Shop the Sale" button follows.
- The email then runs about 9,000px. It has two buttons per product, a TheDesignDesk appointment pitch that appears twice, a Crate Card block, an app download block and a dense legal footer. Everything after the first fold dilutes the "ends today" message. It is also the second furniture-sale send today, after "Hosting Thanksgiving? Up to 30% off dining furniture" (7/10). The 10/19 free-shipping push ("Hours left for free shipping!") showed the same fatigue risk. The bigger "up to 50%" here is a fair escalation over the 30% in the earlier send.
- Judgement: a solid 7. The offer and deadline are strong, but the email is far too long, and the subject and sender name don't say it's kids and nursery furniture.

## What's working

- The subject and the hero agree: "ends today! up to 50% off furniture" is the headline, with a "Shop the Sale" button right below it.
- The top hero caption shows a specific sale price against the regular price, which makes the discount concrete.
- Each product block has a name, price, a short description and a Shop Now button.
- One block carries a customer quote ("The chair is absolutely beautiful…"), which adds a little social proof.
- The "we love these for you" product grid gives a lower-commitment way to browse.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long (about 9,370px), with six or more distinct modules after the hero. The urgency fades well before the footer.
- Each product block has two buttons (Shop Now plus a "Shop the Nursery Bedding Sale" or "Shop the Furniture Sale" link). That splits the click decision.
- TheDesignDesk pitch appears twice, and the Crate Card and app-download blocks come after it. These are off-offer and compete with the sale.
- The subject says "furniture" with no mention of kids or nursery, while the sender reads "Crate & Barrel Kids SALE". A furniture shopper can't tell this is nursery and kids pieces.
- The legal and fine-print footer is dense. "Up to" pricing is hard to verify, and exclusions aren't visible near the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, three products and one footer. Drop the Crate Card, the app block and the second TheDesignDesk pitch. This is the biggest lift, because every module after the hero works against "ends today".
- 2. Give each product one button, with specific copy such as "Shop the bunk bed" or "Shop gliders". Use one sale-level button at the top and one at the bottom.
- 3. Put the deadline in the hero itself, for example "Ends tonight at midnight PT". Better still, add a live countdown timer, as the 10/20 Bedroom & Nursery send did.
- 4. Say "kids" or "nursery" in the subject so the segment matches the content.
- 5. Show the sale price in large type next to each product name, not as a small caption. State exclusions in one line under the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 50% off nursery & kids furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bunk beds, gliders & dressers: 50% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale prices on bunk beds, gliders and dressers disappear at midnight. Shop before it ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Refresh for fall and save on beds, dressers and cribs. Last day to get up to 50% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with an MSO conditional block and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed problems are about 40 images with missing alt text and several leftover template artifacts. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay capture, so they are unverified. The source is truncated, so everything below the `<head>` is covered only through the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links.** 84 links were skipped as "Tracking/click-redirect domains". Destination validity, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unchecked, so this is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate fires of one pixel, differing only in `n`. `rlcdn.com` is a third-party ad-data domain. The `s=` value is a 64-hex-character token that matches the prefix of `mi_u=dbe8da71…` on the first-party pixels. That suggests a per-recipient identifier passed to a third party, but the source doesn't show what it encodes.
- **First-party tracking pixels.** There are `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel), `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/0eabadb8-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, and six repeats of `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`. The repeats are probably dynamic-content render pings, but the source doesn't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed.** The QA check flagged about 44 images. About 25 are content images under `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…jpg`, for example `4cab4764-ac46-431b-805e-7be49143e621.jpg` and `b13a454d-101e-4997-8330-9c416790d9d5.jpg`, plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. If these carry product or offer content, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing. The check doesn't say whether `alt` is absent or empty (`alt=""`).
- **Decorative and tracking images.** Two images are spacers (`https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`). The rest are tracking pixels. These should carry an explicit `alt=""`. The check treats them the same as content images.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem on mobile.
- **Redundant or broken head markup:**
  - Duplicate charset declarations (`http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>`).
  - Four `format-detection` meta tags that conflict or overlap (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - A leftover template placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - An unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - A `<title>` that doesn't match the subject line.
- **Small text.** The `.f10` class sets `font-size:10px` on mobile, which is below common legibility minimums. I can't tell from the truncated source where it's used.
- **Global resets.** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element. They can override later styles unexpectedly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Check the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated source doesn't show a footer unsubscribe link or postal address, so neither is verifiable.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 84 links were skipped as click-redirect URLs, and the truncated source shows no UTM parameters or final destinations. The mismatch between the "Kids" sender name and the broad "furniture" subject can't be checked without the landing URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Clean the head: keep one charset declaration and one consolidated `format-detection` tag, delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment, and write `&amp;` in the title.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as defects.
5. Resolve the redirect links to their final URLs to verify destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Confirm what the `rlcdn.com` `s=` token encodes, and whether five duplicate fires are intentional.
7. Rerun the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, the unsubscribe link and any inline `font-size:10px` usage.
