# Earn 2x Rewards & get up to 50% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This Crate & Barrel Kids send stacks two offers, up to 50% off and Double Rewards (20% back on a Crate Card), into one very long email. The top works. The hero is a dark green plaid banner reading "the fall sales event: up to 50% off" with a single "Shop Now" button. A Crate Card Double Rewards callout sits directly under it. The render is 680×8953 and was downscaled to about 152px wide, so body copy, prices and fine print are mostly unreadable. I'm judging structure and hierarchy.
- The weakness is repetition and length. "Last Double Rewards this year!" appears at least three times: the top card, a mid-email card, and a red banner. A credit-card promo ("the card you love just got better") closes out the body. After the hero, the email becomes a grid of "up to 50% off" categories and a recycled product carousel. The 2x Rewards offer is a card-holder benefit, so it adds nothing for most readers.
- This is a competent, offer-first promotional email that is bloated and repetitive. It is fine as a seasonal blast. It is not a targeted one, and it is nearly identical to the 2026-11-08 "These ship quick AND earn you Double Rewards ✅" send from the same day. That is the cadence problem a real subscriber would notice.

## What's working

- The hero is offer-first: a large "up to 50% off" headline with one clear "Shop Now" button.
- The subject matches the hero, leading with the offer and the Rewards hook, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The category grid ("up to 50% off furniture / bedding / rugs / playroom") lets a shopper jump to the room or product type they want.
- The red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner carries a credible deadline (through Nov. 17) and gives urgency.
- Lifestyle photography is warm and on-brand for a kids and nursery audience.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards message repeats at least three times, which reads as padding and dilutes the 50% off hero.
- The email is about 9,000px tall. The hero is strong, but everything after it is a long scroll of near-identical "up to 50% off" tiles.
- The credit-card Rewards offer matters only to Crate Card holders, but it takes prime space near the top and again in the body.
- The recycled product carousel ("we love these for you") shows strollers and gear with no visible reviews or sizing. It feels generic rather than personalized.
- Too many CTAs compete: Shop Now, Shop Top Categories, Learn More, Shop All Bestsellers, Shop Now (baby gear), Learn More (card), Download Now, Book a Free Appointment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Rewards messaging to one block, in the hero or just below it, and drop the two repeats. Move the card promo (Learn More) to the footer.
- 2. Trim the body to the hero, six category tiles and one product row. That cuts the length by about 40%.
- 3. Give the product row price and review stars so it can drive clicks instead of just filling space.
- 4. Say one concrete thing in the preheader, such as the deadline or the best category deal.
- 5. Hold sends like this to once a week. Having this and the "ship quick AND earn Double Rewards" send land on the same day invites fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off nursery & kids' rooms ends Nov. 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% back in Rewards + 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding and baby gear marked down through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year. Crate Card holders earn 20% back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive CSS, but about 37 images are flagged for missing alt text. The viewport and text-size rules also cause accessibility problems. The source is truncated and the relay captured no auth or List-Unsubscribe headers, so compliance, personalization and UTM checks can't be fully confirmed. The automated QA pass rate was 57% (0 issues, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 80 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, and no broken links are confirmed.
- **Duplicate third-party pixels:** The same `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…f5a8` pixel fires five times (`n=1` to `n=5`). The same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixel appears six times. Both add redundant requests.
- **Shared recipient identifier:** The `mi_u=dbe8da718e2997cce014546f16c5722eaa3c322f28d40f0a8386f8fd101…` value appears to be the same hash as the rlcdn `s=` parameter. That would mean a recipient-level identifier is passed to a third-party domain (rlcdn.com, which is LiveRamp). Confirm that the privacy policy covers this.
- **Other pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/32e26386-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are additional open or tracking beacons.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 37 images are flagged, in three groups.
  - About 22 are content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|.gif` (for example `26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif`, `a4c4b37d-…gif`) and `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. Screen readers and images-off clients get no content or link context from these.
  - One is the spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - The rest are tracking pixels (rlcdn, mi, dv). These need `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so they are treated as decorative.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing blocked:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` values and prevent user text resizing on iOS.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The two have equal specificity, so the later rule wins at 768px and above and the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is not valid under the declared XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type. `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`).
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` only, so the email makes no dark-mode accommodation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) were found. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper setup but not proof of it.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 80 links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects and weren't resolved, so the UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. The subject promises "2x Rewards & up to 50% off", but the destination pages can't be checked against it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~22 content images. Use `alt=""` with explicit 1×1 dimensions on all tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `body` rules.
3. De-duplicate the rlcdn pixel (five hits) and the `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel (six hits).
4. Review the hashed identifier shared with `sr.rlcdn.com` against the privacy disclosures.
5. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), consolidate the `format-detection` and charset metas, fix the conflicting `img max-width` media queries, and remove the template placeholder comment.
6. Re-check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results on the raw message, bypassing the relay. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check the footer, merge tokens and UTM parameters on resolved links.
