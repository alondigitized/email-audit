# Ends soon: Up to 50% off furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent furniture-sale send, but it repeats today's earlier mail. The subject, "Ends soon: Up to 50% off furniture!", gives a concrete offer and urgency. The body delivers it with a hero bedroom shot, an "ends soon! up to 50% off furniture*" headline, and a "Shop the Sale" button. Below that are four large product blocks, each with a sale price, a "Reg." price and a customer review quote.
- The weakness is stacking. The top banner ("final hours! free shipping on orders $99+") competes with the furniture sale, and the lead image is a small framed shot rather than a full-width hero. This is also at least the third Crate & Kids send today. The earlier ones were "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" and "But what's my holiday aesthetic??", and they repeat the same free-shipping and bedroom/nursery event. A subscriber would see a pile-up, not a new reason to click.
- One limit on this review: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can't read the fine print or the exact preheader. I'm judging layout, hierarchy and visible copy only. Verdict: solid execution, tired cadence, and a mixed message about which offer is the real one.

## What's working

- The headline "ends soon! up to 50% off furniture*" is large and clear, with one simple supporting line beneath it.
- Product blocks show sale and regular prices, so the savings are easy to see.
- Review quotes under products, such as "Solid, well-built bed", add social proof that matters for big-ticket furniture.
- Each product has its own "Shop Now" button, so clicks go to specific items instead of one generic page.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with no visible broken images or overlaps, and the photography is consistent with the brand's look.

## What's weak

- The free-shipping banner at the top competes with the 50%-off furniture offer, so the email has two headlines.
- The CTA ladder is repetitive. "Shop Now" is followed by a grey "Shop the Sale" or "Shop the Furniture Sale" under every product, which dilutes the choice.
- "Ends soon" gives no actual deadline. The earlier sends today already said "ends today", so this one reads as vague and recycled.
- The email is very long. Past the four hero products it adds a "we love these for you" grid, a Crate Card promo, an app download, a design-desk pitch and a footer nav, all after the offer has been made.
- There is a lot of fine print, with an asterisk on the headline and dense legal text at the bottom. That hurts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer. Either make furniture the only headline and drop the free-shipping banner, or send a combined "furniture sale + free shipping, ends tonight" email with a single hero.
- 2. Replace "ends soon" with a real deadline ("ends tonight at midnight") and add a countdown timer, as the brand did in the 2026-10-18 "2 days only!" send.
- 3. Cut the secondary "Shop the Sale" buttons, keep one primary button per product, and trim the email to the hero plus about four products.
- 4. Move the Crate Card and app promos into a separate send, or at least shrink them to a one-line strip.
- 5. Put the savings in the product callout, for example "Save $X" or "50% off", instead of relying on the sale/Reg. price pair.
