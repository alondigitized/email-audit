# The birthday shop is OPEN →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Kids opens the birthday gift shop with a clean, well-organized age-segmented catalog — kids, toddlers, babies — each with a "wishing for" mini-story and clear category CTAs (Shop Kids Gifts, Shop Toddler Gifts, Shop Baby Gifts). It's the same disciplined format that scored well on the 2026-09-16 Halloween shop send ("Spooky season starts NOW 👻→", 7/10) — a genuine seasonal/occasion kickoff rather than a bestsellers rehash.
- Where it loses points is the back half: the email pivots hard away from "birthday shop" into a Venus Williams collab plug and an unrelated "up to 20% off baby essentials" sale — the same collab-churn pattern flagged in the C&B history (5/10 KitchenAid send) and the same baby-essentials promo already sent standalone earlier today (6/10). A birthday-themed email shouldn't be doing double duty as a collab ad and a baby-sale ad in the same scroll.
- Net: solid top half, diluted bottom half. This lands as a mid-pack send — good structure, but it doesn't stay on-message for its own subject line.

## What's working

- The hero clearly states the occasion ("the birthday shop") with a matching illustrated dollhouse/toy scene and a single "Shop Now" CTA.
- Segmentation by age (kids/toddlers/babies) mirrors how a gift-shopper actually thinks, each with its own hero image and CTA button.
- "Exclusive," "Personalize It," and "Bestseller" tags give quick scannable signals on toddler products.
- The "what everyone's wishing for" section closes the browse funnel with Mood Chairs / All Toys / Personalization Shop CTAs — a good universal catch-all.
- No render bugs — images, type, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The email drifts off-topic two-thirds down: a Venus Williams collab banner and a "20% off baby essentials, limited time" sale block have nothing to do with the birthday shop premise.
- The baby essentials sale duplicates the "Up to 20% off the BEST baby essentials starts now →" email already sent today (6/10) — same offer, same day, different wrapper.
- No visible pricing or discount anywhere in the actual birthday shop section — it's all browse CTAs, no concrete offer, so the "birthday shop" itself has zero urgency or incentive to click now versus later.
- "We love these for you" product grid at the bottom has prices but no birthday framing — reads like a generic recommendation carousel bolted onto an unrelated email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Venus Williams collab and baby-essentials sale blocks from this send entirely — they undercut the single-occasion focus that made the Halloween shop email work. Let the birthday shop carry its own email.
- 2. Add a concrete hook to the hero — a "gifts from $X" price anchor, free gift wrap, or a birthday-specific perk (e.g., free personalization) — so there's an actual reason to click today.
- 3. Move "what everyone's wishing for" (mood chairs/personalization) higher, right after the hero, since it's the most universal, highest-intent CTA block.
- 4. If cross-promoting the baby sale is required, gate it to a P.S. line or single small banner, not a full duplicate section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Birthday gifts, sorted by age — shop now →`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: the birthday shop, from baby to big kid`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Personalized gifts, playroom favorites, and free wrap on every order`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From first birthdays to big-kid milestones — gifts they'll actually use`
