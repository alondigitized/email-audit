# NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance send from Crate & Barrel Kids, and the hero makes the offer clear. "up to 60% off clearance" sits over an autumn-leaves photo, with a "Shop Clearance" button underneath. The subject matches the hero, which is good. But it is the third near-identical markdown push in two days. The 2026-10-08 "Up to 60% off NEW markdowns!" send had the same offer, so this one reads as a repeat.
- The body drifts after the hero. A second banner promotes the "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event: Up to 50% off", and it has its own button. That competes with the 60% headline. Then comes a "furniture in as fast as 7 days" block, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo and TheDesignDesk. There are at least five competing CTAs. The product grid is the useful part, but only two of the six items show a markdown price, so the clearance promise is thinly backed.
- Overall it's competent but unfocused. The hero is strong, and the rest dilutes it. The tiny legal block at the bottom hides the real clearance and event terms.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 60% off clearance", with a visible "Shop Clearance" button.
- The subject and hero match, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The category button grid under the hero (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting) makes it easy to go straight to a category.
- The product grid shows real prices, including strikethrough markdowns on the glider ($764.15) and the crib mattress ($314.95).
- The "furniture in as fast as 7 days" block answers a real new-parent worry about delivery.

## What's weak

- Two overlapping offers (60% clearance and 50% Semi-Annual event) split the message.
- The hero subtext ("Selling fast! Shop now") sits on a busy leaf photo and is hard to read.
- Only two of the six grid products show a sale price, so the clearance promise isn't backed up.
- The credit card, app and TheDesignDesk blocks pile on after the product grid and dilute the primary action.
- The dense fine print at the bottom is small, and the clearance and event terms are hard to find.
- This is the second markdown email in two days, so the cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Put the 60% clearance in the hero and move the Semi-Annual event to a later send, or fold it in as a single line.
- 2. Make the product grid clearance-only, with strikethrough and sale prices on every item. This is the biggest lift, because shoppers click when they see a price drop.
- 3. Fix the hero legibility with a solid scrim or bolder type behind "Selling fast! Shop now".
- 4. Cut the credit card, app and design-service blocks, or move them to the footer, so the email has one primary path.
- 5. Vary the creative from the prior day's markdown email, or pull the send back to one markdown email a week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`mi.` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with all 66 links behind tracking redirects. The main confirmed issues are about 20 content images with no alt text and a few markup defects in the visible `<head>`. Compliance and authentication results are unverified, because the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains), so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains are verified.
- The HTML source is truncated, so I can't confirm UTM parameters, link text or `href` targets.
- There are five identical `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=dbe8da71…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons. They are third-party (LiveRamp) pixels carrying a per-recipient hashed identifier in the query string. The `mi_u=dbe8da71…` hash is also appended to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. That is expected for tracking, but the pixels add 5 extra third-party requests.
- `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (confirmed by QA):** the hero `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FHERO.gif` on s7d5.scene7.com, plus about 11 images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (`df8678c4…jpg`, `0c830e43…jpg`, `77f445ee…jpg`, `c48d0b48…jpg` and others). The `m/47/bbf6f9d4…png` image is also missing alt text. With images blocked, the email's offer and product content is unlabeled.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels (low severity):** the `rlcdn.com` GIFs, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8b62532c…`. These should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. I can't tell from the QA output whether they have an empty alt or no alt attribute.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&` (it should be `&amp;`). The `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset tag.
- **Redundant markup:** `format-detection` is declared three times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
- **Leftover template text:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and the `<!----->` comment fragments are malformed.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** and `*{line-height:100%}` are broad resets that could cause unintended inheritance. I can't confirm any actual breakage from the truncated source.
- `lang="en"` is set, and the MSO conditional blocks are present and well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` hash is resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** QA notes the relay may not capture the header, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN):** this is also unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it is worth checking against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and an alignment check requires raw headers.
- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so I can't evaluate them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No data. All links are tracking redirects that were not probed, and the source is truncated. UTM presence and landing page alignment cannot be assessed from this input.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the clearance hero GIF and the product tiles. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The 57% pass rate is partly an artifact of the relay stripping headers.
5. Resolve a sample of the 66 tracking links with redirect-following to verify final destinations and UTM parameters, and feed the full (untruncated) HTML into the audit.
