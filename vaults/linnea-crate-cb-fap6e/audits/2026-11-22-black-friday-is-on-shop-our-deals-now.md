# Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids' Black Friday omnibus send. It is clean and well organised, with a clear "Up to 50% off + free shipping" hero and a tidy grid of category tiles. It's a competent catalog blast, but it is almost the same email as the "Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!" send on 11/20 and the kitchen and toys variants from the past two days. A subscriber who has seen about ten of these will skim it.
- The grid does the work: kids furniture, baby furniture, toys, bedding, playroom, holiday decor, rugs and baby gear, each with a specific "up to X% off" label. After that the email sprawls. A Babylist/Namesake panel, a personalization shop, a product carousel, a gift-card tease, an app-download pitch, a TheDesignDesk pitch and a store locator all follow, with the legal block at the bottom. The offer is strong, but the long tail dilutes it, and the "Black Friday is on!" opener is the same line used on prior sends.
- Verdict: solid execution, low differentiation, and too long for the point it makes.

## What's working

- The hero states "Up to 50% off + free shipping" with a visible "Shop the Sale" button.
- Each tile carries its own discount (up to 50% on kids furniture, up to 30% on toys, up to 20% on holiday decor), so you can see where the deal is without clicking.
- The photography is warm, seasonal and on-brand, with holiday styling that fits the date.
- "Ships Free" callouts on the bedding and baby gear tiles reinforce the shipping offer.
- The kids and baby focus is clear, and the Kids sender name signals relevance.

## What's weak

- It's the same "Black Friday is on!" omnibus as earlier sends, with no new angle or hook.
- The email is extremely long, with at least six stacked secondary modules after the grid (Babylist, personalization, product picks, gift card, app, TheDesignDesk).
- The hero is visually busy. The headline sits over a photo with a lot of texture, so contrast looks weak. The small subline under it is hard to read.
- There are two competing CTAs in the Babylist block ("Shop Kiwi Striped Glider" and "Shop the Sale"), and the page has many CTAs overall. No single hero product is featured.
- The dense legal fine print is heavy, and the multiple exclusions weaken the sense that the offer is straightforward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the grid and one product row. Move the app, TheDesignDesk, gift card and store locator content into a separate evergreen footer or other sends.
- 2. Put a dark overlay or solid color panel behind the hero headline so the offer reads at a glance, and enlarge the shipping minimum text.
- 3. Feature one hero product (for example the glider or the play kitchen) with a price and a "Shop the deal" link, in place of generic "Shop the Sale" repeated.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday midnight", in the hero.
- 5. Collapse the legal copy behind a "See details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids & baby up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nursery, playroom, bedtime: Black Friday deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding and toys, with free shipping on $79+. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cribs, gliders and play kitchens: this week's best prices on kids' favorites.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: it has `lang="en"`, Outlook VML/MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint. Its real issues are missing alt text on roughly 20 content images and a few source-level defects. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the AgentMail relay, and the 78 tracked links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 78 links were skipped as click-redirect/tracking domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unknown.
- **Repeated pixels (informational):**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are five third-party (LiveRamp) pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/102ac91d-…?mi_cid=…` are open/tracking beacons.
  - These add request overhead and depend on image loading. Apple Mail Privacy Protection and image-blocking clients will skew open data.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images.** The QA check flagged about 20 images, including:
  - the hero `2026%5F1122%5FCBK%5FBF%5FDealsGrid%5FREMIX%5FHero…` (scene7.com)
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - about 15 images at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`
  - `2026%5F1105%5FCBK%5FPersonalizedGiftShop%5FSeconda…`
  - the spacer `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`
  
  With images blocked, the Black Friday offer and CTAs would be invisible to screen readers and image-off clients.
- **Pixels and spacers:** The tracking pixels and the spacer should carry `alt=""` explicitly. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or empty.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom. The `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations (in `*{}` and `body`) also prevent text resizing in iOS.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` and should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and leftover tags:**
  - `<meta charset>` is duplicated alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - The template placeholder `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` were left in the head.
- **Dead CSS:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both have the same specificity, and the later rule applies at 768px and wider.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u` and `s` parameters on pixel URLs are populated per-recipient values. The remainder of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported absent. The QA tool itself says this may be a relay capture artifact, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that risks Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender non-compliance for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a brand-aligned subdomain, which is a good sign, but alignment is not verified.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 78 links are redirect-wrapped and were not resolved, so there is no evidence on UTM parameters or landing page match.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, starting with the hero and deals-grid images, and use `alt=""` on pixels and spacers.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers, bypassing the relay, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original delivery.
3. Resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links with a follow-redirects probe, to check UTM presence and destination status.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to restore zoom and text resizing.
5. Change `&` to `&amp;` in `<title>`, and remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder.
6. Consolidate or remove the repeated 6x `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and 5x `rlcdn` pixels, and drop the dead `max-width:600px` rule.
