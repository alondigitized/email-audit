# Don’t miss it: Up to 60% off the Summer Sale!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's biggest, longest send of the week — a full storewide sale hero followed by five more distinct promos crammed underneath (Bear collab, Le Creuset, bakery collab, Design Desk sweepstakes, credit card, app). The subject and hero actually agree for once — "Up to 60% off the Summer Sale" up top matches the "up to 60% off" copy in the render — which is more discipline than most of this week's guide-style sends showed.
- But the execution undercuts the promise. The hero product shown is a $1,499 dresser marked to $1,999 (a ~25% discount, not 60%), so the very first thing your eye lands on contradicts the subject's headline number. After that it's a wall of category links and unrelated modules — Bear cookware, Dutch ovens, a bakery collab, a sweepstakes — that dilute the sale message rather than reinforcing it.
- This lands as a mid-pack send: honest subject-to-hero match earns it credit, but the hero math doesn't back up the "60%" claim and the email sprawls into kitchen-sink territory instead of staying focused on the sale it's named after.

## What's working

- Subject line and hero headline are aligned — "60% off the Summer Sale" appears both in the subject and directly under the hero image, unlike sends where the subject and rendered hero diverge.
- Category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, entertaining, decor, rugs, bed & bath, clearance) gives a clear, scannable way to jump straight into the sale by department.
- Two distinct CTAs up top ("Shop Furniture Sale" / "Shop All Sale") let shoppers self-select scope without extra clicks.
- Clean layout with no render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy anywhere in the send.
- "we love these for you" product grid at the bottom shows real prices and product names (Basque table, Anneli bed, Keane nightstand, etc.), giving concrete browsing options.

## What's weak

- The hero image is a dresser priced $1,499 (sale) / $1,999 (reg) — about 25% off, not the "up to 60%" the subject promises, so the strongest visual signal undercuts the headline claim.
- The email keeps going well past the sale: Bear x C&B kitchen collab, Le Creuset Dutch ovens, a bakery collab, a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, and a credit card pitch all compete for attention in one send.
- No urgency or end-date anywhere in the sale section — "up to 60%" with no deadline reads as evergreen, not a limited-time push.
- The asterisk on "The Summer Sale*" signals exclusions/fine print but nothing clarifies what's excluded anywhere visible in the render.
- By module six or seven, the email has drifted so far from "Summer Sale" that a reader scanning quickly would lose track of what this email is actually about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero product for one that's actually near the top of the "up to 60%" range, or add a smaller supporting image/badge showing a genuinely steep-discount item — right now the math doesn't support the headline.
- 2. Cut the send to the sale plus one supporting module (e.g., the Bear collab OR the sweepstakes, not both) — the current length turns a sale email into a full newsletter and buries the CTA that matches the subject.
- 3. Add a deadline ("Ends Sunday," "3 days left") near the hero to convert this from a standing promo into something worth opening today, echoing the urgency Crate & Kids used effectively in its 2026-08-05 backpack send.
- 4. Move the "we love these for you" product grid up near the hero — it's the most concrete, clickable content in the email and currently sits below five other modules.
