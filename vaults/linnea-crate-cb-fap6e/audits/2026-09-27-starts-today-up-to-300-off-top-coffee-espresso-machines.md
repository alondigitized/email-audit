# Starts today: Up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a focused, well-executed category drop — the entire top two-thirds of the email is coffee and espresso machines, matching the subject exactly. The hero leads with the strongest possible offer ("up to $300 off top coffee & espresso machines") over a lifestyle shot of the actual product line, then walks through a clean ladder of individual machine offers (Breville $300 off, Miele $200 off, Caraway $100 off, De'Longhi 25% off, KitchenAid 20% off) each with its own product shot and Shop Now button.
- Where it loses discipline is exactly where Crate & Barrel emails always seem to — after the "more from the Coffee & Espresso Event" recap grid, it pivots into an espresso-machine "quiz" matchmaker (fine, still on-topic), then a pecan crunch iced latte recipe, a Thanksgiving hosting guide, a "we love these for you" grab bag, and an app download pitch. That's a lot of runway after the actual sale content is exhausted.
- Compared to the brand's recent pattern (9/26 SMEG email, 9/26 Kids clearance, 9/25 furniture clearance), this is one of the tighter, more single-minded sends — it earns a better score than most of that history because it doesn't abandon its subject line's promise halfway through.

## What's working

- Hero headline matches the subject line almost verbatim — no bait-and-switch between subject and open.
- Individual dollar/percent offers are stacked directly under each product photo (25% off, $200 off, $100 off, etc.) — easy to scan.
- Every offer block has its own visible "Shop Now" button, so there's no ambiguity about what to click.
- Product photography is consistent, well-lit, and on-brand throughout (dark counter/marble styling).
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or lorem ipsum visible anywhere in the long scroll.

## What's weak

- The email runs extremely long — coffee content ends roughly 60% down, then pivots to recipes, Thanksgiving, and app-download content that dilutes the single-category focus the subject promised.
- Sale price legibility is inconsistent — some tags read like "Sale $649.95, Reg. $999.95" in small type that's easy to miss against the photo背景.
- No urgency/deadline is visible anywhere in the hero or offer blocks despite "starts today" language in the subject — a screenshot reader can't tell when this event ends.
- The closing "we love these for you" grid feels like generic filler unrelated to coffee, undercutting the otherwise disciplined category focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "Coffee & Espresso Event" recap grid — drop the recipe, Thanksgiving guide, and app-download sections into a separate send. This alone would raise both click-through (less scroll fatigue) and category clarity.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or end-date ("Ends Sunday 9/29") near the hero headline — "starts today" implies urgency the visuals never deliver.
- 3. Bump the sale-price tags to a bolder, higher-contrast treatment so the dollar-off math is legible against the dark photo backgrounds.
- 4. Move the espresso-matchmaker quiz higher, directly under the hero, since it's still on-category and helps undecided shoppers pick a machine — right now it's buried after four product blocks.
