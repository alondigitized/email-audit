# NEW markdowns! More at up to 60% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' clearance email, and it's a kitchen-sink send — a clean "up to 60% off + new markdowns" hero up top, then a cascade of unrelated pitches (backpack bundles, TheDesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes, "we love these for you" product grid, a new credit card push, an app download ad, and a second TheDesignDesk plug) before finally landing on the footer. The hero itself is strong: soft daisy-field photography, clear white CTA button, unambiguous offer copy. But by the third or fourth unrelated module the email loses focus entirely — this reads like five separate campaigns stapled together rather than one clearance push.
- Compare to 2026-08-20's "NEW MARKDOWNS ADDED: Up to 60% off clearance" from sibling brand Crate & Barrel, which scored 5/10 for being direct and offer-forward with no ambiguity — this Crate & Kids version opens the same way but then buries that clarity under unrelated cross-sells. The category nav row (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) is a nice touch for a parent shopper, but everything below it dilutes the "60% off" promise the subject line made.
- Judgement: solid hero, weak follow-through. This would get one scroll-and-click on the clearance CTA at most — the rest of the email is noise for anyone who opened for the sale.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "up to 60% off + new markdowns" with a single white "Shop Clearance" button, no scroll needed on the top fold.
- Category quick-links (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) let a parent jump straight to their need.
- Product grid ("we love these for you") shows real prices and product photography — backpack, car seat, dresser, glider, stroller, activity chair — with no broken images.
- No render bugs — clean layout, legible text, no overlapping elements anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a clearance sale but the email pivots almost immediately to backpack bundles, a $5000 designer sweepstakes, a new credit card, and an app download — the offer gets buried by module three.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere near the hero offer ("selling fast, shop now" is vague, not time-bound).
- The "we love these for you" product grid isn't clearance-priced or discounted — it undercuts the 60%-off promise by showing full-price items right after the sale pitch.
- Credit card and app-download modules are pure cross-sell clutter with zero connection to the subject line's offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — kill or move the credit card, app download, and second TheDesignDesk block to a separate send; a clearance email should stay on-message.
- 2. Move the product grid directly under the hero and mark it explicitly as clearance/sale-priced (show strikethrough original prices) so it reinforces rather than contradicts the 60%-off claim.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero ("ends Sunday" or similar) to convert "selling fast" into credible urgency.
- 4. If the backpack bundle and sweepstakes must stay, badge them as separate from the sale with their own visual break so they don't read as diluting the clearance offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off clearance — new markdowns just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final sale: new markdowns added, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh clearance styles just added — shop before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kids bedding, furniture & more — now up to 60% off`
