# Homepage snapshot · www.crateandbarrel.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Crate & Barrel is doing exactly what I'd expect from them in late May — full Memorial Day sale mode, rewards dollars front and center, and a prompt asking for my phone number before I've even seen the homepage. The brand looks premium and seasonal, but it's pushing outdoor furniture hard when I'm here for tabletop and my registry. I notice "Wedding Registry" is right there in the nav, which saves them, but the hero experience is basically speaking to someone shopping for a lounge sofa, not me.

## What's working

- **Rewards strip at the very top** — "Earn 10%, 20% Back in Reward Dollars\* Shop 5 Categories" with an end date (5/26/2026) is exactly the kind of hook that keeps me from leaving. I'm full-price tolerant and 20% back is real money on a dinnerware set.
- **"Wedding Registry" is in the top navigation row** — as someone who recently registered, this is the one nav link I'm looking for and it's there without hunting.
- **Memorial Day timing is right** — the sale strip and hero are seasonally accurate. No weird "Holiday Gifting" holdover.
- **"Tabletop & Bar" and "Kitchen" in the category nav** — my two main focus areas are both one tap away from the secondary nav bar.
- **Clean, modern visual style** — even through the modal I can see the photography is lifestyle-driven and minimal. Feels like my taste.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the first thing I see** — "Save 10% off full-price items\* — Get alerts about new items, sales and more" with an email AND phone field before I've seen a single product. Asking for my cell number to get a coupon immediately raises my guard.
- **The hero is about outdoor furniture, not my category** — the lifestyle image and all the main CTAs ("Shop Outdoor Furniture," "Shop Memorial Day Event," "Shop Furniture Sale") are for someone outfitting a patio, not a kitchen or dining table.
- **No registry-specific hook above the fold** — I just registered. There's zero "complete your registry," "registry event," or "registry discount" message anywhere I can see, which is a missed opportunity for my exact life stage.
- **No product-level content visible** — everything above the fold is category-level or promotional. I can't see a single specific product with a name or price, which makes it harder to click through with intent.
- **Fine print asterisks are everywhere** — "Reward Dollars\*," "full-price items\*," "Earn $499 $250\*" — I know there are exclusions but the density of asterisks erodes confidence.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the outdoor furniture hero for a tabletop/registry lifestyle shot for logged-in registry holders** — or at minimum, add a registry-completion module immediately below the hero for users with an active registry. A "Your Registry · 12 items still needed" card with a thumbnail grid would convert me instantly.
- **Remove the phone number field from the welcome modal** — email capture on first visit is standard; asking for an SMS number immediately reads as aggressive. Drop the phone field or move it to a second step after email confirm.
- **Promote the registry completion discount explicitly** — Crate & Barrel has a registry completion program. That should be surfaced as a named CTA ("Complete your registry — save 20%") in the loyalty/rewards strip or as its own above-the-fold callout.
- **Add a "New in Tabletop" or "Trending Dinnerware" row** — the homepage sells furniture heavy. Even one editorial row for tabletop/kitchen would give me a reason to keep scrolling instead of going straight to the nav.
