# Going fast: grab their school gear at up to 20% off →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' back-to-school gear push, and the hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: "Selling Fast! get back-to-school gear in time," with a personalized-monogram/free-shipping hook and a photo grid of backpacks stacked directly under it. That's a tight subject-to-hero match — better than the brand's usual pattern of burying the actual offer under a banner (see 2026-08-10's "FINAL DAY: 50% off summer sale," which led with a discount stack instead of the named category).
- But it falls apart almost immediately after. There's no visible discount anywhere near the top — "up to 20% off" from the subject line never appears as a callout in the hero or the product grid, just "Ships Free!" and starting prices. Then the email sprawls: new arrivals banner, a $5,000 sweepstakes for TheDesignDesk, a curated product grid mixing kids furniture with strollers and nursery gear, a new credit card pitch, an app download push, and a second TheDesignDesk callout — five unrelated asks stacked below the one relevant offer. A subscriber opening for "school gear at 20% off" gets one clean screen of it, then six screens of noise.
- Judgement: solid subject-driven hero, but the promised discount is invisible and the email overstays its welcome. This reads as a 5/10 — a decent open driver let down by a bloated, unfocused body.

## What's working

- Hero copy ("get back-to-school gear in time") and imagery (backpack wall) directly match the subject line's "school gear" promise.
- Clear urgency framing — "Selling Fast!" and "time is running out" — feels credible for a school-shopping deadline.
- Product grid is well-organized into scannable categories (backpacks, duffle bags, lunch boxes, water bottles) with a "Shop All Kids Gear" rollup CTA.
- "Ships Free! & Starts at $39" gives a concrete price anchor even without a discount shown.
- No render bugs — images loaded cleanly, text is legible, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- The subject's "up to 20% off" is nowhere visible in the hero or product section — no percentage, no strikethrough pricing, nothing that substantiates the headline offer.
- The email crams in four unrelated pitches after the school gear section (new arrivals, $5,000 sweepstakes, credit card launch, app download) that dilute focus and push the reader far from the original intent.
- The "we love these for you" grid mixes kids backpacks with adult nursery furniture and strollers — inconsistent with a back-to-school ask and confusing category signal.
- Visual hierarchy after the hero degrades into a scroll of disconnected banners rather than reinforcing the school-gear offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual "up to 20% off" badge or price-strike callout in the hero and on each product tile — right now the headline offer is a bait-and-switch between subject line and body.
- 2. Cut the email down to the school-gear offer plus one secondary CTA (new arrivals or the sweepstakes, not both) — the card pitch and app download can move to a dedicated send.
- 3. Swap the "we love these for you" grid to kids-only products; strollers/nursery furniture next to backpacks breaks the back-to-school narrative.
- 4. Add a deadline date or countdown near "Selling Fast!" — "time is running out" without a date is vague urgency, not credible urgency.
