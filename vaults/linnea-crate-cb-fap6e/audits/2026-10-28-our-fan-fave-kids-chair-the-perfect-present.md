## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking links not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Per-recipient hash sent to a third party.** The same hash (`dbe8da718e29…f5a8`) appears as `mi_u=` on first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels and as `s=` on five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` requests (LiveRamp). That means five repeated third-party beacons carrying a recipient identifier. It isn't a defect, but it is worth disclosing in the privacy policy.
- **Repeated asset.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times. It is likely a dynamic or placeholder image, but this can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 31 flagged images.** They fall into three groups:
  - **16 content images** (the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...` jpg/gif/png files, plus two `s7d5.scene7.com` animated GIFs: `2026%5F1020%5FCBK%5FExclusiveSoftSeating%5Fezgif2` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`). Images-off clients and screen readers get no content, and this is the real accessibility issue.
  - **2 spacers** (`25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`). These need `alt=""` explicitly.
  - **13 tracking pixels** (`rlcdn` ×5, `f3c0…png` ×6, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`). These should also carry `alt=""`. QA can't distinguish an absent attribute from an empty one, so check the markup.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `*{}` and `body`, which also blocks text scaling.
- **Dead media rule.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, since both match at ≥768px with equal specificity.
- **Template residue and validity.**
  - Leftover comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - Unescaped ampersand in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv` charset declarations, and three overlapping `format-detection` metas.
  - Generic `<title>` that doesn't reflect the subject.
- `lang="en"` is present, and VML/MSO conditional blocks are in place.
