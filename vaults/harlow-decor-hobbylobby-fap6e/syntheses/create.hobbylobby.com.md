# ### Summary and Recommendations

This email from Hobby Lobby is a departure from the usual weekly ad format, focusing instead on everyday low prices. While the concept is fresh and aligns with a broader retail strategy, the execution is marred by a contradictory CTA and a lack of urgency or clear savings anchors. Here are the key points and recommendations:

### What Worked
- **Distinct Messaging:** The "NEW LOWER PRICES!" headline breaks the monotony of %-off promotions.
- **Specific Price Points:** Clear and credible pricing information enhances the credibility of the EDLP message.
- **Seasonal Hero:** The Spring Essentials banner ties the message to the current season.
- **Clean Render:** No broken images or layout issues.

### What Didn't Work
- **Conflicting CTA:** "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" undermines the everyday low price message.
- **Lack of Urgency:** Without a deadline or savings anchor, the message lacks a sense of immediacy.
- **Flat Product Grid:** A 20-item grid with no hierarchy or featured items fails to capture attention.
- **Weak Subject Line:** The subject line lacks specificity and urgency, making it less likely to engage subscribers.

### Recommendations

1. **Fix the CTA Copy:**
   - Change the CTA to "SHOP NEW LOWER PRICES" or "SEE ALL PRICE DROPS" to align with the EDLP message.

2. **Lead with a Hero SKU:**
   - Feature a single hero product with a dramatic price reduction to anchor the eye and justify the scroll.

3. **Add Savings Anchors:**
   - Include "Was $X → Now $Y" labels on key items to provide concrete proof of price reductions.

4. **Trim the Product Grid:**
   - Reduce the grid to 8-10 items to make it more visually appealing and drive higher click-through rates.

5. **Improve Subject Line:**
   - Use a more specific and engaging subject line, such as "We Just Dropped Prices on Spring Essentials — Starts at $1.99" or "New Lower Prices on 20+ Craft Staples — No Coupon Needed."

6. **Enhance Preheader Text:**
   - Craft a preheader that highlights the most compelling aspects of the offer, such as "Grapevine wreaths, foam bricks, glue guns — prices rolled back for good" or "Permanent cuts on the supplies you restock every season — see what changed."

### Technical QA

- **List-Unsubscribe / RFC 8058 Headers:** Ensure these headers are present to comply with Gmail and Yahoo policies.
- **Plain-Text Content:** Regenerate plain-text from body copy to avoid URL-heavy content.
- **Responsive Design:** Add a second media query for mobile responsiveness at `max-width:600px`.
- **CSS Fixes:** Correct malformed CSS properties such as `border-radius` and `vertical-align`.

By addressing these issues, Hobby Lobby can improve the clarity and effectiveness of their email, enhancing both engagement and conversions.
