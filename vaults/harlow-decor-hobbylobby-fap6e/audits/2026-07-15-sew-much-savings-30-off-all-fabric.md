# Sew Much Savings 🪡 30% Off All Fabric!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby deploying one of their stronger creative hooks — a fabric-pun subject line with a specific 30% off offer — and then wasting most of that setup energy by dropping straight into the same two-column mega-circular product grid that has run on July 2, July 7, July 9, and July 12. The hero earns attention: "30% All Fabric" is prominently placed with a clear typographic hierarchy. But the moment you scroll past it, the email becomes indistinguishable from every other recent Hobby Lobby send, with vinyl, foam boards, acrylic paints, and canvas sets — almost no fabric in sight.
- The disconnect between what the subject promises and what the body delivers is this email's central failure. A subscriber who opened because they sew is served a general art-supply sale. The CTA doesn't help: "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" is the weakest possible call to action for a category-specific offer, sending the user to a circular rather than a fabric department page.
- Compared to the July 14 "Find What You Love With 66% Off The Spring Shop" send — which anchored a lifestyle hero to a specific vertical — this email does something similar in the hero but buries it faster. It's a serviceable promo, not a targeted one.

## What's working

- **Pun subject line with specific offer** — "Sew Much Savings 🪡 30% Off All Fabric!" is memorable, category-appropriate, and states the discount clearly; the needle-and-thread emoji earns its place.
- **Hero hierarchy is clean** — the "30%" numeral dominates the top fold, the category "All Fabric" is readable, and the CTA button has visible contrast.
- **Free shipping threshold visible** — the "$59 or more" banner is prominent above the fold, removing a purchase objection early.
- **No visible render bugs** — the two-column grid loads cleanly; no broken images or overlapping text observed.

## What's weak

- **Body doesn't deliver on the fabric promise** — the product grid is Iron-On Vinyl, Foam Boards, Acrylic Paint, Canvas Sets, Liquid Dye — the only fabric-adjacent item is 37¢ White Rayon Embroidery Floss buried mid-grid.
- **"Click here to view our weekly ad" is the laziest CTA in email marketing** — it sends a fabric-interested subscriber to a general circular, burning the specificity earned by the subject line.
- **Fatigue risk is real** — this is the fifth or sixth promotional send from Hobby Lobby in two weeks in the same mega-circular format; the fabric hero is a veneer on the same template.
- **No urgency signal anywhere** — no end date, no "this week only," no countdown — the 30% off could be running forever.
- **Zero social proof or product depth** — no reviews, no "most popular" callout, no fabric-specific detail (thread count, width, material type) that would pull a sewing-focused subscriber toward a click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the CTA with a fabric-specific deep link** — "Shop All Fabric at 30% Off" pointing to `/fabric` would convert the subject's promise into an actual path to purchase. "View our weekly ad" destroys intent.
- 2. **Curate the product grid to fabric and notions** — show bolts of fabric, interfacing, thread, zippers, batting. Keep vinyl and foam boards for a separate send; mixing them signals "we don't actually know why you opened this."
- 3. **Add an end date to the offer** — even "This week only" or "Ends Sunday" creates the credible urgency that is entirely absent here.
- 4. **Add one lifestyle anchor** — a single image of a finished quilt, a garment in progress, or a fabric stack gives sewing subscribers an emotional reason to click beyond the discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% Off All Fabric Ends Sunday — Stock Up Now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Fabric Haul Is 30% Off This Week 🧵`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quilting cotton, fleece, canvas, and more — all fabric, all 30% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the full fabric department at 30% off — free shipping on $59+.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Hobby Lobby "Sew Much Savings" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for broad client compatibility but has three CSS bugs in the CTA button rule, missing compliance headers at the relay layer, and a plain-text body that is 70% URLs — a material deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text body URL density — HIGH RISK**
QA reports 13,066 of 15,825 plain-text characters are URLs (82.6%). Spam filters score heavily on this ratio; a plain-text alternative should contain readable copy with a small number of links, not a dump of redirect URLs.

**Click-tracking redirect domain**
All links route through `link.hobbylobby.com`. No issue with the domain itself, but UTM parameter presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS typo — `verticl-align`**
In `.lhp2`:
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font {line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle;}
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid property; vertical alignment is silently dropped on elements using this class.

**Missing CTA border-radius values**
In `.ctacr1`:
```css
-moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;
```
All three radius values are empty (unit without a number). The CTA button will render with square corners in Firefox and non-Outlook clients, likely unintentional.

**Empty `color` declaration on CTA**
```css
.ctacr1 {color:; ...}
```
Empty value; the CTA text color falls through to the inherited default rather than whatever was intended.

**Tracking pixel missing alt text**
```
eORqXTkAb7_837015858_129944_2166090_138652.gif
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_...
```
QA-confirmed. Decorative/tracking GIFs should carry `alt=""` explicitly to suppress screen-reader noise and satisfy some client image-blocking behaviors.

**Viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">
```
`user-scalable=yes` is fine, but omitting `width=device-width` means some mobile clients default to desktop viewport width before applying media queries.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unfired tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FIRST NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues confirmed.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent — WARN**
QA confirms no `List-Unsubscribe` header was captured at the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the native unsubscribe prompt; its absence increases spam complaint rates and risks bulk-sender enforcement (Gmail's February 2024 policy requires it for senders above 5k/day).

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent — WARN**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is missing. Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for commercial senders.

**Authentication-Results absent — WARN**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown because `Authentication-Results` was not captured by the relay. Cannot confirm alignment with `create.hobbylobby.com` MAIL FROM. If DKIM is not signing the `create.hobbylobby.com` subdomain, deliverability to Gmail/Yahoo is at risk.

**`noreply@` sender**
`noreply@create.hobbylobby.com` — CAN-SPAM requires a working reply-to address or an alternative opt-out path. Acceptable if a functioning unsubscribe link exists in the body (not confirmed from truncated HTML, but standard practice for Hobby Lobby's ESP).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from the truncated HTML — all links go through `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect, which may append UTMs on resolution. This should be spot-checked: expand one redirect and verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are present on the landing URL. The 30%-off-fabric offer should resolve to a pre-filtered fabric category page, not the homepage.
