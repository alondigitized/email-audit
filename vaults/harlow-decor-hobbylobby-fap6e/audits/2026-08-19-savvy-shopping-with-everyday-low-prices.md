# Savvy Shopping With Everyday Low Prices!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- "Savvy Shopping With Everyday Low Prices!" is, at least, an honest subject line — and for once the render actually delivers on it. This is a straight price-list email: a "NEW LOWER PRICES!" banner up top, then a long, dense grid of individual crafting SKUs each with a specific $ price ($1.99 chargers, $4.99 wreaths, $24.99 clear cast epoxy) all the way down. No lifestyle hero, no seasonal styling, just a catalog dump.
- Compared to the brand's recent run — "Create Your Dream Retreat," "Cozy Living With Inviting Textures," "Add Warmth To Your Home" — this is a real change of pace: less aspirational styling, more utilitarian coupon-circular. That's actually more honest than the thematic sends that buried a small on-topic hero under a generic tile grid, but it also means there's zero emotional hook or visual hierarchy — it reads like a printed weekly ad scanned into an email, not a marketing send.
- Verdict: functionally fine for a price-conscious craft shopper who already knows what they need, but this is a weak acquisition/engagement email — no single CTA, no discount percentage, no urgency beyond a small "Valid Sale Through Saturday, August 22, 2026" footnote that's easy to miss.

## What's working

- Subject and hero module are consistent for once — "NEW LOWER PRICES!" directly matches "Savvy Shopping With Everyday Low Prices."
- Every single product tile shows a real, specific price — no vague "up to X% off" language.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" banner is a clear, concrete, low-friction offer near the top.
- Product photography is clean, on-white, catalog-style and consistent across all ~20 tiles.
- Sale end date ("Valid Sale Through Saturday, August 22, 2026") gives real, credible time-boundedness, even if it's buried in the footer.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer or CTA — the eye has nowhere obvious to land; it's a wall of nearly-identical tiles.
- Fall Essentials banner up top is disconnected from the rest of the email, which is craft/DIY basics, not seasonal decor — mixed messaging in the first fold.
- Extremely long, scroll-heavy layout (20+ SKUs) with no sectioning or prioritization — everything has equal visual weight.
- No loyalty/member pricing or personalization visible despite this being a recognizable repeat sender.
- "In-Store Only" appears on some items (candy wafers) with no online alternative flagged, undercutting the "shop online" CTA structure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tile count in half and lead with 3-4 hero "new lower price" items above the fold with larger imagery — right now nothing is prioritized, so nothing stands out.
- 2. Move the "Valid Through Saturday, Aug 22" urgency line up near the hero banner, not buried in the footer — it's the only real urgency signal in the whole email.
- 3. Resolve the Fall Essentials/decor banner vs. craft-supplies-price-list mismatch — pick one theme for the send, don't stack two unrelated promos in the first two modules.
- 4. Add a single clear primary CTA button ("Shop New Lower Prices") instead of relying on repeated small "In-Store Or Online" links per tile.
- 5. Flag "In-Store Only" items with a distinct visual treatment or move them to a separate section so online shoppers aren't disappointed clicking through.
