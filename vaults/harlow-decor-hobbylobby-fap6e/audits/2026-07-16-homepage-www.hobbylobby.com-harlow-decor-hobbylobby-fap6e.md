# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, I'll be honest — this is about as close to a home run as Hobby Lobby ever swings at me. The gallery wall in that hero image? Oval frames, botanical prints, pink striped wallpaper — that is my living room mood board come to life. They're leading with a 40% off Home Decor sale and making sure I know the free-shipping threshold before I've done anything. It's a simple page, maybe *too* simple, but it's pointed straight at me.

## What's working

- **The 40% off Home Decor headline** — I already know their rotating sale calendar, but seeing Home Decor called out *right now* tells me this week is my week. I'm coming in.
- **The hero image** — Oval frames layered over striped pink wallpaper with botanical watercolor prints? That gallery wall is exactly what I've been building in my dining room. I feel *seen*.
- **Free shipping banner** — "$59 Or More" at the very top means I'm calculating my cart total before I even tap anything. That's the right place for it.
- **"Discounts Valid Online & In-Store"** — As someone who genuinely prefers walking the store, the fact that they explicitly say in-store matters. I don't feel penalized for showing up in person.
- **The deadline copy** — "through July 18, 2026" gives me two days. That's real urgency without feeling fake.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty or rewards callout** — I've been shopping Hobby Lobby for years. If they have a rewards program, I'd want to see my balance or a member benefit nudge right here. Total miss.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — In context it works, but "Shop Home Decor" or "See What's 40% Off" would push me to tap faster. "Shop Now" feels like a placeholder.
- **No product specificity in the hero** — The gallery wall image is gorgeous but I can't tell if those frames are actually in the 40% off sale or if they're lifestyle props. The asterisk on "The Marked Price" gives me a flicker of doubt.
- **No secondary category navigation visible** — The hamburger is there but I can't see the Floral department or Seasonal Decor without an extra tap. For someone who came for faux florals, I'm not immediately reassured they're here.
- **No new-arrivals signal** — Nothing says "just came in" or "trending this week." I want to know if there's anything fresh to discover on this visit.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Home Decor Sale"** — Three extra words, immediate context, zero ambiguity. Could ship tomorrow.
- **Add a Rewards/Loyalty strip below the hero** — Even a simple "Hobby Lobby Rewards — Shop & Earn" bar would close the loop for loyal shoppers. I want acknowledgment that I'm a regular.
- **Add a second hero tile or carousel frame for Floral** — Farmhouse decor buyers almost always double-dip in faux floral. A swipeable second frame showing the floral aisle with a matching sale callout would keep me from having to hunt.
- **Put product names or prices in the hero caption** — Something like "Ornate Gallery Frames from $12.99 — 40% off this week" grounds the lifestyle image in a real shopping moment instead of a Pinterest fantasy.
