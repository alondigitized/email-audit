# Up To 50% Off Seasonal Savings!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again. The subject, "Up To 50% Off Seasonal Savings!", is honest but generic. The render is a very long scroll, about 9,400px, of category blocks. Almost every block has its own percent-off badge: Christmas 25%, Home Decor 40%, Textiles 40%, Mirrors & Lamps 50%, Fall Decor 50%, Football Party 40%, Floral 40%, Hobbies 40%, Crafts 40%, Frames 40%, Yarn 30%, Furniture 30%. The "up to 50%" claim holds up, but no single product or offer leads.
- The email opens with a free-shipping bar ($69+) and a Christmas Essentials banner. Then it moves straight into a dense stack of "Categories Listed" tiles. There's no obvious CTA button anywhere. The tiles are the click targets, and none of them says what to do. A reader gets a catalog, not a reason to act now.
- It follows the same pattern as the 11/11 "All Things Pumpkin! 50% Off Fall" send and the 11/10 "Pillow Pairings" send. It's the fifth or sixth near-identical circular in about a week. The sale end date near the bottom ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 14, 2026") is a real urgency hook, but it's buried at the foot. Verdict: functional, but forgettable and fatiguing.

## What's working

- The subject states a real, checkable offer ("Up To 50% Off"), and the 50% tiles in the body match it.
- The percent-off badges are big and consistent, so a skimmer can scan discounts quickly.
- The free-shipping bar and Christmas banner at the top are seasonal and set a holiday tone.
- The product photography is clean, and the sections are clearly separated by category.
- The sale end date is stated, giving a real deadline.

## What's weak

- There is no single hero offer and no visible CTA button. The eye has nowhere to land.
- The "Categories Listed" and "Does not include…" exclusion text repeats in nearly every block, which makes the offer feel fine-print-heavy.
- The email is extremely long, with about 20 blocks. Most readers won't get past the first few.
- The sale deadline is buried at the very bottom instead of near the top.
- The cadence is heavy. After "All Things Pumpkin!" on 11/11 and "Pillow Pairings" on 11/10, this reads like the same email again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero and one button, such as "Shop 50% Off Fall Decor", and cut the body to 4–5 blocks. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Move the "Ends Saturday 11/14" deadline into the header bar next to free shipping.
- 3. Collapse the repeated exclusion fine print into one line at the bottom.
- 4. Add a visible "Shop Now" button to each block, instead of relying on tile taps.
- 5. Cut the cadence to fewer sends with more distinct themes.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware template (VML/MSO conditionals, `X-UA-Compatible`) with no link failures reported, but the CSS has defects and the responsive breakpoint looks wrong. The QA run was 50% pass, 0 errors, 4 warnings. Two of those warnings (missing headers) are probably relay artifacts and can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and 28 were skipped by design (footer, social and utility links are deprioritized). The skipped links are unverified, so coverage is partial.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,540 of 13,083 characters (about 88%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. This points to long wrapped tracking URLs in the text alternative. It can hurt spam scoring and makes the text version hard to read.
- **Tracking domain differs from the sender domain.** Links and images use `link.hobbylobby.com`, while the From address is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`. Both are subdomains of `hobbylobby.com`, so this is normal ESP link wrapping, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image without alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144038_2393721_152894.gif`. The `/mo/` path and `.gif` extension suggest an open-tracking pixel. If so, the impact is minimal, but add `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Invalid CSS in `.ctacr1`:** `color:;`, `-moz-border-radius:px;`, `-webkit-border-radius:px;` and `border-radius:px;` all have empty values. Clients will drop these declarations, so the intended text color and corner radius on that CTA class are never applied.
- **Typo in `.lhp2`:** `verticl-align:middle` should be `vertical-align`. The vertical alignment is silently ignored.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the only `@media` query visible in the truncated source is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Typical phones are 320–430px wide, so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.mh`, `.image`) likely never fire. The source is truncated, so check the rest of the stylesheet for other breakpoints.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` has no `width=device-width`. This is low severity because email clients mostly ignore it.
- **DOCTYPE:** it is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the `<html>` tag carries XHTML and VML namespaces. This is common in ESP templates and low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the retrieved source. The HTML is truncated, so this is only partly verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these. Hobby Lobby's ESP probably sends them, but this can't be confirmed from the captured data. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. This is a tooling gap, not evidence of failure.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so the physical address can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this is unverified. The truncated source and QA output don't include destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page checks. Tracking appears to be handled by redirect wrapping on `link.hobbylobby.com`, so UTM presence can only be confirmed by following the redirects. No redirect chain data was provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix `.ctacr1` by supplying real values for `color` and `border-radius`, or remove the empty declarations.
2. Correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align` in `.lhp2`.
3. Review the media queries. Move the main responsive breakpoint from 300px to about 480–600px, or confirm other breakpoints exist beyond the truncation.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144038_2393721_152894.gif` so the QA warning clears and screen readers skip it.
5. Shorten the URLs in the plain-text part, or add substantive text copy, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from raw headers outside the relay.
7. Resolve the redirect chains on `link.hobbylobby.com` to verify UTM parameters and landing-page status for the 28 unprobed links.
