# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Well, they didn't waste my time. I open this app and the very first thing I see is a sage-green-and-terracotta bedroom styled exactly the way I'd pin it — warm layered textiles, a classic wood headboard, that earthy botanical energy I live for. And then the 40% off home decor headline hits right below it. Hobby Lobby knows who they're talking to, and right now they're pushing their home decor category hard with a deadline that's basically this weekend.

## What's working

- **The hero bedroom photograph** — sage green and rust/terracotta bedding, stacked books, green table lamp. That's my Pinterest board, rendered. Stops the scroll instantly.
- **"40% OFF Home Decor" in massive type** — I don't have to hunt for the deal. It's the second thing my eye lands on after the image.
- **The May 30 deadline** — "Discounts Valid Online & In-Store through May 30, 2026" creates real urgency. That's Saturday. I need to decide today.
- **Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More** — I always forget the threshold. Having it pinned at the very top means I'm already calculating my cart.
- **"Weekly Ad" button** — I actually use the weekly ad religiously. Putting it right next to the search bar is exactly where I expect it.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — I know Hobby Lobby doesn't have a loyalty program in the traditional sense, but there's nothing here that rewards me for coming back every week. I feel anonymous.
- **"Shop Now" is a lazy CTA** — at 40% off Home Decor, they could say "Shop Home Decor" and be twice as compelling. "Shop Now" could be for anything.
- **The asterisk on "Always"** — "Home Decor Always 40% Off The Marked Price*" — that asterisk makes me suspicious. What's excluded? I have to go looking.
- **No category shortcuts visible** — I can't see Floral, Farmhouse, or Seasonal rails without tapping the hamburger. One scroll could've shown me exactly where I'm going.
- **No people, no lifestyle context** — the bedroom is beautiful but it's a product shot. A 47-year-old woman putting finishing touches on a room would've connected harder.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to "Shop Home Decor"** — straightforward swap, zero dev time, doubles the clarity and reinforces the offer category.
- **Add a short exclusions summary inline** — instead of burying the asterisk disclaimer, put two words like "most items" next to the offer so I don't feel set up.
- **Add a quick-access strip below the hero** — three tappable icons: Floral, Farmhouse, Seasonal Decor. Tap once, I'm there. Right now the hamburger menu hides everything.
- **A/B test a lifestyle shot with a woman in frame** — keep the room, but put someone rearranging the nightstand. Seeing myself in the image converts better than empty rooms for my demographic.
