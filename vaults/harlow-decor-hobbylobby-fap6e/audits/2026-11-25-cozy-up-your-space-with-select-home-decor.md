# Cozy Up Your Space With Select Home Decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is only half honest. "Cozy Up Your Space With Select Home Decor" matches the big hero: a bedroom scene with "Always 40% Off The Marked Price* Home Decor." That is the only offer-led block. The rest of the email is a long product grid of $1.99 chargers, $4.99 foam bricks, wreaths, candy wafers, glue sticks and batteries. Most of that is craft supply, not cozy decor.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" bar and the "Christmas Essentials" banner sit above the hero and compete with it. Since the prior sends led with "50% Off Christmas" (11/24, 11/22, 11/17), this one drops Christmas from the subject while the email still opens with a Christmas banner. A subscriber who has seen five near-identical circulars this week will not get a reason to open this one over the others.
- Judgement: a competent circular with a clear hero, but a weak match between subject and body, and a mixed audience. It lands in the usual mid-range.

## What's working

- The 40% off hero is large, photographic and seasonal, so the eye lands on it first.
- The subject's "home decor" promise matches the hero and the category tiles below it (Doormats, Sheet Sets, Window Curtains, Framed Art, Wall Clocks).
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" bar is a concrete, easy-to-read perk at the very top.
- Product tiles show big price numerals ($1.99, $2.99, $3.99), so prices are easy to scan.
- A date-bound line, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28, 2026," gives a credible deadline.

## What's weak

- The product grid is mostly crafting items (glue guns, foam bricks, epoxy, vinyl), which don't match the "cozy home decor" promise.
- The "Christmas Essentials" banner sits above the hero and splits attention from the 40% off message.
- The 40% offer carries an asterisk and "See Weekly Ad For Details." The "Marked Price" wording is vague, so the savings feel hard to quantify.
- Every tile says "In-Store Or Online," so no product has a distinct call to action. The only real button is "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD."
- The email is very long, and the sale deadline sits at the very bottom. The fine print there is tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero agree on one offer. Either lead with "40% off home decor" or put the Christmas banner below the hero.
- 2. Cut the product grid to 6-8 items that fit the decor theme, such as wreaths, clocks and framed art. Move the glue and craft supplies into a separate craft-focused send.
- 3. Move the deadline ("Through Saturday, Nov 28") up next to the hero and give it an urgency treatment.
- 4. Replace "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" with a specific button such as "Shop 40% Off Home Decor."
- 5. Spell out what "Marked Price" means in one plain sentence near the hero, so the offer feels honest.
