# Shop Now & Save Up to 50% Seasonal Finds!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's seventh weekly-ad send in eight days — the same vertical department-stack template they've now hammered relentlessly since May 17. Home Decor 40%, Textiles 40%, Spring Shop 50%, Graduation 40%, Summer 50%, Party 40%, Florals 40%, Hobbies 40%, Wearable Art, Crafts 30%, Frames 50%, Paper Crafts 30%, Jewelry Making 40%, Sewing, Custom Frames — it's a digital flyer dump with no narrative thread.
- The subject "Shop Now & Save Up to 50% Seasonal Finds!" is generic enough to be interchangeable with any of the prior six sends. The hero is a tiny "Spring Essentials" badge crammed above a wall of department tiles — there's no single anchoring offer, no Memorial Day hook despite the holiday being three days away, and no reason for me to engage with this versus the May 22 "Complete Your Space" send sitting two emails up the inbox.
- Cadence fatigue is real here. This is the most generic version of a format that already plateaued. A 5 at best.

## What's working

- Discount percentages are large and bold — readable at a thumbnail scale.
- Department breadth means almost any crafter sees their category somewhere on the page.
- Consistent template means subscribers know exactly how to scan it.
- "Spring Essentials" hero badge centers the seasonal frame, however lightly.

## What's weak

- Seventh send of the same digest format in eight days — diminishing returns are obvious.
- Subject is the most generic of the brand's recent run; nothing distinguishes it from May 17's "Your Weekly Ad Is Here!"
- No Memorial Day hook despite the holiday being May 25 — May 20's florals send had it, this one drops it entirely.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — fifteen-plus department tiles compete for the eye with no single hero offer.
- "Save Up to 50%" is a soft promise when most tiles read 30-40%; only Spring Shop, Summer, and Frames hit 50%.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with Memorial Day** — it's the holiday literally happening this weekend; a patriotic floral or party hero would print money versus a generic "seasonal finds" wrapper. May 20's 8/10 send proved this works.
- 2. **Pick one hero, not fifteen** — anchor on Spring Shop 50% OR Summer 50% as the hero, demote the rest to a secondary grid.
- 3. **Cut the cadence** — seven sends in eight days is training subscribers to mute the brand. Skip a day.
- 4. **Make the subject specific** — name the holiday or the lead department instead of "Seasonal Finds."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day Weekend: 50% Off Spring Shop & Summer`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Days — 50% Off Florals, Frames & Spring Décor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Holiday-weekend hours inside + your weekly savings stack.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Memorial Day picks: patio, party, and patriotic florals.`

# Technical Audit — Hobby Lobby "Shop Now & Save Up to 50%"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on a legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional template with heavy MSO conditionals — structurally functional but flagged for missing one-click unsubscribe headers, missing alt text on a tracked image, and unverifiable SPF/DKIM via the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Tracking domain `link.hobbylobby.com` is in use (consistent with a Salesforce/ExactTarget-style ESP), confirmed via the image host.
- Plain-text part is >88% URLs (11,594 / 13,144 chars) — indicates the text alternative is essentially a list of tracking-wrapped links rather than a readable fallback. This degrades spam scoring and accessibility for text-only clients.
- No UTM parameters visible in the truncated source for the link.hobbylobby.com asset; cannot confirm `utm_source/medium/campaign` presence on click-through links from the truncated sample.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `[WARN] Image missing alt text`: `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_123960_1218627_133308.gif` (`https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...gif`) — screen readers will announce nothing; image-blocked clients show no fallback.
- Doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional with VML/MSO namespaces — acceptable for Outlook compatibility, not a defect.
- Minor CSS typos in inline styles: `verticl-align:middle` (typo, should be `vertical-align`) and empty values `color:;`, `-moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;` in `.ctacr1` — invalid CSS, silently ignored by clients but indicates template hygiene issues.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` lacks `width=device-width` — may affect mobile scaling in some webmail clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FNAME]`) detected in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe` header not present in captured headers.
- `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) not present — required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules since Feb 2024 for senders >5k/day. High deliverability risk if genuinely absent at origin (vs. stripped by AgentMail relay).
- `[WARN] Authentication-Results` header not captured via relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unverifiable from this sample.
- Physical postal address and visible unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from truncated body.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot evaluate — landing-page URLs and their UTM parameters are not present in the truncated HTML excerpt. No evidence of UTM tagging on the one visible asset URL (`link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...gif`), but that is a tracking pixel/image, not a click destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers at the ESP (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement) — verify whether absence is real or a relay-capture artifact.
2. Add descriptive `alt` attribute to `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_123960_1218627_133308.gif` (or `alt=""` if purely decorative/tracking pixel).
3. Regenerate the plain-text alternative so it contains readable copy, not just a dump of tracking URLs — current 88% URL ratio raises spam-filter scores.
4. Fix template CSS hygiene in `.ctacr1`: replace empty `color:;` and `border-radius:px;` declarations with valid values; correct `verticl-align` typo.
5. Add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta for consistent mobile scaling.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `create.hobbylobby.com` independently (e.g., via raw headers at a real inbox) since the relay strips `Authentication-Results`.
