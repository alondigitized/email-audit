# 🎄 Bring The Season To Life With 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby's subject line promises "50% Off Christmas" with a festive tree emoji, but the rendered email never delivers that offer — the Christmas section (Joy: Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) is a plain category tile grid with zero percentage callouts, and the 40%/30% badges further down the email are attached to Fall, Home Decor, Football Party, Floral, Yarn, and Crafts, not Christmas. That's a genuine subject/body mismatch — the single biggest promise in the subject line is nowhere to be found once you open the email.
- Past that miss, this is Hobby Lobby doing what it does best in September: burying a Christmas-in-September hero under a wall of unrelated Fall/football/yarn tile promos, which is the same category-grid pattern flagged repeatedly in this brand's history (09/03's "Select Home Decor" 4/10, 08/30's "Shop Up To 50%" 4/10). The Christmas hero image itself is genuinely strong — a styled tree, wrapped gifts, a toddler and dog, stockings — probably the best seasonal photography this account has run, but it's let down by a video play-button overlay with no visible video context, and it's immediately followed by generic Fall content that dilutes the Christmas premise entirely.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel visual, false promise in the subject, and a body that pivots away from its own headline within two scrolls. This lands in the same mediocre band as the rest of this brand's recent sends.

## What's working

- The Christmas hero photo (decorated tree, wrapped gifts, stockings, toddler + dog) is the most compelling lifestyle shot this brand has sent recently — genuine warmth and seasonal specificity.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" banner is immediate, concrete, and above the fold.
- The Christmas category tile row (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) is clearly organized with consistent iconography and "In-Store Or Online" tags.
- Sale end date ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, September 5, 2026") gives real urgency and specificity near the footer.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or lorem ipsum — the render is clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "50% Off Christmas" but no 50%-off Christmas offer appears anywhere in the body — the Christmas tiles carry no discount badge at all.
- The email pivots hard into unrelated Fall/Home Decor/Football Party/Yarn/Crafts promos immediately after the Christmas hero, diluting the headline premise.
- The play-button overlay on the hero image implies a video, but nothing below explains or delivers on it — dead-end interaction cue.
- The KinKeeping teaser banner at the very top competes with the Christmas hero for attention before the reader even gets to the subject's actual promise.
- It's a Christmas-in-early-September send with a fall promo majority — the body doesn't commit to either season, weakening both.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the core mismatch first: either put a visible "50% Off Christmas" badge directly on/near the hero image, or rewrite the subject to match what's actually offered (the Fall/Home Decor 40% tiles). This is the single highest-impact fix — right now the subject is a false promise.
- 2. Move all Fall/Football/Yarn/Crafts tiles into a separate send or below a clear section break so the Christmas content isn't undercut by off-season competition within the same scroll.
- 3. Either link the play button to an actual video or remove the play-button overlay — it currently sets an expectation the email doesn't pay off.
- 4. Drop or relocate the KinKeeping banner so the Christmas hero is the first thing seen, matching the subject line's promise.
- 5. Add "In-Store Or Online" percentage badges to the Christmas category tiles themselves so the discount is visible where the category is, not three scrolls later.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Christmas Trees, Decor & More — Save Big`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Deck the halls early: trees, wreaths, stockings all on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on orders $59+ through Saturday.`
