# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the kind of homepage I expect from Hobby Lobby — clean, no-nonsense, and straight to the point on that rotating 50% off deal I already knew was coming. Right now they're pushing The Spring Shop, and the current-season relevance is solid. It reads like a weekly circular brought online, which honestly is fine by me.

## What's working

- **The 50% off Spring Shop callout** — I already track these rotating categories, and seeing the big bold number right away confirms I came to the right place. The sale-end date ("May 23, 2026") is right there below the button, which I appreciate.
- **Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More** — the banner at the very top answers the shipping question before I even ask it. That threshold is reachable for me in a single decor haul.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store" line** — this is the single most reassuring thing on the page for someone like me who still prefers walking the aisles. Confirming it works in-store makes this worth my trip.
- **Weekly Ad button** — I use this religiously. Having it as a prominent navy button up top, before the search bar, shows they know their customer.
- **Clean mobile layout** — the hierarchy is hamburger menu → logo → Weekly Ad / search → hero → offer. Nothing fighting for attention.

## What's weak

- **The hero image doesn't speak to my category** — picnic plates and tomatoes say "kitchen tablescape," not farmhouse decor or faux florals. I'm already a loyal buyer but this image isn't pulling me deeper into my department.
- **No loyalty hook anywhere above the fold** — I've been shopping Hobby Lobby for years and there's no acknowledgment of that. No rewards balance, no member exclusives, nothing.
- **"The Spring Shop®" is vague** — I have to already know what that category covers. A new visitor has zero idea. Even one line of sub-copy ("Seasonal decor, florals, and more") would help.
- **No personality below the offer** — there's a hint of an orange strip at the very bottom of the viewport but it's cropped off. Above that, once you've absorbed the 50% off, there's nothing pulling me to scroll. No secondary category, no lifestyle rail, nothing.
- **Zero farmhouse or floral visual cue** — the entire above-the-fold experience could be for a kitchen/entertaining shopper. I'm here for stems and galvanized buckets, not blue fish plates.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the picnic hero for a farmhouse/faux floral lifestyle shot** — a styled vignette with a wooden tray, dried pampas, and linen textures would land for me and the substantial overlap of decor-focused shoppers who make up Hobby Lobby's core base.
- **Add a one-line descriptor under "The Spring Shop®"** — something like "Seasonal decor, floral stems, entertaining accents" takes two seconds to read and immediately confirms whether this sale is for me.
- **Surface the loyalty/rewards program above the fold** — even a small "Rewards Member? Sign in to see your savings" line near the CTA would make returning shoppers feel seen.
- **Add an in-store callout with more weight** — "Also valid in-store through May 23" is buried in small gray text. Bold it, or give it a small icon. I'm a weekly in-store visitor and that line is my green light.
