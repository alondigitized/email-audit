# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same wall-to-wall category-tile playbook Hobby Lobby has run since late August — now stretched to roughly 25 stacked category blocks running from Home Decor to Furniture, each with its own percentage badge (40%, 50%, 25%, 30%, 60% off). The subject "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price" points at one category, but Home Decor is just the first of dozens of tiles that follow, including a "New Everyday Low Price" candle block that contradicts the urgency framing entirely.
- This is a near carbon copy of the 2026-09-10 "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price" send — same subject, same structure, same critique applies again. The brand has not iterated on this format across at least four sends now (09-03, 09-10, 09-13, 09-17), which suggests either a template default or a segmentation failure that's letting the identical scroll-forever grid go out under different subject lines.
- Judgement: this is deliverability filler, not a targeted send. A recipient interested specifically in home decor gets buried three seconds into a grid that never differentiates itself from a general catalog blast.

## What's working

- The top banner clearly states "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" — a concrete, unambiguous offer visible immediately.
- Each tile has a clean percentage badge and readable "Categories Listed" subtext, so the discount structure is legible.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent block-to-block.
- The Home Decor block (matching the subject) is placed first, so there's at least nominal hierarchy toward the named topic.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Home Decor" but the email is a 25+ category everything-store grid — Furniture, Yarn, Sewing Notions, Jewelry Making, Football Party goods all appear with equal visual weight.
- This is a repeat of the identical 09-10 send with the same subject line — zero creative evolution in a week.
- "Always 40% Off" and "New Everyday Low Price" (candles) appear in the same email, undermining any sense of urgency or event-based promotion.
- No single hero image or CTA — with this many tiles, the eye has nowhere specific to land, so nothing reads as "the offer."
- Christmas is being pushed in mid-September alongside Football Party supplies and Fall decor, which reads as unfocused rather than seasonally sharp.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the 3-4 categories the subject actually promises (Home Decor, Framed Art, Wall Clocks, Mirrors) and split everything else into separate sends — this is the single highest-leverage fix since it would finally make subject and body match.
- 2. Give Home Decor an actual hero treatment (lifestyle photo + one big CTA button) instead of a tile among 25, matching what worked in the 09-15 Christmas Trees send (7.00/10).
- 3. Remove "New Everyday Low Price" language from inside a 40%-off email — it's a self-contradicting signal that should live in its own send.
- 4. Stop reusing the exact 09-10 subject line verbatim; even minor variation would reduce the pattern-matched "seen this already" fatigue for repeat subscribers.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Framed art, wall clocks, tapestries and more — always 40% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Refresh a room this weekend. Home decor at 40% off, no coupon needed.`
