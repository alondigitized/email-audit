# Celebrate Fall In Style With 50% Off 🍂
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, now wearing a fall subject line. The subject promises "50% Off" fall, and the 50% Fall hero does deliver it. But it sits below a free-shipping bar and a "Christmas Essentials" banner with three category tiles. A subscriber who opens for fall sees Christmas first.
- The back half is a 20-plus-tile grid of everyday craft supplies: chargers, foam bricks, wreaths, candy wafers, batteries, glue. None of it is fall-themed, and none of it is 50% off. It reads as a price list, not a campaign. The offer is also qualified by "*See Weekly Ad For Details," and the sale-end date ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 31, 2026") is only in the footer.
- Verdict: a solid, trusted-brand send with a subject and hero that match, but a muddled layout. It follows the 10/27 "Fun & Quirky Decor" and 10/25 "50% Off Fall & Christmas" sends, which were also circulars. Frequency is high and the creative blurs together.

## What's working

- The subject names a concrete offer (50% off), and the fall hero with its big "50% off Fall" badge backs it up.
- The hero is an appealing pumpkin and autumn tablescape that fits the season.
- Six fall category tiles (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) make browsing easy.
- The product tiles have clear prices, size details and in-store or online availability.
- Nothing is broken: no overlapping text or missing images.

## What's weak

- The Christmas Essentials banner and its three tiles sit above the fall hero, so the subject's promise is pushed down the page.
- The fall hero's fine print ("*See Weekly Ad For Details") hedges the 50% claim, and the actual end date is only in the footer.
- The long product grid is generic craft supplies, not fall items. It dilutes the offer and makes the email very long.
- CTAs are repetitive "In-Store Or Online ▶" links on every tile, with no single bold button. "Click here to view our weekly ad" is a plain text bar.
- Several sends in a row (10/23, 10/25, 10/27) use the same circular structure, so this one feels like more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% Fall hero first and move the Christmas banner below the fall categories. If Christmas must lead, change the subject to match it.
- 2. Add one real button on the hero ("Shop 50% Off Fall Decor") and state what is included and when it ends ("Ends Saturday 10/31") right on the hero.
- 3. Trim the product grid to 6–8 fall-relevant items, or group it under a "Low Prices on Craft Staples" header, so it doesn't read as noise.
- 4. Put the deadline in the preheader and near the top of the email, not just the footer.
- 5. Vary the creative. This is the fourth near-identical circular in a week.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: 50% Off Fall Through 10/31`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pumpkins, florals & candles – sale ends Saturday, Oct 31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on orders $59 or more. Shop fall before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based ESP template (Mindbody-style `e-locale` attributes) with several CSS defects and no `lang` attribute. The QA data raises no hard failures, but header-level checks (unsubscribe, authentication) couldn't be verified. The HTML is truncated, so body-level checks such as link targets, UTMs, and the footer are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 15,075 of 17,763 characters (about 85%) are URLs. That is well above the 70% threshold. The long tracked redirect URLs on `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...` are the likely cause. Spam filters can penalize this, and the text version is hard to read.
- **Tracking domain.** Links and images are served from `link.hobbylobby.com`, which is first-party and generally good for deliverability. Probing covered only 12 of 51 links (39 skipped, mostly footer and social). No broken links were reported among the 12 probed. The skipped links are unverified.
- No other link issues are confirmed from the available source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_141893_2261558_150920.gif` has no `alt` attribute. If this is a 1x1 open-tracking pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix. If it is visible content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag has `e-locale="en-US"` and `e-is-multilanguage="false"`, which are non-standard and not read by assistive tech. Add `lang="en"`.
- **Invalid CSS in the `.ctacr1` rule:** `color:;`, `-moz-border-radius:px;`, `-webkit-border-radius:px;` and `border-radius:px;`. These are empty or unitless values that clients drop. The rule's rounded corners and text color won't apply.
- **Typo in `.lhp2`:** `verticl-align:middle` should be `vertical-align`, so the declaration is ignored.
- **Viewport meta:** `content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes"` omits `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may not scale correctly.
- **Responsive breakpoint:** the visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is narrower than almost any phone viewport, so the mobile overrides (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`) likely never fire. This is based on the visible portion only. Other queries may exist in the truncated CSS.
- **Doctype mismatch:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is declared alongside XHTML and VML `xmlns` attributes. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Hobby Lobby` doesn't reflect the subject line. This is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject line. The truncated portion can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Check the raw message from the sending side. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA notes this is expected via the relay. Check `create.hobbylobby.com` DNS records directly.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result is shown. The footer is truncated, so the postal address can't be confirmed.
- The From domain `create.hobbylobby.com` is a subdomain, with `noreply@` as the sender. Replies won't reach anyone, which is a minor concern.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. Links route through `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects, so UTMs would only be visible after following the redirect. The QA run reported no redirect or landing page errors for the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw sent message, bypassing the relay. If missing at the source, add both.
2. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `create.hobbylobby.com` through DNS or a seed test.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `alt` to `.../eORqXTkAb7_..._150920.gif` (`alt=""` if it's a tracking pixel).
4. Fix the `.ctacr1` CSS (`color`, `border-radius` values) and the `verticl-align` typo.
5. Add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta. Confirm the real mobile breakpoint, since `max-width:300px` is likely ineffective.
6. Trim the plain-text part. Shorten tracked URLs, or add more readable copy relative to links.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check UTMs, the footer address, and the skipped links.
