# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby is leading with their signature Home Decor deal this week, but whoever picked that hero image was not thinking about me — that's a Route 66, motorcycle-and-man-cave vibe, not the shiplap-and-dried-botanicals crowd I run with. The 40% off Home Decor messaging is familiar territory; I've known that deal longer than my youngest niece has been alive. It looks clean on my phone, but there's nothing here that makes me feel seen as a returning customer who practically lives in their floral aisle.

## What's working

- **Free shipping threshold** is front and center in the red top banner — $59 is easy to hit in one trip to the faux floral aisle, and I appreciate knowing it before I even start browsing
- **40% off Home Decor always-on pricing** is called out clearly below the hero — I already knew this, but a newcomer would feel like they found a great deal
- **The "Shop Now" CTA** is well-sized for a phone screen, high contrast navy on white, and the date range underneath ("through June 6, 2026") makes it feel current and legitimate
- **"Weekly Ad" button** in the nav is a genuine win for someone like me who plans her week around the rotating sale — it's one tap from the homepage

## What's weak

- **The hero image is a mismatch**: Route 66 wall art and a motorcycle parked in a vintage garage room — that's a Pinterest board for a 55-year-old guy restoring a muscle car, not for me shopping farmhouse wreaths and tabletop botanicals
- **No personalization whatsoever**: I've shopped here dozens of times and the homepage treats me like a stranger; not even a "Back this week?" nudge or a seasonal "You might also love..." module
- **The 40% off claim needs context**: "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" is HL's baseline — as a weekly shopper I'm always hunting for the 50%-off rotating category that beats this. Nothing above the fold tells me what's at 50% this week, which is actually the more exciting number
- **No faux floral, no botanicals, no farmhouse signal anywhere**: not in the image, not in the copy — someone who didn't know the store would have no idea this is where I spend half my decorating budget

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a farmhouse lifestyle room**: shiplap wall, galvanized vase, faux eucalyptus stems, a linen runner on a farmhouse table — that hero would stop me mid-scroll
- **Add a "This Week's 50% Off Category" strip** directly below the hero: even a single line — "This week: Seasonal Floral 50% off" — would bring me in the door faster than any generic Home Decor claim
- **Surface the Weekly Ad content on the homepage**: don't make me tap a separate button to find the sale I came for; show the category grid right below the hero so I can navigate in one move
- **Add a lightweight returning-customer hook**: even just "Shop your recent finds" or a saved-items count badge on the heart icon would make me feel like a valued regular instead of a cold visit
