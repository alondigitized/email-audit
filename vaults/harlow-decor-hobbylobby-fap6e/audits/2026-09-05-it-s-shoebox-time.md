# It’s Shoebox Time!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine outlier in Hobby Lobby's send history — the first email in this stretch that isn't a promotional discount push. It's a partnership email for Operation Christmas Child (Samaritan's Purse), walking subscribers through "Find a Shoebox → Fill With Gifts → Drop-Off" with a clear November 16–23 collection deadline. There's no discount, no % off, no urgency-driven CTA — this is brand-halo/goodwill content, not a sales email, and it should be scored accordingly.
- As a mission-driven send it's well executed: real, warm photography, a clean three-step visual flow, and a genuinely different color-blocked layout (green/red) that breaks the wall-to-wall category-tile monotony flagged in 08/23, 08/27, 08/30, and 09/03 sends. But as a revenue driver it's weak — there's no product, no price, no reason to click beyond charitable participation, and it will underperform on click/conversion metrics that matter for the "everyday deals" cadence.
- Judgement: strong creative departure, low commercial pull. Fine as an occasional trust/brand-affinity send, but it shouldn't be mistaken for demand generation.

## What's working

- Distinct green/red color-blocked layout breaks the repetitive tile-grid pattern seen across nearly every recent send.
- Three-step structure (Find a Shoebox → Fill With Gifts → Drop-Off) is easy to scan and mirrors how a first-time participant would actually think through the task.
- Real, emotionally resonant photography (kids receiving boxes, a parent packing one, volunteers dropping off) rather than stock product shots.
- Concrete, credible date given for National Collection Week (November 16–23, 2026) — real urgency without hype.
- Each section has its own clear CTA button ("In-Store or Online," "Shop Now," "Find Location").

## What's weak

- No offer or discount anywhere — for a subscriber trained on this list for daily % off deals, this may read as off-brand or a non-sequitur.
- "Shop Now" CTA under "Fill With Gifts" doesn't specify what's being shopped — no product category, price point, or hero item shown.
- Hero section ("Send Good News & Great Joy!") and "Pack a Shoebox" banner underneath feel redundant — two CTAs stacked before any content.
- No loyalty/rewards tie-in even though Hobby Lobby has been leaning on member-style messaging elsewhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the vague "Shop Now" CTA with a specific one tied to the actual shoebox-filler product page (e.g., "Shop Stocking Stuffers") so there's a clear commercial path buried inside the goodwill message.
- 2. Cut either the top hero banner or the "Pack a Shoebox" green bar — they're duplicative asks in the first fold.
- 3. Add a small itemized "wow item" product strip (soccer ball, stuffed animal, school supplies) with prices, giving the charitable email a soft commercial layer without undermining the mission tone.
- 4. Tie this into loyalty/rewards messaging (e.g., "earn points on shoebox-filler purchases") to give repeat subscribers a self-interested reason to click, not just altruism.
