# ✨ Elevate Your Holiday Hosting With 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject oversells it. "Elevate Your Holiday Hosting With 50% Off Christmas" promises a 50%-off Christmas offer. The email's big "Hosting For The Holidays" video hero has no discount on it. The only visible 50% block is "50% Off Fall," far down the scroll. Christmas gets category tiles (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) with no price attached. A subscriber who opens for 50% off Christmas will not find it.
- The email looks polished at the top. The hero is a strong table-setting image, and the free-shipping bar ($69+) is clear. After that it turns into the usual long scroll: 50% Fall, 40% Home Decor, 40% Hobbies, 40% Art Supplies, 30% Fabric, 40% Jewelry Making. Six equal-weight blocks compete, so nothing stands out. The sale ends Saturday, November 7, which is real urgency, but it sits in a small footer line.
- Verdict: a competent send with a mismatched subject. It scores like the 11/05 and 11/04 sends, which were also generic circulars.

## What's working

- The "Hosting For The Holidays" hero is large and seasonal, and the table setting is appealing. It fits the subject's hosting theme.
- The free-shipping bar ($69+) sits at the very top and is easy to read.
- The discount numbers ("50%", "40%", "30%") are big and scannable, and the alternating image/text layout keeps the scroll readable.
- The "Christmas Essentials" banner and its six-tile grid give a clear way into holiday categories.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 7, 2026" line gives a credible deadline.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "50% Off Christmas," but the only 50% offer visible is Fall. The Christmas tiles carry no price or discount.
- The email is a six-block discount wall with no single lead offer. Fall at 50%, Home Decor at 40%, Hobbies at 40% and so on all get the same weight.
- The hero is a video play button with no visible CTA copy and no offer. The eye lands on "Hosting For The Holidays" with nothing to click for a price.
- The CTA "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" is a generic bar that sits above the offers instead of being tied to one.
- Fall discounting is front and centre in early November while the hero pushes Christmas. The two messages pull against each other.
- The deadline is buried in small footer text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the email. Either lead with a real Christmas offer or retitle around what's actually in the render (for example, the Fall 50% and Home Decor 40% blocks).
- 2. Put one price on the hero. Add something like "Christmas Essentials, up to 50% off" and a visible button under the video.
- 3. Cut the six-block wall to two or three offers and move Fall lower, since it's seasonally stale next to Christmas.
- 4. Move "Ends Saturday, 11/7" into the top of the email, next to the shipping bar.
- 5. Add price points to the Christmas category tiles ("Trees from $X").
