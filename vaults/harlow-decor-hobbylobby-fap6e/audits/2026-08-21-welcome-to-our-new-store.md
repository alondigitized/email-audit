# Welcome to our new store!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This isn't a promo email at all — it's a new-store-opening announcement for Ontario, CA, and after twelve days of straight discount blasts in this brand history, that's a genuine change of pace. No % off, no urgency banner, just "Be Inspired With Over 80,000 Products!" and a clean four-tile lifestyle collage (framed florals, painted ceramics, craft supplies, sunflower arrangements). The imagery is the best-composed set I've seen from this sender's recent run — bright, on-brand, no stock-photo staleness.
- The problem is the subject-to-body mismatch and the geographic irrelevance: "Welcome to our new store!" reads like a personal onboarding email, but the body is a hyper-local store-opening notice for a specific California address most subscribers don't live near. There's no offer, no personalization, and no clear reason for a non-Ontario-area recipient to act on anything here.
- Net: a well-produced, off-target send. If this went to Ontario-area subscribers only, it's a strong 7; sent broadly with this subject, it's a middling, easily-ignored 5.

## What's working

- Four-image collage (framed art, ceramics, craft jars, sunflowers) is sharp, well-lit, and cohesive — a step up from the tile-grid sameness flagged on 8/20 and 8/19.
- Store address and city are stated clearly up top with a matching "Now Open" badge treatment.
- "Live A Creative Life!" seal and category list (Home Decor, Floral & Wedding, Crafts, etc.) reinforce brand identity without clutter.
- No render bugs — clean spacing, aligned nav bar, working-looking icons throughout.

## What's weak

- Subject "Welcome to our new store!" doesn't match the body's actual content (a physical location opening), which is confusing on first read.
- Zero offer or discount — a hard reset after a week of 40-75%-off subjects, likely to underperform on click given trained subscriber expectations.
- No geographic targeting signal visible — nothing indicates this was filtered to Ontario, CA-area subscribers, so most recipients get an irrelevant local announcement.
- "Click here to take a tour" CTA is vague and generic, not tied to any specific action or product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix subject-body alignment: lead with the actual news. This is a location-opening email, not a "welcome" email — the subject should say so.
- 2. Add explicit local targeting language or an opening promo (e.g., "20% off your first visit") to give Ontario-area recipients a reason to walk in.
- 3. Replace "Click here to take a tour" with a specific action — "Get Directions" or "See Store Hours" — since that's what a new-store email should drive.
- 4. If this is meant for broader (non-local) distribution, reframe entirely around the product collage and drop the store-opening framing, or segment the send so non-Ontario subscribers get a different subject.
