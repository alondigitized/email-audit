# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Right away I know I'm in the right place — those fall wreaths on the hero are exactly what I'd be searching for this time of year, and I can already feel my hands wanting to tap "Shop Now." Hobby Lobby is pushing their Fall category hard with a 40% off sale that expires in literally two days, which is a real kick in the pants to act now. This feels like a store that knows its customer and isn't wasting my time.

## What's working

- **The hero image is a home run.** A styled display of fall wreaths — sunflowers, pumpkins, dried botanicals — against a white shiplap fireplace. That's farmhouse decor in its purest form. I felt a little flutter of "I need that."
- **40% Off Fall with a hard deadline.** "Sale Valid Online & In-Store through July 25, 2026" is printed plainly. Two days left. That's real urgency, not manufactured pressure.
- **The Weekly Ad button.** Front and center, just below the logo. I know the weekly ad better than I know my own grocery list. The fact that it's the first CTA tells me the brand still respects the in-store shopper.
- **Free shipping at $59** is visible in the very first banner. That's a threshold I'd almost certainly clear with a couple of wreaths.
- **Clean mobile layout.** No crowding, no text overlapping the image, no bizarre white gaps.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook visible.** Hobby Lobby doesn't have a traditional rewards program — I know that — but there's zero acknowledgment of me as a repeat customer. No "check your weekly ad picks," no personalization cue at all.
- **"Shop Now" is generic.** In the context of "40% Off Fall" it works, but it could be "Shop Fall Decor" or "Shop Wreaths & Stems" and immediately tell me what category I'm landing in.
- **No product previews below the hero.** I can see the sale is on "Fall" — but which Fall? Wreaths? Stems? Tabletop? I have to tap to find out. A quick row of category thumbnails would let me self-select faster.
- **No human in the imagery.** The decor is beautiful but I can't picture myself in it. A lifestyle shot with a woman my age arranging a wreath over a mantle would hit differently.
- **The asterisk on "40% Off Fall — See Weekly Ad For Details" is a small friction.** I know what it means, but a newer shopper might hesitate.

## Recommendations

- **Add a category strip below the hero** — three or four tappable thumbnail tiles ("Wreaths," "Stems & Branches," "Lanterns & Vases," "Tabletop") so I can land in the exact aisle I want without a detour through a broad Fall page.
- **Sharpen the CTA copy** to "Shop Fall Decor" or "Shop 40% Off Wreaths" — still one tap, but now it tells me exactly what I'm getting into.
- **Add a countdown timer** to the sale end date. "2 days left" next to "July 25, 2026" would have me tapping within five seconds. Right now the date is there but it doesn't feel urgent.
- **Test a lifestyle hero** featuring a woman in her 40s or 50s hanging a wreath on a farmhouse front door — move from styled product shot to aspirational moment.
