# 🏠Renew Your Space With Select Home Decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's mega-circular in editorial cosplay. The subject line borrows the lifestyle angle that made the July 10 "Style Your Home With Hobby Lobby" send earn an 8/10, but the email itself delivers the same stacked two-column tile grid that has now run on July 2, July 7, July 9, and July 12. If you pulled the subject off and replaced it with "Shop Up To 75% Off Savings Inside," the email body would be interchangeable.
- The content sprawl undercuts the subject's promise. "Renew Your Space With Select Home Decor" implies a curated edit; what you get is 15+ category tiles ranging from Classroom Supplies and Dorm Essentials to Jewelry Making and Sewing Notions. "Select Home Decor" it is not. The "Fall Decor" category tile appearing in mid-July is the sharpest signal that this is a templated batch drop with a fresh subject swapped in.
- There are real savings here — 75% off Home Decor and 66% off Furniture are genuinely strong numbers — but the flat, equal-weight tile format ensures those standout offers compete with everything else at once. No single offer wins.

## What's working

- **Home Decor leads at the top** — 40% off is the first thing the eye hits, which creates at least some alignment with the subject line.
- **Discount depth is real** — 75% off Home Decor and 66% off Furniture are legitimately compelling numbers, not the usual 40% on everything.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, imagery loads, text is readable throughout.
- **Summer Shop section** — a brief lifestyle moment mid-email breaks up the tile grid and is at least seasonally appropriate.

## What's weak

- **Subject overpromises, email overdelivers (the wrong way)** — "Select Home Decor" implies a curated edit; 15+ unrelated department tiles is the opposite.
- **Fall Decor tile appears in mid-July** — a visible seasonal mismatch that signals templated batch deploy over intentional send.
- **No primary CTA** — every tile competes equally; there's no anchor button or single "Shop Now" moment the eye can commit to.
- **Fifth mega-circular in 14 days** — this format fatigue was already flagged in the July 12 audit; running it again with only a swapped subject is a cadence problem.
- **"Spring Shop" still referenced** — alongside Summer Shop in July; Spring is over, and it makes the editorial feel stale and unconsidered.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the strongest number in the subject.** "75% Off Home Decor" is the actual headline — use it. The current subject buries the lead behind vague editorial copy and "Select" dilutes the offer before the reader even opens.
- 2. **Cut the category count in half.** Choose five home-adjacent tiles (Furniture, Home Decor, Textiles, Custom Frames, Lighting) and remove Classroom, Dorm, Party Supplies, and Sewing Notions. Let the email match what the subject promised.
- 3. **Remove the Fall Decor tile.** It's July 16. Showing fall content right now reads as inattentive and breaks the "Renew Your Space" seasonal frame.
- 4. **Add a single hero CTA button** above the tile grid — "Shop Home Decor Deals" — so there's one unambiguous click target before the catalog begins.
- 5. **Retire "Spring Shop."** It's three months stale. Summer Shop alone is fine.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — MISSING (QA confirmed)**
The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not detected by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since February 2024) for senders above 5,000 msgs/day. Absence does not mean the header was never sent — it may not be captured by the relay — but it must be verified at the raw SMTP level.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) — MISSING (QA confirmed)**
One-click unsubscribe (required by the same Gmail/Yahoo policy) depends on both `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>` or `<https://...>` AND `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Without the POST header, Gmail will not render its native one-click unsubscribe button, increasing spam complaint risk.

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical mailing address must appear in the email body. Not verifiable from the truncated HTML — should be confirmed against the full render.

**Authentication — UNKNOWN (QA confirmed)**
`Authentication-Results` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Given the sending domain is `create.hobbylobby.com` (subdomain), the DKIM signing domain and DMARC alignment should be verified via `nslookup -type=TXT _dmarc.hobbylobby.com`.
