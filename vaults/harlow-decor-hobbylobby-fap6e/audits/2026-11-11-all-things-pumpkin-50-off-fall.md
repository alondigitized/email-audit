# All Things Pumpkin! 50% Off Fall 🍁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is only half right. "All Things Pumpkin! 50% Off Fall 🍁" matches the big "50% OFF Fall" hero, which sits over a styled table of orange pumpkin dishes. But "All Things Pumpkin" overpromises. The fall grid below it has Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles and Crafts tiles, and pumpkin is only a small part of that. A free-shipping bar and a "Christmas Essentials" banner sit above the hero and pull the eye away from it.
- The back two-thirds of the email is a different message. It is a long stack of everyday-price items: chargers, foam bricks, wreaths, candy wafers, glue, a $49.99 helium tank and a $24.99 epoxy. None of it is fall, and none of it is pumpkin. The "Click here to view our weekly ad" button is a plain text bar, and it sits well below the hero. It is the only real CTA, and every tile is a small "In-Store Or Online" link.
- Against the last week of sends, the 50% Fall offer is the same one as 11/03 ("Serve In Style With 50% Off Fall") and 11/08 ("50% Off Christmas & Fall"). Cadence is heavy and the creative repeats. The subject is a bit more specific than 11/03's, but the email is the same template. Verdict: a solid hero, but diluted by the layout and the cadence.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF Fall hero is large, legible and on-season, with a warm pumpkin-dish photo.
- The subject's "50% Off Fall" matches a visible, honest hero offer.
- The six-tile fall grid (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) lets you pick a category.
- The price tags in the product grid are big and easy to scan.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 14, 2026" footer gives a real deadline.

## What's weak

- "All Things Pumpkin" overpromises, because the email is not mostly pumpkin.
- The free-shipping bar and the Christmas Essentials banner come before the hero and compete with it.
- The back half of the email is unrelated everyday-price items, so the fall message gets lost.
- There is one text-bar CTA and no button on the hero. Every tile just says "In-Store Or Online".
- It is the third near-identical 50% Fall send in nine days.
- The 50% hero fine print ("See Weekly Ad For Details") is small and vague about what is excluded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 50% Fall hero to the top. Put the free-shipping bar and the Christmas banner below it.
- 2. Give the hero a real button, such as "Shop 50% Off Fall Decor", linked to a fall category page.
- 3. Cut the everyday-price grid to four items, or replace it with pumpkin and fall products that match the subject.
- 4. State the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Saturday 11/14".
- 5. Rotate the hero creative, or the offer, so it doesn't repeat the 11/03 and 11/08 sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based, Outlook-conditional template with ESP-wrapped tracking links (`link.hobbylobby.com`). The QA run found no hard failures, only 4 warnings. Most of the compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...` redirector. Only 12 of 51 links were probed and 39 were skipped, so the unprobed links' destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 15,075 of 17,763 characters (about 85%) are URLs. That is well over the 70% threshold, and it comes from long tracking URLs. A text part this heavy can look spammy to filters and is hard to read for text-only clients.
- **Tracking image:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_143392_2221790_152876.gif` follows the same tracking-path pattern as the other links. It may be an open-tracking pixel, so I can't confirm it's a content image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The image above has no `alt` attribute. If it is a tracking pixel, add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The only `@media` query visible is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Phones are about 320–430px wide, so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.h1`, etc.) would not fire on typical devices. The visible source doesn't show another breakpoint, but it is truncated, so a later one may exist.
- **Broken CSS in the template:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values, which look like unrendered template variables. Clients ignore them, but any element using `.ctacr1` gets no text color or radius.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so it has no effect.
- **Markup oddities:**
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag uses XHTML and VML namespaces and non-standard `e-locale` and `e-is-multilanguage` attributes. Clients tolerate this.
  - `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject.
- **Viewport:** `user-scalable=yes` is fine for accessibility.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the truncated excerpt. The `px` and empty-value CSS above are template-variable remnants in CSS only, not in visible content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found (RFC 8058 one-click is not detected). The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the sender does omit them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `create.hobbylobby.com` and the tracking domain `link.hobbylobby.com` are different subdomains, so alignment should be verified at the source.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** The QA run probed an unsubscribe link, but the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the link or a physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The destination URLs are hidden behind the redirector and none of the 12 probed links is detailed in the QA data. No UTM parameters are visible in the excerpt, but any may be appended after the redirect. Landing-page alignment with "All Things Pumpkin! 50% Off Fall" is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture the raw headers from the original delivery, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
2. Add `alt` text (or `alt=""` for a pixel) to the `.gif` at `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_...gif`.
3. Fix `.ctacr1` (empty `color:` and `px` radius) and the `verticl-align` typo.
4. Check whether a responsive breakpoint wider than 300px exists in the full source. If not, raise it to about 600px.
5. Shorten or reduce the tracking URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
6. Re-run link probing over the 39 skipped links, and record the final landing URLs and their UTM parameters.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe, address) and merge tokens.
