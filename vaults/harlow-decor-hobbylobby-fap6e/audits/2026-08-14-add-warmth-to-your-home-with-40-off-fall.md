# Add Warmth To Your Home With 40% Off Fall
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- "Add Warmth To Your Home With 40% Off Fall" is the most on-theme, on-subject Hobby Lobby send in the recent run — the "Fall Indoor Decor" hero with the video-style play button and warm pumpkin/plaid styling actually matches what the subject promises, unlike 08-11's "Cozy Living" bait-and-switch into the generic grid. But it still can't resist collapsing into the same sprawling tile dump this brand runs every day: after the fall hero and six fall-category tiles, it pivots hard into Christmas (50% off), football party, fabric, classroom decor — a full seasonal grab-bag that undercuts the "warmth" framing from the subject line.
- The good news is the fall content up top is genuinely more cohesive than the last several sends — a real lifestyle photo, matching iconography, warm color story. The bad news is that cohesion evaporates by the third scroll and reverts to Hobby Lobby's default: everything, all at once, at 30–50% off, with no seasonal editing discipline. This is a marginal improvement over the 5.00 average this brand's been posting, but not a real departure from the pattern.

## What's working

- Hero image ("Fall Indoor Decor") is a genuine lifestyle shot with pumpkins, plaid pillows, and warm framed art — visually matches the subject's "warmth" promise.
- Fall category icons (Fall Decor, Fall Floral, Fall Kitchen, Fall Party, Fall Candles, Fall Crafts) are thematically tight and use consistent iconography.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" banner is a clear, visible offer near the top.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- The email abandons "fall warmth" by the middle third, pivoting to 50% off Christmas, football party, and classroom decor — none of which support the subject line.
- Visual hierarchy after the hero is just a repeating price-tile pattern (40% / 50% / 40% / 30% / 40%) with no differentiation — the eye has nowhere specific to land.
- Fine print ("Selection Will Vary By Store," "See Weekly Ad For Details") appears on nearly every discount tile, which is a soft disclaimer stacking up across nine separate offers.
- CTA copy is repetitive and generic — "In-Store Or Online ▸" appears a dozen+ times with no variation or single strong primary action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the fall content (hero + 6 fall category tiles) and drop Christmas, football, fabric, and classroom entirely — a focused fall email would finally deliver on a subject line this brand keeps promising and not delivering.
- 2. Replace the repeated "In-Store Or Online ▸" links with one distinct primary CTA button (e.g., "Shop Fall Decor") styled differently from the rest.
- 3. Lead with the 40% Home Decor discount tile immediately under the hero instead of burying it after two rows of category icons — impatient scrollers won't reach it.
- 4. Drop "Selection Will Vary By Store" repetition — state it once near the top instead of on every tile.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pumpkins, plaid, and warm tones — all 40% off through Saturday.`
