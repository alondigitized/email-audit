# Make Hosting & Gift-Giving Easy With Hobby Lobby®
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject only half matches it. "Make Hosting & Gift-Giving Easy" fits the Game Night & Gift Ideas hero, a gift basket with dominoes and a play-button overlay. The rest is a long stack of department offers: 50% off Christmas, 40% off Home Decor, 40% off Floral, 30% off Yarn, 40% off Paper Crafts and 40% off New Year's. The hero has no offer on it and no price, and its play button implies a video that a static inbox render can't deliver.
- The rest of the email is clean and scannable. The alternating image/number rows are a good rhythm, and the big percentages read fast. But it is the same circular that went out on 11/20, 11/22, 11/24, 11/25 and 11/26. The 11/26 "Shop Extended Hours!" email was the same long format with the wrong lead. This one at least leads with something seasonal. The best deal, "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" in the green banner, is competing with five other headline numbers.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but diffuse and repetitive. It is a 6 in a stream of near-identical 6s. The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28" deadline is the most credible urgency in the email, and it sits in the footer.

## What's working

- The 50% / 40% / 30% numerals are huge and easy to scan, and the alternating image/text rows keep a long email readable.
- The green free-shipping banner at the top ($69 threshold) is a clear, universal incentive.
- The Game Night & Gift Ideas hero is seasonal, and the styled basket photo fits the subject's "hosting and gift-giving" theme.
- The blue "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar is a clear, full-width CTA.
- Nothing is broken: images load, nothing overlaps, and the brand voice is consistent.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer, and the play-button overlay promises a video that may not play from the inbox.
- Six equally weighted discount blocks plus a Christmas Essentials banner and a three-tile Kitchen/Bath/Home row leave no single focal offer.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28, 2026" deadline is buried in the footer, so urgency is wasted.
- Nearly every offer is hedged with "Select Categories," "See Weekly Ad For Details" or "Selection Will Vary By Store." That reads as fine print, not a firm promise.
- This is the sixth near-identical circular in a week (11/20 through 11/26 all had the same structure), so there is clear send fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the one best offer in the hero. For example, put "50% Off Christmas" or "30% Off All Yarn" on the Game Night image, and move the deadline up with it: "Ends Saturday."
- 2. Cut the body from about 10 blocks to 3-4 and link each to a landing page for that category, not "weekly ad." Drop the three-tile Kitchen/Bath/Home row or move it below the offers.
- 3. Replace the generic CTA "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas."
- 4. Use a real hero image, or drop the play-button overlay if there is no video. Don't imply motion that a static inbox render can't deliver.
- 5. Pull the free-shipping $69 threshold into the subject, since it is the most concrete universal offer.
- 6. Vary the weekly cadence. Alternate the circular with single-offer sends so the weekly ad doesn't read as the same email each time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping at $69 + 50% off Christmas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Saturday: 50% off Christmas, 30% off yarn`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off home decor and floral. Sale ends Saturday, Nov 28.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a table-based, Outlook-conditioned template sent through a `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking domain. QA reports 0 hard issues and 4 warnings. Header-level findings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are unverifiable because of the AgentMail relay. The source shows a few real CSS defects and a mobile breakpoint that looks wrong.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links detected.** 12 material links were probed and none were flagged. 26 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- **Click and open tracking runs through a redirect domain.** The image `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_145950_3128453_155514.gif` is on the same domain and uses the `/mo/` path. This is probably an open-tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Plain-text part is dominated by URLs.** 10,760 of 12,775 characters (about 84%) are URLs, so the text alternative is mostly long tracking links. That increases spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mobile breakpoint is probably too narrow.** The responsive rules sit in `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Phones are typically 320–430px wide, so the `.fl`, `.fw`, `.mh` and `.image` stacking rules would not fire on most devices. Unless a later, truncated media query covers wider screens, mobile gets the desktop layout.
- **Invalid CSS from unresolved template values:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty `color` and `px` values with no number. Clients will drop these declarations.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so it has no effect.
- **Image missing alt text:** `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_145950_3128453_155514.gif` (QA warning). If it's a 1×1 tracking pixel, use `alt=""`. If it's content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` does not match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the source uses an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype together with XHTML/VML `xmlns` attributes. Clients tolerate this, so it is low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the supplied source. The empty `color:` and `px` values in `.ctacr1` are CSS template artifacts and are covered under Rendering.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are really absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown** because of the relay. This is a verification gap, not a confirmed failure. The From domain is `create.hobbylobby.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing. I can't verify the footer's unsubscribe link or postal address in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. UTM parameters and final landing URLs are not visible in the truncated source or the QA output. Links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirector, so UTMs, if any, are applied after the redirect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the mobile breakpoint.** Confirm whether a wider media query exists (for example `max-width:480px` or `600px`). If not, widen the current one.
2. **Clean the template CSS.** Fix `.ctacr1` (`color:` and `px`) and `verticl-align`.
3. **Set `alt`** on the `/mo/…gif` image: `alt=""` if it's a pixel, otherwise descriptive text.
4. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC directly, since the relay hides them.
5. **Trim the plain-text part** by shortening its tracking URLs or reducing the number of links.
