# 🎄 Deck The Halls With 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**

- The subject promises "50% Off Christmas," and the email never shows that offer. The only percentages on screen are 40% Fall, 40% Home Decor, 40% Party, 40% Floral and 40% Paper Crafts, plus 30% Yarn. A subscriber who opens for half-off Christmas has to hunt for it and won't find it.
- This is also the third Christmas-first send in a week. The 10/11 "Spice Up Your Fall With 40% Off 🍂" and 10/15 "Elevate Your Space With Select Home Decor!" emails opened with the same free-shipping bar and a "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block. This one swaps that block for a video hero, so the discount that earlier sends showed has disappeared. The Christmas push is now the default, and the subject line is the only place the deal appears.
- Judgement: a seasonal, on-brand email with a strong hero and a clean layout. Its main promise is invisible in the render, and the offer is buried under about ten competing tiles. It's a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The Christmas Decor hero is big and warm, and it's clearly the first thing the eye lands on.
- The Christmas Essentials banner and six category tiles (Trees, Floral, Lights, Crafts, Party, Decor) make the seasonal shop easy to scan.
- Nothing is broken: images load and there's no overlapping text.
- The Hobby Lobby header and nav (Weekly Ad, Stores, Shop, DIY) are recognizable and trustworthy.
- The tiles below carry concrete percent-off numbers (40%, 30%, "Up To 75% Off" clearance) in a consistent alternating layout.

## What's weak

- The subject's "50% Off" doesn't appear anywhere on the Christmas hero or banner.
- The hero is a video thumbnail with no discount, no CTA and no product.
- The CTAs are all "In-Store Or Online ▶" links. None has a verb and a noun, and there's no real button.
- The email stacks five non-Christmas 40% tiles (Fall, Home Decor, Party, Floral, Paper Crafts) plus a 30% Yarn tile and the footer chips. That dilutes the Christmas message.
- The sale deadline ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 17, 2026") appears only at the very bottom.
- Fine print like "Select Categories," "See Weekly Ad For Details" and "Marked Price" makes the discounts feel conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "50% OFF Christmas" as a badge on the hero and the Christmas Essentials banner, so the offer in the subject appears on the first screen.
- 2. Cut the non-Christmas tiles or move them below a divider. One send should have one story.
- 3. Replace "In-Store Or Online ▶" with verb-plus-noun buttons, such as "Shop Christmas Trees" or "Shop 50% Off Wreaths."
- 4. Put the "Ends Saturday 10/17" deadline in a banner next to the hero.
- 5. Turn the video thumbnail into a product-led hero with a visible price or discount.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based, tracked-link template. The truncated source and QA run show no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). There are a few CSS and accessibility defects, and several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML was truncated and 33 of 45 links weren't probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links appear to route through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect/tracking domain. 12 material links were probed and 33 were skipped by design (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The results for the probed links weren't included, so click-through health is unconfirmed.
- Plain-text part is 12,773 of 14,919 chars (~86%) URLs. The text alternative is mostly long tracking URLs, which can contribute to spam scoring and makes the text version nearly unreadable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139807_473513_150214.gif` has no `alt`. If this is a tracking pixel, as the `/mo/` path suggests, the impact is low and `alt=""` would be correct. If it is content, it is an accessibility failure.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html>` carries `e-locale="en-US"` but no `lang="en"`, so screen readers get no language declaration.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values, which are dropped by clients.
  - `.lhp2` contains the typo `verticl-align:middle`, so that declaration never applies.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the only visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is below the width of nearly all phones (320px or more), so the responsive rules likely never fire. The source is truncated, so a later query may exist.
- The DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the `<html>` tag carries XHTML/VML namespaces. This is a common ESP pattern and is low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a proven defect. If the headers are really absent, that falls short of Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. Verify against the raw message at the origin or with a seed-list test.
- QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was provided. The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address weren't visible in the truncated source and can't be confirmed here.
- The sender is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`. That is acceptable if the opt-out mechanism works, but it gives replies nowhere to go.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Destination URLs are hidden behind `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects and weren't resolved in the QA data. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "50% Off Christmas" offer couldn't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw origin message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass results. Don't treat the relay warnings as defects until then.
2. Add `alt=""` (decorative) or descriptive alt to `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139807_473513_150214.gif`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Fix the invalid CSS: fill in or remove `.ctacr1` `color:` and `border-radius:px`, and correct `verticl-align`.
5. Confirm a mobile breakpoint above 300px (for example 600px) exists in the full source.
6. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by using fewer or shorter tracked URLs.
7. Resolve the redirect chains to verify landing pages and UTM tagging, and re-run probing on the 33 skipped links, including the unsubscribe link.
