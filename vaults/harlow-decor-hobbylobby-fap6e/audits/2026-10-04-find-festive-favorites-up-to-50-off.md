# Find Festive Favorites! Up To 50% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the Hobby Lobby category-tile wall again, with a Christmas Essentials banner on top. The subject promises "Festive Favorites" and "Up To 50% Off", and the Christmas blocks (Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral and Crafts at 50% off, plus Christmas Light Sets at 25%) do deliver on it. But they sit in the middle of a roughly 30-block scroll. Textiles, books, jewelry making, sewing notes, frames and furniture all get the same visual weight. The festive hook is lost after the first screen.
- It is more honest than the 9/27 "Home Decor Refresh" send, and the Christmas and fall sections are sequenced sensibly. It still has the problem I flagged on 9/25 and 10/1: no single hero, no single CTA, and too many "Always 40%" tiles that aren't news. Compared with the 9/29 "Festive Floral Must-Haves – 50% Off Christmas" send, which led with one big 50% hero, this feels like a step back. My read is a mid-pack 5. It is competent and seasonal, but it is a catalog, not an email.
- Note: the render I viewed was heavily downscaled (680x9800). I'm judging layout and large type, not small print.

## What's working

- The "Christmas Essentials" banner and the free-shipping bar ($59 or more) give the top of the email a clear seasonal and value frame.
- The big "50%" numerals on the Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral and Crafts tiles are readable at a glance, and they match the subject's "Up To 50% Off".
- The product photography is clean and consistent: trees, wreaths and the stocking and snowman tile all look festive.
- A visible sale-end date ("Sale Ends Through Saturday, October 10") in the footer area gives the offer a credible deadline.
- I saw no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no single hero and no obvious primary CTA button. The email is an endless stack of tiles with different layouts.
- The Christmas content, which is the actual subject, is buried below Home Decor, Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps and Fall Decor.
- Many tiles repeat "Always 40% of the Marked Price" and carry "Does not include…" fine print, so the offer reads as evergreen and slightly exclusionary rather than urgent.
- The email is extremely long, so the Furniture and Jewelry Making tiles at the bottom will almost never be seen. Mobile readers will fatigue early.
- The deadline is only in small footer text. It isn't used as urgency in the hero or the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with one Christmas hero and cut the tile count to 6–8.** Move the 50% Christmas blocks to the top, and put fall, the evergreen "Always 40%" categories and hobby categories in a separate send or a compact link row.
- 2. **Add one real CTA button**, such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas", under the hero.
- 3. **Put the deadline in the hero** ("Ends Saturday, 10/10") instead of leaving it in the footer.
- 4. **Replace the repeated "Always 40%" badges with a few featured products and prices** (the 99¢ and 4.99 items are the right instinct).
- 5. **Add reviews or a "top seller" cue** to the featured products for social proof.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on orders $59+. Sale ends Saturday, October 10.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_137941_4841373_148453.gif` has no `alt` (confirmed by QA). The `/mo/` path and `.gif` extension suggest an open-tracking pixel, but the dimensions aren't visible. If it is a pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
