# Shop Stylish & Chic Rugs!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Shop Stylish & Chic Rugs!" and the email delivers... a 20-tile mega-circular where rugs show up once, sharing a single small tile with "Pillows, Rugs, Throws." This is the exact same subject-body mismatch problem flagged in the 2026-08-02 "Score Big 🏈 40% Off Football Party" send (4.00/10) — a specific, narrow subject line slapped onto Hobby Lobby's generic sprawling template. The hero image and lead offer are "Always 40% Off The Marked Price — Home Decor," not rugs.
- Below that hero, the email just keeps going: doormats, sheet sets, bath towels, curtains, quilt sets, shower curtains, bath mats, framed art, canvas art, tapestries, hardware, wall clocks, then a second wave of unrelated category promos (Spring Shop 75% off, Fall 40% off, Football Party 40% off, Classroom Decor 40% off, Floral 40% off, Dorm Essentials 40% off), then an online clearance grid. This is the same sprawl pattern that scored 5.00-6.00/10 in the 7/26 and 7/30 mega-circulars — rugs get zero differentiated treatment inside it.
- Verdict: clean render, recognizable brand, real percentage offers — but a subject-line bait that the body never pays off, buried inside Hobby Lobby's now-familiar kitchen-sink template. Middling score, and a repeat of a documented pattern.

## What's working

- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, grid alignment is consistent throughout.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) is placed prominently at the very top, a real and easy-to-understand offer.
- The 40% Off Home Decor hero uses a warm, styled lifestyle shot (rugs, pillows, an armchair) that's on-brand and inviting even if it's not rug-specific.
- Sale end date ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, August 8, 2026") is stated clearly at the bottom, giving the whole circular real urgency the subject line doesn't use.
- Consistent Hobby Lobby visual identity (logo, typography, color palette) throughout — nothing feels off-brand.

## What's weak

- Rugs — the entire subject line's premise — appear in exactly one shared tile ("Pillows, Rugs, Throws") with no dedicated hero, no rug-specific pricing, and no rug photography leading the email.
- No single primary CTA — every one of the ~20 tiles says the same generic "In-Store Or Online," so there's no clear "click here for rugs" path even for an interested reader.
- Sprawl overwhelms hierarchy: by the fourth screen of near-identical two-column tiles, the eye has nothing new to land on, and the second-wave promos (Football Party, Classroom Decor, Dorm) further dilute the "stylish & chic rugs" promise.
- Every offer is gated behind "See Weekly Ad For Details" — the customer can't tell what the rug discount actually is without leaving the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around an actual rug lifestyle shot with rug-specific pricing (e.g., "40% Off Area Rugs") instead of the generic Home Decor banner — right now the subject and hero are unrelated, which is the single biggest drag on this send's credibility and CTR.
- 2. Cut the tile count. A "Rugs" send should lead with 3-4 rug-adjacent tiles (Area Rugs, Bath Mats, Doormats, Runners) and drop the football/classroom/dorm/spring-shop cross-sell entirely — those belong in their own campaigns, as Hobby Lobby has already proven it can do with the cleaner 7/27 "Fall Commercial" send (7.00/10).
- 3. Give at least the rug tile a specific, non-generic CTA like "Shop Area Rugs" instead of the repeated "In-Store Or Online" — specificity drives clicks more than category volume does.
- 4. Move the "Sale Valid Through August 8" deadline up near the hero and consider echoing it in the subject/preheader — it's real urgency that's currently wasted at the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New Rugs, Runners & Doormats: 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Refresh any room before fall — area rugs, runners & more, 40% off through 8/8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy up your floors: rugs, mats & doormats now 40% off in-store and online.`
