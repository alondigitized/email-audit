# Shop Up To 66% Off Seasonal Savings Inside!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's standard mega-circular — the same stacked two-column tile grid that has now run on July 2, July 7, July 9, and again today. The format is instantly recognizable: category header, percentage badge, two to three product images, small text beneath. Nothing here suggests the brand made a creative decision for this send. The subject line ("Shop Up To 66% Off Seasonal Savings Inside!") is a minor numerical variation on the July 9 "Up To 75% Off" — the discount ceiling went down, which is the wrong direction to signal escalating value.
- The breadth of categories (Home Decor, Textiles, Furniture, Fall Decor, Classroom, Dorm Essentials, Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Books, Art Sale, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing, Frames, Furniture again) is genuinely impressive inventory, but that breadth also dilutes the message to zero. There's no editorial anchor, no season story, no single product moment. It reads as a database dump with badges on it.
- This is a format Hobby Lobby clearly leans on as a high-cadence workhorse, and for high-intent subscribers who already have a category in mind it probably converts fine. For everyone else, it's noise — and after four near-identical sends in ten days, even category-intent subscribers are being trained to ignore it.

## What's working

- The discount badges are visually consistent and easy to scan — 40%, 50%, 66%, 75% — so a shopper already looking for a category can find their number quickly.
- "Fall Decor, Party & Crafts at 40% Off" in mid-July is reasonably timely — fall seasonal buying starts in early-to-mid July for Hobby Lobby's core crafting audience.
- The email renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, no visible layout breakage across the full scroll.
- Category variety is genuinely broad; if you have any craft or home project in mind, there's almost certainly a section for it.

## What's weak

- The subject line ("Shop Up To 66% Off Seasonal Savings Inside!") is weaker than the July 9 "Up To 75% Off" send — a lower ceiling AND it includes a vague "Seasonal Savings" filler phrase. This is a step backward.
- The ALL CAPS exclamation ("Savings Inside!") hits a mild spam-signal note that the rubric penalizes.
- No hero moment — the grid starts immediately at the top with "Home Decor 40% Off," which is the least-differentiated possible opener when they ran the same section on July 9.
- Zero personalization — no name, no category preference, no segment signal anywhere in the visible render.
- Fourth near-identical format in ten days means cadence fatigue is a real problem; this subscriber has no reason to believe tomorrow's email won't say "Up To 70% Off Seasonal Savings Inside!"

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the sharpest single category** — Fall Decor at 40% in July is actually a credible season-forward story. Make that the hero above the grid fold, not buried five rows down. The July 10 "Style Your Home" send (8/10) proved a single editorial moment beats the category dump.
- 2. **Retire the exclamation mark from the subject** — "Shop Up To 66% Off Seasonal Savings Inside!" reads as shouted discount email. Drop the "!" and the filler phrase.
- 3. **Vary the format** — four mega-circulars in ten days is cadence fatigue waiting to churn subscribers. Alternate with one category-deep send (like the July 3 "Small Space, Big Style" at 6/10 or July 5 back-to-school at 5/10).
- 4. **Add a credible deadline** — nothing in the render indicates when this sale ends, removing all urgency.
- 5. **Preheader should complement, not repeat** — if the preheader is currently defaulting to the subject or navigation junk, replace it with the single sharpest category deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall Decor is here — 40% off now through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 66% Off Checklist: Fall, Fabric, Frames & More`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, wearable art, and classroom finds are all marked down this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early fall decor + fabric sale — stock up before it's gone.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Bug 1 — CSS typo, `.lhp2` rule:**
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font {line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle;}
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid CSS property. The vertical-align declaration is silently dropped in all clients. Affects CTA button cell alignment.

**Bug 2 — Empty `color` value, `.ctacr1` rule:**
```css
.ctacr1 {color:; font-weight:normal; ...}
```
An empty `color:` value is invalid CSS. Some parsers will invalidate the entire rule block, stripping the `background-color:#013088` and border-radius from CTA buttons.

**Bug 3 — Unitless `border-radius` values, `.ctacr1` rule:**
```css
-moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;
```
Missing numeric value before `px`. All three declarations are no-ops; CTA buttons will render with square corners in Firefox and WebKit regardless of intended design.

**Accessibility — Missing alt text on tracked GIF:**
```
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_128247_1802026_138651.gif
```
No `alt` attribute present. This appears to be a tracking/open pixel or decorative image. If decorative, `alt=""` must be explicitly set for screen readers and image-blocked clients. If it is a content image, descriptive alt text is required.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
The QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Yahoo Mail (per their 2024 bulk sender requirements) require this header for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. If the header is present upstream but stripped by the AgentMail relay, it must be confirmed in raw SMTP headers directly.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` mechanism) is a hard requirement for Gmail/Yahoo bulk senders as of February 2024. Its absence means Gmail will use its own unsubscribe UI, which may increase complaint rates and suppress delivery to the Promotions tab.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Sending domain is `create.hobbylobby.com`; the from-address domain must have a valid DKIM signature from that domain or a parent alignment. Cannot confirm without raw header access.

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism are expected in the footer — not visible in the truncated source but standard for Hobbylobby sends. Cannot confirm from the provided excerpt.
