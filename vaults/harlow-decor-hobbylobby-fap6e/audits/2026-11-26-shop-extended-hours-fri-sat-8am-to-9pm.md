# Shop Extended Hours! Fri & Sat – 8AM to 9PM
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing: extended store hours on Friday and Saturday. The email delivers something else, a very long circular made of about 20 category blocks. In the render I can't find a hero or banner that mentions the hours. The only hour-related line I can make out is the small "Sale Ends Saturday, November 28" in the footer. Anyone opening for the hours finds a generic weekly flyer. The only link to the subject is a "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" bar and a "Christmas Essentials" banner at the top.
- The offers themselves are strong and seasonal: Christmas 50% off, home decor 40%, textiles 40%, "All Items labeled Fall 90% off, in-store only", and the Black Friday weekend context. But there are too many of them. Nothing stands out, and the eye bounces between 50%, 40%, 30%, 90%, 99¢ and 3.42. At roughly 9,500px tall, it reads as a catalog dump and not a send built around a weekend event.
- Judgement: a standard Hobby Lobby circular with a mismatched subject. It will do fine with people who already shop there. It does nothing to use the urgency of the extended-hours hook.

## What's working

- The "Christmas 50%" block at the top is large and bold, so the main seasonal offer is clear.
- The offers are tied to the time of year: Christmas, Fall clearance, New Year's items and a Football Party theme.
- Category blocks are consistent. Each has a big percentage, a product photo and a short list of what's included.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More" bar is visible at the very top.
- Sale-end date and app/store links sit together in the footer.

## What's weak

- The subject's hook (extended hours, 8AM–9PM) is not shown anywhere prominent in the render. The email doesn't match its subject.
- About 20 stacked offer blocks with no priority. The 90% in-store Fall item is buried in the middle of the scroll.
- The email is extremely long. Most mobile readers will never reach the lower blocks (sewing notions, custom frames, furniture).
- No obvious primary CTA button. Blocks are tappable images and text lists, and I can't see a clear "Shop now" button.
- Fine print is small and sits under many of the blocks. The "Always 40% off the marked price" wording and many exclusions weaken how honest the offers feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an extended-hours banner at the very top: "Open 8AM–9PM Fri & Sat," with a "Find your store" button. This matches the subject and gives people a reason to go in.
- 2. Cut the body to 4–5 blocks. Lead with Christmas 50% and the 90%-off Fall in-store clearance, then add one or two more.
- 3. Add one clear CTA button per block, for example "Shop Christmas 50% off", so people aren't left to guess what's tappable.
- 4. Show the sale-end date and the hours near the top, not only in the footer.
- 5. Gather the exclusions into one short line instead of repeating them under each block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Open 8AM–9PM Fri & Sat: Christmas 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Extended Hours + 90% Off Fall, In-Store`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Christmas, home decor and crafts on sale through Saturday, November 28.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stop by early or late this weekend. Fall clearance is up to 90% off in-store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup is a legacy table/VML template with several CSS defects. The mobile breakpoint is set at 300px, so responsive rules probably never apply on real phones. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because AgentMail doesn't capture those headers. The source is truncated, so findings below are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 29 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). No broken links were reported among the 12, but the other 29 are unverified.
- **Tracking redirect domain:** assets and links go through `link.hobbylobby.com`, a separate subdomain from the `create.hobbylobby.com` sender. This is a common ESP setup, but the click-tracking redirects weren't traced, so I can't confirm the final destinations.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,789 of 13,343 characters (88%) are URLs, so the text/plain alternative is mostly long tracking links. That is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA):** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144514_353159_156873.gif`. The `/mo/` path and filename pattern suggest an open-tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source. If it is a pixel, add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. If it is content, it needs real alt text.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)` holds the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, `.h1`, and so on). Almost no current phone viewport is 300px or narrower, so these rules probably never fire. Stacking and scaling on mobile would then depend on the fluid table widths alone.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has an empty color and unit-less radius values. The rule is dropped or ignored, so the button text color is undefined.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so the property is ignored.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the document uses an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype but declares `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` on `<html>`. This is harmless in most clients, but it is inconsistent.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`:** the tag has `e-locale="en-US"`, which is a non-standard attribute that assistive tech ignores. Add `lang="en"`.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Hobby Lobby` doesn't match the subject line ("Shop Extended Hours! Fri & Sat – 8AM to 9PM").
- **Font stacks:** `.ff {font-family: Arial;}` and `.ff2 {font-family: Arial Narrow;}` have no generic fallback. Arial Narrow is also missing on many mobile systems.
