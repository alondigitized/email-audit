# 🎁 Make Gift-Giving Easy With Hobby Lobby®
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Make Gift-Giving Easy," and the hero delivers on that. "Gift Cards For The Holidays" fills a huge block with wrapped boxes and a play button. It's a clear idea, but nothing behind it is actionable. There's no denomination, no bonus-card offer and no "Buy a gift card" button. The hero is a video thumbnail, so the offer is a mood, not a deal.
- Below the hero is the same Hobby Lobby tile wall as the 10/8 "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off" and 10/7 "Your One-Stop Christmas Shop" sends. It has a blue weekly-ad bar, a Christmas Essentials banner, then alternating 50/40/40/30/40/40 percent-off tiles and a footer of category links. Only the first block has anything to do with the subject. By the second scroll it's a generic circular again.
- This is the sixth or seventh near-identical send in about a week. It's competent and readable, with no render bugs, but it's fatigue-inducing. The gift-card hero is a nice change of pace. It's wasted because the CTA below it is "Click here to view our weekly ad," which has nothing to do with gift cards.

## What's working

- The "Gift Cards For The Holidays" hero is large and clear, and it matches the subject's gifting angle.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" green banner at the top is a concrete, visible offer.
- The percent-off numerals (50%, 40%, 30%) are big, so you can scan the offers fast.
- The alternating image/text tile layout is tidy, with no broken images or overlap.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 10, 2026" line gives a real deadline.

## What's weak

- The gift-card hero has no button, no amount and no link text. The only visible CTA below it is the weekly-ad bar.
- Nothing after the hero is about gifting. The tiles are Christmas, Fall, Home Decor, Fabric, Art and Party, which dilutes the subject.
- The sale deadline is buried near the footer. Nothing in the top half says "ends tomorrow."
- Every tile says "See Weekly Ad For Details" and "In-Store Or Online," which is repetitive and gives no product to click.
- The email repeats creative from 10/7 (Christmas Essentials, 50% off Christmas) and 10/8 (Always 40% Home Decor).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real CTA on the gift-card hero, such as "Shop Gift Cards," and link it to the gift-card page. Replace the weekly-ad bar directly below it.
- 2. Cut the tile wall to three or four gifting tiles, for example "Gifts under $20," "Gifts for crafters," "Stocking stuffers" and "Gift cards." Move the weekly-ad link to the footer.
- 3. Add one honest offer tied to the theme, such as a bonus card with purchase. If there isn't one, lead with the 50% off Christmas offer.
- 4. Put the deadline ("Ends Saturday") in the top banner next to free shipping.
- 5. Vary the cadence by sending a themed email only when it has different creative from the previous day's.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `50% off Christmas decor, 40% off fall. Sale ends Saturday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware template with a clean `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking domain. The automated checks flagged no hard failures, but compliance and authentication could not be verified. The source is truncated, so the footer, body links and `<img>` markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 27 were skipped. The probe results list no broken links, but the skipped links are untested.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,070 of 13,080 characters (about 85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This points to long tracking-wrapped URLs in the text alternative, which can raise spam-filter scores. It also makes the plain-text version hard to read.
- **Tracking domain.** Links and images use the branded `link.hobbylobby.com`, not a generic ESP domain. That is good for deliverability, and there is nothing to flag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139075_4791930_149629.gif` has no `alt`. The `/mo/` path and GIF format suggest an open-tracking pixel, which would make this low impact. The truncated source doesn't show its dimensions, so I can't confirm that.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html>` tag has `e-locale="en-US"` but no `lang="en"`. Screen readers get no language hint.
- **Mobile breakpoint is probably wrong.** The only visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Phones are 320px wide or more, so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, and so on) may never fire. The source is truncated, so a later query could exist.
- **Invalid CSS.**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values, so the declarations are dropped. That class's text color and border radius are lost, and the text may render in the default color on the `#013088` background.
  - `.lhp2` contains the typo `verticl-align:middle`, which is ignored.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with XHTML `xmlns` attributes. Browsers tolerate this and it is a minor point.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The check notes the AgentMail relay may strip it. Treat it as unverified, not as a confirmed sender failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. If the sender really omits it, one-click unsubscribe fails the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed. The `From` address (`noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`) is on a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on that subdomain.
- **Footer content not inspected.** An unsubscribe link was included in probing, but the postal address and unsubscribe markup are in the truncated part of the source.
- **`noreply@` sender.** This conflicts with deliverability best practice because replies go nowhere. It is not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters can't be confirmed.** Links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirector, and the visible source shows no UTM parameters. They may be appended at redirect time, which only a redirect-chain trace would show.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be assessed.** The probe results don't report final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture the raw message headers directly from the mailbox. This would settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` and rule out a relay artifact.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF if it is a pixel, or descriptive alt text if it is content. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Fix `.ctacr1` (empty `color:` and `px` values) and the `verticl-align` typo. Move the mobile breakpoint from 300px to about 480–600px.
4. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by dropping tracking parameters or using shorter redirect IDs.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and probe more of the 27 skipped links. Then check the footer address, the unsubscribe link, UTM propagation and the redirect chain.
