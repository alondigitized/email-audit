# Personalize Your Space With Hobby Lobby®
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's sixteenth-ish email in roughly as many days, and it follows the now-exhausted template: lifestyle hero → cascading department-discount grid. The subject line "Personalize Your Space With Hobby Lobby®" promises a customization angle, but the hero immediately pivots to "Dorm Room Essentials" — a completely different campaign idea. That subject/hero disconnect is the defining flaw of this send.
- The dorm room angle is actually the most seasonally specific hook Hobby Lobby has run in this entire audit window. June is real dorm-prep season, the lifestyle photo earns the premise, and the "Dorm Sweet Dorm" sign in frame tells a coherent story. But the email abandons that thread the instant you scroll past the fold and reverts to the same six-category discount dump that appeared in the June 4 "Select Home Decor" send and virtually every prior email this month.
- A committed dorm-room email could have been an 8 or 9. This one squanders the setup.

## What's working

- **"Dorm Room Essentials" hero** is the most product-specific, occasion-anchored hero Hobby Lobby has run in the recent audit window — it names a real use case and a real season.
- **Free Shipping banner** ("Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!") is visible and prominent at top of email.
- **Multiple concrete % off calls** (40%, 50%, 50%, 40%, 30%, 40%) give the grid real price signals at a glance — no vague "savings" language.
- **Spring Essentials strip** with category icons (kitchen, bath, home) provides navigational structure without clutter.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, legible throughout.

## What's weak

- **Subject/hero mismatch**: "Personalize Your Space" sets up a customization story; the hero delivers "Dorm Room Essentials." These are two different campaign ideas and neither earns the other.
- **Dorm Room thread dropped immediately**: after the hero image, not a single dorm-specific product appears — the grid is the exact same Home Decor / Spring Shop / Toys / Hobbies / Fabric / Jewelry Making dump from every recent send.
- **CTA kills intent**: "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" diverts a warm dorm-room shopper to a general circular rather than a curated collection.
- **No urgency or deadline**: nothing in the email creates a reason to act today vs. next week, and "move-in season" is the easiest credible deadline Hobby Lobby isn't using.
- **Cadence fatigue**: this is the sixteenth-ish Hobby Lobby email in sixteen days. Subscribers who saw the June 4 "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off" send have seen this discount structure before; the subject needs to do more to justify an open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to dorm room end-to-end.** Replace the generic category grid with a curated dorm module: wall art, storage bins, desk accessories, string lights, bedding accents. The hero earns a click when the body delivers what it promised.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA to match the hero.** "Shop Dorm Room Essentials →" sends a motivated shopper to the right destination instead of a catch-all weekly ad.
- 3. **Add a credible deadline.** "August move-in season is 8 weeks out" or "Ships free in time for fall semester" is both honest and urgent — something this email entirely lacks.
- 4. **Fix the subject line to name what's actually inside.** The subject should say "dorm room" to create a coherent inbox-to-body story.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $59+ — wall art, storage & everything move-in`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Hobby Lobby "Personalize Your Space" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders on a standard ESP table-based layout (HTML 4.01 Transitional with MSO conditionals) and is largely functional, but carries three CSS bugs affecting CTA rendering, two missing compliance headers, and a plain-text body so URL-dense it is likely triggering spam filters.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text URL bloat — confirmed spam signal**
QA reports 10,738 of 12,789 plain-text characters (84%) are raw URLs. This far exceeds the ~30% threshold most spam filters use. The plain-text part appears to be auto-generated by the ESP by concatenating all tracking URLs without human-readable anchor text between them. Any major ISP applying Bayesian filtering to the plain-text part will score this poorly.

**Click-tracking domain**
All links route through `link.hobbylobby.com`. No evidence of broken redirects in the truncated source, but SPF/DKIM authentication status is unknown (see §5), which means the tracking subdomain's reputation is unverifiable from this relay capture.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS bug — misspelled property in `.lhp2`**
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font {line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle;}
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid property. Vertical alignment is silently dropped for all elements using this class.

**CSS bug — empty `color` value and invalid `border-radius` in `.ctacr1`**
```css
.ctacr1 {
  color:;                          /* empty — inherits unpredictably */
  -moz-border-radius:px;           /* missing numeric value */
  -webkit-border-radius:px;
  border-radius:px;
}
```
The CTA button styled with `.ctacr1` (background `#013088`) will inherit text color from the parent rather than enforcing white/contrast text, and the border-radius declarations are invalid — the button will render with square corners in all clients.

**Missing `width=device-width` in viewport meta**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">
```
Without `width=device-width`, some mobile clients default to desktop viewport width before scaling. Standard value: `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.

**Tracking pixel missing alt text**
```
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_125439_418354_135734.gif
```
QA confirmed no `alt` attribute. Should be `alt=""` (empty string) so screen readers skip it silently rather than announcing the filename or URL.

**Custom web font in stack**
```css
.ff3ng {font-family: Museo Sans, Trebuchet MS, Verdana, Tahoma, sans-serif;}
```
Museo Sans is a licensed desktop/web font that will not load in email clients. The fallback chain (`Trebuchet MS → Verdana → Tahoma`) is functional, but any text set in `.ff3ng` will silently fall back — worth confirming intended fallback rendering matches design intent.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated source (`%%`, `{{`, `*|` patterns absent). No issues found from the available HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
QA confirms the header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Google and Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since February 2024) and is strongly recommended for CAN-SPAM best practice. If the ESP injects it at send time and the relay strips it, this needs to be verified against raw SMTP headers, not the relay-captured version.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Google/Yahoo for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. A high-volume sender like Hobby Lobby should have both:
```
List-Unsubscribe: <https://...>
List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click
```

**Authentication-Results header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed. The sending domain `create.hobbylobby.com` and link domain `link.hobbylobby.com` should both be covered by DKIM alignment. If DMARC is configured on `hobbylobby.com`, misalignment on the `create.` subdomain could cause failures at strict-mode receivers. Needs verification against full raw headers.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
Not verifiable from the truncated source — should appear in the email footer. No flag raised, but confirm it is present in the full HTML.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The truncated HTML does not include enough resolved link destinations to audit UTM parameters directly. All click URLs pass through `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking redirects, which is expected. The following should be verified against the full source:

- UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) should be appended to all destination URLs after the redirect resolves, not just on the tracking URL itself.
- The subject line references "Personalize Your Space" — confirm campaign landing pages carry matching `utm_campaign` values and that any promoted category pages are live and not returning 404s.
