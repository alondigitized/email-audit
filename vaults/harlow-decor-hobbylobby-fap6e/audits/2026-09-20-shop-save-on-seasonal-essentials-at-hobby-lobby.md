# Shop & Save On Seasonal Essentials At Hobby Lobby®️
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby is back with the exact same wall-to-wall category-tile grid it's been running since late August — "Fall Essentials" free-shipping banner up top, then roughly 25 stacked blocks (Home Decor, Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps, Fall Decor, Christmas Decor, Christmas Trees, Christmas Floral, Christmas Crafts, Christmas Party & Tableware, Christmas Light Sets, Football Party, Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Best-Selling Books, Frames, Art Sale, Paper Crafts, All Fabric, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notions, Custom Frames, Furniture) each with its own "Always 40%" or "50% off marked price" badge. The subject promises "Seasonal Essentials" but there's no seasonal hero — it's the identical undifferentiated everything-store dump this brand has sent for a month.
- This is a subscriber-fatigue email, not a marketing send. There's no single hero image, no lead offer, no reason to scroll past the first three blocks. Compare to 2026-09-15's "50% Off Christmas" send, which at least had a styled hero band — this one has nothing analogous; it's pure list.
- Verdict: low-effort repeat of the exact template flagged repeatedly in prior audits. Score accordingly.

## What's working

- Free shipping banner ($59+) is visible immediately at the top, a real and usable offer.
- Percentage discounts (40%/50%/25%/30%) are large, bold, and easy to scan per category.
- Product photography within each tile (ornaments, trees, fabric, frames) is clean and in-focus, no broken images.
- Category labels are legible and organized in a consistent repeating layout.

## What's weak

- No hero image or lead offer — the eye has nowhere to land; every block looks equally (un)important.
- Subject says "Seasonal Essentials" but the body is the same generic 25-category catalog dump used in the last five sends, not a curated seasonal edit.
- Overwhelming length — a reader has to scroll through 20+ near-identical badge blocks before reaching Furniture at the bottom.
- No single unambiguous primary CTA; each block presumably links somewhere different, diluting any one action.
- No urgency, no dates, no "ends soon" — every discount reads as "Always," which trains subscribers to ignore timing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE seasonal hero (fall or Christmas, since both are mixed in here) and lead with a real styled photo + single CTA, matching what worked in the 09-15 "50% Off Christmas" send — then move the full category grid below the fold as secondary content.
- 2. Cut the tile count drastically — 25 stacked blocks is fatigue, not merchandising; group into 4-5 curated collections max.
- 3. Replace "Always 40%" language with time-bound urgency (e.g., "This week only") to create real reasons to open now versus any other week.
- 4. Differentiate subject from body — if subject says "Seasonal Essentials," the hero must actually show a seasonal essential, not a generic price list.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall Essentials: 40-50% Off Décor & Christmas Crafts`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Fall Refresh Starts Here — Up to 50% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $59+, plus new markdowns on trees, florals & frames.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `This week: fall décor, Christmas prep, and craft supplies all discounted.`
- 5. Add social proof or a "bestseller" flag to at least one tile to break the monotony of repeated badge-only blocks.
