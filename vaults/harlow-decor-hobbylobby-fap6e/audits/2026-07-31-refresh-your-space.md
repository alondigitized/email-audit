# Refresh Your Space
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- "Refresh Your Space" opens like Hobby Lobby's best recent work — the styled bedroom hero with a play button, soft pillow textures, and a clean "Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials" nav strip echoes the lookbook approach from the 2026-07-27 "Discover All Your Fall Essentials Here!" send (8.00/10). For about a third of the email, this reads like a coherent home-textiles circular: doormats, sheet sets, curtains, wall clocks, all tightly on-theme with the subject line.
- Then it falls apart. Past the "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" banner, the email pivots into the same mega-circular percentage grid Hobby Lobby has run all week — 40% Off Fall, 66% Off Spring Shop, 66% Off Summer Toys, 40% Off Classroom Decor, 30% Off Fabric, 40% Off Art Supplies. None of that is "refreshing your space." This is the same identity-crisis pattern flagged in the 2026-07-29 "40% Off Classroom Decor" send (7.00/10), except here it's worse: that email at least kept its promised category up top before drifting; this one drifts into toys, classroom supplies, and fabric — categories with zero visual or thematic connection to a bedroom refresh.
- The hero itself never states a price or percentage — the actual offers only show up six screens down, disconnected from the subject's promise. Net: strong opening creative undercut by the now-familiar sprawl. This lands below the clean lookbook sends and roughly in line with the mid-pack mega-circulars.

## What's working

- The hero image (styled bed, layered pillows, warm natural light) is genuinely appealing and on-brand for "Refresh Your Space."
- The video play button on the hero adds production value not seen in the flatter mega-circular sends (e.g., 07-26, 07-28).
- The top third — Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials nav, then doormats/sheet sets/curtains/wall clocks — stays tightly on-theme with the subject line.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) sits high and is easy to spot.
- No render bugs: images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The subject's promise ("Refresh Your Space") is abandoned after the textiles grid — Summer Toys, Classroom Decor, and Fabric have nothing to do with home refresh.
- No price or percentage appears anywhere near the hero; the actual discounts are buried five-plus screens down.
- Every discount tile repeats "Selection Will Vary By Store / See Weekly Ad For Details" — that's fine print stacked on every single offer, which reads as a hedge, not a deal.
- No single unambiguous primary CTA — it's dozens of "In-Store Or Online" links competing for attention instead of one clear action.
- This is the fourth+ Hobby Lobby send this week reusing the same Fall Essentials banner art and mega-circular tile format (07-26, 07-27, 07-28) — the cadence is fatiguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email at the textiles grid.** Everything from "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" down (Fall, Spring Shop, Summer Toys, Classroom Decor, Fabric, Art Supplies) belongs in a separate circular send, not bolted onto a "Refresh Your Space" lookbook. This single change would do the most to lift the score — it turns a split-identity email into a focused one.
- 2. Put a real number in or near the hero — "Refresh Your Space — Up to 40% Off Home Decor" — instead of making the reader scroll six screens to find a price.
- 3. Give the hero one clear CTA button ("Shop The Look") instead of relying on a video play icon as the only interactive element up top.
- 4. Drop the repeated "Selection Will Vary By Store" boilerplate from every tile, or consolidate it once at the bottom — repeating it under six offers erodes trust in the deal.
- 5. Space out the Fall Essentials banner reuse — it's now appeared in at least three sends this week (07-27, 07-28, and this one); rotate creative to avoid subscriber fatigue.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sheet sets, curtains, wall art and more — all in-store or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your bedroom refresh, from bedding to wall decor, is on sale now.`
