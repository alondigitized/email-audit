# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the same wall-to-wall category-tile grid Hobby Lobby has been running since late August, and it's the exact critique from the 2026-09-03 "Select Home Decor" send with the near-identical name — nothing has evolved between them. The subject promises "Select Home Decor," but Home Decor is one of roughly 25 tiles stacked in a single unbroken scroll (Textiles, Mirrors, Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Tableware, Light Sets, Football Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Books, Frames, Art Sale, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing, Custom Frames, Furniture) — there's no hero, no hierarchy, and no reason the recipient's eye lands on Home Decor specifically.
- The Christmas section is a genuine miss: 50% off decor/trees/floral/crafts/tableware and 25% off light sets are buried roughly a third of the way down a subject-mismatched email in September, which is exactly the kind of promo-in-wrong-context problem the 09-04 "Bring The Season To Life" send also had. Every category repeats "Always 40% OFF The Marked Price," so the discount signal is diluted into wallpaper rather than a single compelling offer.
- Judgement: this is a catalog dumped into an email, not a marketing email. It will read as noise to anyone who isn't already mid-project for a specific category — functional as a reference scroll for existing shoppers, useless as a persuasion tool for anyone else.

## What's working

- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" banner at the very top is a clear, unconditional, easy-to-parse offer.
- Product photography per category is clean and on-brand (fall wreath, tartan textiles, teddy-bear ornaments, black tee, canvas prints).
- Discount badges (40%, 50%, 25%, 30%, 99¢) are legible and consistently placed on each tile.
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- No hero section — the subject line's promise (Home Decor) gets zero visual priority over the other 24 categories.
- Christmas offers (50% off decor, trees, floral, crafts, tableware) are stranded mid-scroll in a September send, disconnected from any seasonal framing.
- Repeating "Always 40% OFF The Marked Price" language across a dozen tiles reads as boilerplate, not a real-time discount, and erodes urgency.
- No single, unambiguous primary CTA — with ~25 competing tiles there's no clear "start here" for the recipient.
- Same structural template as the 09-03 and 09-06 sends — subscribers who opened either of those get a third near-identical wall of tiles this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Build an actual hero: pick one category (Home Decor, since that's what the subject promises) with a single lifestyle photo, one headline offer, and one CTA button above the fold — then let the category grid follow below as a secondary "shop more" section.
- 2. Pull Christmas out of this send entirely, or give it its own dedicated email — 50% off is a strong number that's being wasted mid-scroll in a same-old category dump.
- 3. Cut the tile count. Twenty-five categories in one scroll guarantees nobody scrolls to the bottom (Furniture, Custom Frames, Sewing) — trim to the top 6-8 performing categories and rotate the rest across future sends.
- 4. Vary the discount language tile-to-tile (e.g., "This Week Only," "Today," specific end dates) instead of repeating "Always 40% OFF" — static language kills urgency.
- 5. Give this send its own identity so it doesn't just merge with the 09-03/09-06 grid emails in subscribers' memory.
