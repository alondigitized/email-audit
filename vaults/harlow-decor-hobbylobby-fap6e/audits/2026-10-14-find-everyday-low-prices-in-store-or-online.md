# Find Everyday Low Prices In-Store Or Online!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's "Your Price" everyday-value pitch, and the idea is clear. The headline "Our Everyday Best Price On Thousands Of Items Storewide" is big. The "Look For The 'YOUR PRICE' Sticker" callout, with an arrow pointing at a canvas, teaches the shopper what to look for in store. Below it is a tidy two-column grid of price-forward tiles: $1.99 chargers, $4.99 foam bricks, $3.99 Mod Podge and so on.
- The email has no deal, though. There is no percent off and no urgency beyond a fine-print "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 17." It also sits right after the 10/13 "40% Off" florals send and the 10/8 "Always 40% Off" send, so this "everyday low price" message may feel like a retreat to a subscriber who has been getting promos. Nothing in the render ties to fall or any seasonal moment. The Christmas Essentials banner at the top is the only seasonal hook.
- The content is mostly craft supplies: glue, foam, wreaths, candy wafers, tape. That is a niche mix with no hero product or occasion. It works as a utility reference. It is weak as a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The headline is large and legible, and the "Your Price" sticker callout with the arrow explains the program quickly.
- Price-first tiles ($1.99, $4.99, $3.99) make the value easy to scan, and the product names and sizes are visible.
- The "In-Store Or Online" and "In-Store Only" labels set honest expectations about availability.
- The tiles render cleanly, with no broken images or overlaps.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar and the top nav (Weekly Ad, Stores, Shop, DIY) give a clear route into the site.

## What's weak

- No percent-off offer or promo hook. "Everyday low price" gives no reason to act now.
- The only CTA is a thin blue "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar under the hero. It is generic, and it sends people to the ad rather than a product.
- The Christmas Essentials banner and category row (Kitchen, Bath, Home) compete with the everyday-price message and dilute the focus.
- The email is a very long single scroll of 20 near-identical tiles with no grouping or hierarchy. Items like helium tanks, epoxy and AA batteries don't form a coherent story.
- The fine print about "Your Price" and the exclusion of other discounts is small, and it sits at the bottom of the email, away from the claims it qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one concrete hook. Name a specific price or comparison above the fold, such as "Chargers $1.99, Wreaths $4.99, Mod Podge $3.99." This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Replace the "CLICK HERE" bar with a verb-and-noun button, such as "Shop Your Price Items," placed right under the headline.
- 3. Group the tiles into three or four themed blocks, such as Fall Decor, Glue & Adhesives and Party, each with its own "Shop all" link.
- 4. Move the "Your Price" exclusions into a short line near the hero.
- 5. Cut the Christmas banner from this send, or make it the one clear secondary message.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an ESP-generated, table-based HTML template. All links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` click-tracking domain. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 4 warnings: missing unsubscribe headers and authentication results (probably relay artifacts), a URL-heavy plain-text part, and one image without alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, body and most link markup could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 of 45 links and skipped 33. No broken links were reported among the 12, but the other 33 are unverified. That includes footer, utility and social links.
- **Plain-text bloat (confirmed):** 13,278 of 15,897 plain-text characters (about 83%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% threshold. The cause is long tracking-wrapped URLs. This can raise spam-filter scores and makes the text part hard to read.
- **Tracking domain:** `link.hobbylobby.com` is a first-party branded domain. No issue was found with that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (confirmed):** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139806_750619_149360.gif` has no `alt` attribute. The `/mo/` path and GIF format suggest an open-tracking pixel, which would make this low severity. That is an inference, and QA doesn't say what the image is. If it is visible content, screen readers get no description. Even for a pixel, an empty `alt=""` is the correct markup.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block (confirmed in source):**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values for `color` and `border-radius`. Clients will drop these declarations, so any element using `.ctacr1` loses its radius and relies on inherited colour.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo (`verticl-align`), so the declaration is ignored.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns` attributes (XHTML/VML namespaces). Clients tolerate this, and it is an ESP-template pattern, so treat it as minor.
- **Responsive rules:** the only media query visible in the truncated source is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Typical phone widths are 320px and above, so these rules would not fire on them. Other breakpoints may exist in the truncated portion, so this is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in QA output. The truncated body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain is `create.hobbylobby.com`, so check alignment with the visible From.
- **Body unsubscribe link:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The footer, including the physical postal address, was not visible in the truncated source and is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links are wrapped by `link.hobbylobby.com`, so any UTM parameters appear only after redirect resolution. None are present in the visible source or QA output. QA reported no landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers. Treat their absence as a real defect only if the raw message also lacks them.
2. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original message to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align with `create.hobbylobby.com`.
3. Add `alt=""` (for a tracking pixel) or descriptive alt text to `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139806_750619_149360.gif`.
4. Fix the template CSS: fill in or remove `.ctacr1`'s empty `color` and `border-radius:px` values, and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
5. Shorten the plain-text part, for example by using fewer or shorter tracking URLs, to get the URL ratio under 70%.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and a higher probe limit. That would check the 33 skipped links, the footer address and unsubscribe markup, the media queries, and UTM handling after redirect.
