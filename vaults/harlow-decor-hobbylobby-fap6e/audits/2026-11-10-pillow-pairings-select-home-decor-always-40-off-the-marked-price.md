# Pillow Pairings – Select Home Decor Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is the strongest part of it. "Pillow Pairings – Select Home Decor Always 40% Off The Marked Price" matches a real hero. A big pillow-and-throw photo sits next to a large "Always 40% off the marked price, Home Decor" block, and the subject and hero agree. This is closer to the 11-05 send ("Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off") than to the 11-06 send, which oversold a Christmas offer.
- Below that hero the email loses focus. After the home decor block and its product grid, the render runs through Christmas 50%, Fall 50%, Paper Crafts 40%, Floral 40%, Yarn 30%, Hobbies 40% and a 75% clearance bar. The home decor offer is clear, but every other offer competes with it. "Always 40% off the marked price" is also a standing discount, so it carries no urgency. The only deadline is a small "Sale valid through Saturday, November 14" at the bottom.
- Overall it's a competent, on-brand circular with a well-matched subject. It's still a catalog, not a targeted send, and it won't surprise anyone who has seen this week's earlier Hobby Lobby emails.

## What's working

- The hero photo of layered pillows and a throw is attractive and clearly matches "Pillow Pairings."
- The giant "40% OFF" number is the first thing the eye lands on in the body.
- The home decor sub-grid (Doormats, Sheet Sets, Pillows/Rugs/Throws, Framed Art, Wall Clocks) gives concrete product categories.
- The free-shipping bar ($69+) at the top is clear and prominent.
- The brand voice and layout are consistent and instantly recognizable.

## What's weak

- The email is a very long scroll. Seven more offer blocks follow the hero, and they dilute it.
- "Always 40% off" is a permanent discount with no urgency, and the only deadline is tiny text at the bottom.
- Every CTA says "In-Store Or Online". That is a repeated label, not a verb plus a product.
- There are no prices, product names or reviews. The pillows are never named or priced.
- A "Click here to view our weekly ad" bar and the Christmas banner pull attention away from the home decor story.
- Offers overlap (Christmas 50%, Fall 50%, Home 40%), which makes it unclear which one matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the home decor story. Keep the hero and the product grid, and drop or collapse the Christmas, Fall, Craft and Yarn blocks into one line of links. A themed send for a themed subject would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Name and price a hero product, such as "Textured throw pillows from $X, now 40% off the marked price."
- 3. Replace "In-Store Or Online" with a specific CTA like "Shop Pillows & Throws."
- 4. Add a real deadline near the top. "Through Saturday, Nov. 14" is buried in the footer.
- 5. Add a 3-4 item "pairings" row (pillow + throw + rug) with prices, to match the subject's promise.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pillows, throws, rugs and wall art, all 40% off the marked price through Nov. 14.`
