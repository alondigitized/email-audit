# 🐻 40% Off Un-bear-ably Adorable Party Supplies!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject line "🐻 Un-bear-ably Adorable Party Supplies" promises cute bear-themed party goods, and the hero delivers exactly that — a teddy-bear-and-party-plate lifestyle shot with a bold "Always 40% OFF The Marked Price / Party" banner. That's a genuinely on-target hero for the subject, better aligned than most recent Hobby Lobby sends. But the moment you scroll past it, the email reverts hard to the brand's default: a long, undifferentiated grid of craft SKUs (vinyl, foam board, acrylic paint, canvas sets, Poly-Fil, bandanas) that have nothing to do with party supplies or the bear theme at all.
- This is a bait-and-switch structure — one good hero, then twenty rows of unrelated crafting inventory padding out the email. A subscriber who opens for party supplies gets one screen of relevance and then a scroll-fatigue dump.

## What's working

- Subject-to-hero match is strong: bear imagery and party plates/napkins directly reflect the "un-bear-ably adorable" bear pun.
- Hero offer is clear and prominent: "Always 40% OFF The Marked Price" with bold typography, immediately visible.
- Top utility nav (Weekly Ad, Stores, Shop) and free shipping banner ($59+) are clean and legible, no render bugs.
- Fall Essentials category banner is seasonally appropriate and visually distinct from the craft grid below it.
- Pricing throughout is specific and real (e.g., $4.99, 37¢, $43.99) — no placeholder or broken values.

## What's weak

- After the hero, none of the ~20 product tiles are party supplies — it's vinyl, foam board, canvas, paint, Poly-Fil, bandanas, T-shirts. The subject's promise is abandoned almost immediately.
- No party-specific products (plates, napkins, banners, balloons, favors) appear anywhere in the scrollable grid, despite that being the entire subject-line hook.
- The email is extremely long (20+ tiles) with a flat, repetitive two-column layout and no clear end to the offer — CTA fatigue sets in.
- "Click Here To View Our Weekly Ad" sits as a full-width blue bar with no context on what's in the ad, an unspecific mid-email CTA.
- The bear/party photography is beautiful but appears once and never reinforced — nothing ties the closing sections (bandanas, sweatshirts) back to the theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual party-supplies product row (plates, napkins, table covers, favors) directly under the hero — right now the email sets up an expectation it never pays off, which is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Cut the craft-supply grid down significantly or move it to a clearly separate "Also on Sale" section so it doesn't dilute the party theme the subject sold.
- 3. Replace the generic "Click Here To View Our Weekly Ad" bar with themed copy, e.g. "See All Party Deals In The Weekly Ad."
- 4. Shorten the email overall — 20+ SKU tiles is scroll fatigue; group by category with anchor links instead.
- 5. Reinforce the bear/party visual motif with a small icon or accent color repeated near relevant SKUs, so the theme doesn't disappear after the hero.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Bear-Themed Party Supplies, 40% Off Today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cute bear plates, napkins & party decor — always 40% off the marked price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plan the party: bear tableware, banners & favors, all discounted.`
