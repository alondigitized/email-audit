# Get Inspiration With Our Everyday Low Prices!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a rare Hobby Lobby send that's a single idea: "Our Everyday Best Price," a grid of crafty basics with real price tags. The subject matches the body. The email opens on a free-shipping bar ($69+) and a Christmas Essentials banner, then makes the case for everyday low prices. It's more honest and utilitarian than the 11/03 "50% Off Fall" send or the 10/30 holiday-ready send.
- The execution is a long, uniform two-column grid of products with big prices. It's easy to scan but has no hero offer, no percent-off, and no urgency until a "Sale Valid Through Saturday" line at the very bottom. The one real CTA ("CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD") is a thin blue bar below a mostly empty canvas hero. Nothing in the first screen pushes a click except the Christmas banner, which pulls away from the subject's theme.
- Verdict: a clear, believable price-anchor email that works for existing crafters, but it's a catalog without a hook. Fine for a 5.

## What's working

- Subject and body match: the "Everyday Best Price" headline delivers on the promise.
- Big, bold price numerals ($2.99, $4.99, 99¢) make scanning fast.
- The "Look For The 'YOUR PRICE' Sticker" callout with an arrow explains the pricing system.
- The product grid is consistent and has no visible broken images.
- The footer disclaimer sits at the bottom of the email, and the free-shipping threshold is up top.

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank canvas with a sticker callout, not an exciting offer. It doesn't land as the draw.
- The only CTA is a thin blue bar, and each product's "In-Store Or Online" link is tiny and repeated.
- The Christmas Essentials banner above the fold competes with the subject's theme.
- The email is very long, about 20 products, with no grouping or priority.
- Urgency ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 7") is buried at the bottom, and there's no discount framing to make the low prices feel like savings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer: a bundle of 3 best sellers, or a "price-match" claim, with a real button ("Shop Everyday Low Prices").
- 2. Cut the grid to 6-8 products grouped by project (Vinyl crafts, Canvas & Paint, Project Boards) with a "Shop all" button for each group.
- 3. Move the Christmas banner below the fold or drop it so the theme stays focused.
- 4. Put the Nov 7 deadline up top.
- 5. Make the product links buttons or tap targets.

## Technical Audit: Hobby Lobby, "Get Inspiration With Our Everyday Low Prices!"

**Sender:** noreply@create.hobbylobby.com

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware ESP template with no hard failures. The QA run reported 0 issues and 4 warnings. The main findings are unverifiable unsubscribe headers, a very URL-heavy plain-text part, one image without alt text, and CSS defects in the head. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 33 were skipped, so about 73% of links are unverified. The QA output reports no failures among the 12 probed.
- **Plain text:** 13,326 of 16,078 characters (about 83%) are URLs. That points to long click-tracking wrapper URLs, which can hurt spam scoring and make the plain-text part hard to read.
- **Tracking domain:** Tracked assets are served from `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/…`. This is a branded tracking domain, which is good practice. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether wrapped links use the same domain.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142338_677758_152804.gif` has no `alt`. The `/mo/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, but that isn't confirmed. If it is a pixel, add `alt=""` to be explicit. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Missing `lang`:** The `<html>` tag has `e-locale="en-US"` but no `lang` attribute. `e-locale` is a non-standard ESP attribute that screen readers ignore.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"`. This is harmless in most clients, but the two don't match.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values. The `color` and `border-radius` declarations are dropped, which looks like an unfilled template variable.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo, so the property is ignored.
- **Duplicate `!important` heights:** `.rh20 {height:20px !important; height:5.56vw !important;}` is a deliberate fallback pattern. It may be unreliable in some clients.
- **Viewport:** `user-scalable=yes` does not block zoom, so there is no issue here.
- **Title:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` is generic. Using the subject line would be marginally better.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. I saw no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The `.ctacr1` rule has empty values, but that is CSS, not body content. The truncated body wasn't fully checked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** The header was not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and postal address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but I can't confirm the result.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
I couldn't assess this. Neither the truncated source nor the QA output shows landing URLs, UTM parameters or redirect chains. The 12 probed links reported no failures, but their destinations weren't given.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Headers:** Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. If the AgentMail relay strips them, test on a direct-delivery inbox. If they are really absent, add them.
2. **Alt text:** Add `alt=""` if the `.gif` is a tracking pixel, or descriptive text if it is content.
3. **CSS:** Fix `.ctacr1` (empty `color` and `border-radius`) and the `verticl-align` typo.
4. **Language:** Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. **Plain text:** Shorten the plain-text version by dropping tracking wrappers or trimming the link list, so URLs are well under 70% of it.
6. **Re-run QA:** Run it with the full, untruncated HTML. Probe the 33 skipped links, or at least a sample, and record each link's final URL and UTM parameters.
