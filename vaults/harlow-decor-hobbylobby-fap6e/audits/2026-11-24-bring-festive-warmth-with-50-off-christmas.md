# Bring Festive Warmth With 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is honest. "Bring Festive Warmth With 50% Off Christmas" matches the biggest block in the render. A large "50% OFF Christmas" hero sits over a styled mantel, and the Christmas category grid sits right below it. The "Festive Warmth" phrasing is softer than the 11/22 send, "Hurry In & Save 🎄 50% Off Christmas!", but both lead with the same offer.
- The problem is that the 50% hero doesn't come first. A free-shipping banner, a "Christmas Essentials" banner and a row of Kitchen, Bath and Home tiles come before it. Below it are another 75%, four 40% blocks and an "Up to 75% Online Clearance" bar. That is seven or more competing percentages, so the 50% claim gets diluted. The "Kitchen / Bath / Home Essentials" row also has nothing to do with Christmas, which undercuts the subject.
- Overall it is a competent, on-brand circular but a forgettable send. After 11/17's "'Tis The Season To Save With 50% Off Christmas" and 11/22's near-identical subject, a subscriber has seen this exact offer three times in a week.

## What's working

- The 50% hero is the largest element in the email. Its lifestyle photo and big numerals make the offer unmissable once you reach it.
- The Christmas category grid (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) gives six clear shopping paths under the hero.
- The free-shipping banner at the top ("Orders $69 or More!") adds a second concrete, honest benefit.
- The alternating image and text blocks give the lower half a consistent rhythm, and I see no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer shows a hard deadline: "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28, 2026".

## What's weak

- The 50% hero doesn't appear until about a third of the way down, after the shipping banner, the Christmas banner and the Kitchen/Bath/Home row.
- Seven or more percentage claims (50%, 75%, 40% ×4, "Up to 75%", and several "Always 40%/50%/30%" items) make it unclear which deal matters.
- The CTAs are all "In-Store Or Online ▶". That is a location, not a verb and a noun, and it is repeated about 20 times.
- The offer language is qualified: "*See Weekly Ad For Details", "Select Categories", "Selection Will Vary By Store". A bare "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar sits in the middle of the email.
- The sale deadline is only in the footer. Nothing near the hero creates urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% Christmas hero first, directly under the logo, and move the Kitchen/Bath/Home row below it or cut it. Fold the shipping threshold into a thin strip.
- 2. Cut the lower half to two or three secondary offers. Drop the repeated 40% blocks, or merge them into one "More 40% Off" row.
- 3. Replace "In-Store Or Online" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Christmas Decor" or "Shop Christmas Trees".
- 4. Move "Ends Saturday 11/28" into the hero. The deadline is real, so use it.
- 5. Rotate the hero creative. The offer has been the same all week, so changing the product or the angle would make each send feel less like a repeat.
