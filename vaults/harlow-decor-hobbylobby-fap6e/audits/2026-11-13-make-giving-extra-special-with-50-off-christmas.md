# Make Giving Extra Special With 50% Off Christmas 🎁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject oversells it. "Make Giving Extra Special With 50% Off Christmas 🎁" promises a 50%-off Christmas offer. The email's biggest hero is a "Christmas Gift Supplies" video tile with no discount on it. The only 50% block is Fall, in a small tile about a third of the way down the scroll. Christmas gets category tiles (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights), all labeled "In-Store Or Online" with no percentage. A subscriber who opens for 50% off Christmas has to hunt for it, and it is not there in the render.
- The gift-giving angle is a real improvement over the generic "Seasonal Savings" send on 11-12. The Christmas Gift Supplies hero matches the subject's mood, and the free-shipping-at-$69 banner at the top is a concrete, honest hook. But the discount ladder underneath (50% Fall, 40% Home Decor, 40% Floral, 40% Paper Crafts, 30% Yarn, 40% Hobbies) is the same busy circular layout as 11-10 and 11-11. Nothing here says "gift" beyond the hero.
- Verdict: a decent, tidy layout with a subject that writes a check the body doesn't cash. It's a mid-pack send, about a 5.

## What's working

- The "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" green banner at the top is a clear, specific, non-fine-print offer.
- The Christmas Gift Supplies hero is big and visually rich, and it matches the gifting tone of the subject.
- The percentage-off numerals (50%, 40%, 30%) are large and scannable. The eye can run down the zigzag and read the whole discount ladder fast.
- The sale end date, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 14, 2026," gives a credible deadline.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "50% Off Christmas," but the only 50% tile in the render is Fall. Christmas tiles carry no discount at all.
- The Christmas hero is a video tile with a play button and no offer, no price and no CTA copy.
- The primary CTA, "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD," is generic and sits in the middle of the email below the Christmas tiles.
- Every tile says "In-Store Or Online" and "See Weekly Ad For Details," so there is no specific product or price to click on.
- The send is long, at about 4,300px, with 15 or more competing blocks and no single priority. It is the same circular format as the last several days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and the hero agree. If 50% Christmas is real, put a "50% OFF Christmas" badge on the hero and Christmas Essentials tiles. If it isn't, change the subject to what the email actually shows.
- 2. Replace the video-only hero with a static image carrying a headline, an offer line and a button, such as "Shop Christmas Gift Supplies."
- 3. Rewrite the central CTA from "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" to something like "Shop 50% Off Fall & Christmas Gifts," and move it higher.
- 4. Cut the lower blocks to the three best offers. The mini-banner row (Mirrors & Lamps, Frames, Furniture and so on) can go.
- 5. Give each category tile a real product and price point instead of repeating "See Weekly Ad For Details."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Wrap It Up: Gift Supplies & 40% Off Crafts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ribbon, bows & wrap for every gift, plus free shipping on $69+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall is 50% off through Saturday. Christmas gift supplies are here.`
