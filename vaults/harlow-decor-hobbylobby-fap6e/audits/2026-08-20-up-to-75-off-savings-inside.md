# Up To 75% Off Savings Inside!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- "Up To 75% Off Savings Inside!" is the vaguest possible subject for what is, once again, the exact same undifferentiated Hobby Lobby tile-grid this run has produced daily — Home Decor, Textiles, Christmas Decor, Christmas Trees, Football Party, Dorm Essentials, Wearable Art, Frames, Yarn, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notions, Custom Frames, Furniture, all stacked one after another with zero hierarchy. It's the least focused subject line of the whole recent run (compare 08-18's "Create Your Dream Retreat" or 08-14's "Add Warmth To Your Home") paired with the single largest tile count yet — Christmas trees and football party supplies sitting directly above dorm essentials and classroom decor, in August.
- The "75%" in the subject does technically appear once, on "Classroom Decor 75% Off," buried roughly two-thirds down the page — everything else tops out at 40-50%. That's a bait number for a discount most shoppers will never see. This is a catalog dump wearing a promo subject line, and it's the weakest send in this brand history to date.

## What's working

- Free shipping banner ($59+) is clean and visible at the very top.
- Section labels are legible and consistently formatted (product category, discount badge, "Shop Now" CTA repeated per tile).
- Product photography is crisp with no broken images or rendering bugs across the entire long scroll.
- Discount percentages are bolded and easy to spot per-tile even if the overall page isn't.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "75% Off" but that figure appears on exactly one tile (Classroom Decor) out of ~25 — classic bait-and-switch structure.
- No single hero/lead offer — the page opens straight into Home Decor 40% with no headline module distinguishing this as a "big savings" event.
- Wildly mismatched seasonal signals: Christmas trees, Christmas Crafts, and Christmas Light Sets sit above Football Party and Dorm/Classroom essentials — no coherent narrative for a late-August send.
- This is the longest, most exhausting tile-grid in the brand history reviewed so far (more sections than 08-13's "Select Home Decor" or 08-16's "Fall Essentials" sends), which will kill scroll completion on mobile.
- Repeated "Shop Now" CTA copy on every tile gives no signal about which offer is actually the best one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick the actual best offer (Classroom Decor 75% or Dorm Essentials 75%) and build a real hero module around it above the fold — right now the subject's headline number is invisible until deep scroll.
- 2. Cut the tile count by half. Split Christmas and back-to-school into separate sends instead of one 25-tile catalog dump — this brand's own history shows shorter, single-theme sends (08-18, 08-14) score higher.
- 3. Reorder by season relevance: back-to-school/classroom/dorm first (matches late-August timing), push Christmas to its own dedicated send.
- 4. Replace generic "Shop Now" with category-specific CTA copy so the eye has a reason to stop on any one tile.
