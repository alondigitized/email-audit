# Serve In Style With 50% Off Fall 🍂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject "Serve In Style With 50% Off Fall 🍂" only half-matches it. The 50% Fall hero is real and looks good. It has a big "50% OFF" number over a pumpkin table-runner photo. But it sits below a free-shipping bar, a KINKEEPING banner and a Kitchen/Bath/Home row. "Serve In Style" suggests tableware, and only the Kitchen Essentials tile at the top touches that. The hero itself is generic fall décor, party, floral and crafts.
- The rest is a very long scroll of 50%, 40% and 30% blocks: Christmas, Home Decor, Hobbies, Fabric, Art Supplies, Jewelry Making, then a 75% clearance bar. Almost every block says "See Weekly Ad For Details," and the CTAs are "In-Store Or Online." A subscriber who got the 11/01, 10/30 and 10/28 sends has seen this template four times in a week, and the 50% Fall offer is the same one from 10/28. It's competent and honest, but it's a catalog rather than a campaign.
- Verdict: solid, repetitive and mid-pack. The subject is better than the 11/01 "Season Of Savings" one because it names a real offer. Nothing in the render is broken.

## What's working

- The 50% Fall hero is large, with a clear number and a navy "In-Store Or Online" button.
- The subject names a concrete offer (50%) and matches a real block in the email.
- The Fall sub-grid (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) gives easy product-category entry points.
- Layout is clean, with consistent big discount numerals and no visible broken images or overlaps.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 7, 2026" line gives a real deadline.

## What's weak

- "Serve In Style" promises tableware, but the hero is generic fall décor and crafts.
- The free-shipping bar and KINKEEPING banner push the 50% hero below the first screen on mobile.
- The scroll is very long, with about 10 offer blocks. The "See Weekly Ad For Details" repeated everywhere dilutes the 50% message.
- Christmas 50% competes directly with Fall 50% as an offer, so the email has no single focus.
- The CTAs ("In-Store Or Online") are vague, with no verb and no specific product.
- The fine print under the free-shipping bar is tiny and hard to read, and "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" carries an asterisk, which weakens the honesty signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero and move the offer above the fold. Put the 50% Fall block first, and shrink or move the free-shipping and KINKEEPING bars below it.
- 2. Make the subject match the hero. Either build a real tableware/entertaining hero or drop "Serve In Style."
- 3. Cut the block count to about 4 and split the Christmas, Fabric and Jewelry content into its own send. That would also ease the cadence of near-identical circulars.
- 4. Replace "In-Store Or Online" with verb-led CTAs such as "Shop Fall Tableware" or "Shop 50% Off Fall Decor."
- 5. Add a Rewards or member-pricing cue and a clear countdown to November 7.

# Technical Audit: Hobby Lobby, "Serve In Style With 50% Off Fall 🍂"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a conventional table-based ESP template with Outlook conditionals, and the QA run found no hard failures. The four warnings are mostly capture limits of the AgentMail relay. The HTML was truncated, so link targets, UTM parameters and the footer could not be checked directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 links were probed and 34 skipped, ranked by click likelihood. No probe failures were reported, but the 34 skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking domain:** images and links route through `link.hobbylobby.com`, a first-party tracking domain. That is good practice, and the QA data shows no problem with it.
- **Plain-text part:** `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 12940/15138 chars`. That is about 85% URL characters, which is consistent with long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative. This is a real but low-severity deliverability signal, because the text part is mostly redirect URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (confirmed):** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142337_717751_152803.gif` has no alt text. The `/mo/` path and the `.gif` extension suggest an open-tracking pixel, which would make this low impact. If it is a content image, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Responsive breakpoint:** the only visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is far below typical phone widths (about 320–480px), so the `.fl`, `.fw` and `.image` stacking rules would not fire on most phones. Other queries may exist in the truncated CSS, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Invalid CSS declarations (confirmed in source):**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a misspelled property, so vertical alignment is silently dropped.
  - These are ignored by clients, but the CTA style falls back to inherited color and square corners.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns` attributes. This is harmless in practice and is common in ESP templates.
- **Title:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%` or `[[ ]]` tokens. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool itself says "Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay." This is **unconfirmed**. It cannot be treated as a sender defect without checking raw headers in a direct-delivery inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders over 5,000 messages a day.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** the QA tool says this is "Expected via AgentMail relay," so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**, not failing.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA run reports an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was flagged. The footer was not visible in the truncated HTML, so the postal address is unverified.
- **From address:** `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com` is a subdomain sender. It is acceptable, but replies are not possible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. The visible source includes no `href` values, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment with the "50% Off Fall" offer are unverified. The probed links reported no errors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML and raw headers, ideally from a direct inbox such as Gmail, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, or descriptive alt text if `eORqXTkAb7_…gif` is visible content.
3. Fix the CSS typos (`verticl-align` and the empty `color:` and `border-radius:px` values) in the template.
4. Check that the responsive rules fire at phone widths, since the visible breakpoint is 300px.
5. Shorten or reduce tracked URLs in the plain-text part, so the URL ratio drops below the 70% threshold.
6. Probe the 34 skipped links, and verify UTM parameters and landing-page offer alignment on the primary CTAs.
