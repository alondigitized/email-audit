# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, with a home-decor subject line on top. The subject promises "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price", and the Home Decor 40% block does exist. It sits below a free-shipping bar and a big "Christmas 50%" block, so the subject's topic isn't the first thing you see. The render is about 9,400px tall and runs as a stack of category blocks: Christmas, Home Decor, Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps, Fall Decor, Football Party, Party, Floral, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Crafts, Frames, Paper Crafts, Yarn, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notions, Custom Frames and Furniture. It works as a price catalog, not a focused send.
- This exact subject was used on 2026-10-22 (also scored 5/10), and this send has the same structure. The brand's sends this week (10-21, 10-22, 10-23, 10-25, 10-27, 10-28) rotate the same circular under different subject lines. A subscriber would see this as the seventh near-identical promo in nine days. Judgement: serviceable but repetitive, and the subject and the hero don't match.

## What's working

- The offers are concrete and easy to scan: "40%", "50%", "$3.42", "99¢" and "30%" appear in large type.
- The red-and-green "Christmas Essentials" banner and the fall and football blocks match the late-October season.
- A free-shipping bar and an offer-end line at the top give a clear time boundary.
- Each category block lists specific product types (sheet sets, bath towels, window curtains, candle wax), so you can tell what's included.
- I can't see any broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- The first screen is the shipping bar and a Christmas 50% block, not the home decor the subject names. Home Decor 40% starts a few hundred pixels down.
- Nothing in the render looks like a button. The categories are tiles with "Categories Listed" text and no "Shop Home Decor" call to action.
- The email is about 9,400px long with 18+ competing blocks, so there's no single hero.
- The subject repeats the 2026-10-22 send word for word, and cadence is heavy.
- The "Always 40% off the marked price" claim carries "Does not include Seasonal Department" fine print. Many blocks have exclusions in small type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the subject's own offer first. Lead with Home Decor 40% (framed art, canvas art, tapestries, wall clocks) above Christmas, or change the subject to match whatever really leads.
- 2. Add a visible button to each major block, such as "Shop Wall Decor" or "Shop Textiles".
- 3. Cut the send to 5–6 blocks around one theme. Right now it's the whole circular.
- 4. Don't reuse a subject within two weeks. If the offer is evergreen ("Always 40%"), that is a reason to send it less often.
- 5. Put the exclusions in plain language near the offer instead of in small type.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Framed art, canvas, tapestries and more, plus free shipping on $59+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (0 issues, 4 QA warnings), but it has several code-level defects in the visible CSS and head. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed only 12 of 40 links, so 28 were not verified. Those were mostly footer, utility and social links.
- Links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking redirect domain (the same host serves the tracking GIF). The probe results aren't in the data provided, so I can't confirm that the redirects resolve to the destination pages.
- The plain-text part is 88% URLs (11,540 of 13,082 chars). That suggests long tracking URLs are being printed inline, which looks spammy and is hard to read in text-only clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142403_4840453_152741.gif` has no `alt`. It is probably a tracking or open pixel, so impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values from unresolved template variables. Clients will drop the declarations, so the button color and radius are undefined.
- **CSS typo:** `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is misspelled, so the declaration is ignored.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` omits `width=device-width`.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is far below typical phone widths (about 320–480px), so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, etc.) likely never fire. The source is truncated, so a later query could exist, but none is visible.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns` XHTML and VML namespaces.
- **Missing `lang`:** the `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. It has a non-standard `e-locale="en-US"` instead, which screen readers ignore.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` doesn't match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The empty-value CSS in section 3 is the only template-variable leak, and it is in the stylesheet rather than the visible content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA notes the relay may strip it, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain is `create.hobbylobby.com`, but I can't check alignment from the data provided.
- QA indicates an unsubscribe link exists in the body, since the probe always includes one. The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
I can't assess this fully. The destination `href`s and any UTM parameters aren't in the truncated source or QA output. Because everything is wrapped in `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects, UTM and campaign parameters can only be checked after resolving the redirect chain. The subject's "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price" claim could not be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the template variables behind `.ctacr1` so `color:`, `border-radius:px` and the other empty values get real values.
2. Correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
3. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`. Raise the responsive breakpoint to about 480–600px, or confirm that a later media query exists.
4. Add `alt=""` to the `eORqXTkAb7_…gif` image. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and make `<title>` match the subject.
5. Check the raw `.eml` from the sending ESP for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. This email was captured through the AgentMail relay, so the missing headers may be an artifact of that capture.
6. Shorten the plain-text version, for example by using link labels or shorter redirect URLs.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for the 28 unprobed links, then confirm the UTMs and the landing page for the 40%-off offer.
