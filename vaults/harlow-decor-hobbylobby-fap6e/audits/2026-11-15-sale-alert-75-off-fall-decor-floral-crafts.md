# Sale Alert 🚨🍁 75% Off Fall Decor, Floral & Crafts
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's daily circular again, and the subject oversells it. "Sale Alert 🚨🍁 75% Off Fall Decor, Floral & Crafts" promises a broad 75% event. The render is a very long scroll of about 9,400px, and the 75% only shows up in one mid-email block, "Fall Decor, Floral & Crafts". The email opens on a "Christmas Essentials" banner and a big "Christmas 50%" block. A reader who clicked for 75% off Fall would have to scroll past Christmas, Home Decor and Textiles to find it.
- The email is also dense. Nearly every block has its own big percentage: 50%, 40%, 25%, 30%, 20%. Many carry "Categories Listed" and exclusion fine print. Nothing clearly leads, so the 75% number the subject sells gets lost.
- It follows the same template as the Nov. 8 "Shop Now: 50% Off Christmas & Fall!" and Nov. 11 "All Things Pumpkin! 50% Off Fall" sends. This one has the best fall discount of that run, but the layout buries it. My verdict is average, a 5-6 send. The 75% discount deserves a much better placement than it got.

## What's working

- The subject names a concrete, deeper discount (75%) and the fall season, which fits mid-November.
- The sender is recognizable, and the template looks like a real Hobby Lobby circular.
- The render has no broken images or overlapping text, and the product photos load cleanly.
- Some blocks show specific price points (4.99, 9.99, 99¢, 3.99/7.99) that make the deals feel concrete.
- A dated validity line near the footer gives the sale an end date.

## What's weak

- The 75% fall block sits about a quarter of the way down, below Christmas, Home Decor, Textiles and Mirrors. The hero doesn't match the subject.
- There is no clear primary CTA button. The whole email is a stack of category tiles with no "Shop 75% Off Fall" action.
- Too many competing percentages (50, 40, 25, 30, 20) flatten the hierarchy.
- "Does not include…" and "Categories Listed" exclusion text appears under many blocks. It undercuts the headline numbers and makes the offers feel less honest.
- There is a fifth-straight-day circular cadence, with the Nov. 14 cause email as the only break. Subject fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 75% Fall Decor, Floral & Crafts block to the top as the hero, with a visible "Shop Fall 75% Off" button. Push Christmas below it.
- 2. Cut the email to the top 5-6 categories and drop the long tail (Furniture, Sewing Notions, Custom Frames).
- 3. Say which items are in the 75% block, and put the exclusions in one line instead of repeating them everywhere.
- 4. Add a clear end date and time to the top of the email, not only the footer.
- 5. Alternative subject lines and preheaders:
- **Subject Alt A:** `75% Off Fall Decor & Florals – Ends Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Clearance: 75% Off Floral & Crafts 🍁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 50% off Christmas and 40% off Home Decor this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pumpkins, garlands and picks, marked down through Saturday.`
