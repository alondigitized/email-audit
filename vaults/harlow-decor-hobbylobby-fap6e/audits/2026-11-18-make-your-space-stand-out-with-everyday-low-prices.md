# Make Your Space Stand Out With Everyday Low Prices!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, but the subject is more honest than most. "Make Your Space Stand Out With Everyday Low Prices!" matches the render. The "NEW LOWER PRICES!" banner sits mid-email over a six-tile category grid (crosses, easels, florals, paint sets, canvas, clocks, mirrors, trees, wood blocks). Below it is a long run of price-forward product tiles: $4.99 vinyl, $2.99 foam board, $9.99 canvas sets, $2.99 Rit dye.
- The email has a real offer, but its order works against it. The top is a "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" bar and a big "Christmas Essentials" banner. That banner contradicts the "everyday low prices" subject. The actual hook sits below the first screen. The lower half is a clean, scannable price grid, but it's a utility catalog. There's no single hero and no urgency until the tiny "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 21, 2026" footer.
- Judgement: a competent, honest, low-drama circular. It is better aligned than the Nov 16 commercial email, and as unfocused as the Nov 12 and Nov 15 circulars. It's a 5-6 send.

## What's working

- The subject matches the body: the render is dominated by "NEW LOWER PRICES!" and a price-led grid.
- Big dollar prices ($4.99, $2.99, $1.99, 37¢, 99¢) make the value instantly scannable.
- The two-column product grid is consistent, with clear names, sizes and "In-Store Or Online" labels.
- The free-shipping bar at the top ($69 threshold) adds a concrete incentive.
- The blue "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar is a visible, unambiguous CTA.

## What's weak

- The Christmas Essentials banner takes the prime hero spot, which contradicts the "everyday low prices" subject and splits focus.
- The CTA is a generic "click here" bar rather than a verb plus noun, and the product tiles have no individual buttons, only the small "In-Store Or Online ▸" links.
- The sale deadline (Nov 21) is buried in tiny footer text, so urgency is nearly zero.
- The email is long, about 6,000px, with 20+ similar tiles and no hierarchy or "best deal" callout.
- The "lowered prices" claim has no reference price, so there's no visible savings. Only the T-shirts and sweatshirt show an "Always 20% Off" reference.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with "NEW LOWER PRICES" at the top and move Christmas Essentials below it, or send it separately.
- 2. Put the Nov 21 deadline in the header bar next to the free shipping line.
- 3. Add "was/now" pricing or a "lowered by X%" tag on the best five items.
- 4. Cut the grid to 8-10 tiles and give each a "Shop Vinyl"-style CTA instead of a repeated "In-Store Or Online".
- 5. Replace "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" with "Shop This Week's Ad".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Prices Just Dropped on Canvas, Vinyl & Craft Basics`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping over $69. Lower prices through Saturday, Nov. 21.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based, Oracle Responsys-style template with MSO conditional CSS and a few invalid CSS declarations. The QA run is 50% pass with 0 failures and 4 warnings, and the warnings are mostly unverifiable header and plain-text issues. The HTML was truncated and only 12 of 54 links were probed, so anything below the `<style>` block is unaudited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is low.** 12 material links were probed and 42 were skipped, so about 78% of links are unverified. No broken links were reported among the 12.
- **Plain-text URL bloat.** 16,012 of 18,892 plain-text characters (about 85%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. This points to long click-tracking URLs on `link.hobbylobby.com` printed in full in the text part. It can hurt spam scoring and makes the text part unreadable.
- **Tracking-domain asset.** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144339_4379060_154313.gif` is served from the click-tracking domain. The `/mo/` path and GIF format suggest an open-tracking pixel, but the truncated source doesn't confirm that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The GIF above has no `alt` attribute. If it is a tracking pixel, this is low-impact, and `alt=""` would be the correct fix. If it is content, it is an accessibility failure.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values for `color` and `border-radius`. Clients drop these declarations, so the CTA text colour and radius fall back to inherited values.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo (`verticl-align`), so the vertical alignment never applies.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML and VML `xmlns` attributes. This is common in ESP templates and low risk.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html>` has no `lang`, only the non-standard `e-locale="en-US"`, so screen readers get no language hint.
- **Generic `<title>`.** The title is "Hobby Lobby" and doesn't reflect the subject line.
- **Dark mode.** I saw no `color-scheme` meta tag or `prefers-color-scheme` rules in the visible portion. The rest is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `$(…)` tokens. I can't rule out tokens in the truncated body.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing [WARN].** The QA data says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this may be a capture artifact rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require the header, so this needs verification against the raw message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing [WARN].** RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is also unconfirmed, with the same caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely reason, but this is not verified.
- **Sender address.** The From address is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`. That is a non-replyable sender on a subdomain, which is mildly negative for engagement signals.
- **Footer content.** The unsubscribe link and postal address in the body were not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance. The QA tool did include an unsubscribe link in its probes, but the result isn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. No UTM parameters, landing URLs or redirect chains are visible in the truncated source or the QA output. Links route through `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects, and the final destination and parameter preservation were not reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment) before treating them as defects.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `/mo/…154313.gif` image if it is a pixel, or a descriptive `alt` if it is content.
3. Fix the CSS: give `.ctacr1` real values for `color` and `border-radius` (or remove them), and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and make the `<title>` match the subject.
5. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links, so the text part isn't about 85% URLs.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML with link probing raised above 12 links. This would confirm UTM parameters, redirect landing pages, footer compliance, and any merge-token leaks.
