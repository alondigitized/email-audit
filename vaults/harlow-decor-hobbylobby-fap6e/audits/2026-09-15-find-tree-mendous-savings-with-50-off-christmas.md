# 🎄 Find Tree-mendous Savings With 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby actually delivering on the subject line — "50% Off Christmas" gets a genuine hero band with styled flocked Christmas trees, lanterns, and wreaths, a clean "50% OFF Christmas / Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" badge, and a Christmas-category tile row (Trees, Wreaths, Ornaments, Lights) right underneath it. It's the same structural template as the 2026-09-08 "Fall Must-Haves" send that scored 6/10 — hero-first, then category grid — and it works for the same reason: subject and top-of-fold visual match.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the "Click Here To View Our Weekly Ad" divider. The email pivots hard into a second full campaign — "Fall Essentials" — with its own hero and six more 30-40%-off category tiles (Home Decor, Football Party, Floral, Yarn, Paper Crafts), before finally closing with a clearance banner. In September, sending a Christmas trees email is itself notable — six-week-early holiday merchandising — but burying it under a longer Fall section undercuts the very subject line that earned the open.
- Net: strong top-of-fold, diluted by mid-September running two unrelated seasonal campaigns stacked in one email and reverting to the wall-to-wall tile grid that's plagued nearly every recent send.

## What's working

- Subject ("50% Off Christmas") is matched by an actual Christmas-tree hero image directly below the fold-line promo banners — no bait-and-switch.
- Clear single discount badge (50% OFF) with a visible "In-Store Or Online" CTA button.
- Christmas sub-category tiles (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) are directly relevant to the hero, unlike prior sends' generic grids.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) is visible immediately at the top for added value signal.

## What's weak

- The email crams two full, unrelated seasonal campaigns (Christmas + Fall) into one send, which splits attention and drowns the Christmas offer that got the open.
- Post-divider reverts to the exact wall-to-wall stacked-tile format criticized in the 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-13 audits — six more discount tiles before the email ends.
- Sending a full Christmas assortment on September 15 is jarring positioned above the still-current Fall campaign — no framing (e.g., "get ahead early") to justify the whiplash.
- Generic top strip (Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials tiles) before the hero delays getting to the actual subject-line offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails — one focused purely on Christmas 50% off, one on Fall — instead of merging both campaigns into a single long scroll; this alone would meaningfully lift click-through by removing the mid-email attention split.
- 2. Cut the generic Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials strip at the top so the Christmas hero appears immediately after the header, matching the subject line faster.
- 3. Add a line of context copy near the hero ("Shop early and save") to justify a Christmas email in mid-September rather than let it feel abrupt next to the Fall content.
- 4. Trim the Fall section to 2-3 tiles max if it must stay in the same send, rather than the full six-tile repeat of the standard grid.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Get Ahead: 50% Off Christmas Before It Sells Out`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flocked trees, wreaths & lights — all 50% off, in-store or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop early: Christmas decor at half price while selection is best.`
