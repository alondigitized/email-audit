# Small Space, Big Style At Hobby Lobby®
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- "Small Space, Big Style" is Hobby Lobby's attempt to break the mega-circular mold with an editorial lifestyle hook — and the hero earns it. The "Small Space Living" headline over a cozy, basket-filled bedroom is the most coherent top-of-funnel concept this brand has shown in the last two weeks of audits. The video play button overlay adds intrigue; the room genuinely looks curated and aspirational.
- That coherence lasts exactly one scroll. Below the hero, the email reverts to the identical stacked-tile mega-circular that ran on July 2 ("Home Decor – Always 40% Off"), June 28 ("Fall Starts Now"), and June 25 ("Enhance Your Space") — same layout, same tiles, same sequence (July 4th → Spring Shop → Home Decor → Summer Toys → Fall → Fabric). The "Small Space" concept is completely abandoned the moment you leave the hero. Worse, the only CTA bridging hero and tile dump reads "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR EMAIL AD," which is arguably the weakest CTA copy any retailer of this size should be allowed to use.
- Verdict: a genuinely promising hero wasted by an immediate editorial collapse into a format the subscriber has seen seven times this month.

## What's working

- Hero image is the strongest visual Hobby Lobby has run this week — warm light, real room staging, clear small-space context
- "Small Space Living" headline is direct and category-specific, matching the subject line without restating it word-for-word
- Video play button overlay implies richer content and earns a click on its own merits
- Free shipping threshold ("$59 Or More") is visible in the header without hunting
- July 4th tile is appropriately timed for a July 3 send — continues the solid holiday cadence from the June 30 send

## What's weak

- "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR EMAIL AD" is the worst CTA in the audit window — it implies the email you're reading is not the email, which is actively confusing
- The editorial small-space hook is entirely abandoned below the fold; none of the promotional tiles reference storage, small-room furniture, or organization
- Tile sequence (Spring Shop 50% off, Summer Toys) directly contradicts the "Fall" tile three rows below — the assortment is at war with itself seasonally
- Five separate discount percentages (30%, 40%, 40%, 40%, 50%, 50%) in the same email dilutes every single offer
- No urgency anywhere in the body copy — "Small Space, Big Style" and none of the tiles give a deadline beyond the July 4th implication

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the CTA entirely.** "Shop Small Space Living" or "Explore The Look" tied directly to a small-space collection landing page. "VIEW OUR EMAIL AD" needs to be retired permanently.
- 2. **Curate the tile block to match the hook.** Keep 3–4 tiles (storage, wall decor, furniture, baskets) that actually belong in a small-space context. Drop the unrelated Summer Toys and seasonal contradiction.
- 3. **Add one deadline.** Even "This Week Only" under the hero converts better than no urgency at all.
- 4. **Kill one discount tier.** Pick 40% or 50% — not both in the same email on adjacent tiles.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Small Space Living — Shop The Look This Weekend`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Under 500 Sq Ft? These Pieces Were Made For You`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Storage, wall decor & wicker finds curated for smaller rooms — free shipping $59+`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `The small-space edit is live — 40% off home decor this week only`

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Broken redirect: "My Account" nav link**

The tracked URL resolves to a relative path `/login` rather than an absolute destination, which is an invalid redirect target in email context:

```
URL: https://link.hobbylobby.com/u/nrd.php?p=eORqXTkAb7_4743644_128488_1_49&...
Error: unknown url type: '/login'
```

This link will silently fail or 404 for recipients. The underlying destination should be an absolute URL (e.g., `https://www.hobbylobby.com/login`). This is the only confirmed hard broken link; all other tracked links passed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**WARN — Missing alt text on tracking pixel**

```
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_128488_4743644_137172.gif
```

The open-tracking GIF has no `alt=""` attribute. While this is a 1×1 pixel and no visible content is lost, screen readers may attempt to announce the src filename. Should be `alt=""` to suppress announcement.

**No issues found** in Outlook VML fallbacks, responsive breakpoints, or font stack declarations. The `@media (max-width:300px)` block is present and structurally correct.

**Minor — CSS typo in `.lhp2`:** `verticl-align:middle` (line in `<style>`) is a misspelled property — `vertical-align`. No rendering impact since it's a no-op, but warrants cleanup.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — List-Unsubscribe header not captured**

The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not detected by the AgentMail relay. These headers are required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since Feb 2024). If they are present upstream but stripped by the relay, the relay configuration needs review. If absent at send time, this is a deliverability compliance gap.

**WARN — Authentication-Results header missing**

SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown because `Authentication-Results` was not captured. Cannot confirm `create.hobbylobby.com` is properly aligned for DMARC. Needs verification at the MTA level.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source — should be verified against the full footer.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params)

All outbound links are wrapped in `link.hobbylobby.com/u/nrd.php` redirects, which is expected for click tracking.
