# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby's homepage is doing the one thing I actually showed up for — telling me there's a 50% off sale — but it stops there. The hero is a spring tablescape with cute blue-and-white striped dishes and rattan, which feels more like a Pinterest brunch spread than the farmhouse signage and faux florals I'm here to shop. I know this rotating sale calendar by heart, so the headline lands, but I had to work to mentally map "Spring Shop" to my categories.

## What's working

- **"50% OFF The Spring Shop®" headline** — The size and boldness of that typography is doing its job. I clocked it before I finished loading the page.
- **"Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" banner** — It's the first thing at the top, pinned. A $59 threshold is completely reachable for me in one trip. I appreciate seeing it upfront rather than buried in the cart.
- **Sale deadline ("Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026")** — A real calendar date, not "while supplies last" nonsense. I can plan around that.
- **"Weekly Ad" button in the nav bar** — As someone who checks the weekly ad religiously, having that as a primary nav button (not hidden in a dropdown) is exactly right. It respects my shopping habits.

## What's weak

- **The hero image** — Rattan tray, tomatoes, butter dish, red-striped plates — this is a summer picnic / kitchen scene. I came for farmhouse wreaths and dried botanicals. Nothing in that image says "your stuff is on sale."
- **"See Weekly Ad For Details"** — That asterisk immediately makes me distrust the 50% claim. *Which* items in the Spring Shop? All of them? Certain SKUs? I know from experience it's usually category-specific, but a new or casual shopper would feel baited.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards visibility** — I have a Hobby Lobby account. I can see the little person icon in the nav bar, but there's no "Your rewards balance: $X" or "Member deals inside" hook. That's a wasted opportunity to get me deeper into the funnel immediately.
- **Navigation requires work** — To get to Floral or Farmhouse Decor, I'd have to tap that hamburger menu and hunt. There's no category strip or shortcut visible. For a weekly shopper, that friction adds up.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the picnic tablescape with a farmhouse/floral shot** — Show a shiplap-and-greenery vignette or a basket of faux peonies with the 50% badge overlaid. That single swap would tell me the sale covers what I actually buy.
- **Add a "Rotating this week: Floral & Farmhouse Décor 50% off" sub-line under the headline** — Don't hide the category in the weekly ad. Put the rotating category name right there. I already know the schedule, but that copy would make me tap immediately rather than confirm in the ad.
- **Surface a loyalty/rewards nudge** — Even a small "Earn Rewards on every purchase" line under the "Shop Now" button would signal that Hobby Lobby sees me as a repeat customer, not a drive-by shopper.
- **Add a horizontal category shortcut strip below the hero** — Four tappable icons: Floral, Seasonal Décor, Frames & Art, Fabrics. One tap to my aisle instead of navigating through the hamburger.
