# Hurry In! Up To 75% Off Savings!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's biggest sprawl yet — more than 25 stacked category tiles running from Home Decor down through Custom Frames and Furniture, eclipsing even the 25+-tile "New Week, New Savings!" grid from 7/26 that scored 6.00/10 for the same reason. But there's a real improvement buried in here: unlike the 8/4 rug email (subject promised rugs, delivered a mixed grid) or the 8/2 football email (subject promised 40% off football gear, hero delivered "Fall Essentials / Free Shipping"), the "Up To 75% Off" claim in this subject is actually true — Spring Shop & Summer Toys, select Home Decor, and select Furniture all carry genuine 75% tiles.
- The problem is placement, not honesty. The hero banner at the top is "Fall Essentials" with zero percentage callout, so a reader has to scroll past ~8-10 tiles of "ALWAYS 40% OFF" boilerplate before hitting the 75% off items the subject actually promised. It's the same structural issue as every recent Hobby Lobby send: real, honest offers exist somewhere in the email, they're just never where the subject line points.
- A legible deadline — "Sale Valid Through Saturday, August 8, 2026" — sits at the very bottom, which is the one piece of copy that could have justified "Hurry In!" and it's the last thing anyone will see.

## What's working

- The subject's "Up To 75% Off" claim is backed by real tiles (Spring Shop & Summer Toys 75%, select Home Decor 75%, select Furniture 75%) — a genuine subject/body match this brand hasn't managed in its last two sends.
- Free shipping threshold ($59+) is stated clearly right under the nav, with the Colorado fee caveat disclosed rather than buried.
- The sale end date, "Saturday, August 8, 2026," is a real, near-term (2-day) deadline — not vague evergreen urgency.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent tile formatting across all ~25 sections.

## What's weak

- The hero banner ("Fall Essentials," no percentage) doesn't reference the 75% off hook the subject leads with — the strongest offer in the email is invisible above the fold.
- 25+ stacked tiles with no visual hierarchy — Textiles, Mirrors, Floral, Football, Classroom, Dorm, Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Crafts, Frames, Paper Crafts, Yarn, Jewelry, Sewing, Custom Frames, Furniture all get equal visual weight, so nothing reads as "the" offer.
- The 75%-off tiles are roughly 10 scrolls deep on mobile — most readers will bail on the repetitive "ALWAYS 40% OFF THE MARKED PRICE*" tiles before reaching them.
- "Sale Valid Through Saturday, August 8, 2026" — the one line that earns "Hurry In!" — is buried at the very bottom, after the entire scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to lead with the actual 75%-off tiles (Spring Shop & Summer Toys, Furniture, Home Decor) instead of the generic "Fall Essentials" banner — that's the subject's promise, it should be the first thing visible.
- 2. Move "Sale Valid Through Saturday, August 8, 2026" up next to the hero so the urgency in the subject line has a visible deadline to back it up.
- 3. Cut the circular from 25+ tiles to 8-10 — split lower-priority categories (Sewing Notions, Custom Frames, All Yarn, Jewelry Making) into a separate send rather than one continuous scroll.
- 4. Vary the repeated "ALWAYS 40% OFF THE MARKED PRICE*" boilerplate — right now it reads as templated filler repeated a dozen times rather than curated merchandising.
