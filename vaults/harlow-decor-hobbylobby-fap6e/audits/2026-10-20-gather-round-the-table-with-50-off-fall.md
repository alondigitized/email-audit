# Gather ‘Round The Table With 50% Off Fall 🍂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells a cozy fall table, and the hero delivers it. A big "50% off Fall" badge sits over a rich tablescape of pumpkins, florals and a taper candle. That is a better match between subject and creative than the 10/11, 10/15 and 10/18 sends, whose first screens were Christmas. Fall still doesn't get the first screen, though. A "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar, a Kinkeeping banner and a Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials row sit above it. Fall also loses attention to a second "Christmas Essentials 50% off" block directly below it.
- The rest is Hobby Lobby's usual long stack of 50%, 40% and 30% tiles. The fine print is vague: "See Weekly Ad For Details," "Select Categories," and "In-Store Or Online" on every tile. The real deadline, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 24, 2026," is in the footer, where almost nobody will read it. It's a competent, on-brand send that is clear about the offer and weak on urgency and focus. I'd call it average to slightly above, since the subject and hero finally agree.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong seasonal image with a large, legible "50% OFF Fall" badge, so the offer reads at a glance.
- The subject and hero agree. "Gather 'Round The Table" and the fall tablescape tell the same story, unlike the Christmas-first sends earlier this week.
- The six Fall tiles (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) give shoppers clear paths into the category.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlaps or text collisions.
- The "Online Clearance up to 75% off" banner and the quick-link grid near the bottom catch bargain hunters.

## What's weak

- Fall isn't the first thing you see. The shipping bar, Kinkeeping banner and three Essentials tiles push the hero down the screen.
- The Christmas 50% block competes with the fall offer. Two 50% offers on two seasons dilute the message and repeat the problem from the 10/16 and 10/18 sends.
- The deadline, Saturday, October 24, is buried in the footer. The body has no countdown or "ends Saturday" language.
- CTAs are weak. Every tile says "In-Store Or Online ▸" in small text, with no verb and no product, and the only real button is the small "In-Store Or Online" pill on the hero.
- There is no loyalty or member benefit, and no product detail or social proof. Below the hero it's a wall of percentage tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the fall hero at the top and move the shipping bar and Essentials row below it. If the offer is "50% off Fall," that should be the first screen on mobile.
- 2. Cut or demote the Christmas block on fall sends. Give Christmas its own send, or move it below the fall tiles.
- 3. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Saturday, 10/24," and in the preheader. The date already exists in the footer.
- 4. Replace "In-Store Or Online ▸" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Fall Decor" and "Shop Fall Florals." Make the hero button a solid, high-contrast "Shop 50% Off Fall."
- 5. Cut the tile count. Eight offers across five discount levels is too much choice.
- 6. Add a preheader. I can't see one in the render.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style build with a link-tracking redirect domain (`link.hobbylobby.com`). The QA run had 0 hard failures and 4 warnings, all of them header-visibility or hygiene items. The HTML was truncated, so the unsubscribe link, UTM parameters and most `<a>` tags could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 34 were skipped. None of the 12 returned a failure, but the other 34 are unverified.
- **Tracking domain:** The image at `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_140901_3581005_150053.gif` is on the click-tracking domain. Its `/mo/` path and the missing alt text suggest it is an open-tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the visible source.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,986 of 15,179 characters (about 86%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The plain-text alternative is mostly long tracking links, which is a spam-filter signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The same GIF has no alt attribute. If it is a tracking pixel, this is low severity, but it should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has an empty color and a unitless radius. Both are dropped, so any element using `.ctacr1` falls back to inherited color and square corners.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so it never applies.
- **Responsive breakpoint:** The only media query visible in the truncated source is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. This is below the width of nearly all phones (320px and up), so the mobile overrides (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, `.h1`) would not fire. Another breakpoint may exist in the truncated part.
- **Document structure:**
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the `<html>` tag carries XHTML and VML namespaces.
  - `<title>` is the generic "Hobby Lobby" and doesn't match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the excerpt.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** Not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header:** Not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders. The same relay caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results header:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** The QA run reports that an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but the footer is not in the truncated source. I can't confirm the postal address.
- **Sender:** `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com` is a no-reply address on a subdomain. That is acceptable, but it gives recipients no reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters, redirect targets and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The destinations are hidden behind `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects, and 34 links were not probed. No broken landing pages were reported among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they are missing at the source, add them.
2. Fix the CSS: give `.ctacr1` a real `color` and `border-radius` value, and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
3. Confirm that a breakpoint at 600px or higher exists. If it doesn't, replace or supplement the `max-width:300px` query.
4. Add `alt=""` and explicit dimensions to the `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/…gif` image.
5. Shorten or reduce the plain-text part's link density, for example by trimming duplicate links and adding more descriptive text.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and a wider link-probe budget to verify UTMs, the footer and the remaining 34 links.
