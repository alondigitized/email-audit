# Up To 50% Off Savings Inside!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same wall-to-wall category-tile grid Hobby Lobby has been running since late August, now stretched even further — roughly 25 stacked category blocks from Home Decor down through Furniture, each with its own percentage badge. The subject line "Up To 50% Off Savings Inside!" is generic enough to promise anything and specific enough to promise nothing, and the email delivers exactly that: everything is on sale, so nothing reads as the actual offer.
- There is one genuine bright spot — Christmas has clearly arrived as a real merchandising push, with Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party & Tableware, and Light Sets all getting their own 40-50% blocks with actual lifestyle-adjacent product photography (a decorated tree, wreaths, ornaments, string lights). That's a stronger seasonal signal than the 09-20 and 09-16 sends. But it's buried as block 5 of 25, indistinguishable in visual weight from Doormats and Sewing Notions.
- Judgement: this is a catalog scan dressed as an email, not a marketing send. Compare to 09-23's "Say Hello To Pumpkin Season" (7.0/10) and 09-22's Christmas hero (6.0/10) — both led with one image and one offer up top. This one reverts to the low-scoring grid pattern (4-5/10 range) that's dominated most of this brand's recent cadence.

## What's working

- Christmas category cluster (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party & Tableware, Light Sets) is genuinely seasonal and well-timed for late September planning shoppers.
- Percentage badges are legible and consistent, making scanning easy if a shopper already knows what they want.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" banner up top gives one clear, low-friction incentive before the grid starts.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is structurally sound top to bottom.

## What's weak

- No hero image or lead offer — the email opens straight into a small "Fall Essentials" banner then immediately drops into Home Decor tiles with zero narrative framing.
- 25+ near-identical stacked blocks with no visual hierarchy — everything is 40% or 50% off, so nothing stands out as today's actual deal.
- Christmas (the most current, urgent seasonal category) gets no more visual priority than Sewing Notions or Custom Frames — a subscriber has to scroll past 4 unrelated categories to find it.
- Subject "Up To 50% Off Savings Inside!" with a bare exclamation point reads like generic promo boilerplate, not a real offer — no category, no product, no urgency.
- This is the third+ consecutive send (following 09-20, 09-18, 09-17) using this identical wall-of-tiles template — cadence fatigue is real for a repeat subscriber.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with Christmas as the hero — pull the Christmas Trees or Christmas Decor block to the top with a full-width lifestyle image, matching what worked in 09-22's "Deck The Halls" send (6.0/10). That's the freshest, most defensible seasonal story in this email.
- 2. Cut the grid to 6-8 categories max, or split into two sends (Fall/Everyday vs. Christmas) — 25 stacked blocks guarantees most get zero attention.
- 3. Replace the generic subject with the actual best offer: reference Christmas specifically since it's the strongest visual content in the email.
- 4. Add urgency or a deadline somewhere in the email — nothing here signals why today matters versus any other day.
