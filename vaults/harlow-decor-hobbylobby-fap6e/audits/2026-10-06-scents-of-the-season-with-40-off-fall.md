# 🍂 Scents Of The Season With 40% Off Fall
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- The hero is the best part. A candle, a reed diffuser and pumpkins sit under a big "40% OFF Fall" badge, and it matches the "Scents Of The Season" subject. Fall Candles is also one of the six tiles under the hero. That's the strongest subject-to-creative match Hobby Lobby has had in a while.
- Then the email does what it always does. It adds a Christmas Essentials banner, five more 30–50% off blocks (Home Decor, Party, Fabric, Art Supplies, Jewelry Making), a clearance bar and a footer grid. A subscriber sees "40% off" about eight times, so no single offer stands out. The hero is a good fall-scent send, and the rest of the email dilutes it.
- Compared with the 9/30 "Fall Is In The Air 🍂 Shop 40% Off Fall" send (7/10), this one has the same offer, the same emoji and a similar length. It's a bit more specific on scent, but it's a repeat. It lands in the same place as the 10/5 and 10/1 tile walls: competent and recognizable, but not a send that stands out in the inbox.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and seasonal. The "40% OFF Fall" badge is large, with candles, a diffuser and pumpkins behind it.
- The subject and hero agree. "Scents Of The Season" is backed by a visible diffuser and candle, and the Fall Candles tile.
- The 3x2 tile grid (Fall Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) makes it easy to jump to a category.
- Layout is stable. There are no broken images or overlapping text, and the brand voice is consistent.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 10, 2026" line gives the sale an end date.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. After the fall section it adds Christmas, Home Decor, Party, Fabric, Art Supplies, Jewelry and Clearance, so the scent story is buried.
- Every CTA is a generic "In-Store Or Online ▶". None says what you'd get or do, such as "Shop Fall Candles".
- The deadline is only in the footer. The email creates no urgency above the fold.
- The free-shipping bar ("$59 or More!") and the Kinkeeping banner sit above the hero and push the 40% offer down. The fine print is tiny ("*See Weekly Ad For Details", "Marked Price").
- It's a repeat of the 9/30 send's offer, emoji and framing, and it comes one day after the 10/5 Christmas send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the fall-scent hero, the 6-tile grid, one Christmas teaser and the footer. Drop the Fabric, Art Supplies and Jewelry blocks. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific. Use "Shop Fall Candles" and "Shop Diffusers" instead of "In-Store Or Online".
- 3. Move the sale end date ("Ends Saturday") into the hero and the preheader.
- 4. Add one product close-up with a price or a "Marked Price" example, such as a candle or diffuser at a shown price, so 40% off has a real number attached.
- 5. Move the free-shipping bar below the hero, or shrink it.
- 6. Vary the creative and subject from 9/30 so a fall send doesn't read as a repeat.
