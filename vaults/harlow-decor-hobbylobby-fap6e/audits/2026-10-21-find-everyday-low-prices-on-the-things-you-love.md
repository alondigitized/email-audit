## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's "lower prices" catalog email. A red "NEW LOWER PRICES!" headline sits over a grid of everyday items: crosses, easels, floral arrangements, paint sets and table clocks. A long run of price tiles follows, such as $4.99 Iron-On Vinyl, $2.99 Black Foam Board and $9.99 Blank Canvas Set. The idea is clear and the tiles are clean. The email is also a price list with no story.
- The subject promises "the things you love," but the first screen is a "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar and a Christmas Essentials banner. Neither matches that promise. It's the same Christmas opener as the 10-18 and 10-15 sends. The "Everyday Low Prices" angle also repeats the 10-14 email ("Find Everyday Low Prices In-Store Or Online!"). - Overall it's a competent but forgettable send. The tiles are well built and the prices are easy to read. It has no hero, no deadline up top and no clear reason to act today.

## What's working

- The red "NEW LOWER PRICES!" headline is the strongest element. It's easy to read and tells you what the email is about.
- The price tiles are consistent. Each has a big price, a product name, a size or count and an "In-Store Or Online" link, so the grid is quick to scan.
- Prices are low and concrete: $1.99 Sharpie Markers, 99¢ Bandanas, 37¢ Pacon Poster Board. They read as real value.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar and the Christmas Essentials banner fit the season.
- I can't see any broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject says "the things you love," but the opening screen is Christmas decor and shipping. The email doesn't deliver on it.
- The "Click Here To View Our Weekly Ad" button is a plain blue bar. It sits below a category grid, so it's easy to miss.
- The sale deadline, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 24, 2026," is tiny footer text below 20+ tiles. The urgency is wasted.
- The email is very long, with about 22 near-identical product tiles. There is no lead product, so it feels like a flyer.
- This is the third or fourth "everyday price" or Christmas-banner send in a week. The cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Add a "Lower prices through Sat 10/24" banner above the category grid, and cut the tile list to the best 6–8 items.
- 2. Make the primary CTA a real button, such as "Shop Lower Prices" or "See This Week's Ad," with higher contrast and placement right after the headline.
- 3. Pick one lead product (for example the $9.99 canvas set or 37¢ poster board) and give it a large hero image.
- 4. Differentiate this send from the 10-14 "Everyday Best Price" email. Use a "this week only" or category-specific angle instead of repeating the same theme.
- 5. Match the first screen to the subject. Either move the Christmas banner lower or change the subject to cover both.

# Technical Audit: Hobby Lobby, "Find Everyday Low Prices On The Things You Love!"

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- Tracking runs through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect domain (for example `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_140900_4921409_150052.gif`). No redirect-chain or status data was supplied, so I can't assess redirect health.
- The plain-text part is 16,012 of 18,891 characters URLs (85%, over the 70% threshold). The long tracking URLs are probably inflating the text part, which spam filters can score negatively.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_140900_4921409_150052.gif` has no `alt`. It sits on the tracking domain's `/mo/` path, so it may be an open-tracking pixel. If so, add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html ... e-locale="en-US">` is the only locale marker. `e-locale` is not a standard attribute, so screen readers get no language hint. Add `lang="en"`.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values. Clients will drop these declarations, so the button's color and radius are not applied. It likely comes from an unpopulated template variable.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so the property is ignored.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Phones are 320px or wider, so these responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, and so on) would almost never apply. The HTML is truncated, so there may be other queries I couldn't see. Verify this.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is combined with XHTML and VML namespaces. This is common in email and low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the supplied HTML. The empty values in `.ctacr1` (above) suggest a template variable did not resolve in the CSS, though not in the visible body content.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (QA WARN):** the QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. That makes this unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN):** this has the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN):** this is expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check `create.hobbylobby.com` DNS and raw headers directly.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page URLs could not be evaluated. The supplied source only shows opaque `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking URLs, and no probe destinations were reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the empty `.ctacr1` values and the `verticl-align` typo. Find out why the template variable didn't resolve.
2. Add `alt` to `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_140900_4921409_150052.gif`: `alt=""` if it is a pixel, descriptive text if it is content.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
