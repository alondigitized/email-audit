# National Collection Week – Nov. 16 - 23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a different kind of Hobby Lobby send, and a better one. It isn't the usual weekly circular. It's a cause email for Operation Christmas Child, run with Samaritan's Purse, and it asks readers to pack a shoebox during National Collection Week, Nov. 16–23. The red hero says "Send Good News & Great Joy!" and sits next to a strong photo of a delighted child holding a flashlight. The layout is clean and scannable, with three alternating red and green blocks: Find a Shoebox, Fill With Gifts, and Drop-Off.
- Against the brand's recent run of "50% Off" circulars (Nov. 6 through Nov. 13), this one has no discount at all. The only price language is the fine-print discount disclaimer at the bottom. For a deal-focused subscriber, that is a pattern break. Someone who has been trained to expect percent-off headlines will see a plain, date-based subject and may skip it. For someone who packs shoeboxes every year, it's a useful and timely reminder.
- My judgement: it's a well-built mission email with clear structure and a real deadline, but it's weak as a promotion. It does its job for its own audience and will under-perform on the usual commercial metrics. The "Learn More" button in the hero is the weakest link, since the headline moment has no concrete action.

## What's working

- The hero photo of a child with a flashlight carries real emotional weight, and the Samaritan's Purse logo adds credibility.
- The three-step flow (Find, Fill, Drop-Off) is easy to follow, with one image and one short paragraph per block.
- The red and green blocks alternate, so the long scroll stays scannable and feels seasonal.
- The drop-off block states the dates plainly: "National Collection Week, November 16–23, 2026."
- The CTAs are visible and look like buttons: "In-Store Or Online," "Shop Now," and "Find Location."

## What's weak

- The hero CTA, "Learn More," is vague and small against the huge "Send Good News & Great Joy!" headline.
- The subject is a plain event title. It has no emotional hook and nothing about shoeboxes or giving.
- Nothing links to a specific product, such as a shoebox, a gift bundle, or a supply list. "Shop Now" in the Fill With Gifts block goes to an unspecified place.
- The "Pack A Shoebox" banner is a thin strip that repeats the hero rather than adding information.
- The footer still carries the generic "Discounts provided every day" disclaimer, which clashes with a charity message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero CTA specific and the dates impossible to miss. Change "Learn More" to "Pack Your Shoebox," and put "Nov. 16–23" in the hero itself, not only in the third block.
- 2. Add a short checklist of gift ideas in the Fill With Gifts block, such as school supplies, hygiene items, and a small toy, each linked to its product category.
- 3. Use a more personal subject that names the action and the date, and keep the preheader to new information.
- 4. Replace the footer disclaimer on this send with charity-specific copy, such as how to find a drop-off location.
- 5. Merge the "Pack A Shoebox" strip into the hero, or give it its own job, such as a step counter ("Step 1 of 3").
