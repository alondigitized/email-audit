# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Hobby Lobby doing what Hobby Lobby does — hit me immediately with the 50% off rotating category and a "Shop Now" before I've even scrolled. I recognize the formula because I've been living by this sale calendar for years. Right now they're pushing The Spring Shop, which reads more Americana entertaining than true farmhouse, but the colorful ceramic plates and wicker tablescape in that hero have my attention. Nothing here screams "this was built for you, Harlow," but it's a clean, familiar experience that doesn't push me away either.

## What's working

- **The 50% off headline** — printed in huge black type below the hero, this is the hook I came here for. I know Hobby Lobby's rotating-category calendar and I will always stop to check what's on sale this week.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through July 11, 2026"** — that in-store callout matters to me. I don't fully trust digital-only promos; knowing I can walk in with this makes it real.
- **The "Weekly Ad" button** — it's the second thing on the page after the logo. That's exactly where I want it. I tap that first every time.
- **Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More** — the red banner up top is easy to read and $59 is a realistic order for me. Good anchor to set expectations upfront.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no pop-ups fighting for attention, no layout shift. This loads and just works on my phone.

## What's weak

- **The Spring Shop hero image reads "Italian picnic," not farmhouse** — wicker chargers, colorful polka-dot plates, a pasta box. This isn't my aesthetic. I'm looking for neutral tones, aged wood, white ceramic, maybe a floral sprig. This feels like it's talking to someone younger or more eclectic.
- **No faux floral or decor-specific entry point above the fold** — there's a search bar, but no category tile or banner that says "Floral," "Farmhouse," or "Home Decor." I have to dig.
- **No loyalty or rewards signal whatsoever** — I've been shopping here for years. The fact that there's zero acknowledgment of that — no points balance, no member benefit — feels like a missed opportunity every single time.
- **"*See Weekly Ad For Details"** — that asterisk always makes me pause. The offer looks simple, but then there's a caveat. I know the system, so I'm not spooked, but a newer shopper might wonder what the catch is.
- **"Spring Shop" in early July** — this sale ends in two days. The category name doesn't feel timely. It should feel like a clearance event, not a featured seasonal drop.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to a farmhouse or home-decor lifestyle shot** — show a neutral-toned vignette with shiplap, a galvanized vase, dried pampas grass. That's the Hobby Lobby I drive to every week. The Spring Shop can live in a secondary banner.
- **Add a "This Week's 50% Off Categories" module directly below the hero** — right now I have to guess or tap Weekly Ad. Just list the three or four categories in clean tiles. That's the thing I actually opened this site to find.
- **Surface the rewards/loyalty program above the fold** — even a small "You have 240 points" or a "Rewards Members: extra 10% off" line would make me feel seen as a returning customer.
- **Relabel the end-of-sale banner as a "Final Days" or clearance event** — "Final 2 Days — Spring Shop 50% Off" would actually create urgency and make the July timing feel intentional rather than sloppy.
