# ✨ Make Every Room Feel More Like You With Select Home Decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject promises something the email barely delivers. "Make Every Room Feel More Like You With Select Home Decor" sets up a home-decor email. The top third is that: a "Style Your Home" hero with a video play button and a grid of doormats, sheet sets, curtains, rugs, framed art and clocks. But none of those tiles carries a price or percentage. The actual offers (50% Christmas, 75% Fall Decor, 30% Fabric, 40% Party, Art Supplies and Hobbies) sit well below. None of them is home decor.
- The email is about 4,800px tall, with roughly 20 competing links. The free-shipping banner at $69 is the only offer above the hero. A reader who opens for decor finds a lifestyle pitch with no price, then a long scroll to the discounts. Compared with the 11/17 "50% Off Christmas" send, which had a hero that matched its subject, this one is softer and less honest about where the value is.
- Verdict: pleasant and on-season, but unfocused. It is a catalog dressed up as a theme.

## What's working

- Free shipping on $69+ is in a green banner right under the logo, so the one concrete incentive is seen immediately.
- The "Style Your Home" hero is a warm lifestyle image, and the play button adds a reason to engage.
- The decor grid is consistent and product-led, with clear "In-Store or Online" links.
- The lower offer blocks use big percentage numerals (50%, 75%, 30%, 40%) with alternating image layouts, so they are easy to scan.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 21, 2026" line gives a real deadline.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Select Home Decor" has no offer behind it. No tile in the decor section shows a discount.
- The primary CTA is a flat "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar sitting mid-email, with no verb or product. It reads as generic.
- The email is long, with about 20 destinations, and the hero has no CTA beyond a play icon. Nothing dominates.
- The hero has no button copy. A reader can't tell whether the play button leads to video or to a shop page.
- The real deals (Christmas, Fall Decor) are unrelated to the subject. Fall Decor at 75% is also stale next to Christmas, a mixed seasonal message.
- The deadline appears only at the bottom, and the fine print is tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one decor offer in the hero, such as "Select Home Decor 40% Off", with a "Shop Home Decor" button. If there is no decor deal, change the subject to match the real offers.
- 2. Cut the 3x3 grid down to 4 or 6 tiles and put a price or discount on each.
- 3. Move the weekly ad button directly under the hero and give it a specific verb and noun.
- 4. Put the "Through Saturday" deadline in the preheader and near the top.
- 5. Pick a single season, Christmas or fall, and lead with it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Home Decor Refresh: Shop Before Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free Shipping on $69+ for Your Home Refresh`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, curtains, art and more. Deals end Saturday, 11/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 50% off Christmas and free shipping over $69.`

# Technical Audit: Hobby Lobby, "✨ Make Every Room Feel More Like You With Select Home Decor"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a table-based ESP template with Outlook conditional CSS and a few invalid CSS declarations. The automated QA run found 0 hard issues and 4 warnings. Most of those warnings (headers, authentication) reflect the AgentMail relay's capture limits, not confirmed sender defects. The HTML source was truncated, so link, UTM and personalization checks are only partly possible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and 38 were skipped by design. Footer, social and utility links were deprioritized, and the unsubscribe link was always included. Coverage of the lower-ranked 38 links is unverified.
- Links and images go through the branded tracking domain `link.hobbylobby.com`. For example, the image is `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144223_3417203_155512.gif`. This is consistent with an ESP click and open tracking setup. No evidence of a problem.
- **Plain-text part is 14,534 of 16,716 characters URLs (about 87%).** This is above the 70% warning threshold. It is probably caused by long tracking-redirect URLs in the text alternative. It is a minor spam-scoring and readability risk for plain-text clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144223_3417203_155512.gif` (`https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/…`) has no `alt` attribute. The `/mo/` path and `.gif` extension suggest an open-tracking pixel or spacer. If so, the impact is low, but `alt=""` is still the correct fix. If it is content imagery, it needs real alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag carries `xmlns`, `xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`, `e-locale="en-US"` and `e-is-multilanguage="false"`, but no `lang="en"`. Screen readers rely on `lang`, and `e-locale` is a non-standard attribute that assistive tech ignores.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values for `color` and the border radii. Clients drop these declarations, so the button color and radius are undefined.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo (`verticl-align`), so `vertical-align` is never applied.
- **Mobile breakpoint is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`.** Typical phone widths are 320–430px, so these responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, `.h1`, and so on) are unlikely to trigger on real devices. The email would then render at desktop layout on mobile. The visible portion supports this, but the truncated remainder may contain other breakpoints.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML and VML namespace attributes. Clients tolerate this, so it is low severity.
- **Generic `<title>`** ("Hobby Lobby"). It does not match the subject line, which is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance

It should be checked against raw headers from a direct-to-Gmail or Outlook seed. If the headers are truly absent, the sender misses the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirement.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
