# Sale Alert 🚨🎄 50% Off Christmas!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "50% Off Christmas!" with fire and tree emojis, but the email opens on a "Free Shipping $59+" banner immediately followed by a Fall Essentials-themed hero, then 40%-off tiles for Home Decor and Textiles. Christmas content doesn't show up until roughly a third of the way down this ~30-tile mega-scroll — Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party & Tableware, and Light Sets are all in there, genuinely at 50-75% off, but they're one cluster among Football Party, Classroom Decor, Dorm Essentials, Hobbies, Books, Frames, Art Sale, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing Notions, and Furniture.
- This is the same subject/body mismatch flagged in the 8/4 "Shop Stylish & Chic Rugs!" send and the 8/2 "Football Party" send — a specific, punchy subject line gets used as a hook for what is actually an undifferentiated whole-store catalog dump. It also lands the same week Hobby Lobby already sent a Fall-themed 40% off email (8/7) and a "75% Off" sprawl email (8/6), so a subscriber opening this expecting a focused Christmas sale is getting deja vu wrapped around a bait-and-switch subject.
- Judgment: the Christmas offer inside is legitimate and generous (50% off across six sub-categories), but burying it beneath Fall Essentials and dozens of unrelated tiles undermines the one thing the subject line did right. This scores below the brand's better recent sends (8/5's "Everyday Low Prices" at 7.0) and in line with its worst subject-mismatch offenders.

## What's working

- The 50% off Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, and Crafts tiles are clearly priced and legible with clean product photography.
- No visual bugs — every tile renders cleanly, prices are crisp, no overlapping text or broken images.
- Consistent Hobby Lobby visual system (red price call-outs, script logo, black nav strip) throughout the entire scroll.
- Christmas Light Sets tile calls out specific product detail (LED, battery-operated, indoor/outdoor) — one of the few tiles with real spec info.

## What's weak

- Hero banner is "Fall Essentials," not Christmas — the subject's entire promise is invisible above the fold.
- Christmas content is buried after Home Decor, Textiles, and Mirrors/Lamps tiles instead of leading the email.
- No single primary CTA — this is a 30-tile category directory, not a targeted sale email, so there's no obvious "click here" moment.
- Christmas is diluted by completely unrelated seasonal content in the same send (Football Party 40%, Classroom Decor, Dorm Essentials) — reads like a whole-store clearance, not a Christmas sale.
- No urgency mechanism (no end date, no countdown) despite "Sale Alert" framing in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to match the subject exactly — lead with a Christmas Trees or Christmas Decor image and the "50% Off Christmas" headline in the first screen, not Fall Essentials/free-shipping.
- 2. Move all six Christmas tiles (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party & Tableware, Light Sets) to immediately follow the hero, before Home Decor/Textiles/Football/Classroom content.
- 3. Cut this into two sends: a tight, single-theme "50% Off Christmas" email and a separate general catalog email — stop using a sharp subject line as a wrapper for the full store directory, which is the same misfire as the 8/4 rugs and 8/2 football sends.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") to justify "Sale Alert" urgency in the subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `🎄 50% Off Christmas Trees, Decor & More — This Week Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ornaments, trees, wreaths & tabletop — all 50% off through Sunday.`
