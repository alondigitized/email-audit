# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's standing "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off" positioning send — less a campaign than a policy reminder dressed up as an email. The hero does its job: HOME DECOR 40% is legible at a glance, the lifestyle imagery fills the fold, and the category hierarchy is clear. But the moment the eye moves below the hero, it's the same two-column mega-circular tile grid that ran on July 16, July 19, and July 22. TEXTILES, FLORAL, PAPER CRAFTS, YARN — all at varying percentages, all stacked into the now-familiar grid with no editorial layer connecting them.
- The subject's biggest tell is the word "Always." It's technically honest — Hobby Lobby does hold their home decor at a consistent 40% — but "Always" is also the single word most guaranteed to kill urgency. There is no reason to click today rather than Saturday. After six sends in eight days (the July 21 "40% Off Fall!" send was actually the better version of this same idea), inbox fatigue is a real headwind here.
- This send rates below the July 21 Fall push (7/10), which used the same 40% mechanic but wrapped it in a seasonal moment with a tighter subcategory focus. Strip the "Always" framing, add a weekend deadline, and this email's conversion potential roughly doubles.

## What's working

- Hero reads cleanly — HOME DECOR 40% is visible and unambiguous above the fold, lifestyle imagery supports the category
- Fall seasonal tiles (Fall Floral, Dorm Essentials, Classroom Décor) add relevant back-to-school/fall seasonality
- Category breadth is real: TEXTILES, FURNITURE, FRAMES, YARN, JEWELRY MAKING — a wide shopper has something to click
- No visible render bugs, broken images, or layout breakage in the mega-circular grid
- 40% is a strong discount number; the point-of-entry is easy to parse

## What's weak

- "Always 40% Off" is an urgency-killer baked directly into the subject line — nothing in this email gives a reason to act today
- The asterisk on the hero 40% discount subtly undermines trust and invites fine-print anxiety before the first scroll
- The mega-circular grid below the hero is visually indistinguishable from the July 16 and July 22 sends — six emails in eight days is the cadence that trains subscribers to ignore
- No visible CTA button with action copy anywhere in the send; the unlabeled category tiles are the only clickable surfaces
- Everyday low-price items mixed into the same grid as percentage-off categories muddies the value message

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "Always," add a deadline.** "Home Decor 40% Off — This Weekend" transforms the subject from a standing policy into a time-sensitive event without changing a single product or offer.
- 2. **Collapse the tile grid to 5-6 hero picks.** The July 21 Fall send used three subcategory tiles and earned a 7/10. Twenty-plus tiles dilute attention and make the email feel like a catalog dump, not a curated send.
- 3. **Add one specific hero product.** A single room vignette with a price point (e.g., "Farmhouse Candleholder Set — $14.99") gives the subscriber something concrete to click toward, not just a category.
- 4. **Separate Dorm Essentials from Home Decor.** Dorm and Fall Decor are their own seasonal stories with their own urgency (school starts now). Burying them as tiles in a home decor send wastes the seasonal hook.

## 1. Overview

## Technical Audit — Hobby Lobby "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off"

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-generated HTML blast with Outlook VML/MSO conditional blocks and utility CSS. Two confirmed CSS bugs affect CTA rendering, and missing authentication/unsubscribe headers are the primary deliverability risks.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text body is 88.6% URLs (12,444 / 14,048 chars).** QA flag confirmed. A plain-text part that is nearly all redirect URLs — with no readable prose — is a known spam-filter trigger. Bayesian filters and some corporate gateways score high URL density as bulk spam.

- Evidence: QA finding `[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs`
- Root cause: ESP auto-generated plain-text from the HTML link list without stripping tracking tokens or adding readable fallback copy.

**Tracking pixel missing alt text.** The open-tracking GIF at `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_130657_1913583_138830.gif` has no `alt` attribute. This is the one confirmed image with missing alt per QA.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS typo — `.lhp2` vertical-align:** `verticl-align:middle` (missing `a`). Property is silently ignored in all clients. Affects table cell alignment wherever `.lhp2` is applied to CTA containers.

**Broken border-radius on `.ctacr1`:**
```css
.ctacr1 { border-radius:px; -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; }
```
All three declarations are missing the numeric value (e.g., `4px`). Rendered border-radius falls back to `0` in all clients. The CTA button will render with square corners regardless of design intent.

**Mobile breakpoint is 300px, not the standard 375px.** The sole responsive `@media` rule fires at `max-width:300px`. Virtually no modern smartphone has a viewport below 375px (iPhone SE min is 375px). The layout will not reflow on any real mobile device — subscribers on phones see the desktop/fixed-width layout.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., unfired `{{first_name}}`, `%FNAME%`) are visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent.** QA confirmed the header is not present (or not surfaced through the AgentMail relay). Gmail and Yahoo require this header for bulk senders; its absence suppresses the native unsubscribe UI in both clients and risks complaint-rate escalation.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058).** One-click unsubscribe is not supported. Since February 2024, Gmail and Yahoo enforce one-click unsubscribe for senders exceeding 5,000/day. If volume crosses that threshold this send is non-compliant with both platforms' bulk sender policies.

**Authentication-Results not captured.** SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown — the header was not present in the relay capture. The sending subdomain `create.hobbylobby.com` requires DKIM alignment with the `hobbylobby.com` organizational domain; cannot confirm alignment is in place.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe mechanism: cannot fully assess from the truncated source, but the footer region is typically where these appear — not confirmed absent.
