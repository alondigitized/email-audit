# Deck The Halls With 50% Off Christmas 🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby finally leads with something real: a genuine 50% Off Christmas hero — a woman styling wreaths and garland on a rustic table, a clean "50% OFF Christmas / Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" badge, and a single blue "In-Store Or Online" CTA. This matches 2026-09-15's "Tree-mendous Savings" send almost beat-for-beat, right down to the seasonal wreath styling and the 50% badge placement — so this is now the second near-identical Christmas hero in eight days, not a fresh creative.
- The problem is everything after it. Once you scroll past the Christmas category row (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights), the email reverts to the exact wall-to-wall tile grid this brand has run since late August — Fall, Home Decor, Football Party, Party, Fabric, Art Supplies, then an Online Clearance table. The subject promises Christmas; two-thirds of the email is Fall/Football/fabric. That mismatch is the recurring flaw across this brand's history and it's still unfixed here.
- Net: genuinely the second-best Hobby Lobby send in six weeks (behind or tied with 09-15), but it's a repeat of that same creative with a straight cadence problem, buried under the usual clutter.

## What's working

- Real hero: styled wreath/garland scene with a model, not a flat product photo.
- Clear, legible 50% OFF Christmas badge with one unambiguous blue CTA button.
- Christmas category row directly under the hero (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) keeps the theme going for one more scroll.
- No visible render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" banner is concrete and visible near the top.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Deck The Halls With 50% Off Christmas" but roughly 60% of the email (Fall, Home Decor, Football Party, Party, Fabric, Art Supplies, Clearance) has nothing to do with Christmas.
- This hero is a near-repeat of the 2026-09-15 "Tree-mendous Savings" send — same wreath styling, same 50% badge, same layout — creative fatigue for anyone who opened that one.
- Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials tiles at the very top push the Christmas hero below the fold on mobile.
- "Fall Essentials" banner appears directly under the Christmas category grid, creating jarring seasonal whiplash mid-scroll.
- Wall-to-wall tile grid at the bottom is the same fatigue-inducing pattern flagged in four of the last six audited sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections: Christmas hero + category row, then stop (or follow with one small "also on sale" strip). Move all Fall/Football/Fabric/Art content to a separate send — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Move Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials tiles below the Christmas block, not above it, so the hero is visible without scrolling.
- 3. Vary the hero creative from 09-15's send — different model pose, different product arrangement — so repeat openers don't recognize it as a rerun.
- 4. Drop or relocate the "Fall Essentials" banner; it directly contradicts the subject line's Christmas promise and confuses the scroll sequence.
