# 👋 Say Hello To Pumpkin Season With 40% Off Fall!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This one actually earns its subject line, at least at the top. There's a genuine hero image — a scarecrow, pumpkins, sunflowers, and gourds staged outdoors — with a clean "40% OFF Fall / Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" badge and a single blue "In Store Or Online" CTA. That's a real seasonal moment, tied directly to "Pumpkin Season," and it's the best hero Hobby Lobby has run since the 2026-09-15 Christmas send.
- But it collapses immediately after. Below the hero and a tidy 6-tile fall category grid (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts), the email reverts to the exact wall-to-wall SKU-tile grid this brand has run since late August — 15+ stacked individual product blocks (vinyl, foam board, canvas, paint, bandanas, T-shirts) with no thematic connection to fall or pumpkins at all. It reads like two different emails stitched together.
- Net: a strong open-worthy top third dragged down by the now-familiar bottom two-thirds of undifferentiated inventory. Worth opening, marginal after the first scroll.

## What's working

- Hero image is genuinely seasonal — scarecrow, pumpkins, sunflowers, matches "Pumpkin Season" from the subject.
- Offer is specific and visible: "40% OFF Fall" badge with a clear "In Store Or Online" button.
- The 6-tile fall subcategory grid (Fall Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) is on-theme and well organized.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, pricing displays correctly, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- The bottom ~15 tiles (vinyl, foam board, canvas, paint, dye, bandanas, T-shirts) have nothing to do with fall or pumpkins — same generic SKU dump as the last five sends.
- Visual hierarchy breaks down after the hero; there's no second focal point, just a scroll of individual products with no ranking or grouping.
- "Fall Essentials" banner and hero badge both say "40% off" but the SKU grid below shows flat individual prices with no visible tie-back to that discount.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the same skeleton (hero + tiles + flat SKU wall) as 2026-09-20, 09-18, 09-17, and 09-16 — only the hero image changed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the SKU wall by at least two-thirds — keep only fall-relevant items (candles, florals, crafts) and drop vinyl/foam board/canvas/dye entirely from a fall-themed send; those belong in a general crafts email.
- 2. Add a second mid-email banner reinforcing the 40% fall message before the SKU grid starts, so the discount doesn't feel abandoned after the hero.
- 3. Group the remaining SKUs under visible subheads (e.g., "Fall Crafts," "Fall Decor") instead of one undifferentiated list, so the fall theme carries through the whole email, not just the top.
- 4. Show the discounted price next to "Your Price" on the SKU tiles instead of flat pricing, since the hero promises 40% off but the grid doesn't visibly deliver it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Pumpkin Season Is Here: 40% Off Fall Starts Now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Scarecrows, sunflowers & fall florals — all 40% off in-store or online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your fall refresh: decor, party, floral & crafts at 40% off.`
