# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject sells only one block of it. "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price" is a real offer, and the Home Decor 40% block is in the email. But the email opens on a free-shipping bar and a Christmas Essentials banner. A big "Christmas 50% Off" block follows, so Home Decor doesn't show up until about a fifth of the way down.
- The subject is also identical to the 2026-10-29 send. A subscriber who got that one a week ago will read this as a repeat. "Always 40% off" is a standing price, not news, so there's no reason to open now. The rest is a very long scroll of about 20 category blocks, each with its own giant discount number and no single hero.
- Judgement: competent but undifferentiated. The offers are clear, and the render has no bugs I can see. It is still a catalog dump with a reused subject line, so I'd expect middling engagement.

## What's working

- Every block has a bold, easy-to-read discount number (40%, 50%, 25%, 30%) with the category name beside it.
- The Home Decor 40% block really exists, so the subject is accurate. It has product photos: a clock, canvas art and tapestries.
- The Art Sale and Wearable Art blocks show real price tags (3.42, 9.99, 4.99), which makes the savings concrete.
- A footer line reads "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 7, 2026". That gives a real deadline, and it matches the Christmas and fall seasonality.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- The subject promises Home Decor, but the Christmas banner and the Christmas 50% block take the top of the email.
- The email is a very long scroll with about 20 competing blocks and no single primary CTA. There is no visible button copy, so the eye has nowhere to land.
- It reuses the 2026-10-29 subject verbatim. "Always 40%" also isn't urgent, because it's a standing discount.
- The fine print is dense, with "Does not include…" exclusions under many blocks. That weakens the 50% and 40% claims.
- The free-shipping bar and the red Christmas banner pull attention away from the subject's offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the email with the Home Decor block when the subject is about Home Decor. Better yet, build a short focused send, with one hero and 3–4 product tiles, and keep the full circular for a separate weekly mail.
- 2. Add a real button in each block, such as "Shop Home Decor", instead of leaving the whole image as the click target.
- 3. Vary the subject so the same line isn't sent two weeks running. Tie it to the deadline (ends Saturday) or to a specific product.
- 4. Cut the exclusions down to one short footnote, and move the Christmas and shipping banners below the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Home decor at 40% off, through Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Wall clocks, canvas art & more: 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 50% off Christmas decor and 30% off fabric. Sale ends Nov 7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Frames, textiles, mirrors and more. Prices good through Saturday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard ESP table layout with MSO conditional comments, and the visible source and probed links show no breakages. The QA run flagged a missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header and a missing image `alt`. Most of the remaining warnings come from the relay, and the HTML source is truncated, so much of the body couldn't be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and none are reported as failing. 26 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so their status is unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,233 of 12,776 characters (about 88%) are URLs. This is above the 70% warning threshold and points to long click-tracking URLs on `link.hobbylobby.com`. It is a spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.
- The HTML source is truncated, so I can't verify per-link tracking parameters or redirect chains beyond the probe results.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_143216_555605_152892.gif` has no `alt`. The `/mo/` path may be an open-tracking pixel. If so, add `alt=""` explicitly. If it's visible content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html>` carries `e-locale="en-US"`, which is not a standard attribute. It has no `lang="en"`, which screen readers and clients use.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Invalid CSS in the inline `<style>` block:**
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so the declaration is ignored.
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values (a template variable that never got filled in). Clients drop these declarations, so the radius and colour on that class fall back to defaults.
- **Doctype and namespaces:** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but the root element uses XHTML/VML namespace attributes. Clients tolerate this.
- **Zoom:** `user-scalable=yes` is set, which is good for accessibility.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The empty `color:;` and `px` values in section 3 are CSS template gaps, not recipient merge fields.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** Not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Not found (RFC 8058). One-click unsubscribe may not be supported. The same relay caveat applies, but Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment couldn't be verified. The envelope uses `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`, and links use `link.hobbylobby.com`. Both are subdomains of the brand domain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but alignment is unproven.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing and reports no failure. The footer is in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Destination URLs and UTM parameters sit behind `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects or in the truncated portion. The probe reported no broken CTAs, but it gives no evidence on UTM presence or landing-page match for the "40% off marked price" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to the GIF: `alt=""` if it's a tracking pixel, descriptive text otherwise.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and fix the `verticl-align` typo.
3. Fix the `.ctacr1` template output (`color:;` and `px` radius) so those values are populated or removed.
4. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are sent from the origin. Check the raw message at the sender, because the relay may have dropped them.
5. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC through the sender's own headers, since the relay doesn't expose them.
6. Shorten the tracking URLs or give the plain-text part more readable copy, to bring URL density below 70%.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer compliance elements, UTM parameters, and the 26 skipped links.
