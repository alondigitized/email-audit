# Sale Alert 🚨 50% Off Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells "50% Off Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts," but the first screen of the email is Christmas. A "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar, a "Christmas Essentials" banner and a big "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block come before anything fall. The fall block does exist: "Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" with a 50% badge. But it sits roughly a quarter of the way down, below Christmas, Home Decor, Textiles and Mirrors & Lamps.
- This is the same mismatch as 10/15 ("Select Home Decor" opening on Christmas) and 10/11 ("Spice Up Your Fall" opening on Christmas). Hobby Lobby sends one department-store template and rewrites the subject line. The email below the fold is a long wall of about 15 department blocks. They are mostly 40% off, with a few 50%, 30% and 99¢ deals. It is clear and honest about the discounts. But nothing leads, and there is no single button to click.
- My judgment: it is a competent catalog and a weak campaign email. The subject makes a fall promise that the layout doesn't keep. The 50% claim is real, but the reader has to scroll to find it.

## What's working

- The 50% claim in the subject is backed by a visible 50% badge on the fall decor, party, floral and crafts block.
- Percent-off numbers are large and consistent, so the discount scans quickly even in a very long email.
- The footer shows a credible deadline ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 24") and a free-shipping bar sits at the top.
- Product photography is clean, and I see no broken images or overlapping text.
- The email is seasonally current. Christmas, fall, football party and Halloween-adjacent items all match mid-October.

## What's weak

- The fall block that the subject promises is buried below the Christmas, Home Decor and Textiles blocks.
- The email is a very long stack of about 15 category blocks with no hero and no primary button. Nothing tells the eye where to go first.
- Fine print under many blocks ("Does not include seasonal or Cricut…", "Categories listed") weakens the headline 50%. Fall and Christmas are called out as separate exclusions, which is confusing.
- Cadence is heavy. This is the 5th or 6th promo in about a week, and the 10/16 and 10/15 emails also opened on Christmas.
- The deadline is only in the footer, so the subject's "Sale Alert" urgency has no support up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the fall block the hero. Put "50% Off Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" with a "Shop Fall Now" button first, and move Christmas below it. This is the single biggest lift, because the open-to-click path would match the subject.
- 2. Add a deadline line next to the hero, such as "Ends Saturday 10/24," instead of only in the footer.
- 3. Cut the department wall to the 4-5 best offers (fall, Christmas, football party, art sale, fabric). Fewer tiles would help the reader decide.
- 4. Add an actual button to each block, with specific copy like "Shop Fall Florals."
- 5. Give the exclusions their own short line instead of repeating them under every block.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
