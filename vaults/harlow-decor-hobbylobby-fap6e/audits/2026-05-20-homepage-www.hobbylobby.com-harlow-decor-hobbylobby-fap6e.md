# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is classic Hobby Lobby — no frills, no nonsense, straight to the sale. The big 50% off The Spring Shop is the whole point of the page, and honestly I already knew this week's rotating category before I even opened the app. What I'm not getting is any pull toward my actual aisles — the hero is a flat-lay of picnic food and tableware, which has nothing to do with why I'm here (farmhouse decor, faux florals, seasonal arrangements). It feels like the brand knows its regulars will convert anyway, so it didn't bother dressing the window for us.

## What's working

- **The 50% off callout** — that big, bold number is exactly what I came for. I scan for the rotating category first thing every week and this gives it to me in under two seconds.
- **Sale deadline copy** ("Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026") — I'm skeptical of digital-only promos, so seeing "In-Store" explicitly called out makes me trust this. That matters.
- **Free shipping threshold** ($59 or more) — visible right at the top. Attainable in one decor order, and I don't have to hunt for it.
- **Weekly Ad button** — surfaced right below the logo, prominent placement. That's my first stop every time and they know it.
- **Clean, fast render** — page loaded quickly with no jank, no overlapping text. On a phone that's not nothing.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is completely off-target for me** — tomatoes, a wicker tray, and patriotic-looking dishes. That's a summer entertaining spread, not a decor shot. I don't come to Hobby Lobby for tabletop picnic styling; I come for galvanized buckets, faux eucalyptus, and tiered trays.
- **"The Spring Shop" is a vague container** — I can't tell if faux florals and farmhouse accents are actually in the sale or if it's all coastal/patriotic tableware. The lack of category specificity means I have to tap through just to find out.
- **No loyalty hook anywhere** — I've been shopping here weekly for years. There's no rewards callout, no account greeting, nothing that says "we recognize you." That feels like a miss.
- **Zero decor imagery** — not a single wreath, stem, vase, or shiplap-adjacent product in frame. My category is invisible above the fold.
- **"See Weekly Ad For Details"** fine print undercuts the offer — it makes me wonder what's excluded before I've even tapped Shop Now.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the picnic flat-lay for a seasonal decor shot** — a spring wreath, a faux floral arrangement in a galvanized vase, or a farmhouse tablescape. That's the customer spending real money in the Spring Shop.
- **Add a category preview strip** — two or three thumbnail tiles under the hero ("Faux Florals · Frames & Mirrors · Seasonal Décor") so I know the sale includes what I'm after before I tap anything.
- **Surface the rewards/loyalty program above the fold** — even a one-line "Earn points on every purchase" badge would acknowledge that I'm a repeat customer, not a first-time visitor.
- **Make the deadline more urgent visually** — "3 days left" or a simple countdown chip next to the May 23 date would push me from browsing to buying without being manipulative.
