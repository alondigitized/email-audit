# Open Soon - Plan a Creative Escape!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from Hobby Lobby's usual sprawling-discount-tile format, and it's a clean, honest send — a new-store announcement for their Ontario, CA location, opening soon. There's no discount claim here at all, no "40% off" or "75% off" headline, which is a first compared to every prior Hobby Lobby email in the brand history. The subject "Open Soon - Plan a Creative Escape!" actually matches what's inside: an opening announcement with an address, a "Live a Creative Life!" badge, and category imagery.
- The problem is that this is a hyper-local, single-store announcement blasted as a generic promotional email with zero targeting cues visible in the render — there's no indication this is filtered to recipients near Ontario, CA, and for anyone outside that market this is entirely irrelevant. It also completely lacks a price incentive or urgency, which will hurt clicks regardless of how well-composed the layout is.
- Judgement: well-executed for what it is, but it's the wrong email for most inboxes unless it's geo-targeted — and the render gives no evidence it was.

## What's working

- No render bugs — all four product images loaded cleanly, text is crisp, and the badge graphic renders sharp.
- Clear informational hierarchy: store name, address, and "Opening Soon" headline are the first things the eye hits.
- Subject line promise ("Open Soon - Plan a Creative Escape!") is fully honest and delivered inside the email — a rarity for this brand per the history log.
- Category list (Home Decor, Floral & Wedding, Yarn & Needle Art, etc.) gives a concrete preview of what the new store carries.
- "Click here to take a tour" gives a clear secondary CTA for people curious about the format.

## What's weak

- No visible offer or incentive anywhere — no opening-day discount, no coupon, nothing that would drive a store visit beyond curiosity.
- No date given for the opening — "Opening Soon" with no timeframe undercuts urgency entirely.
- No obvious geo-targeting signal in the render; a recipient outside Southern California has zero reason to open or click this.
- Primary CTA is ambiguous — there's no button at all, just a text link ("Click here") buried mid-email, easy to miss on a skim.
- The four product photos are generic lifestyle shots (frames, plates, sunflowers) that could be lifted from any Hobby Lobby send — they don't specifically evoke "new store opening."

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an opening-day incentive — a "Join us opening week for an exclusive discount" line would give this email a reason to convert, not just inform.
- 2. State a concrete opening date or window ("Opens August 23") — "Soon" with no date kills urgency.
- 3. Add a real CTA button (e.g., "Get Directions" or "Save the Date") near the top, not just a text link buried below the product grid.
- 4. Confirm/tighten geo-targeting so this only reaches recipients near Ontario, CA — for everyone else this is dead weight that drags down engagement metrics.
