## What's working

- The hero photo itself — the gilt frames leaning on the mantle, the little sconces, the cozy throw on the chair — that's my exact aesthetic, no guessing required.
- "40% OFF Home Decor" in giant type with "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" underneath is plain and honest — I know that pricing structure already, so nothing here feels like a gotcha.
- The date stamp "Discounts Valid Online & In-Store through August 15, 2026" — I appreciate that they spell out it works in-store too, since that's how I actually shop.
- Free shipping banner up top ($59 or more) is easy to spot even though I probably won't use it — I'd rather just swing by the store.

## What's weak

- No people anywhere in that hero shot — just a styled vignette. Fine for the mood, but it doesn't tell me who this décor is "for."
- "Sign up and save" up top is vague — save what, exactly? No rewards balance, no percentage, nothing that tells me it's worth tapping.
- Nothing below the top offer hints at what's new in decor or florals — I'd have to scroll blind to find anything else.
- No reviews, no "best seller" tags, nothing that reassures me a specific frame or piece is worth grabbing.

## Recommendations

- Add a small "40% off ends Sat 8/15 — plan your store trip" note near the Shop Now button, since I mentally track these windows around my weekly visit.
- Put a specific dollar or percentage next to "Sign up and save" instead of leaving it vague.
- Add a thumbnail strip of a few actual Home Decor pieces (a faux floral arrangement, a framed print) right under the hero so I have something concrete to tap before I even hit the category page.
- Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Home Decor" so the button itself tells me exactly where it's taking me.
