# Festive Floral Must-Haves – 50% Off Christmas
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the best structure Hobby Lobby has run in a week. It has a real 50% Off Christmas hero: a large flat-lay of ribbon, plaid and evergreen, with a bold "50% OFF" badge. That's the same play that scored well on 9/22 ("Deck The Halls With 50% Off Christmas"), and it's a big step up from the 9/24 and 9/27 tile walls. Below the hero, though, the email drifts back into the usual multi-offer pile: five Christmas sub-tiles, a Fall Essentials banner, then five 30–40% blocks and a clearance strip.
- The subject promises "Festive Floral Must-Haves". The body doesn't deliver that. Floral is one small wreath tile labelled "Christmas Floral" plus a 40% Floral block far down the page. The hero is generic Christmas décor, not florals. It's a subject and creative mismatch, and it undercuts an otherwise solid send.
- Judgement: a decent offer with a clear hero, weakened by a mismatched subject, seasonal whiplash (Christmas at 50% next to Fall at 40%) and a page that runs about 4,400px tall. It's a small improvement over the recent tile-wall sends, but not a targeted one.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF Christmas hero is large and high-contrast, with a rich holiday image. The eye lands on it first.
- The single blue "In-Store Or Online" button under the hero is clear and visible.
- The five Christmas sub-tiles (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) give useful category paths for holiday shoppers.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 3, 2026" line gives a concrete deadline.
- Consistent 40% "Always" and 30% Yarn callouts are easy to scan, and the brand voice and header stay familiar.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Festive Floral Must-Haves", but the hero shows no florals. Only a small "Christmas Floral" tile matches.
- The top of the email is cluttered: a Free Shipping $59 banner, a Kinkeeping strip, and three Essentials tiles (Kitchen, Bath, Home) all sit above the Christmas hero. The hero is pushed below a typical mobile first screen.
- The offer is buried in qualifiers: "*See Weekly Ad For Details", "Select Categories", "Marked Price". The 50% has no product proof.
- Christmas 50% and Fall 40% compete in the same send, so the seasonal message is confused. The tail (Paper Crafts, Yarn, Party, Clearance, footer link lists) adds length with no focus.
- Every CTA reads "In-Store Or Online" rather than a verb-and-noun action, and there's a full-width "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" bar that competes with the hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the subject and hero. Either lead with a floral-specific hero (wreaths, garlands, stems at 50% off) or change the subject to what the hero shows.
- 2. Cut everything above the hero. Move the shipping banner into a slim bar and drop the Kinkeeping strip and the three Essentials tiles.
- 3. Drop the Fall block or move it to a separate send. Christmas at 50% is the story, and Fall at 40% dilutes it.
- 4. Replace "In-Store Or Online ▸" with specific CTAs like "Shop Christmas Floral" and "Shop 50% Off Wreaths".
- 5. Cap the email at three or four blocks: hero, Christmas sub-tiles, clearance, footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Christmas Florals, Wreaths & Garland`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Christmas Floral Is 50% Off This Week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wreaths, garland, stems and ribbon, all half off through Oct 3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on orders $59+. Deals end Saturday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with MSO conditional CSS, and the QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). The truncated source shows a few CSS defects and a missing `lang` attribute. Header-level compliance and authentication checks are unverifiable because the AgentMail relay did not expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links. 12 material links were probed with no failures reported, and 34 lower-priority links (footer, social) were skipped, so their status is unverified.
- The plain-text part is 85% URLs (13,010 of 15,220 chars). This points to long redirect-wrapped tracking links on `link.hobbylobby.com` and is a minor spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- Tracking and sending domains (`link.hobbylobby.com`, `create.hobbylobby.com`) share the parent domain, so there is no alignment concern.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns=… e-locale="en-US" e-is-multilanguage="false">` has no `lang` attribute. `e-locale` is not a standard attribute, so screen readers get no language hint.
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_138642_3237672_147641.gif` has no `alt`. The `/mo/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, though the truncated source doesn't confirm it. If it is decorative or a pixel, `alt=""` is still recommended.
- **Invalid CSS in `.ctacr1`:**
  - `color:;`
  - `-moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;`
  - These are empty values, likely unresolved template variables. Clients drop them, so any CTA using this class won't get the intended radius or text color.
- **Typo:** `.lhp2` uses `verticl-align:middle`, which is ignored.
- **Breakpoint:** the visible responsive block is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is far below typical phone widths (about 320–430px), so those rules won't fire on most devices. The source is truncated, so other breakpoints may exist further down.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is combined with XHTML/VML `xmlns` attributes. This is common in email and usually harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The empty `.ctacr1` values in section 3 are the only sign of template variables that weren't populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says AgentMail may not relay them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven absence. If the headers are truly missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (which require one-click unsubscribe) would be violated.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** the QA probe always includes one, and no failure was reported.
- **Physical address:** not visible in the truncated portion, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. The truncated source doesn't show CTA hrefs, and the QA data has no landing-page or UTM results. Nothing is flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify the raw headers from the original sender (not the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
2. Fix the `.ctacr1` template output so it has real values for color and border-radius. Correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Add `alt=""` (decorative) or a descriptive `alt` to the `.gif` at `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_…gif`.
4. Shorten the plain-text part, for example by adding real text content or using shorter link wrappers, to reduce the URL ratio.
5. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML to check the responsive breakpoints, CTA hrefs and UTMs, and the footer address and unsubscribe markup.
