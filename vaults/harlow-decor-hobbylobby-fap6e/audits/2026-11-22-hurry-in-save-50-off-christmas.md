# Hurry In & Save 🎄 50% Off Christmas!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is honest. "Hurry In & Save 🎄 50% Off Christmas!" matches the top of the render, which opens with a "Christmas Essentials" banner and a big "CHRISTMAS 50% Categories Listed" block. After that it turns into a very long scroll, about 9,950px. It runs through Home Decor 40%, Textiles 40%, Mirrors & Lamps 50%, Fall Decor 75%, Party 40%, Floral 40%, Hobbies 40%, Crafts 40%, Frames 40% and more. Each block has its own percentage.
- The 50% Christmas offer leads, but it competes with a dozen other percentages. This is the third near-identical circular in a row. It looks like the 11-17 "'Tis The Season To Save With 50% Off Christmas 🎄" send, and the 11-19 "Up To 75% Off" send used the same layout. "Hurry In" is generic urgency with no deadline in the subject. The only date is a small "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28" line at the very bottom.
- Judgement: a competent, on-season circular, but undifferentiated and overlong. The render was downscaled to about 137px wide, so I could not read the fine print or confirm button copy.

## What's working

- The subject matches the hero, which is a large Christmas 50% block at the top.
- A "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" strip sits above the hero and adds a second concrete incentive.
- The 🎄 emoji fits the season, and the subject is not shouting.
- Product photography is clean and consistent, and each category block has a bold percentage as its focal point.
- I saw no broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage in the render.

## What's weak

- At least a dozen competing percentages (25%, 30%, 40%, 50%, 75%, 99¢) dilute the 50% Christmas message.
- There is no obvious primary CTA button. Blocks read as category tiles, and I could not see verb-led button copy.
- The real deadline ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 28") is a tiny line at the bottom. "Hurry In" gives no reason to hurry.
- Repeated "Categories Listed" and "Does not include…" lines add exclusion language, and the fine print is hard to read.
- The scroll is far too long, and the Christmas items shrink to a 25% light-sets callout in the first block.
- It is the third near-identical circular in a week (11-17, 11-19, 11-20), so there is cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, with 3-4 product tiles and one clear button such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas". Link the rest through a "More deals" grid. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Move the real end date into the top banner, for example "50% off Christmas ends Sat 11/28".
- 3. Put specific products in the hero, such as trees, ornaments or garland, and link each to its own page.
- 4. Reduce the exclusion language and move any legal text under a clear "See details" link.
- 5. Vary the send. Don't run the same circular layout on consecutive days.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Trees, garland & ornaments: half off through 11/28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on $69+. Sale ends Saturday, November 28.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Christmas decor, crafts & party supplies are 50% off. See what's in.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware layout with several CSS defects, no `lang` attribute, and a heavily tracked, URL-dense plain-text part. The QA run (50% pass, 0 failures, 4 warnings) found no confirmed hard failures. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 material links were probed and 30 were skipped. No probed link was reported broken, but the 30 skipped links, mostly footer and social, are unverified.
- **The plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,890 of 13,451 characters (88%) are URLs, against the 70% threshold. This points to long redirect-wrapped tracking links on `link.hobbylobby.com`. It risks spam-filter scoring and gives plain-text readers a poor experience. The plain-text part is the evidence here. The HTML source was truncated before the body links.
- **The tracked image has no alt text.** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144506_1663774_155173.gif` is flagged by QA. The `/mo/` path and GIF format suggest an open-tracking pixel. If it is a 1×1 pixel, the missing alt is low impact, but it should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`. Its dimensions couldn't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html>` tag has `xmlns`, `xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`, `e-locale="en-US"` and `e-is-multilanguage="false"`, but no `lang="en"`. `e-locale` is a non-standard attribute that assistive tech ignores.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty and unit-only values. The declarations are dropped, so that CTA gets no text color or radius. Its `color` falls back to inherited or link defaults.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so the property is ignored.
- **The only responsive breakpoint seen is very narrow.** The first media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Typical phones are 360–430px wide, so those mobile rules won't fire on them. The source is truncated, so other breakpoints may exist further down. Verify before treating this as confirmed.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional while the root tag uses XHTML and VML namespaces. This is harmless in most clients.
- **Generic `<title>`.** The title is only "Hobby Lobby". This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN).
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

The QA tool itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Treat them as unverified, not confirmed defects. QA states an unsubscribe link was always probed and reported no failure, so there is no confirmed body-level unsubscribe problem. The physical postal address couldn't be checked in the truncated source.

The envelope is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`, a subdomain separate from the likely primary domain. Its SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment needs a DNS check.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. The truncated HTML and the probe results include no destination URLs or UTM parameters. Links route through `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects, so final landing URLs and parameter preservation weren't validated. The QA output doesn't report whether the "50% Off Christmas" CTAs land on a matching promo page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
2. Fix the CSS: give `.ctacr1` a real `color` and `border-radius` value (for example `3px`), and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
3. Confirm the mobile breakpoint. Raise it to about 480–600px if `300px` is the only one.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF, or add `width="1" height="1"` and `role="presentation"` if it is a pixel.
5. Shorten the plain-text part by using fewer or shorter tracking URLs, or by trimming link-heavy blocks.
6. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox. This confirms `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) without relay stripping.
7. Re-run the probe with the 30 skipped links included, and record each CTA's final URL, UTM parameters and landing-page promo match.
