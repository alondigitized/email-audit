# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a long weekly-circular email dressed up with a home decor subject line. The subject promises "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price." But the first screen is a free-shipping bar and a "Christmas Essentials" banner. Below them is a big "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block, and Home Decor only appears second. It's the same mismatch as the 10/15 "Elevate Your Space With Select Home Decor!" send and the 10/18 "Sale Alert" send, which also opened on Christmas.
- Past the top, the email is a catalog: Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps, Fall, Football Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Books, Frames, Art, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing, Custom Frames and Furniture. Almost every block carries a different percentage (25%, 30%, 40%, 50%, $0.99). The home decor offer therefore gets lost in the noise.
- Verdict: the deal is real and the render is clean, but nothing in the email is built to get a click. It reads as a store flyer, not a campaign.

## What's working

- The "Always 40% off the marked price" framing is a clear, concrete offer, and the subject names it directly.
- The Free Shipping threshold ($59 or more) is visible at the very top.
- Real product photos and price tiles, such as the $9.99 vinyl pack and the $4.99 paper craft items, make the deals tangible.
- The Christmas and Fall blocks match the season, as do the football party tile and the "Sale Ends Thursday, October 22" footer.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's hero (Home Decor) is not the first thing the eye lands on. Christmas 50% takes that spot.
- There are about 15 competing category blocks and no single dominant button. The CTA is "Categories Listed" text, not a clear button.
- The sale end date sits in the footer. The email gives almost no urgency up top.
- The fine print is buried and heavy ("Does not include Seasonal Department or Items Marked 'Your Price'"). The footer disclaimer is tiny and dense, and it undercuts how honest the "always 40% off" claim feels.
- The email is a very long scroll, and the subject doesn't mention any of the other categories it covers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put Home Decor first. Open with the 40% block, or change the subject to match whatever actually leads the email. Fixing the subject and hero mismatch would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the circular down to 3–5 blocks, with one dominant button such as "Shop Home Decor 40% Off."
- 3. Move the sale end date ("Ends Thursday") up into the hero area.
- 4. Shorten and simplify the exclusion language near each offer.
- 5. Space out the sends. This is the 7th email in about 9 days, and several are near-identical sales.
