# Bring Cozy Vibes – 40% Off Fall!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's first Fall push of the season, and the hero earns its keep: a warm wreath backdrop, a bold "40% Fall" badge, and three subcategory tiles (Fall Party, Fall Candles, Fall Crafty) that actually advance the seasonal story. For a craft retailer, going Fall in late July isn't early — it's table stakes, and the hero execution is the cleanest Hobby Lobby has sent since the July 14 "66% Off The Spring Shop" send.
- The problem arrives the moment you scroll. Everything below the CTA collapses back into the same stacked mega-circular Hobby Lobby has sent on July 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 19 — including, critically, a "66% Items Labeled The Spring Shop" banner and a "66% Summer Toys" row sitting directly inside an email with "Fall" in the subject line. The subscriber who opened for cozy autumn vibes now sees spring garden imagery and summer toy photography. It reads like someone dropped the wrong discount grid below a new hero.
- The judgement: a good subject line wasted by a body that undercuts its own seasonal premise and then falls back on the same fatigued circular format this list has received six times in nine days.

## What's working

- **Fall hero is immediately legible** — warm wreath imagery, a clear "40%" badge, and fall-specific subcategory tiles above the fold.
- **Subject line is the brand's best in this cycle** — "Bring Cozy Vibes – 40% Off Fall!" has an emotional hook ("cozy vibes") paired with a concrete offer, and clocks in at 32 characters for clean mobile rendering.
- **Free shipping threshold is front-and-center** — "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" at the top is a real secondary incentive, especially for seasonal decorators loading up a cart.
- **Multiple offer depth** — 40% fall decor, 30% all yarn, 75% online clearance give a deal-oriented subscriber multiple reasons to click through.

## What's weak

- **Spring Shop and Summer Toys in a Fall email** — "66% Items Labeled The Spring Shop" and "66% Summer Toys" appear in-body directly beneath the Fall hero. This is the single biggest damage to the send.
- **Same mega-circular playbook again** — This is the seventh email in nine days following the identical stacked-badge grid format. Cadence fatigue is real and the creative system isn't hiding it.
- **No deadline anywhere** — "40% Off Fall" with no end date removes urgency; a subscriber can always come back tomorrow.
- **Preheader is the free shipping banner** — "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" as the inbox preview doesn't extend the fall story; it's a permanent benefit being used as a preheader slot.
- **No single hero product** — The fall section shows category tiles, not one specific item. A cozy $24.99 wreath at 40% off would create more desire than a "Fall Candles" badge.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the Spring Shop and Summer Toys tiles from this send entirely.** Include them in a separate clearance-focused email on a different day. Their presence in this email actively contradicts the Fall positioning and confuses the subscriber's mental model of why they opened.
- 2. **Add a credible deadline to the hero.** "40% Off Fall — Through Sunday" or "40% Off Fall Decor This Week Only" creates urgency without feeling manufactured.
- 3. **Feature one specific fall product in the hero.** A single fall wreath or lantern at $X.XX, now 40% off, beats a category label. Specificity builds desire.
- 4. **Rewrite the preheader** to extend the fall narrative instead of restating the shipping threshold:
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wreaths, lanterns, and fall candles — all 40% off this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop fall before it sells out — 40% off starts now.`
- 5. **Reduce send frequency.** Six emails in nine days means even a clean send like this one lands in a fatigued inbox. Pull back to 3–4 per week with more differentiated creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Fall Decor — Wreaths, Candles & More`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Fall Haul: 40% Off Before September`
