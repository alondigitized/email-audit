# See Our Christmas Commercial 🎄
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-film email, and it is a different kind of send from Hobby Lobby's usual circular. Over the past week the brand sent 50%, 75% and 40% off blasts. This one is a single hero, a play button and no offer. The image is warm and well made. A young man hangs ornaments on a lit tree, and the "Christmas Is What You Make It" headline fits a craft retailer's seasonal pitch. For a subscriber who is tired of the daily percent-off barrage, it is a pleasant change.
- Commercially it is thin. There is no offer, no product, no deadline and no shop path other than the nav bar. The only action is a play button over the photo. The copy "View Our Commercial" is small, light and lies over a busy part of the image. The emotional framing is good, but nothing here moves a shopper toward a purchase. The "Weekly Ad" and "Shop" links in the top nav are the only commercial exits, and they're easy to miss.
- Verdict: a decent brand-mood send, weak as a conversion email. It works as a one-off tonal break after the Nov 14 Operation Christmas Child cause email and the Nov 15 "75% Off" circular. It should not carry a send slot on its own.

## What's working

- The hero image is warm, seasonal and emotionally credible, with a real person decorating a real tree.
- The "Christmas Is What You Make It" headline is large, high-contrast and readable at a glance.
- The layout is clean and short. The hero is the first thing the eye lands on, with no clutter.
- The blue play button is clear and universally understood as a video link.
- The nav bar (Weekly Ad, Stores, Shop, DIY) and the footer links are consistent and tidy.
- This is a tonal break from the discount-heavy sends earlier in the week (Nov 10-15).

## What's weak

- There is no offer at all. No percent off, no coupon, no member perk, so there is nothing to act on.
- The "View Our Commercial" line is thin, small and sits over a blurry, bright area, so it is the weakest text in the hero.
- The play button is the only CTA, and it asks for a video view rather than a shopping action.
- No products are shown. A craft retailer's tree photo could have shown the ornaments, garlands or supplies.
- The hero is tall and mostly image. Nothing below the fold sends the reader into the Christmas assortment.
- The legal and disclaimer block is dense and sits right under the footer. It reads as heavy for such a light email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a shopping path under the hero. Put one button, "Shop Christmas Trim & Ornaments", plus a single price or percent claim, so the mood converts. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make the video CTA a real button with a label, for example "Watch the Commercial (:30)". Put it on a solid contrasting pill instead of thin text over the photo.
- 3. Show three or four products tied to the commercial, such as the ornaments, lights and garland on that tree, each linked to its category page.
- 4. Add a time-bound hook, such as a weekend or Black Friday preview, so there is a reason to click now.
- 5. Cut the disclaimer to what's legally required and move the rest to the online version.
