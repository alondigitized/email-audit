# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby feels familiar the moment I open it — that red banner, the weekly ad button right up front, the big fat sale percentage. It's pushing a Spring Shop event tied to what looks like a summer entertaining tablescape, and the 50% off number is doing a lot of the heavy lifting. It reads like a craft-and-décor store I already know well, not like it's trying too hard to impress me, which I appreciate.

## What's working

- **The 50% off headline** is enormous and unmissable — I know immediately there's a real deal happening, and the Spring Shop category is directly in my wheelhouse even if the photo doesn't show it
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026"** — I'm a skeptic of digital-only promos, so seeing that it works in-store right below the button earns genuine trust from me
- **Free shipping on $59+** in the top banner is a number I can actually hit in one visit; it doesn't feel out of reach for a decor run
- **The Weekly Ad button** front and center is a nod to loyal customers like me who plan their trips around the rotating 50%-off categories — it says "we know how you shop here"
- **Clean, no-friction load** — no pop-up modal demanding my email before I can see anything, which I genuinely appreciate

## What's weak

- **Hero image is wrong for me:** Blue fish plates, red americana items, and a summer picnic flatlay reads "coastal hostess," not farmhouse or faux floral buyer. This could easily have shown seasonal wreaths, greenery arrangements, or galvanized tubs of faux blooms.
- **"*See Weekly Ad For Details"** is the classic Hobby Lobby asterisk game — I know the drill, but it's still a small trust ding every time I see it attached to a big headline number
- **No loyalty/rewards hook anywhere visible** — I've been coming in weekly for a decade; there's no "welcome back" or rewards balance in sight, which makes this feel like a flyer, not a relationship
- **"Shop Now" is generic copy** — for a store where I buy specific things (decor, florals, frames), "Shop Spring Decor" or "Shop Faux Florals" would have pointed me directly where I want to go

## Recommendations

- **Swap the picnic tablescape for a farmhouse spring vignette**: a faux floral centerpiece on a shiplap table, galvanized buckets, a spring wreath — this is Hobby Lobby's actual bestselling decor aesthetic and it would resonate immediately with buyers like me
- **Add a returning-customer layer**: a small "Your Rewards" balance chip or "Welcome back — see your personalized ad" line near the Weekly Ad button would do more work per pixel than anything else on this screen
- **Sharpen the CTA copy** from "Shop Now" to "Shop Spring Décor" or "Shop The Spring Shop" — one tap to the category I care about, no ambiguity
- **Move the in-store callout higher**: "Valid Online & In-Store" should be part of the hero offer block, not below the button in small gray type — for skeptics like me, that's the deciding line
