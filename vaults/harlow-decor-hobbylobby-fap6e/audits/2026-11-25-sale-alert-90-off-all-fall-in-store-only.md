# Sale Alert 🚨 90% Off All Fall! In-Store Only
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance blast: "90% OFF All Fall" over a full-bleed pumpkin-and-garland photo. The offer is the strongest part. The number is huge and sits in the top right of the hero, so it is the first thing you see. The email is a single image with one pill button, and nothing competes with the offer.
- It also has a timing problem. The date is Nov 25, the day before Thanksgiving week's rush, and the brand has spent the past week on "50% Off Christmas" (Nov 24, Nov 22) and "Select Home Decor" (Nov 25, Nov 20). A 90%-off fall clearance reads as an end-of-season dump, and a subscriber who has been shown Christmas all week will see it that way. The "*See Weekly Ad For Details" line sits directly under the headline, so the 90% reads as conditional on first glance. The pill button says "In-Store Only," which is a restriction rather than an action, and it isn't clear that it is clickable.
- Judgement: a clear, eye-catching clearance email that undercuts itself with asterisks, a weak CTA, and no product or location specifics. It's about average for this brand's weekly circular sends, with a 90% hook the others don't have.

## What's working

- The "90% OFF" numeral is very large and high contrast on a light area of the photo, so it reads instantly.
- The autumn imagery (pumpkins, garland, lanterns) matches "All Fall" and is a good-looking hero.
- The layout is clean, with one hero and one button, so the eye has a single path.
- The "Sale Alert 🚨" subject framing and the "In-Store Only" scope tell the reader up front that this is a limited, physical-store deal.
- The Weekly Ad / Stores / Shop / DIY nav row gives the reader easy routes to the ad and to a store.

## What's weak

- The CTA pill says "In-Store Only," which is a restriction and not a verb. It doesn't say what happens when you tap it.
- "*See Weekly Ad For Details" under the headline hedges the 90% claim immediately, and the footer's "marked prices reflect general U.S. market value" fine print adds more doubt.
- "All Fall" is vague. There are no named categories, no example price, and no product shown with a price or a "was/now."
- There is no deadline and no end date, so "Sale Alert" urgency is asserted and not shown.
- The black "90" is placed over busy leaves and the "See Weekly Ad" text sits on foliage. Legibility holds up, but the lower text is slightly muddy against the garland.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA a real action, such as "Find Your Store" or "See Fall Deals," and add a visible end date ("Ends Sunday") next to it. This is the single change that would lift clicks most.
- 2. Say what 90% applies to: "90% Off Fall Decor, Florals & Pumpkins," and cut or shrink the asterisk line.
- 3. Add a row of 3 to 4 priced fall items below the hero so the reader sees real products.
- 4. Lead with the nearest store or a store-locator button, since the deal is in-store only.
- 5. Fix the cadence: don't send fall clearance in the same week as the 50% Christmas push, or tie the two together ("Make room for Christmas").
- **Subject Alt A:** `90% Off Fall Decor — This Week In Stores`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Clearance: 90% Off Ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pumpkins, garlands & lanterns, marked way down at your Hobby Lobby.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clear out fall and make room for Christmas. Find your store.`
