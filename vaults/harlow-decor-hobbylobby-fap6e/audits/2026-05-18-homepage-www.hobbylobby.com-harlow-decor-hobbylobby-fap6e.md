# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I've been walking into Hobby Lobby every weekend for fifteen years, and the first thing I look for when I land on their site is: what's 50% off this week? They answered that question immediately, which is exactly right. This feels like a seasonal-decor brand in full spring mode — the offer is front-and-center, the sale end date is right there, and they've remembered to call out that it works in-store, not just online. What I can't tell yet is whether "The Spring Shop" means my stuff — wreath forms, faux stems, farmhouse signs — or if it's all tabletop and cookware.

## What's working

- **The 50% OFF callout** is doing exactly what it should — I spotted it before I finished loading the page. As someone who has this discount schedule memorized, that confirmation is like a green light.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026"** — this tiny line is huge for me. I'm skeptical of digital-only deals; seeing "In-Store" in plain text means I can plan my Saturday trip with confidence.
- **The free shipping banner** ("$59 Or More") sits right at the top. I regularly order stems and picks in bulk; $59 is a realistic cart for me and I appreciate seeing the threshold upfront without hunting for it.
- **The "Weekly Ad" button** is front-row, above the hero. That's the first thing I tap every time, and they made it a primary nav element — smart placement.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is tablescape, not decor** — I see plates with fish motifs, tomatoes, and a wicker bread basket. That's a coastal grandmother situation, not a farmhouse decor moment. I have no idea if The Spring Shop means wreaths and botanicals or serving ware.
- **No category preview below the hero** — even one row of thumbnails (Floral, Frames, Home Decor, Garden) would tell me immediately whether to stay or go.
- **No loyalty callout whatsoever** — I've been a customer for years and there's no "Welcome back" hook, no rewards balance, nothing that acknowledges I'm a repeat visitor.
- **"See Weekly Ad For Details" is a trust deflation** — it undercuts the 50% promise before I've even tapped. It plants a seed of doubt: *what's excluded?*

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero image for a Spring Decor lifestyle shot** — show a farmhouse mantle with faux tulips and a shiplap sign, or a front-door wreath scene. That's who buys the Spring Shop, not the fish-plate crowd.
- **Add a three-icon category strip below the hero** — Floral & Botanicals / Home Decor / Seasonal. Three taps, that's it. Let me confirm my category is included in the 50% before I commit to scrolling.
- **Replace "*See Weekly Ad For Details" with the actual category name** — "50% off Seasonal Decor, Floral & More" is infinitely more useful than a footnote that sends me somewhere else.
- **Surface a loyalty hook** — even a simple "Hobby Lobby Rewards — check your balance" banner strip would make me feel seen as a returning shopper rather than a first-time visitor.
