# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the 9/27 tile wall again, with a subject line lifted from 9/25. That send was "Warm Up Your Home With Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price". Today's version just drops the "Warm Up Your Home" opener. A subscriber would see two near-identical subjects six days apart and tune out. The render is about 10,000px tall, with roughly 30 stacked category blocks running from Home Decor through Furniture. Home Decor, Textiles, Christmas, Hobbies, Yarn, Jewelry Making, Sewing and Frames all get equal visual weight. Nothing is clearly the lead.
- The top has the right instinct. A Free Shipping banner and a "Fall Essentials" band sit above a large "HOME DECOR 40% OFF" block. But the "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" message isn't a deal. It's the permanent price structure, and the subject wraps it in "Select", which signals fine print. The 9/29 "50% Off Christmas" hero and the 9/30 "40% Off Fall" hero both had a real visual anchor. This email is a step back from both.
- Verdict: it's a catalog dump with a perpetual-discount headline. Nothing in it says "open now". I'd rank it with 9/24 and 9/27, not with 9/29 or 9/30.

## What's working

- The 40% and 50% badges are large and consistent, so the offer reads at a glance on each tile.
- The seasonal mix of fall, Christmas and football party content matches the calendar.
- The Free Shipping banner at the top gives a second, concrete incentive.
- The Home Decor block leads, so the subject and the first big tile agree.
- Brand voice and layout are consistent with prior sends, so it reads as Hobby Lobby.

## What's weak

- There is no single hero or primary CTA. There are about 30 tiles, and "Categories Listed" is not a button.
- "Always 40% Off" has no deadline or reason to act today. "Select" and the "does not include…" fine print undercut the honesty of the offer.
- The subject nearly repeats the 9/25 send, and the creative is the same wall-to-wall tile playbook as 9/24 and 9/27.
- The length is punishing. The lower sections (Yarn, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notices, Custom Frames, Furniture) are buried, and the exclusion text is tiny and unreadable at inbox width.
- No product is singled out, and there are no reviews or price examples. The one exception is a small "$3.42" Wearable Art callout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero, with one headline offer and one button such as "Shop 40% Off Home Decor". Put 3 to 4 supporting tiles under it and link the rest through a single "See all categories" link. This single change would lift all three scores.
- 2. Lead with something time-bound or new, such as a "this week" Christmas or fall offer, instead of the permanent "Always" discount. The 9/29 "50% Off Christmas" hero is the template.
- 3. Show 3 to 4 priced products with real names instead of category labels. That gives a reason to click.
- 4. Move the exclusions into a clearly labeled footer line, and drop "Select" from the headline.
- 5. Vary the subject from the 9/25 send so cadence doesn't read as copy-paste.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware template with ESP click-tracking on `link.hobbylobby.com`. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). The main verifiable problems are template markup defects and a missing `lang` attribute. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health:** 12 material links were probed and none were reported broken. 33 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so their health is unverified. An unsubscribe link was included in the probe set.
- **Tracking domain:** Links and the pixel use the first-party ESP domain `link.hobbylobby.com`, which is good for deliverability.
- **Plain-text URL ratio:** The plain-text part is 13,062 of 14,702 characters URLs (about 89%). This is consistent with long tracking-wrapped URLs, but it is a spam-filter risk. Consider shorter redirect links or more descriptive plain-text content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139139_2568966_147638.gif` has no alt attribute. The `/mo/` path and `.gif` suggest this is an open-tracking pixel. If so, it should carry `alt=""` and a 1×1 size so screen readers skip it. I couldn't confirm it is a pixel from the QA data alone.
- **CSS defects in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty and unit-only values, so those declarations are invalid and ignored.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` misspells `vertical-align`, so it has no effect.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Common phone viewports are 320px or wider, so those rules would rarely or never apply. Other queries may appear in the truncated portion, so this is not confirmed.
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html … e-locale="en-US" …>` has no `lang`, and `e-locale` is a non-standard attribute. Screen readers get no language hint.
- **Generic `<title>`:** The title is "Hobby Lobby" rather than the subject line. This is a minor point.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with XHTML and VML namespaces is acceptable for email, and the `<meta name="viewport">` is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified rather than confirmed missing**. Check the raw headers from the sending ESP. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not present, which is expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Verify against the original message.
- **Footer:** The footer with the physical address and the unsubscribe link falls in the truncated portion. The QA probe did include an unsubscribe link, but the address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. Links are wrapped by `link.hobbylobby.com`, so the final destinations, UTM parameters and landing page alignment with the "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off" offer are not visible. No broken landing links were reported among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the original ESP headers, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
2. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to the `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/…gif` image.
3. Fix the template CSS: remove the empty `color:` and `px` radius values in `.ctacr1`, and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and consider a subject-matched `<title>`.
5. Check that a mobile breakpoint at 480–600px exists. If the 300px query is the only one, raise or add a breakpoint.
6. Reduce the plain-text URL density with shorter tracking links or more descriptive plain text.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, and probe the 33 skipped links, to cover UTM parameters, the footer address and the remaining links.
