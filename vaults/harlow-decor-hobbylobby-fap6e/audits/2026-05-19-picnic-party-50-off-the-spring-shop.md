# Picnic Party! 50% Off The Spring Shop®
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same weekly-ad-digest format as the May 17 "Your Weekly Ad Is Here!" send, but the "Picnic Party" framing gives it meaningfully more personality. The 50% lead is strong, the spring timing is dead-on for mid-May, and the free-shipping banner at the top adds a second reason to care before the hero even loads. For a Hobby Lobby subscriber who crafts or decorates seasonally, this email checks the right boxes in the subject line.
- The failure is in the body. Seven stacked discount blocks — Home Decor, Summer Toys, Party, Hobbies, Fabric, Frames, Online Clearance — turn a focused picnic-theme promo into a printed circular. No deadline, no specific product to covet, no urgency. The only primary CTA sends you to browse everything, which is the digital equivalent of "go to the store."
- This email gets attention but doesn't earn the click. A subscriber who opened it, scrolled through, and closed without acting did exactly what this email trained them to do.

## What's working

- 50% leads the email with visible weight — the number reads clearly even at a thumbnail
- "Picnic Party" theme is seasonal and specific; better angle than the generic "Spring Essentials" from the May 17 send
- Free shipping threshold ($59+) is in the top banner, adding a second incentive upfront
- Subject line is clean, concrete, and short (~38 chars) — reads well on mobile
- 75% Online Clearance callout at the bottom is a strong close if someone scrolled that far

## What's weak

- Zero urgency anywhere — no expiry date, no "this week only," no countdown; "50% off" without a deadline reads like a permanent fixture
- Primary CTA is "view weekly ad" — sends to browse, not to buy, and wastes the picnic-theme momentum
- Seven stacked discount blocks dilute each other; the eye flatlines after the third row
- No single product hero to click — nothing to want specifically, only categories to browse
- Third email in four days (May 16 welcome, May 17 weekly ad, May 19 this send) — cadence is compressing fast

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard expiry to the hero** — "50% Off Through Saturday" or "This Week Only" is the single change with the highest lift; it's the difference between "interesting" and "I should click now"
- 2. **Replace or pair "view weekly ad" with a direct Spring Shop CTA** — one button going straight to the collection beats a generic weekly-ad portal
- 3. **Cut the stacked blocks to 3-4 max** — keep Party, Home Decor, and Clearance; drop the rest or rotate them to a second send
- 4. **Give one category a featured product** — a single picnic basket or outdoor entertaining item with a real photo turns "40% off Party" from a badge into something to want
- 5. **Let a day pass** — three sends in four days is starting to condition subscribers to skip-read Hobby Lobby
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Spring Shop® — Ends Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Picnic's calling: 50% off everything spring`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the Spring Shop before the weekend — free shipping on $59+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tables, baskets, outdoor entertaining — 50% off through Saturday`

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text part is URL-saturated.**
QA confirms 12,692 of 14,888 characters (85%) in the plain-text part are URLs. This ratio is a known spam-filter signal (Spamhaus, Gmail). Plain text should be a human-readable summary, not a URL dump — tracking pixels and redirect URLs should not be mirrored into the plain-text alternative.

No broken redirect domains detected from the truncated source; all visible links route through `link.hobbylobby.com`.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text on a tracked GIF.**
QA flags:
```
Image missing alt text: eORqXTkAb7_837015858_124179_2015399_133302.gif
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_124179_2015399_133302.gif
```
This appears to be an open/tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` (empty, not absent) so screen readers skip it cleanly. An absent `alt` attribute causes some assistive technologies to read the full filename aloud.

**Minor CSS typo in stylesheet.**
```css
.lhp2 { verticl-align: middle; }
```
`verticl-align` is a misspelling of `vertical-align`. This property will be silently dropped in all clients; low severity since `vertical-align` is already handled via the `table` layout, but indicates the CSS was not linted.

**`border-radius` with missing values.**
```css
.ctacr1 {
  -moz-border-radius:px;
  -webkit-border-radius:px;
  border-radius:px;
}
```
All three declarations have unit-only values (`px` with no number). This makes the CTA button square-cornered in clients that would otherwise render the radius — the fallback is cosmetically acceptable but the intent (rounded corners) is not applied.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**List-Unsubscribe header absent.**
QA: `List-Unsubscribe header not found`. This header is not strictly required by CAN-SPAM but is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender policies (effective Feb 2024) for senders >5k/day. Its absence increases the likelihood of Gmail auto-placing the email in Promotions and can trigger bulk-sender violations.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058).**
QA: `List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found`. One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` method variant) is required by Gmail/Yahoo policy alongside `List-Unsubscribe`. Without it, Gmail renders a mailto fallback or suppresses the unsubscribe UI entirely.

*Note: QA flags these as potentially an AgentMail relay capture issue. However, if the headers are genuinely missing upstream, this is a compliance gap, not a tooling gap. Should be verified against raw headers in Gmail > Show Original.*

**Authentication-Results header not found.**
QA: SPF/DKIM status unknown via relay. Cannot confirm pass/fail. Sending domain `create.hobbylobby.com` should be checked directly: `nslookup -type=TXT create.hobbylobby.com` and DKIM selector lookup. An unauthenticated send from this subdomain would fail DMARC alignment.

CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link are expected in the footer (not visible in truncated source) — could not verify from provided HTML.
