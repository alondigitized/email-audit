# Your Fall Refresh Starts Here!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- "Your Fall Refresh Starts Here!" is a rename, not a redesign — this is the exact same wall-to-wall category-tile grid Hobby Lobby has run almost daily since late August, just with a fall-themed subject line slapped on top. The subject promises a seasonal refresh, but the actual email opens with Home Decor and Textiles at 40% off, then dumps into a full Christmas takeover (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Tableware, Light Sets) before sprawling into Football Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Books, Frames, Art Sale, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing, and Furniture — over 20 categories in one endless scroll.
- There's no hero image, no single offer to land on, and no fall-specific merchandising beyond the header banner. It's a category directory with percentages attached, not a curated "refresh." Given the brand history — this is the fourth-plus near-identical grid send in two weeks (08/23 onward, most recently 09/03's "Select Home Decor" grid) — subscribers have seen this exact structure repeatedly, just reshuffled.
- Judgement: functional as a coupon-code index, but it does nothing the subject line promises and nothing to differentiate itself from the last five sends.

## What's working

- Free shipping banner ($59+) is visible immediately at the top, a clear universal incentive.
- Percentages are legible and consistent (40%, 50%, etc.) so price-conscious scanning is easy.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, categories are cleanly separated.
- Product category photography (textiles, mirrors, frames, jewelry) is decent quality and recognizable at a glance.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Fall Refresh" but the email is dominated by Christmas categories (7 of the first ~10 blocks), which is jarring and doesn't match the promise.
- No hero offer or lead image — the eye has nowhere specific to land, it's just tile after tile.
- Extreme length (20+ categories) with no curation means most recipients will bail before scrolling far.
- Zero differentiation from the 09/03 "Select Home Decor" and 08/23-onward sends — same layout, same generic categories, same "Always 40%" framing.
- No fall-specific product styling (pumpkins, harvest colors, tablescapes) despite the subject explicitly invoking fall — a missed opportunity given 09/01's hutch tablescape hero proved the brand can do seasonal styling well.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with an actual fall hero image** — pull the tablescape/harvest styling from the 09/01 "Seasonal Hosting" send and put one styled fall vignette at the top with a single clear CTA, instead of jumping straight into a Home Decor tile grid.
- 2. **Cut Christmas out of a "Fall Refresh" email entirely** — if Christmas needs promotion, send it as its own campaign (as done 09/04 and 09/05); mixing it here undercuts the subject line's promise and confuses the seasonal narrative.
- 3. **Shorten to 6-8 curated categories** most relevant to "fall refresh" (Home Decor, Textiles, Florals, Wearable Art) rather than the full 20+ category catalog — this is an email, not a store directory.
- 4. **Vary the subject/creative cadence** — this is at least the 5th near-identical grid in two weeks; even reordering categories doesn't fool a recipient who's opened the last few.
