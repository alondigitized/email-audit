# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-07-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expect from Hobby Lobby mid-week before a major holiday — clean, patriotic, on-the-nose. The July 4th styled table scene with the basket arrangement and Americana pennants? That's my language. But I've been shopping here long enough to know that "40% off / See Weekly Ad for Details" asterisk means not everything qualifies, and I'll need to pull up the circular before I get too excited.

## What's working

- **The July 4th tableau image** — wicker basket, red/white/blue faux florals, lemonade pitcher, decorative pennant banners — this is exactly the kind of seasonal vignette I'm pinning and recreating at home
- **"40% OFF" in massive type** — I spotted it before anything else; the size and contrast do the job
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through July 4, 2026"** — I appreciate seeing the expiration date stated plainly and the in-store confirmation. I'm not trusting an online-only promo anyway
- **Free shipping $59+ banner at the top** — easy to reach for my typical basket, and it's the first thing I see when the page loads
- **Weekly Ad button** — right there in the header. That's how I actually shop; I check the ad before I even make my list

## What's weak

- **The asterisk on 40% off** — "*See Weekly Ad For Details" immediately puts me on guard. I know the rotating calendar, and this isn't the blanket discount it looks like at first glance
- **No faux floral or farmhouse-specific callout** — the basket scene is close, but there's no headline that says "Seasonal Décor" or "Floral" — someone less familiar might not know this sale applies to what they want
- **"Shop Now" is too generic** — shop *what*, exactly? July 4th everything? Just décor? The CTA works but doesn't pull me toward my specific department
- **Zero personalization for a weekly shopper** — I've been coming here for years. There's no acknowledgment of that. No "Your favorites are on sale" or even a soft loyalty nudge
- **No reviews, no product-level social proof** — the hero is a styled vignette, which I like, but I can't tell if those specific items are even in stock or what they cost

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to something category-specific**: "Shop Seasonal Décor" or "Shop Floral & Home" instead of "Shop Now" — helps me confirm I'm in the right place with one read
- **Drop the asterisk or explain it inline**: Showing "40% off select seasonal décor — see Weekly Ad for exclusions" removes the guessing game and keeps trust high for savvy shoppers
- **Add a "This Week's 50% Off Category" strip below the hero**: I know the rotating calendar by heart, but a first-timer doesn't. A one-liner ("Floral is 50% off this week!") would supercharge engagement for shoppers like me
- **Swap the generic basket scene for a more farmhouse-specific vignette**: Shiplap backdrop, mason jar stems, neutral-toned greenery with a patriotic pop — it's a small tweak but it speaks directly to my aesthetic and differentiates Hobby Lobby from generic party-supply competitors
