# Hurry In – Shop 40% Off Fall Must-Haves!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby actually delivering on its subject line for once — the hero band right up top is a clean "40% OFF Fall / Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" with a warm, on-brand lifestyle photo of a woman styling a fall tablescape. That's a real improvement over 09-06's "Fall Refresh" email, which buried the fall theme under an unrelated tile grid. But scroll past the hero and it's the exact same problem this brand has run almost daily since August: a sprawling, undifferentiated stack of category tiles (Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials, then Fall Decor/Floral/Kitchen/Party/Candles/Crafts, then Christmas, Home Decor, Fabric, Party again, Art Supplies, Jewelry Making, Clearance...) that goes on for what looks like 15+ scroll-screens.
- The subject promises one thing — 40% off fall must-haves — and the email spends its first two screens delivering it, then abandons the theme entirely and reverts to the kitchen-sink catalog dump that's defined this account's last three weeks of sends. It's a stronger open than most recent Hobby Lobby emails but a weaker click than it should be, because the length and repetition (a second "50% Christmas" banner sits right below the fall grid) dilute the one clear offer at the top.

## What's working

- Hero band directly matches subject copy: "40% OFF Fall — Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" with a warm, seasonally-appropriate lifestyle photo.
- Clear CTA button ("In-Store Or Online") on the hero, high contrast, easy to spot.
- "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" banner near the top is a real, unambiguous incentive.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap or broken layout.
- "Fall Essentials" banner section reinforces the theme with a matching illustrated header before the tile grid.

## What's weak

- The email sprawls far past the fall theme into Christmas, fabric, furniture, jewelry-making, and online clearance — a subscriber who opened for "fall must-haves" has to scroll through a dozen unrelated categories.
- A second big banner — "50% Christmas" — appears directly under the fall section, competing with and diluting the subject's single offer, echoing the same broken-promise issue flagged in 09-04's Christmas email.
- Nearly every tile repeats "In-Store Or Online" and "See Weekly Ad For Details" — visually monotonous, no product-level specificity (no photos of actual SKUs, no prices).
- Top nav-style tiles (Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials) appear before the fall hero even loads, pushing the promised offer below the fold on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "Fall Essentials" tile grid — end the send there instead of stacking Christmas, fabric, furniture, and clearance underneath. A focused fall email will outperform a 15-screen catalog dump.
- 2. Move the Kitchen/Bath/Home Essentials nav tiles below the fall hero, or drop them — they push the subject's actual offer below the fold on mobile.
- 3. Swap at least a few "See Weekly Ad For Details" tiles for one or two hero products with real photography and dollar prices, like the 09-02 "Everyday Deals" send did — that email scored better specifically for showing real SKUs.
- 4. If Christmas content must run, give it its own send rather than sandwiching a second "50% Christmas" banner directly under a fall-themed hero — it undercuts the subject's single-topic promise.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy up your home — fall decor, florals & kitchen finds, all 40% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 9/12 — shop fall favorites in-store or online.`
