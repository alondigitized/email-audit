# Hobby Lobby Now Hiring for Ontario, CA
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a staffing email — a new-store hiring push for Hobby Lobby's Ontario, CA location — sent to what is plainly a retail shopper subscriber list. The email itself is clean, honest, and functional: the wage range is upfront ($16.90–$17.90/hr), the hiring event has specific dates and a venue address, and the APPLY NOW CTA is prominent. Execution quality is fine. The problem is entirely one of audience: a national retail shopper list is the wrong channel for a hyper-local staffing event.
- The brand history context makes this worse. The last ten days were wall-to-wall sale promos — 75% off, 66% off, 50% off, everyday-low-price pitches — and now out of nowhere a job posting drops into the same stream with zero transition. A subscriber conditioned to open for deals will be confused at best, annoyed at worst.
- The only way this email works is if the recipient happens to be an Ontario-area shopper who is also actively job hunting. That intersection is small. Hobby Lobby should be routing this to a local jobs board or a specifically segmented "Ontario-area subscribers" list, not broadcasting nationally.

## What's working

- Wage range is visible immediately ($16.90–$17.90/hr) — no bait and switch on compensation
- Hiring event dates and times are specific and credible (Mon Jul 20 – Fri Jul 24, 8am–5:30pm)
- Both the store address and the hiring venue address are listed — reduces friction for actual applicants
- APPLY NOW button is prominent and unambiguous
- Clean render, no broken images or layout issues

## What's weak

- Sent to a national retail shopper list for a single-location staffing event — fundamental audience mismatch
- No framing or bridge to explain to shoppers why they're receiving a job posting
- Full-time vs. part-time breakdown is vague — "Opportunity for Full and Part-Time Positions" tells applicants nothing about what's actually available
- The "Text JOIN to 48793 for sale alerts" module at the bottom is for promotional content — including it in a hiring email is off-message and undercuts the single CTA
- No mention of the new store opening, which would have given shoppers a reason to care even if they're not job seekers

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment to Ontario-area subscribers only** — or use job boards. Sending to the full list is the root problem; fixing this lifts the email's effective impact more than any copy change.
- 2. **Reframe for the shopper audience** — "We're opening a new store near you! Know someone looking for work?" converts this into a referral play and gives non-job-seekers a reason to forward it rather than unsubscribe.
- 3. **Specify the role breakdown** — replace "Full and Part-Time Positions" with an actual count or breakdown ("10 full-time store associates, 20 temporary setup crew") to create tangible urgency.
- 4. **Remove the sale-alert text/SMS block** — it's leftover boilerplate from the promo template and actively confuses the hiring message.
- 5. **Lead with the new store opening** — "Hobby Lobby is opening in Ontario, CA on [date]" gives the whole email a news hook that even a non-applicant shopper finds interesting.
- **Subject Alt A:** `We're opening in Ontario — now hiring store team`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Know someone who'd love working at Hobby Lobby Ontario?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `$16.90–$17.90/hr • Apply July 20–24 at our on-site hiring event`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Ontario location opening soon — full & part-time roles available now`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
Two confirmed CSS bugs in `.ctacr1` (the primary CTA button rule):

```css
.ctacr1 {
  color:;                        /* BUG: empty value — property is invalid */
  -moz-border-radius:px;         /* BUG: missing numeric value */
  -webkit-border-radius:px;      /* BUG: missing numeric value */
  border-radius:px;              /* BUG: missing numeric value */
}
```

- `color:;` is malformed — all three browser engines will discard it. The button text color will inherit from the parent container, which may render as black-on-dark-blue (`#013088` background), failing contrast in most clients.
- All three `border-radius` declarations are missing the number before `px`. The button will render with sharp corners in every client.

Additional typo in `.lhp2`:
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font { line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle; }
/*                                    ^ "vertical" misspelled */
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid property; vertical alignment on `.lhp2` elements will not apply.

`alt` attribute coverage cannot be confirmed from the truncated source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
"Ontario, CA" appears hardcoded in the subject line (`Subject: Hobby Lobby Now Hiring for Ontario, CA`). If this email uses audience segmentation to target multiple markets, the city/state should be a merge token (e.g., `%%LocationCity%%, %%LocationState%%`). Hardcoded geo targeting means the wrong city will display for any recipient outside the Ontario, CA segment if segmentation fails or the template is reused.

`e-is-multilanguage="false"` is set — no locale-switching issues expected for a US-only send.
