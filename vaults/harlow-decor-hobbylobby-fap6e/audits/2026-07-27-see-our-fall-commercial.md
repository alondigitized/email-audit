# See Our Fall Commercial 🍂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's first pure-branding send in the recent run — no discount, no product grid, no coupon, just a hero image and a "View Our Commercial" video CTA under the headline "Fall Is What You Make It." Compared to the "Fall Essentials" lookbook from 2026-07-27 (8/10, hero + real product tiles) or even the circular-grid sends, this one asks for a click with nothing tangible behind it: no percentage off, no linked product, no loyalty perk.
- The creative itself is well shot — warm, lived-in kitchen scene, a kid decorating a fall garland, a dad prepping in the background — and the header/nav/footer are all clean with zero render bugs. But as a marketing email it's thin: the entire commercial value proposition is "watch our ad," which is a brand-awareness play, not a revenue play, and it's landing in the same week as several stronger Fall discount sends.
- Judgement: visually polished, strategically weak. This belongs in an occasional brand-story cadence, not stacked against back-to-back 40%-off sends — recipients who've been trained by Hobby Lobby's recent emails to expect a discount will bounce off a "watch our commercial" ask.

## What's working

- Clean, single-focus hero with no clutter — headline, subhead, and one clear play-button CTA.
- Photography is genuinely warm and on-brand for Fall (garland-making, family kitchen scene).
- No render bugs — nav bar, hero, footer bar, and app-store badges all display correctly.
- Footer/legal block and unsubscribe link are present and legible, keeping the send compliant-looking.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, offer, or promo code anywhere in the email — a hard miss against a brand history full of 40%-off sends.
- The play button links out to a video, not a shopping destination — no product, no category, no "shop the look."
- No urgency, no loyalty messaging, nothing that differentiates this from an ad you'd see on TV.
- Coming right behind the 2026-07-27 "Fall Essentials" 40%-off lookbook, this reads as a step down in commercial intent for the same day/week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pair the commercial with a shoppable strip below the fold — 3-4 product tiles ("Shop the Fall Look," "Shop Wreaths," "Shop Fall Crafting") so viewers who don't want to watch a video still have something to click.
- 2. Add a visible offer badge to the hero itself (e.g., "40% Off Fall" corner tag) — don't force a trade-off between "watch the ad" and "see the deal."
- 3. Move this format to a lower-frequency brand-story cadence (once a season) rather than slotting it into the same week as multiple discount sends — it dilutes the urgency those sends are building.
- 4. Give the CTA button two options: primary "Shop Fall Now," secondary "Watch the Commercial" — let commerce lead, not video.
