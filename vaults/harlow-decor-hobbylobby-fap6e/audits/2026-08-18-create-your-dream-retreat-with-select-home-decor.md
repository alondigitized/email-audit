# 🛏️ Create Your Dream Retreat With Select Home Decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- "🛏️ Create Your Dream Retreat With Select Home Decor" actually lands on-subject for once — the hero module is a styled bedroom scene (floral bedding, olive throw, nightstand vignette) with "Always 40% Off The Marked Price* Home Decor" overlaid directly on it. That's a real improvement over the last several sends in this run, which promised a theme and then buried it under generic tile grids.
- But the discipline lasts exactly one screen. Below the bedroom hero, this reverts to the same sprawling Hobby Lobby format seen on 2026-08-16, 2026-08-14, and 2026-08-13: rows of small product-category tiles (Doormats, Sheet Sets, Bath Towels, Quilt Sets, Wall Clocks...), then a second wave of unrelated seasonal promos (Fall, Christmas, Classroom Decor, Dorm Essentials, Football Party, Crafts, Online Clearance). By the bottom third, "dream retreat" bedroom styling has nothing to do with dorm decor or football party supplies.
- Net: the top of the email is the best-executed hero this brand has sent in the recent history, but the email as a whole is still an everything-store catalog dump wearing a themed subject line as a costume.

## What's working

- Hero image genuinely matches the subject — a styled, cozy bedroom with visible bedding, lamp, nightstand, and throw blanket.
- Single clear headline offer ("Always 40% Off The Marked Price, Home Decor") is legible and sits directly on the hero.
- One unambiguous CTA button ("In-Store Or Online") directly under the offer, no scrolling needed to see it.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) is visible immediately at the top, adding a second concrete offer.
- No render bugs — clean images, no overlapping text, no broken tiles.

## What's weak

- The bedroom theme evaporates after one screen; everything below is generic Home category tiles with no connection to "dream retreat."
- Second half pivots hard into unrelated seasonal categories (Christmas, Classroom, Dorm, Football, Crafts) — same sprawl pattern flagged in 5 of the last 6 sends from this brand.
- No sizing, fabric, thread-count, or material specifics for any bedding/decor item shown — a shopper actually furnishing a bedroom gets zero product detail.
- No social proof or reviews anywhere in the email.
- "40% Off" and "Always" together read as evergreen, not a real promotional event — undercuts urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the bedroom module and the first 2-3 matching tile rows (Bath, Bedding, Window). Split Fall/Christmas/Classroom/Dorm/Football/Crafts into a separate send — this is really two emails mashed into one, and it's the same complaint as the last several audits.
- 2. Add a second lifestyle shot lower in the "dream retreat" section (e.g., a styled reading nook or bathroom) so the retreat theme carries further than one hero.
- 3. Swap "Always 40% Off" for a dated framing tied to the "Sale Valid Through Saturday, August 22, 2026" footer date — pull that deadline up near the hero CTA for real urgency.
- 4. Add one line of product specificity under the hero (e.g., "Quilts, throws & shams — twin to king") so a shopper knows their size is covered before clicking.
