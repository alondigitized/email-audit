# Up To 75% Off Seasonal Savings!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject leads with the wrong offer. "Up To 75% Off Seasonal Savings!" puts 75% up front, but the hero is "Christmas 50% Off". The 75% shows up only about a quarter of the way down, on a fall decor, floral and crafts block, and it's a left-over-season markdown. Anyone expecting a broad 75% event gets a 50% Christmas lead and a stack of 30-40% "Always" prices. The 11-15 send ("Sale Alert 🚨🍁 75% Off Fall Decor, Floral & Crafts") had the same problem.
- The render is a very long scroll, about 9,400px, with roughly 25 offer blocks at nearly equal weight. Nothing is broken, and the "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!" banner and the Christmas hero do carry the top. After that the eye has no clear path. There is no button-style CTA and no single hero product. It reads as a price list.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand circular with a mismatched subject and no focus. It's a mid-pack send at about 5-6, in line with the 11-17 and 11-18 sends.

## What's working

- The top banner, "Free Shipping On Orders $69 Or More!", gives a concrete threshold in the first screen.
- The Christmas 50% hero at the top matches the season and is the biggest, boldest block.
- Large percentage numerals like 40%, 50% and 30% scan quickly, and the "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" repetition is clear.
- Category blocks (Home Decor, Textiles, Hobbies, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry Making) let a crafter or decorator find their lane.
- Sale-ending language is present: "Deal Valid Through Saturday, November 21, 2026".

## What's weak

- The subject promises up to 75%, while the hero is 50% off Christmas. The 75% is only on the fall decor block, and fall decor is past its season.
- With about 25 equal-weight blocks and no sectioning, the eye has nowhere to land after the hero. This is the weekly-circular problem from 11-18.
- There are no visible buttons or "Shop Now" CTAs. The blocks look like images, and the CTA copy is implicit.
- Heavy "Categories Listed" and "Does not include…" fine print under many blocks adds exclusion friction.
- The deadline sits in a small footer line at the very bottom, so the urgency is buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero. If the lead is Christmas 50%, say so. If the 75% is the draw, put that block first.
- 2. Cut the scroll to the 5-6 best blocks, with one hero, one secondary and a visible "Shop Christmas" button. Link out to the full circular for the rest.
- 3. Add real CTA buttons with verb-and-noun copy per section, such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas Decor".
- 4. Move the "Ends Saturday 11/21" deadline into the header or hero.
- 5. Collapse the exclusion fine print into one footnote, not repeated per block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Christmas, Ends Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Christmas 50% Off + Fall Decor Up To 75%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping at $69. Fall decor, floral & crafts up to 75% off through Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Trees, lights and wreaths at half price, plus 40% off home decor.`

# Technical Audit: "Up To 75% Off Seasonal Savings!" (Hobby Lobby)

The HTML source was truncated, so findings from the footer, body links and unsubscribe block are limited to what the QA data shows. Where the source can't confirm something, I say so instead of flagging it.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's QA run had 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The warnings are mostly relay-capture limits, and the HTML has a few real CSS and accessibility defects: invalid CSS declarations, a 300px mobile breakpoint, and no `lang` attribute. Authentication and one-click unsubscribe could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing was partial.** 12 material links were probed and 28 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). No broken links were reported among the 12. The skipped 28 are unverified.
- **Tracking domain.** The links use the branded redirect domain `link.hobbylobby.com`. The QA data shows no probe failures against it.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,566 of 13,124 characters (about 88%) are URLs, against a 70% threshold. This points to long tracking URLs in the text alternative, which spam filters may penalize.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed).** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_145675_4481616_154308.gif` has no `alt`. The `/mo/` path on the tracking domain suggests an open-tracking pixel, so impact is probably low. Add `alt=""` and confirm what the image is.
- **Mobile breakpoint.** The only `@media` query visible in the source is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Common phone widths (320–430px) are above 300px, so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, `.h1`) likely never apply on real devices. Other media queries may sit in the truncated part.
- **Invalid CSS.** `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values for `color` and the radius properties. Clients drop these declarations, so the CTA text colour falls back to the inherited colour. The button's `background-color:#013088` is intact.
- **Typo.** `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` should be `vertical-align`, so the rule is ignored.
- **Missing `lang`.** The `<html>` tag has `e-locale="en-US"` but no `lang="en"` attribute, which screen readers need.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML and VML `xmlns` attributes. This is common in ESP templates and usually harmless, so it is low priority.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found (QA warning).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a violation.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against the raw message at the origin ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment at the source.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** I can't confirm either in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed but gives no result for it.
- **`noreply@` sender.** This isn't a CAN-SPAM violation, but it blocks replies as a feedback channel.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. The links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirector, and the truncated source and QA data show no final landing URLs or UTM parameters. I can't comment on landing-page alignment with the "Up To 75% Off" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the invalid `.ctacr1` declarations: set a real `color`, and a numeric `border-radius` such as `3px`.
2. Add a mobile breakpoint at 480–600px, or confirm one exists in the truncated CSS.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and `alt=""` (or descriptive alt text) to the tracking GIF.
4. Correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
5. Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links in it.
6. Inspect the raw `.eml` at the origin to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
7. Re-run link probing with a higher cap, or probe the 28 skipped links manually, and record the final URL and UTM parameters for each CTA.
