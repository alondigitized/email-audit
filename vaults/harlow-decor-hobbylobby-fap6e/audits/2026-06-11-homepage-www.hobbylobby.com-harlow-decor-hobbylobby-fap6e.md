# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-06-11
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is *my* store. The second I landed on this page, I felt it — a gorgeous styled porch dripping in red, white, and blue stars, flags, and bows, with that big bold "40% OFF July 4th" practically jumping off the screen. Hobby Lobby is doing exactly what they do best: putting a beautiful, livable holiday scene in front of me and making it very clear I can afford to recreate it right now. Classic HL, and for someone like me, that's not a bad thing at all.

## What's working

- **The hero image** — that front-porch vignette with the wreath, the star cutouts, the flag buntings, the cushioned outdoor seating, and the star-print rug is *exactly* what I come here to build. This is aspirational and attainable at the same time. I immediately start mentally shopping the scene.
- **The "40% OFF" treatment** — the type is huge, black, and impossible to miss. I don't have to hunt for the deal. It's the visual center of the page after the hero.
- **"Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!"** — prominently in the red top banner. For a decor run, $59 is nothing. That threshold is real for me.
- **The Weekly Ad button** — I live by the rotating 50%-off calendar, and that blue "Weekly Ad" button is right in the nav bar, first thing. They know their customer.
- **Sale Valid Online & In-Store** — as someone who doesn't fully trust digital-only promos, seeing "Online & In-Store" explicitly stated removes friction immediately.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty or rewards hook** — I've been shopping here for years. If I have points built up or a member offer waiting, I want to see that the second I open the app. Nothing above the fold tells me I'm a known customer.
- **"Shop Now" is vague** — there's nothing wrong with the button itself, but "Shop July 4th Decor" or "Shop the Sale" would tell me exactly where I'm going. "Shop Now" could link anywhere.
- **No product specificity in the hero** — the lifestyle scene is beautiful, but I can't tap a star garland or that wreath and go straight to a product page. The image is inspirational but not interactive.
- **"*See Weekly Ad For Details" fine print** — that asterisk gives me pause. What details? Is the 40% off not on everything? A farmhouse-category carve-out? I'd want to know before I get too excited.

## Recommendations

- **Make the hero image shoppable:** add at least two tap targets on the porch scene — the wreath and the star rug — going directly to product pages. I want to tap the thing I see, not hunt for it.
- **Add a loyalty/rewards strip between the nav and the hero:** even a single line — "Welcome back! You have X points" or "Members save an extra 10%" — would make me feel seen as a returning weekly shopper.
- **Sharpen the CTA copy:** change "Shop Now" to "Shop 40% Off July 4th Decor" — one extra word, but it removes all ambiguity about where I'm headed.
- **Clarify the asterisk:** move the "See Weekly Ad for Details" qualifier *into* a sub-line below the CTA rather than below the offer headline, so it reads as clarification rather than a red flag.
