# Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the Hobby Lobby weekly-ad tile wall again, and it's the third time in two weeks. The subject is lifted from the 9/25 send ("Warm Up Your Home With Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price"). It's also the same subject as the 10/1 send, and the layout is the same as the 9/27 and 10/1 walls. The subject promises Home Decor, and the first tile delivers it: a big "HOME DECOR – ALWAYS 40% OFF THE MARKED PRICE" block with a category list.
- After that the email is about 25 stacked offers, from textiles, mirrors and lamps through Christmas, party, books, frames, fabric, jewelry making, sewing and furniture. The percentages run 25%, 30%, 40% and 50%. Nothing is the hero. Home Decor is only the first of many, so the subject over-promises focus the body doesn't have. The "Always 40%" language also isn't news. It's a standing discount, so there's no reason to open today instead of yesterday.
- Verdict: functional and on-brand, but undifferentiated. A subscriber who saw the 10/1 send would reasonably skip this one.
- Caveat: the screenshot is 680×9800 and was shown to me heavily downscaled. I can see the layout, blocks and big numerals, but not fine print or small CTA text.

## What's working

- The Home Decor hero matches the subject, and the big "40%" numeral reads fast.
- A free-shipping bar ("Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!") sits at the top as a clear secondary incentive.
- Seasonal coverage is current: fall decor and crafts, football party, and a Christmas banner with Christmas Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts and Light Sets tiles.
- A few concrete price points break up the percentages (paper crafts at 4.99, the 99¢ fabric/notions block).
- The layout renders cleanly. I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the brand look is consistent.

## What's weak

- No single hero. About 25 tiles compete, so the eye has nowhere to land after the first block.
- It's a repeat. The 10/1 send used this subject and the same tile wall, and the 9/25 and 9/27 sends were close variants. The "Always 40%" message is not news.
- There's no clear button CTA. The categories are text lists, so the click target is ambiguous.
- Fine print and exclusions are everywhere ("Does not include…", "Select"), which makes the offer feel conditional.
- The email is very long (about 9,800px). Most readers will never reach the lower tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero per send and cut the rest to 4–6 tiles. Today that would be Home Decor or the Christmas 50%-off hero, but not both plus 20 other blocks.
- 2. Add a real button ("Shop Home Decor") under the hero. Link tiles to specific products or collections, not generic category lists.
- 3. Add a real deadline near the top. The only date I can see is in the small footer (deal valid through Saturday).
- 4. Stop reusing subjects. If the offer is a standing one, lead with the new thing (the Christmas 50%, fall candles) and keep "Always 40%" as the body line.
- 5. Put the free-shipping threshold in the preheader and match it to the hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-templated (Oracle Responsys-style `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking) build. QA found 0 failures and 4 warnings. All of the compliance and authentication warnings come from missing headers in the AgentMail capture, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects. The HTML source is truncated, so only the head and CSS could be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 of 44 links were probed and 32 were skipped. The skipped links are footer, utility and social links, which the QA tool deprioritizes. No broken links were reported among the probed ones, and the unsubscribe link was always probed.
- **Plain-text bloat:** the plain-text part is 12,706 of 14,353 characters URLs (about 89%). Every link is wrapped in a long `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking redirect. This makes the text part unreadable and can trigger spam heuristics on text-to-link ratio. It is a deliverability risk, not a functional break.
- **Tracking domain:** all links go through `link.hobbylobby.com`, a first-party subdomain of the sender domain. That is good for alignment, but I couldn't check its SSL or redirect chain from this data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139903_58304_148454.gif`. The `/mo/` path and `.gif` extension suggest this is the open-tracking pixel. If so, the impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would still be correct, and it should be confirmed as decorative.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch:** the document declares `HTML 4.01 Transitional` but also carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` on `<html>`. It also uses a nonstandard `e-locale` attribute. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid markup.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has an empty color value and unit-less radius values. These declarations are dropped, so the `.ctacr1` button has no radius and inherits its text color.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so that declaration has no effect.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` doesn't match the subject. It has little impact in email clients.
- **Fine as-is:** Outlook conditional comments (`mso`) and VML namespaces are present. The viewport meta tag and `X-UA-Compatible` are properly wrapped. A responsive media query exists at `max-width:300px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source or in the subject, `From` or `title`. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes AgentMail may strip these in relay, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. If they are truly absent, this fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe).
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is relay capture, not evidence of failure.
- **From address:** `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com` is a non-replyable sender. CAN-SPAM doesn't prohibit this, but the sending subdomain `create.` can't be checked for alignment from the available data.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked in the truncated source. QA did probe an unsubscribe link and reported no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible, because the destination URLs sit behind `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects. Whether UTM or campaign parameters are appended at redirect time is unknown.
- **Landing page alignment:** not verifiable. QA didn't report the final destinations or any redirect failures, so no issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the headers on a raw send.** Check a direct-to-mailbox copy (not the AgentMail relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. This is the highest priority, because Gmail/Yahoo enforce it.
2. **Fix the CSS.** Correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`. Supply real values for `.ctacr1`'s `color` and `border-radius`, or remove the declarations.
3. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel GIF.** Add real alt text to any content image that lacks it.
4. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Reduce link wrapping or add readable text so the part is not about 89% URLs.
5. **Align the doctype and namespace.** Use either an XHTML doctype with the XHTML namespace or an HTML doctype without it.
6. **Re-run QA with a fuller HTML capture and a larger probe budget.** This would cover merge tokens, footer compliance, UTM handling and the 32 unprobed links.
