# Shop Now: 50% Off Christmas & Fall!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is the most specific it has been in a week. "Shop Now: 50% Off Christmas & Fall!" is a mostly honest summary. The render opens with a Christmas Essentials banner and a big "CHRISTMAS 50% OFF" block, and a "Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts 50% OFF" block sits further down. Unlike the Nov 6 send, "Elevate Your Holiday Hosting With 50% Off Christmas", the 50% claim here leads the body. One fair caveat: the Christmas "50%" is shown with a small "25%" tag beside the Christmas Light Sets, so a few exclusions muddy the headline.
- The email is an enormous scroll, about 14 stacked category blocks, each with its own big percentage. Home Decor 40%, Textiles 40%, Mirrors & Lamps 50%, Football Party 40%, Floral 40%, Hobbies 40%, Crafts 40%, Frames 40%, Paper Crafts 40%, Yarn 30% and Furniture 30% all compete. The eye has no single place to land after the first screen. This is the same "everything is on sale" circular as Nov 1 ("It's The Season Of Savings") and Nov 3 ("Serve In Style With 50% Off Fall"). It is a reliable, honest circular but not a targeted one.
- Judgement: a competent weekly flyer with a better-than-usual subject. It's still too long and too undifferentiated to feel personal.

## What's working

- The subject's "50% Off Christmas & Fall" matches visible blocks in the body, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- The free-shipping bar ($69+) and the Christmas Essentials banner sit at the top, so the hero is seasonal and above the fold.
- The big percentage numerals are readable at a glance, and the "Always 40% off the marked price" device is consistent across blocks.
- Product photos (trees, pumpkin, mug, yarn, jewelry-making supplies) make the categories concrete.
- A "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 14, 2026" line near the footer gives a real deadline.

## What's weak

- The email is a very long scroll of near-identical blocks with no single dominant hero after the top.
- There's no clear primary CTA button. Blocks read as links, but there's no "Shop Christmas" style button.
- Fine-print exclusions repeat under almost every block ("Does not include seasonal, food, or Scotch tape"), which cuts into the offer's honesty.
- The deadline sits at the very bottom in small text, so it can't create urgency near the hero.
- The 50% hero is a wall of text and a small tree grid, while the pumpkin and Fall 50% block is buried midway down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to three blocks: Christmas 50%, Fall 50% and one 40% evergreen. Link the rest to a "See all weekly deals" button.
- 2. Add a visible CTA button to the hero, such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas".
- 3. Move the "Ends Saturday 11/14" deadline into a banner under the hero.
- 4. Pull the exclusion text into one consolidated footnote.
- 5. Put Fall 50% right under Christmas so the subject's second promise is paid off early.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Christmas Trees & Decor: 50% Off This Week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off home decor, textiles and floral. Free shipping at $69+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall clearance at half price, through Saturday 11/14.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is an ESP-templated (link.hobbylobby.com click-tracking) email with no probed-link failures (0 issues, 4 warnings). The confirmed problems are minor: one image with no alt text, invalid CSS declarations, a responsive breakpoint that is probably ineffective, and a plain-text part that is mostly URLs. The HTML was truncated and the headers weren't captured, so compliance and continuity can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text alternative is mostly URLs.** The QA check reports 11312 of 12846 characters (~88%) are URLs. Every link is wrapped in a long `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...` tracking redirect, which bloats the text part and can raise spam-filter scores.
- **Coverage is limited.** 12 links were probed and 28 were skipped by design (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). No broken or redirect-looping links were reported among the 12, but the other 28 are unverified.
- All links go through a branded first-party tracking domain (`link.hobbylobby.com`), which is good for deliverability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142476_1060064_152891.gif`. The QA check flags it and the source doesn't show a purpose for it. If it's decorative or a spacer, it needs `alt=""`. If it's content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Responsive breakpoint is probably ineffective.** The only `@media` query visible is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Almost no phone is that narrow (typically 320–430px), so the mobile rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.h1`) likely never apply. The source was truncated, so there may be other queries I couldn't see.
- **Viewport meta lacks `width=device-width`.** It reads `content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes"`.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values, which are dropped.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` is a typo for `vertical-align`, so the property is ignored.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag carries `e-locale="en-US"`, which is a non-standard attribute that assistive tech ignores.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with `xmlns` XHTML attributes. This is common in email and low risk.
- The `<title>` is the generic "Hobby Lobby".
- The VML/MSO conditional comments are present, so Outlook fallback support is in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML or subject. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the source or in a seed inbox. If they are really absent, that is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** The tool attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, not failed.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these couldn't be verified in the truncated source. The QA step reportedly always probes an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported.
- The From address is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain. A `noreply` sender is not a violation, but it gives recipients no reply path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Insufficient data. The tracked URLs are opaque redirects (`/mo/<token>`), and the QA output doesn't give final destinations or query strings. UTM presence and landing-page match against the "50% Off Christmas & Fall" offer can't be confirmed. The 12 probed links reported no errors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text, or `alt=""` if decorative, to `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142476_1060064_152891.gif`.
2. Add a mobile breakpoint at about 480–600px (`max-width:600px`) and set the viewport to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
3. Fix the invalid CSS: fill in or remove the empty `.ctacr1` values, and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Shorten the tracking URLs or generate a leaner plain-text part, since ~88% of it is URLs.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results against the raw message outside the relay.
7. Re-run the QA with the full, untruncated HTML and the skipped 28 links. Resolve a sample of the redirects to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
