# ‘Tis The Season To Save With 50% Off Christmas 🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject is honest this time. "'Tis The Season To Save With 50% Off Christmas 🎄" matches the biggest block in the render: a large "50% OFF Christmas" hero over plaid kitchen towels and a candle. The Christmas Essentials banner and six-tile category grid (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) reinforce it. A shopper who opens for Christmas gets Christmas.
- The execution is still circular-style. A green free-shipping bar, three home-essentials tiles and a Weekly Ad nav sit above the hero. Below it, seven more percent-off blocks (75% fall, 40% home decor, 40% party, 30% fabric, 40% art, 40% hobbies, 75% clearance) compete for attention. The hero loses focus because the email sells Christmas and clearance fall decor in the same scroll. The CTAs are all "In-Store Or Online ▶" and never name a product.
- Compared with the Nov 15 send ("Sale Alert 🚨🍁 75% Off Fall Decor"), which led with a 75% fall claim, this one is better aligned. It also follows the Nov 16 no-offer Christmas commercial email, so it reads as the promotional follow-through. Solid and functional, but not sharp.

## What's working

- The subject matches the hero: "50% Off Christmas" appears as the largest type in the email.
- The 50% numeral is huge and high-contrast, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The Christmas Essentials banner and six category tiles give clear shopping paths into the seasonal assortment.
- The free-shipping bar ($69+) at the top is a visible secondary incentive.
- The sale end date, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 21, 2026," is stated, which adds credible urgency.

## What's weak

- Too many competing percent-off blocks. Eight distinct offers after the hero dilute the 50% Christmas message.
- Every CTA is the same generic "In-Store Or Online ▶", with no verb and no product.
- The hero copy carries "*See Weekly Ad For Details" and "Select Categories" caveats. The asterisks and "Always 40% off the Marked Price*" language read as fine print and weaken trust in the headline offer.
- The 75% off Fall Decor block still appears after the Christmas section. That is stale seasonal messaging in mid-November, and it repeats the Nov 15 send.
- The email is very long, about 4,300px at this render. The only social proof is absent, and there are no reviews or product-level detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower body to Christmas only. Keep the 50% hero and the Christmas grid, and move fall and general-category blocks to a compact footer strip. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Replace the generic CTAs with specific ones, such as "Shop Christmas Trees" or "Shop 50% Off Decor".
- 3. Make the hero CTA a solid, high-contrast button, "Shop 50% Off Christmas", instead of an outlined "In-Store Or Online".
- 4. Put the sale end date (Nov 21) in the hero, not only at the bottom.
- 5. Replace the stale fall block with a Christmas best-seller or a ready-to-ship-by-Dec-25 message.

The HTML source shows no broken structure, but it contains invalid CSS declarations and a viewport meta tag that lacks `width=device-width`. - All links appear to go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirector, for example the `/mo/` GIF path. Landing destinations can't be verified from the source provided.
- QA probed 12 of 45 links and skipped 33. The probe reported no failures, but 73% of links were not tested, including footer and social links.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_144220_2790595_155515.gif` has no `alt`. Its path and format suggest an open-tracking pixel, in which case this is low impact. That is an inference; if it is content, the missing `alt` is a real accessibility defect.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` has no `width=device-width`, which can cause inconsistent scaling in some clients.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values, so the browser drops these declarations. The `ctacr1` button gets no text color or border radius.

- **Visible media query:** The only `@media` query in the visible source is `max-width:300px`, which is an unusual breakpoint. The source is truncated, so other breakpoints may exist and I can't confirm a responsive problem.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional with XHTML and VML namespaces. This is the legacy pattern and is acceptable for email.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
