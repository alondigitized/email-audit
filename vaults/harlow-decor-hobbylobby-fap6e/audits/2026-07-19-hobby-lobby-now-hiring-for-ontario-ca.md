# Hobby Lobby Now Hiring for Ontario, CA
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a direct resend of the Ontario, CA hiring email first deployed on July 12 — same creative, same wage range ($18.50–$27.90/hr), same interview venue (Homes2 Suites by Hilton Ontario Airport), same bullet structure. The one meaningful change the calendar provides: interviews open tomorrow (July 20), which gives this resend a genuine last-push rationale even if the email doesn't say so explicitly.
- The email itself is clean and honest. The wage range is stated upfront, the interview logistics are specific enough to act on, and the APPLY NOW button is unmistakable. For the narrow audience of Ontario-area residents who were on the fence after the July 12 send, this is a functional nudge. For everyone else on the list — which is to say, the overwhelming majority of Hobby Lobby's retail shopper subscribers — it's an irrelevant repeat with no new hook to re-earn the open.
- The core problem hasn't changed from the first send: this is a staffing email dropped on a shopper list with no segmentation visible. Resending the same creative to the same un-segmented audience one week later, without updating the subject to signal urgency ("interviews start tomorrow"), wastes the only new thing this send has going for it.

## What's working

- **Wage range front-and-center.** $18.50–$27.90/hr is specific, competitive, and visible in the first scroll — no "competitive pay" euphemisms.
- **Interview logistics are complete.** Date window (July 20–24), hours (8am–5pm), and physical interview venue are all in the body. Someone can act on this without clicking away.
- **APPLY NOW button is clean.** High-contrast red on white, unambiguous verb, no surrounding clutter.
- **Timing is defensible.** Interviews start tomorrow — the resend has a real operational reason to exist even if the creative doesn't reflect it.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is identical to July 12.** A subscriber who opened that email and didn't apply now sees the same subject and has every reason to ignore it again. The only news — "interviews start tomorrow" — is buried in the body.
- **No segmentation signal.** This is a hiring email sent to what appears to be a shopper list. Unsubscribe risk goes up every time you hit the wrong audience twice.
- **Interview start urgency is invisible from the inbox.** The subject and any preheader have no "last chance," "starts Monday," or deadline hook despite the window opening in 24 hours.
- **SMS opt-in block feels out of place.** Pitching "Text JOIN to 48793 for sale alerts" inside a hiring email creates a jarring context switch — a job applicant isn't primed for promotional SMS.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to reflect that interviews open tomorrow.** This is the only competitive advantage this send has over the July 12 version. Use it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Interviews Start Monday — Apply Today, Ontario CA`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Chance: Ontario Store Hiring Event Starts July 20`
- 2. **Add a preheader that creates urgency without repeating the subject.**
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Walk-in interviews Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm at Homes2 Suites near Ontario Airport.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `$18.50–$27.90/hr · Full & Part-Time · No phone calls, just show up.`
- 3. **Segment the audience or suppress non-Ontario-area subscribers.** Even a rough geo-filter on CA zip codes would reduce churn from repeat irrelevant sends to shoppers on the East Coast.
- 4. **Remove the SMS sale-alert opt-in from the hiring email.** It's a distraction from the single action this email should drive (apply). Put it back in the promotional cadence where it belongs.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Flagged:**

- **`noreply@` sender address** (`noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`): CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) requires the From/Reply-To address to be a valid mailbox that can receive replies for at least 30 days post-send. A `noreply` address that auto-rejects is a common violation vector. Confirm the ESP is routing replies to a monitored inbox, or switch to a monitored address.
- **Unsubscribe mechanism**: Cannot confirm presence of a one-click unsubscribe link or `List-Unsubscribe` header from the truncated source. Full HTML must include a functional unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(6)) and ideally a `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>` header (RFC 8058 / Gmail bulk sender requirement as of 2024).
- **Physical mailing address**: CAN-SPAM §5(a)(7) requires a valid physical postal address. Cannot confirm from truncated source.
