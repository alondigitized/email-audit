# Shop 50% Off Fall & Christmas Now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again. The subject says "Shop 50% Off Fall & Christmas Now!" and the opening screen backs it up. A free-shipping bar and a Christmas Essentials banner sit above a big "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block, and a "Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts 50% off" block follows further down. That is a better subject-to-creative match than the 10/23 "Make It Festive With 50% Off Christmas" send, where the offer never appeared.
- The email is still a very long stack of department blocks with a different discount on each: 50%, 40%, 30%, 25%, 20%, 99¢. Every block carries a footnote like "Categories Listed," "Does not include Seasonal Department" or "Excludes 'Your Price' items." The reader has to hunt for what is actually discounted. It also runs from Christmas to football party to wearable art to furniture, so nothing is targeted. It's a competent circular but not a persuasive email.
- Cadence is the other issue. This is at least the 7th near-identical promo in 10 days (10/16, 10/18, 10/20 through 10/23, and now 10/25). The "50% off" headline has barely moved, and a subscriber has learned they can skip any one of these. Verdict: clear offer up top, buried by clutter below.

## What's working

- The subject's "50% Off Fall & Christmas" matches the first two big blocks, so the promise is visibly kept.
- The "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar is a concrete, honest benefit at the very top.
- The giant "50%" numerals in the Christmas and Fall Decor blocks are easy to spot.
- The "Sale Ends Saturday, October 31, 2026" line gives a real deadline.
- The seasonal mix (Christmas, football party, fall decor) fits late October.

## What's weak

- There are no clear buttons. The email is tiles and text links, with no single primary CTA.
- The long scroll mixes at least 8 different discounts (50, 40, 30, 25, 20% and 99¢), so the hierarchy collapses after the Christmas block.
- Heavy fine print ("Categories Listed," "Does not include Seasonal Department," "Excludes 'Your Price' items") undercuts how honest the 50% claim feels.
- The Christmas block lists 25% for light sets next to a 50% headline, which muddies the main offer.
- The deadline is at the very bottom, so it creates no urgency near the top.
- The content is repeated from earlier sends, so it adds to subscriber fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a hero plus 3–4 blocks. Lead with one 50% offer (Christmas or Fall, not both) and add a real button such as "Shop 50% Off Christmas Decor."
- 2. Move the "Ends Saturday 10/31" deadline into the top banner.
- 3. Vary the creative week to week. Seven near-identical sends in 10 days trains people to ignore them.
- 4. Put exclusions in a single line under the hero instead of repeating them in every block.
- 5. Segment by interest, such as crafters versus decorators, instead of sending the whole circular to everyone.
