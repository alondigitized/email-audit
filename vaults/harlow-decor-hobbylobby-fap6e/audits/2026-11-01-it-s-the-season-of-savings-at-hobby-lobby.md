# It’s The Season Of Savings At Hobby Lobby®️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, and the subject line is generic. "It's The Season Of Savings" promises nothing specific. The render is a very long scroll of category blocks, each with its own big discount number. The strongest read is that the email is a catalog, not a campaign. The top has a free-shipping bar ($69 or more) and a "Christmas Essentials" banner. A big "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block follows, then a run of 40% and 50% blocks: Home Decor, Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps, Fall, Football Party, Hobbies, Wearable Art, Books, Frames, Art, Paper Crafts, Fabric, Jewelry, Sewing, Custom Frames and Furniture.
- For a crafter, the breadth works in the brand's favor. Almost every aisle has a visible discount. The cost is hierarchy. With roughly 20 blocks all shouting "40%" or "50%", nothing is the hero, and the eye has nowhere to settle. This is the same pattern as the 10/23 and 10/25 sends, which led with Christmas 50%. This one has the same body under a softer subject.
- Judgement: dependable and on-brand, but it is a scroll-and-scan flyer. It will get opens from people already in the habit, and the subject does nothing to earn more. A sharper lead offer and a tighter layout would lift it.

## What's working

- The Christmas 50% block is large and sits near the top, so the headline offer is visible early.
- Each category states its discount as a big number ("ALWAYS 40% THE MARKED PRICE"), so the value is easy to read.
- Craft-specific items carry concrete prices: Super Value Pack Canvas at $9.99, Paper Crafts at $4.99, Sewing Notions at 99¢.
- The "Sale Valid Through Saturday, November 7, 2026" footer gives a real deadline.
- The brand header, free-shipping bar and app-store badges are consistent with earlier sends.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with around 20 stacked offers and no single hero.
- Repeated "40%" and "50%" badges blur together, which dilutes the urgency.
- The expiry date appears only in the footer, so the deadline is easy to miss.
- The "Categories Listed" blocks and the fine print ("Does not include Seasonal Department…") bury exclusions in small text.
- There is no clear primary button. The tiles act as CTAs but are not labeled as actions.
- The subject doesn't match the content, and no category or product is named.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero offer and a visible expiry, such as "50% off Christmas through Saturday". Cut the body to 5–6 blocks, or group the rest under a "More deals" strip.
- 2. Move the sale deadline into the header or hero.
- 3. Add an explicit button to the hero, for example "Shop Christmas 50% Off".
- 4. Move the exclusions into one consolidated note.
- 5. Have the subject name the hero offer and the date.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Christmas Ends Saturday 🎄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% Off Home Decor, Fabric & Art Supplies`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Christmas, fall and craft supplies marked down through Nov 7, plus free shipping on $69+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Canvas packs from $9.99, sewing notions at 99¢, and 30% off furniture this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, Outlook-aware (VML/MSO conditional) template sent through a `link.hobbylobby.com` tracking domain. The QA run had 0 failures and 4 warnings, and most of those warnings are relay or header-capture limitations rather than confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so only the `<head>` and the start of the CSS were reviewable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Limited probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 26 skipped (38 total). The skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, are unverified. The QA output reports no failures among the probed links.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs:** 11,050 of 12,592 characters (about 88%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. Long tracking-wrapped URLs in the text/plain alternative hurt readability for text-only clients and can add a spam-filter signal.
- **Tracking image:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_142475_2827809_152893.gif` is flagged as an image. The `/mo/` path and opaque ID suggest an open-tracking pixel, but that is inferred and not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the same `.gif` has no `alt` attribute. If it is a tracking pixel the impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. If it is a content image, it needs real alt text.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values. The declarations are invalid and dropped, so that CTA class gets no text color or radius.
  - `.lhp2 {… verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo, so `vertical-align` is never applied.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the visible media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. That is below the width of nearly all phones (320px or wider), so the responsive rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.h1`…) would rarely or never fire. Later queries were cut off, so confirm in the full source.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` has no `width=device-width`. This matters mostly in clients that honor the tag.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns` attributes (XHTML/VML namespaces). It is common in email and low risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The QA tool notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If it is truly absent, it conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe may not be supported. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed, and a relay-stripped header is the expected cause. Verify against raw headers at the receiving MTA.
- **Body-level unsubscribe and postal address:** the QA output says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set and reports no failure. The footer address was not visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.
- The sender is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`. A no-reply sender is not a CAN-SPAM violation, but it gives replies nowhere to go.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The visible source contains no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and links are wrapped by `link.hobbylobby.com`. The QA output reports no probe failures but doesn't give final landing URLs or query strings. UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) in the raw headers at the sending MTA. If they are missing, add them.
2. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `create.hobbylobby.com` against the original message headers, since the relay hid them.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking GIF, or meaningful alt text if it is a content image.
4. Fix the CSS: fill in `.ctacr1` (`color`, `border-radius`) and correct `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
5. Raise the mobile breakpoint to about 600px, or confirm later media queries do so.
6. Add `width=device-width` to the viewport meta.
7. Shorten or simplify the text/plain part, for example with fewer or untracked URLs, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
8. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and all 38 links probed to cover UTMs, landing pages and the footer address.
