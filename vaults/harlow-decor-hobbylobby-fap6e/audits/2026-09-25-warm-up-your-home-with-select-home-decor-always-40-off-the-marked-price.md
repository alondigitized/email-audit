# Warm Up Your Home With Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Same wall-to-wall category-tile playbook Hobby Lobby has been running since late August, just with a "Cold Weather Retreat" video hero swapped in at the top. There is a real hero this time — a styled cozy bedroom scene with pillows, throws, and framed art — but it's immediately buried under roughly 20+ stacked category tiles (Doormats, Sheet Sets, Bath Towels, Curtains, Quilt Sets, Pillows, Framed Art, Wall Clocks...) before you even reach a percentage-off badge. The subject promises "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off" but that offer doesn't visually surface until deep in the scroll, competing with Christmas (50%), Fall (40%), Football Party (40%), Fabric (30%), and Jewelry Making (40%) — five other discounts crammed into one email.
- This is nearly identical in structure to 2026-09-17's "Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off The Marked Price" send and 2026-09-20's "Shop & Save On Seasonal Essentials," both scored 4/10 for the same reason: real creative at the top, then an undifferentiated tile dump. The cadence problem is real — this is the fourth near-identical grid layout in six sends.
- Judgement: functional but lazy. The hero video and cozy styling show someone put effort into a seasonal moment, but the email drowns it in the same generic tile wall instead of building on it.

## What's working

- The "Cold Weather Retreat" hero has genuine styling — a cozy bedroom vignette with pillows, throws, and framed art that matches the subject's home-decor promise.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) is clearly visible at the very top, above the fold.
- Category thumbnails are clean, well-lit product photography with consistent framing.
- Sale end date ("Valid Through Saturday, September 26, 2026") is clearly stated near the footer.

## What's weak

- The subject's "40% Off Home Decor" offer is not visually reinforced until far down the email — no badge or callout near the hero.
- Six competing discounts (Christmas 50%, Fall 40%, Party 40%, Football 40%, Fabric 30%, Jewelry 40%) dilute any single offer and create decision paralysis.
- The layout is nearly identical to the 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-20 sends — subscribers who opened those will see no differentiation.
- No single, unambiguous primary CTA — there are 15+ "In-Store Or Online ▶" links of equal visual weight.
- The hero is a video placeholder (play button) rather than a clickable static offer, adding friction for anyone who can't/won't play video in an email client.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a "40% Off Home Decor" badge directly on or next to the Cold Weather Retreat hero so the subject line's promise is visible without scrolling — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Cut the six-way discount competition down to 2 offers max (Home Decor + one seasonal tie-in); move the rest to a "more ways to save" strip.
- 3. Replace the video-only hero with a static image + button fallback so non-video clients get a working CTA immediately.
- 4. Vary the layout skeleton from the last two sends — even reordering categories or changing the tile grid to a 2-column-then-1-column rhythm would break the sameness.
- 5. Consolidate the 15+ identical "In-Store Or Online" links into a single strong CTA button under the hero, with category tiles as secondary navigation below.
