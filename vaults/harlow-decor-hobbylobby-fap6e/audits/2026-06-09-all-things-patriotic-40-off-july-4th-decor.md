# All Things Patriotic 🇺🇸 40% Off July 4th Decor!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's eighteenth-or-so email in roughly as many days, and it arrives as the second July 4th-focused send in 48 hours — June 7's "Sale Alert 🚨 40% Off July 4th Decor Starts This Week!" already established the offer. The patriotic imagery in the hero (red, white, and blue stars and seasonal decor) is appropriate and the 40% off callout is prominent. The killing detail: the hero banner still reads **"Spring Essentials"** — carryover text from the prior campaign cycle — which is the first thing a subscriber reads after clicking a subject line that says "All Things Patriotic." That single copy artifact undermines every good thing the hero is trying to do.
- Below the fold, it's the same unbroken vertical discount grid — Home Decor 40%, Spring Shop® 50%, Summer Toys 50%, Classroom Decor 40%, Floral 40%, Paper Crafts 40%, Online Clearance 75% — that has appeared in every prior send. "Items Labeled The Spring Shop®" is actively competing with the patriotic theme in the same email. Hobby Lobby has the right seasonal product and timing; the execution discipline to make it a tight patriotic story is absent.
- The cadence is now a subscriber-trust issue. Two July 4th emails in two days signals template rotation, not purposeful marketing. The 40% offer is starting to feel like wallpaper.

## What's working

- **40% off July 4th offer leads the hero and is immediately visible** — the discount badge and SHOP NOW button land in the eye's first path.
- **Free shipping on orders $39+ is at the very top** — a real conversion driver for multi-item home decor purchases.
- **Subject line is the sharpest July 4th hook in the series** — "All Things Patriotic 🇺🇸 40% Off July 4th Decor!" is concrete, timely, and comes in under 50 characters.
- **Online Clearance at 75% off** — the deepest discount in the grid; visible and credible as a separate click driver.
- **July 4th hero imagery is seasonally correct** — the patriotic styled decor reads clearly and is well-matched to the campaign calendar.

## What's weak

- **"Spring Essentials" dominates the hero headline** — a subscriber who just opened "All Things Patriotic" reads "Spring Essentials" first; the subject line promise is broken before they scroll.
- **"Items Labeled The Spring Shop®" at 50% off appears in the discount grid** — spring-branded merchandise actively competes with the patriotic theme in the same send.
- **SHOP NOW is a generic CTA** — "Shop July 4th Decor" would reinforce the theme and reduce the gap between subject and click destination.
- **Second identical-discount July 4th push in 48 hours** — no new hook, no new hero product, no reason to act now versus two days ago.
- **No single featured product** — no item name, no price anchor, no detail that makes this send a reason to browse rather than archive.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix "Spring Essentials" to "All Things Patriotic" in the hero** — this is the single highest-impact change, requires one template copy update, and eliminates the most visible credibility break.
- 2. **Feature one hero product with name and price** — pick a bestselling patriotic piece (wreath, flag banner, tabletop) and anchor the hero to it so this send has a different reason to click than June 7's.
- 3. **Remove Spring Shop® from the July 4th send** — suppress or replace that department block with a Patriotic Tabletop or Outdoor Decor section to make the grid coherent with the subject.
- 4. **Add an explicit countdown or deadline** — "25 days until July 4th" or "sale ends June 29" gives the 40% offer a visible end date and distinguishes it from an always-on promo.
- 5. **Rewrite the primary CTA to "Shop Patriotic Decor"** — ties subject → hero → click in a consistent thread.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Patriotic Decor 40% Off — 25 Days to July 4th`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stars, Stripes & 40% Off — Shop Before July 4th`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wreaths, flags, tabletop & more — free shipping on $39+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on red, white & blue before the rush — sale ends soon`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Hobby Lobby: "All Things Patriotic" (June 2026)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard ESP-rendered table layout with four confirmed technical defects: two malformed CSS declarations, a missing plain-text body that inflates URL ratio, and absent authentication/unsubscribe headers at the relay layer. No catastrophic rendering blockers, but the compliance gaps carry deliverability and legal risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Tracking pixel missing alt attribute**
The open-tracking GIF has no `alt` text:
```
src: https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_126217_470940_135172.gif
```
Strict accessibility validators and some spam filters flag undeclared tracking pixels. Set `alt=""` explicitly.

**Plain-text part is URL-saturated**
QA reports 10,907 of 13,037 plain-text characters (84%) are URLs — well above the 70% warning threshold. This indicates the plain-text alternative was auto-generated by stripping HTML tags, leaving only raw link URLs. Spam filters (SpamAssassin, Proofpoint) weight this negatively. A properly authored plain-text part should contain human-readable copy with only destination URLs, not tracking redirects.

**All click links route through `link.hobbylobby.com`**
Consistent use of a dedicated click-tracking subdomain is expected. No bare redirect issues observed in the truncated source.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed `border-radius` on CTA button (`ctacr1`)**
```css
.ctacr1 {
  -moz-border-radius:px;
  -webkit-border-radius:px;
  border-radius:px;
}
```
All three values are `px` with no numeric prefix — the unit is present but the value is missing. This is a CSS authoring bug. The rule is silently ignored by all clients, so the CTA will render with no border-radius. Likely was meant to be `3px` or `4px` matching `.tcta4br`.

**Typo in `.lhp2` vertical-align**
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font {line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle;}
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid CSS property. The declaration is dropped silently.

**Responsive breakpoint is too narrow**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width:300px)
```
The mobile breakpoint fires at 300px. Modern phones render at 375px (iPhone SE) to 430px+. The fluid/responsive layout changes (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`) will not apply to any current smartphone, effectively making this a desktop-only layout on mobile. Standard practice is `max-width:480px` or `max-width:600px`.

**Custom font `Museo Sans` in web font stack**
`.ff3ng` lists `Museo Sans` as the primary face. No `@font-face` or `<link>` declaration is present in the `<head>`. All email clients that block web fonts (Gmail, Outlook) will fall through to `Trebuchet MS` — acceptable, but the fallback should be tested visually since weight/metrics differ.

**ESP attribute leakage**
```html
<html ... e-locale="en-US" e-is-multilanguage="false">
```
These are ExactTarget/SFMC internal rendering attributes. They are harmless but visible to anyone inspecting source; not a security issue, just noise.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `%%firstName%%`, `{{name}}`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent at relay layer**
```
[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found: Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay
[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)
```
Gmail and Yahoo have required `List-Unsubscribe` (mailto or HTTPS) and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` for bulk senders since February 2024. Absence of these headers at the inbound relay means either (a) the relay strips them before delivery to the audit mailbox, or (b) they were never set. If (b), this is a deliverability and inbox-placement issue with Gmail/Yahoo for bulk volume. Confirm with a raw header dump from a direct inbox capture.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent**
```
[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found: SPF/DKIM status unknown
```
Cannot confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass without this header. AgentMail relay may not be forwarding authentication results. Run a parallel test with direct delivery to a Gmail or Outlook inbox and check the `Authentication-Results` header there.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe body copy**
Not verifiable from the truncated HTML. The footer section where these are required was cut off. Assumed present given Hobby Lobby's established program, but should be confirmed in a full-source audit.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters not verifiable from truncated source**
Click links pass through `link.hobbylobby.com` redirects. The final destination URLs and their UTM parameters are not inspectable without resolving the redirects. A full link-resolution pass should confirm:
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on all destination URLs
- `utm_campaign` value is consistent and matches the campaign slug (e.g., `july4-patriotic-2026`)

No obviously broken or mismatched landing page references observed in what was provided.
