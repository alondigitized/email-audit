# Add A Burst Of Color – 50% Off The Spring Shop®
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's third Spring Shop send this month — following June 17's "50% Off The Spring Shop® Crafts" almost exactly, with a softer headline swap ("Add A Burst Of Color") and the same hero mechanics. The hero executes well: lush, colorful imagery with a prominent 50% badge and a clear SHOP NOW button. Above the fold, this is one of Hobby Lobby's tighter sends.
- The wheels fall off immediately after the Spring Shop product thumbnails. A jarring "CLICK HERE TO WIN OUR PRIZE!" contest banner breaks the flow, then the email reverts to the familiar seven-department mega-digest: Home Decor 40%, July 4th 40%, Summer Toys 50%, Classroom Decor 40%, Hobbies 40%, All Yarn 40%, Online Clearance 75%. It's the same circular format that earned the June 18 and June 21 sends a 5/10.
- The real credibility problem is fatigue: a subscriber who opened June 17's Spring Shop email is looking at the same offer, same category, same "50% off labeled items" mechanic — six days later. The July 4th section (actually timely at June 23, one week out) is buried mid-digest when it deserves its own dedicated send.

## What's working

- Hero image is vivid and on-brand — color-forward photography reinforces "burst of color" copy without competing text clutter.
- The 50% badge and "Items Labeled The Spring Shop" qualifier are both legible and honest — no bait-and-switch ambiguity.
- SHOP NOW CTA is immediately visible in the hero, above the fold.
- Free shipping threshold banner ($59+) is persistent at top — removes a common checkout friction objection.
- Spring product thumbnails below the hero (Stickers, Decor, Baskets, Crafts) give a concrete taste of what's in the Spring Shop before any click.

## What's weak

- Near-identical to June 17's Spring Shop send — same offer, same structure, no escalation or urgency to distinguish it for repeat openers.
- "CLICK HERE TO WIN OUR PRIZE!" contest banner interrupts the Spring Shop flow with a tonally mismatched, low-trust pattern.
- Spring Shop hero lands on June 23 — two days after summer begins; the seasonal premise has already expired.
- July 4th section (the most time-relevant item in this email) is buried in the mega-digest rather than featured or even sub-headlined.
- No deadline or end-date anywhere — a subscriber has zero reason to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add urgency with a real end-date.** "Spring Shop 50% off — ends Sunday" would do more to move clicks than any copy change. Without it, every Hobby Lobby promo feels evergreen and therefore deferrable.
- 2. **Break the July 4th section out entirely.** At June 23, July 4th decor is the highest-intent category in the store. It's currently a small thumbnail in row six of the digest; it should be its own dedicated send this week.
- 3. **Retire the Spring Shop hero or reframe it seasonally.** If this must run, "Clear Out Spring — Make Room for Summer" messaging at least explains *why* it's still on sale. The current "burst of color" framing ignores that spring ended.
- 4. **Remove or demote the contest banner.** The "WIN OUR PRIZE" interrupt breaks narrative flow and reads like a third-party insert. Move it to the footer or cut it entirely.
- 5. **Collapse the mega-digest.** Pick two departments to feature below the hero; the seven-section circular kills click focus.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spring Shop Clearance — 50% Off, Final Week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: Spring Shop 50% Off Before Summer Takes Over`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Labeled items only — colors selling fast before the season flips`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up now — Spring Shop prices drop off after this weekend`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on a standard ESP (Salesforce/ExactTarget-family, based on `e-locale`/`e-is-multilanguage` attributes and `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect domain) using legacy HTML 4.01 Transitional with full MSO conditional blocks. ### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Plain-text part is URL-dominated**
QA reports 12,346 of 14,574 characters (84.7%) in the plain-text alternative are URLs. This is a known spam-filter trigger for providers using Bayesian analysis (Gmail, SpamAssaus). The plain-text part should be a human-readable summary with links, not a raw URL dump.

**Redirect domain confirmed active**
All CTA links route through `link.hobbylobby.com` — ESP click-tracking is in place. No bare/untracked links observed in the truncated source.

**Tracking pixel missing alt text**
`https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_127544_395948_136420.gif` has no `alt` attribute. For a tracking pixel this is low risk functionally, but it will trigger accessibility linters and may produce a visible broken-image indicator in clients with images blocked before the `alt` value renders.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS bug — `.ctacr1` empty `color` value**
```css
.ctacr1 {color:; font-weight:normal; ... background-color:#013088; border-radius:px;}
```
`color:;` is an invalid declaration and will be dropped by all parsers. If this class is applied to CTA link text, the foreground color will fall through to the inherited value (potentially invisible against `#013088` background).

**CSS bug — `.ctacr1` malformed `border-radius`**
```css
-moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;
```
All three `border-radius` values are missing the numeric component. These declarations are silently ignored, so the button will render with square corners instead of the intended rounded corners across all clients.

**CSS typo — `.lhp2` `vertical-align` misspelled**
```css
.lhp2, .lhp2 font {line-height:1.2; verticl-align:middle;}
```
`verticl-align` is not a valid CSS property. Vertical centering on `.lhp2` elements will not apply.

**Responsive breakpoint truncated**
The `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)` block is cut off in the source excerpt. This is an artifact of the truncation, not a confirmed bug — but worth verifying in the full source that the block is properly closed.

**MSO/VML namespace declared**
`xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o` are present — appropriate for Outlook VML rendering. No issue.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
QA confirms the header is absent from what AgentMail relayed. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (effective Feb 2024) for senders above 5,000/day. If Hobby Lobby sends at that volume and the header is genuinely missing (vs. stripped by the relay), this is a deliverability-affecting compliance gap.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured**
One-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 is missing. Gmail and Yahoo require this for bulk senders. Same caveat about relay stripping applies — verify against raw headers from a seed inbox.

**`Authentication-Results` header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. The sending domain is `create.hobbylobby.com` (subdomain of `hobbylobby.com`). If DKIM alignment is not configured for the subdomain, DMARC may fail even if SPF passes. Recommend pulling raw headers from a seed address to confirm.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
Not visible in the truncated source — assumed present in the footer. Cannot confirm or deny from the excerpt.
