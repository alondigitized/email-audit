# Spice Up Your Fall With 40% Off 🍂
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises fall, but the email opens on Christmas. The first screen is a "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" bar, a "Christmas Essentials" banner and a big "CHRISTMAS 50% off" block. The actual fall tile, "Fall Decor, Party, Floral & Crafts" at 40% off, is buried about a quarter of the way down, under Home Decor, Textiles and Mirrors & Lamps. Someone who opened for "Spice Up Your Fall" has to scroll past three unrelated sections to find it.
- Below that it's the usual Hobby Lobby weekly-ad wall: more than 20 stacked category tiles, with 40% off nearly everywhere. Football Party, Floral, Hobbies, Crafts, Frames, Paper Crafts and Jewelry Making are all at 40%. Yarn is 30%, and Custom Frames and Mirrors & Lamps are 50%. Each tile also carries small-print exclusions. There is a credible deadline in the footer, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 17, 2026". Nothing in the email says "spice", and no single product or offer is the star.
- Judgement: a competent circular with a mismatched subject line. It's the same wall as 10/8 ("Select Home Decor – Always 40% Off") and 10/5 ("Discover Your Christmas Essentials Here!"). The 10/6 "Scents Of The Season With 40% Off Fall" send at least had a fall hero. This one doesn't.

## What's working

- The footer states a clear end date, "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 17, 2026", which gives a credible deadline.
- The free-shipping threshold ($59) sits in a green bar at the top, where it's easy to see.
- Percent-off badges are big and consistent, so a skimmer can see the discount level in each tile.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.
- The brand look is consistent and instantly recognizable.

## What's weak

- The subject says fall, but the hero is Christmas at 50% off. The fall tile is far below it.
- The email is more than 9,000 px tall, with 20+ tiles at nearly the same weight. Nothing is the hero, and the page works as a catalogue rather than a pitch.
- No visible CTA buttons. The tiles just list categories, and there's no "Shop Fall Decor" verb-plus-noun action.
- Fine print and exclusions are everywhere ("Does not include Seasonal Department", "Does not include candy or food"), which weakens the sense of an honest offer.
- It repeats the cadence of the 10/5, 10/7 and 10/8 sends, so it reads as the fourth near-identical tile wall in a week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the fall tile the hero to match the subject. Lead with Fall Decor, Floral and Candles at 40% off, one image and one button, and push the Christmas block below it.
- 2. Cut the wall to about six tiles. Keep the categories that fit the subject and drop Yarn, Sewing Notions, Furniture and Custom Frames from this send.
- 3. Add a real button on the hero, such as "Shop Fall Decor 40% Off".
- 4. Put the deadline near the top ("Ends Saturday 10/17") instead of only in the footer.
- 5. Vary the creative week to week so it doesn't look like a repeat of 10/8.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Fall Porch Is 40% Off Until 10/17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wreaths, florals & candles marked down through Saturday, plus free shipping at $59.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Christmas is 50% off, so you can start both seasons now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run shows no hard failures (0 issues, 4 warnings), but the checks that matter most for compliance and authentication could not be verified. The visible HTML source has a few real code defects, including a viewport meta tag without a width and a responsive breakpoint that phones won't reach. The HTML is truncated, so findings below are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed links reported no failures. 28 were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so those are unverified.
- The image `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_137949_4046560_149245.gif` is served from the click-tracking domain's `/mo/` path. It is most likely an open-tracking pixel. If so, it needs no meaningful alt text, but `alt=""` would still be correct.
- The plain-text part is 11,312 of 12,845 characters URLs (about 88%). The text alternative is mostly long tracking-wrapped URLs, which is a minor spam-scoring risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` has no `width=device-width`. Some mobile clients may fall back to a desktop-width layout.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** the only `@media` query visible is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Typical phones are 320–430px wide, so the mobile rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.mh`, `.image`, and so on) likely never apply. The source is truncated, so later media queries may exist.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; … -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values.
  - `.lhp2 … verticl-align:middle;` is a typo for `vertical-align`.
  - Clients ignore these declarations, so the intended style is lost.
- **Language:** `<html>` has a non-standard `e-locale="en-US"` attribute but no `lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the language from it.
- **Doctype:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with XHTML, VML and Office namespaces. This is common in email and low risk, but inconsistent.
- **Alt text:** `eORqXTkAb7_837015858_137949_4046560_149245.gif` is missing alt text (the QA warning).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source or the subject line, "Spice Up Your Fall With 40% Off 🍂".

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `create.hobbylobby.com` is a subdomain, and alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer:** the visible HTML doesn't show the physical postal address or the unsubscribe link markup. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result was reported, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links are wrapped in the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect domain. Final destination URLs and any UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source or the QA output, so continuity can't be assessed.
- No landing-page mismatches were reported among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the viewport meta to `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`.
2. Check that the media queries cover typical phone widths (about 480–600px), not only `max-width:300px`.
3. Fix the invalid CSS: fill in or remove the empty `color:` and `border-radius:px` values, and correct `verticl-align`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` at the origin using raw headers captured outside the relay.
6. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the raw message.
7. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a postal address.
8. Capture the redirect chain of the primary CTAs to confirm the final URLs and UTM parameters.
