# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expect from Hobby Lobby — clean, no-nonsense, and loud about the discount. The 50% off Spring Shop push is the whole show above the fold, which I appreciate. It's a practical, deal-first homepage, which suits how I actually shop here: I come in knowing what's on the rotating calendar, and I want confirmation fast.

## What's working

- **50% Off The Spring Shop callout** — Big, bold, centered. I know exactly what category is on sale this week. This is literally why I check the site.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026"** — I'm a skeptic of digital-only promos, so seeing "In-Store" explicitly called out removes my biggest hesitation. I can bring this deal to the physical shelf.
- **Hero image** — The woven charger, ceramic fish plate, and picnic spread aesthetic lands squarely in my wheelhouse. Someone in the merch team knows their Farmhouse Spring customer.
- **Weekly Ad button** — That's the first thing I look for every time I open this site. It's right there, blue pill button, above the fold. Muscle memory satisfied.
- **Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!** — Threshold is stated plainly; I can do the math in my head on whether my cart qualifies.

## What's weak

- **No rewards or loyalty hook anywhere above the fold** — I've been shopping here for years. There's no acknowledgment that I exist as a returning customer. No points balance, no "sign in to see your savings," nothing.
- **"The Spring Shop®" is vague** — What's in it? Floral? Decor? Fabric? The category name doesn't tell me if my faux florals or farmhouse shelving pieces are included. I'm clicking hoping, not clicking knowing.
- **No navigation category shortcuts visible** — On mobile, the hamburger menu is there but it's collapsed. I can't see Floral, Home Decor, or Seasonal at a glance. One more tap between me and my aisle.
- **Hero image is overhead/flat lay, no depth** — The aerial food-styling shot feels more Pinterest-board than "shop this look." I can't see price tags, product names, or a clear tap-to-shop path.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Faux Floral" or "Shop Home Decor" shortcut strip directly below the hero** — even 3–4 category icon pills so I don't have to dig through the hamburger menu on mobile.
- **Replace "The Spring Shop®" subhead with one example product** — e.g., "Wreaths, Planters, Seasonal Decor & More" so I know the 50% hits my categories before I tap.
- **Surface the rewards/account link more prominently** — Move the person icon in the nav or add a "Sign in for rewards" line under the CTA so loyal repeat customers feel seen.
- **Swap the aerial food flat-lay for a lifestyle shot of a farmhouse mantel or front porch vignette** — I'd feel it in my chest and tap immediately. The current image reads "summer entertaining," not "home decor."
