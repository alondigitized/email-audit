# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby is leading with their Spring Shop and a solid 50% off banner — classic HL move that I actually appreciate because I know this rotating sale calendar better than my own grocery list. The page feels seasonal and clean, but the overhead picnic flatlay gives more "coastal grandmother hosting brunch" than the farmhouse warmth I'm really here for. Still, this brand knows their core customer and doesn't waste my time.

## What's working

- **"50% OFF" in giant type** — the first thing my eye lands on after the hero image. That's the Hobby Lobby I know; they've always been good at leading with the deal.
- **Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More** — right in the top banner, first thing I see. I always spend enough to hit that, so it feels like a genuine perk, not a tease.
- **Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 23, 2026** — end date spelled out below the CTA. No vague "limited time" language, no mystery.
- **Weekly Ad button** — prominent in the secondary nav bar. I use this every single week and I appreciate that it's not buried.
- **Clean mobile render** — one column, no clutter, nothing fighting for attention. It loads looking like a real page.

## What's weak

- **Hero image skews coastal/entertaining, not farmhouse** — blue fish plates and a picnic spread are charming, but I came here for cotton stems, chipboard frames, and dried pampas arrangements. This doesn't speak my language.
- **No category navigation without the hamburger** — I have to tap that menu to find Floral, Frames, or Home Decor. A quick-tap row below the hero would get me to my aisle in one motion.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment** — I spend real money here every week and there's no nod to that relationship above the fold. Even a simple "Sign In" with a hint at exclusive deals would help.
- **"See Weekly Ad For Details" asterisk on the main offer** — I know the category exclusions game by heart, but this introduces a hesitation on what is otherwise a clean, confident offer.
- **No faux floral or farmhouse imagery whatsoever** — my specific category is completely invisible above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the picnic flatlay for a farmhouse lifestyle shot** — a shiplap backdrop with a pampas arrangement, a grapevine wreath, and a mason jar or two is on-brand AND speaks directly to Hobby Lobby's core decor buyer.
- **Add a horizontal quick-category strip below the hero** — four tappable icons: Floral | Seasonal | Frames | Yarn. One tap to my aisle, no hamburger digging required.
- **Make the "ends today" urgency explicit** — the sale date is there but it's quiet. A small "Last Day" badge or a subtle "ends midnight tonight" line would turn a passive read into an action trigger.
- **Surface the Weekly Ad preview visually** — the button is there, but a small thumbnail preview ("This week: 50% off Floral") would pull in returning regulars before they even have to tap.
