# Bring Great Joy By Packing A Shoebox!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a cause-marketing send for Samaritan's Purse Operation Christmas Child, and it is off-pattern for Hobby Lobby. The last six sends were tile walls and percent-off banners (10/9 gift cards, 10/8 "Always 40% Off", 10/7 Christmas Shop). Here there is no discount, no product grid and no price. The hero is a smiling child carrying a shoebox on their head beside a red "Send Good News & Great Joy!" block. Below it is a clean three-step story: Find a Shoebox, Fill With Gifts, Drop-Off.
- The layout works. Alternating red and green bands, one idea per band and one button per band make it very scannable, and the emotional hero is the strongest image Hobby Lobby has sent this week. The weakness is commercial. A subscriber who isn't already part of the shoebox tradition has no reason to act now, and the only date, National Collection Week (Nov 16-23), is buried in the last band.
- Verdict: a well-built, warm and different send that will perform for the people who already pack shoeboxes. It is thin as a revenue driver, because nothing in it tells you what to buy or what it costs.

## What's working

- The hero photo is a real, joyful face and the image is large. It breaks the visual monotony of the recent tile-wall sends.
- The three-step flow (Find, Fill, Drop-Off) is easy to follow, and each step has its own button.
- Alternating red and green bands give a clear rhythm and keep the long scroll skimmable.
- The "Pack A Shoebox" banner under the hero gives a plain-language CTA anchor.
- The subject is 37 characters, mobile-friendly and not shouty. It matches the hero's "Great Joy" language.

## What's weak

- No product, price or offer is visible. "Shop Now" under "Fill With Gifts" sends people to something undefined, and there is no 40% off or coupon link to a shoebox-filler category.
- The collection deadline (November 16-23, 2026) is only in the bottom band. It is the only urgency in the email and it is hidden.
- The CTA copy is mixed. "Learn More", "In-Store Or Online" and "Shop Now" are generic or oddly worded, and "In-Store Or Online" reads as a location rather than an action.
- The hero's red box is oversized and tall. Its "Learn More" button is small and low-contrast (navy on red) compared to the headline.
- Samaritan's Purse is only a faint watermark on the hero photo, so the partnership credit is almost invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline and an offer in the hero: "Pack a shoebox by Nov 23 — fillers 40% off." This single change lifts both the click and the revenue case.
- 2. Make "Fill With Gifts" a shoppable band with three or four named items (a soccer ball, school supplies, hygiene items) and prices. The unicorn photo is cute but doesn't link to anything specific.
- 3. Standardize the CTAs as verb + noun: "Find A Shoebox", "Shop Shoebox Fillers", "Find A Drop-Off". Make the hero button larger and white-on-navy.
- 4. Name Samaritan's Purse and Operation Christmas Child in visible text near the top, not just in the photo watermark.
- 5. Add one line of social proof, such as "Join millions of families packing shoeboxes." That would speak to trust.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pack a Shoebox by Nov 23 — Fillers 40% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One Shoebox, One Child, Real Joy`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Drop-off week is Nov 16–23. Find a box, fill it, and find your nearest location.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soccer balls, school supplies and stuffed animals — everything to fill a shoebox, in store or online.`
