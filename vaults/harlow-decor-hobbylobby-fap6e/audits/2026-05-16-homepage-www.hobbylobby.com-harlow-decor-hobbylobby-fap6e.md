# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the Hobby Lobby I know by heart — seasonal 50%-off sale front and center, no noise, no gimmicks. I could've told you this page was up before I even tapped the link, because I track this rotation week to week. It's a returning-shopper-forward homepage that speaks directly to my wallet, not to someone discovering the brand for the first time.

## What's working

- **The 50% OFF headline:** Black on white, enormous type, zero clutter. I knew what week it was the instant the page loaded. This is exactly how Hobby Lobby communicates with people who already know the game.
- **The hero image:** Ceramic goose, pumpkin planter, birdcage, florals against a white picket fence backdrop — that is farmhouse aesthetic, not a generic spring photo. Someone curated this for me specifically.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 16, 2026":** I am genuinely skeptical of website-only deals. Seeing "In-Store" right there on the page makes the offer feel real to me, not like a digital bait-and-switch.
- **Weekly Ad button in the nav:** Right there at the top, no hunting. I pull this up every Sunday anyway — good placement for regulars.
- **Free shipping at $59:** Visible without scrolling. Easy to hit on any decor run.

## What's weak

- **The asterisk:** "*See Weekly Ad For Details" means something's excluded and I have to go read the fine print before I know if my faux stems are actually in the deal. The big 50% OFF number raised my expectations; the asterisk immediately lowers them.
- **No loyalty or rewards callout:** I visit every week. My rewards balance should be somewhere on this screen — even a generic "Check your rewards" link would tell me I'm recognized as a regular, not a stranger.
- **"Shop Now" is too generic:** It doesn't tell me whether faux floral, farmhouse, or seasonal decor is included. "Shop Spring Decor" would pull me faster.
- **No people, no lived-in context:** The product vignette is pretty, but it's objects floating on a tabletop. Show the goose on a farmhouse porch and the basket of stems in a kitchen, and I'm already mentally redecorating my entryway.
- **Sale expiring today:** "Through May 16, 2026" is today's date. That creates urgency, but it also means I probably missed most of the week's selection. That's a mild frustration, not a neutral fact.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a rewards hook in the header:** A persistent "Your Rewards Balance: $X" or even "Sign in to see your rewards" strip would make returning weekly shoppers feel like VIPs instead of anonymous visitors.
- **Rename the CTA to a specific category:** "Shop Spring Decor" or "Shop Faux Floral" — if faux floral is in the 50% rotation, that single word would stop my thumb cold.
- **Swap the product vignette for a lifestyle scene:** Show these pieces *in* a farmhouse setting — on a mantel, a kitchen shelf, a front-porch table. Aspirational context sells the basket, not just the individual item.
- **Replace the asterisk with a brief exclusions callout:** Instead of "*See Weekly Ad For Details," show the top three included categories inline. That way I know immediately if I'm in or out, and I don't have to go dig through a PDF.
