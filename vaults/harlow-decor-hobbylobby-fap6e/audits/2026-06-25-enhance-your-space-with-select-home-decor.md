# Enhance Your Space With Select Home Decor
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the Hobby Lobby mega-circular in its purest form — a subject line that promises "Enhance Your Space With Select Home Decor" and delivers fifteen-plus department promotions stacked top-to-bottom like a printed circular. The hero does open on Home Decor 40% off, so the subject isn't a lie, but the contract breaks immediately: by the second scroll you're past Textiles, The Spring Shop, Furniture, Classroom Decor, Party, Floral, Wearable Art, and Custom Frames. Home Decor was the bait; the whole store is the email.
- Two things stand out as particularly poor for a late-June send. The Spring Shop 50% Off section appears *again* — this is its third appearance in eight days (June 17, June 23, June 25). Summer officially began four days ago; Spring Shop fatigue is real. Meanwhile, Classroom Decor 40% Off shows up on June 25, echoing the premature Back-to-School send from June 19 that earned a 5/10 for the same reason.
- The July 4th section is the one timely bright spot. The rest is the same dense tile grid that has been Hobby Lobby's default format in every send this month, and it increasingly reads as copy-paste circular production rather than intentional email marketing.

## What's working

- **Subject partially delivers:** Hero section does open on Home Decor 40% off, so the top of the email honors the subject's promise.
- **July 4th peg:** Including a July 4th section on June 25 is genuinely timely and gives a natural reason to open and buy now.
- **Breadth as browse:** For a habitual Hobby Lobby shopper, the mega-circular format does work as a scan-everything digest — any one of those fifteen tiles might catch something they need.
- **Dual home decor tiers:** Offering both a 40% and 50% off home decor section gives budget-range optionality for that category specifically.

## What's weak

- **Subject promise vs. email reality:** "Enhance Your Space With Select Home Decor" implies a curated home-decor-focused send; the email is the full store. The mismatch erodes trust over repeated sends.
- **Spring Shop fatigue:** Third Spring Shop 50% off tile in eight days. It's June 25. The Spring Shop positioning feels stale and repetitive at this point in the season.
- **No single primary CTA:** Every section has its own shop-now path; there's no dominant visual hierarchy pulling focus to one action.
- **Classroom Decor is still premature:** July 4th hasn't happened yet — Classroom Decor landing on the same scroll feels jarring and out-of-season.
- **Two Home Decor sections (40% and 50%) with no visible explanation:** The price split appears confusing without any visible rationale for which products qualify for which tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Narrow the email to match the subject.** If the subject promises Home Decor, ship a Home Decor-focused email: hero, two or three specific product categories (textiles, frames, furniture), and one CTA. Move the mega-circular format to a Sunday digest slot and let focused sends drive higher CTR on target days.
- 2. **Retire the Spring Shop placement.** By June 25, spring-labeled sections erode brand freshness. Convert those tiles to a Summer Refresh or Patio & Garden frame.
- 3. **Add a visible sale-end date to the hero.** "40% Off — This Week Only" or "Ends Sunday" would create the urgency the subject completely lacks.
- 4. **Explain the 40% vs. 50% Home Decor split.** A single line ("40% on accents, 50% on wall décor") would prevent confusion and increase click confidence on both tiles.
- 5. **Move July 4th to the hero.** It's the most time-relevant peg in the email. Leading with it gives a reason to act *now* that the Home Decor angle doesn't.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Home Decor — Shop Before July 4th`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Home, Less. 40–50% Off Select Decor This Week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Frames, textiles, furniture and more — sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Refresh every room before the holiday weekend`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
