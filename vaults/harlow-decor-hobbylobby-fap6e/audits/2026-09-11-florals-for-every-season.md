# Florals For Every Season 🌻
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Hobby Lobby finally aims a subject line at something the hero delivers — "Florals For Every Season" gets a genuine floral hero (a styled arrangement shot with a video play button) instead of the usual bait-and-switch this brand has been running. That's a real improvement over the 2026-09-04 "50% Off Christmas" send, where the subject promise never showed up in the body.
- But the improvement stops at the hero. Below the fold it's the identical wall-to-wall category-tile grid this brand has run almost daily since late August — 40% Fall, 50% Christmas, 40% Home Decor, 40% Party, 30% Fabric, 40% Jewelry Making — the same critique logged against the 2026-09-06 "Your Fall Refresh Starts Here!" and 2026-09-10 "Select Home Decor" sends. The floral framing is new paint on the same recycled engine.
- Net: a better subject/hero pairing than recent history, still undermined by a generic, offer-soup body and a header that front-loads navigation (Weekly Ad / Stores / Shop / DIY, then a Kinkeeping ad, then three "Essentials" tiles) before the promised floral content even appears.

## What's working

- The hero image and headline ("Seasonal Floral Inspiration") actually match the subject line — a rarity for this brand's recent sends.
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA: "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" in a bold blue bar.
- A clear sale end date is stated at the bottom ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, September 12, 2026"), giving real urgency once you scroll that far.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Consistent brand styling (red logo, navy CTA bars, warm lifestyle photography) throughout.

## What's weak

- The floral promise is abandoned almost immediately — after the hero, it's the same recurring grid of Fall/Christmas/Home Decor/Party/Fabric/Jewelry tiles seen in nearly every recent send.
- Mixing "50% Off Christmas" and "40% Off Fall" in the same block in mid-September is confusing seasonal signaling, the same issue flagged in the 2026-09-04 send.
- Every tile says "See Weekly Ad For Details" — no actual price or product is shown, so the real offer is buried a click away.
- The urgency deadline (Sept 12) is only at the very bottom, not near the top where it would actually influence a click.
- No loyalty/member pricing or personalization anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Keep the floral hero but follow it with floral-specific product tiles (stems, wreaths, arrangements) before pivoting to the general category grid — right now the subject's promise gets one screen, then vanishes.
- 2. Pull the "Sale Valid Through Sat, Sept 12" line up near the hero or CTA bar so urgency does real work instead of hiding in the footer.
- 3. Drop the Christmas tile from a fall/floral-themed send, or split it into a clearly separated "Get Ahead on Christmas" section — the mixed-season stacking reads as scattershot.
- 4. Replace at least one "See Weekly Ad For Details" tile with an actual price or product shot so the email isn't 100% dependent on a secondary click to learn the offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Fall Floral Refresh, 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New stems, wreaths, and arrangements for every room this season`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 40% off Fall Decor, Party & Crafts — through Saturday`
