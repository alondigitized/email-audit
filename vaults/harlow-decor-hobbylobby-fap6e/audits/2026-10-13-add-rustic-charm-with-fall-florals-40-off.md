# Add Rustic Charm with Fall Florals – 40% Off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Fall Florals – 40% Off," and the email only half delivers. The hero is a strong fall tableau under a big "40% OFF Fall" badge. But it covers "Decor, Party, Florals & Crafts," not florals specifically. The floral-specific content is a "Fall Floral" tile and a "40% Floral" block seven screens down. A recipient who opens for florals has to hunt for them.
- This is the usual Hobby Lobby tile wall. It has a free-shipping bar, nav icons, a Kinkeeping banner, three "Essentials" tiles, a fall hero, six fall tiles, a Weekly Ad bar, then Christmas, Home Decor, Floral, Party, Paper Crafts, Yarn, Clearance and a footer grid. That is about 15 competing offers. The 10/11 "Spice Up Your Fall" send had the same problem, opening on Christmas. This one at least leads with fall, but it still buries the subject's promise.
- Judgement: a competent weekly-ad send with a mildly accurate subject and no focus. Fine for browsers, weak for anyone who wants florals.

## What's working

- The fall hero is large and warm. The "40% OFF" badge dominates, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The six fall tiles (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) are easy to scan. Each one has an "In-Store Or Online" link.
- The $59 free-shipping bar at the top is a clear, concrete incentive.
- The footer gives a real deadline: "Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 17, 2026."
- The "Online Clearance – Up to 75%" banner is a good closing hook for bargain hunters.

## What's weak

- The subject says florals, but the floral-specific block sits in the lower third. The hero is generic fall.
- About 15 offers fight for attention. "40% off" appears on at least six blocks, so the number stops meaning anything.
- Christmas Essentials (50% off) sits mid-email, between the fall and home sections. It dilutes the fall message again, as in the 10/11 send.
- Every CTA is "In-Store Or Online ▸". None says "Shop Fall Florals," and the repeated copy makes the buttons blend together.
- The deadline appears only in the footer. The hero has no urgency, and "See Weekly Ad For Details" signals fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make a florals-first send. Use the floral photo as the hero with "40% Off Fall Florals," and cut Christmas, Yarn and Paper Crafts from this email.
- 2. Rewrite the CTAs with a verb and a noun, such as "Shop Fall Florals" and "Shop Wreaths & Stems."
- 3. Put "Ends Saturday 10/17" in the hero and the top bar. Don't leave it in the footer.
- 4. Cut the body to a hero plus one six-tile grid plus one secondary banner. This is the fourth tile-wall send in about two weeks (10/8 Home Decor, 10/6 Fall, 10/11 Fall/Christmas), so a lighter format would stand out.
- 5. Name the exclusions or link them clearly, so "Select Categories" doesn't feel like bait.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Wreaths, stems & pumpkins – 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rustic wreaths, stems and fall picks. Sale ends 10/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on $59+. Online and in store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table-based, Outlook-aware template (MSO conditionals, VML/Office namespaces) with no QA failures, only 4 warnings. The HTML provided is truncated, so findings about the footer, UTM parameters and merge tokens can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Images are served from `link.hobbylobby.com/mo/...`, a branded redirect and tracking domain. The destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 12,964 of 15,153 plain-text characters (~86%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The tracked links are long, so the plain-text part is nearly all link strings. This is a spam-score and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 34 skipped (46 total). The QA report lists no broken links among the 12 probed. The 34 skipped links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_139808_1074850_149358.gif` has no `alt` attribute. If it is a content image, screen readers will announce the filename. If it is a tracking pixel, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Responsive breakpoint:** The media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Almost no phone viewport is that narrow (most are ≥320px), so the mobile styles (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`, `.gf`, `.h1`) likely never apply. This looks like a template bug.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="initial-scale=1, user-scalable=yes">` has no `width=device-width`.
- **Doctype mismatch:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` attribute. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values. Clients will drop these declarations, so any button using `.ctacr1` gets no text colour or radius.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` has a typo in the property name, so the declaration is ignored.
- **Title:** `<title>Hobby Lobby</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject.
