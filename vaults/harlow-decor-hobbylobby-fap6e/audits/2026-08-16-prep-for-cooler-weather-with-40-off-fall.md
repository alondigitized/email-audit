# Prep For Cooler Weather With 40% Off Fall
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Subject promises "Prep For Cooler Weather With 40% Off Fall," and technically the top module delivers — "Fall Essentials" banner, then "Home Decor 40%" and "Textiles 40%" tiles right up top. But that's where the on-subject content ends. By the third scroll, this is Christmas Trees, Christmas Florals, Christmas Crafts, Christmas Light Sets, Football Party, Classroom Decor, Dorm Essentials, Wearable Art, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notions, and Furniture — a sprawling, undifferentiated everything-in-the-store tile dump that has almost nothing to do with "cooler weather."
- This is the same pattern flagged in the brand history for six straight days: a tight, thematic subject line stapled onto Hobby Lobby's stock mega-grid. The fall theming lasts about two tile-rows before the email becomes a full category directory. It's honest in the sense that every tile has a real discount and price, but it's not a targeted "fall prep" email — it's the whole store with a fall-flavored subject.
- Judgement: functional catalog email, weak targeted send. The first fold is decent; everything after row three should have been cut or moved to a separate "shop everything" send.

## What's working

- "Fall Essentials" hero banner and "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More" are both visible immediately at the top with no scrolling needed.
- Home Decor (40%) and Textiles (40%) tiles right under the hero are genuinely on-theme for "prep for cooler weather."
- Every tile has a clear, legible discount badge (40%, 50%, 66%, 25%, 30%) with product photography that renders cleanly — no broken images or overlapping text anywhere in the render.
- Consistent visual system (badge style, "Categories Listed" links, "SHOP NOW" and gift-card CTAs) keeps a very long email from feeling chaotic.

## What's weak

- Thematic drift: by the fourth section the email is selling Christmas trees, Christmas lights, and Christmas crafts under a "cooler weather / fall" subject line — a jarring seasonal mismatch, not just an unrelated category.
- No single hero product or offer — this is 20+ nearly equal-weight tiles, so the eye has nowhere obvious to land and there's no clear primary CTA.
- Irrelevant categories for a "fall prep" framing: Football Party, Classroom Decor, Dorm Essentials, Jewelry Making, Sewing Notions, Furniture — none of these connect to the subject line's promise.
- Email is extremely long (20+ stacked modules); most recipients will bail well before reaching Furniture or Custom Framing at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to the top 3-4 modules (Fall Essentials, Home Decor, Textiles, maybe Mirrors & Lamps) and spin the Christmas/party/dorm/jewelry content into separate, honestly-subjected sends — this is the single biggest lift since it fixes both the subject-mismatch problem and the wall-of-tiles fatigue flagged three days running.
- 2. Promote one hero SKU (e.g., a specific throw blanket or candle set) with a large lifestyle image instead of leading with 20 equal tiles — gives the eye something to land on per the rubric.
- 3. Move the Free Shipping banner directly under the fall hero image rather than above it, so the fall theme is the very first thing seen.
- 4. Drop unrelated categories (Football, Classroom, Dorm, Jewelry, Sewing) from this specific send entirely — they actively undercut the "fall prep" promise.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Prep Starts Here — 40% Off Home & Textiles`
