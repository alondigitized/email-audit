# Make Every Space Holiday Ready 🎄
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Hobby Lobby's weekly circular again, now with a holiday subject line. The subject promises "Make Every Space Holiday Ready 🎄". The render delivers a giant "Christmas Decor, Floral & More" video hero, then a Christmas Essentials grid. But the first screen is a free-shipping bar and a Kinkeeping banner, and the top nav and kitchen/bath/home tiles come before any Christmas content. The holiday promise gets buried under routine furniture.
- The subject is a mood line with no offer. The body then lists a stack of percent-off blocks (50% Fall, 40% Home Decor, 40% Party, 40% Floral, 30% Yarn, 40% Paper Crafts). None of them is a Christmas discount. Compared with 10/23 and 10/25, which at least named 50% Christmas, this send has the least concrete holiday offer. ## What's working

- The Christmas hero is large and visually rich, and it matches the subject's theme.
- The six-tile Christmas Essentials grid (Decor, Trees, Floral, Crafts, Party, Lights) makes category browsing easy.
- The "Click Here To View Our Weekly Ad" bar is a clear full-width button.
- The percent-off blocks use large numerals, so each deal is easy to scan.
- The free-shipping threshold ($59) and the Oct 31 deadline are stated.

## What's weak

- The first screen is a free-shipping bar, a Kinkeepers banner and unrelated Kitchen/Bath/Home tiles, so the holiday promise isn't visible up top.
- The hero is a play-button video tile with no price, offer or CTA text.
- The email shows no Christmas discount at all. The 50% number is attached to Fall, which feels stale at the end of October.
- Many blocks compete (Fall, Home Decor, Party, Floral, Yarn, Paper Crafts, plus a link grid), so there is no single focus.
- The urgency line and the fine print are tiny and easy to miss.

- 2. Replace the play-button video tile with a static image plus a "Shop Christmas Decor" button.
- 3. Cut the lower stack to two or three blocks and drop Fall, which is stale.
- 4. Make the Oct 31 free-shipping deadline a prominent line.
- 5. Put a real offer in the subject.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, Outlook-conditional markup from an ESP, and all links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect domain. The source has some CSS defects and no `lang` attribute. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 material links were probed and 33 were skipped by design. No broken links were reported among the 12 probed.
- **Tracking domain:** Links and the pixel use `link.hobbylobby.com`, a first-party redirect. This is normal, but it means final destinations and UTMs can't be read from the source.
- **Plain-text part:** 12,826 of 15,002 characters (about 85%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is most likely long tracking-wrapped URLs in the text alternative, and it can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_141892_4002613_152740.gif` has no alt text. The `/mo/` path looks like an open-tracking pixel, so this is low severity. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=... e-locale="en-US">` has `e-locale`, which isn't a valid attribute. It should have `lang="en"`, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The visible responsive block is `@media only screen and (max-width:300px)`. Most phones are 320–430px wide, so these rules (`.fl`, `.fw`, `.image`) likely never fire on real devices. The source is truncated, so a later breakpoint may exist.
- **Invalid CSS:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has empty values.
  - `.lhp2` has the typo `verticl-align:middle`, so the declaration is ignored.
  - These are likely template-variable leaks. They are harmless but show that the template didn't fully resolve.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is paired with XHTML/VML namespaces. This is legacy but works in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The empty CSS values in section 3 may be unresolved template variables, but they aren't visible merge tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** Per the QA note, this may be a capture limit of the AgentMail relay rather than a real absence. It can't be confirmed from the data.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so check the raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`, so verify alignment in the raw message.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed but doesn't report the result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unverified. The truncated source and the redirect wrapper show no UTM parameters or landing URLs. The 12 probed links reported no failures, but their final destinations weren't provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. This is the main open compliance question.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Confirm the responsive breakpoint. If `max-width:300px` is the only one, move it to about 480–600px.
4. Fix the empty or typo'd CSS (`.ctacr1`, `.lhp2`) in the template.
5. Shorten the tracking URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links in it.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and resolve the redirect chains. That would let you verify the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
