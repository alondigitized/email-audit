# Score Big 🏈 40% Off Football Party
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "40% Off Football Party" — a specific, timely hook heading into the season — but the email itself doesn't even try to deliver on it. The hero banner at the top is "Fall Essentials / Free Shipping on Orders $59+," and football doesn't show up until roughly a third of the way down the page, as one 40%-off tile among two dozen others (Home Decor, Textiles, Mirrors & Lamps, Furniture, Floral, Dorm, Yarn, Jewelry, Sewing Notions...). This is a bait-and-switch subject line stapled onto Hobby Lobby's now-familiar mega-circular grid.
- Compared to the brand's recent run, this lands closer to the sprawl of "66% Off Spring Shop® & Summer Toys" (2026-07-30, 5.0/10) than to the disciplined lookbook format of "Discover All Your Fall Essentials Here!" (2026-07-27, 8.0/10). It's also seasonally confused — Fall Essentials, Spring Shop®, Summer Toys, and Football Party all compete for space in the same scroll, which undercuts any read of "current campaign."
- The subject is genuinely good — concrete, seasonal, on-brand for August. The execution buries it. Judgement: a strong subject line wasted on a generic circular; anyone who opens for football will bounce before finding it.

## What's working

- Subject line is specific and seasonally on-time (football party prep in early August is a real, credible purchase occasion).
- No visible render bugs — every tile, icon, and price badge displays cleanly with no overlapping text or broken images.
- Consistent Hobby Lobby visual identity (typography, color-coded percentage badges, product photography style) throughout a very long email.
- "Free Shipping on Orders $59+" sits right at the top, giving a low-friction reason to buy regardless of category.

## What's weak

- The football party content promised in the subject is nowhere near the top — the eye lands on "Fall Essentials" first, not football.
- No single clear CTA button anywhere in the render; it's category labels and price badges, not "Shop Football Party" or similar actionable copy.
- Seasonal whiplash: Fall Essentials, Spring Shop®/Summer Toys (75% off), and Football Party are all crammed into one email, which reads as inventory clearance rather than a coherent campaign.
- Nearly every tile carries a "Does not include items labeled Always or 'Your Price' items" disclaimer — that's a lot of buried fine print for a shopper trying to figure out what's actually 40% off.
- This is the same mega-circular format the brand history already flagged as sprawl-prone (07-26, 07-28, 07-30) — the cadence is repetitive and this send doesn't improve on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with football.** If the subject says football party, the hero image and first fold should be a styled football-party tablescape/decor shot with a "Shop Football Party — 40% Off" button, matching the winning lookbook approach from 2026-07-27's Fall Essentials send (8.0/10).
- 2. **Cut the grid down to 5-8 tiles max**, or split into two sends — one for the football/tailgate occasion, one for the general Fall circular — instead of merging both into one scroll-forever email.
- 3. **Add one real CTA button** with verb+noun copy ("Shop Football Décor") near the top, not just a stack of category labels.
- 4. **Drop Spring Shop®/Summer Toys from an August "Fall Essentials" email** — it contradicts the seasonal story and reads as clearance.
- 5. **Move the fine print** ("Does not include...") into a single footer disclaimer instead of repeating it under every tile — it currently reads as buried exclusion language stacked over and over.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Game Day Prep Starts Here — 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jerseys, banners & tableware for your next watch party — 40% off through 8/9`
