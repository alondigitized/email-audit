# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly what I expect from Hobby Lobby — no fuss, no lifestyle-influencer nonsense, just a mantel styled up like something I'd actually want in my own living room and a big bold "40% OFF Fall" banner front and center. It's pushing the fall décor rotation hard, which lines up with the 50%-off category calendar I've got memorized, though this week it says 40, not 50. Reads like a brand that knows its customer is coming in for a specific category sale, not browsing for inspiration.

## What's working

- The mantel photo — pumpkins, gilded turkey, plaid ribbon garland — that's a styled vignette I'd screenshot for my own mantel, not just a product shot.
- The giant "40% OFF Fall" typography is impossible to miss, even glancing at my phone quickly.
- "Sale Valid Online & In-Store through August 8, 2026" — I always check this because I'm going into the store anyway, and it's right there without digging.
- Free shipping banner ($59 threshold) up top, which I'd hit easily on a floral/décor haul.

## What's weak

- "*See Weekly Ad For Details" undercuts the clean 40% off headline — I have to tap away to know what's actually included, and that's the kind of fine-print move that makes me trust digital promos less.
- No sign of my rewards or account status even though there's a little red notification dot on the account icon — makes me wonder what that's about but it's not explained.
- Nothing on this screen tells me which specific fall categories are in the 40% (florals? tabletop? that painting-style wall art?) — I'd want a category strip right under the offer.

## Recommendations

- Swap or supplement the fine-print asterisk with one line of real detail — "40% off Fall Florals & Décor" — right under the big number instead of forcing a tap-through to the Weekly Ad.
- Add a row of category tiles (Florals, Wreaths, Tabletop, Wall Décor) directly below the "Shop Now" button so I can jump straight to faux florals.
- Clear up that red dot on the account icon or turn it into a visible loyalty/rewards callout — I'd actually engage with that if it were spelled out.
