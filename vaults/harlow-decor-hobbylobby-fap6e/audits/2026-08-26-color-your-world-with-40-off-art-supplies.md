# 🎨 Color Your World With 40% Off Art Supplies
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the first send in this brand history that actually leads with a specific art-supply hero instead of the generic home-decor tile grid — acrylic paint palette, canvas, colored pencils, a 40% off badge — and it's a real improvement in relevance for anyone who buys craft supplies. But the follow-through collapses immediately: right under the hero is a "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" banner, and then the email drops into a 20-item, two-column price list (vinyl, foam board, poster board, fabric dye, bandanas, t-shirts, a sweatshirt) that has almost nothing to do with the subject's "Art Supplies" promise. Only a handful of the 20 SKUs (acrylic paint, canvas sets, Sharpie markers) are actually art supplies.
- This is the same pattern flagged on 08/20 ("Up To 75% Off Savings Inside!") and 08/19 — an eye-catching top module followed by an undifferentiated price dump. The subject over-promises a curated art-supplies sale; the body delivers a generic weekly-ad flyer. Judgement: decent hero, weak execution below the fold — the email doesn't reward the click its subject earns.

## What's working

- The hero art-supplies collage (paint palette, canvas, colored pencils, acrylic tube) is genuinely on-theme for the subject and visually distinct from prior sends.
- "40% OFF" badge is large, high-contrast, and impossible to miss at the top of the hero.
- "Shop Now" CTA button is visible directly under the offer with clear placement.
- Free shipping banner ($59+) is present near the top, a concrete secondary offer.
- Clean, consistent price-list formatting (bold price, product name, "In-Store Or Online" link) makes scanning easy once you're in the list.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Art Supplies" but the 20-item body list is majority non-art: vinyl rolls, foam board, upholstery foam, bandanas, t-shirts, a hooded sweatshirt.
- No 40%-off pricing is shown anywhere in the price list — every item shows a flat/"Your Price" number, so the headline offer is never substantiated with an actual discounted line item.
- "Select Categories *See Weekly Ad For Details" buries the offer's real terms in small print under the badge — exactly the "buried fine print" pattern the rubric penalizes.
- The "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" blue banner competes with "Shop Now" as a second CTA immediately below the hero, diluting the primary action.
- Long undifferentiated scroll (20 SKUs, no section headers) repeats the same structural miss called out in the 08/23 and 08/20 audits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the price list down to art/craft-only SKUs (paint, canvas, markers, poster board) that actually carry the 40% discount, and drop vinyl/foam/fabric/apparel items into a separate send — the mismatch between subject and body is the single biggest drag on this email.
- 2. Show the 40% discount applied to at least 2-3 hero items with strikethrough original price → sale price, so the headline offer isn't just a badge with no proof.
- 3. Remove or demote the "CLICK HERE TO VIEW OUR WEEKLY AD" banner — it's a second competing CTA right where eyes land after the hero; move it to the footer.
- 4. Add a category header ("Art & Craft," "Home Textiles," "Apparel") above each block so the list reads as organized sections instead of one long undifferentiated scroll.
- 5. Move "Select Categories" restriction text up next to the "40%" badge itself in a legible (not tiny) size so the offer terms aren't hidden.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Acrylics, Canvas & Markers — Today Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Art Supply Restock Is 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Paint, canvas, brushes & more — plus free shipping on $59+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock the studio: acrylics, canvas sets, and markers on sale`
