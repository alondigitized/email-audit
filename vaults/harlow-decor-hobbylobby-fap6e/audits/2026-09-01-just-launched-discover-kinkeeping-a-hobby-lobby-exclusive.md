# Just Launched: Discover Kinkeeping™, A Hobby Lobby® Exclusive
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a launch email, not a promo — Hobby Lobby is introducing "Kinkeeping," a Karie Robertson-fronted collection/brand, and it's a genuine departure from the wall-to-wall category grid this account runs almost daily (08/27 "Find Decor That Is Always In Season!", 08/30 "Shop Up To 50% Off Your Favorite Items!"). The hero is a real editorial moment: Karie in a styled farmhouse kitchen, a four-generation family video, and a signed quote block — it reads like a lifestyle-brand launch, not a flyer.
- The problem is there's no offer anywhere. Every other recent send in this history led with a percentage — 40% off tablescapes, 50% off favorites — and converted on that. This one swaps the discount for brand storytelling, which is a real risk for a value-shopper audience that's been trained on daily markdowns. It's also the second Karie Robertson touchpoint in two weeks (08/28's tablescape hero used similar styling), so the persona is being built, but the ask here is "watch a video" and "browse categories," not "buy something today."
- Verdict: strong creative execution, best brand-storytelling this account has shown, but weak as a promotional email — no visible discount, no primary CTA, and a category grid that undercuts the premium feel of the hero.

## What's working

- The Kinkeeping hero pairs an authentic lifestyle photo (Karie in a real farmhouse kitchen) with clean wordmark/monogram branding — it doesn't look like stock decor photography.
- The four-generation family video thumbnail is warm and specific, with a visible play button that signals real content, not just another product shot.
- The closing quote block ("Build a life you love...") with Karie's signature adds emotional register this brand rarely attempts.
- No render bugs — every tile, image, and button renders cleanly with consistent spacing.

## What's weak

- There is zero visible offer or price — no %, no $, no BOGO — a sharp break from every prior send in this history, which all led with a discount.
- No unambiguous primary CTA button; the only actionable elements are small "→" arrows under category tiles and a buried "Click here" text link under the video.
- After the strong hero, it collapses right back into the same 8-tile category grid (Framed Art, Tabletop Decor, Tableware, Kitchen Decor...) this brand runs constantly — the launch energy doesn't sustain past the fold.
- The subject promises something "exclusive" and "just launched," but nothing in the body tells you what Kinkeeping actually is, what to buy, or why to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one clear, large CTA button ("Shop Kinkeeping") directly under the hero and again under the video — right now the only way to act is a tiny arrow or buried text link, which will tank clicks even from people who love the story.
- 2. State what Kinkeeping actually sells in one line near the hero (a collection name alone doesn't communicate product category — tableware? decor? both?).
- 3. If there's any launch incentive (early access, intro discount, free gift), surface it near the top — sending zero-offer to a discount-trained list is a real click-rate risk; if there truly is no offer, lean harder into "shop first" urgency (limited launch quantities, etc.).
- 4. Trim the category grid to 4 tiles max and only show ones that map to the Kinkeeping line, or drop it entirely and let this be a pure brand-launch send — right now it dilutes the exclusivity angle.
