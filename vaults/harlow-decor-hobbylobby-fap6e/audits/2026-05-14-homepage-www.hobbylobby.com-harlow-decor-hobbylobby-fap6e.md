# Homepage snapshot · www.hobbylobby.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'll be honest — I walked right into this one. The second I opened the app I saw *The Spring Shop® at 50% off* and my thumb was already hovering over "Shop Now." This is classic Hobby Lobby: seasonal, straightforward, no gimmicks. The spring scene with the ceramic duck and all those potted florals is basically a mood board of my back porch, and the deal is one I already had circled in my mental calendar. It's pushing spring decor hard, and for me, right now, that is exactly the right push.

## What's working

- **The 50% off callout** is massive — practically the whole bottom half of the viewport. They know their customer knows this sale, and they're confirming it without making me dig through the Weekly Ad first.
- **The hero image** is doing real work. A ceramic duck, terracotta pots, a little white garden bench, purple and pink florals — that is *my* aesthetic. I didn't have to translate anything.
- **"Sale Valid Online & In-Store through May 16, 2026"** is exactly the kind of transparency I need. I trust in-store prices more than digital-only promos, and this tells me I can verify it in person.
- **Free Shipping on Orders $59 or More** at the very top of the screen — that threshold is completely reachable for me on a Spring Shop haul.
- **The Weekly Ad button** is prominently placed. That's my bible. Love that it's one tap from the home screen.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty/rewards hook**: I've been shopping here for years and I have no idea if I have rewards waiting for me. A "You have 200 points!" nudge would've made me feel recognized.
- **"Shop Now" is generic**: For a 50% off sale on *The Spring Shop*, why not "Shop the Spring Shop" or "Shop Florals"? I'd feel more pulled toward something specific.
- **The asterisk on "*See Weekly Ad For Details"** quietly introduces doubt. What's excluded? Yarn? Fabric? Faux florals? I've been burned by that asterisk before. It doesn't stop me but it niggles.
- **No product category entry points**: Once I tap "Shop Now," where am I going? I'd love a sub-nav row showing Florals, Farmhouse, Garden — something to tell me the path is short.
- **No people, no lifestyle context**: The product styling is charming but a quick glimpse of someone *arranging* these pieces in a home would've made it feel more aspirational rather than just merchandised.

## Recommendations

- **Add a loyalty balance chip** near the search bar or profile icon — even a simple "Rewards: 350 pts" pulls returning shoppers into account mode and toward a purchase.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Spring Florals & Decor"** — be specific. I know what I want and that specificity would confirm I'm in the right place instantly.
- **Add a 2–3 category quick-link row** below the CTA (Faux Florals | Farmhouse | Garden Decor). One tap to exactly where I'm headed, no hamburger menu archaeology.
- **Clarify the asterisk inline**: instead of pointing to the Weekly Ad, say something like "*Excludes custom framing and prior purchases" right there on the screen. Kills the doubt before it starts.
