# Fall Is In The Air 🍂 Shop 40% Off Fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a much better send than the 9/27 "Home Decor Refresh" tile wall, but it still runs too long. The 40% Off Fall hero is a strong, seasonal, well-composed image. It shows a "Blessed" pumpkin and wreath flat-lay with a big "40% OFF" badge. Above it, a "Free Shipping On Orders $59 Or More!" banner and a "Fall Essentials" strip give the email a clear top. The subject line and hero say the same thing, so the email delivers what it promises.
- After the hero, it turns into a long, undifferentiated product grid. There are about 20 items, from chargers and foam bricks to helium tanks, Mod Podge, batteries, epoxy and crafter's tape. Most aren't fall products. The offers are also mixed. The 40% Off Fall hero and the $1.99–$49.99 "Your Price" tiles are different promotions, and nothing explains how they fit together. Every CTA is a small "In-Store Or Online ▶" link, so nothing tells you what to do next.
- Compared with 9/29's "Festive Floral Must-Haves – 50% Off Christmas" (7/10), this has a real hero but less focus. Hobby Lobby is also sending almost daily, so this one blends into the week's stream of "% off seasonal" emails. It's a decent seasonal send weighed down by a clearance-style product dump.

## What's working

- The 40% Off Fall hero is large and readable, and it's seasonally on point for late September.
- The free-shipping threshold ($59) is stated up front in a bold red banner.
- The "Fall Essentials" strip and the six fall category tiles (Decor, Floral, Kitchen, Party, Candles, Crafts) make it easy to browse by fall topic.
- Prices are large and easy to scan, and each product shows size or count (e.g. 14" and 18" wreaths, 12 oz wafers).
- The email renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The bottom two-thirds is a generic 2-up product grid. Balloon helium tanks, batteries, epoxy and lap trays have nothing to do with "Fall Is In The Air."
- Every CTA is the same tiny "In-Store Or Online ▶" text link. None is a button, and none says what you get.
- The sale end date ("Sale Valid Through Saturday, October 3") sits at the very bottom, so the urgency is buried.
- The 40% Off Fall hero has an asterisk ("*See Weekly Ad For Details") in small type. The prices below it are "Your Price" figures, with no sign of whether the 40% applies.
- There's a "Click Here To View Weekly Ad" bar mid-email. It pulls you out of the offer and adds a second competing destination.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product grid to 4–6 fall-relevant items (wreaths, candles, decor) and drop the batteries, epoxy and helium tank. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Put a real button under the hero, such as "Shop 40% Off Fall Decor," in a solid contrasting color.
- 3. Move the end date ("Ends Saturday 10/3") up next to the 40% badge.
- 4. Put the weekly ad link near the footer, or in the header next to the utility icons.
- 5. Make it clear whether 40% applies to the tiles below, or label those tiles as separate "Weekly Deals."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source shows a few invalid CSS declarations and no `lang` attribute. The QA warnings (missing unsubscribe headers, unknown authentication status) come from the AgentMail relay capture and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The source was truncated, so link, UTM and footer checks rest on limited evidence.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 39 were skipped, so no result is available for most links. The QA report gives no failures for the 12 that were probed.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt attribute.** `https://link.hobbylobby.com/mo/eORqXTkAb7_837015858_138641_4586767_147642.gif` was flagged by QA. The `/mo/` path on the ESP tracking domain suggests an open-tracking pixel, so this is low severity. Add `alt=""` and confirm the pixel is 1x1 and hidden.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 15,075 of 17,757 characters (about 85%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The likely cause is long tracking-redirect URLs in the text part. This can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS in the `<style>` block:**
  - `.ctacr1 {color:; ... -moz-border-radius:px; -webkit-border-radius:px; border-radius:px;}` has an empty `color` value and unit-only radius values. Clients drop these declarations, so the CTA text colour and radius are undefined for anything using `.ctacr1`.
  - `.lhp2 {... verticl-align:middle;}` misspells `vertical-align`, so it has no effect.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag has `e-locale="en-US"` (a non-standard attribute) but no `lang="en"`. Screen readers therefore can't determine the language.
- **Doctype and namespace mismatch.** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an XHTML `xmlns`. This is common in ESP templates and low risk.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `Hobby Lobby` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Rest of the markup.** The source is truncated, so I can't confirm responsive behaviour beyond the visible `@media (max-width:300px)` rule. The MSO conditional blocks and `X-UA-Compatible` handling are present and well formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found.** QA notes the relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, the email will lack native one-click unsubscribe in Gmail and Yahoo. Their bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unverified. The sender is `noreply@create.hobbylobby.com`, a subdomain, so its alignment should be checked with a direct DNS or header inspection.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address were not confirmed** in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link was probed but reports no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. QA reports no UTM or landing-page data, and the visible source contains no `utm_*` parameters. Links go through the `link.hobbylobby.com` redirect domain, so parameters would be applied at redirect time. Resolve the redirects to check the final destination and its parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the CSS: give `.ctacr1` a valid `color` and border-radius units, and change `verticl-align` to `vertical-align`.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, bypassing the AgentMail relay, before treating them as sender defects. If `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is missing, add it.
4. Shorten or simplify the redirect URLs in the plain-text part, or make sure it carries readable copy, so URLs fall well below 70% of the characters.
5. Re-run link probing with wider coverage, including the footer and unsubscribe links, and resolve the redirects to check UTM parameters and the final landing pages.
