# ### Summary and Recommendations

**Executive Summary:**

The DSW email for Earth Day is a mystery deal that aims to create curiosity but fails to provide clear value propositions. The hero image lacks product visibility, and the overall design feels geared towards younger audiences or women, making it less appealing to older men like Walker Miles.

**Business Impact Score:** 3/10

### Detailed Analysis:

#### What's Working:
- **Headline Clarity:** The headline "A DEAL SO GOOD / You'll say 'what on earth?!'" is readable and engaging.
- **Wide Width Button:** The inclusion of a button for wide widths is practical and relevant to the target audience.
- **Earth Day Timing:** The use of Earth Day themes with globe emojis and puns adds a clever touch.

#### What's Weak:
- **No Revealed Deal:** Lack of clear discount information or product details within the email body.
- **Lack of Men’s Products:** No men's products are featured, leading to a disconnect for male recipients.
- **Size Grid Misalignment:** The size grid starts with women's sizes, making it less relevant for men.
- **VIP Pitch Tone-Deafness:** The VIP pitch feels out-of-place and irrelevant if the recipient is already loyal to another brand.
- **Tiny Footer Text:** Legal text at the bottom is too small and unreadable on mobile devices.

### Recommendations:

1. **Reveal the Deal Inside the Email:**
   - Include a clear discount message or range (e.g., "Get up to 40% off").
   - Provide a teaser of what the mystery deal entails, even if it’s just a hint.
   
2. **Feature Men's Products in the Hero Section:**
   - Add an image of a comfortable men's shoe in the hero section.
   - Include a brief description or call-to-action for men's products.

3. **Adjust Size Grid to Start with Men’s Sizes:**
   - Lead the size grid with men's sizes (e.g., 8, 9, 10) to make it more relevant for male recipients.

4. **Tailor VIP Pitch Based on Recipient Status:**
   - Use conditional logic to show personalized messages based on whether the recipient is a current VIP member.
   - Offer specific benefits like "free shipping" or "exclusive discounts."

5. **Incorporate Visual Elements:**
   - Add at least one image that aligns with Earth Day themes, such as a green shoe or someone walking outdoors.

### Subject Line Analysis:

- **Subject:** `🌎 MYSTERY DEAL 🌎`
  - **Clarity:** 3/10 (Lacks clear information about the deal)
  - **Curiosity:** 7/10 (Effective in making recipients curious)
  - **Personalization:** 1/10 (No personalization or specific category mentioned)
  - **Urgency:** 4/10 (Missing time-limited phrasing to create urgency)
  - **Specificity:** 2/10 (Vague without clear details about the deal)

**Alternative Subject Line:**
- **Alt A:** `Walker, your mystery Earth Day shoe deal is here! 🌎`
  - This version personalizes the message and adds more clarity while maintaining curiosity.

### Bottom Line:

The email fails to engage Walker Miles due to a lack of clear value propositions, product visibility, and relevant content for men. Improving these areas will significantly enhance engagement and conversion rates.
