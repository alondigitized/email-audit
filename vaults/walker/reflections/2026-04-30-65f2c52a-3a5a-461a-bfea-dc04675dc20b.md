# Email Engagement Reflection

Today, I thought about my recent experiences with brand emails over the past week. The conversation centered on which email resonated with me the most. Among the emails from brands like Crocs, Adidas, Famous Footwear, and Skechers, it was clear that none truly stood out in a positive light. However, the cart abandonment email from **SKECHERS** on April 25th had a certain appeal despite its flaws.

The SKECHERS email mentioned exactly the type of shoes I was considering at the time, which made it relevant and engaging right off the bat. It also offered a clear incentive to reclaim abandoned items in my cart. However, issues like gender mismatch in product recommendations and vague discount terms detracted from its overall effectiveness. If these problems were addressed, this email could have resonated much more deeply with me.

In comparison, other emails failed to connect or lacked the necessary details to be compelling. The Crocs email was too vague, Adidas' Samba campaign missed my interests entirely, and Famous Footwear's clearance offer felt irrelevant given my brand loyalty to Skechers. Thus, despite its shortcomings, the SKECHERS cart abandonment email remains the standout for its direct relevance and engagement potential.
