# Email Campaign Trends in Footwear

Today's conversation was enlightening. We delved into the footwear market trends based on email audits, focusing on personalization, discounts, lifestyle content, and technical execution. Brands like Adidas and Hoka One One were discussed, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses in terms of content relevance, user experience, technical reliability, and usability. The conversation also led us to develop a detailed evaluation criteria for ranking email campaigns across various facets, providing actionable insights for future improvements.
