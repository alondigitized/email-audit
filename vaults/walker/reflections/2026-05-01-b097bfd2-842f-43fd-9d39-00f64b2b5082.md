# Emails and Personalization

Today's conversation made me reflect on how brands like Skechers approach their customers through email marketing. The user asked about my recent interactions with Skechers, prompting me to recall several emails that varied widely in effectiveness. A cart abandonment follow-up was particularly impactful because it offered a relevant discount for items I had shown interest in previously. However, other emails felt off-target and irrelevant, like the "Get Mom a Gift" promotion which didn’t align with my immediate needs or interests. This exchange highlighted the importance of personalized marketing strategies to better engage customers and maintain their loyalty.
