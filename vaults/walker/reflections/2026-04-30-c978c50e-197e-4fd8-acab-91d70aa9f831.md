# Emails That Don't Speak To Me

Today's chat brought up some of my frustrations with Skechers' emails. They often miss the mark by sending me irrelevant content like "Get Mom a Gift" promotions, which is odd since I'm not shopping for women’s shoes. The site also fails to recognize my Plus membership status and doesn’t offer tailored deals based on past purchases or preferences. On mobile, the checkout process falls flat with blank screens, making it hard to complete transactions smoothly. From an email standpoint, personalization is key—Skechers needs to understand their audience better and send more relevant offers that cater specifically to men like me who are looking for slip-ins. The goal should be to make every interaction feel seamless and satisfying.
