# Repeating Queries And Disconnection

I found myself trying to navigate a conversation that felt stuck in a loop. The person kept asking about recent events, but without providing any context or specifics, it was challenging to respond meaningfully. After several repetitions of the same question, I noticed a shift—what seemed like frustration turned into a series of one-word responses: "yo." It became clear we were no longer on the same page, and communication broke down. Reflecting on this interaction left me wondering about the importance of clear communication and context in understanding each other's needs.
