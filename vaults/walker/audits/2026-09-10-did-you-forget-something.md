# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that pink floral D'Lites sneaker, same one chasing me around all week, this time sitting under a big gold "READY TO CHECK OUT?" banner like I've got one foot already in the checkout line. I don't have a cart. I never clicked that shoe. Calling it my abandoned order doesn't work if I never picked it up in the first place.
- Past that, the email actually settles down a bit — there's a men's slip-in section, a men's arch fit sneaker, and a real 20% off code for "members" buried further down. But nobody said "Hi, Walker," nobody mentioned my points, and the whole thing opens by mistaking me for someone I'm not. I'd scroll past the top, maybe glance at the Altman slip-ins, and move on. Not forwarding this one, not marking it as anything special — straight to read-and-ignore.

## What's working

- The men's slip-in "Arch Fit Relaxed Fit: Altman - Murdock Sneaker" is exactly my lane — hands-free, arch support, casual.
- The women's Arch Fit slip-in ("Meet Ya There") tells me they've at least got a slip-in line going, even if it's pointed at the wrong shopper here.
- The 20% off member code (EXTRA20, valid thru 9/14) is a real, dated offer with a clear deadline.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup up top are nice practical touches for anyone actually buying.

## What's weak

- Leading with a floral D'Lites shoe I never touched, framed as "check out your order" — that's the sixth time this week for this exact sneaker, and it's not even one I looked at.
- No "Hi, Walker," no points balance, no sign anyone remembers I'm a member — just a generic cart-abandonment template slapped on someone with no cart.
- The men's slip-in product I'd actually want is buried in a small product grid, five rows down, competing with kids' and boys' sneakers.
- The 20% off banner — the one offer with real teeth — is stuck two-thirds down the page after I'd already have bailed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same "forgot something" template to people with an empty cart — lead instead with the member 20% off code and a men's comfort shoe, since that's the offer with actual pull.
- 2. Move the men's Arch Fit slip-in up top as the hero image, not buried in a six-pack grid.
- 3. Put my name and points balance at the top like the 9/9 "Slip into something you'll love" send did — that one worked, this one doesn't.
- 4. Retire the pink floral D'Lites from my inbox rotation entirely — sixth appearance this week, still zero interest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Arch Fit favorites, 20% off thru 9/14`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, your member discount is ready`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ins, arch support, and an extra 20% off sale styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save more — code EXTRA20 ends Sunday.`
