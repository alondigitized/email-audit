# The sneakers you *NEED*, from $59.99
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Alright, this one's actually not bad — a hero banner with white sneakers starting at $59.99, "SHOP NOW" right up top, no BOGO nonsense screaming at me. But then it scrolls into "YOUR MID WEEK MOOD BOOST" and "SHOP WOMEN" — and from there it's mules, ankle boots, slingbacks, ballet flats, and heeled sandals for section after section. The sneaker section — "THESE MAKE THE GRADE" — does show men's-style sneakers, so credit for that, but it's buried in the middle of an email that's otherwise built for someone shopping for herself, not her husband.
- I'd have opened this hoping for more of what the subject promised, and about 70% of it isn't for me. I'm not clicking past the one sneaker section, and I'm sure not forwarding this to my buddies at the coffee shop — this is a wife's email, not mine.

## What's working

- The hero banner delivers exactly what the subject promised — sneakers, a real price, a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- "THESE MAKE THE GRADE" section has actual men's athletic sneakers (looks like some Adidas-style styles) that I'd wear.
- "SHOP UNDER $50" and "Under $50" badges are honest, simple price call-outs — I like knowing the number up front.
- Layout is clean, big readable buttons, nothing overlapping or broken.

## What's weak

- "SHOP WOMEN" button and entire mules/heels/mules/wedge sections have nothing to do with me — I skipped past a good third of this email doing nothing but scrolling.
- No men's category button at all — I see "SHOP WOMEN," "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS," "SHOP THE BEST BRANDS" — nowhere does it say "SHOP MEN."
- "Shop by Size" grid at the bottom only goes up to size 11 with no wide-width numbers shown until a separate "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" button — I need wide, and it's an afterthought.
- No mention of arch support, cushioning, or comfort features anywhere — just "mood boost" and "vibe," which isn't how I decide on a shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this by gender the way you clearly can — send me a men's-only version instead of making me scroll past six women's shoe blocks to find one sneaker section.
- 2. Put a comfort callout (cushioned, arch support, all-day wear) right in the sneaker section — that's the actual reason I'd click, not "mood boost."
- 3. Add "SHOP MEN" as a real button next to "SHOP WOMEN" — right now it looks like an oversight.
- 4. Move wide-width sizing up next to the standard size grid, not off to a separate button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's sneakers from $59.99 — comfort that lasts all day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall sneaker restock starts at $59.99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-supported, easy on your feet — grab a pair before fall`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New men's sneakers just landed, starting under $60`
