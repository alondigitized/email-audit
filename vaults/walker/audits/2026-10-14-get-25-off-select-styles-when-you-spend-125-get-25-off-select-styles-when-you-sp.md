## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a few template-hygiene defects: an unresolved placeholder background URL, `@font-face` rules with no `src`, a duplicated subject line, and 3 images with no alt text. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM compliance can't be confirmed, because the HTML was truncated and no auth headers were captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 26. It reported no broken links, but the other 26 (including footer and social links) are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12421637_899683_23439230.gif` looks like an open-tracking pixel. A missing alt on it is low impact.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 11,556 of 13,308 characters (about 87%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The likely cause is long tracking-wrapped links, and it can raise spam-filter scores.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Placeholder background URL:** `.bgwhite {… background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}`
  - It's unresolved and uses insecure `http://`.
  - It will 404 and may trigger mixed-content or blocked-image warnings wherever `.bgwhite` is applied.
- **Web fonts can't load:** every `@font-face` block (`linotype-didot`, `proxima-nova`, `proxima-nova-condensed`, and others) declares only `font-family`, `font-weight` and `font-style`, with no `src`.
  - Clients silently fall back to `Arial` or `Times New Roman`.
  - One block (`proxima-nova` 700 italic) has `font-family` declared twice.
  - `ITC Garamond Book Condensed` is also unquoted-multiword and has no `src`.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `#ffffff` and then as `#58546d`. The later rule wins, so the first is dead code.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Mixed doctype:** an HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with an XHTML `xmlns` attribute and self-closed tags. Most clients tolerate this.
- **Dark-mode link contrast (likely, truncated source):** `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { a {color:#cf0a2c !important;} .bg1 {background:#151415 !important;} }`
  - `#cf0a2c` on `#151415` works out to about 3.3:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 for body text. This applies only where links sit on `.bg1`.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png`
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - the tracking GIF above

  The first two are content images, so they need alt text or `alt=""` if decorative.
- **Page title is a placeholder:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Subject is duplicated, with stray spacing:** `Get 25% off select styles when you spend $125+ , get 25% off select styles when you spend $125+`. The same string appears twice, separated by ` , `. This looks like a subject plus preheader or variant concatenation error.
- **Asset paths come from a different template:** images are served from `/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/`, a Welcome-program folder. The template was reused, and the `XXXXX` placeholder suggests an unfinished template.
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens appear in the visible portion of the source.
