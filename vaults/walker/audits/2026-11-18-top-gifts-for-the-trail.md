# Top gifts for the trail  ⛰️🏃‍♂️

## Executive summary

- This is my eighth HOKA email since the 9th, and I'm tired of the pace. This one is aimed at someone buying a gift for a trail runner, and I'm not that person. I walk on pavement and the mall. The "Trail Gift Guide" headline is big and easy to read on my phone, which I appreciate. But the hero is a tight shot of a muddy lace, so I can't tell what the shoe is.
- Every shoe shown is a rugged trail shoe: Speedgoat, Challenger and a GORE-TEX pitch. None of them is a Bondi or a Clifton, the cushioned walking shoes I'd actually buy. The only real offer is "Free Expedited Shipping", and the asterisk and the 'sign up for texts' condition make it feel like a bait-and-switch. I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- "Trail Gift Guide" is huge, white on dark, and I can read it without squinting.
- The shoe grid has plain labels like "Speedgoat 7 GTX, Men", so I know what I'm looking at.
- The big black "Shop Now" buttons are easy to tap with my thumb.
- The Men and Women versions are shown side by side, so I don't have to hunt for mine.
- The GORE-TEX line is a real, useful feature for wet weather.

## What's weak

- The shoes are all trail shoes. Nothing here is a cushioned walker or a slip-on.
- There's no price, no percent off and no sale anywhere. It's a gift guide with nothing to save on.
- The free shipping is only for a first full-price order and requires a text signup. The fine print at the bottom is tiny and hard to read.
- The hero image is a blurry lace close-up, so no shoe shows up top.
- The "Made for the season" box has small gray text on a photo, which is hard for me to read.
- I've had seven HOKA emails in a row, and this feels like more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the shoes and one real offer. A gift guide with a price or a gift-with-purchase would give me a reason to click.
- 2. Split the send by what people buy. Walkers like me should see Bondi and Clifton, not Speedgoat.
- 3. Make the shipping offer honest. Don't hide the text-signup condition in 8-point type.
- 4. Lighten up the text over the photo, or put it on a solid background.
- 5. Space out the sends. Seven in a row trains me to ignore HOKA.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked links, including the unsubscribe link, returned 403 to the automated prober. It has no plain-text part, and 11 content images lack alt text. The 403s may be bot filtering on the Cordial redirector rather than real breakage, so they need a manual check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] 403 on `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`.** The wrapped link is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ad64d4e…:1/5a90c1ed?jwtH=…`. The JWT payload decodes to `linkUrl=https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261118_HolidayGiftGuide_Trail_Drip3_…&hmail=…&cordial_id=…`.
- **[FAIL] 403 on Unsubscribe.** The wrapped link is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/…/009e4ed0?jwtH=…`, and it resolves to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=…&utm_*…&hmail=…`.
- **Likely cause.** Both failures are on the `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/` redirector and both are 403 rather than 404 or 5xx. That points to WAF or bot rules against the prober's user agent or IP. It does not by itself show the links are dead for recipients.
- **Needs manual verification.** I can't confirm either link works for a real client from the QA data alone.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 26 links were probed, and the other 14 were skipped as footer or social links.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two open-tracking pixels, `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/…` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Having two is redundant, but it isn't a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a multipart/alternative defect and a spam-filter signal.
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images, all on `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/`.**
  - Hero and banner images: `…HolidayGiftGuideTrailDrip3_a.jpg` (1200x1400), `_b`, `_c`, `_d`, `_e`
  - Product images: `US_Speedgoat7GTX_TopLeft_1176677-DMVB.jpg`, `…TopRight_1176674-SHCL.jpg`, `US_Challenger8_BottomLeft_1168717-BNKP.jpg`, `CA_Challenger8_BottomRight_1168716-BYFL.jpg`
  - Other: `US_Q3_SMSCTA_SingleAsterisk.jpg`, `LogoFooterPrimary.png`
- **Effect of the missing alt text.** The email appears to be built from full-width image slices, so with images blocked the content is blank and unreadable to screen readers. Missing alt on the two tracking pixels is expected and harmless.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid CSS.** The `<style>` block has `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}`. These values are empty because a template variable wasn't filled in. Clients drop the declarations, so the impact is low.
- **Viewport meta tags.** There are two, and one of them is `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` with no `http-equiv`, so it doesn't declare a charset. This is cosmetic.
- **Positive.** `lang="en"` is set.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe link.** It is present and points to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`, but the probe got a 403. If real clients are blocked too, that is a CAN-SPAM problem (the opt-out must work), so please click it manually.
- **Authentication headers.** `Authentication-Results` wasn't found. The QA note says this is expected through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown and isn't a confirmed sender defect.
- **Not verifiable from the data.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers weren't provided. The physical postal address isn't visible in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters are present on the decoded links: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email`, and a structured `utm_campaign` (`H_US_BR_20261118_HolidayGiftGuide_Trail_Drip3_STO_A30Day_…`).
- **Campaign name.** It encodes audience-segment details (such as `720TrailPurchasers`, `LYAnniPurchasers`) in a visible URL parameter. That is an internal-naming leak, not a functional bug.
- **Unsubscribe URL.** It carries `utm_*` params and `hmail`, which isn't necessary but is harmless.
- **Possible locale mismatch.** The asset `CA_Challenger8_BottomRight_1168716-BYFL.jpg` has a `CA_` prefix in a `US_`-prefixed campaign. This is inferred from the filename only. Confirm that the Canadian creative wasn't used in the US send and that its link targets `/en/us/`.
- **Landing pages.** I couldn't verify landing page alignment, because the 403s blocked resolution of the probed destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the `return-start` and unsubscribe links manually, with a normal browser user agent and from a residential IP. If they return 200, allowlist the QA prober on the `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/` redirector and document the 403 as a false positive. If they fail, treat the unsubscribe failure as high priority.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part. The current text part is 0 characters.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. Fill in the `<title>`, and remove or fix the empty-valued CSS declarations (`padding-top:px`, `border-top:px solid`).
5. Confirm that the `CA_` asset in a US send is intentional.
6. Capture the raw headers from the original send, not the relay copy, to check `Authentication-Results` and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click).
7. Raise the probe budget, or add a targeted probe, so all primary CTAs are covered. 14 of 26 links were skipped.
