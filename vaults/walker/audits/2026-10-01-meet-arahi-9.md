The bullets on "Confident strides," "Soft landings" and "Weightless feel" are the useful part, but they're short and buried under big photos. - I get the Men's and Women's Arahi 9 side by side, so I don't have to hunt for my version.
- The H-Frame line, "targeted support with a smooth transition into your gait cycle," is the closest thing here to arch-support talk.
- The black buttons are big and easy to hit with a thumb, and the text is readable on my phone.

- The models are all young athletes mid-sprint. Nothing says walking, errands or all-day comfort.
- There's no price and no sizing or width info. I'm left guessing whether it's a $140 shoe or a $90 shoe.
- The free expedited shipping on $250+ is a lot to spend. The bigger "Free Expedited Shipping**" block lower down is only for new text-message signups on a first order, and I had to read the fine print to learn that.
- The "Shop Now" and "Get Free Shipping" buttons don't say what I'd be shopping for. The fine print is tiny and light blue on blue, which is hard on my eyes.
- There are no reviews and no comparison to other cushioned shoes.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is image-heavy and sent through Cordial. It has no plain-text part (0 chars), 14 images without alt text, and two probed links (return-start and Unsubscribe) that returned 403. The 403s are probably bot blocking on the tracked redirects, but that is unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe returned 403.** The link is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…/2a767c3f?jwtH=…`. Decoding `jwtP` shows it redirects to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&…`. The 403 is on the Cordial redirect, probably from the automated probe being blocked, so I can't confirm a real recipient would see it. Test it manually, because a broken unsubscribe is a compliance risk.
- **`hoka.com/en/us/return-start/.` also returned 403.** It wraps the same way, to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&…`. The visible link text ends in a period, which suggests the sentence punctuation was included in the displayed text. Check that the `href` itself doesn't end in `.`. The decoded `linkUrl` doesn't.
- **Two open-tracking pixels are present.** They are `https://e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…` and `https://mg.emails.hoka.com/o/…`. Both lack alt text, which is harmless. Two pixels on different subdomains suggest overlapping ESP and relay tracking, which can inflate open counts.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 14 skipped (footer and social), so those 14 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing.** `Text version is 0 chars` is a real failure. It hurts spam scoring and affects text-only clients and screen-reader or accessibility fallbacks.
- **14 images lack alt text** per QA. This includes the content-bearing images `US_20261001_Arahi9Launch_a.png` (1200x1400), `_b`, `_c`, `_d`, `_e`, `_f`, `_g`, `US_Arahi9_TopLeft_1176570-VWL.png`, `US_Arahi9_TopRight_1176531-SCCG.png`, `US_SMSCTA_DoubleAsterisk.jpg` (1200x1276), `US_20260701_NewFESBanner.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. The layout appears to be mostly full-width images, so with images blocked the email would render as blank or unlabelled. Also confirm whether any are meant to be decorative with `alt=""`.
- **`<title></title>` is empty.**
- **The first `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` or `charset` attribute,** so it declares nothing. Use `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
- **There are two conflicting viewport metas.** One has `maximum-scale=1` only. The other has `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` limits pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Minor CSS invalidity:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;…}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. These are template leftovers and are ignored.
- **Webfonts load from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/…`.** Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so the fallback stack matters. The visible source is truncated, so I couldn't verify it.
