# Merry UGG Season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the tenth or so email in about a week. The "Merry UGG Season" subject doesn't match what I see inside. The top banner says "Early Black Friday Sale, up to 30% off UGG" in big coral numbers I can read without my glasses. Then the whole middle turns into a Birkenstock spread. Birkenstock is a comfort brand I'd actually look at, but the email never says which one it's selling.
- I scrolled a long way past young folks in scarves and a pile of kids' sandals before I got to anything for a 62-year-old guy. There's a "Shop Men's Birkenstock" button, but it's one small black box among five. Nothing about slip-ons, arch support or walking. I'd mark this read. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd only click if I happened to be shopping for a Boston clog.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" at the top is huge and easy to read on my phone without squinting.
- The black "Shop UGG" button is clear and has plenty of tap room.
- Birkenstock is a comfort brand I cross-shop. The clog and sandal photos show real shoes.
- The "Shop Wide Width / Shop Wide Calf" buttons by size are a thoughtful touch for a guy with wider feet.
- The VIP 500 bonus points block is easy to spot. It doesn't decide the purchase for me, but it's a nice extra.

## What's weak

- The subject says UGG season, but half the email is Birkenstock, so what I see doesn't match what the subject promised.
- The models are all young and trendy. The tall guy lying across the other model on a blue backdrop looks silly, not like anything I'd wear.
- The "Merry & Bright" photo is big and has no clear shoe or price attached to it.
- The "Shop Men's Birkenstock" button is lost among four other buttons the same size.
- It's very long. The footer fine print is tiny and I can't read it without zooming, and the "up to 30%" has an asterisk with a wall of exclusions below.
- No price, no sale price on any shoe, no cushioning or arch talk. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one brand per email. If it's UGG, show men's UGG slippers or Tasman slip-ons with a price. If it's Birkenstock, say so in the subject.
- 2. Show actual sale prices under the shoes in the grid, with a men's row near the top.
- 3. Cut the length by dropping the Studio DSW creator block and the kids' row, and use the space for a men's comfort slip-on module.
- 4. Add short comfort cues like "cushioned footbed," "arch support" and "slip-on" under each pair.
- 5. Keep the offer deadline visible near the top instead of in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `UGG & Birkenstock: 30% off comfy slip-ons`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's slippers & clogs, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, easy-on styles for him, now through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top-trending Birkenstock plus 500 VIP bonus points`
