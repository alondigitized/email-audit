- The Black Friday banner (Buy One, Get One 50% off, or 20% off a single pair) is the only thing worth my time. Then I read the fine print and it says the offer was valid 11/12 to 11/17. Today is the 20th. Telling me a deal is "auto-applied at cart" when the dates have passed doesn't build trust, and trust is what I care about when the email has to match what I see on the site.

## What's working

- The big navy "LET'S MAKE IT OFFICIAL" headline is easy to read without squinting.
- One big shoe photo with one big "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is simple. That's how I like to navigate on my phone.
- The Slip-ins name is on the shoe caption. Hands-free is what I shop for.
- The BOGO 50% banner stands out against the white, and the math is easy to follow.
- The Men / Women / Kids list is big, tappable text. No tiny icons to fumble.

## What's weak

- The "more great options" grid is almost all women's styles, plus a bottle of waterproofer spray. Nothing there looks like me.
- The fine print says the offer ran 11/12 to 11/17. That's stale, and it makes the whole email feel like leftovers.
- There's a big blank gap above the hero shoe before it appears. It feels empty on a small screen.
- The product captions under the grid are tiny. I'd have to zoom to read them.
- Nothing mentions my 1000 points or my member status, even though the 11/19 "Holiday Ready" email greeted me by name.
- No price, no arch-support or cushioning detail, no reviews on the shoe I'm supposedly about to buy.

- 1. Fix the stale dates. Don't send an expired promo to someone with a cart, or at least update the fine print to match the real end date.
- 2. Make the offer the hero. If I have a cart, tell me what I save: "Your Slip-ins are 20% off, or buy two and take 50% off the second pair."
- 3. Show my actual cart item with price, size and a comfort callout (cushioning, arch support), not only a name.
- 4. Cut the cart nag cadence. I've had cart emails on 11/05, 11/08, 11/11, 11/17, 11/19 and twice today. One or two is a reminder, more than that is nagging.
- 5. Make the "more options" grid match what I browse: men's slip-ins and walking shoes.
