## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part, 12 images without alt text, and invalid or duplicate head markup. The QA probe also got HTTP 403 on two tracked links, including Unsubscribe. The 403s are probably bot-blocking on the tracking redirect rather than dead links, but that is unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on the "hoka.com/en/us/return-start/" link.** The probed URL is the Cordial click-tracker `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:6ab6afa3.../51d14433?jwtH=...`. The decoded `jwtP` payload resolves to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261002_NewColors_Bondi7_...`.
- **403 on Unsubscribe.** The tracker `.../c2/.../e3d484e2?jwtH=...` decodes to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast...`.
- **Likely cause of both 403s.** Both links go through the same `e.emails.hoka.com` redirect host, and the other 10 probed links apparently passed. That points to bot or WAF filtering on these two destinations, or on the tracker for them. The data doesn't separate a real failure from a probe artifact, so verify both with a real client.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two open-tracking pixels, `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`. They sit on two different subdomains, which is a possible inconsistency in tracking or alignment setup.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 links were probed and 13 skipped, so the other 13 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part.** `[FAIL]` The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative.
- **Missing alt text.** 12 images are flagged. Ten are content images on `images.usw2.cordial.com/1664/...`, such as `US_Bondi7_TopLeft_1110519-ATPR.jpg`, `US_20261002_NewColors_Lifestyle_a.gif` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. The other two are the tracking pixels, where empty alt is acceptable but should be explicit as `alt=""`.
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset` attribute, so it declares nothing.
- **Viewport.** There are two conflicting `<meta name="viewport">` tags. One has `maximum-scale=1` and the other adds `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS.** The mobile rules `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values from unrendered template variables. Clients drop these declarations.
- **Global reset.** `* {... outline:none ...}` removes focus indicators for keyboard users.
- **Not checked.** The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the rest of the markup, such as role="presentation" on layout tables or Outlook MSO conditionals.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. The empty values in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` are template variables, but they are in the CSS, not user-facing text.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link.** One is present, pointing to `options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`, but the probe got a 403 on it (see section 2). If the 403 is real for recipients, this is a CAN-SPAM violation, so confirm it first.
- **Not verifiable.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC results and the physical postal address were not in the data provided (the HTML was truncated). I am not flagging them.
- **Sender.** `info@emails.hoka.com` is a branded subdomain, and the tracking hosts `e.` and `mg.emails.hoka.com` sit under the same parent domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs.** `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261002_NewColors_Bondi7_FES_STO_A60Day_...` are present and consistent across both decoded links.
- **Date match.** The campaign date `20261002` matches the send date.
- **Audience tags in URLs.** The campaign string includes segment tags such as `BondiPLPBrowsers_720BondiPurchasers`. Those are visible to anyone inspecting the URL and may populate analytics campaign names with very long values. This is minor.
- **Landing pages.** The return-start destination could not be verified because of the 403 (see section 2).
- **Stale asset name.** The banner file `US_20260701_NewFESBanner.jpg` carries a July date. It is probably a reused asset, not a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually open both 403 links (Unsubscribe first) in a normal browser and mail client. If they fail, fix the redirect or WAF rules. If they work, allowlist the QA probe's user agent.
2. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative. It is currently 0 chars.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 10 content images and `alt=""` to the two tracking pixels.
4. Fix the head. Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`, fill in `<title>`, and merge the viewport tags into one without `maximum-scale=1`.
5. Remove or default the empty-value CSS in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`. Restore focus outlines on links.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, authentication results and the footer postal address against the raw message.
