# Become a VIP for free (& save)
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a new one from DSW — after a week straight of "20% off" banners, now it's a whole different pitch: join VIP for free. Fine, but the second I scroll it's the same story I've griped about every single day this week — tall dress boots, over-the-knee boots, heeled booties, snakeskin flats. There's a "MEN" tab up top in the nav, which I appreciate seeing acknowledged exists, but nothing past that nav bar is aimed at me. Not one sneaker, not one walking shoe, nothing about cushioning or arch support.
- The "shop by size" grid near the bottom is whole numbers 5 through 11 — no width markers, no men's sizing at all, so even that feels built for someone else. I'm not clicking through a wall of knee-high boots to hunt for men's comfort shoes that probably aren't even in this campaign. This one gets read for two seconds and archived.
- I wouldn't forward this to anybody — my buddies and I don't wear over-the-knee suede boots, wide calf or otherwise.

## What's working

- The VIP points tiers (Club/Gold/Elite, 5-15 points per $1) are laid out clean and easy to read — if I were already a member I could actually parse my benefits here.
- "Free shipping for VIPs" and "in-store pickup" icons near the bottom are useful, practical info, not fluff.
- Layout is uncluttered, one column, big readable text — easy enough to skim on my phone without zooming.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — boots, booties, heels, sneakers-for-women — is women's footwear; I don't see a men's item anywhere in the whole email.
- "Sneakers from $59.99" tile is the closest thing to something I'd wear, and it's still shown as a small thumbnail with what looks like a women's silhouette, no info on style or fit.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or hands-free slip-on — nothing that speaks to how I actually pick a shoe.
- Same brand fatigue as every DSW email this week (2026-09-13 "20% off is leaving," 2026-09-12 "Take 20% off + 4 new trends") — different subject, same irrelevant content underneath.
- Shop-by-size grid tops out with no wide-width or men's numbering visible up front, which tells me this section wasn't built with me in mind either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this list — if DSW has any purchase history on me, send men's comfort/walking styles instead of boots I'll never buy. That's the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Add at least one men's comfort category block (walking shoes, slip-ons, arch support) alongside the boots — right now men are only in the nav bar, nowhere in the content.
- 3. Lead the offer with something concrete for VIP sign-up (a specific $ or % bonus for joining) rather than just "get points" — I don't know what joining actually saves me today.
- 4. If width matters for boots, do the same for men's shoes — call out wide-width availability the way this email does for wide calf.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Join VIP free: earn points on shoes you actually wear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New VIP perks, no fee — see what's in it for you`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Points on every purchase, starting today`
