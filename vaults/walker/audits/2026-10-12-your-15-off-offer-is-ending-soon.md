# Your $15 Off Offer Is Ending Soon
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same CIDER $15/$20/$60 tiered deal I got earlier today in "Go WILD: $15 off 🦁🐍🐆", and this one comes from "DSW 1-Day Only." That's a lot of urgency for a sender I've heard from a dozen times since the 8th. The green countdown banner is clear and the orange offer block is easy to read, so I'll give them that. But I've seen the same thing all week, and the "ending soon" feels like it's been ending since Thursday.
- The shoes in the middle are what let it down for me. A fuzzy lace-up boot, a velcro wedge sneaker, a Mary Jane flat, a leopard-print sneaker, a suede ankle boot and a platform clog. Every one is women's, and none is the cushioned, slip-on walking shoe I'm after. "So new, so you" is not me. The Adidas block is the only part with a brand I know, and it doesn't say whether it's men's. I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the top shows a real deadline, and it's big enough to read on my phone.
- The three-tier offer is plain: $15 off $49, $20 off $99, $60 off $199. I can do that math in my head.
- The black "Claim $15 off" button stands out well against the orange.
- "Shop Men" is one of the links under the hero, so I don't have to dig for my section.
- The "Shop by size" block with wide width is a good touch, since my feet like room.
- The footer is clear about free shipping for VIPs and in-store pickup.

## What's weak

- All six product tiles are women's styles. Nothing is hands-free, cushioned or arch-supported.
- The Adidas "up to 30% off*" doesn't show a single men's or walking shoe. I only see the logo.
- The red "$15, $20, or $60 off!" caption repeats under every shoe. It's small, and it's clutter.
- This is the same CIDER offer as the "Go WILD" email from this morning, so it feels like the fourth reminder this week.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and the exclusions (Skechers is on the list) aren't mentioned near the offer. Skechers is a brand I actually buy.
- I can't tell that "Your $15 Off Offer" is mine in any personal way. It's addressed to everybody.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put men's comfort shoes in the product grid for people who've clicked "Shop Men" before. I'd like to see slip-ons, walking shoes and cushioned sneakers, with the sale price on each shoe in larger type.
- 2. Say in the hero that some brands are excluded, so I'm not surprised at checkout.
- 3. Cut the repeated red captions and show one clear price per shoe.
- 4. Show a men's Adidas shoe in the Adidas block rather than just the logo.
- 5. Space out the sends. One countdown a day is plenty.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's comfort shoes, $15 off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code CIDER takes $15 off $49, $20 off $99, $60 off $199. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Walking shoes and slip-ons for men are included. Brand exclusions apply.`

If they are the same offer, the landing page and email are inconsistent.
