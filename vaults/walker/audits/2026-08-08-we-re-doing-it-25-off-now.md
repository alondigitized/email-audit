# We’re doing it. 25% off NOW!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- My eye went straight to that "25% OFF" banner up top — DSW's been running that number all week and I'll give them credit, it's easy to spot on a phone screen. But then I hit "BIG KICK ENERGY" and a woman in a bright red coat posing next to sneakers, and I knew right away this wasn't built with a guy my age in mind. Scroll down and it's fashion sneakers, heels, sandals, "Shop All Women" — this is a women's fashion drop with a discount code slapped on the front.
- The one bright spot is that "UP TO 40% OFF BIG-DEAL BRANDS" block near the bottom — New Balance, Crocs, adidas, Birkenstock, those are names I actually shop. But it's buried after two full screens of women's heels and mary-janes, and there's nothing about cushioning, arch support, or slip-on comfort anywhere in this email.
- I wouldn't click. I'd glance at the 25% off, register "not for me" within two seconds of seeing that hero image, and move on. Not forwarding this to anyone — my buddies aren't shopping fashion sneakers with me.

## What's working

- The 25% off headline up top is bold and easy to read without squinting — no zooming required.
- I recognize DSW, so the sender wasn't a stranger showing up in my inbox.
- That "40% off Nike, New Balance, Crocs, adidas, Birkenstock" block near the bottom actually lists brands I cross-shop.
- The layout is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, nothing looks glitchy this time.
- There's a VIP rewards mention near the bottom, so at least they're acknowledging loyalty members exist.

## What's weak

- Almost the entire email — hero model, sneaker lineup, "Shop All Women" button, heels/sandals grid — is built for a women's fashion shopper, not me.
- No mention anywhere of comfort, cushioning, or arch support — the stuff that actually gets me to click.
- The men's brands I'd actually want (New Balance, Crocs, Hoka-style comfort shoes) only show up as a small logo strip at the very bottom, after I've already scrolled past two women's-only sections.
- "BIG KICK ENERGY" and "Like confidence in a box" is marketing-speak that means nothing to a guy just looking for a shoe that doesn't hurt his feet by 3pm.
- Shop-by-size chart runs 5 to 11 — that's women's sizing, not mine, so even that felt like it wasn't meant for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with the men's comfort angle up front — if there's a men's cushioned walking shoe on sale, put it where the fashion sneaker model is now. Don't make me scroll past three women's sections to find something relevant.
- 2. Move that "40% off big-deal brands" block (Nike, New Balance, Crocs, Birkenstock) up near the top, or send me a men's-specific version if DSW has my purchase history.
- 3. Add one line of actual comfort copy somewhere — "all-day cushioning," "arch support built in" — instead of "confidence in a box."
- 4. Cut or shrink the heels/athleisure/sandals "confidence boosters" block for recipients who've only ever bought men's shoes from DSW.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New Balance, Crocs, Skechers — 25-40% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — up to 40% off men's comfort styles`
