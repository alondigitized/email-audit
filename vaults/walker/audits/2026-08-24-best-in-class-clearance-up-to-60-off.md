# Best-in-class clearance up to 60% off
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one broke the streak — no coupon math for once. "Up to 60% off clearance" is a real headline, and the size grid right up top (5 through 11) is something I actually use, so credit where due. But then it's sandals, sandals, sandals — women's wedge sandals, strappy flip-flops on a model doing a backbend on the beach — and not one men's shoe anywhere on the page. Steve Madden and Lucky Brand aren't brands I'd ever cross-shop against Skechers or Hoka.
- I scrolled the whole thing looking for something in my lane and came up empty. The "Casual Shoes from $29.99 / Heels from $44.99 / Court Sneakers from $64.99" strip is the closest thing to relevant, and even that's buried under two sandal blocks. Not clicking, not forwarding — this one's for someone else's closet.

## What's working

- The size-grid banner (5–11) up top is a smart, tappable touch — feels like they know their shoppers order by size.
- "Up to 60% off" is a clean, honest-looking headline with no fine-print gymnastics visible.
- The bottom module — free shipping, in-store pickup, DSW VIP rewards — is clear and I know exactly what those benefits are.
- Court sneakers at $64.99 in that bottom row at least brushes up against something I'd wear.

## What's weak

- Every hero image is women's sandals — nothing for a 62-year-old guy who needs arch support and cushioning.
- Two brands called out by name (Steve Madden, Lucky Brand) mean nothing to me; I shop Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance.
- "Back to School" banner with a kid in rollerblades — I've got no kids at that age, doesn't apply.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere — the stuff that actually decides my purchase.
- After a solid week of DSW coupon emails, this is a full pivot to a different email with zero acknowledgment of who's been opening those — feels like a stranger, not a member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this by gender/category — if DSW knows I've clicked men's or comfort categories before, lead with men's clearance, not sandals.
- 2. Swap in a men's casual/walking shoe hero image with a comfort callout (cushioned, slip-on, arch support) instead of the beach sandal shots.
- 3. Move the size-grid module up as the very first thing — it's the strongest, most useful element on the page, don't bury its impact under a "New Styles Added" header only.
- 4. Cut the Back to School block for shoppers without kids in that age range — dead weight for me.
- 5. Tie in the DSW VIP rewards earlier, near the top offer, so it feels earned rather than tacked on at the bottom.
