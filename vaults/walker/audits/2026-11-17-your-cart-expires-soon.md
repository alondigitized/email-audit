# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- The skull sneaker again. It's the same black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I've been nagged about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and the 11/15 "Your cart expires soon!" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends. I have never put anything in a cart. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is a bit presumptuous when I never said I liked them. I wouldn't be caught dead in that shoe.
- The second half redeems it a little. Slip-on boots, a John Deere waterproof slip-in and a Murette slipper are the kind of thing I shop for. The Black Friday "Buy One, Get One 50% Off" banner is a real offer. But it sits below the fold, behind a hero that has nothing to do with me. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "Men's Slip-ins" John Deere waterproof boot and the Scofield slipper are the first things I actually care about.
- BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair is a clear offer, and the banner has good contrast.
- The "Shop All Best Sellers" black bar is big and easy to tap on my phone.
- Big, readable headlines with plenty of white space, so I don't need to zoom.
- The Skechers logo at the top matches the brand I know.

## What's weak

- The skull sneaker is the hero again. It's the same shoe I've seen in at least six sends in two weeks, and it isn't my style.
- "You liked these" implies a cart I never built. It feels like being watched, not known.
- The real deal, BOGO 50% off, is buried under the hero and the six-shoe grid.
- The women's sneakers and boots in the grid are a mismatch for me. Two of the six are clearly women's.
- The fine print is tiny, and "Discount auto-applied at cart. See below for details" is easy to miss.
- The cadence is exhausting. The 11/16 "Did you forget something?" came just yesterday, and this is the same email again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart reminders for a cart that doesn't exist. If I never added anything, send me something based on what I actually browse, like slip-ins.
- 2. Move the BOGO banner to the top and put a men's slip-in hero under it.
- 3. Drop the women's styles from my grid and show cushioning and arch support details.
- 4. Cap abandoned-cart sends at one per day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard table-based template with Attentive click-wrapped links. Automated QA found a failing unsubscribe link (HTTP 400), no plain-text part, and 12 images served over `http://`. The HTML is truncated and 30 of 42 links were not probed, so several checks are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Unsubscribe link returned 400.**
  - URL: `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5HVjkzZ1h1MmZpSFY0ajRYThIDMVN5GPP4jAYgAg&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&...&attn_sid=60ea702f-...`
  - The macro decodes to `unsubscribeLink`. The 400 may come from a token tied to the recipient or session that the probe can't satisfy, so it isn't conclusive. It is still a failure on the one link that must work.
- **Primary CTAs were not verified.**
  - "Complete Your Order" (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/vj73W-uHb...`) and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." (`.../e/c/LoHSjxpM...`) both returned 429.
  - The 429 is rate limiting by the Attentive redirector, so the links are not shown to be broken. Their destinations are unconfirmed, though.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 links were probed and 30 were skipped, so footer and social links are unvalidated.
- **No tracking-pixel issue.** The open pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...`) is HTTPS and present. Its alt text is missing, which is low severity (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images use `http://` sources.** All are on `image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`. Clients and proxies may block or warn on mixed content. The affected images:
  - Logo ("Skechers") and the CTA image ("Complete Your Order").
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`.
  - App Store and Google Play badges.
  - Curbside Pickup, Afterpay and Find a Store.
  - Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` is a content image with no alt, and it's also served over `http://`.
  - The open pixel has no alt. It should have `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the `<head>`.
- **Malformed charset declaration.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it declares nothing. This is visible in the source.
- **Generic alt text.** The alt "Text" on `49468f73-...png` is not descriptive.

No issues found. The visible, truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). I couldn't verify whether cart contents are dynamically populated, because the cart section is past the truncation point.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link fails (400)**, per section 2. This is the main compliance risk.
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars". This can hurt deliverability and spam scoring. It may also be an artifact of the AgentMail relay capture rather than the sent message.
- **Authentication not verifiable.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not a confirmed failure.
- **Not assessable from the data:** the physical postal address in the footer (truncated) and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (not provided).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are Attentive-wrapped (`/e/c/<opaque token>`), so UTM parameters and final destinations can't be read from the source.
- The 429 responses prevented resolving the "Complete Your Order" and product-link destinations. Landing-page alignment, and whether cart state persists, are unverified.
- No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unsubscribe flow.** Test `skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=...` in a real client and confirm it resolves to a working unsubscribe page. Also add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers if missing.
2. **Serve all images over HTTPS.** Change the 12 `http://image.emails.skechers.com/...` sources to `https://`.
3. **Add a multipart plain-text part.** First check whether the 0-char result is an AgentMail capture artifact.
4. **Fix the `<head>`.** Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and populate `<title>`.
5. **Fix alt text.**
   - Give `a6ec5198-...jpg` a descriptive alt.
   - Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
   - Replace the alt "Text" with something meaningful.
6. **Re-run the audit** with throttled probing or retries to resolve the 429s on the CTA links. Capture the full headers so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be checked, and capture the full HTML to check the footer address and the cart-content merge fields.
