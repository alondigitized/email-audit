# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 2/10 (prev 2/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I picked up my phone wanting slip-on shoes with good arch support, no bending over, no fuss. The store part of this — browsing men's shoes, filtering to wide fit, looking at a pair of Arch Fit slip-ins — actually worked okay. But the moment I tried to do anything that mattered — log in, put the shoe in my cart, or look at my cart — the wheels came off. My "Log In" tap did nothing I could see. Adding to cart threw an error I had to hunt for. And when I tapped through to view my cart, I got a **blank white screen**. Nothing. That's the step where you're supposed to take my money, and there's nobody home.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**2/10** — unchanged from yesterday's 2/10, and arguably a lateral move at best. Browsing improved slightly (the men's shoe menu and wide-fit filters are genuinely useful to me), but the cart — the one step that actually converts a browser into a buyer — now renders as a completely blank page. That's not friction, that's a dead end.

## 3. What's Working

- The hamburger menu under "Men" is clean and well-organized: Shoes, Shop by Activity, Comfort Technologies, Collections, and — right there without digging — "Hands Free Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Extra Wide Fit." That's exactly the language I'm looking for.
- The category page for Hands Free Slip-ins leads with big, blue, easy-to-tap filter pills for Wide Fit / Extra Wide Fit / Medium Fit. That's rare — most sites bury width sizing.
- The product page for the Arch Fit 3.0 - Fiddow has a big clear photo, a clean price, and a straightforward size grid (7.0–13.0) with generous tap targets.
- Text throughout is legible without pinch-zooming — decent font sizes on the category and product pages.

## 4. What's Weak

- **Login did nothing visible.** The screenshot after "Log In" is pixel-identical to the homepage. No modal, no form, no account page — I have no idea if I'm signed in or not.
- **Popup dismissal did nothing visible either** — same screenshot as the homepage before it. Either there was no popup to dismiss (fine) or dismiss failed silently (not fine) — I can't tell, and that ambiguity itself is a problem.
- **Add to Cart threw a "Please select a size" error** — meaning the flow let me get to a warning state instead of clearly guiding me to pick a size first. As someone who doesn't always scroll carefully, I could easily miss why nothing happened.
- **View Cart is a totally blank white page.** This is the worst thing in the whole walkthrough. If I'd actually gotten a shoe into my cart, this is where I'd give up and probably close the app for good.
- **Search for "comfort shoes" surfaced women's shoes first** — "Contour Foam - Cozy Fit Pure Comfort" and "Relaxed Fit: Glide-Step Comfort," both listed as Women's. I'd just spent the whole session in the Men's section. Nothing about this felt like it knew who I was.
- Nowhere in this journey did I see cushioning, arch support, or comfort-tech copy called out prominently — I had to infer it from the product name ("Arch Fit"), not from any on-screen benefit messaging.
- The product page flags "Excluded from promotions" right under the price — for a guy who's cross-shopping DSW clearance racks against Skechers, that's a small but real turn-off.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page rendering bug immediately.** A blank page at checkout is a five-alarm fire — it caps revenue at zero regardless of how good the rest of the funnel is.
2. **Make login state visible.** If I tap "Log In" and land back on the exact same homepage, I need some signal — a name, an avatar change, something — telling me whether it worked.
3. **Guide size selection before Add to Cart is tappable**, or scroll/highlight the size grid automatically on error, instead of a text warning I might not see.
4. **Fix gender relevance in search.** If I'm logged in and browsing Men's, "comfort shoes" should not lead with Women's clogs.
5. **Surface comfort/arch-support benefit copy above the fold on product pages** — that's the whole reason people like me choose Skechers over a look-alike at DSW.

## 6. Bottom Line

The browsing experience finally looks like someone thought about a guy like me — wide fit filters, hands-free slip-ins front and center. But none of that matters if I can't actually check out. A blank cart page and a login that goes nowhere are the kind of thing that sends me right back to Amazon or into a Hoka store instead. Score holds at 2/10 until the cart actually shows something.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

This audit covers a mobile session through skechers.com (Homepage → Log In → Men category → Men > Shoes → Product detail → Add to Cart → View Cart → Search). The site is built on Salesforce Commerce Cloud (Demandware) with a heavy third-party marketing/analytics tag stack (30+ async scripts: GTM, Monetate, TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, Taboola, Quantum Metric, PulseInsights, etc.) loaded on every page. Two infrastructure-level issues stand out: a bot-detection/fingerprinting endpoint (Kasada `kpsdk`) returning HTTP 429 repeatedly, and a Bazaarvoice reviews API call using a hardcoded `pid=test` parameter that returns 403.

## Accessibility

16 axe violations across 3 of 8 pages audited (Homepage, View Cart, Search):

- **Critical:** `aria-required-parent` (Homepage) — an ARIA role is used without its required parent role/container, which can break assistive-technology navigation entirely for that widget.
- **Serious (View Cart only):** `document-title` (empty/missing `<title>`) and `html-has-lang` (missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`). Both are baseline requirements — their absence suggests the cart view is rendered via a client-side overlay/route that doesn't inherit the document shell used elsewhere (other sampled pages correctly carry `lang="en"`).
- **Moderate (repeated across pages):** landmark structure problems — `landmark-main-is-top-level`, `landmark-no-duplicate-main`, `landmark-one-main` (View Cart), `landmark-unique`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `region` (content not contained in a landmark), `heading-order`, `page-has-heading-one` (View Cart has no H1).
- **Minor:** `aria-allowed-role` on Homepage and Search — an element's `role` attribute value isn't valid for that element type.

The pattern (duplicate/non-top-level main and complementary landmarks, region violations) indicates nested or duplicated layout wrappers, likely from the third-party widget/overlay scripts injecting their own landmark roles into the DOM rather than page markup issues alone.

## SEO

- View Cart is missing a `<title>` element and an `<h1>` — both are default on-page SEO signals; while cart pages are typically `noindex`, a missing title also degrades browser tab/history/share-link identification.
- View Cart missing `lang` on `<html>` affects both accessibility and locale/hreflang signal consistency for that view.
- No other structural SEO issues (title/meta/lang) observed on the other 7 sampled pages.

Given the volume of async third-party scripts loaded before/alongside main content (30+ tags on Homepage alone), LCP and layout-shift risk from late-loading fonts/widgets should be re-measured with a Web Vitals collector (e.g., Lighthouse or `web-vitals` JS) before drawing conclusions.

## Mobile Optimization

- `--vh: 6.64px` custom property in the root `<style>` indicates a JS-based viewport-height correction (common mobile-Safari 100vh workaround) — functioning as expected, no defect observed.
- No mobile-specific console/network errors distinct from the desktop-shared issues below were observed.
- Font loading uses WebFontLoader (`wf-mulish-*` classes applied post-load), which causes a FOUT/FOIT pattern on first paint — a likely contributor to LCP variability, but unconfirmed without LCP data (see Performance).

## Console & Network Errors

**Network (7 errors):**
- **429 (repeated 4x):** `.../fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.616` — a Kasada bot-detection fingerprinting endpoint being rate-limited. This fires on multiple steps (not just one page), suggesting the client is retrying/re-requesting the fingerprint check faster than the server allows, or the bot-protection layer is throttling this session.
- **429:** `https://www.skechers.com/cart/` — the cart page endpoint itself was rate-limited on at least one request during the session.
- **403:** `BazaarvoiceProductData-GetProduct?pid=test` — a reviews-data API call using a literal `pid=test` placeholder parameter, returning 403. This looks like a debug/placeholder value that reached production request code rather than a real product ID, and is failing auth/lookup as a result.

**Console (31 errors, deduplicated patterns):**
- Repeated `429` failures matching the network errors above.
- Repeated `net::ERR_FAILED` (3x per cluster) — resource load failures, likely correlated with the 429s (browser reports the blocked/throttled fetch as a generic failure) or with ad-blocked/blocked third-party tags.
- Repeated **Summarizer API "Unsupported Summarizer API languages"** warnings — this is Chrome's built-in on-device `Summarizer` API (part of the experimental Prompt/Writer/Summarizer API family) being invoked with a language argument it doesn't support, causing the call to abort. This indicates a script on the page (likely a chatbot/AI-assist or browser-extension-injected feature) is probing `window.ai`/`Summarizer` without validating supported locales first.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the `pid=test` Bazaarvoice call** — audit the reviews-widget integration for hardcoded/placeholder product IDs reaching production requests; this is a functional defect, not just a console error.
2. **Investigate Kasada fingerprint 429s** — repeated rate-limiting on the bot-detection endpoint across multiple navigation steps may indicate the client-side fingerprinting script is re-firing on each SPA transition instead of once per session; check call frequency/caching.
3. **Repair View Cart document metadata** — add a non-empty `<title>`, an `lang` attribute on `<html>` (or ensure the cart view inherits the shared document shell), and a single `<h1>` heading.
4. **Deduplicate landmark roles** — audit for nested/duplicate `main` and `complementary` regions, likely introduced by third-party widget markup; wrap orphaned content in proper landmark elements to resolve the `region` violations.
5. **Fix `aria-required-parent` and `aria-allowed-role` violations** — the critical-severity finding on Homepage should be prioritized first, as it can fully break screen-reader interaction with that component.
6. **Re-run with LCP/CLS instrumentation** — the current run has no captured values for either metric on any step; re-audit with a proper Web Vitals collector before making performance claims, especially given the async-script volume on initial load.
7. **Identify and remove/guard the Summarizer API call** — locate the script invoking `window.ai.Summarizer` with unsupported languages and either gate it behind a feature/support check or remove it if unintentional (e.g., leftover from a browser extension test or an AI-assist feature not fully gated).
