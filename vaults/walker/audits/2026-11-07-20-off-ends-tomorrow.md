# 20% off ends (!)TOMORROW(!)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count of the 20% emails this week. This is the same TINSEL code from the 5th and the 6th, and now the subject tells me it ends tomorrow. The "20% OFF*" at the top is big and easy to read on my phone, and the black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is hard to miss. But the footer says the code is valid November 5–11, so "ends tomorrow" doesn't match the dates I can see.
- I scrolled the whole thing. Every shoe in the grid is a woman's boot, heel, or flat. I didn't see one man's shoe, and nothing about slip-ons, cushioning, or arch support. The only men's item was a link in the nav bar. The UGG 30% block and the free squishy are not for me either.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd leave it unread and maybe check the DSW app if I needed shoes. I wouldn't forward it. My wife might like the UGGs, but I'm not sending a DSW email for that.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF*" headline is huge. I can read it without squinting.
- The black "CLAIM 20% OFF" button is clear and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The nav bar has a MEN link, so I'm not locked out of the men's section.
- The "Shop by Size" block with Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons is a nice touch. Wide width matters to a lot of guys my age.
- The footer is clear about the free shipping, in-store pickup, and 15% off SMS perks.

## What's weak

- Not one men's shoe in the grid. It's boots, heels, mary janes, and fuzzy slippers. I felt like a stranger.
- The ends-tomorrow urgency feels cheap after a week of the same code. The "(!)TOMORROW(!)" in the subject looks pushy.
- The email stacks too many offers: 20% off, 10% off for veterans, 30% off UGG, a free squishy, and a VIP pitch. I couldn't tell which one was the point.
- The squishy is a kid's toy that I'd never want. "Selling fast! Butter get yours ASAP" is cutesy and a bit silly.
- The fine print is tiny and long, and I'd have to zoom to read it. The asterisks make me wonder what's excluded.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Send me a men's grid of slip-ons, walking shoes, and cushioned sneakers from the brands DSW carries, like Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, and New Balance. That would lift this email more than any other change.
- 2. Pick one offer and lead with it. Drop the squishy and the UGG block from a sale-ending reminder.
- 3. Make the deadline match the footer. If the code ends the 11th, say that. Don't say tomorrow.
- 4. Make the fine print bigger, or link to a clean page with the exclusions.
- 5. Slow the cadence. A week of near-identical 20% emails teaches me to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built XHTML template. One probed CTA link (the SMS opt-in) returned HTTP 400. The plain-text part is heavily URL-weighted, and authentication status couldn't be verified. QA pass rate was 62% (1 fail, 2 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA, HTTP 400.** The link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/HOQpjiYeZYRQwkOVbKfR5w~~/AAQRxRA~/We7OpRNO…Q~~`, a roughly 700-character click-tracking wrapper on `ablink.dsw.com`.
  - The probe got a 400 from the redirect host, so the tracking link may be malformed or expired. A bot-probe artifact is also possible, but the QA data doesn't distinguish the two.
  - This is a promotional offer CTA, so a failure here directly costs conversions. Verify manually.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 83 links were probed and 71 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. This is a coverage limit, not a defect.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two image-based trackers:
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261107_P251273_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2`
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/WFso2R2LeJY6MQDGFrmr2w~~/AAQRxRA~/…`
  - The `mi_u=225090073` parameter is a per-recipient identifier in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.** Both are tracking pixels: `o.gif` from `mi.dsw.com` and the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` if not already set, so screen readers skip them. This is low severity.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **Malformed meta tags.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. The charset is not declared in a way parsers will honor, so use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
- **Web fonts.** Futura and HelveticaNow load from `braze-images.com` with `.woff` and `.ttf` files. Many clients, including Gmail, ignore `@font-face`, so these will fall back. This is expected and not a defect unless the fallback stacks are missing. They were not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source or QA output. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** The header is absent because of the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is not a confirmed failure. Check the raw headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 31,086 of 37,918 characters (about 82%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The long tracking-wrapped links inflate the text-to-link ratio, which is a minor spam-filter signal.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address.** The unsubscribe link was reportedly always probed, and the QA output lists no failure for it. The footer and physical address fall outside the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination URLs are hidden behind opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect tokens, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be verified from the HTML.
- The only identifiable campaign marker is `mi_ecmp=20261107_P251273_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_2` on the pixel. The `WM-_Offer` segment contains a hyphen followed by an underscore. That looks like an empty or missing token in the campaign naming convention, and I'm inferring it from the string alone.
- The offer CTA that failed with a 400 is the only link whose landing outcome is known to be broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/HOQpjiYe…`). If it still returns 400, fix or re-issue the tracked link before further sends.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Replace the two malformed meta tags with `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge">`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel and the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.
5. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from raw headers at a seed inbox, since the relay hid them.
6. Trim the plain-text alternative, for example by dropping the tracking wrapper from the text part or reducing link count.
7. Rerun QA with a higher probe limit to cover the 71 skipped links, and confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirects.
8. Check the `mi_ecmp` campaign ID template for the `WM-_Offer` empty-token pattern.
