# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Did you forget something?" cart nag Skechers has sent me all week, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. Last time the nag shoe was the pink flower sneaker. This time the top banner shouts "READY TO CHECK OUT?" and the hero shoe is a taupe slip-in, but the caption underneath still says "D'Lites - Spring Bloom". The photo and the name don't match, and that makes me trust it less. The 10/22 "Final hours to double up on comfort" email earlier today actually knew who I was. This one treats me like a stranger who abandoned a basket.
- The one good thing is down the page: "Members take 20% off and earn a $5 reward on $75+", valid through 10/26. That's a real offer, and I am a Plus member. But it sits below a big empty gap, six shoes and a black bar, so I'd have to scroll to find it. Of the six shoes, three are women's, one is a boy's sneaker, and two are men's slip-ins. I'm a man shopping for comfort, so most of that grid isn't for me.
- Would I click? Probably not, because I have no cart. I might glance at the Contour Foam slip-in, since that's the kind of shoe I wear. I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read.

## What's working

- The "Members take 20% off" and "$5 reward on $75+" block is clear, and it has a deadline of 10/26.
- The "Complete your order" buttons are big, plain, and easy to tap on my phone.
- The men's "Slip-ins: Contour Foam - Cozy Fit" sneaker is the kind of hands-free comfort shoe I look for.
- The Skechers logo and the layout look like what I've seen on the site, so I trust where it came from.
- The category links (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) are large and readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- I never started a cart, so "Ready to check out?" and "Did you forget something?" feel wrong, and a little pushy. It's the 4th or 5th one this week.
- The hero shoe is a taupe sneaker, but the caption says "Spring Bloom", the women's flower shoe from the earlier emails. The picture and the name don't match.
- The best offer, 20% off, is buried below the product grid. The top of the email has no price or member benefit at all.
- Most of the grid is women's or kids' shoes, and there's nothing about cushioning, arch support, or comfort.
- The fine print is long and full of exclusions: sale items, login required, $75 minimum, and rewards paid only after the item ships. "20% off" feels less honest after reading it.
- There are two identical orange and black "Complete your order" buttons plus a "Shop all best sellers" bar, and the large gap between the banner and the shoe makes the layout feel stretched.

## Recommendations

- 1. Open with the member benefit at the top, "Hi Walker, your 20% Plus discount ends 10/26", and put the cart nag second. Send the cart email only when someone actually has a cart.
- 2. Make the shoe in the hero match its caption. Show a real item, with the price and the member price.
- 3. Build the grid from what I look at: men's slip-ins, arch fit, walking. Add a short line on cushioning or arch support under each shoe.
- 4. Pull the key terms (code, minimum, end date) up into one visible line and leave the legal text at the bottom.
- 5. Cut one of the two "Complete your order" buttons, and use specific button copy such as "Shop men's slip-ins".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your 20% member discount ends 10/26`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-ins you viewed, 20% off for Plus members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on comfort slip-ins through Sunday, plus a $5 reward on $75+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cushioned, hands-free styles for men are in the sale. Ends 10/26.`
