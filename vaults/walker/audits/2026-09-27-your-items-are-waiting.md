- The boots & outdoor 20% off banner is a real, specific, dated offer (thru 9/28) — that's the kind of thing that gets my attention.
- Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit "Altman" and the plain white Arch Fit Arcade sneaker in the grid are at least in my comfort-and-simplicity wheelhouse.
- Layout's clean, big buttons, readable text — no zooming needed, which I appreciate.

## What's weak

- That pink floral D'Lites "cart" is not mine and hasn't been for days — I don't have a cart, and this is the fourth near-identical nag email in a row (per the brand history, same shoe, same "waiting" angle since 9/16).

- The "more options" grid is heavy on women's and kids' styles (4 of 6) — for a guy my age that's a lot of scrolling past stuff that's not for me.
- The real offer — 20% off boots — is buried after the fake cart and the mixed-bag grid instead of leading.

- 3. Reorder the "more options" grid to lead with men's/unisex comfort styles for recipients with a male browsing history, not mixed randomly.
