# The Styles Everyone Is Looking For
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Another Skechers in the inbox, and before I even tapped it, I saw that same garbage preview text — raw JSON code spilling out where a real sentence should be. Third or fourth time I've seen this from them in June alone. At this point it feels like their tech team fixed it for a week and then forgot. Frustrating.
- Inside the email, the layout is clean and the BOGO hero loads fine. I'll give them that. What actually stopped my scroll was the ARCH FIT section — that's my language. Arch support, men's options, real shoe pictures. Same with COZY FIT right underneath. Those two sections are doing real work for someone like me. But then there's a big lifestyle chunk for something called BOBS at the top, with a woman in a summer outfit — that's clearly not pointed at me. I don't know what BOBS is, and nothing in the email explains it.
- I'd probably click through to the ARCH FIT men's section. I wouldn't forward this — too scattered to recommend to anyone specific. The BOGO headline is useful but it floats above a women's brand I don't shop, which muddies the pitch before we even get to the good stuff.

## What's working

- **ARCH FIT section lands** — two clear buttons, "SHOP WOMEN'S" and "SHOP MEN'S," and the shoe images look like walkers, not fashion pieces. That's exactly the category I want.
- **COZY FIT follows right after** — slip-ons and cushioned silhouettes, which is comfort-first territory. The pairing of these two sections in sequence felt intentional.
- **BOGO offer is right at the top** — "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF" is readable, specific, and the "SHOP BOGO" button is unambiguous. I didn't have to hunt for the deal.
- **2X POINTS badge in the footer** — I'm a Skechers Plus member, so this registers. Small, but I noticed it.
- **No layout chaos in the body** — the email renders cleanly, no broken image boxes, no overlapping text. After some of the preview-text disasters this month, at least the inside looks professional.

## What's weak

- **BOBS section is the featured block and it's not for me** — women's lifestyle brand, woman in a sundress, "SHOP BOBS" CTA. It's taking prime real estate in an email sent to a 62-year-old man who's looking for walking shoes with arch support. Wrong audience, wrong placement.
- **The broken JSON preview is back** — same problem as the June 3rd "See What Everyone's Adding To Their Cart" email. Skechers clearly hasn't fixed the preheader injection bug, and it's now happening on a second subject line that rhymes with that send too.
- **Subject "The Styles Everyone Is Looking For" says nothing** — it could be a furniture store, a clothing brand, a grocery chain. There's no comfort signal, no men's signal, no arch support, no deal. Nothing that earns the open from my inbox.
- **No deadline on the BOGO** — I saw the offer but couldn't tell if it expires tonight or in three weeks. That kills the urgency.
- **No personalization anywhere in the body** — after the June 1st "Hi, Walker" email that felt like they knew me, this one treats me like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the ARCH FIT section into the hero, or make BOGO comfort-specific** — "BOGO on Arch Fit & Slip-Ins" does double duty: the deal stays, and it targets a comfort-first male shopper instead of the whole catalog. The BOBS section could drop to the bottom or to a women's-segmented send.
- 2. **Fix the preheader, permanently** — this is the second broken JSON preview from Skechers this month with almost identical subject-line framing. A real preheader like "Arch Fit, Cozy Fit, and BOGO 50% off now" would have actually made me tap this faster.
- 3. **Add a BOGO deadline** — even "this week only" adds enough friction to make me act rather than flag for later and forget.
- 4. **Re-inject personalization** — I got "Hi, Walker" on June 1st. I got nothing here. The platform clearly has my name; use it.
- 5. **Reframe the social proof** — "TOP SEARCHED. MOST LOVED." reads like filler copy. Replace with a brief stat or a single customer quote about Arch Fit comfort.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers "The Styles Everyone Is Looking For"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken experience (empty merge token in greeting) and a systemic HTTP asset delivery issue affecting 11+ images. All other categories are structurally sound.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken destination URLs detected.** 26 click-redirect links via `click.emails.skechers.com` were not probed (expected — tracking redirects return non-200 to HEAD requests).

**Third-party pixel exposure:** Three beacon pixels load on open:
- `beacon.krxd.net` — Salesforce/Krux DMP user-match pixels, including one carrying a hashed PII value (`partner_uid=9153f33f…sha25`) and one with a raw subscriber ID (`partner_uid=121173391`).
- `ink1000.com` — secondary open-tracking pixel served over **plain HTTP** (see Section 3).
- `pixel.app.returnpath.net` — Validity/Return Path inbox placement pixel.

These are standard ESP integrations but the raw subscriber ID in the Krux pixel (`121173391`) is transmitted unencrypted over HTTPS — low severity, but worth noting if PII handling is under review.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Systemic HTTP image delivery:** 11 images are served from `http://image.emails.skechers.com` (not HTTPS). Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook 2019+ block mixed-content images by default — these assets will render as broken placeholders for a significant portion of recipients.

Affected assets include the Skechers logo, app store badges, social icons (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube), Curbside Pickup icon, and AfterPay badge:
```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png  ← logo
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-…png
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-…png  ← App Store
… (8 more)
```
Fix: repoint all `src` values to `https://image.emails.skechers.com/…`.

**[HIGH] HTTP open-tracking pixel:**
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&…
```
Also missing `alt=""` — both issues on the same asset.

**[MEDIUM] Missing alt text:** Two images have no `alt` attribute at all — the `o.gif` tracking pixel and `49468f73….png`. Screen readers will announce the filename; the pixel should have `alt=""`.

**CSS desktop/mobile toggle:** The email uses a `min-width: 768px` media query for the desktop/mobile content swap but also has a separate block at `max-width: 480px`. The 481–767px range (small tablets) will render `mobile-content` visible and `desktop-content` hidden per the default styles, which is likely correct but untested in the QA run.

`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` is set globally — this prevents iOS from auto-sizing small text, which is intentional but should be verified against the legal/footer font sizes at small viewports.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**[CRITICAL] Empty greeting slot confirmed by QA:**
> `[FAIL] Empty greeting slot: Greeting has empty name field (e.g. "Hi ,")`

The first-name merge token resolved to an empty string, producing a broken salutation. This indicates either a missing fallback value in the ESP template or a data hygiene issue in the segment. The fix is a conditional default in the template logic, e.g.:
```
Hi %%=IIF(EMPTY(FirstName), "there", FirstName)=%%,
```
(syntax varies by ESP — Skechers uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud based on the `sfmc` string in the Krux pixel).

No other unresolved `%%`, `{{`, or `*|` tokens detected in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / CASL:** Preheader and visible physical address presence could not be fully verified from the truncated HTML, but a footer table structure is present. Full source review required to confirm mailing address inclusion.

**Unsubscribe:** Not visible in truncated source — must be confirmed present in footer. SFMC typically injects a list-unsubscribe header automatically; verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in the raw message envelope.

**Authentication:** Sending domain is `emails.skechers.com`. SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment should be confirmed against the envelope `From` — not verifiable from HTML alone.

**Krux pixel with subscriber ID:** `partner_uid=121173391` is an unmasked internal ID transmitted to a third party on open. Verify this is covered under Skechers' data processing agreements and privacy policy.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Campaign ID `MKG_US_NA_U_MULTI_ENGAGEDOPEN_EN_06052026` appears in two beacon URLs, confirming campaign attribution is instrumented at the pixel level. UTM parameter presence on destination click URLs could not be verified (26 links use click-redirect wrapping — UTM passthrough depends on ESP redirect configuration). This should be spot-checked in the analytics platform post-send.
