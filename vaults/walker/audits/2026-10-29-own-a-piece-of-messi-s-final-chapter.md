## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text alternative (0 chars, the one QA FAIL). It also loads several images over `http://`, including the open-tracking pixel, and a few images lack proper alt text. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **18 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them because they are click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com`). Their destinations and any UTM parameters are unverified.
- **Insecure `http://` links and images:**
  - The "arrow" image is loaded 4 times from `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`.
  - The open-tracking pixel is `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a185d822b0-c5334906-ae16-407a-a03e-1a1f86e55e9d-000000/LVhtIS9HyiRG_wAnzFvGJgVWE…`.
  - Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content may drop these. A blocked pixel would undercount opens.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text part.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This is a multipart/alternative gap that hurts accessibility and deliverability.
- **Charset meta is ineffective.** The source has `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` with no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so parsers ignore it. The MIME header usually covers this, so the risk is low.
- **Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `dv.adidas.com/o/81f63838-5128-4bd9-b902-f14001aae24d?cp_tp=…` has no `alt` attribute.
  - The tracking pixel `…LVhtIS9HyiRG_wAnzFvGJgVWEaABD5dvdtMj2IJyxXc=452` has no `alt`. It is decorative and should have `alt=""`.
- **Weak alt on a decorative image.** The "arrow" image has `alt="arrow"`, which a screen reader announces. If it is purely decorative, `alt=""` is better.
- **Possible mismatch at the breakpoints.** Some rules use `max-width:640px` and others `max-width:639px`, while the desktop rules use `min-width:640px`. That could cause inconsistent styling at exactly 640px. I could not confirm this from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FirstName]`. The truncated source means the body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers are unconfirmed.** `Authentication-Results` was not found, and the QA notes it was expected via the AgentMail relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact, not a sender defect, so verify against the raw headers.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, and the physical address are all outside the truncated source. No violation is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links are wrapped in `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects and were skipped by the QA probe. The final landing URLs and UTM parameters are therefore unknown. Follow the redirects to check them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text alternative.** Send a multipart/alternative message. This fixes the only hard QA FAIL.
2. **Serve all images and the tracking pixel over HTTPS.** That means `image.link.adidas.com` and `click.comms.adidas.com`. Check that both hosts have valid certificates.
3. **Fix alt text.** Add a descriptive `alt` to the `dv.adidas.com` image. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and the arrow icon.
4. **Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` to the charset meta**, or drop it and rely on the MIME header.
5. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers.** This would verify the unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` headers, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, the postal address, and the redirect destinations and UTMs.
