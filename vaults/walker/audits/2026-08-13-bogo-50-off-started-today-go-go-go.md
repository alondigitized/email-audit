# BOGO 50% OFF started today—GO GO GO
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- BOGO 50% off is at least a real, plain number right up top — I'll give them that. But then "SO NEW, SO YOU" rolls in with six pairs of shoes, and every single one is a women's bootie, slingback, or heeled sandal. Not one sneaker, not one flat comfort shoe, not one thing with laces. This is the same DSW pattern I've been getting all week — a decent headline offer wrapped around a product wall that has nothing to do with a guy my age looking for something cushioned and easy on the feet.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" grid with 5 through 11 half-sizes is a dead giveaway this whole layout is built for women's shoes, and "SHOP WIDE CALF" seals it. I'm not digging through this for anything relevant.
- Not opening a second time, not forwarding it, not clicking through. Marking it read and moving on — same as the last five from this brand.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% off headline is big, bold, and easy to read without my readers.
- "In stores & online. Prices reflected at checkout" is honest, plain language — no hunting for the catch.
- The "SHOP MEN" link is right there in the top nav, so at least the door isn't locked, just not what they pushed on me.
- VIP rewards callout (5% back, free shipping, birthday gift) is clear if I were inclined to join.

## What's weak

- Every featured product is a women's heel, bootie, or slingback — nothing for men, nothing comfort-focused, nothing I'd wear.
- "Shop By Size" running 5 to 11 in half sizes confirms this whole layout was built women's-first and men just got a nav link.
- "Wide Calf" option — I don't know a single guy who's shopping boots by calf width.
- No mention of arch support, cushioning, slip-on comfort, or any of the stuff that actually gets me to click a shoe email.
- "School Supply Stock-Up" framing feels like it's aimed at moms shopping for kids, not a 62-year-old buying for himself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least half the "So New, So You" grid to men's casual/comfort styles — sneakers, slip-ons, walking shoes — if this is a mass send that's supposed to include men.
- 2. Drop a men's comfort callout ("Cushioned. Arch support. Slip right in.") somewhere above the fold instead of assuming one nav link covers it.
- 3. If DSW is going to keep segmenting by gender in creative, they should actually segment the send list — I shouldn't get the women's heels version five times in one week.
- 4. Cut "Shop Wide Calf" from a general send or pair it with an equivalent men's-specific callout (wide width is already there, that one's fine).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort styles: BOGO 50% off starts today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `BOGO 50% off sneakers & slip-ons — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, arch-support styles now buy one, get one 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Comfort shoes for men, half off the second pair`
