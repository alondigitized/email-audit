# Look who made the sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the Bondi 9. Now that's a shoe I actually buy. A big cushioned Bondi on the hero, and the email only talks about that one shoe, which I like. But it's the same Black Friday week I've been getting HOKA mail all along. "Not like other sales" and "Black Friday is here" came this morning, and "Don't leave savings on the table" came yesterday. So "Look who made the sale" feels like another nudge from a pushy salesman.
- The Bondi focus is a real step up from the Clifton and running pitches earlier this week. I'm a walker, and the Wide and X-Wide blurb near the bottom speaks to my feet. But the discount is buried. The fine print says "up to 20% off Bondi 9," and the top banner shouts about a free beanie at $250+, which I don't care about. I'd probably tap "Shop Sale," but I wouldn't forward this.

## What's working

- The Bondi 9 hero is huge and clear, and it's the cushioned walker I'd actually wear.
- "Super-cushioned" in the headline speaks right to what I shop for.
- The four product tiles are Women/Men Bondi 9 with big readable labels, and the colors are easy to compare.
- The Wide and X-Wide message is a real fit point, and it matches my "the shoe that fits wins" rule.
- The black-and-white buttons are large enough to tap on my phone, and the text is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- The actual discount isn't in the headline. I have to dig into the tiny footer to find "up to 20% off." The headline just says "on sale."
- The tiles show no prices, so I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- The yellow beanie banner at the top competes with the Bondi story, and a $250 spend is a stretch for one pair of shoes.
- "Better move fast" is vague urgency with no date, and I've heard "going, going" all week.
- The footer fine print is tiny and hard for my eyes. The "Locate a Store / Free Shipping / FLY for 30" links are small, plain text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the number in the hero: "Bondi 9, up to 20% off" with a visible end date.
- 2. Show sale and original prices under each tile.
- 3. Drop the beanie banner or move it below the shoes, so there's one offer at a time.
- 4. Say "walking" and "all-day comfort" in the copy, and lead the Wide sizes higher up.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Send one strong Bondi email instead of a daily pile-on.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bondi 9 now up to 20% off`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Bondi 9 colors, plus Wide and X-Wide sizes. Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Cordial-sent HOKA broadcast with consistent UTM tagging, but the QA run scored 56%. The main problems are a 0-character plain-text part, 403 responses on the unsubscribe and returns links, 14 images with no alt text, and no SPF/DKIM/DMARC data. Several of these are only partly confirmed, as noted below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 403.** The URL is `https://e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:…:1/597ed1ca?jwtH=…`. Decoding the `jwtP` payload shows it redirects to `https://options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=69e560485cc95da09e001cd9&utm_source=US_Broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261127_PM1_Bondi9Sale_FollowUp2_…`. A 403 on a Cordial click-tracking URL is most likely bot-blocking of the QA probe, not a dead link. It is unconfirmed either way, so verify it with a real client.
- **Returns link returned 403.** Link text is `hoka.com/en/us/return-start/`. It is wrapped in the same Cordial tracker and decodes to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/return-start/?utm_source=US_Broadcast…`. The same bot-block caveat applies.
- **Click coverage is thin.** "12 material links probed, 12 skipped" means the CTA and product links were not verified. Only the two footer-type links above were actually tested.
- **Two tracking pixels on different hosts.** One is `e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/1664:…` and the other is `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/eJyM…`. The second host (`mg.emails.hoka.com`) differs from the click-tracking host. Confirm both sending and tracking domains are intentionally aligned and have valid certificates.
- **Possible link expiry.** The JWT payload includes `"ce":86400`. If that is a 24-hour expiry, tracked links, including unsubscribe, would stop working a day after send. This is inferred from the token, not confirmed, but worth checking against Cordial's behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 14 images.** These are 12 content images and 2 tracking pixels:
  - Content images include `H_US_Banner_GWPBeanie_1.jpg`, `H_US_Subcopy_Hero_Bondi9Markdown_2.jpg`, `H_US_CTA_BFEA_1.jpg`, `H_US_Headline_Merch_StylesThatWillSellOut_2.jpg`, the four 600px product tiles (`H_US_WBondi9_TopLeft_1162016-WKP.jpg`, etc.), `H_US_Secondary_Wides_1.jpg`, `US_20260817_Wides_CTA1.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`.
  - Several of these carry the headlines, offers and CTAs, so with images blocked the message and calls to action are not available to readers. Screen-reader users get nothing from them either.
  - The pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Charset declaration is ineffective.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it does not declare a charset.
- **Conflicting viewport tags.** There are two `<meta name="viewport">` tags with different values. One sets `maximum-scale=1` and the other sets `user-scalable=yes`. `maximum-scale=1` restricts pinch zoom on some clients.
- **Invalid CSS in the media query.** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values. These are template variables that were never populated, and clients will drop the declarations.
- **Universal selector reset.** `* {…padding:0px;border:none;outline:none;}` is applied globally and can override table and cell attributes in some clients.
- **Custom web fonts.** The `@font-face` fonts (`TheFutureHOKA`) load from `hoka.com`. I could not see the fallback stack in the truncated source, so I can't confirm a safe fallback exists.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated HTML. The empty `px` values in `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border` are the only sign of unpopulated template variables, and they are in CSS, not content. The rest of the HTML was truncated and could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters.** Multipart/alternative with no text part hurts deliverability. It may be an artifact of how AgentMail captured the message, so confirm it in the raw MIME.
- **`Authentication-Results` header is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `emails.hoka.com` are unknown. This is a gap in the data, not a confirmed failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not checked.** They are not in the QA data, and bulk-sender requirements make them worth verifying.
- **Unsubscribe link is present in the body** (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe`), which is good. Its 403 is covered in section 2.
- **Physical postal address could not be verified.** The footer was truncated, so I can't confirm it is present.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and consistent on the two decoded links.** Both carry `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261127_PM1_Bondi9Sale_FollowUp2_A15Day_720WideXWidePurchasers_365WideTag_7DaySubs`. The campaign string contains no spaces or special characters.
- **Campaign name encodes audience segmentation.** It includes tokens like `720WideXWidePurchasers` and `7DaySubs`. This is not an error, but it is exposed to anyone inspecting the URL.
- **`hmail=<64-hex>` and `cordial_id` are appended to destination URLs.** This is a hashed identifier that persists in landing-page URLs, analytics and referrers. Confirm it is stripped or excluded from analytics collection.
- **Landing-page alignment for the product and CTA links could not be assessed.** Those links were not probed.
- **Image filename `US_20260817_Wides_CTA1.jpg` carries an August date in a November campaign.** This is probably a naming leftover. Confirm it is not a stale creative.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 content images, especially the offer and CTA banners. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part to the MIME message.
3. Verify the unsubscribe and returns tracked links with a normal browser or client. If the 403s were only bot-blocking, allow-list the QA probe or switch to a browser-like probe.
4. Capture full headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) so authentication and one-click unsubscribe can be assessed.
5. Re-run link probing with the 12 skipped CTA and product links enabled.
6. Fix the template: populate or remove `.nav_padding` and `.nav_border`, fill in `<title>`, use `http-equiv="Content-Type"` on the charset meta, and consolidate to one viewport tag without `maximum-scale=1`.
7. Check whether the Cordial `ce:86400` token expiry applies to unsubscribe links.
8. Confirm the sending and tracking domains (`e.emails.hoka.com`, `mg.emails.hoka.com`) are intentionally aligned.
