# The Superstar Returns
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened this because I know adidas and "The Superstar Returns" sounded like it might be a sale on something I'd recognize. What I got was a beautifully shot email of a young woman contorted in a pose showing off a classic sneaker, followed by a World Cup giveaway box. Very polished. Very not for me.
- Not one word about how this shoe actually feels. No arch support, no cushioning spec, no mention of whether my size runs wide. The only "offer" is a sweepstakes I have to enter to win — that's not putting money back in my pocket, and I don't make shoe decisions based on lottery odds. The model looks like she's 30 and training for the Olympics. I'm 62 and trying to get through a Costco run without my heels aching by aisle three.
- I'd mark this read and move on. Wouldn't click, wouldn't forward it to a buddy.

- adidas is a name I trust — the brand alone gave this email its open
- Layout is clean and crisp, no broken images, text doesn't overlap, renders nicely on my phone
- adiClub shows up twice — at least they know I'm a member and they flagged something for me
- The World Cup tie-in is timely; summer 2026, I know the tournament is happening, feels current
- The prize box photo is legitimately creative — a Superstar sitting inside a soccer-field gift box is a nice visual payoff

## What's weak

- The hero model is a young athletic woman in tight gear — not a 62-year-old man, not even close to my demo
- Zero comfort language anywhere in the visible copy — no cushioning, no support, nothing about how this shoe wears after a few hours on your feet
- No purchase offer — the only thing here is a giveaway entry, not a discount or a deal I can act on today
- No visible "Shop Now" or "Buy the Superstar" button in the main body; "LEARN MORE" in small text at the top doesn't count as a CTA
- The Superstar is a retro fashion sneaker from my youth — I remember it from the b-ball courts in the '80s, not from a walking shoe recommendation

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a comfort story for the Superstar** — even if it's a heritage shoe, tell me about the footbed, the leather upper, how it holds up on a long walk. Give me a reason to buy beyond nostalgia.
- 2. **Show a male model, 55–65 years old, wearing the shoe casually** — in a store, on a sidewalk, somewhere I recognize myself.
- 3. **Add a prominent Shop CTA button in the hero section** — right below the Superstar copy, big enough to tap on a phone screen without zooming.
- 4. **Offer a member price** — even 15% off for adiClub turns this from a "nice to look at" into a "maybe I should buy a pair."
- 5. **Give the giveaway a hard deadline** — "Enter by June 19" creates urgency; "enter now" floats away from memory the moment I close the app.
