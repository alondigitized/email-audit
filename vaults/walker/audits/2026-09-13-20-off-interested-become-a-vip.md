# 20% OFF!? Interested? Become a VIP!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW email, fifth or sixth time this week — big "20% OFF" banner, "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid, shop-by-size, and every single shoe pictured is women's: ankle booties, a wedge sneaker, a Mary Jane flat, floral flats, a flip-flop. Not one sneaker, walking shoe, or slide for a guy in the whole thing. I know I got the fine print says Skechers is included in the 20% off, but you'd never guess it from what's actually shown.
- I do notice they finally listed a "MEN" nav link up top, which is more than I can say for their last few sends, but that's it — one word in a header bar. The fine print at the bottom even spells out the date range, September 10-13, so this thing's basically expiring and it's the same booties-and-heels lineup they ran all week. I'm marking this read and moving on — nothing here for my feet.

## What's working

- "20% OFF" banner is big, bold, and impossible to miss scrolling past.
- The MEN nav link exists this time, so at least the door isn't fully closed.
- Shop-by-size grid and wide-width/wide-calf buttons are clean and easy to tap on a phone.
- Fine print clearly states the offer window (Sept 10-13) and that Skechers products qualify — that's useful even if buried.

## What's weak

- Every single product image is a women's shoe — booties, a wedge sneaker, flats, a flip-flop — nothing for me.
- No mention of comfort, cushioning, arch support, or any men's walking/casual shoe anywhere in the visuals.
- This is the same "20% OFF" + shoe grid template I've seen from DSW nearly every day this week, just reshuffled.
- "Shop Men" is one link buried in a nav bar, not a section, banner, or product shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least half the "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid for men's product when the send targets subscribers who've clicked or bought men's before — I've never once engaged with a bootie, they know that.
- 2. Feature a men's comfort brand (Skechers, since the fine print says it qualifies) with an actual product shot and the word "comfort" or "cushioned" in the copy.
- 3. Stop running the identical creative shell daily — vary the offer angle so it doesn't blur into background noise by day three.
- 4. Add a line about the 20% off applying to comfort brands specifically, front and center, not buried in exclusions text.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off Skechers & comfort shoes — men's included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your comfort — 20% off ends today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, slip-on, arch support — all 20% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's comfort styles now included in your VIP discount`
