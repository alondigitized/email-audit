# Let's talk EXTRA savings.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. That's the fourteenth one in about two weeks by my count. The "60% OFF" and code COZY20 are easy to read on my phone, but it's the same "up to 60% off plus an extra 20%" I got yesterday ("Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside.") and the day before ("EXTRA savings with a 🎀 on top!"). I could recite this one from memory.
- The red banner up top is about Minnie Mouse clogs. I'm 62, and that's not for me. The bottom half is "TikTok viral styles" and "From Your FYP." I don't have a FYP and I'm not scrolling TikTok for shoes. The fuzzy lined clogs in the second photo are the only thing I'd look at twice, since cushioned and warm is my kind of comfort. They're shown on a young woman in pink cargo pants, though.
- Would I click? Probably not. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read. If I wanted Crocs, I already know COZY20.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF" and "USE CODE COZY20" are big and dark on a plain background. I can read them without squinting.
- The "Shop Black Friday" button is a big black pill, easy to tap with my thumb.
- The deadline is in the fine print: Sunday, November 29th at 11:59pm MT. That's a real date, not a vague "hurry."
- The two clogs in the hero photo are plain and classic, and a clog is a slip-on, hands-free shoe.

## What's weak

- It's the same offer I've seen for two weeks. Nothing is new here except the Minnie Mouse banner.
- The Minnie Mouse banner and the "TikTok viral styles" section are aimed at someone much younger than me.
- "Almost everything" and "Exclusions Apply" make me suspicious. I have to read the small print to learn what's left out.
- Nothing about comfort. There's no mention of cushioning, arch support or fit, and no price, review or men's shoe. "Shop Black Friday" sends me to who-knows-where.
- The red banner uses small text and sits above the main offer. It pulls my eye away from the real deal.
- The footer links are tiny and crowded, and the legal text at the bottom is hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send me one hero product and one real price. For example, show the lined clog with "Was $64.99, now $39.99 with COZY20." I can do the math on that.
- 2. Send men's and comfort-minded customers something different from the Minnie and TikTok content. A line like "Cushioned all-day comfort for your feet" would fit me.
- 3. Make the button say what it does, like "Shop Men's Clogs," and link it to that category instead of a general sale page.
- 4. Space the sends out. If I get the same discount every day, I stop paying attention to it.
- 5. Drop the young-pop-culture blocks for older customers, and make the legal text bigger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard ESP-built (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) template with a responsive media query, hidden preheader and open pixel. The QA run found no hard failures, only 2 warnings. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and CTA links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links unverified.** The QA run skipped 15 tracking links (`click.crocs-email.com`) because they are click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect status codes and breakage are unconfirmed.
- **Open pixel is well-formed.** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0` is a 1x1 image with `alt=""` inside `<div style="height:1px;overflow:hidden">`.
- **Third-party pixel has no alt.** QA flagged `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`. This is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beacon, so a second tracker fires on open. It should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`. I could not confirm from the truncated source whether it is hidden.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed meta tags.**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does not declare the charset.
  - `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it is a no-op.
  - This may be an artifact of the HTML capture rather than the real source.
  - The charset is probably still set by the `Content-Type` header, so the risk is low.
- **Present and fine.**
  - `lang="en"`, `<title>Crocs</title>` and the viewport meta.
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
  - Apple data-detector and Gmail resets.
  - A 639px breakpoint with mobile utility classes.
  - Preheader hidden with `display:none !important`, plus zero-width character padding.
- **Web font.** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` over woff2/woff with `font-display: swap`. The fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Not assessable.** Body image alt text, link text and contrast are in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The preheader says "code inside", but the promo code is in the truncated section, so I could not confirm it renders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified.** QA reports `Authentication-Results` is missing: "Expected via AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM status unknown". This is a limitation of the capture path, not a confirmed defect. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com` need checking on the original message or by DNS lookup.
- **Footer not inspected.** The unsubscribe link, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and postal address are not in the supplied data. Compliance cannot be confirmed or denied.
- **Sender.** The From is `email@crocs-email.com`, a dedicated sending domain separate from the brand domain. That is normal practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Every link goes through `click.crocs-email.com`, and the redirects were not probed. UTM parameters and landing-page promo state cannot be verified from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA with redirect-following enabled for `click.crocs-email.com`. This would confirm the final URLs, status codes, UTMs and promo-code landing state.
2. Capture the raw message source, including headers, to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC and the `List-Unsubscribe` headers. Alternatively, run DNS checks on `crocs-email.com`.
