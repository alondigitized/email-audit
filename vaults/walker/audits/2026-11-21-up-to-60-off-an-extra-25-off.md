# Up to 60% off + an extra 25% off? 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again. This is the seventh one in about a week, and it's nearly the same pitch as yesterday's "Plus an extra 25% off?!" I can read the big "60% OFF" fine on my phone. But "select sale styles" and "Exclusions Apply" right under it make me think the real discount is much smaller than the headline. The hero shoe is a buckled slide in tan and cream, and it's the only thing I'd call a comfort shoe in the whole email.
- The rest is clogs at $30, a Grinch clog, and leopard-print fuzzy slippers. None of that is for me. I'm looking for cushioned, arch-supported, slip-on walking shoes. I'd skim it in about five seconds. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF + an extra 25% off" block is large and white on dark brown, so I can read it without zooming.
- The "Shop Black Friday Deals" button is a big white pill that's easy to tap with my thumb.
- "Classic Clogs starting at $30" gives me a real price to compare against Hoka and Brooks.
- The footer links are plain and spaced out, so I can find Unsubscribe without squinting.

## What's weak

- "Select sale styles" and "Exclusions Apply" make the headline feel like bait. The fine print says it can't be combined with anything else.
- The Grinch clog and leopard-print slippers are novelty items. Nothing here says arch support, cushioning or walking.
- The footer has two different expiration dates, Nov 21 and Nov 29. I can't tell which deal ends when.
- The "mean one, merry deal" Grinch banner is a different deal stacked in the middle of the email, and it makes the layout feel cluttered.
- It's the seventh send in a week, so it feels like noise now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send frequency, or at least vary the content. Next time, lead with one comfort product I can actually buy, like a men's slip-on or a Literide-style walker, with the real sale price on it.
- 2. State the real deal. Say "extra 25% off, today only, ends 11:59pm" instead of stacking "up to" claims with "exclusions apply."
- 3. Drop the novelty slippers and the Grinch banner. Use that space for a men's comfort section.
- 4. Keep one deadline in the footer so the dates don't conflict.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends tonight at 11:59pm MT. Cushioned clogs and slides included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Classic Clogs from $30, plus 25% off sale styles today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is well-formed ESP-built markup (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `click.crocs-email.com` tracking) with a working preheader, mobile breakpoints and Outlook/Apple/Gmail client resets. The HTML is truncated, so link, footer and compliance checks are limited. The confirmed defects are an invalid charset meta, a third-party pixel with no `alt`, and header and link checks that couldn't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 18 tracking links (WARN: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Final destinations, redirect status and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixel:** QA found `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=9153f33f…`. It is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event call that fires alongside the ESP open pixel (`click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10208&bmt=0`). The two pixels double-count opens. The `d_dpuid` value is a persistent identifier carried in the URL.
- **Open pixel:** The ESP pixel is correct: `width="1" height="1" alt=""`, inside a `height:1px;overflow:hidden` wrapper.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so the browser ignores them. The charset therefore relies on the MIME `Content-Type` header. The preheader's invisible filler characters (`͏ ‌ ­`) could render as garbage in a client that guesses the wrong encoding.
- **Missing alt:** The demdex `<img>` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). It is a hidden tracking image, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is needed for validity.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This only matters in web-view rendering.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set. The preheader is hidden with `display:none !important`. `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is neutralized. `#MessageViewBody` and `u + .email-body` Gmail hacks are included. Responsive utilities cover ≤639px, and `font-display: swap` is set on the `TT Crocs` `@font-face` rules.
- **Truncation:** Body markup, image `alt` text on product and hero images, and font-stack fallbacks are not visible, so they could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication headers:** QA WARN: no `Authentication-Results` header was found (expected via the AgentMail relay). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com` couldn't be verified. This is likely an artifact of the relay, not a sender defect.
- **Footer elements:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source or QA data, so they are unverified. Nothing confirms a violation.
- **From address:** `From: Crocs <email@crocs-email.com>` is clearly identified and consistent with the tracking domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be evaluated. All 18 links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, and the destination URLs are not in the source or probed.
- **Landing-page alignment:** The offer in the subject ("Up to 60% off + an extra 25% off") and preheader ("select sale styles") can't be matched to a destination. Nothing was resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-run QA with redirect-following enabled for the 18 `click.crocs-email.com` links, or resolve them in a headless session. Then check destination status codes and UTM presence.
4. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, or on the footer segment, to confirm the unsubscribe link, postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers.
5. Capture the raw `.eml` from the original delivery path to validate SPF, DKIM and DMARC. The AgentMail relay strips or omits `Authentication-Results`.
6. If open-rate reporting matters, reconcile the ESP and Adobe open signals, since both fire per open.
