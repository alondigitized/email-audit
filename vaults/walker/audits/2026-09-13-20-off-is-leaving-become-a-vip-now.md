# 20% off is leaving 👋 Become a VIP now!
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Same DSW email again — I've lost count this week, this is at least the seventh. Big "20% OFF" banner, "SO NEW, SO YOU" grid, shop-by-size boxes, and once again every single shoe pictured is a woman's shoe: heeled booties, a wedge sneaker with fringe, a Mary Jane flat, a floral flat, flip flops. Not one sneaker, not one men's style, nothing with the cushioning or arch support I'm actually looking for.
- The "20% off is leaving" urgency line might've worked if I hadn't seen the same "1 Day Left," "ends TONIGHT," and "Don't miss" versions of this exact banner five times already this week. At this point it just reads as noise. I'm not opening this one either — straight to archive, same as the last several.
- Not forwarding this to anyone. There's nothing here that speaks to me, and the repetition is actually starting to sour me a little on DSW as a brand worth checking in on.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" number is big and impossible to miss, I'll give it that.
- Code YAYVIP and the terms are spelled out right under the offer, no hunting required.
- Free shipping for VIPs and Afterpay mentioned — fine print, but at least it's visible, not buried.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is women's — booties, ballet flats, a floral flat, flip flops. Nothing for me.
- I've seen this exact layout and banner all week with just a reworded subject line each time — feels like the same email recycled, not a real campaign.
- No men's, no walking shoes, no sneakers, no comfort/cushioning language anywhere.
- "SHOP MEN" is one tiny link in the top nav bar — that's the only nod to me in the whole email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender/purchase history — if I've never clicked a bootie or flat, stop sending me a wall of them. Swap in men's walking shoes and comfort sneakers for guys like me.
- 2. Retire the "20% off is leaving" urgency angle — I've now seen five variations of this same countdown gimmick this week and it's lost all credibility.
- 3. Lead the hero grid with at least one men's comfort shoe if this is going to a mixed list.
- 4. Slow down the cadence — one strong offer a week beats a near-identical one daily.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's comfort shoes, 20% off for VIPs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your size, your 20% off — before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, easy-on styles included in this VIP discount`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off men's walking shoes, in stores and online`
