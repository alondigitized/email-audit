Too bad I had to scroll past what feels like forty pairs of women's heels, mary janes, and ankle booties to get there. This is the exact same tiered $10/$20/$60-off template I flagged yesterday and the day before — dollar amounts didn't even change.
- The Brooks block is the one thing here built for a guy like me, and it's buried two-thirds of the way down under six different women's shoe categories. I'm not digging through "They're Just Soooo You" and "These Make the Grade" — those sneaker photos up top are all women's styles too, low-tops and platforms, nothing that reads as men's walking shoe.

## What's working

- The $10/$20/$60-off tiers are laid out clean and easy to read at a glance — no squinting required.
- Brooks running shoe at "$75" is a real, specific offer in a category I actually care about.
- "Shop Wide Width" and "Shop Wide Calf" buttons near the bottom are a nice nod to guys like me who need actual room in a shoe — though again, buried.
- Free shipping and buy-online-pickup-in-store icons are visible without me hunting for them.

## What's weak

- Every single hero and mid-email product shot is a women's shoe — heels, slingbacks, booties, mary janes — nothing men's until the Brooks banner near the very bottom.
- "SHOP MEN'S" isn't even offered as a top button; it's Women, Kids, and "Shop Everything," so I'm not sure "everything" even includes my size run.
- The sneaker section ("These Make the Grade") looks unisex-ish but every shoe shown reads as a women's silhouette — no arch support or cushioning callout anywhere.
- Subject says "$10 off" but the email is really a wall of full-price browsing before you ever see a men's item — bait and switch on relevance, not on price.
- This is the same offer structure as the last two DSW emails I've reviewed this week — nothing new to see here.

- 2. Add a "SHOP MEN'S" button next to Women/Kids up top so guys don't have to guess if "Shop Everything" applies to them.
- 3. Swap one of the "They're Just Soooo You" product rows for men's casual/walking styles — right now it's 100% women's inventory before the fold.
- 4. Call out arch support or cushioning specifically in the Brooks banner copy — that's the detail that gets me to tap, not just a price.
- 5. Stop reusing the identical $10/$20/$60 template three sends in a row — rotate the creative even if the discount math stays the same.
