# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same cart nag again, my sixth from Skechers since 11/20. The shoe is that women's black Contour Foam Slip-in with the rose-gold eyelets I keep seeing. "Let's make it official" is cute, but I never put that shoe in a cart. It's the same creative under a new subject line, just like the "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart expires soon!" sends earlier today.
- The bottom half is better for me. There's a row of men's slip-in boots and a slipper, and a big black "Shop All Best Sellers" bar. I also noticed the Black Friday banner says Buy One, Get One 50% Off. Then I read the fine print and it says the offer was valid 11/12 to 11/17. That's five days ago, and the banner is still shouting "Countdown." That makes me trust the whole email less.
- Would I click? Maybe the men's boots, if I were shopping. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "Complete Your Order" button is big, dark blue and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The text is large and the layout is one simple column, so I don't have to squint on my phone.
- The men's slip-in boots and the Murette slipper in the "more great options" row are my kind of shoe: comfortable and easy to get on.
- BOGO 50% off, or 20% off a single pair, is a clear offer. The black banner makes it hard to miss.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is plain and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The fine print says the offer ended 11/17, but the banner says "Black Friday Countdown." It looks stale, and it feels a little dishonest.
- The hero is a women's shoe with rose-gold trim again, so it doesn't fit me.
- There's a big empty gap above the hero shoe before the picture starts, so I scroll past blank space.
- It's my sixth cart reminder in three days, and the same shoe every time. That's nagging.
- The product captions under the grid are tiny. Nothing says cushioning, arch support or price, which is what I'd compare against Hoka or Brooks.
- Nothing mentions my points balance, even though the 11/21 email greeted me by name with 1000 points.

## Recommendations

- 1. Check the offer dates before sending. Either refresh the promo to a live one or drop the "Countdown" language. This is the one change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Show the item I actually left in the cart, with its size and price. Stop reusing the same hero in every reminder.
- 3. Add a line of comfort details under each shoe, such as "Arch Fit, cushioned insole, hands-free," plus a price.
- 4. Cap reminders at two. After that, send something different, like a back-in-stock or low-size alert.
- 5. Trim the blank space above the hero and enlarge the captions.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Slip-ins are still in your cart`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walker, your size is running low`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free comfort, saved for you. Free returns if they don't fit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 1,000 points are ready to use at checkout.`
