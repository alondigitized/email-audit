# [Final hours] BOGO 50% off shoes
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the ninth BOGO email in about five days. Yesterday I got "BOGO 50% OFF! ENDING SOON!" and "BOGO 50% off ends *TODAY*". Now "Final hours" shows up, and the green banner is the same one I've seen all week. The only change is the countdown, which now reads 06:43:48. That is the third "ending soon" email in one day, and I've stopped believing the deadline.
- Once I got past the banner, the body is a pile of women's shoes: strappy heels, a Mary Jane, tall boots, a heeled bootie. There's a men's link up top, but not one man's shoe is shown. Nothing here mentions slip-ons, cushioning or arch support, so I got nothing I'd actually buy.
- I'd mark it read and move on. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. If I wanted a BOGO deal I'd already know about it, because they've told me nine times.

## What's working

- The green BOGO 50% OFF banner is huge and easy to read on my phone. I knew the offer in a second.
- The countdown clock sits right on top. It's the only thing that's different from the earlier sends.
- "Shop Men" is one of three buttons under the banner, so I could get to men's shoes.
- Size links (5 to 11) and "Shop Wide Width" are handy. Wide width matters for my feet.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup and Afterpay are listed near the bottom. That's useful, though it's small and easy to miss.

## What's weak

- Every shoe shown is women's. Heels, Mary Janes and boots. I'm a 62-year-old man and I see nothing for me.
- The size row stops at 11. The wide-width link helps, but I see no men's sizes up to 12 or 13. The email doesn't feel like it's for me.
- Nine BOGO emails in five days. The "Final hours" urgency feels fake, and it makes me trust the brand less.
- "Shop Under $50" and the other buttons sit under tiny product images. I can't tell what the shoes cost, and there's no brand or comfort detail.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny. It lists brand exclusions and says the second pair has to be "equal or lesser value". I'd have to squint to read it.
- The "Oh what fun!" and "Made to mix & mingle" copy is cute holiday talk. It isn't about the shoe fitting me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by who I am. Send me men's comfort and walking shoes (Skechers, Hoka, Brooks, New Balance) in the hero. Don't send me heels.
- 2. Cut the cadence. One BOGO send, then one real last-call, not three "ending" emails a day.
- 3. Put the price and the brand under each shoe, and add one line about cushioning or slip-on comfort.
- 4. Move the free shipping and VIP perks up near the top. Make the fine print readable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off ends tonight: men's walking shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 2nd pair 50% off, comfort brands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hoka, Brooks, New Balance and Skechers included. Wide widths in stock.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends at midnight. Free shipping for VIP members.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

There are also minor accessibility and markup gaps, and authentication status couldn't be verified. QA pass rate was 62%, with 1 failure and 2 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  - The probe didn't capture the final destination.
  - SMS opt-in links sometimes reject non-mobile or bot user agents, so a probe-specific 400 is possible. A real recipient may see the same failure, so verify it from a mobile client.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 32,183 of 37,557 characters (~86%) are URLs, because every link is wrapped in a long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking token. That makes the plain-text alternative close to unreadable and can add a spam-filter signal.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** Only 12 of 83 links were probed, so the other 71 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image missing `alt`.** The image is `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/Hlga8yJu4E-3-IxfXyiGww~~/AAQRxRA~/CyQZ-7zkvmCfoKygqG5QPZfFuVen-l8QbvtKm2VP-p_-4N2-gA0_bldmxsi8s`. Screen readers will announce the tracked URL or filename, or skip the image.
- **Tracking pixel missing `alt`.** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif` has no `alt` attribute. It should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so assistive tech ignores it.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Malformed meta declarations.** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so they do nothing. Charset then depends on the MIME `Content-Type` header, which is usually fine but not guaranteed.

- **Authentication unverified.** The `Authentication-Results` header wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a sender defect. Confirm with the raw headers from a direct-delivery test.
- **Unsubscribe and sender identity.** The QA data reports no unsubscribe failure, and an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address weren't available in the supplied data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

Landing-page alignment with the "BOGO 50% off shoes" subject wasn't verified because the redirect destinations weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations

2. **Add alt text.** Use a descriptive `alt` on the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image and `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
