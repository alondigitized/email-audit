- Zero price or percentage off anywhere in this email — fourth or fifth time this week I've had to guess what something costs.
- "Recommended for You" shows the same black Classic Clog and Baya Clog I've already seen in three other emails, not anything related to this new "Echo Element" shoe.
- A Kids' Classic Clog in my recommendations tells me this brand has no idea who I am.

# Technical Email Audit — Crocs "The Echo Clog Goes Sneaker"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce/ExactTarget (crocs-email.com) commerce template with responsive MSO/Gmail hacks in place; no functional blockers, but several hygiene issues around mixed-content images and a personalization/merge-token leak in alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Open-tracking pixel present and correctly hidden: `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?W2JYWMLWA4YERN47ILBL3DFN7Y.10199&d=10199&bmt=0` (1x1, wrapped in `height:1px;overflow:hidden`).
- Secondary tracking/beacon pixel to Adobe/Demandware: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` — also correctly hidden (`visibility:hidden;display:none`).
- 29 outbound links route through `click.crocs-email.com` redirect wrapper; QA skipped HTTP probing on these per policy (expected behavior for click-tracking domains, not a defect). Final destinations/UTM parameters are not verifiable from the truncated source — see Section 6.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 images missing `alt` text (QA-flagged):
  - Demdex tracking pixel (`event?d_dpid=...`) — acceptable, it's a non-visual beacon.
  - 4 product recommendation images from `10745993.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/685c6ddd06fd7c003f1cd785/i/{1,2,3,4}/...` — these are visible/content-bearing product images and should have descriptive `alt` attributes for screen readers; currently blank.
- Mobile responsive CSS (`@media max-width:639px`) and Outlook/Gmail-specific hacks (`#MessageViewBody`, `x-apple-data-detectors`, `mso-hide`) are present and structured correctly — no issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Merge token leak in alt attributes**: the 4 recommendation images use the recipient's raw email address as the `alt` value — e.g. `alt="walker@agentmail.to"` — instead of a product name/description. This indicates the personalization template is passing the subscriber-key/email merge field into the `alt` attribute by mistake (likely a fallback when the product-name token resolves empty). This both breaks accessibility (screen readers announce the recipient's email) and unnecessarily exposes PII in the HTML source of every sent copy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link, physical mailing address, and email authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are not present in the truncated HTML/QA data provided — cannot confirm compliance status one way or the other from available evidence. Recommend re-running the audit against the untruncated source/full header set before drawing a compliance conclusion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All CTAs route through the `click.crocs-email.com` redirect wrapper; actual landing-page URLs and UTM parameters are not exposed in the truncated source (QA explicitly skipped probing these 29 links). No continuity issues confirmed, but none can be ruled out either — flag for re-check with full/untruncated link list.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the recommendation-engine template so product images render real product-name `alt` text instead of falling back to the recipient's email address (`walker@agentmail.to`) — both an accessibility and PII-exposure fix.
2. Convert the 6 `http://` image assets to `https://` (App Store badge, Google Play badge, "Find Your Store" icon, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube icons — all served from `image.crocs-email.com`) to prevent mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-rendered clients.
3. Re-run the audit against the full (untruncated) HTML and raw sending headers to confirm unsubscribe/CAN-SPAM compliance and to validate destination URLs/UTM parameters on the 29 tracking-wrapped links.
