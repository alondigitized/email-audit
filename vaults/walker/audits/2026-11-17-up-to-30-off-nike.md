**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and this one at least isn't UGG. Nike is a name I know, and the big "30% OFF*" with the Swoosh is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. That's a win after a week of boots and UGG.
- Then I scrolled and the Nike promise fell apart. Below the hero I see women's boots, heeled booties, Chelsea boots and a pair of rain boots. - I'd open it for the Nike angle, but I probably wouldn't click. I see nothing for a man who wants a cushioned slip-on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF*" hero is huge and readable on my phone without squinting.
- The Nike Swoosh is a brand I recognize, and it makes a nice change from the UGG sends I've been getting.
- The "SHOP NOW" button is plain and easy to find.
- The "Shop by Size" block with half sizes (5 to 11) and "Shop Wide Width" is useful. Wide width matters to me.
- The footer with free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and the "DSW VIP" bar tells me about the rewards, even though points don't decide my purchase.

## What's weak

- The Nike hero doesn't match the products below. I see no Nike shoes at all, which feels like bait-and-switch.
- Almost everything pictured is women's: boots, heels, booties. The men's nav tab is there, but no men's product is shown.
- "Select styles" and "Details below" make me doubt the discount, and the real terms are buried in tiny print at the bottom.
- The UGG "Merry UGG Season" block is still here, after the UGG sends on Nov 16 and 17 ("Up to 30% off UGG is on!"). It's the same email again.
- The text under the hero ("Select styles in stores & online. Prices as marked.") is small and thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show actual Nike shoes in the hero or right below it, ideally men's walking or casual styles. If the sale is only 30% off a few styles, say which ones.
- 2. Add a men's row: slip-ons, cushioned walkers, wide widths. Lead with comfort, not gift boots.
- 3. Move the fine print up near the hero, or at least name the exclusions.
- 4. Drop the UGG block when the send is about Nike, or at least vary it from last week's emails.
- 5. Cut the number of sends. This is the twelfth email or so in a week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Braze/SendGrid-style template with tracked links. It has one confirmed broken link (HTTP 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA). The QA pass rate is 62%, and the HTML source was truncated, so the checks below are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returns HTTP 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/Le9BM0ngFRTdpcHUIqnuWg~~/AAQRxRA~/jPyyTrW8I4Ij8k…`
  - The probe's link-text field also shows empty text before the label, so the anchor may be image-wrapped or have malformed content.
  - A 400 from a click-tracking redirect can come from the bot probe, an expired token, or a malformed SMS-deeplink target. It should be re-tested with a real client before it is treated as a live outage.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 71 links were probed and 59 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261117_P251284_V1_W-_Offer_MI_Ni…` is a second, separate tracker (`mi.dsw.com`) alongside the `ablink.dsw.com` click wrapper. This is not a defect, but it means two tracking domains need to stay healthy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - The `ablink.dsw.com/q/wvY00G8GGb9CP1fhvZVXSQ~~/…` image has no `alt`.
  - The `o.gif` pixel has no `alt`. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration takes effect. Charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME `Content-Type`.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Deliverability signal:** the plain-text part is 26,803 of 30,894 characters URLs (87%). The tracked URLs are very long and bloat the text part, and spam filters can read a URL-heavy text part as a negative signal.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. `mi_u=225090073` in the pixel URL is a populated recipient identifier, not a failed merge.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a test-harness limitation, not evidence of failure. Verify on the sending side with DMARC reports or a direct-delivery header.
- **Unsubscribe:** the QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none is reported failing. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source or the QA data, so they are unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be confirmed. All links go through `ablink.dsw.com` wrappers whose destinations and UTM parameters are opaque. Only the pixel's `mi_ecmp` campaign ID (`20261117_P251284_V1_W-_Offer_MI_Ni…`) is visible. Resolve the redirect chains for the primary Nike CTAs to check landing-page alignment with the "Up to 30% off Nike" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link in a real client. If it still returns 400, fix the SMS opt-in destination or token. This is the only confirmed hard failure and it blocks the 15% SMS-offer conversion path.
2. Add `alt` text to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image, and `alt=""` to `o.gif`.
3. Fix the meta tags: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
5. Shorten the text-part URLs or use a link-light text alternative to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Run a full probe of the 59 skipped links, and resolve redirects on the primary CTAs to verify UTMs and landing pages.
7. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com`, and the `List-Unsubscribe` one-click headers, outside the AgentMail relay.
