# 30% OFF BOOTS!? DECK YEAH!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and I'll give them this much: the "30%" is so big I could read it on my phone without my glasses. But it's boots, and the pictures are mostly women's shoes. I see a loafer, a mary jane, a pink high heel and a slingback, plus a fuzzy winter boot. Nothing here is a cushioned, slip-on walking shoe for a man my age.
- The subject is a pun ("DECK YEAH") that I have to read twice. It's also the fifth or sixth "Early Black Friday" email this week. On 11/11 I got "LAST CHANCE FOR $15 OFF!" and "3 words: Early. Black. Friday." The UGG 30% emails ran the three days before that. I'm tired of it, and the deadline doesn't feel real.
- Would I click? Maybe "Shop Men" out of curiosity, since I was already in the email. Would I forward it? No. I'd read it and move on.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF BOOTS*" hero is huge and easy to read on a phone, with no squinting.
- The code BOOTFRIDAY is shown right in the hero, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "Shop Men" link is right under the button, so I can skip the women's stuff.
- The "Shop by Size" section includes wide width and wide calf buttons. That's useful for people with wider feet.
- The free shipping, in-store pickup and VIP rewards icons are clear and easy to read.

## What's weak

- Every product in the grid is women's or a dressy style. There's no man, no men's boot and no comfort shoe in sight.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support, slip-on, or brands I shop like Skechers, Hoka or Brooks.
- The hero says "Details below." The fine print is at the bottom, in small light text on dark, and I can't read it comfortably. It also says brand exclusions apply.
- The pink kids' gift banner with Sonic and Hello Kitty has nothing to do with me.
- The product grid prices aren't shown. Only two tiny red notes ("Extra 30% off reg. price!", "Up to 50% off clearance!") sit under a couple of shoes. They're hard to read and they muddy the 30% message.
- The size numbers look like buttons, but I can't tell if they're clickable. Nobody has told me they filter anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show men's boots in the hero or the first row. Include a comfort-forward style, like a cushioned chukka or a waterproof walking boot, with the price and the sale price.
- 2. Add a short line about comfort, like "cushioned, easy-on boots from brands you trust."
- 3. Cut the cadence. Send one Black Friday email with one offer, not a new one every few hours.
- 4. Make the fine print larger and lighter, with the brand exclusions near the offer.
- 5. Put the end date in the hero, not just in the fine print at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off boots, code BOOTFRIDAY`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's boots 30% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned, waterproof and easy to slip on. Valid 11/12–11/15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code BOOTFRIDAY in stores or online. Wide widths too.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built, table-based XHTML Transitional template with dark-mode support. The automated QA flagged one failing CTA link (HTTP 400) on the SMS opt-in, two alt-text gaps, and a URL-heavy plain-text part. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks (unsubscribe, footer, UTMs) could not be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failing CTA (FAIL):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned **HTTP 400**. Its URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/hosS8RC907DPZo8424cc1g~~/AAQRxRA~/D8wDCgw6...`. This is a high-value conversion link.
  - A 400 from an ESP click-tracker can be a probe artifact, such as a bot user-agent or a missing header, rather than a dead link. A manual browser or mobile-UA check is needed to confirm.
  - The QA output shows an empty label for the 400 (`Broken link (400):`), so the response body gave no diagnostic detail.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped). Link health for the rest is unknown.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0.../o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261112_P261649_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` is a third-party open pixel on `mi.dsw.com`. It also carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`) in the query string. This is normal for an ESP, but it is a privacy consideration. Apple Mail Privacy Protection will inflate its open counts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2):**
  - The linked image `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/tk9rDFbn7Wu1sZQ70L9WGQ~~/AAQRxRA~/5AyIqR9_BmDjhUVOrbOG0ZWJctyDLfchYSsL-oWyq9TerNbHLW...` has no `alt`. It is wrapped in a tracked link, so screen readers will announce the raw URL.
  - `o.gif` has no `alt`. As a tracking pixel it should have `alt=""`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv`, so the charset and IE-mode declarations are inert. The charset may be set only by the HTTP header. This could be an artifact of sanitizing the source.
- **Fonts:** Web fonts load from `braze-images.com` (`.woff` and `.ttf`). Clients that ignore `@font-face` will fall back. The source is truncated, so the fallback stacks could not be verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The numeric `mi_u` value in the pixel URL looks resolved.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status could not be verified. The QA notes this is expected via the AgentMail relay. Treat it as an unverified item, not a confirmed failure. Check the raw headers at the original receiving MTA.
- **Unsubscribe:** The QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure for one. `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers and the physical postal address were not in the data provided, so they are unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 26,248 of 30,610 characters (about 86%) are URLs. This is over the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter signal. It is caused by the long opaque `ablink.dsw.com` tracked URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Every link is wrapped in an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect, so final destinations and UTM parameters could not be inspected from the source. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261112_P261649_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan...` on the pixel. Boots landing-page alignment with the subject line could not be verified. No issues are confirmable.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the SMS opt-in link** (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/hosS8RC9...`) with a real browser and mobile UA. If it still returns 400, fix the Braze link or the SMS opt-in destination before further sends.
2. **Add alt text:** descriptive `alt` on the linked image (`.../q/tk9rDFbn7Wu1sZQ70L9WGQ~~/...`), and `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`, and fix the two meta tags with `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Shorten or trim the plain-text part** (fewer links, or a shorter tracking-domain path) to bring URL density under 70%.
6. **Increase probe coverage** and capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) to close the unverified gaps.
