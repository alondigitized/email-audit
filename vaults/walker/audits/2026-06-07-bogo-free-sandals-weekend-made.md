## Executive summary

- Well, there's the Inflation Relief banner. I didn't even have to look for it — my eye went right to that red-and-yellow block like it always does now. Seven days running from Shoe Carnival and that banner has been in every single one. I could describe it from memory at this point. It is what it is.
- Once I got past the banner, I realized pretty quickly this one is not for me. BOGO FREE Women's Sandals is the big centerpiece, there's a kids' section underneath it ("Big Style for Littles"), and then a $14.98 kids' sandal sale — which, by the way, was the same price point they ran on June 4th. I'm 62, I'm a man, and I am not shopping for my granddaughter's sandals from a promotional email. The 50% off in-store section buried near the bottom is gender-neutral but it's in-store only, and I'd need to dig to find out what qualifies.
- Would I click? No. Mark as read and move on. Wouldn't forward it either — not my category, not my people.

- The BOGO FREE graphic is bold and legible — I could read it even on a small screen without squinting.
- Multiple concrete price points are visible: $10 off, BOGO, $14.98, 50% — Shoe Carnival doesn't bury the offers.
- The Shoe Perks loyalty $10 reward at the bottom is a nice reminder; I can see it without hunting.
- Seasonal timing is right — early June, sandals, summer — the calendar logic holds.
- Email renders cleanly; nothing broken or cut off.

## What's weak

- The entire email is women's and kids' — I do not see a single men's product or men's CTA. I'm invisible here.
- That Inflation Relief banner is showing up for the fifteenth time. The message has gone stale; I'm tuning it out.
- The preview text in my inbox is a raw tracking URL — "https://click.email.shoecarnival.com/?qs=ABB7..." — which tells me nothing and looks like spam.
- The BOGO is buried below "Sunday Mood" and a lifestyle photo spread; on my phone I had to scroll before I even got to the main deal.
- Seven emails in seven days is too much. I'm not mad, but the cadence is dulling the sense that any single send matters.

- 3. **Fix the preheader.** A tracking URL in the inbox preview is embarrassing and makes me wonder if the email is legitimate before I even open it.
- 4. **Surface the 50% off in-store offer higher** — that's the broadest, most gender-neutral deal in the email and it's buried near the footer.
