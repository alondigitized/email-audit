# Pete Crow-Armstrong is 2026 National League™ MVP

## Executive summary

But it's the second baseball email in two days, after the Brewers pitcher on 11/12. I walk for a living, not for the box score. Under the photo is a big "MVPCA" and one line of congratulations. Then a black button that says "Shop 4040v8."

- "Shop 4040v8" means nothing to me. It's a model number with no picture, no price and no explanation.
- There's no offer at all. The 11/08 email at least had "up to 25% off select shoes," and this one gives me no reason to click today.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML has template leftovers (a placeholder title and a placeholder background-image URL) and incomplete font and dark-mode CSS. QA found two images without alt text and a text part that is mostly URLs. The source is truncated, so the body, footer and header checks below are limited to what was provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 material links were identified and 11 were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified for nearly all links. This limits the audit and is not itself an email defect.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports 6417 of 7554 characters (about 85%) are URLs, which is over the 70% threshold. This is likely the long tracking-wrapped `link.email.newbalance.com/...` URLs. Long redirect URLs in the text part can hurt spam scoring and readability.
- **Placeholder URL:** the inline CSS has `.bgwhite {... background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}`. It is an unresolved placeholder and uses non-HTTPS `http://`. Any element with class `bgwhite` will request a nonexistent host, and clients may show a mixed-content warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://link.email.newbalance.com/custloads/818512772/NBUS/myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`
  - `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12631859_73684_23902731.gif`
- **Template-name leak:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is a leftover from the template. It can show in some clients and web-view tabs.
- **Asset path:** the shipping banner is served from a `myNB_Welcome` folder, which suggests asset reuse from a welcome template. This is a hygiene note only.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, so it does nothing.
- **Fonts:** the `@font-face` rules for `proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot` and the others have no `src` descriptor. They load nothing, so every client falls back to Arial or Times New Roman.
- **Doctype:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns` and XHTML-style self-closing tags. Clients tolerate this, but it is inconsistent.
- **CSS conflict:** `.bg1` is defined twice, first as `background-color:#ffffff` and then as `background:#58546d`. The later rule wins, so the first is dead code.
- **Dark-mode contrast:** `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { body {background:#151415 !important;} a {color:#cf0a2c !important;} }` puts red links on a near-black background. By my calculation that is about 3.3:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 for normal text. The blanket `a` selector may also override link colors set elsewhere. The rule is truncated, so I can't confirm further.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens or fallback strings were visible in the provided source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** QA found no `Authentication-Results` header, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is an observability gap in the test setup and not a confirmed sender failure. The sender domain is `email.newbalance.com`.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the footer is cut off in the truncated source, so the unsubscribe link, postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` headers can't be verified. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source and QA output don't include any landing-page URLs or UTM parameters. Link probing skipped 11 of 12 links, so landing-page alignment with the "Pete Crow-Armstrong 2026 NL MVP" subject is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace `<title>New Balance Template</title>` with the campaign name or subject line.
2. Remove or fix `.bgwhite`'s `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder, and use HTTPS if a background image is actually needed.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`. Add `alt=""` if it is decorative, and give the `/mo/...gif` a meaningful `alt` or `alt=""`.
4. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part, for example by using a shorter tracking domain path or fewer links, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
5. Either give the `@font-face` rules a `src` or remove them, and drop the duplicate `.bg1` rule.
6. Change the dark-mode link color to one with at least 4.5:1 contrast against `#151415`. Scope it to content links rather than every `a`.
7. Fix the `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">` tag, or remove it.
8. To close the audit gaps:
   - Run probing on all links.
   - Capture raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from a direct send, not the relay.
   - Review the full HTML for the footer and UTM parameters.
