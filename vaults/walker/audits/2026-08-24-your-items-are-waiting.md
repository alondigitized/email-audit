**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Well, would you look at that — that pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sneaker is back for at least the fourth time this week, still sitting in a cart I never filled. "We noticed you noticing us" — no, you didn't, because I never noticed that shoe in the first place. That's not a callback, that's a broken tracking pixel treating me like some 20-something who wandered off mid-checkout.
- The one bright spot is buried below the fold — a men's slip-in Arch Fit and a men's Relaxed Fit Murcado sneaker showed up in the "more great options" grid, which is at least in the right neighborhood for my feet. But I have to scroll past a women's floral shoe, two kids' basketball sneakers, and a women's BOBS flat to get there. And the 20% off code down at the bottom is real money, but it's siteword generic, not tied to anything I'd actually buy.
- I'm not clicking "Complete Your Order" on a shoe I never touched. I'd scroll to the men's row, maybe, but honestly this one's getting marked as read and forgotten, same as the last three.

## What's working

- The men's Arch Fit slip-in and Murcado slip-in in the grid are actually my lane — cushioned, slip-on, no laces to fuss with.
- The 20% off Skechers Plus code is a real, specific offer with a visible deadline (thru 8/25).
- Clean layout, big readable text, one clear blue CTA button — easy to navigate on my phone without zooming.
- No render bugs — everything loaded, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- The hero product, again, is a shoe I never looked at — this "cart reminder" premise falls apart when the item is wrong every single time.
- Grid below is scattershot: boys' basketball sneakers, women's BOBS, women's floral — maybe one in five items is something I'd wear.
- No fit/sizing/arch-support detail anywhere, which is the thing that actually gets me to click.
- Cadence is exhausting — this is the same cart-abandon trick I saw yesterday and the day before, just with a different headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cart-abandonment logic — don't send me a "your cart" email for a shoe I never added. If there's no real cart, send a browse-based recommendation instead, built off what I've actually clicked (comfort brands, men's slip-ins).
- 2. Lead the hero image with the Arch Fit or Murcado slip-in, not the floral D'Lites — you already have the right products, just buried in the second row.
- 3. Add one line of fit/comfort copy under the hero shoe — "cushioned insole, no-tie slip-on, arch support built in" — that's the sentence that gets me to tap.
- 4. Tie the 20% off code to the men's comfort category specifically, not a sitewide blanket offer.
