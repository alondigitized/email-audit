# adiClub members get up to 60% off for Black Friday early
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- It's another adidas email, and I stopped counting them a while back. This one is easy to read, I'll give it that. The banner says "UP TO 60% OFF" in big white letters on red, and I didn't need to squint. But the picture is a close-up of red-and-white fabric, with no shoe in it. I'm 62 and I shop for cushion and arch support. Nothing here tells me whether any of that is on sale.
- The offer is bigger than the 30% and 50% deals from the 10th and the 5th. It's still "up to," so I don't know what I'd actually pay. The three buttons are Men, Women and Kids. Not one says walking, comfort or slip-on. And "log in now" means I have to sign in before I even see a price.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already thinking about Black Friday. I'd probably check Hoka or Brooks first. I wouldn't forward it to a friend. It gets a quick read and then I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" headline is huge and readable on my phone without zooming.
- The adiClub logo at the top tells me this is for members, so it feels like early access and not a mass blast.
- The buttons are big black blocks with white text. They're easy to tap with my thumb.
- The layout is short and clean. There's one banner, one paragraph and three buttons, with no clutter.
- It matches the Black Friday timing, so the promo doesn't feel stale.

## What's weak

- The banner shows fabric and no shoes. I can't tell whether this deal covers anything I'd wear.
- "Up to 60%" is vague. I don't know which styles get 60% and which get 10%.
- "Log in now" before I can see anything is a hurdle.
- Men, Women and Kids is the whole menu. There's no walking, cushioned or slip-on option for a guy like me.
- There's no end date, only "before they're gone." After the countdown clock on the 5th, this feels softer.
- The tiny preheader line at the top is small and gray, and it's hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real shoe in the hero with a real price, like "Cloudfoam walking shoe, now $54." That would change the email the most.
- 2. Add a button for comfort, such as "Shop walking and comfort shoes."
- 3. Say what the member perk is and when it ends, for example "Member early access through Friday at midnight."
- 4. Show two or three real products with was-and-now prices under the banner.
- 5. Let me tap through to the sale without logging in first.
