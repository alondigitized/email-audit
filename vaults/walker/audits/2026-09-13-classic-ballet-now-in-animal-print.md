# Classic Ballet: Now in Animal Print
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- A leopard-print Mary Jane flat with a strap across the top — that's a women's shoe, plain as day, and there's not a single thing here that's for me. No offer, no percentage off, no price at all, just "wild & wearable" and a bunch of animal print. Crocs has sent me the handbag pitch (2026-09-11), the Yu-Gi-Oh! clogs (2026-09-10), and the leopard platform slide (2026-09-08) this same week, and now here's leopard print again in a different shoe. I'm starting to think their list just doesn't have a "guy" filter on it at all.
- The "Recommended for You" block at the bottom is the only thing worth a glance — a plain black Classic Clog and a tan Classic Crafted Clog with a real outsole, both look like something I'd actually wear. But that's buried under a whole email built around a shoe I'd never buy for myself or anyone I know. I'm marking this one read and moving on — nothing to forward, nothing to click.

## What's working

- The "Recommended for You" row at the bottom shows a couple of plain black clogs and a crafted clog with real tread — those actually look like men's casual shoes I'd consider.
- Layout's clean and readable, big type, no clutter, nothing hard to tap.
- No cartoon charms or teenager logos plastered on this one at least.

## What's weak

- Hero shoe is a leopard-print Mary Jane strap flat — that's a women's dress shoe, not a hands-free slip-on for a guy like me.
- No offer anywhere — no percent off, no price, nothing that tells me why I should act today.
- "360-degrees of comfort" callout is on a shoe I have zero interest in, so the comfort pitch is wasted on me.
- Zero mention of cushioning, arch support, or all-day wear that'd actually get my attention.
- Same animal-print well Crocs already went to on 2026-09-08 with the Dylan slide — feels like the same idea reheated for the third time this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the send with the "Recommended for You" shoes — the black Classic Clog or the Classic Crafted Clog — instead of burying men's options under a women's leopard flat.
- 2. Put an actual offer in the hero — a percent off or "buy 2 save" beats "wild & wearable" with nothing attached.
- 3. Segment better — I've gotten leopard print, a handbag, and Yu-Gi-Oh! clogs in five days. None of it was for me. Split the list by who actually buys what.
- 4. If you're sending me a women's-only product, at least pair it with a men's cross-sell so there's a reason for me to keep scrolling.
