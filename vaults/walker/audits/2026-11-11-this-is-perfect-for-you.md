# This is perfect for you!

## Executive summary

- Finally, a shoe that isn't the skull sneaker. The GO WALK Flex Plus in plain black is a walking shoe I'd actually wear. But "Still thinking about it?" is another cart nag, and I never put anything in a cart. After the 11/05, 11/08 and two "cart" emails today, I'm tired of being told I'm "thinking about" things I never looked at.
- Then I reach the 20% off banner, and it says "Valid thru 11/10." Today is 11/11. The deal ended yesterday. I don't trust a brand much when the email says the offer is over. ## What's working

- The GO WALK Flex Plus is a cushioned walking sneaker in a style I'd wear, and it's the first non-skull hero this week.
- The "Take Another Look" button is wide, dark blue and easy to tap on my phone.
- The six-shoe grid includes Slip-ins and Arch Fit styles, which is the hands-free, arch-support stuff I want.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids and so on) is big, plain and easy to hit.
- The member 20% banner is the biggest text on the page, so the offer is easy to spot.

## What's weak

- The 20% off banner says "Valid thru 11/10," so it's expired on the day I opened it. The fine print says the same, "11/5/26 – 11/10/26."
- "Still thinking about it?" claims I browsed or carted something. I didn't, and this is the cart-nag pattern again.
- Half the grid is Women's shoes, which are no use to me. The product captions are tiny and hard to read without zooming.
- There's no price on any shoe, so I can't do the price-versus-quality math I care about. There are no reviews and no comfort details either.
- The bottom is cluttered: texting, app download, curbside, Afterpay and Klarna, and social links. It buries the point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send a 11/10 deadline on 11/11. Suppress expired offers, or swap in a current one.
- 2. Only send "Still thinking about it?" when someone really has a cart or a viewed item. Otherwise use a neutral line like "Our most comfortable walking shoe."
- 3. Show prices, star ratings and a short comfort note ("Arch Fit, 4.7 stars") under each shoe. Increase the caption text size.
- 4. Fill the grid with men's Slip-ins and walking styles when the subscriber is male.
- 5. Cut the footer clutter down to one or two items.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is sound. The QA run flagged a failing unsubscribe link (HTTP 400), no plain-text part, and 14 images served over `http://`. The malformed charset meta tag and the empty `<title>` come from the HTML source. SPF/DKIM could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned HTTP 400** (QA `[FAIL]`, link text "Unsubscribe"): `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=ChQwME5H…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r&attn_lid=m57&externalId=2U3eG`. The decoded `attn_macro` is `unsubscribeLink`. This is a tokenized, per-recipient Attentive URL, so a 400 may be a probe artifact: the token may be single-use or tied to the requester's headers or IP. Treat it as unconfirmed until it is opened in a real client.
- All links appear wrapped in the `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…` click-tracking redirector, for example the "web version" link. This is standard, but destination URLs can't be read from the source.
- Only 12 of 43 links were probed. The other 31 were skipped as low priority (footer, social), so their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 images use `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/...`**. They include the logo (`m/1/dde00662…png`), the hero "Take Another Look" (`m/8/58e7efe1…jpg`), the app-store badges and the social icons. Clients that block mixed content, or that proxy images, may drop them. The host should serve HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `217247_BBK_HERO_LG` (`images.skechers.com/image;width=auto,format=auto/217247_BBK_HERO_LG`)
  - `m/5/a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg`
- The tracking pixel `skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…` also has no alt text. A pixel normally uses `alt=""`, so this is low severity.
- **The charset meta tag is malformed:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. This matters in clients that ignore the HTTP or MIME charset.
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- The responsive CSS (`@media (max-width:620px)`), the `stack` classes, the `mso-` properties and the hidden preheader are all present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{`, `%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The HTML is truncated, so this was checked only against the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link failed the probe.** See section 2. If the 400 is real, it breaches the CAN-SPAM requirement for a functioning opt-out mechanism.
- **Plain-text fallback is 0 characters** (QA `[FAIL]`). This is a deliverability signal and an accessibility problem. It could be an AgentMail relay artifact, so check the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA `[WARN]`). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `msgs.skechers.com`.
- The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not available in the data, so they could not be assessed.
- The physical postal address in the footer is outside the truncated HTML, so it could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Nothing is confirmable. Click URLs are opaque Attentive redirect tokens, and the HTML is truncated. UTM parameters and the final landing URLs are not visible, and the redirect targets were not resolved in the QA data. Product images load from `images.skechers.com`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Open the unsubscribe URL in a real client. If it still returns 400, regenerate the token or fix the Attentive flow. This is a compliance blocker.
2. Serve all `image.emails.skechers.com` assets over HTTPS. That means 14 images, including the hero and logo.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to `217247_BBK_HERO_LG` and `a6ec5198-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the open pixel.
4. Fix the charset tag to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` or `<meta charset="utf-8">`, and populate `<title>`.
5. Include a `text/plain` MIME part. First check whether the relay is stripping it.
6. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for the primary CTAs to verify UTMs and landing pages.
