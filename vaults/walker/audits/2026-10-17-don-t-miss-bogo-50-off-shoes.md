# Don't miss BOGO 50% off shoes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- It's DSW again, and this is the fifth BOGO email in about three days. After "BOGO 50% off shoes ends SOON" earlier today and "Buy one, get one 50% off shoes" yesterday, this one is a rerun with a new subject line. The green banner at the top says "BOGO 50% OFF*" in big white letters, and I understood it in two seconds. Buy one pair, get 50% off a second pair.
- Then the email turns into a fashion spread. It shows Frye boots, Timberland boots, Keds Mary Janes and Reebok, and the models are young women. Nothing in it is a cushioned walking shoe, a slip-on, or anything with arch support. The "Shoe Box Shuffle" game and the rewards pitch are noise to me. I'd read it, because the BOGO is real and I do buy two pairs at a time. I probably wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The green "BOGO 50% OFF*" banner is huge and clear, and the sub-line explains it in plain words.
- "SHOP NOW" is a big white button, with Women, Men and Kids links right under it. I can tap those on my phone.
- The "Shop by size" grid is easy to read, with large numbers and wide-width buttons. Wide width matters to my feet.
- Free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and Afterpay are listed in one tidy row.
- The email is on-brand for DSW and matches the stores and the site.

## What's weak

- Every product shown is aimed at women. Frye, Keds and the models were all women's. I had to look for a men's link.
- There's nothing about comfort, cushioning, arch support or slip-ons. For me that's the whole reason to buy a shoe.
- The footnote text in the legal section is tiny, and the exclusions are long. They include Skechers, Clarks and Hoka-type brands, I think, and that's buried at the bottom. I cross-shop those brands, so it's a surprise.
- The Shoebox Shuffle game and the "join free" banner are clutter. I only came for the sale.
- The "ends 10/25" deadline is down in the fine print and not up top. It also keeps coming every day, so "Don't miss" doesn't feel urgent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date (10/25) in the top banner, and show the top excluded brands up front. Don't hide them in the small print.
- 2. Send one BOGO email a week, not one a day. That alone would fix most of what bugs me.
- 3. Add a men's comfort row, with cushioned walking shoes and slip-ons. Name the brands and show the prices. Show a men's model who looks like me.
- 4. Cut the game block, or move it below the size grid.
- 5. Make the legal text bigger, or put it behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off ends 10/25: men's walking shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two pairs of comfy shoes for less`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned slip-ons and walkers, buy one, get one 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 10/25. See which brands are excluded.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/SendGrid-built XHTML template. Automated QA found one failing link (the SMS opt-in CTA, HTTP 400), two missing-alt images, and a text part that is mostly URLs. Authentication and footer compliance could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failing CTA:** the link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned **HTTP 400** when probed. The URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/TnQtFSvQIUQD6YefzmGWVA~~/AAQRxRA~/UlW4-BTiu7…`, a SendGrid click-tracking wrapper. A 400 from a tracking redirect can be a bot-probe artifact (malformed or stripped token), so confirm manually in a real client. If it also fails there, the SMS acquisition CTA is dead.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 20,826 of 25,569 characters (about 81%) of the plain-text part are URLs, mostly long `ablink.dsw.com/...` tracking strings. This raises spam-filter risk and makes the text part hard to read.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 63 links were probed and 51 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, image 1:** `src="https://ablink.dsw.com/q/dyLaFbRMAOYLyAah_UVxRg~~/AAQRxRA~/SFj8nZa9qfa3e_…"`. Add descriptive alt text.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261017_P261502_V1_W-_Offer_Multi`. It should have `alt=""` and ideally `role="presentation"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers fall back to a default language.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so they do nothing. Charset is not declared reliably in the HTML, though the MIME header likely covers it.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is the same for every send.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The HTML source was truncated, and no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible portion or in the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication unverified:** the QA data shows `Authentication-Results header not found`. This is a limitation of the AgentMail relay, not evidence of a failure. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` are unconfirmed.
- **Unsubscribe and address not checked:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure for it. The footer, physical postal address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not visible:** all links go through `ablink.dsw.com` wrappers with opaque tokens, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be read from the source. The Movable Ink pixel carries `mi_ecmp=20261017_P261502_V1_W-_Offer_Multi`, which does identify the campaign.
- **Landing pages not validated:** apart from the 400 on the SMS opt-in, no other probed link was reported as failing. Whether the destinations match the BOGO offer in the subject line is not confirmed by the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually test the SMS opt-in link (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/TnQtFSvQ…`) in a real client. If it fails, fix the link or its tracking token in Braze/SendGrid.
2. Add `alt` text to the first image. Set `alt=""` on `o.gif`.
