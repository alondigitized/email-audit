# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the skull sneaker again. It's the black UNO "Alma De Guerrero" I've been nagged about on 11/05, 11/08, three times on 11/11, and again through 11/16 and 11/17. I have never put it in a cart. "You liked these, and you weren't wrong" is just wrong. I never liked them. After this many sends it reads like a machine talking to itself, not a store that knows me.
- The lower half is better. Slip-ins boots and sneakers, a waterproof pair, and a Black Friday Buy One Get One 50% off banner are all things I'd look at. But they sit under a hero I don't want, behind a "Complete your order" button for an order I never started.
- One more thing. The fine print says the offer ran 11/12 to 11/17, and today is 11/18. So the deal in the email looks expired, and it's attached to a "your cart expires soon" scare. I wouldn't click. I might scroll for the slip-ins, but I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The Buy One Get One 50% off banner is big, plain, and easy to read on a phone. It also gives a 20% off single pair fallback.
- Several of the "more options" shoes are slip-ins with relaxed fit, waterproof boots and cozy cushioning, so they speak to how I shop.
- The navy "Complete your order" button and the black "Shop all best sellers" bar are large and easy to tap.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. Category links like Men, Women and Sale are big enough for my thumbs.

## What's weak

- The hero is the same skull sneaker I've ignored all month. It's a flashy Day of the Dead design, nothing like my casual comfort style.
- "You liked these" is false. I never added anything to a cart, and the claim hurts my trust in the brand.
- The fine print says the offer is valid 11/12 to 11/17, but this arrived 11/18. The deal looks expired, and the countdown is not believable.
- The slip-ins and comfort details are down in a small grid. The text under each shoe is tiny, with no prices and no arch support or cushioning callouts.
- Nothing tells me I'm a known customer. There's no points balance or member perk, and no men's walking focus up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart reminders to people with no cart, and stop repeating one product. If it must go out, lead with a men's slip-in comfort shoe and put the Buy One Get One banner above the fold.
- 2. Fix the offer dates so the fine print matches the day the email lands. Don't send expired promos.
- 3. Put prices, sizes in stock, and cushioning or arch support notes under each shoe, in bigger text.
- 4. Add a comfort-focused row, such as "Slip-ins for all-day walking", and mention member pricing if I have it.
