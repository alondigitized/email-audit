# Clearance prices just dropped…
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. The subject promised clearance prices, and the top banner does have a number: "Extra 10% off clearance online" with code GOLDEN, plus "always 30% off in stores." That's the same banner I've seen all week, since the 9/28 "Get Extra 10% Off Clearance!" email and the two "Extra 10% off" sends yesterday and today. The "Mood Boost" headline in big orange script is cute, but it tells me nothing about a shoe or a price.
- Below the banner it's a long scroll of grids. I see a chunky high-top sneaker, a clog, booties, flats, and heels with a floral strappy sandal. Down in the sneaker block, the green and burgundy retro trainers look like something I might wear, but nothing is labeled with a price or a comfort feature. Nothing says cushioned, arch support, or slip-on. "Oh, and Happy UGG Season" in the preview didn't show up anywhere in the email that I could find.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I'd scroll to the sneakers, squint, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for a real sale on Hoka or Brooks.

## What's working

- The top banner gives me a real number, 10% off plus 30% in stores, and a code I can copy.
- The sneaker block is the only part that looks like it was made for me. Low-profile walking-style shoes in earth tones, and mostly unisex.
- The "Shop by Size" number row is big and easy to tap with my thumb. The Wide Width and Wide Calf buttons are a nice touch for feet like mine.
- The black "Shop" buttons are large and clear, and the DSW logo up top makes it feel trustworthy.

## What's weak

- It's the seventh or eighth clearance email this week with the same 10% off. The urgency is gone.
- Almost nothing is aimed at a 62-year-old man. Most of the grids are women's flats, heels, and sandals, and I see only a few men's-looking styles in the sneaker block.
- No prices on any shoe except the "Under $50" grid. I can't do my price-versus-quality math.
- No comfort, cushioning, or arch-support language anywhere. Nothing is hands-free or slip-on either.
- The "Happy UGG Season" preview text never pays off in the body.
- The fine print is tiny and runs long. I'd need to zoom on my phone to read the exclusions on that asterisk.

- 1. Put a men's comfort row near the top, such as slip-on walkers or cushioned sneakers from Skechers, Hoka, and Brooks, with real prices and the clearance markdown shown.
- 2. Tell me what's new. If there's nothing new this week, send fewer emails or rotate the creative.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-style build on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with dark-mode CSS and tracked links on `ablink.dsw.com`. One CTA link failed its probe with a 400, the plain-text part is almost entirely URLs, and there are some accessibility gaps. The HTML and QA data were truncated, so the body, footer and headers were not fully visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (FAIL, HTTP 400):** The link with text "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" points to `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/HkcqoN1fSqk4uupf2Lll9w~~/AAQRxRA~/ir_FfFv5fl1siNRF7zCckaIARomAK…`. The 400 response carried no status text.
  - This is a primary conversion CTA, so a real 400 would mean a dead SMS sign-up.
  - A 400 from a probe can also come from the tracker rejecting non-browser requests or a missing device context, so this needs a manual check in a real client.
  - The tracked URL is about 700 characters long, which adds to the plain-text problem in section 3.
- **Opaque click tracking:** All material links go through the `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…` and `/q/…` redirectors. I can't confirm final destinations from the source I was given.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 63 were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). The unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, linked image:** `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/Cb_ZrmjkxgMSRJl2waApXw~~/AAQRxRA~/PDef9DBVLW1ks0QQ…`. If this image is wrapped in a link, screen readers get an unlabeled link. If images are blocked, the layout loses its content.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel (low severity):** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20260930_P260598_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B` is a Movable Ink pixel. It should have `alt=""`.
- **Plain text is 87% URLs:** The QA warning says 30,991 of 35,625 characters are URLs. The long tracked links make the text part hard to read and look spammy to some filters.
- **Invalid meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration does anything.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` element has no `lang` or `xml:lang`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive, which is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The body past the truncation point wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
No issues flagged by the QA data. The unsubscribe link was probed and did not fail. I can't verify the physical address, the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, or SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, because the footer and headers were not provided. The `From: dsw@e.dsw.com` subdomain is consistent with the `dsw.com` link domains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
I can't confirm any issues. Destinations are hidden behind `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be checked from the source. The only campaign identifier visible is Movable Ink's `mi_ecmp=20260930_P260598_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B`. The one CTA whose destination I most need to check, the texts opt-in, returned a 400.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Test the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link manually on mobile. If it really returns 400, fix the Braze link template or the SMS opt-in destination before further sends.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the linked image at `ablink.dsw.com/q/Cb_ZrmjkxgMSRJl2waApXw~~…`, and set `alt=""` on the `o.gif` pixel.
3. Fix the meta tags: use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Shorten the tracking URLs, or write a hand-authored plain-text version with fewer raw links, to bring the URL ratio down.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers so the footer, unsubscribe headers, authentication results and UTM parameters can be checked.
