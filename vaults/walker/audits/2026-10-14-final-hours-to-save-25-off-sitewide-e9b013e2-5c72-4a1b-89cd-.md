## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed broken personalization token (`Hi ,` greeting) and several markup and pixel defects, including a scheme-less Return Path pixel URL and HTTP-only images. The HTML supplied was truncated and 32 tracking links were not probed, so link, footer, and header compliance could not be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 32 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains (QA WARN), so destinations, redirect chains, and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **Broken pixel URL:** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no scheme (`https://` or `//`). Clients will resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the deliverability and inbox-placement pixel never fires. It also has no `alt`.
- **HTTP tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITE_ENGAGEDL7D_EN_10132026` is non-HTTPS (QA WARN). It can be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling, and it has no `alt`.
- **Third-party beacons:** Three `beacon.krxd.net` pixels are in the hidden div. One is a user-match whose `partner_uid` is a 64-hex string (`9153f33f…ed00`), apparently a SHA-256 hash of an identifier. This is cross-domain data sharing and should be checked against the privacy policy. They also have no `alt`, and `width="0" height="0"` is unreliable across clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image alt text:** Two images flagged by QA lack `alt` and use `http://`:
  - `o.gif` (ink1000)
  - `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  The second is a content image on the brand's own domain. HTTP-only delivery risks blocking or a mixed-content warning, and the missing `alt` leaves it unannounced to screen readers and blank when images are off.

  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
