# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- There it is again — that same pink floral D'Lites - Spring Bloom sitting right at the top like it's the star of the show. Seventh, eighth time this week now, I've lost count. I never put that shoe in a cart, I never even clicked on it, so "did you forget something" is a pretty funny question to ask a guy who never had it in the first place.
- That said, credit where it's due — this one at least scrolled down into a real product grid, and I did spot the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit and the Men's OG Anunoby basketball shoe mixed in there. Not exactly my aisle — I'm not lacing up for basketball — but at least it's not 100% floral this time. The 20% off code with "COZY FIT & GLIDE STEP" language actually speaks my language for once.
- Bottom line: I'm not clicking on that cart reminder because it's not my cart, and I'm getting numb to it. If they'd led with the Slip-ins section instead of the phantom floral shoe, I might've scrolled with more patience. Not forwarding this, not marking it important — straight to skim-and-archive.

## What's working

- The 20% off "COZY FIT & GLIDE STEP" banner uses language that's actually relevant to what I look for in a shoe.
- Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit - Altman - Murdock Sneaker showed up in the grid, which is a shoe I'd actually consider.
- Layout is clean, text is big enough to read without zooming, buttons are chunky enough to tap.
- Shop-now-pay-later and curbside pickup icons at the bottom are a nice practical touch even if I don't use them.

## What's weak

- The hero "READY TO CHECK OUT?" plus the pink floral D'Lites is a false alarm — that has never been my cart, not once, all week.
- The "Complete Your Order" button appears twice, both tied to a shoe I've never touched — feels like it's just badgering me now.
- Buried in with the men's stuff is a basketball shoe and a boy's Garage Sneaker (twice!) — neither's for me, and the boy's shoe duplicated is just clutter.
- No sizing, arch support, or "why this shoe" detail on any of the men's picks — just a photo and a name, nothing that helps me decide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the fake cart-abandonment gimmick entirely for someone like me who's never touched that shoe — lead instead with the "Find your comfort" style approach from 8/29 that actually put a guy my age front and center.
- 2. Swap the hero image from the floral D'Lites to the Men's Slip-ins Relaxed Fit or the brown leather Garza-Gervin from the "This is perfect for you!" send — that one actually landed.
- 3. Cut the duplicate Boy's Garage Sneaker tile and the basketball shoe — neither belongs in a men's-comfort-shopper's inbox.
- 4. Add a line of real product info under the men's shoes — arch support, slip-on vs lace, memory foam — something with substance, not just a name.
