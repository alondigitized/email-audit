### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text alternative missing**

```
Text version is 0 chars
```

CAN-SPAM does not explicitly require a plain-text part, but its absence triggers higher spam scores in SpamAssassin and Postfix-based filters. RFC 2822 best practice and most ESPs require a non-empty `text/plain` MIME part. This is a deliverability risk.

**[WARN] `referrer: no-referrer` suppresses click attribution**

```html
<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer" />
```

This instructs browsers to strip the `Referer` header on navigation from the email. Any landing page relying on the HTTP `Referer` header for attribution (outside of UTM params) will receive no referrer signal. Low risk if UTMs are correctly applied to all links, but compounds any UTM gaps.
