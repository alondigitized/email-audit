The "Shop Men's" button is there, but it's one of four equal buttons, so I have to hunt for it.

- "Hi, Walker" with Member status and the 1000-point, $5 redeem line makes me feel like a known customer. It matches what I liked in the earlier "Final hours to double up on comfort" send.
- "20% OFF SITEWIDE" is huge, white on navy, and the eye lands on it right away.
- The $5 reward on $75+ is a concrete second incentive, and it doesn't make me do points math.
- The text is big and the buttons are wide, so they're easy to tap on my phone.
- The Slip-ins shoe in Best Sellers is the hands-free comfort I actually shop for.

## What's weak

- Four equal buttons (Women's, Men's, Boys', Girls') make me hunt for mine. Men's isn't first.
- The shoes have no names, prices or sale prices, and they don't mention cushioning or arch support. I have no idea what the 20% saves me on a shoe.
- The "Valid online only thru 10/26" line is tiny, and the fine print at the bottom is a wall of text. The exclusions (sale items, select styles) are buried, so I don't fully trust "sitewide."
- The red canvas sneaker doesn't look like me. Nothing in the email shows a man my age, and nothing shows comfort features.
- The "Shop Additional Styles" list is plain. "Wide" is a nice touch, but it sits at the bottom, where I'd miss it.

- 3. Say "ends Sunday 10/26" up in the hero, not just in tiny type. Put the main exclusions in one plain line just under the offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect, an empty greeting that renders "Hi ,". The source also has a scheme-less tracking pixel URL, several non-HTTPS or alt-less images, and ineffective `<meta>` declarations. SPF/DKIM/DMARC, unsubscribe and physical-address compliance, and UTM alignment can't be assessed from the truncated HTML and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed in source):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…">` has no `http(s)://` scheme. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails and the Return Path/Validity seed-tracking pixel never fires.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITEREWARD_FULL_EN_10222026` is plain HTTP, and the QA check flags it as likely to be blocked or rewritten. It will often fail to load in clients that block mixed content.
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 32 tracking/click-redirect links (`click.emails.skechers.com` and similar). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party identifiers in pixel URLs:** the `beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif` requests pass a 64-character hex value (`partner=…_sha25…&partner_uid=9153f33f…`) and a subscriber ID (`partner_uid=121173391`). That is a hashed identifier plus a subscriber key sent to a third party. This is a data-sharing and privacy-review item, not a rendering defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  - `cd4aa7e9-775d-439d-9828-5e9ed23fdb5a.jpg`

  The last two are content images served from `image.emails.skechers.com`. With images off, or in a screen reader, they have no text equivalent.
- **Non-HTTPS content image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-…png` can be blocked or flagged as mixed content. The sibling `.jpg` is served over HTTPS, so this is inconsistent.
- **Ineffective meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declaration is valid or applied.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }` stops users from enlarging text in WebKit clients.
- **Layout tables:** the visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`) have no `role="presentation"`. The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm this for the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL: empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as "Hi ," with the name field empty. The merge field resolved to an empty string, and there is no fallback or conditional wrapper. This is the only confirmed hard failure and accounts for the 71% pass rate.
- The tracking IDs (`partner_uid=121173391`) did populate, so the subscriber record resolved. The first-name attribute is the likely gap.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` was not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unverified. This is a test-pipeline limitation, not a confirmed defect.
- **From domain:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, and the tracking domains (`click.`, `image.emails.skechers.com`) match it, which is good for alignment.
- **Unsubscribe and address:** the truncated HTML doesn't include the footer, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. No `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header data was provided. I'm not flagging these as missing.
- **No-reply sender:** the `no-reply@` address is a minor responsiveness concern, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** All 32 click links are SFMC redirect wrappers (`click.emails.skechers.com`) that QA didn't resolve, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status are unknown.
- **Campaign ID:** the only visible identifier is `PRO_US_NA_U_SITEREWARD_FULL_EN_10222026`, in the `krxd` and `ink1000` pixels. It can serve as a reference for confirming UTM `utm_campaign` parity once the redirects are resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting:** add a merge-field fallback (for example, `%%=IIF(Empty(@firstName),"Hi there","Hi "+@firstName)=%%`), or suppress the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel:** change it to `https://pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=…`.
3. **Upgrade HTTP assets to HTTPS:** this covers the `ink1000.com` pixel and the `image.emails.skechers.com/.../dde00662-…png` image.
4. **Add `alt` attributes:** use descriptive text on the two content images, and `alt=""` on the decorative or tracking images (`o.gif`).
5. **Correct the meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge" />`. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, drop `maximum-scale=1`, and add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. **Resolve the redirect chains:** follow the 32 click-tracking links to confirm final destinations, status codes and UTM parameters.
7. **Verify authentication and footer:** re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`. Confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address in the full, untruncated HTML.
8. **Review the third-party pixels:** have privacy/legal review the `krxd.net` hashed-ID and subscriber-ID sharing against the brand's consent disclosures.
