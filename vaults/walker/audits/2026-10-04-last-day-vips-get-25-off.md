# Last day! VIPs get 25% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost track of how many of these I've gotten since 10/01. The giant white "25% OFF*" on purple is the same banner from "Happy VIP Days" and from "VIP's 25% off ends *tomorrow*" yesterday. Nothing here is new except the word "Last day!" It's easy to read on my phone, and I'll give them that.
- Then I scroll and it's all boots, booties and heels. A Dr. Scholl's sneaker is the only thing I'd wear. The "Shop Men" link is there, but no man's shoe is shown anywhere. I'm a 62-year-old guy looking for something cushioned and slip-on, and this is a women's boot catalog.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd maybe tap "Shop Men" out of habit if I needed shoes this week. I wouldn't forward it. A "last day" email is fair, but after eight in a row the urgency doesn't land.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF*" is huge, white on purple, and I can read it without zooming.
- The code VIPFUN is stated plainly, along with "regular-price styles only," so I know what the deal covers.
- The nav bar and the four "Shop" links (Women, Men, Kids, Everything) are big and easy to tap.
- Each product shows the crossed-out price next to the sale price, so I can do the math quickly.
- The "Shop by Size" block with "Shop Wide Width" is a thoughtful touch for people with wider feet.

## What's weak

- Every one of the nine products is a women's boot, bootie, heel or sandal. Only the Dr. Scholl's sneaker is close to my taste, and nothing is for men.
- It's the same purple banner I've seen all week, so it feels like a stranger's mass mailing, not something for a known customer.
- The preview promises "extra points & a free shoe protection spray," but I can't find either in the email body. That feels like a bait-and-switch.
- The fine print takes up a huge chunk of the bottom. It lists excluded brands, including Skechers, and that's a brand I buy. It's buried where I won't see it.
- Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-on. It's all fashion, with no comfort details.
- The main "Become a VIP" button is pale teal with light text. It's less prominent than the black "Shop Now" button below it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by gender. Show me men's comfort and walking shoes, such as slip-ons and cushioned sneakers, in the product grid. Right now I get women's boots.
- 2. Put "Last day" and "ends tonight at midnight" in the hero, and cut the repeated banner. Eight near-identical sends in four days is too many. Sending one fewer would make this one feel real.
- 3. Make the "Become a VIP" button darker or more contrasting, and use a single clear CTA such as "Shop men's comfort shoes."
- 4. If you promise bonus points and a free spray in the preview, show them in the body near the top.
- 5. Move the brand exclusions (Skechers, Clarks) up near the offer. Don't bury them below the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 25% off men's comfort shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: VIP 25% off, code VIPFUN`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shoe protection spray and bonus points, today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ons, walking shoes & wide widths. Code VIPFUN ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed broken link: the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returns HTTP 400. It also has two images missing alt text and a plain-text part that is mostly tracking URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so only the QA data and the visible head (meta tags, styles, font-faces) could be checked.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (HTTP 400):** the link text is "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off". The wrapped Braze/SendGrid-style URL is `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/Yw6-K72WMdt6lZOhP6kUng~~/AAQRxRA~/duf7nDFk…Q~~`. It is about 900 characters long.
  - The 400 may come from a stale or malformed tracking token, or from the probe not following the redirect the way a mail client would. QA cannot tell which.
  - The link is a revenue-adjacent SMS opt-in CTA, so a real 400 would break the conversion path.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 61 skipped. The 61 skipped links were not validated, so other broken links may exist.
- **Link wrapping:** every link goes through `ablink.dsw.com` click-tracking redirects. This is expected, but it means the final destination and its UTM parameters can't be confirmed from the email source.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/i7cuEqRb6Xq4fNAYfsWr8Q~~/AAQRxRA~/3AC_Nqh_Sneqq4A1LqVgMQvyEapbRFrVaRCAdKxl6Rxgrklwy6eq1iRfd9Ghg` is a tracked image with no alt attribute. If it's content imagery, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing for it.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261004_P261342_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B` is an open-tracking pixel. A missing alt on it is low impact, but `alt=""` is still preferred.
- **Head markup:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv` attribute.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` also has no `http-equiv` attribute.
  - Both are inert without it, so the charset declaration is ineffective. The email still renders correctly if the charset comes from the Content-Type header.
- **Fonts:** custom fonts load from `braze-images.com` (`.woff` and `.ttf`). Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so fallback stacks matter. They were not visible in the truncated source.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags and a `prefers-color-scheme` block are present. `.dm-bg-white` forces `#ffffff` in dark mode. That is intentional but worth checking for contrast.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom in supporting clients, which is an accessibility concern.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The tracking pixel carries `mi_u=225090073`, a rendered per-recipient ID, which is expected.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe:** QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and none failed. I couldn't check its placement or the physical postal address because the source was truncated.
- **Authentication headers:** the QA data has no SPF, DKIM, DMARC or `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` results. These are unverified, not confirmed absent.
- **Plain-text deliverability [WARN]:** 27,164 of 31,608 characters (about 86%) of the plain-text part are URLs, well over the 70% threshold. The long tracking URLs inflate this and can hurt spam scoring. The plain-text part also appears to be an auto-generated dump of the HTML links.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Landing page alignment couldn't be verified. The destinations are hidden behind `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, and the QA data doesn't include resolved final URLs or UTM parameters.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261004_P261342_V3_WM-_Offer_MI_B` on the tracking pixel. The `WM-_` segment looks like an empty or unresolved token, but that is an inference.
- The subject says "Last day! VIPs get 25% off!" while the broken CTA promises "15% Off" for text opt-in. These are different offers, so continuity can't be confirmed without the destination pages.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix or re-test the 400 CTA.** Request `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/Yw6-K72WMdt6lZOhP6kUng~~/…` with a mobile user-agent and follow redirects. If it still fails, regenerate the SMS opt-in link in the ESP.
2. **Raise probe coverage** beyond 12 of 73 links, or run a full crawl of the CTA, hero and product links, to catch other 4xx/5xx responses.
3. **Add `alt` text** to the content image (`…/3AC_Nqh_…`). Set `alt=""` on the `o.gif` pixel.
4. **Correct the head meta tags** to `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=Edge" />`.
5. **Rebuild the plain-text part** with short, readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
6. **Verify the unsubscribe and authentication items** from full headers: SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com`, plus `List-Unsubscribe` and one-click `List-Unsubscribe-Post`.
7. **Check the `mi_ecmp` value** for the empty `WM-_` segment, and confirm UTM parameters on the final landing URLs.
8. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
