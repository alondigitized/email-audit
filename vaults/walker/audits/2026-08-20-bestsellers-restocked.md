# Bestsellers restocked
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- Now this is the New Balance email I've been waiting for — a chunky 9060 in a brown suede colorway, front and center, no models, no fine print circus. It's a clean product-first send: "Now's your chance," bestseller restocked for fall, two clear buttons. That's how you sell a shoe to a guy my age — show me the thing, tell me it's back, let me decide.
- That said, this is a chunky dad-sneaker style, not the smooth slip-on walker I actually wear these days, and there's zero mention of comfort, cushioning, or arch support anywhere in the copy — it's all "bestselling" and "trending," style talk. I'd look at it for a few seconds, maybe click through out of curiosity on the brown pair since it's a good-looking shoe, but I wouldn't forward this to anybody. It's a step up from the last few New Balance sends though — no wooden chairs, no twentysomething runners, just shoes.

## What's working

- One hero shoe, one clear product shot, no clutter — I know exactly what they're selling.
- "Now's your chance" plus "just restocked for fall" gives me a real, current reason to look, not some vague seasonal filler.
- Two buttons, "Shop now" and "Visit a store" — I like having the store option since I'd rather try these on anyway.
- Free shipping over $75 banner up top is honest and simple, no code-hunting required.
- The color grid at the bottom (black, white, gray, tan combos) lets me compare options without extra scrolling per shoe.

## What's weak

- Not a whisper about comfort, cushioning, or arch support — for a guy who buys with his feet first, that's the whole pitch missing.
- This 9060 is a bulky retro-dad-shoe silhouette, not a hands-free slip-on or a clean walking shoe — doesn't quite match how I actually shop.
- No price shown anywhere, not even the "up to 30% off" they've been running lately — after Tuesday's 30%-off code email, no price here feels like a step backward.
- "What's trending" footer tile is vague — trending with who, doing what, that tells me nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a line about the cushioning or comfort tech in the 9060 — even one sentence would connect this to guys like me who care about how it feels on a long walk, not just how it looks.
- 2. Put a price or percentage-off somewhere near the hero button — after seeing a straight discount code Tuesday, a restock with no price at all reads like a step back.
- 3. Swap "Visit a store" language for something with a nudge toward trying it on for fit — "Try it in-store" plays better to the comfort-first crowd.
- 4. Cut or rename "What's trending" — replace with something concrete like "Also back in stock" tied to actual product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The 9060 is back — here's your size`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fan favorite restocked: New Balance 9060`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bestselling comfort, back for fall in four colorways`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab it before it sells out again`
