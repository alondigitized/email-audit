# Black Friday Deals Start Now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- Crocs again, and this time the deal finally changed. After a week of "2 for $50" (most recently "Black Friday Early Access Ends Tonight!" on 11/15), I get "Up to 60% Off." I can read that on my phone. But "select styles" and "exclusions apply" make me wonder which shoes that actually covers.
- The hero shows a fuzzy-lined black clog on a red gift box, and that's the one thing I'd call useful for me. After that the email drifts. There's a model in a hooded blanket, leopard-print slippers, and kids' clogs. I'm a 62-year-old guy who wants cushion and arch support, and none of the three is for me.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent-looking sale email sent to someone who has already seen a lot of Crocs this month. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- "Up to 60% Off" is big and black on light gray, so I can read it without zooming.
- The lined clog on the gift box is simple and shows me a real product.
- The "Shop Black Friday Deals" button is big, black and easy to tap with my thumb.
- The free blanket with a $100 purchase is a clear perk, and the "Get Cozy" button is plain.
- The footer states the end date, Sunday, November 29th, at 11:59pm MT, so I know when the sale ends.

## What's weak

- "Select styles" and "Exclusions apply" under the 60% make the offer feel hedged. I can't tell if my clogs are in it.
- The blanket section has a woman in a hooded wrap, and the animal print slippers are loud. Neither is aimed at me.
- Nothing says hands-free, slip-on, cushioning or arch support. It's all price and novelty.
- The "TikTok Shop Black Friday: Now on your FYP" banner is lost on me. I don't know what an FYP is.
- It has two "Doorbusters" buttons in each of two sections, which reads like the same button twice.
- I got "Early Access Ends Tonight" earlier today, and now "Deals Start Now." The timing doesn't line up with the last one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one best-selling men's or comfort clog and put the real price on it, instead of "up to" and "select styles."
- 2. Add a men's comfort row with slip-on, cushioned styles and a few lines on fit and support.
- 3. Put the end date near the top. It's buried in the footer.
- 4. Cut the second "Shop All Doorbusters" button in each section.
- 5. Don't send a new "start" email right after an "ends tonight" one.
