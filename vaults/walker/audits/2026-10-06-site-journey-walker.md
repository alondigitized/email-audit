# Daily Journey: Walker Miles on www.skechers.com
**Score:** 3/10 (prev 4/10) · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-06**
## Full review
## 1. Executive Summary

I'm 62, on my phone, and I want a comfortable slip-on. The front of the Skechers site gets me close, but I never got to a cart. The homepage opens with "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cushioning" across the top, which is exactly what I'm after. The Men's menu is clean and easy to read. Then the trip fell apart:

- The login step looked the same as the homepage, with no sign I was logged in.
- The first shoe I landed on was a $225 racing shoe marked "Excluded from promotions."
- Add to Cart didn't add anything because no size was chosen. It showed "Please select a size" in red.
- The cart page was a blank white screen.
- Searching "comfort shoes" showed women's shoes first.

## 2. Business Impact Score (1-10)

**3/10.** Yesterday was 4/10, so this is a step down. I only have yesterday's score, not its screenshots, so I can't say exactly what changed. The blank cart page is the worst thing I saw today, and I can't buy anything if the cart is empty. If yesterday's cart loaded, that's the regression.

## 3. What's Working

- **Comfort filters up top.** "Slip-ins," "Wide Fit," "Arch Fit" and "Max Cushioning" are in the first thing I see. That's a good sign for someone like me.
- **The Men's menu drawer is easy.** The text is big, each row is a wide tap target, and there's a Back button. A 62-year-old could use this without help.
- **Men's Shoes has the right shortcuts.** "Hands Free Slip-Ins" and "Walking Shoes" are right at the top, with a Filter button and a Sort button I can reach.
- **The size buttons are big.** They're easy to tap with a thumb, and the red size warning is plain.
- **The homepage photo of a couple my age** made me feel the brand might actually be for me.

## 4. What's Weak

- **The cart page was blank (Step 8).** I'd leave right here and go to Hoka or Brooks.
- **The Add to Cart step didn't work (Step 7).** It showed "Please select a size" and never told me whether anything was added. The cart badge still said 0 in the other screenshots.
- **Login did nothing visible (Step 3).** The screen was identical to the homepage. There was no sign-in form, no "Hi, Walker," and nothing personalized.
- **The popup step showed nothing (Step 2).** It was the same screen as Step 1, so I can't say whether anything was dismissed.
- **The first product was wrong for me (Step 6).** The Aero Elite is a $225 racing shoe with no reviews and is "Excluded from promotions." It's not a comfort shoe, and the banners say BOGO 50% off, so it feels like a bait-and-switch.
- **"Applied at Cart" shows up everywhere.** I can't see my real price until I check out. Some shoes get 50% off a second pair and some don't, and I can't tell which.
- **The "comfort shoes" search put women's shoes first (Step 9).** It found 1,581 results, but the first two were women's and I had to scroll to find anything for a man.
- **No comparison against Hoka, Brooks or New Balance.** Nothing helps me work out whether it's worth the money.
- **The small text and the cut-off "Max Cus…" tab** are hard to read on a phone.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Fix the cart page first.** Don't ship a blank cart. Show an empty-cart message with a "Keep shopping" button at minimum, and run an automated check on this page.
2. **Confirm Add to Cart.** Show a clear "Added to cart" message and update the cart badge. Highlight the size section if it's missing, rather than only putting a red message at the bottom.
3. **Show a logged-in state.** A greeting, my name, or recently viewed comfort styles would tell me the login worked.
4. **Sort comfort shoes by my category.** If I came in through Men, show men's shoes in the search results. Put Slip-ins and Arch Fit at the top of "comfort shoes."
5. **Show the sale price on the product page.** I shouldn't have to wait until the cart to learn what I'll pay.
6. **Add a "why Skechers vs. other comfort brands" section.** Show cushioning and arch support side by side, plus the price per year of wear.
7. **Make the text bigger and let the filter tabs wrap** instead of scrolling off the screen.

## 6. Bottom Line

Skechers has the right comfort categories in the right places, and the menus are simple enough for me. Nothing after that got me to a purchase. The cart was blank, the first shoe was a racing flat, and the search put women's shoes first. I'd walk away and look at Hoka or Brooks. Score 3/10, down from 4/10.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary

The journey was rate-limited by Kasada bot protection. The fingerprint endpoint (`/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.825`) returned 429 five times, and `/cart/` returned 429. The View Cart findings are probably an error page, not the real cart.

- **Wrong page URL captured:** Homepage, Log In and Men category all record `https://www.skechers.com/`, and their HTML samples are the same document. Navigation may not have happened, or the URL was captured before the route changed.
- **Missing metrics:** LCP and CLS were not captured for any step, so no Core Web Vitals verdict is possible.
- **HTML is hydrated DOM:** The samples are post-JS DOM, not server HTML. They contain `wf-*-active` classes, injected `truste_*` styles and a runtime-set `--vh`. Findings about markup structure may come from injected scripts, not the server template.

## Accessibility

18 axe violations across 3 steps: 2 critical, 2 serious on Homepage, 2 serious on View Cart, the rest moderate or minor.

**Homepage**
- **Critical:** `aria-required-parent` (ARIA roles used outside their required parent).
- **Critical:** `button-name` (buttons with no accessible text).
- **Serious:** `link-name` (links with no accessible text).
- **Landmarks:**
  - `landmark-no-duplicate-main` (more than one `main`).
  - `landmark-main-is-top-level` (`main` nested inside another landmark).
  - `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` (`aside` nested inside another landmark).
  - `landmark-unique` (landmarks that cannot be told apart).
  - `region` (content outside any landmark).
- **Other:** `heading-order` (skipped heading levels) and `aria-allowed-role` (minor).

**Search "comfort shoes"**
- `aria-allowed-role`, `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`, `landmark-unique` and `region` repeat from the Homepage.
- The shared header and footer template is the likely source.

**View Cart**
- **Serious:** `document-title` (no `<title>`) and `html-has-lang` (no `lang` attribute).
- **Moderate:** `landmark-one-main` and `page-has-heading-one`.
- The Homepage has `<html lang="en">`, so the missing `lang` here points to a different document. This is consistent with the 429 error response for `/cart/`. These four findings should be re-tested against a real cart render.

## SEO

- **View Cart:** No `<title>`, no `<h1>` and no `main` landmark. If that is the real cart template, it should be fixed. A 429 or error body is more likely, and that response should be non-indexable.
- **Homepage:** `<html lang="en">` is present. No issues found in the visible head excerpt, but it was truncated before any `<title>`, meta description or canonical tag. Those could not be checked.
- **Heading order:** `heading-order` on the Homepage affects document outline.

## Performance

- **TTFB:**
  - Homepage is 504ms, which is borderline against the 800ms "good" threshold.
  - Log In and Men category are 415ms. The identical values suggest a carried-over or non-fresh measurement.
  - Steps 4–6 and Search are 28–30ms, and View Cart is 142ms. Those are likely cache hits, SPA transitions or error responses, not full document loads.
- **LCP and CLS:** Not captured, so no data.
- **Third-party load:** The Homepage head loads about 25 async third-party scripts:
  - GTM and gtag (three destinations)
  - TikTok
  - Pinterest
  - Snapchat
  - Reddit
  - Facebook
  - Nextdoor
  - Taboola
  - Yahoo
  - Bing
  - Quantum Metric
  - Monetate
  - Pulse Insights
  - Impact
  - RTB House
  - Bloomreach
  - Tune
  - Stackadapt
- **Font loading:** Fonts load through `webfontloader` from cdnjs, which adds a third-party dependency and a font-swap risk for CLS.
- **bfcache:** The "unload is not allowed" policy violations (4) come from a script registering an `unload` handler. Browsers block this, and it also disables back/forward cache.
- **GTM environment:** The GTM container URL carries `gtm_auth=…&gtm_preview=env-1`. That is a non-default GTM environment snippet running on production, and it should be verified as intentional.

## Mobile Optimization

- **`--vh` workaround:** `--vh: 6.64px` is set inline, which indicates a JS-computed viewport height workaround, though the viewport used for testing is unknown.
- **Pointer events:** A global `click` listener (`js-ignore-click`) blocks link navigation until `app:JsLoaded` fires. Taps before hydration are silently ignored on slow mobile networks.
- No other issues found.

## Console & Network Errors

**Network (6)**
- Five 429s on `/{uuid}/{uuid}/fp?x-kpsdk-v=j-1.2.825`, the Kasada fingerprint endpoint.
- One 429 on `https://www.skechers.com/cart/`.
- The 429 on `/cart/` is a bot-mitigation block, not an application bug. It makes every View Cart result unreliable.

**Console (30 total, 10 shown)**
- 429 and `net::ERR_FAILED` resource loads (matching the Kasada calls).
- "Unsupported Summarizer API languages" (2): a script calls Chrome's built-in Summarizer API without a supported language.
- "Permissions policy violation: unload" (4).

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the audit harness first.** The Kasada 429s mean the cart and later steps are not trustworthy.
   - Use a warmed session and reduce request rate.
   - Add a failure gate that skips axe, SEO and cart findings when the response is 429.
2. **Verify step URLs.** Confirm each step actually navigated, since three steps report `https://www.skechers.com/`.
3. **Capture LCP and CLS reliably.** Register the `PerformanceObserver` before navigation, using `buffered: true`, and store the values per step.
4. **Fix the Homepage axe criticals:**
   - Give `button-name` and `link-name` elements accessible text.
   - Correct the `aria-required-parent` roles.
   - Keep a single top-level `<main>`, and remove or relocate the nested duplicates.
5. **Remove `unload` listeners** (use `pagehide` or `visibilitychange`) so bfcache works. Identify which third-party tag registers them.
6. **Audit the tag load.** Defer or consolidate the ad pixels behind consent and a single tag manager, and confirm the `gtm_preview=env-1` snippet is intentional in production.
7. **Re-run the View Cart check** against a real 200 cart render to confirm whether the missing `<title>`, `lang` and `<h1>` are real.
