# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is leading with their running line here, and they've got a young basketball-type sitting on bleachers for the hero shot — not exactly speaking to me. The discount code up top is nice, and I'm glad to see Slip-ins and Arch Fit right in the nav, but beyond those two quick links I'd have to dig to find anything made for a guy my age looking for something comfortable to wear around town.

## What's working

- **The discount banner:** "SAVE 25% OFF $100+ Code: SAVINGS" is right at the top in big blue letters — I see it the second the page loads, and it gives me a reason to stay.
- **The quick-category nav:** Slip-ins, Arch Fit, BOBs — three of those four links are squarely in my wheelhouse. One tap and I'm where I want to be.
- **"COMFORT THAT PERFORMS" headline:** The word *comfort* registers with me even if the athlete underneath it doesn't look anything like me. At least the brand isn't hiding what it's trying to sell.
- **The Slip-ins shoe peek at the bottom:** That partial product shot in navy and orange actually catches my eye — I want to scroll to see the full shoe.

## What's weak

- **The hero athlete:** "OG Anunogy" and "A Force to Be Reckoned With" — I had to read that twice. I don't know who that is, and seeing a twenty-something with a basketball bag tells me this page wasn't built for me.
- **No loyalty hook at all:** I've bought from Skechers before. I'd expect something like "Members save extra" or a points reminder. Nothing.
- **Skechers x Care Bears:** I get it, collabs sell, but that strip is taking up real estate right under the nav and it's not speaking to a 62-year-old man looking for arch support.
- **No free-shipping info visible:** I almost always check before I add to cart. I don't see it anywhere in this screenshot.
- **No CTA button on the hero:** The main hero image is just sitting there with no button. "Shop Now" lives on the Care Bears strip, not on the thing I'm actually looking at.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or rotate the hero for a lifestyle shot of someone my age:** A 60-something walking on a trail or strolling a farmers market in Slip-ins or Arch Fit would close the demographic gap fast and signal that Skechers isn't just for athletes.
- **Add a loyalty / rewards strip directly under the category nav:** One line — "Skechers Elite Members earn 2x points today" — gives returning shoppers a reason to log in and spend.
- **Put a visible CTA button on the hero:** Even just "Shop Slip-ins" in white on blue, sitting over the bottom corner of that hero image, would give me somewhere obvious to go.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top bar** alongside the discount code — something like "SAVE 25% OFF $100+ • Free shipping on $50+ | Code: SAVINGS" — so I know the deal before I start filling a cart.
