# [Last Chance] Join VIP, get 25% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. This is the same purple "25% OFF*" banner I've seen all week, since "Happy VIP Days" on 10/01. Two things bug me. The subject says "Last Chance" and I got "Last day!" this morning. The banner also says "It only happens twice a year…" Twice a year, and I get it in my inbox every day.
- The offer is clear enough: 25% off regular-price styles with code VIPFUN. But the "BECOME A VIP" button makes it sound like I have to sign up first. The rest is a long scroll of women's loafers, heels, platforms and clogs. I saw a few sneakers and one pair of men's-looking black boots near the bottom. I didn't see anything built for a 62-year-old guy who wants cushioned, slip-on, arch-supported shoes.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd skim, see mostly women's shoes, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. My wife might like the Mary Janes, but I'm not sending her a fifth copy of the same email.

## What's working

- The purple "25% OFF*" banner is big and easy to read on my phone. I know the deal right away.
- The code VIPFUN is printed right in the banner, so I don't have to hunt for it.
- The "SHOP MEN" link sits right under the banner, so I can skip straight to my section.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" row has "SHOP WIDE WIDTH." I have wider feet, so that's a useful touch.
- The icons for free shipping, in-store pickup and the app are simple and clear.

## What's weak

- It's the same banner I've gotten for days ("Happy VIP Days," "2 days left," "Last day!"). The "Last Chance" urgency doesn't ring true anymore.
- The "BECOME A VIP" button makes it unclear whether I'm already a member or have to join first.
- Almost every product shown is women's: loafers, heels, platforms and booties. Nothing is clearly for me.
- No cushioning, arch support, slip-on or walking language anywhere. I can't tell what's comfortable.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and I can't read it without zooming. It also has the exclusions behind that asterisk.
- It's a very long scroll with four or five different "SHOP" buttons. There's no single clear thing to tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same banner every day. Send one VIP reminder and one real last-day email. After that, show different product in each send.
- 2. Put a men's comfort row near the top. Show slip-on walkers and cushioned sneakers, with wide widths and arch support called out.
- 3. Make the button say what happens, like "Shop 25% Off Now." Mention joining only in a short line below it for non-members.
- 4. Cut the page down. Two sections and one clear button would work better than the whole catalog.
- 5. Make the fine print bigger, or link to the full terms.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Comfort picks, 25% off, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Wide widths and cushioned walkers included. Code VIPFUN, regular-price styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: take 25% off men's sneakers and slip-ons in stores and online.`
