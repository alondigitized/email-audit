# Flash Sale: 30% Off Baya Clogs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Well, now this one I can actually use. A plain Baya Clog, one color, 30% off, and a real deadline — Sunday night. It's not a monster card, it's not a leopard flat, it's not a purse. It's a shoe, on sale, at a percentage I can do the math on in my head. That said, I don't know a Baya from a Classic Clog — it's basically the same slip-on I've seen in every one of these emails, just with a different name slapped on this week's discount.
- The pink "SALE" graphic is loud and doesn't add anything — I get it, sale's the point, you already told me that up top. I'd click through mostly out of habit at this point, since Crocs has sent me a shoe I could actually wear five times in the last two weeks. Wouldn't forward it to anybody, but I might actually tap "Shop the Sale" this time just to see what a pair runs after the discount.

## What's working

- One shoe, one color, one percentage off — no charms, no cartoons, no leopard print, no purses.
- "Flash Sale" plus an actual expiration date in the fine print (Sunday, September 27th) gives it real urgency instead of the vague kind.
- Free shipping banner up top is a genuine plus for me — I don't like paying to ship a $30 shoe.
- Big legible "Shop the Sale" button, easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- I still don't know what a "Baya Clog" is versus the other ten clogs they've pitched me — no size range, no arch support or cushioning mention, nothing about fit.
- No price shown anywhere — just "30% off," so I can't do the math without leaving the email.
- The big pink "SALE" graphic eats a lot of scrolling for zero new information.
- "Refer a Friend" and app download blocks come right after, which feels like they're trying to upsell me before I've even bought anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price next to the 30% off — "$44.99, was $64.99" does more work than a percentage alone for someone like me deciding if it's worth the trip.
- 2. Say something about comfort or fit — arch support, cushioned footbed, true to size — since that's the whole reason I'd pick Crocs over just buying whatever's on the DSW clearance rack.
- 3. Cut the giant pink "SALE" text graphic in half or drop it — it's pure decoration with no product info.
- 4. Move "Refer a Friend" and app download further down, after order-related links, so the flow stays focused on the sale itself.
