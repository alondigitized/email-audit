# Last chance to join VIP for first dibs!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count. Since the 20th I've gotten "Become a VIP to shop 30% off first!", "Save 30% as a VIP (Sign up for free!)", "There's still time!" and "Last day to join VIP…" this morning. Now it's "Last chance." Same pink Black Friday banner and the same giant black "30% OFF*." Whoever is scheduling these at DSW isn't counting. The "last" in "last chance" has now lost all meaning for me.
- The banner at the top does say "The season's biggest deals are on," and the page is clean enough. The 30 is big enough for my eyes without squinting. But I'm not a "First dibs" kind of guy, and the tote bag and the sneaker grid don't make me feel like DSW knows me. Would I click? Probably only to check whether the 30% covers a pair of walking shoes. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The giant "30% OFF*" is readable on my phone without zooming.
- The "SHOP WIDE WIDTH" button is a nice nod. Wide feet are a real thing at my age.
- "SHOP MEN" is a visible link right under the hero, so I can skip the women's stuff.
- The "SHOP BY SIZE" block is simple, with big tap targets and no fuss.
- The "FREE SHIPPING FOR VIPs" and in-store pickup icons are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- I've seen this same creative about seven times in three days. It's wearing out my goodwill.
- The "Last chance" subject doesn't match what I actually see, which is a sale that's running all week. I don't trust it.
- The "SO NEW, SO YOU" product grid is mostly women's boots and trendy sneakers. There's nothing cushioned, nothing slip-on, and no arch-support talk for a guy like me.
- The asterisks are everywhere. "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" plus a wall of tiny fine print makes me wonder what's excluded. Skechers is in the exclusion list, and that's a brand I buy.
- The "BECOME A VIP" button is the main ask, but the subject promised first dibs. The page says "Black Friday starts… NOW," which contradicts it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same email twice a day. Send one VIP pitch, then follow up only with something new.
- 2. Show comfort and walking shoes for men, such as Hoka, Brooks and New Balance, with a line like "Cushioned all-day walkers, 30% off."
- 3. Put the exclusions in plain words near the offer. If the brands I want are excluded, tell me up front.
- 4. Make the subject match the page. Say what the VIP perk actually is.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday 30% off starts now (code inside)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Walking shoes 30% off: Hoka, Brooks, New Balance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code BFDEALS in stores and online through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wide widths and all-day cushioning, plus a free tote at $75.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has two confirmed defects: a 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA and an unrendered `%%gender%%` merge token. The 50% QA pass rate counts only 4 checks, and the HTML was truncated, so a few categories can't be fully assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (400):** the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link (`https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/Q7Ztvescko8NOBFXgLm5YQ~~/AAQRxRA~/FBMbsy…`) returned HTTP 400 when probed. The QA record shows an empty link label before the link text, so the anchor markup should be checked.
  - A 400 from a Braze/SendGrid `ablink` click-tracking URL can come from the probe (headers, missing user context). It can also come from a malformed or expired token. This needs a manual click-through before it is treated as a live outage.
  - If it is real, it breaks the highest-value conversion path in the email: the 15% SMS offer.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 77 links were probed and 65 were skipped. The other links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261122_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` carries a per-recipient `mi_u` ID. That is expected for an open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/mQwqbZ6HDdvhP6mlmY1q3g~~/AAQRxRA~/LtQG7UdkSz…` is a linked, tracked image with no alt.
  - `o.gif` is the tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Charset and compatibility meta tags are inert:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither declares anything. The charset is then declared only by the HTTP/MIME headers. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` respectively.
- **Plain-text version:** 27,782 of 33,548 characters (83%) are URLs, which trips the plain-text WARN. The cause is long tracked links such as the one above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL: raw token `%%gender%%` is present in the delivered message.** This is an unresolved Marketo/Salesforce-style token. DSW sends through Braze, which uses Liquid (`{{ }}`).
  - It is likely an unrendered conditional or segmentation variable. If it sits in a URL or attribute, it may also explain the 400 above.
  - The truncated HTML doesn't show its location, so the exact placement is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found:** this is attributed to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown, not failing. No sender-side issue is confirmed.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the QA data lists no failure, and the probe always includes the unsubscribe link. The footer was cut off in the truncated source, so it can't be verified here. No issues confirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers:** not provided in the input, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing URLs can't be seen from the source. Only the 400 CTA was resolved, and it never reached a landing page.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261122_P261708_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` in the pixel URL.
- UTM and landing-page alignment is unverifiable from this data, with no issues confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click the SMS opt-in CTA from a real client. If it returns 400, fix the Braze link or its token. If it works, tune the QA prober's request headers.
2. Find where `%%gender%%` appears in the source. Convert it to the platform's Liquid syntax with a default, or remove it.
3. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`, and a descriptive alt to the `ablink.dsw.com/q/…` image.
4. Add `http-equiv` to the charset and IE compatibility meta tags.
5. Probe the remaining 65 links, or at least all repeated CTAs, to rule out more broken tracking links.
6. Capture the raw headers (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) from the original message, bypassing the relay, to complete the compliance check.
