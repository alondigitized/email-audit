## Executive summary

- Two collabs archives ago I'd have stopped reading, but this one's got a real hero graphic and a real number — "up to 30% off" right at the top — so at least it doesn't waste my time hunting for the offer. Then I scroll down and it's the same back-to-school kids-with-a-bike-and-a-basketball shot I've already seen twice this month from this exact brand, now bolted onto a completely different sale with its own "up to 40% off." Two offers, two audiences, one email — that's not a sale, that's a stapled-together newsletter.
- Nothing in here — not the "Collaborations Archive" grunge graphic, not the back-to-school teens — has a thing to do with a guy my age looking for a slip-on with arch support. I don't know what a "collaborations archive" even is, and nobody bothered to tell me.
- I'd mark this one read and move on. Not annoying enough to unsubscribe over, but nothing here makes me want to tap "Shop Now" either.

## What's working

- The top banner gives me a real number — "Up to 30% off" — before I even have to scroll, so I know what I'm dealing with fast.
- Clean black-and-white "Shop Now" bar up top is easy to spot and easy to tap.
- No broken images or garbled text — everything rendered the way it should.

## What's weak

- Two unrelated sales crammed into one email — "Collaborations Archive" (30% off) and "Back to School Sale" (40% off) — makes it feel like a dumping ground rather than one clear offer.
- "Collaborations Archive" means nothing to me — no product shown, no shoe, no explanation of what's actually being sold.
- Back-to-school photo again is teenagers with a bike and a backpack, same beat as three other adidas sends I've gotten this month — nothing for a 62-year-old.
- Not one shoe pictured anywhere in this email. For a footwear brand that's a strange miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two separate, focused sends — one offer per email, one clear hero product per offer. Cramming two sales together dilutes both.
- 2. Show an actual shoe. If there's a men's comfort or walking style in either sale, put it front and center — that's the only thing that gets me to tap.
- 3. Explain "Collaborations Archive" in plain English — what is it, why should I care, who's it for.
- 4. Stop reusing the same back-to-school teens-on-a-bike photo — I've seen it enough times to tune it out completely.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Limited release: adidas Collabs Archive, 30% off`
