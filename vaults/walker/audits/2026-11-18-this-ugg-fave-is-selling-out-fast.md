# This UGG fave is selling out fast 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and UGG again. By my count that's the twelfth email in about a week. Yesterday's "Up to 30% off UGG is on!" and "Up to 30% off Nike (!!)" came before this one. This time there's a real price at the top: "NOW ONLY $89.96" on the classic slipper. That helps, because I can see what I'd pay. But the screen is mostly pink UGG boots and gift tags for her, him and the kids. Where's my comfort shoe?
- The subject says "selling out fast," and the body does back that up with "Selling fast in stores and online." It's pushy, though, and I've seen this pitch all week. I might tap the slipper because slip-ons are my thing. But the rest is gift-wrapped boots and a girl in a hat. I'd skim it, maybe click once, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The "NOW ONLY $89.96" price is large and easy to read on my phone without glasses.
- The slipper in the top banner is a slip-on, and that's what I wear. It's the one thing here that fits me.
- The "FOR HIM" tag with a "SHOP MEN'S UGG" link lets me skip straight to my section.
- The banner offers 500 bonus points on UGG and Birkenstock, which is a nice touch. Birkenstock is a comfort brand I'd consider.
- The size buttons and "Shop Wide Width" are practical for someone like me.

## What's weak

- Nothing here says cushioning, arch support or all-day walking comfort. It's all fashion and gifts.
- The "Early Black Friday Sale" tag has no end date, so the urgency feels hollow.
- The "FOR HER" and "FOR KIDS" blocks take up most of the scroll. I had to go a long way down to reach anything for men.
- "More crowd-pleasers" at the bottom finally shows New Balance and Puma, but only as tiny, unpriced pictures. That's the comparison shopping I'd actually do, and it's buried.
- The fine print is tiny and hard to read on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one men's comfort slip-on and its price, and put "Shop men's slippers" right under it. Skip the gift-tag blocks for people like me.
- 2. Show New Balance and Hoka comfort styles higher up, with prices.
- 3. Add a real end date to the sale, like "Ends Sunday."
- 4. Mention comfort features such as cushioned footbed, easy on and off, and arch support.
- 5. Make the small text and links bigger so I can tap them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `UGG slippers, $89.96 — easy on, easy off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Men's UGG slippers at $89.96 this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned comfort for him, plus 500 bonus VIP points`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Sunday. Men's slip-ons from $89.96`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken CTA (HTTP 400 on the SMS opt-in link) and a 62% QA pass rate. Its `<meta>` declarations are malformed, and SPF/DKIM could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the source and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken link (400)** on the CTA "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off":
  `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/k-oIqVLptRaAoSo3aV6aCQ~~/AAQRxRA~/T7w0wOqD…` (Braze click-tracking wrapper). The QA report also lists an empty link-text field next to this URL.
  - A 400 from the tracker could mean a malformed or expired token, or the tracker rejecting the QA probe. The data doesn't distinguish these, so verify manually in a real client.
  - If it fails for real subscribers, it breaks the email's primary SMS-acquisition CTA.
- Only 12 of 57 links were probed and 45 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. The unsubscribe link was always included in the probe and raised no failure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Ineffective charset declaration:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` attribute, so it doesn't declare an encoding. The same applies to `<meta content="IE=Edge" />`, which is missing `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`. Clients that rely on the meta tag could mis-render the emoji in the subject and body.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - The image at `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/4OLi7WToApjllwAommqzQQ~~/…` has no alt attribute. If it is content or a CTA, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
  - The `o.gif` tracking pixel (`https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…`) has no alt attribute. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, since it is decorative.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>DSW</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.
- **Dark mode:** the `.dm-bg-white { background: #ffffff !important; }` class is identical to the light-mode value, so it doesn't invert. Treat this as a possible issue only, because the source is truncated and I can't see where the class is used.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping the header, so it is a tooling gap, not confirmed non-compliance. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` from raw headers.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is 85% URLs** (19,205 of 22,553 chars). The very long Braze tracking URLs dominate it, which hurts the plain-text version's readability and is a mild spam-filter signal.
- The unsubscribe link was probed without failure. The footer is truncated, so I couldn't confirm the physical postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.

- The one CTA probed on this point (the SMS opt-in) returns a 400, so it never reaches a landing page.
