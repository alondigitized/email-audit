## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clean, consistent UTM structure on every tracked link, but it ships with no plain-text part, no alt text on 16 images, and several markup defects in the head and CSS. The 12 probed links all failed (403/406), but this is most likely the ESP click tracker rejecting the automated probe, not a confirmed dead link.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed.** 11 returned 406 and the unsubscribe link returned 403. All are Cordial click-tracking redirects on `e.emails.hoka.com/c2/1664:...:1/<id>?jwtH=...&jwtP=...&jwtS=...`. Every link failed the same way, regardless of destination. That points to bot or User-Agent filtering on the tracker, not a broken destination. I can't confirm real-world click failure from this data. A re-probe with a browser-like User-Agent would settle it.
- **Malformed product parameter.** The women's Speedgoat 7 Hike GTX link (`/70fc3d7b`) decodes to `https://www.hoka.com/en/us/women-hiking/speedgoat-7-hike-gtx/1176671.html?dwvar_1176671&utm_...`. `dwvar_1176671` has no `_color=` suffix and no value, unlike the other product links (for example `dwvar_1176677_color=SRYG`). It will probably land on the default variant instead of the pictured colorway (`SZL`).
- **Inconsistent category slugs.** Product paths use `women-hiking`, `mens-trail-hiking-shoes`, `womens-trail` and `mens-trail`. Some of these may be redirects or non-canonical paths.
- **Link expiry (unverified).** The JWT payload contains `"ce":86400`. If that is a link lifetime, tracked links could stop working after 24 hours. I couldn't confirm the meaning from the source.
- **Coverage gap.** 16 links were not probed, including the footer and social links, so they are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** 16 images have no alt attribute. These are `US_20261021_SpeedgoatFamily_a/b/c/d/e.jpg`, the six Speedgoat 7 product tiles (`..._a.jpg`, `..._b.jpg`, `..._MiddleLeft_1176677-SRYG.jpg`, `..._MiddleRight_1176674-SHCL.jpg`, `..._BottomRight_1171929-ARMR.jpg`, `..._BottomRight_1171928-ALVB.jpg`), `US_Q3_ShoeFinder_Blue.jpg` and `LogoFooterPrimary.png`. Two tracking pixels (`e.emails.hoka.com/o/p/...` and `mg.emails.hoka.com/o/...`) also lack alt, but pixels are harmless. Because the alt is missing, every image link reports its filename as link text (for example `[image: US_Speedgoat7GTX_MiddleLeft_1176677-SRYG]`). With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the email has no readable content or CTAs.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>`.
- **Invalid charset meta:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it doesn't declare the charset.
- **Duplicate viewport meta tags** with conflicting values: `maximum-scale=1` in one and `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=yes` in the other. `maximum-scale=1` can block pinch-zoom on some clients.
- **Invalid CSS:** `.nav_padding {padding-top:px !important;padding-bottom:px !important;}` and `.nav_border {border-top:px solid ;}` have empty values and are dropped by parsers.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` rules load `TheFutureHOKA` from `www.hoka.com/on/demandware.static/...`. Most clients ignore them and fall back, which is normal, but make sure the fallback stack is defined. The truncated source doesn't show it.
- The source was truncated, so table structure and Outlook conditionals weren't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. Tracked URLs carry per-recipient `hmail=` and `cordial_id=` values, and they were populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing.** The QA check reports a text version of 0 chars. This is a deliverability and accessibility problem, and spam filters can penalize HTML-only mail.
- **Unsubscribe link present** in the HTML (`options.hoka.com/unsubscribe?cid=...`). It returned 403 to the probe, which is probably the same bot filtering as above. Verify it manually.
- **Unsubscribe URL carries UTM parameters** and `hmail=`. This is harmless but unnecessary.
- **Not verifiable from the data:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC, because `Authentication-Results` is absent (the QA tool notes the AgentMail relay), and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058 one-click, required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules). The postal address isn't visible in the truncated source either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are consistent on every decoded link: `utm_source=US_Broadcast`, `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=H_US_BR_20261021_SpeedgoatFamily_STO_A60Day_..._AIRFMMedium`.
- No `utm_content` or `utm_term`, so clicks on the hero, product tiles and shoe-finder can't be told apart in analytics.
- `utm_source=US_Broadcast` is a send-type label, not a source name. This is a reporting-hygiene point, not a failure.
- Destinations match the campaign: `/en/us/speedgoat/` for the hero tiles, PDPs for the product tiles, and `/en/us/return-start/` for the returns link.
- The malformed `dwvar_1176671` parameter in section 2 is the one continuity defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the tracked links with a browser-like User-Agent to confirm the 403/406 responses are bot filtering, and manually click-test the unsubscribe link.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 16 images, with `alt=""` on the tracking pixels. Alt text on the hero and product tiles is the priority, because they carry the CTAs.
3. Add a multipart plain-text alternative.
4. Fix `dwvar_1176671` by adding `_color=SZL`, and normalize the category slugs.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from a raw-header capture, since this pipeline can't see them.
6. Clean the head: fill in `<title>`, change the charset meta to `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, merge the two viewport tags and drop `maximum-scale=1`, and remove or complete the empty-value CSS rules.
7. Add `utm_content` per module, and drop the UTMs from the unsubscribe link.
