# A journey that will last forever 🇦🇷🩵
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, this one caught me off guard — it's a tribute email for Messi, thanking someone named "Leo" in Spanish, "Gracias por este viaje, Leo." I'm guessing Messi's retiring or leaving a team and adidas is running some kind of send-off. Problem is, I'm not Leo, I don't speak Spanish, and I have no idea what "journey" they're even talking about. There's a "Shop Now" banner at the top, but then the whole rest of the email is just this moody photo of Messi in two different jerseys and a big headline — no product, no price, nothing for me to actually buy.
- This is the least relevant thing adidas has sent me yet, and that's saying something after all the skateboarders and K-pop stars. At least those had a shoe attached to them. This is pure sports-fan sentimentality for soccer diehards, and I don't follow soccer. I'd mark this as read and move right past it — nothing here to click, nothing to forward.

## What's working

- The black-and-white photography is striking, I'll give them that — good production value.
- The "Shop Now" banner at the very top at least gives me one way to get to the site if I wanted to.
- Clean layout, no clutter, easy to scroll through on my phone.

## What's weak

- The whole email is in Spanish ("Gracias por este viaje, Leo") with no English translation anywhere — I don't know who Leo is or why I'm thanking him.
- There's no shoe, no product, no offer, no price anywhere in this email — it's just a tribute photo and a headline.
- Zero relevance to my life — I'm not a soccer fan, not "Leo," and there's nothing here about comfort, cushioning, or casual shoes.
- The email looks unfinished — there's a big blank gap between "Gracias por este viaje, Leo" and the footer links, like content is missing.
- No CTA at the bottom near the message itself — just the top banner, which feels disconnected from the tribute content.

## Recommendations

- 1. If this is going out to general subscribers like me, either don't send it, or pair it with an actual product tie-in — a retro Messi-inspired shoe or apparel line I could actually buy.
- 2. Translate or at minimum caption the Spanish text so non-Spanish-speaking subscribers understand the context.
- 3. Fix that blank gap in the body — it looks broken, like a section didn't load.
- 4. Add a real CTA near the image, not just a banner up top disconnected from the story.
- 5. Segment this — send it to soccer/Messi fans who'd actually care, not blanket-blast it to someone shopping for cushioned walking shoes.
