# Black Friday is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's New Balance again, and this time it's Black Friday itself. The top of the email is a box of red ornaments with the NB logo, and that's the whole hero. It looks nice, but it's a Christmas decoration, not a shoe. I'm a 62-year-old man who wants cushioned walking shoes, and I had to scroll a long way before I saw any footwear.
- The offer is mixed. The preview says 40% off regular-priced and 50% off clearance. The hero says "Up to 40% off select styles." The banner says free shipping on all orders. Which is it? The fine print under the hero says exclusions apply, and the dates run November 19 through December 28. That's not much of a "here" or a deadline. I've now had a Black Friday preview, a holiday list and "top styles" this week, so this feels like the same sale in a new coat.
- Would I click? Maybe, because the WX608v5 is a shoe I'd actually look at. I wouldn't forward it, though. I'd skim it and move on.

## What's working

- The "Free shipping on all orders" line at the top is plain and easy to read.
- The price tiles (Under $50, $50–$100, Over $100) fit how I shop. I do the price-versus-quality math.
- The grid has some shoes I'd consider. The white WX608v5 and the black Fresh Foam 680v9 look like everyday walking shoes.
- "Walking" is in the footer links next to Running and Lifestyle, so I can find my category.
- The text is big and the buttons are wide, so they're easy to tap on my phone.

## What's weak

- The hero is ornaments, not shoes. The offer is above the fold, but nothing I'd buy is.
- The discount message is muddled. "40% off," "up to 40% off select styles," "50% off clearance" and "exclusions apply" don't add up to one clear deal.
- The shoe grid shows no prices, no sale prices and no cushioning or arch-support details. I can't do my math from this email.
- Some picks don't fit me. A kids' bungee-lace Rave Run, a neon-green 9060R and a red Rave Run v2 are all aimed at other people.
- It's the same sale I've seen all week, including the Nov 26 "Black Friday preview" sends. "Black Friday is here" lands flat after that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a men's walking shoe in the hero with a real price, like "Was $89, now $53," instead of the ornaments.
- 2. Pick one offer and say it once. Either "40% off" or "up to 40% off select styles," not both.
- 3. Add sale prices and a comfort tag under each shoe, like "Cushioned" or "Arch support."
- 4. Add a men's walking row and drop the kids' shoe.
- 5. Give the sale a real end date up top instead of November 19 through December 28 in small print.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh Foam and 608 walkers, now 40% off. Free shipping on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by price: under $50, $50–$100, over $100. Sale ends Dec 1.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: New Balance, "Black Friday is here"

**Scope note:** The HTML source was truncated, so I could only check the `<head>`/CSS and the QA findings. I didn't see the body, footer, anchors or headers. Anything I couldn't check is marked "not verifiable" rather than called clean.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no failures in the automated QA run (0 issues, 2 warnings), and none of the 12 probed links were reported broken. It does have three images without alt text, a plain-text part that is almost entirely URLs, and leftover template artifacts in the CSS and `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 25 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). The 25 skipped links are not verified.
- **Plain-text alternative is bloated.** 13,067 of 14,845 characters (about 88%) are URLs. This fits long redirect-wrapped tracking links, and it can raise spam-filter scoring and makes the text part hard to read.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://link.email.newbalance.com/mo/egR1f966kc_818512772_12751552_74406_24081326.gif` is an open-tracking pixel. See §3 for its missing alt.
- **Placeholder asset URL in CSS:** `.bgwhite {background-image: url('http://image.XXXXX.png');}`. It is an unresolved placeholder over insecure HTTP. It will 404 or be flagged as mixed content if `.bgwhite` is applied anywhere in the body.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 3 images):**
  - `ShippingIcons_06.png` (`.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingIcons_06.png`)
  - `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` (`.../myNB_Welcome/UpdatedImages/ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`)
  - `egR1f966kc_..._24081326.gif`, the tracking pixel
- **Shipping banner and icons:** if they carry text such as shipping thresholds, the missing alt hides that information from screen readers and from clients that block images. If they're decorative, they need `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixel:** it should have `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **`<meta content="IE=edge" />`:** it has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
- **`@font-face` blocks have no `src`:** examples are `proxima-nova`, `linotype-didot` and `ITC Garamond Book Condensed`. They can't load any font, so clients fall back to Arial or Times New Roman. If the web fonts are intended, they aren't being delivered.
- **Duplicate `.bg1` rule:** it is defined as `#ffffff`, then redefined as `#58546d`. The later declaration wins, which may be unintended.
- **Doctype mismatch:** the HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is paired with an `xmlns` attribute and self-closed `<meta ... />` tags. Most clients tolerate this.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` meta tags, `:root` and `prefers-color-scheme` are present. Dark-mode banner assets exist (`ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png`). The dark-mode rule `a {color:#cf0a2c !important;}` overrides all link colors, including links on dark backgrounds (not verified against the body).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The `<head>` contains no unresolved merge tokens. The only unresolved placeholder is the `XXXXX` asset URL noted in §2. Body-level tokens could not be checked because the source was truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **Authentication:** the `Authentication-Results` header was not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. It is not a confirmed sender defect. Verify against the raw message at the final recipient.
- **Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe headers:** not verifiable. QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing and reported no issue. The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not shown.
- **Physical postal address:** not verifiable because the footer was truncated.
- **Template metadata leak:** `<title>New Balance Template</title>` is an unedited template title. It shows in some clients and in browser-view tabs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All wrapped links appear to route through the `link.email.newbalance.com` redirector, and the final destination URLs were not exposed in the captured data. **UTM parameters and landing page alignment are not verifiable** from this input.
- The shipping assets come from a `myNB_Welcome` path. This is a reuse of the welcome-series template in a promotional send. Confirm the shipping messaging is still accurate for the Black Friday context.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `ShippingBanner_DarkMode_03.png` and `ShippingIcons_06.png`, and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixel.
2. Shorten or restructure the plain-text part. Use fewer or shorter links, and include readable copy rather than mostly tracking URLs.
3. Remove the `http://image.XXXXX.png` placeholder from `.bgwhite`. Add a `src` to the `@font-face` rules or remove them.
4. Replace the `<title>`, fix the `IE=edge` meta to `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, and remove the duplicate `.bg1` rule.
5. Capture the full raw message (headers plus the complete HTML) to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, and the final UTM parameters on destination URLs. This audit couldn't confirm those.
