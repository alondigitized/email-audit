# Your cart is one click away! Cyber Week deals won't last
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Ninth cart nag since 11/20. I rolled my eyes at the subject, then saw the hero shoe and sat up. This time it's a men's-looking slip-in, the GO WALK Glide-Step 2.0 Cozy Fit Walker, in taupe and black. It isn't that women's black Contour Foam shoe with the rose-gold eyelets I've been shown all week and never touched. That's the first time one of these "cart" emails looked like it was meant for a 62-year-old guy.
- The black banner at the top, "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE," does the heavy lifting. It's the same deal as yesterday's "Our biggest sale of the year is now live!" The "Let's make it official" headline under it is cute but wastes a screen. I'd click, mostly for the Glide-Step. I wouldn't forward it, because it's the same Cyber Week email I've already seen five times.

## What's working

- The hero is a hands-free slip-in with a cushioned sole. That's what I shop for, and it finally isn't a women's shoe.
- The black "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner is huge and readable on my phone.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" tells me I don't have to hunt for a code.
- The red Skechers Plus box says "Extra 15% off everything." That's a real member perk, and it's easy to see.
- The "Shop Additional Styles" list (Women, Men, Kids…) is big, tappable text. My thumbs like it.

## What's weak

- The grid of "more great options" is mostly women's shoes. Four of the six are women's boots or sneakers, and one tile is a bottle of waterproofer spray. Only the Garlan men's shoe is for me.
- The subject says "Cyber Week deals won't last," but there's no actual deadline anywhere. The fine print runs to 12/01, and you have to squint to find it.
- "Complete your order" is a bit pushy, and I never put that shoe in a cart. It feels like they're guessing.
- The product captions are tiny. I'd have to zoom to read them.
- The fine print is a wall of text. Some of it is useful ("30% off select shoes, 40% boots/slippers, 50% apparel"), but it's buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill the grid with men's slip-ins and walking shoes, and drop the spray. Show a price or a "from $X" under each shoe.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the banner, like "Ends 12/1," so "won't last" is believable.
- 3. Show the actual cart item with its size, and say so if it's low in stock. If the shoe isn't really my cart, don't call it my cart.
- 4. Say what the percentages apply to in plain words up top: "30% off shoes, 40% off boots."
- 5. Make the product captions bigger.
