# Want 20% off?? Become a VIP >>>
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Another "join our club" email, and once again DSW's showing me a wall of women's shoes — clogs, ankle booties, wedge sneakers, mary janes. Not one pair I'd put on my own feet. The big blue "20% OFF" is bold enough I can't miss it, I'll give it that, but the fine print underneath says regular-price only, and by the time I get to "SO NEW, SO YOU" it's clear this isn't new for me at all.
- The "become a VIP" pitch is fine in theory — I don't mind a rewards program — but they're asking me to sign up for exclusive access to inventory I wouldn't buy anyway. And this is what, the fourth or fifth DSW email this week? I remember the BOGO 50% blitz from Tuesday through Friday, then the boots-and-heels "From The Brown Up" one, now this. It's a lot of noise for one retailer in my inbox.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding. Straight to "mark as read" and probably time to think about unsubscribing if this keeps up.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" hero number is huge and easy to read without my readers on.
- "SHOP MEN" is at least present as a nav link up top, even if nothing below uses it.
- No BOGO carnival barking this time — headline is a clean percentage, not stacked exclamation points.
- "Shop by Size" grid is a nice idea in general — I like when a retailer makes sizing easy to jump to.

## What's weak

- Every single product tile — clogs, booties, wedge sneaker, mary janes — is a women's shoe. Nothing cushioned, nothing that reads "comfort," nothing I'd wear to walk the dog.
- The "Shop by Size" numbers (5 through 11) are women's sizing, not men's — another sign this whole email was built for someone else and sent to me anyway.
- Dark background with small white/gray text in the footer and details section is tough to read on my phone.
- "CAN YOU KEEP A SECRET??" with the exclamation mark graphic feels like it's talking to somebody younger — not really my speed.
- Sign-up friction: I have to "become a VIP" before I even get the discount, and the fine print buries "valid September 10-13" — box store has too many conditions for a shoe I don't want anyway.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment by past purchase or browsing history — if I've ever clicked men's or comfort-brand shoes, don't send me an email that's 100% women's product. This is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Swap at least the top row of product tiles for men's casual or walking styles — a slip-on, a cushioned sneaker, something in a Skechers/Hoka lane — so the VIP offer actually has something for me to redeem it on.
- 3. Lead the fine print higher — tell me up front it's "regular-price only" and the exact dates, not buried five screens down.
- 4. Cut back the send frequency — I've gotten close to daily DSW email this week; space it out so each one feels like news, not noise.
- 5. If sizing grid stays, add a men's size row or a toggle, since right now it silently assumes women's sizing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your VIP discount is ready — 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals in men's casual, extra 20% off for members`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign up free, save 20% on regular-price styles`
