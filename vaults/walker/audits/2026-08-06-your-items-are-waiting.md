## What's weak

- Two of three "waiting" cart items are Care Bears kids' shoes — one covered in cartoon bears, one hot pink — I never touched either one.
- This is the same recycled cart from Monday's "cart expires soon" email, just with a new headline slapped on top.
- The big offer at the bottom — 25% off kids' back-to-school styles — has nothing to do with me or my actual cart item.
