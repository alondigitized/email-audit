# Friday Deals: Up to 70% OFF
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- Shoe Carnival again. I've lost count this week. Since the 13th I've had the same $10 / $20 / $30 off coupon in my inbox, and here it is once more with a Black Friday label on it. The "Up to 70% OFF" banner at the top is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. The "up to" is doing a lot of work, though. When I scroll down I find boots at $19.98, Nike at 30%, and Skechers at "up to 30%." Nothing in there gets near 70%.
- The Skechers box is the one that caught my eye, because Skechers is what I wear. It's squeezed into the bottom corner next to Clogs, and it says "up to 30%" while Tuesday's email said 25% off the entire Skechers stock. This is a long email, and the shoes I'd actually buy are buried in it. I'd skim it, maybe tap the Skechers link, and not forward it to anybody.

## What's working

- The big red "70% OFF" banner is readable at a glance, even with my glasses off.
- The $10 / $20 / $30 coupon ladder is simple to follow, and the code SAVEMORE is clear.
- The barcodes for the coupons are handy if I'm standing in the store.
- Skechers and Hoka-type comfort brands get their own box, so I can find Skechers without hunting.
- The Shoe Carnival logo and the red and gold look match what I've seen from them all week, so I trust it's the real sender.

## What's weak

- The 70% headline doesn't match anything below it. The best deals I can see are 50% off clogs and 30% off Skechers, which feels like bait.
- The Skechers box sits in the bottom corner, small and half-width, and says only "up to 30%." There's no shoe in it, no slip-on, no arch support.
- The email is very long. I scrolled and scrolled, and the tap links ("Shop Boots," "Shop Clogs") are small text, hard for my thumbs.
- The pictures show women's and kids' boots, Nike/Jordan, and Family Athletics. I see no men's walking or casual shoes at all.
- The fine print under the coupons is tiny and has a long list of excluded brands. I can't read it without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the headline honest. Lead with the real best deal, or show which brand gets 70%.
- 2. Give Skechers a full-width spot near the top with an actual shoe in it. Show a men's slip-on or walking shoe with the real discount.
- 3. Cut the length. Four or five tiles is plenty, and send me only the categories I actually click.
- 4. Make the "Shop" links big buttons that are easy to tap.
- 5. Put the exclusions list in plain, larger text, or link to it, so the offer feels honest.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Men's Skechers walking shoes, 30% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10, $20, $30 off, code SAVEMORE, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your coupon on cushioned comfort shoes. In store and online through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skechers up to 30% off, plus clogs at 50% off. Weekend only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible head and CSS are standard, and the QA run shows 0 failures and 1 warning. Two tracking pixels have problems, though: one has a malformed URL and the other has an unresolved merge token. Compliance and link integrity can't be confirmed, because the HTML is truncated and the 28 tracking links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved merge token in a pixel URL.** `https://0di86k1v.emltrk.com/v2/0di86k1v?d=[UNIQUE]` still contains the literal `[UNIQUE]`. The per-recipient ID was never substituted, so opens from this pixel can't be attributed to a recipient.
- **Malformed pixel URL.** `https://ieezzy5m.emltrk.com/v2/ieezzy5m?i=72180589&t=utm_campaign=SCA_20261120_friday_deals_AM​` has two problems:
  - It nests a UTM key inside another parameter (`t=utm_campaign=…`) instead of using `&utm_campaign=…`, so parsers will read `t` as `utm_campaign=SCA_…`.
  - The QA output shows a trailing invisible character (likely U+200B zero-width space) after `_AM`. That can corrupt the campaign value or cause a mismatch on the tracking server.
- **28 tracking links not verified.** All were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pixels lack alt attributes.** Both emltrk.com images are flagged. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. This is low severity.
- **Link styling removed globally.** `a, a:link, a:visited, … { text-decoration:none !important; color:inherit !important; }` strips underline and color from every link. Any text link not otherwise styled (for example with a button wrapper) has no visual affordance. This is an accessibility risk, not a confirmed failure.
- **Invalid meta tags.** `<meta content="IE=edge" />` has no `http-equiv`, and `<meta content="text/html;" charset="utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Both are ignored, so charset still works through the `charset` attribute. This is low severity.
- **Doctype mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, but `<html>` carries `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the meta tags use XHTML-style self-closing. This is harmless in practice.
- **Inconsistent breakpoints.** The email uses 450px, 600px and 660px media queries, plus a `.responsive-text` rule at 600/601px. This is likely fine, but it could cause odd layouts between 600px and 660px.
- **Not verifiable.** Alt text on content images, the preheader and the layout tables are beyond the truncation point.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unrendered token confirmed:** `?d=[UNIQUE]` (see section 2).
- No other tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** The QA check says this is expected through the AgentMail relay, so it is not evidence of an SPF/DKIM failure. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `email.shoecarnival.com` remain unverified.
- **Unsubscribe link, physical address and `List-Unsubscribe` header can't be assessed.** They fall past the truncation and the headers weren't supplied. No violation is asserted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The campaign identifier `SCA_20261120_friday_deals_AM` is present, but it is misplaced inside the malformed pixel URL (see section 2).
- Whether the 28 click-through URLs carry UTMs, and where they land, can't be confirmed because the HTTP probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ESP template or merge so `d=[UNIQUE]` is substituted at send time. Add a pre-send check that fails on any literal `[…]` or `%%…%%` token in a URL.
2. Rewrite the first pixel as `…?i=72180589&utm_campaign=SCA_20261120_friday_deals_AM` and strip the zero-width space.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Keep an underline or distinct color on inline text links, or confirm every link sits in a styled button.
5. Fix the meta tags with `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` and `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
6. Re-run QA against the full HTML and the raw headers. Resolve the redirect chains for the 28 skipped links and capture `Authentication-Results` and `List-Unsubscribe` from the original message, not the relayed copy.
