# Up to 40% Off Select Styles
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- HOKA's back in my inbox and — I hate to say it — I'm getting a little numb. I open this and the big "Up to 40% Off Shoes" hits me right away, which is fine, I like a clear offer. The Clifton 10 is right there in a product card and I know that shoe, so there's a moment where I think, okay, maybe there's something worth checking. But then I look at yesterday's email — nearly identical subject line, same percentage, same general pitch — and I start to wonder if they're just copy-pasting and hoping I'll click eventually.
- The layout is clean and the text is large enough that I'm not squinting on my phone. The "Shop Sale" button is right there, no mystery. I'll give them that. But the first shoe they show me is the women's Clifton 10 in pink, and then the men's grey one follows after. I'm a 62-year-old guy — lead with me, not as an afterthought. The bottom section about signing up for texts for free shipping on a full-price order is completely irrelevant when I'm already in a sale email.
- I'd probably tap "Shop Sale" quickly just to see if the men's Clifton 10 is in there at a good price — but I'm not forwarding this to anybody, and if the next send is another variation of "40% off," I'm going to start hitting unsubscribe.

## What's working

- **The offer reads instantly.** "Up to 40% Off" in giant text, no hunting around — I got the point before I even scrolled.
- **The Clifton 10 Men's is visible.** That's a shoe I actually track; seeing it by name in a product card gives me a real anchor.
- **"Shop Sale" is clean.** One button, clear label, nothing clever — that's how I like it.
- **No visual clutter or broken images.** The email rendered fine on my phone, text was readable without zooming.
- **"Summer Kickoff Sale" framing works.** Seasonally on-point; feels timely, not like leftover inventory copy.

## What's weak

- **Women's shoe leads the product section.** The pink Clifton 10 Women's appears first; I'm a male buyer — swap the order and make me feel like the primary audience.
- **No comfort or fit language anywhere.** Not one word about cushioning, arch support, or walkability — the only things that actually make me buy a HOKA. The whole email could be selling tennis balls for all it tells me about why the shoe matters.
- **The text sign-up module is dead weight.** "Sign up for HOKA texts... and we'll give you free expedited shipping on your first full-price order" — I'm here for a sale, not a text subscription, and definitely not a full-price deal.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** Yesterday's email was "Up to 40% Off Select Shoes." Same brand, same percentage, nearly identical subject line — I'm starting to feel like I'm being dripped on.
- **No urgency with a real deadline.** "Before they're gone" is soft. Give me a date: "Sale ends Sunday" would move me a lot faster.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the product order — men's first.** If there's any segmentation happening, a male subscriber should see the men's Clifton 10 in grey leading, not following the women's pink.
- 2. **Add one comfort signal to the hero.** Underneath "40% Off Shoes," something like "Including our most cushioned walking styles" would stop me mid-scroll instead of treating me like any shopper.
- 3. **Give the sale a hard end date.** "Ends Sunday, May 25" beats "before they're gone" every time — it's the difference between browsing later and clicking now.
- 4. **Cut the text sign-up block or move it to a PS at the very bottom.** It breaks the momentum of a sale email by pivoting to a totally different ask.
- 5. **Vary the subject line cadence.** Two near-identical subjects in two days trains me to tune them out. Even rotating to the product name would help.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clifton 10 Men's: Now 40% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cushion Picks Are On Sale — Today Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Summer Kickoff: markdowns on the walking shoes you've been watching. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clifton 10, Bondi, and more — up to 40% off through this weekend.`
