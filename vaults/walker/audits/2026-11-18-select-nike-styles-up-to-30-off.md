# Select Nike Styles Up to 30% Off 👟
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I've lost count of how many this week, and the last one, "This UGG fave is selling out fast," was only yesterday. This time the top banner is Nike, and the big "UP TO 30% OFF*" is easy to read on my phone without my glasses. That's better than another UGG send. Then I scrolled and the email turned into a holiday shoe catalog: boots, heels, rain boots, sandals, and UGG again near the bottom.
- The Nike hero promises something the rest of the email doesn't deliver. I see the Swoosh at the top and not one Nike shoe anywhere below it. Nothing here is hands-free, cushioned or arch-supported. It's mostly women's fashion boots and a leopard-print ankle boot. I might tap "Shop Now" on the Nike banner out of curiosity, but I wouldn't forward this to anybody. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 30% OFF*" in huge black type is readable without zooming.
- The Nike logo is a brand I know, and it matches the subject line.
- "Black Friday Deals" in the pink banner tells me what time of year it is.
- The "Shop by Size" block with wide width and wide calf buttons is useful. I wear wide, so I'd use it.
- Free shipping, in-store pickup and the Afterpay line are easy to find near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject says Nike, but I never see a Nike shoe. The banner is a logo and a button, so I don't know what the 30% applies to.
- Nearly all of it is women's boots and heels. Nothing here is for a 62-year-old man who wants a comfortable walking shoe.
- The UGG block with "NOW ONLY $99.96" is the same UGG push I've been getting all week.
- The "Your Weekly Holiday Recap" section and the five "Shop ..." buttons compete with the Nike offer. The email is very long, and I lost track of what the main point was.
- The top line, "Early access to the Black Friday Sale starts soon for VIPs", is tiny and tells me to join a program rather than giving me a deal. The asterisk fine print is far down at the bottom in tiny type.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put actual Nike men's walking and running shoes in the hero, with a sale price, so the subject line is backed up by what I see.
- 2. Cut the number of sections. Give me one offer, one product grid and one button.
- 3. Send me men's and comfort content if I've been opening those. Cushioned or arch-supported picks would get my attention.
- 4. Make the fine print readable, or say "select styles, ends Sunday" in the banner.
- 5. Hold off on repeating UGG. I've seen it plenty.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's main technical defect is that the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returned HTTP 400 when probed. There are also a few head-level markup and accessibility gaps, and SPF/DKIM could not be verified through the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below are limited to what was visible or reported by QA.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (400):** "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" → `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/qVIxTVsBD__tT3Wo2BkZFw~~/AAQRxRA~/49RUoVak19Cj0g…`
  - The tracked redirect returned 400 instead of 3xx. This is the SMS-acquisition CTA, so a real failure loses that conversion path.
  - The QA output shows an empty link-text/URL label field, and the URL ends in a bare `…Z` segment. It may be a truncated or malformed token, or the click tracker may reject the automated probe. Re-test with a browser UA and a fresh send before treating it as a confirmed production break.
- **[WARN] Plain-text URL bloat:** 27,888 of 31,986 characters (about 87%) of the plain-text part are URLs. The plain-text alternative is dominated by long `ablink.dsw.com` tracking strings, which raises spam-filter risk and makes the plain-text version hard to use.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 60 were skipped. The other links are unverified, and only the footer/utility links were deprioritized.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images):**
  - `src=https://ablink.dsw.com/q/08Quvr2HB-AB39yCtPKP7w~~/AAQRxRA~/YPMt1JsBtyZK57…` is a content image tracked through `ablink`, with no `alt`.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261118_P250961_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_N` is a tracking pixel with no `alt`. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Invalid `<meta>` declarations in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`, so it is not a valid charset declaration.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`, so it does nothing.
  - Clients that sniff encoding without the HTTP header may mis-render the 👟 emoji or special characters.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on mobile.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has none, which affects screen-reader pronunciation. The `<title>` is the generic "DSW".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=225090073` value in the pixel URL is a resolved numeric ID. The truncated portion of the HTML was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dsw.com` is unverified. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is a test-harness gap rather than a confirmed sender failure.
- The unsubscribe link is always probed and produced no failure. The footer, physical address, and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were not in the supplied data, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All links are opaque Braze/SparkPost-style redirects (`ablink.dsw.com/f/a/…`, `/q/…`), so UTM parameters and final landing pages cannot be confirmed from the source. A redirect-chain trace is needed.
- The only observable campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261118_P250961_V1_WM-_Offer_MI_N` in the Movable Ink pixel.
- The 400 on the SMS opt-in CTA means that path has no confirmed landing destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the SMS opt-in link:** re-probe with a real browser UA, follow the redirect chain, and regenerate the tracked link if it still returns 400. This is highest priority.
2. **Correct the `<head>` meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. **Add `alt` text** to the tracked content image, and `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel.
5. **Reduce plain-text URL weight** by shortening tracking links or trimming duplicate links in the text part.
6. **Capture full headers** (`Authentication-Results`, `List-Unsubscribe`) by sending directly to a mailbox that preserves them, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be confirmed.
7. **Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML,** with probe coverage raised above 12 of 72 links, to resolve the unverified continuity and footer-compliance items.
