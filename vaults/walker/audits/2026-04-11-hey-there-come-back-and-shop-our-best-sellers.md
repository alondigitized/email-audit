# Hey there, come back and shop our best sellers!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-04-11**
## Executive summary

- A standard win-back email that leans on a Jordan brand moment and a "best sellers" browse prompt, but lacks any personalization, urgency, or offer to actually motivate a lapsed customer to return. The SMS acquisition module is the most prominently designed element in the email — which is a misplaced priority for a re-engagement send. The email is clean and functional, but it argues no compelling reason to come back beyond "we exist."
- Inoffensive execution, low conversion potential. Nothing is broken, but nothing earns a click either.

## What's working

- **Jordan hero is solid.** "Jordan Is Here" with a clean product shot immediately signals newness and brand credibility. It's the strongest content in the email.
- **SMS sign-up module is well-designed.** Yellow background, bold copy, clear CTA — visually the most polished block.
- **Benefit icons are readable.** Free in-store pickup, free shipping, same-day delivery, and flexible payment methods are laid out cleanly with icons and short copy. Easy to scan.
- **Layout is mobile-appropriate.** Two-column grid for product tiles, legible text sizes throughout.

## What's weak

- **"Welcome!" is the wrong headline for a win-back.** This reads like a new subscriber email, not a re-engagement. There's no acknowledgment that the recipient has shopped before or any hook tailored to a lapsed customer.
- **No offer.** Win-back emails without a discount, exclusive access, or time-limited incentive dramatically underperform. There's nothing here to overcome inertia.
- **Product grid is vague.** Two tiles say "Shop Best Sellers" and "Shop Athletics Under $70" — these are category prompts, not product recommendations. A recipient who hasn't engaged recently gets no signal about what's worth clicking.
- **Third product tile is unlabeled.** The bottom-left image in the grid has no visible CTA or label — it's an orphaned image.
- **SMS ask buried mid-funnel is an odd priority.** Asking a lapsed customer to join your SMS list before you've re-earned their attention is backwards. This module should be a footer element in a win-back.
- **"Shop Now" CTA under the headline leads nowhere specific.** Without context, it's an empty prompt.
- **Shoe Perks account creation CTA is at the bottom.** If the recipient is already a customer (which they are, since this is a win-back), prompting them to create an account suggests the send list isn't properly segmented.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace "Welcome!" with a re-engagement hook.** Something like "We've missed you — here's what's new" or "Your favorites are back in stock" acknowledges the relationship.
- 2. **Add an offer.** Even 10% off or free shipping on next order gives a lapsed customer a reason to act now vs. later (or never).
- 3. **Show actual products.** Pull 3–4 specific SKUs as visual tiles with price. "Best sellers" as a category link is too abstract for someone who needs a reason to re-engage.
- 4. **Fix or label the unlabeled product tile.** If it's an intentional image, it needs a CTA. If it's a grid error, remove it.
- 5. **Move SMS sign-up to the footer.** Prioritize the conversion moment (return to shop) before the acquisition moment (join SMS).
- 6. **Remove the "Create an Account" CTA** from win-back sends, or replace it with "Sign In to Your Account" to match the actual audience.
- 1. **Verify UTM params** in all Attentive link destinations before reporting campaign attribution. Pull a decoded version of one `upn=` URL to confirm.
- 2. **Add `http-equiv` to charset meta** to prevent rendering issues in Outlook 2007–2016.
- 3. **Replace U+034F characters** in the preheader padding with only soft hyphens (U+00AD) or zero-width non-breaking spaces (U+FEFF) to reduce spam-filter risk.
- 4. **Populate `<title>`** with a descriptive value (e.g. "Shoe Carnival — Best Sellers") for screen reader compatibility.
- 5. **Re-evaluate the global link style reset** (`text-decoration:none; color:inherit`) — at minimum, retain underlines on CTAs or use sufficient color contrast to distinguish them.
- 6. **Add a first-name merge token** to the subject or headline; confirm fallback default is set.
- 7. **Provide full HTML** to complete compliance review of footer (address, unsubscribe link).
- 8. **Audit the CDN logo asset date** (2024-10-30) — confirm it matches current brand assets.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**All links are Attentive click-tracking redirects** in the form:
```
https://shoe-carnival.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=u001.<base64-encoded-payload>
```

- The `upn=` token encodes both the destination URL and the subscriber identifier. The final destination URL cannot be inspected directly from the source without decoding the payload.
- **Cannot confirm UTM parameters** are appended to destination URLs. This is a deferred risk — if UTM params are not embedded in the Attentive link builder, click attribution will be missing in analytics.
- Logo link in `row-1` uses the same tracking wrapper; confirm the homepage destination includes `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`.

**CDN asset URL** for logo:
```

```
The filename is Base64-encoded (`1857::Company Logo.png::2024-10-30T22:09:58.074263071Z`). This exposes an internal asset-ID/timestamp schema in a public URL, though it is low-severity. The asset dates to **October 2024** — confirm it is still the current brand logo.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Empty `<title>` tag** | `<title></title>` — screen readers in some email clients (e.g. VoiceOver + Apple Mail) will announce a blank document title |
| **Link underlines and color suppressed globally** | `#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit !important; text-decoration: none }` — disables visual link affordance inside Outlook/Exchange web clients; can fail WCAG 1.4.1 (use of color alone to distinguish links) |
| **`charset` meta missing `http-equiv`** | `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` should be `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` for full Outlook/legacy client compatibility; the bare form may cause garbled characters in older renderers |
| **Preheader padding technique** | Uses mixed U+034F (COMBINING GRAPHEME JOINER) and U+00AD (SOFT HYPHEN) invisible characters to flush the preheader — functional but U+034F is flagged by some spam filters; soft hyphens are the safer-only approach |

No `role` or ARIA attributes present, which is expected for email. Image alt text (`alt="Shoe Carnival"`) is correctly set on the logo.
