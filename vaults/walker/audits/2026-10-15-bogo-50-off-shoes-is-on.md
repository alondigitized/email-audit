# BOGO 50% off shoes is on!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- DSW again, and the second BOGO email today. The "Stock up! BOGO 50% off shoes" one came this morning, so this feels like the same sale sent twice. After last week's CIDER $15-off countdowns I'm glad the timer bar is still gone. The big green banner is easy to read. Buy one pair, get half off a second pair, and I got it in two seconds.
- It isn't aimed at me, though. "Tis the season to gift early" and the "So New, So You" grid are mostly women's boots, a mary jane and a clog. The only thing close to my feet is one gray sneaker, which is marked "Up to 40% off", not BOGO. The Shoebox Shuffle game with the laughing woman is not for a 62-year-old looking for cushioned slip-ons. I'd skim it and probably click through once to check the men's side. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The green banner says "BOGO 50% OFF*" in large type, and the "Shop Now" button is plain and easy to tap.
- The "Shop by Size" block with a Wide Width button is useful to me. Comfort shoppers often need that width.
- The fine print lists Skechers among the qualifying brands, which is good to know since I cross-shop them.
- The email has a barcode for in-store use and a Shop Men link, so I can use it on the DSW trip I already make.
- The look matches earlier DSW sends, so I trust it's the real thing.

## What's weak

- Five of the six products are women's, and none are comfort-led. The one sneaker I'd look at isn't even on the BOGO deal.
- I had to scroll to the tiny gray fine print to find that the sale runs Oct 15-18. The banner never says so, and the asterisk makes me wary.
- The Shoebox Shuffle game takes up a lot of space and has nothing to do with buying shoes. It also wants me to join the VIP program first.
- Product labels like "BOGO 1st pair only!" and "Limited time!" are small pink text that's hard for me to read on a phone. The size numbers are small too.
- It's the second BOGO send today, after "Stock up! BOGO 50% off shoes". I'm getting fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the dates in the banner ("Oct 15-18 only") and lead with a men's walking or slip-on shoe. Show the real BOGO math, for example "Two pairs, about $X each".
- 2. Send one BOGO email per sale window, not two on the same day. If there's a second one, make it a men's or comfort version.
- 3. Add a "Comfort & Walking" row with cushioned and slip-on picks, and label which brands count for BOGO.
- 4. Give the VIP game less space. Make the tags and size numbers bigger for phone readers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off through Sunday: men's walking shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Two pairs, half off the second. Ends Oct 18`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cushioned sneakers and slip-ons included. Skechers too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy one, get one 50% off. In stores and online through Sunday.`

# Technical Audit: DSW, "BOGO 50% off shoes is on!"

## 1. Technical Summary

- **Only 12 of 68 links were probed.** The other 56 (footer, utility, social) are unverified. The unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure.
- **Every link goes through `ablink.dsw.com`.** This is expected for click tracking. It hides the destination, so I can't confirm where any link lands.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Two images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/kMYvyxpIBCP3zM-kfnTBDw~~/…cQF6IKrdLZXWF_QyrUNNQQ…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=…` (a tracking pixel)
  
  The pixel should carry `alt=""`. The first image needs checking: if it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. This blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Two `<meta>` tags have no effect:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither is a valid directive. Charset falls back to the HTTP `Content-Type` header.
- **`text-size-adjust: none` is set** (together with `-ms-text-size-adjust: none`) in the reset CSS, which disables text auto-scaling on some clients. Low severity.
- **Web fonts load from `braze-images.com`** (`.woff` and `.ttf`). Clients that ignore `@font-face` will fall back. I can't see the fallback stacks in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated source can't rule them out in the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA check notes it was "Expected via AgentMail relay", so this is a limitation of the capture, not a confirmed failure. Verify against the raw headers from the sending domain (`e.dsw.com`).
- **Unsubscribe link:** it was probed and not flagged, but the footer isn't in the truncated source. I can't verify the physical postal address or the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers.
- **Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (24,852 of 29,628 chars). Long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can affect spam scoring.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com`, and the probe results don't expose final destinations or UTM parameters. The only campaign identifier visible is the pixel's `mi_ecmp=20261015_P261418_V1_WM-_Offer_Stan…` (truncated). No continuity issue is confirmed.
