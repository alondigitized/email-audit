- The black countdown banner at the top is the only thing that speaks to me. It says "Black Friday deals—up to 60% off," which is the same message as the 21st, 22nd and 26th. - The headline "CAMPEONES. SPAIN." is large and plain. I can read it on my phone without zooming.
- The black "SHOP NOW" and "ENTER NOW" buttons are big, high-contrast and easy to tap.
- The countdown banner gives a real deadline, and "up to 60% off" is a number I can understand.
- The layout is a single column with lots of white space, so it's easy to scroll.
- The sender and logo are clearly adidas, so I know who it's from.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for me. A soccer jersey and a lifestyle sneaker with a gum sole have nothing to do with cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.
- The 60% off banner is the only real offer, and it's pushed to the top and doesn't lead to a product. It also says nothing about which shoes are included.

- Every model is a young guy. There's nobody my age, and no walking or comfort shoe anywhere.

- 2. Send me something for my segment. A cushioned walking shoe with the price and an arch-support callout would do it.
- 3. Pick one theme per email. The World Cup jersey and a raffle together make the email feel like filler.
- 4. Put the member perk in plain words, such as "adiClub members get an extra X% off," instead of an entry form.
- 5. Slow down the cadence. After the November 21st to 26th run of near-identical sends, an email with something new would stand out.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: adidas, "Celebrate La Roja ⚽️🏆"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, responsive media queries). Confirmed problems are the missing plain-text part, several non-HTTPS image sources including the open-tracking pixel, missing `alt` attributes, and an ineffective charset declaration. The source was truncated and the auth headers weren't available, so several areas can't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains (`click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/...`), so final destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is served over HTTP:** `http://click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/010201a217f40d44-b08acac5-…-000000/CvYkyPC4lDs9D3Iu4BDKBYyc7YoV4mNr_MpcesfKLJk=452`. Clients that block mixed or insecure content may not load it, which would undercount opens. The request also goes over cleartext.
- **Four `http://` references to the "arrow" image:** `http://image.link.adidas.com/lib/fe6515707c62007e7715/m/3/b0c011a2-d468-414b-a8a2-8595b3ec7b34.jpg`. They may be blocked or rewritten by some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the content image:** `https://dv.adidas.com/o/7c0bdeb9-b641-4cf5-bbae-0ec5047df61c?cp_tp=…`. With images off, or in a screen reader, this content is lost or read as a filename.
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel** (`click.comms.adidas.com/CI0/…=452`). It should be `alt=""` with explicit `width="1" height="1"`. Screen readers may announce the URL.
- **The charset meta is ineffective:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"` or `charset=` attribute, so it declares nothing. The emoji in the subject and any non-ASCII body text rely on the MIME header alone.
- **The "arrow" image has an `alt` of `"arrow"`,** a non-descriptive name that the QA tool flagged as a filename-style label. This is minor and likely decorative, so `alt=""` is more appropriate.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the truncated source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **The plain-text alternative is missing.** QA: `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. A multipart/alternative email with no text part can hurt spam scoring and leaves text-only clients with nothing.
- **Authentication could not be verified.** QA: `Authentication-Results header not found`. This is attributed to the AgentMail relay stripping it, so it is an unknown rather than a confirmed sender failure. The sending domain is `us-news.comms.adidas.com`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment should be checked from raw headers.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** the footer and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were outside the truncated source and not provided, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are wrapped in `click.comms.adidas.com` redirects and were not followed, so any UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are hidden from this audit.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain part.** The QA FAIL is the highest-priority fix.
2. **Switch every asset URL and the tracking pixel to HTTPS** (`image.link.adidas.com`, `click.comms.adidas.com`). Confirm the hosts serve valid TLS.
3. **Add `alt` text:** descriptive for `dv.adidas.com/o/7c0bdeb9-…`, and `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` for the pixel and decorative arrow.
4. **Fix the charset declaration:** `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8">`.
5. **Re-run the audit with full headers and untruncated HTML** to verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe`, the footer address, and the resolved destination URLs and UTMs behind the 19 tracking links.
