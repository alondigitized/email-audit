# Ends Today! BOGO 50% Off for Members
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- I open this one and my first thought is: *they're doing BOGO 50% off and it ends today — that's a real deal.* The offer is big, it's front and center, and the urgency is credible for a July 11 date. "Ends Today" isn't just a subject line trick — after the July 5th "Final Call: Extra 15% Off" that actually ended, I've got reason to believe them when they say the clock is running out.
- What pulled me up short, same as the July 8th Back to School send: that banner at the very top says "BACK TO SCHOOL." I'm 62 years old. My kids are grown. I don't need back-to-school anything. Skechers has my purchase history and my Plus account — I'd expect them to know by now that I'm not buying sneakers for a sixth-grader. And then there's that same JSON garbage sitting in the preview slot again. Eight, nine emails in a row now with that raw schema code staring back at me from the inbox. I've given up being surprised by it, but it still chips away at the feeling that these folks have their act together.
- Still — BOGO 50% off is BOGO 50% off. I'd tap through to look at the Men's section. I probably wouldn't forward this to a friend, but I'd click.

## What's working

- **The offer is impossible to miss.** "BUY ONE GET ONE 50% OFF" sits dead center in the hero at a size I can read on my phone without squinting.
- **The "or 20% Off Single Pairs" fallback is honest.** If I only need one pair, I've still got a deal. No bait-and-switch.
- **"Ends Today" + "Last Chance" is credible here.** Given what Skechers has done on prior sale finales, I believe this one actually closes.
- **Men's section has its own block and button.** I don't have to scroll through women's pages to find my stuff — "SHOP MEN'S" gets me right there.
- **Skechers Plus is called out prominently.** This is a member offer. I'm a member. That small branding cue makes me feel like this email was meant for me, at least a little.

## What's weak

- **"Back to School" is up top again — still not my aisle.** The July 8th send already swung and missed on this; running it back a third day feels lazy.
- **The preview text is still raw JSON schema code.** Nine Skechers emails in a row with that junk in my inbox. This is not a one-time glitch — it's a systemic problem nobody is fixing.
- **Kids' section eats a third of the scroll.** I have zero need for kids' shoes. Real estate I'm skipping past.
- **Apparel section comes in late with its own 25% off offer.** I'm here for shoes. Splitting my attention between two different deals and two different percentages muddies the message.
- **No comfort language anywhere in the hero.** No cushioning, no arch support, nothing that tells me these are shoes built for how I walk. The deal is clear; the shoe quality is invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "Back to School" for the 60+ segment entirely.** If they're going to personalize the Plus member send, they should swap that hero banner for "Members-Only BOGO" or "Your Skechers Plus Exclusive" — anything that doesn't remind me I have no school-age children.
- 2. **Fix the broken preview text.** This is the most urgent change across all Skechers emails — it has been broken for every send I've reviewed. One line of real preheader copy would immediately lift open rates from this audience.
- 3. **Lead with a comfort signal in the subhead.** Something like "Built for all-day wear — buy one, get one 50% off" would tell me *why* to care about these shoes, not just what discount is waiting.
- 4. **Collapse the Kids section below the fold** or remove it for this segment. Put the Men's block and Men's Apparel side-by-side instead.
- 5. **Pick one offer and own it.** Hero = BOGO 50%. Apparel 25% off is a nice add-on, but burying it below Kids dilutes both.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% Off Ends Tonight — Members Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Chance: Buy One Pair, Get One Half Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up on walking shoes while the deal lasts — today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Plus member price: 50% off the second pair. Ends at midnight.`
