# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Well, it's another "we saved these for you" send, but this time there's no skull sneaker. After the cart nags on 11/05, 11/08, three on 11/11 and the "Your cart expires soon!" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends earlier today, I braced for it. Instead I get six shoes, and I never put any of them in a cart. The "saved for you" line is a stretch, but I'll take a different shoe over the same one.
- Two of the six are men's slip-ins that suit me. The Murette slipper looks like a good house or errand shoe, and the Respected boots are waterproof. Half the grid is women's sneakers, though, so it doesn't feel built for me. The Buy One Get One 50% Off banner is the real news. It's a lot easier to read than the shoe captions.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the slip-in slipper, and only because it's Black Friday week. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread until the sale ends.

## What's working

- The big "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" headline is easy to read on my phone without zooming.
- The Buy One, Get One 50% Off banner is clear. The dark background and large white type make it the second thing I notice.
- Two men's slip-ins, the Murette Scofield Slipper and the waterproof Orson boot, match what I actually wear.
- The Men, Women and Kids rows are wide and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The fine print states the dates, 11/12 to 11/17, so I know this ends today.

## What's weak

- The "saved for you" framing implies a cart I never built. It's the same trick as the earlier cart nags.
- Half the products are women's sneakers, so it doesn't feel aimed at me.
- The shoe captions are small, underlined and cramped. My eyes struggle with them.
- No prices appear on any shoe. I can't do the price-versus-quality math, and "20% off a single pair" gives me no dollar figure.
- It never mentions arch support or cushioning, which is what I shop for.
- The sale ends today, but the email never says so up top. The deadline is buried in the fine print.
- This is at least the sixth Skechers email in a few days. The urgency is hard to believe when they send something almost every day.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline and the offer in the hero: "Ends tonight: Buy One, Get One 50% Off." Drop the fake cart framing.
- 2. Show men's items first for male subscribers, with prices and a one-line comfort note such as "Arch Fit, hands-free."
- 3. Make the captions larger and drop the underlines. Make each shoe a tappable card with a price.
- 4. Show the sale price on each shoe so I don't have to work it out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: Buy 1, Get 1 50% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Slip-ins for men, 50% off the 2nd pair`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hands-free slip-ins and waterproof boots. Discount applies at checkout.`
