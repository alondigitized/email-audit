After the "Your cart expires soon!" sends on 11/05, 11/08, 11/11 and again all through 11/16 and 11/17, a big red "30% OFF SITEWIDE" banner feels like a breather. I can read it without squinting, and the "Hi, Walker" and the points line at the top tell me they know who I am.
- The email loses me after that. Four big photo tiles for Women, Men, Kids and Apparel, and not one shoe. The men's tile shows a guy in a bomber jacket and slacks. I shop for cushioned, slip-on walking shoes, and nothing here says "comfort" or "arch support." The 30% is also locked behind logging in as a Plus member, only through 11/22, and "exclusions apply." - The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" headline is huge and I get the offer in two seconds.
- "Hi, Walker" and the "1000 points, you have enough for a $5 reward! REDEEM" line show they know me as a member.
- The red banner with "Black Friday Early Access" fits the season.
- The Doorbuster block ("starting at $49.99") gives me a second reason to look.
- Each tile has its own button, and they're wide and easy to tap on my phone.
- It's a break from the repetitive cart reminders I've had all month.

## What's weak

- There's no footwear anywhere in the photos. I came for shoes and got jackets and jeans.
- Nothing mentions comfort, cushioning, arch support, slip-ins or walking.
- The fine print is tiny and hard to read. "Not stackable," "exclusions apply," "must be logged in," and the 11/22 end date make me doubt the 30% is really sitewide.
- The Doorbuster section has no product, no picture and no brand. "Starting at $49.99" tells me little.
- The footer is crowded with links, Klarna and Afterpay, and app badges. The Sale and Gift Guide links are tiny compared with the hero.
- Four category tiles and then a second list of the same categories is repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real shoe in the hero or the Men's tile, such as a slip-in walker or a cushioned Go Walk with the sale price, and show 30% off that exact pair. Say "Slip-ins" and "arch support" in the copy.
- 2. Replace the apparel-heavy grid with a "Comfort Walking Shoes" row of three or four shoes with star ratings.
- 3. State the sale end ("Ends 11/22 at midnight") near the hero button in plain, large text, and shorten the exclusion fine print.
- 4. Show real Doorbuster products with the before and after prices.
- 5. Tie it to my history. I've looked at the same sneaker for two weeks, so show me comfort styles I might actually buy.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Early access is open! 30% off sitewide for members"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`click.emails.skechers.com`) with one confirmed personalization failure (empty greeting), one malformed tracking pixel URL, and two insecure `http://` image sources. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Scheme-less pixel URL (source evidence):** `<img src="pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1db…" width="1" height="1" />` has no `http(s)://`. Clients resolve it as a relative path, so the request fails. Return Path/Validity inbox-placement tracking won't register. The QA run did not flag it.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026` is loaded over plain HTTP. It may be blocked or rewritten, and it has no `alt`.
- **Third-party beacons:** Four `beacon.krxd.net` calls (user-match and ad-impression) are present, in addition to the SFMC open pixel.
  - The `partner=…_sha25` parameter value looks like a SHA-256 hash of an identifier, so this is likely hashed-email sharing with a third party. I could not confirm that from the data.
  - These are hidden with `display:none` and `width/height=0`, so they have no accessibility impact.
- **Click links:** 33 click-tracking links were skipped by the QA probe, so redirect targets are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, with `http://` sources (QA):**
  - `o.gif` (ink1000.com)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` at `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/…`
  - The `.png` is a content-hosting image, not a pixel, so it may be blocked as mixed content or flagged by clients. Its missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap. The host serves HTTPS presumably, so switching the scheme is likely trivial.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text scaling disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS from resizing text.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` lack `http-equiv`, so neither declaration takes effect. Charset detection falls back to the HTTP/MIME headers.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Layout tables:** The visible tables (`<table cellpadding="0" cellspacing="0" border="0">`, `<table class="widthFull" width="640">`) have no `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **FAIL (QA):** The greeting renders as "Hi ," with an empty name slot. Either the AMPscript or personalization-string lookup returned empty for this subscriber, or there is no fallback or conditional wrapper. Without a fallback ("Hi there," or no greeting), any subscriber lacking a first name sees this.
- No raw `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Authentication-Results header not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable. The AgentMail relay likely stripped them, so this is a tooling limitation, not a confirmed sender fault. From domain: `emails.skechers.com`.
- **Unsubscribe / physical address:** Not assessable. The footer is outside the truncated source, and no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers were provided.
- **Third-party data sharing:** The Krux user-match beacons (§2) may require disclosure and opt-out coverage under state privacy laws. This depends on the privacy policy and cannot be confirmed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Insufficient data. UTM parameters on the 33 click links were not exposed (links are wrapped in SFMC redirects and not probed). The campaign ID `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11182026` appears in the beacons, so verify it is carried through to the landing-page UTMs. The pass rate is 71% (1 issue, 1 warning).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a fallback for the empty greeting:** Use an AMPscript `IIF(Empty(@firstName), "Hi there,", Concat("Hi ", @firstName, ","))` or drop the greeting when the name is empty.
2. **Fix the Return Path pixel:** Prefix it with `https://`.
3. **Switch http:// images to https://:** `o.gif` (ink1000.com) and the `image.emails.skechers.com` `.png`.
4. **Add `alt=""` to decorative images** and meaningful `alt` text to the `.png` if it carries content.
5. **Fix meta tags and accessibility basics:**
   - Use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">`.
   - Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
   - Add `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
   - Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. **Review the Krux beacons** for privacy-disclosure and consent coverage.
7. **Re-run QA with full headers and the complete HTML:** Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, `List-Unsubscribe` and the footer address, and resolve the click-tracking redirects to validate UTMs.
