# Start your holiday shopping NOW!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. I got the "Happy VIP Days" email earlier today with the same giant "25% OFF*" banner, and now this one leads with that banner and then switches to a holiday pitch. The subject says start holiday shopping NOW, but the top of the email is still the VIP sale. So I wasn't sure which message I was supposed to act on. The asterisk is also back. It says "regular-price styles only," which means the clearance I've been chasing all week is excluded.
- The lower half is a "Holiday Pre-Check" collage with a woman hugging a pile of boots and sneakers, a kid, and a pair of Timberlands. It's pretty, but I'm a 62-year-old man looking for cushioned slip-ons, and I don't see a single comfort claim anywhere. The price tiers (under $25, $50, $75) and the size grid are useful. The wide width button is a nice touch, but it's buried near the bottom.
- Would I click? Maybe the "Shop Men" link, if I were already planning to buy. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread if it shows up again tomorrow.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF*" on purple is big and readable on my phone, and the code VIPFUN is right there.
- The price-tier bars (Under $25 / $50 / $75 / Over $100) fit how I shop. I decide on money first.
- "Shop Wide Width" is something I'd actually use, and the size grid makes it quick to jump to my size.
- The Timberland boot and the men's "Just for him" tile at least acknowledge that men shop here.
- Free shipping for VIPs, in-store pickup and the 15% text sign-up are all spelled out in the footer area.

## What's weak

- The subject says holiday, but the hero is the VIP sale. Two messages fight for the top spot.
- The asterisk and "regular-price styles only" mean the 25% off doesn't apply to the clearance items I've been watching. It feels like fine print I have to hunt for.
- The imagery is mostly women's boots and a kid. The one men's shoe is a work boot, not a comfort walker.
- Nothing about cushioning, arch support or slip-on ease. It's all gifting mood and no shoe facts.
- The small size numbers and the dense legal text at the bottom are hard to read without zooming.
- It's another near-identical DSW send, after a week of "Extra 10% off clearance" and today's "Happy VIP Days."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the holiday gift guide or lead with the VIP 25% offer, not both stacked.
- 2. State the exclusions in plain words right next to the offer, such as "Not valid on clearance," instead of only using an asterisk.
- 3. Add a men's comfort row with named brands (Skechers, Brooks, New Balance, Hoka) and labels like "slip-on" or "arch support." Use gift picks for dads and granddads.
- 4. Put the size and wide-width shortcuts higher, and make the text bigger.
- 5. Space out the sends. I'd rather get one strong email than four similar ones.

# Technical Audit: DSW, "Start your holiday shopping NOW!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent, table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. QA found one confirmed broken link, the "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA, which returned HTTP 400. The other findings are minor: a plain-text part that is mostly URLs, two images without alt text, and malformed `<meta>` tags in the head. Only 12 of 68 links were probed, so link coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken CTA (confirmed by QA, FAIL):** The "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" link returned HTTP 400.
  - URL: `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/_7sfB2-DYOdJVweWBQWaSg~~/AAQRxRA~/Uqm3dgvE…`. It is a very long Braze/SendGrid-style wrapped tracking URL, over 700 characters.
  - The QA output shows no destination, so I can't tell whether the redirect failed or the probe was rejected. A 400 on a signed click-tracking URL can also come from bot or user-agent filtering. Re-test it with a real mobile client before treating it as a live outage.
  - If it is real, the SMS opt-in offer is unreachable for every recipient.
- **Probe coverage is partial:** 12 links were probed and 56 were skipped, so I can't confirm the state of the other links. Footer and social links were deprioritized by design.
- **Tracking pixels:** The source includes `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb0…/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261001_P260927_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…`. This is a dynamic-content pixel carrying a user ID in the query string (`mi_u`). It is not a defect, but the `mi_u` parameter is a per-recipient identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/-zD7qIrBjLOnDd0t-WEHOg~~/AAQRxRA~/r2VOsHO7…` has no alt attribute.
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif` has no alt attribute.
  - Both look like tracking pixels. Setting `alt=""` is the correct fix, so screen readers skip them.
- **Ineffective meta declarations in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` has no `http-equiv="Content-Type"`.
  - `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` has no `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"`.
  - Without `http-equiv`, neither tag does anything. Charset then depends on the HTTP/MIME headers.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Generic `<title>`:** The title is `DSW`, which says nothing about the message. This is minor.
- **Truncated source:** I can't verify table roles, the alt text on the remaining images, or the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (QA WARN):** 21,576 of 25,709 characters (about 84%) are URLs. That exceeds the 70% threshold and can raise spam-filter scores, and the part is hard to read in text-only clients. It is typical of long wrapped tracking links in Braze auto-generated text.
- **Unsubscribe:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure for it. I can't confirm its presence or the physical postal address from the truncated source.
- **Authentication headers:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe` headers were not provided, so I can't assess them. The sending domain is `e.dsw.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** All links are wrapped in `ablink.dsw.com` redirects, so I can't see the destination URLs or any UTM parameters.
- **Campaign identifier:** The only visible one is `mi_ecmp=20261001_P260927_V1_W-_Offer_Occas…`, which is in the Movable Ink pixel.
- **Landing-page alignment:** Not assessable. The broken SMS opt-in CTA is the only continuity failure confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the SMS opt-in link.** Re-test `ablink.dsw.com/f/a/_7sfB2-DYOdJVweWBQWaSg~~/…` with a real client. If it still returns 400, repair the Braze link or its destination. This is the highest priority.
2. Add `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible"` to the two meta tags.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and set `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
4. Shorten the plain-text part with a leaner text template or fewer wrapped links, so the URL share falls below 70%.
5. Re-run the audit with full headers and the full HTML to cover authentication, `List-Unsubscribe`, the postal address and UTM verification.
