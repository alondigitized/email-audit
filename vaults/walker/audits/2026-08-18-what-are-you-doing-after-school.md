# What Are You Doing After School?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- "What Are You Doing After School?" — well, I'm 62, I'm not doing anything after school because I haven't been in school since Nixon was president. This whole email is a young family's back-to-school lookbook: a woman in Birkenstock Arizonas striking a pose, a teenage boy in a hoodie holding a basketball, a little kid with a water bottle heading out to play. None of it's me, and none of it's for me.
- Buried in there is one shoe I'd actually consider — the Boston Clog shows up twice, and that's a legit comfort slip-on style I've looked at before. But it's dressed up in "Post Practice Reset" gym-locker copy aimed at a teenager, not pitched on cushioning or arch support the way I'd want. The Shoe Perks 2X/3X points and the free $10 reward at the bottom are the only things that made me pause, and only because I'm already a member.
- Not clicking, not forwarding. This is the fifth "back to school" email from Shoe Carnival in about a week — same playbook different headline — and I'm getting close to just muting these until fall actually shows up.

## What's working

- The Boston Clog is a real slip-on comfort shoe I'd wear, even if the ad copy around it wasn't for me.
- Shoe Perks 2X/3X points on Birkenstocks and the $10 reward at pickup are concrete, easy-to-understand offers.
- Clean layout, big readable type, no glitches or overlapping text — easy enough to scroll on my phone.

## What's weak

- Every model is decades younger than me — a young woman, a teen boy, a kid — nothing says "this brand sells shoes for guys my age."
- "Time to Chill," "Post Practice Reset," "Get Outside" — all locker-room/back-to-school talk, nothing about comfort, cushion, or arch support in the actual copy.
- This is the fourth or fifth back-to-school themed send this month (the "TAKE NOTE" and "Fit Check" ones both hit the same notes) — it's starting to feel like the only setting on their dial.
- No section for men's casual or walking shoes at all — just kids, teens, and a woman's sandal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give me a "Dad/Grandpa comfort pick" module — a men's walking shoe or slip-on with cushioning called out by name, not buried under a kid's basketball photo.
- 2. Drop the school theme for a week — I'd bet a chunk of your list isn't shopping for a classroom, and the repetition is numbing whatever urgency the campaign's going for.
- 3. Lead with the Shoe Perks $10 reward and point multiplier higher up — that's the one thing in here that actually moves a loyalty member like me.
- 4. If Boston Clog is the crossover product for older shoppers, say so — swap "Post Practice Reset" for language about all-day comfort and support.
