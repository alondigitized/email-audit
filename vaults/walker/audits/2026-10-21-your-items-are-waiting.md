# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- It's the pink flower sneaker again. This is the third or fourth cart nag I've gotten this week, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. "We noticed you noticing us" is creepy, and it's the same line as the 10/19 "Your Items Are Waiting!" send. The hero is the D'Lites Spring Bloom, a women's shoe with embroidered flowers. That's not me.
- The "more great options" grid is where it gets better. There's a men's slip-in with Contour Foam, a waterproof slip-in boot, and a relaxed-fit slip-on, which is the kind of shoe I wear. But it sits under a hero I don't care about, and two of the six tiles are boys' light-up sneakers. The "Members take 20% off" banner is a real offer, but it's buried below the grid, and it expires today. I'd skim it, maybe tap the slip-on, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The men's slip-ins in the grid (Contour Foam, Relaxed Fit, waterproof boot) are what I shop for.
- The "20% OFF" members banner is big and easy to read, and the "Valid thru 10/21" deadline is real.
- The large navy "Complete Your Order" button is easy to tap on my phone.
- The category list (Women, Men, Kids, Sale) is simple, with big tap targets, so I can find Men fast.
- The Skechers logo and look match what I see on their site, so I trust it.

## What's weak

- I have no cart, so "Your cart is still waiting" is false, and it makes me trust them less.
- The hero shoe is a women's floral sneaker. It's the same one they've shown me all week.
- Two boys' sneakers in the grid have nothing to do with me.
- The 20% off is below the fold, and the fine print says "select full-priced styles." Nothing says which of these shoes qualify.
- The product captions are tiny and I'd have to squint. There are no prices, and nothing about arch support or cushioning.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send cart emails to people with no cart. If you must, use a real browsed item, and for me that means a men's slip-in.
- 2. Move the 20% members banner to the top, right under the logo, and say which shoes it applies to.
- 3. Fill the grid with men's comfort styles, with prices and a line like "Arch Fit" or "Cushioned."
- 4. Make the product captions bigger.
- 5. Drop the "we noticed you noticing us" line.
