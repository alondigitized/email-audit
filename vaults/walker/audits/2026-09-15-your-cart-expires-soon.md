# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- There she is again — that pink floral D'Lites sneaker, front and center like it's the only shoe Skechers sells. This makes six or seven sends this week with that exact pair chasing me around, and it's not even close to something I'd wear. Below it there's actually a decent men's grid — the Arch Fit Arcade, the Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman — but I have to scroll past a floral shoe and a "you liked this" lie to get there.
- The 20% off code for Plus members is real and I am a member, so that part's fine. But leading with a shoe that has nothing to do with me, again, makes me trust this brand less every time it lands, not more.
- I'm not clicking on the hero. I might scroll down to the men's section out of habit, but this is a mark-as-read, and if anything I'd tell a buddy "watch out, they never learn your size."

## What's working

- The 20% off Plus member banner is clear, has a real code (PREVIEW), and a real date range (thru 9/20) — no hunting for it.
- The men's grid further down has actual product I'd consider — the Arch Fit Arcade and the Slip-ins Relaxed Fit Altman look like real walking shoes with cushioning.
- "Shop All Best Sellers" and category links are big, tappable, easy to read without zooming.
- Payment/pickup/app footer options are laid out cleanly, not cluttered.

## What's weak

- Hero shoe is a pink floral D'Lites — same one that's chased me all week in "Did you forget something?" and "Your cart expires soon!" I never even looked at this shoe.
- "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG" is flat wrong — I never liked or clicked this.
- Real men's product is buried two grids down, past a hero and a big CTA button that only serves the wrong shoe.
- This is the same D'Lites-chasing playbook as three other emails this week — the cadence is exhausting, not persuasive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero to one of the men's shoes already sitting in the grid below — the Arch Fit Arcade or the Altman — so the "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button actually points at something I'd buy.
- 2. Stop recycling the same floral D'Lites across every "cart" and "forgot something" email — rotate creative or suppress repeats within the week.
- 3. If personalization can't nail my browsing history, drop the "you liked these" framing entirely and just lead with the 20% off banner — that's honest and I already respond to it.
- 4. Move the Plus member discount banner above the hero image so it's the first thing I see.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Arch Fit picks are 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save on Arch Fit, Slip-ins and more — code PREVIEW`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall Preview Sale: 20% off regular-priced styles for Plus members`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Cart-abandonment email built on Attentive's ESP (msgs.skechers.com). Two confirmed broken links (including the unsubscribe link) and a missing plain-text MIME part are the critical issues; remainder are HTTPS/alt-text hygiene warnings.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Product image link "Men's OG Anunoby Limited Edition..." returns HTTP 401 — broken. Redirect target behind Attentive's click-tracking wrapper (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/0XD89NEG...`) is rejecting/blocking the request.
- Two additional tracked links ("Complete Your Order", "You liked these and you weren't wrong.") returned 429 during probing — rate-limited, not confirmed broken, but should be manually re-verified since they're primary CTA/cross-sell links.
- All content links route through Attentive's `e.attentivemail.com/e/c/...` click-tracking redirector, consistent with expected ESP behavior.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Image `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` missing `alt` text.
- Open-tracking pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open`) missing `alt` text — low impact (tracking pixel, not user-facing), but flagged by QA.
- 12 of 12 non-utility images served over `http://` instead of `https://` (hero logo, product image, app store badges, Afterpay, Find a Store, social icons). Non-HTTPS image sources are commonly blocked by mail clients (e.g., Outlook, Gmail proxy over HTTPS-only), degrading render.
- Responsive/mobile CSS (`@media max-width:620px`) and MSO-conditional Outlook handling present and structurally sound.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- No issues found in the visible markup.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
