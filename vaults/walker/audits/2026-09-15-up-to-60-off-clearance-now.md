# Up to 60% off clearance NOW
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least this one's got an actual number on it — "up to 60% off" beats another "become a VIP" nag. But two seconds in, same story as every DSW email this week: I scroll past a whole grid of clearance shoes and it's boots, strappy heels, sandals, a leopard-print bootie. One pair might squeak by as unisex sneakers, the rest is nothing I'd wear to walk the dog. I'm not the customer for this grid, and by now I've seen this dark clearance layout, the size-picker boxes, and the "SHOP BY SIZE" bar enough times that I'm scrolling on autopilot.
- I'd mark this one read and move on. Not annoyed enough to unsubscribe, not interested enough to click. If Skechers or Hoka sends me something with an actual walking shoe and a real discount, that's getting the tap — this one's just noise in a week of noise.

## What's working

- The headline number — "up to 60% off" — is clear and easy to read at a glance, no squinting needed.
- "Online only, prices as marked" plus the in-store 30% mention is honest about the split, I appreciate not being tricked into thinking it's storewide everywhere.
- Big obvious "SHOP CLEARANCE" button, I know exactly where to tap if I wanted to.
- Size grid up top is a nice touch for anyone who actually shops DSW regularly — not for me though.

## What's weak

- Every single shoe in that "JUST DROPPED" grid is women's — booties, strappy sandals, a leopard heel, low block sandals. Nothing here says "62-year-old guy who wants cushioned slip-ons."
- No men's category shortcut anywhere near the top — I have to hunt through the nav bar for "MEN" myself.
- This is the same dark clearance template, same "SHOP BY SIZE" strip I've now seen close to a dozen times this week from this brand.
- Random detour into Hispanic Heritage Month / DSW Foundation messaging feels bolted on — nice cause, wrong spot in a clearance sale email, and it just adds more scrolling before I get to anything useful.
- Wall of "conveniences" icons (pickup, repair, price matching) and footer legal text just drags the email out longer with nothing new to offer me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the "JUST DROPPED" grid by gender, or at minimum lead with a couple of men's styles if you're sending this to men on the list — right now it reads like a blanket send.
- 2. Cut the Hispanic Heritage Month block out of a clearance-offer email entirely; it belongs in its own send, not wedged between the shoe grid and the store amenities.
- 3. Add a "MEN'S CLEARANCE" button next to or instead of the generic "SHOP ALL CLEARANCE" — give me a direct path.
- 4. Trim the email — conveniences icons, DSW Foundation blurb, and legal disclaimers could all be one shorter footer instead of three separate blocks.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New men's styles just marked down for fall`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sneakers, slip-ons, and more — up to 60% off online`
