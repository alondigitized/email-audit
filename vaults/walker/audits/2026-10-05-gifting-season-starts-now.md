# Gifting season starts… NOW!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- DSW again. The banner is pink this time, not purple, and I'll give them that. "Holiday Pre-Check" with a woman holding a pile of boots and sneakers is friendly enough. But it's the first week of October. I just got "BOGO 50% off starts…NOW!" this morning, and now they're telling me gifting season starts NOW. Everything starts NOW with these people.
- The top of the email is built for women's shoes: UGG boots, a woman in a yellow dress, a platform clog. The two buttons under that are "Shop Early Gifting" and "Shop All Women." I had to scroll a long way to find "We got gifts for guys, too." That line reads like an afterthought. The men's row is a work boot, a white sneaker and a boat shoe. The red "BOGO 50% off!" and "BOGO 1st pair only!" under them are tiny, and I almost missed them.
- I'd skim it and not click. If my wife was shopping for boots I might forward it to her, but nothing here talks to my feet. Nothing mentions cushioning, arch support or slip-ons.

## What's working

- The pink "Holiday Pre-Check" hero is easy to read and looks different from the purple VIP banners I've been getting all week.
- The men's row has real shoes I'd wear: a boot, a clean sneaker and a boat shoe. They have price-style tags, so I know what I'm looking at.
- "Gifts for any budget" with Under $25, $50, $75 and Over $100 is simple. I can tap one without thinking.
- The size grid runs from 5 to 11 with wide-width and wide-calf buttons. It's big, easy to read and easy to tap.
- The footer is clear about VIP, in-store pickup and free shipping.

## What's weak

- Women's shoes get the whole top of the email. A 62-year-old guy has to scroll past that to reach one row of men's shoes.
- The BOGO note under the men's shoes is tiny red text, and "1st pair only" confuses me. Is it half off the second pair or not? It's the best offer in the email and it's buried.
- The UGG section is a big yellow banner for something I'm not shopping for. The "VIPs earn points" bubble is small and I can barely read it.
- The size grid starts at 5 and the men's range is hard to pick out. Nothing tells me if the sizes are men's or women's.
- I've had at least ten DSW emails in a week. "Gifting season starts… NOW!" is the same urgency again with nothing new behind it.
- Nothing about comfort, cushioning or arch support anywhere, and no walking shoes, slip-ons or Hoka-type brands.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero by half and put the BOGO 50% offer in big type at the top. Right now it's the smallest thing in the email.
- 2. Send me a men's version. I've bought men's shoes from you before, so lead with men's boots, sneakers and slip-ons instead of women's UGGs.
- 3. Spell out the BOGO: "Buy one, get the second pair 50% off. Ends Sunday." Put a real date on it so I believe it.
- 4. Add a comfort row for walkers, with cushioned, slip-on and arch-support styles.
- 5. Space these out. Three emails in a day is why I stop reading them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally conventional (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, tracked links through `ablink.dsw.com`), but its "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" CTA returned HTTP 400 and the plain-text part is almost entirely URLs. Only 12 of 61 links were probed, and the HTML source was truncated, so compliance and continuity checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken CTA (400):** the link "Opt-In to Texts Get 15% Off" returned 400 from `https://ablink.dsw.com/f/a/YLyfObIhYZI7dG8xxBvxRg~~/AAQRxRA~/Ytuxen1VnV-v-oEp…`.
  - The redirect token is roughly 600 characters long.
  - A 400 on a tracking-domain redirect often means the token was rejected, either because it is malformed or expired, or because the probe was blocked as a bot. The QA data can't tell these apart, so confirm with a real-client click test.
  - If the failure is real, the SMS opt-in offer is dead for all recipients.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is >70% URLs:** 21,869 of 25,129 characters are URLs. This points to very long tracked URLs and a plain-text part generated from the HTML links. It is a spam-filter risk and gives a poor text-only experience.
- **Probe coverage:** 49 of 61 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized), so their health is unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:**
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/e6f23fb024fdcb2cc855b9ed672710d4/o.gif?mi_u=225090073&mi_ecmp=20261005_P261395_V1_W-…` is a third-party open pixel, separate from the `ablink` click tracking.
  - It carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u=225090073`) in the query string.
  - No functional defect is confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text on two images:**
  - `https://ablink.dsw.com/q/9SKrnMiGpx-f4ir8WIC9pg~~/AAQRxRA~/_wIn2inL3KlVrB_f_pjmGKdQU7QC39hXI4OfqUfrBk58EVTW0l…`
  - `https://mi.dsw.com/p/up/…/o.gif`
  - Both look like tracking or open pixels, so the screen-reader impact is low. Adding `alt=""` is still correct for decorative or tracking images.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Malformed meta tags:** `<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />` and `<meta content="IE=Edge" />` have no `http-equiv` attribute, so neither does anything. Charset declaration then depends on the HTTP headers.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Title:** `<title>DSW</title>` is generic and doesn't reflect the subject line "Gifting season starts… NOW!".
- **Other source observations:**
  - Web fonts (Futura PT, Helvetica Now) load from `braze-images.com` as WOFF/TTF. Outlook and many clients ignore them, which is a fallback-stack concern. The visible source doesn't show the stacks, and the HTML was truncated.
  - Dark mode is declared (`color-scheme: light dark`, `prefers-color-scheme` rules), with no defect visible in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (such as `{{ }}` or `%%` patterns) appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The rest of the markup wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- The QA output says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and none is flagged as failing.
- The visible source and QA data do not contain the following, so I can't confirm them either way:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers
  - the physical postal address
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
- Sender is `dsw@e.dsw.com`, a dedicated subdomain, with link branding on `ablink.dsw.com`.
- **Compliance note:** the CTA that failed is an SMS opt-in. If it is broken, the TCPA-consent flow is not working, though the email itself isn't violating anything.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated because every probed destination is hidden behind an opaque `ablink.dsw.com` redirect token.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261005_P261395_V1_W-_New_NewEarl…` on the open pixel. It is truncated, so I can't verify it against the landing pages.
- Landing-page alignment is not verifiable except for the 400 on the opt-in CTA.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or re-test the SMS opt-in link.** Re-issue the `ablink` token if it is expired or malformed, and confirm it resolves in a real client.
2. **Shorten the plain-text part.** Strip tracking from the plain-text URLs or condense the links, so URLs fall well below 70% of the text.
3. **Correct the meta tags:** use `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" content="IE=edge">`.
4. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
5. **Add `alt=""`** to the two tracking images.
6. **Widen audit coverage.** Re-run with the full HTML and raw headers to check unsubscribe headers, authentication results, UTM parameters and the 49 unprobed links.
