# Your Skechers Plus Monthly Summary is Here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- Well, here's the second Skechers email in my inbox today — this one after "Member Month is Here!" that already showed me my points and welcomed me back. The moment I see the big blue block with 1,000 POINTS and a $5 REWARD badge underneath it, I'm paying attention. That's real money sitting in my account, and I want to know how to use it. No fluff, no guessing what the email's about — it gets right to the numbers.
- But something's wrong before I even crack it open. Whatever is sitting in the preview pane where a normal sentence should be looks like a chunk of computer code — "@context," "http://schema.org" — that kind of gibberish makes a guy like me wonder whether to even trust tapping this thing. Not a good first impression from the inbox.
- Inside, the email is clean and useful. The 2X points offer is ticking down — expiring today, June 1 — which honestly does push me to act. The "We Picked These Just for You" shoes look like they could be my kind of casual walkers, but I'd need a name and a size chart to know for sure. I'd click through to check my reward and probably grab a look at those shoes. I would not forward this one — the broken preview text alone would make me embarrassed to pass it along.

## What's working

- **The points balance headline does the job** — 1,000 in large type, stacked against a clean dark background, lands immediately.
- **The $5 reward is surfaced clearly** — "You've Earned a $5 Reward" with the coin badge right below the balance; I don't have to hunt for it.
- **The 2X points deadline creates real urgency** — "Thru 6/1" means I have to move today, not next week, and that's a decision nudge that actually works on me.
- **The "picked for you" shoes look like casual walkers** — low-profile, cushioned-looking styles, not performance runners or dress shoes I'd never buy.
- **Free shipping and returns are called out in the footer** — that matters when I'm comparing with DSW on the same day.

## What's weak

- **The preview text is completely broken** — raw JSON-LD schema markup leaked into the inbox preview. From my phone it looks like spam or a busted email, and I nearly didn't open it.
- **This is the second Skechers email today** — "Member Month is Here!" already hit my inbox this morning. Two from the same sender on the same day with largely the same loyalty message feels like they're burning through my patience.
- **"Shop or Answer Questions" CTA is muddy** — "VIEW MY ACCOUNT" doesn't tell me what kind of questions I'm answering or how many points I get. I need one more sentence of specificity or I skip it.
- **The "picked for you" shoes have no labels** — no model name, no price, no arch support or slip-on callout. They could be anything. My thumb won't know where to land.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the preheader immediately** — this is priority one. Replace the leaked JSON with something like: *"$5 reward waiting + 2X points end tonight."* That sentence would have changed my open likelihood significantly.
- 2. **Spread these two emails out by at least 24 hours** — a monthly summary and a "Member Month is Here!" landing on the same day feels like a scheduling accident, not a strategy.
- 3. **Label the "picked for you" shoes** — model name, starting price, and one comfort callout (slip-on, wide width, arch support) per shoe. That's what makes me click the individual tile instead of bailing to the homepage.
- 4. **Give the question survey CTA some teeth** — change "VIEW MY ACCOUNT" to "Answer 3 Questions, Earn 50 Points" and I'll tap it without a second thought.
- 5. **Move the 2X points deadline to the email subject or top hero** — right now it's buried inside the banner. "2X Points Ends Tonight" in the subject line would do more work than the entire banner section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Walker, your $5 reward is ready — 2X points end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$5 reward inside + double points end tonight, June 1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `1,000 points earned. Your $5 reward is waiting — plus 2X points until midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Skechers Plus Monthly Summary (05/28/2026 Send)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a systemic HTTP/HTTPS mixed-content problem across 12+ production images, and includes one asset served from a staging subdomain (`image.devemails.skechers.com`). These issues will cause broken images in security-enforcing clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook.com) and represent the highest-priority fixes before the next send.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**HTTP open pixel — ink1000.com**
```
http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8107766165&mi_ecmp=TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_05282026_A
```
Served over plain HTTP. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy/block non-HTTPS resources, meaning open-rate measurement for those clients will be undercounted.

**Missing protocol on Return Path inbox-monitoring pixel**
```
pixel.app.returnpath.net/pixel.gif?r=964ce1dbf0dcef79cbad82c12fe25342701e4499
```
The `src` attribute has no scheme (`http://` or `https://`). Behavior is client-defined — most will treat it as a relative URL, which is invalid in email and will silently fail to load.

**Third-party DMP sync pixels (Krux/Salesforce)**
```
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_sha25&partner_uid=9153f33fe5cd...
https://beacon.krxd.net/1x1_usermatch.gif?partner=LprH63a7_sfmc_100007688_subid&partner_uid=121173391
```
These pixels pass a SHA-256 subscriber hash and raw subid to a third-party DMP on open. See §5 for compliance implications.

**24 click-redirect links** — all routed through `click.emails.skechers.com`. UTM parameter verification was not possible without resolving those redirects (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Staging asset in production email**
```
https://image.devemails.skechers.com/lib/fe33157075640674761d74/m/1/summary-tier-blue-angle.png
```
This image is pulled from a `devemails` subdomain. If that host has restricted access or different cache TTLs, it will break for recipients or produce inconsistent results. Needs to be migrated to `image.emails.skechers.com`.

**12 images served over HTTP — will be blocked**

A representative set:
```
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png  (Skechers logo)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/49468f73-...png
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/0ec56c9f-...png  (App Store badge)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/9bb508d0-...png  (Google Play badge)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/b782e0aa-...png  (Instagram)
http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/11/a5c4cae0-...png  (Facebook)
... (7 additional footer icons)
```
The `image.emails.skechers.com` domain supports HTTPS — these are incorrectly hard-coded as HTTP. The logo breaking on first render is the highest-visibility failure.

**Missing alt text — 4 confirmed images**
- `o.gif` (ink1000.com open pixel) — acceptable to omit alt here if `alt=""` is set, but it is absent
- `summary-tier-blue-angle.png` — decorative tier graphic, no alt
- `9010649e-...jpg` — unknown content, no alt
- `49468f73-...png` — unknown content, no alt

Images without `alt=""` at minimum will render as broken-image icons in clients that block images, rather than collapsing gracefully.

**Google Fonts `@import` inside `<style>` block**
```css
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Mulish:wght@400;700;900&display=swap');
```
This `@import` is nested inside a `<style>` tag within a `<td>`, not in `<head>`. Gmail strips `<style>` blocks not in `<head>`. The font will silently fail in Gmail (the majority client for most lists), and the fallback stack is not visible in the truncated source — needs verification.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Prevents iOS Mail and Safari from scaling text for readability. WCAG 1.4.4 requires text to be resizable up to 200% without loss of content. This is a blanket override that affects all text in the email.

**Duplicate mobile media query blocks**

Two nearly identical `@media only screen and (max-width: 375px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 640px)` blocks appear in the `<head>`, then a third `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)` block is in a separate `<style>` tag in `<body>`. The duplication inflates payload size and creates rule-ordering ambiguity.

**Malformed charset meta tag**
```html
<meta content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```
Missing `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. Should be:
```html
<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=utf-8" />
```

### 5. Compliance

**Third-party data sync without in-email disclosure**

The Krux beacon at `beacon.krxd.net` transmits a SHA-256 subscriber hash (`partner_uid=9153f33f...`) and raw subid to Salesforce DMP on open. Under GDPR/CCPA, behavioral data sharing with a third-party DMP at point of open requires disclosure. Confirm this is covered in Skechers' privacy policy and that the subscriber's consent basis extends to DMP syndication.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link** — not visible in the truncated source. Assumed present in footer based on standard ESP templates; requires confirmation against the full HTML.

**Authentication headers** — not provided; cannot audit SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status from HTML alone. Verify via mail headers from a test send.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 24 outbound links are wrapped in `click.emails.skechers.com` redirects, which prevents direct UTM inspection. The campaign ID `TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_05282026_A` is present in pixel parameters, indicating tracking is configured at the ESP level. Verify that click-redirect destinations append consistent UTMs (e.g., `utm_campaign=TRG_US_EN_MONTHLYSUMMARY_1_05282026_A`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_source=skechers`). Cannot confirm without resolving redirects.
